About Joe
Joe Fallin is an Oklahoma City – area dentist and small-business professional who has spent his career serving patients and building roots in the community.
He has spent decades serving patients and building strong relationships with families across District 85.
Joe’s healthcare background gives him a unique perspective on how public policy impacts everyday life for Oklahomans.
He understands the challenges that working families face when accessing care and the pressures that small businesses face when regulations and costs rise, and the importance of building safe neighborhoods where people want to live and raise their children.
The people of District 85 care about their quality of life, strong schools, safe streets, and their financial security.
These are exactly the priorities that Joe wants to bring into the legislature.
He’s running for Oklahoma House District 85 because he believes the job of a State Representative is straightforward:
Protect public safety, respect taxpayers, and deliver practical solutions that improve everyday life for Oklahomans.
Joe values family, hard work, and community service.
He believes that effective leadership starts with listening to the concerns of everyday people.
