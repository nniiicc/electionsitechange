What I’m Fighting For
Common Sense Over Chaos.
Truth Over BS.
Florida Over the Billionaires.
I’m not a career politician—I’m a veteran, a dad, and a small business guy who’s tired of watching regular people get crushed while the powerful keep cashing in.
These are the fights that matter to me—and to the people of Florida’s 16th District.
Working Families
Look, first and foremost, let’s not sugarcoat it: the economy is in the toilet.
And no, it’s not just some Wall Street rollercoaster that you can ignore.
We’re talking real people’s lives: rents are climbing, wages are stuck, insurance costs are blowing up, and property taxes are rising while home values sink.
Tariffs are just gasoline on that fire.
So, the line is simple: the so-called “economic revival” is a mirage.
We need policies that actually lift everyday Floridians up, not just hand gold bars to the billionaires.
Immigration & Crime: Facts Over Fear
Photo: American Immigration Council.
Here’s where we cut through the noise.
Trump’s fantasy of a “migrant invasion” and a “crime wave” is just that—a fantasy.
Crime exists and immigration issues are real, but these are problems we can address without turning America into a North Korea knockoff.
You don’t destroy democracy to fix a problem that requires all of us to fix.
We’re not going to militarize our streets just because someone’s peddling fear.
We solve these issues with smart policies and respect for liberty, not paranoia and propaganda.
Healthcare for all
Let’s get real: Republicans have turned healthcare into a horror show.
They’ve convinced people to vote against their own well-being.
We’re here to say: no more.
Universal healthcare isn’t some scary monster—it’s common sense.
The current patchwork of employer-based or profit-driven plans is a failure.
We need a system that actually covers everyone without bankrupting them.
Period.
Democracy
And, about democracy?
We’ve got to stop this top-down nonsense.
Democracy isn’t a command center.
It’s a ground-level movement.
When you let a handful of people at the top dictate everything, you squash the voices of everyday Americans.
Our campaign stands for a democracy where everyone’s a stakeholder, not just a chosen few.
In America, there’s no office more important than that of citizen.
Photo: PBS.
Common sense over chaos: Abortion & Guns
Let’s cut through the noise: most Americans agree we need common sense on both abortion and guns.
But, Donald Trump and the extremists on the right AND left have turned these issues into political footballs: weaponized for division, distraction, and power grabs.
Nobody wants the government forcing birth.
Nobody wants unstable people walking into schools with AR-15s.
But that’s exactly what we’re getting, because the loudest voices treat every conversation like a culture war instead of a chance to fix what’s broken.
We can protect reproductive freedom and protect public safety.
These aren’t radical ideas—they’re mainstream.
But, under Trump’s influence, the government has made “freedom” mean forcing women into childbirth and flooding our neighborhoods with unregulated guns.
That’s not freedom.
That’s chaos.
And, we’re done pretending it makes sense.
