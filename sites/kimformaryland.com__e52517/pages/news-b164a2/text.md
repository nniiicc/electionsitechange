Kimberly Simmons Robinson Elected to the Republican Central Committee
June 23, 2026
On June 23, 2026, Kimberly Simmons Robinson received the highest number of votes among Republican candidates across all eight districts in Prince George’s County, Maryland.
Through the vote of the people, Kimberly was elected to serve as a member of the Republican Central Committee for a four-year term.
