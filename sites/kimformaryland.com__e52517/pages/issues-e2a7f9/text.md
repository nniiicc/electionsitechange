Meet Kimberly Issues News Volunteer Contribute Some Issues Mental Health I am committed to advancing mental health policies that strengthen access to care, reduce stigma, and ensure that individuals and families can get the support they need when they need it.
Mental health is public health, and I believe our community must prioritize practical solutions that expand services, improve early intervention, and connect people with affordable, high-quality treatment options.
By working collaboratively with providers and community organizations, we can build a system that better supports overall well-being and helps the public live healthier, more stable lives.
The Right To Life I'm committed to protecting the lives of our society’s most vulnerable members, including babies not yet born, their mothers, the elderly, the chronically ill, and people with disabilities.
I will stand against the radical interest groups that push unlimited abortion until birth, funded by taxpayer dollars, and assisted suicide in our state.
The majority of Americans do not believe their hard-earned tax dollars should be used to pay for elective abortions and the majority of parents oppose their minor daughters being taken for secret abortions without their prior knowledge or consent.
I support life-affirming alternatives to abortion, which respect the life and dignity of both mother and child, such as the vital services and material resources provided by Maryland’s pregnancy help centers.
Term Limits On Congress I Signed the Term Limits On Congress Pledge, because we have had decades of the same people, and decades of the same problems.
Term limits make room for problem-solvers with fresh ideas and solutions.
In most industries, we expect competition to keep things fresh and accountable.
With a 13% approval rating and over a 90% reelection rate, Congress has had neither for a long time.
Regularly opening seats restores voter choice and allows more people to run with a real chance of being elected.
This increases the number of qualified candidates that run and increases voter choice.
Term limits don't replace your vote; they make sure it counts again.

See My Pledge Link: https://openbadgefactory.com/obv3/credentials/b29f8f99d8e9cad51e6ab5d55a46e16b5af5c4a6 Principles for Trusted Elections is a cross-partisan initiative led by Team Democracy in collaboration with The Carter Center and The Gerald R.
Ford Presidential Foundation.
The Principles are built around five pillars that help ensure confidence in our elections: 1.
Honest Process — impartial, lawful elections with equal access for all eligible voters.
2.
Nonviolent Campaign — campaigns free from threats, intimidation, and violence.
3.
Secure Voting — every eligible vote counted, with safeguards to ensure integrity.
4.
Responsible Oversight — transparent administration with clear roles and accountability.
5.
Trusted Outcomes — legal dispute resolution and peaceful transfer of power.
