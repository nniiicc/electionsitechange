Maryland Candidate for Delegate in District 23 Kim For Maryland Kimberly Simmons Robinson is a lifelong resident of Prince George’s County, Maryland, where her deep family roots have shaped her unwavering commitment to community service.
With more than 40 years of experience working alongside local leaders and nonprofit organizations, she has dedicated her life to strengthening communities throughout the Washington, D.C., Maryland, and Virginia (DMV) region.
Raised in a middle-class family, Kimberly brings a personal understanding of the everyday challenges faced by working families.
Her lived experiences continue to inform her approach to leadership and her passion for advocating on behalf of others.
Her platform is centered on common-sense solutions—promoting safer communities, protecting citizens, and creating opportunities for families, individuals, and businesses to thrive.
She is committed to lowering taxes and fees to make Maryland a more affordable place to live, while advancing practical, bipartisan policies that address the real needs of residents.
Kimberly brings a diverse professional background spanning federal government service, private industry, and entrepreneurship.
Known for her strong organizational skills and ability to execute effectively, she approaches every endeavor with focus and determination.
At her core, she is both a teacher and a lifelong learner, continuously seeking knowledge and growth.
As a devoted mother of three grown children, Kimberly values family above all, second only to her faith in Christ.
She homeschooled her two younger children during key seasons of their education and continues to guide all three in practical knowledge, life skills, and wisdom.
She finds joy in her family and remains deeply grateful for life’s many blessings.
