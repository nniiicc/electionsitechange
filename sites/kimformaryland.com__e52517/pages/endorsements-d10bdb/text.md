Arthur Simmons WMATA Station Manager I am writing to offer my personal endorsement of Kimberly Simmons Robinson for Maryland State Delegate.
I support her because she is focused on the issues that directly affect our day-to-day lives in the community and in the state of Maryland as a whole.
She prioritizes public safety, which is foundational for strong neighborhoods and thriving families.
She also understands the importance of keeping taxes and fees low, while still ensuring that essential services are funded responsibly.
I appreciate her commitment to creating real opportunities for individuals, families, and local businesses.
That includes fixing the educational system so students are better prepared for the future, paying attention to environmental concerns in a practical and sustainable way, and holding government accountable for how it spends taxpayer dollars.
Just as important to me is her emphasis on disciplined budgeting—making sure that state spending is thoughtful, transparent, and focused on results rather than waste.
That kind of approach is what builds trust between elected officials and the communities they serve.
For these reasons, I am proud to support Kimberly Simmons Robinson for State Delegate and encourage others to support her as well.
