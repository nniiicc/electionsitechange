PROVEN RECORD
Real Priorities, Real Results
The Kansas Secretary of State has two primary duties – securing and accurately executing our elections, and being the key agency to facilitate establishing and operating businesses in Kansas.
As a State Representative and Chair of the House Elections Committee, I have championed and enacted into law strong election safeguards: ensuring only US citizens vote, restoring election day as the deadline for casting ballots, and strengthening paper audits of our elections.
My economic record has prioritized enacting reforms to recruit new jobs and businesses to Kansas, reduce government regulation, and reduce income, property and sales taxes for all Kansans.
As a business owner, I have interacted with the Secretary of State office and have experienced what works and what needs improvement to keep Kansas open for business.
