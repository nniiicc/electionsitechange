Child Advocacy Center of Lapeer County Most people don’t know this exists until someone they know needs it.
The Child Advocacy Center of Lapeer brings law enforcement, Child Protective Services, prosecutors, and medical professionals together so a child only has to go through one interview, in one safe place, with one trained advocate, and then can immediately get the help they need.
For families going through the process, they also provide food, toiletries, diapers, and other essentials at no cost.
For the broader community, they offer free car seats to qualifying families, certified car seat training, and free babysitter courses open to anyone who wants to learn.
Visit caclapeer.org → Rx Kids Rx Kids serves mothers in communities across Michigan, including Bridgeport Township, the City of Saginaw, and Buena Vista Township.
In those three communities, moms of babies born on or after January 1, 2026, get $1,500 during pregnancy and $500 a month for the first six months after birth, with no strings attached.
The idea is simple: financial stress during those early months is one of the biggest risk factors for poor outcomes for both mother and child, and a direct cash investment removes that stress in a way that no single service or program can replicate.
The money goes directly to the family to use how they need it most, whether that’s rent, groceries, transportation, or something else entirely.
Programs like this are how communities actually invest in their own future, and I’m glad to see it taking root here.
Visit rxkids.org → Mid-Michigan Veterans Treatment Court Also known as the Saginaw County Veterans Treatment Court When a veteran ends up in legal trouble, this program offers a different path than the standard court process.
Participants follow a structured treatment plan under the court’s supervision, paired with a fellow veteran who serves as a mentor.
The goal is to address what led to the legal trouble in the first place, whether that’s substance use, mental health, or readjustment struggles, and connect veterans with the resources to stay on the right path long term.
They don’t have a website, but they’re real people doing real work, and they’re worth knowing about if you or a veteran in your life could use this kind of help.
