Crane A1 Map, Michigan Independent Citizens Redistricting Commission District 26 covers a wide stretch of mid-Michigan, mostly rural and small-town, with some suburban edges near Flint and Saginaw.
It’s currently represented by Republican Sen.
Kevin Daley, who is term-limited in 2026.
That makes this an open seat.
The commission that drew these lines exists because Michigan voters approved it in 2018, and I got to help put it on the ballot as a Voters Not Politicians volunteer.
Am I in District 26?
If you’re wondering whether you live in District 26, the easiest check is the town list below.
If your township, village, or city is listed, you’re in the district.
You can also look up your address at the Michigan Voter Information Center , which shows your registration, your districts, your polling place, and your sample ballot.
Absentee ballots go out starting September 24, statewide early in-person voting runs October 24 through November 1, and Election Day is November 3.
You can register online or by mail through October 19, and in person at your city or township clerk’s office with proof of residency through 8 p.m. on Election Day.
