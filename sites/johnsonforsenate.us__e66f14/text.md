Why I’m Running
I grew up on my family’s old dairy farm in Fostoria and I’ve worked as a grocery clerk, IT technician, construction laborer, school photographer, and licensed chauffeur.
Today I work full-time in IT, and I’m running for State Senate to do what’s best for everybody in this district.
At just about every job I’ve had, I was told to slow down, not because I wasn’t doing good work, but because there wasn’t enough time to train me on everything I was willing to take on.
When I looked at what this district actually needs from its senator, it felt like I’d finally found the right place for that energy.
I want to be the kind of senator you can actually call, no matter how you voted, when you’re getting the runaround from the state on a permit, a license, or even a question no one wants to answer, and I’d never send you off to somebody from your own party instead.
That’s what constituent service means to me, and it’s at the heart of everything I’m running for.
My Priorities
Constituent-Focused Service
Your senator’s office should be a resource you can rely on.
I’ll make it my job to help residents cut through state bureaucracy and get real answers from Lansing.
Affordability: Car Insurance & Healthcare
Michiganders deserve relief from some of the highest car insurance rates in the nation and rising healthcare costs.
I’ll fight to protect the Healthy Michigan Plan and push for real car insurance reform.
Public School Funding Reform
The state ties roughly 90% of a district’s per-student funding to a single count day in October.
I’ll work to fix how Michigan funds public education so teachers can focus on teaching, not attendance gimmicks.
Roads & Infrastructure
Our roads are an everyday reality for every driver in District 26.
I’ll push for infrastructure investments that fix our roads and make our communities more connected and more attractive to employers.
Jobs & Workforce Development
District 26 needs more local employers offering full-time positions with good benefits.
I’ll support workforce training and retraining programs that connect residents with in-demand skills.
Listening Tour
I’m on a listening tour across the townships, cities, and villages of District 26, going to local government meetings to find out what people are actually dealing with and what they want changed.
