Emboldening the Working Class & Reclaiming Rural Power
My platform is a simple one that’s focused on putting money in the pockets of working families and retirees — not the ultra wealthy or monopoly-like corporations.
I want to ensure our communities are safe places to live, work and play.
We need a representative that works for us here in West Central Wisconsin.
Here are my solutions to some of the greatest problems I’ve been hearing from you on the campaign trail:
- Cap out-of-pocket costs on essential drugs like insulin, asthma inhalers, and cancer medications at $30/month.
- Expand Medicare to cover vision, dental, and hearing.
- Reverse Medicaid and Affordable Care Act cuts and fund rural hospitals.
- Take on price-gouging corporations to reduce everyday costs.
- End the costly Iran War, which is costing taxpayers $2 billion per day and driving gas and diesel prices through the roof.
- Protect our communities from unchecked hyperscale data centers that raise utility rates by moratorium on new development and ending tax breaks.
- End surveillance pricing to stop companies from using personal data to charge people more than their neighbors.
- Protect our data privacy and prevent big corporations from profiting from our personal information.
