His childhood taught him the importance of hard work, responsibility, and faith.
Jonathan understands that Arizona families are paying the price for an economy and government that put billionaires ahead of working people.
We Arizonans deserve representation that will go to battle for us – not just the wealthy and well-connected.
The Warrior Up Agenda puts Arizona families first To honor the Navajo culture, the Warrior Up Agenda is based around four pillars.
Traditional Navajo teachings use the number four as a framework that organizes our philosophical and concrete worlds.
The number four represents balance and harmony.
