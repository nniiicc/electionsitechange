The blunder has drawn national attention, highlighting Nez’s long history of working with both parties to get things done and exposing the lengths Crane’s billionaire backers are willing to go.
“Eli Crane and his billionaire backers are so scared of an Indigenous sheepherder from Shonto, Arizona, they rushed attack ads against me.
But they care so little about the voters of AZ-02 they couldn’t get it right.
The voters of AZ-02 are tired of their broken promises and disregard for the real challenges they are facing.
I guess they don’t care to know my face, but on November 3rd they’ll hear my voice,” said Nez .
