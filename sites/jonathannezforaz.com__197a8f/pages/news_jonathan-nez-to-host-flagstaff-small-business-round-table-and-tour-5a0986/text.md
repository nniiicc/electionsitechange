FLAGSTAFF, AZ — Jonathan Nez, candidate for Arizona’s 2nd Congressional District, will host a small business roundtable and tour local businesses in Flagstaff to hear directly from business owners about the challenges and opportunities facing Northern Arizona’s economy.
The event will bring together a diverse group of small business owners to discuss key issues, including affordability, workforce challenges, access to capital, and strengthening local supply chains.
Following the roundtable, Jonathan Nez will visit several small businesses in the Flagstaff area to highlight their contributions to the local economy.
