A NEW VOICE FOR RURAL ARIZONA Jonathan Nez was born in Northern Arizona and grew up on dirt floors without electricity or running water.
He’s dedicated his career to improving the lives of hardworking families like his, not playing politics.
As President of the Navajo Nation, he worked with the Trump administration to expand water rights for rural Arizona and broke with his own party to create local jobs and lower energy costs.
