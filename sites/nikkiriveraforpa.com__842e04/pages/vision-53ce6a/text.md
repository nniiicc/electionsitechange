NOW Strong Public Education for Every Child Every child in Pennsylvania deserves access to a high-quality, fairly funded public education.
Nikki supports academic rigor across all school districts, diverse extracurricular opportunities, and learning environments that promote strong academic, social, and emotional outcomes.
Nikki advocates for responsible cyber charter tuition reform to ensure taxpayer dollars are used effectively and not spent on duplicative services.
Economic Opportunity & Affordability Hard work should provide stability.
Nikki believes in good-paying jobs that reflect a fair day’s work and wages that keep pace with the cost of living.
Families should not have to spend more than 30% of their income on housing, and safe, affordable housing must be accessible and available to all.
She is committed to keeping utilities dependable and reasonably priced while ensuring our communities have the infrastructure needed as energy demands grow.
Safe, Inclusive & Sustainable Communities Everyone—residents, immigrants, and visitors alike—deserves to feel safe in our communities.
Nikki supports safe streets for walking, biking, and driving, as well as efficient and reliable public transportation.
She promotes fostering inclusive environments free from discrimination and protecting clean air, water, and soil for future generations.
Nikki supports our local law enforcement and emergency responders in our community, and has profound respect for those who put others’ safety and wellbeing before their own.
Accountable & Forward-Thinking Leadership Our communities deserve model leadership rooted in transparency, responsibility, and long-term planning.
Nikki is committed to thoughtful decision-making that prioritizes people, sustainability, and shared prosperity.
When the most vulnerable among us are well cared for, we are all healthier and stronger as a community.
