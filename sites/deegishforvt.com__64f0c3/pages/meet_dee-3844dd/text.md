Meet Dee
My family and I moved to Sharon in 2008 after my husband's retirement from the US Navy.
We are deeply involved in our community.
My professional experience here in Vermont includes budget and audit preparation, accounting, grants management and administration for regional planning commissions and nonprofits.
I hold a Master's degree in Business Administration from Western Washington University and a Master's of Environmental Law and Policy from the Vermont Law and Graduate school.
I currently serve and volunteer with the:
- Sharon Planning Commission
- Sharon Community Trust
- Sharon Energy Committee
- Sharon Opioid Settlement Action Committee
- Sharon Food Shelf
- Baxter Library Board
- Sharon Sprouty - run/walk
My family is similarly community motivated.
My husband serves on the Sharon Selectboard, the Ashley Community Forest, and Vermont Coverts, my daughter is a special educator and my son is currently farming and environmental organizing.
My vision for the future of Vermont is a state where the natural beauty, habitat and ecosystems are cherished and safeguarded, where everyone is respected and valued and can live proudly without fear or prejudice.
A state where everyone has access to
- safe, affordable housing . healthy, local food
- universal health care . quality education
- secure employment . civil rights and due process
I will work hard to tackle the affordability challenges of increasing property taxes and education funding and will always advocate for voting and reproductive rights while making sure impacted communities are heard, treated with respect and dignity, and have a seat at the table.
