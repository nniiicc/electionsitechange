ABOUT ME
Putting My Experience
to Work
I’m Tom Fisher, a Guam attorney and sole practitioner at Fisher & Associates, P.C.
My career began in public service as a U.S.
Navy Judge Advocate (JAG) in Guam and Hawai‘i, followed by work at the Office of the Attorney General of Guam prosecuting serious violent crimes—eventually serving as first assistant to the chief prosecutor.
After that, I moved into private practice representing the indigent, the accused, and the elderly.
I also served as a senator in the 37th Guam Legislature (2023–2025).
I’m running for Attorney General of Guam in 2026 to put real courtroom and public-service experience to work restoring trust and delivering fair, effective justice.
The Fisher Four
A Justice System Built for Everyone.
The Law is Blind
- Zero tolerance for racism in the justice system.
- Everyone pays their fair share of taxes to strengthen public services.
- No “pari-pari” system — no special treatment for insiders.
- The law is enforced equally for every person, every time.
Enforce the Law
- Strict enforcement of procurement laws and legal processes.
- Legal action against government agencies when laws are broken.
- Holding public offices and officials accountable.
- Restoring trust through transparent, lawful government.
Efficient
Prosecution
- Effective prosecution that follows cases all the way through.
- Ending excessive dismissals, delays, and wasted taxpayer dollars.
- Building cases correctly before filing charges.
- Addressing over 1,000 dismissed cases that left victims without closure.
- Hiring and training more local attorneys.
- Ending constant reassignment of cases to multiple prosecutors.
- Taking responsibility within the Attorney General's Office.
Power to
Protect
- No child left behind in the justice system.
- Strong advocacy for families in need.
- Strengthening the role of Assistant District Attorneys.
- Prioritizing victims, public safety, and human dignity.
