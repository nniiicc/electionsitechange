Cindy’s Achievements & Values
Key Legislative Achievements
Worked with legislators and non-profit organizations, statewide, to combat the foster care crisis and provide services and resources to help with family reunification.
Working for lower taxes, with four tax cuts passed in the last two sessions resulting in nearly half a billion dollars in savings for tax payers in the first year.
Supported free school breakfast for students statewide, regardless of income.
Fought for the right of Arkansans to keep and bear arms.
Co-Sponsored the bill creating Arkansas Children and Teens Online Privacy Protection Act.
Supported legislation that prohibited PBM’s (Pharmacy Benefit Managers) from obtaining certain pharmacy permits in the state of Arkansas, which results in lower costs of prescription drugs for Arkansans.
Fought to strengthen laws protecting children from sexually explicit material to include computer generated materials.
Fought to increase support for victims of human tracking and to create tougher laws on those convicted of trafficking of a person and/or grooming of a minor.
