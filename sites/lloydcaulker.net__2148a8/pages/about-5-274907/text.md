Bio I am Lloyd Caulker, a candidate for House District 15 in Jacksonville, Florida, and the Founder and Senior Pastor of Gospeler Ministries.
With a rich academic background in Business Administration and Information Technology, I bring an interdisciplinary approach to addressing the unique challenges of our community.
My passion for community service, social justice, and fostering unity has been the cornerstone of my journey, guiding my commitment to improve educational opportunities, economic development, social equity, and healthcare access in our district.
I believe in the power of our community to create a brighter future through shared values and collective action, and I am dedicated to being a voice for change and progress in Jacksonville.
My Story I am Lloyd Caulker, a narrative that unfolds from my beginnings in Freetown Sierra Leone, West Africa, to my academic pursuits in the United States, where I earned an Associate of Science (A.S.) in Information Technology from Florida State College at Jacksonville and a Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) from the University of North Florida.
These achievements laid the foundation for a career distinguished by a fervent desire to apply technology for societal benefit.
My professional journey is marked by diverse roles, from Open Systems Technician III at Black Knight Financial Services to Client Success Manager at I-Tech Resources Inc.
These positions have allowed me to develop a unique skill set and broad perspective, fueling my relentless drive for excellence.
In the heart of my community leadership is my role as the Founder and Senior Pastor of Gospeler Ministries.
This ministry is built on principles of compassion, service, and faith, serving as a beacon of hope and support for those seeking spiritual guidance and community connection.
Gospeler Ministries is envisioned not just as a religious institution, but as a dynamic hub of empowerment, where individuals are encouraged to embrace authenticity, engage in service, and cultivate a profound sense of purpose and belonging.
Under my leadership, Gospeler Ministries welcomes everyone with open arms, offering a nurturing environment for exploring faith, facing life’s challenges, and finding strength in God through teachings, mentorship, and pastoral care.
My commitment to community engagement extends beyond the walls of Gospeler Ministries.
I am actively involved in missionary work and outreach initiatives aimed at uplifting marginalized communities around Jacksonville, addressing social injustices, and alleviating suffering.
Whether through organizing food drives, volunteering at homeless shelters, or leading educational initiatives, I embody a spirit of service and selflessness, inspiring others to join me in making a real difference in the lives of those in need.
My journey is a testament to the impact of combining passion, expertise, and vision to drive meaningful change.
From advocating for sustainable energy and technology to leading in community and spiritual engagement, I have cultivated a multifaceted approach to leadership that serves as an inspiration to those aspiring to effect positive change.
As we face the challenges of the 21st century, I am reminded that dedication, innovation, and compassion are key to building a brighter, more sustainable future for the generations that follow.
This is why I am running for Florida House of Representative District 15.
My diverse experiences and deep commitment to service and community empowerment have prepared me to represent and advocate for the needs and aspirations of our community.
I am dedicated to bringing about meaningful change and ensuring a prosperous, equitable future for all residents of District 15.
