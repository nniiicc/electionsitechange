Legislative Priorities in Action
Representative Hamilton Grant is delivering on the promises he made to District 79—championing legislation that addresses South Carolina’s most urgent challenges.
From affordable housing to public education and workforce development, his work in the State House reflects his deep commitment to building a better future for all South Carolinians.
Improving Our State’s Housing Crisis
- Religious Institutions Affordable Housing Act (H.3458)
- Allows churches and faith-based organizations to build affordable housing while keeping their tax-exempt status.
(In Committee)
- Housing Discrimination Protections (H.3336)
- Prevents discrimination in housing based on disability and source of income.
(In Committee)
- South Carolina Mentoring Day (H.3345)
- Designates January 20 as “South Carolina Mentoring Day” to recognize the role of mentorship.
(In Committee)
Building a Sustainable South Carolina Workforce
- Small Business Construction Impact Relief Act (H.4467)
- Creates a state-run loan program to help small businesses offset construction-related disruptions.
(In Committee)
- Educator Assistance Act (H.3196)
- Enhances teacher contracts, planning time, professional development, and leave policies.
(Signed Into Law)
- Workforce Readiness & FAFSA Access (H.3197)
- Establishes remediation programs, requires FAFSA completion before graduation, and improves access to career data.
(In Committee)
- Doula Access & Reimbursement (H.3108)
- Requires Medicaid and private insurance to cover doula services.
Establishes a statewide doula registry and certification process.
(In Committee)
Protecting the Integrity of Public Education
- Universal Head Start and Early Head Start Act (H.3573)
- Expands early childhood education access statewide through Head Start programs.
(In Committee)
- Teen Dating Violence Prevention (H.3036 & H.3317)
- Requires schools to educate students about teen dating violence and implement related policies.
(In Committee)
- Racial Disparity in Academic Testing Reports (H.3206)
- Requires schools to post public reports showing racial disparities in standardized test scores.
(In Committee)
- Panic Alert Systems & Weapons Detection in Schools (H.3258 & H.3237)
- Improves school safety through required mobile alert systems and studies on weapons detection.
