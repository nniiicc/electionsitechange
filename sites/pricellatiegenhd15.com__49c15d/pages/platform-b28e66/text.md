KEY ISSUES Life I believe every life has value, and I will work to defend the unborn while also supporting policies that honor life at every stage.
Strengthen Support for Mothers and Families Pass legislation that increases access to prenatal care, parenting support, and adoption services.
Women facing unplanned pregnancies will have more resources, reducing the pressure to consider abortion and supporting life in early stages.
Promote Life-Affirming Education Programs Fund educational programs in schools and communities that teach about the value of life, alternatives to abortion, and support for families.
Encourages a cultural respect for life while giving practical alternatives for expecting parents.
2nd Amendment The right to bear arms is what protects every other freedom we enjoy.
I will fight to make sure it is never taken away.
Legislative Protection of Gun Rights Introduce bills that prevent state or federal overreach on legal gun ownership.
Ensures responsible citizens retain their right to own firearms.
Promote Responsible Gun Ownership Programs Focus on community programs for safe gun handling, storage, and training.
Reduces accidents and crime while maintaining constitutional rights.
Parenthood & Childhood Parents should have the freedom to raise their children according to their values, without government interference or harmful social agendas.
Childhood should be protected, not politicized.
Parental Choice in Education Support legislation that allows parents to choose schools, homeschool options, and curriculum transparency.
Parents gain control over their child’s education, avoiding unwanted political or ideological influence.
Strengthen Protections Against Harmful Content for Children Implement laws that restrict exposure to inappropriate media or social agendas for minors in schools and public programs.
Ensures children grow up in safe environments aligned with family values.
Economy/Expanding Opportunity The next generation deserves more than broken promises from government.
We need stronger trade schools, support for small business, and more pathways to success that don’t require crushing debt. ​ Expand Trade & Vocational Schools Increase funding for technical training, apprenticeships, and vocational education programs.
Provides alternative career paths with lower debt and strong earning potential.
Support Small Businesses and Entrepreneurship Reduce bureaucratic hurdles and provide tax incentives for small business owners.
Encourages local economic growth, job creation, and financial independence.
Safety & Security Strong community means secure neighborhoods, supporting self-defense rights and law enforcement, and real solutions for border security.
Safety is the foundation of freedom.
Strengthen Law Enforcement & Community Programs Increase funding for police training, community policing, and neighborhood watch initiatives.
Improves unity between law enforcement, crime prevention, and trust between citizens and law enforcement.
