01 Fight the Green Energy Agenda Rick supports local control over industrial wind and solar siting so communities, homes, schools, and property values are protected.
02 Ban Biosolid Waste on Farmland He supports safeguarding Oklahoma food, water, and farmland from toxic sludge and other harmful agricultural waste practices.
03 Stop Hostile Foreign Land Ownership Rick backs stronger protections to keep Oklahoma land out of the hands of hostile foreign interests and defend long-term state sovereignty.

HEAVENER, OK - State Representative Rick West has officially filed for re-election, announcing his campaign to return to the Oklahoma House and continue his work defending the people of House District 3 against government overreach, corporate influence, and special-interest politics.
West, a cattleman from LeFlore County, has built a reputation as one of the Legislature’s most conservative voices and as a lawmaker free of outside influence, refusing lobbyist gifts or contributions and sponsoring legislation driven directly by constituents, not outside interests.
“I didn’t go to Oklahoma City to make friends with lobbyists or carry water for state agencies.
I went there to fight for the people back home, and that’s exactly what I intend to keep doing.” Rep.
Rick West During his time in office, West has taken on some of the most powerful forces at the Capitol, including: Fighting utility rate hikes and demanding accountability from regulators Opposing corporate immunity schemes that would shield chemical companies from liability Stopping government overreach that threatened Oklahoma hunters’ rights Standing against lab-grown meat initiatives that endanger Oklahoma’s agricultural economy Supporting policies that protect property rights, reduce government waste, and defend rural communities West’s record reflects a consistent commitment to conservative principles.
He earned one of the highest scores in the Legislature on Oklahoma’s Conservative Index and maintains a lifetime average of 97%, placing him among the most reliable defenders of limited government and individual liberty.
“Serving in the Legislature is a trust, and that trust belongs to the people, not lobbyists, not bureaucrats, and not special interests.” Rep.
Rick West As part of his re-election effort, West is expanding his outreach to voters through his official campaign website, where constituents can stay informed, share ideas, and get involved in the campaign.
“I want to hear directly from the people I represent.
The best ideas don’t come from the Capitol.
They come from the folks back home.” Rep.
Rick West Voters are encouraged to visit RickWestForDistrict3.com to learn more about West’s work and priorities and to support the campaign effort.
West emphasized that his campaign will remain focused on the same principles that have guided his service from the beginning: faith, family, freedom, and a government that answers to the people.
“My loyalty is not for sale.
It remains right here at home with the people I represent, and that’s not going to change.” Rep.
