West, a cattleman from LeFlore County, has built a reputation as one of the Legislature’s most conservative voices and as a lawmaker free of outside influence, refusing lobbyist gifts or contributions and sponsoring legislation driven directly by constituents, not outside interests.
“I didn’t go to Oklahoma City to make friends with lobbyists or carry water for state agencies.
