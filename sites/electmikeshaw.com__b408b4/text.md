Affordability
Lowering everyday costs
Mike Shaw is focused on practical, conservative tax relief and workforce solutions that help families keep more of what they earn.
- Created a 60-day state grocery tax holiday through his amendment to HB527.
- Introduced HB647 to phase out the remaining state grocery tax on food and finish the job permanently.
- Supported overtime tax relief as a separate way to reward work and put money back in family budgets.
- Helped pass the Central Alabama Homebuilding Academy (CAHA) framework to train skilled tradespeople and address rising home construction, repair, and maintenance costs.
Too often in Montgomery we worry how government will figure out how to make ends meet, but families have no choice but to figure it out - at the grocery store, when the tax bill is due, and when we buy or maintain a home.
Affordability ought to be viewed from the perspective of the people.
