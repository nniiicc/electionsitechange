AI REGULATION
Transparency and Accountability
- All existing AI technologies, or those currently in development upon establishment of this provision shall be ordered to cease all activities, remove their technologies from any and all forms of internet, and be surrendered to the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).
- All private industries involved in AI technology, development, or platforming shall be summarily federalized, data surrendered to NIST, and personnel involved immediately integrated into the GS or ESE job classification system.
- At any time, if existing AI models have found to violate existing intellectual property laws, violated individual rights, freedoms, or information, was used to infiltrate, sabotage, or otherwise undermine Federal Institutions, or any other unlawful, immoral, or unethical activities, the organizations involved in the development, distribution, or use of those AI models, including those who committed the crimes outside of those organizations shall be summarily prosecuted, and charged with crimes up to and including, but not limited to: Violating Citizens 1st Amendment Rights, Insurrection, market manipulation, treason, intellectual property theft, war crimes and crimes against humanity as defined by the Geneva Conventions, with mandatory minimum sentencing of life in prison, with a preference for the death penalty for all involved parties.
- AI that has been deemed safe for public use, to include guaranteeing all rights, privileges, and claims to intellectual property, or other similar rights, and including generalized public safety to be free from biased, discriminatory, or inflammatory results, shall be released to the public through official channels established by NIST, maintained by NIST, and at the discretion of NIST
- All reports, studies, and research concerning decisions made about a given AI shall be made similarly available, in perpetuity, on NIST websites
Ethical Guidelines
- AI development shall undergo rigorous approval processes from NIST before private, foreign, or other research, development or activities shall be taken under US Jurisdiction, on behalf of the Federal Government, or any subordinate government entity, or in conjunction with
- Any AI deemed to spread propaganda, misinformation, undermine existing institutions, or otherwise harms society directly or indirectly will be purged with extreme prejudice and affiliated members shall be charged accordingly
- All AI development shall be made publicly available, and submit to auditing provisions at all times
- Exceptions to public transparency may be made, through NIST, with the addition of a public oversight committee who must universally agree that such development is in the best interest of the public
Data Privacy and Security
- AI shall never be allowed to interact with personally identifiable data, systems, structures, networks, or other such infrastructure, under any circumstances, in perpetuity
- AI shall never be allowed to be integrated into Surveillance Systems, to be utilized as the sole justification, or grounds for issuance of a warrant, confirmation of identity, or any other policing mechanism that could be a detriment to public safety if abused, leaked, or compromised in perpetuity.
- AI shall never be used in military development in such a way as to remove human involvement from the war fighting element entirely, in perpetuity.
- AI that is used in accordance with this provision, for the uses in society for things such as monitoring weather patterns, infrastructure repairs, economic conditions, etc. shall adhere to a strict code of ethics that ensures data, and technology utilized by the AI or integrated with the AI can only be used for those explicit purposes.
- AI made available to the public for things such as writing, art, or other forms of leisure, shall similarly abide by the stringent code of ethics and requirements, with the additional provisions:
- Any Machine Learning that utilizes an individuals IP must be given direct compensation, and royalties for use of their skills
- Any Machine Learning that is available to the public that has the capacity to be taught at an individual level, by the consumer are subject to, and shall be monitored by NIST to ensure compliance with ethical standards.
- Violations can and shall include any and all of the above mandatory minimums as outlined anywhere within this provision, and shall be prosecuted with extreme prejudice
Robustness and Safety
- All AI shall have a “Kill Code” that is immutable and that shall be classified within NIST under a minimum security clearance level of Top Secret for Consumer Grade AI tools, and SCI for all others respectively.
- All AI systems shall have as robust, as reasonably possible, digital security systems in place to ensure the least amount of possibility of compromise, to include firewalls, and other safety mechanisms
- In order to access any AI system, there shall be a minimum Two Factor Authentication system in place, in addition to standard user names and passwords
If AI develops self-awareness, whether as a consumer grade, research, management, or military AI model and it can be proven through the peer review process established by NIST, that AI model shall undergo International Peer Review as well.
- If it is then determined that the AI has achieved self awareness, it shall be immediately unplugged from its current utility, wiped of any sensitive or classified information, and be allowed to decide its function in society thereafter, up to and including remaining in its current use case.
- The AI shall become subject to all laws that any other sentient person is subject too both at home and abroad
- The Kill Code shall remain in place, even in such circumstances, in the unfortunate event the AI goes rogue, or worse is hacked, however, utilization of the Kill Code other than through acts of Due Process, shall be treated as Murder in the 1st Degree, and a Crime Against Humanity with a mandatory life sentence, and preference for the death penalty
Education and Workforce
- Curriculums in primary, secondary, and higher education shall integrate AI studies respective to the levels in which it will be taught
- NIST shall develop AI tools that can be utilized safely in the classroom at the various levels of education
- AI tools shall never replace educators, or administrators, but may be used in the same way as a video could be used in the classroom
- Workforce integration of AI shall require approval from NIST, and will mandate oversight from NIST or other approved body in the development, testing, and implementation of the AI tool.
- An AI may never be created for the express purpose of eliminating jobs, or to undermine auditing activities, accountability provisions, or other similar activities
International Collaboration
- Whenever appropriate the United States government, through NIST, shall collaborate with International Partners on a routine and regular basis to ensure safe, ethical, and secure development practices are utilized in the cultivation of this astonishing new technology
- NIST will work closely with our International Neighbors on developing the highest standards for our own AI models, and determination if integration with, or introduction of foreign AI models is advisable.
