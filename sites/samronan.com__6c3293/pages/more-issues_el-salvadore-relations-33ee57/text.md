EL SALVADOR RELATIONS
Diplomatic Relations
- The issuance of an ultimatum, to culminate within 30 calendar days, to return, unmolested, unharmed, and without delay every single detainee that was sold, deported to, stolen to, or otherwise the result of the previous administrations actions that lead to the arrival of detainees from US lands to El Salvador, or face the full wrath and fury of the United States Armed Forces
- The United States will further submit a suit before the International Criminal Courts (ICC) to demand that El Salvador shut down its inhumane prison systems, and face punishments for human rights violations.
- Once these conditions have been resolved peaceful relations may resume with El Salvador
Trade Relations
- Trade will cease until such time as diplomatic relations return to normal
Military relationship
- Failure to abide by the ultimatum or the ICC shall result in military intervention with extreme prejudice to facilitate the diplomatic goals of the United States of America, with the primary focus on liberation of wrongfully deported, detained, or otherwise captive personnel as the result of the previous administration’s heinous and barbarous actions
- If other nations, or the UN should take Umbridge to these activities, then they to shall face diplomatic, economic, or military consequences.
It is the policy of the United States of America to leave “No one behind”.
Cultural Relations and Strengthening
- Cultural relations and strengthening shall resume upon amicable resolution of any diplomatic or military conflicts
