CHILD CRIMES ENFORCEMENT
Child Crimes Enforcement and Prevention
Prevention
- The most important facet of Child Crimes Enforcement or reforms to existing policies, is to ensure these crimes never happen in the first place, therefore the Department of Justice (DOJ) shall establish, or reconcile any existing departments within their agency, to be reorganized under the Child Crimes Prevention Department (CCPD)
- The DOJ shall submit a report to the Department of the Treasury (DOT), Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS), and the Department of Labor respectively, outlining the material conditions that lead to the highest likelihood of child crimes
- The DOJ shall coordinate with the DHHS with regards to any prevailing medical conditions, or circumstances that could lead to Child Crimes and utilize that information in the CCPD.
The DOJ shall partner with HHS to establish the following support systems:
- Universal Childcare
- Federalization of Foster Care
- Federalization of Adoption
- Federalization of Invitro Fertilization
- Federalization of Child Services
The DOJ will further coordinate with any and all appropriate agencies to develop public training and awareness with regards to available child care support systems in place, or in development, and financial assistance that may be available
- Public education requirements will teach age-appropriate methods of understanding dangerous situations, and unhealthy living conditions, whereby children can report adults without risk to themselves at home.
- Teen pregnancy will be a circumstance that society will have to contend with for as long as humanities need to sexually reproduce, therefore, improving education, support, and access to contraceptives to experimenting youth will be paramount in preventing untimely pregnancies, or complications
- The DOJ and NIST shall coordinate methods to prevent exploitation of children and minors on social media, the internet in general, and mitigate interaction with possible predators, or predatory activities
- Among these actions will include eliminating any similarity to gambling in applications, activities, or other interactives that are directly or indirectly geared towards children
- Additionally, any actions, activities, algorithms, or designs qualities that can, or are known to create addiction or similar responses to them shall be summarily banned from all products, services, and other means, generally not just those that target children and adolescents.
Prosecution and Enforcement
- Child Crimes are considered especially malevolent in society, and justifiably so, wherefore, in accordance with this provision, all child crimes, or crimes committed against children, that receive a conviction by a jury of peers, shall constitute a minimum sentence of life in prison, with a preference for the death penalty
- The Epstein Flight Logs, suicide of Epstein, and the Conviction of G.
Maxwell all coincide with the legal requirement of preponderance of the evidence, and the DOJ, through the CCPD shall prosecute everyoneon that list, associated with the people on that list, and are otherwise found to be directly or indirectly involved in the activities that lead to being on that list and seek the Death Penalty in all cases
- Additionally, human trafficking of children, judges, and agencies that authorized, conducted, and facilitated the deportation of unaccompanied minors, criminal proceedings of minors without legal counsel, or similar heinous abuses of the rights of minors, shall be similarly charged, prosecuted and sentenced to Death whenever possible.
- Preponderance of the Evidence shall be the minimum standard within which it is necessary to justifiably convict someone charged of a crime against children.
