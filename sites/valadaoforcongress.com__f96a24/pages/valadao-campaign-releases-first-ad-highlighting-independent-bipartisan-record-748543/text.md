VALADAO CAMPAIGN RELEASES FIRST AD HIGHLIGHTING INDEPENDENT, BIPARTISAN RECORD
Hanford, CA –Today, David Valadao For Congress released its first ad touting David’s long history of bipartisan and independent leadership for the Central Valley.
The ad focuses on David’s work reaching across the aisle working with President Obama to bring water to Valley farmers, as well as standing up to Republican leadership fighting to give DREAMers a pathway to citizenship.
