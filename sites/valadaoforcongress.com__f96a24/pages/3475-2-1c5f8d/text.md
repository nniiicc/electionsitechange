Victor is a Democrat and an immigrant who has crossed party lines to support David because he has seen first-hand the hard work David has put in for the Central Valley to protect DREAMers and bring more water to communities and farmers.
In 2016, voters in the 21st Congressional District supported Democrats at the top of the ticket by a more than 15% margin, but still sent David to Congress because of his independent record of accomplishment.
CLICK HERE TO WATCH THE AD Victor: I’m a Democrat, and I support David Valadao.
David is a person that you can trust, a person that you can talk to.
A person that will listen.
David did do a lot to bring water to the Central Valley.
He stepped in to help out the DREAMers, to help out the immigrants.
Me being an immigrant myself, that meant a lot.
When you see the person’s true values, I think that speaks more than whatever party you’re on.
I support David because of his family values and I believe he’ll bring those values to Congress.
DV : I’m David Valadao and I approve this message ### David G.
Valadao represented the 21st Congressional District from 2013-2019, which includes Kings County and portions of Fresno, Tulare, and Kern Counties.
