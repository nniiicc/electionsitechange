It’s her home.The ad is narrated by Wendi, who lives in the Central Valley and discusses the impact and importance of David’s leadership crossing the aisle, supporting and working on the DREAM Act to provide a pathway to citizenship for the children of undocumented immigrants brought to America.
CLICK HERE TO WATCH THE AD Wendi : My mother brought me here when I was about 3 or 4.
She just wanted a better opportunity for all her kids.
This country is all we’ve ever known.
It’s our home.
For DREAMers, having the right people in office – it means everything.
David Valadao is a champion for DREAMers.
As a son of immigrants himself, he can relate with us.
He cares about others.
He’s kind, generous, hard-working.
He’s been there for us.
David is someone you can trust.
