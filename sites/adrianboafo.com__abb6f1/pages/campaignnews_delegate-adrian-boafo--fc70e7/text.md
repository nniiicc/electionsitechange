DONATE Delegate Adrian Boafo endorsed by Congresswoman Sarah Elfreth Feb 25, 2026 BOWIE, MD – Today Delegate Adrian Boafo received the endorsement of Congresswoman Sarah Elfreth in his candidacy for Maryland’s 5th Congressional District.
“The 5th Congressional District needs a fighter who has a record of representing the District and legislating real solutions.
Having worked with Adrian on behalf of Anne Arundel County since my time in the Maryland Senate, I know his experience and energy is exactly what we need in Congress.
President Trump’s relentless attacks on Maryland and our hardworking federal workers requires a delegation of active, dedicated partners in Washington who are ready to work on day one,” said Congresswoman Sarah Elfreth .
“He’ll build on his legislative success to fight to lower costs, rebuild the federal workforce, and protect Social Security, Medicare, and SNAP.
I’m excited to endorse him as we usher a new generation of leadership in Congress.” “I’m so grateful to have the support of Congresswoman Sarah Elfreth," said Del.
Adrian Boafo .
“She understands the real-world impact policies in Washington have on our communities.
I’ve stood with her to protect immigrant communities, lower costs for Marylanders, and deliver opportunities for our residents at the state and federal levels.
In Congress, we’ll keep up the fight for working families and look forward to helping make life better in Anne Arundel County and for all Marylanders.” This latest endorsement adds to the nearly two dozen endorsements Adrian has earned from leaders throughout the 5th Congressional District, highlighted by Congressman Steny Hoyer’s strong endorsement of Adrian’s campaign.
Adrian is the only candidate in this race to have received the endorsement of current members of the Maryland delegation., The Democratic Primary Election is June 23, 2026.
To learn more about Adrian visit adrianboafo.com .
