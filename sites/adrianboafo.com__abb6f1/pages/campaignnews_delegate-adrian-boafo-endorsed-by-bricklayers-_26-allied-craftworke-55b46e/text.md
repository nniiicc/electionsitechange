Delegate Adrian Boafo Endorsed by Bricklayers & Allied Craftworkers Local 1
Apr 22, 2026
BOWIE, MD – Today Delegate Adrian Boafo received the endorsement of Bricklayers and Allied Craftworkers Local 1 in his candidacy for Maryland’s 5th Congressional District.
“I’m excited to endorse Adrian Boafo, who has been such a strong labor champion,” said President Scott Garvin.
“From sponsoring legislation to crack down on employee misclassifications to establishing strong Project Labor Agreements, I’m excited to endorse Delegate Boafo in his campaign for Congress so we can pass the PRO Act, protect prevailing wages, and strengthen our workforce.”
“It’s an honor to have the backing of BAC Local 1," said Del.
Adrian Boafo.
“With nearly 100,000 members nationwide, this endorsement reflects the movement we're building together.
I look forward to leading the fight for working families in Congress.”
This latest endorsement adds to the nearly two dozen endorsements Adrian has earned from leaders throughout the 5th Congressional District.
The Democratic Primary Election is June 23, 2026.
To learn more about Adrian visit adrianboafo.com.
