DONATE Delegate Adrian Boafo Endorsed by Professional Firefighters of Maryland Apr 27, 2026 BOWIE, MD – Today, Delegate Adrian Boafo received the endorsement of the Professional Firefighters of Maryland in his candidacy for Maryland's 5th Congressional District.
"I'm excited to endorse Adrian Boafo, who has had our back and been a champion for the issues facing our firefighters," said President Jeff Buddle .
"This legislative session, he delivered, helping pass landmark legislation giving our members the right to use medical cannabis off duty to manage chronic pain.
That's what fighting for workers looks like.
I believe we must elect Delegate Boafo to Congress so we can expand the PSOB program, pass the PRO Act, and continue funding research to protect firefighters from exposure to PFAS chemicals." "It's an honor to have the backing of Maryland's firefighters," said Delegate Adrian Boafo .
"With thousands of members, this endorsement reflects the movement we're building together.
I look forward to leading the fight as a champion for labor in Congress." This latest endorsement adds to the more than 30 endorsements Adrian has earned from leaders and unions throughout the 5th Congressional District.
The Democratic Primary Election is June 23, 2026.
To learn more about Adrian visit adrianboafo.com .
