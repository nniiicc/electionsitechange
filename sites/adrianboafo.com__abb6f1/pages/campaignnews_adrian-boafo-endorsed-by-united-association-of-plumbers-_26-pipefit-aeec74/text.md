Adrian Boafo Endorsed by United Association of Plumbers & Pipefitters and International Brotherhood of Electric Workers Local 26
May 7, 2026
"Adrian Boafo understands what's at stake for our union membership and the issues that matter most to working families," said IBEW Business Manager Chris Cash.
"With 13,000 members across the state and the largest trade union presence in the Mid-Atlantic, we are proud to endorse Adrian Boafo for Maryland's 5th Congressional District."
"We are thrilled to endorse Adrian Boafo in his campaign for Congress," said UA Director Jason Ascher.
"He has worked tirelessly to deliver for unions as a Delegate, and in Congress he will protect prevailing wages, fight back against Right-to-Work laws, and support the infrastructure initiatives that power our economy right here in the 5th District."
"It is an honor to have the backing of UA Plumbers and Pipefitters and IBEW Local 26 in my run for Congress," said Delegate Adrian Boafo.
"With thousands of members across the state, these endorsements reflect the momentum of this campaign — driven by unions and working families — and are a testament to our grassroots movement."
These latest endorsements add to the more than 30 endorsements Adrian has earned from community leaders, elected officials and unions throughout the 5th Congressional District.
The Democratic Primary Election is June 23, 2026.
To learn more about Adrian visit adrianboafo.com.
