YOUR REPRESENTATIVE
BRIAN ECHEVARRIA
I am Brian Echevarria, and I am honored to serve as your Republican representative for North Carolina’s 82nd District.
I’m a husband, a father, a Christian, and a proud North Carolinian who believes deeply in the values of faith, family, and freedom that make our state strong.
As a conservative and former GOP County Chairman in Cabarrus County, I’ve always believed that leadership is about character, accountability, and opportunity for everyone.
My experience as a small business owner and community advocate has taught me that when government steps back, our people step up — and North Carolina thrives.
Since being elected, I’ve worked to strengthen our economy, reduce taxes, protect parental rights, and ensure our children receive a world-class education.
I’ve stood firm for our constitutional freedoms and supported legislation that honors our veterans, first responders, and teachers.
I’m running for re-election because I believe our best days are still ahead.
Together, we can continue building a North Carolina where families prosper, communities are safe, and government serves the people — not the other way around.
