Session Success and the Next Generation’s Fight
Our first long session has been a big success.
It has been a pleasure to work with the unique men and women who represent other districts.
We had quite a few bills make it through committees and the House floor.

We passed an amazing budget.
Republican House leadership has been strong and supportive of members.

Please consider donating and volunteering as much as you can.
It will make all the difference to the next generation.
