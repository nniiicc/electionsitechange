Frequently Asked Questions Learn more about Brian Echevarria’s responsibilities and services.
What is Brian Echevarria’s role?
Brian Echevarria serves as a member of the North Carolina House of Representatives, representing District 82.
How can I reach his office?
You can contact Representative Brian Echevarria via email at Brian.Echevarria@ncleg.gov or by phone at (919) 733-2126.
What are his main policy focuses?
Echevarria focuses on economic growth, education, public safety, and property tax issues affecting his neighbors.
What is his approach to community engagement?
Representative Brian Echevarria values feedback and frequently participates in community events to address local concerns.
He insists on representing the people to the government and always being on their side.
