Personally Identifiable Information PII is any information that is “linked” or is “linkable” to a particular individual.
“Linked” means we can identify the individual from the information itself (e.g., their name).
“Linkable” means the information could be linked to a particular individual only if it is combined with other information derived from another source (e.g., a full [as opposed to partial] Internet Protocol address is generally linkable to a computer owned by an account holder who subscribes to a particular internet service provider).
PII may include, but is not limited to, an individual’s name, Social Security number, physical address, email address, Internet Protocol address, phone number, or birth date.
