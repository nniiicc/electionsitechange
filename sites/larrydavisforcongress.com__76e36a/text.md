LARRY'S STORY
A life long Louisianan and middle-class worker, Larry Davis holds deeply rooted Christian conservative beliefs that guide both his personal life and his commitment to public service.
He believes in personal responsibility, respect for life, and the dignity of work, and he understands firsthand the challenges and rewards that come with building a life through perseverance and integrity
Just as America was built on faith in God, so too are Larry’s conservative beliefs.
As a LICENSED MINISTER, Larry leans on prayer and dependency on Christ through every decision and action he takes.
He understands that implementation of biblical morality and careful prudence is paramount when drafting policy that will impact millions, just as our founding fathers believed when drafting the very legislation that would continue to guide our great country today.
