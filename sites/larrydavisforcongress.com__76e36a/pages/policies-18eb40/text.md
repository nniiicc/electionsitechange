CORE POLICIES
01
Economic Growth and Inflation Control
Lowering costs for families and small businesses, reducing federal spending, securing American energy independence, and promoting workforce freedom.
Small business protection, deregulation, and pro-job tax policy.
02
Border Security and National Sovereignty
Strong border enforcement, physical barriers where necessary, crackdown on cartels, mandatory employment verification, and tightening asylum loopholes.
03
Public Safety and Law Enforcement Support
Support police, increase penalties for violent crime, federal support to combat human trafficking, fentanyl enforcement, and restoring order in high crime cities.
04
Protecting Parental Rights and Local Control of Education
Parents as the primary decision makers in children’s education.
Transparency in curriculums.
Prohibiting federal mandates related to ideology and gender policies in schools.
School choice and support for vocational programs.
05
Energy Independence and Reduced Dependence on Foreign Nations
Increase domestic drilling, expand refining capacity, support nuclear advancement, reduce regulatory burden on energy producers.
Lowering costs for the middle class.
06
Fiscal Responsibility and Government Reform
Balanced budget approach.
Ban on unvetted omnibus bills.
Eliminate wasteful federal programs.
Term limits advocacy.
Reforms to emergency powers and agency overreach
07
Constitutional Freedoms and Civil Liberties
First Amendment protection.
Second Amendment support.
Strong restrictions on federal censorship and surveillance programs.
Oppose digital ID systems and central bank digital currency.
08
Military Strength and Defense Readiness
Improve recruitment.
Ensure military spending focuses on readiness, not ideological programs.
Strengthen cybersecurity and missile defense systems.
Support veterans through healthcare streamlining and faster claims.
09
Healthcare Affordability and Medical Freedom
Reduce pharmaceutical middleman power.
Increase transparency.
Support free-market health insurance competition.
Protect medical freedom and prohibit federal vaccine mandates.
10
Agriculture, Rural Development, and Infrastructure
Support family farms, protect land from foreign ownership, expand rural broadband, improve local roads, and protect water systems.
11
Technology, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Privacy
Guardrails on AI used by government.
Ban sale of American data to foreign adversaries.
Protect children from harmful social media algorithms.
Promote free-market innovation without heavy federal interference.
12
Election Integrity and Confidence in Elections
Voter ID.
Clean voter rolls.
Ban ballot harvesting except for caregivers and military.
Uniform rules for federal elections.
