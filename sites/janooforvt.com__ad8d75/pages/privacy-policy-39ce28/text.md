Amanda Janoo for Governor: SMS Terms & Privacy Policy
By providing your phone number and opting in to text messages from Amanda Janoo for Governor, you agree to receive recurring campaign text messages, including updates, event invitations, and fundraising appeals.
Message Frequency: Message frequency varies.
You may receive up to several messages per month depending on campaign activity.
Rates: Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
Opt-Out: You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
After opting out, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further texts.
Reply HELP for assistance.
Support: For questions or support, contact us at info@janooforvt.com or visit janooforvt.com.
Privacy: We respect your privacy.
Your phone number and opt-in consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties under any circumstances.
This program is operated by Amanda Janoo for Governor.
Participation is voluntary and not a condition of any purchase or donation.
