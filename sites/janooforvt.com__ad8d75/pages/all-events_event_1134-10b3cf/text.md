Thursday Night Weekly Phone Bank for Amanda Janoo!
Attend our weekly Thursday night phone banks for Amanda Janoo!

You will be trained on how to use our phone banking tool, given training and a script for how to connect with voters and time to practice making calls.
We will use this time to connect with voters around the state to spread the word about Amanda and make sure they have everything theey need to vote in the primary on August 11.
