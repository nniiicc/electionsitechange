It’s always great to have visitors from the district at the Capitol.
During the last session, we had quite a few people visit and I always welcome meeting with people.
Recently, I was named the honorary state Representative for the DeKalb GOP and spoke at their meeting.
As you’re probably aware, DeKalb County is heavily Democrat and as a result, conservatives have no representation in the Georgia State House.
Although I obviously don’t represent DeKalb County voters, I’m pleased to be able to listen to their concerns provide them with some insight.
