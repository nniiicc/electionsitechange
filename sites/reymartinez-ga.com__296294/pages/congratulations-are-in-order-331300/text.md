Congratulations to two of the best–Walton County Sheriff Joe Chapman and Walton County Commissioner Mark Banks for 20 years of public service working for our Citizens.
Also, congratulations to my good friend Dekalb County Republican Party Chairwoman Marci Baer McCarthy for working so hard on behalf of Republicans in Dekalb County and throughout Georgia.
We were able to make historic Republican wins because of her dedication and commitment.
