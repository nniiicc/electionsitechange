MEET BRIAN HARRISON
The most effective, conservative fighter in Texas
A native Texan, graduate of Ovilla Christian School, and proud member of the fightin’ Texas Aggie class of 2004, Brian and his family have long been involved in Ellis County and committed themselves to protecting our fundamental, Constitutional rights.
Brian was recruited to serve as President Trump’s Chief of Staff of the U.S.
Department of Health and Human Services, the largest government agency in the world, where he oversaw a staff of over 83,000 and a budget of over $1.4 trillion, helped secure the border with Title 42, slash burdensome regulations, and defunded Planned Parenthood.
Brian’s work has been repeatedly praised by President Trump!
Now serving as a Texas State Representative, Brian has been acclaimed by conservative leaders like Sen.
Ted Cruz, who said, “Brian Harrison is a fighter for freedom and the type of leader we need in the Texas Legislature,” and WarRoom host Steve Bannon, who called Brian “a true warrior down in Texas who’s fighting the uniparty establishment!”
Grassroots America, the largest conservative grassroots organization in Texas, named Brian the “Ace of Champions” and “The Most Fiscally Conservative Member of the Texas Legislature!”
Conservative leaders have referred to Brian as the “most impactful state legislator in America” for the victories he’s achieved against the Austin Swamp and his unwavering and unapologetic fights against abusive property taxes, big government spending, corporate welfare, and nanny state bureaucrats and regulations that seek to control how Texans live their lives.
Brian has, many times single-handedly, delivered the changes conservative voters have demanded for years.
No one has fought harder or delivered more major victories for Texas like these:
- Forcing the President of Texas A&M out after exposing his cover up of radical trans and DEI ideology being taught on campus
- Successfully led the charge to end COVID vaccine mandates in Texas
- Helped prevent people with noncitizen IDs from being given regular ballots
- Forced the Texas Medical Board to fire the Planned Parenthood official serving as Medical Director
- Stopped the Texas Workforce Commission from treating “gender identity” and “sexual orientation” as protected classes

- Brought unprecedented transparency to the corruption in Texas government through social media, generating millions of views and engaging tens of thousands of grassroots activists from across Texas and America to force actions from the liberal establishment
Because of his rare courage to battle the biggest names in the mainstream media in the defense of liberty, Brian won the Voice of the American Dream Award by the conservative Club for Growth Foundation, which honors an individual who has demonstrated exceptional achievements in communications and media, alongside an unwavering commitment to preserve and promote the principles of economic freedom and limited government.
Brian has met with thousands of constituents, local businesses, and community leaders to best understand the issues facing the district, and he remains laser focused on delivering the most freedom, most opportunity, and most empowerment for Texans to live their lives, run their businesses, and prosper in the Lone Star State!
He has also previously worked in the White House, Pentagon, and in small family businesses based in Ellis County, where he lives with his wife, Tara, and their four children: Hudson, Tucker, Hayes, and Violet.
