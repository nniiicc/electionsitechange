Yury Polozov Merrimack 10 The Platform Fighting for New Hampshire Ron's positions are straightforward: lower taxes, less government, more freedom.
Here's where he stands on the issues that matter most to Sullivan District 4.
No New Taxes Opposing any income tax, sales tax, or broad-based tax that would erode the New Hampshire Advantage.
Taxes on Granite Staters go up only over Ron's objection.
Second Amendment As the founder of New Hampshire Arsenal, Ron will fight any attempt to restrict the constitutional rights of law-abiding gun owners.
No new gun laws.
Period.
Housing & Cost of Living Young people can't afford to stay in New Hampshire.
Ron will push to reduce regulatory barriers to housing development and cut the property tax burden that is pricing families out.
Jobs & Economy A business-friendly environment attracts employers and raises wages.
Ron will work to cut red tape, reduce the cost of doing business, and bring good-paying jobs back to our communities.
Local Control Decisions about our towns should be made in our towns — not mandated from Concord.
Ron will protect the authority of local communities to govern themselves.
Limited Government Government works best when it does fewer things well.
Ron will oppose regulatory overreach, fight unnecessary spending, and keep the government out of your life wherever possible.
