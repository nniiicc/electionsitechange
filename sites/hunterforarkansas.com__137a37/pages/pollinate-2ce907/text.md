We aren't spending money on buttons or yard signs but hope that you will make your own and post photos of what you made!
Here are some templates you can download and print to get you started
Print the sign file on paper and cut out the letters to glue to your poster board, then lightly spray the board with clear water proof shellac or epoxy to weatherproof it.
Print the buttons on glossy photo paper or card stock and glue a discount store magnet to the back to wear on your shirt or hat.
