District zoning is an important part of the election process in a Representative Democracy like ours.
This past couple of years, we’ve been hearing a lot about redrawing districts.
Gerrymandering is the process of drawing district lines to manipulate communities in an unfair effort to keep a particular group in power, who could not maintain power based simply on their broad public support.
This is opposed to drawing district lines to ensure that communities feel best represented in their state government.
Our Arkansas motto is “Regnat Populus” or “the People Rule”.
