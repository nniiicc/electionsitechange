MEET BLAKE SANDERS
Blake Sanders is a husband, father, landscape architect, professor, and the Immediate Past Mayor of West Pelzer.
Elected in November 2024, he is now serving his first term in the South Carolina House of Representatives.
Sanders grew up in West Pelzer.
Sanders and his wife, Ashley, returned in 2007 to put down roots and raise their family in the community.
