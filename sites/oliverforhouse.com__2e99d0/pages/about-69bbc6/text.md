I'm running for House District 47 because I've spent my career sitting across from Oklahoma families in some of their hardest moments, and too many of those hard moments are made harder by decisions being made at the Capitol.

Indigo Oliver is a licensed mental health therapist and small business owner from Mustang who has spent her career helping families heal and thrive.
A proud product of Oklahoma public schools, she earned her Master's degree from the University of Oklahoma before opening her own private practice in 2021 and has seen firsthand how the decisions made at the Capitol shape the lives of real families in our community.
She knows what it looks like when the systems families depend on start to fail them.
And she believes District 47 deserves someone at the Capitol with real-world experience in mental health, education, and healthcare who will fight to make those systems work for everyone.
