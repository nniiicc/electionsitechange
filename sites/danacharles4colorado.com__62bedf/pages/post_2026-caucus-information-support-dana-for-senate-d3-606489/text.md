First time attending caucus?
Here’s what to expect.
Caucus is a neighborhood meeting, not a formal polling place.
You’ll check in, meet other voters from your precinct, and take part in discussions about party priorities and local leadership.
What happens You sign in and verify your party affiliation Participants may nominate or discuss candidates and party platform issues Some attendees volunteer to serve as delegates for later assemblies What to bring A valid ID A willingness to listen, speak up if you want, and engage respectfully Good to know You do not need to be an expert on policy or politics You are not required to speak or volunteer Caucus is typically short and locally run, often at a school, library, or community space If you’ve ever felt like decisions are made without everyday people in the room, caucus is one of the few moments where that isn’t true.
