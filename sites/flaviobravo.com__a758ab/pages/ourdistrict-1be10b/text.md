Every 10 years, the Arizona Independent Redistricting Commission (IRC) redraws and adopts new legislative district maps for Arizona.
Legislative District 26 is home to over 230k people with over 64 languages spoken.
Education Our district includes Alhambra Elementary School District (AESD), Isaac School District #5, Phoenix Elementary School District #1, and the Washington Elementary School District (WESD).
We are home to several Phoenix Union High School District (PXU) schools including Alhambra, Bostrom, Carl Hayden, Desiderata, Linda Abril, Metro Tech, and Phoenix Digital Academy.
We are also home to the Glendale Union High School District, including Cortez and Washington High School.
Our district includes several private schools ranging from elementary to college: St.
Matthew School, Saints Simon and Jude Cathedral School, Bourgade Catholic High School, and Grand Canyon University.
