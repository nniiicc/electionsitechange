Accomplishments
This legislative session, in 2026, Senator Bravo led bipartisan legislation signed by Governor Katie Hobbs to help combat sex trafficking along 27th Avenue and the I-17 corridor by holding buyers accountable, supporting survivors, and strengthening public safety.
In April 2026, Senator Bravo received the Advocate in Action Award by ALL in Education.
This award honors individuals who move beyond conversation and into impact - leaders who show up for students, families, and communities with intention and accountability.
In 2024, Senator Bravo collaborated with the Southwest Autism Research & Resource Center (SARRC) to pass bipartisan legislation creating a new license plate in support of youth with Autism.

During the 2024 budget negotiations, Senator Bravo introduced and passed an essential reform so that teachers across our public school districts will now receive their employment contracts earlier with more time to prepare for the upcoming academic school year.
In 2023, Senator Bravo worked with his colleagues to secure $60M for the historic Homeless Shelter and Services Fund administered by the Arizona Department of Housing.
