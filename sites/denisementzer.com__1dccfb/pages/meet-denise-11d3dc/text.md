My prior 10 years of experience on the Mount Clemens City commission has offered valuable lessons.
I have experience with municipal budgets, taxes, enterprise funds, and special assessments.
I have learned when to compromise and when to stand up and hold my ground.
I have learned to listen.
I’m known for doing my homework on any issue that comes before the commission and being a budget hawk.
I pushed for quarterly departmental reports to be made at City Commission meetings to improve transparency.
In the State Legislature, I voted to establish an Ethics and Oversight Committee and helped pass the BRITE Act, which increased reporting and disclosure requirements for registered lobbyists and required state-level public officials to submit comprehensive financial records on an annual basis.
I also voted to REPEAL Michigan's pension tax on seniors and to give working and middle class families a tax break.
I helped secure $10 million in the state budget over the past two years to demolish the old and abandoned Victory Inn and replace it with hundreds of new construction units right here in Macomb County .
I grew up right here in Clinton Township and graduated from L'Anse Creuse High.
My father retired as a Detective Lieutenant from the Macomb County Sheriff's Department after 34 years of service.
My brother still serves in the Sheriff's Department.
I earned a degree in Business Administration from Baker College while working full-time and taking night classes.
In 2011, I purchased a home in Mount Clemens and became interested in City issues.
In 2013 I was elected to serve on the Mount Clemens City Commission and served for nearly 10 years.
In 2022, I was honored to earn the support of District 61 and represent us in Michigan's House of Representatives.
In 2024, I again won the support of the people of Mount Clemens, Clinton Township, and eastern Sterling Heights.
This year, I took a big step and filed to run for the third time as your State Representative in District 61 to continue the fight for our rights and for good governance.
I am committed to transparency and fiscal responsibility.
I am an advocate for affordable healthcare, preserving all of our individual rights and freedoms, including every Michigander's right to full bodily autonomy, a well funded public education system, economic growth and infrastructure as part of my family legacy of public service.
