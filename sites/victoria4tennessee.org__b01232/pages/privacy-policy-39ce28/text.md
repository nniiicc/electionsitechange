PRIVACY POLICY
This Privacy Policy explains how Victoria4Tennessee ("Committee," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you access or use our websites, mobile sites, and mobile applications that link to this Privacy Policy, including victoria4tennessee.org (collectively, the "Site").
We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time.
If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, in some cases, may provide additional notice (such as a statement on our homepage or a direct notification).
We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about our practices and your choices.
1.
Information We Collect
Information You Provide to Us We collect information you provide directly, such as when you sign up for email or SMS updates, request information, fill out a form, sign a petition, volunteer, register for an event, make a donation or purchase, or otherwise contact us.
This may include your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, employer and occupation, payment information (typically collected by a third-party processor such as ActBlue), and any other information you choose to share.
Donor Disclosure Requirements Federal and/or state election law may require us to collect and publicly disclose the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of individuals whose contributions to the Committee exceed $200 in an election cycle.
Automatically Collected Information When you use the Site, we automatically collect:
- Log and usage data: browser type, app version, access times, pages viewed, IP address, and referring page
- Device information: hardware model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information
- Transactional information: donation or purchase amount, date, and related details
- Approximate location: typically derived from your IP address
- Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 6 below
Information from Other Sources We may obtain information about you from third parties, such as mailing list providers, publicly available sources (including voter files, where permitted by law), and donation platforms like ActBlue if you contribute through them for our benefit.
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Operate, maintain, and improve the Site
- Process donations and transactions and send related confirmations
- Respond to your questions, comments, and requests
- Send updates about the campaign, issues, events, volunteer opportunities, and requests for donations or support
- Encourage voter registration and turnout, and help you find polling locations
- Comply with legal and campaign finance obligations, including donor disclosure and recordkeeping
- Analyze Site usage and personalize content, features, and advertising
- Detect and prevent fraud and protect the rights, property, and safety of the Committee and others
3.
Consent to Communications
By providing your email address, phone number, or other contact information on this Site or through an ActBlue donation or sign-up form, you consent to receive periodic communications from Victoria4Tennessee, including email updates, fundraising appeals, event and volunteer announcements, and text messages.
Text Messages (SMS/MMS)
- Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies.
- Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any donation or purchase.
- Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for their marketing purposes.
Data may be shared only with (1) vendors and service providers performing work on our behalf, who may not use it for their own purposes, or (2) as required by law or legal process.
- To opt out, reply STOP to any message.
Reply HELP for assistance.
- For program support, contact us using the information in Section 11; email is not an acceptable method of opting out of text messages.
Email By submitting your email address, you agree to receive email communications from Victoria4Tennessee.
You may unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly.
General You may withdraw consent to any communications at any time; doing so will not affect the legality of messages sent before withdrawal.
4.
Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share it:
- With vendors, consultants, and service providers performing work on our behalf
- In required public donor disclosures (name, address, occupation, employer for contributions exceeding $200 per election cycle)
- With affiliated committees or party organizations, where legally permitted
- In response to lawful requests, subpoenas, court orders, or to meet legal or regulatory obligations
- To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Committee, its staff, volunteers, or others, or if we believe your actions violate our policies
- In connection with a reorganization, merger, or transfer of Committee assets
- With your consent or at your direction
- As aggregated or de-identified data that cannot reasonably identify you
5.
Online Petitions and Social Sharing
Petitions: If you sign an online petition, your name, city, state, and any submitted comments may become public and may be shared with elected officials, the press, or included in public compilations.
Social Sharing Features: The Site may include social sharing tools (e.g., a Facebook "Like" button).
Using these features may share your activity with your social network or the public, depending on that platform's privacy settings.
Review the relevant platform's privacy policy for details.
6.
Cookies, Analytics, and Advertising
We and our service providers use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to operate the Site, understand usage, and measure the effectiveness of our communications.
We may also use tools such as Google Analytics or Meta (Facebook) Pixel to deliver and measure advertising across the web based on your visits to our Site.
- Most browsers accept cookies by default; you can adjust your browser settings to block or delete them, though this may affect Site functionality.
- To opt out of interest-based advertising, visit Google's Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.
- With your consent, we may send push notifications to your mobile device; you can disable these in your device's notification settings at any time.
7.
Third-Party Links and Services
The Site may link to or integrate third-party services, such as ActBlue for processing donations.
Information you provide to those third parties is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.
We encourage you to review those policies before providing personal information.
8.
Data Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
9.
Data Retention
We retain personal information as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, meet campaign finance recordkeeping requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
10.
Your Privacy Choices and Rights
- Promotional emails: Unsubscribe using the link in any email.
- Text messages: Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
- Push notifications: Adjust your device settings.
- Cookies: Adjust your browser settings or use the opt-out tools listed in Section 6.
Depending on your state of residence, you may have additional rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to opt out of certain data sharing.
To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 11, and we will respond as required by applicable law.
