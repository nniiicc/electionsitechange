Meet Aaron Issues News Volunteer Contribute Aaron is the dedicated, experienced, and qualified conservative running to be the next State Representative for Greenup and Boyd Countians in the 98th District.
A ninth-generation Kentuckian, he is a native of South Shore, current resident of Russell, and a graduate of the University of Kentucky and Greenup County High School.
He is a husband to Brittany and a father.
Thompson has the required experience to make him an effective State Representative.
As an Executive Director and Field Representative in the Kentucky Governor’s Office and Department for Local Government, Thompson worked closely with leaders across state government to deliver for Kentuckians each and every day.
Before serving in the Governor’s Office, Thompson was a Director of Special Projects for a U.S.
Congressman, and assisted Kentuckians navigate the federal government.
