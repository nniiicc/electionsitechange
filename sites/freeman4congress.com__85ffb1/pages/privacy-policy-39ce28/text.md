Introduction The Committee to Elect Kymone Freeman ("the Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our supporters, volunteers, and visitors to our website.
This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information in connection with our campaign for public office in Washington, D.C.
By using our website or providing your information to the Campaign, you consent to the practices described in this policy.
2.
Information We Collect We may collect the following types of information: Personal Information: First name, last name, email address, mobile phone number, and ZIP code when you sign up for our email list, volunteer, or contact us.
Volunteer Information: Your preferred volunteer role, availability, and areas of interest within the campaign.
Donation Information: When you donate through our third-party payment processor (Donorbox), we may receive your name and donation amount.
We do not store credit card or banking information on our servers.
Voter Interest Data: Your selected primary policy interest (e.g., D.C.
Statehood, Education & Schools, Affordable Housing, Public Safety, Small Business Support).
Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our website, including IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited.
3.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Communicate with you about campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities; Process and acknowledge your donations and volunteer sign-ups; Send you text messages (SMS) and emails if you have provided express consent; Understand which issues matter most to D.C. residents so we can better represent your interests; Comply with applicable campaign finance and election laws in the District of Columbia and federal regulations; Improve our website and campaign outreach efforts.
4.
SMS & Text Messaging Consent By checking the SMS consent box on our forms, you agree to receive recurring automated and non-automated text messages from the Committee to Elect Kymone Freeman at the phone number you provided.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying "STOP" to any message.
For help, reply "HELP." We do not sell or rent your phone number to third parties.
5.
Campaign Finance Disclosure In accordance with the District of Columbia Office of Campaign Finance (OCF) and the Federal Election Commission (FEC), certain donor information — including your name, address, occupation, and employer — may be required to be reported and made publicly available.
This is a legal requirement and is not optional for contributions above applicable thresholds.
6.
How We Share Your Information We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share your information with: Our CRM and email service providers for the purpose of managing campaign communications; Third-party payment processors (Donorbox) for the purpose of processing donations; Compliance with applicable D.C. and federal election laws, court orders, or law enforcement requests; With your explicit consent, with partner organizations aligned with our campaign's mission.
7.
Data Security We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
8.
Your Rights You have the right to: Request access to or a copy of the personal information we hold about you; Request that we correct inaccurate information; Request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements; Opt out of email or SMS communications at any time using the unsubscribe link or by replying "STOP"; Withdraw consent for volunteer outreach at any time.
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@freeman4congress.com .
9.
Children's Privacy Our website is not directed at individuals under the age of 16.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
11.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
