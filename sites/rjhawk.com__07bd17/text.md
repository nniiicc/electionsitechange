DONATE STANDING FOR FREEDOM DISTRICT 81 STATE REPRESENTATIVE RJ HAWK EXPLAINS WHY HE'S RUNNING FOR RE-ELECTION ABOUT MEET RJ HAWK The focus of my campaign is standing against government overreach, continuing to cut taxes, and creating more opportunities for Arkansans to land good-paying jobs.
I have a record of putting more money back in your pocket.
In the Arkansas House, I voted to cut the state income tax rate from 4.4% to 3.9%.
In the 2026 special session, I supported cutting the top individual income tax rate again—from 3.9% to 3.7%—and reducing the top corporate rate from 4.3% to 4.1%.
We have also delivered real property-tax relief.
I co-sponsored raising the Homestead Tax Credit to $500, then co-sponsored the 2025 increase from $500 to $600.
In the 2026 fiscal session, I supported raising it again to $675.
If re-elected, I will keep working to lower taxes and make life more affordable for families in District 81.
In 2023, we passed the largest education reform this state has ever seen.
I was proud to co-sponsor that legislation and vote for it.
In 2025, I continued that work by co-sponsoring bills to support our schools, including a measure to keep the sales-tax exemption on instructional materials when schools lease textbooks instead of buying them.
Having another opportunity to serve the people of Saline County at the state level is an honor.
District 81 is home, and it is an area where I won every precinct in the 2022 election.
I look forward to re-engaging my friends, neighbors, and community—and listening to their concerns.
Giving back is a family priority.
I have led fundraising efforts for the Fellowship of Christian Athletes, the Child Advocacy Center, Special Olympics, and the Cystic Fibrosis Foundation.
I’ve always believed that a strong community and a strong state require people who give back.
I’ve been fortunate, and I want to see folks get a hand up when they need one.
I spent 14 years on 103.7 The Buzz and still broadcast Arkansas high school football, basketball, and baseball state championships on Arkansas PBS.
I own Realty One Group Pinnacle, a real estate brokerage in Bryant.
My wife, Brianna, and I have a daughter, Addison, and a son, Austin.
I am a conservative Republican and an unapologetic supporter of the Second Amendment.
In my spare time, I enjoy hunting, fishing, and calling high school sports on local radio.
ABOUT THE ISSUES LOWER TAXES & TAX CUTS As your State Representative, I have consistently supported cutting Arkansas income taxes so hardworking families and job creators keep more of what they earn.
I voted for the reductions that brought the top individual rate down (from 4.9% to 3.7% so far) and have backed the longer-term “Pathway to Zero” goal of eliminating the state income tax while protecting essential services.
I have also voted to lower your property taxes and eliminate the state grocery tax.
Lower taxes mean more money in your pocket, stronger local businesses, and a more competitive Arkansas that attracts jobs and investment—not higher taxes that punish success.
WELFARE TO WORK I believe the best anti-poverty program is a job.
That is why I support policies that move people from government dependence to self-sufficiency through work requirements, workforce training, and real opportunity.
Arkansas has led on requiring able-bodied adults to work, train, or volunteer in exchange for benefits, and I will continue fighting for reforms that restore dignity, reduce long-term dependency, and ensure limited taxpayer resources help those who truly need temporary assistance while opening pathways to permanent employment and independence.
SMALL BUSINESS OWNER & REALTOR IN THE COMMUNITY I am not a career politician—I am a small business owner and realtor who lives and works right here in our community.
As principal broker/owner of Realty One Group Pinnacle and longtime operator of Hawk Media Solutions, I understand the daily challenges of meeting payroll, serving customers, navigating regulations, and building something lasting.
That real-world experience shapes every vote I cast: I fight for policies that make it easier—not harder—for local entrepreneurs, homeowners, and families to succeed in District 81 and across Arkansas.
HEALTHCARE - PUSHING BACK ON PBM'S AND BIG PHARMA TO LOWER COSTS Prescription drug costs are too high because middlemen and special interests have stacked the deck against patients and independent pharmacies.
I cosponsored and strongly supported Arkansas’s landmark PBM reform (HB 1150) that bans anti-competitive practices, prohibits pharmacy benefit managers from owning pharmacies in ways that create conflicts of interest, and puts patients and community pharmacies first.
I will keep pushing back against PBM abuses and big-pharma pricing power so Arkansans pay less for the medicines they need and so local pharmacies can continue serving our communities.
