A farmer’s land matters.
So does a neighbor’s home.
When a large energy project affects both, each deserves a real voice before the decision is made.
I support substantially amending PA 233.
Township zoning should receive substantial deference, and state review should be a limited backstop for unreasonable obstruction.
Residents need a chance to participate early enough to change a proposal.
Michigan’s Public Act 233 establishes a state siting process for certain large renewable-energy and energy-storage facilities under specified conditions.
It took effect November 29, 2024.
The official law and MPSC guidance explain the process.
