Why are property taxes changing in Ottawa County?
30-second answer
Property-tax notices and bills describe different parts of the assessment and collection process.
To understand a change on a particular property, compare the assessor’s notice with the tax bill and ask the local assessor to explain the figures.
Michigan Treasury provides official guidance on assessments, exemptions and appeals.
Why it matters locally
Property taxes are part of housing costs.
A countywide explanation does not establish why an individual homeowner’s bill changed.

The campaign’s housing statement discusses predictable property taxes and living costs.
