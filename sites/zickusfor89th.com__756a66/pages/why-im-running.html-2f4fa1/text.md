I’ve served on the school board and planning commission, practiced law, and taught bioethics at Grand Valley State University.
This is the community where we’ve built our life.
I put myself through college and law school, working full time and earning scholarships.
I know what it means to watch every dollar.
That’s part of why I care about what public money pays for—and whether it helps people.
On the Allendale school board, I voted for our first Early Childhood Center and led the Early College Initiative, a path for students to begin college in their junior year and graduate with an associate degree from MCC.
Good schools need more than praise.
They need decisions that give students real opportunities.
I’m running to bring that work to Lansing.
Homes people can afford.
Healthcare they can reach.
Schools that prepare students for what comes next.
And a voice for the people who live with the consequences of land and water decisions.
You may disagree with me on some issues.
I’ll still listen and take your concerns seriously.
We have work to do together. — Meegan Zickus Read the fuller account of my education, family, and public service Follow Meegan Connect with the campaign on social media.
