Grand Valley State University · Allendale Photo: Gpwitteveen / Wikimedia Commons · CC BY-SA 3.0 · Resized and compressed; display may crop.
I spent ten years on the Allendale school board.
We balanced budgets while building schools and providing busing throughout the district.
I know those decisions matter to a family trying to give a child a good start.
I want parents and local boards to have a strong voice, with decisions made close to the classroom and the kitchen table.
Schools need responsible funding and room to respond to their own students.
I voted for our first Early Childhood Center and led the Early College Initiative.
More students should have a useful next step, whether that’s college, a trade, or a job.
Here’s what I learned on the school board.
