A first home, a place to raise a family, somewhere smaller when it’s time to downsize: we need room for all of those choices.
I’ll work with local communities to make it easier to build attainable homes and address the roads, utilities, and review processes that can stand in the way.
I also want taxes and living costs that families can plan around.
People who already live here deserve a voice.
So do the families trying to find a place here.
We can take both seriously and get to work.
