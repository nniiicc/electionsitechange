You should be able to trust the water coming from your tap or well.
And you shouldn’t have to become an environmental expert to get a straight answer about contamination.
On the planning commission, I advocated for groundwater protection, PFAS education, and public water for new subdivisions in an area facing dry wells and aquifer concerns.
Clean water is part of planning for growth.
I’ll work for prompt answers, clear cleanup information, and accountability for contamination.
New development needs to account for the water supply, existing neighborhoods, and farmland.
The people who depend on them deserve a say.
