Everything Fits Within Three Core Priorities
The reason I'm running for Congress comes back to one idea: government should answer to you, and right now, it's broken.
It answers to whoever writes the biggest check, and that explains almost everything else.
Your bills climb while the monopolies that set them stay protected.
The cost of living crisis is the receipt for corruption.
My priorities fit together and fuel one another:
- We must fight corruption, eliminate fraud, and get the corporate money out of politics.
- Fix the cause, and we can lower the cost of living, taking on the price-gougers shielded by the politicians they buy out.
- And when families can get ahead instead of just getting by, we can protect our future — our water, our land, our kids, and a country that isn't handing them the bill.
Each one unlocks the next, paid for by ending corruption and taking on tax cheats, not by growing the debt.
None of this is out of reach.
It's just never been the priority of a bought-out establishment of career politicians.
Let's bring in new leaders who understand how it all fits together — and who are ready for the fight.
