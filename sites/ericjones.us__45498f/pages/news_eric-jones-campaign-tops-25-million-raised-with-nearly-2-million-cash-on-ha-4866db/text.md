Not a Dime from Corporate PACs
“This campaign is being built by people who are tired of politics as usual and want a representative who will fight for them, not corporate interests,” Jones said.
“Reaching $2.5 million raised without special-interest money sends a clear message: The status quo isn’t working.
Voters are ready for change.”
Approximately $2.2 million was raised from individual donors, with the balance coming from Jones’ contributions to his own campaign.
The campaign reported that more than $1 million was raised in the most recent quarter alone, all without taking a dime from corporate PACs, federal lobbyists, or special interests.
The strong fundraising performance reflects growing momentum behind Jones’ message of affordability, accountability, and putting working families first.
With more than $2 million cash on hand, the campaign is well-positioned to expand voter outreach, grassroots organizing, and earned media efforts throughout California’s Fourth Congressional District.
Jones’s fundraising success highlights a sharp contrast with the current incumbent’s long reliance on corporate and special-interest contributions.
The Jones campaign will continue focusing on kitchen-table issues facing families across the district, including the rising cost of housing and utilities, corporate accountability, and restoring trust in government.
Jones is a candidate in California’s 4th Congressional District, which stretches from Napa to Placer County, including Napa, Davis, Woodland, Lincoln, Petaluma, and Marysville.
