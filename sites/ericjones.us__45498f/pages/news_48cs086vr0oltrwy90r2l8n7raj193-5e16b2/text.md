More than 50 residents attended the event.
The town hall focused on the issues most impacting Napa and surrounding communities, including the economic pressures facing local winemakers and farmers, the region’s housing affordability crisis, and ongoing water challenges.
“People across this district are feeling the strain, from farmers and winemakers navigating rising costs and uncertainty, to families struggling to afford housing, to communities grappling with water issues,” said Jones.
“I will continue to reach out across the district to listen, to learn, and to make sure Congress is focused on the real challenges people are facing every day.” Jones’ New Day tour is taking him to communities across the 4th District, with a focus on engaging voters, hearing directly from residents, and building a campaign rooted in local priorities and practical solutions.
### About the District: California’s 4th Congressional District stretches from Napa and Sonoma Counties through parts of Lake, Yuba, Sutter, Yolo Solano, and into Placer and Sacramento counties, including the communities of Napa, Davis, Woodland, Petaluma, Lincoln, and Marysville.
