Meet Eric Jones
“My mother told me it would get better”
My mom was a nurse, working 60-hour weeks in rural Maine.
My father was an opioid-addicted disabled veteran, asleep most nights in a recliner in the middle of our 750-square-foot home.
Before I was 10, I was getting him out of that chair and into bed at 5 a.m., clearing the pill bottles, and making breakfast for myself.
Our family relied on food stamps, Medicaid, disability checks, and the support of our church.
My mom told me I would build a better life than the one we had, and that this country would give me the chance.
She was right.
I went looking for scholarships, got out of our small town, and was the first in my family to go to college.
I took the best-paying job I could and helped my dad get better healthcare.
The moment I could, I moved out to California, and my wife, Rachel, joined me.
I helped grow a business and build companies.
We identified talented people and solutions to modern problems, and then we deployed energy, resources, and dealmaking to these ideas we believed in.
We created thousands of jobs.
My focus was primarily in innovating healthcare models to expand access to care.
We were very successful.
My kids will never see a stack of unpaid bills on a kitchen counter.
I lived the American Dream.
I'm raising two kids in Napa now, and I can't honestly promise them the same thing my mother promised me.
I'm running for Congress because I am so fed up with our government robbing us — you, your children, my children — of the future we deserve.
They sit there, protecting their power, collecting corporate donations, and ignoring our needs.
I'm running because I need to fight for our children's future and for the promise of America — and our current leaders are, at best, ignoring the task.
Nobody has earned a lifetime appointment in Congress just for showing up.
I've signed a term limit pledge, I take no corporate PAC money, and I want a stock trading ban and an end to gerrymandering.
We deserve a government that works to improve our lives and better our future — not one that enriches itself and protects the power of its career politicians and megacorporations.
CA-4 is nine counties: Colusa, Lake, Napa, Placer, Sacramento, Sonoma, Sutter, Yolo, and Yuba.
I'm running to fight for every voter in all nine counties.
I'm running to redeem the American Dream for my kids, and for yours.
