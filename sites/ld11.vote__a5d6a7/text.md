Cesar Aleman is running for the Arizona State House to serve the community he has called home since childhood and to bring people together around policies that improve daily life in LD11.
Raised in South Phoenix and Laveen Cesar moved with his family from El Paso, Texas to Arizona in 1992 at age 9.
He attended Laveen Elementary, now Laveen Education Center, and graduated with the first class at Cesar Chavez High School.
Service before politics From 2009 through May 2020, Cesar worked with Boys & Girls Clubs of Metro Phoenix across the Valley, serving youth, families, and community partners.
He now works in the Laveen area and serves through Laveen Baptist Church.
Why Cesar is running As LD11 grows, Cesar believes the district needs policy changes that actually improve the community.
He is running to represent people rather than political agendas and to help Republicans, Democrats, Independents, and every neighbor work together where common ground is possible.
