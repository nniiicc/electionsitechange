Campaigns aren’t won behind a desk—they’re won by showing up, listening, and working side-by-side with our neighbors.
That’s why I’m out in our communities every chance I get.
From coffee chats and town halls to parades and local fundraisers, these events are a chance for us to connect, talk about the issues that matter most, and work toward solutions together.
Check back often for updates on where I’ll be next.
We’ll post new events here as soon as they’re confirmed so you can join in and be part of the conversation.
