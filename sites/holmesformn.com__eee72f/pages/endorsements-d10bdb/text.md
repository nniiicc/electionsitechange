Together, these groups represent the values we share in southeastern Minnesota.
These aren’t just names on a list — they’re neighbors, workers, and advocates who believe in putting common sense ahead of politics.
Trusted by people who put our communities first.
Organizations AFL-CIO Minnesota The Minnesota AFL-CIO is the state's largest federation of labor unions, representing workers across industries including construction, manufacturing, healthcare, education, public service, transportation, retail, and the skilled trades.
Teamsters Joint Council 32 Teamsters Joint Council 32 represents truck drivers, warehouse workers, delivery drivers, sanitation workers, public employees, airline workers, and other transportation and logistics professionals across the Upper Midwest.
IBEW Local 343 IBEW Local 343 represents electricians and electrical workers across southeastern Minnesota, supporting the skilled trades that build, maintain, and power our homes, businesses, schools, and critical infrastructure.
SEIU MN State Council SEIU Minnesota State Council brings together healthcare workers, janitors, security officers, airport workers, nursing home employees, and public service workers who care for Minnesota's families and communities.
AFSCME Council 5 AFSCME Council 5 represents local government employees, corrections officers, public health workers, social service professionals, highway workers, library staff, and other public employees who keep Minnesota communities running.
Education Minnesota Education Minnesota represents teachers, education support professionals, school nurses, counselors, higher education faculty, and other educators working to ensure every Minnesota student has access to a quality public education.
MAPE MAPE represents Minnesota's professional state employees, advocating for fair workplaces, quality public services, and the professionals who serve communities across the state.
314 Action Fund 314 Action is the only organization dedicated to recruiting, training, and electing Democratic scientists to public office.
Save the Boundary Waters Action Fund Save the Boundary Waters Action Fund is a 501(c)4 dedicated to protecting the Boundary Waters Wilderness from the threat of sulfide-ore copper mining in the watershed of the Boundary Waters.
MN 350 Action MN 350 Action is a grassroots organization that brings Minnesotans together to advocate for clean energy, environmental justice, and the protection of the communities, land, air, and water that sustain us.
FairVote Minnesota FairVote Minnesota believes in a democracy that is inclusive, representative, participatory, civil, and responsive to the demands of the majorities of voters.
DFL Rural Caucus The DFL Rural Caucus delivers DFL values in rural communities — comprehensive health care for working families, world-class K-12/pre-K education, reproductive rights, support for beginning and family farmers, support for vital main street businesses, and border-to-border broadband.
DFL Environmental Caucus The Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party Environmental Caucus (DFLEC) is a community of Minnesota DFLers who share a common goal of protecting our planet, our state’s ecosystems, and all those who call them home.
DFL Senior Caucus The DFL Seniors Caucus advocates for the interests of older Minnesotans by promoting policies that strengthen retirement security, affordable health care, long-term care, and the ability of seniors to live with dignity and independence.
Stonewall DFL Stonewall DFL represents the DFL in the 2SLGBTQIA+ community and represents the 2SLGBTQIA+ community in the DFL.
50501 Minnesota 50501 Minnesota is a grassroots non-partisan organization dedicated to building connections, relationships and bridges across Minnesota resulting in a stronger, more representative democracy built on community, equity, and justice for all.
Moms Demand Action Moms Demand Action is a grassroots movement of Americans fighting for public safety measures that can protect people from gun violence.
Women Winning Women Winning is a Minnesota organization dedicated to recruiting, training, and supporting women from all backgrounds who support reproductive freedom to run for public office and lead in their communities.
