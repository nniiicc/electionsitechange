Aug 25 Written By Maren Schroeder Friends, I’m incredibly excited to announce that I’m running again to represent our communities in the Minnesota House.
Here in 24A, we work hard, we look out for our neighbors, and we deserve leaders who will do the same.
I’m running to ensure our rural communities have a strong voice willing to stand up for them in St.
Paul.
We need a representative committed to fully funding our schools, making health care and child care accessible, standing with family farmers, and keeping our small towns strong and vibrant.
This campaign won’t be won by insiders or special interests.
It’ll be powered by our friends and neighbors coming together.
I’d love for you to be part of it!
Here are a few ways you can get involved: Donate today to help us spread our message.
Sign up to volunteer And most importantly — join us for our Campaign Kick-Off Event on 9/9/25!
You can RSVP here .
It’ll be a chance to come together, hear more about our vision for 24A, and show that our communities are ready for change.
Together, we can make sure our towns have the respect, resources, and representation we deserve.
