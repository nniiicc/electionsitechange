Ashok Baddi is a community leader, small-business owner, and former Democrat who left the party after becoming disillusioned with blind partisanship and the belief that party loyalty should come before country and community.
With more than two decades of civic and faith-based service, Ashok brings practical, real-world experience as the owner of Minuteman Press Rochester Hills and as a board member of a local Hindu temple.
Guided by the principles of Sanatana Dharma, or Hinduism, he is running for State Representative in Michigan’s 56th District to support working families, protect small businesses, defend faith and community values, and restore accountability in state government.
