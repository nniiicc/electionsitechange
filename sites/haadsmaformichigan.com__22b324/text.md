Meet Jim Haadsma
Former State Representative Jim Haadsma is a native of Muskegon and has lived in Battle Creek for close to four decades.
He has fought and won for hardworking people and their families for 38 years to ensure they get fair treatment and equal opportunity in the workplace as a lawyer.
As State Representative from 2019 to 2024, he is committed to building upon the work he achieved for District 44.
