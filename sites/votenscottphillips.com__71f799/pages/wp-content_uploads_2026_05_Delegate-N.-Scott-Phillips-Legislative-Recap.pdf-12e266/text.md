Scott Phillips introduced and led legislation focused on strengthening public safety, promoting fairness within the justice system, expanding economic opportunity, and ensuring accountability in government.
The bills below reflect a continued commitment to delivering thoughtful, community-centered solutions for District 10 and the State of Maryland.
Justice & Public Safety HB 0491 / SB 531 – Criminal Procedure: Victims of Sexually Assaultive Behavior Enhances protections for survivors by strengthening requirements around consent and communication.
HB 1054 – Criminal Law: Ethics Violations, Malfeasance, Misfeasance, and Nonfeasance Addresses accountability by establishing clearer timelines for prosecuting public ethics violations.
SB 323 – Juvenile Court: Youth Charging Reform Reducing the number of offences for which youth are charged as adults.
SB 0791 – Criminal Procedure: Community Trust Act Ensuring due process and legal protections for the immigrant community.
