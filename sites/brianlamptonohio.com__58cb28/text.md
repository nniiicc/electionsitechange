Fighting for Greene County.
Representative Brian Lampton
Proudly Serves Ohio’s 70th House District
With over 30 years as a small business owner and decades of leadership in the community, Brian brings real-world experience to the Ohio House.
A graduate of the University of Dayton, Brian lives in Beavercreek with his wife Karen, and together they support our local schools, military families, and small businesses.
Brian Lampton | On The Issues
- Supporting Small Businesses As a longtime business owner, Brian knows that Ohio’s economy thrives when our entrepreneurs thrive.
He’s pushing for fewer burdensome regulations and lower taxes so that local businesses can hire, grow and serve our community.
- Tax Relief & Fiscal Responsibilty Brian believes that hardworking Ohioans deserve relief from ever-rising property taxes, lower income tax burdens, and a government that spends wisely and efficiently.
- Protecting Consumers & Healthcare As Insurance Committee Chair, Brian fights for more affordable, accessible insurance options, supports paid leave for organ donors and reforms that protect you instead of protecting bureaucracy.
- Supporting Our Veterans & Military Families With a strong military presence in our region, Brian is dedicated to easing transitions for military families, improving school safety and giving our veterans the support they have earned.
- Strong Families & Safe Communities Brian is committed to protecting our Second Amendment rights, improving distracted-driving laws, keeping our kids safe in school and advocating for pro-life policies that defend the most vulnerable.
- Strong Schools for a Better Futures Brian is committed to fair school funding that eases the burden on local taxpayers while supporting teachers, parents, and students.
He believes in safe schools, strong academics, and giving families more choices in education.
