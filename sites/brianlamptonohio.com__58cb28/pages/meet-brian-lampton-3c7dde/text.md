Representative Lampton proudly accepts the role of Chairman of the House Insurance Committee again this term, and he continues his service on the Veterans and Military Development Committee, while also serving on the Medicaid and the Small Business Committees.
He also retains leadership as the House Chair of the Joint House and Senate Business First Caucus.
Lampton has become an active member of the National Council of Insurance Legislators working on national legislative priorities and model legislation.
He was also recognized with the NFIB Guardian of Small Business Award for his support of Ohio’s small business community.
His priorities are to serve his constituents, reduce cumbersome taxes, and help small businesses thrive in Ohio.
Representative Lampton has been the owner and operator of Lampton/Engle & Associates Insurance for over 30 years.
Representative Lampton has been an active community member for several years.
He has previously served as the Vice President of the Beavercreek 9/11 Memorial Committee, President of both the Beavercreek and Fairborn Chambers of Commerce, President of the Beavercreek Rotary Club, Board member of the County Board of Elections, member of the Charter Review Committee and Vice Chair of the Greene County Republican Party.
In his free time, Representative Lampton enjoys announcing Beavercreek High School football and basketball games.
He lives in Beavercreek with his wife Karen and their two German Shepherds.
