A rational advocate for personal choice
Peace and prosperity occur only when individuals are free to make rational choices for themselves, their families, and their communities, unimpeded by excessive government regulation.
To create a stronger community through greater personal choice we must:
1.
Dismantle our outmoded, racist, and classist system of school assignment that forces
children into a school based solely on a street address.
Expand opportunities for parents
to select the best educational options for their children through initiatives such as
educational savings accounts, vouchers, and scholarships.
Reduce occupational licensing rules, zoning laws, and other regulations that, while well-intentioned, do more harm than good to our economy.
Create an economic environment that enables entrepreneurs to easily foster new innovative businesses and empower individuals with greater freedom to utilize their natural talents to better our communities
Reduce government regulations that hinder or prevent the introduction of innovative and lower-cost healthcare services and introduce price competition into our state healthcare system so that individuals may have greater freedom to select the best healthcare options for their families.
