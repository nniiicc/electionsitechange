Libertarian Party of North Carolina Candidates File for 46 Races￼
RALEIGH (Dec. 16) – The 2024 election season has officially kicked off and the Libertarian Party of North Carolina (LPNC) has mobilized to give voters a chance to choose prosperity and Liberty.
This December, 46 Libertarian candidates filed for 44 races, ranging from the highest office in North Carolina to a number of local elections.
