This district is realizing the benefits of the population shift back to urban areas.
The walkability of our community combined with the growth of new businesses is creating an influx of diverse new residents, commingling with the existing population.
This diversity of thought, backgrounds, skills, and ideas, combined with our centralized downtown location should lead to a plethora of opportunities for our residents in the coming decades.
This is a truly fantastic community with nearly unlimited potential.
When people are free to choose how to best live their lives and when individuals are empowered to make the best decisions for their families, communities become stronger and more resilient.
When government intervenes with rules and regulations, it serves only to empower politicians and entrenched interests, while communities become dependent and less secure, and progress is stifled.
Only free individuals can ensure progress.
We must limit the power of our state government and return freedom of choice to the people.
Individual rights, personal responsibility Smaller government when it’s possible, more efficient government when it’s necessary.
