Can You Limit or Opt Out Of The Information Collection
For Visitors:
You do not have to provide us with any personally identifiable data to visit our website or support the campaign.
You can limit personal data collection by simply choosing not to supply us with such information.
For meta-data collected by cookies, please see the Note On Cookies, below.
Certain information or services can not be available if you determine to limit the information you share with us.
For example, if you select to request our newsletter and decide you do not want to share your information, then you do not have to share it.
You will not be able to access the personalized email newsletter but will be able to access the website-specific one.
SMS Opt-Out:
If you are receiving text messages from us and wish to stop receiving them, simply respond with either “STOP” or “UNSUBSCRIBE” to the number from which you received the message.
Once we receive your message, you will no longer receive further text messages from us.
Note on Cookies
Users can control the use of cookies at the individual browser level.
Your browser may offer you a “Do Not Track” option, which allows you to signal to operators of websites, web applications, and services that you do not wish such operators to track certain of your online activities over time and across different websites.
