Phil Fox is running for Missouri House of Representatives, District 2 — bringing nearly four decades in education, a lifetime in rural Missouri, and a commitment to conservative values to Jefferson City.
For the Farms, Schools, and Families of District 2.
ABOUT PHIL
Phil Fox grew up on a farm and has spent his career in public education; 20 years as an agricultural education and science teacher, followed by 19 years as a school administrator.
He calls Laredo home with his wife, Kristie, and takes great pride in their three grown daughters, each married and building lives of their own, along with their granddaughter who made him a grandfather.
Phil currently serves as superintendent of the Grundy County R-5 School District in Galt.
Phil is running because he believes the people of District 2 deserve a representative who knows this community, shares its values, and will fight for its generations to come.
CORE VALUES
- I believe strong families are the foundation of strong communities.
- I am committed to protecting life and supporting policies that reflect that belief.
- I will stand firm in defending our constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
- I proudly support our police officers and local sheriff’s departments who work every day to keep our communities safe.
key priorities
STRONG SUPPORT FOR LOCAL SCHOOLS

I will advocate for policies that strengthen our farms, improve rural roads and infrastructure, and support the livelihoods of our farming communities.
