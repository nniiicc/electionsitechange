Affordable Healthcare Healthcare should never be a luxury.
It is a basic human right.
Ohio is home to world-class hospitals, cutting-edge treatments, and innovative medical research — yet for too many families, access to care remains out of reach.
Skyrocketing premiums, high deductibles, and rising prescription drug costs force people to choose between paying rent and filling a prescription.
That is unacceptable.
I will fight to: Expand access to affordable coverage by fully funding and protecting Medicare, Medicaid, and CHIP.
Protect Medicaid expansion and ensure rural hospitals and community clinics remain open and fully staffed.
Lower prescription drug costs by increasing oversight of pharmacy benefit managers and ending unfair pricing practices.
Protect people with pre-existing conditions from discrimination by insurance companies.
Invest in mental health services, preventive care, and community health centers to strengthen care at the local level.
Recent threats to Medicaid funding put our entire healthcare system — especially rural communities — at risk.
When hospitals close or reduce services, costs rise for everyone.
Instead of cutting coverage, we should expand access and support the doctors, nurses, and healthcare professionals who keep our communities healthy.
Healthcare should work for patients — not corporations.
I support patient-centered reforms that stabilize costs, expand coverage, and move us toward universal, affordable care for every Ohioan.
Because no family should ever have to choose between their health and their livelihood.

I am proud to stand with Ohio’s workers and the unions that have long fought for fair wages, safe workplaces, and economic dignity.

Defend pensions and retirement security so workers can retire with dignity after a lifetime of hard work.
Fight for wages that keep up with the cost of living and ensure no one working full time lives in poverty.
Promote safe, fair workplaces with strong enforcement of labor standards.
Oppose efforts to weaken unions or roll back worker protections.

I also believe we must prepare Ohio’s workforce for the jobs of the future.
That means expanding access to job training, apprenticeship programs, and career technical education in high-demand industries like clean energy, advanced manufacturing, skilled trades, and technology.
We should partner with unions, community colleges, and local employers to ensure workers have clear pathways to good-paying careers.
A strong economy starts with strong workers.
By protecting labor rights and investing in workforce development, we can create an Ohio where hard work is respected, rewarded, and protected.
Because when workers thrive, Ohio thrives.

Common-Sense Gun Safety Gun violence is a public safety crisis — and Ohio families deserve real solutions, not political talking points.
Gun violence has become one of the leading causes of death for children and teens.
Yet instead of advancing practical safety measures, the Statehouse has passed laws like permitless concealed carry and policies that allow teachers to be armed with minimal training.
More guns in more places is not a strategy for safer communities.
We can protect constitutional rights while also enacting responsible safeguards.
As your State Representative, I will fight for: Red Flag laws (Extreme Risk Protection Orders) that allow courts to temporarily remove firearms from individuals who pose a threat to themselves or others.
Stronger accountability measures for gun owners , including safe storage requirements and liability standards.
Better tracking and enforcement to prevent firearms from falling into the hands of those legally prohibited from possessing them.
Evidence-based, data-driven policies that reduce violence while respecting responsible gun ownership.
Legislation requiring a 10-day waiting period for firearm purchases to help prevent impulsive acts of violence.
Protecting our children requires smart prevention strategies, community investment, and laws that reflect the overwhelming support of Ohioans for common-sense gun reforms.
This is not about partisanship — it’s about safety.
We can defend the rights of responsible gun owners while taking meaningful steps to reduce gun violence and protect our families.
District 45 are demanding action.
It’s time our leaders put public safety first.

District 45 thrives when our small businesses thrive.
