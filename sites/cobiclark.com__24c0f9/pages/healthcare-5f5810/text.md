The Problem
Healthcare gets more expensive when government blocks competition, limits new facilities, and buries patients under mandates and paperwork.
Cobi's Better Way
Repeal certificate-of-need laws that block new clinics.
Require price transparency so patients can compare costs.
Expand direct primary care and telehealth.
Encourage competition to lower prices and improve access.
Reduce barriers that limit providers and innovation.
Why it Matters
Patients deserve affordable care, faster access, and the freedom to choose what works for them.
Healthcare should work for patients, not gatekeepers.
