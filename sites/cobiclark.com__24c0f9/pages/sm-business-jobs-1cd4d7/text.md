Small Business & Jobs
Let people build, hire, and grow.
The Problem
Small businesses face licensing hurdles, regulations, and rising costs that make it harder to hire, grow, and keep more of what they earn.
Cobi's Better Way
Cut red tape that slows hiring and growth.
Simplify licensing and permitting.
Make it easier to start and expand a business.
Level the playing field for small businesses, vs. large corporations.
Support a competitive economy where jobs grow naturally.
Why it Matters
Strong local businesses create opportunity, competition, and resilience in our community.
The best jobs program is a freer economy.
