The Problem
Families are getting squeezed.
Rents are rising, homeownership is out of reach, and excessive regulations and delays are preventing enough housing from being built.
Cobi's Better Way
Cut Permitting delays and unnecessary local barriers.
Make it easier to build more housing of all types.
Reduce regulations that increase construction costs.
Limit building codes to safety – not costly mandates.
Protect property rights and allow communities to grow.
Why it Matters
Housing gets more affordable when supply can finally catch up with demand.
If we want lower housing costs, we need fewer roadblocks, faster permits, and more homes.
