Civil Rights
Individual liberty.
Equal protection.
Limited government.
The Problem
Government overreach and unequal enforcement threaten our freedoms, safety, and constitutional rights.
Cobi's Better Way
Uphold the right to keep and bear arms for every individual.
Protect free association and oppose compelled speech or participation.
Criminalize masked arrests and ensure accountable law enforcement.
Limit government to protecting rights, not managing outcomes.
Keep government neutral in matters of conscience and belief.
Ensure privacy, due process, and protection from abuse of power.
Why it Matters
A free society respects your rights, your choices, and your dignity – no matter who you are.
True equality means equal rights for all –
not special treatment for some.
