Accountability that matters.
The Problem Too many people are living on the streets while current policies cycle individuals through temporary solutions without addressing root causes.
Public spaces become unsafe, communities are strained, and those struggling with addiction or mental health challenges often don’t get the help they actually need.
Cobi's Better Way  Focus on mental health and addiction treatment as root causes.  Support community-based, faith-based, and private recovery programs.  Shift resources toward long-term recovery, not just temporary shelter.  Maintain safe and accessible public spaces for everyone.  Prioritize accountability and measurable outcomes in homelessness spending.
Why it Matters A better approach to homelessness improves public safety, restores dignity to those in need, and ensures resources actually help people rebuild their lives – not remain trapped in the system.
Real compassion means helping people recover, not leaving them stuck on the streets.
