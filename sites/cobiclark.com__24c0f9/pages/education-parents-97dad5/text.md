Education & Parents
Put students first and parents back in the driver’s seat.
The Problem
A one-size-fits-all system leaves too many students behind and puts politics ahead of learning and parents.
Cobi's Better Way
Expand flexible learning options and school choice.
Respect the role of parents in education decisions.
Reduce bureaucracy that pulls resources from classrooms.
Focus funding on outcomes and student success.
Ensure transparency and accountability in our schools.
Why it Matters
Families are different, students are different, and education should reflect that reality.
Education should fit the student,
not force the student to fit the system.
