Energy & Gas Prices
Affordable energy means affordable living –
and a stronger economy.
The Problem
When policy drives up costs, families pay more at the pump, businesses pay more to operate, and Washington falls behind.
Cobi's Better Way
Support reliable, abundant energy supply.
Remove barriers that drive up fuel and electricity costs.
Invest in nuclear, geothermal, and next-generation energy.
Stop policies that punish commuters and working families.
Let competition and innovation lower prices.
Why it Matters
Every dollar government wastes came from someone’s paycheck, rent, or grocery budget.
Affordable energy is not a luxury.
It is part of a strong Washington.
