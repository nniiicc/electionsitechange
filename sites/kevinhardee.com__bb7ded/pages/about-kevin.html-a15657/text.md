I am proud to call Horry County home.
I was born and raised here, graduated from Horry Georgetown Technical College in 1986, and have spent much of my life working to serve the community that has given so much to my family and me.
My public service began in 2001, when I was elected to the Horry County Council representing District 10.
I served on County Council until 2008, including service as Vice Chairman and Chairman of the Public Safety Committee.
I also served on the board of the Waccamaw Economic Opportunity Council.
In 2013, I was elected to represent District 105 in the South Carolina House of Representatives.
Since then, it has been an honor to represent the people of our area and work on the issues that matter to our families, businesses, and communities.
My legislative service has included being a former member of the Labor Commerce and Industry Committee where I served as Chairman of the Insurance Sub-Committee.
I am currently serving on the Ways and Means Committee and the Rules Committee. l also currently serve as Vice Chairman of The Grand Strand Area Transportation Study and a former board member of the NBSC/Synovus - Loris Branch.
Most importantly, l am a husband, father, and grandfather.
My wife, Tina, and I live on our family farm in Loris, and our family keeps me grounded in the values that have shaped my life: faith, family, hard work, and service to others.
