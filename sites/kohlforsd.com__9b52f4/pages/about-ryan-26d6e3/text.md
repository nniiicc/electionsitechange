top of page Home Updates Priorities & Issues About Ryan Donate About Me I was born and raised in Revillo, SD, the youngest of four boys.
Growing up on my family's farm, I learned the value of hard work, determination, and perseverance.
I graduated from Grant-Deuel School in Revillo and went on to serve in the South Dakota National Guard as a member of the 2/147th Field Artillery. ​ After graduating from South Dakota State University, I was commissioned into the United States Air Force and spent the next nine years on active duty serving as an Air Force Intelligence Officer.
While on active duty, I was stationed in Texas, Germany, and Hawaii, deploying three times in support of the Global War on Terrorism to Iraq, Afghanistan and the Southern Philippines. ​ After leaving active duty, I returned to South Dakota to work on the family farm, although I continued to serve in the Air Force Reserve until retiring in 2023.
With dual passions for agriculture and education, I earned a teaching certificate from SDSU and taught at Grant-Deuel for two years, while working on the family farm.
With the closure of Grant-Deuel, I still substitute teach, but have refocused my professional time to my farm, ranch, and rental property business. ​ In 2016, I married my Air Force sweetheart, Kimberly.
We have three children aged 4 to 8 and reside in Milbank, SD.
We attend Central Church in Milbank, where I teach Sunday School. ​ ​ Contact Follow Ryan Father.
