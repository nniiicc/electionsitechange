Early Voting for All
All registered voters* can vote early, with no excuse needed.
You can walk in, show your photo ID, and vote without any extra paperwork.
You can vote early at the Pickens County Voter Registration office at 222 McDaniel Ave in Pickens, or at our local early voting site:
Location: Central-Clemson Library, 105 Commons Way, Central, SC
Dates: October 19 - October 31 (you can vote on Saturdays but not on Sundays).
Times: 8:30 a.m. - 6 p.m.
If you are not available to vote during this time or on election day, or circumstances prevent you from voting in person, you can vote Absentee by Mail.
Please follow directions here to apply for and mail in your ballot.
You must apply for a mail-in ballot before Oct. 5.
Election Day is Tuesday, Nov. 3
If you do not choose to vote early, you can go to the ballot box on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3.
Find your voting location here.
Be sure to double-check where you vote, as this information may have changed from previous years.
You can vote in person on Election Day from 7 a.m. - 7 p.m.
Everyone in line before 7 p.m. will be allowed to vote.
Bring a photo ID.
