It’s going to take a large community to flip a seat in the state house – but we are confident that folks are ready for change.
If you want a new voice in Columbia and positive change for Pickens County, please support Claiborne’s campaign through volunteering your time, talents or treasure.
Donors
We'd be honored by a donation of any amount to support this campaign.
Donate online
If you’d like to save credit card processing fees and/or avoid party emails, we accept checks!
Checks can be made to “Linvill for SC House 3” and mailed to: Campaign Treasurer, 110 Brookwood Drive, Clemson, SC 29631.
For required reporting, please include your phone number, occupation and employer in the memo line.
Volunteers
We’re looking for volunteers interested in communications and design, canvassing/door-knocking, event planning, phone banks and more.
If you’d like to volunteer time or talent, please email LinvillforSCHouse3@gmail.com and we’ll find a fit for your skills and interest.
All ages welcome.
