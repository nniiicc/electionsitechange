“Every day, we see the chaos being brought to our communities by a President who wants to crush dissent, erase our history and destroy our democracy by undermining free and fair elections.
They are driving up costs, undermining working families and trying to sell off our public lands to the highest bidder.” Hoyle went on to say, “While they’re working against you, it’s more important than ever to have someone who will fight for you.
And that’s what I do every single day.” The most powerful tool we have to defend democracy is your vote and I hope to earn the support of the people of this district again to represent them in Congress.
“I’m running for re-election to send an unmistakable message that working people in this country come first,” said Val Hoyle.
