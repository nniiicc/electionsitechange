I've said for months that Kristi Noem should be impeached.
Yesterday, she was fired.

Taxpayer dollars should never be treated like a personal piggy bank for political allies or vanity projects.
She was never fit to serve, and I'm grateful to the many people who spoke out and organized against her abuses.
Your advocacy made a difference, from stopping an ICE facility on the Oregon Coast to demanding accountability nationwide.

So no matter who holds the title of "Secretary," we must keep holding them accountable and calling out their corruption.
