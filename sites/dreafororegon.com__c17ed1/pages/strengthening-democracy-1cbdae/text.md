Our democracy is facing threats we haven’t seen in generations.
Recent years have been marked by efforts by political leaders to stoke hatred, racism, and misogyny.
Failures of our system of checks and balances have also sounded an alarm.
I am committed to protecting our democracy and increasing access to the ballot box.
Ensuring equal access to voting and campaign finance reform are some of my top priorities.
