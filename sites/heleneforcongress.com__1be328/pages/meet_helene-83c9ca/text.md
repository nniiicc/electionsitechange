A Voice for the People.
Helene Neville is a nurse, public health leader, professional athlete, entrepreneur, author, mother, and grandmother, and is currently running as an independent candidate for North Dakota U.S.
Congress.
For over 42 years, she has dedicated herself to helping others—working in hospitals, communities, schools, and nationwide.
She has held federal public health leadership roles, responded to crises, and championed healthier communities.
She has built businesses.
She has traveled more than 16,679.70 miles on foot across North America, meeting people where they are and listening to their stories.
Her journey is built on compassion, perseverance, and service.
Helene Neville’s story is unlike any other — and she’s ready to bring that leadership to Washington.
