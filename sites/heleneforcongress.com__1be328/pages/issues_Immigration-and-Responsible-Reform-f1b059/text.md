Our current system is outdated and inefficient, leaving families in limbo and creating unnecessary backlogs in the legal process.
I support comprehensive immigration reform that strengthens a fair and efficient legal system.
That includes: Increasing resources to reduce immigration court backlogs and processing delays Expanding lawful immigration pathways for workers and families Providing stability for individuals brought to the United States as children through programs like DACA Maintaining a humane refugee and asylum system consistent with American values Immigration policy should reflect both the rule of law and the dignity of the people involved.
Our strength as a nation is not only measured by the laws we pass — but by the people we protect.
Our challenges around housing, food costs, and healthcare are driven by complex economic factors.
Blaming vulnerable people does not solve those problems.
Responsible policy does.
We are strongest when we expand opportunity, defend liberty, and treat others with humanity and fairness.
That is not rhetoric — that is responsibility.
North Dakota Immigration North Dakota has a long tradition of welcoming newcomers and relies on immigration to help address workforce shortages.
The state has experienced significant labor gaps across industries, including healthcare, agriculture, and energy.
To help meet these needs, North Dakota established the Office of Legal Immigration to assist businesses in recruiting and retaining foreign-born workers.
These efforts help support economic growth while strengthening communities across the state.
Immigration plays an important role in supporting North Dakota’s workforce and economic stability.
A balanced approach should support legal immigration pathways, workforce needs, and community integration while maintaining respect for the rule of law.
