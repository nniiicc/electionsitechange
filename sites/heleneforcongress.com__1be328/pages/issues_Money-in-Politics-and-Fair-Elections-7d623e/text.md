Citizens United and the Influence of Big Money The 2010 Supreme Court decision in Citizens United v.
Federal Election Commission allowed corporations and outside groups to spend unlimited amounts of money in elections.
Since then, super PACs and outside spending have dramatically increased the influence of large donors and special interests.
These groups can raise unlimited funds and spend heavily on advertising and messaging, often without full transparency about their sources of funding.
This makes it harder for voters to know who is trying to influence elections.
Transparency is essential.
Voters have the right to know who funds campaigns and how that money can influence policy decisions.
