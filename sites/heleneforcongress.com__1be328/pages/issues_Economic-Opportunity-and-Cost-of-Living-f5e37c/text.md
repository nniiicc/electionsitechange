Economic Opportunity and Cost of Living
North Dakotans indicate that escalating prices and higher spending are worsening their financial challenges.
Many families find it hard to afford everyday costs such as food, housing, and healthcare.
Some report delaying medical treatments, skipping meals, or falling behind on bills.
The median household income in North Dakota is approximately $69,090, yet rising costs for housing, food, healthcare, and utilities continue to strain family budgets.
North Dakota’s effective property tax rate is 0.94%, ranking 19th highest in the nation, adding additional pressure for homeowners.
Economic opportunities mean more than job growth - it means families can afford to live, save, and plan for their future.
We need policies that:
- Strengthening wages and workforce opportunities
- Reduce the cost of healthcare and prescription drugs
- Support affordable housing and childcare
- Promote fair markets for food and energy
A strong economy is not measured only by growth - it is measured by whether families can afford groceries, pay their bills, and build a stable future.
Economic policy should strengthen families, support workers, and create opportunities across North Dakota.
