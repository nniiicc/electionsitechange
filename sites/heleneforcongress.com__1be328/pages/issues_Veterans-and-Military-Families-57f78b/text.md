Veterans and Military Families
We must continue working to:
• Streamline access to Veterans Affairs services
• Reduce barriers to healthcare and mental health services
• Expand VA supportive housing programs
• Improve job training and employment opportunities
• Support military families and caregivers
Service to our country is deeply personal in my family.
Four of my siblings served in the military, three of them made it a career.
One sister served in the Gulf War.
I also remember the draft.
I was thirteen years old and saw the worry on my mother’s face as she feared my eldest brother might be called to serve.
That moment left a lasting impression on me.
For many veterans, some of their greatest battles begin when they return home.
We owe them more than gratitude — we owe them care, opportunity, and respect.
In 1995, I served as the Iowa Persian Gulf Study Group Liaison, participating in a population-based study to assess health conditions among military personnel deployed during the Gulf War.
This work reinforced my understanding of the long-term health impacts of military service.
North Dakota Veterans
North Dakota offers a range of benefits for service members, veterans, and their families, including:
• Educational benefits
• Property tax exemptions for disabled veterans
• Veterans’ preference in public employment
• Veterans aid loan programs
• Grant programs
• Transportation assistance
These programs are important, but we must continue strengthening support for veterans across
North Dakota.
Veterans are not just part of our past — they are part of our communities, our workforce, and our future.
Supporting veterans means honoring their service with action — not just words.
