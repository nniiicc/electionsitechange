More than 40 years ago, I met a loving, humble, compassionate, and truly extraordinary young woman named Helene Quintal — now Helene Neville.
Over the decades, I have watched her grow into an incredible mother, grandmother, humanitarian, friend, visionary, and one of the bravest and most resilient people I have ever known.
It is difficult to fully put our friendship into words — you simply had to know her to understand the impact she has on people.
We first met at work in positions that mainly involved sorting and distributing reports.
Somehow, Helene made even that fun.
She never seemed concerned about titles or recognition because she always had bigger dreams and aspirations — especially running.
Every year, our company competed in the Corporate Cup Relays against other major companies, and Helene was one of our star runners.
She competed with determination and heart, helping our team secure victories year after year.
As time went on, Helene left the company to begin building her life and family.
My husband and I were honored to attend her wedding reception — my husband even served as the DJ.
I still laugh remembering her special song request: “Let It Whip.” We danced all night and had an unforgettable time.
Over the years, Helene never stopped challenging herself or inspiring those around her.
From bodybuilding, to coaching youth basketball with my husband, to climbing mountains, to running marathons, her adventures never seemed to end.
One of the accomplishments I was most proud to witness was when she organized the very first Des Moines Marathon almost single-handedly.
She created a beautiful course showcasing the city’s landmarks and attracted runners from across the country and around the world.
It was an incredible event and a reflection of who Helene is — someone who pours her entire heart into everything she does.
I have also been blessed to know and love her family.
Her mother and stepfather treated my family as their own, and I will never forget their kindness and generosity.
When my first husband passed away in 2008, Helene traveled to be there for me during one of the most difficult times in my life.
Her presence, support, and comforting spirit meant more than words can express.
Later, when I remarried, she welcomed my new husband with the same kindness and respect she had always shown my first husband.
That is simply who Helene is — loyal, compassionate, and full of grace.
Through every valley and challenge, especially her health battles, Helene has remained transparent, faithful, and inspiring to countless people.
Her willingness to share her journey has touched more lives than she will ever know.
Her strength, perseverance, and faith are a true testament to her character.
Her mother would be incredibly proud of the woman she has become because Helene carries the very same loving and giving heart.
Keep rethinking the impossible, my friend.
I cherish our friendship and pray that God continues to protect and guide you every step of the way.
Until next time… “Let It Whip!”
— Leona Cochran
Leona Cochran
