It’s getting worse.
Social Security is not a handout.
It is an earned program funded by workers and employers throughout their entire careers.
Calling it a benefit suggests that recipients did not earn it, when in fact they supported the program year after year.
Words matter.
When officials use language that minimizes Social Security, it weakens public understanding of the commitment our nation made to its workers.
These programs must be protected and described accurately.
Statespersons know that it is Social Security.
Our current leaders are politicians who are the ones who view it as a federal benefit.
Current projections suggest the program might encounter funding shortages within the next decade if no action is taken.
That means policymakers must act responsibly to protect benefits and strengthen the program for future generations.
We must defend and enhance this vital program.
I support responsible reforms that: Protect benefits for current retirees Strengthen long-term funding Ask the wealthiest Americans to contribute fairly Preserve retirement security for future generations Workers who contribute to Social Security throughout their careers deserve assurance that the program will be there when they retire.
Retirement security also means protecting pensions.
After decades of hard work, Americans deserve to receive the retirement benefits they earned.
A secure retirement should not be uncertain.
After a lifetime of work, Americans deserve stability, dignity, and peace of mind.
North Dakota Social Security Social Security is crucial to North Dakota’s economy and community stability.
Over 146,000 residents receive benefits, amounting to around $3 billion each year.
These benefits help seniors remain independent, assist families, and strengthen local economies — particularly in rural areas.
Protecting Social Security isn't solely about numbers.
It’s about honoring the promise we made to workers, seniors, and families.
