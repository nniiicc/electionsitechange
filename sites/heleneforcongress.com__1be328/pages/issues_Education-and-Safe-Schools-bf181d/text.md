When schools partner with health providers, counselors, and career services, families can more easily access the support they need to keep students healthy, engaged, and on track.
Supporting educators is equally important.
To attract and retain the best teachers, we must ensure they are paid fairly and have the resources they need to succeed in the classroom.
I support: Investments in education for disadvantaged students and students with disabilities Strong funding for teacher professional development Expanded Pell Grants and affordable pathways to higher education Strengthening community colleges and career training programs Education is one of the most important investments we can make in the future of our communities and our country.
North Dakota Education In North Dakota, schools play a vital role in rural communities.
When schools struggle, communities feel the impact.
Teacher shortages, funding challenges, and safety concerns affect students, families, and local economies.
North Dakota ranks 40th in the nation for teacher salaries, making it more difficult to attract and retain educators.
We must work to strengthen teacher recruitment, retention, and support.
Students also need access to nutrition, mental health support, and safe learning environments.
Hungry children do not learn, and safe schools are essential for student success.
Investing in education strengthens communities, supports families, and prepares the next generation for success.
