Scams that Swindle
Scammers rely on one thing: trust.
They create relationships, impersonate authority figures, and use emotional pressure to convince people to act quickly — before they have time to think.
STOP.
Pause.
Verify.
There are countless scams circulating today, including:
• Romance and online dating scams
• Sweepstakes and lottery scams
• Tech support scams
• IRS or government impersonation scams
• Grandparent emergency scams
• Work-from-home schemes
• Investment and Ponzi scams
• Scholarship and inheritance scams
Social media scams are also on the rise.
Fraudsters often create fake profiles claiming to work overseas, in the military, on oil rigs, or with international organizations.
They build trust and then request money for travel, emergencies, or documents.
North Dakota Scam Concerns
North Dakota residents are increasingly being targeted by phone and internet scams.
Rural communities and seniors are especially vulnerable.
Scammers often use "spoofing" technology to make calls appear to come from local 701 numbers or trusted institutions.
Recent reports in North Dakota include individuals losing thousands of dollars to arrest warrant scams and online relationship fraud.
These scams are not just financial crimes — they are a public safety and public health concern.
We must:
• Increase fraud awareness and education
• Strengthen consumer protections
• Improve law enforcement coordination
• Support seniors and vulnerable populations
• Hold scammers accountable
Protecting North Dakotans from fraud is about protecting families, savings, and peace of mind.
No one should lose their life savings to a scam.
We must ensure that North Dakotans are informed, protected, and supported.
PLEASE BE AWARE!
