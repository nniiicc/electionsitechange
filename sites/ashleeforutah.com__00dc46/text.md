ASHLEE MATTHEWS STANDS FOR WORKING FAMILIES I’M ASHLEE MATTHEWS, AND I’M PROUD TO REPRESENT THE HARDWORKING FAMILIES OF HOUSE DISTRICT 37.
Every year, more Utahns tell me the same thing: they’ve lost trust in politicians.
Honestly, I understand why.
The good news is, I’m not a career politician.
I’m a mom.
I’m a wife.
I’m a state employee.
And I’m your neighbor.
I know what it’s like to balance work, family, and a budget that never seems to stretch quite far enough.
I understand the challenges of raising children while helping care for aging parents.
I know what it means to worry about rising costs, crowded roads, and whether the opportunities that built Utah will still be here for the next generation.
Representative government works best when the people making decisions understand the realities of everyday life— not because they’ve read about them, but because they’re living them.
We need representatives who know what it’s like to sit at the kitchen table and figure out how to pay the bills.
Legislators who understand the transportation and infrastructure challenges facing the West Side.
Policymakers who are committed to protecting both Utah jobs and Utah’s natural resources.
There’s an old saying: “If you’re not at the table, you’re on the menu.” That’s why I’ve worked hard to make sure the voices of District 37 are heard at the Capitol.
During my time in the Legislature, I’ve stayed focused on the issues that matter most to our families and our communities—and I’ve built a record of results to prove it.
This November, I’m asking for your support so I can continue fighting for the people I serve and continue being a voice – for US.
