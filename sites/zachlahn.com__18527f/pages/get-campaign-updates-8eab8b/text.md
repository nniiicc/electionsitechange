Help Zach Win Iowa for You .
Every policy decision should start with one question: What will this change do to our community?
That question has been missing from politics for too long, and it’s the foundation of Zach’s Iowa First approach: think long-term, protect what makes Iowa strong, and put our communities first.
Iowa First Values We need to be able to draw the line.
Protect life.
Put Iowa families first.
End benefits for illegal immigrants.
Keep politics out of our schools and stop letting partisan nonsense redefine the values our communities were built on.
Make Iowa Healthy Again Too many Iowa families have a cancer story.
Zach’s published plan calls for independent safety testing of agricultural products, more Iowa-focused cancer research, and additional action on improving our water quality.
Keep Iowa Land in Iowa Hands Who owns Iowa soil twenty years from now matters.
A quarter of our land is owned by people who don’t live in Iowa – investors who treat Iowa like an annuity.
Zach will require human-level disclosure of who owns our land, tighten and enforce the laws to stop hedge funds and foreign adversaries from buying up our farms and homes, and give young Iowans a fair shot at owning a home and building a farm.
Make Education #1 Again Parents should have access to excellent schools.
Zach’s education plans include giving teachers more room to innovate, reducing reliance on standardized testing, strengthening public schools, and directing Iowa universities toward Iowa students and Iowa problems.
Keep Iowa’s Kids in Iowa Our kids should not feel like they have to leave Iowa to find opportunity.
Like many Iowa kids, Zach left, then came home.
His goal is to make sure the next generation never has to leave to find opportunity.
That means homes young families can afford, farms young Iowans can start, schools that are number one again, and an Iowa that puts Iowans first.
Count On Clean Water Clean water should be something every Iowan can count on.
Zach’s plan invests in water monitoring, targeted conservation, and proven solutions like wetlands, saturated buffers, and cover crops—while working with farmers, not against them.
Improve Affordability A great Iowa life should still be attainable.
Zach’s platform includes lowering the property tax burden on Iowans, expanding paths to homeownership, and banning hedge funds from buying single-family Iowa homes.
Expand Food Freedom Make Iowa the country’s real food capital.
Zach wants to make it easier for Iowa farmers and producers to sell directly to Iowa families and create innovation incentives for farmers who diversify into food production.
