Villages in the 52nd District,such as Wauconda, Island Lake, Volo, South Barrington, Fox River Grove and Barrington all have been promoting local fall festivals in order to drive customers to their downtown businesses.
Please partake in these great events and check your community’s calendars for upcoming events.
Our great main-street businesses are still coming back from Covid lockdowns and need our support more than ever.
Please join me at Volo Fest on September 17th, Oktoberfest in Island Lake on September 23 & 24, the Norge Ski Club’s Jumptober Fest in Fox River Grove, and many more fall events in our local region.
