Meet Jubilee → Explore the Issues → On Your November Ballot: Workers’ Rights and Equal Protection This November, Vermonters have the opportunity to strengthen our Constitution with two important protections.
I support voting YES on both Proposal 3 and Proposal 4.
Proposal 3: Protecting Workers’ Rights Working people deserve a voice in the decisions that affect their livelihoods.
Proposal 3 would protect employees’ right to organize and bargain collectively with their employer in the Vermont Constitution.
I support this amendment because workers deserve the power to stand together for fair wages, safer workplaces, and dignity on the job.
Proposal 4: Protecting Equal Rights Everyone deserves equal protection under the law.
Proposal 4 would explicitly prohibit the State from denying equal treatment based on race, ethnicity, sex, religion, disability, sexual orientation, gender identity, gender expression, or national origin.
It would also preserve the ability to adopt measures that address discrimination against historically marginalized groups.
Vote YES on Proposal 3 and Proposal 4 on November 3, 2026.
At a time when people’s rights are under attack, Vermont must strengthen its commitment to protecting everyone’s dignity and freedom to live peacefully and thrive.

Children and Families I introduced and helped lead the passage of H.657, now Act 136.
