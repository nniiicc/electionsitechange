I am honored to be endorsed by the Washington State Council of Firefighters.
I have worked alongside firefighters and EMS throughout my career.

I also know their workload is about to grow.
As rural clinics and hospitals close, wildfires increase, and behavioral and mental health needs go unmet, more of those calls land on our fire and EMS crews.
That will keep getting worse unless we push back on what is happening both federally and in Olympia.
Thank you to the firefighters and the whole WSCFF team for trusting me to carry your voice to Olympia and push back.

Beyond grateful that Transportation for Washington has endorsed my campaign for the 39th Legislative District!
In Snohomish and Skagit counties, getting around is about more than a commute.
It's whether a senior can reach a medical appointment, whether a worker can get to a job, ... whether a student can get to school safely, and whether emergency responders can reach us quickly.
For people with disabilities, reliable and accessible transportation can be the difference between independence and isolation.
And in our rural communities, where distances are long and options are few, it can decide whether people can fully take part in community life.
Every person in our district deserves safe, reliable, accessible ways to get where they need to go.
Thank you to Transportation for Washington for your trust.
I'll bring my budgeting experience and my commitment to working across agencies and communities to make sure the disability community and rural communities have a voice in Olympia.
