She believes the best solutions come from focusing on what families and communities actually need—not partisan politics.
In Olympia, Ida will bring that same independent, practical approach to the issues she hears about every day from people across our district.
MAKING LIFE MORE AFFORDABLE Families and seniors across our district are feeling squeezed by the rising cost of housing, childcare, healthcare, insurance, groceries, and other everyday necessities.
Too many people are wondering whether they will be able to afford to stay in the communities they call home.
Ida believes Olympia needs to make affordability a priority.
She will work to expand access to affordable housing and childcare, lower healthcare and prescription drug costs, and protect programs that give working families greater economic security.
Ida also believes affordability requires responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
Throughout her career, she has managed complex public programs and multimillion-dollar budgets, and she will bring that same focus on fiscal responsibility, transparency, and accountability to Olympia.
STRONG SCHOOLS, STRONG FAMILIES & MORE PATHWAYS TO OPPORTUNITY For decades, Ida has worked to protect vulnerable children and strengthen families.
She knows that giving every child the opportunity to succeed starts with safe and stable families, strong public schools, and support before families reach a crisis.
Ida will fight to fully fund our public schools and special education and ensure educators and school staff have the resources they need to help every student succeed.
She also supports strong early learning and affordable childcare because families should not have to choose between going to work and finding safe, quality care for their children.
And Ida knows there is no single path to success.
She will work to expand career and technical education, registered apprenticeships, skilled trades, workforce training, and college opportunities so young people can choose the path that works for them and local employers can find the skilled workers they need.
SAFE, HEALTHY COMMUNITIES Throughout her career in child welfare and juvenile justice, Ida has worked alongside law enforcement, courts, prosecutors, victim advocates, behavioral health providers, first responders, and families.
She has seen firsthand that lasting public safety requires both accountability and prevention.
Ida supports adequately staffed, well-trained law enforcement and first responders and will work to ensure they have the resources necessary to keep our growing communities safe.
She also supports investments in behavioral health and substance-use treatment, victim services, youth intervention, and programs that address problems before they become crises.
Ida does not believe supporting law enforcement and demanding accountability are competing goals.
Both are essential to building public trust, supporting victims, and creating communities where everyone feels safe.
GOOD JOBS & STRONG LOCAL ECONOMIES Ida believes economic development should create good-paying jobs, strengthen local businesses, and improve the quality of life in the communities where development occurs.
She supports workers’ right to organize and bargain collectively and believes people who work hard should be able to support themselves and their families.
She will support strong labor standards, apprenticeships and workforce development, and investments that create family-wage jobs here at home.
Ida will also fight for the infrastructure our growing communities need—from roads and transportation to schools, utilities, fire and emergency services.
She will work with labor, local employers, small businesses, and community leaders to expand economic opportunity and ensure Snohomish and Skagit County communities remain great places to live, work, and raise a family.
COMMUNITY VOICE & RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT Our communities deserve a meaningful voice in decisions that affect their neighborhoods, natural resources, and quality of life.
New technology and development can create opportunity, but economic development should strengthen our communities—not come at their expense.
As communities in our district navigate proposed data centers, Ida believes residents deserve a seat at the table and transparent information about potential impacts on electricity costs, water, infrastructure, farmland, the environment, and surrounding neighborhoods.
Ida supports a pause on new high-capacity data center development until stronger safeguards are in place to protect ratepayers, natural resources, and surrounding communities.
The same standard should apply to battery storage facilities proposed in our area.
Neighbors deserve to understand potential fire and safety risks, environmental impacts, and siting decisions before projects move forward—not after.
Artificial intelligence itself is advancing faster than many of the laws intended to govern it.
Ida believes Washington needs enforceable standards and real accountability to protect workers’ livelihoods, personal privacy, consumers, and community safety as these technologies continue to develop.
Ida also believes large corporations—not local families and small businesses—should bear the costs of the infrastructure their developments require.
She will support transparency, strong safeguards, responsible development, and meaningful local decision-making so our communities can benefit from economic growth without sacrificing the resources and quality of life that make them special.
INDEPENDENT LEADERSHIP THAT PUTS OUR COMMUNITIES FIRST Ida isn’t running to serve a political party.
She’s running to serve the people and communities of this district.
Throughout her career, she has worked with law enforcement, educators, Tribes, military families, courts, state and local governments, nonprofits, and people with very different perspectives to solve complicated problems.
She knows that progress happens when people listen to one another, find common ground, and stay focused on results.
That’s the approach Ida will bring to Olympia.
She will work with anyone willing to put community needs first, protect taxpayer dollars, and deliver results for local families.
