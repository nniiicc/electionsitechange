Real Results for Our Communities.
For more than 25 years, Ida Keeley has been trusted to manage public programs, oversee multimillion-dollar budgets, lead teams, and bring people together to solve complicated problems.
Now she’s running for State Representative to bring that experience to Olympia and make government work better for the people it serves.
ACCOUNTABILITY.
FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY.
RESULTS.
Ida knows leadership is about more than having good ideas.
It’s about managing responsibly, asking tough questions, holding people accountable, bringing different perspectives to the table, and delivering results.
Throughout her career at the local, county, and state levels, Ida has: Managed public budgets, federal grants, and protected taxpayer resources.
Led large teams and complex public programs.
Built strong, effective teams, while holding employees accountable.
Brought government agencies, community organizations, Tribes, nonprofits, and families together to solve difficult problems.
Helped build and lead organizations serving children and families.
Served on and led nonprofit, community, and statewide boards.
Worked directly on legislation and helped turn good ideas into policy.
Built a reputation for being accessible, collaborative, effective and focused on results.
Obtained and maintained accreditation and/or passed State and Federal audits to agencies under her direction.
Ida is an Independent Democrat who believes the people of the 39th District deserve a representative who will put their communities ahead of partisan politics.
She’ll bring the same approach to Olympia that she’s brought to her career: listen, bring people together, manage responsibly, and get things done.
