Data Centers A VISION FOR PENNSYLVANIA With decades of real-world experience leading businesses, performing under pressure, and building practical solutions, Rich Szabo knows how to get results.
He believes government should serve the people, not burden them. ​ As State Representative, Rich will fight to lower taxes, restore accountability in our schools, protect constitutional rights, support law enforcement, and put Pennsylvania families and local communities first. ​ Rich also believes major development, including data centers, warehouse expansion, and large-scale solar projects, must be done properly.
Residents deserve a voice before decisions are made, and taxpayers should not be left paying the price for rushed approvals or weak planning. ​ Join Rich in bringing practical solutions, local accountability, and principled leadership back to state government.
Rich Szabo is a lifelong advocate for common-sense conservative values, entrepreneurship, and strong communities.
As a small business owner and community leader in Pennsylvania, Rich has spent his career solving problems, creating jobs, and standing up for what works.
Now, he’s running for State Representative in the 115th District to bring that same practical approach to Harrisburg.
Rich's Bio PRIORITIES Rich is committed to bringing common-sense leadership to Harrisburg to strengthen our education system, protect the place we call home, and support working families through a stronger economy.
These priorities come directly from the needs and values of District 115 residents.
Growth Done Right Protecting Communities from Poor Planning District 115 is facing serious pressure from data centers, warehouse expansion, and large-scale solar projects.
Rich believes growth should not be rushed, rubber-stamped, or forced into places where it creates long-term problems for residents. ​ Major projects must be judged by their impact on roads, water, stormwater, noise, emergency services, utility demand, property owners, and taxpayers.
Development should fit the community, not force the community to fit the development. ​ Rich will fight for local control, stronger public notice, full impact reviews, and enforceable standards that put residents first.
Education Putting Students and Parents First Education isn’t political to me — it’s personal.
I believe parents should have a real voice in what their children are being taught.
Our schools should focus on strong academic fundamentals, practical life skills, and preparing students for whatever path they choose — college, the trades, the military, or the workforce.
We need to support our teachers, expand career and technical education, and make sure funding actually reaches the classroom.
Our kids deserve opportunity, not bureaucracy.
Environment Protecting What Makes District 115 Home Living here, I understand how much our natural surroundings matter.
Our lakes, forests, and open spaces aren’t just scenery — they’re part of our way of life. ​ I believe in responsible stewardship that protects clean water and preserves our environment for the next generation.
We can conserve our natural resources while still respecting property rights and supporting local jobs.
Environmental policy should be practical, balanced, and rooted in common sense — not mandates that hurt working families.
Economy Supporting Working Families and Small Businesses As a small business owner, I understand how hard it is to meet payroll, manage regulations, and keep the doors open.
Government shouldn’t make that harder. ​ I will fight for lower taxes, predictable regulations, and responsible spending so families can keep more of what they earn.
A strong economy doesn’t come from growing government — it comes from empowering workers, entrepreneurs, and local businesses to succeed. ​ When our small businesses thrive, our entire community thrives.
