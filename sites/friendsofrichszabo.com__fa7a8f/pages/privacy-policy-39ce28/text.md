Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 02/26/2026
Friends of Rich Szabo (“Campaign,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting any information you provide through this website.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded.
Information We Collect
Information You Provide Voluntarily
We may collect personal information when you:
- Sign up for updates
- Submit a contact form
- Volunteer
- Make a donation
- Subscribe to email or text communications
Information collected may include:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Donation details (processed securely through third-party providers)
Automatically Collected Information
When you visit this website, we may automatically collect certain technical data, including:
- IP address
- Browser type
- Device type
- Pages visited
- Date and time of visit
This information helps us improve site functionality and user experience.
How We Use Your Information
We may use collected information to:
- Respond to inquiries
- Send campaign updates
- Coordinate volunteers
- Process donations
- Improve website performance
- Comply with Pennsylvania campaign finance reporting requirements
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
Donations & Legal Compliance
Online donations are processed through secure third-party payment platforms.
We do not store full credit card numbers on this website.
Donor information may be retained and reported as required by Pennsylvania election law.
Cookies & Tracking
This website may use cookies or analytics tools to improve user experience and measure website performance.
You may disable cookies through your browser settings.
Some features of the website may not function properly if cookies are disabled.
Third-Party Links
This website may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.
Data Security
We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect personal information.
However, no method of online transmission is completely secure.
Children’s Privacy
This website is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
We do not knowingly collect information from minors.
Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
The updated version will reflect a new effective date.
