A New Voice for New Hampshire
I'm running for State Representative because I believe it's time to bring the next generation of Granite Staters into the conversation.
As a long-time Durham resident, I'll fight for strong public schools, affordable healthcare and housing, environmental protection, and a government that listens.
I'm ready to serve every member of our community with energy, compassion, and a commitment to putting people first.
Quick Facts About Siena
- Has lived in Durham for 15 years.
- Studies political science and international affairs at the University of New Hampshire (and minors in Italian studies).
- Is the Chair of the UNH College Democrats, Vice-Chair of the NH College Democrats, and a executive member of the Strafford County Democrats.
