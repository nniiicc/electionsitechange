Leadership &
Community Impact
Mayor Hull has dedicated his life to public service, with an unwavering commitment to improving Rio Rancho and New Mexico for all who call it home.
Elected Office
- Mayor, City of Rio Rancho (2014–2026) — Longest-serving mayor in Rio Rancho's history, leading the city for more than a quarter of its existence.
Statewide & Regional Leadership
- New Mexico Municipal League — President (2025–2026); Vice President (2023–2024); Director (2017–2019)
- Mid-Region Council of Governments — Chairman (2018–2020, 2024–2026); Vice Chairman (2016–2018, 2022–2024)
- Founder & Chairman, New Mexico Mayors Caucus (2015–2024)
- Chief Elected Official & Board Member, New Mexico Workforce Connection (2017–2026)
- Board Member, New Mexico Metropolitan Transportation Board (2017–2026)
- Board Member, New Mexico State Workforce Development Board (2017–2019) — gubernatorial appointment
- Trustee, New Mexico Self Insurers Fund (2021–2026)
- Honorary Commander, Kirtland Air Force Base (2016, 2024) — selected twice; paired in 2016 with the commander of the 377th Air Base Wing, Kirtland's host wing
