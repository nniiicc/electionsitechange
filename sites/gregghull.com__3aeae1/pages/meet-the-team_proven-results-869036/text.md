Proven Leadership.
Gregg Hull has a record of getting things done.
As Mayor of Rio Rancho, he helped deliver results in job creation, infrastructure, water, public safety, and accountable government.
Now he’s running for Governor to bring that same results-driven leadership to New Mexico.
Gregg’s Record As Mayor The graphics below highlight key results from Gregg Hull’s time leading Rio Rancho.
Gregg helped Rio Rancho attract major private investment, support high-paying jobs, and earn national recognition as a place to start and grow a business.
Gregg helped lead major infrastructure improvements, including road projects, residential road repairs, citywide fiber optic installation, and new community assets like a senior center, library, and Campus Park.
Rio Rancho earned national recognition for livability, affordability, family life, retirement, and good governance during Gregg Hull’s time as Mayor.
Gregg helped bring Rio Rancho’s aquifer recharge system online, replace aging water lines, improve the utility’s bond rating, and invest in long-term water infrastructure without adding unnecessary debt.
Public safety was a top priority under Gregg Hull’s leadership.
Rio Rancho added officers, expanded support programs, and remained one of New Mexico’s safest cities for more than a decade.
Gregg Hull’s record includes clean audits, responsible budgeting, a permanent fund, and national recognition for well-managed city government.
