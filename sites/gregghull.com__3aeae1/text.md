Proven Leadership For New Mexico
Gregg Hull is the proven leader Rio Rancho counts on.
A longtime resident, entrepreneur, and family man, Hull has taken on the big challenges to improve public safety, grow the economy, invest in infrastructure and increase the quality of life for Rio Rancho Residents.
Gregg wants the same success story for New Mexico, and he is ready to work together with all New Mexicans to write our next chapter.
Combined with David Gallegos’ extensive legislative experience and deep understanding of New Mexico's regulatory landscape, this ticket offers a stable, results-oriented alternative for our state's future.
