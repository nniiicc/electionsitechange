Volunteers help voters learn who Heather is, what she is fighting for, and how to vote.
Reach More People A sign or mailer helps.

Build a Better Campaign This campaign should feel local because local people built it.

Volunteer Roles What Volunteer Work Looks Like Pick the work that fits your time and comfort level.
Some roles involve talking with voters.
Some do not.
Both matter.
General Questions What if I am not sure what I want to do?
Choose “I’m Not Sure Yet” on the form.
A volunteer will follow up, ask what sounds comfortable, and help you pick a first task.
What if I only have an hour?
That is enough.
Local campaigns need one-hour helpers, one-day helpers, and regular volunteers.
Start with what you have.
Do I need experience?
No.
The campaign will train you.
If you care about Ward 5 and want Heather to win, there is useful work for you.
Talking to People (In-Person) What does knocking doors mean?
You visit voters in Ward 5 with a short script and campaign literature.
You introduce Heather, ask what issues matter to the voter, and record basic responses for the campaign.
New volunteers get training before they start.
Do I have to argue with people at the door?
No.
The goal is a respectful conversation, not a debate.
If someone is busy, uninterested, or upset, you thank them and move on.
What does helping at events mean?
You help with setup, sign-in, greeting people, handing out materials, answering basic questions, or cleaning up.
You do not need to be the loudest person in the room.
What does hosting a meet-and-greet mean?
You invite a small group of friends, neighbors, coworkers, or family to meet Heather.
The campaign helps with the plan, talking points, and follow-up.
What does holding signs mean?
You stand with campaign signs at approved locations or events.
Some people wave.
Some people stand quietly and smile.
Both count.
What does dropping literature mean?
You leave campaign literature at doors.
You usually do not knock or talk with voters.
This is a good role if you want to walk and help without starting conversations.
Talking to People (Remotely) What does making calls involve?
You call voters from a campaign list.
The campaign gives you a script, a goal for the call, and a way to record responses.
Calls help reach voters who are harder to contact at the door.
What does sending texts involve?
You send campaign-approved messages to voters or supporters.
Texting helps remind people about events, voting information, volunteer shifts, and Heather’s campaign.
Helping in Other Ways What does filming a video endorsement mean?
You record a short video saying why you support Heather.
The campaign may use it online, with your permission, to help other voters hear from real Ward 5 supporters.
What does putting up yard signs mean?
You help deliver, place, or track yard signs for supporters who requested them.
The campaign will give you locations and instructions.
What does data entry mean?
You help enter voter responses, volunteer signups, event notes, or other campaign information.
This helps the campaign follow up with the right people.
What does design or writing mean?
You help with graphics, emails, social posts, flyers, website updates, or other campaign materials.
The campaign will give you a clear task and review before anything goes public.
What does research mean?
You help find useful information for the campaign.
That might mean checking voting information, local issues, public records, event opportunities, or press contacts.
What does sending a letter to the editor mean?
You write a short letter to a local paper about why you support Heather or why an issue matters in Ward 5.
The campaign can help you choose a topic and keep it within the paper’s word limit.
