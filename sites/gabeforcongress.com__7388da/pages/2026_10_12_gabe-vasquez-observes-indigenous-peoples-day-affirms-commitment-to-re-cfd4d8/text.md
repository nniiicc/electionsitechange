MAGA candidate Greg Cunningham has been too busy putting Trump first to acknowledge them.
Las Cruces, NM – Today, Democratic candidate and incumbent Gabe Vasquez recognized Indigenous Peoples’ Day by reaffirming his commitment to respecting Tribal sovereignty, upholding our nation’s trust and treaty responsibilities, and delivering results for the seven sovereign Tribal nations he represents in Congress.
“As the representative of New Mexico’s 2nd district, I have the honor of representing and being a partner to seven sovereign nations in Congress — and it’s a responsibility I take seriously,” said Democratic candidate and incumbent Gabe Vasquez .
“Tribal sovereignty must be upheld, and our Tribal communities deserve a federal government that adheres to its treaties and responsibilities and delivers on promises made — from water rights to education to healthcare to public safety, I’m fighting for more investment in Tribal communities.
On Indigenous Peoples’ Day, I’m proud to stand with New Mexico’s Pueblos and Tribes and will keep fighting alongside them to make sure they are well-represented in Washington.” Vasquez has delivered real results for New Mexico’s Tribes and Pueblos and is leading the fight to hold the government accountable to its treaty obligations and deliver a better standard of living for Indian County : Leading the fight in Congress to protect Tribal sovereignty and defend New Mexico’s Tribes and Pueblos from rogue prediction markets that infringe on Tribal revenue Delivered over $4 million directly to Indian Country for public safety, housing, and clean water through Community Project Funding $2,900,000 for Pueblo of Acoma to build new housing for seniors $1,092,000 for Ramah Navajo to protect and improve drinking water for the entire community $900,000 to help Isleta Pueblo combat wildfires Delivered increased funding to hold the Department of Defense accountable for the legacy of military testing and unexploded ordnance on Tribal lands Fighting alongside New Mexico’s Tribal communities to deliver their long-overdue water settlements, securing a milestone hearing to advance this critical legislation Leading the effort to better education in Indian Country with the Parity for Tribal Educators Act Leading the Parity for Tribal Law Enforcement Act to improve public safety in Indian Country While Gabe has been busy showing up, listening, and delivering for New Mexico’s Tribal communities, Cunningham has been too busy doing Trump’s bidding to acknowledge them or even speak about Tribal issues on the record.
Gabe has proudly been endorsed by the Mescalero Apache Tribe.
To date, he has also earned the endorsements of AFGE Local 1050; AFT New Mexico; Brady PAC; The Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers (BLET), Committee of Interns and Residents of New Mexico; AFSCME Council 18; New Mexico Carpenters Local 1319; CWA Unidos Local 7076; Education Votes; Equality PAC; Giffords PAC; Human Rights Campaign; IBEW Local 611; Latino Victory; League of Conservation Voters; Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate; MoveOn; National Wildlife Action Fund; NRDC Action Fund; National Committee to Preserve Social Security and Medicare; New Mexico State Council of Machinists; New Mexico Federation of Labor; New Mexico Professional Fire Fighters Association; New Mexico State College Dems; The Next 50; Organizers in the Land of Enchantment (OLÉ); Repro Freedom For All; Semilla Action; Sierra Club; SMART Local 49; Stop Gun Violence PAC; and Young Democrats of New Mexico.
Gabe is the only Democrat to have won New Mexico’s 2nd Congressional District twice consecutively.
In 2022, he beat incumbent Yvette Herrell by the tightest margin in the country (0.7%).
Last cycle, Gabe again defeated Yvette Herrell, this time by 4%— the same year that Donald Trump won the district by 2% — making him one of just 14 Democrats to win a district that went for Trump in 2024.
Gabe has continued to win over Democrat, Republican and Independent voters because he is willing to work with anyone to deliver results for New Mexicans on the issues they care most about: lowering the cost of living, expanding access to health care, protecting our public lands, and fixing our broken immigration system.
In November, he will set another historic record by winning New Mexico’s 2nd Congressional District for a third consecutive cycle.
Voters can learn more about the campaign and Gabe’s record by visiting www.gabeforcongress.com .
