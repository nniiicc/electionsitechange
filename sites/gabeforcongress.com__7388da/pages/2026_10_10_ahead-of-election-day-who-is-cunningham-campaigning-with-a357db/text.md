October 10, 2026 As MAGA donors pour millions into the race for NM-02, House Speaker Mike Johnson is set to headline fundraiser for MAGA extremist Greg Cunningham in Las Cruces on Oct.
12 Las Cruces, NM – On Monday, October 12, House Speaker Mike Johnson will return to New Mexico’s 2nd Congressional District to headline a campaign fundraiser for his fellow MAGA extremist, Greg Cunningham, in Las Cruces.
MAGA donors have made it clear they think they can buy the election for NM-02 and fill the seat with a Trump loyalist through Cunningham, who has said his “ number one priority ” in Congress would be to carry out President Trump’s agenda.
Cunningham’s own campaign materials promoting the forthcoming fundraiser noted, “Republicans have committed $8.8 million in general election media spending, compared with $4.8 million on the Democratic side.” In addition to MAGA’s top official in Congress, House Speaker Mike Johnson, Cunningham has chosen to campaign alongside a questionable cast of characters, including : President Trump — Earlier this week, President Trump re-upped his glowing endorsement of Cunningham , urging voters in NM-02 to vote for Cunningham.
Back in August, Speaker Johnson noted that President Trump “ loves ” Cunningham.
A potential felon — One of the donors listed on the Host Committee for Cunningham’s upcoming Las Cruces fundraiser on Oct.
12 is “Michael Bursum.” A Las Cruces man of the same name was charged with two felonies — aggravated battery upon a person with intent to cause great bodily harm and conspiracy to commit a 3rd degree felony — in May 2026.
A man who pleaded guilty to drug trafficking — In September, Cunningham’s team produced and ran an ad with the National Republican Congressional Committee that featured Raymond De La Cruz, Cunningham’s self-proclaimed barber and friend of 30 years.
There was just one problem — as the Washington Examiner reported , “De La Cruz pleaded guilty in 2001 to two charges of cocaine trafficking with intent to distribute” and “was then arrested in 2005 for violating his probation, which he admitted to, extending his probation by two years.” A founding father of ICE — As ICE ramps up its mass deportation campaign and comes under increasing criticism for its use of violent tactics that have resulted in bodily harm and death to American citizens, the Congressional Leadership Fund (CLF), a super PAC endorsed by Speaker Mike Johnson, ran an ad in support of Cunningham that featured Bob Martinez, who KOAT reported is the “former director of the federal agency that later became ICE.” The ad was also questioned for its misleading claims about Vasquez’s public safety record.
And the folks bankrolling this operation?
MAGA Inc. : Cunningham has received over $403,000 in right-wing extremist PACs and an additional $816,000 from No Going Back PAC — a Trump-directed dark money group.
“Dime con quién andas y te diré quién eres,” said Patricia Socarras Santiago, campaign spokesperson .
“Greg’s campaign is bankrolled by MAGA donors who think they can steamroll the voters of NM-02, buy this seat in Congress, and fill it with a MAGA extremist, Greg Cunningham, who will rubber stamp Trump’s reckless policies that hurt New Mexico families.
The upcoming MAGA fundraiser on October 12 is just more evidence that if we are the company we keep, a vote for Greg is a vote for Trump and more of his MAGA extremism.” This marks Speaker Johnson’s second stop to New Mexico to fundraise for Cunningham this cycle, as Cunningham welcomed Speaker Johnson — the mastermind behind the “One Big Beautiful Bill” that cut healthcare coverage for 89,000 New Mexicans and food assistance for 60,000 hardworking New Mexico families — to New Mexico with open arms, hosting him at an out-of-district event in Albuquerque in August.
At the August event, Cunningham refused to take questions from the media , and dozens of protestors made their objections to Cunningham’s MAGA extremism heard outside the venue.
To date, Gabe has also earned the endorsements of AFGE Local 1050; AFT New Mexico; Brady PAC; The Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers (BLET), Committee of Interns and Residents of New Mexico; AFSCME Council 18; New Mexico Carpenters Local 1319; CWA Unidos Local 7076; Education Votes; Equality PAC; Giffords PAC; Human Rights Campaign; IBEW Local 611; Latino Victory; League of Conservation Voters; Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate; MoveOn; The Mescalero Apache Tribe; National Wildlife Action Fund; NRDC Action Fund; National Committee to Preserve Social Security and Medicare; New Mexico State Council of Machinists; New Mexico Federation of Labor; New Mexico Professional Fire Fighters Association; New Mexico State College Dems; The Next 50; Organizers in the Land of Enchantment (OLÉ); Repro Freedom For All; Semilla Action; Sierra Club; SMART Local 49; Stop Gun Violence PAC; and Young Democrats of New Mexico.
Gabe is the only Democrat to have won New Mexico’s 2nd Congressional District twice consecutively.
In 2022, he beat incumbent Yvette Herrell by the tightest margin in the country (0.7%).
Last cycle, Gabe again defeated Yvette Herrell, this time by 4%— the same year that Donald Trump won the district by 2% — making him one of just 14 Democrats to win a district that went for Trump in 2024.
Gabe has continued to win over Democrat, Republican and Independent voters because he is willing to work with anyone to deliver results for New Mexicans on the issues they care most about: lowering the cost of living, expanding access to health care, protecting our public lands, and fixing our broken immigration system.
In November, he will set another historic record by winning New Mexico’s 2nd Congressional District for a third consecutive cycle.
