LEGISLATIVE HIGHLIGHTS
ACT 8609 Territorial Park System – protecting our islands for future generations.
ACT 8814 Higher Education Scholarship Act – providing FREE tuition at UVI for
part-time and online students.
ACT 8555 Bilingual Education Act – ensuring all VI students have the tools for
success.
ACT 8547 Firearm Declaration – limiting the importation of gun components.
ACT 8773 Horse Racing Commission Separation – allowing for St.
Croix’s
Commission to operate independently.
ACT 8540 GERS Stabilization Act – protecting current and future retirees.
ACT 8465 First Time Homebuyers Program – providing downpayment grants for
home ownership.
ACT 8688 V.I.
Behavioral Health Act – establishing a comprehensive framework for
addressing mental health and homelessness.
ACT 8790 Driver Safety Act – to expand eligibility for Limited Purpose Identification
Cards and Driver’s Licenses.
ACT 8788 Ratepayers Bill of Rights – expanding the rights of consumers when
dealing with local utilities.
ACT 8784 Copper Contaminated Water Relief Act – Mandating WAPA to provide
free drinking to impacted St.
Croix residents.
ACT 8857 IDEA ACT – mandating further digitization of government records and
services.

penalties and resources.
BR 23-0276 Miya’s Law/Tenant Safety Act – to establish guidelines for the
