DONATE Meet Holt: Holt Persinger is proud to serve the people of Barrow and Jackson Counties in the State House.
Elected in February of 2023, Holt quickly got to work on keeping his word and delivering for the people of District 119.
Holt may have entered the 2023 Legislative Session mid-way through, he didn’t let it slow him down.
He quickly joined Republican colleagues in passing legislation to return taxpayer dollars back to taxpayers, support law enforcement, hold radical District Attorneys accountable, and protect our Georgia values.
Holt and his wife Jennie, who works for the Barrow County School System, know that our communities here in Barrow and Jackson are the best places to live in Georgia.
Holt is proud to serve in the State House so he can fight to keep it that way.
A small businessman, Holt is now works as a Business Development Manager for LJA Engineering.
In the House, Holt serves on the Budget and Affairs, Economic Developments and Tourism, and Natural Resources and The Environment Committees.
