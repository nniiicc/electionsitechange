Meet Jacquie Jacquie grew up in Raytown, where she learned the values of hard work, faith, and community.
In 2016, she and her husband moved to Lee's Summit, where they have enjoyed raising their family and becoming deeply involved in the community they proudly call home.
Together, they own a local small business and understand firsthand the challenges and rewards of running a business in today's economy.
Jacquie is actively involved in her children's school community and volunteers with Breakthrough T1D, advocating for families and working to make a difference beyond her own household.
Rooted in service and committed to protecting working families, supporting first responders, strengthening the local economy, and defending constitutional freedoms, Jacquie is running to ensure every family has the opportunity to thrive.
