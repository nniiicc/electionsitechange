State Rep.
Cythia Neely (D-Flint) will host a stop on the Energy Reliability, Resilience and Accountability Task Force Listening Tour on Wednesday, Aug. 16, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m.
In the wake of hundreds of thousands of power outages in Michigan, this event allows community residents to voice their concerns about energy in Flint and hear about what the Legislature is doing to lower energy costs, increase access to renewable energy and make our grid more resilient.
WHO: State Reps.
Cynthia Neeley (D-Flint) and Helena Scott (D-Detroit), chair of the House Energy, Communications, and Technology Committee and the Energy Reliability, Resilience and Accountability Task Force.
WHAT: A stop on the Energy Reliability, Resilience and Accountability Task Force Listening Tour.
WHEN: Wednesday, Aug. 16 from 11 a.m.-1 p.m.
WHERE: 1101 Saginaw St., Flint, MI 48502, inside the dome at City Hall
