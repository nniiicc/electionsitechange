Balancing the Budget Home About News Volunteer Donate Contact Balancing the Budget Planning a balanced budget helps governments to avoid excessive spending and allows them to focus funds on areas and services that require them the most.
Furthermore, achieving a budget surplus can provide funds for emergencies, e.g., if the government wishes to increase spending during a recession without having to borrow.
