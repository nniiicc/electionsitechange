About Bridget Dr.
Bridget Fitzgibbons is running to be the voice of hardworking Arizonans — standing up for the issues that matter most: lower costs, a strong economy, public safety, secure borders, excellent schools, and fiscal responsibility.
Physician A physician with advanced training in psychology, Bridget has also served as an addictions counselor and public-school systems consultant from early childhood through 12th grade.
She’s worked directly with educators, administrators, and families to improve outcomes, promote safety, and build pathways to careers.
Small Business Owner Bridget is a Flinn-Brown Fellowship alum, a Dodie Londen Excellence in Public Service Scholar, and a former board member of the Arizona Naturopathic Medical Association.
She was appointed to the Arizona Senate President’s Anti-Human Trafficking Task Force and is a respected small business owner and key opinion leader in the medical community.
Principled Leadership Raised by civic-minded educators — including the nuns who shaped her early years — and by a family rooted in public service (with relatives serving in the United States Air Force, United States Navy, Chicago Fire Department, and Chicago Police Department), Bridget understands the value of integrity, discipline, and service.
Community Leader Bridget grew up surrounded by the challenges of drugs, overdoses, crime, and teen pregnancies — and rose above them.
She knows what’s at stake for Arizona families and is ready to fight for a stronger, safer future.
