ZACH MAIER for KANSAS ZACH MAIER for KANSAS KANSAS Learn more Meet Zach Priorities Fund public schools Protect your privacy Grow your prosperity Keep Kansas innovating Bring back balance Endorsements Join our movement Volunteer Donate I'm Zach.
I'm a proud dad, a Kansas farm kid, a K-State computer engineer, and a small business owner.
I'm running to build a stronger future for our kids here in Shawnee and Lake Quivira.
As your next representative for House 39, I will fight to fully fund our public schools, to protect your privacy, and to help your family earn and keep more.
My entire career, I've been solving everyday problems.
Now I'm ready to do the same in Topeka.
Let's build a better Kansas together!
Zach's Priority #1 Fund public schools I'm a public school kid.
I thrived in special ed programs, and they launched me into K-State engineering and every success since.
Now my kids are part of the Shawnee Mission schools, and I want to ensure every student in USD 512 and USD 232 gets that same head start.
My top education commitments are to: Increase funding for K-12 classrooms Fully fund special education Keep public dollars in public schools Preserve public university budgets Every tax dollar spent on public education pays itself back multiple times in economic value.
It's time for Kansas to do the right thing and invest in our kids' futures.
Zach's Priority #2 Protect your privacy I believe privacy is a fundamental human right.
The government has no business dictating what you do in the privacy of your own home, your doctor's office, or your place of worship.
I will fight to defend your freedom to live your life the way you choose.
My top privacy commitments are to: Protect your private medical decisions Pass robust data protection laws Defend LGBTQ+ freedoms and equality Promote a privacy-first digital economy Your private life and relationships do not belong to the government or big business.
It's time for Kansas, forever the free state, to lead the way in personal and digital liberty.
Zach's Priority #3 Grow your prosperity Your hard work doesn't go as far as it used to.
I'll fight for better paying jobs, stronger local businesses, and lower property taxes.
One solid answer to rising costs is helping your family earn more and keep more.
My top prosperity commitments are to: Help local governments lower property taxes Bring accountability to state tax breaks Legalize and tax recreational marijuana Stand up for unions and working Kansans You are already smart with your money.
You work hard, save for the future, and make smart investments.
It's time for Kansas to do the same with your tax dollars.
Zach's Priority #4 Keep Kansas innovating I'm a builder.
I founded two companies, and I built parts of Google Maps that you use every day.
I have two decades of experience in software, security, and artificial intelligence.
I know the tradeoffs of new technology, and I'm ready to help Kansas navigate whatever the future brings.
My top innovation commitments are to: Help schools adapt to modern tech Accelerate startups and small business Invest in local R&D programs Attract more modern manufacturing New technology like AI is fundamentally changing how we learn and work.
It's time to get Kansas prepared to build on that opportunity.
Zach's Priority #5 Bring back balance I'm not a career politician and don't want to become one.
But as an engineer, I'm ready to step up and fix what's broken.
Topeka needs to get back to hard work, collaboration, and respecting the will of the people.
My top balance commitments are to: End the one-party supermajority Pass every law in the open Stop backdoor constitutional amendments Keep big money out of our politics Your government should promise a better future for you and your family.
It's time to cut the Topeka tricks, look to the future, and run Kansas with optimism and integrity.
