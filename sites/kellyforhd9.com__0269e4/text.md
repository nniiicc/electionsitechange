Raised in a town of just 34 people, Steve learned early the value of hard work, personal responsibility, and looking out for your neighbors.
In a community built on logging and ranching, there was no room for excuses—only accountability.
Steve dedicated his career to public service, serving as a Sheriff’s Deputy and rising to Captain with the Washoe County Sheriff’s Office in Nevada.
Over decades of service, he led officers, protected communities, and remained focused on keeping families safe.
Now serving as your representative, Steve Kelly continues to bring that same steady leadership to Helena—standing strong for public safety and the people of House District 9.
Steve believes government should be simple, accountable, and focused on results—not control.
His priorities are clear: protect public safety and support law enforcement, solve problems instead of creating them, cut taxes and wasteful spending, and reduce unnecessary regulations so Montanans can thrive.
Steve Kelly is committed to delivering common-sense leadership, strong communities, and Montana values for House District 9.
