MENTAL HEALTH & FENTANYL CRISIS INITIATIVE Representative Salzman created, implemented, and leads the Northwest Florida Mental Health Task Force.
This Task Force has set precedent across the state for collaborative care models and many agencies have adopted the best practices state wide that were recommended through her leadership.
This task force has also brought together a fundamental group of organizations who have committed to making a meaningful impact on access to mental health care in the panhandle.
