This CDE is the national professional practice standard necessary to diagnose autism as well as other developmental or behavioral disorders.
With this policy broadened, more children in need of a CDE will be able to be covered under Medicaid and receive this crucial evaluation. • As a result of CS/HB 229 – Hazardous Walking Conditions for K-12 Students: • A study to be conducted by the Office of Program Policy Analysis and Government Accountability (OPPAGA) has been ordered by the Speaker’s Office to determine what conditions are considered safe for students walking, including busy roadways, overpasses, and bridges.
