Donate Meet Jack The Issues Volunteer Meet Jack Up north beginnings My father was the proud union steward for the Papermaker’s in International Falls.
It was a time when one income could support a family.
My parents and we five children enjoyed many outdoor activities.
Carrying on that legacy, my wife and I have also instilled in our four children an appreciation for the natural wonders of our beautiful state.
I deeply appreciate Minnesota’s commitment to preserving its natural assets for future generations to enjoy.
A life in the district For most of my career, I have been CEO at a long-term care facility in Mora.
Thus, I have spent years dealing with legislators, regulations, oversight, and most important, the needs of senior citizens and their caregivers.
As Executive Director of Mora’s Housing and Redevelopment Authority, I am well aware of the urgent need for affordable and safe housing, especially in rural areas.
I have since retired, but I am still an active member of their board.
This has strengthened my commitment to improving access to all housing, but more specifically affordable housing.
Protecting the future Due to recent events, I have grown more concerned about the ever increasing threats to our constitutional rights.
We all must roll up our sleeves and get actively involved in preserving our democracy.
