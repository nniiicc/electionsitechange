Hi!
I’m Iva.
I’m a rural, working mom; the wife of a Marine combat veteran; and a healthcare access advocate.
From regularly having to travel 40+ minutes for prenatal then pediatric care to balancing work and motherhood because of childcare costs to watching my husband navigate accessing the care he earned serving our country, I know what it’s like to feel unseen and unsupported by existing institutions.
From my work, I know that my family’s situation is far from unique.
That’s why I’m running for the Kentucky State House: to fight for a Kentucky that works for ALL of us—regardless of our identity, income, or zip code.
KENTUCKIANS DESERVE ACCESS, STABILITY, AND OPPORTUNITY.
ACCESS
Healthy communities are communities that have access to
- medical, dental, and vision providers;
- affordable healthcare;
- clean air and water; and
- reliable broadband & cell service.
Preventive health is cheaper than treatment or emergency care.
Eastern Kentuckians deserve policies that put this care—whether in person or via telehealth—in our backyard.
STABILITY
Safe communities are communities in which we
- earn a living wage;
- can afford our homes;
- don’t have to choose between groceries, utilities, and medication; and
- can rely on safety nets when we fall on hard times.
Economic hardship significantly increases substance abuse and crime rates.
When people can afford the basics, desperation’s grip loosens.
OPPORTUNITY
Thriving communities are communities that
- offer free childcare and Pre-K so families have more choice;
- have fully funded, quality public schools;
- value both colleges and trade schools; and
- invest in small businesses.
Education and entrepreneurship move communities forward, but they are becoming increasingly out of reach.
It’s time to take another look at, and adjust, our priorities.
