Paul Jacobs, standing up for our values.
Paul Jacobs joined the U.S.
Navy out of high school during the Viet Nam era.
Paul and his wife Rhoda have lived in southern Illinois for nearly 50 years raising four children and now enjoying their grandchildren.
Paul was an optometrist, with offices in several southern Illinois communities, for most of this time.
He also opened and operated Von Jakob Winery in Alto Pass.
Paul (Republican) was elected as state representative in November 2020.
He is pro-life, pro-parental rights, pro-gun rights, pro-public safety, and for minimizing government expeditures and overreach.
