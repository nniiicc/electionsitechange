Home About Paul News Recent Activity Privacy Policy Contact Privacy Policy Overview This Privacy Policy outlines how the Committee to Elect Dr.
Paul Jacobs (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information you provide when using our website or other online platforms displaying this policy (“Sites”).
By using our Sites, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
We may update this policy periodically; your continued use of the Sites constitutes acceptance of those changes.
1.
Information We Collect a.
Voluntarily Provided Information: We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, postal address, phone number, mobile number, and geographic location when you fill out forms, sign up for updates, make donations, or communicate with us. b.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: If you opt in to receive text messages, we collect your phone number and related data necessary to deliver campaign-related SMS/MMS communications. c.
Automatically Collected Information: When you visit our Sites, we may automatically collect non-personal data about your visit, such as IP address, browser type, operating system, referring/exit pages, device identifiers, and timestamps.
This information helps us analyze usage and improve site functionality.
2.
How We Use Information We may use the information we collect to: Communicate with you and respond to inquiries; Send campaign updates, alerts, and information; Improve and personalize our online content and services; Process contributions and comply with legal requirements; Maintain compliance with election laws and regulations.
Text Messaging Data: Text messaging opt-in data and consent will only be used to send you campaign-related messages such as alerts, donation requests, and updates.
Payment Information: When you make a contribution or purchase through our Sites, we may collect payment information (such as credit or debit card details) to process your transaction securely.
Payment data is processed by a third-party payment processor and is not stored by Jacobs for Illinois beyond what is required for compliance with campaign-finance reporting laws.
3.
Sharing of Information We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
We may disclose information only: To trusted vendors or service providers who perform functions on our behalf (such as web hosting, payment processing, or email distribution), under strict confidentiality agreements; As required by law, subpoena, or other legal process; To protect against misuse, fraud, or unauthorized use of our Sites.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, sold, or transferred for any purpose, except as required by law or necessary to provide messaging services.
4.
Data Security We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your personal information and maintain security safeguards against unauthorized access or disclosure.
However, no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed 100% secure.
6.
Email Sign-Ups If you provide your email address through our Sites, you may receive periodic updates, event invitations, or fundraising messages from Jacobs for Illinois.
You can unsubscribe at any time by following the “unsubscribe” link included in each email.
We process unsubscribe requests promptly, though some messages already in progress may still be delivered.
7.
Third-Party Links and Services Our Sites may include links to third-party websites or services not operated by Jacobs for Illinois.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third parties and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.
Social Media and Public Forums: Our Sites and related pages (including Facebook, Instagram, and X) may allow users to post comments or other content publicly.
Any information you post may be viewed and used by others, and we cannot guarantee its privacy.
Please avoid sharing personal details in public forums.
8.
Children’s Privacy Our Sites are not intended for individuals under the age of 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
Parents and guardians are encouraged to monitor their children’s online activity.
9.
Donations and Legal Disclosures State law and Illinois State Board of Elections regulations may require us to collect and disclose certain donor information, including name, mailing address, occupation, and employer for contributions exceeding reporting thresholds.
This information is disclosed only as required by law.
10.
Visiting from Outside the United States If you access our Sites from outside the U.S., you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.
11.
Updates to This Policy We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements.
Updates will take effect when posted.
Please review this page periodically for the latest version.
