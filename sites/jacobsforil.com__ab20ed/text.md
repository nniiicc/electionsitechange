I am currently in my third term as your state representative.
Due to Senator Dale Fowler's decision to not run for another term, I have decided to run for the Illinois Senate.
The senate district comprises my current representative district as well as the district of Representative Patrick Windhorst.
The primary election will be held in the spring of 2026.
I look forward to continuing to work for and with my current constituents and to get to know the voters in my expanded senate district.
Representative Paul Jacobs
