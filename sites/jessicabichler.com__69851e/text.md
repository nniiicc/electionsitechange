Jessica Bichler
Jessica Bichler is a working mom and a small business owner who has called Wilmington home for almost 15 years.
She has spent over a decade supporting the health and well-being of families across New Hanover County as a yoga teacher and studio owner.
Jessica's vision for New Hanover County is simple — a community that works for all of us, where everyone can afford to live, work, and thrive.
