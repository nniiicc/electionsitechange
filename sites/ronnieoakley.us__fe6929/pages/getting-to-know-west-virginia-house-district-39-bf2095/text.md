It is made up of communities, families, small businesses, schools, roads, water systems, and everyday people whose concerns deserve to be heard in Charleston.
Throughout this campaign, Ronnie Oakley is focused on learning from the people who live across the district and making sure local concerns are part of the conversation.
What Matters Across District 39 The concerns people raise may look different from one community to another, but many of the same themes come up again and again: reliable infrastructure, affordable utilities, access to healthcare, strong schools, safe communities, government accountability, and economic opportunity that actually benefits the people who live here.
Why District Boundaries Matter West Virginia’s House of Delegates now uses single-member districts, so district boundaries play a much bigger role in how communities are represented in Charleston.
Understanding where District 39 begins and ends helps residents better understand which communities are included in the district and why local representation matters.
