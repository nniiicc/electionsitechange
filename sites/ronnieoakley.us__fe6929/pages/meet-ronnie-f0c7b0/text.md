MEET RONNIE
Ronnie Oakley is running for the West Virginia House of Delegates in District 39 because he believes our communities deserve practical leadership, real accountability, and a representative who listens to the people he serves.
A Practical Voice for District 39
Ronnie believes public service should start with listening.
The people who live, work, raise families, run businesses, and build their lives in our communities should have a real voice in the decisions made in Charleston.
He wants to bring a practical, common-sense approach to the Legislature: asking questions, demanding accountability, and remembering who elected him in the first place.
Community First
Ronnie’s campaign is focused on the everyday concerns people across District 39 are talking about: dependable water and infrastructure, responsible growth, accountable government, protecting families, and making sure decisions made at the state level actually reflect the needs of the communities they affect.
He believes good representation means being accessible, staying informed, and never forgetting that elected officials work for the people.
Real-World Experience
Ronnie brings the perspective of someone who has worked, solved problems, dealt with real-world responsibilities, and understands that government decisions have consequences for ordinary people.
He is not running to become part of a political class.
He is running to bring practical judgment, common sense, and accountability to the House of Delegates.
A Representative Who Listens
Ronnie believes representation starts with being available to the people you serve.
He wants constituents to know they can reach out, ask questions, raise concerns, and expect an honest response.
Whether the issue is happening in Charleston or right here at home, Ronnie’s goal is to stay connected to District 39 and carry those voices with him to the Legislature.
Why Ronnie Is Running
Ronnie is running because he believes District 39 deserves a strong, independent voice focused on results instead of political games.
He wants to help build a state government that is more accountable, more responsive, and more willing to listen to the people affected by its decisions.
For Ronnie, this campaign is about serving the community, protecting what makes our area special, and making sure the people of District 39 have someone in Charleston who will speak up for them.
