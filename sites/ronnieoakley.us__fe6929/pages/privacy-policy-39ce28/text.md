PRIVACY POLICY
Last updated: September 4, 2026
Ronnie Oakley for House District 39 respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit ronnieoakley.us, submit a campaign form, sign up for email updates, or interact with services linked from this website, and how that information may be used.
Information We Collect
We may collect information that you voluntarily provide through this website, including your name, email address, phone number, volunteer interests, preferred contact method, and any message or other information you choose to submit through a campaign form or email signup.
How We Use Your Information
We may use the information you provide to respond to questions, communicate about campaign activities, coordinate volunteers, fulfill campaign sign requests, send campaign updates when you have chosen to receive them, and otherwise operate Ronnie Oakley’s campaign for West Virginia House District 39.
Email Communications
If you sign up to receive campaign emails, we may send you updates about Ronnie Oakley’s campaign, events, volunteer opportunities, and other campaign-related information.
You can unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in campaign emails.
Website Data and Cookies
This website may use cookies or similar technologies to support basic site functionality, improve performance, understand how visitors use the site, and support services provided by third parties.
These technologies may collect information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general usage data.
Third-Party Services
This website may use or link to third-party services that help operate the campaign, including email marketing, website hosting, form processing, analytics, and campaign contribution services.
Those third parties may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.
Campaign Contributions
If you choose to make a campaign contribution, payment and donor information will be handled through the campaign’s authorized contribution-processing service.
Contribution information may also be subject to reporting and disclosure requirements under applicable campaign finance laws.
How We Share Information
We do not sell personal information.
We may share information with service providers that help operate the website and campaign, or when required to comply with legal, regulatory, or campaign-finance obligations.
Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information submitted through this website.
However, no website, email system, or online transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Your Choices
You may unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in those messages.
You may also contact the campaign to request that personal information you previously submitted be corrected or deleted, subject to any legal or recordkeeping requirements that may apply.
Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information submitted through this website is handled, contact the campaign at ronnie@ronnieoakley.us.
