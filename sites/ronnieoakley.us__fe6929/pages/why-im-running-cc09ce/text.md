Because District 39 Deserves a Strong Voice Too often, decisions made in Charleston can feel far removed from the everyday realities families, workers, and small businesses face here at home.
I believe representation starts with being accessible, listening carefully, and carrying the concerns of District 39 into every vote and every conversation at the Capitol.
Because Government Should Work for the People State government should be accountable, transparent, and focused on solving problems instead of creating more red tape.
I want to bring a practical, common-sense approach to Charleston and make sure taxpayers can see where their money is going, why decisions are being made, and who is responsible for the results.
Because Families Deserve Accountability Families who interact with West Virginia’s child welfare system deserve fairness, transparency, and accountability.
CPS has an enormous responsibility, and with that responsibility must come strong oversight, clear standards, meaningful due process, and consequences when the system fails the children and families it is supposed to protect.
Reform should strengthen child safety while also protecting the rights of families and making sure decisions can withstand real scrutiny.
Because District 39 Deserves Someone Who Will Show Up Representation should not begin and end with an election.
I believe a delegate should be present in the community, available to constituents, willing to return calls and messages, and ready to stand beside the people he represents when problems arise.
District 39 deserves a representative who is visible, accessible, and willing to do the work every day.
Because I Believe We Can Do Better West Virginia has enormous potential, but reaching it will take leaders who are willing to listen, think independently, and focus on practical solutions instead of political games.
I’m running because I believe District 39 deserves thoughtful, determined representation and because I’m ready to work for the people who call this place home.
