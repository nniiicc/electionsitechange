ISSUES
Border Security
In the Texas Legislature, Eddie Morales was the only member who put forward a comprehensive, common-sense plan to secure our border while protecting the communities who call it home.
He knows the challenges — and the opportunities — that come with living along the border, and he’s focused on solutions that keep Texans safe, support our economy, and treat people with dignity.
Improving our Infrastructure
Texans pay into the system but rural and border communities like ours are being left behind.
Eddie Morales is fighting to bring home our fair share of infrastructure funding so our communities aren’t stuck with unsafe roads, stalled projects, or rising construction costs they can’t afford.
Real Opportunity for South and West Texas
Eddie Morales knows what it takes to create jobs — because he’s done it himself.
As the owner of multiple small businesses, including a local tortilla factory that has employed families in Eagle Pass for over 30 years, Eddie understands firsthand how policy impacts the bottom line.
In the Legislature, he’s fighting to bring high-skill, high-wage jobs to our region and make it easier for local businesses to thrive.
Champion for our Public Schools
A quality education is the key to a better future — for our kids, our workforce, and our economy.
As the son of working-class parents who taught him the value of hard work and giving back, Eddie Morales is committed to strengthening our public schools and expanding access to job training across South and West Texas.
Accessible, Affordable Healthcare for Every Texan
Every Texan deserves access to quality health care — no matter their zip code, income, or insurance status.
In rural and border communities, outdated policies and underfunded systems are putting lives at risk.
Eddie Morales is fighting for common-sense solutions to expand access and support the people who keep our communities healthy.
Safe Schools and Stronger Mental Health Support
Every parent deserves to know their child is safe at school — and every student deserves to learn without fear.
Eddie Morales is working to make our schools safer and to expand access to mental health care, especially in rural communities where help is hardest to find.
Protecting Rights.
Defending Freedoms.
Eddie Morales believes Texans should be free to live their lives without government overreach.
Whether it’s the right to protect your family or make personal medical decisions, he’ll always stand up for your freedoms — and fight for common-sense protections that keep our communities safe.
