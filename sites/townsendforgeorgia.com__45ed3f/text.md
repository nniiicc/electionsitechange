Rick Townsend Will Fight for Georgia Families
Rick Townsend’ has been fighting for Georgia's families for three decades, as a teacher, coach, career-technology supervisor, assistant principal, principal, assistant superintendent, CEO of a college & career academy, and superintendent.
He has worked at all levels of education from pre-kindergarten through twelfth grades.
He has worked across the State of Georgia, from rural to suburban communities, and economically disadvantaged areas to wealthier school systems.
There are only three things that matter to Rick when it comes to issues and legislation:
- Will it benefit families in Georgia?
- Will it make the lives of parents and children better?
- Will it strengthen families and communities across Georgia?
