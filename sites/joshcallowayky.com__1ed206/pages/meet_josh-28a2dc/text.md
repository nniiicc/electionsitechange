Home Meet Josh Volunteer Contribute Meet Josh Representative Josh Calloway is a proven conservative voice in Frankfort—grounded in Christian faith, guided by the Constitution, and unwavering in principle.
He represents Kentucky’s 10th House District, which includes Breckinridge County and part of Hardin County.
Josh’s early education—both in private Christian school and through homeschooling—reinforced the values of faith, family, and hard work that guide him to this day.
He later earned his Safety and Health Management Certification from Eastern Kentucky University.
Professionally, he worked in the manufacturing and pipeline industries, gaining firsthand experience with the challenges facing Kentucky families and small businesses.
In the legislature, Josh has served on the Agriculture Committee, the Economic Development and Workforce Investment Committee, the Small Business and Information Technology Committee, the Education Committee, and the Elections and Constitutional Amendments Committee—always standing firm for conservative values, limited government, and the protection of parental rights.
Beyond the Capitol, Josh is deeply rooted in his community.
He serves as a preacher, volunteer jail chaplain, music director, and pianist at Pleasant View Baptist Church.
He also leads Hope Through Truth Ministries and the Hope Academy, a transitional living home for at-risk men.
Josh and his wife Angela live in Irvington, KY, where they raised their five children and are blessed with five grandchildren.
