Public Safety
Jonathan believes that every community in Hawaiʻi deserves to feel safe, supported, and protected.
He stands with the dedicated men and women in law enforcement who work each day to keep our neighborhoods secure, and he values the trust and partnership between officers and the communities they serve.
He also believes that criminal accountability is essential for real public safety.
When crimes go unanswered, families lose confidence in the system meant to protect them.
Jonathan supports efforts that ensure consequences are clear, fair, and consistent so that our communities can thrive without fear.
Jonathan is committed to safe communities where families can live, work, and raise their keiki without worrying about rising violence or repeat offenders.
Strong communities begin with clear expectations, responsible enforcement, and a justice system that prioritizes the well‑being of the public.
He also supports the right of law‑abiding citizens to defend themselves, their families, and their property.
People should be able to protect their ʻohana when facing unlawful threats, and Jonathan believes that responsible self‑defense is a fundamental part of community safety.
