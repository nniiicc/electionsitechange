Meet Jonathan Issues News Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Lowering Taxes Lowering Taxes Jonathan believes that a healthy community depends on fairness , clarity , and responsible stewardship in how public resources are managed.
He values efforts that make life more affordable for Hawaiʻi’s families and reflect respect for the people who call these islands home.
This includes an interest in simplifying Hawai'i's tax code to relieve burdens on small businesses, as well as exploring ways to ease financial burdens on everyday essentials by removing state taxes on groceries and medications.
As for Hawaii state property tax for our Kamaaina, Jonathan will focus attention on removing property taxes for Kupuna on a fixed income.
