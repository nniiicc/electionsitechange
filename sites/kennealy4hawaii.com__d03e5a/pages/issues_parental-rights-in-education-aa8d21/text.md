Parental Rights in Education
Jonathan believes that parents—not government systems—carry the primary responsibility and authority for their children’s education.
Yet in Hawaiʻi, families often feel shut out of decisions that directly affect their keiki.
Too many policies are made with little transparency, leaving parents to discover changes only after they’ve already been implemented.
Jonathan supports restoring a clear parental voice in the classroom and ensuring that families have full access to what their children are being taught.
He also believes that transparency should be the standard, not the exception.
Parents deserve straightforward information about curriculum, materials, and school policies—without having to fight for it.
For families who choose to educate at home, Jonathan is committed to protecting homeschooling freedom.
Hawaiʻi’s regulations can place unnecessary burdens on responsible parents, and he supports keeping home education flexible, family‑directed, and free from overreach.
Jonathan’s position is simple: Hawaiʻi should trust parents, not sideline them.
Families deserve respect, clarity, and the freedom to guide their children’s learning.
