Jonathan would like to implement tax exemptions for Kupuna care facilities, rehabilitation centers, special needs programs.
He also believes that strengthening pathways out of homelessness begins with ensuring people have access to meaningful care and reliable resources, honoring their worth and humanity.
Jonathan values efforts that improve Hawaiʻi’s medical and educational resources, recognizing that families thrive when they can remain on the islands they call home.
His focus is on listening to the community and exploring ways Hawaiʻi can support families, uplift local resilience, and nurture the well‑being of future generations.
