Meet Jonathan Issues News Contribute Voter Information Hawaiʻi holds its statewide elections every even‑numbered year.
The primary election takes place on the second Saturday in August, and the general election follows on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November.
Under Hawaiʻi’s vote‑by‑mail system, all elections are conducted through mailed ballots.
Every registered voter is automatically sent a ballot to the mailing address listed on their voter registration, typically about eighteen days before each election.
