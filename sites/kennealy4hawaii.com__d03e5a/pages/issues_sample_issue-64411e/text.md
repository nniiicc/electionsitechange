Self-sustainability
Jonathan believes Hawaiʻi’s long‑term strength depends on nurturing a more self‑sustaining island future.
He values approaches that honor our natural resources, support local resilience, and strengthen the connection between our land and our people.
This includes an interest in revitalizing local agriculture—encouraging the return of community‑based farming for beef, poultry, fish, and other essential foods.
A renewed commitment to local production supports a farm‑to‑table mindset and reflects the belief that Hawaiʻi thrives when it can rely on its own abundance.
He also values the restoration of local manufacturing and the development of sustainable recycling systems that reduce waste and promote responsible stewardship.
Many countries, such as Sweden, have demonstrated how innovative recycling practices can transform nearly all waste into energy or reusable materials.
Jonathan sees these global examples as inspiration for how communities can care for their environment with creativity and integrity.
