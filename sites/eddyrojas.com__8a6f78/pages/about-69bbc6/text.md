ABOUT ROJAS
A NEW VOICE FOR
MIAMI IN WASHINGTON
Eddy Rojas came to Miami from Cuba with a dream and a work ethic that wouldn't quit.
Over the past 20 years, he built a successful service company from the ground up — going from parking cars to leading one of Florida's most respected service businesses.
He knows what it means to work hard, raise a family, and fight for the American Dream.
Now, Eddy is running for Congress to bring that same dedication and honesty to Washington — because Miami's hardworking families deserve a representative who truly understands their struggles.
