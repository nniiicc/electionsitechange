Biography
About John Kavanagh
“I have dedicated my life to public service — first as a police officer on the streets of New York, then as a college professor, and now as your representative in the Arizona Legislature.”
Senator John Kavanagh is a proven conservative leader dedicated to protecting public safety, defending taxpayers, and preserving Arizona values.
First elected to the Legislature in 2006, he has earned a reputation as a strong, consistent voice for limited government, law and order, and individual freedom.
Before entering public office, John served 20 years as a police officer and detective sergeant with the Port Authority Police of New York and New Jersey.
John holds a Bachelor's degree from New York University, a Master's in Government from St.
John's University, and a Ph.D. in Criminal Justice from Rutgers University.
He later served as a criminal justice professor at Scottsdale Community College, helping train future law enforcement professionals.
He currently teaches government at A.S.U.
In the Legislature, John has fought to secure the border, protect Second Amendment rights, cut taxes, defend parental rights, and promote election integrity.
He has also served in key leadership roles, including Senate Majority Leader, Senate President Pro Tempore, and Chairman of the Appropriations Committee, working to keep Arizona financially strong.
John and his wife Linda, former mayor of Fountain Hills, raised their two sons in the community they proudly call home.
