Cut Property Taxes
In the Texas legislature, Brad will continue to cut property taxes each legislative session.
Cutting property taxes has been a long-time goal of Brad’s.
In The Woodlands, Brad spearheaded the effort to pass a 3% Homestead Exemption for all residential homeowners.
This is the first time in the history of The Woodlands that homeowners have been given a homestead exemption by the Township.
Support Public Safety
Brad Bailey will shut down all liberal efforts to defund police and release dangerous criminals.
He has always worked to cut crime.
In The Woodlands, Brad successfully increased the number of officers on patrol by 13% and expanded the mounted patrol presence in business areas.
Supporting Excellence In Education
Brad will continue his dedication to strengthening Texas education and delivering the excellence that Texas families deserve.
Even at the local level, Brad has had this dedication.
He worked with Tomball ISD and local landowners to keep an intermediate school from moving which avoided children from being bussed for more than 8 hours a week to a school in Tomball.
Brad Bailey will ensure that Texas’s new school choice program — empowering parents and students across the state — is fully implemented and expanded during the next Legislative Session.
He will be a champion for teachers, parents, and most importantly, students.
Improving Disaster Response
Improving insurance and state and federal disaster response is a huge priority for Brad Bailey.
Locally, he worked with community partners to demand accountability for the failure of service response in the wake of Hurricane Beryl’s destruction.
Border Security
As Chairman of The Woodlands Township, Brad Bailey has been a staunch supporter of enforcing our nation’s immigration laws to protect the safety and security of the community.
He opposes all attempts at amnesty and has worked closely with Montgomery County Sheriff Wesley Doolittle to strengthen partnerships between local law enforcement and federal authorities through initiatives like the 287(g) program and close coordination with ICE, ensuring The Woodlands upholds the rule of law while addressing the challenges of illegal immigration.
Brad fully supports President Trump’s bold actions to secure the border, which stand in stark contrast to the reckless open-border policies of the Biden administration that undermined national security and strained local resources.
Brad believes we must prioritize the safety of American citizens and restore order at our borders through strong, decisive leadership and enforcement.
