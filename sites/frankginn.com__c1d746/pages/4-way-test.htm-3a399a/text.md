4 Way Test
The 4 Way Test which is a part of ROTARY International is used as a measure to recognize community members who have impacted their community.
The test, which has been translated into more than 100 languages, asks the following questions:
Of the things we think, say or do
1.
Is it the TRUTH?
2.
Is it FAIR to all concerned?
3.
Will it build GOODWILL and BETTER FRIENDSHIPS?
4.
Will it be BENEFICIAL to all concerned?
I promise to apply a similar test to my work under the Gold Dome on behalf of the 47th Senate District.
My test which will determine how I will vote on every issue is:
Will it help families?
Will it grow jobs?
Will it lower taxes?
Will it increase personal freedom?
