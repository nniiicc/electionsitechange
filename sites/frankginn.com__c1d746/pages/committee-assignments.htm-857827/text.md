Committee Assignments Senator Ginn serves as Chairman of the Transportation Committee.
He is also a member of the Appropriations, Natural Resources and Environment, Regulated Industries and Economic Development and Tourism committees.
Ginn states, "I look forward to continuing to represent the citizens living in the 47th district.
We in the Senate serve all of the Citizens of our Great State, we just have that privilege given to us by the voters at home.
I am honored to have been appointed to chair the Transportation committee.
As Chairman, I will work to ensure that Georgia’s economy continues to grow and our state continues to be recognized as the #1 state to do business".
Senator Frank Ginn represents the 47th Senate District, which includes Barrow and Madison counties and portions of Clarke and Jackson counties.
