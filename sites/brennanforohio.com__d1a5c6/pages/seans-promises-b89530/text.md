Sean's Community Service District 14 Maps Sean's Photo Gallery Sean’s Useful Web Links Testimonials Sean City Council Record Sean's Promises Contact Sean Sean Brennan's Promise My promise is to continue to work hard to make our district a better place in which to live and raise a family. * Bipartisanship : I will continue to build relationships with colleagues on both sides of the aisle in order to find common ground on the issues in order to create policies that positively benefit all Ohioans. *Safety : Continue to support our police, fire, and emergency medical service heroes by providing them with the equipment and technology they need to do their jobs. *Roads: Continue to support state funding to ensure safe, smooth driving for our residents and visitors. *Sewers: Continue to support state funding to assist efforts to address our cities' sewer systems and to support grant funding to take the burden of paying for it off of the taxpayers. *Economic Development: Continue to work hard to retain and attract new businesses to enhance services, create jobs, grow the tax base. *Senior Services: Continue to support excellent services to our elder residents who have paid their dues. *Recreation: Continue to support activities and amenities for the health and enjoyment of all of our residents. *Housing: Continue to support the enforcement of housing codes to ensure that our neighborhoods remain aesthetically pleasing and property values continue to climb.
