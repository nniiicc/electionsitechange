Behind the chair, I’ve built lasting relationships, listened to countless stories, and gained a real understanding of the challenges everyday families and working people face.
My work has taught me that caring for people, earning trust, and truly listening matter.
My faith, my family, and the principles that protect our liberties are at the center of who I am.
