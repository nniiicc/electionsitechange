Proven Leadership for Florida House District 67
Lieutenant Colonel Lisette Bonano is a mother, a 28-year U.S.
Army veteran, an educator, and a constitutional conservative running to represent Florida House District 67.
From serving at the Pentagon to deploying in Iraq and supporting military readiness in Afghanistan, Lisette has spent her career protecting freedom, strengthening institutions, and bringing people together to solve difficult problems.
She is prepared to bring that same discipline, courage, and accountability to the Florida House.
Lisette is fighting for a more affordable Florida, safer communities, stronger schools, parental rights, responsible development, and the constitutional freedoms that protect every American.
She will support local businesses, stand with law enforcement and first responders, improve transportation infrastructure, and demand greater transparency from government.
District 67 deserves a representative who will listen, lead, and fight for the people—not the political establishment.
Join LTC Lisette Bonano as she works to protect our children,
defend our freedoms, and build a stronger future for Tampa Bay.
