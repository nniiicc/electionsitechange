Home News & Updates Calendar Endorsements Volunteer Contact Voting Info Donate Back to News & Updates HAPPY THANKSGIVING 2023 11/23/23 • Announcements Happy Thanksgiving!
The first Thanksgiving was held by the Pilgrims of Plymouth Colony in present-day Massachusetts.
In search of religious freedom, the Pilgrims had left their home in England for the New World.
After enduring a difficult first year, they held a feast to thank God for His provisions and blessings.
This is the precious heritage we defend every day.
We are thankful for what God has given us, and we aim to be good stewards of the resources He has entrusted us with today.
We recognize the hand of providence throughout our nation's history, and how He has blessed us with liberty.
We strive daily to protect our cherished heritage of faith, family, and freedom.
I am Lisette Bonano, a grandmother, a veteran of two foreign wars, and a candidate for Florida House District 67.
I share your concern about events throughout the world and how they affect us here at home: inflation, corruption, unrest, and war.
We must fight Bidenflation by saying no to higher taxes, so that families can afford to pay their bills, and still have enough money to enjoy their holidays and save for retirement.
We need to offer our children's teachers the salary required to attract and retain the best-qualified professionals.
We need to support our schools so they can provide our children the level of education we expect from the greatest nation on earth.
We need to exercise our right of parental control over our public schools to protect our children from sexual and political predators.
We need to support our public colleges and universities, so they can expand their pioneering research into technologies that will make Tampa a magnet for health care, cybersecurity, criminal justice, education, for this century and the next.
We need to hold our public colleges and universities accountable for the growth of hate groups on campus.
We need to expand our roads, railroads and port facilities to match the growth of our population, and to facilitate the growth of our economy.
We need to protect our communities, power lines, and critical infrastructure from severe weather.
We need to protect our environment, as our green areas not only provide a habitat for local wildlife, they also enhance the quality of our lives.
We need to strengthen our families and our communities by joining hands as Americans to say NO to the hate-mongers.
And above all, we need to ensure that our government represents and serves us - all of us - not just a favored few, but "We The People"!
Together, we are "More Than Conquerors", able to overcome any obstacle, and to succeed through any adversity.
Happy Thanksgiving!
May you see God’s grace and beauty all around you as you count your many blessings.
Let us join hands with our friends, families, and neighbors to help the less fortunate - not just for this holiday season, but always.
God loves us all, and He calls on us all to love each other.
