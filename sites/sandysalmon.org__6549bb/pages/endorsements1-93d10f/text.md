Endorsements
Iowa Association of Business and Industry (ABI) - Friend of Iowa Business
"ABI is proud to endorse candidates who, like our organization, have placed Iowa's workforce at the top of their priorities.
We want to ensure the reforms and policies that have benefited Iowa employers and the hundreds of thousands of individuals they employ stay in place.
ABI and Iowa Industry Political Action Committee (IIPAC) strive to identify candidates for the Legislature on both sides of the aisle who understand and appreciate the challenges businesses are up against on a daily basis.
Candidates are evaluated on their support for a competitive business climate and their focus on a regulatory climate conducive for job creation.
Our organization is confident these candidates will approach their role as elected officials with job creation and economic stability in mind."
Iowa Farm Bureau Federation – Friend of Agriculture
"Candidates earn the Friend of Agriculture designation by supporting issues that directly impact Iowa’s agricultural economy.
We thank you for recognizing the role agriculture and rural communities play in shaping the future of our state.
With one in every five Iowans employed in agriculture and ag-related industries, it is essential to have strong advocates for agriculture representing Iowa.
We must elect leaders who will continue to be champions for agriculture and the hard-working Iowa families who make their living feeding and fueling the world."
Iowa Renewable Fuels Association (IRFA)
"On behalf of Iowa's biodiesel and ethanol producers, the Iowa Renewable Fuels Association wants to thank you for your continued support for renewable fuels.
IRFA works continuously with our members to support legislators who understand the value renewable fuels provide to Iowa's economy."
Iowa Right to Life
