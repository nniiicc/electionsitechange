This policy outlines our practices regarding the collection, use, and sharing of personal information through our website.
We value the trust you place in us and handle your personal information with the utmost care and respect.
Types of Information We Collect Personal Identification Information: This includes any information that can identify you personally, such as your name, email address, postal address, and phone number.

Usage Data: We gather information on how you access and interact with our services, including, but not limited to, your IP address, browser type, browser version, the pages of our website that you visit, the time and date of your visit, and other diagnostic data.
How We Collect Your Information Direct Submissions: When you register for events, subscribe to our newsletters, or engage with us directly through email or other means, you voluntarily provide us with your personal information.
Use and Sharing of Your Information Pierson for Colorado uses the information collected to: · Provide, maintain, and improve our services and communications. · Inform you about updates, events, and activities related to our party. · Support and respond to your inquiries and feedback.
We uphold strict privacy policies to ensure that your personal information is not sold, rented, released, or traded to third parties without your consent, except in cases where we are legally obligated to do so.
Any sharing of personal information with third parties is strictly for essential operational purposes in line with your privacy rights.
Your Privacy Rights SMS Opt-Out: If you no longer wish to receive text messages from us, you can opt-out by simply responding with “STOP” to the number from which you received the message.
Upon receiving your request, we will cease sending you further text messages.
Protecting Your Data Pierson for Colorado takes serious measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
We implement suitable physical, electronic, and managerial procedures designed to safeguard the information we collect, ensuring both your data protection and your privacy rights.
