Proactive Care Model: Shifts healthcare from a reactive, crisis-only system to a proactive model that prevents emergencies before they start by delivering essential care directly to the community.
Telehealth-at-Dispatch Efficiency: This system lets clinicians do real-time telehealth consultations at the point of 911 dispatch, so they may assess patients remotely and fix non-emergency problems without having to send an ambulance.
The "Medic-at-Your-Door" Service: Sends highly trained medical professionals to your home for follow-up care, management of chronic diseases, and preventative screenings.
Advanced Clinical Integration: Includes nurse practitioners in response teams so they can provide high-level diagnostics on-site, making sure that care is safe, effective, and directed by a doctor.
Mobile Mother & Prenatal Health: Conducts home-based prenatal and postpartum checkups to lower the high rate of maternal health issues that could be avoided.
Decentralized Reproductive Access: Offers home-based advice and screenings for STI/HIV prevention.
Drastic Emergency Room Diversion: Prevent non-urgent patients from going to expensive emergency rooms and direct them toward home-based care, which saves the state's Medicaid safety net money.
Lessening of Fire Department Overhead: Cuts down on "nuisance" calls, which lowers the operational expenses of local fire departments and keeps specialist units "in service" for life-threatening crises.
