As your candidate for the Colorado State Legislature, I’ll work to ensure our state is safe, prepared, and resilient—especially against the growing threat of wildfires.
My Priorities Strengthening Emergency Response Fully fund emergency services and training for first responders.
Prioritize mental health support for firefighters and EMS personnel.
Building Resilient Communities Develop a public statewide wildfire hazard map.
Invest in disaster preparedness programs and responsible land use practices.
Community Engagement and Education Implement fire safety education programs to improve wildland/urban interface awareness.
Create fire service apprenticeship opportunities.
Promote neighborhood safety initiatives.
Accountability in Insurance Demand transparency in insurance policies and claims.
Prevent financial discrimination and "bluelining" in high-risk areas.
Advocating for Policy Changes Create a State Fire Marshall position that can be the central hub for code enforcement and wildfire response.
Strengthen fire codes and expand grant programs for home retrofits.
Support wildfire resilience strategies and ecosystem health initiatives.
Fund statewide wildfire detection cameras that can identify a fire and accurately transmit the location to responders.
Public safety starts with preparation and action.
