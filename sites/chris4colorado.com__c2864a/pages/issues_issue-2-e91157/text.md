This principle is essential to ensuring dignity and economic stability for all Coloradans.
Here’s why this matters: Rising costs for housing, food, and necessities outpace wages, leaving many families struggling.
Economic inequality continues to widen, impacting communities across the state.
Fair wages help individuals and families build a secure future and contribute to local economies.
A liveable wage benefits everyone: It enhances the stability of households.
It boosts productivity by creating a motivated, engaged workforce.
It attracts skilled workers and businesses looking for strong, sustainable communities.
Workers must also have the right to organize: Organizing allows employees to negotiate for better pay, benefits, and safe working conditions.
It builds a fair balance of power between workers and employers.
It fosters collaboration and shared success in the workplace.
When prioritizing fair pay and workers’ rights, we uplift families, communities, and the economy.
