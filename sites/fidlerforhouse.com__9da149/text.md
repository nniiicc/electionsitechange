Representative Jennifer Fidler Announces Re-Election Bid for Alabama House District 94
“Local Roots.
Proven Leadership.”
Silverhill, AL — Alabama State Representative Jennifer Fidler has officially announced her campaign for re-election to House District 94, pledging to continue delivering strong, practical leadership for Baldwin County.
From a heritage family of Baldwin County, Representative Fidler brings more than 25 years of public service experience to the State House.
From managing public works as Fairhope’s longtime director to serving on Fairhope’s planning and zoning board, she’s known for her no-nonsense approach and deep commitment to the community.
“I’m a problem solver,” Representative Fidler said.
“I’ve spent most my life working in the public service field, making things better for our towns, our families, and our future.
I ran for office because I wanted to stand up for our area.
I wanted to be an advocate for Baldwin County and represent that voice in Montgomery—and that’s what I’ve done.”
Since being elected in 2022, Representative Fidler has earned a reputation for standing up for her district.
Representative Fidler serves on the Agriculture and Forestry Committee, the Public Safety and Homeland Security committee, the Urban and Rural Development Committee, the City and County Government Committee, The Local Legislation Committee, and the Baldwin Delegation.
Rep Fidler is on the steering committee for the rural caucus where she also serves as Treasurer.
Rep Fidler has recently been named Co-chair of the placemaking subcommittee for the Developing a Roadmap for Rural Communities committee.

About Representative Jennifer Fidler
Representative Fidler isn’t just from Baldwin County—she’s part of its fabric.
She grew up here and built a respected career in public service rooted in hard work, integrity, and resulted in one of the best-known small towns in America.
Before being elected to the State House, she managed personnel, two multimillion departments that included infrastructure, storm recovery, and long-term planning for Fairhope, always keeping community needs at the forefront.
She’s a proud supporter of local agriculture and small-town values.
“I am humbled to be able to serve our citizens in this capacity and look forward to working and serving our citizens in the district, in our state and our country over the next several years.
I ask for your vote and support as we continue to work.”
“Baldwin County has grown, and we face new challenges—but the values that built Baldwin haven’t changed,” Fidler said.
“Faith, family, service, and accountability.
That’s what I stand for and will continue to be an advocate of.”
