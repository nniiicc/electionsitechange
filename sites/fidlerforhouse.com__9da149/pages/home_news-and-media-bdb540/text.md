Used for the like, share, comment, and reaction icons Jennifer Fidler #ago HURRICANE UPDATE / OUTAGES EMC REPORTS: roughly 52,300 meters out in the storm, but have restored 32,500.
We are prioritizing getting circuits back online, which we currently have 14 out.
That will restore a large portion of the outages.
400+ linemen and right of way personnel being mobilized today at our basecamp.
FAIRHOPE REPORTS 400 meters out, but most back on.
RIVIERA REPORTS Meters restored since 9am Friday approximately 21,000 5,000 meters restored since midnight Less than 5,000 left to restore.
We have an additional 100 contractors working right now.
As we recover from the hurricane, I want our District 94 neighbors and families across Baldwin County to have reliable information about power outages and emergency updates.
The apps are a good way to keep updated. 🔌 Baldwin EMC Download the Baldwin EMC app: iPhone Android Check the outage map or report an outage at 251-989-6247.
To enroll in outage texting, text BALDWIN to 85700 from the phone number on your account, then text OUTAGE to report an outage. (apps.apple.com, play.google.com, baldwinemc.com) 🔌 Riviera Utilities Download the Riviera Utilities app to report outages and receive updates: iPhone | Android.
Check the outage map and status.
Report outages: Foley: 251-943-4999 | Daphne: 251-625-4999. (apps.apple.com, play.google.com, rivierautilities.com) 🔌 Fairhope Utilities Report outages at 251-928-8003, available 24 hours a day.
Follow Fairhope’s outage and road closure updates. (fairhopeal.gov, fairhopeal.gov) 🔌 Robertsdale Utilities Report outages at 251-947-8950, Monday–Friday, 7 a.m.–4 p.m., or 251-947-2222 after hours. (robertsdale.org) 🔌 Alabama Power Check the outage map and storm center or report an outage at 1-800-888-2726. (alabamapower.com) 📲 Baldwin County EMA For county information and emergency notifications, download the Baldwin County AL Commission app: iPhone | Android.
Sign up for Alert Baldwin to receive emergency alerts by text, phone, or email. (apps.apple.com, play.google.com, baldwincountyal.gov) For official storm and recovery announcements, visit Baldwin County EMA’s storm updates and baldwincountyal.gov. (baldwincountyal.gov) Please report outages directly to the provider listed on your electric bill, stay away from downed power lines, and call 911 for immediate danger. (baldwinemc.com, alabamapower.com) Thank you to our utility crews, first responders, and everyone helping their neighbors.
Please check on those around you, especially anyone who may need extra assistance—we have always been a community that takes care of one another.
I feel like God sent a hug yesterday as we dodged this bullet, so I’m thankful.
It’s time to help neighbors as they need.
Stay safe in your clean up.
God bless, — Representative Jennifer Fidler Alabama House District 94 ...
