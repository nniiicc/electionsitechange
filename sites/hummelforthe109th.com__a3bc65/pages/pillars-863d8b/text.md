Solutions by Community
Our region is growing — and that’s a good thing when it’s done right.
That means planning ahead, investing in infrastructure, and making sure municipalities have a real seat at the table before decisions are made.
Strong communities don’t happen by accident.
They’re planned — together.
Public Safety, Mental Health, and Addiction
Public safety isn’t just about enforcement — it’s about prevention.
When mental health and addiction go untreated, police are asked to fill gaps they weren’t meant to handle.
We can do better by investing in treatment, recovery, and support systems that reduce crises and keep communities safer.
Personal Interaction
State representatives don’t spend most of their time making speeches or in their offices.
They spend it listening, problem-solving, and showing up.
In rural PA, the job is often about helping people navigate state systems, advocating for communities that don’t always get attention, and bringing local realities into statewide decisions.
It’s relationship-based.
It’s showing up in the whole 109th to listen.
And when it’s done well, communities are heard at the state level, and steady work leads to lasting change.
Protecting Local Jobs and Farms
In Columbia County and across the 109th, our farms, small businesses, and skilled trades aren’t separate issues — they’re all part of how our communities make a living.
Protecting local jobs means:
• Preserving working farms
• Supporting agritourism and farm markets
• Encouraging farmer-first solar solutions
• Investing in workforce development and skilled trades
• Supporting small businesses
• Creating opportunities that keep young people here
When local businesses succeed and farms stay in production, our communities stay strong.
Cost of Living
For many families across the 109th, everyday life is getting more expensive.
Housing, medical care, groceries, transportation — the basics add up quickly.
Communities also need safe places to walk, bike, and stay active so families can stay healthy.
Addressing the cost of living means focusing on the things that affect daily life:
• Housing affordability
• Medical care costs
• Food insecurity
• Transportation access
• Trail systems and walkability
• Better health outcomes
• Reliable basic services
Strong communities are built when people can afford to live, work, and raise a family right here at home.
