David is a dedicated family man, Air Force veteran, successful business owner, and committed community leader who has called Neenah home since 1993.
Married to his wife Lucy for 38 years, David and Lucy are proud parents of five grown children.
Together, they enjoy traveling, kayaking, daily walks, spending time with friends and family, and working out.
For the past 30 years, David and Lucy have been active members of Christ the Rock Community Church, where they have volunteered extensively across multiple departments and causes.
David proudly served in the United States Air Force.
After his military service, he built a distinguished career in radon mitigation and environmental safety.
He is the current owner of Radon Safe Homes and Compassion Homes.
Previously, he owned Radon Specialists of WI, which he successfully sold in 2019.
Recognized nationally for his expertise, David served on the national board of the American Association of Radon Scientists and Technologists (AARST), where he held leadership roles including Vice President and President.
Address Rising Costs:
As a husband, father, and small business owner, I see the impact rising costs have on the family budget.
Groceries, energy prices, housing, childcare costs, and property taxes are stretching families thin.
The middle-class is the backbone of Wisconsin's economy and I will work to provide relief for families, retirees, and those with kids in childcare.
You deserve to keep more of your hard-earned money.
Healthcare Affordability:
Having access to affordable healthcare is important for Wisconsin families, especially with continued rising costs.
I believe in price transparency for healthcare, protecting those with pre-existing conditions, supporting postpartum care for mothers, and finding effective solutions to reduce costs and increase access.
Empowering Parents and Putting Kids First:
I support efforts to ensure transparency, options, and information are available to parents to make the best decisions on their child's education.
Providing a quality education requires adequate resources in the classroom and accountability to ensure students are succeeding.
