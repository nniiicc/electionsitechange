Donate Meet Richard Ivy Serving The Community Richard Ivy is a lifelong Muncie resident, public servant, and community leader with more than 25 years of service to Delaware County.
He currently serves as Human Resources Director for the Muncie Sanitary District, where he oversees personnel operations and works to ensure accountability, efficiency, and responsible stewardship of public resources.
Previously serving as Deputy Mayor for the City of Muncie, Richard has built his career on honesty, integrity, fiscal responsibility, and results-driven leadership.
His experience in city government, infrastructure oversight, workforce management, and community development has prepared him to effectively represent District 34 at the Statehouse.
Richard believes in a grassroots approach — listening first, working collaboratively, and always putting people before politics.
His priorities include strengthening infrastructure, supporting public safety, expanding economic development, and creating greater opportunities for youth, seniors, and working families across Indiana.
A husband, father, grandfather, and active member of Antioch Missionary Baptist Church, Richard remains committed to restoring trust, transparency, and compassion in public service.
