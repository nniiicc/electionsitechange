ISSUES
Neal Jackson: Your Voice for Life and Liberty
Pro-Life
I am pro-life and believe that abortion is destroying the future of our country.
Scripture clearly states, that life begins at conception and therefore, we must protect the rights of the unborn.
As a father of five and a former foster parent, I will fight every day for life.
Champion for Personal Freedom
The strength of North Carolina is the character of our people, not the size of our government.
Individuals, families, and communities should be the ones that make the choices that affect them, not a dictatorial government.
I will continue to work to return more decisions and control to We the People.
Parental Rights
The most important things in life are those closest to home: our faith, our family, and our freedoms.
I will fight every day to protect traditional family values and return decisions to parents for the raising of their children.
Public Safety
The movement to defund police departments has brought about increased crime, murder, and rape in every demographic.
Police officers risk their lives every day protecting our families and ensuring the safety of our neighborhoods.
We should value their service and give them the needed resources for success.
Gun Rights
The US Constitution gives each citizen the right to protect ourselves, our families, and our property.
The second amendment is the visible symbol of our freedom and one of the protections against tyranny.
As a gun owner with a concealed carry permit, I will always fight to defend the second amendment.
Education Champion
Education is one of the most important issues North Carolina faces.
The key to a child’s success is access to quality education.
I, therefore, support school choice for parents.
I will work to strengthen all of the options available to parents, public, charter and private.
Jobs Champion
Work was ordained by God since the beginning of time, and I promise to work to ensure an environment where businesses want to relocate to North Carolina for our common sense tax and regulatory environment.
