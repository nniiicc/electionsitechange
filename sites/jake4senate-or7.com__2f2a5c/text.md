Policy Priorities
Community Safety
We prioritize safety by providing unwavering support for law enforcement, improving our mental-health crisis response, and investing in common sense crime prevention.
Election Integrity
Election integrity means making every legal vote count and every illegal vote impossible.
I will work to clean up voter rolls, eliminate non-citizen voting, require photo ID to register and to vote in person, and end automatic voter registration.
Infrastructure
I will prioritize investing in our roads, bridges, and rural mobility to keep Oregon moving.
I am committed to lowering gas taxes and ensuring every dollar spent is efficient and accountable.
Our Future Together
I believe in a state that works for everyone.
My vision for Oregon is built on stability, security, and opportunity for every family.
Economic Stability
I will work to help small business growth and attract major industry to Oregon through responsible fiscal stewardship.
By revitalizing our timber industry, expanding workforce training, and promoting entrepreneurship and invention, we will build a robust economy.
Supporting Veterans & Families
As a veteran who served in the Air National Guard, I am dedicated to expanding healthcare, benefits, and job opportunities for those who wore the uniform.
My commitment is also deeply personal; as the father of a disabled daughter, I will fight for affordable housing and robust support for all disabled individuals and their families to ensure our most vulnerable neighbors are never left behind.
Restore Real Education Standards
We must get back to basics in education by restoring the three R’s—reading, writing, and arithmetic—and protecting our classrooms from CRT and socialist indoctrination.
On top of that, we should restore strong civics and history instruction, end sex education below 7th grade, restore rigorous high school graduation standards, and strengthen workforce-ready education that prepares students for real jobs.
