Joshua Bryant Announces Run for State Senate Grant Hodges November 5, 2021 Rogers, Arkansas (November 5, 2021) - Representative Joshua Bryant announced today his campaign for Senate District 32.
The district covers eastern Benton County and part of northeastern Washington County.
A small business owner and Marine Corps veteran, Rep.
Bryant was elected to the Arkansas House of Representatives in 2020, where he took on the Little Rock establishment and stood strongly for conservative principles.
"I didn't go to Little Rock for a paycheck or to make friends with lobbyists and special interests.
I went to represent the people who elected me, to fight for their constitutional rights and to make state government work for them," said Bryant.
"I passed legislation that expanded 2nd Amendment rights in Arkansas, and I passed the Earn and Learn Act, which allows Arkansans to learn on the job and apprentice while earning a paycheck, removing barriers by the state that prevented people from working and providing for their families.
"When President Biden issued his unconstitutional executive order mandating vaccines for private businesses with 100 employees or more, I filed legislation to protect Arkansans from having to choose between their conscience and their livelihood.
Though the Legislature did not take all the steps I thought were necessary to counter Biden's federal government overreach, we did pass my bill to require exemptions from the federal mandate.
I didn't cower to the Left or big business special interests; I stood for my constituents, for hard-working people who want to keep their job without being forced to sacrifice their rights and freedoms.
"We cannot afford to sit back and leave the hard work to others.
I have never shied away from a fight and doing my part for my country.
I've served in uniform as a Sergeant in the Marine Corps, I served my community locally as a Benton County Justice of the Peace, and I've been honored to serve as a Representative in Little Rock.
My priorities have never changed: God, Family, and Country.
I promise to take those values and our shared conservative principles with me as I earn your vote to be your voice in the Arkansas Senate." Learn more and join our campaign at www.BryantForArkansas.com.
About Joshua Bryant: Joshua Bryant is an Arkansas native, raised in Benton County and graduating from BCCS in Rogers in 1996.
The next year he joined the United States Marine Corps, where he served five years honorably to the rank of Sergeant.
After leaving military service, Bryant spent twelve years working for the Federal Aviation Administration, where he focused on making air travel safer and more efficient.
He joined a construction venture in 2014 and, together with his business partner, grew the company into one of the most successful firms in Northwest Arkansas.
Joshua has been married to his wife Jill for 23 years and is a proud father to a son and a daughter.
The Bryant family attends Engaged Ministries Church in Lowell.
