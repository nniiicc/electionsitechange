Senator Tom Cotton Endorses Rep.
Joshua Bryant for State Senate District 32 In a statement endorsing Representative Joshua Bryant’s campaign, Senator Tom Cotton said, “Joshua Bryant served as a Marine and is now fighting for our conservative values in Little Rock.
When President Biden pushed unconstitutional mandates, Representative Bryant fought back and protected Arkansas.
I fully endorse Joshua Bryant so he can continue that courageous leadership in the Arkansas Senate.” “I’m honored to have the endorsement of Senator Cotton,” said Bryant.
“He is a powerful voice for our state in Congress, and he understands what is at stake for our country right now.
As veterans and elected officials, we share a dedication to public service, and as conservatives we both believe in fighting for liberty, limited government, and the Constitution.
I thank Senator Cotton for his support in this campaign.” Joshua Bryant is a Republican candidate for State Senate District 32.
He is a Marine Corps veteran, a business owner, and is currently in his first term in the Arkansas House of Representatives.
