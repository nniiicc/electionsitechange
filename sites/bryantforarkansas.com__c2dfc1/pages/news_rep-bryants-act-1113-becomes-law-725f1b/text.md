On January 14, 2022, Act 1113 became law.
Rep.
Joshua Bryant was the lead sponsor of the law, which allows for exemptions from President Biden’s overreaching federal mandate on COVID-19 vaccinations.
It allows workers the option to test weekly or show natural immunity to the virus.
