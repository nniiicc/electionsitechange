Representative Joshua Bryant is your conservative Republican candidate for Arkansas Senate District 32 in 2022
About
Representative Joshua Bryant is an Arkansas native, raised in Benton County and graduating from high school in Rogers in 1996.
The next year he joined the U.S.
Marine Corps, where he served for five years.
He spent 12 years working for the Federal Aviation Administration, focused on making air travel safer and more efficient.
He joined a construction venture in 2014 and grew the company into one of the most successful firms in NWA.
Joshua has been married to his wife Jill for 23 years and is a proud father to a son and a daughter.
The Bryant family attends Engaged Ministries Church in Lowell.
