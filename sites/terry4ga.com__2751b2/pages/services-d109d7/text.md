Constituent Services
How We Can Help
Serving the people of our district means more than representing you at the State Capitol—it means helping you navigate state government when you need assistance.
Our office is here to help constituents connect with Georgia state agencies, find information and resources, and address problems involving state programs or services.
Whether you need help understanding an agency process, are having difficulty getting a response, or simply aren’t sure where to start, please reach out.
While we cannot guarantee a specific outcome or intervene in legal matters, we can help identify the right agency, make inquiries on your behalf, and work to ensure your concerns receive appropriate attention.

How do I search for a bill?
Use the Legislation → Advanced Search feature on the General Assembly website:
https://www.legis.ga.gov
You can search by:
- Bill number (e.g., HB 123, SB 456)
- Session year
- Keywords in the bill title or summary
Each bill page includes:
- Current status (committee referrals, votes, amendments)
- Full text of the bill and any amendments
- Sponsor(s) and session information
How Do I Look Up Statutes & Legislative History
If you know a statute in the Official Code of Georgia Annotated (O.C.G.A.) and want the legislative history:
- Georgia Archives guide:Researching Legislative Intent or Legislative History
https://www.georgiaarchives.org/research/legislative_intent
Steps include:
- Find the statute in the O.C.G.A. and review its history line.
- Identify the bill number and session year.
- Use the General Assembly website to locate the bill text and amendments.
- Review Journals or committee minutes if more context is needed (though Georgia legislative history is limited).
More guidance: https://www.law.uga.edu/researching-legislative-history-enacted-georgia-statute
Where can I find voting information?
- Georgia Voting Information (Georgia.gov):
https://georgia.gov/voting
How to register, vote absentee, view a sample ballot, and find your county registrar.
- Voter Resources (Secretary of State):
https://sos.ga.gov/voter-resources
Guides for early voting, election-day voting, absentee voting, and registration checks.
- My Voter Page (MVP):
https://www.mvp.sos.ga.gov
Check registration status, find your polling place, view your sample ballot, and see your elected officials.
- How-to Guide: Voting (Secretary of State):
https://sos.ga.gov/how-to-guide/how-guide-voting
Details ID requirements, voting hours, provisional ballots, and more.
