02 Reducing Pharmaceutical Prices.
Before Jason helped author the most comprehensive PBM reform in Florida history, just three PBMs controlled roughly 80% of the entire prescription-drug market.
Now, with more competition in the market, patients are seeing lower prices for the medicine they depend on. ~80% Share of the drug market controlled by just three PBMs when Jason acted SB 1550 The landmark 2023 Prescription Drug Reform Act, signed into law 18,000 Floridians protected from losing access to high-cost HIV medication How Jason delivered Sponsored SB 1550 (2023) , requiring PBMs to hold a certificate of authority under the Florida Insurance Code, mandating manufacturer disclosure of large wholesale-price increases, and adding anti-steering protections so patients aren't forced into PBM-affiliated mail-order pharmacies.
Protected patient choice for independent pharmacies in Sanford, Lake Mary, and Oviedo , keeping neighborhood pharmacists in the network.
In 2026, he set aside his own follow-up PBM bill late in the session to preserve a stopgap funding measure of nearly $31 million for Florida's AIDS Drug Assistance Program, keeping roughly 18,000 people from losing access to high-cost HIV medication.
03 Fighting The Opioid Crisis.
The opioid epidemic has torn through Seminole and Orange counties for too long.
Jason is pairing tough-on-crime laws with expanded recovery resources to save lives.
Over six budget years, Jason has directed more than $27.5 million to prevention, treatment, and recovery programs serving District 10, from care coordination for people in crisis to prevention programs that reach kids before addiction starts. $27.5M+ Directed to opioid prevention, treatment & recovery serving District 10 3,330 Youth reached through Boys & Girls Clubs prevention programming 2 laws Toughening penalties near treatment centers and for deadly dealers How Jason delivered Secured Local Funding Initiative Requests including the Hope & Healing Center (#M) , Project Opioid (#M) , and the Orlando Health Opioid Navigator ($500K) , which connects patients to treatment after discharge, serving nearly # people in District 10.
Sponsored SB 190 (2022) , adding enhanced penalties for distributing controlled substances within 1,000 feet of a treatment facility, recovery residence, or clinic.
Sponsored SB 280 (2023) , expanding murder liability for dealers whose drugs cause a fatal overdose, with tougher penalties for repeat offenders.
04 Expanding Mental Health Resources.
Too many families hit a wall when they try to get mental health care: coverage they can't understand and clinics they can't afford.
Jason has worked to knock that wall down, on both the insurance side and the ground level.
His record earned him the Florida Mental Health Advocacy Coalition's 2022 Legislator of the Year award, and he followed it up by funding a brand-new free clinic right here in Seminole County.
2022 Named Legislator of the Year by the Florida Mental Health Advocacy Coalition $125,000 Secured to launch a free mental health clinic in Seminole County ~1,200 Uninsured & underinsured residents the new clinic aims to serve each year How Jason delivered Sponsored SB 1024 (2021) , Increasing Access to Mental Health Care, requiring insurers to notify patients of their mental-health-coverage rights and the state complaint hotline, and requiring the state to track and report coverage complaints every year.
Secured $125,000 (LFIR #1465, 2025) to establish a free and charitable mental health clinic in Seminole County for uninsured, underinsured, and economically disadvantaged residents.
Paired state dollars with existing county-funded providers, including IMPOWER's counseling for homeless and aging-out foster youth, so the money reinforces local care instead of duplicating it.
