The real challenges facing Seminole and North Central Orange, and how Jason is solving them.
Protecting Our Rural Boundary Jason is a farmer who will protect rural communities from overzealous development.
In the Senate, Jason passed legislation to create the Florida Wildlife Corridor , which protects millions of acres of Florida Heartland for generations to come.
A Winter Springs native who understands the importance of conserving our rural land, Jason will fight to keep our rural boundary intact.
Affordability Inflation, property taxes, and insurance premiums have squeezed household budgets across Seminole and Orange, hitting teachers, first responders, and working families the hardest.
Jason wrote the homestead exemption that saves teachers, first responders, and military families an estimated $81 million in its first year, and backed the gas-tax and baby-necessity tax holidays.
He'll keep pushing targeted tax relief and property-tax reform while holding the line on state spending so Florida stays affordable.
Insurance Homeowners and drivers across the district have faced years of rising premiums driven by lawsuit abuse, reinsurance costs, and storm risk.
Jason supported the 2022–2023 tort reforms now lowering costs statewide, with auto-insurance refunds and 2026 rate cuts beginning to reach drivers.
He'll keep the pressure on insurers to pass savings to policyholders and bring more private carriers back into Florida.
Local Springs The springs and spring-fed rivers that define Central Florida's outdoors, the Wekiva, the St.
Johns, and the greater Silver Springs basin, are seeing lower flows and rising nutrient pollution as the region grows.
Jason's septic-to-sewer law cuts the nitrogen loading that fouls spring water, and his Wildlife Corridor work protects the land that recharges the aquifer.
He'll keep funding spring and river restoration and defend the Wekiva-Ocala Greenway as a buffer against development-driven pollution.
Animal Welfare Floridians expect humane treatment of animals and real oversight of the exotic-animal attractions that dot the I-4 corridor, an expectation state law hasn't always kept up with.
Jason secured state support for the Central Florida Zoo to care for rescued animals and protect its national accreditation, and backs tighter FWC permitting for exotic-animal imports.
He'll keep pushing for stronger oversight so animals are protected before a crisis, not after.
