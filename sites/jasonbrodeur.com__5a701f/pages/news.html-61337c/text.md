Press Release · August 25, 2026 Seminole County Sheriff Dennis Lemma Endorses Jason Brodeur for Re-Election Lemma praises Brodeur's leadership and commitment to keeping Central Florida safe.
SANFORD, FL — Seminole County Sheriff Dennis Lemma today endorsed State Senator Jason Brodeur for re-election to the Florida Senate.
“Senator Jason Brodeur has shown exceptional leadership throughout his time in Tallahassee.
He has been a leading voice in the fight against the opioid crisis, securing critical resources for treatment and recovery,” said Sheriff Dennis Lemma.
“He has had the tough conversations needed in equipping and protecting the men and women who put their lives on the line for Florida communities every day.
I am proud to endorse Jason Brodeur for the Florida Senate.” “I am honored to have the support of Sheriff Dennis Lemma,” said Brodeur.
“Dennis has dedicated his career to protecting Seminole County and has earned the trust of law enforcement officers and families across our community.
I am grateful for his friendship, his leadership, and his partnership as we continue working to keep Central Florida safe.” Lemma's endorsement adds to the growing coalition of local leaders supporting Brodeur's re-election campaign and his record of delivering results for Central Florida.
Jason Brodeur serves as President Pro Tempore of the Florida Senate and represents Senate District 10.
A longtime Seminole County resident, local business owner, and former member of the Florida House of Representatives, Brodeur has built a record of turning Central Florida's priorities into action.
He has delivered results to protect Florida's water and natural resources, strengthen public safety, expand access to health care, support small businesses, and bring critical funding home to Seminole and Orange counties.
Brodeur continues to work across the aisle to solve problems and improve the quality of life for the families and communities he represents.
