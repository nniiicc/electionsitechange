Seminole County and North Central Orange voters have three easy options.
Confirm your registration, then choose the way that works best for you.
Vote by Mail In Florida, vote-by-mail requests do not carry over year to year, so you must submit a new request each general election.
Request your ballot from your county Supervisor of Elections, fill it out at home, and return it early.
Vote Early Early voting lets you cast your ballot in person before Election Day at designated sites.
Early voting runs Monday, October 19 through Sunday, November 1 — 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. in Seminole County and 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. in Orange County.
Check your county Supervisor of Elections for locations.
Vote on Election Day Polls are open on Election Day at your assigned precinct.
Confirm your polling place and bring a valid photo and signature ID.
