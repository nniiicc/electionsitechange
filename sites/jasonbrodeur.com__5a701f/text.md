A record of service.
Jason Brodeur grew up in the district, attending Jackson Heights Middle School and Oviedo High School.
He earned two degrees from the University of Florida, spent nearly 12 years at Procter & Gamble, and founded a local health care consulting business in Sanford.
He served on the City of Sanford Charter Review Board and the Seminole County Planning and Zoning Commission, and led the Seminole County Regional Chamber of Commerce as President and CEO before representing the community in the Florida House and Senate.
