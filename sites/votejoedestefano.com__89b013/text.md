Meet Joe
Joe DeStefano was first elected to the New York State Assembly in 2018 and represents Assembly District 3.
His public-service background includes the Medford Fire Department, Medford Volunteer Ambulance, the Suffolk County Sheriff's Office and local civic and fire-district organizations.
Priorities
Joe focuses on public safety, affordability, local control and taxpayer accountability.
Joe focuses on taxes, utility costs, state spending and household costs on Long Island.
DeStefano has publicly supported local authority over land-use, zoning and development decisions.
Joe highlights fiscal oversight, responsible government spending and stronger enforcement against fraud and waste.
