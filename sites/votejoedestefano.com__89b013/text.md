☰ Meet Joe Priorities Record News Get Involved Donate New York State Assembly • 3rd District Meet Joe Donate Meet Joe A Lifetime of Community Service Joe DeStefano was first elected to the New York State Assembly in 2018 and represents Assembly District 3.
His public-service background includes the Medford Fire Department, Medford Volunteer Ambulance, the Suffolk County Sheriff's Office and local civic and fire-district organizations.
New York State Assembly First elected in 2018 Assembly District 3 Central Brookhaven / Suffolk County Public Safety 27 years with the Suffolk County Sheriff's Office First Responder Service Fire and emergency medical service background Priorities Current Policy Areas Joe focuses on public safety, affordability, local control and taxpayer accountability.
Public Safety Joe supports stronger tools for law enforcement and courts and emphasizes support for first responders.
Affordability Joe focuses on taxes, utility costs, state spending and household costs on Long Island.
Local Control DeStefano has publicly supported local authority over land-use, zoning and development decisions.
Taxpayer Accountability Joe highlights fiscal oversight, responsible government spending and stronger enforcement against fraud and waste.
Record of Service Public Service Before Albany DeStefano's biography describes decades of service in emergency response, public-safety communications and community organizations.
Medford Fire Department Public service beginning in 1980.
Medford Volunteer Ambulance Long-term emergency medical service involvement.
Suffolk County Sheriff's Office 27 years as a Public Safety Communication Supervisor.
Community Leadership Roles with civic, fire-district and public-employee organizations.
In the Assembly Issues & Legislative Work Energy Costs DeStefano has called for changes to state energy policy and has cited rising utility costs and grid reliability.
Emergency Services Joe supports volunteer firefighters, EMS personnel and emergency dispatchers.
Public Safety He has advocated changes to New York's criminal-justice policies and greater discretion for judges and prosecutors.
Government Oversight Joe has supported legislation intended to strengthen Medicaid-fraud reporting and enforcement.
Local Control He supported Brookhaven's authority to consider its own data-center moratorium while opposing a statewide moratorium.
