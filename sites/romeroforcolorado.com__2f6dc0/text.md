MEET DWAYNE ROMERO
Forged in Service
United States Army Engineer Officer Dwayne Romero is a husband, father, combat veteran, small business owner, and Democratic candidate for Colorado’s Third Congressional District.
Raised by a single mom, Dwayne understands the challenges working Coloradans are facing.
At age 12, Dwayne started folding laundry at his family’s apartment complex to help the family make ends’ meet, an experience that taught him hard work, responsibility, and the value of a dollar.
