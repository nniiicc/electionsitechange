Meirose, BS.Ed., MS.Ed., Ed.D., SGT (Ret) Proud Grandfather Join The Team Follow on Facebook Guarding Top Priorities First Amendment “Congress shall make no law respecting an establishment of religion, or prohibiting the free exercise thereof; or abridging the freedom of speech, or of the press; or the right of the people peaceably to assemble, and to petition the Government for a redress of grievances.” Our First Amendment rights are fundamental to the success and future of the State of South Dakota.
It is the government’s responsibility to ensure that these rights are protected and not infringed upon.
Tenth Amendment “The powers not delegated to the United States by the Constitution, nor prohibited by it to the States, are reserved to the States respectively, or to the people.” The past few years we have seen a stronger encroachment of the Federal Government into the sovereignty of the States.
It is very important that we keep local governments strong and the policy and decision making as close to the people as we possibly can.
Sanctity of Life Human Life begins at conception.
As a society we must work to protect human life from the moment of conception to the moment of death.
These actions not only include the end to abortions but also to ensure that the sanctity of life is maintained throughout life.
We work toward strong families, available medical and mental health care, and the dignity of life of our elder population.
Taxes Our tax structure needs to maintain a balance between what is fair and reasonable for individuals and businesses to pay and providing essential government services.
