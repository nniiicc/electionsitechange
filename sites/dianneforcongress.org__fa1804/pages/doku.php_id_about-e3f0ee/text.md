Dianne Blais had numerous jobs in Washington, DC and Virginia before becoming an active volunteer while raising 4 children and hosting over 100 foster children.
She has been active in numerous organizations including the League of Women Voters although now she is mostly involved in peace organizations – she is horrified by U.S. policy with other countries – our bombs, sanctions and interference in other countries' governments.
The US Peace Memorial Foundation lists many of the peace activists and peace organizations she admires.
For about ten years, she has been most involved with:
Women's International League for Peace and Freedom
Jane Addams founded WILPF in 1915 - Dianne is convener of the Jane Addams branch of WILPF US.
Dianne supports and would appreciate if you'd sign the petitions at World Beyond War and Project Enduring Peace (PEP).
She shares her vision for the world with Founding Mothers Movement for peace and gender equity.
Ralph Nader and Jill Stein are models she tries to emulate.
