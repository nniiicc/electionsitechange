Chris’ Priorities
“As a former police officer and prosecutor, I took an oath to serve and protect Kansas families.
That’s what I will do as Attorney General – put Kansans first and ensure no politician or corporation is above the law.”
– Chris Mann, candidate for Kansas Attorney General
The Attorney General’s office shouldn’t be about Team Red or Team Blue; it should be about Team Kansas and making sure every Kansan feels safe.
The Attorney General of Kansas should have one priority – protecting Kansans.
Chris Mann knew from an early age that he wanted to serve and protect his community.
Following in his father’s footsteps, he chose a career in law enforcement.
But his life was forever changed when, on duty, Chris was struck by a drunk driver during a routine traffic stop in 2002.
The crash and resulting injuries ended his career in uniform.
But Chris knew he wanted to continue to help the people of Kansas, so he became an Assistant District Attorney in Wyandotte County.
Chris prosecuted everything from traffic violations to murders, always focused on protecting Kansans.
Then, as a Senior Staff Attorney with the Kansas Securities Commission, Chris fought to protect consumers from scams, fraud, and corporations that rip people off.
Now, with his own law firm, Chris represents victims of drunk driving crashes.
As a former police officer and prosecutor, Chris has stood toe-to-toe with violent criminals and fought to ensure victims and survivors receive the justice they deserve.
He will bring this same dedication to public safety to the Attorney General’s office.

As Attorney General, Chris will be laser-focused on public safety solutions that confront this crisis head-on, including targeting drug traffickers, supporting local and state law enforcement in getting dangerous drugs off our streets, ensuring all communities have Naloxone readily available, and strengthening prevention and treatment programs.
