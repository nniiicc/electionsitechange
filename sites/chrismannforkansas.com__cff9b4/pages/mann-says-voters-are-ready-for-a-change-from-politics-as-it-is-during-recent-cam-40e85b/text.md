Skip to content Chris Mann for Kansas Meet Chris Priorities Get Involved News Donate Mann says voters are ready for a change from ‘Politics as it is’ during recent campaign fundraiser in Emporia Monday September 28, 2026 Tagan Trahoon | KVOE It is the opinion of Democratic Candidate Chris Mann that voters are tired of “politics as it is” and ready for a change.
Mann shared this thought with residents and KVOE News during a fundraiser in Emporia supporting his campaign for Attorney General Monday evening.
With just over 30 days remaining before the general election, Mann says he and his campaign have been “corner to corner” across Kansas, where he says voters have shared their fatigue with current politics and expressed a desire to see “A public servant” in office.
Mann, a former police officer, prosecutor and victim advocate, says that desire is what makes him, in his opinion, the ideal candidate for the role.
