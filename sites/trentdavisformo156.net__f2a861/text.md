Meet Trent Davis
TRENT DAVIS is a Southwest Missouri native.
Born and raised in the Branson general area, Trent has chosen to settle with his wife and three daughters in the same place he was raised.
Trent knows and believes in the same values of other SWMO community members: Hard work, strong faith in our community, and the belief that our families are the core of a great state.
TRENT understands the hard work needed to face the challenges of SWMO residents.
He has relied on neighbors and friends to build the great community in MO-156.
Trent also understands those who have families formed in bonds of shared experiences.
TRENT DAVIS wants to represent MO-156 in Jefferson City not because he believes he already knows what SWMO residents need, but because he cares about every member of the community and wants to work for every single one of us.
