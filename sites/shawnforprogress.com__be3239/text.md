Why I'm Running.
For too long, we've let ourselves be governed by people who are more interested in controlling us than they are in actually improving our lives.
And the consequences for Iowa have been devastating.
They've starved our schools, poisoned our water, robbed our communities, looted our land, forced Des Moines' will on our bodies, the list goes on...
And what did we get for it?
We didn't get any healthier.
Our kids didn't get any smarter.
Nothing got any cheaper...or easier...or better.
And families left the state in record numbers.
But the good news is the power these people used over us is actually ours, and we can wake up and take it back at any time.
We start by electing people who prioritize Forward Progress.
For us.
