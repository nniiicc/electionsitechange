Keith has also passed valuable border legislation to push back on the failures of the Biden administration at the border, while giving Texas law enforcement officers the support and authority they need to keep our communities safe.
Keith founded his electrical contracting business in 1983 and has grown the company to 225 employees.
He also owns and operates a cattle ranch and is a member of the Texas Farm Bureau.
Keith served on the Forney ISD School Board, including multiple terms as president.
He is also a founder and past-President of the Forney Education Leadership Foundation, and serves on the Board of the Baylor Scott and White Medical Center – Sunnyvale, TX.
A graduate of Dallas Baptist University, Keith and his wife, Annette, have been married for 40 years.
They attend the First Baptist Church of Forney.
They have two grown daughters and four grandchildren.
