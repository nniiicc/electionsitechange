Chelsea's priorities are practical and local.
Priority 1 Priority 2 Priority 3 Strong Schools and a Strong Start Early learning, safe classrooms, respected educators, transparent spending, and school funding that gives every child a fair chance.
Healthcare You Can Count On Protect access close to home, reduce the pressure of prescription costs, and keep medical decisions between patients and their providers.
An Economy Where Working Families Can Thrive Strengthen paychecks, protect workers facing serious illness or disability, and help local small business grow.
Priority 4 Homes Families Can Afford Expand neighborhood-scale housing choices, make smart state investments, and help communities grow without losing what makes them home.
Priority 5 Investing in Our Communities Bring our tax dollars home for local businesses, public spaces, infrastructure, and projects families are proud to use.
