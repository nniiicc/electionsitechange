Why I'm Running
Our country is at a turning point and we need Republicans who will fight, lead, and deliver.
I was raised by a grammar school teacher and a minister.
We were poor at times, later lower middle class.
I started working at 14 years old.
I was raised on personal responsibility, discipline, hard work, and love of country.
Those values shaped who I am.
As an attorney, I've built a career cutting through noise and focusing on results.
In Congress, I will support strong Republican leadership to secure the border, restore law and order, rein in reckless spending, and protect our constitutional freedoms.
I believe we need leaders who will fight, lead, and deliver — not manage decline.
After one term, success means safer streets, stronger election integrity, real spending oversight, affordable living for working families, and schools that prepare young Americans for independence — not dependency.
Our District
California's 30th Congressional District spans some of the most iconic communities in Southern California — from Burbank and Glendale to Pasadena, La Crescenta, the Hollywood Hills, and the rural communities of Agua Dulce and Acton.
These are neighborhoods built by hardworking families, small business owners, and dreamers.
They deserve a congressman who knows these streets, understands the challenges, and will fight for real results.
Scott has lived in this district his entire life.
He's not a career politician looking for a seat — he's a neighbor who's ready to serve.
