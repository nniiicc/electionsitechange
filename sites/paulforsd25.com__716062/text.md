Paul isn't a career politician.
He's a husband, father, patriot, and public servant who spent 34 years with the U.S.
Department of Defense, managing high-level mapping, logistics, and contractor operations in roles that demanded accountability, precision, and the public's trust.
After retiring, he and his wife moved to Colorado to be closer to their sons.
Now he's stepping forward to serve the community they proudly call home.
Leadership is about responsibility, teamwork, and keeping your word, even when the stakes are high.
