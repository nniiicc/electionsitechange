Colorado contribution limits.
State law caps contributions to a state
senate candidate committee at $225 per person, per election, and the
limit applies separately to the primary and the general, so an individual may give up to
$450 across the full cycle.
A registered small donor committee may give
$3,100 per election.
Corporations and labor unions may not contribute to a candidate
committee at all.
Contributions are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal
income tax purposes.
