Colorado contribution limits.
State law caps contributions to a state senate candidate committee at $225 per person, per election , and the limit applies separately to the primary and the general, so an individual may give up to $450 across the full cycle.
A registered small donor committee may give $3,100 per election.
Corporations and labor unions may not contribute to a candidate committee at all.
Contributions are not deductible as charitable contributions for federal income tax purposes. $25 Puts a yard sign in a neighbor's lawn where hundreds of cars pass it every day. $50 Covers the literature for a full precinct walk: one neighborhood, every door. $100 Funds a batch of mailers to households the campaign hasn't reached at the door yet.
