Paul isn't a career politician.
He's a husband, father, patriot, and public servant who has spent a lifetime solving problems, leading with integrity, and serving others.
After earning a degree in cartography from the University of Maryland, he dedicated 34 years to the U.S.
Department of Defense, where he managed high-level mapping, logistics, and contractor operations supporting America's military.
Working in positions that required a Top Secret security clearance taught him the importance of accountability, precision, and earning the public's trust.
After retiring, Paul and his wife moved to Colorado to be closer to their sons and build the next chapter of their lives in the community they now proudly call home.
As Paul watched the challenges facing Colorado and our nation, he felt called to step forward and serve again.
Encouraged by members of his community and grounded in his Christian faith, he entered this race believing leadership begins with courage, conviction, and service.
In His Words Why I'm Running Colorado deserves principled leadership that listens first, serves faithfully, and puts people above politics.
Throughout my career I learned that leadership is about responsibility, teamwork, and keeping your word, even when the stakes are high.
Paul Kohlhoff Paul is committed to earning the trust of every voter in Senate District 25 by working across the community, protecting constitutional freedoms, and advocating for safer neighborhoods, stronger families, honest elections, and a government that is transparent, accountable, and responsive to the people it serves.
He takes no dark money, and he'll be transparent about who funds this campaign.
