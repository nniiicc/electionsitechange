Email Paul → 01 Protecting Parental Rights Families, not government, have the primary voice in raising their children.
Parents are partners, not spectators, and every kid in District 25 deserves a school that teaches the fundamentals and a system that respects the family behind the student.
Paul's Commitment Ensure families, not government, have the primary voice in raising their children.
Full curriculum transparency so parents see what their kids are taught.
Respect the rights of parents in every decision that touches their children's education, health, and upbringing.
02 Crime & Public Safety Safe neighborhoods are the first job of government.
From Broomfield to Westminster to Northglenn, families deserve to feel secure in their homes, and our officers deserve leaders who back them up.
Paul's Commitment Support law enforcement recruitment, training, and retention.
Hold criminals accountable, with real consequences for violent crime, auto theft, and fentanyl dealing.
Put victims and their families ahead of politics.
03 Election Integrity Self-government only works when every voter can trust the count.
Paul will promote secure, transparent, and trustworthy elections, so every legal vote counts and every result is beyond question.
Paul's Commitment Promote secure, transparent, and trustworthy elections.
Back the audits, signature verification, and clean voter rolls that protect every legal ballot.
Support our county clerks and election judges with the resources to do the job right.
04 Government Accountability Government should answer to the people it serves.
Taxpayers deserve to know their voices are heard and their government works for them, not the other way around.
Paul doesn't take dark money, and he'll be transparent about who funds this campaign.
That's the standard he holds himself to, and the standard he'll bring to the Capitol.
Paul's Commitment No dark money.
Full transparency about who funds this campaign.
Support requiring groups that spend in our elections to disclose their donors.
Public, plain-language explanations for every vote he casts.
05 Affordability & Your Paycheck Groceries, gas, rent, fees on everything.
Colorado families are stretched thin, and too many politicians treat your paycheck as their budget fix.
Paul will defend TABOR and the taxpayer protections that keep Colorado affordable.
Families along the US 36 corridor are already stretched; the answer isn't to weaken the limits that protect your wallet.
Paul's Commitment Defend TABOR and the taxpayer's right to vote on tax increases.
Oppose new taxes and the fee end-runs that dodge a public vote.
Make housing easier to build, not more expensive to permit.
