Apr 14 Written By Julia Andrews I am excited to announce that I will be running to represent Milton and Westford in the Vermont House.
The people in our community deserve to be represented by someone willing to take on the issues impacting our everyday lives.
My vision for Vermont is a welcoming place for young people to settle, a place for families to put down roots, and a place for our seniors to age with dignity.
This requires an approach to our state's key issues of housing, healthcare, and education through the lens of affordability, and greater stability.
Now more than ever, our state is under financial pressures that require complex, structural changes to address.
Nibbling around the edges is simply not enough.
We need people who are willing to roll up their sleeves, listen, disagree, and take the facts seriously.
I am a person who can do that work.
In every role I’ve held in my community from Selectboard member to Food Shelf founder, I’ve been recognized as someone who listens, who does their homework, and who knows how to get things accomplished.
I look forward to bringing my energy and expertise back to the Statehouse in 2026 so we can work together towards creating a stronger, more vibrant state.
To learn more about my campaign, please reach out to me directly.
I’m looking forward to hearing what’s important to you.
