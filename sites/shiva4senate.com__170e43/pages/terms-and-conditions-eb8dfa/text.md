Program Description When you opt in, SHIVA 4 SENATE will send you recurring text messages (SMS and MMS) about Dr.

2.
How You Join (Your Consent) You join the Program only by taking a clear, affirmative step to opt in.
To join, go to the SHIVA 4 SENATE text message sign-up form at https://shiva4senate.com/text-updates/ , enter your mobile number, check the optional text-message consent box (the box is never pre-checked) and submit the form.
After you opt in, the first message we send you will confirm your enrollment and tell you how to get help and how to opt out.
Giving us a phone number for another purpose — for example, when you donate, volunteer, sign a petition or pledge your vote — does not by itself enroll you in the Program.
Your consent to receive text messages is voluntary.
It is not a condition of making a donation or purchase, volunteering, or taking part in the campaign in any way.
By opting in, you agree to receive recurring text messages from SHIVA 4 SENATE at the mobile number you provided, including messages sent using automated technology.
Your consent applies only to text messages from SHIVA 4 SENATE .
It does not authorize any other candidate, committee, organization or third party to text you, and we do not transfer or assign your consent to anyone.
3.
Message Frequency This is a recurring message program.
Message frequency varies and may increase around campaign events, fundraising deadlines and Election Day.
4.
Cost Message and data rates may apply.
SHIVA 4 SENATE does not charge you for these messages, but your mobile carrier’s standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile plan.
Contact your carrier for details about your plan.
5.
How to Opt Out You can cancel at any time.
Reply STOP to any message from us.
You may also reply END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, REVOKE or OPT OUT.
When you opt out by text, we will send you one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed.
After that, you will receive no further messages from the Program unless you opt in again.
You can also opt out by emailing shiva4senate@shiva4senate.com or calling 855-324-6624 .
Please tell us the mobile number you want removed.
We will honor any reasonable request to stop receiving our text messages, and we process email and phone requests promptly.
If you change your mind, you can rejoin at any time by opting in again.
