About Pamala
Ready to Serve
Born and raised in Wildwood, Florida, Pamala Kaye Bivins is a devoted mother and grandmother who has dedicated her life to serving her community.
Her deep roots in Central Florida give her a firsthand understanding of the challenges and opportunities facing District 52 families.
With a career built on public service, faith, and a commitment to her neighbors, Pamala brings integrity and real-world experience to every challenge.
She has spent 25 years fighting for the residents of Wildwood — and she's ready to take that fight to Tallahassee.
