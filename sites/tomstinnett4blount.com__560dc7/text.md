Tom Stinnett has dedicated his life to serving our community and has been a respected leader protecting our conservative values and beliefs.
After earning degrees from East Tennessee State University and the University of Tennessee, Tom was blessed to return home and help educate the next generation of children as a STEM teacher, drivers instructor, and successful coach of the football, track and golf teams in the Maryville City School System.
Tom has continued his service to the community serving as a Meals on Wheels driver, Emergency Medical Service Board, Blount County Community Action Agency, Friendsville Volunteer Fire Department, the Blount County Football Association and is a past Deacon at the Central Baptist Church in Alcoa.
For the last nine years, Tom has served as County Commissioner, where he championed efforts to improve infrastructure and create jobs, increase public safety, and enhance public education.
Tom is now running for the state house to keep serving our community and protect the conservative values we hold dear.
His word is his bond and Tom Stinnett will never embarrass Blount County.
