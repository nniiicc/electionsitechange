The Washington Council of Police & Sheriffs (WACOPS) has endorsed Drew Stokesbary for reelection in 2022!
Drew has consistently fought back against the “Defund the Police” movement, and as the Republican budget lead, he’s been a leader in the Legislature on increasing funding to police departments.
Law enforcement groups like WACOPS support Drew because they know Drew will always support them in Olympia.
From the WACOPS website:
The Washington Council of Police & Sheriffs is recognized as the largest, oldest, and most influential organization representing law enforcement professionals in Washington State.
We are committed to protecting the rights and quality of life of law enforcement officers through legislative action, legal defense, and the provision of quality long-term disability, life, and other insurance products.
WACOPS membership is made up of law enforcement guilds from across the state.
The more agencies involved, the stronger voice we have.
Drew is also endorsed by a number of other law enforcement organizations, including the Fraternal Order of Police and Washington State Patrol Troopers Association.
