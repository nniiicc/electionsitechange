Endorsements Drew Stokesbary receives NRA endorsement FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Drew Stokesbary, candidate for State Representative in the 31st Legislative District, was endorsed by the National Rifle Association (NRA) today.
An NRA representative wrote that Stokesbary’s “strong support for the Second Amendment and working knowledge of the issues surrounding this most important debate are greatly appreciated.” “I will always stand up to protect our Constitutional rights, including the right of the people to keep and bear arms,” said Stokesbary.
In addition to the NRA, Drew Stokesbary has the strong support of other pro-Second Amendment organizations.
He was previously endorsed by the Hunters’ Heritage Council and the Gun Owners’ Action League, the political arm of Washington Arms Collectors.
