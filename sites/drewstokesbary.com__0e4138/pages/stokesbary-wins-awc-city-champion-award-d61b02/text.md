I’m incredibly excited to receive the “City Champion Award” from the Association of Washington Cities.
AWC represents all 281 of Washington’s cities and towns before the state legislature, the executive branch and state regulatory agencies.
Even more exciting is that this is the second time I’ve received AWC’s City Champion Award.
I was previously given the City Champion Award during my first term in the Legislature for my work in developing, advocating for and successfully passing comprehensive police body cam legislation.
Here’s what AWC said about my role in the Legislature on behalf of cities:
