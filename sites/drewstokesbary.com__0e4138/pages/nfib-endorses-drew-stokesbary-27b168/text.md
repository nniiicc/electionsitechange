Endorsements National Federation of Independent Business endorses Drew Stokesbary FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Save America’s Free Enterprise Trust, the political action committee of the National Federation of Independent Business (NFIB) in Washington State, has endorsed Drew Stokesbary’s campaign for State Representative.
In a letter, NFIB wrote that Drew Stokesbary “demonstrates a strong understanding of the importance of small business to our state’s economy” and a “willingness to fight to protect our free enterprise system.” Small business represents nearly 96 percent of all businesses in Washington state and employs nearly half of the state’s private sector workforce.
NFIB, “the Voice of Small Business,” is the nation’s leading small business association with nearly 8,250 members in Washington state and 350,000 across the country.
Learn more at nfib.com/washington .
Drew Stokesbary was previously endorsed by the Auburn Area Chamber of Commerce, which has more than 475 business partners from Auburn, Algona, Pacific and unincorporated south King and north Pierce Counties.
