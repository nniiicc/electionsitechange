Endorsements Unanimous endorsement from State Patrol Troopers Association I have tremendous respect for the work done by law enforcement officers.
They help keep our communities safe, and often put their own lives at risk in doing so.
That’s why I’m so honored to receive the unanimous endorsement of the Washington State Patrol Troopers Association , the collective bargaining unit representing the Troopers and Sergeants of the Washington State Patrol.
Jeff Merrill, President of the Troopers Association , wrote: Your commitment to service to the state of Washington with honor and integrity are principles that our members demand.
Your understanding of public safety matters and your position on issues critical to every Trooper’s professional and personal life make you the clear choice.
The citizens of our state will be fortunate to have an individual of your caliber in such an important position.
Be assured that our members stand solidly in support of your re-election campaign.
I am tremendously appreciative to have the Trooper’s support in my re-election campaign, and I hope to have the opportunity to return to Olympia and continue advocating on behalf of law enforcement.
