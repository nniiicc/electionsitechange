Endorsements Washington State Farm Bureau endorses Stokesbary FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Today the Washington State Farm Bureau announced it has endorsed Drew Stokesbary’s campaign for the State House of Representatives.
The decision by the Farm Bureau Board of Trustees followed the recommendation of the King-Pierce County Farm Bureau, and was based on Stokesbary’s understanding and support of the agricultural community and Farm Bureau policies.
“Farmers form the backbone of our country.
In the Valley and on the Plateau, local farmers contribute significantly to the economic vitality of our region,” said Stokesbary.
“I am honored to have their support in my campaign, and look forward to promoting agricultural policies in the Legislature.” The Washington State Farm Bureau is a voluntary, grassroots advocacy organization representing the social and economic interests of farm and ranch families at the local, state and national levels.
Learn more at www.wsfb.com .
