Platform
- Defend core principles of KWA
- Tax credits/state grants for farmers who utilize water efficient technologies
- Expanded authority for communities to set their own water conservation metrics
- Make sure property tax re-designs do not shift tax burden onto agricultural land
- Legislation for property tax relief when crop yields drop
- Expand sales tax exemptions on farming equiment and servicing
-Opposed to foreign beef market fixing
- Continue fighting for Right to Repair
Agriculture
Infrastructure
-Update and expand power grid in order to negotiate lower energy costs for consumers
-Increase educational funding and opportunities for trade and vocational schools
-Update neglected roads and bridges
-Update or remove outdated building codes and permits to increase housing availability and lower housing costs
Tax Relief
- Propose a cap on property tax increases
- Create a fairer tax system by shifting tax burden from the working class to mega corporations
- Increase state and local revenues through sin taxes and potentially, marijuana legalization
-Reduce taxes on small businesses
Healthcare
-Take advantage of existing funds available for re-opening rural hospitals
-Increase facilities and funding for mental health and addiction disorders to reduce homelessness and property crime
- Put a cap on out of pocket costs for prescription medication
- Bring more insurance providers into Kansas to increase competition and lower costs
Anti-Surveillance
- Reduce or eliminate prevalence of surveillance technologies like Flock cameras
- Stricter legal processes for access to data collected by surveillance technologies
- Require transparency for any institution using such technology, with regular auditing and user logs made easily and readily available to the public
- Continue fighting for 4th amendment protections regarding all surveillance technology use
Economic Development
- Create built-in, state wide environmental and financial protections for communities sought out for data centers and other potentially predatory industries
- Create pathways to develop local talent and build infrastructure that encourages them to stay local
- Reduce or eliminate outdated and redundant permitting, licensing, and zoning policies that discourage small business growth and development
Marijuana Legislation
- Start with medical legalization, in particular as a less addictive alternative to opiates
- Potentially move forward to decriminalization for small personal amounts
- Consider the financial opportunities of full legalization for state wide tax relief
Accountability
- Term limits
- No stock trading for those in significant elected positions of power
-Ban price gouging and predatory loans
-Stricter enforcement of monopoly violations
-End Citizens United, reduce corporate spending in elections
- Harsher penalties for entities that cause health and/or environmental harm
-Stricter enforcement of labor laws and wage theft violations
