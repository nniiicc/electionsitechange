United We WIN .
Affordable Healthcare Affordable Healthcare should mean lower premiums, lower prescription drug costs, expanding Medicare coverage, and guaranteeing every American access to quality care as a basic right.
Families should never have to choose between their health and their paycheck.
Small Business Strong small businesses drive local jobs and economic growth, which is why we must cut red tape, expand access to capital, and protect Main Street entrepreneurs from unfair corporate competition.
Stable Energy Prices Stable energy prices come from investing in American clean energy, strengthening domestic supply chains, and protecting families from price spikes driven by corporate greed and global instability.
Veteran Services Honoring our veterans means fully funding VA healthcare, expanding mental health and housing services, and delivering earned benefits on time without bureaucratic delays.
Government Accountability Government accountability means no one is above the law, tax dollars are spent responsibly, and elected officials answer directly to the people, not special interests or corporations.
Affordable Housing Affordable Housing strengthens communities by lowering rent, increasing the housing supply, making homeownership achievable for working families; plus, ensuring seniors, and veterans have safe, stable places to live.
Education, Trade, and Technology Investing in strong public school education, skilled trades, and modern technology creates good-paying jobs, builds a competitive workforce, and prepares Americans for the economy of the future.
Social Security Social Security must be protected and strengthened, so seniors and people with disabilities can retire with dignity and financial security after a lifetime of hard work.
Voting Rights Protecting voting rights ensures every eligible citizen can vote freely, securely, and without barriers so our democracy truly represents the will of the people.
Fair Living Wage A fair living wage means full-time work should pay enough to cover food, housing, healthcare, and transportation without relying on government assistance.
