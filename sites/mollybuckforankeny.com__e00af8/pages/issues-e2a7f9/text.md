My Priorities
Clean water is fundamental to Iowa’s health, agriculture, and future.
We must implement sustainable water policies that protect our rivers, lakes, and drinking water while supporting farmers, businesses, and communities.
By working together on practical solutions, we can ensure safe, clean water for every Iowan and future generations.
Clean Water
Strong public schools are essential to Ankeny’s future.
Iowa must restore consistent funding, support teachers, and invest in innovative, forward-thinking policies that prepare every student for success.
By strengthening public education, we can return Iowa’s schools to their historic position as among the best in the nation while supporting families and growing our communities.
Education
Iowa faces one of the nation’s most serious cancer challenges, and we must respond with urgency.
By investing in prevention, expanding access to screenings, supporting cutting-edge research, and ensuring patients and families receive the care they need, we can reduce cancer’s impact and build a healthier future for every Iowan.
Cancer Advocacy
Working families in Ankeny deserve relief from rising costs and economic uncertainty.
We should pursue practical policies that lower everyday expenses, strengthen household budgets, and create opportunities for financial stability.
By focusing on affordability, we can help families thrive, support local businesses, and build a stronger, more resilient community.
