With Your Help,
I'm Ready to Win
I firmly believe that Iowa legislators should be reaching out to their constituents to solve Iowa issues.
Too much of our legislation is being written by outside entities and geared toward making Iowa run like Washington DC - partisan and tone-deaf to the needs of the people.
I want to represent the common Iowan, working to get legislation passed that is pro-Iowa.
I want to find win-win solutions for Iowans as often as we can.
I believe Iowans are Iowans first and we have more in common than divides us.
As your representative, I will work in the Iowa House to create win-win situations for all Iowans.
I’m optimistic that by listening to one another, we can fix the way the Statehouse works.
