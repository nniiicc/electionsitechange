Get to Know Molly Buck
Candidate for Iowa House District 41
Molly Buck is a mom, teacher, former legislator, and colon cancer survivor.
She never thought she'd get into politics, but was inspired to run because she knew our students, teachers and working families needed a voice in our state legislature.
Now, as Iowa's public schools are underfunded, Ankeny's working families are paying more and receiving less, cancer-causing nitrates are polluting our water, and the reproductive rights of women are being stripped away, Molly is determined to do something about it.
That's why she is running again in 2026 to represent the people of North Ankeny (District 41).
