Below is a list of bills we have formally endorsed, organized by policy area and status.
CREATE AI Act – Makes AI research tools and infrastructure more accessible to innovators and universities, ensuring the U.S. leads responsibly in artificial intelligence.
Track the bill's progress here .
NSF AI Education Act – Expands AI education and workforce training so students and workers can thrive in an AI-driven economy.
Track the bill's progress here .
Keep STEM Talent Act – Lets top global STEM graduates stay in the U.S. to build the next generation of companies here.
Track the bill's progress here .
Keep Innovators in America Act – Protects the Optional Practical Training program so U.S.-educated international talent can stay in the United States to work, drive innovation, strengthen our workforce, and keep America competitive.
Track the bill's progress here .
SKILL Act – Incentivizes employers to invest in workforce training and apprenticeships that equip workers with in-demand skills in an AI-driven economy.
Track the bill's progress here .
SPEED and Reliability Act – Speeds up approval for major energy transmission projects to modernize our grid and strengthen energy reliability.
Track the bill's progress here .
SUPPLY Act – Expands financing for homeowners to build ADUs, helping ease the housing shortage.
Track the bill's progress here .
Trade Review Act & Prevent Tariff Abuse Act – Restores Congress’s role in setting tariffs to protect innovation and economic stability.
Track the Trade Review Act's progress here and the Prevent Tariff Abuse Act's progress here .
