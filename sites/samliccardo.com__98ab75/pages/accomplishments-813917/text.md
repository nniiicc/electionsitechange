Skip navigation menu Meet Sam Taking Back Congress Our Work Together News Get Involved Donate Our Work In the least productive Congress in modern history, Sam has spent his first term proving that pragmatic, bipartisan progress is still possible.
He came to Washington to work bring down the cost of living, defend democratic institutions, and deliver tangible results for the Peninsula, Silicon Valley, and the Coastside — and he wasted no time.
He authored five bipartisan housing bills--all focused on accelerating the construction of affordable housing, and expanding housing supply-- and four have already passed both the House and Senate.
He brokered an Affordable Care Act tax credit extension proposal that secured a dozen GOP and Democratic cosponsors to help break a stalemate over reducing health care premiums for 22 million Americans.
He became an original cosponsor of long-awaited bipartisan legislation to provide a sensible framework for regulating crypto stablecoin that became law.
Sam's leadership extended well beyond individual bills.
In July 2025, he spearheaded the New Democrat Coalition's first-ever Innovation Agenda — a policy framework for keeping America competitive in the technologies, industries, and jobs of the 21st century.
He helped pass a bipartisan funding bill that brought more than $3.2 million home to California's 16th District, strengthening coastal resilience, growing the innovation workforce, and bolstering local public safety.
His office returned more than $3 million owed to district residents through constituent casework, and he launched two major regional initiatives — the Wildfire Resilience Partnership and the Partnership for the Coastside's Future — to coordinate the long-term work of protecting homes, jobs, and ecosystems along the Peninsula and the coast.
It's Sam's focus: results over rhetoric, partnership over partisanship, and a relentless focus on the issues that impact people's lives.
There's much more work ahead — and Sam has just gotten started.

Housing Affordability Accelerating Housing Construction Leading/co-leading five bipartisan housing bills to accelerate the construction of affordable and market-rate housing as part of a larger housing package expected to receive a House Floor vote.
Introduced additional bipartisan housing legislation focused on cutting red tape, modernizing reviews, and increasing housing supply.
Authored the RESIDE Act to help convert vacant office buildings into affordable housing opportunities.
Protecting Families from Homelessness Led dozens of Members of Congress in successfully opposing proposed changes to the federal Continuum of Care program that would have pushed thousands of extremely low-income families into homelessness.
Secured federal funding for local affordable housing and homelessness reduction projects throughout the district.
Lowering Costs for Working Families Reducing Health Care Costs Introduced the Fix It Act , the only bipartisan ACA premium tax credit extension designed to lower health insurance premiums while saving taxpayer dollars.
Fighting Cost Increases Introduced the bipartisan PACE Act to reduce bank and financial transaction fees for consumers and modernize payment systems.
Supported efforts to eliminate cost-inflating tariffs that raise prices for consumers and businesses.
Delivering Direct Results for Constituents In 2025, resolved more than 1,400 constituent service cases across the district.
In 2025, helped return more than $3.4 million to residents across the district.
Innovation & Economic Competitiveness Building a Pro-Innovation Agenda Founded Innovation for Good to expand a pragmatic, pro-innovation coalition focused on solving major national challenges.
Crafted the Innovation Agenda , later adopted by the largest caucus in Congress-- the 115-member New Democrat.
Coalition.
Convened bipartisan forums with leading voices in artificial intelligence, including Jensen Huang, Dario Amodei, and Sam Altman.
Strengthening America’s Workforce & Innovation Economy Introduced the bipartisan Keep Innovators in America Act to help retain highly skilled international talent educated in the United States.
Drafted legislation to encourage partnerships between employers, community colleges, and universities to prepare workers for AI-driven industries.
Advanced proposals to incentivize renewable energy and battery investments supporting data centers and advanced manufacturing.
Climate, Infrastructure & Resilience Protecting Communities from Climate Risks Established the Wildfire Resilience Partnership to accelerate wildfire prevention and infrastructure investment across the district.
Launched the Partnership for the Coastside’s Future to coordinate local climate resilience and infrastructure planning.
Advanced efforts to reduce wildfire-related property insurance costs through fire safety investments and regional coordination.
Securing Federal Investments Secured tens of millions of dollars in federal funding for affordable housing, infrastructure, homelessness reduction, and public safety projects across Silicon Valley and the Peninsula.
Defending Democracy & the Rule of Law Standing Up to Corruption & Abuse of Power Authored H.R.
1712, the MEME Act , to prevent elected officials from profiting from cryptocurrency schemes and other financial conflicts of interest.
Introduced H.R.
3454, the Protect Our Constitution and Communities Act (POCCA), to prevent unconstitutional withholding of congressionally appropriated funds.
Introduced H.R.
4998, the SUN Act , to address unlawful military deployments in American cities.
Introduced H.R.
6533, the Military in Law Enforcement Accountability Act , to strengthen protections against improper military involvement in domestic law enforcement.
Protecting Constitutional Rights Advocated for voting rights, reproductive freedom, and protection of Social Security, Medi-Cal, and CalFresh funding.
Opposed cuts to healthcare and food assistance programs impacting working families and seniors.
Bipartisan Leadership & Legislative Results Delivering Results in a Divided Congress Introduced 13 bills during his first year in Congress, attracting more than 250 bipartisan cosponsors.
Cosponsored 92 bills in his first year in Congress, focused on housing, affordability, innovation, public safety, and constitutional protections.
Built bipartisan coalitions to move legislation forward during one of the least productive congressional sessions in modern history.
