When I first ran for State Representative in 2024, my message was simple: Vermont needed a stronger focus on affordability and a better political balance in Montpelier.
Since then, Vermonters have taken an important step by ending the legislative supermajority.
That has created greater balance, encouraged more thoughtful debate, and strengthened accountability in state government.
But our work isn't finished.
Families continue to struggle with rising costs.
Property owners deserve confidence that their rights will be respected.
Every Vermonter should feel safe in their home and community.
As your State Representative, I remain committed to advancing practical, common-sense solutions that address these challenges while being a responsible steward of taxpayer dollars.
I believe government works best when it listens, spends wisely, and stays focused on the everyday concerns of the people it serves.
That is exactly what I intend to do as Castleton State Representative.
