About Dr.
Larry He
Dr.
LARRY HE is a neighbor, a common-sense Democrat, and a District Leader who fights for our community every day.
A first generation immigrant, Dr.
He shares our values and is deeply committed to fighting for safer, cleaner neighborhoods, high-quality schools, affordable living, and policies that help small businesses and families thrive.
As Chief of Staff to Assemblyman William Colton, he helps thousands of local families by bringing essential services directly into our neighborhoods, including food pantries, neighborhood cleanups, senior services, legal assistance, immigration support, and aid for small businesses and nonprofit organizations.
