Larry He for State Senate CLEANLINESS & SAFETY Clean streets reflect a community that cares about itself.
We will invest in our young people and our neighborhoods at the same time.
What We Will Do Create a year-round SD-17 Civic Stewardship Corps employing local youth to clean their own neighborhood.
Working with DYCD and local nonprofits and community organizations, we will establish a paid corps of SD-17 residents aged 16–24 focused on corridor cleaning, graffiti removal, park upkeep, and tree maintenance.
Research shows that every $1 invested in youth employment programs like SYEP returns $3 in benefits through reduced crime, higher graduation rates, and long-term employment.
Participating youth are 38% less likely to be convicted of a felony.
Getting young people engaged in their community, off their phones and invested in where they live, builds neighborhood pride and reduces crime long term.
