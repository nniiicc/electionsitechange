Doug's plan to
Lower Costs & Tackle the Affordability Crisis
Affordability isn’t just a slogan, it’s what families feel at the gas pump, in the grocery aisle, and when the bills come due.
Doug’s priority is simple: deliver immediate relief now and raise take-home pay long-term so Alabama families can breathe again.
The Challenge
- Whether at the gas pump, in the grocery aisle or when paying monthly bills Alabama families are being squeezed at every turn.
- Wages aren’t keeping up with rising costs, leaving too many working people falling behind.
- Utility decisions are made with virtually no real public input or utility accountability, impacting what families pay each month.
- Small fixes aren’t enough: families need immediate relief and long-term solutions that raise incomes and lower costs for good.
Doug’s Plan
- Deliver immediate cost relief
- Suspend the state gas tax when gas prices spike, delivering a fast, common-sense way to put money back in people’s pockets.
- Fully eliminate taxes on groceries so families keep more of every paycheck.
- Make sure paychecks keep up with prices
- Raise the minimum wage and pair it with broader strategies that help workers earn and keep more.
- Renew the “no tax on overtime” law so that extra work actually pays off.
- Put people back in control of utility decisions
- Keep the Public Service Commission accountable to voters, not insulated from them.
- Push for real public representation at PSC meetings so families aren’t shut out of decisions that affect monthly bills.
What This Delivers
- Lower day-to-day costs for families
- Bigger paychecks and better take-home pay
- Stronger accountability where utility costs are decided
Why It Matters
Families aren’t looking for excuses — they’re looking for leadership.
Doug’s plan starts with relief, then moves to reforms that raise incomes and lower costs for the long haul.
