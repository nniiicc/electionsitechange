DOUG'S PLAN TO
Build More Affordable Housing
Housing costs are rising, and too many families are being priced out.
Doug’s priority is to increase supply, support smart growth, and align wages with costs so working people can afford a safe place to live.
The Challenge
- Housing costs are rising, and too many Alabama families are being priced out of safe, stable homes.
- Incomes aren’t keeping up, so even modest apartments are out of reach for many working people.
- When housing becomes unaffordable, families have less to spend on essentials, and communities and businesses struggle.
- State policies haven’t kept pace with the need for affordable housing, leaving too many without options.
Doug’s Plan
- Increase the supply of affordable housing
- Treat housing as a statewide priority, not an afterthought, and use state policy to help increase affordable options.
- Partner with local communities to expand housing in ways that fit local needs.
- Support smart growth that keeps communities livable
- Encourage housing growth that supports jobs, schools, and infrastructure so communities can grow without pricing people out.
- Connect housing policy to wage growth
- Recognize the reality: if incomes don’t rise, housing will keep slipping out of reach.
Doug’s plan ties housing strategy to stronger wages and better jobs.
What This Delivers
- More attainable rent and homeownership options
- More stability for families
- Healthier local economies and stronger communities
Why It Matters
This isn’t just about buildings — it’s about stability, dignity, and opportunity for Alabama families.
