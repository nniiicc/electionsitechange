DOUG'S PLAN TO
Keep Alabama Dollars in Alabama
Alabamians already play the lottery — they just have to cross state lines to do it.
Doug’s priority is to let voters decide, keep that revenue here, and invest it back into Alabama communities.
The Challenge
- Alabama is the only southern state without a lottery, so Alabama families cross state lines every week to buy tickets in a neighboring state, and that money never returns.
- While politicians argue, Alabama loses hundreds of millions of dollars each year that could be invested in education, healthcare, and infrastructure.
- Other states invest those dollars in their communities, while Alabama watches opportunity walk out the door.
Doug’s Plan
- Put the decision in voters’ hands
- A state lottery shouldn’t be a left to special interests in Montgomery.
Alabamians should decide.
- Stop sending revenue out of state
- Families cross borders into Georgia, Tennessee, Florida, and Mississippi to buy lottery tickets every day, but none of it comes back.
- A lottery could generate hundreds of millions of dollars annually that stays in Alabama.
- Invest proceeds in Alabama priorities
- Use revenue to strengthen communities — including options like education, healthcare, infrastructure, and other voter-directed priorities.
What This Delivers
- New in-state revenue without raising taxes on working families
- A voter-driven decision
- Dollars invested back into Alabama communities
Why It Matters
This is one of the clearest examples of Alabama falling behind due to inaction.
Doug’s plan is simple: keep Alabama dollars in Alabama and let the people decide.
