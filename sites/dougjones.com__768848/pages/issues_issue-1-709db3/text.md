Doug’s priority is to open up government by increasing transparency, enforce accountability, and give people real control over decisions that shape their lives.
The Challenge: Nearly two-thirds of Alabamians across party lines say their government doesn’t care what they think.
Power is concentrated in Montgomery, making it easier for special interests to influence decisions and harder for regular people to have a say.
Lawmakers can trade stocks and futures in industries they oversee, raising real concerns about conflicts of interest.
Alabama is one of just a few states without early voting and with limited citizen-led ballot initiatives, making it harder for people to participate and hold leaders accountable.
Sadly, Alabama has embraced the partisan gerrymandering wars wich denies so many people a meaningful say in both the state and federal government.
Doug’s Plan End conflicts of interest clearly and permanently Ban statewide elected officials from trading individual stocks while in office.
Period.
And strengthen reporting requirements for members of the legislature.
Give people a stronger voice Allow citizen-led ballot initiatives and referendums.
If government won’t act, the people should be able to.
Expand access and accountability in elections Join the 47 other states with early voting so participation is easier and more secure.
End practices that reduce competition and accountability (including straight party, one-box voting).
Put more decisions closer to home Give local governments more authority to solve problems where they live — not only where power is concentrated.
Pass a constitutional amendment banning partisan gerrymandering and creating a commission to draw all political district, state or federal.
What This Delivers Less corruption and insider influence More transparency and public trust More responsive government that solves problems Why It Matters When power is concentrated, special interests win.
Doug’s plan shifts power back where it belongs: with the people.
