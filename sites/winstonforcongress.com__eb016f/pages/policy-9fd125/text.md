The promise was that prosperity would "trickle down." Instead, we got four decades of wage stagnation, union decline, automation without compensation, offshoring without accountability, and corporate subsidies while communities crumbled.
The result: a fragile, top-heavy economy where productivity gains enrich shareholders instead of the people who make civilization work.

Fiscal Surplus Through Inclusion Reaganomics: Debt Spiral Wage suppression reduces consumer demand Lower demand reduces GDP growth Tax cuts for wealthy don't stimulate economy Result: Rising debt, stagnant growth FDR 2.0: Inclusive Surplus Economic dignity increases participation Higher demand drives sustainable GDP growth Broad prosperity generates stable tax revenue Result: Fiscal stability, shared wealth Demand-side economics isn't a spending problem.
It is an investment in sustainable prosperity that pays for itself through inclusive growth.
If automation replaces labor, it must fund liberty.
Winston's full AI and data center policy follows one rule: technological progress must serve people, pay its own costs, and share its extraordinary gains with the public.
Data Centers Must Pay Their Full Cost Private developers must pay for the power, grid, water, roads, emergency services, and environmental mitigation their facilities require.
Residents should not subsidize private infrastructure through taxes or higher utility rates.
AI Gains Must Build Economic Security Extraordinary AI productivity should help fund Universal Income and a federal Employer of Last Resort paying displaced workers at least $50,000 annually.
People Must Be Protected Enforceable protections must cover privacy, children, creators, digital likeness, deepfakes, worker displacement, and high-risk AI systems.
