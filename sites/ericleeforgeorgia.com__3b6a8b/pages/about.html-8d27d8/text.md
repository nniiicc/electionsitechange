Since becoming Senior Pastor in 1999, he has guided Springfield through extraordinary growth, expanding membership from just over 100 to more than 7,000 and building a multi-generational institution that provides education, outreach, and critical services to families throughout the region.
Reverend Lee is a graduate of Morehouse College and Vanderbilt University Divinity School.
He has dedicated his life to serving others, working in workforce development, advocating for children and families, and partnering with local governments and organizations to strengthen communities across metro Atlanta.
Throughout his career, Reverend Lee has remained deeply engaged beyond the pulpit, serving on boards and participating in initiatives focused on education, economic opportunity, and community development.
His leadership has earned statewide recognition, including being named one of Atlanta’s most influential pastors and completing the Leadership Georgia program alongside his wife.
Whether supporting schools, mentoring youth, or helping families navigate challenges, Reverend Lee has built a reputation for bringing people together and delivering real, measurable impact (outcomes).
Reverend Lee is a dedicated husband of 33 years and the father of 5 children.
His wife, Meik Lee, is a respected Speech Pathologist in the Newton County School System for over 25 years and shares his dedication to public service.
Reverend Lee is running for State Senate because he believes our communities deserve leadership that is both principled and practical.
Reverend Lee offers leadership that understands the challenges families face every day.
He is committed to creating opportunity for all that addresses rising costs and economic development to education and quality of life, he is focused on solutions that strengthen families, support local communities, and move District 42 forward.
