Fighting for Hoosiers and Their Families
Husband, Father, & Hoosier Conservative Leader
A proud grandson of two lifelong General Motors employees, Kyle Pierce remains committed to advocating for the people he represents.
Issues Kyle Champions
- Hoosier families should be able to keep more of what they earn.
Kyle supports responsible budgets, lower taxes, and property-tax reforms that provide meaningful relief to homeowners.
During his time in office, Kyle has supported hundreds of millions of dollars in tax relief, including property-tax relief for Hoosier homeowners.
He will continue working to control government spending, eliminate waste, and ensure taxpayer dollars are used responsibly.
- Government should work for citizens—not force citizens to navigate outdated rules, unnecessary regulations, and confusing processes.
Kyle has supported reforms that streamline government, reduce burdensome regulations, and make administrative agencies more accountable.
He will continue working to eliminate obsolete government functions, improve transparency, and ensure Hoosiers receive fair treatment from state agencies.
- Item descriptIndiana should remain a place where employers can invest, grow, and create good jobs.
Kyle supports a competitive tax environment, reliable infrastructure, affordable energy, and fewer unnecessary regulatory barriers.
He also believes economic-development programs should produce measurable benefits for taxpayers and meaningful opportunities for Indiana workers.
- Not every successful career requires a four-year degree.
Kyle supports apprenticeships, trade schools, technical certifications, and work-based learning programs that help students gain valuable skills without incurring excessive debt.
Schools, training providers, and employers should work together to ensure Hoosiers are prepared for available jobs in advanced manufacturing, healthcare, construction, financial services, technology, and other growing industries.
- New financial technologies can create jobs, expand access to financial services, and strengthen Indiana’s economy.
Kyle supports clear and responsible rules that encourage innovation while protecting consumers, investors, and the integrity of Indiana’s financial system.
He has worked on policies addressing digital assets, financial regulation, investor protections, and emerging technology.
Kyle will continue working to make Indiana a leader in responsible financial innovation.
- Parents should have meaningful choices and a clear understanding of what their children are being taught.
Kyle supports school choice, curriculum transparency, and policies that place students and families, not government systems, at the center of education.
He also supports reducing unnecessary administrative burdens so teachers and school leaders can spend more time educating students.
- Hoosiers deserve access to affordable care and clear information about the cost and quality of medical services.
Kyle supports policies that increase transparency, strengthen competition, and give patients more control over their healthcare decisions.
He will continue working to hold powerful healthcare institutions accountable while improving access to mental health and behavioral health services.
- Kyle believes government must respect constitutional rights, personal responsibility, and Hoosiers' freedom to make decisions for themselves and their families.
He will continue to oppose unnecessary government mandates and defend individual liberties against government overreach.
- Hoosiers deserve elections that are secure, accurately administered, and worthy of public confidence.
Kyle supports clear election laws, consistent administration, and safeguards that protect election integrity while ensuring eligible voters can participate in the democratic process.
