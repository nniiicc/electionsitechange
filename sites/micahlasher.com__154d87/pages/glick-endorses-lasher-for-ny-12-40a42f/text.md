Dec.
15, 2025 Outgoing Assembly Member Deborah Glick is endorsing her colleague Micah Lasher to succeed Rep.
Jerrold Nadler in the 12th Congressional District, Lasher’s campaign announced on Monday.
“Micah Lasher is exactly the kind of leader the Democratic Party needs in Washington right now – someone with the energy and resourcefulness to battle Trump and the Republicans and a legislator who’s taken on tough fights and gotten results,” Glick said in a statement.
“I’ve known Micah for years and seen his commitment to issues that have been important to me throughout my career – from protecting the environment, to ensuring access to abortion, to securing the civil rights of LGBTQ New Yorkers, to combatting antisemitism.” Read the full article here.
