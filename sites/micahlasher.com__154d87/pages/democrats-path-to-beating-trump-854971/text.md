Harry’s special roundtable this week focuses on the fortunes of the Democratic Party.
Three top political thinkers—Micah Lasher, Amanda Litman, and Michael Steele—assess Democrats’ prospects in the midterms and 2028.
They break down what the party needs to do—beyond opposing Trump—and how it can harness the energy and message of recent progressive successes without falling victim to intra-party division.
Finally, they offer their ideas for the longer-term vision of the country that the Democrats should stand for.
