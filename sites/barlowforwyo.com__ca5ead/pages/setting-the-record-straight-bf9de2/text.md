Wyoming voters deserve the facts.
Below are the claims you may have heard or seen and what I actually voted for.
Claim: "Eric Barlow voted to spend Wyoming tax dollars on illegal immigrants." The Facts FALSE The vote referenced in the ad was not about spending tax dollars.
It was a procedural vote on introducing legislation that would have changed eligibility for certain public benefits.
The bill never became law.
I have never voted to create a Wyoming program that spends taxpayer dollars on illegal immigrants.
The Record HB 0135 (2016) – Restrictions on Public Benefits • I voted against introducing the bill. • The bill never became law.
Claim: "Eric Barlow voted to fund sanctuary cities." The Facts FALSE Wyoming has never had a sanctuary city.
I voted for the 2025 law banning sanctuary cities.
The ad relies on votes involving an earlier bill with a different enforcement mechanism, not support for sanctuary cities.
The Record HB 0105 (2018) – Earlier sanctuary-city proposal • I voted against versions of this bill.
2025 Sanctuary City Ban • I voted for the bill that became law.
Claim: "Eric Barlow voted against keeping boys out of girls' sports." The Facts FALSE I voted for Wyoming's law protecting girls' sports.
The Record SF 0133 (2023) – Keeps boys out of girls sports 7-12 grades. • I voted for the bill, and it became law.
Claim: "Eric Barlow voted to allow sex-change procedures on children." The Facts FALSE The vote referenced in the ad dealt with one proposed criminal penalty.
It was not a vote to allow these procedures.
I voted for legislation that made it illegal to perform sex-change procedures on children.
And it is law today.
The Record SF0111 (2023) - criminal-penalty proposal I voted against this bill because it did not define the proposed criminal intent of trying to change a child’s sex SF0144(2023) and SF099(2024) I voted for the legislation banning providers from performing these procedures on minors .
Claim: "Eric Barlow voted to allow sexual orientation and gender identity to be taught to kindergartners." FALSE This claim misrepresents what the 2023 bill was about.
Voting against that bill was not a vote to allow classroom instruction on sexual orientation or gender identity; the bill also only applied to grades K–3 and had legal and enforcement problems.
In 2024, I voted for a stronger parental rights law covering all K–12 students.
It became law.
The Record SF 0117 (2023) • I voted against the bill.
SF 0009 (2024) • I voted for the improved bill protecting parental rights for all K-12 students Claim: "Eric Barlow sponsored legislation to raise gas and diesel taxes." The Facts FALSE The bill cited in the ad was a lodging tax for tourism promotion.
It had nothing to do with gasoline or diesel.
I have never sponsored legislation raising Wyoming's fuel tax.
The Record HB 0134 (2020) – Lodging tax for tourism promotion • I voted for the bill.
2013 Fuel Tax Increase • I voted against the increase.
2020 Fuel Tax Proposal • I voted against introducing the bill.
Claim: "Eric Barlow voted to raise taxes on lodging and minerals." The Facts FALSE I did support a lodging tax that is paid primarily by visitors and helps promote Wyoming tourism.
But the claim about mineral taxes is backwards.
I helped reduce Wyoming's coal severance tax.
The Record HB 0134 (2020) – Lodging tax supporting Wyoming tourism • I voted for the bill.
HB 0075 (2025) – Coal severance tax reduction • I cosponsored the bill and voted for it.
Claim: "Eric Barlow passed taxpayer-funded healthcare for legislators." The Facts FALSE The ad cites the wrong bill.
The actual legislator healthcare proposal failed in the Senate and never became law.
I did not sponsor it.
The Record SF 0081 (2023) • The bill cited in the ad.
It had nothing to do with healthcare.
SF 0062 (2023) – Legislator healthcare proposal • I voted for the bill. • It failed in the Senate and never became law. • I was not the sponsor.
