I’m committed to fostering and uplifting community.
Here’s how I’ve done that so far:
- The Change Collective I joined the first-ever Change Collective cohort in 2023.
The Obama-created collective taught me how to take civic action on a local and national scale.
I committed my time to working across the political aisle in an effort to build tolerance through strong and genuine leadership.
- Nonprofit Work I’ve spent years dedicating my time, on and off the clock, to local nonprofits.
For nearly 5 years I sat on the Board of Directors at Gigi’s Playhouse, and dedicated the same amount of time as a Senior Communications and Advocacy Manager at DuPage and South Suburbs Habitat for Humanity.
- We The Community In 2025, I launched the first season of my community-based podcast, aptly named, with the inspiration of building and growing a strong and cohesive community.
I’ve interviewed changemakers both locally and nationally to encourage conversation across the aisle in a time increasingly calling for global, national, and local change.
