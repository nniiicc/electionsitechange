VOTING INFORMATION
Primary Election
Early voting for the Primary Election will begin on Thursday, May 2, 2024 through Thursday, May 9, 2024 from 7 am until 8 pm.
The Primary Election Day is May 14, 2024.
General Election
Early Voting for the General Election begins on Thursday, October 24, 2024 through Thursday, October 31, 2024 from 7 am until 8 pm.
The General Election Day is November 5, 2024.
To vote in person on election days, vote at your assigned polling place.
You can find your assigned polling place using this Voting Location Lookup tool.
