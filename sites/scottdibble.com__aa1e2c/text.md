Scott's Vision Standing Up for Democracy and the Rule of Law Scott believes leadership means meeting difficult moments with courage.
As federal actions brought fear and disruption to Minnesota communities—including the tragic killing of Renee Nicole Macklin Good and Alex Jeffrey Pretti—Scott has been clear and unwavering: Minnesota must uphold constitutional rights, demand accountability, and protect the dignity and safety of every resident.
He recognizes what is happening, an authoritarian president is using Minnesota as a test ground to deploy state sanctioned violence, violate constitutional rights and freedoms, overturn the rule of law and undermine our democracy.
He continues working with colleagues to document civil-rights abuses, strengthen legal protections, and advance laws that defend the rule of law — while remaining focused on the kitchen-table issues families face every day: affordability, opportunity, and economic security.
Minnesota must meet this moral test with compassion, courage, and an unyielding commitment to justice.
