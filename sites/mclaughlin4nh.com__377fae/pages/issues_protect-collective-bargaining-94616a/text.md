Collective Bargaining
Both my wife and I were lifelong union members.
Without a doubt, working in a union improves an employee’s quality of life with both improved pay and benefits.
This does not just benefit the union worker.
It benefits the NH economy.
A well paid worker is able to do the things that make an economy thrive.
They buy cars, they take vacations, go to sporting events, concerts, restaurants, and plays.
They contribute to charities.
In essence, the fact that they make a decent living goes a long way to allowing so many other businesses to thrive.
I will work to ensure Right to Work laws do not pass in the New Hampshire Legislature.
I will work hard in Concord to ensure that all employees have the ability to form a union and collectively bargain with their employer for the conditions of their employment.
