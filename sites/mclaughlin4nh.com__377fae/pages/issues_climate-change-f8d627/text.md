Clean Energy and Climate Change
Energy costs are a growing burden for New Hampshire families.
From heating oil in the winter to rising electric bills year-round, too many Granite Staters are paying more just to keep the lights on and their homes warm.
Addressing climate change isn’t just about protecting our environment, it's also about lowering energy costs and building a more reliable energy system.
By investing in energy efficiency, expanding renewable energy like solar, and modernizing our grid, New Hampshire can reduce costs for families while strengthening our economy.
