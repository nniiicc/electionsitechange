Amanda Mogoi is a Kansas healthcare provider, small business owner, and mother running for Kansas House District 100 because she believes Kansans deserve leadership rooted in compassion, practical solutions, and real community connection.
Born from a deep concern for the future of Kansas families, Amanda’s campaign is grounded in the values she lives every day: caring for people, listening without judgment, and meeting challenges head-on with empathy and common sense.
As a healthcare provider, she has seen firsthand how political division and policy failures impact real people — from patients struggling to access care to families overwhelmed by rising costs and uncertainty.
Those experiences pushed her to step forward and run for office.
Amanda is especially passionate about protecting bodily autonomy, expanding access to affordable healthcare, supporting public schools, and ensuring Kansas communities — including rural communities — have the resources they need to thrive.
She believes elected officials should spend less time fueling culture wars and more time solving problems that affect everyday Kansans.
A proud mom and advocate for vulnerable communities, Amanda often speaks openly about the worries many families carry today: concern for children, for trans youth, for immigrant families, for aging grandparents relying on Medicare and Social Security, and for loved ones living with disabilities.
Those concerns are personal to her, and they shape the kind of leadership she wants to bring to Topeka.
Amanda’s approach is deeply influenced by what she calls “Midwest Kindness” — the idea that Kansans can disagree respectfully, care for one another through difficult times, and still work together toward meaningful progress.
She believes people are exhausted by negativity and political theater, and that there is still room for hope, decency, and collaboration in public service.
Outside of work and campaigning, Amanda enjoys spending time with her family, caring for her ever-growing collection of mostly-surviving houseplants, and staying connected to the community she calls home.
Through her campaign, Amanda hopes to help build a Kansas where everyone feels safer, healthier, heard, and more hopeful about the future.
