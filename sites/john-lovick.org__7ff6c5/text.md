Meet John
For over 40 years, John has served our community.
It is not hyperbole to say that public safety runs deep in his blood.
After his time in the Coast Guard, the State Patrol, and his service as Snohomish County Sheriff, John took his expertise to the State Legislature to see results.
Working with our community and the other elected leaders of our state, John is legislating to make Washington a place anyone would be excited to call home.
From affordability to public safety, there is still work to be done, and John asks for your vote to continue representing the diverse needs of our district.
