Caroline Eason is an upper-Cleveland county native.
After graduating from Burns High School, she earned her Doctor of Pharmacy degree from UNC Chapel Hill.
She was raised in a family of small business owners, which ultimately led to her public service aspirations.
She is a strong Christian, and a lifelong conservative.
Now, she is running to represent District 110 in the North Carolina General Assembly.
EXPERIENCE Dr.
Eason became active in politics a few years ago when her family’s pharmacies faced potential closure due to consolidation in the healthcare industry, making legislation the only remedy.
She has also participated in the legislative process to support previous state-level legislation, including testifying before the NC House Health Committee in support of a bill.
Dr.
Eason served in a health policy role in the last year of her doctorate studies at UNC Chapel Hill.
Additionally, she served a term as an appointed member of a legislative steering committee that advises on healthcare policy in Washington, DC.
POLICY & BELIEFS Dr.
Eason's advocacy extends far beyond her healthcare roots.
She recognizes that the challenges facing pharmacies are also affecting every industry, from farmers, to community banks, to painters, to printers and beyond.
She believes that Main Street- not Wall Street- is the backbone of Cleveland and Gaston county’s economies, and she is committed to championing local job creation, strategic and managed growth, and fiscal conservatism.
