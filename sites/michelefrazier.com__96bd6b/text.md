Meet Michele
Michele grew up in her family’s flower and greenhouse business in Oneonta - one of the largest geranium growers in the state.
In this family business, surrounded by acres of flowers, Michele learned from an early age that everyone deserves to grow in their gardens and flourish.
Today, Michele is ready to fight for rural New Yorkers, so they can plant their roots, grow their families and tend to their communities.
