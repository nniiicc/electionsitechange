The Legislative Page Program in the Idaho State Legislature is for high school seniors between the ages of 17 through 19 and occurs during the legislative season, beginning in January.
Applications are accepted in the summer and fall and applicants are notified in early December by mail.
Access the application here, or read about Elyse Woodland’s experience in the Page Program, where she discusses what pages do, how to participate in the program, and the balance of school work and government.
