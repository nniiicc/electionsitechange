Meet Karen
Karen Hurd is currently the WI State Representative of the 69th Assembly District.
She holds a Master of Biochemistry degree as well as a Master of Public Health degree.
She is a nutritionist.
She is actively involved in the community life in multiple ways, is a veteran of the United States Army, writer, and strong advocate for the constituents in North Central Wisconsin.
She is married to her husband Steven for 48 years.
She has five grown children and seven grandchildren.
She has been a resident of rural Wisconsin for 30 years.
