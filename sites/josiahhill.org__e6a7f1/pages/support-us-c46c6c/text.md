Minnesota Political Contribution
Refund Program (PCR)
The Minnesota Contribution Refund (PCR) program lets those eligible to vote contribute to a political party and receive a refund of their contribution.
The maximum refund contributors may receive is $75 per person or $150 for married couples.
Individuals may file one PCR application per year.
There are no opportunities to amend a PCR application.
Only financial contributions may qualify, not donations of goods or services.
How to Get Your Refund
- Contribute to the Josiah Hill for MN House campaign.
- Once your donation has been received and processed, you will be mailed a physical receipt and PCR form to complete and mail to the Minnesota Department of Revenue.
- You will receive a full refund from the State of Minnesota in about six weeks.
