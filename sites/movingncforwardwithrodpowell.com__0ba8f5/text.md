Rod Powell
“I am not a Raleigh insider or a slick politician.
I’m a retired school teacher who has spent my life serving North Carolina communities.
I’m running for NC Senate District 44 to fight for working families, public schools, rural communities, and the people who are being ignored in Raleigh.”
Priority Issues
There are many issues facing our communities, but these are the issues I hear most about across Cleveland, Lincoln, and Gaston counties.
Building strong public schools
I will fight for stronger public schools, better teacher support, and more resources for students and classrooms across our communities.
Protecting rural healthcare
I will support efforts to expand rural healthcare access, attract medical providers, and improve access to clinics, telehealth, and mental health services.
Ensuring our roads and infrastructure can keep up
I will push for better planning and investment in roads, traffic relief, water systems, broadband, and infrastructure that keeps pace with growth.
Lowering everyday costs
I will fight for all our community members, especially working families, who are dealing with rising utility bills, insurance costs, housing costs, and the everyday pressures facing ordinary people.
