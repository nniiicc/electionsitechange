FAQs Everything you need to know about getting involved in our grassroots campaign How much time will volunteering take?
We offer flexible opportunities ranging from 2 hours to ongoing commitments.
Every contribution matters, no matter how small.
What volunteer roles are available?
We're looking for folks to help out with door-to-door canvassing, making phone calls, planning events, boosting our social media presence, and reaching out to the community.
Do I need previous experience?
You don't need any previous political experience to join us.
We offer all the training and support you'll need to feel comfortable and confident in your volunteer role.
Can students get involved?
Absolutely.
We welcome students who want to make a difference in their community and learn about civic engagement.
Are there remote volunteering options?
Yes.
We offer digital volunteering opportunities like social media support, phone banking, and virtual event assistance.
What is the age requirement?
Volunteers must be 16 or older.
Younger supporters can help spread our message with parental consent.
How do I sign up?
Fill out our online volunteer form or attend one of our monthly orientation meetings.
Will I receive training?
We provide comprehensive training for all volunteer roles to ensure you feel confident and prepared.
Is transportation provided?
We help coordinate carpools and provide resources for volunteers who need transportation to campaign events.
How are expenses handled?
Most volunteer activities are unpaid.
Some roles may receive reimbursement for approved campaign-related expenses.
