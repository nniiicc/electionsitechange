Lisa “Malkie” Vider is a businesswoman, community member, and problem solver running for New York State Senate to deliver real results for our families, seniors, and neighborhoods.
Lisa “Malkie” Vider is a healthcare administrator, business leader, and community advocate with more than 25 years of real-world management experience.
Born and raised in the Borough Park and Flatbush sections of Brooklyn, Lisa is the daughter of a New York City public school special education teacher and a healthcare professional.
She attended Shulamith High School and later Brooklyn College, beginning her career as a teenager working in her family’s assisted living facility.
What started as a part-time job became a lifelong calling.
After her father’s passing, Lisa continued his legacy — expanding her healthcare credentials, deepening her operational expertise, and ultimately serving as administrator of the same facility.
In that role, she manages complex regulatory requirements, oversees staff and budgets, and ensures high-quality care for seniors and families.
Lisa understands how government decisions impact real people because she navigates those policies every day.
After marrying her husband Avner in 2013, Lisa chose Long Island to raise their family.
They reside in Hewlett Bay Park with their three children, Tamar, Dvir, and Adir.
Lisa is deeply involved in her community.
She serves on the Board of the Brandeis School, supports Teach NYS in advocating for fair education funding, and volunteers with NYSHFA/NYSCAL, the leading voice for New York’s skilled nursing and assisted living providers.
She and her family are active members of their local synagogue and community institutions.
Lisa’s leadership is rooted in service, accountability, and results — values she brings to everything she does.

Few people in Albany understand our state’s health care crisis like Lisa Vider.
Our state currently spends over 44% of its annual budget on health care, which unnecessarily drives up our taxes.
As a leading New York state health care professional for over twenty-five years, Lisa knows better than anyone that we can provide our families with better care and accessibility without raising taxes, if we understand how the system works.
Assisted Living Facilities, such as the one Lisa proudly operates is a perfect example.
Such facilities were created by Albany years ago to provide a necessary level of care to those who needed our compassionate help, but did not require a higher level of more expensive care.
Lisa found ways to ensure that New Yorkers received the care that they deserved, at a level that they needed, at a major cost savings to the state budget.
