These early experiences laid the foundation for his passion to be a man of The People, valuing diligence, integrity and compassion.
David then began a 35-year business career in engineering, manufacturing and construction.
He credits his education and these broad experiences with making him a problem solver, one who can better define problems and develop better solutions.
Alongside his many achievements, life has thrown significant curveballs his way, including the devastating heartache of a son with mental illness.
