Dan Koh
Democratic Nominee, Massachusetts' 6th Congressional District
Dan Koh is the Democratic nominee for Congress in Massachusetts' 6th District.
An Andover resident and the son of Korean and Lebanese immigrants, Dan served as a senior official in the Biden White House and at the U.S.
Department of Labor, and as chief of staff to Boston Mayor Marty Walsh.
He's running to lower costs for working families, make housing and child care more affordable, and defend our democracy.
