They deserve a government that supports them in return.
Right now, too many seniors are being squeezed by rising costs, limited housing options, and property taxes that continue to climb while incomes remain fixed.
Addressing the cost of living for seniors starts with giving more support to local government.
When the state increases local aid, it helps reduce the pressure on cities and towns that would otherwise be forced to raise property taxes.
For seniors living on fixed or limited incomes, every increase can make it harder to remain in the homes and communities they have spent a lifetime building.
I will fight to increase local aid so municipalities have the resources they need without placing more of the burden on residents.
Senior housing is also a growing challenge that requires real attention.
Massachusetts needs more affordable housing options designed specifically for older residents so they can age with dignity, stay close to family, and remain active members of the communities they love.
Our seniors have given so much to this state and to our communities.
