TAX RELIEF FOR BILLERICA FAMILIES
We need to prioritize targeted tax relief for hard working Billerica families, not multimillionaires living in Chestnut Hill mansions.
I support tax policies that actually help Billerica families afford to live in Massachusetts, while maintaining necessary revenue to fund vital services.
This includes:
- Raising the Child and Dependent Tax Credit to $750
- Increasing the Earned Income Tax Credit to 50% of the federal credit
- Raising the rent deduction to $5,000
- Expanding the Circuit Breaker Credit so our seniors can stay in their homes.
