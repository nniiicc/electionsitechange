THE ISSUES THAT MATTER MOST
Billerica families deserve a representative who fights for them on the issues that actually affect their daily lives.
Below is where Daniel stands.
EDUCATION AND WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT
Education is the foundation of opportunity.
I’ll fight to strengthen our public schools and expand career and technical education so every student is prepared to succeed.
GOVERNMENT REFORM & TRANSPARENCY
Government works best when it is open, accountable, and worthy of the public’s trust.
I’ll fight for stronger transparency laws, tougher ethics rules, and reforms that make Beacon Hill more accountable to the people it serves.
FIGHT FOR EVERY DOLLAR OUR TOWN DESERVES
While neighboring communities have secured hundreds of thousands, even millions of dollars in grants and earmarks for their residents, Billerica has been left with far less than what we deserve.
That must change.
TAX RELIEF FOR BILLERICA FAMILIES
We need to prioritize targeted tax relief for hard working Billerica families, not multimillionaires living in Chestnut Hill mansions.
LOWERING ENERGY COSTS
We must fight to lower energy costs and hold the energy monopolies accountable.
CUTTING THE RED TAPE
Whether you are a hardworking family trying to buy your first home or a small business owner trying to grow and create jobs, government bureaucracy keeps getting in the way.
PROTECT YOUR FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS
Everyone deserves the freedom to live with dignity, privacy, and equal protection under the law.
I’ll stand up for civil rights, reproductive freedom, and protections against discrimination so every resident is treated with fairness and respect.
SUPPORTING OUR VETERANS
Our veterans deserve more than gratitude.
I’ll fight to ensure they have access to housing, mental health care, earned benefits, and economic opportunities that honor their service.
INFRASTRUCTURE & TRANSPORTATION
Strong communities depend on strong infrastructure.
I’ll fight for the investments Billerica needs to improve our roads, modernize water and sewer systems, and make commuter rail a more reliable, practical option for residents.
AGING WITH DIGNITY
Seniors who spent a lifetime building our communities deserve the ability to age with dignity and remain in the places they call home.
I’ll fight to ease the cost of living, expand senior housing, and protect older residents from being priced out of their communities.
