DANIEL DARRIS-O'CONNOR WILL Fight to
PROTECT YOUR FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS
Massachusetts has a long tradition of protecting individual liberty, personal freedom, and equal treatment under the law.
Every resident deserves the freedom to live with dignity, privacy, and security, without fear of discrimination, harassment, or government overreach.
I believe government has a responsibility to protect people’s rights, not restrict them.
No one should face discrimination because of who they are, who they love, how they worship, where they come from, or the personal decisions they make about their own lives and healthcare.
Respect, fairness, and basic decency are not partisan issues.
They are core values that strengthen our communities.
I also believe deeply personal medical decisions should remain between individuals and their doctors, not politicians.
That includes protecting reproductive freedom and ensuring Massachusetts remains a place where those rights are secure.
As your State Representative, I will always stand up for equal protection under the law, defend fundamental rights, and work to ensure every resident is treated with dignity, fairness, and respect.
