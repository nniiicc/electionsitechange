dANIEL dARRIS-o'cONNOR - ON THE ISSUES
CUTTING THE RED TAPE
Massachusetts has a red tape problem.
Whether you are a hardworking family trying to buy your first home or a small business owner trying to grow and create jobs, government bureaucracy keeps getting in the way.
Permitting delays, outdated regulations, and layers of unnecessary hurdles drive up costs and kill opportunity.
We need more homes on the market so prices become manageable and homeownership is achievable again.
Massachusetts should be the easiest place in New England to start, grow, and run a business.
Right now it is one of the hardest.
I will fight to cut every unnecessary regulation and bureaucratic barrier standing in the way of job creators and families alike.
When government gets out of the way, people thrive.
