That is why I am proud to endorse Naomi Riess for Colorado House District 59.
Naomi has lived and worked in Southwest Colorado for more than three decades.
Her professional experience in land-use planning, combined with her extensive service to local schools, agriculture, youth programs, businesses, and community organizations, has given her a deep understanding of the district and the people she hopes to represent.
Naomi will bring practical experience, careful preparation, and a strong commitment to local control to the Colorado House.
This race is critical to maintaining balance in the Colorado Legislature and ensuring that rural Coloradans continue to have a strong voice at the State Capitol.
I encourage the voters of House District 59 to support Naomi Riess and help elect a representative who is ready to work hard, defend their interests, and lead with integrity.
