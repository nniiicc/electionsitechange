The NFIB Colorado PAC has endorsed Naomi Riess for Colorado House District 59, recognizing her as a pro-small-business candidate who will advocate for Main Street employers and local economic opportunity.
NFIB represents small-business owners across Colorado and notes that small businesses employ nearly half of the state’s workforce.
Its endorsements are based on candidates’ positions and records on key small-business issues.
Naomi was one of only four Colorado House candidates included in NFIB’s second round of 2026 legislative endorsements.
