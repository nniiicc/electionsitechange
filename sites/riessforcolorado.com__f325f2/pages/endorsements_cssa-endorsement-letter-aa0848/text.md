The Colorado State Shooting Association is proud to endorse Naomi Riess for Colorado House District 59.
Naomi understands that responsible firearm ownership, hunter safety, wildlife conservation and public safety are important parts of Colorado’s heritage.
She is committed to defending the constitutional right to keep and bear arms while representing the values and traditions of Southwest Colorado.
CSSA encourages the voters of House District 59 to support Naomi Riess, a strong advocate for individual liberty, responsible gun owners and the Second Amendment.
