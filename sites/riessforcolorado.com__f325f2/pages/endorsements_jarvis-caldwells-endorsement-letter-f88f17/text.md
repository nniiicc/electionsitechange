Naomi brings decades of business, community and leadership experience to this race.
She understands what it takes to manage budgets, solve problems, work with people and make responsible decisions.
She also understands the challenges facing families, employers, farmers, ranchers and rural communities across Southwest Colorado.
At the State Capitol, we need more representatives with real-world experience who understand the consequences government decisions have on working families and local communities.
Naomi will bring a practical, thoughtful and independent voice to the Colorado House.
She will fight for affordability, economic opportunity, responsible government spending, public safety, parental rights, private property rights, local control, water and agriculture.
Most importantly, she will ensure the people of La Plata, Montezuma, Archuleta and San Juan counties are heard.
House District 59 is one of the most important and competitive legislative races in Colorado.
It is a highly winnable seat, and electing Naomi would help restore balance at the State Capitol while strengthening the voice of rural Colorado.
We need Naomi’s experience, judgment and leadership in the Colorado House.
She is prepared to serve, ready to lead and committed to representing Southwest Colorado with strength and integrity.
I strongly encourage voters throughout House District 59 to support Naomi Riess.
