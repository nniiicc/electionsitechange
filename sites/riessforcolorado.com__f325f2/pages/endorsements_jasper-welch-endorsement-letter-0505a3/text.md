Her preparation, curiosity, and respect for differing perspectives consistently stand out.
Through her work in land-use consulting, Naomi has helped individuals and businesses navigate permitting and regulatory processes with clarity and care.
She understands state and local regulations in real-world terms and brings a practical, thoughtful approach to complex issues.
She asks the right questions and understands the broader economic and community impacts of policy decisions.
Naomi Riess understands the needs of the people of House District 59, and I am confident she will serve our community with sound judgment and common sense.
