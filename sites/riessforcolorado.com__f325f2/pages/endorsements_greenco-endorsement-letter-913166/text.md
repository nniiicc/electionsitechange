On behalf of the Green Industries of Colorado (GreenCO) and its member associations, we are pleased to share our endorsement of Naomi Riess’s candidacy for Colorado House District 59.
GreenCO believes that Naomi’s approach to public policy and her commitment to District 59 align with the priorities and objectives of our industry.
Colorado’s green industry represents thousands of businesses and workers across the state, including the landscape, horticulture, nursery, greenhouse, arboriculture, irrigation, and related sectors.
GreenCO is committed to being a constructive partner in developing policies that promote responsible water use, economic opportunity, workforce development, environmental stewardship, and a strong business climate.
GreenCO looks forward to working with Naomi during the campaign and, if elected, throughout her time in the legislature.
We hope to build a strong and productive relationship based on open communication, sound policy, and a shared commitment to finding practical solutions for Colorado.
GreenCO is proud to endorse Naomi Riess’s candidacy and wishes her great success in the upcoming election.
We look forward to working together to advance policies that support Colorado’s communities, businesses, and the continued success of our green industries.
Glenda Mostek
GreenCO Selection Committee
