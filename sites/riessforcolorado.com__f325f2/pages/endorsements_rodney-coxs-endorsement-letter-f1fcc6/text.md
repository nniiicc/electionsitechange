I am pleased to endorse Naomi Riess for Colorado House District 59.
As a fifth-generation Montezuma County resident and longtime small-business owner, I care deeply about making sure the people who represent us understand rural Colorado.
Naomi does.
She has spent decades working with landowners, local governments, families, and businesses across Southwest Colorado.
What I appreciate most about Naomi is her practical approach.
She does her homework, listens carefully, and looks for solutions that make sense for the people who actually have to live with the consequences.
I believe Naomi Riess will be that representative, and I am proud to support her.
Rodney Cox
