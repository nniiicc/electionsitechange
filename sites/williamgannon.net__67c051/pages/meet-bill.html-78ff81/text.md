SENATOR BILL GANNON
Bill fell in love with New Hampshire, boating, fishing, swimming and target shooting during his youth.
He spent childhood summers on our local ponds and has made New Hampshire his forever home.
Bill attended Saint Anselm College in Manchester, where he graduated with a B.A. in Political Science, before earning his J.D. from Massachusetts School of Law.
He currently runs a small construction business and is a practicing attorney.
Bill and his wife, Janice, have four children educated in our New Hampshire public schools.
He is a Sandown Lions Club member and served as volunteer youth soccer and basketball coach for many years.
From 2014-2016, Bill served as a member of the New Hampshire House of Representatives, acting as a frequent floor speaker for the Election Law Committee.
Bill was first elected to the New Hampshire Senate in 2016.
He has previously served as Vice Chairman of the Senate Transportation Committee and Chairman of the Senate Commerce Committee.
Bill is currently serving his fourth term as Senator for New Hampshire's 23rd District and serves as Chairman of the Senate Judiciary Committee.
As Senator, Bill works tirelessly to ensure that the voices of his constituents are represented in Concord.
Bill has a reputation of being a strong Republican leader and a proven problem solver, delivering real results for New Hampshire families.
