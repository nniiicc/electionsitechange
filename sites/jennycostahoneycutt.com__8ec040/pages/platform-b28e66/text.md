Standing up for the Lowcountry with principled, America First solutions that strengthen our communities, secure our borders, and protect our way of life.
PUT THE LOWCOUNTRY FIRST
Jenny’s first responsibility is to the people of South Carolina’s First Congressional District.
Every decision she makes, every issue she champions, and every vote she takes will be grounded in what’s best for the Lowcountry – its families, its economy, and its way of life.
Washington needs leaders who remember who they work for and Jenny will.
SUPPORT THE RULE OF LAW AND A SECURE BORDER
Jenny believes safe communities are the foundation of strong communities.
She will always stand with law enforcement and support policies that keep violent criminals off our streets.
This includes ensuring our border is secure – stopping the flow of deadly drugs like fentanyl and criminal illegal aliens from harming our families, our neighborhoods, and our economy.
Whether on our streets or at our border, Jenny will always put the safety of the Lowcountry first.
RESTORE FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY
Washington’s reckless spending threatens the nation’s economic stability and harms families.
As our Member of Congress, Jenny will combat wasteful spending, end waste, fraud, and abuse of government programs, restore fiscal discipline, and work to balance the budget.
Jenny knows taxpayer dollars are just that – taxpayers’ hard earned money – she’ll stand up for working families by guarding every dollar they send to Washington.
DEFEND THE CONSTITUTION
Jenny believes strongly in the Constitution and will defend it without compromise.
From protecting free speech to safeguarding religious liberty to stopping government overreach to ensuring an impartial justice system and the separation of powers, Jenny will stand firm every day in Congress.
The Bill of Rights is not negotiable – and Jenny will ensure it remains fully protected.
She is a proven free speech advocate and defender of the Second Amendment.
PROTECT OUR LOWCOUNTRY VALUES
Jenny believes every life is precious and deserving of protection, and she will be a steadfast advocate for the unborn.
As a girls’ sports coach and former collegiate athlete, Jenny is committed to keeping men out of women’s sports.
As an avid outdoors person, Jenny understands the importance of conserving our natural resources while defending our freedoms.
Jenny will always stand firm for the conservative values that define the Lowcountry.
PROTECT FAMILY FINANCES
Jenny knows families are struggling with skyrocketing costs – from groceries to housing to healthcare.
She will champion policies to lower costs and advocate for responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, so families can plan, save, and thrive.
You work hard for your money; your government should work just as hard to protect it.
Jenny will protect your family’s paycheck by promoting policies that bring real affordability back to the Lowcountry.
MAKE AMERICA HEALTHY AGAIN
As a mom, former college athlete, and girls’ youth sports coach, Jenny has long made health and wellness a priority for her family.
Long before it became a national movement, she believed America should focus more on preventing illness, not simply treating it.
In Congress, Jenny will fight chronic disease, put children’s health first, improve the quality and transparency of our food, bring greater transparency to healthcare costs, and hold federal health agencies and special interests accountable.
Making America healthy again starts with common sense: healthier children, healthier families, and a greater focus on prevention.
