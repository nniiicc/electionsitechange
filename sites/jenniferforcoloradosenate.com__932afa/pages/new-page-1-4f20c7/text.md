The so-called Democratic Socialists of America (DSA) and their allies in the Democratic Socialist wing promise "equity" and "justice," but deliver class warfare, bigger government, and policies that erode the very foundations of liberty that built this nation.
The DSA's vision is heavy taxation on the productive, government control of key industries, "public ownership" of corporations and housing, a 32-hour workweek with full pay, open borders with voting rights for non-citizens, defunding police, wealth redistribution, and even rewriting our Constitution, a direct assault on the real working class.
It replaces individual ambition and earned success with dependency on an all-powerful state.
This isn't empowerment; it's oppression dressed in socialist rhetoric.
True freedom comes from limited government, free enterprise, and personal responsibility, not from bureaucrats deciding winners and losers or confiscating wealth through endless taxes without genuine representation.
WE WARN OF THE CLEAR DANGERS To freedom: Expanded government control over healthcare (Medicare for All with no limits), energy (Green New Deal mandates), education, and housing leads to rationing, shortages, and lost choices.
History shows socialism stifles innovation and traps people in mediocrity.
To the real working class: This movement is a purposeful Union Buster.
Radical union power, anti-business policies, and "tax the rich" schemes raise costs for everyone, kill jobs, and burden small businesses and families who already struggle with inflation.
The working class thrives under capitalism's mobility, not under class envy that punishes success.
To inclusion and unity: DSA-style identity politics and radicalism foster division, exclusion of dissenting voices, and cultural erosion the opposite of the American melting pot where anyone can rise through hard work regardless of background.
To representation and fiscal sanity: Massive spending, debt, and "democratic" overhauls (like abolishing checks and balances or expanding the electorate irresponsibly) amount to taxation without real accountability, saddling future generations while elites exempt themselves.
We reject DSA radicalism that ranges from anti-Israel (that alienates the American Jewish Community) extremism and defunding law enforcement to dismantling borders and imposing top-down economic control.
America was founded on self-reliance, constitutional liberty, and equal opportunity under the law, not enforced outcomes or "public ownership" that inevitably becomes elite control.
Leaders across the board from school boards to Congress must champion free markets, secure borders, strong families, law and order, and fiscal responsibility.
These principles lift all boats, reward merit, protect the vulnerable without creating permanent underclasses, and safeguard the freedoms that make America exceptional.
Reject the siren song of democratic socialism.
It promises utopia but delivers stagnation, division, and lost liberty.
Choose opportunity, self-determination, and the American Dream instead.
Our working families, communities, and future depend on it.
