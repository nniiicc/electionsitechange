I'm deeply passionate about this community, public service, and securing a bright future for our children.
That is what motivates me to fight relentlessly for our rights and our freedom.
Government overreach is unjust, dangerous, and fundamentally disgusting; it threatens everything we hold dear, and I will always stand against it to protect the liberty of our families and our community.
How will I tackle government overreach?
I support policies that limit government interference and promote individual liberties, ensuring your voice is heard.
What is my stance on education?
I champion Education Savings Accounts (ESAs) for all families, providing educational choice without raising taxes.
How do I plan to support families?
I advocate for low taxes, policies that help families and individuals buy homes, and the fostering of strong, safe communities.
