Candidate for Colorado State Senate, District 34
Jennifer Qualteri is a lifelong Coloradan and native of Senate District 34.
She earned a Bachelor of Science in Business from the University of Colorado with a focus on marketing and sales, followed by an MBA from the University of Denver emphasizing entrepreneurship and accounting.
A single parent who raised her children in Denver, Jennifer understands the daily challenges families face.
She built a career in public service with the State of Colorado and retired in 2024 as a Cost Accountant, bringing practical experience in responsible financial management to her candidacy.
Jennifer believes homeownership remains one of the strongest paths to financial security, equity, and generational wealth.
She supports innovative approaches including the responsible use of emerging technologies such as 3D printing that can help more Coloradans build or own homes affordably and outright.
She holds that families, not government, are the best decision-makers for children and that the state should step back from overreaching into the role of raising kids.
She champions Education Savings Accounts so individuals and families can afford the education and training needed to pursue their chosen careers.
Low taxes and safe neighborhoods are non-negotiable priorities.
Jennifer is running for Senate District 34 because government overreach has gone too far, and it is time to restore common-sense limits that put families, opportunity, and personal responsibility first.
