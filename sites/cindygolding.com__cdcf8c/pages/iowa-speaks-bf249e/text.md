The ICGA PAC is bipartisan by design, with equal representation from both sides of the aisle through farmer-members from each of Iowa’s nine crop reporting districts, appointed by the ICGA President.
The committee evaluates every candidate on ICGA policy alone, regardless of party, reviewing survey responses alongside established records on the priorities in our policy book.
