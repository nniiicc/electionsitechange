Mad About Your New Property Tax Assessment?
Remember – your local school board, city council and county supervisors determine your property tax.
Let them know!!
The law in Iowa states that “assessed value” is equal to “market value”.
The assessor finds the market value by analyzing recent sales, but also looks at the replacement cost (the amount it would cost to rebuild the property) less depreciation.
Assessed value should be as close to market value as possible.
After considering all relevant information, assessments are sent out to homeowners by April 1 of the assessment year.
A homeowner will not actually pay the taxes on these assessments until September of the following year.
What if a homeowner completely disagrees with the assessment of their home and property?
Fortunately, there is a process for you to argue your case.
The first step is to file with the local Board of Review.
Here is the form needed: Board of Review Petition.
Additionally—this website will describe the process: https://paab.iowa.gov/appealing-yourassessment.

For the Kids 2023 Choose Iowa Calendar Contest Iowa Secretary of Agriculture Mike Naig is encouraging school-age students to participate in the 2023 Choose Iowa Calendar Contest hosted by IDALS.
Artwork submitted should feature at least one aspect of Iowa agriculture, with an emphasis on food, livestock, or crop production.
Submissions will be judged on both creativity and the connection of agriculture to everyday life.
Winners will be announced at the 2023 Iowa State Fair.
Details: • • • • • • • students up to 18-years-old Pictures should be drawn on plain white, 8.5 by 11-inch paper in a horizontal orientation using only black lines.
The pictures should not be colored in.
Entries can be emailed to chooseiowa@iowaagriculture.gov ….. or mailed to the Iowa Department of Agriculture and Land Stewardship, Attn: Choose Iowa Calendar, 502 E 9th St., Des Moines, Iowa, 50319.
Each submission should include basic information about the artist (on the back of the entry or a separate sheet), including the artist’s name, age, grade, school, and hometown, as well as the name, email and phone number of a parent or guardian.
For consideration, entries must be postmarked or e-mailed to IDALS by June 1, 2023.
