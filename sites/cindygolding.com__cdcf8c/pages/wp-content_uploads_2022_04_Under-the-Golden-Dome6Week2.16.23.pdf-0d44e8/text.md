ESG is a method of scoring companies based on Environmental, Social, and Governance policies.
Under the Biden administration the focus on these scores in the private sector has given larger influence to companies based on how “green” or “diverse” a company is and not their financial performance.
Adani Group’s status as “sustainable” in these funds was supposed to protect investors’ money.
However, the Adani Group has faced several allegations of misconduct, fraud, and tax evasion over the years.
This resulted in the group and all of its companies being removed from investment funds, Credit Suisse and Citigroup have stopped accepting bonds of Adani Group companies as collateral, and the S&P Dow Jones Indices has removed Adani Enterprises from its sustainability index.
They have now lost over $100 Billion dollars of investor funds.

Veterans Affairs Veteran Code Modernization Advances This week, the Iowa House passed House File 259 unanimously.
2 This bill modernizes the Veterans Service Officers (VSOs) code by updating the definition of veteran, it adds requirements for the Iowa Department of Veterans Affairs and county VSOs to ensure VSOs are adequately trained to assist veterans.
This bill also updates the membership of the county commissions of veteran’s affairs, and updates who pays for the funeral expenses of a deceased veteran.
It was announced at the House Veterans Affairs Committee that $200 million more veterans’ benefits are coming in to the state of Iowa than the previous years.
The legislature wants to ensure that Iowa veterans are receiving all benefits they need, and Iowa’s VSOs are on the front line working with the veterans to make that happen.
