Representative Cindy Golding House District 83 Under the Golden Dome Summer Edition #3 Hi friends, Summer is in full swing, but so are my legislative duties.
The month of June was busy on several fronts, with several constituent meetings, legislative planning sessions, and regular business responsibilities, the time flew.
We were blessed with 5 of our grandchildren for the entire month, as well.
Life is complicated but full of excitement!
As you are all probably aware, in a 3-3 ruling, the Iowa Supreme Court upheld an injunction against the 2018 “Heartbeat” bill.
The ruling did not say the law was unconstitutional, but that the justices were “uncertain” about the intent of the legislature.
With an extremely unusual interpretation, one justice called the law “hypothetical” because the current legislature had not passed a similar bill.
If this “logic” were applied to all law, any section of the Iowa Code could be thrown out as “hypothetical” unless each section of the Code were passed in each General Assembly of the legislature.
The result of this judiciary over-reach, the Governor, by proclamation, has called a special session to convene tomorrow.
Click to see: Proclamation of Special Session The proposed legislation is available here HSB255.
