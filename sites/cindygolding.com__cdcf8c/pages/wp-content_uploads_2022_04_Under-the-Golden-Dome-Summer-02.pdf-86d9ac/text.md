Here are some of the education reforms we passed as requested by parents, teachers and administrators: House File 604 – Teacher Empowerment Signed into Law While striving to improve Iowa’s educational systems, we engaged all stake holders.
This bill is the result of teachers willing to come forward.
Classroom teachers came to us with shocking stories of the student behavioral issues they are asked to deal with on a daily basis.
Too often, these teachers are not getting the support they need from their administrations.
In many ways, they are hindered in their ability to discipline students that are disruptive and they are unable to protect themselves against students who are violent.
This bill (HF604): • • • • Allows teachers to make a complaint directly to the ombudsman’s office regarding violence in the classroom and requires the ombudsman to investigate.
Requires the school district to ensure their teachers know their rights regarding teacher immunity when coming into physical contact with a violent student.
Includes teacher whistleblower protections.
Require schools to develop an escalated discipline policy for disruptive or violent students.
This policy must include the ability to suspend or permanently remove a student from a class, expel, or move the student to an alternative learning environment.
The policy must also include the ability to send the student to counseling with parental consent.
House File 430 – BOEE Reform Signed into Law (Board of Educational Examiners) • • As one of our top priorities for the year, the Iowa House set out to reform the way the Board of Educational Examiners addresses serious complaints against teachers and ensure that parents are also represented on the board.
We accomplished this goal by making the following reforms to the Board of Education Examiners o Changes the make-up of the BOEE, which currently is all education bureaucrats.
This bill changes the makeup to four members of the general public, 8 licensed practitioners and the director of the Department of Education or a designee. o Requires mandatory reporters to report child abuse for all children up to 18. o Requires schools to keep an employment history of school employees that potential employers can review.
This will help ensure that bad actors cannot avoid discipline by hopping from school to school.
2 Prohibits districts from entering an agreement that doesn’t allow the district or the employee to discuss the reason for termination of a contract.
We know the vast majority of teachers are great people who are doing great work for our students.
But there must be a system in place to remove the ones that are harming our children. o • Senate File 496 – Parent Empowerment Signed into Law A number of House bills were consolidated in the more comprehensive Senate bill.
There are several pieces contained in the final bill. • • • • • • • Establishes a parent's or guardian's right to make decisions affecting their children. o Prevents schools from having policies that keep secrets from parents about their child’s gender identity. o If a student requests an accommodation at school for a gender identity that is different than their gender at birth, the teacher must report that information to the administration and the administration must tell the parents.
It also removes the teacher from the situation so they can stick to what they really signed up to do – teaching.
Authorizes the parent or guardian of a student to enroll the student in another attendance center within the same school district in certain specified circumstances.
Prohibits curriculum on gender identity or sexual orientation in K-6th grade.
Modifies provisions related to student health screenings. o Schools must get parental consent prior to giving surveys and must share who created and sponsored the survey as well as how the data is being used and stored.
Modifies school district library programs. o Ensures all books in schools are “age-appropriate.” o It explicitly states that age appropriate books do not include books that contain graphic images or descriptions of a sex act. o This specifically DOES NOT BAN BOOKS, as claimed by Democrats and the liberal press.
