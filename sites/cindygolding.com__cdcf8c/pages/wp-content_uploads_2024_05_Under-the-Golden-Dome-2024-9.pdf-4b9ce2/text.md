Every time we change the clocks a question rises - Why do we subject ourselves to this?
For centuries people have created systems to adjust to the changing seasons.
While Benjamin Franklin is often credited with the idea, it was actually implemented in Europe long before it came to the US.
The concept has an on-again, off-again history, beginning in 1918 during World War 1, then again in World War 11, and finally “permanent” in 1966.
There are strong views on both sides of the issue and the topic regularly comes before the legislature to “fix.” Last year, the Iowa House State Government Committee actually had a bill (HF498) to make CDT permanent.
I was quite surprised by the debate about which was healthier - Standard time or Daylight Savings time.
Because of the controversy, it didn’t make it to the floor last year or this year.
Some Highlights of Iowa House Week #9 Mental Health Priorities and Child Protection Measures We have all heard the call for increased focus on mental health issues.
The House has responded by advancing several bills to help address mental health care in Iowa.
These bills expand access to mental health care, increase workforce, increase Medicaid rates, create sustainable long-term funding of the mental health regions, and provide an emphasis on Past Issues Translate children’s mental health care.
Here are just a few of those: Subscribe HF 2402 - Children’s Mental Health – provides an enhanced rate for psychiatric medical institutions that care for children with specialized needs.
Now assigned to the Senate Health and Human Services Committee.
HF 2397 - Access Center Transportation – requires authorized payments to ambulances transporting mental health patients in crisis to an access center at a similar amount to when transporting to an ER.
Now assigned to the Senate Health and Human Services Committee.
HF 2523 - Social Media for Minors – requires parental consent for minors to access social media.
There are many studies that show increased depression in youth using social media.
Now waiting assignment to a Senate Committee.
Addressing AI (Artificial Intelligence) Pornography We needed updates to the Iowa Code to protect Iowans, especially children.
With these bills, we can ensure that there is no loophole in the law that would allow someone who commits this disturbing offense to avoid proper punishment.
HF 2240 - expands the definition of harassment to include using fake or altered images of another in a sexual manner without their consent.
Now waiting a vote in the Senate.
HF 2241 / SF 2243- addresses AI-generated porn related to minors.
This bill makes it a felony to create or possess an AI-generated pornographic content of a minor.
Now waiting assignment in the Senate HF 2610 / SF 2380 - Election Integrity and Voter Protection (my particular passion) Creates an affidavit envelope for absentee voters to affix their signature and verification number.
This will ensure Voter ID will be required, even if you vote absentee.
Removes the use of ballot drop boxes, which have become unnecessary with the COVID-19 pandemic behind us.
Strengthens the Safe at Home program to protect the confidentiality of voters who are victims of domestic violence, sexual assault, trafficking, stalking or assault.
Bans ranked choice voting in Iowa.
Requires Absentee ballots to be received by close of business the Monday before Election Day.
If the Auditor has not received your absentee ballot by Monday, you can then vote on election day to be sure your vote is counted.
Now awaiting Senate action.
Please notice - all the action taken by the House must be voted through the Senate.
