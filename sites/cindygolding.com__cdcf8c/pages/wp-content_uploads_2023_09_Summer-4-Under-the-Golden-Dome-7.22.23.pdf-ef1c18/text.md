Some things to know about the Heartbeat Bill – HF732 1) It does NOT have a firm 6-week deadline for an abortion.
Detection of a fetal heartbeat may vary greatly for each pregnancy.
2) It does NOT stop a physician from terminating a pregnancy if the mother’s life is at risk.
3) It DOES make allowance for reported victims of incest and rape for up to 20 weeks.
4) It DOES make allowance for medical emergency procedures beyond 20 weeks in cases of miscarriage and other fetal or maternal issues.
5) It DOES respect the life of both the mother and the child.
