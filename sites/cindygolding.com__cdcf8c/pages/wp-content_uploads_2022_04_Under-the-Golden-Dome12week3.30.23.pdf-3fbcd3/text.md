Representative Cindy Golding House District 83 Under the Golden Dome #12 As we leave the Capitol this week, we finished a marathon of 12 weeks.
The Second funnel was fairly intense with many conversations across the Rotunda.
Until this week we rarely saw Senators in the House Chamber and I hadn’t spent much time in the Senate.
I also saw more cross-party collaboration than any time in the past 12 weeks.
The push was to get Senate bills through a House Committee and House bills through a Senate Committee.
The next step will be to bring those surviving bills to a vote on the floor of the House or Senate.
Several pieces of legislation were set aside until next January with interim committees assigned to “work out the bugs”.
Other bills were moved to “Ways & Means” to survive the survive the funnel.
Have a wonderful weekend.
