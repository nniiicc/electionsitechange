Representative Cindy Golding House District 83 Under the Golden Dome Happy New Year 2024 Hello!
Happy New Year to all of you!
My last newsletter covered a few of the conferences I attended along with several of my colleagues.
Those meetings were focused mainly on policies that would help strengthen families, enhance education, and encourage stronger citizen involvement.
In November, we attended a WallBuilders Conference in Dallas, TX where we heard from some of our country’s outstanding experts on the foundation of our Constitution.
It was particularly interesting to see original documents of the Founding Fathers and to understand how much of our history has been twisted or misrepresented.
We discussed the challenges facing Iowans and learned how we can strengthen our Constitutional rights within our own state.
In December, an expert litigator on eminent domain, Terri Griffiths, and I organized a meeting in Denver, Colorado for State legislators from several states.
The Alternative Energy and Security Task Force focused on serious issues surrounding the production and risks of unreliable alternative energy sources.
In 2021 the electric-grid failure in Texas caused over 200 deaths and threatened the entire electric-grid structure.
This was a warning to all of us.
Iowa’s winters are much more severe than Texas so we can’t risk the same mistakes.
National experts on ground water presented research proving heavy metal contamination from solar farms and from their “companion” lithium battery storage banks.
Other researchers presented documentation on the dangers of CO2 sequestration, and the unconstitutional use of eminent domain for many of these projects.
NOAA (National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration) scientists discussed the effect of the sun on climate and the lack of evidence that CO2 is the villain.
At Jaxon Labs we learned of Chinese “back door” electronic components in our transformers and possible solutions we can implement at the state level.
The Perry School incident has brought school safety and teen mental health issues to the forefront – again!
The funding of SROs (School Resource Officers) and the possibility of mandating various building security measures have been proposed.
I will be seeking input from superintendents around my district to help frame legislation that works.
Emotionally-driven, knee-jerk legislation rarely works and no law will stop those with evil intent.
Taking guns away from law-abiding citizens is not the solution.
We need to look deeper into the mental health crisis, and find true solutions.
I will be working on that.
