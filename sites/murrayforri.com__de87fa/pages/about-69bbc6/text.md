Meet Melissa
It’s an honor and a privilege to represent Woonsocket and North Smithfield residents in the Rhode Island Senate.
I live and work in Woonsocket and share my life with my partner, Michael, our pug Elliot, and our cat Marcel Noel.
I currently serve as the Chair of Senate Committee on Health and Human Services, Vice Chair of the Senate Education Committee, member of the Senate Housing and Municipal Government, and Secretary of the Permanent Joint Committee on State Lottery.
I was born and raised in northern Rhode Island and attended Woonsocket public schools.
I have an AS in Criminal Justice with a focus in Forensic Science.
I was the first openly queer person elected to the Woonsocket City Council in 2013.
I served two terms before being the first woman elected to State Senate District 24 in 2018.
When I’m not performing legislative duties, I divide my time between working for a Woonsocket-based small business, supporting local events and organizations, and volunteering in the community.
A lifelong animal lover, I have fostered and volunteered for numerous animal rescues for over 25 years.
