New law requires crime lab to audit DNA databank for missing offender profiles

Edwards and Sen.
Melissa A.
Murray that was signed by Gov.
Dan McKee this week.
The databank is used to search previously collected profiles for matches with DNA collected at crimes scenes to identify potential suspects.
State law requires offenders convicted of violent crimes to submit DNA samples for entry into the databank.
