What are the Issues I'm Focused On?
Affordable Health Care: Lower cost and expand access to medical care, reproductive healthcare, and mental/behavioral healthcare
Strong Public Schools: Fully fund education, support students and teachers
Grow Business, Support Workers: Tackle rising costs, support small businesses and workers
Affordable Housing: Build more housing, keep communities livable for all, and maintain local control
Protect Rights & Equity: Defend freedoms and ensure fairness for every Rhode Islander
Climate & Environment: Invest in clean energy and protect our natural resources
Accountable Government: Deliver transparent, responsive leadership that puts people first
