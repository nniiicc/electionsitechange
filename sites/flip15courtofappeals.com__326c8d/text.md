Welcome to the Texas Justice Tour
This week, the three of us are starting something together.
We’re running for the Texas 15th Court of Appeals: Jerry Zimmerer for Chief Justice, Tom Baker for Place 2, Marc Meyer for Place 3.
Let’s start with a fact that should bother every Texan, no matter how you vote: not a single justice on this court got there by winning your vote.
A court that rules on your water, your paycheck, your family, and your rights was filled entirely by one person, and until this election, you never had a say in it.
That’s what’s on the ballot November 3rd.
We’ll be showing up across the state, and we mean everywhere: the cities and the small towns, the places that only get remembered every two years.
We’ll ask what’s weighing on you.
We’ll take it back with us.
Because a court can’t answer to people it doesn’t know.
The central issue is straightforward: do these seats belong to the governor who appointed them, or to the voters?
We believe the law and the Constitution favor the voters.
No statewide court can claim true impartiality if it is insulated from accountability.
On November 3rd, help us make this the People’s Court.
— Jerry, Tom, and Marc
Tour stopJuly 2026 · Hidalgo County
What a day in Hidalgo County!
Yesterday we launched our Texas Justice Tour, and there was no question in my mind about where it had to start.
It had to start in the Valley.
For many, the 15th Court of Appeals feels like something that happens somewhere else: in Austin, behind closed doors, far from the people it’s supposed to serve.
Governor Greg Abbott appointed every justice on this court.
Not one of them was elected.
Now, Tom, Marc, and I seek to be the first elected justices of the 15th Court of Appeals.
I spent the day with folks who actually make our democracy work.
I met with party leadership like Richard Gonzales at the Hidalgo County Democratic Party’s CEC meeting, and with the grassroots organizers who knock the doors and make the calls that no one ever sees.
I was introduced to the room by Reggie Richardson, President-Elect of the Hidalgo County Bar Association.
I got to talk with grassroots activist Chrisleda Vera, Michelle Vallejo of Mujeres Unidas, and Julio Salinas, our Democratic candidate for State Representative in District 41.
And I ate very well, 107 Café being one of the best breakfasts I’ve had in a while.
What struck me most was how tightly this community holds itself together, right down to the small businesses.
I’ll fight to make Greg Abbott’s court the People’s Court: accountable and faithful to the Constitution.
Thank you to everyone who shook my hand and shared a story yesterday.
It’s the honor of my life to ask to represent you across this state.
— Jerry Zimmerer
