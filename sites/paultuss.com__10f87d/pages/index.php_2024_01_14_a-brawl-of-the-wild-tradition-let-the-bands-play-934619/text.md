Paul Tuss, Opinion, Havre Daily News 11/24/2023 Montanans just enjoyed the largest sporting event of the year, with more than 27,000 of us coming together in person to witness this year’s version of the Brawl of the Wild.
The football game and intrastate rivalry between the Montana Grizzlies and Montana State Bobcats is 122 years old and began only eight years after Montana obtained statehood in 1889.
By any measure, this contest is so much more than just another game and has become an important part of the fabric of Montana.
