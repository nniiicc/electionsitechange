Who is Sam Martin?
Sam Martin is a lifelong Arizonan, raised in Yuma by a public school teacher and a deputy city clerk.
With more than 20 years of experience in public facing service and technical roles, Sam is an expert communicator and negotiator.
Sam also brings subject matter expertise in: Cybersecurity / cybercrime Privacy Cloud computing Horticulture, especially low water agriculture Integrated pest management Photography Radio Vaccines Cat herding, dog whispering Healthcare Consumer advocacy Sam is a resident of Oracle, Arizona.
