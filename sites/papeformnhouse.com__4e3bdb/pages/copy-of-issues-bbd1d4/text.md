MCCL PAC Endorses Steve Pape for House District 35B
The Minnesota Citizens Concerned for Life (MCCL) State Political Action Committee (PAC) is pleased to endorse your candidacy in the 2026 election for Minnesota House of Representatives representing District 35B.
Our endorsement is based on your 100% response to the MCCL Candidate Questionnaire, and your commitment to the protection of innocent human life.
NFIB Minnesota PAC Endorses Steve Pape for House District 35B
The NFIB Minnesota PAC has officially endorsed Steve Pape (House District 35B) in its third round of 2026 legislative endorsements.
Recognizing his strong commitment to championing Main Street, the endorsement highlights Pape as a trusted ally who will fight against burdensome workplace mandates and high taxes, helping Minnesota small businesses grow, create jobs, and remain competitive.
Honored to Receive the MPPOA Endorsement
I am proud to receive an endorsement from the Minnesota Police and Peace Officers Association.
The MPPOA serves as the largest public safety labor and professional advocacy group in Minnesota, representing over 10,000 licensed law enforcement officers, dispatchers, correctional officers, and firefighters.
I have always respected the work of our law enforcement and those who maintain public safety.
I will continue to support the men and women who put their lives on the line everyday to ensure our community stays safe and well cared for!
