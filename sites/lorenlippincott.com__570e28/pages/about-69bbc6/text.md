Home About District 34 Issues News Get Involved Donate Donate Home About District 34 Issues News Get Involved About Loren Lippincott State Senator Loren Lippincott is a fifth-generation Nebraskan, lifelong conservative, and proud representative of District 34 in the Nebraska Legislature, which includes Hamilton, Merrick, and Nance counties, as well as part of Hall County including Grand Island.
Elected in 2022, Senator Lippincott has emerged as a strong voice for rural Nebraska, defending traditional values, protecting constitutional freedoms, and championing conservative fiscal policy.
Senator Lippincott was raised on his family’s farm north of Central City, where he still lives and works today.
He learned the value of hard work and service early, attending a one-room schoolhouse, earning the rank of Eagle Scout, and serving as class president and student body president in high school.
He walked on to the Nebraska Cornhuskers football team during his freshman year at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, which was also Tom Osborne’s first year as head coach.
While earning a degree in broadcast journalism, Lippincott worked multiple jobs—including stints at KOLN-TV, KBHL-FM, and the Nebraska Legislature, where he served as a page.
After graduation, he continued his broadcasting career before joining the U.S.
Air Force.
There, he graduated third in a class of 700 from Officer Training School, became an instructor pilot, and later served as an F-16 pilot stationed at Ramstein Air Base in Germany.
He was recognized as his squadron’s overall Top Gun following rigorous competitions in air-to-air and air-to-ground combat.
After ten years of service and achieving the rank of Captain, he transitioned to a 30-year career with Delta Air Lines, retiring as an international captain on the Boeing 767.
Since entering the Nebraska Legislature, Senator Lippincott has been a leading advocate for property tax relief, pro-life protections, Second Amendment rights, and policies that support Nebraska families, agriculture, and faith-based communities.
He currently serves as Chairman of the Legislature’s Rules Committee, where he helps shape the body’s procedural governance, and also sits on the influential Appropriations Committee, where he plays a direct role in crafting the state budget with a focus on responsible spending, educational investment, and rural priorities.
Senator Lippincott is a member of Central City Bible Church and is the proud father of two sons and grandfather to three grandchildren.
He is seeking a second term to continue fighting for fewer regulations, lower taxes, and the Nebraska values that define District 34.
