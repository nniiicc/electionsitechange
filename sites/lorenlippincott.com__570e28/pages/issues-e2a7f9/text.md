Dad gave me my first gun when I was about ten.
Having guns around the farm was a way of life.
The Second Amendment gives us the right to own a gun whether for hunting, collecting, or personal protection.
I support a Castle Doctrine law because you have a right to protect your home from intruders.
In 2023, I voted for LB77, which established Constitutional Carry in Nebraska because I believe you should be able to carry a gun without getting a permission slip from the government.
In the Legislature, I have always, and will always, defend the Second Amendment.
Property taxes continue to squeeze Nebraska farmers, families, manufacturers and seniors.
In neighboring Kansas and Iowa the state funds a bigger share of the cost of public schools than Nebraska.
It is time to increase the state share of funding for public schools … and reduce the over-reliance on property taxes to fund education.
In 2024, I voted for LB34, which allows taxpayers to receive a direct credit on their property tax statements for school district taxes and ensures the credit increases annually and grows with the economy
There is a lot more work to be done and I continue to fight every day to reduce property taxes.

In the Legislature, I have and always will vote to protect innocent life.

In the Legislature, I have and always will vote to protect our kids.
