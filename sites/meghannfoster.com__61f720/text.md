Meet Meghann Meghann Foster is running for the Iowa Senate to represent District 43 , serving Coralville, North Liberty, Solon, and West Iowa City.
She is the former Mayor of Coralville, with more than eight years in local government and a record of community-centered leadership focused on affordable housing, access to services, and people-first growth.
Meghann has served on the Coralville City Council, the Planning and Zoning Commission, and numerous regional boards.
Her leadership is hands-on and community-driven, guided by a commitment to strengthening families, expanding access to essential services, and ensuring that community voices are heard.
