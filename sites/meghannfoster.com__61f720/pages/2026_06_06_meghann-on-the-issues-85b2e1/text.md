Meet Meghann Issues News Volunteer Donate Campaign Meghann on the Issues June 6, 2026 0 Here are Meghann’s expanded responses to the recent Vote 411 questionnaire. __ What steps will you take to ensure everyone living in our state has access to quality and affordable health care?
Iowa’s health care infrastructure has been decimated under a decade of one-party rule.
Rural communities have been hit the hardest, as we see more and more clinics closing down.
In addition, the attacks on reproductive healthcare have had a negative impact, as Iowa ranks dead last in the nation for obgyns per capita.
I believe every Iowan deserves access to affordable, high-quality health care no matter their income or ZIP code.
If elected, I will: Fight to protect and expand Medicaid Support legislation that will lower prescription drug costs Support legislation that would expand access to mental health care Work to proctect Iowa’s rural hospitals and clinics and expand access in underserved areas Protect reporductive freedom, including opposing efforts to criminalize doctors and women for providing/seeking life-saving treatment What will you do to combat the underlying issues driving the inflation rate such as in rising costs of housing, childcare, energy, and groceries?
Affordability is a top concern for Iowans of all ages and stages.
We need practical strategies that lower costs for working people instead of shifting the burden onto them.
Iowans are in desperate need of relief, and it’s vital that we create and deliver on solutions in a meaningful way.
I support policies that will: Increase the minimum wage Protect homeowners (including mobile home residents) and renters from predatory practices and price gouging Address rising insurance costs by strengthening consumer protections and oversight of premium increases Strengthen and expand programs to support first-time homebuyers Support the growth of affordable, local childcare programs and expand the eligibility requirements for childcare assistance Strengthen and expand support for community-based food programs Support policies that strengthen competition and consumer protections, so large corporations can’t drive up grocery, energy, and household prices unchecked.
What actions will you take to uphold the constitutional balance between federal and state authority in the administration of our state-run elections?
Unfortunately, we are seeing numerous attempts to undermine and politicize the integrity of our elections through partisan overreach.
These efforts are dangerous and undermine one of the most sacred parts of our democracy.
I believe: Iowa should continue to administer its own elections with policies that emphasize fairness, security and transparency, and consistency with both the U.S. and Iowa Constitutions.
We can protect the integrity of our elections while also protecting every eligible citizen’s right to vote.
Qualified county auditors should be elected by the people they serve and are not appointed through a partisan political process.
We should support policies that give local election officials the resources they need to maintain accurate voter rolls through lawful and transparent processes, and protect the independence and professionalism of the public servants who administer our elections.
I will also fight to protect the privacy of Iowans sensitive information as well as bipartisan, transparent redistricting processes.
What are the most important challenges facing our state, and what do you propose to address them?
Affordability and health care costs/access (see above) Cancer crisis: Iowa is facing a growing cancer crisis, with some of the fastest-rising rates in the nation.
Keeping Iowans healthy requires bipartisan solutions, with a strong focus on water quality, research, and community-driven public health initiatives.
Attacks on public education: Iowa’s public education system has fallen dramatically in national rankings.
A decade of one-party rule has set our public schools up for failure due to severe underfunding and growing political interference in classrooms.
We need to restore Iowa’s tradition of excellent public schools by: Fully funding public education Supporting our teachers through increased pay and providing them with adequate resources they need to succeed Ensuring every student, no matter where they live or what kind of support they need, has access to a high-quality education.
Attacks on civil rights: Civil rights are not a zero-sum game.
They ensure every Iowan, regardless of race, gender identity, religion, disability, or sexual orientation, have the freedom to live without fear of discrimination.
Unfortunately, Iowa has rolled back protections that once ensured safety and equal rights.
These rollbacks harm communities already at risk, and send the message that Iowa is an unwelcoming place.
This has far-reaching implications for economic growth (we’re dead last in the nation) population growth (young people are leaving the state in droves) and more.
Civil rights are for everyone, and they must be restored to ensure every person can live, work, and thrive in our state.
Water quality: Iowans are fed up with the status quo and want to see meaningful action around water quality issues.
While some steps have been taken, we need to do more, including: Ensuring honesty and transparency around causes and expanding testing/monitoring Keeping the nitrate sensor network fully funded Investing in infrastructure to help smaller communities and folks who use private wells Adequate nutrient-reduction strategies (voluntary practices aren’t enough) Investing in educational efforts What three actions will you support to protect, preserve, and enhance America’s democracy and why?
It is in all of our best interests to protect and strengthen our democracy and ensure our government elevates the needs of the public first and foremost.
This requires electing leaders who are thoughtful, empathetic, knowledgeable, and committed to public service as a responsibility to the people and not a path to personal gain.
It also requires strong oversight and accountability to prevent corruption and ensure government remains transparent and responsive.
We must work together to listen to each other and work through disagreements in a productive way, seeking common ground as often as possible.
I support: Protecting fair and secure elections (see above) Increasing transparency and reducing the influence of money in politics.
