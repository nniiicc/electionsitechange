In doing so, it has dismissed legitimate concerns about human life, environmental harm, our moral standing in the world, and the economic well‑being of ordinary Americans.
Today, thousands of U.S. troops are being positioned for a potential invasion—alone, without allied support, without secure supply lines, and without a clearly defined mission.
This places our service members in grave danger and reflects a profound failure of civilian leadership and accountability.
America’s historic role as a moral leader and defender of democracy is being eroded.
Our troops are increasingly placed at risk by wars of aggression, territorial ambitions, and the absence of meaningful oversight over military decision‑making.
Yet many of Florida’s elected officials have responded with either uncritical praise for the administration or troubling silence at a moment when our troops’ lives are on the line.
I call on my fellow Floridians in District 10 to insist that Senators Scott and Moody and Congresswoman Cammack fulfill their constitutional responsibilities.
They must demand transparency, accountability, and proper legislative oversight of our civilian military leaders.
Our service members—and our nation’s moral integrity—deserve nothing less.
Dr.
Joseph E.
Thornton American Solidarity Party 2026 Candidate for Florida Senate District 06 Affordability Affordability is more than the price of a house or a tax bill.
Affordability is an ecosystem of jobs with income justice, family support, education, and community health.
We need leadership that fosters engagement and genuine problem-solving.
We have to recognize that all politics is local, and national political talking points rarely address the daily economic realities facing our families in Senate District 6.
I will work to bring a new paradigm for problem-solving in the public sphere.
Affordability is not a single number or policy domain—it is the sum total of how our communities actually thrive.
It cannot be reduced to a scorekeeping game for politicians to win elections; it demands human-centered leadership that protects workers, relieves debt burdens, and builds lasting local wealth.
1.
Workforce Dignity & Income Justice Protecting Jobs from AI Displacement: Champion legislative safeguards like the Work With Dignity Act and The Florida AI Act to ensure artificial intelligence is used to assist human workers rather than eliminate entry-level and career positions.
Family-Sustaining Wages: Support policies that make a family wage a reality, allowing single-income households to live with dignity.
Worker Ownership: Expand employee ownership models, cooperatives, trade guilds, and union rights to prevent corporate exploitation and keep capital in the hands of working families.
2.
Family Support & Educational Relief Direct Family Subsidies: Support pro-family economic policies, including cash subsidies for stay-at-home caregivers, “baby bonuses,” and favorable tax treatment for growing families.
Student Debt Reform: Address the crippling debt burden on young adults and recent graduates by allowing student loans to be dischargeable in bankruptcy and expanding public funding for higher education.
3.
Healthcare Affordability & Medical Debt Cancellation Medical Debt Relief: Advocate for the immediate cancellation of existing medical debt so that a health crisis does not lead to financial ruin.
Controlling Costs: Support federal and state price negotiations on prescription drugs to stop corporate exploitation of captive patients, and oppose hospital mergers that limit community-based care and inflate prices.
4.
Housing & Local Community Wealth (Distributism) Local Land & Housing Access: Support community land trusts, tenant protections against unjust evictions, and neighborhood-scaled housing developments to increase availability and lower prices.
Reforming Punitive Zoning: Reform rigid zoning laws that unfairly target poor and working-class citizens, opening up opportunities for small family businesses, local agriculture, and neighborhood enterprises.
