DARRELL JACKS N DARRELL JACKS N Democratic candidate with an Independent voice for District 74 Home I about I ENDORSEMENTs i issues I donate Contact: Darrell@VoteDarrellJackson.com F OR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Darrell Jackson Ann ounces Campaign for Ohio’s 74th House District SPRINGFIELD, Ohio — After 26 years serving Clark County with the Clark County Sheriff’s Department, retired deputy and longtime law enforcement officer Darrell Jackson has announced his candidacy for the 74th Ohio House District.
“Ohio’s strength comes from public servants committed to fairness and opportunity,” Jackson said.
“In recent years, our middle class has carried the burden of poor policy choices.
I’m running to restore common sense, strengthen our schools and communities, and ensure economic opportunity reaches every Ohioan.” Jackson emphasized the need for leaders who reject special‑interest agendas and focus on practical solutions.
“People in Clark County are tired of just getting by.
They want leaders who work hard, listen, and deliver results.
That’s the kind of representative I intend to be.” A lifelong Clark County resident, Jackson lives with his wife, Gina Carney Jackson, who has worked in healthcare for 36 years.
He is committed to restoring good‑paying jobs, prioritizing children’s health and education, and strengthening community well‑being.
Jackson looks forward to meeting voters across the district ahead of Election Day 2026.
