Home Why I'm Running Issues Two Americas Contact Donate Volunteer ABOUT SCOTT Scott Huffman is a dad, a Navy veteran, and a working professional in information technology and cybersecurity.
He is a longtime community advocate who believes public service should be about showing up, listening, and working for everyday people.
Scott has twice been nominated by the Democratic Party to run for Congress, and he continues to stand up for democracy, the rule of law, and a government that works for everyone — not just the wealthy or the well-connected.
He believes our democracy is strongest when leaders respect voters, protect constitutional rights, and are accountable to the people they serve.
As a candidate for NC House District 76, Scott is committed to fighting for working families, fair wages, affordable healthcare, strong public schools, and respect for teachers and public employees.
He supports voting rights, ending gerrymandering, protecting women’s reproductive freedom, addressing climate change, advancing equality, supporting veterans, improving public safety, humane immigration reform, criminal justice reform, and ensuring every child has a fair shot at a better future through our public schools.
