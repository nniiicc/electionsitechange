I know what it means to fight.
Right now I'm fighting advanced prostate cancer.
I'm still running, I'm going to beat cancer, and I'll fight just as hard for the people of Rowan County.
We were promised that if we worked hard and played by the rules, we'd have a shot at the American Dream.
Then Raleigh rigged the game.
For 16 years, Republicans have run our state for big corporations and their donors.
They handed out corporate tax breaks and free tax dollars for the school voucher scam.
They let Duke Energy raise our bills and rolled out the welcome mat for unregulated AI data centers.
Meanwhile they never found time to raise a $7.25 minimum wage.
People here are living paycheck to paycheck.
Teachers work second jobs.
Our roads and bridges are crumbling.
Families are one illness away from losing everything.
I'm a Navy veteran, a dad, and a small business owner.
I've spent more than 30 years working in IT and cybersecurity.
After the Navy I came home with nothing but what I'd learned serving my country, started a business, and created good jobs right here.
I know what it's like to make payroll.
I know what it's like to lose sleep over a bill.
House District 76 needs someone in Raleigh who works for us, not for Duke Energy, big tech, or big donors.
That means lower bills, fully funded schools, a living wage, healthcare for all, and voting rights for every North Carolinian.
Cancer didn't stop me, and the folks in Raleigh won't either.
It's time for leaders who put people over power.
That's why I'm running. ​Scott Huffman We need volunteers to help in all areas of our campaign.
