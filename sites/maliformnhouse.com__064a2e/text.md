The Way Forward is Together
Mali Anderson is running for Minnesota State House of Representatives for District 17A.
She is passionate about making a change for rural Minnesotan's in her district.
House District 17A consists of most of McLeod County, including the cities of Hutchinson, Glencoe, Silver Lake and Stewart, the Cokato area of southwest Wright County, three townships in western Sibley County, and the Dassel-Darwin area of southeastern Meeker County.
Mali's Focus Areas
Defending Constitutional Rights
Mali believes in defending the human and constitutional rights of all Minnesotans.
Universal Healthcare
Mali understands the importance of healthcare for all.
She expresses gratitude for government programs that have assisted her and her children with housing and medical care and supports the rights of others to have the same opportunities.
