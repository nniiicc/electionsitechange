She is passionate about making a change for rural Minnesotan's in her district. ​ House District 17A consists of most of McLeod County, including the cities of Hutchinson, Glencoe, Silver Lake and Stewart, the Cokato area of southwest Wright County, three townships in western Sibley County, and the Dassel-Darwin area of southeastern Meeker County.
Join the Movement About Mali Mali knows what it's like to experience hardship.
She has endured homelessness, is a sexual assault survivor, and a disability advocate.
She recognizes the importance of community and the strength found in the support of government programs for those in need.
