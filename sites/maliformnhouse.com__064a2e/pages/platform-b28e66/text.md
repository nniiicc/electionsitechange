Mali Understands that the Way Forward is Together
Advocacy
Mali knows what it's like to be a rural Minnesotan.
She understands the importance of upholding the rural lifestyle of those in her district.
If Mali is elected she will be an advocate for farming families, women-owned businesses, union workers, teachers, and all those in her district.
Community Engagement
Mali wants to uphold the social programs that have been so meaningful for her and her family's health and wellbeing.
If elected she will fight to get universal healthcare for all, expand rural transportation, increase minimum wages to living wages, advocate for more affordable and accessible housing options, increase companion animal friendly housing options, support community food programs, and work to create a more welcoming and inclusive community for those in her district.
Mali is endorsed by the DFL Rural Caucus
Defending Human Rights and Upholding the Constitution
Mali believes in the rights of all Minnesotans.
She is a strong supporter of upholding human rights and ICE OUT MN.
She also believes in upholding the constitutional rights of those in her district.
If elected, Mali will fight to uphold human and constitutional rights.
Mali is passionate about social service- this is Mali cleaning the highways in Meeker County
Environmental Advocacy
Mali believes in holding corporations accountable to the environmental damages that they are doing to Minnesota's sacred lands.
If elected, Mali will hold these corporations accountable for the harm they have already done to our states lands and help to prevent future harm.
Education Reforms
Mali understands the importance of a good public education system and will work to support educators.
She hopes to expand funding for Head Start programs, increase community school supply drives in the district, and improve benefit programs for teachers across the state.
