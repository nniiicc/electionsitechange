Frequently Asked Questions Are contributions tax-deductible?
Contributions to political campaigns are not tax-deductible.
However, your support directly funds our efforts to fight for the issues that matter to District 127 families.
Are there contribution limits?
Yes, Pennsylvania campaign finance law sets limits on individual contributions to state legislative campaigns.
Please contact us if you have questions about contribution limits.
Can I contribute if I'm not from District 127?
Yes!
U.S. citizens and permanent residents from anywhere can contribute to Pennsylvania state legislative campaigns, subject to applicable limits and regulations.
How will my contribution be used?
Your contribution supports voter outreach, community events, campaign materials, and operations.
We're committed to running an efficient campaign that maximizes the impact of every dollar.
