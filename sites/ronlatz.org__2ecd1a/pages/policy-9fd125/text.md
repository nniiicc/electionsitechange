Public Safety & Justice Reform
Gun Violence Prevention
Ron has led Minnesota's efforts to prevent gun violence, strengthen public safety, and reduce tragedies in schools and communities.
- Authored Minnesota's Extreme Risk Protection Order (Red Flag) law, which allows temporary removal of firearms from individuals in crisis and has already been used hundreds of times to prevent suicides and violence.
- Authored universal background checks to close private sale and gun show loopholes
- Co-led Senate passage of the largest gun violence prevention package in state history, including measures to:
- Ban assault weapons and high-capacity magazines
- Enact safe storage requirements to prevent accidental shootings and youth access
- Reenact a ban on binary triggers
- Advocated for investments in violence prevention research, violent crime prevention teams, and school safety specialists to address root causes of gun violence.
- Supports establishing and funding a dedicated Office of Gun Violence Prevention at the Minnesota Department of Health to coordinate statewide prevention efforts.

Ron believes in effective, accountable policing and transparency in the justice system.
- Supported body camera transparency and law enforcement accountability measures.

Protecting Courts, Preventing Violence, and Strengthening Public Institutions

- Supports policies that protect democratic institutions while ensuring elected officials can continue serving the public safely and effectively, and the public can be safe while visiting the Capitol.

He will keep leading the fight to pass an assault weapons ban, invest in violence prevention, and build a public safety system that is effective, accountable, and worthy of the public's trust.

Immigration and Civil Liberties
As a direct response to Operation Metro Surge and ongoing federal immigration enforcement overreach in our communities, Ron Latz advanced his ICE accountability package through the Minnesota Senate this session, working to build safer and more just communities.
ICE package that Ron chief-authored:
- Essential Spaces — Protecting childcare centers, schools, hospitals, and courthouses from unlawful immigration raids.
- Preserving Investigations — Ensuring that state investigators can access crime scenes and evidence related to federal law enforcement
- Right to Sue — Allowing Minnesotans to sue in state court when their federal rights have been violated
- Unmask ICE — Barring federal agents from covering their faces while performing official duties unless it's for protection from smoke or other chemicals
- Render Aid — Requiring that law enforcement help people they injure once any threat has been neutralized
Ron Latz is committed to holding the federal government accountable for its actions and strengthening state-level protections to ensure Minnesota is prepared for similar challenges in the future.
He is also focused on ensuring that all Minnesotans, regardless of where they were born, have access to safe housing, healthcare, education, and the full protection of their rights.
Ron Latz knows that Minnesota is not just made better by immigrant communities; they embody and challenge us to live up to our own values: hard work, resilience, and hope.

Transportation, Infrastructure, and Roads
Highway 7 Pedestrian Bridge
Ron helped find alternative funding sources to construct the Highway 7 pedestrian bridge, improving safety and access for District 46 residents.

Southwest Light Rail
Ron has been a key legislative advocate for Southwest Light Rail, which will connect downtown Minneapolis to Eden Prairie through St.
Louis Park and Hopkins.
The 15-mile extension is expected to open in 2027 and will carry 34,000 riders per day by 2040.
The project includes 8,690+ housing units planned within walking distance of transit stations, with 900+ specifically designated as affordable housing.
The project represents $3.1 billion in regional development investment and creates walkable communities with 10+ miles of new sidewalks and 6+ miles of bike facilities.
DWI Reforms — Passed Unanimously
Ron championed legislation strengthening protections against repeat offenders.
It extends the lookback period for DWI offenses and improves ignition interlock program requirements.
Ron introduced this legislation in response to the tragic Park Tavern crash in St.
Louis Park, demonstrating his commitment to keeping dangerous drivers off our roads and learning from local tragedies.
Ron Latz is committed to expanding safe, accessible, and reliable transportation options that serve Minnesotans and protect our environment.
He believes we must invest in stronger public transit while also ensuring our roads and vehicles meet high safety standards, so people can get where they need to go safely and efficiently.
