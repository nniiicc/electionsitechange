“I’m proud to support Kristina Moorhead for State Senate,” said Nelson.
“She brings integrity, strong leadership, and a real commitment to serving our community.
Kristina listens, works hard, and focuses on practical solutions that matter to families and local businesses.
She has my full support.” Nelson’s endorsement adds to the growing momentum behind Moorhead’s campaign, which is focused on delivering commonsense, results-driven leadership for Hoosiers.
“I’m honored to have Miles’ support,” said Moorhead.
“His dedication to our community and belief in bringing people together to get things done means a great deal.
I look forward to continuing to build a campaign centered on listening, collaboration, and real solutions for the people of District 29.” The endorsement reflects growing momentum for Moorhead’s campaign as it continues to build a broad coalition of support across Senate District 29.
Moorhead is running for Indiana State Senate District 29 to put her proven policy experience to work—cutting costs for families, ending government overreach, and building an Indiana where every child and every Hoosier has the opportunity to thrive.
