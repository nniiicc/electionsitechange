DONATE About Kristina My name is Kristina Moorhead, and I’m running for Indiana State Senate District 29 because I believe government works best when it listens to—and understands—the people it serves.
I didn’t set out to work in politics, but a debate tournament at Ball State unexpectedly sparked a lifelong passion for public policy and its power to shape real lives.
After earning a Master of Public Affairs from Indiana University-Indianapolis, I went on to serve in state government, including as a Deputy Director of the Indiana Office of Medicaid Policy and Planning.
Over the past 20 years, I’ve worked on health policy in 23 states — inside government, with AARP, in the private sector, and as a consultant — giving me a clear view of what works, what doesn’t, and how decisions at the Statehouse affect families every day.
I’m a lifelong Hoosier who grew up on the east side of Indianapolis and in New Palestine, raised by parents who taught me the value of community, hard work, and showing up for others.
Today, I’m a wife and mom of three.
On the weekends, you can find me cheering on my kids from the sidelines, coaching youth sports, or enjoying our beautiful city and state parks with my family.
I’m running for State Senate to bring common sense, compassion, and real-world experience to the decisions that shape our future.
Indiana deserves leaders who know this state, love it, and are ready to do the work—and I’d be honored to earn your trust and your vote.
On The Issues I’m running because I care deeply about the future of Indiana—and because the decisions made at the Statehouse affect families like mine every single day.
AFFORDABILITY I know how hard-working families are struggling just to get by.
Too many people can no longer afford the basics—groceries, property taxes, utilities, or childcare—even when they’re doing everything right.
It’s time for our government to stand with working Hoosiers, lower everyday costs, and make sure our economy works for everyone.
PUBLIC EDUCATION As a mom with children in public schools, I am passionate about fully funding K-12 and higher education to invest in the children who are the future of our state.
Research shows that investing in education leads to major economic gains of both salaries and gross domestic product – critical to creating a state where Hoosiers can achieve their own American dream.
HEALTHCARE As a former Medicaid Deputy Director, I know how to design and lead state programs that provide quality services to Hoosier and maximize taxpayer dollars.
Shortsighted cuts may look good on paper, but they often end up costing families and taxpayers far more in the long run.
REPRODUCTIVE FREEDOM I believe in reproductive freedom as the human right to control your own body, fertility, and reproductive health without political interference or coercion.
We must protect access to abortion, contraception, fertility treatment, maternal healthcare, and safe childbirth for all Hoosiers.
GOOD GOVERNANCE I’m concerned by how blurred the lines have become between state and federal authority, and between the legislative and executive branches.
Respecting those boundaries isn’t just about process—it’s about accountability and protecting our democracy.
ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY I believe Indiana can attract businesses without sacrificing our environment or settling for jobs that don’t pay a living wage.
Growth should benefit working families, not just corporations.
OUR CHILDREN I want Indiana to be a place where my children—and yours—choose to stay, raise families, and build their futures.
I’m running to help make that Indiana a reality.
