Luis Garcia is a retired 28-year firefighter/paramedic, former business owner, licensed claims adjuster, and lifelong South Floridian.
Originally from Colombia, Luis became a naturalized citizen after serving in the United States Marines.
He is fluent in Spanish and French and grew up in a Miami home shaped by faith, hard work, love, and strong moral values.
Luis is a father of two adult sons and enjoys hustle dancing, community service, and helping others.
- High School Graduate, Miami Dade Coral Park, 1984
- Miami Dade College, 93 semester credits completed
- Retired Florida Licensed Firefighter
- Florida Licensed Paramedic
- Florida Licensed Claims Adjuster
Luis has dedicated much of his life to service, emergency response, and community advocacy.
- Founder of a 501(c)(3) organization that has helped save over 500 people across the United States with Narcan
- Emergency Response Team Coordinator for the No Kings Protest in Palm Beach Gardens
- Founder of an ICE Watch Legal Observer Team serving Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, and Martin Counties
- Volunteer experience as a Guardian Angel, Police & Fire Explorer Scout, CPR/First Aid/AED instructor, and child safety seat technician
- CNN GoFundMe Hero Nominee
- Fire Department Lifesaving Award
- Sun Sentinel Community Volunteer Award
- GoFundMe Hero of the Year
Luis’s life motto is:
“Help All.
Hurt None.
For a Better Tomorrow.”
If elected to the Florida House of Representatives for District 91, Luis Garcia is committed to meeting with all constituents, including Republicans, Democrats, and Independents, with the goal of helping the community heal, reconnect, and move forward.
