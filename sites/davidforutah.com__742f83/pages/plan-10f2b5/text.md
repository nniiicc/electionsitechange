Cut the Cost of Living
It is becoming impossible for Utahns to afford to live here.
The cost of living is an existential crisis, and we need to act fast.
We need to:
- Cut taxes for working Utahns
- Ban sales tax on essentials
- Break up monopolies
- Provide free school meals for all kids
- Invest in everyday Utahns to solve the housing crisis
Take Back People Power
We, as ordinary people, need to take back power from the special interests that run this state.
The broken two-party system has failed us; it’s time we fix it.
We need to:
- Let the people amend the Constitution
- Use Ranked Choice Voting for all elections so third parties have a chance
- Ban gerrymandering for good
- Ban corporate money from politics
Protect Public Lands
Powerful people are trying to destroy and privatize our public lands.
We need to:
- Create more state parks to keep Utah’s natural beauty available to the public
- Declare a state emergency to Save the Great Salt Lake
- Modernize policy to improve our water efficiency and conservation efforts
Fight for Renters and Workers
Lawmakers have rigged the system for landlords and bosses.
Everyday Utahns are working harder than ever and still falling behind; we need to:
- Pass a Renters’ and Workers’ Bill of Rights
- Strengthen unions so the people have more power to band together
