Education
As a longtime educator, Adrianne Mathis believes that every child deserves access to a high-quality education and every teacher deserves the support needed to help students succeed.
Strong schools create stronger communities, and investing in education is one of the most important investments we can make in our future.
Priorities:
- Supporting successful implementation of the Blueprint for Maryland’s Future
- Recruiting and retaining high-quality teachers
- Expanding workforce development opportunities
- Strengthening career and technical education programs
- Ensuring students are prepared for college, careers, and lifelong success
Economic Opportunity
A strong economy begins with strong local communities.
Adrianne is committed to supporting small businesses, creating opportunities for workers, and helping families build financial stability.
Priorities:
- Supporting small business growth and entrepreneurship
- Expanding workforce development and job training programs
- Encouraging local job creation
- Investing in economic opportunities that keep talent in Southern Maryland
- Supporting policies that help working families thrive
Infrastructure & Connectivity
Reliable infrastructure is essential for economic growth, public safety, and quality of life.
Adrianne believes residents should have access to safe roads, dependable transportation, and modern technology regardless of where they live.
Priorities:
- Expanding access to reliable broadband and internet service
- Improving transportation systems and road safety
- Supporting infrastructure investments that prepare our community for future growth
- Strengthening connections between local communities and economic opportunities
