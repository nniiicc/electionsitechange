Supporting Local Businesses & Creating Opportunity
Small businesses are the backbone of our local economy.
As a small business owner herself, Jessica understands the risks entrepreneurs take, the pressures employers face and how important a healthy business climate is to creating good jobs.
Jessica will support policies that:
- Help small businesses start, grow and create jobs.
- Reduce unnecessary bureaucracy.
- Expand workforce and skilled-trades training.
- Connect workers with good-paying careers.
- Invest in roads, transportation, utilities and other infrastructure.
- Ensure our communities receive their fair share of state investment.
Jessica believes Illinois can protect workers, support businesses and grow our economy at the same time.
