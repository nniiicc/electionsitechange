Jessica will work to: Ease the property-tax burden on homeowners.
Oppose unnecessary taxes and fees that make Illinois less affordable.
Protect taxpayers by demanding responsible state spending.
Support policies that help families build financial security.
Make Illinois a more affordable place to live, work and raise a family.
Jessica believes families should be able to put down roots in our communities without wondering whether rising costs will eventually force them out.
