Strong Schools
Every child deserves an excellent education regardless of their ZIP code.
Jessica will fight to protect funding for local schools while making sure state dollars are being spent effectively and responsibly.
She supports:
- Strong public schools.
- Fair state funding for local school districts.
- Supporting teachers and education professionals.
- Career and technical education that prepares students for good-paying careers.
- Partnerships between schools, community colleges, unions and employers.
- Responsible investment that puts students and classrooms first.
Strong schools don’t just benefit students.
They strengthen neighborhoods, protect property values and help communities grow.
