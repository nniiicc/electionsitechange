Responsible & Ethical Government
Trust matters.
Residents should be able to expect that elected officials are spending taxpayer dollars responsibly, making decisions transparently and putting the public interest ahead of politics.
Jessica supports:
- Greater transparency in government.
- Strong ethical standards.
- Responsible budgeting and fiscal discipline.
- Accountability for taxpayer dollars.
- Making government easier for residents and businesses to navigate.
Good government isn’t partisan.
It is about doing the job the right way.
