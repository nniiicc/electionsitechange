Safe Communities & Supporting First Responder
Families deserve to feel safe in their homes, neighborhoods and communities.
Jessica supports the police officers, firefighters, paramedics and emergency personnel who answer the call when residents need them most.
She will work to ensure first responders have the training, staffing, equipment and resources necessary to protect our communities while promoting responsible, effective public safety policies.
Public safety should never be reduced to a political talking point.
It is one of government’s most fundamental responsibilities.
