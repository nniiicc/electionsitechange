Infrastructure & Bringing Resources Home
The 97th District sends tax dollars to Springfield.
Jessica intends to make sure our communities receive their fair share back.
That means advocating for investments in:
- Roads and bridges.
- Water and sewer infrastructure.
- Schools.
- Parks and community facilities.
- Public safety.
- Economic development.
- Transportation.
Jessica will work with local officials and community leaders throughout the district to identify priorities and fight for the resources needed to get them done.
