VOTE Josh TARSKY ON NOVEMBER 3 IMG_7409 IMG_9007 1/6 Support Josh's campaign for re-election $25 $50 $75 $100 $250 Other About Josh US Army Special Forces Josh's commitment to public service began on September 11, 2001, when on one of his first days at Yale University he watched as terrorists attacked Americans on our own soil.
Shortly after graduation he enlisted in the US Army as a Special Forces (Green Beret) Soldier.
During multiple deployments to Afghanistan and South America, Josh became convinced that nothing was more important to a society’s well being than sound laws and a strong education system.
Special Forces Soldiers receive medals for valor Teaching Leaving active duty, Josh matriculated at Harvard University where he received a Master in Education and dedicated himself to fixing America’s education system.
After graduation, Josh taught English in Lawrence during the day and spent his evenings earning a law degree from Suffolk University with help from the GI Bill.
2015 Pat Tillman Foundation Tillman Scholar School Leadership Josh transitioned to education administration and is currently the principal of a grades 6-12 public Middle-High School.
While he has implemented positive changes and policies as a building based principal, he knows he can help more people across the Commonwealth as your State Representative.
Attorney Combining nearly 20 years of military experience with his law degree, Josh currently serves the Massachusetts National Guard as a Judge Advocate General (JAG), assisting soldiers and commanders make decisions based on sound legal reasoning.
Father Josh lives in Needham with his wife and three children.
He enjoys running and hiking through the Needham Town Forest.
