Governor Maura Healey and Lieutenant Governor Kim Driscoll Endorse Democrat Joshua Tarsky for State Representative
NEEDHAM, MA – Governor Healey and Lt.
Governor Driscoll have endorsed Democratic Nominee Josh Tarsky in his campaign to become the next State Representative for the 13th Norfolk District.
“Josh Tarsky has dedicated his life to public service.
As a military veteran, educator, and union leader, Josh has worked to improve lives.
Josh will be a strong partner in our efforts to ensure every student has access to high-quality learning experiences and to invest in a greener future,” said Governor Healey and Lt.
Governor Driscoll.
“We're confident he will deliver for the residents of 13th Norfolk District and our Commonwealth."
Candidate Tarsky had the following to say: “Governor Healey and Lieutenant Governor Driscoll have been brilliantly serving our Commonwealth as its leadership, and it is an honor to have their endorsement.
They have fought hard for every Massachusetts resident, and I look forward to working with them in the future.”
The endorsement from Governor Healey and Lieutenant Governor Driscoll is evidence of Tarsky’s qualifications as a Democrat and his strong support from the Democratic Party.
The General Election is Tuesday, November 5th.
Please visit the campaign website, joshuatarsky.com, for more information.
