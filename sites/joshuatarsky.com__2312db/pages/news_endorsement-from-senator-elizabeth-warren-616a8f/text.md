Senator Elizabeth Warren Endorses Democrat Joshua Tarsky for State Representative
NEEDHAM, MA – United States Senator Elizabeth Warren has endorsed Democratic Nominee Josh Tarsky in his campaign to become the next State Representative for the 13th Norfolk District.
"From his service in the U.S.
Army Special Forces as a Green Beret building girls’ schools and vocational schools in Afghanistan, to his time as an ELA and ESL teacher and teachers’ union leader at Lawrence High, and in his current roles as principal of Holbrook Middle-High School and a Judge Advocate General, Josh has exemplified public service and a commitment to bettering our shared future,” Senator Warren said.
“He is someone we can count on to deliver for Needham, Dover, and Medfield, and I'm proud to support him.”
Candidate Tarsky had this to say: “Senator Warren is a fierce advocate for the American people and the citizens of Massachusetts, and the Commonwealth is much better with her representing us down in our nation’s capital.
I’m honored to have her endorsement.
I’m looking forward to seeing her reelected come November.”
The Senator’s vote of confidence is further evidence of Tarsky’s qualifications, experience, vision for the future, and his abilities to advocate for the 13th Norfolk’s citizens.
Josh Tarsky became the Democratic nominee for the 13th Norfolk on Tuesday, September 3rd after winning in the Democratic primary.
Please visit the campaign website, joshuatarsky.com, for more information.
