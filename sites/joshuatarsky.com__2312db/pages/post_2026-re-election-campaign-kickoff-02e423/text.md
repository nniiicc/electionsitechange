All Posts Press Releases In The News Election 2026 Search 2026 Re-election Campaign KickOff Committee to Re-elect Joshua Tarsky Mar 4 1 min read I’m excited to announce that I am running for reelection as your State Representative for Norfolk’s 13th District.
Serving our communities has been one of the greatest honors of my life.
Together, we’ve made meaningful progress supporting our local schools, improving infrastructure, and advancing initiatives that make our neighborhoods safer, healthier, and stronger.
Our office has been here for you every step of the way: helping families navigate state programs, assisting local businesses, and listening to your ideas at community events, office hours, and meetings at local restaurants and coffee shops across the district.
That commitment to accessibility and service is at the heart of everything we do.
I’m proud of what we’ve accomplished together, and I’m ready to keep building on that progress.
There’s more work to do, and I’m energized for what’s ahead.
I hope you’ll join us as we officially kick off the campaign — details below!
I would love to see you there.
