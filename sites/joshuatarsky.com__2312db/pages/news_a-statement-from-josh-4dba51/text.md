A Statement from Josh The Committee to Elect Joshua Tarsky Oct 14, 2024 Josh Speaks To The Memory of His Mother After a long illness, my mother passed away on Sunday, October 13th.
I will be taking time to focus on my family this week and unfortunately will not be available to participate in the League of Women Voters’ forum.
I’d like to apologize to Mr.
Dermody and the League as I know much preparation and planning has gone into this event.
I’m hopeful we can reschedule for a later date.
Thank you all for your condolences as well as your patience.
I am grateful for the supportive community we share.
