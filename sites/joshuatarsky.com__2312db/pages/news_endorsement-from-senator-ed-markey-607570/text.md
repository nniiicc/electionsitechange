Senator Ed Markey Endorses Democratic Nominee Josh Tarsky for State Representative
NEEDHAM, MA – Senator Ed Markey has endorsed Democratic Nominee Josh Tarsky in his campaign to become the next State Representative for the 13th Norfolk District.
Senator Markey had this to say of the campaign: "I'm pleased to endorse Josh Tarsky for election as State Representative.
Not only has he been a respected leader serving our Nation in uniform but Josh's combined experience as an educator and as an attorney provide him with a unique blend of skills that will make him an excellent legislator for the 13th Norfolk District.
I'm excited to see how he will continue his amazing public service, this time on Beacon Hill, and look forward to partnering with him to help make our Commonwealth a better place in which to live."
"Senator Markey has been a beacon of hope and change for this nation for many years, dating back to his own time as a State Representative," said Tarsky.
"He has embodied what it means to be a true leader, and it is an incredible honor to have his endorsement."
The Senator’s vote of confidence is further evidence of Tarsky’s qualifications, experience, vision for the future, and his abilities to advocate for the 13th Norfolk’s citizens.
Josh Tarsky became the Democratic nominee for the 13th Norfolk on Tuesday, September 3rd after securing a majority of the vote in the Democratic primary.
The General Election is Tuesday, November 5th.
