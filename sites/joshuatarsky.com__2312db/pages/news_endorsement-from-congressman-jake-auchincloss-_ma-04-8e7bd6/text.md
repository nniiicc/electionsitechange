Congressman Jake Auchincloss (MA-04) Endorses Joshua Tarsky for State Representative
NEEDHAM, MA – Congressman Jake Auchincloss (MA-04) has endorsed Democratic Nominee Josh Tarsky in his campaign to become the next State Representative for the 13th Norfolk District.
The 13th Norfolk District, which comprises Needham, Dover, and Precincts 1 and 2 of Medfield, is one of the many state districts that Congressman Auchincloss represents in Washington, D.C.
“Educators are the most impactful public servants in America today.
Josh Tarsky has been both a public-school teacher and principal, grappling with issues of curriculum, teacher burnout, students’ mental health, social media, and more.
His voice and vote on education is important at the State House,” said Congressman Auchincloss.
“I’m proud to endorse this fellow veteran and look forward to working with him on behalf of Needham, Dover, and Medfield.”
Democratic Nominee Tarsky had this to say: “I’ve admired the Congressman’s work since becoming our Representative in 2021, and I rest easy at home knowing that it’s him who advocates for our needs in our nation’s capital.
My family and I are honored by his endorsement, and I look forward to collaborating with him to make our Commonwealth and country stronger.”
The Congressman’s vote of confidence is further evidence of Tarsky’s qualifications, experience, vision for the future, and his abilities to advocate for the 13th Norfolk’s citizens.
Josh Tarsky became the Democratic nominee for the 13th Norfolk on Tuesday, September 3rd after securing a majority of the vote in the Democratic primary.
The General Election is Tuesday, November 5th.
Please visit the campaign website, joshuatarsky.com, for more information.
