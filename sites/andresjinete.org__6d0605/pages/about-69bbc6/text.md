Who Is
Andres Jinete?
Fellow renters know him as a member of Central Jersey Tenant Union and a staunch advocate for rent control and tenant rights.
The business community will tell you how Andres helped them collect signatures and brought their concerns to council when ICE disrupted their livelihood.
Working together with organizers, non-profits and religious institutions, Andres helped build and expand Rapid Response to support families across Central Jersey.
As the cost-of-living skyrocketed in town, Andres carpooled with seniors to the food bank, and raised money for accumulated rent.
He worked at the community library for many years, making programs for adults and families.
Now, Andres is running to be YOUR REPRESENTATIVE in the 12th Congressional District.
His integrity is not for sale.
