Peter for Senate About Connect Get Involved Contact Popular Issues: Affordability Education Economic Development About Welcome to the official campaign homepage for Peter Antinozzi, one of three candidates for NC State Senate - District 32 in 2026.
Making life affordable, lowering taxes, improving healthcare options, increasing teacher pay, expanding school choice, and creating more well‑paying jobs is my agenda for Forsyth County.
A good starting point to learn more about my plans to serve the community effectively is my opening remarks at the senate debate held on June 29, 2026.
Affordability Eliminate sales tax on groceries AND prepared foods.
Permanent 10 cent reduction of the state sales tax on gasoline.
Reduce the citizen burden of personal income taxes for state tax revenues- same tax rate for corporations as individuals.
Support state incentives that create job growth and expand manufacturing in Forsyth County.
Education Increase teacher pay to attract and retain high quality educators.
Reduce spending on corporate curriculum, testing, and personal development consultants.
Retain Opportunity Scholarships to support school choice where state funding follows the student.
Full forensic audit of WS/FCS finances- past and present.
Economic Development My position is effective economic development will require coordinated efforts between Forsyth County’s General Assembly representation, local government, and business partners.
I support state incentives for projects that create jobs, establish affordable housing, retain generational home ownership, and increase manufacturing capabilities.
I do not support state incentives for office buildings, entertainment/luxury housing developments, building public schools on contaminated land, nor state tax exemptions for data centers.
