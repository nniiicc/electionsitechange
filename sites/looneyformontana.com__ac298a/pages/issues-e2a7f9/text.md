Taxes should be minimal, fair, and never used to redistribute wealth.
When government has a surplus, that money should go back to taxpayers.
I oppose a statewide sales tax.
Judicial reform Our justice system must put victims first, and it must be fair, transparent, and accountable to the people it serves.
I've seen it from the inside: in detention with the Lewis and Clark County Sheriff's Office, in the Justice Court, and in Montana courtrooms every week since.
Victims come first.
Victims should be informed and heard at every stage, especially when the accused is released.
Judges, not algorithms.
Decisions about who is released before trial should be made by judges weighing the facts of each case, not by computer risk scores.
Open the books.
Montanans deserve to see how the system performs: release decisions, missed court dates, and repeat offenses.
Public data lets voters judge the courts for themselves.
I'm glad to talk through the details in person.
Call me at (406) 431-2542 or ask when I knock on your door.
Education freedom Parents—not government—should decide how their children are educated.
I support school choice, homeschooling families, and curriculum transparency.
As a homeschooling father of six, I understand firsthand the importance of parental rights in education.
Property rights & housing Private property ownership is a fundamental right.
I will fight to remove regulatory barriers to affordable housing while protecting property owners from government overreach through zoning and eminent domain abuse.
Second Amendment I will defend our God-given right to keep and bear arms without compromise.
I oppose any firearm registry, gun buy-back programs, and so-called "gun free zones" that leave law-abiding citizens defenseless.
Protecting Montana's way of life Our public lands must remain accessible for hunting, fishing, and recreation.
I support state management of our lands and responsible stewardship.
I believe in the sanctity of life and will defend the traditional family as the foundation of our community.
Frequently Asked Questions Why are you running for House District 81?
I'm running because Montana families deserve a representative who understands their challenges firsthand—someone who has served in the military, worked in law enforcement, raised a family here, and built a business in our community.
I want to bring that real-world experience to Helena.
What is your top priority?
Property tax relief and judicial reform.
Families shouldn't be taxed out of homes they already own, and our justice system must be fair, transparent, and accountable to the people it serves.
With seven years in law enforcement and decades of experience in our courts, I've seen what works and what doesn't.
What will you carry to Helena?
I'm a Republican, and these are the positions I'll carry to Helena: property tax relief , courts that put victims first , parental rights in education , Second Amendment protections , and defending Montana's way of life .
