Privacy Policy
Last Updated: November 1, 2025
Holly Jeffreys for HD 86 Campaign is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit.
1.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website:
- Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and other contact details when voluntarily provided (e.g., signing up for updates, donating, or requesting a yard sign).
- Payment Information: If you make a donation, we collect payment details via a secure third-party processor.
- Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing activity on our site, collected through cookies and analytics tools.
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Process donations and comply with campaign finance laws.
- Send campaign updates, event invitations, and voter information.
- Respond to inquiries or volunteer requests.
- Improve our website and campaign outreach.
3.
Sharing of Information
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except in the following cases:
- Legal Compliance: If required by law or campaign finance regulations.
- Service Providers: Trusted third-party vendors that assist with website management, donation processing, and outreach.
- No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
4.
Cookies & Tracking Technologies
Our website may use cookies and analytics tools to enhance user experience and track engagement.
You can adjust your browser settings to disable cookies, but this may affect functionality.
5.
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information, but no website or transmission is 100% secure.
6.
Third-Party Links
Our website may contain links to third-party sites (e.g., donation platforms, social media).
We are not responsible for their privacy policies.
7.
Your Choices
You can unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our messages.
8.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy periodically.
Changes will be posted on this page.
