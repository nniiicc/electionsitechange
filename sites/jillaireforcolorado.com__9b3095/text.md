I am a mom, community volunteer, small business owner, and leader in the fight for democracy nationwide.

With only 89 days to run, I lost by just 110 votes.
I am grateful for the 28,000 neighbors and friends, old and new, who trusted me to bring common sense solutions to politics and to make life more affordable for working families. ​ ​ Now I'm running again because we need a representative who cares about and will stand up for the issues our community values. ​ ​ ​ ​ Jillaire wants to know what issues are most important to you.

Access to Health Care Access to high quality health care and the privacy to make one’s own health care decisions are critical matters for our next state representative.
I support policies that protect access to reproductive care, promote enhanced health care coverage, and invest in increasing the number of health care providers.
Civility Since 2017 I ’ ve been engaging in tough conversations about the controversial topics facing our nation with community members who don ’ t always see eye to eye.

By leading out with civility, I've opened pathways to communication that invite productive conversation and positive action.
Gun Violence Prevention This is an issue that I really care about.
I have four children who grew up in the post-Columbine world experiencing active shooter drills.
I was in a kindergarten classroom many years ago during one of those drills.
It was scary for me, and it’s scary for our kids.
