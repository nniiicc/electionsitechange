Jillaire on the Issues
JILLAIRE BELIEVES IN
Civility
As your state representative, I will not back down from taking on the issues that matter to the residents of HD19.
Since 2017 I’ve been engaging in tough conversations surrounding the controversial topics facing our nation with community members who don't always see eye to eye.
By leading out with civility and respect, I’ve opened pathways to communication that invite productive conversation and positive action.
As a leader in a nonpartisan nonprofit dedicated to ethical government, my main political experience has been working with people from all along the political spectrum.
As your legislator, I will stand up for the important issues our community cares about while listening and communicating respectfully with anyone who engages in good faith.
Most candidates say that they can work across the aisle, but I actually have experience doing it—while maintaining my commitment to progressive values.
In a district like HD19, where more than 50% of voters are unaffiliated, I believe this is a skill that is really needed.
To demonstrate my commitment to civility, I signed the Colorado Democratic Party's Clean Primary Campaign Pledge.
- I will run a clean and truthful campaign.
- My campaign will be based on facts and issues.
- I will focus on my vision for this office and what I have to offer.
- I will focus on my values, identifying problems in the community and my ideas for solving them.
- Identifying differences on policies, platform or positions is fair if factual and not distorted.
