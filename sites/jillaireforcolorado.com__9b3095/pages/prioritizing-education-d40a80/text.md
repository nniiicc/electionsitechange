I am passionate about ensuring that our public education system serves all of Colorado's children and teachers. ​ Right now we’re funding schools at 1989 levels, and that’s just not good enough for 2026.
We are asking schools to do more — in terms of testing, social/emotional health, and safety, to name a few areas.
Students and teachers need access to new technology.
School psychologists need lower caseloads.
Teachers need better pay.
Public money needs to stay in public schools. ​ HD19 schools are among the best funded in the state, but given the risk to funding with each budget cycle, we need to seek more stable solutions.
Funding education requires a variety of solutions and ongoing dialogue with everyone affected.
My experience bringing people together for nuanced, solutions-based conversations around education policy and funding is one of the reasons I am running to be your representative.
