About the event
Join Congressman Joe Neguse, local elected officials, and other community members in support of Jillaire McMillan.
Host committee members include Erie City Councilor Emily Baer, former Colorado Democratic Party Chair Pat Waak, Rep Jennifer Bacon, Rep Andy Boesenecker, Rep Kyle Brown, Rep Junie Joseph, Rep Karen McCormick, Colorado Democratic Party Chair Shad Murib, Nancy George, Reilly Jackson, and Deb Zuniga.
The Congressman and Jillaire will speak and answer questions at about 1pm.
The address of the event will be shared two days before the event with those who have RSVPed.
