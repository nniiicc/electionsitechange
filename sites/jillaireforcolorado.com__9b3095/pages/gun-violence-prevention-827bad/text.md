I have four children who grew up in the post-Columbine world experiencing active shooter drills.
I was in a kindergarten classroom many years ago during one of those drills.
It was scary for me, and it’s scary for our kids.
I want guns to stay out of schools.
I want to maintain current concealed carry permit standards.
We need to keep guns out of the hands of our children and those who pose a threat to themselves and others — even when that’s just temporarily.
We do not need military-grade guns in the hands of civilians.
All of these reforms can be accomplished without infringing on the Second Amendment rights of the many law-abiding gun owners in HD19. ​ To continue to make progress on common sense gun laws in Colorado, we need someone who can bring all constituents and legislators together from across the political spectrum to engage respectfully.
I am that candidate.
I have the skills and experience to have those necessary conversations in a way that will lead to understanding and results.
