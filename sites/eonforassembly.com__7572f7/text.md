About Eon Huntley
Eon is a father, retail worker, lifelong Brooklynite, democratic socialist and three-term PTA president.
He’s running for State Assembly to win universal childcare, freeze the rent, and build a New York City every family can afford.
Eon has always been a tenant, growing up in East New York and in NYCHA’s Pink Houses.
His father immigrated from Guyana while his mother is a second-generation Brooklynite.
He attended New York City public schools and the Parsons School of Design before working as a fashion designer and clothing salesman.
As a member of the bargaining committee for Workers United Local 340, Eon fought for and won crucial healthcare coverage for his fellow workers.
Eon guided his daughters’ school community through the COVID years as a three-term PTA president, co-founding a mobile food pantry that fed hundreds of Brooklynites every month.
He and his wife Rebecca, a longtime public school teacher, have lived in Bedford Stuyvesant for 18 years.
Eon is running for State Assembly to fight for the affordable, beautiful Brooklyn we all deserve.
He believes nobody should be priced out of basic human experiences like raising a family, pursuing higher education, growing old in our own homes, or even going to the doctor.
Eon answers only to the voters and rejects all corporate donations.
In Albany, you can trust him to fight for universal childcare, fully-funded public education, and freedom from Trump’s assaults on our civil liberties and our neighbors.
Our District
Assembly District 56 includes the beautiful and dynamic Brooklyn neighborhoods of Bedford Stuyvesant and Crown Heights.
A rich legacy of Black power & culture permeates its iconic brownstones, tree-lined blocks and bustling streets.
We gather with neighbors on stoops and in parks.
We swim in community pools and frequent our local small businesses and restaurants.
Our community is beautiful, but it’s being squeezed every day by landlords, bosses, and privatizers.
In District 56, we know how to look out for our neighbors and put our community first.
We deserve representation that does the same.
