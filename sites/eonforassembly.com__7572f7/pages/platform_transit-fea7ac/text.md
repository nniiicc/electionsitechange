Transit
As residents of Bed-Stuy and Crown Heights, we deserve trains and buses that provide frequent, reliable, and safe service.
We need to fix the MTA, make our buses fast and free, and build a network of protected bike lanes in our neighborhood.
World-class transit, in combination with common-sense street design to minimize traffic accidents, will make the streets around our schools and parks safer for children and families while creating a more accessible district for all of us.
