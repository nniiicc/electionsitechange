The American Israel Public Affairs Committee is a lobbying group that pushes for strong U.S. and Israel relations and heavily influences policy in Washington D.C.
Through its affiliated PACs, it spent over $100 million in the 2022 election cycle, and reports show a majority of sitting members of Congress, including roughly 60 percent of the House, have received support from AIPAC-backed funding networks at some point.
Roughly 60% of the House has received AIPAC-backed funding, while the United States has delivered over $150 billion in aid to Israel, with AIPAC spending only a fraction of that amount to influence U.S. politicians.
How much influence does AIPAC have AIPAC’s influence comes from its ability to organize donors, shape messaging, and support candidates across both parties.
Its network has helped direct significant funding into key races, often backing candidates who align with its policy goals and opposing those who do not.
The bigger picture Since World War II, the United States has provided Israel with over $150 billion in aid, while pro-Israel lobbying groups like AIPAC have spent a fraction of that amount on political influence in Washington.
Critics point to this imbalance as a sign of outsized influence relative to cost, while supporters argue it reflects a strategic alliance backed by shared interests.
