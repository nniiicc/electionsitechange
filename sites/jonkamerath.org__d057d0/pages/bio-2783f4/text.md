Biography Jon Kamerath has been happily married to his wife, Eileen, for over 22 years, and has been a resident of Clark County for 30 years.
He is a man of strong faith, and has been a Sunday School teacher, and a volunteer financial clerk for his church.
He has a profound respect for the Founding Fathers and the Constitution.
He graduated from the Practical Nursing program of the Community College of Southern Nevada in 2004.
Jon understands human suffering and ministering to it, having been a nurse for several years; he retired in 2023.
Jon was a member of the US Chess Federation.
When he was a member of his high school chess team, they won the Utah State Championship.
He has participated in 3 triathlons and has run 21 marathons.
