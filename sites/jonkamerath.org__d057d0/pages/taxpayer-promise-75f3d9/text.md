A Promise to the Taxpayers
I promise to the people of Nevada, and to the American people, that I will:
- Vote against any bill that will increase income taxes, inheritance taxes, and any other form of taxation.
- Vote against any bill that adds to the national debt (which is over 40,000,000,000,000!).
- Fight against any attempt to rob the honest, hard-working American people of their hard-earned money.
*I will not accept any donations whatsoever, because I know how much we are all struggling in these hard times when most of us are living paycheck to paycheck.
Sincerely,
Jon Kamerath
Candidate, NV Congress Dist. 3
