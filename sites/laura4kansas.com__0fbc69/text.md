As your neighbor, I am devoted to bringing our community’s voice to Topeka.
Your Voice for 26 Born in California, raised in Nebraska, and now living in the Free State, I fell in love with Kansas and the community I found here.
From the sunflowers, to the history, and especially the Kansas sunsets, I find a new reason to feel lucky every day.
My parents were both teachers who taught me the value of working to serve others.
In my life, I have worked as a childcare worker, a catering server, and now as an administrative assistant where I work every day to solve accountants’ problems.
All three jobs made me a professional at giving voice to people’s problems and conflicts and finding solutions.
I hope to do the same for my community, where so many feel unheard.
Priorities Education – Kansas must properly invest in our community’s future and ensure public schools have the resources they need.
Health – I want to work with other legislators to increase access to affordable and preventative healthcare so our community can thrive.
Data Centers – We can and must ensure that our local environment is protected, that zoning laws work for us, and resist being exploited by large companies with no accountability.
