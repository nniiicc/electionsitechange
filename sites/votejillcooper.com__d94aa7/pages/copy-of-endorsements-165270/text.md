REP.
COOPER ON THE ISSUES PRIORITIES FOR A STRONGER PA Times are tough and so are the challenges currently plaguing our commonwealth, but Rep.
Cooper believes in the strength and resiliency of the people of Pennsylvania, and her priorities reflect a common-sense approach to the issues that affect all of us.
That's why she's fighting every day to deliver real results for the people of the 55th Legislative District.
THE ECONOMY, JOBS, & INFLATION Fighting Inflation: I am committed to reducing the financial burden on Pennsylvania families by supporting policies that keep taxes low and curb unnecessary government spending.
Job Creation: By cutting red tape and investing in local businesses, I will continue to create opportunities for good-paying jobs right here in Westmoreland County.
Pro-Business Environment: I believe in fostering a strong, competitive marketplace by supporting small businesses and removing barriers to entrepreneurship.
THE BORDER CRISIS Securing Our Border: I am committed to supporting border states as they combat the dangerous flow of illegals into our country.
We can't afford to allow Pennsylvania to become a proxy border state, and we must do our part to prevent our communities from falling victim to violent criminals and deadly drugs.
Ending Sanctuary Policies: I will work to end sanctuary city policies in Pennsylvania that undermine federal immigration law and put our citizens at risk.
Upholding the Rule of Law: I stand with law enforcement and immigration officials, ensuring they have the tools and resources needed to enforce the law effectively.
STANDING UP FOR SENIORS, VETERANS, & LAW ENFORCEMENT Protecting Seniors: I will continue to fight for legislation that protects the dignity and financial security of our seniors, including access to affordable healthcare and prescription drugs.
Honoring Our Veterans: Veterans deserve our utmost respect and support.
I will continue to advocate for expanding veteran healthcare programs, job training, and mental health services.
Supporting Law Enforcement: I am proud to stand with our police and first responders.
I am committed to ensuring they have the resources and respect they need to keep our communities safe, and will always oppose reckless attempts to defund our police.
HARNESSING PENNSYLVANIA ENERGY Energy Independence: Pennsylvania's natural resources are crucial to our energy independence.
I will continue to support policies that responsibly harness our energy resources and reduce reliance on foreign oil.
Affordable Energy: I am committed to ensuring energy prices remain low for Pennsylvania families by supporting the production of clean coal, natural gas, and other energy resources.
Environmental Stewardship: I believe in a balanced approach to energy that prioritizes both job creation and environmental responsibility, ensuring a brighter, more sustainable future.
CULTIVATING EXCELLENCE IN EDUCATION Parental Rights in Education: I support empowering parents with greater control over their children’s education, ensuring that families have a say in what is being taught in our schools.
Improving School Choice: Every child deserves a high-quality education.
I will continue to fight for policies that expand school choice, giving parents more options for their children’s success.
Focusing on Results: Our education system should focus on results and accountability.
I will work to ensure that funding goes to classrooms, not bureaucratic overhead, improving student outcomes and increasing test scores across the board so we can remain competitive.
SAFEGUARDING OUR RIGHTS & FREEDOMS Protecting the 2nd Amendment: I am a staunch defender of every law-abiding Pennsylvanian's constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
I will continue to stand against any attempt to infringe upon our constitutionally protected 2nd Amendment rights.
Defending Free Speech: Freedom of speech is a cornerstone of our constitutional republic, and I will fight to protect it, especially in the face of increasing censorship on social media and in public institutions.
Religious Freedom: I believe in protecting the religious freedoms that form the bedrock of our nation's values, ensuring that all Americans can practice their faith without fear of persecution.
