"It's easy to be a conservative.

Bob Titus is your State Representative for Missouri’s 139th District — serving the western portion of Christian County, including Nixa, Clever, and Billings.
After earning the trust of voters, Bob is proudly serving in Jefferson City to defend Missouri values and fight for the freedoms our families rely on.
Bob’s Priorities for Missouri:
- Expanding school choice and educational freedom
- Defending the right to bear arms
- Standing up to Big Tech censorship
- Advancing strong pro-life policies
- Fully supporting and funding law enforcement
Bob is a devoted husband, father of 10, and proud grandfather.
For 30 years, he has owned and operated a local family business.
He holds an accounting degree from Missouri State University (MSU) and is an active member of the Missouri Republican Assembly (MRA).
Representative Bob Titus brings real-world experience, unwavering conservative convictions, and a deep commitment to serving the people of the 139th District.
