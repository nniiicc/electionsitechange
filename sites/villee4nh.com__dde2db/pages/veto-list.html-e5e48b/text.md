Veto List
If I'm elected Governor, here's a list of things I would veto:
- Any sales tax or state income tax, or any other new tax, or any tax increase.
- Any return to the licensing requirement for carrying a concealed pistol or revolver.
That is, any repeal of constitutional carry.
- Any abortion ban that doesn't include an exception for rape at least in the first trimester, or at any time to protect the life of the mother.
I encourage all gubernatorial candidates to make their veto list public.
This may be the single most important thing a voter needs to know about a candidate.
