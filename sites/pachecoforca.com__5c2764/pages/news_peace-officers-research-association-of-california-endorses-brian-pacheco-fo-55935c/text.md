PRESS RELEASE
Peace Officers Research Association of California Endorses Brian Pacheco For Assembly
85,000 Public Safety Officials Stand Behind the County Supervisor for District 27 campaign
Fresno County Supervisor Brian Pacheco has earned the endorsement of the Peace Officers Research Association of California (PORAC), a labor organization representing more than 85,000 public safety members statewide, in his campaign for Assembly District 27.
PORAC's endorsement reflects Pacheco's track record of supporting public safety professionals and strengthening communities throughout the Central Valley.
"Supervisor Pacheco has consistently demonstrated his commitment to the law enforcement community and the safety of our neighborhoods," said Brian Marvel, PORAC President.
"His leadership in Fresno County has made a real difference for our members and the families we serve.
PORAC is proud to endorse his campaign and look forward to partnering with him to advance policies that protect our officers and ensure public safety remains a priority."
"I'm grateful for PORAC's endorsement and their commitment to keeping our communities safe," said Supervisor Pacheco.
"Strong communities depend on well-resourced law enforcement agencies and officers who have the support they need to do their jobs effectively.
I look forward to working with PORAC to advance policies that strengthen public safety and give our officers the resources and tools necessary to keep our neighborhoods safe."
This endorsement marks a significant milestone in Pacheco's campaign, demonstrating strong support from public safety officials.
Pacheco's campaign has secured backing from major labor organizations including the Fresno Deputy Sheriff's Association, the California Federation of Labor Unions, and California Teachers Association, as well as endorsements from Assembly Speaker Robert Rivas, Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria, and Congressmen Jim Costa and Adam Gray.
