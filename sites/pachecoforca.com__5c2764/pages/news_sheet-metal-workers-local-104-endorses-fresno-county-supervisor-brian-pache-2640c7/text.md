SMW Local Union 104’s endorsement underscores Pacheco’s commitment to supporting working families as a driving force of the Central Valley.
"County Supervisor Pacheco understands what working families need to succeed," said Rick Werner, SMW Local Union 104 President.
"He's proven it through his work in Fresno County.
SMW Local Union 104 is proud to endorse his campaign and looks forward to working together to ensure our members have access to good jobs and the support they deserve." “I’m grateful for the Sheet Metal Workers’ Local 104 endorsement,” said Supervisor Pacheco.
“Our communities depend on the hard work and expertise of tradeworkers every single day.
I'm committed to supporting policies that ensure our workers have access to the resources they need to build strong futures for themselves and for our communities.” This endorsement adds significant momentum to Pacheco’s campaign, signaling strong backing from union leaders in California.
Pacheco's campaign has secured backing from major labor organizations including the California Federation of Labor Unions, California Teachers Association and the Fresno Deputy Sheriff's Association, as well as endorsements from Assembly Speaker Robert Rivas, Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria, and Congressmen Jim Costa and Adam Gray.
