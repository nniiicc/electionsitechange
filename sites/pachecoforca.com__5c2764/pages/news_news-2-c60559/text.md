Fresno County Supervisor Brian Pacheco Secures Endorsement from Fresno Deputy Sheriff's Association
Today, the Fresno Deputy Sheriff's Association (FDSA) announced its endorsement of Fresno County Supervisor Brian Pacheco in his race for Assembly District 27.
Pacheco has worked closely with local law enforcement throughout his service as a County Supervisor, supporting public safety resources, effective coordination across agencies, and a commonsense approach to keeping neighborhoods safe
“I’m grateful for the Fresno Deputy Sheriff’s Association’s support, and I’m proud to earn the endorsement of the deputies who are out there every day protecting our families,” said Brian Pacheco.
“As a County Supervisor, I’ve worked side-by-side with law enforcement on the real issues we face—public safety staffing, emergency response, and making sure deputies have the tools and support they need to do their jobs.
In Sacramento, I’ll bring that same practical approach, and I’ll always have their back.”
The FDSA's endorsement reflects Brian Pacheco’s proven commitment to working alongside public safety officials to address the Valley's most pressing needs.
A fourth-generation dairy farmer and longtime local leader, Pacheco has earned a reputation for listening and getting things done and he's ready to bring that same approach to District 27.
Fresno County Supervisor Brian Pacheco has spent decades serving his community, from the Kerman Unified School District Board to leadership roles at Community Regional Medical Center.
His record has earned him the support of current District 27 Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria, Speaker of the Assembly Robert Rivas, Congressman Adam Gray, the California Democratic Party, and other leaders across the Central Valley.
