A ribbon cutting ceremony will be held on Tuesday, July 1 at 5:15 PM at 2503 Lake Road, Suite A-100.
“It has been an honor to serve the residents of House District 12 during my first legislative session in the Texas House,” said Rep.
Wharton.
“We achieved meaningful results for our district—cutting property taxes, investing in water infrastructure, passing common-sense bail reform, and supporting the men and women of law enforcement.
Opening this district office is another important step toward providing direct, accessible service to the people we represent.” The Huntsville District Office will be open Monday through Thursday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and will serve as a resource for constituents seeking assistance with state agencies, legislative questions, or other district-related matters.
Representative Wharton’s district staff includes: -Patti Utley, District Director -Julie Porter, Constituent Services Director -J.T.
Langley, Constituent Services -Makenna McDaniel, Legislative Aide -Michelle Moya, Legislative Aide Constituents can contact the office by phone at (936) 649-0510, or visit in person at 2503 Lake Road, Suite A-100, Huntsville, TX 77340.
Later this year, Representative Wharton will also launch mobile district office hours to serve residents across all counties in House District 12, ensuring more convenient access to services and support.
“JoLynne and I invite everyone from across House District 12 to join us for the ribbon cutting on Tuesday, July 1 at 5:15 PM.
We look forward to welcoming the community, introducing our staff, and officially opening the doors to this new chapter of constituent service,” said Wharton.
