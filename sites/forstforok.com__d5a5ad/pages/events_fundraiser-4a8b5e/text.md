Fundraiser Mon, Oct 12 | The Summit, The Overlook Room Join us for a fundraiser event for State Senate Candidates in our area.
REGISTER NOW Time & Location Oct 12, 2026, 5:30 PM – 7:00 PM The Summit, The Overlook Room, 15 W 6th St, Tulsa, OK 74119, USA About The Event Join us for an evening supporting local leadership at an upcoming campaign fundraiser benefiting Oklahoma State Senate candidates, including Aaron Forst, Nominee for Senate District 34.
The reception takes place on Monday, October 12, 2026, from 5:30 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. in The Overlook Room at The Summit Club, located at 15 W.
6th St. in Tulsa, OK.
The event features special guests including Mike Mazzei, Republican Nominee for Governor; Congressman Kevin Hern, Nominee for U.S.
Senate; President Pro Tempore Lonnie Paxton; and Representative Mark Tedford, Nominee for OKCD-1.
In addition to Aaron Forst, proceeds from the evening benefit fellow State Senate leaders and candidates Senator Bill Coleman, Senator Grant Green, Senator Todd Gollihare, Senator John Haste, Senator Bryan Logan, and Senator Ally Seifried.
Suggested contribution tiers for each candidate are $3,500, $2,500, $1,000, $500, $250, and $100, though all contributions are welcome.
