Activism Aaron Serves Our Community HELPING THOSE IN NEED I am committed to supporting families in our community, which is why I proudly partner with the Owasso Community Resources team.
Whether it is gathering donations, helping stock shelves, or stepping in wherever they need an extra hand, I believe in showing up for the people who rely on these services.
Together, we can make a real difference for local families.
SHOWING KIDS THE LOVE OF CHRIST I have always believed in investing in the next generation, which is why I volunteer my time working with children as a coach in elementary programs at church.
Helping kids grow in confidence, character, and teamwork is one of the most rewarding parts of what I do.
Supporting them today builds a stronger community for tomorrow.
SPEAKING WITH OUR LEADERS I make it a priority to speak directly with our leaders so our community has a real voice at the table.
I have met with Congressman Kevin Hern, Senator James Lankford, Lt Gov Matt Pinnell, along with many others to discuss issues that matter to Oklahoma families, making sure our concerns are heard and represented.
Staying involved at this level helps me advocate effectively for the people I serve.
COMMUNITY INVOLVMENT Connecting with people across our community is one of my favorite parts of public service.
I make it a point to get out, meet residents where they are, listen to their experiences, and speak at local gatherings whenever I am invited.
These conversations help me understand what matters most to families and allow me to share ideas that move our community forward.
INVESTING IN THE NEXT GENERATION Investing in the next generation is something I take seriously.
I actively participate in programs that help high school students prepare for life beyond graduation, including school activities and youth retreats.
Spending time with these students, encouraging their goals, and helping them build skills for the future is one of the most rewarding parts of my work.
