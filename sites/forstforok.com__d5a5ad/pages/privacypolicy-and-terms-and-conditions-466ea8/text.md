Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions– Forst for State Senate
Forst for State Senate (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our campaign, including through our website, forms, and text messaging programs.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
- Name
- Phone number
- Email address
- Mailing address (if provided)
- Any other information you voluntarily provide
We may also collect limited technical information such as IP address or browser type when you visit our website.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Send campaign updates, alerts, and newsletters
- Provide event invitations and volunteer opportunities
- Process donations
- Respond to inquiries
- Send SMS/text messages if you opt in
Text Messaging (SMS) Policy
By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Forst for State Senate, including automated messages.
- Message frequency may vary
- Message and data rates may apply
- Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time
- Reply HELP for assistance
SMS opt-in information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
Information Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share your information only:
- With trusted service providers who assist in campaign operations (such as texting platforms or email services)
- When required by law or legal process
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.
Your Choices
You may:
- Opt out of emails by using the unsubscribe link
- Opt out of text messages by replying STOP
- Contact us to update or remove your information
Third-Party Links
Our website or messages may contain links to third-party sites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites.
Children’s Privacy
Our campaign does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Updates will be posted with a revised effective date.
