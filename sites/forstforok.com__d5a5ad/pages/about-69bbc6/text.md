ABOUT ME
Meet Aaron Forst
WHERE AARON COMES FROM
A Lifelong Oklahoman with Deep Roots and a Heart for Service
Aaron proudly hails from Oklahoma, where he was born and raised.
Growing up in the small town of Verdigris, He earned an honors diploma and was also honored to be one of the first 54 graduates of Verdigris High School—a foundation that taught him the value of hard work, faith, and community.
Driven by a passion for learning and service, Aaron pursued degrees in Emergency Medical Services, Biomedical Engineering, Nanotechnology, and Business Administration—studying at Tulsa Community College, Rogers State University, and Purdue University.
This diverse education reflects his commitment to both people and progress.
For over eleven years, his family has called Owasso home.
Aaron loves the city’s energy and opportunity, while still cherishing its small-town heart.
Aaron is proud to be part of this community, & even more proud to serve it.
AN ACCOMPLISHED CAREER
Aaron learned the value of hard work getting his first job at 14 as a paperboy for the Tulsa World, a role he held through high school and most of college.
Aaron began his professional career as an Emergency Medical Technician, where he developed a lasting appreciation for the bravery and sacrifice of our first responders.
Today, He honored to be endorsed by The Owasso Fire Union.
Aaron's path later led him into Biomedical Engineering, where he had the privilege of helping design and implement medical equipment during the construction of both St.
John Owasso and St.
John Broken Arrow.
These experiences deepened his understanding of the healthcare system and its critical role in our communities.
Eventually, Aaron stepped into entrepreneurship, launching two startup small businesses that have created more than 30 jobs right here in the Owasso area.
Both of which continue to thrive.
He credits every step of his success to his faith in the Lord and the lifelong lessons of hard work, dedication, and sweat equity.
A HEART FOR PUBLIC SERVICE
For Aaron, volunteering isn’t just a good deed, it’s a calling.
The Lord calls us to help others, and He has committed his life to answering that call.
He proudly served as a Watch D.O.G.S.
Dad at a local elementary school, coaches kids at First Church in Owasso where he help teach young minds about the love of Christ, and he facilitated a monthly men’s group to encourage spiritual growth and accountability.
Every summer Aaron goes with our church to CO where we take over 100 high school age children camping and hiking in the mountains to learn more about Jesus.
His church also partners with Habitat for Humanity, and Aaron has had the blessing of getting his hands dirty—literally—helping frame homes for families in need.
Beyond the church, Aaron is an active member of the Owasso Chamber of Commerce and serve on the Advocate Owasso Committee, where he works to represent small business interests and engage on state and local policy issues.
He and his wife have also had the privilege of fostering three amazing children.
That experience opened their eyes to real issues within the DHS system, leading them to speak at the Capitol and partner with state lawmakers to push for much-needed reform.
Through every season—whether in business, ministry, or advocacy—Aaron has learned that real leadership begins with service.
FUN FACTS
Aaron holds a Black Belt in Taekwondo.
In 2008, Aaron rode his motorcycle all the way to the West coast and while he was there, he ended up as a contestant on The Price is Right, where he actually won a car!
A few years later, Aaron won a motorcycle in a church raffle.
Some people say he is luck but Aaron just call it blessed.
Aaron is also proud of his rich heritage.
He is a direct descendant of Robert the Bruce, the legendary King of Scotland, and he has ancestors who came to America on the Mayflower.
While he is rooted in history, his heart is firmly focused on building a better future for Oklahoma.
