Kennebunkport, ME (207) 604-3491 Donate Home About Events Issues Join Us Contact Education Home / Education America’s schools are failing, and our children are paying the price.
A strong education system is essential for our nation’s future prosperity and global competitiveness.
Every child deserves a high-quality education and the opportunity to meet rigorous standards in core subjects.
Despite ranking fifth in the world for education funding, the United States ranks just 24th in science, 39th in math, and 39th in reading.
We must do better if we want our kids to compete and succeed in a global economy.
Parents deserve a stronger voice in their children’s education.
They should be treated as partners, not bystanders.
We need to get back to the basics: math, science, history, and English , so students graduate ready for college, trade school, or the workforce.
Investing in our children means investing in America’s future.
But we can’t just pour more money into a system that isn’t improving.
We must measure results, reward what works, and embrace reforms like school choice to ensure every student has the best shot at success.
