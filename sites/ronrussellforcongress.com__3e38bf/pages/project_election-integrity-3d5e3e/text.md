Americans must believe in and trust our elections.
Restoring faith in America’s election system is paramount to preserving our democratic principles and ensuring the integrity of our republic.
No citizen should ever doubt that any U.S. federal, state or local level is secure.
We can ensure the integrity of our elections, without depriving individuals of their right to vote.
Reforms such as voter ID are common-sense measures that ensure that every eligible citizen trusts that there vote counts and that the outcomes reflect the true will of the people.
All across Maine, I hear stories of how Mainers’ feel like their vote doesn’t matter.
I spent a career in the military to ensure that we could hold elections.
Restoring faith in our election system is not just about fixing processes; it’s about reaffirming the foundational belief that each American’s voice matters and that our democracy is resilient and just.
