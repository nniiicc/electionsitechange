District Information
The Creative Corridor
The Main Street Corridor features vibrant artwork, local eateries, and trendy shops.
Skyline
Beautiful at sunrise, sunset, and every minute in between, Little Rock’s skyline offers a glimpse of the growing business and entrepreneurship in our state.
UAMS
University of Arkansas for Medical Sciences (UAMS) is the state’s largest public employer and features cutting edge research labs, health care facilities, and education programs.
Pinnacle Mountain
Located on the western side of the district, Pinnacle Mountain State Park represents Arkansas’s diverse and spectacular terrain.
Arkansas State Capitol
District 32 features the center of government, policy, and politics in our state — the Arkansas State Capitol.
Hillcrest
The Hillcrest Historic District neighborhood features wonderful turn-of-the-century homes, restaurants, and shops.
Riverfront Park
Little Rock’s Riverfront Park offers an abundance of outdoor activities, sights, and infrastructure marvels like the Big Dam Bridge.
War Memorial
Since 1948, War Memorial Stadium has brought Arkansans from every corner of the state together for sporting events, concerts, and more.
Chenal Promenade
The Chenal Promenade is a premier location in Central Arkansas for shopping, entertainment, and eating local.
Roberts Elementary
High quality Little Rock School District facilities.
