Health Care
Brad Chambers supports single-payer healthcare for PA & lower insurance & prescription costs.
In the Pennsylvania House of Representatives, Brad will:
- Advocate for a single-payer system that will lower healthcare costs AND render workplace negotiations less fraught in Pennsylvania.
- Fight ANY attempt to take healthcare choices out of the hands of individual Pennsylvanians and put them into the hands of lawmakers.
- Work to form a prescription drug review board to push for lower drug prices on behalf of all Pennsylvanians.
- Work to codify Women’s reproductive rights into the Pennsylvania Constitution.
- Support common-sense healthcare reform that will lessen the burden born by families to allow all Pennsylvanians to live happier, healthier lives.
