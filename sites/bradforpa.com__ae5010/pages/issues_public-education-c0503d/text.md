Public Education
Brad Chambers supports fair funding for our public schools & lower property taxes for homeowners.
In the Pennsylvania House of Representatives, Brad will:
- Reduce reliance on property taxes by correcting the funding disparity that currently allows Pennsylvania to pay 10% less into K-12 education than other states.
- Evaluate the Basic Education Funding formula to make sure public schools in the 41st and all of Lancaster County get their fair share.
- Expand the PA Student Teacher Support Program, which provides an income to teachers-in-training who agree to stay and work in Pennsylvania for at least 3 years.
- Secure funding for expanded trade and vocational training programs in Lancaster County.
