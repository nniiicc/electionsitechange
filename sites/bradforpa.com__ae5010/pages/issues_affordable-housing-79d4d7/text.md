Affordable Housing
Brad Chambers supports quality housing that Lancaster County families can actually afford.
In the Pennsylvania House of Representatives, Brad will:
- Support First-time Homebuyers Savings Accounts to help young adults and renters prepare and save for future homeownership.
- Increase funding for affordable housing non-profits focused on both building and financing for first-time homebuyers and low-to-middle income families, such as the PHARE program.
- Expand support for the Whole Home Repairs program to alleviate the administrative backlog preventing PA homeowners and landlords accessing these crucial funds.
- Incentivize developers to build more affordable housing units and mixed-income neighborhoods.
- Fight for better enforcement of laws designed to safeguard tenants’ rights, prevent unjust evictions, and protect tenants from negligent or predatory landlords.
- Champion proven programs that support at-risk families and keep our unhoused population safe, putting them back on a path to self-sufficiency.
