HD-41—comprising Columbia Borough, East and West Hempfield Townships, Mountville Borough, and part of Manor Township— is recognized as Lancaster County’s most competitive State House District.
Chambers previously ran in 2024 in a campaign that knocked more than 26,000 doors.
Chambers, a labor administrator and community advocate, intends to run on a campaign that fights for housing that is affordable for all, the funding our schools and children deserve, and lifesaving universal healthcare coverage.
Chambers has also obtained a number of significant endorsements from across the 41st District and Lancaster County.
Brad Chamber’s endorsements include: State Representative Ismail Smith-Wade-El (HD-49) - “Brad is my first endorsement of the 2026 cycle, and that’s no accident.
People in Lancaster are tired of politicians who have a lot of bark, but are leashed to their corporate donors.

Brad’s unwavering loyalty to his community, his outstanding work ethic, and his commitment to serving absolutely all of his neighbors earn him a spot on the ballot and the opportunity to serve in the PA General Assembly.” County Commissioner Alice Yoder - “It is my pleasure to endorse Brad Chambers for PA House in District 41.
Brad is hardworking and committed to serving his community and I know he will be an excellent representative for his district— which includes me!

I have full confidence that when elected, as he is ALREADY doing, Brad will tirelessly fight to bring equitable education funding, health care reform, and affordable housing to benefit his district constituents and all Pennsylvanians.
