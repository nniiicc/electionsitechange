Brad is ready to fight for people-focused solutions to Pennsylvania’s challenges and against elected officials who have prioritized special interests and political parties over hardworking families.
The status quo has let us down for over a decade and it's time for leaders who are willing to confront inequalities in education, housing, and healthcare in Pennsylvania.
Working Class Roots.
Brad Chambers has felt the struggles of working folks firsthand and witnessed the failure of our elected officials to address them.
Brad’s mother, Michelle, a hardworking single mom, often worked multiple jobs and long hours to provide for their family.
As the oldest of three brothers, Brad was often left in charge of his siblings and earned a sense of duty and responsibility from a very young age.
Real Life Experience.
Brad Chambers understands the urgent and pervasive challenges we face in Pennsylvania because he’s grappled with them in his own life.
After graduating from high school, Brad earned a Bachelor’s degree from Millersville University.
However, even with a college education, Brad faced challenges finding work in a depleted job market and subsequently found himself unable to afford a place to live or a visit to the doctor.
It shouldn’t take a lucky break to get by in Pennsylvania.
Brad was able to land on his feet but he knows that many Pennsylvanians aren’t so fortunate.
With the support of family and friends, as well as determination, hustle, a bit of luck, and helpful state programs, Brad was able to land on his feet, find a good job, and eventually became a homeowner in Columbia, PA.
But Brad Chambers understands that many in Lancaster County struggle to find affordable housing for years , often leading to prolonged instability that prevents those families from thriving.
Brad currently works in labor administration, connecting directly with working folks and witnessing the challenges we face in today’s economy day in and day out.
Why Brad is running.
Brad Chambers is running to represent us in the State House to address the very issues he, his loved ones, and his community have faced and continue to face every day in Lancaster County.
His vision is the Pennsylvania we deserve; one where no one has to wonder if they’re going to have a roof over their head, if they can afford their medication, or if their children will have the tools they need to succeed.
That’s what Brad means when he says that Pennsylvania should work for all of us .
