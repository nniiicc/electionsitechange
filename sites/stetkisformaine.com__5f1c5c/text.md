Meet Joel
Joel Stetkis is a carpenter, County Commissioner, husband, and grandfather who understands the challenges facing rural Maine families.
Joel has spent his life working with his hands, running a business, and serving his community.
As a County Commissioner, he has fought to keep government accountable, protect taxpayers, and make sure rural communities are not ignored by politicians in Augusta.
Joel believes Maine families deserve lower taxes, affordable energy, safer communities, and leaders who stand up for their constitutional rights.
In the State Senate, Joel will bring common sense, real-world experience, and conservative leadership to fight for the people of rural Maine.
