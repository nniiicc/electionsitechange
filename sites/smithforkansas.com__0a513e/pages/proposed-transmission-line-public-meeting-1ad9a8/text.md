Meeting Information: Tuesday, September 29th at 6:00 p.m.
Central Time Colby Community College 1255 S.
Range Ave.
Colby, KS 67762 The open house will run from 3:00 to 6:00 p.m.
(CT) The Public Hearing will begin at 6:00 p.m.
(CT) If you are unable to attend but want to watch the meeting, it will be livestreamed and archived for later viewing on the Kansas Corporation Commission YouTube Channel .
The KCC is also accepting written comments through October 9, 2026.
The KCC will issue an order on or before February 15, 2027.
