Usually campaign season is full of high-pressure tactics claiming some “deadline” for a fund-raising goal in a desperate plea for money.
Don’t fall for it!
There are no such deadlines other than what a candidate makes for themselves.
If there truly is a fund-raising emergency, it is entirely self-inflicted!!
There are filing deadlines and finance report deadlines, but nothing for campaign contributions from regular folks like you.
I have managed my campaign finance account very frugally, and have not actively solicited any fundraising since the pandemic because my campaign philosophy matches my taxation philosophy: “Use only what is needed because that money is far better off in the taxpayers’ pocket!” Knowing that massive inflation was on the horizon with all of the “free money” from the federal government, I stopped asking for campaign contributions from the people of the 120th district that first election cycle after the pandemic.
I could see that families were going to need every extra dollar they could get.
I do still receive some unsolicited contributions to my campaign and for that I am very grateful.
It is an honor to represent the people of northwest Kansas in the Kansas House of Representatives!
