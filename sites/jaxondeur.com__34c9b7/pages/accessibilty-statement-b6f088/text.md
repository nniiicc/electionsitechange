Issues About Contact Endorsements DONATE Accessibility Statement Last Updated: February 7, 2026 The Committee to Elect Jaxon Deur for State Representative is committed to ensuring that jaxondeur.com is accessible to all users, including individuals with disabilities.
We strive to provide a website experience that is inclusive, user-friendly, and aligned with widely recognized accessibility standards.
What web accessibility is Our Commitment We aim to make our website accessible in accordance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, which outline best practices for designing accessible digital content.
Our ongoing efforts include: Improving website structure for screen readers Ensuring keyboard navigability Providing text alternatives for non-text content when possible Maintaining sufficient color contrast Reviewing content for clarity and readability Ongoing Improvements Accessibility is an ongoing process.
We regularly review our website to identify areas for improvement and update features to enhance usability for all visitors.
If you encounter any accessibility barriers, we welcome your feedback.
Feedback and Support If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our site, or if you have suggestions for improving accessibility, please contact us: Committee to Elect Jaxon Deur for State Representative PO BOX 291 Fremont, MI 49412 Email: jaxondeurcampaignmedia@gmail.com Website: jaxondeur.com We will make every effort to respond promptly and assist you with the information or service you need.
Third-Party Content While we strive to ensure full accessibility on our own pages, some third-party tools or links integrated into the site may not fully meet accessibility standards.
We encourage users to reach out if these present issues so we can work toward improving the experience.
