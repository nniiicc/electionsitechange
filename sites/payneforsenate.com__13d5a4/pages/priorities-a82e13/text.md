Priorities In Georgia Legislature
- Low Taxes Keeping taxes low is essential for fostering economic growth and providing families with greater financial freedom.
By minimizing the tax burden, individuals and businesses retain more of their hard-earned income, encouraging investment, job creation, and innovation.
Responsible fiscal policies that prioritize efficiency and accountability help ensure government resources are used effectively without imposing excessive taxes.
Maintaining low taxes supports a thriving economy, empowers citizens, and strengthens our community's future.
- Education Educating our next generation is fundamental to the continued prosperity and strength of our nation.
It requires investing in quality education that equips children with critical thinking skills, creativity, and the knowledge necessary to navigate an increasingly complex world.
Ensuring access to safe, well-funded schools and highly trained teachers is essential.
Additionally, integrating technology and modern teaching methods can prepare students for future careers while fostering a lifelong love of learning.
Supporting education reforms that close achievement gaps and promote equity will help every child reach their full potential, securing a brighter future for all Americans.
- Youth - Next Generation Addressing juvenile justice requires a balanced approach that holds young people accountable while recognizing their capacity for growth and change.
Being tough on crime is essential to maintain public safety and uphold the rule of law.
However, when it comes to juveniles, a purely punitive approach often fails to address the underlying issues that lead to bad decisions.
Instead, investing in smart rehabilitation programs can provide these youth with the tools, support, and guidance needed to make better choices in the future.
By focusing on education, mental health services, and community support, we can reduce recidivism, promote positive development, and build safer communities.
It is not just about punishment—it’s about creating opportunities for juveniles to turn their lives around and become productive members of society.
