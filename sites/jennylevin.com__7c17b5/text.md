The most effective leaders are those who can approach a situation from many different perspectives, as well as understand the impact their actions will have.
I am an Emergency and Trauma Nurse and have worked in emergency medicine for over ten years.
I have seen the impact shortcomings in the healthcare system have had on my patients, my family, and my community.
As the challenges we face continue to grow, I have decided to dedicate myself to serving others in a new way.
While working on the frontlines of COVID, I have seen the importance of decisions based on accurate information and scientific research.
As an active member of the community I understand why it is essential to stand up for the rights of all of my neighbors, especially those who are most vulnerable.
As someone who has made a career of heading into chaos while human instinct is to take cover, I am ready to take on the challenges we are facing together.
I am so proud to be a part of this community and look forward to getting to know you even better.
Your voices and your experiences matter.
I am ready to advocate for you and elevate your voices as your representative.
