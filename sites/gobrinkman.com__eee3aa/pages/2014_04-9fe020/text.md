Tom Brinkman been a consistent defender of the 2nd amendment.

That’s why we sent the National Association for Gun Rights Candidate Survey to candidates across the country.
I’ve got good news and bad news for you in Ohio House District 27.
The good news is that Republican candidate Tom Brinkman has returned his NAGR Candidate Surveys 100% pro-gun.
Tom Brinkman has also signed the Gun Rights Leadership Pledge, promising to sponsor pro-gun bills in the Ohio General Assembly.
Brinkman has pledged his support for solid pro-gun “Constitutional Carry” legislation.
In fact, as a State Representative years ago, Brinkman was a lead sponsor of the Ohio “Constitutional Carry” bill, which simply states you shouldn’t need permission from a bureaucrat to exercise your God-given right to defend yourself and your family.
Brinkman has also pledged to vote for legislation to protect Ohio from unconstitutional federal anti-gun restrictions , such as Ohio House Bill 99.
This bill would have blocked the enforcement of Obama’s un-constitutional Executive Orders, including his efforts to strip our veterans of their gun rights.

Peter Stautberg refused to answer his Gun Rights Survey.

Stautberg also refuses to oppose Obama’s radical anti-gun agenda in Ohio.
Stautberg even refuses to oppose legislation that would deny Ohio veterans of their gun rights without due process.
The National Association for Gun Rights has been conducting surveys on candidates across the country for many years.
Time and again the results speak for themselves.
Gun-grabbers in pro-gun districts prefer to dodge the issue rather than defending your 2nd Amendment rights.
When politicians refuse to take a stand on guns, you can be almost certain that the rest of your Constitutional rights are not on their list of priorities.
That’s why the National Association for Gun Rights is dedicated to keeping constituents informed about the true positions of our elected officials and candidates.
With the support of nearly 3.5 million members nationwide, 20,000 in Ohio, we are giving people across the state a loud voice in defense of our gun rights.
And trust me, the politicians can hear you.
There is no better time to lobby them than when they are seeking your vote at election time.
