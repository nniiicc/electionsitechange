Campaign Priorities
Pay Teachers More to Strengthen
Public Schools
- Create a competitive statewide pay floor so teachers can build a career anywhere in Kansas, ensuring quality educators aren't concentrated only in the districts with the most wealth.
- Fully fund public schools and Special Education to meet the state's legal obligations.
- Protect class sizes, expand course offerings and ensure every student has access to opportunity.
Cut Taxes for Working families
- Lower the property tax burden by ending reckless fiscal policies that shift costs onto homeowners.
- Stop corporate giveaways that drain public revenue and increase the tax burden on everyday Kansans for local services.
- Deliver responsible tax reform that prioritizes small businesses, seniors, and working families.
Let Kansas Put
Issues on the Ballot
- Give citizens the power to advance policies when the legislature refuses to act.
- Break the gridlock that blocks progress on healthcare, wages, and economic relief.
- Restore accountability so government responds to voters, not just political insiders.
