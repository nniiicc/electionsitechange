He is running for re-election to represent this diverse district and the common values that our communities share: respect and tolerance, hard work, New Jersey common sense, and the unshakeable idea that everyone should have the opportunity and tools to succeed.
Strengthening families is at the core of Rob’s purpose for serving our community.
He understands the pressures and challenges families face in these difficult times.
Rob is working in Congress to create solutions to those challenges – to make today better, and tomorrow a place where we can all thrive and reach our full potential.
Rob grew up valuing service that drives positive change in our communities.
He was raised to believe in the importance of giving back and taking care of our neighbors.
Today, Rob is raising his family in the shadow of the Statue of Liberty and continues to take to heart all she represents – the welcoming place where the American journey begins and the promise of America that works for everyone.
These ideals guide Rob and they guide this campaign.
Rob was elected to the U.S.
House of Representatives in 2022.
He has focused his legislative priorities on uplifting working families, championing organized labor, prioritizing future-forward infrastructure, addressing climate change, protecting reproductive choice , and creating a high quality of life for all.
Upon his arrival in Washington , Rob was elected by his peers to serve as Freshman Representative on the House Democrats’ Steering and Policy Committee, which works with House Democratic leadership to set policy priorities.
Rob also was appointed to serve as Regional Whip for the House Democratic Caucus, a post in which he assists in advancing Democrats’ legislative priorities.
Since taking office, Rob has delivered more than $12 billion in federal dollars for New Jersey’s Eighth Congressional District – for transformative improvements to our transportation systems, protecting our environment, funding community projects, and much more.
He is committed to finding comprehensive solutions for our toughest issues and serves as a voice for our shared priorities.
Rob serves on the House Energy and Commerce Committee, the oldest continuously-standing committee in the United States House of Representatives, with the broadest jurisdiction of any authorizing committee – touching upon healthcare, the environment, energy policy, telecommunications, privacy, cybersecurity, consumer protection, interstate and foreign commerce, and many other areas.
In this capacity, Rob’s leadership gives the working families of New Jersey’s Eighth District a seat at the table on their top priorities including affordability, healthcare accessibility, economic growth, and environmental justice.
Rob leads as DCCC Northeast Vice Chair, fighting to protect your values and helping Democrats take back the House Majority in 2026.
Rob also serves as Finance Co-Chair for CHC BOLD PAC, the campaign arm of the Congressional Hispanic Caucus, to continue investing in and expanding Latino representation nationwide.
He is a member of several caucuses, ranging from the House Pro-Choice Caucus to the Dads’ Caucus.
Rob previously served on the House Transportation and Infrastructure Committee, where he advocated to expand mass transit via the development of the Gateway Program, the most important infrastructure project in the country, which recently received $6.9 billion from the U.S.
Department of Transportation – the largest-ever mass transit grant in American history.
Rob was also a member of the Committee on Homeland Security, where he focused on the cybersecurity challenges facing our country.
He continuously works toward a humane and comprehensive approach to our immigration system, fights Republicans’ hard-right narrative, and advocates for the most vulnerable among us.
Before Congress, Rob served as a Commissioner of the Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, where he chaired the Governance and Ethics Committee and served as a member of the Finance Committee, which manages the Port Authority’s multi-billion-dollar annual budget.
Rob earned an undergraduate degree from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and a J.D. from Rutgers Law School.
He and his wife, young daughter, and son live in the Lafayette neighborhood of Jersey City.
