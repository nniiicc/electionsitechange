Make the Road Action New Jersey Endorses Congressman Rob Menendez for Re-Election in 2026
NEW JERSEY – Make the Road Action New Jersey today endorsed Congressman Rob Menendez for re-election.
Make the Road Action is one of New Jersey’s largest immigrant advocacy organizations and has worked closely with Congressman Menendez to fight for immigrant communities across New Jersey.
“When our immigrant communities have come under attack by the Trump Administration, Rob has been there every step of the way to protect our neighbors and to fight for our communities,” said Nedia Morsy, Director of Make the Road Action New Jersey.
“Rob did not hesitate to face down a group of armed ICE and HSI agents at Delaney Hall when the Trump Administration tried to stop Rob and his colleagues from doing their job.
He is the fighter we need in this moment and we are committed to returning him to Congress to continue the fight.
Our members are proud to endorse him and will be working tirelessly to educate voters on his record of delivering for our neighbors.”
“In these trying times, Make the Road Action is one of our most important partners in safeguarding our neighbors and communities,” said Congressman Menendez.
“I will always stand tall against the lawless assault that the Trump Administration has unleashed on our cities and towns.
I am proud to be in this work with Make the Road Action and am thankful for their advocacy and support.”
