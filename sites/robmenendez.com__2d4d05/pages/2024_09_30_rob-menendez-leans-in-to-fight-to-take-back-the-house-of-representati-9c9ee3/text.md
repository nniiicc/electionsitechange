Rob Menendez Leans in to Fight to Take Back the House of Representatives with $200K in Support for House Democrats
The financial support includes a donation of $150,000 to the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee and raising more than $50,000 for BOLD PAC, the political arm of the Congressional Hispanic Caucus.
“With so much at stake, I’m proud to support my fellow Democrats and will do everything I can to make sure we win this November and ensure that Hakeem Jeffries is the next Speaker of the House,” said Congressman Rob Menendez.
“And it is critical that when Kamala Harris becomes President, she has a Congress that not only will work with her to improve people’s lives, but one that looks like and reflects the people we represent.”
