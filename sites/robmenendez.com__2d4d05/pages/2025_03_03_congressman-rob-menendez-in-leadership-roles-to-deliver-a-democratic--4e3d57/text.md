Rob Menendez (NJ-08) has joined their leadership team and will serve as one of four regional vice chairs for the 2026 cycle , alongside Reps.
Lizzie Fletcher (TX-07), Sara Jacobs (CA-51), and Mark Pocan (WI-02).
In this capacity, Rep.
Menendez is on the frontlines working to keep Republicans in check and leading Democrats to take back the House Majority in 2026.
“As House Republicans continue to fail the American people, House Democrats are holding them accountable, contrasting Republicans’ chaos with our message of fighting to lower costs and keeping our communities safe,” said DCCC Chair Suzan DelBene .
“Rob Menendez is a proven, effective, and enthusiastic communicator whose role as one of the DCCC’s Regional Vice Chairs will play a critical part in the team effort to retake the House Majority.
Rob’s tireless advocacy for working families is the exact kind of leadership we need to be successful in the midterms.” “In the past two months, voters across the country have seen Republicans’ playbook in action as they sow disorder, challenge the frameworks of our democracy, and fundamentally disregard the priorities of their constituents,” said Rep.
Rob Menendez .
“We have heard the demand for a different direction for our country and House Democrats are prepared to deliver for the American people – always putting people over politics.
I am excited to help lead this effort as DCCC Regional Vice Chair as we work to bring about our shared mission: returning normalcy to the country, making life more affordable, protecting freedoms, and creating opportunity for all Americans.” In addition to his leadership with the DCCC, Rep.
Menendez is serving alongside Rep.
Salud Carbajal (CA-24) as Finance Co-Chair for CHC BOLD PAC – the campaign arm of the Congressional Hispanic Caucus – during the 119th Congress, under the leadership of Chairwoman Linda Sánchez (CA-38).
“My friend, Rep.
Rob Menendez, is a champion for our communities and their priorities,” said CHC BOLD PAC Chairwoman Linda Sánchez .
“I am excited to welcome Rob as a Finance Co-Chair as we continue investing in Latino campaigns nationwide, building upon our success, and expanding our Caucus as we retake the House in 2026.
His new leadership roles at BOLD PAC and the DCCC prove his deep commitment to protecting our democracy and putting working families first.” As a DCCC Regional Vice Chair and Finance Co-Chair for CHC BOLD PAC, Rep.
Menendez is stepping up to lead and hold Republicans accountable, advance policies that put people first, and work tirelessly to restore Democratic leadership in the House.
