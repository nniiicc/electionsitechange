32BJ SEIU Endorses Congressman Rob Menendez for Re-Election
NEW JERSEY — 32BJ SEIU has endorsed Congressman Rob Menendez for re-election to Congress in the 2026 cycle.
“Congressman Rob Menendez has been a real partner to working families in NJ-08,” said Ana Maria Hill, Vice President of 32BJ SEIU and New Jersey State Director.
“Whether it’s protecting collective bargaining rights, supporting federal investments that create good union jobs, or standing up for immigrant and frontline workers, Rob has earned the trust of our members.
We’re proud to endorse his re-election.”
“I am proud to earn the continued support of 32BJ SEIU and their members,” said Congressman Menendez.
“It’s been an honor to fight alongside 32BJ SEIU for better workplace conditions, the right to organize, protecting our immigrant communities, and advocating for every worker to have a living wage.
I look forward to continuing the fight for pro-worker policies and progressive values alongside 32BJ – an incredible ally in this critical work.”
32BJ SEIU is a member-driven union representing 15,000 service workers in New Jersey and a total of 185,000 service workers across 12 states and Washington, D.C.
Since being elected to Congress, Rep.
Menendez has supported important labor legislation such as the PRO Act and fought for working families to have access to affordable, quality healthcare, stable housing, a clean environment and a fair shot at the American dream.
He is a member of the Congressional Labor Caucus and holds a 100% lifetime score from the AFL-CIO.
