“Rob Menendez has been an outspoken champion and vital ally for Asian Americans, Native Hawaiians, and Pacific Islanders in New Jersey and across the country,” said ASPIRE PAC Chair Congresswoman Marilyn Strickland.
“From Delaney Hall to the halls of Congress, he has wielded his voice and power to defend immigrants and demand accountability from those at the very top.
Rob will not back down from a fight to protect our communities, and ASPIRE PAC is proud to endorse Rep.
Menendez for re-election.” “Representing our extremely diverse district, including the many AANHPI communities across our cities and towns in Congress is a distinct honor,” said Congressman Menendez.
“I am proud to have earned ASPIRE PAC’s support, which reflects the work we do every single day to fight for every single person in our district, whether it is delivering constituent services, bringing billions of dollars home for our cities and towns, or standing up to the Trump Administration when it goes after our community members.” ASPIRE PAC supports candidates of AANHPI descent and incumbents who represent large communities of AANHPI voters.
As one of the only PACs dedicated to investing directly in the AANHPI community, ASPIRE PAC offers a voice for the AANHPI community and works to mobilize the AANHPI vote.
