Jane has diligently fought for our First and Second Amendment rights, parental rights in education, property rights, and the right to life.
Dr.
Jane has championed and passed many pieces legislation which support our freedoms, including bills which promote adoption over abortion and protect our right to bear arms.
One of Dr.
Jane’s top priorities is supporting marriage and protecting children, as well as promoting independence and rewarding hard work.
Supporting Montana Ranchers and Farmers: Dr.
Jane knows there’s no more important job than supplying our communities with food, and she has fought in the legislature for our farmers and ranchers.
She has made sure ag producers’ water rights are protected, ranchers are free to control predator populations, and that those who provide us with our food always have a seat at the table when related policies are made at the Capitol.
Protecting Montana Jobs and Reducing Wasteful Spending: As a business owner and director of a non-profit, Dr.
Jane knows the importance of hard work, a balanced budget and making smart spending choices.
She will continue to work hard to ensure that waste is reduced, vital services are protected, and tax dollars are used wisely - and sparingly.
Rural Montana is losing jobs as traditional Montana jobs are regulated out of existence.
Dr.
Jane will cut regulations, stand up for agriculture and responsible resource extraction, and protect rural Montana jobs.
She is committed to growing the economy for all Montanans and supporting businesses that create good-paying Montana jobs.
Protecting our Border and Ensuring Fairness for All Montanans: Dr.
Jane is the single most qualified legislator poised to address the impacts of our border crisis in Montana.
She’s been to the southern border and experienced firsthand the threats posed to our Montana way of life.
Dr.
Jane is committed to passing state-level laws that remove illegal aliens, protect legal Montana citizens, and crack down on illegal drugs in our state.
Dr.
Jane believes Montana jobs are for Montanans, our healthcare resources are for American citizens, and our schools must prioritize serving American children and teaching American values – not villainizing our children based on their heritage and skin color.
I mproving Healthcare Affordability and Quality: Dr.
Jane has been a crusader for quality, accessible healthcare and individual medical freedom.
With her expertise, she will continue the fight for top-notch healthcare, while reducing costs and safeguarding critical access to services for all Montanans.
Montanans deserve healthcare transparency, coupled with increased competition, innovation, and more patient engagement and rights in their care.
Dr.
Jane proudly sponsored, and had signed into law, a Patient Bill of Rights in the 2023 legislative session.
Reducing the cost of healthcare – while ensuring that quality of care improves, and patient rights are protected – is a top priority for Dr.
Jane and she will continue to fight for it in Helena.
Protecting Access to Public Lands: Public lands must be kept in public hands.
Like other Montanans, Dr.
Jane enjoys her time spent outside in the beautiful area we live in.
She understands the importance of keeping rivers and lands open to all Montanans and is proud to stand with sportsmen, hikers, hunters, and public land users of all kinds in maintaining and increasing public access for generations to come.
Please feel free to reach out to Dr.
Jane at any time with any questions or concerns.
