Priorities
Attorney General Kris Mayes is the fighter Arizona families need: someone who leaves politics at the door, stands up for everyday Arizonans, and gets results.
A former Republican, Mayes has picked a lot of fights — and won.
She took on Saudi Arabian mega-farms draining Arizona’s water supply.
She fought greedy corporations that gouged Arizonans on rent and groceries.
She went after drug smugglers, elder abusers, and scammers.
And who benefited?
Everyday Arizonans — small business owners, farmers and ranchers, parents, and kids.
