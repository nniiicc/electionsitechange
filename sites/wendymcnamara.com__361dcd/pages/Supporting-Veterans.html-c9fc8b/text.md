Wendy McNamara honors and respects those who have fought and served our country.
The sacrifices of family and of life are gifts to our freedom that are immeasurable.
For this reason, she strongly supports our veterans and policies that help them.
She believes in waiving the maximum hiring-age restrictions previously placed on veterans who apply to become police officers and firefighters.
She advocates for allowing for the proper burial of deceased veterans who do not have living family members.
She also believes in the establishment of penalties for stolen valor, making it a crime to profit financially by lying about military service.
Wendy McNamara strongly supports our veterans!
