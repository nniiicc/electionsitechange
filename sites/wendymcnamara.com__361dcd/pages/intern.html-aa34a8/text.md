STATEHOUSE (Sept. 28, 2019) — State Rep.
Wendy McNamara (R-Evansville) and fellow Republican members of the Indiana House of Representatives are seeking interns for the upcoming 2017 legislative session, which runs from January through April.
“Many of our students and graduates who intern through our program go on to become full-time members of the House Republican staff,” McNamara said.
“This opportunity gives interns the chance to build their resumes, network and apply the knowledge they learned in the classroom.”
College sophomores, juniors, seniors, graduate students, law students and recent graduates are eligible to apply.
Benefits include a bi-weekly compensation of $700 and opportunities to earn academic credits and a scholarship.
McNamara said these are full-time positions, Monday through Friday, with a typical work day starting at 8:30 a.m.
Internships are available in various departments, including legislative, communications, technology, fiscal policy and policy.
The deadline to apply is Oct. 31.
For more information and to submit applications, visit www.indianahouserepublicans.com/internship.
