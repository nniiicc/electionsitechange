Illinois Education Association Illinois Education Association, IEA is the largest union in Illinois.
I am honored to have their endorsement.
IEA is a union of more than 135,000 members composed of Illinois elementary and secondary teachers, higher education faculty and staff, educational support professionals, retired educators and college students preparing to become teachers.
The IEA was founded in 1853 to serve the interest of public education in Illinois.
The IEA became an affiliate of the National Education Association in 1857, an organization currently totaling 3.2 million teachers and school employees working to make our good public schools even better for all students.
