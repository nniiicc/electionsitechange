Plenty of us are exhausted by the noxious polarization of good people who are community minded in this state.
It is time for new kinds of representatives who don’t abide by party lines, but who abide by the needs of their constituents.
Representatives who are not afraid of learning and growing.
We need people in this country who serve their communities and not their positions of power.
It is time to vote for candidates who work across the aisle and especially candidates who know what it is like to work blue collar jobs as well as white collar in order to best meet the needs of the diverse peoples our district houses.
Often, the loudest narratives leave out the good work towns like ours achieve every day.
I’m here to tell you that I see you and I want to work for a more secure and fruitful future; one which never forgets the labor each voter has invested in the Granite State.
