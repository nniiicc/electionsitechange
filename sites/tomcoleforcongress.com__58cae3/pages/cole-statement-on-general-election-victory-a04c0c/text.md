House of Representatives.
“I am greatly honored to continue representing the people of the Fourth Congressional District of Oklahoma, and I am grateful for the strong support of voters in this general election.
“The American people have been faced with numerous challenges the past two years, including the consequences of raging inflation, Biden’s disastrous open border, an ongoing energy crisis and mounting security threats.
I remain committed to delivering solutions that address these problems.
“While I am willing to work with anyone to find solutions that reflect the priorities of the American people, my job is to fight for the views, values and interests of Oklahoma in general and the constituents of the Fourth District in particular.
