In August of 2003, I purchased my residence and made Rogers my home.
Understanding what is on constituents’ minds is very important to me.
I value the feedback I receive from our great voters.
I have and will continue to dedicate myself to meeting voters in person in our district.
My education includes a Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering from the University of Missouri—Columbia and a Masters of Business Administration from the University of Arkansas—Fayetteville.
I worship at Cross Church in Rogers and participate in the Single's Ministry there.
When I actually do have down time, I enjoy my hobbies of vintage video games and computers, such as the Atari 2600 and the TI 99/4A which I still have from my childhood.
During my tenure, I have stood for the people of our district and I am running for re-election to continue to do so.
I have never and will never let Little Rock tell our district what to do.
