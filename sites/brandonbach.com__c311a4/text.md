Where I Stand Get Involved Meet Brandon A Derry Mom Ready to Fight for Liberty A mom of five, wife of 25 years, small business owner, and proud New Hampshire resident — Brandon has built a life rooted in family, personal responsibility, and a deep love for our community.
As a parent with children in both Derry public schools and homeschool programs, she has seen firsthand that every child learns differently — and that parents, not government, should have the freedom to choose the path that's right for their family.
As owner of Atelier Creative, a marketing and design agency, Brandon understands what it takes to balance a budget, create jobs, and navigate government regulations while building something meaningful.
She knows the challenges facing entrepreneurs, families, and taxpayers because she lives them every day.
When she's not working, you'll find her with her family, volunteer teaching, doing Pilates, or renovating her historic home.
She cares deeply about preserving the character, values, and freedoms that make New Hampshire such a special place to live.
She's running because New Hampshire's future depends on protecting the rights and liberties of its citizens — and she believes government works best when it is limited, accountable, and respectful of the people it serves. — Brandon Bach Where I Stand Fighting for Our Freedoms in Concord 01 — LIBERTY Protecting the Second Amendment The Second Amendment is not a government-granted privilege — it is a natural right recognized and protected by our Constitution.
The right of self-defense belongs to every individual, and government should not stand between citizens and their ability to protect themselves and their families.
Brandon will oppose legislation that infringes on the rights of law-abiding gun owners and support policies that strengthen individual liberty, personal responsibility, and constitutional government.
New Hampshire's tradition of freedom depends on protecting the rights of its citizens — and she'll remain a consistent voice for those freedoms.
02 — FAMILIES Education Freedom Parents have both the right and the responsibility to direct the education of their children.
Government should support families, not dictate how children are educated.
As a mother with children in both public schools and homeschool programs, Brandon understands that no single educational model works for every family.
She supports policies that protect parental rights, expand educational freedom, and let funding follow the student — whether a family chooses public school, charter, private, homeschool, or microschooling.
Every family is different.
Parents deserve the freedom to choose.
03 — TAXPAYERS Property Tax Relief Owning a home should provide security and independence — not a lifetime lease from the government.
Rising property taxes are making it harder for families, seniors, and retirees to remain in the homes they've worked hard to build.
Brandon will support controlling spending, reducing unfunded mandates from Concord, and returning decision-making authority to local communities.
No one should be forced out of their home because government spending grows faster than their ability to pay.
Protecting property rights means helping people keep what they've earned.
Rooted in Family Family, Faith & New Hampshire Values For Brandon, this campaign is personal.
As a wife of 25 years and mom of five, she's running to protect the future of the community her family calls home.
From renovating a historic home to volunteer teaching, she's invested in preserving the character, values, and freedoms that make New Hampshire such a special place to live — and she'll carry that perspective to Concord every single day.
