About District 6
Oregon Senate District 6 is a diverse and hardworking region made up of small towns, rural communities, and working lands.
From farms and ranches to main streets and waterways, District 6 reflects the backbone of Oregon’s economy and outdoor heritage.
Counties represented include:
- Lane County
- Linn County
- Parts of Marion County
