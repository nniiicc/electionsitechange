Diana Kushnar for State Representative Home About Values Get Involved Donate Diana Kushnar for Maine State Representative Donate Now Meet Diana Get Involved Scroll About Diana Dear Gorham, Diana Kushnar is a lifelong Gorham resident, devoted wife, and proud mother of three who understands firsthand the challenges facing Maine families.
As a homeowner, she knows that rising property taxes, increasing costs, and government spending are making it harder for families to get ahead and harder for the next generation to build their future here in Maine.
Diana believes Gorham’s success should be shaped by the people who live here-not by mandates from Augusta.
She supports local control, responsible growth, and thoughtful development that strengthens our community while preserving the character and values that make Gorham such a special place to call home.
For nearly a decade, Diana dedicated her time to coaching youth volleyball, mentoring young athletes, building confidence, teaching teamwork, and investing in the next generation.
Her experience working in commercial filmmaking sharpened her skills in organization, collaboration, problem-solving, and attention to detail-qualities she will bring with her to the State House.
As a wife, mother, and homeowner, Diana understands what it means to live within a budget.
Families make difficult choices every day, carefully managing their finances and ensuring every dollar serves a purpose.
She believes state government should be held to the same standard.
Diana is running because she wants to help ensure Gorham remains a place where families can afford to live, work, raise children, and retire.
She believes economic opportunity, responsible government, and strong communities go hand in hand.
By protecting taxpayers, supporting local businesses, encouraging responsible growth, and keeping decision-making close to home, she believes Maine can remain a place where future generations can thrive.
Diana isn’t running for higher office or political ambition.
She’s running because she loves Gorham, believes in the people who call it home, and knows our community deserves a strong voice in Augusta.
Together, we can protect what makes Gorham special, create opportunities for the next generation, and build a brighter future for all Maine families.
