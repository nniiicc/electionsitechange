Something about Mary
Prior to being elected to the legislature, Mary was a licensed childcare provider and small business owner.
She graduated from University of Minnesota (Duluth) with a degree in psychology
Mary is a bulldog in the Minnesota legislature and has fiercely advocated for our values in District 12B.
Since being elected in 2010, Mary has consistently been a strong voice for the Second Amendment, the unborn, parental rights, limited government, and election integrity.
Mary lives Alexandria and is a mom of three.
She is a member of the Alexandria Covenant Church, which is part of the Evangelical Covenant Church.
