About The Candidate
Shawn Mickelonis
Shawn Mickelonis is committed to serving the city he loves.
A former state representative and current chairman of the Greater Dover Republican Committee, Shawn brings years of experience in public service and a proven record of leadership.
He is running for State Senate District 4 to ensure Dover remains safe, affordable, and thriving for every resident.
Why Shawn Is Running
"Our citizens deserve leadership that protects families, keeps neighborhoods safe, and makes our communities affordable.
I will fight to lower taxes, tackle drugs and homelessness, ensure affordable housing, and hold government accountable.
Together, we can make New Hampshire a stronger, safer, and more prosperous state for everyone."
