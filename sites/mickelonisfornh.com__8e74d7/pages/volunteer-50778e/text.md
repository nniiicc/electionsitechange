New Hampshire has always been strongest when neighbors step up for one another.
That’s how this campaign will win—and how we’ll bring practical, honest leadership to the State Senate.
Shawn Mickelonis is running to put people first, protect our New Hampshire values, and make government work better for everyday Granite Staters.
But we can’t do it alone.
We need volunteers who are ready to help build a grassroots campaign powered by the community.
Why Volunteer
When you volunteer, you’re helping to:
- Talk directly with voters about the issues that matter most
- Strengthen our local communities and civic engagement
- Support a campaign focused on common sense, accountability, and opportunity
- Make a real difference in a close, local election
Whether you have an hour a week or want to be deeply involved, there’s a place for you on this team.
Ways to Get Involved
You can volunteer in many ways, including:
- Door Knocking & Canvassing – Have friendly conversations with neighbors
- Phone Banking & Texting – Reach voters from home or with the team
- Events & Community Outreach – Help at local events, town halls, and meet-and-greets
- Sign & Visibility Efforts – Show support across the district
- Campaign Support – Data entry, organizing, and behind-the-scenes help
No experience is necessary—we’ll provide training and support.
