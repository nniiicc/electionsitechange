Rollinsford Tue, Nov 3, 2026 On The Issues Delivering For NH State Senate District 4 Keeping Our Communities Affordable New Hampshire families are being squeezed by rising housing costs, higher taxes, and inflation.
Shawn Mickelonis supports practical policies that encourage responsible development, reduce unnecessary regulations, and protect homeowners and renters alike—so people can afford to live, work, and raise families right here in District 4.
Fiscal Responsibility & Taxpayer Protection Shawn believes government should live within its means—just like families and small businesses do.
He supports disciplined budgeting, opposition to wasteful spending, and protecting New Hampshire’s tradition of low taxes so working families keep more of what they earn.
Supporting Law Enforcement & Public Safety Safe communities start with respect for law enforcement and first responders.
Shawn strongly supports those who serve on the front lines and believes in policies that uphold public safety while respecting constitutional rights.
Education That Puts Families First Parents—not bureaucrats—know what’s best for their children.
Shawn supports educational options that give families flexibility and choice, while maintaining accountability and strong local schools that prepare students for success.
Protecting Individual Liberties Shawn is a firm believer in personal freedom and limited government.
He supports policies that respect constitutional rights, uphold the rule of law, and keep government from overreaching into the everyday lives of Granite Staters.
Common-Sense Leadership Shawn Mickelonis brings a practical, level-headed approach to public service—focused on solutions, not politics.
He believes in listening to constituents, working across differences when possible, and always putting the interests of local communities first.
New Hampshire deserves representation that reflects its values: independence, responsibility, and common sense.
That’s what Shawn Mickelonis is committed to delivering for Senate District 4.
