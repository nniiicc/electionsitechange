About Shawn Mickelonis
Shawn Mickelonis is an political leader, entrepreneur, community advocate, and entertainment executive from New Hampshire with a proven record of public service and private-sector achievement.
Born and raised in the region, he has dedicated his career to strengthening local communities, supporting economic growth, and advancing the creative industries.
Political Leadership & Public Service
Shawn began his political career in the New Hampshire House of Representatives, representing Strafford’s 1st District from 2006 to 2008, where he focused on public safety, education, and community development.
He continued his civic engagement through leadership roles within local parties and committees, serving as Chairman of the Greater Dover Republican Committee and pursuing higher office, including campaigns for the New Hampshire State Senate and Executive Council.
In 2025, Shawn announced his candidacy for Mayor of Dover, New Hampshire, centering his platform on fiscal responsibility, housing affordability, public safety, small-business support, and accountable local governance.
Education
Shawn is a graduate of Somersworth High School (Class of 1999) and earned a Bachelor of Science degree from the University of Massachusetts Lowell, providing a strong foundation for his leadership in both business and public service.
Business & Entrepreneurial Leadership
As a seasoned business owner and executive, Shawn brings strategic leadership and operational expertise to the private and public sectors alike.
His entrepreneurial experience informs his pragmatic approach to economic development and community growth.
In the entertainment industry, he serves as Chief Operations Officer of Afimi Music, a respected music enterprise.
Under his leadership, the company has guided high-impact creative projects that have earned RIAA multi-Platinum awards, demonstrating his influence in executive production and business management within the arts.
Community Engagement & Achievements
Beyond his professional work, Shawn is deeply involved in his community through volunteer efforts, civic initiatives, and leadership roles designed to improve quality of life and expand opportunity for residents across New Hampshire.
Shawn’s combination of public service experience, entrepreneurial initiative, and creative industry leadership positions him as a versatile leader committed to delivering meaningful results across multiple sectors.
