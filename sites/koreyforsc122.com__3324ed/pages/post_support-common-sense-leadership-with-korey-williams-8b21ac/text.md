A native of rural Hampton county, who previously ran for the seat, he feels called to serve as a champion for the people of the district, citing his deep understanding of the issues most impacting local communities.
He is committed to being a voice of reason in the state house and a voice for the people of district 122, advocating for their needs and interests.
