I am deeply honored by the trust you've placed in me, and I'm grateful to every volunteer, supporter, donor, and voter who helped make this moment possible.
This victory is about more than one campaign—it's about a shared belief that District 122 deserves leadership that listens, shows up, and works tirelessly for the people it serves.
Now, our attention turns to the general election in November, where I'll face my Republican opponent.
Over the coming months, I'll continue meeting with neighbors across our district, listening to your concerns, and sharing my vision for a stronger future.
I believe we can build an economy that works for everyone, strengthen our public schools, support our first responders, expand access to healthcare, protect our natural resources, and ensure every family has the opportunity to succeed.

Whether you're willing to volunteer, host a meet-and-greet, display a yard sign, contribute to the campaign, or simply talk with your friends and neighbors, your support will help us carry this momentum through November.
This campaign has always been about putting people before politics and finding practical solutions that move District 122 forward.
Together, we can build a brighter future for our community.
Thank you for believing in this campaign, and thank you for believing in what's possible.
I'm excited for the journey ahead, and I look forward to earning your vote this November.
