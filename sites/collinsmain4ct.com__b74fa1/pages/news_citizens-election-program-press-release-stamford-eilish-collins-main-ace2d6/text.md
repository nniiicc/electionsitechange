Beyond the momentum indicated, it also means that she has reached the threshold required for the Citizens’ Election Program grant pending confirmation by the State Elections Enforcement Commission.
“From throughout Stamford, the fast outpouring of support from our diverse and passionate community is truly humbling.
Thank you to every single person who donated to fuel our impactful movement to bring positive change,” said Collins Main.
“The enthusiastic response in such a short amount of time says loud and clear that residents are ready for new leadership in the 146th State House District and are responding with real enthusiasm for our grassroots campaign.
At this time, I look forward to speaking with more Stamford voters about their concerns and hopefully earning the Democratic City Committee’s formal endorsement.” About Eilish Collins Main With a successful career in marketing and business development in the EdTech sector, Eilish Collins Main brings valuable skills to public service as a communicator and consensus builder.
She is an active member of the Stamford Democratic Party, and volunteers with Building One Community, the League of Women Voters, the Police Activities League, and the Saint Francis Epispocal Church outreach programs.
Collins Main also serves as a Personnel Commissioner for the City of Stamford.
To learn more about her campaign, visit collinsmain4ct.com.
