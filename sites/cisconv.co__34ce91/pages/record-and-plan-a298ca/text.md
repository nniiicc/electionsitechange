Cisco’s Record and Plan for Nevada Families
As Nevada’s Secretary of State Cisco Aguilar has worked to make it easier to do business and invested in systems to ensure our elections are free, fair, and transparent.
He knows growing our economy and creating opportunities starts with letting small business owners focus on their operations by:
Modernizing
Business Licensing
Cisco has prioritized updating Nevada’s business infrastructure to reduce wait times for small business owners, reduce system outages, and ensure Nevada businesses are able to focus on their customers – not government compliance.
Making Nevada more competitive and business friendly
He’s working to ensure Nevada businesses can adjust business license fees in response to market conditions which is bringing in more businesses and jobs to our state.
Strengthening Election Integrity
by prioritizing cybersecurity and serving as a staunch advocate for election workers, members of local communities who keep our democracy running.
He championed a bipartisan bill that makes it a felony to harass or dox election officials, a measure that passed the Nevada legislature unanimously.
Promoting Civic Engagement
Cisco is recruiting a new generation of poll workers, including a partnership between his office and Vet the Vote, which enlists veterans and military family members.
He’s also focused on boosting voter turnout among Native American communities and young voters.
