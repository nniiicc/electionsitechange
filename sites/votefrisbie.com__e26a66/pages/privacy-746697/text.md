Frisbie . ← Back to Home Privacy Policy Committee to Elect Steve Frisbie for State House The Committee to Elect Steve Frisbie for State House ("the Committee," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through votefrisbie.com and our related communications, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Information We Collect We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including when you sign up for updates, RSVP to an event, volunteer, or contact the campaign.
This may include: Name Email address Mailing address Telephone number (including mobile number) Employer and occupation, where required by campaign finance law Event RSVP details and volunteer interests We may also automatically collect limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited, in order to operate and improve the website.
How We Use Your Information To send campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer opportunities, and requests for support To respond to questions and constituent or supporter inquiries To process and acknowledge event RSVPs and contributions To comply with Michigan campaign finance reporting requirements To improve the performance and content of our website Text Messaging and SMS Communications If you provide your telephone number to the campaign, you consent to receive calls and text messages from the Committee to Elect Steve Frisbie for State House and its authorized agents, including messages sent using automated technology.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency may vary.
Messaging may include requests for donation.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message.
For assistance, reply HELP .
Opting out of text messages will not affect your ability to receive other communications from the campaign that you have requested.
Text messaging opt-in data, consent and related collected personal information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, unless required by law.
Information Sharing We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with vendors, consultants, and service providers who perform services on our behalf — such as email, texting, event, and payment providers — solely so they can perform those services for the campaign.
These providers are required to protect your information and may not use it for their own purposes.
As stated above, text messaging opt-in data, consent, and related collected personal information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, unless required by law.
We may disclose information where required by law, including Michigan campaign finance disclosure requirements, or in response to a valid legal process.
Contributions Contributions to the campaign are processed by a third-party payment processor (WinRed).
Payment card information is submitted directly to that processor and is governed by its own privacy policy and security practices.
Federal and state law require campaigns to report certain contributor information, including name, mailing address, employer, and occupation, which becomes part of the public record.
Cookies Data Security We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect.
However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Children's Privacy Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.
Your Choices Text messages: Reply STOP to any message to opt out; reply HELP for help.
Email: Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any campaign email.
Phone calls: Ask to be removed when contacted, or use the contact information below.
Access or deletion: Contact us using the information below to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, subject to recordkeeping required by law.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date below.
Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
