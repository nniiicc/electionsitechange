First Term Results
Delivering for Calhoun County
In his first year, Steve has authored and advanced legislation that cuts taxes, keeps communities safe, protects local control, and invests in our district.
Signed Into Law
Sponsored HB 4181 as part of a bipartisan road funding package signed by the Governor, directing fuel tax revenue to repairing the local roads and bridges families, farmers, and first responders rely on.
Passed House
Advanced legislation to permanently lower Michigan's income tax and end double taxation on electronic device trade-ins.
Co-sponsored measures eliminating sales tax on baby essentials like diapers and children's clothing.
District Investment
Co-sponsored the $115M Public Safety Trust Fund for officer recruitment and equipment.
Secured $32K for Albion police body cameras and $125K for Springfield's public safety carport.
Authored and passed HB 4486 to block local gas bans, keeping energy affordable.
Co-sponsored legislation restoring local control over wind and solar siting decisions.
House Floor
Drawing on 40 years as a paramedic, Steve authored HB 4847 to protect Good Samaritans and expand bleeding-control training in Michigan schools.
Jobs & Growth
Advanced aviation industry legislation supporting Battle Creek's aerospace sector and WMU Aviation.
Led the effort to bring a missile defense mission to Fort Custer.
Funding Secured
Secured LDSI funding for Bronson Health Foundation to expand training across Calhoun County, teaching parents and educators to recognize warning signs and stop child abuse.
Bipartisan
Led a bipartisan coalition urging DHS to retain the Federal Protective Service MegaCenter and its jobs.
Advocated for Burmese TPS holders facing deportation.
Finance Committee
Advanced HB 5318 to modernize fundraising tax exemptions for veterans organizations, churches, and nonprofits — updating the cap from $25K to $75K.
Steve Frisbie has spent his entire career answering the call — whether racing to save a life, serving his neighbors on the County Commission, or teaching the next generation at Kellogg Community College.
He brings that same urgency to Lansing.
Elected in 2024 to represent Michigan's 44th House District, Steve defeated an incumbent by building a broad coalition across Battle Creek, Springfield, Albion, and the surrounding townships.

Steve represents Battle Creek, Springfield, Albion, and the townships of Bedford, Pennfield, Convis, Lee, Clarence, and Sheridan.
As Vice Chair of the House Finance Committee, he fights every day to cut taxes, reduce wasteful spending, and make government work as hard as the people it serves.
With over 150 in-district meetings, regular office hours in Battle Creek and Albion, and a 100% attendance record in Lansing, Steve is one of the most accessible and active representatives in the state.
What Steve's Fighting For
Priorities for Calhoun County
Permanently lower Michigan's income tax, eliminate wasteful spending, and keep more money in working families' pockets.
Fully fund local and state police, invest in officer recruitment, and give first responders the support to keep our neighborhoods safe.

Record classroom funding with high standards.
Pay teachers what they deserve while making sure every dollar reaches students.
Michigan can't just compete — we must lead.
