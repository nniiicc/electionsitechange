Priorities for Washington
Mary Fosse is a dedicated leader who fights for our community and working families.
Coming from the working class means Mary understands our struggle, and she cares about us.
In the State Legislature, Mary is instrumental in:
- Expanding Childcare access and affordability
- Funding our schools, paraeducators, and capital improvements in our school districts
- Protecting workers and working families by strengthening workplace safety, workers’ rights, and safety nets for those with caregiver responsibilities
- Increasing apprenticeship opportunities and trade pathways for youth and those seeking a second chance
- Prioritizing increased investment and policy improvements in housing and behavioral health arenas
- Passing policy to ensure safe communities for all and diversify our public safety workforce
