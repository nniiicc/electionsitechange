Know Your Candidate: Greg Abdouch California Assembly for District 45
Gio is joined by Greg Abdouch, candidate for California State Assembly District 45, for a conversation about his story, his priorities, and what he hopes to bring to Sacramento on behalf of the district he aims to represent.
Greg discusses the challenges facing local families, small businesses, and communities, including affordability, public safety, accountability, and the need for common-sense leadership in state government.
In this episode, Gio asks how Greg plans to fight for District 45, improve quality of life for residents, and make sure their concerns are heard in Sacramento.

Team Abdouch/The Rock Church Launch First Ever Community Backpack Giveaway
More than 500 local students will receive a new backpack and a 22-piece school supply kit through a first-of-its-kind partnership between Team Abdouch, The Rock Church, local businesses, generous donors, and volunteers.
The Community Backpack Giveaway will take place on Saturday, August 1, at The Rock Church Food Distribution Center.
