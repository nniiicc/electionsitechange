This page informs website visitors about our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for those who choose to use our website and related services, including SMS communications.
By using our website or participating in our campaign’s communications, including SMS messaging, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this privacy policy.
Information collection and use To provide a better experience and fulfill campaign-related communications, we may collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, email address, and postal address.
This information is used to contact or identify you for campaign notifications, event invitations, contribution processing, and compliance with applicable campaign finance laws.
Log data When you visit our website, we may collect log data, including your computer’s internet protocol (IP) address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of visit, time spent on those pages, and other relevant statistics for security and site improvement purposes.
Third-party service providers We may engage third-party companies to host and manage our website, process donations and payments, and assist with campaign communication and analytics.
These third parties have access to your personal information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or misuse it.
Security We use commercially reasonable measures to protect your personal information.
However, no internet transmission or storage method is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Links to other websites Our website may contain links to external sites not operated by us.
We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those sites, as we have no control over and assume no responsibility for their content or practices.
Children's privacy Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
SMS / text messaging privacy policy By providing your mobile number and opting in, you consent to receive campaign-related SMS communications from Greg Abdouch for CA Assembly – District 45.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
To stop receiving messages, reply STOP at any time.
For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at (909) 371-5675.
Your mobile number and any related data will only be used to transmit SMS communications and will not be shared, sold, or used for unrelated purposes, except as required by law or to protect our legal rights.
Compliance disclosure We may disclose your information to comply with campaign finance regulations, government requests, or to protect our campaign's legal rights and obligations.
Changes to this privacy policy We may update this privacy policy periodically.
Any changes will be posted on this page and are effective immediately upon posting.
Contact us If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or your data, please contact Greg Abdouch for CA Assembly – District 45 at (909) 371-5675 or via the contact information on this website.
