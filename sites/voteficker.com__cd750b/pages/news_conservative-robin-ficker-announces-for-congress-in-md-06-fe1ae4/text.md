BOYDS, MD – Today, conservative Robin Ficker is launching his campaign for Congress in
Maryland’s Sixth Congressional District, his long-time home.
Republicans lost Maryland’s Sixth by just over six percentage points and less than 24,000 votes in 2024, while being outspent nearly 6-1.
Robin Ficker pledges to seed his race with a seven-figure contribution to ensure that he can go toe-to-toe with the Democrat machine.
Robin Ficker announced his campaign with the following statement:
“I’m running for Congress because Western Maryland deserves a fighter—someone who will stand up for our Sixth District and stand strong with President Trump.
For too long, radical liberal elites in Washington and Annapolis have ignored us while our jobs have vanished.
That ends today.
I’m committed to bringing jobs back to Western Maryland, widening I-270 to ease our traffic, and working alongside President Trump to slash spending, grow our economy, and keep our communities safe and prosperous.
As your Congressman, I will never vote to raise your taxes or increase the deficit.
I’ll work to cut red tape and simplify regulations, making it easier to start and run a business.
Together, we’ll bring high-paying energy and manufacturing jobs back to our region.
We will no longer let Western Maryland take a back seat to the Green New Deal agenda.
The upcoming midterms will be a crucial test for President Trump’s vision, and he needs a real fighter in Maryland who will stand by him.
I’ll stand with President Trump every step of the way to make Western Maryland great again.
The first step is securing the border for good, supporting ICE to deport criminal illegal immigrants, and cracking down on fentanyl trafficking in our communities.
I am the only candidate with a proven record, the trust of voters, and the resources to win this race.
I’m ready to fight, and I’m ready to deliver on day one.”
Conservative Robin Ficker is a tireless advocate for Western Maryland, legendary sports fan, fiscal hawk, and day-one Trump supporter who is running for Congress to deliver a majority for President Trump, secure the border for good, cut the deficit, fix transportation issues, and bring jobs to Western Maryland.
