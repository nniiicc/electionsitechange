With the midterm election just weeks away, the two candidates vying for Virginia’s Sixth Congressional District are making their case to voters.
Republican incumbent Ben Cline addressed a crowd at the Salem Rotary Club, while the Democratic Challenger Beth Macy met with members of Spectrum, a group that has advocated for racial justice in the Roanoke Valley for decades.
