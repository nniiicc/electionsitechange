After months of redistricting uncertainty, the general election for Virginia’s 6th Congressional District is set with just one Democrat and one Republican in the race after this week’s filing deadline.
Incumbent Republican Rep.
Ben Cline will face off against Beth Macy, who is the only Democrat left after several challengers dropped out of the race in recent weeks.
