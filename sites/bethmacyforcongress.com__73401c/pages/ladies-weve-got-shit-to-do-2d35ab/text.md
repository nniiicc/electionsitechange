Cline also refused to support a bill outlawing child marriage.
And the SAVE Act?
That could make it harder for women to vote if they took their husband’s last name at marriage, along with putting up many other barriers to make voting harder.
How else to interpret this other than Congress wants to make it harder for women to vote?
