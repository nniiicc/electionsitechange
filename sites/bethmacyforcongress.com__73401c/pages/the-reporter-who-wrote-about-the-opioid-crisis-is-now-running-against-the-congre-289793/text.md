For 25 years, Beth Macy’s job was to ask other people uncomfortable questions.
On Nov. 3, voters in Virginia’s 6th Congressional District will decide whether she gets to ask them from a seat in Congress.
Macy, the Roanoke author whose book “Dopesick” reshaped the national understanding of the opioid epidemic and became an Emmy- and Peabody-winning Hulu series, is the Democratic nominee against four-term Republican Rep.
Ben Cline of Botetourt County.
