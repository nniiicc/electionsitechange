Republican congressman Ben Cline announced this week his intention to run for a fifth term representing Virginia’s sixth congressional district in the U.S.
House of Representatives.
Standing in his way is Roanoke-based author Beth Macy, who wrote the New York Times bestsellers Dopesick and Factory Man.
“I think I’m the candidate for this moment,” said Macy.
“I’m a fighter for this region, and I’m not going to back down.”
