Hi Friends!
We kick off our gorgeous Virginia fall Monday with a big political tradition – the Buena Vista Labor Day Parade.
I’m looking forward to the hand waves, hugs and conversations about where the heck this country is going – and why I believe I can best serve you in the 6th District.

Look for more of my town halls this fall where we can talk and learn from each other.
Hope to see you in the 6th!

WHERE BETH HAS BEEN
HOT SPRINGS: We had such an amazing turnout (almost 140 people!) at my visit to the Hot Springs Fire Department and Rescue Squad in Bath County.
With friend and ally, U.S.
Sen.
Mark Warner, we held a productive discussion of the issues facing our communities in the 6th District.
WOODSTOCK: During my stop at the Shenandoah Valley County Fair, cattleman Scott Helsley and his daughter Bayleigh shared their concerns over the future of farming.
Of Trump’s plan to import beef, Scott said, “I think it’s a crock.” When I am in Washington, I will work to give our farmers the support they need – not to try to ruin their livelihoods.
VERONA: While visiting a free mobile clinic I met Staunton’s Stephen Ritchie, who lost his USAID contractor job a year ago, along with his health insurance, because of DOGE.
“The job market’s so tough right now; thousands of people will apply for one job, and AI does all the screening,” even some of the interviews.
“I have a master’s degree, and I speak four languages,” he told me.
“Getting down is the worst part.” Asked how he stays upbeat, Ritchie said, “I volunteer at the local animal shelter.” Then he proudly whipped out a picture of the couple’s new little rescue mutt on his phone.
I will fight to repeal the One Ugly Bill, especially the cruel Medicaid and SNAP cuts, and to enact Medicare For All so that hard-working people and the disabled can continue to get care that is cost-effective and humane.
