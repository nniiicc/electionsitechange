Why you should vote for me?
- I am a Veteran, union member, and small business owner investing in a better future for the residents of Missouri.
I want a future based on the interests of the people, to benefit the people as our area expands and diversifies.
The Union Hall and the Marine Corps provided foundational values including honesty, commitment, and the importance of community.
As we see district 41 expand, we need more representation of the interests and benefits of hard working men and women who make up our Community; many of whom are union members and small business owners.
As a small business owner in our community, I recognize the importance of our voices and the role we have in securing our future.
Our interests, voices and needs are aligned to better our lives by protecting our common goals.
- The concerns of any representative should be based on the concerns and needs of the people being represented, giving them a voice in their government.
Many people in the community are Veterans, Union Members, agricultural experts, and parents of children in the surrounding school districts.
As a Veteran, I have observed the need for change in Veteran healthcare on the state level as it may not accurately reflect the needs of our residents who are Veterans.
As a Union Member, it is frustrating to see the same issues being brought to ballot over and over again in contradiction to the voice of the residents.
As a small business owner I see changes in state taxes which could negatively impact the ability of small businesses in our area to continue to grow.
- My main goal is to provide a voice to the hardworking residents in Missouri District 41.
I aim to engage with as many residents as I can to learn more about the issues of priority which our local community is concerned about.
I aim to bridge the gap between politics and voter to better serve the residents of Missouri District 41.
Where is Missouri House District 41?
As of 2023 The Missouri House District 41 consists of the areas around Troy, Moscow Mills, Cave, and Fountain N’ Lakes, in Lincoln County Missouri.
In order to check if you are within District 41, please review the resources for a closer look at the areas included.
