Re-elect Steve Malagari
State Representative of PA’s 53rd District
Serving Lansdale, Hatfield, Souderton, Telford, and Franconia & Montgomery Townships
Priorities
Protecting Choice
Steve believes that access to reproductive care, including family planning, IVF, and abortion services, should be a fundamental right.
Defending Democracy
Our right to vote and hold fair elections is under attack.
Steve is fighting to defend these rights.
Fighting Rising Costs
Steve is fighting to protect working families and seniors from rising costs.
He supports fair wages, lower costs for health care and medicine, and property tax relief.
Meet Steve Malagari
Steve Malagari has represented the 53rd district in Harrisburg since being first elected in 2018.
He’s been a tireless, passionate advocate for the places and people that he knows so well.
Steve has lived in our district since birth; he knows us, and we know him.
He shares our values and has worked hard to support his community – our community.
Whether working for fair wages, to support seniors, to improve our roads and bridges, or to support our schools, Steve has been there for us.
