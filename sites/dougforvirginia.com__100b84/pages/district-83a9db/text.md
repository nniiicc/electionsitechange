Virginia’s 7th Congressional District
The following counties and city make up Virginia’s 7th Congressional District:
All of:
- Culpeper County
- Orange County
- Spotsylvania County
- Greene County
- Madison County
- Stafford County
- King George County
- Caroline County
Part of:
- Prince William County (southeastern half)
- Albemarle County (a small sliver)
Independent City:
- Fredericksburg
