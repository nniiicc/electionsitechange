If they win, Zayas would be the first Latina to ever serve on the high court, and Jamison would be the third Black woman in Ohio state Supreme Court history.
The Democrats will gain control of the court if either Zayas or Jamison wins, which will come in handy if (or really, when ) Governor DeWine and the Republican-controlled state legislature try to push more abortion restrictions through." While neither candidate has declared how they will rule on issues that come before the Court, Cosmopolitan and many other organizations across the country have voiced concern that Fischer, Jamison's opponent, has openly opined that there is no right of abortion in the Constitution.
