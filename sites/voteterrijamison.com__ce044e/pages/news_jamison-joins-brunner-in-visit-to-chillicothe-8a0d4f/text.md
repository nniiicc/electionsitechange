Home About Terri Biography Judicial Philosophy Volunteer Email Signup News Events Voter Info Endorsements Donate to Judge Jamison News / Jamison Joins Brunner in Visit to Chillicothe 31 May Tuesday, 9:24 AM · 2022 Jamison Joins Brunner in Visit to Chillicothe Joining a justice for a road trip is an exciting event, but Terri Jamison and Jennifer Brunner have known each other for a long time so it's a trip they might take anytime.
But this time's different as Jamison is joining Brunner, not only for a visit to Chillicothe, but on the road to the Ohio Supreme Court.
"If all goes well, Jamison said, I'll be joining her as an Associate Justice after the election on November 8th." The two were paired while Jamison was in law school at Capital through the women's law association.
Brunner, also a CapLaw grad, agreed to mentor a student and Jamison was the one.
