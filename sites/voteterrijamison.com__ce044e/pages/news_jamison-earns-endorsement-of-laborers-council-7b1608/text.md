Ralph Cole stated about Jamison, "Your willingness to educate yourself on our membership and purpose, along with your continued leadership on Ohio's 10th District Court of Appeals has earned our endorsement.
On behalf of the working families of the Ohio Laborers' District Council, you have our support and our pledge to work for your election to Ohio's Supreme Court."
Jamison said, "I'm overwhelmed and humbled with the outpouring of support and the Labor Council's endorsement for my campaign.
Earning this endorsement goes a long way toward bringing the election home for all Ohioans to have Equal Justice Under the Law."
