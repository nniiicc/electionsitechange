Register to Vote | Update Your Registration
To register to vote or to update your voter registration information, go to the Ohio Secretary of State webpage located here https://olvr.ohiosos.gov/
You must register or update your voter registration (if you moved, changed your name, or have not voted in recent elections) no later than 30 days prior to an election.
If you register or update your information after the deadline, the change will apply for the next election.
