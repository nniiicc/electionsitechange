Judge Jamison is currently a judge on the Ohio Court of Appeals, Tenth Appellate District seated in Columbus, Ohio.
With her combined service on the Court of Appeals and the Franklin County Domestic Relations-Juvenile Branch, she has more than 12 years of experience.
She is a success story.
As a single parent - nontraditional student, she went to Franklin University, graduating with a B.S. in Business Administration, cum laude, and Capital University Law School, graduating with a Juris Doctor.
Her work history began in the West Virginia Department of Welfare as a day care provider trainer, eligibility worker and fraud investigator; but includes being a former coal miner and member of United Mine Workers of America; a small business owner for more than 16 years; private practitioner who stood beside families in crisis, not corporations or special interest groups; and, hearing officer for the State of Ohio UCRC where she paid fair share in OCSEA.
She's a community leader who received a Doctorate in Community Leadership, hon. causa.; a member of numerous community and legal organizations, on the Franklin University Advisory Board for Global Healthcare Education, Capital University Law School Board of Counselors, a Blood Ambassador for Red Cross; and, she's giving back through the Judge Terri Jamison Scholarship for Single Parents and recognizing Hidden Figures within our community who work diligently to create opportunities for others.
She served as a subject matter expert in the following documentaries: PUSHOUT:Criminalization of Black Girls in School and the "Walking Logo".
