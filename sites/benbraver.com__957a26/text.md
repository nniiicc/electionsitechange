That's why Ben's motto is simple: Be Braver.
Ben grew up in Hillsborough County going to Benito Middle School, King High School, and USF (Go Bulls!).
He studied economics, interned for Congresswoman Kathy Castor, and directed State & Local policy for the Young Democrats of America.
After graduating, Ben's experience teaching at Title 1 schools showed him exactly what families need.
He'll fight for fully funded public schools , Universal Pre-K, and free public college so Florida keeps its talent in state.
Ben with community volunteers Ben connecting with voters Ben knows firsthand how insurance companies obstruct care from watching his mother, a physician, spend hours every day on insurance paperwork instead of patient care.
He understands how a universal healthcare system would save families thousands a year.
Ben is a strong advocate for the environment and against energy-sucking data centers.
When DeSantis attacked student privacy and took over colleges, Ben led thousands in a state-wide protest, appearing on MSNBC and NPR.
As a proud Jewish man he uplifts his Jewish Values of Tikkun O'lam and will redirect 350 Million in state funds from Israel back into our neighborhoods.
Ben doesn't take corporate money.
His policy platform is built around ending the corporate capture of Florida's government by improving affordable housing, workers' rights, and clean energy.
At a time when prices rise and services shrink — we need bolder policy, we need to Be Braver .
