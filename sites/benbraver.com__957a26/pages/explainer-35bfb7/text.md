As an Economics graduate and teacher running for Florida State House District 65, Ben is eager to fight against DeSantis's harmful policies that are making life harder and more expensive for working families across our state.
Ben believes economics should work for everyone, not just the wealthy elite.
His experience teaching in Title 1 schools—where many students qualify for free or reduced-price lunch—showed him daily how economic inequality affects children's ability to learn and thrive.
With his teaching background and degree in Economics, he breaks down how DeSantis's tax cuts for corporations and attacks on workers are driving up costs while cutting essential services that families depend on.
Ben's mission is clear: use smart economic policy to lower costs, create good jobs, and build an economy that actually serves working people.
As a teacher in Title 1 schools, Ben witnessed firsthand how underfunding devastates classrooms and hurts the families who need support most.
When DeSantis launched his devastating cuts to education funding, Ben didn't just watch—he organized massive resistance across every Florida public university, mobilizing thousands of students and educators to fight back.
His classroom experience with struggling families combined with his economic expertise gives him unique insight into how policy affects real people.
His fearless advocacy has earned him platforms on CNN, MSNBC, and NPR, where he exposes how Republican policies hurt families' wallets and champions economic solutions that put people first.
