Information We Collect We may collect the following categories of personal information: Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and ZIP code or mailing address.

Volunteer & Engagement Data: Areas of interest, volunteer availability, event participation, and campaign engagement history.
Communications: Messages, inquiries, survey responses, and other information you provide when contacting the campaign.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Send campaign updates, event invitations, fundraising appeals, and other political communications by email, phone, or SMS.
Coordinate volunteer activities and respond to your questions or requests.
Process donations securely through our payment processor.
Comply with applicable federal and Florida campaign finance reporting requirements.
Analyze and improve our website, communications, and campaign outreach.
Send recurring automated SMS messages related to campaign updates, voter outreach, volunteer opportunities, fundraising, and election reminders.

Email Communications By providing your email address, you consent to receive campaign emails from Ben Braver for Florida House District 65.

Sharing of Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share your information only in the following circumstances: Service Providers: Trusted vendors who assist with fundraising, email delivery, text messaging, website hosting, analytics, and campaign operations, all subject to confidentiality obligations.
Legal Compliance: As required under applicable federal, state, or local campaign finance laws.
Legal Process: When required by subpoena, court order, or other applicable law.
We do not share your personal information with third-party commercial advertisers.
Data Retention We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary to: Operate the campaign; Comply with applicable campaign finance and legal requirements; and Resolve disputes or enforce our legal obligations.
Security We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Children's Privacy Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can promptly remove it.
Your Rights & Choices Subject to applicable law, you may: Request access to the personal information we maintain about you.
Request correction of inaccurate information.
Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal recordkeeping requirements.
Opt out of campaign emails or text messages at any time.
To exercise these rights, contact us at Contact@BenBraver.com .
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically.
