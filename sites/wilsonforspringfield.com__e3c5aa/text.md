Adam Wilson
Join Springfield's Working Class Comeback
Adam is a sixth-generation Oregonian, lifelong Lane County resident, small business owner, and proud father of three.
Growing up, Adam learned hard work firsthand swinging a hammer and changing tires.
Now he's a financial advisor, helping families balance their budgets.
Adam is running for Oregon's 7th State House District to bring fiscal responsibility, common sense, and practical leadership to Salem for Springfield families.
The Fighter Springfield Deserves
- Affordable Housing for Oregonians Reduce regulatory building costs and keep single-family homes for working class families by stopping out-of-state investment firms from buying up local homes.
- Holding Government Accountable Adam’s fiscal expertise and small business leadership will ensure our tax dollars are spent wisely fixing roads, keeping us safe, improving K-12 education, and holding government accountable before the state ever asks us for more money.
- Keeping Data Centers in Check Require big corporations to bring their own power, pay their fair share of taxes, stay out of communities that do not want them, and ensure local families do not pay higher utilities.
- Training & Restoring Our Workforce Prioritize Career and Technical Education and fix our unemployment system so people can get back to work—not stuck in a broken process.
- Real Solutions for Homelessness and Crime Repeal House Bill 3115 to restore local control so Springfield can clear homeless camps.
Adam will propose legislation to end open-air drug use and give local police the authority to ensure jail or treatment for repeat offenders.
