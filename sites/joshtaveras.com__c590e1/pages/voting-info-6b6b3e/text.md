Voting Resource #1
Your last chance to vote is on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3.
Polls will be open from 6 am to 9 pm.
To vote on Election Day, you need:
- Use an accepted form of ID
- The New York Voter Protection Hotline (866) 390-2992, for any questions you may have
On Election Day you must vote at your assigned precinct.
Some polling locations may change; make sure to check your polling location before heading to the polls on November 3!
Stay hydrated and bring a snack.
