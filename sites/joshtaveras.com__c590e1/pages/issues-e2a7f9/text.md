Where I Stand On The
Issues
Focused on the issues that matter to New York Senate District 8
-
Affordable Housing For All Long Island families are struggling to find homes they can afford.
I will push for policies that increase affordable housing, support renters, and help families stay in their communities—because no one should have to choose between rent and food.
-
Healthcare for All Healthcare is a human right.
I will fight to ensure every Long Islander has access to quality, affordable healthcare, including mental health services, preventative care, and prescription coverage, so families can focus on living, not worrying about medical bills.
-
Education is Fundamental Every child deserves access to a quality education, no matter their zip code or family income.
I will fight for stronger schools, modern classrooms, and programs that prepare students for the real world, ensuring Long Island’s kids can reach their full potential.
-
Clean Air, Clean Water Environmental protection is non-negotiable.
I will work to reduce pollution, protect our waterways, and invest in clean energy, ensuring that every Long Islander can breathe clean air and drink safe water.
-
Expanding Parks and Open Spaces Our communities need green spaces to thrive.
I support expanding and improving parks, trails, and public spaces so families, kids, and seniors have safe, beautiful places to gather, play, and connect with nature.
-
Abortion is Healthcare Reproductive rights are healthcare rights.
I believe that every person should have safe, legal, and accessible options for their own body and future.
I will protect these rights and fight attempts to restrict access in New York and across the country.
