Meet Donni
Donni Steele is a lifelong resident of Oakland County who has built her life, career, and public service around family, community, and hard work.
A small business owner and realtor for decades, Donni understands the challenges facing local families and businesses.
She began her public service as an Orion Township Trustee and Treasurer, where she helped guide growth and saved taxpayers money through fiscally responsible practices.
Today, Donni represents District 54 in the State House, where she has been a strong voice for fixing Michigan’s roads and addressing the priorities of her constituents.
Now, Donni is running for State Senate to continue fighting for the well-being and prosperity of Oakland County.

Priorities
As Chair of the Transportation Appropriations Subcommittee in the House, Donni has led efforts to fix Michigan’s roads—from driveway to highway—without raising taxes.
Her work on her committees—Appropriations, LARA, Public Health, and Capital Outlay—has been guided by one goal: delivering results for Michigan families.
Michigan faces multiple challenges: security and public safety, accountability, transparency, maintaining fiscal responsibility, and education.
Donni proposes revitalizing the economy by supporting small business growth through reduced regulation and fostering competitive environments.
Job retention and attracting talent remain priorities, along with addressing inflation and reevaluating energy policies to promote Michigan’s economic interests.
Donni strongly opposes defunding law enforcement and supports their crucial role in protecting public safety.
She believes education transparency is vital, with funds directed toward classroom learning and student support.
She also advocates robust support for veterans and seniors, opposing unfair pension taxation and ensuring comprehensive care and benefits.
Donni’s goal is to rejuvenate Michigan’s economy, retain its population, and enhance citizens’ well-being in the Senate.
Running for Michigan’s 23rd Senate District
Senate District 23 includes all of Addison Township, Almont Township, Atlas Township, Brandon Township, Clarkston, Dryden Township, Hadley Township, Highland Township, Independence Township, Metamora Township, Oakland Township, Orion Township, Oxford Township, and Rose Township, as well as part of Waterford Township.
