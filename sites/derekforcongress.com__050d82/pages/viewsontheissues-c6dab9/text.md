Read my full plan Housing and Cost of Living Wall street shouldn't be allowed to manipulate housing prices in our district the way they do.
They often use something called "comp manipulation".
Comp manipulation is when a large company purchases a few hundred homes in a given district or area, then sells a handful of them to a subsidiary company they also own for hundreds of thousands of dollars more than they just paid.
This can artificially increase the price of the other homes they own in that area because they now have sale comps that show an increase in "value".
This is a manipulative tactic hedge funds use to increase the value of homes on the books and thus drive their overall value up.
It's wrong and it should be illegal.
This type of manipulation drives up costs for everyone and makes regions of our country simply unaffordable for most families.
