Getting Big and Dark Money Out of Our Elections
Taking money from big corporations, billionaires, PACs and special interest groups is a choice, not a requirement.
If the democrats truly believed in ending citizens united, they would lead by example and simply refuse or ban big and dark money in their party.
Banning Stock Trading by Elected Officials
All elected officials and cabinet members should be banned from trading individual stocks so they can’t profit from insider knowledge.
The incumbent in district 5 has had multiple stock act violations and is described by google as a "prolific" stock trader in congress.
Ending Gerrymandering with Fair Districts
Districts will be drawn using Census data, AI, and simple square or rectangle shapes, so voters pick their leaders not the other way around.
No carve outs or loopholes that will benefit one party over the other.
Just straight forward fair maps across the country.
My beliefs about changing the status quo:
Over the last year as I began thinking about running for office, I found myself thinking a lot about why the "kitchen table" issues seem to never change here in America.
The more I thought about it and researched these issues I couldn't help but come to the same conclusion.
If we don't start by fixing the foundation of our government, how it is structured and how it operates then no changes will ever last and turn into the solutions we need.
The chain on the bike is broken and our politicians on both sides just want to change the tires and call it a fix.
We need to fix the chain first or nothing else will work as intended.
Our country needs bi-partisan legislation that provides solutions that will stand the test of time instead of partisan band-aides that appease the fringes of the two-party system while empowering the other fringe to dig in even more.
I know what I am trying to do here seems crazy, going up against the political machine with no political experience, no big financial backers and no name recognition but someone new needed to stand up and say enough is enough, so here I am.
I know it might be weird making political posts online for the first time in your life or having political discussions with friends or family that you've never talked with about these things before.
I'm asking you to get out of your comfort zone so we can enact real meaningful change to a system that really needs it.
I heard a saying a while back that says, "Everything you've ever wanted is just outside your comfort zone" and that saying rings so true with this moment right now.
So, I'm asking you to get out of your political comfort zone and help us bring true meaningful and lasting change to Washington.
