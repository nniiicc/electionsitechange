Joe Hohenstein is a Democrat serving as State Representative of Pennsylvania’s 177th Legislative District, which includes the neighborhoods of Bridesburg, Fishtown, Port Richmond, Northwood, Frankford, Kensington, Wissinoming, and Harrowgate.
First elected in 2018, Joe is seeking re-election so he can continue the work of educational, environmental, and economic justice for the 177th District, the City of Philadelphia, and the entire commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
