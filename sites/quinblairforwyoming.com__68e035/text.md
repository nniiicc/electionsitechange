Why Quin is Running
Quin Blair is running because too many people in Park County feel ignored by politics in Cheyenne.
Politics has become more focused on division and extreme agendas than on the people they're actually elected to represent.
As a business owner and father raising his children in the community that raised him, Quin believes House District 24 deserves leadership that listens, shows up, and focuses on practical Wyoming solutions.
This campaign is about restoring real, responsible representation, protecting the Wyoming way of life, and making sure local families have a strong voice in Cheyenne.
