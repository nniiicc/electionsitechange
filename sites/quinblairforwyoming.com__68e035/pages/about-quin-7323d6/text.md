Quin Blair is a Cody businessman, father, and community advocate running to represent Wyoming House District 24.
Quin is raising his children in the same community that raised him.
For him, Park County is not just where he lives.
It is where he works, serves, raises his family, and plans for the future.
He believes this community has given him a great deal, and running for office is one way to step up and give back.
Through his work with Blair Hotels, one of the largest private employers in Park County, Quin understands the challenges facing local businesses, working families, and the tourism economy that helps support so many jobs in the area.
He has spent his career helping promote Cody and Park County, working to strengthen the local economy while protecting the character and quality of life that make this place special.
Quin currently serves as Chairman of the Park County Travel Council and previously served as Vice Chair of the Wyoming Office of Tourism Board of Directors.
He has also served on the Cody Country Chamber of Commerce Board of Directors and has been involved in local fundraising efforts and community organizations, including the Cody Fireworks Committee, Buffalo Bill Art Show & Sale, Cody Culture Club, and PEAKS to Conga, which supports people battling cancer with non-medical financial assistance.
Quin is running because too many people in Park County feel ignored by politics in Cheyenne.
He believes House District 24 deserves representation that listens, shows up, and puts local families ahead of political agendas.
As a representative, Quin will focus on protecting public lands, defending constitutional freedoms, strengthening local schools, supporting responsible economic growth, making tourism work for the people who live here, and keeping government accountable to the taxpayers who fund it.
For Quin, public service starts with listening.
He is running to bring real, responsible representation back to House District 24 and make sure Park County has a strong voice in Cheyenne.
