| Defending the 2nd Amendment |
| |
| The 2nd amendment should be defended without question or apology.
It is a foundational safeguard written to ensure that the balance of power always includes the people.
Any attempt to weaken or reinterpret it away from its original meaning should be met with serious resistance in the civic and legislative process.
The right to bear arms is a constitutional guarantee rooted in the founders’ understanding of liberty and self-governance.
History shows that when fundamental rights are treated as flexible, they are at risk of being lost.
That is why preserving this right is essential to preserving all other rights that depend on it. |
