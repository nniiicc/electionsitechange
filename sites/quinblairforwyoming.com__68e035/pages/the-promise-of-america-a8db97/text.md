Happy Independence Day!
This is one of my favorite holidays of the year.
Between barbecues, fundraising for fireworks, and watching them with pride, I always take a moment to reflect on the meaning behind the celebration and feel grateful for the country I call home.
Today, I want to share what Independence Day and the principles it represents mean to me.
The Principles of Freedom I believe that preserving the liberties guaranteed by our Constitution is one of our greatest responsibilities as Americans.
The principles of personal freedom, limited government, and individual responsibility were instilled in me from a young age and shaped the values I carry today.
I have worked to pass those same ideals on to my children, teaching them the importance of protecting the freedoms generations of Americans have fought to preserve.
Our constitutional rights are not simply traditions to be remembered, but commitments that must be defended and carried forward for future generations.
That commitment to safeguarding our freedoms was one of the reasons I chose to seek a seat in the legislature.
I believe elected officials have a duty to protect the rights of the people they represent and remain faithful to the principles on which our nation was founded.
Whether I am fighting to strengthen property rights, standing with our veterans who sacrificed for our country, or defending our cherished Second Amendment rights, I approach every issue with that responsibility in mind.
I will continue working to honor the vision of our Founding Fathers by ensuring that the freedoms we enjoy today remain secure for generations to come.
This is where I live.
This is where I'm raising my family and building my future.
I'm running to make sure the people of House District 24 have someone in Cheyenne who never forgets that.
I'd be honored to have your support.
