Jorge H.
Figueroa is a respected business and community leader, entrepreneur, and advocate for economic opportunity across Central Florida.
Born in San Juan, Puerto Rico, and raised in the Villa Esperanza public housing community of Río Piedras, Jorge learned early the value of hard work and perseverance.
At sixteen, he moved to Wisconsin to learn English and later earned a Bachelor’s degree in Finance and Spanish from the University of Wisconsin–Parkside, followed by an MBA in Global Management from the University of Phoenix.
Jorge’s professional background encompasses finance, higher education, and marketing.
In 2017, he founded Omicron Business Group (JF Media LLC), a full-service marketing agency that helps small businesses expand through strategic media, branding, and community partnerships.
The company later expanded to create OBG Center, a business hub designed to support small and medium-sized enterprises in scaling their operations, developing cohesive business structures, and accessing professional office, meeting, and coworking spaces.
To further enhance its marketing capabilities, The Agency Promotion was launched as the evolution of JF Media, continuing its mission of driving business growth and visibility.
As President of the Puerto Rican Chamber of Commerce of Central Florida, Jorge has led efforts to strengthen the region’s small business ecosystem, support minority entrepreneurs, and connect the Hispanic community to new economic opportunities.
Under his leadership, the Chamber grew substantially, particularly during the pandemic, becoming a vital resource for business resilience and recovery.
Grounded in his Christian faith, Jorge believes public service should reflect the command to “love thy neighbor.” In a difficult chapter decades ago, it became a defining moment in his spiritual journey.
God used that moment to reshape Jorge’s life and revealed to him what faith and redemption truly mean.
The experience deepened his commitment to grace, integrity, and second chances.
That commitment has shaped his approach to leadership – uniting people, solving problems, and opening doors for others.
Jorge also served on the Osceola County Housing Finance Authority, and serves the American Business Immigration Coalition’s Business Advisory Board, where he advocates for common-sense sense bipartisan immigration and workforce policies.
Grounded in faith and family, Jorge lives in Kissimmee with his wife, Bilda Acevedo, and their daughters, Lemishka Rodríguez and Aneishka González.
He is running for the Florida House to address the affordability crisis, reform Florida’s broken property insurance system, and expand pathways to economic mobility for families across Central Florida.
