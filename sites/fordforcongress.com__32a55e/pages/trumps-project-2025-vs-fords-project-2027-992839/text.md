Ford’s Project 2027 By Ford for Congress Campaign Team / January 13, 2026 Trump’s Project 2025 vs.
Ford’s Project 2027 A Clear Plan to Protect Democracy and Move Us Forward Trump already put his plan into motion.
Now we see the damage.
Project 2025 is not theory.
It’s a blueprint that has already weakened norms, diminished accountability, and pushed power toward the presidency at the expense of everyday Americans.
Project 2027 is my framework for real congressional action — to block further erosion, roll back harm already done, and protect the freedoms and fairness that make America strong.
1.
Defending the Civil Service and Independent Institutions Trump’s Threat Trump and his allies have already moved to weaken career civil service protections and replace nonpartisan professionals with political loyalists through Schedule F.
Project 2025 is designed to make this permanent, putting political loyalty above public service.
My Plan I am running to fight for a permanent ban on Schedule F-style classifications.
I will work to strengthen protections for whistleblowers, inspectors general, and federal scientists, and push to reinforce the independence of the Office of Special Counsel.
“We believe in a government that works for the people, not one hijacked by political operatives.” 2.
Preserving the Rule of Law and DOJ Independence Trump’s Threat The independence of the Department of Justice has already been tested through political pressure, public attacks on prosecutors, and efforts to undermine impartial enforcement of the law.
Project 2025 seeks to formalize political control over federal law enforcement.
My Plan I am running to fight for clear legal protections that safeguard DOJ independence.
I will work to advance legislation that blocks political interference and push for strong congressional oversight when justice is abused for political ends.
“Justice must serve the law, not the president.” 3.
Restoring Checks and Balances Trump’s Threat Executive power has already been stretched beyond constitutional guardrails through abuse of emergency powers, disregard for oversight, and erosion of checks and balances.
Project 2025 aims to lock in that concentration of power.
My Plan I am running to restore accountability.
I will fight for meaningful congressional oversight of major executive actions and push for transparency and legal justification whenever power is exercised.
“No president should be above the law or beyond the reach of the people.” 4.
Protecting Immigrant Families and Refugee Protections Trump’s Threat Families have already been separated, asylum protections weakened, and detention expanded.
Project 2025 seeks to normalize mass deportations and eliminate humanitarian safeguards.
My Plan I am running to protect immigrant families.
I will work to uphold asylum protections, limit ICE overreach, invest in community-based alternatives to detention, and oppose federal efforts to punish compassionate communities.
“We protect families, not cages.” 5.
Safeguarding Reproductive Freedom and Gender Equality Trump’s Threat Roe v.
Wade has already been overturned, and access to reproductive healthcare has already been restricted.
Project 2025 aims to make these losses permanent and expand them nationwide.
My Plan I am running to fight for federal protections for reproductive freedom.
I will work to codify Roe v.
Wade, protect access to contraception and IVF, and expand family planning services.
“Decisions about your body belong to you, not the government.” 6.
Defending LGBTQ+ Equality Trump’s Threat Federal protections for LGBTQ+ Americans have already been rolled back, and discrimination has been encouraged through policy and rhetoric.
Project 2025 would eliminate remaining safeguards.
My Plan I am running to fight for full equality under the law.
I will work to advance the Equality Act, restore nondiscrimination protections, and support programs that protect LGBTQ+ youth.
“We don’t erase people.
We protect them.” 7.
Protecting Voting Rights and Democracy Trump’s Threat Voting rights have already been weakened through court decisions, state suppression laws, and election interference.
Project 2025 would further limit federal protections and oversight.
My Plan I am running to protect the right to vote.
I will fight to pass the John R.
Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act and support national standards that make voting accessible and secure.
“Every voice must count.
Every vote must be safe.” 8.
Defending Public Education and Truth Trump’s Threat Public education has already been targeted through censorship, underfunding, and attacks on inclusive curriculum.
Project 2025 would escalate these efforts and push privatization.
My Plan I am running to defend public education.
I will oppose federal censorship, fight to expand funding for public schools, and push back against voucher schemes that drain classrooms.
“We fund truth, not fear.” 9.
Advancing Climate Responsibility and Environmental Justice Trump’s Threat Environmental protections have already been rolled back, and climate science undermined.
Project 2025 would strip agencies of authority and reverse progress.
My Plan I am running to protect our future.
I will fight for clean energy standards, invest in environmental justice, and support solutions that create jobs and protect public health.
“Clean air.
Clean water.
A livable future.” 10.
Defending Public Health and Science Trump’s Threat Public health institutions have already been politicized, weakening trust and preparedness.
Project 2025 would further undermine science-based decision-making.
My Plan I am running to restore trust in science and public health.
I will fight to protect the independence of health agencies, expand preparedness, and ensure transparent communication.
“Science saves lives.
We listen to it.” 11.
Protecting Digital Rights and Truth Trump’s Threat Surveillance has expanded while misinformation has flourished.
Project 2025 seeks to weaken safeguards and target dissent.
My Plan I am running to protect privacy and free expression.
I will work to advance digital privacy protections, support independent journalism, and promote digital literacy.
“Freedom of speech.
Freedom from manipulation.” Project 2027: A Path Forward Project 2027 is not about one person fixing everything.
It is a clear plan for how we push back, protect our rights, and move forward together.
The damage is already underway.
The choice now is whether we accept it or confront it.
I am running for Congress to fight for that future.
La Shawn K.
Ford Candidate for Congress, Illinois’ 7th District Project 2027 is a governing framework proposed by La Shawn K.
Ford, reflecting Democratic values and constitutional principles.
