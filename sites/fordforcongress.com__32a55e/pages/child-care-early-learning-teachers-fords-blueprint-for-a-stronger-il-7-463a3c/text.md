Ford’s Proven Record in Springfield Real Wins for Children, Families & Providers During his tenure in the Illinois General Assembly, La Shawn K.
Ford helped shape nearly every major victory for early childhood education and child care in the state.
His leadership has directly impacted hundreds of thousands of families across Illinois, particularly in communities that rely on affordable, reliable care to work, learn, and rebuild.
Key Accomplishments Include: Restored Child Care Funding: Reversed devastating cuts to child care programs and protected access for working families.
Gave Providers a Seat at the Table: Created the Illinois Commission on Child Care so providers have a direct voice in shaping policy.
Expanded Eligibility for Families: Helped more working parents qualify for high-quality, affordable child care.
Lowered Family Co-Pays: Reduced out-of-pocket costs so families could keep more of their hard-earned income.
Expanded Preschool for All: Opened access for more children to enter school ready to learn and succeed.
Invested in the Workforce: Increased scholarships, training opportunities, and professional development for early educators.
Raised Reimbursement Rates: Helped child care centers stay open and pay staff fair, competitive wages.
Ford did not just support these policies.
