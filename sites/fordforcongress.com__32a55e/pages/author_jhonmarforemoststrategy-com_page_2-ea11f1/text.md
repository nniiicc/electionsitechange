Women for Ford – A Governing Platform
Women for Ford is about real leadership, not symbolism.
I was raised by a strong mother and I am raising a daughter of my own.
I understand that women face different challenges at every stage of life, as students, workers, mothers, caregivers, and seniors.
My record shows that I have delivered for women before, and in Congress I will fight to protect women’s rights, healthcare, economic security, and dignity with action, accountability, and law.
I have stood with women in Illinois, and I am ready to stand with women in Washington.
