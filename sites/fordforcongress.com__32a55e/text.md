FORD 19 Years of Public Service & Community Focused Lawmaking La Shawn K.
Ford was born to a teenage mother in challenging circumstances in the Cabrini-Green housing projects of Chicago.
Adopted by his grandmother, Ford’s family later moved to the West Side’s Austin community, where they instilled in him the importance of hard work and education.
After facing numerous challenges, including a house fire, he developed a strong work ethic, earning money from shoveling snow, mowing lawns, collecting bottles and cans for recycling, and stocking produce in the local community grocery store.
