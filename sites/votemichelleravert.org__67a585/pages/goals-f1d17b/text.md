PROMOTE ENTREPREURSHIP
Michelle Ravert knows that the key to economic growth, while maintaing our rural culture, is entreprenurship.
Bringing big industry in or building warehouses in District 36 does not bring econimic growth.
She will create programs to help constituents identify marketable skills, obtain low-interest financing, learn the ins and outs of small business, market and grow their business, which will in turn grow the local economy.
The economy grows when people succeed.
EXECUTE PLAN for affordable housing in District 36
Michelle Ravert spent 25 years developing private equity funds that have made the rich richer, increased rent, and made home ownership out of reach for many.
She knows that government can create the same types of funds (REITS) that focus on capital preservation and redevelopment rather than profit and new construction.
By leveraging public services, rents will be lower and housing will become more affordable throughout the district.
Economic growth should not be measured by new construction, but by home ownership.
