PERSONAL 30-year resident of Chesapeake City Two children who attended public schools K-12 Grandchildren currently attend public schools PROFESSIONAL Current Parole & Probation Agent in Kent County Former Legal Secretary in the Office of the Public Defender Former Managing Director of a Maryland State Agency 25 years of professional experience in Investment Management, rising to the level of Vice President PUBLIC SERVICE 20+ years of volunteer work with the Boy and Girl Scouts in Cecil County Served on the Board of Directors for two local non-profits EDUCATION 2021-A.A. in General Studies, Cecil College (4.0 GPA) 2022-B.S. in Legal Studies (Pre-Law) with a minor in Political Science, University of Maryland Global Campus (Summa Cum Laude) WHY AM I RUNNING FOR OFFICE?
TO BRING THE PEOPLE AND GOVERNMENT TOGETHER The people and all levels of government have become too far removed from each other.
We need to bring that back together.
People should not be working to pay for their government, the government should be working for the people!
TO REENGAGE DISENFRANCHISED VOTERS In the last decade, there were many races with unopposed candidates on the ballot, leaving the electorate feeling no need to vote, and even robbed of their vote.
Voters should have a choice every election and have a voice about who serves them in government.
TO BUILD COALITIONS AND SOLVE COMPLEX CHALLENGES It is amazing how much can be accomplished when people work together.
Engaging non-profits, state agencies, and the public enables cross-functional collaboration to holistically solve challenges.
I have met all four counties' commissions/council, as well as most of the municipal councils/commissions in the District.
We will work together to solve the challenges in District 36.
ENSURE STATE LAW FITS IN DISTRICT 36 Annapolis passes too many one-size-fits-all bills that just don't seem to fit in District 36.
I will advocate for amendment or exclusion to ensure all State legislation fits the counties, municipalities, and people of District 36.
TO ADDRESS THE NEEDS OF THE PEOPLE Do you know what the District 36 Delegates are doing in Annapolis?
