The Blueprint, although great in concept, has become just another unfunded mandate that is bringing some counties on the Eastern Shore to the brink of bankruptcy.
We need a voice in Annapolis to push back on the one-size fits-all legislation that just doesn't fit our District.
I have been traveling the District, attending County and Municipal Meetings, to understand the challenges brought on by legislation that has been forced upon them that just doesn't work here like it might on the other side of the Bay.
Things are different here and we need an advocate to shield our local governments financial harm.
As a Democrat, in the super-majority in the House of Delegates, I can be effective at advocating for amendments and/or excluding certain counties and/or municipalities, Our current delegation is relegated to the corner to sit and watch while Democrats go to work.
I implore you to elect a Democrat to work for you...to understand you...to advocate for you...and be your voice in Annapolis.
In the end, if you want anything done in Annapolis, you have to elect a Democrat.
