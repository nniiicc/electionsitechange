Some argue that cutting spending is the only way keep taxes in check.
We can keep taxes in check if we increase revenue through means other than taxes.
The State of Maine collects revenue from all 50 States and the District of Columbia and Maryland can too.
The next time you see a tractor trailer on the road, take note of the license plate on the trailer.
A large majority of trailers on U.S roads are registered in Maine.
Why?
Maine allows out-of-state residents and companies to register trailers and charges fees that are lower than home states.
Not only that, Owner Operators and Companies can register commercial or semi-trailers for up to 12 years, avoiding annual renewals.
To top it all off, there is no redundant safety inspection required.
State Inspections are completely duplicative since strict safety standards are set at the Federal Level.
Maryland can collect revenue from outside the State.
We just have to think outside of the box like Maine.
