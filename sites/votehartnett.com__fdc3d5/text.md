A Strong Voice for the Lowcountry
A Mount Pleasant native, small-business owner, and father of three,
Tom Hartnett brings a lifetime of Lowcountry experience to the South Carolina House.
As a member of the House Education and Public Works Committee and Chairman of the Motor Vehicles and Public Safety Subcommittee, Tom is working to keep our communities safe, improve our roads and infrastructure, strengthen education, and ensure the Lowcountry has a strong voice in Columbia.
KEEPING OUR COMMUNITIES SAFE
Supported pay increases for state law enforcement and $24.5 million to strengthen the Palmetto 800 communications system used by law enforcement, firefighters, and first responders.
IMPROVING ROADS & INFRASTRUCTURE
Helped advance $344 million for roads and bridges to improve infrastructure, address congestion, and keep pace with South Carolina's continued growth.
STRENGTHENING EDUCATION
Supported raising the minimum starting teacher salary to $50,500, along with additional investments in classrooms, school safety, buses, and school facilities.
