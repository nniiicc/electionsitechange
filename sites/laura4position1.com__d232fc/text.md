More interviews & appearances Meet Laura A lifetime of earning it Laura grew up in Wenatchee, graduated valedictorian, and put herself through college at Seattle Pacific University working at Ken’s Market on Queen Anne — graduating summa cum laude before going on to the University of Washington School of Law.
She has worked in some capacity since she was eleven years old.
She practiced family law for 30 years, serving as a Commissioner pro tem in Snohomish County Superior Court for 18 of them — where she was, in her own words, “immediately humbled at the scope of the workload involved.” She was a Rigos Bar Review lecturer teaching Family Law and Community Property.
She is married to Jason Colberg, and has two adult sons and a granddaughter.
