Contribution Limits
| Contributor | Per Election | Per Cycle (Primary + General) |
|---|---|---|
| Individual | $2,400 | $4,800 |
| Married Couple | $4,800 | $9,600 |
| PAC / Union / Business | $2,400 | $4,800 |
| Cash (any source) | $100 max (check required above $100) | |
WA Supreme Court is a statewide judicial race.
The $2,400 limit applies per election — the primary and general are counted separately, so the maximum per person per cycle is $4,800 ($9,600 per married couple).
Name, home address, employer, and occupation are required by WA law for contributors whose total exceeds $250.
Contributions are not tax-deductible.
