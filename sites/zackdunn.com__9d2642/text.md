TX - 121 Meet Zack Dunn Zack Dunn was raised in District 121 by two military parents who taught him the value of service over self - a principle that guided his life's decisions.
Now, he's running for office because District 121 deserves a pragmatic, reasonable representative that will fight for the residents of District 121 at the Capitol.
Read More > About The Issues Veteran Support With a deep respect for those who served, Dunn will push to improve physical and mental healthcare for veterans.
He’ll make access to critical services simpler, faster, and easier.
Economic Security Dunn is committed to attracting high-paying jobs, strengthening workforce development, and investing in vocational careers.
He’s focused on building seamless education-to-career pipelines so that families can build stable, thriving futures right here at home.
Community Safety Zack will fight to reduce sexual assault and domestic violence through common sense policy changes.
He will ensure our police, fire, and EMS have the resources they need to be safe and keep us safe Public Education As a product of federal and state public schools, Zack understands the importance of a quality public school education.
Funding our public schools and investing in our teachers are vital steps to ensuring the success of our children across the district.
