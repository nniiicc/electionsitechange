MEET ZACK
Zack Dunn was raised in District 121 by two military parents whose service and sacrifice instilled in him the value of service over self.
That principle has guided his life’s decisions and fuels his desire to serve the residents of District 121.
He attended Ronald Reagan High School before enrolling at UTSA, where he served as student body president.
During his college years, he worked for both the City of San Antonio and Bexar County and interned at the White House.
After graduating from UTSA, Zack attended the University of Pennsylvania Law School.
Following law school, he worked as a corporate attorney in private equity, handling high-stakes transactions where deal values regularly exceeded $10 billion.
He later brought that experience to public service as an attorney for the U.S.
Air Force, representing and supporting service members stationed domestically and deployed abroad.
He most recently served as a Special Victims Unit prosecutor in the Bexar County District Attorney’s Office, where his trial experience and daily work focused on protecting victims of sexual assault and domestic violence.
