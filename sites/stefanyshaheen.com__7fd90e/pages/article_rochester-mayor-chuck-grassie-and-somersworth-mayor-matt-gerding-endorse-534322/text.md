Stefany Shaheen has now been endorsed by every Democratic Mayor in New Hampshire’s First Congressional District
Rochester, N.H. – Citing her record of supporting small businesses and standing up for New Hampshire’s communities, Rochester Mayor Chuck Grassie and Somersworth Mayor Matt Gerding endorsed Stefany Shaheen in New Hampshire’s first congressional district primary.
Stefany visited local businesses in downtown Rochester with Mayor Grassie, where they spoke to small business owners about the challenges they’re facing as a result of the Trump Administration.
She also stopped by a diner and small businesses in Somersworth, where Mayor Gerding, former Somersworth Mayor George Bald, and City Councilor Bonnie McNair spoke about why they believe she is the strongest person to fight for New Hampshire’s first district in Congress.
With the endorsements of Mayors Grassie and Gerding, Stefany Shaheen now has the support of every Democratic Mayor in New Hampshire’s first congressional district, including Portsmouth Mayor Deaglan McEachern, and Dover Mayor Dennis Shanahan, as well as Portsmouth Assistant Mayor Joanna Kelley and numerous former Mayors and Assistant Mayors.
“People in Rochester elected Mayor Grassie because of his longstanding commitment to the City and the families and businesses who call it home.
Mayor Grassie shows up and always fights for Rochester.
Mayor Gerding is a dynamic and trusted force in Somersworth.
He knows what it takes to deliver meaningful change for people on issues like housing, education, or public safety.
I am honored to have their support, as well as the support of all the Democratic mayors in New Hampshire’s first congressional district,” said Stefany Shaheen.
“Right now Donald Trump and Congressional Republicans are crushing small businesses on Main Street while funding endless foreign wars instead of health care for American families.
Our communities need trusted leaders who will fight for lower costs, safer streets, stronger schools and better days ahead.
No one fights harder than a mom for her kids and that’s how I will fight for all New Hampshire families,” added Shaheen.
“When Stefany sees a problem that needs a solution she doesn’t wait around for someone to fix it for her – she jumps in and tackles it head on.
Now, as costs are rising higher and higher and families are struggling to make ends meet, we need a fighter like Stefany in Congress,” said Rochester Mayor Chuck Grassie.
“I know Stefany will work tirelessly to stand up for our communities.”
“Stefany is a lifelong Granite Stater, and has earned the trust of our state throughout her years of hard work.
People in Somersworth and throughout the state need someone who will stand up to President Trump and work tirelessly to address the challenges that families are facing every day,” said Mayor Matt Gerding.
“With Stefany in Congress, we know we will have a representative that has our back and will keep our best interests at heart.”
Stefany built a business from the ground up, giving her a deep, practical understanding of the challenges our entrepreneurs face daily.
She’ll fight relentlessly to roll back harmful tariffs and cut the bureaucratic red tape that’s holding our mom-and-pop shops back.
We need a proven leader in our corner who truly puts Main Street ahead of Wall Street monopolies, and Stefany is exactly the champion our small businesses deserve, said former Somersworth Mayor George Bald.
Stefany’s own experience on the City Council demonstrates her commitment to public service and has given her a first hand view of the daily challenges cities and towns across New Hampshire face, from managing tight budgets to investing in schools and infrastructure.
She’s got a proven record of bringing people together to solve complex problems and I know she’ll bring that same determined results-first approach to fighting for us in Congress.
We need a dedicated public servant like Stefany fighting for us, and I am proud to stand with her in this campaign,” said City Councilor Bonnie McNair.
In addition to the Democratic Mayors of New Hampshire’s first congressional district Stefany Shaheen has earned support from Teamsters Local 633, IBEW 490, former Governor John Lynch, environmental steward Dudley Dudley, former State Senators Jon Morgan and Tom Sherman, Testing for Pease Founder Andrea Amico, Mary Carey Foley, Portsmouth Police Chief Mark Newport, Strafford County Sheriff Kathryn Mone, as well as hundreds of local small business owners, elected officials and community leaders.
