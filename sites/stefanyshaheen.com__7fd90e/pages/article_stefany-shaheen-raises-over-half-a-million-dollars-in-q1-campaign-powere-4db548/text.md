Portsmouth, NH – More than 2,600 Granite Staters have contributed to Stefany Shaheen’s campaign for Congress, which helped her raise over $500,000 in the first 90 days of 2026.
Just this quarter, the campaign received more than 9,000 contributions and 95% of those contributions were $100 or less.
Stefany is not taking corporate PAC money.
She will report having over $1,100,000 on hand.
A reflection of the depth and breadth of her support, Stefany has raised more money from New Hampshire than any other candidate of either party, and has more New Hampshire donors than any other candidate in the NH-01 race.
Last quarter, she raised over two times more from New Hampshire than ALL the other Democratic primary candidates combined.
“Stefany Shaheen has dedicated her life to standing up for what’s right and her campaign is powered by thousands of Granite Staters who know she’ll fight tirelessly for them, the way moms fight for their kids,” said campaign manager Emma Greenberg.
“New Hampshire knows she is a fighter and she won’t back down—period.
Stefany grew up here, raised a family here, built a business here, and served on the City Council and Police Commission in her community.
She is sick of seeing Granite Staters get left behind while billionaires and corporate special interests get the big breaks.”
Stefany has consistently held a double digit lead in public polling.
She was up 33-10 in the latest poll from the University of New Hampshire.
Stefany Shaheen’s people-powered campaign is earning the support of Granite Staters in every corner of the state, including Teamsters Local 633, former Governor John Lynch, numerous mayors and former mayors, environmental steward Dudley Dudley, former State Senators Jon Morgan and Tom Sherman, Testing for Pease Founder Andrea Amico, Portsmouth Police Chief Mark Newport, as well as hundreds of local small business owners, elected officials and community leaders.
