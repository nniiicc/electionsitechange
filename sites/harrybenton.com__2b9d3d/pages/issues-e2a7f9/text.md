COST OF LIVING
Harry is fighting to make life more affordable for our families.
He supports banning hidden “junk fees” on goods and services and has worked to lower healthcare costs including banning price-gouging on essential medicines.
Harry is also working to expand affordable and accessible childcare.
EDUCATION
Harry believes all our kids deserve a world-class education, from early childhood to college or career training.
He is pushing for more funding for local public schools to reduce our reliance on property taxes and to strengthen investment in vocational education.
HARRY HAS ALSO BEEN A FIERce ADVOCATE FOr SPECIAL EDUCATION AND THOSE WITH DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES.
VETERANS
HARRY IS ADAMANT IN ENSURING THAT WE TAKE CARE OF OUR VETERANS.
AS A BOARD MEMBER OF VETERANS OUTREACH OF ILLINOIS, HE IS INSTRUMENTAL IN WORKING TO GET A TINY-HOME COMMUNITY BUILT FOR HOMELESS AND TRANSITIONING VETERANS.
AS STATE REPRESENTATIVE, HARRY PASSED A BILL THAT IMPROVED CHILDCARE ON MILITARY BASES, INCREASED STATE ASSISTANCE FOR HOMELESS VETERANS, AND OFFERED HELP TO VETERANS SEEKING PROFESSIONAL LICENSES.
