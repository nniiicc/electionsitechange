AMELIA’S PRIORITIES
- AFFORDABILITY Arizona families are being squeezed by rising housing costs, utility bills, and the high cost of healthcare.
As a doctor, Dr.
Amelia Gallitano has seen what happens when people delay care because they can’t afford it.
She will work to lower healthcare and prescription drug costs, expand access to care, address the housing shortage, and protect families from unfair utility rate hikes so working families can get ahead, not fall behind.
- PUBLIC EDUCATION Arizona’s public schools are underfunded and stretched thin.
As an educator, Dr.
Gallitano knows what students and teachers need to succeed.
She will fight to fully fund public schools, raise teacher pay, and ensure ESA programs are transparent, accountable, and held to the same standards as public schools.
Strong public schools are essential to Arizona’s future.
- HEALTHCARE Healthcare should be affordable and accessible for every Arizona family.
Dr.
Gallitano has spent her career treating patients and training future doctors.
She will work to expand access, lower costs, and ensure healthcare decisions are guided by evidence, not politics.
- WATER Arizona’s future depends on how we manage our water.
Dr.
Gallitano supports science-based water policy that protects long-term supply, holds large users accountable, and ensures sustainable growth for our communities.
- PUBLIC SAFETY Dr.
Amelia Gallitano believes public safety means supporting law enforcement, investing in prevention, and addressing the root causes of crime.
That includes ensuring first responders have the resources they need, strengthening community-based programs, and expanding access to mental health care.
Amelia is committed to practical, balanced solutions that keep Arizona families safe while strengthening the communities we all call home.
