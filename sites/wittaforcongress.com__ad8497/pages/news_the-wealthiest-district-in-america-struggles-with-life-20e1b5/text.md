Home | About | Why | Mission Get Involved | News | Donate Donate Press Release The Wealthiest District in America Struggles with Life May 12, 2026 https://youtu.be/sPU_ac3jhT4 In America's wealthiest and most diverse district, a stark reality plays out daily: families are forced to choose between life-saving medication and paying rent.
This isn't a failure of the people—it's a failure of leadership.
For decades, residents of District 6 have watched their tax dollars fund wars and corporate profits while their own neighborhoods crumble.
With over 90 languages spoken and a population ranging from those earning under $20k to billionaires, the district holds immense potential.
Yet, discrimination, rising costs, and corporate greed have left the majority struggling.
Current Congressman Jason Crow has served three terms taking money from Wall Street, Weapon Manufacturers, AIPAC, prioritizing donors over constituents.
While local nonprofits try to patch the cracks, the system itself remains broken.
This campaign is about more than just politics; it's about reclaiming a future where leadership is accountable to the people, not the powerful.
It's time to stop choosing between survival and dignity.
It's time to take District 6 back.
