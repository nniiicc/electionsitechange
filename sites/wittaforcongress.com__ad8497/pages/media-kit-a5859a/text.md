Biography Official Portrait The Velvet Revolution (1989): During his final year of medical school at Charles University in Prague, Dr.
Witta was actively involved in the Velvet Revolution against communism.
As he says: “As a student, I fought communism not for capitalism.
I fought it for cultural, religious and social freedom.” Medical Research: Received a Ph.D. in Genetics and while working at the National Institutes of Health in Bethesda, Maryland, discovered the first transcription factor that can convert a cell into a neuron.
Medical Practice: Completed internal medicine residency in Pennsylvania and oncology fellowship at the University of Colorado, where he identified effective drug combinations for cancer treatment.
Mountain Blue Cancer Care Center (2015): Founded the cancer care center in Denver, treating thousands of patients and witnessing how insurance premiums and pharmaceutical costs create severe hardships for those battling cancer.
Charitable Organizations Founded All The Children of Light Nonprofit providing education and aid to Syrian refugee and orphaned children—often risking his own safety to deliver help.
Youth Soccer Initiative Nonprofit helping minority children in Colorado participate in soccer, giving them opportunities every child deserves.
Anthiogen (2018) Raises funds to cover cancer patients' insurance premiums throughout Colorado.
Empower Her Color (2020) Supports minority women facing healthcare, psychological, and social challenges.
Key Policy Positions Healthcare Reform Make healthcare affordable, reduce prescription drug costs, and eliminate corporate influence on medical decisions.
Campaign Finance Reform Repeal Citizens United, curb dark money, and restore accountability to elected officials.
Economic Justice Fight for policies that make life affordable for working families and their children.
Veterans Support Address the psychological, medical, and financial challenges veterans face after serving their country.
Published Work “Cancer Amongst Us and the Rise of All the Children of Light” (2023) Book describing patients' struggles and exposing how financial interests—bankers, pharmaceutical firms, and insurers—dominate U.S. politics.
