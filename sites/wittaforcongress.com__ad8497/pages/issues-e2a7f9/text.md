Reform
Healthcare Affordability
The Problem
After 25 years in the exam room, I've watched too many families walk in worried less about their diagnosis than about whether they can afford to be sick.
Young people can't afford coverage.
Premiums are out of reach for those just starting out—so they go without, and one accident or illness becomes a financial catastrophe.
A third of families can't meet their deductible.
They have "insurance" that still doesn't let them see a doctor.
Drug prices ignore both science and the rest of the world.
Americans pay many times what other countries pay for the exact same medicine.
Insurance monopolies still aren't held to account.
Even after the 2020 reform, loopholes remain—and consolidation keeps driving your costs up with too little competition.
The Rx
Free Care for Pregnant Women & Everyone Under 26
Free healthcare for pregnant women and everyone under 26—protect families at the two moments that shape a lifetime of health.
Cap Deductibles at $2,000
Cap out-of-pocket deductibles at $2,000—so having insurance actually means you can afford care.
Fair Drug Prices
Drug prices that reflect real science and world prices—saving taxpayers and families billions.
Finish the Job on Insurance Monopolies
Close the remaining McCarran-Ferguson loopholes and enforce real competition.

Respect
Equality & Justice for All
The Problem
Working families built this district.
Right now, too many are being priced out of it—and their kids are paying the price.
Home is slipping out of reach.
Rising HOA fees, surprise special assessments, climbing rent, and property taxes that go up every year—until good families are pushed toward eviction from homes they've worked their whole lives for.
Our young people can't keep up with a changing world.
Too many finish school without the education or training to compete in today's economy—while the biggest corporations in the district profit here and invest almost nothing back into the next generation.
Kids are being priced out of the things that keep them safe.
The cost of youth sports has doubled—putting it out of reach for more than half the kids in this district.
When kids have nowhere to belong, it costs all of us: in neighborhood safety, in their health, and in their future.
Our Solutions
Cap Rent & HOA Fees
Cap rent and HOA fees, and hold management companies accountable—no more piling on costs that push good families toward eviction.
Corporate Partners for Our Youth
Turn the district's biggest corporations into partners for our youth—building corporate-funded education and job-training pipelines so our young people can compete right here at home.
Youth Sports & After-School Programs
Support youth sports and after-school programs—because when kids have somewhere to belong, neighborhoods get safer.
