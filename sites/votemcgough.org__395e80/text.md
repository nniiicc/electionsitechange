We need more respect in our debate and less extremes in government.
As your Senator in Concord, I will be committed to a common sense and logical approach, honoring our shared values of fiscal responsibility and individual liberty.
Here’s my commitment to you: Empower Families: Trust families and parents to make choices selecting a doctor or the best school for their children.
Protect Constitutional Rights: The constitution is clear about Natural Rights of citizens, including free speech, privacy, property ownership, and self-defense.
These rights must be upheld.
Championing Economic Freedom: Foster innovation through low taxes and minimal regulation.
This is proven to create jobs and provide a wealth of products and services.
Cutting Costs: The costs of living and inflation are out of control.
We need to cut costs of housing, healthcare, and consumer expenses.
I promise to support tax cuts to do just that.
