Patrick Binder with his family Patrick Binder is a carpenter and builder who owns Timber & Stone Outdoor Spaces.
He designs and builds decks, patios, and outdoor living spaces for families across Manchester and southern New Hampshire.
He also creates pro-New Hampshire apparel at The Granite Republic.
A heritage American with Mayflower roots, Patrick is a husband, father, and Catholic.
Married since 2008, he skates, skis, fishes, and passes the lacrosse ball with his son.
After living in three countries and traveling to more than ten, he chooses New Hampshire — and Manchester — every day.
His family has called Manchester home since 2017.
He directs NH First, a citizen advocacy group, and Manch Strong, a policy group focused on practical, resident-first solutions.
He volunteers with Piscataquog.org and local organizations.
He is running because Manchester needs a senator who has built businesses with his own hands, who knows the real cost of bad rules, and who will put residents first — not insiders, not special interests, not foreign agendas.
