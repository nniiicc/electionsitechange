Healthcare
Too many Ohioans worry about whether they can afford to see a doctor or pay a medical bill.
Scott will work to expand access to quality care while tackling rising costs, so healthcare is both available and affordable—not a luxury—for everyone.
The massive cuts in Federal Funding to Medicaid and to the Affordable Care Act marketplaces has created a large increase in uninsured individuals in Ohio, especially in rural areas such as ours.
More cuts are on their way in Medicaid (more than $900 billion over 10 years as well a push make the ACA even more unaffordable.
When people become uninsurable, their only access to healthcare is the Emergency Room which creates a huge strain on the Hospital & other healthcare systems that start reducing costs by staff reductions and available services.
A report from the Ohio Capital Journal recently outlined the Medicaid cuts meant 996,000 fewer jobs nationwide are expected in 2029, half of which will be health-related, including in hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, or nursing homes.
States with the largest job losses include Ohio.
In addition, when the number of people uninsured rises, private insurance premiums go up.
We have already seen the beginning of these rising costs.
