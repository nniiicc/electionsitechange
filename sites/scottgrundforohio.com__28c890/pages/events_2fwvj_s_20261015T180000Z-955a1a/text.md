About this event
Your candidates for U.S.
Congress, Ohio House, and Ohio Senate are coming to Mount Vernon (and they're bringing pizza)!

Join Jerrad Christian, Scott Grund, Scott Charland, and Vince McGrail to find out how we can use Congress and the statehouse to fix the data center takeover, ban Flock cameras, and fight for the working class.
LOCATION: In downtown Mt.
Vernon at the ReStore/Habitat for Humanity meeting room
This event is free and open to all constituents.
If you are unable to attend but would like to submit a question or share your thoughts, please email info@ScottGrundForOhio.com
We look forward to a productive, community-driven conversation.
