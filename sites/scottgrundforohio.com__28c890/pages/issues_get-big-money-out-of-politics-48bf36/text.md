Get big money out of politics
Ever since the U.S.
Supreme Court ruling called Citizens United vs.
Federal Election Commission, corporations and other outside groups have been allowed to spend unlimited money on elections.
This opened the door for Super PACs (political action committees) that can not only spend these unlimited amounts, but hide the original source of the dollars (Dark Money).
The amount of CORRUPTION fueled by this dark money has increased to record levels here in Ohio, as well as, non-responsive representation.
