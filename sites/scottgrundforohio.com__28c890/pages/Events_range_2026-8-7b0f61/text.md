Community Town Hall in COSHOCTON
Join Scott, candidate for Ohio House of Representatives, for an open and honest community town hall on Tuesday August 18, 2026.
This is your opportunity to ask questions, share your concerns, and discuss the issues that matter most to our district.
Scott will outline his vision of representation in the Ohio General Assembly for Knox, Holmes and Coshocton Counties.
LOCATION: In Roscoe Village Visitor Center 600 N.
Whitewoman St., Coshocton
This event is free and open to all constituents.
We would like attendees to register in advance at ScottGrundForOhio.com to help us prepare for seating.
If you are unable to attend but would like to submit a question or share your thoughts, please email info@ScottGrundForOhio.com
We look forward to a productive, community-driven conversation.
