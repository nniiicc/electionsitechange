Home Meet Scott Issues Get big money out of politics / stop corruption in State government Even though the Citizens United decision covers national as well as state elections, a number of states are putting into place very affective "work arounds".
The first state to pass legislation in this regard is Hawaii.
The Hawaii State government has communicated to any corporation that wants to do business in their state (and get permission to do so through the Secretary of State's office) is prohibited to make political contributions to any candidate running for an office within the state (i.e. office of governor on down).
This becomes effective in 2027.
Montana has a similar policy being placed on the ballot in November 3rd.
There are other ideas being discussed, mostly from the Democratic party side, here in Ohio to greatly reduce the corruption in State government.
