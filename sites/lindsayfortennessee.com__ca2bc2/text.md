First Time Caller, Long Time Listener Hi!
I'm Lindsay, but you might know me as Bird.
I didn’t set out to run for office.
I’m a mom, I work, and I juggle the same real-life challenges as so many families in our community.
But, there was a day in January, I realized something: caring about our community isn’t enough if we’re not willing to step up and serve it.
I'm running to bring practical, people-first leadership to Nashville that is focused on affordability, education, healthcare, responsible growth, and making sure our community has a real voice.
I believe in leadership that’s grounded in respect, common sense, and care for our neighbors; not division or political games.
My faith matters to me, and I take seriously the words in red in the Bible: Jesus calls to love our neighbors, show mercy, and serve the least among us.
Those values shape how I see public service.
Faith should call us to serve people, not control them.
Likewise, government should work for people, not control them.
