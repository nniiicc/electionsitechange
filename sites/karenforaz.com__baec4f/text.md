Why I’m Running
I am a third-generation Arizona native and have deep roots in the state.
With my background in accounting, I know what it takes to create budgets and analyze data.
As a long time public education advocate I have volunteered my time in classrooms, fundraising, coaching, working on campaigns, and for various charitable organizations with the goal of creating a better Arizona.
As an elected member of the Madison Elementary School District governing board, I know what Arizona schools, teachers, and students need.
I am deeply passionate about public education, safety in our schools and communities, and ensuring that Arizona families can thrive.
I look forward to representing LD4 in the State House, and working on sensible solutions to the challenges we face.
