AFSCME 〰️ Endorsed by College Democrats of CT A Strong Voice for Clinton, Killingworth & Westbrook I'm Cinzia Lettieri— a teacher, a mom, and someone who grew up in Clinton and is now proud to be raising my daughter here with my husband.
For nearly 15 years, I've worked in education, policy, and advocacy across the country.
After thousands of conversations with neighbors across Clinton, Killingworth, and Westbrook, one thing is clear: people want someone who listens, works hard, and puts community values first and makes sure our communities are heard in Hartford.
That's the representative I intend to be.
I'm running to lower the cost of living, strengthen our schools, support local businesses, protect our shoreline, and make sure our communities remain places where people can build a life and age in place.
Let’s Get There Together!
A Lower Cost of Living and the Ability to Age in Place Everyone deserves the opportunity to afford to live—and stay—in the community they call home.
I'll work to lower everyday costs and support policies that help residents of all ages put down roots and age with dignity.
Great Schools-Strong PreK-12 pathways to College & Career Great schools build strong futures.
As an educator and a mother, I know that investing in our children—from PreK through graduation and into college or careers—is one of the most important commitments we can make to our communities.
Working For the Community Listening to community leaders and voters on issues that affect them will be the central driving force to my efforts in Hartford.
Women's Rights & Dignity for all Women’s rights are human rights—and they must be protected, not politicized.
I will fight for policies that ensure safety, dignity, and opportunity for all women and girls, from strong legal protections to real support in our communities.
State Funding to Fix Aging Infrastructure Now more than ever, our small community buisnesses need someone in their corner.
Creating and investing in sustainable town infrustructure will provide the backbone to keeping our local treasures in business and thriving.
