A Strong Voice for Clinton, Killingworth & Westbrook
I'm Cinzia Lettieri— a teacher, a mom, and someone who grew up in Clinton and is now proud to be raising my daughter here with my husband.
For nearly 15 years, I've worked in education, policy, and advocacy across the country.
After thousands of conversations with neighbors across Clinton, Killingworth, and Westbrook, one thing is clear: people want someone who listens, works hard, and puts community values first and makes sure our communities are heard in Hartford.
That's the representative I intend to be.
I'm running to lower the cost of living, strengthen our schools, support local businesses, protect our shoreline, and make sure our communities remain places where people can build a life and age in place.
