Localism as our core philosophy
Localism is a political and economic philosophy that prioritizes local control, community self-reliance, and decentralized power over centralized or global authority.
Localism is the only viable solution to Big Government, Big Business, and Big Banking.
What Localism looks like in practice:
Food
Buy, grow, and eat locally.
Farmers markets.
Community gardens.
School farm-to-table.
Energy
Community solar.
Locally owned utilities.
Home energy independence.
Economy
Shop locally.
Community banks/insurance programs.
Support small business and local trades.
Governance
Local control over zoning and land use.
Participatory budgeting.
Local say over resource decisions.
Community
Local schools with local input.
Mutual aid—tool libraries, skill-sharing, childcare co-ops.
