You deserve a representative who represents you, not corporate interests.
Despite being outfunded 10-to-1 by our opponent who accepts all the corporate money, we still think we can win.
Stats show that 92% of the time, the candidate who raises the most money wins in the general election.
We're the 8% because our campaign actually has morals.
We represent the people's interests, not the billionaire class.
We think that's the bare minimum you should ask of your politicians.
