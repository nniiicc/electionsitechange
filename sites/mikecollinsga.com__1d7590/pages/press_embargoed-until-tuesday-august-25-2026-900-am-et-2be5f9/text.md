MIKE COLLINS FOR U.S.
SENATE LAUNCHES NEW AD: “DELIVER”
Jackson, GA — U.S.
Senate Candidate Mike Collins today launched his first television ad of the general election, “Deliver,” contrasting Collins’ record as a conservative businessman with Jon Ossoff’s six years of empty promises.
“As a trucker, I couldn’t talk all day and never deliver,” Collins says in the ad.
“But that’s what Jon Ossoff does.”
The ad opens with Collins standing in front of his 18-wheeler, a nod to the trucking company he built from a single truck, before taking aim at Ossoff’s record on healthcare, taxes and the rising cost of living.
“Jon Ossoff had six years to deliver for Georgians, and instead he’s fallen in line with the socialist left while working families at home get crushed by higher prices,” said Josh Siegel, campaign manager for Mike Collins for Senate.
“Ossoff votes with the far left in Washington and depends on millions of dollars from liberal donors outside Georgia to keep him there.
Mike Collins is the only candidate who will answer to Georgians, has a record of delivering bipartisan results for families, and will bring a workhorse mentality to the United States Senate.”
