Top of GOP Ticket Ready for Victory
Jackson, GA — The top of the GOP ticket is ready to work together for victory this November.
Gubernatorial nominee Rick Jackson and Senate nominee Mike Collins endorsed each other today.
Rick Jackson:
“As a small businessman and owner of a trucking company, Mike Collins has spent his life delivering for others.
He continued that service in Congress, passing bipartisan legislation to make America safer.
In the Senate, he’ll continue to deliver for all Georgians.”
Mike Collins:
“Rick Jackson has an incredible story, work ethic, and a proven track record of success in business.
That’s what we need serving in government: someone who knows how to make government work for the people, not the other way around.”
