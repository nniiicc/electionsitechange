FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE — March 1, 2026
Today, community leader Mary Nesgoda officially announced her DFL candidacy for the Minnesota House of Representatives, seat 22B.
“I’m running because the families of House 22B deserve a representative who understands their daily challenges — from the cost of healthcare to the quality of our public schools,” said Nesgoda.
“I’m ready to bring that experience to the State Capitol.”
Nesgoda is running on the DFL ticket with a platform focused on affordable healthcare, fully funded public schools, economic opportunity, and environmental stewardship.
“This campaign isn’t about me — it’s about us,” Nesgoda continued.
“Together, we can build a district where every family has what they need to thrive.”
The campaign is seeking volunteers and grassroots donations to build a people-powered movement in House 22B.
To get involved, visit mnesgoda22b.org or donate today.
