Who I Am #
I am a lifelong Minnesotan and community advocate running as the DFL candidate for Minnesota House 22B.
With deep roots in our community, I understand the challenges facing working families in our district.
Why I’m Running #
I’m running because I believe House 22B deserves leadership that reflects our values — strong schools, affordable healthcare, good jobs, and a government that works transparently for the people it serves.
Too many families in our district are struggling to make ends meet while the cost of healthcare, childcare, and housing continues to rise.
I’m ready to fight for policies that make a real difference in people’s lives.
My Vision #
I envision a Minnesota where:
- Every family can afford quality healthcare
- Every child attends a well-funded public school
- Every worker earns a living wage
- Every community is a safe place to live
