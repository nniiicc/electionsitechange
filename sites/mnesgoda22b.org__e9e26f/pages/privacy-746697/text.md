Privacy Policy #
Last updated: March 1, 2026
The Mary Nesgoda for House 22B campaign (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
Information We Collect #
- Voluntarily provided information: When you sign up to volunteer, make a donation, or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, and occupation/employer (as required by campaign finance law).
- Automatically collected information: Our website may collect basic analytics data such as page views, referring URLs, and browser type.
How We Use Your Information #
- To communicate with you about the campaign, events, and volunteer opportunities
- To process and report donations as required by Minnesota campaign finance law
- To improve our website and outreach efforts
Information Sharing #
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.
We may share information as required by law, including campaign finance disclosure requirements.
Donations #
Donations are processed through ActBlue.
ActBlue’s privacy policy governs information collected during the donation process.
Under Minnesota and federal law, we are required to report the name, address, occupation, and employer of donors who contribute over $200 in an election cycle.
