Issues
Focus on the needs of the People of Wisconsin
The focus needs to be back on the needs of the people, we are often overlooked and forgotten by the politicians following the party line.
We need representatives in office that know how to work together to best serve the people that elected them.
As an independent candidate, I have no party line to follow or cross which gives me a unique perspective and opportunity to work with both sides to find solutions to the problems facing our State.
Reduce wasteful spending
We must work on cutting down wasteful spending and be good stewards of tax money.
Reducing funds going towards unneeded expenses and pet projects will allow for more money to be allocated to areas of need at the local level without requiring us to raise taxes.
Ensure funding of emergency services
Our emergency services do a lot for all of us, and it is only right to ensure that they are properly funded.
If we reallocate surplus funds from Madison's unnecessary spending, we can help fund emergency services statewide.
We must ensure that our first responders have the proper equipment and the best training available.
We must also set a standard of pay that reflects the importance of the work done by our first responders.
By doing this, we can do our part and help our first responders continue to operate at the level of excellence that the people of Wisconsin deserve.
Empower local government
Empowering our local government is an important step, we are our local government and have a much better grasp on what is needed in our communities than State governments.
Reallocating funds from wasteful spending in Madison to local governments to use in much needed areas will empower local governments to operate effectively to maintain and improve our communities.
Grow the agricultural community
We must invest in the future of our agricultural communities to keep farms and farmland in the hands of domestic farmers and teach the younger generations the value of agriculture.
By bolstering programs such as 4H and FFA as well as enticing social communities such as Veterans to Wisconsin, we can stay true to our agricultural roots.
Limiting the sale of Wisconsin farmland to foreign assets and making it available to citizens of our State will ensure a future for our growing agricultural community.
Election reform
We must all work together to commit to fair elections and reform the election system.
Removing barriers to ballot access and unfair election finance rules will allow for the best candidates to run for office.
Elections should be about finding excellence and sharing new ideas, not which party can fund their candidate better.
Protect and empower local small businesses
Local businesses are the hearts and souls of our communities, and it is our duty to ensure that they are protected and empowered.
Too often we see small businesses overregulated to the point of being financially insolvent.
We need to work to provide an environment that fosters growth in our locally owned businesses and allows for creativity without restrictions being stifling.
Responsibly fund our schools
There should never be a time where our schools are financially unable to provide the best education for the future leaders of our State.
K12 funding should always be a priority in our budget.
We must work to ensure our schools always have the funding they need, however, we must do this responsibly.
Raising property taxes when the people of Wisconsin are already struggling to make ends meet is irresponsible and does not serve the best interest of the People of Wisconsin.
Representatives had an obligation to cut waste to find funding instead of passing the bill to the people and communities they are supposed to represent.
Make Wisconsin attractive to our Veteran community
Wisconsin has a strong heritage of service to the nation, and we need to honor that service by making Wisconsin a place that Veterans desire to move to.
While there are programs in place for Veterans, transplanted Veterans have to maintain a residency of 5 consecutive years before they can even access most of those programs and benefits.
By contrast, anyone who moves to Wisconsin is eligible to run for office after maintaining a residency for 1 year.
It's easier for a transplant to our State to hold office than it is for a Veteran to access State benefits.
We need to focus on issues facing this community and keep the faith with these men and women that have sacrificed so much for us.
