Rock County Neighbors for Responsible Development Community Forum
I recently had the opportunity to attend a public forum hosted by the Rock County Neighbors for Responsible Development to discuss hyperscale data centers.
It was great to have Charlie Berens and his team bring some very needed laughs to the people who are facing this fight.
The main purpose of this event was to give people the opportunity to speak out about the concerns they have on this issue.
It is incredibly important that our elected officials hear and listen to the people who will be affected by these developments.
Unfortunately, it seems as if we are not being truly heard by many of the People we have elected to represent us.
While some of our representatives are listening, many seem to have made their decisions to go against the people they are elected to represent.
Even many of those who have shown support are only providing half measures.
Guardrails and protections can very easily become a roadmap for developers to follow.
Instead of Guardrails, we need to make Wisconsin far less attractive for these kinds of developments.
We need these developers to look elsewhere and to leave the beauty of Wisconsin alone
