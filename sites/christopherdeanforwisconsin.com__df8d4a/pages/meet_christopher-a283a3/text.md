Meet Christopher Issues News Volunteer Home Contribute Events Yard Signs Meet Christopher Christopher served as an Infantryman in the United States Army where he deployed to Iraq in support of the Global War on Terror.
Christopher held leadership roles throughout his military career and was awarded the Combat Infantry Badge along with an Army Commendation Medal with device for valor.
Upon his medical retirement from the military in 2010, Christopher and his family relocated to Wisconsin and settled in Center Township.
Shortly after his return from the military Christopher took a job in the automotive industry as a Parts Delivery Driver.
Within one year his skills at building effective teams led him to management roles within the auto industry.
As an automotive parts house manager, Christopher was able to build relationships with local businesses that allowed him to turn a failing business into an industry leader in the area.
On a volunteer basis, Christopher has served as a youth football coach for the Parkview Youth Football program where he mentored youth in football skills as well as being a role model.
He also volunteered with the Red Cross to support vital blood drive campaigns.
Christopher lives in Center Township with his fiancé, Chasidy and her son, along with his three daughters.
