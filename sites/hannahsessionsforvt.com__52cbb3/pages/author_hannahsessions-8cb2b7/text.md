I’m Hannah Doton Sessions and I am running to be one of your two State Senators representing Addison County and the adjoining towns of Huntington, Buel’s Gore and Rochester.
I am running because my life experience has taught me not only to work hard but also to ask questions and listen, three skills that will help me represent you in Montpelier.
I want to build pragmatic coalitions to help improve the lives of current and future Vermonters and Addison District residents.
I believe that the government needs to work for the common good of all.
I love Vermont and Addison County.
I was born and raised here by parents who exposed me to all that this incredible area has to offer.
My family roots go back to a farm in Vermont in 1786 (before Vermont was a state).
My great-grandfather, Edward Bliss Doton, served in the State Legislature in the late 1950s, and my relatives still farm on that hillside near Pomfret.
I’ve always known that Vermont is a special place.
I graduated from local schools and came back to Addison County after attending Bates College in Maine where I majored in Political Science and Studio Art.
I am a farmer, a business owner, and a parent.
For 25 years my husband and I have owned and operated a farm and cheese business.
I know first-hand what it takes to start and run a business in this state.
During that time I also served for decades on several local and state-wide school, housing and land conservation boards, broadening my knowledge about how state policies and funding affect the daily lives of Vermonters.
Our two kids are now grown and looking to someday settle in Vermont.
A Vermont that is affordable to our next generation is personal to me!
Addison County is incredibly diverse and complex.
We encompass farms of all sizes, working forests, lake communities, manufacturing, nature, recreation, incredible arts, a vibrant retirement community, the nation’s smallest city, and a world renowned college.
We are such a special place because of all of us.
I have been blessed to know and be inspired by this community throughout my life and it would be my great honor to represent you in Montpelier.
I humbly ask for your support~
Hannah
