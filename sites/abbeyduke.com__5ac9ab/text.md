Welcome!
I’m Abbey Duke
I serve in the Vermont State House of Representatives for District Chittenden-17, including parts of Burlington’s Old and New North Ends.
I was appointed in May, 2024 for the final week of the session and then I won a full term in the November election.
I ran to bring my perspective as a small business owner, a parent and a neighbor to statewide issues.
I love Burlington and I love Vermont.
And I believe my skills, experiences and temperament will serve our district well.
I bring a pragmatic, data-driven, collaborative leadership style to advocate for the needs of our district.
I am here to listen to your concerns and ideas, to learn about and understand key issues and to do my very best to represent our district in Montpelier.
I will continually look for ways that our state government can do a better job of serving the people of Vermont and Burlington.
