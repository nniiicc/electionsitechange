ABOUT KARLTON HOWARD
Putting People First
WHO'S KARLTON HOWARD
Karlton L.
Howard hails from Augusta, Georgia, born to the late Henry L.
Howard and Thelma Jones Howard.
His educational journey began in Augusta's public school system, culminating in graduation from Lucy C.
Laney High School.
He pursued higher education at LaGrange College, earning a Bachelor of Arts in Business Administration, followed by theological studies at the Augusta extension of American Baptist Theological Seminary, where he received his certificate in Theology.
Karlton then advanced his studies at Columbia International University in South Carolina, enrolling in the Masters of Divinity program.
EXPERIENCE AND QUALIFICATIONS
Karlton L.
Howard has been actively involved with numerous religious, civic, and social organizations throughout his career.
He has contributed significantly to various initiatives and held key roles within these entities:
- Advisory Board Member for Senior Citizens Council on Aging.
- Board of Directors for Burke County Partnership/Communities in Schools.
- Augusta Aviation Commission, where he served as Chairman and Co-Chairman.
- Green Grove, where he held multiple positions including Director of Christian Education, Chairman of Deacons ministry, Revival Coordinator, and Men’s Sunday school teacher.
- Past moderator of Noah Ebenezer Central Baptist Association.
- Member of the Augusta Baptist Ministers Conference, having served as treasurer.
- Vice President for the Tenth District Congress on Christian Education.
- President of the Tenth District Convention of the General Missionary Baptist Convention of Georgia.
- Member of the Hampton’s Ministers Conference.
- Instructor for the Augusta Theological Seminary, conducting extension classes at Noahs Ark Missionary Baptist Church.
- Executive Producer/Host of “The Parade of Quartets” on ABC affiliate WJBF TV in Augusta, Georgia, consistently airing since 1953.
The Parade of Quartets is the recipient of the Georgia Association of Broadcasters 2013 Lifetime Achievement Award for over 6o years of broadcasting.
The program has been designated as the longest continuous running gospel program in Georgia and possible in the United States.
MISSION STATEMENT
Putting People First
As a lifelong resident of Georgia House District 129, I am uniquely positioned to continue to serve as a dedicated advocate for its people.
With deep roots in the community, I bring a commitment to upholding spiritual morals and values in public service, recognizing their power to drive positive change.
Inspired by the legacies of Henry and Wayne Howard, I aim to continue their tradition of selfless service and prioritize the needs of our constituents.
Guided by the principle of giving more than receiving, instilled in me by my family, I pledge to prioritize the welfare of the people above all else.
My mission is to "Put People First" by addressing critical issues such as the lack of affordable housing and the displacement of our seniors due to the influx of multi-family housing.
Through proactive and compassionate leadership, I strive to uphold the dignity and well-being of every individual in District 129.
OUR DISTRICT
Karlton Howard seeks to continue to represent District 129, aiming to enhance the quality of life for all its residents.
With a focus on education, healthcare, housing, and fostering unity, he pledges to drive positive change.
Backed by a track record of community service, Karlton is wholeheartedly devoted to making impactful differences.
