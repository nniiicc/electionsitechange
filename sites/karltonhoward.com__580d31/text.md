KARLTON HOWARD
Putting Experience to Work
Karlton Howard is a dedicated leader working tirelessly to make our community a better place.
With years of experience in public service, he is committed to continuing to serve the people of District 129 with integrity and passion.
Karlton Howard is commitment to the community entails:
- Elevating early education and emphasizing trades and technical skills.
- Advocating for increased access to affordable housing and combating senior displacement.
- Pursuing intelligent, inclusive economic progress and urban development.
- Tackling healthcare inequalities, particularly for the uninsured and under-insured.
- Striving for unity in Augusta through inclusive initiatives.
- Fostering growth and aiding small local businesses with opportunities and support.
