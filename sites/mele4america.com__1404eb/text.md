Meet Gregg Mele The people’s seat should belong to the people.
Gregg knows that public service is a duty—not a prize, a platform, or a blank check.
Raised by blue-collar parents, Gregg built a career in law, business, finance, technology, and the classroom.
He understands what federal decisions look like at the kitchen table: higher bills, fewer choices, and too little accountability.
He is ready to stand up to waste, defend individual liberty, and fight for every New Jersey family that feels forgotten by Washington.
