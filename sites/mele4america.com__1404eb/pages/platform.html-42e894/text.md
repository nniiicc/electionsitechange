Gregg Mele believes every federal decision should pass a simple test: Does it make New Jersey families safer, freer, and better able to build a future?
Affordability Public safety Accountability Education Standing with Israel Healthcare 01 Affordability & the economy Make life affordable again.
Families cannot budget their way around a federal government that keeps spending, borrowing, and regulating without discipline.
Gregg will fight for responsible budgets, lower barriers to American energy production, competitive markets, and relief from needless federal rules that raise the cost of housing, food, transportation, and doing business.
Require honest cost estimates and a clear funding plan before Congress votes.
Expand reliable American energy to lower costs and strengthen national security.
Help small businesses compete, hire, and grow without Washington standing in the way.
02 Immigration & public safety Secure the border.
Enforce the law.
Protect our communities.
A nation has both the right and the responsibility to know who is entering and to enforce its laws fairly.
Gregg supports legal immigration, a secure border, and policies that put the safety of New Jersey neighborhoods ahead of political fashion.
He will stand with law enforcement and first responders while demanding performance, transparency, and equal justice.
Restore operational control at the border and enforce existing immigration law.
Support police, firefighters, emergency personnel, and the communities they serve.
Reject policies that transfer the cost of federal failure to local taxpayers.
03 Government accountability No more blank checks.
Public service is a duty, and taxpayers deserve receipts.
Before Congress approves another program or appropriation, lawmakers should explain what it costs, how it will be paid for, who is responsible, and how the public will know whether it worked.
Gregg will press for measurable goals, regular audits, and consequences when government fails.
Publish costs, funding sources, and expected results in plain language.
Audit programs for waste, duplication, and failure before expanding them.
Fight for New Jersey's fair share instead of accepting more unfunded mandates.
04 Education & parental rights Put students, parents, and learning first.
Parents are partners—not obstacles—in their children's education.
Gregg supports strong academics, safe schools, honest civics, local accountability, and meaningful opportunity for every child.
Federal policy should empower families and teachers, not impose one-size-fits-all mandates from Washington.
Protect parents' right to know and participate in consequential school decisions.
Prioritize literacy, mathematics, science, civics, and career preparation.
Keep education decisions close to students, families, and communities.
05 Standing with Israel Stand with allies.
Confront terrorism and antisemitism.
America is safer when it is clear, credible, and loyal to democratic allies.
Gregg supports Israel's right to defend its people and believes the United States must confront terrorism, hostage-taking, and antisemitism without equivocation.
Strength and clarity deter aggression; mixed signals invite it.
Maintain a strong security partnership with Israel.
Oppose terrorist organizations and the networks that finance and enable them.
Call out antisemitism wherever it appears, including in public institutions.
06 Patient-centered healthcare More choice.
Clearer prices.
Better care.
Healthcare decisions belong with patients, families, and medical professionals—not distant bureaucracies.
Gregg will work to make costs understandable before care is delivered, expand meaningful choices, and encourage competition that rewards quality.
Reform should strengthen the doctor-patient relationship instead of burying it in paperwork.
Demand real price transparency so patients can compare cost and quality.
Expand choice and competition while reducing administrative burdens.
Keep medical decisions centered on patients and their trusted providers.
Help carry the message Put common sense back to work in Washington.
