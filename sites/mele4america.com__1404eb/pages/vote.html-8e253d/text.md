Official mail-ballot instructions Request a ballot online → 02 Use a Secure Drop Box Authorized drop boxes are available 24 hours a day unless a location notes otherwise.
Ballots must be deposited by 8:00 p.m.
November 3.
Use a drop box in the county where you are registered.

Do not take a mail ballot to an early-voting or Election Day polling site.

Official early-voting locations 04 Vote on Election Day Vote Tuesday, November 3, from 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. at your assigned polling place.
Confirm your polling place before leaving.

If you received a mail ballot but have not returned it, you may vote only by provisional paper ballot at the polls.
