How it works
Ten conversations can change an election.
The Mele 10 is personal, respectful, and focused on participation.
You do not need to be a professional organizer—you just need to follow through.
- 01 Choose your ten.
Think of family members, friends, neighbors, coworkers, or community members you can reach personally.
- 02 Start the conversation.
Ask what matters to them.
Listen first, then explain why this election and this district deserve their attention.
- 03 Check voter registration.
Make sure each person is registered and has current information.
Use New Jersey's official voter portal for authoritative guidance.
Visit Vote.NJ.gov
- 04 Make a voting plan.
Help each voter decide whether to vote by mail, during early voting, or on Election Day—and identify when and where.
- 05 Follow up.
A timely reminder turns good intentions into action.
Check in before voting begins and again before the deadline.
- 06 Celebrate the effort.
Recognize the people who participated and consider a small, supporter-funded gathering to keep the civic habit growing.
- 07 Pass it on.
Invite someone else to take the challenge.
Ten becomes twenty, twenty becomes a neighborhood, and a neighborhood becomes a movement.
