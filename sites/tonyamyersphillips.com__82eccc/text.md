Advocating for underrepresented and disenfranchised communities for over 25 years
State Representative Tonya Myers Phillips represents Michigan’s 7th House District- Detroit, Hamtramck, and Highland Park.
Tonya Myers Phillips is an attorney and leader with a lifelong history of service and advocacy on behalf of the community.
Tonya was born and raised in the City of Detroit.
She is the daughter of a Detroit Public Schools teacher and union member.
