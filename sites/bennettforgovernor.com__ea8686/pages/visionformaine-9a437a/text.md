Families are struggling to keep up with day-to-day expenses.
From high rents and inaccessible mortgages, rising energy bills that can’t be met, crushing health care costs, child care that is out of reach, and taxes that keep climbing – too many Mainers see no relief in sight.
This isn’t bad luck.
It’s the result of years of failed leadership.
Party insiders have been fighting to win the next election instead of working for a better Maine.
Too often, special interests, insiders, and party agendas come before the people who sent them to Augusta.
It doesn't have to be this way.
Maine can once again be a place where hard work pays off, where families can afford to put down roots, where young people can build their futures without leaving, where small businesses can grow, and where our communities can thrive.
Government should tell the truth, do the work, and deliver results.
That is what this campaign is about.
It is about lowering the cost of living, growing Maine’s economy, and restoring trust in government.
And making sure every Mainer knows they have a voice in the decisions that shape our future.
As an independent, I answer to Mainers, not party bosses, not special interests, and not the insiders who keep protecting a system that is failing our state.
This campaign is about something bigger than one election.
It's about deciding what comes next for Maine.
Are we going to keep accepting the same politics that have left too many families behind?
Or, are we going to choose a different path – one grounded in honesty, independence, and a relentless focus on getting results.
I believe our best days are still ahead.
More affordable.
More honest.
More independent.
