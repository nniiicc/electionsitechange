Meet Sarah
A Career Dedicated to Kentucky's Kids and Families

the Jefferson County Board of Education, and former U.S.
History
teacher in Louisville’s public schools.
With 16 years of teaching experience in our classrooms, Sarah has
spent much of her career helping our students succeed.
And she
knows exactly what the General Assembly should be doing to help
support Kentucky’s kids, families, and communities.
