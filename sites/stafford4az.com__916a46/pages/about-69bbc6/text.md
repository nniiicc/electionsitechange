Eric is running for Arizona State Senate in Legislative District 29 (LD29).
LD29 covers parts of Surprise, El Mirage, Glendale, Goodyear, Litchfield Park and Peoria, plus the unincorporated communities of Wittmann and Morristown, in Maricopa County's northwest West Valley.
Explore the LD29 guide Who is running for Arizona State Senate in LD29 in 2026?
Eric Stafford, the Democratic nominee, is running against Republican incumbent Sen.
Janae Shamp, who has held the LD29 Senate seat since 2023.
Voters will decide the race in the November 3, 2026 general election.
Is Eric Stafford a Democrat?
Yes.
Eric is the Democratic nominee for Arizona State Senate in LD29.
He is running a people-first campaign to lower costs, raise wages and strengthen public schools, and he takes no corporate PAC or dark money.
When is the election?
The general election is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
The voter registration deadline is October 5, early voting runs October 7 through October 30, and the last day to request an early ballot is October 23.
Get ready to vote What is Eric Stafford's background?
Eric is a lifelong Arizonan who was born in Phoenix and lives in Goodyear.
He graduated from West-MEC (Western Maricopa Education Center) in 2016 and Pima Medical Institute in 2018.
He is a tradesman: a certified A&P (airframe and powerplant) aircraft mechanic and a machinist who has worked in airplane hangars and machine shops.
He has also worked as a veterinary technician.
He got his start in politics as a field organizer on U.S.
Senator Mark Kelly's campaign.
Is Eric Stafford a Clean Elections candidate?
Yes.
Eric is running through Arizona's Clean Elections system, so his campaign is powered by small-dollar contributions from voters.
He takes no corporate PAC or dark money, and every dollar is publicly reported.
