Goodyear is split among several Arizona legislative districts, and part of the city is in Legislative District 29 (LD29).
If your address is in LD29, you help choose the district's state senator on November 3, 2026, and Eric Stafford is running to represent you.

Growth that works for residents
Goodyear is one of the fastest-growing cities in the country.
New neighborhoods, warehouses and industrial projects bring jobs, but they also bring traffic, higher housing costs and pressure on the water supply.
Eric wants the state to require environmental impact assessments for large industrial projects, prioritize water and utility infrastructure before approving new developments, and make sure new development includes affordable housing commitments.
What Eric will fight for in Goodyear
- Water and utility infrastructure before new large-scale development
- Environmental impact assessments for major industrial projects
- Affordable housing commitments in new developments
- Protect existing neighborhoods from industrial encroachment
- Tax relief and support for small, local businesses

Who represents Goodyear in LD29 today?
If you live in the LD29 part of Goodyear, your state senator is Janae Shamp, who has held the seat since January 2023.
Your state representatives are Steve Montenegro and James Taylor.
On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, LD29 voters will choose their next state senator in a race between Democratic nominee Eric Stafford and Republican incumbent Sen.
Janae Shamp.
