Privacy Policy
1 Introduction
Eric Stafford for Arizona Senate ("Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information provided by supporters, volunteers, and website visitors in LD29 and across Arizona.
2 Information We Collect
When you interact with our website or campaign, we may collect the following information:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- ZIP code
- Other information you provide voluntarily (e.g., donations or volunteer sign-ups)
- Website usage information collected through cookies and analytics (see Cookies & Analytics below)
3 How We Use Your Information
We use your information for campaign purposes only, including:
- Sending updates, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities
- Requesting donations and providing campaign information
- Sending voting reminders and election information
- Improving our website and campaign outreach
4 Text Messaging & Email Communications
By providing your phone number or email, you consent to receive communications from the campaign.
You may receive up to 1 text message per day.
Standard message and data rates may apply.
You can opt out of text messages by texting STOP or opt out of emails by clicking "unsubscribe" in any message.
5 Sharing of Information
We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
Information may be shared with trusted service providers who assist with campaign operations, in accordance with applicable federal and state election laws.
6 Cookies & Analytics
We use Google Analytics to understand how people find and use our website, such as which pages are visited and which links or ads brought visitors here.
Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to collect information like your browser and device type, the pages you view, and your general location (based on your IP address).
Google collects and processes this information under its own privacy policy.
We use it only in summary form to improve our website and outreach, and we do not sell it.
If you arrive through one of our campaign links, your browser also remembers that link's campaign details (such as "utm_source") on your device for up to 30 days, and they are included with any form you submit so we know which outreach is working.
- You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.
- You can opt out of Google Analytics on every website with Google's opt-out browser add-on.
7 Security
We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information we collect.
However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
8 Your Rights
You may request access to, correction, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us directly.
You may also unsubscribe from campaign emails or texts at any time as described above.
9 Third-Party Links
Our website may contain links to third-party sites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external websites.
10 Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.
Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
11 Contact Information
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please .contact us.
Thank you for supporting Eric Stafford's campaign for Arizona Senate.
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information as we work to put LD29 families first.
