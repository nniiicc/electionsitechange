Find My Legislator A city that is outgrowing its infrastructure Surprise has grown from roughly 30,000 residents in 2000 to more than 140,000 at the 2020 Census, making it one of the fastest-growing cities in Arizona.
New subdivisions, warehouses and data-center proposals keep arriving faster than the roads, water supply and schools that have to support them.
Eric believes growth has to work for the people who already live here, not just developers.
That means putting water and utility infrastructure first, giving neighbors a real voice on major projects, and making sure new development includes homes working families can actually afford.
Retirees and families in Sun City Grand and beyond Surprise is home to both young families and retirees, and both are feeling the cost of living.
Eric supports lowering prescription drug costs through state bulk purchasing and price-transparency laws, protecting coverage for pre-existing conditions, and expanding access to affordable care through AHCCCS.

Who represents Surprise in LD29 today?
If you live in the LD29 part of Surprise, your state senator is Janae Shamp, who has held the seat since January 2023.
Your state representatives are Steve Montenegro and James Taylor.
On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, LD29 voters will choose their next state senator in a race between Democratic nominee Eric Stafford and Republican incumbent Sen.
Janae Shamp.
