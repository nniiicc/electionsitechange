About Legislative District 29
LD29 is located in Maricopa County and includes parts of Surprise, El Mirage, Glendale, Goodyear, Litchfield Park, Wittmann, Sun City Grand, and surrounding West Valley communities.
It is one of the fastest-growing districts in Arizona.
Common zip codes in LD29: 85374, 85378, 85379, 85387, 85388, 85361
