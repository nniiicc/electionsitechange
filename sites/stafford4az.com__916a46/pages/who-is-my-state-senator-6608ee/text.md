Arizona has 30 legislative districts, and each one elects one state senator and two state representatives every two years.
Your senator depends on your legislative district, not your city, because district lines don't follow city limits.
The fastest way to know for sure: enter your home address in the Arizona Legislature's official lookup.

Common questions
What cities are in Arizona Legislative District 29?
LD29 covers parts of Surprise, El Mirage, Glendale, Goodyear, Litchfield Park and Peoria, plus the unincorporated communities of Wittmann and Morristown, all in Maricopa County's northwest West Valley.
District lines don't follow city limits, so check your address with the Arizona Legislature's Find My Legislator tool.
Who is the current state senator for LD29?
Janae Shamp has represented LD29 in the Arizona Senate since January 2023.
LD29's two state representatives are Steve Montenegro and James Taylor.
How long is an Arizona State Senate term?
Two years.
Each of Arizona's 30 legislative districts elects one state senator and two state representatives every two years, and lawmakers are limited to four consecutive terms in the same chamber.
Is Avondale in LD29?
Mostly no.
Avondale is largely in a neighboring legislative district.
District lines don't follow city limits, though, so enter your address in the Find My Legislator tool to be sure.
