Projects that big should never move forward without the people who live next to them having a real say.
Eric will fight to guarantee community input on major projects like the Wittmann rail hub, to require environmental impact assessments for large-scale industrial development, and to protect rural neighborhoods from industrial encroachment.
Water, heat and rural life Wittmann and Morristown sit at the edge of the desert, where groundwater and extreme heat are daily concerns.
Eric supports protecting Arizona's groundwater, smart water conservation, heat protections for outdoor workers, and support for local farms and food producers.

Who represents Wittmann in LD29 today?
If you live in the LD29 part of Wittmann, your state senator is Janae Shamp, who has held the seat since January 2023.
Your state representatives are Steve Montenegro and James Taylor.
On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, LD29 voters will choose their next state senator in a race between Democratic nominee Eric Stafford and Republican incumbent Sen.
Janae Shamp.
