El Mirage, the city along Grand Avenue between Surprise and Youngtown, lies within Arizona's Legislative District 29 (LD29).
El Mirage voters help choose LD29's state senator on November 3, 2026, and Eric Stafford is running to represent you.

A working community that deserves a working-class senator
El Mirage is a city of working families, commuters and neighbors who keep the West Valley running, a short drive from Luke Air Force Base.
Eric has spent a decade in blue-collar jobs, in machine shops, airport hangars, veterinary clinics and service work, and he knows what it is like when 40 hours a week isn't enough to pay the bills.
In the Senate, Eric will fight for living wages, stronger workplace safety enforcement, an end to wage theft, and real heat protections for people who work outdoors through Arizona summers.
Schools that get the funding they need
Families in El Mirage and Surprise are served by the Dysart Unified School District.
Arizona's school funding has lagged the nation for years.
Eric supports competitive teacher pay, more money for school buildings and resources, audits of voucher spending, and expanded vocational and apprenticeship programs for students of every age.
What Eric will fight for in El Mirage
- Living wages and protection from wage theft
- Extreme-heat protections for outdoor workers
- Competitive teacher pay and full funding for public schools

Who represents El Mirage in LD29 today?
If you live in the LD29 part of El Mirage, your state senator is Janae Shamp, who has held the seat since January 2023.
Your state representatives are Steve Montenegro and James Taylor.
On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, LD29 voters will choose their next state senator in a race between Democratic nominee Eric Stafford and Republican incumbent Sen.
Janae Shamp.
