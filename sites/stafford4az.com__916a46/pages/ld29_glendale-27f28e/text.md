Arizona's Legislative District 29 (LD29) includes part of western Glendale near Luke Air Force Base.
Most of Glendale is in other legislative districts, so look up your address to confirm, and if you're in LD29, Eric Stafford is running to be your state senator.

Home to Luke Air Force Base
Luke Air Force Base, where the Air Force trains F-35 pilots, is part of the West Valley's economy and identity, and thousands of veterans and military families call LD29 home.
Eric believes we owe them more than gratitude.
We owe them real support, from healthcare to housing to good jobs when they return to civilian life.
What Eric will do for veterans and military families
Eric will push to expand state-funded veteran healthcare and cut wait times, increase funding for mental health and PTSD treatment, build skills-to-trades pipelines for veterans, and protect military families from predatory lending and housing scams.
What Eric will fight for in Glendale
- Expand state-funded veteran healthcare and reduce wait times
- Increase funding for veteran mental health and PTSD treatment
- Veteran employment programs and skills-to-trades pipelines
- Protect military families from predatory lending and housing scams
- Keep Luke Air Force Base a strong community partner

Who represents Glendale in LD29 today?
If you live in the LD29 part of Glendale, your state senator is Janae Shamp, who has held the seat since January 2023.
Your state representatives are Steve Montenegro and James Taylor.
On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, LD29 voters will choose their next state senator in a race between Democratic nominee Eric Stafford and Republican incumbent Sen.
Janae Shamp.
