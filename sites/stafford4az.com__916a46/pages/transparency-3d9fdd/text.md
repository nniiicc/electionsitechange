Arizona Clean Elections Candidate
Eric is a certified Clean Elections candidate through the Arizona Citizens Clean Elections Commission.
This means his campaign is publicly funded by Arizona voters — not special interests, lobbyists, or corporate PACs.
Clean Elections candidates qualify by collecting $5 contributions from registered voters in their district — proving grassroots support before receiving any public funds.
Eric reached his goal and has qualified for Clean Elections funding.
Thank you to every LD29 voter who chipped in $5!
