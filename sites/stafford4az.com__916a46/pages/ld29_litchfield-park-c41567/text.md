Find My Legislator A small city with deep West Valley roots Litchfield Park grew out of the farm settlement the Goodyear Tire & Rubber Company founded in 1916 to grow cotton, and it's named for company executive Paul W.
Litchfield.
More than a century later, it's a close-knit community surrounded by some of the fastest growth in Arizona.
Eric wants to protect established neighborhoods like Litchfield Park from overdevelopment and industrial encroachment, and to invest in shade, green space and cooling infrastructure that keeps our streets livable in the summer.

Who represents Litchfield Park in LD29 today?
If you live in the LD29 part of Litchfield Park, your state senator is Janae Shamp, who has held the seat since January 2023.
Your state representatives are Steve Montenegro and James Taylor.
On Tuesday, November 3, 2026, LD29 voters will choose their next state senator in a race between Democratic nominee Eric Stafford and Republican incumbent Sen.
Janae Shamp.
