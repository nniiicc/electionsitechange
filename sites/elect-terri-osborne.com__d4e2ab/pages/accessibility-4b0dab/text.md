Accessibility Statement
Terri Osborne for Alabama State House District 96 is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all visitors to our website.
We strive to provide a website in which all functionality and content are accessible to all individuals.
We are continually improving the user experience and updating our site regularly to make it as accessible as possible.
Our website strives to meet the Level AA Success Criteria of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 to the greatest extent feasible.
Ongoing Efforts
Accessibility is an ongoing effort.
We regularly review our website to identify and address potential accessibility barriers.
As technology and standards evolve, we remain committed to improving accessibility for all users.
Need Assistance?

Third-Party Content
Some areas of this website may include links to third-party platforms or services.
While we strive to work with providers that prioritize accessibility, we cannot control the accessibility of external websites.
