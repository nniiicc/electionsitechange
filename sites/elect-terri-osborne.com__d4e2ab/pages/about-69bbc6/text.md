FOR THE LEAST OF THESE 💙
I’m Terri Osborne, a 30-year Daphne resident and geologist running for Alabama State House District 96 to fight for affordable living—from housing to healthcare—and protecting our natural resources.
I’m running to represent each and every one of my neighbors, but especially those who continue to be overlooked and underserved by elected officials.
Instead of being resigned to support the least-bad candidate or checking out of civic engagement entirely, I hope you’ll use your voice and your vote so that I can be a voice in the State Legislature for the least of these in our community.
As a Trans Woman, I know the urgency of defending bodily autonomy and equal rights for all.
I’m running to be a voice for the forgotten, to challenge the status quo and career politicians who answer to the wealthy and politically powerful rather than the people they represent.
