About me Zach Werhan is a fifth-generation Kansan.
He grew up in Wichita, where he met his wife, and together they are raising two young children at home.
Zach earned his undergraduate degree from Kansas State University and his master's degree from Wichita State University.
He holds licenses as both a Clinical Professional Counselor and a Master Addiction Counselor, runs a private clinical practice, teaches as an adjunct instructor.
In his practice, Zach works daily with Kansas families navigating a broken mental health system — dads who can't get care for their kids through KanCare, families stretched thin by rising property taxes, and young people who graduated high school without the tools to take care of their own minds.
After years of hearing what doesn't make the news, Zach asked himself who was going to Topeka to fight for these families.
When he couldn't find a good enough answer, he decided to go himself.
Zach is running for the Kansas House because he already knows what's broken in District 83 — and he believes this district deserves someone who doesn't need a briefing to figure it out.
Zach and his family are proud to call Wichita home.
