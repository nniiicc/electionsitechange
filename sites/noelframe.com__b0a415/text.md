Noel’s Priorities for the 36th
With Sen.
Noel Frame at the helm, the 36th Legislative District has been a leader in moving structural change for Washington State on the issues that impact our communities most.
Learn more about her track record of progressive wins!
Economic Prosperity for All of Us
Noel championed investing in people as our economic recovery strategy.
Increasing family wage jobs, providing access to affordable homes and childcare, and supporting small businesses will help our state prosper.
Rebalancing our Tax Code
This year, Noel led on the groundbreaking Millionaires Tax, a huge step toward fixing our upside-down tax code and ensuring Washington’s wealthiest pay their fair share.
As House Finance Committee Chair, Noel passed the Capital Gains Tax, and as Senate Ways & Means Committee Vice Chair of Finance, she is leading the way on fair taxes for working families and small businesses.
Strengthening Our
Diverse Communities
Noel has reformed systems in partnership with youth in foster care and juvenile rehabilitation, individuals with disabilities, and communities of color.
Repairing past harm and undoing institutional racism is critical to building a strong, inclusive community.
