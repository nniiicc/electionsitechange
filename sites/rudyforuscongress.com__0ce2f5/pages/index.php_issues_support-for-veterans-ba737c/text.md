As of 7 July 2026 HR333 is supported by 3 and HR303 is supported by 9 out of 438 representatives and both were referred to the Subcommittee on Disability Assistance and Memorial Affairs.
These bills have been repeatedly reintroduced for many congressional sessions and are languishing in Congress for years and the politicians are taking forever to do what is right.
