Robin Lockett, Tampa organizer and director at Florida Rising, announces run for state house.
After going to bat day after day for residents at Tampa City Hall, Robin Lockett wants a change of scenery.
Last month, the 61-year-old Regional Director of Florida Rising’s Regional filed paperwork to become the next State Representative for District 63 where Dianne “Ms Dee” Hart-Lowman is termed-out.
The filing comes three years after Lockett took long time, well-liked Tampa City Councilman Guido Maniscalco to a runoff for the District 2 seat in city hall.

Hart-Lowman Endorses Robin Lockett for Florida House District 63
TAMPA, FL — Florida House Representative Dianne Hart-Lowman has endorsed Robin Lockett in the race for Florida House District 63.
Hart-Lowman said Lockett would bring strong leadership to the Legislature and praised her work on public education, equitable health care and affordable housing.
“It is my honor to endorse Robin Lockett as the next State Representative for House District 63,” Hart-Lowman wrote.
“Robin is a fierce leader and fighter, and I know she will carry that same energy with her in the legislature.”
