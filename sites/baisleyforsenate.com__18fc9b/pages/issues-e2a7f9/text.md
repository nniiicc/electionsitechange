As a businessman and state senator, Mark has proven he can innovate and win.
He understands that prosperity doesn’t come from Washington mandates — it comes from unleashing the private sector.
In the United States Senate, he will fight to cut federal taxes, roll back job-killing regulations, restore American energy dominance, and make the US a magnet for employers — not a playground for unelected bureaucrats.
Safe, Prosperous Communities Mark Baisley is committed to making Colorado safe for everyone—no matter where they live.
Mark will work with law enforcement to give them the support and funding they need to keep us safe.
Similarly, Mark will work to end the dangerous programs that put violent criminals back into our neighborhoods before they’ve rehabilitated.
You can count on Mark to put your family first and to make Colorado safe.
Recent policies may have put our families in danger, but Mark knows we can make our communities safe and prosperous again.
Immigration People from around the globe see the exceptional character of American liberty and naturally believe that they also deserve to live in an environment of Natural Law — and they do.
More than any other nation, America is enriched by immigrants “yearning to breathe free.” And each year, 1.2 million people are legally invited to live in the United States.
These welcomed souls are vetted to ensure that they: Are not violent or dishonorable Will be self-reliant contributors Will proudly assimilate into American culture Those applicants who are not fortunate enough to share in the American inheritance should establish Natural Law in their native countries by pledging to each other their lives, their fortunes and their sacred honor.
Agriculture America’s farmers and ranchers remain the backbone of our domestic economy and national food security.
They feed our families and fuel significant portions of rural economic activity.
At the same time, they face structural cost pressures that punish hard work and make sustained profitability harder to achieve.
Senator Mark Baisley has a consistent record of fighting government overreach, cutting red tape, and backing private sector solutions in the Colorado State Senate.
He understands that durable prosperity comes from freer markets rather than expanded bureaucracy or dependency programs.
