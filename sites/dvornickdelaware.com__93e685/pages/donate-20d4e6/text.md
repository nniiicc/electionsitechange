Vote!
Election Day – Tuesday, November 3, 2026
- Polls are open 7:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m.
- Poll workers will ask for proof of identity, but a photo ID is not required.
If you do not have identification with you, you can complete an affidavit to vote if your eligibility is confirmed.
Here are the key points of the 20th House District election:
- The district elects one member to the Delaware House of Representatives.
- To vote, you must:
- Be registered to vote in Delaware.
- Be at least 18 years old by Election Day.
- Reside within the boundaries of the 20th House District.
MAP
Delaware offers three ways to vote:
- Election Day voting at your assigned polling place.
- Early voting during the state's designated early voting period.
- Absentee voting if you qualify under Delaware law.
