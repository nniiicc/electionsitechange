DONATE VOLUNTEER GET YARD SIGNS About Gene Dvornick ​ With more than 40 years of experience in local government, public safety, financial management, and community leadership, Gene has built a career dedicated to serving Sussex County.
Known for his integrity, fiscal responsibility, and commitment to public service, he has focused on solving problems, protecting taxpayers, and strengthening the communities he serves. ​ Gene believes public service is about making a positive difference in people's lives.
His leadership is guided by five core values: Commitment, Integrity, Honesty, Dedication and Excellence.
Whether managing a town, leading first responders, or volunteering in the community, he has consistently focused on listening, building consensus, and delivering results. ​ ​ As the Republican candidate for Delaware House District 20, Gene is committed to supporting first responders, promoting responsible growth, strengthening the local economy, and ensuring government remains accountable to the people it serves.
Experience & Accomplishments Town Manager, Georgetown (2007–Present) – Leading municipal operations, budgeting, infrastructure planning, and community growth.
Delaware Town Manager of the Year – Honored twice, in 2009 and 2024, for outstanding leadership.
Former Milton Town Council Member (2004–2007) – Bringing both elected and administrative experience to public service.
Volunteer Firefighter & Fire Chief – More than 40 years of service, beginning as an EMT in 1985 and serving in numerous leadership roles.
Financial Professional – Master of Accountancy from Virginia Tech and Master of Public Administration from the University of Delaware, providing the expertise to manage budgets responsibly and protect taxpayer dollars.
Dedicated Community Volunteer – Longtime mentor with Big Brothers Big Sisters of Delaware and Guiding Eyes for the Blind, and board member for organizations including the Rehoboth Beach Film Society, First State Community Action Agency, CAMP Rehoboth and La Plaza Delaware.
State and Local Governmental Memberships – Delaware League of Local Governments and Sussex County Association of Towns, and Son of the American Legion.
PLATFORMS1 OUR MISSION Gene Dvornick's priorities for District 20 Fiscal Responsibility Taxpayers deserve a government that spends wisely and lives within its means.
Drawing on decades of financial management experience and a Master of Accountancy, Gene will advocate for responsible budgeting, transparency, and eliminating waste while protecting essential public services. ​ Smart Growth & Infrastructure Sussex County continues to grow rapidly.
Gene believes growth should be planned responsibly, balancing economic opportunity with investments in roads, utilities, public safety, and infrastructure while preserving the quality of life that makes our communities special. ​ Strong Public Safety After more than 40 years in the volunteer fire service, Gene understands the importance of supporting firefighters, EMS professionals, law enforcement, and emergency management personnel.
He will work to ensure first responders have the resources, training, and equipment needed to keep our communities safe. ​ Local Government Solutions Having served as both a Town Manager and elected Town Council member, Gene knows that the best solutions often begin at the local level.
He will advocate for policies that respect local decision-making and provide municipalities with the tools they need to serve residents effectively. ​ Economic Opportunity Small businesses are the backbone of Sussex County's economy.
Gene supports policies that encourage entrepreneurship, reduce unnecessary regulatory burdens, attract investment, and create quality jobs while maintaining fiscal responsibility. ​ Government Accountability Gene believes public office is a public trust.
He is committed to honest, transparent leadership, listening to constituents, and making decisions based on what is best for the people of District 20, not politics. ​ Community First Throughout his career, Gene has focused on serving others.
Whether mentoring youth, volunteering in the fire service, or leading nonprofit organizations, he believes strong communities are built through service, collaboration, and respect for every individual. ​ Practical Leadership Gene isn't running to make headlines – he's running to solve problems.
He brings decades of real-world experience managing budgets, leading teams, responding to emergencies, and delivering results.
His approach is simple: listen, work hard, build consensus, and put the interests of Sussex County families first.
