Accessibility
This campaign website should be usable by as many Arkansans as possible — including people who use keyboards, screen readers, or reduced-motion preferences.
We design public pages with clear headings, visible focus states, click-to-play video (no autoplay of campaign statements), and text alternatives for photographs.
Transcripts are published when reviewed and ready.
If you encounter a barrier on this site, email kelly@kellygrappe.com with the page URL and a short description.
We will work to fix it.
Official election and registration services are provided by the State of Arkansas and county clerks—not by this campaign.
