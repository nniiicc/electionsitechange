Future service model A voter education hub, not a scavenger hunt Kelly believes the Secretary of State should be proactive about voter education: clear dates, clear steps, plain-language ballot information, and explanations people can replay or share.
Key dates The 2026 dates are on this page now — registration October 5, early voting October 19 through November 2, Election Day November 3.

What is on the ballot Voters deserve plain-language explanations of offices, measures, and what a vote can actually change.
How voting works Early voting, absentee voting, ID questions, polling places, county clerk roles, and election commission roles should be explained before confusion spreads.
Results and trust Public education should explain counting timelines, certification, safeguards, and audits in language people can understand.
The goal is service, not spin: a modern office should help Arkansans understand how to use their vote and where official answers live.
