Roles
Starter roles
One person can wear two hats at first—names help the work feel real, not bureaucratic.
- County lead Holds the rhythm, convenes the team, and is the steady line back to campaign staff.
- Events lead Owns RSVPs, room basics, and clear volunteer jobs on event days.
- Outreach lead Keeps invitations going—relational follow-up, institutions, and neighbor-to-neighbor invites.
- Voter education lead Makes registration, deadlines, and voting steps plain enough to share at a kitchen table.
- Media / story lead Captures honest field notes, photos, or quotes so your county’s truth travels accurately.
