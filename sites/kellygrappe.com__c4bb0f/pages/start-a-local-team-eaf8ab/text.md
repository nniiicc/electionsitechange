Do I need experience? − No.
Reliability and curiosity beat résumés.
If you can show up twice in a row, you can learn the rest.
Is this a partisan club? + We’re honest about values—but teams win when they welcome neighbors who don’t share every label.
The work is civic, not performative.
What if my county is mostly one party? + So is much of Arkansas.
The goal is lawful, respectful visibility—neighbor to neighbor, without surprise tricks.
How much time is this? + Most starter teams run on a light weekly touch and one monthly rhythm.
We’ll help you right-size it to your life.
