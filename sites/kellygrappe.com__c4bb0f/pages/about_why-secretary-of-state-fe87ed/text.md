Back to overview Office priorities Get involved Overview Experience Why I'm running Journey Community The People's Voice Initiatives & petitions Kelly is running for Secretary of State because the work that has formed her is the work this office demands: discipline when the rules and the resources do not line up, patience with people who are new to a process, and fierce protectiveness of public trust when cynicism is the cheap exit.
Telecom operations taught her to run real teams , to see process as something you improve on purpose, and to design service so the next person in line is not paying for the last person’s shortcut.
Farming and a small market taught her that neglect and favoritism both have consequences you cannot market away—small operators feel every friction in fees, time, and paperwork, and the office should be built to reduce that drag without lowering standards.
Civic work taught her that democracy is a skill —and that Arkansans will step up when someone meets them with training and respect , not a lecture from on high.
Ballot initiative campaigns taught her that direct democracy is logistics —petitions, notaries, volunteers, and a process that should stay centered on citizens , not dark money and outside interests.
The Secretary of State role is where the threads meet: the systems that make elections , records , and commerce legible in real life— all 75 counties , year after year.
The job is not to win a cable debate.
The job is to administer with steadiness —so that a county clerk in a small office gets answers when something breaks on a Friday, a first-time filer in a garage enterprise gets clarity without being made to feel small, and a voter can trust that the rules were followed even when the outcome is not what they wanted.
That is the standard Kelly is offering: lawful, equal, and transparent —and yes, that includes welcoming tough questions from opponents because confidence without scrutiny is not confidence at all; it is theater.
She is a Democrat who does not expect a free pass on scrutiny —and who believes you earn statewide office by showing up for the whole state , including voters, clerks, and business owners who will never agree on everything else.
