EDUCATION & YOUTH EMPOWERMENT
Education is the foundation of opportunity and long-term community success.

AFFORDABLE HOUSING
Affordable housing is essential to stable families, strong neighborhoods, and economic opportunity.
Aletia Haynes Timmons is committed to policies that expand access to safe, quality, and affordable housing for working families, seniors, and fixed-income residents.
