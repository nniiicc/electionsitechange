top of page VOTE DAISY Balais HARTSFIELD Home About Professional Profile Professional Profile Events Contact Donate VOTE DAISY Balais HARTSFIELD Meet the candidate Daisy Balais Hartsfield Daisy Balais Hartsfield is a lifelong Waipahu resident who serves as your incumbent Representative, coming from an immigrant family.
Raised in Waipahu, she attended Waipahu Elementary School, Waipahu Intermediate School, and Waipahu High School — continuing her education at the University of Hawaii at Manoa, earning a Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) in Psychology, a Master of Social Work (M.S.W.) from the Thompson School of Social Work & Public Health – Concentration in Child and Family, and a Juris Doctor (J.D.) from the William S.
Richardson School of Law.
Daisy wants to give back to the community that raised her.
As a lifelong Waipahu resident, she offers a unique blend of deep-rooted community values and extensive public service experience.
Having served as a House Representative, government attorney, and administrator of a State Department, she has dedicated her career to protecting our most vulnerable populations and improving the lives of Hawai`i residents. ​ As your incumbent Representative, she has leveraged her legal expertise and government knowledge, gaining a reputation as a lawmaker who asks tough questions.
Her lived experience makes her the most qualified candidate to address our district’s needs.
She is committed to building transparent systems, supporting our local economy, and easing burdens on Hawaii’s families while honoring our ancestral values.
Daisy will continue to be a strong advocate for Waipahu, collaborate across the aisle, and remain the accessible, responsive legislator you, and Waipahu, deserve.
Get to know more Daisy supported bills that provided access to affordable healthcare, housing, and social services.
She supported law enforcement and efforts to decrease crime.
She also participated in community activities to build stronger relationships with one another.
Safer Neighborhoods As your incumbent Representative, Daisy is actively working towards Waipahu's future. ...and so much more!
Better Commute and Roads Daisy will improve roads, explore ways to shorten commutes, and monitor transit-orieted development to ensure it done in a smart and responsible way.
Daisy believes that local businesses are key to Waipahu's success.
Supporting local shops, creating a stable workforce, and keeping economic growth right here in our town will help to build a robust future that can make the American Dream a reality.
