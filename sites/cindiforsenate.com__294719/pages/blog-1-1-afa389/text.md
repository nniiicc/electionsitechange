My family, my team, and I hope that you had healthy, restful holidays.
After taking a few days off, the campaign is full steam ahead!
It is now official!
I have filed for, and have received confirmation that I am a candidate for the primary election on May 5, 2026!
At this time, I don’t know of any primary challengers, but they do have a few more weeks to file.
Even if I, or your favorite candidate doesn’t have a primary challenger, it’s still critical that you assert your right to vote.
We have early voting in Indiana, and mail in voting in some instances.
We are called now, more than ever, to do our civic duty and vote!
The process to sign papers to be a candidate was much simpler than I had anticipated.
Logan, Sally, and I, plus our spouses met together and signed on to represent our districts in Southern Indiana.
We also got to sit in the gallery of the Senate to watch a session.
Lawmakers are speeding through this session to save taxpayer dollars since they were called into session early over the redistricting debacle.
The session was about 15 minutes long and I’ve heard auctioneers speak more slowly!
Getting ready to go into the statehouse to file!
It’s official!

We’re still ahead of the game, because most people don’t get started until the January official signing as a candidate or even later.
As you might know, I’m one of those people who loves to claim a whole month to celebrate my birthday (November 12) regardless of the year.
My mom passed away at age 38 of a genetic cancer so still being here is worth celebrating!
I’ve posted a video asking my friends and family to contribute to my campaign.
As it happens, I’m turning 49 and I’m running to represent district 49, so it only makes sense to ask for a $49 contribution.
I’ve been so honored by the response.

Eddie Glaude, Jr.
I got to meet him and tell him about the campaign.
He had some encouraging words to share.

Eddie Glaude, Jr., author of We Are The Leaders We’re Looking For.

To be honest, I took about a half a lap, but my youngest, who I call Sporty Spice had to win.

As the board chair of Holly’s House ( www.hollyshouse.org ) it was my honor to attend the 4th annual Brian Turpin service awards.
Retired Detective Brian Turpin was determined to bring a children’s advocacy center to Evansville after hearing about the model at a conference more than #ago.
His hard work and dedication brought it to life, and is now nationally accredited and a grantee from the World Childhood Foundation (Queen Silvia of Sweden’s organization).
