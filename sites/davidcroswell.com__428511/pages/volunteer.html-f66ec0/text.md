Volunteer
Help win this race, neighbor to neighbor.
Winning a campaign in East Honolulu takes neighbors talking to neighbors.
Whether you have five minutes or five hours, you can help.
Ways to help
There is useful work for every schedule.
Small, consistent acts add up: one conversation, one sign, one coffee, one call, one shared post.
- Knock doors
- Host a coffee or meet-and-greet
- Wave signs
- Make calls
- Deliver signs
- Share content online
- Help with events
- Write a letter of support
