Please try again later Why I'm Running After much prayer and thoughtful conversations with my wife, Michele, our family, and so many people across our community, I made the decision to run for State House District 138.
It has been an honor to serve the people of Harris, Muscogee, and Troup Counties, and together we have made real progress.
I believe we need steady, experienced leaders who will stand up for us and advance commonsense, conservative solutions to the challenges we face.
I grew up here, raised my family here, built a business here, and have served here.
I have deep roots in our community.
I have never been interested in the spotlight or titles, but I have always been passionately committed to serving our community—from comforting friends and neighbors in times of need to fighting for our interests at the State Capitol— I have always worked to provide a strong voice for our families when they need it most.
I’m running because, now more than ever, our community needs a strong conservative voice at the State Capitol to secure a bright and prosperous future for our families and to ensure our area is not overlooked in decisions about our tax dollars, economic development, transportation, education, or the protection of our values.
Meet Vance A small businessman, Vance owns and operates, Vance Smith Contracting and Consulting, which provides business development services and consulting to Pond & Co.
Previously, Vance was President & CEO of Vance Smith Construction Co. that was founded in 1974.
Vance has a long record of serving our community.
He serves Harris, Muscogee and Troup Counties in the State House of Representatives.
Prior to that, he served on the Harris County Commission and as Commission Chairman.
In his time in public service, Vance earned a reputation for listening to those he served, doing the right thing, and protecting taxpayers.
