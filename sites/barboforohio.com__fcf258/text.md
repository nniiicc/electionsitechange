John’s Priorities
Properly Fund Public Schools
I believe in the importance of strong public schools, and will work to fully fund the bipartisan Fair School Funding Plan.
Responsibly Reduce Property Taxes
I will work to lower property taxes without endangering our schools, safety forces, or local government services.
End the Data Center Giveaway
I will work to end the wholesale giveaway of our taxes and natural resources to tech companies and data centers.
Honesty and Transparency
Ohioans deserve honesty and transparency in Columbus.
I will work to end the corruptiion and restore faith in government.
