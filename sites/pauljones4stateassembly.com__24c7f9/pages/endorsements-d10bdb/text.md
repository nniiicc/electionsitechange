After nearly two decades of single-party control, families, small businesses, and community members across District 62 are demanding accountable leadership, safer neighborhoods, and economic vitality.
The respected leaders endorsing Paul Jones share a unified mission: restoring balance, safeguarding rights, and making California prosperous and golden once again.
Steve Hilton Candidate for Governor A former senior advisor to the UK Prime Minister, policy thinker, and veteran broadcaster, Steve Hilton leads the "Golden Slate" to champion common-sense solutions in Sacramento.
His endorsement reflects full confidence in Paul Jones to advance reform in the State Assembly.
Hilton is building a grassroots coalition focused on dismantling regulatory gridlock, lowering the cost of living, restoring public safety, and making government directly accountable to working families.
Gloria Romero Candidate for Lieutenant Governor A former Democratic State Senate Majority Leader and dedicated education reformer, Gloria Romero brings courageous cross-party leadership to the Golden Slate.
Her backing highlights Paul's focus on empowering parents and improving local schools.
Romero has spent decades fighting for educational freedom, civil rights, and state government transparency, proving that protecting children and families transcends partisan lines.
Michael Gates Candidate for Attorney General As Huntington Beach City Attorney, Michael Gates has repeatedly stood up against Sacramento overreach to defend constitutional rights and local control.
His support reinforces Paul's commitment to law and order in Assembly District 62.
Gates represents the defense of public safety and rule of law, focusing on repealing soft-on-crime mandates, supporting law enforcement officers, and keeping communities secure.
Herb Morgan Candidate for State Controller An accomplished finance executive and investor, Herb Morgan brings essential private-sector economic discipline to state financial oversight.
His endorsement signals Paul's commitment to fiscal sanity and protecting taxpayers.
Morgan aims to bring rigorous audits, structural transparency, and fiscal responsibility to California's budget, curbing wasteful spending and lowering tax burdens on working citizens.
Bishop Juan Carlos Mendez Spiritual Leader & Community Advocate A prominent voice for faith, social justice, and community empowerment across Southern California, Bishop Mendez brings moral clarity and deep local trust to Paul Jones's campaign.
Bishop Mendez mobilizes faith-based organizations to address humanitarian challenges, strengthen family structures, and revitalize underserved neighborhoods from the ground up.
Churches in Action Network of Over 2,500 Churches Representing a massive, compassionate coalition of over 2,500 local churches, Churches in Action operates on the frontlines of community outreach.
Mobilizing thousands of volunteers, Churches in Action actively serves the needy, feeds the hungry, and supports displaced families, demonstrating the transformative power of local, faith-driven initiative over bureaucratic mandates.
David Hernandez President, Los Angeles Hispanic Republican Club A tireless advocate for economic opportunity, civil rights, and civic participation, David Hernandez leads one of the region's premier Hispanic leadership organizations.
Hernandez is instrumental in uniting Hispanic families, entrepreneurs, and neighborhood leaders around free-enterprise principles, parental rights, and safer communities across Los Angeles County.
Gun Owners of California GOC As the premier defense organization for second amendment rights in the state, Gun Owners of California endorses Paul Jones for his unwavering commitment to constitutional liberties.
GOC fights daily in Sacramento to safeguard civil liberties, defend self-defense rights for law-abiding citizens, and challenge unconstitutional mandates.
