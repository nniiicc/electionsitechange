Why I'm Running Paul Jones is a candidate for California State Assembly District 62.
He has been a California resident since 1973 and has lived in Lakewood since 2001.
Raised on Chicago's South Side as the eldest of four children in a working-class family, Jones experienced significant hardship early in life.
After serving 23 months in combat during the Vietnam War as a United States Marine, Paul returned home and built a career spanning the entertainment industry and public education.
He is a disabled veteran as a result of injuries sustained during his service.
In retirement, Jones pursued formal study in politics, which led him to seek public office.
District 62 includes Huntington Park, South Gate, Lynwood, Paramount, Bellflower, and Lakewood — where he and his family have resided for more than two decades.
