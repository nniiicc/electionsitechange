Keep More of What You Earn
Making South Carolina a place where families can build a future means cutting the tax burden, not growing government.
House Republicans delivered a major income-tax cut, lowered rates, and put the state on a path toward a flatter, simpler system — closer to zero income tax — so working families and small businesses keep more of their own money.

That means a larger share of a home’s value is shielded from property taxes, and more of a fixed income stays in the household.
