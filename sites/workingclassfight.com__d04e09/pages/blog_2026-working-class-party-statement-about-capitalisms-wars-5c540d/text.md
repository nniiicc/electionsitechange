Since 1856, 170 years ago, only two parties have run the country.
No matter which party was in power – Democrat or Republican – they worked to enrich the capitalist class at the expense of working people.
For 170 years, both parties took the country to war, after campaigning on the promise to keep the country out of war.
Exploitation of the workers, enormous profit for the bosses, wars on other people – this is the long record of both parties.
We are workers, proud of our class, and proud of those who came before us.
Eugene V.
Debs was condemned to prison in 1918 because he dared to say that the workers have no use for the capitalists and their wars.
He spoke for the workers and farming people.
We have no use for the capitalist class.
They are the main enemy of democracy.
They plunder the population, they corrupt the court system, and they control the election system.
They ruthlessly exploit the working class and devastate the country.
The capitalist class plunges the country into foreign wars – today against Iran, tomorrow against, perhaps, Cuba.
The day after, who knows, maybe against China.
The workers of other countries are not our enemies.
The capitalists are not our friends.
Wars for conquest and plunder do not serve us.
Workers slaughter each other while our masters become fat from the spoils of war.
The capitalist class is carrying out a class war here at home against working people.
The time has come for working people to organize our own war, a war of the working class against the exploitation, rapacity and violence of capitalism.
It is time for workers to stand together as one class, regardless of race or nationality, sex or age.
It is time to fight to throw out the rule of the capitalist class; time to work for the emancipation of our class and of humanity.
These words came out of the speeches of Debs, someone who proudly called himself a revolutionary socialist.
They can be the workers’ guidepost as the capitalist world rushes into its next war.
