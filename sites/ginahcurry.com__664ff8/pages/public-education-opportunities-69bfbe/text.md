Public Education Opportunities Gina is a strong supporter and advocate of public education.
While maintaining an equivalent and equitable school situation is difficult it is not impossible.
Since participating on the school board my goals are simplistic but difficult when adjusting and advocating culture change.
While many people look at the complexity of public education she finds that some of the equitable changes are found in large financial choices like new buildings, fields, and programs.
Yet these changes can also be made by listening to the various groups of people.
In the 164th we are diversified with people of all nationalities, platforms, cultures, and dreams.
We must hear them all and make adjustments as needed.
