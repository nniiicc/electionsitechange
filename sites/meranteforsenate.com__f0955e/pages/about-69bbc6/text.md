Merante Anthony J.
Merante is an accountant and businessman whose career has centered primarily on accounting, tax services, and executive roles in small and mid-sized companies, particularly in the Yonkers and Westchester County area.
Education and Professional Credentials • Bachelor of Science in Accounting, St.
John's University (1982) • Became a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) in 1987 • Member of the New York State Society of CPAs since 1988 This background established him in the accounting and financial management field early in his career.
Political Career Merante entered local politics as a community advocate and civic leader.
He served for years as president of the Sprain Lake Knolls Civic Association, where he worked on neighborhood issues and local initiatives.
In 2017, he was elected to the Yonkers City Council for the 6th District and served two terms on the council.
Personal Life Merante is married to Yolanda Merante, and the couple has two children.
The family has deep ties to the Yonkers community, and their children attended Yonkers public schools.
