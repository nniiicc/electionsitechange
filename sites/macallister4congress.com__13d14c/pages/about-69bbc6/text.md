Tyler Macallister has spent his life on the South Coast.
A lifelong Cape Codder, he settled in Mattapoisett with his wife, Cindy, in 2001 — where, together with their daughter, Alexandra, and their black lab, Zeke, they built a life centered on family, hard work, public service, and protecting the coastal way of life that makes Southeastern Massachusetts unique.
For more than two decades, Tyler has served Mattapoisett in multiple community leadership roles, including five terms as an elected member of the Select Board.
That experience — balancing budgets, listening to neighbors, and getting things done at the local level — is exactly what's missing in Washington.
After years of watching the federal government grow more disconnected from the people it's supposed to serve, Tyler believes Massachusetts deserves leadership grounded in real-world experience, common sense, and accountability.
He's running for Congress in the 9th District to be a steady, accessible voice for the Cape, the Islands, the South Coast, and the South Shore — and to protect the fishing heritage and coastal communities that define this corner of the Commonwealth.
