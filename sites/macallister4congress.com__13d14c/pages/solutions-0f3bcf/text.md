Home About Solutions Events Media Get Involved Yard Sign Ballot Positions Donate How to Vote BALLOT POSITIONS ☰ Common-sense solutions Tyler loves this district — Cape Cod, the Islands, the South Coast, and the South Shore — and he’s ready to fight for every corner of it.
Here’s where he stands.
I’M WITH TYLER.
Cost of Living Groceries, fuel, and housing keep climbing while paychecks stay flat.
Tyler will take on the mandates and Washington spending driving up kitchen-table costs, and fight for common-sense policy that rewards work and lets families in the 9th get ahead instead of just getting by.
Reliable, Affordable Energy Families and seniors shouldn't have to choose between heating their homes and paying the bills.
Tyler backs an all-of-the-above energy approach that puts reliable, affordable power ahead of ideology — and works to drive our sky-high energy costs back down.
Taxes & Bringing Our Dollars Home We send our hard-earned money to Washington and watch it disappear.
Tyler will fight to cut the waste, hold Washington accountable for every dollar, and bring our tax dollars back home to the Cape, the Islands, the South Coast, and the South Shore.
Sustainable Fishing & the Working Waterfront The South Coast was built on the water, and our fishermen deserve a fighter in Washington.
Tyler will stand up to the federal overreach and bad rules choking the fleet, and push for practical, science-based policy that keeps our working waterfront alive for the next generation.
Our Coastal Way of Life The coastline, the harbors, and the heritage of Southeastern Massachusetts are what make this place home.
Tyler will fight to keep our way of life in local hands — protecting the shore, the small businesses, and the traditions that outside interests keep trying to trade away.
Offshore Wind & Data Centers No industrial wind farms in our fishing grounds, and no data centers draining our grid and our water.
Tyler will stand against the projects that ship profits out of state while sticking our fishermen and ratepayers with the bill — and demand our coast come first.
Accountable Government Tyler will be the 9th District's bridge to Washington — carrying our voice straight into the room and bringing real accountability home.
He'll bring the same open, honest, results-first leadership he showed in local government to every community on the Cape, the Islands, the South Coast, and the South Shore.
Public & Child Safety Every family deserves to feel safe in their own neighborhood.
Tyler will back our local police, stand up for common-sense child-safety standards, and fight to keep the communities of the 9th District places where our kids can grow up secure.
Veterans & Seniors Our veterans and seniors kept their word — Washington should keep its word to them.
Tyler will fight to protect the benefits they earned through a lifetime of service and work, and make sure the promises made to them are promises kept.
I’M WITH TYLER.
