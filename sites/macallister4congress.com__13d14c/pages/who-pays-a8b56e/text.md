I'm not going to tell you what that means — I don't know what it means, and nobody who wants your vote should pretend to.
But it left me with a question, and I haven't been able to get a straight answer to it from anybody.
Every one of them has an end date They were built with one — about twenty‑five years.
When that day comes, somebody writes a check for the tugs, the cranes, the barges and the cutting.
Denmark already ran the experiment Vindeby, the first offshore wind farm in the world, went in the water in 1991.
It ran twenty‑five years and came down in 2017, with rising maintenance cost a stated reason.
About 1,800 European offshore turbines reach the end of their design life by 2030.
Nearly 20,000 by 2040.
And here is the part that should worry you The federal rule says the removal money goes up before the turbines do — 30 CFR 585.
Vineyard Wind asked to be let out of it, and BOEM said yes.
The money isn't due until year fifteen of a twenty-year contract , and the approval letter never says how much.
It calls it “a BOEM-determined amount.” Read the approval letter yourself — it's four pages Add your name No new project approved until the removal money is posted first, in a number the public can read.
