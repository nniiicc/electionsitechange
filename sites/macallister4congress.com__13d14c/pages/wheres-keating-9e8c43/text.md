Macallister for Congress Massachusetts’ Ninth Congressional District The Honorable William R.
Keating United States House of Representatives 2372 Rayburn House Office Building Washington, DC 20515 September 17, 2026 Formal request for an in-person debate Dear Representative Keating: My name is R.
Tyler Macallister, and I am seeking the Massachusetts Ninth Congressional District seat in the November 3, 2026 general election as a Republican.
I would like to schedule a series of three debates within Massachusetts Congressional District 9.
Toby Leary of Cape Gun Works in Hyannis has offered to host the debate, and Ed Lambert of WXTK has offered to moderate debate #1.
The second and third debates would occur between September 28th and October 16th at a mutually agreeable location and with a mutually agreeable moderator.
I am requesting that the first in-person debate occur the week of September 21–25.
Failure to respond to this letter and to my other inquiries — including multiple submissions through your Congressional contact portal — shall be deemed by me and my campaign as a refusal to publicly discuss the policies and needs of this district, and your accomplishments supporting Congressional District 9 in Washington, DC.
I will also call your office and leave another message.
Respectfully, R.
