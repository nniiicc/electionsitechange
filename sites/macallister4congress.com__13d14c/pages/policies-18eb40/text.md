Policy Agreement
Federal campaign-finance notices for Macallister for Congress, plus our privacy policy and terms.
Tyler Macallister is a candidate for the U.S.
House of Representatives (Massachusetts's 9th Congressional District).
Federal elections are governed by the Federal Election Commission (FEC).
Contributions to the campaign are subject to federal law:
- Contributions are not tax-deductible for federal income-tax purposes.
- By law, the campaign may only accept contributions from U.S. citizens and lawfully admitted permanent residents (green-card holders).
It may not accept contributions from foreign nationals or corporations.
- Contributions are subject to federal per-person, per-election limits.
- Federal law requires the campaign to report the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of individuals whose contributions exceed $200 in an election cycle.
- Contributions must be made from a contributor's own funds, not reimbursed by another person, and not made on a corporate or business card.
