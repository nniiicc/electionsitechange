The Priorities
Where Tyler stands
Common-sense priorities for Cape Cod, the Islands, the South Coast, and the South Shore.
Protect the fishing fleet and the working waterfront with practical, science-based policy that keeps the South Coast economy strong for the next generation.
Bring down the price of groceries, fuel, and housing so working families can get ahead instead of just getting by.
Keep our communities and our kids safe — backing local law enforcement and common-sense safety standards across the district.
Cut the waste, end the games, and make Washington answer to the district — not the other way around.
Honor the promises made to those who served and protect the benefits seniors have earned over a lifetime.
Protect the coastline, the harbors, and the way of life that makes Southeastern Massachusetts home — with a balanced, common-sense approach.
