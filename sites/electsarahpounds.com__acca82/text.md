Before I’m a candidate, I’m a wife, a mom to four incredible daughters, and a lifelong Oakland County resident.
I believe government should work better for everyday families like mine.
For years, I've worked to solve problems in our community by serving on Oakland County housing and community development boards, expanding housing opportunities, and advocating for families.
The more I served, the more I realized I wanted a seat at the table where those decisions were being made.
That's why I'm running for State Representative—to bring practical experience, fresh ideas, and the perspective of a working parent to Lansing.
