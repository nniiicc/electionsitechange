Our current Representatives are no longer in touch with what the people of Western North Carolina are asking for.

Get to Know Me I was born in Madison County and moved to Boone in 1990 to attend App State.
I grew up the child of parents who were educators in the public school system.
We also lived on a farm where we grew 10 acres of burly tobacco and tended numerous gardens.
Over the years we’ve had numerous farm animals.
After moving to Boone, I studied Political Science at Appalachian State University.
During college I worked my way through school at Pizza Hut.
This is where I developed a love for working in food.
In 1998 I had an opportunity to go back to App State and continue working in the food industry.
During my 30+ years in Boone, working at App State, I got involved in campus and the surrounding community.
I have been Staff Senate President, a member of the App State Board of Trustees, Chair of the Occupational Health and Safety Committee for 15 years, Watauga County Planning Board for 8 years (4 of those as Vice Chair) chaired the County Strategic Planning Committee, served on the WAMY Board, the App Theatre Board, the Watauga Arts Council, the Watauga Department of Social Services Board, the Watauga County History Museum and the Southern Appalachian Historical Association as well as many other committees and Non Profits.
I am married to Shelley Wallin and we have a beautiful daughter, Keller, who is a Senior at Watauga High School.
We attend Grace Lutheran Church, where I have had many roles and currently am an Assisting Minister.
