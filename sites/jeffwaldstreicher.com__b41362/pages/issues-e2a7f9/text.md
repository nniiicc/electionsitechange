ISSUES
Education
"Like me, Jeff Waldstreicher has kids in our public schools, so he knows how important it is to keep our schools great.
I'm voting for him because I know he'll always work to ensure public schools have the resources they need."
-- Anne Balcer, Kensington
Jeff believes that every child deserves a great education.
This is deeply personal to Jeff: all three of his children attend Montgomery County Public Schools just like he did.
As State Senator, Jeff has fought to keep our public schools strong.
Jeff's Plan:
- Close the achievement gap by reducing class size and increasing school funding to ensure that all students, regardless of zip code or family income, can get a high-quality education.
- Pay teachers fairly so they can spend their time and effort focusing on what's important--equipping our kids for success in the 21st century.
- Listen closely to parents, who deserve a responsive and helpful school system.
- Invest in The Blueprint for Maryland’s Future, our state’s once-in-a-generation education reform package.
- Protect our students by advocating for commonsense gun reform and opposing any efforts to arm teachers in Maryland.
Gun Violence
“Jeff Waldstreicher has always been a vocal supporter of gun violence prevention policies.
He prioritizes the issue of reducing gun violence in our community and isn’t afraid to stand up to the gun lobby.”
-- Jen Pauliukonis, Fmr.
President, Marylanders to Prevent Gun violence
As a husband, father, and proud progressive, Jeff believes we must crack down on gun violence.
As State Senator, he's shown a relentless commitment to our shared values, including commonsense gun safety reforms.
Jeff's Record of Achievement:
- Received "Gun Sense Candidate of Distinction" by Moms Demand Action (2018)
- Banned "bump stocks," which turn guns into automatic weapons
- Passed "red flag" legislation, to ensure that folks who are a threat to themselves or others can't obtain guns
- Stopped convicted domestic abusers from being able to own a gun
- Co-sponsor of legislation to ban “ghost guns,” which are wreaking havoc in Montgomery County
Criminal Justice Reform
“Jeff has lived a life dedicated to service and problem-solving, with an unwavering dedication to racial reconciliation and justice.
He walks the walk; he does the work.
Joining with Black leaders like Chairman Will Smith and Senator Jill Carter, Jeff has led on critical criminal justice reforms, including expanding re-entry opportunities and decriminalizing cannabis.”
-- Henriot St.
Gerard, community leader
Jeff believes in racial justice and reforming our criminal justice system.
As Vice Chair of the powerful Judicial Proceedings Committee, Jeff is a recognized leader in ensuring our justice system is fair and equitable.
Jeff's Record of Achievement:
- Passed bold legislation to provide Marylanders with a second chance by automatically clearing their criminal records of minor offenses
- Fought for re-entry services for formerly incarcerated Marylanders
- Helped lead the charge to pass the Justice Reinvestment Act, Maryland’s historic criminal justice reform legislation
- Voted to decriminalize personal amounts of cannabis
