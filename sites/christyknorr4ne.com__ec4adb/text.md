As a former welfare mom and longtime Omaha neighbor, she knows firsthand what working families are up against: the rising cost of basics, the struggle to access care, and systems that too often leave people behind.
Now running for the Nebraska Legislature, Christy is driven by the same values that have guided her entire life, and a belief that every family deserves support to thrive, not just survive.

Filter by what matters to you, then tap any issue to read the full promise.
All priorities Working Families Communities Land & Water Public Safety Working Families + Lower everyday costs Groceries, utilities, and prescription drugs.
Take on the rising cost of basics so families can thrive, not just survive, with real relief on groceries, utility bills, and prescription drugs.
Working Families + Protect paid sick leave No one should choose health over a paycheck.
Protect and expand paid sick leave so no Nebraskan has to choose between caring for their health and keeping their paycheck.
Working Families + Defend earned wages Keep voter-approved wage increases.
Defend voter-approved minimum wage increases and keep wages in step with the real, rising cost of living.
Working Families + Reproductive freedom Personal healthcare decisions, free of interference.
Protect reproductive freedom so every Nebraskan can make personal healthcare decisions without political interference.
Working Families + Dignity for LGBTQ+ Nebraskans Equal rights, safety, affirming care.
Guarantee dignity, safety, and equal rights for LGBTQ+ Nebraskans, including access to affirming healthcare and freedom from discrimination.
Communities + Invest in what families rely on Housing, childcare, healthcare workers.
Invest in affordable housing, healthcare workers, and childcare so parents and caregivers can work without going broke.
Communities + Responsible property tax relief Cut burdens without gutting schools.
Cut property tax burdens responsibly, without sacrificing public schools or the essential services working families depend on.
Communities + Fix marijuana delays Legalize and regulate, sensibly.
Support legalizing and regulating marijuana in a way that fixes the delays and uncertainty families face today.
Land & Water + Clean water for rural Nebraska Confront nitrate contamination.
Confront well-documented challenges in Nebraska's agricultural system: nitrate contamination, overuse of groundwater, and the strain industrial operations place on rural communities.
Land & Water + Hold polluters accountable Clear standards, real oversight.
Strengthen oversight of large corporations whose practices contribute to water pollution, soil degradation, or public-health risks, ensuring they meet clear environmental standards.
Land & Water + Stand with family farmers Sustainable, long-term land health.
Support family farmers and responsible producers with sustainable practices that improve soil health and protect long-term land and water quality.
Public Safety + Justice that protects survivors Accountability for serious offenders.
Strengthen Nebraska's criminal justice system so it protects survivors, supports safe communities, and holds serious offenders accountable.
Public Safety + Close the assault loophole End felony-to-misdemeanor plea-downs.
Close loopholes in state law that allow individuals charged with felony sexual assault to plead down to a misdemeanor, so consequences reflect the seriousness of the crime.
Public Safety + Mental-health crisis response Trained responders who de-escalate.
Expand mental-health crisis response teams, social workers, and trained professionals who can de-escalate situations and connect people with care.
