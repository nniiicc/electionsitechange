Assemblymember Cecilia Aguiar-Curry First woman mayor of Winters Cecilia has a track record of getting things done as a community leader and businesswoman, Planning Commissioner, City Councilmember, and first woman Mayor of Winters.
She continues that focus on accomplishment as Assembly Majority Leader.
With degrees in accounting and business, Cecilia has specialized in water use, strategic planning and community outreach.
Cecilia brings a common-sense approach, based on years of experience, to the challenges facing California and District 4.
Business woman Family Farmer Her passion and appreciation for agriculture started when she was a child, working summers and after school on the farm, and as a co-owner of the family farm.
As a member of the Assembly Ag Committee, Cecilia is fighting for the ag industry and particularly small, family farmers.
About the 4th District The newly drawn 4th Assembly District includes all of Yolo, Napa, Lake and Colusa counties, along with the city of Sonoma and communities of Boyes Hot Springs, El Verano and Eldridge.
