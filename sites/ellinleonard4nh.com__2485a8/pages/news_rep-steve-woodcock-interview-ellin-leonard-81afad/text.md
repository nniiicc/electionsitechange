Steve Woodcock Endorses Ellin Leonard on WMWV Radio Steve Woodcock: Representative Steve Woodcock here.
Ellin, you’re running for Conway’s State Rep in Concord.
Ellin Leonard: I am because we need to make sure that Conway’s hard working people are represented and heard loud and clear in the State House.
Rest assured while you're back here in Conway volunteering locally, I will be in Concord saying what needs to be said and I will not back down.
Woodcock: I watched you for over 20 years as a library trustee, Conway budget committee member, volunteer in the Valley always working hard in the background, just doing the hard work.
You know New Hampshire can't continue to cut state taxes to help the wealthy and pass that cost back down to a local level.
Leonard: Those cuts show up on our property tax bill, increase our rents and make it tough to live in Conway.
Those lost dollars could help our young families and our seniors make ends meet Woodcock : Please vote for Ellen Leonard, Ben Colbert and David Paige as Conway’s representatives.
Let's get back to helping people and not making their life more difficult.
