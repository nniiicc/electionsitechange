Meet Laura Priorities Donate Laura Murvartian, Candidate for Georgia State Senate, District 48 Long before I ever thought about running for office, I moved when I was eight years old from Mexico to Minnesota, where my parents sought a better life for us and worked tirelessly in pursuit of the American Dream for our family.
I am running for office because the dream my parents achieved through hard work is slipping away for too many families living in Georgia and in this district.
I am ready to apply my work ethic, values, and experience as a business executive, small business owner, and nonprofit leader to ensure everyone in Georgia has the opportunity to thrive.
And I am running because I feel an obligation to give back to the country that has given me and my family so much, and to the state in which my husband and I raised our children.
This year, the stakes are too high for me to sit this one out.
Meet Laura Why I’m Running I am running for State Senator because I believe that silence and inaction means acceptance, and I refuse to accept less than what hardworking Georgia families deserve.
I am running for office because I know we can do better.
Together, we can make Georgia a place where everyone has the opportunity to thrive, where our rights are protected, and where we all feel safe.
