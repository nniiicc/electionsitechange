Senator Mark Finchem has earned the endorsement of the Arizona Police Association (APA) for his 2026 re-election campaign, reflecting his longstanding support for Arizona’s law enforcement community and public safety priorities.
The endorsement was approved by the APA Board of Directors following discussions regarding key public safety issues facing Arizona communities.
In a message to Senator Finchem, APA representatives expressed their appreciation for his work on behalf of law enforcement and confirmed the organization’s support for his re-election effort.
“The APA board approved our recommendation to endorse your re-election campaign,” wrote APA representatives.
“The APA greatly appreciates your support of public safety.”
Senator Finchem said he is honored to receive the endorsement and remains committed to supporting the men and women who serve and protect Arizona communities.
“I am so grateful for the confidence and support of the Arizona Police Association,” said Finchem.
“I know from experience that our law enforcement officers put themselves on the line every day to keep Arizona families safe.
I will always fight for policies that support public safety, uphold the rule of law, and ensure our officers have the resources they need to do their jobs effectively.”
The Arizona Police Association represents law enforcement professionals across the state and advocates for policies that strengthen public safety and support Arizona’s police officers.
