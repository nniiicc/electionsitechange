These parties, trapped in a cycle of ideological warfare, often fail to represent the real interests of our citizens.
They have become more focused on defeating one another than on solving the challenges facing our state.
In Nevada Senate District 6, voter registration shows a clear opportunity for change.
33% of voters are Democrats, 26% are Republicans, but the largest group, a plurality of 41%, are non-partisan or belong to third parties .
This means that if independents and non-partisan voters unite, we can win this district and break free from the cycle of partisan gridlock.
As an Independent candidate, Brad Lee Barnhill offers a fresh approach to politics.
It’s time for civil debate—where we listen to one another rather than shout across party lines.
People who yell at each other can’t hear what the other person is saying .
This divisiveness keeps us from addressing the issues that truly affect Nevadans, from economic security to education, healthcare, and beyond.
One of the most common misconceptions heard on the campaign trail is that voting for an Independent is a “wasted vote.” This couldn’t be further from the truth.
Let’s break that myth down.
Why Voting Independent is NOT a Wasted Vote Partisanship Is the Problem, Not the Solution Nevada’s major parties have been locked in a battle of extremes for nearly a generation.
Instead of working together to move the state forward, partisan politics have stalled progress and divided our communities.
As #YourIndependentVoice, Brad Lee Barnhill is not beholden to the divisive rhetoric of either party.
Brad can build bridges, work across the aisle, and focus on solutions that benefit all Nevadans—not just those who fit neatly into a political box.
We Deserve a Government That Works for Everyone When you vote for an Independent, you are voting for someone who will put people over party.
Unlike career politicians tied to special interests or rigid party platforms, #YourIndependentVoice represents you —the voter.
By focusing on what’s best for Nevada, Brad Lee Barnhill will advocate for common-sense policies that promote economic stability, educational excellence, and a prosperous future.
Breaking the Two-Party Monopoly Voting for an Independent is a direct challenge to the two-party system that has stifled true democracy in Nevada.
With 41% of District 6 voters registered as non-partisan or third-party , the numbers show that independents hold the key to real change.
When you cast your vote for an Independent, you send a clear message that you won’t settle for more of the same.
We need new voices in our government, voices that can bring fresh ideas and innovative approaches to the table.
Brad Lee Barnhill’s Vision: A Diverse, Resilient Nevada Nevada is famous for its tourism and gaming industries, but these sectors also expose us to economic risks.
The COVID-19 pandemic was a harsh reminder of the dangers of relying too heavily on one industry.
When tourism came to a halt, businesses closed, jobs disappeared, and families struggled.
We cannot afford to wait for another crisis to show us that we need economic diversity .
As #YourIndependentVoice in the Nevada Senate, Brad Lee Barnhill is committed to policies that promote a more resilient economy—one that provides stable, long-term job opportunities for all Nevadans.
This means investing in education, fostering technological innovation, encouraging sustainable practices , and supporting a business-friendly environment that helps local entrepreneurs thrive.
Brad Barnhill has a plan for economic diversification in Nevada A Vote for an Independent Is a Vote for Nevada’s Future Brad Lee Barnhill is running for Nevada Senate District 6 to bring real change to our state.
Brad believes in a future where people work together to solve problems, where leaders engage in civil debate , and where the economy is built to withstand challenges.
Your vote is your voice, and every vote counts.
A vote for an Independent is not just a vote against the status quo—it’s a vote for progress, for unity, and for a Nevada that is stronger, more diverse, and more resilient.
This election, you have the power to choose a new path.
