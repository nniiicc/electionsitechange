I’ll modernize systems for auditability and accessibility—ensuring every legal vote counts without barriers or fraud.
No more partisan games; just honest, efficient processes that rebuild trust for voters across the spectrum.
Support Nevada Businesses & Economic Growth (Business Registrations & Securities Duty) Register entities, regulate securities to protect investors, provide efficient licensing.
From small family businesses to entrepreneurs, I’ll streamline filings and crack down on fraud—fostering job creation and prosperity without unnecessary red tape or favoritism.
Uphold Records, Integrity & the Constitution (Official Record-Keeper Duty) Maintain legislative/executive records, State Seal, bonds; serve on key boards (e.g., Records Committee, Participatory Democracy).
Transparency is non-negotiable.
I’ll protect public access to records, defend constitutional rights, and promote civil participation—ensuring government serves Nevadans, not special interests.
Consensus & Civil Governance The Secretary of State should unite, not divide.
I’ll lead with respect, listen to all sides, and deliver common-sense reforms that protect freedom, liberty, and our shared future.
