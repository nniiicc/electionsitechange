Meet Shane
Born and Raised in Jefferson City.
Shane is a lifelong Jefferson City resident, husband, and father of three.
He's a small business owner of Kampeter Remodeling and co-owner of GKS Management, a Lincoln University graduate, and served Missouri for 11 years as a civilian employee in the Missouri Army National Guard.
He's a parishioner at St.
Stanislaus, a Knights of Columbus member, a homeless shelter volunteer, and a youth sports coach.
He knows this community because he is this community.
