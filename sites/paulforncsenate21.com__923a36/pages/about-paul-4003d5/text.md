"Leadership isn't about holding a title—it's about serving people with integrity, compassion, and purpose." Early Life & Family Legacy Paul's story begins with a family legacy rooted in faith, service, and community leadership.
His family's history spans Apex, North Carolina, Aberdeen in Moore County, and Martinsville, Virginia—places that shaped generations committed to serving others.
Apex, NC A great-grandfather built a church and named it after his wife, so she could serve the community.
Aberdeen, Moore County Another great-grandfather raised three boys and two girls — the foundation of the family's Moore County roots.
Martinsville, VA A great-uncle helped lead "Martinsville for Jesus," carrying the family's ministry legacy forward.
These roots continue to inspire Paul's commitment to serving every community with faith, integrity, and purpose.

Faith Leadership Faith has been the guiding force behind Paul Taylor's lifelong commitment to serving others.
As an Elder at Kingdom Impact Global Ministry, he leads with compassion, integrity, and a servant's heart, believing that true leadership begins by putting people first.
His ministry extends beyond the church, focusing on mentoring individuals, supporting families, strengthening communities, and inspiring others to live with purpose and hope.
Paul's dedication to faith is deeply rooted in his family's legacy of ministry and community service.
Inspired by generations who used their faith to uplift others, he continues that tradition by building partnerships, encouraging unity, and serving those in need throughout District 21.
Whether through outreach, advocacy, or community engagement, Paul believes faith should be demonstrated through action, creating lasting impact and bringing people together for the common good.
Founding the First African-American Caucus in Cumberland County Recognizing the importance of broader community representation, Paul helped establish the first African-American Caucus in Cumberland County.
The organization created opportunities for civic engagement, leadership development, and meaningful dialogue between residents and local government.
