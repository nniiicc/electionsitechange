ABOUT PAUL TAYLOR
Rooted in Faith, Driven By Community
From military service overseas to community leadership throughout Cumberland County, Paul Taylor has dedicated his life to serving others.
His journey is built on faith, leadership, and a commitment to creating stronger communities and greater opportunities for every family.
"Leadership isn't about holding a title—it's about serving people with integrity, compassion, and purpose."
Early Life & Family Legacy
Paul's story begins with a family legacy rooted in faith, service, and community leadership.
His family's history spans Apex, North Carolina, Aberdeen in Moore County, and Martinsville, Virginia—places that shaped generations committed to serving others.
These roots continue to inspire Paul's commitment to serving every community with faith, integrity, and purpose.
Military Service
Paul proudly served in the United States Army, where he developed the discipline, resilience, and leadership skills that continue to guide his public service.
Throughout his military career, he served alongside allies across multiple countries, strengthening international partnerships while protecting American interests.
Faith Leadership
Faith has been the guiding force behind Paul Taylor's lifelong commitment to serving others.
As an Elder at Kingdom Impact Global Ministry, he leads with compassion, integrity, and a servant's heart, believing that true leadership begins by putting people first.
His ministry extends beyond the church, focusing on mentoring individuals, supporting families, strengthening communities, and inspiring others to live with purpose and hope.
Paul's dedication to faith is deeply rooted in his family's legacy of ministry and community service.
Inspired by generations who used their faith to uplift others, he continues that tradition by building partnerships, encouraging unity, and serving those in need throughout District 21.
Whether through outreach, advocacy, or community engagement, Paul believes faith should be demonstrated through action, creating lasting impact and bringing people together for the common good.
Redistricting Testimony
Paul has consistently advocated for fair representation and equitable redistricting that reflects the voices of every community.
His testimony before public bodies has emphasized transparency, accountability, and equal representation in government.
"Every community deserves a voice that is heard, respected, and represented fairly."
