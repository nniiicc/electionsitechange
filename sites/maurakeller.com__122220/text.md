Meet Maura Keller
Maura Keller served 26 years in the US army, rising from enlisted MP to Lieutenant Colonel.
She later served as a health care worker in the Atlanta VA.
Now, Maura’s committed to serving the people of Georgia’s 3rd Congressional District.
- Born in Massachusetts, Maura Keller has lived in Georgia for over 30 years.
As a second-time candidate for Congress, she is committed first and foremost to her loving husband, 4 children, and 2 grandchildren.
Maura enlisted as a Military Policewoman in the US Army out of high school, qualified as an officer, and received the General Douglas MacArthur Leadership Award for her commitment to leadership.
After retiring, she contracted for the DOD before re-training into healthcare.
That’s when she moved into to the Atlanta VA.
- Working at the VA, Maura found that, sadly, dedicated service wasn't enough to help all of her patients.
No amount of following up or calling different offices could fix the quality-of-life-affecting issues her veterans were facing.
Then, when she reached out to her Congressman for help, she got nothing in return.
That’s why she decided to run for Congress, first in 2024 and again in 2026, to actually solve fixable problems in District 3.
She's for helping veterans.
She's for lowering costs.
She's for fixing healthcare in rural areas.
She's for serving the people.
- Maura is rolling out the biggest ground game Democrats have ever seen in Georgia’s 3rd.
Her campaign will knock on more doors, talk to more voters, and reach across the aisle to persuade more conservatives than ever before.
