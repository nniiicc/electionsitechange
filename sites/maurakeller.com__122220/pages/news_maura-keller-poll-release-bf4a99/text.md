Link to Public Release Memorandum .
Survey Overview: The survey finds Brian Jack leading Maura Keller by 5 points on the U.S.
House ballot, a margin within the survey's margin of error.
Keller ties for the highest share of any Democratic candidate tested, and Democratic support varies by 6 points from one race to the next among the same respondents.
The Public Service Commission District 3 race is tied, and a majority of likely voters say the country is on the wrong track.
House results: Jack receives 50% and Keller receives 45%, with 5% undecided.
The 5-point margin falls within the survey's margin of error of ±5.6 percentage points.
Statewide results: Republican Rick Jackson leads Democrat Keisha Lance Bottoms 51% to 45% for governor.
In Public Service Commission District 3, Fitz Johnson and Peter Hubbard are tied at 43%, and 14% of respondents are undecided, the largest undecided share of any race tested.
In District 5, Josh Tolbert leads Shelia Edwards 45% to 39%.
Methodology: VoteNexus, Inc. conducted a survey of 301 likely voters in Georgia's 3rd Congressional District from October 2 to 7, 2026.
Respondents were randomly selected and completed the survey online after receiving a unique link by text message.
Results are unweighted.
The reported margin of error is ±5.6 percentage points at the 95% confidence level.
Candidate order was randomized, and ballot results include respondents who lean toward a candidate.
Percentages may not total 100% because of rounding.
This survey was commissioned by Maura Keller for Congress.
