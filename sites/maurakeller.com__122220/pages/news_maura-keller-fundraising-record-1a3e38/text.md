No candidate has ever raised more than $150,000 in this section of Georgia, and Keller's campaign did so by June 30th, with four months left in her election cycle.
“Our campaign is grassroots funded,” Keller said.
“Over 2,000 individual donors have supported us so far, proving that we don't need to rely on big money corporate PACs and lobbyist groups to fund ourselves.” An increased interest in her campaign comes as Keller focuses in on key issues that reach across the aisle: Data Centers, Social Security, Healthcare, and Veterans Rights.
“Breaking District 3's fundraising record shows just how different this year actually is,” Keller continued, “The amount of bipartisan support for us is mind-blowing.
Nearly every day, lifelong Republicans are stopping me at the grocery store, calling me up to tell me I have their vote.
In 2026, you better believe we'll shock some people up in Washington.” Maura Keller is a retired Lieutenant Colonel who served 26 years in the United States Army, and a former healthcare worker at the Atlanta VA hospital.
She is also a wife, mother of four, and owner of several rescue dogs.
Keller’s frustration with the lack of support for veterans drove her to run for Congress, and her platform is focused on veteran benefits, affordability, healthcare, and rural investment.
