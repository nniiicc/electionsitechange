Is Ben McAdams angling to get his old job back?
The former Democratic congressman who lost to Republican Burgess Owens by a razor-thin margin in 2020 appears to be preparing for a comeback bid—this time on potentially friendlier turf.
McAdams filed an updated statement of candidacy with the Federal Election Commission on Thursday morning, signaling a potential run in what’s currently Utah’s 4th Congressional District.
But thanks to court-ordered redistricting, McAdams might not even end up running in the 4th.
