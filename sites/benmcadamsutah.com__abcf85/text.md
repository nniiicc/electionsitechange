Ben McAdams has always and will always stand up for Utah.
Ben is a 7th-generation Utahn and grew up in West Bountiful, raised by his school-teacher mom, who cared for six children largely alone.
Ben’s mom worked harder than anyone he’d ever met, but no matter how hard she worked, money was always tight.
Some months, the lights went out, and they needed help just to put food on the table.
Ben’s mom taught him the power of hardwork, perseverance and education but most of all, she taught him that kindness and caring for others always carries the day.
To help his family get by, Ben started working at age 11 and since then, he’s worked nearly every job you can imagine – farm work, fast food, janitorial, he even repaired guitars for a while.
After high school, Ben served an LDS mission in Brazil where he saw first hand how basic government services could transform individual lives.
Motivated by his experience and the values instilled by his mom, Ben has committed his adult life to serving Utahns.
From working for Salt Lake City Mayor Ralph Becker, to serving in the State Senate, to Mayor of Salt Lake County and then to Congress – Ben McAdams has always done what’s right, even when it’s hard.
His achievements include:
- Bringing together diverse stakeholders to pass landmark LGBT non-discrimination legislation in Salt Lake City
- Working across the aisle to pass bills to protect Utahns from financial fraud and pyramid schemes in the State Senate
- Championing innovative programs like “Pay for Success” which increased early childhood education in Salt Lake County, which has become a model for the state and the nation.
- Protecting the environment by passing legislation to reduce smog pollution and increase funding for energy efficiency programs.
- Prevented the Trump Administration from restarting nuclear weapons testing which has been linked to cancer in Utahns
Now, Ben is running for Congress because what’s happening in Washington isn’t working for people.
Politicians in both parties are out of touch.
They don’t understand what it’s like to struggle to make ends meet.
Donald Trump and an ineffective Congress are literally pulling food out of the mouths of children, ripping health care away from families, and running our economy to the brink all to put more cash in the pockets of the ultra-wealthy.
With your support, Ben will go to Congress to do what he’s always done – improve the lives of Utahns, even when it’s hard.
Ben lives in Salt Lake City with his wife, Julie, and their four kids.
Ben is running for Congress on a platform focused on lowering housing costs, expanding access to affordable health care, and making everyday life more affordable for Utah families.

Ben’s got a proven record of delivering on policies that support families, safeguard freedoms, and rebuild trust in public service.
