Accomplishments
Senator Maroney worked to pass comprehensive data privacy protections for Connecticut residents, making Connecticut just the 5th state in the country to do so.
- Senator Maroney spearheaded legislation over three years to establish robust data privacy protections for Connecticut residents.
- The legislation grants Connecticut consumers five key rights related to their data privacy.
- It imposes significant responsibilities on corporations regarding data handling and privacy practices.
- The bill has received widespread acclaim for providing some of the strongest privacy protections in the United States.
- International Association for Privacy Professionals, CT Mirror Connecticut Public Radio New Haven Register
