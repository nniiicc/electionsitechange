Her #1 priority is to combat our affordability crisis and provide support to the creation and maintenance of vibrant business environments.
This means opposing attempts to impose new taxes and finding ways to make it easier for businesses to be created here, move here, and hire employees here.
Nancy will fight for every constituent’s access to quality education, including school choice options and vocational training.
She is committed to making our communities safe again, which requires strengthening and enforcement of laws that control the treatment of criminals, as well as prohibiting the possession and use of illegal drugs.
Nancy will apply her business acumen to the state budgeting and appropriations processes, and will support legislation designed to ensure fiscal accountability.
She is fiercely committed to bringing common sense decision-making back to Nevada.
Issues Economy & Affordability Life is no longer affordable in Nevada.
Everything from gas to groceries costs more.
As your Assemblywoman for Nevada Assembly District 12, I will focus on lowering taxes, strengthening and diversifying economic development, and cutting the red tape small business owners face.
I will work to lower the cost of everyday items like food and gas by repealing the laws passed in recent sessions that made everything so much more expensive.
I will support affordable housing initiatives in Clark County and will fight against additional taxes on gas, as well as efforts to impose a state income tax.
If we apply common sense practices and financial insight to state law and budgeting decisions, we can avoid becoming California 2.0.
Read more → Water Our water future is not a political issue; it’s a matter of life and death.
We need strong leadership to prevent further erosion of our Colorado River water rights.
I will look for ways to invest in new water supply and recycling technologies, as well as water storage facilities.
Developers must be more accountable for water usage – we cannot allow them to claim water at the expense of Nevadans.
Read more → Healthcare Nevada families need relief from health insurance premiums and prescription costs, which keep climbing.
Healthcare costs must be controlled and more transparent.
Additionally, our healthcare system is lagging population growth, leading to long wait times and lack of quality solutions.
I will work to expand the availability of providers, promote state-of-the-art treatments, and ensure patients with pre-existing conditions are protected.
Read more → Public Safety If we don’t have safe communities, we don’t have anything.
Sadly, our legislature has passed several laws over the years that protect criminals over victims and laws that make crimes easier to commit with little to no penalties.
As your Assemblywoman for Nevada Assembly District 12, I intend to repeal pro-criminal policies and will prioritize the rights of victims.
I will also work to stop the flow of illegal drugs into Nevada to combat the growing Fentanyl problem.
I am clearly different from the career politicians in Carson City who have tried repeatedly to legalize drugs that will place Nevada residents at further risk.
I will back our police and first responders and will ensure common sense is applied to any legislation that impacts public safety.
Read more → Energy Independence The rate of growth in Nevada’s population is above the national average.
We are also experiencing a data center construction boom to feed AI, with major facilities coming online that will add enormous new demand to our grid.
Nevada cannot be dependent on California for its energy supply.
Their policies, their pricing decisions, and their grid reliability are not under Nevada control — yet Nevada families and businesses pay the price when those choices fall short.
We need energy that Nevada controls — produced here for the people who live here.
As your Assemblywoman for Nevada Assembly District 12, I will support an all-of-the-above energy strategy that keeps the lights on, keeps rates affordable, and keeps Nevada’s economy growing.
Read more → Education One of the most pressing reasons I am running for Nevada Assembly District 12 is to safeguard future generations.
Kids today don’t have the same opportunities I had as a child.
I support teachers and kids.
I want stronger, better schools, which means we must hold school boards and districts accountable.
We need programs like Read by 3 and reinstatement of the Opportunity Scholarships.
I support school choice, particularly in underperforming areas, and programs to attract more teachers to our schools.
Incentives can be created to bring the very best to the Silver State, while giving more resources to our existing teachers.
We can fix the education system in Nevada with common sense policies and oversight to ensure accountability.
