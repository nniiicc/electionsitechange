Governor Joe Lombardo
Governor Joe Lombardo endorses Nancy "Nan" Roecker for Assembly District 12
"I’m proud to endorse Nan Roecker for Nevada Assembly District 12.
With more than two decades of private-sector experience, Nan understands what businesses need to grow and succeed.
She will bring a practical, business-minded perspective to Carson City and help us cut red tape, create jobs, and expand opportunity for Nevada families."
