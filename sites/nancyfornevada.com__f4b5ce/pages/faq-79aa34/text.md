About Issues Endorsements FAQ Contact Donate ESP Log In Frequently Asked Questions Answers to the questions voters ask about Nancy "Nan" Roecker and her candidacy for Nevada Assembly District 12.
Who is Nancy Roecker? + Nancy "Nan" Roecker is the Republican candidate for Nevada State Assembly District 12 in the 2026 election.
She is a technology consultant with over 20 years of experience helping businesses select and implement financial software, a mother of three, and a grandmother of two who lives in Las Vegas.
What district is Nancy Roecker running in? + Nancy is running for Nevada State Assembly District 12, which covers Calico Ridge, Henderson, Lake Las Vegas, and Sunrise Manor in Clark County.
Why is Nancy running for office? + Nancy is running because she wants to make sure our children have the same opportunities she did — to grow up in a safe neighborhood, to get a good education, and to enjoy economic prosperity where the American Dream is within reach to everyone willing to work for it.
Her top priorities are combatting the affordability crisis, restoring public safety, defending educational opportunity, and bringing common-sense fiscal accountability to Nevada.
What party does Nancy Roecker belong to? + Nancy Roecker is a Republican candidate.
When is the election for Nevada Assembly District 12? + The Nevada Assembly District 12 race is on the November 2026 general election ballot, with the Republican primary in June 2026.
Check ClarkCountyNV.gov/vote or NVSOS.gov for exact dates, registration deadlines, and your polling place.
What are Nancy Roecker’s positions on the issues? + Nancy’s focus areas are: (1) Economy and affordability — lowering taxes, opposing a state income tax, opposing new gas taxes, cutting red tape for small businesses, and supporting affordable housing; (2) Education — accountability for school boards, school choice in underperforming areas, Read by 3, and reinstatement of Opportunity Scholarships; (3) Public Safety — backing police and first responders, repealing pro-criminal policies, prioritizing victims’ rights, and stopping the flow of illegal drugs into Nevada; (4) Energy Independence — reliable in-state power for Nevada families and businesses, ending dependence on California for energy supply.
What is Nancy Roecker’s professional background? + Nancy is a technology consultant with over 20 years of experience helping thousands of businesses select and implement financial software solutions.
She brings that operational and financial discipline to state budgeting and appropriations work.
