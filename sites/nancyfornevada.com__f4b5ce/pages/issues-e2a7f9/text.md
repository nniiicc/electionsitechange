Economy & Affordability
Life is no longer affordable in Nevada.
Everything from gas to groceries costs more.
As your Assemblywoman for Nevada Assembly District 12, I will focus on lowering taxes, strengthening and diversifying economic development, and cutting the red tape small business owners face.
I will work to lower the cost of everyday items like food and gas by repealing the laws passed in recent sessions that made everything so much more expensive.
I will support affordable housing initiatives in Clark County and will fight against additional taxes on gas, as well as efforts to impose a state income tax.
If we apply common sense practices and financial insight to state law and budgeting decisions, we can avoid becoming California 2.0.
