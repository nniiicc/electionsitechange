Racial Justice
While we worked to fight systemic racism in the Biden administration, President Trump is using the power of the federal government to target people of color at every turn.
- We cannot limit our response to only the most visible abuses, such as unlawful ICE operations or the deployment of the National Guard.
- We must confront every action that systematically erodes justice and equality in American life, from attacks on fair housing and labor protections to efforts that undermine public education, environmental justice, and civil rights enforcement.
