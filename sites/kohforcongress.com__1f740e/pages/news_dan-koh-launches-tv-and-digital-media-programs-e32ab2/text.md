PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH LAUNCHES TV AND DIGITAL MEDIA PROGRAMS
Andover, MA – Today, Dan Koh's campaign launched its first ad, "Bullies," highlighting Dan as the fighter we need in Washington to take on Trump and lower costs for families.
This robust media campaign is only possible thanks to Dan's strong grassroots fundraising, a record for a Massachusetts House candidate.
Powered without a dime of corporate PAC money from supporters in all 39 cities and towns in MA-6, Koh has built an enduring grassroots coalition of labor leaders, local elected officials, and community leaders, along with endorsements from the Massachusetts AFL-CIO, President Biden, Vice President Harris, and Secretary Buttigieg.
"I've spent my career taking on tough fights," says Koh.
"Trump's targeting anyone who gets in his way.
But bullies only win when we don't fight back.
Trump's not backing down, and neither am I.
With 49 days left until the primary, voters across the district are going to see and hear our positive vision of what we’re fighting for," said Koh.
"This campaign was built by thousands of people in every single city and town in this district who are ready for a fighter with the experience to take on Donald Trump and win.
That grassroots strength is what's powering this program, and it's what's going to win this race."
The new advertising builds on months of on-the-ground organizing, more than 100 endorsements from labor organizations, elected officials, and community leaders, and a fundraising operation that has out-paced every other candidate in the race.
The ad will run across broadcast, cable, and streaming platforms serving the Boston media market, along with a coordinated digital program targeting voters throughout MA-6.
With 49 days remaining until the September 1 Democratic primary, the Koh campaign says its investment in television and digital advertising reflects the strength of the grassroots operation built over the past several months — and its confidence heading into the final stretch of the race.
