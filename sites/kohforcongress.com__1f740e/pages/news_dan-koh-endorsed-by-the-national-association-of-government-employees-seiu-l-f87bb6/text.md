PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH ENDORSED BY THE NATIONAL ASSOCIATION OF GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES SEIU LOCAL 5000
Andover, MA – Dan Koh is proud to announce he has received the endorsement of the National Association of Government Employees SEIU Local 5000 (NAGE) in his bid for Congress in Massachusetts’ 6th Congressional District.
“At a time when our country has been turned upside down by the damaging politics of President Trump and his administration, Dan Koh is exactly the type of leader that we need in Congress,” stated NAGE National President David J.
Halway.
“Dan will be a leader with the courage to speak truth to power and serve as a counterweight to the entrenched power in Washington DC.”
“I’m deeply grateful for this endorsement from the National Association of Government Employees, particularly as we face the longest government shutdown in history – with no end in sight due to Republicans’ self-serving, chaotic brand of governance,” said Koh.
“Right now families and small businesses across the sixth district are hurting,” continued Koh.
“From housing costs to childcare to grocery bills, too many of us are being priced out of our own communities.
This shutdown is only making things harder for some of Massachusetts’ most dedicated public servants and the communities they call home.”
“I’m honored to stand alongside NAGE’s over 50,000 members - including over 25,000 Massachusetts residents – to combat the MAGA agenda, defend our shared values, and rebuild thriving, affordable communities across our Commonwealth.” Koh concluded, “Together we will not just fight, we will win.”
