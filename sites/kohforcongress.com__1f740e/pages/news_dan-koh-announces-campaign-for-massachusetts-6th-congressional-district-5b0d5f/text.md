PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH ANNOUNCES CAMPAIGN FOR MASSACHUSETTS’ 6TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT
Andover, MA – Today Dan Koh announced his candidacy for Massachusetts’ 6th Congressional District, vowing to put his experience to work to fight back against Donald Trump and MAGA extremism.
“Our country is in crisis.
We need someone who is ready on Day 1 to fight for working families, protect our free speech rights and immigrant neighbors, and make health care and child care more affordable.
That’s why I’m running for Massachusetts’ 6th Congressional District,” said Koh.
Dan Koh’s experience ranges from Senior Aide in the White House, Chief of Staff at the Department of Labor, Chief of Staff to the City of Boston, and Andover Town Select Board Member.
Dan has played key roles in lowering prescription drug costs, strengthening our infrastructure, and providing key housing and educational resources for Massachusetts communities, including millions to the 6th District.
Koh currently attracts millions of views in his fight against MAGA on Fox News, CNN, MSNBC, and through his media platform, “The People’s Cabinet.”
Born to a Lebanese-Korean immigrant family, Dan takes the fight against MAGA personally.
His story is only possible in a proudly diverse America that cares about our most vulnerable and values all contributions.
And as a Dad of two young kids, Dan sees firsthand how Trump’s tariffs have ballooned the costs of raising a family.
“I refuse to sit on the sidelines and watch Trump and his rich friends dismantle our country.
I’m asking for the families of Massachusetts’ 6th District to send me to Congress, so I can take the fight directly to Trump and deliver the results our community deserves.”
Dan and his wife Amy Sennett are the proud parents of Theodora (5) and Jack (2).
He is an avid runner, having completed 83 marathons, including 15 Boston Marathons.
