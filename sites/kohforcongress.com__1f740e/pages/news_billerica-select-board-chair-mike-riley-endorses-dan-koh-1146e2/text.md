PRESS RELEASE
BILLERICA SELECT BOARD CHAIR MIKE RILEY ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Billerica Select Board Chair Mike Riley announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District primary
“I am incredibly proud to endorse Dan Koh for Congress,” Chair Riley said.
“I have been a follower of Dan’s career and leadership since his time running the office of Boston Mayor Marty Walsh.
His success there followed him and Mayor Walsh to Washington.
During his time in the Biden administration, Dan helped write legislation, such as the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, that provided tangible benefits for Billerica and communities throughout the country.
Dan’s fighting spirit gives me hope for our country, and I know he will always look out for the people of the Sixth District.”
Koh expressed his gratitude for the endorsement, underscoring his admiration of Chair Riley’s track record and character as a local leader.
“I am honored to have the endorsement of Chair Riley,” Koh said.
“Mike has always looked out for his community and serves Billerica with dignity and fervor.
From his work balancing the municipal budget to serving on the town’s planning board, Mike is the kind of diligent leader that makes a community great.
I am incredibly grateful to have his support and I look forward to working alongside him to build a more affordable Sixth District.”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 75 endorsements from local and national elected officials and community leaders, union locals, President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising a record-breaking $3.5M+ since launch, a record for an MA House candidate.
