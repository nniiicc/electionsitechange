PRESS RELEASE
CONGRESSWOMAN PRAMILA JAYAPAL (WA-07) ENDORSES DAN KOH
Peabody, MA – Today, U.S.
Congresswoman Pramila Jayapal (WA-07) announced her endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District general election.
“I’ve spent my career fighting for working families, immigrant communities, and an economy that works for everyone.
I’m proud to endorse Dan Koh for Congress because I know he shares that same fight.
At a time when families are being squeezed by rising costs under this administration, we need leaders in Congress willing to take on powerful interests and stand up for working people.
Dan has the experience, conviction, and commitment to do exactly that.”
Koh, whose campaign is focused on fighting the Trump administration's corruption and lowering costs for working families, thanked Congresswoman Jayapal for her endorsement.
“I’m honored to have the endorsement of Congresswoman Jayapal,” Koh said.
“Her relentless advocacy for working families and deep dedication to her constituents are exactly the kind of leadership I aspire to bring to Congress.
I’m proud to have her support, and I look forward to working alongside her to lower costs, fight back against this administration's corruption, and deliver for Massachusetts families.”
Koh is the Democratic nominee for Massachusetts’ Sixth Congressional District.
He was endorsed by President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, Secretary Pete Buttigieg, and more than 100 local and national elected officials, unions, and community leaders.
