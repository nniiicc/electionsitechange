PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH LAUNCHES NEW AD FEATURING DEMOCRATIC LEADERS
Peabody, MA – Today, Dan Koh's campaign launched a new ad, “Democrats You Trust,” featuring President Biden, Vice President Harris, the Massachusetts AFL-CIO, and local Democratic leaders who have endorsed his campaign for Congress.
Dan is running to clean up Trump’s corruption in Washington and lower costs for families.
"This campaign is about building the kind of coalition that can actually deliver for working families.
President Biden, Vice President Harris, the AFL-CIO, and Democratic leaders across this district aren't just endorsing a candidate, they're backing a fight to take on Trump's corruption and bring down costs for the people who sent us here to fight for them.
I'm proud to have this team behind me.
Together, we're going to win this race and get to work," said Koh.
The new advertising follows a newly released poll that shows Dan with a 19 point lead in the field, building on months of on-the-ground organizing, more than 100 endorsements from labor organizations, elected officials, and community leaders, and a record fundraising operation that has out-paced every other candidate in the race.
This ad follows Dan’s initial ads, “Bullies,” “Mess,” and “Future,” which has been airing on broadcast, cable, and digital platforms, highlighting Dan as the fighter in this race with the experience to take on Donald Trump and win.
The ads will run across broadcast, cable, and streaming platforms serving the Boston media market, along with a coordinated digital program targeting voters throughout MA-6.
With 19 days remaining until the September 1 Democratic primary, the campaign says the size and pace of this advertising program — its second wave in a matter of weeks — reflects both the resources and the organizing strength it has built to close out the race.
