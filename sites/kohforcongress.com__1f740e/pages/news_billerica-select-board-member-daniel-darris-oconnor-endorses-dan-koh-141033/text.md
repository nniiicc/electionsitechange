PRESS RELEASE
BILLERICA SELECT BOARD MEMBER DANIEL DARRIS O’CONNOR ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Billerica Select Board Member Daniel Darris O’Connor announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District primary.
“I am excited to endorse Dan Koh for Congress,” Select Board Member O’Connor said.
“Dan is exactly the kind of Democrat we need right now — he will stand up against the Trump administration’s corruption while protecting our most vulnerable communities.
He will work hard to secure Medicare for All and will never leave our LGBTQ neighbors behind.”
Koh shared his excitement to have Select Board Member O’Connor’s endorsement, underscoring their shared vision for a Democratic party that does not shy away from a bold affordability agenda.
“I am so honored to have the endorsement of Select Board Member Daniel Darris O’Connor," Koh said.
“Daniel knows that the fight for our democracy cannot wait.
We also share a vision of a Democratic party that prioritizes protecting our most vulnerable constituents while presenting a bold vision for making the country more affordable.
I am excited to have his support as we work to fight the Trump administration’s corruption.”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 75 endorsements from local and national elected officials and community leaders, union locals, President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising a record-breaking $3.5M+ since launch, a record for an MA House candidate.
