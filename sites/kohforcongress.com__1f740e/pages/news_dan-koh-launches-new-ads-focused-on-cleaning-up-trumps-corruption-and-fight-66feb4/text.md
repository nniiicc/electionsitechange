PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH LAUNCHES NEW ADS FOCUSED ON CLEANING UP TRUMP’S CORRUPTION AND FIGHTING FOR OUR FUTURE
Peabody, MA – Today, Dan Koh's campaign launched two new ads, “Mess" and “Future.” As the dad to two young kids, Dan is committed to cleaning up Trump’s corruption in Washington and fighting for future generations.
"As a dad to two young kids, I think about their future every single day.
Right now, Trump and his allies are more focused on lining their own pockets than on lowering costs for families across the country.
I've dedicated my career to getting things done for families, not corporations, and as a dad, I'm not going to sit back while my kids' future gets sold off to the highest bidder.
That's why I'm in this fight,” said Koh.
The new advertising builds on months of on-the-ground organizing, more than 100 endorsements from labor organizations, elected officials, and community leaders, and a record fundraising operation that has out-paced every other candidate in the race.
These ads follow Dan’s first ad, “Bullies,” which has been airing on broadcast, cable, and digital platforms, highlighting Dan as the fighter in this race with the experience to take on Donald Trump and win.
Dan’s campaign has built a strong grassroots coalition of labor leaders, local elected officials, and community changemakers, along with the Massachusetts AFL-CIO, President Biden, Vice President Harris, and Secretary Buttigieg.
The ads will run across broadcast, cable, and streaming platforms serving the Boston media market, along with a coordinated digital program targeting voters throughout MA-6.
With 33 days remaining until the September 1 Democratic primary, the campaign says the size and pace of this advertising program — its second wave in a matter of weeks — reflects both the resources and the organizing strength it has built to close out the race.
