PRESS RELEASE
ANDOVER SCHOOL COMMITTEE MEMBER DAVE CROW ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Andover School Committee Member Dave Crow announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District primary.
“As an Andover School Committee Member, I've seen firsthand how federal decisions impact our classrooms and our families.
Essex County needs a representative who truly understands our community and will fight for us from day one — and Dan Koh is that person,” Crow said.
“Dan is the candidate with the experience and passion necessary to hit the ground running in D.C. and advocate for our schools, our communities, and everything that makes Andover and this district so great.
That is why I am proud to endorse him for Congress.”
Koh expressed his gratitude for Crow's endorsement, emphasizing the importance of collaboration with local leaders across the Sixth District.
“I’m proud to have the support of School Committee Member Dave Crow,” said Koh.
“As a dad of two young kids, the fight for accessible, quality education and childcare is personal for me.
Dave understands firsthand how federal decisions impact our classrooms and our families, and I’m excited to work with local leaders like him to ensure every student in the Sixth District has the support they need to succeed.”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 100 endorsements from local and national elected officials and community leaders, union locals, President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising a record-breaking $4.5M+ since launch, a record for an MA House candidate.
