Reproductive Rights
The Supreme Court’s overturning of Roe v.
Wade was an outrage.
My daughter is growing up in a country where she has less say over her body than my mother did.
Every person should have the ability to make decisions about their own body, no matter what state they live in or what ZIP code they call home.
- When we win back the House, I will support legislation, such as the Women’s Health Protection Act, that codifies the abortion protections once enforced by Roe.
Reproductive freedom is a fundamental right, and I will fight tirelessly to defend it.
