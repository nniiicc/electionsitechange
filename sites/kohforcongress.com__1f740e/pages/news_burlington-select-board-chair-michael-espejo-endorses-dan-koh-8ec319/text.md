PRESS RELEASE
BURLINGTON SELECT BOARD CHAIR MICHAEL ESPEJO ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Burlington Select Board Chair Michael Espejo announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District primary.
"Dan brings a results-driven approach to public service that reflects what I value most in local government,” said Espejo.
“On the Burlington Select Board, we focus on getting things done for people.
Dan understands that mindset, and has the track record to take on big fights and win.
Burlington is better when we have leaders who put community first, and that's exactly why I'm proud to endorse Dan Koh for Congress."
Koh expressed his gratitude for Chair Espejo’s endorsement, emphasizing the importance of results-driven local leadership and a shared commitment to putting our local communities first.
“I’m incredibly grateful for Chair Espejo’s endorsement,” Koh said.
“Local leaders like him know that public service is about getting things done for the people you represent.
He has shown time and again what it means to put people first in Burlington, and I’m honored to have his support.
Let’s Go!”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 100 endorsements from local and national elected officials and community leaders, union locals, President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising a record-breaking $3.5M+ since launch, a record for an MA House candidate.
