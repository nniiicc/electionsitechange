PRESS RELEASE
BEDFORD SELECT BOARD MEMBER TERRENCE PARKER ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Bedford Select Board Member Terrence Parker announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District primary.
"Dan has experience on the local level as a Select Board Member for one of our local towns; a Chief of Staff for Massachusetts' largest City; as well as a policy adviser in the White House.
In these troubled times, we need both a proven leader and a fighter who can hit the ground running in Washington: Dan is just that.
I wholeheartedly endorse him for Congress in the 6th District,” said Select Board Member Parker.
As a former Select Board Member in Andover, Koh expressed his gratitude for the endorsement, emphasizing the importance of local leadership in this campaign.
"I'm so grateful for this endorsement from Select Board Member Parker," Koh said.
"Our district works best when local leaders unite and work together to build a brighter future for all.
I’m excited to have the Select Board Member join our fight for families in Bedford and across the Sixth.”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 50 endorsements from local and national community leaders, union locals, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising a record-breaking $2M+ in its first fundraising quarter, a record for an MA House candidate in history.
