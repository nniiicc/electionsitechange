PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH ANNOUNCES RECORD-BREAKING $2M+ RAISED IN LESS THAN ONE QUARTER
His supporters gave a median contribution of $50 and included individuals from 100% of the cities and towns in the district.
It is a record for any Massachusetts House candidate in history.
“I’m deeply humbled by the thousands of people who are dedicated to this campaign to lower costs and fight for our democracy,” said Dan Koh.
“What matters most isn’t numbers — it’s the conversations, the doors knocked, and the trust built by listening to working families, labor leaders, grassroots supporters, and community members across the district.
That’s what wins, and that’s how we’ll deliver on day one.”
A dad of two young kids and the son of a Korean-Lebanese immigrant family, Koh takes the fight against our affordability crisis and MAGA extremism personally.
His story is only possible in a proudly diverse America.
Koh will bring his experience in the White House, Boston City Hall, and on the Andover Select Board to Congress to deliver for families on day one.
“I’m asking the voters of Massachusetts’ 6th District to send me to Congress to take the fight directly to Trump and MAGA Republicans who are driving up costs for working families,” Koh said.
“The response to this campaign shows that people want leaders who are willing to do the work, listen first, and fight hard to get results.”
The record-breaking fundraising is powered by individuals, with the campaign receiving over 3,000 contributions since mid-October.
It is without a dime of corporate PAC money – a pledge Dan has made to stop corporate influence in elections.
Koh’s campaign continues to build momentum with support from dozens of local elected and community leaders, more than ten labor unions, Vice President Harris, and a growing grassroots movement across the district.
