Housing will become more affordable if we prioritize building more homes that people can actually afford.
I will support legislation that makes it easier for people to secure permits needed to build additional housing , while also being mindful of environmental protection.
We must build upon the work done in the Biden Administration to make housing more affordable.
I will support expanding the low-income housing credit to incentivize greater investment in additional affordable housing units in the district.
We need to ensure that our seniors are able to age-in-place , which is why I will advocate for zoning changes that permit for smaller and more affordable housing options, granting seniors the opportunity to downsize without leaving their community and family behind.
I will draw upon my experience at all levels of government to collaborate with state and local officials to create an ecosystem that allows for a thoughtful growth in housing stock across the Sixth.
