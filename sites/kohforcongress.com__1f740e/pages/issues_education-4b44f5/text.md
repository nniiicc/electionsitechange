Education
A strong public education system is essential to a strong democracy.
- Our schools must be properly funded, and I oppose the Trump’s cuts to education that leave students and teachers behind – especially those students that need additional support.
- I support raising teacher pay and ensuring educators receive the benefits they deserve.
Our classrooms work best when they are inclusive, and I will fight back against conservative attacks on DEI programs and efforts to tie federal funding to discriminatory policies.
- Keeping students safe also means passing comprehensive gun violence prevention laws so schools remain places of learning, not fear.
