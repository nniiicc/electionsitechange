PRESS RELEASE
BEDFORD SELECT BOARD CHAIR DAN BROSGOL ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Bedford Select Board Chair Dan Brosgol announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District primary.
“I’m proud to endorse Dan Koh for Congress,” Brosgol said.
“Serving on both the Bedford School Committee and now as Chair of the Select Board has shown me how important thoughtful, collaborative leadership is at every level of government.
I am confident that Dan has the experience and judgment to deliver real results for our communities, and I know he will be an effective advocate for the Sixth District.”
As a former Andover Select Board Member, Koh expressed his gratitude for the endorsement and emphasized the important role of local leaders in this campaign.
“I’m so grateful for this endorsement from Chair Brosgol,” Koh said.
“From the School Committee to the Select Board, he has dedicated years of service to Bedford, leading with thoughtfulness, collaboration, and a commitment to strengthening his community.
I’m honored to have his support as we continue building a campaign that delivers real results for Bedford and communities across the Sixth District.”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 100 endorsements from local and national elected officials and community leaders, union locals, President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising a record-breaking $3.5M+ since launch, a record for an MA House candidate.
