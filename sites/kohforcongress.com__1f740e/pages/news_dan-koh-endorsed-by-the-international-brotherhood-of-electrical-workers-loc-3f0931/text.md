PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH ENDORSED BY THE INTERNATIONAL BROTHERHOOD OF ELECTRICAL WORKERS LOCAL 103
Andover, MA – Today, Dan Koh announced he has received the endorsement of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 103 in his bid for Congress in Massachusetts’ 6th Congressional District. 103 represents more than 10,000 people and is well-known for its dedication to empowering workers and delivering for its members.
“IBEW Local 103 is proud to stand with Dan Koh because he has always put working families first,” said Lou Antonellis, Business Manager and Financial Secretary-Treasurer of IBEW Local 103.
“Dan shows up and listens, and truly understands the affordability crisis hitting our communities.
He fights for opportunity and safety for every family across Massachusetts.
With our values under attack, we need a leader in Congress who delivers results.
Dan Koh is that champion.”
"I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of IBEW Local 103," stated Koh.
"Hardworking individuals and families across the sixth district are facing numerous challenges, including escalating housing costs, rising expenses for childcare and healthcare, and even hate-motivated violence targeting our immigrant neighbors.
Massachusetts needs leaders committed to making our communities more affordable, upholding the right to free speech and organizing, and ensuring the opportunity for our children and neighbors to build better lives.
These challenging times demand leaders who are prepared not only to fight but also to win.
I have the experience to confront Trump, defend our shared values, and, crucially, secure victories while fighting to make life better for all.
The support of the IBEW is critical for our people-first, affordability-focused campaign.
I am grateful for Lou’s steadfast leadership during a critical time and the entire union for all they do to empower workers all across the district.”
