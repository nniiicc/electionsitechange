PRESS RELEASE
DAN KOH ENDORSED BY LABORERS LOCAL 22
Andover, MA – Dan Koh is proud to announce he has received the endorsement of Laborers Local 22 in his bid for Congress in Massachusetts’ 6th Congressional District, representing over 2,000 workers.
“Dan Koh is a fighter for working people, who is proud to stand with unions,” said Danny Ottaviano, Business Manager of Local 22.
“He is the only candidate with the experience at all levels to fight and be effective on Day 1, and is the person we need representing us in DC.”
“I am incredibly grateful for the endorsement of Laborers Local 22,” Koh stated.
“The hardworking Laborers and their families are the bedrock of our Massachusetts communities.
Unfortunately, they are increasingly being forced out of these very communities due to soaring housing prices, rising childcare and healthcare expenses, and even hate-based violence targeting our immigrant neighbors."
“Massachusetts needs leaders who will fight to make our communities more affordable, the right to free speech and to organize, and the opportunity to build a better life for our kids and our neighbors.”
“These times demand leaders who are not only willing to fight but also know how to win.
I possess the experience to confront Trump, defend our values, and, most importantly, win.
With the support of the Laborers and working families across Massachusetts, I will achieve this as the next Member of Congress for the 6th District.”
