PRESS RELEASE
ANDOVER LEADERS ENDORSE DAN KOH FOR CONGRESS
Andover, MA – Today, the Koh for Congress Campaign announced early endorsements from ten community leaders in Andover.
Together, these local leaders released the following statement: “We worked closely with Dan while he served as Vice Chair of the Andover Select Board.
We believe deeply in his ability to deliver for working people because we’ve seen him do it.
His work at the town, city, and national levels will make him effective on Day One to deliver for the 6th district.”
“I’m honored by the support of so many leaders in my town who I have worked alongside as an elected official, and believe in my ability to deliver on Day One,” said Koh.
“These Andover leaders know my commitment to our communities – we’ve been on the front lines of these fights together.
And just like in Andover, I will never stop working to deliver results for the people of the 6th District.”
The Andover leaders endorsing Koh for Congress are:
- Select Board Vice Chair Ellen Townson
- School Committee Vice Chair Lauren Conoscenti
- Former Select Board Chair & Former School Committee Chair Annie Gilbert
- Former Select Board Chair Paul Salafia
- Former School Committee Chair Joel Blumstein
- Former School Committee Chair Susan McCready
- Former School Committee Chair Shannon Scully
- Former School Committee Chair Tracey Spruce
- Former School Committee Vice Chair & Former Finance Committee Member Paul Murphy
- Former School Superintendent Claudia Bach
