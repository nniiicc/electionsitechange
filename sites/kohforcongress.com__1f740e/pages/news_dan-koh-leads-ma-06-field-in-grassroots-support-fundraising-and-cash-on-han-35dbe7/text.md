DAN KOH LEADS MA-06 FIELD IN GRASSROOTS SUPPORT, FUNDRAISING, AND CASH ON HAND
Andover, MA – Heading into February, Dan Koh’s campaign for Congress has continued its dominant momentum, with the most endorsements and resources of any campaign.
Koh reported a dominant fourth-quarter fundraising performance, with contributions from every city and town, a median donation of $50, and outraising every other candidate in the race combined.
He led the field in reported in-district donations.
According to the latest campaign finance reports, Koh raised more in Q4 than all other candidates combined, underscoring the scale and momentum of his campaign.
Koh also reported more cash on hand than the rest of the field combined, giving his campaign unmatched capacity to reach voters across the district.
Beyond the topline numbers, Koh’s campaign continues to demonstrate broad grassroots strength.
In Q4, Koh received more reported individual donations than all other candidates combined, reflecting deep and widespread support, with a median contribution of $50.
He also leads the field in endorsements, with 27 current or former elected officials and fourteen union locals on Team Koh.
“This campaign is powered by thousands of people who believe it’s time to fight back against Trump’s economy and his disastrous Administration to deliver real results for working families,” said Dan Koh.
“I’m grateful for the incredible support we’re seeing across the district, and I’m focused on continuing to build a campaign that’s strong enough to win and be effective on day one.”
Dan Koh is running for Congress to fight for working families, lower costs, and deliver results for communities across the district.
He previously served in the Biden Administration, Boston City Hall, and on the Andover Select Board.
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to the support of 27 local community leaders, 14 union locals, and Vice President Kamala Harris, while raising a record-breaking $2M+ in its first fundraising quarter, the most for an MA house seat in history.
