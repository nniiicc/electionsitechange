PRESS RELEASE
CONGRESSMAN PAT RYAN ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Congressman Pat Ryan announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District primary.
Ryan, a combat veteran and former Ulster County Executive, represents New York's 18th Congressional District.
“Throughout his career, Dan Koh has shown what public service is all about: putting people first and delivering results,” said Congressman Ryan.
“From serving his community at the local level to helping lead major initiatives in the Biden administration, Dan has consistently focused on making government work better for working families.
At a time when our democracy and fundamental freedoms are under attack by the Trump administration, we need leaders like Dan who are ready to hit the ground running and fight for the people they serve.
I’m proud to endorse Dan Koh for Congress because I know he’ll bring that same sense of purpose, urgency, and commitment to Washington.”
Koh expressed his gratitude for Congressman Ryan’s endorsement, highlighting the Congressman’s commitment to public service and his leadership in defending our democratic values.
"I’m honored to have the endorsement of Congressman Pat Ryan," Koh said.
"Congressman Ryan has dedicated his life to serving others, from his tours in Iraq to his work defending our fundamental freedoms in Congress.
He understands what it means to put service before self and to stand up for the people he represents.
I’m grateful for his support and proud to have him on our team as we work to lower costs for working people, protect our democracy, and build a brighter future for the next generation.”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 85 endorsements from local and national elected officials and community leaders, union locals, President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising a record-breaking $3.5M+ since launch, a record for an MA House candidate.
