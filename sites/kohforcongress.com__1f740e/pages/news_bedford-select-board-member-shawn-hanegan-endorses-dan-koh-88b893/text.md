PRESS RELEASE
BEDFORD SELECT BOARD MEMBER SHAWN HANEGAN ENDORSES DAN KOH
Andover, MA – Today, Bedford Select Board Member and Chair of the Bedford Democratic Town Committee, Shawn Hanegan, announced his endorsement of Dan Koh in the Massachusetts 6th Congressional District Primary.
“I am honored to endorse Dan Koh for Congress,” Hanegan said.
“Dan is a knowledgeable, experienced leader filled with many ideas on how we can make this district more affordable for all.
He is also deeply passionate about local issues, and will work closely with municipal officials throughout the district to benefit residents living in each of our cities and towns.”
Koh expressed his gratitude for Hanegan’s endorsement and applauded the work Chair Hanegan has done to make his community more affordable for generations to come.
“I am so grateful to have the endorsement of Select Board Chair Hanegan,” Koh said.
“He is just as eager as I am about implementing policies that lower costs for Sixth District families, whether it’s supporting programming that lowers costs for renewable energy or advocating for zoning laws that promote an increased supply of affordable housing.
I am thrilled to have Select Board Chair Hanegan’s support as we fight the corrupt Trump administration and build an economy that works for everyone in the Sixth District.”
Koh’s campaign has gained critical momentum thanks to over 75 endorsements from local and national elected officials and community leaders, union locals, President Joe Biden, Vice President Kamala Harris, and Secretary Pete Buttigieg, while raising $3.5M+ since launch, a record for an MA House candidate.
