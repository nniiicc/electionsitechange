1 I’m running because families in Kalispell and across Northwest Montana need a senator focused on affordability, public safety, healthcare, and protecting our freedom to live our lives without unnecessary government interference.
What makes your campaign different?
2 This campaign is rooted in local service, practical problem-solving, and independence.
I’m not running to score points for a party.
I’m running to represent the people of this district and deliver results.
What are your top priorities?
3 Kyle’s top priorities are affordable living costs, healthcare access, public safety, infrastructure planning, and protecting personal freedom.
How will you represent both Kalispell and the wider district?
4 By listening, staying grounded in the district’s needs, and working with people across neighborhoods, professions, and viewpoints to find solutions that make sense for Montana.
