Christopher H.
Jones is a lifelong Oklahoman dedicated to service, integrity, and community.
Born and raised in Del City, he graduated from Del City High School in 1992 and went on to serve his country for 13 years—four years in the United States Marine Corps and nine years with the Oklahoma Army National Guard.
His military service included deployments to Iraq in 2006 and 2007 and vital humanitarian work during Hurricane Katrina.
After earning his degree in Business from MCU, Christopher continued his commitment to public service as a correctional officer for six years, working in both state and federal facilities.
His leadership experience, discipline, and belief in second chances have shaped his understanding of justice, accountability, and the importance of strong communities.
A devoted husband to his wife, a registered nurse of nearly 30 years, Christopher is also a proud father and stepfather of four children.
Now retired from his professional career, he feels called once more to serve the people of Oklahoma.
Motivated by concern for the direction of government and a passion for restoring trust and common sense, Chris Jones is ready to lead with honesty, respect, and dedication to the values that define Oklahoma.
Chris a proud Chickasaw tribal member, avid reader, and enjoys spending time participating in the Chickasaw Honor Guard.
