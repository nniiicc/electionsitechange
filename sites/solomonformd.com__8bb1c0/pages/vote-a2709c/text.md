Vote in the Primary by June 23
- The deadline to register to vote or update your voter registration information is Tuesday, June 2, 2026 for the Primary Election.
Your voter registration must be up to date with your current address.
To register to vote or to check your registration status, click here.
- To vote by mail, you must request a mail-in ballot from the State Board of Elections or your local board.
Your request for a mail-in ballot for the Primary Election must be received by Tuesday, June 16, 2026 and Friday, June 19, 2026 if you want to receive your ballot online.
Mail-in ballots must be postmarked or placed in a designated ballot drop box by 8 pm on June 23, 2026 for the Primary Election.
Request your mail-in ballot and see more details on dropbox locations here.
- Early voting will be held for 8 days and begin on Thursday, June 11, 2026 through Thursday, June 8, 2026 from 7 am until 8 pm for the Primary Election.
- Primary Election Day is Tuesday June 23, 2026.
To vote in person on election days, vote at your assigned polling place.
