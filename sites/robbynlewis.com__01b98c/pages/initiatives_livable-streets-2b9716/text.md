The Coalition was convened in October 2019 by Del.
Robbyn Lewis to create a platform for resolving conflicts about community development, specifically with respect to cars, transit and street conditions.
Originally established as an advisory group of residents and neighborhood associations close to the Fayette/Orleans road corridors, the group has since evolved into a broad coalition, including schools and non-profits, anywhere that people are interested in creating healthier communities.
For more information, please visit LivableStreetsBaltimore.com The Livable Streets Coalition is managed and resourced solely by Del.
