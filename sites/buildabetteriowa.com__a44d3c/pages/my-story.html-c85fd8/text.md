MATT ROBINSON FOR IOWA Home MY STORY Issues Press VOLUNTEER DONATE I'm Ready to Build a Better Iowa I've dedicated my life to building...
Whether it's building a new school, a hospital, or replacing a roof on a neighbors house I have been building up to this moment.
Now I'm ready to take on a new task.
I'm running to represent you so we can build a state that works for working people, a government that takes care of those who bring this state to new heights.
We need a change and the time is now. ​Dubuque has been the only place I’ve called home.
I grew up in these neighborhoods I hope to now represent.
I’m a proud Mustang, graduating from Hempstead High School in 2011.
After graduation instead of pursuing a degree like many of my friends, I learned through blood and sweat as I started my career in the building trades.
I’m grateful for the opportunities I had, and just like many of you I was set to forge a path on my own.
It didn't take long to understand the challenges of a working-class Iowan; a job that relies on the weather and the clock could mean an unsteady paycheck, or filing for unemployment seasonally and not being afforded benefits.
I have weathered some tough times along the way.
I was raised in a lower-middle class family so we had to find a way to make things work.
I always had a feeling, “should it be this hard?” Times are changing and wages in Iowa are not.
There is a war on working class Iowans.
Unemployment benefits, healthcare, and anything intended to give folks a hand up has been on the chopping block all while giving tax breaks to the wealthiest Iowans.
I knew I wasn’t alone.
I had friends and family in the trades who were experiencing the same struggle, the same fears.
But when I looked at our state and the government that I expected to be supporting us I couldn’t find someone advocating for workers and the everyday Iowan.
I couldn’t see someone like me there.
In 2022 I decided to run for the Iowa Senate.
Though the results weren’t what I was hoping for, I was thankful for the opportunity to meet so many people just like me and I vowed to never give up on building a better Iowa.
After the 2022 election, I saw an opportunity to grow, and I joined the United Brotherhood of Carpenters Local 678.
Now as a 4th year apprentice, I couldn't be happier learning new skills on my way to becoming a journeyman carpenter.
Joining the Carpenters Union gave me better support, opportunities, and protections than what I had as a non-union worker but I want to see that all workers are provided the same safety and security regardless.
It was also an honor and privilege to serve the past two years as the Chairman of the Dubuque County Democrats.
I’m grateful my fellow Democrats trusted me to guide the county party through the past election cycle.
This opportunity allowed me to meet a wide array of folks.
The same message was said over and over; things aren’t getting better.
So here we are today - Iowa remains a state where the population has not grown in a decade, and wages have remained stagnant.
At the same time, costs are skyrocketing, and Iowans’ paychecks are squeezed for every penny.
But the Iowa legislature continues to ignore these problems.
It’s time that Des Moines began focusing on BUILDING A BETTER IOWA.
Union Carpenter I’m so grateful to be a part of this brotherhood, being able to stand alongside my brothers and sisters to fight for fairness & equality, better wages, and fair workplace standards.
I get to wake up every morning, put on my hard hat & tool-belt, advance infrastructure, and make a difference in my community.
I’m so lucky to have a union that has my back and fights for me but also elevates my voice and helps me fight for working class folks.
My Priorities Grassroots Connections I would not be where I am today without the outpouring of dedicated volunteers who have helped me grow over the past few years.
Its because of them, I’ve been able to reach out to so many people in our Dubuque community - allowing me to listen to folks from all different walks of life.
They’re part of the team that will build a better Iowa.
That team is always growing and we need your help.
Join us as we take on special interest groups and ensure Des Moines is working for the everyday Iowan.
