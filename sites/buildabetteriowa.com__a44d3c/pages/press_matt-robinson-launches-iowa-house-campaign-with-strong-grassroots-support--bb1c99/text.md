| Matt Robinson Launches Iowa House Campaign with Strong Grassroots Support, Raising Over $13,000 6/12/2025 Dubuque, IA – Matt Robinson, Democratic candidate for Iowa House, kicked off his campaign this past weekend with a powerful show of local support, raising over $13,000 in his official campaign launch.
The event drew over 100 community members, highlighting strong grassroots enthusiasm for his vision of building a better Iowa.Of the funds raised, 98% came from local, small-dollar donors, underscoring a broad base of community support and a campaign powered by everyday Iowans.
"I'm incredibly humbled and grateful for the outpouring of support from our neighbors and friends,” said Robinson.
“To see so many people invest in this campaign — not just financially, but with their time and energy — means the world.” The event included speeches from Assistant Democratic Leader, State Representative Heather Matson, State Representative Lindsay James, and Robinson himself, who emphasized his commitment to everyday Iowans.
With the campaign off to a strong start, Robinson says he's focused on building momentum and bringing real change to the Statehouse.
“This campaign is about building a better Iowa — together,” Robinson said.
“We’re done waiting on solutions from out-of-touch politicians.
I’m ready to fight for working class Iowans.
It’s time we do more than just merely get by, we need paychecks that keep up with costs and the security to know if we work hard and do the right thing we will be able to thrive.” | For press inquiries Please email: MattRobinsonForIowa @gmail.com |
