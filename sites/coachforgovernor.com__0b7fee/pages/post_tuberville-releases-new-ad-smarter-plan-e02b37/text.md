Tuberville Releases New Ad: 'Smarter Plan'
AUBURN – Coach Tommy Tuberville today unveiled a new ad called "Smarter Plan." In the ad, he talks about Doug Jones' plan to eliminate the CHOOSE Act and reduce school choice options for Alabama families.
Watch the ad here or read excerpts below.
"Liberals – they love to tell you how brilliant they are," Tuberville says in the ad.
"But their education plan kicks tens of thousands of children out of the school of their choice.
We've got a smarter plan.
Let's start by protecting school choice and empowering parents, reward the good teachers while holding the poor ones accountable, and focus on workforce development.
Education isn't just about making our kids college-ready or career-ready, they should be life-ready.
I'm Tommy Tuberville – let's educate, not indoctrinate."
