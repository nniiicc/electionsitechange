Montgomery Circuit Court Dismisses Residency Case Against Senator Tommy Tuberville
MONTGOMERY – Today, an Alabama judge dismissed the Democrats' residency lawsuit against Coach Tuberville, ruling that the plaintiffs’ sole legal claim — quo warranto — cannot be used at this stage to remove a certified major-party nominee from the ballot.
The court noted that Alabama law gives political parties authority to resolve primary-election contests, that the Alabama Republican Party already heard this same eligibility challenge and ruled that Coach met the Constitution’s “resident citizen” requirement, and certified him as the Republican nominee for Governor.
The court held it lacked jurisdiction to review that decision or the plaintiffs’ quo warranto claim and granted Coach Tuberville’s Motion to Dismiss — another loss for the Democrats’ residency hoax.
