Coach Tuberville Drops New Ad: 'Bumper Sticker'
AUBURN – Coach Tommy Tuberville today unveiled a new ad called "bumper sticker." In the ad, he contrasts the differences between Democrats' campaign slogan versus his.
Watch the ad here or read excerpts below.
"If Democrats slapped a slogan on their bumpers, it would be this one: 'more taxes, less results,'" Coach Tuberville opens in the ad.
"My bumper looks a little different.
As your Senator, I helped pass the largest working-class tax cuts in history and delivered Alabama its biggest tax refund ever.
As your governor, I'll cut taxes even further, starting with the elimination of the state's grocery tax.
And I'll audit every state dollar, ensuring your tax dollars deliver real results.
'Less taxes, more results' – that's my slogan."
