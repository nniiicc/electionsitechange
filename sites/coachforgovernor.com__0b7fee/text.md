ABOUT COACH TUBERVILLE
"A great champion and man of courage"
– President Donald Trump
Tommy Tuberville is a former college football coach and U.S.
Senator from Alabama.
A standout athlete at Southern Arkansas University, Tuberville rose through the coaching ranks before leading Ole Miss, Auburn, Texas Tech, and Cincinnati.
He is best known for his time at Auburn, where he led the Tigers to an undefeated 13-0 season in 2004 and became one of the winningest coaches in college football history.
In 2020, Tuberville was elected to the U.S.
Senate as a proud member of the America First movement.
He serves on the Armed Services, Agriculture, Veterans' Affairs, Education, and Aging committees, where he champions conservative values, a strong military, and support for America's veterans.
Inspired by his father, a decorated World War II veteran and Purple Heart recipient, Coach Tuberville has dedicated much of his life to serving others.
He and his wife, Suzanne, have two sons, Tucker and Troy.
