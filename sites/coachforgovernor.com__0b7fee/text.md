A standout athlete at Southern Arkansas University, Tuberville rose through the coaching ranks before leading Ole Miss, Auburn, Texas Tech, and Cincinnati.

Inspired by his father, a decorated World War II veteran and Purple Heart recipient, Coach Tuberville has dedicated much of his life to serving others.
He and his wife, Suzanne, have two sons, Tucker and Troy.
