Northwest Oregon's Fiercest Advocate
Courtney Bangs is an educator, Clatsop County Commissioner, and mom of three with deep roots in Northwest Oregon, where she and her husband are raising their family on their sixth generation farm in Knappa.
Courtney understands firsthand the reality that rural families face every day: unaffordable housing and childcare, jobs and working forests under pressure, and aging roads that our tax dollars never seem to fix.
While Portland politicians continue to find ways to threaten our values with their one-size-fits-all agendas, Courtney Bangs will stand up for Northwest Oregon and fight to protect our rural way of life.
Courtney will:
- Protect working forests and natural resource jobs
- Cut red tape so housing can actually get built
- Fix rural roads, bridges, and dikes for flood protection
- Demand accountability for every tax dollar sent to Salem
- Support local law enforcement and keep communities safe
- Defend local control of our schools, lands, and livelihoods
