Legislative Record Bills Signed Into Law Housing HB 4375 2023 Established the Grand Rapids city landbank.
Gave cities the tools to establish land banks, acquiring and repurposing vacant, abandoned properties for housing.
HB 4675 & 4679 2024 Strengthened the long-term financial sustainability of community land banks.
Land banks can keep operating and serving communities for years to come.
HB 5557 2024 Required cities' master plans to include a written, intentional housing investment strategy.
Cities are now legally accountable for planning housing growth.
HB 5806 (PA 30 of 2026) 2026 Creates a Low-Income Housing Tax Credit for the State of Michigan.
Signed into law, expanding access to affordable housing across Michigan.
Civil Rights HB 4475 2023 Made hate crimes explicitly illegal and punishable under Michigan law.
Communities targeted by hate have stronger legal protection and a clearer path to justice.
HB 4717 2023 Required all real estate professionals to complete fair housing training.
Buyers and renters across Michigan are treated more equitably at the point of sale.
Public Safety HB 4143 2023 Expanded universal background checks for all gun purchases in Michigan, closing loopholes that allowed sales without vetting.
Every gun sale in Michigan now requires a background check.
Economic Development HB 5096 2024 Reformed renaissance zones to better support community redevelopment, revitalization, and commercial investment in underserved areas.
State economic tools are now better targeted at the communities that need them most.
Health HB 5171 2024 Improved maternal health and postpartum care standards, expanding access to critical services for new mothers and their families.
Better health outcomes for Michigan mothers and newborns.
