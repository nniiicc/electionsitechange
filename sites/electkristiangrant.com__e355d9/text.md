Grant A proven leader delivering real results for Grand Rapids & Wyoming State Representative Kristian Grant serves Michigan's 82nd District, Grand Rapids and Wyoming, where she is currently in her second term.
Her leadership is grounded in both policy and practice.
In the legislature, she has passed more than 10 bills into law focused on housing, economic development, and public safety.
Outside of Lansing, she is actively engaged in real estate development and neighborhood revitalization, bringing a hands-on perspective to the work.
Prior to serving in the legislature, she spent six years on the Grand Rapids Public Schools Board, including two as Board Chair.
She believes in delivering results that expand opportunity, lower costs, and create long-term stability for residents.
