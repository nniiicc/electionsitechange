CONTRIBUTE About Shane Meet Shane Abbott, Northwest Florida Fighter Shane Abbott is a lifelong resident of Northwest Florida who understands the unique issues facing our rural and coastal communities.
As a pharmacist and local small business owner, Shane is using his expertise in Tallahassee to fight for lower drug costs, a stronger economy, and safer communities.
Learn More District 5 Fighting For You Our Priorities Shane Abbott is committed to delivering real results for Northwest Florida families.
Affordability & Tax Relief Representative Abbott is championing House Joint Resolution 207 to provide permanent, inflation-proof property tax relief by creating a new 25% homestead exemption for Florida homeowners.
He remains dedicated to cutting wasteful government spending to ensure that living in the Sunshine State remains affordable for every family and senior.
Lowering Prescription Drug Prices As a pharmacist, Shane utilized his professional expertise to help pass landmark legislation that cracks down on "middleman" Pharmacy Benefit Managers who drive up the cost of life-saving medication.
He continues to fight for total transparency in the drug market to ensure patients pay the lowest possible price at the pharmacy counter.
Cracking Down on Illegal Immigration Shane believes that the rule of law is the foundation of a safe society and has consistently voted to ban sanctuary cities and strengthen E-Verify requirements across Florida.
He stands firm in his commitment to protect our borders and ensure that state resources are used to serve legal citizens rather than subsidize illegal immigration.
Protecting the Right to Life Guided by his faith and family values, Shane is an unwavering advocate for the unborn and supports legislation that promotes a culture of life in Florida.
He recently filed HB 663 to strengthen civil protections for unborn children, ensuring that Florida remains a leader in defending the sanctity of human life.
Standing up for the Second Amendment Representative Abbott is a staunch defender of the constitutional right to bear arms and believes every law-abiding Floridian has the right to protect their home and family.
He has consistently opposed restrictive gun control measures and supports policies that uphold the Second Amendment without compromise.
Supporting the Trump-DeSantis Agenda Shane is a proud ally of the bold, conservative leadership that has made Florida the "Free State" of the nation and a model for the America First movement.
He works tirelessly to advance a platform of economic freedom, parental rights in education, and a total rejection of radical federal overreach.

Abbott Files Landmark Legislation to Provide Lasting Property Tax Relief Representative Shane Abbott has officially filed HJR 207, a constitutional amendment designed to future-proof the homestead exemption.
By shifting from a flat-dollar exemption to a percentage-based model, Abbott's plan ensures that tax relief scales alongside rising home values, providing meaningful savings for Panhandle homeowners and first-time buyers alike.
Learn More December 18, 2025 Abbott Champions New Funding and Resources for Florida's Rural Counties With the filing of HB 723, Representative Abbott is doubling down on his commitment to Northwest Florida's rural communities.
The proposed legislation seeks to expand the Small County Outreach Program and protect essential Medicaid providers in rural hospitals, ensuring that residents in fiscally constrained counties have the same access to quality infrastructure and healthcare as those in larger cities.
Learn More November 12, 2025 Fighting for Transparency: Abbott Continues Push Against Pharmacy Middlemen Drawing on his decades of experience as a pharmacist, Representative Abbott is leading the charge in Tallahassee to further regulate Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs).
Following the success of his previous reforms, Abbott is working on new measures to eliminate "junk fees" in the prescription drug supply chain, directly lowering the out-of-pocket costs for seniors and families at the pharmacy counter.
