Upcoming Events
Listening Session
Representative Michelle Bos-Lun and Democratic nominee Chris Lundberg will host a Listening Session on Thursday, Oct. 15 at noon to listen to concerns from residents of the three towns they hope to serve together as state representatives in the next biennium.
The Windham-3 district includes the towns of Westminster, Rockingham, and Brookline.
The new legislative session will begin in January.
The session will be held in the Main Floor meeting area of the Rockingham Free Public Library in Bellows Falls, Vermont.
“This will be an opportunity for residents of the Windham-3 district to share their priorities and let us know if they have any requests for legislation for the next session.
All are welcome to come ask questions and raise concerns or they can come listen to what others share” Representative Bos-Lun said.

Contact Representative Bos-Lun.

Early Childhood Candidate Forum (Bellows Falls)
Rockingham Free Public Library, 65 Westminster St., Bellows Falls, VT
Chris Lundberg will participate in this free in-person regional candidate forum hosted by Building Bright Futures.
All are welcome.
These forums are an opportunity for families, the early childhood workforce, and community members to hear from local candidates for Vermont’s House and Senate about where they stand on issues related to children and families.
Candidates will be asked about issues that matter to Vermont families and communities, including questions about economic security, housing, early childhood workforce shortages, and access to prekindergarten programs.

Join Amanda for coffee and a short talk followed by questions.
