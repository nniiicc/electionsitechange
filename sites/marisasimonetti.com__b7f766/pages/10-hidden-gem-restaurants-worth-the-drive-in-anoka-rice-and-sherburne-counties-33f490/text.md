Paul.
If you're looking for mouthwatering meals off the beaten path, head out to Anoka, Rice, or Sherburne County.
These restaurants are the kind of places locals love — and visitors are lucky to stumble upon.
Here's your guide to 10 unforgettable small-town eats that are 100% worth the drive. 🥞 1.
Sparky’s Café – Anoka, MN What to try: Buttermilk pancakes & homemade sausage Locals call this place "the breakfast heart of Anoka." It’s no-frills, just fluffy pancakes, crispy hashbrowns, and the kind of bottomless coffee that keeps you chatting for hours.
Come early — it fills up fast. 🌮 2.
Los Ocampo Taqueria – Elk River, MN (Sherburne County) What to try: Al pastor tacos & horchata This family-run taqueria tucked in a quiet strip mall beats most Twin Cities taco joints.
The meat is perfectly marinated and they don’t skimp on the cilantro.
Grab extra napkins — it's messy in the best way. 🍕 3.
Pizzeria 201 – Montgomery, MN (Rice County) What to try: Signature garlic chicken pizza This historic building houses one of Minnesota’s most underrated pizza spots.
Every crust is handmade, and they use ingredients from local farms.
It’s got that small-town charm and big-time flavor. 🍔 4.
King’s Place – Miesville, MN (Rice County) What to try: The “Kingburger” with onion rings Tucked away in a town of under 150 people, this burger joint draws crowds from all over southern Minnesota.
It’s casual, cozy, and consistently ranks as one of the best burgers in the state. 🍝 5.
Trattoria da Vinci – Anoka, MN What to try: Shrimp fettuccine Alfredo A hidden gem for Italian food lovers.
The pasta is fresh, sauces are rich, and the lighting is romantic enough for a date night.
And yes, they have a killer tiramisu. 🐟 6.
The Nordic Brewpub – Monticello, MN (Sherburne County) What to try: Walleye fish fry and house-brewed IPA You’ll find great beer and even better vibes here.
Rustic wood interiors, live music on weekends, and classic Minnesota comfort food.
The walleye is flaky and delicious. 🍜 7.
Cafe Donuts & Deli – Faribault, MN (Rice County) What to try: Vietnamese pho & fried rice Surprise!
This donut shop also serves up authentic Vietnamese food — and it’s incredible.
Try the pho with rare beef, then finish with a fresh donut for dessert. 🥗 8.
Serum’s Good Time Emporium – Anoka, MN What to try: Award-winning wings This quirky, retro bar and grill is packed with antiques and flavor.
Their wings win contests year after year, and the portions are generous.
It’s a great place for a casual night out. 🥪 9.
Whistling Pickle – Zimmerman, MN (Sherburne County) What to try: Pastrami on rye A true small-town gem.
Friendly service, creative sandwiches, and the kind of soups your grandma used to make.
Locals love it, and for good reason. 🍨 10.
Lonsdale Country Market & Deli – Lonsdale, MN (Rice County) What to try: Homemade ice cream and deli sandwiches Half convenience store, half deli, this place is a surprise delight.
Grab a scoop of their fresh strawberry ice cream and take in the friendly small-town energy. 📍 Why These Spots Matter More Than You Think These restaurants aren’t just tasty — they represent: •Local jobs and family-owned businesses •Unique flavors that can’t be replicated by chains •Destinations that boost rural tourism and fuel nearby shops Next time you think of dining out, consider swapping the city skyline for a country road and a memorable meal. 💡 Tip: Make a Weekend of It If you’re driving to Rice or Sherburne County, pair your food crawl with: •A stop at a local farmers market •A walk through a nearby state park •A visit to a quirky antique shop You’ll support local economies and discover what makes rural Minnesota so special. 🔗 Related Reads: Affordable Housing & Homeownership in Minnesota Things to Do in Minnesota 👤 About the Author Marisa Simonetti is a Minnesota-based housing advocate, real estate investor, and 2026 candidate for U.S.
