From secret burger joints to family-owned diners and unforgettable taco trucks, Savage, Rosemount, and New Prague have their fair share of mouthwatering spots that fly under the radar.
Here’s your guide to the best hole-in-the-wall restaurants in Scott and Dakota Counties—and why they deserve a visit (or five). 🍳 Savage, MN — Where Big Flavor Hides in Small Kitchens 1.
Windmill Cafe This cozy spot in downtown Savage is a local legend.
Serving breakfast and lunch only, the Windmill is known for homemade pancakes, greasy-spoon hashbrowns, and that warm, neighborly energy you only get in small-town diners. 📍 Why we love it: Cash-only.
Fresh.
Local.
If it’s your first time, go for the cinnamon swirl French toast.
2.
El Loro Mexican Grill Tucked in a strip mall off Highway 13, this local favorite serves sizzling fajitas, house-made salsa, and margaritas that pack a punch. 📍 Hole-in-the-wall rating: High.
The outside looks generic, but the inside is fiesta-ready and always packed with locals.
If you're thinking of selling your house in Savage MN, these hidden gems add charm and neighborhood pride that buyers notice. 🥪 Rosemount, MN — Blue-Collar Bites with Big Heart 1.
House of Coates This spot is technically just outside Rosemount, but locals claim it as their own.
Family-run since 1962, House of Coates is a biker bar-meets-diner that serves the legendary "Coates Burger." 🍔 Pro tip: Ask for it “deluxe” and don’t skip the onion rings.
2.
Morning Glory’s Bakery & Cafe A blink-and-you’ll-miss-it café on Hwy 3, Morning Glory’s serves homemade pies, cinnamon rolls, and hearty breakfasts. 🥧 Hole-in-the-wall charm: No website.
No digital menu.
Just great food and old-school service.
Need to sell your home in Rosemount MN?
Let buyers know they’re moving to a city with soul—and cinnamon rolls bigger than your face. 🌮 New Prague, MN — Old World Flavor Meets Midwest Hospitality 1.
Ettlin’s Ranchero Supper Club On the edge of town, Ettlin’s is where steakhouse classics meet Czech-inspired side dishes.
Think wiener schnitzel with mashed potatoes in gravy—and yes, they have Old Fashioneds on tap. 🥩 Vibe: Retro wood paneling, cozy booths, and a regular crowd that spans generations.
2.
Mi Jalisco Mexican Restaurant Authentic, fast, and ridiculously affordable.
This family-run restaurant has loyal fans from all over Scott County, and they serve the best enchiladas in the region—no contest. 🌮 What to order: Chicken mole enchiladas and house rice.
Even if you’re just exploring selling your home in New Prague MN, restaurants like these are proof that this small town knows how to eat. 🔍 What Makes a Hole-in-the-Wall Restaurant So Special? •They don’t advertise.

And for homeowners, they’re a reminder that location isn’t just about schools or commute times—it’s about culture, flavor, and authenticity. 🏠 Real Estate Tip: Highlight Local Flavor When preparing to list your home in Savage, Rosemount, or New Prague, don’t just talk about square footage.
Talk about the experience of living in your neighborhood.
A Saturday breakfast at Windmill Café or a Friday dinner at House of Coates says this is a place with heart.
