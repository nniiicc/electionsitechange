Marisa is an Independent got involved in politics after volunteering in her son's Kindergarten classroom and seeing how tirelessly our teachers work to invest in our children's futures, yet are constantly in jeopardy of losing vital funding support.
She also volunteers in the school lunchroom and sees the children blessed with free lunches, allowing them to thrive and grow.
Her heart is especially with the many Minnesotans who feel the system is broken and unfair.
She is currently doing work within the justice system to hold Brady/Giglio and other constitutional violations accountable.
Marisa is a mother, businesswoman, and holds a Bachelor's degree in Psychology from the University of Minnesota.
Our state has so much to offer, from beautiful Duluth, the State Fair, Minnehaha Falls, the Guthrie Theater, and many historic buildings such as Paisley Park and Mill City Museum.
She volunteers in the community and stands up for families and workers to ensure they receive the support they need so they can thrive, grow, and connect in our beautiful state of Minnesota.
