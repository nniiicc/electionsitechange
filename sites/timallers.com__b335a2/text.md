Who Is Tim?
Your neighbor, not a talking point Tim’s story starts here.
He is from this area, shaped by this area, and still calls Central New York home.
For almost 20 years, work took him to Colorado, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Virginia, Florida, Tennessee, Rhode Island, Ohio, and West Virginia, but he always came back.
He came back because family is here.
His kids are here.
His friends are here.
He came back because of the people, the pace, and yes, even the weather.
This is home, and that matters.
Why Now?
Because people feel taken for granted Across party lines, Tim hears the same frustration: too many people feel like this corner of New York has been taken for granted for too long because of one letter in front of a name.
Folks are not happy with the direction their towns have gone over the last 20 to 25 years.
Tim is running to disrupt that power structure, challenge the local establishment, and do the work of making government answer to people again instead of expecting people to answer to the establishment.
