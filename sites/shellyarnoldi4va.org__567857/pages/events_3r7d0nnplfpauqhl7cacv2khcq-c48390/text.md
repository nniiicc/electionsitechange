About the Event
The Jefferson Forum is pleased to announce the relaunch of the popular Federal Policy Dinners previously held by our predecessor, the Thomas Jefferson Institute.
These dinners bring together business and community leaders from around Virginia to engage in discussions of Federal policy that impacts our great Commonwealth.
Prior speakers have included former Chair of the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission Mark Christie, Economist Steve Moore, former Heritage Foundation President Edward Feulner, Jr., Robert Woodson and former Virginia Governor James Gilmore to name just a few.
