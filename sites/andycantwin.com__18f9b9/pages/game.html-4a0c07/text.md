Rig the Map
Politicians don't win elections.
They draw them.
See how it works.
Takes about 3 minutes
How to Play
- Pick a district from the numbered buttons below the grid.
- Tap cells to assign them to that district.
Each district needs exactly 5 cells.
- Cells can connect side-by-side or diagonally.
They just have to touch.
- Tap a cell again to clear it.
Or use the eraser.
- Fill all 5 districts, then hit Submit Map.
The catch?
You're drawing districts to guarantee a winner before anyone votes.
