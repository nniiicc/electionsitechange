He is also the co-chair of the Eugene chapter of the Pacific Green Party.
In April of 2024, Justin, along with other concerned faculty and staff at the University of Oregon, founded the UO Faculty and Staff for Justice in Palestine to help support and protect the student protesters.
Justin's top priorities include ending the genocide in Palestine and reining in our military spending, bringing federal dollars back to Oregon’s Congressional District 4 to help address the housing and unhoused crisis, and ensuring strong protections for our federal lands.
