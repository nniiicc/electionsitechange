Since 1988, Representative Diaz has served as the Senior Pastor of
Grace Chapel in Benson, and serves as Chaplain for AZ Legislative District 19.
Representative Diaz has been married to Mary (Twinkie) Diaz for 52 years June 1st 2026.
They have 3 children, 11 grandchildren and 5 great grandchildren.
Lupe Diaz grew up around the mining industry on a small quarter and appaloosa horse ranch in
Bisbee AZ.
This upbringing helped imbue Lupe with an appreciation for the importance of our
natural resources to our state and local economy.
He went to work at Fort Huachuca on a Worker Trainee program, where he learned building
maintenance before eventually making his way up to contract compliance officer.
During his
time at Fort Huachuca, he was saved and gained an intense interest in Biblical Theology and the
Christian life.
Eventually, when the time came for him to take a different job within civil service,
he chose instead to pursue a calling as a full-time pastor.
In 1988 he received a call to pastor a small rural church in Benson, AZ.
Today, the church has
grown considerably, and he is still the lead pastor at Grace Chapel Benson.
In 1998 they opened
Grace Christian Academy and until 2023.
Lupe answered other calls within the Benson community where he served as President of the Benson /San Pedro Valley Chamber of Commerce for two terms, and four years as a City Councilman.
Lupe currently serves as a State Representative for District 19, which he has done since taking
the Oath of Office on November 17, 2020.
