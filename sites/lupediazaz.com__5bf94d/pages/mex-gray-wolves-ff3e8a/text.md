They have already killed 24 cows.
Wolves are known to pull out a baby calve from the cow

In the White Mountains of AZ and western NM, bus stops are protected by chain link fences so wolves cannot attack children waiting for their school bus.
Children in this area are not allowed to play outdoors unless supervised, due to possible wolve attacks.
