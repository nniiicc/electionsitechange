Corey Rosenlee was born and raised in Hawai’i.
I have spent 30 years working as a public school teacher, and more than 20 years at Campbell High School.
My mom was a special education teacher at Wilson Elementary School and my dad was a social work professor at UH-Mānoa.
Later, my father launched Adult Friends for Youth, a nonprofit organization that uplifts disadvantaged children. ​ In 2015, I was elected to be the President of the Hawai’i State Teachers Association, where I worked to give our keiki the schools they deserve and end Hawai’i’s chronic teacher shortage.
I fought hard to improve our public school system, so that our islands’ keiki could reach their highest potential.
I am currently a member of the 'Ewa Neighborhood Board.
I believe Hawai'i needs real solutions to our most pressing problems, like making Hawai'i affordable, giving every child a quality education, and caring for our kūpuna.
Together, we can give every an opportunity to thrive in our island home.
