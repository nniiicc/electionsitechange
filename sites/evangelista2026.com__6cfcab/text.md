Restore Accountability & Public Integrity Read More Public trust requires meaningful oversight.
Michael will work to ensure that no public official or special interest is above the law by: Reestablishing the Public Integrity Task Force Strengthening cooperation with the Office of the Public Auditor and Department of Public Safety Supporting safe and confidential channels for reporting government misconduct Taking corruption and misuse of taxpayer funds seriously 03.
Build a Strong & Responsive Attorney General’s Office Read More A strong Attorney General’s Office begins with strong people.

04.

Michael will: Enforce the Open Government Act Promote timely access to public records Improve public-information practices Reduce unnecessary delays and bureaucratic barriers Make government actions easier for residents to understand Public records should be open and disclosed.
05.
