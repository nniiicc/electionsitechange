About me
Like most, my path hasn’t been easy.
My grandparents moved to Lincoln County in 1976.
My grandpa built his career as a drywaller and so did my father.
My father was the 1st to graduate from High School(LHS-81).
I went on to graduate with my Bachelor of Science in Geology from Central Washington University(2010).
While in school I worked in a restaurant as a busser, host, waiter, and day bartender.
After college I went straight into the oil field and worked as an onsite geologist.
Now on year 17 I have been able to leverage my skill set to work from home doing remote geosteering.
This wasn’t by accident or given to me, I had to make a lot of hard decisions and stand up for myself and my abilities.
After my son was born my family moved to Lincoln County in 2017 because I always saw this as the best place on earth and I wanted to follow in my grandparent’s footsteps.
