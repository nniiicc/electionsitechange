Meet Julia A fifth generation Oklahoman, Julia is the daughter of an Air Force veteran and a minister.
Julia learned the value of giving back to her community from an early age.
Julia has served for over 8 years in the Legislature and is the current Democratic Minority Leader of the State Senate.
