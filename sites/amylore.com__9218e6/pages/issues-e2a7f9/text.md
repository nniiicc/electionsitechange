Focused on What Matters Strong Schools Every child needs access to high-quality education and the opportunity to succeed.
Amy will focus on optimal student outcomes, excellence in teaching, and sustainable deregulation that eases administrative burdens and promotes professional, local governance.
Smart Government Hoosiers need practical solutions and responsible spending from their leaders.
Whether it is education or health care, housing or public safety, sound policy comes from prioritizing freedom and accountability – cutting red tape wherever possible and demanding accountability for every taxpayer dollar.
Economic Opportunity Government does not create jobs.
It creates an environment where Hoosiers can create jobs.
The state’s role is to protect personal freedom and access to the market by providing high-quality education, limiting regulatory burdens, keeping infrastructure sound, and making communities safe.
Community Investment Vigo County is at its best when local people, institutions, and businesses collaborate to solve problems close to home.
State government should be a partner in that, there to foster opportunity and encourage the growth, innovation, and philanthropy that make Terre Haute and West Terre Haute strong.
