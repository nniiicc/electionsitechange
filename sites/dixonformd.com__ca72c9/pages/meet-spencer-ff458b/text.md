I’m running for Delegate because too many people feel like our economy isn’t working for them and our democracy isn’t responding to them.
Born and raised in Anne Arundel County, I hold a deep commitment to public service.
H aving come of age in the wake of the 2008 financial crisis, my family experienced the uncertainty and struggle that too many are forced to overcome daily.
I saw my mom, diagnosed with a chronic illness before ACA protections for pre-existing conditions, spend long afternoons fighting with insurance companies when she should have been focused on healing.
This experience, and the knowledge that it was happening in millions of homes across the country, was my political awakening and first call to service.
I spent five years on the campaigns of Democrats who fought for fairness, including Senator Elizabeth Warren’s 2020 presidential campaign.
When not organizing for those candidates and causes, I worked for a nonprofit promoting ACA enrollment while earning my Master’s in Public Policy with a focus on health care.
After organizing in the 2022 midterms in Maryland on the team that elected Governor Wes Moore, I served as Senator Dawn Gile’s Legislative Director, helping to pass legislation that meets the critical needs of Anne Arundel County residents.
I now work for the Maryland Prescription Drug Affordability Board where I continue the fight for a more affordable and fair Maryland.
An organizer, a public servant, and a fighter, I’m ready to amplify the voices of working Marylanders and build a strong community for all.
I can be reached at spencer@dixonformd.com.
