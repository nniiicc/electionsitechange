Community Runs After the cross-district tour, Tawn began running the townships that were not on the original 15-leg route.
Community Run August 15 Henrietta Township (Pleasant Lake) 4.77 miles • Taxes & Local Control A loop around Pleasant Lake starting and ending at Pleasant Lake County Park.
Lower taxes, protect homeowners, and keep decisions local.
Community Run August 25 Tompkins Township 3.9 miles • Election Integrity A 3.9-mile loop starting and ending at Tompkins Town Hall.
Proof of citizenship, photo ID, and clean voter rolls.
Community Run August 29 Hanover Village & Township 6 miles • Cost of Living Door to door through the village and township.
Cut taxes, no tax on tips, overtime, or Social Security, and lower energy costs.
Community Run September 5 Liberty Township (Round Lake) 5.4 miles • Liberty & Ending Political Lawfare A rainy loop around Round Lake from 130 Steves Scenic Dr.
Equal justice, no two-tier legal system, and an end to using courts as a political weapon.
Community Run September 12 Lyndon Township (Run Woodstock) 5 miles • First Responders & 9/11 Flower Power 5 Mile at Run Woodstock.
Tawn finished in 43:54 — 4th overall among the women — dedicated to police, firefighters, and the memory of September 11, 2001.
Community Run September 25 Brooklyn Village 4.6 miles • Every Village on the Map Met at Swain Memorial Park, canvassed the village, and finished at Village Creamery on Main Street.
Community Run September 29 Freedom Township (Pleasant Lake) 5 miles • Freedom — Speech, Faith, and Property A sunset loop from Freedom Township Hall around Pleasant Lake.
Neighbors, yard signs, and a township whose name should still mean something.
Community Run October 3 Sandstone Township (Parma) 5.4 miles • Property taxes and the schools A loop through Parma village.
Neighbors kept coming back to the tax bill, a break for seniors, and what the schools are teaching.
Community Run October 1 Webster Township (Parks Lake) 4.6 miles • Protecting the Unborn From Marquis Drive near Parks Lake to 9122 Huron River Drive.
