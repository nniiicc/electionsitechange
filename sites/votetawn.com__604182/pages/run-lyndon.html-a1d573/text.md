The Flower Power 5 Mile at Run Woodstock — a single trail loop starting and ending at Woodstock Camp on Bartell Road in Gregory.
Tawn ran the Flower Power 5 Mile through the woods and fields of Lyndon Township on Saturday, September 12 — the day after she opened Run Woodstock with the National Anthem.
She finished in 43:54 and placed 4th overall among the women.
This leg is dedicated to the police officers, firefighters, and first responders who run toward danger so the rest of us can live in peace.
A Washtenaw County community run in honor of those who serve — and in memory of September 11, 2001.
On September 11, 2001, first responders ran into the fire.
Tawn runs in Lyndon Township to honor that courage — and to stand with the police and firefighters who still put their lives on the line for Michigan families.
