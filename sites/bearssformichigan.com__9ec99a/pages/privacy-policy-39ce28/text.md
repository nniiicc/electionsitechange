You are encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy to stay informed of updates.

COLLECTION OF YOUR INFORMATION
We may collect information about you in a variety of ways.
The information we may collect on the Site includes:
Personal Data
Personally identifiable information, such as your name, email address, and telephone number, and other information you voluntarily provide to us when you sign up for updates, submit a form, donate, RSVP, volunteer, or otherwise communicate with us.
You are under no obligation to provide personal information.
Personal information is collected and retained only when you choose to submit it to us through the Site.
SMS / Mobile Messaging Data
If you provide your phone number, you may receive campaign-related text messages (including informational and promotional messages).
We may collect and store your phone number, consent status, message history, and related metadata (such as delivery status and timestamps).
“Mobile information will not be shared.
All categories exclude textmessaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information willnot be shared with any third parties.”

Donation Data
If you make a donation through the Site, your payment is processed by a third-party payment processor.
We may receive information related to your donation (such as name, address, email, employer/occupation where required, amount, and transaction confirmation).
We do not store full payment card numbers; payment information is handled by the third-party processor subject to their privacy and security practices.
Derivative Data
Information our servers automatically collect when you access the Site, such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, access times, referring/exit pages, and pages you viewed.
Device and Usage Data
Information about the device you use to access the Site (such as device type, unique device identifiers where available, and approximate location derived from IP address).
