September 8, 2026
Kentucky Right to Life Endorses Representative DJ Johnson for Kentucky House District 13 in 2026 General Election
OWENSBORO, KY - Kentucky Right to Life has endorsed Representative DJ Johnson for re-election in Kentucky House District 13.
The endorsement recognizes Representative Johnson's consistent record of supporting policies that protect the dignity of human life and strengthen families across Daviess County.
"Protecting life is not a talking point for me.
It is a commitment I keep every time legislation reaches the House floor."
Kentucky Right to Life is the state's oldest and largest single-issue pro-life organization, educating voters and advocating for laws that defend life from conception to natural death.
Kentucky Chamber PAC Endorses Representative DJ Johnson for Kentucky House District 13 in 2026 General Election
OWENSBORO, KY - The Kentucky Chamber Political Action Committee (PAC) has endorsed Representative DJ Johnson for Kentucky House District 13 in the upcoming general election on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Candidates endorsed by the Kentucky Chamber PAC have demonstrated a strong commitment to advancing policies that support job growth, economic development, and a higher quality of life for Kentucky families and businesses.
"I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Kentucky Chamber PAC.
Daviess County works when our employers can grow and our families can find good jobs close to home, and that is the work I take to Frankfort every session."
The Kentucky Chamber PAC evaluates candidates based on their understanding of key business issues, past voting records, and responses to the organization's 2026 legislative candidate survey.
Only candidates who completed the Kentucky Chamber PAC's legislative candidate survey and are participating in the general election were considered for endorsement.
The Kentucky Chamber PAC is the independent political arm of the Kentucky Chamber of Commerce, the state's largest broad-based business organization.
