Privacy Policy
Menon for Vermont House respects your privacy.
We collect limited personal information that you choose to provide through this website, such as your name, email address, phone number, and town.
This information is collected when you submit a form to join the campaign or request information.
We use this information to:
- Share campaign updates and information about local issues
- Communicate about events and opportunities to get involved
- Respond to inquiries you initiate
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
Your information may be stored and processed using trusted third-party services, including Squarespace (website hosting and form storage) and Google Workspace (email communication), solely for campaign communication and administrative purposes.
If and when campaign emails are sent, you will have the ability to unsubscribe from future communications.
If you have questions about this privacy policy, you may contact us at campaign@menonforvermont.com with the subject “Privacy Policy.”
