Meet Elaine Priorities Endorsements District Map Contact Donate Priorities Elaine's campaign focuses on several key priorities: Family Healthcare Today, many Americans are forced to rely on crowdfunding platforms to cover essential medical expenses.
Although the United States is the wealthiest industrialized nation, it remains the only major developed country without a system of universal healthcare.
Access to medical care should be treated as a fundamental right and guaranteed to all citizens.
Elaine believes in accessible and affordable healthcare for all residents of District 25.
As a nurse, she understands the importance of quality care and will fight to ensure everyone can access it.
As a small business owner, Elaine has experienced the undue burden that is being placed on small businesses in order to provide healthcare to their employees.
Education Elaine advocates for quality education for every child in District 25.
She opposes diverting resources away from public schools and supports initiatives to strengthen our education system.
Freedom Artificial Intelligence (AI) Artificial intelligence (AI) offers significant technological advancement but also introduces substantial societal challenges.
Currently, automated license plate recognition (ALPR) systems, such as Flock Safety cameras, monitor daily public transit and have raised oversight concerns regarding data misuse.
Furthermore, AI-driven applications are increasingly used in cyberattacks targeting government infrastructure and civilian data, while malicious actors utilize deep-fake imagery and synthetic voice technology to execute sophisticated financial scams.
Together, these developments make discerning authentic content from misinformation on social media platforms exceptionally difficult.
In response to these challenges, Republicans generally advocate for industry-led self-regulation to foster innovation.
However, self-regulation historically fails to provide adequate public protections.
To address these vulnerabilities, Elaine pledges to implement robust regulatory frameworks and guardrails designed to safeguard the privacy and data rights of all Georgia citizens.
Datacenters Right now, across Georgia, data centers are rapidly expanding into local communities.
Public utility companies, such as Georgia Power, are using eminent domain—and the threat of it—to force the sale of private land and residential properties to build the massive high-voltage transmission lines required to power these data centers.
While data center operators often promise economic growth, local communities frequently face rising electricity bills as utilities pass the costs of grid expansion onto everyday ratepayers.
Additionally, tech companies benefit from significant state-legislated sales tax exemptions on equipment, reducing potential tax revenue that would otherwise benefit the public.
Elaine believes that data centers should only be allowed when they are legally obligated to offset all resulting utility rate hikes and infrastructure costs.
Elaine will also push for reforms that ensure data centers fully contribute to local and state tax bases.
Fixing our Broken System Elaine is committed to enacting comprehensive legislative and systemic reforms to restore integrity, transparency, and accountability to our government.
Legislative Term Limits: Elaine will champion legislation establishing a three-term limit for Georgia House Representatives to ensure fresh perspectives and continuous public accountability.
Campaign Finance Reform: Elaine pledges to reject all corporate and billionaire contributions, remaining fully beholden to the citizens of District 25.
Ethics and Anti-Corruption: Elaine will introduce strict ethics bills prohibiting elected officials from holding financial stakes in companies that bid on or possess state government contracts.
Ending Gerrymandering: Elaine will advocate for the creation of an independent, non-partisan commission to draw future district maps, ensuring voters choose their representatives rather than politicians choosing their voters.
Reproductive Rights Reproductive Rights are on the ballot in 2026.
Elaine is a strong supporter of reproductive freedom and healthcare privacy, believing that every woman has the RIGHT to make her own healthcare decisions along with her doctor.
We must not allow government to take away this fundamental and essential right.
Second Amendment and Safe Storage Laws Elaine supports responsible gun ownership and advocates for safe storage laws, especially when there are minors in the household, in order to prevent accidents and tragedies.
Marijuana Legalization Elaine supports the legalization of marijuana and use of the resulting tax revenue in order to fund education, provide opioid treatment programs, reduce property taxes and invest in our communities.
Finances Economic Development Elaine is committed to fostering a thriving business environment in our community.
As a long-time business owner, she knows the challenges that small businesses face.
She'll work to attract investment, create job opportunities, and support local businesses, including initiatives for affordable housing.
Move Georgia Toward Energy Self-Reliance Georgia is not and has never been an oil-state.
Therefore, if Georgia can grow its own energy infrastructure, then we can keep more money and jobs in our state helping ordinary Georgians.
The Green Technology and Sustainability Market is expected to grow over 400% by 2030 ( source ).
Elaine will work to actively encourage and support local Green Technology, build out Green Infrastructure and actively work to attract Green Technology investment in her district.
