How can you serve District 92?
By representing your neighborhood, community or subdivision as member of the District 92 Coalition.
The coalition meets quarterly but its primary focus is to know what's going on in their area; know the needs and concerns of the residents and relate that information back to Representative Taylor.
Let's be informed, engaged and purposefully working in the community...for the community!
We're looking for individuals who are active in their community to serve as points of contact to the Capitol.
