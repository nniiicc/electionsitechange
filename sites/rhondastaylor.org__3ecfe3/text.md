FROM THE NEWS Summer is Here!
And now that the session is over, our Constituents Speak Series is back up and running! ​ We meet every 4th Saturday at the Conyers-Rockdale Library at 10 am for breakfast and discussion.
The topics vary and are related to how we can work together to keep Rockdale the place we love to live, work and play. ​ ​ Join Us, bring a neighbor, bring a friend! ​ The summer months are also the best time to share your ideas, your policy concerns and any possible laws you would like to see implemented in Georgia. ​ Sharing early allows time for research and review of the data before the next session begins. ​ Need A Private Meeting? ​ If you're in need of a private meeting with Representative Taylor, simply reach out to the state office at 404-656-0265 to schedule an in office appointment.
Your concern must not be campaign related ​ For any district needs, call the same number and the administrator will take your query and provide assistance.

Taylor wins election for a 4th Term!
Representative Taylor was reelected to another term to serve the approximately 58,547 constituents of district 92 which now covers only Rockdale County.
