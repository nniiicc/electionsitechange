Tillett Hello Fellow Conservatives Thank you for your willingness to be involved in the necessary but difficult and sometimes tenebrous work of governance and service to our community.
I seek your vote as your next Senator for District 1.
I am a Christian, conservative, proud American, father, grandfather, husband, businessman, and public servant.
My parents instilled in me a decent respect for America and all of its finest institutions including: a divinely inspired system of government, adherence to rule of law, personal accountability, fiscal responsibility, FREE ENTERPRISE, religious freedom, defense of and adherence to our Constitutions, US and State, as well as the importance of family.
It has been an honor to serve our State and district as Senior Resident Superior Court Judge, serving as Judge for over 30 years, having held court in 86 of our 100 counties.
Previously, I worked with the Legislature at the highest levels, chaired local projects and legislative endeavors, as well as district and statewide innovations.
I would appreciate your support.
Sincerely, Jerry R.
Tillett North Carolina’s Issues As a Christian, Conservative, Public Servant and Businessman, Jerry Tillett believes in God, Family, Country and Community.
Here are his top priorities as your next State Senator.
Law & Order Law and Order form the bedrock of civil society, enabling the protection of rights, the resolution of conflicts, and the promotion of public welfare.
Free Enterprise An economic system where individuals and businesses operate for profit with minimal interference from the government.
Economic Prosperity The state of wealth in our district measured by growth, jobs, innovation, and stability.
Quality of life for Residents The quality of life for residents includes wealth, employment, opportunity, health, education, safety, and independence.
Open Debate With Respect Respect and civility are crucial for constructive discussions to promote the free exchange of ideas.
Greater insight may be gained by entertaining diverse viewpoints.
