As a Christian, Conservative, Public Servant and Businessman, Jerry Tillett believes in God, Family, Country and Community.
Here are his top priorities as your next State Senator:
Law and Order
Law and Order form the bedrock of civil society, enabling the protection of rights, the resolution of conflicts, and the promotion of public welfare.
Free Enterprise
An economic system where individuals and businesses operate for profit with minimal interference from the government.
Economic Prosperity
The state of wealth in our district measured by growth, jobs, innovation, and stability.
Quality of Life for Residents
The quality of life for residents includes wealth, employment, opportunity, health, education, safety, and independence.
Open Debate with Respect
Respect and civility are crucial for constructive discussions to promote the free exchange of ideas.
Greater insight may be gained by entertaining diverse viewpoints.
