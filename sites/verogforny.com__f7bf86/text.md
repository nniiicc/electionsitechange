Donate Veronica Gonzalez for NYS Assembly 74th District Get to know Veronica Veronica Gonzalez a true community voice for our families and district Veronica Gonzalez is a proud Hispanic New Yorker, community advocate, district leader, mom, small business owner, author, and common-sense leader running to represent the 74th Assembly District in Albany.
Born into a hardworking family that believed in faith, opportunity, and personal responsibility, Veronica understands firsthand the challenges facing families in Manhattan today — rising costs, public safety concerns, struggling small businesses, and a government and career electeds that too often ignore everyday residents with their failed socialist policies and agenda.
Veronica has been a strong voice for: Restoring public safety, bail reform, and ending policies that make communities less safe, investing more in our first responders, and mental health services Fighting for transparency, wasteful spending, rooting out fraud and abuse in City Hall and Albany, and ethical government Supporting small businesses burdened by excessive regulation and taxes Support home owners and renters against tax increases Fighting back against socialism and failed socialist policies that wreck New York Fight in Albany to pass eBike regulations such as Priscilla's Law Repeal Congestion pricing and tolls Fight to keep hospitals, youth, family, veteran, and senior centers open Protecting public housing residents against RAD/PACT, supporting affordable housing, and Mitchell-Lama 2.0 developments Standing up for parents and families, gifted students, youth sports programs, open parks and green spaces, and community centers Protecting seniors and reforming state guardianship laws to safeguard family rights Homeless services & NGO reform In a district where many feel unheard, Veronica represents a new generation of Hispanic leadership — independent, fearless, and focused on results rather than politics, insiders, or elites She is running for Assembly to give the 74th District a voice that will fight for safer streets, economic opportunity, and a future where working families can thrive.
The Lower East Side, West Village, East Village, Gramercy, Kips Bay, Midtown South, and Assembly District 74 deserves a voice that never backs down.
Veronica Gonzalez has been on the front lines fighting for our communities and families against socialism.
Now, she’s ready to take that fight to Albany.
Join the movement, get involved, and help us deliver real results for Assembly District 74.
Veronica is committed to bringing balance, conservatism, accountability, and real representation back to Albany.
She believes the government should protect all citizens, provide public safety, defend small businesses, preserve affordable housing for working families and seniors, and ensure taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly.
Veronica's Pledge Serving Our Community Born and raised in the Lower East Side, Veronica Gonzalez has spent decades serving her community.
Through nonprofit partnerships, community outreach, and out of her own pocket, she has donated thousands of book bags and school supplies, secured tens of thousands of dollars in direct assistance to families throughout NYC, helped NYCHA tenants get backlog apartment repairs, provided free haircuts for local children, and organized year-round toy, clothing drives and holiday meals for inner-city youth, homeless families, and veterans who served our country with honor.
Veronica isn’t a career politician — she’s our neighbor, small business owner, advocate, mom, and champion who shows up.
