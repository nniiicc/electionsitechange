Veronica Gonzalez is a proud Hispanic New Yorker, community advocate, district leader, mom, small business owner, author, and common-sense leader running to represent the 74th Assembly District in Albany.
Born into a hardworking family that believed in faith, opportunity, and personal responsibility, Veronica understands firsthand the challenges facing families in Manhattan today — rising costs, public safety concerns, struggling small businesses, and a government and career electeds that too often ignore everyday residents with their failed socialist policies and agenda.
Veronica has been a strong voice for:
- Restoring public safety, bail reform, and ending policies that make communities less safe, investing more in our first responders, and mental health services
- Fighting for transparency, wasteful spending, rooting out fraud and abuse in City Hall and Albany, and ethical government
- Supporting small businesses burdened by excessive regulation and taxes
- Support home owners and renters against tax increases
- Fighting back against socialism and failed socialist policies that wreck New York
- Fight in Albany to pass eBike regulations such as Priscilla's Law
- Repeal Congestion pricing and tolls
- Fight to keep hospitals, youth, family, veteran, and senior centers open
- Protecting public housing residents against RAD/PACT, supporting affordable housing, and Mitchell-Lama 2.0 developments
- Standing up for parents and families, gifted students, youth sports programs, open parks and green spaces, and community centers
- Protecting seniors and reforming state guardianship laws to safeguard family rights
- Homeless services & NGO reform
In a district where many feel unheard, Veronica represents a new generation of Hispanic leadership — independent, fearless, and focused on results rather than politics, insiders, or elites
