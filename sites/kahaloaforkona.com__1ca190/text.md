Representative Kirstin Kahaloa has dedicated her career to improving the well-being of our community.
She was elected to the State House of Representatives in 2022 representing Kona’s District 6, Kailua to Hōnaunau.
Now she is running for re-election to continue her work.
Kirstin brought her passion for service to the State House, and the combination of her experience and commitment makes her the most qualified person to keep representing West Hawaiʻi’s District 6 in the State House.
