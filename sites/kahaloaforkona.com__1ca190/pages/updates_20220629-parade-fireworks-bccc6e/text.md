Jun 29 Written By Ilihia Gionson Kirstin and the ʻohana will be marching in the Kailua Kona Community Independence Day Parade, then enjoying the fireworks over Kailua Bay!
The parade starts at 6 p.m. on July 4, going from Kekuaokalani Gym, along Aliʻi Drive to Wālua Road.
The fireworks follow at 8 p.m.
See more information at the parade website - see you there!
