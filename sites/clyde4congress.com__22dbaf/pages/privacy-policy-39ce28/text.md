Privacy Policy Andrew Clyde for U.S.
Congress Effective Date: 03/09/2026 Andrew Clyde for U.S.
Congress (the "Campaign," "we," "us") respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, volunteer, or otherwise interact with us online.
1.
Information We Collect a.
Information You Provide We may collect personal information you choose to provide, including your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, and zip code, when you submit forms such as contact, volunteer, event RSVP, sign-up, or donation-related forms. b.
Donation-Related Information If you donate, a third-party payment processor typically processes the transaction.
We may receive donation-related information such as name, contact information, amount, date, and confirmation.
We generally do not receive your full payment card number. c.
Text Message Opt-In Information If you opt in to SMS or MMS communications, we collect your phone number, consent records, and messaging activity. d.
Information Collected Automatically We may automatically collect IP address, device identifiers, browser type, pages viewed, links clicked, and approximate location derived from IP address.
We may use cookies and similar technologies.
2.
How We Use Information We use information to: Communicate with you and respond to requests.
Send updates, newsletters, and messages you request.
Manage volunteering, events, and outreach.
Improve our website and communications.
Protect against fraud and security incidents.
Comply with campaign-finance and election laws and enforce our rights.
3.
How We Share Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with: Service providers and vendors supporting operations (hosting, email, analytics, CRM, texting providers, payment processors).
Legal and safety disclosures as required by law or to protect rights and safety.
Campaign-finance disclosures when donor information must be reported or disclosed as required by law.
4.
Text Message Policy Opt-in texts are used for campaign-related communications, including fundraising, informational, and volunteer messages.
Text messaging opt-in data, consent, and related collected personal information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, unless required by law. ​ Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency varies.
Reply STOP to opt out.
Reply HELP for help.
6.
Your Choices You may: Unsubscribe from emails via the unsubscribe link in any email.
Opt out of text messages by replying STOP.
Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and operational limits.
7.
Data Security We use reasonable safeguards to protect your personal information and employ industry-standard security technologies.
However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
8.
Third-Party Links Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such third parties.
We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those third parties when accessing their websites or services.
9.
Children's Privacy Our website is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will take steps to remove such information from our records.
10.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.
Updates are effective upon posting of the revised policy on our website.
We encourage you to review this page periodically.
11.
