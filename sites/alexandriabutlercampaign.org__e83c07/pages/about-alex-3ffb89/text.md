Early Promise Raised in Wichita, Kansas by her grandmother after being removed from her mother’s care, Alexandria was reading by the age of three and spent her academic years in gifted programs.
Despite early brilliance, her teenage years brought struggle, including teen pregnancy, spiritual conflict, and emotional isolation.
A Survivor She survived personal trauma—including abortion, adoption, and the challenges of raising a transgender child—experiences that now shape her compassionate yet principled conservative worldview.
A Small Business Owner As a former small business owner and homeschooling mother, Alexandria deeply understands the struggles facing working-class families, especially those abandoned by government overreach.
A Commitment to Us Alex proudly serves as VP of Communications for UCA – Urban Conservatives of America, a faith-based, non profit organization, which stands for faith, family, and freedom in American Urban Communities.
Alexandria means “helper of mankind” – her purpose-filled name is fulfilled today, as she stands as a voice for the voiceless—committed to rebuilding strong families, defending parental rights, securing the nation, and restoring opportunity for all Americans.
About Texas' 9th Congressional District Texas’ 9th Congressional District is a vibrant and diverse region located in the heart of the Houston metropolitan area.
The district includes portions of southwest Houston, Missouri City, Stafford, and parts of Fort Bend and Harris Counties.
Known for its rich cultural heritage and dynamic communities, District 9 is home to hardworking families, thriving small businesses, and a strong spirit of resilience.
From bustling urban neighborhoods to growing suburban areas, the district represents the values of faith, family, and opportunity.
With a focus on economic growth, education, and public safety, District 9 stands at the crossroads of tradition and innovation, ready to shape the future of Texas and our nation.
