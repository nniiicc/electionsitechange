Border Security & Immigration Alexandria supports strong border enforcement paired with compassion for those already here.
Just as we lock our doors to protect our homes, a secure border is essential to national safety.
That said, we must remain open to those who follow proper legal processes.
I support a pathway to citizenship for those already living here—especially second-generation immigrants.
But this must go hand in hand with securing our southern border and completing the wall.
Parental Rights & Education Reform Alexandria believes deeply in parental authority and local control of education.
Parents must retain full authority over their children.
Handing over unchecked power to school systems erodes parental rights.
Education should be tailored to the unique needs of each district.
A one-size-fits-all national curriculum does not serve students or educators effectively.
Economic Policy Alexandria champions small business recovery, deregulation, and Trump-era economic policies.
She understands that small businesses are the backbone of our nation and seeks to protect, promote, and support them.
As a former small business owner, I witnessed firsthand how devastating government overreach during COVID was to entrepreneurs.” “It’s clear: under conservative leadership, the economy thrives.
Under liberal policies, regulation and instability hurt working families by incentivizing corporate greed and economically crushing local small businesses.
Healthcare Alexandria advocates for healthcare that builds wealth and protects dignity.
As a mother, faith leader, and someone who has personally experienced the challenges of economic hardship, I understand that true healthcare reform must go beyond coverage—it must create opportunity.
I believe healthcare should not just treat illness; it should help families thrive.
