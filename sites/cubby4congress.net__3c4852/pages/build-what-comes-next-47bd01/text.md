The central policy question of this campaign is not simply what government should provide, but what government can help people and communities build for themselves.
1.
FINISH THE JOB IN FLINT
Resolve the Water Crisis Completely
Advocate for special federal investment sufficient to finish replacement of remaining water-service infrastructure all the way from municipal systems into affected homes.
Public trust requires government to accept responsibility for making harmed communities whole.
2.
BUILD WHAT COMES NEXT
A Citizen-Led Regional Economic Plan
Convene municipal governments, businesses, labor, educational institutions, civic organizations, neighborhood leadership, entrepreneurs, and ordinary citizens in an open regional economic-revitalization planning process.
Federal resources should support a locally rooted strategy rather than impose another Washington formula.
3.
HEALTHCARE WORTHY OF MICHIGAN
Benchmark the Best
Pursue accessible, affordable, preventive healthcare and study high-performing systems, including Massachusetts, to strengthen primary and preventive care, reduce financial burden, and improve outcomes.
4.
EDUCATION FOR THE AI ECONOMY
Reinvest in Michigan's Human Capital
Invest in universities, community colleges, technical education, apprenticeships, workforce pathways, lifelong learning, and affordable higher education so Michigan people can lead rather than merely endure technological change.
5.
HOMES PEOPLE CAN ACTUALLY AFFORD
Build Permanent Community Wealth Through Community Land Trusts
Expand genuinely affordable construction and rehabilitation while supporting Community Land Trusts and other models that preserve affordability and community control over land across generations.
6.
GROW MICHIGAN FROM THE GROUND UP
Connect Rural and Urban Agriculture Through an 'Agriculture of the Middle'
Connect rural farmers, smaller and midsized producers, urban agriculture, food entrepreneurs, processors, distributors, institutions, and consumers while rebuilding aggregation, processing, distribution, markets, institutional purchasing, land access, and new-enterprise pathways.
Kwabena “Cubby” Nkromo is a grassroots civic leader, better food policy advocate, Green Party organizer, and congressional candidate rooted in community empowerment and a culture for service.
