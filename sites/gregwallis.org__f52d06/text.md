MEET GREG WALLIS
Greg Wallis proudly serves the people of California's 47th Assembly District and is running for re-election because he believes the best is yet to come for the Coachella Valley and Inland Empire.
As a third-generation Californian, he believes that our state should be the land of economic opportunity and social mobility.
Greg's core beliefs, namely the importance of hard work, independence, and perseverance, reflect the California mindset.
That's why he's lowering costs for working people, protecting us from crime, and cleaning up Sacramento's corruption.
By focusing on affordability, opportunity, and cutting-edge technology, Greg is confident he can help our region rise to the level of innovation California is known for.
