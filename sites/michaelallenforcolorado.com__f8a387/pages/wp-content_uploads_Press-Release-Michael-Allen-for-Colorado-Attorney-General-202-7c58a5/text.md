Statement from Michael Allen on Supreme Court Taking Up Boulder Climate Case Colorado Springs, Colorado - Fourth Judicial District Attorney and Republican candidate for Attorney General Michael Allen today issued the following statement on the Supreme Court’s decision to review Boulder’s climate lawsuit and urged his Democrat opponents to let Coloradans know if they support using the courts to pursue these climate lawsuits: “Boulder shouldn’t be trying to set national energy policy from a Colorado courtroom – and Colorado shouldn’t be dragged into costly, politically driven climate lawsuits that do nothing but drive-up energy bills.
This is the wrong fight, in the wrong place, at the wrong time for working families.
These lawsuits won’t change the climate – but they will raise costs.
When cities sue energy producers for billions, those costs don’t disappear; they are passed on to Colorado families in the form of higher energy prices and everyday expenses.
At a time when families are already stretched thin, we cannot afford policies that act as a backdoor energy tax.
Colorado needs an all-of-the-above energy strategy that protects our environment while ensuring families can count on reliable, affordable energy.
Colorado voters deserve transparency from the Democratic field for Attorney General.
Secretary of State Jena Griswold and every Democrat running statewide for Attorney General should clearly state whether they support using the courts to pursue these climate lawsuits.
Voters deserve to know exactly where they stand.
As Attorney General, I will focus on protecting Colorado consumers and defending the rule of law – not advancing national political agendas through our courts.” For more information about Michael Allen’s campaign please visit www.michaelallenforcolorado.com.
