NC HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES MEMBER JEFF ZENGER (R-FORSYTH) PROUDLY BACKS DANA CAUDILL JONES FOR NC SENATE
Jeff Zenger is a principled conservative serving House District 74 in the North Carolina House of Representatives.
He is a small business owner that has grown his business into a successful company that builds commercial and residential structures across the state.
Jeff is a strong believer and previously served as a missionary in the metro Baltimore area for many years.
Jeff is a devoted husband and has raised four wonderful children with his wife.
He has spent his life serving others and does an excellent job for constituents in House District 74.
