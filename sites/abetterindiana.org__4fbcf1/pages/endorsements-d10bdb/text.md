The Summit is a non-partisan statewide coalition that works to strengthen rural and small-town communities by bringing together local leaders, volunteers, and candidates committed to improving the lives of Hoosiers across Indiana.
Their work focuses on issues that matter to rural communities, including economic opportunity, healthcare access, housing, education, civic engagement, and building stronger local communities.
The Indiana Rural Summit believes rural Indiana deserves a voice, a choice, and a vote, and they support candidates who are committed to listening to their communities and working toward practical solutions that improve everyday life for Hoosiers .
Indiana Rural Summit MADVoters Indiana MADVoters Verified Politician (MVP) Jack Chance has been designated a MADVoters Verified Politician for the 2026 election.
MADVoters is a grassroots nonprofit founded by Hoosiers to strengthen civic engagement through advocacy, outreach, education, and voter resources.
The organization works to build a more informed, empowered, and engaged Indiana while promoting fairness, transparency, and accountability in state government.
The MVP designation is awarded to Indiana Statehouse candidates who demonstrate strong alignment with progressive policy goals and a proven commitment to community engagement, integrity, accountability, and transparency.
Candidates must earn a qualifying score in the MADVoters Voter Guide and complete an additional evaluation demonstrating their commitment to building grassroots power across Indiana.
