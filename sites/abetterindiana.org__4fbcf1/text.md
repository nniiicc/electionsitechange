Meet Jack
Born and raised a few miles down the road in Tipton and now living in Kokomo, Jack Chance grew up in a working-class family that taught him the value of perseverance and helping your neighbor.
His years serving low-income seniors and families in housing showed him just how broken Indiana’s systems are and how much better they could be.
Jack is running for State Representative so that everyday Hoosiers have a champion fighting for fairness, dignity, and opportunity.
Because A Better Indiana starts with all of us.
Building A Better Indiana
My priorities for our community:
- Housing Safe, stable, and affordable homes through the Fair Hoosier Housing Act by protecting renters, supporting good landlords, and strengthening communities.
- Hoosier Jobs First A plan that puts Indiana workers at the front of the line by supporting unions, apprenticeships, manufacturing, and small businesses.
- We the People Our government begins with the words We the People for a reason.
Representation does not end on Election Day.
Public comment is a right, not a privilege.
Hoosiers deserve to be heard before decisions are made.
- Lower Utility Costs Abolishing the bureaucratic and billionaire-loyal Indiana Utility Regulatory Commission (IURC) and ensuring that every rate increase is approved by voters at the ballot box.
- Healthcare The Better Indiana Health Plan lowers costs, protects rural hospitals, and ensures no Hoosier is one illness away from financial ruin.
- Education Our Indiana Constitution is clear: “No money shall be drawn from the treasury for the benefit of any religious or theological institution.” Yet school vouchers siphon public dollars into private religious schools.
It’s time to end the fraud that is School Choice Vouchers.
What A Better Indiana Looks Like
A Better Indiana looks like a place where working people can get ahead, seniors can age with dignity, and families can afford a safe home without fear of sudden rent hikes or unexpected bills.
It’s an Indiana where the rules are fair, the roads are safe, and no one has to choose between medicine and groceries.
It’s a state that puts Hoosiers first — not corporations, not political insiders, not the well-connected.
A place where good jobs stay here, small towns aren’t forgotten, and every kid has a shot at a better future no matter their ZIP code.
A Better Indiana is built on the same values many of us were raised with: help your neighbor, do what’s right, show up when it counts.
That’s the Indiana I believe in and it’s the one we can build, together.
