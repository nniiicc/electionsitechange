September 14, 2026 FRANCESTOWN, N.H.
Tim Coffin, an Independent candidate seeking to represent Deering and Francestown in Concord, today announced that he will respectfully decline the Democratic Party’s nomination resulting from write-in votes cast during the primary.
“I am sincerely grateful to everyone who took the time to write in my name,” Coffin said.
“Their confidence and encouragement mean a great deal to me.
I entered this race as an Independent because I believe the people of Deering and Francestown deserve representation focused on their needs rather than party interests.
To remain faithful to that commitment, I will continue as an Independent.”
Coffin will continue his campaign as an Independent through the November general election.
Drawing on four decades of experience in municipal finance, he is emphasizing careful stewardship, practical judgment, and financially sustainable policies for both communities.
“I welcome the support of Republicans, Democrats, and Independents alike,” Coffin said.
“I would be honored to earn your vote in November and to serve Deering and Francestown with integrity, respect, and sound financial judgment.”

DEERING/FRANCESTOWN, NH — Tim Coffin, independent candidate for New Hampshire State Representative in District 27, has submitted signatures well above the required threshold to qualify for the November ballot.

Francestown, New Hampshire — June 4, 2026 Tim Coffin today announced his candidacy for New Hampshire State Representative for Hillsborough District 27, representing Deering and Francestown.
Running as an Independent, Coffin is launching his campaign with a simple message: common-sense leadership, common ground, and practical solutions that put local communities first.
"I’m running because our communities deserve thoughtful, forward-looking representation that focuses on results instead of partisanship," said Coffin.
"The people of Deering and Francestown want honest leadership, careful stewardship, and decisions that are genuine, sustainable, and fair." Coffin brings more than 35 years of experience in the municipal finance markets, where he has worked with local governments, advisors, and investors to help finance essential public projects.
His professional background includes support for investments in public schools, clean water, health care, and transportation—experience he says has given him a practical understanding of how public policy affects everyday life.
His campaign will focus on protecting the quality of life in Deering and Francestown, supporting responsible fiscal decisions, strengthening local infrastructure and public services, and encouraging a more respectful, solutions-oriented political culture in Concord.
Coffin says he will work to ensure residents have an independent voice who listens carefully, weighs issues thoughtfully, and serves the district with integrity.
"This campaign is about bringing people together around practical ideas and a shared commitment to our towns," Coffin said.
