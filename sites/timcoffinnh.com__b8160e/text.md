I'm committed to showing up and listening to you about the issues that genuinely matter and working hard to faithfully represent our district in the State House.
Let's work as a community to make sensible decisions that are prudent and forward looking.
I have close to 4 decades of experience in municipal finance , partnering with public officials and investors across America to get needed projects built and paid for - public schools, road & bridges, clean water, etc.
That experience shapes my vision for what financially-sound communities look like, and how we best make life affordable now & in the future.
My family and I believe it's a privilege to live in this beautiful area and truly appreciate the service of those who helped make it what it is today.
The communities we enjoy didn't happen by accident, they were built by generations of town residents, employees and volunteers.
It's in that spirit that I would welcome the opportunity to serve as your State Representative.
Thank you.
