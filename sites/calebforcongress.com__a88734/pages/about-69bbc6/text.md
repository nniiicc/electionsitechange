I’m proud to call Louisiana home.
My life and career have been built on service, leadership, and a deep commitment to community.
I served our country for 10 years in the United States military, rising to the rank of Sergeant First Class (E7).
During my time in uniform, I deployed to Afghanistan, lived in Alaska, and led teams across the country in a wide range of missions.
Those experiences shaped my leadership style—grounded in discipline, accountability, and the ability to bring people together to accomplish a common goal.
Through advanced military training and real-world leadership challenges, I learned how to make decisions under pressure and deliver results.
After my military service, I continued leading in the private sector as a department manager at Sam’s Club, where I was responsible for managing operations, developing teams, and serving the needs of my local community every day.
At my core, I believe in community.
I believe in showing up, listening, and working with people from all walks of life to solve real problems.
My leadership has always been about unity, respect, and getting things done—not for recognition, but because it matters.
I’m running to bring that same commitment to service and leadership to Louisiana—focused on strengthening our communities, supporting working families, and building a future we can all be proud of.
