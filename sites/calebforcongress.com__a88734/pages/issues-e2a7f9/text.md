PLATFORM ISSUES & PLATFORM Caleb Walker is running to put Louisiana families first with a focus on affordability, stronger communities, and a future our children can believe in.
PRIORITY ISSUE Lower the Cost of Living Groceries, insurance, housing, and everyday costs are squeezing Louisiana families.
Caleb will fight for working people who are doing everything right and still falling behind.
1 Groceries & Everyday Costs Affordability Affordability means ensuring Louisiana families can earn enough to keep up with today’s economy.
Caleb will fight to lower the cost of groceries, housing, insurance, healthcare, and other everyday necessities while supporting better wages that allow hardworking people to provide for their families, build savings, and get ahead.
Caleb will fight to lower the cost of groceries and the everyday necessities Louisiana families rely on.
2 Insurance & Housing Relief Caleb will work to make insurance and housing more affordable for families across Louisiana’s 3rd District.
3 Relief for Working Families Working families who are doing everything right should be able to get ahead—not merely survive.
4 Pay That Keep Pace With Economy Caleb will fight for wages and pay raises that keep pace with today’s economy, so hardworking people can afford rising costs and build financial security.
Taking Care of Those Who Take Care of Us Teachers, healthcare professionals, and essential workers strengthen our communities every day.
Caleb will fight for the pay, protections, and opportunities they deserve.
PRIORITY ISSUE Invest in People Louisiana depends on the people who educate our children, care for our families, and serve during emergencies.
It is time we invest in those who invest in us.
1 Teacher Pay & Tax Relief Caleb will fight to increase teacher pay and make the first $50,000 of every educator’s salary exempt from federal income tax.
2 Healthcare Worker Student-Loan Forgiveness Caleb will provide student-loan forgiveness for healthcare workers who serve Louisiana families and strengthen access to care.
Seniors, Families & Children 3 Hazardous-Duty Pay for Essential Workers Caleb will secure hazardous-duty pay for essential workers required to serve during declared natural disasters.
Protecting Louisiana's Future Caleb will defend our coastline, create good-paying jobs, and demand a transparent government that answers to the people—not special interests.
PRIORITY ISSUE Building a Stronger Louisiana Louisiana’s future depends on protecting our communities, growing local opportunities, and preserving our coast for the generations who will call it home.
1 Defend Louisiana's Coastline Caleb will fight for coastal restoration, stronger flood protection, and storm-resilient infrastructure that safeguards our homes, businesses, seafood industry, and coastal communities.
2 Grow Good-Paying Jobs Caleb will support Louisiana’s energy, agriculture, maritime, healthcare, technology, and skilled-trade industries—creating careers that allow our people to build a future without leaving home.
3 Government Accountable to the People Caleb will demand transparency, responsible spending, accessible constituent services, and a government that answers to Louisiana families—not special interests or political insiders.
4 Climate Change & Responsible Carbon Capture Caleb will confront global warming with practical, science-based solutions while supporting responsible carbon-capture projects that reduce emissions, protect communities, create jobs, and preserve Louisiana’s energy leadership.
Putting Louisiana First Lowering costs, investing in people, and protecting Louisiana’s future are more than campaign promises—they are the foundation of a stronger 3rd District.
This campaign is about bringing government back to the people it serves.
Together, we can build a Louisiana where hard work is rewarded, families can afford to thrive, and every community has a voice in Washington.
