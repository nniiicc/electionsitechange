Property Taxes Keeping Idaho, Idaho Another big concern of residents is maintaining the Idaho quality of life.
First, we need to get a handle on the growth.
We need to invest in critical infrastructure like roads, bridges and water systems before it is too late and overloaded.
We also have to be preserving as much of our open space as possible.
We need to protect our public lands.
We have what the pioneers were looking for in a state.
Our outdoor recreation is one of the biggest benefits of living in Idaho.
As an outdoorsman, I believe in the motto of keeping public lands in public hands and we must defend our land at all cost.
The Outdoor Idaho way of life needs to be preserved for future generations.
Keeping Idaho, Idaho Education Growth in Idaho needs to pay the way for more schools to be built and teachers hired.
We are definitely behind, our classrooms are overcrowded.
We need to support parents and parents' rights when it comes to their children's education.
We need to support Career and Technical education.
CWI is a great example of one of our schools that offers a pathway to success to our high school students to be able to personalize their education based on their career interests and unique learning needs.
