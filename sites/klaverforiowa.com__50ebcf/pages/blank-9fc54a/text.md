Beau Klaver is a 6th generation Iowa farmer from rural Hamilton County seeking election for Iowa House of Representatives District 55 to serve the people of Hamilton, Franklin, NW Story County and SE Wright County.
Beau is a strong Christian who believes in faith, family and service.
All his decisions are guided by his Lord and Savior Jesus Christ.
Beau started his own business several years ago and knows what it takes to run a small business in Iowa.
He is very passionate about the future of Iowa for generations to come.
He believes in providing local healthy food options for Iowan's.
Beau knows that rural communities and agriculture are the foundation to our great state and will be an advocate for the communities he desires to serve.
