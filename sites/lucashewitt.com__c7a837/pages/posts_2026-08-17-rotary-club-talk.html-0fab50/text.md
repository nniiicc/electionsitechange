Talking With the Waterloo Rotary Club
On Monday, August 17, I had the privilege of speaking to the Waterloo Rotary Club at the Waterloo Convention Center - about 30 minutes on why I'm running for Iowa House District 61 without a party, how a no-party representative actually operates at the statehouse, and what I'd want the people of Waterloo to get out of it.
The questions afterward were exactly the kind I like: direct, practical, and skeptical in the right ways.
Cedar Valley Now's Scott Fenzloff was there and interviewed me after the talk.
