Hagans May 28 Written By Tracey Tackett “Tracey is a phenomenal advocate for Epilepsy Foundation Ohio.
She has hosted fundraisers in honor of her daughter, Morgan and donated funds to the foundation.
In addition, she has been apart of our Advocacy program as a Champion, recently going to D.C. to speak with key members of Congress about epilepsy and the many issues that stand before Congress concerning people living with epilepsy.
Tracey has also spoken to members of the Education Committee about the importance of Seizure Safe School Legislation.
Lastly, Tracey now sits on the Advisory Board for Epilepsy Foundation Ohio.
We are excited to have her and see the impact she will have throughout Ohio.” --Dr.
Crystal L.
