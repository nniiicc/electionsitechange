Endorsement: Julie Turner
“I have known Tracey Tackett for more than 20 years, dating back to her teen years at Shawnee High School.
Tracey is a well-rounded, civic minded member of the Clark County Community.
She has given boundless time and energy to better herself by attending the Clark County Leadership Academy and Wittenberg University to earn a degree in Organizational Leadership.
I have witnessed her innovation, dedication, flexibility, honesty and most importantly a desire to better her community.
With her background in non-profit leadership, business community relations and success in the small business arena; she has more than shown the community that she will infuse the Clark County team with a hands-on, pro-active style.
I have witnessed a good business sense and her ability to attentively listen to her friends and acquaintances.
Through good times and personal tragedy, Tracey has shown grace and perseverance.
My only wish is that I still lived in Clark County to cast my vote for Tracey Tackett, Clark County Commissioner.”
-Julie Turner, Treasurer, Clark-Shawnee Local Schools (retired), American Cancer Society Volunteer, Ohio Dept. of Education, Project Coordinator, Ohio Leadership Advisory Council
