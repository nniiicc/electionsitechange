Most that know her can identify her as a philanthropist, a business owner, a visionary and someone who cares and acts on causes to help better her community.
In a professional aspect, I first met Tracey in 2014 when I served as the Development Manager.
Tracey utilized her time, talent and interpersonal skills to raise public awareness through her events and marketing.
One example is where Tracey incorporated her passion for the arts that introduced a therapeutic art program.
The therapeutic art program grew every class because its creative outlet empowered individuals and their families to express their journey through art.
Tracey is continuously acquiring new skills to accomplish her goals.
She demonstrates a can-do attitude in philanthropy and in the classroom.
Her commitment to eternal learning received notable academic honors at her local university and acceptance to Master’s Leadership program at The Ohio State University.
For those reasons, I can attest that Tracey will excel in any avenue to where she sets her sights.
Her creativity, charisma and dedication make her a great asset to public service--- GO TRACEY! -Rebecca Cantrell, MA ABS, MPA, CNP Tracey Tackett Previous Previous Endorsement: Deacon Norman G.
