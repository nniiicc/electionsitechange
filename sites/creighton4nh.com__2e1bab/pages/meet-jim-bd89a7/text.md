Jim Creighton is a retired Army Colonel, and Tax Fighter running for the New Hampshire State Senate to help protect the New Hampshire Advantage and bring practical, common-sense leadership to Concord.
Jim and his wife, Lieutenant Colonel (Retired) Tamasine Wood-Creighton, moved to Antrim after more than 30 years of military service.
They chose New Hampshire because of its independent spirit, strong communities, natural beauty, and Live Free or Die values — and quickly knew they had found home.
During his Army career, Jim served in leadership positions around the world, including Afghanistan, Germany and Korea, retiring as a Colonel after more than three decades of service.
He led soldiers and large organizations in some of the world's most difficult environments, focusing on teamwork, accountability, and problem solving.
After retiring from the military, Jim founded Eagle Point Global Solutions, a leadership and consulting firm focused on helping organizations solve complex challenges and build stronger teams, and Ursa Major Northeast Guides.
Jim also serves as the Vice President of the National Association of the 10th Mountain Division which provides support for soldiers, veterans, families of the 10th Mountain Division as well as a U.S.
Delegate to the International Federation of Mountain Soldiers.
In New Hampshire, Jim has served as Supervisor of the Checklist in Antrim and volunteers with the Crotched Mountain Ski Patrol, American Legion, Veterans of Foreign Wars, and local youth programs.
He and Tamasine also help mentor students interested in attending West Point and serving their country.
Jim graduated from West Point and has taught and lectured on leadership and international affairs at colleges and universities across the country.
Now, Jim is running for State Senate to fight for lower taxes, stronger communities, support for veterans and law enforcement, and policies that keep New Hampshire affordable, safe, and free for future generations.
