Supervisor Jodi Schulteis Endorses Jim Piwowarczyk for Assembly, Praises His Fiscal Conservatism Posted June 7, 2024 by Jim Piwowarczyk in Endorsements , News , ticker Washington County Supervisor Jodi Schulteis has endorsed Jim Piwowarczyk for the 98 th Assembly District, praising his law enforcement background, entrepreneurship, and fiscal conservativism.
“I confidently recommend Jim Piwowarczyk for WI Assembly District 98.
He prioritizes public safety and conservative fiscal responsibility,” Supervisor Schulteis said.
Schulteis joins a growing list of top local officials who have endorsed Piwowarczyk, who is running as a Republican in the Aug.
13 election.
Among others, Piwowarczyk has been endorsed by Washington County Executive Josh Schoemann, District Attorney Mark Bensen, Sheriff Marty Schulteis, Waukesha County Sheriff Eric Severson, former Hartford Mayor and current Washington County Treasurer Scott Henke, state Rep.
Bob Donovan, and former Republican Attorney General candidate Eric Toney.
You can read more here: https://jimforwi.com/jim-piwowarczyks-endorsements/.
“Jim’s background of law enforcement and entrepreneurship sets him apart from the career politician.
He’s hardworking and kind with a spirit of integrity and sense of humor.
I’m honored to endorse Jim!” Jodi Schulteis added.
Jodi Schulteis is the Washington County supervisor for District 16.
She is chairperson of the Human Services Committee and a member of the Executive Committee.
“I am thrilled to receive the endorsement of an accomplished local leader like Supervisor Schulteis,” Piwowarczyk said.
“I am humbled by the strong support I am receiving from local officials in this race.
I will work hard to earn the support of district voters, who tell me that they are deeply concerned about the economy, border, education, election integrity, and the state of the country.” You can learn more about Piwowarczyk’s campaign at https://jimforwi.com/.
