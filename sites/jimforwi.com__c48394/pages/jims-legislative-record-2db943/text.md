Home Donation Events Contacts FAQ About Us Jim’s Legislative Record for the 98th District (2025-2026 Session) In my first term, I authored 20 bills, chaired the Speaker’s Task Force on Government Efficiency and Modernization, and served as vice chair of the Criminal Justice and Public Safety Committee.
We didn’t just talk about common-sense reforms.
We got them done.
Below is every bill I authored, coauthored or cosponsored during the 2025-26 legislative session, grouped by issue.
In the Wisconsin Legislature, Assembly members author or coauthor Assembly bills and cosponsor Senate bills, so most Senate bills I cosponsored are the Senate versions of Assembly bills listed here.
Click any bill number to read it on the Legislature’s website.
Vetoed by Governor Evers The Assembly passed bills requiring sheriffs to cooperate with ICE, banning gender-transition surgeries for minors, and reforming the University of Wisconsin.
Governor Evers vetoed all three.
These were common-sense measures that deserved to become law, and I’ll keep fighting for them.
