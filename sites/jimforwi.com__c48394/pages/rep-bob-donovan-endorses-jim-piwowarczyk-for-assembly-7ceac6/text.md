Rep.
Bob Donovan of Wisconsin’s 84th Assembly has endorsed Jim Piwowarczyk for Assembly.
Piwowarczyk, 53, the co-editor of Wisconsin Right Now conservative news site, is running for the Assembly District 98, which spans parts of Washington and Waukesha Counties.
A small business owner and former law enforcement officer, Piwowarczyk, of the Town of Erin, is running as a Republican.
Donovan said, “I’ve known Jim for many years.
He’s bright, intelligent, and blessed with common sense, which is sadly a rare commodity these days.
He will make a great member of the Wisconsin legislature, and I’m proud to endorse his candidacy.”
Piwowarczyk was previously endorsed by Washington County Executive Josh Schoemann, Washington County Treasurer Scott Henke, and Rep.
Barbara Dittrich.
The 98th district includes Hartford, Erin, Richfield, Lisbon, Merton, and Sussex.
It has no incumbent.
You can read more about Piwowarczyk’s campaign here.
On Tuesday, Donovan, R-Greenfield, announced that he is running for reelection to the state Assembly.
However, due to the redrawing of legislative maps, he is seeking re-election in the 61st District instead of the 84th District.
Piwowarczyk, who is supporting Donald Trump for president, is a lifelong conservative running on a platform of public safety, parents’ rights in schools, delivering tax cuts, and border security, among other issues.
