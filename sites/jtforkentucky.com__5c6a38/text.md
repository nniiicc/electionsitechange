Biography As a lifelong resident of Henderson County and native of Reed, J.T. has remained committed to community involvement.
He began his professional career at his alma mater, Henderson County High School, as an agriculture teacher and FFA advisor.
He currently serves as Assistant Principal of Career and Technical Education.
J.T. is a member of the Henderson Education Foundation, Henderson Chamber of Commerce Board, Henderson Republican Party Executive Committee, and Murray State Hutson School of Agriculture Alumni Board.
He is also a past participant in the Henderson Leadership Initiative.
Following graduation from Henderson County High School, he went on to receive a Bachelor’s in Agriculture Science and two Master's in Education from Murray State University where he also served as student body President and was recognized as Spring 2019 Outstanding Graduate.
Issues Facing Kentucky Education Government Spending Government Spending As a teacher, J.T. knows the challenges of our educational system.
First and foremost, school safety is among J.T.'s top educational priority.

He also understands the importance of limiting the weight of standardized testing and thinks the government should not over-regulate schools and teachers.
