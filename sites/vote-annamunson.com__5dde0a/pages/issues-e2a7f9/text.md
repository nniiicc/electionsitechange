Issues Some of Anna's Priorities ​Bring Down the Cost of Living Oregon has become increasingly unaffordable for seniors, working families, and small business owners. ​ As a Salem resident, Anna will fight to lower taxes and fees while opposing any effort to raise the state gas tax.
Prioritize Education Oregon’s education system is not delivering the results students and families deserve.
Academic performance in reading and math ranks near the bottom nationally, and recent policy decisions have lowered standards rather than raising expectations. ​ As a mother and grandmother, Anna will prioritize students and academic standards to ensure every child has a fighting chance at success. ​ Make Housing More Affordable As the wife of a disabled Vietnam Veteran, Anna understands how the cost of housing strains the budgets of everyday Oregonians just like her.
Anna will fight to lower the cost of housing by increase funding for affordable housing, removing barriers to middle housing, and prioritizing infrastructure that will support new housing in our community.
Public Safety and Community Stability Communities across Oregon are facing challenges related to homelessness, drug addiction, and public safety.
These issues affect quality of life, economic stability, and public trust. ​ It’s time for compassionate and real solutions to crime and homelessness.
Anna will join with folks from across the aisle to find the community-based solutions we need to keep our Communities Safe Hold Data Centers Accountable Data centers cannot be allowed to drive up our cost of living and destroy our environment.
Anna will fight to hold data centers accountable and vote to ban FLOCK cameras and AI surveillance.
