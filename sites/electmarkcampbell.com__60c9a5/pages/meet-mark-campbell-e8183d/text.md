Meet Mark Campbell
Mark Campbell has dedicated nearly four decades to serving the people of Huber Heights.
First elected to Huber Heights City Council in 1986, Mark has served continuously since 1990.
His fellow councilmembers have selected him to serve as Vice Mayor five times, most recently in 2025.
Throughout his time in public service, Mark has earned a reputation as a steady, experienced leader who listens, brings people together, and focuses on getting results.
Mark also understands what it takes to run a business, create jobs, meet a payroll, and make responsible financial decisions.
He currently serves as a Performance Manager for Gerber Collision & Glass.
Before joining Gerber, Mark spent nearly 40 years as President of Wayside Collision Centers.
A graduate of Wayne High School, Mark has remained deeply involved in the community he calls home.
His service has included involvement with the Miami Valley Military Affairs Association, the City Hall Building Committee, the YMCA Fundraising Campaign Committee, and the Huber Heights Charter Review Commission.
Mark is a devoted Christian, a husband of nearly 50 years, and the proud father of five adult children.
He is running for State Representative because the Miami Valley deserves a proven leader who understands our communities and knows how to turn good ideas into real results.
