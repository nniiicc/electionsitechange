Mark Campbell’s Priorities
Families across the Miami Valley are being squeezed by rising property taxes, grocery bills, utility rates, healthcare expenses, and housing costs.
Mark believes state government must tighten its own belt before asking working families to sacrifice more.
At the Statehouse, he will fight to cut unnecessary spending, provide meaningful tax relief, and make homeownership more affordable.
Ohio should be a place where families can work hard, get ahead, and build a secure future—not simply struggle to keep up.
Making Ohio More Affordable
Taxpayers deserve to know that their money is being spent responsibly.
Mark will bring a business-minded approach to state government by demanding accountability, investigating fraud, eliminating wasteful spending, and making government programs work more effectively.
He will work to ensure taxpayer dollars are directed toward essential services and the people who genuinely need assistance—not lost to bureaucracy, mismanagement, or abuse.
Ending Waste, Fraud, and Abuse
Mark understands firsthand how excessive regulations and government delays can make it harder for businesses to grow, create jobs, and serve their customers.
He will fight to eliminate unnecessary red tape, streamline state government, and make Ohio a better place to start and grow a business.
When local businesses succeed, they create jobs, strengthen communities, and provide greater opportunities for Ohio families.
Cutting Government Red Tape
Mark believes every child deserves the opportunity to receive an excellent education and build a successful future.
He will work to ensure state resources reach local classrooms, support students and teachers, and help communities address challenges such as addiction, mental health, and workforce readiness.
Our tax dollars should be focused on helping children learn and preparing them for life—not expanding bureaucracy.
