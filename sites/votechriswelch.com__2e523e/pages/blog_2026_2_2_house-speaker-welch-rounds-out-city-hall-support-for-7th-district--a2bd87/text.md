House Speaker Welch Rounds Out City Hall Support for 7th District State Committeeperson with Alderpersons Taylor, Rodriguez and Conway
CHICAGO — House Speaker Emanuel “Chris” Welch’s support for 7th District State Central Committee person continues to span the 7th with newly announced endorsements from Alderperson Jeanette Taylor (20th Ward), Alderman Mike Rodriguez (22nd Ward), and Alderman Bill Conway (34th Ward).
Aldermen Rodriguez and Alderman Conway also serve as Cook County Committeeman for their respective wards.
Welch says he is grateful and humbled by the growing number of supporters calling for him to represent them at the state party level.
“Every day I work to be supportive of colleagues and communities across the state,” said Welch.
“It’s a part of my ‘WHY’.
Such tremendous support is an honor.
In this moment, in particular, you see leaders standing together to guard our values and fight back against fascism, racism, and all of the other ‘isms’ that limit who we are and what we can become.
People across Illinois have spoken clearly: Our rights are inalienable—not subject to the whim of politicians.
Our future is ours to shape together—not something dictated to us by a would-be strongman.
There’s more work to be done.
That’s the work I hope to bring to my role as State Central Committeeperson.
I’m so proud to make that work my priority every day.”
Alderman Rodriguez said, “Speaker Welch has long been a tireless advocate for working families, fighting to expand opportunity, protect workers’ rights, and ensure every community has a fair shot at success.
His leadership, integrity, and deep commitment to public service are exactly what Illinois needs at this critical moment.”
An Illinois State Central Committeeperson (SCC) plays a crucial role in the governance and leadership of the Democratic Party at the state level.
Their primary function is to represent their congressional district within their party's State Central Committee, which serves as the party’s governing body in Illinois.
As Congressman Danny Davis prepares to retire, a growing and diverse group of leaders is now calling for Welch to represent them and the 7th District on the Democratic Party of Illinois’ State Central Committee.
