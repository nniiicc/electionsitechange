LEARN MORE ABOUT STEPHANIE Why Support Stephanie?
Smart Budgeting for a Brighter Future Stephanie believes Vermont needs to make better use of the money it already collects.
Her approach emphasizes fiscal responsibility, protecting essential services, and examining spending carefully before asking taxpayers for more.
Listening to Your Concerns Good representation starts with listening.
Stephanie wants to hear directly from the people of Chittenden 24 about the issues affecting our families, businesses, schools, and communities, and bring those concerns with her to Montpelier.
Innovative Approaches to Community Issues Not every problem requires a bigger program or a bigger budget.
Stephanie believes government should look for practical solutions, measure whether they work, and consider their cost to taxpayers before committing additional public resources.
Empowering Local Voices Vermonters deserve to know how decisions are made and how their tax dollars are being spent.
Stephanie supports greater transparency, open communication, and giving residents a meaningful opportunity to participate in decisions that affect their communities.
