Investing in Our Children's Future
The Problem
Every child in Michigan deserves a world-class education — whether their path leads to a university or a union hall.
The Plan
Everyone, regardless of background, deserves access to high-quality education in their own backyard that prepares them for a rapidly changing world.
We can get there together by fostering strong relationships between teachers, family and administration and investing in early childhood and Pre-K funding.
Push for a statewide required reading curriculum
Reverse the post-2020 decline in reading and math scores
Invest in early childhood and Pre-K funding
Strengthen trades and workforce development pathways
Foster collaboration between teachers, families, and administration
Make Michigan a top-tier education destination
The Impact
This plan directly benefits families across all three communities — NW Detroit, Oak Park, and Royal Oak Township.
Every family deserves to thrive, not just survive.
