Mike Reilly received the endorsement of the Uniformed Firefighters Association.
Mike stated “I am extremely grateful to the men and women of the Uniformed Firefighters Association for their support.
Firefighters risk their lives in service to our community each and every day.
We need to support them and ensure they have the resources they need!”
“Thank you to President Gerard Fitzgerald, Staten Island Trustee Eric Bischoff, Firemarshall Representative Steven Tagliani and the Firefighters who joined us earlier today outside Engine Company 164 – Hook & Ladder 84 on Drumgoole Rd.”
