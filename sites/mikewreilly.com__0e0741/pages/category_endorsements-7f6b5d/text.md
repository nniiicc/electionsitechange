Mike Reilly Receives Endorsement of United Federation of Teachers
Mike Reilly stated: “I am extremely proud to announce the United Federation of Teachers (UFT) & New York State United Teachers (NYSUT) have endorsed my candidacy for the South Shore State Assembly.
I am truly thankful to have the support of our teachers.
I will continue to advocate and fight for the resources they need to support our students, families and school communities!”

Reilly Racks Up Endorsements in Bid for the 62nd Assembly District
The Staten Island Republican, Conservative and Independence Parties have endorsed Michael Reilly as their candidate for the 62nd Assembly District.
The parties join NYC Councilman Joseph Borelli and Minority Leader Steven Matteo in supporting Reilly for the South Shore Assembly seat.
The Assembly seat is currently held by Assemblyman Ronald Casterina, who is a candidate for Surrogate Judge.
