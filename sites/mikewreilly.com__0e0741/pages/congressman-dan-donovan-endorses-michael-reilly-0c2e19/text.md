Congressman Dan Donovan endorsed Michael Reilly in his bid to become the next Assembly Member from the 62nd Assembly District.
Reilly is running for the seat that is being vacated by current Assemblyman Ron Castorina who is running for Surrogate Judge.
“When you think of the definition of public service, you think of Mike Reilly,” said Congressman Dan Donovan.
Throughout his life, Mike has been working to make Staten Island a better place, and he’s exactly the kind of effective champion we need in Albany.
I’m proud to endorse him, and I look forward to partnering with him on important issues like transportation and infrastructure.
Reilly said, “I am extremely grateful for Congressman Donovan’s kind words and strong support.
Dan has a lengthy track record of working hard for Staten Island and I look forward to partnering with him, to build on that success for our Staten Island community.
