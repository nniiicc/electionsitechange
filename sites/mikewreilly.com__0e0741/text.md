STAY UPDATED ON MICHAEL REILLY VOLUNTEER DONATE Meet Michael Reilly Michael, his wife Mary and their 3 children live in Eltingville.
Mike is a retired New York City Police Lieutenant and also served in the United States Army Reserves.
Mike has been a tremendous advocate for the entire Staten Island community.
He has a knack for building coalitions and bringing people together to develop common sense solutions to community problems.

Below are some of the things we have accomplished to improve our Staten Island community through advocacy.
01 Fought to restore yellow school bus service for our 7th and 8th graders, writing bi-partisan legislation.
LEARN MORE 02 Worked alongside Councilman Ignizio and his staff to advocate for NYC DOE to invest $700m to replace hazardous PCB light fixtures in NYC Schools, expediting Staten Island.
LEARN MORE 03 Developed a Public Address/Fire Alarm System Replacement Program, improving emergency communications for 1400 school buildings across NYC.
LEARN MORE 04 Developed a Comprehensive Safety and Security Plan for New York schools, which included locking all doors, adding buzzer entry systems, cameras, panic alarms, and more.
LEARN MORE 05 Partnered with the NYPD Highway Unit’s Drug Recognition Coordinator to develop a Citywide Drug Driving Awareness Training for Patrol Officers.
LEARN MORE 06 Developed NYC DOT “Slow Zones” to improve traffic safety in our communities.
LEARN MORE 07 Introduced legislation with the NYS Senate and Assembly to prohibit U-Turns in school zones.
