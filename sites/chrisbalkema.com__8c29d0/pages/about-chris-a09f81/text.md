About Chris
Chris Balkema has been steadfastly committed to serving his community—a passion demonstrated throughout his 30-year career at Caterpillar and his 13 years on the Grundy County Board, including six years as Chairman.
That unique combination of private sector experience and proven local government leadership now guides his work as the elected State Senator for Illinois’ 53rd District.
Chris brings a results-driven, commonsense approach to Springfield—rooted in his strong conservative values and a deep understanding of the challenges facing families, farmers, and small businesses across Central Illinois.
As Board Chairman, Chris led efforts that resulted in four consecutive years of property tax levy reductions—proof that with discipline and focus, government can live within its means.
He has since dedicated his energy to serving his community at the state level.
With both of his sons now in college, Chris and his wife are embracing the “empty nest” phase of life—opening the door for Chris to expand his public service and make an even greater impact.
He remains committed to listening to his constituents, earning their trust, and delivering real results for the 53rd District.
What Chris is Fighting For
Pro-Life
Chris is pro-life and will work hard to protect the unborn as well as life at all stages.
2nd Amendment
Chris is a staunch supporter of the Second Amendment and believes that the cumbersome FOID card system must be reformed.
Property Tax
Property tax reform is absolutely critical – Chris will advocate for new funding mechanisms to relieve the crushing financial burdens that Illinois residents are currently under.
Energy
Illinois has the resources to be an energy exporter – Chris will champion for energy sources that deliver results – not just wind and solar.
