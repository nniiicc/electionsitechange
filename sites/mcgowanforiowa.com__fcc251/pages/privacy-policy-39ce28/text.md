Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 6/25/25
McGowan for Iowa (“we,” “our,” or “the Campaign”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information when you use our website:
- Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address, occupation, and employer (if you make a donation).
- Donation Information: Credit/debit card data and billing details (processed securely via our payment provider).
- Automatically Collected Data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and usage patterns via cookies and analytics tools.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data
If you opt in to receive SMS/text messages from the Campaign, we collect your phone number and related preference data.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Process donations and comply with campaign finance laws.
- Communicate with you about campaign news, events, and fundraising.
- Respond to your inquiries or requests.
- Improve our website and digital outreach.
Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share your data with:
- Trusted service providers who help us operate the website and campaign (e.g., email platforms, donation processors).
- Legal authorities or regulators (e.g., the Federal Election Commission), as required by law.
- Partners or consultants working with the campaign, under confidentiality agreements.
Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
Your Choices
You can:
- Opt out of email communications by clicking “unsubscribe” in our messages.
- Contact us to access, update, or delete your personal information.
Children’s Privacy
Our site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.
Updates to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time.
The “Effective Date” above will reflect the most recent changes.
