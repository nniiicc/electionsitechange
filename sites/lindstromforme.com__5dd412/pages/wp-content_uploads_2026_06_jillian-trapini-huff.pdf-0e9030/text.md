She isn’t interested in political theater or partisan talking points.
She’s interested in solving problems.
In a time when it feels like politics is designed to divide us, Shannon has a remarkable ability to bring people together, focus on the facts, and keep the conversation centered on what actually matters to families and communities.
Scarborough needs a strong voice in Augusta.
We need someone who understands our community, knows how government works, and can hit the ground running on day one.
Mary Shannon’s background in public policy, combined with her years of local leadership, gives her that rare combination of vision and practical experience.
She understands that while national politics may dominate the news, it is state and local decisions that shape our schools, housing, taxes, public services, and quality of life every single day.
I’ve watched Mary Shannon do the hard work of leadership.
She takes the time to understand issues deeply, values process, respects differing viewpoints, and never loses sight of the people affected by the decisions being made.
That kind of leadership is increasingly rare.

Scarborough and Maine would be well served by Mary Shannon Lindstrom.
