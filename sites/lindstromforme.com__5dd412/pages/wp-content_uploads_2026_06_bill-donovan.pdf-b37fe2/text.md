For the last nearly two years I have been the Scarborough Town Council’s representative to the Scarborough School District.
This has been a very significant time for Scarbrough Schools as efforts have been proceeding to undertake long overdue upgrades to several of our schools.
Being the Council’s rep has allowed me to closely evaluate the School Board’s leadership in advancing these initiatives.
Asking a community to support a multi-million dollar undertaking is no mean feat.
What I have observed is Mary Shannon Lindstrom, chairman of the School Board, show remarkable skill in presenting a clear picture to Scarborough residents of the need for the upgrades.
In meeting after meeting I had the opportunity to observe Mary Shannon resolve problems and advance this important project.
That experience leaves me totally confident that Mary Shannon has the judgement, the values, and leadership skills to represent Scarborough in Augusta.
Bill Donovan
