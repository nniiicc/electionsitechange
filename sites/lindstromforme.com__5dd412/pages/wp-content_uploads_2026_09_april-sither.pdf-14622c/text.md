I am writing to encourage my fellow Scarborough residents to support Mary Shannon Lindstrom for State Representative.
Now more than ever, we need collaborative, thoughtful leadership in Augusta.
Specifically, I am looking for leadership that understands the unique needs of our community and knows how state-level decisions affect the people who live here, as well as the challenges those decisions can sometimes bring to our town elected officials.
I had the privilege of serving with Shannon on the School Board for a year, and then worked alongside her as I transitioned to the Town Council while she continued her service on the Board.
Throughout that time, I saw firsthand her willingness to listen, work collaboratively, and find common ground.
Her years of public service have given her a deep understanding of the needs of Scarborough residents and the challenges municipalities face when navigating decisions made at the state level.
Just as importantly, Shannon has spent years building relationships across our community.
I am confident those relationships, along with her experience and thoughtful approach to public service, will be an enormous asset to Scarborough in Augusta.
I am eager to support a candidate who will bring a strong local perspective, a collaborative spirit, and a genuine commitment to serving our community.
I hope you will join me in supporting Mary Shannon Lindstrom for State Representative.
April Sither Scarborough resident
