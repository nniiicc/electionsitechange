It is my pleasure to offer my strongest recommendation for Shannon Lindstrom, the Chair of the Scarborough School Board as she seeks election to the Maine State Legislature.
During her tenure as School Board Chair, Shannon has demonstrated exceptional leadership, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to serving the people of Scarborough.
She has consistently approached every decision with thoughtfulness, respect for differing viewpoints, and a focus on what is best for students, families, educators, and taxpayers alike.
Her ability to bring people together, listen carefully, and build consensus has earned the trust and respect of colleagues and community members.
Whether addressing complex budget issues, long-term planning, or challenging policy decisions, she has shown sound judgment, transparency, and a willingness to make difficult decisions with fairness and accountability.
Beyond her leadership skills, she is deeply committed to public service.
Shannon understands that effective government requires collaboration, careful stewardship of public resources, and a genuine desire to improve the lives of the people she represents.
These are qualities that will serve her—and the citizens of Maine— exceptionally well in the State Legislature.
I am confident she will bring the same dedication, professionalism, and common-sense leadership to Augusta that she has brought to the Scarborough School Board.
Shannon will be a thoughtful, accessible, and effective advocate for her constituents.
I wholeheartedly endorse Shannon Lindstrom’s candidacy for the Maine State Legislature and encourage voters to support her.
Maine will benefit from her experience, character, and commitment to public service.
Sincerely, John JP Kelleher III Scarborough School Board Member
