Scarborough and Gorham voters are fortunate to have Sophie Warren and Mary Shannon Lindstrom on the ballot.
Sophie Warren is running for State Senator in District 30.
She is a three term State Representative whose focus includes civil rights, food insecurity, environment, pensions, energy and so much more.
Mary Shannon Lindstrom is running as an Independent for State Representative in District 124.
She has proven her leadership, competence, and dedication during her years on the School Board and as Chairperson of the School Board.
These two women have accomplished so much in their positions.
In addition to a candidate’s accomplishments, I vote for people with deep seated qualities of integrity, humility, intelligence, dedication, empathy, humor, and compromise.
Sophie and Mary Shannon have these qualities “in spades”.
Scarborough and Gorham, please vote for Sophie Warren and Mary Shannon Lindstrom.
We need these two women in our State Governance!
Nina McKee Scarborough resident
