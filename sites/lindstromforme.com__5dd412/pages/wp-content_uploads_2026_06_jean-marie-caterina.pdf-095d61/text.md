May 30, 2026 It is my honor to enthusiastically endorse Mary Shannon Lindstrom for State Representative in District 124, Scarborough.
I first met Mary Shannon several years ago in her role as Chairperson of the Scarborough School Board.
I have always been impressed by her ability to work cooperatively with all parties and bring about solutions that are in the best interest of her constituents.
She is an independent thinker with great integrity, empathy, and knowledge of what it takes to move all parties to win-win solutions.
This is what is needed in Augusta.
Please join me in supporting Mary Shannon for this position.
She will be great for Scarborough…and Maine!
Jean-Marie Caterina Scarborough, Maine Former, Scarborough Town Councilor Current, Cumberland County Commissioner
