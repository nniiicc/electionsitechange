Support Mary Shannon Lindstrom this November!
I’ve had the privilege of working closely with Mary Shannon Lindstrom over the past few years.
During my term as the Chair of the Scarborough Town Council, we were on the heels of a new school referendum defeat.
Shannon was serving as Chair of the Scarborough Board of Education, and we immediately got to work on a new process in pursuit of a solution that the community could embrace.
During that time, I was able to see the passion that drives Shannon, and it comes from a deep connection to our community members.
She understood fully the impact our decisions have on our seniors, business leaders, parents and children alike.
She has a genuine interest in the opinions of all around her.
She was an integral part of the team that was able to put aside personal preferences and help implement a process that regained the faith of our community members in our local government.
With those tireless efforts and with the help of our passionate community members, we were able to successfully pass a new school bond last year.
Shannon personifies all that we would want in a political leader.
She is caring, thoughtful, engaged and driven.
I have full confidence that Scarborough will be best represented in Augusta with Shannon representing District 124.
Nick McGee Scarborough
