To the Editor, As our upcoming election approaches, I am writing to express my enthusiastic support for Mary Shannon Lindstrom for Maine State house Representative District 124.
In a time when finding true leadership can feel challenging, Shannon stands out as a person of exceptional character, dedication, and proven service to our community.
I have had the privilege of watching from the sidelines as she has served our town for years, most notably through her impactful tenure on the school board and her tireless work on various volunteer committees.
In every role she takes on, she proves herself to be remarkably hardworking.
She doesn't just show up to meetings; she does the heavy lifting, thoroughly researches the issues, and listens intently to the concerns of residents.
Shannon is a highly ethical individual who treats every person with respect and approaches governance with complete transparency.
With her, what you see is what you get.
Her authenticity in both her words and her actions is a breath of fresh air.
She makes promises she intends to keep, and she never compromises her values for political expediency.
Our community needs a leader in the legislature who understands our local values, possesses a deep-rooted commitment to public service, and commands the work ethic to deliver real results.
I urge my fellow neighbors to join me in voting for Mary Shannon Lindstrom this November.
We deserve a representative who works hard, acts ethically, and speaks with genuine authenticity.
Sincerely, Katy Foley Scarborough, Maine
