I am proud to support Mary Shannon Lindstrom for State House Representative, District 124.
Over the past six years, Mary Shannon has served our community on the Scarborough School Board, including as Chair.
As a town councilor, I have had the opportunity to collaborate closely with her and have seen firsthand her thoughtful, steady approach to leadership.
She values collaboration and has worked consistently to strengthen the partnership between the School Board and the Town Council — an important connection for Scarborough residents.
One example of her leadership stands out in particular to me.
When the initial school project proposal failed, Mary Shannon did not walk away from the challenge.
Instead, she took the time to listen to the residents’ concerns, refine the proposal, and help lead it to approval.
That kind of persistence and responsiveness is exactly what we should expect from our elected leaders.
Throughout her service, Mary Shannon has been a strong and consistent advocate for students.
She approaches decisions with a clear focus on what students need to succeed and is not afraid to speak up on their behalf.
That commitment to advocacy — listening carefully, speaking thoughtfully, and working persistently — is exactly what Scarborough needs in its representative.
I am confident she will bring that same dedication and willingness to advocate for our community to Augusta.
Mary Shannon Lindstrom will be an effective voice for Scarborough, and I encourage others to join me in supporting her.
