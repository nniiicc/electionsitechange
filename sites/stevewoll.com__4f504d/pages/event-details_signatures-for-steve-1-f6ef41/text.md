About the event
Before Steve Woll can appear on the November ballot, we need 1,500 signatures from registered voters in Virginia's 3rd Congressional District.
Every signature brings us one step closer to giving Hampton Roads a real choice in 2026.
Tabling events are some of the most important - and most fun - volunteer activities of the campaign.
You'll be stationed at a high-traffic location in the district, talking to neighbors about why this race matters and asking registered voters to sign Steve's petition.
What to expect:
• A short briefing on the ballot access process and how to talk to voters
• All materials provided - table, clipboards, pens, and literature
• A team of fellow volunteers alongside you
• Steve may stop by to meet supporters in person
You don't need to be a political expert.
You just need to believe that voters in VA-03 deserve a name on the ballot that isn't controlled by either party.
Sign up for a shift at a location near you.
