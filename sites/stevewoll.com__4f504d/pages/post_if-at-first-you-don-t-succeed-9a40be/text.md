Steve Woll Apr 28 1 min read Fail, fail again.
The news today that the former FBI Director James Comey was indicted by a federal grand jury at the request of the Department of Justice could not be more ridiculous, and is bound for the same dust bin as DOJ's first indictment of Comey.
If it were not so harmful, it would be funny.
At a time when the Department of Justice has jettisoned or shed major portions of its professional legal staff, and those prosectors who are left are compelled by higher authorities to waste time on asinine prosecutions like this, who is looking at any actual law breaking or threats.
We are at war with a country that is known to have active cells in this and other countries, and the FBI and DOJ prosecutors are spending unknown hours looking at and investigating pictures of seashells ?!?!?
Whiskey Tango Foxtrot.
