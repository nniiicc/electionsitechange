Earlier today, Wednesday June 10th, President Trump announced that follow strikes against Iranian targets earlier this week, the U.S. will "hit 'em hard again today".

It is a level of irresponsibility that puts Signalgate to shame.

No matter where you stand on the Iran war, there can be no justification for this gross dereliction of duty by our nation's most senior leaders.

Let us hope that we're nearing that bottom, before thousands more Hampton Roads service members have to risk their lives for irresponsible leaders.
