About the event
The most powerful thing in politics is still a real conversation with a real neighbor.
Door-knocking is how campaigns are won - and for an independent campaign without party infrastructure, it matters even more.
Join us for canvassing in your precinct.
We'll train you, give you a turf map and a script, and send you out in pairs to talk to voters about Steve Woll and what he's running on.
What to expect:
• 30-minute training before you head out - no experience needed
• A canvassing app on your phone with your turf and voter list
• A script and talking points - you'll feel prepared
• Debrief and refreshments when you return
