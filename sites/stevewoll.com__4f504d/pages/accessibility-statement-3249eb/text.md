1.
Our Commitment Steve Woll and stevewoll.com are committed to ensuring that this Site is accessible to all visitors, including people with disabilities.
We believe that everyone deserves equal access to information and the ability to engage in the political process, regardless of ability.
2.
Accessibility Standard We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).
These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those with visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor impairments.
3.
Measures We Take To support accessibility, stevewoll.com aims to: Provide text alternatives for non-text content (images, icons, etc.) Ensure content can be navigated using a keyboard alone Use sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds Ensure forms and interactive elements are clearly labeled Make content compatible with screen readers and other assistive technologies Avoid content that flashes or strobes in ways that could trigger seizures Write in plain, clear language that is easy to understand 4.
Known Limitations We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of this Site.
Some content or features may not yet fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
If you encounter a barrier, please let us know (see Section 6) and we will make every effort to provide the information or functionality you need in an accessible format.
5.
Third-Party Content Some content on this Site is provided by third-party services (such as embedded videos, social media feeds, or analytics tools).
We cannot always control the accessibility of third-party content, but we encourage our partners to meet the same accessibility standards we hold ourselves to.
6.
Feedback and Contact We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of stevewoll.com.
If you experience any difficulty accessing content or have suggestions for improvement, please contact us: stevewoll.com - inbox@stevewoll.com We aim to respond to accessibility inquiries within 5 business days.
7.
Formal Complaints If you are not satisfied with our response to your accessibility concern, you may contact the relevant accessibility authority in your jurisdiction.
In the United States, this may include: U.S.
Department of Justice — www.ada.gov U.S.
Access Board — www.access-board.gov 8.
Updates to This Statement We review and update this Accessibility Statement periodically as we make improvements to the Site.
The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
This statement reflects our ongoing commitment to digital accessibility.
It does not constitute a legal guarantee of full WCAG compliance.
If you require assistance accessing any content on this Site, please reach out and we will do our best to help.
