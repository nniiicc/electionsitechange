Why Independent?
We are at a crossroads - a point where strong, ethical leadership and a clear, executable plan for success are needed, and neither Republicans nor Democrats are providing it.
Here in Hampton Roads, home to over 220,000 veterans who have volunteered to put their lives on the line for the country, our Congressional representatives are doing no better - they follow their party line through thick and thin, even when we the voters say we want something different, as we are so clearly saying now.
That ends now.
I am not running to take orders from a political party or partisan special interests.
I am running because the problems facing our country are substantial and demand answers that partisan politics cannot deliver.
Because our economy is often rigged against the people who build it.
Because our communities deserve better than to be torn apart by self-interested outsiders who benefit from divisiveness and polarization.
Neither party has the credibility or the will to fix any of it, but, along with Independent candidates running in dozens of Congressional districts across the country, that's all I care about- fixing our problems.
