Lower taxes and cut wasteful spending Valley families are struggling: groceries cost more, gas prices are up, rent keeps climbing.
As Mayor, Kevin balanced budgets and delivered for working families without raising taxes.
In Congress, he will work to cut wasteful government spending, lower taxes, and put more money back in the pockets of hardworking Central Valley families.
Backing the blue and restoring law and order While politicians in Washington and Sacramento defunded the police, Kevin Lincoln backed the blue, delivering an 18% pay raise and a $42 million budget increase to keep officers on the streets.
In Congress, he will fight soft-on-crime policies like early prisoner release and reduced sentences for repeat offenders that put our communities at risk.
Artificial intelligence The United States is locked in a defining competition with China and other rivals to dominate artificial intelligence, and America must win.
Kevin supports the President's AI Action Plan because it prioritizes American innovation and national security, removes burdensome regulations, builds out our domestic AI infrastructure, and creates high-paying jobs for working families.
The future of AI must be built in America, by Americans, and put to work growing our economy and keeping us ahead of the rest of the world.
Blockchain/crypto Kevin believes digital assets and blockchain technology represent the next frontier of American economic leadership, and Congress must create clear pathways for this industry to grow here rather than drive it offshore.
He supports the CLARITY Act and the GENIUS Act, opposes IRS broker rules that treat software developers like financial intermediaries, and believes Americans have the right to self-custody their digital assets without being punished for it.
America must set the rules for this revolution rather than cede the future to foreign competitors.
