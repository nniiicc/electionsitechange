Congressional District 13
The newly drawn 13th Congressional District spans five Central Valley counties: Stanislaus, Merced, San Joaquin, Madera, and Fresno.
The district includes all or parts of Merced, Modesto, Stockton, Madera, Ceres, Los Banos, Atwater, Turlock, Lathrop, Patterson, Livingston, Mendota, Newman, Firebaugh, Gustine, Dos Palos, Winton, and Garden Acres.
