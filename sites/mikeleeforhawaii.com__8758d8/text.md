Please explore my website and learn more about me – my roots, my ‘ohana, my priorities as your Representative, and my life experiences that have led me down this path.

About Mike Born and raised in Honolulu, Mike Lee is a respected educator, small business owner, community leader, and devoted family man.
Leading with integrity and compassion, he has extensive experience advancing community initiatives with government, local businesses, non-profits, and schools.
Mike understands the challenges facing residents in District 50.
His priorities in the State House will address the concerns of Kailua and Kāne‘ohe Bay neighbors like you.
