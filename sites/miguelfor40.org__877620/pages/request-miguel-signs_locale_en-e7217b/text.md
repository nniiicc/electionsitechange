It's election season and signs help with visibility.

Use this form to help us ensure you get a sign.
How?
- Preferred option - If you'd like to pick up a sign in person (and get to see the office or pick up other swag too), you can drop by the office Saturdays 10am-3pm
- Address: 3248 W Montrose
- If you'd like us to deliver signs to your home, we will be in touch as our volunteers have capacity to deliver.
Types of signs:
- Window signs - These are 11"x17" and double sided.
These are ideal for internal placement.
- Yard signs - These are corrugated plastic and can withstand the elements.
The size is 18"x24".
They can be staked in the yard or can also be hole punched and tied to a fence.
Note: these will be available in January but we would like to collect interest so we can deliver them when they are ready.
