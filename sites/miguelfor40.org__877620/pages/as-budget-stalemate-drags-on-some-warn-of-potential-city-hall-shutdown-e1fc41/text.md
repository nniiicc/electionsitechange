Dec 08, 2025
Originally published in Block Club Chicago.
Excerpt:
Activists with the Latino Union of Chicago, Raise the Floor Alliance and other groups held a press conference Monday at City Hall calling on the City Council to fund a $500,000 pilot program to establish a “safe hiring site” for day laborers next year.
The site would be a specific location that would offer a waiting area for workers to connect with possible employers while protecting them from scams as well as immigration law enforcement.
“It would provide a structured and dignified alternative to waiting on street corners, replacing fear and uncertainty with organization, opportunity and hope,” said Miguel Alvelo-Rivera, executive director of Latino Union of Chicago.
