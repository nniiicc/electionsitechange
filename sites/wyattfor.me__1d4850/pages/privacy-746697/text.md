Introduction Wyatt Beaulieu for Maine House 60 (“the Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information obtained through wyattfor.me, campaign forms, email communications, text messaging, donation pages, volunteer activities, events, and other campaign operations.
2.
Information We Collect We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, mailing address, telephone number, donation information, volunteer interests, event responses, survey responses, and other information you choose to submit.
3.
How We Use Information We may use information to communicate with supporters and voters; send campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer opportunities, advocacy and issue messages, surveys and polls, fundraising requests, and get-out-the-vote reminders; process donations; respond to inquiries; organize volunteers and events; improve the website and campaign operations; maintain records; comply with legal obligations; and protect the Campaign, its users, and its systems.
4.
Text Messaging When you voluntarily provide a mobile number and opt in to campaign text messages, we may send recurring automated or manually initiated messages relating to campaign updates, events, volunteer opportunities, advocacy and issue messages, surveys and polls, fundraising, and voting.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.
No mobile information will be shared by us with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
All categories described in this Privacy Policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
See also our Text Message Terms & Conditions .
5.
Sharing of Information We may share information with vendors and service providers that assist with website hosting, communications, data management, payment processing, compliance, analytics, event management, and other campaign functions.
These providers may use the information only as necessary to perform services for the Campaign or as required by law.
We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, court order, governmental request, campaign-finance reporting requirement, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud or abuse.
We do not sell personal information.
6.
Donations Donations may be processed by an outside payment processor or campaign-finance platform.
Payment information is handled by that provider according to its own privacy and security practices.
Campaign-finance laws may require the Campaign to collect and publicly report certain donor information, including name, address, occupation, employer, and contribution amount.
7.
Data Security and Retention We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information.
No website, transmission method, or storage system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Information may be retained as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, legal compliance, recordkeeping, dispute resolution, security, or other legitimate purposes.
8.
Your Choices You may unsubscribe from campaign emails by using the unsubscribe link provided in an email.
You may stop text messages by replying STOP.
You may contact the Campaign to request correction or deletion of information, subject to legal and campaign-finance recordkeeping requirements.
9.
Children’s Privacy The website and messaging program are not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
10.
External Links The website may link to third-party websites or services.
The Campaign is not responsible for the privacy, security, or content practices of those third parties.
11.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
