Bill McCaskill’s Priorities for North Carolina
Bill McCaskill isn’t a career politician—he’s a public servant who has spent his life helping others.
Now, he’s ready to bring that same dedication to Raleigh.
Fully Fund Public Schools in NC
North Carolina ranks near the bottom in per-pupil education spending.
Bill McCaskill will work to fully fund public schools, increase teacher pay, and ensure every student in Randolph County and across North Carolina has access to a high-quality education.
Key goals:
- Increase funding for North Carolina public schools
- Raise teacher salaries to competitive levels
- Ensure schools are fully staffed and resourced
Every child deserves a quality education, and every teacher deserves respect and support.
Lower Costs for Working Families in North Carolina
Families in Asheboro and throughout Randolph County are facing rising costs for groceries, housing, gas, and utilities.
Bill will support policies that grow the North Carolina economy while easing the financial burden on working families.
He agrees with Roy Cooper.
The people in power should work to make stuff cost less for everyone.
Bill’s focus:
- Attract new businesses and jobs to Randolph County
- Support economic growth across North Carolina
- Make everyday living more affordable for working families
A strong economy should work for all North Carolinians, not just a wealthy few.
Pass a Responsible North Carolina State Budget – On Time
In 2025, North Carolina was the only state to fail to pass a budget on time.
Bill McCaskill believes responsible leadership means delivering results.
Bill will:
- Work across party lines in the North Carolina Legislature
- Pass balanced, timely state budgets
- Prioritize teachers, law enforcement, and public service workers
Government should work efficiently, and deliver results people can count on.
