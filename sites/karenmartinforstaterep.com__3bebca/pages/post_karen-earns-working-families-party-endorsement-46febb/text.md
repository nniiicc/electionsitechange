Karen Earns Working Families Party Endorsement!
Karen Martin, candidate for State Representative District 99 representing East Haven,
has received the endorsement of the Working Families Party (WFP) and will appear on an additional line
for the upcoming election ballot.
Securing this additional ballot line provides voters with a clear opportunity to support Martin's vision for
working-class families.
The endorsement highlights a platform focused on economic relief, fair wages, and
policy initiatives designed to make everyday life more affordable for local families.
In addition to her focus on affordability, Martin is committed to ensuring East Haven receives its fair share
of state resources.
She will fight tirelessly to secure maximum state funding for local education and
infrastructure projects to relieve the burden on municipal taxpayers.
"I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Working Families Party and to carry their banner on the
ballot," said Martin.
"This additional ballot line gives our community a clear choice to vote for economic
fairness.
My priority is fighting for the people of East Haven.
That means making life more affordable,
ensuring we get our fair share of state funding for our schools and roads, and always voting to bring more
resources back home to our town."
The Working Families Party endorses candidates who demonstrate dedication to social, economic, and
environmental justice.
Martin’s alignment with the WFP reinforces her track record of advocating for
practical solutions that directly benefit working individuals and families.
As the campaign progresses, Martin remains focused on grassroots organizing, listening to voter concerns,
and building a coalition dedicated to bringing affordable change and strong state advocacy to East Haven.
