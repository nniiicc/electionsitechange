Rescheduled Grand Opening - East Haven DTC Headquarters!
Updated: 21 hours ago
Karen Martin invites East Haven residents to the grand opening of the East Haven Democratic Town Committee Headquarters on Sunday, October 4, at 11:00 a.m.
The celebration will take place at 370 Hemingway Avenue and will include visits from Congresswoman Rosa DeLauro and Connecticut State Treasurer Erick Russell, along with an opportunity to meet East Haven’s Democratic candidates.
The event will also serve as the official kickoff of the campaign’s community canvassing effort.
“I hope you’ll stop by, meet our candidates, and help us celebrate the opening of our campaign headquarters,” Karen said.
“This election is about the future of East Haven, and our campaign is powered by the energy and participation of people throughout our community.”
Event Details
Date: Sunday, October 4
Time: 11:00 a.m.
Location: 370 Hemingway Avenue, East Haven
Featuring: Congresswoman Rosa DeLauro, Connecticut State Treasurer Erick Russell, and East Haven’s Democratic candidates
Also: Community canvass kickoff
Karen looks forward to welcoming supporters, volunteers, and neighbors to the new headquarters as the campaign enters its final weeks.
Come meet the candidates, learn how you can get involved, and help us kick off the next phase of the campaign!
