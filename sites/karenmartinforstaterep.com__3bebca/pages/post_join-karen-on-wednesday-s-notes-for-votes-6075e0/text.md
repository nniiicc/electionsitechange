Campaign Staff #ago 1 min read Karen Martin invites friends, supporters, and neighbors to join her campaign every Wednesday evening for “Notes for Votes,” a community postcard-writing gathering at the East Haven Democratic Town Committee Headquarters.
Volunteers will write postcards to East Haven neighbors, helping Karen and East Haven’s Democratic candidates connect personally with voters throughout the community.
“Our campaign is built on conversations between neighbors,” Karen said.
“A handwritten postcard is a simple but meaningful way to reach someone, share why this election matters, and encourage them to make their voice heard.” Event Details When: Every Wednesday Time: 6:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m.
Location: East Haven DTC Headquarters Where: Stop & Shop Plaza, above SuperCuts Address: 370 Hemingway Avenue, East Haven Activity: Writing postcards to East Haven neighbors Enjoy some food and snacks, spend time with friends, and help the campaign reach more voters.
Whether you can stay for the entire event or stop in long enough to write a few postcards, every note (and every vote) makes a difference.
