Campaign Staff #ago 1 min read Karen encourages East Haven residents to make their voices heard during Connecticut’s 14-day Early Voting period, beginning Monday, October 19.
Early Voting provides residents with the flexibility to cast their ballots in person before Election Day—including Saturdays and Sundays.
East Haven voters may vote at Town Hall on any day that works best for their schedules.
“Voting should be accessible and convenient for every eligible resident,” Karen said.
“Whether you vote early or on Election Day, the important thing is to make a plan and make sure your voice is heard.” East Haven Early Voting Dates: Monday, October 19 through Sunday, November 1 Regular hours: 10:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. every day Extended hours: 8:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on Tuesday, October 27, and Thursday, October 29 Location: East Haven Town Hall Address: 250 Main Street, East Haven Entrance: Lower Level You do not need to wait until Election Day or vote at your usual neighborhood polling place during Early Voting.
Simply visit the East Haven Town Hall, lower level during the dates and hours listed above.
If you prefer to vote on Election Day, polls will be open on Tuesday, November 3, from 6:00 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. at your assigned polling location.
Choose the day that works best for you and cast your vote for Karen Martin for State Representative!
