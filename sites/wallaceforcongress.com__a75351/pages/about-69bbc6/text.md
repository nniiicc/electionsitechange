Rachel was born and raised in Pottsville, Pennsylvania, and is a proud graduate of Pottsville Area High School.
She has spent her career in public service.
She started out working for her Congressman, Tim Holden, and went on to serve in the U.S.
State Department, the U.S.
Senate, and the White House, where she was Chief of Staff for the Office of Management and Budget.
So she knows how the federal government works – and how it doesn’t.
