James Taylor
- James Taylor is a lifelong Republican who has been active in the Arizona GOP in a variety of roles, including as a Precinct Committeeman, State Committeeman, LD29 State Delegate, and LD29 District Treasurer.
He has also been active helping to elect Republicans up and down the ticket.
- In 2024 he was asked to run for office because he was known as a solid conservative with deep roots here in the West Valley.
He took up the challenge, got elected, and went to work, serving the voters of LD29.
- He is a husband, father, and grandfather, as well as a 2nd Amendment enthusiast.
He and his wife Renee are both active in their church and community.
