In the ad, Ford highlights his decades-long career in public service as a firefighter, an educator and Blaine City Councilmember.
Chris will bring this experience to the state legislature and is ready to do what matters most to him — putting people first to get things done.
Transcript: I’m Chris Ford running for State Senate.
I’ve dedicated my life to serving Minnesotans.
As a firefighter for 21 years, a paraprofessional at Blaine High School, and as a Blaine City Councilmember, I have seen firsthand the issues that face our state.
I know what’s at stake, and why we need leaders who will fight to lower your costs at every stage of life.
As your Senator, I’ll put people first to get things done.
ABOUT CHRIS FORD: Chris Ford has dedicated his life to being a public servant in Minnesota.
He served as a firefighter for 21 years, including 13 years as Captain in Rochester.
He works as a paraprofessional at Blaine High School, helping to educate our kids.
Chris served his community as a Park Board member and member of the Neighborhood Traffic Commission.
And now, as Blaine City Council Member, he is leading efforts to make our communities safer, healthier and more affordable and to restore accountability in government.
Chris Ford is running for State Senate to be our trusted public servant in Saint Paul.
Our community has trusted Chris Ford to protect us, to educate our kids and to represent our communities for decades.
Chris Ford has already delivered.
We can trust him to serve us and to deliver safer communities, lower costs, better schools and affordable health care.
