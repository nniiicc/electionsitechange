Patty García Organizer Strategist Coalition Builder The daughter of Mexican immigrants.
When institutions failed, Patty stepped in.
She has challenged abusive enforcement practices, pushed federal agencies to change course, and helped bring funding and services directly into communities under pressure.
While others offer talking points, Patty has delivered solutions rooted in lived experience—working across lines, confronting power when necessary, and producing results that people can actually feel in their daily lives.
That’s the kind of leadership she’ll bring to Congress.
A leader shaped by the people doing the work—not by political convenience or Washington talking points.

02 Helping tenants and homeowners Navigating rising costs and displacement.
03 Working with labor and community partners To secure resources for neighborhoods that have been locked out for too long.
Her leadership has focused on turning real needs into real outcomes—protecting family stability, keeping people housed, and making government respond to the people it’s supposed to serve.
