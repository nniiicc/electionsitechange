Happy 250th America!
Representatives and Senators from your Addison and Rutland County Republican delegations marched in the Brandon parade on Friday, July 3rd.
And on Saturday the Addison County Republicans marched in the Bristol 4th of July parade.
We were well received by the crowds lining the streets in both towns as together we celebrated the 250th anniversary of the signing of the American Declaration of Independence.
It was a great way to celebrate the victories we’ve won here in Vermont over the past two years and the 250th birthday of the greatest nation on Earth!
I was particularly happy to meet the young men volunteering for our Ferrisburgh Fire Department as they prepared to march in Bristol.
I’m so glad these young men are stepping up to engage and help our community.
Thank you for your service!
Happy 4th of July America!
