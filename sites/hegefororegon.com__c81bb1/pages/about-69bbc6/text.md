ABOUT ME I’m Scott Hege, and I’m running for Oregon House District 52.
I’ve lived in Wasco County since 1991 and have spent more than 30 years working, serving, and investing in our communities.
I’m proud of my 16 years as a Wasco County Commissioner, including serving as Chair, and of what we’ve accomplished by focusing on practical priorities—strengthening our economy, fully funding law enforcement, maintaining our roads, and delivering quality services while being responsible with taxpayer dollars.
Before serving on the County Commission, I was Executive Director of the Port of The Dalles, where I worked to create jobs and grow our local economy.
I hold a degree in mechanical engineering and a master’s in business administration.
I’m also a Level 2 Alpine ski instructor at Mt.
Hood Meadows, where I’ve enjoyed sharing my passion for skiing and winter sports for more than 13 years.
I also consult in the construction industry.
My wife, Betsy, and I have been married for more than 40 years.
We enjoy our children and grandchildren, and spending time together at our rural property in Wasco County.
I'm running for House District 52 because I believe we can make Oregon more affordable, safer and more prosperous—and leave our children and grandchildren a state we can be proud of.
Candidate for House District 52, which includes The Dalles, Hood River, Mt Hood, Corbett, and portions of Sandy and East Multnomah County.
