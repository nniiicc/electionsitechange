Make your plan to vote
There are several ways to cast your ballot in this year’s election.
We expect this to be a high-turnout election in which every vote matters, so please make your voice heard.
Thanks for being a voter!
Vote
by mail
Return your ballot in the mail.
To arrive by Election Day, the Secretary of State recommends mailing your ballot no later than Wednesday, September 2.
Or, drop your mail ballot in a secure dropbox anytime between now and Election Day.
Vote
Early In Person
In-person early voting has begun!
You can cast your ballot at an in-person early voting location during business hours from now through Tuesday, September 8.
Hours vary by location.
Click the button below to find the early voting location and hours for your city or town.
Vote
on Election Day
Election Day is Wednesday, September 9.
Polling locations are open from 7 a.m.* to 8 p.m.
Hours vary by location.
Click the button below to find the early voting location and hours for your city or town.
