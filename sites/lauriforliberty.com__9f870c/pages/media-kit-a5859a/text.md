She’s not running to climb a political ladder.

Lauri believes the Secretary of State’s office should be efficient, transparent, and neutral.
It oversees elections, supports businesses, and maintains critical public records.
At its core, it should operate as a service to the public.
Her campaign focuses on modernizing outdated systems, protecting the integrity of elections, and ensuring every Hoosier can access the services they need without frustration.
Shillings has been a consistent advocate for ethical leadership and civic engagement.
She is not running to advance a political career, but to do the job well.
Hoosiers deserve an office that works.
Shillings is running to make sure it does.
