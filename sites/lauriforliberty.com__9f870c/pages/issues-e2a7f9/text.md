Lauri Shillings On the Issues
Indiana’s Secretary of State should serve the people—not political parties.
I believe government should be efficient, fair, and focused on serving the people who fund it.
The Indiana Secretary of State's office oversees elections, registers businesses, and maintains public records for seven million Hoosiers.
It should do all three things well — not as a political instrument, but as a public service.
Join the campaign.
Support independent leadership for Indiana.
Three Core Priorities
- Protecting the Integrity and Privacy of Indiana Elections
- Serving Small Businesses and Entrepreneurs
- Educating and Empowering Indiana Voters
- Indiana voters deserve confidence in their elections — and confidence that their personal information is protected.
I will run this office with clear rules, transparent processes, and independent leadership that answers to Hoosiers, not to party bosses or federal agencies.
The Secretary of State's office should never be used as a tool to advance a political agenda or to hand sensitive voter data to outside interests.
Your registration information — your address, your primary ballot history, your driver's license number — belongs to you.
I will treat it that way.
Election administration is not a partisan job.
I will not run it like one.
- I have registered a business in Indiana.
I know what it costs — in time, money, and frustration — when the government makes you fight it just to get legal and get to work.
The Secretary of State's office registers every LLC, every corporation, every nonprofit in this state.
That system should be fast, reliable, and intuitive.
Right now it is not.
The website has broken links, outdated interfaces, and processes that require business owners to become bureaucracy experts just to complete a routine filing.
I will fix that.
Not because it is a campaign promise — because I have the professional background to actually do it.
I have spent more than twenty years building systems that serve people efficiently.
I will bring that same standard to this office.
Indiana should be one of the easiest states in the country to start and grow a business.
I am running to make sure the Secretary of State's office is never the reason it is not.
- Indiana consistently ranks near the bottom in voter turnout.
That is not acceptable — and it is not inevitable.
Most Hoosiers who do not vote are not apathetic.
They are confused, discouraged, or simply unaware of how the process works and what their options are.
That is a failure of voter education, not a failure of the voters themselves.
As Secretary of State I will expand clear, accessible voter education resources so every Hoosier understands how to register, where to vote, what is on their ballot, and how to request an absentee ballot.
I will make that information available in plain language, through every available channel, well before every election — not just the ones that get national attention.
More informed voters make better choices.
I trust Hoosiers to make their own decisions when they have the information they need to make them.
Why This Race Matters and Why I’m Running
This race will determine whether the Secretary of State’s office continues down its current path—or is reset with independent, accountable leadership.
Ballot access, voter confidence, and trust in government are all on the line.
I am not running to build a political career.
I am not running for the next thing.
I am running because this office touches the daily life of every Hoosier who has ever voted, started a business, or needed a public record — and it deserves someone who will actually show up and do the job.
