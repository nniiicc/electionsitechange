The Wingate Sweet Corn Festival and Tractor Show is held at the Kenneth W.
Bane Community Park in Wingate, Indiana.
Organized by the Coal Creek Volunteer Fire Department and Athletic Boosters, this annual tradition features freshly roasted sweet corn, a classic tractor and gas engine show, a community parade, and family-friendly activities.
