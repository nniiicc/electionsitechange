As co-owner of Vintage Vehicles, she intimately understands the challenges facing local businesses.
Currently serving as a board member of the Novato Downtown Business Association, Tief is deeply connected to her community’s heartbeat.
Additionally, she is the current Secretary of the Marin Republican Central Committee, immediate past President of the Novato Republican Women and a former CAGOP Delegate, advocating for her district’s values.
Married with two adult children, Tief’s dedication stems from her family’s inspiration.
She envisions a brighter future for all communities within California State Senate District 2.
A Local Leader As a candidate, Tief is committed to upholding conservative values that resonate with the needs and aspirations of the community.
Key issues she stands for include advocating for limited government to emphasize individual freedoms and personal responsibility.
Tief supports fiscal responsibility, which means lower taxes, balanced budgets, and prudent government spending.
