About Heather Baxter
Republican Nominee for Secretary of State
As a dedicated public servant, I am committed to restoring public trust in the electoral process and elevating customer service from the Secretary of State’s office.
Many South Dakotans have brought ideas and requests to me for improvements they’d like to see in the office.
I plan to address your concerns and get to work on day one.
I am a strong proponent for election integrity and accountability.
First and foremost, I support our auditors and support local control.
I stand with President Trump and echo his concerns over identified security issues in our elections such as citizenship and identification requirements, inaccurate voter rolls, mail in ballots, and voting machine issues.
I fully support robust post-election audits, improved hand counting methods, and thorough disaster recovery plans.
South Dakota already has strong laws in place, but there is always room for improvement.
Cast Vote Records (CVRs) are the federally required digital audit trail for our electronic tabulators.
Currently only 4 of 66 counties can fully audit the elections via review of Cast Vote Records (CVRs).
This must change.
I will ensure all counties have the proper audit tools available to them and the required training.
I also support the development of emerging technologies that can deliver total transparency, security, and the ability to fully audit elections from start to finish.
In addition, I will protect and enforce South Dakota’s proof of citizenship laws and work to restore civic engagement in the local election process.
I look forward to partnering with auditors, reducing fees and regulations, implementing office efficiencies to assist South Dakotans, reducing precinct size, reviewing compliance, and leading the office in maximizing production while minimizing expenses.
