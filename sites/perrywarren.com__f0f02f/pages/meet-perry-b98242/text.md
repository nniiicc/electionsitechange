Meet Perry Events Home Issues CONTRIBUTE Endorsements Join Our Team Fellows/Interns How-To Register to Vote Vote By Mail Meet Perry Perry Warren is the State Representative for Pennsylvania House District 31, serving Lower Makefield Township, Newtown Borough, Newtown Township, Upper Makefield Township and Yardley.
Perry was elected to the Pennsylvania House of Representatives in 2016, and was re-elected in 2018, 2020, 2022 and 2024.
Perry is a community leader and, before representing the district in Harrisburg, served in local office as a member of the Newtown Borough Council for seven years.
During his time on Borough Council, Perry helped pass bipartisan budgets, worked to preserve our parks and open space, and helped write the Borough's anti-discrimination ordinance, which protects the LBGT+ community from discrimination in employment and access to public accommodations.
Perry has cosponsored and voted for similar legislation in the PA House of Representatives.
As State Representative Perry has been a tireless advocate for our community.
He has advocated for increased funding for our schools, preservation of our environment and support for our local economy.
He introduced bills that have been signed into law to protect children, support local businesses and reduce insurance costs.
He has introduced legislation that has passed in the House that will make our communities safer, protect our children, reduce plastic waste and protect and expand health insurance coverage.
Perry supports local events and is a presence throughout our communities, meeting and listening to friends and neighbors.
A graduate of Colgate University and the University of North Carolina School of Law, Perry co-founded and grew a regional law firm, owned a small business and is a former public school teacher.
Perry and his wife, Liz, a registered nurse, live in Newtown and have five children.
