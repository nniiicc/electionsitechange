Meet Perry Events Home Issues CONTRIBUTE Endorsements Join Our Team Fellows/Interns How-To Register to Vote Vote By Mail How-To Register to Vote Vote By Mail How To Register to Vote Step 1: If you are not sure whether you are registered to vote in Bucks County, check your voter registration status here .
Step 2 : Fill out a voter registration application form.
You can either fill a form out online or print out a form and mail it to the Bucks County Board of Elections at 55 E Court St.
Doylestown, PA 18901.
Make sure to fill out the contact information section in case the Board of Elections finds any discrepancies and needs to confirm your information.
Reminder : The last day Pennsylvanians are able to register to vote in the general election is # days before the election.
The last day to register to vote in the primary election is # days before the election.
Step 3 : The Board of Elections will then review your form.
Step 4 : Once processed, the Board of Elections will send you a voter identification card in the mail.
This will have your name, address, party affiliation, and polling location on it.
Do not lose it.
If you do not receive your voter ID card two weeks after your application is processed, you should call the Bucks County Board of Elections at 215-348-6154.
