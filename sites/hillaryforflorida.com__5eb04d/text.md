Hillary Cassel is a proven problem-solver who puts the people of House District 101 first.
A lifelong Floridian, Hillary grew up in South Florida and earned degrees from Tallahassee Community College and Florida State University, before receiving her law degree from Nova Southeastern University.
She has lived and worked in Broward
County for over two decades.
Hillary began her career as a prosecutor in the Broward State Attorney’s Office, fighting to keep our neighborhoods safe.
Later, she co-founded her own law firm, where she’s spent years helping families take on Big Insurance and win.
In the Florida House, Hillary has never been afraid to stand up to anyone and do what’s right for her district.
She’s secured tens of millions in funding for local infrastructure, school safety, hurricane resiliency, and support for veterans and seniors.
Her focus is simple: get things done that make life better for the people she represents.
