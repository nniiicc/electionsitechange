Spencer Meline is a small business owner, husband, and father of four who was born and raised in Washington.
He built his businesses from the ground up, gaining firsthand experience managing payroll, navigating regulations, and leading teams through growth and uncertainty.
That experience shaped his understanding of how federal policy affects real employers, workers, and families.
Spencer supports parental rights with parents playing a meaningful role in education.
He believes there are many paths to success in education—responsibility, discipline, and hard work are essential.
Faith is central to Spencer and his family and guides how they live, work, and serve their community.
Spencer has spent years hiking trails, climbing peaks, and fishing remote lakes throughout the wilderness of Washington’s 8th District.
Those experiences shaped his deep appreciation for the land and his commitment to responsible stewardship of the public lands that make this region unique.
Spencer is not a career politician—he is a citizen who believes the next generation should inherit the same opportunities and freedoms that built this country.
