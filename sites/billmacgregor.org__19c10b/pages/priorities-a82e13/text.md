As State Representative, he is working tirelessly to improve and expand services.
Bill led the charge on legislation to regulate social media companies and limit their ability to show addictive social media content to teenagers online.
He also proposed legislation to expand interstate licensure access for telehealth providers, allowing the Commonwealth’s residents to access quality healthcare services even when traveling or temporarily living outside of the state.
He’s worked closely with colleagues and reproductive equity groups to highlight postpartum services and accessibility through legislation and the state budget.
But he knows there’s more work to do to ensure we’re delivering quality care for everyone in the Commonwealth.
Mental Health As a father of a four-year-old and a five-year-old, Bill understands the challenges parents face in affording and accessing childcare.
Funding and state support of Massachusetts’ Early Education & Care programs need to catch up to our K-12 programs - we can’t have the best primary schools in the country and leave our kids who are 5 years and under behind.
Parents are spending over $17,000 a year on childcare, especially in the Greater Boston area, and the entire state is paying the price.
Bill is the lead sponsor of legislation to provide a state-level tax credit for child and dependent care.
This would mirror the federal tax credit for families with children enrolled in childcare and providers caring for a dependent.
Since becoming State Representative, Bill has supported funding for early education, universal school meals, and organizations that provide childhood care for families and their kids.
Childcare Constituent Services For over a decade, Bill served in former City Councilor Matt O’Malley’s office as Chief of Staff and liaison to West Roxbury and Roslindale.
Nobody knows the importance of being a responsive elected official as Bill does.
Since becoming State Representative, Bill has continued his 10+ years of work in the community he grew up in.
Bill and his team have resolved hundreds of constituent concerns each year, actively participated in every neighborhood in the 10th Suffolk district, and organized events and youth sports leagues to keep the community engaged.
He has also been a tireless advocate and champion for the organizations and nonprofits serving the district.
Bill has secured over $2 million in funding for local groups doing important work in West Roxbury, Roslindale, Jamaica Plain, and Brookline.
Bill prides himself on responsiveness and on showing up, and has done so every day since being elected in 2023.
The 10th Suffolk District is home to the largest percentage of seniors in the City of Boston, and as State Representative, Bill has been committed to supporting them.
Bill has the lead sponsor on securing over $1.1 million for the senior programming PILOT managed by Ethos at the Elks in West Roxbury, giving seniors activities and social events 3 days a week.
He has introduced legislation making senior tax credits automatic, rather than requiring seniors to reapply every year.
He has hosted coffee hours and Q&A sessions at each of the district’s senior homes to ensure residents who choose to age in the 10th Suffolk District know their State Representative is fighting for them. < Senior Services While working in Councilor O’Malley’s Office when he chaired the Environment, Resiliency, and Parks Committee, Bill worked tirelessly to lower Boston’s greenhouse gas emissions and ensure Boston uses greener, cleaner energy.
With Bill’s help, the office delivered a paperless paystub option for city employees and passed a local ordinance to protect our wetlands.
Bill wants to ensure that we’re leaving his children - and all children - a healthy and green world to inhabit.
That’s why he’s led the charge to get money and resources for green space across the district, advocated for a ban on third-party energy suppliers taking advantage of ratepayers, and secured over $7 million for clean energy and open space funding in the district.
Environment & Climate Policy Cost of Living and Inflation: Reducing the cost of living for residents in the 10th Suffolk District and beyond will always be at the forefront of Bill’s mind when making decisions as a legislator.
We can’t continue to ask Boston residents to pay skyrocketing bills without giving them any support.
Access to Reproductive Health Care: Bill believes in the fundamental right to choose.
He supports the actions the Massachusetts legislature has taken in recent times to expand accessibility and protections to reproductive health and gender-affirming care for Massachusetts residents and residents from other states seeking care here.
He looks forward to continuing the work to protect access to critically important care when elected.
While in Councilor O’Malley’s office, Bill worked to expand access to menstrual products in Boston Public Schools.
As a State Representative, Bill will work tirelessly to create and maintain a world that his daughters and all women can live in equitably.
Seniors & Elder Affairs: For too long, seniors in the 10th Suffolk have gone without a full time senior center.
As your State Representative, Bill will partner with stakeholders to work toward a full-time senior center that can serve the district and provide reliable and centralized senior programming.
Transportation: Bill, alongside Rep.
Bob Consalvo, introduced Boston’s Home Rule Petition for MBTA Commuter Rail fare equity (Bill H.3638).
This legislation designates all of Boston’s neighborhoods - including West Roxbury and Roslindale - as Zone 1A, which will reduce the price of a single ride on the train from $6.50 or $7 down to $2.40.
Nursing: Bill’s wife, Lisa, is a nurse at Brigham and Women’s Hospital.
Bill knows that Lisa and her colleagues across the Commonwealth sacrifice their time, mental health, and sometimes safety to serve our communities.
Bill supports creating safe patient to nurse ratio guidelines at the legislative level, and will support efforts to expand the health care worker repayment program that Gov.
Baker introduced during his final months in office.
Labor: Bill grew up in a family of union workers.
His father was a union worker, and his brother is currently a member of the Brookline Fire Fighters Local 950.
Bill supports the rights of workers to organize in their workplace.
