On the Issues Principled - Constitutional - Conservatism No matter where you stand on the issues surrounding our recent elections, every Arizonan will benefit from reforms that increase transparency, improve accuracy and speed, punish lawbreakers, and allow for verifiable results.
The candidate you support may win or lose, but you deserve to know that the election was properly run and that the results are accurate and truthful.
Election Integrity Arizona spends billions of dollars every year on our K-12 schools.
In return, we need to ensure that those schools are properly preparing children with the skills they will need.
Our schools are not the place for social experiments or grooming kids to support the latest radical fad.
I support accountability measures, school choice (including ESAs that allow parents to choose the best school for their kids), parental involvement in curriculum issues, and a return to focusing on core skills.
Education Inflation is hammering us here in Arizona, driving up the price of goods, eroding our savings, and decreasing the value of our money.
There isn’t much we can do at the state level to solve this national problem, but we can keep our economy growing and continue to attract higher-wage jobs to protect Arizona families.
We need to keep our taxes and regulatory burden low, and do whatever we can to help Arizonans deal with the consequences of inflation until such time as our federal government fixes things.
Less Inflation, More Jobs With the Trump administration, border crossings are at historic lows.
These efforts contribute to protecting Arizona citizens by decreasing the flow of fentanyl and crime.
I will support continued border enforcement to ensure the safety of our communities.

Parental Rights Our individual freedoms, granted to us by God and protected by the US Constitution, are under constant attack.
You can count on me to defend them and to work to restore any rights that have been lost or infringed upon.
That includes our right to life, our medical liberties, and our 2nd Amendment rights.
Individual Rights Not only is our federal government continuing to restrict our access to, and use of, Arizona land, but foreign nationals are buying significant farm land near our military bases.
These events are bad for our tax base, our water supply, and our national security, and I will fight to protect Arizona land and water from its abusers, foreign and domestic.
Arizona Land/Water I will defend our sisters, daughters, and granddaughters against this new radical transgender agenda that is targeting them.

I’m glad to see our current legislature taking steps to restore and protect these freedoms and will continue the fight and ensure each Arizonan has the ability to choose the right healthcare for their individual needs.
