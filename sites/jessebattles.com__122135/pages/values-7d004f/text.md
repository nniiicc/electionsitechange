Jesse’s Values
The principles behind every vote he will cast in Montgomery.
- Grounded in Christian Faith & Moral Conviction Jesse’s leadership is anchored in deep Christian faith and clear moral principles.
He brings traditional values to public service, standing firm on biblical truths and ensuring our shared faith and values heritage remain the compass for every decision he makes.
- Defending Pro-Life Values Jesse is pro-life and believes every child is a gift from God.
He will stand firmly for the right to life and our Christian values while making sure mothers have the support they need.
From strengthening rural healthcare and pregnancy resource centers to improving adoption and supporting working families, Jesse will fight for a culture that protects life and strengthens families across Alabama.
- Uncompromising Integrity & Public Accountability Jesse believes public service is a sacred trust, not a path to personal power.
He is committed to absolute transparency, fiscal responsibility, and keeping his word to the people of District 10, ensuring government works for the citizens, not special interests.
- Defending Family, Community, & Local Control Strong families and tight-knit communities are the backbone of Northeast Alabama.
Jesse will fight to protect the traditional family structure, honor local heritage, and ensure decisions affecting our schools and neighborhoods stay with parents and local leaders, not Montgomery.
- Safeguarding Personal Liberty & Individual Responsibility Jesse is a fierce defender of individual freedom, self-reliance, and fundamental constitutional rights.
He will stand as a strong line of defense against government overreach, protecting every citizen’s right to live, work, and raise their family free from state interference.
- Stewardship of Our Natural Resources & Heritage District 10 is blessed with incredible land, waterways, and outdoor traditions.
Jesse takes seriously the responsibility to protect our rivers, parks, and natural treasures, preserving our outdoor heritage so future generations can enjoy the same blessings we have today.
