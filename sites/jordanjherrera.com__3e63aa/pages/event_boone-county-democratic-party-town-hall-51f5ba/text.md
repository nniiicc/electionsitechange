Boone County Democratic Party Town Hall
Join Jordan Herrera in Columbia
Jordan Herrera is headed to Columbia.
The Boone County Democratic Party is hosting a town hall with Jordan on Tuesday, October 13, from 6:00 to 8:00 PM at the ARC, 1701 W.
Ash St.
Bring your questions.
Jordan spent nearly 16 years in the Air Force, deployed to Iraq and Afghanistan, and later worked constitutional law at the Missouri Attorney General’s office before running for Congress.
He wants to hear what’s on your mind, whether that’s healthcare costs, rural infrastructure, or what Washington owes veterans.
Seating is limited, so plan to arrive early.
More details on parking and format are coming as the date gets closer.
