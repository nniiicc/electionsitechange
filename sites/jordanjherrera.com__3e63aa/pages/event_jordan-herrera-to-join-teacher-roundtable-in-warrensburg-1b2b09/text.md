Jordan Herrera to Join Teacher Roundtable in Warrensburg
Jordan Herrera will take part in a roundtable conversation hosted by the Warrensburg Community of Teachers Association on Wednesday, October 28, at 4:15 PM at Ridge View Elementary, 215 S.
Ridgeview Dr. in Warrensburg.
The gathering brings together local teachers, elected officials, and candidates for an open conversation about the future of education in the Warrensburg School District and across Missouri.
Several other local politicians and candidates have also been invited.
