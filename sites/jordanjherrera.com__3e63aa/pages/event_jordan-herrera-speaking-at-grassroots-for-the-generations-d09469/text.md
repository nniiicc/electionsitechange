Jordan Herrera Heads to Grassroots for the Generations in Kansas City
Jordan Herrera, the Democratic candidate for Missouri’s 4th Congressional District, will speak at a public forum hosted by Grassroots for the Generations on Tuesday, October 14, at 5:00 PM at Country Club Christian Church in Kansas City.
Grassroots for the Generations is a nonpartisan civic organization that invites candidates who have an interest in saving and preserving our democracy to speak directly with the community about the issues facing the country.
Jordan has made it a priority to show up everywhere voters want to hear from him, whether that’s a union hall, a church, or a county fair.
This is another chance for people to ask him directly where he stands.
