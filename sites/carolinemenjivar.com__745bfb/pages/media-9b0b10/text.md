What Voters Need to Know About Senator Caroline Menjivar
Caroline Menjivar has a track record of service.
She's a U.S.
Marine veteran, worked as an emergency medical technician, and a nonprofit leader at MEND in Pacoima, helping families access food and essential support.
As a State Senator, she's bringing her lived experiences to pass policies that ensure every Californian has the support they need to stay safe, healthy, and be able to afford to live in California.
- Restored millions in funding to help homeless families reunify with their children
- Restored cuts to programs that help children in foster care
- Protected health services for vulnerable families
- Restored millions for emergency housing assistance for senior citizens
She is focused on lowering costs for working families, pushing to protect access to affordable housing and health care.
