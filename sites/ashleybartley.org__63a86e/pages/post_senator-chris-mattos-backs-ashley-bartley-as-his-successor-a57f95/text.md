Senator Chris Mattos Backs Ashley Bartley as his Successor
It means a great deal to have the support of Senator Chris Mattos.
I’m proud to share his endorsement:
“After serving with Ashley Bartley in Montpelier for the last four years, I can’t think of a better person to represent our communities in the Vermont Senate.
Ashley has been a leading voice on housing and affordability, and she understands the challenges facing Vermont families.
I’ve seen firsthand how hard she works for her constituents, always willing to listen, advocate, and fight for the communities she represents.
Essex, Milton, Westford, and Fairfax would be lucky to have Ashley as their Senator.
I’m proud to endorse her and encourage you to join me in voting for Ashley Bartley on or before November 3rd.“
- Senator Chris Mattos
