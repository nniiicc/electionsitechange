Ashley Bartley #ago 1 min read Neighbors, I'm writing to ask you to join me in voting for Ashley Bartley for State Senate.
Many of us have been frustrated by the Senate split in Franklin County.
Now is the time to make sure our senator understands the needs of rural Franklin County and the people who live and work here.
She has worked her tail off for us in the House over the last four years.
As a REALTOR, I've watched clients leave Vermont as they've become taxed out of their home, or because the cost of childcare in the state they were born and raised in had simply become too high.
I've watched Ashley fight to keep Vermont affordable and within reach for families who want to stay here.
As an Essex small business owner, I've also felt the burden of Montpelier's top-down approach, and I've watched Ashley stand up for Vermont's small businesses every step of the way.
Fairfax hasn't had a resident serve in the Vermont Senate in thirty years.
Let's change that!
You won't find someone who cares more or fights harder for Fairfax, and for Vermont.
Ashley is a fellow mom and a friend.
Please join me in voting for Ashley Bartley for Vermont State Senate on or before November 3rd.
