Ashley Bartley Nov 6, 2024 1 min read Serving in Montpelier and representing the two amazing communities of Fairfax and Georgia has been one of the biggest honors of my life.
I thank each and every one of you for supporting my re-election effort and I am excited to get back to work in January.
Vermont is faced with many crises that seem to continue with little action.
We still have a housing crisis.
We still have a property tax crisis.
We still have an affordability crisis.
We need to expand our maternal care.
We must ensure our veterans are properly cared for.
I am but one of one-hundred and eighty unique voices in Montpelier.
But with a more balanced Montpelier, Governor Scott, Carolyn Branagan and I are in a stronger position to fight for what’s right.
I remain committed to continued weekly communication, responding year-round to your requests, and expand upon my outreach efforts.
In the coming weeks, Carolyn and I will be holding two listening meetings, one in both communities.
Carolyn and I remain in constant contact, regardless of whether we are in session or not.
Contact us anytime or can follow along with any of these social media platforms.
