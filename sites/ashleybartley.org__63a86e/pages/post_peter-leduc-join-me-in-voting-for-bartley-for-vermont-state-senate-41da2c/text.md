Ashley Bartley #ago 1 min read This letter is from Peter LeDuc, a resident of Fairfax.
I've known Ashley Bartley for a couple of years now, and I couldn't be more thrilled to endorse her candidacy for the Vermont State Senate.
We're a young family, and like so many Vermonters, we're doing our best to provide for our kids and build a life of opportunity here.
Ashley's votes to keep property taxes lower, to oppose increased fees, and to support local control over development in our communities are exactly what voters in Milton, Fairfax, Westford, and the rural parts of Essex Town need.
As my representative for the past four years, Ashley has kept us informed with frequent updates, sometimes more than once a week, on what's happening in Montpelier.
When constituents had questions, she was always reachable.
She never claimed to have all the answers, but she proved time and again that she's always listening.
We need leaders who are true to their word, stay positive, and lead by example.
Fairfax has had that in Ashley Bartley for the last four years, and I hope all of us in the district get to have that in her for many more!
Please join me in voting for Ashley Bartley for Vermont State Senate.
