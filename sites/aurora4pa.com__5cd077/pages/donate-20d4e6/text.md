Contributions to Aurora Stuski for Congress are not tax deductible for federal income tax purposes.

Contributions must be made from personal funds and may not be reimbursed by any other person.
Contributions from corporations, labor unions, federal government contractors, and foreign nationals (non–green card holders) are prohibited.

- You are a U.S. citizen or lawfully admitted permanent resident (green card holder)
- This contribution is made from your own funds
