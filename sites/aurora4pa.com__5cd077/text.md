Josh Shapiro is directly trying to tie the hands of the public and force data centers into areas where they are strongly opposed removing the ability of counties and municipalities to have any say wether or not data centers can be put in your backyard.
We need to come together as a community to fight his tyrannical legislation.
Emails have surfaced between real estate developers and Governor Shapiro’s administration about ways to limit appeals of Pennsylvania families opposed to massive data center projects in their hometowns, like the proposed 2.2. million square foot facility in Plymouth Township .
Emails with Shapiro’s Chief Transformation Officer Benjamin Kirshner show the request for legislation that would require anyone who wants to appeal a data center project to post a bond that is worth double the cost of the project .
Wow.
That means you would have to post a $20 million bond to oppose a $10 million data center.
Shapiro is also trying to hide Amazon’s identidy as the developer behind many data center projects.
