Meet Pete ….
Pete Ferrell is a lifelong rancher, conservationist, and community leader from Beaumont, KS.
A fourth-generation steward of the Ferrell Ranch, founded by his great-grandfather in 1888, Pete has spent his career blending practical ranching experience with a passion for sustainable agriculture and rural economic development.
Raised in the small Flint Hills community of Beaumont, Pete attended the town's three-room grade school before graduating from Leon High School.
He earned a bachelor's degree in Anthropology from Grinnell College, where he developed a lasting interest in archaeology through fieldwork in the American Southwest.
After returning home, Pete dedicated himself to the family ranch, eventually becoming its general manager.
Along the way, he became an early advocate for regenerative grazing and innovative land management practices.
His commitment to conservation and stewardship has earned numerous state and national recognitions, including awards for soil conservation, wildlife habitat improvement, and sustainable agriculture.
Community service has always been an important part of Pete's life.
Over the years he has served on the Butler County Community College Board of Trustees, the Leadership Butler Board, and numerous nonprofit and agricultural organizations.
Most notably, he spent three decades on the Board of Directors of The Land Institute, including serving as board chair, helping advance groundbreaking work in perennial grain agriculture before becoming an emeritus board member in 2025.
Today, Pete continues to operate Ferrell Ranch LLC with his family while serving on the boards of several conservation, watershed, and renewable energy organizations.
He and his wife, Kay, enjoy spending time with their children and grandchildren, and Pete remains passionate about reading, hiking, Americana music, and helping the next generation of ranchers build healthy land and thriving rural communities.
