First elected to the County Council in 1998, Guy has since advanced the causes of our community in the House of Delegates, and does so now in leadership in the Senate of Maryland.
He currently serves as the Senate Budget and Taxation Chairman, ensuring a seat at the table for all of his constituents at the highest level of legislative decision making.
Guy has been a steady leader in the face of a public health pandemic.
Since entering public office, Guy has been a tireless advocate for education.
While on the County Council, he fought for renovations and additions to 31 schools, supported the construction of 8 new schools, and funded smaller class sizes in the early grades.
Since entering state government, he has fought for funding to keep our education system #1 in the state, including funding for Howard Community College and for the University System of Maryland.
As a former State Director of the Sierra Club, and former board member of the Chesapeake Bay Trust, Guy brings his experience to his role in the budget process on the Budget and Taxation Committee, and to the Chesapeake Bay Commission as one of its members.
Guy has always worked to create the change he wants to see in the world.
In high school, while earning his Eagle Scout award, he organized his troop to clean a vacant lot and build a playground for area children.
After moving to Columbia, he became Chairman of the Kings Contrivance village board and started a neighborhood beautification program.
Even now, he helps provide rides for senior citizens as a volunteer driver for NeighborRide.
As our Senator, Guy has worked for us.
He has secured funding for projects, and championed policies that support all of our community.
Above all, has never forgotten his true calling: service.
SEE GUY'S TIMELINE OF WORK Guy's Accomplishments Education & Our Children’s Future: In the Maryland Legislature and on the County Council, Guy has made education and our #1 ranked school system his top priority.
Over the last 15 years, he has contributed to significant changes that have improved student performance and school facilities across Howard County and Maryland.
On the higher education front, he has led efforts to expand Howard Community College and many universities around the state.
As the Chairman of the Committee that works to pass the State's budget, Guy is in a strong position to advocate for Howard County students, parents, and our top-rated teaching and support staff.
Keeping Our Neighborhoods Safe: Guy has worked to improve our public safety system and help keep our communities safe.
He has been effective at making sure our first responders have the resources they need to provide excellent service, particularly during the COVID-19 pandemic.
He also has supported legislation to protect citizens against sex offenders and to strengthen domestic violence laws.
Quality of Life: With a personal and professional commitment to the natural environment, Guy has led numerous efforts to promote environmental protection, land preservation, growth management, and community planning.
Guy advocates for our library system, new and upgraded senior centers, and for new and upgraded parks and recreational facilities for Howard County residents in Annapolis.
Economic Growth & Fiscal Responsibility: Serving as the Chairman of the Maryland Senate Budget and Taxation Committee, on the House Appropriations Committee, and as a member of the Howard County Council, Guy has helped to develop and vote for two decades of straight balanced budgets.
During this time, the county and the state received AAA bond ratings for outstanding fiscal management.
Guy has also supported legislation and responsible zoning practices to aid innovative business growth, technology transfer, and workforce development.
