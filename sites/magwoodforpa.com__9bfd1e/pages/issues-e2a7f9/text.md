The issues
ADDRESSING AFFORDABILITY, BRINGING IN MORE COMMUNITY INVESTMENT, AND FIXING GOVERNMENT ARE TOP PRIORITIES
Rebuilding Communities, Strengthening Schools, and Ending Food Deserts
Davon Magwood's campaign is built on a strong commitment to revitalizing communities and improving the quality of education in the 55th district of Pennsylvania.
He believes that strong, vibrant communities are the foundation of a thriving society, and he is dedicated to working across party lines to secure the resources needed for rebuilding.
Davon understands that well-funded schools are critical to the future success of the district, and he advocates for policies that ensure every child has access to a quality education, regardless of their zip code.
Additionally, Davon is committed to ending food deserts in the district by promoting initiatives that increase access to fresh, affordable food in underserved areas.
He supports expanding grocery stores, farmers' markets, and community gardens to ensure that all residents have access to healthy food options.
Economic Growth Through Tax Reform and Infrastructure Investment
As a progressive Democrat, Davon Magwood supports bipartisan efforts to make Pennsylvania a more attractive place for businesses by lowering the state's Corporate Net Income (CNI) tax rate.
Recognizing that Pennsylvania has one of the highest flat CNI tax rates in the nation, Davon sees tax reform as essential to fostering economic growth and job creation.
In addition to tax reform, Davon is a strong advocate for investing in infrastructure, particularly in underserved communities.
He believes that modernizing roads, bridges, and public transportation is key to economic development and job creation, helping local businesses thrive and improving the quality of life for residents across the district.
Healthcare Access and Affordability
Healthcare is a cornerstone of Davon Magwood's platform.
He is committed to expanding access to affordable healthcare for all residents of the 55th district.
Davon supports policies that would lower prescription drug costs, protect and expand Medicaid, and ensure that every resident has access to mental health services.
He believes that healthcare is a fundamental human right and that no one should have to choose between paying for healthcare and other essential needs.
Davon also advocates for increased funding for rural healthcare facilities, ensuring that even the most remote areas of the district have access to quality medical care.
Innovative Community Engagement
Davon Magwood's campaign is rooted in direct, meaningful engagement with the people of the 55th district.
He believes in meeting voters where they are, whether that's through town halls, community events, or door-to-door conversations.
Davon prioritizes listening to the concerns and ideas of his constituents, ensuring that their voices shape his policy positions.
By fostering a strong connection with the community, Davon aims to build a campaign that truly represents the needs and aspirations of the district, making every voter feel heard and valued.
Additionally, he is committed to transparency and accountability, promising regular updates and open communication with his constituents if elected.
A Woman's Right to Choose
Davon Magwood is a staunch advocate for reproductive rights.
He firmly believes that a woman has the right to make decisions about her own body without government interference.
Davon is committed to safeguarding access to safe and legal abortion services, ensuring that every woman has the freedom and autonomy to make the choices that are best for her health and life.
Fair Housing
Davon Magwood is a strong advocate for affordable housing and renters' protection from slumlords.
He believes that everyone deserves access to safe, decent, and affordable housing.
Davon is committed to ensuring that renters are protected from exploitation and substandard living conditions by supporting policies that hold landlords accountable and promote fair housing practices.
His goal is to create housing policies that prioritize residents' well-being and foster strong, healthy communities.
Raising the Minimum Wage
Davon Magwood strongly supports raising the minimum wage.
He believes that every worker deserves a living wage that reflects the true cost of living and ensures economic security.
Davon advocates for a fair and just economy where hardworking individuals can support themselves and their families without struggling to make ends meet.
He sees raising the wage as essential to reducing income inequality and fostering economic growth within communities.
The Environment
Davon Magwood believes that clean air and water are fundamental rights, and he is committed to being a good steward of our natural resources.
He advocates for holding corporate polluters accountable to protect the health and well-being of those who work and live in the district.
Davon supports significant investments in renewable energy, public transit, and climate adaptation initiatives.
Davon Magwood is dedicated to creating permanent, high-quality, green union jobs in House District 55.
He believes that environmental protection and economic prosperity can and should go hand in hand with economic prosperity.
Fair Districts
Fair redistricting is essential for a just and representative democracy.
It involves drawing electoral district boundaries in a way that accurately reflects the population and avoids partisan gerrymandering.
By ensuring that redistricting is transparent and equitable, we can guarantee that every voter’s voice is heard and every community is properly represented.
Fair redistricting promotes integrity in the electoral process and helps create a political system where all citizens have a meaningful say in their government.
My goal is to support a fair redistricting process that reflects the true demographics and needs of our communities, ensuring that every voter’s voice is heard and every community is properly represented.
By prioritizing fairness and transparency, we can strengthen our democracy and build a more inclusive and representative political system."
