KEY DEADLINES
OCTOBER 19
Monday, October 19 is the last day to register before the November 3, 2026 midterm election.
You can register online before midnight.
It is also the recommended day to apply for your mail ballot.
https://vote.pa.gov/Register
Your mail-in ballot should be returned by mail before October 27, you may choose to drop it off in-person on election day.
We recommend returning your mail-in ballot by mail before October 27.
November 3
Tuesday, November 3, 2026 is the election.
You can vote in-person at your polling location from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
