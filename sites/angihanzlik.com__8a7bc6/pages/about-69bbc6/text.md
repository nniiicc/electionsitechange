Meet Angi
Angi is a Navy veteran and small-business owner who is proud to call Chamberlain and District 26B home.
After serving 20 years in the United States Navy, she and her family chose South Dakota as the place to put down roots and raise their family.
This community was the first place that truly felt like home and is where Angi and her husband, Jarrod, raised their three boys, William, Jack, and Andrew.
Over the past 11 years, Angi has been actively involved in the community through local leadership, business investment, and service.
She co-founded Berry + Basil in 2020 and serves on the South Dakota Retailers Association Board of Directors.
Through her work as a business owner and community advocate, she has gained firsthand insight into how policy decisions affect families and small businesses.
Angi is running for the South Dakota House of Representatives in District 26B as a Republican to be a collaborative, solutions-focused voice for the district.
Her priorities include economic growth, education, and supporting strong local communities.
