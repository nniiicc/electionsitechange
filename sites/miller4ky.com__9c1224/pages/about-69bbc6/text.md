A Lifetime of Service to Kentucky
L.
Scott Miller is a lifelong public servant, attorney, and former Kentucky State Police Lt.
Colonel.
With decades in law enforcement, he has stood beside the men and women who protect our communities every day.
As a former KSP Trooper and Lt.
Colonel, he understands the sacrifices made by first responders — because he has lived them.
That experience shaped a deep commitment to public safety, the rule of law, and the families who call Kentucky home.
He and his wife are proud parents and grandparents running to ensure Kentucky remains safe, prosperous, and full of opportunity for future generations.
Decades of Service to the Commonwealth
- Trooper Kentucky State Police Began his career on the front lines, patrolling Kentucky communities and answering the call when families needed help most.
- Lt.
Colonel Kentucky State Police Command Rose through the ranks to lead troopers across the Commonwealth — managing operations, training, and the standards that protect every Kentuckian.
- Attorney Private Practice Brought a career of legal experience to clients across the region, with a focus on the rule of law and fair treatment.
- Candidate Kentucky House District 88 Now running to bring that same steady, principled leadership to Frankfort on behalf of the people of District 88.
