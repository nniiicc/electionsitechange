Lowering the Cost of Living
Keep taxes low, encourage job creation, and reduce unnecessary government costs so families keep more of what they earn.
Priorities
Seven priorities grounded in Kentucky values — built around the people, businesses, and traditions that make District 88 home.
Kentucky families are working harder than ever, but too many are feeling the strain of rising prices.
From groceries and utilities to housing and healthcare, the cost of living continues to challenge hardworking families every day.
For too long, politicians from both parties have spent more time arguing than solving the problems Kentucky families face.
While families have been tightening their budgets, too many elected officials have been focused on politics instead of practical solutions.
As your State Representative, I will get to work on day one.
I'll fight for policies that make life more affordable by keeping taxes low, encouraging job creation, reducing unnecessary government costs, and supporting economic growth that benefits working families.
I believe government should create opportunities for success, not make everyday life more expensive.
Kentucky should be a place where families can afford to buy a home, build a career, save for the future, and keep more of what they earn.
Making life more affordable will be my top priority because strong families are the foundation of strong communities.
