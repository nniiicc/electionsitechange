All policies Charles Booker for U.S.
Senate, Kentucky By cutting Medicaid, Andy Barr has turned his back on some of our most vulnerable Kentuckians.
And, despite being in Washington for over a decade, Andy Barr has no record of standing up for vulnerable Kentuckians.
On day one, I would commit to: Expanding Medicaid's Home and Community-Based Services to provide support for Kentuckians with intellectual, developmental, or physical disabilities; Opposing Medicaid cuts that threaten specialized medical, dental, and community providers relied upon by disabled Kentuckians; Providing targeted federal grants to raise wages for direct support professionals (DSPs) who provide care and support to disabled Kentuckians, resolving staff shortages that limit care access.
Modernizing SSI/SSDI Limits to put more money in the hands of families caring for disabled Kentuckians and complement programs like STABLE Kentucky.
Restoring education grants cut by the Trump Administration for specialized student learning and transition programs under the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act (IDEA).
Expanding support for employers and training providers that offer vocational rehabilitation and competitive integrated employment initiatives to close the employment gap for Kentuckians with disabilities.
