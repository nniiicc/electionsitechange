All policies Charles Booker for U.S.
Senate, Kentucky Every Kentuckian should be able to build a good life, no matter where they live.
Too many roads, bridges and water systems are past their limits, and too many families still lack reliable internet.
I will: Fight for federal dollars to repair Kentucky’s roads and bridges, including long-delayed projects that connect our regions.
Finish the job on rural broadband so every home, farm and small business can get online.
Upgrade drinking water and wastewater systems so no Kentucky family has to boil its water.
Strengthen flood and disaster resilience in Eastern and Western Kentucky communities hit repeatedly by storms.
Ensure these projects are built by Kentucky workers earning good union wages.
