You hear me say that at every campaign stop, and I mean it.
Every single one of you matters to me, especially the vulnerable Kentuckians.
But politicians like Andy Barr have turned their back on our most vulnerable.
Andy Barr has been in Congress for fourteen years and yet has done far more to harm vulnerable Kentuckians than help them.
That’s why I’m committed to reversing the harm.
We’re going to expand critical Medicaid services to help support Kentuckians with intellectual, developmental, or physical disabilities.
I’m also going to oppose any cuts to Medicaid that threaten specialized care relied upon by disabled Kentuckians.
We’ll make sure every vulnerable Kentuckian gets the care they deserve.
