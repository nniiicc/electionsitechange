SENATE LEADERSHIP​ INTERN ROLE OVERVIEW Booker for the Commonwealth is the campaign to elect Charles Booker to the United States Senate from Kentucky.

The campaign is seeking a Leadership Intern to support the candidate, General Consultant, and campaign leadership with scheduling, travel, administrative responsibilities, and special projects.
