I look forward to continuing to represent you in the Montana Legislature
Dear Neighbors –
I’m honored that you have elected me to represent you in the Montanan Legislature as the representative for House District 94 (now House District 99).
As your representative, I will work diligently to do the best job I can in the Legislature and to carefully consider each and every vote I take on your behalf.
While I have been elected as a Democrat, I represent everyone in this district.
On many issues I know there are solutions that will serve every Montanan well and I will work to find them.
Whether you are a Republican, Democrat or Independent, I value your views as a Montanan.
I welcome your thoughts on bills that will come before the Legislature and I will listen carefully to your views.
Please feel free to contact me about problems that you think the Legislature should address or with ideas that you believe can move us forward.
There’s much more that unites us than divides us as we work together for Montana.
Thanks and I look forward to hearing from you.
