Welcome to the website of Vote Valerie Gaydos (“we,” “our,” or “us”).
Your privacy matters to us.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your data.
1 .
Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
Personal Information: If you sign up for updates, volunteer, contact us, sign a petition, or leave a comment, we may collect your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, and any information you voluntarily provide.
We do not request sensitive information such as passwords, payment details, or government identification numbers through our contact or sign-up forms.
Please do not submit sensitive information through our contact forms or comments.
Text Message Opt-In Information: If you opt in to receive text messages from our campaign, we collect your mobile phone number and your consent status for this purpose.
See our Terms of Service for full details on our Mobile Messaging Program.
Automatically Collected Information: When you visit our website, we may automatically collect information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages visited.
This information is collected through cookies and analytics tools.
Children’s Privacy: This website is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take prompt steps to delete that information from our records.
If you believe we may have collected information from a minor, please contact us at info@votevaleriegaydos.com.

- Operate, maintain, and improve our website and content

- Send campaign updates, newsletters, or text messages if you have opted in

5.
Cookies, Analytics & Website Tracking
Cookies: We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience and analyze our traffic.
When you visit our website, a cookie consent banner allows you to customize your preferences across the following categories:
- Necessary – Required for basic site functionality.
Always active and cannot be disabled.
- Functional – Enables features such as social media sharing and third-party integrations.
- Analytics – Helps us understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., page views, traffic sources).
We use Google Analytics for this purpose.
Non-essential cookies (Functional and Analytics) are only activated after you provide consent through our cookie consent banner.
You may update your preferences at any time by accessing the consent tool on our website.
You may also adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies; however, some features of the site may not function properly.
Analytics: We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated, non-personal usage data.
We do not use session replay, keystroke logging, or other technologies that record or intercept user communications on our website.
Do Not Track Signals: At this time, our website does not respond to Do Not Track signals.
You may control tracking through your browser settings and the cookie consent tool available on our site.
Data Retention: We retain personal information you voluntarily submit through contact forms, sign-up forms, or the texting program only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, or as required by applicable campaign finance or election law.
