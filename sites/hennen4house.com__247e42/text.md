Brad Hennen is running for State Representative in District 15A to keep Southwest Minnesota strong for families, farmers, small businesses, and the next generation.
Built Here.
Fighting for You.
Brad Hennen was born and raised on a family farm near Ghent in Lyon County, where he learned the value of hard work, personal responsibility, and showing up for your neighbors.
He and his wife Barb raised their nine children here, and now, as grandparents, they care deeply about the future being built for the next generation
Brad is running because he believes Southwest Minnesota should be a place where families can build a life, grow a business, and put down roots — without feeling like they have to leave to succeed.

Too often, decisions made in St.

Lower the Cost of Living
