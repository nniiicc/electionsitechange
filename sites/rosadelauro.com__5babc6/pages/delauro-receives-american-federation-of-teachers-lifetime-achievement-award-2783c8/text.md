“I am grateful to receive this award, but I am more grateful for our nation’s teachers,” said DeLauro.
“There is no one I would rather partner with in a fight than America’s teachers.
President Trump and his billionaires allies are seeking to destroy public education – they are determined to make education a privilege for a wealthy few.
But I never back down in a fight, and neither does America’s teachers.
The Trump administration should not expect us to start now.
I am grateful for this award, and I am ready to keep fighting.” Along with DeLauro, Labor movement leaders Dolores Huerta and Lee Saunders also received lifetime achievement awards for their work.
“Organized labor and education are the building blocks of the middle class,” DeLauro continued.
“And the middle-class is the backbone of the United States.
I will always stand with our teachers and our labor unions.” Photos of the event can be found here .
