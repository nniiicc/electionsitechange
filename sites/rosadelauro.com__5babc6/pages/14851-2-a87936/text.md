Connecticut Labor Backs DeLauro
NEW HAVEN – Today, the Friends of Rosa DeLauro campaign rolled out a slate of endorsements for Congresswoman Rosa DeLauro from organized labor groups in Connecticut.
These endorsements come from the Connecticut AFL-CIO, Connecticut State Building Trades, SEIU Connecticut 1199NE, and the Connecticut Laborers District Council.
“Rosa DeLauro knows that unions build a strong middle-class as we fight together for the middle class, working class, and the vulnerable. ,” said DeLauro Campaign Manager Allison Dodge.
“From fighting for the PRO Act to standing up to the Trump administration’s attacks against organized labor, Congresswoman DeLauro is leading the fight in Congress to protect workers’ right to organize and secure the pay and benefits they have earned.
Unions are powered by the people, and so is this campaign.”
“Representative Rosa DeLauro has dedicated her career to fighting for working people in Connecticut and across our nation,” said Ed Hawthorne, President of the Connecticut AFL-CIO.
“From defending the right to organize and collectively bargain through her advocacy of the PRO Act, to expanding access to affordable health care, to fighting for safeguards that ensure AI is used to support workers and not displace or undermine their rights, Rosa has never wavered in her support of working families.
At a time when workers’ rights are under attack and corporate CEOs are rushing untested AI systems into the workplace, Connecticut needs a fighter like Rosa in Washington.
The Connecticut AFL-CIO is proud to once again unanimously endorse Representative DeLauro for re-election.”
“From securing federal investments for projects that provide jobs and apprenticeship opportunities for our members to ensuring contracts comply with Davis-Bacon and include Project Labor Agreements as well as prevailing wage protections, we know we can count on Rosa DeLauro,” said Keith Brothers, President of the Connecticut State Building Trades and Connecticut Laborers District Council.
“It is her constant and continued commitment to our members and their families that has earned our trust and our endorsement for re-election.”
“Congresswoman Rosa DeLauro has a proven track record of fighting to improve the lives of working people, both in Connecticut and across the nation,” said Rob Baril President SEIU 1199NE.
“Congresswoman DeLauro has been a champion for SEIU members through countless struggles, and that is why SEIU is proud to endorse Congresswoman DeLauro and stand by her side in her race for re-election in 2026.”
