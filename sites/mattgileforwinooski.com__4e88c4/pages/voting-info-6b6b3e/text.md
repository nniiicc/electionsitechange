Our polling location in Winooski is the Winooski Senior Center 123 Barlow Street, Winooski, VT.
Hope to see you there!
But you don’t have to wait till voting day, early voting is open !
You can vote Mon-Fri 8am-4pm at Winooski City Hall, 27 West Allen Street Check your voter registration on the VT Secretary of State’s website .
You can register on voting day, but you can be certain your vote will count if you register before voting day.
For more information on how/where to vote you can visit Winooski’s official page on voting.
