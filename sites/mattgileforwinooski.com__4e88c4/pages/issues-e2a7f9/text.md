ICE cannot be reformed into a tool for justice.
ICE must be abolished.
I want to keep ICE out of our schools, hospitals, and government buildings.
Families should be free to get medical treatment, attend school or work, and live their lives without fear.
Act 150 provides some legal protections from ICE activity in schools and medical facilities and is a great first step.
But we must go further and ensure federal agents are held accountable and guarantee access to legal representation in immigration hearings.
Build Public Housing Housing is a human right.
As the cost of living continues to increase, the need for affordable housing becomes clearer.
All Vermonters deserve a safe place to live that doesn’t break the bank.
I support updating our zoning laws to allow for increased development.
Private developers will not save us.
We must invest in public housing at scale if we are going to address the housing crisis we are facing.
Protect Renters I want to ensure that no one is exploited because their home is someone else’s investment.
To ensure fair treatment in an unequal power dynamic, we must end no-cause evictions, provide the right to counsel in eviction proceedings, require landlords offer tenants the first chance to buy the home they live in if the landlord decides to sell, and create a rental registry to ensure our housing is safe.
Invest in Public Transit Housing must be built in coordination with an expansion of public transit.
Public Transit will make our communities more accessible and increase opportunities for those who can’t or choose not to drive.
Decreasing reliance on cars will make our Green Mountain State even greener and communities even more lovely!
Increase Labor Power Unions are the greatest tool workers have to improve our conditions.
All workers must be empowered to join in a union with their colleagues.
I believe in the rights of workers to organize and collectively bargain.
We must ensure that all workers in Vermont have the right to stand together and ensure fair, safe, and equitable working conditions.
Solidarity Forever!
Universal Health Care for Vermonters Health care is a human right.
We must implement and fund Act 48 to provide universal health care for all Vermonters.
This is the only way to address the current inequities in our system, and to avoid the impending and foreseeable crisis that will be created when the federal administration kicks tens of thousands of Vermonters off Medicaid and removes subsidies for the private insurance exchange.
Passing universal primary care is the next step to providing fully universal health care and we must begin to implement that now.
Protect Public Education Public schools are the center of our communities.
Our public schools must serve those communities and educate our children about the diversity of people in the world.
Our state government must have educators back when the federal government threatens schools for teaching about racial justice or protecting trans students.
Imposing austerity on our schools will not address issues created by spiraling health care costs and our housing crisis.
Tax the Rich We can build a Vermont where everyone is housed, where everyone has access to the health care they need, and where our public institutions and infrastructure directly address the needs of our communities.
But only if we tax the rich.
I support the Prosper Act, which would raise income tax on the top 5% and impose a surcharge unearned investment income, which is taxed well below the rate that working people pay on their income.
