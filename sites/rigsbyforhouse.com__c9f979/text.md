Patients Families Students Teachers Seniors Serving the families of our community is an honor, and one I don't take for granted. ​ I decided to run for this position because, as a local pharmacist, I was tired of seeing my customers struggle to afford the medicine they needed; being forced to choose between paying for healthcare and feeding their families. ​ Since being elected, I've helped pass legislation that increases price transparency on prescription medication, reduce the tax on groceries by half, and delivered $1.5 billion in savings for taxpayers across Alabama. ​ I would be honored to earn your trust and your vote on November 3rd, to continue delivering solutions, just like I have during this first term.
Phillip Rigsby About Phillip Phillip was born and raised in Huntsville, graduated from S.R.
Butler High School in 1994, earned his Doctor of Pharmacy from Auburn University in 2000, and purchased Huntsville Compounding Pharmacy and The Medicine Shoppe Pharmacy in 2015.
He married Pamela in 1998; she taught special education at Sparkman High School before staying home with their boys and now works Monrovia Middle School teaching 7th grade special education, and serves as secretary on the board of Next Step Farms. ​ Phillip and Pamela are proud parents of two adult boys.
Their oldest, Preston, is married to Kensley, and they have a daughter named Kennedy Grace, Phillip and Pamela's first grandchild.
Their youngest, Parker, is completing his Master's in Mechanical Engineering at Auburn University.
They are members of Gracious Savior church and are proud to call North Alabama home.
