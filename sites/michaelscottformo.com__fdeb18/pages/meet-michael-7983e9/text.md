Michael Scott
Michael Scott is a proud public servant, community leader, and advocate for opportunity, fairness, and strong communities.
He serves as Pastor and Vision Team member at The Table, a vibrant and inclusive faith community.
Known for his deep commitment to outreach, Michael works tirelessly to support families, strengthen neighborhoods, and uplift those who are often overlooked or underserved.
With a background in organizational development and leadership, Michael has created programs used in schools, businesses, and churches to inspire the next generation of changemakers.
He’s served on multiple community boards and continues to lead initiatives that promote economic growth, civic engagement, and social support.
Michael’s campaign is rooted in the belief that government should work for the people not just the powerful.
He brings a compassionate, hands-on approach to public service—listening first, showing up always, and focusing on practical solutions that help families thrive.
At home, Michael is a devoted husband and father of four—three daughters and a son welcomed into their family through adoption at age 13.
He and his family live in Lee’s Summit, where they remain deeply connected to the community.
Whether in neighborhoods, places of worship, or city halls, Michael leads with heart, always focused on building a better future for the next generation.
