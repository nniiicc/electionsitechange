A problem solver.
A 45-year-old entrepreneur, Kansas State Computer Engineering graduate, and lifelong Kansan with a 20-year track record of business growth and community service.
From his roots on a family farm to founding a technology firm serving clients throughout the US, Al brings a unique blend of technical expertise, stability, and a deep commitment to the families of District 100.
The Platform Guiding Principles 01 Limited Government Reducing administrative waste frees up resources.
Al opposes the government "picking winners and losers," believing innovative solutions are found in a free market.
02 Lower Taxes Lowering the tax burden returns power to individuals and families.
When businesses are free to grow, the entire community benefits from shared prosperity.
03 Local Control Al believes issues are best handled at the most local level possible.
Empowering families and communities prevents government overreach and avoids misaligned incentives.
The Issues Standing Firm For Our Values Protecting Life Al stands firmly for the protection of the unborn.
Every life is a gift.
He will fight to ensure our laws reflect a culture that values, protects, and supports all life from conception to natural death.
2nd Amendment The right to keep and bear arms is fundamental.
Al is an uncompromising defender of the 2nd Amendment and will oppose any legislative efforts that infringe upon the constitutional rights of Kansans.
Economic Growth Prosperity is built from the bottom up.
Al is dedicated to promoting policies that lower taxes, reduce regulatory burdens, and empower Kansas entrepreneurs.
He believes that a robust economy is the best way to create opportunities for all, ensuring Kansas remains a competitive place to live, work, and raise a family.
Engineering Mindset For over two decades, Al has built and managed a technology firm from the ground up.
In the private sector, waste isn't just a nuisance — it's a barrier to growth.
Al wants to bring that same problem-solving mindset to Topeka.
