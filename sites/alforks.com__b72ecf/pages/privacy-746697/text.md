How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect for the following purposes: To send you campaign updates, news, and information about Al Jirak's campaign for Kansas House District 100.
To contact you about volunteer opportunities and campaign events.
To understand how people find our website and improve our outreach efforts.
To measure the reach and effectiveness of our digital outreach.
To comply with applicable laws and regulations regarding campaign communications.
How We Protect Your Information We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information you provide.
Your data is stored securely and access is restricted to authorized campaign personnel only.
Sharing Your Information We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
We may share your information in the following limited circumstances: Text and Email Opt-In Data Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
Email Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your email address opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
Voter Data and Campaign Advertising Our campaign may use publicly available voter registration information and other lawfully obtained voter data for political campaign and election purposes.
This may include information such as name, mailing address, precinct, voting district, phone number, email address, and publicly available voter history information.
We may use this information to communicate with voters, identify likely supporters, organize volunteers, encourage voter registration and turnout, and deliver campaign messages through mail, phone, text, email, digital advertising, and social media platforms.
We may also use voter contact information to create custom advertising audiences on platforms such as Facebook, Instagram, Google, or other advertising services.
When we do this, the information may be hashed or otherwise processed for matching purposes, and the advertising platform may use it to help us show campaign-related messages to voters.
We use this information only for political campaign and election-related purposes and not for commercial purposes.
