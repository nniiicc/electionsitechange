About John
John Tansey is a seventh‑generation Vermonter with deep local roots.
Born and raised in Windsor, Vermont, he has always called Windsor home, even during years spent overseas.
His lifelong connection to the community shapes his love for Vermont and commitment to public service.
John and his wife, Nida, have been married for 39 years and have three children and six grandchildren.
Family is central to their lives.
All three children graduated from Windsor High School, attended college in Vermont, and now live in the state.
Their grandchildren attend Vermont public schools, reinforcing John’s belief in strong schools, safe communities, and opportunities for young people.
After graduating from Windsor High School in 1980, John earned a bachelor’s degree in Applied Economics and Management (Agricultural Economics) from Cornell University.
He later received a Master of Arts in Liberal Studies from Dartmouth College, focusing on regional economic development.
His education prepared him to understand budgets, local economies, and long‑term planning.
John’s career has centered on leadership and administration.
For more than 20 years, he has served as Executive Director of the Frank J.
Guarini Institute for International Education at Dartmouth College, working with faculty to design, manage and improve educational programs.
Earlier, he worked in research administration at Dartmouth and spent seven years overseas with the Peace Corps, serving in the Philippines, Uzbekistan, Bangladesh, and the Republic of Georgia.
For nearly 40 years, John has been active in public service.
He has served on and chaired numerous boards, including the Windsor School Board, Windsor Southeast Supervisory Union Board, Windsor Selectboard, Windsor Improvement Corporation, and the boards of trustees for Mount Ascutney Hospital and Valley Regional Hospital.
He also served on the boards of Windsor on Air and the Springfield Regional Development Corporation, and spent three years on the state Building Bright Futures Council after appointment by Governor James Douglas.
In his free time, John enjoys family activities, fishing, walking, reading, traveling with his wife, and cutting firewood.
A lifelong Boston sports fan, he played football at Cornell and later coached football in Windsor.
John is known for his honesty, hard work, and fiscal and social responsibility.
He brings proven leadership, careful financial management, and a respect for listening to others, and is ready to serve Vermont with integrity, energy, and common sense.
