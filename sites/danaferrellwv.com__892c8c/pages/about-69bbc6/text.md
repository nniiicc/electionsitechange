Skip to content Dana Ferrell WV 39th House of Delegates Home About Dana News and Events Issues Get Involved Campaign Donations About Dana Our Approach About Me I am married to Tracy Ferrell and the father of son Brooks (23) and daughter Danica (11).
We have a rescued husky and a shelter cat.
I graduated from Sissonville High and have a Master’s Degree from Marshall University.
I am a teacher and have taught in Kanawha County since 1989.
I love all sports and I have coached multiple youth teams in the community.
I was selected “Coach of the Year” by Charleston Newspapers in 2004 for high school volleyball at GWHS.
I was instrumental in bringing the WVSSAC State Volleyball Tournament to the Charleston Civic Center.
I’ve been a life-long business owner and currently operate West Virginia’s only all-sports network at RSN Sports.
I’ve been a successful real estate developer having completed one of county’s nicest subdivisions at Seneca Valley Estates.
I am a gun owner and believe every law abiding American has the right to do so.
We own an RV and love to camp and enjoy the outdoors.
I am a Fellowship of Christian Athletes (FCA) huddle coach and have taught Sunday School at both Fisher Chapel and Aldersgate United Methodist churches.
I was baptized in the Poca River at Rocky Fork.
I’ve been blessed to sit down to business with CEO’s of Panasonic and Pioneer Electronics in Chicago but spend the next Friday with a trip to the Dairy Queen in Sissonville.
