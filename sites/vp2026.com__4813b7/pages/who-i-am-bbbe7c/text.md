My life
I was born January 18th, 1996, a Thursday.
My first time working I was 8, my father had me on a roof, tearing off shingles with no safety equipment.
I have done plumbing, electrical work, Hvac, service work, roofing, pharmaceutical distribution for a Medicare company, management roles, computer repair, welding, soldiering, I even built the website you are currently on!
The list can continue until you're bored, I've been called a Jack of all Trades.
I know what a hard day's work is, and I'd like to work for you, the everyday Oklahoman, and write laws to make life better for us all, not just those at the top.
At age 11, I was invited to the National Youth Leadership conference due to my academic proficiencies, and I was given the ability to visit and learn in Washington DC, Harper's Ferry, West Virginia, as well as Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, taking extra civics courses, and learning more of the founding fathers, the government body, how it functioned, and we held mock trials for passing laws, we were taught of Storer College, how integral in the education of newly freed persons it was, and of John Brown the abolitionist, whose raid on the Confederate munitions and intention to free all enslaved with a revolt is cited as one of the main catalysts for the Civil War's actual ignition.
The lessons I learned during this time I have always carried forward.
Later on, I went to college at Cameron University, double majoring in Psychology and Computer Science; my first semester I completed with 18 credit hours, which is equivalent to a semester and a half of work and was on the Dean's honor roll.
I have lived in Lawton for most of my life, briefly living in Kansas for a summer, and Canada for another, but Lawton has always been my home, and I know Oklahoma is more than a group of Christian Nationalists, we are a diverse and loving people, and we must rise above simple squabbles and strive for a more open and free tomorrow.
