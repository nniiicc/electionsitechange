Property Tax Relief for Georgia Families
During the 2026 Legislative Session, delivering meaningful property tax relief was a top priority for Georgia House Republicans.
We were proud to support the final passage of SB 33, which provides stronger protections for homeowners across our state.
The legislation limits increases in the assessed value of homesteaded property to the rate of inflation, helping protect homeowners from sudden and unexpected increases in property tax bills.
SB 33 also created the Local Homestead Option Sales Tax (LHOST), giving local communities a new tool to use sales tax revenue to directly offset homeowners’ property taxes without raising Georgia’s current overall sales tax cap.
During the 2026 Special Session, nearly 100 local bills were introduced to give voters in communities across the state the opportunity to approve LHOST and lower their property taxes.
Unfortunately, none received the constitutionally required 120 votes for passage due to repeated opposition from House Democrats.
When we return in January, property tax relief will remain a top priority for House Republicans as we continue fighting to protect homeowners from rapidly rising property taxes and make the dream of homeownership attainable for Georgia families.
