Record-Breaking Year + Coastal Retreat
Georgia has witnessed another record-breaking year for new investment and job creation across our state, with more than 436 companies expanding their footprint in Georgia and over 25,300 new jobs created for hardworking Georgians.
These expansions and investments aren’t an accident—they are the direct result of our commitment in the General Assembly to creating opportunity and fostering an economic environment where businesses of every size can invest, grow, and succeed.
The best news is that this success isn’t concentrated just in metro Atlanta.
In fact, 75% of expansions and new business locations occurred outside of the 10-county metro Atlanta region, reaffirming our commitment to ensuring that every Georgian, regardless of their ZIP code, has access to a good-paying job and the opportunity to build a brighter future in the community they call home.
The House remains committed to building on this momentum by strengthening our workforce, investing in the infrastructure our communities need, and supporting policies that keep Georgia the best state in the nation to live, work, raise a family, and do business.
