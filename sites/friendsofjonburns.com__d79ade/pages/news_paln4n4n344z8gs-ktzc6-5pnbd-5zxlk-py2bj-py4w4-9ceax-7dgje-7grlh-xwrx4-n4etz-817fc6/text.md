Insurance Affordability Reform
During the 2026 Legislative Session, addressing rising insurance premiums was a top priority for the Georgia House.
We were proud to deliver meaningful insurance reform for Georgia’s drivers, families, and businesses with the passage of House Bill 1344.
HB 1344 increases approximately 40 fines on insurance companies when laws or policy provisions are violated during the claims process, strengthens enforcement of uninsured motorist laws, and cracks down on insurance fraud.
The bill also improves storm claim processing and enhances the Fortified Homes program to help Georgians better protect their homes from storm damage.
Coupled with last year’s historic lawsuit reform legislation, policyholders across our state are already seeing results.
More than 10 major insurers have announced and filed rate decreases, saving Georgia drivers up to 10% per insured vehicle on their auto policies and delivering millions of dollars in annual savings to hardworking families.
