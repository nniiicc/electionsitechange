Georgia Wildfire Updates
Families and communities across our state are facing devastating loss as wildfires continue to spread.
Please join me in praying for those impacted, as well as the brave firefighters and first responders on the front lines.
We are grateful for the tireless efforts of our state, local, and federal partners who are working around the clock to protect their fellow Georgians and bring these fires under control.
On Wednesday, Governor Kemp declared a state of emergency and a burn ban in 91 counties across the state, which allows for every possible state resource to be mobilized to assist with response efforts.
The Georgia Forestry Commission is working alongside the U.S.
Forest Service, the Georgia Department of Natural Resources, the Georgia Department of Agriculture, the Georgia Department of Public Safety, the Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency, the Georgia Department of Transportation, the National Guard, and local first responders to contain the fires.
Please visit https://gatrees.org/current-wildfire-information-and-resources/ to stay up-to-date on the most recent information and safety guidance from state officials.
