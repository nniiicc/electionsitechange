2026 Ballot Questions
In addition to the statewide and local officials on the ballot this November, Georgians will have the opportunity to vote on three ballot questions that will impact the future of our state.
I hope you’ll join me in voting YES on all three measures.
Learn more about each question and what you can expect to see on your ballot below:
Ballot Question #1 - Increase Acreage Limit for Farmland Conservation
Shall the Constitution of Georgia, for the purpose of protecting family farmland, be amended so as to increase the maximum acreage to qualify for assessment and taxation as a bona fide conservation use property from 2,000 acres to 4,000 acres?
If ratified, this constitutional amendment will support Georgia farmers by doubling the amount of agricultural land eligible for conservation, helping preserve family farms and protect working farmland from unchecked development.
Ballot Question #2 - Require Nonpartisan Elections for Probate Judges
Shall the Constitution of Georgia be amended so as to require that all probate court judges be elected in nonpartisan elections?
If ratified, this constitutional amendment will require Georgia probate judges to be elected on nonpartisan ballots rather than through partisan primaries, helping strengthen the integrity and impartiality of Georgia’s judicial system.
Ballot Question #3 - Authorize Creation of Next Generation 9-1-1 Fund
Shall the Constitution of Georgia be amended so as to authorize the General Assembly to dedicate certain existing revenues, including revenues reallocated by law, to a nonlapsing fund to be used exclusively for the expansion, maintenance, and operation of emergency 9-1-1 systems in this state?
If ratified, this constitutional amendment will strengthen public safety and modernize Georgia’s emergency services system, ensuring first responders have the resources they need to serve communities across our state.
