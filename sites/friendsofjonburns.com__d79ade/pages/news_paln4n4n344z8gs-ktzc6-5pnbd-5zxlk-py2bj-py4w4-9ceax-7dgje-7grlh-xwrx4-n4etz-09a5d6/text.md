This week, I joined House members and candidates in Macon, Columbus, and Atlanta as we shared our vision for Georgia’s future and the work we’re doing to keep our state the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
From strengthening economic opportunity and addressing the cost of living for hardworking families to investing in education, strengthening public safety, and fighting to lower property taxes, House Republicans have delivered real results for Georgians.
We look forward to sharing that message across the state and earning the support of Georgia voters as we head toward November.
Supporting House Republican Candidates in Macon On Monday, I joined our Middle Georgia delegation in Macon for a reception in support of Eric Wilson, Randy Peters, and Melissa Stuckey, who are running to join our work in the Georgia House.
Each of these candidates brings valuable experience and a commitment to serve the communities they hope to represent.
Middle Georgia deserves strong, conservative leadership, and we look forward to welcoming them to the House next year to champion the policies and priorities that will address affordability, expand opportunity, and strengthen the communities they are proud to call home.
Pictured with members of the Georgia General Assembly and candidates Randy Peters, Melissa Stuckey, and Eric Wilson at a reception in Macon Supporting Rep.
Carmen Rice and Rep.
Gerald Greene Middle Georgia has been blessed with incredible leaders who have worked tirelessly to strengthen education, create good-paying job opportunities, support our veterans and military community, and make life better for each of their neighbors.
That’s why I was proud to join Rep.
Carmen Rice and Rep.
Gerald Greene in Columbus on Tuesday to support their reelection to the Georgia House.
During her time in the House, Carmen Rice has supported countless measures to strengthen K-12 education for students in every corner of the state, including this year’s historic Georgia Early Literacy Act of 2026, which will place a literacy coach in every K-3 elementary school across the state.
She is also a strong proponent of policies that support our veterans and increase resources for our active-duty military families here in Georgia.
During his 44 years of service in the House, Gerald Greene has been a steadfast champion for rural communities, protecting agriculture, lowering property taxes, supporting law enforcement, and helping hardworking families keep more of what they earn.
I look forward to continuing to serve alongside these two incredible leaders as we continue advancing a strong policy agenda that ensures Georgia’s future is strong and prosperous.
