Home About Priorities Contact Donate Make Michigan More Affordable As your State Representative, I have been committed to making Michigan more affordable for all residents.
My first priority in the new session will be to reverse the Democrats’ recent income tax hike from 4.05% to 4.25%.
This rate hike has placed an unnecessary burden on hardworking families.
I will also focus on reducing energy bills and housing costs to alleviate the financial pressures many Michiganders face.
Additionally, I believe that access to affordable and local childcare options is essential for every family to thrive.
These initiatives will be at the forefront of my work as I strive to create a more prosperous and sustainable future for our state.
Prepare All Kids For The Future I am dedicated to preparing all Michigan children for a successful future.
I will advocate for a return to the basics, emphasizing real-life skills that equip students for the challenges ahead and skills our economy is seeking.
Ensuring all schools are safe and successful is critical, as every child deserves a secure and supportive learning environment.
Additionally, I will work to empower parents in their children’s education, giving them the tools and influence they need to help guide their kids toward a bright future.
These goals are central to my commitment to building a stronger, more resilient Michigan.
Government Accountability I’m committed to demanding accountable and effective government for all Michiganders.
Ethics and accountability in government are non-negotiable, and I will work tirelessly to ensure our leaders act with integrity and transparency.
I am focused on delivering better value for your tax dollars and ensuring public funds are spent wisely, not on frivolous pet projects.
Additionally, I’ll fight for transparent and fair elections, ensuring every vote is counted and every election is conducted with the highest standards.
These principles will guide my efforts to restore trust and efficiency in our state government.
Protecting Our Second Amendment Rights I strongly support our Second Amendment rights and will always fight to protect our right to bear arms.
Every law-abiding citizen has the right to defend themselves, their families, and their property.
Unfortunately, under Democratic leadership in Lansing, laws have been passed that infringe on these rights and put law enforcement at greater risk.
I will work to roll back these harmful policies and ensure that the rights of responsible gun owners are safeguarded while promoting the safety and security of our communities.
Building Safer Communities I’m dedicated to building safer communities for stronger families across Michigan.
That starts with recruiting, retaining, and supporting more police officers, which is essential to keeping our neighborhoods secure.
To defund the police is preposterous.
I will also work to eliminate sanctuary cities and counties in Michigan, ensuring our laws are upheld consistently.
Additionally, I’m committed to supporting crime victims, ensuring they receive the justice and resources they need.
Together, we can create safer communities where families can thrive.
Affordable & Reliable Energy I’m focused on ensuring Michigan has reliable energy for the future.
This means prioritizing local control and local energy production so our communities have a direct say in how their energy is generated.
I will continue to advocate for modernizing nuclear power generation, recognizing it as a vital component of a clean and dependable energy mix.
Additionally, I’ll work to expand natural gas production to ensure we have the energy resources needed to support our economy.
These steps are vital for securing Michigan’s sustainable and resilient energy future.
Attract High-Paying careers for the Future I’m committed to attracting high-paying careers for Michigan’s future by creating an environment where businesses can thrive.
We can unlock new opportunities and drive economic growth by supporting innovation and entrepreneurship.
I believe in fostering a level playing field for all, so I’ll work to stop the practice of government picking winners and losers.
I will focus on building a strong economy that benefits everyone, not just a select few.
Strengthen Our Communities I am dedicated to strengthening and empowering our communities by addressing key issues impacting rural and urban areas.
Fixing our crumbling infrastructure is a top priority to ensure safe and reliable roads, bridges, and utilities for all Michiganders.
This session, I supported a plan that allocated $1 billion to actually fixing the roads instead of paying for pork projects.
The Democrats voted this down, holding the majority in the House of Representatives.
I’m also committed to preserving our natural resources, protecting the Great Lakes, and safeguarding our farmland.
These are essential to our Thumb-area economy and our way of life.
I will work to ensure they are preserved for future generations.
Grow Our Economy I’m focused on growing Michigan’s economy by fostering an environment where businesses and career providers can succeed.
By supporting innovation and entrepreneurship, we can unlock new industries and create more opportunities for Michiganders.
Allowing the free market works; the government should not pick winners and losers through corporate handouts.
I aim to build a thriving economy that benefits everyone and encourages growth across all sectors.
Secure Michigan’s Future for ALL Generations Securing Michigan’s future for all generations is essential.
I will prioritize Michigan companies over foreign adversaries to keep our economy strong and secure.
Ensuring equal justice and upholding the rule of law are fundamental to creating a fair and just society, and I’ll work to protect these principles.
