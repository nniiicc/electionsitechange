Many people have asked me why I’m running for State Representative.
It’s simple.
I want to protect our neighborhoods from the corrupt south side politics of Mike Madigan, the Speaker of the Illinois House for nearly forty years.
Our communities have always been represented by people who were independent from political power brokers.
People that grew up here take pride in their neighborhoods and work hard to support their families.

I will work hard to be the Independent voice we all need in Springfield.
Sincerely,
