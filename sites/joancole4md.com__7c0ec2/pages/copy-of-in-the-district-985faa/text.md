The People We Serve
DISTRICT 31
District 31 includes your neighborhood, local schools, and nearby parks.
It represents face-to-face conversations, shared stories, and a community worth fighting for.
From front porch chats in Pasadena to local town halls in Glen Burnie, our campaign is grounded in showing up and listening to every corner of each neighborhood.
We are building this movement block by block, ensuring that no family from the Magothy to Curtis Creek is left behind.
We share the same local roads, school zones, and water systems, which is why working together as one strong district helps us fix our local problems and build a better future for everyone in our area.
