About Stefanie Bord
Stefanie Bord has deep roots in Northwest Houston.
She’s spent years organizing neighbors, supporting local schools, and standing up for working families.
Through her leadership as a precinct chair, Stefanie has built trust across communities and delivered real results on the ground.
She believes in responsive, inclusive representation that brings people together to solve problems.
Stefanie’s campaign is powered by the community — not special interests — and she’s ready to be a strong voice for every resident of HD-126.
Stefanie’s Fight For Safe Communities
Stefanie’s priorities reflect the real needs of our community.
She will fight to fully fund public education and support teachers, lower property taxes for homeowners, expand access to affordable healthcare, protect reproductive rights, and invest in flood control to keep families safe.
As a volunteer with Moms Demand Action, and Gun Sense Candidate who is also endorsed by Everytown, Stefanie has worked alongside parents, educators, and community leaders to advocate for common-sense gun safety measures and fight for safer schools.
She knows that every child deserves to learn without fear, and she’ll bring that same commitment to the Texas Legislature.
Stefanie believes that government should work for everyone — and she’s committed to delivering practical solutions that make life better for families across House District 126.
