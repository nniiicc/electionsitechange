About half of Texas House District 126 is in US Congressional District 38 (CD38), with Democratic Nominee Melissa McDonough, (currently represented by Rep.
Wesley Hunt), and half is in US Congressional District 2 (CD2), with Democratic Nominee Shaun Finnie (currently represented by Rep.
Dan Crenshaw).
There is a teeny tiny bit of Texas House District 126 in CD29, currently represented by Democratic Nominee and incumbent Rep.
Sylvia Garcia.
Redistricting of Texas’ Congressional Districts, (Texas House Districts were not changed), means you may be in a new district this election!
