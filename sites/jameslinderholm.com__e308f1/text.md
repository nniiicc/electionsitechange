A Business Leader for House District 20 James Linderholm has spent over three decades serving the families of Huntsville and the Tennessee Valley.
Born in Illinois to a family of educators, he grew up in England, studied law at Oxford and practiced in London then Birmingham, AL, before moving to Huntsville in 1991 to pursue a career as a professional sports coach.
He has built Huntsville Gymnastics into one of the top training facilities in the region, with athletes at all levels right up to NCAA Div.
1, and has expanded it to Madison and Hampton Cove.
He has volunteered on state and regional committees and is a National Instructor for USA Gymnastics.
With his legal background and the proven track record of building a business from the ground up, James brings a unique blend of executive experience and advocacy to the job of representing Huntsville and North Alabama.
His campaign is rooted in a simple promise: to listen, to find solutions, and to get results.
