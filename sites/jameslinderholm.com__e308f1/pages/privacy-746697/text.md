Last updated October 13, 2026 What we collect Volunteer form: your name, mobile phone, email, ZIP code, how you'd like to help, when you're free, any notes you add, and whether you agreed to receive text messages.
Yard sign form: your name, delivery address, phone, email, sign size, and any notes.
Where you came from: if you arrived from one of our QR codes, texts, or links, we note which one (for example "door-hanger QR code") so we know which outreach works.
Why we collect it To contact you about volunteering, deliver yard signs, and keep you updated on the campaign.
That's it.
Who sees it Only campaign staff and volunteer coordinators of Linderholm for District 20.
Form submissions are handled by our form provider, which processes them only on our behalf.
Donations are handled entirely by our donation processor, on its own secure page and under its own privacy policy.
We never sell your data We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.
Text messages If you check the text-message box, we may send you campaign texts about volunteer shifts, events, and voting.
Message frequency varies.

Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.
Your consent is not shared with third parties for marketing.
Keeping it safe This website stores no personal information itself.
Forms are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our form provider.
