Education, Workforce, and the Future of Our District
Our schools and workforce systems must prepare students for opportunity — whether that means college, skilled trades, or entrepreneurship.
In Assembly District 113, workforce alignment is critical.
Local employers need skilled workers.
Families need accessible childcare.
Schools need predictable funding.
Education policy in Albany influences:
- School aid formulas
- Charter and vocational programming
- Workforce training grants
- Early childhood education access
I support:
- Strong, stable school funding
- Expansion of career and technical education
- Apprenticeship programs aligned with local industry
- Parental engagement in education
- Accountability in higher education spending
We must ensure that students graduate ready to succeed — and ready to stay in New York if they choose.
