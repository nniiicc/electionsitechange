MEET ALLEN
Allen Caruso is a small business owner, job creator, and lifelong Upstate New Yorker running for New York State Assembly in District 113.
Born in Plattsburgh and raised in Saratoga, Allen understands firsthand the values that define our region — hard work, family, independence, and community.
- Small Business Owner who operates a health-conscious restaurant and supports his workforce.
- Community Advocate who fights for children & families and victims of abuse as a supporter of organizations like Wellspring and Shelters of Saratoga.
- Real Estate Professional who helps low-income families repair and improve their homes.
- Supporter of Saratoga Bridges, an organization that provide support for individuals with disabilities.
- Member of the Lions Club where he works to combat hunger for vulnerable local families.
- B.S. degree in Business Administration from SUNY Plattsburgh
- Endorsed by small businesses, union members, firefighters, veterans, nurses, teachers, police officers, school bus drivers, clergy, and so many more!
Allen believes Albany has made it too expensive to live, work, and own a business in New York.
Families are stretched thin.
Small businesses are struggling to make payroll.
Young people are leaving the state.
He’s running to change that.
