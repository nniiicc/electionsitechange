GOVERNOR GEORGE E.
PATAKI ENDORSES ALLEN CARUSO FOR NEW YORK STATE ASSEMBLY IN 113TH DISTRICT
SARATOGA SPRINGS, NY, Sept. 14, 2026 — Former New York State Governor George E.
Pataki today announced his official endorsement of small business owner and community leader Allen Caruso for the New York State Assembly in the 113th District, which encompasses portions of Saratoga, Warren, and Washington counties.
Three-term Governor Pataki cited Caruso’s commitment to restoring fiscal discipline, advancing commonsense environmental solutions, and most importantly making New York affordable for families, seniors, and small business owners as key reasons for his support.
"Allen Caruso represents the exact kind of common-sense leadership New York needs in Albany today," said former Governor George E.
Pataki.
"He understands that hardworking New Yorkers need the price of everyday necessities to come down, real property tax relief, and practical policies that protect our environment without destroying our economy.
Allen’s affordability agenda will deliver meaningful results for working families, and I am proud to stand with him in this crucial race."
Caruso’s campaign platform emphasizes key legislative priorities designed to lower the cost of living and strengthen local communities:
- Affordable Living & Senior Relief: Eliminating property taxes for seniors—ensuring primary residences remain safe havens for retirees—and calling for an independent audit of state government to cut taxes and spending by 30 percent.
- Ending Illegal Immigrant & Corporate Welfare: Putting a stop to billions in free programs for illegal immigrants and tax credits for the film industry.
- Commonsense Environmental Policies: Repealing the state’s costly and impractical electric school bus and car mandates and pursuing commonsense policies that protect our environment and our freedoms.
- Public Safety: Supporting tough, effective laws that protect neighborhoods and put criminals where they belong - behind bars.
In addition to his legislative platform, Caruso brings a broad background in business, community service, and local advocacy:
- Small Business Owner: Operates a health-conscious local restaurant in Saratoga Springs and focuses on creating quality jobs and supporting his workforce.
- Dedicated Community Advocate: Supports local non-profit and community organizations, including Wellspring, Shelters of Saratoga, Saratoga Bridges, and the Lions Club, where he works to combat hunger for vulnerable families and advocate for victims of abuse and individuals with disabilities.
- Real Estate Professional: Works with low-income families to help repair and improve their homes.
- Broad Coalition Support: Earned endorsements from local small businesses, union members, firefighters, police officers, veterans, nurses, teachers, school bus drivers, and clergy.
- Education: Holds a B.S. degree in Business Administration from SUNY Plattsburgh.
"Receiving the endorsement of Governor Pataki—a leader who made New York more affordable for working families, protected our environment, and championed commonsense governance—is a true honor," said Allen Caruso."Families across Saratoga, Warren, and Washington counties are struggling under rising costs, oppressive property taxes, and burdensome state environmental mandates.
I look forward to bringing a small businessman’s approach to Albany that will result in eliminating property taxes for seniors, repealing impractical electric mandates, and reducing the cost of state government by thirty percent."
Caruso is running on the Republican, Conservative, and Vote Affordable party lines.
The general election for New York State Assembly District 113 will take place on Tuesday, November 3, 2026, with Early Voting running from October 24 through November 1.
