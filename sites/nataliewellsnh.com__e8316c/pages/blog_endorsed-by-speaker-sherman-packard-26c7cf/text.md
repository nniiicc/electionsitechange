I had the honor of serving with Natalie in the New Hampshire House during 2021-2022 term when I served as Speaker.
Natalie was a principled, hardworking member of our Republican majority who showed up, did the work, and never forgot who she was there to represent.
Natalie is a conservative who understands what matters most to Granite Staters: safety, prosperity, and freedom.
She has been a strong voice for fiscal responsibility, parental rights, and protecting our New Hampshire values.
In Concord, she was someone I could count on to stand for limited government and common-sense solutions, and to respect the institution of the House while fighting for her district.
We need proven, principled Republicans like Natalie Wells back in Concord.
I ask the voters of Bradford, Henniker and Warner to join me in supporting Natalie Wells for State Representative.
