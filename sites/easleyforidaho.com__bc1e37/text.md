Proud to be endorsed by: Building a Contrast for Our Future The current majority leadership representing District 22 holds influential positions as the sponsor and co-sponsor of Idaho’s school voucher legislation.
Occupying key Senate and House Majority leadership roles, these representatives benefit from significant financial backing that supports a specific policy agenda focused on private school subsidies.
This race presents a clear contrast for our community.
While the current leadership continues to push for vouchers that redirect public funds and into private institutions, Amanda and Karlie Easley are focused on a different priority: strengthening our public schools and serving everyday families.
We believe that District 22 deserves representation that prioritizes the local classrooms and healthcare services that form the backbone of our district.
View our Platform Returning Balanced Leadership to Idaho The rising tide of extremism within our government does more than divide—it prevents us from building a thriving Idaho.
Our goal is to steer state leadership back to its fundamental duty: providing effective results for every citizen.
We seek an organized governance model that puts local schools, health services, and smart spending ahead of partisan theater.
Let's move past the division and serve the people.
We’re committed to steady, common-sense leadership that protects your freedoms without the noise of extremism.
By strengthening education and healthcare, we can build a future rooted in integrity, opportunity, and respect for every community.
Karlie Easley is a hospice nurse, a mother, and a fourth-generation Idahoan running to bring common-sense leadership back to the statehouse.
After years of caring for Treasure Valley families in both emergency and hospice settings, she knows firsthand the challenges Idahoans face.
Karlie is focused on what matters—strong schools, reliable infrastructure, and accessible healthcare—and ensuring government works for everyone.
About Karlie Learn More About Karlie About Amanda Amanda Easley is a small business owner with a passion for solving real-world problems.
Raised by a Vietnam veteran and retired police officer and a public school teacher, Amanda was instilled with a deep respect for service and community.
She began her career as a veterinary technician and has helped companies across the animal health industry turn strategy into action.
Amanda lives in Idaho with her wife, Karlie, their son, and their pets and is running to bring practical, solutions-focused leadership to the issues that matter most—strong schools, accessible healthcare, and thriving communities.
