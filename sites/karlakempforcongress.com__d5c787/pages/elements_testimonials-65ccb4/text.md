FLORIDA DEMOCRATIC AGRICULTURAL CAUCUS
ENDORSES
KARLA KEMP FOR FLORIDA SENATE, DISTRICT 18
LABELLE, FL — Today, the Florida Democratic Agricultural Caucus (FDAC) announced its endorsement of Karla Kemp for Florida Senate, District 18, ahead of the November 3 general election.
The Florida Democratic Agricultural Caucus (FDAC) endorsed Karla Kemp based on her responses to a detailed FDAC questionnaire addressing a range of Florida agricultural issues, including rural communities; food security; farm labor; agricultural land preservation; environmental stewardship; climate resilience; invasive pests and diseases; and agricultural research and innovation.
“Karla Kemp understands the statewide significance of Florida agriculture and rural issues, particularly the need to keep the food we buy affordable and healthy, ensure that families can earn a proper living from their farms, preserve agricultural lands against urban sprawl, and improve our farms and ranches so they are environmentally sustainable.
Harmful algae blooms and red tides we see on our beaches and waterways are caused by nutrient pollution from multiple sources, particularly from our farmlands and urban areas.
We must make dramatic progress on both fronts if we want to improve and preserve our farms and our environment,” said Dr.
John Capece, FDAC President.
“Karla understands how the decisions of the Florida Legislature directly impact food costs and farm economics.
Those realities are the pillars of our food security.
She is also sensitive to the fact that agriculture affects water quality, climate change, and the natural habitats outside our cities.”
“The Florida Democratic Agricultural Caucus is proud to endorse Karla Kemp for Florida Senate, District 18,” Capece said.
“We believe she reflects the rational, honest approach to governance Florida so desperately needs right now.”
Florida Senate District 18 covers part of Pinellas County in the Tampa Bay area.
About FDAC
The Florida Democratic Agricultural Caucus works within the Florida Democratic Party to recruit, strengthen, and inform candidates and voters in support of affordable food prices, healthy food supplies, farmers, rural communities, and an environmentally sustainable food system.
FDAC endorses candidates it judges qualified to serve, as evidenced by their record of prior service, and in the absence of such a record, based on interviews and responses to questionnaires.
