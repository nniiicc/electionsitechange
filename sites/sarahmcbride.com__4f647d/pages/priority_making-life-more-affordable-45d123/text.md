Making Life More Affordable
The American Dream is unaffordable and inaccessible for too many.
Delawareans across our state are struggling under rising costs and insufficient wages.
Too many families are working harder than ever but still finding it harder to afford a home, put food on the table, save for the future, or simply get ahead.
I believe our economy should reward work, not just wealth.
That’s why I’m fighting to lower costs for working families and make it easier for people to build a good life here in Delaware.
That means increasing the supply of housing so more families can afford to buy or rent a home, taking on corporations driving up everyday costs, and supporting tax relief that puts more money back into the pockets of working- and middle-class families, not billionaires.
I oppose this administration’s across-the-board tariffs that raise prices on everything from groceries to household goods.
These policies act as a hidden tax on working families and small businesses, making it harder for them to make ends meet and compete.
I also opposed the so-called “One Big Beautiful Bill” because it prioritized massive tax breaks for the wealthiest Americans while paying for them by taking food assistance and ripping healthcare away from Delawareans.
At a time when too many people are already struggling, we should be expanding opportunity, not making it harder to afford the basics.
I’ll always work to lower costs, create more good-paying jobs, protect programs that help families succeed, and build an economy where everyone who works hard has a fair shot at achieving the American Dream.
In Congress, I’ve:
- Cosponsored the American Affordability Act, comprehensive legislation to lower the cost of living by reducing housing, childcare, healthcare, energy, and education costs for working families.
The bill expands affordable housing by increasing the Low-Income Housing Tax Credit, creating new incentives to convert vacant commercial buildings into homes, and providing tax relief for first-time homebuyers and renters.
It restores clean energy incentives to lower utility bills, enhances the Child Tax Credit and childcare tax credits, makes health insurance more affordable by expanding premium assistance, and helps lower the cost of higher education through expanded tax credits and student loan relief.
- Cosponsored the Prevent Tariff Abuse Act, to rein in President Trump’s reckless use of tariffs that have driven up prices for American families and businesses.
The bill would require congressional approval before a president can impose tariffs under the guise of a national emergency, helping prevent unnecessary price increases on everyday goods and reduce costs for consumers.
- Backed the bipartisan 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, which became law, to increase the supply of affordable housing, lower housing costs, expand homeownership opportunities, and strengthen federal housing programs.
The law helps communities build more homes, supports innovative housing solutions, expands financing for affordable housing, strengthens protections for renters, and improves efforts to prevent and end homelessness.
- Cosponsored the Restoring Food Security for American Families and Farmers Act, to reverse harmful SNAP cuts made in congressional Republicans’ Big Ugly Bill, restore nutrition assistance for vulnerable families, and strengthen local food systems.
- Backed the Billionaires Income Tax Act, to ensure the wealthiest Americans pay their fair share by requiring billionaires to pay taxes on the growth of their publicly traded assets, helping create a fairer tax system and generate revenue for public investments.
