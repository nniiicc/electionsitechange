I am proud to have helped ban assault weapons in Delaware and pass the most significant gun safety package in the state’s history.
In Congress, I have supported efforts to strengthen background checks, ban assault weapons, and institute safeguards to prevent dangerous individuals from getting guns.
In Congress, I’ve: Backed the Bipartisan Background Checks Act , to expand background checks to nearly all firearm sales, helping keep guns out of the hands of people who are prohibited from possessing them while protecting responsible gun ownership.
Supported Ethan’s Law , to help prevent accidental shootings by encouraging the safe storage of firearms and establishing secure storage requirements when children could access a gun.
Cosponsored the Assault Weapons Ban Act , to prohibit the sale, manufacture, transfer, and importation of military-style assault weapons, helping reduce the risk of mass shootings and make communities safer.
