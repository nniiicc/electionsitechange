Workers’ Rights & Strong Unions
Every worker deserves a living wage, basic protections, good benefits, and the support necessary for them to thrive.
That is why, when I ran for Congress in 2024, I promised I’d be one of the first members to sign onto the PRO Act and fight for worker protections.
Because the backbone of the American middle class is the right to organize and form strong unions.
For 40 years, Congressional Republicans have sought to weaken federal protections for workers, undermine the enforcement of national labor laws, and strip workers of their right to organize.
In order to restore the American middle class, we must do more to support unions and empower workers.
That means raising the federal minimum wage to at least $15/hour and setting it to rise with inflation.
It also means ensuring that workers are able to use the leave that they’ve earned in ways that work best for them and their families.
Just as I did in the State Senate, I have continued working arm in arm with unions to enhance protections for the right to organize, fully fund agencies that look out for workers, and ensure that as new industries and economies emerge, they are powered by a unionized workforce.
I will also fight to codify President Biden’s executive order that federal projects are contracted with unionized workforces.
I will continue to push back, using my vote in the House, on this administration’s attack on public sector unions.
In Congress, I’ve:
- I am an original cosponsor of the Protecting the Right to Organize (PRO) Act to strengthen workers’ right to organize, bargain collectively for better pay and benefits, and hold employers accountable when they violate workers’ rights.
- I introduced the Striking and Locked Out Workers Health Care Protection Act to ensure workers and their families cannot lose their health insurance simply because they are exercising their right to strike or are locked out during a labor dispute.
- I introduced the Holiday Pay Act to guarantee workers receive overtime pay when they work on federal holidays, ensuring they are fairly compensated for giving up time with their families and loved ones.
- Authored the bipartisan FAIR Leave Act and Flexible Leave Act, to modernize the Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) by expanding workers’ access to leave and providing greater flexibility to care for themselves and their loved ones.
Together, these bills eliminate outdated restrictions on leave for spouses who work for the same employer and allow workers to use FMLA leave intermittently, better reflecting the realities of modern work and family life.
- Delivered federal dollars to projects like the Delaware Container Terminal to create good-paying union jobs.
