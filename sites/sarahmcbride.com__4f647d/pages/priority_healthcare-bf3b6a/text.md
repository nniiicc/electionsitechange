Healthcare
Healthcare is a human right.
In the wealthiest nation on earth, any person left without health coverage is a moral failing.
I support policies that advance our country toward the ultimate goal of universal coverage.
At a minimum, we should expand Medicare to cover dental, vision, and hearing while lowering the age of eligibility.
I support Medicare for all of our neighbors who want it.
The Big Ugly Bill made the largest cuts to Medicaid in American history.
Rubber-stamped by Congressional Republicans, these cuts threaten the healthcare safety net that thousands of Delawareans and millions of Americans rely on.
The consequences will be felt far beyond those who lose coverage.
Hospitals and providers are facing more uncompensated care, emergency rooms are becoming even more overcrowded as people delay treatment, and an already strained healthcare workforce is facing even greater pressure.
I will continue to fight to protect Medicaid from the administration’s cruel agenda.
From nurses and physician assistants to dentists and physicians, healthcare providers are the backbone of our healthcare system.
Everyone should be able to access a quality provider near them, regardless of whether you live in a small town in Sussex or just north of Wilmington.
Too often, I hear from patients across Delaware that waiting times for essential care are simply too long.
At the same time, our healthcare workforce is being pushed to the brink, caring for more patients with fewer resources while facing burnout and staffing shortages.
As I have successfully done in the State Senate, I will continue to support our healthcare workforce by bringing federal investments to Delaware for education and career support.
In Congress, I’ve:
- Cosponsored the Medicare Dental, Vision, and Hearing Benefit Act, to expand Medicare by covering dental, vision, and hearing care, ensuring seniors can access the comprehensive healthcare they need.
- Supported the CONNECT for Health Act, to permanently expand access to telehealth by allowing more patients to receive care remotely, increasing access to providers, and improving access to mental health services.
- Cosponsored the Protecting Health Care and Lowering Costs Act, to reverse the healthcare and Medicaid cuts included in the Big Ugly Bill and permanently extend Affordable Care Act premium tax credits after Congressional Republicans allowed them to expire, helping keep health coverage affordable for millions of Americans.
