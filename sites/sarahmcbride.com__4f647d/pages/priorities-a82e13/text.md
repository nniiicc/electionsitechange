Priorities
Delaware is a small state, but when we work together, we make a big impact.
While I was a State Senator, we passed paid family and medical leave, raised the minimum wage, enhanced access to healthcare, expanded mental healthcare support, and focused on the issues that shape people’s day-to-day lives.
In Congress, I’ve worked through bipartisan gridlock to deliver federal investments that strengthen our communities: improving infrastructure, expanding affordable housing opportunities, supporting good-paying jobs and workforce training, investing in climate resilience along our coast, enhancing public safety, and bolstering our rural communities.
When the current administration took actions that threatened Delaware families and workers, I fought back — and won.
I successfully led bipartisan efforts to reverse harmful cuts to the Delaware Manufacturing Extension Partnership, protecting jobs and manufacturers across our state, and to preserve nearly $1 billion for home healthcare after proposed Medicare payment cuts threatened seniors’ access to care.
This progress is driven by the everyday Delawareans I hear from when I travel across our state — from the single mom in Claymont to the healthcare worker in Dover to the farmer in Ellendale — who are worried about not being able to afford groceries, gas, healthcare, housing, and energy bills.
These are the things keeping our neighbors up at night — and I hear you.
It’s simply not right that working people are paying the price for misguided policies that are causing costs to skyrocket.
As a state of neighbors, we know the way to get things done is by rising above partisan politics to find lasting solutions that strengthen our state.
By listening first, leading with compassion, and staying rooted in our shared values, we can build on the progress we’ve made so far.
Because when we put the focus back onto working people, not the wealthiest among us, we create an economy that works for all Delawareans — and that’s when we all thrive.
We’re just getting started, and I hope you’ll join me in continuing that work to put Delawareans’ voices front and center in Washington.
