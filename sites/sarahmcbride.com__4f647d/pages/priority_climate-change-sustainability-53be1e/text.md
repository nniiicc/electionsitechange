Climate change poses an urgent threat to both our planet and our home, Delaware, the nation’s lowest-lying state.
The transformational Inflation Reduction Act was the single largest investment in green infrastructure and the most profound attempt to rescue our climate in history.
But the current President’s efforts to dismantle those investments have driven up energy costs for families, threatened good-paying clean energy jobs, and put our climate and economy at risk.
Building on that progress must remain a top priority.
We must increase investment in renewable energy, make green technologies affordable and accessible for consumers and businesses, and ensure that the sustainable industries of the future employ a local and unionized workforce.
In order to achieve net-zero emissions as soon as possible, the United States must demonstrate commitment to both domestic action and global investment.
In Congress, I’ve: Cosponsored the Energy Bills Relief Act , comprehensive legislation to lower household energy costs by expanding access to affordable clean energy, strengthening the electric grid, and helping families reduce their utility bills.
The bill restores clean energy tax credits, expands programs such as LIHEAP and the Weatherization Assistance Program, invests in grid modernization and community solar, and streamlines permitting to speed the delivery of reliable, lower-cost energy projects.
Cosponsored the Delaware River Basin Restoration Program Reauthorization Act , to reauthorize the Delaware River Basin Restoration Program through 2032 and continue supporting projects that improve water quality, restore wildlife habitat, reduce flooding, and strengthen the health of communities throughout the Delaware River watershed.
Cosponsored the Defend Our Coast Act to prohibit leasing for exploration, development, or production of oil or gas in the mid-Atlantic.
Delivered millions of dollars in Community Project Funding to support sand replenishment at Delaware’s Beaches.
