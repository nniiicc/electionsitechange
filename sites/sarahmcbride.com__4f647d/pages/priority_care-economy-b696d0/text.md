Care Economy
The cost and availability of care affect nearly every family in Delaware.
Whether it’s finding affordable childcare, taking time away from work to care for a new baby or a loved one, or helping an aging parent or a person with a disability remain safely at home, too many families are forced to make impossible choices because care is too expensive or simply unavailable.
Building a stronger care economy means making childcare universal and affordable, guaranteeing paid family and medical leave, supporting family caregivers, and ensuring older adults and people with disabilities can access high-quality care.
These investments strengthen families, support workers, grow our economy, and make life more affordable.
That’s why House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries appointed me to co-chair the House Democratic Caregiving Working Group, and why I serve as Chair of the New Democrat Coalition’s Care Economy Task Force.
In these leadership roles, I’m bringing together lawmakers, caregivers, businesses, providers, advocates, labor leaders, and employers to advance practical and bold solutions that expand access to childcare, paid leave, home care, and long-term care.
I’ll continue fighting to build a bold care economy that gives every Delaware family the support they need to care for their loved ones without sacrificing their financial security.
In Congress, I’ve:
- Built support for the FAMILY Act, to create a permanent national paid family and medical leave program that guarantees up to 12 weeks of paid leave for workers facing major life events.
The bill ensures workers can take time to welcome a new child, recover from a serious illness, care for a loved one, respond to a family member’s military deployment, or escape domestic violence without having to choose between their family, their health, and a paycheck.
- Backed landmark legislation to deliver universal access to affordable, high-quality child care and early learning, including the Child Care for Every Community Act and the Child Care for Working Families Act.
Together, these bills would cap child care costs, ensure many families pay no more than $10 a day for care, expand access to Pre-K and Head Start, and invest in the child care workforce to improve quality, raise wages, and expand access for families.
- Cosponsored the American Family Act, to permanently expand the Child Tax Credit and provide monthly payments that help families afford essentials like food, housing, and child care.
The bill would increase the value of the credit, make it fully available to low-income families, and build on the successful 2021 expansion that helped lift millions of children out of poverty.
- Led a bipartisan effort to protect seniors’ access to home healthcare, successfully pushing the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) to scale back President Trump’s proposed Medicare payment cuts that would have threatened care for older Americans.
Working across the aisle, I helped preserve an estimated $915 million for home health providers, protecting in-home care that helps seniors recover safely at home, avoid unnecessary hospitalizations, and maintain their independence.
