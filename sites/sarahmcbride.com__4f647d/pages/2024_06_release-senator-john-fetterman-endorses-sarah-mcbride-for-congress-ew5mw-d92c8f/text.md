Governor John Carney Endorses Sarah McBride for Congress
Wilmington, DE — Today, Delaware Governor John Carney, Delaware’s former member of Congress, announced his support of State Senator Sarah McBride’s campaign for Delaware’s open Congressional seat.
In the week following the news that Sarah McBride is the lone Democratic candidate for Congress, Delaware Democrats across the state have coalesced around McBride’s candidacy.
“Sarah McBride has been a strong advocate for all Delawareans.
During my time as Governor, I’ve worked with Senator McBride on paid family and medical leave, gun safety legislation, and efforts to remediate lead to keep Delaware children healthy,” Governor Carney said in a statement announcing his support.
“She is a hardworking public servant who cares about the details and the impact that legislation can have on folks up and down the state.
Sarah will be an effective voice in the U.S.
House of Representatives, and I am pleased to share my support for her.”
In response to the announcement, Senator Sarah McBride said, “I am honored to have the support of our Governor and former member of Congress, John Carney.
Under the Governor’s leadership in Delaware, we’ve been able to deliver on key Democratic priorities, like paid family and medical leave and the most significant gun safety package in our state’s history.
Governor Carney has dedicated his life to public service on behalf of all Delawareans and I’m so grateful to have his support in my campaign to represent Delaware in the U.S.
House.”
Since launching her campaign a year ago, McBride has built a campaign that has made her the frontrunner for the seat by earning a wide coalition of endorsements, beating fundraising records, and leading a grassroots organizing operation that has reached thousands of Delaware voters.
McBride has been endorsed by more than twenty Delaware unions, eight Delaware statewide elected officials, dozens of her colleagues in the General Assembly and counting, community organizers, and national advocacy organizations.
She has also been endorsed by Democratic leadership in the U.S.
House, as well as caucuses representing nearly every Democrat in the U.S.
House of Representatives, including both the Congressional Progressive Caucus and the moderate NewDems Coalition caucus.

ABOUT SARAH MCBRIDE
Sarah McBride represents roughly 50,000 Delawareans in the First State Senate District, which includes parts of Wilmington and Brandywine Hundred.
Sarah grew up in Wilmington and has been advocating for her community for decades.
She worked for former Governor Jack Markell, the late Attorney General Beau Biden, and served in the Obama White House.
Most recently, she served as the national spokesperson for the Human Rights Campaign, the nation’s largest LGBTQ equal rights organization.
When McBride was elected in November 2020, she became the first openly transgender state senator in American history.
In just her first term, McBride passed the landmark Healthy Delaware Families Act, providing paid family and medical leave to workers throughout the First State and marking the largest expansion of Delaware’s social safety net in decades.
She also passed legislation expanding access to health care, requiring mental health and media literacy education in public schools, promoting green technologies, preventing lead poisoning in youth, and protecting workers and families.
McBride currently serves as chair of the Senate Health & Social Services Committee and is a member of the Senate’s Judiciary Committee, Education Committee, Banking, Business, Insurance, and Technology Committee, and Executive Committee.
Sarah married her late husband Andrew Cray in 2014 and is the proud aunt of seven.
She is a graduate of Cab Calloway School of the Arts and American University.
McBride has taught public policy at the University of Delaware and is the author of the 2018 memoir, “Tomorrow Will Be Different,” which includes a foreword from President Joe Biden.
