U.S.
Senator Chris Coons Endorses Sarah McBride for Congress
Wilmington, DE — Today, Delaware’s U.S.
Senator Chris Coons announced his endorsement of State Senator Sarah McBride for Congress.
This announcement marks the news that every statewide elected official in Delaware has endorsed Sarah McBride for Congress.
“I’m thrilled to join Senator Carper and Congresswoman Blunt Rochester in endorsing Sarah McBride to be our next member of the congressional delegation,” said Senator Coons in a statement.
“Sarah has been my state senator for the past four years, where she passed paid family and medical leave, raised the minimum wage, and ensured that Delaware is a safe haven for reproductive health care.
“Sarah has accomplished all this by embracing bipartisan and collaborative leadership in the General Assembly.
Nearly every piece of legislation Sarah has drafted, introduced, and passed has gained bipartisan support.”
“It’s critical that we send proven leaders to Washington.
We need our next member of Congress to deliver real results for our state.
Sarah is that proven leader and that’s why I’m proud to endorse her for Congress.”
In response to the announcement, Senator McBride said, “Senator Coons is a tireless and effective voice on behalf of Delawareans and I couldn’t be more thrilled to have his support in this campaign.
In Congress, we’ll work together to deliver for all Delawareans, to bring down costs facing families, expand access to health care and child care, and defend reproductive freedom.
I am honored to have the endorsement of Delaware’s full federal delegation as I run to join them in Congress.”
Since launching her campaign a year ago, McBride has built a wide coalition of endorsers, set fundraising records in Delaware, and created a grassroots organizing operation that has reached thousands of Delaware voters.
McBride has been endorsed by more than twenty Delaware unions, five Delaware statewide elected officials, 23 of her colleagues in the General Assembly and counting, community organizers, and national advocacy organizations.
She has also been endorsed by caucuses representing nearly every Democrat in the U.S.
House of Representatives, including both the Congressional Progressive Caucus and the moderate NewDems Coalition caucus.

ABOUT SARAH MCBRIDE
Sarah McBride represents roughly 50,000 Delawareans in the First State Senate District, which includes parts of Wilmington and Brandywine Hundred.
Sarah grew up in Wilmington and has been advocating for her home state for decades.
She worked for former Governor Jack Markell, the late Attorney General Beau Biden, and served in the Obama White House.
Most recently, she served as the national spokesperson for the Human Rights Campaign, the nation’s largest LGBTQ equal rights organization.
When McBride was elected in November 2020, she became the first openly transgender state senator in American history.
In just her first term, McBride passed the landmark Healthy Delaware Families Act, providing paid family and medical leave to workers throughout the First State and marking the largest expansion of Delaware’s social safety net in decades.
She also passed legislation expanding access to health care, requiring mental health and media literacy education in public schools, promoting green technologies, preventing lead poisoning in youth, and protecting workers and families.
McBride currently serves as chair of the Senate Health & Social Services Committee and is a member of the Senate’s Judiciary Committee, Education Committee, Banking, Business, Insurance, and Technology Committee, and Executive Committee.
Sarah married her late husband Andrew Cray in 2014 and is the proud aunt of seven.
She is a graduate of Cab Calloway School of the Arts and American University.
McBride has taught public policy at the University of Delaware and is the author of the 2018 memoir, “Tomorrow Will Be Different,” which includes a foreword from President Joe Biden.
