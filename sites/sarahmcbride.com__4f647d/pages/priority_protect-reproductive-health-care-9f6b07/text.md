Protect Reproductive Health Care
As MAGA extremists continue to criminalize abortion, I will fight to protect the private relationship between women and their doctors.
Everyone deserves access to comprehensive, culturally competent reproductive and maternal healthcare.
In my time in the State Senate, I proudly worked to make Delaware a safe haven for reproductive care.
And now, in Congress, I have continued the fight for reproductive freedom for all.
In 2022, I helped expand Medicaid coverage for postpartum care in Delaware, and I’ll continue these efforts in Congress to protect access to the full range of reproductive healthcare, strengthen maternal healthcare, and address the unacceptable maternal health disparities that disproportionately affect women of color.
In Congress, I’ve:
- Cosponsored the Women’s Health Protection Act, to codify the protections of Roe v.
Wade into federal law by establishing a nationwide statutory right to provide and receive abortion care free from medically unnecessary restrictions.
- Supported the Ensuring Women’s Right to Reproductive Freedom Act, to protect the right to travel across state lines for abortion care and prevent states from interfering with patients or those helping them access care.
- Cosponsored the Right to IVF Act, to protect the right to access fertility treatments and ensure insurance coverage for assisted reproductive technologies.
