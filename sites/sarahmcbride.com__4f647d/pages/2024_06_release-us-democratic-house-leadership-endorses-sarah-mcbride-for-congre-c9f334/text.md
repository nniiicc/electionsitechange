Democratic House Leadership Endorses Sarah McBride for Congress June 17, 2024 Sarah McBride receives the endorsement of the highest-ranking Democratic leaders Wilmington, DE — Today, House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries, House Democratic Whip Katherine Clark & House Democratic Chair Pete Aguilar announced their support of State Senator Sarah McBride’s campaign for Delaware’s open Congressional seat.
The joint statement on the endorsement said , “As Leaders of the House Democratic Caucus, we are honored to announce our strong endorsement of State Senator Sarah McBride for Delaware’s open Congressional seat.
Sarah is ready to deliver for the people of Delaware.
She is a proven leader who will fight for affordable health care, accessible child care, and our friends in organized labor.
“We stand with Sarah McBride and could not be more excited to work with her this year.” In response to the announcement, Senator Sarah McBride said , “I am deeply honored to receive the support of the leadership of the Democratic Caucus.
In a time of divisiveness, the Democratic leaders of the House have remained steadfast in their commitment to the values of our party and delivering for working families across the nation.
I could not be more excited to get to DC to serve in a Democratic majority come 2025 with future-Speaker Jeffries, future-Majority Leader Clark, and future-Majority Whip Aguilar.” This follows Delaware’s Congresswoman Lisa Blunt Rochester endorsing McBride to succeed her last week.
Since launching her campaign #ago, McBride has built a campaign that has made her the frontrunner for the seat by earning a wide coalition of endorsements, beating fundraising records, and leading a grassroots organizing operation that has reached thousands of Delaware voters.
McBride has been endorsed by more than twenty Delaware unions, five Delaware statewide elected officials, 23 of her colleagues in the General Assembly and counting, community organizers, and national advocacy organizations.
She has also been endorsed by caucuses representing nearly every Democrat in the U.S.
House of Representatives, including both the Congressional Progressive Caucus and the moderate NewDems Coalition caucus.

Sarah grew up in Wilmington and has been advocating for her community for decades.
She worked for former Governor Jack Markell, the late Attorney General Beau Biden, and served in the Obama White House.
Most recently, she served as the national spokesperson for the Human Rights Campaign, the nation’s largest LGBTQ equal rights organization.
When McBride was elected in November 2020, she became the first openly transgender state senator in American history.
In just her first term, McBride passed the landmark Healthy Delaware Families Act, providing paid family and medical leave to workers throughout the First State and marking the largest expansion of Delaware’s social safety net in decades.
She also passed legislation expanding access to health care, requiring mental health and media literacy education in public schools, promoting green technologies, preventing lead poisoning in youth, and protecting workers and families.
McBride currently serves as chair of the Senate Health & Social Services Committee and is a member of the Senate’s Judiciary Committee, Education Committee, Banking, Business, Insurance, and Technology Committee, and Executive Committee.
Sarah married her late husband Andrew Cray in 2014 and is the proud aunt of seven.
She is a graduate of Cab Calloway School of the Arts and American University.
McBride has taught public policy at the University of Delaware and is the author of the 2018 memoir, “Tomorrow Will Be Different,” which includes a foreword from President Joe Biden.
