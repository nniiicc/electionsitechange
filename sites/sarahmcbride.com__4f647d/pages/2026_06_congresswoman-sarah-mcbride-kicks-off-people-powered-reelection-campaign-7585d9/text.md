McBride Emphasized Her Steadfast Commitment to Making Life More Affordable for Working People and Families Across Delaware REHOBOTH BEACH, DE – Today, standing before more than 400 supporters gathered in Rehoboth Beach after completing a historic statewide tour of visiting all 57 of Delaware’s municipalities in just 57 hours, Congresswoman Sarah McBride (DE-AL) officially announced her campaign for reelection to Delaware’s at-large seat in the U.S.
House of Representatives.
Rep.
McBride gave a rousing speech to supporters at the conclusion of the tour, highlighting her proven track record of delivering results on behalf of all Delawareans.
“Over the last 57 hours, I’ve had the opportunity to hear directly from Delawareans in every one of our state’s 57 municipalities,” said Rep.
McBride.
“Whether I was talking with small business owners, workers, parents, veterans, retirees, or students, I heard the same thing: people want leaders who are focused on lowering costs, solving problems, and delivering results.
That’s exactly what I’ve worked to do in Congress, and that’s why I’m running for re-election – to continue delivering for and defending Delaware.” During her first term in Congress, Rep.
McBride has proven to be an effective, collaborative, and results-driven leader, leading with grace while delivering meaningful progress for Delawareans.
She has: Returned more than $6 million back to Delawareans in the form of backlogged benefits, putting more money back into the pockets of her constituents; Brought home more than $160 million in federal investments for vital community projects in Delaware; Introduced seven bipartisan bills — all focused on improving life for Delawareans: from protecting the agriculture sector to expanding opportunities for entrepreneurs to combating scams against seniors — one of which has already been signed into law; Led on bipartisan legislation that affirms her commitment to ensuring workers are treated with dignity and builds on her work to pass paid family and medical leave in Delaware; and Taken on the Trump administration, working across the aisle to reverse harmful cuts to manufacturing funding and home health care in Delaware.
Rep.
McBride’s re-election campaign enters the cycle with significant momentum and one of the strongest grassroots operations in Delaware political history.
Since launching her congressional campaign, more than 40,000 individuals have contributed to her campaign, helping build a broad coalition of supporters across Delaware and throughout the country.
“Sarah McBride is exactly the kind of leader Delaware needs in Washington,” said Delaware Governor Matt Meyer.
“She’s collaborative, hardworking, and relentlessly focused on delivering results.
She’s already proven that she can build consensus and make a real difference for Delawareans.
I’m proud to support her re-election and know she’ll continue fighting for our state every day.” “Sarah McBride has been a tireless advocate for Delaware families and an effective leader from day one in Congress,” said U.S.
Senator Chris Coons.
“She brings people together, works across the aisle to get things done, and never loses sight of who she’s fighting for.
I’m proud to endorse Sarah for re-election and look forward to continuing our work together on behalf of the First State.” “Sarah McBride has brought compassion, determination, and an unwavering commitment to public service to Congress,” said U.S.
Senator Lisa Blunt Rochester.
“She has hit the ground running, delivering for Delaware families and demonstrating the kind of thoughtful leadership that makes our state proud.
I’m honored to endorse Sarah for re-election and excited for all she will continue to accomplish on behalf of Delaware.” The 57 in 57 Tour highlighted McBride’s belief that every community deserves to be heard and represented, regardless of size, geography, or politics.
Rep.
McBride will work through Election Day to earn the vote of every Delawarean so that their voices can continue to be heard in Washington, DC.
