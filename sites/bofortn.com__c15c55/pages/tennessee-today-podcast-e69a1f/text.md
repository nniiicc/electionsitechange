I’m Tennessee State Senator Bo Watson, and I created Tennessee Today to give Tennesseans a clear, honest, and accessible view of what’s happening in our state government.
For over 18 years, I’ve served in the Senate, working to bring transparency, accountability, and smart policymaking to the forefront.
Now, I’m bringing those same values to this podcast—so you can get the facts without the political spin.
What You’ll Hear on Tennessee Today : Straightforward breakdowns of key legislative topics Candid conversations with guests and policy experts Behind-the-scenes insights from inside the Senate Real talk about how policies affect you, your family, and your community Whether you’re a concerned citizen, a curious listener, or just want to stay informed, Tennessee Today is here to help you stay connected to the issues that matter.
