Who does Senator Watson represent?
District 11 encompasses a large majority of Hamilton County, Tennessee.
This includes the cities of Chattanooga, Collegedale, Lakesite, Red Bank, Sale Creek, Signal Mountain, Soddy-Daisy and the surrounding areas.
