Hamilton County Mayor Weston Wamp’s 2026 State of The County Address
Last week, Hamilton County Mayor Weston Wamp came to our campus - the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga - to deliver this year’s State of the County Address.
On this episode of “Scenic Roots, we bring you that address - and a moderated conversation that followed between:
- Mayor Wamp
- State Senator Bo Watson of North Chattanooga, chairman of the Tennessee Senate’s Finance, Ways and Means Committee
- Stuart McWhorter, Deputy Governor and Commissioner of the Tennessee Department of Economic and Community Development
- Will Reid, Commissioner of the Tennessee Department of Transportation
- David Salyers, Commissioner of the Tennessee Department of Environment and Conservation
