Tennessee Today With Senator Bo Watson
Tennessee Today is a podcast hosted by State Senator Bo Watson that explores public policy, state government, and the legislative process in Tennessee.
Through conversations with policymakers, community leaders, and subject-matter experts, the show breaks down key issues facing the state and provides listeners with insight into how decisions made at the Capitol affect Tennesseans.
The podcast offers behind-the-scenes perspectives on legislation, policy priorities, and the people shaping Tennessee’s future.
