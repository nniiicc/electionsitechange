Officials Seek Public Input on McDonald Farm State Park
State Senator Bo Watson highlighted the state's $45.3 million investment in the future McDonald Farm State Park, emphasizing the project's role in preserving the property, expanding outdoor recreation, and strengthening Hamilton County's economy.
Watson emphasized the state's investment in the project, saying, "It's banking on the people of Tennessee and the visitors that come to our state to help drive the economy here, and it's part of Hamilton County's new narrative."
The new state park is expected to preserve the property while expanding recreation and supporting long-term economic growth in the region.
