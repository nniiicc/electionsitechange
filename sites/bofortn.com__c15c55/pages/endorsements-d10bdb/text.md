“I’m proud to endorse my good friend and longtime colleague, Senator Bo Watson, for re-election to the Tennessee Senate
I’ve served with Bo for many years, and I know him to be a conservative, a fighter and a principled public servant who always puts Tennessee taxpayers first.
As Chairman of the Senate Finance, Ways and Means Committee, Bo has played a critical role in keeping Tennessee fiscally strong, holding the line on responsible spending and cutting taxes whenever possible.
But beyond his record in the Senate, Bo is a good man and a good friend.
I encourage the people of Senate District 11 to re-elect Senator Bo Watson.”
-Lt.
Governor McNally, State of Tennessee
