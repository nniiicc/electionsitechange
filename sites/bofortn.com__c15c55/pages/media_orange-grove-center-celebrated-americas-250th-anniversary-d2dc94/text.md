Orange Grove Center celebrated America's 250th Anniversary
Chairman Bo Watson joined fellow legislators for a visit to Orange Grove Center, where they toured an intermediate care home that provides around-the-clock support for individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities.
After the tour, they attended Orange Grove's annual cookout with residents, families, and staff.
Following the visit, Watson praised Orange Grove's longstanding impact on the community, saying, "Orange Grove is iconic—not just in this area, but across the state of Tennessee.
It has a long history in this community, and people have recognized the care they provide here for decades."
