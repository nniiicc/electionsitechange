New Direct Flight from Chattanooga to Denver Launched by United Airlines
Chattanooga Metropolitan Airport (CHA) is welcoming an exciting new addition to its flight network, as United Airlines announces the launch of nonstop seasonal flights from Chattanooga (CHA) to Denver (DEN) starting May 23, 2026.
State Senator Bo Watson of Tennessee highlighted the economic benefits of the new service, emphasizing that “Securing direct service to Denver strengthens Tennessee’s position as a competitive hub for business and tourism.”
