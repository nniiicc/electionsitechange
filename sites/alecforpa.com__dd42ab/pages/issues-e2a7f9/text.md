Tax reform
In 2021, with a $150K budget surplus and $600K in federal ARPA funds, Democratic members of Plymouth Borough Council who had just won re-election requested a tax increase that would make Plymouth the second highest taxed municipality in Luzerne County behind none other than Wilke-Barre City.
Alec fought this tax increase at home and in Harrisburg would fight to adopt Municipal Taxpayer Protection legislation limiting tax increases based on budget surpluses without judicial approval.
