Beth Hinebaugh is running for the House of Delegates with a clear goal and unwavering purpose: to revitalize Wheeling and make West Virginia thrive again.
She is committed to fighting for policies that create an economic environment where good-paying jobs and a better quality of life are the standard, not the exception; policies that strengthen families and work toward ending the population decline and mass migration of our young people, and policies that build a better future for us all.
As a small business owner and job creator, she knows that growing our state’s economy is driven by common-sense tax and regulatory reforms that encourage business expansion and investment.
Experience That Matters Beth isn’t a politician.
She’s an entrepreneur with 20 years of business experience, a mother of eight, and a former police officer for the City of Wheeling.
She knows firsthand the challenges that businesses and parents face.
She’s tough on crime and tireless in the fight against the fentanyl and opioid crisis tearing apart our communities.
