The Iowa Constitution gives the state the right to take private property for public use, with just compensation.
It does not authorize the taking of private property for private use, and this should not be allowed.
A CO2 pipeline built by a private company, with the sons of three former governors (Branstad, Vilsack, Culver) and a former President of the Iowa Board of Regents (Rastetter) as investors, raises a peculiar odor long before the first shovel hits the dirt.
That odor does not pass my sniff test.
There is a country where the state can take your land, give it to somebody else, and you have no power to stop them.
That country is not America.
