MeetBenton Sawrey
Benton Sawrey is a dedicated public servant and lifelong resident of Johnston County running to fight for lower taxes, less regulation, and our traditional, eastern North Carolina values.
Sawrey was born and raised in Smithfield, where generations of his family have called home.
He is a proud graduate of Smithfield-Selma High School and later followed in his father and grandfather’s footsteps by attending N.C.
State University.
After graduation, he attended and earned a law degree from the University of South Carolina School of Law.
In his regular job as an attorney at a local law firm, Sawrey works with small-business owners, farmers, and families on complex regulatory, tax, litigation, and transactional matters.
His professional experience in the legal and business communities provides him with a deep understanding of the challenges facing North Carolina’s economy and the real-world impact of government policy on local enterprises and agricultural operations.
Sawrey is committed to serving his community.
He served two terms on the Board of Trustees at Johnston Community College as well as being a member of the Board of Directors for the Partnership for Children of Johnston County and the Downtown Smithfield Development Corporation prior to being elected to the North Carolina Senate.
Since taking office in 2023, Benton has been a commonsense conservative leader in Raleigh.
This past session, he led the North Carolina Senate with the most bills that he authored having passed the chamber.
He has successfully worked to rein in overregulation, protect our community’s values, control government spending, and lower North Carolina’s tax burden on families, small businesses, and farmers.
The job is not done – Johnston County deserves an effective advocate in the North Carolina Senate that delivers results.
Issues For This Cycle
Income Taxes
Sawrey wants to eliminate North Carolina’s income tax on families, small businesses, and farmers.
In 2023, Sawrey voted to lower the income tax rate by over 50% and control government spending over the next eight years.
There’s more work to be done and we can responsibly drive the rate to 0% and join eight other states around the country.
Farmland Preservation
Johnston County is one the largest farming communities in North Carolina and Sawrey is proud to champion state funds for conservation easements and farmland preservation.
Sawrey has co-sponsored legislation that passed into law and provided additional protections for voluntary agriculture districts this past session.
Sawrey will continue to advocate for more funding to protect private property rights and allow families to continue working hard to put food on the table.
Healthcare
The cost of healthcare has gone up and our outcomes aren’t up to par.
We don’t have enough doctors or hospitals.
Sawrey has sponsored legislation to provide greater access to doctors, nurses, and pharmacists in our rural communities as well as legislation that will allow more investment in healthcare facilities around the state.
Sawrey will continue to fight for better outcomes and access, lower costs, and pricing transparency so you know what’s in your healthcare bill.
Stronger Schools
Every child deserves access to a high-quality education that meets their unique needs, regardless of ZIP code or family income.
Supporting school choice means empowering parents to select the educational environment that best serves their children—whether that is a traditional public school, charter school, trade school, private school, or homeschool option.
Sawrey voted to expand North Carolina’s opportunity scholarship program and will continue to advance policies that encourage creativity and options for students in all forms of schools.
Traditional Values
Sawrey fought to pass legislation that kept biological males out of women’s sports and was a lead sponsor on the Parent’s Protection Act, which affirmed and codified parental rights with respect to healthcare, education, and a child’s upbringing.
Sawrey will continue to advocate for and advance policies that reflect our community and protect our values.
Law Enforcement & First Responders
Sawrey supported the bipartisan Back the Blue Pay Act – a $200 million investment in first responders that unanimously passed the Senate and provides raises, step-pay adjustments, and bonuses to law-enforcement officers along with changes to line-of- duty death benefits.
The bill also includes long-overdue increases in the Firefighters’ and Rescue Squad Workers’ Pension Fund and the National Guard Pension Fund.
Sawrey will fight to see that this passes and is signed into law by the governor so that we invest in those that put their lives on the line daily.
