I believe strengthening labor protections means advocating for fair wages , safe working conditions , and the right to collective bargaining , which allows workers to have a meaningful seat at the table.
By supporting our local unions—from trades and manufacturing to educators and public service workers—we invest in a more stable middle class and a more resilient economy.
Supporting labor advocacy isn't just about policy; it’s about respecting the dignity of work and ensuring that every Utahn can provide a secure future for their family Environmental Stewardship The Great Salt Lake is facing severe shrinkage driven by overuse of upstream water, drought, and rising evaporation rates—creating exposed lakebed that threatens air quality, ecosystems, and Utah’s long‑term water security.
I support bills that will further protect the lake and provide for a sustainable future Water conservation: Utah faces widespread water‑supply challenges, leading to additional proposals such as leak‑tracking requirements for utilities, new water‑rights rules for areas in the Lower Basin, and exemptions for certain municipal water suppliers Wildlife and green space are important to every community and a balance must be found between environmental protections and allowing continued growth Good Governance Government functions best when decisions are made as close to the people as possible.
Utah’s strong tradition of local governance reflects the constitutional principle that power should be limited, balanced, and rooted in the will of the people.
The state should respect the ability of cities, counties, and school districts to manage their own affairs–not promote bureaucratic, one-size-fits all mandates.
Redistricting is a big issue in Utah.
I believe that voters should choose their representatives.
We need to protect the Independent Redistricting Commission (IRC) Judicial Independence is a key component of any democracy.
I oppose supreme court expansion from 5 to 7 justices and believe that vetting standards should remain non-partisan Voting access and election integrity : the existing universal vote-by-mail system works in Utah!
It seems fundamentally wrong to make it harder to vote.
Term limits : when politicians are in office for too long, they take the citizens for granted and stop listening to the will of the people Inclusion and Representation Equality : I believe we need to uphold protections for all members of our society.
In Utah, we have a rich culture that includes many from different backgrounds, beliefs, and orientation.
This is what makes Utah great!
We should make all individuals feel welcome rather than seeking ways to marginalize their views and backgrounds.
