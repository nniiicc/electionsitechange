Standing Up for St.
Louis
We fought back attempts to gut child labor laws.

The U.
S.
Supreme Court just this year, in an 8 to 1 ruling, declared such a law Constitutional.
I will continue that fight as well as supporting the effort to restore women’s rights to make their own reproductive decisions.
