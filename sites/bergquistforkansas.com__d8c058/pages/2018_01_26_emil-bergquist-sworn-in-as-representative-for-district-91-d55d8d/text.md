Emil Bergquist Sworn in as Representative for District 91
January 26, 2018
In Topeka this week, Emil Bergquist was sworn in as Kansas Representative for the 91st District, which includes parts of Kechi, Maize, Park City, Valley Center, and Wichita.
Emil fills the remainder of the term begun by Rep.
Greg Lakin, who was appointed chief medical officer for the Kansas Department of Health and Environment by Governor Sam Brownback.
Emil was selected to fill the position in an special convention held by the Sedgwick County Republican Precinct Committee on January 20, 2018.
He will serve the remainder of the term and plans to run for reelection this fall.
