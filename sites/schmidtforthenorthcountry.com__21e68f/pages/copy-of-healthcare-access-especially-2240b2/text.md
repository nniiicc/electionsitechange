Infrastructure & Rural Quality of Life
Outdated roads, bridges, water systems, and broadband hurt families, businesses, and local economies.
Rural communities are often left behind while projects prioritize cities.
I will push for strategic investment in infrastructure that benefits the North Country, protect clean drinking water, expand broadband access, and ensure projects hire local workers.
This approach strengthens communities and keeps money circulating within our district.
Problems
- Roads, bridges, water systems, and broadband are outdated
- Small towns fall behind while cities get priority
- Projects often hire outside contractors
Solutions
- Prioritize rural infrastructure investment
- Fix water systems and protect clean drinking water
- Expand broadband access
- Require infrastructure projects to hire local workers
