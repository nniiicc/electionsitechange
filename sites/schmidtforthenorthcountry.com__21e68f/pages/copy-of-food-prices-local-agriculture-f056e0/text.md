Healthcare Access (Especially Rural)
Rural healthcare in NY-21 is under threat: hospitals are closing, travel times for care are long, and mental health services are fragmented.
I will expand rural healthcare funding, bring mobile clinics and telehealth to underserved areas, and fully fund community-based mental health services.
By integrating housing, treatment, and crisis response, we can ensure that everyone has access to the care they need when they need it.
Problems
- Hospitals closing or understaffed
- Long travel times for care
- Mental health services are fragmented or nonexistent
Solutions
- Expand rural healthcare funding and workforce incentives
- Support mobile clinics and telehealth
- Fund real community-based mental health care
- Integrate housing, treatment, and crisis response instead of neglect
