Peace, Not Endless Wars
Our tax dollars are too often spent on conflicts abroad while communities at home struggle with basic needs.
I will fight to restrain foreign intervention and redirect resources to domestic priorities: infrastructure, jobs, healthcare, and veteran services.
America-first spending ensures that families in NY-21 see tangible benefits from their tax dollars while our veterans receive the care and support they deserve.
Problems
- Trillions spent overseas
- Communities at home struggle with basic needs
- Veterans return to underfunded services
Solutions
- Restrain foreign intervention
- Reinvest tax dollars at home
- Fully fund veterans’ healthcare and support services
- Focus federal priorities on NY-21 first
