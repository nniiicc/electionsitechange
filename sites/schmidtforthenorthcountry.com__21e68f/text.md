ABOUT CHRISTOPHER SCHMIDT
Let's Shake Washington Up!
Christopher Schmidt NY-21 is dedicated to being the voice of the hardworking people in New York’s 21st District.
With a background as a day laborer in the working class and as a community organizer, Christopher is committed to bringing real change to Washington, advocating for accountability, pro freedom, and the protection of rural communities.
Christopher Schmidt is a working-class independent running for the U.S.
House to put NY-21 first.
Not beholden to Democrats or Republicans, Chris fights to keep our district free from outside influence in Washington.
He knows what people really want: stable jobs, stronger infrastructure, affordable and accessible health care, lower taxes, and keeping our dollars local.
With fresh ideas and practical solutions, Christopher Schmidt tackles the problems that the parties can’t—or won’t—fix.
