Letter to Governor Sununu About Face Coverings from 5/14/20
On May 14th, 178 Democrats (myself included) signed and sent a letter to Governor Sununu, urging him to require face coverings in public to decrease the transmission and spread of COVID-19.
He did not see fit to put this requirement into place, but if he had, there is no doubt that the total number of cases in NH would be lower.
