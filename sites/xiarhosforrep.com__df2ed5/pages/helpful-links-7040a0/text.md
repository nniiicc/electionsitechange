State Services
Unemployment Assistance
If you are unemployed and need assistance, you may be able to apply for Unemployment Insurance benefits from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
The following link has helpful information to get you started with the process.
https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-unemployment-benefits
If you think you may qualify for benefits but have questions or concerns, or if you encounter unexpected problems during the application process, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.

Registry of Motor Vehicles
In Massachusetts, motor vehicle registration and driver licensing is conducted by the state Registry of Motor Vehicles (RMV).
Here are some links to helpful resources:
- Registry of Motor Vehicles Site: https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massachusetts-registry-of-motor-vehicles
- How to renew your driver’s license: https://www.mass.gov/how-to/renew-your-drivers-license
- Vehicle registration: https://www.mass.gov/vehicle-registration
- Identification requirements (“Real ID”): https://www.mass.gov/guides/massachusetts-identification-id-requirements
- Learner’s Permits: https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-passenger-class-d-learners-permit
If you need help with RMV issues, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
