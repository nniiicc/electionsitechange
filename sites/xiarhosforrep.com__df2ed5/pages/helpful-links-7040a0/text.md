Helpful Links Xiarhos Committee 2025-02-19T07:25:25-05:00 Local Services The 5th Barnstable District includes portions of three towns: Barnstable, Bourne, and Sandwich.
Each of these towns has its own local government, which is responsible for overseeing municipal services and education at the local level.
Here are links to the municipal government sites for each town: Click Here to Visit the Town Web Site Town Hall 367 Main Street Hyannis MA 02601 (508) 862-4956 Click Here to Visit the Town Web Site Town Hall 24 Perry Avenue Buzzards Bay, MA 02532-3441 (508) 759-0600 Click Here to Visit the Town Web Site Town Hall 130 Main Street Sandwich, MA 02563 (508) 888-4910 State Services Unemployment Assistance If you are unemployed and need assistance, you may be able to apply for Unemployment Insurance benefits from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
The following link has helpful information to get you started with the process. https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-unemployment-benefits If you think you may qualify for benefits but have questions or concerns, or if you encounter unexpected problems during the application process, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
Housing MassHousing and the Massachusetts Housing Partnership (MHP) provide a number of programs to expand the availability of affordable housing opportunities across Massachusetts, including rental and home purchases.
Here is a link to several sites for further information: MassHousing: https://www.masshousing.com/ Mass Housing Partnership: https://www.mhp.net/ Local Links: Barnstable Housing Committee Bourne Affordable Housing Sandwich Housing Authority If you need help finding affordable housing resources, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
Registry of Motor Vehicles In Massachusetts, motor vehicle registration and driver licensing is conducted by the state Registry of Motor Vehicles (RMV).
Here are some links to helpful resources: Registry of Motor Vehicles Site: https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massachusetts-registry-of-motor-vehicles How to renew your driver’s license: https://www.mass.gov/how-to/renew-your-drivers-license Vehicle registration: https://www.mass.gov/vehicle-registration Identification requirements (“Real ID”): https://www.mass.gov/guides/massachusetts-identification-id-requirements Learner’s Permits: https://www.mass.gov/how-to/apply-for-a-passenger-class-d-learners-permit If you need help with RMV issues, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
Department of Revenue The Department of Revenue (DOR) is responsible for overseeing taxation by the state and child support enforcement.
Here is a link to their site: https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massachusetts-department-of-revenue If you need help contacting DOR and finding answers to questions, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
Children and Education There are a variety of state agencies responsible for issues related to children and education.
Here are several useful sites: Massachusetts Department of Children and Families (DCF) Department of Early Education and Care (EEC) Department of Elementary and Secondary Education, Massachusetts (DESE) Department of Youth Services (DYS) Local Links: Barnstable Public Schools Bourne Public Schools Sandwich Public Schools If you need help with issues involving children and education, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
Veterans and Families If you are a veteran, first and foremost… THANK YOU for your service!
Massachusetts has a variety of programs that are available to assist veterans in our state.
Here is a link to some helpful information: https://www.mass.gov/orgs/massachusetts-department-of-veterans-services Local Links: Barnstable Veterans Services Bourne Veterans Agent Sandwich Veterans Services If you are a veteran and you need help finding resources, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
Seniors The Executive Office of Elder Affairs promotes independence, empowerment, and well-being of older people, individuals with disabilities, and their families.
There are many different resources available, including assistance with housing and health care.
Here is a link to the EOEA site for further information: https://www.mass.gov/orgs/executive-office-of-elder-affairs Local Links: Barnstable Council on Aging Bourne Council on Aging Sandwich Council on Aging If you are a senior citizen or family member and you need help finding resources, please feel free to contact my office for assistance.
