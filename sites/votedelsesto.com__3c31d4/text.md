I've lived in Hudson for 22 years with my wife and our three kids.
Like a lot of you, I've spent countless evenings and weekends at our local schools, ballfields, and parks, coaching, cheering, and pitching in wherever our town needed a hand.
Professionally, I work in healthcare software, an industry built on analyzing complex data, cutting through red tape, and designing systems that actually work.
I know how to bring people together to solve big, systemic problems and that is exactly the skill set I want to bring to Concord.
Hudson has sent the exact same party slate to the State House for well over a decade.
Yet, that straight-party voting hasn't stopped our local property taxes from climbing, and it hasn't stopped our state school funding from getting squeezed.
I'm running as an independent because I'm not interested in scoring points for a political party or engaging in nationalized culture wars.
I am interested in getting practical, tangible results for Hudson.
If we want representatives who actually listen to our community, we have to send someone who owes absolutely nothing to the party bosses or campaign whips on either side.
That is the job I am asking you to hire me for this November.
# Years in Hudson # Kids raised here # Party bosses Where I Stand The fights worth having for Hudson No slogans and no talking points handed down from a party.
Here's exactly what I'll work on in Concord.
01 Lower Hudson's Property Taxes Every round of tax cuts in Concord pushes the bill downhill onto Hudson homeowners.
I'll fight for a state budget that stops balancing itself on the backs of our local property taxpayers.
02 Fix How We Fund Our Schools Concord has quietly shifted the burden of education onto local homeowners.
While the state pours millions of state education tax dollars into voucher programs that fund private tuition, our local public schools are left underfunded.
This forces Hudson property taxpayers to pick up the slack.
03 Reform our Election System The two parties keep pulling further apart.
I support a single open primary ballot where every Hudson voter, declared or not, picks the best candidates to advance.
End the performative "pick a party, then un-pick it" dance.
04 Tackle Health Care Costs Drawing on a career in technology and my last ten years in healthcare software, I know where bureaucratic waste hides.
I'll bring that real-world experience to Concord to drive down costs and increase price transparency for NH families.
05 Protect What We Love About NH Clean water, open space, and a healthy environment aren't partisan.
They're part of why we live here.
I'll be a common-sense steward of the New Hampshire we hand to our kids.
