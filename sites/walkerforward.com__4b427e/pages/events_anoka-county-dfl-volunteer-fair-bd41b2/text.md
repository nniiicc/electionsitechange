This Saturday afternoon, the Anoka County DFL will be hosting a Volunteer Fair in Blaine.
You’ll get a chance to meet with all of the campaigns across Anoka County and learn about various opportunities within each organization.
From doorknocking, postcard writing, to poop-scooping (we may or may not have horses in parades this year!).
Join us from 4p-7p at Aquatore Park in Blaine to learn more.
Matt Woestehoff, our campaign manager, will be there, as Brian is enjoying the tranquil waters of the BWCA while they’re still pristine.
