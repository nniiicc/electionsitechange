It is organized by a volunteer committee (a registered non-profit) and supported by local businesses, organizations, and residents.
From bounce houses and games to local vendors, classic cars, live bands, and fireworks, the goal is simple, create a safe, fun place for neighbors to connect, celebrate summer, and enjoy everything Andover has to offer.
You will find most events around the Andover City Hall and Community Center area.
