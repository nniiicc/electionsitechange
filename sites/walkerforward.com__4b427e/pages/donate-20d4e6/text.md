Political donations are not tax-deductible under federal law.
However, if you're a Minnesota resident, you may be eligible for the Political Contribution Refund (PCR) — a state program that refunds individual contributions up to $75 (or $150 for married couples filing jointly).
Walker Forward is a registered Minnesota political committee, so your donation qualifies.
Who can donate and what are the limits?
Individual contributions are welcome from U.S. citizens and permanent residents.
Under Minnesota campaign finance law, corporate and union contributions are prohibited.
Individual contributions to a state legislative candidate are limited per the Minnesota Campaign Finance Board guidelines.
