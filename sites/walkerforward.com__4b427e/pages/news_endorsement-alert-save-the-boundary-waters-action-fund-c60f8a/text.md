Endorsement Alert: Save the Boundary Waters Action Fund
We’re thrilled to share that Brian Walker has gained the endorsement of the Boundary Waters Action Fund.
We would have posted about it sooner, but, well, Brian was actually in the BWCA enjoying those pristine waters that he’s so dedicated to protect.
Our natural resources, and specifically the BWCA, are a treasure worth preserving.
That’s why Brian is so happy to have his first endorsement of the season be from the folks at Save the Boundary Waters Action Fund.
