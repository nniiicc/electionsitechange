Election Day
Cast your ballot in person
Each ward and precinct in 31A has its own voting location.
Please make sure you check where to go!
We appreciate all the support throughout this season, and hope for a smooth election day.
If for any reason you have problems, always know you have the right to talk to one of the election judges at your polling place.
They’re there to help, and are wonderful stewards of our democracy.
If you are in line by 8pm, your vote will be counted.
Not registered?
Minnesota allows for same-day registration at your polling place.
Make sure you bring the appropriate proof required (visit mnvotes.gov for details)
