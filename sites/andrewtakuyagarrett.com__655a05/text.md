A RECORD OF RESULTS.
Since you elected me in 2022 to represent House District 22, I have worked to provide responsive representation and deliver meaningful results for Mānoa, McCully-Mōʻiliʻili and Tantalus.
It is an honor to serve the community my family and I call home, and I remain grateful for the trust you have placed in me. ​ As chair of the House Committee on Higher Education, I have championed college affordability, workforce development, responsible governance, and a stronger University of Hawaiʻi system.
I believe education remains one of our most powerful tools for expanding opportunity, strengthening our economy, and making it possible for future generations to build their lives here at home. ​ My work also extends beyond the State Capitol.
I have helped secure investments in our neighborhood schools and community facilities, advanced protections for educators and sports officials, and supported stronger government ethics and transparency.
Here in the district, I have worked directly with residents and government agencies to address flooding, stream maintenance, public safety, emergency preparedness, and the everyday concerns that affect our quality of life. ​ There is still much more to accomplish.
I remain guided by a simple commitment: listen carefully, work collaboratively, and never lose sight of the people I was elected to serve.
By bringing people together and staying focused on practical solutions, I believe we can continue moving our community and our state forward—together.
