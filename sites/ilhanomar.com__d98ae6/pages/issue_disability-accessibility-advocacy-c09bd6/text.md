Protect People with Disabilities People with disabilities deserve equal access to housing, education, healthcare, jobs and transportation, free from discrimination, violence and harassment.
I will continue to fight for policies that protect our disabled community and ensure that they have equal access to human rights.
More than 1 in 4 (28.7 percent) adults in the United States have some type of disability.
Less than 5% of the national housing supply is accessible.
Moreover, less than 1% of housing is accessible for people who use wheelchairs.
1 in 4 adults with disabilities 18 to 44 years do not have a usual health care provider.
1 in 4 adults with disabilities 18 to 44 years have an unmet health care need because of cost in the past year.
Persons with disabilities were victims of 26% of all nonfatal violent crime, while accounting for about 12% of the population.
Vision and policy priorities Ensure People with Disabilities have Equal Access and Resources Cost of living has skyrocketed while disabled workers are being paid sub-minimum wages and are unable to find accessible housing or afford healthcare.
We must ensure that the disabled community has access and can afford these basic human rights.
Pass the Raise the Wage Act to increase the minimum wage and the Transformation to Competitive Integrated Employment Act to eliminate the sub-minimum wage for workers with disabilities.
Fight for strong funding for affordable and accessible housing, in addition to the $1,000,000 secured for the People Serving People’s Modernization Project for Families Experiencing Homelessness and Disability and $514,000 secured for the Low Income Housing Improvements for Persons with Disabilities in Minneapolis, Golden Valley and St.
