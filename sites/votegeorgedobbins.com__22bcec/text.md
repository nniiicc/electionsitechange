Why I Am Running I was born and raised in Wayne County.
My wife and I chose to settle down in our hometown (Lyons) because we wanted to raise our family in the same community that we were raised in.
I am running for the State Assembly because I want my two-year-old daughter to love this place as much as we do.
The Republican Party is the party of working people.
The 130th Assembly district is full of hard working men and women who want a better life for their children.
I believe that conservative, Republican principles, rightly-applied, can deliver that promise to them.
