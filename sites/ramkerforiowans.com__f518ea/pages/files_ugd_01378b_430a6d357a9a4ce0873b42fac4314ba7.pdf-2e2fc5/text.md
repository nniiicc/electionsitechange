RAMKER for Iowans September 2, 2025 For media inquiries or additional information: Facebook: Nathan Ramker for Iowans Email: ramkerforiowans@gmail.com DAVENPORT, Iowa — Nathan Ramker has announced his candidacy for Iowa House District 98, which covers southwest Davenport, Buffalo, and portions of Blue Grass.
The seat will be vacated by Representative Monica Kurth, who has served in the seat for a decade.
This will be Nathan’s second run for House District 98, after narrowly losing by 45 votes in one of the tightest legislative races in the state in 2024.
Nathan Ramker was born in Marshfield, Wisconsin and graduated from McDonell High School.
He and his wife, Rebecca, moved to Iowa in 2005 and have been married for 35 years.
Nathan and Rebecca have seven children, five of whom live in House District 98, along with all 13 of their grandchildren—keeping the Ramker family firmly rooted in the community.
Nathan and Rebecca have spent their entire lives as small business owners.
For more than 30 years, they operated a Kirby vacuum distributorship that grew from 20 to over 75 independent contractors.
Today, Nathan and Rebecca operate a number of small businesses with their adult children.
Their family businesses continue to support the local economy and families, with one business endeavor focusing on medicinal CBD and another on providing childcare to more than 70 children.
Beyond their own ventures, Nathan has supported young entrepreneurs through silent partnerships, helping them launch new businesses and take part in Iowa’s growth.
The Ramkers are also active in public service.
Both Nathan and Rebecca volunteer with the Scott County Sheriff’s Department through the Volunteer In Police Services (VIPS) program, working with deputies to strengthen safety in local neighborhoods.
Ramker’s campaign emphasizes protecting private property rights, and he is especially opposed to the use of eminent domain for CO₂ pipelines.
He is also focused on providing property tax relief, lowering income taxes, cutting red tape, increasing transparency in education, supporting law enforcement, protecting constitutional freedoms, championing entrepreneurship, and creating opportunities for young Iowans.
As a husband, father, lifelong entrepreneur, and “Papa” to 13 grandchildren, Ramker is running to ensure that Iowa remains a place where families can prosper, values are respected, and future generations can thrive.
His campaign reflects a commitment to hard work, strong communities, and protecting the freedoms that define Iowa.
