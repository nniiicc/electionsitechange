Priorities & Vision
If elected, I will focus on advancing common-sense solutions that strengthen our communities, support Iowa families, and protect the values we share.
My top priorities include:
- Property Tax Relief – Ensuring families, farmers, and seniors can keep more of what they earn by pursuing responsible, sustainable tax reform.
- Strong Economy – Supporting small businesses, encouraging job growth, and fostering an environment where Iowa workers and entrepreneurs can thrive.
- Freedom & Public Safety – Defending our constitutional rights while ensuring law enforcement has the tools and support needed to keep our communities safe.
- Parents’ Rights & Education – Empowering parents, increasing transparency, and ensuring our education system focuses on student success and local control.
- Elderly Care Reform – Promoting dignity, accountability, and quality care for our seniors through meaningful and responsible reforms.
I am committed to representing District 98 with integrity, accountability, and a strong voice for the people who call this community home.
About Iowa House District 98
Iowa House District 98 is a diverse and growing area in Scott County, encompassing the communities of Buffalo, Blue Grass, and portions of Davenport, along with surrounding rural townships.
The district stretches along the Mississippi River, blending small-town values, agricultural heritage, and expanding suburban neighborhoods.
This unique mix of communities represents the heart of eastern Iowa—where hardworking families, local businesses, farmers, and commuters all contribute to a strong and vibrant region.
From the riverfront in Buffalo to the neighborhoods of west Davenport and the close-knit community of Blue Grass, District 98 reflects both Iowa’s tradition and its future.
Residents of District 98 care deeply about:
- Supporting local businesses and economic growth
- Protecting property rights and lowering taxes
- Ensuring safe communities and strong law enforcement
- Preserving agricultural roots while encouraging responsible development
- Strengthening schools and empowering parents
As our district continues to grow, it’s important to have leadership that understands both the rural and urban needs of our communities and is committed to representing every corner of District 98 with integrity and common sense.
