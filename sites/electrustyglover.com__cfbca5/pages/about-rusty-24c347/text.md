Born and raised in Mobile County, Rusty spent 25 years teaching and coaching at Mary G.
Montgomery High School in Semmes.
His classroom wasn’t just about history—it was about building character, leadership, and community pride.
Rusty took that same commitment to Montgomery, where he served in the Alabama House of Representatives and the Alabama Senate for a combined 16 years.
During that time, he worked closely with local leaders, educators, small business owners, and families to protect conservative values and keep Alabama strong.
Today, Rusty is running to represent Senate District 34 once again , because he knows leadership isn’t about titles—it’s about trust.
He’s ready to put his experience back to work for you.
