Last updated: July 18, 2026 This policy is provided for transparency so you know what happens with your information when you visit this website.
It was written by the campaign, not by an attorney, in plain language.
If anything here is unclear or you have questions about your information, please contact us — we're happy to help.
Information We Collect We collect information in a few different ways, depending on how you interact with the site: Newsletter sign-up.
When you sign up for campaign updates (in the site footer or elsewhere), we collect your email address and, depending on the form, your phone number and ZIP code.
Volunteer form.
When you fill out the volunteer form on our Get Involved page, we collect your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, the volunteer interests you select (such as phone banking or canvassing), and your stated availability.
Information you send us directly.
If you email us at info@ambrose4congress.com or otherwise contact the campaign, we keep whatever information you choose to share in that message.
We do not collect payment or donation information ourselves — see "Donations and ActBlue" below.
How We Use Information We use the information we collect to: Send you campaign updates, event notifications, and other communications you've signed up for.
Follow up on volunteer sign-ups and coordinate volunteer activities.
Respond to questions or messages you send us.
Understand how people use the site so we can improve it.
Comply with legal obligations, including federal campaign finance and disclosure laws.
We do not sell your personal information.
Who We Share Information With We use a small number of outside services to run this site and the campaign.
We share information with them only as needed for them to do their job: EmailJS.
Our newsletter and volunteer forms are submitted through EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, which processes the information in those forms in order to deliver it to the campaign.
Analytics providers (such as Google Analytics).
Certain actions on the site, like clicking a Donate button, may be recorded as anonymized analytics events to help us understand site activity.
ActBlue.
Donations are processed entirely by ActBlue, an independent, secure third-party platform — see "Donations and ActBlue" below.
BigCommerce.
Our campaign store, hosted at store.ambrose4congress.com , runs on BigCommerce, a separate e-commerce platform — see "Third-Party Websites and Platforms" below.
We may also disclose information if required to do so by law, including disclosures required of federal candidate committees (see the next section).
Federal Election Commission Disclosure Ben Ambrose is a candidate for the U.S.
House of Representatives, and this campaign is required by federal law to report certain information about contributors — such as name, address, occupation, and employer — to the Federal Election Commission (FEC), as required by federal campaign finance law.
Reports filed with the FEC become part of the public record.
This requirement applies to campaign contributions and is separate from, and not affected by, whether you sign up for our newsletter or volunteer with us.
Donations and ActBlue When you click a Donate button on this site, you are taken to ActBlue (secure.actblue.com), an independent third-party platform that federal campaigns commonly use to process online donations.
This campaign website does not collect or store your payment card information — ActBlue handles your donation and any payment details under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review.
Third-Party Websites and Platforms This site links out to other platforms we don't control, including ActBlue for donations, our campaign store on BigCommerce (store.ambrose4congress.com), and our social media accounts.
Each of those platforms has its own privacy policy governing the information it collects, and we encourage you to review those policies before providing information to them.
Email Communications and Opt-Out If you sign up for campaign updates, you can unsubscribe at any time.
Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link, or you can email us directly at info@ambrose4congress.com and we will remove you from our list.
Data Security We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us, including relying on established, reputable third-party services (such as EmailJS and ActBlue) to handle form submissions and payments securely.
That said, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.
Children's Privacy This website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at info@ambrose4congress.com so we can remove it.
Changes to This Policy We may update this privacy policy from time to time, for example as the site or campaign tools change.
When we do, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
We encourage you to check back periodically.
Contact Us If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your information is handled, please contact the campaign at info@ambrose4congress.com .
