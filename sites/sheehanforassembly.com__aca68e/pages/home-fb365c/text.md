Lower the Cost of Living
Wisconsin families are feeling the pressure of rising costs.
We need to find practical solutions that make everyday life more affordable.
Fund Education
Public schools are the backbone of our society.
We must change Wisconsin’s school funding formula to ensure schools have the resources to educate our kids.
Expand Healthcare
Wisconsin could add 30,000 to 45,000 residents to our Medicaid program, all paid for with federal dollars.
Our state legislature has blocked that coverage.
We should do that immediately to protect the health of tens of thousands of Wisconsinites.
