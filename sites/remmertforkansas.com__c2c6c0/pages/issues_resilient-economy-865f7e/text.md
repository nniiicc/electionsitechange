Rural Innovation Economic growth in House District 13 means creating an environment where our small businesses, family farms, and local industries can grow profitably.
I am committed to assissting local entrepreneurs with support for new ideas and fledgling businesses.
Money alone is not enough.
Rural economic development requires knowledge, networking, and access to support for entrepreneurs.

Let's give our young people reasons to stay, build families and businesses, and invest in our rural communities.
