Fun!!!
People don't stay in places that aren't fun.
A thriving rural economy requires more than just jobs—it requires a community where people want to live, play, and raise their families.
I believe that investing in our local parks, community celebrations, and supporting local events, are vital pieces of economic development.
Fun is economic development.
In rural communities, non-profits, churches, and charities, do amazing work.
They fill the gaps that neither government nor private businesses can manage.
We must find every opportunity to support these vital local groups.
