JACK Staunch Republican: I’m a staunch Republican and support each plank of the Utah Republican Party Platform.
I have considered the various planks of the Republican Platform in every vote I’ve taken during my time in office.
Free Market Constitutional Conservative: I’m a free market constitutional conservative, and I believe that the free market and capitalism are the best solutions to reduce poverty and improve our lot in life.
Government intrusion into our life and pocketbook should be at a minimum.
Government works for the people, not the other way around.
In my time as a State Representative, I have voted to cut taxes each and every year.
Hard Worker: The job of a State Legislator is part-time, but it takes a lot of personal commitment and effort.
In my service as a State Representative I’ve shown that I'm willing and able to put in that time and be present where and when I’m needed.
I hold my Christian and Republican values dear and know that you don't compromise on values.
I also understand that I must listen to, learn from, and work with constituents, other legislators, and relevant, interested parties to do the business of the state for the benefit of us all.
Electrical Engineer: I’m a professional electrical engineer with nearly 40 years of experience in the power sector.
In my professional life I’ve learned to solve problems and find practical solutions – that’s what engineers do.
I believe there is great value in having diversity of opinions and backgrounds in the legislature.
We need engineers in addition to the lawyers, doctors, tradespeople, merchants, ministers, landscapers, accountants, and others.
Our Founding Fathers had a mix of careers and lifestyles, and we should too.
As a representative in the Utah State Legislature, I will continue to work to derive and balance conservative budgets, negotiate practical solutions to the issues in our state, and introduce the laws of physics to the capital, for the benefit of our district.

They came here primarily to find safety and refuge from the persecution that forced them out of their homes.
Eventually, my ancestors were driven to the colonies in Mexico, and then to Arizona, where my parents grew up.
I was born in Provo, Utah, but raised in Lukachukai, Arizona, in the middle of the Navajo Reservation.
I worked hard in the family business, Totso Trading Post, which consisted of a general store, gas station, and café.
After serving a mission for the Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints in Santiago, Chile, I studied at BYU and graduated as an Electrical Engineer.
I met the love of my life, Leslie, at BYU and have been married to her for over 40 years.
We have three adult children and are experiencing the joys of being grandparents.
My professional career of working for non-profit electric cooperatives has taken me from Garkane Power in Kanab, Utah, to Midwest Electric in Roby, Texas, and finally to Dixie Power in St.
George, Utah.
I also worked for 30 years as an engineering consultant for the National Rural Electric Cooperative Association International Program in 30 developing countries all over the world.
I’m a Lifetime Member of the NRA and a First Family member and Graduate of Front Sight.
I love to travel with my family, do Family History (my 32 pioneer ancestor families settled 32 different communities in the State of Utah), and have run four (St George) Marathons.
I’ve taken the Rick Green and David Barton Constitution Alive Course, and enjoyed singing bass in a Barbershop Quartet for the ten years prior to starting work in the Legislature.
Political Experience : I’m a life-long Republican; my first vote was for Ronald Reagan in 1980 when I was 19 years old, and I have never voted for anyone who wasn’t a Republican.
Other than voting, I didn’t get involved in the political process until after the U.S.
Congress passed the 2005 Energy Policy Act.
Since then, I served in many volunteer positions in the Republican Party, including County and State Delegate, Precinct Chair, District Chair for Legislative District 62 (now 73), and Vice-Chair of the Washington County Republican Party (WCRP.) I also served several terms as a member of the Utah Republican Party (UTGOP) State Central Committee.
Additionally, I served on the UTGOP Convention Rules Committee, the WCRP Bylaws Committee, the UTGOP Caucus/Convention Committee, moderated the Q&A Session at the Lincoln Day event, and ran the special elections for mid-term replacements for county offices.
These positions helped me understand the deliberative process and prepared me to work with other elected representatives.
During my time as an elected State Representative I’ve diligently served in my assigned committees, working groups, and caucuses.
I’ve also passed the bills and earned the awards listed elsewhere in this website.
