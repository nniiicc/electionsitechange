Michele Tafoya is building a broad coalition of support across Minnesota, earning endorsements from the Minnesota Police and Peace Officers Association (MPPOA), Police Officers Federation of Minneapolis, and the Minnesota Farm Bureau.
From law enforcement to Minnesota’s farmers and agricultural community, these endorsements reflect the confidence Minnesotans have in Michele’s leadership, common-sense approach, and commitment to putting Minnesota first.
Minnesota Endorsements
Minnesota Police and Peace Officers Association
The Minnesota Police and Peace Officers Association (MPPOA) was founded in 1922 and is the largest association representing licensed peace officers in Minnesota.
For more than a century, MPPOA has advocated for public safety, professional law enforcement, and the interests of peace officers and their families.
Its endorsement of Michele Tafoya is a strong vote of confidence from Minnesota’s law enforcement community.
Police Officers Federation of Minneapolis
Since 1972, the Police Officers Federation of Minneapolis has represented police officers employed by the City of Minneapolis and the Minneapolis Park and Recreation Board.
With more than 580 members, the Federation is one of the largest non-affiliated law enforcement unions in Minnesota.
The Federation’s endorsement of Michele Tafoya reflects its confidence in her commitment to public safety and support for the men and women who keep Minneapolis safe.
Minnesota Farm Bureau
The Minnesota Farm Bureau Political Action Committee’s (MFB PAC) endorsement of Michele Tafoya reflects the confidence Minnesota’s agricultural community has in her leadership and her commitment to standing up for farmers and rural communities.
National Endorsements
Winning For Women
Winning For Women is dedicated to supporting and advancing the next generation of conservative women leaders.
Their endorsement of Michele Tafoya highlights the strength of her campaign and her commitment to bringing strong conservative women to the U.S.
Senate.
United Coalition of Public Safety
Founded in 2017, the United Coalition of Public Safety (UCOPS) is a nonprofit social policy and education organization dedicated to building stronger, safer communities.
UCOPS works to bring people together by connecting law enforcement and citizens and advancing a national conversation about public safety and law enforcement.
UCOPS’ endorsement of Michele Tafoya recognizes her commitment to standing with law enforcement, supporting those who keep our communities safe, and making public safety a priority in the U.S.
Senate.
