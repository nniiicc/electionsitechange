Linda Pierce for District 30 Service in our community Meet Linda A career built on connecting and protecting our community.
Linda Pierce has spent her career bringing people together and standing up for the things our communities share.
She serves as Director of Development at the Connecticut Forest & Park Association , the state's first conservation organization, founded in 1895, which connects people to Connecticut's forests, parks, and Blue-Blazed Hiking Trails.
Earlier, she worked at the Prudence Crandall Center , supporting survivors of domestic violence, and with Rebuilding Together New Britain , helping neighbors stay in safe and healthy homes.
She began her career as an educator and holds a Master of Fine Arts from the University of Massachusetts Amherst.
Across every role, her work has come back to the same purpose: connecting people to the resources they need, and protecting the places and values we hold in common.
