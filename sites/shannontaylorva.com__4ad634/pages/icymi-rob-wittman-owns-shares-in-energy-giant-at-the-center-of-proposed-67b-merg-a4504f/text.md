While Virginians struggle with rising energy prices, Wittman is personally invested in the major utility company that could jack up electricity bills even further across the Commonwealth and refuses to say whether he’ll sell his shares.
According to Dogwood, there are “concerns over how such a deal would impact Virginians’ rising energy costs …NextEra recently agreed to pay $150 million to settle allegations that the company lied about its involvement in political interference schemes in Florida.
It’s a highly profitable company that’s been criticized for benefiting from a $7 billion rate hike in Florida.” “While Virginians struggle with rising utility costs, Rob Wittman has spent his decades in Congress getting richer.
He has traded more than $2.5 million worth of stocks while in office, including in industries he’s supposed to be regulating,” said Taylor.
“He has become part of the Washington corruption that is costing Virginia families, and as we fight to stop this merger that could cost us even more, he is siding with his investments yet again.
That’s exactly why I’ve called for a ban on stock trading by members of Congress — because your representative should be working for you, not their own portfolio.” ABOUT SHANNON: Shannon Taylor is a battle-tested prosecutor with 25 years of experience and a 90% conviction rate in homicide cases, taking on the toughest threats to Virginia families and delivering justice.
A native of Charlottesville and graduate of the University of Virginia and the University of Richmond Law School, she began her career as a prosecutor in Richmond before serving as a Special Assistant in the U.S.
Attorney’s office.
In 2011, she flipped the Henrico Commonwealth’s Attorney’s office from Republican to Democrat, becoming the first woman elected to the role.
She has since been re-elected three times, proving she knows how to win tough races.
As Commonwealth’s Attorney, Taylor has pioneered programs to support victims of human trafficking, created a task force to tackle the opioid epidemic, and led prosecutors statewide in pledging not to criminalize women or doctors for abortion care.
She has worked alongside law enforcement to keep families safe, fought fraud that harms working people, and partnered with gun safety advocates to take illegal guns off the streets.
Shannon Taylor has built her career on protecting Virginians and delivering justice for families regardless of their politics.
