It was a huge success!
It was very gratifying to see the hundreds of people that attended.
I remember just three short years ago when we had around 20 people in attendance.
Our guest speaker was Andrew Yang.
He gave us a brief history of the party and his vision for the future.
He also shared the party progress taking place in Utah and across the United States.
It is exciting to see the momentum in our party and how our message is resonating with others across the country.
Some exciting things are ahead – stay tuned!
In addition to hearing from Andrew, each county group was able to meet and hear directly from their individual candidates and ask questions.
It was wonderful to meet many of you in person and hear about your concerns.
In between sessions, I was able to meet many of the other 23 candidates from throughout Utah.
I am very excited to work with these talented and passionate folks to effectuate positive change in our state beginning next year.
I am so humbled and grateful for your support.
If you haven’t volunteered with us or donated to the campaign, please consider doing so now.
Without your support, I could not run this race.
