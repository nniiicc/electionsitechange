His campaign is focused on three urgent priorities that impact families, the economy, and the future of the state.
Saving the Great Salt Lake The Great Salt Lake is essential to Utah’s environment, public health, and economy.
Colin is committed to bold, science-driven action to stabilize and restore the lake, protect water resources, and ensure long-term sustainability for future generations.
Affordability for Utah Families From housing costs to everyday expenses, too many Utahns are feeling the squeeze.
Colin supports practical solutions to increase housing supply, reduce cost burdens, and make it easier for working families to get ahead and stay in their communities.
Supporting Children Facing Food Insecurity No child in Utah should go hungry.
Colin will advocate for stronger partnerships between schools, community organizations, and state programs to ensure children have reliable access to nutritious meals—both during the school year and outside of it. .
Our Mission Colin Smith is a leader with a career rooted in the outdoor behavioral healthcare field, where he has worked extensively in roles focused on leadership, program development, and client-centered care.
Drawing on this experience, he is now running for the Utah State Senate in District 13 as a candidate with the Forward Party.
His campaign brings a perspective shaped by years of guiding individuals and teams through complex challenges, emphasizing resilience, accountability, and real-world problem solving.
