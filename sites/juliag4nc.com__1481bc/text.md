A Fresh Voice For Progress
Hi, I’m Julia Greenfield and I am honored to be your House District 100 Representative in the North Carolina General Assembly!
Together I believe we can shape a future for all North Carolinians that reflects the better angels of our shared human nature.
A future that emphasizes the right to receive an exceptional education and outstanding healthcare, the right to earn a living wage, the right to clean air and water, and the right to vote.
A future where anyone can feel safe to love whomever they choose to love, where a woman has the right to make her own reproductive health decisions, and where children can be safe in school and on the playground.
I look forward to working with and learning from YOU, the people of District 100, as we stride toward a better tomorrow for all North Carolinians.
Thank you for this opportunity and for your support.
I pledge to do my very best to merit your confidence in me.
