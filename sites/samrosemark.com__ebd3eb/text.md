A lifetime in District 44, ready to serve you in the State Senate.
This community — North St.
Paul, Maplewood, Oakdale, Little Canada, Landfall, and Pine Springs — has been my home my entire life.
From growing up in my mom’s hair salon to rolling dough at my dad’s family bakery , I learned the value of hard work, the power of community, and the importance of making every dollar count.
Those early experiences shaped who I am and inspired me to be of service to others through listening , integrity , and honor .
I am honored to serve as a member of the ISD 622 School Board , where I work alongside families , educators , and community members to make sure every student has the support they need to succeed.
I was elected in a special election, winning every precinct with 62.5% of the vote , and I take seriously the responsibility that comes with that trust.
That experience — l istening, solving problems, and delivering results close to home — is what I will bring with me to the Minnesota State Senate.
I’m running for State Senate because I believe we can build a Minnesota where strong public schools, affordability, sustainability, and opportunity go hand in hand .
A Minnesota where families can get ahead, students can thrive, and government is focused on solving real problems, not creating more of them.
This campaign is about all of us.
I hope you’ll take a moment to learn more about my story and join us by signing up to volunteer and being part of this grassroots, people-powered movement.
Together, we can build a stronger community, a stronger district, and a stronger Minnesota.
