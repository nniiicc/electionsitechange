The Platform
Five priorities for District 6
It comes down to one thing: making it affordable to live and raise a family here.
Homes you can afford, good local jobs, strong schools, healthcare that does not break the bank, and neighborhoods we are proud of.
01
Working families are getting priced out, and Carson City's new housing law was not written for a district like ours.
Katie will fight to make housing relief actually reach District 6 renters and the families it left behind.
02
Bills keep going up but paychecks stay flat.
Katie will bring good local jobs and back the small businesses that hire our neighbors.
03
Our kids deserve schools we can brag about.
Katie will fight for the teachers and the results District 6 students have earned.
04
Nobody should go broke getting sick.
Katie will fight to make healthcare something District 6 families can actually afford.
05
Every family deserves clean, safe neighborhoods.
Katie will take on the homelessness and drug problems people see on their own blocks.
