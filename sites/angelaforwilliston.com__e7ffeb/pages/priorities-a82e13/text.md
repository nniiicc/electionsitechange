Priorities
Social Media, Big Tech, and AI Regulation
Shine a light on the urgent need to protect youth, adults, and democracy through state-led reform of social media platforms, AI proliferation, and data monetization.
Gun Violence Prevention
Enact safer gun laws, including a ban on assault-style weapons like the AR-15.
Intersectional Equity
Identify and abolish systemic and institutional inequities with an understanding of the multi-layered effects of racism, misogyny, homophobia, and all other forms of oppression.
Education
Reimagine public education from birth through adulthood to create a state where lifelong learners thrive, and equity and joy are centered from the start.
Climate Initiatives
Push for climate action and innovation aligned with the Vermont Climate Action Plan.
