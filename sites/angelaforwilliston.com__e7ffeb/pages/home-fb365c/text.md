Leading with curiosity and an open heart.
A little bit about me…
Born in Burlington, I spent parts of my childhood in Central Vermont and the Northeast Kingdom.
I moved to Williston in 2010 after living outside of Vermont for about 15 years because I knew I wanted to raise my kids here.
From 2019 to 2025, I served on the Champlain Valley School District Board of Directors, including three years as Board Chair.
I’ve also connected with our incredible community through membership in the Williston-Richmond Rotary Club, the pandemic-era Williston Joy Brigade, the Education Justice Coalition of Vermont’s organizing committee, and currently as a volunteer with the Williston Community Justice Center.
In every aspect of my life, from my work as a journalist, to my time on the school board – and certainly as a parent of two kids – my inherent curiosity has served me well.
I bring that curiosity, and my core values of equity, integrity, and compassion, to the legislature in your name.
I promise to push for people-centered policies that honor the dignity, humanity, and value of all Vermonters.
