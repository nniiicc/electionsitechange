Priscilla Ghaznavi Independent candidate for
U.S.

Priscilla Ghaznavi is running for Congress in New York’s 7th District with a clear belief: the American Dream is alive when opportunity, accountability, and access move together.
That belief is grounded in how she was raised, the life she has built and the work she has done.
Born in Washington, D.C., to immigrant parents from Iran and Bolivia, Priscilla grew up in a household grounded in education, hard work, and respect for the systems that make progress possible.
Her childhood unfolded on the National Mall, learning to ride a bike, flying kites beneath the Washington Monument, and walking the steps of history at the Thomas Jefferson Memorial and President Kennedy’s final place of rest.
Public service and civic responsibility were part of everyday life, shaping her understanding that government works best when people engage, participate, and lead.
New York has been her home for over three decades.
From Chelsea to Greenpoint to Williamsburg, Priscilla has built her life in the very communities she now seeks to represent.
She knows the rhythms of the district, its strengths, its challenges, and the opportunities waiting to be realized.
Her record reflects consistent engagement in organizing, advocating, and bringing forward ideas that translate into action and measurable impact.
Designing our future together is the work she has been doing for years.
Priscilla is a designer by trade, with a career that spans seven publicly traded companies across business and creative leadership.
She builds systems, aligns teams, and delivers results that scale.
She understands how decisions move from concept to execution and how to ensure they serve people in tangible ways.
Her academic background includes a bachelor’s degree in Marketing with a focus on International Trade, as well as additional degrees in design, merchandising, and advanced studies in Milan.
While in college, she was elected President of New York State Delta Epsilon Chi (DECA), representing colleges and universities statewide, an early demonstration of her ability to lead, organize, and advocate.
In May 2026, she earned her fourth degree, in Entrepreneurship, and chose to spend graduation day in the community meeting neighbors and collecting the signatures needed to secure her place on the general election ballot.
Her perspective is both global and deeply local.
Fluent in English and Castellano, Priscilla has traveled extensively throughout the Americas, Europe, West Asia, Asia, and North Africa and Australia.
These experiences sharpened her understanding of how policy shapes economies, and everyday life across different communities, and how the United States can lead with strength and clarity.
At home, her leadership is equally substantive.
As past President of her Homeowners Association, during the COVID-19 pandemic, she transformed a financially strained organization into a stable, well-managed community with strong reserves.
She worked across budgets, contracts, governance, and resident concerns, delivering outcomes that are structured, sustainable, and repeatable.
Her approach to leadership is disciplined and clear: listen closely, solve problems iteratively, and build solutions that last.
Priscilla is running as an independent voice rooted in action, accountability and results.
Her campaign is centered on a simple, unifying idea: Our Future™.
A future shaped with intention, built through action.
Forward with focus, purpose, and a commitment to deliver for the people of New York’s 7th Congressional District.

HOUSING
Housing is the foundation of stability, and homeownership is the pathway to generational wealth.
I will advance policies that expand access to affordable housing and homeownership for working families while ensuring property taxes reflect income realities rather than just rising market values.
My focus is on protecting families through life's uncertainties so they can stay rooted in their communities.
Strong housing policy builds ownership, stability, and long-term opportunity.
HEALTH CARE
Healthcare should be a fundamental human right, and access should be made available for every person.
Expanding mental health services into schools, neighborhoods, and community spaces so care is present where people live their lives.
Prevention, early support, and continuity of care will define the system.
Healthcare will be accessible, community-based, and centered on the full well-being of every individual.
NEURODIVERSITY
Every individual deserves the opportunity to reach their full potential.
I will champion federal investments that expand community based services, strengthen support for individuals with autism, intellectual and developmental disabilities, traumatic brain injuries, and mental health challenges, while increasing resources for the families and caregivers who stand beside them.
Every person deserves support that leads to independence, the opportunity to live with dignity, purpose and connection.
EDUCATION
Education in New York's 7th District will lead in computer science and career readiness.
I will work to establish a direct pipeline from our schools to the jobs growing in our district through early exposure, skills training, and real industry partnerships.
Students will graduate with both knowledge and opportunity in hand.
Education will serve as a launchpad into the future economy.
INNOVATION
I support innovation in Web 3.0 to keep our district and country globally competitive, while establishing clear guardrails that prioritize transparency, accountability, and long-term impact.
I will support policies that keep our district at the forefront of technology while establishing clear standards for transparency, security, and long-term responsibility.
Builders, users, and communities will operate within a trusted and well-defined framework.
The future of technology should be both dynamic and dependable.
This is about building a digital future that people can trust.
Innovation and integrity must go hand in hand.
CHILD CARE
Affordable, dependable childcare strengthens families and keeps our economy moving.
I will champion federal investments that expand access to quality childcare, strengthen the childcare workforce, and give working parents greater flexibility to guild careers, businesses, and financial security.
Childcare should create opportunity for children and parents alike, giving families the foundation they need to work, grow and thrive.
ELDER CARE
Our seniors built our communities and should not be priced out of them.
I will fight for tax relief that reflects fixed incomes, not inflated market values, and ensure access to community-based support systems that meet a wide range of needs, ®aging with dignity, stability, and connection, not isolation or financial stress.
Protecting our elderly is protecting our foundation and their evolving needs.
The strength of our district will reflect how we care for those who came before us.
WOMEN'S RIGHTS
Women's rights are economic, healthcare, and human rights, period.
Advocating and advancing equal pay, workplace equity, and comprehensive reproductive care and support.
Systems are designed to ensure fairness, dignity, and opportunity at every stage of life.
Exploring how equity can be measurable.
THE ARTS
The arts are the shared infrastructure of culture and identity, and a vital force in our community and economy.
I will work to expand federal programs that provide direct funding to support artists in our district, while creating more opportunities for artists to share their work in public and community spaces.
I'm focused on making various areas of the arts reachable, visaible and locally impactful.
Investing in the arts means investing in the voices and futures of our communities and diverse cultures.
Creative expression will be visible, accessible, and sustained.
The arts will continue to shape our identity and drive local and global vitality.
PUBLIC SAFETY
Public Safety is rooted in stability, opportunity, and connection.
I will work to deliver criminal justice policies that integrate social services, life skills, workforce development, and long-term support systems to guide individuals toward completing their full path to stability, rather than abandoning them after their first step.
Safer communities come from solving root causes.
Safety will be built through sustained investment in people.
SMALL BUSINESSES
Small businesses and entrepreneurs turn ideas into jobs, opportunity, and generational wealth.
I will champion federal policies that expand access to capital, strengthen SBA resources, open doors to federal contracting, and give entrepreneurs the tools to start, grow and scale their businesses.
By removing barriers to opportunity and investing in the people wiling to build, we can keep more businesses and more of the wealth they create rooted in our communities.
SHARED SPACES
Our shared spaces should be safe, clean, and truly usable for everyone in the community.
Prioritizing federal support the helps local governments maintain public spaces while addressing the root causes of disorder connecting individuals facing addiction and mental health challenges to real services, not just pushing them from one block to another.
This is about restoring dignity and safety at the same time, with solutions that are both compassionate and effective.
Public space should belong to the public fully, safely and without compromise.
LOWER COSTS
Lower costs will be achieved through targeted, practical policy that reduces the financial burden on working families.
I will address the drivers of high costs across housing, healthcare, and everyday essentials with a focus on efficiency, transparency, and fairness.
Relief will be tangible and consistent.
Economic stability will be built into daily life.
