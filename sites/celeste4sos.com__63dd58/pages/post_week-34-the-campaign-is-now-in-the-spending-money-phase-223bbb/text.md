The Secretary of State race is not getting much media attention, but voters have their ballots now so everyone can see that my name is the last of the five listed.
The major-party candidates are always listed first, followed by minor-party candidates, and then unaffiliated candidates.
The gubernatorial and competitive congressional races are getting the most attention.
In those races, partisanship matters!
The governor can influence and veto legislation.
Control of Congress may flip as a result of these midterm elections.
No candidate in the Secretary of State contest has a statewide profile.
Without looking at your ballot, can you name the D and R candidates for Secretary of State?
More than half of Colorado’s voters are unaffiliated, and many voters who do identify with a political party are tired of the demand for blind party loyalty.
You do NOT have to vote a straight-party ballot – especially for a nonpartisan-type office like Secretary of State.
