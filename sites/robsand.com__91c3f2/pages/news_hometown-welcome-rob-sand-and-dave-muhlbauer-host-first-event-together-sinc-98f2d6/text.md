Hometown Welcome: Rob Sand and Dave Muhlbauer Host First Event Together Since LG Announcement in Western Iowa
MANNING, IA – Yesterday, nominee for governor Rob Sand and candidate for lieutenant governor Dave Muhlbauer held their first joint event since Dave was announced as Rob’s running mate.
Around 200 Iowans from the surrounding community attended the moderated Q&A in Manning to ask questions and hear directly from Rob and Dave about their vision to put public service over politics in Iowa.
The crowd included Republicans, Democrats, and independents from across western Iowa, highlighting the broad appeal of the Sand-Muhlbauer ticket.
