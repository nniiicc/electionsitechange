Buck Fever: Rob Sand Submits Trophy Whitetail to Big Buck Contest
DES MOINES, IA – On Friday, candidate for governor Rob Sand submitted his 206-inch gross trophy whitetail to the archery non-typical section of the Big Buck Contest at the 2026 Iowa Deer Classic, the state’s premier three-day hunting convention celebrating Iowa’s storied whitetail tradition.
Rob’s buck made its journey from its mount on the walls of the Auditor’s office — affectionately known as the “public sector Cabela’s” — to the Classic, where it was on display alongside other trophy whitetails from across the state.
The submission also coincided with the launch of the campaign’s new website, HuntingWithRob.com, where people can learn more about Rob’s hunting background, the story of harvesting his 206-inch buck, and how Rob will take his values as a hunter to the governor’s office.

Des Moines Register: Governor candidate Rob Sand enters buck into Iowa deer contest
- State auditor Rob Sand, a Democratic candidate for governor, isn’t shy about his love for hunting.
He’s been doing it his whole life, but last year, he harvested a buck of a lifetime.
- The deer was impressive enough — 209 inches gross, a measurement of all the deer’s points, length and spread — that he decided to enter it in the 2026 Iowa Deer Expo’s Iowa’s Biggest Buck Contest.
- “The best part about tagging this buck here was my dad … was in town that night, so I was able to bring it over to him and to celebrate with him and tell him the story,” he said.
