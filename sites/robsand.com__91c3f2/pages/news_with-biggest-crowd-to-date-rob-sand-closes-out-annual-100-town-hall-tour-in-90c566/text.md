With Biggest Crowd to Date, Rob Sand Closes Out Annual 100 Town Hall Tour in Polk County
DES MOINES, IA – Last week, nominee for governor Rob Sand wrapped up his 2026 100 Town Hall Tour in Polk County, with more than 1,800 Iowans packing the Val Air Ballroom for the largest town hall to date.
This marks the second year in a row as a candidate for governor — and the ninth year overall as a public servant — that Rob has held a public town hall in every Iowa county.
Last week, Rob traveled to Poweshiek, Tama, Black Hawk, Scott, and Polk counties, connecting with nearly 4,000 Iowans in their communities and answering their honest, unscripted questions.
Throughout the tour, Iowans packed union halls, VFWs, college campuses, and community rooms to take part in one of Iowa’s most sacred political traditions: a public town hall.
Rob believes this is what public service should be about: showing up, listening, answering questions, and being accountable to the people you serve.
As governor, Rob will keep doing exactly that, because you can’t serve people well if you aren’t willing to hear from them.
He’s even gone as far as proposing mandatory public town halls for elected officials as one of his top policy priorities.
