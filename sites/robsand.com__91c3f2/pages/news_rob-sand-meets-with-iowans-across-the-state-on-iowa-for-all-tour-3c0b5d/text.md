On Friday, Rob started in Marshalltown with a tour of the JBS Plant, then met with small business owners and local government leaders for a roundtable discussion.
He then stopped by the Iowa Veterans Home to meet with residents and answer their questions, and ended the day in Waukee for a rally with supporters.
Rob spent Saturday meeting with voters in Newton and St.
Ansgar to hear firsthand about the issues that matter most to their communities before joining Iowans at Mason City’s Oktoberfest.
On Sunday, he spent time with festivalgoers and walked in the Madison County Covered Bridges Festival in Winterset.
Rob kept his foot on the gas Monday, starting with a roundtable with small business owners and community leaders in Dubuque before visiting local businesses in Waterloo, and sitting down with college students at the University of Northern Iowa and Coe College to hear about their hopes for Iowa’s future.
He closed out the day with a rally in Cedar Rapids, continuing to make the case for bringing people together, putting Iowans ahead of politics, and making our state not redder or bluer, but better and truer.
