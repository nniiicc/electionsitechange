The first ad, titled “ One Reason ,” features Wayne from O’Brien County, a self-described “tax-hating Republican” that has never voted for a Democrat for governor – until now.
Wayne is supporting Rob because he knows he supports low taxes for everyday Iowans as they face a crisis of costs and that he’ll hold corrupt politicians accountable – regardless of party.
The second ad, titled “ Down the Road ,” focuses on Laurie, a cancer survivor from Earlville.
In the ad, Laurie highlights Zach Lahn’s support for cuts that endanger rural Iowa hospitals and his efforts to strip health care away from everyday people.
Laurie warns that Zach Lahn won’t have to live with the consequences of his actions because he can just fly back to Kansas to avoid them.
