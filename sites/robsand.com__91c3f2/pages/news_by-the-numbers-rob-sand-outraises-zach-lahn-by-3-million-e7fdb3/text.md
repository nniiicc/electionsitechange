BY THE NUMBERS: Rob Sand Outraises Zach Lahn by $3 Million
DES MOINES, IA – Yesterday, the Rob Sand campaign reported raising a record-breaking $9.38 million in less than two months, breaking state records by more than three times the previous record and outraising his opponent by $3 million.
The Sand campaign also received more individual contributions, including from registered Republican voters and Iowa voters in all 99 counties.
By the Numbers:
- Team Sand: 33,500 individual contributions this reporting period, with donations from 2,400 registered Republicans over the course of the campaign
- Lahn: 431 this reporting period
- Team Sand: 95% of contributions $100 or less
- Lahn: 61% of contributions $100 or less
- Team Sand: average contribution $280
- Lahn: average contribution $14,786
- Team Sand: $17.9 million cash on hand
- Lahn: $1.04 million cash on hand
Read more about Rob’s “record-breaking” haul below:
- Des Moines Register: Sand’s record haul widens cash gap in Iowa governor race
- Democratic state Auditor Rob Sand announced raising nearly $9.4 million, bringing his total fundraising for the election cycle to a record-breaking $37.8 million.
- Republican nominee Zach Lahn notched about $6.4 million in total fundraising — a figure that was buoyed significantly by major donations from the Republican Governors Association.
The national group has given $5 million directly to Lahn’s campaign since the primary, and it has paid for another $15,000 in advertising on his behalf.
- Iowa Capital Dispatch: Sand breaks Iowa fundraising record; Lahn receives large party PAC donations
With total expenditures accounted for, the Sand campaign has more than $17.95 million on hand as of July 14.
- Despite having more than $7 million on hand before expenditures, the Lahn campaign has spent more than $5.9 million during the fundraising period, leaving more than $1.04 million on hand.
- Radio Iowa: Sand with fundraising lead over Lahn in Iowa governor’s race
- The reports cover fundraising from May 27 through July 14.
Sand’s campaign says over 33,000 people donated during that seven week stretch.
About 78% of that money came from the Republican Governors Association.
- KWQC: Sand raises more than $9M in less than 2 months in Iowa governor race
- With four months until the election, Sand’s campaign has $17.9 million in cash on hand, according to his campaign and the Iowa Ethics and Campaign Disclosure Board.
His campaign manager said the financial haul shows Iowans are ready for change.
