Skip to content About Endorsements Get Involved News Issues Store Donate Latest News Nevada State Controller Andy Matthews Endorses Cody K Whipple for Congress in Nevada’s 4th Congressional District October 15, 2026 Las Vegas, NV – Andy Matthews, Nevada’s State Controller and Republican candidate for reelection, has endorsed Cody K Whipple for Congress in Nevada’s 4th Congressional District, adding his support to Whipple’s growing coalition of Nevada leaders backing his campaign.
Matthews, who has served as Nevada’s State Controller since 2023, previously represented Assembly District 37 in the Nevada Legislature and served as President of the Nevada Policy Research Institute.
His public service has focused on government transparency, fiscal accountability, and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
“Nevada needs leaders in Washington who understand that government should work for the people, not the other way around,” said Andy Matthews.
“Cody Whipple understands Nevada because he has spent his life here.
He is a fourth-generation Nevadan, a rancher, and a businessman who knows firsthand the challenges facing families, small businesses, and rural communities.
I’m proud to endorse Cody because I believe he will bring a strong Nevada voice to Congress and stand up for fiscal responsibility, individual liberty, and accountable government.” Whipple welcomed Matthews’ endorsement, highlighting the Controller’s record of advocating for greater transparency and accountability in government.
“Andy Matthews has spent his career standing up for Nevada taxpayers and demanding greater accountability from government,” said Cody K Whipple.
“I’m honored to have his support.
Andy understands that Nevada doesn’t need more Washington politics.
We need leaders who will fight for our families, businesses, ranchers, and communities and put Nevada First.” Whipple has built his campaign around a Nevada First agenda focused on lowering costs, cutting unnecessary regulations, supporting small businesses and ranchers, strengthening public safety and border security, and protecting Nevada’s way of life.
His campaign has emphasized bringing a local voice to Washington, D.C., rather than allowing Washington priorities to dictate the future of Nevada.
Matthews’ endorsement adds to a growing list of Nevada leaders and organizations supporting Whipple’s campaign, citing his Nevada roots and commitment to putting Nevada families and communities first.
“This campaign is about one simple principle: Nevada First,” said Whipple.
“I’m grateful to have Andy Matthews standing with us as we work to bring Nevada’s values, priorities, and voice to Washington.” To learn more or get involved, visit codyk4congress.com .
