Skip to content About Endorsements Get Involved News Issues Store Donate Latest News Mesquite Conservatives Endorse Cody K Whipple for Congress in Nevada’s 4th District April 23, 2026 HIKO, NV – The Mesquite Conservatives have officially endorsed Cody K Whipple in his campaign for Congress in Nevada’s 4th District, citing his strong commitment to conservative principles and his unwavering focus on putting Nevada first.
In a statement accompanying their endorsement, the Mesquite Conservatives praised Whipple’s character and leadership, stating: “Cody Whipple is a man of integrity, someone we believe can be trusted to do what is best for Nevadans.” Cody K Whipple, a businessman, rancher, and longtime advocate for Nevada families, continues to build momentum across the district with a Nevada First focus—prioritizing lower costs of living, energy independence, border security, and policies that protect Nevada jobs, land, and water.
“I’m honored to earn the support of the Mesquite Conservatives,” said Whipple.
“Their endorsement reflects a shared belief that Nevada deserves strong, principled leadership that puts our state—not Washington politics—first.
Together, we’re fighting to protect our way of life and ensure a brighter future for every Nevada family.” The endorsement underscores growing grassroots support for Whipple’s campaign as voters across the 4th District rally behind his vision for a safer, stronger, and more prosperous Nevada.
