HIKO, NV – Cody K Whipple, Republican candidate for Congress in Nevada’s 4th Congressional District, today announced the endorsement of State Senator Jeff Stone.
Senator Stone, a conservative leader and advocate for lower taxes and supporting small businesses, said Whipple is the right leader to represent Nevada families in Congress, and flip Nevada’s 4th Congressional District from blue to red.
“I’m proud to support Cody K Whipple for Congress.
Cody is a true conservative who will fight to lower taxes and the cost of living, make healthcare more available and affordable for Nevada families, and stand up for the small businesses and workers who drive our economy,” said Senator Jeff Stone.
“Unfortunately, Democrat Steven Horsford has spent years in Washington voting with the far-left agenda that has fueled inflation, higher energy costs, and border policies that Nevada families simply cannot afford.
Nevada deserves a representative who will put Nevadans first, and Cody Whipple is the leader who will bring common-sense conservative leadership to Congress.”
Whipple welcomed the endorsement and said he looks forward to working with Senator Stone to advance policies that strengthen Nevada’s economy and support working families.
“Senator Jeff Stone has been a strong voice for conservative values and economic growth,” said Whipple.
“I’m honored to have his support as we work to restore fiscal responsibility and lower taxes for Nevada families.”
Cody K Whipple is a Nevada businessman, rancher, and community leader who has spent more than 25 years building a successful company serving first responders, school districts, construction companies, and businesses across four states.
His campaign for Congress is focused on lowering the cost of living, strengthening the economy, securing the border, and restoring accountability in Washington.
This endorsement adds to growing support for his campaign across Nevada’s 4th congressional district.
