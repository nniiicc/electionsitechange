Skip to content About Endorsements Get Involved News Issues Store Donate Latest News Nevada Farm Bureau President Bevan Lister Endorses Cody K Whipple for Congress March 9, 2026 Fourth-generation Nevadan and rancher earns support from state agricultural leader HIKO, NV – Today, Bevan Lister, President of the Nevada Farm Bureau, announced his endorsement of Cody K Whipple in his campaign for Congress, citing Whipple’s deep Nevada roots and strong understanding of the needs of rural communities.
“Cody Whipple understands the needs of all Nevadans, and as a rancher and 4th generation Nevadan, he can be trusted to advocate and deliver for us in Congress,” said Bevan Lister, President of the Nevada Farm Bureau.
Lister’s endorsement underscores growing support for Whipple among Nevada’s agricultural and rural leaders who are seeking strong representation in Washington, D.C.
Whipple expressed appreciation for the endorsement and emphasized the importance of protecting Nevada’s agricultural heritage and rural way of life.
“I’m honored to have the support of Bevan Lister,” said Cody K Whipple.
“Nevada’s farmers and ranchers are the backbone of our state, and I’m committed to ensuring their voices are heard in Washington.
I’ll always fight to protect our land, water, and the hardworking families who keep Nevada strong.” Cody K Whipple’s campaign for Congress is focused on putting Nevada and Nevadans first.
This endorsement adds to growing support for his campaign across Nevada’s 4 th congressional district.
