HIKO, NV – Cody K Whipple, candidate for Congress, today announced the endorsement of Joe Brown, who voiced strong support for Whipple’s leadership and commitment to Nevada values.
Joe Brown has decades of community and civic leadership in Las Vegas, serving as Commissioner of the Nevada Gaming Commission, the Nevada Department of Wildlife, and the Nevada Athletic Commission, among many other community leadership roles.
Brown praised Whipple’s deep understanding of Nevada and his readiness to fight for the state in Washington D.C.
“Cody Whipple’s Nevada credentials are without compare,” said Joe Brown.
“He is a true leader and champion for Nevada.
You can count on him to fight for our principles in Washington, D.C.; to protect our way of life.”
Cody K Whipple welcomed the endorsement, emphasizing the importance of earning the trust of respected Nevada leaders and community voices.
“I’m honored to have Joe Brown’s support,” Whipple said.
“Nevadans deserve a representative who will stand up for our communities, protect our values, and put Nevada first—always.”
The endorsement adds momentum to Whipple’s growing coalition of supporters who believe in a strong, independent voice for Nevada in Congress.
