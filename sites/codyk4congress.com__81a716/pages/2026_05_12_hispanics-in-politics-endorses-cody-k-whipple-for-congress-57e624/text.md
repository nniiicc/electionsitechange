LAS VEGAS, NV – Hispanics in Politics has officially endorsed Cody K Whipple in Nevada’s 4th Congressional District race, citing his commitment to service, community engagement, and dedication to issues impacting Hispanic families across Nevada.
The endorsement marks another significant show of support for Whipple’s growing Nevada First coalition, which continues to bring together community leaders, working families, veterans, small business owners, and advocates from across the state who are ready for new leadership focused on Nevada values and Nevada priorities.
“Your willingness to serve is an inspiration, and we are grateful for the dedication you bring to your campaign,” said Hispanics in Politics in a statement announcing the endorsement.
“Your platform and commitment to the Hispanic community align closely with our mission, and we are confident you will be a strong voice for our community.”
Whipple, a fourth-generation Nevadan, has centered his campaign on lowering costs for families, supporting small businesses, strengthening our border, supporting every parent having a voice in their children’s education, and ensuring every Nevadan has a voice in Washington.
“I am honored to earn the endorsement of Hispanics in Politics,” said Cody K Whipple.
“Nevada’s Hispanic community plays a vital role in the strength, culture, and future of our state.
I look forward to continuing to earn the trust of voters by fighting for opportunity, economic growth, and policies that put Nevada families first.”
The endorsement adds to the momentum behind the Whipple campaign as supporters across Nevada rally around a message of hard work, integrity, and putting Nevada First.
