Local Business Leader Joins Growing District-Wide Support
HIKO, NV – Today, Las Vegas area REALTOR and business leader Alex Garza announced his endorsement of Cody K Whipple in the race for Congress, citing Whipple’s decades of business experience and proven commitment to serving communities across Nevada and the West.
Garza, a respected member of the Southern Nevada business and real estate community, said Whipple’s background as a successful entrepreneur and problem-solver makes him the right choice to represent Nevadans in Washington.
“I endorse Cody K Whipple for Congress.
He’s spent the past 25 years building a successful business, and serving first responders, school districts, construction companies, and businesses in four states.
I’m confident he will bring that same get-it-done approach to Congress and deliver on lower energy, housing, and healthcare that Nevadans are missing,” said Alex Garza.
Whipple welcomed Garza’s support and emphasized the importance of community leaders stepping forward to help bring real-world experience and practical solutions to Congress.
“Alex Garza understands the challenges facing families, homeowners, and businesses in Southern Nevada,” said Cody K Whipple.
“He knows how important it is to have leaders who will focus on results—lowering costs, expanding housing opportunities, and strengthening our economy.
I’m honored to have his support.”
Garza joins a growing coalition of community leaders, business owners, and advocates across Nevada who are backing Whipple’s campaign.
Supporters point to Whipple’s record of building a successful company and working closely with first responders, school districts, construction firms, and businesses across four states as evidence that he will bring practical leadership and accountability to Washington.
Whipple, a fourth-generation Nevadan and business owner, is running for Congress to restore economic opportunity, strengthen local communities, and deliver commonsense solutions that lower costs for Nevada families.
This endorsement adds to growing support for his campaign across Nevada’s 4th congressional district.
