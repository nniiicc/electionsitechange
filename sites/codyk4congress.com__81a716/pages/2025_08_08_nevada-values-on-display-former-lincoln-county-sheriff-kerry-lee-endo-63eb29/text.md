Lincoln County, NV—In a powerful show of support for Nevada’s future, retired Lincoln County Sheriff Kerry Lee has officially endorsed Cody K.
Whipple in his campaign for Nevada’s 4th Congressional District.
This endorsement underscores a shared, unwavering dedication to putting Nevada first and elevating public safety as a top priority.
Whipple is challenging career-politician and Democrat Congressman Steven Horsford.
Cody Whipple, a fourth-generation Nevadan rancher and small business owner, brings lived experience to leadership.
His decades of ranching, small business success, and his creation of a critical two-way communications network for first responders across the Desert Southwest represent Nevada values in action.
A Nevada-First Vision
- Rooted in Rural Nevada: Raised on his family’s cattle ranch in Hiko, Cody knows firsthand the value of self-reliance, perseverance, and community.
- Bolstering Public Safety Infrastructure: Through his communications business, Cody has enhanced emergency response capabilities by connecting first responders, schools, and agencies across rural and urban Nevada.
- Trusted by Law Enforcement: The endorsement from Sheriff Kerry Lee reinforces that Cody’s commitment to safety is recognized and validated by seasoned public servants.
“As we face politics as usual from Washington D.C. representatives, we need someone who represents the heart of Nevada,” said Cody K.
Whipple.
“Sheriff Lee’s endorsement reminds us that real leadership isn’t about politics—it’s about character, action, and putting the people and safety of our state first.
This is my promise to Nevada.”
