Las Vegas, NV – Congressman Burgess Owens has endorsed Cody K Whipple for Congress in Nevada’s 4th Congressional District, adding his support to Whipple’s growing coalition of conservative leaders, elected officials, law enforcement, community leaders, and Nevadans from across the district.
Owens, a former NFL player and conservative leader who represents Utah’s 4th Congressional District, praised Whipple’s leadership, determination, and commitment to delivering results for Nevada families.
“In football, success comes down to basic fundamentals: discipline, toughness, and execution under pressure.
Cody Whipple brings that gridiron grit to the fight for our conservative principles.
Cody knows leadership isn’t given, it’s earned by meeting expectations with results,” said Congressman Burgess Owens.
“Nevada doesn’t need more career politicians playing defense for the status quo; Nevada needs instead a proven leader willing to go on offense to cut wasteful spending, secure our borders, and protect our freedoms.
I am proud to endorse Cody Whipple for Congress because he has the backbone to deliver real wins for Nevada families.”
A fourth-generation Nevadan, rancher, and small-business owner, Whipple is focused on Nevada First: lowering costs, cutting government waste and red tape, securing the border, protecting individual freedoms, supporting small businesses and ranchers, and ensuring Nevada has a strong voice in Washington, D.C.
“I am honored to have the endorsement of Congressman Burgess Owens,” said Cody K Whipple.
“Congressman Owens understands what it takes to compete, lead, and deliver results, and he has taken that same discipline and determination to Washington.
His support reinforces what this campaign is all about: putting Nevada first and fighting for the conservative principles that make our state such a great place to live, work, and raise a family.”
Whipple added, “Nevadans don’t need another career politician who is comfortable with the status quo.
They need a leader who understands our communities firsthand and is willing to go on offense for Nevada.
I’m ready to bring that fight to Washington.”
As a fourth-generation Nevadan, Whipple is running to bring a local voice and Nevada First perspective to Congress and deliver results for families, businesses, ranchers, veterans, and communities across the 4th Congressional District.
