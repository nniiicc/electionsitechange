Las Vegas, NV —Tonight, GOP businessman and rancher Cody K.
Whipple issued the following statement in response to fellow candidate Aaron Hill’s decision to suspend his campaign for Nevada’s 4th Congressional District and throw his support behind Cody:
“I want to begin by expressing my heartfelt gratitude to Aaron Hill—for his service to our nation, his bold run in this race, and the principled decision he’s made tonight.
Aaron’s statement speaks volumes about his commitment to putting country before self and ensuring our shared conservative agenda moves forward.
As he emphasized, ‘by working together we’re stronger,’ and I couldn’t agree more.
“Aaron’s vision of secure borders, support for veterans, affordable communities, and lower costs is exactly what this campaign is built on.
I am humbled by his endorsement and fully embrace his call for unity.
“To the volunteers, donors, and supporters of Aaron Hill: thank you.
I pledge to honor your efforts, to listen to your ideas, and to carry the torch of our shared conservative values to Washington.
“We are entering a pivotal moment—not just for Nevada’s 4th District, but for the future of our country.
Together, with Aaron’s backing and your continued support, we will deliver economic independence, protect individual freedoms, champion responsible stewardship of our land and resources, and ensure those who’ve worn the uniform never stand alone.
“Tonight, we are moving forward and we are working together—veterans to ranchers, urban to rural—we will win this seat and make Nevada proud.”
