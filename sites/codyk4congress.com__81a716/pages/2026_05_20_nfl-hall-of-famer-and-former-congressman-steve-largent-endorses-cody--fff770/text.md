HIKO, NV – Cody K Whipple for Congress proudly announced today the endorsement of Steve Largent, one of the most respected figures in both professional football and public service.
Largent, a Pro Football Hall of Fame inductee and longtime standout for the Seattle Seahawks, built a legendary career defined by hard work, discipline, and integrity.
During his 14 NFL seasons, he earned seven Pro Bowl selections and retired as the league’s all-time leader in receptions, receiving yards, and touchdown catches.
Following his football career, Largent served four terms in the United States House of Representatives representing Oklahoma’s 1st Congressional District.
Whipple said he was honored to receive the endorsement from one of the most accomplished athlete-statesmen in American history.
“Steve Largent represents the very best of leadership — a relentless competitor, a principled public servant, and someone who has always led with integrity,” said Cody K Whipple.
“His career both on and off the field is an example of what can be accomplished through hard work, consistency, and faith in the American people.
I’m proud to have his support as we continue building a Nevada First movement focused on putting Nevada families, workers, and communities first.”
Largent’s endorsement adds to the growing momentum behind the Whipple campaign as supporters across Nevada rally behind a candidate focused on strengthening the economy, protecting Nevada values, supporting veterans, and delivering results for working families.
