I was encouraged to hear a continued focus on rebuilding our economy in ways that reward hard work, support small businesses, and create pathways to prosperity for the next generation.
When we prioritize growth, innovation, and fiscal responsibility, we empower communities across our nation – especially here in Nevada.
“I was also proud to hear a clear commitment to strong foreign policy leadership.
America’s role on the world stage matters.
Peace through strength, standing by our allies, and confronting emerging threats with resolve are essential to keeping our nation safe and preserving freedom at home and abroad.
“The President’s support for mandatory national voter ID reflects a commitment to protecting the integrity of our elections.
Confidence in our democratic process is foundational, and ensuring secure, fair, and transparent elections strengthens trust in our system of government.
“Finally, I applaud the President’s commitment to parental choice and educational freedom.
Parents deserve a meaningful voice in their children’s education.
Expanding educational choice opens doors, encourages innovation, and helps ensure every child has access to an environment where they can thrive.
“As we move forward, I remain committed to working with anyone who shares the goal of building a stronger, safer, and more prosperous America, and as always will put Nevada and Nevadans first!” To learn more or get involved, visit codyk4congress.com .
