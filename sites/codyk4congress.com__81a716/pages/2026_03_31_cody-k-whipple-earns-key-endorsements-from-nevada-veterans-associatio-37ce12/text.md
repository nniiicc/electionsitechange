These endorsements highlight Whipple’s strong commitment to supporting veterans, military families, and the values of service, leadership, and country that define Nevada’s veteran community.
“Earning the support of organizations that represent the brave men and women who have served our nation is a tremendous honor,” said Cody K Whipple.
“Our veterans have sacrificed so much to protect our freedoms, and I am committed to ensuring they receive the care, respect, and opportunities they deserve here at home.” The endorsement of the Nevada Veterans Association, a respected voice for veterans across the state, recognizes Whipple’s leadership, integrity, and understanding of the challenges facing Nevada’s veteran population.
Veterans in Politics is a nonpartisan organization dedicated to advancing policies that support veterans and promote civic engagement.
“I understand the importance of putting veterans first,” said Whipple.
“As your next Congressman, I will always stand with those who stood for us.
Together, we will work to build a stronger, more supportive future for Nevada’s veterans and their families.” To learn more or get involved, visit codyk4congress.com .
