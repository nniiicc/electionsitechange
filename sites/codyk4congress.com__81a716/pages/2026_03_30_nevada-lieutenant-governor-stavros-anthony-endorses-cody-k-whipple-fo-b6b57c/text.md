HIKO, NV – Cody K Whipple, Republican candidate for Nevada’s 4th Congressional District, today proudly announced the endorsement of Nevada Lieutenant Governor Stavros Anthony, adding to the campaign’s growing momentum and coalition of respected conservative leaders across the state.
A fourth-generation Nevadan, small business owner, and rancher, Whipple has built his campaign around fighting for working families, supporting small businesses, protecting Nevada’s natural resources, and restoring accountability in Washington.
In announcing his endorsement, Lieutenant Governor Stavros Anthony praised Whipple’s deep roots in Nevada and commitment to conservative principles:
“As Nevada’s Lieutenant Governor and a longtime public servant, I’ve spent my career fighting for the principles that make our state strong: limited government, public safety, and economic freedom.
That’s why I’m proud to support Cody K Whipple for Congress.
As a fourth-generation Nevadan, Cody understands our heritage, our communities, and the importance of protecting our land, water, and way of life.
He’ll stand up in Washington, D.C. to defend Nevada values, push back against federal overreach, and fight for policies that put Nevadans first.
Cody Whipple is the kind of leader who will represent Nevada with strength and integrity.”
Whipple welcomed the endorsement, emphasizing the importance of strong leadership and shared values:
“Lieutenant Governor Anthony has spent decades serving the people of Nevada.
I am honored to have his support as we continue building a campaign focused on putting Nevada first.
Together, we will fight to protect our freedoms, strengthen our communities, and ensure that Washington works for the people, not the other way around.”
Raised on his family’s ranch in Hiko, Whipple understands firsthand the challenges facing both rural and suburban Nevadans.
Over the past 25 years, he has built a successful small business serving first responders, schools, and critical infrastructure across the region, giving him real-world experience in job creation and public safety.
Whipple’s campaign continues to gain support from leaders across Nevada who recognize his commitment to a “Nevada First” agenda focused on lowering costs, supporting public safety, defending constitutional freedoms, and protecting the state’s land and water resources.
