As law enforcement officers, first responders, and community leaders continue to join his coalition, the message is clear: Nevada’s protectors trust Cody Whipple to have their backs.
“I am honored to earn the endorsement of Kenny Taylor,” said Whipple.
“Our public safety community deserves leaders who listen and act in their best interest.
