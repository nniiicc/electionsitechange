Nevada Leaders Back Whipple’s Nevada First Vision for NV-04
HIKO, NV – Cody K Whipple, Republican candidate for Nevada’s 4th Congressional District, today announced the endorsement of Jacob D.
“Jay” Bingham, who praised Whipple’s commitment to putting Nevada families, businesses, and communities first.
Bingham’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition of Nevada leaders, business owners, community advocates, and grassroots supporters rallying behind Whipple’s Nevada First campaign.
“As a former firefighter and Clark County Commissioner, I’ve always believed results matter and we need to look beyond labels,” said Jay Bingham.
“Cody Whipple is the kind of leader who puts Nevada first.
He understands our communities, our economy, and the challenges facing families in this region.
For those from any party looking for a practical, solutions-oriented voice in Congress, I encourage them to support Cody K Whipple.”
Whipple thanked Bingham for his support and emphasized the importance of bringing common-sense leadership to Washington.
“I am honored to have Jay Bingham’s endorsement,” said Cody K Whipple.
“Jay has demonstrated a deep commitment to Nevada and understands what is at stake for our state.
For too long, Nevada families have been burdened by rising costs, limited access to healthcare, and policies that put Washington politics ahead of Nevada priorities.
My campaign is built on a simple principle: Nevada First.
That means fighting every day for lower energy costs, more affordable housing, better healthcare access, safer communities, and greater economic opportunity.
Together, we can bring accountable leadership to Congress and deliver the results Nevadans deserve.”
Whipple’s Nevada First platform focuses on addressing the issues that matter most to Nevada families, including reducing the cost of living, expanding economic opportunity, supporting law enforcement, protecting rural communities, strengthening water and energy infrastructure, and improving access to quality healthcare.
As the campaign continues to build momentum across the district, Whipple remains focused on earning the support of voters who are ready for a representative who will put Nevada’s interests first and work tirelessly to achieve meaningful results.
