Skip to content About Endorsements Get Involved News Issues Store Donate Latest News GOP Floor Leader Assemblyman Gregory Hafen II Endorses Cody K Whipple for Congress in Nevada’s 4th District April 16, 2026 HIKO, NV – Today, Cody K Whipple announced the endorsement of Assemblyman Gregory Hafen II, a respected conservative leader and advocate for Nevada families, small businesses, and rural communities.
Assemblyman Hafen’s endorsement underscores the growing momentum behind Whipple’s campaign and his commitment to putting Nevada first.
As a fourth-generation Nevadan, businessman, and rancher, Whipple has built his campaign around a Nevada First agenda—focused on lowering the cost of living, protecting Nevada’s natural resources, and standing up for the state’s workers, families, and small businesses.
“Cody Whipple is a Nevada First Republican who will protect and defend Nevada values and is the clear choice to defeat Democrat Steven Horsford and flip this congressional seat red!” said Assemblyman Gregory Hafen II.
Whipple thanked Assemblyman Hafen for his support and emphasized the importance of electing a representative who understands the unique challenges facing Nevada communities.
“I’m honored to have Assemblyman Hafen’s endorsement,” said Whipple.
“He’s been a strong voice for Nevada families, and I’m ready to take that same Nevada First fight to Washington—lowering costs, securing our water and energy future, and ensuring our state has a seat at the table.” Whipple’s Nevada First priorities include tackling inflation and rising costs, supporting law enforcement and first responders, expanding economic opportunity, and fighting to protect Nevada’s way of life.
With support continuing to grow across the district, Whipple’s campaign is building a coalition of voters ready for new leadership that will deliver real results for Nevada.
