Las Vegas, NV – Senate Republican Leader, Senator Robin Titus (R-17) has officially endorsed Cody K Whipple for Congress in Nevada’s 4th Congressional District, adding her support to Whipple’s growing campaign to bring a strong Nevada First voice to Washington, D.C.
Senator Titus, a longtime advocate for rural Nevada, cited Whipple’s deep Nevada roots, experience as a fourth-generation Nevadan, rancher, and small-business owner, and commitment to putting the needs of Nevada families and communities ahead of Washington special interests.
“It’s time to take back CD4 and send Cody Whipple to Congress,” said Senator Robin Titus.
“Cody Whipple is a fourth-generation Nevadan, rancher, and small-business owner who understands the challenges facing our rural communities and working families.
He will fight for lower costs, less red tape, stronger border security, and policies that put Nevadans first—not Washington special interests.
“I’m proud to endorse Cody Whipple for Congress.
Nevada needs leaders who actually live the values of this state.
Cody is one of them.”
Whipple welcomed Senator Titus’s endorsement, saying her support underscores the importance of electing leaders who understand Nevada and are willing to stand up for the state’s families, businesses, ranchers, and rural communities.
“Senator Titus has been a strong voice for Nevada and has never lost sight of the people and communities she represents,” said Cody K Whipple.
“I’m honored to have her support and share her belief that Nevada deserves a representative who will put Nevada First.
I’m running to bring our values, our priorities, and our voice to Washington and deliver results for the people of NV-04.”
As a fourth-generation Nevadan, rancher, and small-business owner, Whipple has built his campaign around a Nevada First agenda focused on lowering costs, cutting unnecessary regulations, securing the border, supporting small businesses and ranchers, and protecting Nevada’s way of life.
Senator Titus’s endorsement adds to Cody Whipple’s broad coalition of Nevada leaders and organizations supporting his bid for Congress.
