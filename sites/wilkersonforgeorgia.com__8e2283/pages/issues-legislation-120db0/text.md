As a parent of two graduates from Cobb County public schools, David understands firsthand the importance of providing every student with a high-quality education that is properly funded.
His advocacy for K-12 education has been a priority throughout his career.
David has consistently pushed for increased funding to ensure that schools have the resources they need to support students and teachers.
In addition to his focus on funding, David has also been a strong advocate for school safety.
He co-sponsored and helped pass key legislation aimed at improving safety protocols in schools, ensuring that students and educators are protected in a safe and secure learning environment.
David stands firm in his opposition to the voucher program passed by Republicans, which he believes undermines the funding and support for public schools.
He is committed to making sure that every child has access to the best possible education, without diverting resources away from the public education system.
David's Commitment to Affordable Education
Due to rising costs, the dream of a college education or attending a technical school has become out of reach for too many families.
David understands that in today’s world, a high school diploma alone is no longer enough for students to succeed.
That's why he is committed to ensuring that all students have the opportunities and resources they need to pursue higher education or technical training.
To make that dream a reality, David has introduced legislation aimed at reducing the barriers that prevent students from accessing critical scholarships.
One of his key initiatives is working to remove the reliance on outdated standardized tests to qualify for the Zell Miller Scholarship.
David believes that a student’s high school achievements, including hard work, dedication, and overall performance, should be valued more than a single day’s test score.
This change would ensure that more students, particularly those who excel in the classroom and contribute to their communities, can take advantage of opportunities that help them succeed after high school.
David is dedicated to leveling the playing field, making sure that financial barriers and the limitations of traditional testing do not stand in the way of a student's future.
David's Leadership and Commitment to Progressive values
David is proud to be a Democrat, and as a leader in the Democratic Caucus, he continues to advocate for our progressive values.
He has been a staunch defender of policies that prioritize the well-being of all Georgians.
David has fought hard against the presence of guns in our schools and on college campuses, ensuring that students and educators are safe from unnecessary harm.
David is also a strong pro-choice advocate and believes in equal rights for all citizens, standing up for fairness, justice, and the dignity of every individual.
Under Republican leadership, however, families in Georgia are facing an ongoing poverty crisis.
Georgia is one of only two states where child poverty rates have increased, and it’s one of a small number of states where poverty rates for seniors are on the rise as well.
Despite these alarming trends, Republicans have failed to make the necessary investments in our future.
David believes we can do better.
He advocates for proven policies, such as childcare tax credits and earned income credits for working families, that can help lift people out of poverty and provide real support to those in need.
