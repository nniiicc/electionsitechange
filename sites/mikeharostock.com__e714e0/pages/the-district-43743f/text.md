Mike Harostock is a candidate for State Representative in Pennsylvania’s 121st Legislative District.
He’s running because he believes our district deserves common-sense leadership which listens, solves problems, and delivers results – not politics as usual.
The 121st Legislative District includes the city of Wilkes-Barre, the townships of Bear Creek, Buck, Plains,and Wilkes-Barre, and the boroughs of Bear Creek Village and Laurel Run.
