This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through brianhawkinsforflorida.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Information We Collect We collect information you voluntarily provide when you fill out a form on our website, such as your name, email address, mobile phone number, ZIP code, and any message you send us.
Providing your phone number and opting in to text messages are entirely optional.
How We Use Your Information We use the information you provide to communicate with you about the campaign, respond to your inquiries, coordinate volunteer activities, and, where you have consented, send you updates by phone call or text message that may include requests for donations.
Text Messaging (SMS) If you provide your mobile number and opt in, you consent to receive calls and text messages from the Campaign.
Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency may vary.
Messaging may include requests for donation.
You may opt out at any time by replying "STOP," and you can reply "HELP" for assistance.
Text messaging opt-in data, consent and related collected personal information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, unless required by law.
Sharing of Information Except as described above regarding text messaging opt-in data, we do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with campaign staff, vendors, and service providers who help us operate the campaign, and as otherwise required by law.
Your Choices You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying "STOP." To be removed from our email or contact lists, or to request that we update or delete your information, contact us using the information below.
Contact Us If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@brianhawkinsforflorida.com .
