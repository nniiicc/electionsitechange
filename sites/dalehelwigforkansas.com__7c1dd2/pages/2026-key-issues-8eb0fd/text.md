As your Kansas State Representative, I am dedicated to addressing the key issues that impact our community and our great state.
My platform is built on conservative principles, a commitment to defending our constitutional rights, and a focus on fiscal responsibility.
I believe in transparent governance and ensuring that the voices of the people are heard in Topeka.
Please explore my stances on the crucial issues below.
If you have any questions or would like to discuss my positions further, please do not hesitate to reach out.
I firmly believe that every life is a precious gift and must be protected from conception to natural death.
As your State Representative, I will consistently stand up for the unborn and support policies that promote a culture of life in Kansas.
We must ensure that our laws reflect the fundamental right to life for all individuals, especially those who cannot speak for themselves.
The Second Amendment of the United States Constitution is a fundamental right, not a privilege.
I stand firmly in defense of the right of law-abiding citizens to keep and bear arms.
We must protect our constitutional liberties against government overreach and ensure that Kansans can always defend themselves, their families, and their property without infringement.
When evaluating Medicaid Expansion in Kansas, we must ask the tough questions about long-term sustainability and the impact on our state budget.
While ensuring access to healthcare is important, we cannot responsibly commit to an expansion that risks defunding other critical services or raising taxes on hardworking Kansans in the future.
We must prioritize protecting the vulnerable who already rely on the system before extending benefits to able-bodied adults without dependents.
A thorough, analytical approach is required to protect our financial future while delivering care where it is most needed.
Ensuring our children receive a high-quality education is paramount to the future of Kansas.
I believe in fully funding our public schools while simultaneously requiring strict accountability and transparency in how those funds are allocated.
We must prioritize directing dollars into the classroom to support our dedicated teachers and students, rather than expanding administrative bureaucracy.
By taking a pragmatic, forward-looking approach to education finance, we can provide every student with the opportunity to succeed while respecting the hardworking taxpayers of our state.
Property taxes are placing an undue burden on hardworking Kansas families, small business owners, and retirees on fixed incomes.
We must work to rein in out-of-control local spending and ensure that property valuations reflect reality, not just an opportunity for government to collect more revenue.
I support measures to increase transparency in the taxation process and will fight to implement meaningful property tax relief so Kansans can afford to keep the homes they've worked so hard to build and maintain our communities' prosperity.
We must ensure that our welfare system serves as a temporary safety net for those who truly need it, rather than a permanent way of life.
By implementing common-sense work requirements for able-bodied adults, we can promote independence, encourage personal responsibility, and provide pathways to meaningful employment.
True compassion means helping individuals break the cycle of poverty and achieve their full potential through hard work and opportunity.
