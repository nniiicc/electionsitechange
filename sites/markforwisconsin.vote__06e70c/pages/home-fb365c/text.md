MY VISION FOR WISCONSIN WISCONSIN’S 1ST SENATE DISTRICT Our District is distinctly Wisconsin.
Located on Wisconsin’s “thumb,” the 1st Senate District (SD-01) stretches from the tip of Washington Island all the way to the southeastern shores of Lake Winnebago.
SD-01 is home to nearly 180,000 Wisconsinites (2020), hundreds of miles of shoreline, and Wisconsin’s famous Door Peninsula.
The district comprises all of Door and Kewaunee counties, nearly all of Calumet County, as well as parts of Brown, Manitowoc, and Outagamie counties.
