Skip to content Menu Home Meet Chris Priorities Testimonials Let’s Connect Donate Chris Erwin For Georgia Republican Candidate for District 32 for the Georgia House of Representative Priorities Education – Workforce Development We must provide a quality education for all of our citizens.
It is critical that preschool programs are offered to our youngest citizens and that technical colleges are supported to develop a strong workforce.
I will work tirelessly to see education and education workforce come first.
Economic Development Georgia leads the country in job development.
Supporting new industries and small businesses creates jobs and is what bolsters economic growth.
Our Republican leadership has led the way in this economic boom in Georgia.
I will use conservative values to ensure economic prosperity for Georgia.
Election Integrity All elections should be secure, fair, and accessible.
I voted to support SB202 which restores voter confidence.
This bill was a collaborative effort between the House and the Senate, incorporating ideas from both chambers and parties in its final version.
We have uniformly expanded early voting hours, meaning all Georgians have equal access to early voting, no matter where they live.
We have done away with the subjective signature “verification” process for absentee voting, instead requiring voter ID for all voters – regardless of whether you choose to vote absentee, early, or on Election Day.
