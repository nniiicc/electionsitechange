ABOUT BLAINE Committed to Community.
Focused on Results.
Born and raised on a family farm west of Sherwood, Blaine learned early the value of hard work, responsibility, and strong values.
Those ideals have guided him throughout his life and his 40-year career in the banking industry.
Blaine currently serves as Vice Chair of the Board of Directors and Senior Executive Vice President at First International Bank & Trust, overseeing five markets and 28 branch locations.
His career has been defined by strategic decision-making, fiscal responsibility, and leadership rooted in accountability.
This experience gives Blaine a clear understanding of the challenges facing small businesses, workforce recruitment, housing, and infrastructure—insight he will bring to the Legislature.
