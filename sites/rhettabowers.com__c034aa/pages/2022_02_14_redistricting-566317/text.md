In the new HD 113 map, Rep.
Bowers represents parts of Garland, Mesquite, and Rowlett.
HD 113 now has less of Rowlett and completely lost Sunnyvale, Dallas, Seagoville, Combine, and most of Balch Springs.
The district gained more parts of Garland and Mesquite.
“If I am losing you please know you can always contact my office for help, and my office will help you get connected with your new representative.
If I have kept or gained you as a constituent, then I am looking forward to having you in the district, working alongside you, and having the chance to serve you in the Texas House.” – Rep.
Rhetta Andrews Bowers
