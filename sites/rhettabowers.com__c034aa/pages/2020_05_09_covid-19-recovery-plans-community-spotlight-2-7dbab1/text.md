Let’s celebrate our Teachers!
Hopefully you let our HD-113 teachers know how much they are appreciated and commend them for keeping our students engaged and learning through this pandemic.
Teachers are important members of our community and their efforts help shape the future through our children.
Many teachers have had to learn how to quickly adjust to a virtual classroom model, modifying their lesson plans and teaching methods to this “new normal”.
I recently spoke with Dr.
Ricardo Lopéz, Garland ISD Superintendent, commending him on the school district’s great work preparing teachers and students for virtual learning.
The district was recently featured in a news broadcast as being a model for virtual learning that other school districts around the country are looking at and trying to imitate.
Speaking of teachers making the adjustment, I also want to recognize a teacher that has become somewhat of a celebrity.
Her name is Kayla Speed, a GISD teacher at Pearson E.S. and somewhat of a online celebrity.
She was featured on the news this week for her innovative Quarantined with Mrs.
Speed YouTube channel.
With the help of her husband, who is a bit of a video magician, her videos get hundreds of views and her following is growing.
Thank you again Kayla and all teachers across HD-113 for what you do!
Thanking cafeteria workers providing needed meals to students!
This week, my staff delivered our official hand sanitizers to some of our best and brightest on the front line – our district cafeteria workers!
Thursday, we delivered more hand sanitizers and gave them a hand with food distribution and curbside pickup.
There is great reward and fulfillment in service and it’s amazing to see teamwork and systems during crisis!
All across the district, our cafeteria workers are doing a wonderful job and providing meals to families in need.
It has been inspiring to see the community come together this way to help others and I have met some wonderful people.
THANK YOU TO ALL THE CAFETERIA WORKERS!
