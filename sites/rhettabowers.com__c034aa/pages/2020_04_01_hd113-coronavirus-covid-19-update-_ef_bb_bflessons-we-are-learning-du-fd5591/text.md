We have witnessed contrasting and sometimes conflicting leadership styles since the coronavirus was first detected in the U.S.
We can debate who has done a better job, but truthful, consistent, people-centered leadership is what we need.
Next, access to affordable healthcare can no longer be considered optional for citizens of one of the wealthiest countries in the world.
