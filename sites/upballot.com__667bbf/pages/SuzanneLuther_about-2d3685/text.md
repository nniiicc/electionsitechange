Suzanne is a former elected member of the Jefferson City School Board, where she served as chairperson of the policy committee and delegate to the Missouri School Board Association.
She was an educator in Jefferson City for 16 years before retiring to pursue entrepreneurial interests.
A Jefferson City native, she raised her family here.
She earned bachelor’s and master’s degrees from the University of Missouri.
Suzanne serves on the Cole County MU Extension Council and Parents as Teachers Advisory Board..
She is a former board member for the Historic City of Jefferson A graduate of the Jefferson City Chamber of Commerce Leadership Class, she also is a member of Rotary International and the Downtown Jefferson City Association.
