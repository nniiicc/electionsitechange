0 Skip to Content Open Menu Close Menu Open Menu Close Menu Meet Heather Fronk Heather Fronk is a community advocate, public servant, and the Republican candidate for Ohio House District 58.
She is the founder and CEO of Clothed In Strength, a nonprofit organization dedicated to helping working single mothers move from government assistance to financial independence.
With more than 15 years of experience in corporate banking and service on numerous nonprofit boards, Heather has worked alongside organizations such as Rotary International, Junior Achievement, the Salvation Army, United Way, and Second Harvest Food Bank to strengthen families across Northeast Ohio.
A former Mrs.
Ohio America, Heather is passionate about empowering women, supporting working families, and expanding economic opportunity.
She lives in Poland, Ohio, with her husband Steve and their two daughters.
