I write to let you know that you are not alone during these uncertain times.
As the COVID-19 response is ever-evolving, this draft already seems old as I move to hit send.
I am heartened to report that the Massachusetts Legislature, a body designed to act deliberatively, has acted expeditiously and collaboratively to respond to this crisis with the Governor as an ally and partner.
Some legislative highlights include: an initial $15 million dollar economic stimulus, waivers for unemployment insurance eligibility as well as an unemployment extension, housing safeguards and small business assistance.
I will follow-up with more detailed emails outlining legislative measures as they arise.
