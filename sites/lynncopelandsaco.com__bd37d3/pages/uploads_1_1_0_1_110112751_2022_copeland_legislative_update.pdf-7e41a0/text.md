EXPANDING ACCESS TO HEALTH CARE The people of Maine deserve accessible and affordable health care.
Among the many bills we passed this year to make health care more accessible, I am particularly proud of our legislation to expand the Children’s Health Insurance Program to provide coverage to an additional 40,000 Maine kids.
I was also pleased that the Legislature took action to bolster Maine’s behavioral health care programs to help those with mental health concerns, people with substance use disorder and others who have been negatively impacted by the pandemic.
I am glad that behavioral health care is being recognized as essential health care and getting the attention it deserves.
We also funded measures to provide financial relief to retired Mainers by exempting retirement pensions from income tax and improving retired public employees’ cost-of-living adjustment.
I was proud to support this legislation to help our older residents live more comfortably.

Thanks to the legislation we passed, all students from the high school graduating classes of 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2023 who enroll full-time in Maine community colleges will receive two years of free education.

CHAMPIONING OUR ENVIRONMENT This year, the Legislature passed major initiatives to equip Mainers with the tools they need to help protect our environment and natural resources in the years to come.
One of our greatest environmental achievements this year was passing legislation to reduce and mitigate contamination from PFAS, chemicals that do not naturally degrade and negatively affect the health of humans and wildlife.
We dedicated significant funding for testing, mitigation and assistance to farmers who have discovered that their land, crops and livestock are contaminated.
Maine is one of the first states in the nation to tackle this environmental issue.
