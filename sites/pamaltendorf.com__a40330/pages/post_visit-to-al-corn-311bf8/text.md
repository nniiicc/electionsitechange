Visit to Al-Corn’s Ethanol Plant on May 6th, 2022
Updated: Jun 28, 2022
I enjoyed joining Rep.
Steve Drazkowski for a tour of Al-Corn Clean Fuel’s ethanol plant in Claremont, MN.
Al-Corn produces clean, renewable ethanol and is an economic engine for the Claremont community while creating strong markets for Minnesota corn growers.
It is critical that our state policy makers focus on reducing red tape and unnecessary regulations to ensure businesses like Al-Corn can continue to succeed.
