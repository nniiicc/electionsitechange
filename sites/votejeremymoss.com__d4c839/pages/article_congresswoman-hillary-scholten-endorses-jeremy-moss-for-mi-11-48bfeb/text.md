Congresswoman Hillary Scholten Endorses Jeremy Moss for MI-11
Oakland County, MI – Today, Congresswoman Hillary Scholten (D-MI-03) announced her endorsement of Senator Jeremy Moss for Michigan’s 11th Congressional District.
“Jeremy Moss is a tested and trusted leader who knows what it means to stand up for the people he represents,” said Congresswoman Scholten.
“I’m proud to endorse him for Michigan’s 11th District and I look forward to working with him in Washington to lower costs for working families, crack down on corruption in politics, and protect our democracy.
The stakes in November could not be higher, and Jeremy Moss has what it takes to deliver for Michiganders.”
“Congresswoman Scholten has won tough elections, built strong coalitions, and delivered for Michigan families.
I’m proud to earn her endorsement and work together to lower costs and restore our rights.
I’m eager to join her in this fight in Congress,” said Senator Moss.
The momentum behind Jeremy Moss’ campaign continues to grow as absentee ballots are now in voters’ hands and Michiganders are already casting their ballots.
Building on a broad and expanding coalition of labor unions, elected officials, and community advocacy groups, the campaign will announce a series of major endorsements throughout the week that underscore the growing support for Jeremy’s campaign to lower costs, protect our democracy, and deliver results for Michigan families.
As early voting gets underway, the race continues to demonstrate that Jeremy has the momentum and the coalition to win on August 4th.
