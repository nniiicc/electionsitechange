Gretchen Whitmer Endorses Jeremy Moss for Congress November 24, 2025 Governor says Moss will continue to deliver results for Oakland County families in 11th Congressional District Oakland County, MI – Today, Gov.
Gretchen Whitmer announced her endorsement of Michigan Senate President Pro Tempore Jeremy Moss (D – Bloomfield) in his race for the open 11th Congressional District.
Over the last seven years, Gov.
Whitmer and Sen.
Moss have partnered to pass some of the most consequential legislation at the State Capitol, from delivering a billion dollars in tax relief for working families and seniors to protecting voting rights and expanding civil rights .
The Governor expressed her confidence that Sen.
Moss will continue to deliver real results for Oakland County in Congress.
“Jeremy Moss is a tough, tested leader who fights like hell every day to make Michiganders’ lives better,” said Gov.
Whitmer.
“As a member of Congress, I know he will stand up for working families, lower costs for Michiganders, and fight for our freedoms.
He has always had Michigan’s back, and I’m proud to support him.” With the Congressional Republicans’ attacks on our economy, fundamental freedoms, and democracy, Michigan leaders are rallying behind Sen.
Moss’ tested and trusted leadership for Oakland County.
Sen.
Moss is one of the most effective legislators in Lansing, with Gov.
Whitmer signing 23 of his bills into law tackling some of Michigan’s most pressing issues, including making housing more affordable.
“I’m proud to earn Gov.
Whitmer’s early support in this race to represent Oakland County in Congress.
During our many years of partnership, we’ve worked to deliver tax relief, protect our rights, and stand up for Michigan families.
I’ll do the same in Congress.” Sen.
Moss said .
“With all of the chaos coming out of Washington – from reckless tariffs driving up everyday costs to the President’s lawlessness threatening the foundation of our democracy– I’m ready to harness my experience and energy to fight for the 11th District.” This endorsement from the Governor leads Sen.
Moss’ strong coalition of support to succeed Rep.
Haley Stevens in the open 11th Congressional District.
Sen.
Moss previously announced endorsements from Secretary of State Jocelyn Benson, Attorney General Dana Nessel, US Rep.
Kristen McDonald-Rivet (D-MI), and many more.
To learn more about Jeremy Moss’s campaign for Congress, visit votejeremymoss.com .
