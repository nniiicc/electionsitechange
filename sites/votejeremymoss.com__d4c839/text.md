Meet Jeremy
Jeremy Moss is running for Congress in this critical moment because we need bold new leadership to take action and fight back.
An Oakland County native and a barrier-breaking lawmaker, Jeremy has a record of delivering results and reforming government institutions to better serve Michiganders.
As our State Senator and President Pro Tem of the Michigan Senate, he successfully implemented effective gun safety laws, protected and expanded reproductive freedom, and provided tax relief for seniors and working families.
In Congress, he’ll push back against Donald Trump’s reckless policies that are tanking our economy, fight looming threats to Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid, and be unafraid to hold this administration accountable for defying the rule of law.

Why He's Running
Lowering Costs for Oakland County
Rising everyday costs are burdening Oakland County families.
Jeremy is leading the efforts in the State Legislature to crack down on corporate greed and price gouging.

Donald Trump and this administration are causing uncertainty and chaos – erratic tariffs are increasing costs, national security leaks are putting us at risk, and unlawful actions are threatening our democracy.

Jeremy will stand up in bold ways to protect our basic rights.

And when Roe was overturned, he acted swiftly to safeguard reproductive healthcare.
