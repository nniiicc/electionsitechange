Justice Hennesy Events
The St Cloud Kickoff event was held on November 18 to a large turnout of supporters from across central Minnesota.
In addition to local attorneys and other legal professionals, leaders in business, education, public health, and medicine showed their support for Justice Hennesy as she officially launched her campaign for election.
Dyan Ebert and Eileen Dauer hosted the event at Quinlivan & Hughes to a standing room only crowd as Justice Hennesy delivered her remarks emphasizing her commitment to promoting legal access to Minnesotans from all walks of life.
Stay tuned for additional events to be held throughout the state, including the Twin Cities, Rochester, Duluth, Moorhead, and more in St Cloud.
St Cloud Kickoff Event a Big Success
Justice Hennesy’s Twin Cities Kickoff Campiagn was met with great enthusiasm.
The event was hosted by Charlie Nauen and Melissa Stull at Lockridge, Grindal, and Nauen on December 8, 2025.
Justice Hennesy underscored her commitment to equal justice for all to an audience of legal and business professionals and answered a number of questions including the importance of a shared commitment to fairness, integrity, and the rule of law.
We are excited about the momentum and thankful for the growing community supporting the campaign.
