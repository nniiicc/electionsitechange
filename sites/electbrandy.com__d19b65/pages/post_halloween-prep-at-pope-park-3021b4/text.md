Here, volunteers Julia Lyons and Kathleen McGillycuddy lead the parade of hundreds of costumed characters over the bridge and around the park.
The annual event was sponsored by the Neponset River Greenway Council, with support from the state Department of Conservation and Recreation (DCR), as well as several other helpers like state Reps.
