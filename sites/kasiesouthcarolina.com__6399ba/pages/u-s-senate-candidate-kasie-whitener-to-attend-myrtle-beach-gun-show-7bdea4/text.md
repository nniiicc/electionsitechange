Senate Candidate Kasie Whitener to Attend Myrtle Beach Gun Show Candidate emphasizes accessibility and dialogue with South Carolina voters February 6, 2026 (Blythewood, South Carolina) – Dr.
Kasie Whitener, Candidate for US Senate, will join the Horry County Libertarian Party at the Myrtle Beach Gun Show on February 7, 2026 from 11:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. and February 8 from 11:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. at the Myrtle Beach Convention Center.
Their table will be the first table attendees greet as they enter the show.
“Events like the Myrtle Beach Gun Show are a valuable opportunity for me to speak directly with voters,” Dr.
Whitener said.
“And being with our friends in Horry County is a great way to help spread awareness of my candidacy.” Myrtle Beach is the fastest-growing metropolitan area in the nation.
With all of the new residents, Myrtle Beach must meet the challenges of employment, infrastructure, and affordability.
Dr.
Whitener and the South Carolina Libertarian Party welcome the opportunities that such rapid growth offers for South Carolina.
“Our state is a wonderful place to build businesses, raise families, and participate in our community,” Dr.
Whitener said.
“I’m always interested to learn what drew people to South Carolina.” The event also offers Dr.
Whitener the opportunity to learn more about what businesses and citizens are thinking about.
Hearing directly from citizens is a top priority for Dr.
Whitener as this 2026 midterm election offers South Carolina the chance to make a big change toward liberty in our state and nation.
“I’m visiting all 46 counties to meet people on specific issues and talk about their values and priorities in 2026,” Dr.
Whitener said.
The Myrtle Beach Gun Show is sponsored by C&E Gun Shows and includes a variety of personal protection items.
Local businesses, retailers, and advocacy groups will be present.
The event expects up to 500 exhibitors and between 2,500 and 5,000 attendees.
