KASIE DAY in CAYCE, S.C.
March 29, 2026 (Blythewood, South Carolina) – It’s the second annual Kasie Day in Cayce, S.C. celebrating Kasie’s birthday and also her fellow Kasie, Kacie, Casey, KC, and Cayces.
Any spelling, any age, all are welcome to celebrate the phonetic “KAY-cee” in Cayce, South Carolina on March 29th every year.
This year’s celebration will take place at Steel Hands Brewing from 1 pm until 4 pm and include games and prizes for persons and pets named Kasie (any spelling).
Hosted in tandem with the Kasie Whitener for US Senate campaign.
Kasie Day is in its second year.
Last year included an official proclamation from the Town of Cayce declaring March 29th to be “Kasie Day.”
“It’s a fun way to invite our radio audience,” said Kasie Whitener, morning show co-host on 100.7 The Point, “And really anyone named Casey to get together and celebrate.” Last year was also held on March 29th, a Saturday, and also included a get-together at Steel Hands Brewing in Cayce.
Including the campaign Kasie Whitener for US Senate this year changes the dynamics of the event a little, but still gives people a chance to celebrate and congregate.
All are welcome and encouraged to patronize Steel Hands Brewing at 2350 Foreman Street in Cayce between 1 and 4 pm to meet Kasie live, get campaign materials like postcards, stickers, signs, and t-shirts, and learn more about the #1MillionKnow campaign.
