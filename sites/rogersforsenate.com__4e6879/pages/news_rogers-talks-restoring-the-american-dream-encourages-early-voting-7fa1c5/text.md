ROGERS TALKS RESTORING THE AMERICAN DREAM, ENCOURAGES EARLY VOTING
DETROIT, MI — Army veteran, former FBI street agent, former House Intelligence Chair, and candidate for U.S.
Senate Mike Rogers today hosted a press conference emphasizing how he will restore the American Dream in Michigan.
He also encouraged absentee and early voting, which starts tomorrow.
Mike was joined by members of the Albanian and Chaldean communities, who came to America to pursue the freedom and opportunity our nation offers.

“This really is about the America we want next.
What country are we going to give our kids?...
Something happened in that primary.
Somebody got elected who really doesn't believe in the fundamental greatness of America.
He has spent a lot of time talking about anti-American ideas.
He wants this next generation of Americans to give up on the idea that someone can come here from a really tough environment and actually make it…He believes that if you want the American dream, you should move to Canada.
The American dream is on the ballot.
We have to decide.
You want to give your family divisiveness and anger and separation.
Or, do you want to give them that promise of America that I grew up with, and that these fine folks who immigrated to this country came here and said, ‘I'm going to make it because I believe in the American dream.’”
– Mike Rogers, Candidate for U.S.
Senate
Mike Rogers was joined at the press conference by the following speakers:
“I am the Eaton County prosecutor and the Republican nominee for attorney general… My father-in-law was originally from Italy, came over here to America.
I love to go to his house to see the American flags and American eagles combined with his Italian heritage.
He was a true believer in the American Dream.”
— Doug Lloyd, Candidate for Attorney General
“We came from Albania, where government control and deprivation robbed people of their freedom and opportunity.
My father went 38 years without seeing his own sister — even though she lived just 25 miles away, across the border.
That experience taught our family never to take our freedoms — or our voice — for granted.
Because when you come from a family that had its rights stripped away, you understand just how precious the right to make your voice heard really is.
So we have to use it.
And we have to vote for Mike Rogers.”
— Mark Gjokaj, Albanian American whose family fled communism to pursue the American Dream
“My parents left Iraq and brought me to this country because they believed in what America promised.
Not living in a country where the radicals decide what cards you hold, if any.
I come from a family that knows what it means to have your rights stripped away, where having the wrong opinion can land you in prison, or worse.
Like many of you, they left a country where Christians were a minority, and where government policies were designed to hold families like ours down — limiting their freedom, their opportunity, and their ability to build a sustainable and equitable future.
They came to America in pursuit of the American Dream…vote for Mike Rogers.”
– Mike Kassab, Chaldean American whose family pursued the American Dream after fleeing religious persecution
“Much of my life’s work has been dedicated to defending a community that knows what happens when people lose their security, their freedom, and their voice.
After ISIS devastated ancient Christian communities across Iraq, we worked to help families rebuild their lives.
Those experiences have taught me never to take the freedoms we have in America for granted — especially our right to make our voices heard.
And that means voting… So don't wait until Election Day if you don't have to…This Senate race is truly a fight for the soul of our nation, and that is why I am here today supporting Mike Rogers.”
– Zina Kiryakos, Baghdad-born Chaldean-American attorney and President of the Iraqi Christian Foundation
