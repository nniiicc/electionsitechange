MICHIGAN CHAMBER OF COMMERCE ENDORSES MIKE ROGERS FOR U.S.
SENATE
DETROIT, MI — The Michigan Chamber of Commerce Wednesday endorsed Army veteran, former FBI street agent, and former House Intelligence Chair Mike Rogers to represent Michigan in the U.S.
Senate.
The endorsement underscores how Mike’s “breadth of experience will be critical as decisions in Washington continue to have significant implications for Michigan – from manufacturing, trade and energy to taxes, regulation, workforce and national security.”
“Michigan is a state with a unique set of interests that demand a strong voice in Washington.
Mike Rogers understands what’s at stake for Michigan employers and brings the experience to navigate complex issues, work across the aisle and deliver results.
We’re proud to once again endorse Mike and believe he will be a strong, effective advocate for Michigan businesses, workers, and communities in the U.S.
Senate.”
— Jim Holcomb, Michigan Chamber President & CEO
“Michigan is home to the American Dream, where if you work hard, you can get ahead.
But for too long, that Dream has been pushed further and further out of reach.
When I get to the Senate, I’ll bring it back.
I’ll fight to restore good-paying manufacturing jobs, to lower taxes, and to cut the Democrats’ excessive regulations.
I'll keep Michigan a place where you want to build a business and start a family.
And I’ll make sure our communities thrive.”
— Mike Rogers, Candidate for U.S.
Senate
