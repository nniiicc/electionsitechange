Senate Mike Rogers today welcomed three endorsements while on the campaign trail in Saginaw County.
In Saginaw, he received the endorsement of Sheriff Bill Federspiel, a Democrat, who said, “It is going to be great to have a U.S.
Senator who not only understands law enforcement but is willing to answer the phone to get the resources here to push back on what is a rising violent crime rate in Saginaw County and Saginaw.” He also received the endorsement of Midland County Sheriff Scott Holzinger.
These endorsements came following a ride-along with Sheriff Federspiel.
He also met with Saginaw residents to discuss public safety.
In Reese, he also received the endorsement of the Michigan Corn Growers Association who said, “His priorities match what Michigan corn farmers need from Washington.” They go on to say, “He listens to growers, and he is focused on the markets and the certainty farmers need.” This endorsement came following a meeting with local farmers at Laracha Farms.
