Senate Mike Rogers today joined Sean Hannity on Fox News’ Hannity to call out Conman Abdul El-Sayed for his repeated lies to Michiganders.
He’ll flip-flop on everything except his support for terrorist organizations.
WATCH THE FULL INTERVIEW HERE .
On Conman Abdul El-Sayed’s Repeated Lies: “This guy is unbelievable.
If [Abdul El-Sayed] has any skill, it’s that he can look you in the eye and lie.
And as a former FBI agent, I can tell you that makes a great conman.
But it doesn't make [him] somebody you ought to trust to have around your family.
He lied about the Muslim Brotherhood, and all of that information is coming out.
He's lied about being a physician.
I mean, the list is going on.
And he lied about his support for law enforcement… Here's the good news: His own words are pretty damning.
He has said, ‘Let's defund the police.’ He said, ‘Let's take guns from police.’ He said, ‘Let's have open borders.’ And by the way, [he said] ‘we owe the world to have open borders and allow illegal immigrants just to flow across and put them on Medicare.’ I mean, these are his own words.
These are not things we're making up and putting on an ad that says this is something that we think he is.
These are his own words.” On Conman Abdul El-Sayed’s Failed Rebranding: “What [Abdul El-Sayed]'s trying to do now is remanufacture who he is in the last 20 days [before the election] to get some votes.
I don't think it's working.
I don't think people are buying it.
I mean, we have coalitions with Jewish Democrats.
We have coalitions with African Americans around the state that should be locked up for Abdul El Sayed.
They're not.
And if you don't have your base 19 days away from an election, I'm telling you, you're going to have a hard time getting elected.
And that's why you're seeing now he's coming out with: ‘Well, no, really, I'm for law enforcement’ [and] ‘No, really, I'm not for the New Deal.’ Which he lied about; he supports it.
We have all of his video saying he supports it.
I've never seen anything quite like this.
It makes him extreme, and it makes him dangerous.
But his ability to look at you and lie, boy, that's a gift I hope nobody gets.” On Abdul El-Sayed’s Failure to Condemn Terrorism: “And this is the crazy thing about this, Sean.
Think of this: of all the things [Abdul El-Sayed]'s trying to flip-flop or change or say he's not that person — even though we have all this video saying he is — one thing he won't change is he won't condemn the Muslim Brotherhood.
He won't condemn Hamas.
He won't condemn the terrorist group, [the] IRGC.
That's the enforcement arm of the Iranian terrorist state.
He won't condemn any of it.
So, that is the oddest thing to me [that] he holds that position.
But he's trying to flip around on everything else that he does, trying to confuse people.
I just don't think people are buying it.” Learn more about Conman Abdul’s lies and extremism at StopAbdul.com .
