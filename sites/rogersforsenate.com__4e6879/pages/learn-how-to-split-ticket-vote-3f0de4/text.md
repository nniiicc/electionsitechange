MAKE SURE YOUR VOTE COUNTS
You can vote a straight Democratic ticket AND for Mike Rogers for U.S.
Senate without spoiling your ballot.
How to vote split ticket:
- Mark the Democratic Party in the straight-ticket box.
- Find the U.S.
Senate race and mark Mike Rogers (Republican).
- Leave the rest of the partisan races unmarked (they will already be counted as Democratic).
Return your absentee ballot by mail no later than October 20th.
Or, return your ballot in person to your city or township clerk's office.
