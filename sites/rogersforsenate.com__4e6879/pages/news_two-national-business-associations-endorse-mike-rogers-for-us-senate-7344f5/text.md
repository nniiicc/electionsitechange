SENATE Sep 21 Written By Hinson Peed DETROIT, MI — The National Federation of Independent Business (NFIB) and National Roofing Contractors Association (NRCA) this past week endorsed Army veteran, former FBI street agent, and former House Intelligence Chair Mike Rogers to represent Michigan in the U.S.
Senate.
The NFIB is a non-profit, member-driven organization that represents small and independent business owners in all 50 states.
They cheered Mike as a “legislator who will fight for policies that allow small businesses to compete in our economy.” The NRCA is one of the construction industry’s most respected trade associations.
It represents all segments of the roofing industry, including contractors, manufacturers, and engineers.
They cited Mike’s “commitment to policies that will strengthen small businesses, expand opportunities in the skilled trades, and create a favorable business environment in Michigan.” “Michigan was built on hard-work.
Our small businesses and working families deserve an ally in the U.S.
Senate to help our state thrive,” said Rogers .
“That includes lowering taxes and cutting the Democrats’ excessive regulations that are crushing our business community.
I’m grateful for the National Federation of Independent Business and National Roofing Contractors Association’s support as we fight to get Michigan working again.” These endorsements come on the heels of the Detroit Regional Chamber’s endorsement of Mike Rogers for U.S.
Senate earlier last week.
Learn more about Mike Rogers and his plan to get to work for Michigan at RogersForSenate.com .
