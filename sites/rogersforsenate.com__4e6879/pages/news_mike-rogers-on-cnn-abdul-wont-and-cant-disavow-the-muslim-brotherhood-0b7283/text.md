In the last eight years in Michigan, that's why people are looking for a change here.
If we don't change this outcome, if we don't keep jobs — 200,000 jobs we've lost in manufacturing since 2000.
Our median wage slipped to $10,000 below the median average.
I tie that directly to our manufacturing loss with that new plant coming, with reshoring happening in our suppliers.
I mean, Detroit Diesel is now adding a third shift.
They haven't done that in two decades.
So, all of that is really incredibly important for our long-term benefit in the economy.
When the war is over — and it will end, and I've called for it to end — guess what?
Prices are coming down, and then we're in really good shape in Michigan to compete for the next generation of advanced manufacturing.” On Abdul’s Family Ties to the Muslim Brotherhood: “Listen, I've never called him a terrorist.

And I think that just adds complexity to the geopolitical stage.
And it looks to me like the President's trying to do something to bring down diesel prices.

I do worry about the optics of it, but I understand what his motives are, and his motives are to try to get prices back down on diesel.
Diesel's hurting us.

The second most diverse crop in the country comes out of Michigan.

It's not just Iran.

If the strategy works, hey, good on him because that means we're going to pay less for diesel here in the United States.” On Obama Coming to Michigan: “The coalitions, I have to tell you, are very, very exciting.
