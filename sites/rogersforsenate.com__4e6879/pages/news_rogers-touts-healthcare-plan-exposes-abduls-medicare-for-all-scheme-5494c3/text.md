Senate Mike Rogers today promoted his healthcare plan, Healthcare that Works for Working Families , during a press conference in Taylor.
He also exposed Conman Abdul for how his “Medicare for All” scheme takes money out of Michiganders’ pockets to line his own family’s.
LISTEN TO THE FULL PRESS CONFERENCE HERE .
“I just can’t think of a more important issue to the people of Michigan than healthcare and their rising costs.
How do we get to the bottom of this?
The way we don’t do it is blow up the system that we have.
We fix the system that we have.
We have to open [the] books to make sure…when patients show up, they understand what their costs are doing… bringing down prescription drug prices.
I think there’s even more that we can do there.
And if you pay for care and pay for insurance, you get coverage…We’re going to bring it back to patient and doctor-centered care… It’s a little suspect to me that this is an interesting trend for Mr.
Abdul.
The largest campaign contributor to his campaign [his father-in-law], also benefits from Medicare processing in a clinic that he has.
So, more Medicare, more money for Abdul’s family for sure.
You start peeling this onion back; it starts to look pretty ugly in a hurry… I’m a cancer survivor… Abdul was, I thought, disparaging to women who suffer [from] breast cancer.
He called them privileged in some way, that we would spend this money on breast cancer research.
That’s a slap in the face to tens of thousands of Michigan women who get diagnosed with breast cancer every year.
I’m starting to believe he lacks the maturity to be a United States Senator.” — Mike Rogers, Candidate for U.S.
Senate Mike Rogers was joined at the press conference by the following speakers: “A serious vaccine reaction left me with a lifelong medical condition, including issues with blood clots.
So, when I talk about access to healthcare, I’m not talking about something hypothetical.
I’m talking about my life…That’s why Abdul El-Sayed’s Medicare for All plan terrifies me.
I cannot afford a healthcare system that causes longer waits for critical care or losing access to the doctors I rely on…I’m supporting Mike Rogers because his healthcare plan focuses on making care more affordable and accessible, while holding insurance companies accountable when they delay or deny the care patients need..” — Chére Bernhard, Michigander living with chronic illness “I practice family medicine, and unlike Abdul El-Sayed, I’m a real physician… You can give every American a Medicare for All card tomorrow.
But if the system can’t meet the demand — if patients struggle to find a doctor, physicians have less time with each patient, or people wait longer for appointments — you haven’t solved the underlying problem.
You’ve simply changed who pays the bill — taxpayers…Abdul has misdiagnosed the problem — and Medicare for All is the wrong prescription.
Michigan needs healthcare that puts patients first, strengthens the doctor-patient relationship, and makes quality care more affordable and accessible.
That’s why I support Mike Rogers.” — David Patterson, Family Doctor “Before I ever served in the Legislature, I was a nurse… So, when politicians start talking about completely restructuring healthcare in America, I don't hear talking points.
I think about patients.
I think about nurses.
I think about the people actually delivering care.
And that's why Abdul El-Sayed's Medicare for All plan concerns me so deeply...
I know what happens when a healthcare system is stretched too thin.
Patients wait.
Providers become overwhelmed.
And the people who need care the most suffer.
And here's something else Michiganders deserve to know.
Abdul wants to put every American into a Medicare for All system.
But his own wife is a practicing psychiatrist whose private practice opted out of Medicare…Why should Michigan patients and doctors be forced into a system that his own household has chosen not to participate in?
We absolutely need to fix healthcare.
But we should fix what's broken without destroying what works.
And that's the difference with Mike Rogers.
Mike has a plan that tackles the problems families are actually facing.” — Jamie Thompson, State Representative Additional Resources: Mike’s Healthcare Plan : Healthcare that Works for Working Families Abdul’s own words on how Medicare for All will break our healthcare system further here.
