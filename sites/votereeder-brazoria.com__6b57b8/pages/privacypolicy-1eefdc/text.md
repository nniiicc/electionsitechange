This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, maintain, and protect information provided through our website, forms, email communications, text messaging program, events, donations, volunteer activities, and other campaign activities.
By using this website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.
We may update this policy periodically, and any changes will be posted on this page.
Information We Collect We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, employer, occupation, donation information, volunteer information, survey responses, and other information you choose to provide.
We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you use our website, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referring website, pages visited, and other website activity.
How We Use Your Information We may use your information to communicate with you about the Campaign, including campaign updates, fundraising, GOTV and voting reminders, advocacy, events, volunteer opportunities, polling, surveys, and other campaign-related activities.
We may also use information to operate and improve our website and campaign activities, process contributions and requests, maintain campaign records, and comply with applicable campaign-finance, reporting, and other legal requirements.
Email Communications If you choose to receive campaign emails, we may send you campaign updates, fundraising appeals, voting and GOTV reminders, advocacy updates, event invitations, volunteer opportunities, and other campaign communications.
You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link provided in our emails.
Text Messaging If you voluntarily opt in to receive text messages, Karen Reeder for Texas House District 29 may send recurring automated campaign updates, fundraising messages, GOTV reminders, advocacy messages, event invitations, polling messages, and other campaign-related communications to the number you provided.
Donations may be solicited.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency varies.
Reply STOP to opt out.
Reply HELP for assistance.
Consent to receive text messages is not required to make a donation, use our website, or otherwise support the Campaign.
Mobile Information and SMS Consent No mobile information will be shared by us with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Cookies and Website Analytics We may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors interact with our website and improve its functionality and performance.
Your browser may allow you to control or disable cookies.
Disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain portions of the website.
Service Providers We may use service providers to assist with website operations, communications, fundraising, campaign technology, analytics, and other campaign activities.
Information may be provided to service providers when necessary for them to perform services on behalf of the Campaign, subject to applicable privacy and legal requirements.
The mobile-information and SMS-consent restrictions described above continue to apply and are not overridden by this section.
Third-Party Websites and Services Our website may contain links to third-party services, including donation processors and social media platforms.
Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.
We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.
Donations Campaign contributions are subject to applicable campaign-finance laws and reporting requirements.
Information about contributors may be collected, maintained, and publicly disclosed when required by law.
Security We take reasonable measures to protect information in our possession and limit access to individuals and service providers who need it for legitimate campaign purposes.
However, no website, electronic transmission, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Children’s Privacy This website and the Campaign's services are not directed toward children.
We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children through our website.
Your Choices You may unsubscribe from campaign emails using the unsubscribe link included in an email.
You may opt out of campaign text messages at any time by replying STOP.
You may contact us regarding your communication preferences or information at info@votereeder-brazoria.com .
Certain information may need to be retained when required by campaign-finance, recordkeeping, or other applicable laws.
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, services, or applicable requirements.
Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
