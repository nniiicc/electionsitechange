When care is too expensive or out of reach, families delay treatment and small problems become more serious and costly.
Expanding access, protecting essential services, and ensuring reliable transportation options will help families get the care they need when they need it.
I support a practical, patient-centered approach that lowers costs and improves outcomes: Expand access to affordable healthcare coverage Protect essential services, including mental health care Support preventive care and early intervention Keep healthcare decisions between patients and doctors Provide non-emergency medical transportation options Strengthen Local Economy Families in Brazoria County are working hard, but rising costs—from energy bills to housing, and everyday expenses—are making it harder to get ahead.
Building a strong local economy means lowering those burdens while creating good-paying jobs and expanding opportunity.
I believe economic growth and affordability must go hand in hand, with practical policies that support working families, strengthen local businesses, and create long-term stability.
I will support legislation to: Cap excessive energy rate increases Deliver meaningful property tax relief Expand targeted tax relief for working families Address rising utility and insurance costs Support job growth and workforce opportunities Address rising numbers of homeless Improve Infrastructure Reliable infrastructure is the backbone of a safe and growing community.
From roads and drainage to power and communications, these are the systems families depend on every day and during emergencies.
Strategic investment and long-term planning are essential to keeping pace with growth and protecting homes and businesses.
I bring an engineer’s, solutions-driven approach focused on results that work.
I will: Strengthen power grid reliability Repair and maintain roads and transportation systems Protect communities from harmful side-effects of Data Centers Improve Flood Protection and Increase Community Safety Flooding is one of the greatest challenges facing House District 29.
Families deserve confidence that their homes, businesses, and neighborhoods are better prepared before the next storm arrives.
That means investing in smarter flood mitigation, improving drainage systems, strengthening emergency communication, and using technology to provide timely alerts that help keep people safe.
I will work to bring together state and local partners to build resilient communities that are ready for whatever Texas weather brings.
I will: Improve drainage and reduce flood risks Invest in flood mitigation and storm preparedness Upgrade emergency communication systems Strengthen Public Schools Public schools are central to both community strength and long-term economic success.
When schools are underfunded, students, teachers, and local economies all feel the impact.
Keeping taxpayer dollars in classrooms ensures students have the tools to succeed and businesses have a well-prepared workforce.
I believe in making sustained investments in public education so every child has a fair opportunity to thrive: Fully fund public schools and update funding formulas for growing districts Increase teacher pay and improve retention Modernize classrooms with updated materials and technology Expand mental health and safety resources Oppose voucher programs that divert public funds Lower Property Taxes For many families, property taxes are one of the largest and most unpredictable expenses they face.

A fair and transparent system should provide stability and real relief—not shift costs or create confusion.
