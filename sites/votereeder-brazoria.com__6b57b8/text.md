ABOUT ME
I grew up learning that leadership starts at home.
Born and raised in Augusta, Georgia, my life was shaped by faith, family, and my lovely community.
Those lessons followed me everywhere: be honest, work hard, show compassion, and when something isn’t right, don’t stay quiet; stand up.
Today—as a mother, mechanical engineer, and community-builder—I am proud to call Brazoria County home.
Why am I running?
• Relief for working families
• Healthcare people can afford
• Schools our kids deserve
• Communities that bounce back
• Property taxes under control
I believe that something precious is slipping away: fairness, common sense, and the voice of everyday Texans like you & me.
I want a Texas where there's:
“I’m here for people.”
I see a Texas worth fighting for: one where compassion leads, people matter, and every voice is heard.
WHAT BRAZORIA DESERVES
Brazoria County deserves leadership that delivers the basics right: safe streets, reliable roads, strong schools, and affordable healthcare.
Seniors deserve dignity.
Children deserve opportunity.
Families deserve a government that listens, follows through, and works for them.
MY PROMISE TO YOU
Every family should feel safety, fairness, and opportunity.
As a mother, I will fight for you just as much as I fight for my own children — I want a path to a better future for all, regardless of race, identity, or goal.

PRIORITIES
Safer Communities
Modern emergency alert systems, better coordination between agencies, and faster information during storms and industrial incidents.
Safer Communities
Modern emergency alert systems, better coordination between agencies, and faster information during storms and industrial incidents.
Stronger Public Schools
Keep taxpayer dollars in classrooms, support teachers, update materials and technology, and ensure every child has a fair chance to thrive.
Affordable Healthcare
Expand access to care, protect essential health services, and create non-emergency medical transportation so seniors and families can reach their doctors safely.
Reliable Roads and Flood Protection
Repair roads, reduce traffic congestion, improve drainage, and protect homes from flooding.
Lower Property Taxes
Push for a fair, transparent tax system that helps homeowners, seniors, and veterans stay in their homes.
A Leader with Heart

and bring common sense back to public service.
