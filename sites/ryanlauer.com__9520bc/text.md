Donate now About Ryan Representative Ryan Lauer is a fifth generation Hoosier and graduate of Indiana University with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biochemistry.
Elected to the Indiana State Legislature in 2018, he has demonstrated leadership and experience with a strong conservative record serving the people.
He was elected to the Bartholomew County Council in 2010 and served as President of the Council.
As a Legislator, he has authored and passed bills that have helped Hoosiers and protected the vulnerable.
Ryan lives in Columbus with his wife, Blair, three daughters, Awyn, Lillian and Sidney and son, Isaac.
He is a member of Grace Lutheran Church in Columbus and former Chairman of the Grace Lutheran Church congregation.
