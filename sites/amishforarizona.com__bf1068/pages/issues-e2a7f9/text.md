CIVIL SOCIETY
STOPPING ANIMAL CRUELTY
Our nation should never tolerate cruelty to animals, and all animals deserve humane treatment, including those raised for food.
The mistreatment of animals runs counter to our long-standing legal traditions and religious values, and addressing it helps prevent the spillover of viruses, violence, and other threats to our communities.
Businesses must root out animal cruelty in their supply chains, research programs, and operations to stay aligned with their customers and the norms of a civil society, because helping animals helps us all by building a society where people, animals, and nature live in balance.
As a State Representative, Dr.
Shah led efforts to crack down on puppy mills, end the unwarranted mutilation of cats through declawing, and halt the sale of kangaroo parts in the United States to stop the mass slaughter of kangaroos in their native habitats.
In Congress, he will fight to stop puppy mills, staged animal fighting, and horse slaughter for human consumption.
As a physician, he will champion more effective, human-relevant testing methods to end the crude use of dogs and primates in research, and he will honor states' rights and local authority to combat factory farming so communities can make democratic decisions to protect animals at the local, state, and federal levels.
