Program description Vote Ezequiel Morfin (“we,” “us,” “our”) operates a text-messaging program to communicate with supporters and voters on behalf of Ezequiel Morfin for Washington State House, 14th Legislative District.
Messages may include campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer recruitment, get-out-the-vote reminders, and donation asks.

Opt-out You can opt out at any time by replying STOP (or STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT) to any message.
