Education & Early Years As a lifelong resident of District 27, I'm a proud 2008 graduate of Izard County Consolidated School District.
My education journey continued at Arkansas State University, where I earned both my Bachelor's in Mid-Level Education and Master's in Educational Leadership.
These foundations, combined with my experiences in both athletics and academics – from state championship basketball to competitive chess – shaped my understanding of leadership and strategic thinking.
Professional Experience My career in education has evolved from teaching 7th and 8th grade science to serving as a technology coordinator.
Now, as a Digital Learning Specialist with the Arkansas Department of Education, I work from both Melbourne and Harrison cooperatives, serving every school district in our area.
This position gives me unique insights into the challenges and opportunities facing our rural schools.
Community Roots At 31 years old, I represent a new generation of leadership while maintaining deep connections to our district's values.
My work through both the Northcentral Arkansas Educational Service Cooperative and Ozarks Unlimited Resources Educational Service Cooperative allows me to maintain strong ties throughout District 27, serving Izard, Searcy, Newton, and parts of Stone County.
Technology Leadership My background in educational technology has positioned me to understand both traditional needs and modern challenges facing our district.
