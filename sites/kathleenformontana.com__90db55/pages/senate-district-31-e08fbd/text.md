Senate District 31
Montana Senate District 31 (SD 31) is located entirely within Gallatin County and includes a large portion of the city of Bozeman.
The specific boundaries of SD 31 are defined by the 2023 redistricting plan, which is in effect for elections from 2024 to 2032.
The district is comprised of two contiguous House Districts:
- House District 61 (HD 61)
- House District 62 (HD 62)
