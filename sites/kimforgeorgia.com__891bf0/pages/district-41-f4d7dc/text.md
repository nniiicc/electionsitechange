Kim Jackson serves as Senator for Georgia State Senate District 41, representing a portion of DeKalb County.
The new district includes parts of the cities of Avondale Estates, Brookhaven, Clarkston, Pine Lake, Stone Mountain, Stonecrest, and Tucker.
The State of Georgia passed new maps for the legislature as part of a special session in November 2023 after a federal judge invalidated all Congressional and state maps for Georgia.
To find your new legislators, log in to the state’s “My Voter Page” and scroll down to “My Districts & Elected Officials”.
