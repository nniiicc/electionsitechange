Legislation Passed While in Office
- SB 75: Provided lease termination process for victims of stalking
- SB 324: Created an Address Confidentiality Program for victims/survivors of stalking, domestic/dating violence, human trafficking
- SB 531: DeKalb School Board salary raises
- SB 562: Restructuring aspects of City of Pine Lake
- SB 577: Streamlining waste removal and removal fee collection from former tenants in DeKalb Co.
- SB 83: Creating fairer processes between insurers and trial lawyers who help those filing car accident injury claims
- SB 170: Ensures that rural Georgia hospitals have backup generators during natural disasters and emergencies
- SB 427: Streamlines the process for internationally-trained doctors to receive their Georgia License to help fill doctor shortages in underserved areas
