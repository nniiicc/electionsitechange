As a proud union member and advocate, Simon Glaser is eager to be a voice for the hardworking Minnesota farmers and their biggest champion in the state legislature when it comes to addressing the critical issues they are facing.
He is ready to work across the aisle to guarantee farmers not only have a seat at the table but also help bring down costs, stabilize the farming economy across the state, and address the need for affordable housing and healthcare access in agricultural communities.
“Simon not only demonstrates a strong commitment to Minnesota’s farm families and rural communities, but also a desire to find solutions to the critical issues they are facing.
This is why the Minnesota Farmers Union Political Action Committee (MFU PAC) is pleased to endorse his candidacy for Senate District 24,” said Gary Wertish, Chair of the Minnesota Farmers Union.
“I am proud to have earned the endorsement of the Minnesota Farmer’s Union PAC.
A hard day’s work should earn you a fair day’s pay and a good livelihood, and ou r farmers are some of the hardest workers among us,” said Simon Glaser.
