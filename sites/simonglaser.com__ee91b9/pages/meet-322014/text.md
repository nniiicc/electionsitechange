Simon has always lived a life of public service -- in the schools, as a union officer, and as a volunteer leader in the community.
Now Simon is working hard to bring these skills and experiences to the State Senate, guided by the idea that government works best when all Minnesotans are guaranteed both dignity and opportunity.
With decades of experience as a teacher, organizer, and advocate, he has built a reputation as a leader who listens, collaborates, and focuses on results.
A Commitment to Education and Empowering the Next Generation After graduating from the University of Wisconsin-Madison, Teach for America gave Simon his start in the public schools outside New Orleans.
After his two-year teaching commitment in Louisiana, he earned a master’s degree in education from the University of Pennsylvania and has since spent over 30 years educating students and working for teachers.
As an English teacher in Rochester Public Schools, he has worked tirelessly to ensure that every student—no matter their background—receives the high-quality education they deserve.
His passion for education goes beyond the classroom.
As an active member of Rochester’s teachers’ union, he has served in many roles, including vice president, negotiations team member, and government relations representative —fighting for fair wages, better working conditions, and policies that benefit both teachers and students.
A Record of Organizing and Advocacy Simon believes in the power of grassroots organizing to build a better community.
He has been a strong voice in Education Minnesota’s Legislative Action Committee, where he has worked to shape policies that support students, educators, and schools.
Simon has served as a delegate to the state AFL-CIO, and national elected delegate to the NEA.
He has organized legislative candidate screenings, direct lobbying efforts, and get-out-the-vote initiatives to ensure that public education and uplifting workers remain priorities in Minnesota.
Beyond education, Simon has been deeply involved in community advocacy.
Working alongside local community and faith-based groups, he has mobilized volunteers, led phone banks, and trained community members to advocate for policies that strengthen our local community.
With Simon Glaser, you get a leader who stands up, speaks out, and fights for working people.
Together, we can build a future that works for all of us.
