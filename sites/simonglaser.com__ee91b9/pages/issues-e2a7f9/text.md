I believe that we need to send an advocate for working people, not a career politician, to the state senate to deliver real results.
I am running to lower costs, fight fraud, and take on our broken system.
Lowering Costs Continuing my advocacy for Minnesotans who work for a living As a union leader, I have spent decades fighting for better wages, pensions, and healthcare.
This is the work I will continue at the legislative level in St.
Paul.
I know that life has become unaffordable for hard working Minnesotans, and I will prioritize action to bring down costs on housing, transportation, healthcare and groceries.
Fighting Fraud We deserve a government we can trust Fraud is a serious problem in our state, and our government failed to protect our tax dollars.
I am dedicated to defending the tax dollars of the public, and ensuring that those funds get to the places and people they are supposed to.
That’s why I will proudly work across the aisle with Republicans to ensure that fraudsters are held accountable to the fullest extent of the law.
Fixing Our Broken Healthcare System A doctors’ appointment should never break our banks Healthcare costs have skyrocketed to unacceptable extremes.
Minnesotans need to be able to access healthcare without it emptying their bank accounts or putting them in debt.
I will fight to ease and expand healthcare access, and rein in the out of control costs.
Defending Rural MN From Big Tech Rural communities deserve a voice No one should be powerless when powerful interests bring their agenda to your doorstep.
I support pausing new construction on hyperscale data centers until we’ve established thorough protections, review processes, and regulatory systems to ensure they do not threaten to destabilize rural livelihoods.
