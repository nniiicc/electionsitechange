0 Skip to Content Meet Bryan Donate Open Menu Close Menu Meet Bryan Donate Open Menu Close Menu Meet Bryan Donate Meet Bryan Bryan King was born in Springdale, Arkansas and is a graduate of Green Forest High School in Carroll County.
He was accepted to The University of Arkansas at Fayetteville where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in Animal Science.
Bryan took what he learned in school and applied it to his daily life on the farm; he now owns and operates Triple K Farms in Green Forest, AR.
Besides his love for farming, Bryan also loves his local community and felt a calling to serve publicly.
He was elected to the Arkansas House of Representatives in 2007 and served until 2013 where he represented House District 91.
He was a member of the House Insurance and Commerce Committee, the Arkansas Legislative Council, where he was Chairman of the Personnel Committee, the Joint Budget Committee and the House Public Health, Welfare and Labor Committee.
In 2013, he transitioned from the House over to the State Senate where he represented Senate District 5 and served as the Chairman of Legislative Joint Auditing Committee and a member of the Senate Judiciary, Senate State Agencies Governmental Affairs, Senate Rules, Resolutions & Memorials, Joint Performance Review, Joint Budget Committee (JBC), the Arkansas Legislative Council and JBC Claims.
Bryan was re-elected to the Arkansas State Senate- District 28 in 2022 after defeating Bob Ballinger.
Bryan still feels that calling to serve and is again running for the State Senate.
He humbly asks for your support and vote on March 3, 2026.
Bryan and his wife, Shana, live in Farewell with Elvis and Petey their beloved rescue pets.
