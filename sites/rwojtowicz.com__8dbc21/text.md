FIXING MICHIGAN SCHOOLS ​ Public schools are Failing our children, I 100% support parental choice when it comes to the education of our district’s Students.
Parental involvement is the key to the success of students.
As a member of Chippewa Valley Schools' Board of Education, I've worked tirelessly to deliver a quality education to Michigan Students- one where Parents play an active role.
Government schools, practicing, ignoring the education of our children along with the fast growing Administration and Union control that is attacking parents and the hard-working teachers and staff bulling them to push ideology & ignore what they want to do most which is to educate the children.
The reading retention bill, which allows 3rd graders to be promoted to 4th grade, even if they are several grades behind in reading comprehension did nothing to advance the education of our students.
This is just plain wrong.
MICHIGAN'S BROKEN ROADS Did you ever wonder why it seems the roads are never in good repair?
It’s because they’re not .
Road categories are Good, Fair or Poor.
Currently 25% are rated good, 42% are in fair condition and 33% are rated poor condition.
The Governor is touting her “success” on fixing the roads, but the problem is they are falling apart faster than they are being fixed.
A solution can be attained by eliminating the billions of dollars spent on pet projects and corporate welfare allotments and utilizing those funds for infrastructure projects. ​ I am excited to work with the House Republicans and their Roads plan, to not only fix our roads but to also have oversite on how they are repaired and replaced so they have a long lifespan.
ILLEGAL IMMIGRATION Michiganders need to understand, we are a border state.
There are many Illegal immigrants, criminally coming across our northern border, this along with several counties declaring their sanctuary County status with their sheriffs saying they will not honor the ICE retainer agreement. ​ Not only are criminals coming into our state but our state is also selling or giving foreign entities control of Michigan property in the tune of over six million acres, financially costing Michiganders and causing much pain and anguish Firstly, the Michigan legislature should support our border patrol and ICE officer recommendations.
Secondly, the legislature must cease with all the incentives provided to illegals and foreign entities.
Without the incentives, we're bound to see fewer illegal immigrants.
DEFENDING THE 2ND AMENDMENT The current Democrat majority has led to a series of firearms restrictions that, in my opinion, are an unconstitutional infringement on our God given right to defend ourselves and our families.
As your State Representative, I'll fight tirelessly for the rights of law-abiding Michiganders to keep and bear Arms, as the Founding Fathers intended.
CRIME Protecting the People of Michigan should be at the top of our Government's to-do list, and it's time to start treating those who jeopardize the safety of their fellow Michiganders as what they are: criminals.
While Democrats try to introduce bills that incentivize and reduce penalties for those who perpetrate crime, I want to give our men and women of Law Enforcement the tools and abilities to effectively combat it. ​ It's time to start getting tough on child predators, rioters, looters, and thugs, it's time to crack down on sanctuary cities, and it's time to ensure our police are enabled, equipped, and empowered once again.
