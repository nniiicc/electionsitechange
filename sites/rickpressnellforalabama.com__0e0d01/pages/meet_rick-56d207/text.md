Meet Rick
EMAIL+ Eastprecinct@yahoo.com Phone: 256-431-1018.
I was born and raised in Limestone County and currently live near the Elk River.
I’ve spent ample time in Lauderdale County.
At 17, I attended West Limestone and Clements High School leaving school to join the Marine Corps.
I earned a GED while serving 4 years active duty in the Corps as (Infantry and Rifle and Pistol Marksmanship Instructor).
As a civilian I was hired by the Huntsville Police Department and worked there as police officer, investigator and police sergeant during that time I joined the Alabama Army National Guard.
Shortly after the 9/11 attacks, I took an extended tour of duty at the Pentagon G3 Antiterrorism Branch.
Two years later the National Guard offered me an opportunity to return to Active Duty in AGR Title-10 status.
I eventually retired from the Army in 2017 with 24+ years of active duty (AGR) Title 10; at the rank of Colonel (O6) (Signal and Quartermaster w/Top Secret SCI security clearance).
Including Alabama National Guard reserve time, I served over 38 years in military service.
Reserve service ran concurrent with my full-time law enforcement career (1981 and 2004) when I also retired from HPD.
I am 67 years old.
I’m a combat veteran - deployed to Afghanistan in 2005.
I’ve also served at National Guard Bureau in Arlington, VA, Central Command HQ in Tampa, FL and am a graduate of the US Army War College at Carlisle, PA where I earned a master’s degree in strategic studies.
I earned an associate’s degree from Calhoun Community College, a bachelor’s degree in criminal justice from Athens State University and a master’s degree in justice and public safety from Auburn University-Montgomery.
I was a founding member and elected President of the Madison County Police Benevolent Association and also President of the Madison County Shooting Sports Association.
Since retiring I have been active in the community with environmental protection initiatives in coordination with Tennessee Riverkeepers and obtained master naturalist certification.
The majority of my time has been spent maintaining a few rental homes and rehabbing other vacant dilapidated homes.
I do most of the repairs and maintenance myself as I like working with my hands and believe this helps alleviate the housing affordability issues in N.
Alabama.
This sole proprietor entrepreneurship gives me a great appreciation for the building and home repair trades.
I’ve been married for 35 years, have one daughter and two grandchildren.
