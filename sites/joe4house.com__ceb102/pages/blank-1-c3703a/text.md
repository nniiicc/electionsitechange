About Joe Issues Donate Contribute Contribute Privacy Policy Joe Bustos for State House | Last Updated: June 2, 2026 Joe Bustos for State House ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the information you provide when you interact with our campaign, including through our website at www.joe4house.com and our SMS messaging program.
Information We Collect We may collect the following information: Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, and mailing address when voluntarily provided.
Donation Information: Payment details submitted through our secure donation portal.
Usage Information: General website analytics such as pages visited and browser type.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: • Send campaign updates, event announcements, and volunteer opportunities via SMS and email. • Process donations in compliance with applicable campaign finance laws. • Improve our website and communications. • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.
SMS Messaging When you opt in to our SMS program, your phone number is used solely to send messages related to the Joe Bustos for State House campaign.
We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your phone number or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Data Sharing We do not sell or rent your personal information.
We may share information with trusted vendors who assist us in operating our website or conducting campaign activities, strictly under confidentiality agreements.
We may also disclose information when required by law, such as campaign finance reporting requirements.
Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure.
Your Choices Opt Out of SMS: Reply STOP to any text message at any time.
Contact Us: To request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, contact us through our website at www.joe4house.com.
Children's Privacy Our website and SMS program are not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Changes will be posted on our website at www.joe4house.com with an updated effective date.
