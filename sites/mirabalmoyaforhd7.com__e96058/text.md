EVENTS TANYA DELIVERS FOR DISTRICT 7.
For the past four years, Tanya Mirabal Moya hasn't just talked about Valencia County's priorities — she's delivered on them.
Here's the record she's proud to stand on, and the work she's still fighting to finish.
Safer Streets, Stronger Communities Tanya backed our first responders with the tools and resources they need to keep us safe, and she's stood up for victims of crime and their families when they needed a voice most. ​ She's not done — Tanya is still fighting to make sure every neighborhood in District 7 is a place families feel safe to live, work, and raise their kids.
Fighting for Our Students Tanya is delivering the infrastructure families count on — flood control, paved roads, and safer streets that protect our homes and keep our community moving. ​ She's not done — there's more work ahead to give Valencia County the modern, reliable infrastructure it deserves for generations to come.
Investing in Valencia County's Future Tanya secured $2.2 million for a Valencia County sports complex and event center — an investment that brings visitors, dollars, and opportunity right here to our community. ​ She's not done — Tanya will keep working to attract good jobs and economic growth so our kids can build their futures without leaving home.
About Tanya Mirabal Moya is a second-generation Valencia County resident who has spent her life — and the last four years as our State Representative — fighting to make District 7 a better place to live, work, and raise a family.​ ​ A proud graduate of Belén High School, Tanya's path to public service ran straight through Valencia County.
She's put in the hours on an exploration drill rig, served local families as an insurance agent, and stepped into the classroom as a physical education teacher.
After earning her teaching license, "Coach T" — as her students know her — returned to Belén High to teach biology, physics, and chemistry, and to coach youth sports teams across our community. ​ That same dedication is what she carries to the Roundhouse.
For the past four years, Tanya has represented District 7 in the New Mexico Legislature, fighting for safer streets, stronger schools, and the working families who make Valencia County home — including standing up for the educators and school employees who keep our kids learning. ​ But for Tanya, this has never been about a title.
She drives the same roads, sent her kids to the same schools, and walks the same streets she's working every day to make safer.
Tanya and her husband Jason have been married for 21 years and have four children — Jacee, Joshua, Tori, and Trenten — and one grandson, Quintin. ​ When you vote to re-elect Tanya Mirabal Moya, you're voting for someone who has never forgotten where she comes from — because she never left.
