PAID FOR BY ED JACKSON FOR STATE SENATE FRANK LAWRENCE, TREASURER
Senator Ed Jackson’s proven record supporting our Tennessee values
Fighting illegal immigration
- Created the Centralized Immigration Enforcement Division to coordinate implementation of immigration policies with the Trump Administration and local governments
- Strengthened the sanctuary city ban by making it a Class E felony for local officials to adopt sanctuary policies in violation of state law
- Invalidated out-of-state driver’s licenses issued to illegal immigrants
- Enhanced state-issued ID standards to distinguish between citizens and non-citizens
- Supported deployment of Tennessee National Guard to the southern border
Combatting violent crime and enhancing public safety
- Voted for “Truth in Sentencing” law requiring violent criminals to serve their full sentences without parole
- Supported funding for over 100 new State Troopers
- Backed Savanna’s Law establishing a new domestic violence registry
- Passed Marsy’s Law to protect crime victims
- Voted for law requiring Department of Safety to coordinate with local governments to remove homeless encampments
- Created the crime of human smuggling
- Supported a Constitutional Amendment to deny bail for violent criminals
Improving Tennessee schools
- Supported hundreds of millions of dollars for teacher pay raises
- Backed plan for minimum starting salary of $50,000 for every Tennessee teacher beginning with the 2026 school year
- Voted for $500 million to improve career and technical education in middle and high schools
- Ensured every high school student has access to computer science and coding classes
- Supported new laws requiring greater oversight of instructional materials in schools to ensure appropriateness for age and maturity levels
Ensuring merit/dismantling DEI
- Outlawed DEI departments in state government, local governments, and public universities
- Ended race-based selection for membership of state boards and commissions
- Prohibited race-based discrimination in admissions, scholarships, and financial aid at community colleges and universities
- Removed Critical Race Theory from Tennessee schools
Protecting our Second Amendment freedoms
- Outlawed state and local governments from maintaining a firearms ownership registry
- Prohibited disclosure of financial transaction data from sales of firearms and ammunition
- Enhanced civil liability protections for firearms and ammunition manufacturers
- Repealed the ten cent per box “privilege tax” on ammunition and shotgun shells
