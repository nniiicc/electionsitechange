Senator Heather Gustafson
It’s an honor to serve as your State Senator and to be a voice for our district in St.
Paul.
As a mom, teacher, and longtime neighbor, I know the opportunities and challenges families here face, and I share the values of our community: strong schools, good jobs, safe neighborhoods, and individual freedom.
From White Bear Lake to Lino Lakes, I’ll keep listening, showing up, and working to solve problems together.
Politics can feel ugly at times, but I believe we do better when we work through our differences and I’ll always be open to good ideas and solutions, no matter which party they come from.
Proud to represent Vadnais Heights, White Bear Lake, Lino Lakes, North Oaks, Gem Lake, Circle Pines, Centerville, White Bear Township, and Birchwood Village.
