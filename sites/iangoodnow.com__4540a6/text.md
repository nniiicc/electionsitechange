Ian on the Issues
Housing
Safe, affordable housing is a human right.
The housing crisis sits at the intersection of almost every issue our community faces.
Funding Vermont’s Future
We need to fully fund and prioritize the state initiatives that make our communities safe, supportive and affordable for all people.
Everyone must pay their fair and proportional share.
Criminal Justice
Our criminal justice system needs to be supported by investing in a justice system that achieves justice rather than overly prolonged and needlessly punitive outcomes.
Working For Brattleboro
We need to better connect our municipal efforts with the work being done in Montpelier so that the residents of Brattleboro are better served by every level of government.
