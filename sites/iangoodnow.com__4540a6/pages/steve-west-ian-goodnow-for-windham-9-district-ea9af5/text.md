Steve West: Ian Goodnow for Windham 9 District
Ian, as an accomplished young man with a vibrant mind and a sturdy character, would strongly represent the needs of the people of his district, while contributing a younger, hopeful voice to the Vermont State Legislature, one of the last true bastions of representative democracy in the country.
His passion for policy-plus-action is precisely what is needed in Montpelier.
I have found him to be whip-smart, kind-hearted and intensely devoted to whatever he focuses on.
He is invested in and committed to Brattleboro, and will bring many skills to Montpelier.
Steve West (Brattleboro Reformer)
