Oscar Heller: Ian Goodnow for State Rep
Ian and his wife Ruth Shafer are new homeowners.
They’re thinking about starting a family.
Ian’s just beginning his law career.
Ian and Ruth love Brattleboro for all its virtues but, like so many of us, are still trying to figure out how to build a sustainable life here.
It isn’t easy, and support from Montpelier will be crucial in making it happen.
Ian has a long-term, personal stake in Brattleboro’s future.
I’ve come to know Ian well over the last two months.
I think he’ll do great things for us, our town, and our state.
I hope you’ll join me on Aug. 13 and place your vote for Ian Goodnow.
Oscar Heller (Brattleboro Reformer)
