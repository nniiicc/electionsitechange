Missy Swim: Ian Goodnow for State Rep
Personally, I know Ian to be a man of great integrity and honest to a fault.
His enthusiasm is infectious.
At the candidate’s forum, he made a statement that resonated greatly with me — He said he is running so that his generation can live in Vermont and thrive and so that the aging population in Vermont can live out their lives in the place they love with dignity — affordably.
As the mother of a 24-year-old who moved away from Vermont to thrive because that was nearly impossible here, and with aging parents, those two statements meant a great deal to me.
Ian wants a Vermont where all can flourish, and I have all the confidence in the world that no one will work harder to see that vision realized than Ian will.
Missy Swim (Brattleboro Reformer)
