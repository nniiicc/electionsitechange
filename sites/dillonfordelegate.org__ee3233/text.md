Volunteer Contribute Governor's Response to Dillon Why The House?
Volunteer Contribute Governor's Response to Dillon The Challenge We Must Fight Now Liberty or Death America is the first of its kind.
No prior nation has been founded on the idea that all men are Created equal and that our rights come from God, not government.
This is the freedom we must protect and preserve at all costs before our children become serfs to globalists whose greed and lust for power knows no end.
Life at Conception The Constitution of the United States protects all innocent life as a basic right without exception.
The right of every child to be be born must be upheld.
Save Traditional Families As the most basic essential unit of society, the traditional family brings benefits to society beyond anything government can create.
As such, traditional families must be valued and protected.
Lower Utility Costs Unlock our natural energy sources in West Virginia, hold utilities accountable, and create competition to bring utility prices lower.
Keep Your Guns The arming of the citizenry both prevents the likelihood of foreign invasion as well as the descent into domestic tyranny.
Additional benefits such as sport, self-defense, and hunting are also valuable.
Real Industrial Jobs Harness our WV metallurgical coal to support coke and steel to build America, not fake "Green Energy" jobs that depend on corporate welfare and drive up energy prices.
Make Elections Secure The survival of our republic depends upon free, fair, and honest elections.
Every effort must be made to ensure that our elections are secure so they reflect the results of elections the authentic votes of the citizens.
Bring Prices Down Families are facing increasing pressure on their budgets at the grocery store.
Meat is especially expensive.
We must bring competition to the meat packing industry and give the small producers a fair shake to drive down prices.
Water that Works Public water infrastructure that is clean, reliable, transparent, and accountable to the people is critical to strong communities and families.
Lock Criminals Away Neighborhoods are being overrun with thieves, illegal aliens, and drug activity.
These are all connected, and we must enforce the laws, deport illegals, and address the root causes of lawlessness.
Save Local Education America was built on small schools and churches spread throughout our rural communities.
We must ensure that rural children are not discriminated against nor bussed to outside neighborhoods simply to attend school.
Stop Corporate Influence Corporations and other large interest groups wield undue influence in our elections.
We must not allow these groups to buy influence with our lawmakers.
Keep Your Money Citizens today are expected to pay taxes and user fees on everything from vehicle stickers to tacked-on utility fees.
These are parasitic losses on hard working families.
Our Christian Foundation America was founded on faith in God and belief in the scripture found in the Holy Bible.
Recognizing this fact does not violate the establish clause of the 1st Amendment.
Stop the Spying Government exists to protect the rights of the people, not manage their daily behavior.
Collecting data on normal citizen activities is antithetical to our founding principles.
It has to end.
Make Govt.
Transparent Every year government creates new departments, boards, commissions, authorities, while hiding information behind layers of legal bureaucracy.
It is past time to shrink government.
Repent and Turn Immorality and degeneracy are at crisis levels in America.
If we do not repent and turn away from sin we will invite further judgments from a Holy God.
No Forced Vaccines No government has the authority to require citizens to receive foreign substances within their bodies against their will.
Prayer in Schools Congress and most state legislatures begin their sessions with prayer.
The students of WV school should enjoy the same freedom.
Fix the Roads Division of Highways receives billions of taxpayer dollars to maintain the roads.
We have to ensure that such money is being used effectively and transparently.
Respect the Police The role of police is to maintain tranquility and order.
We should focus on helping the police fulfill their mission, not finding fault with them.
Love Your Neighbor The most fundamental rule of society--many laws could be erased with the practice of this simple concept.
Grow Local Foods The response to COVID exposed the need for locally produced, healthful foods grown by local farmers.
Dillon supports local agriculture.
Protect our Children Children are the most vulnerable people in society.
We must protect them from online and physical threats to their innocence and safety.
Support Our Teachers Teachers should be equipped and allowed to focus on teaching their students, not compliance with state mandates.
Processess should be streamlined to allow teachers to access and utilize funds in the classroom without jumping through unnecessary hoops.
Defend The Constitution The founders knew that concentrated power was not good for freedom in a republic.
Congress and legislature must hold presidents and governors accountable when they step on the constitution.
Strong Rural Communities Local communities are disappearing as governments and corporations collude through central planning.
This trend has to be reversed through a regulatory structure that helps rather than hurts local businesses. © Copyright.
