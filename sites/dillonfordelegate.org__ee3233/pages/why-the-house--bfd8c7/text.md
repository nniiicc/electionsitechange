Volunteer Contribute Governor's Response to Dillon Heading "The People's House" The House of Delegates is one of two chambers in West Virginia's bicameral legislature.
It is composed of representatives who bring the concerns of their constituents before the lawmaking body of West Virginia's government.
As the chamber with the shorter elected term of 2 years, members of the House of Delegates are generally considered closer to the people than the Senate with its 4 year term.
Henry Corby Dillon has forged positive relationships with multiple legislators in the House over the years, and he looks forward to working with them to advance legislation which makes West Virginia a freer and better state.
In the House of Delegates, Henry Corby Dillon will fight to pass pro-life, Constitutionally sound legislation which lowers burdens on working families, improves education, and strengthens our local communities. © Copyright.
