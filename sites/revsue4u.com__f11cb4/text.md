MEET SUSAN
Susan is an ordained minister in the United Church of Christ, and has lived in O’Fallon for over 18 years.
She is grounded in her faith, and has three sons.
She obtained her BA in History from UMKC, her Master of Divinity from Texas Christian University, and completed her Chaplain Residency at Children’s Medical Center, Dallas.
Susan works as an interim pastor in the United Church of Christ, as a Hospice Chaplain, and takes Emergency Call at local hospitals.
Susan has been involved in St.
Charles County Equality Coalition, St Charles Progressive Democrats, National Women's Political Caucus - STL, Missouri Womens Political Caucus, Empower Missouri, Missouri Jobs with Justice, and is an active member in the United Church of Christ.
Susan is running to be our next State Senator to ensure we have someone who cares about us in Jefferson City.
Missouri deserves better than politics powered by anger.
