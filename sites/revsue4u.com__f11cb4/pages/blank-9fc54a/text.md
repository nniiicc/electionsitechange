Susan Shumway DONATE VISION ABOUT GET INVOLVED GET MERCH SOLUTIONS Solutions for Our Home Healthcare Healthcare should be within reach for every family, not a luxury reserved for those who can afford it.
We must ensure care is affordable and accessible, with trained, capable professionals who have the support they need to do their jobs well.
That means keeping reliable healthcare workers here by improving working conditions and stability across the system.
When our healthcare workforce is strong, our communities are healthier, safer, and more resilient.
Strong Communities ​Strong communities are built when people who work hard can afford their basic needs and build a stable life.
We must raise the minimum wage and ensure it keeps pace with rising prices, so working families aren’t falling behind every year.
A fair, progressive tax system should reward work and ask the wealthiest to pay their fair share, easing the pressure on middle- and working-class families.
By protecting the right to unionize and lowering everyday costs, we can strengthen communities from the ground up and give people real economic security.
Education Every child deserves a fair shot, and that starts with strong, fully funded public schools.
We should guarantee free school meals so no student is trying to learn while hungry, and ensure classrooms have the resources they need to succeed.
Keeping good teachers in our public schools means paying them fairly, respecting their work, and giving them the support to stay.
When we invest in public education, we invest in our kids, our families, and the future of our communities.
Infrastructure Strong infrastructure means safe roads, dependable bridges, and clean, reliable water systems people can count on every day.
In fast-growing communities like O’Fallon, St.
Charles, and Lake Saint Louis, we must invest in maintaining and improving what we already have so families can get where they need to go safely.
Protecting clean water and modernizing aging systems is essential for public health and long-term stability.
When we invest in infrastructure, we invest in the safety and future of our communities. housing within reach Affordable housing is essential for strong, stable communities.
Families, seniors, and workers should be able to live near their jobs, schools, and support networks without being priced out.
We must expand access to affordable, safe housing options while protecting existing neighborhoods and keeping costs from spiraling out of reach.
When housing is stable and affordable, families are stronger and communities thrive.
