Thank you to everyone who joined us for a fabulous Addison Senate District campaign kickoff event last week in Middlebury!
Nearly 200 people gathered with me and Hannah Sessions to celebrate the launch of our (re)-election campaign.
Hannah introduced herself to the crowd, told her story as a daughter of Addison County, and talked about why she’s running to be a farmer senator.
I talked about my work in the Senate for constituents and why this election matters so much for our communities and Vermont.
We showed everyone that we will be a great team for the Addison District in the State House.
With so many friends and supporters there, we felt energized and ready to get out in our communities to talk with voters about our message, our work, and how we will represent them in Montpelier.
Thank you to Red Dog Riley for the wonderful music and to Danielle Boyce and her incredible staff at American Flatbread for the delicious food and terrific service.
The weather was also perfect!
If you’ve already made a contribution to the campaign, thank you!

This race matters and is Vermont Democrats’ best chance to flip a senate seat blue and elect a team that represents the values and priorities of the Addison District.
Thank you for your support!
