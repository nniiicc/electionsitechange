There are nine months until Election Day.
I have committed to running for another term and I need your help.
For the past seven years, I have been a bold, engaged leader for the Addison District in the Vermont Senate, delivering on childcare, education, health care, tax reform, human rights, housing, open government, and more.
I know January was a really hard month.
Watching the federal government invade an American city and kill people in the streets has been devastating.
Last week, I stood on the Senate floor and talked about how my heart has ached for the people of Minnesota .
At the same time, I am focused on protecting Vermonters and tackling the challenges we’re facing here, many that have been made worse by Republican decisions in Washington.
We want to maintain and expand the Democratic majority in the Vermont Legislature.
But, right-wing organizations have already started pouring millions of dollars into Vermont to defeat Democrats and reverse progressive policies.
Every time I run, I raise nearly every penny from grassroots donors, like you!
Can you help me again?
Help me fight back and continue to be there for you!
Pitch in $26 for 2026 so we can be ready for Election Day .
Thank you.
I am deeply grateful for your support.
