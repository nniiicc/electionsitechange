Meet the Candidate About Daniel Daniel and his wife, Marisa, are longtime residents of North Olmsted.
They raised their four children in the community, all of whom attended North Olmsted schools, and they are now grandparents to three, with another on the way.
Daniel is the son of an Irish immigrant, and Marisa is the daughter of Italian immigrants.
They are active members of St.

Conway Author Site Family · Faith · Community Daniel and Marisa Grandparent duty Back in Ireland Transparency A Strong Honest Voice for the People My dad asked me whether I was running as a Democrat or a Republican.
I told him I'm running as your North Olmsted neighbor—one whose property taxes have also risen nearly 40% since 2020.
Like you, I want clear answers.
Why is so much more money suddenly needed, and where is it going?
Party labels are not my concern.
The people serving in our local governments are working hard and trying to do the right thing.
I respect that.
But when homeowners feel they have to move, or risk losing homes they worked their whole lives to pay for, it's clear that good intentions alone are not enough.
"We feel so defeated." A resident from Old Brooklyn said that to me recently.
It stayed with me.
No one should feel that way about their home.
This is one of the reasons I am running to be your next State Representative.
Where is all the money going?
It is what everyone is asking.
Cuyahoga County's financial report is over 400 pages.
North Olmsted's is over 250 pages.
The vast majority of taxpayers cannot understand them and are effectively left in the dark.
You shouldn't need to be a CPA to understand how your tax dollars are spent.
