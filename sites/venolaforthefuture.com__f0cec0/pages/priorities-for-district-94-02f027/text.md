I’m running to represent every family in District 94 with the same standards, respect, and urgency.
My focus is simple: strengthen the systems that shape daily life, (education, economic opportunity, and healthcare), so families can build stable, thriving futures.
I will advance these priorities in the Georgia General Assembly .
Educator & Student Support Support educators.
Care for students.
Prepare the workforce.
Retain teachers in high-need schools and hard-to-staff roles Expand school-based mental health supports Strengthen literacy, math, and career pathways Build stronger family–school partnerships Why this issue matters to you: Strong schools anchor communities and open doors for every student.
Economic Opportunity & Cost of Living Work should pay, and opportunity should be within reach.
Align workforce training with in-demand jobs and real wage growth Support small and local businesses that invest in training and advancement Treat childcare as workforce infrastructure Increase cost-of-living transparency and consumer protections Why this issue matters to you: Families shouldn’t fall behind while doing everything right.
Healthcare Access & Stability Affordable care before a crisis, not after.
Expand access to affordable healthcare through responsible, phased approaches Increase mental and behavioral health capacity Strengthen maternal and postpartum care Invest in community clinics and the healthcare workforce Why this issue matters to you: Healthy families are more stable, productive, and resilient.
A Unified Vision for District 94 These priorities work together: Strong schools prepare students for opportunity Economic opportunity helps families earn and keep more Accessible healthcare keeps people healthy enough to work and learn This is a practical, people-centered agenda focused on prevention, earning power, and long-term stability .
I will advance these priorities in the Georgia General Assembly .
