Raised by a family of hard workers who taught her the value of service, Venola has spent her life showing up, not just for her own children, but for every person striving for opportunity, dignity, and a fair shot.
With a reputation for listening first and acting with purpose, Venola has built her career around empowering others, whether she was working in classrooms, leading community initiatives, or building systems that help people access the resources they need.
Those experiences gave her a front-row view of what’s working and what’s not, and the clarity to know what real leadership looks like.
That’s why she’s running to represent Georgia House District 94 .
Venola is driven by a simple principle: public service should reflect the real lives of the people it touches.
In communities across District 94, from DeKalb to Gwinnett, families are juggling rising costs, unpredictable access to care, questions about opportunity, and frustration with systems that feel distant or broken.
Venola knows these challenges are interconnected, and she believes the role of a state representative is to bring practical solutions, clear priorities, and steady leadership to the table.
Her life and work have taught her that progress is possible when people are empowered, heard, and supported.
Venola doesn’t just talk about change, she listens, collaborates, builds consensus, and gets things done.
In everything she has done, as a parent, as a community partner, and as a professional, Venola’s focus has remained the same: strengthening the everyday foundations that allow families to thrive.
That means bringing the same standards of accountability and urgency she’s practiced in her own life to her work in the Georgia General Assembly, and standing up for policies that expand opportunity, protect families, and make government work for all of us.
