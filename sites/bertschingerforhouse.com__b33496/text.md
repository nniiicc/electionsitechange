Meet Sara Bertschinger
Endorsed Republican Candidate for Minnesota State House – District 20B
Greetings, Republicans and fellow American patriots.
Sara Bertschinger here, and I work in and around Rochester as a home-care nurse.
My husband and I live on our family’s centennial homestead near Pine Island.
Through my work and community involvement, I have seen firsthand the dedication, strength, and resilience that define the people of southeastern Minnesota.
I believe that every citizen has both the privilege and the duty to participate in civic life.
With that conviction, I am stepping forward to represent our shared values — faith, family, fiscal responsibility, and freedom — in the Minnesota Legislature.
My campaign is focused on issues that matter most to our communities: supporting family farms and small businesses, advancing quality education, ensuring government accountability, promoting family values and defending the rights and freedoms that form the foundation of our Constitutional Republic.
Working together, we can preserve the Minnesota we love — strong, self-reliant, and free.
