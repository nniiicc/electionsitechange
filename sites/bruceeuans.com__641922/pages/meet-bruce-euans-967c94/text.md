Meet Bruce Euans
Husband.
Father.
Business Leader.
Conservative.
Bruce Euans is running for the Ohio House because he believes District 9 deserves a representative who is grounded in real life, guided by conservative values, and committed to serving the people — not the political establishment.
Bruce is married to his wife, Michelle, and is the proud father of one child, age 19.
He is a Minerva Park resident who was born, raised, and has lived most of his life in Ohio House District 9.
A graduate of Northland High School, Bruce attended The Ohio State University and is licensed through Hondros College of Business.
Bruce was raised in a small business family with a 47-year history, giving him an early understanding of hard work, personal responsibility, customer service, and the challenges faced by local employers and families.
He went on to build a 40-year business career in the design/build industry, focusing on management and administrative leadership roles.
Throughout his career, Bruce has earned a reputation as a steady, proven leader under pressure.
He understands how to manage complex responsibilities, solve problems, and work with people to get things done.
Bruce is not a politician.
He is a hardworking Ohioan who believes government should be transparent, accountable, and focused on the needs of the people it serves.
