Bruce’s Priorities
Families deserve to feel safe in their homes, neighborhoods, schools, and places of work.
Bruce will support policies that strengthen public safety, hold criminals accountable, and help communities address the challenges that threaten local neighborhoods.
1
Safe Communities
Police officers, firefighters, EMTs, and first responders are on the front lines every day.
Bruce believes they deserve strong support, proper resources, and leaders who have their backs.
In the Ohio House, Bruce will work to ensure public safety departments have the tools they need to protect our communities.
Assist Police & Fire Departments

Support Military, Veterans & Their Families
3
Bruce believes Ohio must honor its commitment to the men and women who serve our country.
Active duty military members, veterans, and their families deserve support, opportunity, and respect.
Bruce will fight for policies that help veterans transition successfully, access resources, find good jobs, and build strong futures here in Ohio.
The Second Amendment is a fundamental constitutional right.
Bruce will defend the rights of law-abiding Ohioans and oppose efforts that punish responsible citizens instead of addressing crime and violence directly.
Protect Second Amendment Rights
4
Ohio Jobs & Families
5
Bruce knows the value of hard work.
Raised in a small business family and with a 40-year business career of his own, he understands the challenges faced by workers, families, and employers.
Bruce will support policies that encourage job growth, strengthen small businesses, reduce unnecessary burdens, and help make Ohio a place where families can thrive.
Ohioans work hard for their money.
The government must be responsible in how they spend taxpayer dollars.
In the Ohio House, Bruce will fight against government waste, fraud, and abuse and hold those who misuse taxpayer dollars accountable.
