Who Is Tom Mehaffie?
When I was 22, I bought Breski Beverage and I have been in business for 30 years.
Like you, I understand the role small businesses play, providing jobs and fostering local connections.
Public service has always been my passion.
Whether serving on community boards or being active in organizations like the Prince Edwin Spring Creek Masonic Lodge, the Lower Swatara Lions Club and Zembo Shrine, I’m committed to the well-being of our community.
I am proud of the reputation I’ve earned as someone who stands up for my constituents, finding common ground for strong public policies is not only my job, it’s my passion.”

Working for us.
