Your California Privacy Rights Subject to applicable exemptions, California residents have the following rights under the CCPA: Right to Know: You have the right to request information about the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the categories of sources, the business purpose for collection, and the categories of third parties with whom we share data.
