November 3 · General Election Get your city's voter guide See the guide → Meet JENNY RAE Jenny Rae built her career the way most Orange County families build theirs – by starting a business, taking the risk, and making it work.
She holds a B.A. in Economics from the University of Virginia and an MBA from Columbia Business School, but her real education came from co-building companies in energy and financial services, then buying and running a private tech-enabled services firm from the ground up.
That's the experience Sacramento and Washington are missing: someone who's met payroll, not just voted on it.
As Executive Director of CAL DOGE, Jenny Rae has spent the past year doing what career politicians won't: digging into state spending line by line and calling out the waste, fraud, and mismanagement that make California one of the most expensive, least accountable places in the country to live and run a business.
Now she's running for Congress in California's 47th District to bring that same discipline to Washington.
The small business owners, the dual-income families stretched thin by gas and grocery prices, the entrepreneurs who came here to build something – they deserve a fighter who's actually found the waste, not just talked about finding it.
Jenny Rae has been featured in Forbes, Business Insider, and other national outlets for a simple reason: she's actually done the things most people in Washington only talk about.
Now she's bringing that record to Congress.
Meet Jenny Rae Live Step One: Cut The Waste.
Lower Your Costs.
Every dollar Sacramento burns on fraud, duplication, and bureaucratic bloat is a dollar that isn't going toward lower gas prices, lower insurance premiums, or a housing market families can actually afford.
Jenny Rae has spent the past year at CAL DOGE finding that waste line by line – and she knows exactly how much it's costing you.
This isn't an abstract fight about the size of government.
It's about your grocery bill, your gas tank, and your rent.
Jenny Rae knows what works, and she's running for Congress to put that discipline to work for CA-47 – cutting the waste that's driving up the cost of living here.
