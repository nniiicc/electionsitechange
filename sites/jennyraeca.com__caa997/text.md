Jenny Rae Le Roux is a business leader, author, and strategic advisor running for Congress in California's 47th Congressional District.
She holds a B.A. in Economics from the University of Virginia and an MBA from Columbia Business School.
She is the Executive Director of CAL DOGE (California Department of Government Efficiency) and previously co-built companies in the energy and financial services industries before purchasing Management Consulted, a private tech-enabled services company.
Jenny Rae Le Roux is running for Congress in California's 47th Congressional District, which covers Orange County, including areas like Irvine, Newport Beach, Dana Point, Laguna Niguel, and Huntington Beach.
Jenny Rae Le Roux's platform centers on making government more efficient, rooting out waste and corruption, and fighting for an affordable California.
As Executive Director of CAL DOGE, she is focused on reducing government waste so hardworking Californians can thrive.
She champions fiscal responsibility, government accountability, and opportunity for working-class families and entrepreneurs.
CAL DOGE is the California Department of Government Efficiency, an initiative led by Jenny Rae Le Roux as Executive Director.
CAL DOGE's mission is to make California work for the people who call it home by identifying and eliminating government waste, improving efficiency, and ensuring taxpayer money is spent responsibly.
Jenny Rae Le Roux has been endorsed by the Republican Party of Orange County, the OC Congress of Republicans, Carl DeMaio (Radio Host and Government Reform Advocate), Steve Hilton (Political Commentator and Former Senior Advisor to the UK Prime Minister), the full OC Board of Education, OC Supervisor Don Wagner, State Assemblymembers Diane Dixon (District 72) and Laurie Davies (District 74), Huntington Beach City Attorney Michael Gates, Stephen Sheldon of Orange County Water District, and Vice Mayor of Irvine James Mai.
