About Nick
Nick Miller is an Army veteran, working-class Missourian, and candidate for State Senate in Missouri’s 18th District.
He’s not a career politician and has no interest in becoming one.
He’s running because working people deserve real representation, real liberty, and a government that answers to citizens instead of donors.
Over the last 15 years, Nick has worked across supply chains in both the private and public sectors, including regulated industries where mistakes carry real consequences.
From ammunition and healthcare to cannabis, his experience has shown him how policy affects real people, and how often government stops serving the public and starts serving power.
