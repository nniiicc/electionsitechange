" REVITALIZE OHIO INDUSTRY AND MANUFACTURING Instead of investing in data centers and AI, lets get back to Ohio's roots and put the middle class back to work with good paying jobs.
President Trump has secured $8.8 Trillion in foreign investment; most of that is in the production/manufacturing realm.
Ohio's leaders should be incentivizing that investment to land here in Ohio.
Bringing businesses back to Ohio will bring jobs back to the middle class.
The influx in businesses/jobs will increase the tax base and help offset the property tax funding gaps. " ELECTED OFFICIALS SHOULD RELATE TO THEIR CONSTITUENTS What keeps you up at night?
