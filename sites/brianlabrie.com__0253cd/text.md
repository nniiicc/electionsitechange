4,694 ft World Record Pumpkin Launch About Brian A Granite Stater Through & Through.
I'm a New Hampshire native and a conservative small business owner with a passion for my community and a track record of getting things done.
Owning multiple successful small businesses across the state, I understand first-hand the challenges facing local entrepreneurs.
I currently serve as Assistant Majority Leader in the New Hampshire House of Representatives, sit on the Bedford Conservation Commission, and serve as President of the New Hampshire Snowmobile Association.
On a lighter note — I hold the long-distance world record for pumpkin launching at 4,694.68 feet, earned over nearly a decade as a headlining personality on the Discovery and Science Channels.
Bedford, with my wife Kerry and our three rescue dogs, has been one of the best decisions of my life.

Majority Leader Bedford Conservation Commission "The best person to decide how to spend your hard-earned money is you ." Where I Stand The Issues That Matter.
Common-sense conservative leadership for Bedford and New Hampshire — built on lower taxes, individual liberty, secure borders, and unwavering support for the men and women who keep us safe.
01 Lower Taxes Lower taxes mean a stronger economy, more opportunities, and greater financial freedom.
I've fought to cut red tape, oppose every tax hike, and unlock New Hampshire's full economic potential.
02 The Second Amendment Responsible gun ownership is a cornerstone of our liberties.
I'll continue to defend the constitutional right to bear arms while supporting strong measures against criminal activity.
03 Back the Blue Coming from a law-enforcement family, my roots run deep with our men and women in blue.
I stand against "Defund the Police," will protect qualified immunity, and back our officers without apology.
04 Strong, Secure Borders Open-border policies have fueled the opioid crisis and human trafficking — and we're seeing it hit New Hampshire directly.
I support firm enforcement and adequate funding for both our southern and northern borders.
05 Election Integrity I oppose centralized elections and nationwide mail-in voting, and I'll stand with New Hampshire's First-in-the-Nation Primary.
Secure elections are the cornerstone of our democracy.
06 Economic Freedom Less government intervention, fewer regulations, and a workforce empowered to thrive.
As a small business owner, I know what makes our economy work — and what gets in its way.
07 Protect Our Resources Conservation and limited government go hand-in-hand.
Through market-driven solutions and local empowerment, we can protect our water and lands without heavy-handed government overreach.
08 Confront the Opioid Crisis The opioid epidemic still demands action.
We must fortify the border, strengthen law enforcement cooperation, and ensure those struggling with addiction have the resources to recover.
Stewardship Conservation, The Right Way.
New Hampshire Republicans have a unique chance to uphold our values while protecting what we love about this state.
Balancing conservation with limited government aligns seamlessly with conservative principles.
By championing responsible stewardship — market-driven solutions, eco-friendly farming, and private investment in sustainable water management — we protect our water and lands without excessive government intervention.
That's why I serve on the Bedford Conservation Commission and as President of the NH Snowmobile Association.
