Paula believes we owe them more than gratitude — we owe them action.
Expand veterans' healthcare access: Fight for better VA services and community health options so veterans can get timely, quality care.
End veteran homelessness: Prioritize housing assistance and rapid rehousing for veterans experiencing homelessness.
Support mental health services: Expand access to PTSD treatment, counseling, and peer support programs for veterans and their families.
Create veteran career pathways: Partner with employers on hiring programs and help veterans translate their military skills to civilian careers.
Honor refugee veterans: Recognize and support the unique service of Vietnamese and other refugee veterans who fought alongside American forces and now call California home.
