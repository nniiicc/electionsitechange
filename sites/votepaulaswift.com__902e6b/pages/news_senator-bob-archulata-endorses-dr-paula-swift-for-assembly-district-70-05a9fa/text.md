PRESS RELEASE
Senator Bob Archulata Endorses Dr.
Paula Swift for Assembly District 70
Seal Beach, CA – Today, Dr.
Paula Swift, candidate for California State Assembly District 70, announced the endorsement of Senator Bob Archuleta, underscoring the growing coalition of elected leaders who believe Dr.
Swift is the right leader to represent Orange County families in Sacramento.
“I am proud to endorse Dr.
Paula Swift for California’s 70th Assembly District,” said Senator Bob Archuleta.
“Paula has built a career defined by service, integrity, and a commitment to improving the lives of others.
She understands the needs of working families, veterans, small businesses, and students, and she has the experience to deliver practical results.
Senator Archuleta continued, “Paula is a collaborative leader who listens, brings people together, and gets things done.
I know she will be an effective voice in Sacramento, and I encourage the voters of the 70th Assembly District to join me in supporting Dr.
Paula Swift for State Assembly.”
Dr.
Swift welcomed the endorsement with gratitude.
“I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of Senator Bob Archuleta,” said Dr.
Paula Swift.
“His distinguished service—as a United States Army veteran, State Senator, and Presidential Appointee to the Board of Visitors of the United States Military Academy at West Point has been guided by integrity and a lifelong commitment to serving others.
I am grateful for his confidence in our campaign.
Together, we share a commitment to bringing people together, solving problems, and delivering results for the families of the 70th Assembly District.”
Assembly District 70 encompasses communities across Orange County including all of Westminster, Stanton, Los Alamitos, Fountain Valley, and Garden Grove, along with portions of Seal Beach, Santa Ana, and Huntington Beach.
The General Election will take place on November 3, 2026
