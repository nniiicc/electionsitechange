Honoring & Supporting Our Seniors
Paula was raised by parents who left the segregated South to build a better life in California.
They taught her the value of hard work, faith, and service — lessons she carries with her every day.
Our seniors built this community.
Paula will fight to ensure they can age with dignity, security, and the respect they’ve earned.
- Lower prescription costs: Cap out-of-pocket drug costs so seniors don't have to choose between medication and other essentials.
- Expand in-home care options: Help seniors stay in their homes and communities with affordable in-home support services.
- Support senior centers and programs: Fund community programs that keep seniors active, connected, and engaged.
- Combat elder abuse and fraud: Strengthen protections against scams, financial exploitation, and abuse targeting vulnerable seniors.
