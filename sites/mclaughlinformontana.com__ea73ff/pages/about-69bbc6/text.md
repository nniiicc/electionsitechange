Meet Kate Kate McLaughlin - HD9 - McLaughlin For Montana Raised in rural Vermont, with 8 siblings, Kate learned early what it means to stretch a dollar, care for family, and be a part of a community that helps one another.
Kate moved to Kila, Montana in 2001 and lived in a remote off grid log cabin.
That journey, from poverty to stability , shaped her belief that no one who works hard should be left behind.
Kate spent years in service work—jobs that required resilience, empathy, and grit —before building a small business with her husband.
Kate is a musician, a creator, a wife, the mother of three sons and the grandmother of a fourth generation Montanan.
Families First: Integrity Always Kate has worked in home healthcare- where she assisted and cared for veterans, the elderly and physically disabled patients in their homes.
Kate worked with Dream Adaptive as an Operation Specialist.
Their mission is outdoor recreation for all.
Kate was involved in day to day administrative duties, fundraising, interactions with volunteers, donors, participants and staff.
Kate worked for the Abbie Shelter as an advocate in the city courts ( Whitefish, Kalispell, Columbia Falls) for women and children who had experienced sexual assault and/or domestic violence.
Kate has worked with the Glacier Institute, whose mission is to strengthen connections to the natural world through outdoor education.
Kate and her husband have owned a restaurant business.
She learned quickly how to do the financial aspect ( payroll, A/P, A/R) along with employing, working and running a small business.
Currently, Kate is volunteering and serving as treasurer/president elect with Soroptimist International of Whitefish, a local and global organization that empowers females through education.
