Which party will you caucus with?

Why Don't You Sign Pledges?
I generally don’t sign pledges, although I did endorse the proposal to uncap the House of Representatives.

Second, even if I agree with a legislative proposal, it might look very different by the time it gets to the floor of the House because of changes sought by lobbyists and approved by party bosses.
Third, as a true independent, I don’t want to answer to a party boss, but I also won’t be owned by any interest group.
