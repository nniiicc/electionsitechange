JOIN THE TEAM Meet Bill Volunteer Register to Vote A MESSAGE FROM Secretary Bill Galvin Dear Bay Staters, I wish to inform you that I will be seeking to extend my service as Secretary of State through 2030.
I have come to this decision to address three specific challenges: Protecting the rights of Massachusetts Citizens to Vote.
The rights of our citizens to vote are presently under attack by the Trump Administration.
The federal administration is seeking to limit the rights of Massachusetts citizens to register to vote and the right to vote by mail.
This administration is also seeking without any need, detailed personal information about Massachusetts voters.
My office is actively resisting these efforts in federal court at this time.
Interference with the popular vote and process of the Electoral College in 2028.
As we anticipate the 2028 Presidential election I am greatly concerned about federal efforts to interfere with the conduct of this election.
The events of January 6, 2021 and the ongoing effort to manipulate congressional districts in 2026 compel me to anticipate other interference in 2028.
As the longest serving state elections administrator in the nation, it is my intention to expose and resist any such efforts wherever attempted.
Get a complete and accurate count in Census 2030.
As the Massachusetts liaison for Census 2030, I am greatly concerned about efforts now underway to limit the process and procedures for the count in April 2030.
Trump inspired challenges would damage an accurate count in Massachusetts by seeking to limit the counting of group quarters and exclude nonnative born residents.
The effect of these policies would be to limit federal aid and representation and injure our economy.
In 2020 I led the effort that resulted in our population count exceeding previous federal estimates.
I wish to make the 2030 count accurate and inclusive.
I hope to have the opportunity to finish my work, and be found worthy of your support.
Sincerely, William Francis Galvin Secretary of the Commonwealth Bill's Priorities Election Integrity Safeguarding our democratic system through impartial, free and fair elections.
Expanding Voter Access Expanding access to voting, including early voting, voter pre-registration and vote-by-mail.
Protecting Consumers Protecting consumers from financial fraud by regulating the Commonwealth’s financial services sector.
