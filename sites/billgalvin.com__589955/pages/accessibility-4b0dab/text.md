Website Accessibility
The Galvin Committee is committed to ensuring that information on this website is accessible to all persons with disabilities.
If you cannot access content or use features on our website due to a disability, please let us know how we can assist and/or make improvements by emailing campaign@billgalvin.com.
We are continually working toward meeting the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.1.
Thank you for your partnership in helping to ensure that our website is accessible to all visitors.
