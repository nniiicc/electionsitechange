STATE REP.
LAUREN MELO ENDORSES LIESA PRIDDY for FLORIDA HOUSE
Immokalee - Frontrunner Liesa Priddy is proud to announce Lauren Melo has endorsed her in the race for House District 82.
Representative Melo is the very popular incumbent who is leaving the seat to run for the Florida Senate.
Obtaining this endorsement is seen a bellwether of things to come.
“I’m proud to endorse Liesa Priddy for Florida House District 82,” said Rep.
Melo.
“A conservative Republican fighter and third-generation Florida rancher from Immokalee, Liesa understands the values and needs of our communities.
Her commitment to protecting rural lands, supporting a growing Naples, and serving eastern Collier and Hendry Counties makes her the right choice for Florida’s largest House district.”
Priddy expressed her gratitude for the endorsement, saying, “Lauren Melo’s endorsement truly means a great deal to me.
She’s been a fearless champion for conservative values and a dedicated advocate for our region.
I’m honored by her support and will work hard to earn the trust of District 82 voters, just as she has since 2020.”
This endorsement adds to Priddy’s growing list of supporters, which includes Representatives Adam Botana, Yvette Benarroch and Demi Busatta, former Representatives Matt Hudson and Joe Spratt, LaBelle City Commissioner Barbara Spratt, Everglades City Mayor Howie Grimm, Everglades City Councilman Mike McComas, Collier County School Board Member Timothy Moshier, Hendry County School Board Member Amanda Nelson, and Drew Montez Clark, who ran against Melo when the seat was last open five years ago.”
