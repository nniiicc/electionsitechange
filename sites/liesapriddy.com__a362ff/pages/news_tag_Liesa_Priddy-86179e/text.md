Southwest Florida sheriffs boost Liesa Priddy’s growing House District 82 campaign.
Two of Southwest Florida’s top law enforcement officers are lining up behind Liesa Priddy in the House District 82 race.
Collier County Sheriff Kevin Rambosk and Hendry County Sheriff Steve Whidden have both endorsed Immokalee rancher Priddy.
Their support adds two influential law enforcement voices to a campaign that has been gaining steady backing from local leaders.
