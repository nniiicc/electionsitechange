His Story From refugee to Minnesota State Senator Foung Hawj immigrated from Laos over four decades ago to build a new life in America.
His path from a war-torn homeland to the Minnesota State Senate reflects a lifetime of perseverance and service.
“I could never have imagined I could one day be elected to public office.” Today Foung represents Senate District 67 on the East Side of St.
Paul.
He chairs the Environment, Climate & Legacy Committee and serves on the Jobs & Economic Development and Transportation committees.
English Hmong Lao Thai Fishing Canoeing Gardening Career Multimedia Producer Digital Motion LLC, 1996–present IT Manager Minnesota Council of Nonprofits, 2002–2006 Series Producer Twin Cities Public Television, 1991–1996 Computer Systems Administrator K&A Litho, 1990–1991 Education Master's, Applied Science & Technology Rochester Institute of Technology Bachelor's, Computer Science / Media Arts University of Kansas Languages Fluent in English, Hmong, Lao and Thai, with basic Chinese and American Sign Language.
