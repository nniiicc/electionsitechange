A Message from Foung Dear Eastsiders, I arrived at the Capitol this year under extraordinary circumstances.
Federal enforcement was targeting immigrants and communities of color, and three Minnesotans were killed.
Gun violence had taken the lives of children and elected officials.
Rising costs were pushing families to the edge.
I led the charge to help Minnesotans through these challenges.
We passed $40 million in rental assistance , I advocated for gun violence prevention, and my office connected neighbors with housing support and other resources.
Our district, and your voices, guide my work every day.
It is a privilege to work alongside you and uplift your stories at the Capitol.
