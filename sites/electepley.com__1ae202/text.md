GET INVOLVED Join our team DONATE VOLUNTEER TODAY About Judge Chris Epley Putting Experience to Work Judge Chris Epley is the Presiding and Administrative Judge of the Ohio Second District Court of Appeals.
Prior to his election, Judge Epley practiced law for over twenty years with experience as a prosecuting attorney, a magistrate, and a law professor teaching a generation of students how to be appellate attorneys.
He is a member of the bar in Darke, Champaign, Clark, Greene, Miami, and Montgomery Counties, the Dayton Bar Association's Inn of Court, the Lawyers Club of Dayton, and is the former president of the Oakwood Rotary Club where he is still an active member.
