ABOUT JUDGE CHRIS EPLEY
Judge Chris Epley is a highly respected legal professional with an extensive background in appellate law.
As the current administrative and presiding judge of the Ohio Second District Court of Appeals, Judge Epley is recognized for his fair and impartial approach to the law.
Judge Epley's diverse legal experience has given him a deep understanding of the law and the legal system, which he uses to serve the people of Ohio with integrity and dedication.
