I am a wife, a mom, and a small business owner.
I am a homeschooling mom who is deeply involved in my daughter’s education and committed to making sure she has the best opportunities to learn and grow.
Beyond my family, I am passionate about building community, supporting other small and local businesses, and helping families connect and thrive.
Through volunteering, mentoring, and simply showing up for other parents and children, I see the challenges our community faces and the ways our voices are often not being heard.
I want to bring attention to those issues and make sure families, children, and small businesses have a strong voice in local decision-making.
Why I’m Running As a parent and small business owner in Federal Way, I live the realities many families are facing right now.
The rising cost of living and growing financial strain are not minor issues.
They impact our homes, our livelihoods, and the future we are working hard to build.
I believe leadership should reflect the real experiences of the people it serves, and our community deserves representation that listens, asks tough questions, and stands up with clarity and accountability.
That is why I am stepping forward to make sure families and local businesses have a strong voice at the table.
MISSION STATEMENT Standing for my community My mission is to represent our community and make sure our voices are heard in the decisions that shape our community.
I am committed to showing up, listening, and ensuring those decisions reflect the people who live and work here.
Together, we can build a community where every voice matters and every perspective is considered.
I will work tirelessly to make sure our community’s needs and priorities are never overlooked.
