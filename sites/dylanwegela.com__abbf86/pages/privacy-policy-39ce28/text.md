USE OF YOUR INFORMATION When you register, contribute, sign up to volunteer, or take any other action on our Site, we may ask you to give us contact information, including your name, address, telephone number, mobile telephone number and/or e-mail address.
We may obtain information about you from outside sources and add it to or combine it with the information we collect through this Site.
We use this information to operate this Site, send you news and information about People for Dylan Wegela activities, solicit your participation in People for Dylan Wegela activities, and related uses.
We use your e-mail address to send such information by e-mail, and may use your telephone number to call you or send you a text message or phone call for these purposes.
Additionally, we may share your information as follows, or as otherwise described in this Privacy Policy: To companies that assist us in maintaining this Site or with the People for Dylan Wegela activities, but only for purposes of providing services to People for Dylan Wegela and with the strict requirement that any such company will never itself have the right to contact you for any reason; To comply with applicable law or legal requirements (for example, a lawful subpoena), to protect our rights or property, or to protect our supporters from fraudulent, abusive, or unlawful conduct, or if we reasonably believe that an emergency involving immediate danger of death or serious physical injury to any person requires disclosure of communications or justifies disclosure of records; With candidates, organizations, committees, or groups that we believe share the same political or social viewpoints or objectives as People for Dylan Wegela (except for mobile opt-in data and consent).
Submitting your personal information (including e-mail address) on this page will sign you up to receive communications from People for Dylan Wegela.
We may also use your first name to indicate the latest actions taken on this site.
CHILDREN People for Dylan Wegela complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act.
People for Dylan Wegela does not solicit and does not accept personally identifying information from any person under 13 years of age.
Visitors to this Site who are under 13 years of age should not use this Site except under the supervision and with the assistance of their parent or legal guardian, and should never submit any personally identifying information to the Site.
For example, visitors under 13 years of age should not register, purchase or use services or take any actions on or through this Site.
CREDIT CARD INFORMATION; PROTECTION OF PERSONAL INFORMATION When you purchase services through this Site, we collect credit card information from you.
That information is used solely for processing your contribution; is not maintained by People for Dylan Wegela; and is only disclosed to comply with legal reporting requirements.
People for Dylan Wegela uses industry standard security measures to protect against the loss, misuse or alteration of all of the personally identifiable information that you provide to us.
Our server is located in a locked, secure environment.
Permission to access your personally identifiable information is granted only to you and People for Dylan Wegela employees or contractors who need to know that information to provide services to you and who are required to keep the information confidential.
Although we make good faith efforts to store information collected by this Site in a secure operating environment, we cannot guarantee complete security.
CONTRIBUTIONS TO People for Dylan Wegela – PUBLIC INFORMATION People for Dylan Wegela is required to file regular reports with the State of Michigan that publicly disclose the name, address, occupation and employer of persons who contribute over $100 during an election cycle, along with the amount and date of the contribution.
