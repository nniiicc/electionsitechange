Arizona Power Rankings show which figures have highest profile in state politics
The top Republican, at No. 2, is ex-NFL player Jay Feely.
The former Arizona Cardinals kicker is running for the seat being vacated by U.S.
Rep.
David Schweikert in the 1st Congressional District.
President Donald Trump has endorsed both Feely and one of his opponents, state GOP Chair Gina Swoboda.
Swoboda is No. 37 in the Arizona Power Rankings.
