Judge Ben Smith Launches Campaign for 5th Court of Appeals, Place 6
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Judge Ben Smith Announces Candidacy for Texas Fifth Court of Appeals
Collin County, TX – May 21, 2025 – The Honorable Ben Smith, presiding Judge of the 380th District Court in Collin County, proudly announces his candidacy for Justice of the Texas Fifth Court of Appeals, Place 6.
The Fifth Court of Appeals serves Dallas, Collin, Grayson, Kaufman, Rockwall, and Hunt Counties, and Judge Smith is committed to bringing his extensive judicial experience
and dedication to fairness to this critical appellate role.
Judge Ben Smith has served as the 380th District Court Judge since his appointment by Governor Rick Perry in 2012.
He has been elected by the citizens of Collin County four times.
A graduate of Boston University School of Law, Judge Smith brings over 20 years of legal experience, including his tenure as a district judge and prior work as a prosecutor and private attorney.
He has presided over complex civil, family, and criminal cases, earning a reputation for impartiality, efficiency, and adherence to the rule of law.
His notable achievements include reducing case backlogs and implementing innovative court procedures to enhance judicial transparency in Collin County.
“I am honored to run for the Texas Fifth Court of Appeals,” said Judge Ben Smith.
“The citizens of Dallas, Collin, Grayson, Kaufman, Rockwall, and Hunt Counties deserve appellate justices who uphold the law with integrity and clarity.
My experience as a district judge and my commitment to fair, consistent rulings make me ready to serve on this esteemed court.”
The Texas Fifth Court of Appeals reviews civil and criminal appeals from trial courts across the six-county district, serving a pivotal role in shaping legal precedent and ensuring justice.
Judge Smith’s campaign focuses on promoting judicial efficiency, transparency, and fidelity to the Texas Constitution.
His extensive legal background, bolstered by his education at Boston University School of Law, and his reputation for balanced rulings position him as a strong candidate for the appellate bench.
For more information about Judge Ben Smith’s campaign, including volunteer opportunities and ways to support, please visit www.judgebensmith.com.
