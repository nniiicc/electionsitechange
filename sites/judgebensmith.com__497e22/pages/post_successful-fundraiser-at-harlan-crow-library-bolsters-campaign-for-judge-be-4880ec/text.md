Successful Fundraiser at Harlan Crow Library Bolsters Campaign for Judge Ben Smith
Updated: Aug 10
Plano, TX – February 9, 2026 – Judge Ben Smith, presiding judge of the 380th District Court and candidate for Justice on the Fifth Court of Appeals, celebrated a triumphant fundraiser on February 5, 2026, at the prestigious Harlan Crow Library in Dallas.
The event, themed “Save the Court!!,” drew hundreds of enthusiastic supporters, featuring a live performance by renowned Texas country artist Pat Green and the presence of numerous dignitaries from the political and community spheres.
The evening was a resounding success, uniting conservatives, legal professionals, and community leaders in support of Judge Smith and his fellow Republican candidates for the Fifth Court of Appeals, including Judge Ashley Wysocki and Matthew Kolodoski.
Attendees enjoyed an inspiring atmosphere amid the historic surroundings of the Harlan Crow Library, known for its impressive collection of historical artifacts and commitment to American heritage.
“Events like this remind us of the importance of strong, principled leadership on our appellate courts,” said Judge Ben Smith.
“I’m deeply grateful to everyone who joined us, from our incredible supporters to the dignitaries who share our vision for liberty and justice.
Pat Green’s performance was the perfect touch, energizing the crowd and underscoring the Texas spirit we all cherish.”
Highlights of the night included networking opportunities, discussions on the role of the judiciary, and a showcase of the candidates’ commitments to upholding constitutional values.
With hundreds in attendance, the fundraiser not only raised significant funds for the campaigns but also fostered a sense of community and momentum heading into the 2026 elections.
Photos from the event, capturing the lively crowd, Pat Green’s performance, and interactions with dignitaries, are available on Judge Ben Smith’s Facebook page (http://facebook.com/judgebensmith), where 75 images were shared the following day, reflecting the high energy and strong turnout.
For more information about Judge Ben Smith’s campaign or to get involved, visit his official page or contact the campaign team.
Judge Smith continues to serve Collin County with distinction, bringing over a decade of judicial experience to his bid for the appellate court.
About Judge Ben Smith
Judge Ben Smith is the presiding judge of the 380th District Court in Collin County, Texas.
A dedicated public servant, he is running for Justice on the Fifth Court of Appeals, Place 6, in the 2026 election.
His campaign focuses on fair, efficient justice and protecting Texas values.
