Joey Cohen-Saban was born and raised in South Brooklyn — the son of immigrants and a proud first-generation American.
He grew up in the tight-knit neighborhoods of Midwood and Gravesend, attended the Yeshivah of Flatbush, and went on to earn his degree from George Washington University.
At GW, Joey became a leader on campus — spearheading campaigns to combat antisemitism, support survivors of sexual violence, promote interfaith collaboration, and strengthen the US-Israel relationship.
Those weren't just college activities — they were the values that would define his career in public service.
After college, Joey came right back to Brooklyn.
Today, he and his wife Florence are raising their son Hayim and daughter Violet on the very same block where Joey grew up.
He serves as a Brooklyn Democratic Party district leader and as chief of staff to State Senator Sam Sutton, where he fights every day for the neighborhoods he calls home.
Joey is also a board member of the Sephardic Community Federation, reflecting his deep commitment to Brooklyn's vibrant Sephardic Jewish community.
In 2024, Joey ran for this seat and came within just 270 votes of defeating the Republican incumbent.
That razor-thin margin proved that this community is ready for new leadership.
In 2026, Joey is running again — stronger, better organized, and more determined than ever to deliver for Brooklyn.
Joey and his family in the community they're fighting for.
Joey is running for the New York State Assembly because the families of District 45 — Midwood, Gravesend, and the surrounding Brooklyn neighborhoods — deserve a representative who actually lives their reality.
Someone who walks the same streets, sends their kids to the same schools, and shops at the same local businesses.
From fighting antisemitism to securing proper funding for yeshivas, from keeping neighborhoods safe to pushing back on irresponsible policies that hurt Brooklyn communities — Joey is ready to be the voice Albany needs to hear.
