Real positions on the issues that matter most to the families of District 45.
1
Fighting Antisemitism
Antisemitic attacks in New York have surged to record levels.
Joey Cohen-Saban will champion proactive legislation in Albany to protect all New Yorkers from rising antisemitism and hate crimes.
As a proud Sephardic Jewish community leader, Joey will speak out forcefully against attacks on Israel and ensure that Jewish communities across Brooklyn feel safe and supported.
Joey will push for stronger hate crime enforcement, increased security funding for houses of worship and community centers, and education programs that promote understanding and combat bigotry at every level.
Joey engaging with constituents across South Brooklyn.
2
Neighborhood Safety
Every family in Midwood and Gravesend deserves to feel safe walking to school, shopping on the avenue, or sitting in the park.
Joey will fight for safe neighborhoods by strengthening the relationship between our communities and local police precincts.
Joey supports smart policing strategies that keep streets safe while building trust.
He will advocate for increased foot patrols, better community policing initiatives, and resources to address quality-of-life crimes that erode neighborhood safety.
Building trust across South Brooklyn.
3
Education & Yeshiva Funding
Brooklyn's schools and yeshivas educate tens of thousands of children, yet they continue to be shortchanged by Albany.
Joey will fight relentlessly for proper state funding for yeshivas and all schools in our district — because every child, regardless of where they learn, deserves the resources to succeed.
Joey will work to increase per-pupil funding, secure capital improvement grants for aging school buildings, and ensure that nonpublic schools receive their fair share of state education dollars.
Visiting preschools and community schools.
4
Immigration
Brooklyn is a community built by immigrants — Joey's own family is proof of that.
But the current approach to migrant shelter expansion has been irresponsible and has placed unfair burdens on residential neighborhoods without meaningful community input.
Joey will fight to protect residential communities from haphazard shelter placements and demand that Albany address the needs of current New Yorkers — including housing, healthcare, and education — before taking on commitments it cannot fulfill responsibly.
5
US-Israel Relationship
The bond between the United States and Israel is a cornerstone of security and shared democratic values.
Joey will be a strong, unwavering voice in Albany for the US-Israel relationship — pushing back against efforts to undermine this critical alliance.
As someone who led pro-Israel advocacy at George Washington University, Joey understands the importance of this relationship and will work to ensure that New York State continues to stand firmly with Israel.
6
Quality of Life
The quality of life in our Brooklyn neighborhoods is under pressure — from aging infrastructure and inadequate sanitation to overcrowded transit and unchecked development.
Joey will be a tireless advocate for the everyday issues that make our community livable.
From cleaner streets to better parks, from noise enforcement to responsible development — Joey will fight to ensure that District 45 remains the kind of place where families are proud to put down roots for generations.
