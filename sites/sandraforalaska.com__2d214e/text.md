Development vs environment?
I believe in responsible development that ensures the health and economic well-being of Alaskans, our families and friends, as well as the water, air, fish, plants, berries and moose we rely on to feed our families and live the sweet Alaska life.
It's not either / or -- it's both, at the same time.
I believe in developing a diverse range of energy projects to lift Alaskans back up - out of the poverty levels we're seeing now.
This is our path forward.
