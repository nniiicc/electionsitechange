We’ve tried to write this in plain language.
If anything is unclear, please contact us using the information at the bottom of this page.
Information We Collect Information you give us directly.
When you sign up for updates, volunteer, contact us, attend an event, or make a contribution, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any message you send us.
Contribution information.
If you make a contribution, we (and our payment processor) collect the information required to process it and to comply with campaign-finance law.
Under Alaska law, this includes your name, address, occupation, and employer.
Payment card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored by us.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: • Send you campaign updates, newsletters, and other communications you’ve requested • Respond to your questions and messages • Coordinate volunteer activities and events • Process and acknowledge contributions, and meet our legal reporting obligations • Understand and improve how our website is used • Comply with applicable laws How We Share Your Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information in these limited circumstances: • Service providers.
We use trusted third parties to operate the campaign, including our website host, our email and supporter platform (Action Network), and our payment processor (Anedot).
They may access your information only to perform services for us. • Required public disclosure of contributions.
Campaign contributions are subject to public reporting.
We are required to report certain contributor information — including name, address, occupation, and employer for contributions above the thresholds set by law — to the Alaska Public Offices Commission (APOC).
That information becomes part of the public record.
Please keep this in mind when you contribute. • Legal compliance.
We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the campaign or others.
Cookies and Do Not Track We may use cookies to operate the site and understand how it’s used.
You can usually control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect how parts of the site work.
Some browsers offer a “Do Not Track” (DNT) signal.
There is currently no industry-standard way to respond to these signals, and we do not currently respond to DNT signals .
Data Security We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect.
However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Your Choices • Unsubscribe.
Every email we send includes a link to unsubscribe.
You can opt out of email updates at any time. • Update or correct.
You may ask us to review, update, or correct the personal information we hold about you. • Removal.
You may ask us to remove your information from our lists, subject to any information we are legally required to retain (such as contribution records).
To make any of these requests, contact us using the information below.
Children’s Privacy Our website is intended for adults and is not directed to children.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we’ll revise the “Effective date” above.
We encourage you to review this page periodically.
