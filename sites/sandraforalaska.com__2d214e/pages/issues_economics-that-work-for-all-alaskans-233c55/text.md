An Economy that Works for all Alaskans
- Negotiate with the Federal Government to maximize benefits of Medicaid/Expansion.
Many states maximize their benefits in this way and I think we should be at the table for resources that guarantee the health and well being of Alaskan residents.
- Ensure companies making profit from Alaska's resources contribute a fair share to Alaskans as stated in our *Constitution.
Close the S Corp loophole and return to a simple competitive gross calculation on oil tax.
- Corporate Income taxes on out-of-state digital and internet businesses.
- Ensure a balanced budget.
- Protect our sovereign Permanent Fund.
- Development of sustainable agriculture, along with policies that promote greater food self-sufficiency to improve food security.
*Alaska Constitution, Article 8, Section 2: The legislature shall provide for the utilization, development, and conservation of all natural resources belonging to the State, including land and waters, for the maximum benefit of its people.
