Top Priorities: ENERGY: Reliable, Diverse and Affordable Ethics in Government: Accountability to Alaskans EDUCATION - Fully Fund for Quality An Economy that Works for all Alaskans ENERGY: Reliable, Diverse and Affordable My husband and I built our own off-grid house.
Solar provides the lion's share of our power needs except for a few months in the winter.
Using energy storage and solar we have reduced our fuel bill well over half.
Alaska is missing the boat if we do not utilize all power resources available to us.
Develop small to mega sized resource and energy projects that are both environmentally and financially sound: for the benefit of the Alaskan people, with a vision towards balance and consideration of future generations.
Streamline and make wise appointments to State Energy organizations - quality, ethics and expertise over politics.
Enhance the reliability and efficiency of the power transmission system in the Railbelt region using mid-length storage on the Intertie and by upgrading the lines.
Small and diverse projects can help communities in big ways.
Build the AK LNG with a priority for in state gas for Alaskan residents - IF the economics are beneficial AND it is done in an environmentally responsible manner, while preserving Alaska's long term economic interest.
