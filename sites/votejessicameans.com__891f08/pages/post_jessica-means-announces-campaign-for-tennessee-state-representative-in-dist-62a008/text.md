Jessica Means Announces Campaign for Tennessee State Representative in District 1
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
February 17, 2026
BLOUNTVILLE, Tenn. — Jessica Means, a Sullivan County mom, farmer, small business owner, and county commissioner, announced today that she is running for the Tennessee House of Representatives in District 1, pledging to bring steady, family-first leadership and a renewed focus on showing up for the people she represents.
“House District 1 deserves a representative who is present, accessible, and grounded in this community,” Means said.
“I’m running because I believe leadership is about responsibility; listening to families, doing the work, and following through.”
Means is a lifelong Tennesseean and has been married to her husband, Matt, for more than 18 years.
Together, they are raising a large family rooted in faith, responsibility, and service.
The Means family also owns and operates M-Squared Construction, where Jessica brings a real-world understanding of budgets, accountability, and what it takes to make decisions that work in practice; not just on paper.
Currently serving on the Sullivan County Commission, Means has built a reputation as a prepared, engaged leader who stays involved beyond the meeting room.
In addition to her public service, she homeschools her children, teaches exercise classes, and volunteers regularly in youth and community activities.
“This campaign isn’t about noise or political theatrics,” Means said.
“It’s about families, schools, and local control.
It’s about making sure decisions are made with care and common sense, and that people know their representative is paying attention.”
The campaign will focus on issues that matter most to Sullivan County families, including education outcomes, cost of living pressures, support for small businesses, and keeping communities strong and safe.
Means has emphasized education as a priority, advocating for parent-focused, student-centered policies that deliver real results while respecting teachers and local schools.
Jessica Means’ campaign is built around a simple promise: leadership that shows up.
“Too many people feel like their voices don’t matter,” Means said.
“I want families in District 1 to know they finally have someone who listens, who’s present, and who will work every day to earn their trust.”
The Republican primary election will determine representation for Tennessee House District 1, which is entirely contained within Sullivan County.
Means’ campaign will run a local, grassroots-focused effort centered on direct voter contact, community engagement, and disciplined, transparent leadership.
For more information or to get involved, visit VoteJessicaMeans.com or follow the campaign on social media.
