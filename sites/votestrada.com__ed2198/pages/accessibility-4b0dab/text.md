Our commitment
Joe Strada for Congress believes that every voter in Florida's 11th Congressional District deserves equal access to information about the campaign and the 2026 General Election.
We are committed to making this website usable for people with a wide range of abilities, including those who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, voice control, magnification, or other assistive technologies.
Accessibility standards
We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA criteria across this website.
This includes providing sufficient color contrast for text and interactive elements, using semantic HTML and landmark regions, ensuring keyboard accessibility, supporting reduced-motion preferences, and maintaining clear focus indicators.
What we do
Our website uses semantic headings and landmarks so visitors can navigate efficiently with assistive technology.
Photographs include descriptive alternative text.
Interactive elements such as buttons, links, and form fields have visible focus indicators and are large enough to use on mobile devices.
We support browser zoom and text resizing, and we respect reduced-motion settings for visitors who prefer less animation.
Accessible voting in Florida
Florida provides accessible voting options for voters with disabilities.
Every polling place in Florida's 11th Congressional District offers accessible voting equipment, and election workers are available to assist voters who need help.
Voters may also request vote-by-mail ballots and accessible formats through their county Supervisor of Elections.
For official guidance, contact Sumter County, Lake County, or Orange County Supervisor of Elections, or visit the Florida Division of Elections website.
Third-party content
Some pages of this Site may link to or embed content from third-party services, such as contribution platforms, government election resources, or social media networks.
Joe Strada for Congress does not control the accessibility of those external services and encourages you to consult their accessibility statements.
Report a barrier
If you encounter any difficulty using this website, or if any content is inaccessible to you, please let us know so we can correct it.
Include the page address, a description of the issue, and the device or assistive technology you are using.
You can reach us through the campaign contact form.
Continuous improvement
Accessibility is an ongoing effort.
As we update this website throughout the 2026 campaign, we will continue to review pages, fix barriers, and incorporate feedback from visitors.
Thank you for helping us build a more inclusive campaign.
