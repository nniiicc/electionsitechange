Joe’s plan begins with three commonsense reforms to make Congress less profitable, less permanent, and finally accountable for the money it spends.
1 Congress Is Not a Stock Exchange Investigate Congressional Insider Trading When members of Congress make suspiciously well-timed trades, the American people deserve answers.
Joe supports serious investigations into potential congressional insider trading and real accountability whenever a public official uses privileged information or public office for personal financial gain.
2 Congress Is Not a Retirement Plan Enact Term Limits Joe supports firm congressional term limits so new leaders can rise, new ideas can be heard, and public officials remember that they work for the people.
Experience has value, but Washington was never supposed to become a lifetime career.
3 Congress: Put Away the Credit Card Balanced Budget Central Florida families cannot spend without limits, ignore the bill, and leave the balance to their children.
Congress should not be allowed to do it either.
Joe supports a balanced budget amendment requiring Washington to live within its means, confront wasteful spending, and make honest decisions about national priorities.
The national debt clock should be a warning, not a scoreboard.
