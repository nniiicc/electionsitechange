Strada: On behalf of the Executive Board, Political Committee, and 1,400 members of the Orange County Fire Fighters Association, IAFF Local 2057, I am proud to endorse your candidacy for the U.S.
House of Representatives, Florida’s 11th Congressional District, in the November 3, 2026, General Election.
Your commitment to safe communities and your experience developing a skilled workforce provide a strong foundation for public service.
Building a successful business requires investing in people, providing meaningful training, and retaining experienced employees.
Those same principles are essential to maintaining dependable fire protection and emergency medical services.
We appreciate your investment in paid training, career development, and opportunities for employees to advance.
Our members know that experience cannot be replaced overnight.
Recruiting qualified personnel is only the beginning; keeping them requires competitive compensation, dependable benefits, safe working conditions, and respect for the work they do.
Local 2057 members protect more than 1.5 million Orange County residents and a community welcoming tens of millions of visitors each year.
As Central Florida grows, our public-safety agencies must have the staffing, equipment, and resources necessary to meet increasing demands.
Protecting our communities also means protecting the health and well-being of the professionals answering the call.
Congress has an important role in supporting local emergency services through funding, training, disaster preparedness, and protections for first responders and their families.
We look forward to working with you on these priorities and ensuring that the voices of firefighters, dispatchers, inspectors, and other public-safety professionals are heard in Washington.
Local 2057 is proud to endorse Joe Strada for Congress, Florida’s 11th District.
We wish you success in the General Election and look forward to a productive working relationship.
Sincerely, Christopher G.
