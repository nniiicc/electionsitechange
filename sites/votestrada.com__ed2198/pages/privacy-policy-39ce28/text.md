Introduction
Joe Strada for Congress is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how the campaign collects, uses, shares, and safeguards information that you provide when you visit votestrada.com or interact with Joe Strada for Congress online.
By submitting your information through this website, you authorize Joe Strada for Congress to use that information for campaign purposes, subject to the terms outlined below.
Information we collect
We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you sign up for updates, volunteer, request a yard sign, submit a question, contribute, or otherwise engage with the campaign.
This may include your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, ZIP code, voter registration status, and any message or preferences you share.
How we use your information
When you submit information to this website, you authorize Joe Strada for Congress to use that information for campaign purposes.
This includes sending you campaign updates, event invitations, voting reminders, and volunteer opportunities; coordinating volunteer activities and voter outreach in Florida's 11th Congressional District; responding to your questions and requests; complying with federal election law and campaign-finance reporting obligations; and improving this website and our communications.
Contribution information is collected and processed by our secure contribution platform, not directly through this website.
How we share your information
Joe Strada for Congress does not sell your personal information.
We may share information with campaign staff, volunteers, and carefully selected vendors who help us operate the campaign, manage this website, send email and text messages, and analyze outreach efforts.
These parties are required to use your information only for the services they provide to the campaign.
We may also share information when required by law, including federal campaign-finance regulations.
Text messages and phone calls
If you provide your phone number, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Joe Strada for Congress.
Message and data rates may apply.
You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive, or by contacting us using the information below.
Email updates
If you join our email list, you can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any campaign email.
We aim to process unsubscribe requests promptly.
Data retention
We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.
Security
We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect the information we collect.
However, no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.
We encourage you to use strong passwords and caution when sharing information online.
Children's privacy
This website is not directed to children under 13, and Joe Strada for Congress does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.
Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
Contact us
Questions about this Privacy Policy?
Please reach us through the campaign contact form.
