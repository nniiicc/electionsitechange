Twelve Things Fred Will Fight For
- Restore the ACA tax credits.
Bring back the subsidies Congress let expire, after premiums for subsidized Missourians more than doubled.
- Cap insulin at $35 a month for everyone.
Extend the senior cap to people with private insurance.
- Cap drug costs at $2,100 a year.
Bring Medicare's out-of-pocket cap to private plans so that health issues don't mean bankruptcy.
- Give family caregivers a tax credit.
Up to $5,000 for the working families carrying the cost of caring for a parent or spouse.
- Let families use pre-tax accounts for a parent's care.
Your HSA should cover your mom, not just your kids.
- Let Medicare negotiate more drug prices.
Build on a program already lowering prices for seniors.
- Let people buy into Medicare early.
"Lose your job at fifty-eight and you're buying insurance in the worst market there is.
Let people buy into Medicare early.
They pay the premium.
It's an option, not a handout."
- Get cheaper generics to market faster.
End the patent games that block lower-cost competition.
- Kill surprise medical bills.
Enforce the ban so an out-of-network bill never blindsides a family again.
- Get medical debt off credit reports.
Stop a doctor's bill from wrecking your credit and your shot at housing.
- Fix prior authorization.
Stop insurers from overruling your doctor with red tape.
- Cut the home care waiting lists.
Let seniors age at home instead of waiting years for help or being pushed into a nursing home.
What's At Stake
Washington made healthcare more expensive and more confusing.
The 2025 budget law cut $911 billion from Medicaid and let the ACA tax credits expire.
Fred will fight to reverse the cuts, protect Medicare and Medicaid, and put money back in families' pockets.
