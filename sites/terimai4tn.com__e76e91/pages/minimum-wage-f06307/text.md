Home Contact Us Donate Privacy Policy Mental Health & Opioid Recovery Posted 3 years ago by Teri Mai Tennessee continues to face significant challenges related to mental health and opioid addiction.
Families in many communities require greater access to counseling, crisis intervention, recovery programs, and long‑term treatment options.
These services are often limited, especially in rural areas.
The opioid crisis affects not only individuals, but entire families and neighborhoods.
Many residents in District 92 know someone who has struggled with addiction or mental health concerns.
Improved access to mental health services and recovery resources can strengthen families, reduce overdose rates, and help people return to work, school, and community life.
Voters consistently emphasize the importance of addressing these needs with urgency and compassion.
