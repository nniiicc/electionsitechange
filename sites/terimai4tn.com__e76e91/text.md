I am running for the Tennessee House of Representatives in District 92, representing the communities of Williamson and Marshall Counties.
My goal is to bring attention to the everyday experiences, ideas, and needs of the people who call this district home.
As a lifelong Democrat, I have always been engaged in community work, public service, and civic participation.
Stepping forward as a candidate is a new chapter for me—one shaped by listening to neighbors, seeing challenges up close, and recognizing the importance of ensuring that every resident has the opportunity to be heard.
I am committed to open communication, accessibility, and constructive dialogue with people across our district.
Your perspective matters, and I welcome every opportunity to learn from the community I hope to serve.
If you’d like to connect, please feel free to reach out by phone or email.
