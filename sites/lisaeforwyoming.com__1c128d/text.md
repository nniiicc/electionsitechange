Frequently Asked Questions Who is Lisa Engebretsen? + Lisa is a lifelong Casper native, local small business leader, and dedicated public servant.
She has served as Vice Mayor on the Casper City Council, President of the Casper Board of Realtors, and President of the Wyoming MLS.
She is running for the Wyoming State Senate in District 29 to bring common-sense, conservative leadership to Cheyenne.
What are Lisa’s core conservative priorities? + Lisa’s campaign is built on three unshakable pillars.
First is Faith, keeping Wyoming values and strong faith at the heart of our community.
Second is Family, protecting our children, supporting parents , and preserving our unique Wyoming way of life.
Third is Freedom, defending your constitutional liberties from government overreach at every level.
Where does Lisa stand on local economic growth and businesses? + As a small business leader, Lisa knows that local job creators are the backbone of Wyoming.
In the State Senate, she will fight to limit government intervention, cut red tape, protect private property rights, and support policies that allow entrepreneurs and small businesses to thrive.
How will Lisa protect constitutional rights and the Second Amendment? + Lisa is fully committed to defending the United States and Wyoming constitutions.
She stands firmly behind the Second Amendment and will fiercely protect the God-given right of Wyoming citizens to keep and bear arms without government intrusion.
What is Lisa’s stance on the sanctity of life? + Lisa is a proud pro-life leader.
She believes every life is sacred and will consistently vote to protect and defend the unborn at every stage, aligning with Wyoming’s deep-rooted family values.
What is Lisa’s background in local government? + Lisa has a proven track record.
As the former Vice Mayor and member of the Casper City Council, she has consistently stood up for local taxpayers, fought for balanced budgets, and focused on transparent, accountable local government.
