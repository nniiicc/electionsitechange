This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.
Information we collect.
We collect the information you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, mailing address, ZIP code and mobile number, when you sign up, volunteer or contact us.
Like most websites, our hosting provider may also log basic technical information, such as browser type and the pages visited.
Contributions.
Online contributions are processed by Anedot, a third-party payment processor.
We do not store your payment card number.
Contributor information is collected and reported as the Michigan Campaign Finance Act requires.
How we use information.
We use it to respond to you, send the campaign updates you asked for, organize volunteers and comply with the law.
Text messaging.
If you provide your mobile number and check the consent box on our form, you agree to receive text messages from the Committee.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help.
Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, except as required by law.
Sharing.
We do not sell your personal information.
We share it only with vendors who help us run the campaign, such as email and payment services, and when the law requires it.
Text messaging opt-in data and consent are excluded from this sharing.
Your choices.
You can unsubscribe from email with the link in any message, reply STOP to any text, or contact us to update or remove your information.
Children.
This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.
Changes.
We may update this policy.
The effective date above shows the latest version.
