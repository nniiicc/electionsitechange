DONATE Greg is a champion of community change and a long-time advocate for victims' rights.
His 50 year public service career spans across education, government, U.S.
Congress, and non-profit organizations.
Deeply rooted in District 39 as a sixth generation resident, Greg grew up on the land his great-great grandfather purchased at the end of slavery.
Coming from a working class family, Greg’s values are rooted in equity and economic opportunity for all.
Driven by his humble beginnings, Greg has devoted his life to ensuring that anyone who requires assistance receives the support they deserve.
Focused on tackling key issues in education, affordable housing, economic expansion, and public safety, Greg is continuing his legacy of uplifting residents in District 39, Montgomery County, and across Maryland in the House of Delegates.
