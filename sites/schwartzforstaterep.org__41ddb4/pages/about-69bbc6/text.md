My History.
My History.
My History.
I grew up in a working-class family in the Pocono Mountains.
My dad was a welder and my mom stayed at home to raise our family.
My parents taught me from a young age the values of hard work and service to others.
That's why, after the September 11th attacks, I immediately volunteered to serve in the United States Air Force.
I became a Tactical Response Force leader protecting nuclear facilities and leading troops under pressure.
The military taught me the importance of discipline, accountability, and honest leadership.
After a medical retirement, I earned degrees in political science, philosophy, criminal justice, and legal studies.
