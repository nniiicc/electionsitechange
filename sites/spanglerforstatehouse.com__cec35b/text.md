Diana Spangler FOR GEORGIA STATE HOUSE 16 FOR THE PEOPLE I am a former Gulf War Army wife, advocate, and paralegal in the state of Georgia.
My family have been residents of Paulding County for 21 years; I am a mother of three amazing children, with one of them experiencing a learning disability.
As a young working mother, I had experienced firsthand the challenges families face with affordable childcare.
My experiences and advocacy for the community provide opportunities for me to understand the struggles our communities face with access to health care, mental health care, food security, work stability, housing, and education.
I have seen the impact decisions made at the state level have on the quality of life of Georgians and want to work to improve circumstances for people in our communities.
