New Canaan Town Website Darien Town Website Stamford Town Website Tom's Endorsements About Tom Tom O'Dea brings public service, legal experience, and deep community involvement to the 125th District.
Tom O'Dea has served seven terms as State Representative since 2013 and previously served on the New Canaan Town Council from 2006 through 2012.
He is a trial lawyer at Diserio Martin in Stamford, a graduate of Providence College and Columbus School of Law, and a youth coach who has spent more than 20 years on New Canaan sidelines.
He and his wife Kerry have three children who attended New Canaan public schools.
