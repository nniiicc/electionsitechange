STANDING UP FOR YOU
- REDUCING COSTS FOR RESIDENTS Bob helped cut over $600 million in taxes, expanded the child tax credit by $250 per child and the earned income tax credit for working families, and capped the property tax rate on cars.
- PROTECTING REPRODUCTIVE FREEDOM Bob helped pass the Reproductive Freedom Defense Act, which codifies Roe v.
Wade in Connecticut and keeps politicians out of women’s personal healthcare decisions.
- IMPROVING ACCESS TO HEALTHCARE Bob has worked to make healthcare more affordable and available for our families.
He capped the cost of insulin at $25 and invested in healthcare workers to address shortages.
- CREATING OPPORTUNITIES FOR YOUTH Bob increased funding for local school districts, providing more funding and training to support student mental health, and he passed funding for programs and local nonprofits to support at-risk youth.
- INVESTING IN NORWALK AND DARIEN Bob brought over $300 million home in direct funding for Norwalk and Darien, including school construction and renovations, new playground equipment, pickleball courts, flooding prevention, and improvements to roads and bridges.
- TAX CUTS AND BALANCED BUDGET Cut income tax rate for retirees by expanding tax exemption on pensions, annuities, and IRAs.
Continuing 100% exemption on Social Security benefits on state income taxes for single filers under $75,000 and joint filers under $100,000
- SUPPORTING OUR SENIORS Improved elder care by enhancing the quality of home-based services through new training, increasing access to care for seniors who choose to age in place, and expanding state oversight of nursing home operations to protect the health and dignity of their clients.
- PAID SICK DAYS FOR ALL WORKERS Led passage of a new law which expanded paid sick days in Connecticut for the first time in more than a decade.
Over the next three years, every worker at every business will be entitled to use up to five paid sick days a year.
