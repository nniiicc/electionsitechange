Platform -

- Cancer Rates - Cancer Rates are skyrocketing in Iowa.
- Eminent Domain - Against Eminent Domain for Private Companies.

- Property Taxes - Need to be addressed and kept low.
Once Iowa runs out of money, property taxes will be going up.
- Brain Drain - Iowa has become less welcoming to professionals including Doctors, Educators, and Business Owners
