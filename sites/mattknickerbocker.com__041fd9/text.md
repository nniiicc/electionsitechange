Democrat Matt Knickerbocker has a long career of delivering for the people of our region.
With three decades of service to Bethel as First Selectman and on the Board of Education, we can trust Matt to be ready to serve on Day One.
Holding a Masters’ in Public Administration, Matt has also fought for our communities as a director of the Connecticut Conference of Municipalities and as Chair of the Housatonic Resource Recovery Authority.
A resident of Western Connecticut for fifty years, Matt and his wife Adrienne have two children and four grandchildren.
He’s running to restore integrity to our Senate seat.
