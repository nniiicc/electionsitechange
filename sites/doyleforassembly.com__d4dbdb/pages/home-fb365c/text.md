About Steve
Steve has been a La Crosse County resident his entire life.
He learned the value of hard work and financial responsibility at a young age from his parents, Pat and Elaine, who worked several jobs to raise their six children.
Listening to You
Steve puts people over politics and takes the time to listen to his constituents, reaching out to you for your opinions on the issues that matter most to you.
He believes in transparency and accessibility in governance, ensuring that every voice is heard and considered in his decision-making process.
Promises Kept
Steve demonstrated his commitment to bipartisan efforts by advocating for tax relief that supports working families.
Additionally, he supported seniors by working to extend SeniorCare and reduce prescription drug costs, and upheld his dedication to education by opposing significant budget cuts to public education.
