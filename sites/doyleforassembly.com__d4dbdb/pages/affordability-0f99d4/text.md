They can be reached by: Calling 2-1-1 (landline) or 1 (800) 362-8255 (cell phone) Chating online at www.211wisconsin.communityos.org/chat-211 – available 24/7 Text your ZIP code to 898211 – available Monday through Friday 9:00 a.m. to 9:00 p.m.

Food Food Banks, Pantries, and Meal Services: • Wafer: Local food pantry with mobile options and senior programs. • Couleecap: Local food pantry connection program. •La Crosse Meals on Wheels: (608) 784-4623 • La Crosse ADRC: Congregate dining and home-delivered meal programs for the elderly of La Crosse County. • Trempealeau County ADRC: Congregate dining and home-delivered meal programs for the elderly of Trempealeau County.
SNAP: • Access Wisconsin: Federal benefit that can help pay for groceries.
WIC: • WIC Application: Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children.
Housing Rental Assistance: • Division of Energy, Housing and Community Resources: A number of programs that support affordable housing. • Section 8: Rental assistance program funded by the federal government (currently out of funding). • Rural Housing Assistance: Rental assistance for individuals earning under 80% of the county median income.
Home Ownership Assistance: • Rural Housing Assistance: Mortgage and property tax assistance. • SWCap: Counseling and assistance for getting an affordable mortgage.
Emergency Shelter: • Couleecap: Stable housing options for those in need. • Catholic Charities: Overnight warming center. • Salvation Army: Short-term housing for individuals experiencing homelessness.
Eviction Assistance: • Couleecap: Limited financial asssitance to prevent eviction. • Reach Center: Tenant resource center.
Home Repairs: • Habitat for Humanity: Home improvement project assistance.
Energy, Utilities, and Weatherization Energy Assistance: • Division of Energy, Housing, and Community Resources: Low income energy assistance program. • Home Energy +: Energy assistance, HVAC repair, and water system repair. • Keep Wisconsin Warm: Energy assistance for low income individuals.
Weatherization: • Project Home: Assistance with weatherization projects such as insulating exterior walls or replacing inefficient furnaces.
Medical Care Health Insurance: • Covering Wisconsin: Counseling to assist in finding available and affordable health insurance.
Medical Transportation: • MTM: Non-emergency medical transport.
Free Clinics: • Wisconsin Association of Free and Charitable Clinics: List of clinics that provide medical care. • St.
Clare Health Mission: Walk in Clinic Family Services Diaper Bank: • The Parenting Place: Diapers for pickup.
Childcare: • Wisconsin Shares Child Care Subsidy: Assistance with childcare costs.
