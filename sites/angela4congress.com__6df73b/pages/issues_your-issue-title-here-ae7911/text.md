Angela is fighting for
Healthcare for All
Healthcare is a human right — not a privilege reserved for the wealthy or well-connected.
In CA-34, too many people are forced to skip doctor visits, ration prescriptions, or avoid care altogether because they simply can't afford it.
Angela is fighting for a healthcare system that works for everyone — no premiums, no copays, no exclusions based on income, immigration status, or employment.
- CA-34's uninsured rate is 15.6%, well above the national average of 9.8%
Co-Sponsoring Medicare for All
Angela will co-sponsor Medicare for All (H.R. 3069) — a comprehensive, single-payer system that guarantees care to every person in America.
It would:
- Eliminate premiums, copays, deductibles, and surprise bills
- Cover vision, dental, mental health, and reproductive healthcare
- Expand access to gender-affirming care, addiction treatment, and long-term care
- End the influence of private insurance companies
Investing in Mental Health and Substance Abuse Care
- Increase Medicaid reimbursement rates and federal grant funding for mental health and substance use disorder services
- Integrate these services into primary care and community health networks
- Expand peer support programs, housing-first recovery models, and culturally competent care
Strengthening Local Community Health Centers
- Expand federal funding for community health centers to serve everyone, regardless of insurance, immigration status, or ability to pay
- Invest in mobile clinics, translation services, and telehealth infrastructure
- Ensure clinics have staffing and resources for comprehensive, trauma-informed, culturally competent care
