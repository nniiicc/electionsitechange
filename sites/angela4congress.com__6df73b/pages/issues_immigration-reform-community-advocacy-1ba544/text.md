Angela is fighting for
Abolish ICE & Immigration Justice
CA-34 is home to one of the largest immigrant populations in the country — and our district's strength is rooted in the diversity, resilience, and contributions of immigrant families.
Angela is the daughter of a deportee, born before ICE existed, and she has seen what family separation does to a household.
A Pathway to Citizenship for All
- A pathway for DACA recipients, TPS holders, and essential workers
- Protections against arbitrary deportations and permanent family separation
- Legislative solutions ending reliance on outdated, punitive immigration quotas
Protecting Mixed-Status Families and Expanding Access to Services
- Full access to essential social services for mixed-status households — healthcare, housing support, pandemic relief
- Ending discriminatory policies that exclude immigrants from federally funded programs
- Expanding eligibility for driver's licenses, in-state tuition, and other state-level supports regardless of status
- Federal protections against ICE surveillance in sensitive locations like schools, hospitals, and courthouses
Investing in Legal Aid and Community Integration
- Legal representation in immigration court, especially for asylum seekers and unaccompanied minors
- Community integration programs: ESL classes, workforce development, civic engagement
- Modernized, humane asylum processes that respect international law
If we are moving forward with Abolish ICE framing, add:
- Abolish ICE and end the mass deportation apparatus
- Oppose AI surveillance tools built for immigration enforcement, including commercial AI and facial recognition
- A right to counsel in immigration proceedings
- Protect schools, clinics, courthouses, and workplaces from enforcement actions
- Enforce labor law so employers can't use the threat of calling ICE to silence workers
- End for-profit immigration detention
