STEPHANIE’S PRIORITIES Stephanie Popescu is running to make Maryland a place where people don’t just get by, they get ahead.
Right now, too many families are feeling the pressure.
Groceries, housing, and child care cost more.
And for a lot of families, it’s getting harder to plan for the future with a sense of stability.
Stephanie is focused on delivering real results for District 42, starting with lowering costs, strengthening schools, and keeping our communities safe.
She believes government should be accountable to the people it serves, spend responsibly, and support a stronger economy that creates opportunity for everyone.
It’s a commonsense approach focused on the issues that matter most in people’s everyday lives, not party politics.
Stephanie isn’t just interested in talking about these problems.
She’s running to solve them.
Stronger Economy Accountable Government Fiscal Responsibility Lower Cost of Living Better Schools Safe Neighborhoods MEET STEPHANIE Stephanie Popescu is a public school teacher and mom who understands firsthand what families across Maryland are going through.
Early in her career, she worked three jobs just to make ends meet while teaching, an experience that gave her a front row seat to the real challenges people face every day.
From rising costs to growing uncertainty, she knows families are stretched thin.
Stephanie is running for State Senate to be a voice of reason who listens, holds government accountable, and delivers real results for communities across District 42, from Towson, Lutherville, and Timonium to Cockeysville, Hampstead, White Hall, and beyond.
