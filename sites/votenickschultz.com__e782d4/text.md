Meet Nick Get Involved Endorsements District 44 Media Contact Donate Meet Nick Nick Schultz was elected to serve the 44th District in the California State Assembly in November 2024, where he currently serves as the Chair of the Assembly Public Safety Committee.
Previously, he served on the Burbank City Council as a Mayor and Councilmember.
On the Council, he was instrumental in policies that increased economic opportunity, reduced crime, expanded environmental protections, and expanded affordable housing.
