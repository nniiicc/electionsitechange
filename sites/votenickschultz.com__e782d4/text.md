Nick’s Policy Priorities
HOUSING
Nick is fighting to make it easier to build needed housing and expand programs to help renters and first-time homebuyers.
SCHOOLS
Local teachers support Nick because he’s a champion for fully funding our system of public education and lowering barriers for college students.
COSTS
Nick is working with urgency to stop the rising costs of food, gas, utilities, and healthcare; which working families can’t afford.
JOBS
Nick understands the importance of promoting innovation to create career-oriented jobs and supporting local businesses, including entertainment jobs.
ENVIRONMENT
On his first day in office, Nick introduced a bill that later became law to protect California’s rivers and endangered species from the Trump Administration.
PUBLIC SAFETY
As Chair of the Public Safety Committee, Nick is working to prevent crime and keep communities safe.
