The first step to voting in this year’s elections is registering to vote.
Wisconsin allows same-day registration for in-person voting.
You may also register via mail or online up to 20 days prior to an election.
Learn more on voter registration here.
Wisconsin provides for registered voters to cast an absentee ballot for any reason.
Absentee voting can be done by mail or by early in-person absentee voting.
To learn all of the details on how to vote absentee or request your absentee ballot online by clicking here.
