Source: Ballotpedia – Alabama State Senate District 22 ABOUT SENATE DISTRICT 22 Alabama State Senate District 22 covers a large portion of southwest Alabama, including Escambia County, Washington County, parts of Mobile County, and northern Baldwin County, with communities such as Bay Minette, Robertsdale, Atmore, Brewton, Chatom, and surrounding towns and rural areas.
This district is built on hard work, strong families, and historical roots in agriculture, forestry, manufacturing, and small business.
From our growing communities to our rural farms, the people of District 22 resemble the very values that define south Alabama.
Residents across the district deserve safe communities, strong schools, reliable infrastructure, and economic opportunities that allow them to live, work, and raise the next generation right here at home.
We need leadership that understands both the challenges and the opportunities facing southwest Alabama.
