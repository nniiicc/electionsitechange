TERRY WATERS KNOWS THE VALUE OF HARD WORK – BECAUSE HE’S LIVED IT.
Raised on a family farm in Robertsdale, Terry Waters learned early the value of hard work, personal responsibility, and doing right by others.
After graduating from Robertsdale High School, he joined the United States Army and served both at home and overseas, including time in Germany during the fall of the Berlin Wall.
After his service, Terry returned home to farm, build a family business, and raise his family with his wife, Anita.
A proud husband, father, and grandfather, Terry is running to serve the people who raised him and to be a strong, reliable voice for District 22 in Montgomery.
