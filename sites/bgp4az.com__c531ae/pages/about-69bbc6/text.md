HER STORY
SHE KNOWS WHAT IT MEANS TO WORK FOR EVERY MILE.
Bernadette Greene-Placentia is a mom, small-business owner, long-haul trucker, and Democrat running for Congress in AZ’s 8th.
Raised in a union family, she was selected as a Congressional Youth Scholar in Washington, D.C., and attended the University of Arizona on a full-ride Army ROTC scholarship before a car accident changed her path.
She earned her Commercial Driver’s License in 1997 and went on to log more than 4 million miles over 27 years on the road while building a small trucking business rooted in hard work and service.
She is running to make life more affordable, protect reproductive freedom, strengthen Social Security and Medicare, support workers and unions, and restore accountability in Washington.
Bernadette was raised in the rural Midwest, where neighbors had each other’s backs, doors were often left unlocked, and hard work was not a slogan.
It was how families survived and communities stayed strong.
She brought those values into trucking, one of the toughest and most male-dominated industries in America.
She earned her place mile by mile, learned the business from the ground up, and went on to run a successful trucking company of her own.
That experience taught her how government decisions land in the real world.
She has dealt with fuel prices, insurance bills, regulations, payroll, equipment repairs, and the pressure of keeping a small business alive.
She understands what working people face because she has faced it herself.

Bernadette is running for Congress because too many people in Arizona’s 8th District feel ignored by career politicians.
She believes public service should be about solving problems, protecting earned benefits, defending personal freedom, and making sure every family has a fair chance to get ahead.
She is a Democrat, but she will represent every person in the district, no matter their party, where they came from, or who they voted for before.
She believes neighbors can disagree without treating each other like enemies regardless of party.
Bernadette would bring a working-class woman’s voice to Congress and fight for every family in AZ’s 8th.
