She has run a small business, met payroll, dealt with rising costs, and worked in an industry where safety, reliability, and accountability are not optional.
Her approach is simple: protect the promises America has made to the people, make government work more efficiently, and invest in the people and systems that keep our communities moving.
NO POLITICAL FOG.
Every priority on this page answers one question: does it make life safer, more affordable, and more secure for the people of Arizona’s 8th District?
AF LOWERING EVERYDAY COSTS AFFORDABILITY Working families deserve a fair shot at getting ahead, not just getting by.
Bernadette knows families across Arizona are feeling the strain of higher prices for groceries, gas, housing, and everyday necessities.
She believes Washington should focus on practical solutions that lower costs, strengthen American jobs, and make it easier for working people to build a secure future without breaking the family budget.
Lowering the cost of groceries and everyday essentials Support policies that help reduce fuel and energy costs.
Expand access to affordable housing and responsible homeownership.
Fight inflation with practical, fiscally responsible solutions that put working families first.
SS KEEPING AMERICA’S PROMISES SENIORS Social Security and Medicare are earned benefits, not bargaining chips.
Bernadette believes Americans who worked hard, paid into the system, and played by the rules deserve security in retirement.
This issue is deeply personal—her mother was forced to ration insulin because of its cost, a decision that ultimately contributed to her passing.
Bernadette will fight to protect Social Security and Medicare from reckless cuts while working for practical solutions that lower prescription drug costs and ensure seniors can afford the care they need.
Require very high earners to contribute more fairly to Social Security.
Lower prescription costs by forcing big pharma to renegotiate prices.
Support preventive care that keeps seniors healthier and reduces costs.
Strengthen Medicare's role in delivering dependable, affordable healthcare.
Strengthen and fully fund SNAP while making it easier for eligible families, seniors, and veterans to access healthy food.
HC CARE YOU CAN REACH AND AFFORD HEALTHCARE Getting sick should not mean losing your savings or driving hours for basic care.
Bernadette will work to make high-quality healthcare more affordable, more transparent, and easier to reach.
She will protect coverage for people with pre-existing conditions and expand access in underserved and fast-growing communities.
Protect coverage for pre-existing conditions.
Expand care in underserved and rural areas.
Require clear, understandable prices before patients receive a bill.
Support practical ideas that lower costs without lowering the quality of care.
Gradually lower the age of medicare eligibility.
VA HONOR THE COMMITMENT VETERANS Veterans kept their promise to America.
America must keep its promise to them.
Bernadette’s father proudly served in Vietnam, and she understands that military service affects entire families.
After struggling to receive timely care through the VA, he experienced medical complications and passed away following a heart attack.
That loss drives Bernadette’s commitment to ensuring no veteran is left behind.
She will work across party lines to strengthen veterans’ healthcare, improve access to timely, quality care, and demand accountability for every public dollar spent.
Reduce VA intake and appointment wait times.
Improve and expand mental-health services.
Preserve community-care options when the VA cannot provide timely care.
Build dependable, long-term funding for veterans and their families.
IM SECURE BORDERS, FAIR IMMIGRATION Immigration America deserves an immigration system that is secure, orderly, and true to our values.
Bernadette believes we can secure our borders while treating people with dignity and respect, and without sacrificing humanity.
She supports strong border security, modern technology, and the resources needed to stop illegal trafficking and keep our communities safe.
At the same time, she believes our immigration system should be fair, efficient, and provide an earned pathway to citizenship for people who follow the law, contribute to our economy, and are committed to becoming Americans.
We do not have to choose between security and compassion—we can have both.
Strengthen border security with modern technology and adequate staffing.
Crack down on drug trafficking, human trafficking, and organized crime.
Create a fair, efficient immigration process that reduces backlogs and delays.
Support an earned pathway to citizenship for law-abiding immigrants who contribute to our communities.
EV PROTECTING WHAT WE SHARE Environment Clean air, clean water, and responsible stewardship are investments in our future.
Bernadette believes protecting Arizona’s natural resources doesn’t have to come at the expense of good-paying jobs.
She supports practical, commonsense conservation that keeps our air and water clean, protects public lands, and ensures future generations inherit a healthy Arizona.
By investing in responsible infrastructure, water security, and American energy innovation, we can strengthen our economy while preserving the places that make Arizona home.
Protect Arizona's water resources and strengthen long-term water security.
Keep our air clean and safeguard public lands for future generations.
Support responsible energy innovation that creates American jobs.
Invest in conservation and infrastructure that strengthens communities and protects natural resources.
AZ SAFE, STRONG, READY FOR GROWTH Infrastructure Roads, water systems, power, and broadband are the backbone of a strong economy.
Maintaining the systems we use every day is not a luxury.
It is about safety, economic strength, and national pride.
Bernadette will protect infrastructure funding while pushing projects to move faster and deliver better value.
Repair roads and bridges and strengthen water systems.
Cut needless delays without cutting safety standards.
Prioritize qualified local contractors and American-made materials.
Expand reliable broadband and energy infrastructure that creates American jobs.
ED OPPORTUNITY STARTS HERE EDUCATION AND SKILLS Every student deserves a strong school and a real path to a good-paying career.
Bernadette believes strong public schools are the foundation of opportunity and self-reliance.
She will focus public dollars on classrooms, community colleges, universities, and the career training programs that prepare people for work that is in demand.
Invest in public K-12 schools and support educators.
Expand vocational, technical, and apprenticeship programs.
Make student borrowing fairer and more manageable.
Provide targeted relief for borrowers misled by predatory schools.
Create incentives for careers in teaching, nursing, and public safety.
NX A FAIR SHOT AT THE AMERICAN DREAM NEXT GENERATIONS Young people should be able to build a life, buy a home, and plan for the future.
Too many young adults face rising housing costs, job insecurity, heavy student debt, and serious mental-health pressures.
Bernadette will work with anyone who is serious about practical solutions.
Support pathways to stable jobs and first-time homeownership.
Address youth mental-health needs with accessible, evidence-based care.
Improve digital literacy in an age of social media, artificial intelligence, and targeted misinformation.
Make education and training lead to real economic opportunity.
18W RESPECT THE PEOPLE WHO KEEP AMERICA MOVING TRUCKERS AND SMALL BUSINESSes Bernadette knows the road, the rules, and what it takes to keep a small business alive.
Predatory lease-purchase agreements can trap drivers in debt while denying them a fair path to ownership.
Bernadette supports stronger protections for small-business truckers and will bring the voices of drivers directly to Washington.
Fight abusive lease-purchase practices and hidden fees.
Demand clear contracts honest accounting, and transparent broker fees.
Protect independent drivers and true small-business ownership.
Mandate detention pay and access to basic needs after two hours of wait time.

Unions and workers' rights Working people deserve the freedom to stand together for better wages and safer workplaces.
Unions helped build America’s middle class by fighting for fair pay, benefits, job security, safety, and equal treatment.
Bernadette will defend the right to organize and oppose efforts that weaken workers’ ability to bargain together.
Protect workers' right to organize and bargain collectively.
Support fair wages, reasonable benefits, and safe working conditions.
Oppose any legislation that weakens union jobs or the right to organize.
Make sure federal policy values work, not just wealth.
Oppose retaliation against employees who speak up or organize.

Bernadette will protect access to contraception and emergency care, support families as they welcome a child, and keep fighting for equal opportunity at work.
Defend reproductive freedom and access to contraception.
Protect emergency medical care.
Expand paid parental leave and make quality childcare more affordable.
Enforce equal opportunity and equal treatment in the workplace.
No ceilings.
No barriers.
No BS.

Term Limits pledge and will support legislation that turns that promise into action.
Support enforceable term limits for members of Congress and Judges.
Demand that elected officials follow through on public pledges.
Put constituents ahead of party, donors, and personal profit.
Bring more real-world experience and new leadership to Washington.
