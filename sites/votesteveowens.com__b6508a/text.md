Massachusetts 29th Middlesex District The Massachusetts 29th Middlesex District includes some of the country’s most significant cultural landmarks: Historic Brattle Street, the Perkins School for the Blind, Fresh Pond, Mount Auburn Cemetery, and the Watertown Arsenal .
It’s also a source of technological innovation, a center for education, and home to some of the most engaged and accomplished residents of the Commonwealth.
