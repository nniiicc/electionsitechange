Priorities for District 124
My priorities are guided by practical experience, fiscal responsibility, and a commitment to strengthen the communities of District 124.
Strong communities begin with families who can build stable lives close to home.
I will support policies that help working families, seniors, veterans, farmers, and small-business owners meet today’s needs while building opportunities for tomorrow.
Government should not make ordinary life harder.
It should use public resources responsibly, protect taxpayers, and help communities build for the future.
I am focused on practical solutions.
District 124 deserves leadership that listens, respects taxpayer dollars, and understands that rural communities have different needs from larger cities.
My priorities are rooted in respect for the pillars of our nation and state, provide all equal service, assure accountability, and providing the opportunity to build a good life close to home.
