Strong communities begin with families who can build stable lives close to home.
I will support policies that help working families, seniors, veterans, farmers, and small-business owners meet today’s needs while building opportunities for tomorrow.
Government should not make ordinary life harder.
It should use public resources responsibly, protect taxpayers , and help communities build for the future.
I am focused on practical solutions.
District 124 deserves leadership that listens, respects taxpayer dollars, and understands that rural communities have different needs from larger cities.
My priorities are rooted in respect for the pillars of our nation and state, provide all equal service, assure accountability, and providing the opportunity to build a good life close to home.
Our Communities The best ideas for our communities do not always begin in Jefferson City The begin with the people who life here, work here, raise families here, and understand what our communities actually need.
I will work hard to make sure our voices are heard before decisions are made that affect our homes and future.
Rural Hospitals and Rural Health Rural Missouri needs an affordable, and accessible healthcare.
Rural homeowners, small businesses, farmers and working families have rural medical needs.
Hospital services and emergency care should be plentiful and available without traveling an unreasonable distance.
Protecting and recognizing the needs and path to investing in rural health care is a path to strengthening our rural economy.
Strong Public Education Public schools are the heart of rural community.
I support strong public education and real opportunity for every young person to build a future.
Our children should have the tools, instruction, and opportunities they need to succeed at the hightest level, wethere they choose college, military, agriculture, or another path.
Agriculture and Economy Agriculture is more than an industry.
It is part of the identity, economy, and future of rural Missouri.
I support family farms, private property rights, responsible stewardship, and clear rules, and making sure farmers and landowners have a voice before decisions are made that affects their property or livelihood.
Our economy also depends on small businesses, local employers, and the communities surrounding Fort Leonard Wood and waters.
Growth should strengthen local families, protect our resources, and create opportunities for people who already live here, Veterans and Military Families We know that men and women who serve our country- and the families who serve beside them- deserve more than our gratitude and flag waving.
Strong communities help veterans and military families build lives, here through good schools, accessible opportunities, and support for military spouses and children.
I will work to make sure those who have serve can continue to thrive in the communities they helped strengthen .
Protecting Our Communities No one should be overlooked because of where they live.
Protecting our communities means protecting clean water, public safety, reliable infrastructure, private property, and the resources that make rural Missouri a good place to live.
We should solve problems before they become crises and leave our communities stronger for our children and grandchildren.
Accountable Government Government exists to serve the people- not the other way around.
I believe in responsible budgets, careful use of taxpayer dollars, transparency, and long-term stewardship.
Public officials should be accessible, explain their decisions, and never lose sight of the people those decisions affect.
Hard work should produce real results Every proposal I support will be measured by one question: Does it strengthen the families, farms, businesses, and communities of District 124?
