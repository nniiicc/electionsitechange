Tara Hallmark for Missouri House District 124 I General Election Campaign I Join the Campaign
Practical leadership for rural Missouri.
Tara Hallmark is running for Missouri House District 124 to help rural families move beyond survival and have the opportunity to thrive.
Thank you for visiting my campaign website for Missouri House District 124, serving Miller and Pulaski counties.
Our communities deserve practical results: access to a doctor, strong schools, reliable infrastructure, support for agriculture and small business, and a paycheck that stretches.
Tara’s perspective comes from working, adapting, military family life, supporting a household, paying bills, building a career, learning from experience, and continuing to serve others.
Her background includes work across federal, military, state, and local public service systems.
Her experience includes emergency management, disaster recovery, public safety, instruction, agriculture, and small business.
Those experiences shaped her belief in practical stewardship, equal treatment, and accountable public service.
She believes representation should be neighbor-first, service-first, practical, balanced, and accountable.
No one should be overlooked no matter where they live.
