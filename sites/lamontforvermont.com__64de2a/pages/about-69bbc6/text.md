My goal - both as a human and as a Representative- is to uplift all people.
As your legislator, I work to ensure we live in a safe and inclusive environment where peoples’ needs are met, and all can thrive.
—
I am a mother, organizer, advocate, facilitator, trainer, writer, and equity and transformative-justice consultant who lives in Morrisville.
I am a graduate of People’s Academy and a parent to two students in our school district, and I am deeply rooted in this community.
For more than a decade, my work has centered on equity, inclusion, youth development, community wellness, and helping people build stronger connections.
I have served as an early-childhood educator and youth advocate, founded Developing D.I.I.V.A.S. to empower young women, and trained others in Prevent Child Abuse Vermont curricula.
I currently serve on the Board of Directors of Laraway Youth and Family Services and facilitate the Lamoille Equity Team.
Since joining the Vermont House in 2023, I have worked to bring constituent experiences and people-centered policy into the State House.
LEGISLATIVE WORK
During the 2025–2026 legislative session, I serve on the House Committee on General and Housing and the Canvassing Committee.
I introduced H.440, a proposal concerning protections against no-cause eviction, after hearing from people directly affected by housing instability and from community advocacy organizations.
I continue working to ensure housing policy reflects the experiences of tenants, property owners, families, and communities.
I have also advocated for accessibility, visitability, adaptability, and universal design to be considered earlier in housing planning.
Designing for people of different ages and abilities from the beginning can promote equity and reduce the cost of later modifications.
CURRENT FOCUS
— Protecting housing stability and reducing avoidable displacement
— Expanding affordable, safe, and accessible housing
— Ensuring constituent and community voices inform policy
— Supporting youth, families, recovery, and community wellness
— Advancing equity and access in state decision-making
