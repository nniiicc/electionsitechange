Privacy Policy
Kent for Senate is committed to protecting the privacy of personal information gathered from our supporters and visitors to our website at kentwvsenate.com.
If you have questions about these policies, we encourage you to contact us by email at kentwvsenate@gmail.com.
Collection and use of personal information
Kent for Senate collects personal information voluntarily supplied by visitors to this website.
We may use such voluntarily provided information to understand your interests or contact you for future support.
To receive information from Kent for Senate, volunteer, or donate, you may choose to provide personal information such as your name, postal address, telephone number, and email address.
How personal information is used
If you provide us with personal information through this website, we will use it for the purposes for which you submitted it, such as volunteering activites.
We may also use your information in other ways, such as emailing you about Kent for Senate news or fundraising campaigns.
We may also use your name, postal address, and telephone number to contact you by mail or phone.
Disclosure of information to third parties
Kent for Senate uses third-party vendors to provide functionality via this website and in offline business operations.
The information you provide on this website may be shared securely with vendors to provide services such as sending emails or processing credit card transactions on our behalf.
Use of email address
When you donate to Kent for Senate or complete another data form on this website, you will be asked to supply your email address.
Email addresses gathered from donors or subscribers to Kent for Senate are only available to campaign staff managing this information for purposes of contacting you or sending you emails based on your request for information, and to contracted service providers and vendors for purposes of providing services relating to our communications with you.
For those who opt-in to our email news list, Kent for Senate will only use your email address to send information about Kent for Senate and related partners, such as news, events, special programs, and fundraising.
Kent for Senate complies with the provisions of the Federal CAN-SPAM Act.
All marketing email messages contain a link to unsubscribe from Kent for Senate email news list.
You may also subscribe or unsubscribe to Kent for Senate email news list at any time by emailing kentwvsenate@gmail.com or by sending a notice by United States mail to the address provided at the end of this policy.
Monetary transactions
If you choose to donate or purchase event items via this website, your credit card information will be used only for the one-time transaction through our secure online payment system.
In the case of a recurring (e.g., monthly) donation or pledge, the information will be retained securely to process each recurring gift.
Kent for Senate does not otherwise retain credit card information or transmit credit card information to other parties.
Kent for Senate will provide an acknowledgment of donations if a method of contacting each donor, such as an email address or a mailing address, is provided to us.
How we store and protect your personal information
Your personal information is stored behind secure networks, and it is Kent for Senate policy to keep it confidential.
We do not share your information with third-party vendors or organizations outside of Kent for Senate’s direct work.
All personal information provided to Kent for Senate via this website is transmitted using SSL (Secure Socket Layer) encryption.
SSL is a proven coding system that lets your browser automatically encrypt or scramble data before you send it to us.
Use of “cookies”
Our site may use cookies to enhance your experience while using our site.
Cookies are pieces of data that websites and/or web browsers store on a user's computer for future reference.
Your browser is probably set to accept cookies.
However, if you prefer not to receive cookies, you can alter the configuration of your browser to refuse cookies.
Personal information from children
Kent for Senate does not seek to collect personal information about children through our website.
Visitors under 13 years of age should ask a parent or legal guardian for assistance when using the site and should not submit any personal information to the site.
For example, visitors under 13 years of age should not make donations or subscribe to Kent for Senate email news list.
If we learn that we have collected personal information via this website from a child under the age of 13, we will delete that information.
These practices are in place to ensure child privacy and safety and in compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
To learn more about COPPA, visit the Federal Trade Commission.
External links
Links to other websites do not imply an endorsement of the materials disseminated at those websites, nor does a link to another site imply that the organization or person publishing at that site endorses any of the materials on this site.
Kent for Senate is not responsible for the materials contained on any website linked to this site.
If you visit the websites of any of our partners, please consult their privacy policy.
Our privacy policy applies only to your use of the Kent for Senate website and direct interactions with Kent for Senate.
