Homegrown Solutions • Farm-to-Table Fundraising Dinner
Join us for a special farm-to-table dinner supporting Kent Gilkerson for West Virginia Senate.
Meet candidates, current officeholders, community leaders, and neighbors who share our commitment to bringing positive change to West Virginia.
All candidates and current officeholders who support good change for the people of our state are warmly invited to attend.
County organizations, unions, community groups, businesses, and other supporters are invited to host a table.
Farm-to-Table Dinner Menu
First Course
Roasted pumpkin bisque with toasted pepitas and spiced coconut cream
Second Course
Mixed greens with candied pepitas, dried cranberries, pickled onions, fresh apple, and warm spiced maple-balsamic dressing
Smoked Chicken Entrée
Herb-and-spice-seasoned chicken breast, slowly smoked and served with farm-fresh green beans with onion and black pepper, and sautéed corn and peppers with honey-basil-lime dressing
Vegan Entrée
Roasted acorn squash stuffed with quinoa, aromatic vegetables, fresh apples, cranberries, toasted pepitas, herbs, and spices.
Served with farm-fresh green beans with onion and black pepper, and sautéed corn and peppers with honey-basil-lime dressing.
Dessert Choices
Chocolate brownie with strawberries and traditional ice cream
or
Vegan apple crisp with oat milk ice cream
Dietary Information
The soup, salad, smoked chicken entrée, and vegan entrée are gluten-free and dairy-free.
The vegan apple crisp with oat milk ice cream is also gluten-free and dairy-free.
The chocolate brownie is served with traditional dairy ice cream.
