Who wants a yard sign?
We need YOU to volunteer your time and talents to help elect Kent Gilkerson for West Virginia State Senate - District 10!
In the coming months, there are many ways you can make a real difference:
- Put up yard signs in your community, especially in Greenbrier, Fayette, Monroe, Summers, and Nicholas counties.
- Share our social media posts.
- Host a phone banking event.
- Organize a party so your friends can meet Kent.
- Knock on doors in your community.
- Contribute donations for gas, signs, and events.
Every action matters.
If you’re ready to help, please reach out.
We will win together!
