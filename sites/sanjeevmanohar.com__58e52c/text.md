Since taking office, Sanjeev has worked to represent Nashua Ward 5 with integrity, independence, and a commitment to practical problem-solving.
He has brought his experience as a scientist, educator, and longtime Nashua resident to the State House—carefully examining legislation, listening to constituents, and standing up for our community.
Sanjeev's priorities remain clear: lowering costs for working families, strengthening public education, protecting individual rights, supporting veterans, safeguarding our environment, and ensuring that state government remains accountable to the people it serves.
There is still much more work to do.
Sanjeev is running for re-election to continue providing thoughtful, evidence-based leadership and to make sure Nashua has a strong and effective voice in Concord.

Practical Solutions.
Sanjeev believes that good public policy begins with facts, informed by the people we serve, and focused on solving real problems.
He listens to constituents, works collaboratively, and strives to make decisions that strengthen our community and protect taxpayer dollars.
Together Sanjeev Will Help Maintain Healthy Communities Healthy communities begin with healthy people.
Sanjeev will continue working to expand access to affordable healthcare, strengthen mental health services, protect our clean air and water, support veterans and their families, and promote safe, healthy neighborhoods across New Hampshire.
Bolster Public Education Every child deserves access to an excellent public education.
Sanjeev will continue working to strengthen our public schools, support teachers, expand opportunities in science and technology, and ensure that public education funding is fair, transparent, and accountable.
Support and Honor our Vets Our democracy depends on protecting constitutional rights, voting rights, equal opportunity, and the rule of law.
Sanjeev will continue defending reproductive freedom, LGBTQ+ rights, workers' rights, and every citizen's right to participate in free and fair elections.
Grow New Hampshire's Economy A strong economy creates opportunity for everyone.
Sanjeev will continue working to expand affordable housing, strengthen workforce development, support small businesses, improve transportation, and help ensure that every family has the opportunity to succeed.
Lower Costs for Granite Staters New Hampshire families continue to face rising costs for housing, groceries, healthcare, and everyday necessities.
Sanjeev will continue supporting practical policies that strengthen our economy, help small businesses succeed, promote innovation, and make state government more accountable to taxpayers.
We owe our veterans and military families more than our gratitude; we owe them our support.
Sanjeev will continue advocating for access to quality healthcare, employment opportunities, housing, mental health services, and the benefits they have earned through their service to our nation.
