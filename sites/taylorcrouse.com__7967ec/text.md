Taylor Crouse is a lifelong Northwest Missouri native, small business owner, and dedicated community leader from St.
Joseph.
As an experienced entrepreneur in insurance and real estate, Taylor has spent his career building local businesses and serving his neighbors.
He’s actively involved in civic life, including serving on the Buchanan County Republican Central Committee, the Missouri Western State University Board of Governors, and previously on the St.
Joseph City Council.
Taylor and his family are rooted in the conservative values of our community.
Taylor is running for the Missouri House to bring commonsense leadership and core conservative values to District 11.
His platform focuses on fiscal responsibility, supporting small businesses, and uplifting hardworking blue-collar families.
He’s committed to reducing government red tape, keeping taxes low, and protecting individual freedoms from government intrusion.
Taylor will fight for smarter infrastructure investment, stronger local economies, and policies that empower We the People—not government—to shape the future of local communities.
