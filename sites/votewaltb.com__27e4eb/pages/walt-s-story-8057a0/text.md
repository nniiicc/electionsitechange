WALT BLACKMAN REPIBLICAN FOR ARZONIA Please Sign My Pettion WALT'S STORY WALT'S STORY Walt Blackman’s journey is a testament to resilience, leadership, and trailblazing service.
From 2019 to 2022, he proudly served as the State Representative for Arizona’s 7th Legislative District.
But his impact goes far beyond the chamber floor—Walt made history as the first Black American Republican ever elected to the Arizona House of Representatives.
To understand Walt’s convictions, you have to understand his roots.
His life has been shaped by powerful mentors, formative struggles, and a relentless drive to lead with integrity.
From humble beginnings to the halls of power, Walt’s path reflects a deep commitment to turning adversity into opportunity and turning principled beliefs into action.
EARLY LIFE DISCOVERING WALT'S ROOT'S A Journey of Influence and Values Walt Blackman spent his formative years in a family with his father, who proudly served in Vietnam and as a Law Enforcement officer in the United States Air Force for 26 years.
Born at Lajes Air Base in Portugal, he was raised in an environment that valued God, family, integrity, hard work, honesty, personal responsibility, patriotism, and honor.
His parents emphasized the importance of doing what is right, even when no one is observing.
During high school, Walt lived in Wiesbaden, Germany, where he gained valuable insights into various cultures and customs from around the globe.
These experiences broadened his perspective and solidified his commitment to serving his community and country with compassion and understanding.
From Military Upbringing to Military Service: A Journey of Duty, Honor and Country Walt Blackman’s unwavering dedication to country and service was shaped early on by the example of his father, a man of integrity, discipline, and deep patriotism.
Inspired by that legacy, Walt proudly followed in his father’s footsteps, serving over 21 years in the United States Army with honor and distinction.
As a front-line Tank Commander and Platoon Sergeant, Walt led soldiers through some of the most dangerous combat zones in Iraq and Afghanistan.
His fearless leadership and steadfast loyalty to his fellow warfighters earned him the Bronze Star Medal for heroic combat actions in Iraq.
After retiring honorably from active duty in 2016, Walt was recognized with the Copper Shield Award by the Unified Arizona Veterans for his lifelong commitment to veterans and national service.
He and his wife chose to settle with their children in the peaceful community of Snowflake, Arizona, nestled in the heart of the White Mountains, where he continues to serve with purpose, principle, and pride.
ONE SERVICE TO ANOTHER Walt is a dedicated Arizona lawmaker, championing essential issues like pro-life initiatives, immigration reform, and education funding.
He is a strong advocate for Second Amendment rights, limited government, and fiscal responsibility while also emphasizing law and order, border security, and religious liberty.
As Chairman of the Judiciary and Government Committees, and a recipient of the CPAC Conservative Legislator and Emerging Leaders Awards, Walt is committed to serving his community and ensuring a bright future for Arizona.
