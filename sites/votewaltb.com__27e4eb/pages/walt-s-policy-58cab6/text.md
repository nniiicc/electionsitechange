LEADING THE WAY FOR QUALITY HEALTHCARE IN AZ Walt's Healthcare Plan is a trailblazing initiative aimed at revolutionizing Arizona's healthcare system.
This forward-thinking strategy is crafted to meet the specific challenges of the state, focusing on improving services in rural areas, attracting new medical talent, creating a competitive healthcare environment, and making healthcare affordable for all residents of Arizona.
Central to this plan is the commitment to enhancing healthcare access and quality statewide, especially for underserved communities, ensuring that every Arizonan has access to exceptional medical care.
An integral part of Walt's Healthcare Plan is its approach to pain management.
The plan advocates for the improvement of pain management techniques in a safe and responsible manner.
This includes balancing the need for effective pain relief with the critical objective of addressing the opioid crisis.
The plan proposes several strategies to achieve this: COMPETITIVE AND AFFORDABLE Provide tax breaks or subsidies to healthcare providers who offer affordable care or adopt innovative healthcare models.
Support healthcare initiatives that focus on preventative care and wellness programs, reducing long-term healthcare costs. ​ Encouraging the establishment of more healthcare providers and facilities to increase competition, leading to better services and lower costs.
ATTRACT POST-MEDICAL SCHOOL GRADUARES Offer competitive salaries and signing bonuses for recent medical school graduates who choose to practice in Arizona, with additional incentives for those who select rural or underserved areas.
Develop mentorship and career development programs that connect new doctors with experienced practitioners in Arizona, fostering professional growth and networking.
RETAINING DRS IN RURAL AREAS Implement loan forgiveness and financial incentive programs for doctors who commit to working in rural areas for a specified period.
Increase funding for rural healthcare facilities to improve working conditions and resources, making these areas more attractive to healthcare professionals.
