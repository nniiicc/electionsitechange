The Future Starts With Honest Conversations
We believe transparency builds trust.
Here are answers to the most common questions about Jade, her vision, and the campaign.
Who is Jade Simmons?
Jade Simmons is a creative force — a world-class concert pianist, internationally recognized speaker, author, business leader, and servant-hearted changemaker.
Now, she’s bringing her unique blend of inspiration, innovation, and people-first leadership to the U.S.
Senate race in Texas as an Independent candidate.
Why is Jade running as an Independent?
Because independence is the only way to truly put people over politics.
Jade believes Texans are tired of being forced to choose between extremes.
As an Independent, she’s free to focus on solutions, not sides — serving all Texans, not just those in one party.
What does “The Future is Independent” mean?
It’s more than a slogan — it’s a movement.
“The Future is Independent” means creating a Texas that’s safer, stronger, and more inclusive for everyone.
It’s about unleashing innovation, empowering communities, and putting purpose back into public service.
What makes Jade different from other candidates?
Jade isn’t a career politician, she’s a purpose-centered leader who’s already proven her ability to move people and mobilize change.
Her campaign isn’t built on politics-as-usual; it’s built on service, creativity, and courage.
She’s not waiting to win to start serving — she’s already doing it.
What issues will Jade focus on?
Jade’s priorities are shaped by Texans themselves through our Listening Parties across the state.
Key focus areas include:
- Education – empowering teachers and students with modern tools and funding.
- Healthcare – increasing accessibility and affordability for families.
- Economic Opportunity – supporting small businesses and working families.
- Public Safety – creating safer communities through prevention, not division.
- Human Dignity – protecting rights and ensuring every Texan is valued.
How is “Serving All the Way to the Senate” different?
Unlike traditional campaigns that start serving after election day, Jade’s team is already in motion.
Through initiatives like The Least of These volunteer efforts, Women in Business Workshops, Texas Kids Are the Future School Tour, and Inspiration Giveaways to local charities, Jade is proving what servant leadership looks like — now.
