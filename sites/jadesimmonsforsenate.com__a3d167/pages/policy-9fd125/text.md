Independence from Party Control Most Texans don’t wake up thinking in red or blue.
They think about their families, their work, and whether anyone in Washington actually understands their lives.
Healthcare That Works for People, Not Bureaucracies Healthcare should lead to healing, not harm.
That means making care more affordable, more accessible, and more human without forcing one-size-fits-all solutions on the entire country.
Humane Immigration Reform and Border Security We have spent too long choosing between human dignity and border security as though we cannot have both when we definitely can.
Texas border communities deserve a border that is secure, predictable, and governed by law.
But securing the border should not mean treating every person who crosses it as a criminal.
TEXAS-FIRST TECH: AI, DATA CENTERS & TEXAS’S FUTURE I would lead the charge for the creation of a Texas-First scorecard that evaluates every major publicly supported data-center project against a common set of transparent measures.
In order of priority, community impact/public cost, resource impact/capacity, and economic return would be measured.
While economic gain for Texas is desired, money (and especially greed) cannot be the only deciding factor.
Strength and Stewardship: A New American Doctrine for War, Peace, and Global Responsibility Resolve Without Recklessness.
Diplomacy First.
Readiness Always.
America must rebuild its reputation as a nation whose strength is matched by its judgment, whose commitments can be trusted, and whose power serves a clear purpose.
Our allies must know that our word means something.
Our adversaries must understand that our commitments are serious.
And the world must see a United States that can defend its interests without abandoning its principles.
Women’s Empowerment & A Fresh Take on Life and Choice Women deserve systems that respect their work, preserve their rights, stops treating opportunity like a favor, and unblocks the tools that strengthen their families, and protect their lives and their livelihood.
Statement of Belief: Honor God, Serve People My faith is at the core of who I am, how I serve, and how I see others and the world around me.
Since the beginning of time, religion has been abused and misused in the quest for power and control and I believe we are at the height of that abuse again.
Therefore, it’s important you know what role my faith will (and will not) play in how I lead.
The Thurgood Marshall Plan The Thurgood Marshall Plan is a proposed generational investment in opportunity, capacity, and economic mobility that is designed to remove measurable barriers that continue to prevent talented Americans from reaching their full potential and accessing everything that should be available to them.
