Texas Kids are the Future School Tour
Jade teams up with World-Class Experiential Emcee and Percussion Entertainer Elec Simon to do a School Tour of Texas in Fall of 2026.
Some of Jade’s first performances as a young pianist were for kids in schools, first as Miss Illinois performing school assembly programs for k-12 audiences, then as an artist on the Young Audiences roster in Texas, as a guest-artist-in-residence for the Cliburn Foundation for kids in the DFW area, and finally as a supplementary part of her own concert touring in cities across the nation.
She and entertainer Elec Simon, former STOP Sensation and sought-after hype man for some of the largest corporate and business conferences, will perform for kids across the state inspiring them to dream big and become the future leaders Texas deserves.
