Senate collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide when you visit or interact with our website.
We are committed to respecting your privacy, protecting your information, and ensuring your trust as we work together to build a better future for Texas.
1.
Information We Collect We collect information from you in a few ways, depending on how you use our Site: Information You Provide Directly When you: Sign up to receive updates, volunteer, or contact the Campaign Register for events or service opportunities Make a donation or purchase merchandise Fill out a form or communicate with us You may provide information such as: Name, email address, phone number, mailing address Employment information (required by FEC for donations) Payment information (processed securely through a third-party provider) Any additional information you choose to share Information Collected Automatically When you visit our Site, we may automatically collect: IP address and browser type Date, time, and duration of your visit Pages viewed and referral links Device information 2.
How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Communicate updates, events, and opportunities to support Jade’s campaign Respond to inquiries and requests Process donations and comply with legal reporting requirements Improve our website and outreach efforts Ensure compliance with federal election laws We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketers.
Your trust is foundational to this movement — we treat your data with the same respect we extend to your voice.
3.
Email and Text Communications If you sign up for campaign updates, you may receive emails or text messages from the Campaign.
You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of our emails or by replying STOP to any SMS message.
We strive to communicate only when we have meaningful updates, opportunities, or calls to action that align with our mission.
4.
How We Protect Your Information We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
Payment information submitted for donations is processed through secure, encrypted third-party payment platforms that comply with all applicable privacy and financial regulations.
5.
Sharing of Information We may share limited personal data with trusted service providers who help us operate the campaign — such as email delivery services, event management tools, and payment processors.
These partners are bound by confidentiality agreements and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose.
We may also disclose information when required by law or to comply with legal obligations under the Federal Election Commission (FEC) or other government authorities.
6.
Links to Other Websites Our Site may include links to other websites (for example, social media pages or partner organizations).
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external sites.
We encourage you to read their privacy policies when visiting them.
7.
Children’s Privacy Our Site is intended for adults.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.
If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.
8.
Your Choices and Rights You have the right to: Access or update your personal information Unsubscribe from communications at any time Request that we delete your information (where legally permissible) To exercise these rights, please contact us at info@jadesimmonsforsenate.com .
9.
Updates to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in law or campaign operations.
Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.
Your continued use of the Site after updates constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
10.
