Corey Johnson has spent nearly two decades protecting communities as a firefighter, paramedic, and emergency services leader.
For the past ten years, he has served the City of Williston in fire and EMS leadership, helping guide the department’s growth while overseeing operations that protect families across western North Dakota.
Now, Corey is running for the North Dakota House to bring experienced, steady leadership to District 23, focused on strong public safety, responsible energy development, and respect for taxpayers.
