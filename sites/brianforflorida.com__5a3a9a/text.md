Brian Hodgers is not running as a generic political brand.

Priorities Fighting for the issues that shape daily life in District 32.
Lower Insurance Costs Brian will keep pushing for legal reforms, stronger anti-fraud enforcement, and smarter building standards so families stop absorbing runaway premium increases.
Cut the Tax Burden He supports lowering property taxes, ending unnecessary taxes on insurance policies, and demanding that government live within its means.
Protect the Lagoon Brevard's waterways are core to the local economy and quality of life.
Brian supports basin management, stormwater projects, and practical septic-to-sewer solutions.
Secure the Border Brian backs stronger immigration enforcement, support for law enforcement, and ending state-level incentives that attract illegal immigration.
Fix Transportation Population growth has outpaced road capacity.
Brian wants targeted infrastructure investment, better traffic flow, and safer response routes for first responders.
Defend Families and Schools Brian is pro-life, pro-family, pro-Second Amendment, and supports school choice, parental rights, and stronger campus safety.
