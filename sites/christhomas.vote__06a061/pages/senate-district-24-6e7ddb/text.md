PA Senate District 24
Senate District 24 contains parts of Montgomery and Berks counties.
The district includes the following municipalities:
Montgomery: Collegeville Borough, Douglass Township, East Greenville Borough, Green Lane Borough, Limerick Township, Lower Frederick Township, Lower Pottsgrove Township, Lower Salford Township, Marlborough Township, New Hanover Township, North Wales Borough, Pennsburg Borough, Perkiomen Township, Pottstown Borough, Red Hill Borough, Schwenksville Borough, Skippack Township, Towamencin Township, Trappe Borough, Upper Frederick Township, Upper Gwynedd Township, Upper Hanover Township, Upper Pottsgrove Township, Upper Salford Township, and West Pottsgrove Township.
Berks: Amity Township, Bally Borough, Bechtelsville Borough, Boyertown Borough, Colebrookdale Township, District Township, Douglass Township, Earl Township, Hereford Township, Longswamp Township, Pike Township, Rockland Township, Topton Borough, and Washington Township.
