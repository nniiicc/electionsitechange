From cutting taxes and creating jobs, to funding law enforcement and investing in our kids’ futures, Governor Shapiro has been a Governor for all Pennsylvanians — and tonight, he reiterated his commitment to bringing people together to get stuff done and move our Commonwealth forward.
Below is Governor Shapiro’s closing statement in tonight’s debate, as delivered: “I believe every Pennsylvanian should have the freedom to chart their own course and the opportunity to succeed.
Four years ago I was sworn in on a stack of bibles.
I was not given a magic wand that day to solve every problem, but I was given a unique opportunity as the 48th Governor to help solve problems, to help bring people together, and get stuff done.
“I see it every day as I crisscross Pennsylvania.
I see it with the farmer, the dairy farmer, who now has a lifetime of opportunity ahead of them because we landed the Chobani deal and increased dairy production by 30 percent.
I see it in the apprentice who pulled me aside and told me how proud he was of his job and his ability now to be able to buy a truck.
I see it with the excited teacher who got a student teacher stipend and is now teaching here in our Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
“I’m proud of what we’ve gotten done together.
