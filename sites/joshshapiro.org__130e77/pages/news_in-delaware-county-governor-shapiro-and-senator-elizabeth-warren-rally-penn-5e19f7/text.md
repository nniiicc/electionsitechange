This link leads off-site. es October 16, 2026 In Delaware County, Governor Shapiro and Senator Elizabeth Warren Rally Pennsylvanians, Highlight the Stakes of These Midterms PENNSYLVANIA – Last night, Governor Josh Shapiro and Senator Elizabeth Warren rallied local Democrats at the Delaware County Democrats’ annual dinner.
During the dinner, Governor Shapiro and Senator Warren underscored the stakes of the midterm elections and thanked Delaware County Democrats for their hard work knocking on doors, talking with their neighbors, and turning out to help elect Democratic leaders up and down the ballot in November.
From lowering taxes for more than 39,000 Delaware County residents through the Working Pennsylvanians Tax Credit, to launching a nearly $100 million revitalization effort for the City of Chester — Governor Shapiro is continuing to deliver for the people of Delaware County.
During his address, the Governor underscored that by sending Democrats like Elizabeth Moro to Harrisburg, Pennsylvania Democrats will finally be able to raise the minimum wage, build more housing, and codify abortion access in Pennsylvania.
Governor Shapiro has been clear that these midterm elections will be a referendum on the chaos, cruelty, and corruption coming from Washington, D.C. — and here in Pennsylvania, Governor Shapiro is leading the fight to win up and down the ballot this November to get even more stuff done and keep moving the Commonwealth forward.
Read below for highlights from Governor Shapiro’s and Senator Warren’s remarks at the Delaware County Democratic dinner: Senator Elizabeth Warren : “I am so filled with hope to be here with you tonight because I see what we can do.
Pennsylvania is at the heart of it.
“You bring your four Congressional candidates that you’re talking about, your competitive races, and right here — we’ve got the House.
You bring your Governor and bring your state legislature, and you remind us what is possible.
“I am so deeply grateful that I am here with the faithful — the people who have worked so hard year after year after year to try to make this a country that works better for working people.
We’ve had some hard losses on this but it’s close enough, it’s within reach.
It is in reach because of the work that you have done, and work that you will do.
“It is an honor to be here with you tonight.
It is an honor to have the chance to say thank you.
And it is an honor to bring up here to talk with you, the man who two years ago when Donald Trump and Elon Musk chainsawed my beloved Consumer Financial Bureau… Who said ‘alright, if that’s what they’re going to do in Washington, then we’re going to build better protection right here in Pennsylvania.’ A man who has the right values and, and this is the critical part, the courage and determination to deliver — your Governor, Josh Shapiro.” Governor Josh Shapiro : “I’m grateful that everyone is here tonight, and I know that some of you came here tonight out of joy and excitement because you see great things happening in Delaware County.
You see the energy, you see the momentum, and you’re pumped up for what’s going to happen in 19 days.
“I also know that a bunch of you came here tonight because you’re angry, because you’re scared, and because you’re frustrated and worried, because of the chaos and the cruelty and the corruption coming from this president.
I want you to know that there is an answer to all of that, and you are the answer.
“When we faced dark moments, [Pennsylvania] took this nation toward the light.
No one’s coming to do this work for us now.
It’s up to us that we’ve got the power and we’ve got the responsibility.
“#ago, our nation was founded here, and in 19 days on Election Day, the nation will be looking to us to see what we’ve decided, to see if we can change the makeup of the Congress of the United States, to see if we can win a trifecta in Harrisburg, but they’re also going to be looking to see that when we were faced with the challenge, did we stand up to the tyrant?
Did we stand down in the face of his power?
Did we let Donald Trump sap away our optimism?
“I believe I know the answer to that question because I’ve seen what Delco is able to do.
You know how to win elections.
You know how to look toward the future.
You know what your goal is, and I know you’re going to step up and make it happen.
“Delco, thank you for all you’ve done for me over the years.
Thank you for what you will do for our Commonwealth in 19 days.
Thank you for how you will help heal our nation in three weeks.
Delco, the power is in your hands.
