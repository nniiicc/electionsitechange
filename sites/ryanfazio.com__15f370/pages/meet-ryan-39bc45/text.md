After earning a degree in economics from Northwestern University, he returned to Connecticut to work and build a life — working professionally in energy, agriculture, and shipping in Wilton and Stamford before entering public service.
Serving His Community Ryan has been an active volunteer for over a decade, believing that real change starts close to home.
He spent seven years tutoring charter school students and served as a Meals On Wheels volunteer.
He also held positions on Greenwich’s Representative Town Meeting and Energy Efficiency Committee.
