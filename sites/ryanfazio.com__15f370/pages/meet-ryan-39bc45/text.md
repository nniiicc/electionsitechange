Proud Connecticut Native
Ryan was born in Norwalk Hospital, raised in Greenwich, and graduated from the Greenwich Public Schools.
After earning a degree in economics from Northwestern University, he returned to Connecticut to work and build a life — working professionally in energy, agriculture, and shipping in Wilton and Stamford before entering public service.
Serving His Community
Ryan has been an active volunteer for over a decade, believing that real change starts close to home.
He spent seven years tutoring charter school students and served as a Meals On Wheels volunteer.
He also held positions on Greenwich’s Representative Town Meeting and Energy Efficiency Committee.
Fighting For Change
In the State Senate, Ryan has fought relentlessly to make Connecticut more affordable:
- Co-authored SB7 (2023) to disclose hidden utility taxes
- Co-authored SB4 (2025) to reduce “Public Benefits” charges
- Authored SB333 (2024) supporting municipal home rule
Major Distinctions
Ryan’s leadership has earned recognition across Connecticut:
- Named the 2023 AARP of Connecticut ‘Affordable Utilities Champion’
- Named the Council of Small Towns’ 2023 ‘Town Crier’
- A lifetime perfect score from the CT Business and Industry Association
- Multiple law enforcement organization endorsements
