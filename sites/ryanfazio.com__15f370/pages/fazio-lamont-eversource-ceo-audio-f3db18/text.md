Nolan also said the end of the election would be “helpful” as Eversource pursues a rate hike that would increase the average customer’s bill by 18 percent.
“Now we know what Eversource executives say about Lamont when they think Connecticut families aren’t listening,” said Senator Ryan Fazio .
“Ned Lamont can pretend to stand up to Eversource, but its CEO clearly believes the Governor is their friend and that all the tough talk will disappear after the election.
“The CEO specifically cited the sweetheart deal and windfall of $2.4 billion from the sale of their water subsidiary to an un-regulated entity supported by Ned Lamont.
I warned and fought against this deal for two years.
The CEO also cited prospective profitability from a billion dollars of spending on metering installations across Connecticut that Ned Lamont is running for re-election promising to support.
“Hard-working families have watched electric rates climb 38 percent under Lamont, and now Eversource wants another 18 percent from them.
We cannot afford four more years of politicians like Lamont saying one thing before Election Day and doing another after it.
As Governor, I’ll fight the big utilities that are raising costs, eliminate the public benefits charge, and cut electric bills by 20 percent.” Background The audio is from the Utilities, Midstream, & Clean Energy Conference in New York, NY (9/29 – 10/1) Senator Fazio’s plan to lower electric costs can be found here Home Meet Ryan News Issues Volunteer Contact Home Meet Ryan News Issues Volunteer Contact Facebook X-twitter Tiktok Instagram Youtube Paid for by Fazio for Connecticut Inc., Paolo Accomando, Treasurer.
