Sam didn’t come from politics, he prosecuted criminals.
As an Assistant State Attorney for both Clay and Duval counties, Sam Garrison worked with law enforcement and helped safeguard our communities.
That’s why Sam is a staunch defender of the Second Amendment.
He knows that gun ownership by law-abiding citizens is a bulwark against crime.
Most importantly, Sam is a husband and father who believes it is the responsibility of each generation to defend the American Dream and pass it on to our children.
That’s why Sam decided to serve Northeast Florida in the State Legislature, where he quickly established himself as a voice for limiting government, lowering taxes, protecting life, defending families, creating jobs, fighting crime, and safeguarding our constitutional rights.
Throughout his time in the Florida House of Representatives, Sam has listened to Floridians, letting their priorities inform his decision making.
Sam has worked to address some of our state’s greatest challenges, including protecting our communities, creating jobs, promoting mental health, fighting for lower insurance premiums, cutting taxes, and supporting a more affordable Florida.
The people of Northeast Florida reelected Sam in 2022 and his colleagues in the legislature chose him to serve as Speaker of the Florida House of Representatives for 2026-28.
As Speaker and as a lawmaker, Sam will protect our values, preserve individual liberty, and keep Florida free.
Clay County can count on Sam Garrison to keep delivering for us.
