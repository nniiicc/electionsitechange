ABOUT ME
Putting My Experience
to Work
As a husband and father, I understand the concerns faced by the citizens and families of East Hartford and Manchester.
I am the product of a working class family who worked hard so I could have a better life.
I decided that long ago I would commit myself to our community—a community that gave me the opportunity to take advantage of all that our towns have to offer: safe neighborhoods and good schools.
For much of the last decade I have worked toward improving our community, and I have served in appointed and elected office for that reason.
I owe it to you and your families to utilize my education, experience and energy to serve a greater purpose.
I look forward to talking with you to listen to your concerns and ideas about how to make government more responsive to the people who allow me the privilege to serve in elected office.
