The historic County Court House and most of Main Street along with half of downtown Murfreesboro are in the district.
Most of Murfreesboro’s shopping districts, including The Avenue, Public Square and Stones River Town Center are all in the 34th District.
All of the right-side of Hwy 231 from I-24 to the County line is in the district.
From HWY 231 West to SR99, along SR41A to downtown Eagleville to Salem HWY.
Everything South and East of HWY 99 are in the 34th District.
Puckett Creek is the border of the district North to Franklin Rd for most of the district.
The exceptions being Marymont and Muirwood neighborhoods.
From Franklin Road to I-24 Gresham Land, Blaze Dr, Braxton-Bragg and Fortress Blvd form the rest of the 34th boundaries.
