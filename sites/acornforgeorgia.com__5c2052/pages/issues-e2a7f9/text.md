STEPHEN ON THE ISSUES: ​ Immigration: Merit Based Immigration System I will advocate for a merit-based immigration system that prioritizes individuals who possess skills and talents that contribute to our economy and society. ​ Military/Veterans: ​ ​ Quicker Access To Care.
I pledge to maintain well funded VA Hospitals and Clinics in Columbus and Newnan.
Our veterans sacrificed everything overseas and now it is time we take care of them at home with exceptional Mental and Health Care Services.
Financial: Audit & Defund ​ I will lead the charge into auditing and defunding efforts outside our borders that no longer mutually benefit our State and weaken our strategic positions domestically and abroad. ​ Debt The National Debt is spiraling out of control, Omnibus bills are being passed with all kinds of hidden legislation.
I vow to oppose legislation that House Republicans have not had the chance to carefully inspect. ​ Schools: ​ Georgia Values What is being taught in schools should reflect the values we have at home.
I want a school system that teaches our children reading, writing and arithmetic, not radical liberal ideas about sexual orientation and anti-american programming.
More Choice I believe we should give parents and students the ability to make the best decisions for their families.
