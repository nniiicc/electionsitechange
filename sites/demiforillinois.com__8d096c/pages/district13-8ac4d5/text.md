13th District Map
The 13th State House District of Illinois encompasses a diverse, vibrant mix of neighborhoods on Chicago's North Side and along the Lakefront.
This area stretches from the river to the lake and includes neighborhoods such as Uptown, Andersonville, Buena Park, Sheridan Park, Ravenswood, Northcenter, Wrigleyville, Lake View, Southport, Lincoln Square, Bowmanville, Winnemac, Arcadia Terrace, Budlong Woods, and West Ridge.
We will be adding warming centers to our map as it cools off across the city.
Check our events out for mutual aid projects to deliver cold gear and food throughout the winter.
