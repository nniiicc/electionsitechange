Our Future.
Affordable Healthcare The state of Illinois needs legislators ready to respond to the billions of dollars in health care cuts Donald Trump has imposed on our state.
Demi will fight to expand mental health access locally, and help build a resilient Illinois healthcare system that meets people where they are; affordable, comprehensive, and without stigma. ​ Reproductive Freedom Demi will use every possible legislative tool to protect those seeking abortions from a savage and relentless nationwide assault on our rights.
Every person should have full control over their body and future.
Demi will fight to expand access to abortion care, contraception, fertility treatment, and maternal health. ​ ​ Housing Rights Rising rents and property taxes shouldn’t displace longtime residents.
Demi supports rent control, eviction prevention, and increased affordable housing so everyone can thrive in the communities they call home.
She will be a leader in our state's response to the Trump administration's cruel federal housing program cuts. ​ ​ Strong Public Schools Demi believes every child deserves a well-funded public education.
She’ll fight for universal pre-K, equitable CPS funding, safe transportation, and support services that help students succeed—no matter their zip code. ​ ​ Economic Justice From expanding apprenticeships to protecting union rights, Demi is a champion for working families.
She’ll support small businesses, fair wages, and economic growth rooted in equity, not exploitation. ​ ​ ​ Climate Action Our lakefront and neighborhoods need bold climate solutions.
