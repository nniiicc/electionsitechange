JOIN ME Privacy Policy A Legal Disclaimer The Philippe Cicchini for Michigan State Senate campaign collects limited personal information to communicate with supporters, comply with Michigan campaign finance law, and process donations securely through trusted third-party providers.
We do not sell or trade personal information.
Financial contributions are processed by Donorbox and Stripe, and required donor information may be publicly disclosed as required by Michigan law.
Effective Date January 13, 2026.
Information We Collect We may collect the following information: ​ 1.
Information you provide voluntarily Name Email address Mailing address Phone number Messages submitted through contact or volunteer forms Donation-related information, including employer and occupation, as required by Michigan campaign finance law 2.
Information collected automatically IP address Browser and device information Pages visited and time spent on the website ​ This information helps us understand how the website is used and improve our outreach.
Donations and Michigan Campaign Finance Law Online donations to the campaign are processed through Donorbox, which uses Stripe as its payment processor. ​ The campaign does not collect or store your credit card or bank account information.
Payment information is handled securely by Donorbox and Stripe under their respective privacy and security policies.
Under the Michigan Campaign Finance Act, certain donor information—including name, address, employer, occupation, amount, and date of contribution—may be reported to the State of Michigan and made publicly available. ​ ​ For more information, please review: Donorbox Privacy Policy Stripe Privacy Policy How We Use Your Information We use collected information to: Respond to inquiries and communications Process contributions and comply with Michigan campaign finance reporting requirements Provide campaign updates and event information Coordinate volunteers and supporters Improve website functionality and effectiveness ​ We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information for commercial purposes.
Email and Text Communications If you provide your email address or phone number, you may receive campaign updates, event invitations, or requests for support.
You may unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link provided.
You may opt out of text messages by following the instructions included in the message.
Information Sharing We may share information only: When required by law, including campaign finance reporting obligations With trusted service providers who assist with website hosting, communications, or donation processing, subject to confidentiality obligations ​ We do not share personal information for advertising or commercial use.
Data Security We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information.
However, no online transmission or storage system is completely secure.
Children’s Privacy This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Changes to This Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
Contact If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is used, please contact the campaign through the website’s contact page.
