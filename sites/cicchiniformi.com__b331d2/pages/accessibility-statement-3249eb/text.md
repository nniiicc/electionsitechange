Accessibility Statement
The Philippe Cicchini for Michigan State Senate campaign is committed to making this website accessible to as many people as possible, including individuals with disabilities.
We aim to follow generally recognized accessibility standards, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, and we are continually working to improve usability.
If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this site or need information in an alternative format, please contact the campaign through the website’s contact page so we can assist you.
