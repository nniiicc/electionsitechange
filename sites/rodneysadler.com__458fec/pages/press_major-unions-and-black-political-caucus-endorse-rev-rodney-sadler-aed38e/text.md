RODNEY SADLER February 3, 2026 Charlotte, NC — On Monday, February 2nd, Democrat Rev.
Dr.
Rodney Sadler announced his recent endorsement by three major organizations representing Black and working-class people across Charlotte: the Black Political Caucus, SEIU 32BJ, and Workers United.
“As we enter Black History Month, we recall the powerful role of the Black freedom movement and the multiracial labor movement in all our past struggles for freedom and dignity,” said Rev.
Dr.
Sadler.
“From the 1870’s, when our state’s first Black elected representatives enshrined the right to vote and public education into our NC constitution, to Rev.
Dr.
Martin Luther King standing with striking sanitation workers, we must learn from and build on the struggles of previous generations.” “I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Black Political Caucus and Advance NC, two extraordinary organizations fighting for the rights of Black people in NC; and the endorsements of SEIU 32BJ, Workers United, and UNITE HERE, three unions that bring together thousands of essential workers across race to fight for a state that puts people over profits.” The Black Political Caucus endorsement has long played a decisive role in Charlotte primaries, and is highly influential in House District 106, whose primary electorate is more than 60% Black.
SEIU and UNITE HERE have both decided close primary elections in the past two years.
Last year, SEIU members helped JD Mazuera Arias win election by 37 votes in City Council District 5.
In 2024, UNITE HERE knocked more than 15,000 doors to help educator Rodney Pierce unseat longtime incumbent Michael Wray by 34 votes.
It is rare for all of these organizations to come together in support of the same campaign.
To date, Rev.
Dr.
Sadler has the endorsement of 13 organizations, Governor Josh Stein, and more than 30 local community, faith, and elected leaders.
A full list of endorsements is available at RodneySadler.com/Endorsements Rev.
Dr.
Sadler is running for office on a platform of strong public schools, homes we can afford, wages we can live on, community safety, accessible healthcare, voting rights, and a state government that fights for working families instead of just pointing fingers and blaming scapegoats.
He is challenging longtime incumbent Rep.
Carla Cunningham, who has voted with Republicans almost 85% of the time, and declared on the House floor that “All cultures are not equal.” Rep.
Cunningham’s largest past contributors include Duke Energy and Unitedhealth Group.
More information on Rev.
Dr.
Sadler and his campaign is available on his website, RodneySadler.com.
