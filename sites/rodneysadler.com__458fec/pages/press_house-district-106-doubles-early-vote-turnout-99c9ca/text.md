Rodney Sadler and fourteen-year incumbent Rep.
Carla Cunningham, House District 106 has more than doubled the early vote total from the 2024 primary.
Election Day is Tuesday March 3rd.
In 2024, 2,816 people voted early in the NC House District 106 primary.
So far in the 2026 primary, 5,665 people in NC House District 106 have voted early.
Rev.
Rodney Sadler is the only candidate who is on the HD106 ballot this year who was not on the 2024 ballot.
“When we first launched this campaign,” said Rev.
Rodney Sadler, “we set out not just to win one election, but to build a movement.
By turning out in record numbers, the people of this district are building a movement that chooses love over fear and people over profits.” “Working people in North Carolina are hungry for a different kind of politics,” said Gabriel Gold Hodgkin, Campaign Manager for Rev.
Rodney Sadler.
“As Trump and his billionaire buddies try to lock in permanent power, we must choose how to respond.
Will we fold and collaborate?
Will we promise a return to a status quo that never worked for most people?
Or will we have the courage to fight for a future where working families can thrive?
Rev.
Rodney Sadler is bringing that much-needed prophetic voice, and that is why twice as many people have voted early in this primary.” Rev.
Sadler is running for office on a platform of strong public schools, homes we can afford, wages we can live on, community safety, accessible healthcare, voting rights, and a state government that fights for working families instead of just pointing fingers and blaming scapegoats.
He is challenging longtime incumbent Rep.
Carla Cunningham, who has voted with Republicans almost 85% of the time, and declared on the House floor that “All cultures are not equal.” Rep.
Cunningham’s largest past contributors include Duke Energy and Unitedhealth Group.
More information on Rev.
Dr.
Sadler and his campaign is available on his website, RodneySadler.com.
