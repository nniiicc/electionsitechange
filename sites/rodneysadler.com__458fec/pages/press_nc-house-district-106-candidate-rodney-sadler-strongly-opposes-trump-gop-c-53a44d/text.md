Dr.
Rodney Sadler calls on President Donald Trump and the GOP-controlled Congress to stop the federal government shutdown and restore full food assistance to NC families.
Sadler is a candidate running for NC House District 106 (Mecklenburg County) seat for the March 3, 2025 Democratic Party Primary.
Democratic State House Candidate Rodney Sadler said, “It is outrageous and unconscionable to cut off assistance to our neighbors in need of food and healthcare.
These are necessities that everyone should have a right to.
We have a moral obligation to stop the actions of President Trump and the GOP-controlled Congress that are hurting millions of people unnecessarily.
I will do everything I can to reverse these delays and cuts.” The shutdown will result in delaying payments by the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), which 1.4 million North Carolinians, including nearly 580,000 children, depend on for food.
About 80% of SNAP beneficiaries are working adults.
At least 143,000 residents of Mecklenburg County who rely on SNAP benefits are not receiving them right now.
Mecklenburg County has the most SNAP beneficiaries of any county in the state.
On Friday, thanks to leadership from Attorney General Jeff Jackson and other leaders around the country, federal judges ruled that Trump must use emergency funds to make SNAP payments.
But those payments will come late and will be only half of the food assistance that NC families depend on.
Democrats are fighting to prevent Medicaid cuts and to extend tax credits for the Affordable Care Act (a.k.a.
“Obamacare”) health insurance policies by including them in the continuing resolution.
These tax credits are going to expire this year which will double and even quadruple health insurance costs for millions of people nationwide.
