RODNEY SADLER RAISES $100K December 16, 2025 Charlotte, NC — On Tuesday, December 16th, Democrat Rev.
Dr.
Rodney Sadler announced that his campaign had passed its year-end fundraising goal of $# “This campaign is about making Mecklenburg affordable for working families,” said Rev.
Dr.
Rodney Sadler.
“We are up against big corporate money and extremist Republican donors, who will stop at nothing to protect the status quo.
I am humbled that hundreds of everyday people – including teachers, mechanics, stagehands, baristas, and retirees – have chipped in to build this movement to put the people first.” In addition to this significant fundraising benchmark, Rev.
Dr.
Sadler has earned an early endorsement from UNITE HERE Local 23, the hospitality workers’ union, along with endorsements of more than twenty local community leaders.
More than seventy supporters joined him to kick off his campaign on December 1st.
With this groundswell of grassroots support and fundraising, Rev.
Dr.
Sadler has rapidly built the only viable campaign to unseat twelve-year incumbent Representative Carla Cunningham in the March 3rd primary.
Successful primary challenges in the NC General Assembly are rare, but not unprecedented.
Examples include Rep.
Rodney Pierce, who unseated Rep.
Michael Wray in 2024, and Sen.
Val Applewhite, who successfully unseated Sen.
Kirk deViere in 2022 – despite a crowded field – in part thanks to impressive fundraising and an endorsement from Gov.
Roy Cooper.
Rep.
Cunningham has voted with Republicans more frequently than any other Democrat in the NC House and declared on the House floor that “All cultures are not equal.” Rep.
Cunningham’s largest past contributors include Duke Energy and Unitedhealth Group.
Rev.
Dr.
Sadler is running for office on a platform of strong public schools, homes we can afford, wages we can live on, community safety, accessible healthcare, voting rights, and a state government that fights for working families instead of just pointing fingers and blaming scapegoats, and lining the pockets of the wealthy few.

Dr.
