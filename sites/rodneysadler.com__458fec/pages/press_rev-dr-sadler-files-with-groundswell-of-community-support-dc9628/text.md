Charlotte, NC — On Monday, December 1st, crowds of community supporters joined Democrat Rev.
Dr.
Rodney Sadler to file and kick off his primary campaign for NC House District 106.
With twenty supporters in purple shirts cheering and escorting him into the Mecklenburg County Board of Elections, Rev.
Dr.
Sadler drew the largest crowd of any candidate filing that day.
More than seventy community members joined that evening for a lively Campaign Kickoff Party.
In less than three months since announcing his candidacy, Rev.
Dr.
Sadler has established himself as the candidate with the values, platform, and campaign that it will take to unseat Carla Cunningham in the March 3rd Democratic Primary.
As of the end of the third day of filing, he remains the only candidate to have filed for NC House District 106.
Rev.
Dr.
Sadler is running for office on a platform of strong public schools, homes we can afford, wages we can live on, community safety, accessible healthcare, voting rights, and a state government that fights for working families instead of just pointing fingers and blaming scapegoats, and lining the pockets of the wealthy few.
He is challenging twelve-year incumbent Representative Carla Cunningham, who has voted with Republicans more frequently than any other Democrat in the NC House, and who declared on the House floor that “All cultures are not equal.” Rep.
Cunningham’s largest past contributors include Duke Energy and Unitedhealth Group.
More information on Rev.
Dr.
Sadler and his campaign is available on his website, www.RodneySadler.com.
