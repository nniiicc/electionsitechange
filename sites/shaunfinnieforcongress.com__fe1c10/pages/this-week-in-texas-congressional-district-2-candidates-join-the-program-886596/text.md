This Week in Texas we focus on Congressional District 2–which covers parts of Montgomery and northern Harris Counties.
We speak with both candidates; Democrat Shaun Finnie and Republican Steve Toth, who beat incumbent Dan Crenshaw in his hotly contested primary.
Now they head the general election, and our political insiders Angel Carroll and Chris Beavers offer their insight into that race and to the lingering partial government shutdown.
Plus, a report from Pooja Lodhia about the state’s new hemp law.
