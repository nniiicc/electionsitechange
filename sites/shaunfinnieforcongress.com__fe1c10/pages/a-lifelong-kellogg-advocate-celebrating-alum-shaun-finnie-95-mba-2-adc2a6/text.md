No one embodies the Kellogg spirit like Shaun Finnie ’95 MBA, who wore a purple shirt and purple socks to his Wade Fetzer Award interview.
This award recognizes outstanding dedication in support of the Kellogg alumni community and celebrates Shaun for his unwavering loyalty.
As a self-described “poster boy” for Kellogg, he has inspired countless students and alumni across Kellogg and Northwestern.
