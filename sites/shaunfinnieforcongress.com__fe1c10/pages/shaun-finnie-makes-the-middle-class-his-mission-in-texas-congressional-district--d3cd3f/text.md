The former investment banker centers his campaign on rebuilding the middle class, lowering healthcare and housing costs, strengthening public education, supporting childcare, and creating an economy in which working people can prosper alongside responsible businesses.
Rebuild the middle class: Finnie says the strength of a nation depends on the size and economic security of its middle class.
Confront the affordability crisis: He prioritizes wages, groceries, tariffs, housing, childcare, education, healthcare, and retirement security.
Expand healthcare choices: He supports restoring enhanced Affordable Care Act assistance and establishing a public insurance option to compete with private insurers.
Invest in opportunity: He advocates universal prekindergarten, affordable childcare, public schools, trade programs, community colleges, and accessible universities.
Replace extremism with representation: Finnie contrasts his economic agenda with Steve Toth’s emphasis on hard-right social politics and says voters want practical, bipartisan leadership.
Finnie makes a persuasive common sense argument without treating economic growth and social responsibility as opposing forces.
A healthy economy needs businesses that can succeed, workers who earn enough to participate, and public institutions that give every family a genuine opportunity to thrive.
