Shaun Finnie told the Kingwood Area Democrats’ Fourth of July gathering that he entered politics because he could no longer watch Congress surrender its independence and operate as a rubber stamp for presidential power.
Drawing on three decades in investment banking, Finnie presented himself as a pragmatic Democrat focused on household economics, affordable healthcare, public education, reproductive freedom, and bipartisan problem-solving.
He contrasted those priorities with Republican nominee Steve Toth’s fixation on ideological and cultural conflicts that neither lower costs nor improve daily life.
- Finnie said dissatisfaction with the government compelled him to act rather than merely complain.
- He pledged to represent the entire district and work with Republicans, Democrats, and independents.
- He centered affordability, healthcare, public schools, economic stability, and women’s freedom.
- He characterized Toth as a radical culture warrior who substitutes social division for practical solutions.
- He argued that TX-02 can become competitive by mobilizing Democrats while winning independents and traditional Republicans tired of political extremism.
This campaign should become a referendum on whether TX-02 wants another representative manufacturing enemies or a public servant prepared to confront the problems affecting every family.
Finnie offers Republicans, independents, and Democrats a credible off-ramp from extremism and congressional dysfunction.
