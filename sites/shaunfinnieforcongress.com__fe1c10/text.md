Shaun Finnie for Congress
Candidate Statement
“Every Texan deserves a responsible government that supports growth, not impairs our success.
As a father, a businessman, and a taxpayer, I understand the frustrations that our families are facing - a national debt growing out of control, the cost of living becoming unaffordable, and Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid being put on the chopping block.
Once elected, I’ll apply the same lessons I’ve learned throughout my business career to government - never spend more than what we can afford, ensure everyone has an equal opportunity to support their families and succeed, and never jeopardize essential benefits that matter most.
That means protecting Social Security, Medicare, and Medicaid benefits for all middle-class Americans.”
Shaun Finnie
