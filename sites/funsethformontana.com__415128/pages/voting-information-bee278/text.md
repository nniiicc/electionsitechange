Voting Information
House District 21
Trevor Funseth is running for the Montana House of Representatives in District 21.
The Montana Legislature convenes every odd year for a period of 90 days.
Trevor's term will be served in 2027.
HD 21 includes Black Eagle and the Great Falls neighborhoods of Valley View, Riverview, and Skyline.
How to Vote
You can register to vote any time of year, Monday through Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm at the Cascade County Elections Office, 325 2nd Avenue N.
Montana allows absentee voting (vote by mail).
You can check your voter registration status by searching for yourself on the Secretary of State webpage.
Primary Election: June 2, 2026
General Election: November 3, 2026
