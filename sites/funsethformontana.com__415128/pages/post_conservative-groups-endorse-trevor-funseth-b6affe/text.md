Conservative Groups Endorse Trevor Funseth
Updated: May 11
Just about two months out from election day, conservative organizations have started endorsing candidates.
So far, I am honored to announce that every major conservative group endorsement for Montana House District 21 has gone to me, and none have been announced in favor of my opponents.
I have been endorsed by:
- Montana GOP
- Americans for Prosperity
- Make Liberty Win
- Young Americans for Liberty
- Montanans for Limited Government
- Republicans for Freedom
- Cascade County Republicans PAC
- School Freedom Fund
- Montana Chiropractic Council
These endorsements are not arbitrary.
When there is an incumbent in the race, organizations rate the incumbent based on their voting record.
In a race like this one, with no incumbent, organizations will interview candidates and ask them about their stances on a wide range of policies.
By giving me their endorsement, these groups are confident that, after interviewing every candidate, they believe I am the most capable of advancing conservative principles of limited government, low taxation, individual liberty, and family values.
I am truly honored to have their support and thank them for their tireless and often thankless work year-round defending our values.
