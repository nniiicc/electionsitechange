DONATE Meet Trevor Trevor Funseth grew up in HD 21 and attended Sacajawea Elementary, North Middle School, and CMR.
He is now an attorney specializing in estate planning, business matters, and veterans law.
Trevor is grateful to have deep roots in Great Falls, and can often be found with his friends and family golfing at Hickory Swing, eating at Borrie's, or playing Bingo at the Halftime.
Trevor served on the Great Falls Public School Board.
He has degrees from both Montana State University and the University of Montana, though his first loyalty is to the Bobcats.
Legal Experience As an attorney, Trevor specializes in issues impacting our seniors, local businesses, and veterans.
He helps his clients save on taxes, start businesses, and complete their estate planning.
An expert in his field, Trevor presents educational courses for other attorneys in his practice area.
A legislator needs to understand the law, and Trevor has a deep understanding of the Montana Code.
He is highly qualified in tax law, business regulation, and property matters.
Montanan Trevor grew up in a family of fishermen, hunters, and campers.
His happy place is the Little Belt Mountains.
He is a member of the Electric City Pickleball Club and, most importantly, serves as the Head of North Pole Security for Santa's Sleigh during the annual Parade of Lights.
Trevor's other hobbies include distance running and playing Johnny Cash on his guitar.
He has a ginger cat named Ludacris.
