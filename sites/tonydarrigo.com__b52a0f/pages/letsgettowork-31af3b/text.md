Solving Florida’s Housing Crisis Florida is facing an unprecedented housing affordability crisis.
With population growth among the fastest in the nation, our housing supply simply can’t keep up.
Rents have soared—up 36% for a one-bedroom apartment between March 2020 and October 2022.
Florida now ranks highest in the nation for cost-burdened renters.
This crisis wasn’t inevitable—it was the result of policy choices.
By staying fully open during the Covid-19 pandemic, Florida’s economy did enjoy a temporary boom, attracting workers and families from across America.
But that short-term success brought long-term pressure: our housing market was flooded, and now, hyperinflation in our most populated areas is making it harder than ever for everyday Floridians to find a place they can afford.
My promise: I will fight for targeted, effective solutions so that our economic success lifts everyone—not just a lucky few.
My Plan for Florida’s Housing: Increase Affordable Housing Supply: Champion fast-track incentives, zoning reforms, and public-private partnerships to build more affordable homes and apartments—so every Floridian has a real shot at finding a place to live.
Protect Renters: Advocate for tenant safeguards to prevent unjust evictions and excessive rent hikes, without overburdening responsible landlords.
Support First-Time Homebuyers: Expand down payment assistance and create low-interest government-backed mortgages capped at 5%—helping working families own their future.
Invest in Community Infrastructure: Ensure new housing is paired with investments in transportation, schools, and essential services—building stronger, healthier neighborhoods for all.
Fight Speculation and Abuse: Crack down on predatory real estate practices and large corporate landlords who drive up prices at the expense of local residents.
Prioritizing Homeownership, Not Speculation The foundation of the American Dream is owning a home—not just renting from corporate landlords.
To restore balance and create real opportunity: Homeownership as the Priority: Every county will be required to maintain at least a 75% owner-occupancy rate, ensuring most homes are lived in by the families who own them.
Capping Speculation: A maximum of 25% of all residential properties in each county can be held for investment and rental purposes.
Fair Rebalancing: To achieve these levels, investors with the highest number of properties will be required to gradually sell off excess holdings, leveling the playing field for families who want to put down roots.
Putting People Before Profits in Hospitals and Prisons It’s time to de-privatize hospitals and prisons—so real rehabilitation and true care can finally happen.

My promise: I will fight to bring prisons back under public control, with a focus on rehabilitation, education, and true pathways back to society—breaking the cycle of recidivism and restoring justice.
Making Healthcare About Healing, Not Profit Private hospitals charge sky-high fees for basic items and services—not to help people, but to boost bottom lines and please shareholders.
This is why insurance is expensive yet covers less and less for working families.
By de-privatizing hospitals, we can: Lower outrageous healthcare costs Pay doctors and nurses fairly, with room for meaningful raises Ensure insurance programs deliver real, quality care without inflated costs This vision puts people first—delivering the care and second chances every American truly deserves.
