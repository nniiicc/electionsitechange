Real Healthcare Security Every American deserves healthcare they can rely on—and actually afford.
I support a major expansion of Healthcare.gov, partnering with only insurers who honor our commitment.
No one will pay more than 50% of their weekly income for premiums, with full system coverage for all essential care Deductibles/max out-of-pocket capped at 20% of monthly income Employers will cover only deductibles—not full healthcare plans—removing outdated coverage requirements, and freeing up hours and opportunities for workers No more “benefit cliffs”—just real, predictable healthcare security.
Universal Healthcare: Covering Every American, No Exceptions Healthcare isn’t just a privilege—it’s a fundamental right.
Too many families in America still face the fear of getting sick, knowing a single hospital visit could mean bankruptcy.
No one should have to choose between health and financial survival.
My vision for Universal Healthcare: Guaranteed Coverage for All: Every American, no exceptions—regardless of age, job, income, or health history.
Affordable and Predictable Costs: Caps on premiums, low deductibles, and out-of-pocket costs tied to what families can truly afford—not what insurance companies dictate.
No More Gaps or Surprises: All essential treatments, medications, doctor visits, mental health, and prevention are covered.
No more loopholes, exclusions, or hidden bills.
Choice and Quality: Freedom to choose your doctors.
Investment in quality care and innovation—not bureaucracy or corporate shareholder profits.
Security for Working Families: A system where hard work is rewarded, medical crises don’t destroy futures, and children grow up healthy, strong, and ready to achieve their dreams.
I know the difference universal coverage makes—because I’ve lived through medical crises myself.
With universal healthcare, every American will finally have the dignity, freedom, and peace of mind they deserve.
It’s time to make healthcare a right—for everyone Restoring the American Dream—for Every Family Too many Americans feel the American Dream is out of reach.
The high cost of living, unfair opportunities, and daily uncertainty discourage families from building their future.
I believe we can—and must—reignite hope and possibility for every working family.
Affordable, Clean Energy for All We can and should make energy a free, accessible resource.
By investing in wind farms, water turbines, and solar arrays—built on federally owned land and funded by ending oil and fuel industry subsidies—we’ll power our communities sustainably.
Electricity should be for everyone—not a bill families struggle to pay.
Utility companies will maintain our upgraded grid, not profit from it.
Driving Toward a Fair Future It’s time to make electric vehicles practical and affordable.
With federal investment and requirements for automakers, we’ll ensure every family has access to: Electric vehicles for five, with a 400-mile range, lasting 500,000 miles—for under $25K out-the-door A $20,000 direct rebate for qualifying electric vehicles Charging infrastructure and fueling stations, built on public land, with funds previously given as oil subsidies Fair lending means a fair shot at the American Dream.
Let’s ensure every family has an honest path to build, repair, and reach for a better future—without fear of financial ruin.
Fair, Modern Credit for All America’s credit system should empower—not punish—people.
Limit credit reporting to the past 3 years.
Eliminate credit inquiries from reports.
Create a clear, single credit score—no more confusion or hidden formulas.
What you see is what lenders see.
If not, lenders face fines equal to the loan value.
If major credit bureaus won’t lead, a public, government-run system will protect all Americans.
Cracking Down on Auto Industry Abuse Historic inflation and crushing repossessions have roots in broken auto lending and sales practices.
Cap auto loan interest rates at 15%.
Anything above is predatory—and should be criminal.
Launch a Universal Car Value Department: trade-ins are valued fairly, with dealers facing fines for lowball offers.
Every used car must come with a free six-month, bumper-to-bumper warranty to guarantee quality and honesty.
Truth and Transparency in Car Pricing No more sticker shock, no more hidden fees: The price you see is the price you pay, including all fees and taxes—no exceptions, no fine print.
Dealer “add-on” packages must be optional and removable at your request.
Dealers are required to provide clear, complete pricing—both on paper and digitally.
If a dealer fails to honor the advertised price, they pay: you get the car free, and they cover taxes and registration.
Ending Predatory Lending: Protecting Families, Restoring Fairness Too many working Americans are being trapped and torn down by predatory lenders—banks and finance companies that prey on desperation with sky-high interest rates and hidden fees.
This has to end.
My commitment to you: Cap on Interest Rates: No loan—whether it’s for a car, a home, or emergency expenses—should come with absurd, life-ruining rates.
I will fight to cap all consumer loan rates at fair, affordable levels, ending the days of 15%, 25%, or higher charges.
No More Fine Print Traps: Lenders must be required by law to present all terms clearly and simply—no more hidden fees, surprise penalties, or confusing contracts.
Protecting the Most Vulnerable: We’ll prohibit abusive payday lending, title loans, and other practices that target people in crisis.
All Americans deserve safe, honest, transparent credit when they need it.
Accountability for Abusive Lenders: Lenders who violate these standards will face tough penalties and lose the right to operate—because ripping off working families should be criminal, not business as usual.
A Candidate Who Truly Understands Working Americans I know what it’s like to live paycheck to paycheck—because I’m still doing it.
Like 62% of Americans, I face the stress of unexpected bills, medical emergencies, and the sharp rise in everyday costs like summer electricity.
That’s why I’m running for Congress.
I’m committed to fighting inflation responsibly, restoring real affordability, and making sure America’s prosperity reaches working families—not just the wealthy or well-connected.
Every hardworking American deserves stability, comfort, and the freedom to plan for their future.
Today, our challenges are real: homelessness is at crisis levels and inflation is at an all-time high.
But I have both the practical experience and the determination to take action.
I’m ready to tackle these issues head-on—so that real people, not special interests, benefit from the progress we create.
Real Service—For the American People I refuse to accept the status quo when I know it can change.
With your support, we’ll restore sanity and normalcy to Congress.
I am committed to ending the gridlock, setting aside personal agendas, and working tirelessly for the people who truly matter—our constituents.
Our leaders were elected to serve us—not themselves.
That’s my pledge: No vendettas.
No grudges.
Just results.
And here’s my promise to working families: I will introduce a bill to eliminate federal income tax for every household earning under $75,000 per year.
Because real relief starts with letting you keep what you earn.
Putting Children and Families First Free Meals for Every Student No child should have to learn on an empty stomach.
Every student will receive free breakfast and lunch at school—no cost, no stigma, no paperwork for families.
Healthcare for Every Child Every child deserves a healthy start in life.
I will fight to guarantee free healthcare for every child in America, from birth through age 18—because parents shouldn’t have to worry about a bill when their child needs care.
Overhaul of Child Protective Services will be in the plans for the best of the child.
Fair Homeownership for All The dream of homeownership shouldn’t be out of reach for the average American.
If banks won’t change their rigid criteria to open doors for working families, I will push for the United States Government to become the lender—offering fair, rock-bottom mortgage rates.
Government-backed home loans will never exceed 5% interest.
Interest paid will be reinvested—funding programs that help citizens, not lining the pockets of private banks.
It’s time for a housing market that serves people, not just investors.
True Conservative Values—Rooted in the Constitution As a former real Republican, I am a Constitution purist—grounded in America’s founding principles and true conservative values.
I believe in limited government over the lives of everyday citizens.
Regulation should exist to keep Corporate America in check and to ensure law and order, not to micromanage the people.
Government’s job is to aid, assist, and empower every American in their pursuit of prosperity and happiness—not to control how we live our lives.
My commitment is to restore government to its true purpose: serving the people, not wielding power over them.
Unlike today’s so-called “Republicans,” who seek power for themselves by misleading the public, I stand for genuine constitutional conservatism and honest, transparent leadership.
Every Decision, Vote, and Bill Will Be for the Benefit of All of my Constituents And All Americans I will Fight For OUR Entitlements.
(Medicare, Medicaid, and Social Security) Republicans want to defund and eliminate these programs that almost 200,000 People in My Florida District 13 Rely on for their basic needs and survival.
I WILL FIGHT TO INCREASE SOCIAL SECURITY and ENTITLEMENTS.
MY ULTIMATE GOAL is to LOWER the RETIREMENT AGE so we have more time to ACTUALLY ENJOY OUR RETIREMENT that we earned!
A Campaign Free from Dark Money and Harmful Lobbyists Our democracy belongs to the people—not to billionaires, corporations, or shadowy special interests.
Too many politicians take “dark money” and answer to lobbyists who put profits and power above the needs of ordinary Americans.
I will not be one of them.
My pledge to you: No Dark Money: I refuse all untraceable or undisclosed donations—my campaign will be #% transparent.
Every dollar I accept will be from sources that can be tracked, traced, and held accountable.
No Harmful Lobbyist Influence: I will not accept contributions from lobbyists or organizations that work against the health, safety, and prosperity of our people—whether it’s big oil, big pharma, predatory lenders, or any group putting profit over public good.
For the People, Not for Sale: I work for you—my constituents, your families, your future.
Every decision I make, every vote I cast, will be for the public interest, not for special interests.
You deserve leadership that can never be bought or bullied.
My campaign is powered by people, by honesty, and by hope.
Together, we can restore integrity to our government.
