On the Issues
As a Minnesota resident, you deserve a government that works as hard as you do.
William Beck is committed to protecting your taxpayer dollars, lowering your expenses, strengthening neighborhoods, and ensuring every child in your community has the opportunity to succeed.
His priorities are simple: Accountability, Affordability, Academic Excellence, and focusing on a brighter future for Minnesotans.
Affordability
You're feeling the continued squeeze from rising costs, higher taxes, and policies making everyday life more expensive.
It’s time to put working people first and bring some common sense back to the budget.
• Cut the cost of living by reining in excessive fees and mandates that relentlessly raise prices for working families and individuals.
• Protect your paycheck by fighting tax hikes and pushing targeted tax relief on essentials like childcare and everyday necessities.
• Stop taxing what you’ve earned by ending the tax on Social Security benefits and putting a moratorium on property tax increases.
• Restrain the growth of government bureaucracy to help lower your household costs and put more dollars in your pocket.
Accountability (Fraud)
As a Minnesotan, you deserve a government you can trust.
You deserve a government that safeguards taxpayer dollars instead of letting them slip into the hands of scammers and insiders.
You want accountability, transparency, and real consequences for fraudsters.
• Strengthen oversight and transparency to ensure state programs produce results and stay accountable.
• Crack down on waste and abuse so every tax dollar supports real people, not scammers or political insiders.
• Restore trust in government by enforcing tough auditing and stopping the kind of fraud that’s burned Minnesota before.
• End the revolving door by stopping politicians and top officials from cashing out into the very industries they are supposed to regulate.
Education
Every child in your community deserves a quality education, and every parent deserves a say in how their kids are taught.
High quality education and improved test scores benefit our entire state.
• Support teachers and staff by investing in the classroom, not bureaucracy, so educators can focus on students.
• Improve classroom discipline so all students can learn, free from distractions.
• Improve vocational opportunities by promoting career, technical, and life-skill programs, preparing Minnesota kids for real-world success.
• Protect women’s sports by ensuring girls and women compete on a level playing field.
Girls and women deserve fair athletic competition and safety in public and private spaces.
Data Centers
As Big Tech expands across Minnesota, we need policies to protect taxpayers, workers, and local communities - not special deals that leave you paying the tab.
AI poses unique opportunities, but also has significant concerns as usage continues to grow.
• Support responsible development that helps communities grow without leaving taxpayers on the hook for increased costs.
• Ban NDAs in data-center negotiations so local officials can’t hide project details from the people they serve.
• Keep core government services staffed by people - not outsourced to unaccountable or unreliable technology or Big Tech firms.
Environment & Natural Resources
Minnesota’s natural resources are central to our way of life.
We can protect what we love while still supporting good jobs and strong communities.
• Back responsible mining to create good-paying local jobs and fueling community growth, while enforcing strict safety standards and protecting Minnesota’s environment.
• Get clean water flowing by navigating the grant process to secure Hastings and Cottage Grove the funds they need for modern water treatment.
• Grow smart, protect what we love by encouraging thoughtful development that doesn’t steal from our natural beauty or our children’s future.
Healthcare
Healthcare should be affordable, accessible, and centered on you, the patient - not bureaucracies or insurance red tape.
Minnesotans deserve real choices and real control over their care.
• Lower costs through choice and competition, accessing care you can trust without breaking the bank.
• Empower Minnesotans with HSAs and direct primary care, giving you more control over your everyday health needs.
• Require hospitals, clinics, and insurers to clearly disclose prices up front, so you know the real cost of care before you walk in the door.
• Allow more local clinics and providers to open their doors, increasing healthcare access in underserved areas.
