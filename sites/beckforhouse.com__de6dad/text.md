A Leader Who Puts Minnesota Families First
William Beck is a husband, father of two, and lifelong Minnesotan.
He and his wife, Mya—who serves on the Hastings City Council—are raising their family with the same hopes shared by millions across our state: affordable living, safe communities, and a future where the next generation can thrive.
A committed grassroots leader, William has served Minnesota in roles that reflect his values of service, integrity, and community:
- Charter School Board Director
- Church Youth Leader
- Disability Advocate
- Hastings HPC Member
William has earned a reputation as a fierce but pragmatic advocate—someone who listens, leads, and fights for working families rather than special interests.
Now, he's running for the Minnesota House 41B seat to revive the American Dream in Minnesota
