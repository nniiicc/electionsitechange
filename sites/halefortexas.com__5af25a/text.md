Meet Alexander Hale
Alexander Hale is running to bring principled, constitutional, and robust leadership to Texas's 7th Congressional District.
Alexander is a devout Christian who believes in limited federal government, market-based solutions, and the principles that have made America exceptional.
He's committed to defending Houston's energy economy while addressing the real issues facing TX-07 families: affordability issues, rising healthcare costs, and public safety.
