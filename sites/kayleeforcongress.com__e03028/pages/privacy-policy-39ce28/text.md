PRIVACY POLICY
Last Updated: May 27, 2026
Kaylee for Congress (“the Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting the privacy of our supporters, volunteers, and website visitors.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, sign up to volunteer, or make a contribution.
1.
INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including but not limited to:
- Personal Identifiers: Name, mailing address, email address, and phone number.
- Financial Data: When you make a contribution, payment processing is handled securely by ActBlue.
We receive transaction details (such as amount and date) and federal compliance information (employer and occupation), but we do not store your credit card numbers.
- Volunteer & Engagement Data: Areas of interest, volunteer availability, and messages submitted through our contact forms.
2.
HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We use the information we collect to:
- Connect you with grassroots volunteer opportunities and campaign updates.
- Process and verify contributions in accordance with Federal Election Commission (FEC) regulations.
- Optimize our digital advertisements and website performance.
- Send campaign-related text messages and emails (you may opt out at any time).
3.
PHONE NUMBERS & TEXT MESSAGING (SMS) Compliance
The Campaign deeply values your communication privacy.
- No mobile information or phone number data will be shared, sold, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
- All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
- If you receive a text message from the campaign, you can reply "STOP" at any time to opt out of future messaging.
4.
THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURES
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with trusted vendor partners and service providers performing services on our behalf (such as data analytics, email delivery, or compliance tracking) under strict confidentiality agreements.
We may also disclose information if required to do so by law or FEC guidelines.
5.
COOKIES & TRACKING CODES
Our website utilizes tracking cookies, Meta Pixels, and Google Analytics to evaluate user engagement and deliver relevant digital advertisements.
You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though some features of the site may function differently.
6.
CONTACT US
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: info@kayleeforcongress.com.
