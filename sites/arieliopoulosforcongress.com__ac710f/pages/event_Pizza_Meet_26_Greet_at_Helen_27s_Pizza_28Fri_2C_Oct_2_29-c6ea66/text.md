A fresh voice for New Jersey CD 8 Candidate for New Jersey's 8th Congressional District A little about me I was born and raised in Jersey City, where my parents worked tirelessly as local small business owners to build a better life for our family.
As the son of immigrants, I grew up watching their sacrifices, determination, and commitment to serving our community.
Those lessons shaped my understanding of hard work, responsibility, and the importance of giving back.
"I'm running for Congress because our community deserves a representative who actually shows up, listens, and delivers — not one who plays politics while families struggle." I was fortunate to be raised in a vibrant downtown neighborhood community that felt like an extended family.
Through local traditions and lifelong friendships, I learned the value of service, respect for others, and pride in where I come from.
My Greek heritage especially instilled in me a deep appreciation for resilience, perseverance, and the importance of preserving our history and identity for future generations in our society.
Just as important was my upbringing in the Greek Orthodox Church, where regular attendance and involvement in parish life helped shape my character and values.
The faith, compassion, and sense of community I experienced there continue to guide me today as a husband, father, educator, union representative, and community advocate.
The lessons I learned from my family, my faith, and my heritage remain at the heart of who I am and why I am committed to serving others — and most importantly, doing the right thing.
