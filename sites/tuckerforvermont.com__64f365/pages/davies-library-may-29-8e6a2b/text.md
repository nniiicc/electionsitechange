Campaign Launch: David Tucker for the Vermont House
Please join David Tucker and his team at the official campaign launch of Tucker for Vermont.
- When: Friday, May 29th | 5:00 PM – 7:00 PM
- Where: Davies Memorial Library, 532 Maple St., Waterford, VT
David is running for the House seat for Caledonia-1 District (Barnet, Ryegate, and Waterford).
This is a district-wide launch, and we look forward to getting together with neighbors from all three towns!
Join David and his team, enjoy delicious hors d’oeuvres, and learn more about the campaign.
David will share his plans and perspectives on issues that matter to the people of our towns.
There will also be opportunities to learn how you can get involved and join the campaign team.
Community Support: We will be collecting non-perishable food items during the event for donation to the Waterford Community Cupboard.
Bring a friend—we look forward to seeing you there!
Please RSVP to info@tuckerforvermont.com
