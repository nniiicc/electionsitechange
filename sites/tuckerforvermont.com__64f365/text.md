He’s been standing for the Northeast Kingdom, talking back to the “folks from Burlington and Montpelier who think they know better,” for most of his life.
I’m David Tucker and I’m running for the Vermont legislature.
In the Northeast Kingdom, when a storm knocks a tree down across the road, folks don’t ask whose job it is to clear the road — they show up with their chainsaws in hand.
That’s how Vermonters work: together, quietly, no spotlight, no headlines.
We believe schools should teach our children to think clearly, work hard, and stand tall – whether they’re working as a mechanic or heading to college.
We believe the beauty of our forests and rivers belongs both to the postcard AND to the people who live right by them.
Protecting our land should never mean taking away our freedom to build a life on it.
We believe help should be there when life gets tough – whether that’s illness, loss of a job, mental health difficulties, substance recovery, or a family in crisis.
Everyone deserves a fair shot to get back on their feet.
And we believe the Kingdom should stay a place where our children and our grandchildren can live, work, and raise their families, without leaving the hills they love.
The people of the Northeast Kingdom don’t wait for permission to make things better.
We look out for each other.
We do what needs doing, whatever it is.
Let’s get to work.
