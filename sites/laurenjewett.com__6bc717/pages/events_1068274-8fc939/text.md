Tammany, Washington, and Tangipahoa parishes!
Come whenever you’re available between 8:30 AM and 6 PM.
Whether you have 20 minutes or a couple of hours, bring your signs, a friend, and your enthusiasm!
This is a community-wide invitation, not a staffed event at every site.
There may not be other supporters at each location.
Sign up so we can help connect people planning to attend the same area.
EARLY VOTING SITES FOR REFERENCE: 📍 ST.
TAMMANY • Slidell: The Towers Building, 520 Old Spanish Trail. • Covington: Justice Center Parking Garage, 601 N.
Jefferson Ave. • Mandeville: Administrative Complex, 21490 Koop Drive, Building A. 📍 WASHINGTON • Franklinton: Washington Parish Courthouse, 900 Washington St. • Bogalusa: Northshore Technical Community College, 1710 Sullivan Drive. 📍 TANGIPAHOA • Amite: Registrar of Voters, 110 N.
First St. • Hammond: Parish Governmental Building, 15475 Club Deluxe Road.
IMPORTANT: These addresses identify voting sites, not sign-waving meeting points.
All sign waving must take place outside the 600-foot campaign-free zone measured from the polling-place entrance.
Keep sidewalks and driveways clear.
Put away campaign signs and apparel before entering the zone to vote. ☀️ Bring water and sunscreen, wear comfortable clothes and walking shoes, and grab a hat or sunglasses! 🗳️ Vote early, life happens, and plans change.
Don’t wait until Election Day to make your voice heard!
