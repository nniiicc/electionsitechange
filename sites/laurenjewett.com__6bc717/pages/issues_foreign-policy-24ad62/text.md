Background
As a special education teacher, Lauren’s daily work is rooted in a simple truth: you cannot build a successful future without first creating a safe, supportive foundation for everyone.
When we look at foreign policy, the same logic applies.
True national security is not just about military strength.
It is about fostering global stability through diplomacy such as investments in human infrastructure, specifically education, healthcare, and sustainable development.
Just as a chaotic environment hinders a student's ability to learn, global instability, poverty, hunger, and systemic inequality threaten our safety at home.
Moreover, Lauren views issues of war through a disability justice lens, and sees how international conflict can deepen harm and trauma for disabled individuals.
As a country, we must lead in protecting civilians from disasters of war, ethnic and religious hatred, and human rights abuses and aim to engage in international relations that prioritizes diplomacy and everyone’s human rights.
