It is not just about owning a firearm, but about personal responsibility, self-reliance, and the freedom to protect what matters most.
It’s a right worth preserving, not because we glorify guns, but because we value the principles behind them: safety, liberty, and trust in the individual. ​ The right to keep and bear arms is part of our country’s long-standing tradition of personal responsibility. ​ It’s not about fear, it’s about the peace of mind that comes with being able to protect your family. ​ I support lawful access to firearms, along with strong, common-sense training and safety practices .
This right should be protected for law-abiding citizens, especially in rural and remote communities where help isn’t always nearby. ​ ​ And it’s worth having honest, respectful conversations about how to uphold this right while keeping everyone safe.
