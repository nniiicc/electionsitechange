Personal Exemption
Q: Does every individual taxpayer get the $100,000 exemption?
A: Yes.
The framework applies equally to every single American worker, without
exception:
Universal Baseline: The first $100,000 of your individual income is completely
tax-exempt.
Massive Middle-Class Relief: Wipes out 100% of federal income tax liabilities for
65% to 70% of American households.
No Marriage Penalty: If both spouses work, each individual gets their own
$100,000 exemption.
A married couple pays $0 in federal income tax on their
first $200,000 of combined income.
Flat Rate Above the Line: You only pay a flat 20% on the money you earn above
your personal $100,000 baseline.
