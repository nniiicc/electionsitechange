Laura Andreson is a physician, mother, and advocate in Williamson County, TN.
With over two decades of experience as an OB/GYN, she has delivered thousands of babies and supported countless families through the years, gaining a deep understanding of the challenges facing Tennessee families and a strong connection to her community.
Laura is stepping up to lead with the same compassion and commitment she’s shown in her practice - because Tennessee families deserve better, and she’s ready to fight for them.
Tennesseans Deserve Good Healthcare.
Laura Andreson believes that decisions about healthcare should be made by patients and their doctors - not politicians.
She will fight to protect access to affordable healthcare, defend reproductive freedom, and expand mental health services so families can get the care they need.
Laura supports strengthening public health systems, lowering prescription drug costs, and ensuring that rural and underserved communities are never left behind.
Tennessee Children Deserve Support.
Laura believes every child deserves to feel safe and supported.
She is committed to fully funding public education, reducing class sizes, and ensuring schools have the resources they need to support students’ mental and emotional well-being.
Laura will stand up to vouchers, gun violence, and discrimination, and work with educators and parents to create learning environments where every child can thrive.
Tennesseans Deserve Dignity.
Laura believes every neighbor deserves dignity and opportunity, no matter who they are, where they come from, or whom they love.
She will fight for fair wages, affordable housing, and strong worker protections so everyone can build a stable life.
Laura is committed to protecting civil rights, supporting LGBTQ+ communities, standing with immigrants, and ensuring that our laws reflect compassion, fairness, and respect.
