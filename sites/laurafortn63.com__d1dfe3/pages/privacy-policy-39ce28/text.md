Laura Andreson for TN63 ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy.

By using our site, you consent to the practices described here.
1.
Information We Collect
We collect personal data in the following ways:
- When you contact us or sign up: This includes your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, or other details you voluntarily provide through forms (e.g., volunteer sign-ups, contact forms, petitions, event RSVPs, merchandise purchases), including if you opt in to receive text message (SMS) notifications.

This information is submitted to the FEC and may be made publicly available.
- Automatically via analytics and advertising tools We use Google Analytics and advertising platforms to collect non-personal usage data, such as:
