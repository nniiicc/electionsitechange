Fighting For Affordability
Shelby knows that our families are feeling the squeeze of higher prices.
While the causes often come from outside the state capital, Shelby is working every day to deliver lower costs.
Shelby has cut taxes for working families, delivered record funding for our public schools, and cut taxes and regulations for small businesses so they can lower prices and invest in workers.
Working Across the Aisle To Lower Energy Prices
Shelby helped lead the fight to repeal the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative (RGGI) because it was a large tax increase on the middle class.
Shelby worked across the aisle to end the scheme, which outsourced our energy policy to other states and imposed significant costs on ratepayers.
Instead, Shelby is working to increase energy production and drive down energy prices, saving consumers money.
Record Funding For Our Schools
Shelby worked with both sides to pass record funding for our schools.
Not only does our budget invest in students at never before seen levels, it also sends more money to local school districts.
Coupled with the largest property tax relief in two decades, Shelby has been working to protect local taxpayers and save our families money.
