For decades, Bill listened to families, solved real problems, and showed up when people needed help.
That same approach guides his priorities as a candidate for State Representative.
These are the issues Bill is focused on—because they’re the ones families talk about every day.
Safe Neighborhoods Everyone deserves to feel safe where they live.
Bill believes safe neighborhoods are built through strong communities, accountability, and common-sense policies that protect residents while respecting fairness and dignity.
Families should feel confident letting their kids play outside, seniors should feel secure in their homes, and neighbors should look out for one another.
Bill supports efforts that strengthen public safety, support first responders, and address problems before they escalate—so our neighborhoods remain places people are proud to call home.
STrong Public Schools Public schools are the foundation of opportunity.
With more than 35 years spent in Northeast Philadelphia schools, Bill understands what works—and what doesn’t.
He believes students succeed when schools are safe, supported, and focused on learning, and when parents are treated as true partners in their children’s education.
Bill will advocate for policies that support educators, strengthen classrooms, and ensure every child has the chance to grow and thrive—no matter their background.
Affordable Living Families shouldn’t be priced out of the communities they love.
Rising costs are putting pressure on working families and seniors alike.
From housing to everyday expenses, too many people are being stretched thin just trying to get by.
Bill believes government should help ease that burden by supporting policies that keep communities affordable, help families stay in their homes, and protect seniors living on fixed incomes.
ACCESS TO QUALITY HEALTHCARE Quality healthcare should be affordable and within reach for every family.
Too many working families and seniors are struggling with rising healthcare costs, high insurance premiums, and prescription drug prices that continue to climb.
No one should have to choose between seeing a doctor and paying their bills.
Bill will advocate for policies that expand access to affordable care, protect coverage for those who need it most, lower prescription drug costs, and ensure seniors and working families can see their doctors without financial hardship.
Support for Seniors & Working Families Our community should take care of the people who take care of it.
Seniors deserve stability, dignity, and access to the services they rely on.
Working families deserve policies that recognize how hard they work and how much they contribute.
Bill will fight for practical solutions that support caregivers, protect seniors, and ensure families have access to reliable resources and services—without unnecessary red tape.
Practical Leadership & Real Solutions Bill Griffin isn’t interested in political games or extreme positions.
He believes in listening first, working across differences, and focusing on solutions that make everyday life better.
As State Representative, Bill will put people before politics and work every day to strengthen neighborhoods, support families, and protect the future of Northeast Philadelphia.
If you have an issue that matters to you, Bill wants to hear from you.
