The cameras are installed in locations throughout the valley, but Nevada currently has no state law governing the use of Flock cameras by law enforcement or private security.
Late last month, the Nevada Legislature’s Interim Judiciary Committee voted unanimously to have staff draft a bill to regulate the cameras for next year’s legislative session.
A few days later, the Nevada Latino Legislative Caucus called for law enforcement to stop using the cameras until legal safeguards are in place.
