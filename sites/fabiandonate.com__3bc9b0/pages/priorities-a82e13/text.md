PRIORITIES
Investing in Nevadans
Protecting Our Livelihoods
Nevada's future depends on whether we invest in our people, our workers, and our ability to compete in the 21st-century economy.
Investing in Nevadans: Building a 21st-Century Economy
Diversify Nevada's economy, attract good-paying jobs, and make sure Nevadans — not out-of-state corporations — benefit from our growth.
- Diversify Nevada's economy beyond a single sector.
- Bring manufacturing and industrial jobs back to our state.
- Invest in industries of the future — not yesterday's economy.
- Compete aggressively with other states for jobs and innovation.
- Make sure public investments deliver real returns for Nevada families.
Economic development should be about jobs, wages, and opportunity — not giveaways or corporate favoritism.
Defending Jobs in the Age of Automation & Big Tech
The next wave of economic disruption is already here.
We must act before automation replaces the livelihoods of Las Vegas workers.
- Prepare workers before jobs are automated out of existence.
- Invest in training tied directly to real, local jobs.
- Keep automated and high-tech jobs rooted in Nevada.
- Hold big corporations accountable when innovation comes at workers' expense.
The future should work for Nevadans, not against them.
Affordable Health Care - Without Taking More From Your Paycheck
Large hospitals rake in profits while Nevadans are left with growing expenses and less in their bank accounts.
That has to change.
- Lower costs through mandated price transparency.
- Protect patient privacy and the right to choose your own provider.
- Protect paychecks from unnecessary fees and deductions.
- A system that works for patients, not health care monopolies.
Nevadans work hard for every dollar.
Our health care system shouldn't make it harder to get ahead.
Education That Prepares Students for Real Careers
Education should open doors, not hold students back.
We need pathways that lead directly to good-paying jobs.
- Aggressively expand public school choice through magnet and CTE programs.
- Connect graduation requirements directly to workforce needs.
- Invest in skills that lead to good-paying jobs.
- Give families more pathways within the public education system.
When education works for our students, the economy follows.
A Fair Shot for Small Businesses & Working Families
Nevada works best when the small guy can compete.
Cut the red tape and level the playing field against corporate giants.
- Cut unnecessary red tape and standardize permitting statewide.
- Reduce registration and maintenance costs for small employers.
- Expand investments to small businesses and give them a fair shot at state projects.
A strong economy isn't built from the top down — it's built from the ground up on the backs of the working class.
Housing That Working People Can Actually Afford
Working families are paying the price for government inaction and red tape that blocks housing and drives up rents.
- Hold cities accountable when they fail to address homelessness or block housing.
- Cut red tape that slows down construction and drives up costs.
- Ban junk fees and hidden charges that inflate rents.
- Incentivize ADU construction to increase supply in our neighborhoods.
Working people shouldn't be locked out of their own communities because government refuses to do its job.
