Button The State of Maryland Legislative Scholarship is offered by State Delegates and Senators to Maryland residents who plan to pursue a post-secondary credential full-time (12+ credits per semester) or part-time (6-11 credits per semester) at a two-year or four-year Maryland college, university or private career school​.
Students m ust apply to their local Senator or Delegate directly.
All in-state majors are eligible!
1.
Complete the FAFSA Application - Students must complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) by March 1st , if the delegate requests that the Office of Student Financial Assistance (OSFA) will be responsible for identifying and awarding local students.
OSFA will administer awards based on need,​ as demonstrated on the student's FAFSA. ​ 2.
FAFSA Exception - The student must file the Maryland State Financial Aid Application (MSFAA) instead of completing the FAFSA, if eligible for in-state tuition, but ineligible to receive federal aid.
To create a student account and complete the MSFAA online, click here .
3.
Out of State Exception - Award recipients may use this scholarship at an out-of-state school if your major is not available at any Maryland institution and if your legislator​ agrees .
In order to verify your major as unique, you must complete and submit the online application by the July 1, 2024 deadline.
4.
Renewal of Award - Students must re-apply each year to their local delegate and, if required, complete the FAFSA by March 1st.
Conditions/regulations and eligibility must be met.
To learn more about the delegate scholarship award, go to the Maryland Higher Education Commission Website .
You can also email MHEC at osfa.mhec@maryland.gov If you still need more information, email Delegate Fraser-Hidalgo .
