LEGISLATIVE SCHOLARSHIP
Did you know that residents of Maryland who are currently enrolled in or plan to enroll, whether full-time or part-time, in a degree-seeking undergraduate, graduate, or professional program may qualify for a legislative scholarship?
If you’re in Maryland and considering higher education, it’s worth looking into this potential source of funding!
