David Fraser-Hidalgo has represented District 15 in the House of Delegates since 2013.
He is a member of the Environment & Transportation Committee, a member of the Environment Subcommittee and is Chair of the Energy Subcommittee
Under David's leadership he has:
- Sponsored legislation ensuring the reliability of EV charging stations while also enhancing financial incentives for residential EV charging installations.
- Help Establish time-of-use rates to improve grid reliability and promoting ambitious yet achievable solar storage goals for Maryland.
David has been awareded:
- The Legislator of the Year from the League of Conservation Voters
- The Caspar R.
Taylor Jr. award by the Speaker of the House in recognition of his steadfast commitment to public service and the integrity of the House of Delegates.
A resident of Montgomery County since he was 5 years old, David is product of Montgomery County public schools, Montgomery College, and St.
Mary’s College of Maryland.
David is a widower and lives in Boyds, MD with his two children, Samantha and Scott.
In his free time, he enjoys gardening, hiking, running, and biking on the many outdoor trails that Montgomery County has to offer.
