ISSUES
List of Services
-
Education As a product of Montgomery County public schools and the father of two young children, David knows how important a strong public education system is to our community.
He believes in a robust public education system that allows all students to rise based on their ability and aptitude, regardless of race, ethnicity, socioeconomic status, gender, sexual orientation, or religious affiliation.
David was proud to vote in support of the Blueprint for Maryland’s Future, a historic funding plan to recruit new educators, expand career schooling options, and invest in educational equity.
List Item 2
-
Economy Having run a successful small business in Maryland, David knows it is critical for our state to have a versatile and resilient economy that promotes self-sufficiency.
This can only be accomplished by ensuring that all workers earn a living wage, are free to advance in their chosen careers without fear of discrimination, and are provided with ample opportunities to achieve work-life balance.
List Item 3
-
Jobs As a former small business owner in Montgomery County, David knows that a vibrant business community provides its workers with safe and secure employment by adopting common-sense policies that encourage businesses to locate to, remain in, and succeed in the state of Maryland.
That is why David has been a leader in the fight to increase the minimum wage in the state and in Montgomery County, while at the same time balancing the needs of our business community.
-
Public Safety David’s first job after college was as a police officer in Montgomery County.
He strongly believes in protecting our citizens, businesses and public organizations from all types of crime.
David believes that those of us who need help can receive it promptly and efficiently from police officers and through expanded social services.
-
Environment As the lead sponsor of the 2017 fracking ban, David strongly believes we have a moral duty to protect the environment for our children and for future generations.
He is a strong supporter of expanding the use of alternative energy and reducing our dependence on fossil fuels.
The Maryland League of Conservation Voters has named him a “Green Champion” every year since 2017.
David is proud to serve on the Environment and Transportation Committee in the House of Delegates.
-
Immigration As the son of an Ecuadoran immigrant, David fully understands the difficulties people face when coming to the United States.
David believes the immigration process should not be overly burdensome and that all residents should receive equal protection and benefits under the law.
David is proud to serve as the Chair of the Maryland Legislative Latino Caucus.
-
Electric Future As the Chair of the Motor Vehicle & Transportation Subcommittee and the lead sponsor of the Clean Cars Act for the past three sessions, David knows that the way forward is investing in electric vehicles.
David is committed to ensuring that Maryland is a leader in this movement.
He doesn't just talk the talk, he walks the walk, as a proud owner of a Tesla.
-
Bipartisanship David believes in a Maryland state government where the Governor, legislators, public officials, and the employees of state agencies work together to help Marylanders with due regard to political and philosophical differences.
David always bears in mind that accomplishing the people’s business is the highest priority.
List Item 1
-
Women In 2017, David was asked by then-president of the Maryland Women's Legislative Caucus Delegate Ariana Kelly to co-chair the sexual harassment policy workgroup.
The recommendations from this workgroup became law during the 2018 legislative session, ensuring that the General Assembly fosters a safe workplace for women and men alike.
David was awarded the legislator of the year award in 2018 by the Women's Caucus for his efforts.
David is a strong supporter of women and women’s rights, especially centering on healthcare, financial security, education and job training.
A supporter of Roe v.
Wade, David is proud of his vote to expand abortion access and will continue to fight any attempts to erode a woman’s fundamental right to privacy and freedom to choose.
