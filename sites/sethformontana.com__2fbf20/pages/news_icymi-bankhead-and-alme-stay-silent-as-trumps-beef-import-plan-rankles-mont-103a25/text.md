Hungry Horse News: "Trump's beef import rankles Montana farmers, Senator"
By Chris Peterson, September 2, 2026
- President Trump’s plan to import 300,000 metric tons on tariff-free ground beef into the U.S. has rankled Montana politicians and those running for office [...] Beef prices have surged in the past few years and a good steak in Montana starts at about $11 to $12 a pound and goes up from there.
- “Washington, D.C.’s plan to flood our market with 660 million pounds of foreign beef is a short-sighted political stunt that undercuts the Montana ranchers who raise the best beef in the world.
As Montana’s Independent voice in the U.S.
Senate, I will push to immediately pass the bipartisan American Beef Labeling Act and reinstate mandatory country-of-origin labeling, because Montana producers deserve a fair shake and consumers deserve to know exactly where their meat is coming from,” Bodnar said in a release.
“One thing I’ll never do is bury my head in the sand and abandon Montana ranchers because it’s politically convenient, which is exactly what Kurt Alme is doing right now when he stays silent to appease his political bosses in D.C.”
- Senatorial candidates Alani Bankhead, a Democrat, and Kurt Alme, a Republican, did not respond to a request for comment.
