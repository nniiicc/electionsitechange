SARAH SWAGER
Former UM Vice Provost for Student Success
"I'm from Sheridan, Montana and worked in higher education for decades.
I've seen presidents come and go.
Seth was one of the most supportive leaders I have ever worked with.
When he arrived, UM had one female dean.
When he left, more than half our deans were women.
I watched him hire two female provosts, which is the #2 position at the university.
He listened to them.
I sat at the table and on the executive team, and my voice was always valued.
And while I worked to juggle my responsibilities as a leader and my responsibilities as a daughter caring for aging parents, Seth supported me every step of the way."
ARLENE WALKER-ANDREWS
Former UM Vice Provost for Academic Affairs
"I spent more than 45 years in higher education settings: as a secretary, undergraduate, graduate student, professor, administrator, and volunteer.
I recognize the difference between saying you support the people in those positions and actually doing so.
In the workplace, women, especially, encounter those who discriminate based on age, gender, race, appearance, and education.
Universities are not immune to those forces, which call for leadership that meets that challenge.
Seth Bodnar did so.
He promoted women across UM; he appointed women to higher administrative positions; and he relaunched the Women's Leadership Initiative to empower women and recognize their contributions.
I know personally several of the talented women who were recognized and promoted under his leadership."
GRACE GARDNER
UM Associate Dean, Missoula College
"I have been part of the University of Montana community since 2005, serving in a variety of roles while earning all four of my degrees from UM.

Based on my own experience, Seth Bodnar has consistently demonstrated integrity, professionalism, kindness, and genuine respect for the people around him.
Throughout every stage of my personal and professional journey, Seth was unwavering in his support.
He believed in me as a leader, encouraged my growth, and created an environment where I felt trusted and valued.
I have worked with many leaders over the course of my career, and he is among the very best.
He leads with humility, works incredibly hard, and invests in people.
I will always be grateful for the opportunity to have worked under his leadership and for the example he has set for our university community."
