At 18 years old, he raised his right hand at West Point and swore an oath to support and defend the Constitution of the United States, not one political party or the other.
Seth served in combat as a platoon leader in the 101st Airborne Division in Iraq.
He became a Green Beret and commanded a Special Forces detachment on deployments around the world.
He later returned to Iraq as a special assistant to the Commanding General.
Seth is also a businessman and served 8 years as the President of the University of Montana, where he delivered record high retention and graduation rates.
MESSAGE Our political system is broken and neither national party is fighting for Montana.
Both are too busy waging partisan warfare and answering to D.C. party bosses.
Seth believes Montanans deserve an Independent senator who shows up, answers only to Montanans, and gets things done.
This election gives Montanans a choice to reject the broken system and send a clear message: Montana comes before party.
Voters should hear Seth’s biography and top priorities.
Make the Montana way of life a reality again Lower costs for working Montana families by fighting tariffs and ending the Iran War that are driving up prices.
Protect Montana’s public lands and keep them in public hands for future generations.
Put Montanans in charge of data center development by opposing projects that raise utility rates, threaten our water, or move forward without meaningful local input and approval.
Reform Our Broken Political System Ban members of Congress from trading individual stocks so public service can’t be used for personal profit.
Reject corporate PAC money Enact term limits for members of Congress so Washington doesn’t become a permanent career.
Refuse to caucus with either party.
Seth believes Montana deserves a Senator who isn’t afraid of Donald Trump and doesn’t answer to Chuck Schumer.
Restore Common Sense and Accountability Secure the border and fix a broken immigration system that neither party has been willing to solve.
Restore fiscal responsibility by cutting waste, demanding accountability for taxpayer dollars, and getting serious about a national debt that both parties have helped create.
Defend the Constitution and the checks and balances that prevent any president or political party from having unchecked power.
