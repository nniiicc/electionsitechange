Being exposed to politics at a young age inspired me to earn my degree in Public Policy from the Georgia State University and to become involved by running for State House.
After serving two years in the Georgia House of Representatives, I am more prepared than ever to humbly and accurately represent the people of Cherokee and Cobb counties.
Please take a moment to review my position on important issues in the state and country.
Your engagement is vital as we work together to address these key concerns and continue building a better Georgia.
Local Control I am dedicated to championing the empowerment of our communities by advocating for local decision-making, ensuring that choices are made by those who are directly impacted.
It's crucial for local communities to have the autonomy to determine their own futures without directives from higher levels of government.
True good governance, a content citizenry, and a thriving economy are rooted in local control.
Second Amendment I firmly believe that the right to keep and bear arms is an unquestionable freedom that must be protected as staunchly as every other right guaranteed to Americans.
If we allow one right to be legislated away, we put all our rights at risk.
I will passionately defend the Second Amendment, safeguarding every citizen's fundamental right to bear arms.
Education I have firsthand experience with the benefits and shortcomings of Georgia's public education system, having gone through it from kindergarten through college.
From Boston Elementary to Etowah High School and Georgia State University, my education within Georgia’s system has shown me where it excels and where it needs improvement.
I will use this experience to ensure that every local school district receives full state support to provide the best education possible for all students in Georgia.
I believe successful schools should operate without state interference.
My focus on education is on the continued expansion of school choice with the goal of providing families with quality and accessible educational opportunities of their choosing.
Additionally, parental rights in education are paramount; parents should have ultimate authority over their children's education, free from government overreach.
Transparency I firmly believe that transparency is the cornerstone of an honest government.
As elected representatives, we are accountable to the people of Georgia, who deserve full visibility into our actions and decisions.
It is their right to ensure that their government acts in the state's best interest.
I am committed to promoting transparency in all government dealings, cultivating an atmosphere of trust and accountability.
Protecting Children I am committed to protecting our children in every aspect of their lives and upholding parental rights.
I believe in maintaining the integrity of female sports and restrooms by ensuring biological males do not participate in female activities or use of facilities.
I firmly oppose the use of puberty blockers and genital mutilation surgeries for minors.
The fight against sex trafficking is crucial, and I will advocate for stronger laws to protect our most vulnerable.
Additionally, I stand firm on my commitment to the sanctity of life.
Constituent Power I am committed to being accessible and listening to the voices of my constituents.
My role as a legislator is to represent the people of District 22, not myself.
I welcome and encourage every voter to share their opinions and concerns with me.
The government should embody the will of the people, and I am dedicated to ensuring that it reflects the collective voice of our community, not the interests of a few.
Business Growth I come from a family of small business owners, and I have seen firsthand the benefits of doing business in Georgia.
I am dedicated to ensuring our state continues to cut unnecessary red tape, maintain low taxes, and remain an excellent place for businesses to thrive.
Small businesses are the backbone of our economy, and I believe we must do everything possible to support their success.
Healthcare I recognize that healthcare costs are skyrocketing, and hospitals are closing, creating a crisis that we must address.
I am committed to finding creative solutions to streamline payment processes and make healthcare costs transparent and understandable.
Additionally, I will work to protect independent pharmacies, reduce prescription costs, ensure access to quality healthcare in rural areas and uphold medical freedom.
This means advocating for the right of individuals to make their own healthcare decisions without government mandates, preserving the freedom to choose doctors, treatments, and health plans that best meet their needs.
