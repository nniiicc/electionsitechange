Campaign Pillars
- Strong Economy, Local Opportunity LESS GOVERNMENT, MORE FREEDOM.
Montana thrives when our people, not bureaucrats, drive growth.
Jim believes the best way to grow Montana’s economy is by unleashing the private sector.
That means cutting red tape, streamlining permits, reducing unnecessary taxes, and managing natural resources wisely to create jobs and revenue.
With fewer government barriers, Montana can remain competitive, encourage innovation, and ensure prosperity for the next generation.
- Defending Constitutional Freedoms PROTECTING THE RIGHTS THAT MAKE MONTANA STRONG.
Freedom must never be compromised.
Jim is a firm defender of the 2nd Amendment and believes the right to bear arms “shall not be infringed.” He opposes gun registries, government overreach, and “gun-free zones” that disarm law-abiding citizens.
His commitment extends beyond firearms to safeguarding all liberties—ensuring government remains accountable to the people, not the other way around
- Education That Empowers Parents PARENT CHOICE = POWER & COMPETITION Families, not government, know what’s best for their kids.
Jim supports school choice, charter schools, homeschooling, and strong local school boards free from federal agendas.
He believes education should reflect community values and empower parents to make the best decisions for their children.
Competition and freedom in education will lead to better outcomes for all Montana students
- Agriculture & Natural Resources BACKBONE OF MONTANA’S ECONOMY & CULTURE Protecting ranching, farming, logging, and mining for future generations.
Jim’s platform emphasizes protecting Montana’s ranchers, loggers, and miners.
By responsibly managing natural resources, he believes we can strengthen local economies, keep taxes low, and preserve Montana’s way of life.
From agriculture to energy, Montana’s natural wealth should work for Montanans first.
- Responsible Government & Fiscal Discipline ACCOUNTABILITY, TRANSPARENCY, & NO WASTE Government should work for the people, not itself.
Jim believes in limited government that funds only what is necessary and rejects unfunded federal mandates.
With Montana’s budget surpluses, his priority is simple: return the money to the people.
He supports revenue-sharing with local governments to ease tax burdens and opposes runaway spending or new taxes that hurt working families.
- Securing Our Borders & Rule of Law UPHOLDING LEGAL IMMIGRATION & ENFORCING THE LAW.
Respect for law and sovereignty keeps Montana strong.
Jim stands firmly against illegal immigration and the relocation of unvetted refugees to Montana.
He supports securing the border, enforcing visa laws, and penalizing employers who hire illegal workers.
Montana must protect its communities, honor the rule of law, and ensure resources are reserved for legal residents
