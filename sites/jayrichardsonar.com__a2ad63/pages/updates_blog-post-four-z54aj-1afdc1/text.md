Week 1: 95th AR Legislative Session
The 95th Arkansas State Legislative Session was gaveled into session on Tuesday, January 21st.
Although a short week, there was much activity as we headed into this season.
Here are a few highlights from the first week at the Capitol.
- Leadership of the 95th Arkansas Legislative Council was identified for the committee Charis and Vice Chairs.
- Note: No Democrats were appointed to leadership in either the House or Senate.
- Bill presented: Retail Convenience Store Act, on behalf of Chanell Moore who was shot and killed in a convenience store.
- Spoke to a group of High School students about public service and AR government.
