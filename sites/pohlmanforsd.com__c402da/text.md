About John
John has called Sioux Falls home since 1984.
He and his wife Stacey, both natives of Pierre, have been married for 41 years, raised three daughters in District 10 and are now proud grandparents.
A graduate of the University of South Dakota, John has built a career as a business owner and leader, helping grow a respected marketing company and creating opportunities for others along the way.
Over the years, he has worked closely with state and community leaders to promote economic development, strengthen the visitor industry, support agriculture, improve public safety, help fund education and advance research and innovation across South Dakota.
John is known for bringing people together, solving problems and focusing on results, not rhetoric.
He is a graduate of Leadership Sioux Falls and Leadership South Dakota and has received local, statewide and national recognition for his professional achievements.

Positions & Beliefs
Steady, Practical Leadership
John Pohlman believes state government should be focused on common sense solutions that protect taxpayers, strengthen communities and preserve South Dakota values.
Fiscal Responsibility
South Dakotans expect government to live within its means.

Public Safety
Safe communities are the foundation of a strong state.
John supports law enforcement and policies that protect families and neighborhoods.
Stronger Communities
From economic development to workforce growth, John will focus on policies that help Sioux Falls and South Dakota continue to thrive for years to come.
South Dakota Values

John will stand firmly for those values.
Results Over Rhetoric
John believes leadership is about solving real problems, not chasing headlines.
He will bring a steady, thoughtful approach to the legislature.
