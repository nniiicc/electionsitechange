VISION &
PRIORITIES
James Bush III’s vision for Florida House District 109 is rooted in a simple belief: leadership should bring results home.
For decades, he has served as an educator, minister, community advocate, and experienced public servant, working with families, students, seniors, faith leaders, small businesses, and neighborhood organizations across Miami-Dade County.
District 109 deserves more than campaign promises.
It deserves proven leadership, practical solutions, and a representative who understands the daily needs of Miami Gardens, Opa-locka, North Miami, Brownsville, Westview, West Little River, Gladeview, Bunche Park, Golden Glades, Biscayne Park, Opa-locka North, and surrounding Miami-Dade communities.
James Bush III is running to keep District 109 on the move with real leadership, real experience, and real results.
01
JOBS, WORKFORCE & ECONOMIC
DEVELOPMENT
District 109 must develop stronger pathways to good jobs, small business growth, entrepreneurship, and local investment.
James Bush III will continue working to connect residents with workforce training, career readiness, business development resources, and economic opportunities that help families build stability and wealth in their own communities.
02
NEIGHBORHOODS & PUBLIC SAFETY
Public safety means prevention, partnership, and quality-of-life solutions.
James Bush III supports community-centered approaches that strengthen neighborhoods, reduce violence, improve safety, and connect residents with needed services.
Safer communities are built through trust, opportunity, youth investment, neighborhood revitalization, and steady leadership.
03
SENIOR SERVICES, HOMES & DIGNITY
Seniors helped build District 109, and they deserve respect, protection, access, and support.
James Bush III will prioritize senior programming, wellness resources, food security, housing, healthcare access, transportation support, and services for fixed-income seniors.
A stronger District 109 must honor its elders not only with words, but with action.
04
EDUCATION & STUDENT SUCCESS
James Bush III believes strong schools build strong communities.
His education priorities include support for public schools, teachers, students, scholarships, career training, STEM programs, college access, and pathways that help young people move from the classroom to opportunity.
As a longtime educator, he understands that every student deserves the tools, mentorship, and support needed to succeed.
05
COMMUNITY WELLNESS & FAMILY
Healthy communities require access to food, healthcare, mental wellness resources, nonprofit partnerships, and preventive services.
James Bush III will continue supporting efforts that help families, seniors, children, and vulnerable residents get the resources they need close to home.

Why I'm Running...
I am running for Florida State Representative in House District 109 because this community has shaped my values, strengthened my faith, and deepened my commitment to public service.
For decades, I have worked alongside families, students, educators, business owners, faith leaders, seniors, and community advocates across Miami Gardens, Opa-locka, North Miami, Westview, West Little River, Gladeview, Brownsville, Bunche Park, Golden Glades, Biscayne Park, Opa-locka North, and communities throughout Miami-Dade County.
I know what the people of Florida House District 109 want: strong schools, safer neighborhoods, better jobs, support for seniors, opportunities for young people, and real investment in our communities.
I am running because leadership is about service.
It means showing up, listening, and delivering results that make a difference.
Throughout my career as an educator, minister, community advocate, and former State Representative, I have worked to expand educational opportunities, support workforce development, strengthen community organizations, invest in youth programs, and advocate for seniors and families.
The work is not finished.
District 109 still needs experienced leadership, proven results, and a clear vision for the future.
I am not asking you to believe in empty promises.
I am asking you to look at the work, the service, and the record.
Together, we can keep District 109 moving forward with real leadership, real experience, and real results.
— James Bush III
