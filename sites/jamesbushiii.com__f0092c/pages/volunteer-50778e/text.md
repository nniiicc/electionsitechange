Ways to Get Involved...
Look at volunteering as a way of investing in yourself and your community.
Leadership does not only happen behind a podium.
Sometimes leadership is a clipboard, a conversation, a ride, a reminder, a prayer, a phone call, or a hand extended at exactly the right moment.
By joining Team James Bush III, you can:
* Build your own leadership and communication skills
* Meet people who care about the future of District 109
* Help voters understand the issues and make a plan to vote
* Support students, seniors, families, faith communities, and small businesses
* Be part of a movement focused on service, dignity, opportunity, and results.
