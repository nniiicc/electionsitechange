A Message from James Bush III: Championing Seniors, Parental Rights, and Education in District 109
In Florida House District 109, leadership means protecting the values that define our community.
For James Bush III, that foundation rests on three inseparable pillars: supporting our senior citizens, safeguarding parental rights, and elevating the quality of our education system.
1.
Senior Citizens: Honoring and Protecting Our Elders
Our seniors are the bedrock of our neighborhoods, having spent their lives building the community we enjoy today.
James Bush III believes it is our solemn duty to ensure they can live with dignity, security, and peace of mind.
- Affordable Living & Protection: Fighting against soaring rent increases and protecting property values so fixed-income seniors aren't priced out of their lifelong homes.
- Comprehensive Services: Expanding vital local social services, healthcare access, and dedicated senior programming across Miami Gardens, Opa-locka, and North Miami.
2.
Parental Rights: Families Know What's Best for Their Children
James Bush III believes that the family structure is foundational to a thriving society.
He has consistently stood firm in his conviction that parents must have a powerful seat at the table when it comes to the guidance, upbringing, and education of their children.
- A Right to Know: Advocating for transparency so parents are fully informed and intimately involved in the decisions affecting their children’s lives and well-being.
- Faith and Family Values: Grounding policy choices in values that support parental authority and protect the innocence of the next generation.
3.
Education: Providing Real Paths to Generational Wealth
As a retired thirty-year educator, James Bush III doesn't just talk about education—he has lived it.
He views a robust, well-funded educational system as the ultimate equalizer for youth in District 109.
- Classroom Excellence: Supporting teachers, public schools, and career training initiatives that prepare students for modern jobs.
- Expanding Higher Education: Continuing his legacy of creating scholarship opportunities for local students to attend college, ensuring our youth can climb the economic ladder without paralyzing debt.
The Record Matters: "Leadership isn't about following political trends; it’s about listening to the community and delivering results that strengthen families and protect our most vulnerable." — James Bush III
