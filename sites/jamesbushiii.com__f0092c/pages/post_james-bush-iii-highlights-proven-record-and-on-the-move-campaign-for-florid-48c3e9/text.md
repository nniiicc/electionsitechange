MIAMI-DADE COUNTY, Fla. — James Bush III, candidate for Florida State Representative in House District 109, is sharpening his “On The Move” message for voters across Miami Gardens, Opa-locka, North Miami, Westview, West Little River, Gladeview, Brownsville, Bunche Park, Golden Glades, Biscayne Park, Opa-locka North, and Miami-Dade County.
His message is direct: District 109 needs experienced leadership that can bring resources home.
James Bush III Highlights Proven Record for Florida House District 109
“This election is about proof, not promises,” said James Bush III.
“I have spent decades serving students, families, seniors, churches and neighborhoods.
You deserve a representative who knows the work, listens to people and delivers results.”
Bush’s campaign is focused on education, economic opportunity, public safety, senior services, youth development, and community investment.
His record includes major funding wins, legislative action, and long-term service throughout Miami-Dade County.
“I am running to keep Florida House District 109 moving forward,” Bush said.
“That means stronger schools, better jobs, safer neighborhoods, respect for seniors and real investment in every part of this district.”
Election Day is August 18, 2026.
Voters are encouraged to make a plan to vote, volunteer, donate, request a yard sign, and stay connected with the James Bush III campaign.
