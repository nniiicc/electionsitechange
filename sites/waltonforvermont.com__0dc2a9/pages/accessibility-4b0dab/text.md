We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 AA and to correct barriers when people find them.
What is built in The site uses semantic headings, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus states, labeled forms, strong contrast, responsive layouts, screen-reader status messages, and reduced-motion support.
Media and outside services Campaign media, downloadable files, and third-party services are reviewed for accessibility as they are added.
Images should include descriptive alternative text, and video should include captions.
Report a problem If something blocks access, use the contact form and choose “Website problem.” Tell us the page, device, browser, and assistive technology if you are comfortable doing so.
Ongoing work Accessibility is a launch requirement and an operating practice.
It will be included in content, design, and release review.
