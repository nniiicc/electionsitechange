This page explains what the website collects, why it is collected, and what choices you have.
What we collect When you submit a form, we collect the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, town, volunteer interests, message, event RSVP, or email preferences.
We may also receive referral and campaign-link parameters so we can understand which outreach brought you here.
Why we collect it Campaign staff may use the information to respond, coordinate volunteers, manage events, send requested updates, improve the website, keep required records, and protect forms from abuse.
Outside services Website hosting, Supabase database services, an approved donation provider, and a future email delivery provider may process information for the campaign.
Privacy-conscious analytics may be enabled later.
Invasive tracking is disabled by default.
Campaign-finance information Donation providers may collect information required by federal law.
Political-campaign records can have legal retention or public-disclosure requirements, so the campaign cannot promise that every finance record can be deleted.
Deletion requests Use the contact page to ask what information the campaign holds or request deletion.
The campaign will honor requests where it is legally and operationally permitted to do so.
Data protection Access is restricted to authorized campaign users.
No website can promise perfect security, but the campaign will minimize collection and respond to known risks.
