Projects / Organizing institution
The campaign’s broader Vermont organizing platform—bringing Keep Vermont Independent into one durable structure for local chapters, public work, political independence, and long-term institution building.
Independent Vermont is being built as a durable organizing platform for Vermonters who believe the state should exercise independent judgment, develop its own institutions, and stop treating national party loyalty as a substitute for political representation.
Keep Vermont Independent is the public campaign at the center of that work.
Independent Vermont is intended to become the larger structure through which meetings, research, local groups, public projects, and future independent candidates can be organized.
