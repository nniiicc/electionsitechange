Independence does not mean isolation.
It means entering every relationship from a position of strength, self-respect, and accountability.
Vermont is not merely a market, a congressional district, or an administrative territory.
It is a place with its own history, character, obligations, and judgment.
01 / THE CONDITION Distance hides consequences.
Decisions affecting Vermont are increasingly made by institutions that do not live with the consequences.
National organizations write the script.
Federal power defines the range of possible action.
Dependence starts to look like realism.
It is not enough to complain about that arrangement.
Vermont must become more capable of acting for itself.
The answer is not withdrawal.
The answer is independence.
