A public meeting, research, and discussion series examining who actually holds power over Vermont’s government, economy, land, housing, institutions, and political choices.
All projects Who Governs Vermont? begins with a question that is simple to ask and difficult to answer.
Power in Vermont is divided among elected officials, federal agencies, national political parties, major institutions, property owners, nonprofit organizations, corporations, regulators, and sources of outside funding.
This project creates public rooms in which Vermonters can examine those relationships directly.
It may include meetings, research, interviews, public records, visual maps, reports, and local organizing.
The answer should be Vermonters.
First, Vermonters need to understand the question.
Public meetings Put the question in the room.
Town halls, campus discussions, interviews, and public events create places where Vermonters can examine power directly.
Upcoming events Research & reports Map the relationships.
Public records, institutional mapping, original sources, reports, and interviews can make power visible and open to public challenge.
