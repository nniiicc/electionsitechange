Hello World Peace is an independent media and communications venture being built to create original programming, develop enduring media properties, and reach audiences directly.
Its ambition is broader than a campaign media operation: a home for video, journalism, interviews, entertainment, cultural work, and new formats that combine media, technology, politics, and public participation.
Political movements, creators, businesses, and public projects increasingly depend on media systems owned by institutions with different interests.
Hello World Peace is an attempt to build durable media properties and direct audience relationships instead of renting temporary attention from those systems.
