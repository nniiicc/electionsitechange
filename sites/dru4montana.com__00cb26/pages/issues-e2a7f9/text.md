Watch Video Homeschool Rights Did you know Montana ranks 3rd in the nation for homeschooling?
9% of students in our state are homeschooled, only Alaska and Tennessee have higher rates.
And that's excellent news: Homeschool kids frequently score 15-30% higher on standardized tests than public schooled children.
They also tend to receive higher ACT & SAT scores.
And they have a higher college graduation rate than their peers.
We should follow in the footsteps of our sister-state Wyoming and do away with homeschool laws that make school administrators, and not parents, the default guardians of children.

Watch Video Gun Rights Gun rights are simply property rights.
And your property rights are sacred and should not be infringed upon.
The Founding Fathers wrote the Second Amendment because an armed populace is a free populace.
Never forget: The first shots of the Revolutionary War were fired when the British tried to confiscate the Colonists' weapons.
Before slaughtering the Lakota people at Wounded Knee, the US Army disarmed the tribe.
The first "common sense gun control" measures enacted in this country were to keep minorities such as slaves, Catholics, or Natives Americans from owning guns.
Guns are the great equalizer of society.
As senator, I would never sign away this God-given right from my fellowman.

Libertarian Principles Watch Video What does a Libertarian believe?
A libertarian believes nobody has the right to infringe on you or your property.
A libertarian believes that voluntary contracts must be upheld.
A libertarian believes in the free market.
Libertarians adhere to the principle of Non-aggression, rejecting the initiation of force against their fellowman.
A libertarian believes in peace.
A libertarian believes your rights aren't granted by the government, but exist outside of man-made systems.
A libertarian believes in protecting the rights of the smallest minority on earth, the individual.
