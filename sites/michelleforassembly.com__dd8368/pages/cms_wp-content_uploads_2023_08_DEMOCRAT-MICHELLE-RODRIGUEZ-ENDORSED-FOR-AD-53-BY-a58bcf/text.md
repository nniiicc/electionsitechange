DEMOCRAT MICHELLE RODRIGUEZ ENDORSED FOR AD 53 BY DIVERSE, PIONEERING WOMEN LEADERS Assemblymember Lisa Calderon joins colleagues Buffy Wicks, Speaker Pro-tem Cecilia Aguiar Curry, Tina McKinnor and 35 other legislators and local elected leaders in supporting Michelle Rodriguez (Pomona, CA) Michelle Rodriguez announced today that she has earned the endorsements of Assemblymembers Lisa Calderon, Buffy Wicks, Cecilia Aguiar Curry and Tina McKinnor.
“Michelle Rodriguez – the mother of a deputy sheriff, firefighter and healthcare worker – is a strong voice for working class women and families, standing up for all Californians,” Assemblymember Calderon said.
“I’m proud to endorse Michelle for State Assembly, because she is on the side of the people.” “I’m honored to be supported by these lawmaking pioneers who have guided our state to recovery,” Rodriguez responded.
“I look forward to working side-by-side with them, fighting for better schools, quality, affordable healthcare, and common sense solutions to the housing and homelessness crises.” Campaign finance reports filed by the July 31, 2023 deadline reveal that Democrat Michelle Rodriguez, supported by more than 35 legislators plus local elected leaders, is dominating all other candidates in both total fundraising and in cash-on-hand.
Rodriguez, who is a member of the California POST Commission, focused on improving law enforcement training, has direct experience in the 53rd district working in health care and our schools, and is a mother and grandmother.
She and her husband, Assemblymember Freddie Rodriguez, raised their four children in Pomona with an emphasis on public service, leading them to pursue careers in health care and as first responders.
