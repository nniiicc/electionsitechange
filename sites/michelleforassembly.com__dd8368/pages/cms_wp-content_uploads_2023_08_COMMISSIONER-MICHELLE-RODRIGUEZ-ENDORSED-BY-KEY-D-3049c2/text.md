FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE July 18, 2023 COMMISSIONER MICHELLE RODRIGUEZ ENDORSED BY KEY DEMOCRATIC LAWMAKERS, SURPASSES $215,000 IN FUNDS RAISED FOR ASSEMBLY CAMPAIGN (Pomona, California) – Michelle Rodriguez, a member of the Commission on Peace Officers Standards and Training (POST), announced today that she has added six more Democratic lawmakers to her list of supporters for her candidacy in the 53rd State Assembly seat: Assemblymember Jaquie Irwin Assemblymember Blanca Pacheco Assemblymember David Alvarez Senator Melissa Hurtado Senator Monique Limón U.S.
Congressman Kevin Mullin “I’m proud to be endorsed by so many Democratic leaders.
We will never stop fighting for working class families and kids,” Rodriguez said.
“As my campaign continues to gather momentum, I’m pleased to have earned the support of lawmakers who also believe we must invest in our schools and universities, create jobs, build houses that working Californians can afford, and make our neighborhoods safer." Rodriguez, who in addition to serving as a State Commissioner focused on improving law enforcement training, has direct experience in the 53rd district working in health care and our schools, and is a mother and grandmother.
She and her husband, Assemblymember Freddie Rodriguez, raised their four children in Pomona with an emphasis on public service, leading them to pursue careers in health care and as first responders.
