MICHELLE RODRIGUEZ DOMINATES ALL OTHER CANDIDATES IN FUNDRAISING IN AD 53 Mother of deputy sheriff, firefighter and healthcare worker has raised $222,000 and has more cash-on-hand than all other candidates combined (Pomona, CA) Campaign finance reports filed by the July 31, 2023 deadline reveal that Democrat Michelle Rodriguez is dominating all other candidates in both total fundraising and in cash-on-hand.
