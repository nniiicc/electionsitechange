Senate Common-Sense Texas Priorities 01 Affordability Lower the financial pressure on Texas households by evaluating federal policy through its effect on everyday living costs.
02 Economy & Jobs Support entrepreneurship, American manufacturing, skilled trades, technology and policies that reward work and investment.
03 Border Security & Immigration Secure America's borders, enforce immigration law and maintain a functioning legal immigration system.
America can be both a nation of immigrants and a nation of laws.
04 Veterans America's commitment to veterans doesn't end when the uniform comes off.
Improve access to healthcare, employment, housing assistance and transition services.
Pass — the Take Care of America's Veterans Act .
05 Social Security & Medicare Protect retirement security while confronting the programs' long-term financial challenges honestly.
06 Small Business Small businesses create jobs, strengthen communities and provide economic mobility.
Reduce unnecessary bureaucracy and encourage entrepreneurship.
07 Artificial Intelligence & the American Worker America must lead the AI revolution.
But American workers must participate in the prosperity it creates.
Promote AI education, workforce retraining, apprenticeships and responsible innovation.
08 Education & Workforce Development College should not be presented as the only road to success.
Expand skilled trades, certifications, apprenticeships, technical education and career pathways.
