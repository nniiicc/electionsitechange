Certified Write-In Candidate
The Texas Secretary of State’s certified list identifies Avery Ayers as a properly filed declared write-in candidate for U.S.
Senator in the November 3, 2026 General Election.
How Write-In Votes Are Reviewed
Texas law provides that, when write-in voting is permitted, a vote may be counted when the voter’s intent is clearly ascertainable unless another law prohibits counting it.
