How to Cast a Write-In Vote Voting systems and ballot procedures can vary by county and polling location.
Follow the instructions provided with your ballot and ask an election worker for assistance when needed.
Go to your polling location and check in as you normally would.
Ask an election worker if you need help locating information about your candidate .
Follow the instructions provided with the voting system and ballot at your polling location.
Locate the U.S.
Senator race on your ballot.
Select the write-in option .
A registered write-in candidate’s name is not printed with the other candidate names.
Ask an election worker for the certified list if you need to confirm your candidate .
Enter the candidate’s name in the write-in field using the method provided by the voting system.
Review the entry for your candidate and the rest of your ballot before casting it.
Follow the voting system’s instructions to cast your completed ballot.
How Write-In Votes Are Reviewed Texas law provides that, when write-in voting is permitted, a vote may be counted when the voter’s intent is clearly ascertainable unless another law prohibits counting it.
For instructions specific to your ballot or voting equipment, contact your county election office or ask an election worker at your polling location.
