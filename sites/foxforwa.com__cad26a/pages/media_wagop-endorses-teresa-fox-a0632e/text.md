August 16, 2026 Campaign Staff The Washington State Republican Party has endorsed Teresa Fox for U.S.
Congress in Washington’s 6th Congressional District , adding statewide Republican support to a campaign already backed by Republican organizations across all six counties of the district.
The endorsement marks another important step forward for the Fox for Washington campaign as it enters the general election and continues building support across Clallam, Grays Harbor, Jefferson, Kitsap, Mason, and Pierce counties.
Teresa has already earned the support of the Republican Party organizations in all six counties that make up Washington’s 6th Congressional District.
The Washington State Republican Party endorsement now adds the support of the state party as Teresa takes her campaign directly to voters throughout the district.
“I am deeply grateful for the support of Republicans across this district and now from the Washington State Republican Party,” Teresa said.
“This campaign has always been about the people we represent, the communities we serve, and making sure their voices are heard in Washington, D.C.” The endorsement comes as Teresa continues emphasizing public safety, constitutional rights, responsible government, strong representation for all six counties, and a campaign centered on People over Politics .
Her message throughout the district has been consistent: Washington’s 6th Congressional District deserves a representative who will listen to its communities, defend individual liberty, and put the people of the district ahead of political interests.
The growing Republican support also reflects the campaign’s expanding organization heading into the general election.
Six counties.

Teresa Fox and the Fox for Washington campaign are grateful for the confidence placed in her by Republican leaders and organizations throughout Washington and look forward to carrying that momentum into November.
About Teresa Fox Teresa Fox is the Republican candidate for U.S.
Congress in Washington’s 6th Congressional District, representing Clallam, Grays Harbor, Jefferson, Kitsap, Mason, and portions of Pierce County.
