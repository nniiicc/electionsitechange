Teresa Fox calls to lower the temperature.
BREMERTON, WA, July 15, 2026 — The climate of politics has become more about demonization than constructive debate.
Right here in the 6th Congressional District our US Representative Emily Randall stokes the hyperbole of polarization in the face of tragedy and confusion.
In a recent statement Randall referred to Federal ICE Agents as a "fascist police force." Teresa Fox stepped forward to run for US Representative in the 6th Congressional District to bring responsibility, accountability, and maturity back to the 6th District.
She said of the Randall statement: “Representative Emily Randall's statement following the tragic deaths in Texas and Maine is deeply disappointing.
Rather than allowing independent investigations to establish the facts, she immediately declared these incidents to be "murders" and labeled federal law enforcement officers "fascists." Every loss of life deserves a thorough, transparent, and independent investigation.
If mistakes were made or excessive force was used, those responsible should be held accountable.
That principle applies to every law enforcement agency, including ICE." "Inflammatory rhetoric that presumes guilt before investigations are complete serves neither justice nor public safety.
It undermines due process and further divides an already polarized nation.
Congress should be focused on ensuring accountability and improving transparency, including expanding the use of body cameras, and protecting both the public and the law enforcement officers who put themselves in dangerous situations every day.
Investigations into both incidents are ongoing, and key facts remain disputed." "Americans can support secure borders while also insisting that law enforcement operate professionally, transparently, and within the law.
Those goals are not mutually exclusive.
Representative Randall's decision to prejudge these cases and demonize an entire federal agency does not bring us closer to the truth.
It only fuels anger before all the evidence is known." ### About Teresa Fox Teresa Fox is a hands-on safety professional and emergency manager who has spent her career protecting workers and strengthening institutions in high-accountability environments.
Not a career politician, she is running to restore citizen service to Congress, focusing on fiscal responsibility, public safety, and practical solutions for the 6th Congressional District.
