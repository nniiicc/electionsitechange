April 13, 2026 Campaign Staff FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE April 13, 2026 info@foxforwa.com foxforwa.com Teresa Fox Announces Congressional Campaign Kickoff Public Event for District 6 Featuring Dave Reichert in Bremerton Bremerton, WA - On Apirl 19, Teresa Fox, local occupational safety professional and emergency manager, will officially launch her campaign for Congress in Washington State’s 6th Congressional District.
The kickoff event will be held at the Kitsap Golf & Country Club at 2pm.
Teresa Fox will be joined by former Sheriff and Congressman Dave Reichert and current Washington State Republican Party Chair Jim Walsh to outline a vision for the district that places the priorities of working families over divisive ideological activism.
“While our communities are working hard to keep shipyards operating, timber moving, and small businesses alive, our current representative has been focused on building a national political brand and aligning herself with the most extreme faction of Congress,” Fox said.
“The people of the district deserve better than a member of Congress who spends more time chasing national headlines than fighting for the industries that sustain our communities.” Event Details What: Teresa Fox Congressional Campaign Kickoff When: April 19, 2026, 2pm Where: 3885 NW Golf Club Hill Rd, Bremerton, WA 98312 Who: Teresa Fox, Dave Reichert, Jim Walsh, and community supporters Since filing for candidacy in March, Teresa Fox has already garnered an early endorsement from the Kitsap County Republican Party.
The kickoff marks the transition into the next phase of the grassroots powered campaign aimed at bringing responsibility, accountability, and true representation back to the 6th Congressional District.
Members of the press are invited to attend.
Teresa Fox will be available for brief interviews immediately following speaking events.
About Teresa Fox: Fox enters the race with more than two decades of experience leading safety and emergency management programs in maritime, industrial, and government settings.
She has worked in shipyards, naval facilities, and offshore operations where the stakes are high and leadership requires accountability.
Her career includes seven years overseeing environmental and safety operations for the Naval Inactive Ships Maintenance Office in Bremerton, leadership roles at Olympic College and the Washington State Department of Labor and Industries, and service as Environmental, Health, Safety and Emergency Manager aboard a 380-foot fish processing vessel in the Gulf of Alaska responsible for the safety and medical readiness of a 232-member crew.
Fox said her decision to run was driven by frustration that Washington’s 6th District has lost its voice in Congress.
