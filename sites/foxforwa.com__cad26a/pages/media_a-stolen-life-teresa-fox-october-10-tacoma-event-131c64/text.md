On Saturday, October 10, congressional candidate Teresa Fox will stand with victims, their families and federal law-enforcement officers at the Northwest ICE Processing Center in Tacoma.
Joining Fox will be Angel Mom Agnes Gibboney, founder of Angel Families of America and the mother of Ronald da Silva.
A STOLEN LIFE Ronald was 29 years old when he was shot and killed in 2002 while standing in a driveway with a friend.
His mother later testified before Congress that the man who killed Ronald had previously been deported from the United States and returned. https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO06/20170427/105888/HHRG-115-GO06-Transcript-20170427.pdf For more than two decades, Angel Mom Agnes Gibboney has carried her son’s story across the country, advocating for victims, their families, immigration enforcement and public safety.
The Federation for American Immigration Reform preserved Ronald’s story in the video A Stolen Life: The Ronald da Silva Story.
WATCH A STOLEN LIFE: THE RONALD DA SILVA STORY https://www.fairus.org/media/10476 Ronald’s story is one reason October 10 matters.
Behind every immigration statistic, every political argument and every policy debate are real people.
There are victims whose lives cannot be restored.
There are mothers, fathers, children and families who live permanently with what was taken from them.
And there are law-enforcement officers who continue to report for duty every day to enforce the laws of the United States.
Ronald da Silva’s story has been documented in California Globe coverage and in FAIR’s A Stolen Life: The Ronald da Silva Story.
STAND WITH ICE The October 10 gathering is unapologetically in support of ICE and the men and women of law enforcement.
It is also a day to remember victims and to give their families a voice.
Fox and those gathering in Tacoma will honor lives lost, stand beside families left behind and recognize the federal officers who continue doing a difficult and increasingly politicized job.
The purpose is not simply to discuss immigration as an abstract political issue.
It is to remember who bears the consequences when federal immigration laws are not enforced.
OCTOBER 10 IN TACOMA A separately organized demonstration opposing ICE activity is also scheduled in Tacoma that afternoon.
Organizers of that march have announced plans to gather near the Puyallup Tribe Administration Building before marching to the detention center.
The demonstration is being organized by individual tribal and community members and is not an official event of the Puyallup Tribal government. https://www.kiro7.com/news/local/organizers-announce-another-ice-out-be-held-tacoma-oct-10/RZD2GEW325FIRFVH3SO4HBMB4E/ ] With both gatherings taking place on October 10, Tacoma will become a focal point for a national debate over immigration enforcement, public safety, federal law enforcement and the responsibilities of Congress.
Fox believes that debate should be open, peaceful and substantive.
AN OPEN INVITATION TO CONGRESSWOMAN EMILY RANDALL Teresa Fox is publicly inviting Congresswoman Emily Randall, the incumbent representative for Washington’s 6th Congressional District, to attend and engage in a civil, substantive discussion.
Randall has herself focused publicly on the Northwest ICE Processing Center, including raising concerns about the treatment of "brown people" and conditions at the facility.
Fox welcomes the opportunity for the two candidates to discuss these issues for the first time this campaign, directly and civilly before the voters they are asking to represent.
If Randall accepts, October 10 could provide voters with a rare opportunity before the November 3 general election to hear directly from both candidates about immigration enforcement, public safety and the role of Congress.
Washington’s statewide voting period begins October 16, when ballots are required to be mailed to registered voters.
Whether or not that conversation takes place, the purpose of the afternoon will not change.
Remember the victims.
Stand with their families.
Support the men and women enforcing our nation’s laws.
And make sure the stories behind the statistics are never forgotten.
EVENT DETAILS Stand With ICE Saturday, October 10, 2026 2:00–4:30 PM Northwest ICE Processing Center 1623 E J Street Tacoma, Washington Featured Guest: Angel Mom Agnes Gibboney, Founder of Angel Families of America Members of the public, media, elected officials, veterans organizations, civic organizations and community leaders are invited to attend.
The Fox campaign asks everyone present, regardless of viewpoint, to remain peaceful, respect lawful instructions and allow those with opposing views to exercise their First Amendment rights.
