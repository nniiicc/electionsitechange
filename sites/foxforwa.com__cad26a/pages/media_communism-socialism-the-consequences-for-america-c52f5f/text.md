October 11, 2026 Campaign Staff Communism, Socialism & the Consequences for America Sunday, October 11, 2026 | 2:00–5:00 PM Gig Harbor Vintage Aero Museum | Gig Harbor, Washington Teresa Fox for U.S.
Congress, WA-06, invites you to join us for a special afternoon gathering focused on Communism, Socialism & the Consequences for America.
This event will bring together powerful voices, firsthand experiences, and important perspectives for a meaningful discussion about freedom, government, history, and the choices facing our country.
Featured Speakers Agnes Gibboney , Angel Mom Founder, Angel Families of America Cezar-Victor Nastase Alliance for the Union of Romanians Jim Walsh Chairman, Washington State Republican Party Presenting a special “State of the States” address Teresa Fox Candidate for U.S.
Congress, WA-06 Teresa will share her personal connection to communism and why the lessons of history remain relevant to the choices Americans face today.
Master of Ceremonies David McMullan will serve as Master of Ceremonies for the afternoon.
Guests are invited to attend, listen, engage, and be part of this important conversation in one of Gig Harbor’s most distinctive settings.
Event Details Date: Sunday, October 11, 2026 Time: 2:00–5:00 PM Location: Gig Harbor Vintage Aero Museum Hosted by: Teresa Fox for U.S.
