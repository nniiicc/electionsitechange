Teresa Fox's grandfather started Fox Auto Electric in downtown Bremerton, and her uncles Jim and Leon owned Fox Motors.
Her dad ran Tom Fox Automotive, and Uncle Bobby operated his own repair business as well.
The Fox brothers were later inducted into the Kitsap Sports Hall of Fame as the first family of racing in Kitsap County.
"But more important than trophies or race wins was how they treated people." When military families downtown could not afford repairs, they fixed cars anyway.
When customers struggled with payments, they worked with them.
When the community needed sponsorship dollars, they stepped up.
That spirit of work hard, give back, and take care of your neighbors is the legacy Teresa Fox carries forward.
