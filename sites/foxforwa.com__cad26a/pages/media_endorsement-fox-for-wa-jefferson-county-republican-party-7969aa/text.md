I look forward to campaigning this year with the pride of support from the Republicans in Jefferson County.
Of all the counties on the Olympic Peninsula, perhaps Jefferson has suffered most from the extreme ideological activism in political representation.
We must get back to pragmatism and common sense in governance.
Jefferson County has been a focal point of what happens when only the voice of the few dominates government representation.
Public safety and public trust have been lost.
We must do better for all Jefferson County residents.
I volunteer to bring humble stewardship to Congress.
I volunteer to honor all voices in Jefferson County and the 6th Congressional District.
We must course correct away from the divisions of the fringe to get back to economic vitality and safe streets.
