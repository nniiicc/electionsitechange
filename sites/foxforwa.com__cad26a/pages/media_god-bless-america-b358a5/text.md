August 15, 2026 Campaign Staff Teresa Fox believes patriotism is more than a word.
It is a legacy carried forward by generations of Americans who sacrificed, served, endured, and stood for this country.
In this message, Teresa reflects on the strength and resilience of the patriots who came before us, including the family history she carries with her, and what their example means for Americans today.
She speaks about the blood that has been shed for this country, the responsibility that comes with American citizenship, and the deep love of country that continues to inspire Americans willing to stand for its future.
For Teresa, patriotism is rooted in remembrance, gratitude, courage, and responsibility.
It means honoring those who built and defended this nation.
It means understanding that freedom was never guaranteed without sacrifice.
And it means recognizing that every generation has a role in preserving the country it has been entrusted with.
