October 1, 2026 Campaign Staff FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE | October 1, 2026 Teresa Fox Campaign Announces October 10 Tacoma Gathering Law & Order or Chaos in the Streets? | Stand With ICE TACOMA, WA – The Teresa Fox for U.S.
Congress (WA-06) campaign will host a public gathering on Saturday, October 10, 2026, at the Northwest ICE Processing Center in Tacoma, Washington.
The event will honor victims of crime and their surviving families while bringing attention to immigration enforcement, public safety, and the men and women responsible for enforcing federal immigration laws.
Featured speaker Angel Mom Agnes Giboney , founder of Angel Families of America, will address the human impact of crimes committed by illegal aliens.
Teresa Fox , candidate for U.S.
Congress in Washington's 6th Congressional District, will address immigration enforcement, public safety, and her campaign's positions on these issues.
Organizers intend this to be a peaceful public gathering.
Attendees are welcome to bring American flags and signs.
Event Details Date: Saturday, October 10, 2026 Time: 2:00 PM – 4:30 PM Pacific Location: Northwest ICE Processing Center (NWIPC) Address: 1623 E J Street, Tacoma, WA 98421 Featured Speakers: Teresa Fox, Candidate for U.S.
Congress, WA-06 Agnes Giboney, Founder, Angel Families of America Admission: Public event Additional information and event updates are available at FOXFORWA.com .
