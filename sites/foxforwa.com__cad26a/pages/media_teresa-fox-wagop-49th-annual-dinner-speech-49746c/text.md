October 17, 2026 Campaign Staff A Message of Patriotism, Purpose & Hope Teresa Fox Speaks at the WAGOP 49th Annual Dinner | September 18, 2026 What a wonderful evening celebrating the spirit of America and the enduring values that make our nation extraordinary!
On September 18, Teresa Fox had the privilege of addressing fellow patriots at the Washington State Republican Party's 49th Annual Dinner, an evening filled with fellowship, shared purpose, and hope for our nation's future.
As America celebrates its 250th anniversary, we are reminded that the freedoms we cherish were never guaranteed.
They were secured through courage, sacrifice, and the unwavering determination of generations who came before us.
And now, it is our turn to carry that legacy forward.
Teresa believes America's greatest strength has always been its people.
Hardworking families, dedicated neighbors, and everyday Americans who love their country enough to stand up for its future.
The American spirit is alive and well, and our nation's greatest chapters are still waiting to be written.
We invite you to take a few minutes to watch Teresa's remarks from this special evening and share in the optimism, patriotism, and determination that bring us together.
