Home About Teresa Mission Media DONATE Back to Media & News Campaign Update Sharon Trask Endorses Teresa Fox for Congress Sharon Trask, a Mason County Commissioner, has officially endorsed Teresa Fox for US Representative in Congressional District 6 July 4, 2026 Campaign Staff The Fox for Wa campaign is pleased to announce the personal endorsement by Sharon Trask, Mason County Commissioner, for Teresa Fox running as a Republican for US Representative in the 6th Congressional District in Washington State.
Mason County is one of six counties in the 6th Congressional District.
Sharon Trask has been a servant to the people of Mason County during her term as Commissioner.
Trask said of the endorsement: "Teresa brings real-world experience, strong leadership, and a commitment to serving the people of Washington's 6th Congressional District.
She understands the challenges facing working families, small businesses, and our rural coastal communities because she has spent her career working alongside them." The Fox for Wa campaign is powered by people instead of corporate or political associations.
With all six county Republican Parties endorsing Teresa Fox, we turn our eyes and motivations toward the Primary Election in August with a unified Republican Party backing Fox as a candidate.
Fox said of the endorsement: "Serving in elected office is a privilege to represent the people.
Sharon Trask is a brave woman who stepped forward as a citizen volunteer to lead in her county.
I am honored and humbled to have the endorsement of a successful woman in politics, and a knowledgeable elected official.
We must get the fringe activism out of political office, and return to a pragmatic stewardship of governance for all of 6th Congressional District." Back to all articles Show Your Support Get your official Fox for Congress yard sign and put it proudly on display.
