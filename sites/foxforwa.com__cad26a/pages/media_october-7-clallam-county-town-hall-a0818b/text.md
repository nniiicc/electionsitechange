Join Teresa Fox on October 7 at Sequim Prairie Grange for an open community town hall discussing public safety, public lands, and the concerns of Clallam County residents.
October 2, 2026 Campaign Staff Wednesday, October 7, 2026 | 6:00 PM (Pacific) Sequim Prairie Grange #1108 290 Macleay Road, Sequim, WA 98382 Clallam County, I Want to Hear From YOU!
Teresa Fox, candidate for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Washington’s 6th Congressional District, invites Clallam County residents to an open town hall focused on two issues affecting our region: public safety and the proposed transfer of public lands.
Join the conversation on our Facebook Event!
This evening is intended to be a conversation, not a speech.
Teresa will begin with a brief introduction and opening remarks about the evening’s topics before opening the floor to attendees for questions, concerns, and discussion.
Let’s Get the Conversation Started Before October 7!
What do YOU want to talk about?
What are you seeing when it comes to public safety in your community?
What questions or concerns do you have about proposals involving our public lands?
How could decisions made at the federal level affect Clallam County?
What do you believe your representative in Congress needs to hear from the people who live here?
Residents are encouraged to join the conversation on the Facebook event page ahead of the town hall, submit questions, and invite neighbors and friends to participate.
Event Information Date: Wednesday, October 7, 2026 Time: 6:00 PM Pacific Location: Sequim Prairie Grange #1108 Address: 290 Macleay Road, Sequim, WA 98382 Host: Teresa Fox for U.S.
Congress | WA-06 Admission: Open to the public Bring your questions, share your experiences, and take part in a community discussion.
Incumbent Congresswoman Emily Randall has also been invited to attend and participate.
