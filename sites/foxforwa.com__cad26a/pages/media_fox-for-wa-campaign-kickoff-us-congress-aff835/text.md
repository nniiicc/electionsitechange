The Fox Campaign kickoff event featured free food, lots of smiles, and a positive attitude as the Republican candidate launches her official campaign.
Former Congressman Dave Reichert was the featured speaker bringing his positive words and endorsement to the Fox for Wa campaign.
Other speakers included Dave McMullan the Chair of the Pierce County Republican Party, and Jim Walsh the Chair of the Washington State Republican Party and State Representative in the 19th Legislative District.
The highlight of the event was Teresa Fox providing her first public speech to an attentive and supportive crowd of a diverse group of Washington voters.
People I talk to are upset primarily about taxes, crime & extreme left representation.
Those will be priorities of this campaign.
I am dismayed about the state of affairs on the Olympic Peninsula.
I have been to every corner of the District more than a time or two and know it very well.
The coast has been decimated by decades of far left policy.
Aberdeen & Hoquiam are boarded up.
Bremerton, Tacoma and our other cities have deteriorated.
Crime is out of control.
People are living & dying on the streets throughout the District like a third world country.
I have gone to many meetings throughout the District, I see how many people are ready for a change, and I feel there is a groundswell of support from a broad base of fed-up voters.
The tide is really turning.
We are at a tipping point in Washington.
I volunteer to help lead our governance back to representing all voters in the District for economic prosperity and public safety.
The campaign kickoff event raised funds to propel the campaign forward into filing week in Washington State.
Teresa Fox expects to earn the endorsement vote of all the county Republican Parties in the 6th Congressional District in the coming weeks.
The campaign is built and focused on cooling the current divisive political rhetoric and action of the incumbent Emily Randall, and getting back to what leadership and representation are meant to be in the District - Prosperity for All.
Teresa Fox comes from outside of the political system.
She is a citizen volunteer organizing a grassroots campaign that includes a diverse group of citizens that recognize representation is not meant to be the sword of ideological extremism.
