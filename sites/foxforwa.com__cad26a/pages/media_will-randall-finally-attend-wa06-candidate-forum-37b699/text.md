GIG HARBOR, WA – Mark your calendars!
Another congressional candidate forum is scheduled for Monday, October 19, at Heron's Key in Gig Harbor.
Despite months of outreach and invitations, Republican congressional candidate Teresa Fox says incumbent Congresswoman Emily Randall has yet to participate in a single face-to-face forum, debate, town hall, or meeting with her throughout the 2026 election season.
Not once.
Both candidates have been invited to Monday's event.
With Election Day just weeks away, voters have yet to see these two congressional opponents share a stage.
The question remains: Will Randall finally sit still long enough for voters to hear from both candidates?
