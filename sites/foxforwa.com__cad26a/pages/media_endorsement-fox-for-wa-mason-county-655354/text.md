On Monday night the Mason County Republican Central Committee became the sixth county to endorse Teresa Fox for Representative in Congressional District 6.
The Mason County Republican Party is a strong group of conscientious patriots.
Mason county is the bridge between South Puget Sound and the Olympic Peninsula.
The county's beauty is matched only by its strength and resilience.
I am so proud to have these proud patriotic Washingtonians in my corner.
We are building a grassroots campaign that is powered by people.
I am excited to listen and learn about the important issues that face our communities from the sound to the coast.
You will have a voice of common sense, logic, and problem solving in Washington DC with Teresa Fox.
