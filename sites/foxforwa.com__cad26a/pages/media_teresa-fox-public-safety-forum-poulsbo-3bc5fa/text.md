August 2, 2026 Campaign Staff Teresa Fox joined community members and fellow candidates at the Public Safety Forum at Crystal Grange in Poulsbo, Washington , for a candid conversation about public safety and the challenges facing communities across Washington’s 6th Congressional District.
During the forum, Teresa spoke directly about the importance of protecting families, supporting safe communities, strengthening accountability, and making sure the voices of local residents are heard in the decisions that affect them.
Public safety is not an abstract issue.
It affects families, neighborhoods, businesses, schools, law enforcement, and the everyday quality of life throughout the district.
