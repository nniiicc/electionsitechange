May 15, 2026 Campaign Staff FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE May 14, 2026 CONTACT: info@foxforwa.com foxforwa.com Endorsement Fox for Wa - Pierce County Republican Party Teresa Fox is proud to receive the endorsement from the Pierce County Republican Central Committee.
Teresa Fox was ecstatic to speak to the Pierce County Republicans and receive their full support and endorsement on Wednesday night.
The economic vitality of Pierce County has been neglected for too long by Emily Randall.
We must get back to the business of healthy employment in the county and across the 6th Congressional District.
Puget Sound is the economic gateway to the world.
I am committed to unlocking the tools of our economic assets in the region for good paying jobs and strong families.
We are building a grassroots campaign that is powered by people.
I am excited to listen and learn about the important issues that face our coastal communities.
You will have a voice of common sense, logic, and problem solving in Washington DC.
Teresa Fox has three qualities that impress me.
A feisty spirit to win, a knowledge of the issues and understanding of a Congresswoman's role in representing her district, and a passion to change the awful current leadership with honesty and integrity.
Teresa will work for the people.
I strongly endorse Teresa Fox for Congress WA-06.
