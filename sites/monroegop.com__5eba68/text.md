DONATE DAVE FERRIS Candidate for New York State Assembly District 137 WEBSITE ABOUT DAVE Dave Ferris is a businessman, veteran, and lifelong Monroe County resident who is ready to fight for the people of New York—and deliver real results. ​ As the owner and operator of Roc Auto Service, Dave and his wife Chan have built a trusted local business grounded in honesty, hard work, and accountability.
Their 4.9-star rating reflects the same principles Dave will bring to Albany: do the job right, treat people fairly, and stand by your word. ​ Dave proudly served in the United States Army, continuing a family tradition of service.
After his honorable discharge, he earned a degree in Marketing with a minor in Accounting from the Rochester Business Institute, where he was selected to deliver the commencement speech. ​ For more than 25 years, Dave has served the Town of Gates through roles on the Planning Board and Zoning Board of Appeals, and as Chairman of the Gates Republican Committee and Town Leader.
He understands how government works—and where it’s failing the people it’s supposed to serve. ​ Now, Dave Ferris is running for the New York State Assembly with a clear mission: fix what’s broken and restore common sense to state government.
Dave’s priorities include: ​ Lower Taxes & Cost of Living Dave will fight to reduce the tax burden on families, seniors, and small businesses.
He supports cutting wasteful spending and putting money back in the pockets of hardworking New Yorkers. ​ Support Small Businesses As a small business owner, Dave knows firsthand how overregulation hurts local employers.
He will push to eliminate unnecessary red tape, reduce fees, and create an environment where businesses can grow and create jobs. ​ Public Safety & Strong Communities Dave supports law enforcement and believes every family deserves to feel safe.
He will work to strengthen public safety laws, ensure police have the resources they need, and hold criminals accountable. ​ Fix Albany & Increase Accountability Dave believes Albany is out of touch.
He will advocate for term limits, greater transparency, and stricter ethics rules to restore trust in government. ​ Keep New Yorkers in New York Too many families are leaving the state.
Dave will fight to make New York more affordable, more competitive, and a place where people want to build their future—not move away from. ​ Protect Seniors & Working Families Dave will stand up for those on fixed incomes and working families struggling with rising costs, ensuring they are not left behind by bad policies. ​ In 2023, Dave ran for Monroe County Legislator, continuing his long-standing commitment to public service.
Now, he’s stepping up to bring real-world experience and independent leadership to Albany.
Dave lives in Gates with his wife Chan, their daughter Mariah, and their two dogs, Brooklyn and Dior.
He remains active in the community as a board member of the Italian American Sports Club and the Italian Civic League.
Dave Ferris isn’t a career politician—he’s a veteran, a business owner, and a neighbor who understands the challenges you face.
And he’s ready to fight for you.
