Linda Sánchez in 41st congressional district Press Release | April 21, 2026 Sánchez’s record of delivering for her constituents continues to resonate in Southern California Whittier, Calif. – Congresswoman Linda T.
Sánchez today announced that she has received the endorsement of two leading Latino organizations, the Avance Democratic Club and Latinas Lead California .
Avance is the country’s largest Latino club dedicated to elevating Latino voices and advancing equity in government.
Latinas Lead California works to increase the number of Latinas in elected office, appointments and other prominent leadership positions.
“I’m honored to receive the endorsement of these two leading Latino organizations,” said Congresswoman Sánchez.
“In Congress, I have been a fighter for our community, standing up to Trump and his administration’s tactics targeting Latinos.
These endorsements are a reminder of the work we still have to do to lift up Latino families to ensure they can build a future for themselves and their families.” A proud Latina and the daughter of Mexican immigrants, Sánchez has championed expanding economic opportunity, strengthening worker protections and holding ICE accountable.
She has earned a reputation for principled leadership and a commitment to uplifting communities often left behind in the political process.
With Avance Democratic Club and Latinas Lead California’s support, Sánchez’s campaign continues to mobilize Latino voters and build a coalition rooted in community, engagement and the belief that together, we can achieve meaningful change in an era when our community is under attack.
