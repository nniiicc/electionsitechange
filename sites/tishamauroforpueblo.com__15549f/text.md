Legislative Focus
Representative Tisha Mauro is the Vice-Chair for the Agriculture, Water and Natural Resources Committee and a member of the House Business Affairs & Labor Committee.
Representative Mauro represents House District 46 which focusing on legislation that conserves and protects Colorado’s water, land and air.
As a chile farmer herself, she’s an advocate for the workers, small business owners, farmers, and ranchers who keep local economies running.
In the last three legislative sessions, Rep.
Mauro has championed legislation to expand and improve EV charging stations across the state, increase railroad safety for workers and local communities, prevent and respond to wildfires, and incentivize economic development and investment in Southern Colorado.

Pueblo Chili Farmer, Mom, Legislator
A word from Tisha…
I proudly represent Pueblo in the Colorado House of Representatives, now serving my second term, because I believe our community deserves leadership rooted in hard work, family, and real-life experience.
Growing up on a family farm in Pueblo and raising my two sons here shaped my belief that when people work hard, government should work just as hard for them.
Before being elected, I worked in small business, giving me firsthand insight into the challenges facing local employers and working families.
I ran for office to be a voice for Pueblo’s farming community, small businesses, and families who too often feel overlooked, and in office I have worked to advance legislation that promotes economic development right here in Pueblo.
I am running for re-election to continue delivering results, protect what makes our community strong, and ensure Pueblo’s priorities are heard at the State Capitol.
