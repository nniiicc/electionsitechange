David Sutman Jr. is a Tennessee-based independent candidate for U.S.
Senate, running to represent the working men and women too often overlooked by career politicians and party agendas.
Born and raised with the values that define Tennessee — hard work, personal responsibility, and looking out for your neighbors — David understands firsthand the challenges facing everyday families.
Like many Tennesseans, he has experienced the pressure of rising costs, economic uncertainty, and a system that feels increasingly disconnected from the people it serves.
David comes from a working-class background and has built his experience in the real world — not in political offices, but in business, problem-solving, and navigating the same economy that millions of Tennesseans face every day.
He believes leadership shouldn't come from those insulated from consequences, but from those who live them.
Frustrated with a political system dominated by party loyalty, special interests, and empty promises, David made the decision to run as an independent.
His campaign is built on a simple principle: Tennessee deserves representation, not political theater.
David is not running to become a politician — he's running to do a job and be accountable for it.
