Gary Pretlow is a Democratic member of the New York State Assembly, representing District 89.
He was first elected to the chamber in 1992.
He previously represented District 87 in the New York State Assembly from 2003 to 2013 and District 84 in the Assembly from 1993 to 2002.

Gary Pretlow is currently the Chair of the Racing and Wagering Committee and has assisted in legalizing Fantasy Sports operations and Mobile Sports Betting in the state of New York.
Also, Assemblyman Pretlow is the current Chair of the Westchester Delegation, which consists of Assembly members that represent various assembly districts throughout Westchester.
