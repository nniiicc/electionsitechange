Minnesota residents: the state refunds your first $75 ($150 per couple) — your gift can cost you nothing.
How the refund works Minnesota's Political Contribution Refund program will refund your contribution, up to $75 per person ($150 per couple) , directly from the state.
If you're eligible to vote in Minnesota under Minn.
Stat.
Sec.
201.014, your support costs you nothing.
Make your contribution below and check the box asking for an official receipt.
Within a few business days you'll receive your Form EP-3 receipt by email.
The receipt is issued by the Minnesota Campaign Finance Board and signed by the campaign treasurer.
Print the EP-3, attach a copy to Minnesota Form PCR (Political Contribution Refund Application), and mail both to MN Revenue's Political Contribution Refund mail station, in a separate envelope from your state income tax return, with no staples.
The Form PCR deadline is hard: April 15, 2027 for 2026 contributions, no amendments and no extensions.
The state issues your refund directly to you.
The $75 per person ($150 per married couple) cap is per calendar year, across all eligible candidates and political party units combined .
If you've already received the maximum refund this year for contributions to other DFL candidates or party units, an additional contribution to this campaign won't be refundable until the next calendar year.
