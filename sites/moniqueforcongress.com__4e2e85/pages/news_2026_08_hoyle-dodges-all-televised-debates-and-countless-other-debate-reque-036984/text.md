Republican challenger Monique DeSpain has accepted every televised debate invitation extended to her, along with every other debate and forum request she has received.
A televised debate broadcast out of Eugene would reach 93 percent of the residents of the Fourth Congressional District.
Hoyle has instead declined all televised debates as well as refusing to participate or respond to countless other debate requests from media outlets and concerned citizen groups.
Voters across the district are entitled to hear her answer for her long record of supporting failed Governor Tina Kotek and pushing the same agenda that has brought skyrocketing cost of living, homelessness and crime across Oregon.
“As an elected Member of Congress, it is unacceptable to turn down the opportunity for a televised debate and be transparent and accountable to your constituents,” DeSpain said.
“This confirms what we have long known about Val Hoyle.
She is afraid to face the people who pay her salary.
She does not want to answer for her 217 violations of the STOCK Act, she does not want to answer for the homelessness crisis that has exploded during her and Tina Kotek’s time in office, and she does not want to answer for the extreme environmental views that have left Oregon and the Pacific Northwest exposed to out-of-control wildfires.
I challenge Val Hoyle to reverse course, accept a televised debate invitation, and debate the issues on air in front of the people we both want to represent.
She should not hide from public scrutiny.” DeSpain, a retired U.S.
Air Force Colonel with 30 years of service and a licensed Oregon attorney, is challenging Hoyle in Oregon’s Fourth Congressional District in the 2026 general election.
