BIOGRAPHY U N I TE D S T AT E S AI R F O RC E COLONEL MONIQUE J.
DESPAIN Colonel Monique J.
DeSpain is the Air National Guard Assistant to the Commandant, USAF Judge Advocate General’s School, Maxwell Air Force Base, Alabama, and a member of The Judge Advocate General’s Air National Guard Leadership Council.
In these capacities, Col DeSpain advises and assists the AFJAGS Commandant, the ANG Director, TJAG and the ANG Assistant to TJAG on matters of policy, programs, operations and utilization of ANG judge advocates and paralegals, their training, and the interface of Air Force, Air Force Reserve and Air National Guard legal programs.
Col DeSpain also supports the Oregon National Guard by performing special projects and advising the A-TAG Joint Staff Dual Status Commander on domestic operations law.
She also supports the ANGRC on various special projects to include investigations.
Col DeSpain was commissioned at Officer Training School, Lackland AFB, Texas, in 1989.
She served six years on active duty as squadron section commander in units ranging in size from 400 to 1,000 members and group executive officer at Seymour Johnson AFB, N.C., and Sheppard AFB, Texas.
In 1995, Col DeSpain transferred to the Oregon Air National Guard, Portland, Ore., when she left active duty to attend law school at Willamette University.
Positions she held include 142nd Fighter Wing Support Group Executive Officer and Services Flight Commander.
From 1999 through 2002, Col DeSpain lived and worked in Singapore and was assigned to PACAF, Air Force Reserve, CAT B, IMA.
Her projects included support to the joint staff of the Office of Defense Cooperation at the U.S.
Embassy, Singapore, and support to the active duty staff judge advocate, 497th Combat Training Squadron, Port of Singapore Authority Sembawang.
In 2000 and 2002, Col DeSpain was the U.S.
Department of Defense Representative for Asian Aerospace, the world’s third largest biannual air and trade show.
Upon her return to the states in 2002, Col DeSpain transferred to the 62d Airlift Wing, Office of the Staff Judge Advocate, McChord AFB, Wash.
In 2006, she served in CAT A Reserve as the Staff Judge Advocate, 939th Air Refueling Wing, Portland, Ore.
She deployed for four months to Aviano Italy and Camp Bondsteel, Kosovo, in 2007, working closely with the Army JAG Corps, State Department and Kosovar judiciary in developing their Rule of Law programs.
After nine years in the AF Reserve, Col DeSpain returned to the Oregon Air National Guard where she served as the 142 FW SJA for seven years.
In 2009 she deployed to support the 48 FW/JA, Lakenheath RAF, United Kingdom, for one month.
In 2013, began her three year assignment as ANG Assistant to the Command Staff Judge Advocate, PACAF.
Col DeSpain has been active in various national level leadership positions to include 1AF ANG/JA liaison, executive officer to ANG Assistant to the Air Combat Command SJA, and Air National Guard special victims’ council program initiative committee chair.
Col DeSpain has held various executive level and general counsel positions in the private sector where she specializes in change management, strategic planning and leading teams to increase profits.
She is admitted to practice law before the Supreme Court of Oregon and the United States Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces.
