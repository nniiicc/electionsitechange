Val Hoyle’s false attacks and tells voters plainly where Monique stands.
The ad opens with Hoyle’s own record.
Last year, Hoyle acknowledged failing to disclose 217 stock transactions on time, a violation of the federal STOCK Act, which requires members of Congress to promptly report their trades.
She paid a fine.
Rather than answer for it, Hoyle has spent this campaign misrepresenting Monique’s positions, including a recycled 2024 attack ad that prompted a cease and desist letter from the DeSpain campaign last month.
In “Broken Trust,” Monique speaks directly to voters about what she believes.
She supports Oregon’s vote by mail system.
She opposes a national abortion ban and believes women’s healthcare decisions belong with women.
And she will protect Oregon’s rivers and forests.
“Val Hoyle broke stock trading law over two hundred times — now she’s lying about me,” DeSpain says in the ad.
“Here’s the truth: I support vote by mail, because Oregonians support it.
I oppose a national abortion ban — women’s healthcare decisions belong with women, I’ll protect our rivers and forests.
Val Hoyle breaks the law.
Now she’s breaking your trust.
I’m Monique DeSpain.
I approve this message because you deserve the truth.” “Broken Trust” begins airing today.
About Monique DeSpain Monique DeSpain is a retired U.S.
Air Force Colonel, attorney, and mother running for Congress in Oregon’s 4th District.
After 30 years of military service, she has dedicated herself to public service in Oregon, focusing on restoring a prosperous economy, addressing the state’s affordability crisis, and reducing the regulatory and tax burden on small businesses and working families.
