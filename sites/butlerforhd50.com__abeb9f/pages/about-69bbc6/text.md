Meet Tommy
Colorado Born & Raised, University of Northern Colorado Class of 2012 (Go Bears!)
Greeley City Councilman—Serving Ward 1 2019-2026
Co-Founder and Owner of the Greeley Bagel Company
Greeley-Weld Habitat for Humanity Board Member
Commissioner 19th Judicial District Commission on Judicial Performance
After graduating from the University of Northern Colorado, my wife Molly and I decided to call Greeley our home because we love this community.
We bought our first house here because this is where we wanted to put down roots.
I decided to run for the Greeley City Council because my neighbors deserve someone that will fight for them.
In my six years of service, I have brought long-overdue infrastructure funding to East Greeley.
I have fought to increase funding for affordable housing in Greeley, which has led to great developments like Star Rise Apartments and Hope Springs.
It is time to continue that fight at the state level.
It would be an honor to earn your vote.
