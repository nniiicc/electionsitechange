Vice Chair of the Libertarian Party of Texas Lifelong Montgomery County Resident IT Project Manager Champion for Individual Liberty Join the Movement Donate Now Meet Jessi Cowart I’m Jessi Cowart, a lifelong resident of Montgomery County and candidate for Texas House District 15.
As Vice Chair of the Libertarian Party of Texas, I’ve spent years standing up for Texans who feel unheard by the two-party system.
My roots here run deep — three generations of my family have called this community home since my grandfather built his first house here in 1970.
My husband, Bobby, and I are both graduates of Oak Ridge High School, where we met, and our children are second-generation War Eagles.
I’m also an IT Project Manager, which means I understand what it takes to cut costs, balance budgets, and manage priorities efficiently — skills Texas desperately needs in government.
I’m not running to add to the noise — I’m running to bring balance back to Austin with a voice of reason, respect, and liberty.
