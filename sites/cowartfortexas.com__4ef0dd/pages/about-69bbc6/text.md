Rooted in Texas, Driven by Community
I’m Jessi Cowart, a lifelong resident of Montgomery County and candidate for Texas House District 15.
My family has called this community home for three generations — since my grandfather built his house here in 1970, before The Woodlands was even founded.
My husband, Bobby, and I both graduated from Oak Ridge High School, where we met.
Today, our children are second-generation graduates, and that deep connection to this community drives my commitment to serve.
As a Project Manager by profession, I’ve built a career on cutting costs, improving efficiency, and solving problems with integrity.
Those same principles guide how I’ll lead — with fiscal responsibility, transparency, and respect for every taxpayer.

What makes Jessi different from other candidates?
Unlike career politicians, Jessi comes from a background in IT project management, where results, efficiency, and teamwork matter.
She’s not driven by party loyalty or personal gain — she’s motivated by a genuine desire to make government work smarter, not bigger.
As Vice Chair of the Libertarian Party of Texas, Jessi brings a balanced approach — combining fiscal responsibility with compassion for individuals and families.

What does the Libertarian Party stand for?
The Libertarian Party stands for maximum freedom and minimum government.
We believe individuals should have the right to make their own choices — as long as they don’t harm others.
That means fiscal responsibility, personal liberty, free markets, and peaceful, voluntary cooperation.
Jessi’s campaign embodies these values — because freedom, when paired with responsibility, works best for everyone.
