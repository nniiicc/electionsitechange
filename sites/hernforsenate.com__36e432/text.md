Proud Conservative Meet Kevin Born on an Air Force Base, Kevin Hern gained an early appreciation for the sacrifices that military families make.
In high school, Kevin earned an Architectural Drafting Certificate in concurrent Technical enrollment, which he used to work and pay for his Engineering degree.
Later in life, he earned his MBA.
Fighting for Oklahoma Promises Kept Since taking office, Kevin has been hard at work supporting and advancing President Trump’s Make America Great Again agenda.
On The Issues Protecting Conservative Values Each day, Kevin is fighting to secure real results and deliver for the people of Oklahoma.
Below are some of the major promises he's kept in Congress: Support our Veterans Cosponsored H.R.299 and H.R.550 to honor and recognize Blue Water Navy and WWII Merchant Mariners veterans.
Support a Balanced Budget Cosponsored H.J.Res.22 for a balanced budget amendment to address national debt and curb spending.
Secure the Border Voted for H.R.695 to fund border wall and cosponsored H.R.768 to defund sanctuary campuses.
Put Americans Back to Work Advocating for small business capital access and opposing policies that harm free enterprise.
Protect Religious Liberty Cosponsored H.Res.637 to protect Christmas symbols and opposed removing “so help me God” from oath.
Protect Life Cosponsored HR 369, HR 20, HR 616, and HR 962 to protect human life and limit abortion funding.
Protect Freedom of Speech Opposed H.R.1 to limit federal overreach, protect free speech, and state election control; cosponsored H.R.949.
Protect 2nd Amendment Rights Cosponsored H.R.38 for concealed carry reciprocity; voted against H.R.8 and H.R.1112 to protect Second Amendment rights.
Counter China Introduced the Promoting Responsible Oversight to Eliminate Communist Teachings for Our Kids (PROTECT Our Kids) Act, legislation to prohibit federal funding through the Department of Education for any school which continues to host a Confucius Classroom.
