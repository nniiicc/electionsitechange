About Pete Karas
Meet Pete Karas
Third Party Candidate for Wisconsin Secretary of State
Pete Karas is a small business owner, former Racine City Council member, and lifelong Wisconsinite running for Secretary of State to give voters something they haven’t had in decades: a real alternative to the two-party system.
Born and raised in Racine, Pete is the youngest of five children.
He graduated from Horlick High School and earned his degree from UW–LaCrosse.
Today, he lives in Muskego and is a proud father to two adult children, Halley and Brad, and a grandfather.
Pete has worked in both the public and private sectors, from managing teams at an insurance firm to launching and running his own business.
He is also an avid outdoorsman and a die-hard fan of the Brewers and the Badgers.
Pete has never voted for a Democrat or a Republican for president.
He believes the two-party system is not just broken but fundamentally anti-democratic.
Both major parties rely on the same corporate donors and protect the same political establishment.
They claim to oppose each other, but behind the scenes, they maintain a system that limits real voter choice and blocks third-party candidates from gaining ground.
Pete knows voters deserve more voices and more choices.
He is running as a third-party candidate with the Green Party because the party rejects corporate influence and fights for a government that is transparent, accountable, and people-powered.
The Green Party has consistently worked to expand ballot access, protect voting rights, and challenge the dominance of the two-party system.
Pete’s campaign is about staying true to his values and standing up for the thousands of Wisconsinites who are ready for political reform.
Pete saw the system’s barriers firsthand during the effort to get Jill Stein on the Wisconsin ballot, when both Democrats and Republicans tried to block a legitimate third-party candidate.
That experience confirmed what Pete already knew, the political establishment is not afraid of losing to each other.
They are afraid of losing their grip on the system.
The office of Secretary of State in Wisconsin is largely administrative, not legislative.
It should be focused on serving the public, supporting small businesses, improving civic access, and managing essential services.
This is a role that demands competence, experience, and accountability, not party loyalty.
Pete has the leadership skills and administrative background to do the job with professionalism and integrity.
This race is one of the few statewide elections in the country where a third-party candidate can win.
It is a rare opportunity for voters to safely send a message that they are ready to challenge the two-party monopoly and build something better.
Pete’s campaign is organized, growing, and powered by everyday people who want to take politics out of the hands of the few and return it to the many.
Vote Pete Karas for Secretary of State and help move Wisconsin toward a future where elections are fair, choices are real, and democracy works for everyone — not just the parties at the top.
