Volunteer Low-Effort Ways to Support Pete Karas’ Campaign You don’t have to knock on doors or make phone calls to make a difference.
If you believe the two-party system is broken and want to see a third-party candidate win statewide office in Wisconsin, there are plenty of quick, easy ways to help spread the word about Pete Karas’ campaign for Secretary of State.
Every action below can be done from home, on your phone, or while going about your day.
The goal is simple: get more people talking about Pete and about breaking the two-party duopoly.

Record a Short Video: “Why I’m Voting for Pete” Post it as a Reel, TikTok, or Story.

High authenticity.
Big impact.
