Looking to enroll in a 2-4 years Maryland College or University as a full-time student?
For those living in Maryland District, 35A you may qualify for a scholarship.
To find out if you live in District 35 A, click here.
The application process is now on-line.
Please contact our office at scholarshipdelegate35A@gmail.com for information on how to receive the link to the application.
The application period will run from the beginning of session in January till April 15.
Please Note
You may use this scholarship at an out-of-state school if your major is not available at any Maryland institution.
In order to verify your major as unique, you must complete the Unique Major Application and submit it to MHEC for approval.
The application can be found on the MHEC website https://mhec.maryland.gov.
The approval you receive from MHEC can be forwarded separately.</em
Please do not wait for final approval before submitting your delegation scholarship application.
You will still need to submit your scholarship application with the other required documentation by the April 15 deadline.
