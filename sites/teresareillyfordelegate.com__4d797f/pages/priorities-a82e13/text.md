As your Delegate, I will focus on effective solutions that work for the citizens of Maryland, including families, farmers, and small businesses.
My conservative principles are a reflection of my respect for fellow working people and their rights as guaranteed by the United States Constitution.
I will facilitate greater openness and responsiveness in State Government, citizen inclusion, and fiscal responsibility in fulfilling the duties of public office.
I will enact laws that protect the interest of Marylanders.
I will fight for:
- Balancing the budget through less spending (not more taxes)
- Defending the constitutional right to bear arms, which is a cornerstone to keeping our freedoms as Americans
- Reducing crime by enforcing existing laws, not creating new laws that infringe on our liberties
- Stopping overregulation on our farmers & small businesses
- Creating tax incentives for farmers & local businesses to grow jobs in our communities
- Directing public school resources to the classroom
