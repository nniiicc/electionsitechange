As I listen to the concerns of residents in our district, I’m convinced this is an essential step to ensure we fully understand the implications of data centers and put necessary safeguards in place.

Every community is different, and that’s why I believe we need practical, flexible policies that give local leaders the tools to address their own housing needs instead of single-solution mandates from Lansing.

View more Local Economies I'll work to put more state resources behind Michigan’s Main Street businesses by requiring large corporations receiving state aid to invest in Michigan-based small businesses.
We’ll expand access to financing, technical support, and growth opportunities while creating a one-stop Office of Small Business Growth to cut red tape, connect entrepreneurs with resources, and help small businesses create good-paying jobs.
