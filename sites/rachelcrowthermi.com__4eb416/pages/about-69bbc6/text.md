As a current Courtland Township Trustee and longtime community volunteer, Rachel has built a record of thoughtful leadership grounded in transparency, accountability, and collaboration .
Her service with the Rockford Area Community Foundation, Rockford’s Got Talent fundraiser, the Courtland Township Parks Committee, Experience Grand Rapids, and the Kent County Lodging Association, along with being named the 2016 Joe Tomaselli Hotelier of the Year, reflects a lifelong commitment to strengthening local economies and building vibrant communities.
With deep roots in the community, Rachel believes in bringing people together to build a strong future for everyone.
