Meet Vanessa
Vanessa has served on the Redmond City Council for nearly 7 years.
She brings decades of public, nonprofit, and private-sector experience advancing equity, affordability, and climate action.
As a working parent and conservation leader, she has championed childcare, housing, environmental protection, transportation connections, a vibrant economy, and inclusive growth locally and regionally.
