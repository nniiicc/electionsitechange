Standards for Law Enforcement Identification Citizens have the right to know if they’re being stopped by a Law Enforcement Officer or if they’re being kidnapped by a masked man with a van.
Requiring Local, State, and Federal Law Enforcement Officers in Vermont clearly identify themselves and not wear facial coverings or disguises (outside of certain hazardous situations) should be the standard.
This is safer for citizens as they will now know who is talking to them and how they should respond.
This is also safer for officers as many Vermonters carry guns and will not hesitate to defend themselves from a kidnapper.
