The People’s Health Trust
A 25-Year Plan for Healthy Alaskans
Goal
Ensure every Alaskan can afford quality healthcare regardless of where they live, while reducing long-term costs instead of simply spending more money.
1.
Create the Alaska People’s Health Trust
Instead of healthcare being driven primarily by insurance companies, create an independent public trust that purchases healthcare on behalf of Alaskans.
The Trust would:
Negotiate statewide prices for hospitals and prescription drugs.
Operate independently from political influence.
Be governed by healthcare professionals, business leaders, workers, and citizens.
Publish every contract and financial report online.
2.
No One Goes Broke Getting Sick
Work toward eliminating:
Deductibles
Co-pays
Surprise medical bills
People should make healthcare decisions based on what keeps them healthy, not what they can afford that month.
3.
Expand Primary Care Across Alaska
Invest heavily in:
Rural clinics
Mobile medical teams
Telemedicine
Dental care
Vision care
Behavioral health
Every community deserves access to a primary care provider without flying hundreds of miles whenever possible.
4.
Grow Alaska’s Healthcare Workforce
Offer scholarships and loan forgiveness for Alaskans who become:
Nurses
Doctors
Physician assistants
Mental health counselors
EMTs
Dentists
Pharmacists
In exchange for several years serving in Alaska communities.
5.
Lower Prescription Drug Costs
The State should:
Negotiate bulk purchasing.
Expand use of safe generic medications.
Increase price transparency.
Create an emergency medication assistance program.
6.
Invest in Prevention
Pay for prevention because it costs less than crisis care.
Increase funding for:
Diabetes prevention
Cancer screening
Heart disease
Mental health
Addiction treatment
Nutrition
Exercise programs
Every dollar spent preventing illness saves multiple dollars in future healthcare costs.
7.
Modernize Emergency Services
Expand:
Air ambulance coordination
Rural paramedic training
Tele-emergency medicine
Behavioral health crisis teams
Reduce emergency room overcrowding by helping patients receive the right care in the right setting.
8.
Increase Transparency
Require hospitals, clinics, and insurers to publicly report:
Prices
Patient outcomes
Wait times
Infection rates
Patient satisfaction
Competition works better when people know what they’re paying for.
9.
Reduce Waste Before Raising Taxes
Require annual performance audits to identify:
Administrative duplication
Fraud
Unnecessary paperwork
Inefficient purchasing
Savings should be reinvested directly into patient care.
10.
A Sustainable Funding Model
Rather than relying entirely on higher taxes:
Capture savings from reduced administrative costs.
Reduce preventable hospitalizations through preventive care.
Negotiate lower healthcare prices statewide.
Consider your proposed automation/AI contribution model, where businesses replacing large numbers of workers contribute to long-term public healthcare funding.
Seek every available federal matching dollar without surrendering Alaska’s decision-making authority.
Expected Results
Within 10 years:
More doctors and nurses practicing in Alaska.
Shorter wait times.
Lower insurance costs.
Better rural access.
Improved mental healthcare.
Lower prescription drug prices.
Better health outcomes.
Healthcare focused on patients instead of paperwork.
This plan also complements the priorities you’ve discussed throughout your campaign: investing in Alaska’s workforce, improving access in rural communities, demanding transparency, and planning beyond the next election cycle rather than the next budget cycle.
Alaska’s healthcare system is expensive partly because of geography and workforce shortages, so addressing those root causes is just as important as changing how care is financed.
