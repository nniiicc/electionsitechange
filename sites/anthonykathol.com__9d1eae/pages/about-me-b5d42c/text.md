South Dakota District 27 State Senate
A leader who delivers with passion and proven results.
From serving our nation to engineering healthier communities to educating the next generation, my professional and civic life has been guided by a commitment to service, responsibility, and faith.
As a public servant, educator, small-business owner, and commissioned lay minister, I have developed an understanding of the challenges and opportunities facing District 27.
My candidacy is founded on shared values and common-sense solutions.
I am committed to strengthening our rural health care systems and public infrastructure; ensuring the safety and security of our communities by supporting first responders and local law enforcement while upholding the constitutional right to defend oneself and one’s property; advancing an education system that prioritizes student achievement and accountability; upholding the sanctity and value of life from the womb to the tomb; and fostering sustainable economic growth that is transparent, protects our agricultural and ancestoral heritage, respects private property rights and tribal sovereignty, and preserves local control.
With your support, I will work diligently to represent District 27.
Integrity, transparency, and accountability are the pillars that undergird my candidacy, and will continue to do so if elected.
As a former public servant and registered professional engineer, I will continue to be a good steward of government resources by practicing fiscal restraint and eliminating waste, fraud, and abuse of your hard-earned tax dollars.
Together, we can build a stronger, more resilient community and secure a prosperous future for our families.
Committed to responsible leadership, I respectfully ask for your vote as we move forward united in purpose and for a brighter future.
