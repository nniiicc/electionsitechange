It was a busy day on the campaign trail!
This morning, I got up early to do some sign-waving in the early morning fog at the entrance to Bennett County Elementary School.
I saw lots of happy faces as I waved to parents and students on their way to school.
Next, I drove to Rapid City to be interviewed by KOTA Territory News reporter Karli West at 1 PM.
It was a great interview, and I felt good about how it went!
The interview will air during the 5:30 PM news broadcast on Tuesday, October 13.
Livestream broadcast here: https://www.kotatv.com/news/ Be sure to tune in, and let me know what you think!
After the interview, I stopped at Dinosaur Park and drove down Skyline Drive to take in the views.
I hadn't been there in years, so it was nice to revisit the area.
I'll be using some of these photos in an upcoming Get Out the Vote campaign video.
Kathol for District 27 at Dinosaur Park in Rapid City (October 12, 2026) Finally, I headed back to Martin, checking on my campaign signs along the way.
It was dark by the time I reached my last signs along Highway 18 at Gordon Junction.
Thankfully, they were all holding up well against the wind.
I made a few minor repairs, but overall, the signs are standing strong!
I finally pulled into my driveway around 8:30 PM.
A special thank-you to everyone hosting my campaign signs!
I appreciate you keeping an eye on them throughout this vast district.
Running a campaign across an area the size of Connecticut and Rhode Island combined is no small undertaking, and your support makes a real difference.
Have a great week, everyone, and thank you for following my campaign!
