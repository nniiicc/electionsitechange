One of the Best Days on the Campaign Trail
Today was one of the best days on the campaign trail, serving as a reminder of why I enjoy living in this small-town community.
I started my day early by attending morning Mass.
I then spent the rest of the day putting up yard signs in my hometown of Martin and enjoyed meeting and visiting with supporters who agreed to host one of my orange campaign signs.
I ended the day with a hamburger steak supper at Susan's H&H Bar and Grill, where I met more supporters, and then finished with a sunset walk while praying my rosary.
I have steadily built a coalition of Independents, Republicans, and Democrats who are supporting my campaign.
They see a candidate who isn't seeking political power for himself, but who wants to represent and serve all the voters in District 27.
As we head into October, I will attend bazaars, smokers, festivals, and cattle sales to meet as many voters as I can.
As a candidate, I continue to be blessed by the opportunity to hear your stories, concerns, and ideas.
Those conversations continue to motivate me as we enter the homestretch of this campaign.
If you live in the Martin area and would like to host one of my campaign signs, please contact me at Kathol4D27@goldenwest.net.
Please note that I will be picking up all of my campaign signs after the election.
Take care and God bless.
