My Pledge to the Voters of District 27
I, Anthony Kathol, hereby pledge that if I should be elected as your state senator, I will do my best to write, call, text, video chat, or send an email reply to address your concerns.
It is not only fitting that I reply to the voters of District 27 because they deserve to be heard, but also it is common courtesy to respond to my constituents, even those who may not have elected me into office.
I may not always agree with my constituent’s position, nor will I have all the answers that the voters are seeking, but I am willing to listen, to learn, and to find common sense solutions to complex problems that face our district and our state.
January 20, 2026
