For three weeks, I have been busy putting up my campaign signs, driving 1,347 miles while taking in the beautiful scenery across District 27.
Today, I finished putting up my last jumbo sign, with a few yard signs peppered in along the way.
See GPS map.
I have outhustled my opponents by working hard to make sure my campaign signs are visible throughout the district.
My signs can be seen from Oglala to Red Shirt, from Belvidere to New Underwood, and along US-18, State Highways 73 and 44, and BIA Routes 2 and 27 corridors, as well as many points in between.
Putting up signs is, by far, one of the hardest parts of a campaign—especially when you are doing it by yourself in one of South Dakota's largest political districts geographically—an area the size of Connecticut and Rhode Island.
However, I have received many comments from voters who have seen my signs, and I am not done yet.
I still have many yard signs to put up.
Imagine the possibilities—and the work I can accomplish as your state senator—if I am willing to put in this much time and effort to earn your vote.
My campaign signs may be pumpkin orange, but after Halloween, I need you to get out and vote Red — Vote Anthony Kathol for District 27 State Senate!
