ABOUT
Senator Randy Price is running to serve his third term in the State Senate for District 13 which covers Clay, Cleburne, Randolph, Chambers and Lee counties.
While serving in the State Senate, Senator Price has improved our roads and infrastructure, supported volunteer fire departments, protected our gun rights, the right to life and our conservative values.
Senator Price is the Chair of the Tourism Committee and serves on the Agriculture, Conservation and Forestry; Banking and Insurance; Confirmations; Education Policy; Transportation and Energy; Finance and Taxation General Fund, and Joint Transportation Committees.
Randy Price is married to Oline, and they have two sons: Cameron and Hunter.
Hunter is married to Lisa, and they have two children, Brooklyn and Walton.
He and his wife are members of the First Methodist Church of Opelika.
