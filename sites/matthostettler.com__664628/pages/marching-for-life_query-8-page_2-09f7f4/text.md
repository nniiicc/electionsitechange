Every mother, like every pre-born child, is fearfully and wonderfully made and loved by their Creator.
It is our job to communicate that truth with all the grace and compassion that the Good News exemplifies.
They must know that they are valuable, they are loved, and must be protected.
It is my hope that the law of the land will soon reflect the Truth.
