Meet Judge Bell
Judge George Bell has dedicated over two decades to the law in North Carolina.
Serving as a Superior Court Judge since 2019, he has presided over more than 100 felony jury trials, including high-stakes cases involving first-degree murder and violent crimes.
A Cornell-educated leader and former wrestling captain, Judge Bell brings discipline, integrity, and legal excellence to the bench.
Education & Legal Background
- B.S. in Business Management, Cornell University (Wrestling Team, Fraternity President)
- J.D., Regent University School of Law (ABA Moot Court Best Brief in the Nation)
- Judicial District 26C – Superior Court Judge since 2019
