Michael Sheperek For Colorado State House District 49 SheperekForColorado.Com Bio Michael Sheperek is a Coloradan.
Raised in the small mining town of Rico on Colorado’s West Slope, Mike moved to Boulder and graduated from CU with degrees in Biology and Electrical Engineering.
He built a successful career as an expert in memory storage systems, earning over 40 U.S. patents in data storage and algorithmic development.
Mike was a lead IP (intellectual property) developer for several prominent technology companies.
A dedicated family man, a commercially rated aircraft pilot, musician, hiker, biker, cook, and mechanic — and is even considering building his own experimental airplane.
As a longtime resident of Boulder and now Larimer counties, Mike wants to bring back aQordability, sustainable tax policy and preserve the liberties that are being eroded by today’s one-party rule.
This happens with cogent policies, properly debated issues and balance in the Colorado State Legislature.
