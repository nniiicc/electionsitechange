Early Voting Information for the June 23rd, 2026 Primary Election
Over the course of nine days, from Saturday, June 13th to Sunday, June 21st, all eligible Democratic voters may vote at any of the following early voting locations in the county they reside.
Hours vary by day and by county
This means if you live in Monroe County, you are able to vote at any of the 15 Monroe County early voting locations listed below.
If you live in Ontario County and wish to vote early, you can do so at any of the three Ontario County early voting locations listed below.
By voting early, you get to decide when voting is most convenient for you!
