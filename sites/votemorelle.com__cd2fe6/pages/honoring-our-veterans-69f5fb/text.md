DONATE HONORING OUR VETERANS As the son of a Korean War veteran, Joe grew up understanding that the freedoms we enjoy in this country come from the sacrifice of brave patriots who wore the uniform and defended our nation.
When servicemembers return to civilian life, Joe firmly believes we have an obligation to ensure they have access to the care, support, and benefits they have earned.
In Congress, Joe previously served on the House Armed Services Committee, where he worked to ensure active-duty military personnel and veterans have access to the resources and support they were promised.
He helped pass the Keeping our PACT Act which has now helped over 1.5 million veterans who experienced toxic exposure access care.
Joe continues to lead efforts to strengthen services for veterans in crisis, expand and improve health care for veterans and their families, and ensure our Armed Forces have the tools they need to keep our nation safe.
In Congress, Joe has co-sponsored legislation including: Veteran Caregiver Re-education, Re-employment, and Retirement (Veteran Caregiver RRR) Act Would give caregivers compensated by the U.S.
Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC) the support and access to benefits they deserve.
The Commitment to Veterans Support and Outreach Act Would allow the VA to provide grants to states and Tribal governments to improve outreach and assistance so veterans and their families are fully informed about available benefits and programs.
The Elizabeth Dole Home and Community Based Services for Veterans and Caregivers Act Would expand home and community-based services for veterans with disabilities and older veterans, helping more veterans receive care in their homes and communities.
The Dental Care for Veterans Act Would expand access to dental care for more veterans enrolled in the VA health care system.
The Patient Advocate Tracker Act Would improve transparency and accountability within the VA by strengthening how veteran concerns about health care services are tracked and addressed.
