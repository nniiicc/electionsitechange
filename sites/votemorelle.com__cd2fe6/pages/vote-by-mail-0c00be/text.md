DONATE VOTE BY MAIL Early Vote by Mail Early Vote by Mail allows registered voters to apply for an early vote by mail ballot without needing to provide an excuse.
Voting by mail is the most accessible way to vote, and you are able to apply online!
For more information about Early Vote by Mail, please visit the New York State Board of Elections Website !
Absentee Voting You may qualify for an absentee ballot if you meet any of the following criteria: you are absent from your county (or, for New York City residents, absent from the five boroughs) on Election Day; you are unable to appear at the polls due to a temporary or permanent illness or disability; you are the primary caregiver for someone who is ill or physically disabled; you are a resident or patient of a Veterans Health Administration hospital; or you are in jail or prison for reasons other than a felony conviction, including those awaiting trial or serving a sentence for a misdemeanor. ​ For more information about Absentee Voting, please visit the New York State Board of Elections Website !
