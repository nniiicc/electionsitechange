02 / No backing down Taking on the Big Fights.
When powerful institutions need to be held accountable, Pat Burke doesn't look the other way.
He Took on Utility Monopolies Pat Burke is taking NYSEG to court to stop a massive rate hike, standing up for hardworking Western New Yorkers who are sick of being squeezed by a monopoly utility that keeps demanding more of their money.
He Challenged the Diocese Pat Burke was the first elected official to call for Bishop Malone’s resignation and has never stopped fighting for survivors of abuse at the hands of the Catholic Church, demanding accountability from powerful institutions and justice for those they failed.
He Stood with Survivors Advocated for stronger statewide investigations of institutional and historical sexual abuse, including alleged cover-ups and failures to report.
Protecting vulnerable people Protecting Survivors of Assault & Abuse.
From strengthening survivors' rights to supporting local services, Burke's record includes legislation, advocacy and funding for people affected by abuse and assault.
Strengthening Survivors' Rights Child Victims Act & Adult Survivors Act Burke supported passage of the Child Victims Act and Adult Survivors Act, expanding opportunities for survivors to seek civil justice.
Closing Gaps in the Law Rape Is Rape Act Helped advance the Rape Is Rape Act, updating New York law to recognize more forms of nonconsensual sexual penetration as rape.
Standing with Survivors Demanding Diocese Accountability Burke was the first elected official to call for Bishop Malone's resignation and has continued pressing for accountability for abuse and institutional failures.
Supporting Survivors Locally Investing in the Family Justice Center Directed discretionary funding to the Family Justice Center to support its work with people experiencing domestic violence and abuse.
Personal Safety & Confidence Funding Women's Self-Defense Classes Supported self-defense classes for women through discretionary funding, helping make practical safety training accessible.
Protecting LGBT Youth The PENCE Act In Erie County, Burke helped ban the harmful practice of conversion therapy on minors—part of his broader work to protect vulnerable young people.
Institutional Accountability Institutional Sexual Assault Accountability Act Burke proposed the Institutional Sexual Assault Accountability Act (ISAAA) to expand specialized State Police investigations of institutional and historical sexual abuse, including alleged cover-ups and failures to report.
This is proposed legislation, not an enacted law.
