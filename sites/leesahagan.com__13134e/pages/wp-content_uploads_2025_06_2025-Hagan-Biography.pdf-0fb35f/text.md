Representative Leesa Hagan House District 156 Biography Leesa was born and raised in the great state of Georgia.
She spent most of her childhood in Riverdale just south of Atlanta.
However, in 2009, she and her husband Mike settled in Toombs County after deciding they wanted to raise their two young boys in a small town.
As the home of Mike’s maternal family for six generations, New Branch, just south of Lyons was an easy choice.
The hardworking, dedicated people of this community make it a great place to raise a family and call home.
Leesa and Mike met at the University of Georgia and have been married since 1996.
They have two grown sons who are Eagle scouts and currently attend college.
All the Hagans are active members at Grace Community Presbyterian Church in Lyons and are involved in the community.
Leesa is a proud member of the Lyons Lions Club, the Greater Vidalia Chamber of Commerce, and the Toombs County Republican Party of which she was the Chairman from 2019 – 2021.
After graduating from UGA with a degree in education, Leesa began a teaching career that included 13 years of classroom experience in public, private, and home school.
Because she has a passion for small businesses and understands the vital role they play in the local economy, Leesa founded a website design and SEO consulting company that specializes in helping small business owners reach more of their customers online.
Leesa was elected to the Georgia House of Representatives in the 2021 special election.
She serves the 156th district which includes all of Toombs, Montgomery, and Wheeler counties and portions of Tattnall, Telfair, and Ben Hill counties.
Leesa currently serves on the following House standing committees: • Rural Development, Chairman • Appropriations, Education subcommittee • Agriculture and Consumer Affairs • Natural Resources and Environment • Industry and Labor • Transportation Leesa has been selected to participate in several leadership programs since her election including The State Legislative Leaders Foundation Emerging Legislative Leaders Program at the University of Virginia (2022), The University of Georgia’s Legislative Leadership Institute (2023), Republican Leadership for Georgia (2024), GOPAC’s Emerging Leaders Summit (2024), and the White House State Leadership Forum (2025).
