Donate Now Important Issues for Arizona Opportunity and Prosperity My highest priority is creating long-term prosperity for Arizona citizens.
I believe economic and educational opportunity, ensuring safety and security, and the free exercise of individual rights provide the best foundation for individual success.
The Economy Fiscal restraint, responsible tax policy and regulatory reform are key to combating inflationary pressures and fostering economic growth.
Over the long-term, expanding adult career training, attracting growth industries, and building a business environment that encourages innovation create stepping stones that lead to individual prosperity.
Health Care Healthcare decisions should be made by patients and doctors, not disinterested bureaucracies.
Innovative free-market solutions control costs, provide state-of-the-art care and rapidly adapt to public health emergencies.
In any healthcare system, affordability, access to medical facilities, and individualized and timely care are critical and my primary focus.
Election Integrity A well-regulated election gives the public confidence in the political process and encourages voter participation.
I support election reforms that enhance transparency and security, maximize voter convenience, and count every legal vote.
Pro Life and Parental Rights I believe families are the foundation of a great nation, and parents are the best advocates for their children.
I will always fight for legislation that respects life and protects parental rights.
Veterans We owe a debt of gratitude to our veterans for their unwavering service to our country.
I support programs that improve veteran healthcare services, reduce suicide and homeless rates, and provide career and vocational education opportunities for our state's veterans.
Law Enforcement The men and women who work in law enforcement dedicate themselves to serving our communities and risk their lives on our behalf.
They deserve our respect, but also the resources they need to protect themselves and every Arizonan.
The Border An open border is national security risk that also jeopardizes the health and safety of Arizona residents.
Under the previous administration, fentanyl overdose became the leading cause of death for teenagers in Pima county while families across the state were devastated by illegal immigration's economic, criminal, or health impacts.
I stand by my pledge to oppose sanctuary cities and to support state measures to mitigate the damage that was caused by a porous border.
