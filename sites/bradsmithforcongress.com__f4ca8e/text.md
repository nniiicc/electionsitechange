About Brad The Issues Family-Supporting Jobs Raise minimum wage, expand apprenticeships, and reward companies that create and expand jobs right here at home.
Affordable, Usable Healthcare as a Right Lower costs, protect coverage for pre-existing conditions, and keep care close to home, from rural communities to large metros.
Education & Skills for Every Path Increase access to tech schools, college, trades, apprenticeships, and lifelong learning so every worker can thrive.
Protect Our Water & Energy Future Protect our water, energy grid, and local communities by requiring transparency, strong safeguards, and accountability for massive data center developments before they move forward.
Stand with Workers & Skilled Trades Restore prevailing wage, strengthen Project Labor Agreements, and protect the right to organize.
Safe and Sensible Gun Policies to Protect Our Communities As a responsible gun owner, I support common-sense safety measures such as universal background checks, red flag laws, and secure storage.
These policies protect our children while respecting the Second Amendment.
Keep Family Farms Strong Fair milk pricing, year-round legal workforce solutions, broadband on every farm, and policies that level the playing field so family farms can compete and thrive.
Protect Women’s Medical Rights We’ll defend reproductive freedom and ensure women, not politicians, have the right to make their own healthcare choices.
Protect LGBTQ+ Rights Defend marriage equality, strengthen protections against discrimination and stand against political attacks on LGBTQ+ people.
Everyone deserves the freedom to live safely, openly, and authentically.
Sustainable & Clean Energy for Our Future We’ll grow Wisconsin’s renewable energy economy, creating good-paying local jobs while protecting our land and lakes for the next generation.
That means investing in solar and wind, promoting clean water standards, and ensuring WI leads in clean energy transition.
A House That Represents the People Uncap the House so every vote counts, reduce the influence of big money, and rebalance power so everyday people are heard in Washington.

Wisconsin Education Association Council WEAC focuses on improving members’ working lives through collective action, legislation, professional development, and public relations, while also advocating for children and the strength of public education throughout Wisconsin.
Wisconsin AFL-CIO The Wisconsin State AFL-CIO represents union members in the state of Wisconsin, and fights for social and economic justice for all people.
A democratically elected body dedicated to representing the interests of all working people at the state and local level.
Service Employers International Union Wisconsin The Service Employees International Union (SEIU), is a union of about 2 million diverse members in healthcare, the public sector and property services who believe in and fight for our Vision for a Just Society: where all workers are valued and all people respected.
SMART Local 18 SMART (the International Association of Sheet Metal, Air, Rail, and Transportation Workers) is a 21st-century labor Union dedicated to protecting and improving the quality of life for men and women working in sheet metal, shipyards, manufacturing and transportation across the U.S. and Canada.
IBEW Local 494 IBEW Local 494 is a labor union representing approximately 2700 members.
Local 494 is committed to improving the working conditions and providing employment to all electrical workers in the counties that it serves: Milwaukee, Waukesha, Washington, Ozaukee, Fond du Lac, and Sheboygan.
ATU Local 998 The Amalgamated Transit Union – the largest labor union representing transit and allied workers in the U.S. and Canada – fights for the interests of its hard-working members and promotes mass transit.
Local 998 represents bus operators and mechanics in Milwaukee and Sheboygan.
Sierra Club Sierra Club is the largest and most influential grassroots environmental organization in the country.
For more than 130 years, we’ve been working to promote clean energy, safeguard the health of our communities, protect wildlife, and preserve our remaining wild places through grassroots activism, public education, lobbying, and legal action.
National Organization for Women The National Organization for Women is dedicated to its multi-issue and multi-strategy approach to women’s rights, and is the largest organization of feminist grassroots activists in the United States.
NOW has hundreds of chapters and hundreds of thousands of members and activists in all 50 states and the District of Columbia.
Our Wisconsin Revolution Our Wisconsin Revolution is a homegrown movement started here in our state by people living here.
Our Wisconsin Revolution is about making Wisconsin a true democracy — with a government & economy that are of, by and for the people.

BAC endorses candidates committed to reforms including congressional term limits, banning congressional stock trading, overturning Citizens United, limiting lobbying influence, and strengthening ethics and accountability in Washington.
Moms Demand Action Moms Demand Action is a grassroots movement of Americans fighting for public safety measures that can protect people from gun violence.
We know that gun violence is preventable, and we’re committed to doing what it takes to keep families safe.
