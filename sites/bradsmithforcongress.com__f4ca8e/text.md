We need Brad Smith in Congress fighting for hard-working Wisconsin families.
I’m Brad Smith 👋
I’m a dad, veteran, and business builder.
I’m a Democrat running for Congress to deliver good-paying jobs, affordable healthcare, training that leads to real opportunity, and to protect the Wisconsin we love.
The Issues
- Family-Supporting Jobs Raise minimum wage, expand apprenticeships, and reward companies that create and expand jobs right here at home.
- Affordable, Usable Healthcare as a Right Lower costs, protect coverage for pre-existing conditions, and keep care close to home, from rural communities to large metros.
- Education & Skills for Every Path Increase access to tech schools, college, trades, apprenticeships, and lifelong learning so every worker can thrive.
- Protect Our Water & Energy Future Protect our water, energy grid, and local communities by requiring transparency, strong safeguards, and accountability for massive data center developments before they move forward.
- Stand with Workers & Skilled Trades Restore prevailing wage, strengthen Project Labor Agreements, and protect the right to organize.
- Safe and Sensible Gun Policies to Protect Our Communities As a responsible gun owner, I support common-sense safety measures such as universal background checks, red flag laws, and secure storage.
These policies protect our children while respecting the Second Amendment.
- Keep Family Farms Strong Fair milk pricing, year-round legal workforce solutions, broadband on every farm, and policies that level the playing field so family farms can compete and thrive.
- Protect Women’s Medical Rights We’ll defend reproductive freedom and ensure women, not politicians, have the right to make their own healthcare choices.
- Protect LGBTQ+ Rights Defend marriage equality, strengthen protections against discrimination and stand against political attacks on LGBTQ+ people.
Everyone deserves the freedom to live safely, openly, and authentically.
- Sustainable & Clean Energy for Our Future We’ll grow Wisconsin’s renewable energy economy, creating good-paying local jobs while protecting our land and lakes for the next generation.
That means investing in solar and wind, promoting clean water standards, and ensuring WI leads in clean energy transition.
- A House That Represents the People Uncap the House so every vote counts, reduce the influence of big money, and rebalance power so everyday people are heard in Washington.
