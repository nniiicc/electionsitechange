State Senator Jodi Habush Sinykin Endorses Brad Smith for Congress
Mequon, WI - June 12, 2026 — The Brad Smith for Congress campaign is proud to announce the endorsement of Wisconsin State Senator Jodi Habush Sinykin, a respected environmental advocate, attorney, and coalition builder known for her work protecting Wisconsin's water resources and communities.
Last week, the Smith campaign highlighted a series of endorsements from leaders and community voices across Wisconsin's 6th Congressional District, reflecting the growing coalition coming together behind Smith's campaign and its vision for new leadership in Washington.
"Brad Smith understands that protecting Wisconsin means protecting our water, our energy grid, and the communities that depend on them," said Habush Sinykin.
"He'll stand up to special interests and fight for sustainable, responsible growth that puts Wisconsin families first."
Habush Sinykin has built a career bringing together diverse stakeholders to solve complex challenges facing Wisconsin.
Before serving in the Wisconsin State Senate, she worked as an environmental attorney and advocate, helping advance efforts to protect Wisconsin's natural resources and strengthen safeguards for future generations.
At a time when Wisconsin communities are grappling with the impacts of large-scale energy and data center development, Habush Sinykin has been a leading voice for responsible planning, environmental stewardship, and ensuring local communities have a seat at the table when decisions affect their future.
"Jodi has spent her career standing up for Wisconsin communities and protecting the resources that make our state special," Smith said.
"She understands that economic growth and environmental stewardship are not competing priorities.
They're both essential to building a stronger future for Wisconsin families.
I'm honored to have her support."
The endorsement builds on the momentum generated last week, when supporters from across the district publicly joined the campaign's growing coalition of Democrats, independents, and community leaders united around bringing practical, people-focused leadership to Washington.
Throughout the campaign, Smith has emphasized the fundamentals that win elections: grassroots organizing, coalition-building, and earning the trust of voters through direct engagement.
The campaign has built one of the strongest volunteer networks in the district, reached thousands of voters through direct voter contact, and led all challengers to the incumbent in fundraising, with overwhelming support coming from individual donors within Wisconsin's 6th Congressional District.
"Building a winning coalition requires bringing people together around shared values and practical solutions," Smith said.
"Jodi's endorsement reflects exactly the kind of leadership and partnership that Wisconsin needs more of."
