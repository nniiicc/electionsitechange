Donate About My name is Keith Nolan, and I’m running as an Independent candidate for Tennessee House District 31 (Bledsoe, Sequatchie, Van Buren, and Rhea counties).
This campaign isn’t about my vision, it’s about yours.
I’m running to listen, to represent your values, and to defend the liberties guaranteed by the United States Constitution.
Transparency, accountability, personal responsibility, and real cooperation are the principles that guide me.
For the past 10 years, I’ve been an activist in the Liberty movement, fighting for smaller government, individual rights, and sound money.
Public service runs in my family, and growing up in the Chattanooga area instilled in me a deep respect for hard work and community.
Since 2020, Sequatchie County has been home.
As a working electrician, I know what it’s like to bust your knuckles earning a dollar only to watch it disappear to taxes and inflation.
I believe in free and open markets, peace through cooperation, and the simple truth that the people of District 31 know what’s best for their families and communities not distant bureaucrats.
If you’d like to know exactly where I stand on the issues that matter to you such as taxes, education, Second Amendment rights, economic growth, or anything else please don’t hesitate to reach out.
I’m always available at Keithnolan4tn@gmail.com .
Thank you for your time.
I’d be honored to earn your trust and your vote.
