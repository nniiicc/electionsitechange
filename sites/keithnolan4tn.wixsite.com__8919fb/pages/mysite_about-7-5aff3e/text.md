Tennessee First Agenda
My fellow Tennesseans of District 31, (Dunlap, Pikeville, Dayton, Spring City, and Spencer) I am proud to announce today that I am officially throwing my name in the hat to serve as your next Independent State Representative in the Tennessee House.
For too long, hardworking families in the Sequatchie Valley and beyond have watched their paychecks shrink while government grows bigger, more expensive, and more intrusive.
That ends now.
Here’s the bold, common-sense agenda I will fight for every single day in Nashville:
We will explore every responsible avenue to repeal the crushing burden of property taxes and put homeownership back within reach of every Tennessee family.
We will follow Florida’s lead and make gold and silver legal tender once again.
Giving you real money that holds its value, not fiat paper that politicians can print at will.
We will defend your God-given Second Amendment rights with no compromises, no apologies, and maximum protection under the law.
We will bring back home economics in our public schools so the next generation learns the life skills such as budgeting, cooking, personal finance that no standardized test can teach.
We will rip the wet blanket of overregulation off Tennessee’s hemp industry and unleash hundreds of high-paying jobs producing American-made concrete, insulation, biofuel, clothing, and countless other products that can be grown right here in District 31.
We will make state and local government services so lean, responsive, and customer-focused that you’ll swear you’re dealing with a private business, not a bureaucracy.
We will deliver the largest tax breaks in Tennessee history for our independent contractors, gig workers, and 1099 entrepreneurs, the backbone of our economy.
And we will pioneer direct primary care so you can see a doctor without begging an insurance company for permission or drowning in red tape.
District 31 is blessed with natural beauty, hardworking people, and unlimited potential.
But that potential will never be realized by more government, it will be realized by YOU: the farmers, the small-business owners, the families, and the dreamers who call this corner of Tennessee home.
I’m not running to grow government.
I’m running to get it out of your way and put YOU back in the driver’s seat.
In a divided time, real progress starts with what we agree on.
Both major parties’ platforms share solid ground on growing American manufacturing jobs, supporting small businesses, preparing kids with real workforce skills, protecting Medicare/Social Security, lowering drug costs, and caring for veterans.
For Bledsoe, Sequatchie, Rhea & Van Buren Counties, this means:
- Leveraging Watts Bar Nuclear and local supply chains for good-paying trades & manufacturing jobs that provide benefits.
- Expanding apprenticeships & vocational training so kids can build careers here at home.
- Strengthening rural healthcare access, lowering prescription prices, and delivering for our veterans & seniors.
As a TN Air National Guard member, small business owner, and Independent candidate, I’m focused on practical solutions that put **economics upstream** and deliver results for working families; not partisan gridlock.
Together, let’s make District 31 the freest, most prosperous, and most opportunity-rich district in the Volunteer State.
I ask for your vote, your voice, and your vision.
Let’s get to work.
God bless you, God bless District 31, and God bless the great state of Tennessee.
