About Benyde
Mexican born, Utah raised, living in South Jordan and passionate about my community.
I am here to give the people in District 48 a choice!
I grew up in South Jordan, left during college and now that I’m raising a family of my own decided to do so right here in this beautiful city of South Jordan.
I’m a former University educator.
A mother to the sweetest baby girl and spouse to the most loving and supportive husband.
I’m passionate about fair and equal education for our children, a sustainable future with clean air, infrastructure that is built to last, and housing that is attainable.
Because family starts at home, I vow to fight for affordable housing that has the family focus in mind, not just financial portfolios.
To help spread information on how to advocate for our community and show up when it matters most.
I encourage you to use your voice, go out and vote and stay informed.
