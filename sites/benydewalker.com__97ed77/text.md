Benyde is a longtime South Jordan resident, former educator, and devoted mother whose love for her family drives everything she does.
She is advocating for equal education regardless of wealth, affordable and fair housing, and clean air because every family deserves the same future she wants for her own.
Key Issues ❊
Equal Education regardless of wealth
For money intended for public school to remain within public schools.
Putting our focus back on our educators and giving them the support they need to continue helping shape the next generation.
01
Affordable and fair housing
"Affordable does not mean cheap.
It means accessible.
It means fair.
It means possible.", housing that serves families, not just financial portfolios.
By doing this we create community, community people want to be a part of and protect, grow and invest themselves in.
02
Clean Air.
My goal is to hold companies responsible for polluting our air, our water and our cities accountable!
To fight for cleaner air for our families and families to come.
Making polluters responsible and taking away the tax burdens it brings on to the taxpayer, and putting the fees of medical and environmental liability back on the companies responsible for polluting our cities.
Promote company transparency and accessibility.
To stop corporate greed of the few to harm the lives of the many.
