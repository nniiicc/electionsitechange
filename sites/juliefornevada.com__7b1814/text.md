ABOUT JULIE
Meet Julie
Julie Butler and her husband, Rod, have lived in Carson City since 1995.
Married for 34 years, the couple raised two sons who have stayed in the Carson City area and are an integral part of the couple's lives.
The couple welcomed a daughter-in-law to the family in 2024.
Their youngest son recently got engaged, and the couple is thrilled to soon have another daughter-in-law in the family.
Julie’s professional career includes 30 years of service working for the State of Nevada in multiple agencies and in positions of increasing responsibility.
She understands the purchasing and contracting processes, budget and legislative processes, and has been instrumental in bringing several bills forward on behalf of the agencies she has led.
As your Republican voice for Assembly District 40, Julie will bring her passion for service and government efficiency to work for you, serving with honesty, integrity, and a focus on the kitchen table issues important to all of us.
