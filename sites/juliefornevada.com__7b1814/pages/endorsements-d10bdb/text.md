ENDORSEMENTS I’m proud to endorse Julie Butler for Assembly District 40.
She brings more than three decades of dedicated public service, along with a deep, working knowledge of how state government operates.
At a time when Nevadans want accountability, efficiency, and results, Julie’s experience truly matters.
Julie is committed to public service, fiscal responsibility, and making government work better for Nevadans.
I’m proud to support her campaign and confident she will be a strong voice for Carson City and for our state.
Julie has proven that she knows how to get things done and put people first.
She understands the challenges our communities face and has the drive and compassion to tackle them head on.
I’ve worked with a lot of leaders over the years, and I can say without hesitation that Julie is exactly the kind of public servant we need in the Assembly Joe Lombardo Nevada Governor Congressman
