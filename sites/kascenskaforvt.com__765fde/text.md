Representing The Needs Of The NEK The 2026 Legislative Session Begins on January 6, 2026 Vermont General Election - November 3, 2026 Vermont State Representative John Kascenska Representing the Essex-Caledonia District Towns of Bloomfield, Brunswick, Burke, East Haven, Ferdinand, Granby, Guildhall, Lunenburg, Maidstone, Victory House Committee on Appropriations House Canvassing Committee Joint Carbon Emissions Reduction Committee Vermont National Guard and Military Affairs Caucus Rural Caucus Vermont Community Development Board jkascenska@leg.state.vt.us About Born in Torrington, Connecticut, John’s family moved to Southern Vermont in 1969, and he has lived in the Northeast Kingdom since 1992 raising two sons.
John is a graduate of Poultney High School, Lyndon State College, and completed graduate degrees from Virginia Polytechnic Institute & State University in Recreation, and from North Carolina State University in Forestry.
John worked in higher education for 32 years with an emphasis on the outdoor recreation industry, given his lifelong interest in outdoor recreation activities that include hunting, fishing, skiing, mountaineering, and mountain biking.
Throughout his more than 30 years living in the Northeast Kingdom, he has actively served his community as President of the Burke Area Chamber of Commerce, Treasurer and President of Lyndon Rescue, Inc., Trustee of the Burke Mountain Club, Corporator & Trustee of Northeastern Vermont Regional Hospital, and Honorary member of the Northern Vermont University – Lyndon Alumni Council.
John is a three-time elected Justice of the Peace for the Town of Burke.
John is the founder and owner of Kingdom Adventures Mountain Guides, LLC, a Vermont-based business that provides certification courses in first aid, wilderness medicine, and avalanche education safety programs.
He also works with a team of mountain biking trail ambassadors providing information and education support to the visitors who ride Kingdom Trails during the summer season.
On March 7, 2022, John was appointed to the Vermont House of Representatives by Governor Phil Scott to represent the towns of Burke, Lyndon and Sutton that comprised the Caledonia 4 District, serving as a member of the House Committee on Commerce and Economic Development, and as a dedicated member of the VT National Guard & Veterans Affairs Caucus during the legislative session.
From the beginning, John was an active committee member working with his legislative colleagues to craft final versions of various bills that were successfully passed into law by the General Assembly.
This included historic economic development legislation to address healthcare shortages, training experiences to create career changes and upskilling for workers, and the provision of forgivable loans for businesses to get them back on their feet.
On November 5, 2024, John was elected to return to Montpelier to represent the Essex-Caledonia House District that includes the towns of Bloomfield, Brunswick, Burke, East Haven, Ferdinand, Granby, Guildhall, Lunenburg, Maidstone, and Victory. in the Northeast Kingdom.
John’s ability to listen to all perspectives through extensive and comprehensive committee testimony; asking thoughtful questions; and making informed decisions that take into consideration the impact of potential legislation on the residents he represents are essential qualities that John brings to the Statehouse every day.
At the beginning of the 2025 legislative session, John was honored to be appointed to the House Committee on Appropriations by the Speaker of the House, and work with fellow committee members and the administration to draft and pass the state budget bill out of the House for fiscal year 2026 to meet the goal of adequately funding current obligations balanced with additional needs in our state.
John’s budget areas of responsibility include serving as the committee liaison to the Vermont State Colleges; the University of Vermont; the Vermont Student Assistance Corporation; Vermont Broadband; and Public Utilities.
John has made a point of participating in monthly meetings with each town in the district throughout the year to provide legislative updates, and to stay personally connected to his constituents to listen to and bring their local, regional, and statewide concerns back to the Statehouse.
