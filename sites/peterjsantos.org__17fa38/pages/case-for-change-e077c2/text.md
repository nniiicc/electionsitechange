01 Seven officials were charged with corruption.
None ever went to trial. ▾ In 2023 the office charged seven officials — five from the Transit Authority, two from Public Health.
In 2024 both cases were thrown out for good.
The office had earlier advised the same agencies it was now prosecuting — a conflict of interest — so the court removed it and set deadlines for an outside prosecutor.
Those deadlines were missed.
Both cases ended.
Dismissed with prejudice means permanently closed.
Those officials can never be prosecuted for these alleged crimes again.
What Peter would do differently: conflict-aware case management, experienced prosecutors, and the conduct rules followed — so a case is won or lost on the facts, never on the office's own mistake.

A federal lawsuit (U.S.
District Court of Guam, Case No.
1:2026cv00006) is ongoing.
These are allegations.
The case is pending in federal court and no finding has been made.
What Peter would do differently: hire on merit through the regular process, and keep family and personal relationships out of it — so no one has to wonder.
Sources: Office of the Governor of Guam ↗ · KUAM News ↗ 03 22 agencies were left without a lawyer — until the Supreme Court said no. ▾ In February 2024 the Attorney General told twenty-two government agencies his office would no longer be their lawyer.
In May 2024 the Supreme Court of Guam ruled that Guam law does not allow that — the office owes those agencies loyalty, and must serve them.
What Peter would do differently: the office's first duty is to serve Guam's government and people — not to score points.

What Peter would do differently: a fully staffed, proactive division from day one — elder scams, price gouging and identity theft first.
