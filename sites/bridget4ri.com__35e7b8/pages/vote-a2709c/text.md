Vote by mail - must be received by end of Election Day, November 3 Mail ballots will been sent to all voters who completed and returned their mail ballot application before the October 13 deadline.
If you requested a mail ballot and have not received it, call the state Elections Division at 401-222-2340.
Visit the Secretary of State’s website for complete vote-by-mail information .
Your mail ballot must be returned (received, not just postmarked) by Tuesday, November 3 at 8:00 pm.
You can also return your mail ballot in one of the secure mail ballot drop boxes in Rhode Island.
In most towns, the drop box is located outside Town Hall — in East Greenwich the drop box is in front of the EG Police Department.
