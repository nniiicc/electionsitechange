I live in North Kingstown with my husband, Will, my sons, Oscar and Elliot, and our little pug Dolly.
As a member of the Senate Committee on Health & Human Services and a past member of the Committee on Environment & Agriculture, I work to expand access to health care services and lower costs for patients.
I collaborate with leading environmental experts to craft legislation that will help all Rhode Islanders enjoy a future that is clean, waste-free and powered by renewable energy.
My professional background is in the nonprofit sector.
I have worked with national organizations expanding access to health care; with food banks helping make sure that children, families, and seniors have enough to eat; and with animal welfare organizations to stop cruel and inhumane treatment.
These days, when I am not doing Senate business, you can find me substitute teaching in the North Kingstown Schools — a job that has confirmed for me firsthand that our teachers are amazing and need more support!
I love our beautiful state, and I am determined to help make it a happier, healthier, more equitable place to live.
