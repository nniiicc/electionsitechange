ABOUT Leadership You Can Count on Meet Michelle Michelle Axtman is a wife, mother, aspiring cattle rancher, proud veteran, and public servant representing District 7 in the North Dakota Senate.
She served 12 years on active duty as a pilot in the U.S.
Air Force and continues to serve today as a Major in the Air Force Reserve bringing discipline, accountability, and mission-focused leadership to everything she does.
In 2022, Michelle was elected to the North Dakota Senate and has been committed to strengthening education and workforce development, supporting responsible budgeting and tax relief, and advancing initiatives that grow North Dakota’s economy while supporting families and communities.
In her first two sessions, she has championed numerous bills and landmark legislation, including:  Authoring and serving as the prime sponsor of the historic legislation creating a state-wide bell-to-bell phone-free schools policy;  Sponsoring legislation in 2023 that provided a $500 Primary Residence Tax Credit, paving the way for more significant property tax relief in 2025;  Co-sponsoring legislation that established a Task Force to streamline state government and reduce waste.
Michelle’s work has earned national recognition.
She was selected as a Hunt-Kean Fellow , an honor awarded to just 40 leaders nationwide who are driving impactful education policy and innovation.
She has also received recognition as a Friend of School Psychologists for her work on mental health issues, and she was selected by the State Legislative Leaders Foundation to participate in the Emerging Leaders Program.
