Michelle Axtman, Greg Vetter, and Steve Sauter Submit Over 600 Signatures; Certified for June Primary Ballot Feb 24, 2026 | Press Release District 7 Republicans Endorsed by over 600 signatures BISMARCK, ND – Today District 7 republican team Sen.
Michelle Axtman, Greg Vetter, and Steve Sauter announced that the North Dakota Secretary of State has certified their candidacies for the June primary election after submitting more than 600 signatures from District 7 constituents.
The certification ensures all three candidates will appear on the June primary ballot representing North Bismarck.
The team said the strong signature support reflects grassroots enthusiasm across District 7 and a shared commitment to conservative leadership, fiscal responsibility, and protecting North Dakota values.
“We are grateful for the hundreds of neighbors who signed our petitions and continue to encourage our campaign,” the candidates said in a joint statement.
“We look forward to earning the trust and support of District 7 voters on June 9.” All three candidates emphasized their focus on lowering property taxes, supporting families, responsible budgeting, economic growth, and keeping District 7 a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
