Critical rural county will host Biggs, Kerr, and other prominent elected officials
GILBERT, AZ—The Biggs for Arizona campaign is hosting a campaign rally Saturday evening in Casa Grande to motivate Pinal County voters as the campaign season heats up.
Pinal County is home to Legislative District 16, one of the top battleground districts in the state, and plays a critical role in rural turnout efforts.
The event is open to the public with doors opening at 5:00pm and the program starting at 6pm.
Attendees should RSVP HERE while press should RSVP directly to drew@biggsforarizona.com.
Doors will be open to the press at 4:00pm with an expected media gaggle featuring Andy Biggs and Sine Kerr at approximately 7:15pm.
