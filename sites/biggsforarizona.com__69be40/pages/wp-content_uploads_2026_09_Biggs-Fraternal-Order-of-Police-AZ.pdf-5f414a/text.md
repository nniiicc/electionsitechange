Fraternal Order of Police Arizona State Lodge Paul Sheldon Patrick Scherden President Secretary August 31, 2026 Congressman Andy Biggs Candidate for Arizona Governor Dear Congressman Biggs, On behalf of the Arizona Fraternal Order of Police (F.O.P.) and its over 9,400 members, I am pleased to announce that the Arizona F.O.P. has endorsed you for Arizona Governor.
Your endorsement by the F.O.P., one of Arizona's largest police organizations, is based on your demonstrated support of significant public safety issues and reflects our belief that you best understand the difficult job faced by members of the law enforcement community.
Your support of those efforts has not gone unnoticed.
The Arizona F.O.P. represents the interests of professional law enforcement officers working in agencies throughout the State.
We are committed to improving the working conditions of law enforcement officers and the safety of those we serve through education, legislation, information, community involvement, and employee representation.
The Arizona F.O.P.'s efforts result in many benefits and rights for Arizona law enforcement officers.
Nationally, the F.O.P. is the world's largest organization of law enforcement officers, with more than 382,000 members.
The F.O.P. is the voice of those who dedicate their lives to protecting and serving our communities, states, and nation.
If we can assist, please contact President Sheldon or our legislative counsel, Don Isaacson.
Fraternally, Paul Sheldon, President Fraternal Order of Police Arizona State Lodge www.azfop.com
