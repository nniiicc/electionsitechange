Stone, Executive Director Arizona Conference of Police & Sheriffs 2015 West River Road, Suite 141 Tucson, AZ 85704 dstone@azcops.org azcops.org September 2, 2026 RE: Endorsement for Congressman Andy Biggs for Arizona’s Governor Dear Congressman Biggs: The Arizona Conference of Police & Sheriffs (AZCOPS) is a statewide organization representing more than 4,500 members throughout Arizona.
Following extensive research and consideration by AZCOPS' Legislative Committee and Executive Board, AZCOPS is proud to endorse you for election for Arizona's Governor.
AZCOPS examines several factors when determining whether to issue endorsements, including a candidate's record of accomplishments, commitment to public safety, and willingness to work collaboratively with the leadership of our member organizations.
Your unwavering support for law enforcement and public safety is appreciated by our members and the communities they serve.
We wish you continued success in your campaign and look forward to working with you on issues important to Arizona's law enforcement professionals.
Respectfully, David N.
Stone Executive Director Arizona Conference of Police & Sheriffs
