Ad contrasts Biggs’ plan to cut taxes and lower costs on Arizonans versus Hobbs’ terrible record on affordability and pushing tax hikes
GILBERT, AZ—The Biggs for Arizona campaign released a new :30 ad, “Clear Choice”, contrasting the optimistic vision of Andy Biggs’ economic plan versus Katie Hobbs’ terrible record on affordability and pushing tax hikes on Arizonans.
The ad is currently running as part of a $250K media campaign this week on OTT and CTV platforms for targeted Arizona voters.
Andy Biggs has worked to put more money in the pockets of Arizonans, from voting for No Tax on Tips and Overtime to his vision of eliminating the state income tax in Arizona.
It’s a clear contrast with the terrible economic record of Katie Hobbs, who vetoed No Tax on Tips three times, cosponsored a bill to double the state’s gas tax, and has driven Arizona down to 45th nationally in affordability.
