Two new ads will highlight Katie Hobbs’s Culture of Corruption and Andy Biggs’s vision to eliminate the state income tax
GILBERT, AZ—The Biggs for Arizona campaign and the Republican Party of Arizona have placed a $1.75M coordinated ad buy for broadcast, cable, and streaming TV across Arizona.
The ad buy starts today and goes for three weeks, which does not include ongoing targeted digital efforts from the Biggs for Arizona campaign.
The two new ads will focus on Katie Hobbs’s culture of corruption stemming from the Sunshine Residential pay-to-play scheme and a contrast ad featuring Andy Biggs critiquing Katie Hobbs’s history of vetoing tax cuts and her support for doubling the state’s gas tax.
