Stops in Flagstaff and Glendale before ending Monday in Yuma
GILBERT, AZ—Andy Biggs and Sine Kerr hit the campaign trail across Arizona on Labor Day Weekend, finishing with a stop this evening in Yuma for an event hosted by the Yuma County Republican Party.
Kerr met voters at the Coconino County Fair in Flagstaff, while Biggs spoke to the Assyrian community after service at St.
Peter’s Cathedral of the Assyrian Church of the East in Glendale.
Both Biggs and Kerr will make their way to Yuma Monday night to speak at this evening’s Yuma County Republican Party BBQ.
There will be a media availability at 5:15pm at the Elks Lodge, 1917 W. 32nd Street in Yuma before the BBQ event starts at 6pm with remarks from Biggs, Kerr, and featured speaker political commentator Scott Jennings.
