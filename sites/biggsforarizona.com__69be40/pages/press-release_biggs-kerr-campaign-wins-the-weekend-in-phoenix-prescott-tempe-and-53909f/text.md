Water security, affordability, and keeping Arizonans safe were repeatedly mentioned at campaign events throughout the weekend by the Republican ticket.
With just over 3 weeks until Election Day, Andy Biggs and Sine Kerr have been criss-crossing the state while Katie Hobbs is skipping some of Arizona’s most important political events like the Salt River Project PIC Picnic , a traditional gathering of candidates to support one of the state’s largest water and power providers.
On Friday night, the Biggs/Kerr campaign hosted Biggstock in Downtown Phoenix where Andy and his three daughters joined their family house band for an evening of cover songs in front of hundreds of supporters.
Coverage of the event by the Arizona Republic can be found HERE and a live stream of the concert can be found HERE .
After rallying with Erika Kirk on Saturday morning in Phoenix before supporters went out to canvass in one of Arizona’s most competitive legislative districts, Biggs and Kerr attended the SRP PIC Picnic in Tempe and laid out their plans to secure Arizona’s water future.
To finish out Saturday, Biggs and Kerr spoke at the Fall Meeting of the Arizona Federation of Republican Women in Prescott to hundreds of supporters who were thrilled to see their ticket.
