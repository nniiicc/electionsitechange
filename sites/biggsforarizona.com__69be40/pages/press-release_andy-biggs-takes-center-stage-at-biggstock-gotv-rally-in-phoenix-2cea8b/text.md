Biggs, a longtime musician, will be on vocals, keyboard, and guitar throughout the evening and will also be joined by Phillip Rhodes on drums, who previously played for the Arizona-based band Gin Blossoms.
Nine years after the first Biggstock, the band is getting back together as ballots go out to Arizona voters this weekend.
Biggstock is taking place at The Duce in Downtown Phoenix and is free of charge, although donations to Biggs/Kerr for Arizona are appreciated.
The event starts at 7pm with the band expected to take the stage by 7:30pm for their set.
Media should RSVP to drew@biggsforarizona.com .
A riser will be available for media members at 5pm.
