Biggs returns to hometown for 1st General Election rally in Pima County
GILBERT, AZ—The Biggs for Arizona campaign is hosting a campaign rally tonight in Tucson to motivate Pima County voters as the general election campaign begins.
A Tucson native, Andy Biggs will lead a lineup of speakers including Republican Lt.
Governor nominee Sine Kerr.
Speakers include:
- Former Border Patrol agent Art Del Cueto
- State Representative Michele Peña
- Senate President and Republican nominee for Attorney General Warren Petersen
- Congressman Eli Crane
- Republican nominee for Lt.
Governor Sine Kerr
- Republican nominee for Governor Andy Biggs
The event is open to the public with doors opening at 6:00pm and the program starting at 7pm.
Attendees should RSVP HERE while press should RSVP directly to drew@biggsforarizona.com.
Doors will be open to the press at 5:00pm with an expected media gaggle featuring Andy Biggs and Sine Kerr at approximately 8:15pm.
