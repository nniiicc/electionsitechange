Nominee highlights Biggs/Kerr ticket’s focus on affordability and supporting Latino small business owners from taxes and regulations GILBERT, AZ—Republican nominee for Lt.
Governor Sine Kerr took the Biggs/Kerr campaign’s message directly to Latino voters in a recent 40-minute podcast interview with Prensa Arizona, one of the largest Spanish-language publications in the state.
The interview covered a number of in-depth topics, allowing Kerr to speak directly to the Latino community about how she and her running mate Andy Biggs will improve the lives of Latinos across Arizona.
From affordability to water to protecting educational freedoms for all families, Kerr made a strong case for the Biggs/Kerr ticket putting money back in the pockets of Arizonans.
