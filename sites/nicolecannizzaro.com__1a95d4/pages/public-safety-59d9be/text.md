PUBLIC SAFETY
Nicole is the only candidate for Attorney General with public safety and law enforcement experience.
As a prosecutor, she won awards for her work to fight for justice for crime victims and for busting a major identity theft ring operating in Las Vegas.
As our next Attorney General, she’ll support law enforcement’s efforts to tackle drug and human trafficking, prevent gun violence and get untraceable ghost guns off the streets, protect kids online, and crack down on fraud and scammers targeting Nevadans.
As a state senator, Nicole passed laws to tackle AI generated child pornography, made it harder for domestic abusers to get guns, protected seniors with tougher laws on identity theft, and gave law enforcement better tools to fight high-level drug trafficking.
She fought to protect law enforcement funding at the state and local level, and she helped prevent layoffs of up to 25% of the Las Vegas Metro Police’s sworn officers.
