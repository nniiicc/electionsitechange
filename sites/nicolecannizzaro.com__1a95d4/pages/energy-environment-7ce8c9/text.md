CLEAN ENERGY AND ENVIRONMENT
Nicole will work to protect Nevada’s clean energy economy and our state’s beautiful natural environment.
She’ll take on corporate polluters and protect access to public lands for all to enjoy.
She knows that states like Nevada will see disproportionate impacts from a changing climate, and she’ll protect Nevadans from bad actors who seek to take advantage of extreme heat, fires, or other climate related issues.
Nicole has been a champion for protecting public lands and growing our state’s clean energy economy.
She’s passed laws that have led to new solar energy development that created thousands of good paying jobs.
And she’s opposed plans to sell off our public lands for private development with no benefits for ordinary Nevadans.
