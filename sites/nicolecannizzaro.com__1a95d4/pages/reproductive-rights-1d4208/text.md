PROTECTING REPRODUCTIVE RIGHTS
As Attorney General, Nicole will use every tool at her disposal to protect Nevadans’ reproductive rights, including abortion rights, birth control, and the right to IVF.
If the federal government or other states try to infringe on Nevadans’ rights, she will take them to court.
And she’ll protect women and doctors from prosecution or harassment for seeking or providing legal reproductive health care.
In the Legislature, Nicole had a 100% rating from Planned Parenthood.
She led the fight to pass a state constitutional amendment protecting abortion rights.
She’s passed bills to expand access to birth control and to remove outdated criminal penalties for abortion from Nevada law.
She even passed a law protecting women and doctors in Nevada from other states’ attempts to prosecute them for seeking or providing reproductive health care.
And she stood up to Governor Lombardo when he sided with far-right extremists who want to ban IVF.
