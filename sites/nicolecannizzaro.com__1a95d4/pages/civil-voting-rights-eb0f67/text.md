CIVIL AND VOTING RIGHTS
Many of our civil and fundamental rights are under attack, and Nicole will fight back to defend those rights as Attorney General.
She will stand up for ordinary Nevadans who are subject to illegal discrimination, and she will vigorously defend the integrity of our elections and every eligible Nevadans’ right to vote.
It doesn’t matter whether threats to Nevadans’ civil and voting rights come from in state or from Washington, she’ll be ready to go to court to protect Nevadans from having their rights infringed.
