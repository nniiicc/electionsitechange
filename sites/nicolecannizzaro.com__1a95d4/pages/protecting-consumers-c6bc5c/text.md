TAKING ON BIG CORPORATIONS AND PROTECTING CONSUMERS
Nicole will be an Attorney General who fights for ordinary Nevadans, not the wealthy and big corporations.
She’ll go after the corporations and corporate investors who want to rip Nevadans off and raise the cost of everything, from groceries, to housing, and prescription drugs.
She will protect Nevadans’ right to organize and ensure that our wage and benefits laws are enforced.
She will take on wage theft and worker misclassification because Nevadans should be paid what they earned.
And she’ll target fraudsters and cyberscammers who try to steal from Nevadans, especially our seniors.
As a state senator, Nicole has passed laws to protect consumers from being overcharged by utility companies, cracked down on landlords who charge unfair or hidden fees, funded construction of more housing people can afford, created jobs that pay enough to live on, and voted to cap prices for high-cost prescription drugs.
