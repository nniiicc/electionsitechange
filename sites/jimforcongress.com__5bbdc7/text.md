What I'll fight for Southwest Florida first Border Security Secure the border and deport illegal immigrants I'll stand with President Trump to secure our border, enforce our immigration laws and deport those who are here illegally.
Cost of Living Lower the cost of living Families here are paying too much for groceries, housing and insurance.
I'll fight to bring those costs down.
Taxes Make the Trump tax cuts permanent I'll vote to make President Trump's tax cuts permanent so Southwest Florida families and small businesses keep more of what they earn.
Budget Balance the budget and cut wasteful spending Every family and business in Southwest Florida lives within its means.
Washington should too.
I'll vote to cut waste and balance the budget.
Economy Get government out of the way I'll push to cut regulations so local businesses can grow and invest here.
I'll also work to privatize government functions, from expanding school choice to defunding the Department of Education and cutting the Postal Service in half.
Veterans A full VA hospital Our veterans shouldn't have to drive across the state for care.
I'll work to turn our local VA clinic into a full VA hospital.
Roads & Housing An inland interstate bypass A new route connecting Immokalee and Lehigh Acres opens up affordable housing and gets working families to their jobs.
Power Grid Harden our electrical grid After every hurricane, families wait too long to get the lights back on.
I'll push to harden our grid so Southwest Florida recovers faster.
Clean Water Septic to sewer Converting septic systems to sewer cuts the nutrients that feed algae blooms and red tide along our coast.
Lake Okeechobee The right water at the right time The Caloosahatchee needs enough fresh water to keep the estuary healthy.
I support moving more water south and getting the flows right.
Everglades Finish Everglades restoration I'll fight to fund and finish Everglades restoration so more clean water moves south, where it belongs.
Offshore Drilling Extend the offshore drilling ban Our beaches, water and tourism economy are worth protecting.
I'll work to extend the ban on oil drilling off Florida's coast.
