Protecting Public Safety Public safety isn’t political—it’s personal.
After losing my son to violent crime, I’ve made it my mission to ensure our communities are safe.

Expanding Education Options Every child deserves a great education—regardless of their zip code.
I support expanding school choice so families can find the best fit for their children.
Let’s give teachers the tools they need and empower parents to be true partners in education.
Defending American Values I believe in an America where every person is treated with dignity—no matter their background, faith, or where they come from.
My platform is rooted in freedom, opportunity, and respect.
I also believe in standing by our allies, including Israel, to keep America and the world safe.

I support an all-of-the-above energy strategy that keeps our lights on, creates local jobs, and breaks our dependence on foreign powers.
We can lead the future—right here at home.
Restoring Fiscal Responsibility It’s time to rein in wasteful government spending.
I’ll fight for transparency, balance the books, and work across the aisle to bring accountability and civility back to Congress.
We owe it to our kids and grandkids to get this right.
