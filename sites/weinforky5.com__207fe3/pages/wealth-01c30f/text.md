Mikel Wein for U.S.
Congress, KY District 5 Log In Resources Home Wealth Inequality The Issue Today’s economy is broken.
The top 1% have more wealth than the bottom 93% of earners.
One person, Elon Musk, owns more wealth than 52% of Americans; enough to buy the presidency and further enrich himself and his fellow billionaires.
Ordinary people are working two or three jobs just to afford necessities, while multinational corporations directly profit from keeping their workers in perpetual financial insecurity.
Our nation’s founders agreed that we needed fundamental laws to protect citizens from government overreach.
It is time we were honest about the need to approach workers in this country the same way, and establish legislation to protect them from government and corporate greed.
Put differently, the American people need to recognize that a class war has already begun, and so far, we have refused to fight back.
Eastern Kentucky has a strong history of fighting back, and we need to re-gain the tools that would help us reclaim our share of the wealth: a vibrant National Labor Relations Board, stronger laws that protect worker’s rights, and renewed ability to form unions.
Background Info Two Sides to Everything by Shaunna Scott tells the story of working class consciousness in Harlan county Feb 2025 study found almost $80 trillion redistributed from bottom 90% to top 1% between 1975 and 2023, including $3.9 trillion, or enough to give people a $32,000 raise, in 2023 alone.
