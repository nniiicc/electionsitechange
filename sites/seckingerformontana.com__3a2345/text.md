JOSH SECKINGER: THE CHOICE IN HD-62 Josh Seckinger has represented House District 62 in the Montana Legislature with a record that includes bipartisan legislation, public-land access, river management, housing advocacy and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
One example: Josh sponsored and passed HB 763 , bipartisan legislation designed to reduce red tape in Montana’s Block Management Program.
The bill passed the House 97–1 and the Senate 48–2 before being signed into law.
“HB 763 is a commonsense solution that cuts bureaucratic red tape, making it easier for landowners to participate… truly represents a win-win solution: enhanced public access alongside respect for private property rights.” — Montana Chapter of Backcountry Hunters & Anglers BEFORE YOU VOTE, LOOK AT THE RECORD.
