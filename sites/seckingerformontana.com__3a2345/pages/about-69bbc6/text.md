Who is Josh Seckinger?
Josh Seckinger moved to Montana in 2009 and has called the Gallatin Valley home for nearly two decades.
After studying political science at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, Josh built a working life in Montana across construction, food distribution, newspaper production, seismic mapping and the outdoor industry.
He also worked for an environmental attorney and has spent years guiding fly-fishing trips on the Yellowstone, Gallatin, Madison and Smith rivers.
That broad experience gives Josh a practical understanding of seasonal work, small businesses and the challenges facing people who earn a living in Montana.
It also shaped his commitment to public access, private property rights, responsible conservation and the outdoor economy.
In the Legislature, Josh focuses on affordability, strong public schools, individual freedom and responsible use of public funds.
He believes decisions should be grounded in good information, measurable results and local knowledge.
Josh believes in collaboration and common sense over partisanship.
He works to represent hardworking Montanans, listen to different perspectives and build bridges across political divides.

As a member of the House Natural Resources Committee, Rep.
Seckinger was a tireless advocate for responsible stewardship and the protection of Montana’s public lands.
He sponsored the session’s only pro-public lands resolution and successfully championed HB 763, which strengthens incentives for landowners to participate in the state’s block management program , expanding public access by encouraging cooperation between private landowners and outdoor enthusiasts.
