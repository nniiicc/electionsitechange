Request Your AB Join the Team A Fresh Perspective for District 59 I am John Reny and I am officially announcing my candidacy for the Maine House of Representatives District 59 seat.
I'm a born and raised Mainer of Franco-American descent, a life-long resident of Augusta, and the son of a World War II Veteran who always taught me to work hard, pay my bills, and whatever was left over was mine.
As a small business owner the last 34 years, I'm working harder than I ever have, like most Mainers, and there is little to nothing left over.
Therefore, I am committed as your next representative to ensure Tax Dollar Efficiency, Tax Dollar Accountability, for Tax Dollar Affordability.
Whether it be high and rising energy costs, the education of our children, encouraging small business incentives, and strengthening job growth opportunities, I offer a direct, fair and honest approach to protect the integrity, principles, and values that Maine has always been known for.
So join me, John Reny, in 2026 where I hope to earn your vote to become your next State Representative for District 59.
Life-long Augusta resident 34-year small business owner Focused on affordability and accountability The Path Forward John's commitment to the people of District 59 is built on four clear priorities.
01 Lower Energy Costs Promoting efficient, sensible energy policies that help you, not special interests.
02 Reduce Taxes Promoting responsible spending and greater transparency in government.
03 Grow Jobs & Opportunity Cutting red tape and making it easier to start and run a Maine business.
04 Invest in Our Children & Schools Ensuring our graduates are ready to enroll, enlist or start a career in technology or trades.
