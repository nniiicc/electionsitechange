CAMPAIGN PRIORITIES 1 Cut Taxes Lukas is a true conservative, and he believes in minimizing taxes.
Lukas will seek to decrease property tax, which is driving up housing costs and in reality makes it so you never truly own your home!
He will also support other means to take government tax burden off of peoples’ shoulders, such as the Child Tax Credit, reducing the income tax, cutting government tax burden on small businesses and more!
2 Address Housing Crisis High housing prices and declining home ownership rates are some of the biggest issues we’re facing in the valley.
Lukas believes it is critical to bring back the American Dream of attainable home ownership.
To do this he will support decreasing taxes and fees in all facets of the housing sector.
Lukas will also support banning non-citizens that don’t live in Montana and foreign corporations from owning land to stop the exploitation by foreign adversaries of one of our state’s greatest resources, which also pushes prices up.
3 Judicial Reform The Montana Judicial System is run by radical far-left judges and they constantly strike down important conservative legislation on many issues, be it abortion, drag shows, education, energy or election integrity.
Lukas will fight to bring back true checks and balances and will support anything possible to curtail the out-of-control liberal courts, ranging from transparent elections to defunding to impeachment.
4 Illegal Immigration Lukas will fight strongly against Illegal immigration and mass migration.
He will support revoking the licenses of companies found to intentionally hire illegal aliens, using state resources to deport illegal aliens out of the state and criminalizing aiding and abetting or harboring illegal aliens in our state.
