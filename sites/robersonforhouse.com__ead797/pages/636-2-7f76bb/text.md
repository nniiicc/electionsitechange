North Carolina Speaker of the House Appoints Representative James Roberson to House Select Committees on Helene Recovery and Property Tax Reduction and Reform .
The House Select Committee on Helene Recovery will focus on assessing storm impacts, coordinating state and local recovery efforts, and identifying long-term solutions to strengthen disaster preparedness and resilience for communities affected by Hurricane Helene.
The House Select Committee on Property Tax Reduction and Reform shall study options to reduce the property tax burden on taxpayers in North Carolina.
The Committee shall consider reforms that provide tax relief to taxpayers while balancing potential impacts on local government revenues.
Representative Roberson is grateful for the opportunity to serve on these important committees.
