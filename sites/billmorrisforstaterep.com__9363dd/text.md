Find Out More Bill Morris has dedicated his life to serving the people of St.
Clair County.
From his years as Mayor of Moody to his leadership on the St.
Clair County Board of Education and the Central Alabama Water Board, Bill has worked tirelessly to make our communities stronger, safer, and better prepared for the future.
Now, Bill Morris is ready to take that same dedication to Montgomery — to represent Alabama House District 50 with proven experience and trusted leadership.
A Record of Real Results Former Mayor of Moody (1992–2003) — led steady city growth and infrastructure improvements General Manager, Leeds Water Works — responsible for essential public services Member, St.
Clair County Board of Education — supporting students, teachers, and families Appointed by Lt.
Governor Will Ainsworth to the Central Alabama Water Board Serves on the St.
Clair County Republican Executive Committee and Sheriff’s Foundation Focused on What Matters Most Bill’s campaign is built around three key priorities: Backing Law Enforcement Standing strong with those who protect our communities.
Workforce Development Expanding job training and career opportunities for local residents.
Education Excellence Ensuring every school has the tools and resources needed for success.
