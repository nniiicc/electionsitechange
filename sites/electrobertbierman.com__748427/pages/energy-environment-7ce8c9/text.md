Sustainable Energy and Protecting Our Environment
Minnesota's clean air and beautiful lakes are some of our most cherished treasures.
As Minnesotans, we value our natural resources and understand the need to preserve and protect them.
Cleaner air helps combat rising childhood asthma rates.
Reduced emissions aid farmers facing increased field flooding, and clean water preserves the fishing and game-hunting populations residents and visitors enjoy.
“A healthy environment benefits everyone.”
Now is the time to invest in renewable energy, create high quality jobs and secure Minnesota’s energy independence.
Pollution, unclear air, and contaminated water harm all living beings.
With the DFL leading, we passed some of the most advanced legislation in the country to address the issues of PFAS and nitrate pollution.
I will continue to work with people on both sides of the aisle to ensure Minnesota’s air, water, and soil are protected and improved.
We are the stewards of our environment, responsible for the land and water that connect to communities beyond our borders.
The next generation is depending on us to safeguard their future.
