Community Coffee Hours Every Month
Community Coffee Hour is one of my favorite parts of representing you!
These casual meetings with neighbors and constituents allow us to have meaningful conversations about the issues important to our community and families.
All viewpoints are always welcome.
I enjoy having these civil discussions where we can all listen and learn from each other.
I invite you to join me and our neighbors to share what's on your mind, ask any questions you may have, and contribute your input on how we can keep our communities moving forward.
Each month we will continue to have one or two Saturday community coffee hours.
We will meet in person at Dunn Brothers Coffee, Curiouser Coffee, or Galaxie Library in Apple Valley.
Please check this page above for dates, times and locations or email me for information at getinvolved@electrobertbierman.com.
I look forward to many informative and spirited conversations!
