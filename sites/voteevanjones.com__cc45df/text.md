Meet Evan
Evan Jones isn't a career politician.
He's a tradesman, a U.S.
Army veteran, and an elected Port of Longview Commissioner who raised his hand when volunteers were needed to serve - same as he's always done.
Evan drives 110 miles every morning because the family-wage jobs that should exist in the 20th keep going somewhere else.
In his role as Port Commissioner, Evan watched the Port award a contract to a contractor with a dangerous safety record and he couldn't stop it under current Washington law.
Evan wrote the district's state representative asking why - and got a letter back defending the contractor, not workers or taxpayers.
That is why Evan is running.
The 20th deserves someone in Olympia who understands what it costs when Olympia gets it wrong - and will fight to bring the work home.
The Platform
- Bring the Work Home Washington's permitting gridlock is killing projects and driving family-wage paychecks to Oregon and Idaho.
Evan will cut the red tape and fight to keep those jobs - and those paychecks - right here in the 20th.
- More Taxpayers, Not More Taxes We don't need to raise taxes.
We need more businesses and workers paying into the system.
Fix permitting, get the projects, grow the base.
Evan knows the difference between taxing people more and giving them more to pay taxes on.
- Every Worker Comes Home Whole At the Port of Longview, Evan watched a contractor with a dangerous safety record win a public contract - legally, under current law.
He's running to close those loopholes and make sure our tax dollars fund safe, accountable contractors.
No exceptions.
- Not Olympia's.
Ours.
The 20th keeps sending someone to Olympia who votes no from the minority.
He will stop the policies that don't fit our district — on guns, on permitting, on Seattle decisions that land wrong here.
Evan thinks like us, and he'll be in the majority where arguments get made before the vote.
- The Oath Comes First Evan swore an oath to the Constitution when he enlisted - not to a party, not to a political agenda.
He's running to be in the majority because that's where the 20th can actually be heard.
But his oath hasn't changed, and it won't.
That includes defending the Second Amendment.
- Invest in the Trades College isn't the only path to a good life.
Evan came up through the trades and knows what earning your journeyman card means for a family.
He'll fight to expand vocational training and apprenticeships so the next generation of workers in the 20th can build careers right here at home.
