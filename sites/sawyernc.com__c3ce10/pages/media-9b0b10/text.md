About Senator Vickie Sawyer
Vickie Sawyer is a successful entrepreneur, community leader, and public servant dedicated to serving the people of Iredell County and northern Mecklenburg County.
She currently serves as Co-Chair of the Senate Committee on Transportation.
As an honor graduate of UNC Charlotte and a former PTO President, Vickie is a strong advocate for education.
She knows that a firm foundation of conservative principles paired with common-sense policy solutions are the key to making our state the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
