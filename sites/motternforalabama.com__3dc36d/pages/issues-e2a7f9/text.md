I have always been a partisan of principles.
Principles that unite all of us." "I believe, wholeheartedly, in the core freedoms that define America and that I believe define Alabama—the freedom of speech, the freedom of worship, and the freedom from government interference in how we raise our kids, make a living and go about our lives.
I believe in personal independence and the Divine Rights of Man.
I believe that the people of Alabama—our families, our workers, our veterans, our students—deserve better than the noise coming out of Montgomery." Educational Freedom and Libraries "We need to keep the government, regardless of party, out of the classroom—we must allow our educators to teach, our students to learn and our community school boards to operate without fear or political interference." I OPPOSE government overreach in classrooms; including book bans, phone restrictions, or curriculum censorship.
I OPPOSE lawmakers censoring what students can say, read, or learn in our public schools.
I OPPOSE outside interference in the management of local and public libraries, which includes censorship of your media, literature or public programs organized by the community for the community.
I SUPPORT giving control of classrooms back to educators and local school boards, free from the political interference of any party.
I SUPPORT strong safety programs that protect our kids while they attend our public schools.
Which is why I am against compromising communication tools that could save student lives. (e.g., phones in schools).
Infrastructure "We need leadership that cares less about soundbites and sideshows and more on the real issues.
We need to address our infrastructure!" I SUPPORT establishing a statewide Dam Safety and Inspection Program to protect our towns, cities and public safety.
I SUPPORT repairing Alabama’s aging locks, roads, and inland waterway systems before they fail.
I SUPPORT expediting planned transportation routes in our towns and cities, like Calera, where stalled trains harm public safety and impede daily life.
I OPPOSE under-funding critical public services in favor of headline-chasing legislation.
I OPPOSE environmental negligence that leads to the contamination of the water in our homes.
Dam Safety As it stands, Alabama is the only state in the entire country that does not have a mandatory statewide dam safety or inspection program.
Our current program, which is voluntary and only just allowed for public dams to take part on a case by case basis in 2024, simply does not do enough.
If elected, I will sponsor and champion legislation to create a comprehensive, mandatory and statewide dam safety program for Alabama.
I’m calling for the following: A publicly accessible statewide dam inventory and database.
Regular safety inspections by qualified engineers on all dams in the state.
Enforceable deadlines to make repairs and alterations called for in inspections.
Advertised and available Emergency preparedness plans for high-risk areas.
Financial and technical support for private dam owners to meet enforced safety standards should it be the only option.
Rights and Religion "We don’t need politicians quoting scripture all the while acting in the opposite interest of their contents.
And we certainly don’t desire lawmakers who wrap themselves in the flag one day and introduce legislation that spits on it the next." I SUPPORT religious freedom and a government that respects faith by keeping politics out of religion.
I SUPPORT the individual liberties of families to raise their children and live their lives without government overreach.
I SUPPORT reducing the burdens on working families and re-directing taxpayer dollars toward services that actually serve the public.
I OPPOSE political manipulation of religion to justify laws that harm or divide our communities.
I OPPOSE government interference in personal parenting choices and everyday freedoms.
I OPPOSE the suppression of votes or political redistricting done to protect incumbents instead of communities.
Public Service and Law "The people of my district deserve a senator who will listen to the people—not the lobbyists.
A Senator who cares about our firefighters and understands that the benefits they’ve earned through selfless service are not something we should roll back when they are needed most." I SUPPORT fully funding essential public services and ensuring ALL our firefighters maintain their health and lifetime death benefits they have earned.
I SUPPORT the notion that no one is above the law, especially those who are entrusted to enforce it.
I OPPOSE the use of plain-clothes policing, which dramatically increases the chance of abuse, lack of transparency, and misidentification.
This presents dangers to both the public and our law enforcement officers.
I OPPOSE the use of masks, or other methods, intentionally meant to obscure the identity or allegiance of law enforcement officers.
Such methods create the possibility for the impersonation of police and public confusion.
Lottery "I don’t endorse a government that isn’t interested in finding solutions or allowing Alabamians to vote on legislation those in power are afraid might pass." I SUPPORT giving Alabamians the right to vote and decide for themselves whether or not they would like a lottery.
Marijuana "I support Alabama's fledgling marijuana and hemp industry.
I view it as a vital step toward compassionate care, personal choice, and a rollback of decades of ineffective policy that have endangered both the public and law enforcement." "I want to be perfectly clear, I do not personally partake in or enjoy the effects of marijuana or CBD.
That being said, I have smoked marijuana in the past.
I have ingested marijuana edibles in the past.
I have consumed both CBD and THC products in the past.
While I have not consumed any element of marijuana in years, I happily support the rights and freedom of others to do so if they please." I SUPPORT decriminalization of marijuana and the broad development of Alabama's marijuana and hemp industry.
I SUPPORT small businesses that pursue the American dream.
I SUPPORT more research to better understand marijuana's effect on the body and mind.
I OPPOSE HB445 and view it as a needless attack on small business and economic development in Alabama.
Second Amendment Rights "I fully support the second amendment and gladly own a variety of firearms.
That being said, I am also for common sense gun regulations which serve to protect my fellow citizens." I SUPPORT the second amendment and encourage all Americans to utilize their right to bear arms.
I SUPPORT background checks.
I SUPPORT preventing individuals with an established history of violence or domestic abuse from owning firearms in the same capacity we use against felons.
I SUPPORT the ban on fully-automatic weapons for civilian use and believe it should also be applied to law enforcement.
I SUPPORT gun safety programs and gun safety education.
I OPPOSE the banning of assault weapons.
I OPPOSE the ban on sawed-off shotguns.
I OPPOSE the over-regulation of firearm types and measurements.
