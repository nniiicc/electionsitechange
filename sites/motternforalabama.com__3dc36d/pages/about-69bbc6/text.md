Home About Issues News Volunteer Contact Donate Home About Issues News Volunteer Contact Donate Meet Don My name is Donald J.
Mottern, I have proudly called this state my home for nearly my entire life.
I was born in Mobile and raised in the suburbs of Birmingham.
I spent my summers digging holes, for fun, right next to Fort Morgan in Gulf Shores.
I graduated from high school in Alabaster and I left the University of Alabama in Tuscaloosa with a bachelors degree in public relations and two minors in political science and history.
I'm a journalist and former writer for the Shelby County Reporter who has been recognized with multiple awards from the Alabama Press Association, primarily for my writings dealings with human interest and editorial topics.
I've been a small business owner and have worked in the hotel, food service and mortgage industries.
I believe, wholeheartedly, in the core freedoms that define America and that I believe define Alabama: the freedom of speech, the freedom of worship and the freedom from government interference in how we raise our kids, make a living and go about our lives.
I believe in personal independence and the Divine Rights of Man.
While I have always loved political discussion, I feel compelled to run in this election and lend myself to public service due to the longstanding inaction by our state's leadership.
Everyone I know and everyone I meet, seems to agree that Alabama has been in a bad way for a long time.
I’ve listened to the same conversations - year after year, election after election.
Conversations about how our state is talked about, how our leaders act, and why Alabama always seems to be fighting for single shreds of dignity at the back of the line instead of leading from the front.
I’ve heard this said by people from every walk of life, every background and from both parties.
I, like so many others, am tired of listening to countless jokes made at our expense.
I'm tired of watching politicians drag the state that I love through the mud, for nothing more than the sake of their own selfishness and career advancement.
We know that we can do better.
And we know well enough that those who have remained at the helm of the system for decades will never fix what they broke.
The people of my district deserve a senator who will listen to the people, not the lobbyists.
A senator who will protect individual liberty regardless of who you are; and not impose an ideology in return for the time of day.
A Senator who cares about our firefighters and understands that the benefits they’ve earned through selfless service are not something we should roll back when they are needed most.
This state needs a senator who understands that freedom doesn’t come from more control, it comes from less interference and letting our grand American experiment play out naturally.
That’s what I’m offering you: not more noise, but more focus.
Not more division, but a return to dignity, responsibility, and a local guidance that wants to bring Alabama to the front of the line.
It’s going to be an uphill fight, but it's a fight that I intend to win in 2026, with your help.
