Here’s where this legislation stands: ​ Does this legislation serve a clear and necessary purpose?
Yes.
This act addresses the real and preventable dangers of unintentional firearm injuries and accidents, especially among children, by establishing a permanent fund for voluntary firearm safety education—without infringing on the rights of lawful gun owners. ​ Does it align with constitutional principles and the rule of law?
Yes.
The act explicitly protects the Second Amendment rights of Arkansans while promoting voluntary safety practices through education and community engagement.
It respects individual liberty and avoids any form of state coercion or firearm restriction. ​ Does it respect taxpayer dollars and avoid new burdens?
Yes.
The fund is supported by reallocating a portion of existing sales tax revenue already generated from firearm and ammunition sales—meaning no new taxes, fees, or financial burdens are created.
It also welcomes private and federal grants to expand impact. ​ Will it improve civic health and strengthen our social fabric?
Yes.
By encouraging responsible ownership, reducing preventable tragedies, and supporting community-driven safety programs, this act builds trust, supports public safety, and demonstrates a unifying approach to gun safety that respects both rights and responsibilities. ​ Does it offer long-term value and strengthen the Republic?
Yes.
The act invests in preventative measures that promote public safety without compromising constitutional freedoms.
It models a responsible, locally grounded approach to a nationally relevant issue—enhancing both civic cohesion and institutional trust. ​ Final Verdict: This legislation strengthens the long-term health and integrity of Arkansas — and the Republic. ​ Why it matters: The Arkansas Firearm Safety Funding Act offers a principled, voluntary, and fiscally responsible path to reduce firearm-related accidents and promote safe ownership.
