Arkansas Firearm Safety Funding Act
The Arkansas Firearm Safety Funding Act supports voluntary gun safety education—without raising taxes.
By dedicating a portion of existing sales tax revenue from firearm and ammunition sales, it promotes responsible ownership, safe storage, and community-based training.

Does this legislation serve a clear and necessary purpose?
Yes.
This act addresses the real and preventable dangers of unintentional firearm injuries and accidents, especially among children, by establishing a permanent fund for voluntary firearm safety education—without infringing on the rights of lawful gun owners.
Does it align with constitutional principles and the rule of law?
Yes.
The act explicitly protects the Second Amendment rights of Arkansans while promoting voluntary safety practices through education and community engagement.
It respects individual liberty and avoids any form of state coercion or firearm restriction.
Does it respect taxpayer dollars and avoid new burdens?
Yes.
The fund is supported by reallocating a portion of existing sales tax revenue already generated from firearm and ammunition sales—meaning no new taxes, fees, or financial burdens are created.
It also welcomes private and federal grants to expand impact.
