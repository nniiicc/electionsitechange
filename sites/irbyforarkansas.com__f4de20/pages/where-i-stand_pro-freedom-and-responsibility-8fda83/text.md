Pro-Freedom & Responsibility
Freedom is not just the ability to make choices—it is the right to live without unnecessary interference from the government.
However, with this freedom comes a fundamental responsibility: to protect our rights and preserve the values that bind us together as a society.
We, the people, must take the lead in safeguarding both our individual freedoms and the moral fabric of our communities.
Defending Freedom: Protecting Our Rights and Responsibilities
Freedom means more than just personal choice—it’s about protecting the fundamental rights that allow us to live freely and responsibly.
I will always stand firm in defending your First Amendment freedoms, including freedom of religion, freedom of speech, and freedom of assembly.
The government should never dictate your faith, silence your voice, or control your way of life.
But true freedom also comes with responsibility.
We the people—not the government—are the ones who must preserve the moral and civic fabric of our society.
This responsibility falls on individuals, families, and communities, not on the state.
By upholding these shared values, we protect the very freedoms that allow us all to thrive.
Strengthening Our Shared Liberty: Upholding Rights and Supporting Each Other
Liberty isn’t just about protecting our own rights—it’s about ensuring that all Arkansans are able to enjoy their freedoms in a strong, supportive community.
We the people must work together to safeguard each other’s rights and promote a society based on shared responsibility.
True freedom requires a commitment to strong families, vibrant communities, and a citizenry that actively supports one another.
As Arkansans, we know that our liberty depends not on government intervention but on our collective will to uphold values and act with moral responsibility.
This is how we create a society where everyone can thrive—through shared effort, not government mandate.
Maintaining freedom is a responsibility we all share.
