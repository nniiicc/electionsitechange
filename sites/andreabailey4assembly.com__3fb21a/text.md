Serving the 133rd Assembly District is an honor, and over the past two years in the New York State Assembly, I have worked every day to be a strong voice for our communities.
I bring common sense, local values, and practical experience to every decision, always putting the people of our district first.
My background includes more than 20 years in the private sector and over a decade of public service as a Geneseo Town Board Member, Livingston County Clerk, and now your State Assemblywoman.
Those experiences have prepared me to advocate for affordability, economic opportunity, agriculture, public safety, and stronger communities while standing up for local decision-making.
I remain committed to listening, leading, and delivering results for the families, businesses, and communities of the 133rd Assembly District.
