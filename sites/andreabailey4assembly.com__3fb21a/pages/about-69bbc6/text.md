DONATE Log In About Andrea Download Andrea's Bio Anchor 1 Meet Andrea It is an honor to serve as your New York State Assemblywoman for the 133rd Assembly District, representing all of Livingston County and portions of Monroe, Ontario, Steuben and Wyoming counties.
When I was first elected in 2024, I went to Albany committed to being a strong, common-sense voice rooted in the values of the communities I represent.
After serving my first term, I am proud of the work we have accomplished, but I also know there is much more to do.
That is why I am asking for your support as I seek re-election.
I am a lifelong Livingston County resident and live in Geneseo with my husband, Jon.
Our three adult children, Morgan, Ryan and Lyndon, also call this area home.
My roots here run deep, and they have shaped how I approach public service and the decisions I make in Albany.
Before being elected to the Assembly, I served four years as Livingston County Clerk, following four years working in the Clerk’s Office overseeing day-to-day operations.
I also had the privilege of serving on the Geneseo Town Board.
These experiences gave me firsthand knowledge of how decisions made in Albany affect our local governments, taxpayers and families.
Before entering public service, I spent more than 20 years in the private sector working in operations, human resources and customer service.
I have carried those experiences with me throughout my time in government.
I believe in listening, understanding the problem and finding practical solutions that work for the people we serve.
Delivering for the 133rd Getting results means more than casting votes in Albany.
