Join Us Our Priorities Healthcare Healthcare doesn’t work if it’s out of reach.
I support affordable insurance paired with real access to care — including mental health services and strong local providers.
Health isn’t built only in clinics and hospitals; it’s built in our homes, schools, workplaces, and communities, and our policies should reflect that reality.
Education Strong public schools are the backbone of prosperous communities.
Schools nurture and guide our next generation, are Moorhead’s largest employers, and are central to workforce development.
With so much at stake, fully funding our public schools must be a priority.
Affordability Affordability is the concern I hear most often from families.

Strong state partnerships can build on local solutions and help make everyday life more affordable.
