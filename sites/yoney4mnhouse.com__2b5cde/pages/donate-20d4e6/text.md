Did you know you can get up to a $75 refund on your donation?
The Minnesota Political Contribution Refund Program (PCR) is designed to empower individual Minnesota voters to support campaigns with small donations.
Individuals can receive up to a $75 refund, and couples up to $150!
Learn more about the political contribution refund at https://www.revenue.state.mn.us/political-contribution-refund
