Jaelynn’s platform is a community-informed declaration of what is possible when we hold corporations accountable and invest in universal care.
Healthcare, childcare, housing, these are not radical ideas, but common needs were.

Let’s fight back together because we share more needs than differences.
We CAN pass corporate accountability legislation by closing loopholes and taxing the rich.
Washington state has one of the fastest growing economies and state GDP in the nation, but our investment in public goods do not reflect a deeper commitment to our progressive values.
We CAN pass a corporate income tax We CAN tax capital gains We CAN pass the wealth tax and the high earner payroll tax We CAN tax the billionaire class avoiding taxes We CAN tax AI and advanced computing corporations and data centers We CAN tax private prisons and close tax loopholes carved out for corporations that are costing our state billions of dollars in potential progressive revenue.
We CAN house our communities.
Housing is a human right, not a commodity.
Decommodify housing.
Land and housing belong in public, cooperative, and community-stewarded hands — not on the speculative private market in the hands of private equity.
We CAN fight displacement and gentrification with community stewardship of the land.
We CAN limit how much your landlord can raise rent from 10% down to 5%.
We CAN reform the housing trust fund investment priorities We CAN open a public bank and decommodify housing, and reclaim the profits going to private equity We CAN build more social housing and permanent supportive housing.
We CAN build permanent supportive housing with public partnerships with communities.
We CAN ban junk fees and enforce tenants rights We CAN give tenants the opportunity to purchase (TOPA/COPA) and give community first rights when residential and public land go up for sale before the private market.
We CAN keep our communities safe.
Community Safety and Prevention, Not Criminalization: Community-based crisis response, independent police oversight, and an end to mass surveillance and mass incarceration — not slogans, but a fully funded alternative system.
We CAN expand and align a statewide behavior health crisis response system We CAN invest in prevention We CAN end the surveillance state We CAN hold police accountable and subjected to independent oversight We CAN end qualified immunity We CAN pass common sense gun responsibility legislation by investing in community-led solutions We CAN have universal access to healthcare.

We FIGHT because for universal healthcare coverage We FIGHT for care and reinvestment in our Washington Health Trust We FIGHT for a Healthcare system, not a commodity We FIGHT for trans protections We FIGHT to defend abortion and gender-affirming care from federal attacks by FIGHTING for expanded state programs and investments.

We CAN tax the rich to pay for statewide democracy vouchers so all voters have spare money to spend on state races.
