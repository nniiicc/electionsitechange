Meet Katy
I am a lifelong Washingtonian, born and raised right here in the heart of Washington State.
I grew up in Port Orchard and graduated from South Kitsap High School before settling in Gig Harbor.
I’ve traveled to more than 35 countries, which has shaped the way I see the world.
This region is home in every sense of the word, and it has shaped who I am and why I’m running to serve.
I am a wife, mother, nonprofit leader, and business owner.
I’ve been married to my amazing husband for 18 years, and together we are raising our three beautiful children.
Being a wife and mother has deeply influenced how I see the needs of our community — from education and safety to opportunity and affordability.
My family is my greatest joy and the driving force behind my commitment to build a better future for every family in our district.
Key Endorsments
Together, we can build safer communities, strengthen families, support small businesses, and fix systems that are failing the very people they are meant to serve.
We can have both compassion and accountability.
We can help more people with less waste.
And we can restore common sense, safety, and hope to Washington State.
