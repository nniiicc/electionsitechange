House District 104
With our Highway 70 to the north and the Page Extension to the south, our new district encompasses the heart of St.
Charles County.
Many residents of St.
Peters will call the new 104th District home, as will some residents of Harvester.
Our district contains many beautiful and instantly recognizable landmarks and community staples such as Wapelhorst, Laurel, and City Centre Park, as well as the McClay Branch Library.
The 104th is also home to schools in the Francis Howell and Fort Zumwalt Districts.
