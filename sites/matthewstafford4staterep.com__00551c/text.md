Matthew Stafford Announces Agricultural and Economic Development Commission
WARREN, MI — August 14, 2026 — Republican Michigan House District 11 candidate Matthew Stafford today announced the creation of the Agricultural and Economic Development Commission, a new initiative focused on strengthening agriculture, small business, manufacturing, investment, and job creation throughout Michigan.
Stafford said the commission will bring together agricultural stakeholders, farmers, business owners, manufacturers, economic-development leaders, and community representatives to identify practical policies that can encourage investment and expand economic opportunity.
“Michigan has everything we need to be an economic powerhouse,” Stafford said.
“We have hardworking families, world-class agriculture, manufacturers, entrepreneurs, and small businesses.
What we need is leadership willing to bring those people together and remove the barriers that stand between them and opportunity.” The commission will focus on several key priorities: - Supporting Michigan farmers and the agricultural economy. - Expanding opportunities for small businesses and manufacturers. - Encouraging private-sector investment and job creation. - Identifying unnecessary regulatory and bureaucratic barriers to growth. - Strengthening local economies and communities. - Developing practical policy recommendations for the Michigan Legislature.
Stafford emphasized that agriculture and economic development should not be treated as separate issues.
“When agriculture succeeds, communities succeed,” Stafford said.
“When small businesses grow, families have more opportunities.
When manufacturers invest, Michigan workers benefit.
We need an economic-development strategy that recognizes how connected all of these pieces are.” The commission will seek input from people working directly in agriculture, business, manufacturing, and economic development and will use that input to develop policy recommendations.
“My goal is simple: bring the people who actually create jobs and build our communities into the policymaking process,” Stafford said.
“I don't want government making decisions in a vacuum.
I want farmers, business owners, manufacturers and working families at the table.” Stafford is the Republican nominee for Michigan House District 11 and is seeking to represent residents of the district in the Michigan House of Representatives.

Father’s Rights & Child Support Reform
Michigan State House District 11 Policy Platform
Executive Summary
This proposal outlines a reform agenda aimed at modernizing Michigan’s family court and child support system to ensure fairness, accountability, and equal treatment of both parents—while prioritizing the best interests and financial stability of children.
The current system is widely criticized for:
Inconsistent custody outcomes
Financial penalties that do not reflect actual ability to pay
Administrative enforcement that can escalate poverty Limited
enforcement of parenting time
rights for non-custodial parents, This reform package seeks to balance parental rights, child welfare, and fiscal responsibility.
Core Policy Pillars
1.
Equal Presumption of Shared Parenting Goal: Establish a legal preference for shared parenting time when both parents are fit.
Reforms: Create a rebuttable presumption of 50/50 parenting time in custody decisions.
Require courts to justify deviations with written findings based on child safety or abuse risk.
Standardize custody determinations across counties to reduce inconsistency Rationale: Research in family law generally shows children benefit from stable, frequent contact with both parents when safe and appropriate.
2.
Child Support Modernization Goal: Align child support obligations with real-world financial capacity and parenting time.
Reforms: Adjust support calculations to reflect actual overnight parenting time Require real-time income verification updates (not outdated annual assumptions) Cap support obligations to prevent orders that exceed realistic ability to pay.
Include consideration of new dependents and household financial burden Rationale: The current formula can create arrears accumulation that becomes unpayable, undermining long-term compliance.
3.
Enforcement Reform & Debt Relief Pathways Goal: Shift from punitive enforcement to compliance-based recovery.
Reforms: Reduce automatic license suspensions for child support arrears when partial payment history exists.
Create structured “compliance plans” that prevent incarceration-driven job loss.
Establish a child support debt restructuring program for long-term arrears.
Prioritize wage attachment at reasonable percentages rather than fixed punitive levels Rationale: Excessive enforcement can reduce earning capacity, which ultimately reduces child support recovery.
4.
Parenting Time Enforcement Goal: Ensure custody orders are actually enforced.
Reforms: Strengthen penalties for repeated denial of court-ordered parenting time.
Allow expedited hearings for interference cases.
Create enforceable remedies including makeup parenting time.
Improve tracking of custody violations through standardized reporting Rationale: Court orders without enforcement undermine trust in the legal system.
5.
Mediation & Early Resolution First Approach Goal: Reduce litigation and emotional conflict in custody disputes.
Reforms: Require mandatory mediation before contested custody trials (except abuse cases) Expand access to neutral family mediation services Incentivize cooperative parenting agreements through reduced court costs Rationale: Litigation increases costs, delays outcomes, and often intensifies conflict.
6.
Transparency & Judicial Accountability Goal: Improve consistency and public confidence in family courts.
Reforms: Publish anonymized data on custody outcomes by county Require written justification for non-standard custody rulings Standardize guidelines across judicial districts
Economic & Social Impact- Increased workforce participation among non-custodial parents, Reduced incarceration and enforcement costs, Improved child well-being through increased parental involvement, Lower long-term dependency on public assistance programs.
