Life
I believe every life has value, and I won’t place greater or lesser worth on any life.
As a society, we must meet the responsibility of protecting the next generation, from conception to natural death.
Unfortunately, in the first year of the DFL trifecta in 2023, they passed some of the most radical abortion laws in the world.
Seventy-one percent of Minnesotans believe there should be at least some limits on abortion, and I stand with them in defending the right to life for all Minnesotans.
