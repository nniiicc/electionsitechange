Liberty
Our nation has the greatest form of government in the history of the world.
The starting point for the rule of law is our Constitution.
It was written by a generation who experienced the fire of revolution and it was this experience that created our way of life.
The first three words of the Constitution are “We the People…” and we must remember that it was written to protect the People from tyranny, not to be used or ignored by government to wield power.
