Medical Freedom
Six years ago, few could have imagined living in a country where government and large corporations would pressure individuals to take a medical test or an experimental injection just to keep a job, attend school, or travel.
It’s unacceptable that people are forced to give up their constitutional rights simply to protect their livelihood and basic freedoms.
We must stand up and defend the medical freedom of all Minnesotans.
Our society is founded on individual liberty, and we cannot afford to surrender that freedom.
