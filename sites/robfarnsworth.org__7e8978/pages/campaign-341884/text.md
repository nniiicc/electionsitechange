Contribution Refund
Did you know you can give $75 to help get Rob re-elected and get a full refund?
If you are a Minnesota voter, you may apply for a refund of all or a portion of contributions made to our campaign via the Political Contribution Refund Program (PCR).
To obtain a refund, complete the following steps:
- Donate to our campaign online or mail a check to Rob Farnsworth for Senate, 1429 Merryview Lane, Hibbing MN 55746
- Fill out the PCR Form
- Attach your original contribution receipt from the Committee to Elect Robert Farnsworth to the PCR Form
- Mail the form and donation receipt to the Minnesota Department of Revenue, Political Contribution Refund, 600 N Robert Street, St.
Paul MN 55146-1800
The maximum refund that contributors may receive is $75 per person or $150 for married couples.
You (and your spouse if filing a joint application) may file only one PCR Form for each calendar year you made contributions, even if you contributed to more than one candidate or party.
The refund program is not tied to the state income tax form.
A taxpayer may file a claim for the PCR immediately after making the contribution.
