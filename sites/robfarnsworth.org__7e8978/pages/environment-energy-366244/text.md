Environment/Energy
I believe that responsible citizens must be good stewards of the Earth.
While climate-change theories evolve, I am convinced that it is in our best interests to pursue a policy of energy independence.
I do not support politically motivated schemes that pick winners and losers within the energy sector while passing the costs on to homeowners and businesses.
Minnesota energy providers must be allowed to provide the least expensive energy possible while maintaining our proven record of protecting the environment.
