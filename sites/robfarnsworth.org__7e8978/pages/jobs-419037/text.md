Jobs
Everyone in Minnesota who wants to work should be able to find a good job.
Unfortunately, many businesses are leaving the state, or avoiding it altogether, because of our unfriendly business climate.
That trend has only sped up in recent years as more anti-business laws were passed during the trifecta.
I was proud to support permitting reform in the last few sessions, but there’s still a lot of work to do to make Minnesota a place where job creators can succeed.
