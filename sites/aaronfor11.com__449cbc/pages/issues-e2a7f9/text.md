Issues
If I'm elected, I will fight to bring the change we need to Pierre.
I will be a State Senator who is on your side and a Legislator who listens.
I will represent District 11 with honesty and integrity, and focus on the people I was elected to serve.
I will work to build a good quality of life for all the people of our state, grounded in common values like freedom, opportunity, and security for all.
This means focusing on issues like:
- Affordability: From groceries to childcare, rent to property taxes, healthcare, and more, South Dakotans are feeling the squeeze of higher costs.
I’ll fight for policies that lower the cost of living and keep more money in your pocket.
- Fully Funding Education: Our kids shouldn’t have to settle for "good enough" or being an afterthought in the state budget.
I’m committed to ensuring our schools and teachers have the resources they need to give the children of South Dakota a world-class education.
Investments in education are an investment in the present and future of our state.
- Protecting Your Rights: I will fight to ensure that the freedoms of the people of South Dakota are respected and that our constitutional rights are shielded from government overreach.
- Cleaning Up Politics: Big-money donors and special interests have too much power in our state's politics.
I will work to tackle the corrupting influence of big money and ensure that your voice—and your vote—carries more weight than their donations.
- Putting a Check on Data Centers: I am not against technological advancement or economic development.
But growth cannot outpace accountability, nor should it come at the expense of local communities or existing ratepayers.
If elected, I would support legislation to ensure transparency, accountability, and local control.
As a sign of my commitment to finding solutions, I have signed on to the Data Center Accountability Pledge, available at https://dcapsd.org/.
