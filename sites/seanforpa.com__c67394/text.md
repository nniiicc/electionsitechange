No One Works Harder Than Northeast Democrat Sean Dougherty
A lifelong resident of Northeast Philly, Northeast Democrat Sean Dougherty knows that our community is built on a foundation of faith, family, and hard work.
That’s why no one will ever work harder than Sean when it comes to supporting and strengthening the neighborhoods we call home.
From the playgrounds of the Northeast to the basketball courts of Chestnut Hill College and the Philadelphia public defender’s office, Sean has succeeded through teamwork, collaboration, and a relentless commitment to getting things done.
