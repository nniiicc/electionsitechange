Allison on the Issues
A fresh, new voice for our community, Allison is running to bring bold, honest leadership to the legislature.
She’s committed to standing up for working families, fully funding public schools, and delivering real results for House District 108.
Allison believes every Texan deserves to live with dignity, safety, and equal opportunity.
She’s focused on solving real problems and standing up for what matters: strong public schools, affordable healthcare, safe communities, and economic opportunity for all.
- A proud public-school parent, Allison supports fully funding our public schools and keeping public taxpayer dollars in public school classrooms.
She will hold voucher programs accountable and protect neighborhood schools from more funding cuts that could lead to larger class sizes and increased school closures in the district.
She believes public taxpayer dollars should support public schools, not private schools and religious charter schools that are not held to the same accountability standards as our outstanding public schools.
- As a homeowner, Allison knows Texans need real property tax relief.
She will fight for a fairer tax system that eases the burden on homeowners and renters while ensuring large corporations pay their fair share.
Allison supports limiting dramatic appraisal increases, expanding property tax exemptions for seniors and veterans, and closing corporate tax loopholes that undermine funding for our local public schools.
- Allison’s family includes medical professionals who provide quality care for patients in this district every day.
She supports expanding Medicaid in Texas, a move that will bring our federal tax dollars home and lower our health care costs locally.
She will fight for greater access to affordable health care, especially for families with special needs children, lower prescription costs for families and lower premiums for individuals and small business owners.
- Allison will fight for home insurance reforms to address Texans' growing concerns about soaring premiums, unfair claim denials, and a lack of transparency.
She supports stronger consumer protections, greater oversight of insurance companies, and increased transparency in how rates are set so families have access to affordable, reliable coverage when they need it most.
- As a Texas native, Allison understands the importance of protecting our state's natural resources and the growing strain that large data centers place on our water supply and electric grid.
She supports stronger oversight and responsible regulation of data centers to ensure our communities, infrastructure, and natural resources come first.
- Allison will advocate for expanded pathways to homeownership for first-time buyers, including increased access to affordable home loan programs for teachers, social workers, and other essential community workers who help make Texas communities stronger.
- Allison will fight to make life more affordable for Texas families by addressing rising costs in healthcare, housing, childcare, and everyday essentials.
She believes working families deserve an economy that works for them—not just the largest corporations.
- Every Texas family deserves the freedom to build a future where they feel safe, supported, and respected.
Allison will work to protect individuals and families from discrimination, defend personal and relgious freedoms, and ensure every child has the opportunity to thrive.
She believes Texas is strongest when we reject division and extremism, focus on solutions that bring communities together, and create a state where every family has the chance to succeed.
