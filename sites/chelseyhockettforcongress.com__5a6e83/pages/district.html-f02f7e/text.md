Here is how it ended up governing this year's election.
August 2025 The Legislature passes a new congressional map after Democratic lawmakers leave the state to deny a quorum.
November 18, 2025 A three-judge federal panel rules 2–1 that the map is likely a racial gerrymander and orders Texas back to its 2021 map.
December 4, 2025 The U.S.
Supreme Court pauses that ruling, so the new map is used for the March primaries.
April 2026 The Supreme Court reverses the lower court.
The 2025 map governs the 2026 midterms, though the underlying lawsuit may continue.
