Fighting for families
in Manchester & Litchfield
With 20 years of service in the U.S.
Navy and more than 15 years working in education, Leonard brings the experience we need to solve our most pressing challenges.
These are some of the top priorities he will be focused on serving you in Concord.
No Income or Sales Tax
Not now, not ever.
Leonard swore an oath to our country when he served in the U.S.
Navy, and his word is his bond.
Leonard will never support creating an income or sales tax here in New Hampshire.
Funding our public schools
Education is the foundation of opportunity.
Having taught in Manchester elementary schools and spending over a decade advising students at SNHU, Leonard knows exactly where the system is falling short.
Lowering property taxes
Property taxes in New Hampshire are too high: plain and simple.
Leonard believes in keeping more money in the pockets of the people who earned it, and will fight to lower the property tax burden on New Hampshire families.
Making housing more affordable
Young couples, working families, and recent graduates are being priced out of Manchester and Litchfield.
Leonard is committed to finding real, practical solutions that keep New Hampshire families rooted in their communities and give first-time buyers a real shot at homeownership.
Leonard Bell: A Record of Service.
Leonard Bell is a retired U.S.
Navy Veteran and Manchester, NH, resident who has dedicated his life to service.
After 20 years in the military, he went on to earn his Master of Education and build a career helping veterans and their families succeed at Southern New Hampshire University.
With over 30 years of experience in leadership and mentoring, Leonard knows what it means to show up for others.
