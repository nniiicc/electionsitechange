MEET RON
Ron Stephens represents Georgia's 164th District and chairs the House Economic Development & Tourism Committee.
A pharmacist for more than 46 years, he co-authored the Stephens-Day Bill, Georgia's first floating homestead exemption, which now protects homeowners in over 70 taxing districts statewide.
He and his wife, Janice, live in the Savannah area and are members of Richmond Hill Baptist Church.
