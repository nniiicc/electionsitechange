Pete Boudreau for New Hampshire State Representative
Pete Boudreau has lived in New Hampshire his entire life — born and raised in Manchester, and an Auburn resident for the past 30 years.
For 35 years, Pete served the public first as a Master Plumber and volunteer firefighter, then as a Manchester firefighter and lieutenant, retiring in May 2026.
Now he's ready to bring that same commitment to Concord.
Pete believes New Hampshire deserves leaders who put people first — not politicians chasing their own agendas.
With honesty, respect, and integrity, he's running to tackle the issues burdening our working families: high property taxes, broken education funding, and a state government too divided to solve real problems.
