Joe McKenna proudly serves the 18th Worcester District in the Massachusetts House of Representatives.
A lifelong resident of Sutton, Joe brings a commitment to fiscal responsibility, government accountability, and putting the needs of Massachusetts families first.
Joe is dedicated to restoring common sense to Beacon Hill and ensuring that the voices of his constituents are heard on every issue that matters to our community.
Read More About Joe Joe's Agenda is You Prioritizing Our Community Restoring balance, fiscal responsibility, and common sense to Beacon Hill.
Affordability - Taxes Fighting MA's crushing tax burden and opposing new taxes to make our state competitive again.
Cost of Living Addressing high costs of housing, utilities, daycare, and groceries affecting working families.
Utility Rates Stopping misguided climate mandates that drive energy costs to astronomical levels.
Local Aid Increasing state funding to cities and towns that know best how to serve their communities.
Education Supporting our teachers and schools from preschool through state colleges and universities.
Second Amendment Protecting constitutional rights of law-abiding citizens while addressing real public safety needs.
Our Community The 18th Worcester District Consisting of the town of Douglas, Precincts 1 and 4 of the town of Oxford, the town of Sutton, Precinct 1 of the town of Uxbridge, and the town of Webster, all in the county of Worcester.
