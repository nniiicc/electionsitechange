Meet Bridget
State Representative Bridget Walsh Moore knows about tough fights.
That’s why she’s taken on the Missouri GOP to stand up for disability rights, workers rights, and access to healthcare.
Click here to help send Bridget back to represent District 93 in the Missouri House.
A Champion for Missouri’s Workers
During her first legislative session, Bridget introduced the Missouri Employment First Act, which supports state-led employment efforts for Missourians with disabilities.
She also introduced the Missouri Earned Family and Medical Leave Act, which would grant up to six weeks of paid leave to every state employee.
Bridget understands that Missouri’s working families are the heart and soul of our state, and she will always be their champion in Jefferson City.
Putting Our Community First
Since joining the legislature, Bridget has consistently made herself accessible to constituents through attending community meetings, canvassing the district’s neighborhoods, and responding to constituent mail.
The bottom line: Bridget always shows up for the community that raised her.
