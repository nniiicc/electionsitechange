Smart Growth for South Carolina Job Creation & Business Building Infrastructure Public Safety Education Smart Growth for South Carolina We need to plan for the future.
Our Legislature should be thinking at least fifty years ahead on roads, schools, housing, and the infrastructure that comes with them.
This does not mean giving up the small town character of towns across our state including ours in Red Bank and South Congaree .
View more Job Creation & Business Building We must continue to attract jobs in manufacturing and service sectors, as well as jobs in emerging sectors.
We need to support and expand our workforce training in our high schools, technical colleges, colleges, and universities.
View more Infrastructure We must invest in improving our infrastructure.
The Legislature needs to invest in roads, schools, broadband, water, and sewer systems to support growth and improve quality of life.
To that end we must be able to trust leaders and decision making in agencies like SCDOT and Dominion Energy, and we will support strong oversight of these agencies tasked with maintaining our infrastructure.
View more Public Safety Everyone deserves to be safe where they are.
Our Legislature needs to ensure that state and local law enforcement, fire departments, and EMTs have the resources they need to do their jobs, and do them well.
We must also fight the root causes of crime by expanding economic opportunity, housing and access to homeownership, and cracking down on repeat offenders.
View more Education We must fully fund public schools as our own laws require.
Teacher pay must continue to rise to keep that pay competitive with surrounding states.
We must empower teachers to teach their students and not merely prepare students for tests.
