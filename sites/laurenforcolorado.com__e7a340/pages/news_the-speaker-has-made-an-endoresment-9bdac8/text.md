SPEAKER MIKE JOHNSON ENDORSES REP.
LAUREN BOEBERT FOR CONGRESS
COLORADO– Speaker of the House Mike Johnson today announced his endorsement of Congresswoman Lauren Boebert for Colorado’s 4th Congressional District.
“Since the day she was elected, Congresswoman Lauren Boebert has been a relentless force for conservative governance and a tireless advocate for Coloradans,” said Speaker Johnson.
“She has stayed true to her conservative principles and fought to produce real results for Colorado, from defending energy producers to increasing critical resources for our veterans and supporting rural ranchers and farmers.
I am proud to endorse and fully support her campaign for Colorado’s 4th Congressional District because we need fighters like Lauren Boebert in Congress.”
“I am grateful to have the support of Speaker Mike Johnson,” said Congresswoman Boebert.
“I look forward to working with him to help get President Trump reelected and defend our majority.
Now more than ever, conservatives need to work together to secure our Southern border, lower inflation, stop the Swamp’s out-of-control spending, and prevent the Biden Regime from trampling on Coloradans’ Constitutional rights.”
