Lauren Boebert Introduces Legislation to Abolish the ATF Mark Zuckerberg is reversing Facebook’s censorship policies Trump continues to push for U.S. expansion LEGISLATIVE UPDATES ABOLISH THE ATF: Congresswoman Boebert has introduced legislation to abolish the Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco, Firearms, and Explosives as this agency has tried too often to chip away at our Second Amendment rights.
Congresswoman Boebert is leading the charge to make sure that doesn’t happen as this agency is unconstitutional, duplicative, and a waste of our tax dollars.
THE CONVEY ACT SIGNED INTO LAW: Congresswoman Boebert’s Clifton Opportunities Now for Vibrant Economic Yields (CONVEY) Act was officially signed into law this past weekend.
This law directs the Bureau of Land Management to sell a parcel of land to Mesa County for economic development in Clifton.
Team Boebert is so proud that we were able to make this happen for the Western Slope and create job opportunities for Coloradans.
