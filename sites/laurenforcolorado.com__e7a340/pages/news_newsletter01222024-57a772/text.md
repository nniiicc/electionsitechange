28 Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act - To require “This bill generally prohibits school athletic programs from allowing individuals whose biological sex at birth was male to participate in programs that are for women or girls.
Specifically, the bill provides that it is a violation of Title IX of the Education Amendments of 1972 for federally funded education programs or activities to operate, sponsor, or facilitate athletic programs or activities that allow individuals of the male sex to participate in programs or activities that are designated for women or girls.
Under the bill, sex is based on an individual’s reproductive biology and genetics at birth.” Status: Passed Lauren was proud to vote ‘Yay’ on this bill , along with all 216 Republicans present at time of vote, and 2 Democrats.
