North Pueblo Train Derailment Earlier this week, a train derailment tragically took the life of a truck driver just outside of Pueblo on an I-25 overpass.
While there were no other serious injuries, the loss of life was deeply saddening and a full investigation of what took place is underway.
Lauren has been engaged with stakeholders like the NTSB, CDOT, BNSF Railway, and others from Sunday evening onwards to make sure that a full accounting of what went wrong is revealed and that clear steps can be taken to make sure this does not happen again.
