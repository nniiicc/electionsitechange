Daily Wire interview: Female Business Owner Who Confronted Beto On Guns Launches GOP Bid For Congress
12.9.2019
-
Lauren Boebert, who burst on to the national stage earlier this year when she confronted Democratic presidential candidate Robert Francis O’Rourke in Colorado over his extremist anti-gun proposals, announced today that she is running for U.S.
Congress as Republican in Colorado’s 3rd Congressional District.
