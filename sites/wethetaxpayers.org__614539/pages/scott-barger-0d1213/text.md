The Scott Barger campaign isn’t about one man’s journey into public service.
It’s a story of the faith, work, patriotism, and civic mission of the people he hopes to represent in Harrisburg.
Meet Scott
& Beth
Scott Barger was born and raised in Altoona, graduated from Blair County Christian School, and earned degrees from Grace College and Grace Theological Seminary.
Following 15-years as a pastor, he retired from full-time ministry to help lead the family business as general manager and talk show host for WRTA.
Scott and his wife, Beth—a public school teacher—are raising their children in Hollidaysburg.

Agenda
ENACT A SCHOOL REPORT CARD
Hold schools accountable for declining results through a Florida-style public measurement.
BLOCK THE SHAPIRO ENERGY TAX
Permanently protect PA from the job-destroying Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative (RGGI).
ENACT WELFARE-TO-WORK
Boost family incomes and independence by requiring healthy, able-bodied citizens to perform work or community service to renew benefits.
DELIVER STATE PENSION REFORM
Shift new state workers to a 401-k-style retirement program, instead of the costly, budget-busting defined benefit plans.
DEFUND PLANNED PARENTHOOD
Pass Judy Ward’s plan to shift millions in tax dollars from Planned Parenthood to health centers who do not perform abortions.
PROTECT THE VOTE
Require Voter ID, clearer voter deadlines, and more transparency on outside, third-party funding.
RETAKE THE HOUSE
Support the election of a GOP House Speaker and the election of a conservative, pro-life, pro-taxpayer Republican House Majority.
