I am honored to continue to represent the best interests of our Windham community.
In my first term, I passed legislation to help kids who are struggling to read ( HB377 ) and legislation that requires the tabulation of votes to be done publicly ( HB243 ).
I also passed legislation to reform our family courts ( HB1006 and HB1564 ), and a bill to keep families safer by preventing violent convicted felons from assuming new identities ( HB1003 ).
In my second term, I created a tax credit to encourage businesses to invest in child care ( HB1433 ).
I also passed legislation to bring more transparency to special education complaints ( HB76 ).
As your State Representative, I will continue to fight to preserve what makes New Hampshire special, including the constitutional freedoms we experience and the community values we share.
I will listen to you, learn from you, fight for you, and legislate for you.
I would be honored to earn your vote in the Windham Primary Election on September 8th, 2026.
About Me I have lived in Windham for the past 9 years with my husband, Johny, and our 4 children.
I grew up in Southern New Hampshire, earned my undergraduate degree in Computer Information Systems, was formerly an engineer in the software industry, and was a professional photographer.
I am an active member of the community, and previously served as a board member on three local non-profit boards: President of Windham Moms Alliance, President of Windham Moms Fund, and Treasurer of Friends of the Library of Windham.
I also serve on the board of REaDY to Lead NH, a leadership mentoring program for women who wish to serve in an elected official position.
