As a lifelong Rhode Islander and committed public servant, I have worked tirelessly to improve the lives of the people I represent.
In the Senate, I proudly serve as Secretary of the Senate Committee on Environment and Agriculture, member of the Labor and Gaming, Finance, and Artificial Intelligence and Emerging Technologies Committees, as well as the Act on Climate Commission, where I work to advance policies that protect our environment, strengthen our workforce, promote fiscal responsibility, and prepare Rhode Island for the opportunities of tomorrow.
I remain focused on advancing policies that strengthen our communities, expand opportunity, and enhance the quality of life for all residents.
I am seeking re-election to continue delivering results, addressing the challenges facing our state, and building a stronger, more equitable future for District 28 and all Rhode Islanders.
