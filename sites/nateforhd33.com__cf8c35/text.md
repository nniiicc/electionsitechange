The Platform Three Pillars for HD33 Where Nate stands for the working families, small businesses, and communities of HD33.
01 Economic Freedom for Families Working families and small businesses in HD33 deserve the freedom to build real prosperity and keep more of what they earn, not endless new mandates, regulations, and spending from Denver that drive up costs and take away our choices.
Eliminate, not increase, wasteful government spending and burdensome regulations that make housing, energy, and everyday life more expensive for families.
Protect our access to all reliable, affordable energy sources including natural gas and reject forced all-electric timelines and unrealistic green mandates that raise costs and risk reliability for families and businesses.
Prioritize practical, freedom-first solutions where local control and district level decisions are fundamentally better.
We will empower our communities and families rather than subjecting us to one-size-fits-all state control from Denver.
02 Smaller Government & Local Control HD33 deserves government that works for us, not one that grows bigger, more expensive, and farther from the people it serves.
We must return authority to our communities, families, and local leaders where decisions are made best.
Pursue smart deregulation by reviewing and repealing unnecessary laws, bills, and regulations that burden families and small businesses, freeing up our community financially.
Empower local communities and district leaders and residents to make decisions on housing, development, and everyday issues instead of one-size-fits-all mandates from Denver.
Demand full transparency and accountability on how our tax dollars are spent ensuring HD33 residents have clear visibility and real input so every dollar serves our priorities, not distant bureaucracies.
03 Safe Communities & Strong Families Safe neighborhoods, protected children, and empowered parents are the foundation of a prosperous HD33.
We must support our local law enforcement, hold criminals accountable, and give parents real control over their children's education and well-being.
Fully back our police and first responders so they have the tools and support needed to keep our communities safe.
Ensure criminals especially those who traffic or harm children face real consequences, including life sentences for child sex trafficking, so victims are protected and deterrence is strong.
Give parents transparency and authority in their children's education and healthcare decisions including fair girls' sports based on biology and protections against irreversible medical procedures on minors because families, not the state, should guide what's best for our kids.
