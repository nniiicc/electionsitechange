It is anticipated that the final voter turnout in Benton County for Primary and Nonpartisan Election Day will be around 15% of all registered voters.
These numbers are small for a county our size - with more than 184,000 registered voters and an estimated population of 325,000.
So, if you would like to see leadership changes locally, make a plan to vote and encourage at least five of your friends to go vote as well.
Early voting begins February 17 and ends March 2.
You’ll have the opportunity to decide the Democratic candidates for U.S.
Senate and Governor in this November’s election.
You’ll also be deciding who wins in non-partisan races and ballot initiatives that can have a major impact on your family’s lives.
Visit AR Votes to learn about the candidates and issues and get ready to vote on March 3!
Don’t forget to double-check where your polling place is located.
See you at the polls!
