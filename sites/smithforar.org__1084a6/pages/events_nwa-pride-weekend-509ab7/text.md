Join me and your neighbors in celebrating the 22nd annual NWA Pride Weekend.
What began as a grassroots effort has grown to serve more than 45,000 people annually, advancing LGBTQ dignity, visibility, and belonging across Northwest Arkansas, the state, and the Mid-South Region.
Events kickoff with the Trans March Rally and NWA Pride Festival at 6pm on Friday, June 26 and culiminate with the NWA Pride Parade at 5pm on Saturday, June 27.
Find out more about the state’s largest LGBTQ Pride celebration at Pride Northwest Arkansas.
