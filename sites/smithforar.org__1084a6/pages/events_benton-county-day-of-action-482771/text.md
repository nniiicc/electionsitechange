Let's Build Benton County!
Join us on Saturday, October 3 at 10 AM for a Benton County Day of Action!
Let's get trained to knock on doors and talk to Benton County voters about our Democratic candidates in seats we can flip: Dan Holtmeyer for State Representative District 15 and Mitchell Smith for State Representative District 13.
Other statewide and local candidates will be in attendance.
Benton County is growing fast, and a lot of those new neighbors have never had a Democrat knock on their door.
This is a great chance to have all Dems on deck!
It'll be worth the trip to Rogers to work hard for a better Arkansas and to put these seats in play!
