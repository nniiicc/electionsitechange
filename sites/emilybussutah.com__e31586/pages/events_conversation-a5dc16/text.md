Join Senator Emily Buss and former New Jersey Governor and EPA Administrator Christine Todd Whitman for a casual evening of conversation about the health of the Great Salt Lake, the environmental impact of data centers, and other environmental challenges facing Utah and the nation.
Hosted by the Emily Buss for Utah campaign in collaboration with the Forward Party of Utah and Stewardship Utah, the evening will feature a short conversation moderated by our partners at Stewardship Utah.
Senator Buss and Governor Whitman will discuss what state and federal governments can do to address these challenges, and the role independent lawmakers can play in advancing practical solutions across political divides.
A Q&A will follow, with plenty of time to meet and speak with both guests.
Light refreshments, charcuterie and desserts will be served.
Contributions start at $100.
All proceeds benefit the Emily Buss for Utah campaign.
