A Conversation About Utah’s Environment
Join Senator Emily Buss and former EPA Chief Christine Todd Whitman for a conversation about the Great Salt Lake, the impact of data centers, and environmental challenges facing Utah and the nation.
Stewardship Utah will moderate a short discussion and Q&A on the role independent lawmakers can play in finding solutions.
Guests will also have time to meet both speakers.
Contributions start at $100 and include admission for you and one guest.
