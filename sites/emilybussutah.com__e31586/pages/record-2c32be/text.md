Her legislative work, committee assignments and votes are public records.
This page brings that information together so District 11 residents can see what she has worked on since taking office.
Legislation Sponsored During the 2026 General Session, Emily sponsored six measures: SB 189 — School District Funding Amendments Legislation addressing funding for school districts experiencing significant growth.
SB 247 — Road Funding Amendments Legislation addressing transportation and road funding.
SB 291 — Student Transportation Amendments Legislation addressing student transportation.
SB 297 — School Social Worker Amendments Legislation addressing school social workers.
SB 320 — School-based Food Pantry Amendments Legislation addressing food pantries in schools.
SJR 17 — Resolution Encouraging Innovation in Legislative Analysis and Information Services A resolution focused on innovation in how legislative information and analysis are developed and used.
These measures were listed by the Legislature as filed at the conclusion of the 2026 General Session.
View Emily's Official Legislative Record Other Legislative Work Emily also served as Senate floor sponsor for legislation originating in the Utah House.
That included HB 22 , Vintage Vehicle Amendments , which was signed by the governor.
She also co-sponsored legislation on issues including first-responder health , pediatric care , and other matters considered during the 2026 session.
COMMITTEE WORK Emily serves on committees alongside Republican, Democratic and Forward Party legislators.
Her assignments have included the Senate Government Operations and Political Subdivisions Committee and the Senate Health and Human Services Committee , as well as the General Government Appropriations Subcommittee .
