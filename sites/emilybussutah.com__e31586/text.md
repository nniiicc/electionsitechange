Emily Buss Utah State Senate | District 11 Her approach is simple: look at each issue on its merits, listen to the people she represents, and make decisions based on what she believes is right for District 11 rather than automatically following a Republican or Democratic party position.
Emily joined the Utah Senate in December 2025 and is now running to continue representing District 11.
Meet Emily See Her Record Representing District 11 Representation should start with the people who live here.
Emily does not sit with the Republican or Democratic caucus.
Since taking office, her public voting record includes votes alongside Republicans, votes alongside Democrats, and votes where members of both parties were on the same side.
Her votes are public, and residents can see them for themselves.
Emily’s Plan for District 11 Issues Roads & Traffic District 11 includes some of Utah's fastest-growing communities.
Transportation infrastructure needs to keep pace with that growth, with greater attention on west-side roads, congestion, and long-term transportation planning.
Water, Energy, & Data Centers Large-scale development can bring significant demands on water, power, wastewater systems, and public infrastructure.
Emily supports greater transparency around those impacts so communities understand the costs and benefits before major decisions are made.
Cost of Living Housing, transportation, property taxes, childcare, and everyday expenses continue to put pressure on Utah families.
Emily's focus is on practical policy that addresses those pressures without creating additional burdens for residents.
Protecting Children Emily supports stronger accountability and penalties for child sexual abuse and policies that put the safety of children first.
