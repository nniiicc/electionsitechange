Bob Kressig Endorses Drew Stensland - My parents taught me to work hard, serve others, and that nothing changes unless someone is willing to lead.
Drew Stensland is a lifelong Cedar Falls resident raised in a union household by a firefighter and an AEA professional who taught him the value of service, accountability, and showing up for your community.
From an early age, Drew stepped up to lead, serving as Student Body President at Cedar Falls High School and as the first student representative on the Cedar Falls School Board.
At the University of Northern Iowa, Drew was elected Student Body President, where he expanded civic engagement, helped create a UNI student seat on the Cedar Falls City Council, and played a key role in implementing the #PanthersVote effort, one of the highest student turnout initiatives in the country.
For the last six years, Drew has worked in Iowa State Auditor Rob Sand’s office, where he leads the Public Innovations and Efficiencies (PIE) program, helping to save Iowa communities millions of dollars by making local government work better.
Drew continues to serve Cedar Falls and Black Hawk County as a local Democratic leader and community advocate. ​ ​ Now Drew is ready to step up and heed the call as our next representative for Iowa House District 75 Volunteer News Donate Are you in Iowa's House District 75?
