Nancy VanderMeer (608) 237-9170 Governor Signs VanderMeer Remote Dispensing Pharmacy Bill into Law Madison, WI – On April 2, 2026, Governor Evers signed legislation into law authored by Representative Nancy VanderMeer (R-Tomah) and Senator James (R-Thorp) to improve pharmacy access across the state, with a focus on rural and underserved communities facing closures, reduced hours, and workforce shortages that limit timely access to essential medications.
Senate Bill 832, now 2025 Act 168, updates statutes to allow any licensed pharmacy in Wisconsin to operate as a telepharmacy, which is defined as the delivery of pharmaceutical care by registered pharmacists to patients at a distance using telecommunications, provided it meets the existing safeguards established by the Pharmacy Examining Board.
“I’ve heard directly from residents across the 70th Assembly District just how vital legislation like this is to our communities.
I’m grateful the governor signed the bill into law,” VanderMeer shared.
### The 70th Assembly District includes Jackson County and portions of Monroe and Juneau counties
