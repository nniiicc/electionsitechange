Nancy VanderMeer (608) 266-8366 Statement on Biennial Budget Address Madison – Representative Nancy VanderMeer (R-Tomah) released the following statement on Governor Evers’ 2025-27 biennial budget address: “Our governor has proven himself over and over to be an inept financial manager incapable of grasping how to balance the state’s checkbook.
What we heard last night were largely some of his same, tired proposals that expand welfare and grow government and comically, some tough talk about President Trump and his policies.
I guess now that his friends Joe Biden and Kamala Harris have been categorically admonished by the people of Wisconsin and citizens throughout the country, he just now cares about the catastrophic damage that has been, and can be inflicted upon the residents of our great state via harmful policies originating out of Washington, D.C.
“Through responsible budgeting, legislative Republicans have built up the largest rainy day fund in state history and cut taxes by over $31 billion in the last decade.
I look forward to working with my colleagues to once again deliver a responsible budget for taxpayers throughout our state that promotes taxpayers keeping more of their own money, invests in infrastructure, prioritizes public safety, increases access to health care, and more.” ### The 70th Assembly District includes Jackson County and portions of Monroe and Juneau counties
