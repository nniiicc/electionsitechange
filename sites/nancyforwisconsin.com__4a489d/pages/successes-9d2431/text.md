REWARDING NANCY'S EFFORTS
SUCCESSES &
RECOGNITIONS
While official legislative listings detail her career milestones, specific organizational honors highlighting Representative Nancy VanderMeer's active 2025–2026 session include:
Outstanding Legislator Award
Bestowed by the Wisconsin Counties Association in recognition of her ongoing support and advocacy for local county governments and infrastructure needs.
Working for Wisconsin Award
Presented on behalf of Wisconsin Manufacturers & Commerce (WMC) for her pro-growth voting record supporting local businesses, regulatory streamlining, and workforce retention throughout the legislative session.
Legislator Leadership Recognition
Formally recognized by her colleagues and appointed to the leadership team as the Majority Caucus Secretary for the Wisconsin State Assembly during the 2025-2026 session.
Community Health Advocacy Recognition
Acknowledged informally by local rural health networks for her work authoring the Remote Pharmacy Dispensing Act (Act 168), which improved access to care in under-served communities.
