JOHN’S STORY
I Could Be Fishing.
Instead, I’m Running.
After 37 years of public service — in the Air Force and in civil service — John had earned the right to slow down.
But watching working families struggle and politics become about power instead of people, sitting on the sidelines stopped feeling like an option.
From a union household, to military service, to Hanford — John stepped up because Central Washington deserves a representative who has actually served.
