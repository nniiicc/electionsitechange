Senate Record My Resume Contributions Contact Dear North Kingstown and Exeter Residents, Many of us wonder what the future holds and whether our children and grandchildren will have the opportunity to achieve the American Dream.
How can we best protect and provide for our families—both today and tomorrow?
These are questions that touch all of us, regardless of our backgrounds or political beliefs.
Rhode Island should be a place where hard work is rewarded with opportunity, and where our communities, neighborhoods, and quality of life are protected for future generations.
If we are truly committed to solving the challenges before us, we must rise above our differences and recognize that what unites us as Americans is far greater than what divides us.
It is an ambitious goal, but for the sake of our children and the future of our state, it is one worth pursuing.
If I am honored to serve in the Rhode Island House of Representatives, I pledge to work every day to bring people together, seek practical solutions, and help build a stronger, safer, and more prosperous Rhode Island for everyone.
