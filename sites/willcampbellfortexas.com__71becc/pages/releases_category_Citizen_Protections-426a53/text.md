TPTRP - The Surplus Waterfall and Dedicated Funds
The Texas Property Tax Replacement Plan (TPTRP) comes with a key citizen protection - this consists of a surplus waterfall where entities are required to save surplus funds into predefined funds designed to remove dependence on bonds, and al return unused taxes back to the citizens.
Preventing government bloat and overspending with additional citizen protections.
