Will Campbell
I was born in Galveston, TX and raised all over Texas before graduating from Wimberley High School in 2003.
A few weeks after graduation, I went to MCRD San Diego to become a United States Marine, and then to Camp Pendleton, CA for infantry training to become MOS 0311 Rifleman Scout.
I served 10 years on active duty, serving in both OIF and OEF in combat, including Operation Phantom Fury (Fallujah Iraq) and Mountain Lion (Northern Hindu Kush Afghanistan).
During my last few years, I successfully trained 1600 Marines in Infantry tactics, techniques, and procedures, as well as over 600 foreign law enforcement and military personnel with various allies across the world.
I was honorably discharged in 2013 after injuring both of my hips, and started my civilian life with my wife and four children in California.
Eventually, we returned back to Texas, this time settling in the Dallas Fort Worth metroplex, starting in Arlington, ending in Cedar Hill, TX as of 2018 where we live today.
I currently work for a consulting firm based out of Georgia as the VP of Operations and Technology.
