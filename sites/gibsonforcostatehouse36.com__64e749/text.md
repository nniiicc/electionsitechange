Why I’m Running
I was asked to run by the Libertarian Party of Colorado because voters in House District 36 deserve a practical alternative to higher taxes, unnecessary regulations, and expanding government.
I believe government should respect individual liberty, defend constitutional rights, live within its means, and remain accountable to the people it serves.
I am running to bring that perspective—and a working Coloradan’s experience—to the State Capitol.
