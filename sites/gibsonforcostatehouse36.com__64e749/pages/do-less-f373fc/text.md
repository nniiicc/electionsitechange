That might sound strange coming from someone running for office.
Most politicians promise more—more programs, more spending, and more control over your daily life.
I’m making a different case: we’ve tried “more” for a long time, and it hasn’t made life simpler, freer, or more affordable.
Doing less doesn’t mean doing nothing.
It means doing what actually matters—and getting government out of the way of everything else.
It means fewer rules that don’t make sense.
Fewer taxes that punish hard work.
Fewer decisions made by people who don’t know you, don’t live your life, and won’t face the consequences.
And it means more space for you.
More room to build a business without jumping through hoops.
More freedom to make your own choices without asking permission.
More trust in individuals, families, and communities to solve problems better than distant institutions can.
I’m not promising to fix everything.
No honest candidate should.
But I am promising to tell you the truth: government doesn’t need to run your life to serve you well.
In fact, it serves you best when it knows its limits.
So yes—this campaign is about doing less.
Less overreach.
Less waste.
Less noise.
And in return, restoring something we’ve been losing for far too long: More freedom.
Yes, We Can Do Less—and live better because of it.
