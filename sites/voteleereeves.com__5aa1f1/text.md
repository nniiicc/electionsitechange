Lee earned his Bachelor of Arts from Emory University and his Juris Doctor from Georgetown University.
He and his wife, Claire, are raising their three children in Tennessee and are active members of their Christian faith community.
In his first term, Lee quickly established himself as an effective and thoughtful lawmaker, earning recognition as one of the Tennessee Journal Freshman of the Year as well as a recipient of the Team Kid Hero Award.
As he continues to serve the people of Williamson County, Lee is committed to providing access to quality education and developing Tennessee into the healthiest state in America.

Lee Reeves believes parents know what’s best for their children’s education.
That’s why he supports expanding Tennessee’s school choice program, giving more families access to high-quality educational options and ensuring students aren’t limited by their ZIP code.
Ensuring Tennesseans’ Health Rep.
Reeves is working to make Tennessee the healthiest state in the nation.
Drawing from his own experience with type 2 diabetes, he is advancing solutions focused on prevention and helping Tennesseans improve their health before chronic disease takes hold.

Reeves supports requiring public entities that hire lobbyists to disclose that activity and follow the same reporting rules as private organizations, ensuring accountability and restoring trust in government decision-making.
