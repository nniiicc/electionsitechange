Privacy Policy
This Privacy Notice for Friends of Mike McFall (“we,” “us,” or “our”) describes how and why we may access, collect, store, use, and share (“process”) your personal information when you use our services (“Services”), including when you:
- Visit our website at https://www.votemcfall.com or any website of ours that links to this Privacy Notice
- Sign up for email updates, volunteer information, or event invitations
- Make a donation through third-party platforms we link to
- Engage with us in other related ways, including campaign communications and events
If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our Services.
If you have questions or concerns, please contact us at info@mikemcfall.com.
SUMMARY OF KEY POINTS
This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice.
You can find more details by reviewing the full notice below.
- We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and contact information.
- Donations are processed by third-party platforms such as ActBlue.
We do not store full payment information.
- We use cookies and similar technologies for website functionality, analytics, and communications.
- We do not sell your personal information.
- Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information.

1.
WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?
Personal Information You Disclose to Us
In Short: We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us.
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you sign up for email updates, volunteer opportunities, or events; make a donation through linked third-party platforms; contact us; or otherwise interact with our Services.
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Contact preferences
Sensitive Information
In Short: We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information beyond political engagement.
We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information such as race, ethnicity, religion, sexual orientation, or health information.
Engagement with a political campaign may be inferred from your interactions with the Services.
Payment Information
Donations are processed by ActBlue.
We do not directly collect or store full credit card or bank account numbers.
Please review ActBlue’s privacy policy for more information.
If merchandise is offered in the future, payments may be processed by additional third-party providers, which will handle payment data according to their own privacy policies.
Information Automatically Collected
In Short: Some information is collected automatically.
- IP address
- Browser and device characteristics
- Operating system and language preferences
- Referring URLs
- Pages viewed and time spent on the site
Location Information
We may receive approximate location information (such as city or state) inferred from IP address.
We do not collect precise GPS location data.
Google APIs and Maps
Our website may include embedded Google Maps.
We do not receive personal location data from Google Maps.
Google’s data collection is governed by its own privacy policies.
Information From Other Sources
In Short: We may receive limited information from third-party platforms.
This may include information from social media platforms when you interact with our content, or from service providers that assist with campaign communications.
Lawful voter file use, if applicable in the future, is governed by election and privacy laws.
