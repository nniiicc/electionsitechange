New Hampshire has the nation’s largest state legislature, making it a hub for many ideas and perspectives.
As your representative, I have worked effectively within the House, bringing a fresh perspective to Concord that delivers results for the people of Franklin and Northfield.
Delivering for Parents and Students.
As a student who recently graduated high school, goes to college, and now serves in the legislature, I’ve seen the challenges within our public education system firsthand—both as a student and policymaker.
I’ve worked to advocate for students’ and parents’ rights, passing legislation to keep students safe and parents in charge of directing the upbringing of their children.
Advancing the New Hampshire Advantage .
New Hampshire remains a rare beacon of hope in a region of the country dominated by poor policies, and I’ve been pleased to help keep it that way.
Through my work in the House, I’ve supported gun rights, opposed the creation of state sales and income taxes, promoted more housing, and supported lowering government spending to lower taxes.
Let me continue this record so we can continue to help the Granite State thrive.
