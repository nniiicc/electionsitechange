My vision is a district where families feel safe, supported, and heard—and where leadership actually shows up.
In Georgia, 1 in 5 adults experience a mental health condition each year, yet more than half go without treatment.
Our families deserve better.
Our Mission I’m running to expand access to mental health care, fully fund public schools, and strengthen economic security for Georgia families.
In Forsyth County and across our district, rapid growth has outpaced investment in schools, infrastructure, and services.
Georgia ranks near the bottom nationally in access to mental health care, and our classrooms are still underfunded despite record state surpluses.
Leadership should mean showing up, listening, and delivering real results—not excuses.
(We’re in this together) THE REALITY FAMILIES ARE FACING • Georgia ranks in the bottom 10 states for access to mental health care (Mental Health America) • More than 1 million Georgians are uninsured • The recommended school counselor ratio is 1:250— many Georgia schools exceed 400–500 students per counselor • Housing costs in metro Atlanta have increased 30–40%+ in recent years • Many districts still spend less per student (adjusted for inflation) than before the 2008 recession Families are being asked to do more with less—and it’s not sustainable.
