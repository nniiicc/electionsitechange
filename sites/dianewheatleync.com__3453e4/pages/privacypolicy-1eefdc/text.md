Privacy Policy Diane Wheatley for NC House Effective Date: February 5, 2026 Diane Wheatley for NC House (“we,” “our,” or “the Campaign”) is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, make a contribution, or communicate with the Campaign, including via SMS/text messaging.
Information We Collect Personal Information You Provide We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including: Name Email address Mobile phone number Mailing address Employer and occupation (when required for campaign finance reporting) Information submitted through contact forms, volunteer sign-ups, or event RSVPs.
Payment information is processed securely by third-party payment processors.
We do not store full credit card numbers.
Automatically Collected Information When you visit our website, we may automatically collect limited technical data such as: IP address Browser type and device information Pages visited and time spent on the site This information is used only to operate and improve our website.
How We Use Your Information We use collected information to: Communicate campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities Send requested emails or text messages Process donations and comply with election laws Respond to inquiries Improve campaign outreach and website functionality Text Messaging & Mobile Communications If you opt in to receive SMS or MMS messages from the Campaign: Message and data rates may apply Message frequency may vary Consent is not a condition of purchase or donation You may opt out at any time by replying STOP You may request assistance by replying HELP Important Mobile Data Privacy Notice Mobile opt-in data will not be sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
Mobile messaging information is used only to deliver Campaign messages that you have consented to receive.
Sharing of Information We do not sell or rent your personal information.
All service providers are contractually required to protect your data and may not use it for any purpose other than providing services to the Campaign.
SMS opt-in data is never shared for marketing purposes.
Cookies & Tracking Technologies We may use cookies or similar technologies to ensure proper website functionality and basic analytics.
You can control cookie settings through your browser.
Your Choices Email: Unsubscribe using the link in any email SMS: Reply STOP to opt out at any time Access or deletion requests: Contact us using the information below We may retain certain information as required by law.
Data Security We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.
However, no system can be guaranteed 100% secure.
Children’s Privacy This website is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
External Links Our website may link to third-party websites.
We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
Policy Updates We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
