Alissa grew up barefoot in Houghton, NY, a town with six stop signs.
Before she could vote she had taken an oath to defend the United States , and spent years serving overseas, giving back to the country she loves.
Alissa worked at Baghram Airfield helping oversee flights into, out of, and around Afghanistan.
Information and photos presented here do not imply endorsement by the Department of the Army or the Department of Defense.
After returning from service Alissa was a teacher for underprivileged youths , helping her young neighbors turn their lives around while raising her daughters in Lockport.
In 2025 she was working at the Department of Veteran Affairs helping veterans secure their benefits when she was fired by Elon Musk during the cuts made by DOGE.
She became involved in her local community organizing town halls to talk about Claudia Tenney’s votes in Washington D.C.
After she got no response from Claudia for months, Alissa decided to run for congress.
For Alissa, public service means exactly that: service.
She believes in serving her country, showing up for her community, and putting people before personal ambition.
She’s not in this for herself.
She’s in it to serve ALL of us.
