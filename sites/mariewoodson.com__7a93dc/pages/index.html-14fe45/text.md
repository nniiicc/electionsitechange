A note of thanks.
My sincere appreciation to the residents, supporters, and volunteers for their unwavering support as I was re-elected as State Representative for District 105.
Since my first term in 2020, I have always been working for everyone in the District and Florida, and I will continue to bring dedicated, caring, and dependable leadership to all residents.
I am humbled and honored to continue the work.
During my previous terms, I sponsored, debated, and fought for legislation that benefits the lives of Floridians, delivering on my pledge to never forget the families, businesses, and communities who placed their confidence in me.
Some of the bills I filed were signed into law, and I secured over $6.7 million in the state budget for the District and local projects.
