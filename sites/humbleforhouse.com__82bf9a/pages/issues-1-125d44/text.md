Topics & Issues 2nd Amendment Ben Humble is a proud supporter of the Second Amendment and the constitutional right of law-abiding citizens to keep and bear arms.
As a lifelong hunter, sportsman, and defender of personal liberty, Ben believes firearm ownership is not just a tradition, but a safeguard against government overreach and a cornerstone of individual freedom.
In Harrisburg, he’ll stand firmly against any efforts to infringe on these rights and will support legislation that protects responsible gun ownership for future generations.
