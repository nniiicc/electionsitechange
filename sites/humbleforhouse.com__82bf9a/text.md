Ben Humble is running for the Pennsylvania House of Representatives to bring common-sense leadership back to Harrisburg.
A lifelong resident of the district and a key part of his family’s small business, Ben understands the real-world challenges facing families, workers, and small towns across Greene and southern Washington Counties.
From creating local jobs to protecting our rural way of life, Ben is ready to stand up and fight for the values that matter most to us.
About Ben Humble
Ben Humble is a lifelong Greene County resident and public servant running for State Representative in Pennsylvania’s 50th District.
A husband, father of two, and President of the Waynesburg Borough Council.
Ben brings real-world experience and a servant’s heart to the table.
He’s proudly pro-life, pro-Second Amendment, and a lifelong Republican committed to protecting our values and fighting for the hardworking people of our district — because this campaign isn’t about politics, it’s about We the People.
