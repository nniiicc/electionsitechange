Dave Shallenberger Shares Your Cares
Leadership You Can Trust
Role of Government
Limited government involvement with local control and fiscal responsibility.
- Government exists because of the Consent of the governed.
- Government should be restrained from intruding on the freedoms of its citizens.
- Protect our God-given rights as citizens.
Republican Party Platform
I fully support and affirm the Utah Republican Party Platform.
- Appreciate the productive citizen.
- Affirm the infinite worth of all individuals.
- Seek the best possible quality of life for all.
Constitution
I fully support the US Constitution and the Utah Constitution.
- I believe our constitutional government is based on moral and spiritual foundations.
I will demand honesty, integrity, morality, and accountability and work to expose and stop corruption.
- The Constitution was created to safeguard citizens through legal protections.
I firmly resist any excessive actions by the government.
- I will ensure our state’s conservative values are not forgotten and reaffirmed in the Legislature.
Education
My wife and I came through our local school system, and our kids are enrolled in our public schools.
It is my priority to ensure our schools are funded and focus on empowering parents, teachers, and students.
- Children should have access to quality education and innovation.
- Focusing on policy and programs at the local level can empower principals and teachers more effectively.
We can enhance their success in our communities by tailoring training and financial support to their specific needs.
- Parents have the primary right and responsibility to educate their children.
Teachers need parental support to maximize the learning experience.
- Strong international programs like Dual Language Immersion and STEM programs.
David has several lifelong friends from these—like Cesar from Madrid and Emil and Darko from Macedonia.
Environment
Conservation should be balanced with concern for economics and private property.
- Public access to public lands for recreational activities.
- Market-based solutions to protect the environment.
- Protect Utah lands and environmental rights.
Taxation
I believe in limited taxation to support limited government.
I also believe in citizen engagement.
When considering tax reform, I will work to ensure that all policy goes through a public vetting process.
- I believe in limited taxation to meet essential public needs.
- I agree with the Republican Party platform: taxation should be equitable, simple, and minimal.
- I will be a strong opponent to over-taxation and will oppose any bill that seeks to waste taxpayer money.
Economic Development
I support a strong, diversified local economy based on a well-trained and well-educated workforce that encourages a business-friendly environment, a safe workplace, and an entrepreneurial spirit.
- I will support legislation that encourages free enterprise and private initiatives.
I believe that market forces will dictate regulation and that the government’s role should be limited.
- I will promote the attraction of high-quality jobs to our district and encourage local educational opportunities to support these jobs.
- I will support business-friendly planning for economic growth, particularly with roadways, water usage, and air quality.
I believe economic growth (not higher taxes) will provide a greater funding pool.
Increased funding for the necessary support of the military, law enforcement, and educational needs within the local community.
Healthcare
I support changes to health care that reduce the burden placed on future generations.
- I will support free-market solutions to enhance quality and accessibility in healthcare.
- Support minimal government intervention, promoting innovation and competition.
- Support private health insurance options and health savings accounts.
- Support efforts to reduce healthcare costs and increase patient choice
