The ad features Leesa Weisz, Arizona’s first Homeland Security Director and a former prosecutor in the Arizona Attorney General’s Office, who credits Governor Hobbs for cracking down on financial fraud.
Weisz says that while many politicians only talk about taking on corruption, Hobbs took action to prevent further abuse and recover taxpayer money.
“Katie Hobbs took on scammers, stopped fraud, and helped recover $140 million for Arizona taxpayers,” said Michael Beyer, Communications Director for the Hobbs campaign.
“She led the fight to protect taxpayer dollars and hold bad actors accountable, and she’ll keep putting Arizonans first by cracking down on fraud, stopping corruption, and putting more money back in Arizonans’ pockets.” Watch “ Crackdown ” here and read a full transcript below: WATCH “ CRACKDOWN ”: LEESA WEISZ: During approximately 20 years that I served at the Arizona Attorney General’s office, the majority of my time was spent prosecuting fraud.
LEESA WEISZ: Governor Katie Hobbs led a massive crackdown on financial fraud in the state of Arizona.
She prevented further abuse and recovered taxpayer funds.
LEESA WEISZ: Governor Katie Hobbs is standing up to the scammers who are ripping off Arizona taxpayers.
