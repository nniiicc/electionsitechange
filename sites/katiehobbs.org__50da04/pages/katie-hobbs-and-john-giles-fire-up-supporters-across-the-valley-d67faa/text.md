Katie Hobbs and her running mate, former Mesa Mayor John Giles, rallied supporters and kicked off canvass launches as part of the Copper State Victory coordinated campaign’s last weekend of voter contact before Arizonans start voting.
Katie rallied volunteers and organizers at the Peoria Copper State Victory coordinated campaign office’s canvass launch, emphasizing the importance of their work before they headed out to talk with voters about the stakes of this election.
Meanwhile, John headed to his hometown of Mesa, where he joined organizers and volunteers at the East Valley campaign office.
He thanked the canvassers and reminded them that the deadline to register to vote is tomorrow, urging them to spread the word.
Today’s events are part of a broader effort to meet voters where they are, backed by a robust statewide field program reaching communities across Arizona.
From Scottsdale and Prescott to Tucson, Sierra Vista, Yuma, Maryvale, Bullhead City, and beyond, Copper State Victory canvassers are knocking doors and talking directly with Arizonans in their communities.
