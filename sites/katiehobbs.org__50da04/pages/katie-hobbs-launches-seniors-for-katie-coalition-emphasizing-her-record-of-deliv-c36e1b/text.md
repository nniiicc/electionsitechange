Katie Hobbs and Mayor John Giles launched Seniors for Katie, a coalition consisting of senior leaders and community members working to re-elect her.
Hobbs marked the launch in Casa Grande, alongside President of the Arizona Alliance for Retired Americans, Linda Somo, and Executive Director of the Arizona Alliance for Retired Americans, Dora Vasquez.
Hobbs highlighted her commitment to helping older Arizonans age with dignity by lowering the cost of prescription drugs, cutting taxes for seniors, expanding affordable housing options, and strengthening retirement security across the state.
“Every Arizonan deserves to age with dignity, security, and access to quality healthcare,” said Gov.
Katie Hobbs.
“That’s why, as governor, I’ve fought to lower costs, expand access to care, erase $1 billion in medical debt for nearly 700,000 Arizonans, and strengthen services and support systems that our seniors rely on.
Our seniors deserve top-of-the-line care after a lifetime of hard work, and we’re fighting to deliver that.” The Arizona Alliance for Retired Americans, a grassroots organization dedicated to protecting the health, economic security, and civil rights of older Arizonans, endorsed Katie’s campaign because of her record of cutting taxes for seniors, lowering the cost of critical medications, and expanding affordable housing options.
As governor, Katie has delivered on the issues that matter most to Arizona seniors: lowering costs and expanding access to healthcare.
This year, she signed a bipartisan budget providing $1.4 billion in tax relief for hardworking Arizonans, including a larger standard deduction for 88 percent of taxpayers, a new $6,000 deduction for Arizona seniors, and the elimination of taxes on tips and overtime pay.
She has also fought to expand access to care, lower prescription drug costs, erase $1 billion in medical debt for nearly 700,000 Arizonans, and strengthen services and support systems that help Arizona seniors get the care and stability they’ve earned after a lifetime of hard work.
