Katie Hobbs embarked on her Arizona First Tour , crisscrossing the state to hear firsthand the challenges people are facing and share her vision for moving Arizona forward.
From making tortillas at a family-owned restaurant in Yuma to visiting a seventh-generation farm in Cochise County, opening campaign offices, listening to healthcare providers, and meeting with local business owners and supporters, Katie Hobbs traveled across the state to hear directly from Arizonans and highlight her record of lowering costs and growing Arizona’s economy.
The Arizona First Tour brought her to Gilbert , Prescott , Yuma , Scottsdale , Pearce , Sierra Vista , and Tucson , where she met with healthcare professionals, social workers, farmers, restaurant owners, brewers, volunteers, and working families.
Along the way, she heard firsthand how Washington’s cost-hiking agenda—from tariffs driving up the price of essential goods to rising healthcare costs—is affecting Arizona families and small businesses.
Throughout the week, Hobbs visited Alliance Behavioral Care in Gilbert to discuss expanding access to affordable healthcare; met with the owners of Wild Iris Coffee, The Chile Pepper, Tombstone Brewing Company, and the New York Pizza Department to hear how Washington’s tariffs and misguided policies are affecting local businesses; toured Curry Seed & Chile Company with seventh-generation farmer Ed Curry to discuss water security and agriculture; celebrated National Drive-Thru Day at the Burger King where she worked in high school; and opened Copper State Victory coordinated campaign offices in Prescott, Yuma, Scottsdale, and Sierra Vista, continuing to build the grassroots infrastructure needed to win in November.
The tour concluded in Tucson, where Hobbs launched a weekend of action canvass, visited another local small business, and rallied nearly 700 supporters alongside Senator Mark Kelly, former Congresswoman Gabby Giffords, Congresswoman Adelita Grijalva, Mayor Regina Romero, and congressional candidate JoAnna Mendoza, emphasizing the campaign’s momentum and her commitment to continuing to lower costs and deliver for Arizona families.
