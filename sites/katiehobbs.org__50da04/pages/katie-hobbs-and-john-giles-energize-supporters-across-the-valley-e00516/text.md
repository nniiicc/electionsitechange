MARYVALE – Today, Gov.
Katie Hobbs and her running mate, former Mesa Mayor John Giles, rallied supporters and kicked off canvass launches as part of the Copper State Victory coordinated campaign’s second weekend of action in the general election.
Katie headed to the West Valley where she met with volunteers and organizers at the Maryvale Copper State Victory coordinated campaign office’s canvass launch.
There she emphasized the importance of their work before they headed out to talk to Arizonans about the stakes of this election.
Meanwhile, John visited the Scottsdale office where he thanked canvassers and volunteers and rallied them before they headed out to knock doors.
Today’s events are part of a broader effort to meet voters where they are, backed by a robust statewide field program reaching communities across Arizona.
From Scottsdale and Prescott to Tucson, Sierra Vista, Yuma, Maryvale, Bullhead City, and beyond, Copper State Victory canvassers are knocking doors and talking directly with Arizonans in their communities.
