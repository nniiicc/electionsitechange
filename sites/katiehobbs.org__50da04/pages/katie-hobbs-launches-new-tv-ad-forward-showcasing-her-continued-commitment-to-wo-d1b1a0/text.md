It contrasts that record with Andy Biggs, who has spent his time in Washington backing policies that leave Arizona families paying more.
The ad then looks ahead to what Hobbs will keep fighting for in her second term: protecting Arizona’s water, cracking down on wasteful spending, and making life more affordable for working families.
“Katie Hobbs has kept her promise to bring people together to solve Arizona’s toughest challenges, and she has the record to show for it.
Meanwhile, Andy Biggs has spent his time in Washington backing policies that’ve left working families paying more,” said Michael Beyer, Communications Director for Elect Katie Hobbs.
“Arizonans deserve a governor who keeps moving this state forward, and that’s exactly what Katie will do in her second term: protect our water supply, crack down on fraud, waste, and abuse, and lower everyday costs.” Watch “Forward” here and read a full transcript below.
WATCH “FORWARD” KATIE HOBBS: Arizona, it’s been the honor of my life to serve as your governor.
HOBBS: We’ve done a lot to make our state more affordable and safer.
HOBBS: But Andy Biggs, he’s spent his time in Washington backing extreme policies that have driven up prices.
HOBBS : Here in Arizona, we know how to work together.
HOBBS: We’ll keep protecting our water, crack down on fraud and wasteful spending, and prioritize lowering costs.
HOBBS: There’s so much more to do, so let’s get to work.
Together we can keep Arizona moving forward.
