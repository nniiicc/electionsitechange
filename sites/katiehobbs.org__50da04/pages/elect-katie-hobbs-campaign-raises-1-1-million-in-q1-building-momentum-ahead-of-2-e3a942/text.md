Katie Hobbs announced that her campaign committee, Elect Katie Hobbs, has raised over $1.1 million in the first quarter and has over $4 million cash-on-hand ending the quarter.
In just the last three months, the campaign earned the support of over ten thousand donors, with more than 93 percent of contributions under $100.
Contributions also came from every county in Arizona, and 40 percent of contributions were from brand-new donors, showing that supporters in every corner of Arizona are energized to send Gov.
Hobbs back for a second term in 2026.
“From Kingman to Nogales and Vernon to Yuma, Gov.
Hobbs is receiving strong support because Arizonans are ready to re-elect a problem-solver who understands the struggles Arizona families face, having lived them herself,” said Nicole DeMont, Gov.
Hobbs’ Campaign Manager.
“Our campaign is proud to be fueled by grassroots supporters in every county in Arizona who know that Gov.
Hobbs is focused on real solutions, including securing the border, cutting everyday costs, and putting aside petty partisan politics to get things done for Arizona families.” These strong fundraising numbers build on the momentum of a new poll this month showing Gov.
Hobbs with a +14 positive job approval rating.
As Republicans continue to fight amongst themselves over who is more out-of-touch with Arizona families, Gov.
Hobbs will continue to roll up her sleeves and find commonsense solutions for all Arizonans.
