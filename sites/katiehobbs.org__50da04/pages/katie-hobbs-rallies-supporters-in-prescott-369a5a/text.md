PRESCOTT – Today, Gov.
Katie Hobbs launched a canvass in Prescott as part of the Copper State Victory coordinated campaign’s last weekend of voter contact before Arizonans start voting.
“Right now, Washington is making life more expensive for Arizonans across the board— from groceries to gas to housing.
This election offers us a clear choice between a governor who is focused on lowering costs and creating opportunities for Arizonans to thrive, or one focused on backing Washington’s cost-hiking agenda,” said Gov.
Katie Hobbs.
“That’s why it’s so important we turn out to vote this November, and why the work our volunteers and organizers are doing every day is vital in this election.”
Today’s events are part of a broader effort to meet voters where they are, backed by a robust statewide field program reaching communities across Arizona.
From Scottsdale and Prescott to Tucson, Sierra Vista, Yuma, Maryvale, Bullhead City, and beyond, Copper State Victory canvassers are knocking doors and talking directly with Arizonans in their communities.
