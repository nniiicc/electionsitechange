National Recognition: Jeff Wilson Featured in Nature on Energy Leadership 4/17/26
Jeff Wilson was recently featured in Nature, one of the world’s leading scientific journals, for his perspective on energy policy and the future of American power.
In the article, Jeff highlights the need for strong, practical leadership to secure America’s energy future.
Drawing on his experience as a nuclear engineer and U.S.
Navy veteran, he emphasizes the importance of energy independence and expanding nuclear power.
“Illinois needs someone that’s articulate, scientific-minded, practical-minded, and that can provide common-sense solutions, particularly with energy,” Wilson said.
Illinois leads the nation with 11 nuclear reactors, and Jeff points to the real impact this has, from reliable energy to job creation across the state.
Jeff’s focus is simple: strengthen American energy, support innovation, and deliver results that benefit working families.

CHAMPAIGN, Ill.
(WCIA) — The final day to cast your vote in the primary elections is just weeks away, and Monday night, two Republican candidates were trying to gain support for their run representing the 13th congressional district.
Republican candidates Joshua Loyd and Jeff Wilson used the forum to let voters in Champaign know where they stand on issues like renewable energy, education and agriculture, among others.
And they acknowledged the need to get people voting during the historically low turnout primary season.
