ABOUT JEFF
WILSON
Jeff Wilson is a U.S.
Navy Veteran, public servant, proven leader, and Republican running for Congress to support President Trump with America First policies.
As a former crew member on a nuclear-powered submarine and past U.S.
Naval Reserve commissioned officer, Jeff gained invaluable leadership qualifications in strategic planning and crisis management.
His military service instilled in him a deep love of America, devotion to duty, and commitment to national security.
With a master’s degree in Nuclear Engineering from the University of Illinois and numerous leadership roles in the Nuclear Industry, Jeff knows the vital importance of maximizing America’s abundant resources to ensure our country’s energy dominance and security.
Jeff, along with his wonderful wife, Becky, is deeply rooted in the community he serves.
They are very proud of their 8 children and 11 grandchildren.
As a husband, father and grandfather, Jeff knows how essential it is to love and protect families.
When the family flourishes, America prospers.
Now serving on the Champaign County Board, Jeff has fought for fiscal responsibility, transparency, and policies that prioritize people.
His background gives him a unique perspective on veterans’ issues, public safety, and economic growth.
He decided to run for Congress because he wanted to bring principled leadership and a results-oriented approach to Washington, grounded in his lifelong commitment to Christian faith.
Jeff is running for Congress to restore American values, work with President Trump to continue to secure our borders, make the economy work for all, and protect individual freedoms in the Land of Lincoln.
He is dedicated to serving with integrity and honor and has a clear vision for a stronger Illinois and a stronger America.
