Kyle is a Wichita-born native to the area.
He is a husband and father, and enjoys bike rides, gardening, and spending time with family and friends over a meal.
With a wide range of perspectives from nearly a decade of public school teaching experience, as well as from employment at Spirit, a fellowship sponsored by AARP Kansas, and volunteer work in the community, non-profits, and with his church, Kyle brings a clear vision for a healthier and more prosperous Kansas.
He now works as a local CDL delivery driver, which he enjoys.
Kyle is running for the Kansas House of Representatives to stand up for the rights and well-being of all Kansans.
