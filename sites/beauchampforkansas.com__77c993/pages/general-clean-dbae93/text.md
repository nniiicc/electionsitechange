District 82 House District 82 is where small-town values meet growing opportunity.
Stretching across parts of Derby and Mulvane, Sedgwick and Sumner counties, the district is home to families, small businesses, farmers, veterans, educators, and hardworking people from all walks of life who care deeply about their community and the people within.
Our district is built on strong public schools, safe neighborhoods, and a time-honored tradition of helping our neighbors when they need a hand up.
As our communities continue to thrive, we have an opportunity to invest in better housing and development, reliable roads, and accessible transportation, quality healthcare, excellent schools, and an economy that allows families to put down roots and thrive. ​ In Topeka, Kyle will serve the people of District 82 (and beyond) as a representative who puts people and good policy before politics.
