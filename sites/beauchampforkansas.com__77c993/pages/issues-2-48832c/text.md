Affordability
Every Kansan deserves the chance to build a stable, secure future.
We'll lower taxes for working families, make housing more affordable, reduce everyday costs, and put more money back into people's pockets.
By creating good-paying jobs and making smart investments in our communities, we can make Kansas a place where families don't just get by—they get ahead.
Tax Reform
Lower taxes help families, strengthen communities, and create more opportunities for everyone.
Allowing people to keep more of what they earn puts more money back into household budgets for savings, everyday expenses, and future goals.
At the same time, tax policies should provide stability and predictability for homeowners by helping protect against rapidly rising property tax burdens.
A fair, balanced tax system can support both economic growth and long-term financial security.
Education
Fully funding education and special education is essential to ensuring that every student has the opportunity to succeed.
I plan to support students, teachers, and parents by fully funding public education and special education so schools have the resources they need.
Investing in education helps reduce class sizes, improve student outcomes, provide necessary support services, and strengthen communities by preparing future generations for success.
Educators and school staff should also earn a thriving wage that reflects the vital role they play in shaping Kansas' future.
Healthcare
Healthcare for Every Kansan Every Kansan deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare, no matter where they live.
We'll strengthen and protect our rural hospitals so families don't have to drive hours for emergency care or basic medical services.
By ensuring larger hospitals and healthcare systems do their fair share, we can reduce the strain on small community hospitals and keep healthcare available in every corner of Kansas.
