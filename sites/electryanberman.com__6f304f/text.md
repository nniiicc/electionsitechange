Ryan Berman A Brief Introduction Meet Ryan Ryan Berman is a former two-term state representative who won a landslide re-election race in 2020 despite Joe Biden winning the contested swing district and record-setting spending by his democratic opponent.
Ryan is a married father of two and life-long Michigan resident.
