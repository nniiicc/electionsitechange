Issues
Public Safety
The health, safety, and welfare of Michigan residents is of the utmost importance, and our state deserves a defender like Ryan.
Ryan has an extensive background in law enforcement and has forged strong relationships with law enforcement officials across Michigan, sponsored legislation to improve funding and training for police officers, and fiercely advocated against efforts to “defund the police.”
As a state senator, Ryan will support our law enforcement, protect our communities, and ensure that our first responders have the resources they need.
Government Transparency and Accountability
Ryan has always believed our state government should be accountable to the taxpayers that fund it.
As a state senator, Ryan will continue his efforts to build trust in government by advancing transparency, accountability, and integrity within all levels of government.
A Prosperous Michigan
As a state representative, Ryan championed reforms to make it easier to live, work, and raise a family in our state.
Unfortunately, partisan politics and abusive government overreach have worked against these ideals contributing to our small businesses and economy being decimated.
As a state senator, Ryan will put people over politics and will combat abusive government overreach that has wreaked havoc on our communities.
Education
Positioning future generations for success by providing them the best education possible has always been at the top of Ryan’s priority list.
He has been a fierce advocate for the schools, teachers, and students of our community.
Ryan supported the largest investment for students in state history, fought for funding increases in early education programs, and sponsored legislation providing $1.3 billion to schools so that they had the resources to craft educational and safety standards while COVID-19 impacted the state.
Consumer Protection
In the legislature, Ryan worked to protect consumers and Michigan families.
He was a leader in helping sexual assault survivors pursue justice, fighting to protect nursing home residents from dangerous government policies, and helping catastrophic auto-accident survivors keep their coverage to maintain the comfort and care they deserve.
As a state senator, Ryan will continue his commitment to protecting consumers and Michigan families.
