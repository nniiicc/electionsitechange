Strengthen the Criminal Justice System Lisa's Bachelor's Degree in Political Science with an emphasis on Public Law and a minor in Constitutional Studies, coupled with nearly four years as the Senior Policy Advisor for Commissioner Tom Sakievich, Lisa brings her Criminal Justice System (Law enforcement, Court system, Corrections) knowledge and experience to the State Legislature.
Lisa’s well-rounded understanding of the criminal justice system, gained during her time as a senior policy advisor, provides valuable insight in her role as a member of the House Law Enforcement and Criminal Justice Committee.
