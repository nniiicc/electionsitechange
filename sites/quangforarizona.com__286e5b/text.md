Key Legislative Achievements Anti-Communism Education (HB 2008, 2022): Requires Arizona high schools to include anti-communism curriculum in social studies, sharing stories of those who fled communist regimes.
Fentanyl Trafficking Crackdown (HB 2607, 2025): Imposes enhanced mandatory sentencing for fentanyl dealers, including a five-year minimum for 100+ grams, strengthening penalties to protect communities.
Public Safety & Law Enforcement: Advanced measures, including a ban on child-like sex dolls, expanded national criminal record access for DPS, and helped secure over $70 million for law enforcement, veterans, and roads.
Second Amendment Defense: As House Judiciary Chairman, blocked more than anti-2A bills and earned multiple Legislator of the Year awards from pro-Second Amendment groups.
