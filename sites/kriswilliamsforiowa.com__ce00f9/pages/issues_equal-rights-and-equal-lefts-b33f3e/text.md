It is past time to stop mere advocacy and take concrete action to protect these rights.
One of my biggest critiques of the status quo is the reluctance of officials to stand strong on their beliefs without folding to political convenience; it’s a credibility issue that fails their constituents.
I believe in the simple principle: "Your right to swing your fist ends where another person's nose begins." Our laws should protect people from harm, not police feelings.
LGBTQ+ Rights: Love is Love No one is trying to "turn" anyone else gay.
If a husband and wife can share a kiss in public, so can anyone else.
Transgender Iowans want the same things we all do: to support their families, access healthcare without financial ruin, and live with respect.
They belong in sports consistent with their gender identity; studies show trans women do not have an unfair advantage and are not overrepresented among top athletes.
This isn't about biology—it's about dignity.
Women's Rights: Protect Bodily Autonomy I am pro-choice because it is both the right and the popular position.
Every state that has put abortion rights on the ballot has voted to protect them.
Yet in Iowa, dangerous laws are causing harm: women are facing permanent damage or even death, we've lost OBGYNs and medical workers, and young families are leaving.
Now, online extremists are targeting women's right to vote.
We must lock in these protections and not budge an inch.
Immigration Rights: A Path to Dignity Comprehensive immigration reform (Mass Amnesty) is more popular than mass deportation.
Immigrants built this country, but we’ve seen federal agents terrorize communities, even murder, over paperwork.
Meanwhile, the process takes decades, and employers who exploit undocumented workers get a slap on the wrist.
We need efficient, responsible background checks and a clear path to status.
This protects workers from slave wages and strengthens labor rights for everyone.
Iowa should become a sanctuary state, ending local collaboration with ICE attacks on our neighbors.
As one of the few states not in what the ACLU calls "constitution-free zones," we deserve basic protections, not having people attacked at their homes and place of work.
Labor Rights: Restore Worker Power Iowa’s so-called "right-to-work" laws are a lie that only protects an employer's right to fire you without cause.
Unions are the backbone of the 40-hour work week, weekends, and fair wages.
We need stronger protections for unions, easier organizing processes, and strict penalties for companies that break labor laws.
It’s time to tip the scales back in favor of workers.
When did it become radical to say, "treat others how you want to be treated"?
Why is a dignified life under attack by ultra-wealthy corporations?
I will fight to defend the rights of all Iowans.
