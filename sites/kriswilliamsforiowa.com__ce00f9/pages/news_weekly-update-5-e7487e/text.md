Weekly update #5
The Kris Williams for Iowa House campaign team was hard at work again this week!
Here is the weekly update:
What got done:
- Endorsements:
- Run for Something (RFS) - a national organization that recruits and supports the next generation of progressive leaders for state and local office.
They’ve already helped elect over 1,400 candidates across the country, and now their grassroots army and experience is standing with us in IA-HD08.
- Iowa Progressive Democrats of America (PDA Iowa) -the grassroots arm of the progressive movement – fighting for Medicare for All, a Green New Deal, worker rights, and getting big money out of politics.
They don't endorse establishment-backed candidates who take corporate PAC money.
- We also received the 2026 Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate distinction.
Kris Williams holds to a strong commitment to common-sense gun safety.
- Attended District 4 Caucus (5/2) - Kris went as a delegate and served on the platform committee.
He was nominated for the State Central Committee and State Platform Committee.
Unfortunately, he was not selected for the Central Committee, and he is waiting to hear the Platform Committee results.
He spoke with other delegates who care about clean water, fair taxes, and putting people over profit.
There was positivity surrounding the points Kris made about party change and serving working people.
- Attended the Rob Sand strategy session (5/6) - It was exciting to see the large turnout of folks excited about Sand for governor.
We discussed future opportunities for coordinated campaigns and heard about Sand's strategy moving forward in his campaign.
- Hosted the second weekly office hour (5/7) - livestream office hour for questions and a behind-the-scenes look at the work of a first-time candidate.
Will be weekly on Thursdays, 7-8 PM.
