Sep 21 2026 PRESS RELEASE Weekly Update 9/21 43 days out - park events, SALT Center, and postcard shoutout What we did Good Morning!
6 weeks until the election.
Last week we had a couple of park events and met some new supporters.
The Pride Cookout at Snell Park was great, getting to meet many of the folks behind the scenes of Pridefest and hear about their biggest concerns.
Shockingly, much of it was affordability and being sick of the status quo politics.
Kris toured the Shining A Light Together (S.A.L.T.) Center , a resource hub for people experiencing homelessness.
They are a warming and cooling shelter and help with: ID Health Insurance / Medicaid Food Stamps / SNAP Job Applications Housing Mail Address Phone use Medical Transportation Doctor Appointments Clothes Other needs Location - First Baptist Church - 28 N 10th St, Fort Dodge Online - Facebook page Ongoing need - Men’s clothes - sizes 28-38 waist pants, and S-M shirts.
