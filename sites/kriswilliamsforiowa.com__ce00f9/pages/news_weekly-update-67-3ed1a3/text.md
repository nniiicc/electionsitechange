May 22 2026 PRESS RELEASE Weekly update #6/7 We hope you all had a great week.
Kris Williams and his team have once again been working hard to get him elected in District 8.
Here is the week 6 update: What got done: Got endorsement from Next Century Forum , which backs candidates who represent the best interests of younger generations.
We are very grateful for their support.
A little about NCF: ¨Founded in 2025, Next Century Forum is a network of young progressives building power across the Des Moines metro and beyond.
We’re organizing, learning, and showing up for the issues that matter—locally and statewide.
From city halls to the statehouse, we’re here to make Iowa’s next century more inclusive, equitable, and unapologetically bold.¨ Attended “Make It OK” mental health event – they spoke about the urgent need for accessible mental health services, including for veterans.
We can’t keep treating mental health as an afterthought, and it should be as important as physical health.
Attended City Council meeting – a provision passed to help cover fire department costs for non‑EMS falls (especially at nursing homes, where insurance expenses for a specific ‘care,’ but just sends first responders).
This will help the city budget and ensure fair reimbursement.
