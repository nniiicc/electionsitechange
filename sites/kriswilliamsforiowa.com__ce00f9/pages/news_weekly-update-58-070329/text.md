Hey Everyone, Back to our weekly email updates – July was BUSY!
The campaign is being well received, and I thought you all might like a peek behind the curtain (maybe it's because I'm a nerd).
I wanted to show you how District 8 actually stacks up.
As the state party might've written us off, the numbers tell a different story.
Among likely voters, we're looking at 34% Democrats, 45% Republicans, and 13% Independents – plus a solid base of Independent voters we're reaching every day.
That's all before we talk about reaching out to voters across the spectrum, and those we inspire to sign up!
We know people power beats money power.
When campaigns focus on the people they're looking to serve – instead of wealthy donors – we win.
