Oct 7 2026 PRESS RELEASE Weekly Update 10/7 4 weeks out - Forum, Fundraiser, and Friends What we did Hey, # days until the election!
Last night was the candidate forum, and this Sunday is our Spaghetti fundraiser.
Events Meet Chris Jones & Clean Water Candidates in Kiron Friday, October 9th @ 5:30 PM Kiron Memorial Park, 301 South Grove Street, Kiron, Iowa Come hear from Secretary of Agriculture and Land Stewardship candidate Chris Jones and other local Clean Water Candidates!
Also speaking: Rachel Burns for Iowa House Dustin Durbin for Iowa House Kris Williams for Iowa House Bill Owens for Iowa House Spaghetti and Live Music Fundraiser Sunday, October 11th @ 6 PM Snell-Crawford Park, Fort Dodge, Iowa Come support Kris, enjoy live music, and some dinner!

Friend-raising Push We all have those friends and family who are politically homeless, or those who’ve never voted.
We have about 200 people we email, if half help get 10 of their friends to vote, we win.
If you have a friends/family that is frustrated, or knows we aren’t better off than we were #ago, let’s talk.
Kris is nearing 3k doors knocked personally, across the spectrum, and many being people who haven’t voted in years or this might be their first time.
We are strong believers in giving people a reason to vote, not just “lesser evil” voting.
Every vote counts!
