Karaoke with the Candidates!
Get excited for Karaoke with the Candidates on August 15th from 6-9 pm!
The event will be at River Hops Brewing in Fort Dodge, IA.
You will get a chance to hear from the following speakers:
Kris Williams - candidate for Iowa House district 8
Rachel Burns- candidate for Iowa House district 7
India May - candidate for Iowa House district 58
Dave Dawson - candidate for US Congress
John Norwood - candidate for IA State treasurer
State and National candidates will speak at 7, Iowa House candidates will speak around 8 pm.
If you are a candidate and would like to join in on the fun, it's not too late to reach out!
All are welcome to come on out and get your groove on!
