CAMPAIGN ON!
Officially on the ballot with paperwork filed, graduated (finished classes), and Website Update!
Williams is challenging the failure of “trickle-down” economics, noting that CEO pay has skyrocketed to nearly 300 times that of their workers within a single generation.
His campaign commits to delivering tangible results for Iowans, contrasting his vision with what he describes as an ineffective status quo.
A Platform for Working Iowans: The Williams campaign is built on a foundation of actionable policies designed to address the daily struggles of Iowa families: People Over Profit: Raising the minimum wage to a living wage, expanding public transportation, protecting consumers and small businesses from corporate monopolies, and investing in publicly owned clean energy.
Fully Fund Public Schools: Halting the diversion of public dollars to private institutions and guaranteeing every child a great education.
Health Care for All: Ensuring every Iowan has a right to accessible, affordable healthcare.
Improve Water Quality: Implementing common-sense protections for our drinking water and closing loopholes for agricultural and industrial runoff.
Universal Childcare: Treating childcare as an essential public good, not a luxury commodity, to support working families.
“This campaign is about making government work for hard-working people,” Williams stated.
