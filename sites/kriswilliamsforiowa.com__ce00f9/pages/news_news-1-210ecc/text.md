A Platform for Working Iowans:
The Williams campaign is built on a foundation of actionable policies designed to address the daily struggles of Iowa families:
- People Over Profit: Raising the minimum wage to a living wage, expanding public transportation, protecting consumers and small businesses from corporate monopolies, and investing in publicly owned clean energy.
- Fully Fund Public Schools: Halting the diversion of public dollars to private institutions and guaranteeing every child a great education.
- Health Care for All: Ensuring every Iowan has a right to accessible, affordable healthcare.
- Improve Water Quality: Implementing common-sense protections for our
- drinking water and closing loopholes for agricultural and industrial runoff.
- Universal Childcare: Treating childcare as an essential public good, not a luxury commodity, to support working families.
“This campaign is about making government work for hard-working people,” Williams stated.
“I’m not here to be nice, but to deliver real results.”
