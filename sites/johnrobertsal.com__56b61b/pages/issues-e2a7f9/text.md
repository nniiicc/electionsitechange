top of page GET INVOLVED DONATE HOME ABOUT THE ISSUES LOWERING THE COST OF LIVING Our fight against inflation and rising costs requires strong leadership in Montgomery that prioritizes fiscal discipline and eliminates wasteful spending.
By cutting unnecessary programs and rolling back job-killing regulations, we can ensure Alabamians keep more of their hard-earned money through lower taxes while unlocking the full potential of our local economy.
Success lies in strategic investments in workforce development, partnering with key institutions like UAH and Calhoun Community College, and equipping Alabamians with the high-demand skills needed to secure good-paying jobs and build a brighter future.
DEMANDING NORTH ALABAMA'S FAIR SHARE For too long, Montgomery has treated North Alabama like its personal ATM, sending our tax dollars down I-65 with little return.
I will advocate relentlessly for our district and ensure that our money comes back home, where it belongs.
When North Alabama receives the resources it generates and deserves, our families prosper, businesses expand, and the entire state benefits.
HONORING ALABAMA'S HEROES AND SUPPORTING OUR MILITARY We owe an immeasurable debt to Alabama’s veterans, active-duty military personnel, and their families.
I will make sure they receive the first-rate benefits, healthcare (including critical mental health services), and employment opportunities they have earned through their sacrifice.
Strengthening Redstone Arsenal and supporting the vital defense and aerospace industries is paramount for our region’s economy and our nation’s security.
INVESTING STRATEGICALLY IN NORTH ALABAMA'S INFRASTRUCTURE North Alabama is the economic engine of our state, and we deserve infrastructure that matches our contributions.
For too long, we’ve received only pennies on the dollar from Montgomery to support the fastest-growing region in Alabama.
This neglect must end.
I will fight for the critical investments we need to expand highways, repair aging bridges, and deploy modern digital infrastructure.
These improvements are essential to keeping our region competitive and ensuring North Alabama continues to drive our state’s economy forward.
EMPOWERING PARENTS WITH EDUCATIONAL FREEDOM AND EXCELLENCE Parents—not career bureaucrats in Montgomery or Washington—must have the final say in their child’s education.
I will support policies that guarantee curriculum transparency and expand school choice, empowering families to select the best educational environment, whether public, private, charter, or homeschool.
We must equip teachers with resources to tackle learning loss and boost literacy while demanding that our schools focus on academic excellence.
CHAMPIONING ALABAMA'S FARMERS Alabama’s farmers are central to our economy and heritage.
I will fight to cut burdensome government regulations, expand domestic and international market access, and foster agricultural innovation.
We will prioritize the protection of our precious farmland from federal overreach and hostile foreign ownership, especially China.
Promoting fair trade and supporting our farmers ensures food security, strengthens communities, and preserves Alabama’s identity.
FUELING INNOVATION AND HIGH-TECH JOBS IN NORTH ALABAMA North Alabama is a national leader in innovation, anchored by assets like Redstone Arsenal, Cummings Research Park, and our world-class universities.
To fully unlock our region’s potential, I’ll champion STEM education, forge stronger ties between industry and education, and help attract investment in cutting-edge sectors like artificial intelligence, advanced manufacturing, and aerospace.
By seizing these opportunities, we can create high-paying jobs and build a stronger, more prosperous future for North Alabama.
ACHIEVING AMERICAN ENERGY DOMINANCE Alabama must take the lead in securing American energy independence by championing an "All-of-the-Above" strategy that ensures reliable, affordable energy for every Alabamian.
This approach means responsibly harnessing all of our God-given resources—coal, natural gas, nuclear power, and renewables—while cutting burdensome regulations that stifle production and drive up costs for families.
By lowering power bills for families, we can create good-paying jobs, lower energy costs, and position Alabama as a powerhouse in America’s energy future.
FIGHTING FOR ALABAMA FAMILIES Strong families and fundamental freedoms are the bedrock of Alabama’s success.
I will advocate for pro-family policies that uphold traditional values, make childcare more affordable for working parents, and ensure our schools prioritize academic excellence over political agendas.
By standing firm for families and protecting our core values, we can preserve Alabama’s way of life and build a brighter, more secure future for the next generation.
