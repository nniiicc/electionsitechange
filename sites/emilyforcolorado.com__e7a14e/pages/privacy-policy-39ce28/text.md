Thank you for visiting our website.
Emily Sirota for Colorado is committed to protecting your privacy.
We will do our very best to keep your information secure and campaign communications generated from this site will allow you to opt out of subsequent messages.
In an effort to customize your interactions with the campaign, we may provide the opportunity for you to voluntarily submit certain personal information such as your name, postal and email addresses.
These opportunities for voluntary disclosure intend to keep you apprised of campaign developments and will not be sold or distributed for commercial marketing purposes.
When you make a financial contribution, state law requires that we make a best effort to report certain identifiable donor information including name, mailing address, occupation and employer name.
Payment data, including credit and debit card and banking information, is not stored in our database.
Please note that third parties, including Facebook, may use cookies, web beacons, and other storage technologies to collect or receive information from our website and elsewhere on the internet and use that information to provide measurement services and target ads.
If you choose, you can opt-out of the collection and use of information for ad targeting.
For more information, or to opt-out, please visit NAI Consumer Opt Out.
The privacy practices set forth in this statement pertain only to this website.
If you have questions or comments about our privacy policy, please contact us at emilysirotaforcolorado@gmail.com
