Critical Deadlines: FAFSA Form – June 30, 2026 Scholarship Application – April 15, 2026 Scholarships are offered as part of the Maryland Legislative Scholarship Program and available to students for financial assistance with the cost of post-secondary education (beyond high school i.e. college, career school, and graduate school) and are administered through the Maryland Higher Education Commission (MHEC).
Rules regarding program eligibility, submission and processing of personal financial and other sensitive data, determination of final award amounts, and distribution of awards can be found at https://mhec.maryland.gov/preparing/Pages/FinancialAid/descriptions.aspx .
The site also lists other grant and scholarship programs for which you may be eligible.
Below you will find the link to my Delegation Scholarship Application for the 2026-2027 academic year.
To ensure consideration for the 2026-2027 academic year, the completed application and required associated documents must be returned to my Annapolis Office by April 15, 2026.
ONLINE SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION FORM (Online is the preferred submission method.
Requires a Google or Gmail account sign-in.
Email, mail and fax instructions are below.) Awards are not automatically renewed.
If you were awarded a scholarship last year and wish to be considered to receive one this year, you will need to re-apply.
In order to apply for this Scholarship, the student must first file the free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA ), submit it before the application deadline and attach a copy to the application.
Applications will be scored by a committee of volunteers and awards made based on the funds available, number of applicants, and strength of each individual application.
Preference will be given to students who reside within the boundaries of the 12A Legislative District.
To confirm your district, please click HERE .
Awards are generally made by the end of June.
In the case of High School Seniors, every effort is made to complete award determination decisions in time for the award to be announced at each school’s senior award ceremonies in May and June.
Some exceptions to application and award deadlines may be made in cases where the award is for a semester other than the fall.
Please contact us by email or phone to request an exception to the deadline.
Please review the instructions carefully and include all of the requested documentation.
Should you have any questions or need further information, feel free to contact my office, Terri.Hill@House.Maryland.Gov or 410-841-3378, with any questions, problems or concerns regarding the scholarship, application or process.
Thank you for your commitment to furthering your education and for taking the time to apply.
