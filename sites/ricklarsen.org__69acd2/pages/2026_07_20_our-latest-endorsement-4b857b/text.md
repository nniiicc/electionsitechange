That means fighting to lower costs for working families, protect Medicare and Medicaid, strengthen rural hospitals, and improve access to care for veterans, seniors, and children.
I am proud to share that our campaign has earned the endorsement of the Committee to Protect Health Care, an organization that understands what is at stake in the fight to protect affordable, accessible health care.
In Congress, I voted to allow Medicare to negotiate prescription drug prices for the first time in our nation's history, which lowers costs for seniors and taxpayers.
I have fought to protect Medicaid from devastating cuts, supported efforts to extend Affordable Care Act tax credits, and worked to preserve vital access to care in our rural communities.
I believe health care is a right, and I support universal, affordable coverage.
That is why I am fighting to reduce health care costs and expand access to high-quality health care that fits families' budgets.
At a time when families are struggling with high prices and a weak job market, Republicans in Congress are doubling down on a health care crisis of their own making.
The Committee to Protect Health Care is on the frontlines of fighting back against these attacks, and I am proud to stand with them.
