If you have moved, changed your name, are newly eligible, or had a change in your circumstances since the last election, your voter registration may need to be updated.
Even if you think your registration is current, I encourage you to check it today.
It only takes a few minutes, and it can help make sure there are no surprises when it is time to cast your ballot.
Check Your Registration Voting is one of the most important ways we can have a say in the decisions that affect our families, our communities, and the future of Northwest Washington.
So today, please take a moment to check your registration and encourage a friend or family member to do the same.
Then, before Election Day arrives, make a plan to vote.
