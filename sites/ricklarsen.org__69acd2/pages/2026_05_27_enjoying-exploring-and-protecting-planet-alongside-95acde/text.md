The Sierra Club's slogan is "Enjoy, explore, and protect the planet." I cannot imagine a better place in the country to do just that than Northwest Washington.
Our district is one of the most scenic and diverse in the country.
Northwest Washington's natural resources are some of the prides of our nation.
We represent the Puget Sound, America's largest estuary, as well as vital species like our orcas and salmon that call those waters home.
From rolling hills, stunning valleys, and beautiful landscapes along the Puget Sound and the Salish Sea to our diverse ecosystem that supports large migrations of shore birds, beloved salmon returns, and the iconic bald eagle.
Northwest Washington is beloved by environmentalists and adventurers alike.
Washington is a beautiful state, and it is a privilege to work with the Sierra Club's Washington chapter to mitigate climate change and protect our home.
