I am excited to share that, in addition to the incredible organizations working on the frontlines of our toughest issues, I have earned the endorsement of four impactful groups this cycle: the Snohomish County Democrats, the San Juan County Democrats, the Island County Democrats, and the Skagit County Democrats.
I am committed to lowering costs, bringing investments home to Northwest Washington, holding the administration and ICE accountable, combating fentanyl in our communities, protecting reproductive freedom, and supporting our veterans — but no one can accomplish these goals alone.
I am grateful to have the support of these local Democratic organizations.
Through teamwork, shared expertise, and coalition-building for Washingtonians, we can make those priorities a reality.
I am humbled by their support and am eager to continue important work for our communities alongside them.
