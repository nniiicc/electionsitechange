Accomplishments
Throughout my time in Congress, I have been committed to delivering tangible results for Northwest Washington.
This year alone, I have secured federal funds for 15 new community projects, that is $13.9 million of your tax dollars coming back home.
These federal dollars focus on meeting our community's needs and have supported critical projects across Northwest Washington.
The funds have helped feed hungry families, improve transportation and infrastructure needs, connect communities, assist with mitigating and recovering from floods, invest in and support local law enforcement, provide additional support and resources for education, deliver investments for economic development, and support our senior citizens.
I am committed to delivering for the residents of Northwest Washington and will continue to advocate for these critical resources for our community.
