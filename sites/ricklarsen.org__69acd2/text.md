READ MORE October 7, 2026 I support tough, upfront restrictions on data centers: Data centers require enormous amounts of electricity and water to operate.
Without strong guardrails, the costs of building and running these facilities can be passed on to ordinary ratepayers through higher electricity bills and increased strain on local water systems.
At the same time, the public infrastructure that supports our communities, including the power grid, roads, bridges, and schools, requires significant investment.
Data center development should strengthen these systems, not strain them.
