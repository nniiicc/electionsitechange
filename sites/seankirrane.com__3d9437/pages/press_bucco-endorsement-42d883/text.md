Bucco , today announced his endorsement of Sean Kirrane for Congress in New Jersey's Fifth Congressional District.
Representing the Legislative District that includes West Milford, the Leader pointed to Kirrane's background and understanding of the issues facing residents in the district's more rural communities.
In a statement, Senator Bucco said: “Sean Kirrane has spent his career solving problems, improving broken systems, and delivering results in the private sector.
His business background has given him firsthand experience managing budgets, creating efficiencies, and leading organizations through complex challenges.
He understands that Congress needs more leaders with real world experience who know how to fix problems instead of creating them.
Sean will bring a practical, solutions focused approach to Washington while continuing to advocate for stronger healthcare access, economic opportunity, and accountable government for the people of New Jersey's Fifth District.” — Senate Minority Leader Anthony M.
Bucco Kirrane expressed his appreciation for the endorsement and reaffirmed his commitment to the region.
“I am honored to have the support of Senate Minority Leader Bucco.
He has earned the trust of his constituents by consistently fighting for practical solutions and putting his communities first.
West Milford and the surrounding area deserve a representative who understands the unique challenges facing rural communities, particularly when it comes to healthcare access, infrastructure, and affordability.
