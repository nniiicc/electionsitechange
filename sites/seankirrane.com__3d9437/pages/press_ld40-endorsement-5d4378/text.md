“Sean Kirrane brings a strong business background and a real world understanding of how policy impacts jobs, growth, and opportunity.
He knows what it takes to meet a payroll, balance a budget, and make tough decisions—experience that is sorely lacking in Washington.
Combined with his commitment to lower costs, safer communities, and accountable government, we are confident he will deliver for the people of the Fifth District.” — Senator Kristin Corrado, Assemblyman Christopher DePhillips, and Assemblyman Al Barlas Kirrane issued the following statement in response to the endorsement: “I'm honored to earn the support of Senator Corrado, Assemblyman DePhillips, and Assemblyman Barlas.
They've built a reputation for standing firm on principle and delivering for their constituents, and I'm proud to have them alongside me.
