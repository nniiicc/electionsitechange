“Sean Kirrane understands the concerns families in Passaic County and across North Jersey are facing every day.
From rising costs to public safety and economic uncertainty, people want leadership that is accessible, focused, and willing to fight for common sense solutions.
Sean has shown he is prepared to take on that responsibility and be a strong voice for the Fifth District in Congress.” — Senator Joseph Pennacchio, Assemblyman Brian Bergen, and Assemblyman Jay Webber Kirrane welcomed the endorsement and emphasized the importance of building broad support throughout the district: “I'm grateful to Senator Pennacchio, Assemblyman Bergen, and Assemblyman Webber for their support and confidence in our campaign.
These are leaders who have consistently stood up for taxpayers and defended the interests of their communities.
