Danny Rosellini for Texas | Candidate for TX House of Representative
Protecting Texas Families.
Why I’m Running
I’m running because too many hardworking people in our community are being left behind.
It’s time to bring relief, restore opportunity, and make our community a place where everyone can build a secure and hopeful future.
- Seniors are fighting to stay in the communities they helped build.
- Young families are struggling to afford their first home and put down roots.
- Renters are facing rising prices that make it harder to stay in their homes.
- Small businesses are overwhelmed by high taxes, soaring insurance costs, and energy bills.
Who I am.
I’m Danny Rosellini, a lifelong Texan, proud family man, and someone who believes in bringing common sense and real-world experience to Austin.
Occupation: Former Senior Corporate Executive, Small Business Owner, and Investor
Education: Texas Christian University, B.A. in Business
Hometown: Dallas native; 45-year resident of Carrollton and Coppell
For over five decades, I’ve lived, worked, and raised my family right here in our community.
In the corporate world, I earned a reputation as a “Disruptor” someone who challenged the status quo and delivered results.
I’m not a career politician, and that’s exactly why I’m running.
We need more business-minded leaders in Austin who will listen, lead with integrity, and focus on common-sense solutions that improve everyday life for Texans.
District 115 Priorities That Put Texans First.
PROPERTY TAX RELIEF.
- Keep Texans in their homes.
- Support meaningful tax compression and stronger homestead protections.
- Take measurable steps toward eliminating property taxes altogether.
FIX PUBLIC EDUCATION.
- Put funding back in the classroom—not into bureaucracy.
- Increase transparency and accountability at every level.
- Expand career and technical education pathways to prepare students for real-world success.
- Pursue real structural reform, not just more spending.
PUBLIC SAFETY & ACCOUNTABILITY.
- Back law enforcement and stand with victims.
- Hold judges and prosecutors accountable when they fail to enforce the law.
- Ensure no one is above the law, including politicians.
LOWER ENERGY COSTS.
- Stop forcing families and small businesses to subsidize massive AI data centers.
- Require infrastructure costs to be paid by those driving demand.
- Protect grid reliability without raising household bills.
INSURANCE REFORM.
- Tackle rising insurance rates that are straining family budgets.
- Increase competition and transparency to drive down costs.
- Hold insurers accountable to Texas consumers.
My Commitment.
