abortion
I’m a pro-life Libertarian, and I believe human life has inherent value and deserves protection.
At the same time, I recognize that in a free society, not every deeply moral and emotional issue is properly resolved through federal power.
That’s why I support returning the question of abortion policy to the states and to the democratic process closest to the people.
I also believe there should be no federal taxpayer funding used to subsidize abortion services, and no federal funding directed to organizations like Planned Parenthood for abortion-related care.
If this is a moral issue with deep disagreement across the country, then Americans should not be forced to financially participate in it through federal taxation.
I align with a constitutional approach similar to Ron Paul and Thomas Massie in the sense that I believe these decisions belong at the state and local level, where communities can reflect their own values while still respecting individual rights and due process.
That allows for democratic accountability without concentrating yet another deeply personal issue under federal control.
Ultimately, my position is simple: protect life where I can, respect constitutional limits, and ensure that this issue is decided as close to the people as possible, not dictated from Washington.
