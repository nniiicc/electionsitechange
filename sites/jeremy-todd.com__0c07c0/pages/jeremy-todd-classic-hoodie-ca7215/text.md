We use print-on-demand for order fulfillment, which means your order is printed when you order it, so it can take just a little longer to get than you're used to with a big retailer.
That said, most orders arrive within 6-9 days depending on your area.
What can we say, we're not Amazon, but our quality also doesn't suck.
2. do you accept returns or replacements?
We generally do not accept returns or exchanges for buyer's remorse, as products are made on-demand.
However, for damaged, defective, or misprinted items, you can submit a problem report within 30 days of delivery for a free reshipment or refund from our printer.
They typically require photographic proof of defect.
3. why are your products cheaper than aoc's?
Not only are our products cheaper, they're probably better quality.
While the campaign makes some profit off our products and we are capitalists, we're more concerned with flooding Kentucky with merchandise that's like a big middle finger to the establishment.
Ultimately, socialists are just greedier than we are.

While we try to price everything affordably, larger sizes require more material to make and therefore cost more.
