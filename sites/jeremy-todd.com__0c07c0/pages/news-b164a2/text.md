Congress in Kentucky’s 4th District Grassroots Campaign Delivers Over 750 Signatures in 12 Days, Bringing America First Voice to November Ballot Kentucky – June 2, 2026 – Jeremy Todd, the Libertarian nominee for U.S.
House of Representatives in Kentucky’s 4th Congressional District, today announced that his campaign has successfully secured ballot access for the November 2026 general election.
In just 12 days, more than 30 dedicated volunteers mobilized across the district, collecting over 750 signatures from voters demanding a real choice—one independent of special interests, foreign lobbies, and billionaire donors.
This grassroots effort ensures Kentucky voters will have an uncompromised America First option on the ballot.
“Our district has spoken clearly: what happened in our own backyard cannot be tolerated,” said Jeremy Todd.
“Kentucky isn’t for sale.
We will not allow Washington insiders or outside money to simply purchase a seat in Congress.
This campaign is about sending a loud message that our freedom doesn’t go to the highest bidder.” Todd’s platform emphasizes core principles of liberty, fiscal responsibility, and putting Kentucky and America first.
Key priorities include: Bringing U.S. troops home and ending endless foreign entanglements.
Reducing out-of-control government spending and national debt.
Rejecting the influence of special interests and foreign lobbies in American policymaking.
Holding accountable those who prioritize donors over constituents.
Todd is running to represent working families, not be entrenched political interests.
His campaign is powered by everyday Americans who believe in limited government, personal freedom, and principled leadership.
Following the recent Republican primary in which incumbent Rep.
Thomas Massie was defeated by a heavily funded challenger, Todd’s candidacy provides a critical alternative for voters seeking independence from big-money politics.
