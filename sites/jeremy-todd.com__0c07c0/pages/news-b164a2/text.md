Grassroots Campaign Delivers Over 750 Signatures in 12 Days, Bringing America First Voice
to November Ballot
Kentucky – June 2, 2026 – Jeremy Todd, the Libertarian nominee for U.S.
House of Representatives in Kentucky’s 4th Congressional District, today announced that his campaign has successfully secured ballot access for the November 2026 general election.
In just 12 days, more than 30 dedicated volunteers mobilized across the district, collecting over 750 signatures from voters demanding a real choice—one independent of special interests, foreign lobbies, and billionaire donors.
This grassroots effort ensures Kentucky voters will have an uncompromised America First option on the ballot.
“Our district has spoken clearly: what happened in our own backyard cannot be tolerated,” said Jeremy Todd.
“Kentucky isn’t for sale.
We will not allow Washington insiders or outside money to simply purchase a seat in Congress.
This campaign is about sending a loud message that our freedom doesn’t go to the highest bidder.”
Todd’s platform emphasizes core principles of liberty, fiscal responsibility, and putting Kentucky and America first.
