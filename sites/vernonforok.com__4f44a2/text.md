FIGHTING FOR WESTERN OKLAHOMA’S FUTURE
EDUCATION
Oklahoma ranks last in education, and that must change.
Rick will work to recruit and retain teachers, improve reading levels, expand STEM and career-tech opportunities, and keep politics out of the classroom so students can thrive.
RURAL ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
Rick will fight to bring jobs, industry, and housing to rural communities, making sure our region has a real voice when companies and leaders make decisions about Oklahoma’s future.
PROTECTING PRIVATE PROPERTY RIGHTS
From land and water to agriculture, government overreach threatens our way of life.
Rick will always defend the rights of Oklahoma families, farmers, and ranchers against burdensome regulations and unfair tax hikes.
