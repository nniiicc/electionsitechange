Upcoming Local Festivals...Come Meet and Talk With Becky...
The Kentucky Apple Festival in Paintsville, Johnson County, takes place from October 1–3, 2026.
Originally started to celebrate the local apple harvest in Johnson County, the Kentucky Apple Festival has grown into a massive annual autumn tradition that transforms downtown Paintsville into a lively street fair every first weekend in October.
Celebrating its 62nd year alongside America's 250th anniversary, the official theme for 2026 is appropriate and nostalgic: “As American as Apple Pie.”
Where to Find Team Becky:
Find us at our Johnson County Democrats Booth, walking around enjoying the fun, and in the Parade.
The 30th Annual Martin County Harvest Festival takes place on September 18 and 19, 2026, in downtown Inez, Kentucky.
Celebrating 30 years, this two-day community event features live music, carnival rides, food and craft vendors, a parade, professional wrestling, hayrides, pet shows, and family activities.
