Education It is important that students in Baltimore County are learning in safe, secure schools across the county.
I was instrumental in establishing the policy changes for the ALICE active-assailant response, and advocating for stronger policy around emergency preparedness.
I also fought to support the goals of the Maryland's Safe to Learn Act, helping to strengthen coordination between schools and law enforcement and improve safety standards in our schools for both students and staff.
Opioid Crisis The opioid crisis affects us all — our families, friends, and neighbors.
It fuels crime and devastates communities.
We must continue working together to address this crisis with a comprehensive, long-term approach.
Redistricting Redistricting should be about fair representation, not political advantage.
Maryland’s districts have been heavily gerrymandered, and we must work together — across party lines — to restore integrity and trust in our elections.
