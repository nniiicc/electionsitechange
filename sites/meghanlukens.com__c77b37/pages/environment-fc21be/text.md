I am fortunate to have grown up in Colorado, the most beautiful state in the union, and therefore I am dedicated to maintaining our beautiful public lands and addressing climate threats.
Coloradans are seeing the threats of climate change on a daily basis, and I have proudly sponsored and supported bold, action-oriented climate policy in the state legislature that supports local economies.
In order to maintain our amazing Colorado culture, it is essential that we combat climate change.
My environmental legislation spans water conservation, stream restoration and pollution prevention.
I have also worked to expand solar energy access and support Colorado's outdoor recreation economy.
My 2026 Sponsored Legislation: HB26-1008 : Colorado Outdoor Opportunities Act This sweeping piece of legislation strengthens Colorado's management of outdoor recreation, wildlife and conservation through better coordination with local communities, tribes and landowners.
SB26-016 : Prohibit Discharge of Preproduction Plastic Materials We banned the discharge of plastic pellets into Colorado's land and water, targeting a leading source of microplastic pollution.
SB26-171 : Disposal of Preproduction Plastic Materials This law requires certified disposal sites for preproduction plastic materials, helping keep them out of Colorado's waterways and environment.
SB26-101 : Local Government Landfill Methane Emission Reduction Regulations We eased the cost of methane emission compliance for publicly owned landfills by expanding access to state funding sources.
HB26-1342 : Knowingly Luring Bears This law prevents dangerous human-bear conflicts by increasing penalties for people who negligently attract bears.
My 2025 Sponsored Legislation: HB25-1014 , Streamline Certain Processes Within the Division of Water Resources This bipartisan law makes three main changes to water management in Colorado: water well permit expiration, groundwater permitting, and the water rights abandonment process.
These increased efficiencies and process improvements allow the Department of Water Resources to focus on their essential responsibilities by saving time on paperwork and administrative tasks.
My 2024 Sponsored Legislation: HB24-1362 : Measures to Incentivize Graywater Use This bipartisan law encourages and supports water conservation efforts through utilizing graywater in new residential builds.
SB24-197 : Water Conservation Measures This bipartisan law protects Colorado’s water supply by implementing well-informed improvements to our drought protection systems.
HB24-1379 : Regulate Dredge & Fill We restored critical protections for Colorado’s streams, rivers, and wetlands.
It creates a program to protect our critical water resources from construction impacts, drawing from effective federal practices but tailored to Colorado's needs.
HB24-1435 : Colorado Water Conservation Board Projects This bipartisan law funds Colorado’s water conservation projects, seeking to maintain our water for the years to come.
My 2023 Sponsored Legislation: HB23-1137 : Making Solar Garden Payments Fair and Stable This law streamlines the process of accessing solar electricity to build more community solar gardens and save Coloradans money.
SB23-177 : Funding the Colorado Water Conservation Board We allocated $95 million for continuing and new water conservation projects across the state.
SB23-270 : Restoring Natural Stream Systems This law creates a pathway for the state to undergo projects to restore healthy stream conditions.
HB23-1294 : Pollution Protection Measures We protected Coloradans from air pollution by giving impacted communities a voice in the emissions permitting and enforcement process.

Protect the Western Slope’s water resources.

Work to reduce carbon emissions.
Represent constituents and stakeholders invested in climate change, water, and our outdoor recreation economy.
