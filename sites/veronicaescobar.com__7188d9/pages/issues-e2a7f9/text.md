VETERANS
More than 50,000 veterans call our community home, and looking out for them is something I take seriously.
When people come back from service, they shouldn’t have to fight to get care, find work, or rebuild their lives.
I’m proud to have voted for:
- Expanded access to healthcare for veterans exposed to toxicity
- Better access to service animals for our veterans.
- The largest cost-of-living benefit increase in 10+ years.
- Increased food assistance for low-income veterans
- Housing solutions and rental assistance for veterans.
I’ve also helped bring federal funding to El Paso, including a new VA healthcare center, and introduced legislation to make sure it opens ready to serve veterans the right way.
We owe it to those who served to keep our promises when they return.

IMMIGRATION REFORM
Washington has failed to fix our broken immigration system for decades.
That’s why, in 2023, I led the introduction of the Dignity Act, the only bipartisan, comprehensive immigration reform bill in over a decade, working alongside a Republican colleague to deliver real solutions.
My plan takes a practical, common-sense approach: strengthening border management, modernizing legal immigration, and restoring dignity to people navigating a broken system.
The Dignity Act creates a pathway to legal status for long-term undocumented immigrants, protects Dreamers, expands legal pathways for workers and families, and improves how we process asylum claims.
I’ve also led efforts to keep families together, protect civil rights, and ensure accountability in our immigration system—because we can have a secure border and stay true to our values at the same time.
It’s time to move past political talking points and finally get immigration reform done.

SERVICEMEMBERS
With Fort Bliss in our backyard, our community is closely connected to the men and women who serve.
For many, El Paso is where deployments begin—and where they come home.
I’ve worked to make sure service members and their families have what they need, from better pay and housing to access to healthcare and childcare.
That includes supporting pay raises for enlisted troops, expanding mental health services, and cutting costs for military families.
I’ve also backed legislation to restore housing benefits, protect service members from discrimination, and ensure servicewomen and military families can access the care they need—no matter where they’re stationed.
At Fort Bliss, I’ve helped secure major investments to strengthen readiness, improve facilities, and build a stronger pipeline between our local students and military careers.
Supporting those who serve means paying attention to the details that affect their daily lives.
