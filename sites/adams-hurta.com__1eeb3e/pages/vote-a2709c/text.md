Where to Vote
Hi!
Thanks for planning your vote for Michael Adams-Hurta.
Election Day for the Runoff is Tuesday, May 26!
Each county in the 14th District has their own set of polling locations.
Voting is open from 7 AM to 7 PM at each location and on each day.
Click the following links to find your county's polling centers for early voting and election day:
*In Waller County, Washington County, and Colorado County, you may only vote at the specific polling place assigned to your precinct on May 26, but you may vote at any location during early voting.
Email the campaign at info@adams-hurta.com or call at 713-489-3298 if you need help identifying your precinct.
In each of the other counties in the 14th Court of Appeals, you may vote at any polling location in the county.
Thank you for voting!
If you still have any questions about where or how to vote, please contact us and we will do what we can to help out.
