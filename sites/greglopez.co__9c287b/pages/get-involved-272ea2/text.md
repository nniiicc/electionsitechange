Greg has set a goal of building a team of game-changers statewide and we are well underway!
We are placing passionate and talented people on our team in leadership roles in all 64 counties of Colorado and strategizing with our regional leaders in the urban corridor. ​If you are interested in having a voice and being a part of “Breaking the Divide” in Colorado, join us!
Petition Signature Volunteers Petition Signature Volunteers are the driving force behind our grassroots efforts in obtaining the 1,000 signatures per Congressional District as required by the Secretary of States office.
As a volunteer, you’ll be asking registered voters to help Lopez/Romero to get onto the November general election ballot.
What Does a Petition Signature Volunteer Do?
Begin circulating petitions on Thursday, May 14, 2026.
Engage with registered voters to explain the petition process and the candidate’s platform.
Utilize approved methods: in-person, door-to-door, public and private events.
Training will be provided on how to be in compliance with collecting signatures.
Knock Doors Canvassing is an effective way to ensure voters learn about the upcoming election, and will be the most direct way you can help!
We door knock in neighborhoods to provide voters with key information about the election, our campaign, and Winsome.
You can canvass to help the campaign in two ways: Persuade Swing Voters – Our team of local experts will assign you a targeted list so you’re reaching the swing voters we must win in November.
We’ll equip you with the key information to get them to commit to voting for Greg.
Increase Voter Turnout – Many Coloradans don’t realize we have elections every year!
A simple face-to-face conversation can increase voter turnout by over 10%.
Don’t worry!
You won’t have to do this alone, we can pair you up with a seasoned pro!
We’re knocking doors in every part of the state and would love your help to do it!
Phone Calls Phone calls are one of the easiest and most effective ways to persuade swing voters and turn out supporters for Greg.
Our system gives you a targeted list so every call makes an impact.
Ways you can help: Voter Persuasion Calls – Contact targeted voters and inform them about our People Over Politics platform and persuade them to vote Lopez November 4th.
Absentee Ballot Sign Up Calls – Reach out to key voters to encourage them to fill out an Absentee Ballot Request Form and bank their vote.
Volunteer Recruitment Calls – Help the campaign by reaching out to volunteer prospects to get them on board with our team.
The best part?
You can make calls from anywhere—at home, on the go, or with a group – and at any time that fits your schedule.
Whether you have 15 minutes or a few hours, your calls help us reach the voters we must win in November.
