DAVID SOLANA Independent for Congress Meet David Why I'm Running Why Independent Issues Sign Up Meet David Why I'm Running Why Independent Issues Sign Up Photographer: Andres Garcia WHO IS DAVID?
I was born in DC in 1982 , and I moved back home in 2013.
I served America as a Peace Corps Volunteer from 2007-2009 and again from 2010-2011.
I have owned a small business since 2013.
I have been a teacher in schools at home and abroad.
I am an avid whitewater kayaker.
I volunteer my time teaching kayaking, guiding rafts, and teaching rescue for a veteran-focused organization , Team River Runner.
I have spent a large part of this year holding DC Water accountable for the massive January sewage spill into the Potomac River.
I was born in Washington, DC, to an Army family.
I moved around more than most, though less than plenty, managing to grow up in the Mid-Atlantic before heading to the Midwest for my undergraduate studies at the University of Illinois. **I graduated with a B.S., majoring in biology and Spanish and minoring in Chemistry.**I interned in laboratories that studied infectious diseases and vaccines.
I learned that learning itself is the key to innovation, and the federal government has been the critical driver of American innovation since World War II.
My first job post-college was as an elementary school English teacher in Spain.
I followed that up by teaching high school English in France, and then returning to the US and working as a substitute teacher.
Seeing those three different education systems showed me how America’s education system reinforces economic disparities .
Serving my community and our country is important to me.
I wanted to lean in to the best of America, and share it with the world, so I joined the Peace Corps.
I served for two years, came home to the Great Recession, and found that I could not get a job.
There are a lot of things government can do to help people during economic downturns; but the current debt load we carry means the next economic downturn is going to be so much more difficult for all of us .
The time to start solving the excessive national debt is now.
I returned to volunteer for Peace Corps Response while I applied to graduate school.
My Peace Corps service had focused on small business development in an agricultural economy.
Seeing the good that was possible through well intentioned business practices, I opted to pursue an MBA at the University of Virginia.
I built a career bridging the gap between research & development and on-farm implementation of new technologies.
I worked with multi-national companies and companies with fewer than 10 people to bring novel technologies to farmers.
I learned the critical role of government in business as I saw how many times an excellent innovation or improvement for workers would be shelved because the competition would undercut the price of the improvement.
We must use legislation to ensure businesses compete on their merits, not on their willingness to pay a living wage or provide to health insurance to their employees.
