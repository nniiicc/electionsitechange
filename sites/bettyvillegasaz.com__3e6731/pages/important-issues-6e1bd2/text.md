top of page Villegas for AZ ABOUT BETTY Media Blog ABOUT LD 20 OUR ISSUES RE-ELECT BETTY CONTACT Your Issues Are My Issues As your state representative, I am committed to advancing legislation that is fair, equitable, and inclusive.
My focus will be on fostering community and economic development that benefits all residents, ensuring that everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
Voter Information Support Donate Housing Affordability Everyone deserves a safe, stable, and affordable place to call home.
I am committed to addressing rising housing costs, reducing eviction disparities, and expanding housing opportunities for renters and homeowners alike, so that more families can thrive in our community.
Protect Public Education Every student deserves access to high-quality public education.
I will work to overhaul the ESA voucher system, prioritize funding for public schools, improve facilities, support educators and school staff, and expand career training and college opportunities for Arizona students.
Protect Civil, Human Rights, and Democracy Everyone desrved equal rights, equal protection, and an equal voice.
I am committed to protecting civil liberties, including voting, women's reproductive, LGBTQ+, and transgender rights.
I oppose anti-immigration policies and will advocate for an inclusive, fair, and just community for all.
Protecting the Right to Vote Ensuring that every eligible voter has the opportunity to participate in fair, secure, and accessible elections is crucial for a thriving democracy.
As a responsible citizen, I am committed to defending voting rights to give every community member a voice.
Climate & Environmental Justice Everyone deserves clean air, safe water, and a healthy environment.
I will support equitable environmental solutions and policies, promote clean energy, protect our air and water, and work to preserve our cultural and natural landscapes and resources that connect us to our shared heritage.
