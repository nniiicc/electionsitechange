Pink in the Park Event
Local Breast Cancer Survivors to be Honored at Pink in the Park Event.
St.
Cloud, MN – On Wednesday, July 17th, nine local breast cancer survivors will be honored at the Pink in the Park event during the St.
Cloud ROX baseball game.
Among these distinguished honorees is Minnesota State Representative Bernie Perryman.
The honorees will be recognized before the game and will collectively throw out the first pitch.
In a show of support for these brave women and all those affected by breast cancer, the St.
Cloud ROX players will don pink jerseys provided by Alliance Imaging.
The game will begin at 6:35 pm at Joe Faber Field in St.
Cloud.
Join us in celebrating the strength and resilience of these incredible survivors and supporting breast cancer awareness.
