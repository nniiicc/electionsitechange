ABOUT Zach
Zach Dorholt is a lifelong Minnesotan, mental health professional, dedicated father of three girls, and community advocate committed to making our state a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
With degrees in Community Development and Counseling Psychology from St.
Cloud State University, and a Graduate Certificate in Marriage & Family Therapy from St.
Mary’s University of Minnesota, Zach has spent his career expanding access to mental health care, supporting working families, and building stronger communities.
Elected to the Minnesota House in 2012, Zach served as Vice Chair of the Higher Education Committee, championing affordable education and student success.
He currently serves on the St.
Cloud Area School Board, where he works to ensure every child has access to quality public education.
Zach is also a nonprofit leader and co-founder of the Independent Music Collaborative of Central Minnesota, promoting youth engagement, local arts, and sober spaces.
His service spans multiple boards focused on sustainability, public health, and civic engagement.
