For a majority of my time as your representative, I’ve been the voice in Springfield for the same region.
In 2021, maps throughout our state were redrawn based on the 2020 census data.
In 2022, these maps came into effect.
While the center core of the district was maintained, the shape of the boundaries made a shift to the east, changing the shape of the district dramatically.
Below, you can see a before and after comparison of how the district has shifted.
I look forward to getting to know voters in the new additions the 69th, and I will continue to be a voice for voters in Springfield!
