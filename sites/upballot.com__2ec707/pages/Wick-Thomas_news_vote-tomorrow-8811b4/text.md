Looking back at the last 9 months is surreal.
So many people have helped get this campaign to where it is today.
Parks Cleanups, book giveaways, youth rock registration drives, drag queen storytimes, youth art contests, giving out native seeds as we knocked thousands of doors, phone calls, late nights, murals, fireworks cleanups, ren fest, pride parades, paint nights, neighborhood meetings, rescuing banned books, and so much more.
We've worked so hard to change the game, to open up the doors of power to new people, and to put every dollar from the campaign back into the community.
We've worked so hard for so long, and tomorrow is the day we find out the results of all of it.
Thank you to everyone who has donated, volunteered, offered advice, and supported this crazy dream of mine.
Please vote tomorrow and help me get to Jeff City so I can keep working to make Missouri, and the political system work better for us.
I don't know what tomorrow will bring, but I am so grateful that we are going on this journey together.
If we were able to do this much good in 9 months, imagine what we can do in the next two years.

What a year it has been!
