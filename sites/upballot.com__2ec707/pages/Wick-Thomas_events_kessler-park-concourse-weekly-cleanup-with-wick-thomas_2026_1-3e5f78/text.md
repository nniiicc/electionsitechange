Kessler Park Concourse Weekly Cleanup With Wick Thomas
The Concourse @ Kessler Park, Kansas City
Every week from 9-10 am we will be working to remove trash from Kessler Park.
KC Parks Ambassadors will provide the bags and gloves if you can spare an hour to help.
Let's work together to keep the Concourse and Kessler Park beautiful!
