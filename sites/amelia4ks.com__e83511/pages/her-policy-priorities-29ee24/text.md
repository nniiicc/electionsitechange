Skip to content Amelia for Kansas Home About Amelia Her Policy Priorities Contact Amelia Donations Amelia believes government works best when it focuses on improving everyday life for Kansas families.
Her priorities are rooted in affordability, opportunity, personal freedom, and building stronger communities — from protecting public education and voting rights to expanding access to child care, elder care, and healthcare.
She is committed to practical, people-focused solutions that help every Kansan thrive.
Public Transportation Reliable and affordable public transportation is essential to building an economy that works for everyone.
For too many Kansans, the high cost of owning and maintaining a vehicle places an enormous strain on household budgets.
Amelia supports investments in public transportation and infrastructure that expand access to jobs, education, healthcare, and essential services while reducing the financial burden on working families.
Voting Rights A strong democracy depends on every eligible voter having easy and secure access to the ballot box.
Amelia believes voting should be accessible, transparent, and protected from unnecessary barriers.
She supports policies that expand voter access, protect mail voting, and ensure every Kansan can make their voice heard without undue obstacles or confusion.
Elder Care Affordable, high-quality care for children, seniors, and vulnerable loved ones is essential for strong families and a strong economy.
Amelia believes Kansas families should not be forced into financial hardship simply to access the care their loved ones need, and she supports policies that expand access to affordable child care, strengthen long-term care services, and support caregivers so families can thrive.
Reproductive Rights Personal healthcare decisions should remain between patients and their doctors.
Amelia believes reproductive freedom is a fundamental part of personal liberty and supports protecting access to contraception, abortion care, and comprehensive reproductive healthcare for all Kansans.
Education Kansas public schools are the foundation of strong communities and future opportunity.
Amelia believes every child deserves access to a fully funded, high-quality public education regardless of their zip code, including students in special education and gifted programs who too often lack the resources they need to succeed.
She opposes efforts to undermine public schools through underfunding and voucher schemes and supports investing in teachers, classrooms, and the services that help every student reach their full potential.
Affordability The rising cost of living is putting enormous pressure on Kansas families.
From housing and groceries to healthcare, transportation, and utility bills, too many people are struggling just to make ends meet.
Amelia believes Kansas deserves leadership focused on lowering everyday costs, expanding economic opportunity, and making communities more affordable so working families can build stable, secure futures.
