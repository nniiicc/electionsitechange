My name is David Hackney, and I am honored to serve as your State Representative
Let’s be Clear, Times are Tough
All of us are struggling.
In these challenging times, it’s clear that we can’t wait for a fractured federal government to solve the problems we face.
It’s up to us in Washington to solve Washington’s problems.
While we’ve made meaningful progress at the state level over the last four years, the work is far from finished.
For too many families, the math just isn’t adding up, and I’m doubling down on our efforts to bridge that gap.
My focus is singular: delivering bold, state-led solutions that drive down the cost of living and put more money back in the pockets of the people who keep our community running.
As a former Assistant US Attorney with experience working with the United Nations, the Nature Conservancy, and employment and labor law, David brings an incredible breadth of experience to Washington’s 11th District.
