Thomas Chittenden 17 Jun 2020 • 1 min read The best part of public office is talking with voters.
I work hard to keep open and ongoing conversations with the people I serve.
To help do that this Saturday, June 20th, from 1 pm to 4 pm, I'll be outside of the Church Street Tavern to have a socially-distanced meet and greet with free face masks to hear from you and what you care about.
I want to know what you think about important issues facing Vermonters.
So please come down to Church Street this Saturday to talk.
And if you want to know my thoughts on the important issues please visit my website www.thomaschittenden.com/issues .
You can also give me a call at 802.233.1913 or an email at thomas@thomaschittenden.com.
Democracy demands our participation so please come down to Church Street this Saturday to have a conversation.
