Candidate Forum hosted by Moose Lake Area Chamber
Wednesday, September 23rd
6:30 p.m.
Moose Lake Community School
No RSVP Required.
Wear your Mary for Minnesota Shirt!
The Moose Lake Area Chamber of Commerce will host a nonpartisan Candidate Forum on Wednesday, September 23, 2026, from 6:30 to 8:00 p.m. at the Moose Lake Community School Auditorium.
The following candidates have been invited to participate:
Minnesota Senate District 11: Sen.
Jason Rarick (R, incumbent) and Mary Carlson (DFL)
Minnesota House District 11A: Rep.
Jeff Dotseth (R, incumbent) and Chris Swanson (DFL)
The forum will give community members an opportunity to hear directly from the candidates about issues affecting Moose Lake and the surrounding region.
Questions will represent a variety of community perspectives and subject areas, including education, health care, local government, economic development, issues affecting the future of area students, and other topics relevant to the region
