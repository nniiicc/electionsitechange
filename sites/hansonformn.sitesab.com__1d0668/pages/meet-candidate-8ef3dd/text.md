My name is Nicholas Hanson, and I’m running for Minnesota House of Representatives, District 9A .
I’m stepping forward because I believe greater Minnesota deserves more than politics as usual – we deserve real representation.
For too long, the voices of our communities have been overshadowed by party loyalty, political leaders and decisions made far from from home.
I’m not running to serve a party or a political machine.
I’m running to serve THE PEOPLE WHO LIVE HERE – the families, workers and neighbors who make this district what it is.
My goal is simple: to make sure that when legislation is written and passed, greater Minnesota is heard, respected and never forgotten by the metro.
Representation should mean listening first, acting with integrity, and standing up for constituents even when it’s inconvenient.
