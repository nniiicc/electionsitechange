Ballots in Snohomish County for the 2026 general election were mailed on October 9th.
The last day to vote is November 3rd at 8PM PST.
If at all possible, USE A BALLOT DROP BOX IN YOUR COUNTY, because completed ballots placed in drop boxes in other counties are often mailed via USPS to their county of origin.
This means that even if YOU returned your ballot on time, delivery delays may result in your ballot not making it to the elections office until it’s too late to be counted.
If you MUST us USPS to return your ballot do it as soon as possible, due to delays in postmarking and other delivery delays, to ensure your vote is counted.
