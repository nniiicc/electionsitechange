Travis Tranel for 49th Assembly
Representative Travis Tranel (R-Cuba City) was elected to the Wisconsin State Assembly in 2010 with 57 percent of the vote defeating a two-term incumbent Democrat.
In 2024 he was re-elected to an eighth term by the citizens of southwest Wisconsin with 63 percent of the vote.
ABOUT TRAVIS
Travis, a 5th generation dairy farmer, grew up on his family’s dairy farm in southern Grant County, near Cuba City.
There Travis learned the conservative values of hard work, family and the importance of living within one’s means..
