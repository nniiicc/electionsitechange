Home Meet Julie Issues Volunteer Contribute District 15 Map Voter Information Contact Hi, I'm Julie McGill.
I grew up right here in Fort Wayne, in a home where we clipped coupons, waited for the sales, and stretched every dollar.
Today my husband and I are raising our three kids just a mile from the home I grew up in.
Like many Hoosier families, I've experienced both the good times and the hard ones.
I know what happens when state government works, what happens when it doesn't, and how much those decisions matter to the people living with them every day.
I'm running because families like mine deserve someone who actually understands what they're up against.
I do.
And I’m ready to do something about it.
Hard Work Should Still Count for Something I believe that if you work hard and play by the rules, you should be able to get ahead.
But today, that promise feels further and further out of reach.
I've talked to families who are doing everything right and still can't seem to get ahead.
They're working hard, paying their bills, and trying to save for the future, but the cost of living continues to rise faster than their paycheck.
And the truth is, almost any family is closer to crisis than they realize.
One diagnosis.
One accident.
One layoff.
In a single moment, everything can change — I know, because I lived through it.
That's why I believe government should help prevent small setbacks from becoming lifelong crises.
It should create opportunity, remove unnecessary barriers, and give people the chance to get back on their feet with dignity.
Indiana works best when hardworking families have a fair chance to succeed.
That's the future I'm fighting for.
