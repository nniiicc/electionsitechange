This campaign is powered by neighbors who believe Hoosiers deserve practical leadership, accountable government, and solutions that make everyday life better.
Whether you have a few minutes or a few hours, there’s a meaningful way to help.
Easy Ways to Help
- Spread the Word: Tell friends, family, and neighbors why you support Julie.
- Engage on Social Media: Follow, react, comment, and share campaign posts.
- Show Your Support: Display a yard sign, wear campaign gear, or share materials.
- Encourage People to Vote: Share voting information and remind others to make a plan.
- Help Tell Our Story: Record a testimonial, write a letter to the editor, or help with photos and videos.
Not sure where to start?
Tell three people why you support Julie and ask them to take a closer look at the campaign.
Ready to Volunteer?
Sign up for a specific activity or shift through SignUp.com.
Opportunities include knocking doors, delivering materials, calling or writing voters, helping at events, greeting voters at the polls, and more.
No experience is needed.
Training, instructions, and materials will be provided.
