Real Priorities.
Government should work for the people who live here, work here, and raise families here.
Healthcare Every Hoosier deserves healthcare that is affordable, accessible, and centered on patients, not paperwork.
Whether you are taking your child to the doctor, managing a chronic illness, caring for an aging parent, or seeking mental health support, you should be able to get quality care without facing confusing rules or overwhelming costs.
Healthcare is also a massive economic engine for Indiana.
Programs like Medicaid and the Healthy Indiana Plan return billions of federal tax dollars to our state, keeping local hospitals open and supporting thousands of vital healthcare jobs.
When managed responsibly, these investments simultaneously improve health outcomes, protect taxpayers, and strengthen local economies.
By focusing on practical, fiscally responsible solutions, we can lower costs, expand access, reduce waste, support working families, and build a healthcare system that works better for everyone.
Read More Public Education Indiana’s public education system is facing a quiet but serious crisis.
Graduation rates look strong on paper, but college and career readiness is slipping, experienced teachers are leaving because of stagnant mid-career pay, and academic standards have been watered down.
This is more than an education issue.
It is also a taxpayer issue and an economic issue.
Public education dollars are increasingly being redirected to subsidize private school tuition while public schools receive a smaller share of state funding, leaving local taxpayers to make up the difference.
When our schools fail to deliver strong academic preparation, we weaken Indiana’s future workforce and make it harder for our children to compete for good-paying jobs.
Parents deserve educational choices, and taxpayers deserve accountability.
I will fight for stronger academic standards, better teacher pay, transparent funding, and a level playing field for every school receiving public dollars.
Read More Affordability Indiana should be a place where working families can afford to thrive, not just scrape by.
The squeeze Hoosiers feel isn't an accident.
Over the years, the rules have been tilted to serve powerful corporate interests while regular families pay more for the basics.
Nobody who works forty hours a week should have to choose between fixing the car and filling a prescription.
We need to stop shifting the burden onto families, keep our hard-earned tax dollars working here at home, and put an end to corporate price-gouging on everyday essentials.
Real affordability means a hard day's work actually covers your bills with something left over for the future.
That takes leaders who answer to Hoosier families, not the special interests writing the rules.
Read More Government Accountability Government works best when it is transparent, accountable, and focused on serving the public.
Hoosiers work hard and pay their taxes expecting the state to spend those dollars responsibly, however when one party holds unchecked supermajority power, waste, favoritism, and closed door deals become easier to hide.
Indiana doesn't have a revenue problem.
It has an accountability problem.
A nearly billion dollar Medicaid forecasting error, unrecovered overpayments, secret land deals, and lawmakers policing themselves are all symptoms of our government not having enough independent oversight on those in power.
I will fight for stronger ethics laws, transparent spending, independent oversight, honest lawmaking, and reforms that give Hoosiers a greater voice in their government.
Read More Workers' Rights Working people built Indiana's economy, and they deserve to share in its success.
The forty-hour week, overtime pay, workplace safety laws, and employer-provided benefits are expected today, but they became common in large part because workers organized and demanded better.
Those gains helped build America's middle class, giving families the security to buy homes, raise children, and plan for the future.
That security is harder to come by now.
Hoosiers are working hard, but many are still finding it tough to get ahead, as the cost of living keeps climbing.
In Indiana, hard work should be enough to get ahead, and I'll fight to make that true again.
When workers have the chance to succeed, families are stronger, businesses are more productive, and the whole state benefits.
Read More Utilities & Solar Power Indiana families deserve reliable, affordable utilities that support a growing economy instead of driving up the cost of living.
Rising electric bills, aging infrastructure, and policies that shift costs onto ratepayers are making it harder for families and businesses to get ahead.
Rural communities also need reliable power and high speed internet to attract new employers, support local businesses, and create good paying jobs.
Infrastructure is one of the smartest investments we can make because it lowers costs, strengthens our economy, and prepares Indiana for future growth.
By modernizing our energy, water, and broadband systems, expanding access to affordable solar energy, protecting consumers, and ensuring new development pays its fair share, we can lower long term costs, create good paying jobs, strengthen our communities, and build an economy that works for Hoosier families.
Read More Public Safety & Crime Safe communities are built on more than just strong police departments.
They require modern laws, better technology for investigators, and a justice system that holds repeat offenders accountable while putting victims first.
Many property crime cases still go unsolved, and offenders can exploit gaps between city, county, and township jurisdictions to avoid meaningful consequences.
We can do better.
By updating our laws to better identify patterns of repeat criminal behavior, fully supporting our law enforcement officers, expanding treatment courts for addiction-related crimes, and ensuring victims have a strong voice in the process, we can build a justice system that prevents tomorrow’s crimes instead of simply reacting to today’s.
Read More Personal Freedoms Every Hoosier deserves equal protection under the law and the opportunity to build a good life, regardless of background.
Real leadership means defending our constitutional rights while focusing on the challenges families actually face every day.
Government should respect personal privacy, protect religious freedom, and stay out of private medical and family decisions, not fuel divisions that pit neighbors against each other.
Serving people means finding common ground and making sure every Hoosier can live safely, work hard, and take part fully in our communities.
A fair and inclusive community requires practical solutions that support everyday needs rather than partisan fights.
Progress happens when leaders work across party lines to solve issues, so everyone has a genuine chance to succeed.
