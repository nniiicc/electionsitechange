Julie McGill
I am a mom of three, a business owner, and a lifelong Hoosier who is tired of seeing workable solutions sidelined while families pay the price.
I believe in practical action that helps families and small businesses thrive.
Life-Long Neighbor —
Roots matter.
I’ve spent nearly my entire life within the same square mile, partly by accident but mostly by choice.
My husband and I decided to raise our family here, buying our first home just steps from my childhood front door.
Today, we live only a mile down the road in our forever home.
I am not just passing through this district.
I am deeply invested in its past, its present, and its future.
After earning my degree from Purdue Fort Wayne and a master’s in actuarial science from Ball State, and gained experience at Lincoln Financial Group learning how to take on complex problems, analyze risks, and develop real solutions.
However, nothing could have prepared me for the arrival of my first child, who was born with profound disabilities.
Overnight, my life shifted to doctor visits, therapies, and navigating a system buried in red tape.
When our private insurance refused to cover the feeding tube supplies my son needed (and 13 years later continues to need) to survive, Medicaid became our only option for his lifeline.
I quickly learned that our healthcare systems are often stressful, overwhelming, and unforgiving for those they are meant to serve.
Without a roadmap to follow, I was forced to learn the system the hard way by asking questions, piecing things together, and spending hours on hold.
Those struggles helped me gain a practical understanding of where our state systems fail and how easily families fall through the cracks.
I believe that if you see a problem, you have a responsibility to act.
That’s why I have spent my recent years turning my personal experience into public advocacy:
- Building Solutions: In 2022, I founded Family Solutions Home Care to create a model that values caregivers and puts families first. to address the systemic failures I witnessed, specifically in how we compensate caregivers and support the family unit.
I have turned my personal struggle into a professional mission, helping families navigate a system they should not have to fight alone.
- Advocating for You: I’ve never been one to sit on the sidelines.
From 2023 to 2025, I served on the founding leadership team of Indiana Families United for Care, helping to establish a statewide coalition committed to protecting access to vital Medicaid services for vulnerable Hoosier families.
In this role, I worked to bridge the gap between state policy and real-life needs—so families weren’t left behind by the system meant to serve them.
- Standing for Our Neighbors: Receiving The Arc of Indiana’s 2024 Family Advocate Award as part of a statewide advocacy team was a defining moment in my journey.
Our advocacy proved that our voices have power and that when we demand better from our leaders, we get results.
I am ready to take that same award-winning dedication to the Statehouse to ensure our community is a top priority, not an afterthought.
A Foundation of Hard Work —
I was raised in a union household and am now a union wife, with a deep respect for hard work and fairness.
I grew up believing that if you worked hard and played by the rules, you should be able to provide for your family and build a life right here in Fort Wayne.
For generations, that promise held true.
Today, it is slipping away.
Even families doing everything right feel the squeeze.
For many households, financial security is far more fragile than it appears.
A single injury, layoff, or unexpected diagnosis can quickly push a family from stability into hardship.
As the cost of living rises, affordability has become a daily concern for too many of our neighbors.
That is why I believe in hand-ups rather than handouts.
While these support systems provide a vital safety net, they must be managed with the fiscal responsibility and oversight required to ensure they remain sustainable for the long term.
We need fiscally responsible systems that help people through a rough patch, restore stability, and allow Hoosiers to survive with their dignity intact.
Why I Am Running for State Senate —
My path to public service was shaped by lived experience rather than politics.
I have spent years helping families navigate systems that too often created new barriers.
I have seen the real-world consequences that occur when essential services fail and affordability is treated as an afterthought.
Rather than accepting these failures, I am stepping up to help fix what is broken.
Too often, policies are written by people disconnected from the reality of families who are doing everything right and still falling behind.
This detachment is nowhere more evident than in our state’s current Medicaid crisis.
District 15 deserves a representative who understands the stakes because they have lived them, listens to neighbors, and works hard to deliver practical solutions.
As your Senator, I will lead with integrity and make sure state government works for Fort Wayne’s families and small businesses instead of political insiders or outside interests.

This campaign is about building something honest and lasting.
It is powered by neighbors who believe Indiana can do better when leaders are accountable and focused on results.
Whether you live in Senate District 15 or work alongside it, your voice matters.
Real change happens when neighbors come together, speak up, and vote for the future they want.
I would be honored to earn your trust, your support, and your vote.
