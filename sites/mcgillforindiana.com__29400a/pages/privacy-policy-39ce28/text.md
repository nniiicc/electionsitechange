This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, protect, and share information when you visit McGillForIndiana.com, communicate with the campaign, volunteer, subscribe to campaign communications, complete a form or survey, attend an event, make a contribution, or otherwise interact with us.
Information We Collect We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including: Name Email address Phone number Mailing address or ZIP code Volunteer interests and availability Communication preferences SMS opt-in status and consent records, including the date, method, and source of consent Survey, petition, or form responses Event registration information Contribution information Other information you choose to provide We may collect this information through our website, sign-up forms, surveys, petitions, event registrations, volunteer forms, contribution forms, direct communications with the campaign, and other campaign-related interactions.
Payment card information submitted through third-party contribution processors is processed by those providers and is not stored by the campaign.
How We Use Information We may use information to: Operate and improve our website Respond to questions and requests Communicate about campaign news and issues Send newsletters and campaign updates Coordinate volunteers and supporters Provide event information Provide voter and election information Send fundraising communications Send text messages to individuals who have consented to receive them Record and manage communication preferences, consent, subscriptions, and opt-out requests Process contributions and provide confirmations Analyze website usage and communications Maintain campaign records Comply with campaign finance, election, and other legal requirements Email Communications If you choose to receive campaign emails, we may send newsletters, campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, event invitations, voter information, fundraising requests, and other campaign-related communications.
You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link contained in campaign emails or by contacting us.
Text Messaging and Mobile Information If you choose to receive text messages from McGill for a Better Indiana, we may send campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, event information, voter and election information, fundraising requests, promotional communications, and other campaign-related messages consistent with the consent you provided.
We send text messages only to individuals who have provided the consent required for the type of messages being sent.
Providing a phone number to the campaign by itself does not constitute consent to receive campaign text messages.
Message frequency varies and is typically 2–4 messages per month.
Frequency may increase around Election Day, major campaign events, or other time-sensitive campaign activities.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
After opting out, you may receive a final message confirming your request.
You will not receive additional campaign text messages unless you subsequently choose to opt in again.
For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at info@mcgillforindiana.com or 260-333-5171.
Consent to receive text messages is not required to volunteer, donate, submit a form, use our website, or otherwise support the campaign.
Mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, and text-message consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
We may disclose mobile information to service providers solely as necessary to operate our messaging systems, maintain consent and opt-out records, and deliver communications on our behalf.
Those providers may use the information only as necessary to provide services to the campaign and not for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
We may collect information about interactions with campaign text messages, including whether you interact with links contained in those messages.
Wireless carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.
Sharing of Information We do not sell or rent personal information.
We may disclose information to service providers, vendors, consultants, contractors, staff, or volunteers who need access to perform services for the campaign.
They may use the information only as necessary to provide those services and are expected to protect its confidentiality.
We may also disclose information: When required by campaign finance or election laws In response to lawful subpoenas, court orders, or governmental requests When reasonably necessary to protect the campaign, our website, or others In aggregated or de-identified form that cannot reasonably identify an individual No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
SMS opt-in information and text-message consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
This restriction does not prevent us from using service providers solely as necessary to operate our messaging program and deliver messages on our behalf.
Your Privacy Choices You may contact us to request that we verify, correct, update, or remove personal information we maintain about you.
You may also request changes to your communication preferences.
Requests may be submitted to info@mcgillforindiana.com .
We may retain information when reasonably necessary to comply with campaign finance, election, tax, recordkeeping, legal, or other applicable requirements, to document consent or opt-out requests, or to protect the rights and integrity of the campaign.
Contributions and Campaign Finance Reporting Online contributions may be processed through third-party services such as ActBlue.
The campaign does not store complete payment-card information processed by those services.
Contribution information may be used to process contributions, provide confirmations, maintain required records, and comply with applicable campaign finance and election laws.
Certain contributor information may be disclosed publicly when required by campaign finance law.
McGillForIndiana.com may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website and understand how visitors use it.
We use Google Analytics to measure website traffic and activity, including information such as pages viewed, referral sources, device and browser information, general geographic information, and website interactions.
We do not intentionally provide Google Analytics with names, email addresses, phone numbers, or other information intended to directly identify individual website visitors.
You may control or delete cookies through your browser settings.
Data Retention We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including campaign operations, communications, recordkeeping, legal compliance, and documenting consent and opt-out requests.
We may retain certain information for longer periods when required or permitted by law.
Data Security We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, destruction, or unauthorized sharing.
These safeguards may include access controls, confidentiality expectations for individuals handling campaign information, reasonable security practices, and data-protection requirements for service providers.
However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Children's Privacy Our website and campaign communications are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.
Third-Party Websites Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services, including contribution processors and social-media platforms.
We do not control and are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third parties.
We encourage visitors to review the applicable privacy policies before providing personal information.
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
