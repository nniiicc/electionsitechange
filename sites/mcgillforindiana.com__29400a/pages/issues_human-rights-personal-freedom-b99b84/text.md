Personal Freedoms
Dignity, Fairness, and Freedom for Every Hoosier
Every Hoosier deserves to be treated with dignity, fairness, and respect under the law.
Government exists to protect our freedoms, not pick winners and losers or interfere unnecessarily in people's private lives.
I believe people can disagree respectfully while still defending each other's rights.
My focus is making sure every Hoosier has the opportunity to live safely, work hard, care for their family, and participate fully in our communities regardless of who they are or where they come from.
Indiana has become increasingly divided by political fights that pit neighbor against neighbor.
Meanwhile, families are more worried about paying their bills, finding affordable healthcare, keeping good schools, and building secure futures.
We don't have to agree on every issue to agree that every person deserves to be treated fairly, that government should respect individual liberty, and that everyone deserves equal protection under the law.
Real leadership means protecting constitutional rights while focusing on solving problems that improve people's everyday lives.
Where I Stand:
- Equal Rights: Every Hoosier deserves equal protection under the law regardless of race, religion, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, or background.
- Personal Freedom: Government should protect individual liberty whenever possible and avoid unnecessary intrusion into personal medical decisions, private lives, and family choices.
- Religious Liberty; Every Hoosier should have the freedom to practice their faith or no faith at all without government favoritism or discrimination.
- People with Disabilities: As someone who has spent years helping families navigate disability services, I will continue fighting for accessibility, equal opportunity, community inclusion, and stronger support systems for people with disabilities and their caregivers.
- Protecting Privacy: Personal medical information, financial information, and personal data should remain private.
Hoosiers deserve strong consumer privacy protections and safeguards against misuse of their personal information.
- Safe and Fair Elections: Every eligible Hoosier should be able to vote without unnecessary barriers, while maintaining secure elections that the public can trust.
Not every issue has an easy answer, and reasonable people will sometimes disagree.
That doesn't mean we stop listening to one another.
I believe government should protect individual rights, treat every person with dignity and respect, and spend more time solving the everyday challenges Hoosier families face than fueling political division.
I will work with anyone who has good ideas, regardless of political party, because serving Hoosiers matters more than partisan politics.
That includes issues such as reproductive healthcare, LGBTQ rights, religious liberty, immigration, criminal justice, and public safety.
These are important issues, and reasonable people will not always agree.
My commitment is to protect constitutional rights, treat every Hoosier with dignity and respect, and focus on practical solutions that strengthen our communities instead of deepening political divisions.
