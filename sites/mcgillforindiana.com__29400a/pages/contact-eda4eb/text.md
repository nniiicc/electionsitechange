We Want to Hear From You!
Whether you have a question , want to share an idea , or are i nterested in getting involved , we welcome hearing from members of our community.
This campaign is committed to listening, learning, and responding thoughtfully.
While we are a small team, we do our best to read and reply to every message.
How to Reach Us Use the form below to contact the campaign.
You can also reach out if you are interested in volunteering, hosting a conversation, or learning more about where we stand on the issues.
What to Expect After submitting the form, you will receive a confirmation that we have received your message.
