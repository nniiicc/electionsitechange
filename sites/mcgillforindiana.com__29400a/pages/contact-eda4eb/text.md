We Want to Hear From You!
Whether you have a question, want to share an idea, or are interested in getting involved, we welcome hearing from members of our community.
This campaign is committed to listening, learning, and responding thoughtfully.
While we are a small team, we do our best to read and reply to every message.
How to Reach Us
Use the form below to contact the campaign.
You can also reach out if you are interested in volunteering, hosting a conversation, or learning more about where we stand on the issues.
What to Expect
After submitting the form, you will receive a confirmation that we have received your message.
A member of the campaign will follow up as soon as possible.
If you do not hear back right away, please know it is not a lack of interest or appreciation, just a small team doing the work.
Respectful Communication
This campaign values respectful and constructive dialogue.
Messages that are threatening, abusive, or harassing may not receive a response.
Privacy Note
Information submitted through this form is used only for campaign purposes and handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy.
Stay Connected
If you'd like to receive campaign updates by email or text, you can opt in by checking the appropriate box (or both).
These options are completely optional and are separate from any response to the message you send us.
You can opt out of campaign communications at any time.
