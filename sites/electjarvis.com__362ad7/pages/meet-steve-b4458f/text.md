Steve moved to Davidson County with his parents at the age of 14, and immediately knew he wanted to make Davidson County his home for life.
He has proven himself to be a successful business owner of 30 years as the Owner/President of Jarvis Construction.
Steve is a graduate of the UNC School of Government’s Advance Leadership Corps program and is a mentor with the Local Elected Leaders Academy.
Steve’s experience in the development of property in the county through contracting new housing, renovations, and light commercial work since 1990, has built a reputation of integrity for commercial building, fine home construction, and home improvements.
It is this reputation that has earned his company a record in good standing with the Home Builders Association and Better Business Bureau.
He is a person of faith and believes that God should take precedence over our lives.
Steve's Values.
Promote a Quality Education System for Our Children Education is not a one-size-fits-all endeavor.
Our children, the future leaders of our state, deserve a quality education tailored to their diverse needs and abilities.
We must empower parents and students with choices at the local level, expand vocational training options through our community colleges, and continue to support our higher education institutions.
These steps are crucial for training a workforce ready to compete in a global and diverse economy. ​ Safety in our communities and schools remains a top priority.
As your senator, Steve is committed to ensuring that our educational institutions have the resources necessary to provide a safe and enriching learning environment for all students. ​ Serve the People, Promote Jobs, and Lower Taxes Steve's tenure on the Davidson County Board of Commissioners and in the NC State House has been marked by his dedication to fiscal responsibility.
His commitment to controlled spending, low taxes, and a balanced budget has helped attract businesses to our state, stimulating economic growth and ensuring that money stays in the hands of local communities, businesses, and families. ​ Limiting government spending enables businesses and individuals to invest in our communities, innovate solutions, and create job opportunities that contribute to North Carolina’s continued success. ​ Address Regulatory Reform Steve understands from his own experience as a business owner that excessive regulations can impede economic growth.
Our state leaders have successfully reduced corporate tax rates and controlled government spending, which has helped our economy thrive.
However, some areas still require additional support to stimulate job creation and strengthen local economies. ​ Steve advocates for a streamlined regulatory process, ensuring that all necessary rules undergo a thorough review and approval process as if newly proposed.
This regulatory reform is vital for fostering job growth and economic prosperity in both rural and urban communities alike. ​ Steve is dedicated to continuing his work to address these critical issues, ensuring a prosperous future for all North Carolinians.
Legislative Accomplishments.
During his tenure at the General Assembly, Steve has played a pivotal role in advancing key conservative achievements.
In the 2023-2024 Legislative Session, the North Carolina General Assembly enacted several significant conservative victories.
These include: Repealing pistol purchase permit requirements.
Prohibiting gender reassignment surgeries for minors.
Ensuring that sports competitions are sex-specific to protect women's sports.
Requiring cooperation between sanctuary sheriffs and federal immigration agents (ICE) to remove criminal illegal aliens.
Implementing one of the most substantial income tax reductions in North Carolina's history.
