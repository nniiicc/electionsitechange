Vote for a Voice that Counts!
Shawn Kaeser for District 11 A Teacher for a Change Our community deserves a voice when decisions are made at the Statehouse about our schools, our property taxes, and our future.
Currently, we have no seat at the table .
Shawn Kaeser is running to change that.
As a trusted Dublin teacher for 31 years and a father of 9, Shawn has the deep roots and the practical experience to deliver real tax relief, stronger schools, and the affordability our families need.
Don’t let our community be irrelevant in Ohio government.
Dublin and Hilliard should be helping lead the conversation.
The Issues The issues affecting Ohio families are the same ones Shawn lives every day.
As both a teacher and a father, he is uniquely qualified to take those lived experiences straight to the Statehouse and deliver the results our community needs.
Education Academic Rigor.
Critical Thinking.
Problem Solving.
Smartphones, social media, and culture wars, have reshaped childhood and the critical school years.
Academic proficiency is at the lowest levels in decades.
It is time for a reset for Ohio’s schools.
Focus on Academic Content More Books, Less Screens Eliminate Grade Inflation Build Grit and Resilience Prioritize Regular Attendance Promote Career Connected Learning Serve Gifted & Talented Offer Ohio History in High School Preserve Organic Intelligence Tax Relief Common Sense.
Moderation.
Balance.
Taxes are too high.
Property, sales, and income taxes have pushed many of Ohio’s citizens into financial hardship, with the elderly often carrying the heaviest burden.
Striking a balance between providing essential services and lowering taxes requires a disciplined approach and responsible spending.
Currently Dublin and Hilliard are not part of the conversation regarding tax relief and reform and they need to be!
Reduce Government Spending Reform Property Tax Lower Taxes Live.
Work.
Dream.
The American Dream is slipping out of reach for too many Ohio families as the cost of basic necessities continues to climb.
State-level policy must refocus on the fundamentals: making Ohio an affordable, thriving place to live, work, and raise a family.
Protecting that dream requires urgent, common-sense action to lower costs and ensure that our residents can retire with peace of mind and future generations can confidently build their lives right here in our communities.
