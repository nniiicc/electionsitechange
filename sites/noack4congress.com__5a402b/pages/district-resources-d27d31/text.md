About Illinois’ 2nd District This is Mike’s home.
Here’s a look at the communities he’s running to represent.
Illinois’ 2nd Congressional District stretches from Chicago’s South Side through the south suburbs of Cook County and Will County , all the way to the farmland and small towns of Kankakee County — where Mike grew up in Bonfield.
