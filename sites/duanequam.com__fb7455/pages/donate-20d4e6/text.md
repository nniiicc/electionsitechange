Note!
Under Minnesota’s Political Contribution Refund program, Minnesota reimburses up to $75 of qualifying political contributions per year per person.
Joint donations are reimbursed up to $150.
Please consider increasing your donation by the amount of your reimbursement.
Thank you for considering a contribution to the campaign.
Your money will fuel my efforts to improve the lives of all Minnesotans.
We can only accept individual donations; corporate donations cannot be accepted.
Questions about donations can be addressed to the campaign Treasurer.
