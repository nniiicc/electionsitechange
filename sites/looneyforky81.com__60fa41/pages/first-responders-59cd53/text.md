As a candidate for the Kentucky State House of Representatives District 81, and a health care provider, I stand proudly with our law enforcement officers, firefighters, EMS personnel, and all first responders across the Commonwealth.
First responders display unwavering commitment to protecting our communities—often in the face of danger and sacrifice—deserves not only our gratitude but our full support.
I understand the risks they take, the burdens they carry, and the critical role they play in maintaining safety and order.
When elected, I will work to ensure our first responders have the resources, training, and legislative backing they need to serve effectively and return home safely to their families.
Supporting them isn’t just good policy—it’s a moral obligation.
Farmers & Small Businesses As a member of Preserve Madison County, I believe part of the strength of our Commonwealth lies in the hard work of our farmers and the entrepreneurial spirit of our small business owners.
They are the backbone of our local economy, our communities, and our way of life.
I am committed to championing policies that protect family farms, ensure access to fair markets, reduce unnecessary regulations, and promote Kentucky-grown products.
Our farmers deserve the tools and support necessary to thrive in a changing economy—whether that’s access to new technologies, rural broadband expansion, or protection from unfair trade practices.
Likewise, I will advocate for our small businesses—the mom-and-pop shops, tradespeople, and local innovators—who create jobs and invest in our neighborhoods.
That means cutting red tape, supporting vocational education, expanding access to capital, and standing up for local businesses against out-of-state corporate overreach.
Together, we can preserve the values that make Kentucky strong: self-reliance, community, and hard work.
I will work every day to make sure Frankfort listens to the voices of Eastern Kentucky—not just the big cities or special interests.
