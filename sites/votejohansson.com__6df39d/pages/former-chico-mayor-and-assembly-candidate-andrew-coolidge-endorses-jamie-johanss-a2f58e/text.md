ABOUT ISSUES VOLUNTEER ENDORSEMENTS TESTIMONIALS LETTERS TO THE EDITOR MEDIA PHOTOS VIDEO PRESS RELEASES NEWS RESUME DONATE → Blog Layout Former Chico Mayor and Assembly Candidate Andrew Coolidge Endorses Jamie Johansson for Assembly Johansson advances to the general election with Coolidge's full support CHICO, Calif. - Former Chico Mayor and District 3 Assembly candidate Andrew Coolidge, who earned 25 percent of the votes in the primary election and did not advance, endorsed Jamie Johansson for the Assembly seat, adding to the growing coalition of independent-minded voters who believe they should determine the district's future.
Coolidge’s endorsement comes on the heels of the Secretary of State certifying the election results on July 10th.
Johansson and Coolidge’s combined votes accounted for nearly 55% of the total votes in the district and 62% of the votes in Butte County, the largest county in the district.
“Over the past year and a half, I traveled across the North State listening to families, farmers, business owners, doctors, educators, and community leaders,” Coolidge said.
“What I heard repeatedly is that people want practical solutions, strong representation, and someone who understands the unique challenges facing rural California.
I believe Jamie Johansson is the right person to carry that fight forward.” said Coolidge.
He highlighted Johansson’s deep roots in agriculture, his experience as a small business owner, and his years of leadership representing North State farmers and communities.
Johansson, the grassroots candidate, said the November election will ultimately be decided by the people who live, work and raise their families in District 3.
“Andrew ran a campaign centered on service, experience, and improving the quality of life for North State residents,” Johansson said.
“I’m grateful for his support and look forward to working with him and the many community leaders across the district who share our commitment to moving this region forward.” Assembly District 3 stretches across six Northern California counties and includes thousands of farming families, small businesses, and rural communities that have long fought for a stronger voice on issues including affordability, wildfire prevention, water reliability, public safety, and protecting agriculture.
Learn More About Jamie Johansson Jamie Johansson is a first-generation farmer, former president of the California Farm Bureau Federation, former Oroville City Councilman and lifelong advocate for California agriculture and rural communities.
He and his family live on their farm in south Chico.
Johansson is a Christian, a boy scout leader and a big fan of the Durham High School Trojans.
