How to Write Me In
Five simple steps to cast your vote for a real alternative on November 3, 2026.
Check your voter registration
Confirm you are registered to vote in South Carolina Congressional District 1.
This includes voters in Charleston (partial), Berkeley, Dorchester (partial), Beaufort, Colleton (partial), and Jasper counties.
Check registration at scvotes.gov
Find your polling place, or request absentee
Vote in person on Election Day or during early voting.
If you cannot make it to the polls, request an absentee ballot from your county elections office.
Find your polling place
Check in and get your activation card
At your polling place, check in with a poll worker.
They will hand you a blank activation card.
Take it to any open ExpressVote touchscreen machine and insert the card when prompted.
(Voting absentee by mail?
Open the paper ballot that arrives in your envelope.)
Find U.S.
House of Representatives, District 1 and tap Write-In
The screen steps you through each contest.
When you reach U.S.
House of Representatives, District 1, tap the Write-In box, type CLAYTON CUTERI on the on-screen keyboard, then tap Accept.
The machine marks the selection for you.
(On a paper absentee ballot, fill in the Write-In oval and write CLAYTON CUTERI on the line in pen.)
Review and cast your ballot
On the review screen, check the name is spelled correctly, then tap Print Ballot Card.
Take the printed card to the scanner (a separate tabulator machine) and feed it in.
Your vote is cast.
(Absentee voters: sign the return envelope and mail it back before the deadline posted at scvotes.gov.)

Common Questions
Will Clayton be on the primary ballot?
No.
As a write-in candidate, Clayton Cuteri will only appear as a write-in option on the November 3, 2026 general election ballot.
You do not need to vote in the June primary to vote for Clayton in November.
What if I can’t spell “Cuteri” exactly right?
Your vote still counts.
South Carolina counts write-in votes based on voter intent: as long as the name you write clearly identifies Clayton Cuteri and no other candidate with a similar name is running, the ballot will be counted for him.
The safest thing is still to write Clayton Cuteri exactly, and the spelling guide at the top of this page is there to help.
But don’t skip the race if you’re unsure: a recognizable attempt at his name is better than leaving it blank.
Source: guidance from the South Carolina State Election Commission, April 2026.
