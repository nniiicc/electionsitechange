Systems Thinking
at the Household Level.
The same principle that guides district-level infrastructure planning applies at home.
When water, energy, food, and land use are designed together — instead of treated separately — you get resilience instead of strain.
- Reduces municipal water demand — less pressure on shared systems
- Reuses water already paid for — no new infrastructure needed
- Native plants need far less water — and belong here
- Grows local food with recycled water — farm-to-table starts at home
- Reduces grid load and energy bills — rural stability from the bottom up
