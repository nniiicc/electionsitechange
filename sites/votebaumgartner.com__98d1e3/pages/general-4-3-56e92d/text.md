Celebrating Reardan Mule Days
Team Baumgartner also attended this year’s Reardan Mule Days.
Our team and volunteers had a great time walking in the parade, visiting with families, and connecting with neighbors throughout the day.
They even had the opportunity to catch up with State Representative Joe Schmick during the festivities.
A special thank you to John and Jane who have graciously volunteered their truck for our summer parade season.
I’m so grateful to our incredible volunteers and to the Reardan community for the warm welcome and continued support!
