The Red, White, and Blue: Fairfield Flag Day Parade
Team Baumgartner also marched in the Fairfield Flag Day Parade this past weekend.
We proudly walked the route carrying our signs and handing out candy through the streets lined with cheering families.
It was a wonderful opportunity to celebrate the red, white, and blue alongside our neighbors, honoring the freedoms and values that make our country so strong.
The day was filled with patriotism, smiles, and a great sense of community pride.
We loved being a part of this special tradition and want to thank the Fairfield organizers and everyone who came out to make the event so memorable!
