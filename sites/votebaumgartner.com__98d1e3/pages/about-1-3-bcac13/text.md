Summer Parkways: Serving Smiles on Spokane’s South Hill
This year’s Summer Parkways on Spokane’s South Hill was especially fun since it took place on the first day of summer break for many local families!
The event once again brought together cyclists, walkers, runners, and families to enjoy the car-free streets.
It was great to see so many people connecting with their friends and neighbors while enjoying a beautiful summer day.
Team Baumgartner had fun handing out popsicles and greeting members of the community.
Thank you to everyone who stopped by, and to the organizers and volunteers who made Summer Parkways such a success once again!
