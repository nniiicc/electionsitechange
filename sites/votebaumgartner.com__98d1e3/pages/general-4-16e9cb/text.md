My team had a great time marching in the parade, waving to the enthusiastic crowds, and celebrating the agricultural spirit that brings our community together. ​ Even more exciting, we were thrilled to sponsor two powerful combines competing in the derby.
We cheered them on as they battled it out for victory!
A huge thank you to everyone who came out to support the event.
Events like these highlight the hard work and camaraderie of our farming community, and we’re grateful to be a part of it!
