Lind Combine Demolition Derby and Parade
Click on the picture below for a video of Team Baumgartner at Lind Parade!
This past weekend, Team Baumgartner proudly rolled into Lind for the annual Combine Demolition Derby and community parade.
My team had a great time marching in the parade, waving to the enthusiastic crowds, and celebrating the agricultural spirit that brings our community together.
Even more exciting, we were thrilled to sponsor two powerful combines competing in the derby.
We cheered them on as they battled it out for victory!
A huge thank you to everyone who came out to support the event.
Events like these highlight the hard work and camaraderie of our farming community, and we’re grateful to be a part of it!
