Medical Lake Founders Day: Celebrating Our Heritage
It was great to be back at the Medical Lake Founders Day Parade along with my family and campaign team last weekend.
The streets were lined with neighbors waving flags and cheering, and I really enjoyed visiting with the many community groups taking part.
The children especially enjoyed handing out candy, and I counted 331 high-fives along the route!
Thank you to the Medical Lake community for the warm welcome, and to all the volunteers who came out to join us.
We are so grateful for your wonderful support!
