DONATE ​ Colville Rodeo Parade: Riding Proud, Riding Strong Team Baumgartner was proud to participate in this year's Colville Rodeo Parade, one of Northeast Washington's favorite summer traditions.
My team had a great time handing out candy, high-fiving, and visiting with many members of the community.
It was especially rewarding to see so many families and local organizations come together to celebrate.
Thank you to the organizers and volunteers who made the parade such a success, and everyone who came out to cheer us on.
Special thanks to the Stevens County Republican Party for their great support and enthusiasm.
