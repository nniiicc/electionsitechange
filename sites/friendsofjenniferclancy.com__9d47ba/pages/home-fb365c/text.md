I'm Jennifer Clancy.
I am your neighbor.
We probably have some things in common.
I serve my community as a licensed mental health professional.
I am a former public school counselor and former small business owner.
I married my college sweetheart (local police officer and former educator), and together, we are raising 5 children.
I am an enthusiastic cheerleader at their school events.
You may have heard me in the stands!
I love our community.
I am running for legislative office.
I am campaigning to represent our Northern St.
Mary's County community in Annapolis as your Maryland State Delegate representing Legislative District 29A.
Delegates represent the voices of their constituents (that's YOU) on local issues at the State level and ensure those interests are included in policy, legislation and funding appropriations that make a direct impact on the community back home.
I've been a community advocate for over 20 years: Former Public School Educator & Educational Counselor Maryland Licensed Clinical Professional Counselor, Board- approved Supervisor Former Charles County Commission for Women, Legislative Representative Women of Action Charles County, Chair Healthcare Committee Co-founder "SOMD Mask Makers" volunteers, sewing 4500+ masks, donated to local first responders, healthcare providers during the COVID19 pandemic.
