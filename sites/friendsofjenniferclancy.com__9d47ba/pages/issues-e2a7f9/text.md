What changes are our neighbors asking for?
As I learn more from YOU, this list will grow.
Here are just a few: Integrity within and diplomatic working relationships between the Judicial, Executive and Legislative Systems entrusted to Elected Leaders through fair elections, accountability, and oversight.
Providing opportunities to gain valuable knowledge and relevant experience through fully funded public libraries , public schools , access to job readiness training , GED programming , and career and technical education .

Understanding the relationship between Federal and State Constitutional rights of all members of our community, including Due Process and 2A supporters.
Promotion of Civil Rights and Social Justice , ensuring dignity and worth of every member of our community.

When we work towards building our Community , we thrive.

As a Mental Health Now candidate, I will lead and support policies that expand access, improve crisis response, invest in youth mental health, and strengthen our mental health workforce.
As an Educator endorsed Candidate, I will endorse the ESP Bill of Rights and work to ensure funding to support universal pre-k, free school meals, expansion of our career pathways programming, and school based mental health supports.
As a RJM Action candidate, I pledge to strengthen policies that expand equitable access to menstrual and sexual healthcare products, protect informed choice and decision making between individuals and providers, and commit to the removal of discriminatory barriers to care.
As a ATU and UA endorsed candidate, I am committed to policies that are Pro-worker, Pro-union, Pro- public transit, Pro-trades, Pro-public good.
EDUCATION I believe parents, caregivers, and community members share the responsibility to ensure that each child in our county is offered quality, affordable, safe, and accessible educational experiences that meet developmental, cognitive and emotional needs.
Maryland's Constitution guarantees a "thorough and efficient system of free public schools" in Article VIII, Section 1 for all students regardless of citizenship status.
As a former public school educator, school counselor, and parent, I support continued funding of our county public schools and libraries.
I am also a former homeschooling parent.
We have friends who pay tuition to send their children to private schools.
I believe that every family has needs that require differentiated learning environments and I respect their right to fund alternative educational opportunities for their child(ren).
I also support public trade/career development pathways and higher educational institutions.
I know firsthand that choice matters for the wellbeing of all our students in their pursuit of lifelong learning.
I promise to listen, learn, and advocate for the various needs that our families bring to the table.
HEALTH CARE State insurance (Medicare, Medicaid, Maryland Children's Health Program ), private insurance, and concierge plans demonstrate that everyone has varying access and resources available to ensure their personal and family's wellbeing.
I believe everyone, regardless of circumstance, should be provided opportunities to accessible and appropriate health and wellbeing services.
I pledge to advocate for increased access and maintained funding to support those vulnerable in our community.
COMMUNITY Affordability, Justice, Culture, Rural Character...I believe our county, regardless of zip code, is comprised of neighbors.
How we work together for the common good makes a difference for all of us .
