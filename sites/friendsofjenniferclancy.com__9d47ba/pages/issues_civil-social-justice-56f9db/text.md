COMMUNITY

Affordable Housing
It is abundantly clear from polling that our neighbors are seeking to set roots in St.
Mary's County, MD, but are finding the housing market inaccessible.
Housing insecurity is real.
There are neighbors who are unhoused, in transitional housing, and struggling to secure state and county funded housing assistance.
Economic, Civil, Criminal & Social Justice
I honor our Constitutional promise that all members of our community shall be afforded the rights to Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness.
The historical significance of our Nation's past merging with the changes we are witnessing currently are worth our collective consideration.
I am a proud and fierce advocate for the advancement of these promises for our LGBTQIA+, BIPOC, Immigrant, and Neurodivergent communities here in St.
Mary's County and nationwide.
I promise to always Stand on the Side of Love.
- Investing in youth development, recreation & career expansion
- Intentional community development to encourage social cohesion & collaborative safety efforts
- Make St.
Mary’s County the place to do business = more careers, not just jobs
- Expanding economic opportunity in our defense, agriculture & aquaculture industries
- Support Small Businesses and Local Unions
- Ensuring an equitable justice system that accounts for disparities in criminalization among historically marginalized communities.
- Enforcing law & order even in cases where the accused might be wealthy or privileged.
- Application of Restorative Justice practices, as the Courts deem appropriate.
- Honoring victims and survivors of violent crimes
- Expanded protections for children, elders, victims of hate crimes, domestic and sexual abuse, and animals, by going after their perpetrators & prosecuting them to the fullest extent
- Wealth, fame, privilege should never make you exempt from justice
