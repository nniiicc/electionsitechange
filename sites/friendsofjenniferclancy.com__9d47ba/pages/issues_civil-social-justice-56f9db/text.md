Affordable Housing It is abundantly clear from polling that our neighbors are seeking to set roots in St.
Mary's County, MD, but are finding the housing market inaccessible.
Housing insecurity is real.
There are neighbors who are unhoused, in transitional housing, and struggling to secure state and county funded housing assistance.
I am a firm believer that housing is health care and a human right.
I have spoken to a Commissioner about this issue, who generously took time to teach me about what they have learned from county residents and the budgetary constraints presented.
We both agreed on the importance for our neighbors to be afforded opportunities for lower cost rentals and home ownership.
We discussed the challenges they are currently facing in negotiating for housing in our rural county, specifically related to land preservation in our agrarian-based economy.
While we do profit tremendously from the PAX Naval base, Commissioners, Developers, delegates, and related county commissions are not in agreement about how increasing housing will impact the infrastructural and organizational needs of the county.
Every proposed housing unit will require changes to budgets, traffic patterns, roadway and bridge safety & integrity assessments, septic, well and public works installations, not to mention public school impact.
State funding must be allocated to St.
Mary's County so we can ensure safe and affordable housing is guaranteed to all who choose to call St.
Mary's County home.
Economic, Civil, Criminal & Social Justice I honor our Constitutional promise that all members of our community shall be afforded the rights to Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness .
The historical significance of our Nation's past merging with the changes we are witnessing currently are worth our collective consideration.
I am a proud and fierce advocate for the advancement of these promises for our LGBTQIA+, BIPOC, Immigrant, and Neurodivergent communities here in St.
Mary's County and nationwide.
