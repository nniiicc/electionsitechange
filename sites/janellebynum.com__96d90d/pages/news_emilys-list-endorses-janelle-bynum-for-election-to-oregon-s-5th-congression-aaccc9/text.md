EMILYs List Endorses Janelle Bynum for Election to Oregon’s 5th Congressional District
State Representative Janelle Bynum released the following statement upon receiving EMILYs List's endorsement:
“I’m thrilled to be endorsed by EMILYs List, the nation’s largest resource for women in politics.
As a woman who is outraged that girls throughout the country have fewer rights than I did for most of my life and a mom who wants my daughters to have the same rights as my sons, I know firsthand our community deserves a congresswoman who will protect abortion access and fight for our freedoms.
As Lori Chavez-DeRemer continues to side with her extremist colleagues and abandon Oregon’s values, we are putting the coalition together to flip this seat and elect a pro-choice majority to Congress.”
