Janelle Believes in
STRENGTHENING THE ECONOMY AND CREATING JOBS
Our country doesn’t thrive unless our local economies thrive, and that means creating good jobs close to home, supporting small businesses, and bringing down the cost of living.
In the State House, Janelle championed these issues, leading the passage of the Oregon CHIPS Act, the most pro-business, pro-worker, pro-Oregon collaboration in history.
She brought that same focus to Congress to ensure that we’re improving Oregon’s business environment, supporting local businesses and rural economies, and bringing down costs.
