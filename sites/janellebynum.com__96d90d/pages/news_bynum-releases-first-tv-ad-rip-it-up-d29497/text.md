Bynum Releases First TV Ad, “Rip It Up”
Clackamas, OR – Today, Congresswoman Janelle Bynum’s campaign released its first ad of the cycle, “Rip It Up.” The ad, which focuses on Congresswoman Bynum’s work to improve schools, create good-paying jobs, and lower the cost of housing, will air on broadcast and cable as part of a six-figure ad buy.
“Everyone knows the status quo isn’t working for anyone that isn’t a billionaire,” said Bynum.
“Oregonians sent me to Congress to take on the establishment and deliver real results – so that’s exactly what I’ve done.
I’m running to keep fighting for lower costs, better care, and more opportunity.”
