Bynum Decisively Wins Primary, Ready for General Election Fight
Clackamas, OR – Today, Congresswoman Janelle Bynum released the following statement after advancing to the general election as the Democratic nominee for Oregon’s 5th Congressional District.
She will now face Trump loyalist and extremist Patty Adair in November.
“I’m grateful for the trust that voters have placed in me once again, but I’m not taking a victory lap on tonight's results – my focus remains on the general,” said Rep.
Bynum.
“The job continues tomorrow morning.
Families are still dealing with rising costs, our democracy is still under attack, and Republicans are always willing to put good money behind a bad candidate.
On to November.”
