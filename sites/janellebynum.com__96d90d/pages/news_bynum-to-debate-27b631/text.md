Bynum to Debate
Clackamas, OR – Today, Congresswoman Janelle Bynum announced that she will participate in a debate with Trump loyalist and extremist Patty Adair on October 10th hosted by KTVZ and the League of Women Voters of Deschutes.
She released the following statement:
The Bynum for Congress campaign will continue to review proposals for debates submitted by August 15, 2026.
Accepted proposals will be notified by August 30, 2026.
We encourage those that wish to host a debate to partner with other community organizations and media to ensure fair representation of our district.
Debates proposals can be submitted to press@janellebynum.com
