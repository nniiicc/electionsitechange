Bynum Reports Massive Quarter 1, Raising over $750K
“I am grateful for all the support we have received and that my work in Congress is resonating with the voters here in Oregon,” said Bynum.
“My constituents tell me that costs are too high and opportunity is too scarce – that’s why I’ve made addressing those issues my top priorities in Washington.
With the continued support of Oregonians, I’ll keep fighting against the cruelty and nonsense coming from the White House and for a better life for everyone in Oregon.”
She’s fighting for families over special interests with a strong grassroots campaign that over 16,000 people have donated to this cycle.
