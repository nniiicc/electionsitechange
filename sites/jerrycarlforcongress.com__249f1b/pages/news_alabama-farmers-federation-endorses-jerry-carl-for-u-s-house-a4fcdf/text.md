The Alabama Farmers Federation announced its endorsement of Jerry Carl for Alabama’s First Congressional District Thursday.
Carl, a Republican who served in the U.S.
House from 2021-2025, is running for his former seat after U.S.
Rep.
Barry More, R-Enterprise, beat him in a close GOP primary in 2024.
Moore is now running for U.S.
Senate.
“I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of the Farmers Federation and Alabama’s outstanding farm community,” Carl said in a statement.
“Our farmers represent the very backbone of this nation — men and women whose hard work and dedication sustain our way of life.”
