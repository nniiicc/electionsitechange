Skip to main content Skip to footer Opens in a new tab Home About Issues News Donate Sweet Tea and Politics: Jerry Carl’s Kitchen Table Politics March 2, 2026 On this weeks episode of Angela’s Sweet Tea and Politics podcast, listeners across Alabama will hear a wide-ranging and energetic conversation with congressional candidate Jerry Carl about the future of Alabama and the priorities facing District One.
From the outset, the discussion focused on kitchen-table issues: rising grocery and gas prices.
Carl connected the two, noting that transportation costs directly affect what families pay at the store.
He emphasized reducing regulations on farmers, supporting Alabama’s agricultural community, and encouraging more locally grown fruits and vegetables to lower costs .
On energy, he argued that expanded domestic production and deregulation are key to bringing fuel prices down, pointing to pipeline expansion and increased supply as tangible solutions that would benefit families in Mobile, Baldwin County, and the Wiregrass.
