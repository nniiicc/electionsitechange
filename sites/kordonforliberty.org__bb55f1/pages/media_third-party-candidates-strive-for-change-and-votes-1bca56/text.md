For him, that means keeping taxes low, deregulating health care at the local level, and lowering costs and regulations for new businesses.
Laszacs—and a record number of Libertarian candidates, as well as candidates from various other parties that aren’t Republican or Democratic—are on Wake voters’ ballots this year.
They want to shift voters’ thinking, even if winning an election is an unlikely scenario.
