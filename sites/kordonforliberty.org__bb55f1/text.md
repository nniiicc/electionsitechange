Residents crave solutions and the protection of their freedom… it turns out that these are one and the same.
Foremost, our economy, future education, and the very systems require my attention.
Elect me, and I will leverage the same superb effectiveness that I demonstrated at the 2026 LPNC convention where I received unanimous approval for legislation amending our platform.
Housing is our greatest ongoing problem and is caused by wrong priorities encouraging population inflow without enough housing built.
Meanwhile, the Parent’s Bill of Rights was good, but families deserve more: choice of alternatives, local control, the teaching of virtue, and funding that goes with the student’s family.
None of that will last, however, if we do not implement reforms for a brighter future.
We need protection through moderate, staggered term limits.
Instant run-off ranked voting would give voters nuance and make elections fair for third parties.
Most importantly, independent redistricting would defeat biased gerrymandering which circumvents your democracy.
All of that with the addition of transparency from the government will reduce our dangerous polarization.
In all things, liberty and justice will be my guiding principles.
