This privacy notice for Kevin For Idaho (https://kevin.dems4idaho.org) (“we,” “us,” or “our“), describes how and why we might collect, store, use, and/or share (“process“) your information when you visit our website.
What personal information do we process?
When you visit, use, or navigate our website, we may process personal information if you voluntarily fill out a “Contact Us” form and click Submit.
Do we process any sensitive personal information?
We do not process sensitive personal information, only name, email address, phone number for inclusion in our private database which is used solely for processing information directly involved with Kevin For Idaho.
Our website automatically collects meta data about every visit as is common with every public website created using WordPress.
Meta data is anonymous information about your internet ip address, browser name, operating system and other technical, non-personal information.
Cookies?
Cookies are small data files that all browsers may write to your on-line device for purposes of streamlining future visits to sites and for tracking meta data as described above.
The WordPress environment of this website may set cookies on your device, but NO use of that data is engaged in our design of the site.
If you disable cookies in your browser, this website will
continue to be fully functional.
Do you receive any information from third parties?
We receive information from public databases, (VAN, Idaho Secy of State), Idaho Democratic Party, social media platforms, and other outside sources such as sign-up lists at public events.|
How do you process my information?
We process your information to provide, improve, and administer our website, communicate with you, for security and fraud prevention, and to comply with law.
We may also process your information for other purposes with your expressed consent.
We process your information only for the purpose of sharing Democratic events and issues.
In what situations and with which types of parties do we share personal information?
We may share information with the Idaho Democratic Party to update their public databases.
No information whatsoever (name, address, email, phone) will be shared with or sold to any third parties for marketing/promotional purposes.
How do we keep your information safe?
We have organizational and technical processes and procedures in place to protect your personal information.
We do NOT store your information in this web site or on any public facing computer server.
However, no electronic transmission over the internet or information storage technology can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, so we cannot promise or guarantee that hackers, cybercriminals, or other unauthorized third parties will not be able to access, steal, or modify your information during transmission on the Internet or while residing on private equipment used in the management of Kevin For Idaho.
What are your rights?
Depending on where you are located geographically, the applicable privacy law may mean you have certain rights regarding your personal information.
How do I exercise my rights?
The easiest way to exercise your rights is by contacting the WEBMASTER: bbaker@dems4idaho.org 208 572-0723.
We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.
