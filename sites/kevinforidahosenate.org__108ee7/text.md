BIO
Kevin Gerrard is a lifelong East Idaho resident who is proud to call District 31 home.
After moving to Ririe as a teenager, he graduated from Ririe High School and built his life and career in the region.
Today, he lives in Rigby with his husband, Justin.
Kevin is a REALTOR® with Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices Silverhawk Realty East Idaho, where he works directly with individuals and families navigating some of the most important financial decisions of their lives.
Through his work, he regularly sits down with people who are dealing with rising costs, housing challenges, and long-term planning for their future.
He currently serves on the Snake River MLS Board of Directors and has previously served on the Local Political Involvement Committee.
He has also served as Vice President of the Ririe Coalition for Community Development and as a member of the Ririe Chamber of Commerce.
Kevin has volunteered at the Idaho Falls Soup Kitchen and believes strongly in giving back to the community.
