PRIORITIES Where I stand 01 Infrastructure I believe South Carolina should continue improving our roads and bridges while making sure SCDOT delivers on the investments taxpayers are making.
02 Veterans I want South Carolina to remain a welcoming home for active-duty military members, veterans and their families.
03 Public Safety I will continue supporting the law enforcement officers, firefighters and EMS personnel who protect our neighborhoods and respond when we need them most.
04 Accountability I believe the law should apply equally and that South Carolinians deserve strong safeguards to hold public officials accountable.
05 Transparency I believe South Carolinians should have access to information about legislation and government decisions that affect their lives.
06 Opportunity I support lower taxes, less unnecessary red tape and an environment where individuals and private businesses have the opportunity to grow.
