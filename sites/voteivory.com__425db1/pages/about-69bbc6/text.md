Pleasant, Utah (Sanpete County) in 1963.
He grew up in Tempe, Arizona, graduating from McClintock HS in 1981.
He served a mission for the LDS Church in Guatemala.
Ken met and married his wife Becky in 1988.
Becky is a past recipient of the Golden Apple Teacher Award.
They have four amazing children and three adorable grandchildren.
Experienced Leadership Ken Ivory, JD, served nine years in the Utah House of Representatives before returning to the private sector in 2019.
He was later called back into service by the voters of his district and is proud to once again represent our district.
He maintains a general business and estate planning law practice.
Ken also taught American Federalism at Utah Valley University.
He chaired the National Center to Restore the Balance of Government for the American Legislative Exchange Council, an organization that honored him as the national State Legislator of the Year in 2014, and he is the author of “Where’s the Line?
