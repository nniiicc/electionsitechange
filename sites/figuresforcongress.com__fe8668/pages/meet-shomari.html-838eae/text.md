Born and raised in Alabama, Shomari Figures represents Alabama's 2nd District in Congress.
He is a true public servant who is dedicated to improving the lives of the people of Alabama.
In Congress Delivering for Alabama.
Congressman Shomari Figures is delivering for the people of Alabama's 2nd District.
Expand Access to Healthcare Successfully pushed to keep Jackson Hospital open and introduced legislation to help prevent rural hospitals across Alabama's 2nd District from closing.
Provide Relief to Farmers Introduced legislation to provide a $15 billion, tariff relief program for small and mid-sized farmers, who are the backbone of our economy.
Invest in Local Infrastructure Brought home nearly $6 million for infrastructure projects across Alabama's 2nd District to improve roads, ports, and water and sewage systems.
Support Law Enforcement Delivered $3.3 million to help local law enforcement keep communities safe.

For Shomari, Alabama is home.
It is where he was raised in his grandfather's church and educated from kindergarten to law school.
After graduating from John L.
LeFlore High School , he went on to the University of Alabama , where he then followed in his father's footsteps into the university's law school.
Shomari's parents inspired him to dedicate his life to advocating for the people of Alabama.
His father, Michael Figures , was a State Senator and lawyer who fought for over two decades to expand rights for Black people in Mobile and across Black Belt Alabama.
His mother, Vivian Figures , served on the Mobile City Council, currently serves as a State Senator, and has worked to protect women's rights and improve health and education in Alabama.
The family Home is everything.
Shomari is the proud husband of a former school teacher and strong advocate for our communities, Dr.
Kalisha Dessources Figures , and father of four: Micah, Novah, Zorah and Erzah .
It's that next generation, and every family across Alabama's 2nd District, that drives his work: better schools, accessible healthcare, good jobs, and communities where every child can grow up safe and supported.
