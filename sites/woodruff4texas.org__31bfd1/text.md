Woodruff has spent more than 30 years building businesses, leading teams, and solving real-world challenges.

His experience as a collegiate and professional athlete taught him the importance of discipline, teamwork, preparation, and accountability.

He encourages community service, ethical leadership, personal responsibility, compassion, and civic engagement.
His work reflects a commitment to the constitutional freedoms and equal protections that safeguard every American.

His life has been shaped by faith, hard work, integrity, and service, and those same values guide his vision for the people of HD92.
