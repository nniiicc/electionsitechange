Power to the People: Revive the 1997 Electric Restructuring Act Why We Need to Bring Back the Free Market: ​ The Blueprint Already Exists : The 1997 Electric Restructuring Act (17 OK Stat § 190.2) legally codified the goal of a competitive retail energy market—we don't need to reinvent the wheel, we just need to unfreeze the law.
End the Monopoly Tax : Territorial utility monopolies eliminate competition, meaning providers have no free-market incentive to lower your rates or improve your service.
Let Capitalism Thrive : When companies are forced to compete for your business, the consumer wins.
A deregulated market drives innovation, forces competitive pricing, and rewards efficiency.
True Consumer Choice : Oklahomans deserve the fundamental capitalist right to shop around for their electricity provider, just as they do for car insurance, internet, or any other vital service.
