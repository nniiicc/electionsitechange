A Commitment to Oklahoma.
For Booker T.
Newton, Oklahoma isn't just a place to live—it's a legacy.
As a third-generation Oklahoman, husband, father, and proud grandfather, his roots in our soil run deep. ​ When his active duty ended, his commitment to his brothers and sisters in arms did not.
Knowing firsthand the challenges of coming home, Booker stepped up to train veterans nationwide, guiding them safely back into civilian life and ensuring no one is left behind.
He is carrying that same warrior's spirit to the State Capitol.
Booker is running because he believes District 32 deserves a leader who knows how to fight for them, who understands the true meaning of sacrifice, and who will work tirelessly to build a stronger Oklahoma for the next generation.
