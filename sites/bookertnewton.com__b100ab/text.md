top of page Home Meet Booker T Platform Get Involved Contact DONATE Star Meet Booker T Booker T Newton is a community leader, husband, father and grandfather.
His family roots run deep, he is a third generation Oklahoman whose passion for service started a young age.
He is a former active duty army veteran who spent multiple years standing up for America values across the world.
Now retired from the army, he was recruited to train other veterans across the country on how to transition back to civilian life.
He is carrying that service forward to represent our community at the State Capitol and advocate for a better Oklahoma.
