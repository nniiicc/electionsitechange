Bring Down the Cost of Living for Working Iowans
Too many Iowans are living pay check to pay check, making impossible choices because of their financial realities.
It’s the mom making the impossible choice, do I pay my utility bill or buy my child a winter coat.
The Dad, do I put food on the table for my family, or pay the rent.
The daughter, do I care for my aging parent or go to work and earn a paycheck.
No one should have to make these kinds of choices.
DC politicians are turning a blind eye to challenges that hard working families face in our state.
Instead of fighting for us, they are more concerned with their billionaire donors, and the predatory corporations that care more about their bottom line than the person behind it.
