Skip navigation menu About Endorsements Issues News Volunteer Events Store Donate About Endorsements Issues News Volunteer Events Store Donate About Lindsay Lindsay James’ journey to public service began long before she decided to run for Congress.
As a chaplain, she sat beside people in their hardest hours—listening without judgment, offering hope, and finding common ground where none seemed possible.
Those moments taught her that each person matters, and their struggles deserve attention.
As a state representative, Lindsay James has been a voice that refuses to be drowned out—a dynamic force with a ready smile and the unshakable conviction: that government should serve the people, not the powerful, and that no one—no child, no farmer, no working family—should be pushed aside.
Rooted in Iowa values and guided by a deep moral compass, she leads with the kind of fire that speaks truth to power, painting a vision of Iowa where opportunity is not a privilege for the few but a promise to us all.
A Proven Record of Bringing People Together In the legislature, Lindsay worked across party lines to: Co-found Iowa’s first bipartisan, bicameral caucus—breaking down barriers between Republicans and Democrats to solve problems.
Expand access to affordable insulin, ensuring Iowans can get the life-saving medication they need without facing financial ruin.
Protect mobile home residents from predatory corporate landlords looking to price-gouge hardworking families.
Champion affordable housing and mental health care, advocating for policies that strengthen communities from the ground up.
Whether she’s working across the aisle to pass bipartisan solutions, or standing firm against extremist attacks on Iowa’s values, Lindsay puts people first – always.
Fighting for Your Future Lindsay is running for Congress to level the playing field for all Iowans and restore hope in the American dream.
Too many Iowans are facing impossible choices just to get by.
From the dad dipping into emergency savings to buy pizza for his child’s birthday, to the mom deciding between a winter coat or food on the table, to the senior choosing whether to pay for life-saving prescriptions or rent—these are the daily struggles of our neighbors.
From young families burdened by skyrocketing childcare costs to single parents forced to choose between caring for their kids or saving for a home, every Iowan deserves leaders who truly see them—and who will never stop fighting for them.
In Congress, Lindsay will fight for: Good-paying jobs that pay the bills and allow Iowans to save for the future.
High-quality, affordable, and accessible healthcare for every family.
Strong schools that keep our kids safe and prepare them for the jobs of the future.
Clean water and a healthy environment for generations to come.
Affordable child care, elder care, and national paid family and medical leave for all families.
Lindsay’s Promise Politics should be about solving problems, not playing partisan games.
Lindsay James will listen, lead with compassion, bring people together, and build a future where every Iowa family can build the life they deserve—no matter their ZIP code.
