You can register online, through the mail, or at the polls on Election Day.
Register to vote To pre-register to vote, complete a voter registration form and return it to your county auditor’s office .
The pre-registration deadline is October 19, 5pm in person. and 11:59 pm online.
You can register to vote online by visiting the Iowa Department of Transportation website Absentee Voting Absentee by Mail For information on how to vote by absentee ballot - click here Absentee in Person You may cast an absentee ballot in person at your county auditor's office before any election.
You must vote with your absentee ballot at the auditor's office.
Some counties have satellite absentee voting stations to provide in-person absentee voting at places other than the auditor's office.
You must mark the absentee ballot at the auditor's office or the satellite station and leave it with the officials.
You cannot take the ballot home with you.
To find out whether a satellite station is planned for an election, contact your county auditor or iwillvote.com .
Voting on Election Day Polls are open from 7:00 am to 8:00 pm on Election Day.
If you are in line before 8PM, you can stay in line & vote.
Registering to Vote on Election Day You may register and vote on the same day during early voting and on election day.
You must go to the correct polling place for your current address on election day.
If you are unsure of your polling place, Find Your Precinct/Polling Place .
At the polling place, you must prove both who you are and where you live.
Find your Polling Place: To find your polling place to vote, Find Your Precinct/Polling Place .
