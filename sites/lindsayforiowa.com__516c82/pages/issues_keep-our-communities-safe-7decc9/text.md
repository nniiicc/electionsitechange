Keep Our Communities Safe
Pass Commonsense Immigration Reform: Congress has failed in its duty to fix our broken immigration system.
We need to invest in border security, increase the number of immigration judges to deal with the massive backlog of cases, and create a path to citizenship for hard working, taxpaying people in this country.
Reform ICE: ICE has been used to terrorize innocent and law abiding American citizens in cities across this country.
We need new leadership, new training methods, and get back to the basics of targeting the worst of the worst.
Common Sense Gun Safety: We need to get dangerous weapons out of criminals hands and follow our police officers guidance.
We must close loopholes, mandate violent history checks, pass red flag laws, and ban assault weapons.
As a mother, I should not be scared to send my child to school.
Invest in Law Enforcement: Our police officers are on the front lines of the systemic problems in our society.
We need to ensure law enforcement is fully funded, paid well, and their benefits are protected.
