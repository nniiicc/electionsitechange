Stand With Veterans
Restore Funding to the VA: Veterans across the district have said that they love their healthcare providers at the VA.
It's imperative that Congress reverse cuts to VA staff so the our veterans can begin to get the care they have not only earned, but love, in their community again.
Expand Mental Health: Iowa is one of the worst states in the country for mental health treatment.
Congress must act to ensure that veterans are able to navigate any mental health challenges no matter their zip code.
Support and Expand the PACT Act: Those who have served our country should never question if they can afford care.
Pass the Major Richard Star Act: Medically retired, combat-injured veterans deserve to receive their full amount in benefits without an unfair offset.
Get Veterans in Homes: Congress must prioritize building new, affordable homes and communities that can support veterans at home.
