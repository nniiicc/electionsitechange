Kids Over Clicks
Put the Duty of Care on Big Tech: Social media and AI companies know just how damaging their products can be - it's time we hold them accountable to safety standards.
Parental Consent and Accountability: Companies must notify parents to what a child can see or do in their app and notify them of any changes.
Exclude Kids automatically from targeted advertising: Corporations should never be allowed to make a profit at the expense of our kids.
Congress must protect our children's' personal information and clearly define and limit what can be collected.
End the Infinite Scroll and other addictive measures: Corporate greed has infected social media, resulting in companies doing anything they can to get and keep your kids attention.
It's time for infinite scroll to end and for screen limits to become available for parents on all apps.
Ensure "good-faith" actors only online: Congress must prohibit online platforms and chatbots from impersonating licensed professionals and be held accountable when they promote damaging content to young people.
