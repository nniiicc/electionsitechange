ALL ARTICLES PRESS RELEASES LATEST UPDATES MEDIA ADVISORY Search ASSEMBLY & SENATE REPUBLICANS OPPOSE ‘REVIVE ACT,’ CITE STATE OVERREACH, DEFEND LOCAL CONTROL Mar 24 1 min read Updated: Jul 14 Assembly Republican Leader Ed Ra (R-Franklin Square), Town of Hempstead Supervisor John Ferretti, and members of the Assembly and Senate Republican Conferences will host a press conference today, March 24, at 10:30 a.m. to voice opposition to the REVIVE Act ( A.10632 ).
The proposed legislation undermines municipalities’ local control by fast-tracking retail spaces and parking areas to be converted into residential and mixed-use developments.
The measure threatens local zoning authority, erodes community input and forces a one-size-fits-all approach to development on neighborhoods across New York.
