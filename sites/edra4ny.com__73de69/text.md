He represents parts of the towns of Hempstead and North Hempstead in Nassau County.

LEARN MORE A MESSAGE FROM ED Dear Friends, For more than a decade, you — the hardworking taxpayers, families, and small business owners—have trusted me to fight for you in the state Assembly.
I value that responsibility, which is why I stand up for everyday New Yorkers and against Albany’s reckless spending, high taxes, and regulations that drive families and jobs out of our state.
Under Albany’s one-party rule, household budgets are being crushed by increasing costs, localities are hurt by Albany’s overreaching mandates, and local businesses operate in a hostile climate that chases away opportunity.
As Assembly Republican Leader, I will keep working to: Lower taxes and eliminate mandates driving up your bills.
Support our police and first responders, and fight back against any misguided policies that threaten community safety.
Ensure decisions in our schools are made by parents, educators, and local boards.
Cut through Albany’s red tape, invest in infrastructure, and make New York truly business-friendly so jobs stay and grow here I will never stop putting our communities first and standing up for working families, seniors, and small businesses.
Thank you for your continued trust and support.
