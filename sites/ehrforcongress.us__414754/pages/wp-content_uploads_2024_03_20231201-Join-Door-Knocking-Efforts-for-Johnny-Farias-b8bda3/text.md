FL-28's Phil Ehr to Join Door-Knocking Efforts for Johnny Farias in Florida's 118th House District's Upcoming Dec.
5th Special Election DATE: December 1, 2023 MIAMI – Democratic Congressional candidate and fellow Navy veteran Phil Ehr will join Johnny Farias, who is running in the Dec.
5th special election for Florida's vacant 118th House District seat, kicking off get-out-the-vote efforts at 9 a.m.
Saturday at the West Kendall Regional Library.
Ehr endorsed Farias on Thursday, emphasizing Farias's dedication to South Florida's working families and to tackling the homeowners' insurance crisis exacerbated by Republican inaction in Tallahassee and D.C.
