Four years of being trusted to be fair.
In January 2021, George Flint took the bench of the 401st District Court as an elected State District Judge.
For the next four years, his job was not to take a side.
It was to hear every side, weigh the facts, apply the law the same way to everyone, and make the call that had to be right.
People who did not always agree with George Flint still trusted him to be fair.
That is the rarest thing in public life, and it is the truest measure of a person's judgment.
A courtroom does not reward the loudest voice.
It rewards the steadiest one.
That is the temperament George would carry to Austin: listen first, weigh honestly, decide under the law, and treat every constituent by the same rules.
Plano and Allen do not need someone learning how to make hard decisions.
They need someone who already has, thousands of times, with the stakes real and the record public.
George Flint spent a career on the trusted side of that difference.
