01 The Record Back the Blue Ensure public safety by funding and supporting law enforcement.
Safe communities are the foundation of a thriving Texas.
George will fight to fully fund our police departments, oppose defunding efforts, and ensure our officers have the tools, training, and legal protections they need to keep HD 70 families safe.
Oppose any efforts to defund or restrict law enforcement Expand qualified immunity protections for officers Support mental health resources for first responders No.
02 The Record Secure Our Border Maintain our national sovereignty by securing our southern border.
An unsecured border is a crisis for all Texans - threatening public safety, straining public resources, and undermining the rule of law.
George will support every state-level tool available to secure the southern border and stop illegal crossings.
Support Operation Lone Star and state border security funding Oppose sanctuary city policies in Texas Crack down on fentanyl and human trafficking networks No.
03 The Record Eliminate Property Taxes Eliminate real estate taxes for homeowners.
Property taxes are forcing long-time Texans out of their homes.
As a homeowner and judge who has seen the devastating effects of tax foreclosures, George believes no Texan should lose their home to the government.
He'll fight to eliminate property taxes on homesteads.
Phase out homestead property taxes through alternative revenue streams Oppose local tax rate increases Strengthen homestead exemptions in the interim No.
04 The Record Ban Taxpayer-Funded Lobbying Eliminate taxpayer funded lobbying.
It's outrageous that your tax dollars fund lobbyists who lobby against your interests.
George will lead the charge to ban government entities from using public money to hire lobbyists - restoring accountability and ending this corrupt practice.
Ban cities, counties, and school districts from paying lobbyists Require full disclosure of any government-funded advocacy spending Close loopholes that allow indirect lobbying through associations No.
05 The Record Build Reliable Energy & Water Promote the development of reliable energy and water resources.
After Winter Storm Uri, Texans know grid reliability is a matter of life and death.
George supports an all-of-the-above energy strategy that prioritizes dispatchable power and long-term water security for a growing Collin County.
Require baseload generation standards for grid reliability Support natural gas, nuclear, and other dispatchable energy sources Invest in water infrastructure to serve a rapidly growing North Texas No.
06 The Record Promote Education Options Ensure quality education through fair competition.
Every child deserves a great education regardless of their zip code.
George supports school choice - empowering parents to select the best learning environment for their children, whether public, charter, private, or homeschool.
Expand Education Savings Accounts (ESAs) for all Texas families Support charter school growth and accountability Protect parental rights in curriculum decisions No.
07 The Record Fix Healthcare Reform healthcare for every Texan.
George will fight to give uninsured Texans access to the same negotiated prices that large insurance companies receive - reducing out-of-pocket costs and expanding access to primary care by allowing more qualified professionals to provide services.
Require transparent pricing so patients know costs upfront Expand scope of practice for nurse practitioners and physician assistants Support direct primary care models that reduce administrative overhead No.
08 The Record Bring Transparency to Government Improve transparency and fight fraud and abuse.
As a judge who presided over civil and criminal cases for years, George has seen firsthand how government opacity breeds corruption.
He will push for full fiscal transparency, sunset unnecessary agencies, and audit programs rife with waste.
Require real-time online disclosure of all state expenditures Strengthen and enforce the Texas Sunset Review process Eliminate redundant state agencies and consolidate functions No.
09 The Record Defend Life, Liberty & Pursuit of Happiness Protect our fundamental rights.
George is a pro-life conservative who believes life begins at conception and that our constitutional rights - from the Second Amendment to religious freedom - are non-negotiable.
He will stand against any effort to erode the liberties that define Texas.
Defend the sanctity of life at every stage Protect Second Amendment rights without compromise Fight for religious liberty in the public square How George Decides Nine conclusions, one standard.
Government earns each dollar and each grant of power it takes from the families of Plano and Allen, or it gives them back.
