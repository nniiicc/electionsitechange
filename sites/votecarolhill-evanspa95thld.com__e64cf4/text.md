Re-Elect Carol Hill-Evans to Continue Her Advocacy for the 95th
Carol Hill-Evans has served as the representative for Pennsylvania's 95th Legislative District since December 2016, building her reputation as a dedicated public servant with a strong focus on community issues and social justice.
Prior to her election to the Pennsylvania House of Representatives, Carol served on the York City Council for eight years, which gave her a deep understanding of local government and the specific needs of her constituents.
One of the key legislative areas Carol has championed is education.
She has worked to secure increased funding for public schools in her district, recognizing the critical role that education plays in the community's long-term success.
Carol also has been an advocate for veterans, sponsoring bills aimed at improving services and support for those who have served in the military.
This includes legislation to provide housing assistance and job training for veterans, addressing both immediate and long-term needs.
Carol has also been a strong proponent of healthcare access and affordability.
She has supported measures to expand Medicaid and improve mental health services, understanding that healthcare is a foundational issue that affects every other aspect of life.
Her efforts have helped to ensure that more residents in her district can access the care they need, leading to a healthier community.
Her service has brought tangible benefits to the 95th, including improved infrastructure, better educational outcomes, and enhanced support for veterans and low-income families.
Carol Hill-Evans' dedication to her constituents is evident in her legislative priorities and her active engagement with the community, making her a vital advocate for the people of the 95th Legislative District.
Re-electing Carol Hill-Evans on Nov. 3 would allow her to continue the progress she has already made in her efforts to improve the lives of everyone who lives and works in the 95th.
