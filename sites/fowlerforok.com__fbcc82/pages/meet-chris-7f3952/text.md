Oklahoma is home.
The Air Force brought me to Tinker Air Force Base in 2010, and Oklahoma has been home for most of the years since.
Kim grew up in Oklahoma City, and much of our family lives here in Southwest Oklahoma City.
This has always been home base for us.
When the Air Force sent us to Okinawa, Japan, from 2018 to 2022, we could have gone anywhere afterward.
We chose to come home to Oklahoma.
We've been married 14 years, and we're raising our two daughters in Moore Public Schools.
Service I retired from the Air Force as a Senior Master Sergeant in 2024 after serving 20 years.
For most of my career, I served as a Career Enlisted Aviator and deployed five times in support of counterterrorism and counterdrug missions.
As I progressed through the enlisted ranks, I learned how to lead under pressure, take care of people, and be accountable for results.
As a Senior Enlisted Leader, my job was to know my people and fight for them.
That's what a State Representative should do.
Post-mission crew in front of an MC-12W in Afghanistan.
A fair shot for every family.
I'm grateful to have built a good life with Kim in South Oklahoma City because taxpayers invested in me.
The GI Bill and tuition assistance paid for my education.
A VA loan helped us buy our first home.
And we never had to worry about the bills when our daughters were born.
When we came home in 2022, I saw how many families don't get that kind of chance.
Childcare had become one of the biggest bills a young family carries.
Our nieces and nephews were leaving Oklahoma to find good jobs.
Older relatives were stretching thin retirements to cover insurance and medical bills.
Taxpayers invested in me.
Now it's my turn to make sure every family here gets a fair shot.
Built by listening.
Over the past year, I've knocked nearly 15,000 doors across District 91, listening to neighbors who feel like state government isn't working for them anymore.
With a bachelor's in Computer Science and a master's in IT Project Management, I know what questions to ask when Big Tech comes to the Capitol, and what the answers mean for our utility bills, our water, and our privacy.
I'll put that knowledge to work protecting our communities and holding these companies accountable.
From underfunded schools to rising insurance bills, people are ready for something better, and I'm ready to deliver it.
