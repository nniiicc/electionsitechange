Housing Crisis
The housing shortage in Boro Park has reached critical levels, forcing many families to live in unsuitable conditions.
Statistics show that our community faces unique challenges:
- Our district has 30% less residential space than comparable neighborhoods
- 41% of our housing consists of 1-2 family homes, limiting density options
- 42.2% of residents spend over half their income on rent
- Our poverty rate stands at 30%, with 60% of our community relying on government assistance
R' Nachman Caller is committed to implementing comprehensive solutions that respect our neighborhood's character while dramatically increasing available housing.
