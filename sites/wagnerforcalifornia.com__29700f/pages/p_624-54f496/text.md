Worse yet, the video appeared to show the organizers telling individuals to use other voter names when signing.
You can watch the story HERE .
In response Orange County Supervisor Don Wagner made the following statement: “It is no wonder voters are losing faith in our elections.
This shocking and disturbing video is just another example of bad actors trying to undermine the process and despite the Secretary of State’s office saying they will investigate this blatant criminal activity I have no faith that will happen.
I expect swift action.
We know where this crime took place and the individuals in the video can be seen clear as day.
Justice delayed is justice denied.
When I’m Secretary of State, I will make sure any and all fraud of our elections will be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law.
This is exactly why we need a voter id law in this state.
We have to protect the integrity of our elections.” ABOUT DON WAGNER As a former State Assembly Member, Irvine Mayor, College Board Trustee, and Chairman of the Orange County Board of Supervisors, Supervisor Don Wagner has a distinguished career of public service and a well-earned reputation as a straight-shooter, fiscal watchdog and champion for working families, small businesses, first responders and veterans of our armed services.
Don and his wife, Megan, are longtime residents of Orange County.
Don received his bachelor’s degree from UCLA and his Juris Doctorate from the University of California, Hastings College of Law.
