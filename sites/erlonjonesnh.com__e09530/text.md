Erlon Jones
A voice for rural New Hampshire — defending our liberties, our way of life, and our seat at the table in Concord.
A Neighbor Who Shows Up
Erlon Jones is a proud resident of Chatham, New Hampshire — a volunteer lieutenant firefighter who answers the call when his community needs him most.
He serves as chairman of the Town of Chatham Trustees of the Trust Funds and is curator of the Fryeburg Fair museum.
A graduate with a master's degree from Granite State College, Erlon brings both stewardship and dedication to everything he takes on.
When he isn't helping others, you'll find him hiking, fishing, and camping across the mountains and valleys that make this corner of the state so special.
Now he's stepping up to preserve that way of life and protect the individual liberties that form the bedrock of New Hampshire values.
The Winning Platform
Common-sense priorities to keep New Hampshire free, affordable, and accountable — with a real voice for the North Country.
No Income Tax.
No Sales Tax.
The citizens of New Hampshire are best equipped to make their own fiscal decisions.
A limited state government with lower taxes fosters that freedom and protects family budgets.
Lower Electricity Costs
New Hampshire ratepayers face rising electricity costs in a cold-weather state.
Our energy policy and legislation should be geared toward reducing this burden and building a realistic energy future.
Clean Up the Sununu Youth Center
The State of New Hampshire has a duty to provide accountability and oversight to this institution.
The repeating cycle of resident mistreatment, assaults on staff members, and general disorder should not be tolerated.
Real leadership will work collaboratively to address this problem and avoid expensive lawsuits that are costing taxpayers millions of dollars.
Education Funding and school choice
Public schools in New Hampshire are one of our greatest assets and should continue to be fully funded and supported.
Parents and students should also be provided with options for non-traditional education options such as charter, private, and religious schools.
A balanced and fair approach to this issue best serves all stakeholders involved.
