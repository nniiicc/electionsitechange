Safest State in the Nation Accomplishment Kelly fixed the broken bail system, strengthened efforts to combat drug trafficking, and is backing our first responders.
Protecting the New Hampshire Advantage Accomplishment Kelly is holding the line against new taxes — no income tax, no sales tax, EVER!
Delivering a Brighter Future Accomplishment Kelly delivered historic education funding, got cell phones out of the classroom, expanded childcare scholarships, and is leading efforts to improve kids’ reading.
Fighting to Lower Electric Bills Accomplishment Kelly is pushing utilities and regulators to lower costs and working to bring more energy to our state.
Making Healthcare Affordable and Accessible Accomplishment Kelly fought to fund mental health and substance use treatment, protected Medicaid, and secured the largest federal award in New England for a new landmark health care program to make care more affordable and accessible across New Hampshire.
Protecting New Hampshire’s Outdoors Accomplishment Kelly is stopping efforts to turn New Hampshire into a dumping ground for out-of-state trash and protecting our state’s forests for timber and recreation.
Tackling the Housing Crisis Accomplishment Kelly signed new laws to speed up construction and make it easier to bring more housing to our state.
