To protect the North Country’s timber and tourism economy, Kelly secured a deal to keep the Connecticut Lakes Headwaters Forest accessible for industry and recreation.
When the United States Forest Service said it would close important research forests in our North Country, Kelly advocated directly to the federal government to ensure they would remain open.
Kelly also stood with local lobstermen on our Seacoast against overregulation, defending a historic New Hampshire industry and pushing the federal government to cut unnecessary red tape.
To safeguard our commercial fishing industry and protect wildlife, she has halted efforts to bring costly, invasive offshore wind projects to our state.
Kelly further protected our land and water by standing with Granite Staters against landfill projects in places like Forest Lake State Park and is fighting for a siting process that puts community input over the interests of big companies, preserving the natural beauty of New Hampshire for future generations.
