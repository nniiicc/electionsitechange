Making Healthcare Affordable and Accessible
Kelly promised to protect our most vulnerable citizens, and she has delivered.
She protected Medicaid and kept eligibility levels among the highest in the country, ensuring families, seniors, and people with disabilities can access the care they need.
She successfully fought for more funding for mental health services, services for Granite Staters with developmental disabilities, and addiction recovery programs so those in need can get the care they deserve.
Kelly also secured the largest federal funding award in New England to expand access to care and lower costs across the state.
By investing in providers and communities, Kelly is helping build a stronger, more accessible health care system for families across New Hampshire.
