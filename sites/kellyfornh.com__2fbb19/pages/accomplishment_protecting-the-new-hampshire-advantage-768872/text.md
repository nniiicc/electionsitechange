Protecting the New Hampshire Advantage
New Hampshire stands apart from our neighbors.
While Massachusetts has embraced higher taxes and bigger government, Kelly is holding the line against an income tax, sales tax, or any tax increase.
For families and small businesses, the New Hampshire Advantage isn’t just a talking point, it shows up in their budgets and everyday decisions.
In her first year, Kelly has focused on fiscal responsibility, making our government more efficient, and supporting important programs without any new taxes.
Preserving the New Hampshire Advantage is essential to maintaining our competitive edge and attracting people who want to live, work, and raise a family here.
Kelly has made clear she will hold the line.
