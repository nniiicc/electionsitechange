Home About Kelly Accomplishments News Join Team Kelly Donate Join Team Kelly Donate August 24, 2026 | Press Release NEW AD: Democrats Have Serious Issues with Opioid Lobbyist Warmington NASHUA, N.H. — Today, the Ayotte campaign released a new digital ad highlighting New Hampshire Democrats’ long-standing issues with Opioid Lobbyist Cinde Warmington’s history of lobbying for Purdue Pharma and calling OxyContin a “miracle drug”.
The ad features Warmington’s 2024 Democratic primary opponents, Joyce Craig and Jon Kiper, and includes a moment from the primary debate in which Craig asked Warmington when she would apologize for her record.
“For years, Democrats across New Hampshire have voiced their issues with Opioid Lobbyist Cinde Warmington’s record of lobbying for Purdue Pharma, calling OxyContin a ‘miracle drug’, and advocating for a notorious pill mill that later bankrolled her campaigns,” said Ayotte campaign spokesman John Corbett.
