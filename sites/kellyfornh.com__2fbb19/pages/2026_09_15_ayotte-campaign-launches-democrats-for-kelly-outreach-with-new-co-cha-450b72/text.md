Home About Kelly Accomplishments News Join Team Kelly Donate Join Team Kelly Donate September 15, 2026 | Press Release Ayotte Campaign Launches Democrats for Kelly Outreach with New Co-Chairs NASHUA, N.H. — Last week, Governor Kelly Ayotte’s announced an across-the-aisle outreach effort with the re-launch of its Democrats for Kelly coalition.
Five prominent New Hampshire Democrats will serve as Co-Chairs and will support the campaign’s efforts to reach across the aisle and emphasize Governor Ayotte’s record of bipartisan leadership on housing, public safety, childcare, and other issues that matter to Granite Staters regardless of political party.
Democrats for Kelly Co-Chairs: Merrimack County Sheriff David Croft – Canterbury Former State Senator Bobby Stephen – Manchester Former Manchester School Board Member Arthur Beaudry – Somersworth Bernie Plante – Nashua Jay Surdukowski – Concord “Whether it’s public safety, housing, mental health, or education, Governor Ayotte has worked across the aisle on the issues that matter most to Granite Staters,” said Merrimack County Sheriff David Croft, Co-Chair of Democrats for Kelly.
