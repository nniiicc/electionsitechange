NASHUA, N.H. — Last week, Governor Kelly Ayotte’s announced an across-the-aisle outreach effort with the re-launch of its Democrats for Kelly coalition.
Five prominent New Hampshire Democrats will serve as Co-Chairs and will support the campaign’s efforts to reach across the aisle and emphasize Governor Ayotte’s record of bipartisan leadership on housing, public safety, childcare, and other issues that matter to Granite Staters regardless of political party.
Democrats for Kelly Co-Chairs:
- Merrimack County Sheriff David Croft – Canterbury
- Former State Senator Bobby Stephen – Manchester
- Former Manchester School Board Member Arthur Beaudry – Somersworth
- Bernie Plante – Nashua
- Jay Surdukowski – Concord
“Whether it’s public safety, housing, mental health, or education, Governor Ayotte has worked across the aisle on the issues that matter most to Granite Staters,” said Merrimack County Sheriff David Croft, Co-Chair of Democrats for Kelly.
“When you have a leader who delivers real results for the people of our state, it doesn’t matter what letter is next to their name, and Governor Ayotte has made it clear that she’s working for all of New Hampshire.”
