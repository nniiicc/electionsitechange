According to their latest campaign finance reports, Gov.
Kelly Ayotte is sitting on a $3 million war chest that’s seven times bigger than that of Democratic nominee-to-be Cinde Warmington of Concord.
And that’s after Ayotte has spent more than $1 million on TV and streaming platforms.
Warmington has spent so far $9,000 on TV.” — Union Leader , 8/21/26 “Warmington has also been snubbed by the sort of high-profile Democrats who would be expected to back her campaigning, including the party’s 2024 nominee for governor, former Manchester Mayor Joyce Craig.
According to Warmington, Craig rejected a direct appeal for an endorsement.
And while Democratic New Jersey Gov.
Mikie Sherrill traveled to New Hampshire to campaign for U.S.
Rep.
Maggie Goodlander and congressional candidate Maura Sullivan this week, Warmington — who would become a fellow member of the Democratic Governors Association if elected — did not make the cut.” — NH Journal , 8/20/26 “Not until November, but still part of the polling, Republican incumbent Kelly Ayotte holds an 11-point lead over Democratic challenger Cinde Warmington in the governor’s race, 49% to 38%.” — WMUR , 8/20/26 “In 2024, Massachusetts Gov. and New Hampshire native Maura Healey was a permanent fixture on the Granite State campaign trail… Healey chairs the Democratic Governors Association’s Women Governors Fund but has yet to weigh in on this year’s race or throw her support behind Warmington.
