Home About Kelly Accomplishments News Join Team Kelly Donate Join Team Kelly Donate September 9, 2026 | Press Release Granite Staters Can’t Trust Opioid Lobbyist Cinde Warmington NASHUA, N.H. — Opioid Lobbyist Cinde Warmington is officially the Democrat nominee for Governor of New Hampshire, and her disqualifying record is on full display for Granite Staters.
“Opioid Lobbyist Cinde Warmington is officially the Democrat nominee for Governor, and her record is on display for all to see: decades of lobbying for Purdue Pharma, advocating for a notorious pill mill, and putting her own self-interest ahead of New Hampshire,” said Ayotte campaign spokesman John Corbett.
“Voters will reject Opioid Lobbyist Warmington this November.” Opioid Lobbyist Cinde Warmington: Disqualified from Ever Serving as Governor As a lobbyist for Purdue Pharma, Cinde Warmington testified before the New Hampshire Senate in 2002 defending the company and advocating for looser restrictions on OxyContin, calling it a “miracle drug” with “very few side effects”.
In the same hearing, state health officials and law enforcement were sounding the alarm about its dangers.
“We believe that Purdue Pharma has manufactured a drug which is highly addictive and it’s in a form… they produced in a form which makes it abuseable; that is a problem that they need to correct, and I hope they do.” — Former DHHS Commissioner Donald Shumway “We have had a number of night break-ins, armed robberies, unarmed robberies, and strong-arm robberies which Oxycontin had been demanded.
The people who are abusing these drugs are willing to risk committing more serious crimes because nothing else matters but getting those drugs. ” — Nancy Wiggins, New Hampshire State Police Warmington’s excuse for her lies on behalf of Purdue Pharma is that she was duped into working for them.
Advocated for a Notorious Pill Mill For more than a decade, Warmington served as a lawyer for Dr.
Michael O’Connell and his PainCare network , a chain of New Hampshire pain clinics whose practices were found to have worsened the opioid crisis in the Granite State, including a provider taking illegal kickbacks for prescribing opioids.
Warmington repeatedly fought on PainCare’s behalf: she represented O’Connell after he surrendered his medical license, helped negotiate a settlement for a doctor tied to the kickback scandal, lobbied against legislation that would prevent doctors with revoked licenses from owning a medical practice, and even met with NHPR to defend PainCare’s public image .
After Warmington defended O’Connell and his pill mills for years, O’Connell fueled her political campaigns with nearly $50,000.00 in donations .
