In an interview on CloseUp with WMUR’s Adam Sexton, Warmington was asked whether she would apologize to Granite Staters and refused, dismissing her record of putting special interests over New Hampshire families.
“Opioid Lobbyist Cinde Warmington refuses to apologize and take responsibility for misleading the public by claiming that OxyContin was a ‘miracle drug’,” said Ayotte campaign spokesman John Corbett.
“Not to mention her many years spent defending one of New England’s most notorious pill mills, which was also one of the biggest donors to her political campaigns.
