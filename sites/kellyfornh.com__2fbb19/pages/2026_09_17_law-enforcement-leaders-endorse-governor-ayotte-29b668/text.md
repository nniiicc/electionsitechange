Home About Kelly Accomplishments News Join Team Kelly Donate Join Team Kelly Donate September 17, 2026 | Press Release Law Enforcement Leaders Endorse Governor Ayotte NASHUA, N.H. — Today, Governor Kelly Ayotte accepted endorsements from the following law enforcement associations at a joint endorsement event in Manchester: New Hampshire Troopers Association New Hampshire Police Association Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office Employees Union Manchester Association of Police Supervisors Manchester Police Patrolman’s Association New Hampshire Fraternal Order of Police “New Hampshire is the safest state in the nation thanks to the men and women in law enforcement who protect and serve their communities each day, and I’m honored to have endorsements from law enforcement associations across our state,” said Governor Ayotte.
“We fixed the broken bail system, restored the retirement benefits our first responders were promised, increased penalties for refusing DUI testing, and more, and I look forward to continuing that important work together.” “The New Hampshire Troopers Association is proud to endorse Governor Kelly Ayotte for re-election,” said Stefan Czyzowski, President of the New Hampshire Troopers Association.
“From day one, Governor Ayotte has ensured that New Hampshire Troopers are the best equipped and the best trained Troopers in the Country.
Her unwavering support for the men and women of the New Hampshire State Police guarantees that the citizens of the Granite State receive the highest degree of law enforcement service possible.
We need Governor Ayotte to stay in the corner office to keep New Hampshire the safest state in the nation.” “As the President of the New Hampshire Police Association, it is my honor to endorse Governor Kelly Ayotte for re-election so we can keep our state #1 for public safety,” said Mike Geha, President of the New Hampshire Police Association.
“We were proud to work alongside the Governor to advance key public safety priorities, and we know she will continue to ensure our law enforcement officers have the resources and backing we need to do our jobs.” “Governor Ayotte has consistently demonstrated her commitment to supporting the men and women of law enforcement and recognizing the critical role we play in protecting the citizens and communities of New Hampshire,” said Roman Kelly, President of the Hillsborough County Sheriff’s Office Employee Union.
“Her advocacy for law enforcement and public safety reflects an understanding of the challenges facing today’s law enforcement professionals and the importance of providing officers with the resources, support, and policies necessary to effectively perform their duties.
We are proud to stand with Governor Kelly Ayotte and offer our full endorsement.” “Governor Ayotte has worked with members of law enforcement throughout her career, and Manchester officers have seen that relationship firsthand,” said Rob Megowen, President of the Manchester Association of Police Supervisors.
“From her time as Attorney General and United States Senator to her first term as Governor, she has consistently engaged on issues that directly affect the men and women responsible for keeping our communities safe.
Her work on bail reform and her commitment to addressing the Group II retirement system are especially important at a time when police departments across New Hampshire are working hard to recruit and retain experienced officers.” “Governor Kelly Ayotte was with our department through some of its worst days, and throughout her career, she has never wavered in her support for the Manchester Police Department and law enforcement across New Hampshire,” said Neil Pentinnen, President of the Manchester Police Patrolman’s Association.
“From fixing the broken bail system, to restoring Group II retirement benefits, to promoting road safety, Governor Ayotte has been a champion for public safety in the corner office, and the Manchester Police Patrolmen’s Association is proud to endorse her for re-election today.” “We are proud to endorse Governor Kelly Ayotte for re-election,” said Garrett Lemoine, President of the New Hampshire Fraternal Order of Police.
“From prioritizing officer wellness and ensuring our officers have the support they need to do an incredibly difficult job, to strengthening public safety and standing up for the retirement benefits our first responders have earned, she has consistently had the backs of law enforcement.
We know we can count on Governor Ayotte to continue standing with the men and women who put their lives on the line every day to keep our state the safest in the nation.” How Governor Ayotte is Keeping New Hampshire the Safest State in the Nation: Successfully passed a bipartisan law to fix New Hampshire’s broken bail system and shut the revolving door for violent criminals.
Signed laws to protect victims of domestic violence and human trafficking.
Strengthened drug enforcement efforts and closed the DUI test refusal loophole.
Restored retirement benefits for first responders.
Supported a cancer screening program for firefighters.
Earlier this month, the Ayotte campaign launched a more than 150-member “First Responders for Kelly” coalition, further highlighting the Governor’s commitment to keeping New Hampshire the safest state in the nation.
The Governor has also received endorsements from the New England Police Benevolent Association (NEPBA) and the Professional Fire Fighters of New Hampshire (PFFNH).
