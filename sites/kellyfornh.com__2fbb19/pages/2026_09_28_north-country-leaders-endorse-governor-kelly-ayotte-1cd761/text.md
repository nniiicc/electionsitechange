NASHUA, N.H. — Last week, the Ayotte campaign announced the launch of a North Country for Kelly coalition.
The coalition brings together elected officials, business owners, and community leaders from across the North Country in support of Governor Ayotte’s re-election campaign.
“Too often, the North Country gets overlooked, but Governor Ayotte has made sure our communities have a voice in Concord,” said State Representative Arnie Davis, North Country for Kelly Chair.
“From protecting the land that makes this region so special to delivering record funding for rural healthcare, she’s delivered on the issues that matter most.
Governor Ayotte has proven, time and again, that the North Country has a true champion in the corner office.”

How Governor Ayotte has delivered for the North Country:
- Delivered the largest rural health transformation grant in New England
- Improved site evaluation process on new landfills that prioritizes local input
- Secured an agreement for the Connecticut Lakes Headwaters that preserves recreation, tourism, forestry, and conservation interests
- Fought against landfill projects near Forest Lake State Park in Dalton
- Invested in security efforts along our Northern Border and supported North Country law enforcement
- Successfully advocated to keep North Country research forests open
- Supported the North Country’s tourism economy by opening the Connecticut Lakes Headwaters Working Forest OHRV trails for the full season
