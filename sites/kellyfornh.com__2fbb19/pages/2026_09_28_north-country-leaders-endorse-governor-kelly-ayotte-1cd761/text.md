Home About Kelly Accomplishments News Join Team Kelly Donate Join Team Kelly Donate September 28, 2026 | Press Release North Country Leaders Endorse Governor Kelly Ayotte NASHUA, N.H. — Last week, the Ayotte campaign announced the launch of a North Country for Kelly coalition.
The coalition brings together elected officials, business owners, and community leaders from across the North Country in support of Governor Ayotte’s re-election campaign.
“Too often, the North Country gets overlooked, but Governor Ayotte has made sure our communities have a voice in Concord,” said State Representative Arnie Davis, North Country for Kelly Chair.
“From protecting the land that makes this region so special to delivering record funding for rural healthcare, she’s delivered on the issues that matter most.
Governor Ayotte has proven, time and again, that the North Country has a true champion in the corner office.” North Country for Kelly Coalition: State Representative Arnie Davis, Chairman State Senator David Rochefort State Representative Mike Ouellet State Representative Marc Tremblay State Representative Lori Korzen State Representative Peter Morency State Representative James Tierney County Commissioner Ray Gorman County Commissioner Robert Theberge Coös County Sheriff Keith Roberge Berlin Mayor Robert Cone Berlin City Councilor Steve Korzen Former Coös County Sheriff Gerry Marcou Hon.
