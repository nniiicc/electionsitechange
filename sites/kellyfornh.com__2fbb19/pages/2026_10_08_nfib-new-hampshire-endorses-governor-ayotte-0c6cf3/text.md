Home About Kelly Accomplishments News Join Team Kelly Donate Join Team Kelly Donate October 8, 2026 | Press Release NFIB New Hampshire Endorses Governor Ayotte NASHUA, N.H. — Today, the National Federation of Independent Business NH PAC (NFIB NH PAC), New Hampshire’s leading small business political action committee, endorsed Governor Kelly Ayotte for re-election during a visit to Landscape Plus, a family-owned and operated business in Goffstown.
“New Hampshire small businesses are proud to support Governor Kelly Ayotte for re-election this year,” said NFIB New Hampshire State Director John Reynolds.
“Governor Ayotte shares Main Street’s priorities: small business tax relief, growing the workforce, better healthcare, and lower energy costs.
We can count on her to make the Granite State an even better place to own, operate, and grow a small business.” “Having helped my husband start a small landscaping and snow removal business, I’ve seen firsthand the challenges our small businesses face to create jobs and make ends meet,” said Governor Ayotte.
“I’m proud of the work we’ve done to ensure they can continue to grow — from passing new laws to tackle the housing crisis, to supporting workforce development programs, to fighting to lower energy costs, and more.
But we have more work to do to make sure New Hampshire remains a great place to do business.
I’m proud to have NFIB New Hampshire’s endorsement, and I look forward to working with our small business community to keep our state a beacon of economic opportunity.” How Governor Ayotte is delivering for small businesses: Passed 12 new bipartisan laws to make it faster and easier to build housing Supported workforce development and career and technical education programs at New Hampshire’s community colleges Delivered tax relief for small businesses Opposing harmful Canadian tariffs Fighting to lower energy costs ### Donate to Keep New Hampshire Safe, Prosperous, and Free $5 $10 $25 $50 $100 $250 Want to donate another amount?
