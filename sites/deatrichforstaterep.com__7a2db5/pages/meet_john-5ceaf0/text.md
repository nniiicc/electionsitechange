Meet John Issues News Volunteer Contribute Meet John Born in Pottsville, I spent my early years growing up on the East Side of Pottsville.
I grew up in our family business; Deatrich and Son Auto.
My father started his own business when I was 12 years old, my Mother worked full time and put herself through Nursing school.
I witnessed first hand, early on, what hard work looked like and learned quickly that to get ahead, you have to work hard.
No one modeled that better than my parents.
In 1989, the business grew, we moved to a new building in Palo Alto, and took up residence there as well.
I became a career and technology teacher at Schuylkill Technology Center in 2002.
Then a new position at Berks Career and Technology Center in 2007.
Around that same time I was elected to council in Palo Alto, served as Finance Chairman, then council president.
The last 6 years I’ve been in the position of Mayor of Palo Alto.
Having been in many positions over the last couple decades has afforded me a plethora of experience in public service.
In my personal life, I have enjoyed racing cars at various dirt tracks throughout Pennsylvania, I’ve raced quads in D6, and continue to be involved in racing either myself, helping my son, or friends.
