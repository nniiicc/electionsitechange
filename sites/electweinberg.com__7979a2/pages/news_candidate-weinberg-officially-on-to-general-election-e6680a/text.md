Candidate Jacob Weinberg Officially on to General Election
Chandler, Arizona - After Candidate Jacob Weinberg completed his 2 weeks of National Guard training in Northern Arizona, he came back to the great news that he was officially nominated to represent the Democratic Party in the general election for the Legistlative District 13 State House election in November.
When asked how he celebrated, Weinberg said "How did I celebrate?
By knocking on doors in 111 degree weather to meet more voters in the district.
That's where I'll be the rest of the summer because our work has just begun." Weinberg said he wanted to thank all his supports who helped make everything happen and he appreciated all their efforts to keep the momentum going to November.
