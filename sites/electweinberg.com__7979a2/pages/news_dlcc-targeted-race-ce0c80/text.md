Among them is Jacob Weinberg, a veteran and candidate for the state house in Arizona's 13th legislative district.
"I am proud of this recognition.
LD13 is key to flipping control of the legislature, and the backing from the DLCC reflects the strength of our campaign,” said Jacob Weinberg.
“We’re going to keep fighting to win this seat by appealing to voters across the political spectrum." Democrats require gaining 4 seats in the Arizona House to flip control.
Weinberg's race is a critical piece of the equation, as the margins in LD13 make it a chamber-deciding district.
ABOUT JACOB WEINBERG: Jacob Weinberg is a 20-year U.S.
Army combat veteran and business leader running to flip a seat in the Arizona House this fall.
After his military service, Jacob continued leading teams and supporting veterans in his community.
Jacob loves calling Arizona home and is excited to give back to his community by working to build lasting economic opportunities and improve access to quality education for all Arizonans.
This year, Jacob, with the support of the DLCC’s Target Races program, is in a prime position to flip a vulnerable Republican district and help Democrats gain the four seats needed to take majority control of the Arizona House.
