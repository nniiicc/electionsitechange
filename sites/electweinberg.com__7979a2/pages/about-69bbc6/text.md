Chicago Roots Out to the Desert Born and raised in a small suburb of Chicago, IL.
I am the son of a teacher and a building manager, who taught me the value of hard work, honesty, and integrity.
I came to Arizona to attend the University of Arizona, where I earned my Bachelor's degree in Economics and was commissioned into the U.S.
Army after the September 11th terrorist attacks.
As a reconnaissance platoon leader and staff officer in the 82nd Airborne Division, I earned my Parachutist Badge, Ranger Tab, Combat Action Badge, and Expert Infantryman Badge, in addition to combat deployments to Iraq with Joint Special Operations Command.
Upon leaving active duty I returned to Illinois, where I earned master’s degrees from the University of Illinois Chicago and University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, became a CPA, served as a Cavalry Troop Commander in the Illinois Army National Guard, and built a successful career in accounting.
But after years living in Illinois, Texas, and Florida, I knew Arizona was calling me home.
Planting Roots in Arizona Arizona had always felt like home, so I returned to build my life and career here.
At Deloitte, I rose to senior manager, help growing a new office to more than 400 professionals and leading portfolios in financial services and gaming that contributed over $500 million in revenue — all while earning my Master’s in International Relations from Harvard University.
At the same time, I continued serving in the Arizona Army National Guard, where I currently serve at the rank of Lieutenant Colonel.
I currently am the CFO of a Software Development and Technology Solutions company based out of the East Valley.
Looking Towards the Future Since returning to Arizona, I have focused on building strong roots in the community through civic engagement and service.
I was honored to be named 2024 Member of the Year by the Phoenix Committee on Foreign Relations, serve as an international visitor host for Global Ties Arizona, and serve on the executive committee of Arizona’s best rugby club, who are back-to-back union champions and the 2025 national runners-up.
Personally, I’m always looking to broaden my horizons by seeking out new adventures, embracing new challenges, and pushing myself beyond my limits.
That mindset has taken me to the summit of Mount Kilimanjaro, to the USA Triathlon National Championships, and into conversations with leaders at the local, state, national, and international levels — as well as astrophysicists, neuroscientists, AI researchers, and community leaders all working to make the world better.
