If you have not met Bonnie, this website is an introduction.
Bonnie was raised by working class parents who pulled themselves up from poverty through sacrifice, hard work, and emphasizing education.
Bonnie's mother was a phone operator and union steward.
Her father was a student at UC-Berkeley completing his Bachelors, Masters and Ph.D.
In the summertime, her father was a park ranger, which meant the entire family was backpacking, fishing and exploring.
After her father became a university professor, her parents moved and eventually settled in Texas.
Bonnie graduated with a B.B.A. from Texas A&M University and a J.D. from South Texas College of Law.
After law school, Bonnie got married, started a family, and began her career as an Assistant District Attorney.
A few years later, Bonnie and her husband moved their family to Central Florida and made it their permanent home.
Bonnie and her husband raised all 3 of their children in Central Florida while volunteering at their children's schools, in church, and in their community.
Just like her parents, Bonnie sacrificed, worked hard, and emphasized education.
Bonnie is the only candidate who can claim Florida House District 42 as her home for nearly three decades.
She knows this community.
We need to get back to basics with a respected voice working for our community in Tallahassee.
A Respected Voice Attorney A successful attorney since 1995, Bonnie began her career as a prosecutor and then moved to private practice.
She has handled complex business and real estate litigation matters, and has been a strong advocate for consumers and victims of fraud and domestic violence.
She has worked for both national and boutique law firms before founding The Jackson Law Firm, P.A. d/b/a Jackson Law International with her husband, Michael.
Today, their international law practice has offices in Florida, California, and Germany.
Leadership Bonnie is a principled community leader, local business owner, and longtime Orange County resident.
For nearly 30 years, Bonnie has called our community home—raising a family, building a successful business, and watching the area grow.
Bonnie will bring her leadership, legal expertise, and business experience to Tallahassee.
With a focus on transparency, Bonnie is committed to ensuring that taxpayers are protected and Orange County families have a voice and effective advocate in our State Capitol.
Service Faith & Family Bonnie’s extensive record of service includes her appointments to public advisory boards for the City of Winter Park, including the Parks & Recreation Board and the Tree Preservation Board.
She also served on the Board of Directors for UCP Child Development Centers.
Bonnie was also recognized by the Legal Aid Society of Orange County for her dedication to volunteer service as a Guardian Ad Litem for abused and neglected children, and she earned the Florida Bar’s Meritorious Public Service Award for her volunteer work on the Bar’s Grievance Committee.
Faith & Family Faith & Family Bonnie and Michael live in Winter Park, Florida where they raised their three children.
Over the years, their children attended Dommerich Elementary, Maitland Middle, Winter Park High School, St.
Margaret Mary, and Bishop Moore Catholic High School.
Their family continues to grow as they welcomed a wonderful son-in-law last year and a daughter-in-law this year.
Bonnie attends St.
Margaret Mary Catholic Church and has served as a Sunday preschool teacher and in various ministries.
She is fluent in Spanish and remains committed to giving back to her community.
