Veteran Issues
My father served during World War II in 1945 in the Army Air Corp as a waistgunner flying in the B-17 bomber.
He survived 25 missions over Europe.
Older brother served in the Air Force during the Vietnam War as a crew chief while flying in C-130.
Currently have a great nephew serving in the Air Force with numerous duties during the Iran War.
I recently talked with a veteran that told me that he was a republican until the Trump administration made their employee cuts to the Veterans Administration.
He has had huge delays trying to get into the new Veterans Administration Hospital in North Las Vegas due to the staffing cuts.
Our veterans deserve more that promises-they deserve quality, accessible healthcare and unwaveing support.
Jan will work to expand services and ensure every veteran gets the care they have earned.
Health Care
Being a cancer survivor, I know the importance of health care.
When I first moved to Nevada I was told by numerous people if you get a serious health concern leave Nevada and seek it else where.
Well I did find out that was the case and had to go back to Minnesota for my cancer treatments.
The clinic in Nevada was so back logged and couldn't see me for months.
So I left home for 3 months to get my successful treatments.
If elected my main job is to get more qualified doctors and nurses in Nevada so no one like me has to go out of state.
