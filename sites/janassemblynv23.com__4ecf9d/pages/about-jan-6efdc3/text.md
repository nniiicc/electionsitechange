Jan Allen Aspelund is running to represent the people of Assembly District 23!
Born and raised in a small town in North Dakota (Enderlin) where he lived with his parents, 3 brothers and 1 sister.
Married in 1983 to Denise and living with Norman, Nikki and Auggie (dogs).
Moved to Henderson in 2004 after being transferred by his company that he worked for in Minnesota for 21 years.
His job description was manager of a water treatment company.
His duties where to supply proper chemicals that would conserve water usage due to the Southern Nevada water situation.
Retired in 2023, but still consulting on water conservation of large HVAC systems.
Today he is wanting to help the great state of Nevada and its people live in an environment which is safe, keeping taxes low and ensuring the future of our water resources.
Jan has a Bachelor of Science degree from Minnesota State University Moorhead and an Associate degree of science from North Dakota State School of Science.
Also has OSHA 30 certification and Legionella certification from University of Nevada Reno School of Medicine.
Golf chair from the (NPFMA) Nevada Professional Facility Managers for the annual charity golf tournament.
Charities include: Nevada Childhood Cancer, Opportunity Village, Lutheran Social Services of Nevada, Assitance League of Las Vegas and Shriners Childrens Hospital Golf Tournament.
Hobbies include golf, fishing, biking and walking.
Avid sports fan of the Las Vegas Raiders and Vegas Golden Knights!
