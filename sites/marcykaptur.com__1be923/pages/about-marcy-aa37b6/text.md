Growing up in a UAW household, she saw firsthand what happens when Washington puts powerful corporations ahead of working families — factories close, jobs disappear, and communities like ours pay the price.
That’s why Marcy got into public service: to take on the powerful interests hollowing out our communities, give working families a real voice in Washington, and bring home the jobs and investment Northwest Ohio deserves.
Marcy represents America’s “Big Middle” — not the extremes, not the ultra-wealthy, and not the Washington insiders.
She doesn’t care which party has the idea: if it helps Northwest Ohio, she’s for it; if it hurts Northwest Ohio, she’s against it.
She’s a true friend of veterans, seniors, first responders, and working people — a voice for all our communities in the People’s House.
