Data centers and your power bill
When the Decaturish Q&A for the May primary asked all three District 41 candidates how the state should regulate data centers, a multi-billion-dollar issue driving up Georgia Power bills for residential customers, Sen.
Jackson's full answer was two letters, "NA".
In the general election Q&A published September 29, she called for a statewide moratorium on new data centers.
Youth gender medicine: out of step with the medical consensus
Two no votes and one Did Not Vote on bills limiting gender-transition treatment for Georgia minors.
She also dedicated multiple episodes of her own podcast to opposing it, on God, Goats, and Government Episode 39 she asked: "What is the value of a trans youth's life?"
Out of step with the medical consensus shift.
When the AMA and the American Society of Plastic Surgeons reversed their support for pediatric gender medicine, citing weak evidence and irreversible harm, Sen.
Jackson voted with the old position anyway.
Georgia parents deserve a senator who follows the evolving medical consensus, not the political one.
