SB 34, SB 408, SB 421 (2026) Sen.
Jackson: Co-Author SB 34 SB 408 SB 421 Three data center bills she co-authored.
SB 34, as filed in 2025, would have barred utilities from putting the cost of serving data centers into anyone's rates unless the data centers paid all, or at least a substantial share, of that cost.
SB 408 would end the data center equipment sales tax break sooner.
SB 421, the Data Center Transparency Act, would bar local governments from signing nondisclosure agreements about anyone's electricity or water use.
None of the three reached a Senate floor vote.
On February 25, 2026, a Senate committee replaced SB 34 with a substitute that dropped the limit on passing data center costs to other customers.
In its place, the substitute required contracts with customers of 100 megawatts or more to include terms meant to protect other customers from those costs.
