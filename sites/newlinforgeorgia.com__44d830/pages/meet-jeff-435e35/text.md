He has lived in the Atlanta area for the past 36 years and 15 years at his current home in DeKalb County.
After serving five years as the Chief Revenue Officer for a software and technology company focused on the heavy building materials industry, Jeff is now the Executive Director for the Georgia Ready Mixed Concrete Association.
Jeff is a long-time member of both the Atlanta Track Club and Atlanta Triathlon Club.
He is married to Christine and they have three adult children and welcomed their first granddaughter in April.
The Newlins are parishioners at Immaculate Heart of Mary Catholic Church where Jeff is a ministry leader.
