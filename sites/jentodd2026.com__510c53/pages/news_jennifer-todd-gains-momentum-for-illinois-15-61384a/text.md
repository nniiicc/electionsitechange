Mary Miller for congressional seat in 2026 news article Local Democrat to challenge Mary Miller News article Hundreds protest Trump policies, Miller in Mahomet PRESS RELEASE Jennifer Todd Launches Listening Tour Across Illinois's 15th Congressional District Oct 15 2026 PRESS RELEASE Jennifer Todd Gains Momentum for Illinois 15 Jennifer Todd, Democratic Candidate for Congress in Illinois-15, has outraised any Democratic candidate running who has run against the Republican incumbent Mary Miller.
The end-of-quarter FEC report total is $192,472.81, with contributions from the 3rd quarter totaling $81,432.27.
The campaign has continued raising money since then, and the current total raised as of the morning of October 15th is $203,544 Todd said, “While we are not raising Texas-sized money, we have raised more than any other candidate has against Mary Miller.
More importantly, I am hearing from Democratic, Independent, and Republican voters across the district that they are so happy to be heard.
As a result, they are turning out at events across the district and turning up to support our campaign.” In addition to donor support, Todd is racking up endorsements, including support from her Primary Partners (the candidates she defeated in the primary election): Paul Davis, Randy Raley, and Kyle Nudo.
To date, Todd has been endorsed by: The Justice Coalition Illinois Federation of Teachers (IFT) National Women's Political Caucus (NWPC) Coles County Democratic Central Committee Logan County Democratic Central Committee Citizen Action Liuna Downstate Illinois Laborers' District Council National NOW PAC Illinois AFL-CIO Illinois NOW PAC Illinois Nurses Association Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers & Trainmen Illinois State Legislative Board Warren County Democratic Central Committee She has been recommended by AFSCME.
Todd, a second-generation nurse, mom, daughter of a veteran, raised in a union household, won the Democratic nomination for Congress in the 15th Congressional District.
Her former Democratic primary opponents have subsequently joined together in support of her campaign.
