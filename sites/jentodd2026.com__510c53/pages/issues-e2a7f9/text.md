Affordability
Across central Illinois, families are working hard, but too many are still struggling to keep up with the cost of living.
I know this reality firsthand; I have lived the struggle too, and I understand what it means when wages do not stretch far enough.
From groceries and gas to housing and childcare, everyday expenses are rising faster than wages.
People should not have to choose between paying their bills and planning for their future.
I will fight to lower costs for working families, taking on price gouging, bringing down prescription drug prices, and making housing more affordable.
I will support policies that grow good-paying jobs, strengthen local economies, and put more money back in people’s pockets.
I will also work to ease the burden of childcare and everyday expenses so families can get ahead, not just get by.
I believe that if you work hard, you should be able to afford your life, and I am committed to making that a reality for families across the district.
