At a time when division too often defines politics, this moment stands apart.
It sends a clear and powerful message.
This race is bigger than any one campaign.
It is about people, purpose, and the future we choose together.
Randy Raley called on supporters to join him in backing Todd’s leadership, saying, “Jennifer Todd has my full support and my wholehearted endorsement.
She’s ready to lead, ready to do the work, and ready to represent this district the way it should be represented.
I trust her, and I believe in her ability to carry this work forward.” Kyle Nudo framed the election as a defining choice, adding, “This has always been about compassion and courage.
That’s why I stand with Jennifer Todd.
If we want real change, we must come together and rally behind her.” Paul Davis emphasized the shared experiences that shaped his confidence in Todd, stating, “We’ve listened to families across this district who are struggling with rising costs, healthcare challenges, and failing infrastructure.
Jennifer understands these challenges, and she’s ready to fight for real solutions.” Together, these endorsements reflect more than support.
They reflect momentum.
A coalition built on trust, shared experience, and a belief that the people of this district deserve better.
Jennifer Todd expressed deep gratitude and a renewed sense of purpose.
“I’m incredibly honored to have the support of Randy, Kyle, and Paul,” Todd said.
“They know firsthand what this journey demands, and their immediate unity shows what’s possible when we put people over politics.” “This is about more than an election,” she continued.
“It’s about building a future where working families can thrive, where healthcare is accessible, where our infrastructure supports growth, and where every person feels seen, heard, and represented.” With a unified team and a growing grassroots movement, the campaign now turns toward the general election with energy and determination.
“Together,” Todd said, “we are stronger.
