jimmyforhouse.com Accessibility Statement
Last Updated: January 08, 2024.
General
jimmyforhouse.com strives to ensure that its services are accessible to people with disabilities and has invested a significant amount of resources to help ensure that its website is made easier to use and more accessible for people with disabilities.
We maintain a strong belief that every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort and independence.
Disclaimer
jimmyforhouse.com continues its efforts to constantly improve the accessibility of its site and services in the belief that it is our collective obligation to allow seamless, accessible and unhindered use also for those of us with disabilities.
Despite our efforts to make all pages and content on jimmyforhouse.com fully accessible, some content may not have yet been fully adapted to the strictest accessibility standards.
This may be a result of not having found or identified the most appropriate technological solution.
Here For You
If you are experiencing difficulty with any content on jimmyforhouse.com or require assistance with any part of our site, please contact us during normal business hours and will be happy to assist.
Contact Us
If you wish to report an accessibility issue, have any questions or need assistance, please contact jimmyforhouse.com Customer Support on our Contact Page.
