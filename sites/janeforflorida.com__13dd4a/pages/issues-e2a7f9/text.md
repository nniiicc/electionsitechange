She knows firsthand that today’s economy is not working for Floridians.
No one should have to work two or three jobs to keep a roof over their heads.
Jane will fight to ensure all of our Seminole and Orange County neighbors can be financially secure.
Hold the insurance industry accountable to Florida taxpayers - by capping insurance rate hikes and making sure hardworking Floridians are paid what they are owed after a natural disaster.
Expand policies available under Citizens Property Insurance Corporation.
Invest in local small businesses, further developing the region’s economy and creating job opportunities.
Education As a high school teacher and now, as a professor at the University of Central Florida, Jane knows the importance of our public schools.
She will always advocate for classrooms that work for all of District 37’s students.
Treat professional educators as professionals - respecting their classroom knowledge and expertise and paying them what they deserve.
Fully fund our public schools with an emphasis on reducing class sizes - leading to more individualized learning and more positive outcomes.
Implement stronger accountability and transparency standards for charter and private schools that accept state funds.
Healthcare Floridia families are facing too many barriers to high-quality healthcare.
Locally and at the state-level, Jane will work to expand access to qualified, competent care that meets each individual set of needs.
Ensure equitable access to high-quality healthcare by expanding Medicaid in Florida.
Restore access to comprehensive reproductive healthcare by voting against any further restrictions on abortion care and working to rollback earlier bans passed by an extremist legislature.
Support initiatives that ensure appropriate staffing levels in care facilities for the safety of patients and caregivers.
Protect the Rural Boundary Jane understands it is critical that we protect Seminole and Orange County's voter-approved Rural Boundary, which limits overdevelopment, protects our natural resources, and preserves agricultural lands.
Jane will fight back against attempts to dissolve Rural Boundary protections and preempt local control.
Close legislative loopholes created by 2025's SB 180, which allow developers to sue over rural boundary protections.
Respect the will of the people by preserving our local freedom to protect our Rural Boundary.
Support legislation that further protects Florida's sensitive conservation lands.
