About Kim 31+ Years of Service Kim Townsend has dedicated her life to the people of Anderson and Madison County .
With over three decades of public service, she has built a reputation as a tireless advocate, a proven leader, and someone who gets things done.
As CEO of the Anderson Housing Authority since 2018, Kim inherited a troubled agency with a failing HUD designation — and turned it around completely.
Under her leadership, the agency secured over four million dollars in grants and restored its standing as a model for the region.
"I've spent 31 years showing up for this community.
Now it's time to take our story — and our needs — to the Statehouse." A breast cancer survivor , Kim understands firsthand the importance of accessible healthcare, strong community support systems, and the resilience it takes to fight for what matters.
She brings that same fight to everything she does.
