This is a remarkable achievement, given that only 10 to 15 percent of all bills introduced in the Legislature actually become law.
With his 49% average, Roger ranks as one of the most effective and prolific legislators in the United States .
Roger has led the Legislature to enact significant, landmark reforms, including rewriting the state’s domestic violence laws and DUI laws, establishing a statewide entitlement to high-quality pre-school, and strengthening the law relating to police use of deadly force, among many other measures.
