DONATE Campaign Issues “Fight for the things that you care about.
But do it in a way that will lead others to join you.” ― Ruth Bader Ginsburg Unity, Healing & Bridging Divides Montana is stronger when we focus on what connects us, not what divides us.
I will work to build bridges across political and cultural lines to find real, shared solutions.
This includes meaningful healing with Indigenous communities—honoring history, listening deeply, and walking forward together.
Transparency & Public Trust Public service means accountability, honesty, and integrity.
I will demand transparency from institutions and fight for policies that keep power with the people not special interests.
Montanans deserve a government that works for them, not behind closed doors.
Protecting Public Lands & Sustaining Our Natural Resources ​​ Montana’s lands, rivers, and skies are part of who we are.
I will fight to protect clean air, safe water, and open public lands for future generations.
Stewardship isn’t optional—it’s our responsibility. ​ Cost of Living & Economic Security Too many Montanans are being priced out of the communities they’ve called home for generations.
I will support policies that keep housing affordable, protect working families, and ensure fair wages.
No one should have to choose between making ends meet and putting down roots in Montana.
LGBTQ+ Rights, Reproductive Freedom & Bodily Autonomy Every person deserves to live openly, safely, and with dignity.
I will fight to protect and strengthen LGBTQ+ rights and civil liberties.
I will stand firmly for a woman’s right to make her own healthcare decisions—including the right to choose—without government interference.
We must create communities where every person’s identity, body, and choices are respected—not policed.
Accountability & Human Dignity in Immigration Policy No government agency should be weaponized against vulnerable people or those who stand with them.
I will advocate for humane, just immigration practices that reflect our values.
Every person—citizen, advocate, or asylum seeker—deserves dignity and due process.
