Our Story Keith Allen and his wife, Morgan, live in the rural Kenyon area and are the proud parents of two wonderful children.
A lifelong advocate for agriculture, small business, and rural communities, Keith has spent more than two decades working in farming, agricultural business, and public service.
Cleaning Up Government The first duty of government is to be worthy of the people’s trust.
When taxpayer dollars are wasted, when fraud goes unchecked, and when government agencies refuse accountability, that trust is broken.
That’s why I will continue fighting for stronger oversight of state government, including the creation of an Independent Office of the Inspector General.
Minnesotans deserve to know where their money goes and whether it is being spent wisely.
Government works best when it remembers who it works for.
Making Minnesota Affordable Again Families across Minnesota are tightening their belts.
Government should do the same.
Property taxes continue to climb.
Regulations continue to grow.
Mandates handed down from Saint Paul make life more expensive for homeowners, businesses, schools, and local governments.
We need to reduce those burdens.
We need tax relief for working families and seniors living on fixed incomes.
We need policies that encourage people to stay in Minnesota, raise a family here, start a business here, and retire here.
A strong economy begins with affordable living.
Common-Sense Budgeting Every family knows you cannot spend money you do not have.
State government should learn the same lesson.
We must focus our resources on what matters most: public safety, roads and bridges, quality schools, and essential services.
Wasteful spending may make headlines today, but responsible budgeting creates opportunities for generations.
Our children deserve opportunity, not debt.
Standing with Working People Every family knows you cannot spend money you do not have.
State government should learn the same lesson.
We must focus our resources on what matters most: public safety, roads and bridges, quality schools, and essential services.
Wasteful spending may make headlines today, but responsible budgeting creates opportunities for generations.

Supporting Parents and Communities Parents know their children better than any government agency ever will.
Strong schools require strong partnerships between parents, teachers, and local communities.Families deserve a voice in their children’s education and confidence that local decisions will remain in local hands whenever possible.
Backing Public Safety A community cannot prosper if its people do not feel safe.
We must continue supporting the men and women who put on a badge every day and stand between order and chaos.
They deserve our respect, our support, and a seat at the table when decisions affecting public safety are made.
Safe communities are the foundation upon which everything else is built.
The Work Ahead The challenges facing Minnesota are real, but so is the strength of our people.
I believe in the common sense of the people I represent.
I believe in the character of our communities.
And I believe that when government listens to the people instead of talking down to them, there is no problem we cannot solve.
My mission remains simple: fight fraud, protect taxpayers, strengthen our communities, and leave Minnesota better than we found it.
That is the work before us.
And with your help, it is work we will accomplish together.
