Delegate Gabriel Moreno is a former nonprofit CEO and an unwavering fighter for immigrant children and their families.
He began by helping unaccompanied children in the immigration system.
In 2018, when federal leaders began separating immigrant families at the border, Delegate Moreno was on the ground in Texas detention centers and children's camps, working to reunify families that the federal government had torn apart.
Delegate Moreno has served on state and county commissions including the Maryland Attorney General's Commission on Hate Crime Response and Prevention, the Governor's Commission on Hispanic and Latin American Community Affairs, and the Howard County Local Children's Board.
Beyond direct service, he shapes policy through this work and as the first Hispanic male to serve on the Howard County Democratic Central Committee.

A two time delegate to the Democratic National Convention in 2020 and 2024, Delegate Moreno continues to champion inclusive representation at every level of government.
Across nonprofit leadership, legal advocacy, and public service, he works to bridge government and the communities it too often overlooks, helping ensure families have the safety and opportunity they need to build their futures.
He is driven by a simple purpose to make Maryland a place where families belong and thrive.
Delegate Moreno earned degrees from Texas A&M University, the University of Texas at El Paso, and Massachusetts School of Law.
He is licensed in Maryland, Massachusetts, and the Supreme Court of the United States.
Fluent in Spanish, Delegate Moreno lives in Jessup with his spouse Jessica and their two children.
Delegate Moreno will be running for reelection and is part of the 2026 Team 13 slate with Senator Guy Guzzone, Delegate Pam Lanman Guzzone, and Amy Brooks to represent District 13, and has been appointed to serve the remainder of Delegate Vanessa Atterbeary's term for the 2026 legislative session.
