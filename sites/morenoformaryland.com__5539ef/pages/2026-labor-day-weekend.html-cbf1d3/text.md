Labor Day Holiday Travel Information
Labor Day Weekend Travel Overview
The Labor Day holiday period marks the unofficial end of the summer travel season.
Maryland’s toll facilities are expected to see increased traffic over the holiday period, especially the Chesapeake Bay Bridge as motorists travel between the Eastern and Western shores.
The Maryland Transportation Authority (MDTA) reminds motorists to prioritize safe driving and help ensure everyone reaches their destination safely.
To help motorists plan ahead and avoid peak traffic, the MDTA is sharing recommended crossing times for the Bay Bridge, along with important travel safety information.
Wishing everyone a safe, restful, and happy Labor Day weekend!

Chesapeake Bay Bridge Travel
More than 345,000 vehicles will cross the Bay Bridge between Friday, September 4, and Labor Day Monday, September 7.
Motorists should stay on US 50 on both sides of the bridge to keep local roadways open for first responders and residents.
Best Times to Travel Across the Bay Bridge
Two-Way Traffic Operations: Weather and traffic permitting, two-way traffic operations will be implemented to help alleviate eastbound delays at the Bay Bridge.
Two-way traffic operations may be limited based on westbound traffic volumes, as bridge officials strive to mitigate delays on both sides of the bridge.
Two-way traffic operations are prohibited during bridge wind warnings/restrictions, fog/limited visibility, or times of precipitation, and may be prohibited during incidents.
Expect Peak Travel Delays: Expect travel delays in both directions—often simultaneously—at the bridge and along the US 50 corridor during peak travel hours throughout the holiday travel period.
The primary cause of congestion is traffic demand exceeding available lane capacity—not two-way traffic operations, timing, or traffic management.
Baltimore Metro Area Highway Advisories
I-695 Beltway Southeast Section Closure: For motorists traveling in and around the Baltimore metropolitan area, please note that the southeast section of the I-695 Beltway is closed at MD 173 (Exit 1) on the Outer Loop and at Broening Highway (Exit 44) on the Inner Loop.
For more information, visit mdta.maryland.gov/HarborCrossings.
I-95 Express Toll Lanes (ETL) Alternative: Drivers may consider the I-95 Express Toll Lanes in their travels between Baltimore and Harford counties, which feature northbound lanes to Mountain Road (MD 152).
Toll rates are available at mdta.maryland.gov/TollRatesTables.
Highway Work Zone Safety: As a reminder, highway work zones throughout the region may have no shoulders and/or narrow lanes.
For the safety of motorists and workers, the MDTA urges drivers to slow down, stay focused and remain alert when traveling through or near work zones.

Travel Safety Reminders
The MDTA reminds motorists to prioritize safe driving and help ensure everyone reaches their destination safely:
-
Allow Extra Travel Time: Drivers should allow extra time to get to their destinations.
Speeding equals less time to react and increases the severity of a crash.
-
Never Drive Distracted: Set your GPS before you begin your trip to help reduce distractions while driving.
Distracted driving puts you, your passengers, and everyone else on the road at serious risk.
-
Maryland "Move Over" Law: In Maryland, motorists must Move Over for all stopped vehicles displaying any caution signals or warning lights when approaching from the rear.
Slow down if you cannot safely move over into an available lane not immediately adjacent to the stopped vehicle.
-
Vehicle Breakdowns & Highway Help: The MDTA Police and Emergency Response Units will have additional patrols on duty to respond quickly to incidents and disabled vehicles during the holiday period.
If your vehicle breaks down, exit the roadway.
If that isn’t possible, remain inside your vehicle and call #77 for assistance.
For emergencies, please dial 911.
-
Zero Tolerance for Impaired Driving: The MDTA Police remind drivers that it is never acceptable to drive while impaired by alcohol, prescription drugs, over-the-counter medications, or illegal substances.
Impaired driving endangers your life and others.
During the holidays, law enforcement officers will remain vigilant and target dangerous driving, seat belt violations, and criminal activity.
To report aggressive or dangerous driving on Maryland toll roads, call MDTA Police Dispatch at 410-537-7911.
-
See Something, Say Something: Travelers also are reminded to pay attention to their surroundings and report suspicious activity to law enforcement.
If you see something, say something.
If there is an emergency, call MDTA Police Dispatch or dial 911.
