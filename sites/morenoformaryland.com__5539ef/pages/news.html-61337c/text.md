READ MORE JUL 07 2026 Maryland Lawmakers Announce Special Session on Congressional Redistricting Maryland lawmakers will convene for a special legislative session from August 3-5 to consider a state constitutional amendment regarding midcycle congressional redistricting.
Announced by Senate President Bill Ferguson and House Speaker Joseline Peña-Melnyk, the session aims to establish clear standards for drawing congressional maps in response to a 2022 court ruling.
The proposed constitutional amendment would ultimately be placed before Maryland voters for final approval.
The decision has drawn criticism from Republican lawmakers, who argue it is a partisan effort to change election rules and maintain the Democratic majority in the state's congressional delegation.

Replacement ballots will be automatically mailed to all affected voters who requested them before May 14, 2026, to ensure election security and integrity.
