Maryland Supreme Court Secures Question 3 on Ballot; Delegate Moreno Supports Vote FOR
On September 3, 2026, the Supreme Court of Maryland issued a 5-2 ruling ensuring that Constitutional Amendment Question 3 (Electoral Districts – General Assembly and Congressional Representatives) will appear on the November general election ballot with updated language.
Overturning lower court orders, the Supreme Court directed state elections officials to use revised, clearer ballot language.
Delegate Moreno announced his full support for Question 3 and encourages voters to vote FOR this Amendment.
