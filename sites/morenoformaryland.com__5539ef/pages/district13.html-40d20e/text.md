As we move through the 2026 election cycle, there have been important developments in our delegation.
Delegate Jen Terrasa has announced that she will seek the position of Howard County Clerk of the Court and will continue serving through the remainder of her term.
Additionally, Delegate Vanessa Atterbeary announced her resignation in December 2025 to focus on her campaign to become the next Howard County Executive.
In January 2026, Governor Wes Moore appointed Gabriel Moreno to fill the vacancy, and House Speaker Joseline A.
Peña-Melnyk swore him in on January 13, 2026.
Delegate Gabriel Moreno is excited to share that he will be running for reelection and that he will be joining the 2026 Team 13 slate of candidates alongside Senator Guy Guzzone, Delegate Pam Lanman Guzzone, and Amy Brooks.
