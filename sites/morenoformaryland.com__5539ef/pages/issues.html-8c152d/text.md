Investing in Great Public Schools for All Every student deserves the chance to reach their full potential in a safe, properly resourced school.
Achieving this vision means securing robust and equitable funding for public schools, standing up for high professional standards, and ensuring that the people who make learning possible, teachers, education support professionals, and staff, are respected and fairly compensated.
Policies must be in place so that school buildings are modern, secure, and healthy environments, with class sizes that foster meaningful learning and attention for every child.
Key priorities for Maryland's future include: Elevating educators and support staff by guaranteeing living wages, protecting collective bargaining rights, and providing secure retirement plans so educators can build lasting careers and serve their communities.
Championing student wellbeing through expanded mental health services and wraparound supports, ensuring every student and teacher has ready access to counseling, social workers, and healthy environments.
Upholding accountability and equity by making sure any school that receives public funds, whether traditional or charter, follows strong standards on safety, nondiscrimination, achievement, and employee rights.
Promoting justice and inclusion with policies that close achievement gaps, recruit and support a diverse educator workforce, and expand curriculum to reflect the rich history and cultures of Maryland's communities.
When the foundation of public education is strong and fair, students thrive, family stability grows, and local communities prosper.
Maryland can lead the way with schools that inspire, prepare, and protect every child, no matter their background or zip code.
Read More Housing & Infrastructure Families should never be forced to choose between paying rent and putting food on the table.
Everyone deserves a safe, stable place to live, and our policies must reflect that basic value.
For too long, we have made it harder for people to buy homes, especially first time buyers and working families, locking multiple generations out of the chance to build stability and wealth.
By failing to create enough affordable and attainable housing, we have allowed entire communities to suffer, where young people cannot afford to stay, parents struggle to keep a roof over their heads, and grandparents are pushed out of neighborhoods they helped build.
Maryland needs comprehensive housing and infrastructure solutions: Expanding affordable housing production by increasing state funding for affordable rental construction, protecting tenants from displacement, and implementing rent stabilization measures to keep housing costs within reach for working families.
Opening pathways to homeownership through expanded down payment assistance programs, first time homebuyer tax credits, and reforms to outdated zoning laws that artificially restrict housing supply and drive up costs.
Investing in sustainable infrastructure by modernizing roads, bridges, public transit, water systems, and broadband networks with green technology that creates jobs today while protecting our environment for tomorrow.
Protecting housing stability by strengthening eviction prevention programs, increasing emergency rental assistance, and ensuring that no family loses their home due to temporary financial hardship.
Building climate resilient communities through infrastructure investments that reduce flooding, improve stormwater management, and retrofit older buildings to meet modern energy efficiency standards.
When families have stable, affordable housing and communities have modern infrastructure, everyone benefits.
Children perform better in school, workers are more productive, and neighborhoods become places where people can build wealth and put down lasting roots.
Maryland must lead on housing policy so that the next generation has the same opportunities to thrive that previous generations enjoyed.
Read More Protecting Maryland's Sovereignty States have the constitutional right to chart their own course, and Maryland has long been a leader in protecting the freedoms and values that matter most to our families.
But right now, we face relentless attempts from Washington to override local decision making, dismantle environmental safeguards, and impose a one size fits all approach that ignores what Marylanders actually need.
We cannot afford to sit back while federal overreach threatens the progress we have worked so hard to achieve.
The path forward requires: Fortifying our state laws on reproductive healthcare, environmental standards, and voting rights so that no matter what happens in Washington, Maryland remains a place where people's rights are protected.
Supporting legal challenges to unconstitutional federal actions by equipping our Attorney General with the resources needed to defend Marylanders in court.
Rejecting partisan interference from agencies that attempt to undermine Maryland's ability to govern itself on healthcare, climate policy, education, and public safety.
Our communities thrive when we have the freedom to shape policies that reflect our priorities and values.
When distant politicians try to dictate how we live, work, and raise our families, it weakens the fabric of local democracy and erodes the trust people have in their own institutions.
Maryland will continue to lead by example, proving that states can be laboratories of progress even in challenging times.
Read More Protecting Maryland's Environment and Future Maryland's natural beauty is part of who we are.
From the Chesapeake Bay to our forests and rivers, our environment sustains our economy, our health, and our way of life.
But right now, our air and water face growing threats from unchecked development, outdated energy systems, and industries that prioritize profit over the wellbeing of our communities.
We need leaders who will fight to preserve what makes Maryland special for generations to come.
The priorities that will protect our future include: Accelerating the shift to clean, renewable energy so Maryland can meet ambitious climate goals, reduce harmful emissions, and create good paying jobs in solar, wind, and green infrastructure while lowering energy costs for families.
Saving the Chesapeake Bay by cracking down on pollution from agriculture, stormwater runoff, and industrial sources, ensuring our waters stay clean for fishing, recreation, and drinking water that families depend on.
Demanding smart oversight of data center expansion before approving projects that could strain our electrical grid, drain water supplies, and increase pollution, requiring thorough environmental impact studies and meaningful community input rather than rushing forward with industry friendly policies that prioritize short term revenue over long term consequences for our neighborhoods and natural resources.
Advancing environmental justice by ensuring that low income communities and communities of color are not forced to bear the burden of pollution, highway expansion, and industrial facilities, and instead receive investment in clean air, green space, and healthy neighborhoods.
Holding corporate polluters accountable by requiring the biggest contributors to climate change and environmental damage to pay their fair share for cleanup and mitigation, not passing those costs onto taxpayers and families.
When we protect our environment, we protect our children's health, our property values, and the natural treasures that make Maryland home.
Clean air and water are not luxuries or political talking points.
They are essentials that every Marylander deserves, and they require leaders willing to stand up to powerful interests and fight for what is right.
Read More Economic Opportunity Real prosperity starts with good jobs, thriving small businesses, and making sure language is never a barrier to opportunity.
For too long, economic policy has favored those at the top while working families struggle to get by.
When work is hard to find or does not pay enough, families cannot cover the basics and entire communities suffer.
Good jobs do more than pay the bills.
They support strong families, reduce stress, allow people to plan for the future, and create the foundation for vibrant neighborhoods.
Maryland needs an economy that works for everyone, not just the wealthy and well connected.
Building inclusive economic growth requires: Supporting small businesses and entrepreneurs by increasing access to capital, cutting red tape, providing technical assistance, and creating incentives for businesses that hire locally and pay fair wages, especially in underserved communities.
Investing in workforce development through expanded apprenticeship programs, vocational training, and partnerships with community colleges that prepare workers for high demand jobs in healthcare, technology, green energy, and skilled trades.
Championing language access and immigrant inclusion by requiring multilingual services in government agencies, supporting English language programs, and ensuring that immigrants and multilingual families can fully participate in the economy without facing unnecessary barriers.
Protecting workers' rights and union power by strengthening collective bargaining protections, opposing right to work laws, increasing penalties for wage theft and misclassification, and defending the constitutional right of workers to organize for better pay and conditions.
Raising wages and strengthening labor standards by supporting a living wage indexed to inflation, eliminating the subminimum wage for tipped workers, expanding paid family and medical leave, and ensuring workplace protections cover all workers including gig economy employees.
When working people have good jobs with fair pay and strong protections, everyone benefits.
Businesses thrive when customers have money to spend.
Communities flourish when families have economic security.
Children succeed when parents are not working multiple jobs just to survive.
Maryland must build an economy where hard work is rewarded, entrepreneurs can succeed, and prosperity is shared by all who contribute to it.
Read More Standing Up for Every Community Maryland's greatest asset is the rich tapestry of people who call this state home.
From longtime residents to new arrivals, from families of every background and identity, our communities are stronger because of our differences, not in spite of them.
But today, we are witnessing a coordinated assault on the dignity and safety of our neighbors, targeting immigrants, Black and Brown communities, and LGBTQIA+ individuals.
These attacks are designed to sow fear and division, and we must respond with unity and action.
Essential protections include: Safeguarding immigrant families by blocking collaboration with federal immigration enforcement and prohibiting unidentified agents from conducting operations in Maryland communities.
Securing personal data by enacting robust privacy protections that prevent state and local agencies from sharing resident information with federal authorities for purposes of surveillance or targeting.
Ensuring comprehensive healthcare access for LGBTQIA+ Marylanders, including gender affirming treatment and prevention medications like PREP and PEP, positioning Maryland as a safe haven for those seeking essential care.
When we fail to protect the most vulnerable among us, we all lose.
Discrimination does not just harm individuals, it tears at the bonds that hold neighborhoods together and sends a message that some people matter more than others.
Maryland can and must be a place where everyone is free to live authentically, contribute fully, and know that their government will stand with them, not against them.
Our commitment to justice and equality is not negotiable.
Read More Community Safety All Marylanders deserve to feel safe in their homes, on their streets, in their places of worship, and throughout their communities.
True safety is not created through enforcement alone but through building real trust, connection, and a sense of shared responsibility between residents, law enforcement, and community organizations.
Real public safety means addressing the root causes of crime, including poverty, lack of opportunity, untreated mental illness, and substance abuse.
It means recognizing that government cannot and should not do this work alone, and that some of the most effective solutions come from community based organizations that are already on the ground doing the work.
Building safer communities requires: Partnering with community organizations by investing state resources in nonprofits and grassroots groups that provide violence intervention, youth mentorship, conflict mediation, and reentry support, filling critical gaps where government lacks the trust, cultural competence, or capacity to reach those most at risk.
Expanding crisis response alternatives by funding mobile crisis teams that pair mental health professionals with trained responders to handle behavioral health emergencies, reducing unnecessary arrests and connecting people to treatment instead of incarceration.
Investing in prevention and opportunity through after school programs, job training, recreational facilities, and summer youth employment that give young people constructive pathways and reduce the conditions that lead to crime.
Strengthening accountability and transparency in law enforcement by supporting robust civilian oversight, mandatory reporting of use of force incidents, and policies that build community trust while ensuring officers have the resources and training they need.
Supporting victims and survivors by increasing funding for victim services, trauma counseling, legal advocacy, and restorative justice programs that help communities heal and prevent cycles of violence.
When communities have the resources they need to address safety holistically, crime decreases, trust increases, and neighborhoods become places where families want to stay and build their futures.
Maryland must embrace a comprehensive approach to public safety that recognizes the vital role of community partners and invests in both prevention and accountability.
Safe communities are built from the ground up, not imposed from the top down.
