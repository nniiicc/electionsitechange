Delegate Gabriel Moreno is a former nonprofit CEO and immigration attorney with more than a
decade of frontline service.
He began his career representing immigrant unaccompanied children,
and in 2018 he joined a national team responding to family separations under the first Trump
administration.
Delegate Moreno was sworn into office on January 13, 2026.
He previously served on the Howard
County Democratic Central Committee as its first Hispanic male member and was twice selected as
a delegate to the Democratic National Convention.
Delegate Moreno lives in Jessup with his
family and is fluent in Spanish.
