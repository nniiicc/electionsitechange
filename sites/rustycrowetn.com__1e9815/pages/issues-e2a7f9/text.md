Healthcare
As Chairman of the Senate Health and Welfare Committee, Senator Crowe has spent decades fighting to improve the health and dignity of every Tennessean.
He is largely responsible for establishing home and community-based care in our state, and has authored many of Tennessee's elder abuse and human trafficking laws.
He co-sponsored landmark legislation to end secret billing, ensuring no patient ever receives a bill they weren't warned about.
When the health of our seniors, our families, and our most vulnerable citizens is on the line, Rusty Crowe has a record of getting results.

That didn't happen by accident.

Veterans
Rusty Crowe served his country in the U.S.
Army Security Agency, intercepting enemy communications in Southeast Asia during the Vietnam War.
That experience never left him.
As Chair of the Joint Select Committee on Veterans Affairs and now a member of the Governor's Armed Forces and Veterans Committee, Rusty has been the "go-to" senator for Tennessee veterans.
