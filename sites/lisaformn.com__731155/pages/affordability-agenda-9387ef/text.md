Lisa Demuth’s Affordability Agenda for Minnesota
As Minnesota House Speaker, Lisa Demuth has fought to reduce the cost of everyday living for residents of all ages and stages of life.
As Lisa travels the state, Minnesotans are all saying the same things – stop the fraud, get our money back and make Minnesota affordable.
We’re hearing from seniors who struggle to stay in their homes and young families who can’t afford a house because of exploding property tax increases.
We’re hearing from moms and dads who work overtime to pay for childcare while countless fraudsters open fake day care centers to steal the taxes paid on that overtime.
To all Minnesotans, help is on the way.
Seven measures to make Minnesota more affordable
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will push to pass the following affordability measures:
Property Tax Relief
Minnesotans have seen their property taxes skyrocket.
Lisa Demuth will solve this problem as Governor because she understands that property taxes must come down.
Statewide levies rose 6.9% for 2026 (cities +8.7%, counties +8.1%), with 18 counties seeing double-digit hikes.
Lisa Demuth will provide $150 million in property tax cuts, lower property taxes by allowing schools to opt out of certain unfunded mandates from the state and allowing local government noncompliance of unfunded mandates.
As Governor, Demuth will return a portion of future surplus in the form of property tax rebates.
All Car Tabs $99 or Less
In 2023, Democrats passed a law that tied registration fees to a higher percentage of MSRP, which increased the costs of car tab fees as high as $700 to $880 or more for newer or pricier vehicles.
One Democrat legislator backed by Sen.
Klobuchar suggested that if the DFL regains total control of state government, they would consider “quintupling” those fees.
A 2026 bipartisan fix trimmed those rates by about 18 percent, but it has left most drivers still facing hundreds of dollars in car tab fees.
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will ask the legislature to pass a bill that will establish a $99 maximum car tab fee: for initial registration and every future renewal, regardless of vehicle value.
Right now, the rate Minnesotans pay to register their automobile depends on a formula almost nobody can explain.
Lisa Demuth’s plan is simple: it’s $99 to register your car, and it’s $99 or less every year thereafter.
If you are paying less than $99 now, your tab fees stay the same.
No formulas, no depreciation tables, no surprises.
Tax reduction for working families and job creators
Under nearly 16 years of Democrat control, Minnesota's income tax rate has climbed to the sixth highest in the nation.
According to a 2026 Minnesota Chamber of Commerce report, Minnesota ranks 44th in the nation in overall tax competitiveness.
Our corporate tax rate is the second highest in the nation at 9.8%.
Minnesota is also in the bottom tier of states in job growth and growth in median household income.
This is an unsustainable path for the next generation.
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will work with the legislature to cut income taxes for working families and individuals by reducing the lowest two tax brackets.
Demuth will also lead the effort as Governor to put Minnesota on a trajectory to boost its business tax competitiveness.
She will lower tax rates, reduce mandates and cut red tape.
Lower taxes for working Minnesotans and job creators means more opportunities for growth for the next generation.
Remove Automatic Gas Tax Increases.
Under one-party Democrat control in 2023, the gas tax was indexed for automatic increases for inflation: Drivers will pay $423 million over four years for this Walz priority.
These kinds of regressive taxes hurt lower-income Minnesotans the most.
Lisa Demuth will put a stop to these automatic increases in the gas tax as Governor.
No Tax on Social Security
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will fully eliminate Minnesota’s state tax on Social Security benefits — no income caps, no partial subtraction.
Minnesota is one of just 8 states that still taxes Social Security benefits.
It’s time for Minnesota to join the vast majority of states across our nation that have eliminated the tax on Social Security for their residents.
More than 470,000 Minnesotans will benefit from the elimination of the state tax on Social Security.
That averages about $1,276 in savings per filer.
Lisa Demuth is fighting to end this tax at the state level for good.
No Tax on Tips, No Tax on Overtime
In 2025, the federal government allowed tax filers to deduct tips and overtime pay from their income tax returns.
Minnesota does not automatically conform its tax code to federal changes — so a server or a factory worker pulling overtime gets the federal break, then has to add that same income back in and pay Minnesota tax on it.
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will sign state conformity legislation so that Minnesota’s tax code matches the federal tip and overtime deductions dollar-for-dollar.
The federal government has already eliminated taxes on tips and overtime for servers, nurses, and factory workers pulling extra shifts.
Lisa Demuth will ensure our state government gets out of the way, so Minnesota workers keep that money, too.
Agency and Fraud Savings
Since 2010, the state budget has more than doubled, and we have not seen a commensurate doubling of state productivity or benefits to our citizens during that time frame.
Lisa Demuth will direct her commissioners to find a total of $1 billion in savings across all agencies.
We will also save at least $1 billion by cracking down on the rampant fraud in state government programs, which has been estimated at $9 billion alone in 14 high-risk Medicaid programs.
This directive will require state agencies to budget based on money the state actually has instead of granting every budget request as Democrats have done for years.
Families budget based on their income – they don’t get to just demand higher pay.
The government should do the same.
