Over the last five years, Minnesota has fallen from #7 in overall education rankings to #21, according to the Annie E.

02 Safety for All Students in Every School Under the Demuth-Wilson administration, schools and classrooms will be safe from disruptions and violence.
We’ll achieve this by ensuring school safety funding for all students.
This funding follows the student like books and buses.
If we don’t put school safety first, nothing else matters.
As Governor, Lisa will ensure school safety funding to protect Minnesota students is expanded to every classroom — not just public schools.
Other measures Lisa will take to improve safety in our classrooms include: School security grants.
Flexibility for school funding streams to be used for safety improvements School Resource Officers are available to every school.
Mandatory minimum prison sentences for repeat gun criminals and for straw purchasers who enable violent crime.
03 $1,700 Federal Tax Credit As Governor, Lisa Demuth will opt-in to the new federal education tax credit to empower parents and fund our schools.
These new federal education tax credits will be available to Minnesota taxpayers regardless.
The decision is simply to let Minnesota schools keep Minnesota money.
By opting Minnesota into the $1,700 tax credit, this will unlock scholarships for public, charter and private students that need help – free money to schools to benefit students with no expense to Minnesota.

04 Focus on Fundamentals As Governor, Lisa Demuth will remove “woke” standards adopted by the Walz status quo and replace them with evidence-based math and science of reading (phonics).
The latter was passed this past biennium, over the objections of DSA Democrats in the legislature.
It was what the “Mississippi Miracle” attributes itself to.
Lisa Demuth will also ensure that the teaching standards propagated by the teacher licensing board are focused on preparing our teachers to prepare the next generation of workers, not the next generation of activists.
This includes: Science of Reading - Empower schools to fully implement literacy through the Science of Reading to boost learning.
Evidence-Based Math - Reform both teacher preparation and instructional practices to improve instruction and learning for math.
Classrooms to Careers - Refocus career/technical education and workforce development to engage high school students in career exploration and skills development.
Tutoring and Individualized Learning - Expand on successful models of Reading Corps and Math Corps by providing tutoring and one-to-one assistance to struggling learners.
05 More Flexibility, Fewer Mandates Under the Demuth-Wilson administration, the Minnesota Department of Education will audit and provide user-friendly performance reports, so communities and families will know where their hard-earned tax dollars are going.
Minnesota is spending the most per student in history and we are not getting results.
Not only that, but we should also not live in a world where families and teachers themselves must pay for classroom supplies.
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will shine a light on where the money is going, so the local communities can know if their tax dollars are making it to the classrooms and helping their kids learn: Top-to-bottom review of state mandates on schools focusing on mandates providing little or no academic or instructional purpose while adding costs.
Create a system of waivers for schools to seek relief from state requirements while preserving their commitment to core academic achievement and effective instruction.
Seek a review and revision of all state academic standards, to restore high standards and expectations for student success.
06 Empower Parents and Local Schools Parents have a fundamental right to direct the education of their children and to be involved in the decisions made in our schools.
Promoting parental involvement in development and approval of curriculum and learning materials consistent with effective teaching and learning.
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will ensure local schools are empowered to seek solutions that best fit the needs of their students and staff.
