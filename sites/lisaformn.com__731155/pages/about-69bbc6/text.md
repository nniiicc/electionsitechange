Donate Home About Priorities Fraud Prevention Plan Affordability Agenda Education Plan Public Safety Plan Store Take Action Donate Meet Lisa Demuth Lisa was elected to the Minnesota House of Representatives in 2018.
A lifelong resident of Minnesota, she and her husband, Nick, have built their lives around faith, family, and community.
Together, they’ve raised four children and are proud grandparents of seven.
Before entering public service, Lisa ran a small business and spent 11 years on the ROCORI School Board, where she fought for parents’ rights, fiscal discipline, and quality education rooted in local values.
Now serving her fourth term in the Minnesota House, Lisa has earned a reputation as a principled conservative and a strong voice for working families, small businesses, and rural communities.
In 2022, her colleagues elected her House Minority Leader, where she led Republicans to a historic result in the 2024 elections—holding every GOP seat and flipping three more to deliver only the second tied House in Minnesota history.
In February 2025, Lisa made history as the first Black lawmaker and first Republican woman to serve as Speaker of the House.
As Speaker, she delivered on conservative priorities - working to pass a commonsense budget that achieved the largest reduction in state spending in Minnesota history, strengthened the economy, and protected taxpayers.
Lisa continues to stand for faith, family, freedom, and the hardworking people of Minnesota.
