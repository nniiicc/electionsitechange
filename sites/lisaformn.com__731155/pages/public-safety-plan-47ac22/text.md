Minnesotans are being failed in this regard – not because of the brave peace officers who serve citizens across this state, but because of the failed political leaders who have made it more difficult for our law enforcement officers to keep the peace.
Public safety is also an economic problem.
We need our largest cities to be booming.
That cannot happen if they are not safe.
Lisa Demuth’s Public Safety Plan is based on the premise that justice and safe communities are only achieved when we no longer treat criminals as victims while the actual victims are ignored.
To Make Minnesota Safer, Lisa Demuth, as Governor, will take the following steps to improve our public safety in our largest cities and across our state: THE FULL PLAN THE DEMUTH-WILSON PUBLIC SAFETY PLAN 01 Punish Criminals, Not Law-Abiding Minnesotans Too often, anti-law enforcement politicians let violent criminals out to reoffend and hurt more Minnesotans.
Under Gov.
Demuth, criminals will be punished, not law-abiding Minnesotans who should be safe in their own communities.
Gov.
Demuth will fight for the following anti-crime measures: 3 Strikes f or Violent Crimes: Increased sentences, no early release and consecutive sentences for criminals who commit a third or subsequent violent crime.
Increased Penalty f or Fleeing Police.
Increase the penalty and drivers' license revocation for people who flee police in a reckless manner.
Increased Penalty f or Assaulting a Peace Officer.
Increase the penalty to a felony for assaulting a peace officer.
If you assault a police officer, you’re going to jail.
Electronic Monitoring i f n o Bail.
If a defendant is being held pre-trial for a violent crime, the judge must place the defendant on electronic monitoring if no bail is ordered.
End Open-Air Drug Markets a nd “Homeless” Camps.
As Governor, Lisa Demuth will sign an executive order that ends open-air drug markets and “homeless” camps.
On any land that falls under the jurisdiction of the state, the camps will be immediately cleared, and Demuth’s administration will provide support to cities to clear camps.
A Demuth-Wilson administration will work with the legislature to expand the state’s ability to take care of homeless camps when cities refuse to.
Increase Safety a t t he University Of Minnesota.
Bolster dedicated funding for the Minneapolis Police Department and the Hennepin County Sheriff’s Department to support the University of Minnesota during high-crime times.
The U of M should be a jewel of our state and a place all Minnesotans feel safe.
Local Government Aid Should Be Used First f or Basic Services Like Police.
Taxpayers in Buffalo shouldn’t be paying for woke Minneapolis policies.
A safe Minneapolis benefits the entire state, and state dollars should go to police first.
People should feel safe to take their families to the Twins or the Guthrie and Gov.
Demuth would ensure this happens.
02 Protect Our Kids Kids should be safe in their schools.
Gov.
Demuth will advocate for school safety funding directed by each district, so they can adapt based on their own needs instead of following top-down mandates that don’t fit any district well.
School Safety Funding Directed b y Each School District: Provide additional funding to the Minnesota School Safety Center in the Department of Public Safety for school safety and security assessments and grants to local schools.
03 Support Law Enforcement Gov.
Demuth will push for funding to support the law enforcement teams that keep our communities safe.
Bolster Funding f or Violent Crime Enforcement Teams.
Violent Crime Enforcement Teams (VCETs) are multi-jurisdictional teams that investigate complex drug and organized gang crimes.
There are VCETS throughout Minnesota, whose work to get violent criminals off the streets could always use a boost in revenue.
The additional amount is scalable.
Strengthen Law Enforcement Recruitment a nd Retention Efforts.
Build on legislative efforts to recruit peace officers by tapping into potential pools that help mitigate the shortfall of officers so many jurisdictions face in our state.
Retain peace officers by offering financial incentives developed in consultation with the key legislators, pension commission, and law enforcement associations.
Ensure First Responders i n Every Community Have t he Training a nd Resources They Need.
This has been a top priority for Lisa Demuth as a legislator, and will be when she is governor, as she will provide a dedicated funding source for ongoing training reimbursement.
04 Punish Fraudsters Fraud hurts Minnesotans, and Gov.
Demuth will hold fraudsters accountable for the damage they cause.
Add A Third-Tier Penalty f or Embezzlement o f Public Funds o f More Than $100,000.
Put fraudsters in prison for 20 years for embezzling funds of more than $100,000 and require restitution.
Sign t he “Fraud Isn’t Free Act.” Suspend payments for suspected fraud, require state employees who were complicit to be fired, and implement other components of Leader Niska's " Fraud Isn't Free Act ." 05 Increase Judicial Accountability Crime-enabling judges keep letting criminals out to reoffend.
Gov.
Demuth will require sunlight in sentencing, so Minnesotans can see what’s really happening when judges depart from minimum sentences.
Provide Sunlight i n Sentencing Reductions & Case Dismissals: This would require the creation of a publicly searchable database that documents sentencing reductions and case dismissals.

Demuth Will Never Pardon a Child Rapist, Especially t o Avoid Deportation.
