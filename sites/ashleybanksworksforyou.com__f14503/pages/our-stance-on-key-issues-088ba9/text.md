Our stance on key issues
Health policy
Ensuring every resident has access to affordable, high-quality healthcare is a foundational priority.
This includes strengthening community health resources, improving mental health support, and addressing the barriers that prevent families from receiving the care they need.
Finally, I am committed to advocating for universal healthcare—because access to quality care should be a right, not a privilege.
Social policy (including housing)
Building a fair, safe, and supportive district means advancing policies that promote equity, protect civil rights, and strengthen the social fabric of our communities.
A central part of this is affordable, stable housing — expanding access, improving housing conditions, and supporting initiatives that prevent homelessness.
Strong social policy ensures that every resident has the opportunity to live with dignity and security.

Our mission
The change we hope this page creates is a deeper understanding of the mission behind Banks For IL-2 — inspiring readers to feel more informed, more connected to the issues, and more confident that positive, community-driven change is possible.
We want people to walk away feeling empowered, hopeful, and motivated to learn more, stay engaged, and be part of building a stronger, more unified Illinois’ 2nd District.
