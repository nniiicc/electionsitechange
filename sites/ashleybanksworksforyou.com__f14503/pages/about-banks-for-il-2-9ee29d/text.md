I bring 41 years of lived experience, resilience, and service, having spent most of my life living, working, and raising my family here.
I survived a rare medical condition that taught me the importance of access to quality healthcare and accountability.
I have worked in a variety of jobs, school bus company, Chicago Transit Authority, truck driving, warehouse, and ride-sharing, giving me firsthand knowledge of the challenges working families face.
I am running because too many laws meant to protect people are not upheld.
My priorities include **infrastructure**, ensuring safe water and modern pipelines; **economic development**, revitalizing businesses and services; and **healthcare access**, restoring local emergency care.
Im also advocating for Universal Healthcare.
My campaign is guided by community, unity, and putting people first.
I live here, work here, and know the needs of this district.
I am not a candidate you need to track down, I am already here and always have been. **Ashley Banks works for you.** Truly Independent, Truly People-First Banks For IL-2 isn’t driven by party agendas or special-interest pressures.
The campaign is built around community needs, not partisan loyalty.
Unity As A Core Strategy, Not A Slogan While many campaigns talk about unity, Banks For IL-2 frames unity as a working method – bringing residents together to collaborate on real solutions instead of fueling division.
