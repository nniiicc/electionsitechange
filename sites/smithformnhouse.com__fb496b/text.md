Representation Rooted in Reality, Not Privilege
I’m running because Minnesotans are hardworking, care for others, and make decisions based in logic.
But we all know the current system isn’t working.
We deserve a high-functioning government for the people, by the people.
I’ve spent my career developing strong businesses, and advocating for people in some of their most important life decisions.
I know what it means to work hard and still feel like the government isn’t working for me.
It’s time to take the politics out of public service, and elect someone that can work to create a state that uses its resources efficiently for its hard-working people – to allow the life they deserve.
