The journey began at seven years old with a simple, curious question: “Dad, why are we Democrats?” Her father’s answer became the North Star of her life: “Because our family… we believe in taking care of each other and being good neighbors.
We’re blue-collar and hardworking; we aren’t rich or powerful, and the Democrats always stand up for the little guy.” That spirit of resilience and community wasn’t just a philosophy; it was a family tradition.
Darci’s grandfather began his life as a humble farmhand before entering the steel industry.
He didn’t just punch a clock; he fought for the dignity of his fellow workers.
His passion for justice led him from the factory floor to the executive board of the Steelworkers Union, where he spent his entire career as a tireless advocate for the laborer and a strong voice in the Democratic Party.
Leadership Rooted in Heart Advocacy is truly in Darci’s blood.
She took the lessons of her upbringing—the value of a hard day’s work and the necessity of looking out for your neighbor—into the boardroom.
For nearly 20 years, as an executive educator and developer of professional salespeople, she didn’t just build careers; she mentored people, proving that leadership is most effective when it is rooted in empathy.
When Darci pivoted into Real Estate, she found a deeper calling.
In one of the most stressful and significant milestones of a person’s life, Darci became more than a Realtor; she became a trusted resource, a steady guide, and the ultimate advocate for families seeking their piece of the American Dream.
A Vision for Minnesota Today, Darci Smith is ready to bring that lifelong commitment to the people of Minnesota.
Her career has been defined by the ability to: Unite diverse groups toward a common goal.
Organize complex systems into real results.
Inspire others to work toward a better future.
Darci Smith isn’t just running for office; she is stepping up to continue the work her family started generations ago.
She believes that when we lead with a love for people and a backbone of advocacy, there is no limit to what Minnesota can achieve.
