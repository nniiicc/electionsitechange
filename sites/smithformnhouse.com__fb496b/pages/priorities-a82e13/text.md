Investing in childcare and education should look like: The cost of childcare doesn’t exceed the means of working parents.
Public funds come with accountability and transparency, preserving the integrity of the programs accepting them.
Competitive compensation of educators and caregivers secures safe and quality care for our children.
Healthcare as a Human Right We are one of the wealthiest states in one of the wealthiest countries in the world.
No one should have the misfortune to be forced into bankruptcy because they got sick.
I plan to advocate for: Reducing costs by eliminating administrative bureaucracy, and allowing providers to spend more time caring for patients.
Ensuring families have the freedom to choose their care in partnership with their doctors, without being bound by a “network” that’s been mandated by a corporate insurance company’s greed.
Stewardship over Profit Minnesota is defined by its 10,000 lakes, its majestic state parks, and its vast Northwoods.
For me, these aren’t just resources to be exploited for a bottom line; they are a sacred trust we hold for future generations.
I will always prioritize The long-term health of our air, water, and soil over short-term corporate gains.
Defending our Boundary Waters and shorelines, ensuring our waters remain swimmable and fishable.
Reducing reliance on outside sources to power our every-day lives.
Improving agricultural sustainability, empowering our ranches and farms to thrive.
People over Politics.
Ending Corruption in Government.
Public service should never be about personal gain.
I intend to foster a system where: Representatives are working for the people, not elite corporate donors who have more access to lawmakers than working Minnesotans.
We implement ranked-choice elections that allow options for Minnesotans who know that “winner-takes-all” elections don’t produce fair representation.
We welcome participation in democracy by lowering barriers to registration and giving equal access to all American Citizens.
