I live and work right here in District 40.
As the owner of Sunset Berry Farm and Adventure Park, my husband and I built our small businesses from the ground up — creating jobs, supporting tourism, and strengthening our local economy.
As a substitute teacher in Summers County, I see firsthand the challenges facing our students, families, and educators.
Education, workforce opportunity, and community stability are deeply connected.
I also serve as President of the West Virginia Agritourism Association, working to connect farmers, educators, tourism leaders, and policymakers to build sustainable rural economic growth.
I’m running because I believe public office is an obligation to the people who live and work here — not outside interests, not lobbyists, and not corporations.
District 40 deserves leadership that listens.
I love hearing from residents of Monroe and Summers County, so feel free to reach out!
