Primary Election Watch Party
Thank you to all who showed up for our Watch Party for the Primary Election.
We packed the place at our favorite new hangout, El Toro Loco, on Murfreesboro Pike for the festivities!
Jayla Thomas (TN House District 55) and Michele joined forces to bring out the voters for a little apres-election soiree.
Humberto at the restaurant crafted some specialty cocktails for the ladies - a Jayla T strawberry margarita, and a Michele V - tasty concoction and - of course -- it was purple!
