July 2026 Michele Vetter Honored to Receive Endorsement from Log Cabin Republicans of Tennessee Article written by user in the category Endorsements NASHVILLE, Tenn. – Michele Vetter, Republican candidate for Tennessee House District 52, is honored to announce that she has received the endorsement of the Log Cabin Republicans of Tennessee.
The endorsement recognizes Michele's commitment to principled leadership, respectful dialogue, and representing the interests of all residents of District 52.
"I am grateful for the endorsement from the Log Cabin Republicans of Tennessee," said Vetter.
"One of the reasons I decided to run for office is because I believe we accomplish more when we focus on what unites us instead of what divides us.
Every person in District 52 deserves to have a representative who will listen, treat them with respect, and work hard on the issues that affect our daily lives." Vetter said her campaign has always been centered on practical solutions that improve the quality of life for everyone in the district.
"Whether we're talking about public safety, improving our infrastructure, protecting our neighborhoods, or preserving our natural resources, these are priorities that matter across every neighborhood and every background.
I believe good leadership means bringing people together, finding common ground where we can, and always putting our community first." As the campaign continues, Vetter remains committed to meeting with residents from all walks of life, listening to their concerns, and working toward thoughtful, responsible solutions that strengthen District 52.
"I appreciate the confidence the Log Cabin Republicans have placed in me," Vetter said.
"If elected, I will work every day to be a representative who serves every constituent with integrity, respect, and a genuine desire to make District 52 an even better place to live, work, and raise a family." Categories Endorsements Uncategorized Authors user Events - Meet Michele!
