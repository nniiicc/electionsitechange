Donate Michele Vetter: Committed to District 52 Neighbors For M Vetter began as a grassroots initiative inspired by Michele’s commitment to public service and to protecting and preserving the Priest Lake communities.
Our mission is to amplify community voices, uphold integrity in government, and encourage active participation across all neighborhoods of District 52, representing our residents with the professionalism and pride that they deserve.
DONATE Issues Safety: Safe neighborhoods are the foundation of a strong community.
Michele believes every family in District 52 deserves to feel secure in their homes, schools, and public spaces.
Addressing crime requires a balanced approach that supports law enforcement while also strengthening the conditions that make communities thrive.
Michele supports equipping first responders with the resources they need, improving coordination between local and state agencies, and investing in prevention strategies that help keep neighborhoods stable and safe.
Her goal is simple: protect residents, support those who serve, and ensure our communities remain places where families can live, work, and raise children with confidence.
Infrastructure & Responsible Growth District 52 is part of one of the fastest-growing regions in the country, and our infrastructure must keep pace with that growth.
Michele believes that roads, highways, and public services should be expanded and improved in a thoughtful way that reflects the needs of residents.
For too long, rapid development has outpaced planning, creating traffic congestion, strained infrastructure, and rising housing costs that make it difficult for many families to afford to live in the communities they love.
Michele supports responsible growth that prioritizes infrastructure improvements, protects the character of established neighborhoods, and ensures development works for residents rather than overwhelming them.
Smart planning today will help keep District 52 livable and accessible for future generations.
Conservation and Community Preservation The natural beauty of District 52, including its lake communities, parks, and green spaces, is one of the things that makes our area such a special place to live.
Michele believes these spaces should be protected and preserved so that residents and future generations can enjoy them.
She supports efforts to keep our lakes clean, our parks safe, and our green spaces well maintained for families, children, and outdoor recreation.
Michele also believes communities should have a voice when large-scale developments threaten the character and environmental health of residential areas.
By protecting our natural resources and ensuring thoughtful land use, we can keep our neighborhoods cleaner, greener, and safer while preserving the quality of life that residents value.
