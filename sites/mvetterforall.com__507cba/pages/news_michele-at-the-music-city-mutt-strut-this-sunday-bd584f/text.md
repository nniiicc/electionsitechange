May 2026 Michele at the Music City Mutt Strut this Sunday!
Article written by user in the category Uncategorized Michele will be at the Music City Mutt Strut this Sunday, May 17, so if you're at the event, be sure to pop by and say hello to her!
The Mutt Strut happens 6:45-10:30am at the Shelby Bottoms Greenway in East Nashville.
The event is put on by the Nashville Humane Association, and all proceeds raised to to save the lives of homeless and at-risk shelter pets.
Registration for the event is only $#and it saves a life of a precious pet!
Michele is an avid pet lover and can many times be found around the Priest Lake area with her beloved Rottweiler, 'Zilla (short for Godzilla).
Any opportunity to help animals is dear to Michele's heart.
She urges everyone to come out, support a very worthy event, and hang with her in the process!.
