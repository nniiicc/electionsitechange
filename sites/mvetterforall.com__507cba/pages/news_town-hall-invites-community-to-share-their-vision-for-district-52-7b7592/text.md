June 2026 Town Hall Invites Community to Share Their Vision for District 52 Article written by user in the category Uncategorized Nashville, TN — June 25, 2026 — Residents of District 52 are invited to attend a Town Hall with Michele Vetter , candidate for the Tennessee State House, on Saturday, July 11, 2026 , from 10 a.m. to noon at the Southeast Metro Nashville Police Precinct Meeting Room , located at 2467 Murfreesboro Pike, Nashville, TN 37217 .
The event, themed “Talk with Michele about the vision for District 52,” offers an open forum for community members to discuss local priorities, ask questions, and voice concerns about issues affecting the district.
Attendees will have the opportunity to engage directly with Michele Vetter and share ideas for strengthening neighborhoods, improving public safety, and supporting local families.
The Town Hall is free and open to the public.
Seating is limited, and early arrival is encouraged. 🐾 PLUS: “Dog Days of Summer” Pet Drive!
We’re collecting items to support local shelters and rescue groups.
If you’re able, please bring something to help our four‑legged friends, such as: • Pet treats • Leashes • Toys • Pet food • Cat litter • Any pet‑related supplies Every donation makes a difference for animals in need.
For more information, please contact the event organizers or visit Michele Vetter’s campaign page for updates.
