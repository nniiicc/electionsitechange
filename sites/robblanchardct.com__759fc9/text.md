Rob Blanchard is running for Connecticut’s 28th State Senate District.
A husband and father raising a young family in Fairfield County, Rob is focused on making Connecticut more affordable by tackling the rising costs of housing, healthcare, and childcare, while supporting strong schools and vibrant local communities.
He brings experience from both the public and private sectors, including his service as Deputy Majority Leader of Fairfield’s Representative Town Meeting.
Rob is committed to ensuring families across Bethel, Easton, Fairfield, and Newtown can afford to live, work, and thrive.
Rob lives in Newtown with his wife, Samantha, their mischievous toddler, Theo, and their dog, Penelope.
