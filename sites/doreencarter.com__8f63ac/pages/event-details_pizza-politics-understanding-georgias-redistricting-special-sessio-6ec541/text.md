About the event
Join Representative Dar’shun Kendrick (HD 95) and Representative Doreen Carter (HD 93) for an engaging community conversation over pizza as Georgia prepares to enter a Special Session on June 17, 2026.
Learn about the Callais redistricting case, how Georgia arrived at this point, and what the court's decision could mean for legislative districts, representation, and future elections.
Special guests Curt Thompson, Chair of the Gwinnett Democratic Party, and Ray Harvin, Chair of the African American Caucus of the Gwinnett Democratic Party, will provide additional insight and answer questions from attendees.
Whether you're a longtime voter, community advocate, or simply interested in how redistricting affects your community, this is an opportunity to get informed, ask questions, and engage in meaningful dialogue about Georgia's political future.
