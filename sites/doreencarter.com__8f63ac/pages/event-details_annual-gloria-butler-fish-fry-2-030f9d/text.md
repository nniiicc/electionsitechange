About the event
Annual Gloria Butler Fish Fry
For years, Senator Gloria Butler brought our community together through her annual fish fry: a time for neighbors, friends, families, and community leaders to connect, fellowship, and simply enjoy one another.
Following Senator Butler's retirement from the Georgia Legislature, I made a commitment to continue this special tradition and proudly keep it in her name.
Her legacy of service and dedication to our community deserves to be celebrated and carried forward.
Please join us for the Annual Gloria Butler Fish Fry, hosted by State Representative Doreen Carter, House District 93.
Come enjoy good food, great fellowship, and an opportunity to connect with neighbors from across our community.
