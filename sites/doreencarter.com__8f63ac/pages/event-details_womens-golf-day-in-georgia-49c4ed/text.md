About the event
Join us for Women's Golf Day in Georgia, a day of fellowship, friendly competition, networking, and empowerment hosted by State Representatives Sheila Jones and Doreen Carter.
Whether you're an experienced golfer, a beginner, or simply interested in learning the game, this event offers something for everyone.
Participants may choose to play 18 holes, 9 holes, or take part in the Golf Clinic designed for those who want to improve their skills or be introduced to the game.
The day will include a continental breakfast, on-course activities, competitive prizes, and an Awards Reception celebrating participants and winners.
Proceeds from the event will support scholarships and community programs that educate, empower, and uplift youth, seniors, and families throughout Georgia.
