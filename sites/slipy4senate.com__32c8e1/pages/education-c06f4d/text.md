Education
Everyone deserves an equal opportunity to learn.
Taxes go to public schools; and if you decide you want your child in a private school, you pay for that.
All students, regardless of where they live, should have access to the resources and opportunities they need to succeed academically and prepare for the workforce of the future.
With the right investments and support, rural Minnesota’s education system can thrive, preparing students for success in an ever-changing world.
