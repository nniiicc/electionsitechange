Accountable Leadership
Accountability isn't just a promise—it's a responsibility to the people who put their trust in you.
Government works best when elected officials remember who they work for: the people.
Accountable leadership means being transparent about decisions, listening to constituents, and taking responsibility for the choices made in office.
It means showing up, asking tough questions, and making sure taxpayer dollars are spent wisely and effectively.
South Carolinians deserve leaders who put public service ahead of personal or political interests.
She will work to make government more transparent, responsive, and focused on what matters most to constituents.
She takes the “Representative” in State House Representative seriously.
Jenna will propose and champion policies that:
- Ensure elected representatives are accessible to the people they serve, not just during election season, but year-round, via multiple methods:
- Regularly scheduled Town Halls
- Regularly scheduled Office Hours
- Attentive, timely email, mail, and phone correspondence
From day one, Jenna will lead with integrity and work in partnership with the people of District 112.
She will listen before legislating, seek input from the community, and make sure constituents have a meaningful voice in the decisions that affect their lives.
She will keep District 112 informed about what’s happening in Columbia and clearly explain, in plain language, how and why she votes on their behalf.
