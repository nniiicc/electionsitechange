AGUA DULCE, CA – Today, George Whitesides continued his campaign momentum, announcing over $880,000 raised in the second quarter of 2025, marking another record-breaking quarter in this must-win seat for Democrats.
His cash-on-hand is over $1.4 million.
This brings Whitesides’ total raise for the 2026 cycle over $1.6 million after finishing the first quarter as the second-top raising frontline Democrat nationwide.
