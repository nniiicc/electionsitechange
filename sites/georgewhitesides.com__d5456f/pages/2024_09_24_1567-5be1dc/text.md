New Whitesides TV Ads Highlight Rep.
Mike Garcia’s Own Words Opposing Abortion Even For Victims Of Rape Or Incest
Garcia: “I am pro-life without exception”
AGUA DULCE, CA – Today, aerospace businessman George Whitesides released two ads highlighting Rep.
Mike Garcia’s national abortion ban with no exceptions for rape or incest, which would override California’s protections.
“While Rep.
Mike Garcia is ‘pro-life without exception’ and wants to ban abortion nationwide, I’ll vote to codify Roe v.
Wade and protect abortion rights,” said George Whitesides.
Garcia cosponsored the national abortion ban (HR 616 and HR 1011), which “would ban abortion nationally with no exceptions.”
Rep.
Garcia’s “misleading” opening ad sought to deceive voters on his poor voting record on choice and Social Security and was called out in the Los Angeles Times and New York Times as working to “blunt a liability.” As Garcia campaigns on a national abortion ban, has voted against reducing health care and prescription drug costs, proposed a budget to cut Social Security and Medicare, and was caught in a stock scandal profiting off alleged insider trading, Whitesides is leading in recent polling.
Fox News recently ranked CA-27 as “Lean Dem,” while the New York Times called this race the “heart of the fight for control of the House of Representatives.” This district is primed to flip as President Biden won by over 12%, and Rep.
Mike Garcia only won by 333 votes in 2020.
Whitesides continues his campaign as a top congressional candidate fundraiser, having the largest cash-on-hand advantage over any incumbent challenger nationwide and doubling incumbent Rep.
Mike Garcia’s 2024 fundraising haul.
