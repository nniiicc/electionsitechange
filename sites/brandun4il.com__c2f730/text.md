Meet Brandun
Serving has been the mission of Brandun Schweizer’s life.
Brandun served our country in the Marine Corps, including five combat tours, before retiring as a Master Sergeant.
After the Marines, Brandun and his wife, Amy, made Danville their permanent home, are raising their three boys here, building a small business, and Brandun went to work as a proud IBEW member.
Brandun serves as State Representative for the 104th District.
The uniform may have changed, but the values he learned in the Marines have not: keep your word, put the mission first, and never forget who you serve.
That means putting the people of Champaign and Vermilion Counties ahead of the politicians and power brokers.
Brandun is fighting in Springfield to lower costs, hold government accountable, and make sure every taxpayer dollar is spent honestly.
Brandun and Amy also run Tiny Troops Soccer, a program that teaches young children on military bases the fundamentals of the sport.
What started during his deployments now operates on dozens of bases in the United States and overseas.
Brandun and Amy live in Danville with their three sons.
Fight Rising Costs
Families across the region are feeling squeezed.
Property taxes, utility bills, groceries, housing, and everyday expenses keep climbing while politicians keep finding ways to spend more.
Illinois needs to be more affordable for people who live and work here.
Brandun is fighting to:
- Slash property taxes and deliver real relief so families can stay in the homes and communities they call home
- Lower utility costs so families keep more of what they earn
- Bring down the cost of everyday life, including healthcare and the price at the pump
