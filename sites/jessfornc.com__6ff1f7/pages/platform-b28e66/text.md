Why this campaign matters This campaign starts with a simple belief: government should work for the people who do the work.
Education That Builds Opportunity Strong schools.
Strong futures.
Public education is the foundation of strong communities.
We fight for well-funded rural schools, well-paid teachers, and community colleges that connect students to good jobs close to home.
Education is not a cost.
It’s an investment in our growth.
Healthcare Close To Home Care you can reach.
Care you can afford.
Rural hospitals and clinics are lifelines.
We protect local healthcare, expand mental health and substance-use services, ensure families get quality care close to home.
Healthy people make strong communities.
Jobs, Wages & Local Economies Work should pay.
Growth should stay local.
Economic development should lift working people.
We support small businesses, job training tied to real employers, modern infrastructure, and fair wages.
When workers earn enough to live, local economies thrive.
Infrastructure For The Future Broadband, roads, and utilities that work.
Modern infrastructure isn’t optional, it’s mandatory.
Broadband, safe roads, and reliable utilities are basic tools for education, healthcare, and economic growth.
We can build for the future without putting people or the environment at risk.
Agriculture Built To Grow The backbone of this District.
Fair crop insurance, water access for drought years, and real investment in family farms that feed this nation.
When farmers struggle, rural economies suffer.
We fight for the people who grow our food, not the corporations that profit from it.
Veterans & Working Families Respect service.
Support stability.
Veterans and working families deserve more than thanks.
They deserve results.
That means strong transition services, housing stability, fair pay, and dependable public support.
Service, in all its forms, deserves dignity.
Honest, Accountable Government Show up.
Listen.
Deliver.
Good government is transparent, present, and focused on results.
Not cheap headlines.
We put district needs first, listen to constituents, and measure success by whether life actually improves for the people we serve.
Data Centers & Mandatory Moratoriums Investments that don’t harm us We need a stronghold against the predatory nature of today’s data centers.
I will not allow our people to be poisoned for industry profits.
Our district welcomes innovation, we also have a rigorous standard for companies to abide by.
