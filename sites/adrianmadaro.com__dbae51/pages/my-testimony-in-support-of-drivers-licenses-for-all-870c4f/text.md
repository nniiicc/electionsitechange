My Testimony in Support of Driver’s Licenses for All
This session, I was proud to submit testimony in support of H.3456, An Act relative to work and family mobility during and subsequent to the COVID-19 emergency.
It’s long past time for Massachusetts to allow undocumented immigrants to drive with a license.
It’s a commonsense measure that will improve traffic safety, reduce racial inequities, and uphold the worth and dignity of immigrant families.
