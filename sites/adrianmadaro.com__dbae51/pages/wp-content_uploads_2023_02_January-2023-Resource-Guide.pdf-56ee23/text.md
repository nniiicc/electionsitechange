It's Time to Register for BPS!
"When it comes to your child's future, timing is everything." - Boston School Finder If your child will go into grades K0, K1, K2, 6, 7, or 9 in September 2023, priority registration for Boston Public Schools is open today through Friday, Feb.
3, 2023.
Did you know that less than 50% of Black and Latino families registered on time for Boston Public Schools—compared to over 70% of white families—in 2018?
When families don’t register on time during their child’s priority registration round, they don't get their top choice schools.
This means that when you register your child for school matters.
To better your chances of getting your child into a great-fit BPS school, make sure you register on time!
