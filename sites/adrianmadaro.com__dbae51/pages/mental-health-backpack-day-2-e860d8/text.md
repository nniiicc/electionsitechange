LGBTQ+ (Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, Transgender, Queer) Resources: The Trevor Project : Offers text and call lines for LGBTQ+ youth dealing with mental health issues.
They also have resources and FAQs for LGBTQ+ youth The Borum at Fenway Health : If you want gender affirming healthcare, access to HIV medication, or have any other questions about medical issues, the Sidney Borum, Jr.
Health Center is a program of Fenway Health that provides safe, non-judgmental care for young people ages 12–29 who may not feel comfortable going anywhere else.
