In the legislature, Rob has consistently voted to build more affordable housing.
And he personally takes the time to learn about the unique needs of Southeast and Northeast Portland neighborhoods when it comes to their housing challenges.
In 2023, Rob voted for House Bill 611, which was a landmark law that limited rent increases in Oregon.
Those protections were extended to manufactured home parks in 2025 (HB 3054).
Rob voted for HB 3644, creating a statewide shelter system and ensuring our tax dollars are being used responsibly.
Rob also voted for HB 2138 in 2025, cutting red tape to ensure that more housing is built faster.
Rob understands it's not enough just to get people off the street—Oregon also has to invest in long-term supportive housing.
House District 42 includes portions of Southeast & Northeast Portland.
