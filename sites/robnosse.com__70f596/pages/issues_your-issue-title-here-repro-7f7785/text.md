They met in 1992 and got married in Canada when it became legal to do so in 2002.
He has been active on issues important to the LGBTQ+ community since he moved to Oregon.
Back in 2015 in Rob's first term in the legislature he helped to pass a bill to ban "conversion therapy" for adolescents and youth.
In 2023, Rob was crucial in passing House Bill 2002, which was Oregon's landmark gender-affirming care law.
As Chair of the House Committee on Behavioral Health & Health Care, Rob is in a key position in the legislature to STOP bills that discriminate against the LGBTQ+ community.
He has successfully stopped Republican bills that aim to target and discriminate against transgender Oregonians from passing.
Rob also supported HB 2008, a data privacy protection bill that stops tech companies from selling geolocation data.
This bill will go a long way in protecting the privacy of LGBTQ+ people from Big Tech companies.
House District 42 includes portions of Southeast & Northeast Portland.
