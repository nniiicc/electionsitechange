He spent over a decade as a union organizer with SEIU 503 and then almost another two decades with the Oregon Nurses Association, fighting for higher wages, better benefits, and safer working conditions.
Rob continued his union advocacy in the legislature, and he's consistently been a strong voice for working families.
In 2023, Rob championed House Bill 2697, Oregon's Hospital Staffing law, to improved nurse-to-patient ratios so Oregonians receive the individual care they deserve.
In 2025, Rob proudly voted for Senate Bill 916, guaranteeing striking workers access to unemployment insurance.
This law ensures striking workers are able to support their families while negotiating contracts with their employers.
Rob also voted for SB 426, combatting wage theft in the construction industry, and he voted to fund new investigators and enforcement resources to ensure unpaid workers receive the payment for their work that they earned.
Rob Nosse will always stand with unions and working families as he knows the ability to come together at work and collectively bargain for better wages and benefits and working conditions is among the best anti-poverty programs that exist.
