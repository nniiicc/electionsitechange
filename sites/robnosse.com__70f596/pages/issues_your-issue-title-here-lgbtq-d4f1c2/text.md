Rob Nosse believes in
REPRODUCTIVE HEALTH
Rob Nosse is a fierce defender of a person's right to access reproductive health care including abortion services.
Oregon has some of the best abortion laws in the country, and Rob has fought to keep it that way.
In 2026 Rob worked to pass HB 4127, ensuring that Planned Parenthood stays open and operational for Oregonians.
In 2023, Rob championed House Bill 2002, which was Oregon's landmark abortion access law.
This bill solidified our state's support for abortion access and inclusive health care.
Oregon is a safe haven for those seeking help who come from more conservative states.
Rob will always fight to ensure reproductive healthcare is protected, despite Republican efforts to make abortion illegal elsewhere in the country.
