Volunteer for a stronger West Virginia
Join our grassroots movement and help us bring a stronger voice to the 14th District.
Your involvement, big or small, makes a real difference in shaping the future of our communities.
Ways you can help
We need your help to reach voters where they live.
This means knocking on doors, making phone calls, attending community events, assisting with campaign events, putting up signs, and talking with friends and neighbors about why this campaign matters.
Additionally, we need support with social media, graphic design, photography, organizing, fundraising, and other behind-the-scenes work.
You don’t need to be an experienced political organizer; if you care about West Virginia and want to make a difference, there is a place for you on this campaign.
This is a grassroots campaign built by ordinary people.
Every door knocked, every conversation started, and every hour volunteered brings us closer to winning and giving the people of the 14th District a stronger voice in Charleston.
Why volunteer with us?
By volunteering, you’ll be part of something bigger than a political campaign.
We’re building a grassroots movement focused on giving working families, communities, property owners, students, teachers, and working people a stronger voice in the decisions that affect their lives.
Volunteers will have opportunities to talk directly with their neighbors, help shape the conversation, organize in their communities, and see the impact of their work firsthand.
Your time and effort genuinely matter.
We’ll work hard, have some fun along the way, build relationships, and celebrate our victories together.
When we win, it will be because ordinary people came together and decided their communities deserved better.
