A platform for a stronger West Virginia
Discover Jason Armentrout's commitment to protecting property owners, fully funding public education, and strengthening our economy for a prosperous future in West Virginia.
Protecting property owners and communities
My top priority is making things right regarding MARL, Valley North, and Fundamental Data.
I am committed to protecting West Virginia property owners and communities while ensuring economic development benefits the people who live here.
We must ensure that our state's growth truly serves its residents, safeguarding their interests and fostering sustainable prosperity.
Investing in our public education
I am dedicated to fully funding and protecting our public schools, public post-secondary institutions, and workforce training centers.
A strong educational foundation is crucial for the future of West Virginia.
By investing in our students and educators, we empower the next generation and equip our workforce with the skills needed for success.
Strengthening our economy for all
My goal is to strengthen our economy so West Virginia remains an affordable place to live, work, raise a family, and build a future.
We can achieve this by creating opportunities that benefit all residents, not just a select few.
Through thoughtful policies, we can foster economic growth that ensures long-term stability and prosperity for every West Virginian.
