Your contribution helps us build a grassroots campaign dedicated to bringing a stronger voice to Charleston for the people of the 14th District.
Your donation makes a difference
Every donation, regardless of its size, helps us take this campaign directly to the people of the 14th District.
Contributions help pay for signs, literature, digital outreach, advertising, campaign events, travel, and the basic costs of running a competitive grassroots campaign.
But a donation is about more than helping a candidate win an election.
It is an investment in giving working families and local communities a stronger voice in Charleston.
It helps us stand up for our public schools and post-secondary institutions, protect property owners and communities affected by projects like MARL and Valley North, hold powerful interests accountable, and build an economy that makes West Virginia an affordable place to live and raise a family.
This campaign is not being built by corporate interests or political insiders.
It is being built by ordinary West Virginians.
Every contribution helps us reach another voter, have another conversation, and bring our message to more communities.
You can donate digitally on my VENMO account to the left.
Who we are fighting for
I believe the people most likely to support this campaign are ordinary West Virginians who believe their communities deserve a stronger voice in Charleston.
That includes working families, teachers and school employees, union members, small-business owners, retirees, young people, parents, and anyone who cares about the future of our communities.
I also expect support from people who believe that economic development should benefit the people who live here—not just corporations and outside investors—and from those who want fully funded public schools, strong public institutions, good-paying jobs, and an affordable place to raise a family.
Most importantly, I’m asking for support from people who believe politics should be about people, not power or money.
This campaign will be built on grassroots support, with ordinary people giving what they can because they believe West Virginia can and should do better.
