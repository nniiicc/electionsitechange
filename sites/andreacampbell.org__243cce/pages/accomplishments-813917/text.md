Andrea Joy Campbell’s Record of Results
Fighting for the People of Massachusetts
Standing Up to Donald Trump
Protecting Your Right to Vote
Trump tried to disqualify eligible voters and throw out lawfully cast ballots.
Andrea fought back, co-leading a lawsuit with 24 states to block his unconstitutional voting order and winning in court when Trump’s Justice Department demanded Massachusetts voter data.
When Trump directed the Post Office to take control of mail-in voting, requiring states to overhaul their entire election systems in a matter of weeks, Andrea co-led a coalition of 25 states to sue to stop it, and won a preliminary injunction blocking the rule from taking effect before November.
Defending Democracy and Civil Rights
Trump has attacked voting rights, gutted diversity programs, and weaponized federal agencies against communities of color, immigrants and LGTBQ+ communities.
Andrea has fought back at every turn, in court and in communities across Massachusetts, making clear that everyone’s rights and humanity are protected under Massachusetts law.
Protecting Massachusetts’ Economy
Trump’s reckless policies have threatened Massachusetts jobs, research funding, and economic stability.
Andrea has gone to court to protect NIH research grants, defend public universities and massive clean energy projects, and block funding cuts that would devastate communities, successfully protecting over $3 billion for Massachusetts.
Stopping Illegal ICE Arrests
Andrea knows ICE has gone too far.
She went to court and won, blocking the Trump Administration’s efforts to force Massachusetts agencies to cooperate with ICE.
She went to court to stop Trump from illegally stripping immigrant students of their visas and securing the release of a Massachusetts resident wrongfully arrested by ICE.
She issued a Know Your Rights guide for immigrants and families, and launched a statewide portal with Governor Healey to report ICE misconduct.
No one in Massachusetts should live in fear of an agency operating outside the law.
Fighting for Clean Air and Clean Water
Trump’s EPA has tried to dismantle the protections that keep our air clean, our water safe, and our communities healthy.
Andrea took the Trump Administration to court and won, defending offshore wind projects in Massachusetts including Vineyard Wind and South Shore Wind, bringing down energy costs, adding clean jobs, and addressing the climate crisis.
Andrea led a coalition of 24 states to sue the EPA for rescinding the landmark Endangerment Finding that underpins all federal greenhouse gas regulation.
She’s also challenged Trump’s attacks on clean vehicle standards, solar energy programs for low-income households, and environmental justice grants.
And when Trump’s administration struck deals to cancel three major offshore wind projects that could have powered three million Northeast homes, Andrea put the Department of Interior on notice that she’ll see them in court.
Protecting Birthright Citizenship
On his first day in office, Trump signed an executive order trying to end birthright citizenship, a constitutional right guaranteed for over 150 years.
Andrea co-led the lawsuit the very next day, won a nationwide injunction, and has fought the case all the way to the U.S.
Supreme Court – and WON.
The Fourteenth Amendment is the reason a Black girl from Roxbury could grow up to be your Attorney General, and she’s not backing down.
Standing Up For Reproductive Freedom
Reproductive freedom is under relentless attack, and Andrea has been fighting back from day one.
She launched the state’s first confidential hotline for abortion care, co-sponsored a law protecting Massachusetts doctors and nurses from out-of-state prosecution, fought to keep mifepristone available through telehealth, and defended women’s access to no-cost birth control.
In Massachusetts, reproductive freedom is protected, and Andrea will never stop fighting to keep it that way.
Stopping Trump’s Illegal Tariffs
Trump tried twice to impose illegal tariffs that drove up the cost of groceries, gas, dairy, lumber, and cars.
The Supreme Court struck down the first round, and, in a lawsuit brought by Andrea and more than 20 other states, a federal court struck down the second.
Nearly 90% of the cost of Trump’s tariffs fell on American consumers and businesses.
Andrea isn’t letting him keep raising prices illegally.
Saving Libraries, Museums, and Public Services
When Trump tried to illegally dismantle four federal agencies that fund libraries, museums, minority-owned businesses, and services for the unhoused, Andrea sued with a coalition of 20 other attorneys general and won.
Massachusetts’ cultural institutions and the communities they serve are protected.
Defending Public Media
Trump signed an executive order to cut federal funding for NPR and PBS, threatening local stations and the free educational programming millions of Massachusetts families rely on.
Andrea filed a brief alongside 22 other attorneys general to block it, because access to trusted public media and educational resources shouldn’t depend on who is in the White House.
Protecting Housing Programs
When Trump’s Department of Housing and Urban Development tried to illegally cap funding for permanent housing programs, threatening to remove 90,000 families across the country from stable housing, Andrea co-led a coalition of 22 states to sue, and won.
Tens of thousands of Massachusetts residents facing housing insecurity kept their homes because of that victory.
And when the Trump Administration attempted to place unfair conditions on funding that threatened state and local agencies’ ability to enforce fair housing laws, Andrea sued, leading HUD to drop all of the illegal conditions.
Making Massachusetts More Affordable
Taking On Corporate Landlords
Your rent is too high, and Andrea is doing something about it.
She successfully sued five of the nation’s largest corporate landlords and the rent-pricing software companies they used to collude and artificially inflate rent, securing a $7 million multistate settlement.
She also secured a first-of-its-kind $10 million settlement for homeowners targeted by predatory mortgage lending practices.
Making Sure Workers Get Paid
Every worker deserves to be paid what they earn.
Andrea has sued more than 600 employers for cheating workers out of wages and ignoring paid sick leave, recovering more than $35 million in back pay and penalties for workers across Massachusetts.
In FY 2026, Andrea took more than 1,200 enforcement actions against more than 1,100 employers for violating various protections, helping more than 16,000 workers and assessing nearly $12.7 million in restitution for unpaid wages and penalties!
Cracking Down on Junk Fees
Hidden fees and deceptive billing are costing Massachusetts families, and Andrea is leading the nation in cracking down on them.
Andrea issued historic regulations to protect consumers from junk fees – from concert tickets and hotel bookings to subscription services and apartment rentals.
She secured a $6.5 million settlement with a major home security company for burying illegal charges in customers’ bills, and she’s not stopping there.
Blocking Unfair Utility Rate Hikes
When gas and electric companies tried to pad their profits on the backs of Massachusetts families already struggling with sky-high bills, Andrea fought back and won.
She has blocked unfair utility rate hikes, saving Massachusetts families more than $2.5 billion in energy bills.
Standing Up For Gig Workers
Gig workers deserve a fair wage and real benefits.
Andrea secured a nation-leading settlement with Uber and Lyft, delivering a $34/hour minimum wage, healthcare, and paid family leave for thousands of drivers and their families across Massachusetts.
Increasing Affordable Housing Supply
Massachusetts doesn’t have enough housing, and that’s driving up costs for everyone.
Andrea enforced a statewide law requiring communities served by public transit to allow multifamily housing, opening the door to more homes in more neighborhoods and making it easier for families to afford to stay here.
Lowering Your Health Care Costs
When UnitedHealth tricked Massachusetts residents into buying junk insurance that left them without real coverage, Andrea took them to court and won a $165 million judgment.
She’s also sued the nation’s largest insulin manufacturers for using deceptive pricing schemes to put life-saving medicine out of reach, securing $17.85 million in settlements over widespread price manipulation.
Making Insulin More Affordable
No one should ration a life-saving drug because a pharmaceutical company rigged the pricing system.
Andrea sued the nation’s largest insulin manufacturers for using deceptive pricing schemes to drive up costs, fighting to ensure every Massachusetts resident who needs insulin can afford it.
Blocking Airline Price-Fixing
JetBlue and American Airlines tried to form an alliance that would have eliminated competition and driven up the cost of flights in and out of Boston.
Andrea went to court to block it, and won.
Traveling shouldn’t cost more just because airlines decided to stop competing with each other.
Taking On Amazon’s Monopoly
Amazon used its market dominance to force vendors into agreements that drove up prices for consumers across Massachusetts, often without their knowledge.
Andrea joined the FTC and a coalition of states in suing Amazon for these monopolistic practices.
No company, no matter how powerful, should be able to rig the market against the people it serves.
Winning Back Money from Google
Andrea secured a $700 million multistate settlement with Google for anticompetitive conduct in its app store, including $1.4 million directly for Massachusetts.
When powerful tech companies abuse their market position to squeeze out competition and charge consumers more, Andrea holds them accountable.
Making Concerts Affordable Again
Live Nation used its monopoly over venues like House of Blues, MGM Music Hall at Fenway, and Leader Bank Pavilion to drive up ticket prices and crush competition.
When the federal government settled, Andrea kept fighting alongside 26 other state attorneys general, and a jury found Live Nation guilty of operating an illegal monopoly.
Protecting Our Kids and Our Communities
Holding Big Tech Accountable For Harming Our Kids – Historic $17 Billion Meta Settlement
Social media companies knew their platforms were addicting young people and hurting their mental health, and they hid it.
Andrea filed lawsuits against Meta and TikTok for deliberately designing their platforms to exploit young users.
In August 2026, Andrea and a bipartisan coalition of attorneys general reached a landmark $17.1 billion settlement with Meta, the largest state consumer protection win in history outside the Big Tobacco settlements of the 1990s.
Massachusetts could receive up to $516 million, including a guaranteed $366 million over the next decade.
The settlement requires Meta to automatically set 2-hour daily time limits, block access at night, mute notifications during school hours, and stop endless scrolling.
Andrea isn’t backing down on TikTok either – and in the meantime, she’s advocating for statewide social media legislation that would codify these protections across the tech industry.
Taking On The Teen Vaping Epidemic
JUUL illegally targeted children with its marketing and sparked a teen vaping epidemic.
Andrea took them to court and won, securing a historic $600 million settlement to hold them accountable and protect young people from exploitation.
Enforcing Child Labor Laws
No child should be sent to work in unsafe conditions or cheated out of wages just because an employer thinks they can get away with it.
Andrea has cracked down on employers across Massachusetts for violating child labor laws, from restaurants to construction sites, making sure young workers are protected, paid fairly, and not put in harm’s way.
Protecting Gender-Affirming Care For Trans Youth
The Trump administration has launched wave after wave of attacks on gender-affirming care, and Andrea has fought back every single time.
When RFK Jr. issued a declaration falsely claiming gender-affirming care was “unsafe and ineffective” and threatened to strip Medicare and Medicaid reimbursements from providers who kept treating trans patients, Andrea sued, and a federal judge permanently blocked the threats.
When Trump tried to strip gender-affirming care from coverage as an essential health benefit under the Affordable Care Act, Andrea co-led 20 states in suing, and a federal court struck down the provision.
When Trump finalized a rule prohibiting Medicaid and CHIP from covering gender-affirming care for minors, Andrea co-led 22 states in filing a new lawsuit to block it.
Gender-affirming care remains legal and protected in Massachusetts, and Andrea will never stop fighting to keep it that way.
Defending Our Kids’ Education
Trump has tried to gut teacher training programs, cut student grants, and dismantle the Department of Education.
Andrea went to court to stop him.
Every child in Massachusetts deserves access to a quality public education, and that’s worth fighting for.
Giving Young People A Voice
Andrea created the first-ever AG Youth Advisory Council to bring young people directly into the work of shaping policy and advocacy across the Commonwealth.
The people most affected by these decisions deserve a seat at the table.
Protecting Young People From Predatory Gambling Operators
Gambling companies have found new ways to target young people, and Andrea is cracking down.
She’s leading a bipartisan coalition of states to shut down illegal sports betting on Kalshi, which offers sports wagers to teenagers against Massachusetts law.
She has also worked with the Massachusetts Gaming Commission to limit how licensed sportsbooks can market their products and prohibited the use of gambling data to target people with gambling or substance use disorders.
Holding the Opioid Industry Accountable
The opioid crisis has claimed more than 20,000 Massachusetts lives over the last two decades, and Andrea is making sure those responsible are held accountable.
She secured a historic $7.4 billion national settlement with Purdue Pharma and the Sackler family – including $108 million for Massachusetts – for their role in creating and profiting from the opioid epidemic.
Andrea also reached a $350 million national settlement with Publicis Health, the marketing firm that helped push addictive opioids to doctors and patients.
Fighting Gun Violence and Keeping Communities Safe
Every person and family in Massachusetts deserves to feel safe in their community.
That’s why Andrea is taking on the organized criminal activity – including drug trafficking operations, illegal gun sales, and commercial sex trafficking networks.
She’s using every tool available to go after bad actors, hold them accountable, and make Massachusetts communities safer for everyone.
