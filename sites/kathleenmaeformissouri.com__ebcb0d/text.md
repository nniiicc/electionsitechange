How to write her in
A write‑in vote only counts if it’s marked correctly.
Here’s the general process — three steps, on most ballots.
1
Look for a blank line near the bottom of the U.S.
Representative section, usually labeled “write‑in.”
2
Print KathleenMae Rogers clearly on the line — first and last name, exactly as filed.
3
Many ballots also need the adjacent oval or box filled in for the write‑in to be counted — don’t skip it.
Exact ballot format varies by county — confirm the write‑in process with your local election authority before Nov 3.
