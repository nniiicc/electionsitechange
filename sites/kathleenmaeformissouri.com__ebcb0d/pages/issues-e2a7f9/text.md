Policy Focus Support family farms through agricultural research and university partnerships Invest in rural infrastructure — roads, bridges, broadband, water systems, utilities Strengthen healthcare, education, and workforce development access in rural areas Encourage competitive markets for farm and rural business innovation Support responsible stewardship of agricultural land and resources Missouri’s agricultural heritage and its future are not competing ideas.

Policy Focus Improve rural healthcare and mental health access for veterans Strengthen and expand veteran benefits oversight, reducing delays Support transition programs connecting veterans with education and employment Strengthen first responder workforce development and mental health resources Congress must honor service by ensuring stable, fulfilling lives, access to care, and room to recover.

Public investment in cultural spaces supports education, tourism, and local economic activity.
Policy Focus Support libraries, museums, parks, and historic preservation Encourage arts, recreation, and cultural programs that enrich community life Invest in public gathering spaces for families and neighbors Support partnerships that preserve tradition while enabling new cultural expression Economic development builds material strength; culture gives communities their identity.
Close · back to all issues “Missouri’s rivers, lakes, forests, parks, wildlife habitats, and public lands are the heart of our state.” Our natural resources provide clean water and air, support agriculture and local economies, and sustain hunting and fishing traditions.
Conservation means balancing environmental protection with economic activity.
Policy Focus Support responsible stewardship of land, water, forests, and wildlife Encourage partnerships among farmers, landowners, researchers, and communities Invest in clean water systems, healthy forests, and recreation infrastructure Promote science-based innovation and practical conservation practices Healthy wildlife populations depend on healthy habitats — future generations deserve continued access to Missouri’s natural heritage.
Close · back to all issues “Every American possesses constitutional rights that government has a responsibility to protect, not grant.” Government must safeguard constitutional freedoms, apply the law fairly, and remain accountable.
A free society requires trust in equal application of the law and due process.
Policy Focus Support legislation that respects constitutional limits and protects individual liberties Defend equal protection and due process for every American Strengthen federal agency oversight to ensure accountability Restore checks and balances that limit government power The Constitution frames the relationship between people’s freedoms and limited government power; Congress must uphold that framework faithfully.
Close · back to all issues “Public office is a public trust — elected officials have a responsibility to listen to their constituents, explain their votes, and be accountable.” Americans feel disconnected from their representatives when national politics overshadows local concerns.
Representation requires ongoing communication, transparency, and accountability — not just at election time.
Policy Focus Hold regular town halls across the district, including rural communities Clearly communicate votes, priorities, and legislative decisions Maintain accessible constituent services Support transparency and strong congressional oversight Uphold equal application of the law Effective representation begins with listening.
The office belongs to the people it serves.

Policy Focus Maintain a strong, well-trained, well-equipped military Support diplomacy and partnerships that reduce the likelihood of conflict Require clear objectives and congressional oversight before military force is used Honor commitments to service members and military families Evaluate the true economic and human costs of military action Lasting security requires strength, discipline, accountability — and asking hard questions before risking lives.
