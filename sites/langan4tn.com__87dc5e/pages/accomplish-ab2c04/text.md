Enact Term Limits at the state level: No one should sit in their seat forever.
It is time we implement term limits for all Senators, Representatives and Judges in Tennessee Community Centered Districting: In order for the people and communities of Tennessee to be truly represented we must commit to non-partisan districts that strive to keep our communities together.
Pass Ranked Choice Voting.
Affordability Introduce and pass the Sales Tax Relief Act to end the state sales tax on Groceries Clothing under $50 Footwear under $100 Introduce and pass a Tennessee Right to Privacy Act to End data mining and surveillance of Tennesseans.
Ban Surge Pricing.
Ban Surveillance Pricing.
