Inside Tennessee - Round Table with the Candidates DONATE BRYAN LANGAN TENNESSEE SENATE DISTRICT 7 Hi, my name is Bryan Langan, and I’m running to bring accountability to our government and affordability to the people of Tennessee.
A graduate of the University of Tennessee, and a current resident of the Karns community, I’m running for Tennessee Senate to represent the people of District 7.
Having worked in blue collar jobs for nearly three decades, my experience in the service industry, management and transition to the trades means I know first-hand the challenges Tennessee workers face.
I believe that instead of listening to the corporations and special interests, it is time for the voice of people to be heard and hope to earn the vote of every hard-working Tennesseean in District 7.
For the past 15 years the Tennessee GOP has held all three branches of our state government.
They could pass anything they want to make life better and more affordable for Tennesseans.
Instead, under their leadership, we have seen virtually zero job growth in two years , more Tennesseans are in poverty in 2025 than 2024 , we are dead last in per student spending and are the third most violent state per capita .
On top of that, our costs are higher, services lower, and our wages haven’t kept up with the cost of living.
Our legislature continuously sides with corporations and profit over the hard working people of Tennessee, passing legislation that allows corporations to take advantage of our workers, pollute our waterways and destroy our land.
Tennesseans are fed up, and we deserve better.
Our government should be working on the issues that improve the lives of Tennesseans instead of the divisive and oppressive politics of our current supermajority.
My focus will be on delivering tangible benefits to the people of Tennessee and making sure their voices are represented long after I’m gone.
That’s why I will work to pass campaign finance reforms that curb corporate influence in our elections and will introduce legislation to enact term limits for all Senators, Representatives and Judges in Tennessee.
This November I hope I will have earned your vote and the privilege to represent you in Nashville.
When elected, I will work to defend the rights of all Tennesseans, make Tennessee more affordable and ensure future generations have access to the natural beauty of our state.
Accountability Campaign Finance Reform: I will introduce legislation to reform our campaign laws at the state level to get money out of politics and ensure the will of the people is not drowned out by the financial influence of corporations and special interests.
Enact Term Limits at the state level: No one should sit in their seat forever.
It is time we implement term limits for all Senators, Representatives and Judges in Tennessee Community Centered Districting: In order for the people and communities of Tennessee to be truly represented we must commit to non-partisan districts that strive to keep our communities together.
Pass Ranked Choice Voting.

Support Our Teachers and Fully Fund Our Public Schools.
