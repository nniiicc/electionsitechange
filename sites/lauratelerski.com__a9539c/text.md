ABOUT LAURA
Laura Damphousse Telerski has represented Nashua’s Ward 8 in the NH House since 2018 and is Deputy House Democratic Leader.
Laura serves on the House Finance Committee as ranking member of Division III, which focuses on the portions of the budget dealing with the Department of Health and Human Services.
She has focused on advancing policies aimed at supporting hardworking Granite Staters, including helping low-income parents with summer grocery assistance as well as filing legislation to make it easier for families to enroll in the federal school meals program.
During her first two terms in the House, Laura was a member of the House Transportation Committee, serving as Clerk and Deputy Ranking Member, where she was the prime sponsor of legislation to remove fees for handicapped parking placards and worked to close a loophole in the state’s DUI statute to make our roads safer.
Laura is a member of the Executive Committee, and previous chair of the NH House Democratic Victory Campaign Committee, where she recruited, trained, and supported strong candidates to elect and sustain a Democratic Majority in the NH House.
Laura grew up in Nashua and is a product of Nashua public schools.
She holds a bachelor’s degree in History from Georgetown University and a master’s degree in Middle Eastern Studies from the University of Chicago.
Laura and her husband, Jason, have three adult children, Noah, Ben, and Alanya.
