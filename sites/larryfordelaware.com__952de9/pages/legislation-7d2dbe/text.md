HB 210 with HA 1 ✓ Signed Into Law An Act to Amend Titles 3, 7, and 16 of the Delaware Code Relating to the Environment This landmark environmental legislation strengthens Delaware's protections across agriculture, natural resources, and public health.
By amending three separate titles of the Delaware Code, HB 210 reflects Larry's commitment to clean air, clean water, and a sustainable future for Claymont and District 7 families.
