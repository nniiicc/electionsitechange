Larry's Story Larry Lambert is a lifelong resident of Claymont and the 7th District.
His story is one of perseverance — he lost his big brother and both of his parents at a young age.
At 20, he took on the responsibility of raising his little sister while becoming the first Lambert to graduate from college.
His message to her: "No matter what we go through, we never give up." Larry attended Maple Lane Elementary, Brandywine High School, and earned his degree from Temple University (BA Communications, Cum Laude).
He taught Sunday School at Concord Baptist Church, served as an art teacher for 8 years at the Claymont Boys & Girls Club, substituted at Brandywine High School, and spent 9 years in corporate banking before entering public service.
Larry has actively worked to build bridges between Delaware's corporate and nonprofit sectors to bring resources into the 7th District — road repairs, jobs, workforce development programs, mentors, homeownership seminars, air quality monitors, murals, air conditioners, free COVID-19 testing sites, and boxes of fresh food for thousands of local families.
