Workforce & Retirement Delaware EARNS Act (HB 205) Larry sponsored the Delaware Expanding Access for Retirement and Necessary Savings program — a state-facilitated retirement savings plan allowing eligible employees to build financial security on a voluntary basis.
This landmark legislation ensures that working families across District 7 have access to affordable retirement options.
Public Health Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention (HB 222 & HB 227) Larry championed legislation to simplify blood lead level screening requirements and improve compliance with testing and reporting mandates.
These bills require physicians to complete updated training and strengthen the Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention Act — protecting the health of Delaware's children.
Healthcare Access Reproductive Health Protections (HB 455) Larry co-sponsored legislation to protect healthcare providers who perform, recommend, or provide reproductive health services, ensuring patients across Delaware can access the care they need without barriers.
Affordable Housing Housing Affordability & Tenant Protections As Vice-Chair of the Housing Committee, Larry leads the fight to keep District 7 families in their homes.
He has advanced policies on homeownership seminars, tenant rights, and affordable housing development across Claymont and northern Delaware.
Environment Pollution Accountability Act (HB 210) Larry introduced the Pollution Accountability Act to increase penalties and enforcement for commercial polluters in Delaware.
Co-sponsored with House Speaker Melissa Minor-Brown and Sen.
Nicole Poore, this legislation responds to long-standing concerns from overburdened communities in District 7.
Proudly serving Claymont and the communities of Delaware's 7th District.
