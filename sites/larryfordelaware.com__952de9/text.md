Larry Lambert is a lifelong Claymont resident who lost his brother and both parents at a young age.
At 20, he took on the responsibility of raising his little sister — while becoming the first Lambert to graduate from college.
His message to her: "No matter what we go through, we never give up." His decades of service have been recognized by Senator Joe Biden before the US Congress, on the Oprah Winfrey Show, and in a proclamation by State Treasurer Colleen Davis.
