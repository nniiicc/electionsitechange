Henry Hostetler for NC State Senate Home Platform About Contact Endorsements Donate Henry Hostetler for NC State Senate Platform Henry Hostetler: A Vision for District 5 Hostetler is running on a "Common Sense for North Carolina" platform that prioritizes the needs of families and small businesses in the eastern part of the state.
He advocates for a lean government that stays out of the way of job creators while ensuring that the state's infrastructure and education systems are robust and accountable to the taxpayers.
Economic Growth & Tax Relief: Support continued reductions in the state income tax to keep North Carolina competitive and allow working families to keep more of their hard-earned paychecks.
Education & Parental Rights: Champion the "Parents’ Bill of Rights" to ensure transparency in curriculum and empower parents to be the primary decision-makers in their children's upbringing and education.
Safe Communities: Back "pro-law enforcement" legislation that provides first responders with the resources they need to combat the opioid crisis and keep local neighborhoods safe.
Fiscal Responsibility: Advocate for a balanced state budget that prioritizes essential services like infrastructure and disaster resilience while eliminating wasteful government spending.
Agricultural Support: Protect the interests of eastern North Carolina’s farming community by reducing over-regulation and supporting policies that keep local family farms viable.
