Program Description
By opting in, you agree to receive recurring automated and manually initiated text messages from Cheryl Hewes for State Senate.
Messages may include campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer opportunities, advocacy messages, fundraising messages, and get-out-the-vote information.
2.
Consent
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a donation, volunteering, or receiving campaign information.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
3.
Opting Out
You may cancel at any time by replying STOP.
After you send STOP, we may send one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed.
To request assistance, reply HELP or contact the Campaign directly.

Privacy
