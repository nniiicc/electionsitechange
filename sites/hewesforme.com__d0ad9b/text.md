Cheryl isn’t a slick DC politician — she’s a hardworking member of the Ellsworth community who understands the challenges Maine families face every day.
As a health care worker at Northern Light Maine Coast Hospital in Surgical Services, she sees firsthand the importance of reliable, accessible care.
As a bookkeeper and small business owner, she knows exactly what it takes to balance a budget and stretch a dollar.
She has dedicated her life to service — as a former middle and high school teacher, and as a Sunday School teacher at Downeast Calvary Chapel for 27 years.
She is a devoted wife, mother of four, and proud grandmother of 14.
