About Warren
Congressman Warren Davidson is currently Chairman of the National Security & Illicit Finance subcommittee, part of the House Financial Services Committee.
He also serves on the House Foreign Affairs Committee.
Warren was born and raised in Ohio.
After high school, he enlisted in the Army and later earned an appointment to West Point.
After graduation, he was honored to serve as an infantry officer in The Old Guard, the 75th Ranger Regiment, and the 101st Airborne Division.
After the Army, he earned an MBA from the University of Notre Dame and spent 15 years starting, acquiring, and growing manufacturing companies in Ohio.
Warren and his wife Lisa have been married over 30 years and have two adult children.
