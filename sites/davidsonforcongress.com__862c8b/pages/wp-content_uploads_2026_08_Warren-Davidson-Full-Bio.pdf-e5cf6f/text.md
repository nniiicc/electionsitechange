Warren has represented Ohio’s 8th Congressional District since 2016.
He currently serves as Chairman of the National Security & Illicit Finance subcommittee, a part of the prestigious House Financial Services Committee, where he is focused on helping grow our economy, instill fiscal discipline, advance innovation, and limit runaway government expansion that threatens to bankrupt America.
He also serves on the House Foreign Affairs Committee.
After high school in 1988, Warren enlisted in the Army as an infantryman.
As part of the 3rd Infantry Division, he was stationed in Germany in November of 1989 and witnessed the fall of the Berlin Wall.
Warren’s commanding officers recognized his potential and helped him earn an appointment to the United States Military Academy at West Point.
He graduated from West Point in 1995 near the top of his class with an officers' commission, a degree in American history, and minor in mechanical engineering.
Warren and Lisa married the same year and have since had two children who are now adults.
After West Point, Warren went to Army Ranger School and ROP indoctrination in 1996, subsequently leading as an officer in the elite 75th Ranger Regiment, 101st Airborne Division and The Old Guard.
After the Army, Congressman Davidson returned home, worked with his father, and earned an MBA from the University of Notre Dame.
He spent fifteen years starting, acquiring, and growing manufacturing companies in Ohio.
In 2016, he was elected to serve as a Member of Congress in the U.S.
House of Representatives.
Warren believes it is not compassionate to bankrupt America, financially or morally.
In Congress he works to defend freedom against growing attacks and advance a pro-growth economic agenda that keeps America the world’s greatest land of opportunity.
