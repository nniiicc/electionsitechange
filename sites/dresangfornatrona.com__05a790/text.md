About Christopher Dresang
Roots & Service
I was born and raised in Casper.
I went to NCHS, raised my family here, and I've spent my career serving Natrona County kids — in classrooms, in the juvenile justice system, and on the boards working to keep our children safe.
I'm running for House 35 because Natrona County deserves a representative whose loyalties are here — not in Cheyenne, not in Virginia, and not with outside money.
