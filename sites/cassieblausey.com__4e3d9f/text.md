Meet Cassie Cassie Blausey is running for State Representative in Kentucky’s 29th District.
She is running to bring steady, thoughtful, and effective leadership to Frankfort, motivated by putting people over partisanship.
A lawyer by trade and a teacher at heart, her work in education policy has provided her with valuable insights into the challenges facing local families.
Cassie understands the struggles Kentuckians face in accessing quality healthcare, education, and support services.
She is determined to put people first in every policy decision to make real, compassionate, and lasting change.

About District 29 Nestled in southeastern Jefferson County, Kentucky House District 29 is defined by vibrant neighborhoods and natural beauty.
The district includes the neighborhoods of Ashville, Heritage Creek, Seatonville and part of the Fern Creek neighborhood.
District residents enjoy the rolling hills of Charlie Vettiner Park, the peaceful waters of McNeely Lake, and easy access to the Gene Snyder Freeway.
