Cassie Blausey is running for State Representative in Kentucky’s 29th District.
She is running to bring steady, thoughtful, and effective leadership to Frankfort, motivated by putting people over partisanship.
Her work in education policy has provided her with valuable insights into the challenges facing local families.
Cassie understands the struggles Kentuckians face in accessing quality healthcare, education, and support services.
She is determined to put people first in every policy decision to make real, compassionate, and lasting change.
Compassionate Leadership Cassie has lived a life rooted in three core values: curiosity, compassion, and courage.
Her curiosity drives her to listen and understand the challenges people face every day.
Compassion motivates her work to help others and build a more inclusive and caring community.
Her courage emboldens her to stand up for what is right, even when facing tough challenges.

Fighting for the People of Kentucky As a Catholic, Cassie’s faith is a cornerstone of her identity and values.
Her spiritual journey guides her commitment to serving the poor, the vulnerable, and the marginalized.
She believes in a practical government that acts as a force for good by listening to the people it serves.
This belief drives her to address critical issues, such as the healthcare crisis and the loss of benefits for Kentucky families.
Whether Cassie is tackling the growing healthcare crisis, addressing the loss of benefits like Medicaid and SNAP for thousands of Kentuckians, or strengthening our schools and communities, she is committed to fighting for the people of Kentucky.
Helping Every Person Thrive Cassie is running because she believes in creating a better, more compassionate government.
Her goal is to build a government that truly works for the people it serves.
Recognizing that the trust between leaders and the community has been broken, she is prepared to undertake the necessary work to rebuild it.
She will always show up, follow through, and prioritize the needs of Kentuckians.
Cassie believes that by working together, we can make Kentucky a state where every person can thrive.
