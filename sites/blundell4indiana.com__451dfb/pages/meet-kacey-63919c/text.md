DONATE A little about Kacey...
Kacey Blundell is a wife, mother, and civil servant.
Kacey has lived in District 38 for over 20 years.
While not originally from the Hoosier state, through her professional career she has not only watched the needs of Hoosiers increase but has stood alongside them in getting necessary help and services to improve Hoosier lives.
Kacey graduated from Terre Haute North Vigo School and Indiana State University.
While at Indiana State University, Kacey immersed herself in the political world by assisting on campaign work and becoming an intern with the Indiana State Senate. ​ Kacey currently works in the local criminal justice system and is actively helping those obtain not only better jobs but help them secure positive and productive futures post serving their debt to society.
As the wife of a public school teacher and mom of 2, Kacey understands that when your local schools succeed the whole community succeeds.
Both Kacey and her husband are active in their community outside of their everyday jobs.
One can see them volunteering or coaching a local sports team.
