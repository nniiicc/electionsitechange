Where is House District 34?
House District 34 is located in Cobb County and includes portions of Kennesaw, Marietta, Acworth, and surrounding communities. ﻿ Who represents me in Washington?
House District 34 is represented in Washington, D.C. by Georgia’s two U.S.
Senators, Raphael Warnock and Jon Ossoff, as well as members of the U.S.
House of Representatives.
The district falls within both Congressional District 11, represented by Barry Loudermilk, and Congressional District 14, represented by Clay Fuller.
Can I watch floor sessions or committee meetings online?
Yes.
Floor sessions and many committee meetings are available to watch online through the Georgia General Assembly’s website, allowing you to stay informed and engaged in the legislative process.
