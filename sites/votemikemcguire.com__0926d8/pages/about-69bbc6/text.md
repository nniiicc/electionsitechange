Meet Mike McGuire
When Northern California needs results, Mike McGuire delivers.
Mike wasn’t the best student, but he is a champion for our kids and public schools.
Mike helped spearhead the creation of tens of thousands of new childcare slots, invested record funding for public schools, implemented free universal school meals for every public school student in the state, expanded tuition-free college for tens of thousands of students, and developed the plan that saved one of Northern California’s top universities from going under.
While Donald Trump and his Republican accomplices in Congress cut federal firefighters, Mike has led the way making California more wildfire safe—doubling the number of CalFire firefighters, making sure we have a full-time firefighting force for a full-time fire season, deploying a record number of fire prevention projects across California, getting more state fire engines on the road, and putting more firefighting aircraft in the sky.
At 16, Mike began working full-time, helping put himself through college.
At 19, fed up with the crumbling schools he attended, Mike ran for School Board — and won.
That set him on a lifelong path of public service rooted in community, hard work, and results.
As a County Supervisor, Mike fought to keep local hospitals open, improve public schools, and protect open space.
In the State Senate, he’s delivered billions for wildfire recovery and prevention, expanded rural healthcare, and invested in affordable housing and education.
Mike continues to deliver for rural California — leading on climate resilience, holding PG&E accountable, and making sure small towns get their fair share.
On the personal side, Mike and his wife Erika are raising their son Connor, with the help of Mike’s mom, in the one-stop-sign town of Geyserville.
They share their lives with Gertrude – their senior citizen pug – and a bevy of chickens.
