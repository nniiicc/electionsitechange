Wyoming Needs Steady, Responsible Leadership
After 12 years in the Wyoming House, I stepped away following my Senate race.
I didn’t plan to return.
But after watching the direction of the Legislature—and hearing from many of you—I believe House District 20 once again needs experienced, common-sense conservative leadership.
We need steady leadership focused on real solutions—not distractions.
During my time in the House, I delivered results for Sublette County:
- Protected our county during redistricting
- Supported our minerals industry, agriculture, tourism and wildlife
- Defended our water resources
- Strengthened rural healthcare
- Supported education
- Shepherded responsible property tax relief
- Made responsible budget decisions
I’m a proven, common-sense conservative who believes in local control, transparency, and getting things done.
That’s why I’m running for House District 20.
I’m not afraid to take a tough stand, and will always tell you what I believe.
Experience
As former Speaker of the House, Albert Sommers focused on jobs, education, Wyoming’s water, local control, responsible property tax relief, and the natural resources of Southwest Wyoming.
He has built the relationships and respect in Cheyenne to once again take the reins for House District 20.
Integrity
Albert Sommers is an independent thought leader who believes in real-life solutions for problems our Wyoming communities face every day, and he will fight for Sublette County and LaBarge.
Community
Albert Sommers has been an advocate for small Wyoming communities throughout his political career.
He recognizes that jobs are essential for families, and that schools are the heart of our small towns.
He believes that the best government is that closest to the people, local control.
