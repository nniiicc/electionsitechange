Support Public Lands and Wildlife
Wyoming’s public lands are a cornerstone of our heritage and our economy.
I’ll fight to keep these lands open in the multiple-use tradition.
I oppose any state or federal legislation or policy that promotes the broad or indiscriminate sale, transfer or exchange of public lands.
I do support responsible, locally supported land exchanges, adjustments or disposals under existing laws and procedures when they demonstrably enhance public access, natural resource use or community benefit.
The working landscapes of Wyoming provide habitat for some of the most impressive game herds in the nation, and our headwater streams provide some of the best trout fishing in the world.
I have been a strong advocate for increased funding to the Wyoming Wildlife and Natural Resource Trust(WWNRT).

Protecting Our Water Resources
Water is the lifeblood of Wyoming—and nowhere is that more important than in the Green River Basin.
Our water supports:
- Agriculture and ranching
- Energy production
- Wildlife and fisheries
- Local communities and future growth
I have a proven record of protecting Wyoming’s water, including helping establish the Wyoming Colorado River Advisory Committee to ensure our state has a strong voice in interstate water discussions.
HB0222 - Colorado river advisory committee.
I will continue to:
- Defend Wyoming’s water rights against outside pressures
- Ensure Wyoming gets its fair share of Colorado River Basin water
- Support responsible water storage and infrastructure
- Protect flows that sustain agriculture, fisheries, and local economies
- Oppose federal overreach that threatens state control of water
The Green River Basin is central to Wyoming’s future.
We must protect it—for our communities, our economy, and the next generation.
