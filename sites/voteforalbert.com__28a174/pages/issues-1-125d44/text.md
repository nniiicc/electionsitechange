The state and local tax revenue generated from the Minerals Industry powers Wyoming, and is critical to funding local government and K12 education.
The Oil&Gas industry began in our valley decades ago in the LaBarge area, and the natural gas fields in Sublette County are some of the largest in the country.
I was a strong supporter of the minerals industry during my time in the Legislature.
Bills I have supported include: SF0037 - Wyoming energy authority SF0018 - Orphan site remediation funding HB0 243 - Oil and gas tax-new production(tax break)(co-sponsor) SJ0003 - Federal suspension and orders on oil and gas production(resolution in support of oil&gas) Support Agriculture Agriculture is the foundation of Wyoming’s spirit, after all we are the Wyoming Cowboys.
Agriculture is an integral part of Wyoming’s economy.
As a fourth-generation Sublette County rancher on both sides of my family, I’ll stand with our farmers and ranchers by protecting Wyoming’s water, supporting the fight against noxious weeds and predators, reducing burdensome regulations, and funding high quality Agriculture programs in Wyoming’s Community Colleges and at the University of Wyoming.
Working agricultural lands protect the landscapes and wildlife that make Wyoming who we are.

Support Public Lands and Wildlife Wyoming’s public lands are a cornerstone of our heritage and our economy.
I’ll fight to keep these lands open in the multiple-use tradition.
I oppose any state or federal legislation or policy that promotes the broad or indiscriminate sale, transfer or exchange of public lands.
I do support responsible, locally supported land exchanges, adjustments or disposals under existing laws and procedures when they demonstrably enhance public access, natural resource use or community benefit.
The working landscapes of Wyoming provide habitat for some of the most impressive game herds in the nation, and our headwater streams provide some of the best trout fishing in the world.
I have been a strong advocate for increased funding to the Wyoming Wildlife and Natural Resource Trust(WWNRT).
