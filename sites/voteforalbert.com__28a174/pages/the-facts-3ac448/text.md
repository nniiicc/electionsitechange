I support both because they provide real value to rural Wyoming.

SF 61 (2023) — Voted Against Legislator Per Diem Increase
✗Voted for legislator Per Diem Increase.
I did support increasing legislator per diem rates.
Those rates had been at $109 per day for years.
Per Diem is intended to reimburse legislators for hotel and meal costs during sessions and legislative meetings. $109 was not even covering hotel costs in Cheyenne during sessions, let alone covering meals.
This was long overdue and is now tied to the federal per diem.
This bill also increased the per diem for all of the members of the appointed boards and commissions.
These individuals volunteer their time, but should be adequately compensated for their hotel and meals.

HB 17 (2024)
✗Voted for limiting number of fishers allowed in a body of water and tracking them to enforce regulations.
I absolutely supported this bill, and in fact it was my concept brought to me by not only fishing outfitters in Sublette County but the general public.
Sublette County rivers are getting overrun by guided boats, mostly out of Jackson.
This bill was designed to protect the quality of the fishing experience, the fishery and other aquatic resources and species in this state, by allowing the commission to set limits on the number of commercially guided fishing boats allowed to launch into any river or lake.
Since I left the legislature, this bill has passed and has been signed into law.
