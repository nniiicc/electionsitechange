Folder: Issues Back All Issues Minerals Agriculture Public Lands Second Amendment Education Pro-Life Protect Girls' Sports Voter Integrity Water The Facts About Contact Donate About Albert Sommers Albert Sommers has lived on a Sublette County ranch all of his life .
All four grandparents were early homesteaders in the Green River Valley.
Sommers attended high school in Sublette County and graduated with honors from the University of Wyoming in 1981 with a Bachelor of Science degree in Electrical Engineering.
After university, he returned home to help run the family ranch.
Albert and his family have been awarded several conservation awards, including the 2012 Wyoming Leopold Conservation/Environmental Stewardship Award, the 2013 Landowner of the Year Award from the Wyoming Game and Fish Department , the 2012 Outstanding Contributions to Sage Grouse Initiative , the 2019 Kurt Bucholz Conservation Award from the Wyoming Stock Growers Agricultural Land Trust , and the 2022 National Private Lands Fish and Wildlife Stewardship Award from the National Association of Fish and Wildlife Agencies.
A lifetime of experience working for Sublette County and Wyoming Leadership positions Albert served six terms in the Wyoming House of Representatives Leadership positions Majority Whip, Speaker Pro Tempore, Majority Floor Leader , Speaker of the House 2023-2024 Sublette County GOP Chairman Precinct Committeeman, Precincts 1-3 - Sublette County Legislative Committees Management Council Appropriations Committee Education Committee Minerals Committee Select Committee on School Finance Chairman Broadband Taskforce Data Security Taskforce Opioid Addiction Taskforce Wyoming’s Colorado River Advisory Committee Select Committee on Water Governor-appointed Committees Wyoming Wildlife Taskforce Wyoming Brucellosis Coordination Team Wyoming Livestock Board (2007- 2013) Tri-Governor (Wyoming, Idaho, and Montana) Committee to review the Yellowstone Grizzly Bear Conservation Strategy Wyoming Sage Grouse Conservation Plan Working Group Other Appointments and Positions Sublette County Planning and Zoning Commission (2002-2011; Chairman 2005-2011) Green River Basin Sage Grouse Work Group Rock Springs BLM District Multiple Use Advisory Board Past President, Green River Valley Cattlemen’s Association Past President, Upper Green River Cattle Association …and more Real Solutions—Not Distractions.
