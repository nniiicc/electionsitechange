Reclaiming our voice in Hartford with common sense and local pride.
I’m running for State Senate because I’ve lived what so many people are going through: rising costs, broken promises, and leaders who seem completely out of touch.
I’m not a career politician.
I’m a former Southington Town Council member, a small business owner, and a dad trying to make things better for the next generation.
I started my first business with a pickup truck and a plan I wrote in high school.
I built HQ Dumpsters from the ground up, created jobs, and did what small business owners across Connecticut do every day: worked hard and kept going, even when the system made it harder than it needed to be.
Eventually, I had to sell that business.
And I’ll be honest: it’s easier working for someone else now than it ever was working for myself.
That says a lot about how hard it has become to build and sustain a small business in Connecticut.
In Hartford, I’ll focus on the basics: lowering costs, expanding access to child care and housing, and making sure small businesses and working families actually have a fair shot.
If you're tired of politicians who say the right thing and do nothing, I hear you.
I’m running to change that.
