Affordable Housing and Child Care
Families in the 16th District should be able to afford a home, rent, and dependable child care without giving up the chance to build a future here.
JACK’S STANDARD
Housing and child care policy should help families stay in Connecticut while respecting local needs and protecting essential town services.
WHY JACK UNDERSTANDS
Jack has raised a family and built a business in this region.
He understands that housing and child care costs determine whether families can stay, work, and put down roots in our communities.
WHAT JACK WILL DO
• Support workforce housing near jobs, schools, transportation, and existing infrastructure.
• Expand affordable child care capacity through provider support, workforce training, and practical assistance for families.
• Help towns secure and coordinate state funding for housing, child care, and infrastructure projects that meet local needs.
HOW YOU’LL KNOW IT’S WORKING
Jack will publish an annual district report showing new affordable homes, available child care slots, and the state investments delivered to our communities.
WHAT WOULD HELP YOUR FAMILY STAY AND THRIVE HERE?
Tell Jack how housing or child care costs are affecting your family and what Connecticut should do.
