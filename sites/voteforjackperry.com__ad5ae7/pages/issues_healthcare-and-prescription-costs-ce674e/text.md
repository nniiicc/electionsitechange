JACK’S STANDARD State healthcare policy should lower out of pocket costs, make prices understandable, and hold insurers, pharmacy benefit managers, providers, and state programs accountable for the prices and results families receive.
WHY JACK UNDERSTANDS Jack built a small business, made payroll, and raised a family here.
He knows healthcare costs affect household budgets and the ability of local employers to create good jobs.
WHAT JACK WILL DO • Require clearer prescription prices and stronger accountability from companies participating in state programs. • Expand access to lower cost generics, biosimilars, community care, and telehealth without lowering quality. • Protect Connecticut healthcare coverage and services by reducing administrative waste and directing savings toward patient care.
HOW YOU’LL KNOW IT’S WORKING Jack will publish an annual prescription affordability report tracking price changes, access to lower cost options, and savings delivered to Connecticut families.
