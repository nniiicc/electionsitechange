Strong Public Schools and Career Pathways
Every student in Southington, Cheshire, Wolcott, Prospect, and Waterbury should have access to a strong public school, the support to succeed, and a clear path toward college, training, or a career.
JACK’S STANDARD
Every education investment should help students learn, support teachers, strengthen parent partnership, or connect young people to a future in Connecticut.
WHY JACK UNDERSTANDS
Jack is a parent, small business owner, and job creator.
He knows schools and employers must work together so young people can build a future close to home.
WHAT JACK WILL DO
• Protect classroom funding, special education services, and meaningful parent partnership.
• Expand career and technical education, paid internships, and apprenticeships connected to local employers.
• Invest in early learning and student support so children are ready to learn and prepared for what comes next.
HOW YOU’LL KNOW IT’S WORKING
Jack will publish an annual district education dashboard tracking graduation, career credentials, apprenticeships, and student outcomes after graduation.
