Jack Perry believes in
Responsible Tax Relief and Town Funding
Families and local employers are carrying higher costs while towns are asked to deliver more with limited resources.
JACK’S STANDARD
Tax relief must help people keep more of what they earn without weakening the schools, public safety, and essential services their towns rely on.
WHY JACK UNDERSTANDS
Jack built a small business from a pickup truck, created local jobs, and made payroll.
He knows tax relief only matters if it helps families and local employers while keeping essential town services strong.
WHAT JACK WILL DO
• Audit state fees, mandates, and programs that raise costs without delivering clear value.
• Pursue responsible tax relief for working families and local employers while protecting essential town services.
• Make state funding for the district’s five communities more transparent, predictable, and tied to measurable results.
HOW YOU’LL KNOW IT’S WORKING
Jack will publish an annual district funding report showing what each community receives, what it supports, and whether the state delivered the promised results.
