A Resolution Affirming the Commitment of the Republican Party of Texas to Combatting Anti-Semitism, Support for Honorable U.S.
Military Members, and Texas Military Heroes WHEREAS, the United States and Israel have maintained a strong relationship since 1948, when the United States was the first to recognize Israel as a sovereign country; and WHEREAS, acts of anti-Semitism are on the rise across the United States since the attack from Hamas on Israel; and WHEREAS, American soldiers have been deployed to assist Israel and allies in other ongoing conflicts around the world; and WHEREAS, Texans have always answered the call to protect, serve, and defend the United States in all branches of the military, with more than 1.5 million Texans having answered the call; now THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Republican Party of Texas hereby: 1. affirms our commitment to unwavering support for the sovereign nation of Israel; 2. supports the right of Israel to defend herself and protect her citizens from Hamas and 3.
