Texas GOP Denounces Anti-Semitism and Calls for Congress to Defund the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East (UNRWA) WHEREAS, the Texas State Republican Executive Committee, in December 2023, unanimously passed the “Resolution Supporting Israel’s Right to Exist, Advocating the Destruction of Hamas, and Mourning the Innocent Who Were Murdered”; and, WHEREAS, the State Republican Executive Committee, in December 2023, unanimously passed a second resolution affirming its commitment to unwavering support for the nation of Israel and denouncing all forms of anti-Semitism; and WHEREAS, Israeli intelligence reports shared with multiple media outlets allege that at least 190 workers for the United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East (UNRWA) were Hamas or Islamic Jihad operatives; and WHEREAS, intelligence shows that 12 UNRWA staff members took part in the October 7, 2023 attacks, including nine who worked as teachers in the agency's schools, seven who directly participated in the raid into Israeli territory, during which fighters killed 1,200 people and captured more than 240 hostages, and that one of the raid participants from the UNRWA took part in the kidnapping of a hostage; and WHEREAS, Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu has said that UNRWA is “perforated with Hamas,” has in the past called on the United States, Israel's top ally and the agency's biggest donor, to roll back its support of the UNRWA, and has praised the Donald Trump Administration for defunding the agency; and WHEREAS, During Joe Biden’s presidency, United States taxpayers have been the largest funders of UNRWA, having contributed more than $340 million in 2022, now THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Republican Party of Texas opposes anti-Semitism and will always oppose and not associate with individuals or groups which espouse antiSemitism or support for attacks on Israel; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Republican Party of Texas calls on President Joe Biden and the Texas delegation to Congress to eliminate U.S. funding to UNRWA; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Republican Party of Texas reiterates its support for the nation of Israel and condemnation of Hamas and its terrorist attacks.
