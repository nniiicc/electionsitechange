Cecilia Castellano, a devoted advocate for public education, affordable healthcare, and economic development, officially declares her candidacy for State Representative in HD80.
With a faith-based commitment to serving the people of our district, Cecilia presents a comprehensive platform that prioritizes the needs and aspirations of the community.
Cecilia Castellano firmly believes that investing in our public schools is investing in our future.
Her educational platform stands on five pillars: She is an ardent supporter of public schools, vowing to reject vouchers that undermine public education.
She will strive to increase teacher pay and student allotment for all school districts.
She will fight for significant school safety investments to foster safe learning environments in our schools.
Cecilia is also dedicated to ensuring that educational opportunities for disabled and special needs students are expanded and enriched.
She aims to improve graduation rates and enhance access to technical trades and pathways to military careers, empowering the next generation with diverse opportunities.
Recognizing the pressing healthcare needs of rural Texans, Cecilia Castellano advocates for Medicaid expansion to provide essential healthcare services to those in need.
She is committed to safeguarding rural hospitals from closures and fostering access to quality healthcare for all.
Addressing the shortage of doctors and nurses is a key aspect of her healthcare agenda
Cecilia is a staunch supporter of legislation that upholds patient rights and curtails excessive fees and charges for healthcare.
She will take a community collaboratives approach to address our challenges with mental health, homelessness, drug addiction, and teenage pregnancy, ensuring comprehensive care for every citizen.
Cecilia Castellano is President of AZTECA Designs & Construction.
As a firm proponent of economic development, she pledges to bring jobs and prosperity to rural Texas.
She envisions abundant small business opportunities through collaboration with the state government.
By working together regionally, she aims to secure our community’s fair share of funding for broadband development, water infrastructure, and essential road, highway, and bridge projects.
Property tax relief is a cornerstone of her economic vision, providing homeowners and business owners with financial stability.
She adamatly supports the bipartisan tax relief bill passed by the Texas Legislature and asks voters to approve this legislation in the upcoming November Constitutional Election.
