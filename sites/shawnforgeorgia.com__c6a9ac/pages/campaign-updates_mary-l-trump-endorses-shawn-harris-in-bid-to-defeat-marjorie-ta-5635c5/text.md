Rockmart, GA — Congressional candidate Shawn Harris today announced the endorsement of Mary L.
Trump — bestselling author, psychologist and outspoken critic of extremism in American politics — in his campaign to unseat Rep.
Marjorie Taylor Greene.
“Shawn Harris has shown the courage, integrity, and commitment to our country that we desperately need in Congress,” said Mary Trump.
“He’s a veteran, a leader, and someone who understands the mission of serving Georgia families who work hard for their communities.
Marjorie Taylor Greene has brought nothing but chaos and cruelty to the people she was elected to serve.
Shawn offers Georgia’s 14th District a chance for dignity, decency, and real representation.”
Harris, a veteran and Polk County cattle farmer, welcomed the endorsement as further proof that momentum is building behind his campaign.
“I am deeply honored to have Mary Trump’s support,” said Shawn Harris.
“She has been fearless in speaking truth to power, and her voice underscores what’s at stake in this race.
Together, we are building a movement that rejects division and extremism and fights for opportunity, security, and accountability for every family in Northwest Georgia.”
The endorsement marks a high-profile boost to Harris’s campaign, which has already drawn national attention with recent appearances on In the Hot Seat and First of All with Victor Blackwell on CNN.
In just 27 days during the last fundraising quarter, Harris outraised Rep.
Greene’s three-month total.
Harris emphasized that his campaign is about restoring dignity to the district and making sure Georgians have a representative who works for them — not for their own fame or personal agenda.
