A Plan For Our Future
With over two decades of experience in both the Executive and Legislative branch, Danny knows what works and what doesn’t work.
Hawai’i needs simple, straightforward, fair and easy policies that we can count on to secure a better tomorrow for us, today.
Click the sections below to find out what Danny will fight for on Day One.
- Lower the cost of housing, healthcare, education, energy, and food.
Hawai’i is too expensive and something needs to be done about it.
- Hawai’i works best when its people are working in jobs that pay well and allow people to save, invest, and look forward to a future where anything is possible.
We must support innovation, empower small local businesses, and educate our young people with the competitive skills to build a stronger economy.
- Hawai’i needs housing for everyone who wants a home and the robust infrastructure to support our communities in order to be successful.
We must ensure that we start building the future, now.
- Keeping our state free from crime, prepared against natural disasters, and well-stewarded in the environment is essential to our future.
- Hawai’i should have the highest level of transparency and accountability.
Taxpayers want their government to be honest and fair, and Danny will fight to make sure Hawai’i is worthy of our people.
No One Left Behind
Faith and hope are essential to the future of Hawai’i, but more and more people are reporting feelings of desperation about their daily lives and have a sense that our state is headed in the wrong direction.
Government without purpose, vision, or compassion forgets the principles of the Declaration of Independence which tells us that America was founded for the pursuit of happiness.
We cannot allow anyone to be left behind.
Danny will give the people a government they can be proud of, and a future worth looking forward to.
