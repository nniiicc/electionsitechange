Priorities Issues Michigan families are in crisis right now.
Here’s where Hassan stands.
1.
Affordability People should be able to afford to live in the communities they helped build.
Housing, groceries, insurance, utilities, childcare and healthcare all became a lot more real to me when I became responsible for more than myself.
Lower the cost of car insurance — Michigan families pay among the highest rates in the country Fight the rising cost of groceries, utilities and childcare that squeeze working families Keep housing within reach for families trying to buy their first home here 2.
Small Business Taxes aren’t theoretical when you’re the one writing the check.
Regulations aren’t words on paper when you have a customer waiting.
I built Printworks Industries from the ground up, so I know what it costs to keep the doors open.
Give small businesses a chance to grow without fighting unnecessary bureaucracy Keep taxes and fees predictable so owners can plan, hire and invest 3.
Schools & Our Kids Parents should have confidence in their schools.
Schools matter differently when you start thinking about where your own child will sit in a classroom.
Cut the regulations burying teachers so they can get back to teaching Give parents a real voice in their children’s education 4.
Real Paths for Young People Not every young person takes the same road.
They should all have a real one.
Build up the skilled trades and career training alongside college Support paths into military service and entrepreneurship 5.
Roads & Infrastructure That Actually Get Fixed When people pay taxes for roads, sewers and infrastructure, they should actually see results in West Dearborn and Dearborn Heights — not years of excuses.
6.
The Constitution Belongs to Everybody A mosque deserves the same protection as a church.
A church deserves the same protection as a synagogue.
And someone who doesn’t practice a religion at all has the same constitutional rights as someone who does.
