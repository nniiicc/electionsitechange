The borough is divided across several congressional districts, and each one covers a different set of neighborhoods.
That is why "who is my congressman in Queens" does not have a single answer, and why the reliable way to find yours is by address.
Finding your district and your representative Enter your address at voterlookup.elections.ny.gov to get your congressional district number.
Once you have the number, the House of Representatives directory at house.gov lists the current member for every district in the country, with their office contact details.
The 6th District New York's 6th Congressional District covers central and northeastern Queens, including Flushing, Forest Hills, Rego Park, Elmhurst, Fresh Meadows, Jackson Heights, Kew Gardens Hills, Bayside, Auburndale, Oakland Gardens, Maspeth, Middle Village and Woodside.
