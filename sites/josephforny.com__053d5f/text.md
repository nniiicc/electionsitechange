The Common Sense Candidate for Queens
As a candidate with a history as a naturalized citizen, I deeply appreciate the rich tapestry of cultures that define our beloved district.
Inspired by my faith and a strong calling to service, I am dedicated to championing policies that honor our community's diversity while promoting safety, prosperity, and unity.
While my principles are guided by my Christian beliefs, my policies aim to resonate with everyone, reflecting our common aspirations and shared values.
New York's 6th Congressional District covers central and northeastern Queens.
Flushing, Forest Hills, Rego Park, Elmhurst, Fresh Meadows, Jackson Heights, Kew Gardens Hills, Bayside, Auburndale, Oakland Gardens, Maspeth, Middle Village and Woodside.
I have built my life and my small business in Queens, and these are the communities I am asking to serve.
Four commitments shape this campaign.
The economy and affordability.
A balanced tax policy that fosters growth and lightens burdens, particularly for our working families and those launching small businesses, together with fair student loan interest rates.
Strengthening families.
Empowering parents in educational and health decisions, with better access to healthcare and affordable childcare.
Education.
Support for public, private and alternative pathways, with curricula spanning vocational training, arts and sciences.
Immigration and the border.
A fair, effective immigration system that honors the dignity of all who seek a life here, stronger inter-agency cooperation on criminal threats, and reform of the cashless bail system.
