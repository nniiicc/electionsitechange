To the Citizens of Missouri and District 32, On behalf of the Missouri Sheriffs’ Constitutional Firearms Alliance (MSCFA), we are proud to formally announce our strong endorsement of Senator Jill Carter for re-election to the Missouri Senate.
The MSCFA was founded by county sheriffs with a clear, uncompromising mission: to protect the constitutional, God-given right of law-abiding citizens to keep and bear arms, and to ensure that public safety policies focus on stopping violent criminals rather than burdening responsible gun owners.
In Senator Carter, the citizens of Southwest Missouri have a tireless defender of the Second Amendment who consistently backs up her principles with bold legislative action in Jefferson City.
Senator Carter’s legislative record demonstrates her unwavering commitment to defending our constitutional liberties: PROTECTING GUN OWNERS’ PRIVACY (SB 1128) Senator Carter took a decisive stand against the weaponization of the financial system by introducing the Second Amendment Financial Privacy Act .
This critical legislation prohibits government entities from keeping registries of privately owned firearms and bans predatory payment networks from using tracking codes to isolate, flag, or discriminate against law-abiding gun retailers and their customers.
STRENGTHENING RIGHT-TO-CARRY PROTECTIONS (SB 1129) Senator Carter has consistently pushed to fortify Missouri’s concealed carry laws.
She understands that a safe community is one where law-abiding citizens are empowered to protect themselves and their families without unnecessary bureaucratic overreach.
As sheriffs, our first and most sacred oath is to uphold the Constitution of the United States and the State of Missouri.
We know firsthand that protecting public safety and defending the right to self-defense go hand-in-hand.
Senator Carter has proven time and again that she will never waver in her defense of the working families, farmers, and gun owners of District 32.
We urge the voters of Newton, Jasper, and surrounding counties to stand with a proven, common-sense leader who fights for our freedom.
Please join us in supporting and re-electing Senator Jill Carter.
