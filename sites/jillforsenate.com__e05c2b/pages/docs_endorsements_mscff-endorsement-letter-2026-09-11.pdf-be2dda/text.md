September 11, 2026 Senator Carter, On behalf of the Executive Board of the Missouri State Council of Fire Fighters representing over 8,000 firefighters, paramedics, EMTs, and dispatchers across the state of Missouri, we are very proud to enthusiastically endorse your candidacy for re-election to the Missouri State Senate.
As a non-partisan organization, the MSCFF and the entire International Association of Fire Fighters takes very seriously our commitment to the citizens we protect, and our duty to evaluate each and every candidate and race individually on behalf of our membership.
We make our endorsements based not only on a candidate’s alignment with our core issues, but also on their ability to be an effective legislator once the election is over.
The women and men of the Missouri State Council of Fire Fighters (MSCFF) are pleased to support your campaign and we hope to see you continue representing our voice in Jefferson City.
The members of our association are honored to protect the communities we serve, and we believe your service to the Missouri Senate will come with the same commitment and pride.
Throughout your term as Senator, you have shown you are willing to listen to the issues affecting Missouri’s Fire Fighters and committed to getting our first responders the tools they need to do their jobs.
We know you will continue that commitment in the Senate going forward.
We look forward to continue working with you.
Fraternally, Cory Hogan Legislative Advisor Missouri State Council of Fire Fighters
