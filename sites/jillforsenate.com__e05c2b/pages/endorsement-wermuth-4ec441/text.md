Endorsement “Senator Jill Carter is still my choice.” Pastor Daniel Wermuth · Joplin As a pastor, I have learned that true leadership is revealed in moments that seldom make headlines.
It is seen in how a person serves when no one is watching, how they respond to those who are hurting, and whether they remain faithful to the people they have been entrusted to serve.
That is why I am honored to endorse Senator Jill Carter.
Over the years, I have witnessed her heart for people.
She has always been accessible, willing to listen, and committed to helping find solutions when families and communities faced difficult circumstances.
I know she has traveled for hours to help deliver life-saving assistance to a family in crisis.
Quietly sit with those whose hearts were broken, sharing their tears without seeking recognition.
I have seen her rejoice with those celebrating life’s blessings and stand faithfully beside others during seasons of loss.
Those moments speak more loudly than campaign promises ever could.
Scripture teaches that those who lead should do so as servants.
Jesus Himself said, “Whoever desires to become great among you shall be your servant.” Throughout her years of public service, I have seen Jill Carter strive to live out that principle.
She understands that representative government exists to give voice to the people it serves.
She listens with humility, leads with conviction, and serves with integrity.
As someone who has walked alongside her, I also recognize that her faith in Jesus Christ is not simply a statement she makes—it is the foundation from which she serves.
I have watched God’s grace shape her compassion, strengthen her resolve, and guide her commitment to the people of Missouri.
In every position of leadership, character ultimately outweighs credentials.
Compassion outlasts campaigns.
Integrity builds trust.
For these reasons, Senator Jill Carter has earned mine.
I wholeheartedly believe she will continue to represent the people of Senate District 32 with wisdom, courage, and a genuine servant’s heart.
It is my privilege to recommend her and to give her my wholehearted endorsement.
