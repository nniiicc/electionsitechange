The Record · Straight from the Senate Journal Gas-Station Gambling Machines Senate Amendment 11 to Senate Bill 30 · April 5, 2023 Unregulated slot-style machines have spread through Missouri gas stations and convenience stores.
No age limit.
No supervision.
No state oversight.
Local news showed kids playing them.
On April 5, 2023, the Missouri Senate voted on Senate Amendment 11 to Senate Bill 30 — an amendment to put those machines under state licensing and regulation.
These requirements are quoted from the amendment’s own text: THE AMENDMENT’S OWN TEXT REQUIRED: “No person under twenty-one years of age shall play video lottery games” — with mandatory adult supervision Machines “placed in a fully enclosed room that is continually monitored by video surveillance and where access to persons under twenty-one years of age is denied” Posted warning signage: “YOU MUST BE AT LEAST 21 YEARS OF AGE TO PLAY VIDEO LOTTERY GAMES” Self-exclusion protections for problem gambling Fines, license suspension, and revocation for violators — with enforcement funding JILL CARTER VOTED YES to regulate the machines and protect kids.
The amendment failed, 11–20.
Not one of those protections became law.
THREE YEARS LATER, THE STATE CAUGHT UP.
April 2023 — Carter votes to regulate the machines and keep kids off them.
The amendment fails, 11–20.
February 2026 — A federal judge rules the machines are illegal gambling devices.
February 2026 — Attorney General Catherine Hanaway sues five retailers for operating them.
April 2026 — Facing prosecution, the state’s largest operator shuts its games off.
She saw it in 2023.
The state acted in 2026.
