Endorsement · September 26, 2026
Representing more than 8,000 firefighters, paramedics, EMTs, and dispatchers
“I’m honored to receive the endorsement of the Missouri State Council of Fire Fighters, representing more than 8,000 firefighters, paramedics, EMTs, and dispatchers.
It’s been an honor to work with rural and urban fire districts, to work with a local fire chief to ensure families could honor fallen firefighters, and to visit with EMTs and pop into fire stations and ambulance stations.
It’s been an honor to celebrate their successes and to listen when they brought real concerns to the table.
That is the job.
“Our EMTs, firefighters, and police are the backbone of every community in District 32.”
Our EMTs, firefighters, and police are the backbone of every community in District 32, and I want them to know how much they are appreciated.
Thank you, MSCFF.
Let’s keep working.” — Senator Jill Carter
