Last updated September 17, 2026.
What you provide The contact form asks for your name, email address, message, and an optional phone number.
It also offers a separate, optional checkbox to request campaign text messages.
The updates form asks for your name and email address and records your request for campaign email updates; it does not enroll you in text messages.
Where it goes When you submit either form, the information goes to FormSubmit, an external form-delivery service, for forwarding to ShawnPackardfor64@gmail.com .
FormSubmit says it retains submissions for up to 30 days.
Copies received in the campaign mailbox may remain there.
The forms submit without leaving this website and display a confirmation when FormSubmit accepts the request.
If an error appears, try again or contact the campaign at the address above or 207-431-8012 .
Your choices Do not include payment-card details, government identifiers, or other sensitive information in these forms.
To ask about a submission or withdraw an email-update or text-message request, contact the campaign at the email address above.
