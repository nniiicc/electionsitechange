Too many families feel that despite promises of relief from Austin, their actual tax bills remain unpredictable and overwhelming. ​ A Proven Council Record: On the City Council, Alan kept his promise to never vote for a tax increase.

Alan believes that protecting our communities requires a comprehensive approach that addresses both immediate crime and the underlying issues affecting our quality of life. ​ Broad Public Safety Initiatives: Alan will work to combat human and child trafficking, crack down on uninsured drivers, address the rise of homelessness, and support mental health initiatives for local youth.
Strong Support for Law Enforcement: Proudly endorsed by the Fort Worth Police Officers Association, CLEAT, and the Tarrant County Law Enforcement Association, Alan will always ensure our local police, deputies, and first responders have the training, resources, and community backing they need to protect our neighborhoods.

Smart growth: Alan will advocate for practical policies that ensure local infrastructure development keeps pace with new residential growth, preserving our community’s livability and affordability.
Supporting Quality Local Schools & Empowering Parents ​ Our children are our future, and every child in Tarrant County deserves access to an excellent education that prepares them for a successful career and life.

Empowering Families: Alan believes in strong partnerships between parents, teachers, and school boards to ensure that local schools remain responsive to the values of the communities they serve.
