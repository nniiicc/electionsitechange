top of page HOME GET INVOLVED MEET ALAN ISSUES ENDORSEMENTS Donate Meet Alan Dedicated to Tarrant County families Alan Blaylock is a husband, father, local businessman, and former Fort Worth City Councilman who believes state government should work for the hardworking families who keep Texas strong.
For Alan Blaylock, Tarrant County isn’t just a spot on a map, it’s home.
Raised right here in Fort Worth, Alan learned early on the values of hard work, personal responsibility, and community service.
It’s where he and his wife chose to build their lives, raise their family, and grow a successful business. ​ Alan understands that effective leadership means setting clear priorities, making disciplined decisions, and delivering results within real-world budgets and timelines.
At a time when families are making tough choices at the grocery store, at the gas pump, and when paying utility bills, the government should be just as careful with taxpayer dollars.
Alan brings a practical, results-driven approach to public service, focused on responsible budgeting, accountability, and making sure every dollar is used wisely. ​ Driven by a desire to give back to the community that gave him so much, Alan stepped up to serve on the Fort Worth City Council.
During his time on Council, he didn’t just talk about relief—he delivered it.
Alan stood firm as a champion for your wallet, maintaining a 100% voting record for lower property taxes, fighting duplicative government red tape, and consistently opposing budget increases that would burden local families. ​ Today, Alan is running for the Texas House of Representatives because our state needs common-sense leaders who focus on the practical issues that affect our daily lives.
He is committed to bringing his business background and local government track record to Austin to hold insurance companies accountable, secure permanent property tax relief, invest in critical local infrastructure, and keep our neighborhoods safe. ​ Alan and his family remain deeply involved in local charities, supporting programs for children’s health and helping victims of domestic abuse.
He is ready to work tirelessly to ensure Texas remains an affordable, safe, and prosperous place for the next generation to call home.
Blaylock Chaired the City of Fort Worth Audit and Finance Committee, served as the President of the Fort Worth Local Development Board, and served as a member of the Regional Transportation Council.
Previously, Alan served on the Heritage HOA Board and the Crime and Control Prevention District Advisory Committee.
He built a tech career as a Senior Product Manager at Nokia and a Project Manager at Owen Oil Tools.
