My story.
1963 - 1987 I was born in Los Angeles, grew up in Irvine, and graduated from Newport Harbor High School in 1981.
I attended UC Irvine for a couple years before I transferred to the Art Center College of Design in Pasadena.
I graduated with a Bachelor of Fine Arts in 1987.
1988 My mom made a brilliant decision when she bought property on Swan River.
I fell in love with it immediately and spent every summer vacation here.
It was, and still is, my happiest, most peaceful place.
1988 - 2013 I worked as a Creative Director in New York and Chicago, creating and shooting commercials.
I still have that belt.
1998 My daughter was born.
And nothing was ever the same.
2017 - today I moved to Bigfork full-time to care for my mom who started showing signs of dementia.
For the last two years Mom has been living at a memory care facility in Kalispell.
I’ve been working remotely for out-of-state clients, as well as hostessing for my friends at Bigfork Wine & Whiskey Lounge.
2025 When I turned 60 I decided to get a Master’s in Psychology — I’ve always been fascinated by human interactions and behavior.
I had such a great time learning and writing, and I feel like I have a better understanding of the dynamics that happen every day.
So the mess of student loans was worth it.
Last summer Charlie & Lucy living their best life.
