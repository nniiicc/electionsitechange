Campaign Platform
District 76
Michigan’s 76th House District is home to more than 92,000 residents across Eaton County’s cities, townships, and rural communities — places where families work, raise children, operate small businesses, and stay rooted to their local traditions.
From the established communities of Olivet, Eaton Rapids, Charlotte, and Potterville, to the residential and agricultural areas throughout Delta Township, Windsor Township, Eaton Township, and nearly all of Oneida Township, the district reflects a broad cross-section of Mid-Michigan life.
It also includes Walton, Brookfield, Hamlin, Carmel, Vermontville, Chester, Benton, and Eaton Rapids Townships, along with the southeastern portion of Grand Ledge.
Established in 1965, the 76th District has long brought together small towns, farmland, neighborhoods, and local institutions that define Eaton County.
Issues
Bill Kaiser is running to represent the 76th District because he believes these communities deserve honest, practical leadership — leadership that respects local decision-making, understands the realities of working families, and focuses on results that matter close to home.
