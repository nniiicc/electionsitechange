Tony Viola is a respected real estate professional, community leader, and lifelong advocate for the Northern Panhandle of West Virginia.
A Follansbee native and graduate of Brooke High School and West Liberty University, Tony built his career on a strong foundation of hard work and service.
As Broker and Owner of Howard Hanna Mortimer Realty, Tony has led the firm to the forefront of real estate in Hancock and Brooke Counties.
His leadership has earned him induction into Howard Hanna’s Champion’s Club and recognition as Realtor of the Year by the Weirton Daily Times and Herald-Star in 2021 and 2023.
Tony’s dedication extends well beyond real estate.
He has served on boards including the Kiwanis Club of Wellsburg, Brooke County Chamber of Commerce, and United Way of the Upper Ohio Valley.
His volunteer work with the Mountaineer Food Bank, Salvation Army, and Toys for Tots reflects his commitment to helping neighbors in need.
Faith is central to Tony’s life.
As an active member of the Promise of Victory Church of God, he approaches service with compassion and conviction.
Whether through community programs, youth mentorship, or revitalization efforts, Tony’s leadership is driven by his belief in a stronger, more connected future for the region. “ I’m a small business owner and lifelong West Virginian who’s spent my life working hard to build a future for my family and community.
I understand the struggles and rewards of building something meaningful.
I’m running to bring common-sense solutions to the table—solutions that focus on community over partisanship and prosperity over political division.
It’s time to create a West Virginia that works for everyone , where every person has a fair shot at success.” - Tony Viola WHY TONY IS RUNNING Tony Viola is running for State Representative to give the Northern Panhandle a strong, dependable voice in Charleston.
His career in real estate and service on local boards have shown him both the challenges and the opportunities facing District 2.
From helping small businesses thrive to ensuring safe, healthy communities, Tony believes the district deserves a leader who listens, acts, and delivers results.
Guided by faith and community values, he is prepared to fight for resources, policies, and solutions that will make the Northern Panhandle the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
