Mail-in Ballot Voting - Click here for updates Early Voting - June 11-18th Primary Election Day - June 23 Early Voting starts June 11th and runs through June 18th from 7:00am to 8:00pm every day.
Anne Arundel County has several early vote locations and you can vote at ANY of them.
The closest ones to District 33A are the Odenton Library and the Crofton Library.
Primary Election Day is on June 23rd and polling locations are in each precinct.
