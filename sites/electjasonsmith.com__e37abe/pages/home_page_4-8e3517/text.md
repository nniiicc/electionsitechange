Communities across Missouri thrive when small businesses have more money to invest, hire, and grow.
To ease the burden felt by Main Street and the working class, Rep.
Smith partnered with colleagues and introduced legislation to cut small business taxes so D.C. bureaucrats aren’t telling these businesses how to spend their money.
This bill, the Main Street Tax Certainty Act, levels the playing field for family-owned businesses and puts more working-class Americans back to work.
