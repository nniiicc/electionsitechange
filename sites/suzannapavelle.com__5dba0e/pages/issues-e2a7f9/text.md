District 19 includes Flagler County and Southeast St.
Johns Couny.
Across the district, families are feeling the same pressure: higher household costs, growth that strains infrastructure, and state decisions that make it harder to trust the future.
Suzanna Pavelle’s top priorities are affordability, responsible growth that protects clean water and quality of life, and strong neighborhood public schools.
She is focused on the practical issues families in Florida, and in particular Flagler County and St.
Johns County feel most directly in their monthly bills, daily routines, and hopes for the next generation.
She has been meeting people, and hearing what matters most to them.
These issues are connected.
When insurance, utility, and housing pressures rise, when growth outpaces roads and water systems, and when public schools are weakened, families lose stability and confidence.
Affordability What is Suzanna Pavelle’s position on affordability?
Suzanna believes families in Flagler County and part of St.
Johns County should be able to afford to stay in the communities they helped build.
She is focused on the costs that hit closest to home: homeowners insurance, utility bills, and the ways bad growth decisions drive up household costs.< For too many people in District 19, the basics feel less secure every year.
People are working hard, but insurance premiums, monthly bills, and infrastructure strain keep making it harder to stay rooted where they live.
Suzanna will fight to: Make lowering homeowners insurance costs a top priority Support practical flood-protection and resilience measures Require growth to pay its fair share instead of burdening current residents Learn more Smart Growth, Water, and Natural Heritage What is Suzanna Pavelle’s position on growth and the environment?
Suzanna supports smart growth that protects clean water, respects infrastructure limits, and preserves the natural heritage of Flagler County and St.
Johns County.
She is not against growth.
She is against growth that leaves local families with more flooding, more traffic, more utility strain, and bigger bills.
Responsible growth helps protect both quality of life and household stability.
If growth drives flooding, wastewater strain, and infrastructure backlogs, residents have to pay.
Growth is coming.
Reckless growth is a choice.
Suzanna will fight to: Put infrastructure first before major development approvals Make growth pay its own way Protect wetlands, waterways, and natural flood buffers Learn more Public Education What is Suzanna Pavelle’s position on public education?
Suzanna believes strong neighborhood public schools are essential to a strong future for District 19.
She supports competitive teacher pay, stable classrooms, and real accountability for every taxpayer dollar spent on education.
Public schools are the backbone of strong communities.
Families deserve neighborhood schools that are well supported, teachers who are respected and fairly paid, and classrooms focused on helping every child succeed.
Suzanna will fight to: Fund neighborhood public schools Support competitive teacher pay and strong learning environments Require accountability for every school receiving taxpayer dollars Learn more One Fight for District 19 Families These priorities all come back to one question: can families build a stable life here and trust that government is working for them?
Suzanna is running to lower the costs people feel, protect the places people love, and strengthen the schools families need with practical leadership and local accountability.

Suzanna Pavelle is running for the Florida House of Representatives in District 19 in the 2026 election.
What areas are in Florida House District 19?
District 19 includes Flagler County and part of St.
Johns County.
Community-level references on the live website should be checked against the official district map before publication.
What are Suzanna Pavelle’s top issues?
Suzanna’s top issues are affordability, responsible growth (for instance, infill first) clean water, and strong public schools.
She frames them as one practical fight for family stability and local quality of life.
Why does Suzanna lead with affordability?
She leads with affordability because families feel it immediately in homeowners insurance, utility bills, and the rising cost of staying in the communities they love.
What does Suzanna mean by smart growth?
Suzanna means growth that fits available roads, drainage, water, sewer, schools, and evacuation capacity before major projects move forward.
What is Suzanna’s education message?
Suzanna believes strong neighborhood public schools, competitive teacher pay, and accountability for taxpayer dollars are essential to a stronger future for District 19.
