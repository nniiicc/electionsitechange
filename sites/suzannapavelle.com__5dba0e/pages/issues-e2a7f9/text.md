What is Suzanna Pavelle running for?
Suzanna Pavelle is running for the Florida House of Representatives in District 19 in the 2026 election.
What areas are in Florida House District 19?
District 19 includes Flagler County and part of St.
Johns County.
Community-level references on the live website should be checked against the official district map before publication.
What are Suzanna Pavelle’s top issues?
Suzanna’s top issues are affordability, responsible growth (for instance, infill first) clean water, and strong public schools.
She frames them as one practical fight for family stability and local quality of life.
Why does Suzanna lead with affordability?
She leads with affordability because families feel it immediately in homeowners insurance, utility bills, and the rising cost of staying in the communities they love.
What does Suzanna mean by smart growth?
Suzanna means growth that fits available roads, drainage, water, sewer, schools, and evacuation capacity before major projects move forward.
What is Suzanna’s education message?
Suzanna believes strong neighborhood public schools, competitive teacher pay, and accountability for taxpayer dollars are essential to a stronger future for District 19.
