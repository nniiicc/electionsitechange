The Oath Of Office endorses Suzanna Pavelle for Florida House District 19
Suzanna Pavelle is proud to announce that her campaign has been endorsed by The Oath Of Office.
The Oath Of Office’s endorsement recognizes candidates who complete the certification process associated with the Politician’s Creed, formally take the oath, and continue to act in accordance with those principles on behalf of their constituents.
Those principles include:
- Accountability to constituents
- Integrity and transparency in public service
- Protection of foundational rights
- Open and ongoing communication with constituents
This endorsement is about how public service should work.
It is not based on a specific policy position or partisan affiliation.
It is a commitment to answer to the people who elected you, communicate honestly, and treat public office as a public trust.
“I am proud to receive the endorsement of The Oath Of Office.
Accountability and transparency are not campaign slogans.
They are the basic responsibilities of anyone who asks to serve the public.
I will listen to the people of District 19, communicate openly, and be accountable for the work I do on their behalf.”
As a candidate for Florida House District 19, Suzanna is running to bring practical, accountable leadership to Tallahassee.
That means focusing on the problems families are actually facing — rising costs, insurance, schools, water, growth, and the future of our communities — and being honest about what a state representative can do.
If elected, Suzanna will fight for:
- Accountability to constituents: Regular, open communication with the people of District 19, not just during election season.
- Integrity in public service: Clear explanations of decisions, votes, and priorities — including when the answer is difficult.
- Protection of foundational rights: Equal rights and a fair opportunity for every person to build a meaningful life.
- Responsible, results-focused governing: Attention to the work that affects families directly, instead of letting culture-war politics crowd out real problem-solving.
The Oath Of Office does not participate in policy advocacy, campaign strategy, or campaign management for endorsed candidates.
Its endorsement recognizes a candidate’s commitment to accountability and civic responsibility.
Suzanna believes public office belongs to the people.
She will show up, listen, answer the phone, and work for a safer, more affordable District 19 where families can build a meaningful life.
