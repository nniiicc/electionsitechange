About the endorsement Suzanna Pavelle’s campaign for Florida House District 19 has been endorsed by Jax NOW PAC.
Jax NOW’s 2026 Candidate Recommendations & Endorsements page says its endorsements are based on candidate questionnaire responses and positions across core issue areas including reproductive rights and justice, racial justice, LGBTQ+ rights, economic justice, and constitutional equality.
“I’m proud to share that our campaign has been endorsed by JAX NOW,” Pavelle said.
“This endorsement means a lot to me.
Reproductive freedom, equal rights, LGBTQ+ rights, and safety for women and families affect everyone.” Pavelle said she is running because she is tired of watching politicians and lawmakers attack people’s rights, ignore working families, and make life harder for regular Floridians to live fulfilling lives.
“Our government is supposed to work for us.
All of us,” Pavelle said.
“Thank you to JAX NOW for your trust and support.
