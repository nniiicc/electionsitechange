Transparency
Transparency is the foundation of accountability in public office.
I am committed to making my actions accessible and clear to all voters.
Here, you’ll find direct links to my voting record and campaign finance reports.
It is important to note that campaign donations are limited to $450 per person/PAC every two years.
Small donor committees can only accept donations of $50 per person annually and are allowed to pool their dollars to make larger contributions to candidates.
By keeping you informed, I hope to build trust and ensure that my decisions reflect our shared values.
