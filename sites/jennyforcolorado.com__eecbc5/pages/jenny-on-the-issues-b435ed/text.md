Key Issues and Priorities I am running for re-election to the Colorado State House of Representatives because it is so important that we have leaders willing to put in the hard work for their constituents – work that results in real policies that make sense for real people.
My priorities and the legislation I've championed in my first term focus on affordability, the environment, healthcare, older adults and education. ​ As your representative, I am dedicated to being a fair, responsive, and hard-working advocate for every voice in Northglenn and Thornton.
I believe in building consensus and actively listening to the needs and concerns of our community.
My goal is to ensure that our shared values and priorities guide the decisions I make on your behalf.
Please know that my door is always open.
I welcome your thoughts and am here to help whenever you need.
