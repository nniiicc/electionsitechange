Protecting our Environment Climate change is an urgent challenge that impacts Colorado’s natural beauty, economy, and way of life.
As a parent, I have seen the impacts of poor air quality on my children's health and on our community’s health.
That’s why I went to work for the Colorado Sierra Club on clean energy and clean air initiatives.
While I was on Northglenn City Council, I lead the charge to build the city’s first all electric city hall, save money through energy efficiency and adopt policies to improve our air quality.
In my first term, I ran legislation to improve energy efficiency in our homes and buildings, crack down on corporate polluters who contribute to our poor air quality and was honored to serve as vice chair of the House Energy and Environment committee.
I believe that protecting our environment is a win for both workers and our economy.
Investing in high-quality, recession-proof jobs that support a family means workers can use their hard earned skills, our kids can study for the jobs of the future and we can keep Colorado colorful and resilient for generations to come. ​ ​ ​ Proudly endorsed by: Conservation Colorado, Colorado Nature Action, Colorado Sierra Club, Healthy Air and Water Colorado.
As your representative, you can count on me to: Fight for Clean Air – I will push for stronger pollution standards to protect our health and air quality.
Expand Clean Energy – I’ll work to increase access to affordable, 100% clean renewable energy across Colorado.
Promote Energy Efficiency – I’m committed to advancing energy-efficient infrastructure to save costs and resources.
Strengthen Wildfire Response – I’ll support more investment in wildfire prevention and firefighting to protect our communities.
Create Sustainable Jobs – I will advocate for family-supporting, recession-proof jobs in clean energy and conservation.
Equip Kids for the Future – I’m dedicated to preparing Colorado’s kids for future high-demand jobs through education and training.
Protect Colorado’s Public Lands – I’ll work to conserve our natural landscapes for future generations to enjoy.
My Sponsored Legislation HB24-1341 State Vehicle Idling Standard This bill allows a local government to enact laws that are at least as stringent as, but no less stringent than, the current state vehicle idling standard.
HB24-1370 Reduce Cost of Use of Natural Gas This bill aims to reduce the cost of use of natural gas infrastructure.
SB24-229 Ozone Mitigation Measures This bill limits pollutant emissions from oil and gas operations, modifies air quality requirements are enforced, requires CDPHE to publish additional enforcement reports, establishes community liaisons, and allows for the plugging of marginal oil and gas wells.
HB23-1005 New Energy Improvement Program Changes The Commercial Property Assessed Clean Energy Program (C-PACE) allows certain property owners to obtain financing for new energy improvements, which under current law include energy efficiency improvements and renewable energy improvements.
The bill expands the scope of the program to include resiliency and water efficiency improvements, such as addressing structural integrity, indoor air quality, and storm water control.
HB23-1161 Environmental Standards For Appliances The bill expands the list of appliances subject to statutory Water and Energy Efficiency Standards and phases in prohibitions on the manufacture, distribution, or sale of certain fluorescent lights and heating appliances.
HB23-1294 Pollution Protection Measures The bill updates procedures and requirements for how pollution control measures are regulated and establishes a legislative interim committee to study and make recommendations about how to improve air quality.
SB23-186 Oil And Gas Commission Study Methane Seepage Raton Basin This bi-partisan bill directed a study on the potential to capture methane seepage in the Raton Basin and use water resulting from such capture.
