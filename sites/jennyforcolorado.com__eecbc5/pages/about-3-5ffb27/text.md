House District 34 is home to just over 87,000 people in Adams County - covering all of Northglenn and a portion of Thornton.
With 28 schools, more than 40 parks, hundreds of acres of open space, access to every type of retail, there is something for everyone!
If you live in Northglenn, you are represented by Senator Faith Winter.
If you live in Thornton, you are represented by Senator Kyle Mullica.
