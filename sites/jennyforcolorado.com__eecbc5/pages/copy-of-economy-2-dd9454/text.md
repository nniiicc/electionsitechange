Affordable Healthcare for All Access to affordable, high-quality health care is essential for every Colorado family to thrive - including access to reproductive health care and abortion.
Health care is a human right, and no one should have to choose between paying their bills and taking care of their health.
In recent years, we've made real progress in lowering the costs of insulin and prescription drugs, but there is still more to do.
I am committed to building on this momentum, defending reproductive rights, and fighting for policies that make health care accessible and affordable for all Coloradans so everyone in our community can lead safe, healthy, and fulfilling lives. ​ ​ ​ Proudly endorsed by: Planned Parenthood Votes Colorado, Cobalt, Blueflower Small Donor Committee, Colorado Organization for Latina Opportunity and Reproductive Rights (COLOR) Small Donor Committee, National Association of Social Workers and Nurses Vote.
As your representative, you can count on me to: Work to lower health insurance costs.
Advocate for the continued decrease in the price of prescription drugs.
Ensure that personal healthcare choices remain between women and their doctors, free from government interference and political agendas.
Defend access to birth control, abortion and fertility treatments.
My Sponsored Legislation HB24-1153 Physician Continuing Education This bill brought Colorado into alignment with the rest of the United States by establishing a continuing medical education requirement (CME) for all physicians in our state- leading to better care and better health outcomes for patients.
HB23-1031 Mental Health Professionals Reporting Exemption This bill created an exemption for mental health professionals from requirements to report information about a patient's sexually transmitted infections to public health entities.
HB23-1077 Informed Consent To Intimate Patient Examination s This bi-partisan bill requires that a patient's informed consent be obtained before performing an intimate examination of the patient while unconscious.
SB23-260 Individual Access To Publicly Funded Vaccines This bill prohibits practitioners from requiring individuals to pay or provide identification and health insurance information to receive a publicly funded vaccine.
