Jenny is standing up for Adams County, our families and our future.
Jenny is a mom of two, small business owner, former union member and a dedicated public servant who has spent her career standing up for working families and fighting for clean air and water.
She is the current State Representative for House District 34 which covers all of Northglenn and North Thornton and is running for re-election in 2026.
From pushing for affordable, accessible child care, standing up for students and teachers, and defending our environment, Jenny is committed to fighting for all of us.
