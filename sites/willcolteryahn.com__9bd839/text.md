Our Community Is Changing and Government Needs to Keep Up
House District 40 is growing fast.
What was once farmland is now a thriving community of families, workers, seniors, and small businesses who care deeply about where we live and where we’re headed.
But while our community has changed, too much of state government hasn’t.
Families are paying more just to get by.
Schools are under pressure.
Seniors and people with disabilities are navigating systems that feel harder than they should.
And too often, decisions at the Statehouse seem disconnected from the real challenges people face here at home.
We deserve leadership that understands this moment and is ready to meet it.
My wife and I chose Avon to raise our family because we believed in this community.
Today, we’re raising our four kids here, alongside neighbors who work hard, care for one another, and want a better future for the next generation.
For more than twenty years, I’ve worked in state social services, helping Hoosiers with disabilities, seniors, and families access the support they need to live with dignity and independence.
I’ve seen how smart investments in people strengthen entire communities, and how harmful it is when systems fail the people they’re meant to serve.
I’m running for the Indiana House because government should make life easier, not harder.
It should be a partner in people’s success.
And it should reflect the values of the communities it represents.
What I Stand For
Lower Property Taxes
Homeowners shouldn’t be left paying more while large corporations receive special tax breaks and deals.
We all want corporations, developers, and big-box chains to pay their fair share so we can lower the burden on homeowners without sacrificing essential local services.
Cut Utility Costs
Hoosiers shouldn’t have to pay inflated utility bills to cover lobbying, executive compensation, or projects that may never benefit them.
I wanat to see stronger accountability for Indiana’s regulated utilities and an end to unnecessary costs being passed along to ratepayers.
Support Seniors & People with Disabilities
I have spent more than 20 years helping seniors and Hoosiers with disabilities live independently and with dignity.
I will fight to protect home and community based services while strengthening oversight so the dollars meant for care actually reach the people who need them.
Make Life Easier for Hoosiers
Government should listen to people and make their lives easier, not add another headache.
I pledge to hold regular town halls, remain accessible throughout the year, and make listening to the people of District 40 a central part of how I serve.
