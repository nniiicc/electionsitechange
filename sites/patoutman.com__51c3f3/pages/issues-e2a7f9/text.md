I believe we can get Michigan back on track by restoring affordability, strengthening our infrastructure, and supporting the communities that make our state strong.
Lower Property Taxes Property taxes are one of the biggest financial burdens facing Michigan families, seniors, and small businesses.
I believe people should be able to stay in their homes without being priced out by rising tax bills.
That means taking a serious look at how government spends money, cutting unnecessary costs, and delivering real, long-term tax relief.
Lower Energy Costs Michigan families have seen their energy bills increase year after year.
We need an energy policy that puts affordability and reliability first.
That means pushing back on costly mandates, ensuring utilities are accountable, and making sure families aren’t stuck paying more for less reliable service.
Lower Auto Insurance Michigan drivers pay some of the highest auto insurance rates in the country, and for too long the system has been stacked against them.
Families shouldn’t be forced to pay outrageous premiums while special interests protect a broken system.
It’s time to bring transparency, competition, and accountability to Michigan’s auto insurance system so drivers finally get the relief they deserve.
Rebuild Michigan’s Infrastructure Michigan’s infrastructure is the backbone of our economy — from roads and bridges to water systems and local utilities.
As Chair of the House Transportation and Infrastructure Committee, I was proud to help lead the largest investment in our road system in state history.
But we are just getting started.
I’m focused on long-term solutions that fix our roads, invest in local communities, and ensure taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly.
Strengthen Small Towns Our small towns and rural communities are the heart of Michigan.
But too many are being left behind as opportunities shrink, schools struggle, and young people are forced to look elsewhere for a future.
We need policies that support farmers, small businesses, and local economies — not ones that burden them with unnecessary regulations or drive opportunity out of our communities.
I’m committed to strengthening rural Michigan by supporting local schools, expanding economic opportunity, and making sure our small towns remain great places to live, work, and raise a family.
Government Accountability Michigan residents deserve a government that is transparent, responsible, and focused on results.
