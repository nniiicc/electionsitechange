Michigan was once the economic engine of America.
Our state built industries, created opportunity, and helped millions of families achieve the American dream.
Today, Michigan still has that same potential.
By lowering costs, strengthening infrastructure, and supporting the communities that make our state unique, we can once again make Michigan a place where families thrive and businesses grow.
During my time in the Michigan House of Representatives, I’ve worked to stand up for the people of our state, pushing back against policies that put special interests ahead of Michigan families.
