I am Becky Stille, a life-long Nebraska resident, and Democrat for U.S.
Congress in Nebraska’s 3rd district running to defeat GOP Congressman Adrian Smith.
I know what the people of Nebraska’s 3rd are currently going through, as my family and I are going through the same struggles.
My neighbors and friends are farmers, business owners, factory workers, first responders, service industry workers, students, senior citizens, and working families.
What I have discovered talking with my friends, neighbors and voters is that we all want the same things.
We want everyone in our great state to prosper, and for those we elect to speak for us do just that; listen and represent we the people.
Just last month, a farmer’s land went up for auction right across the road from my house.
Is this the beginning of what is to come?
I’m running in our state to make sure it is not what our future holds for us.
We absolutely deserve better.
We must bring back funding and grants already approved by the U.S.
Congress.
We need to make sure the tax cuts and incentives benefit us.
We need to protect Social Security, Medicare, and SNAP benefits for those who depend on them.
We need to take our schools back and not ban books or rewrite history to suit certain people.
Our past is what has made all of us who we are today.
We must invest in our schools, ensuring every child in Nebraska’s 3rd, from preschool to college, has the tools they need to succeed.
Here’s what I’ll fight for: On healthcare, I’ll expand access to healthcare by funding rural hospitals, lower prescription drug costs, and the ACA.
We must also make mental health care a priority in our society.
I’ll strongly support our family farmers.
As the next Congresswomen from Nebraska’s 3rd, saving family farms will be my top priority.
On jobs, I’ll work hard every day to create good family wage paying jobs and invest in job training for younger workers and veterans in Nebraska’s 3rd.
I’ll be a champion for Davis-Bacon prevailing wages and Project Labor Agreements, because when the labor and business work together we get a better end product.
I will be a strong supporter of women’s rights and pay equity.
I’ll strongly support a woman’s right to make her own health care decisions privately with her doctor.
On our country, America is one where we embrace compassion for current and future generations.
I’ll fight to protect Social Security and Medicare for our senior citizens.
In America, no one person is above the law.
But, right now we have the MAGA / Trump / Smith team who are dividing our country and crippling our democracy.
Trump and Smith are dismantling the Department of Justice Civil Rights Division, the Department of Education, the National Institute of Health, and politicizing the Department of Defense, and other democratic institutions.
Trump and Adrian Smith’s tariff policies are tanking our retirement accounts and threatening increases to our costs of living.
America’s founding fathers and over 200 years of building our democratic institutions are being torn apart in just over 100 days.
Enough!
It’s time to fire Adrian Smith.
I want to be your voice in Washington.
In November 2026, please vote to save America.
Encourage your friends, family, and coworkers to get involved in our campaign and to Vote!
Please spread the word that there is someone willing to speak up and not be afraid of repercussion.
What are my values?
I value the will of the people and what the people of Nebraska’s 3rd district value.
I will listen to you and speak for your values in the U.S.
House.
I will not avoid the tough questions.
I will be open, honest, and I will not hide behind someone else’s mission.
Together we will stand as one, making our positions clear to all who choose to ignore what is important to the rest of us.
I ask for your support and vote.
Thank you and God bless these United States of America.
