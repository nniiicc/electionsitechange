Aaron Hayes Top Priorities:
- As a teacher and parent, Aaron is running to end the "extremist era" and return to the constitutional promise of predictable, reliable support for every Idaho student.
No more political theatrics; just results for our kids.
- Aaron believes in protecting our shared access to the Panhandle National Forests from privatization while rejecting radical environmental withdrawal in favor of responsible management.
- When corporate handouts lead to budgetary crises, our first responders and teachers pay the price.

Aaron opposes the "draconian" attempts to cut these essential services, recognizing that a healthy Idaho requires a robust social safety net—not ideological hurdles that leave our most vulnerable citizens behind.
