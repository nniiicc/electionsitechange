About Kristina A Neighbor.
A Fighter.
A Proven Leader.
Kristina came to America from Ukraine at age five, settled right here in the 47th Legislative District, and never left.
She raised her three children here, built a career as a sonographer and music teacher, and spent six years on the Covington City Council rolling up her sleeves.
She doesn't need to learn what families in the 47th need — she's one of them.
Elected 2019, defeating a 14-year incumbent with 62% of the vote Re-elected uncontested 2023 — her neighbors trust her record Crime fell 24% from peak; burglary down 56% under her watch Strong SRCC and KCGOP support — institutional backing the 47th rarely sees.
Meet Kristina Announcement Video "Six years on the Covington City Council showed me exactly what South King County families are up against." Watch Kristina's official campaign announcement.
From rising costs to roads that aren't getting funded to public safety stretched too thin — she's seen the problems firsthand from the council table, and she's ready to take the fight to Olympia.

31 min Average Daily Commute The 47th District's average commute is 20% above the state average.

Kristina will oppose it and fight to let Washington voters have their say.
