Contact Us Overview Friends of Kristina ("we," "our," or "us") is the official campaign committee for Kristina Soltys's candidacy for Washington State Senate in the 47th Legislative District.
We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits our website, donates to our campaign, or engages with our communications.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.
By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.
We may update this Policy periodically — the date above reflects the most recent revision.
Your continued use of the site after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
Information We Collect Information You Provide Voluntarily When you fill out a form, sign up for updates, make a donation, volunteer, or otherwise engage with us directly, you may provide: Name, email address, postal address, phone number, and/or mobile number Employer name, occupation, and zip code (required for certain campaign finance disclosures) Payment information when making a contribution (processed securely by our donation platform) Any messages, endorsements, or other content you submit through our forms Information Collected Automatically When you visit our site, certain information is collected automatically through your browser and third-party services we use, including: IP address and general geographic location Browser type, operating system, and device information Pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring URLs Interaction data such as clicks and scroll behavior (used in aggregate, anonymous form) This site renders fonts through Google Fonts.
This service may receive your IP address and browser information as part of delivering font files to your browser.
Information from SMS Interactions If you opt into our mobile messaging program, we may collect your cell phone number, your carrier's name, and the date, time, and content of messages you send to us.
See the Mobile Messaging & SMS section for full details.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Send you campaign updates, newsletters, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities you've requested Process donations and comply with campaign finance reporting requirements Respond to your inquiries and support requests Improve the website and understand how visitors use it Send text messages if you've opted into our SMS program We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
SMS opt-in data and consent information will never be shared with any third party.
We may share information as required by law, including campaign finance disclosure obligations under Washington State and federal election law.
Donor information submitted through our contribution pages is subject to public disclosure requirements applicable to political campaigns.
Mobile Messaging & SMS The following terms govern our Mobile Messaging Program.
This language is required by our SMS service provider and must appear as written.
Mobile Messaging Program Consent By signing up for our Mobile Messaging Program, you consent to receive the following types of text messages from Friends of Kristina: Polling/Voting Text Messages: Such as election reminders and opinion polls.
Public Service Announcement Text Messages: Including legislative updates, member updates, and voter education.
Opt-In Methods Completing a sign-up form on our website and checking the text message consent box.
Providing explicit consent in any other manner as indicated by Friends of Kristina.
Message Terms Messages may be sent using an autodialer or similar technology.
Message frequency may vary depending on your interaction with the Program.
Standard message and data rates may apply.
Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
STOP Command To stop receiving messages, text STOP at any time.
You will receive a confirmation text confirming you have been unsubscribed.
After this, you will no longer receive messages from the Program unless you opt back in.
HELP Command For help, text HELP or contact us at kristina@kristinaforsenate.com .
You will receive instructions on how to use the service.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Donations & Financial Information Online contributions to Friends of Kristina are processed through Anedot ( anedot.com ), a third-party campaign fundraising platform.
We do not store your full credit card or banking information on our servers — payment processing is handled entirely by Anedot, which maintains its own security standards and privacy policy.
By making a contribution through our donation page, you are also subject to Anedot's terms of service and privacy policy.
You must be 18 years of age or older to make a contribution.
Processing fees may apply and are subject to change at Anedot's discretion.
All transactions are final and non-refundable.
Washington State and federal campaign finance law requires us to collect and publicly disclose certain donor information — including name, mailing address, employer, and occupation — for contributions above applicable reporting thresholds.
By making a contribution, you acknowledge that this information may appear in public campaign finance filings.
If you provide your email during the donation process, we may send you campaign-related communications.
You may unsubscribe at any time via the link at the bottom of any email.
We send emails through Mailchimp.
SMS opt-in data is never shared with third parties.
Third-Party Services We use trusted third-party services to operate this website and campaign, including: Mailchimp — email marketing and newsletter delivery Google Analytics — anonymized website traffic analysis Google Fonts — web font delivery Anedot — online donation processing Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.
We encourage you to review them if you have questions about how they handle data.
We do not authorize these services to use your information for their own marketing purposes.
Embedded Content Pages on this site may include embedded content such as videos, social media posts, or images hosted by third parties.
Embedded content behaves as if you had visited the originating website directly — those sites may set their own cookies, collect data about you, and track your interactions with the embedded content.
Security We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including SSL/TLS encryption for data transmitted through this site.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use caution when sharing sensitive information online.
Your Rights & Opt-Out You have the right to: Unsubscribe from email communications at any time via the link in any email we send Opt out of SMS messages by texting STOP to any message we send Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you To exercise any of these rights, contact us at kristina@kristinaforsenate.com or by mail at the address below.
Please note that certain information may be retained as required by campaign finance law regardless of a deletion request.
Children This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.
