Read Full Release ↓ Kristina Soltys Announces Campaign for Washington State Senate in the 47th Legislative District April 7, 2026 • For Immediate Release • kristina@kristinaforsenate.com Covington, WA — Kristina Soltys, a Covington City Councilmember, community leader, and mother of three, has announced her candidacy for Washington State Senate in the 47th Legislative District.
Soltys says she is running to bring practical, community-first leadership to Olympia at a time when too many families are struggling to keep up with rising costs, public safety concerns, and a government that feels increasingly out of touch.
"Families across South King County are working hard, doing their best, and still feeling like they are falling behind.
Rising costs, growing safety concerns, and a sense that the government isn't listening have pushed too many families to the brink.
I believe I've been prepared for a time such as this, and I'm ready to fight for the people of this district." Soltys's campaign focuses on affordability, public safety, transportation, economic opportunity, and accountable government.
She was first elected to the Covington City Council in 2019, defeating a 14-year incumbent with 62% of the vote, and was re-elected unopposed in 2023.
"The 47th District is made up of hardworking families who want safe communities, strong schools, better roads, and a fair shot at building a good life.
That is who I have worked for on the City Council, and that is who I will fight for in the State Senate." The 47th Legislative District is currently represented in the Senate by Democrat Claudia Kauffman.
The 2026 Washington State Primary is scheduled for August 4.

Read Full Release ↓ SRCC Endorses Kristina Soltys for 47th District State Senate For Immediate Release • kristina@kristinaforsenate.com Covington, WA — The Washington Senate Republican Campaign Committee (SRCC) has announced its endorsement of Kristina Soltys for Washington State Senate in the 47th Legislative District.
Soltys is a two-term Covington City Councilmember and the only Republican candidate on the ballot heading into the August 4 primary.

The 47th Legislative District has changed parties four times since 2006 and was decided in 2022 by approximately 2,749 votes — making it one of the most competitive Senate seats in Washington State.
"I'm proud to have the support of the Senate Republican Campaign Committee.
This race is winnable, the district is competitive, and we have a record to run on.
I look forward to putting in the work to earn the trust of every voter across Auburn, Kent, and Covington." Soltys was elected to the Covington City Council in 2019 with 62% of the vote against a fourteen-year incumbent and was re-elected unopposed in 2023.
During her tenure, total crime in Covington fell 24% from its 2022 peak, with burglaries down 56%.
She has also been endorsed by the King County Republican Party and King County Council Vice Chair Reagan Dunn.

I look forward to continuing the conversation with her on the issues we both care about." With Goers's decision, Soltys is now the only Republican on the ballot in the 47th District Senate race heading into the August 4 primary.

They need a senator who has been at the table the whole time.
For six years I've been at that table in Covington, fighting for safer neighborhoods, responsible budgets, and roads that actually move.
That's the senator I'll be in Olympia." Soltys was elected to the Covington City Council in 2019 with 62% of the vote and re-elected unopposed in 2023.

Press Release For Immediate Release King County Republican Party Endorses Soltys for 47th District State Senate The KCGOP Endorsement Committee names Soltys as the party's standard-bearer in one of King County's most competitive Senate districts.
Read Full Release ↓ King County Republican Party Endorses Soltys for 47th District State Senate For Immediate Release • kristina@kristinaforsenate.com Covington, WA — The King County Republican Party has endorsed Kristina Soltys, Covington City Councilmember and Republican candidate for Washington State Senate in the 47th Legislative District.
The endorsement, granted by the KCGOP Endorsement Committee, recognizes Soltys as the party's standard-bearer in one of the most competitive Senate districts in King County.
The 47th District, which includes Covington, Auburn, and Kent's East Hill, has changed hands between parties four times since 2006.
Democratic incumbent Claudia Kauffman won the seat in 2022 by approximately 2,749 votes.
Soltys has served on the Covington City Council since 2019, when she defeated a 14-year incumbent with 62% of the vote and ran unopposed in 2023.
Her council record includes unanimous approval of a Flock Safety license plate reader program that has generated more than 60 investigative leads, and contributed to a 24% reduction in total crime from the 2022 peak — with burglaries down 56%.
"South King County families deserve a senator who has actually delivered results in this community, and I'm ready to take that record to Olympia.
I'm grateful for the KCGOP's confidence, and I'm committed to running a campaign that earns support from every corner of this district." The 47th District Senate seat is a primary target for Republican pickup in 2026.
Soltys is the only declared Republican candidate currently in the race.
