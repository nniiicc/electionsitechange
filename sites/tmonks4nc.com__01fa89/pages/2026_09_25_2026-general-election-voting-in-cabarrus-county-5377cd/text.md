First: Check Your Voter Registration
Before you vote, make sure your voter registration information is current.
The North Carolina State Board of Elections’ Voter Search allows you to check your registration, find your polling place, view your sample ballot, and see other election information.
Important deadline: The regular voter registration deadline for the November election is 5 p.m.
Friday, October 9, 2026.
If you miss that deadline, you may still be able to register and vote during the early voting period through North Carolina’s same-day registration process.
Three Ways to Vote
North Carolina offers three ways to vote:
- Early voting in person
- Voting on Election Day
- Voting by mail
Choose the option that works best for you.
Your vote counts the same regardless of which method you use.

2.
Vote on Election Day
Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Polls will be open from:
6:30 a.m. – 7:30 p.m.
Unlike early voting, you must vote at your assigned Election Day polling place.
If you are in line at your assigned polling place by 7:30 p.m., you will be allowed to vote.
Because polling locations can change, check your individual polling place before Election Day rather than relying on where you voted in a previous election.

Vote by Mail

You do not need a specific reason or excuse to vote by mail.

If you plan to vote by mail, don’t wait until the deadline.
Give yourself enough time to request, receive, complete, and return your ballot.
Don’t Forget Your Photo ID
North Carolina voters will be asked to show an acceptable photo ID when voting in person.
Common examples include:
- North Carolina driver’s license
- North Carolina state ID
- U.S. passport or passport card
- Approved college or university student ID
- Approved state or local government employee ID
- Certain military or veterans IDs
- Certain tribal IDs
If you don’t have an acceptable photo ID, you can still vote.
North Carolina provides an exception process for voters who are unable to show an acceptable ID.
The N.C.
State Board of Elections also provides information about obtaining a free voter photo ID.
Know What’s on Your Ballot
Your ballot depends on where you live.
Before you vote, take a few minutes to review your personal sample ballot.
This allows you to see the candidates, races, and questions that will appear on your ballot before you arrive at the polls.
Cabarrus County’s sample ballot lookup is available for the November 3, 2026 election.
You can also use the North Carolina State Board of Elections’ Voter Search tool to find your voter information and polling location.
