The Working Class Party of Maryland welcomes contributions to support its political campaigns.
Donors must provide a name and address.
Campaign contributions are regulated by Maryland campaign finance rules.
In particular, we must report the names and addresses of donors to the Maryland State Board of Elections.
Please send a personal check made out to "Working Class Party" with your current name and address to:
Working Class Party
PO Box 11023
Baltimore, MD 21212
Important: A person (including an individual, business, or federal committee) can contribute no more than $6000.00 to one campaign finance entity during the four-year election cycle.
The four-year election cycle begins on January 1st following a Gubernatorial Election.
Separate limits apply to money transferred between Maryland campaign finance entities.
A campaign finance entity can transfer up to $6000.00 to another campaign finance entity during the four-year cycle and is not subject to an aggregate limit.
