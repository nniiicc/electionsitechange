- Posted October 20, 2025
Working class people have no party of our own, no party that proposes answers to this country’s problems that could serve the class interests of working people.
The two big parties basically represent and serve the needs and desires of the capitalist class: the bankers, corporate tycoons, financial and real estate speculators.
The working class needs its own party.
Elections cannot eliminate the massive problems we face.
Only our struggles and mobilizations can do that.
But Working Class Party can give people a way to say they want workers to have power over the wealth we produce.
Finally, racism, anti-immigrant prejudice, sexism, and homophobia are a cancer nourished inside the working class by our enemies, aimed at dividing and weakening the working class.
We need all our forces standing together to defend ourselves.
