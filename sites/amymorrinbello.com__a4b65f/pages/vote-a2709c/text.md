EARLY VOTING
Early voting will take place in the Wethersfield Town Hall beginning on October 19th.
Electors will be able to vote from 10am to 6pm all days, except Tuesday (October 27th) and Thursday (October 29th) when early voting will be available from 8am to 8pm.
Early voting will end Sunday, November 1st.
VOTE BY ABSENTEE BALLOT
You may vote by Absentee Ballot, no reason needed!
It’s 4 Easy Steps-
1.
Apply for an Absentee Ballot application at https://oabr-sots.ct.gov/OABR/absenteeBallotReqPortalHome.do or in the Town Clerks office.
2.
Submit your Application, in-person or by mail.
3.Vote your Ballot as soon as it's received.
4.
Return your Absentee Ballot, in-person, to a drop box, or by mail.
VOTE AT THE POLLS
If you wish to vote in person, the polls are open from 6am to 8pm on Tuesday, November 3rd.
You can check your Polling location at https://portaldir.ct.gov/sots/lookup.aspx.
For additional voting information, please visit the Secretary of the States website.
