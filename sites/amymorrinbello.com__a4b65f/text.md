top of page Amy for State Rep Home News & Updates Endorsements Voting Information Get Involved I am running for re-election! ​ ​ I am serving my third term representing Wethersfield, CT in the 28th House District.
I serve as Deputy Majority Leader, Vice Chairperson of the Government Administration and Elections Committee and a member of the Transportation and Public Health Committees.
As your State Rep, I have worked hard for Wethersfield residents, paying attention to the needs of our working families and seniors, supporting our public schools, and increasing funding for childcare and additional mental health support for children.
I continue to fight for women's healthcare and our LGBTQ+ community to make sure that all residents' needs are met, services are available, and we all feel safe in our state.
I’m proud to have worked on a budget that adheres to our fiscal guardrails, delivered the largest tax cuts in state history, built up the Rainy Day Fund and prepaid the state's unfunded pension liability. ​ ​The 2025 legislative session was a session of bipartisan work, compromise, and solutions to mitigate what is happening at the Federal level.
We listened to constituents and focused on important issues and we did it responsibly, with a balanced state budget, while remaining dedicated to paying down the state’s pension debt.
We brought millions of dollars back to Wethersfield through school building reimbursement and grants.
As a lifelong resident of Wethersfield, I attended elementary and middle school in town before graduating from South Catholic High School and Providence College.
I am currently employed at Wesleyan University’s Center for African American Studies.
I was employed for ten years as an Assistant Town Clerk and served on the Town Council (2015-2020) and as Mayor of Wethersfield (2017-2019).
My husband Rich and I have three daughters and both sets of our parents are living in Wethersfield. ​ During this campaign, I look forward to connecting with residents about the issues important to our district.I hope to earn your support in November.
