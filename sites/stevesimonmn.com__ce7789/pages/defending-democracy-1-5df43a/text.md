Secretary of State for All
Steve works hard to serve Minnesotans in every corner of our state.
He knows that the best ideas come from all over Minnesota, so he doesn’t just sit behind a desk in St.
Paul.
He regularly visits all 87 counties, and is now in the middle of his tenth statewide tour.
He visits with residents of all ages, views, and backgrounds – at community events, in schools, at small businesses, in factories, and anywhere else he can get feedback about making our democracy better, providing services for businesses, or protecting those who fear for their safety.
