Erik Aadland I’m proud to endorse Mark Herzfeld for Colorado House District 25.
As the Republican nominee for Congress in Colorado’s 7th District in 2022, I know firsthand how rare it is to find leaders with both real-world experience and the willingness to step forward and serve.
Mark has spent a lifetime serving Colorado, from commanding B-52 crews in the Air Force to decades as a commercial airline captain and community leader.
He understands discipline, responsibility, and the practical realities facing Colorado families.
Mark is focused on the issues that matter most: affordability, public safety, energy reliability, educational choice, and accountable government.
HD25 deserves a serious, steady leader grounded in service rather than political theater, and I believe Mark Herzfeld is exactly that kind of candidate.
