Endorsements
Gary Millspaugh - Retired Aurora Police Lieutenant
As a retired Aurora Police Department Lieutenant, I am proud to endorse Mark Herzfeld as Colorado State Representative in House District 25.
Jefferson County deserves a representative who understands service, accountability, and the daily challenges our families face.
Mark’s distinguished career as a B-52 bomber pilot, 787 Captain, and active volunteer with Genesee Fire/Rescue, shows he knows how to lead under pressure and put others first.
He has mentored youth, supported children’s causes for decades, and consistently served our community.
Unlike career politicians, Mark will fight to cut wasteful spending, deliver full TABOR refunds, crack down on repeat offenders, expand housing supply through smart reforms, and protect our schools and forests.
Mark Herzfeld is the experienced, principled leader HD 25 needs.
I wholeheartedly support him and encourage every voter in Jefferson County to do the same.
Erik Aadland
I’m proud to endorse Mark Herzfeld for Colorado House District 25.
As the Republican nominee for Congress in Colorado’s 7th District in 2022, I know firsthand how rare it is to find leaders with both real-world experience and the willingness to step forward and serve.
Mark has spent a lifetime serving Colorado, from commanding B-52 crews in the Air Force to decades as a commercial airline captain and community leader.
He understands discipline, responsibility, and the practical realities facing Colorado families.
Mark is focused on the issues that matter most: affordability, public safety, energy reliability, educational choice, and accountable government.
HD25 deserves a serious, steady leader grounded in service rather than political theater, and I believe Mark Herzfeld is exactly that kind of candidate.
