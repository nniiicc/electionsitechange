Issues & Positions
HD 25 familes deserve a reasonable cost of living, lower taxes, affordable housing and energy, safe neighborhoods and schools
Cost of Living – Inflation
I want to mitigate the effects of inflation by:
- Eliminating regulations that limit development
- Cutting taxes
- Ensuring full TABOR refunds are returned to taxpayers
- Opposing new taxes, fees, or hidden charges
Housing Affordability and Supply
We can make housing more affordable and available through local control and zoning reform, as well as:
- Streamlining permitting, cutting red tape for builders
- Tax credits for builders of starter homes
- Financial assistance for first-time buyers
- Reducing property tax
Economy and Jobs
I believe we can drive small business and job creation by:
- Cutting taxes and regulations for small business
- Oppose mandates and fees that stifle hiring and growth
- Expand vocational programs and apprenticeships
- Invest in critical infrastructure
Parents' Rights and Education
Parents have a right to safety, transparency and freedom of choice in education, which is why I support:
- Trained resource officers in every school
- Curriculum transparency for parents
- Focus on reading, math, writing and critical thinking
- Options for families – charter, private, homeschool
Energy Policy
Xcel Energy's rate hikes — driven by ideological mandates — could raise electricity bills 55–72% by 2029.
Colorado's utilites can't shut the grid off while the PUC rubber stamps rate increases, so I want to:
- Challenge Xcel Energy rate hikes and hidden fees
- Broaden energy strategy to include fossil fuels and nuclear
- Push utilities to upgrade and harden infrastructure
Wild Fire Protection and Mitigation
HD-25 is in one of the highest wildfire risk areas in the country, which means we must:
- Manage forests aggressively
- Collaborate with Federal agencies on mitigation, management, and emergency response
- Support homeowners to harden infrastructure
Taxes, Spending, and TABOR
Colorado spending has outpaced population growth and inflation, growing at 35-45% since 2018; as a result, I intend to:
- Freeze non-essential hiring
- Cut waste to reduce state government size
- Block TABOR work arounds (“fees”) because we need spending constraints
- Reject tax increases and reduce taxes
Immigration and Border Security
Sanctuary status and aid to illegal immigrants costs taxpayers and their communities too much, and I believe we must:
- Eliminate sanctuary status and cooperate with Federal law enforcement
- Remove those engaged in criminal activity first
- Combat fentanyl trafficking
- Make legal immigration transparent, more streamlined, and accountable
- Control our national borders
Crime and Public Safety
Putting and keeping criminals in jail will reverse negative crime data Colorado is experiencing, so we must:
- Implement tougher penalties and bail for repeat and violent offenders and fentanyl dealers
- Fully fund law enforcement
- Protect qualified immunity for law enforcement
