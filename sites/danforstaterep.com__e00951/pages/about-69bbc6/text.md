Meet dan
Dan Goughnour has spent his life serving the city of McKeesport.
Dan has served with the McKeesport Police Department for 16+ years and he is currently the Captain of Police Detectives.
Dan is a proud member of Teamsters Local #205.
In addition to his day job, Dan has served on the School Board for McKeesport Area School District for the last 3 years.
Dan is currently chair of the Safe Schools Committee.
In his personal life, Dan lives with his wife Lauren in McKeesport.
He is the proud father of three children who attend McKeesport Public Schools like he did.
Dan is running for State Representative because he knows we need dedicated leaders who will build opportunity and keep us safe.
Priorities
- Strengthen public safety by providing funding for firefighters, police, and first responders.
- Boost job training programs to provide opportunities for everyone in our region.
- Support our public schools to prepare the next generation for a thriving future.
- Reduce the cost of groceries and everyday essentials to keep money in our pockets.
