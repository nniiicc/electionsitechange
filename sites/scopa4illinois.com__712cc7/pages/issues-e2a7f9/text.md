Brian is a Navy Commander dad who will bring discipline, transparency, and real school options to Aurora, Naperville, Oswego, and Montgomery families.
Parents in Charge Parents, not bureaucrats, decide what's best for their kids.
Springfield politicians think they know better than Illinois parents what's best for their kids.
The result?
Chaotic classrooms, a curriculum you didn't vote for, and taxes that keep rising while results continue to disappoint.
Brian's Plan: Expand school choice: protect education options for every family Support teachers by providing safer classrooms Mandate transparency: all curriculum, materials, and school contracts available for parents to see Discipline with Dollars You're paying top-tier taxes for bottom-tier results.
That's not conservative or liberal — that's just bad management.
Illinois families pay some of the highest property taxes in the nation.
While Springfield claims to be fiscally responsible, your insurance, utilities, and everyday costs keep climbing.
Brian's Plan: Performance-based budgeting: tie agency and school funding to measurable results for kids and communities Fully audit state spending and contracts Safe, Sane, and Accountable Government Every family has the right to feel safe in their neighborhood and confident in their state government.
Crime is spilling into the suburbs.
Cops are demoralized.
Springfield protects criminals instead of communities.

Brian's Plan: Revise the SAFE-T Act: end soft-on-crime policies that put criminals back on the street Mandatory minimums: tough penalties for repeat violent offenders Support school safety: fund SROs and active-shooter preparedness (Brian is ALICE-certified) Audit big contracts: sunshine laws for grants, NGOs, and all state spending Help Brian bring discipline back to Springfield.
