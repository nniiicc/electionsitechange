Illinois House District 84 covers parts of Aurora, Naperville, Oswego, and Montgomery — fast-growing suburban communities full of working families who deserve better schools, lower taxes, and safe streets.
Brian and his family live right here in the district, and his three kids attend school here.
For the first time in over a decade, this is an open seat.
The incumbent is not seeking re-election — which means IL-84 gets a fresh start, and a clear choice.
IL-84 by the numbers About 110,000 residents across 29 square miles Median age 36 — a district full of young families (2.9 people per household) Median household income $112,896 — 1.4× the Illinois average Median home value $310,000 — and some of the nation's highest property taxes on top of it 52% hold a bachelor's degree or higher Average commute: 30 minutes each way Source: U.S.
