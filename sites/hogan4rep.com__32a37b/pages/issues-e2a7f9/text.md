Home Meet Joe Issues Volunteer Donate Issues Making Life More Affordable Joe Hogan has been laser focused on delivering on the promise of affordability.
Joe has supported a cut to the Personal Income Tax for all Pennsylvanians, targeted tax cuts for childcare, a tax cut for low income Pennsylvanians, and lower taxes for seniors and small businesses.
He’s been named to chair a committee focused on affordability, and he is delivering real savings for our families.
Investing In Our Public Schools Joe Hogan has worked with Republicans, Democrats, and Governor Shapiro to be a champion for our public schools.
Not only has Joe delivered for our students, but he’s also delivering for our taxpayers.
The record funding for our schools means schools are getting more from Harrisburg, and that should mean less from taxpayers in each district.
Fighting To Drive Down Health Care And Electricity Prices Joe has been on the forefront of fighting to lower our energy and health care bills, and he’s delivered.
Joe worked with Governor Shapiro to repeal the Regional Greenhouse Gas Initiative (RGGI) that would have led to higher energy taxes for every consumer.
