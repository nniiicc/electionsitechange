Home Meet Joe Issues Volunteer Donate Meet Joe Joe Hogan has spent his adult life serving the public.
As a member of Congressman Mike Fitzpatrick’s staff, Joe helped everyday people cut through the layers of bureaucracy and get the help they needed.
As a Program Director at the Bucks County Redevelopment Authority, Joe helped small businesses stay in operation during the COVID-19 pandemic.
Joe has also been active in his community, volunteering for the Boy Scouts of America and helping to raise money for countless non-profits.
He also volunteered his time to travel to Poland to help Ukrainian refugees earlier this year.
Joe lives in Langhorne with his wife Janita and graduated from Conwell-Egan Catholic High School.
He has a Bachelor’s and Master’s degree from Penn State and is currently finishing his law degree at Temple University.
Joe is committed to helping working families – especially when it comes to dealing with the cost of everyday life.
He’ll fight for better funding for our schools, more jobs for our residents, and fiscal responsibility in Harrisburg.
