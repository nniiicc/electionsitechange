Meet Terrence From a hard start to a life of service.
Terrence was born in Peoria, Illinois.
As a kid he knew homelessness and what it's like when the systems meant to help you don't.
His foster mother helped him find a path forward.
He went to college as a non-traditional student and earned a degree in criminal justice.
Then a master's in public policy.
He is now working on a doctorate in law and policy.
He served as a state trooper in Tennessee and South Dakota.
In Tennessee he worked the Memphis District and served on the Executive Protection Unit, guarding the Governor and visiting officials.
In 2021 his family moved to South Dakota, where he served as a trooper in the Sioux Falls district.
Most recently, he worked at East River Legal Services, helping South Dakotans get free civil legal help.
His wife is a public school teacher with more than 20 years in the classroom.
Their son goes to the school where she teaches.
They came here for the same reasons most of us stay: safety, community, and hard work.

The Secretary of State runs our elections, keeps business records, and protects public information.
Terrence has a plan for all of it.
01 Elections you can trust Keep the tested, audited ballot counters we use today, with people checking the results.
Post-election audits in every county already prove they work.
Fast, accurate results.
No guesswork.
02 Every eligible voice Let 16-year-olds pre-register when they get a driver's license, so they're ready to vote at 18.
Keep voting close to home, especially in rural counties.
03 Your information stays private When you register to vote, the state holds your personal details.
They deserve real protection.
Privacy gets built into every system from the start.
04 Catch fraud early Create an investigative division inside the office to look into fraud, so real problems get found and fixed quickly.
05 A partner for small business Businesses and nonprofits create jobs and hold our towns together.
Filing with the state should be simple, fast, and friendly.
06 A modern, responsive office Update old systems to cut wait times and paperwork.
When you call or file online, you get accurate answers on time.
