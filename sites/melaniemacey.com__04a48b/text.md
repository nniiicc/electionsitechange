Why I'm Running
Meet Melanie
I have been proud to serve my community - as a Royal Oak City Commissioner, a Library Board Trustee, a PTA board member, a public interest lawyer, and a voting rights advocate - and now I'm running for State Representative to build a future where every family can thrive.
From investing in our parks and infrastructure to passing an environmental action plan and creating a Human Rights Commission, I've worked to deliver real results locally, and I'm ready to bring that same focus and effectiveness to Lansing.

Where I Stand On
The Issues
I believe government should work for everyday people, not just the wealthy and well-connected.
As an attorney focused on voting rights and democracy, a former local elected official, and a longtime community advocate, I've spent my career fighting to make our systems more fair, accessible, and responsive to the people they serve.
I am running to bring that same commitment to Lansing at a time when Michiganders are fighting rising costs, attacks on fundamental rights, and growing frustration with politics that too often ignores working families.
