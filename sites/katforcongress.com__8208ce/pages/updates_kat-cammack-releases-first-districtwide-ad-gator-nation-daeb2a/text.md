Congresswoman Kat Cammack (FL-03) has released her first districtwide ad of the cycle, titled “Gator Nation.”
The ad focuses on how Gator Nation counts on Kat to fight for the economy, secure borders, 2nd Amendment freedoms, and the sanctity of life.
The ad buy will currently focus on targeting voters in FL-03 where they are increasingly consuming video content, from digital and OTT to CTV and others.
Regarding the ad’s release Congresswoman Cammack stated:
“Gator Nation is my home, and I will continue to work tirelessly for my constituents to protect and defend their freedoms.
I will stand with President Trump to get our country back on track and to secure our nation’s borders, strengthen our economy, protect the unborn, and bring sanity back to Washington.”
