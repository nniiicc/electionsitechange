Promises kept.
Kat’s record of fighting for YOU Unlike most in Washington, Kat Cammack has been delivering on the promises she’s made to her district and the American People.
Affordability As Kat travels Florida’s 3rd Congressional District, she hears from families and seniors feeling the squeeze of rising costs and from small business owners working to keep their doors open.
Kat is fighting to bring those costs down, from holding big health insurance companies accountable to cutting taxes and energy bills, so every Floridian can afford the American dream.
When the CEOs of America’s five largest health insurers testified before Congress in January 2026, Rep.
Cammack pressed them on whether any executive ever pays a price when a patient is harmed by a delayed or wrongful denial.
She is pushing for real accountability on behalf of North Central Florida families.
Rep.
Cammack voted for the Ratepayer Protection Act to protect families from paying higher electric bills for the power data centers need.
It passed the House 417 to 3.
Rep.
Cammack championed the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, which made President Trump’s 2017 tax cuts permanent, locking in lower rates and the larger standard deduction for North Central Florida families.
The law also delivered new tax relief on tips and overtime pay and a new $6,000 deduction for seniors.
Small businesses also got lasting certainty, with their key tax deduction made permanent.
Rep.
Cammack stands strong for American farmers and ranchers, fighting to cut red tape and ensure agriculture producers have access to markets for their products.
American agriculture feeds the world and, when government gets out of the way, can help lower food and fuel costs for American families.
Rep.
Cammack supported permitting reform to allow for increased domestic production to lower energy costs for American families.
The Standardizing Permitting and Expediting Economic Development (SPEED) Act would modernize the National Environmental Policy Act by shortening review timelines so projects that bring jobs and economic growth to Florida aren’t held back by outdated regulations.
Border Security During the Biden administration, Kat went to the southern border on multiple occasions and saw the crisis with her own eyes.
Today the border is secure again, and Kat is fighting to keep it that way by making sure Border Patrol agents have the tools and backing they need.
Rep.
Cammack introduced the COP Act to cut off federal funds to any police department that hires people who are not U.S. citizens as officers.
The people who enforce our laws should be Americans.
Rep.
Cammack voted for the largest investment in border enforcement in a generation, paying for new technology and more agents on the ground.
When the Senate tried to pass off faster parole as border security in 2024, Kat called it out on national television and kept fighting for a bill that actually secures the border.
In May 2022, as parents across the country faced empty shelves during the baby formula shortage, Kat exposed pallets of formula stockpiled at a southern border facility.
She went to the border to press the administration to put American families first.
In 2021, Rep.
Cammack brought local police chiefs and sheriffs to the southern border so they could see the crisis firsthand.
In 2021, Rep.
Cammack introduced a bill to get Border Patrol agents back on the front lines by reviewing the Department of Homeland Security’s support contracts.
A version of her bill, reintroduced by Rep.
Josh Brecheen, became law in December 2024.
Constituent Services When a hard freeze hits a family farm or a veteran's paperwork gets stuck at a federal agency, the people of North Central Florida deserve someone in their corner.
For Kat, helping a neighbor cut through the red tape is the most important part of the job.
Kat works hard to provide constituent services, including assistance with federal agencies, federal loans and grants, service academy nominations, and more.
As of October 2026, Rep.
Cammack has returned more than $128 million to constituents and closed more than 14,605 cases since taking office in 2021.
After the February freeze devastated blueberry fields and other crops across North Central Florida, Rep.
Cammack led every member of Florida’s congressional delegation in asking for a federal disaster declaration.
She stood beside Secretary Rollins at USDA when it was signed.
Rep.
Cammack voted for the disaster relief package that made more than $600 million available to Florida farmers hit by Hurricanes Idalia, Debby, Helene and Milton.
Rep.
Cammack received the Outstand New Member of Congress Award from the American Farm Bureau Federation (AFBF) and the Florida Farm Bureau in April 2023 for her strong advocacy for rural communities and support for agriculture policies in Washington.
Fighting Big Government Big government cost Kat Cammack's family their cattle ranch and their home.
She went to Washington so no other family has to go through what hers did.
Today she takes the fight to unelected bureaucrats and cuts the red tape crushing small businesses and family farms across North Central Florida.
Rep.
Cammack introduced the USA Act, which makes Washington review every federal program on a regular schedule and starts cutting off money to programs Congress has stopped reauthorizing.
It passed the House Oversight Committee.
House leaders included Rep.
Cammack’s REINS Act in the 2025 budget reconciliation plans, keeping up the fight to make Congress vote on the most expensive federal regulations.
Rep.
Cammack’s bipartisan DIGITAL Applications Act passed the House to speed up broadband buildout on federal lands by moving permit applications online, helping bring internet access to more rural communities.
Many Americans have to pass a background check to get a job.
Rep.
Cammack believes the people working inside the U.S.
Capitol should meet the same standard.
Her resolution, H.Res.
500 , would put background checks in place for House staff to guard against foreign espionage and keep people with violent records out the halls of Congress.
Fiscal Responsibility Receiving praise from many of the national conservative organizations around the country, Kat Cammack has earned a reputation as one of the most fiscally conservative members of Congress.
She’ll always fight to cut our debt and deficit and ensure more hardworking Americans keep what they’ve earned.
Rep.
Cammack introduced the REINS Act (H.R.
142), which would require Congress to approve all “major rules” costing $100 million or more before they take effect.
An earlier version passed the House in June 2023.
Rep.
Cammack serves on the Republican Study Committee, where she advocates for commonsense, balanced budgets.
Rep.
Cammack cosponsored the Federal Reserve Transparancy Act to give taxpayers a full audit of the Federal Reserve.
Life A staunch defender of Life, Kat Cammack has led the charge in Washington to defend the unborn and be a strong voice for the pro-life movement in Congress.
Rep.
Cammack has repeatedly been a lead sponsor of the Born-Alive Abortion Survivors Protection Act and its discharge petition.
The discharge petition set a record for the most signatures on its first day.
Rep.
Cammack has been endorsed by the SBA List as a pro-life champion.
Read more of her personal conviction as a pro-life champion here.
Rep.
Cammack is a Co-Chair of the House Pro-Life Caucus.
Second Amendment With top ratings from all of the leading Second Amendment advocacy organizations, Kat Cammack has proven herself to be one of the best defenders in Congress for our constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
Rep.
Cammack convenes a Second Amendment Coalition and fought against the politicization of the ATF during the Biden administration.
The NRA has endorsed Rep.
Cammack again for 2026 and gave her an A rating for defending the Second Amendment.
Rep.
Cammack voted to eliminate the $200 federal tax on suppressors and short-barreled rifles, so law-abiding gun owners no longer pay a fee to exercise their rights.
Rep.
Cammack opposed the Biden administration rule tightening federal regulations on stabilizing braces for firearms often used by veterans and other law-abiding gun owners with disabilities.
Cosponsored H.R.
404, the Hearing Protection Act, which would remove silencers from the definition of firearms for the purpose of the National Firearms Act.
It would also treat those acquiring or possessing a firearm silencer as meeting any registration or licensing requirement, giving gun owners greater access to hearing protection.
Cosponsored H.R.
38, the Concealed Carry Reciprocity Act.
This bill would ensure any law-abiding citizen with a concealed carry permit can legally carry in any state that also has a concealed carry law.
Cosponsored H.R.
45, the Firearm Industry Non-Discrimination (FIND) Act.
This bill would prohibit the awarding of taxpayer-funded contracts to entities or organizations that discriminate against firearm manufacturers and businesses.
Cosponsored H.R.
563, the No Retaining Every Gun in a System that Restricts Your Rights Act.
This bill would require the ATF to delete its existing firearm transaction records and allow federal firearms licensees to destroy their records when they go out of business, instead of sending them to the ATF.
Those records essentially create a federal firearm registry, which violates the constitutional rights of law-abiding gun owners.
Veterans & First Responders As the wife of a first responder and the family member of many veterans, Kat Cammack understands the sacrifices our men and women in uniform make each and every day.
Fighting for our veterans and our first responders has been one of the highest priorities for her in Washington.
Rep.
Cammack’s office works to support our local firefighters and first responders through letters of support for federal grants, including the Assistance to Firefighters Grant (AFG) and SAFER grant.
During Rep.
Cammack’s time in office, SAFER and AFG grants have been awarded to Alachua Fire Rescue, Gainesville Fire Rescue, Clay County Fire Rescue, and several others.
When a vendor error put roughly 50 local veterans at risk of losing their homes, Rep.
Cammack stepped in, working with Volunteers of America, VA Secretary Doug Collins, and the Ocala community to keep the Ritz Veterans Village in Ocala open.
Rep.
Cammack meets twice a year with a veterans advisory committee that keeps her informed on the issues facing the military community.
Rep.
Cammack’s district formerly included Camp Blanding, the headquarters for the Florida National Guard.
She still meets each year with the Adjutant General of the State of Florida because of her commitment to our servicemembers and their families.
Rep.
Cammack helps veterans receive the service medals they earned and has hosted numerous medal ceremonies across the district.
Each December, Rep.
Cammack presents a Veteran of the Year award.
Past honorees include a Medal of Honor recipient and the president of the Clay County Veterans Council.
In 2022, Rep.
Cammack championed renaming the Middleburg VA Clinic for A.K.
Baker and led a ceremony in February 2023 to celebrate the renaming.
Rep.
Cammack helped open the Ocala VA clinic.
Rep.
Cammack helped open two new clinics at the Gainesville VA facility in April 2023, one for primary care and one for mental health.
