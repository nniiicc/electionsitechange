Here's how to vote for Zach Scheffler for State Representative in the Franklin-8 House District: ARE YOU IN FRANKLIN-8?
Franklin-8 is a new district.

Albans City Wards 5 and 6, and specific portions of St.
Albans Town.
Explore the map below to find your home address Ente r your street address here to reveal your voting districts .
If your home is inside the Franklin-8 lines, you can vote for Zach!
REGISTER TO VOTE You can register to vote in Vermont at any time, including on Election Day.
If you recently moved or are a new voter, update your voter registration immediately .
You can do this online, on paper, or at your municipal clerk's office.
VOTING BY MAIL WATCH YOUR MAILBOX If your voter registration information was current as of Sept.
1st 2026, your official ballot is set to arrive in the mail by Thursday, Oct.
1st 2026.
FILL OUT AND SIGN YOUR BALLOT Fill in the oval next to Zach Scheffler under "State Representative." Place your ballot inside the voted ballot envelope and seal it.
You must sign the outside of the envelope for your vote to count.
RETURN YOUR BALLOT You can return your ballot by mail, drop it in a secure official drop box, or hand-deliver it to your clerk.
Ballots must be received by 7:00 PM on Election Day, Tuesday Nov.
3rd, 2026.

Albans City...
