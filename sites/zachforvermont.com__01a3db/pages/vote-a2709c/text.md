The General Election is Tuesday, Nov. 3rd, 2026.
Vermont mails ballots to all active registered voters automatically.

Here's how to vote for Zach Scheffler for State Representative in the Franklin-8 House District:
ARE YOU IN FRANKLIN-8?
Franklin-8 is a new district.
It includes St.
Albans City Wards 5 and 6, and specific portions of St.
Albans Town.
- Explore the map below to find your home address
- Enter your street address here to reveal your voting districts.
- If your home is inside the Franklin-8 lines, you can vote for Zach!
REGISTER TO VOTE
You can register to vote in Vermont at any time, including on Election Day.
If you recently moved or are a new voter, update your voter registration immediately.
You can do this online, on paper, or at your municipal clerk's office.
VOTING BY MAIL
WATCH YOUR MAILBOX
If your voter registration information was current as of Sept. 1st 2026, your official ballot is set to arrive in the mail by Thursday, Oct. 1st 2026.
FILL OUT AND SIGN YOUR BALLOT
Fill in the oval next to Zach Scheffler under "State Representative." Place your ballot inside the voted ballot envelope and seal it.
You must sign the outside of the envelope for your vote to count.
RETURN YOUR BALLOT
You can return your ballot by mail, drop it in a secure official drop box, or hand-deliver it to your clerk.
Ballots must be received by 7:00 PM on Election Day, Tuesday Nov. 3rd, 2026.
- If you live in St.
Albans City...
Return your ballot to the City Clerk:
100 N Main St, St.
Albans City, VT
Drop boxes are in the front and back of City Hall.
- If you live in St.
Albans Town...
Return your ballot to the Town Clerk:
398 Georgia Shore Rd, St.
Albans Bay, VT
VOTING IN-PERSON
If you prefer to vote in-person, or if you make a mistake on your mailed ballot, you can vote at your local polling place on Tuesday Nov. 3rd, 2026.
Polls are open until 7:00 PM.
You can register to vote on the same day at your polling location.
