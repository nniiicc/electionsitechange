Vote for Zach!
Vermont is facing rising costs and economic shocks.
That's why St.
Albans needs a legislator who'll protect our community and build local resilience.
As your next state representative, my priorities are grounded in a simple Vermont value: Common good is common sense.
BUILD HOMES
A housing shortage is driving up prices and forcing our friends and neighbors to leave.
While St.
Albans is making progress, we must continue expanding our supply of homes so our workforce remains local, our local businesses are supported, our communities stay vibrant, and more of us can afford to stay in Vermont.
- Push to simplify the rules so homebuilders big and small know exactly where to build.
- Fight for the funding required to lower construction costs and support neighborhood infrastructure.
- Demand housing grant audits to ensure Vermonters get the homes we pay for.
STOP TAX SPIKES
Rising healthcare premiums for our educators drove up the school budgets that sent your property taxes skyrocketing.
Our current representation voted against fixing this problem.
- Fight to lower the healthcare costs driving property tax hikes.
- Work to accelerate the supply of homes, stabilizing rents and expanding the property taxpayer pool.
- Treat childcare as essential economic infrastructure, ensuring working parents have the support they need to remain in the workforce during periods of federal instability.
DEMAND RESULTS
We deserve a government worth paying for.
Vermont's institutions must run efficiently.
- Push for public state auditing of departments and programs to save taxpayers time and money.
- Demand audits of state projects to eliminate mismanaged contracts and obsolete systems wasting time and money.
PROTECT VERMONT
Right now, Washington politics and tariffs threaten our border businesses.
Federal deportation policies are driving the workforce our dairy farms require to operate even deeper underground.
Federal budget cuts endanger local jobs at federal agencies, one of the county's largest employers.
We must insulate our economy from this instability.
- Build a self-reliant local economy capable of withstanding economic shocks.
- Strengthen safety nets for working families impacted by layoffs and federal disruption.
