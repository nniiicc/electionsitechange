I've lived, worked, and volunteered in Franklin County my entire life.
Growing up, I learned that common good is common sense.
My parents volunteered on the town ambulance squad, helped at the local library, shared fresh produce from our garden with neighbors, and started their own businesses to serve others.
I carry those Vermont values with me.
LOCAL CAREER
I'm a Vermont tech worker and a proud state college alum.
My parents' work ethic inspired my entrepreneurship, and I've also been fortunate to work for local businesses and organizations that are deeply invested in this community, including the local newspaper, public access media, and Vermont's very own online social network.
For over a decade, I've started local community groups, served on boards and committees, founded professional organizations, and directed county-wide civic efforts.
All of this experience has taught me how to bring people together to tackle local issues and participate in our democracy.
COMMUNITY COMMITMENT
Alice and I are proud renters with a deep love for St.
Albans.

But as the years go by, more of our friends, family, and neighbors face an increasingly uncertain future.

Doing that takes leadership.
You deserve an accessible representative who will work hard to make a better future possible.
