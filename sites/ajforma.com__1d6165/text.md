I am Andrea James, an Independent candidate for governor of Massachusetts.
My family has been proud residents here for seven generations.
I am the candidate working to put the priorities of our government’s agenda, focused on the needs of the people.
End State Cooperation with ICE & Protect Immigrant Families
Any candidate or incumbent running for Governor of our state of Massachusetts whose priorities are not focused on housing, healthcare, childcare, education, and a better quality of life doesn’t deserve to be your governor.
Housing is a human right.
People should be able to see a doctor without going into debt.
Families should have accessible childcare with people they trust to care for their children.
Equitably funded education is the foundation of democracy.
Massachusetts should not have contracts with ICE, and we deserve a governor who will ban data centers and focus instead on lowering utility costs and promoting green energy solutions.
