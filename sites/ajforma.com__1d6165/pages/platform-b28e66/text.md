WORKERS OVER BILLIONAIRES.
It's Commonwealth.
Not corporatewealth.
Massachusetts residents are told the economy is thriving.
Massachusetts is one of the wealthiest states in the country, but for working people and families, it has never been harder to afford the basics.
Rent keeps going up.
Childcare costs more than many mortgages.
Healthcare bills push families deeper into debt.
And too many people are being priced out of the communities they’ve called home for generations.
That didn’t happen by accident.
It’s the result of a political system that protects wealth and powerful interests while asking everyone else to work harder just to get by.
Politicians talk about standing up to corporate greed while taking money from CEOs, developers, and lobbyists behind closed doors.
Right now, Massachusetts works great for the wealthy and well-connected.
Everybody else is being crushed by the cost of simply trying to live.
I’m running to build a Commonwealth that works for all of us, not just the people at the top.
