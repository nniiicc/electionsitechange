But here’s the truth: about 65% of Massachusetts voters are Independent or unenrolled.
Most people in this state aren’t part of the political club, and they’re tired of being treated like they don’t matter.
And many Democrats no longer feel heard by the leadership that claims to represent them.
The political establishment has grown comfortable speaking to insiders and donors, while everyday people struggle with the cost of housing, childcare, healthcare, and just getting by.
We’re building something different, a movement rooted in working people.
For the families trying to stay in their communities, for those pushed to the margins, and for everyone who feels locked out of decisions that shape their lives.
The corporate donors and career politicians may hold the power right now.
But there are far more of us than there are of them.
Massachusetts can have a government that actually delivers for working people.
But it will take thousands of ordinary Bay Staters standing up, getting involved, and building it together.
