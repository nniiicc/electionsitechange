Katrina Sexton comes from a family dedicated to public service.
Her focus is on protecting public education, improving schools, creating more living-wage jobs, providing accessible healthcare, and prioritizing public safety.
With almost a decade of government experience, she is committed to finding solutions and navigating bureaucratic processes effectively to help working individuals and families.
LIST OF ISSUES EDUCATION Katrina Sexton values education as the foundation for a thriving community.
She is dedicated to ensuring that every child has access to quality education and opportunities for success.
Public education gives every child the opportunity to gain the skills and knowledge needed for a better future.
Homeschooling and private institutions may provide the same, but the public school system is accountable to the public for the public good, not personal beliefs or private interests.
Protecting the public school system is a service for the common good of all people that should be funded adequately.
HOUSING Affordable and safe housing is a fundamental right.
Katrina Sexton is committed to addressing housing challenges and creating sustainable housing solutions for all individuals and families.
Whether it's new homeownership, aging homes, or rentals, people need to be able to live in a safe and affordable home.
Veterans, senior citizens, and families should be able to live in dignity and with humanity without breaking the wallet.
HEALTHCARE Access to affordable and comprehensive healthcare is essential to a healthy, productive population.
Katrina Sexton advocates for policies that prioritize healthcare accessibility and affordability for all.
She knows how devastating medical debt can become and how the lack of healthcare options can lead to significantly worse outcomes for patients and their families, especially in rural, impoverished regions of Kentucky.
ENVIRONMENT Protecting the environment is crucial for the well-being of the current and future generations.
Katrina Sexton supports initiatives that promote environmental conservation and sustainability, including strong regulations on hyperscale data center developments and protecting KY's farmlands for the future of our agricultural industries, such as horse farms, crop farms, and livestock growers.
DIVERSITY Katrina Sexton celebrates diversity and recognizes the importance of inclusion.
She is dedicated to fostering a welcoming and equitable community for people of all backgrounds.
BUSINESS Supporting local businesses and economic growth is a priority for Katrina Sexton.
She is committed to creating an environment that empowers entrepreneurs and strengthens the local economy.
Katrina believes massive tax initiatives and excessive tax breaks for multi-million-dollar AI corporations can lead to an overwhelming tax burden on the working class.
Everyday working people need a collective voice and a seat at the table, not promises hidden in NDAs and boardroom deals.
