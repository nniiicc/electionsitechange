AFL-CIO (Kentucky Labor Union)
Kentucky Labor Union Organization
The Kentucky State AFL-CIO Committee on Political
Education (COPE) strongly supports Kentucky's working
class.
The organization has more than 60 affiliates and more than 131,000 union members from across the Commonwealth of Kentucky.
The labor union organization at-large fights for fair wages, safe working conditions, and stronger communities by empowering workers through solidarity and collective action.
For more information about the Kentucky State AFL-CIO please visit https://ky.aflcio.org.
Sierra Club (Kentucky Chapter)
Environmental Organization
The KY Chapter of the Sierra Club, with 6,000 members
and supporters in Kentucky, works to protect our
communities and the planet.
It is part of the oldest, largest,
and most influential grassroots environmental organizations in the nation, with more than three million members and supporters.
The mission is to ensure all Kentuckians have clean air, clean water, healthy food from healthy soil, and access to the beauty of our Commonwealth's wild, green spaces.
You can read more about the Sierra Club's core values and mission at https://www.sierraclub.org/kentucky.
