Donate Now Meet Ronnie For State Representative Meet Ronnie Heiseler Ronnie Heiseler is a lifelong resident of Haverhill, Massachusetts, where he has spent his entire life building strong ties to the local community.
For over 20 years, he has built a career with the same company, where he currently works as an engineer specializing in MEMS sensor technology.
His career in engineering reflects a commitment to precision, problem-solving, and long-term dedication.
Though new to the political scene, he has been compelled to step forward in service to his community.
He brings a practical, solutions-oriented mindset shaped by decades of technical and collaborative work in industry.
His focus is on applying common-sense leadership to local challenges while remaining grounded in the needs of residents.
He is motivated by a desire to contribute meaningfully to the future of the community he has always called home.
