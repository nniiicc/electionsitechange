Meet Christopher Contract with the People Issues SOLUTIONS Photos Events Contribute News Volunteer Voter Information Meet Christopher Christopher Anger is a 39 year resident of Hernando County.
He has been married for 20 years.
Christopher has 2 adult daughters and one charming grandson.
Christopher has been employed with a small local business for the past 7 years.
Christopher was born in Flint, Michigan and raised in Clio, Michigan.
Christopher enlisted in the United States Army in September of 1983.
Christopher graduated in 1984 from Clio High School and entered the United States Army in July of 1984.
While Christopher was stationed in West Berlin, Germany as an Expert Combat Field Medic with the 5th Battalion of the 502nd Infantry, Berlin Brigade.
Christopher's parents moved to Spring Hill, Florida in May of 1986 with 3 of his brothers and sister.
Upon leaving the Army in May of 1987 Christopher went back to Michigan.
In December of 1987 discovering the job opportunities and the weather were not good, Christopher decided to follow his Parents to Florida.
Christopher has many experiences that may be familiar with many people.
Christopher and his father opened a small glass shop in Homosassa, Florida.
That in turn allowed Christopher to become licensed as a Certified Building Contractor.
When the building sector collapsed in 2009.
Christopher and his father struggled to keep it going.
Then his father got sick and Christopher made the decision to take care of his family.
Christopher went to work for a couple of small local businesses.
Which has allowed Christopher to learn what is required to work for others while understanding the sacrifices that need to be made when you work for others.
As your State Representative Christopher will work to provide what is missing in our elected representation.
That being, COMMUNICATION, TRANSPARENCY, and ACCOUNTABILTY.
Christopher listens to the elected representatives and public servants blaming others for our problems.
Christopher believes the citizens want more.
WE KNOW OUR PROBLEMS, WE WANT SOLUTIONS.
Christopher drafted a contract with the voters.
The contract outlines the issues and opportunities that he believes are neglected and need to be addressed.
Our elected servants should work for the people.
They should listen to the people.
They should also set a higher standard of behavior.
Corporations and Political Action Committees [PAC] provide something that the citizens don't have.
Large amounts of money that the politicians desire for their reelections.
It makes one wonder, what do the donors get in return for their investment?
As a member of the Constitution Party of Florida, Christopher Anger is responsible to the people.
Christopher doesn't have to listen to party leaders that are looking to keep power.
Christopher will be listening to those that got him there, the VOTERS.
He will be willing to listen to good ideas from all sides.
Every decision will be based on [1] HOW DOES THIS AFFECT THE PEOPLE? [2] HOW DOES IT LINE UP WITH THE CONSTITUTION AND BILL OF RIGHTS?
