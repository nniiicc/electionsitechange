WE THE PEOPLE
From:
Christopher Anger
To:
The Citizens of Florida House District 53
Subject: My Contract With the Citizens When Elected
- I will NOT become a realtor or a land developer.
- I pledge to stay engaged with ALL citizens (regardless of political affiliation).
- Financial support will only be received from living breathing people
- (No corporations, developers, or Political Action Committee).
- I will disclose any person or organization attempting to solicit or influence me in any unethical way within 48 hours.
- I will NOT talk at the citizens, I will listen to them!
- I will NOT engage in mudslinging or personal attacks.
- I will seek a solution to return responsible development to the counties and citizens.
- I will seek solutions pertaining to high cost of property taxes, homeowners insurance, and auto insurance.
- I will seek solutions for the protection of our natural resources, such as springs, lakes, rivers, wetlands, and green spaces.
- I will seek a solution to help return a local, responsible, and quality education for our children.
A PEOPLE first solution requires the people to be involved!
