John McQueeney is a dedicated Texan and business leader from Fort Worth with a background in real estate, franchising, and logistics.
A successful entrepreneur, John has helped grow and expand multiple businesses, including Chicken Express locations, with a focus on continued growth and job creation.
As a state representative, he is committed to keeping Tarrant County a place where families can afford to live, work, and put down roots with good schools, quality healthcare, and safe neighborhoods.
A proud alumnus of Texas Christian University, John is a pro-life Christian and an active member of St.
Patrick’s Catholic Church.
He and his wife Katie are the proud parents of their son Jack and daughter Sheridan, who attend All Saints Episcopal School.
John’s faith, family, and conservative values guide his service to Texas House District 97.
