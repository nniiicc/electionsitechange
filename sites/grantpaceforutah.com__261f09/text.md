News: Grant Pace Sworn In to the Utah House of Representatives On Wednesday, May 6, Grant Pace was officially sworn in to serve in the Utah House of Representatives by House Speaker Mike Schultz at the Utah State Capitol.
Pace replaces Tyler Clancy, who is now serving as the state homeless coordinator.
Speaker Schultz welcomed three new members to the House Majority, including Rep.
Pace of House District 60, alongside Rob Bishop and Jackie Larson.
