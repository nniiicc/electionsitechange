My name is Zyaire Webb, and I'm working to be your NC House Representative for District 10.
I am a Wayne County native.
I was born right here in Goldsboro, I was educated through the public school system, and I graduated from East Carolina University in May!
I went to college with the intent to run for office.
I believe in our schools, our county, our state, and this country.
However, I have watched for years how the current administration has put themselves and big money ahead of the hard-working people.
While in high school, I completed my welding degree and graduated from Wayne Community College.
I have been welding since I was 14 and have made hundreds of handcrafted pig cookers, smokers, and fryers.
I am not afraid of hard, dirty work, and I have the calluses to show for it.
Our county needs someone like me representing us in Raleigh.
I look forward to explaining to you my plans.
I want to meet with you, collaborate with you, and fix the problems we have.
