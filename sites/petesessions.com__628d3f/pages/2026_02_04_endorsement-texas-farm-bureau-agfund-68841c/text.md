Endorsement: Texas Farm Bureau AGFUND
February 4, 2026
WACO – As a 32-year Texas Farm Bureau Member and a 27-year Member of Congress, Pete Sessions holds the record of the Texas Congressional Delegation who has passed more legislation off the U.S.
House floor.
Congressman Sessions is deeply proud of his 100% voting record with the Texas Farm Bureau and will continue to stand with the agriculture community.
Pete is honored to accept the Texas Farm Bureau AGFUND Board of Directors’ endorsement, and if re-elected, he will undoubtedly keep fighting hard for farm and ranch families, maintaining that perfect voting record of support.
When asked to comment of AGFUND’s support for Pete, President Russell Boening said, “Texas Farm Bureau (TFB) AGFUND is proud to endorse Pete Sessions in the 2026 primary election.
We have seen firsthand how seriously Pete takes the responsibility of standing up for agriculture.
He listens to farmers, ranchers, and landowners and is always working toward solutions on our priority issues.
As a TFB member with a 100% voting record on issues we have identified as critical to agriculture, Pete has shown a genuine, personal commitment to rural Texas and we are honored to support him.”
