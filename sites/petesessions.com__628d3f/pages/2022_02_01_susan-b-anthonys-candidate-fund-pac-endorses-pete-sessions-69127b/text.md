Anthony’s Candidate Fund PAC Endorses Pete Sessions February 1, 2022 Washington, D.C. – Susan B.
Anthony List’s Candidate Fund PAC announced its endorsement of the Honorable Pete Sessions to represent Texas’ 17 th Congressional District.
As a parent of a young man with Down syndrome, Pete has seen for many years just how important life is and determines it is one of the best blessings from God.
Pete leads on issues pertaining to disabilities and has a long-standing history as an advocate for life legislation.
“Congressman Pete Sessions is a longtime pro-life leader with an outstanding record of standing up for unborn children and their mothers in Washington,” said the Honorable Marilyn Musgrave, SBA List’s Vice President of Government Affairs .
“As a father of five, including a grown son with special needs, Rep.
Sessions deeply understands the value of every life.
For his work to stop taxpayer-funded abortion, dangerous mail-order abortion drugs, painful late-term abortions, and much more, he holds an A+ on SBA List’s National Pro-life Scorecard.
We’re proud to endorse Rep.
Sessions and encourage pro-life Texans in the 17th District to support his re-election.” Hon.
Pete Sessions served in Congress from 1997-2019, where he maintained a 100% pro-life voting record and co-sponsored pro-life priority legislation such as the Pain-Capable Unborn Child Protection Act and the Born-Alive Abortion Survivors Protection Act.
SBA List is a network of 900,000 pro-life Americans nationwide, dedicated to ending abortion by electing national leaders and advocating for laws that save lives, with a special calling to promote pro-life women leaders.
