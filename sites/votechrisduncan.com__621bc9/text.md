Why I’m Running
I’m running for State Senate to make California more affordable, cut red tape, and fight for our region’s fair share.
As a federal prosecutor, Mayor, and Fire Authority Board Member, I worked to make our communities safer and more affordable.
Now, as an attorney fighting Trump's disastrous tariffs and member of the Assessment Appeals Board, I see firsthand how our tax system too often burdens those who can least afford it while letting corporate tax cheats off the hook.
In the State Senate, I’ll work every day to keep our neighborhoods safe, lower costs for families, and hold Sacramento accountable to the people it serves.
