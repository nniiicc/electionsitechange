Any registered Brooklyn voter can sign Joel Anabilah-Azumah's "Independent Nominating Petition" for the General Election on Tuesday, November 5.
Signatures are being collected between April 16 and May 28.
The General Election date is Tuesday, November 5.
Early Voting occurs between October 26 and November 3.
Feel free to vote on Early Voting days if it is more convenient for you.
You can also receive absentee ballots from the NYC Board of Elections.
You do not need to be a Republican to vote for Joel Anabilah-Azumah in the General Election .
You do not have to vote the same party all of the way down the ballot.
If anyone tells you otherwise at your polling station, please contact us so that we can have law enforcement take the appropriate actions.
