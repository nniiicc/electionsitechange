Sean will fight to lower income taxes, lower energy taxes, and stop property tax increases for seniors.
Make Good Schools Great We have good schools in our community, but Sean wants them to be great.
He’ll fight to fully fund them and make sure that our schools get their fair share from Harrisburg.
He’ll also make sure parents have choices in education.
Protect Our Community No more defunding the police – instead we need to invest in our men and women in uniform.
And we need to end the practice of rewarding crime by ending sanctuary cities and catch and release policies.
