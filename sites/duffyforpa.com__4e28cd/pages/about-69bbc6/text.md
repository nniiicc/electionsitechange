I’m Sean Duffy, and I’ll fight for your family every day as your State Representative.” – Sean Duffy Sean is running now because he knows families need our help more than ever.
Too many politicians are more interested in the latest political fight than actually solving the problems our families face.
Sean will always stand up for our families because that’s what he was taught at an early age – prioritize family and fight for what’s right.
Sean has spent his adult life in service of others.
As a student getting his undergraduate degree at Temple and his law degree from Villanova, Sean worked as a police officer.
After graduation Sean joined the military and was commissioned as a JAG Officer where he served as legal counsel to commanders and staff on issues of national security, fiscal, and administrative law.
Now Sean serves as an attorney, helping to balance the needs of our community through land use planning and policy development.
Sean lives in Buckingham with his wife Kerry and their children.
