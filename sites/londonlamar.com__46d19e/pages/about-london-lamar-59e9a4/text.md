Senator London Lamar
London Lamar believes in strong schools, healthy families, and safe communities.
London Lamar is the State Senator for Tennessee’s 33rd District in Shelby County and currently serves as Chair of the Tennessee Senate Democratic Caucus.
Elected to the Senate in 2022 after serving in the Tennessee House of Representatives, she made history as the youngest woman and youngest African American to serve in the Tennessee State Senate.
In the General Assembly, Senator Lamar serves on the Judiciary Committee, the Finance, Ways and Means Committee, and the Joint Ways and Means Committee.
She has helped secure more than $40 million in resources for Shelby County and has championed legislation focused on Strong Schools, Healthy Families, and Safe Communities—from advancing maternal health and protecting access to critical care, to combating human trafficking, reducing gun violence, expanding voting rights, and growing economic opportunity for Memphis families.
