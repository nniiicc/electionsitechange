Employment I have worked diligently to provide in-state employment opportunities for graduating students, sustain and create quality employment within District 10, and encourage local hiring practices.

Veterans As the grandson of a World War II combat veterans and the son of a Vietnam combat veteran, I understand the importance of veteran services.
As a legislator, I have served on the Veterans and Military Affairs interim committee and helped pass legislation crucial for those who have personally risked everything for us.
We must continue to expand mental health programs while providing job training and retraining for our veterans.
Environment I have worked with my colleagues in the legislature and with local communities to preserve and protect the vital natural resources, enhance the quality of life in the district and also maintain and enhance the unique characteristics of each community within District 10.
