House of Representatives, Indiana's 9th District Bio-Tonya Hudson *Authentic *Consistent *Driven As a native of Indiana , born and raised, I was a very active child growing up and was involved in many organizations and activities (Girl Scouts, gymnastics, cheerleading, etc.) and always someone who strived to protect the downtrodden.
I am pro-life.
I have lived in the beautiful hills of Southern Indiana for over 25 years.
I have been a Real Estate Broker for 21 years.
Prior to getting my Real Estate License in 2005, I worked five years at Brown County High School as an Aid in the Nurse’s office.
As a Real Estate Broker, I have the pleasure of assisting homeowners to sell their house as well as helping buyers to get the home of their dreams.
I n my profession, I have the privilege of working with many individuals from many walks of life.
I do so with dignity and without prejudice.
The work entailed for my clients involves contract negotiations and multi-tasking to get transactions completed.
I do my best to handle the unexpected with grace and always strive to present myself in a professional manner.
I have served on State and local committees & boards including as President of the Bedford Board of REALTORS in 2013.
In 2024, I served as Vice Chair of the Libertarian Party of Indiana before running for Lieutenant Governor.
I revere and respect the U.S.
Constitution.
I am a proud Libertarian.
I ran for U.S.
Congress in 2020 and 2022.
In 2024, I was very fortunate to have been Donald Rainwater’s running mate for Lt.
Governor in Indiana (Rainwater/Hudson).
Is it ‘Third Times the Charm’ as I run for Congress in 2026?
I am fortunate to have adopted dogs like “Independence and Justice” from the White River Humane Society.
I encourage all able pet lovers to adopt from their local rescue shelter or the Humane Society.
I am a proud active member of the Lions Club International in Mitchell, Indiana.
My life’s journey and experiences have prepared me to run for office first in 2020 , and again in 2026.
Now is the time for me to represent the people of Southern Indiana in Washington DC, stand up for the U.S.
Constitution and be a ‘real voice’ for Indiana’s District 9.
Be Bold & Vote GOLD.
