Meet Jeffrey Perez
Jeffrey Perez is running for office to bring integrity, accountability, and real-life experience to public service.
Having worked in government, law, and community advocacy, Jeffrey understands how policies affect everyday Oklahomans and is committed to ensuring that government works for the people it serves.
By prioritizing collaboration and common-sense leadership, Jeffrey aims to build a stronger, more responsive government that truly represents the voices of its people.
