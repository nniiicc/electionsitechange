Jimmy Rogers has spent his life working, serving, and raising a family right here in Western North Carolina.
A lifelong Haywood County resident, Jimmy believes leadership is not about titles or politics, but about responsibility.
From running a small family business to serving on local boards and more than two decades on the school board, he has learned that good leadership means listening carefully, making thoughtful decisions, and being accountable to the people you serve.
Guided by faith, grounded in family, and deeply connected to his community, Jimmy brings a steady, common-sense approach to public service.
He understands the importance of fiscal responsibility, respect for local institutions, and decisions being made closest to home.
Jimmy is running for the North Carolina House to represent Western North Carolina with integrity, care, and sound judgment — always putting service before self and the needs of the community first.
