Together, we'll build a stronger future for District 19. — Marcus Cardenas MEET MARCUS A Leader Who Cares About Texas A True Texas Success Story Marcus Cardenas was born and raised on the Southside of San Antonio, where family, faith, and community shaped every part of his life.
Marcus grew up learning the values of hard work, integrity, and service to community.
As a small business owner Marcus has created jobs, supported families, and contributed to the economic vitality of District 19.
Building a Life Through Perseverance After attending St.
Joseph's Catholic School, Kazen Middle School, and South San High School—where he played basketball—Marcus began his studies at Palo Alto College.
In 2007, tragedy struck when his older brother passed away in a motorcycle accident.
Marcus made the difficult decision to set aside his education and step into the family plumbing business to help support his loved ones.
Through hard work and determination, Marcus built Vertex Concrete, a thriving business that has served District 19 for over a decade.
As a proud father to his daughter Isabella and uncle to his nieces and nephews, Marcus understands what Texas families need: opportunity, safety, and a brighter future.

OUR PRIORITIES Fighting for What Matters Marcus is focused on the issues that impact District 19 families every single day Economic Development Creating Jobs.
Growing Businesses.
Building Prosperity.
As a successful small business owner, Marcus knows what it takes to create jobs and drive economic growth.
He'll work to attract new businesses to District 19, reduce regulatory burdens on job creators, and ensure Texas remains the best state in America for entrepreneurs and workers alike.
Marcus will fight for policies that support small businesses, lower taxes, and create high-paying career opportunities for Texas families.
Economic prosperity isn't just about numbers—it's about giving every Texan the chance to achieve their dreams.
Public Safety Backing the Blue.
Protecting Families.
Securing Communities.
Every family deserves to feel safe in their home and neighborhood.
Marcus will be a strong voice for law enforcement, fighting to ensure our police have the resources and support they need to keep our communities safe from crime and violence.
Marcus opposes any effort to defund the police and will work to strengthen penalties for violent criminals.
He believes in smart, effective policies that protect law-abiding citizens while holding criminals accountable.
Public safety is not negotiable.
Education & Opportunity Investing in Our Future.
Empowering Parents.
Supporting Teachers.
Quality education is the foundation of opportunity.
Marcus supports increased funding for schools, better pay for teachers, and empowering parents to make the best decisions for their children's education.
Every child in District 19 deserves access to excellent schools.
Infrastructure & Growth Building Better Roads.
Modernizing Systems.
Planning for Tomorrow.
District 19 is growing, and our infrastructure needs to keep pace.
Marcus will fight for investments in roads, bridges, water systems, and broadband internet to ensure our communities can thrive now and in the future.
Smart infrastructure investment means better quality of life for all Texans.
