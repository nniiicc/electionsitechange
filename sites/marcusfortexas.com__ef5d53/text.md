MEET MARCUS
Marcus Cardenas was born and raised on the Southside of San Antonio, where family, faith, and community shaped every part of his life.
Marcus grew up learning the values of hard work, integrity, and service to community.
As a small business owner Marcus has created jobs, supported families, and contributed to the economic vitality of District 19.
After attending St.
Joseph's Catholic School, Kazen Middle School, and South San High School—where he played basketball—Marcus began his studies at Palo Alto College.
In 2007, tragedy struck when his older brother passed away in a motorcycle accident.
Marcus made the difficult decision to set aside his education and step into the family plumbing business to help support his loved ones.
Through hard work and determination, Marcus built Vertex Concrete, a thriving business that has served District 19 for over a decade.
As a proud father to his daughter Isabella and uncle to his nieces and nephews, Marcus understands what Texas families need: opportunity, safety, and a brighter future.
