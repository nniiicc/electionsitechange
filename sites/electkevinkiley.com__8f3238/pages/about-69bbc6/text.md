About Kevin Kiley.
The son of a special education teacher and a doctor, Kevin grew up in the communities of California’s newly formed 6th District and attended local public schools.
After college, he taught 10th grade English in inner-city Los Angeles, where he chaired the school’s English Department and led his students to significant academic gains.
After graduating from law school, Kevin defended the U.S.
Constitution in California courts and helped prosecute the civil case against China’s Huawei Technologies for intellectual property theft.
As a prosecutor and Deputy Attorney General, Kevin represented the People of California in cases against violent felons.
After his election to the State Assembly in 2016, Kevin authored groundbreaking new laws on freedom of speech, artificial intelligence, privacy, criminal justice reform, and protections for sexual assault victims.
For his work advancing workers’ rights, he was named the National Legislator of the Year by the Association of Independent Workers.
In 2022, Kevin was elected to Congress, where he has worked across party lines to enact measures that will result in lower costs for gasoline, groceries, utilities and insurance, making life more affordable for local residents.
He secured the only BUILD Grant in California, providing $22.5 million to improve traffic near the Highway 65 / Interstate 80 interchange.
He is credited with getting the Lake Tahoe Restoration Reauthorization Act passed through the House and signed into law, unlocking $410 million to protect this national treasure and mitigate the risk of wildfires.
Kevin also led a successful bipartisan effort to restore Medicare funding for senior programs.
A strong advocate for keeping our communities safe, he is focused on holding violent criminals accountable and protecting children from fentanyl.
Kevin serves on the Judiciary Committee, Transportation and Infrastructure Committee, and the Education and Workforce Committee.
He chairs the House Subcommittee responsible for K–12 Education and Childcare and became the first Independent to chair a committee hearing in over 70 years.
He delivers for our region through federal appropriations and grants to reduce traffic congestion, improve public safety, expand access to water resources, and protect our homes from fire.
He has also made constituent services a priority.
Kevin holds a bachelor’s degree from Harvard, a law degree from Yale, and a master’s in secondary education from Loyola Marymount.
Kevin, his wife Chelsee, and their puppy Jemma reside in Rocklin.
The new 6th Congressional District includes parts of Sacramento County, Placer County and Yolo County, including parts or all of these communities:
Sacramento, Roseville, Rocklin, Citrus Heights, West Sacramento, Carmichael, Orangevale, North Highlands, Foothill Farms
Important issues for our region include cost of living, transportation, public safety, housing and healthcare.
Kevin Kiley is the one Independent Member of the United States House of Representatives, which has allowed him to have unique impact for his district.
Following a nationwide evaluation, the nonpartisan Congressional Management Foundation awarded Kevin its award for Best of Constituent Service.
