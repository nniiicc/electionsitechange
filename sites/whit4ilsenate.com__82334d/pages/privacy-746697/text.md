Senate · Illinois Back to Home Campaign Privacy Privacy Policy This policy explains what information the campaign collects through whit4ilsenate.com, how it is used, and the choices available to you.
Effective September 21, 2026 Who is responsible for this website Whit4ilsenate.com is maintained for Whitfield Harrington for U.S.
Senate by Friends of Whitfield Harrington.
In this policy, “the campaign,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Friends of Whitfield Harrington.
Information we collect We collect information you choose to provide through volunteer, contact, contribution, event, email, and text-message forms.
Depending on the form, this may include your name, email address, mobile number, mailing address, ZIP code, county, volunteer interests, availability, skills, professional background, and the content of messages you send.
The website also receives basic technical information such as pages viewed, approximate traffic source, browser or device type, and interactions with the website.
This information helps us understand how visitors use the site and improve its performance.
How we use information We use information to respond to messages, coordinate volunteers, provide requested campaign updates, organize events, communicate about campaign activity, maintain records, protect website security, comply with applicable law, and improve campaign outreach and website usability.
Volunteer information may be shared within the campaign with coordinators and team members who need it to organize campaign work.
We do not publish volunteer contact information on the public website.
Email and text-message choices Recurring campaign text messages are sent only when you separately and affirmatively opt in.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
Consent to recurring texts is not a condition of any purchase, contribution, volunteer participation, or use of this website.
You may unsubscribe from campaign email messages using the unsubscribe instructions in an email.
We may still send a direct response when you contact the campaign or operational messages reasonably necessary to coordinate volunteer activity you requested.
Mobile information and third-party marketing Mobile information will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.
Text-message opt-in data and consent records will not be shared with third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.
We may use service providers to operate the website, store information, send messages, process contributions, analyze website traffic, or support campaign operations.
They may process information only to provide services to the campaign or as required by law.
Data retention and protection We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, recordkeeping, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and security.
Retention periods vary based on the type of information and why it was collected.
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, but no website, network, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.
Your choices You may ask the campaign to correct or update information you submitted, or to stop optional communications, by using the campaign contact page .
We may need to retain certain records when required by law or for legitimate campaign recordkeeping.
Children’s privacy This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through the website.
External websites This website links to contribution processors, social-media platforms, and other services with their own privacy practices.
This policy does not govern information collected directly by those services.
Changes and contact We may update this policy when campaign practices, technology, or legal requirements change.
The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
Questions about this policy may be sent through the campaign contact page .
Please also review our Terms and Conditions .
