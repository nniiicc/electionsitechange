Whitfield Harrington is running for U.S.
Senate because Illinois deserves leadership that listens, tells the truth, and works on the pressures families experience every day.
He was raised in Belzoni, Mississippi, the 20th of 21 children, in a household shaped by the civil rights struggle.
His father served seven years as chairman of the Humphreys County NAACP — taking the post after the first chairman was assassinated and the second survived an attempt on his life — while working as a pastor and carpenter to support the family.
In 1997 he was elected Governor of Mississippi Boys State through the American Legion and served as one of Mississippi's two senators to Boys Nation in Washington.
He earned a degree in political science from Delta State University and worked in banking and insurance, later running his own insurance practice.
He moved to Chicago in 2013, where he serves as a pastor with a ministry reaching people across states, countries, and continents.
His written platform, Renovate the American Dream, centers affordability, access to health care and education, job creation, rural renewal, American manufacturing, and practical government reform.
