Illinois needs a senator who governs from the center, not the extremes." About the Candidate From the Delta to the pulpit: a life of service.
I was raised in a large family as the 20th of 21 children in Belzoni, Mississippi.
My parents made it a point that we were shaped by family, faith, and the civil rights struggle led by my father.
I relocated to Chicago in 2013, where I serve as a pastor with a ministry reaching people across states, countries, and continents.
My earlier career as a financial professional showed me firsthand the challenges Illinois families face every day.
I'm running as an independent candidate for U.S.
Senate in Illinois while helping to form the American Center Party, a new party dedicated to practical, center-focused leadership.
Illinois needs a senator who governs from the center, not the extremes, from Chicago to downstate.

Redirect 10 to 20 percent of overseas military spending back to American soil, securing our grid, rail, ports, and borders, anchored by a proposed joint-use Air Force base and civilian airport in Chicago's south suburbs.
That's not cutting defense; that's investing defense dollars in American communities instead of foreign soil.
