- Issues -
LOWERING INFLATION AND THE COST OF LIVING
Indiana families are facing higher costs for everyday necessities like groceries, gas, housing, and healthcare.
I believe state leaders must remain focused on policies that support economic stability and help families plan for the future.
I support a balanced approach that encourages job growth, keeps taxes low and predictable, and ensures state spending is responsible.
Smart budgeting and long-term planning help keep Indiana on a solid financial footing while allowing families to keep more of their hard-earned money.
I will work with fellow lawmakers to support policies that strengthen the economy and promote affordability for Hoosier families.
CHAMPIONING FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY
As President of the HSE School Board, I have firsthand experience managing a large budget and making careful financial decisions.
Every choice matters when you are responsible for making decisions using taxpayer dollars.
I will bring this same responsible, disciplined mindset to the Indiana Senate, working collaboratively to support sound budgeting and long-term financial stability for our state.
ADVOCATING FOR QUALITY EDUCATION
Strong schools are essential to strong communities and a strong future for Indiana.
My work on the school board has given me a deep understanding of how education policy affects students, parents, and teachers.
I believe in maintaining high academic standards, supporting parents as partners in their children’s education, keeping classrooms safe and focused on learning, and using education funding wisely.
During my term as President, HSE schools had the highest graduation rate in school history.
I will advocate for practical education policies that support student success, respect local decision-making, and prepare students for life after graduation.
I will also promote high standards for our youth because they CAN and WILL meet and exceed them!
PROMOTING PUBLIC SAFETY
Our people should feel safe in their community.
I support law enforcement and first responders and believe they deserve the tools and resources needed to do their jobs safely and effectively.
I also believe in policies that hold offenders accountable while strengthening community safety efforts.
I will work with local leaders and state officials to support public safety solutions that keep Indiana communities strong and secure.
PROMOTING QUALITY AND AFFORDABLE HEALTHCARE
Throughout my medical career, I have been especially committed to addressing the unique healthcare challenges older Hoosiers face, which is evident in my appointment by Mayor Scott Fadness to the Fishers Senior Center Committee.
Listening to patients and helping them through their healthcare journey, whether that involves maintaining their current health, improving their quality of life, or even end-of-life care.
In the State Senate, I will focus on promoting quality and affordable healthcare to our Hoosiers.
