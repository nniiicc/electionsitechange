Meet Carrie
Now more than ever, Nevada families need a fighter in Washington, D.C., someone with the grit to take on career politicians who prioritize politics over the needs of Nevadans.
That fighter is State Senator Carrie Buck.
From humble beginnings on a small farm, working soybean fields and selling sweet corn door-to-door, Carrie learned that nothing in life is given and everything is earned.
Those invaluable life lessons carried her into Nevada classrooms, where, as a principal, she transformed one of Nevada’s lowest-performing schools into one of the state’s best.
Later, she led the growth of the Pinecrest Academy charter school, providing parents with choices, students with opportunities, and teachers with the resources and support they needed to succeed.
Carrie Buck has spent her life giving a voice to the voiceless.
Now she’s ready to fight for Nevada’s families, restore the American Dream, and help President Trump make Washington work for us again.
