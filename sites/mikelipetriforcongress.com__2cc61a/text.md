GALLERY ISSUES Affordability Affordability starts with letting Americans keep more of what they earn.
That means building off the success of the Trump tax cuts - including lifting the SALT tax cap to protect New York families and eliminating taxes on tips, overtime, and Social Security.
We can build a stronger, more prosperous future by also supporting the creation and growth of families—making it easier to buy a home, raise children, and build a life in our communities without being crushed by taxes and red tape.
Back The Blue During the height of the ‘Defund the Police’ movement, I was the #1 supporter of Law Enforcement lauded by the Suffolk County PBA for being the only state legislator from Long Island to 100% Back The Blue.
The men and women of law enforcement are on the frontlines of defense between good and evil in our communities.
These officers and their families deserve and have earned our full and unapologetic support.
Secure Our Border We are a nation of laws.
We must continue to deport those criminals here illegally and demand that every immigrant - like our parents and grandparents - enter this country legally and with pride.
Hardworking law-abiding immigrants make this country great and the open border policies of the past are a slap-in-the-face to all those who came here the right way and played by the rules.
Stand with Israel Any attempt to boycott, divest from, or sanction Israel must be shut down, eliminated, and swiftly dealt with.
Jew Hate, in all forms - from college campuses to the streets of Gaza - cannot be tolerated.
Hamas, Hezbollah, the Houthis, and the Terror Republic of Iran must know that any acts of terrorism will be responded to with relentless and unmatched force.
Reform Healthcare We must restore the bond between patients and their doctors, not insurance companies and bureaucrats who wish to control the system.
It’s time to put control back where it belongs: with individuals and families.
Our vision is a system that emphasizes personal, portable, and preventative care, empowering Americans to choose their own doctors, keep coverage across jobs or states, deliver a price transparency system fit for the 21st century, and focus on staying healthy—not just getting treated when sick.
Protect Women & Children Biological men should never compete in female sports or use female locker rooms or bathrooms - it’s common sense.
We must end the nonsense and protect women and children.
Water Quality & Environment As a State Assemblyman, I Co-Chaired a statewide Water Quality Taskforce aimed at addressing our aging water infrastructure and cleaning up contaminates and environmental hazards.
Our local environment is a precious resource and protecting our ecosystems, wildlife, and waterways is one of my highest priorities.
Blockchain/Crypto Individual sovereignty and free markets are the backbone of American values.
Innovation in blockchain technology has to be protected from bureaucratic overreach.
Congress should pass a unified crypto regulatory framework that defends freedom and ensures America sets the global standard in digital asset innovation.
