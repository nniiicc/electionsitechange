Mary Ann Perez Receives Endorsement from Harris County Young Democrats
Houston, TX — State Representative Mary Ann Perez has earned the endorsement of the Harris County Young Democrats as she seeks re-election to represent Texas House District 144.
The Harris County Young Democrats cited Rep.
Perez’s strong record of service to working families, her commitment to public education, and her advocacy for economic opportunity and access to affordable healthcare as key reasons for their endorsement.
The organization emphasized Perez’s consistent leadership and ability to deliver results for the diverse communities of District 144.
“As young Democrats, we are looking for leaders who take our generation seriously and govern with accountability.
Representative Mary Ann Perez has a proven record of investing in young people, empowering young staff and fellows, and delivering tangible wins for her district,” said Aylin Rodriguez, President of Harris County Young Democrats.
“Her commitment to economic opportunity, voting access, reproductive healthcare, and climate resilience is exactly the leadership Harris County needs.”
“I’m honored to have the support of the Harris County Young Democrats,” said Perez.
“Their energy and engagement are vital to our democracy, and I look forward to continuing to work alongside them to build a stronger future for our community.”
Mary Ann Perez has represented Texas House District 144 since 2016 and is seeking another term to continue advancing policies that promote opportunity, fairness, and economic growth for families across southeast Houston.
Voters are encouraged to participate in the Democratic Primary Election on March 3 and make their voices heard at the ballot box.
