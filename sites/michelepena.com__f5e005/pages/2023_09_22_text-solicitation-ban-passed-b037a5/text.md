Nobody likes being bombarded by unsolicited text messages from spammers and scammers.
I supported and voted for HB2498, a bill introduced by my colleague Republican Representative Gail Griffin.
This bill, which bans unsolicited text messages in Arizona, passed with bipartisan support and was signed into law by Katie Hobbs.
Specifically, the bill prohibits a seller, solicitor, or anyone acting on behalf of a seller or solicitor from initiating an outbound text message to a telephone number that is on the National Do-Not-Call Registry.
