To register to vote in Arizona you must meet the following qualifications (A.R.S. § 16-101): Be a citizen of the United States of America Be a resident of Arizona and the county listed on your registration Be 18 years of age on or before the day of the next regular General Election You cannot register to vote in Arizona if (A.R.S. § 16-101): You have been convicted of a felony and have not yet had your civil rights restored.
For a first-time felony conviction, civil rights are automatically restored upon completion of a person’s sentence and payment of any restitution.
