HB2651, sponsored by my colleague Representative Barbara Parker, received overwhelming bipartisan support as it was signed into law.
This bill will help with our missing foster children crisis in Arizona while helping to combat child trafficking and fight for all children to be able to grow up safe and soundly in the state of Arizona.
