Idaho Dairy Industry PAC Endorses Scott Bedke to be Idaho’s Next Lieutenant Governor
Boise, IDAHO – The Idaho Dairy Industry PAC is announcing its endorsement of Scott Bedke to become Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.
David Claiborne, President of the Idaho Dairy Industry PAC, says Bedke is a leading advocate for Idaho agriculture and has consistently delivered proven, conservative results.
“The Idaho Dairy Industry PAC is endorsing the Lieutenant Governor candidate who understands and fights for the dairy industry, the candidate who embraces the importance of agriculture to Idaho, the candidate who has true and proven leadership skills, and the candidate who has integrity.
That candidate is Scott Bedke,” said Claiborne.
Bedke, a fourth generation Idaho rancher, has a long track record of defending and promoting Idaho’s agricultural industry.
Under Bedke’s conservative leadership, he led the charge to make Idaho the least regulated state in the nation, while protecting Idaho’s land and water rights from the federal government.
“The Idaho Dairy Industry PAC is a driving force behind Idaho’s reputation as an agricultural powerhouse and I am honored to have earned their endorsement,” said Bedke.
“Not only do they advocate for the backbone of Idaho’s economy, but they value integrity, respect, and doing things the Idaho way.
That’s how I’ve conducted my business as a rancher and Speaker, and I will keep it up as Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.”
