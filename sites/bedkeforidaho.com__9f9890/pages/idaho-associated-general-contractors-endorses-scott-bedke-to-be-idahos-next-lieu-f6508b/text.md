Idaho Associated General Contractors Endorses Scott Bedke to be Idaho’s Next Lieutenant Governor
Boise, IDAHO – The Idaho Associated General Contractors (AGC) is announcing its strong endorsement of Scott Bedke to become Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.
Wayne Hammon, CEO of Idaho AGC, says Bedke is committed to working with industry and community stakeholders to achieve sustainable growth.
“Those of us working in Idaho’s construction industry are proud to support Scott Bedke in his race for Lieutenant Governor,” said Hammon.
“Since joining the state legislature two decades ago, Scott has proven time and time again that he’s a friend – not just to Idaho, but to those of us who are building Idaho’s future.”
Under Bedke’s conservative leadership, Idaho has become the least regulated state in the nation, as well as the best place to live, to work, and to raise a family.
As Speaker, Bedke worked to ensure that growth pays for itself, while maintaining and improving the infrastructure to support it.
“Idaho’s business environment and climate are the envy of the nation, and the unprecedented growth in our state presents both challenges and opportunities,” said Bedke.
“Smart growth will be one of my main focuses as Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.
I am proud to have earned the Idaho AGC’s endorsement, because we will work together to ensure all this new growth does not change the Idaho we know and love.”
To donate, or learn more about the Bedke campaign, visit www.bedkeforidaho.com
