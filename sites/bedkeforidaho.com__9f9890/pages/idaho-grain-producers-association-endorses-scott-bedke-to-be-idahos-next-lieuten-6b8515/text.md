Idaho Grain Producers Association Endorses Scott Bedke to be Idaho’s Next Lieutenant Governor
Boise, IDAHO – The Idaho Grain Producers Association, a leading voice in Idaho agriculture, is proudly announcing its endorsement of Scott Bedke to become Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.
IGPA President Lucas Spratling says Bedke is the only candidate in this race who has the knowledge and experience to help navigate the complex issues of Idaho’s agriculture industry.
“Scott Bedke has unparalleled first-hand knowledge of agriculture and water issues.
It is critical that we elect leaders who understand the importance of agriculture and who support Idaho’s rural communities,” said Spratling.
“Scott is that candidate, with the experience and values to be Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.”
Bedke, a fourth generation Idaho rancher, has a proven track record of defending our land and water rights from the federal government.
As Speaker, Bedke has led the charge to turn Idaho into the least regulated state in the nation, provide historic tax cuts, and create a climate where businesses and workers prosper.
“Our agriculture industry is the backbone of Idaho’s economy.
As a fourth generation Idaho rancher, I am proud to have the endorsement of the Idaho Grain Producers Association,” said Bedke.
“Between summer droughts and Idaho’s record growth, we need a leader with proven, innovative results in solving water issues across our state.
I’ve done that as a rancher and Speaker, and will continue to do so as Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.”
