Governor Scott Bedke Announces Re-Election Campaign September 8, 2025 Boise, Idaho – Idaho Lt.
Governor Scott Bedke today announced his campaign for re-election, pledging to continue fighting for our Idaho way of life and ensuring Idaho remains a place of outstanding opportunity for generations to come.
“As your Lt.
Governor, I’ve worked every day to protect our Idaho way of life and our state’s future,” said Bedke.
“I’ve never forgotten that I work for my fellow Idahoans, and that means defending our freedoms and our borders, limiting government interference, and delivering meaningful tax relief.
None of this matters though, unless we assure that Idaho’s children and families continue to have all the best opportunities for the future.
I’m proud of what we’ve accomplished, but there’s more work to do.
As a proud constitutional conservative, I will never stop fighting to keep Idaho safe, strong, and prosperous.” Lt.
Governor Bedke’s re-election effort is being endorsed by, and has the strong support of, Idaho’s current Governor Brad Little as well as Idaho’s three immediate former governors.
Governor Brad Little “Scott Bedke has been a strong conservative partner in leading Idaho forward.
Together, we’ve cut red tape, made Idaho the least regulated state in the nation, delivered record tax relief, and empowered Idaho parents with the tools they need to raise their families in a manner that respects their values and beliefs.
Scott is a trusted conservative who delivers results for Idahoans.
His integrity and leadership make him the right person to continue serving as our Lt.
Governor, and I’m proud to endorse his re-election.
Former Governor C.L.
“Butch” Otter “I’ve worked alongside Scott Bedke for years as he’s served his fellow Idahoans.
I’ve seen firsthand his dedication to defending Idaho’s conservative values and way of life.
From protecting our water rights and land to fighting for historic tax relief and championing strategic investments in schools and infrastructure, Scott has always put Idahoans first.
He brings his rock-solid values of family, faith, and hard work to the Office of Lieutenant Governor every day and is a proven leader we can count on.
Scott has my full support in his re-election campaign.” Former Governor and U.S.
Senator Jim Risch “Scott Bedke embodies the values that make Idaho strong: hard work, faith, family, and conservative leadership.
As a rancher, small businessman and public servant, he understands the needs of his fellow Idahoans and is a proven conservative leader.” Former Governor Dirk Kempthorne “Scott Bedke is the kind of conservative leader Idahoans can trust.
He has consistently defended our Constitution, stood firm against federal overreach and made sure Idaho remains a model for fiscal responsibility.
Scott’s vision, experience and integrity are exactly what Idaho needs to safeguard our values for the next generation.
I’m proud to support his re-election.” With decades of service as an Idaho Representative, Speaker of the Idaho House, and now as Lt.
Governor, Scott Bedke has been a driving force behind Idaho’s conservative policies, including shepherding the largest tax cuts in state history, defending parental rights, and ensuring responsible investments in education and infrastructure.
In his time as Speaker of the House, Bedke drove the conservative budgeting that has strengthened Idaho’s place as one of the top state economies in the country.
He also led the way in protecting Idahoans’ Constitutional rights, especially the 2nd amendment, and settled long-standing water disputes.
The Idaho native graduated from Oakley High School and, together with his brother, runs his family cattle ranch in Southern Idaho.
Scott and Sarah have raised four children and are the proud grandparents of 16 grandchildren.
