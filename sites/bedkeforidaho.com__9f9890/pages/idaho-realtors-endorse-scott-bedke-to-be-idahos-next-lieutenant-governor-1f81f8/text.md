Idaho REALTORS® Endorse Scott Bedke to be Idaho’s Next Lieutenant Governor
Boise, IDAHO – Idaho REALTORS® are endorsing Scott Bedke to become Idaho’s next Lieutenant Governor.
Patrick Malone, President of Idaho REALTORS®, says Bedke’s success with solving problems and delivering results will benefit all Idahoans.
“Idaho is leading the nation in growth, both economically and in population.
Scott Bedke’s vision is straightforward, achievable, and places an emphasis on keeping Idaho the best place to live, to work, and to raise a family,” said Malone.
“Idahoans need a leader who is willing and capable of solving complex issues.
As Speaker, and a fourth generation Idaho rancher, Scott has proven he’s that leader.”
Under his conservative leadership, Bedke helped balance Idaho’s budget, deliver historic tax cuts, and created a positive economic climate for all businesses.
As Speaker, Bedke led the charge to cut red tape, making Idaho the least regulated state in the nation.
“I’ve had the privilege to meet and work with Idaho REALTORS® across Idaho for years, and have been able to witness, firsthand, their drive to ensure that Idaho’s economy is moving in the right direction,” said Bedke.
“Idaho is growing, but we can all agree that this new growth should not change who we are.
As your next Lieutenant Governor, I will continue working to ensure that Idaho is a place we are proud to call home.”
