Home Meet Mark Issues Endorsements Volunteer Contact Contribute Military Service Mark served in the Army continuously in various capacities from 1981 to 2016.
This service included the Army Reserve (part time), as an Army Civilian (full time), and a 2 ½ mobilization to the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA).
The latter assignment was Active Duty from which he was discharged honorably, which qualifies Mark as a Veteran.
While assigned to DIA, he was promoted to Lieutenant Colonel at the Pentagon where he was appointed as the Strategic Issues Cell Chief of the Iraq Intelligence Working Group (IZWG).
This DIA-staffed group prepared daily intelligence briefings for the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs of Staff, SECDEF, and occasionally for the President.
Mark served for 28 years, commissioned as a Military Intelligence officer.
His parallel service as an Army Civilian totaled over 30 years.
