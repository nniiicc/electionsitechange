Where I Stand
A state legislator deliberates and votes on dozens of issues and hundreds of bills each year - legislation affecting Connecticut’s communities, businesses, families, and individuals.
As one of 187 legislators, I cannot personally control every issue or every bill that comes before the General Assembly.
What I can control is how I approach those issues: the principles that guide my decisions, the problems I believe need to be addressed, and the solutions I am willing to pursue.
For this campaign, I am focusing on four major areas:
Affordability.
Education.
Conservation.
Liberty.
Connecticut can do better.
After years of one-party control in Hartford, many of the problems we face today remain unresolved - or have gotten worse.
Republicans are outnumbered two-to-one in the legislature, but being in the minority does not mean being without influence.
We make a difference by shaping bipartisan solutions, mitigating bad legislation, and advancing good ideas that ultimately gain support across the aisle.
Over the coming days, I will be releasing position statements on the individual issues within these four areas.
I will explain the problem, what I believe is causing it, the solutions I support, my legislative record, and what I intend to pursue in the next session.
These are the issues that matter to our families, our communities, and the future of Connecticut - and this is where I stand.
Fighting for Affordability
Connecticut families are being squeezed by rising utility bills, taxes, healthcare costs, and the overall cost of living.
Mark believes state government must live within its means and make it easier, not harder, for working families, seniors, and small businesses to remain in Connecticut.
Mark’s priorities include:
- Lowering electricity bills
- Cutting taxes and controlling state spending
- Addressing rising healthcare costs
Investing in Education
Every student deserves access to a strong education, regardless of the pathway that works best for them.
Mark supports directing more state education funding to local schools, reducing the pressure on municipal property taxpayers, and respecting the choices of students and families.
Mark’s priorities include:
- Shifting more state funding to local schools
- Easing the burden on local property taxpayers
- Supporting public, technical, vocational, charter, private, and homeschool pathways
Defending Our Rural Character
The communities of the 62nd District are defined by their farms, forests, waterways, open spaces, and strong tradition of local decision-making.
Mark will continue working to protect these resources while opposing one-size-fits-all mandates from Hartford.
Mark’s priorities include:
- Protecting waterways
- Conserving farms and open space
- Preserving local control over community decisions
Championing Liberty
Mark believes government must respect the constitutional rights, personal freedoms, and economic independence of every Connecticut resident.
He will continue standing against unnecessary government overreach and defending the freedoms that allow families, businesses, and communities to thrive.
Mark’s priorities include:
- Upholding constitutional rights
- Defending individual liberty
- Promoting economic freedom
