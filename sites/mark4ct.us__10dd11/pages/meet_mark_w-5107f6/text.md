Home Meet Mark Issues Endorsements Volunteer Contact Contribute Military Service Meet Mark Mark resides in Granby with his wife Carole and her daughter Leanna.
Together they have three daughters, two sons, and five grandchildren, who all live with their families nearby.
After Mark served in the Army for over thirty years, he again felt a calling to public service.
This led to his election as your state representative beginning in January 2021.
When not working on legislation, he enjoys interceding with state agencies on behalf of constituents.
As time allows, Mark volunteers with various community organizations.
