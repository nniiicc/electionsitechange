Photo: Iron Range Resources & Rehabilitation Workforce housing, built together Good jobs need places for people to live.
Working across the aisle, I was glad to help secure a $4.58 million grant from Iron Range Resources and Rehabilitation (IRRR) and a $1.5 million state loan for the Wilderness Escape workforce housing project.
It’s housing that people who work in our communities can afford to live in.
And it came together because people sat down at the same table and focused on what northern Minnesota needed.
