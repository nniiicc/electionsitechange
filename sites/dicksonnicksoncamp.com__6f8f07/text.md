I am Mrs.
Tisha Dickson Nickson.
I am a caregiver at heart and have worked in the healthcare industry for 30 years.
Over the decades, I have witnessed a troubling pattern: patients receiving less care while paying more for it.
While sharing my Healthcare for All platform with my son, I realized that he represents the very people I am fighting for.
He works hard every day, yet because the federal minimum wage has remained at $7.25 per hour since 2009, he does not earn a living wage.
He brings home approximately $1,600 per month.
With rent exceeding $1,000, very little remains for utilities, food, transportation, and other basic necessities.
To stay afloat, he relies on a roommate to help cover household expenses.
As consumer prices continue to rise, coverage under the Affordable Care Act has become financially out of reach for many working individuals.
Sadly, many others face the same reality—earning too much to qualify for Medicaid, yet too little to afford private insurance.
Some qualify for only $15 to $30 per month in SNAP benefits, which is not enough to keep pace with today’s food costs.
“Healthcare and food are basic human rights, and no one should have to live without them.” Dickson Nickson is for the people.
I am committed to expanding Medicaid so that working families are not left without coverage.
I will advocate for increased SNAP benefits that reflect the real cost of food today.
I will also work to establish a local mental health hospital to support those struggling with depression, stress, and crisis—before situations escalate into tragedy.
Life is precious.
Healthcare and food are basic human rights, and no one should have to live without them.
