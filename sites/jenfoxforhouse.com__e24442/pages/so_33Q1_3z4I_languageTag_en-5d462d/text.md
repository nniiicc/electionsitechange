FROM THE CAMPAIGN OF JEN FOX 41B Weekly Issue 2 · Tuesday, September 8, 2026 · jenfoxforhouse.com LABOR DAY Both sides of the counter This weekend the International Union of Operating Engineers Local 49 endorsed our campaign.
Local 49’s members run the equipment on Minnesota’s road and bridge projects, including the Highway 61 rebuild through Hastings that is slated to start in 2027.
Their endorsement goes to candidates who will fund that work and pay it a fair wage.
I will.
I make payroll at Spiral, and I am endorsed by the Minnesota AFL-CIO and ten more unions, from Local 49 to Education Minnesota.
Those two things are not in tension.
A district where people are paid enough to spend money downtown is the same district where a small business can keep its doors open.
THIS WEEK Where things stand # days to Election Day, # days until ballots go out.
41B is an open seat, the Minnesota House is tied 67 to 67, and this seat was decided by 510 votes in 2024.
Since the last email: Local 49 joined the endorsement list, the yard sign count passed 375, and the door knocks are set through Election Day, now four days a week : Wednesday at 5, Thursday at 5, Saturday at 11 and Sunday at 2.
If you have been waiting for a good week to start, this is it.

MAKE YOUR PLAN BEHIND THE CAMPAIGN The work nobody sees There are now more than 375 Fox signs up across Hastings, Cottage Grove and the townships.
Every one of them was carried to a yard by a neighbor, on their own time, in their own car, route by route over the last month.
Behind those signs is work that never makes a photo: the people who went back through the sign list address by address so nobody who asked gets missed, the ones who print the walk lists and pair up first-time knockers, and the ones who spend Tuesday afternoons on the phone.
On a weekend about work, that is the work this campaign runs on.
