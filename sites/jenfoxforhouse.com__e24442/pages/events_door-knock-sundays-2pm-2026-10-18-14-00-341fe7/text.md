Door Knock, Sundays 2pm
Sun, Oct 18
|Park High School front parking lot, Cottage Grove
Every Sunday afternoon we knock doors in 41B, starting from the front parking lot of Park High School (8040 80th St S, Cottage Grove).
No experience needed.
You'll get a short walkthrough, a partner if you want one, and a list of a few dozen houses.
Come for as much of the afternoon as you can.
