DONATE ABOUT ME Meet Jen Fox!
ROOTED IN COMMUNITY Our community deserves reliable representation from someone who knows our district and is committed to bringing our voices to the Capitol.
As a lifelong Hastings resident, I couldn’t imagine a better place to live, work, or raise a family.
My decades of involvement in the community, both individually and as a former member of the City Council, has uniquely positioned me with a deep understanding of the issues our district is facing today and the importance of having someone in office who will do something about it. ​ I am also a part owner of Spiral Brewery, a popular business on Second Street in downtown Hastings where I run the Sales and Distribution Division.
I have seen the businesses come and go as they struggle to adapt to an uncertain economy and keep up with ever changing mandates. ​ As a small business owner, I know the challenges our businesses are facing and want to be a partner in creating a thriving local economy that works for everyone in Cottage Grove, Denmark Township, Nininger and Hastings.
READY TO SERVE Minnesota families and small businesses are all feeling the hardships of political and economic uncertainty.
As a lifelong Hastings resident, a small business owner, and former member of the Hastings City Council, I know how to advocate for the issues we care about most and can work across the aisle to get things done.
I am running to bring a voice from the district to St.
Paul on behalf of business owners, local governments, and all residents in District 41B.
I am deeply committed to bringing in voices from our community and holding a seat at the table for those directly impacted by our decisions to ensure no one is left behind or forgotten.
