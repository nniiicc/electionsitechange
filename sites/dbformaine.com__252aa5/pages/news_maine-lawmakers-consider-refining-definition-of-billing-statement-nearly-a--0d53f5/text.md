| The Maine Wire reported on legislative efforts to clarify the scope of Maine's law banning banks from charging customers fees for receiving paper billing statements — a law championed by Senator Bailey.
The article examined how the existing definition of "billing statement" had created ambiguity in enforcement, prompting legislators to consider a technical fix under LD 2061 (An Act to Clarify the Prohibition on Paper Billing Statement Fees).
