| Maine's leading business publication, Mainebiz, reported on the implications of Senator Bailey's newly signed LD 2129 (An Act to Protect Maine People from the Harmful Impacts of Medical Debt).
The article explored how the law benefits not just individual families but Maine's broader economic health by preventing medical debt from spiraling into home foreclosures and wage seizures that destabilize household finances.
