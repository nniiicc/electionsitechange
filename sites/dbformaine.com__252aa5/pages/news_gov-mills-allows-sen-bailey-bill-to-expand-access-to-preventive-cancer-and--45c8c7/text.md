Mills Allows Sen.
Bailey Bill to Expand Access to Preventive Cancer and Cardiac Screenings for Firefighters and Police Officers to Become Law 6/13/2026 0 Comments Governor Janet Mills allowed Senator Bailey's LD 784 to become law, expanding access to preventive cancer and cardiac screenings for firefighters and law enforcement officers in Maine.
Saco Bay News covered the milestone, noting that first responders are at elevated risk for occupational cancers due to toxic exposures.
The law ensures these workers can access early detection screenings that can be lifesaving — reflecting Senator Bailey's ongoing commitment to protecting those who protect Maine communities.
