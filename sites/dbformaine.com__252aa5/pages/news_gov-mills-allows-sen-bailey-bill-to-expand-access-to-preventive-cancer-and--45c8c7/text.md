| Governor Janet Mills allowed Senator Bailey's LD 784 to become law, expanding access to preventive cancer and cardiac screenings for firefighters and law enforcement officers in Maine.
Saco Bay News covered the milestone, noting that first responders are at elevated risk for occupational cancers due to toxic exposures.
