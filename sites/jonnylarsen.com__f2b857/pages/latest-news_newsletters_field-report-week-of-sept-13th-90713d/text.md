For those of us who lived through that day, the memories can still feel remarkably close, where we were, who we were with, and how the world seemed to change in an instant.
For Jonny, those memories are especially personal, tied to his time serving with the US Marine Corps in Washington, D.C.
As we mark this anniversary, he reflects on what he witnessed that morning, the service and sacrifice that followed, and the sense of unity Americans shared in the days after.
