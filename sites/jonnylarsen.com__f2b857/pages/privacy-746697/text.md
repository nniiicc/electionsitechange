By using our website or providing us your information, you agree to this policy.
If you have any questions, email us at privacy@jonnyutah4congress.com .
Information We Collect Information you provide.
When you sign up for updates, volunteer, contact us, or complete one of our forms or ads, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code or mailing address, and any message or information you choose to send us.
If you make a contribution, federal law requires that we also collect your occupation and the name of your employer.
Information collected automatically.
Like most websites, we automatically collect limited technical information — such as your IP address, browser and device type, general location, referring pages, and how you use the site — through cookies and similar technologies, to operate and improve the site and to measure our outreach and advertising.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: • send you campaign updates, news, and opportunities to get involved, and respond to your messages; • process and acknowledge contributions and meet our reporting obligations; • organize volunteers, events, and voter outreach; • operate, secure, analyze, and improve our website and communications; and • comply with the law and protect the rights and safety of the campaign and others.
How We Share Your Information We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any other organization, campaign, committee, or advertiser.
Your information is used by our campaign only.
The one exception is the trusted service providers who operate the campaign on our behalf — for example, our email and text-message delivery tools, website host, payment processor, and analytics and advertising-measurement providers.
They may use your information only to provide those services to us, and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
We may also disclose information when we believe in good faith that it is required by law, subpoena, or legal process, or is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the campaign, our supporters, or the public.
Federal law note.
Federal election law separately prohibits any campaign from selling or using contributor information obtained from FEC reports to solicit contributions or for commercial purposes.
We honor that restriction.
Public Disclosure of Contributions Contributions are processed by ActBlue under ActBlue’s privacy policy .
Federal law requires every federal campaign to report certain information about contributors — name, mailing address, occupation, and employer — for contributions that exceed $200 in an election cycle.
That information is filed with the Federal Election Commission and becomes part of the public record.
This is a legal requirement of running for federal office and is separate from, and not controlled by, this policy.
Text Messages If you provide your phone number, you consent to receive recurring updates from the campaign, including by automated text message.
Consent is not a condition of any contribution or purchase.
Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help.
Email If you give us your email address, we may send you campaign updates and requests to get involved.
You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email, or by contacting us at privacy@jonnyutah4congress.com .
Analytics & Advertising When you sign up through this website, we may share a limited set of the information you gave us — such as your email address, phone number, or ZIP code — with these advertising-measurement providers so they can tell whether an ad we ran led to your sign-up.
Before it is sent, that information is converted into an irreversible, scrambled (“hashed”) form: they receive a string of characters, not your readable email address or phone number.
This information may be sent both from your browser and directly from our servers.
We do this only to measure and improve our own outreach — these providers act on our behalf, and as stated above we do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
Links to Other Websites Our site links to third-party services such as ActBlue, our merch store, and social media.
We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites, which have their own privacy policies.
We encourage you to review them.
How We Protect Your Information We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect your information.
No method of transmitting or storing data is 100% secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
How Long We Keep Your Information We keep your information for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, to run the campaign, and to meet our legal and recordkeeping obligations, after which we take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify it.
Children’s Privacy This website and our forms are intended for adults.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.
If you believe a minor has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.
Changes to This Policy We may update this policy from time to time.
When we do, we will revise the effective date above and post the updated policy on this page.
Your continued use of the site after a change takes effect means you accept the updated policy.
