Rep.
Kennedy votes for the war Last week was another jam packed week of news.
Mike Kennedy, along with the rest of our Congressional delegation, voted NAY on the War Powers Resolution.
My heart aches for the families of the four American service members who paid the ultimate price for our country this month.
The day before, they voted in favor of a $95 billion blueprint to increase funding for the war.
That war has Utah's average gas price above $4 a gallon and the price of oil above $100 a barrel.
The bill that passed to stop Congress from trading stocks only stops members from buying new individual stocks .
What they already own, they keep, and they can still sell.
It did not include the President , Vice President or the Administration .
Bolted onto the bill were voter ID items that would directly impact Utah and our mail-in voting.
It would require you to mail a photocopy of your ID in with your ballot.
Please check out our Facebook page to read more on these issues and many others.
