After the radio interview, I was joined by the Sevier County Democratic Party at Richfield Park.

I spoke with many of them, including a gentleman who aspires to make it to his 100th birthday.

The Devastating Fires Rep.
Mike Kennedy recently blamed the U.S.
Forest Service for not doing their jobs well enough to fight these fires.
In 2025 when he voted to pass the BBB, Kennedy and the rest of our Utah delegation exacerbated this problem.
Visit my FaceBook post to read about how he voted to cut funds and staff that ultimately lead to the dire situation we face.
This past Monday evening, I attended the Widemouth 2 Fire Community Meeting in Millard County to hear updates about the fire that has now spread to Sevier County.
I am thankful for and praise our firefighters who are working long and tiring shifts to keep everyone safe.
They are fighting an uphill battle right now, risking their lives for everyone impacted.
A service and self sacrifice for which I have the utmost respect.
