See a portion of my speech here Chair Woods has been a huge supporter of ours for over a year since we first met in May of 2025 at Kane County Convention.

Fire devistation continues As of Friday, August 14th 2026, the Widemouth 2 fire has been 65% contained.
On August 7th we lost two brave men who were instrumental in battling the fire in an unfortunate helicopter crash.
Elliott from Porterville, CA and Andersen from Stateline, NV, we honor you for your bravery and dedication to safety of our community.
You will not be forgotten, till Valhalla we met brothers.
Semper Fi.
