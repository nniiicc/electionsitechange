About Bulla Local boy.
Twenty years in the lobby.
One foot on the sidewalk, the other on the street outside the hotel.
Bulla Eastman was born and raised in Aliamanu — a proud graduate of Kamehameha Schools and the University of Hawaiʻi.
For the last twenty years he has worked in Waikīkī's hotels and condominiums in safety, security, and risk management.
He started at the Princess Kaʻiulani and the Sheraton Moana Surfrider, then moved to Aqua Hospitality.
When Aston Resorts acquired Aqua, he ran risk management, loss prevention, and OSHA compliance across 50+ hotels, three states, two countries, and 3,000+ employees.
Today he runs his own consulting firm helping hotels, condominiums, small businesses, and nonprofits keep people safe.
He is also an ordained minister who has spent years doing outreach in prisons, on the streets, and in communities — walking alongside individuals and families facing addiction, homelessness, and hardship.
He and his wife Lyn have four children and ten grandchildren.
They live in Waikīkī.
Bulla is familiar with night operations and the back-of-house teams that keep these blocks running — because for two decades he's been one of them.
Over his career in Waikīkī he has partnered directly with HPD, CRU, FBI, ICE, DHS, Secret Service, and the JTTF.
He is running because Waikīkī is slipping — and the people who actually live and work here deserve a representative who shows up, knows their name, and has been doing the work long before the election.
