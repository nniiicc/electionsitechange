Donate Donate Why vote for John?
When people ask me why I fell in love with my husband, my answer has never been about one grand moment.
It was the countless everyday moments that showed me exactly who he is.
I fell in love with a man who listens before he speaks.
Whether he’s talking with a neighbor, a coworker, or someone he just met, he genuinely wants to understand their story.
He believes every person deserves to be heard, and that’s something I’ve watched him practice every single day.
I fell in love with someone who keeps his word.
If he promises to help, he shows up.
If he commits to a project, he finishes it.
If he makes a mistake, he owns it.
Integrity isn’t something he turns on when people are watching – it’s simply who he is.
I’ve also seen his determination.
When he believes something is worth fighting for, he doesn’t give up because the work is difficult.
He studies the issues, asks questions, seeks different perspectives, and works until he finds practical solutions.
He isn’t interested in headlines – he’s interested in results.
Perhaps what I admire most is his humility.
He doesn’t pretend to have all the answers.
He knows leadership is about surrounding yourself with good people, listening carefully, and making thoughtful decisions with honesty and courage.
As his wife, I’ve had the privilege of seeing the person behind the public image.
I’ve seen the quiet acts of kindness that never make the news.
I’ve seen the sacrifices he’s made for our family and for others.
I’ve seen the character that remains steady whether anyone is watching or not.
No candidate is perfect, and my husband would be the first to tell you that.
But I know his heart.
I know his values.
And I know that the same qualities that made me fall in love with him are the qualities that will guide him as a leader: integrity, compassion, perseverance, humility, and a genuine desire to serve.
That’s why I’m proud to stand beside him – not just as his wife, but as someone who believes in the person he is.
I hope you’ll take the opportunity to get to know him, because I believe you’ll see what I saw all those years ago: a good man who is ready to work hard for the people he hopes to represent.
Thank you for your trust, your support, and for being part of this journey with our family.
