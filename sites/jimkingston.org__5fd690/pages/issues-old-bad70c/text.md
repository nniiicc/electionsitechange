Folks across South Georgia are working hard, raising families, serving their communities, and they deserve leaders who take their concerns seriously.
Jim’s spent years listening, showing up, and doing the work, and the issues on this page reflect what he hears in living rooms, on docks, at church, and in small businesses across this district.
This is what the Good Fight means to him… I have visited with Georgians across every county in the First District.
The message is clear: Defend President Trump, Make America Affordable Again, and stop the madness in Washington.
That’s exactly what I plan to do as your Congressman.” -Jim Kingston Lower Taxes = More Economic Freedom Government doesn’t create prosperity, hardworking Americans do.
I will always fight to lower taxes across the board so families keep more of what they earn, and small businesses can thrive.
A simpler and fairer tax code will grow our economy and reward hard work, not punish success.
End the Cycle of Reckless Spending & Control the Debt Washington’s addiction to spending is bankrupting future generations.
I will support efforts to restore fiscal discipline that champions regular order in appropriations, advocates for a return to balanced budgets and holds government agencies accountable for every taxpayer dollar.
Medicaid: Empower States, Protect the Truly Needy Medicaid should be a safety net, not a trap.
I support work requirements for able-bodied adults and no Medicaid handouts to illegal aliens.
We must ensure the most vulnerable get the care they need, without bankrupting the country.
Medicare: Protect Our Seniors & Preserve for Future Generations I will fight to preserve Medicare for seniors who have paid into it while pushing for reforms that ensure its long-term solvency.
That includes rooting out waste, fraud and abuse, and giving seniors more control over their healthcare choices.
Secure the Border, Enforce the Law A nation without borders is not a sovereign nation.
President Trump is restoring order and I’m running for Congress to back him up.
When it comes to protecting our border, the good fight is far from over.
I support deploying more resources for border enforcement to crack down on cartels and traffickers, and a legal immigration policy that is merit-based and focused on safety and our nation’s interest.
Safeguard the America-First Trade Policy I will fight for trade deals that put American workers, farmers and manufacturers first.
No more lopsided agreements that ship jobs overseas or make us dependent on adversaries.
Fair, reciprocal trade is essential to rebuilding our manufacturing base, protecting national security and growing the American middle class.
Rebuild & Refocus Our Military President Trump has shown the world that peace through strength still works.
I support rebuilding our military to ensure readiness and technological superiority.
That means investing in our troops and strengthening our local military installations at Kings Bay Naval Submarine Base, Fort Stewart and Hunter Army Airfield.
Getting Tough on Our Adversaries America is once again regaining its footing on the world stage, but we can’t let up.
I will push for a foreign policy that defends our sovereignty, strengthens our alliances and confronts threats like Iran, Russia and China.
This includes ending the importation of foreign sources of energy, cutting our dependence on Chinese manufacturing for critical goods, stopping the theft of our intellectual property and buying up U.S. farmland.
Protecting Female Athletes Protecting hard-earned opportunities for America’s women and girls is not about hate or exclusion.
It is about common sense and biological reality.
I support the Protection of Women and Girls in Sports Act and the promotion of state and local laws that prevent schools from being punished for protecting female athletes.
Defend Life at Every Stage I am proudly pro-life and I believe it is our moral responsibility to defend the sanctity of life.
That includes banning late-term abortions, ending taxpayer funding for abortion providers and protecting women by promoting adoption and support for expectant mothers.
We must also protect the conscience rights of medical professionals and faith-based organizations that object to participating in abortions.
Uphold the Second Amendment The Second Amendment is not up for negotiation.
As a proud gun owner, I will oppose any attempt by radical gun control advocates to trample on our most fundamental right to protect our families, our property and our freedom.
Put Parents Back in Charge of Education Parents deserve to know what their kids are being taught, and have the final say.
We have to stop woke indoctrination in classrooms, end radical gender ideology in schools and give every family access to a quality education that reflects their values.
Secure Our Elections Free and fair elections are the cornerstone of our Republic.
I support voter ID requirements, banning ballot harvesting and ensuring that every legal vote counts, and only legal votes count.
Stand with Law Enforcement, Crack Down on Crime I proudly back the blue, oppose dangerous soft-on-crime policies and support giving our law enforcement agencies the resources they need to keep our communities safe.
That means tougher penalties for violent criminals, ending sanctuary cities and shutting down the fentanyl pipeline killing Americans.
Empower Rural Georgia Southeast Georgia’s future depends on strong farms, fast internet and modern infrastructure.
I will fight for policies that keep our ag industry competitive, bring broadband to every home and improve roads and bridges without raising taxes.
Unleash American Energy Sky-high energy prices are crushing families and small businesses.
We need to end the left’s war on American energy and restore U.S. energy independence.
