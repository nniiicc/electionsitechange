I built my life in the insurance industry, working with families, small businesses, and community leaders across the state.
I’ve seen firsthand how federal red tape makes everything harder… for farmers trying to stay afloat, for small towns trying to grow, for businesses trying to hire, for families who are trying to chase the American Dream like I am.
Those experiences taught me something simple yet important: Government should work or get out of the way.
And when it doesn’t?
Ordinary people pay the price.
