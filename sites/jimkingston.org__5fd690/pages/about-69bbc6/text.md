A TRUE SON OF SOUTHEAST GEORGIA
I grew up on the docks and dirt roads of Southeast Georgia.
It’s the kind of place where faith comes first, family keeps you grounded, and nobody’s too important to pitch in.
My brother and sisters and I learned early what service looks like.
Dad spent years representing this district, and Mom kept all of us pointed in the right direction.
That childhood gave me a front-row seat to what real leadership feels like: listening more than you talk, working harder than you boast, and never forgetting who you serve.
What I didn’t realize then was how much those lessons would shape the rest of my life.

CHASING THE AMERICAN DREAM
My career hasn’t been in politics.
I built my life in the insurance industry, working with families, small businesses, and community leaders across the state.
I’ve seen firsthand how federal red tape makes everything harder… for farmers trying to stay afloat, for small towns trying to grow, for businesses trying to hire, for families who are trying to chase the American Dream like I am.
Those experiences taught me something simple yet important:
Government should work or get out of the way.
And when it doesn’t?
Ordinary people pay the price.
ROOTED IN GEORGIA VALUES
I’m a son of Savannah.
I was raised with a tin boat, a huge extended family, and a deep love for this region.
I’ve spent most of my life crisscrossing these counties: hosting fish fries, knocking on doors, hauling candidates in my truck, and making the follow-up calls nobody else remembered.
South Georgia has a way of teaching you the value of showing up and following through.
Faith, family, freedom: those aren’t slogans here.
They’re the blueprint.

THE NEXT GENERATION OF CONSERVATIVE LEADERSHIP
Young leaders like Charlie Kirk helped spark a generation of young conservatives who cared deeply about the direction of this country, and that spirit still matters.
Public service is in my DNA, and conservatism is in my veins.
I’m running because my generation is the one paying the bill for Washington’s chaos, and we deserve a seat at the table.
I’ve earned my network the old-fashioned way: relationship by relationship, handshake by handshake.
I’m young, hungry, and ready to do the work.
That’s the difference.
Some people campaign for a title.
I show up to solve problems.
This is a new chapter for a district with deep conservative roots and even deeper pride.
The good fight’s not over, and it’s on us to carry it forward.
A CALL, NOT A CAREER
My wife, Meredith, and I are building our life right here in Georgia’s First District.
We want our kids one day to grow up in the same America we did, a country that rewards hard work, respects freedom, and still believes in the promise of tomorrow.
That’s why I’m stepping up.
Not for a title.
Not to make myself feel important.
Not to tell you what you can’t do.
But because the country that raised me is worth fighting for, and the good fight’s not over.
