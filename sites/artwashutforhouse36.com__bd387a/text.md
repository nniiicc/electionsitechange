Re-elect Art Washut to be your House District #36 Representative
HOUSE DISTRICT 36 Candidate
- Born and raised in Casper
- Grew up in a blue collar family and began working as a young boy
- Attended Casper schools graduating in 1976 from KWHS
- Graduated from Casper College May 1978
- Graduated with honors from Chadron State College December 1979
- Casper Police Department 1980 – 2000
- Officer of the year 1986
- Masters degree in public administration from University of Wyoming 1994
- Retired as the Interim Chief of Police
- Faculty at Casper College – criminal justice, political science and addictionology since 2000
- Married to Megan since 1981 with six children five of whom live in Wyoming and nineteen grandchildren who all live in the state.
