When Wyoming folks call, Art does his best to help.
A few examples:
2026
HB 1 Budget – Veterinarians alerted Art about a critical shortage in food animal veterinarians.
Art co-sponsored a budget amendment as a first step toward addressing this problem.
HB 39 Firearms Restoration of Rights – Art sponsored this bill after a constituent reported changes to the law in 2025 had an unintended consequence.
Art’s bill fixed the problem and passed into law.
HB 8 Stalking of a Minor by an Adult – this bill resulted from a tragic case in Glenrock involving an adult stalking minors.
Art and Senator Boner successfully brought this bill through the Judiciary Committee.
SF 121 Pharmacy Scope of Practice – After meeting with Pharmacists from around the state, Art co-sponsored this bill which is now law.
2025
SF 114 Missing Persons Reporting – A woman from Casper championed this issue and Art happily co-sponsored the bill and worked to get it passed into law.
2024
HB 45 Cap on Property Tax Increases – When property taxes were increasing by double digit percentages, Art acted and co-sponsored the bill that exempted increases above 4%.

HB 54 Wyoming Reads Day – When friends of John Jorgensen approached Art he sponsored this bill Wyoming Reads Day Rotary.org
2021
SF 74 Athletic Trainers Scope of Practice – When Wyoming Athletic Trainers approached Senator Landen with a need, he looked to Art to co-sponsor the bill and carry it in the House.
