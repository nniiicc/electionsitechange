MEET BRANDEN
A Life of Service.
A Future for SC-07.
Branden Brown is a Veteran, former Firefighter/EMT, and Second Amendment advocate with deep South Carolina roots.
From military service to the front lines of emergency medicine, his life has been defined by answering the call.
With a background in Business and Psychology, Branden understands the economic heartbeat of our state and the mental health challenges facing our heroes.
A neighbor who values service over status, he is dedicated to the people of the Grand Strand and the Pee Dee and the future of the 7th District.
