My life has been defined by a simple mission: to protect and serve.
Whether it was as a U.S.
Marine or as a Firefighter/EMT, I was trained to run toward the danger to save lives.
That mission doesn’t change when it comes to the unborn.
I believe that life is a gift from God that begins at conception.
In Congress, I will be an unapologetic voice for the voiceless.
I will fight to end taxpayer funding for abortion providers and support policies that promote adoption and resources for expectant mothers.
Our greatness as a nation is measured by how we treat the most vulnerable, and I will always stand for life.
