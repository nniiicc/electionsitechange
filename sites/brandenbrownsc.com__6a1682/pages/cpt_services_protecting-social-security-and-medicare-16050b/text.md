I believe in the power of a person’s word.
For generations, South Carolinians have worked hard and paid into Social Security and Medicare with the understanding that those benefits would be there when they needed them.
In Congress, I will treat these programs as a sacred trust.
I will oppose any cuts to the benefits our seniors and disabled neighbors rely on.
We don’t have a Social Security problem; we have a Washington spending problem.
I will fight to stop career politicians from using these funds as a piggy bank for their pet projects.
Our elderly and disabled citizens deserve the security and dignity they earned.
