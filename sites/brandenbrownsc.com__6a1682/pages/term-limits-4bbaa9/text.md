Enacting Term Limits
The biggest problem in Washington is that the people in charge have forgotten what it’s like to work for a living.
They’ve spent decades trading favors and growing the government while the rest of us deal with inflation and overregulation.
I’m running to be a Citizen-Legislator, not a career politician.
I support term limits, I support cutting the perks of office, and I support a government that stays out of your way.
If you’re tired of the same names and the same results, it’s time for a new leader who knows the meaning of an honest day’s work.
