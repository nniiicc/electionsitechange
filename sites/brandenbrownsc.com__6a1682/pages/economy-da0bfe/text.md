Cutting Taxes, Spending, and Regulation
For too long, career politicians have treated your paycheck like their personal piggy bank.
In Congress, I will be a watchdog for your tax dollars.
My economic plan for SC-7 is built on three pillars:
- Tax Cuts for All: I will oppose any effort to raise taxes on individuals or small businesses.
We need to let South Carolinians keep more of what they earn to spark local growth.
- Regulatory Reform: For every new regulation proposed, we should cut two.
I’ll fight to get the federal government off the backs of our farmers and coastal entrepreneurs.
- Balanced Budgeting: I will advocate for a Balanced Budget Amendment.
If a family in Marion or Horry County has to balance their checkbook, the federal government should, too.
