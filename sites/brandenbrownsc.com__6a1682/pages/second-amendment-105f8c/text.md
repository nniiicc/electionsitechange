Defending Our Gun Rights
The Second Amendment is the ‘original’ background check on a government that seeks to overstep its bounds.
As your Representative, I will never waiver in defending the rights of law-abiding citizens to protect their families and homes.
I am a fierce advocate for Nationwide Reciprocity.
Your fundamental rights should not end at a state line.
If you are a law-abiding gun owner in South Carolina, your permit should be recognized in all fifty states.
I will fight to ensure that the ‘Patchwork’ of state laws is replaced by a federal standard that respects the Second Amendment nationwide.
