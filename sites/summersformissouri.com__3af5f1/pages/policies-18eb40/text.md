HEALTHY COMMUNITIES
Healthcare
We need universal healthcare for all.
Everyone should have access to a primary care physician to ensure early interventions.
Studies from the National Institutes of Health (NIH) show that improved access to primary care reduces hospitalizations and mortality.
- Hospital Capacity: Freeing up hospital beds by managing health at the primary level.
- Women’s Rights: Complete access to reproductive care and bodily autonomy.
- Vaccine Education: Public health programs to stop the spread of preventable diseases.

Public Safety
Our fire and police officers perform some of the hardest jobs there are, and we need to support them.
Through regular training in de-escalation and safety procedures, they can be prepared for the challenges they face daily.
Safety is the core of a successful community.
We must support our first responders—police and fire departments—with full funding and adequate rest for staff and officers.
- 988 Lifeline: Funding the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline reduces strain on police response times.
With well-trained committees and organizations, we can provide the help needed to those experiencing life-altering and overwhelming situations.
- Crisis Response: Mental health teams should respond to mental health crises, allowing police to focus on police emergencies.
- I personally provided insight to the FBI as part of their Citizens Academy around human trafficking, including behavior patterns to look out for from both predators and victims.

Food
Accessible, affordable, safe food is a human right.
We must build a sustainable food system that protects our soil and our health.
- Remove harmful chemicals and pesticides from our foods: Research in the World Health Organization (WHO) highlights the health risks associated with chemical herbicides.
- Permaculture & Grants: Implementing garden grants and educating on permaculture to empower local food production.
- School Programs: Guaranteed breakfast and lunch for all students.
Data shows that students who are fed nutritious meals learn better, and that’s good for all of us.
Funding for these programs is imperative, for some children, it’s their only opportunity to eat a balanced meal.
Secure Wages
Being one of the wealthiest countries in the world means we should not be struggling to feed our families or pay our bills.
Taking care of our people should be our #1 priority.
Having one job needs to be enough to live with dignity.
- Livable Wage: Recent data shows that $30/hr is becoming the standard for a true livable wage in many areas.
- Current Crisis: Our minimum wage remains stagnant at $7.25 federally and Missouri is $15 under the voter-approved 2024 measure, far below the cost of living.
- Missouri must not become a right-to-work state.
I will do everything I can to make sure our laborers are protected.

Education for All: A Vision for Public Excellence
Investing in our public schools is an investment in our collective future.
By prioritizing inclusive, accessible education, we build a society where every child can thrive from their earliest years through adulthood.
Overview of Missouri's current Education Budget
Stronger Public Schools, Not Vouchers
Public education must remain the cornerstone of our community.
I advocate for:
- Fully funded public programs that serve all children, regardless of background or ability.
Gov Kehoe just signed a budget that leaves an over $190 million deficit in public education, but grants $60 million to private school vouchers.
- Prioritizing public funding for public schools to ensure resources stay in the classrooms that need them most.
- Universal access to Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) to provide a Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE).
Free Appropriate Public Education (FAPE)
Early Childhood Foundation (Ages 2+)
Learning begins long before the first day of kindergarten, and there is strong evidence to support it.
Expanding public education to start at age 2 provides:
- Early Socialization: Reducing the strain on K-12 teachers by helping children develop emotional and social skills early.
- Specialized Intervention: Immediate access to special needs training and evaluations, particularly for children on the spectrum.
- Customized Pacing: A foundation that allows children to learn in the style and pace that fits their unique neurological profile.
Strong Evidence for Early Childhood system
Accessible Continued Education
Postgraduate success should not depend on personal wealth.
We must provide clear, debt-free pathways to:
- Higher Education: Trade schools, nursing certifications, and two-year degree programs.
- Economic Mobility: Allowing graduates to focus on finding a career and contributing to the economy rather than managing student debt.
- Entrepreneurship: Encouraging lifelong learning to support the next generation of business owners and innovators.
Dedicated Special Needs Support
The need for specially trained personnel and adequate funding has never been higher.
Our focus includes:
- Staff Empowerment: Providing the tools and funding necessary to support educators working with ADHD, autism, and other developmental conditions.
- Proper Evaluation: Expanding training for evaluations to ensure techniques are modern, effective, and humane.
- Community Safety: Data shows that a well-educated, well-supported society experiences lower crime and a stronger economy.
