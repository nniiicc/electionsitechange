Pain is the universal teacher.
From the age of 5, I was sold into a world that no one should endure, of child sex trafficking.
After being rescued at age 7, I grew up in a life of hardship without support or healing.
This pain could have defined who I was, but it taught me how to rise above the trials in front of me and be strong.
After A LOT of counseling, I got married, took opportunities to start businesses, raised my 3 amazing boys, whom I adore, and, through the American dream, forged the future I wanted.
But here we are, with a state and nation in decline, where the American dream has turned into the American nightmare, and people are feeling the pain.
I know what it’s like to be vulnerable, unprotected, and unsafe.
It’s not your fault; you have been victimized by a broken system, and I KNOW we can do better.
We have to do better.
My job as your Representative is to help keep you safe.
That’s why I’m running for Missouri State Representative to represent YOU in District 94, because I know what pain is like, and I want to protect the people of my community.
