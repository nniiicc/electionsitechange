Do you live in the 93rd Assembly District?
It’s an honor to serve as your representative in the Wisconsin State Legislature.
I’m working hard to push past politics as usual — and deliver on a bold, pro-public agenda for the Chippewa Valley.
Small, individual donations from the community are precisely the resources we need to get our aspirational message out to as many voters as we can.
In 2024, for the first time in nearly a generation, we saw an opportunity to send new, bold leaders to Madison who can actually get something done for the 93rd Assembly District.
And we took it.
I have been advocating for our community for over a decade, from protesting Act 10 as a teenager, to my years as a special education paraprofessional and journalist, to Director of Communications at Wisconsin Public Education Network, a statewide nonprofit to advocate for public schools — and my personal work with bold champions across Wisconsin.
We can’t afford to go any longer without pro-public leadership in Madison: pro-public education, pro-public land, pro-public health, pro-public participation in democracy, and more.
We have a responsibility to build power in the capitol and advance a bold vision for our shared future.
That sense of responsibility is what drives me to run for the 93rd Assembly District.
