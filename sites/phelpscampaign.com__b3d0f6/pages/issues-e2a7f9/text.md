I believe in the public because I believe in our community.
Fund Strong Public Schools I attended, worked in, and then advocated for Wisconsin’s public schools for decades.
There isn’t a single student in the state today who’s seen a budget that kept pace with inflation.
We must draft, pass, and enact a state budget that values students for what they’re worth: we’ll dramatically boost special education funding, stop passing new unfunded mandates, and fix the school funding formula to reflect students’ real needs.
As your representative, I serve on the Assembly Committee on Education and have introduced a budget amendment to boost special education funding and legislation to cap public spending on private vouchers.
With a majority, we’ll get it done.
Restore Reproductive Freedom When Roe vs.
Wade was overturned, abortion rights were returned to the states.
Wisconsin finds itself in a dangerous limbo with an archaic 1849 ban on the books and the whims of a state supreme court dictating what decisions individuals are allowed to make about their own pregnancies.
That’s why we must introduce a state bill that protects abortion rights and leaves the freedom to choose in the hands of women, LGBTQ+ individuals, and families — not politicians.
I’m prepared to put my colleagues on the record on this issue, and to campaign year-round for candidates at every level who recognize and will uphold the fundamental freedom to access reproductive healthcare.
I’ve proudly supported all pro-choice legislation in the 2025-26 legislative session and serve as Vice-Chair of the Legislative LGBTQ+ Caucus.
Expand Healthcare As a Human Right The Republican-led legislature has repeatedly turned down federal funds to expand Medicaid and cover more Wisconsinites under our fantastic BadgerCare program.
The Chippewa Valley finds itself in a tenuous position due to the abrupt closure of two hospitals, largely due to the lack of public oversight over our hospital chains.
Mental health crises are being funneled into crowded emergency rooms or the other side of the state when it would be healthier and more cost effective to address mental health issues in standalone, proactive mental health clinics.
We must reinstate the hospital chain accountability board, work to fund mental healthcare, accept Medicaid expansion, cover more individuals, children, and families through a public option, and move toward the responsible solution: guaranteed, publicly-funded healthcare with no cost at the point of access.
As your representative, I’ve authored legislation to cap prescription drug costs, fill Affordable Care Act subsidy gaps, and create a BadgerCare Public Option — and our efforts expand postpartum Medicaid eligibility for new parents and breast cancer screenings finally got through the Assembly in early 2026 after years of Republican obstruction.
I also serve on the Assembly Committee on Public Benefit Reform, where I allied with disability advocates to stop a Republican bill that would’ve added unnecessary, harmful red tape to the Medicaid qualification process.
Protect the Environment We can boost labor rights and save the environment by making a series of simple decisions.
By fully funding the DNR, working with labor unions, and moving toward renewable energy, we can protect Wisconsin’s environment for generations to come, strengthen wages and create new union jobs, and respect sovereignty for indigenous nations and populations.
I will always support bills aimed at protecting the environment — and the working class — from corporate greed.
As your representative, I’ve worked with neighboring legislators of both parties to co-author legislation to expand the farmland preservation tax credit.
I’ve also co-authored a Green Amendment, a Climate Accountability Act, and other bills to clean up our water and protect the environment.
Expand Workers’ Rights Labor rights are the backbone of a strong economy, stable families, and a just, healthy society.
Wisconsin has a rich history of leading on the labor movement.
But since public sector employees were stripped of their collective bargaining rights in 2011, gender pay gaps have grown, teacher shortages have reached a crisis point, and morale for public employees has plummeted.
I am a community organizer working to leverage relationships to emphasize the discriminatory nature of Act 10 and Right to Work and get them repealed, restore prevailing wage laws, and empower unions to empower laborers.
We can work together to return to our progressive traditions, tackle poverty, and restore public sector employees’ ability to negotiate for the compensation they deserve.
I’ve co-authored legislation to repeal Right to Work and local labor preemptions, restore prevailing wage laws, and more.
More broadly, I believe in real economic freedom and a cooperative economy.
No one in the Chippewa Valley should be too poor to live, and we must also have the courage to fight back against reckless federal politics.
I introduced a bill to require the State of Wisconsin to fill funding gaps if the Trump Administration freezes congressionally-approved dollars for programs like Medicaid and Meals on Wheels.
Legalize Cannabis Wisconsin is increasingly becoming a bubble.
Despite the fact that legalizing marijuana would be immensely popular, our regressive, Republican-led legislature has kept Wisconsin stuck in the past even as our Midwestern neighbors have moved forward.
By legalizing, taxing, and regulating cannabis, and expunging the records of those unjustly arrested for possession or use, we can keep families together and open up a new stream of public revenue, and I’ve co-authored legislation to do just that.
