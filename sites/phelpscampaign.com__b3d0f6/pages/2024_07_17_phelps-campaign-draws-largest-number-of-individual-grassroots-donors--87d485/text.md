Phelps Campaign Draws Largest Number of Individual Grassroots Donors in 93rd Assembly Race
As the first financial period of the 2024 state-level elections has passed, reports show that Christian Phelps has the largest number of individual donors in the 93rd Assembly District race, demonstrating a grassroots advantage and a broad, diverse base of supporters.
Phelps’s campaign attracted nearly 270 donations from over 210 unique individuals, with a range of donations from $5 to the limit of $1,000, and an average monetary contribution of just $92.
“While partisan elections are far more expensive than they should be, it is humbling to receive individual donations from so many community advocates,” said Christian Phelps.
“This has enabled me, as a millennial candidate, to reach thousands of voters with printed literature, digital outreach, and more, and has set us on a path to win this race in August and again in November.
I am incredibly grateful for the support.”
