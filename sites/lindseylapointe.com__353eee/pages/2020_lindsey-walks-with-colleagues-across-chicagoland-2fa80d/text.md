Lindsey LaPointe was recently joined by her friend and colleague, 5th District Representative Lamont Robinson, for socially-distanced door knocking in the 19th District.
Together, they spoke to voters about Biden, LaPointe, Martwick and the Fair Tax.
Lindsey then canvassed with her friend and former teacher, 51st District Representative Mary Edly-Allen, in the Chicago suburbs.
In the midst of her own race, Lindsey believes it is important to help her colleagues in tight races get across the finish line.
“I’ve seen up close how critical it is to support good, thoughtful and newer legislators who got elected just two years ago.
Up and down the ballot, this election is too important to sit out,” she emphasized.
