Today, Lindsey LaPointe joined fellow North Side Democrats in a successful yard sign pick up drive-thru at the Irish American Heritage Center.
The event was hosted by the 39th Ward Democratic Organization.
Despite the rainy weather, Lindsey and her colleagues worked cheerfully to distribute yard signs for Democratic candidates up and down the ballot, including popular Biden-Harris signs.
Regarding the event, Lindsey said that “it was great to be with people in person today at a distance as we visually show our support and work to elect thoughtful leaders and our first ever woman VP.” She emphasized the importance of preparing for November 3rd, and pushed to build support for the Fair Tax.
Special thanks to Senator Ram Villivalam and the 39th Ward Dems for hosting along with State Rep.
John D’Amico, Commissioner Bridget Degnen and Alderman Sam Nugent.
