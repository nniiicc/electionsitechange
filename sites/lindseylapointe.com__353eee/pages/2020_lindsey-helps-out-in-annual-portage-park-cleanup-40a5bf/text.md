Lindsey joined the 38th Ward Dems, Friends of Portage Park, and the Chicago Parks Foundation or a successful cleanup event on Saturday morning.
The weather was brisk as dozens of community members gathered and cleaned up the park along with spreading mulch!
As a strong supporter of the environment, Lindsey loved the opportunity to help out in one of our beloved parks.
Thank you to Senator Robert Martwick, Commissioner Bridget Degnen, and all of our dedicated neighbors for helping out.
