About Lindsey Issues News Events Contact Donate Select Page A Message from Lindsey As Early Vote Begins Oct 15, 2020 Friends, Early Voting has begun in Chicago; we are now in the midst of the most critical election of our lifetimes.
Every day when I’m out safely talking to voters, I hear frustration, but also hope for change.
Especially after the recent behavior of the president, our Biden-Durbin and LaPointe-Martwick signs are flying out of our campaign office and people cannot wait to vote to return competent and humane leadership to the White House.
Here in Illinois, people are eager to vote for a more fair tax system and to move forward with real environmental protections and climate justice.
Thousands of our 19th district neighbors have already cast their ballots for change.
Please join us to ensure we wake up on November 4th in a world that is more thoughtful and more compassionate.
