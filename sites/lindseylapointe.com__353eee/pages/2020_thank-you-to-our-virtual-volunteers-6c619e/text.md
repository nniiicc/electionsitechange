About Lindsey Issues News Events Contact Donate Select Page Thank You to Our Virtual Volunteers Oct 8, 2020 On Thursday, Lindsey and Senator Rob Martwick joined over a dozen volunteers to phone bank in the 19th House District.
Although we couldn’t be together, the group made hundreds of calls to get out the vote and talk to neighbors about key issues.
On small campaigns like ours, every volunteer and every phone call makes a huge difference.
Through texts, calls, postcards, and door knocking, our volunteers have worked hard to make our campaign safe and successful during a pandemic.
Thank you to all from Lindsey and Team LaPointe.
