Janet Mills will let Maine’s ghost-gun regulation take effect without signing it, requiring serialized parts and background checks for privately made firearms.
Unserialized deadly weapons from mail-order kits or 3D printers are now prohibited under LD 1126; their transfer is now subject to background checks like all other commercially available firearms. “ GIFFORDS [Law Center] is especially thankful to Representative Sam Zager for his steadfast work fighting for this bill, which will undoubtedly save lives.” Governor Janet Mills will allow a bill regulating so-called “ghost guns” to become law in Maine without her signature, according to the Press Herald.
Ghost guns are plastic guns you can make using a 3D printer.
Kits or parts can also be bought, shipped and assembled at home A background check is not required to purchase these parts, making them virtually untraceable.
