Maine State Legislative District 116
District 116 connects the Portland neighborhoods of Deering Center, Deering Highlands, Woodfords Corner, Rosemont, Libbytown, Westgate, and Stroudwater.
It used to be designated District 41 until the state shifted the boundaries and renumbered in response to the 2020 census.
