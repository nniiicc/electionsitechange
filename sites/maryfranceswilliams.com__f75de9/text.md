“As a lifelong resident of Marietta, I am honored and humbled to serve you and your family as your State Representative.
As an advocate for families and children at the Capitol for the past 35 years, I know firsthand that by working together we can improve access to healthcare, fund our schools appropriately, protect our environment and tackle our transportation problems.
As a budget expert, I’m also committed to ensuring our government spends our hard-earned taxpayers dollars wisely and where funding is needed most to ensure a bright future for all Georgians.
I believe that Georgia thrives when we create opportunities for all Georgians to succeed.
I am here to listen and to learn about your needs, and to be your voice under the gold dome.
Please reach out to me if you have a question or concern, an issue you’d like addressed or if you would like to visit the Capitol.
My door is always open and I look forward to continuing to serve you.”
Representative Mary Frances Williams
State Representative, Georgia House District 37
Cobb county
House District 37
Georgia State House District 37 runs right through the heart of Marietta, Georgia, including the communities of Fair Oaks, Cheatham Hill, parts of East Cobb, and downtown Marietta.
