| Property Taxes |
| |
| |
| Wyoming families are struggling with rising expenses, particularly property taxes that make it harder to remain in the homes they’ve earned.
That's why I have championed practical property tax relief to assist families, homeowners, and seniors on fixed incomes.
While serving on the Revenue Committee, I backed measures to reduce taxes for long-term owners and advocated reforms to make property taxes more predictable and manageable.
I believe government should help Wyoming families retain more of their earnings, not make it harder to afford living here. |
