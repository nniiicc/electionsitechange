I wanted to reach out to discuss some of the biggest issues facing Wyoming residents and share my ideas for addressing them.
Property Taxes Wyoming families are struggling with rising expenses, particularly property taxes that make it harder to remain in the homes they’ve earned.
That's why I have championed practical property tax relief to assist families, homeowners, and seniors on fixed incomes.
While serving on the Revenue Committee, I backed measures to reduce taxes for long-term owners and advocated reforms to make property taxes more predictable and manageable.
I believe government should help Wyoming families retain more of their earnings, not make it harder to afford living here.
I would appreciate the opportunity to connect with you personally as we get closer to the election.
I want to learn about your top priorities and answer any questions you may have.
I hope to earn your support.
