I was raised in Wyoming, and it shaped who I am.
From a strong public education to the wide-open land and waters that define our state, I’ve always taken pride in the Wyoming way of life.
I’m running to protect it so our communities stay strong, our way of life endures, and the next generation has even more opportunity than the last.
Over the past term, I’ve stayed focused on results.
That means working through tough issues, asking the right questions, and finding solutions that actually move Wyoming forward.
While others have chosen more noise than progress, I’ve remained committed to doing the job the right way—steady, accountable, and focused on what matters most to the people I represent.
That work has included taking a serious approach to school funding, supporting a more responsible and realistic state budget, and standing up for policies that protect Wyoming’s economy and way of life.
I’ve worked to ensure legislation is thoughtful, not rushed, and that decisions are made with real input from the people they impact.
