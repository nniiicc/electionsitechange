I was born and raised with the values that define our great state including hard work, personal responsibility, and a deep respect for our land and communities.
During my time in office, I have worked to represent those values at the Capitol in Cheyenne, listening to the people of Lincoln and Teton counties while striving to be a strong voice for our district.
I am excited to announce my candidacy for a third term in the Wyoming House of Representatives.
Over the past two terms, I have helped pass meaningful legislation supporting property tax relief, keeping public lands in public hands, supporting Wyoming’s aging population, and strengthening our legacy industries.
There is still important work to be done, and I remain committed to advocating for policies that protect our economy, our freedoms, and our way of life.
I am proud of the work I have accomplished in my role as Chairman of the Travel, Recreation, Wildlife, and Cultural Resources Committee.
In this role, I have had the privilege of supporting the Wyoming way of life—ensuring access to hunting and fishing opportunities, preserving our outdoor heritage, and promoting responsible recreation across our state.
These traditions are central to who we are, and I will continue working to protect and enhance them for future generations.
I invite you to learn more about my campaign, my priorities, and how you can get involved by visiting my website at www.abhd22.com.
Your support, input, and engagement are vital as we continue working together to keep Wyoming strong.
Andrew Byron Want to learn more about the legislature, participate in hearings, submit testimony, track bills, or hear what we have to say on the floor?
