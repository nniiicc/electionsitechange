When I served on the Revenue Committee, I pushed for the long-term homeowners’ 50% property tax reduction, recognizing the value that these members of our communities—often living on fixed incomes—have brought to Wyoming over the past decades.
I personally ran a bill to eliminate the sunset clause on this measure and was pleased to vote for and see it removed in the 2026 session.
