Meet Robyn Bird
This is not about politics for Robyn.
It's personal because she cares about you.
Robyn Lee Bird personifies Port Richmond and the rowhouse neighborhoods of the River Wards.
Four generations in the same Port Richmond rowhouse.
A family that came from Poland through Ellis Island and never stopped serving—from World War I and Omaha Beach to Iraq and today’s firehouses, service runs deep in Robyn’s blood.
Like too many families in our community, Robyn faced devastating loss at an early age.
At just 15 years old, she lost her father to the heroin crisis.
That hardship shaped her life and taught her a lasting truth of the River Wards: we take care of our own.
Today, Robyn fights for Philadelphia's firefighters and paramedics with Local 22–standing side by side with the men and women who run toward danger while politicians make excuses.
She knows that Harrisburg needs a voice that will back them with real resources, proper staffing, and the respect they've earned.
Robyn is running to put public safety first; to support law enforcement and first responders, deliver safer and cleaner streets, strengthen public schools and expand real school choice, grow union and manufacturing jobs, protect affordable healthcare, and defend homeowners and seniors from crushing property taxes and rising utility costs.
This isn't politics for Robyn Bird.
It's personal.
She is ready to take her determination and tenacity to Harrisburg to fight for the River Wards –so we can take back our neighborhoods and make them safe, affordable, and strong again.
Robyn Bird humbly asks for your support so she can be your voice in the State Capitol.
