The more I saw the forces crushing Americans… → from soaring housing and living costs → to crippling personal debt → and the erosion of civility and shared values …the more I knew I had to stand up.

My Transparency Pledge To You ★ I will not accept any PAC money ★ I will not trade individual stocks ★ I will host 10+ town hall meetings annually JACK’S POLITICAL PHILOSOPHY The real divide isn’t left vs. right.
It’s freedom versus control.
In today’s America, freedom is fading.
Control is filling the void.
Regardless of which party is in charge.
If we want to stay the land of the free, we need to stand up and be brave.
Otherwise the forces of control – economic, political, technological – are going to keep giving us a form of freedom, but deny us the power to be truly free.
Policies that get back to basics.
