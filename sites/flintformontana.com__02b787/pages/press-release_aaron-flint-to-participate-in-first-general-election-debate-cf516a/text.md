Home About Issues News Get Involved Contact Store Media Donate Aaron Flint to Participate in First General Election Debate Press Release October 8, 2026 Bozeman, MT – Aaron Flint will face Sam Forstag in the first debate of the general election for Montana’s western congressional district.
Hosted by the Montana Television Network, the 30-minute debate will be moderated by MTN Senior Political Reporter Jonathon Ambarian.
The debate will be taped today and will air on Friday and Saturday.
