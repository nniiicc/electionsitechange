Home About Issues News Get Involved Contact Store Media Donate Flint Crushes Forstag in Final Debate, Exposes Lobbying Record and Delivers a Clear Plan for a More Affordable Montana Press Release October 13, 2026 Missoula, MT – Aaron Flint laid out his plan to make the Montana Dream affordable again in the final debate of the general election, while Sam Forstag once again failed to address his career as a highly paid lobbyist or defend his record of lobbying for a sales tax, higher property taxes, and sanctuary cities.
“Jobs and affordability are my top priority,” said Flint.
“We’re going to invest in trades and trades education.
We’re going to revitalize our timber industry, and make our timber industry great again.
We’re going to mine and manufacture in America again and that’s how we’re going to make the Montana Dream affordable again.” Facts on the Debate: Data Centers Aaron does not support data centers coming into Montana that increase electricity rates, consume Montana’s water resources, or damage our way of life .
Forstag, meanwhile, was called out by his own Democratic primary opponents for misrepresenting his stance on data centers and for being backed by AI and data center billionaires.
Taxes Sam Forstag lobbied for a sales tax and higher property taxes while pocketing taxpayer dollars.
While representing the local governments of Missoula and Bozeman, Forstag supported SB 551 , which would have allowed Montana’s largest cities to implement a sales tax.
He also supported SB 548 , which would have eliminated limits on local property tax increases.
Aaron opposes a sales tax and supports lowering property taxes.
He believes Montanans should keep more of their hard-earned dollars.
Energy Prices Aaron supports expanding American energy production and providing relief for Montana families facing high fuel costs.
He has called for a conclusive end to the Iran war that eliminates the terrorist threat and reopens global energy trade.
Until that happens, Aaron is calling for a national gas tax holiday.
Forstag is backing Green New Deal energy policies and is endorsed by AOC , the proposal’s original author.
Montana families cannot afford an energy agenda that makes it more expensive to drive to work or heat their homes.
Healthcare Aaron has spent years advocating for lower healthcare costs.
He has pushed to lower premiums , celebrated premium reductions and criticized Obamacare for driving costs up .
Forstag, meanwhile, has advocated for a government takeover of healthcare .
Montanans deserve answers about what that would mean for their employer-sponsored and union health plans and how he would pay for it.
Forstag also has promised to repeal the One Big Beautiful Bill , which includes the Rural Health Transformation Program.
Montana will receive $233.5 million in fiscal year 2026 through that program.
Aaron would put those resources to work protecting rural hospitals, expanding telehealth and bringing more healthcare workers to Montana.
He supports keeping taxpayer-funded benefits for eligible Americans, strengthening price transparency, and lowering prescription drug costs so Americans are not paying more for the same medicines sold cheaper overseas.
Public Lands Aaron has consistently supported keeping public lands in public hands .
Congressman Ryan Zinke fought to keep public land sales out of the One Big Beautiful Bill .
He also helped launch the bipartisan Public Lands Caucus and introduced the Public Lands in Public Hands Act .
Zinke endorsed Aaron in part because of his support for public lands .
If Zinke’ Public Lands in Public Hands Act is not enacted in the 119th Congress, Aaron will reintroduce and carry it if elected.
Immigration While lobbying for the ACLU in 2021 and 2022, Forstag opposed HB 200 , which would prohibit state agencies and local governments from enacting or enforcing sanctuary city policies.
He also opposed HB 223 , which would require local law enforcement to hold individuals already in custody when federal immigration detainer requests are present.
Protecting Women’s Sports Forstag lobbied against legislation protecting girls’ sports and restricting gender transition procedures for minors.
Forstag lobbied for the ACLU and opposed legislation protecting girls’ sports and restricting gender transition procedures for minors.
While lobbying for the Montana Library Association, Forstag also opposed a bill restricting drag shows at schools and libraries .
PACs Aaron’s career before running for Congress included 20 years of military service and work as a salaried television anchor and radio host.
Forstag’s campaign allegations that Aaron was personally enriched by corporate PACs or corporate tax cuts.
Forstag’s own lobbying record includes hundreds of thousands of dollars billed while representing his far-left organization and government clients .
Congressional Stock Trading Sam Forstag’s aunt is Susie Lee, one of the most egregious insider traders in Congress.
She violated congressional ethics by failing to disclose over $3.3 million dollars in stock trades.
Aaron has consistently opposed Congressional Stock trading and pledged to ban it if elected.
“Throughout two debates, Montanans have seen exactly who Sam Forstag is: a paid lobbyist trying to run away from the record he was paid to build,” said Flint campaign spokesman Garrett Brown.
“Aaron delivered a clear plan to lower costs and protect our Montana way of life.
