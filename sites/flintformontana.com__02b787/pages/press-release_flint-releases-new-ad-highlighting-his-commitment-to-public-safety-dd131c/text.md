Home About Issues News Get Involved Contact Store Media Donate Flint Releases New Ad Highlighting his Commitment to Public Safety, and Forstag’s Record of Lobbying for Sanctuary Cities Press Release October 8, 2026 Watch the ad here Kalispell, MT — Today, Republican congressional candidate Aaron Flint released a new general election advertisement featuring Gallatin County Sheriff Springer.
In the ad, Gallatin County Sheriff Springer shares why he is backing Aaron Flint for Congress and that Flint will fight to keep fentanyl out of Montana communities and to secure the southern border.
“Lieutenant Colonel Flint will crack down on fentanyl dealers and secure our border,” Springer says in the ad.
“Aaron Flint will keep your families safe.” The ad also highlights Sam Forstag’s record of lobbying to make Montana a sanctuary state .
“Local and federal law enforcement across Montana put their lives on the line to secure our border and keep our communities safe,” said Aaron Flint.
“Unlike my opponent, who lobbied for sanctuary policies and restrictions that would make it tougher for law enforcement to do their job, I will always back the brave men and women in blue and oppose any measures that would restrict their ability to protect our communities.” Watch the ad here: Transcript: Voiceover: Sam Forstag lobbied in favor of sanctuary cities that put criminal illegal aliens back on our streets.
