KALISPELL, Mont. — Combat veteran and local broadcaster Aaron Flint received an endorsement from President Donald Trump.
Flint is running for U.S.
House in Montana’s First Congressional District.
He received endorsements from Rep.
Ryan Zinke, Sen.
Tim Sheehy, Gov.
Greg Gianforte, Rep.
Troy Downing, Attorney General Austin Knudsen and other Montana leaders within the first 24 hours of announcing his campaign.
