WHITEFISH, MT – Flathead County Sheriff Brian Heino and retired Army Lt.
Colonel Aaron Flint joined dozens of local residents in Whitefish today for a rally thanking local Border Patrol and law enforcement agents and exposing Portland lobbyist Sam Forstag’s dangerous soft-on-crime agenda.
Following recent death threats against local agents, Flint called out Portland socialist Sam Forstag’s long and lucrative record of lobbying against immigration enforcement and tougher penalties for violent criminals.
Flint is the Republican nominee for Montana’s Western District.
“Thank you to our hard-working law enforcement in Flathead County and across Montana.
While these officers put their lives on the line, Portland socialist Sam Forstag was lobbying to make Montana a sanctuary state and stop local police from working with immigration enforcement,” said Flint.
“We don’t need more Portland in Montana, people are fleeing bad Portland policies to seek refuge in Montana!
Under Joe Biden and the Democrats’ leadership 30 million illegal aliens poured into our country, many criminal aliens ending up right here in Montana where they went on to commit heinous crimes like assault, rape and murder.
Thanks to the good work of Montana law enforcement and the USBP, many of them are detained or deported.”
“In our communities, law enforcement agencies have always relied on one another to protect the public, especially when resources are limited and a crisis occurs,” said Sheriff Heino.
“Border Patrol is an important partner to local law enforcement, assisting with search and rescue, supporting drug investigations, and protecting our northern border.
We need leaders who understand the importance of those partnerships and will ensure law enforcement and Border Patrol have the resources they need to keep our communities and our nation safe.
Aaron Flint understands that mission and has demonstrated his support for the men and women who serve.
I am proud to support Aaron Flint for Congress.”
While lobbying for the ACLU in 2021–22, Forstag opposed HB 200, which would prohibit state agencies and local governments from refusing to work with federal law enforcement related to criminal illegals and adopting or enforcing sanctuary city policies.
He also opposed HB 223, which would require local law enforcement to hold individuals already in custody when federal immigration detainer requests are present.
Forstag’s lobbying record also includes opposition to SB 220, legislation revising penalties for assaulting peace officers, emergency responders or judicial officers, and HB 391, which would increase penalties for cartel members and drug dealers whose drugs cause someone’s death.
Flint pledged to support Border Patrol and local law enforcement with the resources they need to enforce the law and protect Montana communities.
He also committed to voting to ensure every illegal alien is deported from Montana, easing pressure on housing and healthcare costs for working families.
A stark difference from Forstag’s support for taxpayer-funded healthcare and housing for illegal aliens while Montanans struggle to afford those necessities themselves.
Flint warned that rental homes occupied by people with no legal right to be here leave fewer options for working-class Montanans in Bozeman, Missoula, and the Flathead.
