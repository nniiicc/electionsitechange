KALISPELL, Mont. – Aaron Flint announced he plans to run for Congress Ryan Zinke’s, open seat.
Zinke announced his retirement early Monday morning.
The filing deadline in Montana is this week.
Flint is a combat veteran, who is no Stranger to Montana politics.
He has spent time working for multiple Montana Republicans, before launching his own conservative radio show, “Montana Talks.”
Flint is a father and husband, and a fifth-generation Montanan.
He grew up in Glasgow and the Flathead, going back and forth between mom’s and dad’s house, he is a graduate of the University of Montana where he was student body president.
