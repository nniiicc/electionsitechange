Welcome!
I’m Amy Zimmerman, and I’m running to represent Wisconsin’s 13th Assembly District.
I’m a community leader, mom, project manager, and small business owner, with more than 15 years of experience solving complex problems and leading multimillion-dollar initiatives across health care technology, utilities, and financial services.
Here in our community, I’ve worked to bring people together and get things done - from helping deliver a more inclusive playground at Brookfield’s Wirth Park Tot Lot, to supporting library programming, mentoring Girl Scouts, and building community events that help neighbors create meaningful connections.
I’m running because Wisconsin families deserve leaders who listen, understand complicated systems, and know how to turn good ideas into real results.
Our current Representative, Robyn Vining, is now running for the Wisconsin Senate.
