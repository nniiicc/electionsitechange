DONATE Feature Event : Trivia Night Fundraiser The Truth About the Missouri General Assembly ﻿Do you wonder why, in a state with Republican supermajorities in the House and Senate, it's so hard to get conservative legislation passed?
While there are good conservative legislators in Jefferson City, the sad truth is, many of our Republican legislators do not understand the system of government our Founders gave us, or worse yet, enrich themselves and promote their careers while selling out your rights to special interests and lobbyists.
They then have the audacity to pretend to be conservatives to their constituents.
Our liberties are under attack and I won't stand for it!
What Do I Believe About Government?
The sole purpose of government is to protect your inalienable rights, which come from God.
Government needs to be constrained within the limits of the Constitution.
Government was never intended to meddle in every aspect of your life, over-regulate you, tax you into oblivion, violate your fundamental rights, and bow to the interests of corporations, lobbyists, and global elites in Washington DC, New York, and Europe.
The Spiritual Battle for the Soul of Our Nation and Missouri We are in a spiritual battle, not political, for the soul of Our Nation and the State of Missouri.
Who wins this battle will determine whether the system of government our Founders gave us survives or whether we descend into the dark night of globalism and tyranny.
As John Adams said, "Liberty once lost is lost forever." Join me in this worthy fight to preserve and protect the great state of Missouri for ourselves and generations to come!
