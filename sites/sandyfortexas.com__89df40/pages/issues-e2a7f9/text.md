Home Meet Sandy Issues Updates Volunteer | Get Involved Contribute Contact Issues Sandy Ibáñez is running for the Texas House because representation matters.
She believes that good leadership starts with listening not to political parties, but to people.
Her priorities focus on finding practical, common-sense solutions that strengthen families, create opportunity, and ensure Fort Bend County continues to be a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
Strong Schools and Educated Communities Every child deserves a safe, high-quality education and every teacher deserves the resources and respect to do their job well.
Sandy believes education is the foundation of opportunity and that safe schools are essential for strong communities.
As a former Fort Bend ISD teacher, she understands what classrooms need to succeed and how important it is to keep students and staff safe.
She will fight for fair funding for public schools, support for teachers, and investment in safety, including secure facilities, emergency training, and access to school-based counselors and mental health programs.
Sandy also supports expanding apprenticeships and career and technical education so students graduate ready for college or the workforce.
She believes decisions about education should remain local, because parents and educators know best how to serve their students.
Affordable Living and Fiscal Responsibility Families should not have to choose between keeping a roof over their heads and paying their bills.
Texans deserve fair taxes, transparent budgeting, and accountability from Austin.
Sandy will work to make life more affordable for Texas families by supporting fair and transparent property taxes and addressing rising auto and home insurance costs.
She believes in fiscal responsibility, every tax dollar should be spent wisely, openly, and where it makes the most impact.
Sandy will support smart, balanced growth that encourages local businesses, protects homeowners, and ensures that economic development benefits the entire community.
Health and Well-Being for Every Family Every Texan deserves access to affordable healthcare, family planning and mental health services close to home.
Sandy will advocate for expanding access to healthcare, mental health care, family planning and community support services.
She knows how important it is to have counselors in schools, local clinics in our neighborhoods, and support for veterans, seniors, and families.
Healthy communities make a stronger Texas, and Sandy will work to ensure Fort Bend County has the resources it needs to thrive.
Welcoming and United Communities Sandy believes that strong communities are built on respect, compassion, and opportunity for everyone.
Fort Bend County’s diversity is one of its greatest strengths, and she will work to ensure every family feels safe, valued, and supported.
She believes in keeping families together, treating people with dignity, and creating pathways for all Texans to contribute and succeed.
No family should ever be left behind in the place they call home.
Safe Roads, Reliable Infrastructure, and Rural Investment As Fort Bend County continues to grow, our infrastructure must keep up, from roads and drainage to broadband access and rural support.
Sandy will work to ensure our state invests in the infrastructure that keeps Fort Bend moving forward.
That means safer roads, stronger flood control, expanded broadband access, and reliable power systems that protect families and businesses during severe weather.
She believes we must grow responsibly, maintaining clean air and water, supporting smart energy practices, and keeping our communities healthy and sustainable for generations to come.
Bottom Line Is...
Sandy understands the human impact of policy and how decisions made in Austin affect real lives in Fort Bend County.
She believes it’s time to put people before party and service before politics.
Sandy is committed to building bridges, listening to everyone, and finding common-sense solutions that make life better for working families, small businesses, and all who call this community home.
