October 16, 2025 Contact: Erum Rani (346) 802-7265 | rani@spinpointzmedia.com PRESS RELEASE SANDY IBÁÑEZ WILL RUN FOR TEXAS STATE REPRESENTATIVE, HOUSE DISTRICT 28 FORT BEND COUNTY - Sandy Ibáñez announced her candidacy today for Texas State Representative, House District 28.
The primary election is on March 3, 2026.
The general election is on November 3, 2026.
Sandy Ibáñez, a fifth-generation Texan, lifelong Fort Bend County resident, former educator, and business professional, has announced her candidacy for the Texas House of Representatives, District 28.
Ibáñez is running to ensure that the voices of Fort Bend County families are heard in Austin.
Her campaign is focused on strengthening schools, improving access to healthcare, promoting responsible growth, and building a government that puts people before politics.
“Representation matters,” said Ibáñez.
“Too many families feel unheard and disconnected from the decisions being made in Austin.
I want to bring common-sense leadership to the Texas House, the kind that listens to people, focuses on results, and puts service ahead of politics.” A proud graduate of the University of Houston, Ibáñez earned a Bachelor of Business Administration in Management and Entrepreneurship from the C.T.
Bauer College of Business and the Wolff Center for Entrepreneurship.
She also holds an MBA from Western Governors University and completed the Rice Advanced Management Program.
Ibáñez currently works in commercial real estate, where she advocates for smart growth and community investment.
She previously taught in Fort Bend ISD, an experience that deepened her understanding of the needs of students, teachers, and families.
Her combined background in education and business gives her a unique perspective on the challenges facing working families, local employers, and communities across the district.
Growing up in Fort Bend County, Ibáñez saw firsthand the power of community.
“I was raised to believe that faith, family, and hard work are the foundation of any strong community,” she said.
“Those values continue to guide me today.” Ibáñez describes her campaign as one built on accountability, community and transparency.
She believes that strong leadership begins with listening to constituents and finding common ground.
“Fort Bend County is one of the most diverse and dynamic communities in Texas,” Ibáñez said.
“Our strength comes from working together.
It is time to focus on solutions that make life better for everyone, not partisan agendas that divide us.” For more information, visit www.SandyForTexas.com ### Pol. adv. by Sandy Ibanez Campaign *Disclaimer: Texas’s electronic voting and ballot printing systems do not support diacritical marks (e.g., á, ñ).
As a result, candidate names are printed without these marks to ensure accuracy and consistency across all voting materials.
Therefore, “Ibáñez” will appear as “Ibanez” on the ballot and in some campaign communications.
