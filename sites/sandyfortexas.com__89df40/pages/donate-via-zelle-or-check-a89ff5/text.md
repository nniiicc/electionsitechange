Contribution Guidelines
There are no limits on individual contributions for Texas state campaigns.
Corporate contributions are prohibited under Texas law.
Contributions from labor organizations are not allowed.
Contributions are not tax-deductible.
For contributions over $90, Texas law requires that we collect the donor’s name, address, occupation, and employer.
