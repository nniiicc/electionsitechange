Voter Information
Your voice matters and every vote makes a difference.
Below you will find key information to help you make your plan to vote in the upcoming elections.
Voter Registration
Make sure you are registered to vote and that your information is up to date.
You can check your voter registration status or register to vote online or by mail.
Voter Registration Deadline:
The deadline to register to vote for the 2026 Primary Election is Monday, February 2, 2026.
The deadline to register for the 2026 General Election is Monday, October 5, 2026.
Last Day to Apply for Ballot by Mail (Received, not Postmarked) for the Primary Election is Friday, February 20, 2026.
Last Day to Apply for Ballot by Mail (Received, not Postmarked) for the General Election is Friday, October 23, 2026.
If you have moved, changed your name, or wish to update your party affiliation, be sure to update your registration before the deadlines above, for more information on critical dates, click here.
Primary and General Election Dates
Primary Election Day: Tuesday, March 3, 2026
Primary Early Voting: February 17-27, 2026
General Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026
General Election Early Voting: October 19-30, 2026
During early voting, you can vote at any polling location in your county.
On Election Day, you must vote at your assigned polling place.
