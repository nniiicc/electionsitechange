About Texas House District 28
Texas House District 28 is made up of all or parts of these communities: Beasley, Greatwood, Fairchilds, Fulshear, Foster, Katy, Needville, Orchard, Pleak, Richmond, Rosenberg, Simonton, Sugar Land and Weston Lakes.
