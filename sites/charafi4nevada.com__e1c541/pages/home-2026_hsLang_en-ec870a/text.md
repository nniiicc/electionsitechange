Meet Joey Not a career politician.
A neighbor.
Joey Charafi grew up in Las Vegas and is raising his family here.
When he saw the system failing Nevada kids on trafficking, family support, and basic accountability, he didn’t write a letter to his representative.

The Stop the Traffic Foundation is Joey’s life’s work.
He built it from the ground up to combat child trafficking, support at‑risk youth, and move faster than government can.
Fifteen years running businesses in media, production, and logistics taught him how real economies and real families work.
