A Farmer, Not A Politician
I'm a proud Mont Vernon farmer, small business consultant, and patriot running for the New Hampshire House to put our state first.
My family has called New England home since 1639.
That deep-rooted heritage instilled in me a fierce love for liberty, self-reliance, and the Granite State way of life.
Today, my wife and I own and operate a working farm in Mont Vernon, where we raise animals, grow food, and live the independent lifestyle that defines rural New Hampshire.
Professionally, I consult for construction companies and cutting-edge AI manufacturing firms — giving me real-world experience with the challenges facing job creators: skyrocketing regulations, energy costs, taxes, and government overreach that threaten our economy and our freedoms.
As a constitutional originalist, I believe our God-given rights come from our Creator, not from government.
The individual — not bureaucrats in Concord or Washington — is best equipped to make decisions for his or her own life, family, and business.
My guiding principle is simple: government should be limited, transparent, and accountable to the people it serves.
- Protect property rights and keep taxes low
- Defend the 2nd Amendment and all our constitutional liberties
- Reduce burdensome regulations that drive up costs for families and farms
- Promote school choice and parental rights in education
- Put New Hampshire first by rejecting one-size-fits-all mandates from outside our borders
The Granite State Way
NH As An Innovation Hub
New Hampshire will become America's premier innovation hub for energy, healthcare technology, and compute.
We will achieve this the Granite State way: through bold private enterprise and low taxes, not corporate welfare or crony handouts.
By slashing red tape and fiercely defending property rights, we will attract the world's brightest minds and build an economic engine that rewards entrepreneurs, engineers, and working families.
As your representative, I will champion policies that position New Hampshire to lead the future while keeping our state independent and prosperous.
Education For Tomorrow's Economy
We must modernize New Hampshire education so our students master the skills demanded by rapid technological change — from coding and data science to advanced engineering and emerging industries.
By prioritizing rigorous academics, hands-on learning, and individualized instruction over outdated models, we can deliver world-class preparation that aligns with real workforce needs.
Strong support for charter schools, career-technical programs, and innovative teaching methods will ensure every child has the tools to succeed.
I will champion reforms that put results and opportunity first.
Preserving Our Agrarian Heritage
New Hampshire's agrarian roots are the foundation of our state's character, self-reliance, and way of life.
We must protect family farms, working lands, and rural traditions against overregulation and urban sprawl so future generations can continue to steward the soil, raise livestock, and produce food close to home.
Through smart policies that respect property rights, ease burdens on small producers, and support local agriculture, we will keep New Hampshire's countryside vibrant and productive.
