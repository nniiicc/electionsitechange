Nampa Chamber of Commerce Candidate Forum
April 25, 2024 @ 5:00 pm - 8:00 pm
The May 2024 Primary Election is right around the corner.
Be sure you know the candidates for Nampa’s Legislative Districts before May 21.
The City of Nampa, the Idaho Press, and the Nampa Chamber will host a forum for our community to meet the candidates, hear their responses to the most critical questions, and have the chance to interact with them.
Join us Thursday, April 25 at 5 PM at City Hall, and remember, local elections have a local impact.
