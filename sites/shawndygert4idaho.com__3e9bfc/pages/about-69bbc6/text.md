Shawn Dygert
Integrity, accountability, representation
Shawn Dygert, candidate for Idaho State House of Representatives, District 23B.
Shawn Dygert, is a lifelong Republican with a passion for public service and a deep-rooted commitment to the people of Idaho.
With a background marked by dedication to community engagement and a keen understanding of the issues that resonate with his constituents, he will bring a wealth of experience and a fresh perspective to the legislative arena.
As a potential representative, he has demonstrated a steadfast commitment to addressing the concerns of Idahoans, advocating for policies that foster economic growth, enhance education, and ensure the well-being of residents across the state.
With a reputation for integrity, diligence, and a focus on collaborative solutions, Shawn Dygert stands as a principled advocate for the diverse needs and aspirations of the voters in Ada, Canyon, and Owyhee Counties.
Proudly serving South Ada County, South Canyon County, and All of Owyhee County.
