Last year, our legislative office was pleased to award more than $75,000 worth of scholarships to 105 students across District 10.
These awards provide students attending a 2-year, 4-year, skilled trade or technical institution with funds to help subsidize the cost of enrollment.
The 2026-2027 scholarship application is live!
To access the application, visit the following link.

Eligibility Criteria:
- If you currently attend or will be attending any college or vocational school in Maryland next year, taking at least 6 credits.
- If you live in District 10.
Visit www.mdelect.net to confirm.
How to Apply:
Create a student login on the Maryland Higher Education Commission website: https://mdcaps.mhec.state.md.us/MDCAPS/login.aspx
Complete your FAFSA by Maryland’s deadline.
You can do this online at https://fafsa.ed.gov/ or by mail.
Complete The Maryland State Financial Aid Application (MSFAA Guidelines): Allows qualified children of undocumented immigrants, who qualify for in-state tuition under §15–106.8 of the MD Education Article, to apply and be considered for certain types of State need-based financial aid.
- Available online on your MDCAPS portal to applicants who are ineligible to receive federal aid using the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).
(Note: If you qualify for the MSFAA Application, do not fill out a FAFSA Form)
