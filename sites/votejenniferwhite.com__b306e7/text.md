I am Delegate Jennifer White Holland and I represent District 10 in Baltimore County.
By day, I use my public health training at a local community health foundation to lead and support local grassroots advocacy campaigns.
In Annapolis, I fight for evidence-based public health policies that protect and promote our health and wellbeing.
In the Maryland General Assembly, I work collaboratively with local, state, and federal colleagues to deliver results for District 10 residents.
I have sponsored and voted for legislation that improves access to community services through capital bond initiatives, improve our schools and education workforce, increase access to affordable housing, protect our environment, strengthen Maryland's economic development, and increase access to high quality health care.
I am proud to serve as your Delegate and voice in Annapolis.
