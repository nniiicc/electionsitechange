These are just a few.

Protecting Women's Reproductive Rights Jeff is a staunch advocate for women’s reproductive rights.
He believes decisions about reproductive healthcare are personal and government should not interfere.
Jeff trusts women to make the best choices for themselves and their families and will tirelessly defend their right to choose.
Healthcare Jeff believes healthcare is a human right and that’s why he supports Medicare for All.
For too long, healthcare costs have increased forcing Missourians into bankruptcy due to medical expenses and ensuring everyone has access to affordable, quality healthcare.
Jeff will fight for affordable healthcare solutions.
Gun Safety Gun violence is a pressing issue in our community and action is needed now.
With gun violence as the leading cause of death among children, Jeff supports red-flag laws, mandatory gun-locks, and age requirements to address this epidemic.
LGBTQ+ Rights Jeff and his husband are passionate about protecting LGBTQ+ rights.
They believe these rights are fundamental civil rights, allowing individuals to live their authentic selves.
In Jefferson City, Jeff will be a vocal advocate for equal rights for all.
Education Missouri’s public schools are “fully-funded,” but the reality is starkly different.
Jeff believes we need to invest in our public education and will fight to increase teacher salaries, expand access to Pre-K education, and ensure all schools operate on a five-day week.
Quality education is crucial for every child’s future.
Public Safety Jeff is committed to enhancing community safety by providing resources to emergency services and ensuring everyone’s basic needs are met, especially during challenging times.
He will work to strengthen our community and provide support to those in need.
