ErinForNebraska.com Privacy Policy
Effective Date: June 17, 2025
Welcome to ErinForNebraska.com (the “Site”), the official campaign website for Erin Feichtinger, candidate for Nebraska State Legislature (“we,” “us,” or “our”).
Protecting your privacy is important to us.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information in connection with the Site and related campaign activities.
1.
Information We Collect
a.
Voluntary Personal Information
We collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Donation payment details (processed securely via third-party processors)
- Volunteer sign-up info (e.g., availability, skills)
- Event registration responses
b.
Automatically Collected Data
When you use the Site, we may automatically collect:
- IP address and device type
- Operating system and browser type
- Pages visited, time spent, and clickstream patterns
- Referring URLs and timestamps
This information is typically collected via cookies, web server logs, and similar tracking technologies.
2.
How We Use Your Information
- Communications: Send campaign updates, newsletters, event notices, volunteer coordination, and donation confirmations
- Fundraising: Facilitate donations and issue receipts as required by law
- Campaign Management: Identify volunteer opportunities and coordinate campaign activities
- Site Improvement: Analyze usage trends and improve Site content and functionality
- Legal Compliance: Abide by campaign finance laws, respond to legal requests, and protect our legal interests
3.
How We Share Your Data
We do not rent, sell, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
We may share information with:
- Service Providers: e.g., payment processors, email platforms, volunteer-management tools
- Legal Compliance: authorities or courts when required by law or to protect our rights
- Campaign Operations: vendors supporting campaign infrastructure, under strict confidentiality protections
4.
Cookies & Tracking
We use cookies and similar tools to:
- Keep you logged in
- Remember preferences
- Provide analytics on Site use
You can control or disable cookies from your browser, but this may limit your access to certain Site features.
5.
Data Security
We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data.
However, no online system is entirely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute protection.
6.
Your Rights & Choices
- Access & Correction: Contact us to access or correct your information
- Communications Opt-Out: Unsubscribe via links in our emails or contact us directly
- Browser Controls: Disable cookies or clear browsing history
7.
Children’s Privacy
Our Site is not intended for children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect data from minors under 13.
8.
Links to Other Sites
Our Site may link to third-party sites (e.g., social media, news outlets).
We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
We recommend reviewing their policies.
9.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy as needed.
The Effective Date at the top will reflect revisions.
Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
