Volunteer E-Mail Updates Store CONTRIBUTE NRA Endorses Republican Kris Kobach for Attorney General September 13, 2022 Topeka, Kansas — The National Rifle Association announced today the endorsement and A rating of Republican Kris Kobach for Kansas Attorney General.
Kobach is running against a liberal Democrat who has stated publicly he will not enforce order defend laws he disagrees with.
Kobach is also endorsed by the NRA state affiliate, the Kansas State Rifle Association.
