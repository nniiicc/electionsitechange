Volunteer E-Mail Updates Store CONTRIBUTE Kansas Agriculture Associations Endorse Kris Kobach for Attorney General August 30, 2022 Topeka, Kansas — Large Kansas agricultural associations announced endorsements for Kris Kobach for Kansas Attorney General this week.
The Kansas Grain and Feed Association, the Kansas Agribusiness Retailers Association, and the Kansas Cooperative Council made the announcement through their collective political action committee, the Kansas Agri Business Council.
KABC’s goal is to support candidates who will promote the general welfare of the agricultural industry in Kansas.
Agriculture is Kansas’s largest industry and economic driver, responsible for approximately $64 billion in economic contribution or 42 percent of the state’s total economy, according to the Kansas State Department of Agriculture.
“Kansas farmers, ranchers and agriculture retailers can count on me to uphold the law and defend their interests as the next attorney general,” said Kobach.
“Agriculture is the backbone of the Kansas economy, and I’m proud to be endorsed by these organizations.” These Kansas agriculture associations join a growing number of endorsers of Kris Kobach’s campaign for attorney general.
