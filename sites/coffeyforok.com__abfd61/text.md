Meet Nick
Nick Coffey is a career prosecutor and lifelong Oklahoman with a proven record of upholding the rule of law, protecting our communities, and holding powerful criminal organizations accountable.
As a federal prosecutor for the Western District of Oklahoma, he has spent the past several years leading some of the state’s most high-profile and complex prosecutions.
Nick has successfully prosecuted crimes related to transnational criminal organizations, money laundering, immigration, illegal firearms, drug conspiracies, counterfeiting, and fraud.
