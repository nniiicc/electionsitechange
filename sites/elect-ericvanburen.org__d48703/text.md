WHAT ERIC BELIEVES What I Will Fight For Affordability and Economic Fairness Working families should not have to choose between utilities, housing, childcare, healthcare, and groceries.
I will fight for utility relief, responsible tax policy, affordable housing, stronger wages, and investments that lower the everyday cost of living.
Community-Based Public Safety I support law enforcement, constitutional policing, and consequences for violent crime.
I also support prevention through mental-health services, addiction treatment, youth mentorship, reentry support, and a community CARE Unit that helps address crises before they become tragedies.
Strong Schools and Opportunities for Young People Our teachers, paraprofessionals, and school employees deserve competitive pay and real support.
Our young people deserve safe schools, career training, recreation, mentorship, and a genuine pathway to a successful future.
Local Jobs, Energy and Infrastructure Public investments should create local jobs, reduce utility costs, improve roads and transportation, modernize infrastructure, and prepare residents for careers in energy, construction, technology, and skilled trades.
Responsible Growth and Rural Protection Growth must respect existing communities.
I will defend local water, farmland, property values, rural identity, emergency services, and the right of residents to receive honest information before major development decisions are made.
Accountable Government Government must be transparent about how decisions are made and how public money is spent.
I will use legislation, budget authority, oversight, and public accountability to make sure promises made to District 2 become promises kept.
