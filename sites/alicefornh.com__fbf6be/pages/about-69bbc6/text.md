She decided to run for State Representative in 2024 when her own rights came under attack in New Hampshire.
Ever since, she’s been pushing back against harmful policies at the statehouse and changing minds.
During the 2025 Legislative Session, she filed legislation to address affordable housing and lead poisoning, and worked bipartisanly to pass a bill honoring Gold Star Families that had been stalled in NH for 13 years.
Alice lives in Dover with her fiancé.
Alice’s family first moved to New Hampshire in 2015 and she graduated from Bedford High School, and discovered her passion for problem-solving and public service.
She's called the Granite State home ever since.
Alice previously served as the nation-wide Vice Chair for SEDS-USA (Students for the Exploration and Development of Space), a national student-run non-profit with over 80 chapters across the country focused on developing students into professionals in the aerospace industry.
During college she worked on developing an experimental space telescope for detecting high energy particles at the UNH Earth Ocean Space Center and interned at SpaceX, designing a new housing and heatsink system for the Starship rocket radio controller.
