I am not a politician.” Donate America the Beautiful America is the, “shining city upon a hill”, and Wisconsin is the heart of that city.
Simply by being born in this great nation, we have been given opportunities unlike any other people in the world.
America is truly exceptional, and it is up to us as citizens to work tirelessly to maintain the freedoms we have been endowed.
LIFE As a Navy SEAL veteran who has lost friends in both training and combat, and as a paramedic, father, and grandfather, I have a unique understanding of how precious life is and why it should be protected.
We must respect and cherish life.
LIBERTY As Americans, we are the recipients and custodians of two of the most beautifully written and powerful documents that the world has ever seen; the Declaration of Independence and the Constitution.
We must work together to ensure that our rights, as enumerated in these papers are not infringed upon.
THE PURSUIT OF HAPPINESS We are not guaranteed the right to happiness, we are guaranteed the right to pursue happiness.
The more we are limited by government, the more this right is endangered.
As Americans, we do better when we are free to make our own decisions with our hard-earned money and when government gets out of the way of the American people.
