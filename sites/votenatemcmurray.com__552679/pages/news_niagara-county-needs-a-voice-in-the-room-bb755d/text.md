VIDEOS VOLUNTEER Niagara County Needs a Voice in the Room Jan 30 Written By Nathan McMurray Dear Niagara County, For decades, Niagara County has sent the same kind of politicians to Albany—Republican Party insiders whose main strategy is to complain, posture, and blame everyone else.
And year after year, they come home empty-handed.
Look around.
How are the roads?
The schools?
The investment?
In a partisan era, pretending politics doesn’t matter isn’t realism—it’s surrender.
New York is a Democratic state.
The budgets, the projects, and the money all flow through Democratic leadership.
You don’t bring resources home by yelling from the sidelines; you bring them home by being in the room.
We can keep electing do nothing politicians who collect a paycheck and deliver excuses, or we can be smart, play the game, and send someone who knows how to get results.
I’m running to put Niagara County back at the table—and to bring real money home where it belongs.
