Why Invest in This Campaign?
If you’ve followed Nate over the years, you know this campaign isn’t about an empty suit.
It’s about independence, persistence, and the willingness to stand up to anyone—when it’s right.
If you live in Assembly District 145, contributions between $5 and $250 may be matched up to 12× through New York’s Public Campaign Finance Program.
If you live elsewhere, your donation still helps build an independent, grassroots campaign.
Why This Race Can Be Won Nate isn’t starting from scratch.
After nearly a decade in public life, he has built a large following, strong name recognition, and a proven ability to reach voters.
This district has leaned Republican in recent elections, but the longtime incumbent is gone.
Open seats are different.
With the right campaign, this race is competitive.
Nate has proven he can compete in difficult territory.
In 2018, he came within 0.4% of winning one of New York’s most Republican congressional districts.
He has consistently outperformed Democratic expectations and remains the only Democrat to have won on Grand Island in years.
Why This Seat Matters New York’s Legislature is controlled by Democrats.
Regardless of political affiliation, legislators in the governing majority generally have greater opportunities to shape budgets, legislation, and funding priorities.
For a district like ours, representation with influence matters.
Niagara Falls generates billions through tourism, hydropower, and international commerce.
Our communities deserve a representative who can fight to bring more of that investment home.
This Is Home Nate has lived, worked, and served these communities for years.
He isn’t running because it’s politically convenient.
He’s running because he believes Niagara County deserves stronger leadership and a stronger voice.
If you believe in independent leadership and a campaign powered by people—not political machines—we’d be honored to have your support.
Together, we can win.
