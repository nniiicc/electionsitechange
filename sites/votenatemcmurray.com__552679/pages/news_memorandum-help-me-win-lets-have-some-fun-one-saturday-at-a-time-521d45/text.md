VIDEOS VOLUNTEER MEMORANDUM: HELP ME WIN — LET’S HAVE SOME FUN, ONE SATURDAY AT A TIME Feb 16 Written By Nathan McMurray MEMORANDUM: HELP ME WIN — LET’S HAVE SOME FUN, ONE SATURDAY AT A TIME To: Supporters and Community Leaders Across Niagara County From: Nate McMurray Campaign Re: Building a Grassroots Team for Niagara Falls and Surrounding Communities Date: Presidents’ Day — February 16, 2026 Campaigns don’t run on slogans — they run on people.
And right now, Western Niagara has big problems and not nearly enough organized people working together to fix them.
In parts of the county — especially around Lockport — there’s a long-established political system built on patronage, insider deals, negativity, and fear.
Too often, that system treats Niagara Falls less like an opportunity and more like a problem to keep at arm’s length.
Decisions get made without us.
Resources flow elsewhere.
And we’re told to be grateful for whatever trickles down — or get out of the way.
We don’t have to accept that.
And honestly… we’re not going to.
We’re building our own team — one neighborhood, one block, one conversation at a time — across Youngstown, Niagara, LaSalle, Niagara Falls, North Tonawanda, Wheatfield, and beyond.
A real grassroots crew.
No gatekeepers.
No secret handshake.
Just people who care about this place and want to make it better.
And yes — we’re going to have some fun doing it.
Beginning soon, we’ll meet every Saturday at 10:00 a.m. in Niagara Falls (360 Rainbow Boulevard, Niagara Falls, NY). ☕ Coffee is on me 😄 Good people guaranteed 🛠️ Real work — but a good time, too This isn’t a boring lecture.
It’s neighbors getting together to figure out how to actually win — sharing ideas, making plans, assigning roles, telling stories, laughing a little, and building real momentum week by week.
We’re looking for community captains — people willing to help organize their area, knock on doors, gather signatures, put up yard signs, talk to friends, and become the local spark plug for change.
Let’s be honest: we don’t have a giant political machine behind us.
We’ve got something better — people who care about Niagara County and refuse to give up on it.
That means: Every yard sign matters.
Every new volunteer matters.
Every conversation matters.
Every Saturday matters.
Niagara County faces a choice.
We can drift along and hope things magically improve… or we can grab the oars and steer.
Think of it this way: 🌊 We can go with the flow… 💥 Or we can build our own boat.
If you believe Niagara Falls and our surrounding communities deserve better, come be part of the team that makes it happen.
Will you be a captain for change?
Will you knock doors, gather signatures, rally your neighbors, and jump into the real, nitty-gritty work that wins campaigns?
It won’t always be glamorous — but it will be meaningful, energizing, and yes, even fun.
Show up.
Bring a friend.
Bring your ideas.
Bring your sense of humor.
Help us win — one Saturday at a time.
Let’s do something big, together. 👉 To sign up, please send a message through the contact form at VoteNateMcMurray.com and let us know you want to be part of the team.
We’ll follow up with details on the first meeting.
