Vision for South Dakota
“I wish to empower and galvanize those who have felt voiceless and powerless.”
I will…
- Fight for protections, transparency, and local control when it comes to hyperscale data centers—and oppose using taxpayer dollars or tax breaks to subsidize them
- Support our educators by helping find and allocate the resources needed to hire and retain quality educators and providing them with the necessary resources to run a successful classroom.
- Work to provide accessible, quality healthcare for ALL South Dakotans.
- Take steps to ensure that our state government is accessible, transparent, and accountable to the people.
- Support our Native People and Land.
- Protect each citizen’s right to vote.
- Expand economic and workforce development that supports individuals and families.
