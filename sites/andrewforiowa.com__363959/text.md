I'm running to represent you in Iowa House District 98 because I believe in the potential of our community.
I am committed to strengthening our public schools by ensuring teachers and students have the resources they need to succeed.
I will fight to expand healthcare access and lower costs so no one has to choose between their health and their finances.
And I will work to build an economy that works for everyone, not just a few.
Andrew Fitzgerald was born and raised in Davenport, Iowa.
He has spent his life rooted in the values of hard work, family, and community.
A graduate of Davenport West High School, Andrew got his first job at age 14 working at Hy-Vee.
He built a career in skilled trades, working as a mechanic at Lujack's, in maintenance at Kraft where he was a proud union member, and now as an automation technician at Invio Automation helping drive innovation in advanced manufacturing.
Andrew is the proud father of two teenage sons.
He is an avid sports fan and a lifelong NASCAR enthusiast who has raced dirt cars and road racing motorcycles.
He also enjoys motorsports photography and has worked as media personnel for NASCAR, capturing the energy and excitement of the track.
With deep ties to Iowa's working class community and a commitment to improving the lives of everyday Iowans, Andrew is ready to bring honest leadership and real-world experience to public service.
Iowa faces real challenges that require real solutions.
Here's where I stand.
Education
Iowa was once a national leader in public education.
I'll fight to restore funding, reverse harmful voucher programs, and support our teachers and students.
Increase funding for Career and Technical education to strengthen Iowa's workforce.
Promoting CTE courses in schools so kids know college is not the only option.
Healthcare
Healthcare is a fundamental right.
I support expanding Medicaid, strengthening rural clinics, and making prescription drugs more affordable for all Iowans.
I also advocate for universal healthcare and believe healthcare is a human right.
Per capita, a US citizen pays almost double for healthcare than any other country, it just makes sense to switch.
Economy
Iowa ranks 50th in economic growth, 50th in economic activity and 48th in personal income growth.
I'll support small businesses, invest in job training, and focus on clean energy and infrastructure to rebuild our economy.
Infrastructure
Our roads, bridges, and broadband need urgent investment.
I'll fight for infrastructure improvements that create jobs and improve quality of life.
Workers' Rights
As a former union member, I'll always stand with workers.
I support the PRO Act and oppose right-to-work laws that weaken worker protections.
I also support raising the minimum wage in Iowa to ensure workers can afford to live with dignity.
Clean Water
Iowa has some of the most polluted waterways in the country.
Clean water is a basic right, and I'll hold polluters accountable while investing in real solutions.
This campaign is about more than one election, it's about building a stronger Iowa for our children and grandchildren.
