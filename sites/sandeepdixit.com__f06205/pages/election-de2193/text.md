Find your polling place ↗ The new district map Ohio adopted a new congressional map on October 31, 2025 , and 2026 is the first election held on it.
Ohio's 13th Congressional District now covers all of Summit County, the northern and central part of Stark County including Canton and North Canton, and Kent and Streetsboro in Portage County.
Canal Fulton, Lawrence Township and part of Massillon moved to a neighboring district.
If you live near any of those lines, your district may have changed even though you didn’t move.
