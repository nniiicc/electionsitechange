What’s in Ohio’s 13th for 2026 Under the map adopted on October 31, 2025 , OH-13 covers: Summit County — all of it: Akron and every township and municipality in the county .
Stark County — part of it: the northern and central part, including Canton and North Canton .
Portage County — part of it: Kent and Streetsboro .
That includes Akron, Canton, Cuyahoga Falls, Kent, Stow, North Canton, Hudson, Barberton, Streetsboro, Twinsburg, Green and Tallmadge .
Canal Fulton, Lawrence Township and part of Massillon moved out of the district; Kent and Streetsboro moved in.
