AGRICULTURE AND URBAN WATER SUPPLY, NO SUSTAINABLE WATER PLAN
Water is essential for our farms, businesses, and families, but not having enough is leading to lost jobs and higher priced food.
I will push for the monies that have previously been allocated, to actually be used to increase our water supply and its efficient use.
We need to fix the mismanagement of our water, and I’ll push for laws that unite agriculture, cities, and the environment.
In the past, these groups only looked out for themselves, which got us nowhere.
It’s time to make them work together to protect our water future.
We need to stop sending needed water to the ocean.
We need to develop new sources of water such as desalinization.
We need to develop new storage facilities.
We need to find actual solutions to this problem – not ones that have failed time and time again.
The central valley and California were built on agriculture.
In order for agriculture to thrive, we must insure a sustained water source.
