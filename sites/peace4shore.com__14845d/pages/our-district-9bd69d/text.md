Check Inland ~361,000 people Horse Farms & Hometowns Almost half the district lives in these inland towns, from the horse farms of Colts Neck to Allaire State Park's restored 19th-century iron village.
Fort Monmouth is being rebuilt as homes and businesses in Eatontown and Tinton Falls.
It's commuters, parents and small-business owners, doing the kitchen-table math on childcare, healthcare and the mortgage.
