TONI’S PLATFORM
Simple.
Personal.
Focused entirely on what families need.
Education
Toni believes every child deserves a strong start in life.
That begins with great schools, well supported teachers, and classrooms where students have the tools they need to succeed.
Education should open doors, not limit them.
Toni will work to strengthen public schools, expand access to early learning, and create opportunities for students who want to pursue college, trades, or technical careers.
Children in District 45 deserve the very best, and Toni will fight to make sure they get it.
Affordability
Toni understands how the rising cost of living affects everyday families.
From groceries to utilities to childcare, everything has become more expensive.
She believes families should not have to choose between essential needs.
Toni will work for policies that help reduce financial strain, support working parents, and make it easier for families to build stability and opportunity.
Childcare
Toni has lived the challenge of finding safe, reliable, and affordable childcare.
She knows what that stress feels like.
She wants every parent in District 45 to have access to childcare options they can trust and afford.
She will support efforts to expand childcare programs, improve quality, and provide relief to families who are doing their best to balance work and family life.
Healthcare
Toni believes that healthcare is not a luxury.
It is a basic need.
She has seen families put off doctor visits because of cost, seniors struggle with prescriptions, and parents worry about their children’s coverage.
Toni supports expanding access to affordable healthcare, strengthening mental health services, and keeping local healthcare providers and hospitals strong.
No family should have to suffer because they cannot afford care.
