Meet Campaign Coordinator Christen!
Christen MacGuire has called Laramie home for nearly a decade after moving from Casper to attend the University of Wyoming, where she earned a degree in History.
As Campaign Coordinator for Representative Trey Sherwood's re-election campaign in House District 14, Christen organizes volunteer efforts, including door knocking, postcard writing, phone banking, and community events.
She previously coordinated campaign efforts for the Albany County Democratic Party and remains actively involved in Laramie's downtown business community.
Inspired by her father, who served in the Wyoming Legislature alongside Rep Sherwood, Christen developed an early passion for public service and organizing.
Through her work at Coal Creek Coffee Co. and her involvement in the community, she has built lasting relationships across Laramie and has had the opportunity to work alongside Rep Sherwood in a variety of capacities.
Christen is proud to help lead a campaign rooted in community, collaboration, and service.
