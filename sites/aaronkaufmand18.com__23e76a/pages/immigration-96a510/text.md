Immigration
I am tired of the extreme xenophobia in our society today.
Immigrants are and always have been an important part of the fabric of our community and play a critical role in our economy.
Protecting our friends and neighbors from unfair treatment, harassment, seizure, and deportation with no due process is inhumane and un-American.
We need to stand up together to Trump and an out-of-control ICE and demand that all are treated with dignity and protected by our laws.
We passed laws this year limiting cooperation with ICE.
