Photo Credit: Office of Governor Wes Moore Each year, I award college scholarships to undergraduate college students in our community.
To be eligible you must: Plan to attend as an undergraduate a Maryland college or university or a private career school Complete the 203-24 Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) or the Maryland State Financial Aid Application (MSFAA) _______ Have a family income limit of less than $100,000; And attend school either full or part-time.
Please visit www.fasfa.gov to access the application and MDCaps to access the MSFAA.
My scholarship awards typically average $1,000.
If your major is not offered at a Maryland institution of higher education, you may apply for a unique major designation that would allow you to use my Delegate Scholarship funds for an out-of-state school.
The scholarship application must be filled out completely and returned with all of the required documentation: Application: Part One Email my office at: aaron.kaufman@house.state.md.us Application: Part Two Complete and submit electronically to aaron.kaufman@house.state.md.us Letters and essays must be typed.
One Letter of Recommendation: Academic (teacher or professor) or Non-academic (coach, work supervisor, clergy) Unofficial Transcript from the school’s web portal I would also encourage you to visit the Maryland Higher Education Commission website at www.mhec.state.md.us to learn about other available state scholarships and grants.
Some additional websites you might find useful include: U.S Department of Education Financial Aid - http://www.ed.gov/finaid.html National Association of Student Financial Aid Administrators - http://www.finaid.org Fast Web Scholarship Search - http://fastweb.com Peterson Education and Career Center - http://www.petersons.com Thank you again for your interest and good luck with your application.
