The Economy
I am deeply troubled that Maryland lags behind Virginia in attracting big-name job producing companies that stimulate the economy.
Fairfax County, VA routinely outpaces Montgomery County in terms of job creation.
In order to fund excellent services for our residents we need to be laser focused on economic development to create well-paying jobs and increase the State’s tax revenues.
Bringing smart economic development and growth to District 18 and across state of Maryland will be a top priority for me in Annapolis.
