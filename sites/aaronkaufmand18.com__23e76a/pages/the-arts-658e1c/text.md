The Arts
I vigorously support assisting those in the creative arts.
In our district, we are truly blessed to have two incredible venues, Roundhouse Theater and the Music Center at Strathmore
Additionally, numerous artists, writers, dancers, filmmakers, and other creative folk are living and working in District 18 and in our greater area.
Besides providing entertainment and cultural enrichment to our residents, time and again, studies have shown that the arts are one of the best economic drivers for job creation, tax revenue, and turning around struggling neighborhoods.
As an art and culture lover supporting the arts will always be a priority for me.
