Healthcare
Quality healthcare is something that I have focused on my entire life.
When I am in Annapolis, I will ensure that healthcare decisions are made by patients and their doctors, not by an insurance company.
I will fight to lower prescription drug costs so that people do not have to choose between food and medication.
I will advocate ensuring that people with disabilities and their families are given the support they need to thrive.
I will work to lessen the burden of medical debt on families all across the state.
