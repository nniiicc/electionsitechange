Climate Change
Follow the science and you realize that climate change is the greatest existential threat to us, our children and grandchildren.
That is an incontrovertible fact.
Thanks to the Democrats in the Maryland General Assembly the Climate Solutions Now Act of 2022 is a significant achievement in the fight against climate change.
However, several key provisions of that Act were left out of the final version.
I will fight to get those provisions and other legislation protecting our planet passed.
We must act now.
Also, a key corollary to protecting the environment is to be a strong advocate for environmental justice and to make sure the burdens of climate change do not disproportionately fall on the poor and people of color.
