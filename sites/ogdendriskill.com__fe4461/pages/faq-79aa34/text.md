Frequently Asked Questions

Elections and Logistics
When is the Wyoming Primary Election?
The Wyoming Primary Election is Tuesday, August 18, 2026.
Polls are typically open from 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM.
However, early voting starts July 21, 2026.
What is Wyoming Senate District 1?
The district encompasses all of Crook County, as well as parts of Campbell and Weston counties.
Where can I find my polling location?
Voters can find their specific polling location through the Wyoming Secretary of State's website or by contacting the Campbell, Crook, and Weston county clerk websites.
Political & Legislative Record
How long has Ogden served in the Wyoming Senate?
Ogden was first elected to the Wyoming Senate in 2011.
Ogden was elected by the Senate to serve as Senate President for the 2022-2023 biennium.
What Committees does Ogden serve on?
Ogden serves as a member of the following committees: Senate Appropriations, Select Committee on Capital Financing & Investments, Select Water Committee, NCSL – Natural Resources, Energy and Environment, and NCSL – State Coordinator.

Who does Ogden Driskill represent in the Wyoming Senate?
Ogden works for the people of District 1 and believes government should remain accessible, accountable, and responsive to every community and every family in it.
