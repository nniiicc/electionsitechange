U.S.
Air Force Captain, Fairchild Air Force Base From a young age, I knew I wanted to give back to the country that had given my family a second chance at freedom.
I joined the Air Force and rose to the rank of Captain at Fairchild Air Force Base, serving the nation that my parents had sacrificed everything to reach.
My military training has prepared me to continuously look for process improvement and be efficient to better serve the people.
Spokane County Intergovernmental Affairs Officer After the Air Force, I was hired by Michael Baumgartner, former Spokane County Treasurer and now Congressman, to manage public relations and intergovernmental affairs.
I gained insight on how local government works for the people.
Washington State House Legislative Assistant While working in the WA State House as a Legislative Assistant to State Representative Rob Chase, I gained experience with the inner workings of state government: policy, procedures, relationships, and the pressure points.
I am proud and grateful for State Representative Chase's endorsement.
He is a truthful leader and seeks to recruit, train, and elect the next generation of leaders.
