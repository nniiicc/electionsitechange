Vote Hillary Pham Why I Run Platform Meet the Candidate Endorsements Contact Me Get Involved Volunteer Events Press Donate Back to Press Releases March 23, 2026 Hillary Pham Announces Campaign for WA State Representative, 4th District, Position 1 Liberty Lake, WA — March 23, 2026 — Hillary Pham announced her campaign for WA State Representative of the 4th District during the Spokane County Lincoln Day event, emphasizing a commitment to public safety, education, and economy.
“After two legislative sessions in Olympia and as a lifelong Washingtonian, I believe this beautiful state is worth fighting for,” said Hillary Pham.
“I witnessed firsthand how important it is to encourage free markets and to value small businesses, as they are the backbone of our economy.” The campaign will focus on defending public safety, education empowerment, and economic opportunities, inviting residents to participate in the legislative process and share their perspectives on the future of District 4.
Throughout the campaign, Hillary Pham plans to meet with community members, local organizations, and stakeholders to foster collaborative conversations.
Hillary Pham brings experience as a former legislative assistant to State Representative Rob Chase of the 4th district.
They will be running for office together.
She is committed to conducting a respectful and inclusive campaign grounded in integrity and accountability.
Hillary Pham is a U.S.
Air Force Veteran who has been involved in the Spokane Sheriff Community Oriented Policing Effort (SCOPE), Spokane County Veterans’ Advisory Board and volunteers at the Sacred Heart Medical Center’s Neonatal Intensive Care Unit (NICU).
The campaign for WA State Representative of the 4th District is centered on unity, integrity and active community engagement.
