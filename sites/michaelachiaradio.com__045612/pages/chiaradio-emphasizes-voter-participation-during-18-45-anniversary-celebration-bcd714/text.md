Chiaradio joined Pamela Chapman and community members over the weekend for the third anniversary celebration of 18/45 Medical Marijuana Dispensary.
Chapman, the owner and founder of the business, invited Chiaradio to address attendees during the celebration.
Chiaradio used his remarks to emphasize voter participation in the final weeks before the November 3 general election.
While acknowledging the many policy issues at stake, Chiaradio said those issues ultimately depend on people participating in the democratic process and making their voices heard.
He also emphasized that he views the 2026 election as bigger than his own congressional campaign, pointing to the future of Mississippi politics, the upcoming redistricting process, and statewide elections in 2027.
Chiaradio encouraged Mississippi Democrats to approach the closing weeks of the election with confidence, participate, and encourage their friends, families, and neighbors to vote.
In-person absentee voting remains available for eligible Mississippi voters through October 31, and Election Day is November 3.
Chiaradio thanked Chapman for the opportunity to speak and congratulated 18/45 Medical Marijuana Dispensary on its third anniversary.
