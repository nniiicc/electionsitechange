- January 20, 2026
Quitman, MS — On Saturday, January 17, Democratic congressional nominee Michael A.
Chiaradio joined the Clarke County Democratic Party in the 19th Annual Dr.
Martin Luther King Jr.
Unity Parade in Quitman, Mississippi.
The event drew a strong turnout from across the community, bringing together residents, local leaders, and organizers to honor the legacy of Dr.
King.
During the parade, Chiaradio shared his central mission with attendees: to use the full capacity of the federal government to help restore economic opportunity in Mississippi, strengthen domestic industry, and ensure working families are not left behind.
The campaign is building a renewed belief that progress is possible through organization and participation.
