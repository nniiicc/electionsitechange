Chiaradio attended the 27th Annual Pre-Memorial Day Branch Hill Blues Festival at McIntosh Farm in DeKalb this weekend, continuing his outreach across Mississippi’s 3rd Congressional District.
The event brought together community members from across the region for a day of music, fellowship, and community engagement.
While at the festival, Chiaradio spoke with attendees about the challenges facing working families and outlined his campaign’s priorities, including lowering healthcare costs, investing in infrastructure, strengthening public schools, and supporting local food systems centered around small businesses and family farms.
Chiaradio also had the opportunity to speak briefly during the event, where he emphasized the importance of political participation and building long-term momentum for Democratic organizing across Mississippi.
The campaign continues to expand its grassroots operation across Mississippi’s 3rd Congressional District through direct voter contact, community engagement, and sustained outreach efforts.
