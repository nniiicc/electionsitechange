- April 13, 2026
Meridian, MS — Democratic congressional nominee Michael A.
Chiaradio attended the annual Sipp & Savor Food & Drink Experience this past Saturday evening at Mississippi Arts + Entertainment Experience in downtown Meridian.
Sipp & Savor is the premier fundraising event supporting The MAX, bringing together award-winning chefs, brewers, and distillers from across the state.
The event draws community leaders, small business owners, and residents from throughout Mississippi’s 3rd Congressional District.
Chiaradio spent the evening meeting with attendees, speaking directly with voters, and continuing his campaign’s focus on building relationships across the district.
During the event, Chiaradio also met with Mississippi Lieutenant Governor Delbert Hosemann, highlighting the importance of maintaining open lines of communication across political lines in service of the state.
Chiaradio emphasized that strong communities are built through engagement, respect, and a shared commitment to Mississippi’s future.
While political differences remain, he noted that a competitive and active political environment ultimately strengthens the state and benefits all residents.
Chiaradio’s campaign continues to focus on expanding its coalition by engaging voters of all backgrounds, including independents and those who may not traditionally support Democratic candidates, while remaining committed to strengthening the Democratic Party across Mississippi.
