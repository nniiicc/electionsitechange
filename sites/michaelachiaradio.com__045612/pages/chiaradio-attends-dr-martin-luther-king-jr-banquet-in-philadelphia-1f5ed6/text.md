- January 21, 2026
Philadelphia, MS — Democratic congressional nominee Michael A.
Chiaradio attended the Community in Action Dr.
Martin Luther King Jr.
Banquet held Saturday evening in Philadelphia, Mississippi.
The annual event brought together community leaders, faith leaders, and organizers to honor the life and legacy of Dr.
Martin Luther King Jr. and to reflect on the continued importance of civic participation.
The evening featured a series of inspirational and motivating speakers who emphasized community organizing and the responsibility of citizens to participate in the democratic process.
Chiaradio joined attendees in recognizing Dr.
King’s belief that collective action and civic participation are central to building stronger communities and expanding opportunity.
The banquet served as a reminder of the ongoing work needed to strengthen democracy and encourage participation across Mississippi.
