Chiaradio attended Decatur Day’s Broad Street Bash last weekend in downtown Decatur, joining community members, local leaders, small businesses, and families for one of the city’s largest annual celebrations.
Hosted by the Decatur Chamber of Commerce, the event brought together residents from across the area for a day of entertainment, local vendors, food, and community activities.
Chiaradio was honored to be invited to speak and share his vision for Mississippi’s Third Congressional District.
Throughout the day, Chiaradio met with attendees and discussed the issues that have become the foundation of his campaign, including lowering healthcare costs, investing in infrastructure, strengthening public schools, and building stronger local economies throughout rural Mississippi.
The event also provided an opportunity to speak with voters from a wide range of political backgrounds.
Chiaradio said he was encouraged by conversations with many Republicans who expressed an openness to considering a commonsense agenda focused on improving the quality of life and economic opportunities available to Mississippi families.
As the campaign continues to travel the district, Chiaradio remains focused on meeting voters where they are, listening to their concerns, and building support across traditional political lines.
