- May 11, 2026
Meridian, MS — Democratic congressional nominee Michael A.
Chiaradio attended the 39th Annual Mother’s Day Blues Festival in Meridian this weekend, joining community members for a day of music, fellowship, and celebration.
During the event, Chiaradio had the opportunity to speak briefly and introduce fellow candidate Scott Colom, who is currently running in a highly competitive race and building strong support across the state.
The festival brought together residents from across the region, highlighting the importance of community engagement and shared experiences in strengthening Mississippi’s future.
Chiaradio emphasized the role of grassroots organizing and local connection in building a competitive Democratic presence, noting that events like the Mother’s Day Blues Festival provide an important opportunity to connect directly with voters and community leaders.
The campaign continues to prioritize on-the-ground outreach, working to build relationships and momentum across Mississippi’s 3rd Congressional District ahead of the 2026 election.
