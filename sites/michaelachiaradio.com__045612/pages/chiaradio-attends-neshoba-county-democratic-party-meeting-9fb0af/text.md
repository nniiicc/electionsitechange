- April 1, 2026
Philadelphia, MS — Democratic congressional nominee Michael A.
Chiaradio attended the Neshoba County Democratic Party’s monthly meeting this week in Philadelphia, continuing his outreach across Mississippi’s 3rd Congressional District.
Chiaradio met with members of the party’s executive committee and local leaders to discuss the path forward for the 2026 election cycle and the work required to build a stronger Democratic presence in the region.
The meeting focused on voter engagement, expanding turnout, and strengthening the local infrastructure needed to compete and win across the district.
Chiaradio emphasized the importance of sustained, on-the-ground organizing and collaboration with county leadership.
The Neshoba County Democratic Party expressed strong support for Chiaradio’s campaign and its efforts to build momentum heading into November.
Chiaradio continues to travel throughout the district, meeting with local leaders and voters as part of a broader effort to build a competitive, district-wide campaign.
