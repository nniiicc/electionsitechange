MARION, MS — Democratic congressional nominee Michael A.
Chiaradio attended One Voice’s “Conoce Tus Derechos” community training Saturday evening at Marion Town Hall.
Chiaradio was invited to attend the event in support of the organization and spent the evening meeting and speaking with community members.
The community training focused on constitutional rights, immigration law, voter registration and participation.
Chiaradio has continued to emphasize the importance of participation and community engagement during appearances across Mississippi’s Third Congressional District ahead of the November 3 general election.
Mississippi’s voter registration deadline is October 5, absentee voting is underway for eligible voters, and Election Day is November 3.
