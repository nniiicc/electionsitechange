Chiaradio joined top Mississippi Democrats on Wednesday, October 9th, for the Mississippi Democratic Party’s Fall Fundraiser, featuring former United States Senator Doug Jones of Alabama as the keynote speaker.
The event, held in Rankin County, drew a full house of party leaders, organizers, and grassroots supporters united behind the goal of building Democratic strength across the state.
Chiaradio, who is still the only Democrat running to represent Mississippi’s 3rd Congressional District, was invited to speak briefly during the evening’s program.
His remarks left a strong impression on attendees and reaffirmed his growing reputation as one of the party’s most energetic and determined new voices.
“It was an incredible night for Mississippi Democrats,” Chiaradio said following the event.
“Senator Doug Jones reminded us that we have to work all year if we want to win elections, and I couldn’t agree more.
I’m proud to be part of a team that’s working to prove Mississippi is not a ‘red’ state.” Attendees praised Chiaradio’s remarks, noting his disciplined work ethic and long-term commitment to strengthening the Democratic Party across Mississippi.
His message of persistence, integrity, and economic renewal has resonated with voters throughout the 3rd District and beyond.
“I’m grateful for the encouragement I’ve received from so many great Mississippi Democrats,” Chiaradio added.
“Our movement is growing, and together, we’re going to flip MS-03 blue.” Chiaradio’s campaign continues to build momentum with a message centered on practical leadership, economic competitiveness, and restoring trust in public service.
