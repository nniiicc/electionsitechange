Chiaradio attended the Noxubee County Black Excellence Gala last weekend for an evening celebrating leadership and community service.
Hosted by the Noxubee County Democratic Executive Committee under the leadership of Chairman LJ Brooks, the gala brought together nearly 300 attendees and featured elected officials, community advocates, and civic leaders from throughout the region.
Chiaradio was honored to participate in the program and speak to attendees about the importance of civic engagement and the responsibility each generation has to continue building upon the progress made by those who came before them.
Chiaradio said he was especially encouraged by the atmosphere, saying he believed the evening set the tone for a Democratic victory in November.
As the campaign continues to build momentum across Mississippi’s Third Congressional District, Chiaradio remains focused on expanding voter participation, strengthening grassroots organizing, and building the coalition necessary to compete and win in 2026.
