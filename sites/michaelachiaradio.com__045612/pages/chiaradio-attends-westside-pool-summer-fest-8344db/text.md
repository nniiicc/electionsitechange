- June 12, 2026
PHILADELPHIA, MS — Democratic nominee for Congress Michael A.
Chiaradio attended the Westside Pool Summer Fest last weekend, joining community members for a day focused on supporting efforts to reopen the community pool.
The event brought together residents from across the area and demonstrated the strong community spirit that exists throughout Mississippi’s Third Congressional District.
Chiaradio spent time meeting attendees, listening to their concerns, and discussing the issues facing local communities.
Chiaradio spoke to the crowd about the importance of civic engagement and participation in the upcoming November election.
He emphasized that meaningful change begins when people get involved and make their voices heard at the ballot box.
Summer Fest drew an impressive turnout and served as a reminder of what communities can accomplish when people come together around a shared purpose.
Chiaradio was honored to participate and contribute to an effort designed to benefit future generations.
