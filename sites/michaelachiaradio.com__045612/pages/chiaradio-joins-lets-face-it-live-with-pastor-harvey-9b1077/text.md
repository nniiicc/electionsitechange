Chiaradio joined Pastor Harvey on the “Let’s Face It” podcast for a live interview discussing his campaign for the United States House of Representatives in Mississippi’s Third Congressional District.
The conversation covered a wide range of issues, including Chiaradio’s agenda to invest in infrastructure, strengthen public schools, and build local food systems that keep more wealth in Mississippi communities.

The interview provided an opportunity for Chiaradio to explain in greater detail how his economic plans are designed to expand opportunity, strengthen small businesses and farms, and position Mississippi to compete more effectively in a changing global economy.
