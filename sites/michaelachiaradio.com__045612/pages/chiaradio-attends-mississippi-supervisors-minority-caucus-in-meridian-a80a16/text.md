Chiaradio attended the 2026 Mississippi Supervisors Minority Caucus gathering in Meridian earlier this week, joining local leaders and community members from across the region.
The event brought together elected officials, organizers, and residents to discuss issues impacting communities throughout Mississippi, with a focus on local leadership and regional cooperation.
As part of the event, Chiaradio visited Mississippi Arts + Entertainment Experience (The MAX) and attended a community fish fry at Dumont Plaza, where he met with residents and supporters from across the district.
The visit provided an opportunity to continue building relationships at the local level and engage directly with voters on the challenges and opportunities facing Mississippi’s 3rd Congressional District.
Chiaradio’s campaign continues to focus on expanding outreach, strengthening community ties, and building the foundation needed to compete across all parts of the district in the 2026 election cycle.
