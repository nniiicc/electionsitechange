- February 24, 2026
Philadelphia, Mississippi — On Sunday, Democratic congressional candidate Michael A.
Chiaradio joined the congregation at Goodway Missionary Baptist Church to participate in their Black History Month program.
Chiaradio thanked church members for their warm welcome and emphasized the importance of civic engagement at a pivotal moment for the state and the nation.
He noted that participation in the political process remains essential to shaping the future and strengthening democratic institutions.
Chiaradio’s campaign continues to focus on expanding engagement and building momentum toward what he described as a defining moment for Democrats in Mississippi and across the country.
