Chiaradio has received the endorsement of the Alliance for Retired Americans, a national organization representing more than 4.4 million retirees, older Americans, and community activists, including 21,886 members in Mississippi.
In its October 9 endorsement letter, the Alliance cited Chiaradio’s commitment to strengthening and expanding Social Security and Medicare, protecting the programs from cuts or privatization, lowering prescription drug prices, improving access to affordable healthcare, and supporting pension security and quality long-term care.
The endorsement comes as proposals to raise the Social Security retirement age and reduce future benefits continue to generate debate in Washington.
Chiaradio has pledged never to vote to raise the Social Security retirement age or reduce benefits, emphasizing that protecting the retirement security Americans have earned must remain a federal priority.
The Alliance, led by President Robert Roach, Jr. and Executive Director Richard J.
Fiesta, stated that Chiaradio’s election would help improve the quality of life for older Americans.
Chiaradio expressed his gratitude to the Alliance for its endorsement and reaffirmed his commitment to protecting Social Security, Medicare, and access to affordable healthcare.
