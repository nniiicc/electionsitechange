Chiaradio is a farmer and entrepreneur who raises sheep, pigs, and cows in Shubuta, Mississippi.
Before starting his farm, Chiaradio earned an MBA from Felician University.

His campaign for the United States House of Representatives in 2026 is focused on intelligent public investment that will drive economic competitiveness in rural economies, reinvigorate American industry, and directly benefit the American people.
