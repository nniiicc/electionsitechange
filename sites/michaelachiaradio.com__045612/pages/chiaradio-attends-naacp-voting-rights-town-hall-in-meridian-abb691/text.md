Chiaradio attended the NAACP Voting Rights Town Hall, joining community members, organizers, and advocates committed to protecting voting rights across Mississippi.
The event drew a strong turnout and focused on the importance of civic engagement beyond Election Day.
Speakers and participants emphasized the need for ongoing involvement through volunteering, organizing, and building community and fellowship around the work of democracy.
Attendees discussed the importance of ensuring that voters understand their rights and the role they play in strengthening democratic participation at the local level.
Chiaradio thanked the NAACP and local organizers for their leadership and for creating space for meaningful conversations about participation, accountability, and community power.
The town hall served as a reminder that protecting democracy requires sustained engagement and investment in people, not just moments at the ballot box.
