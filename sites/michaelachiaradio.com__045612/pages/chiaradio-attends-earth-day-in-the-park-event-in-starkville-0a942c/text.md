Chiaradio attended the Earth Day in the Park event in Starkville last weekend, joining community members for a day of fellowship, discussion, and shared focus on the future of Mississippi.
During the event, Chiaradio spoke briefly about the importance of the 2026 midterm elections and the need to build lasting political infrastructure across the state.
He emphasized that long-term success in Mississippi will require sustained engagement beyond a single election cycle, with continued momentum into 2027 and beyond.
Chiaradio also highlighted the importance of community, noting that events like Earth Day in the Park play a critical role in bringing people together and strengthening the relationships needed to support meaningful progress.
In his remarks, he outlined his vision for a more sustainable and competitive economy, centered on environmentally responsible growth and the expansion of Mississippi’s agricultural value chain.
His approach focuses on low-input, capital-efficient farming practices that support small businesses while improving long-term economic resilience.
The event reflects the campaign’s continued commitment to engaging directly with communities across the district and building the foundation necessary to compete and win in future elections.
