- June 4, 2026
Shubuta, MS — Democratic nominee for Congress Michael A.
Chiaradio announced today that he has earned the endorsement of Mississippi Democratic Party Chairman and State Representative Cheikh Taylor.
Taylor currently serves as Chairman of the Mississippi Democratic Party and has represented Mississippi House District 38 since 2017.
A Starkville native and graduate of Howard University, Taylor has become one of the leading voices for Democratic organizing and party-building efforts across Mississippi.
As Chairman of the Mississippi Democratic Party, Taylor has played a major role in strengthening the party across the state while advocating for working people and expanded political participation.
Taylor’s endorsement comes as the Chiaradio campaign continues expanding its grassroots operation across Mississippi’s Third Congressional District with a focus on lowering healthcare costs, investing in infrastructure, strengthening public schools, and supporting local food systems and small businesses.
Mississippi’s Third Congressional District is competitive and can be flipped in November through strong grassroots organizing, voter outreach, and sustained community engagement.
