Chiaradio attended the monthly Pike County Democratic Party meeting, where he met with community members and discussed his campaign’s priorities for lowering costs and strengthening Mississippi’s economy.
Chiaradio outlined his focus on reducing healthcare costs, investing in infrastructure, and strengthening public education through increased funding and higher teacher pay.
He also emphasized the need for strategic investment in domestic production, particularly agriculture, to position Mississippi as a national leader in expanded export opportunities.
Chiaradio highlighted his vision for rebuilding Mississippi’s cities and towns, expanding economic opportunity, and ensuring young people can build their futures in the state.
He praised the strong engagement of Pike County Democrats and pledged continued collaboration to build momentum ahead of the 2026 election cycle.
