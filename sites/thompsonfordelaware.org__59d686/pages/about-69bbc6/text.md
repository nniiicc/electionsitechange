Thompson proudly lives on her family farm in Hartly, where she was raised in a multi-generational farming and small business family.
She learned the importance of faith, hard work, and personal responsibility — values that guide her today.
For nearly a decade, Emily has served Delaware families in state government, strengthening communities and supporting children.
She currently oversees programs that promote the healthy development and well-being of children and families.
A proud alumna of Wilmington University, she earned her undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral degrees there, completing a Doctorate in Prevention Science.
Emily is a committed advocate for agriculture and families, and will bring strong, compassionate leadership that listens, collaborates, and delivers results.
