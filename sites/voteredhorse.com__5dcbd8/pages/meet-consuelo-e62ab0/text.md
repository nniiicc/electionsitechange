My Roots ​ I'm a proud member of the Navajo Nation.
I was born in Boulder, raised in Longmont, attended college in Durango, and built my early career in Boulder before moving to Summit County in 2011 with my partner and our three-year-old daughter.
Colorado shaped my values.
Mountain life shaped my perspective.
I grew up camping, skiing, hiking, and fishing.
I spent school breaks with cousins and grandparents, grounded in family, responsibility, and community.
Showing up matters.
Relationships matter.
Representation matters. ​ Those principles guide how I lead.
Professional Experience in Small Business ​ ​ My career has been rooted in locally owned small businesses.
After working at an independent bookstore and as a licensed CPA specializing in nonprofit auditing, I joined a small property management company as an accountant.
Nearly twenty years later, I still support the owners and tenants of long-term apartment housing. ​ I understand budgets.
I understand accountability.
I see how housing policy and cost of living affects working class people and our communities.
Decisions made at the Capitol ripple through real communities through rent, wages, classrooms, and small business.
Everything is connected.
Proven Leadership Built in Mountain Communities ​ ​ My daughter grew up attending Summit County public schools and is now a high school senior.
Public education is personal to me because it strengthens entire communities and is foundational to opportunity in rural Colorado. ​ ​ ​ Elected to the Summit School District Board of Education in 2019 and re-elected in 2023, I now serve as President. ​ For six years, I've led in a high-cost, tourism-driven mountain community where housing pressures, workforce shortages, and funding challenges intersect daily with education policy.
