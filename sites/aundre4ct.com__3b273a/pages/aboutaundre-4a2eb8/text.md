After being the youngest person ever to be elected to the Connecticut House of Representatives, Aundré Bumgardner currently serves as a Groton Town Councilor and was re-elected in 2019 and again in 2021.
In the State Legislature, Aundré served on the Education, Transportation, and Finance, Revenue and Bonding Committees.
He is excited to continue to fight for Groton, Mystic, and Stonington as your next State Legislator.
Aundré’s platform includes Economic Recovery, Energy Affordability, Environmental Quality, Educational Excellence, and Ethics Reform.
