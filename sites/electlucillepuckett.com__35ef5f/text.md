A Representative Who Listens.
North Carolina House District 91 deserves representation that listens to the people who live here.
From Forsyth County to Stokes County, families are concerned about the cost of living, the future of our public schools, access to healthcare, safe communities, responsible growth, and whether their voices are truly being heard in Raleigh.
I am running to put people back at the center of public service.
I believe government should work for the people and not the other way around.
These are the priorities I will fight for as your representative.
STRENGTHENING PUBLIC EDUCATION Every child deserves a strong public education, regardless of their ZIP code.
I will fight for properly funded public schools, better support and pay for educators, and the resources our students need to succeed.
Public tax dollars should strengthen our public schools—not drain them to expand private-school voucher programs. ​ AFFORDABLE & ACCESSIBLE HEALTHCARE Quality healthcare should be accessible and affordable whether you live in Forsyth or rural Stokes County.
I will fight to protect healthcare access, strengthen rural healthcare, lower prescription and medical costs, and ensure families aren't forced to choose between their health and everyday necessities.
LOWER COSTS & ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY Families are feeling the pressure of higher costs for housing, groceries, utilities, childcare, and other necessities.
I will fight for good-paying jobs, support small businesses, expand economic opportunity, and make sure economic development benefits the people who live and work here, NOT just big corporations.
AFFORDABLE HOUSING Working families, seniors, veterans, and people on fixed incomes should be able to afford a safe place to call home.
I support expanding attainable housing, addressing homelessness with practical solutions, and helping ensure that people who work in our communities can afford to live in them.
SAFE COMMUNITIES I support the Second Amendment while recognizing our responsibility to address gun violence.
Having lost my own son to gun violence, I know the devastating impact it has on families.
I will support responsible gun ownership, violence prevention, youth opportunities, and safer communities while protecting constitutional rights.
RESPONSIBLE GROWTH Growth should benefit our communities and not overwhelm them.
Residents deserve a voice when developments such as data centers could affect our land, water, roads, utilities, and quality of life.
I will fight for transparency, responsible development, protect our natural resources, and meaningful community input.
PEOPLE BEFORE POLITICS Public office belongs to the people.
I will be an accessible and accountable representative who listens to District 91 and NOT just during election season, but every day.
Whether you are Democrat, Republican, or unaffiliated, your voice matters.
I will put the people of Forsyth and Stokes Counties before politics and powerful special interests.
