Meet Lucille
Lucille Puckett is a community-focused leader running for the North Carolina House of Representatives in District 91.
She is committed to putting working families first by strengthening public education, protecting local communities, and ensuring responsible economic development that benefits residents and not special interests.
Lucille believes in transparent government, fiscal responsibility, and policies that improve access to good jobs, safe neighborhoods, and quality healthcare.
Guided by integrity and service, she is running to be a strong, accountable voice for District 91 in Raleigh.
