Meet Michael Rutland
Michael Rutland has spent his life serving others.
"Families deserve practical leadership that listens, works hard, and delivers results."
— Michael Rutland
A life of service.
As a U.S.
Air Force veteran, former Boeing employee, Knights of Columbus leader, and Federal Way community member, Michael understands the value of hard work, personal responsibility, and service.
Throughout his career and community involvement, Michael has focused on bringing people together to solve problems and strengthen the community he calls home.
Today, he is running for State Senate because he believes families deserve practical leadership that listens, works hard, and delivers results.
