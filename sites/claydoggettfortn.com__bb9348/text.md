Rep.
Clay Doggett serves the people of Tennessee with a steadfast commitment to protecting constitutional freedoms, strengthening rural communities, and ensuring that the voices of everyday Tennesseans are heard in state government.
Born and raised in Giles County, he brings to the Legislature a deep understanding of the needs, values, and challenges faced by families, farmers, small-business owners, and local public servants across southern Middle Tennessee.
Before entering public office, Doggett built his life around service—first as a law enforcement officer and later as a small-business owner.
These experiences shaped his conviction that government must remain accountable to the people and that public policy should reflect real-world common sense.
His background informs his work on issues such as protecting constitutional rights, improving public safety, expanding economic opportunity, and defending local decision-making authority.
Doggett is widely recognized for his principled leadership in the General Assembly, where he has championed legislation to uphold the Second Amendment, reduce government overreach, and support Tennessee’s strong tradition of personal responsibility and individual liberty.
His approach blends a respect for the Constitution with a focus on practical, results-oriented policymaking that benefits both rural and suburban communities.
At the center of his life and work is his family.
Clay and his wife, Mary Lauren, make their home in Giles County, where they are raising their four children.
Their commitment to faith, family, and community guides Clay’s service and fuels his dedication to building a safe, prosperous future for the next generation of Tennesseans.
