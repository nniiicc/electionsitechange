There are 101 State House districts and 48 State Senate districts.
Districts are redrawn every 10 years according to total populations within each and lines move based upon the population of a given area.
Districts which are larger in area, are the results of low population to area ratio.

This must change change.
Each of the 101 house districts has only one Representative.
Each of the 48 Senate districts each has only one Senator.
The size and shape of each is governed by population and the Voting Rights act.
