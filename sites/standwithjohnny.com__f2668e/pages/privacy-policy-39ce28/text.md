This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information through standwithjohnny.com, our campaign forms, our email and text messaging programs, and related campaign activities that refer to this policy.
Our text messaging program is voluntary.
We do not sell personal information or disclose it to other organizations for their own marketing, fundraising, or promotional use.
Additional protections for mobile information and text messaging consent appear in Section 4.
1 Information we collect Information you provide: We collect information you choose to give us when you sign up for updates, volunteer, register for an event, complete a survey or petition, make a contribution, or communicate with us.
Depending on your interaction, this may include your name, email address, mailing address, telephone number, interests, volunteer availability, responses, and the content of your communications.
Contribution information: If you contribute, we may receive your contact information, contribution amount and date, transaction status, occupation, employer, and other information needed to process the contribution and comply with campaign finance requirements.
Online contributions linked from our website are processed through ActBlue.
Payment information you enter on a payment provider’s website is handled under that provider’s privacy policy.
Technical information: Our website and service providers may collect your IP address, browser and device information, referring pages, pages visited, access times, approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about your interaction with our website, emails, and message links.
Cookies and similar technologies may be used as described in Section 5.
Messaging records: When you enroll in or interact with our text program, we collect your mobile number, consent and preference records, messages you send us, and related information such as timestamps, delivery status, and opt-out requests.
Consent records may include the signup source and the disclosure presented when you enrolled.
Information from other sources: We may receive information from public records, including voter records, and from campaign service providers or people acting on our behalf.
We may combine that information with information you provide.
A number’s presence in a voter file, donor record, or another organization’s list does not establish consent to our recurring text program.
2 How we use information We use personal information to operate and improve the website; respond to questions; organize volunteers and events; process and record contributions; understand issues and activities that interest supporters; conduct lawful campaign outreach; maintain communication preferences; protect against fraud and misuse; and meet legal, accounting, and campaign finance obligations.
Where you have provided the required consent, we use your mobile number to send recurring campaign text messages about campaign news, events, volunteering, fundraising, and voting reminders.
We also use messaging records to deliver messages, respond to requests, document consent, and honor opt-outs.
We do not treat use of our website or acceptance of this Privacy Policy as consent to receive text messages.
3 When we disclose information Campaign personnel and service providers: We allow authorized campaign staff and volunteers to access information needed for their work.
We also use providers that assist with website hosting, data storage, communications, payment processing, campaign administration, analytics, security, and professional advice.
We limit access to the information needed for those functions.
Providers acting on our behalf must protect that information and may not use it for their own independent marketing or fundraising.
Required reporting and legal process: We disclose information when required by campaign finance law, a valid legal demand, or other applicable law.
Contribution reports may include a contributor’s name, address, occupation when required, and contribution amount and date.
Information filed with election authorities may become public.
We may also disclose information as reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect rights or safety, or respond to legal claims, subject to the additional restrictions in Section 4.
At your direction: We may disclose information when you specifically ask us to do so, such as when you authorize publication of a testimonial.
We do not interpret such a request as permission to transfer your text messaging consent.
We do not sell or rent personal information.
We do not provide supporter information to other candidates, campaigns, political parties, committees, advocacy organizations, or commercial partners for their own marketing, fundraising, or promotional use.
Section 4 controls whenever another provision could otherwise be read to permit disclosure of mobile information or text messaging consent.
4 Mobile information and text messaging privacy We will not sell, rent, trade, or disclose your mobile number, mobile messaging information, or SMS opt-in and consent records to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
This restriction includes other political campaigns, candidates, parties, committees, and organizations, as well as data brokers and lead generators.
Text messaging consent is specific to this Campaign and is not transferable.
Limited processing for our program is permitted.
We may provide the information necessary to operate our text program to messaging vendors, including Twilio, and to carriers and other providers that transmit messages, maintain consent and suppression records, provide program support, or perform messaging compliance functions.
These disclosures do not authorize a recipient to market to you independently or to use your consent for another sender.
We may also disclose mobile information or consent records if legally required.
These limited operational and legally required disclosures do not permit sale of SMS lists or disclosure for another organization’s marketing, fundraising, or promotional use.
The general disclosure provisions in Section 3 do not expand these exceptions.
Messages are recurring, and message frequency varies based on campaign activity and your interactions.
Message and data rates may apply.
You can unsubscribe by replying STOP to a campaign text.
We honor other clear requests to stop as well.
After you opt out, you may receive one message confirming the opt-out; we will not resume program texts unless you provide new consent.

Consent to receive texts is not required to make a contribution or purchase, volunteer, attend an event, or otherwise support the Campaign.

We keep consent and opt-out records to administer the program, respect your choices, and meet applicable recordkeeping obligations.
Additional program details appear in our SMS Terms and Conditions .
5 Cookies and similar technologies 6 Your choices and requests Text messages: Reply STOP to a campaign text to unsubscribe.
You may also contact us at (850) 910-2629 to request that we stop campaign texts.
You do not have to use particular wording when your request clearly communicates that you want the texts to stop.
Email and other outreach: Use the unsubscribe link in a campaign email or contact us to request a change in your communication preferences.
Tell us which channels you want to stop so we can apply your request accurately.
A request to stop texts will not require you to unsubscribe from unrelated communication channels.
Access, correction, and deletion: You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we maintain about you.
We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity before responding.
We will honor rights that apply under applicable law.
We may retain information needed for legal reporting, recordkeeping, fraud prevention, legal claims, and honoring an opt-out.
We cannot remove information from government records or third-party systems we do not control.
7 Retention We keep personal information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including applicable legal and campaign finance requirements.
The retention period depends on the information, its use, and legal obligations.
We retain consent evidence and limited suppression information as needed to demonstrate compliance and avoid contacting someone who has opted out.
When information is no longer needed, we delete it or make it no longer identifiable, subject to lawful retention and routine backup processes.
8 Security We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect personal information against unauthorized access, use, alteration, and disclosure.
Access is limited according to campaign responsibilities and service needs.
No website, storage system, or transmission method is completely secure.
Please do not send payment card details, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive information through ordinary email or text messages.
9 Children Our website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13.
If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.
Our recurring text messaging program is limited to people who are at least 18 years old.
10 Other websites and services Our website may link to ActBlue, social media platforms, and other third-party websites or services.
This policy governs information handled by the Campaign.
A third party’s own privacy policy governs information it collects directly.
Please review that policy before providing information on its service.
Choosing to interact with another organization does not transfer your consent to receive texts from the Campaign.
11 Changes to this policy We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal obligations.
We will post the revised policy with an updated date and provide any additional notice required by law.
A policy update does not by itself give us new text messaging consent, authorize a new sender, or override a prior opt-out.
We will obtain additional consent when required before making a change that requires it.
