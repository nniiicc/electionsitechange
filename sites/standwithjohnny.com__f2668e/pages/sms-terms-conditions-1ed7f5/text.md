Please review these terms before enrolling.
1 Program description Subscribers may receive SMS and MMS messages about Johnny Austin Thompson’s campaign for Florida House District 2, including campaign updates, event announcements, volunteer opportunities, fundraising requests, and voting reminders.
We may use automated technology to send messages.
Messages may include links to campaign resources or contribution pages.
2 Enrollment and consent You enroll by entering your mobile number in a campaign signup form and submitting the form after reviewing the text messaging disclosure presented with it.
By doing so, you authorize the Campaign to send the recurring texts described in that disclosure to the number you provide.
You must be at least 18 and the subscriber or authorized user of that number.
Text messaging consent is optional and is not a condition of any purchase, contribution, volunteer activity, event attendance, or other campaign participation.
Providing a telephone number for another purpose, making a contribution, or accepting general website terms does not enroll you in recurring campaign texts.
Your consent applies only to this Campaign and the subjects disclosed when you enroll.
3 Frequency and charges Message frequency varies with campaign activity and your interactions.
Message and data rates may apply to messages you receive and send, according to your wireless plan.
The Campaign does not charge a separate subscription fee for the text program.
Contact your wireless provider with questions about your plan or charges.
4 How to stop messages Reply STOP to a campaign text at any time to unsubscribe.
We also honor other clear requests to stop, including requests made by calling (850) 910-2629.
After you opt out, you may receive one confirmation text.
We will stop further program messages unless you provide new consent to re-enroll.
You can re-enroll through the campaign’s text signup form.
If your number changes or you are no longer authorized to use it, please unsubscribe or contact us so we can update our records.
5 Help and support Reply HELP to a campaign text for assistance, or call (850) 910-2629.
The HELP function provides program support information; it does not withdraw your consent.
Use STOP or make another clear opt-out request to unsubscribe.
6 Delivery and carrier availability The program is available to compatible U.S. mobile numbers on participating wireless networks.
Availability and delivery depend on your device, carrier, and network conditions.
Delivery is not guaranteed.
Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
If you need official information about voting deadlines, eligibility, or polling locations, consult the appropriate election authority.

We do not sell or rent SMS subscriber data or provide it to affiliates or third parties for their marketing, fundraising, or promotional use.
Messaging providers may process the information needed to operate our program, and legally required disclosures may occur, as explained in our Privacy Policy .
8 Program changes We may change or end the program and may update these terms.
Updated terms will be posted with a revised date, and we will give additional notice when required.
Changes will not enroll you in a different sender’s program, revive a withdrawn consent, or expand your consent to new message subjects without any additional consent that is required.
