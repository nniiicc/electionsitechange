MEET JOHNNY Johnny Austin Thompson is the Democratic nominee selected to represent district 2.
Born and raised in Pensacola, Johnny understands the challenges facing Northwest Florida because he’s lived them.
From skyrocketing insurance rates to failing healthcare access to classrooms stretched thin, families across the district are being left behind.
Johnny is running to change that.
DELIVERING REAL SOLUTIONS Johnny is focused on practical, people-first solutions that strengthen our community, protect our coast, and ensure every family has a fair chance to succeed.
For too long, Northwest Florida has been overlooked by leaders more focused on culture wars than real problems.
Insurance rates keep rising, our health outcomes rank among the worst in the state, and working families are stretched thin.
Johnny’s priorities reflect a different approach: common-sense solutions that strengthen our community, protect our future, and restore honest leadership.
