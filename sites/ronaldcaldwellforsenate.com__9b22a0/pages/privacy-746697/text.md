Ronald Caldwell for State Senate (“we,” “our,” or “the campaign”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
Information We Collect
When you interact with our website, we may collect:
- Your name
- Email address
- Mobile phone number
- Zip code
- Information you voluntarily provide through forms, sign-ups, or donations
We may also collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited for analytics and security purposes.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information you provide to:
- Communicate with you about the campaign
- Send updates, alerts, and voting reminders
- Respond to inquiries
- Comply with campaign finance and election laws
- Improve our website and outreach efforts
We do not sell your personal information.
Text Messaging & Email Communications
If you provide your mobile number, you consent to receive campaign-related text messages, including updates and reminders.
Message frequency may vary.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
You may unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link provided in each message.
Message and data rates may apply.
Sharing of Information
We may share your information with trusted vendors who assist with campaign operations (such as email delivery, texting platforms, website hosting, or compliance services).
These vendors are required to use your information only to provide services to the campaign and to protect it appropriately.
We may also disclose information if required by law.
No mobile information will be shared by third parties with us or by us with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with us or by us with any third parties.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
