He graduated from Saginaw Valley State University with a degree in Business Management.
Timmy is the proud father of two daughters and one son, all of which attend Bangor Public Schools.
He and his wife, Lisa have been married for 20 years.
Before serving Bay County in Lansing, Timmy served as a School Board Member for Bangor Township Schools.
When Timmy is not grilling up delicious BBQ he can be found golfing, camping, or hunting with family and friends.
