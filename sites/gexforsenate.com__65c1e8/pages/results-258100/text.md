Since returning to the Senate in 2023, I've supported tax relief, sponsored legislation that became law and voted for investments in transportation, education and public services.
Lowering Kentucky's income tax I voted for House Bill 1 in 2023 and House Bill 1 in 2025.
The 2023 legislation established a 4.5 percent individual income-tax rate for 2023 and a 4 percent rate beginning in 2024.
The 2025 legislation reduced the rate to 3.5 percent beginning Jan.
1, 2026.
Lower income taxes let taxpayers keep more of what they earn.
I support continuing the work toward eliminating Kentucky's individual income tax.

I also voted for legislation improving career and technical education funding and allowing qualifying dual-credit internship and cooperative training hours to count toward certain trade licenses.
My budget votes supported statewide funding for career and technical education, Work Ready Kentucky Scholarships and Dual Credit Scholarships.
I also supported establishing the Healthcare Workforce Investment Fund to help prepare more people for healthcare careers.

The law allows the Public Service Commission, at an electric utility's request, to extend the period over which fuel-adjustment costs are recovered.
That gives the commission a tool to reduce volatility in customers' bills.
Spreading recovery over time does not eliminate the underlying cost.
Read Senate Bill 172 (opens in a new tab) Changing Public Service Commission oversight to better support ratepayers I supported Senate Bill 8 in 2026.
The law expands the Public Service Commission from three members to five and changes requirements for commissioner qualifications, staffing and operations to allow the commission greater ability to oversee and study state utility actions.

Franklin County: Singing Bridge I worked alongside Rep.
Erika Hancock, Rep.
Dan Fister and other partners to advance funding for the Singing Bridge replacement.
The enacted highway plan lists $12.17 million across right-of-way, utility and construction phases in fiscal years 2026 through 2028.

Boone County: water rescue infrastructure House Bill 900 includes $4.5 million in fiscal year 2027–28 for a Boone County Water Rescue river station.
Carroll County: US 42 bridge work The enacted highway plan programs approximately $31.15 million in fiscal year 2026 for utility work and construction on the US Highway 42 Kentucky River bridge project.
Gallatin County: I-71 improvements The highway plan includes $10 million for construction in fiscal year 2027 on the I-71 project from US Highway 127 to mile point 64.
Owen County: road resurfacing The plan includes approximately $1.96 million combined for resurfacing projects on KY Highway 1982, KY Highway 3548 and KY 325 in fiscal years 2027 and 2028.
Kenton County: infrastructure and economic development House Bill 900 includes $7 million across fiscal years 2026–27 and 2027–28 for Kenton County infrastructure and economic development.
These include countywide and regional investments.
