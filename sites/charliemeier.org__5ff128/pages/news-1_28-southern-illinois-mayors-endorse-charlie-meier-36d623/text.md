The local leaders note Charlie’s leadership, experience, and dedication to the people of Southern Illinois as their top reasons to send him back to Springfield.
“Charlie Meier is exactly who we need fighting for us in Springfield,” said Kevin Timmermann, Mayor of Breese.
“Charlie always picks up the phone, listens, and stands up for our communities.
Now, more than ever, we need a strong, experienced Conservative fighter who knows how to get stuff done - and that’s Charlie Meier.” Charlie Meier’s local mayors also point to his constant presence in their communities and commitment to showing up for every town he represents.
“Charlie Meier works tirelessly for our community,” said Highland Mayor Kevin Hemann.
“He truly listens, cares deeply, and makes himself available to the people he represents.
His commitment to serving others sets him apart.” “I’m truly humbled by the support from so many mayors throughout Southern Illinois,” said Charlie Meier.
“These mayors are the boots on the ground serving their communities every day.
They know the challenges we face, and they know that they can count on me to fight for them every single day.
