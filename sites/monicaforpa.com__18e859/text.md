Monica Weninger is a science educator, Township Auditor, wife, mother, and longtime Bucks County neighbor.
Her family’s story taught her that hard work should still lead to opportunity — but today, too many families are being squeezed by rising costs, higher property taxes, and a government that is not working efficiently enough for them.
Monica is running to bring practical, accountable leadership to Harrisburg: lower property taxes, responsible spending, safe and stable communities, and decisions guided by evidence instead of politics.
