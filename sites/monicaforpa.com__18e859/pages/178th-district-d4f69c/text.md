The communities The towns of the 178th District 178 — Warwick, Northampton, Upper Southampton, Wrightstown — represents the heart of Bucks County.
Diverse towns with one shared need: a representative who actually delivers for families, not one bought by energy companies and lobbyists.
Warwick Township Home to historic Jamison and a close-knit mix of families, farms, and small businesses in the Central Bucks School District.
Famous for housing General George Washington’s headquarters at the historic Moland House Northampton Township One of the district’s largest communities, with strong public schools, active parks, and engaged neighbors.
Northampton Township includes the communities of Richboro, Churchville, and Holland.
Upper Southampton Township Covers 6.6 square miles, operates under the Centennial School District (William Tennent High School), and balances suburban neighborhoods with local parks and small-town character.
Wrightstown Township A rural, charming and close-knit community nestled in the heart of Bucks County that prizes open spaces.
Wrightstown Township is composed of five villages: Penns Park, Pineville, Rushland, Wrightstown, and Wycombe.
