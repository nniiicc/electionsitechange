That includes people of every ability.
We mean it, and we're working at it.
"For ALL the people" isn't just our slogan — it's how we want this website to work, too.
We're committed to making this site usable for everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, captions, or other assistive technology.
Our commitment We are a small, grassroots campaign team doing our best, and we are actively working toward conforming with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA — the widely recognized standard for web accessibility.
We may not be perfect yet, but we're committed to getting better, and we take this seriously.
What we're doing As we build and improve this site, we work to use clear, readable text with strong color contrast; make the site navigable by keyboard, not just a mouse; structure pages with proper headings so screen readers can make sense of them; write descriptive text for images and links; design for mobile and a range of screen sizes; and keep language plain and easy to follow.
