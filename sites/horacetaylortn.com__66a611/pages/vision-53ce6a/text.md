Our most urgent crisis.
Tariffs hit West Tennessee farmers with devastating force, and farming here is generational legacy — not a business line.
I will fight for relief on Day One.
Not someday.
Day One.
- Immediate tariff relief for West Tennessee farms
- Farm-focused federal policy and crop protection
- Protect generational family farms from collapse
- Restore stable export markets for our growers
Having lost my mother to cancer at six, and my first wife at 45, healthcare is not a talking point.
West Tennessee families deserve accessible, affordable care — and protection from being devastated by medical costs.
- Protect and strengthen rural hospitals and clinics
- Lower prescription drug costs for seniors and families
- Expand cancer prevention, screening, and treatment access
- Protect Medicare and Medicaid from cuts
Our service members and veterans have earned every benefit, every protection, and every resource this nation can provide.
I will be their advocate in Congress — not someone who salutes the flag on Veterans Day and forgets them on budget day.
- Full funding for VA healthcare and benefits
- Reduce VA wait times and expand mental health services
- Protect veteran pensions and earned benefits
- Support military families and veteran-owned businesses

That means being present in every county.
