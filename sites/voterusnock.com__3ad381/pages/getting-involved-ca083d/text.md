Since taking office, Representative Jacklyn Rusnock has been a strong advocate for the Muhlenberg Fire Department, working to ensure first responders have the resources they need to keep the community safe.
Through her support of state funding initiatives and local partnerships, she has helped secure critical equipment upgrades and improve access to training opportunities for firefighters.
Her commitment to public safety is reflected in her ongoing collaboration with department leadership, always prioritizing the well-being of both emergency personnel and the residents they serve.
