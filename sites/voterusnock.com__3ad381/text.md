Jacklyn Rusnock for State Representative
of the 126th Legislative District
I'm Jacklyn Rusnock, and I'm running to represent the 126th District in Harrisburg because I believe our community deserves a champion who will fight for our shared values and a brighter future for all.
Rooted in Experience, Driven by Passion:
My journey began right here in the 126th District, surrounded by a family dedicated to service: nurses, doctors, and teachers who instilled in me a deep commitment to healthcare, education, and community.
This foundation shaped my career path, leading me to:
· 20 years as a registered dental hygienist: Witnessing firsthand the impact of healthcare access fueled my passion for ensuring quality care for everyone.
· 10 years as a high school dental instructor: Building a successful dental program from the ground up taught me the value of empowering youth and investing in education.
· Service on the Muhlenberg School Board: Fighting for fair funding and mental health resources for our students and staff solidified my belief in strong public education systems.
Leading with Vision, Focused on Solutions:
As your representative, I bring not only experience but also a clear vision for our district's future.
I'm dedicated to:
· Making healthcare accessible and affordable: Everyone deserves quality healthcare, regardless of income or background.
I'll fight for policies that expand access and lower costs.
· Investing in education for all: Our children deserve excellent, well-funded schools with resources to support their success.
I'll advocate for increased funding and equitable opportunities.
· Combating climate change for a sustainable future: We must act now to protect our planet and ensure a healthy future for generations to come.
I'll support policies that address climate change head-on.
· Protecting marriage equality and women's reproductive rights: Every individual deserves the right to make their own choices about their lives and families.
I'll stand up for equality and women's reproductive rights.
Building a Community Where Everyone Thrives:
The 126th District is my home, and its residents are my family.
I believe in working together to build a vibrant community where everyone feels safe, supported, and empowered to reach their full potential.
I am deeply committed to serving the community.
My diverse experience and passion for healthcare and education make me uniquely qualified to be your voice in Harrisburg.
Join me in this journey.
Let's build a future where healthcare is accessible, education is top-notch, our planet is protected, and all families are valued and respected.
Together, we can make the 126th District a place where everyone thrives.
Thank you for your time and consideration.
I look forward to earning your vote and representing our community in Harrisburg.
