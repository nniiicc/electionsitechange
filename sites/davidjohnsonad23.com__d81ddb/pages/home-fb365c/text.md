Meet Dave Johnson – The Fighter Silicon Valley Needs Dave Johnson is a proud, lifelong native of Santa Clara County who has built his life and career right here in the South Bay.
For 40 years , he’s been a hands-on leader in the commercial construction industry — from marketing and estimating to project management on major design-build projects for high-tech companies.
As a small business owner (running two successful firms since 1994 and 2010), Dave knows firsthand the crushing burden of Sacramento’s endless taxes, skyrocketing regulations, and anti-business policies that are driving up costs and chasing jobs away.
But Dave isn’t just a builder — he’s a bold voice for common sense.
He’s an active writer whose sharp articles cut through the mainstream media spin, making readers question the narratives they’re being fed.
Now serving as Chairman of the Santa Clara County Republican Party , Dave has been a committed conservative his entire adult life.
He’s stepping up to run for State Assembly because he’s fed up with the status quo — and he’s ready to fight.
In Sacramento, Dave will battle to stop the endless tax-and-spend cycle , slash job-killing regulations, and champion Parents’ Rights and School Choice so families — not bureaucrats — control education.
No fluff.

Just a proven local leader with real-world experience who shows up, speaks out, and gets results.
