Schools & Parent Rights A parent has a right to know if their child is considering transitioning, and the Supreme Court stated last June that teachers are obligated to inform the parents.
Plus, I believe that parents should have an "opt out" option to not have to expose their children to questionable or offensive material in the classroom.
Starting this summer, parents will have an opportunity to sign an "Opt-Out" form, which prevents their child from having to sit through "Drag Queen Story Hour" or any other offensive class or program.
The schools will be forced to create alternative curriculum for these students, and this could turn the education system on its head, as they may face having to create another class for all of these people.
This is one way to create the change that is so sorely needed in our Schools.

With famous YouTube Journalist Nick Shirley Voter Integrity / Voter ID We need a voting system that we can trust.
Right now, less than half of the people believe that we truly have fair elections.
Mail in voting is part of the problem.
Yes, it is convenient, but your ballot passes through too many hands to ensure that it is not mistreated.
First and foremost, I believe that Voter ID is a good start.
Non-citizens should not be able to vote.
It seems fair and reasonable that one has to show ID as a condition of voting.
We show ID for everything else, why not voting, which is more important, arguably, than buying a 6-pack of beer.
Second, I believe that we should have one-day voting, in person with SAME DAY RESULTS.
Mail-in voting is not an effective way to handle elections.
Most third world countries have one-day, in person voting.
Results are in by nightfall.
There are bills being proposed in Sacramento to make mail-in voting permanent and to provide MORE, not less time to vote.
This process, while convenient, is ripe with opportunities to mishandle, lose or otherwise swing the vote.
Did You Know: The "typical" mail-in ballot passes through 19 sets of hands before it is counted?
That's crazy!
If there is a breakdown in "curing" that ballot, it be "lost" or miscounted.
Same-day voting where a person turns in their ballot at the polling center and scans it in them self is the best way to make sure your vote COUNTS!
Please do not be fooled by career politicians telling you otherwise...
I will fight for voter integrity and same day voting when I am in Sacramento.
