Quintard Taylor Team Jamila May 15, 2020 1 min read Dr.
Quintard Taylor, founder of BlackPast.org , is giving a virtual lecture via Zoom.
This will be a truly unique campaign event that you won’t want to miss (and I’m not just saying that because he’s my dad!) Titled “Politics and Protest: Exploring the History of African Americans in Washington Politics since 1890 .” This will be a special opportunity to learn about our history in Washington state.
And it’s especially timely as I put my name on the ballot this year.
Join us on Thursday, May 28th, 2020 at 6:00pm on Zoom.
To join us as a co-host or to RSVP contact Madison@bobmanconsulting.com for more information.
