Neighbor to Neighbor: Youth & Politics Panel
Campaign to Elect Jamila Taylor Presents the Neighbor to Neighbor Series.
A virtual conversation series to move our whole community forward.
Episode 1 on June 10, 2020 – Youth Panel on activism, civic engagement and social justice.
Teenagers from South King County shared their thoughts about activism, civic engagement, social justice and some of the key issues important to youth in our community.
Guest panelists are: Waylon Menzia, Joshua Johnson, Alisha Saucedo and Mantak Singh.
* All opinions expressed by the guests are their own. *
