Meet Congressman Adrian Smith.
As a sixth generation Nebraskan, Adrian Smith has made a difference, bringing conservative Nebraska common sense to Congress.
Adrian has consistently been ranked as one of the most conservative members of Congress.
Adrian serves on the Ways & Means Committee, giving Nebraskans a voice on issues important to families, farmers and main street including tax reform and tax cuts, trade agreements, Social Security, and Medicare.
He helped write and pass the most sweeping tax reform since Ronald Reagan was President.
Adrian believes America lost control of the Southern Border during the Biden Administration, resulting in record illegal immigration.
Adrian is working with President Trump to return to the border policies that were working before being recklessly reversed by the Biden administration.
He voted for President Trump’s plan to build the wall and give the U.S.
Border Patrol new resources needed to secure our borders and stop the inflow of drugs and criminals.
Adrian believes life begins at conception and ends at natural death.
He supports and promotes adoption as an alternative to abortion and is unwavering in his support for pro-life legislation and opposition to taxpayer funding for abortions.
Expanding fair trade is critical for Nebraska’s farmers, ranchers and manufacturers.
Whether in our nation’s capital, around the world, or at home in Nebraska, Adrian is working alongside President Trump for fair market access for Nebraska beef, pork, grains and manufactured goods.
Adrian supports our Second Amendment rights and opposes attempts to impose new gun control measures on the American people.
Adrian Smith earned his bachelors degree from the University of Nebraska-Lincoln, and served his community before his election to Congress.
Adrian, his wife Andrea, and their two children reside in Gering.
