About TX-CD38

Notable Locations in District 38
Population
The population of CD38 was 783,719 in 2021, and makes up 16.5% of the total population of Harris County.
Located entirely within Harris County, CD38 contains all or some of the following municipalities:
- Houston
- Jersey Village
- Tomball
- Bunker Hill Village
- Hilshire Village
- Hedwig Village
- Hunters Creek Village
- Piney Point Village
- Spring Valley
Schools
CD38 includes 7 school districts:
- Cy-Fair ISD
- Tomball ISD
- Klein ISD
- Spring Branch ISD
- Katy ISD
- Houston ISD
- Waller ISD
Also included in CD38 are Lone Star College (University Park and Tomball campuses), Houston Community College (Spring Branch campus), and Strayer University.
Harris County Precincts
Harris County Commissioners Court Precincts split up how Harris County manages public safety, transportation, flood management, parks, housing, and more.
The majority of CD38 is made up of Precinct 3 and Precinct 4, with a very small section of Precinct 1.
