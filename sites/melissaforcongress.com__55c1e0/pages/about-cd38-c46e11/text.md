Dozens of energy companies operate in this area, including BP, Shell, Citgo, ConocoPhillips, and Wood Group.
With over 56,000 jobs, it is the second largest employment center in the region.
Memorial The Memorial area is home to over 47,000 residents, most of which are zoned to Spring Branch Independent School District.
Some major landmarks in this area are Memorial City Mall, Memorial Hermann Medical Center, and CityCentre.
Tomball Located in northwest Harris County, the city of Tomball is home to over 12,000 residents, including Melissa McDonough.
It is named after Democratic Congressman Thomas Ball.
The historic Train Depot is located in the heart of downtown Tomball.
Population The population of CD38 was 783,719 in 2021, and makes up 16.5% of the total population of Harris County.
Located entirely within Harris County, CD38 contains all or some of the following municipalities: Houston Jersey Village Tomball Bunker Hill Village Hilshire Village Hedwig Village Hunters Creek Village Piney Point Village Spring Valley Schools CD38 includes 7 school districts: Cy-Fair ISD Tomball ISD Klein ISD Spring Branch ISD Katy ISD Houston ISD Waller ISD Also included in CD38 are Lone Star College (University Park and Tomball campuses), Houston Community College (Spring Branch campus), and Strayer University.
Harris County Precincts Harris County Commissioners Court Precincts split up how Harris County manages public safety, transportation, flood management, parks, housing, and more.
The majority of CD38 is made up of Precinct 3 and Precinct 4, with a very small section of Precinct 1.
