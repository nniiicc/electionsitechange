Cookie Policy
This page provides comprehensive information about how we use cookies on our website to enhance your browsing experience, improve website performance, and deliver personalized content.
Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit our site.
They help us understand how visitors interact with our website, allowing us to offer a smoother and more efficient user experience.
In the table below, you will find detailed information about each type of cookie we use, their purpose, and how long they remain on your device.
We are committed to respecting your privacy and providing transparency about the data we collect through cookies.
For more information on how we handle your personal data, please see our Privacy Policy.
