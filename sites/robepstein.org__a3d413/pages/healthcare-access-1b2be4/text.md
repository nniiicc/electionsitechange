Too many Georgians struggle to afford quality healthcare.
Rural hospitals are closing, insurance costs are rising, and many families are forced to choose between medical care and other necessities.
Gaps in coverage and access persist, especially in underserved communities.
Where Can We Go?
We can build a Georgia where everyone has access to affordable, high-quality healthcare—no matter their income or zip code.
By expanding coverage, investing in providers, and lowering costs, we can ensure healthier families and stronger communities across our state.
How Do We Get There?
