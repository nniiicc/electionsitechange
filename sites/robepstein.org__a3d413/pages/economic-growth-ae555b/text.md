Georgia’s economy is strong in many areas, but too many families and small businesses are being left behind.
Wages have not kept up with the cost of living, and rural communities often lack the investment and infrastructure needed to thrive.
Small businesses face barriers to growth, and economic opportunity is not shared equally across our state.
Where Can We Go?
We can build a Georgia where every community—urban and rural—has the tools to prosper.
By supporting local entrepreneurs, investing in infrastructure, and fostering innovation, we can create good jobs, grow our middle class, and ensure prosperity is within reach for all Georgians.
How Do We Get There?
