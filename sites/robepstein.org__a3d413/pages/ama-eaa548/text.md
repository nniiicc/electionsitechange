Who are you?
I'm a retired software leader seeking to redefine my purpose through public service, guided by my Christian faith—which emphasizes love, compassion, and service to others.
As a Lego enthusiast and father of two amazing sons, Blake (21) and Bryce (18), family is at the heart of everything I do.
For 20 years, I lived in Woodstock, where my wife and I raised our family until she passed away in May of 2025 after a four-year battle with bile duct cancer.
In August of 2025, I moved to the River Green community in Canton to be closer to my sons and begin a new chapter of my life.
Why did you decide to run?
I decided to run for the Georgia House of Representatives because I care deeply about the people of House District 23—our neighborhoods in Canton, River Green, Bridgemill, and the surrounding Cherokee County communities.
Living here, I see firsthand the challenges and opportunities facing our families, from the need for better access to Northside Hospital Cherokee and quality healthcare, to supporting our local schools like Knox Elementary and Cherokee High.
My Christian faith guides me to serve others and work for fairness, compassion, and opportunity for everyone.
I want to help strengthen our local economy so small businesses in downtown Canton and throughout the district can thrive, and make sure our democracy truly reflects the voices of the people—not just the powerful few.
What issues matter most to you?
The issues that matter most to me are the ones that touch people’s everyday lives right here in House District 23: Canton, River Green, Bridgemill, and our surrounding Cherokee County communities.
I care deeply about making healthcare more accessible and affordable, so families in neighborhoods like Great Sky and Laurel Canyon never have to choose between getting well and paying their bills.
I want to expand economic opportunity so every Georgian, whether you’re running a small business in downtown Canton or working in our local schools like Teasley Middle and Canton Elementary, has a fair shot at success.
And I believe we must strengthen our democracy to ensure government works for the many, not just the well-connected few.
At the heart of it all, I want to help build a Georgia where every person in our district and state has the chance to live with dignity, hope, and opportunity.
How will you stay in touch?
Staying in touch starts with being accessible and listening.
I plan to hold regular town halls in Canton, River Green, Bridgemill, and other neighborhoods across House District 23.
I’ll meet with community groups like the River Green HOA, Bridgemill Civic Association, and local school PTAs, and keep open lines of communication through social media, email updates, and neighborhood visits.
I want my neighbors to feel comfortable reaching out anytime with an idea, concern, or question, whether you’re a parent at Knox Elementary, a small business owner in downtown Canton, or hitting up Publix outside in Laurel Canyon.
Serving well means staying connected, showing up, listening, and being responsive to the people who trust me to represent them.
What shapes your leadership style?
My leadership style is shaped by my faith, my family, and my belief in listening before leading.
I’ve learned that good leadership means serving others with humility, building trust through honesty, and always keeping people first.
I try to approach every decision with compassion and common sense, bringing people together instead of dividing them.
My goal is to lead in a way that reflects the values I live by—faith, fairness, and a genuine desire to make life better for everyone in our community.
