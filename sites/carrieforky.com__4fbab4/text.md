Local priorities.
A plan to deliver for every family and neighborhood.
Safe Neighborhoods Our families deserve to feel safe and supported.
I will work to fully fund LMPD, increase patrols in neglected areas, and build stronger partnerships with community programs to prevent crime before it starts.
I will lead efforts to redesign dangerous intersections like Dixie & Greenwood where accidents happen too often and expand youth engagement programs to keep kids on the right path.
Better Schools As a teacher and mom, I know our schools are the foundation of our neighborhoods.
I will demand our fair share of state funding to modernize schools like Iroquois High, support competitive teacher pay to reduce turnover, and hold JCPS accountable for real results.
Our kids deserve a first-class education—no matter their zip code.
Good Jobs & Lower Costs South End families work hard and deserve a fair shot.
I will cut red tape that slows down local small businesses, expand skilled apprenticeship programs so students and workers can get good-paying jobs, and fight for tax relief and lower utility bills to ease the pressure on working families.
Infrastructure That Works Potholes, flooding, and crumbling sidewalks aren't acceptable anymore.
I will secure funding to rebuild our roads, improve storm drainage in neighborhoods like Beechmont and Fairdale, and expand reliable broadband access so our families and businesses stay connected.
It's time to put the South End's infrastructure first—no more backseat treatment.
