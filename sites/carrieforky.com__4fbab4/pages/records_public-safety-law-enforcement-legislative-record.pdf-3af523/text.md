Kentucky General Assembly records show that Roarx filed amendments to the bill.
Among the identified amendments were proposals to remove an enhancedsentencing provision involving three violent-offense convictions and provisions concerning criminal mischief.
Because HB 5 addressed numerous criminal-justice subjects, these amendments are presented as positions on specific provisions of the bill.
Kentucky State Police Retention Proposal In 2024, Roarx filed House Floor Amendment 5 to HB 1, proposing $18 million for one-time Kentucky State Police retention bonuses of $1,000 for each year of service for specified personnel.
The amendment was not adopted.
Although HB 1 ultimately became law, Roarx’s proposed $18 million KSP retention provision did not.
Legislative Outcome Summary Of the nine identified public-safety and first-responder bills listed in this review, none became law.
HB 34, which Roarx co-sponsored in 2026, passed the Kentucky House but did not complete the legislative process.
This finding is limited to the measures identified in this public-safety review and should not be interpreted to mean that no legislation sponsored by Roarx on other subjects has become law.
