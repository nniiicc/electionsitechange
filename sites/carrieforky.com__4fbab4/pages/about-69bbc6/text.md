A Voice for South End Families.
Carrie McKeehan is a lifelong South End resident, teacher, mom, and committed community advocate.
She's not a career politician—she's one of us.
She knows what it's like to raise a family here, work in our schools, worry about rising costs, and see neighborhoods overlooked for too long.
She's running for State Representative because the South End deserves better than empty promises.
"She's ready to fight for safer neighborhoods, better schools, good jobs, and a government that actually works for us." ROOTED IN THE SOUTH END The place she calls home—and always will.
Carrie isn't just running in the South End—she belongs to the South End.
Born and raised in this community, she grew up understanding the hopes and struggles of working families in our neighborhoods.
She went to local public schools, attended church here, and chose to stay here to raise her own family.
The South End isn't just where Carrie lives.
It's who she is.
A CAREER IN EDUCATION Over 20 years helping kids learn and grow.
For over two decades, Carrie has dedicated her career to public education in Jefferson County Public Schools.
As a teacher, she knows what our students face every day.
She's seen the challenges of overcrowded classrooms, underfunded schools, and teacher shortages.
She's watched parents worry about their children's safety and success.
Carrie believes every child in the South End deserves a world-class education—not failing infrastructure, not outdated materials, and not political excuses.
"That's why she's fought for students, parents, and teachers for over 20 years—and why she'll fight for them in Frankfort." A MOM WHO GETS IT Putting family values first—because she lives them.
She knows what it's like to worry about neighborhood crime, rising utility bills, and making ends meet.
She understands the importance of safe streets, strong schools, and a local economy that supports working families.
She isn't running to climb the political ladder—she's running to make life better for families like hers.
Carrie will always put people over politics.
Every family.
Every neighborhood.
Every future.
COMMUNITY SERVICE & LEADERSHIP Service is in her DNA.
Carrie isn't new to serving others.
She's volunteered countless hours in local schools, coached youth programs, and built lasting relationships with neighbors, parents, teachers, police officers, and small business owners.
She's the person people call when they need help—because they know she'll listen, work hard, and never give up on them.
WHY SHE'S RUNNING South Louisville Deserves Better.
For too long, South Louisville has been treated like an afterthought in Frankfort.
Our neighborhoods see neglected roads, failing infrastructure, and rising crime while other areas get the investment and attention they need.
Carrie is running to change that.
Fully fund local police and improve safety on every street.
Demand our fair share of funding for better schools.
Fight for lower costs, lower utility bills, and better-paying jobs.
Cut red tape that holds back local small businesses.
Hold government accountable to us—not special interests.
"I'm not a career politician.
