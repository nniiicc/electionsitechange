Status current through August 19, 2026.
Executive Summary Key finding: Most education measures appearing on Rep.
Rachel Roarx’s sponsor page list her as a cosponsor, not the primary sponsor.
The Kentucky General Assembly marks primary sponsorship with an asterisk; the education-related measures below generally do not carry that mark for Roarx.
She did, however, personally file education-related floor amendments in 2024 and 2025, and she is a co-sponsor on several measures that became law.
Passed / enacted education measures co-sponsored by Roarx include: 2024 HB 446 (public school transportation), HB 447 (student transportation), and HB 695 (Adaptive Kindergarten Readiness pilot).
Measures that stalled or failed include: 2023 HB 88 (education funding initiatives), 2023 HB 203 (history of racism curriculum), 2024 HB 231 (postsecondary tuition protections), 2024 HB 381 (student representatives on school boards), 2024 HB 587 (repeal of public charter-school statutes), 2025 HB 457 (child-labor-law display in schools), and multiple 2026 education bills that did not complete passage.
How to Read This Record Primary sponsor / filed bill: Roarx is listed first or with an asterisk on the official sponsor page.
Co-sponsor: Roarx signed onto legislation introduced by another member.
Amendment sponsor: Roarx personally filed a change to another legislator’s bill.
Passed House only: The measure cleared the Kentucky House but did not complete the full process into law.
Enacted: The bill was signed into law (or otherwise became law).

What the Record Shows ● Roarx’s education record is more heavily weighted toward co-sponsorship than primary sponsorship.
The official sponsor page uses an asterisk to identify primary sponsorship, and the education bills listed above generally do not show Roarx as the primary sponsor. ● Her clearest enacted education co-sponsorships in the 2023–2026 period are transportation and kindergarten-readiness measures from the 2024 session. ● She supported proposals for increased education funding, universal/expanded preschool, teacherrelated supports, tuition/KEES affordability, student representation, special-education caseload limits, and IEP-family resources; several of those proposals stalled in committee. ● Roarx personally filed at least two notable education-related floor amendments in this period.
The 2024 HB 304 amendment did not become law because the bill stalled; the 2025 HB 276 amendment was explicitly defeated on the House floor. ● This document summarizes education-related sponsored legislation and amendment outcomes.
It is not a complete roll-call scorecard of every education bill on which Roarx cast a yes/no vote.
