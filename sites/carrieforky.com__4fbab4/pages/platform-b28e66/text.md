Local priorities.
The South End deserves better than empty promises.
Carrie McKeehan is a teacher, mom, and lifelong South End resident.
She knows our challenges because she's lived them.
Her plan is simple: fight for safer neighborhoods, better schools, good jobs, and a government that actually works for us.
"The South End deserves real results—not empty promises." SAFER NEIGHBORHOODS Our families deserve to feel safe—on every street, in every neighborhood.
Fully fund local police and ensure better patrol coverage in high-crime areas.
Fix dangerous intersections like Dixie & Greenwood with better lighting and traffic design.
Invest in community-based programs that engage youth before they get involved in crime.
Partner with neighborhood groups to identify and solve local safety problems.
BETTER SCHOOLS As a teacher and mom, Carrie knows our schools are the heart of our community.
Demand our fair share of state funding for South End schools.
Invest in modern facilities —so kids don't learn in broken buildings.
Fight for competitive teacher pay to reduce turnover and attract great educators.
Hold JCPS accountable for real results in our classrooms.
GOOD JOBS & LOWER COSTS Working families deserve a fair shot—not rising costs and stagnant wages.
Cut red tape and support local small businesses.
Expand apprenticeships and career training in high-demand trades.
Fight for tax relief for working families and small businesses.
Work to lower utility bills and make everyday costs more manageable.
INFRASTRUCTURE THAT WORKS The South End should never be the city's afterthought.
Secure funding to repave pothole-filled roads and improve sidewalks.
Improve drainage in flood-prone neighborhoods like Beechmont and Fairdale.
Modernize public infrastructure to support local business growth.
Expand reliable broadband to every part of the district.
RESPONSIVE, ACCOUNTABLE LEADERSHIP Carrie isn't running to climb the political ladder.
She's running to serve.
Hold regular community listening sessions and updates.
Maintain real transparency in how state dollars are spent locally.
Work across party lines to get things done.
Always put people over politics .
