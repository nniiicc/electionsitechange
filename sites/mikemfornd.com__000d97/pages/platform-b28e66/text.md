North Dakota GOP Platform
Mike stands behind the North Dakota Republican Party’s Platform.
The platform’s tenants include:
- Individual Liberty
- Personal Responsibility
- Limited Government
- Peace through Strength and the Rule of Law
- Lower Taxes
- Right to Bear Arms
- Family Integrity and the American Moral Tradition
- Quality Education
- Legislative Integrity
Mike has also added an additional mission to make higher education in the state more affordable for in-state students by working with the Bank of ND to reduce interest rates for ND students, and fast track some degrees to reduce the amount of debt students accumulate.
He also has worked with the ND University System to look at more 90-credit bachelor degrees.
“North Dakota is in a great position due to good Republican policies being implemented over the past 25 years.
I will continue to work with Republican leadership to continue this trend and to make things even better than they already are.”
– Mike M.
