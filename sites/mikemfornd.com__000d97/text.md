Re-Elect Mike Motschenbacher
Mike Motschenbacher is a longtime Bismarck resident, husband, and father.
After graduating from college in 1992, he stayed in Bismarck and is proud to call it his home.
Mike and his wife Buffy have been married for 25 years and have four children Carson (22), Molly (19), Emily (18) and Zack (15).
Mike was first elected to the ND House of Representatives in 2022 and has served on the Tax and Finance committee and Political Subdivisions during both of his regular sessions, and during his interim sessions he has served on the Taxation Committee, Education Committee, Workforce Committee and Legislative Audit and Fiscal Review Committee.
Mike’s Priorities
Taxes
Continuing to lower income taxes will put more money in the pockets of North Dakota citizens.
We should strive towards eventual elimination.
Safety
We must crack down on career criminals, support the police and military, and provide safe places where families live, work, go to school, and play.
Family
We should promote strong families and provide opportunity for good jobs, access to affordable housing, and provide incentives for good safe daycare.
