Michael Caw For Georgia House 114 DONATE Affordability For All Meet Michael I am Michael Caw, a retired research and development technician, community volunteer, and proud resident of Newborn, Georgia.
I am running for the Georgia House of Representatives to bring thoughtful, data-driven leadership and a neighbor’s common sense to the State Capitol.
Originally from Wichita, Kansas, I built a career as a Laboratory Research and Development Technician, where I learned the importance of evidence-based decisions, teamwork, and accountability.
After retiring and settling in Newborn, I quickly became a hands-on community leader — volunteering as a Newborn Town Librarian, serving on the Newborn Tree Board, and contributing to the Newborn Comprehensive Plan Update Steering Committee.
Through this work, I have helped strengthen local resources, protect green spaces, and promote smart, balanced growth.
I am running as a Democrat for Georgia House District 114 because I believe good government should work for everyone — not just the powerful few.
Priorities Affordable Healthcare Expand access to medical and mental-health services so families can live healthy, secure lives.
Reduced Tax Burdens Promote fiscal responsibility and policies that keep more money in the pockets of hardworking families and retirees.
Make the rich and corporations pay their fair share.
Responsible Growth & Environmental Stewardship Balancing economic development with protection of District 114's natural beauty and rural heritage.
