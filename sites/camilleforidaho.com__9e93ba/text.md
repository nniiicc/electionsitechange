CONTACT HOME ABOUT ME About Me CAMILLE BLAYLOCK Hi friend - I'm Camille.
I’m a mom of two, a conservative , and a born-and-raised 2C girl who cares deeply about this state. ​ ​ I grew up on the Idaho values we all love - where hard work and self-reliance are rewarded, where we worship God, and where strong families are the foundation of liberty. ​ ​ At home, my husband and I are raising two incredible kids - teaching them to work hard, think for themselves, and stay grounded in their faith.
I want my kids to grow up in that same Idaho but frankly, I’m concerned about where things are headed.
I’ve never been one to sit back and complain… so I stepped up and ran for the Idaho Senate.​ ​ During my time serving ( so far ), I've fought to make the government smaller , more accountable , and actually work for the people it serves.
If you want to see exactly how I’ve done that, you can check out my voting record here . ​ ​ My background is in the private sector, including time working alongside government agencies as a vendor.
It was eye-opening .
The inefficiency and waste are real but friends, the problems are fixable !
That’s why I do this.
I’m not here to maintain broken systems.
I’m here to challenge them, cut waste , and push for something better . ​ ​ ​I’m not here for titles or politics as usual.
I’m here to fight for the Idaho we all know is worth protecting and I hope to earn your support . ​ ​ - Camille FIGHTING FOR IDAHO'S FUTURE LET'S CONNECT I look forward to hearing from you!
