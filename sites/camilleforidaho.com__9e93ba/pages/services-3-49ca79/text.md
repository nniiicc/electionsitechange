ON THE ISSUES
What I believe in.
What I support.
And what I will FIGHT for.
LIMITED GOVERNMENT &
FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY
I’m a fiscal conservative.
I trust Idaho families to manage their money a whole lot more than the government.
My guiding formula: Lower taxes + Less spending + Fewer regulations = Stronger economy.
Simple math if you ask me..
EDUCATION &
SCHOOL SAFETY
As a mom, this one’s personal.
Every kid deserves a strong education and a safe place to learn.
Not too complicated if you ask me.
Add in parental choice, bring back common sense, and suddenly we’re setting kids up to succeed.
AGRICULTURE &
TRANSPORTATION
Ag isn’t just part of Idaho, it's our backbone.
I’ll continue to fight to protect our water, invest in infrastructure, and stand up for the farmers and ranchers who keep this state running.
Because, well, food is good and I'd really like for them to keep producing it here.
LAW ENFORCEMENT
& VETERANS
I back the blue.
Full stop.
Drop the pen.
They deserve the tools to do their jobs and keep our communities safe.
We also have to stop deadly drugs like fentanyl from flooding in by securing our borders and supporting ICE.
And for our Veterans - we don’t just thank them, we show up and care for them.
LIBERTY &
IDAHO VALUES
I’m unapologetically pro-life and a strong defender of the 2nd Amendment.
Those aren’t fringe ideas here, they’re foundational.
And we need to get back to some common sense like men don’t belong in women’s sports or bathrooms... obviously.
I want my kids raised in an Idaho that still knows right from wrong and isn’t afraid to stand up for it.
