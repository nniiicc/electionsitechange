I believe in
Cutting the Cost of Healthcare/Prescription Drugs
I believe that everyone should have access to quality and affordable health care, and that coverage shouldn’t be a matter of income or the population of your hometown.
I am focused on lowering the cost of prescription drugs for working families and seniors by protecting and expanding Medicare’s ability to negotiate prescription drug prices and he has introduced legislation to crack down on big pharma delay tactics that increase prices.
I will always work to defend and improve the Affordable Care Act to protect health care coverage for people with pre-existing conditions.
