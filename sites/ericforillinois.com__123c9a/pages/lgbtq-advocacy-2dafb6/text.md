I Knows What's at Stake — Firsthand
Early in my career as a Meteorologist, I was forced out of a job because being gay went against the station’s “morals clause.” Surviving that experience made me determined to fight against injustice so discrimination doesn’t happen to anyone else.
Today, as an active member of the Congressional Equality Caucus and the first openly LGBTQ+ representative ever elected from Illinois, I bring that personal commitment to every vote, every floor speech, and every conversation with constituents across Central and Northwestern Illinois.
We’re going to do better today for the next generation, the same with LGBTQ issues.
We have to choose to either sit at the table or be on the menu, and I choose to sit at the table.
Where I Stand
Four Core Priorities
From the halls of Congress to the Quad Cities, here's how I'm fighting for LGBTQ+ equality.
Federal Equality Protections
As an active member of the Congressional Equality Caucus, he helps lead the reintroduction of the Equality Act to codify federal non-discrimination protections for LGBTQ+ Americans in housing, employment, and public spaces.
Mental Health & Youth Support
He champions the Pride in Mental Health Act, which directs the government to increase mental health resources for vulnerable LGBTQ+ youth, improve caregiver training, and implement bullying prevention guidelines in schools.
Military & Workplace Equality
Drawing on his my experience with workplace discrimination, I have publicly confronted cabinet officials on the floor of Congress to defend the service rights of LGBTQ+ service members and protect the legacy of historic figures like Harvey Milk from political erasure.
Opposing Anti-Trans Legislation
I am a vocal opponent of state and federal bills that target transgender youth and their families.
My position is clear: politicians have no place in the private medical and personal decisions of families.
That belongs to parents, doctors, and the individuals themselves.
