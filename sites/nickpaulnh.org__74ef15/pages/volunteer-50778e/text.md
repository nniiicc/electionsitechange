Lasting change doesn't come from shouting into the void on social media or waiting for politicians to fix our problems.
It happens when neighbors step up to talk to neighbors on their front porches.
In a New Hampshire House race, the margins are razor-thin—elections here are quite literally won or lost by a handful of votes.
That means your time and your unique talents aren't just helpful; they are the soul of the campaign.
Whether you have one hour a week to knock on a few doors, want to host a yard sign, or prefer to help us stay organized behind the scenes, your hands-on effort determines what kind of voice Rochester will have at the State House.
We are building a campaign run by and for regular, working-class people who are tired of being ignored and are ready to take back what’s ours.
No one is coming to save us.
We have to do it ourselves.
