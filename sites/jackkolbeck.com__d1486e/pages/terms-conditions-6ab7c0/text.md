Program Description.
Friends of Jack Kolbeck for State Legislature operates an SMS messaging program to keep supporters informed about the campaign.
By opting in, you consent to receive recurring autodialed text messages from Friends of Jack Kolbeck for State Legislature.
Consent is not a condition of any purchase or contribution.
Types of Messages.
Subscribers may receive text messages including, but not limited to: campaign updates and news, volunteer opportunities, event invitations and reminders, voting information (including dates, times, and polling locations for upcoming elections), get-out-the-vote reminders, fundraising appeals, and other communications related to the Friends of Jack Kolbeck for State Legislature campaign.
Message Frequency.
Opted-in users may receive approximately 2–5 text messages per month.
Message frequency may increase as election dates approach.
Message and Data Rates.
Message and data rates may apply.
Please check with your mobile carrier for details about your messaging and data plan.
Friends of Jack Kolbeck for State Legislature is not responsible for any charges from your wireless carrier.
Help.
To request more information, reply HELP to any message you receive from us.
You may also email jrkolbeck@sio.midco.net or visit jackkolbeck.com for assistance.
Opt-Out.
To stop receiving text messages entirely, reply STOP to any message you receive from us.
You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, after which you will receive no further messages unless you opt in again.
