LTC Melva Pérez (Ret.) built her career on duty, honor, and selfless service.
She served for almost three decades in the United States Army, rising through the ranks to become a respected leader known for strategic thinking, discipline, and unwavering integrity.
Whether leading troops, managing complex missions, or protecting American freedoms, Melva demonstrated the strength and commitment that defines Texas values.
After retiring from the military, Melva continued serving her community by advocating for veterans, supporting families, and participating in local initiatives that strengthen neighborhoods and uplift those in need.
Now, she's ready to bring her mission-driven leadership to Austin; fighting for safer communities, economic opportunity, and reliable public services for every family in HD 119.
