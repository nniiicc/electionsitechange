There are three ways to vote in Connecticut

Your Vote Matters *
VOTE BY NO EXCUSE ABSENTEE BALLOT
1) request your absentee ballot online or with your town clerk after August 15
2) Receive and complete your ballot.
Be sure to follow envelope instructions clearly
3) Put a stamp on the outer envelope and mail back to the town clerk
OR
Make it a single step by requesting and filling out in person at you Town Clerk’s office
VOTE BY EARLY, IN PERSON
Early Voting runs October 19-November 1
10am-6pm Every Day, including weekends
8am-8pm Extended Hours October 27 & 29
Wallingford: Town Hall, 45 S Main St
Middlefield: Middlefield Community Center, 405 Main St.
VOTE ON ELECTION DAY, IN PERSON
Polls are open 6am-8pm on November 3rd, Election Day
