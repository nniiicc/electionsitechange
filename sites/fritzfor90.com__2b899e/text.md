REPRESENTING WALLINGFORD AND MIDDLEFIELD
This District is more than just my home; it is where I was raised and where I chose to build my life.
As a lifelong resident of Wallingford, my roots here run deep.
I am running for State Representative to bring the citizens of Wallingford and Middlefield a renewed sense of advocacy, accessibility and unwavering commitment to the 90th District.
I believe that our community deserves a representative who truly understands and prioritizes the needs of all its constituents, big or small, no matter party affiliation.
