Meet Brenda.
Brenda Case is a lifelong Southeast Iowan, born and raised in Ottumwa, with deep rural roots and a 28-year career as an IT Project Manager with the Iowa Judicial Branch — where she earned recognition from the Iowa Supreme Court for her dedication to justice.
A community servant, proud member of a union family, and grandmother of two, Brenda understands firsthand the challenges facing working families, farmers, and rural communities.
She's ready to take the fight for fairness, opportunity, and dignity to Des Moines as your District 13 State Senator — because she understands where she comes from, and she knows who she serves: people like you.
Are you located in District 13?
The 13th District of the Iowa Senate is located in southern Iowa, and is currently composed of Davis, Monroe and Wapello Counties, and portions of Appanoose County.
