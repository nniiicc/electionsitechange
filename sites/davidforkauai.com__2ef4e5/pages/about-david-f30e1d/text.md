David's Journey
My journey has been shaped by family, faith, and a deep love for Kauaʻi.
My father started the algae farm here in 2010 to create new economic opportunities while advancing true environmental sustainability for the people of Hawaiʻi.
I first visited Kauaʻi that summer as a high school student, and because of my father’s work here, my family returned every summer after that.
Those early experiences shaped my appreciation for this island and the responsibility we share to care for it.
I completed my Quantitative Economics degree from Cal Poly SLO in 2015 and immediately joined my family's business.
My wife Emily and I got married in 2018 and moved to Kauaʻi full-time to manage the business.
Today, as a husband, father of three young children, and President of Global Algae, I carry on our values of hard work, responsibility, and community service.
Years of experience leading a local business and its team have given me a data-driven, common-sense approach to solving problems and getting things done.
My decision to run for office comes from a commitment to the people and future of Kauaʻi, and a belief that our island deserves leadership that listens, acts responsibly, and always puts Kauaʻi first.
I humbly ask for your vote on November 3, 2026.

About David Hazlebeck
David Hazlebeck is a devoted husband, father, and algae farmer who is deeply committed to serving the people of Kauaʻi.
David and his wife, Emily, were married in 2018 and made Līhuʻe their permanent home right after.
David had long looked forward to living on Kauaʻi, having spent summers here since 2010 supporting his family’s algae farm.
The Hazlebeck family are active members of Kalaheo Missionary Church, where they regularly serve and host an annual marriage and parenting seminar focused on strengthening families and marriages.
David officially joined his family’s company, Global Algae Innovations, in 2015 after graduating from California Polytechnic State University, San Luis Obispo (Cal Poly), where he earned a Bachelor of Science in Quantitative Economics.
During his college years, he played for SLOCORE, the university’s ultimate frisbee team, and served on youth staff at Grace Church, mentoring young people.
He developed the leadership skills that continue to guide his work.
Today, as President of Global Algae Innovations, David leads business operations and helps guide the company’s continued growth on Kauaʻi.
The company operates a 33-acre algae farm in Kāpaia, adjacent to KIUC’s power station.
Their work supports research efforts with the U.S.
Department of Energy and the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, as well as partnerships with local aquaculture farmers.
Algae is one of the fastest-growing sources of protein and oil.
It captures carbon dioxide and converts it into valuable products.
The company earned an XPRIZE Carbon Removal Milestone Award in 2021.
Under David’s leadership, the company has implemented forward-thinking workplace policies, including transitioning toward a four-day workweek to better support employees and their families.
Before joining Global Algae full-time, David served as the founding Athletic Director at San Luis Obispo Classical Academy & High School from 2012 to 2015.
Throughout his life, he has coached many keiki sports teams, including most recently his son’s Līhuʻe Red Sox T-Ball team in Fall 2025.
Coaching has strengthened his passion for mentoring youth and supporting strong families in the community.
David and Emily are also co-owners of Galloping Games, an educational card game company they operate alongside David’s three siblings and their spouses.
Their mission is to inspire families and friends to spend meaningful time together while learning and having fun.
