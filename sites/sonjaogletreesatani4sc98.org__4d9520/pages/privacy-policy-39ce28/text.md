Sonja Ogletree Satani for SC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of our supporters, volunteers, and visitors.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, engage with our campaign via text messages, or receive email communications from us.
We will maintain strict privacy policies to protect the personal information of our users obtained for all communications.
This information is never sold, rented, released, or traded to others without prior consent or legal obligation.
Any sharing of information with third parties is solely for the purpose of fulfilling the organization's obligations to the user.
We guarantee that it will never be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
1.
Information We Collect
a.
Website Usage
Personal Information: We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you sign up for updates, volunteer, donate, or participate in online actions.
Non-Personal Information: We may collect non-personal information such as browser type, IP address, and the pages you visit on our website to improve user experience and campaign performance.
b.
Text Messages
When you opt-in to receive text messages from our campaign, we may collect your mobile phone number and any information you provide in response to our text messages.
Text Message Opt-Out: If you are receiving text messages from us and wish to stop receiving them, simply respond with either “STOP” or “UNSUBSCRIBE” to the number from which you received the message.
Once we receive your message, you will no longer receive further text messages from us.
c.
Email Communications
We collect your email address and any information you provide when you sign up for our email updates or correspond with us via email.
2.
How We Use Your Information
To Communicate: We use your information to send you updates, news, and information about our campaign via email and text messages.
To Improve Our Campaign: We analyze the data we collect to understand supporter behavior, improve our website, and refine our campaign strategies.
To Process Donations: If you make a donation, we use your information to process payments and maintain donation records in compliance with legal requirements.
To Comply with Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our campaign, our supporters, or others.
3.
Information Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us with campaign activities, such as payment processing, email distribution, and data analysis.
These providers are required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes specified by us.
We may share aggregate, non-personal information with third parties to improve our campaign efforts and reach.
4.
Your Choices
Opt-Out of Emails: You can unsubscribe from our email list at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email we send.
Opt-Out of Text Messages: You can opt-out of receiving text messages by replying "STOP" to any text message you receive from us.
Cookies: You can manage cookies through your web browser settings, although disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website.
5.
Security
We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
However, no data transmission over the internet or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.
6.
Children's Privacy
Our campaign is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13, we will take steps to delete that information.
7.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons.
We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on our website and updating the effective date.
8.
Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us at: info@sonjaogletreesatani4sc98.org
