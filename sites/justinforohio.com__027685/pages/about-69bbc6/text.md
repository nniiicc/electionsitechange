Justin Pizzulli
Justin Pizzulli is a lifelong resident of Franklin Furnace, Ohio, and the proud descendant of Italian immigrants who came through Ellis Island in pursuit of the American Dream.
A uniquely driven and accomplished professional, Justin is widely respected for his leadership, grassroots organizing, strategic event planning, and organizational development expertise.
He earned his diploma from Green High School, a Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from Shawnee State University, and an MBA from Marshall University.
In addition, he is licensed by the Federal Railroad Administration as a Commercial Freight Conductor and is an active member of the Portsmouth Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers and Trainmen, Division 511.
With over a decade of experience in real estate, Justin has built a strong reputation as a seasoned and trusted realtor.
He has also played key roles in high-profile campaigns and national advocacy efforts, including serving as Eastern Field Organizer with Christians United for Israel.
Locally, he serves as an elected precinct committee member (Green E) with the Scioto County Republican Party.
Justin’s service in public office has been recognized by the Ohio Farm Bureau, which named him a “Friend of Farmers” for his work on behalf of rural communities.
A proud graduate of the American Legion Buckeye Boys State program, Justin remains deeply committed to servant leadership—often volunteering his time to support local candidates and causes, and always striving to make a meaningful impact in the lives of those around him.
