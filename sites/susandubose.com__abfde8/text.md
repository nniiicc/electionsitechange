Meet Susan DuBose Susan DuBose brings decades of experience in business, community service, and public leadership.
Her background in banking and economic development shaped a practical, results-driven approach that guides her work today.
As the State Representative for District 45, Susan is known for being accessible, prepared, and focused on the issues that affect everyday families.
Her leadership is grounded in conservative principles, faith, and a commitment to making sure government works responsibly.
