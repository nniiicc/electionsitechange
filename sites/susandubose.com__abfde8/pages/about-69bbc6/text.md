Family
Spouse – I’ve been married for 33 years to Dennis DuBose, CPA, Sr Executive VP for Resource Management Service LLC.
Children – Danielle DuBose, CPA and Georgetown Law Graduate, former Tax Adviser to US Senator Pat Toomey.
Drew DuBose- a graduate of Georgia Tech in Aerospace Engineering employed as a design engineer with Lockheed Martin in Fort Worth, TX.
Dennis and I moved to North Shelby County from Mobile in 1988 for me to pursue a career at Compass Bank.
I enjoyed my years as a commercial banker analyzing financial statements, developing business contacts, and visiting job sites.
After starting a family, we decided it was time to put my business career on hold and to become a full-time stay-at-home mom to Danielle and Drew.
It was an honor for me to stay at home, support my husband’s career and raise our children.
Dennis and I consider raising our children to become productive and God-fearing members of society to be our greatest accomplishment.
