Home Issues Events News Opportunities About Tucker Contribute Issues Tucker Smith is running to bring people-first leadership to District 23.
He believes government should work for everyday families — not special interests — and that strong communities are built through listening, accountability, and action.
His campaign is guided by three principles: Fairness.
Access.
Opportunity.
Fairness in how we treat one another.
Access to healthcare, education, and a voice in government.
Opportunity for every family to build a stable, successful future.
Tucker is committed to creating a district where everyone has the chance to thrive.
Fair Taxation and Economic Accountability I believe extreme concentrations of wealth undermine fairness and opportunity.
I support targeted tax increases on the wealthiest Utahns — including higher income, capital gains, estate, and wealth taxes — to limit excessive economic power and reinvest in children, families, and public services.
My goal is a market that works for everyone, not just the top 1%.
Campaign Finance and Democratic Reform I believe no one deserves unchecked political power.
I support campaign finance limits, protecting mail-in voting, defending citizen-led ballot initiatives, and reinstating Utah’s independent redistricting commission.
My focus is building a transparent, accountable government that truly reflects the will of the people.
Universal Access To Education & Childcare Access to opportunity starts early.
I support universal childcare, universal Pre-K, and fully funded, tuition-free K–12 public education — including eliminating school fees and covering meals and extracurriculars.
I believes every child deserves an equal starting point.
Protecting Workers and Strengthening Unions I believe economic opportunity requires quality jobs that pay a living wage.
I support protecting labor unions, strengthening worker protections, and expanding opportunities for workers to organize.
My goal is to ensure that working families share in Utah’s economic growth.
Affordable Housing for All I believe housing is a necessity — not an investment asset.
I support increasing housing supply through zoning reform while discouraging corporate ownership and speculation.
My proposal includes requiring affordable units in new developments, expanding non-market housing options, taxing secondary homes, and limiting corporate dominance in housing construction to ensure real affordability for Utah families.
Fairness.
Access.
Opportunity At the core of my campaign is a simple belief: power comes from the people.
I am committed to dispersing concentrated economic and political power, expanding access to essential services, and building a state where every Utah family has the opportunity to thrive.
