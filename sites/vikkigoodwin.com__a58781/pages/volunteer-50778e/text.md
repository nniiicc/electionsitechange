What is relational organizing?
Relational organizing means using the power of your relationships to get the people you know to take action – in this case, voting and volunteering.
To put it simply, it just means that people talking to people they already know is more effective than strangers talking to strangers.
That means you – yes, you! – are the best person to get your friends and family to vote, and to get them to vote for Vikki Goodwin.
How effective is relational organizing?
So close to Election Day, we’re prioritizing asking people to do the most important thing: get their friends and family to vote early.
This is the essence of relational organizing, and it is proven to work better than any other kind of voter contact.
A conversation between people who know each other is at least 2.5 times more effective than strangers talking to strangers.
Every time you talk to someone you know about Vikki Goodwin, that’s as effective as our campaign knocking 10 doors!
Our campaign's relational organizing goal: We need to contact every potential Democratic voter in Texas.
A unique and critical group of these voters are people you know .
Evidence shows that if you contact them about the election — rather than having a stranger call or text them — they will be significantly more likely to actually vote.
