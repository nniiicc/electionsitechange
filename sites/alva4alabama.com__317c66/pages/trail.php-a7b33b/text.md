Spent time with the men and women of the Alabama Constables Association — some of the most experienced and dedicated peace officers in our state.
Constables serve at the local level, often in rural and underserved areas, and they do the kind of work that doesn't make the headlines but holds communities together.
Talking with these officers reinforced something I have believed my entire career: public safety in Alabama is not a one-size-fits-all conversation.
What a constable in a rural county faces is different from what a city patrol officer faces, and the laws we write in Montgomery have to recognize that.
These men and women deserve a Legislature that listens to them.
Grateful to spend time with this group.
Their service is the kind of quiet, consistent work that strong communities are built on.
