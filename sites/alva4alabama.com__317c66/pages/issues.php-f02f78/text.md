ALVA LAMBERT for Alabama House District 74 Home Why I'm Running Issues Trail Volunteer District Donate Our Priorities Public Safety It all begins with people feeling safe.
When families feel secure in their neighborhoods, everything else follows — businesses invest, property values hold, schools improve, and young people see a reason to stay.
Without public safety, none of that is possible.
As a former Deputy District Attorney and current attorney in private practice with the Police Benevolent Association (PBA) as a major client, I have spent decades standing with the men and women who keep us safe.
I understand both the courtroom and the challenges officers face on the street every day.
My Plan: Support competitive pay and training for law enforcement, ensure prosecutors have the tools needed to combat violent crime, advocate for judicial accountability in the courtroom, and fight for the resources our officers need to keep Montgomery's neighborhoods safe.
Supporting Maxwell-Gunter Air Force Base Maxwell-Gunter Air Force Base is the heartbeat of Montgomery's economy and one of the most important military installations in the country.
The base supports thousands of jobs, drives billions in economic impact, and makes our city a hub for national defense, education, and innovation.
As a charter board member of the Alabama Military Support Foundation , I have worked firsthand to strengthen the relationship between our military community and the people of Montgomery.
I know what the base means to this city — and I know what it takes to protect it.
My Plan: Advocate at the state level for infrastructure and quality-of-life investments that keep Maxwell-Gunter competitive during future base realignment discussions, support military families with housing and education resources, and strengthen partnerships between the base and local businesses to maximize economic impact for District 74.
Healthcare Access With over 21 years of experience as an Administrative Law Judge and Executive Director of the State Health Planning Agency, I have a unique understanding of how healthcare policy impacts families in District 74.
I am committed to ensuring that healthcare decisions focus on patient outcomes and community needs rather than red tape.
My Plan: Protect local hospital accessibility, streamline certificate-of-need processes to encourage innovation, and support mental health resources for our most vulnerable citizens.
Economic Growth Montgomery should be a place where businesses want to build and where the next generation wants to stay.
My experience in business development has shown me exactly how government can either help or hinder economic progress.
My Plan: Reduce unnecessary regulatory burdens on small businesses, invest in local infrastructure that attracts new industries, and support vocational training programs to build a world-class workforce right here at home.
