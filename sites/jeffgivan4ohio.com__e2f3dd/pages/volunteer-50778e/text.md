VOLUNTEER WITH US
Volunteers are the heartbeat of this campaign.
Every door knocked, every phone call made, and every neighbor conversation had brings us closer to the kind of representation Ohio's 78th District deserves.
Whether you have an hour or a whole weekend, your time makes a real difference.
There are many ways to get involved: canvass neighborhoods, join a phone bank, help host community events, assist with yard sign distribution, or support our digital outreach efforts.
Whatever your skill set, we have a place for you.
Fill out the form and our team will be in touch.
