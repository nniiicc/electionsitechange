They deserve reliable access to healthcare, transportation, housing, and the services that allow them to live with dignity.
Raised in a family of veterans and public servants, Rosie understands the sacrifices military families make.
She will advocate for expanded services for veterans, protect programs that help seniors remain independent, and work to ensure rural communities are not left behind when resources are distributed across the state.
