This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.
By using this website, signing up for updates, making a contribution, volunteering, or otherwise interacting with the Campaign, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
Information We Collect We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including: Name Mailing address Email address Phone number Employer and occupation (when required for campaign finance reporting) Donation information Volunteer interests Event registrations Any other information you choose to provide through our forms or communications How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Communicate campaign updates and news.
Respond to your questions and requests.
Process contributions.
Comply with federal and Oklahoma campaign finance laws.
Recruit and coordinate volunteers.
Invite you to campaign events.
Improve our website and outreach efforts.
Send email and text message communications if you have opted in.
We may also use your information to better understand supporter interests and improve campaign communications.
Text Messaging If you choose to receive text messages from the Campaign, you consent to receive recurring automated and non-automated campaign messages at the phone number you provide.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP .
For assistance, reply HELP or contact the Campaign directly.
Your consent to receive text messages is not required as a condition of making a contribution or supporting the Campaign.
Email Communications If you subscribe to our email list, we may send campaign updates, fundraising appeals, volunteer opportunities, and event invitations.
You may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link included in our emails.
Contributions Political contributions may be subject to reporting requirements under Oklahoma law.
When making a contribution, we may collect information necessary to comply with applicable campaign finance laws, including your employer and occupation when required.
Payment information is processed securely through our third-party payment processors.
The Campaign does not store your complete credit card information.
You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although doing so may affect certain website features.
Sharing Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with trusted vendors or service providers who assist us with campaign operations, including: Payment processors Email service providers Text messaging providers Website hosting providers Digital advertising and analytics providers Volunteer and voter outreach platforms These providers may only use your information to perform services on behalf of the Campaign.
We may also disclose information when required by law or to comply with campaign finance reporting obligations.
Data Security We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal information.
However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
External Links Our website may contain links to third-party websites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
