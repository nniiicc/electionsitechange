Strong public schools are the heart of thriving rural communities, and investing in students means investing in Oklahoma's future.
Rosie believes we must recruit and retain excellent teachers by providing competitive pay and the resources they need to succeed in the classroom.
She supports expanding career and technical education, increasing opportunities for higher education, and ensuring schools have the funding necessary to prepare students for the workforce and college alike.
Every student should have the opportunity to build a successful future right here in Oklahoma.
