Strengthen Rural Healthcare
No one should have to drive hours to receive quality healthcare.
Yet too many families in House District 16 struggle to access primary care, mental health services, addiction treatment, and maternal healthcare close to home.
As a counselor, Rosie understands that health is about more than treating illness—it's about making sure people have the support they need before they reach a crisis.
She will work to strengthen rural hospitals, expand access to mental health providers, increase support for substance use recovery programs, and improve healthcare services for veterans and seniors.
Healthy communities are stronger communities, and every Oklahoman deserves quality care regardless of where they live.
