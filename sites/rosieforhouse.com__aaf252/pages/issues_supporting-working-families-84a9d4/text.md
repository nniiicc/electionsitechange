Rosie believes in
Supporting Working Families
Hardworking Oklahomans deserve an economy that works for them.
Too many families are facing rising costs while wages struggle to keep pace, making it harder to afford groceries, childcare, healthcare, and housing.
Rosie will advocate for policies that strengthen working families by expanding economic opportunity, supporting small businesses, improving workforce training, and protecting access to affordable healthcare.
She believes government should help remove barriers to success so that every family has the opportunity to thrive.
