When wages fail to keep pace with the cost of housing, childcare, healthcare, and other necessities, families are forced to make difficult choices that affect their long-term financial stability.
Addressing this challenge requires more than simply discussing wages.
We must create pathways to good-paying jobs, support workforce development, strengthen labor protections, and ensure that economic growth benefits workers as well as employers.
Every Kansan who works hard should have a realistic opportunity to build a stable life, support a family, and plan for the future.
