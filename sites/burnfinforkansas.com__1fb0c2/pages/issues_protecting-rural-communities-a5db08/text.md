Protecting rural communities
The future of Garden City depends on more than preserving what exists today.
It requires creating opportunities that encourage young people and families to build their futures in our town.
Economic development, housing availability, reliable infrastructure, and access to quality healthcare and education all play a role in determining whether rural communities grow or decline.
State leaders must recognize that policies designed for urban areas do not always meet the needs of rural Kansas.
Our community deserves a strong voice in Topeka, someone who does not toe the party line, and someone who brings solutions that reflect the unique challenges and opportunities of rural life.
By investing in rural Kansas, we strengthen the entire state.
