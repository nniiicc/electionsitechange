Issues
As a member of my beloved Garden City, and as an educator, volunteer, union leader, mom and friend, I have spent the last 15 years working alongside the people who make Garden City strong.
I have seen firsthand the challenges facing working families, public schools, rural communities, and local businesses, but I have also seen the resilience and determination of Kansans when we come together to solve problems.
My positions on the issues are shaped not by political ideology or special interests, but by real experiences listening to neighbors, advocating for students and educators, and working to improve the communities we call home.
Campaign Finance
I have committed to refusing corporate campaign contributions and relying instead on individual donations from the people and communities I seek to serve.
Too often, large donors gain outsized influence in the political process, leaving everyday Kansans wondering whose interests are really being represented.
By building a grassroots campaign funded by individuals, I can remain focused on the needs of working families, students, healthcare workers, small business owners, and educators in Garden City, not the demands of powerful special interests.
Strong Public Schools
I have been a public school educator for nearly two decades and am the mother of three public school children.
During that time, I have seen both the strengths and challenges of public education at every level; from teacher preparation programs and classroom management to district initiatives, school board decisions, and state and federal legislation.
As your state representative, I will bring that much-needed experience to the legislature, advocating for parents, teachers, and students while working to strengthen our public schools through needed and complex reforms.

Livable wage
A livable wage is essential for strong families and a strong economy in western Kansas.
According to the Economic Policy Institute's Family Budget Calculator, a worker in Finney County needs roughly $26 an hour to achieve a modest but comfortable standard of living, yet too many hardworking Kansans earn far less.
When wages fail to keep pace with the cost of living, families are forced to make difficult choices about housing, healthcare, and childcare despite working full time.
As your state representative, I will advocate for policies that support livable wages, strengthen local economies, and ensure working Kansans can provide for their families with dignity.

Affordable and Accessible Healthcare
Affordable healthcare should not be out of reach for working families.
By expanding Medicaid in Kansas, we can help more Kansans access quality healthcare, support struggling rural hospitals, and bring our federal tax dollars back home instead of sending them to other states.
When families can afford to see a doctor and hospitals can keep their doors open, our communities are stronger and healthier.
As your state representative, I will support policies that strengthen rural healthcare systems, expand access to specialized medicine, and expand Medicaid so more Kansans can access affordable care close to home.
Strong rural communities depend on strong rural healthcare.

Property Taxes
Rising property taxes are a concern for many Kansas families.
While most property taxes are levied by local entities such as public schools, community colleges, cities, and counties, state decisions play a significant role in determining how much those local governments must raise.
For years, the state has failed to fully fund its share of special education costs, forcing local school districts to redirect local dollars to cover the shortfall.
As your state representative, I will work to ensure the state meets its funding obligations so local communities are not left carrying costs that should be borne by the state, helping reduce pressure on local property taxpayers while maintaining strong public services.
