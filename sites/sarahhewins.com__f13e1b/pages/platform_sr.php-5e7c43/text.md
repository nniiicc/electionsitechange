have been dealt with by our elected representatives.
One of the things I've always done in public office is put together coalitions to get things done.
Instead of towns having to fight these mandates town-by-town, I'll work with other representatives from all affected
towns to review, modify, or reject them at the State House before they are enacted.

As a Select Board member, I've already been working on this issue.
I negociated lower electricity supply costs in 2020, that actually have gone down slightly in 2026,
and are about 5% lower than Eversource's basic supply rate.
But it's the "distribution" and other delivery costs that are increasing so much.
This past year, I set up a meeting with an Eversource representative and our Town Administrator to discuss these charges and to ask why they were so outrageous.
Their representative came to a Select Board meeting and very politely told us absolutely nothing.
When I demanded more specifics from Eversource, they sent two more representatives who told us that Eversource is "upgrading" for "better service" and that’s just the way it is.

As a Representative, I'll be able to put together a coalition to work with the Governor's office to have the DPU and utility companies respond to all of us.
