Need Answers?
Common Questions Answered
We believe voters deserve clear answers and honest conversations.
Here are some of the questions we hear most often on the campaign trail.
Why are you running for Congress?
I’m running because too many hardworking people feel like government has stopped listening.
I believe in leadership that puts citizens first again.
What is your position on the economy?
I believe families and small businesses need relief.
That means practical policies that lower everyday costs, support local growth, and reduce unnecessary government burdens.
What do you believe about government?
I believe government should be limited, accountable, and focused on serving the people — not making life harder for them.
How can we support the campaign?
You can volunteer, attend an event, donate, or help spread the message.
Every contribution helps us reach more people.
