Priscilla A.
Prado is a proud mother of four, a grandmother, small business owner, and community leader serving as State Representative for Wisconsin's 9th Assembly District.
She has called the Polonia and Clark Square neighborhoods home for nearly 17 years — and has spent her career working on Milwaukee's Southside.
She founded Midwest Bookkeeping and Tax Solutions, helping hundreds of small businesses navigate taxes, compliance, and financial growth.
Her leadership extends far beyond business — she has served on boards, mentored entrepreneurs, and advocated tirelessly for Latino families and immigrant communities.
Priscilla is also a two-time heart attack and triple bypass survivor.
That experience deepened her commitment to healthcare access and awareness for women and minority communities.
She is currently working with cardiac specialists at Aurora St.
Luke's to introduce proactive cardiac care legislation in the 2026-2028 session.

Real Results.
These aren't talking points — they're the issues constituents bring up at every door, every town hall, every chance encounter at the grocery store.
01 Immigration Wisconsin is home to hardworking immigrant families who contribute to our communities, businesses, and economy every day.
One of her key priorities is Driver's Licenses for All — allowing residents to obtain driver's licenses regardless of immigration status to improve road safety, ensure drivers are trained and insured, and strengthen public safety for everyone.
Immigration 02 Public Safety Public safety must focus not only on enforcement, but also prevention and opportunity.
Representative Prado believes Wisconsin must address the root causes of crime, particularly among young people, by investing in early intervention, community programs, and youth opportunities.
Strong communities are built through prevention, investment, and trust.
Safety 03 Affordability Wisconsin families are feeling the pressure of rising costs — from high property taxes to the increasing price of child care — while wages have not kept pace.
Representative Prado supports lowering the property tax burden, expanding access to affordable childcare, and promoting fair wages so families can build secure futures and thrive.
Affordability 04 Education Every child deserves access to a high-quality education, regardless of their family's income or zip code.
Representative Prado's priority is ensuring every child has the opportunity to succeed and that families have access to the resources needed to support their children's futures.
Education 05 Healthcare Access As a two-time heart attack and triple bypass survivor, Representative Prado understands how critical access to healthcare and preventative care can be.
She believes Wisconsin must expand access to healthcare services so families can detect health problems early, manage chronic conditions, and break cycles of generational health disparities.
Healthcare 06 Economic Opportunity As a small business owner herself, Representative Prado understands the challenges entrepreneurs face.
She supports policies that promote fair wages, workforce development, and small business growth — because strong economies are built when everyone has the opportunity to succeed.
Economy 07 Clean Environment Representative Prado supports responsible environmental policies that protect Wisconsin's natural resources and ensure sustainable development.
She has expressed concern about the environmental impact of large-scale data centers, and works closely with Rep.
Tenorio and Rep.
Miresse on bills that will protect our state.
