Ethan Sanford for Idaho State Senate Hello, everyone!
My name's Ethan Sanford, and I'm running for Idaho State Senator from Legislative District #34 (a.k.a.
Madison County)!
I've lived here in Idaho for a couple of years now, and I love it here so much that I've decided to start the next chapter of my life here.
If all goes according to plan, Idaho is where I'll have my first post-graduation job, and where I'll be able to start a family one day.
My campaign platform: Protect and Maintain our Water Resources Keep Public Lands in Public Hands Maintain a Balanced Budget Protect Idaho's Medicaid Expansion Protect our Schools, Public and Private Protect our Right to Ballot Initiatives Protect Reproductive Rights === Want to know where I stand on any other issues??
Please reach out and ask (See my Contact Info tab on this website)!
Also keep checking my website; without a doubt, I'll be adding to it.
