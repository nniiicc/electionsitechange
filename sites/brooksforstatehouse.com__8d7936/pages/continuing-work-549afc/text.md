Continuing the Work
Shaundelle Brooks is running for reelection to continue building on the progress already made—delivering safer communities, stronger families, and real opportunity for every resident.
Her focus remains on advancing solutions that protect families, strengthen neighborhoods, and move the community forward.
Second-Term Priorities
- Strengthen violence prevention and community safety through proactive, prevention-focused policies
- Expand mental health crisis response and early intervention, ensuring help is available before situations escalate
- Invest in youth opportunity and prevention programs that create pathways to success
- Support economic stability for working families, including fair wages and access to essential services
- Increase access to affordable housing and stabilize growing communities
- Strengthen support for first responders, including resources, training, and mental health support
- Expand community-based public health resources that address root causes and improve long-term outcomes
- Support small businesses and local economic growth to strengthen the local economy
- Expand education and workforce training pathways that connect residents to real opportunities
- Increase transparency and community engagement, ensuring leadership remains accessible and accountable
