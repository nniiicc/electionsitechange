ANCHORAGE, AK — Today, Put Alaska First released a new ad, “Captain Bob,” highlighting Senator Dan Sullivan’s (R-Alaska) record of delivering for Alaska’s fisheries, and the sharp contrast with Mary Peltola, who told a fisherman asking for help on bycatch, “I’m just one person.
What can I do?”
The ad also draws a sharp contrast with Mary Peltola’s record.
Bob Candopoulos, a longtime Seward charter captain, recalls asking then Congresswoman Peltola to take action on bycatch while she served in Congress, only to hear, “I’m just one person.
What can I do?” While Peltola made excuses, Senator Sullivan listened to Alaska’s fishermen, took action, and delivered results.
The ad also highlights Senator Sullivan’s record of fighting for Alaska’s fisheries by taking action to reduce bycatch, strengthen Alaska’s salmon runs, combat unfair foreign seafood competition, and protect Alaska’s fishermen and fishing communities.
TRANSCRIPT:
I’ve been a sportfishing captain in Seward for almost 50 years.

What can I do?”

He listens, leads, and delivers results.
