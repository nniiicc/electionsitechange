ANCHORAGE, AK — Today, Senator Dan Sullivan’s (R-Alaska) re-election campaign announced the endorsement of the U.S.
Chamber of Commerce, recognizing Senator Sullivan’s strong record of supporting Alaska’s small business, workers, American families, and advancing pro-growth policies that create good-paying jobs and expand economic opportunity across Alaska.
“The U.S.
Chamber is proud to endorse Senator Dan Sullivan, a steadfast champion for Alaska’s small businesses, families and workers,” said former Rep.
Rodney Davis, Senior Vice President of Government Affairs at the U.S.
Chamber of Commerce.
“He has consistently supported pro-growth policies that strengthen the economy and expand opportunity across the State.
We look forward to continuing our partnership with him in another successful term in the U.S.
Senate.”
“I am honored to once again earn the endorsement of the U.S.
Chamber of Commerce.
Our state’s small businesses, workers, and entrepreneurs are crucial to our economy and they know better than Washington bureaucrats how to invest, grow, and create good-paying jobs right here in Alaska,” said Senator Dan Sullivan.
“In the Senate, I’ve fought successfully to cut taxes, put more money in people’s pockets, reduce burdensome regulations, unleash Alaskan energy, and create more opportunities for Alaskans.
I will keep fighting to ensure Alaska’s businesses and workers have every opportunity to succeed.”
The U.S.
Chamber of Commerce joins a growing list of organizations and leaders supporting Senator Sullivan’s re-election campaign.
