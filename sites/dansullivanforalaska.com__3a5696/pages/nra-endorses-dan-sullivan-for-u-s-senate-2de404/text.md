Senate July 13, 2026 “Thank you for standing strong for freedom and defending NRA members and law-abiding gun owners in Alaska and throughout the United States” ANCHORAGE, AK — Today, Senator Dan Sullivan’s (R-Alaska) re-election campaign announced the endorsement of the National Rifle Association (NRA) Political Victory Fund, recognizing Senator Sullivan’s unwavering commitment to defending the Second Amendment and protecting Alaska’s hunters, sportsmen, and gun owners.
In an endorsement letter , NRA Political Victory Fund Chairman John Commerford highlighted Senator Sullivan’s leadership in defending the Second Amendment, writing: “Thank you for standing strong for freedom and defending NRA members and law-abiding gun owners in Alaska and throughout the United States.
Based on your leadership protecting the Second amendment in the Senate, you have earned an ‘A+’ rating from the NRA-PVF for this election.
An ‘A+’ is reserved for a legislator with not only an excellent voting record on all critical NRA issues, but who has made a vigorous effort to promote and defend the Second Amendment.” Read the NRA Political Victory Fund’s full endorsement letter here .
“I am honored to earn the endorsement of the NRA,” said Senator Sullivan.
“The Second Amendment is a fundamental constitutional right and Alaskans understand that better than anyone.
I will always fight to protect the rights of Alaska’s gun owners, defend our hunting heritage, and stand up to those who want to undermine our constitutional freedoms.
I’ll never stop working to ensure the next generation of Alaskans enjoys the same liberties that have defined our state from the beginning.” “In contrast, Senator Sullivan’s exceptional record with that of his opponent, Mary Peltola, who was hand-picked by anti-Second Amendment Democrat Senator Chuck Schumer,” said Campaign Spokesperson Nate Adams.
“Peltola has pledged her loyalty to Schumer, and her first vote in the Senate, if elected, would be to make Schumer the Senate Majority Leader.
This would be a disaster for our Second Amendment rights and for the great state of Alaska.” The NRA joins a growing list of organizations and leaders supporting Senator Sullivan’s re-election campaign.
