ANCHORAGE, AK — The Women for Dan Coalition continued its statewide launch in Fairbanks and Anchorage this week, bringing together women from across the Interior in support of Senator Dan Sullivan’s re-election campaign.
Julie Fate Sullivan joins local women at a Women for Dan event in Anchorage, Alaska.
The Fairbanks and Anchorage events follow the coalition’s successful launch on the Kenai Peninsula and is part of a series of events taking place across Alaska.
The Women for Dan Coalition has already grown to 900 women from communities across the state.
Julie Fate Sullivan joins local women at a Women for Dan event in Fairbanks, Alaska.
Julie Fate Sullivan joined women in Fairbanks and Anchorage to discuss Senator Sullivan’s work to combat fentanyl and human trafficking, protect victims of sexual assault and domestic violence, and put more money back into the pockets of working families across Alaska.
