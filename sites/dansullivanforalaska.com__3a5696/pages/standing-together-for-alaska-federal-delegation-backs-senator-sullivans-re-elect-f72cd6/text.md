Senator Dan Sullivan (R-Alaska) earned endorsements from Senator Lisa Murkowski and Congressman Nick Begich (both R-Alaska) for his 2026 re-election bid against Democrat Mary Peltola.
Murkowski swiftly endorsed Sullivan on Monday , citing their work together in the Senate, despite previously backing Peltola in her previous House races.
Begich endorsed Sullivan on Wednesday , citing Sullivan’s strong record of delivering results for Alaska.
“At various points in Alaska’s history, our state has had an effective delegation that has worked together to deliver real results and right now is one of those points,” said Senator Dan Sullivan.
