ANCHORAGE, AK — Concerned Veterans for America (CVA) Action recently announced its endorsement of Senator Dan S.
Sullivan for re-election to the U.S.
Senate, citing his proven record of fighting for Alaskan veterans, strengthening America’s national security, and delivering results that make life more affordable for families.
“As a Marine Corps veteran representing one of the nation’s largest veteran populations, Sen.
Sullivan understands firsthand the sacrifices of those who serve and has been a steadfast advocate for Alaska’s veterans in Washington.
Beyond his advocacy for veterans, he has demonstrated a commitment to expanding domestic energy production, cutting burdensome regulations, and lowering costs for Alaska families – which is exactly the kind of leadership the state needs.”
