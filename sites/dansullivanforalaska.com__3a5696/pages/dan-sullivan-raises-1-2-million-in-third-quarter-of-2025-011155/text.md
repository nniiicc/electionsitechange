Senator Dan Sullivan’s (R-Alaska) re-election campaign announced it raised $1.2 million in the third quarter of 2025, continuing to build strong momentum ahead of the 2026 election.
The campaign now has roughly $4.8 million cash on hand, the highest cash on hand of any candidate for Senate in the year before an election in Alaska history.
“I’m incredibly grateful for the support our reelection campaign continues to receive.
In our first year back in the majority, we’ve delivered historic legislative wins – unleashing Alaska’s responsible resource development and the opportunity to create thousands of good-paying jobs, securing permanent lower tax rates to support working families, making record investments in our national defense and Coast Guard including 16 new icebreakers and the funding to homeport in Juneau, strengthening our border and upgrading aviation safety across the state,” said Senator Sullivan .
