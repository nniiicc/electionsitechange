Accountability to the Citizens of Arizona
I’m running to bring authentic leadership back to the State Capitol.
No more radical agendas and wasted time with half-measures.
No more burdens forced onto working families.
We must protect our communities, our city, and the future of our state.
- Fiscal Responsibility: I will not vote to increase tax spending or create new tax burdens on LD26 homeowners.
There won't be unchecked spending or frivolous pet projects on my watch.
Money spent should have an evidence-based outcome that benefits the tax payers.
- Safe Streets: Our laws are not being enforced.
Crime and homelessness are exploding because we keep rewarding failure.
It’s time law enforcement is allowed to do their job and violators are held responsible for their choices.
- American Culture Comes First: We must stop allowing harmful ideologies being forced on our communities.
There's simply no excuse for anti-American or anti-family agendas.
It’s time to stand firm and remember what really built this country.
- Family Protection: Parents alone have the right to raise and educate their own children.
I will defend our rights against bureaucrats and activists who want to replace them.
- Election Integrity: Secure our elections with full chain-of-custody on every ballot and same-day citizenship verification as well as same day tally of all the votes.
Restore the precinct voting; One Vote, One Ballot, One Day.
Citizens must decide the future of our district and our state.
- Build Strong Communities: We need to awaken and mobilize our own young people to register, vote, and get involved here in LD26.
Real community is built from the ground up by people who actually care about preserving the American way of life, safety, and character of our neighborhoods.

LD26 deserves better.
We deserve representatives who put the citizens of this district first — all of them, without resentment.
