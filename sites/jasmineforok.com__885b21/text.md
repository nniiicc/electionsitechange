Everyday Oklahomans.
Not lobbyists, not billionaires, not super PACs.
And definitely not AIPAC; I’m not here to sell my voice to the highest bidder.
I believe public service should be about serving people, not padding bank accounts.
So, no corporate PAC money?
Correct.
None.
Zero.
Zilch.
If it comes with strings attached, I don’t want it.
What’s your campaign staff budget look like?
Let’s just say we’re not out here treating politics like a get-rich-quick scheme.
Grassroots means lean budgets, creative problem-solving, and more time with the people instead of endless donor dinners.

Because if you have to buy your way into viability, then the system is broken.
The loudest voice in the room shouldn’t belong to the person with the fattest wallet.

People over politics — every single time.
Education reform that’s honest and equitable.
Healthcare access for all, especially rural and underserved communities.
Support for military families (I’m a military spouse myself).
Protections for Native sovereignty (I’m Chickasaw and proud).
Equity in autism care (my son is autistic and thriving because of early intervention).
Transparency & accountability in government — no excuses.
