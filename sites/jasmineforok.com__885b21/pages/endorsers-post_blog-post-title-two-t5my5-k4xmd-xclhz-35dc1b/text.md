The Collective PAC Endorses N'Kiyla "Jasmine" Thomas for United States Senate in Oklahoma
Today, N'Kiyla "Jasmine" Thomas announced that she has earned the endorsement of The Collective PAC, the nation's leading organization dedicated to building Black political power and increasing Black representation at every level of government.
The endorsement recognizes Thomas' historic campaign for the United States Senate.
If elected, Jasmine Thomas will become the first Black and Native woman to hold this U.S.
Senate seat in Oklahoma history.
A nurse, military spouse, autism mom, and lifelong Oklahoman from Ardmore, Thomas has built a grassroots campaign centered on healthcare access, working families, education, disability rights, rural communities, and ensuring that every Oklahoman has the opportunity to thrive.
"I am deeply honored to receive the support of The Collective PAC,
; said N'Kiyla "Jasmine" Thomas.
"This endorsement
represents the power of communities who have spent generations fighting to be heard and represented.
My campaign has always been about bringing the voices of everyday Oklahomans, working families, veterans, caregivers, rural communities, and people who have been overlooked by our political system... directly to Washington."
"The Collective PAC understands that representation matters," Thomas continued.
"When people see leaders who understand their lived experiences, it builds trust and inspires communities to believe that public service is possible for them too."
Founded in 2016, The Collective PAC works to increase Black political representation by supporting candidates committed to serving their communities and advancing meaningful change through public leadership.
Thomas' campaign has been powered by grassroots supporters across Oklahoma, emphasizing small-dollar donors, community engagement, and a people-first approach to politics.
ABOUT N'KIYLA "JASMINE" THOMAS
N'Kiyla "Jasmine" Thomas is a nurse, military spouse, autism mom, and lifelong Oklahoman from Ardmore, Oklahoma.
She is a candidate for the United States Senate focused on expanding healthcare access, strengthening public education, supporting working families, protecting disability rights, and ensuring every community has a voice in Washington.
