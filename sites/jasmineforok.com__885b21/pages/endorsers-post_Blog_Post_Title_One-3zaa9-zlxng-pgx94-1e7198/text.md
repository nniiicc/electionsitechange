An active-duty military spouse and Chickasaw citizen, she has bold ideas to improve public education, healthcare, childcare, and environmental protection while addressing the cost of living crisis and protecting human rights.
Seating her will ensure a Democratic Senate majority, which can curb extremist policies and start building a better future.
With her first-hand knowledge of the rewards but also challenges of military life, N'Kiyla Jasmine Thomas knows what needs to be done to improve the lives of veterans and military families: ensuring fair military pay reflecting real family costs, fixing inaccurate calculations of base allowances for subsistence and housing, protecting spousal employment, expanding childcare access during deployments, and improving staffing of and access to veteran healthcare systems.
N'Kiyla Jasmine Thomas is well qualified to represent Oklahoma in the U.
S.
Senate.
The Oklahoma Democratic Veterans Federation strongly endorses her.
