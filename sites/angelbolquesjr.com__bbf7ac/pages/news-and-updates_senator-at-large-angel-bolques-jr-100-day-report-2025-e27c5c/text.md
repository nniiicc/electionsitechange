100 Day Report - 2025 Angel Bolques Jr.
05 Jun 05 Jun To the People of the Virgin Islands, It is an honor and my duty to share this 100-day Report with you, highlighting the progress we have made together in the first months of2025.
As your Senator-At-Large, I remain fully committed to building a Virgin Islands that is safer, stronger, and more inclusive for everyone.
From passing meaningful legislation to advancing infrastructure projects, engaging with our communities, and strengthening regionalities, my focus has been on delivering results that benefit every resident of our beautiful Virgin Islands.
