Proposed Marine Tariff Increase Angel Bolques Jr.
08 Oct Reason for the Survey The Office of Senator Angel L.
Bolques Jr., Senator At Large, is conducting this survey to ensure that any proposed rate adjustments by V.I.P.A. reflect transparency, fairness, and the economic realities of Virgin Islanders—especially those on St.
John, where higher import costs already affect daily living.
Your input will help guide legislative oversight, promote accountability, and protect residents and small businesses from unreasonable financial burdens.
