Bolques, Jr. expressed gratitude to the Committee on Budget, Appropriations, and Finance for their strong support of Bill No.
35-0272.
The bill introduces key amendments to the Virgin Islands Code, increasing the motor vehicle rental surcharge from $3.75 to $5.00, primarily affecting tourists.
This measure is expected to generate between $850,000 to over $1 million annually, helping to balance the FY25 budget while safeguarding residents from additional financial burdens.
