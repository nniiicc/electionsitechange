"Our Islands, Our People, Our Future!" - A Coloring Book by Senator At Large Angel Bolques Jr.
Angel Bolques Jr.
28 Jul 28 Jul 🎉 IT'S HERE!
A GIFT FOR THE CHILDREN OF THE VIRGIN ISLANDS! 🇻🇮 📚🖍️I'm excited to share my new FREE coloring book, "Our Islands, Our People, Our Future!" Created especially for the children of the U.S.
Virgin Islands, this book is designed to inspire creativity while introducing young readers to some of the work I've been honored to do as your Senator-at-Large —along with the people, places, culture, and values that make our islands so special.
Inside, children will discover fun coloring pages highlighting: * Our beautiful islands and rich culture * Honoring our seniors * Investing in our youth and education * Caring for our environment * Building stronger communities *Some of the legislation and initiatives I've championed to help improve the lives of Virgin Islanders * And positive messages that encourage children to dream big, lead with kindness, and serve their communities.
This coloring book isn't meant to tell the whole story of my work—it's a fun and educational way to introduce our youngest Virgin Islanders to civic engagement, community service, and the importance of working together to build a brighter future. 🖍️ Download your FREE digital copy today or pick up a printed copy while supplies last!
Parents, grandparents, teachers, churches, youth groups, and community organizations—I invite you to share this resource with the children in your lives.
Together, we can inspire the next generation to love their islands, value their community, and believe in their ability to make a difference.
