As the Senator-at-Large in the Legislature of the Virgin Islands, Angel L.
Bolques Jr. serves as a dedicated representative of the entire territory, encompassing St.
Croix, St.
Thomas, and St.
John.
His role is multifaceted, requiring a commitment to legislative excellence, community engagement, and policy innovation.
His responsibilities include:
- Crafting, introducing, debating, and voting on laws that address the diverse needs of the Virgin Islands.
- Conducting research and collaborating with stakeholders to ensure legislation is data-driven and community-focused.
- Monitoring the implementation of enacted laws to ensure their intended impact is achieved.
- Serving as the voice of all Virgin Islanders, addressing individual and collective concerns.
- Advocating for equitable allocation of resources and services to benefit residents across all islands.
- Facilitating communication between constituents and government agencies to resolve issues efficiently.
- Playing a pivotal role in legislative committees to assess policies, budgets, and programs.
- Driving meaningful discussions and providing oversight in critical sectors such as education, healthcare, public safety, economic development, and infrastructure.
- Ensuring transparency and accountability in government operations through rigorous analysis and review.
- Maintaining an active presence across the territory to listen to residents’ concerns, understand their priorities, and foster trust.
- Participating in community events, town halls, and forums to strengthen relationships and enhance public awareness of legislative initiatives.
- Supporting grassroots efforts that align with the broader vision for the Virgin Islands' development.
- Championing forward-thinking policies that promote economic growth, workforce development, and technological advancement.
- Prioritizing cultural preservation, environmental sustainability, and social equity to safeguard the unique identity and future of the Virgin Islands.
- Collaborating with local, regional, and federal entities to secure funding and opportunities for territorial advancement.
- Providing strategic leadership to inspire confidence and unity among residents and colleagues in the Legislature.
- Driving initiatives to modernize governance, improve public services, and enhance quality of life for all Virgin Islanders.
- Advocating for a vision of progress that balances economic development with the preservation of the territory’s natural and cultural heritage.
Senator Bolques is committed to fostering a transparent, inclusive, and responsive government while ensuring the Virgin Islands continues to thrive as a community and a territory.
