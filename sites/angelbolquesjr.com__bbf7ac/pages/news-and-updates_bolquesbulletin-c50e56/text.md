Donate Contact Gallery Home News and Updates The Bolques Bulletin (A bimestrial newsletter) The Bolques Bulletin (A bimestrial newsletter) 15 Mar 15 Mar Senator-At-Large Angel Bolques, Jr. is excited to announce the release of the inaugural edition of his bimestrial newsletter, the Bolques Bulletin.
This publication intends to keep his colleagues and the territorial public abreast of his team’s activities during this term.
This edition briefly overviews what has transpired since the beginning of his term in the 35th Legislature of the Virgin Islands.
The Bolques Bulletin will be released every two months with the latest information on constituent activity, legislation, and accomplishments from the office of the Senator-At-Large.
“Transparency, community engagement, and communication are vital in building good rapport and public trust,” stated Senator Bolques.
"My office and I look forward to sharing our efforts and work,” said Senator Bolques.
