Donate Contact Gallery Legislative POLICY Agenda 1.
Economic Development & Agriculture Support Local Agriculture: Strengthen food autonomy and security by supporting farmers, fishers, and livestock producers, creating sustainable economic opportunities and reducing reliance on imports.
Expand Small Business Ecosystems: Promote entrepreneurship by simplifying licensing processes, providing funding opportunities, and fostering innovation among youth and aspiring business owners.
Boost International Investment: Position the Virgin Islands as a hub for international banking, commerce, and foreign investment to stimulate economic growth.
Develop Resilient Economic Frameworks: Invest in infrastructure and policies that ensure economic sustainability and adaptability to global changes.
2.
Technological Advancements Cybersecurity Enhancement: Establish robust systems to secure government data and critical services, ensuring the integrity of public operations.
Expand Digital Governance: Implement e-government initiatives to increase efficiency, improve accessibility, and enhance transparency in public services.
Innovate Through Digital Currency: Explore digital finance opportunities to benefit local businesses and foster economic modernization.
Technological Workforce Development: Create training programs to prepare the workforce for emerging tech-driven industries, fostering innovation and job creation.
3.
Housing & Living Opportunities Comprehensive Housing Solutions: Expand affordable housing options for low-income families, workforce members, and seniors, ensuring access to safe and adequate living conditions.
Sustainable Urban Development: Promote environmentally friendly and disaster-resilient housing and community projects.
Inclusive Housing Policies: Advocate for equitable housing opportunities for all residents, emphasizing accessibility for vulnerable populations.
4.
Education & Cultural Preservation Innovative Education Pathways: Develop programs that combine academics with career and technical education to equip students with the tools to succeed in a competitive economy.
Preserve Cultural Heritage: Expand education initiatives that promote Virgin Islands history and culture across all grade levels.
Arts & Creative Development: Support arts education, cultural programs, and community events that inspire creativity and foster cultural pride.
5.
Reform & Public Safety Modernize Public Safety Infrastructure: Improve emergency response systems, disaster preparedness, and law enforcement tools to ensure public safety.
Support Criminal Justice Reform: Advocate for fair and humane treatment of incarcerated individuals, rehabilitation programs, and alternatives to incarceration.
Protect Victims’ Rights: Strengthen support systems and funding for crime victims, ensuring access to resources and justice.
Enhance Legislative Efficiency: Continuously review and modernize laws to reflect the changing needs of society, ensuring equitable and effective governance.
6.
Health & Social Services Improve Healthcare Accessibility: Support initiatives that expand healthcare services, reduce costs, and improve quality for all residents.
Focus on Mental Health: Invest in mental health awareness, counseling services, and treatment programs for individuals and families.
Elder Care Support: Strengthen programs and services for the aging population, ensuring dignity, health, and quality of life in their later years.
7.
Environmental Sustainability & Natural Resources Promote Renewable Energy: Advocate for policies and programs that expand the use of solar, wind, and other renewable energy sources, reducing the territory’s carbon footprint.
Preserve Natural Resources: Support conservation efforts to protect the Virgin Islands’ unique ecosystems, beaches, and wildlife.
Enhance Climate Resilience: Develop infrastructure and policies that help communities adapt to climate change and prevent environmental degradation.
8.
Transportation & Infrastructure Modernize Public Transportation: Expand and improve transportation options, ensuring accessibility for all residents, including those with disabilities.
Enhance Road and Utility Systems: Prioritize infrastructure projects that repair, upgrade, and expand roads, water systems, and electrical grids.
Support Maritime and Aviation Industries: Strengthen the territory’s maritime and aviation sectors to bolster economic opportunities and connectivity.
9.
Youth Empowerment & Community Development Invest in Youth Programs: Create initiatives that promote education, sports, and arts programs for young people, encouraging positive development and leadership.
Strengthen Community Networks: Support neighborhood revitalization efforts and community-led projects that improve quality of life and foster unity.
Address Food Security: Expand community gardens, food distribution programs, and nutritional education to address hunger and promote healthier lifestyles.
10.
Good Governance & Transparency Strengthen Government Accountability: Promote ethical practices, transparency, and fiscal responsibility across all government sectors.
Foster Civic Engagement: Increase community involvement in decision-making processes through outreach, education, and participation opportunities.
Pursue Territorial Self-Determination: Advocate for the development of a Virgin Islands constitution and further exploration of the territory’s political status.
