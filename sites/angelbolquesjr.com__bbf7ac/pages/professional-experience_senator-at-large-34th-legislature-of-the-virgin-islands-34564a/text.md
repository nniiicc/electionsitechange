His responsibilities encompass: Legislative Duties : Proposing, debating, and voting on laws that impact the Virgin Islands, ensuring they align with the community's needs and aspirations.
Constituent Representation : Serving as a liaison between the residents and the government, addressing concerns, and advocating for resources and services that benefit the populace.
Committee Participation : Actively engaging in legislative committees to scrutinize policies, budgets, and programs, thereby influencing decisions on various sectors such as education, healthcare, and economic development.
Community Engagement : Maintaining a visible presence across all islands, attending events, and fostering relationships to stay attuned to the issues affecting different communities.
