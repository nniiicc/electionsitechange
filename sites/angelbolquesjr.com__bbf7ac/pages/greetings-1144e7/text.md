Bolques Jr. is Senator At Large in the Legislature of the U.S.
Virgin Islands, representing the people of St.
Thomas, St.
John, St.
Croix, and Water Island.
On this official website, you can find Senator Bolques’ legislative work, public updates, policy priorities, community outreach, and information related to the Virgin Islands.
Welcome!
I’m Angel L.
Bolques Jr., a proud son of the Virgin Islands, born on the serene shores of St.
Croix, raised amidst the breathtaking beauty of St.
John, and dedicated to serving the people of our territory from my professional base on St.
Thomas.
My heritage spans seven and a half generations, deeply connecting me to the culture, history, and aspirations of these islands.
Throughout my life and career, I have remained committed to addressing the needs of our community, inspired by the values instilled in me by my family and shaped by my experiences in both the private and public sectors.
My journey has been one of service, collaboration, and unwavering dedication to building a brighter, more inclusive future for the Virgin Islands.
A Legacy of Service and Tradition The principles that guide me were rooted in the examples set by my parents.
My late father, Angel L.
Bolques Sr., exemplified compassion, respect, and hard work—cornerstones of our Puerto-Crucian heritage.
My mother, Patricia Powell-Mitchell, brought the traditions of unity, hospitality, and resilience of St.
John into our home, nurturing a deep faith and spirituality that continues to ground me.
These values drive my commitment to inclusivity, integrity, and meaningful leadership.
As a bilingual Virgin Islander, I am privileged to connect with diverse communities and foster understanding across cultures.
By honoring the legacy of my parents, I strive to serve with compassion and purpose, ensuring that the voices of all Virgin Islanders are heard.
A Career Defined by Versatility and Achievement My career spans a range of impactful roles, each shaping my understanding of the challenges and opportunities unique to the Virgin Islands.
These experiences have equipped me to lead thoughtfully and effectively, always with the well-being of our community in mind.
Senator At Large, Legislature of the Virgin Islands Senator At-Large Angel Bolques Jr working every day to solve real issues affecting our people — from cost of living to infrastructure, housing, and public safety.
As Senator At Large, I am honored to represent the entire Virgin Islands, advocating for legislation that improves lives, strengthens our infrastructure, and promotes equity and progress.
From economic development to cultural preservation, my work reflects the values and priorities of our territory.
My legislative efforts include a broad range of measures aimed at improving various aspects of life in the Virgin Islands.
A Vision for a Thriving Virgin Islands Unwavering Commitment to Prosperity, Resilience, and Inclusive Growth My vision for the Virgin Islands reflects a profound dedication to the well-being of every islander and the prosperity of our territory.
By aligning our values with strategic and transformative initiatives, I am committed to building a community where progress is tangible, and opportunities are accessible to all.
This vision is underpinned by policies that foster economic strength, technological innovation, social equity, and environmental sustainability.
1.
Economic Development & Agriculture Economic resilience and food security are cornerstones of my vision.
I will: Strengthen local agriculture by supporting farmers, fishers, and livestock producers, ensuring access to affordable, nutritious food while reducing reliance on imports.
Expand small business ecosystems through simplified licensing, funding opportunities, and youth-focused innovation programs.
Position the Virgin Islands as a hub for international banking, commerce, and investment to stimulate economic growth.
Develop resilient economic frameworks to adapt to global changes and secure long-term prosperity.
2.
Technological Advancements Embracing the digital age is essential for creating a more connected, efficient, and innovative Virgin Islands.
My focus includes: Establishing robust cybersecurity systems to protect government data and public services.
Expanding digital governance to increase efficiency, accessibility, and transparency.
Exploring digital finance opportunities to modernize our economy and benefit local businesses.
Developing training programs that equip our workforce for emerging tech-driven industries.
3.
Housing & Living Opportunities Every Virgin Islander deserves safe, affordable, and sustainable housing.
My commitment is to: Expand affordable housing options for low-income families, seniors, and workforce members.
Promote environmentally friendly and disaster-resilient urban development.
Advocate for equitable housing policies to ensure accessibility for vulnerable populations.
4.
Education & Cultural Preservation Education is the foundation of progress, and our cultural heritage is our strength.
I aim to: Develop programs that integrate academics with career and technical education for student success.
Preserve and celebrate Virgin Islands history and culture through expanded education initiatives.
Support arts education, cultural programs, and community events that foster creativity and pride.
5.
Reform & Public Safety Safety, justice, and governance reforms are critical to our community’s well-being.
I am dedicated to: Modernizing public safety infrastructure for better emergency response and disaster preparedness.
Advocating for criminal justice reform with fair treatment and rehabilitation opportunities.
Protecting victims’ rights by strengthening support systems and ensuring access to resources.
Continuously modernizing laws for equitable governance.
6.
Health & Social Services Health and social well-being are priorities.
My initiatives will: Expand healthcare services, reduce costs, and improve quality for all residents.
Invest in mental health programs and awareness campaigns.
Enhance elder care services to ensure dignity and quality of life for our aging population.
7.
Environmental Sustainability & Natural Resources Our natural resources are treasures to be preserved for future generations.
I will: Advocate for renewable energy programs to reduce our carbon footprint.
Support conservation efforts to protect ecosystems, beaches, and wildlife.
Develop policies that enhance climate resilience and prevent environmental degradation.
8.
Transportation & Infrastructure Modern infrastructure is vital for connectivity and economic growth.
My vision includes: Expanding and improving public transportation systems for accessibility.
Prioritizing road and utility system upgrades and expansions.
Strengthening the maritime and aviation sectors to boost economic opportunities.
9.
Youth Empowerment & Community Development Our youth are the future, and our communities are the heart of the Virgin Islands.
I will: Invest in education, sports, and arts programs to encourage youth development and leadership.
Strengthen community networks through neighborhood revitalization efforts.
Expand community gardens, food programs, and nutritional education to address hunger and promote health.
10.
Good Governance & Transparency Trust and accountability are essential for effective governance.
I will: Promote ethical practices, transparency, and fiscal responsibility across all sectors.
Foster civic engagement to encourage community participation in decision-making.
Advocate for the development of a Virgin Islands constitution and explore pathways for territorial self-determination.
Embark on a Transformative Journey Together In our quest to elevate the Virgin Islands to new heights of prosperity and well-being, your role is pivotal.
This journey is powered by the collective will and support of each one of you.
As we stand at the crossroads of change, your voice and your vote become the catalysts for a new era.
I stand ready to be your champion in these crucial times.
My commitment is to bring innovative ideas and positive solutions to the forefront, ensuring a prosperous future for all Virgin Islanders.
Together, we can build a brighter and more inclusive future for our beloved islands.
