FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Sioux Falls, South Dakota, December 4, 2025—District 9 State House Representative Tesa Schwans today announced her re-election campaign for a second term in 2026.
District 9 encompasses the communities of Northwest Sioux Falls, Hartford, and Wall Lake.
“I’m incredibly honored to serve as your District 9 representative,” said Schwans.
“Over my first term, I’ve enjoyed connecting with the many people of our district and across South Dakota.
I’ve learned so much over the last year—importantly, the challenges are more urgent, the problems more complex, and the stakes higher than ever.
There’s still important work to be done, and I humbly ask for your support as I seek a second term.”
Schwans, of rural Hartford—a licensed cosmetologist and small business owner in Sioux Falls—was elected to her first term as a conservative Republican in 2024, receiving 38.5 percent of the vote—the highest share among three candidates in the general election.
Her victory reflected broad, grassroots support.
“In my three decades as a hairstylist and instructor, I’ve had the privilege of building relationships with people from many walks of life–and gained insight into their needs and challenges,” she added.
“I’m not a career politician.
I’m a community-minded, concerned citizen who stepped up.
I listen, and I care.”
In her first legislative session, Schwans served on the Commerce & Energy and Education Committees and on the Teacher Compensation Review Board.
She sponsored right-to-work legislation with HB 1232, expanding booth rental opportunities for independent salon professionals, and championed property-rights protections, pro-life policy, and reforms to licensing and regulatory frameworks.
Known as a Constitutional Patriot for faith, family, and freedom, Schwans prioritizes protecting individual freedoms, defending families, safeguarding taxpayers, and supporting safe communities.
She has also advocated for at-risk youth, rehabilitation programs, and meaningful education reform.
“Guided by faith and conservative principles, I remain committed to serving with integrity and defending the values that make South Dakota strong,” Schwans said.
Schwans and her husband, Jay, live near Wall Lake with their bernedoodle Wynston.
They have three children and nine grandchildren and enjoy boating, fishing, and the outdoors.
