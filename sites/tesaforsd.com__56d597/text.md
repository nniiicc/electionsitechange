Caring for Families.
Families are the foundation of a thriving society.
Everything strong and lasting is built from the home outward.
As a wife, daughter, sister, mother, and grandmother, my family inspired me to step into public service.
FAMILIES ARE THE FOUNDATION At the heart of this vision are pro-life, pro-family values and respect for the God-given worth of every life.
Supporting mothers and babies, adoption, marriage, and responsible parenting strengthens the stability our communities depend on.
NURTURING SAFETY & WELLNESS FOR ALL Caring for families also means fostering safe, healthy communities by promoting spiritual, physical, and mental well-being.
Addressing the root causes of crime, homelessness, and substance abuse — through awareness, accountability, and pathways to restoration.
EMPOWERING CITIZENS TO SHAPE THEIR FUTURE Opportunity is essential to family well-being.
Families thrive when they can earn a living, shape their future, and access pathways to success.
Free enterprise, job training, entrepreneurship, and educational choice open doors and help build strong futures.
When we care for families, communities become safer, healthier, and more prosperous.
When we lift up families, we lift up every generation that follows.
Committed to Freedom.
Grounded in faith, inspired by my family, and driven by a deep love of freedom, I believe public service means standing up for what is right–even when the path is difficult–defending civil liberties, and ensuring every voice is heard and valued.
Every decision should prioritize the rights, voices, and freedoms of individuals over centralized control or political agendas.
FREEDOM OVER CONTROL My leadership is rooted in loyalty to the Constitution—not to parties, personalities, or special interests, but to the enduring American principles of liberty, justice, and equality under the law.
I am steadfast in protecting and preserving a strong republic.
PRINCIPLES OVER POLITICS I take pride in our nation’s history, its values, and its people.
I champion transparent, accountable government, and state authority—where power belongs to the people and decisions are made openly and honestly.
PEOPLE OVER POWER Above all, I believe in active, engaged citizenship: listening to constituents, respecting diverse viewpoints, and building common ground rooted in shared values.
For me, patriotism is not about division—it is about devotion to country, care for community, and the courage to lead with conviction.
