Idaho Girl
As a native Idahoan and a thoughtful conservative, I care deeply about the future of our communities and the families who call our state home.
My life has been rooted in service—whether supporting my husband during his nine years in the Army, raising our six children, or working alongside parents and teachers as a PTA president.
Through these experiences, I’ve seen firsthand the challenges Idaho families face and the strength of our communities when people step up to serve.
I believe our state legislature should be made up of people who listen, work hard, and focus on practical solutions that help Idaho families thrive.
I’m running for the Idaho State Legislature because I want to bring a strong voice for families, schools, and our community to the Capitol—and continue a lifetime commitment to serving others.
Meet Desi Burbank
So, Why Desi?
My nine years as an Army wife shaped the way I see the world and strengthened my appreciation for service, sacrifice, and duty.
Standing beside my husband as he served our country was one of the greatest privileges of my life.
It taught me the importance of community, because military families depend on one another through every challenge and every celebration.
Our Army friends became family, and we are blessed to have lifelong friendships around the world.
Army Wife
I grew up in Eastern Idaho, and I’m proud to be a Sugar-Salem Digger.
In my family, when the farmers had a bad year, we had a bad year.
Those experiences taught me early what it means to depend on the land, to work hard, and to understand that success and struggle are shared in rural communities.
I was in 4th grade when I discovered my grandfather, former U.S.
Ambassador to Finland Keith Nyborg, in my Idaho history book.
Learning about his service at such a young age left a lasting impression on me, and I strive to follow in his footsteps through a life of service to others.
Community Servant
Nothing has taught me more about service and sacrifice than being a mother.
As a mother of six, I’ve experienced the same challenges Idaho families face every day, from making ends meet and finding quality healthcare to helping our children succeed in school and keeping our communities safe.
Those experiences have given me a perspective that I carry with me every day.
