Latest Spot Features Vivek’s Boyhood Barber Donnie Judd
COLUMBUS — The Vivek Ramaswamy for Governor campaign has launched its latest ad, “DJ’s Barbershop – Cincinnati,” featuring Vivek’s boyhood barber, Donnie Judd.
Known to most as “DJ,” Judd cut Vivek’s hair for much of his childhood at DJ’s Barbershop in Sharonville, just north of Cincinnati.
Vivek has credited DJ as the first entrepreneur he ever knew, and as someone who taught him lasting lessons about business, service, and life.
Vivek recently returned to the shop to introduce his family to DJ.
In a full-circle moment, DJ gave Vivek’s sons their first trim in the same barbershop where he had cut their father’s hair years before.
“DJ’s Barbership – Cincinnati” is now airing statewide on streaming, broadcast and cable.
