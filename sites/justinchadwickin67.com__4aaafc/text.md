top of page Donate Volunteer About Me The Issues Home Justin is a Hoosier who believes in serving his community and doing honest, practical work.
He currently works in the plumbing field , where he understands the value of skilled trades, reliability, and showing up for people when they need help.
Before that, Justin spent years as a music educator and special education teacher , experiences that shaped his strong belief in public education and supporting students of all abilities.
Justin loves being involved in his community and is proud to participate in the Jennings County Players , supporting local theatre and the arts.
For him, community isn’t just where you live, it’s what you contribute to.
He’s a graduate of Jennings County High School and the University of Indianapolis, and he attends First United Methodist Church, where faith, service, and compassion help guide his values.
Justin is also proud to share life with his wife, public school educator, Paige Chadwick, a talented, supportive, and important part of his journey.
And we can’t forget Loki, the fun loving pup that stands by their side!
Justin is running to bring steady, common-sense leadership to District 67: focused on strong schools, working families, and bringing people together .
