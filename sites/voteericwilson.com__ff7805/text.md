About Eric Wilson Eric Wilson is a paramedic, a businessman, and a proven leader who's always answered the call to serve Middle Georgia.
As a paramedic, that call came over the radio — a stranger in crisis, and Eric showing up, no questions asked about who they were or what they believed.
As a small business owner leading MedPro EMS ambulance service, that call came from a community that needed reliable service it could count on.
As Mayor of Forsyth, Eric partnered with residents from diverse backgrounds and political perspectives to achieve meaningful progress for the community because as mayor, every call came from a constituent with a real problem.
Through Eric's entire career: he shows up, and he delivers, for everyone.
Not just the people who voted for him.
Not just the people who agree with him.
Everyone.
Now Eric is stepping up to serve as our advocate in Atlanta, ready to protect the middle Georgia values that make our community great, and ready to answer the call to fight for us.
