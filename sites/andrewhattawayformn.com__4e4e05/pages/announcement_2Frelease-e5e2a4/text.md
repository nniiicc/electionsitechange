Hattaway is running on a platform centered on fiscal responsibility, government accountability, strong local schools, and affordable healthcare for Minnesota families. “ At a time when Minnesotans are feeling the impact of reckless state spending and repeated failures to address fraud, we need steady, common-sense leadership in St.
Paul, ” said Hattaway. “ I’m running to make sure our community has a representative who listens, asks tough questions, and puts taxpayers and families first. ” Hattaway’s campaign draws a sharp contrast to the DFL’s record of unchecked spending growth and ongoing fraud in state programs, which has strained the state budget and undermined public trust.
He believes restoring accountability and discipline in state government is essential to protecting taxpayers and ensuring Minnesota remains affordable for working families.
Hattaway has outlined his campaign around three core priorities: Fiscal responsibility and combating fraud, supporting education and local schools, and affordable healthcare.
Andrew Hattaway lives in Eagan with his family and works as a strategic procurement manager for a Minnesota-based commercial construction company.
His professional background has given him firsthand experience balancing budgets, managing costs, and making responsible, long-term decisions, skills he believes are badly needed at the Capitol.
