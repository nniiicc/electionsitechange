I am running for the Minnesota House of Representatives because our community deserves a representative who brings real world experience, common sense leadership, and a genuine commitment to protecting the future of our neighborhoods.
My career in procurement has taught me how to manage budgets responsibly, prevent waste, and ensure taxpayer dollars are used the way they are intended.
I have seen firsthand how fraud and mismanagement drain resources from the programs families rely on, and I am committed to restoring accountability and transparency in state spending.
As a former firefighter, I know what it means to put community first and make critical decisions under pressure.
That experience drives my commitment to strengthening public safety and ensuring our first responders have the support they need to keep our neighborhoods secure.
As a parent with children in our local schools, I understand the importance of strong classrooms, dedicated teachers, and policies that give every student a chance to succeed.
Education is the foundation of our community, and it deserves to be protected and strengthened.
For the families of District 56B, my focus is clear: responsible spending and combating fraud, supporting education and our local schools, improving affordability for working families, and ensuring safe communities.
These are not partisan ideas.
They are practical priorities rooted in the values we share and the future we want to build together.
This campaign is about all of us.
It is about building a stronger, safer, and more affordable community where every family can thrive.
I am running because I believe in the power of neighbors working together, and I am ready to earn your trust as we move forward.
Together, we can build a future where our tax dollars are protected, our schools are strong, our communities are safe, and every family has the opportunity to succeed.
