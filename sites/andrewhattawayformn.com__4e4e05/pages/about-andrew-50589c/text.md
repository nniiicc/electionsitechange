I’m Andrew Hattaway.
I’m a husband, father, Army veteran, former firefighter, and procurement professional.
My family and I live here in the south metro, and I’m proud to be part of the communities that make up this area.

Outside of work, most of my time is spent with my family and staying involved in the community.

I’m a straightforward person.

I’m not a career politician.
