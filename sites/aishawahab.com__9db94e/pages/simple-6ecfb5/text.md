Aisha Wahab knows what it means to be priced out, counted out, and forced to fight for every opportunity.
From foster care to the State Senate, she has taken on powerful interests, lowered costs, expanded housing, protected healthcare, improved public safety, and demanded accountability from government.
In Congress, Aisha will fight to make life affordable again — lower costs, protect Social Security and Medicare, build housing working families can afford, defend our freedoms, and put people before politics.
It’s Simple
1.
Make Life Affordable
Drive down costs on the basics: housing, healthcare, childcare, utilities, groceries, gas, and prescription drugs.
Aisha will fight to:
- Break up corporate monopolies and crack down on price-gouging.
- Lower utility bills and hold energy companies accountable.
- End corporate profiteering that raises the cost of food, gas, housing, and healthcare.
- Raise the federal minimum wage so work pays enough to live.
- Expand paid family leave and universal childcare so people can afford to work, raise families, and care for loved ones.
People are working harder than ever and still falling behind.
Aisha will fight to lower costs, raise wages, and make sure the American Dream is not reserved for the wealthy and well-connected.
2.
Healthcare for Every American
Healthcare should be a right — not a bill that bankrupts a family.
Aisha will fight to:
- Pass Medicare for All healthcare.
- Lower prescription drug costs.
- Protect Medicare, Medicaid, and the Affordable Care Act.
- Restore funding for programs families rely on, including Medicaid and SNAP.
- Protect reproductive healthcare nationwide, expand contraception access, and defend family planning.
No senior should have to work a minimum-wage job just to survive.
No family should go broke because someone gets sick.
Aisha will fight for healthcare for all, lower prescription drug costs, and protect Medicare, Medicaid, and Social Security.
3.
Housing People Can Actually Afford
Build more affordable housing, protect renters, and help working families become homeowners.
Aisha will fight to:
- Increase federal funding for affordable housing.
- Expand first-time homebuyer assistance.
- Invest in social housing, workforce housing, and deeply affordable housing.
- Protect renters from displacement and corporate landlord abuse.
- Make it easier to build housing near jobs, transit, schools, and services.
Aisha knows what it means to lose a home and still be a renter fighting for the dream of homeownership.
In Congress, she’ll fight for housing working people can actually afford.
4.
Protect Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid, and SNAP
Protect the programs millions of Americans earned, paid into, and rely on.
Aisha will fight to:
- Protect Social Security from privatization.
- Defend Medicare and Medicaid from cuts.
- Restore and protect SNAP and food assistance.
- Strengthen benefits for seniors, people with disabilities, veterans, children, and working families.
Social Security is not a handout — it is an earned benefit.
Aisha will fight anyone who tries to privatize it, cut it, or gamble seniors’ retirement on Wall Street.
5.
Good Jobs, Small Businesses, and the Economy of the Future
Create opportunity in a changing economy while making sure workers are not left behind.
Aisha will fight to:
- Create good-paying union jobs.
- Revitalize small businesses with targeted tax relief and incentives for local hiring and livable wages.

- Provide high-speed internet nationwide.

- Provide free tuition for community college, trades, and occupational training.
The economy should reward work, not just wealth.
Aisha will fight for good-paying jobs, strong small businesses, workforce training, and an economy where working people can actually get ahead.
6.
Education and Opportunity
Every child deserves a strong public education, and every adult deserves a pathway to a good career.
Aisha will fight to:
- Protect Title I and IDEA funding.
- Expand tutoring, after-school, and summer programs.
- Provide free community college.
- Provide free tuition for trades and occupational training.
- Cancel student debt and stop predatory student lending.
Education should open doors, not bury families in debt.
Aisha will fight for strong public schools, debt relief, free community college, and career training that leads to good-paying jobs.
7.
Build for the 21st Century
Repair roads, bridges, transit, water systems, broadband, and clean-energy infrastructure while creating good-paying jobs.
Aisha will fight to:
- Repair roads, bridges, and major public works.
- Fund robust public transit to reduce traffic, pollution, and household transportation costs.
- Invest in renewable energy infrastructure.
- Reduce dependence on fossil fuels.
- Protect clean air and safe drinking water.
- Hold polluters accountable.
- Invest in wildfire mitigation, smoke readiness, extreme-heat protections, and local disaster resilience.
We can lower costs, create good-paying jobs, reduce pollution, and build safer communities at the same time.
Aisha will fight for infrastructure that works for people — not polluters and special interests.
8.
Human Rights, Civil Rights, and Safe Communities
Defend freedom, equal protection, due process, and the dignity of every person.
Aisha will fight to:
- Protect reproductive freedom nationwide.
- Expand equal protection under the law.
- Strengthen enforcement against discrimination in housing, employment, education, and healthcare.

10.
Foreign Policy That End Endless Wars & Invest in People at Home
Stop wasting taxpayer dollars on endless wars and invest in people here at home.
Aisha will fight to:
- End pointless wars that drain taxpayer dollars and drive up fuel, food, and household costs.
- Put diplomacy, peace, human rights, and accountability first.
- Oppose blank checks to foreign governments without transparency, oversight, or clear public interest.
- Ensure taxpayer dollars are spent on Americans’ needs: housing, healthcare, education, seniors, veterans, children, and working families.
- Reject war profiteering and hold defense contractors accountable.
- Bring a people-first foreign policy rooted in peace, safety, dignity, and human rights.
If Washington can find billions for war, it can find the money to feed people, house families, care for seniors, and rebuild America.
Working people are being told there is no money for healthcare, housing, schools, childcare, or Social Security — while Washington spends billions on endless wars and defense contractors.
Aisha will fight to end blank checks, stop war profiteering, and put our tax dollars back into the people and communities that need them most.

Real results.
