About Tim
Pragmatic Conservative.
During his time in the New Hampshire House, Tim earned a reputation as a conservative leader and a pragmatic consensus builder — willing to work with anyone to find the best solution for Granite State families.
A proud father of four, Tim has watched his children grow through New Hampshire's public schools and university system.
That perspective grounds his work in the Senate: every vote should leave the next generation freer, safer, and more prosperous than the last.
Core Priorities
A legislative record built on results, not rhetoric — moving New Hampshire forward.
Tax Relief
Defending the New Hampshire Advantage by opposing any broad-based sales or income tax and easing the burden on working families and small businesses.
Local Control
Empowering our towns to make their own decisions on education, zoning, and public safety — closest to the people they serve.
World-Class Education
Every Granite State child deserves the best possible start.
That means investing in our public schools while empowering parents with real choice in how their children learn.
Lower Energy Costs
Bringing down utility bills by expanding our energy portfolio — including investing in renewables — diversifying supply, and protecting ratepayers from runaway expenses.
