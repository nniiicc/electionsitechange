PRESS RELEASE
Adam Gray Issues Statement on CA-13 Primary Election Results
MERCED, CA – Today, Congressman Adam Gray (CA-13) issued the following statement in response to primary election results in California's 13th Congressional District:
“I appreciate the strong support from voters across California’s 13th District,” said Congressman Adam Gray.
“It means a lot to represent the Valley community where I was born and raised.
Our campaign is focused on putting the Valley first and earning support from Democrats, Republicans, and Independents alike by staying focused on what matters here: lowering costs, supporting working families, and getting government working better.”
