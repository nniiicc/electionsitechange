Senator Gladys A.
Robinson has consistently demonstrated inclusive, effective, and responsible leadership during her tenure in the North Carolina State Senate.
Recognizing the pressing need for affordable healthcare, Senator Robinson has been instrumental in championing policies that prioritize the well-being of all North Carolinians.
Her dedication to quality education is evident through her tireless efforts to secure funding for public schools and expand opportunities for students.
Senator Robinson has also been a steadfast advocate for women's rights, fighting for equal pay, reproductive healthcare access, and combating gender-based violence.
Her commitment to these critical issues sets her apart as a leader who is not only well-informed and experienced but also deeply compassionate and responsive to the needs of her constituents.

Meet Senator Robinson
Senator Robinson has served seven terms in the North Carolina State Senate.
She has led the fight for Medicaid Expansion beginning in 2012, for Leandro Funding for public schools, and has been the strongest voice in higher education, especially funding for HBCUs.
Senator Robinson has worked hard for NC A&T and UNCG and is the former chair of the Bennett College Board of Trustees, home of the Women's Business Center.
She advocates for women's rights, equal pay, and support for small/minority businesses.
Senator Robinson is constantly fighting for the voiceless and her track record shows that.
