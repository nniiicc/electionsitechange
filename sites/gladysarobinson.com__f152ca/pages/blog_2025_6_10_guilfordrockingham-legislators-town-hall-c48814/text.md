Guilford/Rockingham Legislators Town Hall
Yesterday at the Guilford/Rockingham town hall, I was honored to listen and engage with constituents on voting rights, education, and the policies shaping our future.
It was inspiring to hear firsthand the questions and concerns of community members, from protecting civil liberties to strengthening our schools.
I’m committed to carrying these conversations back to Raleigh and working tirelessly to turn your priorities into action.
If you missed the event, you can still find your legislators and stay involved by visiting ncleg.gov/findyourlegislators.
