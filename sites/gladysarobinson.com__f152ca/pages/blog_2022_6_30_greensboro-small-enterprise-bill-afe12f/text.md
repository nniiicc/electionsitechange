Greensboro Small Enterprise Bill
On June 30th, 2022, I presented the Greensboro Small Business Enterprise bill in the Rules Committee and the bill received unanimous approval.
I will continue to be an advocate for small businesses because they support and are invested in the well-being of our local communities.
It’s important that we continue to support small businesses, who help to stimulate local economies and invest in local communities by providing jobs to those who live in those communities.
