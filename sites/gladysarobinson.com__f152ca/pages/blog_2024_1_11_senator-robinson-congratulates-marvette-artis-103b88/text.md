Senator Robinson congratulates Marvette Artis
Congratulations to President Marvette Artis!
On Tuesday, January 9, Marvette Artis was ceremoniously sworn in as the 2024 Greensboro Regional Realtors Association (GRRA) President.
The Greensboro Regional Realtors Association is dedicated to bolstering its members' professional endeavors through the advancement of ethical standards and providing top-notch services, education, and real estate insights within our local market.
President Artis, following her inauguration, provided valuable foresight into the anticipated trends of the 2024 Piedmont housing market.
Despite the resurgence observed in the Guilford County housing sector, the lingering effects of the COVID-19 pandemic and inflation rates persist in influencing inventory levels and interest rates.
Once again, congratulations to President Artis and best wishes for continued success in her endeavors.
