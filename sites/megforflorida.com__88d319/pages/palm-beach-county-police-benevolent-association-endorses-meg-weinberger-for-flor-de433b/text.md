Palm Beach Gardens, Florida – April 26, 2024 – Today, the Palm Beach County Police Benevolent Association (PBA), representing over 5,000 dedicated law enforcement professionals, announced its endorsement of Meg Weinberger for Florida State House District 94.
The PBA expressed its confidence in Meg Weinberger’s commitment to ensuring the safety and security of residents of Florida’s District 94.
The endorsement highlights Weinberger’s unwavering dedication to supporting law enforcement and her commitment to enhance public safety.
“We will ask all our members, their families and friends — and all citizens of State of Florida District 94 who respect the strong and efficient enforcement of our laws, to cast their ballots in support of Meg Weinberger,” stated Palm Beach County PBA President John Kazanjian.
In response to the endorsement, Meg Weinberger expressed her gratitude, stating, “I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of the Palm Beach County Police Benevolent Association.
I have the utmost respect for the brave men and women who serve in law enforcement, and I am committed to working tirelessly to ensure they have the resources and support they need to keep our communities and our residents safe.
The public safety of District 94 is my highest priority.
I will never waiver in fighting for President Trump’s America First and Governor Ron DeSantis’ Florida First policies that prioritize public safety, support law enforcement, and address the needs of District 94 residents.”
