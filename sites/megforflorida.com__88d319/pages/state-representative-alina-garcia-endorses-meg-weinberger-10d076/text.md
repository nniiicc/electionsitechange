WEST PALM BEACH— State Representative Alina Garcia (R) has lent her support and endorsement to help Meg’s effort to continue protecting “parental rights” and “economic prosperity” for the citizens of Florida.
“I proudly endorse Meg Weinberger in Florida’s open House District 94 seat.
Meg has all the tools and conservative bona fides needed to propel the legislature forward in its efforts to continue leading the nation on parental rights, economic prosperity, and defending and protecting our God-given freedoms and liberties,” said Rep.
Garcia.
In response to Rep.
Garcia’s endorsement, Weinberger thanked Rep.
Garcia for extending her support for her state house campaign.
“Our campaign’s momentum to preserve the American way of life for Floridians continues to grow.
Thank you, Rep.
Garcia, for your service and for believing in our America First, Florida First, and pro-family campaign,” said Weinberger.
