PALM BEACH, Fla.
(CBS12) —
Forever Family was recently invited with our Forever Family foster kids to a special place in Palm Beach where all the children got to care for and interact with rescue animals.
Meg Weinberger is the founder of Rescue Life.
Her life has been a journey of love for both people and animals alike.
“So, growing up, my family, we rescued anything that pretty much needed a home,” explains Rescue Life founder Meg Weinberger.
“Two of my sisters were adopted, one with special needs; so, I’ve always had a passion of kind of merging the world of foster kids, special needs, and animals together.”
