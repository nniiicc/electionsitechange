Palm Beach Gardens, FL, April 10, 2024 – Meg Weinberger, candidate for State Representative in Florida’s 94th District, is proud to announce today’s endorsement of her campaign by The Florida Fraternal Order of Police , representing more than 24,000 law enforcement officers.
Mr.
Steve Zona, President of the Florida Fraternal Order of Police said, “As we approach the 2024 elections we are aware that strong leaders are needed to lead Florida to a prosperous and safe future.” “We need leaders that are dedicated to serving their constituents and protecting the citizens in their community.
Meg Weinberger is the choice of the men and women in FOP District Four,” concluded Mr.
Zona.
Meg Weinberger said, “ I am so thrilled to have earned the endorsement from the Fraternal Order of Police, “The Voice of Florida’s Law Enforcement Officers.” I am proud to affirm the “law and order” policies of common-sense America First principles.
Ms.
Weinberger continued, “I stand proudly with our valiant law enforcement officers to protect Florida communities, deliver justice for victims, protect animals, and keep violence off of our streets and out of our neighborhoods.
