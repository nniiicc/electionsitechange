BREAKING NEWS: U.S.
Congressman Gus Bilirakis Endorses Republican Meg Weinberger for FL District 94 State Representative
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Palm Beach Gardens (March 28, 2024) – Today, U.S.
Congressman Gus Bilirakis (FL-12) announced his endorsement of Republican Meg Weinberger in the race for Florida’s 94th House District.
With a longstanding record of dedicated service to the State of Florida and the nation, Congressman Bilirakis’ endorsement stands as a testament to positive change, effective leadership, and commitment to common-sense, America First policies that must be returned to our communities and the country as a whole to restore security and prosperity.
It is important that Republicans continue to vote into office good, honest, respectable, and principled leaders to the Florida Legislature
Congressman Bilirakis was first elected to Congress on November 7, 2006.
He serves as a Senior Member of the Energy and Commerce Committee.
He is the Chairman of the Innovation, Data and Commerce Subcommittee.
Gus is also a Senior Member of the Health Subcommittee and the Communications and Technology Subcommittee.
With 75 bills authored and signed into law since 2015, Congressman Bilirakis was recently designated as the Most Effective Republican Lawmaker in the State of Florida by the Center for Effective Lawmaking at Vanderbilt University.
“It is important that Republicans continue to vote into office good, honest, respectable, and principled leaders to the Florida Legislature,” Bilirakis said.
“I fully support Meg Weinberger’s campaign to help Republican leadership continue winning for Floridians on both the political and social fronts.”
In addition to Congressman Bilirakis, Weinberger has received major endorsements from Veterans for Trump, and Florida State Representative Alina Garcia.
“I am deeply honored and humbled to receive the endorsement of Congressman Bilirakis.
His unmatched service to our nation, tireless dedication to our state and unwavering commitment to public service make his support invaluable.
I am truly grateful to have the confidence and endorsement of such a distinguished and respected Florida and national leader,” said Weinberger.
