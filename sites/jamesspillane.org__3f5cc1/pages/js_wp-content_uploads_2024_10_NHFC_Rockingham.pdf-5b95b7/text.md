The NHFC is pleased to offer this Report Card for Congress, NH Governor, Executive Council, State Senate and Representative races.
The State Representative races in this issue are all in Rockingham County.
They are laid out by county and district number.
These grades are the result of each candidate’s answers to an extensive questionnaire and any available voting records.
In addition, our grade may also use any other reliable information regarding a candidate’s views on Second Amendment issues, such as a downgrade for public support of other anti-gun candidates.
For other Counties please see our website. http://www.nhfc-ontarget.org/ The NHFC, Inc. hopes that you will find this information useful when you vote in our state’s General Elections on November 5h.
