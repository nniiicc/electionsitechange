Legislation Signed Into Law
Through strong leadership and a focus on practical solutions, Senator Seyarto has successfully authored and passed legislation addressing some of the state’s most pressing challenges.
Protecting Communities & Public Safety
Senator Seyarto has taken decisive action to improve public safety and respond to emerging threats:
- SB 19 (2023) established California’s Fentanyl Task Force, bringing together state and local leaders to combat the growing fentanyl crisis.
- SB 67 (2023) implemented real-time overdose tracking to help first responders save lives.
- SB 78 (2025) works to improve roadway safety and reduce dangerous project delays across the state.
- SB 602 (2023) strengthened tools for law enforcement and property owners to address trespassing and criminal activity.
- SB 86 (2023) requires a comprehensive website that provides crime victims with easy access to essential information and services.
This is an expansion of Marsy’s Law (2008).
- AB 1968 (2022) requires CSU and UC campuses to make sexual assault resources easier to find, ensuring victims can quickly access critical support when they need it most.
Standing Up for Veterans & Military Families
A longtime advocate for those who serve, Senator Seyarto has championed policies that honor and support our Veterans:
- SB 67 (2025) expanded Cal Grant eligibility for dependents of active-duty military members stationed outside California.
- SB 82 (2023) ensured fair and consistent access to disabled Veterans’ property tax exemptions.
- SB 920 (2024) supports military families by continuing the Purple Star School Program, helping children navigate the challenges of frequent relocations and deployments.
- AB 1633 (2022) improved coordination to connect student Veterans with critical benefits and services.
Major Win: After years of advocacy, Senator Seyarto helped secure tax relief for Veterans and surviving spouses, allowing up to $20,000 of military retirement or Survivor Benefit Plan income to be excluded from state taxes each year.
Increasing Transparency & Government Accountability
Senator Seyarto believes government should be open, accessible, and accountable to the people:
- SB 259 (2023) requires state agencies to post reports submitted to the Legislature online, making important public information easier to access.
- SB 978 (2024) made California’s state budget more accessible and easier for the public to understand.
- SB 1034 (2024) ensures transparency continues even during emergencies, while allowing flexibility to protect public safety.
Protecting Consumers & Property Owners
From fraud prevention to clearer communication, Senator Seyarto has prioritized protecting Californians:
- SB 255 (2025) created a statewide notification system to alert property owners of recorded ownership changes, helping prevent fraud.
- SB 1096 (2024) requires clear labeling on mailed solicitations to protect consumers from misleading materials.
- SB 520 (2023) clarified property tax protections for homeowners in medical care facilities.
Supporting Education, Nonprofits & Local Communities
Senator Seyarto has delivered practical solutions that support everyday Californians:
- AB 1876 (2022) expanded pathways to address teacher shortages by supporting substitute teachers.
- SB 1044 (2024) updated nonprofit fundraising rules to keep pace with rising costs.
- SB 87 (2025) extended tax relief for volunteer fire departments, helping protect local communities.
Cutting Red Tape & Improving Government Efficiency
Focused on making government work better.
- SB 233 (2025) improves coordination between state and local governments by requiring earlier collaboration on regional housing plans, giving communities more time and clarity to plan for future growth.
- SB 385 (2025) restored flexibility in law enforcement training standards.
- AB 2270 (2022) streamlined toll access for emergency vehicles, improving response times.
- SB 613 (2023) cut unnecessary red tape by exempting small local jurisdictions from burdensome organic waste recycling mandates that were never intended to apply to them.
A Record of Results
From fighting the fentanyl crisis to protecting homeowners and delivering tax relief for Veterans, Senator Seyarto’s record reflects a clear priority:
Putting California families, communities, and taxpayers first.
