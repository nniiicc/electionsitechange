She has dedicated her career to making life better for families through education, advocacy, and justice.
With over 15 years of experience, Staci knows how to fight and win for the people she serves.
Staci doesn’t just talk about solutions—she delivers them.
Her record shows she won’t take no for an answer when it comes to protecting her community.
She brings a relentless spirit, an inquisitive mind, and a fierce power to every fight in the Capitol.
PROVEN RESULTS
- First State Board of Education member in Texas history to successfully secure millions of dollars for elementary school intervention.
