Faith & Families Public Safety & Veterans Economic Opportunity Strong families are the foundation of strong communities.
Samie supports policies that protect parental involvement, strengthen faith-based partnerships, and ensure children have access to safe and supportive environments to grow and succeed.
With 22 years of military service as a Sergeant First Class, Samie understands the importance of safety and service.
He supports first responders, advocates for veterans and their families, and believes every neighborhood deserves to feel secure.
Samie supports job creation, workforce development, and small business growth across DeKalb County.
He believes opportunity should be accessible to every family and every community in District 116.
Samie Conyers believes leadership should be visible, accountable, and focused on results that improve everyday life for the people of District 116.
