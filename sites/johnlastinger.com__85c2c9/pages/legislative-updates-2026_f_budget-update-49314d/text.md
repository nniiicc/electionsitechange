8037102154 Home Legislative Updates 2026 Legislative update - 2026 All Posts Budget Update May 8, 2026 This past Wednesday, I once again voted NO on the state budget.
It came back to the House from the Senate with even more earmarks added in, along with a pay raise for legislators.
At a time when our roads need repair for safety, infrastructure issues continue to grow, and families and businesses are asking for tax relief, I simply could not support a budget that contained many excessive and unnecessary expenses.
We had an opportunity to focus on things that truly matter to South Carolinians, such as education, veterans, first responders, law enforcement, safer roads, and critical infrastructure.
Instead, the budget included projects many taxpayers would likely question, including $158,000 for festivals, $31,500 for folklife showcases, $133 million bailout for Scout Motors, a $32 million Kiawah bailout, and other nonessential spending.
As your representative and as a taxpayer myself, I was frustrated, shocked, and disappointed with some of the wasteful spending in this budget and the lack of concern from many other legislators.
I want my constituents to know that I was against a legislative pay increase and the large earmarks that I felt were unnecessary.
I believe South Carolinians want the government to be fiscally responsible with taxpayers’ hard earned money, not wasteful.
I hope moving forward we can come together to put the needs of our citizens first.
