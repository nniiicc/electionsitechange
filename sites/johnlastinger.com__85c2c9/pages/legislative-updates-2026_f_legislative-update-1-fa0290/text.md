8037102154 Home Legislative Updates 2026 Legislative update - 2026 All Posts Legislative Update May 12, 2026 As we enter the final week of the regular legislative session, here are a few highlights from the State House last week: The House passed the Senate’s expanded Heritage Act (S.508) to strengthen protections for monuments and statues, and we ratified a constitutional amendment (S.582) affirming that only citizens can vote in our elections.
The Senate also passed H.4804, which increases penalties for the sexual exploitation of minors to better protect children and keep our communities safe.
I was proud to co-sponsor this bill, and it now heads to the Governor’s desk.
I’m also pleased the Ivermectin bill passed the House by unanimous consent.
I co-sponsored this legislation as well.
The bill now moves to the Senate.
While I have concerns about portions of the state budget and already addressed that, I do support the investments we made in public safety, law enforcement, teacher pay, school safety, etc.
I’m also continuing to advocate for the Senate’s Homestead Exemption proposal, which would triple the exemption and provide substantial property tax relief for seniors.
Back home in District 88, I’m happy to report that a traffic signal is planned for the intersection of Princeton Road and Highway 302.
I will continue working to improve traffic safety and flow in our community.
I was also honored to attend National Day of Prayer events at the State House and in Irmo, and to help recognize the White Knoll High School Archery Team for winning the State Championship..
Thank you for the opportunity to serve District 88.
