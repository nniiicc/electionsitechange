8037102154 Home Legislative Updates 2026 Legislative update - 2026 All Posts Legislative Update April 29, 2026 It was an honor to recognize one of our own citizens of District 88, Chaplain Colonel Brian L.
Bohlman, on his retirement from the Air Force National Guard after 34 years of faithful service.
We’re grateful for his leadership and dedication.
It was also a privilege to attend Lexington One’s Military Signing Day to recognize high school seniors who have committed to serve in our great United States military.
We’re proud of their dedication, courage, and willingness to serve, and grateful for their families.
God bless our troops.
At the State House, I continue to serve on the Military and Veterans Affairs Subcommittee and the 3M Committee.
The Senate has passed the Homestead Exemption bill, which would triple the exemption and provide real property tax relief to seniors.
I fully support it and believe it should come to the House floor for a vote without delay.
Last week, the House unanimously passed several important bills: • H.3408, Foreign Ownership of Real Estate, to protect our agricultural land from foreign adversaries • H.5538, the Guarantee Banking Act, to prevent citizens from being de-banked for political views • H.5201, the Safe Schools Act, to strengthen school safety planning and emergency response I signed on as a co-sponsor to H.5483, the Educator Safety and Classroom Authority Act, to help address classroom behavior and discipline in our schools.
My list of road concerns with SCDOT continues to grow.
I recently met with several SCDOT department heads about the need for repaving, repainting lines, and new traffic lights.
Please know that I am advocating for road improvements in District 88.
It is a process, so keep reaching out if you have issues you’d like to be included.
As always, it’s an honor to serve our community and the great citizens of District 88.
