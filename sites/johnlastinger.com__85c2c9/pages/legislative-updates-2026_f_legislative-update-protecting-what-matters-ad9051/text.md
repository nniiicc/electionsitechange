8037102154 Home Legislative Updates 2026 Legislative update - 2026 All Posts Legislative Update: Protecting What Matters April 21, 2026 Over the past two weeks, we’ve worked hard in the House to advance legislation that supports families, protects rights, and reflects the values of our community.
Here’s a quick update: Student Physical Privacy Act (H.4756) – Co-sponsored.
Protects privacy in school restrooms, locker rooms, and changing facilities by ensuring these spaces are designated based on biological sex.
The House concurred with the Senate amendment, and it is now headed to the Governor’s desk.
H.5111 – Co-sponsored.
Protects property rights by allowing landowners the freedom to install a private well instead of being required to connect to city water.
S.32 – Provides tax incentives to support pregnancy resource centers that care for mothers and families.
Now headed to the Governor.
H.4611 – Co-sponsored.
Allows paid parental leave following a stillbirth.
This compassionate measure passed the House unanimously.
H.4679 – South Carolina Drone Regulation and Public Safety Act.
Brings our state into alignment with federal standards for safe drone use.
H.4248 – Truth in Labeling Act.
Protects consumers and our local industry by preventing imported seafood from being mislabeled as local.
