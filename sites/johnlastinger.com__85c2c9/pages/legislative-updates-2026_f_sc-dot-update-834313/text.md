8037102154 Home Legislative Updates 2026 Legislative update - 2026 All Posts SC DOT Update May 6, 2026 Several months ago, I received a concern from a constituent regarding the intersection of Princeton Road and Highway 302, particularly during high-traffic times.
After discussing the issue, I agreed that the intersection could be difficult and potentially dangerous, so I immediately added it to the list of concerns for the SCDOT and raised the issue during my next meeting with my DOT contact.
Yesterday, I received confirmation that the traffic study and crash data analysis had been completed, and I’m pleased to announce that the intersection has been approved for a traffic signal.
I am hopeful this improvement will help ease traffic concerns in the area while also making the intersection safer for everyone who travels through it.
I commend the SCDOT for addressing this issue promptly and appreciate the positive working relationship we have built.
I will continue working diligently to advocate for road and infrastructure improvements throughout District 88 to help keep our communities safe and connected.
