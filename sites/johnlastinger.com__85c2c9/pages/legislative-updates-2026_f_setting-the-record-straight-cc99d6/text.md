8037102154 Home Legislative Updates 2026 Legislative update - 2026 All Posts Setting the Record Straight June 8, 2026 Unfortunately, election season often brings a great deal of noise, misinformation, and misleading attacks.
Amid all the claims being made, I want you to hear the facts directly from me.
First, I voted NO on H.4216, the state income tax bill.
While it reduced taxes for some of the state’s highest earners, it also shifted a greater tax burden onto many lower-income South Carolinians.
I could not support legislation that increased taxes on those who can least afford it.
We need lower taxes all around.
Second, misleading ads claim I voted for a pay raise.
Every Representative and Senator voted to revise district office expenses for increased costs.
The legislator salary bill is S.993, and I voted NO on increasing the salary as I do not believe lawmakers should receive higher salaries.
Finally, I voted NO on the state budget.
I support funding for teachers, law enforcement, and infrastructure, but this year’s budget increased by nearly $1 billion and included millions in taxpayer dollars directed to large corporations and special interests.
The budget continues to increase by more than population growth and inflation combined.
I believe taxpayers deserve responsible stewardship of their hard-earned money.
My responsibility is to represent the people of District 88 with integrity, transparency, and fiscal responsibility.
Every vote I cast is guided by that commitment.
I would be honored to earn your vote tomorrow, June 9th!
God bless!
