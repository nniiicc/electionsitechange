About Pastor John Born and raised in Lexington County, I have deep roots in the place I call home.
My wife and I have been blessed with 50 years of marriage, and together we’ve raised two sons, welcomed two wonderful daughters-in-law into our family, and enjoy spending time with our six grandchildren.
We all live and work in Lexington County.
For the past 48 years, I have served as both a public servant and a pastor, walking alongside families through life’s challenges and celebrations.
These experiences have given me a unique perspective on the needs of our community and a heart for service that guides everything I do.
I am passionate about helping people live their best lives, whether that’s by strengthening families, supporting local businesses, improving our schools, or ensuring our neighborhoods remain safe and welcoming.
My commitment has always been to listen, to serve, and to lead with integrity.
