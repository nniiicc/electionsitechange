Edgar Soto for Arizona State Senate - LD17
Leadership That Reflects Our Communities
Edgar Soto is a proud U.S.
Marine Corps veteran, lifelong educator, and community leader who has spent his life serving at every level — in the classroom, on the field, and in leadership roles that support students and families.
With more than 30 years in education, Edgar has worked across the full education system, from K-12 to higher education, helping open pathways for opportunity and strengthening public institutions.
As an educator and coach, he believes in teamwork, accountability, and bringing people together to get results.
Edgar is running for the Arizona State Senate in Legislative District 17 to continue that service — fighting for strong public schools, affordable communities, and a government that works for everyday Arizonans.

About Legislative District 17
Legislative District 17 includes parts of Tucson, Oro Valley, and the Catalina Foothills, stretching north toward Catalina and east toward the Rincon Valley.
