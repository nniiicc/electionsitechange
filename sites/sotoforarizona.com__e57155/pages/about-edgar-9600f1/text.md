As a proud United States Marine Corps veteran, Edgar learned the values of integrity, accountability, and putting others before himself.
Those lessons have guided him throughout his life and career as he has worked to help students, support families, and strengthen communities across Southern Arizona.
Edgar has spent decades working alongside educators, parents, and community leaders, giving him a firsthand understanding of the challenges Arizona families face every day.
He knows that too many people are working harder than ever while struggling to afford housing, healthcare, and everyday necessities, and he believes our state government should be focused on making life better for working families.
That's why Edgar is running for the Arizona State Senate.
He'll fight to protect public education, lower costs, support veterans, safeguard Arizona's water future, and ensure every family has the opportunity to succeed.
For Edgar, this campaign is about service.
He's ready to bring people together, tackle tough challenges, and be the strong voice Southern Arizona deserves at the Arizona Legislature.
