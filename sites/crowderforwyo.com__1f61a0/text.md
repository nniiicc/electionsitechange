Why I'm Running I’m Jessica Crowder, a rancher, business owner, and natural resources policy expert, and I’m running for Wyoming House District 43.
For more than 20 years, I’ve worked at the intersection of agriculture, land management, and public policy, helping shape solutions that support Wyoming’s economy, protect our working lands, and strengthen our communities.
I’m running to bring that experience to the Legislature and ensure Wyoming families, businesses, and communities have a strong voice and a steady advocate.
See My Experience Priorities Our communities deserve leaders who listen, show up, and make decisions with care and responsibility.
Wyoming's future depends on protecting what makes us strong while preparing for what comes next.
In the Legislature, I will prioritize: Supporting Wyoming's core industries Keeping government accountable and fiscally responsible Ensuring local voices guide local decisions Strengthening education for the next generation Protecting and defending the Constitution Expanding access to mental health resources Maintain public lands for public use Learn More I look forward to earning your trust, hearing your ideas, and working together to keep Wyoming strong.
