Education and Parents' Rights Standing Up for Conservative Values Support of Community Leaders Fiscal Responsibility Contact Donate Jessica Crowder for Wyoming House District 43 This is Jessica.
I believe government has a responsibility to serve the people of Wyoming.
That means being accountable to taxpayers, managing public resources responsibly, and passing legislation that reflects the needs of our communities.
The people of Wyoming are hard workers.
I don’t take that lightly and I’ll treat every taxpayer dollar with respect.
Fiscal Responsibility As a ranch and business owner, I understand the importance of making responsible decisions and planning for the future.
Wyoming families and businesses have to prioritize their spending, and state government should do the same.
That means supporting balanced budgets, reducing unnecessary spending, and ensuring public resources are used in ways that genuinely strengthen our communities.
By managing taxpayer dollars carefully and focusing on the services that matter most, government is accountable, transparent, and fiscally responsible.
As your representative, I'll work to: Support balanced, responsible budgets.
Hold government accountable for how taxpayer dollars are spent.
Evaluate legislation based on its value to Wyoming families and communities.
Protect our state's long-term financial stability through responsible stewardship.
Fiscal responsibility means funding core priorities, eliminating waste where possible, and preserving Wyoming's strong financial foundation for future generations.
I would appreciate your vote on August 18th.
Best, Jessica Crowder Republican Candidate for Wyoming House District 43 Endorsements Let's Talk If you have questions for me, or want to chat about the big issues in our district, I would love to get in touch with you.
