Economic Strength & Opportunity Our forefathers placed education at the heart of Wyoming’s constitution, and I share that conviction.
We must provide teachers and students with the resources they need to excel.
A strong education system is essential to Wyoming’s future, and we must support it responsibly so the next generation can learn, grow, and thrive here at home.
Education for the Next Generation Local governments, boards, and community organizations understand their neighbors best.
They live and work alongside you — driving the same roads, teaching in our schools, laboring in our industries, and volunteering their time to strengthen their communities.
Their voices and experience should guide local decision-making.
Local Control Matters I believe government must be accountable, legislation should serve the people of Wyoming, and public resources should be managed through balanced, responsible budgeting that delivers meaningful returns to our communities.
Government Accountability & Fiscal Responsibility Wyoming citizens deserve access to mental health resources without unnecessary barriers.
Mental health support is a service that the government should address seriously.
Because of our rural nature and boom-and-bust economy, Wyoming faces some of the highest mental health challenges in the nation.
I will advance policies that directly confront this issue and ensure that support is available to those who need it.
