Education and Parents' Rights Standing Up for Conservative Values Support of Community Leaders Fiscal Responsibility Contact Donate The Wyoming Coalition for a Healthy Retirement proudly endorses Jessica Crowder for election to the Wyoming Legislature in the 2026 Primary Election.
WCHR represents nearly 70,000 working and retired Wyoming public employees throughout the state.
Our endorsements are earned, not automatic.
Each endorsement reflects a careful review of candidate questionnaires, public statements, and discussions with candidates regarding issues affecting Wyoming's public employees, retirees, and the long-term strength of the Wyoming Retirement System.
Jessica earned WCHR's endorsement by demonstrating a thoughtful understanding of the issues facing Wyoming's public workforce and a commitment to supporting policies that strengthen retirement security for current and future public employees and retirees.
