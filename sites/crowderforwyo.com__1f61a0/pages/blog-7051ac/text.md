| Hi Friends & Neighbors, My name is Jessica Crowder, and I am running to represent House District 43 in the Wyoming State House.
For more than 20 years, I have worked to navigate complex policy issues related to agriculture, natural resources, and land management, all while building and managing multiple small family businesses with my husband, Jason.
Our community deserves thoughtful leadership that puts people first and works to ensure our children can build their lives right here at home.
I am running to bring my understanding of policy and local needs to shape solutions that support Wyoming’s economy, protect our working lands, and strengthen our communities. |
| Meet Jessica |
| Growing up on a cattle ranch in central New Mexico, I learned to appreciate the connection between the land and those who steward it.
This passion led me to study rangeland stewardship at Texas Tech University, earning a B.S. in Range Management and an M.S. in Range Science.
As a graduate student, I focused on prescribed summer fire and grazing management in the Northern Great Plains.
Today, my husband and I own and operate a ranch and various small businesses in Laramie County, including a consulting firm dedicated to land use, agriculture, conservation, and natural resource policy. |
| Wyoming residents deserve leaders who listen, show up, and make decisions with care and responsibility.
The future of our state depends on protecting what makes us strong while preparing for what comes next.
My experience navigating complex policy issues and running a family business has given me a practical understanding of how decisions made in Cheyenne affect people throughout the state.
As your representative, I will prioritize: Supporting Wyoming's core industries Keeping government accountable and fiscally responsible Ensuring local voices guide local decisions Strengthening education for the next generation Protecting and defending the Constitution Expanding access to mental health resources Maintaining public lands for public use I look forward to earning your trust, hearing your ideas, and working together to keep Wyoming strong as your representative for House District 43.
Best, Jessica |
