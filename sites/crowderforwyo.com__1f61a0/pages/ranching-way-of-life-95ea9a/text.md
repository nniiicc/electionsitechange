­ ­ ­ ­ ­ ­ ­ ­ ­ ­ ­ Hi Friends & Neighbors , My name is Jessica Crowder, and I am running to represent House District 43 in the Wyoming State House.
I am running to bring my understanding of policy and local needs to shape solutions that support Wyoming’s way of life.
I’d like to tell you a little more about what that means to me.
Ranching & the Wyoming Way of Life I was raised on a cattle ranch in central New Mexico, where I developed a deep respect for the relationship between the land and those who care for it.
That interest took me to Texas Tech University, where I earned a B.S. in Range Management and an M.S. in Range Science, researching prescribed summer fire and grazing strategies in the Northern Great Plains.
My husband and I now run a ranch and several small businesses in Laramie County, including a consulting practice focused on land use, agriculture, conservation, and natural resource policy.
Ranching, land stewardship, and family life are central to the Wyoming way of life.
It is the reason so many people are drawn here.
I’m running to preserve that lifestyle for every Wyomingite.
Everyone deserves a chance to build a thriving family, access and enjoy public lands, and live by the values that make this place home.
I look forward to earning your trust, hearing your ideas, and working together to keep Wyoming strong as your representative for House District 43.
Best, Jessica Crowder Candidate for Wyoming House District 43 Let's Talk Please do not hesitate to reach out with any questions you may have.
