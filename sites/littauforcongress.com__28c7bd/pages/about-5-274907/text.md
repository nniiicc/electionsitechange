I'm running as a Republican Congressional Candidate, a residing Enterprise Elementary School Board Member, a Christian Mother, a Small Business Owner, and a USCG Veteran to protect the American Dream, defend Freedom for our children's children, build up America, and Fight for a hopefilled Future.
I commit to you: That I will be a servant hearted integrious leader who listens and reflects the heart of our community in Washington DC.
I will go, send me!
My Story I was born and raised in South Dakota.
I grew up riding horses, working with livestock, riding 4 wheelers, participating in 4-H Rodeos, FFA, Pagents and Cheerleading.
At 18 I joined the US Coast Guard.
I was stationed for 2 years on the USCG Cutter Boutwell (Alameda CA), then a Search and Rescue Station in Bodega Bay CA as an Machinery Technician for the remaining 2 years.
I became a committed Christian at age 20 and my love of serving God and our Country has grown continually stronger over the years.
After the Military, I lived in Sacramento, CA for 15yrs. as a stay at home wife/homeschool mom to 4 beautiful kids (Ages 17-21) who are the heartbeat of my life.
I taught Sunday School, led worship, taught Bible studies, mentored women, and volunteered for various community events.
In 2016 I divorced and moved to Redding CA where I've sucessfully raised my children as a single mother.
I've sucessfully owned and operated Rockin Robin's Cleaning Company for 10yrs. as a sole proprietor, and I am the new owner of a local publication called Joyful Living Magazine.
My political journey began when I took a few equipping classed through the Policy Circle.
I am the 2025 reward recipient of the Mary Beth Weiss Award, for diligently applying myself to local civic engagement in my Redding community.
I'm also a residing School Board Member for the Enterprise Elementary School District (24-26). ​ Some of the community advocacy I've done the past 10 yrs. has been; creating a Single Mom's Support Group, helping women self represent in Court, helping women escape domestic violence safely, providing a weekly home group for at risk youth in my community for 6yrs., teaching basic self defense to at risk youth, and creating safety in my neighborhood .
My life has not been easy, but it is rewarding!
With God's help, I have sucessfully overcame many obstacles.
