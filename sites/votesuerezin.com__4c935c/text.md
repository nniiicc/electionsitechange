About sue
Senator Sue Rezin is a proven conservative leader who has delivered real results for the 38th District and across Illinois.
Serving as Deputy Republican Leader in the Illinois Senate, Sue has built a reputation for serious, effective leadership — focusing on fiscal responsibility, public safety, reliable energy, and policies that strengthen families and local communities.
Rather than chasing headlines, she has consistently focused on practical solutions that protect taxpayers and support long-term economic stability.
Recognized as a leading voice on energy policy, Sue has played a pivotal role in preserving Illinois’ nuclear fleet and advancing policies that support reliable, affordable energy for families and businesses.
From advocating for rural healthcare access and childcare affordability to protecting minors online and supporting law enforcement, Sue’s record reflects steady leadership, thoughtful policymaking, and a deep commitment to the people and communities she serves.

What I Will Fight For
Reliable & Affordable Energy
Leading the fight to preserve Illinois’ nuclear fleet, support new reactor development, and ensure a stable, affordable energy grid for families and businesses.
Fiscal Responsibility & Protecting Taxpayers
Advocating for responsible budgeting, opposing unnecessary tax increases, and pushing back against policies that make Illinois less affordable.
Public Safety & Supporting Law Enforcement
Standing firmly with law enforcement, promoting safer communities, and supporting policies that prioritize accountability and public safety.
Protecting Children & Families
Championing initiatives like Safe Screens Healthy Minds and advancing policies that protect minors online and address emerging risks facing young people.
Affordable Childcare & Strong Families
Supporting policies that expand access to affordable childcare, strengthen early childhood development, and ease financial pressure on working families.
Rural Healthcare & Local Community Access
Fighting to protect and expand access to healthcare in regional and rural communities, including support for policies that help stabilize local hospitals and care providers.
