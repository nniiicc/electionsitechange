Americans are feeling the squeeze.
Prices are rising, health care is getting more expensive, and we’ve got a tax system that favors the wealthiest over working families.
Instead of working to strengthen the middle class, Donald Trump has handed tax breaks to the wealthy while his tariffs make everyday necessities like groceries, housing and medicine more expensive.
At the same time, artificial intelligence is changing the way we work, but there’s no serious plan to help workers with the fallout.
Without smart, forward-looking policies, AI could end up creating more inequality instead of opportunity.
We need an economy that rewards work, not just wealth.
That means a fairer tax code, affordable health care, and an innovation agenda that grows the middle class and ensures every worker has a fair shot to succeed.
