Donate $# $# $# Other Meet Mark The Plan Endorsements Take Action Store Donate Meet Mark The Plan Endorsements Take Action Store Donate Meet Mark The Plan Endorsements Take Action Store On the Issues The Plan The Problem Americans are feeling the squeeze.
Prices are rising, health care is getting more expensive, and we’ve got a tax system that favors the wealthiest over working families.
Instead of working to strengthen the middle class, Donald Trump has handed tax breaks to the wealthy while his tariffs make everyday necessities like groceries, housing and medicine more expensive.
At the same time, artificial intelligence is changing the way we work, but there’s no serious plan to help workers with the fallout.
Without smart, forward-looking policies, AI could end up creating more inequality instead of opportunity.
We need an economy that rewards work, not just wealth.
That means a fairer tax code, affordable health care, and an innovation agenda that grows the middle class and ensures every worker has a fair shot to succeed.
Anchor heading for affordability The Plan Making Virginia Affordable Lowering Costs The United States is 3.7 million homes short of meeting basic housing needs.
In the Senate, Mark’s plan is designed to expand housing supply and bring down costs by encouraging rehabilitation of existing structures, speeding up permitting, and making it easier for first-time homebuyers to obtain their first mortgage.
He has also worked to drive down the cost of child care by addressing the child care labor crisis.
Universal Healthcare During his first term in the Senate, Mark helped write the Affordable Care Act, and has worked ever since to strengthen it.
He believes that the only way to lower premiums and keep the health care system sustainable is to ensure every American has access to an affordable basic standard of care.
Without access to health care coverage, people still get sick.
They end up in emergency rooms, which is the most expensive type of care, raising costs for everyone.
Preparing for AI Washington is largely unprepared for the coming AI jobs crisis and we urgently need forward-looking policies to prepare our economy for this new era.
Mark believes the AI companies and big corporations that stand to massively profit from this disruption must do their part, including preparing workers for the economy of the future and paying their fair share of the enormous energy demands AI will place on our grid.
Anchor heading for delivering The Plan Delivering for Virginians Roads, bridges and broadband As a primary author of the landmark bipartisan infrastructure bill, Mark secured funding to expand high-speed internet in rural Virginia and key transportation and infrastructure projects throughout the Commonwealth.
Because of Mark’s work, Virginia leads the nation in access to broadband and is on track to be among the first states with universal broadband access.
Advanced manufacturing jobs Mark wrote the groundbreaking CHIPS and Science Act, which is bringing advanced manufacturing back to America instead of relying on foreign countries for critical technology.
The law has already brought billions in investment benefitting Virginia and spurred innovation.
He has also championed clean-energy projects that are creating thousands of jobs and building a stronger, more competitive economy.
Affordable health care Mark helped cap the price of insulin, lower prescription drug costs for seniors, and is fighting to extend tax credits that make healthcare more affordable for the middle class.
He was also proud to support the PACT Act, which expanded health care coverage and benefits for veterans exposed to burn pits and other toxic chemicals.
Supporting Military Families and Veterans Mark has delivered major improvements for veterans by strengthening VA facilities, expanding access to health care, and modernizing benefits to meet today’s challenges.
He championed legislation to address toxic exposure, improve mental health care, reduce wait times at VA clinics and make sure that veterans can receive the care they deserve closer to home.
Mark has also led efforts to repair substandard military housing so that servicemembers and their families have the stability and dignity they deserve.
Anchor heading for safety The Plan Keeping Americans Safe Winning the battles of tomorrow No state plays a larger role in building and sustaining America’s military might than Virginia.
Mark knows 21st-century security won’t just be about who has the most planes, guns, or missiles.
It’s also about who leads in AI, unmanned systems, advanced computing, quantum, cybersecurity, and other technologies that will define the next generation of warfare, and he’s worked to ensure that the U.S. stays ahead.
Protecting America’s innovation advantage Mark has championed policies to protect critical technologies, boost federal R&D, and protect U.S. intellectual property from theft.
He wrote the bipartisan CHIPS and Science Act to rebuild domestic semiconductor manufacturing so the U.S. is never again dependent on China for the core technology that powers our economy and military.
Strengthening our edge over China As the top Democrat on the Senate Intelligence Committee, Mark has led bipartisan efforts to counter the Chinese government’s technological and economic aggression – from securing supply chains to cracking down on foreign investments that threaten U.S. national security.
Anchor heading for values The Plan Upholding Virginia Values Fighting for a fair shot Mark was the first in his family to graduate from college, and his first two businesses failed – but he got a third shot at success, building a business that created thousands of jobs.
He believes every American deserves that same fair shot to try, fail, and succeed.
Working with anyone Mark has sponsored 85 bills that have been signed into law – by both Democratic and Republican presidents.
He has built bipartisan coalitions, working with Democrats and Republicans alike to deliver on a range of priorities like funding infrastructure projects, increasing access to capital for small businesses, and expanding high-tech domestic manufacturing.
Honesty and integrity Mark knows that trust is the foundation of a healthy democracy.
He has earned the trust of both his Senate colleagues and Virginians by being open, transparent, and accountable, keeping his word, and owning up to tough choices.
Anchor heading for freedoms The Plan Fighting to Protect our Freedoms Protecting free and fair elections Our democratic norms and institutions have never before faced the threats that we face today.
Mark still believes that politics is the best way to make a difference and improve people’s lives, but only if we have a strong democracy and voters are able to make their voices heard.
Mark helped negotiate and pass a revised Electoral Count Act to make clear that the Vice President does not have the power to overturn the will of the people when Congress is certifying the presidential election.
Constitutional Rights Republicans bent the Senate rules to put a conservative super majority on the Supreme Court – with the clear intent to overturn Roe v.
Wade , which they did.
2026 is a critical year for abortion access in Virginia because voters will have an opportunity to constitutionally protect abortion access in the Commonwealth.
Mark will work to ensure this amendment passes as well as other critical state constitutional amendments to protect marriage equality and the restoration of voting rights.
Gun Safety Mark has seen firsthand the devastation that gun violence has brought to our communities and is working to ensure Virginians can go to school, the grocery store, and their place of worship free from the fear of gun violence.
He believes that Americans have the right to own guns, but that we need commonsense solutions like banning assault weapons and background checks to keep our communities safe.
