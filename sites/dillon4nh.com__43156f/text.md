He runs a local business and raises three children with his wife.
The Granite State values he grew up with still guide him today.
As Chairman of Hudson's Board of Selectmen , Dumont handles the town's day-to-day challenges.
He also serves on the Nashua Regional Planning Commission and the House Housing Committee.
These roles give him experience with local budgets, regional development, and housing policy.
Dumont believes in common-sense solutions over political theater .
He wants to protect what makes New Hampshire special while ensuring families can afford to live here.
His approach comes from years of balancing budgets and solving problems at the local level. ★ FIGHTING FOR YOU Key Issues Housing Freedom DILLON DUMONT will eliminate restrictive zoning regulations that prevent families from building affordable housing and force up property taxes for existing homeowners.
He will push to reduce unnecessary permitting delays and bureaucratic red tape that add thousands of dollars to home construction costs, keeping New Hampshire housing accessible for working families without imposing an income tax.
Dumont will protect property owners' rights while streamlining the approval process for new housing developments to ease the shortage driving up costs across Hillsborough County.
Lower Taxes Dillon Dumont will vote against any proposed income or sales tax and work to reduce New Hampshire's property tax burden by eliminating wasteful state spending that forces costs onto local communities.
He will support legislation to cap local property tax increases and push for more efficient government operations that deliver essential services without breaking family budgets.
Dumont will protect New Hampshire's tax advantage by ensuring the state lives within its means rather than passing costs down to homeowners and small businesses.
Government Accountability As a proven local leader who has balanced budgets and managed taxpayer resources responsibly, Dillon Dumont will bring that same fiscal discipline to the State House.
He will fight to eliminate wasteful spending, reduce bureaucratic bloat, and ensure every tax dollar is spent efficiently.
Dumont believes in transparent government that works for the people, not special interests.
