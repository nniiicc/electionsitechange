Why should you care?
…Because we’re wasting our tax dollars and weakening our state
The current prices of all of those services (ie, college, healthcare, childcare, eldercare) have risen dramatically, and ultimately, they: financially debilitate millions of hardworking people; waste taxpayer money; and weaken our state and country.
It’s not right, but we can do better.
The main problem is that the high costs of services do not come from the services themselves (or from staffing costs), but rather, they come from: price-gauging, high profit-seeking, administration costs, and taxes.
And we make our problems even worse when we subsidize those for-profit industries, because it guarantees payment, which then causes the prices to go up even higher!
In the end, we’re just wasting resources and putting band-aids on the broken bones of outdated systems.
But, we can do better.
With my proposals, we will instead spend tax dollars more efficiently, lower operating costs, and deliver high quality services that actually help our state and the people in it - and not just take our money and waste it on broken systems.
