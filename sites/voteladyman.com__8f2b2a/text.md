About Jack
Jack, a seasoned mechanical engineer, has built a distinguished career in the energy sector.
Yet, his impact stretches far beyond the engineering realm.
Jack's dedication led him to serve as Elkins, AR's mayor before relocating to Jonesboro to advance his career.
Upon retirement, he pursued a new path in politics, successfully becoming a State Representative in 2014.
Committed not only to his profession but also to his roles as a loving husband, father, and grandfather, Jack resides in Jonesboro alongside his cherished wife, Linda.
