Chad Gleissl is a Lancaster County native and a proud product of a blue-collar Pennsylvania family, rooted in the Warwick area he’s now running to represent.
His passion for public service began in high school, where classroom debates on current events inspired him to run for the school board at just 17, an early grassroots effort that sparked his commitment to being a changemaker.
Throughout his career in public safety, Chad has built strong relationships with local colleges and communities, focusing on fostering trust with the community he works to protect.
His exceptional service has resulted in Chad winning multiple awards for his contribution to the York Department of Campus Police, including a Medal of Valor and an award for Innovations in Community Policing.
Outside of work and the campaign trail, he values time with family, hosting regular game and movie nights, and enjoys golfing, tackling home projects, and getting involved in his community.
Grounded in service and driven by a deep connection to the district, Chad is committed to delivering practical, community-focused solutions for the people of the 37th District.
