Meet Andrea Issues News Volunteer Contribute Issues Balancing the state budget The purpose of government, by the consent of the governed, is to do things that we cannot do as individuals.
For example, keeping all fire-fighting equipment in excellent condition, optimizing forest growth with controlled burns, putting in nuclear power, putting in a desalinization plant – in other words doing what government can do and individuals can’t.
Balancing the state budget will require a change in goals.
The current budget spends too much money on things that individuals can do.
The assembly members don’t actually work through the ways and means or consider unintended consequences – like having plastic bags at the grocery store and then what???
They fill up the waterways, the landfill (and they won’t disintegrate) – if they had just spent more time thinking it through!
That’s right – we elected the assembly members to write legislation!
We need new enterprises now that the film industry is on the skids and not employing so many people.
California is noted for agriculture and we do supply food for a lot of people.
Are we doing regenerative farming, or just leaching the soil?
We need more manufacturing.
So many businesses have moved out of California.
The top three most pressing issues for the state Taxes high enough to drive businesses to move to Texas and other states.
I would vote to lower taxes and promote a business friendly environment.
We stopped being a production economy.
We need to begin producing things again.
Crippling regulation – of everything!Semi-trucks that bring goods into the state have to be non-gas powered, though for the time being, Lee Zeldin has paused the mandate.Trucks that the state wants cannot carry as much freight, have to stop frequently and for long periods of time to “refuel,” which makes our food and goods much more expensive.Newsom and the “wokesters” don’t care.
I would legislate for fewer regulations.
Poor education, consisting of woke indoctrination and common core rather than seeking truth through tested methods and materials.
We need a contract signed by a parent, the teacher, and the student.
After all, we taxpayers are paying for education and we want some accountability.
Another answer is school choice, providing parental control of the state money mandated for the education of their child, so that they can choose a school, home school, or create a pod with other parents.
The curriculum and methods have degenerated in public schools.
Only New York state outspends us, but we rank 41 st .
Specific policy to improve cost of living Lower taxes.
People will spend the money saved and thus foster growth in the state.
The impact would be immediate.
Curtail federal immigration enforcement California is way out of line regarding immigration into the United States.
It is the federal government’s responsibility to deal with immigration, not the states.
We taxpayers are paying federal agents to enforce immigration law.
The least we can do is cooperate with them rather than threaten them.
Healthcare costs Healthcare used to be private, businesses operated by doctors.
Then commercial businesses, to attract the top talent, offered health insurance to their employees.
The outgrowth of that is that today people feel unprotected if they don’t have a health insurance plan of some kind.
If we went back to private health businesses, the problem would diminish and the government would save a lot of money.
Expanding State Healthcare .
Too much government in every aspect of our lives dogs our every step.
I am not in favor of government and healthcare in the same sentence.
When I grew up, doctors ran their own businesses.
Now doctors march to the beat of the state and they and we suffer for it, because they allow the state to dictate how they run their practices.
We hear stories about the doctors who take work home in the evening after completing a busy day of treating patients.
The state wants to control how much the doctors can charge, too.
Regarding those who are poor: let doctors decide to treat some for a lesser amount and then let them get a tax write-off for doing so.
Contributing to the problem as well is the fact that we do not ask how much our treatment is going to cost.
For example, how much is an appendectomy, or a tonsillectomy?
We don’t shop for services.
If it were private enterprise, we would.
We are good at shopping!

Homelessness If we want to end homelessness, we must not allow it.
After all, the sidewalks are public and the homeless set up camp there.
There are laws regarding urinating and defecating on the sidewalk, but we don’t enforce them.
There are many organizations dedicated to helping homeless people get back on their feet.
We need to tell the homeless about them.
People lose their homes for many reasons, of course.
People lose their jobs, or have health problems, or gamble, or are scammed and can’t make the payments.
Perhaps the county could offer a class titled “What to do when the numbers don’t add up.” State Energy – Oil The government needs to stay out of business – specifically the oil biz.
California supplied 40% of the nation’s oil for over a century.
It has gradually declined.
Now the leadership wants to stop oil production in California entirely.
Why?
It isn’t hurting anything and in fact, it is helping!
Nuclear power is safe and cheap.
Why not legislate for that?
Proposition 36 It makes us feel good to think that we have done something to stop drug and retail theft crimes by offering drug treatment to offenders.
Perhaps the better option is some “tough love” by punishing the crimes more than we have been.
For example, I have seen shoplifting at Target, gone to an employee to report it, and been told that they won’t hold the thief accountable.
This behavior damages our society by not having any standards.
It’s like saying that it doesn’t matter what you do.
Then too, drug offenders will go to a program to be rehabilitated just to stay out of jail.
If they don’t want to quit, it is a waste of our tax money.
If we cannot repeal this proposition, then let’s amend it to allow ONE rehabilitation.
I know people who have been to rehab six times!
Enough is enough!
Gender affirming care Why should the state be involved in gender-affirming care?
If people want to engage in that behavior, it should be at their own risk and expense.
The truth is that gender affirming drugs and change are dangerous for the psyche, hence all the murders and crazy behavior in which transgenders engage.
Biologically, there are two sexes and we need to deal with it.
We certainly do not need to talk about transgenderism to people under the age of 18. for that?
Government ban on social media.
Children belong to their parents and not to the state, therefore the government should not regulate social media use for young people.
It is the parents’ responsibility.
Recently, social media companies have been charged with “addicting” young people to social media.
We would not dream of charging alcohol makers with addiction, when some of their customers become alcoholic.
Artificial Intelligence .
We have managed to adjust to many labor-saving devices.
Machines such as the washing machine have shortened the time and attention required to do the laundry so much that it no longer takes a day to do it for a family.
AI will assist in many ways, but we need to beware.
It is not infallible, since it is programmed by humans.
We can’t give up critical thinking.
Violent crime Just because crime is down statistically doesn’t mean that it is safe everywhere.
Because we are experiencing non-enforcement of our laws, criminals feel emboldened to commit crimes knowing that they will likely not be punished.
It is a dangerous lack of responsibility on our part.
