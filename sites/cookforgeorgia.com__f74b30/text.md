Meet Daniel
A New Generation of Leadership
Daniel Cook is a lifelong resident of District 67 and a proud product of Coweta County.
A graduate of Trinity Christian School in Sharpsburg, Daniel went on to earn his degree from Georgia Tech with Highest Honors in Economics, along with minors in Political Science and History.
His experience spans the public and private sectors—from working in Congressman Drew Ferguson's office on constituent casework, to supporting Governor Kemp's internship program tracking legislation for the University System of Georgia.
Most recently, Daniel served as the Regional Field Director for Congressman Buddy Carter's Senate campaign.
He also spent the summer working as a counselor at a children's summer camp.
When he's not working, Daniel enjoys spending time with his fiancée Jessica, who hails from Ireland.
Together they love exploring Georgia and beyond.
Daniel is running because he believes Georgia deserves leadership that rewards hard work, strengthens families, and creates real opportunity for the next generation.
