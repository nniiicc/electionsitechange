Join the Campaign See the Vision District 67 Meet Daniel A New Generation of Leadership Daniel Cook is a lifelong resident of District 67 and a proud product of Coweta County.
A graduate of Trinity Christian School in Sharpsburg, Daniel went on to earn his degree from Georgia Tech with Highest Honors in Economics, along with minors in Political Science and History.
His experience spans the public and private sectors—from working in Congressman Drew Ferguson's office on constituent casework, to supporting Governor Kemp's internship program tracking legislation for the University System of Georgia.
Most recently, Daniel served as the Regional Field Director for Congressman Buddy Carter's Senate campaign.
He also spent the summer working as a counselor at a children's summer camp.
When he's not working, Daniel enjoys spending time with his fiancée Jessica, who hails from Ireland.
Together they love exploring Georgia and beyond.
Daniel is running because he believes Georgia deserves leadership that rewards hard work, strengthens families, and creates real opportunity for the next generation.

Serve Georgia Investing in those who serve our communities Public Defender Tuition Reimbursement Georgia law school graduates who serve as public defenders for 3 years will receive tuition reimbursement, strengthening fairness in our justice system.
Expanded 'Serve Georgia' Initiative Tuition assistance for teachers in underserved schools, loan support for rural nurses, and incentives for law enforcement and first responders.
Tougher Penalties for Scammers Strengthen laws to protect Georgians from fraud and scams with harsher penalties for those who prey on our seniors, families, and small businesses.
Build Georgia Lowering costs, expanding opportunity, and making life more affordable Married Couples Tax Credit A state tax credit for married couples that increases the longer a couple is married—supporting family stability and financial security.
Housing Affordability & Ownership Cut red tape, restrict institutional investors from buying single-family homes, and make state-owned land available to first-time buyers.
Georgia Grown School Food Set a goal for schools to source 20-#% of food locally, supporting Georgia farmers while providing fresher, healthier meals to students.
Rural Healthcare Stabilization Support struggling rural hospitals and incentivize healthcare professionals to serve in underserved areas across Georgia.
Common Sense Data Center Policy Encourage data center growth while protecting residential communities by directing development to industrially-zoned areas away from neighborhoods.
Celebrate Georgia Honoring our history, culture, and identity The Museum of Georgia A world-class museum complex in Milledgeville showcasing Georgia's history, culture, agriculture, and industries—with free admission for all Georgia students.
Georgia Civics & History Initiative Strengthen education on Georgia history and government, ensuring students graduate with a strong understanding of civic responsibility.
North Georgia Conservation Protect the natural beauty of the North Georgia Mountains while supporting responsible tourism and outdoor recreation.
Tax Relief for Georgians We will fight to eliminate the state income tax and eliminate property taxes on owner-occupied homes under $1 million in value—providing meaningful, lasting relief for middle-class families.
