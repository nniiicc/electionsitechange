House Democratic Leader Brian Meyer Dinner
1234 Maple Street
Municipal, State 12345
Join Jennifer Kirkman on Thursday, October 8th at 7pm in the Burlington Memorial Auditorium Banquet Room for a special evening with Brian Meyer, Democratic Minority Leader of the Iowa House of Representatives and State Representative for Iowa House District 29.
Brian has represented southeast Des Moines in the Iowa House since 2013 and was selected by his colleagues to serve as House Minority Leader in 2025.
Enjoy dinner while hearing firsthand about the issues facing Iowa families, communities, schools, and businesses.
Brian will share his perspective on the work happening at the State Capitol, the challenges and opportunities ahead for Iowa, and the importance of leadership that listens and delivers practical solutions.
This is a great opportunity to meet two leaders committed to strengthening Iowa communities, ask questions, and join a meaningful conversation about the future of our state.
Dinner will be served.
