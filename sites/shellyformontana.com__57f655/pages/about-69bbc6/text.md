Eagle Woman
A member of the Bitterroot Salish, former Chairwoman of the Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes, and the Democratic incumbent for House District 91.
I grew up on a ranch in Arlee which helped shape my work ethic.
My parents also taught me service to the community through their examples.
I raised 4 adult sons, have 7 grandchildren and 2 great granddaughters.
I will work hard to preserve the Last Best Place for these future generations so they can enjoy the fishing, hunting and camping lifestyle that helped form my values.
I am passionate about preserving and protecting Montana and its resources for future generations.
Representative Shelly Fyant
I led community-improvement efforts and as Chairwoman of the Confederated Salish and Kootenai Tribes, I applied my values to working with Montana Governors, state and federal leaders, and influential national and regional organizations and businesses.
My career demonstrates a commitment to serving the community.
- Established an Incident Command System with Lake County Commission Chairman Bill Barron during the COVID-19 pandemic.
- Currently serves as Food Sovereignty Chair for the Arlee Community Development Corporation.
- Tribal council work included the Bison Range return, the Kerr Dam purchase, and the Cultural Waterways Ordinance protecting the Flathead River.
- Fifteen years at Kicking Horse Job Corps Center developing career training for youth ages 16 to 24.
- Service with the U.S.
Forest Service.
- Helped establish the Three Chiefs Cultural Center.
LEGISLATIVE RECORD
I have proven leadership experience in the MT State Legislature during the 69th session.
I served on the State Administration and Veterans Affairs; Local Government; Energy, Technology and Federal Relations; and Legislative Administration Committees during the session.
Interim Committees include Education and the State Tribal Relations Committee.
