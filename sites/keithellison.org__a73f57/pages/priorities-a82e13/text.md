What Keith’s Fighting For
Keith Ellison is bringing down costs for Minnesota families.
As our Attorney General, Keith has taken on price-gouging insulin makers, hedge fund landlords, and price-fixing meatpackers who were illegally driving up prices for medicine, housing, and groceries.
Since day one, Keith has been fighting fraud, securing over 350 fraud convictions since he took office - that's one conviction every 8 days.
As the People’s Lawyer, Keith’s job is to help Minnesotans afford their lives and live with dignity, safety, and respect—and to protect Minnesota from Donald Trump’s dangerous abuse of power.
Keith is an Attorney General who Minnesotans can count on to fight for them—and win.
