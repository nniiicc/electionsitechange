Under Minnesota law, it’s the Attorney General’s job to investigate and prosecute Medicaid (also known as Medical Assistance) fraud.
And Keith runs one of the most effective fraud-fighting units of any Attorney General in America.
His Medicaid Fraud Control Unit has secured over 340 fraud convictions since Keith first took office in 2019 - on average, that’s an investigation, indictment, and conviction every eight days.
Out of every state in the county, Keith’s Medicaid Fraud Control Unit regularly ranks among the best in the nation when it comes to cases charged and convictions secured.* In 2026, Keith pushed through major bipartisan legislation to help in the fight against fraud.
Fighting Fraud
Passing Key Fraud-Fighting Legislation
Keith has no patience for people who steal from taxpayers and the Minnesotans who rely on Medicaid (known as Medical Assistance in Minnesota).
That’s why he led the charge to pass the Medical Assistance Protection Act (MAP Act), a bill that fights fraud by adding new investigators to his Medicaid Fraud Control Unit, expanding its investigative power, increasing penalties for large-scale theft, and more.
The bill passed with overwhelming bipartisan support, and Keith has already started hiring more investigators and prosecutors to go after fraud.
Prosecuting Medicaid Fraudsters
Since taking office, Keith’s Medicaid Fraud Control Unit has prosecuted and convicted more than 340 Medicaid fraudsters.
In 2026 alone, Keith’s fraud unit charged Minnesota providers who billed for services they never delivered and partnered with the federal government on indictments spanning housing stabilization, disability services, and home care fraud.

Fighting Fraud Against Minnesotans
Keith doesn't just fight Medicaid fraud, he goes after scammers, predatory lenders, and dishonest businesses that target Minnesotans directly.
Contractors:
Keith has sued unlicensed contractors and home-renovation scammers who took people’s money and either botched the job or just plain didn’t do it and secured millions of dollars in restitution for Minnesotans who were cheated.
Lenders:
Keith shut down online lenders charging Minnesotans interest rates as high as 800% on loans, which is more than 20 times the maximum allowed under state law.
Keith forced the companies to forgive over $1 million in loans to Minnesotans.
Opioid manufacturers:
Keith recovered more than $630 million for Minnesota from opioid makers, including Purdue Pharma and the Sackler family, who deceptively marketed how addictive and dangerous their drugs really were.
That money is going to treatment and recovery services across Minnesota.
Deceptive pricing and hidden fees:
Keith forced retailers to refund Minnesotans hit with illegal, hidden charges, including a clothing retailer that tacked on a fake tariff surcharge and a home-improvement chain that advertised discounts shoppers could never actually redeem.
Robocalls and phone scams:
Keith sued a telecom company responsible for more than 7.5 billion illegal robocalls, many of them scams aimed at seniors, and warned multiple other companies to stop transmitting illegal robocalls or face lawsuits.
Debt relief scams:
Keith forced fraudulent debt-settlement companies to refund more than $2.8 million to Minnesotans after these companies lied about the services they provide and charged illegal fees.
Keith won’t let scammers prey on people who are already struggling with debt.
