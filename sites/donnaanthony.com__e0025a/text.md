Donna Anthony
Donna Anthony is a lifelong Alaskan, a fourth-generation member of a proud military and service-oriented family, and a long-time resident of the Mat-Su Valley.
Raised with strong values and a deep commitment to service, Donna has spent her entire adult life protecting Alaskans, defending our freedoms, and strengthening our communities.
Following in her grandfather’s footsteps—he was both a Mason and a Shriner—Donna continues her family’s tradition of leadership and civic involvement as a member of the Eastern Star.
She is dedicated to preserving the values that have shaped Alaska for generations.
Donna recently retired as Chief of Police for an Alaska police department, leading officers across multiple communities and overseeing day-to-day public safety operations.
If selected, Donna will officially retire from law enforcement to serve full time in the Legislature.
