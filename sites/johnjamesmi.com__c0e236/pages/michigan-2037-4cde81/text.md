January 26, 2037.
Michigan turns two hundred years old.
How will we celebrate our bicentennial?
A look ahead Close your eyes and picture the party.
It is the summer of 2037.
Fireworks over the Detroit River, the Grand River, Lake Michigan and Lake Superior.
Main Streets full of open storefronts and Now Hiring signs.
Classrooms where every kid can read and do math at grade level.
Paychecks that cover the month and energy bills that do not wreck the budget.
Picture families who chose to build their lives here, and kids who came home after college because Michigan was the best place to start.
That is what eight years of James leadership is meant to deliver.
Not a slogan.
A state our kids stay in.
Great schools Every child reading at grade level, the trades taught alongside college prep, and parents treated as partners instead of an inconvenience.
Prosperity Paychecks that cover the month, energy you can afford, and a state that rewards the people who work, build and grow.
Open businesses Main Streets full of open doors, and a Lansing that gets out of the way of the people who make payroll.
Happy families Safe streets, healthy kids, and a reason for the next generation to build their lives right here.
This is what we’re voting for.
