JOHN JAMES ANNOUNCES HOUSE OVERSIGHT CHAIRMAN JAY DEBOYER AS RUNNING MATE
James And DeBoyer Pledge To Save Michigan From Radical Extremism And Usher in a New Era of Transparency And Common Sense
SHELBY TOWNSHIP, MI — Earlier today, Republican nominee for Governor John James announced small business owner, former local election official and State House Oversight Chairman Jay DeBoyer of Clay Township as his choice for Lieutenant Governor.
James announced his selection of DeBoyer on X, writing:
“I am choosing Jay DeBoyer as my running mate because he has run a business, served in local government, and overseen elections.
He knows how to work with both parties in the Legislature to get things done.
Jay embodies the West Point Honor Code principles I expect in my administration: restoring integrity, competence, and trust in state government.
He will lead the fight against corruption, fraud, waste, and abuse, protecting core services while fixing what’s broken.
Michigan deserves a government as good as its people, focused on excellent schools, safer streets, and an affordable Michigan.
Jay will help us deliver it.”
— John James
“With freedom on the ballot and the future of our state at stake, I’m humbled and honored to join John James as his pick for Lieutenant Governor.
Michiganders face a clear choice between socialism and a bold new direction rooted in freedom, opportunity, and prosperity.
John is the right leader to win this fight, turn our state around, and build a Michigan where future generations can live, work, raise a family, and thrive.”
— Jay DeBoyer
DeBoyer is a small business owner and second-term State Representative serving St.
Clair and Macomb counties.
As Chairman of the House Oversight Committee, he has led efforts to root out waste, fraud, abuse, and government mismanagement.
Before joining the Legislature, DeBoyer spent 12 years as St.
Clair County Clerk and Register of Deeds, where he oversaw elections, and previously served in local government in Clay Township.
Together, James and DeBoyer will root out corruption, cut taxes and energy costs, make communities safer, strengthen education, and bring jobs and opportunity back home to Michigan.
