The latest failure comes as Jocelyn Benson campaigns to continue the same Democrat leadership that has controlled Michigan for the past eight years.
“Michiganders are already overtaxed, and now their government can’t even give them their own money back.
For eight years, Democrats have controlled Michigan and families have paid the price for their incompetence.
Jocelyn Benson represents more of the same failed leadership Michiganders simply cannot afford.
It’s time for accountability, competence, and COMMON SENSE in Lansing.” — John James As of late July, the Michigan Department of Treasury acknowledged that approximately 122,000 taxpayers who filed before the April 15 deadline still had returns in the processing queue.
Recent reporting indicates 66,000 Michiganders are still waiting for refunds.
For eight years, Democrats have controlled Michigan’s executive branch, including Jocelyn Benson, who is now overseeing her own election for Governor as Secretary of State.
Benson touts her experience in Lansing as a qualification for the job, but the results of Democrat leadership speak for themselves: Michigan has fallen to 46th out of 50 states overall.
If you or your family are still waiting on your tax refund, contact your state representative and demand answers.
Michigan families deserve better.
