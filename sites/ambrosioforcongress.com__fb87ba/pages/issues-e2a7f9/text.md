Issues
Ralph Ambrosio's campaign is built on four core priorities to restore common sense, accountability, and prosperity.
Lower the Cost of Living & Boost Economic Opportunity
We have to tackle crushing costs and lack of opportunity through tax cutting and deregulation.
This will lower costs and put more money back in your pocket.
Ensure Cheap & Abundant Energy
Our foolish restrictions on energy sources has sent energy costs soaring and curbed economic growth.
I'll push and all-of-the-above energy strategy that will produce abundant supply while driving down costs.
Restore Public Safety & Secure Our Borders Permanently
Ensure government fulfills its primary function of keeping citizens safe by supporting law enforcement, securing the nation, and defending the right to self-defense.
By focusing on crime prevention and effective policing, we will see gains in public safety, our local economy, and overall quality of life.
Reclaiming Our Future & Challenging the Establishment
Offer a fresh perspective to challenge the status quo and better represent our home of upstate NY.
This means support for term limits, Voter ID, parental rights in education, and rooting out corruption wherever it exists.
We need a government we can trust and that represents all of us.
