A Record of Service and Accountability Born in Hudson and raised in Chatham, NY, Ambrosio has a long and distinguished record of public service.
He served in the US Navy (active duty and reserves) from 1970-1976.
After his military service and graduation from Clarkson University, he joined the New York State Police (NYSP) from 1977 to 1989.
While with the NYSP, he obtained his law degree and served in the Division Counsel's Office.
His career continued in law as an Assistant District Attorney in Greene and Columbia Counties from 1989 to 1996, a public defender, and later as an assistant attorney general under Dennis Vacco.
Ambrosio returned to the NYSP from 1999 to 2010 as an Assistant Counsel before retiring and moving into private practice.
He has been a resident of Delmar, NY since 1985.
Five Core Priorities for NY-20 Ambrosio's campaign is founded on the principle that the federal government must return to its proper, constrained role, prioritizing statutory obligations over political agendas.
His platform, "Five Core Priorities for NY-20," includes: Lower the Cost of Living and Boost Economic Opportunity: Advocating for policies that put more money back into working families' pockets through job creation, tax burden reduction, energy independence, affordable healthcare through market-based reforms, and a 100% tax deduction for all childcare related expenses.
Restore Public Safety and Secure Our Borders Permanently: Prioritizing the primary function of government to keep citizens safe by fully supporting law enforcement, promoting law enforcement partnerships, securing the border, enforcing existing laws, and vigorously defending the people’s right to self-defense.
Protect Our Quality of Life and Natural Resources: Demanding the federal government focus on its legal mandate to address flooding and contamination in navigable waterways, including the Hudson River and Mohawk River, and committing to improved funding for special needs and mental health services.
Government Accountability and Delivering Federal Results: Bringing a "Clean Broom" to root out corruption, advocating for a smaller, more efficient government, and leveraging federal resources for meaningful infrastructure projects for the district.
Reclaiming Our Future: Challenging the Establishment: As a major outsider, Ambrosio is prepared to confront the entrenched political machine, champion government and election integrity with common sense measures like Voter ID, prioritize parental rights over government meddling, and focus on policies that promote merit and excellence in America.
