About Nick Issues Get Involved Events Updates Donate Now Home About Nick Issues Get Involved Events Updates Donate Now About Nick Kimble Welcome to our site!
I am a native St.
Louisan and proud South City resident.
I grew up in the Southampton neighborhood and attended St.
Gabe's and SLU High before going to Loyola University in Chicago.
I studied political science and business.
My wife, Allie, and I own a home in the Princeton Heights neighborhood.
You can usually find us there walking our dog, Sasha.
She loves making new friends, feel free to say hi!
In my professional life, I am an associate on the health care industry team at a global professional services firm.
From a young age, my parents instilled in me some important values.
The first is that public service is a noble and important cause.
My mom has served the City of St.
Louis at SLMPD for 30+ years and my dad served our country as a Marine before becoming a Union Sheetmetal worker for 25 years.
They taught me to work hard, to listen, and that showing up matters.
I have family all across the 82nd district including the Lindenwood Park neighborhood and on the Hill.
We are extremely proud of our South City roots.
We love this City and know that it takes a community to create meaningful change.
As many of you know, St.
Louis has an incredible amount of untapped potential.
There are amazing things going on in our City and in our region.
It will take hard work, listening, and a community effort to fix what is broken.
But there has never been so much opportunity to make that change and unite around a city we all love.
That’s why I am running.
I believe that our best days are ahead.
I am looking forward to getting to know more of you throughout the nine neighborhoods in the 82 nd district and uniting around this renewed effort for our City.
