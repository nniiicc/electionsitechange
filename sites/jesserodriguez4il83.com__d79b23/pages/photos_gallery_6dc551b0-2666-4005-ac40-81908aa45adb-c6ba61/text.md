Meet Jesse Issues News Volunteer Contribute Budget Perspective In the lobby of Illinois House Representative, 75th District Jed Davis's lobby is a block of paper, neatly stacked and reaching a height of over 21 inches and a weight of approximately 53.4 pounds.
This stack represents the $55,000,000,000 Illinois State Budget for fiscal year 2025-2026, effective July 1, 2025.
The $55,000,000,000 budget represents a 53% increase since 2019.
The budget bill was submitted for review and approval with 18 hours lead time before voting on it, at the end of regular legislative session.
18 hrs.
The budget contained more than $1,000,000,000 in new taxes, including 1.
Business/corporate tax changes for $336 million in new taxes 2.
Tax amnesty programs for $228 million in new taxes 3.
Tobacco, vape, nicotine tax hikes for $50 million in new taxes 4.
Telecommunications tax increase for $49 million in new taxes 5.
Sports betting transaction tax for $36 million in new taxes.
6.
Short-term rental/hotel operator tax for $10 million in new taxes.
In case you've lost track, there's more than $291 million in new taxes or "revenue enhancements", as they are euphemistically known in Springfield, that are not listed above.
With so little time to review, let alone debate, the budget was a done deal as were the "revenue enhancements".
What other conclusion could anyone reasonably make?
