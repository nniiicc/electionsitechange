​Statement from Jesse Rodriguez, Republican Candidate for Illinois House District 83, on​ ​the Sentencing of Alejandro Cota​ ​The recent life sentence handed to Alejandro Cota for the "brutal and senseless" double murder​ ​of Christina Chavira and her 13-year-old son, Damian, highlights a failure in our justice system.​ ​On December 26, 2024, Cota inflicted "unspeakable and brutal violence" upon this family,​ ​stabbing a young boy 37 times and severing his spinal cord.
While Cota will spend his life in​ ​prison, he still gets to live—a mercy he did not show his victims.​ ​I stand with Kane County State’s Attorney Jamie Mosser, who stated that "this is precisely the​ ​kind of case for which the death penalty should exist".
For horrific crimes such as the murder of​ ​children and our police officers, a life sentence is "inadequate when measured against the​ ​innocent lives that were taken".​ ​While the incumbent, Matt Hanson, remains silent on this specific atrocity and crime in general, I​ ​am making my position clear.
Illinois must reinstate the death penalty for those who commit​ ​such heinous acts of evil.
We cannot afford leaders who are silent while our communities are​ ​"shook... to the core" by violence.
