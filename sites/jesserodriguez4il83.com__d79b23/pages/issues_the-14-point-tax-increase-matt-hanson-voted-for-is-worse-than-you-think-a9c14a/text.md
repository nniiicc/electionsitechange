Had Enough?
While the Collar Counties (DuPage, Kane, Lake, McHenry, and Will) are being asked to shoulder the full burden of the new 0.25% RTA sales tax increase, they get zero control over how it is spent.

The Representation Disadvantage: The 5 Collar Counties represent roughly 3.3 million residents—comparable to Chicago and suburban Cook County.

Chicago, Cook County, and Springfield can easily team up and outvote our local interests 15 to 5.
Rubber-Stamped by Local Reps:
To make matters worse, our own State Representative Matt Hanson (D-Batavia/Aurora) of the 83rd House District fell right in line and voted YES to tax his own constituents.
It is no surprise to see him rubber-stamp this package; he continues to vote exactly as he is directed by his bosses in the Democratic Party Leadership rather than standing up for the hard-working families in his district.
Siphoning Road Funds: The state is also masking the true cost of this bailout by sweeping "interest" income generated from gas tax revenues.

The Bottom Line:
By continuously hiking sales taxes, diverting infrastructure funds, and on top of Tollway increases to cover unnecessary spending plans, Springfield ensures that Illinois remains stuck with one of the highest tax burdens in the entire country.
