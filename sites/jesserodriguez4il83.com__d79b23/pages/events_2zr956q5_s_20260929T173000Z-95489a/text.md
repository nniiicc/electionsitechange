ELECTION EXPO 2026
About this event
The League of Women Voters is a nonpartisan organization working to protect fair elections and increase civic engagement.
The Leagues of Women Voters of Aurora Area, Central Kane County, and Elgin Area are pleased to invite you to be part of ELECTION EXPO 2026.
This nonpartisan event will be family-friendly and free to the public – with activities designed to inform and engage Kane County voters in advance of the upcoming November 3rd election.
(More information about this event and a list of the official event partners and sponsors can be found at https://lwvckcil.com/election-expo.)
Voters will have the opportunity at ELECTION EXPO 2026 to meet and have face-to-face conversations with candidates running for election on November 3rd.
We urge you to pre-register for a table at ELECTION EXPO now by scanning or clicking on the QR code below and submitting your response by Thursday, August 20th.
There is no fee for your participation, and you are welcome to send a surrogate to the event if you run into a conflict and are unable to attend.
We have established Ground Rules to ensure a successful event, fairness to all candidates, and a nonpartisan, inclusive, family-friendly atmosphere.
