Meet Jesse Issues News Volunteer Contribute Meet Jesse I am Jesse Rodriguez, and I am the Republican Candidate for Illinois House Representative, District 83.
I am a lifelong resident of Aurora, Illinois, Roman Catholic, a navy veteran, small business owner, husband, and father.
While in the navy, I served as an aircrewman in VS-22, a carrier based anti-submarine warfare squadron.
I had the honor of deploying aboard the USS John F.
Kennedy, CV-67, making a “Med Cruise”.
My degree is in Finance from Loyola University, Chicago.
I also earned a master's in business administration from Loyola with a focus on operations management and business ethics.
Except for my time in service, I have worked in a private sector and for the past 15 years, I have owned a small paint franchise serving Aurora and surrounding communities.
My experiences are not unusual or unique.
Many Illinoisians serve our county, establish a career, raise our families, or own a business.
But in the past decade or so, choosing to stay in Illinois to live, love and prosper has been called into question.
Since 2013, over 500,000 native Illinoisians have left Illinois, deciding they can more fully live their lives elsewhere.
This is not sustainable.
Our current leaders seem indifferent to the challenges working families and small business owners are experiencing and are in denial as to why so many Illinoisans are choosing to leave.
Our current anti-family environment (what else could I call it?), was not always the case.
My parents' story serves as an example of what Illinois was: a great state to raise a family.
My father, Joe Rodriguez, was born in San Antonio, Texas.
At 8 years of age, his mother died unexpectant and his father, left his small family to look for work in Michigan.
Upon securing work, my grandfather brought his young family up, remarried and established himself in the Detroit area.
My father, after serving in the Army Air Corp and working various jobs in Michigan, decided to head west and strike it on his own.
He made it as far as Aurora, where after meeting my mother, Socorro Olvera, also from Texas, married after a three-week courtship.
They established their family in Aurora and were married over 50 years.
Why did they choose Illinois to live and grow their family?
It was not the ocean front beaches, scenic mountain vistas, or mild winters; it was for good paying jobs, affordable homes in good neighbors and quality schools.
I know, and you also know, of many families and friends that have given up on Illinois and have moved elsewhere.
You may be reading this with your own exit plans in mind or already in process.
It does not have to be like this.
“Illinois is worth fighting for.” William Kirk, of Washinton Gun Law said this on a recent visit to Illinois.
While his comments concerned Illinois laws restricting our 2nd Amendment rights, his general sentiment applies to challenges being imposed on Illinois families by an indifferent Illinois legislature.
That Illinois families are worth fighting for is why I am running.
I have three core areas focus: School Choice- Parents should decide the best educational option for their children; the money should follow.
Affordable Government- legislation should make living in Illinois more affordable and not add to the cost of doing so.
You know better what to do with your money.
Jobs, Jobs, and jobs.
We should make it easy to do business in Illinois.
Whether you own a small business or are the CEO of a multi-national business; We need to make Illinois should be your first choice.
In addition to the core areas of focus above, I believe legislation should be proposed and voted on with respect and adherence to our US constitution.
I recognizing that our constitution is based on the Judeo-Christan principles with respect to Individual liberty, freedom of religion and government through consent of the people.
That our rights are "unalienable" and that they are "endowed" to us by our Creator and not man or government.
Passing bills of questionable or clear violation of our federal constitution with the attitude the courts can decide the “constitutionality” later is irresponsible.
Thanks for taking the time to read my introduction.
I will elaborate on my positions on the issues page of this site.
With your support, I will work hard to meet your expectations within the beliefs and core focus standards I have outlined above.
Together, let's make Illinois a place we are proud to call home.
