A Tradition of Service
Honoring a Legacy of Service
Military service is a proud tradition in my family.
My father, Joe Rodriguez, and his younger brother, Catarino “Jr.” Rodriguez, set the example by answering the call to serve—my father in the Army Air Corps and Uncle Jr. in the United States Army during the Korean War.
I had the honor of serving in the United States Navy as an aircrewman, inspired by another family hero, my uncle Robert “Bobby” Rodriguez, a Vietnam veteran whose pride in the Navy left a lasting impression on me.
This tradition continues today, with cousins and nephews who have served or are currently on active duty.
In an all-volunteer military, our nation depends on young men and women willing to step forward and defend the freedoms we cherish.
I created this page not for personal recognition, but to honor the generations of my family who have worn the uniform and to reaffirm the values that guide me: God.
