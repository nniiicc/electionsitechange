🚨 Breaking: The Hidden Cost of the Tollway Hike
"They talk about Illinois being the shipping hub of the Midwest but they sure are making it hard to afford living and working here."
- “Anonymous”
Last week, I attended the recent “informational” session regarding the Illinois Tollway Authority’s proposed toll hike to fund the $25 billion "Bridging the Future" project.
While the room was packed with special interest groups and those who stand to gain from the spending, the voices of those footing the bill were largely ignored.
The Tollway Authority completely omitted the massive impact this will have on commercial trucking—and ultimately, your wallet.
I spoke with a local operator who shared the harsh reality behind these numbers:
*Daily Impact: A 30% rate hike would raise daily costs for a single truck from $123 to $159.
*Monthly Surge: That translates to an increase of nearly $800 per truck, every month.
*Annual Burden: With 40 trucks running daily routes, this operator’s annual toll expenses will skyrocket by $400,000—bringing their total toll bill to nearly $2 million annually.
The "Doom Loop": This isn't just about trucking.
These costs are passed directly to you at the checkout line through higher prices on goods and services.
This is the third major toll hike for commercial operators in Illinois (following increases in 2015, 2016, and 2017).
Between rising insurance costs, plate fees, and high fuel prices, Illinois is becoming one of the most expensive places to run a business.
It’s time to stop treating Illinois families and small businesses like a revenue source to be drained.
We need leadership that prioritizes working families over endless spending.
