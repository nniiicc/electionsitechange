Assembly Speaker Robert Rivas Endorses Educator Amy Slavensky for California Assembly District 7
AD 7 is one of the most competitive red-to-blue pickups in 2026
SACRAMENTO, CA—Today, the campaign for Assembly District 7 candidate Amy Slavensky announced that she has been endorsed by Speaker of the California Assembly Robert Rivas.
"You don’t spend a lifetime in education without a deep desire to serve and the know-how to get work done, and that’s why I’m endorsing Amy,” said Speaker Rivas.
"The issues facing California — rising healthcare premiums, rising costs of living, and an imposing budget deficit created by a hostile federal administration — demand leadership, integrity, and the ability to deliver.
I see all these qualities in Amy.
She is the champion District 7 deserves.”
“I am honored by the Speaker’s endorsement,” said Amy Slavensky, candidate for Assembly District 7.
“As both a candidate and a voter, I have been impressed by Speaker Rivas’s leadership and demonstrated commitment to the wellbeing of Californians.
Under his leadership, I see how the Assembly is not just focused on addressing our most pressing issues, but also working to make changes that will protect and strengthen our rights, benefiting District 7 and all Californians in the long term.”
The Speaker’s endorsement is another sign that Assembly District 7 is a prime pickup opportunity for Democrats in 2026.
Democratic voter registration surpasses Republicans by over 5%, with higher than average turnout expected among Democrats as discontent with Republicans at every level of government rises.
In addition to Speaker Rivas, endorsements from the Sacramento Central Labor Council, Plumbers & Pipefitters Local 447, and a growing list of local clubs and community leaders further reinforce strong, community-based support for Amy and her campaign.
California’s 7th Assembly district is located in Sacramento County.
