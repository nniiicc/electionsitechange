Major Support from CTA, CFT, CSEA, and the California Democratic Party’s Pre-Endorsement in Race for California’s 7th Assembly District May 11 2026 For Immediate release Majority Leader Cecilia Aguiar-Curry Endorses Amy Slavensky for California Assembly District 7 SACRAMENTO, CA— Today, Amy Slavensky announced that she has been endorsed by State Assembly Majority Leader Cecilia Aguiar-Curry in the 7th Assembly District race, following the endorsement from Speaker Robert Rivas last month.
"All Californians are looking to the Assembly to lead in a way that meets the moment, and I know that Amy is ready to do so,” said Majority Leader Cecilia Aguiar-Curry.
“Amy knows how to bring folks together and get work done, especially when it’s hard, from healthcare, to education, to affordability.
It’s a trait that many women, like Amy, who have risen through the ranks in their careers possess, and that’s exactly the type of leadership we need in the Assembly now.
I would be proud to count her as a colleague.” “I have so much respect for Majority Leader Cecilia Aguiar-Curry, and it is a true honor to have her endorsement,” said Amy Slavensky, candidate for Assembly District 7.
“Since the start of my campaign, the majority leader’s wisdom and encouragement have been pivotal to me as a first-time candidate.
It speaks to how she leads in the Assembly and the California Democratic Legislative Women’s Caucus, looking for opportunities to uplift the people she serves.” California’s 7th Assembly district is located in Sacramento County.
