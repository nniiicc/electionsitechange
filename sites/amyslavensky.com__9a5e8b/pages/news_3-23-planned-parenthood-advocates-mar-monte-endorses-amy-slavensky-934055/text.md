Planned Parenthood Advocates Mar Monte Endorses Amy Slavensky for California’s 7th Assembly District
SACRAMENTO, CA—Today, Amy Slavensky was endorsed by Planned Parenthood Advocates Mar Monte (PPAMM), which works to elect candidates that support access to reproductive health care and access, a top issue for California voters.
This endorsement adds to the momentum Amy’s campaign is building from additional endorsements from the California Democratic Legislative Women’s Caucus, the California Women’s list, and Women Democrats of Sacramento County.
"We are proud to endorse Amy Slavensky for Assembly District 7 because she will be a true champion for reproductive rights in Sacramento," said Candelaria Vargas, director of public affairs for PPAMM.
"She believes in protecting and expanding Californians' access to reproductive health care, especially during these precarious times of funding uncertainty for all healthcare.
Together with Amy, we fight for Californians' rights and access to reproductive health care without the interference of politicians or the government."
Current District 7 Assemblymember, Josh Hoover, has a track record of poor support for reproductive healthcare, earning an “F” in Reproductive Freedom for All’s 2025 Scorecard and 50% from Planned Parenthood Affiliates of California’s 2023 scorecard.
California’s 7th Assembly district is located in Sacramento County.
