Congressman Ami Bera Endorses Amy Slavensky for California Assembly District 7
SACRAMENTO, CA—Congressman Ami Bera, currently representing California’s 6th District and running for California’s redrawn 3rd District, has endorsed educator Amy Slavensky in the competitive race for Assembly District 7, which overlaps both congressional districts.
"Amy and I share a deep, ongoing commitment to public service and the wellbeing of folks in our community, ” said Congressman Ami Bera.
“Both of us believe in making Sacramento a place where everybody can live a good life and see their hard work pay off.
That means housing and healthcare that’s accessible and affordable, strong and safe public schools for our kids, and more.
President Trump and his allies don’t care how their agenda hurts working Californians, but we do.
I know Amy is ready to do everything possible in the Assembly to fight back and put people first.”
“It’s an honor to be endorsed by Congressman Ami Bera,” said Amy Slavensky, candidate for Assembly District 7.
“We are both committed to serving and protecting our communities from President Trump and his enablers in Sacramento.
I will be proud to be an ally in that fight for Assembly District 7.”
Congressman Bera’s endorsement follows that of Dr.
Richard Pan, candidate for California's 6th District, solidifying Amy’s support from fellow Democratic candidates running competitive campaigns in and around Sacramento County.
