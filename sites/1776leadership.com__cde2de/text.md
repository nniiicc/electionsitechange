Who I Am A LIFE ROOTED IN MICHIGAN.
Beth Socia is a lifelong Michigander who was born in Mt.
Clemens and raised on Harsens Island.
Her campaign is rooted in the values she learned early: faith, family, personal responsibility, integrity, and service to others.
As one of seven children, Beth grew up in a hardworking family that taught her to give back to the community.
Beth worked for both the Sheriff Department and City Police Department, gaining firsthand experience in law enforcement and public service.
She also worked as a Certified Nurse’s Assistant before dedicating herself full-time to raising her family — experience that reinforced her respect for service, responsibility, and the people who hold communities together.
Now, Beth is running to bring a conservative, people-first perspective to Lansing — not to grow government, but to make it answer to Michigan families.
