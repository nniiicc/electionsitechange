Use of Cookies Information we Collect We collect various types of information, including, but not limited to: Personal information (such as name, email address, phone number, and/or your address) provided by you.
Non-personal browsing and site usage data through the use of cookies.
Use of Your Information We use the information we collect to: Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
Communicate with you about the Chris Gilmer-Hill for State Rep campaign and related activities.

Communications may include but are not limited to campaign updates, donation requests, and event details.
Data Sharing and Disclosure Information, including indication of consent, will not be shared with any third party, except: (1) with vendors, consultants, and other service providers who need access to such information to carry out work on our behalf (and who will not use such information for their own purposes); (2) if we believe disclosure is required by any applicable law, rule, or regulation or to comply with law enforcement or legal process.
Contributions to Friends of Chris Gilmer-Hill Friends of Chris Gilmer-Hill is required to file regular reports with the Michigan Department of State that publicly disclose the name, address, occupation and employer of persons who contribute during an election cycle, along with the amount and date of the contribution for individuals who contribute $100 or more over the election cycle.
