About Representative Carol Dalby
- Chair: House Judiciary Committee.
- Graduate of Ouachita Baptist University & the University of Arkansas School of Law (Fayetteville).
- Former President of the Texarkana School Board.
- Appointed to serve as District Judge in Miller County & as a Special Justice to the Arkansas Supreme Court.
- Member: Junior League of Texarkana.
- Former President: Texarkana Regional Arts Council/Women for the Arts.
- President of the Library Commission.
- Chair: Miller County Red Cross.
- Board Member:
- Arkansas Women for Education.
- Texarkana Regional Center on Aging.
- Texarkana Friends of UAMS.
- She and her husband John are members of First United Methodist Church and the proud parents of two grown children.
