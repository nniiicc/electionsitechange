Running for Legislature in District 10
Cindy Johnson
Cindy grew up the oldest of 7, on a farm in northeast Nebraska.
After attending the University of Nebraska-Lincoln for her Bachelor’s degree in Education, she pursued a Master’s degree and eventually obtained her doctorate in audiology.
She and her family made Madison County their home for 17 years.
After working for 9 years, Cindy started her own audiology practice in Norfolk.
Eventually, she made her way to Omaha to Children’s Hospital where she spent 15 years evaluating and treating the hearing of Nebraska’s children and advocating for their educational needs.
Currently, Cindy still practices part time and provides hearing evaluation services, with dedicated appointments for veterans to help those who have served our country.

Everyday items and annual bills are getting more and more expensive.
From groceries, to rent, to property taxes, Cindy knows the financial strain our residents are under.
Nebraskans should be able to thrive, not just survive.
That’s why she’ll fight to bring relief to everyday Nebraskans and support a strong middle class.
Cindy knows that families depend on access to affordable, quality healthcare.
She will work to cut prescription drug costs, while encouraging innovation that ensures Nebraskans are getting the medicine they need, without paying an arm and a leg.
Nebraska voters made their voices heard on several ballot initiatives in 2024.
Now, the Legislature is stalling and overturning them.
As an audiologist, Cindy knows how important listening is; that’s why she will fight to protect the will of the voters and push back against any effort that makes it harder for the people to be heard.
