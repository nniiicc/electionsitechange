About Mike
Deep Idaho Roots
Raised in Idaho since he was ten, Mike has proudly called Soda Springs home for nearly 30 years.
He knows the land, the people, and the values of District 35 because he's lived them.
His wife Shelly — from Downey — and their four children are the foundation of everything he does.
With a Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering and over three decades in heavy industry, Mike brings real-world expertise to the legislature.
He now serves as a contract and consulting engineer, continuing to solve problems with the same practical, results-driven approach he applies in public service.
"I have lived in Soda Springs because of the treasured natural resources — from September elk bugles to a fighting cutthroat on a 5-weight, to a pail full of huckleberries."
Mike and his wife Shelly — along with their four children and son-in-law — are proud to call Soda Springs home.
Mike is honored to represent the shared values of rural Idaho — individual autonomy, freedom, limited government, and stewardship of the land itself — for all of District 35.

Shaping Idaho's Future
Investing in Our Children
Mike values education deeply and recognizes the changes happening — and needed — to ensure Idaho's children become productive, contributing citizens prepared for tomorrow's economy.
- Support strong public school systems in rural Idaho communities
- Advance workforce and technical training tied to Idaho's key industries
- Promote parental involvement and local control of curriculum
- Ensure Idaho students are competitive and career-ready
Stewardship & Access
Protecting Idaho's Public Lands & Waters
Mike doesn't just talk about Idaho's outdoor heritage — he lives it.
From his bow in elk country to fly fishing cutthroat in crystal rivers, these lands are part of who he is and who he fights for.
- Preserve hunting and fishing access on Idaho's public lands
- Protect Idaho water rights and agricultural water supply
- Oppose federal overreach on Idaho's lands and natural resources
- Balance conservation with responsible resource development
- Protect the traditions and freedoms Idaho families have always enjoyed
Keeping Idaho's Economy Competitive
Economy & Employment
The phosphate industry, agriculture, and Idaho National Laboratory are not just employers — they are the backbone of District 35 and critical to our national security.
Mike fights to keep them vibrant and competitive.
- Champion Idaho's phosphate industry as a critical national resource
- Defend agricultural water rights and farm viability
- Support INL's growth as a nuclear energy and defense leader
- Keep Idaho's regulatory burden among the lowest in the nation
- Support small and large businesses as the engine of our communities
