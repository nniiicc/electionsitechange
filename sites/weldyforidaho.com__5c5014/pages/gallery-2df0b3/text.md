Growing up in Lewiston, I learned that leadership should be about stability, transparency, and service, not the division we see today.
As a business owner with over 25 years of real estate experience, I am ready to restore that commitment to our community by listening to every resident and working across the aisle to bring affordability, strong public education, and our doctors back to North Idaho.
I support:
- Affordable Housing & Property Tax Relief
- No Tax on Groceries, Cutting Food Costs by 6%
- Bring Doctors Back & Expand Rural Health Care
- Fund Public Schools & Career Technical Education
- Save Idaho's Heritage by Defending Public Lands & Water
