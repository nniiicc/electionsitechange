My family moved to Charles County in 1977.
I am a graduate of McDonough High School, Class of 1981.
I served eight years in the US Navy as a Nuclear Machinist Mate.
Achieving the rank of E-6 Petty Officer First Class supervising fourteen shipmates.
Serving aboard the USS Nimitz CVN-68, USS Texas CGN-39 and USS Iwo Jima.
The Navy taught me the value of teamwork, integrity and honor.
After completing my Navy service I returned to Charles County.
I worked at the Nuclear Power Plant in Calvert County for thirty-two years, receiving a Reactor Operator License from the NRC.
I retired in February 2022.
I have thee daughters, three granddaughters and a grandson.
I attend St Peters Catholic Church in Waldorf.
