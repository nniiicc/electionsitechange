MEET GABE A Candidate Who Puts His Constituents Before Lobbyists As a lifelong resident of Daytona Beach, my roots in this district's soil are deep and my commitment to this community is personal.
I didn't just grow up here; I've invested my life and education into the success of our region, earning my master's degree in Hospitality & Tourism from the University of Central Florida to better understand the economic engine that drives our home.
In 2016, I affiliated the Libertarian Party of Volusia County because I recognized our district deserved a voice that prioritizes individual liberty over bureaucratic overreach.
Today, as chairman, I continue to lead that charge, ensuring that the values of freedom and self-reliance remain at the fore front of our local political conversation. ​ My philosophy is simple: this campaign is not a pursuit of power, but a job interview.
While my opponents often act as though they are meant to "lead" you, I understand that the role of a senator is to represent you.
I am an avid member of Turning Point USA and a firm believer in the libertarian and conservative principles that protect our way of life.
In Fact , I am prepared to prove that my platform is more consistently conservative than any Republican in this race.
I don't answer to party bosses or special interests; I answer to you.
I will always vote with my conscience, guided by what is truly best for the people of this district through your eyes, not those of Tallahassee lobbyists. ​ If elected, I will work tirelessly to bring a "Florida First" approach to the Florida Senate, focusing on cutting the taxes and regulations that stifle our local entrepreneurs.
We need a representative who views every piece of legislation through the lens of constitutional rights and personal responsibility.
My goal is to ensure Florida remains a bastion of freedom where your family and business can thrive without state interference.
I am asking for your vote so that I can go to work for you, defending our values and ensuring that the voice of our district is heard loud and clear. ​ Trust me with your vote, and you will never regret it. ​ ​ Gabe Wozniak JOIN THE CAMPAIGN!
