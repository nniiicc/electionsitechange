Accessibility Statement This statement reflects our ongoing efforts to enhance the accessibility of Hutchings for House's website.
We are dedicated to making our site accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities.
COMMITMENT TO ACCESSIBILITY At Hutchings for House, we are dedicated to ensuring that our website is accessible and easy to use for everyone.
We believe in providing an inclusive online experience and are committed to adhering to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) [2.0 / 2.1 / 2.2 - select relevant option].
Our team has worked to make the necessary adjustments to ensure that our site is perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust for all users.
ACCESSIBILITY FEATURES We have implemented various accessibility features to enhance the user experience for individuals with disabilities.
Our site has been designed to work seamlessly with assistive technologies, including screen readers and keyboard navigation.
Some of the key accessibility adjustments we have made include: Integration of the Accessibility Wizard to identify and address potential accessibility issues Setting the language of the site to ensure proper interpretation Establishing clear heading structures across all pages Providing descriptive alternative text for images Adopting color combinations with adequate contrast ratios Minimizing the use of motion to prevent distraction Ensuring accessibility of all multimedia content COMPLIANCE AND EXCEPTIONS While we strive for full compliance, there may be certain pages or content on our site that are provided by third parties and are beyond our immediate control.
In such cases, we declare partial compliance and are actively working to address any accessibility barriers.
We are committed to ensuring that all aspects of our website meet the highest accessibility standards.
