As the founder of FIT4MOM Palm Beach, she has built a community supporting moms through pregnancy and early motherhood, giving her a firsthand understanding of the challenges families face every day.
Emily is running for reelection to the State House because Florida has become too expensive for the people who live and work here.
Like so many families, she has felt the strain of rising costs, from groceries and gas to housing and insurance, and has seen how hard people are working just to keep up.
Through her work in public health and in her community, she’s met families doing everything right but still struggling to get ahead.
She’s running for reelection to take on the affordability crisis head-on, to lower costs, fix the property insurance system, strengthen public schools, and make sure Florida is a place where families can build a future again.
Emily believes in practical, people-first leadership that puts working families over politics and delivers real results for the community she calls home.
“Florida families are working hard, but it’s getting harder to get ahead.
I believe in the strength of our community to come together and fight for a future that’s actually affordable, where families can thrive, not just get by.” As your representative for State House District 87, Emily Gregory is committed to lowering costs, standing up for working families, and delivering real solutions that make life more affordable and secure for our community.
