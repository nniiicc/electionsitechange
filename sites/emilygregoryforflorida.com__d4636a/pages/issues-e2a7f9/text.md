From groceries and gas to utilities and healthcare, costs keep rising while paychecks struggle to keep up.
Emily will fight for real solutions that bring down everyday expenses, raise wages, and give working families the breathing room they deserve.
That means standing up to corporate price gouging, supporting policies that ease the burden on household budgets, and making sure Florida is a place where hard work actually pays off.
Fixing the Property Insurance Crisis Homeowners across Florida are being crushed by skyrocketing insurance premiums, with too many families forced to choose between keeping coverage and keeping up with other bills.
Emily will take on the insurance companies that are raising rates and denying claims, and push for accountability and transparency in the system.
She supports expanding affordable, state-backed insurance options, cracking down on predatory practices, and delivering real relief so families can stay in their homes and communities.
Strengthening Public Education Strong public schools are essential to strong communities, but right now, they’re being underfunded and undermined.
Emily will fight to fully fund our neighborhood schools, raise teacher pay, and ensure every student has access to a high-quality education, no matter their zip code.
She believes taxpayer dollars should stay in public schools and be used to support students, teachers, and classrooms, not diverted to unaccountable programs.
Investing in education is key to lowering costs for families and building a stronger future.
