My re-election platform has three key planks:
- Protect our Constitutional Rights - I took an oath to uphold our U.S. and Washington State Constitutions, and I will defend our rights.
This includes fighting expansion of the unconstitutional income tax, defending gun rights, and supporting religious liberty.
- Support our Rural 7th District Way of Life - We need to make farming, logging, and ranching easier and subject to less oversight by Olympia.
We need to streamline and shorten the process for private timber owners to harvest their own timber.
We must protect property rights.
- Increase Washington's Prosperity - Washington is lagging in key indicators of competitiveness and affordability.
We must reduce burdensome taxation and regulation, improve our public education system, and make Washington a more prosperous and affordable state to run a business and raise a family.
