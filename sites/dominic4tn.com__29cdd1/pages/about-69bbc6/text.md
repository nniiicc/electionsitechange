Meet Dominic Howard: The Official District 46 Needs in Nashville For over 30 years, Dominic Howard has been the man behind the plate, on the court, and on the field.
As a professional sports official, he knows that a fair game requires two things: everyone following the same set of rules and someone with the guts to make the correct call under pressure.
Now, Dominic is running for State Representative for District 46 because he’s seen the "rulebook" in Nashville being ignored.
For too long, the game has been rigged for big business and the wealthy, while Wilson County families are left picking up the tab.
With two decades of experience managing complex construction and maintenance projects, Dominic understands infrastructure—and he knows that Wilson County’s growth shouldn't be a burden on your wallet.
He’ll fight to ensure that new developments pay their own way, protecting you from endless property tax hikes.
Dominic’s Game Plan for Tennessee: • Fair Pay for Honest Work: It’s time for a livable wage that respects the dignity of every worker. • Protecting Our Schools: Dominic will move to eliminate the school voucher program.
Public tax dollars belong in public schools, providing every child—whether they are headed for a university, a trade school, or directly into the job market—with a clear path to success. • A Level Playing Field: From housing costs to education, Dominic is running to restore the common middle ground where every resident is represented fairly.
On November 3rd, let’s make the Correct Call for District 46.
