I signed the 12-senator pledge to block the budget until property rights were protected.
I voted yes on HF 639 to put real limits on eminent domain for private pipelines.
When the governor vetoed the bill, I stood firm.
Your land is your land, and I will never stop fighting to protect it.
Pushing the biggest property tax overhaul in decades I fought for Senate File 2472, a bill reforming Iowa’s property tax system.
This monumental reform would give homeowners major property tax relief.
Iowa taxpayers will keep more of their hard earned money in their pockets.
You know how to spend it better than the government!
Delivering for Iowa schools As Chair of the Education Committee, I helped secure over $1.23 billion in new K-12 funding since 2017, with nearly $100 million more proposed this year.
Reading proficiency among third graders jumped 11% in a single year.
After 30 years in Iowa classrooms, education is not a talking point for me.
It is my life's work.
Passed SF 643 into law I drafted and floor-managed legislation requiring the state to reimburse counties for holding sexually violent offenders in county jails.
This common-sense bill passed the Senate, was signed by the governor, and is now law saving our county budget thousands of dollars each year!
It is the kind of practical result that makes a real difference for local governments and community safety.
