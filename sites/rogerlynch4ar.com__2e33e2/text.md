Who is State Representative Roger Lynch?
Choosing to work and raise his family in our area, Roger has always strived to be an active part of our community.
- Married to Sheila for 30 years
- Father of four adult children
- Owner of Lynch HVACR, LLC
- Former Operations Manager at Remington
- Lonoke County Justice of the Peace since 2005
- Treasurer of Grace Chapel Baptist Church Board of Directors for Lonoke County Coalition for Progress
