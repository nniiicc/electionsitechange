Donate Get Involved Meet Rhys A lifelong numbers guy, running because the numbers in Springfield don't add up.
Rhys Read is a certified public accountant by training who spent his career as a corporate controller and treasurer, including running a major pension fund — the person responsible for keeping the books accurate, the budgeting honest, and long-term obligations fully funded.
He knows what it takes to manage other people's money responsibly, because he did it for decades.
Rhys has also given his time to the community, including nine years on the board of the Des Plaines Public Library as its finance committee chairman, where he helped provide fiscal oversight of a public tax levy on behalf of the taxpayers who fund it.
“I've spent my career reading the numbers so leaders could make honest decisions.
Illinois families deserve the same in Springfield.” Rhys has always been an independent-minded, taxpayers-first voice — someone who has judged every candidate and every budget on the merits, not on party loyalty.
That independence is exactly what he'll bring to the Illinois House: a fiscal conservative who reads the fine print, asks hard questions, and answers to the people of District 56, not to Springfield insiders.
Outside of public service, Rhys is a longtime civic volunteer and an active leader in Illinois competitive chess, where he has spent years teaching patience, strategy, and the discipline to think several moves ahead — the same approach he'll bring to fixing our state's finances.

Four priorities for Illinois House District 56 — grounded in a career of reading budgets, not political talking points.
01 / PROPERTY TAXES Property-Tax Relief Illinois homeowners pay among the highest property taxes in the entire country.
Rhys will fight in Springfield to cap unsustainable growth, close wasteful spending, and give homeowners and seniors real relief — not another gimmick.
02 / SPRINGFIELD ACCOUNTABILITY Fiscal Responsibility A certified public accountant by training who spent his career balancing real budgets, Rhys knows the difference between an honest number and a Springfield gimmick.
He'll demand balanced budgets, real accounting discipline, and an end to kicking the can down the road.
Photo: Daniel Schwen, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons 03 / JOBS & THE ECONOMY Jobs and a Competitive Illinois High taxes and heavy-handed government push employers, families, and even our iconic Illinois institutions to look elsewhere.
Rhys will work to keep our state competitive — so businesses, good jobs, and the teams and traditions we love have every reason to stay, grow, and invest right here at home.
04 / QUALITY OF LIFE Safe, Thriving Communities Strong local schools, safe neighborhoods, and reliable services are what make Des Plaines, Schaumburg, Elk Grove Village, Roselle, and Hoffman Estates great places to raise a family.
Rhys will fight to protect and strengthen them.
