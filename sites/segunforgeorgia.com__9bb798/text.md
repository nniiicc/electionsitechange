Segun is focused on the issues families talk about every day: the cost of living, traffic, schools, healthcare, safe neighborhoods, and the future of fast-growing communities across House District 110.
Affordability & Cost of Living Gwinnett families are working hard, but too many are feeling squeezed by housing, childcare, healthcare, and everyday bills.
Segun is focused on practical steps that help residents stay rooted in the communities they love.
Smarter Growth, Better Roads & Modern Infrastructure Growth should bring opportunity, not longer commutes and strained infrastructure.
Segun is working for road, transit, broadband, utility, and stormwater investments that match the pace of life in HD 110.
Strong Schools & a Future Ready Workforce Strong schools are the foundation of strong communities.
Segun supports early learning, literacy, technical education, and workforce pathways that prepare students for college, careers, and a changing economy.

Segun is fighting for better access to coverage, mental health care, maternal health support, and community-based services.
