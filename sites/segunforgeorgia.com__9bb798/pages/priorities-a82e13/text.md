Segun is focused on the issues that shape daily life in Southeast Gwinnett: affordability, education, healthcare, infrastructure, economic mobility, safety, and healthy neighborhoods.
Affordability & Cost of Living Smarter Growth, Better Roads & Modern Infrastructure Strong Schools & a Future Ready Workforce Accessible, Affordable Healthcare Small Business Growth & Economic Mobility Community Safety Through Prevention & Partnership Clean, Resilient & Healthy Neighborhoods Affordability & Cost of Living Gwinnett families are working hard, but too many are feeling squeezed by housing, childcare, healthcare, and everyday bills.
Segun is focused on practical steps that help residents stay rooted in the communities they love.
Expand access to affordable childcare and early learning programs Support measures that stabilize property taxes for long-time homeowners Reduce prescription drug costs through statewide pricing reforms Increase access to affordable housing through workforce housing incentives Strengthen protections for renters facing rising housing costs Smarter Growth, Better Roads & Modern Infrastructure Growth should bring opportunity, not longer commutes and strained infrastructure.
Segun is working for road, transit, broadband, utility, and stormwater investments that match the pace of life in HD 110.
Secure state funding for road expansions and congestion relief Improve traffic flow with synchronized lights and safer intersections Expand high-speed broadband access across HD 110 Support regional transit options connecting residents to job centers Modernize stormwater and utility infrastructure to keep pace with growth Strong Schools & a Future Ready Workforce Strong schools are the foundation of strong communities.
Segun supports early learning, literacy, technical education, and workforce pathways that prepare students for college, careers, and a changing economy.
Strengthen early literacy and Pre-K access Expand STEM, robotics, and technical education programs Build partnerships with Gwinnett Tech and local employers Support HOPE Scholarship access and college affordability Promote workforce training for high-demand industries Accessible, Affordable Healthcare Healthcare should be close, affordable, and responsive to the needs of families.
Segun is fighting for better access to coverage, mental health care, maternal health support, and community-based services.
Support expanding healthcare coverage for uninsured residents Increase funding for mental health and behavioral health services Improve maternal health outcomes through targeted programs Expand telehealth access for rural and suburban families Strengthen community-based clinics and urgent care resources Small Business Growth & Economic Mobility Small businesses create jobs, anchor neighborhoods, and open doors for families.
Segun supports policies that expand access to capital, strengthen local procurement, and help residents build new skills.
Expand access to capital for small and minority-owned businesses Support state-funded technical assistance and business incubators Promote workforce development programs that help residents upskill Encourage entrepreneurship through streamlined licensing and support Strengthen local procurement opportunities for small businesses Community Safety Through Prevention & Partnership Public safety starts before a crisis.
Segun believes in prevention, trusted partnerships, youth programs, safer streets, and mental health responses that help protect every neighborhood.
Support evidence-based gun safety measures Expand youth programs and after-school initiatives Strengthen mental health first responder models Improve traffic safety with better lighting and crosswalks Promote community police partnerships that build trust Clean, Resilient & Healthy Neighborhoods Healthy neighborhoods need clean air, reliable water systems, protected greenspace, and infrastructure that can handle growth.
Segun is committed to resilient communities that remain livable for the next generation.
