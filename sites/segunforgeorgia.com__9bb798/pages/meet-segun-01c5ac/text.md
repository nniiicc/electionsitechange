Working for every family.
In 2014, my family relocated to Georgia and immediately became Gwinnett County residents, making our home in Grayson.
Since then, the growth and progress for our family assures my wife and I that we made the right decision to live in Gwinnett.
I am running for office because I want to see every family in our community thrive.
People in Snellville, Lawrenceville, Grayson and Loganville deserve a State Representative that listens to them, fights for our shared values and is invested in our community.
Segun's public service is grounded in a simple belief: families should not have to fight state government to be heard.
They deserve leadership that shows up, explains the stakes, and works across the issues that shape daily life, from traffic and childcare costs to schools, healthcare, housing, and public safety.
In the State House, Segun brings the perspective of a husband, father, neighbor, and public servant who has seen how quickly Gwinnett is changing.
His priority is making sure that growth creates opportunity, not added pressure, for the people who already call this community home.
