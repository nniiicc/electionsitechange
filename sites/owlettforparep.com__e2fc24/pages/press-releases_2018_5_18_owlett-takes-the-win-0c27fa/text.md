Owlett swept the Special Election as well as the Republican primary, Tuesday night, May 15th, 2018.
Owlett ran two campaigns this spring as he competed against a Democratic candidate for the Special Election seat, to immediately fill the remainder of Matt Baker’s term for PA State Representative of the 68th District, as well as against a Republican opponent in the Republican Primary.
The races came to conclusion last night as the results filed into the county courthouses.
Owlett took 71% of Tioga County and 77% of the vote in Bradford County for the Special Election seat.
For the Primary, Owlett took 78% in Tioga County and 66% in Bradford County, resulting in a party unifying win for the new State Representative.
Readers can visit www.electionreturns.pa.gov for official results.
“I would like to give a heartfelt thank you to all who voted yesterday.
I am honored and so grateful to have the opportunity to serve the people of the 68th District,” said Owlett, “Thank you to all the volunteers, my team and family who supported us every step of the way.” Clint and his team met at the Timeless Destination Restaurant to see the results come in.
“I look forward to starting next week and keeping the voters up to date on the budget process this summer.
Thank you again to all the voters who came out yesterday!” said Owlett.
