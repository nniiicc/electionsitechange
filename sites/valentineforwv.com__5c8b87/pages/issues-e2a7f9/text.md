- An oversized budget surplus isn't a sign of success- it means taxpayers paid more than they should have.
We must reform our tax system to return more of those dollars to the Eastern Panhandle, prioritizing working families over corporations and reinvesting in the communities where that money was earned- especially as households struggle with high cost of living.

I am committed to working with my Eastern Panhandle colleagues to deliver locality pay that retains our workforce, strengthens our economy, and keeps West Virginians working in West Virginia.
