Georgia House District 57
About Georgia House District 57
District 57 is located in Northeast Atlanta.
Miami Circle is the northern border, North Avenue is the southern border and it sits between Peachtree Street on the west and the Fulton/DeKalb Line to the east.
Including these neighborhoods:
- Ansley Park
- Brookwood
- Brookwood Hills
- Garden Hills
- Lenox Park
- Lindbergh/Morosgo
- Lindridge-Martin Manor
- Midtown
- Morningside
- Old Fourth Ward
- Peachtree Hills
- Piedmont Heights (East)
- Sherwood Forest
- Virginia Highland
