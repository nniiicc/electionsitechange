Meet the Team
I’m excited to introduce my new Chief of Staff, Rachel Schiffer.
Rachel is a lifelong Atlantan who has been active in Georgia politics since she was old enough to vote.
She graduated cum laude from Haverford College with a B.S. in political science in 2023.
Rachel worked as a paralegal before joining the GA House Democratic Caucus last year, and most recently worked on the Harris-Walz campaign.
Outside of the Capitol, you can find her singing as a member of the Atlanta Symphony Orchestra Chorus.
You can reach her at rachel@staceyevans.com.
We are also joined by my Intern, Henry Moye.
Henry is a junior at Midtown High School who has a deep passion for politics and law!
At his school, Henry enjoys competing on the Midtown Public Forum Debate team, where he is the captain of a team that frequently attends tournaments on the national level.
Also, Henry is the Managing Editor for the Opinion section of Midtown’s newspaper, the Southerner, where he enjoys writing about a variety of topics, spanning from political current events to education policy.
Outside of school, Henry enjoys spending time with his friends and family, and he also enjoys playing basketball and soccer.
In the future, Henry hopes to major in political science at the University of North Carolina, and then eventually become a lawyer.
Henry is very excited to begin his internship at the House of Representatives and learn more about the importance of political representation in everyday life!
Swearing In
We began session on Monday by being sworn in.
I was thrilled to have my son and husband join me as I took the oath.
State of the State
This week was mostly ceremonial.
All House and Senate members were sworn in and the Governor gave his annual State of the State address.
There was good and bad in his speech.
The good was his commitment to investments needed for hurricane relief in our state.
The bad was how he seemed to double down on his refusal to work with the Legislature to expand Medicaid to cover uninsured Georgians.
Committee Assignments
Committee assignments for the 2025-26 Legislative Session are here.
I’m excited to join the Rules Committee, which is the last line of defense before any bill can be heard in the House.
I’m also glad to join the Judiciary Subcommittee of Appropriations where we will hear from the courts, prosecutors, and public defenders about their budget needs.
I will remain a member of the Judiciary, Retirement, and Technology and Infrastructure Innovation Committees.
Policy Committee
I am the co-chair of the Democratic Caucus’s Policy Committee.
Along with co-chair Rep.
David Wilkerson and our elected leadership, I will be leading my colleagues in shaping our Caucus’s policy positions and developing a unified strategy to fight for bills that will serve the needs of our constituents.
Cervical Cancer Awareness Day
This Thursday, I welcomed Triana James and dozens of supporters to mark Cervical Cancer Awareness Day.
The House adopted HR 28, my resolution honoring the Georgia Cancer Control Consortium, HPV Cancer Free GA, Cancer Pathways, and The Susan Jolley Awareness Program for their work in promoting cervical cancer education and awareness.
