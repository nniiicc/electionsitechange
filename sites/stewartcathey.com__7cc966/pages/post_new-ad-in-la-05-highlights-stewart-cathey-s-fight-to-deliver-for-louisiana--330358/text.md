New Ad in LA-05 Highlights Stewart Cathey’s Fight to Deliver for Louisiana Veterans
| FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE CONTACT: info@stewartcathey.com The Stewart Cathey for Congress campaign announced the launch of a new ad, “Earned,” that will reach voters throughout Louisiana’s 5th Congressional District.
The spot highlights Stewart Cathey’s personal commitment as a veteran to fighting for his fellow veterans by cutting government red tape, protecting the benefits they’ve earned, and ensuring their service is honored with action.
The ad, “Earned,” will run throughout Louisiana’s 5th Congressional District and is part of an ongoing advertising campaign.
“Serving in the military gave me an even deeper respect for the men and women who answered our country’s call.
That’s why I’ve worked to cut through red tape and deliver results for veterans, and in Congress, I’ll keep fighting to ensure they receive the care, benefits, education, and opportunities they’ve earned,” said Stewart Cathey.
