We use the information we collect to:

- Improve our website and better understand how visitors use it
- Respond to your inquiries and requests

Our website may use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to understand site traffic, improve user experience, and support digital advertising efforts, including retargeting on third-party platforms.
You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though doing so may affect certain site features.
Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information:
- With vendors and service providers who assist with campaign operations, such as payment processing, email delivery, and digital advertising, and who are contractually obligated to safeguard your information
- With the Federal Election Commission and other government entities, as required by campaign finance law
- With affiliated committees, party organizations, and allied campaigns, consistent with applicable law
- When required to comply with a legal obligation, court order, or governmental request
- In connection with the reasonable operation of the campaign, such as coordinating with our compliance and fundraising vendors
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information we collect.
However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Children's Privacy
Our website is not directed to individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.
Your Choices
You may unsubscribe from our email communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in each email, opt out of text messages by replying "STOP," or contact us directly to request that we remove your information from our communications lists, subject to any records we are legally required to retain.
