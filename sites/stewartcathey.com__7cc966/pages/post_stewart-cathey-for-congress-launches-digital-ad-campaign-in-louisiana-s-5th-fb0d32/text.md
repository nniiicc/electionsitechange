Stewart Cathey for Congress Launches Digital Ad Campaign in Louisiana’s 5th Congressional District
| FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE CONTACT: info@stewartcathey.com The Stewart Cathey for Congress campaign announced the launch of a new ad campaign today that will reach voters throughout Louisiana’s 5th Congressional District.
The spot highlights Stewart Cathey’s record of service and leadership - and his commitment to continuing that service by fighting for affordability, freedom, opportunity for Louisiana families.
The ad, “Seat at the Table,” will run on social media and digital platforms throughout Louisiana’s 5th Congressional District and is the beginning of an ongoing advertising campaign.
“I’ve been fortunate to serve my country in the Army and my community here at home in Louisiana.
I’ve always believed that leadership means listening, working hard, and standing up for what’s right,” said Stewart Cathey.
“I’m running for Congress because I believe there’s more work to do—protecting our freedoms, making life more affordable, and creating more opportunity for Louisiana families.” View the ad here. |
