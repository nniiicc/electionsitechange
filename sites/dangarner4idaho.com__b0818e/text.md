I will vigorously defend this responsibility and ensure the efficient use of the hard-earned tax dollars you entrust in Idaho.
READ MORE GOVERNMENT OVERREACH The best decisions in government are made at the local level.
Local governments know their community needs and the best way to achieve their goals.
Emphasis on local governance is the easiest way citizens can voice their opinions and actively participate in their government.
Therefore, I strongly feel that federal and state governments should be limited to the provisions in their constitutions and that government should be executed locally.
