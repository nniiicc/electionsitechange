Oct 9 Written By Stacy Molitor Come canvass with Connie in the 87th District!
Where: Maryland Heights Community Center, 2300 McKelvey Road, Maryland Heights, MO 63043 When: Wednesday and Sunday, 3:30p.m. to 6:30 p.m.
Bring your walking shoes, a charged cell phone, sun protection, a water bottle, and a can-do attitude!
Snacks, training, and canvassing materials are provided!
To sign up, please fill out our Supporter/Volunteer Form —this ensures we have enough materials and snacks for our canvassers!
