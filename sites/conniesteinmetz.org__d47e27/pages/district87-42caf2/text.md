It now includes all of Maryland Heights, northeastern portions of Chesterfield, and a small section of unincorporated St.
Louis County located between Maryland Heights, Chesterfield, and Creve Coeur.
District 87 includes portions of the Pattonville and Parkway School Districts.
It also includes Creve Coeur Park as well as Westport Plaza and much of the surrounding business district.
