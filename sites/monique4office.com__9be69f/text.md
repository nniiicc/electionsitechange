Featured Priority: Modernizing Utah’s Unemployment System Work has changed; remote, hybrid, contract, and technology-driven careers are now mainstream.
Our unemployment policies should reflect today’s workforce so qualified Utahns aren’t disadvantaged by outdated assumptions.
We should also address ageism in hiring and support experienced workers re-entering the workforce through retraining and upskilling, without expanding bureaucracy.
As a Republican, I believe in personal responsibility and limited government.
I also believe government should function efficiently and fairly.
If our systems are outdated, it’s our responsibility to fix them while protecting taxpayers and treating Utahns with dignity.
Cost of Living & Housing Study the real drivers of rising costs and focus on solutions that increase supply and fairness.
Encourage affordable housing through smart incentives and responsible development.
Keep the tax burden in check by improving government efficiency and reducing waste.
Jobs, Economy & Small Business Promote a business-friendly environment that supports innovation and entrepreneurship.
Streamline regulations that create unnecessary barriers while maintaining safety and fair competition.
Align education and job training with market needs through partnerships with local employers.
Public Safety & Drug Crisis Support law enforcement and improve coordination to reduce the supply of fentanyl and illegal drugs.
Reduce demand with effective prevention, treatment access, and community awareness.
Address root causes of crime through community engagement and practical support systems.
Education & Parental Rights Prioritize strong fundamentals, especially early literacy, and measure what’s working.
Empower educators with practical, educator-led solutions (not one-size-fits-all mandates).
Support neurodivergent students with more individualized approaches.
Strengthen training and accountability for school administration and budget management.
Accountable & Limited Government Limited government that respects individual freedom and focuses on core responsibilities.
Transparency through accessible data, targeted reporting, and real community feedback.
Oversight that prevents fraud, without costing more than it saves.
