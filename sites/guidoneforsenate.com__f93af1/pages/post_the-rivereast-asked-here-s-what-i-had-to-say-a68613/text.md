The Rivereast Asked.
Here's What I Had to Say.
I'm grateful to the Rivereast for taking the time to sit down with me and discuss my campaign for Connecticut's 19th State Senate District (Columbia, Franklin, Hebron, Lebanon, Ledyard, Lisbon, Marlborough, Montville, Norwich, and Sprague).
This race is about listening to the people of our communities, addressing affordability, supporting public safety, protecting local decision-making, and ensuring that the voices of residents are heard in Hartford.
I appreciate the opportunity to share more about my background, why I'm running, and my vision for the future of our district and everyone in Connecticut.
I invite you to read the article and learn more about my commitment to serving every resident of our District—regardless of political affiliation.
— Jason Guidone
Republican Candidate for State Senate, District 19
