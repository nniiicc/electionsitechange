About John Meet John Isaac John Isaac is a local businessman, financial advisor, legal immigrant, and proud resident of Yonkers.
He is running for New York State Assembly in the 90th District — the district that represents the City of Yonkers.
John believes public office is a public trust — not a political inheritance.
For too long, Albany insiders have treated leadership like it belongs to them.
John is running to return that power to the people of Yonkers.
John immigrated from India at 19 and built a successful career in finance, working at Merrill Lynch and MetLife.
He is now a vice president at Forest Hills Financial Group.
A Yonkers resident for more than 16 years, he lives with his wife, Shelby, and is the proud father of Alisha & Josh, and grateful "son-in-love" Krishna Patel.
End the Status Quo in Albany New York families are facing: • Higher taxes • Higher crime • Higher cost of living • Families leaving the state in record numbers One-party rule has manufactured crisis after crisis.
It's time for change.
John's campaign is built on accountability, common sense, and restoring balance in Albany.
Platform Make Yonkers and New York Affordable New York doesn't have a revenue problem — it has a spending problem.
As a financial advisor, John has spent his career helping families and businesses balance budgets and plan for the future.
Albany must do the same. • Lower taxes statewide • Eliminate the Yonkers personal income tax • Enforce real budget discipline • Stop policies that drive working families out Restore Public Safety Public safety must be the top priority.
Failed bail reform policies have allowed repeat violent offenders back onto our streets.
Our communities are paying the price. • Fix broken bail laws • Support law enforcement • Put victims before criminals • Restore accountability Fix Our Schools Our children deserve better.
The current school funding formula is broken, and Yonkers schools are not receiving their fair share.
Students are falling behind, and families are frustrated. • Fight for fair school funding • Ensure accountability in education spending • Expand real opportunity for students Grow the Economy & Create Jobs Yonkers is home to one of the most important economic opportunities in the region — full casino gaming.
John supports securing full casino status to: • Create jobs • Increase local revenue • Deliver tax relief to residents Albany has failed to act.
John will fight to bring those benefits home.
