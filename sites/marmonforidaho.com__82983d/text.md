Meet the Candidate
About Kent Marmon
Kent Marmon is a conservative Idaho legislator and proud Canyon County community member who has dedicated his service to returning government to its proper role — protecting individual freedom, defending the family, and safeguarding our constitutional rights.
Based in Caldwell, Idaho, Kent has built a reputation as a legislator who shows up for his constituents.
He hosted town halls during both legislative sessions, met with community leaders to address local challenges, and worked one-on-one with residents to help resolve real problems.
Kent Marmon believes in limited government, lower taxes, parental choice in education, protecting life, and defending the 2nd Amendment — and his voting record proves it.
I'm endorsing Kent Marmon for the Idaho House again because he has kept his word to the people of his district, stood up for Idaho's families and has continued to defend conservative values.
Kent has helped lead the fight for limited government, parental choice in education, and continues to protect the unborn and our 2nd Amendment rights.
— Raúl Labrador, Idaho Attorney General
