My Legislative Goals
- to represent all voters in my district, not just the ones with which I agree
- to ensure that the will of petitioners is followed in the initiative process
- to establish reliable voter identification procedures
- to return Oregon to its roots in timber, hunting, and fishing
- to hold schools accountable for properly educating our children and to return the rights of parents to make decisions about their children’s education
- to protect the Constitutional rights of Oregonians, including those of the 2nd Amendment
- to focus public funding on programs all Oregonians can support
- to ensure that federal laws are followed in Oregon
- to look out for the needs of farmers who are often neglected in favor of the wants of city dwellers
- to protect and preserve women’s sports for biological women
- to defend religious liberties
