About Joseph
Joseph McLaughlin
Joseph McLaughlin is a lifelong Mainer who understands the values of hard work, self-reliance, and community that define our state.
Raised in a working-class family, Joe learned early the importance of fiscal responsibility and looking out for your neighbors.
His career in small business has given him firsthand experience with the challenges Maine families and entrepreneurs face every day.
Joe is running for State Representative because he believes Augusta needs leaders who will fight to lower costs, protect individual freedoms, and ensure Maine remains a place where families can thrive.
He is committed to being an accessible representative who listens to constituents and puts District 40's interests first.
As a husband, father, and active community member, Joe knows what's at stake for the next generation.
He's ready to bring practical, common-sense solutions to the State House and work across the aisle to deliver results for Maine.
