As the son of Cuban immigrants, Mike grew up with a deep appreciation for the American dream.
From an early age, he learned the value of hard work, personal responsibility, and the belief that the United States is the greatest country in the world.
His mother spent over 40 years as a public school teacher, instilling in Mike the importance of education.
His father, a structural engineer, led by example — teaching Mike that success comes from hard work, strong values, and always putting family first.
Today, Mike lives by those same principles.
As an attorney, he fights tirelessly for his clients.
And in Tallahassee, he brings those conservative values to the Capitol, standing as a strong, unwavering voice for the people of District 118.
