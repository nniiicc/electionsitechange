Support the Campaign: $10 $25 $50 $100 $250 Other Amount CONCORD SALEM About Joe A Proven Leader for Salem Joe Sweeney is proud to call Salem, New Hampshire home.
A lifelong Granite Stater, Joe grew up in Salem, attended the University of New Hampshire for both his undergraduate and master’s degrees, and has never stopped working to make New Hampshire the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
Joe currently serves on the Salem Town Council and as a State Representative in Concord.
In both roles, he has built a reputation as a tireless advocate for taxpayers, a champion for economic growth, and a leader who gets results for his community.
From cutting taxes and holding the line on wasteful spending, to fighting for safer streets and a stronger town, Joe has been on the front lines of keeping New Hampshire affordable and free.
He believes deeply in preserving our state’s advantage of no sales and no income tax, while working to attract new businesses, support local job creators, and expand opportunities for the next generation.
Joe has also taken on tough fights in Concord, leading the effort to stop sanctuary cities in New Hampshire, advancing real reforms to our retirement system, and pushing for policies that lower energy costs for families and small businesses.
At home in Salem, Joe is committed to strengthening the community.
He has worked to secure major infrastructure improvements, expand access to clean drinking water, and drive smart development that builds on Salem’s role as one of New Hampshire’s most dynamic and fastest-growing towns.
Outside of public service, Joe and his wife Melissa welcomed their first daughter, Maeve, in the summer of 2025.
That growing family perspective fuels Joe’s determination to make sure New Hampshire remains a place where families can thrive for generations to come.
Joe Sweeney’s leadership is rooted in Salem, focused on results, and dedicated to keeping New Hampshire the best state in the nation.
The Platform Fighting for Our Values Joe's record is clear.
He votes for the taxpayer, for the Constitution, and for the future of New Hampshire.
Fiscal Responsibility Keeping the 'New Hampshire Advantage' alive by opposing broad-based taxes and advocating for the complete phase-out of the Interest & Dividends tax.
Election Integrity Championing common-sense reforms to ensure our elections are secure, transparent, and trusted by every voter in the Granite State.
Local Infrastructure Working on the Salem Town Council to modernize our roads, improve water systems, and ensure public safety without wasteful spending.
Limited Government Believing that government should do a few things well, rather than many things poorly.
Less regulation means more opportunity for small businesses.
Individual Liberty Defending the constitutional rights of all New Hampshire residents against government overreach and mandates.
