Burning of Trash: Bad for Our Health When you burn trash, the smoke carries harmful chemicals that affects your health – and that of your family and neighbors – when: • you breathe it • you eat food contaminated with harmful chemicals (e.g., your garden vegetables) • children play in areas contaminated with the chemicals CONSIDER INSTEAD: • disposal: use your residential trash hauler, or your complex’s trash (make sure your trash doesn’t fall from your car!).
If you’re experiencing problems with the hauler or your landlord re: trash, please see my contact below • recycle: residents (not in complexes) may obtain a 65-gallon recycling bin from your hauler.
