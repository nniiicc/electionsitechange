He believes that secure, fair elections are key to a free country and is running for Congress to make sure every legal vote in Rhode Island is counted and that fraud is minimized.
Securing the Vote with Voter ID In Rhode Island, lax voting rules create a risk to the integrity of the election process.
Vic advocates for common-sense security measures to restore voter trust.
Mandatory Voter ID : Vic supports requiring a photo ID to verify the identity of every voter.
This crucial step is the best way to prevent fraud, eliminate duplicate or non-citizen votes, and protect the legitimacy of the ballot without denying access to any eligible citizen.
Preventing Fraud : Secure voting rules are essential to safeguarding the outcome of elections, ensuring that the results accurately reflect the will of the people.
National Standards : In Congress, Vic will push for federal standards on Voter ID, while ensuring those standards respect the rights of individual states to run their elections efficiently.
Join The Campaign Donate Builder & Fighter A Commitment to Unfiltered Truth and Grassroots Action In Rhode Island Vic’s commitment to election integrity is rooted in his experiences challenging establishment narratives and encouraging political engagement.
Challenging Irregularities : As a peaceful participant in the January 6th events, Vic exercised his right to protest irregularities and is now channeling that energy into policy that ensures transparency and security.
Promoting Debate : Vic has founded media platforms dedicated to providing unfiltered truth, countering the biased narratives that he believes undermine public trust in the election process.
Flynn’s Influence on Integrity : Through his community center, The Hollow, Vic hosted events featuring figures like General Michael Flynn, where discussions were fostered on the need for local action to build national integrity.
These gatherings, including screenings of films on irregularities, were designed to educate communities on the critical importance of secure voting.
Rhode Island’s elections must be secure and transparent.
Vic Mellor is partnering with other Rhode Island leaders to charge for election integrity.
