Read the Times of Israel Article Times of Israel Feature Vic Mellor, General Michael Flynn, and The Hollow The Times of Israel featured Vic Mellor in its October 18, 2022 article, “Former White House adviser goes grassroots to spread Christian nationalism.” The piece highlights Vic’s ownership of The Hollow in Florida.
It also focuses on his collaboration with General Michael Flynn at local events centered on protecting children, supporting families, and restoring constitutional values.
The article presents these efforts as part of a larger “Christian nationalist” movement.
In addition, it references Vic’s presence at the January 6, 2021 Capitol events.
The story also notes photos of Flynn at The Hollow alongside individuals wearing Proud Boys attire.
Furthermore, the article points to activities held at the venue.
These include vaccine-waiver distributions, “Biblical citizenship” classes, and community gatherings.
According to the article’s framing, these events reflect broader political concerns.
As a result, the piece attempts to portray Vic Mellor and Michael Flynn as dangerous and extreme.
In reality, such attacks on grassroots organizing only reinforce the importance of what Vic Mellor is doing.
When national media outlets make attacks against a Marine veteran, entrepreneur, and father for opposing overly woke school policies , medical mandates , and government overreach, it simply confirms that the message has real impact.
These issues remain central to many Rhode Island families.
Rising costs, struggling schools, and concerns about personal freedoms continue to shape public debate.
Because of this, Vic Mellor refuses to be silenced.
Rhode Islanders deserve a Congressman who puts families and freedom first.
Instead of yielding to media pressure or political insiders, leadership in Rhode Island needs to challenge the status quo.
Vic Mellor is that fighter, and such attacks from the media only strengthen his resolve.
