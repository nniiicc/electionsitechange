He is running for Congress to represent every family, focusing on the vibrant communities and local institutions that are the true heartbeat of Rhode Island.
A Proven Commitment to Local Families Vic Mellor’s approach to leadership isn’t about grandstanding from a podium, it’s about sitting at the table with local leaders, pastors, and families.
He believes that true representation requires showing up in person to listen, learn, and offer real support where it’s needed most.
Investing in Our Youth : Vic understands that the future of Rhode Island depends on keeping our kids safe and engaged.
He has taken personal action by donating to local Little Leagues, providing uniforms and equipment for junior baseball players to ensure every child has the chance to play and thrive.
Empowering the Community : Vic has been warmly received in neighborhoods across Rhode Island, where he has built genuine friendships based on mutual respect and shared values.
By sponsoring community lunches at churches and donating to their outreach programs, Vic is supporting the spiritual and social pillars that keep neighborhoods strong.
Supporting Local Solutions : Recognizing that local churches are often the first line of defense in keeping kids off the streets, Vic directly supports mentorship programs.
His goal is to empower the organizations already doing the hard work of building safe, healthy communities.
Direct Action Over Politics : While others focus on partisan debates, Vic is on the ground, often the first candidate in years to visit these neighborhoods, ensuring that the needs of working families and small business owners are never ignored.
Builder & Fighter One State, One Future In Rhode Island Vic’s outreach is rooted in the belief that when our neighborhoods are strong, Rhode Island is strong.
He refuses to participate in a socialist style of governance that creates dependency, instead, he invests in local pride and self-sufficiency.
Through his personal generosity and his tireless presence in Rhode Island’s most diverse communities, Vic Mellor is proving that he is a leader who values every voice.
He has the courage, clarity, and heart to fight for the future of every Rhode Islander, with zero apologies.
