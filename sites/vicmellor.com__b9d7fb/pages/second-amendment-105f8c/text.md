The Second Amendment is the cornerstone of American liberty, securing your fundamental right to self-defence and serving as the ultimate check against tyranny.
Vic Mellor is running for Congress as an unapologetic and outspoken Second Amendment champion who views the right to keep and bear arms as vital for freedom.
He is committed to fighting back against unconstitutional gun control that puts guns into the hands of criminals only and makes law-abiding citizens and their families victims.
Vic Mellor is a staunch defender of the Second Amendment, asserting that guns are essential tools for self-reliance and “to repeal a tyrannical government.” His platform is built on action and zero compromise.
- Opposing Unconstitutional Laws: Vic will stand firm against restrictive laws that disarm citizens and infringe on constitutional rights.
He opposes broad bans, including those targeting so-called “assault weapons.”
- Protecting Due Process: He will actively work in Congress to block “Red Flag” laws and other measures that violate the fundamental principle of due process by allowing the government to seize property before a person is convicted of a crime.
- Promoting Citizen Readiness: As per both the Constitution of the United States and the Constitution of Rhode Island, Vic has consistently promoted gun rights at community gatherings, advocating for the importance of an armed citizenry as a defense against potential government tyranny.
Rhode Island’s current strict laws place undue burdens on responsible gun owners.
Vic’s plan aims to restore balance, increase public safety, and empower law-abiding citizens.
Overwhelming statistics point to gun violence being carried out by those who are mentally ill.
Tragically, most of these incidents are red-flagged.
Vic will work with the appropriate agencies to fund a coordinated and effective approach to prevent these tragedies.
- Concealed Carry Reforms: Vic supports easing the permit process for responsible owners, making it easier for citizens to exercise their right to self-defence both inside and outside the home.
- Honoring Out-of-State Permits: He will advocate for laws that recognize and honor concealed carry permits issued by other states, ensuring that Rhode Islanders can travel freely without compromising their right to self-defence.
- Funding Safety Education: Vic supports funding for safety education and training programs for youth, promoting responsible gun ownership from an early age.
- Challenging Federal Overreach: Vic will fight federal encroachments on state gun laws, protecting the rights of Rhode Island citizens and supporting local small businesses like gun shops.
