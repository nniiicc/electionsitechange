A May 29, 2026 report from Agence France-Presse, carried by Barron’s, detailed his meetings in Cuba.
Among those meetings was a conversation with Raúl Guillermo Rodríguez Castro.
He is the grandson of former Cuban President Raúl Castro.
During the visit, Mellor discussed business opportunities and the potential for Cuba to open its economy.
“I got to meet with Raul, and his vision for opening up Cuba with business, I align with it,” Vic said.
“I think Cuba needs it, I think the world needs it.
I think that it is time to move forward, it’s time for change.” Mellor also emphasized the potential for greater economic cooperation with the United States.
He believes expanded trade could play an important role in Cuba’s future prosperity.
According to Mellor, many American businesses could engage with Cuba if conditions allow.
Such opportunities could create benefits for both countries.
Mellor described how expanded trade and investment could create new opportunities for American businesses and workers, particularly in Rhode Island.
As a result, Mellor sees international business relationships as one potential way to create jobs and economic growth at home.
His conversations in Cuba reflect his mission to bring growth, opportunity, and jobs back to Rhode Island.
As a businessman who has built companies from the ground up, Vic Mellor continues to explore avenues that can strengthen American economic interests.
