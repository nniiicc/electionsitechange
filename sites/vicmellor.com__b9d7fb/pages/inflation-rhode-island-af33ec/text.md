Due to Biden’s socialist policies and reckless spending in state Rhode Island, the cost of housing, food, and energy has soared on the federal and state level.
Vic Mellor, a builder and entrepreneur, understands how these rising costs hurt the working class.
He has navigated tough economic challenges by founding businesses that create jobs and stability.
He is running for Congress to bring common-sense fixes to Washington and put Rhode Island families first.
Supporting Working-class Families Vic’s Action Plan: Energy, Spending, and Jobs Vic views out-of-control inflation as a “money grab” and a “power grab” by elites and the government, similar to his critiques of other overreaching policies.
His platform focuses on direct, tangible actions to lower costs for every Rhode Islander.
Rhode Island needs Vic's Experience To Fight Inflation 1.
Achieve Energy Independence to Lower Prices The volatility of global energy prices is a major driver of inflation, directly raising the cost of energy in Rhode Island.
Drill Domestically : Vic will aggressively push for domestic energy production to reduce reliance on foreign sources.
Lowering fuel costs immediately reduces prices for everything, from the pump to the grocery store shelf.
Increase Capacity – Increase Rhode Island’s capacity to bring in and use clean energy from liquid natural gas and nuclear.
2.
Restore Fiscal Responsibility in Washington Vic believes the government must stop fueling inflation through irresponsible spending.
Bring Investment Back To Rhode Island: $20 trillion dollars are being invested throughout America, and none of it is landing here.
Our current federal delegation has chosen political grievance over economic growth.
Vic has 30 years of experience and knows how to negotiate, how to fight, and how to bring investment home and will work the administration being a friendly face for Rhode Island to create jobs, and opportunities.
Cut Wasteful Spending : He will oppose pork-barrel spending and bloated federal programs to ensure taxpayer dollars fight inflation, not fuel it.
Balanced Budgets : Vic advocates for sound fiscal policies and balanced budgets that prevent the creation of excessive money, which devalues the dollar.
3.
Empower Small Businesses and Local Economies Inflation disproportionately harms the small businesses that are the backbone of Rhode Island’s economy.
Buy Local, Hire Local – Incentivize businesses to source from Rhode Island suppliers and employ our residents.
Let’s prioritize Rhode Island’s economy to create jobs and keep dollars in-state.
Small Business Tax Relief : Vic will fight for tax cuts and regulatory relief to help small business owners weather inflation, encouraging them to hire and grow.
Imports Cost Money, Exports Bring In Money : Vic will work to facilitate exports out of Rhode Island, bring in money for jobs, infrastructure, and opportunities.
