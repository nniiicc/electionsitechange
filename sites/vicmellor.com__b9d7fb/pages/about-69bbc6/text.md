That is the mission of Victor “Vic” G.
Mellor.
Drawing on his service as a U.S.
Marine Corps veteran, Vic became an entrepreneur who built a successful business supporting working families for three decades.
He has always been a fierce defender of individual rights, dedicated to ensuring every American has the space to think independently and prosper.
The Builder Creating Opportunity and Community Unity Vics career is defined by action.
For thirty years, he successfully ran a business that supported the working class with real jobs and steady paychecks.
This dedication to local results led Vic to found the Rhode Island First movement, a mission dedicated to putting the needs of our citizens before party bosses or global interests.
His leadership extends far beyond the construction site: Capital and Growth : Recognizing the economic potential of our state, Vic has worked tirelessly to bring major investment dollars back into Rhode Island, creating the foundation for a sustainable jobs revolution.
Global Allied Health Association (GAHA) : As a founding member of GAHA, Vic has been at the forefront of the fight for medical freedom and genuine care, ensuring that healthcare serves the people rather than the bureaucracy.
Diverse Community Outreach : Vic is a bridge builder who has invested his own resources into Hispanic neighborhoods and local churches, bringing diverse communities together to support our youth and keep kids off the streets.
A Resonating Voice : When Vic speaks, people listen.
His message of common sense and freedom has reached over six million people online, proving that his vision for a stronger Rhode Island is exactly what the silent majority is looking for.
The Fighter Never Backing Down for Rhode Island Vic Mellor is home to fight for the state that raised him because he knows people are fed up with the status quo.
He sees the costs soaring, the schools struggling, and the next generation leaving the state to find a future elsewhere.
It is not sustainable, and nobody in Washington is fighting for the working people.
As an outspoken, freedom first leader, Vic refuses to back down against government overreach or radical ideologies that harm our families.
He is ready to serve because he believes in: Restoring Economic Sovereignty : Ending the socialist style of governance that is crushing the working class and ensuring Rhode Island is a leader in trade and manufacturing.
Pioneering Health Reform : As a founding member of a health think tank with strategies now echoed by the Oval Office, Vic is leading the charge to replace a broken sick care system with genuine healthcare.
By championing the four pillars of GAHA (transparency, accountability, patient choice, and medical freedom) he is ensuring medicine serves people, not bureaucracy.
Defending Constitutional Rights : Standing strong for free speech, medical freedom, parental rights, and the Second Amendment, no matter the pressure from the establishment.
Restoring Trust : Advancing election integrity and ensuring we rebuild a system where work pays and personal responsibility is valued over government dependency.

This campaign is about protecting what matters.
Vic Mellor has invested his life in feeding the hunger, healing the people, and building the future.
Vic Mellor is ready to serve, ready to fight, and ready to rebuild Rhode Island with courage, clarity, and zero apologies.
