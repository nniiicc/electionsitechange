Vic Mellor is a builder and entrepreneur who understands that small businesses are the backbone of Rhode Island.
He is running for Congress to slash the red tape that stifles growth and to deliver the resources local owners need to thrive.
Vic Mellor doesn’t just study the economy, he has built businesses from the ground up.
He knows that the socialist style of governance in Washington and Providence has created a climate of overregulation that punishes the very people who provide jobs for our neighbors.
Vic is committed to a Jobs Revolution that puts the power back in the hands of the owners and workers.
- Cutting the Red Tape: Vic will use his congressional influence to incentivize business friendly climate to confront the burdensome regulations and permitting costs that act as a hidden tax on growth.
- Incentivizing Local Manufacturing: Vic understands the importance of economic sovereignty.
He will champion policies that bring supply chains back to Rhode Island, supporting local manufacturers and creating entry level, mid level and high paying jobs in our communities.
- Economic Empowerment Through Grants: Securing larger federal grants that go directly to local Rhode Island businesses is a top priority.
Vic will fight for capital to be reinvested into our community to help small businesses expand, modernize, and outcompete the globalists rather than being swallowed by bureaucracy.
Small businesses in Rhode Island shouldn’t have to navigate a maze of bureaucracy to succeed.
Vic Mellor is dedicated to ensuring that federal resources are accessible and that the political insiders stop picking winners and losers in the marketplace.
- Support for Skilled Trades: Drawing on his experience in construction, Vic will prioritize funding for vocational training and apprenticeships, ensuring our small businesses have a pipeline of skilled, local talent.
- A Voice for the Ignored: Vic is taking his message directly to the shops, restaurants, and startups that both parties have overlooked.
He is the warrior who will go head-to-head with the bureaucracy to ensure our local economy isn’t just surviving, but leading.
