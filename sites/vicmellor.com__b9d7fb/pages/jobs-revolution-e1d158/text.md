Rhode Island was once the manufacturing powerhouse of America.
We built ships, machines, and industry that fueled the nation.
We still have the deep-water ports, the skilled trades, and the strategic coastline needed to lead the jobs revolution and the next economic boom.
The answer is simple.
While thousands of new factories and high-paying jobs are being created across America, none of it is landing here.
Our current federal delegation has chosen political grievance over economic growth.
They’d rather whine about who’s in the White House than put Rhode Island families first.
That’s not just politics, that’s a leadership failure.
Vic is a successful entrepreneur who has spent 30 years employing and supporting families.
He knows how to negotiate, how to fight, and how to bring investment home because he is a businessman with a proven track record.
Vic is running for Congress to end the political paralysis and start the economic revitalization Rhode Island desperately needs.
Vic’s Jobs Revolution is a commitment to economic sovereignty.
- Bring the Money Home: While other politicians are paralyzed by political grudges, Vic will work directly with the administration to ensure federal investment dollars are secured for our ports, manufacturers, and workers.
- Revive the American System: We will stop relying on foreign countries and federal handouts.
Vic will champion policies that bring back local production and rebuild the supply chains we control, making Rhode Island the heartbeat of American industry again.
- Skilled Trades Empowerment: We will support the trades that built this state.
The focus will be on bringing back high-paying manufacturing jobs that allow the working-class family to thrive and stop our kids from leaving the state.
