Michael Levesque In the Cranston Herald Read the full Cranston Herald article Conversation with Vic Mellor on The Cranston Herald Vic Mellor recently spoke with J.
Michael Levesque for the Cranston Herald ’s “Meeting with Mike” column.
The conversation appeared on July 22, 2026.
Levesque highlighted Vic’s background as a Woonsocket native, Marine Corps veteran, and successful businessman.
Vic returned to Rhode Island determined to make a difference.
He also discussed his years building companies in Florida and his work alongside Gen.
Michael Flynn.
Those experiences helped shape his decision to run for Congress in the 2nd District.
The conversation covered Vic’s strong support for President Trump, his commitment to gun rights , parental rights , voter ID, and election integrity , and his decision to self-fund much of the campaign with approximately $3 million of his own money.
Vic also discussed why he chose not to seek the state Republican Party’s endorsement, explaining that he is focused on reaching working-class Rhode Islanders who feel left behind.
Economic Opportunities for Rhode Island Vic also spoke about his recent trip to Cuba.
During the trip, he met with officials to explore potential economic opportunities that could benefit Rhode Island businesses as the island opens up.
He also addressed the federal decision on blizzard-relief funding.
Vic emphasized the need for effective representation in Congress.
In his view, Rhode Island needs a Congressman who can work constructively with the administration rather than simply criticize it.
We’re grateful to J.
Michael Levesque and the Cranston Herald for the thoughtful conversation.
Most importantly, the interview gives Rhode Islanders another opportunity to hear directly from Vic.
It also offers insight into who he is and why he is running for Congress.
