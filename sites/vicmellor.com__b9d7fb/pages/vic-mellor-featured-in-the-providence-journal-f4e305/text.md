The Providence Journal Read The Providence Journal Article Providence Journal Covers Vic Mellor’s Decision to Run for Rhode Island Congress The Providence Journal published an article on October 27, 2025, highlighting Vic Mellor as he considered a run for Congress in Rhode Island’s 2nd District.
The piece notes his background as a former Marine, businessman, and advocate for constitutional values.
In addition, it mentions his friendship with General Michael Flynn and references his presence at the January 6, 2021, Capitol events .
Vic has consistently said he attended peacefully to support election integrity and protest unfair election practices.
Local coverage like this shows Vic’s message is resonating right here in Rhode Island.
The article’s emphasis on January 6 reflects a familiar establishment framing.
However, Vic has remained open about his reasons for traveling to Washington.
He went to support fair elections and peaceful protest.
Furthermore, he remains proud of standing up for what he believes.
Efforts to portray peaceful political participation as dangerous reveal a disconnect between institutions and everyday Americans.
Meanwhile, Rhode Island families in the 2nd District face rising costs, struggling schools, and concerns about personal freedom.
These local challenges deserve greater attention from elected leaders.
Vic Mellor points to his grassroots work as evidence of his commitment to these issues.
He argues that parents should make decisions for their children, the Constitution still matters, and government should remain limited in personal health choices .
Ultimately, Rhode Islanders deserve a Congressman who puts families and freedom first.
Rather than following political pressure or national narratives, Mellor presents himself as an independent fighter for Rhode Island.
