Important Dates & Deadlines for 2026 Mark your calendars.
In Rhode Island, staying ahead of deadlines is the only way to ensure your vote is counted.
Rhode Island Primary Election Voter Registration Deadline: August 9, 2026 Mail-In Ballot Application Deadline: August 18, 2026 Primary Election Day: September 9, 2026 General Election Voter Registration Deadline: October 4, 2026 Mail-In Ballot Application Deadline: October 13, 2026 Early Voting Period: Begins October 14, 2026 Election Day: November 3, 2026 Rhode Island Voter Requirements What to Bring to the Polls To ensure a smooth experience at your Rhode Island polling place, please bring a valid photo ID.
Accepted forms include: RI Driver’s License or State ID U.S.
Passport Military or Student ID Tribal ID Eligibility To vote in Rhode Island, you must be a citizen of the United States, a resident of Rhode Island, and at least 18 years of age by Election Day.
Vic’s Commitment to Election Integrity A System That Is Easy to Vote In, but Hard to Cheat.
Vic Mellor believes that the foundation of our Republic is the trust we place in our elections.
His advocacy for the silent majority is rooted in two core principles.
Transparency: Every Rhode Islander deserves to know exactly how their vote is handled.
Vic supports clear, open processes that eliminate bureaucratic confusion.
Accountability: We must ensure our voter rolls are accurate and that our system remains secure against overreach.
Vic is fighting to return the focus of our elections to the local citizens, ensuring that your vote remains your power.
