To ensure a smooth experience at your Rhode Island polling place, please bring a valid photo ID.
Accepted forms include:
- RI Driver’s License or State ID
- U.S.
Passport
- Military or Student ID
- Tribal ID
To vote in Rhode Island, you must be a citizen of the United States, a resident of Rhode Island, and at least 18 years of age by Election Day.
