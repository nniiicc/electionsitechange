Washington Post Labels Vic Mellor’s Family Venue a “Far Right Playground”
The Washington Post Takes Aim at The Hollow
The Washington Post published a December 17, 2022 article titled “Waterslides and rifles: Inside Florida’s playground for the far right.” The story places a spotlight on Vic Mellor’s community venue, The Hollow, in Venice, Florida.
Written by Tim Craig, the article describes the 27-acre property as a blend of family recreation and patriotic symbolism.
It highlights waterslides, playgrounds, and a man-made lake.
In addition, it points to Founding Fathers quotes, a large Bill of Rights display, and reminders of Second Amendment rights.
The piece notes Vic Mellor’s reported $3 million investment in the venue.
According to the article, The Hollow serves as a gathering place for Republicans, homeschool families, and conservatives who support President Trump, gun rights, and opposition to mandates.
Events and Activities at The Hollow
The Washington Post also highlights several events hosted at the property.
These include “Biblical Citizenship” classes, youth firearm safety training, Patriot Appreciation Day cookouts, and appearances by General Michael Flynn.
The article references photos showing Proud Boys volunteers at a toy drive.
It also mentions Vic Mellor’s presence at the January 6 Capitol events.
Mellor has consistently said he attended peacefully to support election integrity.
As a result, the story portrays The Hollow as more than a family venue.
Instead, it frames the property as a “far right” gathering place tied to dangerous political ideology.
A Different View of The Hollow
Critics quoted in the article describe The Hollow as “super dangerous.” However, supporters see something very different.
To many families, The Hollow was never primarily about partisan politics.
Rather, it was designed as a safe and welcoming place where families could gather, build friendships, and spend time together.
The venue also promotes values that matter deeply to American life.
These include respect for the Constitution, self-reliance, and personal responsibility.
Vic Mellor has described the mission plainly:
“This is a place for families.
They build friendships, and the kids get to run around play, and just be kids.”
Why This Story Matters to Rhode Island
Rhode Island families face many of the same concerns discussed throughout this debate.
Rising costs continue to strain household budgets.
Meanwhile, many parents remain frustrated with failed education systems and politician overreach into what your kids learn and what medical decisions you can make.
Vic Mellor refuses to apologize for defending families and constitutional freedoms.
Attacks like these do not discourage him.
Instead, they strengthen his resolve.
For Mellor, the issue is larger than media criticism.
Rhode Islanders deserve a Congressman who puts freedom and families first.
Likewise, voters deserve leadership that challenges political and media establishments rather than yielding to them.
