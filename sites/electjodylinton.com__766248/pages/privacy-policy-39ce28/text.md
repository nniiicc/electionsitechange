PRIVACY POLICY - THE BASICS
Last Updated: May 5, 2026
Elect Jody Linton | Alabama State House District 91
This Privacy Policy describes how Elect Jody Linton ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through this website.
Information We Collect We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any other information submitted through our contact or volunteer forms.
How We Use Your Information We use the information you provide to contact you about the campaign, send updates, coordinate volunteer activities, and communicate information about Jody Linton's candidacy.
We may contact you by email, phone, or text message.
Text Messaging By providing your phone number and opting in, you consent to receive text messages from Elect Jody Linton.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt out at any time.
Reply HELP for assistance.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
Sharing of Information We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
We may share information with campaign vendors or volunteers who assist in campaign operations, solely for campaign purposes.
Data Security We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
Your Choices You may opt out of communications at any time by replying STOP to any text message, clicking unsubscribe in any email, or contacting us directly.
Contact Us For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: electjodylinton.com
