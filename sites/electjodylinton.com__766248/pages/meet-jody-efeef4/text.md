MEET JODY
LINTON
Devoted Husband
Father and Man of Faith
A conservative entrepreneur with a track record of building successful businesses from the ground up, Jody Linton is committed to doing things the right way—not the easy way.
Jody has worked with one of Alabama’s top accounting firms, Carr, Riggs & Ingram, for more than 13 years.
In this role, he helps businesses strengthen their financial outlook, improve efficiency, and plan for long-term success.
This hands-on experience gives him a daily look at what it takes to grow and sustain opportunity, which is exactly what Montgomery needs more of.
A devoted husband, father, and man of faith, Jody is an active member of First Baptist Church Enterprise, where he serves on the Building and Grounds Committee, the Board of Adjustments. and the Transportation Committee.
He also previously served his community on the Board of Directors for Alzheimer’s of Central Alabama and the local Board of Adjustments, helping guide responsible growth and community development.
Jody believes in limited government, personal responsibility, and fiscal discipline.
In the State House, he’ll put Alabama First.
