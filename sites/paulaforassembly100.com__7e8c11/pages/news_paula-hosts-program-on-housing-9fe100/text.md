Paula hosts Roundtable on Workforce housing
MONTICELLO – Assemblywoman Paula Kay recently hosted a roundtable discussion for Sullivan County’s largest employers, developers, construction unions and officials to meet and share ideas about what they say is a critical lack of housing for residents and employees.
Kay says affordable housing has been a priority of hers since she was elected.
“We need workforce housing—especially in Sullivan County, but also in the Orange County portions of my district as well.”
