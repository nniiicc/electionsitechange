Delay All-electric Mandate
Several Assembly Democrats plan to petition Gov.
Kathy Hochul to roll back the statewide mandate to build all-electric buildings starting next year.
The All-Electric Buildings Act was tucked in the state budget in 2023, and bans new gas hookups in new buildings under seven stories starting Jan. 1.
The mandate would apply to all other buildings constructed after Jan. 1, 2029.
