DISTRICT MAP ASSEMBLY 100
New York State’s 100th Assembly District has a population of almost 131,000 residents.
Culturally, ethnically, and socio-economically diverse, it includes the city of Middletown, the suburban neighborhoods of the Town of Wallkill, and most of the rural towns and villages of Sullivan County.
