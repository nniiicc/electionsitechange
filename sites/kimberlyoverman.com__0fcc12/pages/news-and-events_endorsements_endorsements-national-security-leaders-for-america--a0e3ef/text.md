House of Representatives.
The organization is made up of retired senior military leaders, diplomats, and national security professionals, and it endorses candidates on the basis of judgment, respect for the Constitution and the rule of law, and an understanding that public office carries obligations beyond personal or partisan interest.
In its endorsement, NSL4A argued that America’s security depends on strength at home as well as abroad — a resilient economy, safe communities, a capable military, strong alliances, and support for those who serve and their families.
The organization said it is confident Overman will bring those principles to Congress, and urged voters in Florida’s 12th Congressional District to support her on November 3.
The endorsement carries particular weight in a district home to more than 68,000 veterans, where questions of military readiness, VA access, and support for military families are not abstract.
