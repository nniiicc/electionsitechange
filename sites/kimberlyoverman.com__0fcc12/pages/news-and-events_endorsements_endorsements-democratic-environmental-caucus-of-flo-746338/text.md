Democratic Environmental Caucus of Florida (DECF) Proudly Endorses Kimberly Overman
Kimberly Overman has earned the endorsement of the Democratic Environmental Caucus of Florida, given to candidates who commit to meeting the environmental and climate challenges ahead.
For families in Florida’s 12th District, that crisis isn’t abstract — it shows up in insurance premiums that climb after every storm season, in water quality, and in a coastline that shapes the region’s economy.
Kimberly’s record on environmental stewardship goes back to her service as a Hillsborough County Commissioner, and she intends to carry that work to Washington.
