The Feminist Majority and the Feminist Majority PAC have endorsed Kimberly Overman for Congress in Florida’s Twelfth Congressional District.
In their endorsement letter, the organizations pointed to her record of public service and her work advancing affordable housing, public health, and economic opportunity as the kind of people-centered leadership Congress needs.
The Feminist Majority also framed the endorsement around unfinished business: the Equal Rights Amendment.
The organization argues the ERA is one congressional vote away from becoming the 28th Amendment, and that a Democratic majority could pass the joint resolution affirming it.
Overman has made protecting women’s rights and reproductive freedom central to her campaign, and the endorsement reflects confidence that she would take up that fight.
Overman spent three decades as a Certified Financial Planner before serving as a Hillsborough County Commissioner from 2018 to 2022, including a year as Chair.
She later helped found the Housing Leadership Council of Tampa Bay.
That record — financial security, housing, and public health — is what the Feminist Majority cited in its decision.
