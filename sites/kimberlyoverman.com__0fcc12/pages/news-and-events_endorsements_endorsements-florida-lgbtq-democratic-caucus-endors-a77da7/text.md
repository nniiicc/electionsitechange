Standing up for every member of our community is a core value of Kimberly’s campaign.
She believes in building a Florida where LGBTQ+ residents can live, work, and raise families free from discrimination, and where their rights are protected rather than treated as a political bargaining chip.
That means listening to the families, young people, and neighbors across Florida’s 12th Congressional District who are fighting for equal protection under the law, and carrying their voices to Washington.
The Caucus’s endorsement is a signal to voters, volunteers, and supporters that Kimberly stands with the LGBTQ+ community in the fight for equality and safety.
It’s a responsibility she takes seriously, and a commitment she will carry into office .
