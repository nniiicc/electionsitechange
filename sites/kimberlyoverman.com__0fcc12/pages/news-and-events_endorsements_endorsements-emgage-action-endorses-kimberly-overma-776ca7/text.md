Emgage Action is the nation’s leading Muslim American civic engagement organization, working to build the political power of Muslim communities and to advance policy rooted in human rights, civil liberties, and inclusive democracy.
Its endorsement reflects both alignment on the issues and a candidate’s demonstrated commitment to engaging Muslim Americans as full partners in policymaking.
Throughout her public service, Kimberly has worked to make government inclusive and accessible to every community.
As a Hillsborough County Commissioner, she prioritized appointing and partnering with leaders from diverse racial, cultural, and faith backgrounds, and she has built lasting relationships with Muslim residents, business owners, and civic leaders across Tampa Bay.
She unequivocally denounces Islamophobia and all forms of discrimination, and supports a fair and humane immigration system, strong due process protections, and independent leadership that centers constituents over outside influence.
Emgage Action’s endorsement affirms that Kimberly will ensure Muslim Americans are represented in the decisions that affect their lives, not consulted only after the fact.
It is a commitment to ongoing engagement, partnership, and a foreign and domestic policy grounded in human rights and accountability.
“I am honored to have earned support from organizations like Emgage Action that reflect the values of our community.
