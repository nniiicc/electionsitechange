A 2026 Gun Sense Candidate
Recognized by Moms Demand Action
Kimberly Overman has been named a 2026 Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate, a distinction reserved for candidates who champion gun violence prevention and are committed to governing with gun safety in mind.
Moms Demand Action is the nation’s largest grassroots movement working to end gun violence, mobilizing parents, students, survivors, and neighbors in every state to demand common-sense reforms.
Keeping families and communities safe is a core value of Kimberly’s campaign.
She believes we can protect the rights of responsible gun owners while advancing the common-sense measures a broad majority of Americans support.
That means listening to the parents, students, and survivors across Florida’s 12th Congressional District who are working to end gun violence, and carrying their voices to Washington.
The Gun Sense Candidate distinction signals to voters, volunteers, and supporters that Kimberly stands with them in the fight for safer communities.
It is a responsibility she takes seriously and a commitment she will carry into office.
