Kimberly Overman Endorsed by the Democratic Women’s Club of Florida
The Democratic Women’s Club of Florida has endorsed Kimberly Overman for Congress.
A mother, grandmother, and caregiver who built a career helping families plan for financial security, Kimberly knows that the decisions made in Washington land hardest on women — on their health care, their paychecks, and their ability to care for the people who depend on them.
DWCF’s endorsement adds the strength of a statewide grassroots network to a campaign focused on affordability, individual rights, and economic security for families across FL-12.
