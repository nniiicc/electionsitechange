The political arm of the Brady Campaign — the nation’s oldest gun violence prevention organization, founded through the leadership of Republicans Jim and Sarah Brady — named FL-12 as one of three races nationally that will determine whether the next House has a gun violence prevention majority.
In announcing the endorsement, Brady PAC described Overman as a longtime gun violence prevention advocate and former Hillsborough County Commissioner whose public service has centered on the everyday concerns of Florida families, including whether their children come home safely from school.
The organization pointed to the local-government perspective she would bring to federal decisions about public safety.
Brady PAC President Kris Brown said the election is about what the next majority does with its power, arguing that Americans are exhausted by a Congress that places gun industry profits ahead of lives.
