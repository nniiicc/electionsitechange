Home » News & Events » Endorsements » Endorsements: Democratic Progressive Caucus of Florida Endorses Kimberly Overman
Kimberly Overman is an endorsed candidate of the Democratic Progressive Caucus of Florida (DPCF), the statewide caucus that organizes progressive Democrats around bold, people-first policy and holds candidates to values such as fair wages, workers’ rights, and grassroots accountability.
DPCF endorsements go to candidates who not only share progressive positions but run their campaigns consistent with those values.
Kimberly’s campaign meets that standard.
It is a lean, grassroots-driven effort in which all paid staff, contractors, and consultants are compensated at or above the $15/hour minimum wage the Caucus champions, reflecting a commitment to treating workers fairly.
Her platform, protecting reproductive freedom, strengthening Social Security and Medicare, making housing and insurance affordable, defending voting rights, and advancing economic and climate justice, is squarely aligned with the Caucus’s progressive agenda.
DPCF’s endorsement affirms that Kimberly is the candidate best positioned to carry progressive values into Florida’s 12th Congressional District and to Washington, built on trusted relationships, sustained engagement, and a record of results.
