MEET KIMBERLY
Kimberly Overman is a mom, grandmother, small business owner, and former Hillsborough County Commissioner running for Congress in Florida’s 12th District.
A champion for working families and a proven leader during times of crisis, Kimberly brings both lived experience and a track record of results.
From affordable housing to public health, she has always fought to make government work for the people—not special interests.
Now, she’s ready to take that fight to Washington.
