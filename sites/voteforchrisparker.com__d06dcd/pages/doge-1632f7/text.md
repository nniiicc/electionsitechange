D.O.G.E.
This reporting form is operated by the Chris Parker for State Senate campaign and is not affiliated with or operated by any federal, state, county, or local government agency.
Residents of Indiana Senate District 17 and surrounding communities may use this form to report concerns regarding potential government waste, fraud, abuse, inefficiency, or mismanagement.
Information submitted through this form will be reviewed by the campaign and may be used for policy research, constituent services, or referral to appropriate governmental authorities when warranted.
Anonymous submissions are permitted.
However, anonymous reports are often difficult to verify and may limit our ability to investigate, follow up, or refer matters to appropriate authorities.
This form is not monitored by law enforcement and should not be used to report emergencies or crimes in progress.
If you believe immediate action is necessary, contact local law enforcement or emergency services.
By submitting information, you represent that the information provided is truthful and accurate to the best of your knowledge and that you are not knowingly submitting false, misleading, or defamatory allegations.
