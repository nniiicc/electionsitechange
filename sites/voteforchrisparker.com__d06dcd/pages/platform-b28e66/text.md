Protecting Our Agricultural Heritage!
Grant, Huntington, and Wabash Counties are deeply rooted in agriculture.
In our community, farming is a way of life that shapes character, strengthens families, and serves as the backbone of our local economy.
I believe we can encourage responsible economic growth while preserving the farmland that feeds America and sustains our rural communities.
Stewardship of the land is both an economic and moral responsibility.
That means standing strongly against bad-faith large-scale industrial projects including solar, wind, and carbon sequestration projects to ensure they do not undermine property values, local decision-making, or destroy the long-term vitality of our agricultural economy.
Growth should strengthen rural Indiana, not threaten our way of life.
Fighting for Better "Hoosier First" Local Development & Property Tax Reform!
Economic development should reflect the priorities of local communities and our Main Street economies.
Our district’s future depends on supporting our family-owned businesses, encouraging local job creation, and ensuring that development decisions respect both taxpayers and the community's character.
I will accomplish this by promoting policies that cut unnecessary red tape and wasteful spending to ease the tax burden on our families and local businesses.
Indiana can compete and grow without sacrificing our core values, our small towns or our beautiful farmland.
In addition to reducing the tax burden on Hoosier innovation and families, we must review and expand upon the Tax Reform made on property taxes.
We must go to the heart of the issue, which is the lack of state accountability and local transparency for county property assessments, to ensure fair and accurate appraisals.
To that end, I pledge to continue the fight for lower tax burdens and transparency for my fellow Hoosiers.
Upholding Our Christian Values!
Our communities are strongest when families are strong and our faith is not undermined.
I will continue to stand firmly for the sanctity of life, the importance of strong marriages and families, and the protection of our Christian traditions and heritage.
We must preserve the moral foundations of our society that teach responsibility, compassion, and respect for others.
To that end, government should protect the freedom of churches, schools, and families to live according to their deeply held convictions without interference and social engineering.
If elected as your State Senator, I pledge to strongly defend our faith and our way of life!
Will Unashamedly Defend our God Given 2nd Amendment Rights, & Strongly Support our Law Enforcement
As a staunch defender of the Constitution, I proudly support the Second Amendment as a fundamental right that protects the freedoms of all law-abiding Americans.
The right to keep and bear arms is not just about self-defense, it's about preserving our liberty against tyranny, ensuring personal security, and upholding the principles that make our nation strong.
I will fight tirelessly against any attempts to infringe on this essential right.
Additionally I believe to secure public safety, our great law enforcement officers must be supported and given the necessary tools to keep our streets safe.
I will fully support our police and law enforcement officers and tough-on-crime policies that keep our families and communities.
Fighting for Strong Immigration Enforcement and Protecting Local Jobs for Hoosiers!
A secure immigration system is essential to our nation’s rule of law and our economic stability.
Indiana should prioritize policies that protect jobs good paying jobs for Hoosier workers and ensure that businesses operate on an equal playing field.
I support the strong enforcement of existing laws and cooperation with federal immigration authorities to prevent any illegal activity that harms our communities, whether from illegal aliens or from riots and mob rule.
We must ensure that our immigration and workforce policies benefit our fellow citizens first and not just the rich and well-connected.
To this end, I will encourage and advance policies that promote legal America First employment, provide support to our local families, and ensure a brighter future for our children.
Empowering Parents and Strengthening Education Opportunities!
I am a firm believer that education should equip students for real life not just for standardized tests.
As a lifelong homeschooling parent, I believe that our right to choose our the right education for our children is paramount.
Therefore, I firmly support school choice and believe that educational funding should follow the student, not the system.
Our economy is rapidly changing, and college degrees are no longer a proven path to success; a college education only grows more and more expensive and out of reach.
I will support policies that increase opportunities for our children to gain access to training programs and apprenticeship opportunities where they can learn real skills early in life and have the opportunity to learn while setting themselves up for a successful career without the financial burden that higher education can bring.
