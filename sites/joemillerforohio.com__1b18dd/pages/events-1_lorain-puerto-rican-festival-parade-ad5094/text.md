Join Team Miller for the Lorain Puerto Rican Festival Parade!
Help us support Lorain’s Puerto Rican community while showing your support for Rep.
Joe Miller and his campaign for Ohio Senate District 13.
Participants should arrive at the Boys & Girls Club parking lot for check-in beginning at 11:00 a.m.
The parade will step off at 1:00 p.m. and end near the corner of Clinton Avenue and East 33rd Street, where participants will turn into Oakwood Park.
Please RSVP so we can keep you updated with any additional lineup information.
Campaign shirts and literature will be available.
