Want to help make a difference this election?
Join us for a free canvass training at the North Ridgeville Public Library!
Whether you're brand new to canvassing or just looking for a refresher, this training will help you feel confident talking with voters at the door.
We'll cover canvassing best practices, how to use the MiniVAN app, what to expect while knocking doors, and answer any questions you may have.
No experience is necessary just bring your enthusiasm and a willingness to help elect Democrats up and down the ballot.
We look forward to seeing you there!
