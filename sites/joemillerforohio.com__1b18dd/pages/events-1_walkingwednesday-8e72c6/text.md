Join Team Joe Miller and the Amherst Democrats at Walking on Wednesday in downtown Amherst!
We’ll be set up with the Amherst Democrats along Park Avenue, talking with voters, sharing campaign literature, and showing support for Joe.
Volunteers are encouraged to wear a Miller shirt that will be provided.
Walking on Wednesday is a great chance to connect with voters in a relaxed community setting while enjoying vendors, local businesses, and downtown Amherst.
Come out, have some conversations, and help us build support for Joe Miller and Democrats up and down the ballot.
