meet joe
State Representative Joe Miller, a Lorain County native, graduated from Marion L.
Steele High School and earned degrees in education and history from Bowling Green State University and Ashland University.
His varied career spans teaching, coaching, planning, and management, providing him with a broad perspective on Ohio's business challenges.
After 9/11, he returned to education, teaching at high school and college levels, and remains an active community volunteer.
Joe, who lives in Amherst with his family, advocates for quality education, job creation, renewable energy, and veterans' support.
He has championed equitable school funding, economic revitalization, green energy, veterans' honors, local control in schools, and has been a strong voice against the opioid crisis and for women's rights.
Joe envisions Ohio as a leader in innovation, job creation, and sustainability.
on the issues
Joe Miller is committed to addressing key issues facing Ohioans through comprehensive and forward-thinking policies.
On jobs, he believes in pivoting Lorain & Huron County's economy from its manufacturing past to future-oriented roles that leverage the region's natural assets, supporting workers and businesses to foster a robust middle class.
To combat the opioid crisis, Joe advocates for a compassionate, multifaceted approach that enhances treatment access, education, and strict pharmaceutical regulations.
He staunchly defends reproductive rights, ensuring access to comprehensive healthcare.
In education, Joe seeks to restore Ohio's excellence by opposing detrimental policies and enriching public schooling.
He emphasizes affordable, quality healthcare for all, particularly in the face of opioid-related tragedies.
Lastly, Joe champions environmental protection, advocating for laws that safeguard water, land, and air to prioritize community health and future sustainability.
