Georgia is always on my mind.
In my roles as mother, disability advocate, health insurance advocate, and animal rescue foster mom, I’ve developed two common threads in my work:
Service.
Protection of others.
The issues we face in our district and state are complex.
Voters deserve a leader who knows how to put service first.
A leader who will not back down when protecting rights for all.
While it is important to collaborate with people of all political backgrounds, I will never compromise my integrity or the people I represent.
Serving and protecting others has been my life’s work.
This meaningful work has also led to some of my greatest joys.
Divisions have deepened.
It is an uncertain time for many Georgians.
Our representatives have put politics over people for far too long.
As a public servant, I will always put you first.
As a leader, I will fight for you and your family.
My name is Meredith Greene.
I am running for Georgia House District 26 in Forsyth County.
I am a lifelong advocate.
It’s time for me to advocate for you.
