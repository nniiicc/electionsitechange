An IN PERSON BRJA 13th of the Month Event
A year ago, we gathered to process the 2024 election and look toward 2025’s new and continuing realities.
Our wide-ranging conversation identified some crucial areas of concern: building a culture of democracy; building and wielding power for people; and paying close attention to both race and class in our organizing, education, and politics.
Join us this month as we transition from looking forward to exploring and refining how we can move forward collectively.
BRJA moderator Julia Scott will be joined by panelists Dottye Burt Markowitz, Andy Ellis, and Lawrence Grandpre.
This is a 13th event you won’t want to miss!
