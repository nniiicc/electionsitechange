Mary's County: Let's Build a New Political Future!
Are you ready to shake up Maryland politics?
Join Ellis-Andrews for Governor for an exciting Green Party Community Meeting in Leonardtown!
Meet Andy Ellis – Hear directly from Andy about his vision for a Maryland that has more and healthier political parties, puts people and planet first and builds an economy based on solidarity.
Shape the Future – Let's talk about the work we need to do to build real alternatives to the two-party system and grow the Green Party's presence in St.
Mary's County.
Engage & Discuss – This isn't a lecture—it's an interactive conversation!
Bring your ideas, your questions, and the issues that matter most to your community.
Who should attend?
Greens looking to take action Independents searching for a real political home Democrats and Republicans who want to explore new solutions Anyone fed up with politics as usual and ready to imagine something better Why Now?
Why the Green Party?
People across Maryland see that the two-party system is failing—on justice, democracy, peace, and the climate crisis.
While national Republicans embrace MAGA authoritarianism, St.
Mary's County and Maryland Democrats use their overwhelming majorities to pursue business as usual politics.
We know business as usual politics are not working, we need people powered movements and a bold vision for how we are going to do something better.
The demand for a real alternative has never been stronger.
The Maryland Green Party is in its 25th year, and in 2024, it was the top-performing third party in the state.
Now, we're building something even bigger.
This is your chance to be part of the movement and take action for a Maryland that works for everyone.
