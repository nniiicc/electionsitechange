BODILY AUTONOMY
I strongly believe that politicians should be kept out of family medical decisions.
I oppose any and all obstacles to accessing reproductive healthcare such as abortion and contraception.
Additionally, I strongly advocated against efforts to ban gender-affirming care for minors at the Statehouse, and event sent a letter to Governor DeWine urging his veto.
It is critical that we, as policymakers, support healthcare providers and their ability to practice according to evidence and established best practices.
