Passed HB 352, May 22nd, 2024
House Bill 352 passed the Ohio House in May 2024!
This legislation would create an Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) Study Commission in Ohio.
This study commission will compile experts and individuals with lived experiences to discuss and recommend legislative strategies for addressing the prevalence and long-term effects of ACEs.

Passed HB 5, the Adoption Modernization Act, June 27th, 2023
House Bill 5, the Adoption Modernization Act, unanimously passed the Ohio House on June 27th!
This bipartisan, priority legislation modernizes Ohio’s adoption process and prioritizes the best interest of the child.
By refining Ohio’s current adoption law and eliminating resource barriers for families, this bill will propel our state forward by ensuring that all Ohio children have the accessibility to safe and nurturing homes.
