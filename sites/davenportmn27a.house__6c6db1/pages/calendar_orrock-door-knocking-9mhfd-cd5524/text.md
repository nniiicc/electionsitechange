Come join your like-minded friends across Sherburne County for a day of door knocking!
We'll be knocking doors on Sunday, August 23rd to help get the word out about our local DFL-endorsed candidates, Vanessa Davenport (HD 27A), Charles McConaughay (HD 27B), and Brandon VanDover (SD 27).
These conversations are crucial as we start to build capacity for our BLUE WAVE this November!
Mobilize Sign Up / Facebook Event Page
A brief (~20 min) training is provided and new canvassers will always be paired with someone more experienced, so don't fear if you've never knocked doors before!
We also have water, sunscreen, and snacks on hand.
Door knocking is typically a 2 hour commitment.
It may seem silly, but studies have shown that knocking on doors and meeting voters directly is one of the most effective ways to ensure that people stay engaged in their communities and vote in elections.
We hope to see you there!
Meet-up Location: Orrock Township Hall
Time: 10 AM - 1 PM or 2 PM - 5 PM (pick one or both!)
Candidates: Vanessa Davenport (27A), Charles McConaughay (House 27B) and Brandon Van Dover (Senate 27) plus Doug Chapin (CD6).
