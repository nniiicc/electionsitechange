Protect Taxpayers.
The fraud cases making headlines were not accidents.
Whistleblowers were ignored or punished, and warning signs went uninvestigated.
That is not just incompetence.
It is a system that is broken and easy to game.
I have a plan to fix it: the Minnesota Integrity in Government Act.
It brings the same accountability rules public companies have followed for twenty years to every agency and nonprofit that takes taxpayer dollars.
Stop fraud before the check is cut, instead of investigating after your money is already gone.
"Every dollar lost to fraud is a dollar that could have gone to our schools, our roads, and property-tax relief here in White Bear Lake, Lino Lakes, and across District 36." The Minnesota Integrity in Government Act PREVENT : Every agency and nonprofit that receives taxpayer dollars must have a fraud-prevention plan on file before a single dollar is spent.
CERTIFY : Agency heads personally sign off that their controls are working, or face real consequences.
EXPOSE : Every audit failure is posted publicly within thirty days.
No more hiding problems.
Whistleblowers are protected and rewarded.
