Protect Taxpayers.
Applying proven anti-fraud protections to government spending.
I’m Heidi Heino.
I am a candidate for Minnesota Senate District 36.
I am a business leader and community advocate who has called District 36 home since 2006.
I bring real-world experience and a results-driven approach to public service.
One of my top priorities is to put an end to governmental fraud and protect the investment taxpayers have made into the great state of Minnesota.
Here is just one example of what I will do if I am elected.
The Problem The recent fraud cases we’ve heard so much about recently weren’t accidents.
They involved retaliation against whistleblowers and failure to investigate.
That’s not incompetence, it is a system that is broken and easily skirted.
My Plan I will introduce and fight to pass the Minnesota Integrity in Government Act (MIGA) to apply the proven anti-fraud rules set forth in the Sarbanes-Oxley Act to ALL entities receiving taxpayer dollars.
MIGA brings private-sector financial accountability to government spending through a three-step control system: PREVENT : Every agency and nonprofit that receives taxpayer dollars must have a fraud-prevention plan on file before a single dollar is spent.
CERTIFY : Agency heads personally sign off that their controls are working, or face real consequences.
EXPOSE : Every audit failure is posted publicly within thirty days.
No more hiding problems.
Whistleblowers are protected and rewarded.
This structure mirrors the private-sector standards that successfully restored trust in corporate financial reporting over twenty years ago.
Why It Matters What does this have to do with governmental fraud?
Government and taxpayer-funded organizations are exempt from the rules set forth in Sarbanes-Oxley, leaving the door open to fraud, waste, and abuse.
My Commitment Fight to pass these proven anti-fraud protections Hold officials accountable Stop fraud before it starts We already know this works.
It’s time to protect taxpayers.
What is MIGA?
The Minnesota Integrity in Government Act is a bill to bring private-sector accountability to government: PREVENT fraud before it happens.
CERTIFY that controls work.
EXPOSE failures publicly.
If you take taxpayer dollars, you are accountable to taxpayers.
