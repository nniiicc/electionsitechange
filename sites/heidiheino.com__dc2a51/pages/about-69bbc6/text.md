Why I'm Running I'm running because Minnesota needs strong leadership with the wisdom and experience to deliver meaningful, long-term change — not quick fixes or political theater.
In my day-to-day work, I handle high-stakes conversations, calm tense situations, and bring opposing sides back to the table.
I am particularly effective at identifying underlying issues others miss and developing practical, often unconventional solutions when standard approaches fall short.
My strength is staying steady when others are frustrated, identifying what is out of balance, and negotiating outcomes that are fair, workable, and sustainable — even when no one gets everything they want.
What I Bring Leadership focused on long-term solutions, not short-term gains 🎯 Strategic Thinking I focus on strategic, long-term solutions.
Leadership isn't about quick fixes or band-aids — it's about understanding how decisions ripple over time and designing systems that work not just today, but years down the road. 🔍 Systems Perspective I pay close attention to systems, not just symptoms.
