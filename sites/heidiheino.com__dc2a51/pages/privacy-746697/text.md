This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your information when you interact with our website and campaign.
Information We Collect We may collect the following information when you interact with our campaign: Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and zip code when you sign up to volunteer, join our email list, or contact us.
Volunteer Interests: Information about how you would like to help the campaign.
Donor Information: Information required by campaign finance regulations when you make a contribution (name, address, occupation, and employer).
Usage Data: Information about how you use our website, collected through cookies and similar technologies.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Communicate with you about the campaign, including updates, events, and volunteer opportunities.
Coordinate volunteer activities and respond to your inquiries.
Process and report contributions in compliance with Minnesota and federal campaign finance laws.
Improve our website and campaign outreach efforts.
Information Sharing We do not sell your personal information.
We may share your information with: Campaign Staff and Volunteers: To carry out campaign operations.
Service Providers: Third-party vendors who assist with email communications, text messaging, website hosting, and payment processing.
Government Agencies: As required by campaign finance disclosure laws.
Mobile Messaging Privacy We do not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers or opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
However, we may share your personal information with third-party text messaging platform providers or other trusted service partners as necessary to send you messages under our mobile program.
Spam Protection Forms on this website are protected by Google reCAPTCHA.
When you submit a form, Google may collect device and interaction data to tell person submissions apart from automated ones.
That processing is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Google Terms of Service .
Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.
Your Rights You may request to be removed from our email list at any time by using the unsubscribe link in any campaign email or by contacting us at heidi@heidiheino.com .
To opt-out of text messages, reply STOP to any message you receive.
