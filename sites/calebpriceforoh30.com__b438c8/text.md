Meet Caleb
Caleb was born in Cincinnati and is a proud graduate of Cincinnati Public Schools: Walnut Hills High School (‘20).
He attended St.
Teresa of Avila Catholic Grade School in Price Hill and grew up playing soccer for Total Futbol Academy (TFA) and Kings Hammer.
One of Caleb’s favorite memories growing up is spending hot Summer days at Phillips Swim Club and going bowling at Western Bowl!
Caleb graduated cum laude from the University of Illinois Chicago in Chicago, Illinois in 2023 and is currently finishing his masters degree in Public Administration.
Caleb has experience working with local groups such as starting an organization to raise money for the Flint Water Crisis in 2014 called H2O for Earth, the Special Olympics of Ohio & Kentucky, being a Spanish language translator for a back-to-school supplies drive in Lockland in 2021, and worked with members of Cincinnati City Council to rename sections of Reading Road after former President Barack Obama.
Caleb is a dues paying member of the IBEW Local 1220, from his time working as a barista.
Caleb has worked all kinds of jobs in the service industry and broadcasting industry, such as being a buss boy at Skyline Chili, cashier at Home Depot, and Cincinnati Reds Team Store associate.
Caleb understands the meaning of a dollar earned from a hard day’s work, which is why he plans on being a fiscally smart representative who looks out for the average worker and the average family.
In 2019, Caleb was the recipient of the Semper Fidelis All-American Award, presented by The United States Marine Corps to the top 100 high school juniors across the country for excellence in community service, academics, and athletics.
As a member of the high school Class of 2020, Caleb was dealt challenges from the COVID-19 Pandemic that make him well-equipped to tackle the challenges that Columbus brings.
As a member of Gen Z, Caleb brings a fresh set of ideas and unique skillset of being able to navigate the new challenges of the 21st Century and beyond by having a new set of life experiences that current long-time members of the General Assembly do not possess.
Modern problems require new solutions.
Caleb currently resides in Green Township.
