The 87th District
Thomas Kutz currently serves as State Representative in Pennsylvania's 87th House District in the heart of Cumberland County.
The 87th House District was recently redrawn and had a new configuration beginning in 2022.
The new 87th House District includes Monroe Township, Silver Spring Township, Upper Allen Township, part of South Middleton Township (precincts 1, 2, 6, 7, 8, and 9), part of Lower Allen Township (precinct 2), and all of Mount Holly Springs borough.
When Thomas was elected in 2022, it was an open seat with no incumbent.
