Television advertising records identify “Women for Troy/DSCC/Jackson” as the advertiser on a joint “hybrid” buy, personally approved by Jackson himself.
The Democratic Senatorial Campaign Committee is a national party committee that has received donations from 198 corporate PACS and at least 28 billionaires this cycle alone.
“Troy Jackson wants credit for refusing corporate PAC checks while enjoying an advertising partnership with a committee that cashes them,” said Blake Kernen, spokesperson for Collins for Senator.
“Apparently, according to Troy Jackson, corporate money is somehow more acceptable once it passes through a party committee before it lands directly in his hands..
Troy’s pledge to not accept corporate PAC money or donations from billionaires comes with fine print – and Mainers should read it.” And Women for Troy?
Despite its separate name, it is a campaign committee, authorized by Troy Jackson, and registered with the Federal Election Commission on October 1.
Its donor information is not yet available on the FEC’s public summary page so only Troy Jackson knows who is bankrolling it.
Jackson’s sweeping claims about rejecting corporate influence deserve scrutiny when his own advertising operation partners with a committee that accepts money from billionaires and corporate PACs.
