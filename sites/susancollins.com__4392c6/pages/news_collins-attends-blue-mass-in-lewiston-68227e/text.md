Senator Susan Collins attended the 2026 Blue Mass at the Basilica of Saints Peter and Paul in Lewiston.
Bishop James Ruggieri celebrated the Mass.
Many priests and deacons also attended from throughout the Diocese.
The ‘Blue Mass’ pays tribute to the dedication exhibited by local, state, and federal law enforcement officers, as well as firefighters and emergency responders.
