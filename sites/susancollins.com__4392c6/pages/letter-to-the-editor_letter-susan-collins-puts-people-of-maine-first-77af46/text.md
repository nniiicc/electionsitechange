Susan Collins.
In an “us versus them” political landscape, I believe Collins stands out as a dedicated leader who consistently puts the people of Maine ahead of party politics.
She makes Mainers the “us,” no matter who the “them” is.
Her remarkable work ethic and voting record demonstrate her deep commitment to our state.
She shows up and works tirelessly on our behalf.
Just as importantly, I believe her proven commitment to bipartisanship means she is willing to reach across the aisle to deliver real, meaningful results for Maine families.
For those of us living in rural areas, her leadership on healthcare has been vital.
At a time when I see state-level policies and financial pressures forcing rural hospitals to close or scale back critical services, Collins has remained an unwavering champion for our rural healthcare infrastructure, fighting to keep emergency rooms, clinics, and quality medical services accessible close to home.
As a veteran, I am particularly grateful for her steadfast support of our service members, military families, and veterans.
She has consistently advocated for improved VA services, better healthcare access for rural veterans, and the resources our heroes deserve after serving our nation.
I believe Maine needs Susan Collins’ experienced, loyal, and hardworking voice in the U.S.
Senate.
I hope my fellow Mainers will join me in supporting her.
