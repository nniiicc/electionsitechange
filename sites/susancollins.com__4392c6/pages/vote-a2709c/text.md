In-Person Absentee (Early Voting) October 5 - October 29 Registered voters can vote in-person absentee before Election Day at their city or town clerk's office.
Absentee / Mail-in Voting Request by October 29, 5 PM Any registered voter can request an absentee ballot to fill out at home.
Ballots can be returned via postal mail, deposited in a municipal secure ballot drop box, or returned in person to the local clerk's office.
