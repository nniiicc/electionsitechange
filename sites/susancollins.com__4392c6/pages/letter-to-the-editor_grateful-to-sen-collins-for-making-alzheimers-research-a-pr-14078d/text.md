After a years-long process of doctor’s visits and misdiagnoses, I was diagnosed with Alzheimer’s at the age of 53.
As a widowed mother of three teens, this was a devastating development.
We met with Sen.
Susan Collins, who has a clear vision for how Alzheimer’s prevention, detection, treatment, and care can continue to be improved with Congress’s support.
Thanks to Sen.
Collins’ leadership, Congress has continually increased funding in the years since, reaching a record $3.8 billion in 2024.
