Six Maine airports will receive a total of $2,263,425 to support important infrastructure improvements according to U.S.
Senator Susan Collins, Chair of the Senate Appropriations Committee.
The funding comes from the Federal Aviation Administration’s (FAA) Fiscal Year 2025 Airport Infrastructure Grants (AIG) program, made possible by the Infrastructure Investment and Job Act (IIJA).
