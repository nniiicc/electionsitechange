Showing Up for Maine
Susan Collins believes the people of Maine deserve a Senator who works as hard as they do and will always be there to represent their interests.
Since taking office, Susan has cast more than 9,800 consecutive roll-call votes without ever missing a single one.
This is an outstanding record of commitment.
Her perfect attendance record reflects a simple principle: public service is a profound responsibility that she takes seriously.

Meet Susan Collins
Susan Collins was born into a family with a strong tradition of public service and commitment to Maine.
She is a proven leader who is respected by colleagues on both sides of the aisle, is consistently ranked the most bipartisan Senator, and she has been recognized by her colleagues as one of the hardest working members of Congress.
Susan has a long record of significant legislative accomplishments in such critical areas as health care, improving the economy and growing jobs in Maine, helping Maine’s small businesses thrive, education, national security, veterans, and helping older Americans.
