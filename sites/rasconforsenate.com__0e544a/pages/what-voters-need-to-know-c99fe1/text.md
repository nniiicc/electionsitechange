Sarah Rascón Is Funded By People She Will Represent
- Ninety-Six percent of Sarah's support comes from individual donors, with a median gift of $105.
Her donors are retirees, teachers, small-business owners, and working families from our community.
- Sarah refuses contributions from Big Oil, tobacco, pharmaceutical companies, police associations, private prisons, gun manufacturers, corporate landlords, charter school interests, and companies that undermine workers' rights.

Sarah Rascón's Record
- Helped lead the Upper LA River revitalization, bringing new parks and green space to communities that had been left out for decades.
- Built job-training and STEM programs for local youth.
- Has worked in local government to improved coordination on homelessness, mental health access, affordable housing, school safety, and wildfire recovery.
