Neighbor to Neighbor
This campaign isn’t backed by big political organizations or special interests.
It’s powered by people across the Northwoods who believe representation should feel more personal, more honest, and more connected to the communities living here year-round.
Every contribution helps us:
- travel across the 34th Assembly distrct,
- meet voters face-to-face,
- attend local events,
- print campaign materials,
- and continue building a campaign focused on conversations—not political noise.
Why your support matters
For too long, it’s felt like regular people have less and less of a voice while campaign money and outside interests carry more influence.
This campaign is different.
Merlin is running to represent neighbors, local communities, and the people who actually live and work here—not the interests funding campaigns.
If you believe the Northwoods deserves a representative who listens first and shows up consistently, we’d be grateful for your support.
