What is Pennsylvania House District 45?
Pennsylvania House District 45 is a state legislative district in western Allegheny County.
Voters in the district elect one state representative to serve in the Pennsylvania House of Representatives in Harrisburg.
It is different from a congressional district or a state Senate district.
The district includes Bridgeville, Carnegie, Collier Township, Coraopolis, Kennedy Township, McKees Rocks, Neville Township, Pennsbury Village, Robinson Township, and Stowe Township.
