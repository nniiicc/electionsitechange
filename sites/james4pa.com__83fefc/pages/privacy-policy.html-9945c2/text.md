Privacy Policy for Friends of James Julius
Introduction
Welcome to James4PA.com, the official digital platform for the Friends of James Julius political campaign.
Our commitment to the privacy of our community, comprising supporters, volunteers, and website visitors, is paramount.
This Privacy Policy details the approaches we adopt regarding the collection, use, protection, and disclosure of personal information obtained through James4PA.com, associated emails, texts, and any other digital interactions facilitated by our campaign.
Scope of Policy
This policy extends to information collected:
- On our website, James4PA.com.
- Through electronic communications between you and this website, including emails and texts.
- Via any mobile or desktop applications downloaded from this website, facilitating direct interaction with us.
- During your engagement with our online advertisements and applications on third-party platforms, provided these platforms link to this policy.
Exclusions
- Information collected offline or through other means, including on any site operated by third parties.
- Any third party's collection, including through any content or advertisements that may link to or be accessible from our website.
1.
Information Collection and Use
Personal Information
We collect personal identifiers such as names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, and other contact information.
This collection occurs:
- Directly from you, when you voluntarily provide it to us.
- Automatically, as you navigate through the site, including usage details, IP addresses, and information gathered via cookies and similar technologies.
Purpose of Data Collection
The primary goal of collecting personal information is to send texts and emails for campaign updates, volunteering opportunities, and fundraising appeals, ensuring you stay informed and engaged with Friends of James Julius's activities.
Opt-In and Communication Preferences
SMS participation requires a separate, explicit SMS opt-in.
You may choose the optional, unchecked SMS consent box on our website form and submit it with your phone number.
Our existing Textla program also accepts the keyword YES at 1-844-423-0715.
Joining an email list alone does not subscribe you to text messages.
We use the message types disclosed when you opt in; updating a form or policy does not expand your previous SMS consent.
Opt-Out Mechanism
You maintain the right to opt out of our communications at any time.
Opt-out can be achieved by:
- To unsubscribe, reply STOP to the campaign message you received.
For assistance, reply HELP or email campaign@james4pa.com.
- Unsubscribing via the links provided in our emails.
- Sending an email to campaign@james4pa.com.
2.
Third-Party Services
Mobile information and text opt-in consent data will not be sold or shared with third parties for promotional or marketing purposes.
Except as otherwise stated in this Privacy Policy, we do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise share for marketing purposes your Personal Information with third parties without your consent.
Our website may contain links to third-party websites or services.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such third parties.
We encourage you to review the privacy policies of those third parties when accessing their websites or services.
3.
Data Protection and Security
We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information.
However, it is important to note that no digital platform can guarantee absolute security.
4.
Changes to Our Privacy Policy
We may update this policy to reflect changes to our information practices.
If we make any material changes, we will notify you by email, sent to the email address specified in your account, or by means of a notice on this website prior to the change becoming effective.
We encourage you to periodically review this page for the latest information on our privacy practices.
Contact Us
For questions related to this Privacy Policy, please contact us at campaign@james4pa.com.
