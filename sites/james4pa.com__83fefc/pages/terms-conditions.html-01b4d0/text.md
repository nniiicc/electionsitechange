STOP Command — Unsubscribe
To unsubscribe, reply STOP to the campaign text message you received.
Instructions such as “STOP 2 END” mean that you should reply with the word STOP.
You will receive a confirmation of your unsubscribe request, and no further program messages will be sent unless you provide a new opt-in.
For assistance with an unsubscribe request, email campaign@james4pa.com.
HELP Command — Assistance
For help, reply HELP to the campaign text message you received or email campaign@james4pa.com.
