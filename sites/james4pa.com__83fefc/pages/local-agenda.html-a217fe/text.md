A Local Agenda for District 45 Focus on the work Pennsylvania can do close to home.
James Julius is running to serve the people of District 45 with a practical agenda: lower costs, expand opportunity, strengthen the services communities rely on, defend life, and make state government answer to the people.
Practical by design A state representative should be clear about what the job can accomplish.
A member of the Pennsylvania House can write and vote on state law, shape the Commonwealth budget, oversee state agencies, help municipalities compete for state resources, and provide direct constituent service.
That is where James will put his attention.
No legislator can set every price, control every local decision, or guarantee an agency outcome.
James's commitment is to use the real tools of the office, explain his votes, and measure progress by what helps families and communities in District 45.
Priority 01 Lower household and energy costs.
Pennsylvania should produce more reliable energy, move it efficiently, and make it easier for families and employers to benefit from the resources and expertise already here.
James supports an all-of-the-above approach that values natural gas, nuclear power, renewables, storage, and the infrastructure needed to keep the grid dependable.
What Harrisburg can do State lawmakers can improve permitting without weakening safety standards, modernize transmission and generation policy, reject mandates that unnecessarily reduce reliable capacity, scrutinize taxes and fees that raise household costs, and create predictable rules for long-term energy investment.
A realistic promise State government does not set grocery or gasoline prices.
It can help reduce the pressures behind higher bills by increasing energy capacity, limiting avoidable government costs, and supporting a competitive Pennsylvania economy.
Priority 02 Grow jobs, skills, and opportunity.
District 45 should be a place where people can learn a useful skill, find good work, start a business, and build a life without having to leave the community they call home.
What Harrisburg can do The Commonwealth can align workforce dollars with jobs employers are actually trying to fill, strengthen career and technical education, support apprenticeships and partnerships with the trades, simplify unnecessary licensing barriers, and make state programs easier for small businesses to navigate.
A realistic promise Government does not create lasting prosperity by announcement.
It can build the conditions for growth: useful education, dependable infrastructure, reasonable rules, sound finances, and a workforce ready for the opportunities Pennsylvania can attract.
Priority 03 Fix infrastructure and protect essential services.
Roads, bridges, water systems, emergency response, and other basic services are not side issues.
They are the foundation of safe neighborhoods, a strong local economy, and confidence in government.
What Harrisburg can do A representative can fight for fair transportation and infrastructure funding, help local governments identify competitive grants, support equipment and training for police, firefighters, and EMS, and use budget oversight to insist that state programs deliver value instead of waste.
A realistic promise Municipalities make many local choices themselves.
James will respect that role while making sure District 45 has an active partner in Harrisburg when communities need state coordination, funding, or agency action.
Priority 04 Defend life and secure equal justice.
For more than 50 years, District 45 has defended life.
James will carry that tradition forward by standing for equal protection and equal justice under the law for preborn children.
What Harrisburg can do Pennsylvania lawmakers write and vote on the Commonwealth’s abortion laws.
James will support legislation that provides preborn children equal protection under the law and oppose policies that deny them that protection.
A clear promise James will vote consistently to defend life, explain those votes plainly, and work to advance equal protection and equal justice under the law for every preborn child.
Priority 05 Provide responsive, accountable representation.
The office belongs to the people of District 45.
Residents should be able to reach their representative, understand where he stands, and get help navigating state government when a problem cannot be solved with another form letter or phone transfer.
What the office can do James will maintain accessible constituent service, hold regular conversations across the district, publish clear explanations of major votes, help residents work through state-agency problems, and bring local officials, employers, schools, and community organizations to the table when an issue crosses jurisdictional lines.
A realistic promise Constituent service cannot guarantee that every agency will decide a case a certain way.
It can guarantee that residents are heard, receive honest information, and have a representative who follows through.
