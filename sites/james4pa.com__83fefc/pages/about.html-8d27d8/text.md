Why I'm running
I was born right here in District 45, raised here, and now I'm raising my kids here.
I earned my education here, built my business here, and have spent a lifetime getting to know the communities and people that make this district home.
I'm running because I want to serve you.
A state representative should listen, be available when a neighbor needs help with state government, and stay focused on the work Harrisburg can do for the people who live here.
I want our families to be able to afford the lives they work for, our children to find opportunity close to home, and our communities to receive honest, accountable representation.
This is personal to me because the future we are building is my family's future, too.
Ready to serve
James's work, education, and community service show how he approaches responsibility: listen carefully, solve the problem, and be accountable for the result.
James earned an MBA in finance and quantitative analytics from Carnegie Mellon University and degrees in computer science from Robert Morris University.
That training helps him test assumptions, follow the facts, and understand the tradeoffs behind state policy.
As a father and Assistant Scoutmaster with Scouting America, James knows leadership begins with service, responsibility, and consistently showing up for the people who count on you.
Trust earned by showing up
In 2024, James earned the Republican nomination through a successful write-in campaign.
He went on to earn more than 15,000 votes and 42% of the general-election vote—the strongest Republican showing in District 45 since it was created in 1969.
Those votes are not a line on a résumé.
They represent neighbors who trusted James to listen, work hard, and offer a serious local agenda.
He is running again to earn the trust of the entire district and provide the accessible, responsive representation its communities deserve.
