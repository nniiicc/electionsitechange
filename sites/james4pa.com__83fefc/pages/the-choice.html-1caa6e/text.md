The Race for State Representative James Julius and Brittany Bloam: the choice for District 45.
This is the James Julius campaign’s comparison of the candidates for Pennsylvania State Representative in House District 45.
Explore James’s policy priorities , his background , and the source material below.
My opponent is running for the Pennsylvania House.
Why does so much of her campaign sound like a race for Congress?
A Different Focus My opponent is focused on Washington.
I’m focused on us.
A state representative should begin with the people and communities of the district: listening, helping constituents navigate state government, and working on the decisions Harrisburg can make close to home.
I am running to serve you—the people of District 45—with a practical agenda for affordability, opportunity, infrastructure, public safety, equal justice for preborn children, and accountable government.
Two Ideas of the Job A national political fight, or local public service?
My opponent, Brittany Bloam, repeatedly frames this state House race around national conflict in her own campaign-paid literature.
My opponent's campaign Mail marked “Paid for by Brittany for PA” leads with phrases including “MAGA’s madness,” “Trump’s chaos in Washington,” “Stop ICE and Trump’s overreach,” and “Stop federal overreach.” Voters can judge those priorities for themselves.
I believe the emphasis tells us something important about how each candidate understands the job.
The work I am asking to do I want to use the authority of a Pennsylvania legislator to increase reliable energy capacity, drive down utility costs, improve roads and infrastructure, strengthen career and technical education, support local employers, equip first responders, and demand responsible state budgets.
Those are practical responsibilities that affect whether families can afford to stay, work, and raise their children here.
A Clear Difference on Life On abortion, voters deserve clarity.
An outside PAC promoting my opponent describes my opponent as “100% pro-choice, no matter what.” My opponent’s own campaign literature separately promises to protect abortion access.
That is an extreme, no-questions-asked position on abortion.
I support equal protection and equal justice under the law for preborn children.
For more than 50 years, District 45 has defended life, and I intend to carry that tradition forward in Harrisburg.
Roots and Responsibility Born here.
Raised here.
Raising my family here.
My opponent moved to District 45 five years ago.
I was born right here in this district, raised here, and am raising my kids here.
I know these communities because they have shaped my entire life.
That connection is why I am running: to serve the neighbors, families, workers, small businesses, and first responders who make this district home.
Residency source: WESA reported in February 2026 that my opponent had lived in her current District 45 home for the past half-decade.
Read the report.
My Purpose A name you know.
A legislator you can trust.
Trust starts with showing up, telling voters what state government can actually do, and staying focused on the people you were elected to represent.
I will bring a local agenda to Harrisburg and measure success by results here in District 45—not by how loudly I join the latest national political battle.
A Precise Contrast The question is one of focus, not whether Harrisburg has a role.
Pennsylvania lawmakers can affect state election law, abortion law, Medicaid appropriations, and the use of state funds.
I am not claiming that every issue named in my opponent’s literature is outside the authority of a state representative.
The contrast is the organizing priority: a campaign centered on Washington political combat, or a district-centered agenda built around practical state action and dependable constituent service.
