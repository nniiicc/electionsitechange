The taxes collected by the State of Missouri are growing quickly, and it is vital to ensure that Your dollars will be used appropriately, with appropriate oversight.
I believe it is the duty of the Legislature to ensure that we are only taking from your wallet the smallest amount necessary to fund government functions.
I support tax cuts to offset our rapid tax collection growth, as our citizens deserve to use their own paychecks to fulfill the needs of their families.
Are your Taxes Skyrocketing?
Mark Matthiesen is focused on ensuring that our taxes are not increasing beyond the needs of our government.
Recently many County Assessors have seized the opportunity to take more of your money by over-assessing the value of personal property based on a temporary market shift.
Other Assessors are altering property classifications to overtax your Real Property.
Local taxing districts are finding new ways to spend your money as their revenue increases while still telling you that they don’t have enough money.
I will work to end these abuses!
Health Care Preventing Mandates The Federal Government, as well as several Local Governments have attempted to inflict health care policy that includes mandated vaccines.
Mark Matthiesen does not believe that ANY government has the right to mandate a vaccine in order to hold a job, go to school, or any other function of life.
Support nurses When hospitals mandate that their nursing staff care for more patients at one time than is appropriate, they put safety at risk for both the patients and nurses.
When proper care cannot be provided deadly mistakes can occur.
Studies by The National Institute of Health have shown that the risk of patient death increases by 16% for every additional patient that is assigned to a nurse.
Hospitals across the country are blaming the nurses, leaving the legal burden to them when proper staffing could have prevented the situation form occurring.
Representative Matthiesen has filed legislation that will establish safe staffing levels in Missouri hospitals and require that they are being transparent with the State of Missouri, their staff, and their patients on how well they are meeting those goals.
Environment Conservation of Natural Resources Mark Matthiesen is committed to ensuring that our families have clean running water and unpolluted air.
Mark has passed legislation to create a program enabling communities to investigate potential toxic contamination concerns.
Mark Matthiesen will work to ensure that program is consistently funded and utilized properly.
Radioactive Waste Representative Matthiesen has spent years learning about the radioactive contamination left throughout the Saint Louis Metropolitan region from the Manhattan Project.
While our Federal Government acknowledges the devastating health effects that workers have suffered since they were tasked to build our nuclear arsenal, the residents of the contaminated areas have never been helped.
Representative Matthiesen has traveled to Washington DC along with community organizers to bring attention to the continued health repercussions or our community while pressing the EPA, Army Corp of Engineers, Department of Energy, and Responsible Federal agencies to clean up the mess that they created.
