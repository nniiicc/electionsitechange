Who I Am?
________
As a father of eight and a dedicated community volunteer in La Mirada, I have always believed that our primary responsibility is to protect and nurture our children and the family as the foundation of our culture.
This belief is the cornerstone of my campaign for the California State Assembly in District 64.
Born in Downey, raised in Norwalk and now living in La Mirada; we’ve raised our children in District 64.
I’ve served my community as a business leader and minister of the gospel of Jesus Christ for 41 years .
As a husband, father, and a concerned citizen, it is past time to stand up for our parental authority.
As parents we must determine how to raise our children and ensure a quality education of the highest standard.
I will continue to:
- Protect Parental Authority
- Protect Our Children’s Innocence
- Provide Quality Education
- Ensure Public Safety by Supporting Law and Order
- Be Your Small Business Advocate
- Leading the charge for change in Jesus’ name!
