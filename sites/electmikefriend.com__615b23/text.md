For over a decade, Mike Friend has served as a dedicated community organizer in Florida House District 102, working on the ground to address the real challenges our neighborhoods face.
He understands that strong communities are built on the foundation of shared prosperity, hard work, and mutual support.
Mike brings firsthand experience advocating for expanded healthcare access and fighting for equitable public school funding.
He knows that every family deserves quality medical care without the threat of bankruptcy, and every child deserves a classroom equipped for the future, regardless of their zip code.
In Tallahassee, Mike will champion policies that matter to everyday Floridians.
From tackling the escalating affordable housing crisis and addressing the pressing impacts of climate change to defending our fundamental rights, Mike is committed to delivering actionable progress for our district.

Economy Tackling the Property Insurance Crisis & Strengthening Our Economy Florida's families are being squeezed by skyrocketing property insurance premiums and an out-of-control cost of living.
While working families struggle to make ends meet, corporate special interests continue to get handouts in Tallahassee.
We need an economy that builds from the bottom up and the middle out.
"I will fight to lower property insurance rates, support local small businesses, and build an economy that works for every Floridian, not just the wealthy few." Read more Education Fully Funding Public Education & Supporting Our Teachers Our public schools are the foundation of our community's future.
For too long, we've watched as public funds are diverted to unaccountable private institutions while our local classrooms remain underfunded.
We must ensure every child has access to a world-class public education regardless of their zip code.
"I promise to champion our public school system, fight for meaningful teacher pay raises, and expand access to quality early childhood education." Read more Healthcare Ensuring Accessible Healthcare & Protecting Reproductive Freedom Healthcare is a fundamental human right, not a privilege for those who can afford it.
We must take immediate action to expand Medicaid, lower the crushing cost of prescription drugs, and firmly protect reproductive rights from government overreach.
"I will always stand against efforts to restrict reproductive freedom and work tirelessly to expand healthcare access so every family gets the care they need."
