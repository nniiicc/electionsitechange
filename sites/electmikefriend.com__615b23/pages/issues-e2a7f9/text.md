Priority 01 Tackling the Property Insurance Crisis & Strengthening Our Economy Florida's families are being squeezed by skyrocketing property insurance premiums and an out-of-control cost of living.
While working families struggle to make ends meet, corporate special interests continue to get handouts in Tallahassee.
We need an economy that builds from the bottom up and the middle out.
Mike's Promise "I will fight to lower property insurance rates, support local small businesses, and build an economy that works for every Floridian, not just the wealthy few." Priority 02 Fully Funding Public Education & Supporting Our Teachers Our public schools are the foundation of our community's future.
For too long, we've watched as public funds are diverted to unaccountable private institutions while our local classrooms remain underfunded.
We must ensure every child has access to a world-class public education regardless of their zip code.
Mike's Promise "I promise to champion our public school system, fight for meaningful teacher pay raises, and expand access to quality early childhood education." Priority 03 Ensuring Accessible Healthcare & Protecting Reproductive Freedom Healthcare is a fundamental human right, not a privilege for those who can afford it.
We must take immediate action to expand Medicaid, lower the crushing cost of prescription drugs, and firmly protect reproductive rights from government overreach.
Mike's Promise "I will always stand against efforts to restrict reproductive freedom and work tirelessly to expand healthcare access so every family gets the care they need." Priority 04 Protecting the Right to Vote for Every Broward County Resident Voting rights are under attack.
We need to protect early voting, mail-in ballots, and voter registration.
Every eligible citizen deserves easy access to the ballot box.
I'll fight to strengthen voting protections and ensure fair elections that represent all Broward County residents.
Mike's Promise "I'll defend voting rights and ensure every voice in District 102 is heard." Priority 05 Broward County faces real climate threats — rising seas and extreme weather.
Our community is on the front lines of climate change.
We need bold action to reduce emissions, invest in green infrastructure, and protect our coastlines.
Environmental protection creates jobs and builds a sustainable future for our children.
I'll champion climate solutions that protect our environment and economy.
Mike's Promise "I'll lead on climate action to protect Broward County's future." Priority 06 Gun violence is a public health crisis affecting our families and schools.
We need commonsense gun safety measures that protect our communities while respecting Second Amendment rights.
Universal background checks, red flag laws, and safe storage requirements save lives.
I'll work to reduce gun violence and keep our children safe in schools and neighborhoods.
Mike's Promise "I'll fight for gun safety measures that protect our community." Priority 07 Women's healthcare decisions should be made by women and their doctors, not politicians.
Reproductive freedom is under attack.
Women deserve access to comprehensive healthcare, including abortion services.
I'll fight to protect a woman's right to choose and ensure access to contraception, prenatal care, and family planning services.
Our daughters deserve the same freedoms our mothers had.
Mike's Promise "I'll defend reproductive rights and women's healthcare access." Priority 08 Every person deserves to live authentically and be treated with dignity.
LGBTQ+ Floridians face discrimination in housing, employment, and healthcare.
We need comprehensive anti-discrimination protections and support for transgender youth.
I'll fight to ensure equal rights, protect against discrimination, and create a welcoming community where everyone can thrive regardless of sexual orientation or gender identity.
Mike's Promise "I'll champion equality and protect LGBTQ+ rights in District 102." Priority 09 Fighting Antisemitism, Racism, and All Forms of Hate Hate has no home in Broward County.
I will fight antisemitism, racism, and every form of bigotry that targets marginalized communities.
We must protect our public schools and places of worship so every family can worship, learn, and gather without fear — and so every person can live a safe, dignified life free from violence and discrimination.
Mike's Promise "I'll stand with every neighbor and work tirelessly so all families — no matter their background, faith, or identity — are safe, respected, and free to live with dignity." Priority 10 Building Bridges and Supporting Our First Responders Safer communities are built on trust and partnership.
I will build bridges across neighborhoods, faith communities, and civic groups throughout District 102.
I salute and support the brave men and women of our law enforcement and fire rescue departments who put their lives on the line every day to keep us safe.
Mike's Promise "I'll partner with Broward's law enforcement and fire professionals to strengthen public safety, improve community relations, and keep our neighborhoods secure." Family at the heart of leadership.
Committed to fighting for District 102.
