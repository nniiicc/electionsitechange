Michael “Mike” Friend is a dedicated healthcare professional, community leader, and proud Floridian committed to building a brighter future for District 102.
Born and raised with a strong sense of community service, Mike has always believed in giving back to the place he calls home.
A long-time resident of Davie, FL, he understands the unique challenges and incredible opportunities that exist within our neighborhoods.
Through his extensive education and years of hands-on experience, Mike has developed a practical, results-driven approach to solving problems.
Davie, Florida Family & Roots in Broward County At his core, Mike is a family man.
He lives in Davie, FL with his wife, Hayley, who shares his passion for community involvement.
Together, they are raising three wonderful children: Jayden, Liam, and Myla.
The Friend household is always full of energy, complete with the love of their beloved family pets.
Mike's drive to serve in public office is deeply rooted in his desire to ensure that his children, and all children in Florida, grow up in safe, prosperous, and inclusive communities.
A Career in Healthcare Mike's professional journey is defined by his commitment to patient care and healthcare accessibility.
As a licensed dentist, he built and managed successful dental practices, learning firsthand what it takes to run a small business while prioritizing the well-being of his patients.
Beyond his private practices, Mike has dedicated time to teaching and mentoring the next generation of dental professionals, passing on his knowledge and commitment to ethical care.
Today, he continues his work in the healthcare sector through a leadership role at Solstice Benefits, where he focuses on expanding access to affordable, high-quality dental and vision care for families across the region.
Advocacy & Leadership Mike has never been one to sit on the sidelines when he sees systemic issues affecting his patients and colleagues.
He took on an active leadership role within the Florida Dental Association, advocating fiercely for policy changes in Tallahassee.
Through this work, he fought to improve patient rights, enhance healthcare standards, and protect the integrity of the medical profession from corporate overreach.
Standing Against Hate in All Its Forms Mike believes Broward County is strongest when every neighbor is respected and protected.
He will fight antisemitism, racism, and any form of hate that targets marginalized communities.
That means defending our public schools and places of worship, speaking out against bigotry, and working to ensure every family, regardless of background, faith, or identity, can live a safe and dignified life.
Building Bridges for Safer Communities Safer neighborhoods are built through trust and partnership.
Mike will bring communities together across District 102 and support the brave men and women of our law enforcement and fire rescue departments who serve Broward County every day.
He is committed to strengthening public safety, improving community relationships, and ensuring families across Pembroke Pines, Cooper City, Davie, Plantation, Sunrise, and Southwest Ranches feel secure at home, at school, and in their houses of worship.
A Vision for Florida House District 102 Mike is running to bring a voice of reason, compassion, and practical experience to the State Capitol.
His campaign is focused on the critical issues facing everyday Floridians: Housing & Condo Crisis Tackling skyrocketing costs and ensuring safe, affordable living for all residents.
