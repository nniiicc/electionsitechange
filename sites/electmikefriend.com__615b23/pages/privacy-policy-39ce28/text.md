This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (the "Site") and interact with our campaign.
Please read this privacy policy carefully.
By accessing or using our Site, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all the terms of this Privacy Policy.
Information We Collect Information You Provide We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you interact with the campaign, such as when you sign up to volunteer, make a donation, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us.
This may include: Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
Financial Data: Payment card details and billing address (processed securely by our third-party payment processors).
Compliance Data: Occupation and employer information required by state campaign finance laws for contributors.
Communication Data: Messages, feedback, or any other content you send to us.
Automatically Collected Information When you access the Site, our servers automatically collect certain information about your device and usage, including: Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
Usage Information: Pages viewed, time spent on pages, links clicked, and referring website addresses.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect for various campaign purposes, including to: Provide, operate, and maintain our website and campaign services.
Process and manage your donations and comply with campaign finance reporting requirements.
Communicate with you about campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities.
Respond to your comments, questions, and requests.
Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activities to improve our website and outreach.
Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions or illegal activities.
Campaign Contributions Florida state law requires us to use our best efforts to collect and report the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of individuals whose contributions exceed reporting thresholds.
When you make a contribution, this information will be shared with the appropriate state regulatory agencies and may become publicly available.
Email Communications If you sign up for our email list, we will send you updates regarding the campaign.
You can opt-out of receiving these emails at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of our emails or by contacting us directly.
Text Messages By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS/MMS messages from our campaign.
Standard message and data rates may apply.
You can opt-out at any time by replying "STOP" to any message you receive from us.
Information Sharing & Third-Party Services We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
We may share your information with: Service Providers: Vendors and consultants who need access to such information to carry out work on our behalf (e.g., payment processing, email delivery, hosting).
Legal Requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or other legal processes, including campaign finance disclosures.
Safety and Protection: To protect the rights, property, or safety of our campaign, our supporters, or others.
Data Security & Your Choices Data Security We implement reasonable security measures designed to protect your information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Your Choices You have the right to request access to the personal information we hold about you, request corrections to it, or request its deletion.
To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.
Additional Disclosures Children's Privacy Our website is not intended for children under 13 years of age.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.
Florida Residents If you are a Florida resident, you may have specific rights regarding your personal information under state law.
We are committed to complying with all applicable state privacy regulations.
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
