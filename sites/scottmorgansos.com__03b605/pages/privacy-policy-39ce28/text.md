Privacy Policy
Last Updated: 03/05/2026
We believe your trust matters more than anything, so here’s the plain truth:
- When you sign up, volunteer, or donate to United Kansas, you’re sharing some information with us — things like your name, email, phone number, mailing address, and anything else you choose to give.
We use it to keep you in the loop, invite you to events, and let you know about ways to get involved.
- We don’t sell it.
We don’t trade it.
We don’t share it with anyone who’s not working directly with us to run this movement.
- Like most websites, we do collect basic site stats (pages visited, time on site, browser type) so we can see what’s working and make the site better.
- You can opt out of our emails anytime — just click “unsubscribe” or email us directly.
You can also ask us to show you, update, or delete the information we have about you.
- We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe and secure.
- If we ever change this policy, we’ll post the update here with the new date at the top.
