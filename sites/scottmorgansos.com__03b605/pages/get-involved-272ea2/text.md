United Kansas , a new moderate political party for Kansans who believe in problem solving over partisanship.
We are on the ballot in 2026.
Too often, politics is pulled to the extremes.
Moderation is not weakness.
It is conviction grounded in Kansas values.
Public debate is increasingly driven by outrage and ideological purity, leaving the sensible center without a voice.
United Kansas offers a practical alternative: principled moderation with a backbone.
Our candidates are grounded in a common set of principles and trusted to apply them with judgment, reflecting the needs of their communities.
This approach provides both direction and flexibility and is how we build a collective voice for the broad political center.
We are working for change in the 2026 election, but we are also starting to build something intended to last far beyond a single campaign cycle.

One of the strongest ways to support our work is to register as a member of the United Kansas Party.
With just over 1,000 registered voters, every new member helps build momentum.
You can support us without changing your registration, but becoming a member is a clear next step.
