Skip to content Home Meet Dave Issues Contact Donate (opens in a new tab) Meet Dave Senator Dave Craven represents Randolph, Richmond, Montgomery, Anson, and a portion of Union counties in District 29.
He was elected in 2020 and is the youngest serving Republican member of the North Carolina Senate.
He currently serves as Co-Chair of the Finance Committee.
He serves as a member of the Agriculture, Energy, and Environment, Commerce and Insurance, Judiciary, Pensions and Retirement and Aging, Regulatory Reform, Appropriations on Education/Higher Education committees, and the Joint Legislative Commission on Government Operations.
A native of Randolph County, Senator Craven serves as the Senior Vice President at Fidelity Bank focusing on business development.
He attended the University of North Carolina at Charlotte where he was elected Student Body President and served on the Board of Trustees.
He graduated with degrees in Accounting, Finance, and Political Science.
As an active member of his community and state, Senator Craven was appointed to the board of directors for the Economic Development Partnership of North Carolina in 2016 by the Speaker of the North Carolina House of Representatives.
In addition, he has served as the treasurer, vice chairman, and chairman of the Randolph County Economic Development Corporation.
He has also served on the boards of Randolph Friends of the Zoo, Habitat for Humanity of Randolph County, Communities in Schools, Randolph County United Way, and most recently as President of the Asheboro Rotary Club.
