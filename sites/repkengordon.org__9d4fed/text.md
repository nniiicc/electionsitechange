Whether you have a question, concern, idea, or need help, please contact me according to the instructions below and our office will be in touch as soon as possible.
General Office Contact: If you are a constituent and need immediate assistance: Contact my Legislative Aide, Jo Joseph, at jo.joseph@mahouse.gov or (617) 722-2070.
For constituent services: Contact me at kenneth.gordon@mahouse.gov.
Please be sure to include your name, phone number, and address so that we can prioritize our constituents in the 21st Middlesex District.
I receive many emails each day, many of which asking me to support bills or funding requests.
Please know that I do read each one and take note of constituent positions on issues.
You can help me respond to your inquiry efficiently by doing the following: If you are emailing me about a specific bill, please include the bill number.
Regardless of the subject matter of your email, please include your phone number and address.
If you are not sure who your legislator is, you can check here: Find My Legislator From Ken's Office Office Hours Rep.

Four times a year, Rep.
