Reaching back to his days as a news reporter, Ken has made use of his role as State Rep to bring the newsmakers of the Commonwealth to Burlington, Bedford and Wilmington through his award-wining BCAT government show, “Rappin’ With the Rep.” The show, which can be seen most evenings at 6:30 on BCAT’s Government Channel, was named top community news production of the year in 2014 by BCAT.
