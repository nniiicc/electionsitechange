BIOGRAPHY
Meet Vanesia Johnson
Vanesia R.
Johnson is a Licensed Clinical Social Worker and Board Approved Clinical Supervisor in the State of Texas.
She is the mother of two sons who are proud born and raised Texans.
Since 2001, Vanesia has served as an administrator in the nonprofit and private sector.
She has extensive experience overseeing programs and clinical operations involving substance abuse, HIV/AIDS services, community and medical case management, healthy relationships, mental health services, research, quality assurance, and corporate compliance.
Vanesia earned her master’s degree in Social Work from Tulane University in New Orleans, LA and her bachelor’s degree in Social Work from Mississippi Valley State University, a HBCU in the heart of the Mississippi Delta.
