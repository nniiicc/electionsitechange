Diversity and Inclusion
Advocated in solidarity with Fort Bend Gender Benders, a local youth coalition, fighting to get Fort Bend ISD school board to revise the district’s anti-discrimination policy to make it more inclusive and protective of GLBTQAI+ students
Pioneered and chaired the first ever county endorsed cultural diversity task force in Fort Bend County
Engaged stakeholders through informational sessions to increase awareness about the importance of diverse representation on Fort Bend ISD school board
Organized a successful accountability strategy and team of mental health advocates to address bigotry precluding language access and interpretation services for Asian Americans seeking mental health services with Fort Bend County’s mental health authority
Addressed telephonic and in person language access barriers and interpretation services for Hispanic families’ engagement and support in the re-integration plan of returning youth from the state’s juvenile detention center back into their community
