This community has given so much to me and my family, and I’m ready to put my experience to work for the people, families, and businesses who also call this area home.
Stacy Kohlmeier
Republican Candidate for House District 67
Foundational Priorities
- Support strong public schools - Maintain local control and fully fund our K-12 schools.
- Invest in our local workforce - Support higher education and workforce development to keep our young people learning, working, and building their futures in Kansas.
- Encourage economic growth - Support entrepreneurship and innovation to create new jobs, grow our tax base and improve quality of life.
- Practice responsible fiscal stewardship - Modernize government to improve efficiency and lower cost of service to reduce taxes.
- Strengthen our communities - Invest in infrastructure, affordable housing, and healthcare access to attract and retain our families.
Effective leadership begins with listening.
As I meet with families, business owners, educators, healthcare professionals, farmers, and community leaders, I am committed to learning directly from the people I hope to serve.
The best solutions come from thoughtful conversations, practical experience, careful research, and a genuine willingness to understand the real challenges facing Kansas communities.
Rather than approaching issues with predetermined political talking points, I want to listen carefully, ask questions, study the facts, and work collaboratively to pursue responsible, practical solutions.
My approach is grounded in common sense, integrity, and a deep respect for the people and communities that make Kansas strong.
Guiding Principles
Stacy is committed to serve by these principles to develop ideas and practical solutions that make a difference for all living and doing business in the Manhattan area and across Kansas.
- Government must first align on the goal to ensure that results solve a stated problem and/or optimize an opportunity.
- For government to be most effective, it is important that public matters are assigned to the appropriate government authority for handling.
- To achieve meaningful, positive results for any organization, listening and collaboration across stakeholders is imperative.
- Policies that support economic and business growth in our communities are vital to an improved quality of life for all.
- Robust, respectful debate is important to developing ideas and solutions that result in positive outcomes.
