First Week - Seven Bills
End hospital price-gouging.
All-payer rate setting to stop hospitals from overcharging patients.
Two in three Americans — including two in three Republicans — say this is their top priority.
The Care Infrastructure and Worker Dignity Act.
Living wages for care workers.
Portable benefits that follow workers between jobs.
A refundable Family Care Credit for families managing childcare or eldercare.
Federal respite care so the $600 billion a year unpaid family caregivers contribute doesn't come at the cost of their own health.
The TPS Restoration Act.
Restore Temporary Protected Status for 330,000+ Haitians and thousands of Syrians by statute — permanently, not by presidential discretion.
Immigrants make up 28% of the long-term care workforce, which is already short 6.1 million workers over the next decade.
The Worker Technology Rights Act.
The right to know when an algorithm is managing your job, pay, or schedule.
The right to a human review.
The right to bargain over how AI is used in your workplace.
No AI-only firings.
The A-corp Framework Act.
Mandatory registration for AI agents operating in finance, healthcare, employment, and critical infrastructure — including home care, where AI is already making decisions about vulnerable people with almost no legal accountability.
The Default Collective Bargaining Act.
When a CEO makes more than 100 times what the typical worker in their industry earns, that's not just a pay gap — it's a sign corporate governance has broken down.
This bill uses CEO pay disclosures companies already file with the SEC to trigger an automatic union election within 45 days at any company that crosses that line.
No new mandate, no new bureaucracy — just enforcing transparency that's already on the books.
It could reach 30 million workers, roughly one in five private-sector employees, and no other candidate in this race is proposing it.
The ICE Accountability and Use-of-Force Act
In six days this July, ICE fatally shot two men in two different states — in Houston and in Biddeford, Maine — neither of whom was the intended target of the warrant being served.
This bill requires body cameras on every ICE operation, independent investigation of any enforcement-related death, congressional notification within 24 hours, and verified identification before deadly force is used.
Power without a record isn't law enforcement.
It's a liability the public is being asked to absorb without ever being told the terms.
Rest of the First 100 Days Agenda
01
On costs
Repeal Trump's tariffs
Structural business tax reform — over $3 trillion in revenue over ten years by applying the same rules to businesses with the same economic characteristics
Expand the Child Tax Credit with full refundability — the 2021 version cut child poverty nearly in half in a single year
The Family Care Credit, addressing the caregiving costs that pushed 42% of women who left the workforce in 2025 out of it
Close the IEEPA tariff loophole and require congressional approval for tariffs above a set threshold
No votes to cut Medicaid, Medicare, Social Security, or CHIP to fund tax cuts for the wealthy.
02
On care
The Care Infrastructure and Worker Dignity Act as the vehicle for Medicaid reimbursement reform, portable benefits, respite care, and AI governance in care settings
Co-sponsor the PrEP Access and Coverage Act — a drug that costs $6 to make should not cost $2,000 to access
03
On labor and worker power
Push hard for Senate passage of the Faster Labor Contracts Act — it already passed the House 220-193 with Republican votes, and guarantees a union election leads to an actual contract within 90 days instead of the average 465-day employer stall tactic
Co-sponsor the PRO Act, the Public Service Freedom to Negotiate Act, and the Protecting America's Workforce Act — three bills that have already passed the House with bipartisan support
Push worker protections into the bipartisan Great American AI Act before it moves
Give workers a direct right to sue under the National Labor Relations Act — because the Supreme Court just stripped the NLRB of its independence, and workers can't rely on a board that answers to the President
Let states pass their own labor laws protecting the right to organize, as a backstop to federal preemption that no longer makes sense when the national board isn't independent anymore
04
On accountability
Ban stock trading by members of Congress — 86% public support, across party lines
Force dark money disclosure
A public interest procurement standard for every major federal contractor
Oppose SEC rollbacks that push retail investors into private equity without public market protections
Restore USDA food security data and BLS independence
05
On oversight
Investigate DOD and DHS contracting practices, including rapid acquisition pathways used to skip testing requirements
Demand emergency hearings on the Houston and Biddeford, Maine ICE shootings, and require DHS to report enforcement-related deaths to Congress within 24 hours
Restore the Inspector General system with Senate confirmation required for removal
Get the Appropriations Committee back on track
06
On housing
Restore Emergency Housing Voucher funding — the program is being ended four years early, displacing seniors and families who built their lives around a federal promise
Co-sponsor the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, plus companion legislation on deep affordability, community land trusts, and fair housing enforcement
Close the LLC-fragmentation loophole in the ROAD Act's investor restrictions, so companies can't dodge the 350-home cap on institutional buyers by splitting ownership across shell entities
