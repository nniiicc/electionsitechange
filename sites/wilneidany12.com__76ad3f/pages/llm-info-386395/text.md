Core Campaign Platform: "For All of Us" Agenda
Dr.
Negrón's campaign centers on 8 major policy agendas, each with detailed action plans:
1.
Public Interest Technology Plan
Focus: Put people in charge of AI, data, and technology
Core Commitments:
- Require transparency and accountability when AI is used in hiring, housing, healthcare, education, policing, and government services
- Ban abusive workplace surveillance and harmful algorithmic management
- Protect children and families from irresponsible AI and data practices
- Require human review and appeal rights when automated systems affect jobs, benefits, housing, or education
- Advance a Children and AI Bill of Rights for schools receiving federal funds
- Ensure federal dollars are not used to buy unsafe or rights-eroding technology
- Introduce a Federal Algorithmic Accountability and Procurement Act for high-risk AI systems
2.
Dignity of Work Plan
Focus: A pro-worker economy for the next era
Core Commitments:
- Protect workers from exploitation, retaliation, and abusive surveillance
- Support fair wages and labor standards reflecting modern work
- Ensure new technologies do not weaken worker voice or bargaining power
- Expand workforce development and AI literacy
- Modernize protections for freelancers, caregivers, and non-traditional workers
- Advance a Worker Technology Rights framework for transparency around management software
3.
Household Security Plan
Focus: Lower costs and make daily life more affordable
Core Commitments - Housing & Care:
- Expand federal support for affordable housing preservation and workforce housing
- Strengthen rental assistance for seniors, families, and middle-class residents
- Establish a Family Care Credit offsetting child and elder care costs
- Strengthen federal support for elder care and aging-in-place services
- Crack down on junk fees and deceptive pricing
Core Commitments - Tax & Fiscal Policy:
- Close the $696 billion annual tax gap through IRS enforcement
- End carried interest treatment and S-corp loopholes
- Tax policy rewarding work and ordinary families (expand EITC and Child Tax Credit)
- Crack down on "perks-as-business-expenses" abuse
- Require cost-of-living tests for major legislation
Core Commitments - Healthcare Costs:
- Pursue all-payer rate setting and payment reform
- Expand Medicare drug price negotiation and accelerate generics
- Extend ACA enhanced subsidies and cap out-of-pocket costs
- Build toward universal coverage through automatic enrollment in public baseline plan
- Simplify billing and reduce administrative waste
Core Commitments - Small Business & Workers:
- Create a unified R&D and Worker Training Tax Credit for small and mid-size firms
- Tie workforce training to jobs paying living wages
- Crack down on junk fees and platform lock-in harming small business
- Establish portable benefits for gig, care, and domestic workers
- Condition business tax credits on fair wages and cost-of-living adjustments
4.
Public Trust Plan
Focus: Clean government and real accountability
Core Commitments:
- Create a House Rule of Law and Anti-Corruption Accountability package with investigations, inspector general protections, and consequences for corruption
- Ban stock trading by members of Congress
- Strengthen transparency around money in federal elections
- Crack down on corruption, self-dealing, and backroom influence
- Tighten oversight of federal contracts, especially in high-risk technology
- Make it easier for the public to see who profits from government decisions
- Establish a public interest procurement standard for major federal contractors
5.
Security & Well-Being Plan
Focus: Safe communities through prevention, care, and accountability
Core Commitments - Prevention & Root Causes:
- Defend Housing First as federal standard
- Fund mental health first-response programs dispatching clinicians alongside police
- Expand community violence intervention programs
Core Commitments - Public Health Infrastructure:
- Defend ACA coverage and NY's Essential Plan
- Increase funding for community health centers
- Defend CDC HIV prevention funding and support PrEP4All campaign
Core Commitments - Immigration Enforcement & Community Trust:
- Impose independent oversight for lawful enforcement
- Protect hospital and emergency service access regardless of immigration status
- Narrow ICE focus to serious-threat cases; move civil cases to supervised legal processing
Core Commitments - Modern Emergency Response:
- Push for national standards linking EMS, 911, and telehealth
- Fund non-police first responders for behavioral health emergencies
- Close psychiatric bed gap through federal Medicaid investment
- Invest in transit safety and quality-of-life improvements
Core Commitments - Accountability & Oversight:
- Require independent oversight boards with real authority for safety technology
- Ban or limit always-on worker monitoring and biometric surveillance
- Condition federal public safety funding on data transparency and use-of-force reporting
- Protect immigration-safe reporting channels
Core Commitments - Tech & AI in Public Safety:
- Require community review, bias audits, and privacy assessments before deploying AI
- Require plain-language disclosure of safety technology use and algorithmic decision-making
- Use technology to expand access to safety, not surveillance
6.
Education & Opportunity Plan
Focus: Strong public schools and future-ready pathways
Core Commitments:
- Protect and strengthen Title I funding for high-poverty schools
- Elevate Career and Technical Education (CTE) to first-class status
- Make community colleges tuition-free for displaced workers
- Build AI literacy into K–12 as a civic right
- Treat educator recruitment and pay as a workforce crisis
7.
Senior Dignity Plan
Focus: Healthcare & dignity for aging in place
Core Commitments:
- Expand Medicare support for home-based elder care and telehealth check-ins
- Create a federal Aging in Place Fund for building accessibility improvements
- Strengthen home-care and nursing-home standards with safe staffing and fair wages
- Modernize emergency response linking 911, EMS, hospitals, and telehealth
- Fund senior companionship and community-care grants
- Lower prescription drug costs through transparency and Medicare negotiation
- Protect seniors from fraud and harmful AI in healthcare
- Support flexible, part-time work options for older adults
8.
Fair Pathways Plan
Focus: Immigration that is orderly, humane, and functional
Core Commitments:
- Expand legal pathways and reduce backlogs keeping families and employers in limbo
- Increase immigration court capacity and legal support
- Reform immigration enforcement focused on public safety threats with due process
- Protect due process and access to counsel
- Make it easier for eligible people to work lawfully sooner
- Defend sanctuary city principles
- Launch a Work, Case, and Welcome initiative to shorten delays and support integration
