The Extraction Economy — Why Everything Costs So Much and Where Your Money Went
America's economy isn't broken.
It's been rewired.
It used to circulate money through workers, families, and communities.
Now it pulls money up and out — and calls it growth.
For fifty years, the rules of this economy got rewritten — not by accident, but page by page, by people with good lawyers.
The result has a name: the Extraction Economy.
And here is the part that should matter to anyone, left, right, or in between: an economy built on extraction doesn't just hurt the people at the bottom.
It grows slower for everyone.
We had 4–4.5% GDP growth when the rules made money circulate.
We have had about 2% growth since the rules changed to let it pool at the top instead.
Fairness and growth aren't in tension here.
They're the same fix.
