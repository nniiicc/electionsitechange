Sheriff Mike Ezell is 100% Pro-Life.
As a Christian father and grandfather, Mike Ezell firmly believes in the right to life of the unborn.

While overturning Roe v.
Wade was an important win for the pro-life cause, Mike knows that the fight is not over.
He'll stand up to defend Crisis Pregnancy Centers and other pro-life organizations from the radical left so that young mothers have the support they need.
Mike is honored to be
endorsed by National Right to Life, the nation's oldest and largest pro-life organization.
