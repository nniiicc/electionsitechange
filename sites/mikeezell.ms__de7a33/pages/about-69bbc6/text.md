Meet ﻿ Mike Ezell Congressman Mike Ezell spent more than 42 years as a law enforcement officer fighting to protect the people and communities of South Mississippi.
As our Congressman, Mike is committed to showing up, speaking up, and standing up for our conservative South Mississippi values every day.
S.H.
"Buck" Ezell Congressman Mike Ezell was born and raised in Pascagoula.
His father served on the police force and his mother worked as the secretary at their church.
At a young age, Mike's parents instilled in him the values of hard work and public service.
He bought his own lawnmower at the age of 11 and began his first regular-paying job at the age of 14.
After graduating from Pascagoula High School, Mike followed in his father's footsteps with the Pascagoula Police Department.

"Buck" Ezell A Legacy of Public Service Sheriff Mike Ezell was born and raised in Pascagoula.
His father served on the police force and his mother worked as the secretary at their church.
At a young age, Mike's parents instilled in him the values of hard work and public service.

Mike's wife Suzette looks on as he is sworn in as Jackson County Sheriff While serving full-time as a police officer, Mike attended night school and earned a criminal justice degree from the University of Southern Mississippi.
He is also a graduate of the Mississippi Law Enforcement Training Academy and the FBI National Academy.
Mike ran for and was elected Sheriff of Jackson County in a 2014 Special Election.
As Sheriff, he cleaned up corruption left behind by the previous administration.
He cut wasteful spending, investigated and fired corrupt officers and returned millions of dollars to the taxpayers.
Because of his proven success, Mike was re-elected in 2015 and became the first Sheriff in Jackson County history to run unopposed in 2019.
