Put America & Mississippi First
Sheriff Mike Ezell Will Stand for Us.
Mike Ezell believes in our way of life.
He'll fight back against socialist ideologies and critical theory because he knows America is a nation built on strong principles of faith, freedom, and opportunity.
Mike will work to improve the lives of everyday Mississippians by fighting Biden's inflation.
He'll fight to lower the prices of gas, groceries, and fertilizer by increasing domestic energy production and bringing critical supply chain jobs back to America from China.
He'll work to bring more high-paying jobs to our state and to support every business that calls South Mississippi home, from the biggest shipyard to the smallest family farm.
