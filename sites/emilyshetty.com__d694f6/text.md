Providing Scholarships & Service.
Delegate Shetty prides herself on offering top-notch constituent services to all residents of District 18.
Alongside her District 18 teammates, she has supported over 2,000 constituent cases ranging from assisting residents with unemployment claims, resolving issues with the Motor Vehicle Administration or other state agencies, and working through complex pedestrian safety challenges.
Her office has also provided tens of thousands of dollars of direct scholarship assistance to constituents throughout the community.
“As your Delegate I will continue to work every day to build a community centered on our shared priorities that is inclusive and supportive of everyone.”
— Emily Shetty
