We are being told that everything is broken and our nation is a disaster- and we get it 24/7 on every screen we touch.
And yet, we are still the strongest country in the world, even though our current leadership is working to diminish that power. ​ ​ No institution matters in this country more than our voting system.
Despite recent claims of election fraud, our voting systems are some of the safest and most accurate in the world. ​ There have been very few times in our history where our election system needed as much support as it does now.
You can help, and here is how: ​ ​ Preserve Free and Fair Elections Why do we need to protect elections?
What can be done? ​ ​ While elections laws are set by each state, the local election boards run by an equal number of Democrats and Republicans, ensure that all voters votes will be counted responsibly without bias. ​ ​ Each election, people from the age of 18 sometimes into their 70s all assemble to be trained and run election day.
While machines count votes, paper copies are kept confirming votes.
Each cycle, election boards review the last election and come up with ideas to make the process more assessable for legal voters and to protect from fraud.
Our elections get more accurate, not less. ​ ​ We need to work to make sure that people know how election boards keep their votes accurate and safe.
If you or someone you know is in doubt, the easiest way to do this is to research your own election board.
You can do this online and if you go to their office or call, they will help answer any reasonable question that you have.
The bi-partisan workers on election boards are some of the most dedicated people I have ever met.
And I love that they get along with each other with the common goal of counting our votes. ​ You can support this system by treating the people who work the election process with dignity and respect and encourage others to do so as well.
Speak up when someone spreads falsehoods, and know the facts on how our system works. ​ ​ ​ Key 33 Details – Show Me 33 Let’s talk about voting in our district!
