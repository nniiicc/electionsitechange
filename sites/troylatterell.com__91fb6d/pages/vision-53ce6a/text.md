Issue of The Week
The Latterell Vision
Missouri is an amazing place, a place I am proud to call home.
However I see Missouri drifting—undermining education, neglecting planning, and failing families.
That’s why I’m running for Missouri House District 33:
to listen to voters, protect our future, and put people first.
Supporting Public Education
- Strong Schools & Opportunity: I’ll fight to strengthen our public schools, support teachers, and expand job training - not schemes that drain resources from classrooms into private profit.
Stronger Economy, Stronger Families
- Families in our district deserve the chance to thrive.
That means fixing our roads and infrastructure, supporting local businesses, and bringing in good jobs so every family has the opportunity to succeed here at home.
Fair Taxes & Relief for Families
- Skyrocketing property assessments are squeezing families, and it’s happening because of laws set at the state level.
I’ll work to fix this broken system and find real solutions that give taxpayers relief.
Protecting the Will of the People
- Missourians have spoken loud and clear at the ballot box on healthcare, workers’ rights, and fair government.
I’ll fight attempts to silence voters or overturn ballot initiatives, because your voice should always count.
