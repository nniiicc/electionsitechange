PRESS RELEASE
Aaron Ford Slams Lombardo’s Weak Data Center Executive Order, Reaffirms His Commitment to Halting Tax Breaks And Prioritizing Community Input
Attorney General Aaron Ford released the following statement on Joe Lombardo’s sham of an executive order replicating a MAGA Republican strategy memo:
“Just last month, Joe Lombardo approved new tax breaks for data centers despite communities across Nevada voicing their concerns.
Lombardo has given data center companies more than $240 million in taxpayer dollars that should have been used to help Nevadans struggling to live in his failed economy – and in return, his campaign has been bankrolled with $2 million from MAGA data center and AI billionaires.
In his desperation to cling to power, Data Center Joe is trying to run away from his record with this last-ditch excuse for an executive order that maintains the status quo so many Nevadans are rightfully outraged by: massive giveaways for data center companies and no say for communities.
Lombardo’s executive order is not a reflection of what Nevada communities are asking for — instead, it's nearly copied and pasted from a MAGA Republican strategy memo.
“When I’m Governor, Nevadans will have the final say on whether or not a data center is built in their community — if local governments don’t want them, they shouldn’t be forced to have them.
I will pump the brakes on Lombardo’s data center gravy train, put communities in the driver’s seat, block data centers from raising Nevadans’ costs and depleting our resources, and hold the big data center companies accountable.”
BACKGROUND:
Joe Lombardo is embracing data centers “wholeheartedly” while Attorney General Ford is cutting off their tax breaks, imposing the strictest standards in the nation, and holding them accountable to protect Nevadans’ wallets and natural resources.
Joe Lombardo proclaimed the need to “take care of [his and Trump’s] billionaire friends” – and when it comes to the powerful billionaires trying to build dozens of data centers in Nevada, that’s exactly what he’s doing.
He’s one of the biggest data center cheerleaders running for office anywhere in America:
- The Nevada Current: In addition to being an ardent defender of billionaires, Lombardo is also among the most enthusiastic data center apologists with their name on a U.S. ballot this year.”

- Lombardo: “[Data centers are] a good solution”

Lombardo is taking millions of dollars from data centers, who are among his biggest donors, while he has given data centers over $240 million in tax breaks.
