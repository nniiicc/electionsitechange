Lombardo Says He’s Powerless to Address Affordability After Making it His Entire Campaign in 2022
As Nevadans struggle to afford basic necessities amidst the cost-of-living crisis created by Joe Lombardo and Donald Trump, Lombardo’s message to them is that they shouldn’t expect help from him anytime soon.
In new reporting, Lombardo was caught on tape saying he can’t do anything about affordability — voters’ number one issue this election.
Since Lombardo’s “commanding officer” took office for the second time, the average Nevada family has paid $3,300 more for goods and services.
The contrast in this governor’s race couldn’t be clearer.
As Governor, Aaron Ford will lower costs for Nevadans.
Lombardo, on the other hand, whines that he is “powerless to address affordability” while complaining that his six figure salary isn’t enough.
Lombardo blocked legislation that would lower housing costs, expand paid family leave, guarantee free school meals, and cap prescription drug costs.
