Mitch Clemmons for Congress Priorities Volunteer Donate Your information matters Privacy Policy Effective and last updated August 20, 2026 Our commitment This policy explains how the Mitch Clemmons for Congress Committee (“the campaign,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information through electmitchclemmons.com and related campaign forms.
We use personal information to communicate with supporters, organize volunteers, operate the campaign, and comply with applicable law.
We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.
Information we collect Information you provide We may collect information you choose to submit, including your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, volunteer interests, availability, messages, event registrations, and other campaign-related responses.
Donations Donations are processed on Anedot’s secure website.
Anedot may collect payment and legally required donor information under its own privacy terms.
The campaign may receive donor contact information, contribution details, and compliance records, but this website does not intentionally collect or store full payment-card numbers.
Website and analytics information Our website and service providers may automatically receive technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, referral source, approximate location, IP address, and cookie or similar identifiers.
We may use Google services and WordPress tools to understand site performance and campaign outreach.
How we use information Send campaign news, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities after you provide consent.
Coordinate volunteer activities and respond to questions.
Measure website performance, improve accessibility, and understand which outreach channels are effective.
Protect the website, prevent fraud or misuse, and maintain campaign records.
Comply with election, tax, accounting, and other legal obligations.
Service providers and disclosure We may share information with vendors that help operate the campaign, including Klaviyo for email subscriptions and volunteer records, Anedot for donation processing, Google services for analytics and site administration, and providers for website hosting, security, and technical support.
These providers receive information only as needed to perform services for the campaign.
We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, during a campaign reorganization, or with your direction.
Contribution information may be publicly disclosed when election law requires it.
Email, phone, and your choices Campaign email signups use double opt-in: you must confirm through the email Klaviyo sends before becoming subscribed.
Every marketing email should include an unsubscribe link.
A phone number submitted on the volunteer form is intended for personal volunteer coordination and does not by itself enroll you in automated campaign text messages.
Any future SMS program should use separate consent language.
Cookies and browser controls Data retention and security We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and recordkeeping.
We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
Children and outside links This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.
The site links to third-party services and social networks whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
Changes to this policy We may update this policy as campaign practices or legal requirements change.
The “last updated” date will identify the current version.
