Your information matters
Effective and last updated August 20, 2026
This policy explains how the Mitch Clemmons for Congress Committee (“the campaign,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects information through electmitchclemmons.com and related campaign forms.
We use personal information to communicate with supporters, organize volunteers, operate the campaign, and comply with applicable law.
We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration.
We may collect information you choose to submit, including your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, volunteer interests, availability, messages, event registrations, and other campaign-related responses.
Donations are processed on Anedot’s secure website.
Anedot may collect payment and legally required donor information under its own privacy terms.
The campaign may receive donor contact information, contribution details, and compliance records, but this website does not intentionally collect or store full payment-card numbers.
Our website and service providers may automatically receive technical information such as browser type, device type, pages viewed, referral source, approximate location, IP address, and cookie or similar identifiers.
We may use Google services and WordPress tools to understand site performance and campaign outreach.
- Send campaign news, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities after you provide consent.
- Coordinate volunteer activities and respond to questions.
- Measure website performance, improve accessibility, and understand which outreach channels are effective.
- Protect the website, prevent fraud or misuse, and maintain campaign records.
- Comply with election, tax, accounting, and other legal obligations.
We may share information with vendors that help operate the campaign, including Klaviyo for email subscriptions and volunteer records, Anedot for donation processing, Google services for analytics and site administration, and providers for website hosting, security, and technical support.
These providers receive information only as needed to perform services for the campaign.
We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, during a campaign reorganization, or with your direction.
Contribution information may be publicly disclosed when election law requires it.
Campaign email signups use double opt-in: you must confirm through the email Klaviyo sends before becoming subscribed.
Every marketing email should include an unsubscribe link.
A phone number submitted on the volunteer form is intended for personal volunteer coordination and does not by itself enroll you in automated campaign text messages.
Any future SMS program should use separate consent language.
You may ask to access, correct, or delete information the campaign maintains about you, subject to applicable legal recordkeeping requirements.
You may also ask questions about cookies or analytics.
Contact us using the information below.
Cookies and similar technologies may support security, site functionality, analytics, and campaign attribution.
You can adjust cookie settings in your browser.
Blocking some cookies may affect website features.
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, legal compliance, dispute resolution, and recordkeeping.
We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.
This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.
The site links to third-party services and social networks whose privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
We may update this policy as campaign practices or legal requirements change.
The “last updated” date will identify the current version.
