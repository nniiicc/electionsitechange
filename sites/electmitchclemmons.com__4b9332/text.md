Meet Mitch Clemmons Mitch is a plumber and small-business owner who understands the pressures facing working families.
He believes Congress needs more people who have built businesses, served customers, and fixed real-world problems.
His campaign is centered on practical solutions, local accountability, and clear communication with the people of California’s 41st District.
Priorities for District 41 Affordability and Housing Lower barriers that increase housing costs and expand practical paths to homeownership for working families.
Jobs and Small Business Reduce unnecessary red tape and support conditions where local employers can invest, hire, and grow.
Safe Communities Support law enforcement, first responders, secure borders, and policies focused on public safety.
Why Mitch Is Running Working families deserve a representative who understands what it takes to meet a payroll, solve practical problems, and be accountable for results.
Mitch is running to bring hands-on experience, responsible leadership, and a citizen-servant mindset to Congress.
