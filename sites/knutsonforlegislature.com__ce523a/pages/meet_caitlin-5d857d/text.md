Nebraska Born, Raised, and Educated
Caitlin was born in Lincoln, Nebraska.
She grew up on the east edge of Lincoln in district two with a view of cornfields outside her window.
She attended public schools, and graduated from Lincoln East High School.
She went on to attend Creighton University in Omaha and earned her BA in History.
She continued graduate school at Creighton and completed a MA in Education.
While student teaching, she realized her calling was to become a special education teacher.
Funding was available for a program called STARS, which allowed her to teach with a provisional special education certification while earning a second MA at the University of Nebraska at Omaha specializing in learning disabilities.
Service Oriented
Caitlin was raised with a service oriented ethos.
Work is not just a paycheck, but a calling.
She credits her Jesuit schooling with underpinning how she informs her decisions and moves through the world.
These values include cura personalis, people for and with others, contemplation in action, magis, unity of hearts and minds, and finding God in all things.
She has spent fifteen years teaching in public schools and schools attached to DHHS programs.
She started her career at Benson High School, and in 2014 she was awarded Benson Teacher of the Year.
Caitlin built relationships with students, held them accountable, and worked by their side encouraging them.
Union Member and Supporter
Caitlin has been a proud union member throughout her career, and served two terms on the Omaha Education Association as a high school representative.
Her husband, likewise, is a proud member of IAFF local 644.
When Caitlin was pregnant with her first child, her husband was in paramedic school, which meant her teacher paycheck was the main source of their livelihood.
She understands how difficult it is to stretch a monthly paycheck to cover basic expenses.
Urban and Rural
Like District 2, Caitlin has lived in urban and rural parts of the state.
When her husband completed paramedic school, he accepted a position with Hastings Fire and Rescue.
The family moved to a small farm house outside of Juniata, Nebraska.
They spent the next year building their savings to purchase a home in Hastings.
During their time they frequented the revitalized downtown, and were supporters of local businesses.
After Covid, Caitlin and her family moved back to Lincoln to be closer to her parents.
They reside in District 2, in the same neighborhood she grew up.
