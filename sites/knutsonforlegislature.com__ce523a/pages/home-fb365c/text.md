Why I'm Running
It’s time for Nebraska to go back to the basics.
I spent 15 years as a Special Education Teacher in our public schools and in the Nebraska Department of Health and Human Services.
I know firsthand the issues families, farmers, and our communities are facing.
As a mom of young children and the wife of a firefighter, I chose to run for office to represent the interests of hard-working people and to restore the belief that government is about service, not serving personal interests.
I will go to work for us.
