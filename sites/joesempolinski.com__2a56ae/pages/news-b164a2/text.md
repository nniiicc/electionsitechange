Former Congressman Joe Sempolinski Declares Candidacy For State Assembly
Former Congressman Joe Sempolinski has declared his candidacy for the State Assembly.
Sempolinski is a candidate for the 148th Assembly District currently represented by Joseph Giglio, who has announced that he will not be seeking another term.
Sempolinski currently serves as the Chief of Staff to Mr.
Giglio.
