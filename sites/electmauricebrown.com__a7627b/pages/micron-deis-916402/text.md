Submit Public Comments on the Micron DEIS
Central New York is on the brink of massive change with the proposed Micron project, and residents have a rare opportunity to directly influence that change.
The Draft Environmental Impact Statement (DEIS) outlines both the anticipated problems and the proposed solutions for managing Micron's development.
But the DEIS isn't final—and that's where you come in.
Public comment isn't just a formality.
It's a chance for everyday people to have their voices heard and officially logged into the public record.
These comments are reviewed, documented, and considered in the final environmental planning process.
A Metric or Focus to Explore
We're not going to dissect the technical details of the DEIS here.
Instead, this post is about participation.
Your comment—with your name and your town—matters.
Whether you're concerned about housing availability, traffic, job access, or environmental impacts, your perspective helps shape how these issues are prioritized and addressed.
The public comment portal is available athttps://ongoved.com/micron-draft-eis-2025-comments and will remain open until August 11th.
What the Data Suggests
Past large-scale government funded projects like the I-81 Viaduct Project have shown that community input changes outcomes.
Whether it's stronger mitigation efforts, clearer transportation solutions, or expanded housing commitments, real impact has come from public engagement.
When people don’t comment, agencies assume there is no concern.
But when communities speak up, agencies are obligated to take note.
The Core Message
You don’t have to be an expert to leave a comment.
You just need to be a member of our community.
Whether you rent in Outer Comstock, commute from the Madison County, or work in a trade, your daily life gives you a valid and important lens on how this development will affect Central New York.
Don’t miss the opportunity to be part of the record.
Submitting a comment is as easy as sending an email, takes 5 minutes and could influence decisions for the next 50 years.
What Comes Next
Visit https://ongoved.com/micron-draft-eis-2025-comments, then email comments to: CHIPSNEPA@chips.gov.
The full DEIS is also publicly available at - https://ongoved.com/microndeis2025/ - for anyone who wants to review it in detail.
But even if you don’t read all 1,000+ pages, you can still speak up.
Because big changes are coming to Central New York.
Make sure your voice is part of the conversation.
