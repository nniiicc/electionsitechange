Endorsements: Standing Together for Central New York
Endorsements are about more than politics.
They represent trust, collaboration, and a shared vision for Onondaga County.
I’m proud to be endorsed by local leaders who believe in our work, and I’m equally proud to support other candidates who are stepping up for our community this election season.
Who’s Endorsing Mo Brown?
