Frequently Asked Questions:
Has Maurice Brown officially announced his campaign for New York State Assembly?
Yes.
Maurice Brown announced his candidacy for New York State Assembly District 129 in February 2026.
His campaign focuses on affordability, housing stability, transportation access, and economic fairness for residents of Syracuse and Onondaga County.
What district is Maurice Brown running in?
Maurice Brown is running for New York State Assembly District 129, which includes the City of Syracuse and surrounding communities in Onondaga County.
What issues has Maurice Brown been featured discussing in the news?
Recent media coverage has highlighted Maurice Brown’s positions on affordable housing, tenant protections, public transportation, tax fairness, labor standards, and community investment across Central New York.
