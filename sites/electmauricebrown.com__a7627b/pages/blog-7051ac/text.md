- Governing During a Deficit Is Hard.
Mayor Owens Deserves Credit for Meeting the Moment.
But Syracuse also faces a challenge that makes its financial situation even more complicated.
Roughly half of the parcels in the city are tax exempt, which significantly limits the city’s ability to generate property tax revenue.
Major institutions like Syracuse University and large hospital systems such as SUNY Upstate Medical University and St.
Joseph's Health occupy valuable real estate while contributing little or no property tax revenue because of their tax exempt status.
These institutions are important anchors in our community, but it creates a difficult reality for city government when so much prime land is removed from the tax rolls while residents are still expected to carry the burden of funding essential services.
