Frequently Asked Questions:
Who can vote in this General election?
Registered voters who live in New York State Assembly District 129 are eligible to vote in the General Election.
When is the General election?
The Democratic primary election will take place in November 2026.
Election Day is November 3rd, 2026.
Can I vote by mail or absentee ballot?
Yes.
If you are unable to vote in person, you can request an absentee or early mail ballot through the New York State Board of Elections.
What if I’m not sure I’m registered?
You can check your voter registration status online through the New York State voter lookup tool.
