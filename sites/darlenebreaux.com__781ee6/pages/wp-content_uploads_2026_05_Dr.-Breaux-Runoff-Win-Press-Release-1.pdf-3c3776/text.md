Darlene Breaux Wins Democratic Runoff for Texas House District 149 Community-driven campaign unseats 20+ year incumbent Hubert Vo HARRIS COUNTY, TX (May 27, 2026) — Dr.
Darlene Breaux has won the Democratic runoff election for Texas House District 149, defeating longtime incumbent Hubert Vo and securing the Democratic nomination for the Texas House seat representing parts of Alief, Mission Bend, West Houston, and Katy.
A longtime educator, community advocate, and current president of the Alief ISD Board of Trustees, Dr.
Breaux built her campaign around being deeply present in the community and accessible to the people of District 149.
Throughout the campaign, she met directly with residents at community events, neighborhood meetings, block walks, churches, schools, and small businesses to hear firsthand about the challenges families are facing and the leadership they want to see in Austin.
“This victory is about a community that was ready for change and believed in me to help lead it,” said Dr.
Darlene Breaux.
“I’m incredibly grateful to every voter, volunteer, and supporter who stood with this campaign.
If elected in November, I will be a strong voice and advocate for District 149 in Austin.
Thank you to my team and to my family, my husband Vincent and children Paige, Haley, and Joshua for their constant support and sacrifice throughout this journey.” Dr.
Breaux also thanks Representative Hubert Vo for his decades of public service to District 149 and his contributions to the community over the years.
Her campaign focused on strengthening public safety, fully funding public education, supporting working families, increasing government responsiveness, and ensuring District 149 has a representative who is visible, engaged, and connected to the community year-round, not just during election season.
The runoff drew significant attention across Houston and Texas political circles, with many viewing the race as a new chapter for one of the state’s most diverse districts.
Powered by volunteers and grassroots outreach, the campaign mobilized supporters across the district through phone banking, voter education, social media, and neighborhood block walks focused on direct voter engagement.
Dr.
Breaux will now advance to the November general election for Texas House District 149.
