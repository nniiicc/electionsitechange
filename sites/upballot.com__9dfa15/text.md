Stephanie Hein is proud Southwest Missourian.
It's here where she learned the importance of hard work and has grown her commitment to this community.
She is a product of Missouri’s public education system, and grew up in a family of public educators, and started her professional career in the restaurant industry where she developed her business and financial skills.
Following in her family’s footsteps, Stephanie transitioned her career into higher education.
As an experienced educator, Stephanie advocated to provide students a high return on their education investment and served as a bridge between education and industry so that her students could have the best employment opportunities possible.
Stephanie is a team player and is known for achieving results on big goals.
Stephanie has the skills and common-sense approach needed to reach the big goals of providing economic security, educational opportunity, and healthcare access to all Missourians.
