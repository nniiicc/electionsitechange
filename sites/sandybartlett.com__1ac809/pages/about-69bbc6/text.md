Our youngest attends UMBC after Anne Arundel County Public Schools, our eldest recently welcomed our first grandchild.
Sharing the home is Maggie, our spirited Maltese mix who thinks she’s still the baby.
Service, Stewardship, and Community-Driven Leadership Together Since 2019, I’ve represented District 32 in the Maryland House of Delegates, amplifying neighbor voices from Laurel to Anne Arundel County.
My approach is simple: listen, collaborate, and deliver.
I’ve chaired outreach efforts, built forums, and advanced priorities that strengthen schools, expand healthcare access, protect our environment, and support effective, accountable public safety across Maryland.
Every step reflects community input and measurable results.
Roots in Service, Leadership, and Community Building In 2006, I was appointed to the Anne Arundel County Democratic Central Committee, later elected in 2010 and 2014.
I chaired several subcommittees, launched the Show Your Heart outreach supporting teachers and veterans, and spearheaded a June Community Forum serving western county residents.
Over thirty years, I’ve volunteered tirelessly to elect Democrats, register voters, and keep neighbors engaged in accessible, issues-focused local government.
Those experiences shaped my collaborative style and commitment to transparent, measurable outcomes for every community I represent.
Legal Career, Teaching, and Lifelong Civic Engagement My professional career is rooted in the law.
I earned my J.D. from the Catholic University of America, Columbus School of Law.
I’m admitted to the Maryland State Bar.
I’m active in local bar associations and mentor students, emphasizing ethics, entrepreneurship, and accessible pathways into Maryland’s innovation economy.
I was an Adjunct Professor at Anne Arundel Community College, where I taught various subjects in business law and paralegal studies.
Service, Sisterhood, and Civic Commitment Alpha Kappa Alpha member, NAACP Life Member, Bay CSS board participant, and Waxter Detention Center speaker supporting outreach, mentorship, and community empowerment.
