Delegate Sandy Bartlett advances Laurel MD public safety with prevention, swift accountability, and support for victims.
District 32 community safety efforts focus on data-driven strategies that reduce harm and build trust.
Anne Arundel County safety priorities include youth opportunity, mental health response, traffic safety, and targeted enforcement against violent crime.
We expand reentry supports, domestic violence services, and overdose prevention, while improving training, technology, and transparency.
Collaboration with schools, nonprofits, and local businesses keeps neighborhoods resilient, lowers repeat offenses, and restores confidence in public institutions.
What safety requires now We prevent crime by tackling root causes and strengthening law enforcement capacity.
Credible partnerships, fair processes, and intervention matter.
We invest in proven programs that reduce violence, improve response, and support victims with dignity.  Violence Prevention Focused deterrence, youth mentors, and conflict mediation reduce shootings and build trust across neighborhoods.  Smart Enforcement Data-informed policing prioritizes violent offenders, illegal guns, and hotspots while preserving civil rights and accountability.  Crisis Response Embed behavioral health teams and co-responder models to de-escalate, stabilize, and connect people to care.  Victim Services Expand compensation, housing support, legal aid, and counseling so survivors rebuild safety, stability, and dignity.  Traffic Safety Safer street designs, enforcement, and education prevent crashes for pedestrians, cyclists, and drivers across District 32.
Accountability and second chances Accountability must be firm, fair, and fast, with paths away from violence.
Reentry, housing, and employment support reduce recidivism.
We coordinate with courts and community partners so consequences change behavior and opportunities enable success.  Gun Safety Background checks, safe storage education, and trafficking enforcement keep illegal firearms from our neighborhoods.  Reentry Support Transitional housing, credentialing, mentoring, and job placement reduce recidivism and strengthen families reuniting after incarceration.  Domestic Violence Expand shelters, legal representation, and trauma-informed services; enforce protective orders and coordinate lethality assessment programs.  Youth Opportunity After-school programs, summer jobs, apprenticeships, and tutoring provide safe spaces and real pathways to success.  Community Trust Training, body-worn cameras, civilian oversight, and transparent reporting build legitimacy and sustain collaborative problem-solving.
