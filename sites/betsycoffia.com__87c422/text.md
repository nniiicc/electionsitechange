A Better Future Starts With Community
Together we have the power to elect leaders who will put people and community first, not private profit.
"I have the audacity to believe that peoples everywhere can have three meals a day for their bodies, education and culture for their minds, and dignity, equality and freedom for their spirits.
I believe that what self-centered men have torn down, men other-centered can build up.
I still believe that one day mankind will bow before the altars of God and be crowned triumphant over war and bloodshed, and nonviolent redemptive good will proclaim rule of the land."

Dear Neighbors,
The chaos our nation has endured for the past two years has me thinking a lot about what it means to serve my community under increasingly difficult conditions.
I worry about my neighbors especially this year as my two legislative staff and I surpassed 100,000 constituent calls and e-mails and I increasingly see how many folks in our region are quietly suffering as they lose healthcare, are pushed out of housing because of unaffordable rent, are unable to access proper mental health care, among other growing crises.
And corporate greed paired with federal chaos is making it all infinitely worse.
When it feels overwhelming, I get grounded and clear when I think about the kind of future I want for my community, myself and most of all for my 9-year-old niece to have here in northern Michigan.
And here is where I stand.
We must not fail to stand up for human rights, land and water, and civil liberties for all of our neighbors, because when we do, we leave our own at risk.
We also become lesser as humans and a lesser community.
The rights of women as well as of our immigrant, Native, queer, disabled, Black and brown neighbors, are being stripped away at an alarming pace under this regime.
We can and must do so much better as we look to rebuild what this regime has destroyed.
Since we must rebuild, let’s do it right and leave no one behind.
Let’s ensure civil liberties, protect our clean air and water.
And as a child of the working poor, I know this.
In the richest country on the planet there's more than enough for everyone to have the things we need to survive.
I have never yet come across a truly moral or decent argument as to why any of our neighbors should be hungry, go without a proper roof over their head, suffer illness without medical care or live without clean air or clean water.
These are not privileges or luxuries to be earned, they are human rights.
This is why I’m in public service and this is what I will keep fighting for.
I understand and we all must, that right now our country is in grave jeopardy, our children's future is being lit on fire in front of us, and 100 years of progress is being rolled back by a small group of disturbed, truly sociopathic people whose greed can never be satisfied.
Sociopaths who are trying to tell us that robots are more valuable than us, who are trying to suck the life, the creativity, the intellect, the joy, the humanness, right out of us and then package it to us and sell it to us for a subscription that will mysteriously keep increasing in price.
The cure for that is to become as human as possible.
It’s time to reject the future they are trying to force on us.
Our creative imagination is so much better than their dark, small, cruel fever dreams.
We are more than capable of leaning into everything that makes humans amazing - courage, compassion, generosity, creativity, kindness and more.
Let’s unsubscribe from their future and work together to build the one we want.
One that leaves none of our neighbors behind.
I’m all in.
Join me.
With love and solidarity,
People-focused Policy
For too long, Michigan's government has been dominated by corporate money and private interest at the expense of our communities.
It's time to change that.
Money out of Politics
Get corporate influence out of Lansing
Corporations have far too much influence over too many who are elected to represent us.
This influence gained with nearly unlimited amounts of money is, essentially, legalized corruption.
It needs to end.
Housing and Infrastructure
Repurpose Tax revenue for people and communities
Change state law to expand the allowed uses of revenues collected by hotel bed taxes and implement short-term rental taxes to increase revenue for housing and infrastructure development.
Rein in Hyperscale Data Centers
Michigan is not for sale
I support a multi-year moratorium on hyperscale data center construction in Michigan so we can adequately assess the potential long-term environmental and health impacts on our communities.
Transparency and Ethics
Make our politicians accountable again
Increase disclosure requirements for lobbyists, require state level public officials to submit financial records on an annual basis, reform FOIA laws so governor and legislator communications are public.
Tribal Sovereignty
Upholding Treaties, Collaborative Policy
Michigan has been the ancestral and current home of the Anishinaabe peoples for millennia and the sovereign tribal nations including Grand Traverse Band of Ottawa and Chippewa Indians, are crucial partners in caring for our air, land and water for future generations.
We need to center tribal relationships in a respectful and collaborative way for the benefit of all Michiganders, our Great Lakes, our environment and the future.
Healthcare For All
A human right not a luxury
Provide the medical care we would want for ourselves and our own families, including dental, vision and mental health care as a human right, regardless of a person's wealth or status in society.
It is a moral imperative to ensure none of our neighbors suffers without medical care they need.
