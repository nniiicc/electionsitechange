To the Governor of the State of Michigan, the House Speaker and the Senate Majority Leader, We the undersigned Michigan voters, taxpayers, community leaders, civic organizations, urge you in the strongest of terms to make the Take MI Power Back anti corruption legislation your top priority to take up for votes, passage, and signing into law in the First 100 Days of the 2027-28 legislative term.
The Take MI Power Back legislation includes Rep.
Betsy Coffia and Sen.
Sean McCann’s End Run Citizens United bills HB 6216-6218 and SB 1085-87.
These bills are modeled after the Montana Plan ballot initiative of 2026, and Hawaii law Public Act 11 of 2026, allowing states to redeﬁne corporations’ powers to remove spending into our politics.
These measures offer a much-needed solution to the corrosion the 2010 Supreme Court Citizens United decision has wreaked on our democratic process.
They also continue the important trajectory of Michiganders for Money out of Politics (MMOP) ballot initiative of 2026 which prevents regulated utilities and corporations that receive over $250,000 per year in state and local government contracts, from making political contributions within the contracts’ jurisdiction.
The Take MI Power Back legislation also includes the BRITE Act led by former House Ethics Chair Rep.
Erin Byrnes (HB 4268-73), introduced in both 2023-24 session and 2025-26 session, curbing lobbyist inﬂuence, forcing disclosure for politicians’ dark money PACs, and providing more transparency and accountability to multiple facets of state government.
Finally, the Take MI Power Back Anti Corruption legislation also includes passage of Freedom of Information Act laws to apply to the Governor and legislature.
Shockingly, Michigan is one of only two states in the nation not to apply this transparency to the people directly responsible for passing laws 10 million Michiganders must live with and spending over $80 Billion on our tax dollars per year with passage of the state budget.
For far too long, Michigan has lagged at or near rock bottom of all 50 states for transparency, ethics and ﬁnancial disclosure according to the nonpartisan Center for Public Integrity.
Former and current lawmakers have openly shared that it is common practice for big corporate interests to quietly kill vital policy proposals they don’t like.
Policies that Michiganders need, such as repairing our broken mental health system by requiring insurance companies to properly pay for mental health care, passing bills that would require polluters to pay for their own cleanup rather than foist it on Michigan taxpayers, housing policy that reins in hedge funds and no longer prioritizes corporate proﬁt over affordable shelter, bills that would rein in utility companies continuous rate increases, and more.
Year after year, lip service is repeatedly paid by both sides of the political aisle for many of these reforms, yet behind the scenes they’ve been stymied repeatedly.
This despite the fact Michigan has seen repeated scandals including former Speaker of the House Rick Johnson going to prison for corruption and former Speaker of the House Lee Chatﬁeld being investigated for corruption.
We the people of Michigan, ﬁnd this status quo, which favors the corporate lobby corps and their clients’ priorities over the needs of we the people, entirely unacceptable.
We expect and demand sweeping and comprehensive action within the ﬁrst 100 Days of the 2027-28 session by both the Senate Majority Leader and House Speaker as well as the Governor to ﬁnally get this long overdue business for the people done.
There is a saying “We can afford bad leadership in good times.
We cannot afford bad leadership in bad times.” Michigan and our nation are facing increasingly bad times for everyday people due to the corruption and chaos of the Trump regime and corporate interests that are decimating our economy, healthcare system, housing, job market, environment and more.
Passing the Take MI Power Back agenda pairs with MMOP to clear the deck and refocus our leaders in Lansing where they need to be laser focused - on the needs of We the People.
In a time of increasing instability, struggle and uncertainty for Michiganders and Americans due to the failures and corruption of the Trump regime, it is time for our state legislative leaders and Governor to bring their very best, most transparent, ethical and people focused leadership to rebuilding and bringing relief to Michiganders struggling to afford life in our Great Lakes State.
We expect nothing less and we will be paying close attention.
Sincerely,
