In Michigan, corporations have too much influence Too many who are elected to represent us are influenced with nearly unlimited amounts of money which is, essentially, legalized corruption.
In my three and a half years in the state legislature, I have watched big money interests block critical reforms for vital healthcare access, mental health care, environmental protections, and rising energy costs.
No committee hearings, no floor votes, no change because of the outsized influence of corporate money.
We know monied interests won’t go down without a fight.
But that’s where we come in.
Michiganders for Money out of Politics is a very important step to refocus Michigan politics on We the People rather than big money interests.
And, I am sponsoring several concurrent bills to ensure corporate power in our government is erased in totality, and now we need your help to send a message to the next legislative majority that we expect and DEMAND these long overdue measures be a Day One, Week One priority.
The End Run Citizens United (House bills) 6216-18 allow states to redefine corporations to prevent them from giving to politicians.
The Bringing Reforms in Integrity, Transparency and Ethics (B.R.I.T.E) act will increase disclosure requirements for lobbyists and require state level public officials to submit financial records on an annual basis.
Finally, Michigan is one of two states that doesn’t allow governor or legislator communications to be FOIA’d.
We need to change that with FOIA reforms.
Why does all this matter?
Because if We the People want the bold reforms on everything from healthcare for all to holding corporations accountable, we need a fair playing field.
Right now, we don’t have one.
Let’s change that together.
