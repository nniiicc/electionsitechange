Crafting & Skill Sharing.
Learn A New Skill, Teach A New Skill
Various Dates, Various Locations
Join State Rep.
Betsy Coffia to create, meet your neighbors, and learn a new skill!
This is a free series of events for all ages where you're invited to create any kind of tech-free art or creative endeavor.
Whether you want to draw, paint, make fiber arts, build with Legos, needlepoint, or anything else you can imagine, we'd love to share the experience with you!
We will have some art materials available like markers, a children's coloring book, and construction paper.
For any other projects or creative needs, please bring your own supplies.
We are also using this opportunity to share skills with other attendees, so if you would like to give a demonstration of what you're working on, please bring enough materials for 5-20 people and let us know if you're interested when you RSVP!
End Of Summer Movie Night
Popcorn.
Games.
Relaxation
Movie Night
Join us for a free screening of ‘Inside Out’ on the big screen in the park!
Come early for free popcorn, yard games and face painting starting at 7:00pm.
'Inside Out' will begin at 8:30pm when it's dark enough to begin the movie.
Your RSVP is greatly appreciated so we can ensure we have enough popcorn and space for everyone.
We are hosting a school supplies drive, so you are welcome to bring backpacks, tissue boxes, paper towel, school art supplies, pencils, pens, staples, staples, or other basic school/ hygiene needs.
For families in need, you are welcome to take supplies at the event before they're donated, no questions asked.
Keep an eye on this page for potential weather updates the day of the event.
We expect it will get chilly as it gets dark, so bring blankets and warm clothes!
