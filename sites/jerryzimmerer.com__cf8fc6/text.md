The 15th Court of Appeals
The Legislature created the 15th Court in 2023; it opened for business on September 1, 2024.
Every one of its seats was filled by gubernatorial appointment.
November 3, 2026 is the first time Texas voters get any say over who sits on it.
Exclusive appellate jurisdiction over constitutional challenges to Texas statutes.
Civil cases brought by or against the State and its executive-branch agencies, including public universities.
Appeals in suits against state officers over their official conduct.
Appeals from the new Texas Business Courts — the state's largest commercial disputes.
On the trail
Working Texans deal with state agencies, licensing boards, and public employers — and those disputes run straight through this court's docket.
