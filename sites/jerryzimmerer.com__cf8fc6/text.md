2026 Candidate for Chief Justice, 15th Court of Appeals On the statewide ballot November 3 The 15th Court of Appeals A brand-new court, on the ballot for the first time The Legislature created the 15th Court in 2023; it opened for business on September 1, 2024.
Every one of its seats was filled by gubernatorial appointment.
November 3, 2026 is the first time Texas voters get any say over who sits on it.
State law challenges Exclusive appellate jurisdiction over constitutional challenges to Texas statutes.
The State as a party Civil cases brought by or against the State and its executive-branch agencies, including public universities.
State officials Appeals in suits against state officers over their official conduct.
Business Courts Appeals from the new Texas Business Courts — the state's largest commercial disputes.
