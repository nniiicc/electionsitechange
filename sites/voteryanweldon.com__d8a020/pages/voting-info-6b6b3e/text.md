How To Vote Early You never know what's going to come up on Tuesday November 3rd to distract you from voting or simply make it difficult to make time for it.
A great option to make sure that doesn't happen, is to vote early.
There are a few options available to do that before November 3rd.
Starting October 14th - Monday November 2nd, you can go to the Polk County Elections office Monday - Friday between the hours of 8am and 4:30pm to cast your ballot.
The address is 2309 Euclid Avenue, Des Moines. ​ ​ ​ More information about voting early ca be found at VoterReady.Iowa.Gov.
General Voting Information 2026 General Election Information Voter Registration Information Find Your Precinct & Polling Place Absentee Ballots & Early Voting If you will be out of town on November 3rd and unable to vote in-person on election day, you can still cast your ballot for Ryan Weldon via absentee ballot.
To request an absentee ballot, use this absentee ballot request form and return it to the Polk County Election Office at 120 2nd Avenue, Suite A, Des Moines, IA 50309.
You can mail your ballot or hand deliver your ballot during business hours, but it must be received by 8PM CT on Election Day.
Additional general absentee voter information can be found at the Iowa Secretary of State website.
