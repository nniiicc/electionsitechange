Sandy attended Hamilton Junior High School and Parkersburg High School, then enrolled at Marshall University in the fall of 1976.

Taking the maximum number of courses permitted, Sandy graduated a semester early so he could attend the Federal Law Enforcement Training Center in spring 1980.
