Residents who wish to report damage to property or businesses as a result of recent heavy rainfall and flooding may do so by visiting donaana.gov/DamageAssessment .
Residents should fill the required information with as much detail as possible.
The portal will allow the County to thoroughly document the impact of the recent heavy rainfall that occurred through the County causing widespread flooding and damages to homes, vehicles and property.
For more information residents can call 211, Monday through Thursday from 8:30 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Friday from 8:30 a.m. to noon.
Additional information will be shared as soon as it becomes available on the county's official social media channels.
