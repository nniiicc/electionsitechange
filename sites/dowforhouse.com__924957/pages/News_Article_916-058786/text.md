Rebecca Dow hosting economic development forum on Saturday, Nov.
4 By housereplogin Truth or Consequences, NM – Rep.
Rebecca Dow (R-Truth or Consequences) will host an Economic Development Forum for residents in House District 38.
The forum will take place at the Albert Lyons Event Center in Truth or Consequences on Saturday, November 4, at 10 A.M.
Officials from the New Mexico Economic Development Department and the New Mexico Tax and Revenue Department will be available at the event to discuss the resources available to businesses and communities interested in expanding opportunities and promoting economic growth.
They will also answer questions residents may have about state incentive programs, small business assistance, and state taxes.
“Small businesses are the key to creating more job opportunities for New Mexicans, especially in our rural communities,” Dow said.
“This forum is a chance to meet with representatives from state agencies at one place and time.
I hope District 38 residents will take advantage of this opportunity to gain valuable information on how to start or grow a business in New Mexico.” The Albert Lyons Event Center is located at 2953 South Broadway, next to the Sierra County Fairgrounds.
For more information on this economic development forum, please contact Ryan Hedin at (505) 986-4792.
