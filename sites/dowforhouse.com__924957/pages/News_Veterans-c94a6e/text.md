The Organ Mountain Veteran Coffee Group
The Organ Mountain Veteran Coffee Group (OMVCG) is an exceptional example of veteran service and dedication.
This 501 c 3 organization is made up of veterans who have come together with a shared commitment to serving their community in Organ, New Mexico.
These veterans, who have already sacrificed so much for their country, are now giving back in a meaningful way by completing small service projects for residents in the area.
