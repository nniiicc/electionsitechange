As your next State Senator, my pledge to you is that I will:
- ALWAYS put Nevada families first, and provide a quality education for every child.
- Support the ENTIRE U.S.
Constitution.
- Champion Voter ID laws to ensure the security of our elections with a potential Election Day holiday.
- Stop un-ending Governor-imposed “emergency” mandates by introducing a bill requiring legislative approval with a 2/3 majority of both the Assembly and Senate for any mandates that the Governor wants to enact or extend for more than 30 consecutive days.
- Support Law Enforcement to keep our neighborhoods safe.
- Support adequate and affordable housing, medical care and mental health care for all Nevadans.
- Strive to find ways to conserve water and other natural resources, as well as to protect the environment.
Other important issues include:
- Reducing or repealing the Commerce Tax.
- Supporting our Veterans, the Trades and jobs for a stronger economy.
- Pushing for a livable minimum wage.
- Working to build a more robust and diverse economy for Nevada.
- Introducing legislation to stop the seemingly limitless powers of the Bureau of Land Management in Nevada.
- Proposing an end to Daylight Savings Time here in Nevada.
