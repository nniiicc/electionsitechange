On Saturday, May 16, 2026, the 13th Congressional District Democratic Organization of the Michigan Democratic Party voted to endorse Willie Burton in the Democratic primary for Michigan’s 9th House District — a significant boost for Burton’s campaign and a strong sign of grassroots support within Detroit Democratic politics.
The endorsement recognizes Burton’s long record of public service and community involvement throughout Detroit.
A three-term Detroit Police Commissioner, longtime neighborhood advocate, and civic leader, Burton has earned a reputation as an independent and outspoken voice on issues involving public safety, accountability, economic opportunity, and neighborhood development.
Supporters at the May 16th meeting praised Burton’s decades of involvement in Detroit communities and his willingness to engage directly with residents on difficult issues.
Many viewed the endorsement as support for a candidate with both deep local roots and extensive public policy experience.
Burton’s campaign for the open 9th District seat has continued to gain momentum as voters look for experienced leadership that understands the needs of Detroit neighborhoods.
Supporters say his background in public service and community advocacy gives him a practical perspective that will benefit residents in Lansing.
The endorsement by the 13th Congressional District Democratic Organization is expected to strengthen Burton’s standing heading into the Democratic primary and further energize supporters across the district.
