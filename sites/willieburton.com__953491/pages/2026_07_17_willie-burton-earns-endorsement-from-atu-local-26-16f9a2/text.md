Skip to content Home Contribute Contact Us Meet Willie Burton Updates & Endorsements Helpful Contacts ← 13th District Democrats Endorse Willie Burton for State Representative Willie Burton Earns Endorsement from Teamsters Local 299 → Willie Burton Earns Endorsement from ATU Local 26 Posted on July 17, 2026 by scottyboman Willie Burton Earns Endorsement from ATU Local 26 The Willie Burton for State Representative campaign is proud to announce a major endorsement from Amalgamated Transit Union (ATU) Local 26, representing dedicated transit professionals who keep Southeast Michigan moving every day.
This endorsement reflects confidence in Burton’s longstanding commitment to working families, safe communities, and practical leadership.
Throughout his years of public service as a three-term Detroit Police Commissioner and community advocate, Burton has earned a reputation for listening, building consensus, and standing up for the people he serves.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of ATU Local 26,” Burton said.
“Transit workers provide an essential public service, and I will continue fighting for policies that strengthen our workforce, improve infrastructure, and create opportunities for Michigan families.” ATU Local 26 joins a growing coalition of organizations and community leaders supporting Burton’s campaign for Michigan’s 9 th House District.
With momentum continuing to build, the campaign remains focused on creating jobs, improving neighborhoods, investing in infrastructure, and ensuring every resident has a strong voice in Lansing.
Election Day is Tuesday, August 4.
The Burton campaign encourages all eligible voters to make their voices heard at the polls.
