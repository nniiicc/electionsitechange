Willie Burton Earns Endorsement from Teamsters Local 299
The Willie Burton for State Representative campaign is excited to announce the endorsement of Teamsters Local 299, one of Michigan’s most respected labor organizations representing thousands of hardworking men and women across the region.
This endorsement highlights Burton’s commitment to standing with working families and supporting policies that strengthen good-paying jobs, safe workplaces, and economic opportunity.
As a three-term Detroit Police Commissioner and longtime community leader, Burton has consistently demonstrated a dedication to public service, accountability, and improving the quality of life for Michigan residents.
“I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of Teamsters Local 299,” Burton said.
“Our working men and women are the backbone of Michigan’s economy, and I look forward to fighting for their interests in Lansing.”
Teamsters Local 299 joins a growing list of organizations and community leaders supporting Burton’s campaign for Michigan’s 9th House District.
With strong momentum heading into the election, the campaign remains focused on creating jobs, investing in neighborhoods, improving infrastructure, and ensuring every family has the opportunity to succeed.
Voters in the 9th District are encouraged to cast their ballots on Tuesday, August 4, and join the movement to bring experienced, community-focused leadership to Lansing.
