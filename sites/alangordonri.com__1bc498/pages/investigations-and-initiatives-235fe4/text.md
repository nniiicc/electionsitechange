How Alan would evaluate each proposal Each proposal must answer three questions.
The subjects are different, but the test is the same.
What does the evidence show?
Identify the problem, publish the sources, test the claim, and revise the proposal when the facts require it.
Would it actually help?
Judge the proposal from the resident’s point of view, including its cost, accessibility, and practical results.
Would it be applied equally?
No favored party, institution, industry, or class.
The same legal and ethical standard should apply to everyone.
