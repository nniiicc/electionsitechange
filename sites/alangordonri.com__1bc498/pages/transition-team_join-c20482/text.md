Express your interest → Explore the roles Two leadership positions Civil and criminal legal leadership Civil matters Civil Legal Lead Help develop plans for civil litigation, constitutional and appellate matters, and legal representation of state agencies.
The intended administrative role would include overseeing civil legal operations and exercising lawfully delegated responsibilities.
Criminal matters Criminal Legal Lead Help develop plans for criminal prosecution, prosecutorial integrity, public-corruption enforcement, and coordination with investigative agencies.
The intended administrative role would include overseeing criminal legal operations, with attention to due process, disclosure obligations, and fair enforcement of the law.
Preparing the administration Both attorneys will work with Gordon to: Review the constitutional, statutory, and ethical requirements governing the office.
Develop a lawful allocation of executive and legal responsibilities.
Prepare proposed appointments, administrative orders, and operating procedures.
Plan for uninterrupted civil and criminal legal services when the administration takes office.
Under Gordon’s proposed structure, he would provide executive leadership over administration, budgeting, resource allocation, and policy priorities.
Duly authorized attorneys would handle legal representation and exercise the professional judgment required of licensed counsel.
Experience and qualifications Candidates must be eligible for admission to the Rhode Island Bar and demonstrate substantial relevant legal experience, sound professional judgment, and the ability to lead legal teams.
Attorneys already admitted in Rhode Island are welcome to express interest.
Experienced attorneys practicing outside Rhode Island are encouraged to express interest.
Candidates should identify their current admissions and the basis for their eligibility for Rhode Island admission.
Any required admission or authorization must be obtained before undertaking duties that require it.
Candidates should demonstrate a commitment to constitutional government, impartial justice, professional ethics, and public service.
