MINORITY CONSTITUTIONAL ADVOCATE Proposed three-year pilot inside the Rhode Island Attorney General's Office One Attorney General.
One independent minority voice.
No veto.
THE IDEA WHY CREATE IT?
The Attorney General keeps the office's prosecutorial and litigation authority.
The Advocate adds an independent channel for dissent, complaint review and factual investigation so major decisions can be tested before and after they are made.
Rhode Island law places the Attorney General at the head of the department and gives the office major litigation and prosecutorial responsibilities.
The pilot would create an internal counter-view without creating a second Attorney General.
WHAT THE OFFICE WOULD DO GUARDRAILS Attend major policy and litigation-policy meetings and receive the materials needed for meaningful review.
No power to initiate or dismiss prosecutions, enter a nolle prosequi, direct prosecutors, or bind the State to a settlement.
Issue Minority Reports when the Advocate disagrees.
The report accompanies the decision and becomes part of the permanent official record.
No veto over the Attorney General and no authority to command AG staff.
Receive complaints from people who believe they were treated improperly by the Attorney General's Office and provide written dispositions.
Conduct prosecutorial-integrity reviews, including credible allegations involving Brady/Giglio disclosure, discovery failures, false evidence or other misconduct.
When appropriate, submit factual investigative reports to the court; the court decides whether a violation occurred and what remedy is lawful.
Through retained counsel, file amicus briefs or seek intervention in important constitutional and public-law matters where a materially different nonfrivolous position exists.
Publish systemic findings and an annual Taxpayer Savings Ledger separating verified savings from estimates.
Court filings and appearances use retained counsel and remain subject to court rules, privileges and protective orders.
Qualifications for the Advocate are the same as for Attorney General.
Complaint and investigative files are segregated from ordinary AG access to preserve operational independence.
PILOT FUNDING ~$950,000 / year Proposed source: existing Attorney General operating resources, to the extent legally permissible within appropriation and funding restrictions.
FY2027 budget math: $53.372M total - $2.675M capital = $50.697M operating; $950K / $50.697M = 1.87%.
