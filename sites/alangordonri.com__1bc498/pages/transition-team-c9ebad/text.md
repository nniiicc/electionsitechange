The transition team will help develop the leadership structure, operating procedures, and continuity plans for an administration committed to constitutional rights, impartial justice, and public accountability.
Gordon’s proposed approach combines executive leadership over administration, budgeting, resource allocation, and policy priorities with legal representation and professional legal judgment entrusted to duly authorized attorneys.
Civil and criminal legal leadership Experienced attorneys are invited to express interest in two leadership positions: Civil Legal Lead and Criminal Legal Lead, with intended senior legal leadership responsibilities in the administration.
Candidates must be admitted or eligible for admission to the Rhode Island Bar.
Experienced attorneys practicing outside Rhode Island are encouraged to express interest.
