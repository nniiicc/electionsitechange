LEGISLATIVE PRIORITIES
EDUCATION
Building equity and codifying the responsibility of Michigan’s public education system to put students first with the focus on the success of ALL students.
HEALTHCARE
Working towards closing the gap in healthcare disparities to ensure everyone has access to quality healthcare regardless of income, race, age, or social status.
Bring transparency to prescription drug pricing so that people do not have to decide on whether to buy food for their table or buy their medications.
TRANSPORTATION/INFRASTRUCTURE
Working to repair and modernize Detroit’s streets, sidewalks, and public transit to improve safety, accessibility, and connectivity between neighborhoods.
Prioritize projects that reduce flooding, improve lighting, and expand reliable transit options so every Detroiter can travel to work, school, and community spaces with ease.
HOUSING
Supporting policies that expand access to safe, affordable housing in every Detroit neighborhood.
Champion initiatives that prevent displacement, reduce homelessness, and promote a fair and equitable housing ecosystem.
