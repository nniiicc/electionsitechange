2025-2026 Legislative Highlights
Parental Rights
- The Parental Rights Act affirms that parents have the fundamental right to direct the upbringing, education, and healthcare of their children.
School Privacy
- Legislation focused on student privacy keeps boys out of girls’ restrooms and locker rooms and ensures parents stay informed and involved in decisions affecting their children.
Online Safety
- The South Carolina Social Media Regulation Act gives parents more control over minors’ social media use and requires stronger safeguards from platforms.
Public Safety
- Legislation addressing harmful substances, abortion-inducing drugs, and other threats to children and families helps protect South Carolina communities.
Economic Growth
- Tax relief for small businesses, reforms to reduce red tape, and other pro-growth policies make South Carolina more affordable, competitive, and welcoming to job creators.
Government Accountability
- Judicial selection reform, public school grading transparency, workforce readiness legislation, and SCDOT modernization all help improve accountability and strengthen state institutions.
Healthcare
- H.3996 modernizes healthcare licensing rules for anesthesiologist assistants and helps improve flexibility in healthcare delivery.
