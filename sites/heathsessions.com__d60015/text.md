ABOUT HEATH
Heath is a husband, father, and businessman who is proud to call Rock Hill home.
He and his wife are raising their three children, Emma, Ben, and Asher, and are actively involved in their community.
Heath is a member of Oakland Baptist Church where he has served in various leadership postions.
He is also a graduate of the University of South Carolina and currently serves as Vice President of a local land clearing company.
In the State House, Heath serves on the Medical Subcommittee of the Military, Medical, and Municipal Affairs Committee.
