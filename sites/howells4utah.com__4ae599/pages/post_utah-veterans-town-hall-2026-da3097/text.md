On October 14, eight veterans running for office sat down at one table in Sandy— three Republicans and five Democrats, from county races all the way up to Congress— and spent two hours answering to the veterans we're asking to serve.
It was the first time in Utah that veterans running for office have shared a stage like that across party lines.

Before you go: vote However you vote, make sure your ballot counts.
Ballots have been mailed to active registered voters.
If yours hasn't shown up, call your county clerk.
Not registered yet?
The deadline is Friday, October 23, at 5 PM.
After that, you can still register and vote in person on Election Day, as long as you bring two forms of ID: one that shows who you are and one that shows where you live.
Early voting in Salt Lake County runs October 20 through October 31 at the County Government Center, 2001 S.
State Street.
More locations, including West Jordan City Hall and Draper City Hall, open October 27 through 30 from 3 to 7 PM.
Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.
Your ballot has to reach your county clerk by 8 PM on Election Day.
A postmark doesn't count anymore.
Drop it in an official ballot drop box before the deadline, and don't wait for the last day.
Check your registration, find a drop box, or track your ballot at vote.utah.gov .
