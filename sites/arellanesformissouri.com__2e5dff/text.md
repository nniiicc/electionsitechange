Time for a New Generation in Missouri
Miguel Arellanes is an anti-corruption Candidate, Democratic Progressive, and U.S.
Army Engineer Veteran running for State Representative to bring real representation, accountability, and integrity to Jefferson City.
Growing up on a farm in Oklahoma and then serving in the Army as an Engineer, Miguel understands the importance of protecting our agricultural industry and improving veterans' benefits.
Miguel is running because he believes government has become disconnected from the people it is supposed to serve.
He believes Missouri needs representatives who are willing to challenge a corrupt system and work for the people of Missouri.
His campaign is built on a simple idea that government belongs to the people, and when the people vote, the government should listen.
Elected officials should be accountable to their community and not working back door deals that hurt our people.
Miguel is committed to fighting corruption, demanding accountability from government, and making sure his community has a strong voice in the decisions that affect their lives.
Miguel believes it is time for a new generation of representation in Missouri, one willing to confront corruption and return government to its most basic purpose of serving the people.
Miguel believes Missouri’s government should not be trying to consolidate power in Jefferson city and should not hand power to massive corporations at the expense of local communities.
He will stand up to corporate monopolies that crush local businesses, and concentrate too much economic power in too few hands.
He also believes Missouri should fight back the every growing presence of flock cameras and against data center development and its impact on communities, including energy use, water consumption, infrastructure, utility costs, and the long term interests of Missouri residents.
Miguel served five years in the United States Army with the 74th and 50th Multirole Bridge Companies at Fort Leonard Wood, Missouri, and Fort Hood, Texas, rising to the rank of Sergeant.
During his service, he led soldiers, managed millions of dollars in critical equipment, and served as a Human Resources Coordinator responsible for many administrative and regulation focused duties.
His military service earned him two Army Achievement Medals along with other military honors and strengthened his commitment to accountability, responsibility, and service to others.
Before his military service, Miguel worked in the private sector as an automotive salesperson and restaurant manager, gaining firsthand experience with budgeting, customer service, teamwork, and the challenges faced by working people.
Government should be about working for the people, holding other government officials accountable, responsible spending, protecting local communities, and making sure the voices of our community are heard.
Miguel is running to fight corruption, challenge the political machine, stand up to corporate monopolies while pushing back against unchecked data center expansion, expand opportunity, and help rebuild a government that answers to the people it represents.
It’s time to return government to the people.
We NEED your help to change the direction of politics in Missouri!
