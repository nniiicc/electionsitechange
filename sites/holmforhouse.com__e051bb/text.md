Steve Holm for Minnesota
House District 20B
I grew up on a small farm outside St.
Charles.
I learned early that when something breaks, you don't wait around for someone else to fix it — you figure it out yourself, with whatever you've got.
That's the mindset I carried through 41 years at Mayo Clinic, seven years on the Saratoga Township Board, and every volunteer hour I've put into this community.
And it's the mindset I'll carry to St.
Paul.
District 20B is my home.
You are my neighbors, my friends, my co-workers.
I've worked beside you, waved at you on the highway, and sat across the table from you at township meetings.
I've seen your hard work and your resilience.
I've also seen your struggles — the farm families piecing together second and third incomes just to stay afloat, the folks driving long distances for basic medical care, the quiet worry about what we're leaving behind for the next generation.
You deserve a representative who understands those struggles because they've lived them too.
Someone who will take your concerns seriously, fight for practical solutions, and never lose sight of the people back home.
That's what this campaign is about.
Not party politics.
Not talking points.
Just a neighbor asking for the chance to serve — and a promise to bring it home.
