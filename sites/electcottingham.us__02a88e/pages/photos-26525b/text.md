Filing Day at IL State Board of Elections
(5/26/2026)
On May 26th, 2026, Andrew Cottingham submitted his candidate papers with the Illinois State Board of Elections to officially file his candidacy.
After filing his paperwork, Andrew attended an independent luncheon amongst his fellow supporters, volunteers, and independent candidates to celebrate.
Since filing, Andrew has been challenged by both the Democratic Party and Republican Party of Illinois, who want to see him kept off of the ballot.
“Regardless of what happens next, I am beyond thankful for the experience, and for everyone who volunteered, donated, or signed a petition.
I have not ruled out a write-in campaign just yet, but stay tuned for more updates.” – Andrew Cottingham
