(Click below to register today!) About Me: I am Andrew Cottingham, and I am a 26 year-old political scientist/consultant.
I was raised in the small town of Collinsville, Illinois, which is roughly 20 minutes from downtown St.
Louis.
I grew up in a family that was predominantly Republican and identified as one for most of my life; however, I crossed over to the Democratic Party in 2018, but my time with them was rather brief.
I have worked in fast food, retail, political campaigns, and am currently self-employed.
I have also spent my life volunteering in varying capacities.
In my family, we value our community and serving others, which are essential to who I am.
In 2022, I graduated from the University of Evansville in Evansville, Indiana with a BS in Political Science and minors in history, legal studies, and international studies.
Having spent time on both sides of the two-party system, and having always been anti-establishment, I have realized that my place is within the independent movement.
