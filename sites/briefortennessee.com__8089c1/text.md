Tennessee FARMS RIGHTS RIGHTS Small towns and farmers are the backbone of our state.
For our farmers we have an opportunity here to become more locally independent.
We have the resources.
I am working with a local farmer who has helped write numerous grants in agriculture.
Lets work together to utilize our resources.
RIGHTS RIGHTS RIGHTS The same rules, the same respect for everybody.
We have some of the harshest bills going through and being passed in legislature.
Our laws are meant to protect not discriminate or make people feel less than.
Liberty and Justice for all.
ECONOMY EDUCATION EDUCATION If you are working full time in Tennessee, you shouldn’t be worrying about how to pay your bills.
The $7.25 minimum wage has not changed since 2009 while our cost of living has risen 45%-55% overall.
I want to propose “Tennessee Fair wage Plan” with a two-year review system.
EDUCATION EDUCATION EDUCATION Our public schools are under attack and being defunded.
I will defend our public schools and get the resources they need.
By working with our teachers and administration so every child, no matter where they live or who they are, has a fair chance to succeed—whether that’s college, trade school, or going straight to work.
