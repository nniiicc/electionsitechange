Fellow Oregonians: What does Congress have to do with the State of Oregon?
“Excuses” for not doing what our Legislature and Governor should be doing.
What the Legislature and the Governor should be doing is basing our laws on “Independence”.
Not the Dependency on Federal dollars and strings attached.
We have herd for all to long that our schools are going down-hill and we need more money from the Federal Government to pay for teachers.
We have herd for far too long that we need more money from the Federal Government to pay for roads and bridges.
We have herd for way too long that we need more money from the Federal Government to pay for unemployment benefits.
The excuses for what is going on in our State of Oregon are as endless as the ways the Legislature and Governor have wasted our tax dollars.
Waste caused by the lack of experience in the simple realities of business, lack of rudimentary science, math, economic, and manufacturing background, and the lack experience in working for a living.
Excuses that have led to Oregonians living in the natural resources they know how to handle themselves but are forced not use by someone, someplace outside of the State of Oregon that thinks they know better than us what it takes to live here.
We need a Governor that will not have a blind trust in the Federal Government.
We need a Governor that will not be afraid to cut Federal purse strings and the utter dependency on a system that has turned our Legislature and current Governor into puppets.
We need a Governor that has a background in business, science, and reality.
No more “Excuses” We need a Real Governor!
