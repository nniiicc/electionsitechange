For years, Lisa Hernandez has delivered real results for working families across Cicero, Berwyn, Brookfield, Lyons, McCook, Riverside, and Stickney.
As families face rising costs and growing uncertainty, Lisa is focused on protecting our community and continuing the progress we’ve made together.
Elizabeth “Lisa” Hernandez proudly serves the 2nd District in the Illinois House of Representatives, representing the Southwest Suburban communities of Cicero, Berwyn, Brookfield, Lyons, McCook, Riverside, and Stickney.
Lisa knows firsthand the challenges of raising a family as a working mother.
From her humble beginnings as a grocery clerk and member of the United Food and Commercial Workers Union, to her service in the Cicero Public School System, to her career of service in the State House, Lisa is a proven champion for her community who tirelessly advocates for opportunity and investment in the diverse communities she represents.
As the highest-ranking Latino member of the Illinois House of Representatives, Deputy Majority Leader Lisa Hernandez has a demonstrated track record of delivering for workers and working families in our community.
As a full-time public servant, Lisa has led efforts to secure fair wages, improve working conditions, champion workers’ rights, and promote access to equitable, quality education.
She has delivered investments for public schools and community resources that support generations of working families, seniors, and children in the Southwest Suburbs.
As Chair of the Democratic Party of Illinois, Lisa has led efforts to increase representation and voter engagement in every corner of the state.
Lisa Hernandez is running for re-election to keep delivering for the Southwest Suburbs and to protect the progress our community has made.
