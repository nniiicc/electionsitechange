Results Over Rhetoric.
I am running for Texas State Senate because leadership should be grounded in faith, family, responsibility, and results—not party extremes or political theater.
I am an educator, father, special needs advocate, and small business owner.
I have worked in classrooms, walked with families navigating broken systems, and built programs focused on opportunity, accountability, and second chances.
I understand the real challenges families face because I have lived them alongside you.
I am a Democrat because I believe government has a responsibility to ensure fairness, access, and opportunity—especially for working families, students, and seniors.
At the same time, I believe in fiscal responsibility, strong families, safe communities, and personal accountability.
"I don’t lead from the far left or the far right.
I lead from where families live." The A.C.T.I.O.N.S.
