Montgomery County, Texas — Ron Angeletti today formally announced his campaign for Texas State Senate, District 4, launching a people-centered platform built on practical solutions rather than partisan politics.
Despite District 4's recent history of voting overwhelmingly Republican, Angeletti says his decision to run is rooted not in party strategy, but in community conviction.
"I have studied the numbers.
I know the history.
But I trust the conscience of this community more than I trust election statistics.
When families are concerned about safety, schools, healthcare, water, and opportunity, they are not asking whether a solution is Republican or Democrat — they are asking whether it works." Angeletti's campaign is centered around A.C.T.I.O.N.S. , a seven-point framework reflecting what he calls "real-life priorities across District 4": A — Aging With Dignity C — Community Safety With Accountability T — Teaching & Education That Works I — Infrastructure & Water Stewardship O — Opportunity & Workforce Development N — Neighbor-Centered Healthcare S — Stewardship of Resources At the heart of his platform is a commitment to fiscal responsibility and local tax relief, including fully funding public schools at the state level to ease pressure on property taxpayers.
"Stewardship is not just good policy — it is a moral responsibility.
Taxpayer dollars come from families who work hard for them.
Government should reflect that respect." Angeletti emphasizes that his campaign is not about flipping a political map, but about strengthening trust between elected officials and the communities they serve.
"I do not lead from the far left or the far right.
I lead from where families live." The campaign will focus on community engagement, town halls, faith-based outreach, and direct conversations with voters throughout District 4.
