The district is one of the fastest-growing in Texas, reflecting the broader growth of the greater Houston region.
Families across Harris County and beyond are putting down roots throughout District 4 — bringing energy, diversity, and shared aspirations for strong communities.
With that growth comes both opportunity and the responsibility to plan wisely.
Despite its political history of leaning Republican, District 4 is home to a wide range of voters — suburban parents, working-class families, small business owners, educators, veterans, and retirees — who care far more about results than party labels.
Ron Angeletti's campaign is built on the belief that this district deserves a senator who listens to all of its communities and delivers practical solutions to the real challenges families face every day.
Communities We Serve Montgomery County Jefferson County Harris County Galveston County Chambers County "When families are concerned about safety, schools, healthcare, water, and opportunity, they are not asking whether a solution is Republican or Democrat — they are asking whether it works." — Ron Angeletti Key Issues in District 4 These are the challenges Ron hears about at every door, town hall, and community event across the district.
Education District 4 is home to rapidly growing school districts facing funding shortfalls.
Texas ranks among the lowest in per-pupil state funding, placing the burden on local property taxes.
Fully funding public schools at the state level would provide relief to homeowners and better outcomes for students.
Water & Infrastructure The Sam Houston National Forest, Lake Conroe, Lake Livingston, and the Trinity River all fall within District 4.
Protecting water quality and ensuring reliable infrastructure for fast-growing communities is critical — both for residents today and generations to come.
Healthcare Access Many parts of District 4 are considered rural or semi-rural healthcare deserts.
Residents travel long distances for basic care, and emergency services are stretched thin.
Expanding Medicaid and incentivizing rural healthcare providers would make a real difference for families.
Public Safety Growing population means growing demand on local law enforcement, fire departments, and emergency services.
District 4 needs state investment in first responders, mental health crisis response, and community-based safety programs that keep neighborhoods safe and officers supported.
Economic Opportunity District 4 sits along major corridors connecting Houston to rural East Texas.
Workforce development programs, small business support, and investment in trade skills are essential to ensuring that economic growth in the region benefits working families — not just large corporations.
Aging Population A significant and growing portion of District 4 residents are seniors.
Protecting Medicare and Social Security, expanding in-home care options, and ensuring access to affordable prescription drugs are not partisan issues — they are community priorities.
Your Vote Shapes This District Senate District 4 has been underrepresented for too long.
It is time for a senator who truly reflects the values and priorities of the people who live here.
