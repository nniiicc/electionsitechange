The IUPA represents rank-and-file officers and emergency personnel and fights for better equipment, fair wages and legislation that protects the men and women who keep us safe.
Sam A.
Cabral, a retired detective, leads the organization as International President.
“Our cops don't need a politician who disparaged corrections officers or one who answers to Kathy Hochul, like Blake Gendebien.
They need one who will always go to the mat for them, and back the people who keep us safe,” Constantino said.
“The IUPA is made up of some of the toughest people in our country, and I’m proud to have them in my corner.
I'll make sure every officer in NY-21 and across our country knows I have their back when I’m in Washington.” The endorsement adds to Constantino's growing coalition, which includes close to 400 local elected NY-21 officials.
### About Anthony Constantino Anthony Constantino set the record for most votes in the NY-21 primary after overcoming fierce establishment opposition as a political outsider who built Sticker Mule, a global company headquartered in New York with over 1,000 employees.
He also compiled a 2-1 (2 KOs) professional boxing record after going pro for his 40th birthday.
