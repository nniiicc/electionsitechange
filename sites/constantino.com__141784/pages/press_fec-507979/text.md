Federal law bars foreign nationals from making contributions or expenditures in American elections and from taking any part in a campaign's decision-making.
FEC Deputy Press Officer Myles Martin previously told the Daily Caller “Commission regulations prohibit certain foreign national activity including contributions, donations, expenditures (including independent expenditures) and disbursements solicited, directed, received or made directly or indirectly by or from foreign nationals in connection with any federal, state or local election.” Over the course of Gendebien’s federal violations coming to light, the widespread reporting, and now this federal complaint, Gendebien has refused to answer for any bit of it, ignoring media inquiries, or forcing his staff to stonewall reporters.
