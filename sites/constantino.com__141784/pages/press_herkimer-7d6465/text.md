Herkimer County legislature unanimously endorses Anthony Constantino for Congress
Herkimer County, NY – 17 September 2026 – The Constantino for Congress campaign announced today that Anthony Constantino has earned the unanimous endorsement of the Herkimer County Legislature.
Ray Donley, Majority Leader, said “As Majority Leader, I don't take endorsements lightly.
Anthony Constantino has the character and the commitment NY-21 needs in Congress, and this legislature stands behind him.”
Chairman Peter J.
Campione said “On behalf of this legislature, I'm proud to endorse Anthony Constantino.
He has demonstrated a genuine understanding of Herkimer County, and we believe he will fight for this district in Washington.”
The campaign said the endorsement reflects the kind of grassroots, county-level trust that matters most in this race, which Constantino has.
The endorsement adds to a growing list of local officials across NY-21 who have voiced support for Constantino's campaign ahead of the general election.

About Anthony Constantino
Anthony Constantino set the record for most votes in the NY-21 primary after overcoming fierce establishment opposition as a political outsider who built Sticker Mule, a global company headquartered in New York with over 1,000 employees.
He also compiled a 2-1 (2 KOs) professional boxing record after going pro for his 40th birthday.
