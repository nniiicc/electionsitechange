Restore Medicare Part D Subsidies before January, 2027 If elected to Congress, I will immediately bring a resolution and request a vote to restore Medicare Part D Prescription Subsidies in full.
Cash Flow Initiative - Universal Basic Income If elected to Congress, I would sponsor legislation that would allow for the payment of Universal Basic Income to qualifying families.
Artificial intelligence has ravaged the job market, consequently, something must be done to restore cash to households so that basic survival expenses would be met.
A monthly payment of $1,000 would be paid to families with household income of 65,000 or less.
Cash Flow Initiative - Reparations In 1988, President Ronald Reagan signed a bill into law providing for the payment of $20,000 to the descendants of Japanese Americans that lived in the United States.
During WWll, Japanese Americans were forced to go into concentration camps right here in our country.
President Reagan sought to make amends by making monetary payments to those families.
I would like to sponsor similar legislation to be signed into law regarding the descendants of slaves that actually were forced render forced labor during slavery.
Their work significantly contributed to the wealth-building and ultimate stabilization of America.
Similarly, a law should be passed to pay reparations to the descendants of slaves that help to enrich this country's economy.
The reparations payments would partially be financed by a small tax accessed on every transaction conducted on the New York Stock Exchange.
Pass a Law Making Election Day a Federal Holiday I would like to pass a law declaring Election Day a Federal Holiday.
It would be a day totally committed to the electoral process without distractions or obstacles.
I believe that mail-in has become somewhat of an albatross around our electoral necks.
This law would somewhat relieve the need to manually handle and count millions of ballots and hopefully spur, more voter dedication and participation.
