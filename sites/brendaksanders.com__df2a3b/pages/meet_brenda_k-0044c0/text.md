Sanders Greetings: I am former Judge Brenda K.
Sanders.
I am a Candidate for the U.
S.
House of Representatives for the 12th District in Michigan.
I am a native Detroiter!
I am a former Trial Attorney, former Michigan Judge, Author, Painter, Harpist and former WGPR 107.5 and Blogtalkradio.com Podcast Host.
I continue to practice law as a pro se litigator on a regular basis.
I wrote a book to help pro se litigators that may not be able to afford an attorney.
The name of my book is entitled, "Speak for Yourself"!
I am a graduate of the University of Michigan in Ann Arbor.
I majored in Political Science.
I received my Juris Doctorate degree from the University of Detroit-Mercy School of Law in 1983.
I operated my law practice in the Michigan, tri-county Wayne, Oakland and Washtenaw County areas for twenty-one years.
I focused my law practice in the criminal law, civil law, personal injury, workplace discrimination, and probate areas.
I practiced in both the federal and state courts.
I also worked as a law clerk in the area of medical malpractice for a major Detroit law firm while attending law school.
Additionally, I served as a Washtenaw County Public Defender for a year after I passed the Michigan Bar.
My departure from my judicial position took place after I reported to the Obama Administration FBI that I received a tip about the sudden death of two of my judicial colleagues.
I further warned, that I was in fear for my safety.
Notwithstanding the above-described misfortunes, I am concerned about the dangerous and looming environmental hazards that we presently experience, along with the steady rising price of everything in our economy.
I want to sponsor major efforts to make sure the federal minimum wage is at 25.00 or more, and that it continues to rise regularly and simultaneous to cost of living increases.
Additionally, I will sponsor bills that will promote the passage of laws that will provide for a lineage based six-figure payment of cash reparations to the current descendants of the slaves that were forced to work without compensation.
The slave workforce helped to build our nation's current economy.
Lastly, I will sponsor a bill that will provide for the payment of a monthly payout of $1,200 to qualifying citizens to make up for the loss of job opportunities brought on by the advent of artificial intelligence technology.
I have a proven track record of leadership as a trial attorney, judge, and as an advocate for social change.
If elected, I would like to start my Congressional career by sponsoring a bill to declare Election Day a federal holiday.
I would like to clear as many obstacles to voting as possible.
Voting is a right that must not be curtailed.
The right to vote must be expanded and preserved at all costs!!
I am also will continue to strenuously advocate for laws that will preserve our rights to a chemical free environment.
I want to also sponsor amendments to the Violence Against Women Act to include the prosecution of government officials that offend and abuse the rights of women!
We deserve more protection from our government!
Please also visit me at https://Instagram.com/brendaksanders4thepeople and https://Instagram.com/brendak.sanders to learn more about me.
Thank you for taking the time.
Please feel free to mail a contribution to my campaign mailing address at 4501 Woodward Avenue, Suite 101-B, #117, Detroit, Michigan 48201.
I will see you on or before November 3, 2026.
Thank you once again!
Thank you in advance!
