A Campaign Rooted In Rural Economic Recovery
Reinhold’s campaign centers on economic issues and the belief that Kansans deserve a representative focused on working families—not corporate donors.
Work should mean something.
Dignity shouldn’t be optional.
And stability shouldn’t be a luxury.
“I’m fighting for Kansas, not for big corporations or special interests.
If anyone in Washington wants to push policies that hurt our farmers, our hospitals, our workers, or our towns, they’ll have to go through me first.”
Meeting Kansans where they are at
Reinhold is committed to a “go-everywhere” grassroots campaign.
In the months before announcing, she traveled across the Big First—stopping in Hays, Dodge City, Salina, Hutchinson, and dozens of rural towns—listening to the challenges faced by healthcare workers, farmers, students, and families.
She’s in this fight because she believes Kansans deserve better.
Healthcare you can count on.
Wages you can raise a family on.
Representation that doesn’t disappear when the cameras turn off.
“Politics shouldn’t be something done to people.
Representative government starts with showing up, listening, and earning trust.”
Lauren Reinhold for Kansas News
