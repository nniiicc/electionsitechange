Accessibility Statement
At Lauren Reinhold for Kansas, we are dedicated to ensuring accessibility for all our website users, including those with disabilities.
We strive to make our digital content accessible and user-friendly.
Our Efforts:
- Inclusive Design: We prioritize an accessible design approach, considering diverse users' needs.
- Standards Compliance: Our website aims to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA standards wherever possible.
- Continuous Improvement: We regularly review our site to improve accessibility features.
Accessibility Features:
- Keyboard Navigation: Our site is fully navigable via keyboard.
- Text Alternatives: Images have alternative text for screen reader compatibility.
- Readable Content: Text is structured and styled for clarity and ease of reading.
Feedback and Support:
We welcome feedback on our accessibility efforts.
If you encounter any difficulties or have suggestions, please contact us at info@laurenforkansas.com
Future Steps:
We are committed to ongoing enhancements and updates to improve accessibility.
Your feedback plays a vital role in our efforts to make our digital presence inclusive for everyone.
Thank you for supporting Lauren Reinhold for Kansas.
