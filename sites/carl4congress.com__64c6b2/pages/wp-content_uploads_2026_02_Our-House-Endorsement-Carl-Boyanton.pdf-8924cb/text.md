February 23, 2026 Re: Official Endorsement for United States House of Representatives, MS-04 Dear Carl Boyanton, On behalf of Our House United PAC, we are pleased to formally endorse Carl Boyanton for election to the United States House of Representatives representing Mississippi’s 4th District.
This endorsement follows a structured evaluation process that included a candidate interview and a review of publicly available information.
Our House PAC supports candidates who demonstrate integrity, leadership, and a clear commitment to serving the public interest rather than being influenced by partisan or special-interests.
We evaluate candidates based on their demonstrated respect for constitutional governance, accountable leadership, and voter-first representation.
Throughout our review, you demonstrated an ability to represent constituents beyond partisan boundaries, a respect for constitutional limits on power, and a principled approach to public service grounded in accountability to the people you serve.
Our House United PAC represents a growing national coalition of independent and unaffiliated voters who do not align with either political party but are committed to constitutional governance, accountable leadership, and voter-first representation.
These voters are often decisive in competitive districts and are frequently under-engaged by traditional party structures.
Our House PAC exists to support candidates who are prepared to govern with courage, integrity, transparency, and respect for the people they serve.
Based on our review, we are confident that you bring the character and judgment necessary to represent your constituents effectively.
This endorsement reflects the independent judgment of Our House United PAC and does not constitute coordination with the candidate or their campaign.
We encourage voters in your district to learn more about your candidacy for United States House of Representatives.
Sincerely, Jennifer Esteban Executive Director Our House PAC
