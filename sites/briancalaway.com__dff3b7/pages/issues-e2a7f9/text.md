Issue
Jobs & Economy
Living wages and good jobs for every Arizonan
Arizona has world-class talent in engineering, technology, and innovation.
Brian believes we can build an economy that keeps that talent here while creating real pathways for every Arizonan to participate — not just the lucky few.
- ✓ Support small businesses and local entrepreneurs with reduced regulatory friction
- ✓ Invest in workforce development and job training aligned with industry needs
- ✓ Advocate for living wages that keep pace with Arizona's rising cost of living
- ✓ Attract high-quality employers while protecting workers' rights and benefits
Issue
Education
Fully funded schools and fair pay for teachers
A state that underfunds its schools is borrowing from its future.
Arizona has consistently ranked near the bottom in per-pupil spending.
That stops.
Brian will fight to fully fund classrooms and pay teachers what they deserve.
- ✓ Fully fund K-12 public schools to meet constitutional requirements
- ✓ Increase teacher pay to competitive levels to recruit and retain great educators
- ✓ Expand vocational and technical training for the jobs of tomorrow
- ✓ Ensure every child has access to quality early childhood education
Issue
Healthcare
Affordable, quality care for Arizona families
Healthcare costs are squeezing Arizona families.
No one should have to choose between medical care and keeping the lights on.
Brian will fight for policies that make quality healthcare accessible to every Arizonan.
- ✓ Protect and expand Medicaid (AHCCCS) coverage for Arizona families
- ✓ Lower prescription drug costs through transparent pricing and bulk purchasing
- ✓ Support rural healthcare access and community health centers
- ✓ Prioritize mental health as equal to physical health in coverage and funding
Issue
Affordable Housing
Safe, attainable homes across the state
Arizona is growing fast and housing costs are outpacing wages.
The engineer in Brian sees this as a systems problem with real solutions — we just need leaders willing to actually look and act.
- ✓ Remove barriers to new housing construction and cut unnecessary red tape
- ✓ Expand affordable housing programs and strengthen tenant protections
- ✓ Support first-time homebuyer assistance for working-class families
- ✓ Address homelessness with housing-first solutions that produce measurable results
Issue
Agriculture & Water
Protecting Arizona farmers and our water supply
Water is Arizona's most precious resource.
Our farmers built this state.
Brian will defend both against short-sighted policy and corporate overreach — because without water and food security, nothing else matters.
- ✓ Protect Arizona's water rights and enforce sustainable groundwater usage policies
- ✓ Support family farmers with fair market access and federal program navigation
- ✓ Invest in water infrastructure and conservation technology
- ✓ Hold corporations accountable for excessive groundwater extraction
Issue
Safe Communities
Strong families and neighborhoods across Arizona
Safety means more than policing.
It means strong schools, good jobs, mental health resources, and neighbors who look out for each other.
Brian is committed to a comprehensive approach that actually reduces crime.
- ✓ Support law enforcement with proper training, resources, and accountability measures
- ✓ Invest in community violence prevention programs and youth organizations
- ✓ Expand mental health crisis response as a complement to traditional law enforcement
- ✓ Protect proven public safety measures while respecting constitutional rights
