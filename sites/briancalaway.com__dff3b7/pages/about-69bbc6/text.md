Home → About Brian I am a former science educator that realized around 2010 that we were not cultivating the skills in our children for them to create society with our values in mind.
So I left teaching and began running after school enrichment programs for parents who wanted their children to have these skills.
And now, I not only want future generations but our generation to have a society and a connected community that can provide them with affordable healthcare (not sick care), jobs, housing, and education.
My beliefs stem from FDR's second bill of rights which believes we should all have access to education, jobs, housing, food, healthcare, and everything for us to be successful as humans — because economic rights mean economic freedom for all.
Standing with me is standing with all blue candidates up and down the ballot.
I've knocked on thousands of doors in the past for previous candidates and now I'm out there to represent you and fight the red that is so deeply seeded in our district.
So, I'm asking for you to share your time, talent, and treasure with us so we can best represent you and protect our future.
