Brian grew up in a middle class family just like most of us.
He has worked with kids in public schools for many years and has a deep compassion for the human condition.
He holds a Bachelor's of Interdisciplinary Studies and is passionate about making the world a better place through better policy and better government — a government that is by and for all of us.
His beliefs stem from FDR's Second Bill of Economic Rights: every person deserves access to jobs, housing, food, healthcare, and education — because economic rights mean economic freedom for all.
