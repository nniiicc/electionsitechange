We’ve got exciting news!
Thanks to a gracious in-kind donation from our endorsers at the Progressive Turnout Project, we have the opportunity to hire two fellows to support our campaign’s voter outreach efforts.
Each of these two fellow positions entails 15 hours per week of work and pays a stipend of $1,250 per month (~$20 hourly equivalent).
Fellows will work September 6th through Election Day.
Not only is this a great gig for someone interested in creating meaningful change down in Concord, it is a fabulous learning opportunity—as part of the fellowship, Progressive Turnout Project will provide an hour of online training per week, helping the fellows develop their political organizing skills and grow their resumes.
(The 15 per week commitment is inclusive of this weekly hour of paid time in training.)
For further details, check out this job description.
Our ideal candidate will be local to the Valley, love meeting and talking with new people, and have a strong work ethic.
If you are interested in the opportunity (or know someone else who might be) please let us know using the button below and we’ll reach out!
Deadline: 8/12/22
