Colleen O'Donnell
Colleen O’Donnell is a constitutional conservative with extensive judicial experience at every level of government.
She is running for the Ohio Supreme Court to uphold the rule of law, protect public safety, and preserve a conservative majority on the Court.
Most recently, Colleen served as a U.S.
Immigration Judge in Laredo, Texas, where she handled illegal entry and asylum cases during the height of the border crisis.
She enforced the law as written, never once granted asylum, and consistently ordered the removal of illegal aliens from our country.
Before that, she spent nearly a decade on the Franklin County Common Pleas Court, overseeing serious civil and criminal cases and holding violent offenders accountable.
Colleen also served at the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio, worked as an Assistant Attorney General, clerked in federal court, and represented manufacturers and business clients in private practice.
The proud daughter of retired Ohio Supreme Court Justice Terrence O’Donnell, Colleen has built her own record of conservative leadership through experience and results.
