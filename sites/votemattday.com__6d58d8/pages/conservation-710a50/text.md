What makes Georgia’s 1st Congressional District so unique is that it’s home to six National Wildlife Refuges — the most of any district in the continental United States.
Only Alaska holds more within a single district.
We have two primary goals.
Goal One: Stop the Okefenokee Swamp from being turned over to the United Nations’ UNESCO program.
Goal Two: Secure funding for our six refuges.
-Matt Day
