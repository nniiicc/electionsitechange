Colleagues said that after losing his father at a young age, Matthews looked to Keating as a father figure.
“I looked up to him,” Keating confided.
“I valued his wisdom and judgement.
He liked the old music from the era I came from, and he liked the water, like I do.
We had things in common that we shared, including making this country better.
It wasn’t a one-way street.
As time went on, I was looking for advice from him.”
