Vote By Mail Voting by mail is easy – just request a ballot before October 27th and it will be mailed to you.
You can return it to your town or city clerk or mail it back – just make sure it is postmarked or dropped off on or before election day.
Vote Early You can vote early in person seven days a week from October 17th to October 30th.
Vote on 11/3 You can still safely vote on election day at your regular polling place from 7am to 8pm.
Remember, if you’re in line to vote by 8pm you can cast a ballot – no matter how long the line is.
