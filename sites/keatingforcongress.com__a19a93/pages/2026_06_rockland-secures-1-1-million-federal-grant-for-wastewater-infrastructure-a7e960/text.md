ROCKLAND – June 2, 2026 – The Rockland Select Board announced a massive financial boost for local infrastructure Tuesday night, securing a $1.1 million federal grant from Congressman Bill Keating’s office to expand the town’s phase one wastewater treatment plant upgrades.
The surprise windfall capped off an incredibly efficient, seven-minute meeting that also cleared the way for critical milestones regarding the new fire station project, final utility disconnections for the impending Lincoln School demolition, and key appointments to the Board of Registrars.
