Creating Jobs
Richard knows the importance of getting this economy growing and creating jobs.
He is committed to conservative, pro-business principles that will help create jobs: cutting taxes, reducing excessive regulations, and fully funding worker retraining programs at our community colleges.
Richard rolls up his sleeves, works hard and remains focused on his goal of every worker having an opportunity at a good job.
Through the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, we grew the economy, put more money in middle class Americans’ pockets, and raised wages.
Richard believes we must build on that progress to shrink government and grow our economy once again.
