Cutting Spending
As a solid conservative, Richard is certain of one thing: Washington has a spending problem, not a revenue problem.
That’s why Richard has pledged to not increase taxes as a Member of Congress.
With a skyrocketing national debt, Richard believes it is long past time for conservatives to draw a line in the sand.
We need conservative solutions and steadfast leadership to fix it.
Richard believes Congress should spend taxpayer money as efficiently as possible.
We can count on him to fight to restore fiscal sanity to Washington, DC.
