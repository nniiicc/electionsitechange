Representative Richard Hudson (NC-08) today announced that his re-election campaign raised nearly $310,000 in the second quarter of 2017 with the vast majority of those donations coming from individuals in North Carolina.
This is the second quarter in a row that he has raised over $300,000, bringing his total raised so far this cycle to $658,435.27.
Hudson’s campaign has raised more money from North Carolina donors in the first six months of this year than in any previous year.
“The generous support of my fellow North Carolinians means so much to me,” said Hudson.
