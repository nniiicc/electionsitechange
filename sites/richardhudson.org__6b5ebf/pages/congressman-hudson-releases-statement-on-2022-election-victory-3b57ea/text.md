Congressman Hudson Releases Statement on 2022 Election Victory
SOUTHERN PINES – Congressman Richard Hudson released the following statement on his victory in North Carolina’s 9th Congressional District:
“Renee and I appreciate the outpouring of support throughout this campaign and I am honored for the opportunity to continue serving our community” said Rep.
Hudson.
“I now look forward to following through on our ‘Commitment to America’ to work towards an economy that’s strong, a nation that’s safe, a future built on freedom, and a government that’s accountable.
As Fort Bragg’s Congressman, I will also continue to work every day for our veterans, our troops and their families.”
North Carolina’s 9th Congressional District includes Chatham, Cumberland, Harnett, Hoke, Lee, Moore, Randolph, Richmond, and Scotland Counties.
