Hudson.
“I now look forward to following through on our ‘Commitment to America’ to work towards an economy that’s strong, a nation that’s safe, a future built on freedom, and a government that’s accountable.
As Fort Bragg’s Congressman, I will also continue to work every day for our veterans, our troops and their families.” North Carolina’s 9th Congressional District includes Chatham, Cumberland, Harnett, Hoke, Lee, Moore, Randolph, Richmond, and Scotland Counties.
