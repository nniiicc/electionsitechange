Endorsement List
*A Note on Endorsements*
An endorsement does not mean that the individuals or organizations listed below agree with me on every issue, policy, or political philosophy, nor does it mean that I agree with them on everything.
Each endorsement is given for its own reasons.
Some may support my effort to grow the House of Representatives and restore meaningful representation; others may share my commitment to constitutional limits on government, particular policy positions, political independence, character, or a belief that voters deserve a different kind of representation in Washington.
I welcome support from people with whom I may sometimes disagree.
Representative government does not require complete agreement; it requires people of different perspectives to find common cause where they can while remaining honest about where they differ.
These endorsements should therefore be understood as expressions of support for my candidacy, not as declarations of complete political or ideological agreement.
