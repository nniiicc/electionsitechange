CHAD WESTBERG for Minnesota house
Chad Westberg is the endorsed Republican running for Minnesota House District 30B.
Chad and his wife, Sarah, have been married since 2003 and are raising their three children in Elk River, where they have built their lives and deep roots in the community.
For more than a decade, Chad has been actively involved in the Elk River community as a volunteer football coach, mentoring young athletes and investing in the next generation.
His commitment to service extends beyond the field, reflecting a belief that strong communities are built through leadership, accountability, and showing up.
Professionally, Chad has worked in the ethanol and agricultural solutions industry and operates his own small business.
He understands firsthand the challenges facing Minnesota families and small businesses and is committed to policies that support economic growth and opportunity.
VALUES
Chad is running to bring a strong, reliable conservative voice to St.
Paul, focused on the issues that matter most to families in District 30B:
- Lower taxes and responsible spending
- Stopping fraud and waste in state government
- Supporting law enforcement and first responders
- Protecting life, religious liberty, and parental rights
- Growing the economy and supporting small businesses
- Strengthening schools and ensuring safe communities
Chad believes Minnesota families deserve leadership that reflects their values, respects their hard work, and fights for a stronger future.
