Past Events Join Rep.
Witte and Sen.
Duckworth at the Parade!
Date: Saturday, July 13, 2023 Time: 4:30pm Parade Lineup Starts.
We should be done by 6:30.
Stick around for food, friends and fun post parade!
Parking: McGuire Middle School, or end of parade route.
There is a shuttle that goes between the start and finish.
Location: Holyoke & 210th Street Dress: Dress for warm weather and wear comfortable shoes, we will have Duckworth and Witte t-shirts for all parade walkers!
Who can join?: Bring the whole family!
If you don’t feel like you can walk with us still come and cheer us on from the street as we pass by!
