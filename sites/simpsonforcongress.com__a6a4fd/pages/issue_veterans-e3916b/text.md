The great State of Idaho is home to thousands of men and women who have served our nation honorably both at home and abroad and who are owed a great debt of gratitude for their service.
It is important that on both a state and federal level we not forget the promises made to those who have served, nor the sacrifices our nation’s veterans have made to protect the freedoms we all enjoy as Americans.
While our nation must come to grips with its overspending and growing nation debt, one place where funding must not be cut nor our commitment reduced is in the area of veterans’ benefits and healthcare.
