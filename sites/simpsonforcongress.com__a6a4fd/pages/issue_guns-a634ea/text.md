Therefore, I will not support bans on the ownership of firearms, or legislation that promotes or requires the rationing or taxation of firearms or ammunition.
Contrary to the claims of gun control advocates, gun ownership is not the sole source of our gun-related crime problem.
Illegal firearms trafficking and our “revolving door” justice system, and deficiencies in mental healthcare are significant contributing factors.
I will continue to support legislation to reform our justice system to impose stiff sentences on criminals who use a gun while committing a crime.
