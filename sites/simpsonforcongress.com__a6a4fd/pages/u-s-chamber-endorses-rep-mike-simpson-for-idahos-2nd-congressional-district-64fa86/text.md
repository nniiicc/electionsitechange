Chamber Endorses Rep.
Mike Simpson for Idaho’s 2nd Congressional District May 12, 2022 WASHINGTON, D.C. — Today, the U.S.
Chamber of Commerce announced its endorsement of Representative Mike Simpson (R) to represent Idaho’s Second Congressional District.
The Chamber endorses pro-business candidates for federal office who will vigorously support public policy that will advance economic growth, create jobs, and promote fiscal responsibility.
“The U.S.
Chamber and our members are committed to supporting candidates for public office who champion job creators and the employees, families, and communities they serve.
We know that strong leaders in Washington are critical to addressing economic challenges, accelerating our recovery, and promoting opportunity,” said U.S.
Chamber of Commerce President and CEO Suzanne P.
Clark.
“Rep.
Mike Simpson has demonstrated a commitment to supporting free enterprise and the American business community through pro-growth solutions, and he has earned the political support of the business community.
