Why I’m running
We live in turbulent times.
Citizen trust in government, science and facts, and in each other is at an all-time low.
Now is not the time to sit back and watch.
Rather, this is a time to step up and fight for what is important to citizens of our area, including: strong public schools, affordable and accessible day care, successful farming operations, affordable housing, effective environmental protection, respect for our immigrant neighbors, and open and honest state government.
On the Issues
Public School Financing
Wisconsin’s public schools are under growing financial strain as state funding fails to keep pace with rising costs, shifting the burden to local taxpayers.
I will work to secure sustainable state investment to strengthen public schools and the communities they serve.
Strong Ag Communities
Agriculture is integral to Wisconsin’s economy, yet farmers face growing market pressures.
I will work to protect our producers, strengthen our industries, and support investment in rural communities.
Affordable Housing
Affordable housing is critical to keeping communities strong, yet many working families, seniors, and individuals with disabilities lack accessible options.
I will work to expand state support for workforce and community-based housing to ensure more people can thrive where they call home.
Affordable, Quality Healthcare
Healthcare costs are rising at unsustainable rates, putting increasing pressure on families and communities in the 50th Assembly District.
I will work to advance reforms that prioritize affordability, access, and high-quality care over profit.
Affordable Childcare
Affordable, reliable childcare is essential to supporting working families and a strong economy, yet many childcare educators and parents face rising costs and limited availability.
I will work to expand state investment and support solutions that strengthen childcare access for families and communities.
Disability opportunities
Strong disability services lead to independence, inclusion, and opportunity, yet many families face limited access.
I will work to expand support, strengthen the care workforce, and improve access to housing and services for people with disabilities.
