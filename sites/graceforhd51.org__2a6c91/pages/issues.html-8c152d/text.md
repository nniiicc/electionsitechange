Veterans, Military Families & Holloman AFB
Holloman AFB isn't a side issue in HD51 — it is the district's economy, a third of its population, and the reason most local families either serve, used to serve, or work alongside someone who does.
Real-World Context
- Holloman is home to the 49th Wing, training F-16 and MQ-9 Reaper pilots (including allied Spanish, British, and Italian aircrews) and shares water rights and regional infrastructure with the City of Alamogordo.
- Alamogordo and Holloman won the 2025 Altus Community Support Award for the second time in three years, recognized in part for the Alamogordo Thrive Guide connecting incoming military families to housing, childcare, healthcare, and spouse-employment help, and for securing $34.2 million for a new Holloman Elementary School and $59.6 million for Holloman Middle School.
- New military-adjacent housing — including the Sendero Alamo Apartments — is underway to address a real housing shortage for enlisted families; 2026 Basic Allowance for Housing rates rose nearly 12% for the Alamogordo Military Housing Area, reflecting how tight the local rental market has become.
- Spouse employment remains one of the hardest parts of a Holloman assignment, given Alamogordo's small civilian job market.
- PFAS groundwater contamination has been documented in and around the base since 2016 — a known concern for both base personnel and neighboring well users.
- MainGate United, the Alamogordo Chamber of Commerce, the Otero County Commission, the City, and Alamogordo Public Schools have formally adopted the "Five and Thrive" framework and "Project R.I.S.E." to coordinate community support for the base.
Grace's Policy Commitments
- I will work across party lines to protect Holloman's mission and advocate at every opportunity — budget hearings, capital outlay requests, and constituent casework — for the base's long-term viability and growth.
- I will champion continued state support for military-family housing, childcare access, and spouse-employment programs, building on what the Thrive Guide and base partners have already started.
- I will push for state and federal accountability on the PFAS contamination near Holloman, including testing support and remediation funding for affected residents and well owners off-base.
- I will support veteran-owned small business development and workforce pipelines that let military spouses and transitioning veterans build careers in Otero County instead of leaving the area.
- I will continue Alamogordo's tradition of greeting Honor Flight veterans home on New York Avenue, and I will fight for the benefits, healthcare access, and recognition every veteran in this district has earned.
