Independent Candidate for State Representative · Serving New Mexico House District 51
Your neighbor living District 51's fight
Grace Nagamine is a lifelong Alamogordo resident, wife, mother, grandmother, humanitarian, founder of Royal Angels, former Mrs.
New Mexico (Today's International Woman Pageant, 2018–2019), and the Independent candidate for New Mexico House District 51.
She believes leadership begins with faith, compassion, and service.
About Grace
Raised right here in Alamogordo.
Grace has been a member of Mountain View Church for over ten years, where she serves in the nursery.
Grace has experienced homelessness, survived domestic violence, served as a caregiver for family members, worked as a CNA at Casa Arena, and founded Royal Angels — inspired by her granddaughter, Melanie.
These experiences shaped her commitment to children, families, seniors, veterans, law enforcement, mental health, disability advocacy, and community partnership.
