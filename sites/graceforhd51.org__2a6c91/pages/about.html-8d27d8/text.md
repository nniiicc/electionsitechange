Her story Servant leadership, lived firsthand "I don't advocate for these issues because I simply read about them — I advocate for them because I've lived them.
I am running to bring servant leadership, integrity, compassion, and common-sense solutions to Santa Fe — and to make sure roads, water, and the infrastructure that holds our community together are never again someone else's problem to fix later. " — Grace Nagamine Roots in Alamogordo Grace Nagamine is a lifelong Alamogordo resident — wife, mother, grandmother, humanitarian, founder of Royal Angels, and former Mrs.
New Mexico (Today's International Woman Pageant, 2018–2019).
Raised by Ramona Torres, in ICC Church, Grace has been a member of Mountain View Church for over ten years, where she serves in the nursery.
She believes leadership begins with faith, compassion, and service.
A Personal Story Grace has experienced homelessness, survived domestic violence, served as a caregiver for family members, and worked as a CNA at Casa Arena.
She founded Royal Angels, inspired by her granddaughter, Melanie.
These experiences shaped her lifelong commitment to children, families, seniors, veterans, law enforcement, mental health, disability advocacy, and community partnership — the same commitments that anchor her campaign platform today.
Veterans & Military Roots Grace's father served in the U.S.
Air Force.
Her brother, Sergeant Alfred R.
Lewis, honorably served in the U.S.
Army with the 3rd Infantry Division and the 77th Engineer Company, 94th Engineer Battalion, earning the Army Commendation Medal with Valor, the Army Achievement Medal, and other decorations.
This family history anchors Grace's commitment to Holloman Air Force Base, veterans, and military families — who make up roughly a third of District 51's population.
Why She's Running District 51 is anchored in Alamogordo and Otero County — home to Holloman Air Force Base, White Sands Missile Range, NMSU-Alamogordo, and a high concentration of military retirees.
Grace is running because roads, water, child welfare, senior care, and support for Holloman's families have been treated as someone else's problem to fix later for too long.
She's running to make sure that stops.
Why Independent Grace is running as an Independent because District 51's problems — a water system decades behind on maintenance, a child-welfare system under court-ordered reform, a housing shortage for military families — don't belong to one party.
She answers to her neighbors in Alamogordo and Otero County, not to a party platform written somewhere else.
Integrity Publishing her capital outlay requests every session, not deciding them behind closed doors.
Community Partnering with the local organizations already doing the work — COPE, Love INC, Mountain View Church, and more — instead of reinventing it.
Independence Representing every household in District 51, regardless of party registration.
Service Bringing the same hands-on approach she's used as a CNA, caregiver, and founder of Royal Angels to the Roundhouse.
