From his early days serving his classmates as a student leader in Coweta County, to representing thousands of students as Executive Vice President of Student Government at Georgia State University, to gaining firsthand experience at the White House and the Georgia State Capitol, JerMarkus Jacobs has always been committed to serving others and moving Georgia forward.

erMarkus A.
Jacobs is a proud son of Newnan, Georgia, raised with a deep belief in service, community, and faith.
From an early age, JerMarkus understood that leadership means standing up for others and working to make life better for those around you.
JerMarkus was educated entirely within the Coweta County School System, where he developed his passion for leadership and public service.
He graduated from East Coweta HighSchool in the Class of 2021.
During his time there, JerMarkus served as Class President and became one of the school’s first Student Ambassadors.
In these roles, he worked to connect students with leadership opportunities and organized community outreach efforts that gave back to the people of Coweta County.
His leadership and dedication did not go unnoticed.
JerMarkus was nominated by his senior class as “Most Likely to Change the World,” a recognition that reflected his commitment to service and his vision for a better future.
JerMarkus served as Executive Vice President at Georgia State University from 2025-2026.
JerMarkus continued his education at Georgia State University, where he is currently pursuing a degree in Political Science with a minor in Public Policy.
While at Georgia State, he has continued to demonstrate a strong commitment to leadership and civic engagement.
JerMarkus served as Executive Vice President of the Student Government Association on the Atlanta campus, representing the interests and voices of approximately 40,000 students.
However, JerMarkus’s calling to serve began long before college.
JerMarkus loves to spend his time engaging with the community and supporting local initiatives like the Kroger Bag-a-Thon and BINGO at Benton House.
At just seven years old, he witnessed the power of compassion and service through his parents.
They were actively involved in their community in Atlanta, where they spent time serving, praying with, and uplifting people experiencing homelessness and hardship.
Watching their dedication to helping others left a lasting impression on JerMarkus.
It was during these early moments that he realized he wanted to dedicate his life to helping people and strengthening communities.
That calling eventually led him to public service and politics.
Public Service is nothing new for JerMarkus Jacobs.
JerMarkus served as a White House intern in the fall of 2023.
JerMarkus has had the opportunity to gain firsthand experience at the highest levels of government.
He served as an intern at the White House Office of Presidential Correspondence, where he worked to help process and respond to messages from Americans across the country.
This experience gave him insight into how government connects with everyday citizens.
He also worked on several political campaigns during his time in college, gaining valuable experience engaging with voters and learning how grassroots organizing can drive meaningful change.
JerMarkus also interned for two semesters at the Georgia State Capitol, where he learned the legislative process firsthand and helped track legislation impacting communities across the state.
These experiences strengthened his understanding of how policies are created and how they directly affect the lives of everyday Georgians.
