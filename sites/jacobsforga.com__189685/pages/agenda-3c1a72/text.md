JerMarkus's Plan for GA Senate District 6
Smart Growth & Responsible Technology
Smart growth means developing our communities in a thoughtful, sustainable, and future-focused way.
I support responsible technology use that improves infrastructure, expands digital access, and ensures innovation benefits everyone.
By investing in modern solutions, we can create smarter cities while protecting our natural landscape.
Environmental Conservation
Protecting our environment in District 6 is essential for the health and future of our communities.
I support policies that conserve water, protect land, and promote sustainable use of our natural resources.
By prioritizing environmental stewardship today, we can ensure a cleaner and healthier Georgia for future generations.
Strong Schools & Workforce Development
Strong schools create strong communities.
I believe in supporting educational opportunities that prepare students for their future.
By strengthening partnerships between schools, industries, and training programs, we can ensure students graduate with the skills needed to succeed in today’s and tomorrow’s economy.
Youth & Community Advancement
Young people and community members should have meaningful opportunities to grow, lead, and shape their future.
I support programs that invest in youth leadership, mentorship, and community engagement.
By empowering the next generation and strengthening community connections, we can build a more vibrant society.
