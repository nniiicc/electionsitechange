Alan T.
Martinez is a lifelong New Mexican, having grown up in Northern New Mexico and living in the Metro area for the last 34 years, first in Albuquerque and now in Sandoval County.
Alan was involved in small business in Espanola and Albuquerque before starting his career with the New Mexico Department of Veteran Services (NMDVS), where he served Veterans and their families for over 25 years.
He started with NMDVS as a file clerk in 1993 and ended his career as the Deputy Secretary and Legislative Director in December 2018. ​ Alan was elected by his peers to serve the House Republican Caucus as the Minority Whip in May 2023 and serves the caucus in this leadership position.
He has been able to use this position to increase his visibility in advocating for House District 23 and the rest of the citizens of New Mexico. ​ His passion for serving others is why Alan now wants to serve the people of his community as their Representative for House District 23.
This district covers parts of the Town of Bernalillo west of the Rio Grande as well as northern Rio Rancho.
Alan wants to make sure that our state government is responsive to the needs of the citizens of this district.
His 25 years of experience with state government gives him better insight on navigating the sometimes-complicated bureaucracies of state and local government agencies.
He understands that the role of a state representative is an everyday responsibility, not only during the legislative session or leading up to an election.
He is ready to serve his constituents all year long. ​ Alan currently serves as a local board member of the United States Selective Service System for the State of New Mexico.
His lifelong passion for service is what moved Alan to continue serving the people of House District 23.
