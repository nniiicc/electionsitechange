Answers to the many questions I get asked Who won the 2020 presidential election?
Will you certify election results?
Joe Biden won the 2020 election.
I’ve said that consistently, including in debates.
I have always said I would certify the election results that Biden won. ⸻ What distinguishes you from your opponent?
I believe representation means listening first and putting Vermont ahead of politics.
My career has been about serving others—not building a political career.
I have led soldiers in combat, worked with allies around the world, and helped businesses create jobs.
I will work with anyone, Republican, Democrat, or Independent, if it benefits Vermont.
My focus is on results, not partisan headlines. ⸻ What are your top three priorities if elected?
Make Vermont more affordable by reducing inflation, lowering energy costs, and cutting unnecessary federal regulations.
Strengthen Vermont’s economy by supporting farmers, small businesses, manufacturers, and expanding workforce opportunities.
Improve public safety by securing our borders, supporting law enforcement, and ensuring our communities remain safe while protecting constitutional rights. ⸻ Where do you stand on abortion?
Women have a right to abortion in Vermont.
Vermont protects the right to abortion under state law, and its Constitution protects personal reproductive liberty. ⸻ How would you support small businesses in Vermont?
Small businesses are the backbone of Vermont’s economy.
I would work to reduce burdensome federal regulations, simplify the tax code, expand access to capital, improve broadband and infrastructure, and fight for policies that allow family-owned businesses, farms, and entrepreneurs to compete without unnecessary government interference. ⸻ What should Congress do, if anything, about inflation?
Congress needs to stop overspending.
Inflation is driven in part by excessive government spending, supply chain disruptions, and higher energy costs.
We need responsible budgeting, affordable domestic energy, and policies that encourage production rather than increasing costs for families and businesses. ⸻ Do you support Donald Trump’s tariffs on Canada?
No.
Canada is our neighbor and an important partner for Vermont.
We should stay at the negotiating table, resolve disputes, and avoid raising costs for Vermont families and businesses. ⸻ What should Congress do, if anything, about the national debt?
The national debt threatens future generations.
Congress must return to responsible budgeting by reducing waste, eliminating fraud and abuse, prioritizing essential programs, and evaluating every dollar spent.
Fiscal responsibility should not be partisan—it should be common sense. ⸻ Do you support war with Iran I’ve been in combat, I’ve served in Europe, Asia, the Persian Gulf, and the Middle East.
Military action must be a last resort for national security interests and have clear objectives, endstate, and exit plan.
I do not believe this administration thought through the consequences.
I pray for peace and support serious diplomacy immediately to prevent a wider war and lower energy prices. ⸻ How would you create or protect jobs in Vermont?
I want Vermont to become a place where young people can build careers instead of feeling they have to leave.
That means supporting manufacturing, agriculture, forestry, tourism, technology, and skilled trades while reducing unnecessary regulations that discourage employers from investing here. ⸻ What changes would you make to the U.S. healthcare system?
Healthcare should be affordable, transparent, and accessible to everyone.
I support reducing administrative costs, expanding telehealth, protecting rural hospitals, and giving patients more control over their healthcare decisions.
Washington should help remove barriers, not create new ones. ⸻ How would you address the rising cost of prescription drugs?
Americans should not be denied lifesaving medications.
We should increase transparency, encourage competition through faster approval of generics and address practices that unfairly limit competition while protecting innovation. ⸻ How can healthcare access in rural or underserved regions be strengthened?
Rural communities need stronger healthcare infrastructure.
That includes supporting rural hospitals, expanding telemedicine, investing in broadband, strengthening the healthcare workforce, and ensuring federal reimbursement policies don’t disadvantage rural providers. ⸻ What role should the federal government play in public health?
The federal government should provide research, coordination during national emergencies, and support for states.
Public health decisions should be guided by science, transparency, and respect for individual liberties.
States and local communities should maintain flexibility whenever possible. ⸻ What changes, if any, would you make to the legal immigration system?
Our legal immigration system should be faster, more efficient, and responsive to America’s workforce needs.
We should reduce unnecessary delays while maintaining thorough security screening.
Legal immigration has always strengthened our country when properly managed. ⸻ How should the federal government address undocumented immigrants currently living in the U.S.?
We must first secure the border and enforce existing immigration laws.
Individuals who have committed violent crimes should be prioritized for removal.
Long-term solutions should be debated through Congress, but future policy must not encourage additional illegal immigration or place unfair financial burdens on American taxpayers. ⸻ Where do you see opportunities for bipartisan immigration reform?
There is broad agreement that America needs secure borders, an improved legal immigration system, and better technology at ports of entry.
We should also address workforce needs in industries like agriculture while maintaining the rule of law. ⸻ What do you see as the greatest national security threats facing the United States?
China’s growing influence, cyberattacks, terrorism, border security, and the growing national debt all threaten America’s long-term security.
Strong national defense begins with economic strength, secure borders, and supporting our military while caring for veterans. ⸻ What is your position on January 6?
I’ve been consistent; anyone who broke the law on January 6 should be held accountable.
Violence against law enforcement and attempts to disrupt the peaceful transfer of power are wrong.
The same standard of accountability should apply to everyone. ⸻ How should Congress balance defense spending with other priorities?
National defense is a constitutional responsibility and must remain strong.
At the same time, every taxpayer dollar should be spent wisely.
We should eliminate waste while ensuring our military has the training, technology, and readiness necessary to deter our adversaries. ⸻ Under what circumstances should the U.S. become involved in foreign conflicts or go to war?
Military force should always be a last resort.
When diplomacy fails and America’s national security, treaty obligations, or citizens are directly threatened, we must act decisively with clear objectives, congressional oversight, and a defined strategy. ⸻ Do you support the widespread use of Flock cameras and surveillance of private citizens, for example, as included in H.R.
3684 (2021)?
Public safety matters, but so do constitutional rights.
I support technology that helps solve serious crimes when used with strong oversight, transparency, and safeguards against abuse.
Americans should never be subject to unnecessary government surveillance or warrantless tracking. ⸻ What is your opinion on the overseas manufacturing of American consumer products?
America should make more products at home whenever possible.
Rebuilding domestic manufacturing strengthens our economy, creates good-paying jobs, improves supply chain security, and reduces dependence on foreign adversaries.
Vermont manufacturers should have the opportunity to compete and grow. ⸻ What should the federal government’s role be in education?
Education decisions are best made by parents, teachers, and local communities.
The federal government should support states through funding, research, career and technical education, and special education while avoiding one-size-fits-all mandates. ⸻ What is your stance on climate change and policies to decrease carbon emissions?
Climate change is an issue we should pursue practical solutions that protect both our environment and our economy.
Innovation, cleaner technologies, responsible forestry, and American-made energy solutions can reduce emissions without placing excessive costs on working families or driving jobs overseas. ⸻ What balance should be struck between environmental protection and economic growth?
We don’t have to choose between protecting the environment and growing our economy.
Good policy encourages innovation, supports conservation, and allows businesses, farmers, and manufacturers to succeed while protecting Vermont’s natural resources for future generations. ⸻ Do you support congressional term limits?
Why or why not?
Yes.
I support reasonable congressional term limits because fresh perspectives improve government and encourage accountability.
Public service should be about serving the country—not building a lifelong political career. ⸻ In your view, what is the biggest challenge facing Vermont?
Affordability.
Too many families, seniors, young professionals, and small businesses are struggling with rising costs.
If we don’t reverse that trend, Vermont will continue losing people, workers, and economic opportunity. ⸻ How would you bring more federal resources to Vermont?
A member of Congress should aggressively advocate for Vermont.
I will work with federal agencies, committee members, and colleagues from both parties to ensure Vermont competes for infrastructure funding, broadband expansion, rural healthcare investments, agricultural programs, veterans’ services, and economic development grants.
Bringing federal dollars home requires relationships, persistence, and showing up.
