Early voting centers will be open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM , including on Saturday and Sunday.
Anyone in line at 8 pm will be allowed to vote.
You can find your early voting location HERE .
If you’re not yet registered to vote, you can still register in-person during early voting.
To register to vote, go to an early voting center in your county and bring a government document with your address (this can be your MVA-issued license, ID card, change of address card, paycheck, bank statement, or utility bill.).
Vote by Mail Request a mail-in ballot !
You can return your ballot by mail or at one of the many drop box locations, which can be found here .
Please note the following deadlines: To receive your ballot by mail or fax, you must submit your request by Tuesday, June 16, 2026 .
To receive your ballot as an emailed link, you must submit your request by Friday, June 19, 2026 .
Please make sure mail-in ballots are postmarked or dropped in a drop box no later than 8:00 PM on Tuesday, June 23rd !
Voting on Election Day You can vote in-person on Election Day (Tuesday, June 23rd) by going to your assigned polling place between 7:00 AM and 8:00 PM .
If you have any questions or need help getting to your polling location, please email info@jamieraskin.com.
