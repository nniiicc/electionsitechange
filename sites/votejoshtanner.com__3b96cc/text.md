“Strong, conservative and decisive leadership on behalf of all Idahoans” Josh Tanner is an Idaho native, Conservative Republican and a patriot who believes that God has given our country a unique blessing and responsibility to uphold for future generations.
He resides in Eagle, ID with his wife and two young children.
Josh Tanner is currently Eagle Fire Commissioner and has served as Chairman of the Eagle Fire District since 2018.
He received his education at Boise State University, achieving an Executive MBA and a Bachelor of Science in Construction Management.
He is a local business owner with an entrepreneurial spirit, who loves to be in the great outdoors of Idaho with his family.
He has a strong desire to serve and represent Idahoans by ensuring a limited, yet effective, and fiscally responsible government that preserves our traditional values and great resources.
