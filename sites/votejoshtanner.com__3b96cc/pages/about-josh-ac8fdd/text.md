He believes that God has given our country a unique blessing and a responsibility to uphold it for future generations.
Josh Tanner is currently your State Representative for District 14, serving on the budget and resource committees.
He works hard to ensure government is limited, effective, and fiscally responsible.
Since 2018, Josh Tanner has served as Chairman of the Eagle Fire Commissioners, making sure that tax dollars are spent wisely in the pursuit of public safety.
Josh Tanner has an entrepreneurial spirit, earning an Executive MBA from Boise State University and starting multiple businesses in Eagle.
He loves Idaho’s great outdoors and spends as much time as possible out there with his wife and three children.
