Men in Washington DC are voting to exclude the cost of reproductive health services, including prenatal care and delivering babies from coverage under insurance plans.
Locally, Dr.
Enoch was one of the organizers of the 2020 Women’s March that for the past few years has been on the frontlines fighting for women’s reproductive rights and women’s right to make their own healthcare choices.
Dr.
Enoch believes that healthcare decisions should be made between a woman and her doctor, including decisions concerning women’s reproductive health.
The incident that Dr.
Enoch discusses in this video where old white men were deciding women’s health care was an article that appeared in the Huffington Post.
Out of that meeting, these men decided that insurance shouldn’t cover the cost of maternity care, but that it was important that viagra be covered.
We need a representative that will not put the needs of women in the background, while ensuring their own wellbeing.
