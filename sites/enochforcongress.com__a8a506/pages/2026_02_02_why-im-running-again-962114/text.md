Why I’m Running Again…
I’m running for office again because I believe our country deserves thoughtful leadership, accountability in government, and a renewed commitment to the people our system is meant to serve.
Throughout my career in business and technology, I’ve worked to build solutions, manage complex challenges, and bring people together to solve problems.
Those experiences have shown me that good leadership requires responsibility, transparency, and the courage to make decisions that put the public interest first.
Today, many Americans feel that their voices are no longer being heard in Washington.
Too often, politics has become about power rather than service.
I believe government should work for the people it represents—protecting our freedoms, strengthening our communities, and creating opportunities for future generations.
I’m running because I believe we can restore common sense, responsible leadership, and respect for the rule of law.
That means defending our Constitution, making thoughtful decisions about national security, supporting economic growth and innovation, and ensuring that government remains accountable to the people.
This campaign is about building a stronger future—one that prioritizes responsibility, opportunity, and the well-being of the American people.
I’m running to serve, to lead with integrity, and to ensure that our government once again reflects the values and interests of the people it represents.
