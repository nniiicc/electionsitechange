As a mother of four and grandmother of ten, I've seen firsthand how important strong schools, engaged families, and dedicated teachers are to preparing the next generation for success.
Supporting Education Wyoming's public schools are an important part of our communities, and they deserve strong support.
I will work with local school boards and teachers to ensure our schools have the resources they need while making sure education funding is spent responsibly and focused on helping students succeed.
I also believe families should have educational options that fit their children's needs .
Parents should have the ability to stay informed, ask questions, and be involved in the decisions that affect their children's education.
I will support policies that strengthen Wyoming's schools, protect parents' rights, and keep education decisions as close to our local communities as possible.
Local schools, parents, and educators understand the needs of their students better, and I believe they should have the flexibility to make the decisions that best serve Wyoming families.
During the last legislative session, I enjoyed hosting Girls State representatives from Johnson County School District #1 at the Wyoming State Capitol.
I had the chance to show them the House of Representatives floor in Cheyenne and visit with these impressive young women.
