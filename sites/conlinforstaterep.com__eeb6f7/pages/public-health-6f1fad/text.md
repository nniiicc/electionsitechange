Priorities
Public Health
No citizen who has the misfortune of suffering from a pre-existing condition or a sudden decline in health should have to choose between going into debt or leaving a condition untreated.
Jennifer wants to:
- Fund more drug treatment centers to address the opioid crisis
- Invest in mental health clinics to help those suffering from depression and anxiety
- Support wellness initiatives for residents, including the many veterans who live in our district
- Lower the cost of prescription drugs to help seniors and those with chronic illnesses
- Support initiatives around gun safety, such as the safe storage of firearms to limit violence and suicide
