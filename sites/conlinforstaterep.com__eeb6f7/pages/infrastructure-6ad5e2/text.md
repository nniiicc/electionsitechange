Priorities
Infrastructure
District 48 has a variety of infrastructure challenges that, if fixed, will not just help residents in their daily lives, but also help the district and state economically.
Jennifer wants to:
- Invest in repairing and maintaining our roads and bridges at all levels
- Increase access to high-speed, affordable internet
- Renovate our schools, including improving air filtering systems
- Upgrade our water infrastructure systems to alleviate flooding and PFAS contamination
- Create well-paid, local jobs around sustainable infrastructure initiatives
