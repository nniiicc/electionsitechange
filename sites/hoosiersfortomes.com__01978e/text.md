Jim Tomes For Indiana State Senate
I am Jim Tomes and I am the Republican candidate for re-election as your Indiana State Senator in District 49.
I believe in limited government, I will uphold and defend our constitution and will work hard for the people to maintain their freedoms our Creator & great Nation have bestowed upon them.
Since my election to the Indiana Senate in 2010, I have worked tirelessly to give the the citizens of Indiana a voice in Indianapolis.
With your support, I will continue addressing the concerns and needs (large or small) of the people of Posey and Vanderburgh counties every day of the year.
Indiana’s 49th Senate district contains all of Posey County and portions of
Vanderburgh County.
