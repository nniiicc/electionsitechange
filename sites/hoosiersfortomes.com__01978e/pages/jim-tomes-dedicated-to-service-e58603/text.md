Senator Jim Tomes has spent a lifetime serving his country, his community, and the working families of Senate District 49.
A U.S.
Army veteran who served with the 101st Airborne Division during the Vietnam War, Jim learned early on the true meaning of duty, discipline, and sacrifice.
That same sense of duty guided his 33-year career as a professional truck driver and union steward.
Unlike career politicians who spend their lives in administrative offices, Jim understands the value of hard work, long hours, and keeping the American economy moving forward.
As Chair of the Senate Committee on Veterans Affairs and The Military, Jim brings his firsthand service experience to the Statehouse.
He leads the charge to ensure Indiana’s service members, veterans, and their families receive the support, tax relief, and resources they have earned.
His leadership is not rooted in political ambition, but in a proven track record of service to his nation and state.
Deeply Community Focused and Always Accessible For Senator Jim Tomes, representing Posey County and Vanderburgh County isn’t just a title—it’s a personal commitment.
Raised and grounded in Southwest Indiana alongside his wife, Margie, Jim has lived the same values, faced the same challenges, and shared the same hopes as his neighbors.
Jim believes that elected officials work for the people, not special interest groups.
That’s why he makes responsiveness the core of his constituent service.
Whether it’s answering phone calls, responding directly to emails, or meeting with local residents at community gatherings, Jim ensures every Hoosier voice in District 49 is heard.
His community focus translates into legislative action: Defending Local Property Rights: Jim fought to protect landowners from overreach by ensuring property owners have fair, independent options when navigating state floodplain regulations.
Standing Up for Local Workers: Drawing from his decades as a union steward, Jim has never hesitated to put local workers and working families ahead of party bosses.
Supporting Law Enforcement and First Responders: As Ranking Member of the Homeland Security and Transportation Committee, Jim championed pay increases for Indiana State Police and local officers to keep communities safe.
From his active involvement as a member and former commander of Wadesville VFW Post 6576 to serving as a Eucharistic minister at St.
Wendel Catholic Church, Jim’s faith, family, and community guide everything he does.
Driven by a Commitment to Indiana’s Future To keep Indiana moving forward, we need a clear vision for economic growth, educational excellence, and constitutional freedom.
Senator Jim Tomes has consistently delivered conservative results that build a stronger, more prosperous future for Indiana.
Protecting the Hoosier Wallet Jim supported landmark tax cuts dropping Indiana’s income tax to 2.9%, putting over $1 billion back into the hands of Hoosier taxpayers.
He championed total state income tax exemptions on military pay for active duty and National Guard personnel and reduced tax burdens for small businesses.
Investing in the Next Generation Jim knows that Indiana’s future depends on the strength of our schools and families.
He helped deliver record increases in K-12 education funding, eliminated textbook fees for families, increased funding for career and technical vocational training, and secured vital funding for school safety grants.
Preserving Our Constitutional Freedoms A nationally recognized defender of constitutional rights, Jim founded the 2nd Amendment Patriots grassroots organization and has spent decades protecting the individual liberties guaranteed by the U.S. and Indiana constitutions.
The Clear Choice for District 49 Southwest Indiana needs a senator who stands tall for conservative principles, works tirelessly for working families, and never forgets where he came from.
Senator Jim Tomes has proven that real leadership means putting people first, protecting hard-earned tax dollars, and safeguarding the values that make Indiana great.
Join us in keeping a proven leader, dedicated veteran, and true champion for our community in the Indiana State Senate.
