Meet Mike Kennedy Congressman Mike Kennedy is a family physician, attorney, and father who has spent his career serving others and standing up for Utah families.
As a doctor practicing medicine in Utah County for more than two decades, Mike has seen firsthand how Washington’s failure to control inflation, secure our border, or restore fiscal discipline makes life harder for everyday Americans.
Before being elected to Congress, Mike served ten years in Utah’s part-time State Legislature, where he built a strong conservative record focused on fiscal responsibility, limited government, and protecting individual liberty.
