Meet Mike Kennedy
Congressman Mike Kennedy is a family physician, attorney, and father who has spent his career serving others and standing up for Utah families.
As a doctor practicing medicine in Utah County for more than two decades, Mike has seen firsthand how Washington’s failure to control inflation, secure our border, or restore fiscal discipline makes life harder for everyday Americans.
Before being elected to Congress, Mike served ten years in Utah’s part-time State Legislature, where he built a strong conservative record focused on fiscal responsibility, limited government, and protecting individual liberty.
In Congress, Mike is working to lower costs for families, strengthen the economy, secure our border, and ensure Utah and America remain strong, prosperous, and free.
Saturday at Representative Kristen Chevrier’s Celebrate America picnic and along with Senator Brady Brammer, I was glad to join neighbors and hear from people across our community.
We may not see every issue the same way, but we can still come together, put differences aside, and celebrate the freedoms and shared ideals that make America great.
Every vote cast in Washington helps shape the freedom, opportunities and future our children and grandchildren will inherit.
I take that responsibility seriously, and I’m honored to receive an A grade from Moms for America Action for my proven record of defending faith, family, freedom and parental rights.
I’m working to protect our Constitution, support families, and make Washington smaller and Utah stronger.
As a father and grandfather, I want the America we leave behind to be every bit as free and full of opportunity as the one we inherited.
