About Senate District 13
Senate District 13 covers communities across east Alabama, including Cleburne, Clay, Randolph, Chambers, and the eastern part of Lee counties.
This district is made up of small towns, rural communities, proud schools, farms, small businesses, and many church communities that form the heart of Rural Alabama.
Jamie Forsyth is committed to representing every community in District 13 with honesty, fairness, and strong rural values.
