GET TO KNOW
GIGI
GiGi Hayes is an attorney, educator, wife, and mother who has spent her career advocating for families, justice, and opportunity.
A proud lifelong resident of Jefferson County, GiGi understands the challenges facing our communities because she lives them every day.
GiGi currently serves as a licensed attorney in the State of Alabama and as a professor at Miles Law School, where she educates and mentors future leaders.
Her professional experience spans legal practice, business management, and community education, giving her a unique perspective on how policy impacts real people.
She is deeply committed to accessible leadership, transparency, and service rooted in community needs.
