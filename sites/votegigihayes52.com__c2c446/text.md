Attorney GiGi Hayes is running for Alabama House of Representatives District 52 because our communities deserve leadership that listens, shows up, and delivers real results.
With a proven record as an attorney, educator, and community advocate, GiGi is committed to building safe neighborhoods, strong schools, and real economic opportunity for families across Jefferson County.
Key Issues
Safe & Thriving Communities
Public safety starts with strong neighborhoods.
GiGi will invest in mental health care, trauma support, community driven safety solutions, and infrastructure improvements like lighting and sidewalks.
Health & Home Stability
Everyone deserves affordable healthcare and a safe place to call home.
GiGi will fight to expand Medicaid, lower healthcare costs, and increase access to affordable housing.
Economic Mobility & Workforce Opportunity
GiGi supports job training, small businesses, and financial literacy so working families can build real, lasting wealth.
Strong Public Schools
Strong schools build strong communities.
GiGi will advocate for fully funded schools, competitive teacher pay, and expanded STEM and career pathways.
