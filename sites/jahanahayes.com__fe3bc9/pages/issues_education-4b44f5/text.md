Education
As a Member of the Committee on Education and Workforce and the 2016 National Teacher of the Year, Congresswoman Hayes continues to prioritize equitable, affordable, and high-quality education for all students.
As a teacher in Waterbury Public Schools, she saw how sustained federal investment is necessary to ensure all students receive a free and appropriate education.
This includes supporting teacher preparation and pipeline programs and fully funding the Individuals with Disabilities Education Act.
