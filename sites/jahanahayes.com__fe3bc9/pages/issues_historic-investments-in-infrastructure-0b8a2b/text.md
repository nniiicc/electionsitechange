Infrastructure
Congresswoman Jahana Hayes has brought back over $1 billion in real infrastructure investments to the 5th Congressional District of Connecticut.
From highways and bridges to sidewalk improvements, and water infrastructure, Congresswoman Hayes has remained committed to ensuring all parts of the district share in these investments.
For years, towns across the district saw crumbling, unsafe infrastructure.
Congresswoman Hayes has secured direct funding for towns and municipalities to address their infrastructure needs.
