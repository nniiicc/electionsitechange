Labor
As a long-time union member, Congresswoman Hayes knows strong unions protect the rights of workers.
She has been a supporter of the Protecting the Right to Organize Act, a comprehensive proposal to protect workers’ right to come together and bargain for higher wages, better benefits, and safer workplaces.
Workers should have the right to collectively bargain and negotiate with their employers if they see fit, and Congresswoman Hayes plans to continue to support those rights.
