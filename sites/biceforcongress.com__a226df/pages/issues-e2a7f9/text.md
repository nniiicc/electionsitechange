Supporting Veterans.
Congresswoman Bice authored the “Protecting Military Veteran Families with Disabilities Act, ” which was signed into law.
She continues to champion legislation that expands veteran access to high-quality healthcare, job training, and education benefits.
With over 300,000 veterans in Oklahoma, Bice is helping ensure each of them receives the support and respect they’ve earned.
Defending the Border.
Defending the Border.
Congresswoman Bice is committed to maintaining order at the southern border.
She successfully passed an amendment redirecting $25 million in unused Afghan Security Forces funds to the National Guard, bolstering their ability to assist U.S.
Customs and Border Protection.
Bice supports common-sense immigration reforms and stronger enforcement to protect national security and uphold the rule of law.
Backing the U.S.
Constitution.
Backing the U.S.
Constitution.
Congresswoman Bice has a proven track record of defending our constitutional rights.
In Congress, she has opposed unconstitutional mandates and supported legislation that protects freedom of speech, religious liberty, and due process.
She is a strong defender of the Second Amendment and continues to oppose efforts that threaten the rights of law- abiding gun owners.
Cutting Federal Spending.
Cutting Federal Spending.
Congresswoman Bice is working to rein in Washington’s out-of-control spending and bring fiscal responsibility back to the federal government.
She introduced legislation to eliminate duplicative federal grant programs and streamline funding across agencies.
Bice is a strong supporter of eliminating waste, fraud and abuse in federal programs and continues to push for accountability in how taxpayer dollars are spent.
Securing American Energy.
Securing American Energy.
Congresswoman Bice is a national leader in the fight to strengthen American energy independence.
She supports Oklahoma’s oil and gas industry which is responsible for producing over 10,000 barrels of oil per day and reduces our reliance on hostile foreign nations.
Bice opposes regulations that threaten domestic energy production and American jobs.
Strengthening Oklahoma Agriculture.
Strengthening Oklahoma Agriculture.
Congresswoman Bice recognizes agriculture as an essential pillar of Oklahoma’s economy.
She supports policies that cut unnecessary regulations, create new opportunities for ag products, and help farmers and ranchers to grow.
Bice is proud to be endorsed by the Oklahoma Farm Bureau and continues to advocate for those who feed and fuel the nation.
Standing with Oklahoma Families.
Standing with Oklahoma Families.
Congresswoman Bice is leading a bipartisan effort in Congress to encourage employers to expand access to paid family leave.
Her legislation encourages small businesses to offer this benefit by expanding existing tax credits, helping new parents take time off without financial strain.
A mother of two, Bice knows parents shouldn’t have to choose between a paycheck and caring for a newborn or adopted child.
Serving OK-05.
Serving OK-05.
Congresswoman Bice is committed to delivering results for Oklahoma’s Fifth Congressional District.
She’s helped over 3,000 constituents resolve issues with federal agencies and continues to cut through burdensome red tape.
In Congress, she has introduced 73 bills and co-sponsored 690 more.
Bice continues to help constituents navigate the bureaucracy while improving resources that benefit the district.
