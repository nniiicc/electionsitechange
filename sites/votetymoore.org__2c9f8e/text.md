Ty Moore
FOR OHIO
Ty Moore isn’t a career politician; he’s a neighbor, a local business owner, and a family man who has spent his life serving our community.
For nine years, he was a Noble County Commissioner, known for his dedication to community development and responsive local governance.
As a third-generation owner of R.C.
Moore Lumber Company, he knows what it takes to build a business and support a thriving local economy.
“I will always be grateful to the citizens of Noble County for allowing me to be their commissioner.
The experience has been very rewarding and has prepared me for my next endeavor.
The people of the 95th district will know they have someone working hard for them in Columbus.” - Ty Moore
Ty Moore is the State Representative for Ohio’s 95th House District, bringing a wealth of experience in public service and business leadership to the Statehouse.
