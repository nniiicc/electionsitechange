Katie has served as the Legislative Counsel to Congressman Riley Moore (WV-02), where she helped craft legislation rooted in West Virginia values.
Before returning home to serve her own Congressman, she worked as a Policy Advisor to Virginia Governor Glenn Youngkin, contributing to one of the most energetic reform-minded administrations in the country.
Katie's experience in public service is extensive and spans some of the most demanding political environments in America.
She served as an intern in the White House Office of Press and Communications, and subsequently served on the Trump Campaign's Regional Communications Team in 2020, where she was Regional Communications Coordinator for the Ohio Valley.
Katie also proudly represented her home as Miss Berkeley County in 2015, a title that helped spark her commitment to serve and uplift the community she loves.
Katie is a graduate of George Mason University and the University of Richmond School of Law.
My Priorities Bring high-paying, family-supporting jobs to West Virginia Berkeley County is growing — and Katie Rose is committed to making sure that growth translates into real economic opportunity.
She'll fight to attract industries that offer good wages, strong benefits, and careers that let West Virginia families thrive without leaving home.
Expand school choice so families have access to great educational options Every child deserves a great education, and every family deserves the freedom to choose what that looks like.
Katie supports expanding school choice so parents — not bureaucrats — decide what's best for their kids.
Invest in water, sewer, roads, and bridges to keep up with local growth Berkeley County is one of the fastest-growing areas in West Virginia, and our infrastructure needs to keep pace.
Katie will prioritize the roads, bridges, water, and sewer systems that keep our communities safe, connected, and ready for the future.
Recruit and retain top firefighters, police officers, and teachers Safe communities and strong schools depend on the people who show up every day to serve them.
Katie will fight for the pay, resources, and support our first responders and teachers deserve — so Berkeley County can attract and keep the best.
Cut taxes to keep more money in the pockets of hardworking West Virginians West Virginians work hard for what they earn.
Katie believes government should take less of it.
She'll push for commonsense tax relief that puts money back where it belongs — with the families and small businesses that earned it.
