Join the Campaign Donate About Edwin A Washington Native Fighting for You Edwin Feller grew up on a Whatcom County dairy farm, where he learned the value of hard work, discipline, and fiscal responsibility.
His parents taught him to love God; to love America; and to love his neighbors.
Politics in our state and our country have become wrapped in partisan ideology.
Feller believes true representation is not bound by party, but to the ideals and inspirations of the district we call home.
Washington District 2 deserves more than a life-long ribbon-cutter who shows up like clockwork every two years begging to be re-elected.
Feller will bring a common sense approach to Congress focused on responsible economics and accountable public safety.
We Washingtonians are in a competition with the rest of the country and the world, and the current leadership in our state and the 2nd Congressional District has us headed in the wrong direction.
He supports robust law enforcement, secure borders, fair elections, and balanced budgets.
Feller supports Title IX protections for women and girls.
He believes that our first responders deserve respect, appreciation, and support.
He adheres to the principles of sovereignty for the individual and the nation, and that the rule of law protects those principles.
Feller believes that the affordability crisis is self-inflicted due to taxes being too high, and the government has grown too big.
Law & Order Fiscal Responsibility Family Values Secure Borders The Issues Five Pillars for Washington's Future Common sense policies focused on safety, prosperity, and protecting our families.
Public Safety Safe Streets Mandate Implementing a federal Safe Streets mandate tying grants to local cooperation, designating cartels as foreign terrorist organizations, and ending sanctuary policies to restore law and order in our communities.
Energy Sovereignty Cascadian Independence Dismantling radical environmental mandates, streamlining energy permits, expanding nuclear and hydroelectric capacity, and repealing federal carbon taxes to deliver affordable, reliable American-made energy.
Protect Women's Sports Biological Integrity in Athletics Codifying that "sex" in Title IX refers exclusively to biological sex, ensuring fairness and safety for female athletes across our collegiate and high school programs.
Protect Our Children Child Protection Act Implementing a federal ban on irreversible medical procedures for minors, prioritizing long-term psychological health and parental rights over ideological experimentation.
Merit-Based Immigration End Chain Migration Overhauling immigration to prioritize nuclear families and a merit-based points system, ensuring legal immigration serves American workers and our local economy.
