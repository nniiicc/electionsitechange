Housing Shouldn’t Cost a Fortune 🏠
I recently met with Mia and Fabian, a young couple from Fresno who’ve been renting for years, working hard and saving every dollar they can in hopes of buying their first home.
Stories like Mia and Fabian’s aren’t rare; they're a reflection of what too many families across our community are facing.
Every family deserves a safe, affordable place to call home, but for far too many in the Central Valley, that’s becoming impossible.
According to the Fresno Bee, nearly 63% of renter households in Fresno are considered "rent-burdened," meaning they spend more than 30% of their income on housing.
Fresno Bee
With rents rising faster than wages, families are being forced to make painful choices every month: stay in a cramped space they can barely afford, or move even farther from work and school, adding hours to their day.
