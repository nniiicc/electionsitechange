State Assemblymember Mark Gonzalez Endorses Annalisa Perea for State Assembly
District 54 Assemblymember Gonzalez throws his support behind Councilmember Perea for District 31.
FRESNO, CA – Councilmember Annalisa Perea's campaign for Assembly District 31 has secured the endorsement of District 54 Assemblymember Mark Gonzalez.
Assemblymember Gonzalez joins a growing coalition of leaders backing Perea’s campaign, such as Speaker of the Assembly Rivas, State Senator Anna Cabellero, and State Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria.
This endorsement underscores the deep trust that Central Valley's most respected public servants have in Perea’s commitment to District 31.
“Assemblymember Gonzalez's support means a lot to me," said Councilmember Perea.
"When leaders from across California believe in what we are building here in District 31, it tells me we are on the right track."
Annalisa Perea currently serves on the Fresno City Council, where she has led efforts to expand affordable housing, invest in public safety, and strengthen workforce development programs.
A certified city planner and former community college trustee, Perea carries forward a lifelong belief in education, equity, and opportunity for every family.
