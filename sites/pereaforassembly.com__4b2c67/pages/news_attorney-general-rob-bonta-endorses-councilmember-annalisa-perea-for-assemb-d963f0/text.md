8 2026 PRESS RELEASE Attorney General Rob Bonta Endorses Councilmember Annalisa Perea for Assembly Fresno City Council member is honored and grateful to receive the support of one of California’s most accomplished leaders FRESNO, CA - Attorney General Rob Bonta has endorsed Fresno City Councilmember Annalisa Perea for Assembly District 31.
The support from one of California’s top-ranking and most respected leaders highlights Annalisa Perea’s readiness to serve the people of Assembly District 31.
Attorney General Bonta emphasized Councilmember Perea’s effectiveness as the reason for his endorsement.
“I have had the chance to partner with Councilmember Perea on several occasions.
During our work together she was laser focused on getting things done for the people of Fresno.
Councilmember Perea is an effective consensus builder and will be a strong leader for the Central Valley the moment she joins the Assembly - that is why I am endorsing her candidacy.” Councilmember Perea expressed deep gratitude for Attorney General Bonta’s support.
“I am deeply grateful and honored to have the trust and support of a public servant who has accomplished so much for the people of California.
If Assembly District 31 voters decide to send me to the legislature, I will look forward to working with Attorney General Bonta to keep our towns and neighborhoods safe.” Annalisa Perea currently serves on the Fresno City Council and was elected Council President in 2024.
A lifelong Central Valley resident and certified city planner, Perea’s work has focused on delivering results for working families, expanding opportunity, and supporting those who serve our communities and our nation.
