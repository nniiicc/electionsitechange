Dr.
Sam Alexander and his wife of 39 years, Marsha, live on their farm in Fair Play, MO.
They have three daughters, four grandchildren, and four great-grandsons.
A board-certified Emergency Room Physician for over 30 years, Dr.
Alexander serves as a senior partner with the Emergency Physicians of Springfield, affiliated with Cox Health Systems.
He earned his BA/MD from UMKC Medical School in 1982.
Heavily involved in medical career outreach programs for over twenty-five years, Dr.
Alexander has mentored medical students, residents, and first responders; he has been a non-paid director of rural Southwest Missouri fire and rescue districts for over thirty years.
Sam has been a pilot since 1984 and is an active farmer, managing family land on the Gasconade River, a ranch, and a hay farm in Fair Play, Missouri.
Sam and Marsha enjoy spending time with family hunting, shooting sports, fishing, spending time on the water, hiking, and reading about American history.
