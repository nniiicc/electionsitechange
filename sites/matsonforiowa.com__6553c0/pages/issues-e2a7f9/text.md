Elections should be about forward-thinking ideas – a vision for where we want our state to be and enacting policies that will work to get us there.
We need elected leaders who are willing to engage in hard conversations across party lines with each other and with the people in our communities.
Honest conversations – built from trusted partnerships – are needed now more than ever as we seek to solve some very big challenges and harness tremendous opportunities.
My focus, as always, is on ensuring economic security and economic opportunity for everyone, in every community across Iowa.
Life is about more than surviving, it’s about thriving.
Whether it’s economic development that prioritizes small business entrepreneurship and the uniqueness of each community, committing to making Iowa #1 in public education again, making housing more affordable for homeowners and renters, cleaning our water and protecting our soil, investing in our direct care workforce, or protecting personal health care decisions, we need state policies to ensure Iowans have the opportunity to flourish together.
