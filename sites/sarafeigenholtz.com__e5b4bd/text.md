State Senator Feigenholtz: Fighting for the 6th District
As a kid I grew up on the streets of Lakeview.
While my mother took care of her patients in her North Clark Street office, I went exploring and fell in love with this community.
It’s part of my DNA.
Throughout my time in office, it has transformed from a hard-working industrial corridor to a lively and desirable community known for its vibrant neighborhoods, excellent schools, and well-maintained parks.
I wake up every day ready to fight for our families on the critical issues:
- Education: Ensuring every child, regardless of circumstance, has access to a high-quality education.
- Safety: Working to make our neighborhoods safer by banning assault weapons and advocating for increased resources for law enforcement.
- Healthcare: Guaranteeing access to care and allowing our seniors to age with dignity.
- Human Rights: Leading the successful fight to keep abortion legal in Illinois and advocating for our LGBTQ+ community at every turn.
Representation means listening.
My office conducts a listening tour after every legislative session because your feedback is essential to ensuring our district thrives.
I am committed to earning your continued support by resolving outstanding issues and bringing essential resources to our communities.
I will always fight for you and make sure the 6th district’s voice is heard in Springfield.
Warmest regards,
Sara Feigenholtz, State Senator 6th District
Environment
Climate change is undeniably and rapidly affecting our lives on a local and global scale.
With the Trump administration’s blatant attempt to sabotage the progress our country has made, the Illinois General Assembly must step up and shape the future of renewable energy for Illinois.
Community Safety
Everyone deserves to live in a safe community.
Passing responsible gun legislation is of paramount importance in an age when school shootings, church and synagogue massacres are becoming more and more common.
I fight every day in Springfield to keep illegal guns off our streets and provide law enforcement with the tools they need to prevent and solve crime.
Reproductive Rights
As the daughter of a doctor, fighting for health care access has always been a priority.
Having sole ownership over decision making on matters regarding bodily autonomy is a fundamental human right.
I am proud to have twice received the Illinois Planned Parenthood Profile in Courage Award for passage of the Contraceptive Equity Act and HB40.
