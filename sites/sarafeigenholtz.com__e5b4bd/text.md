While my mother took care of her patients in her North Clark Street office, I went exploring and fell in love with this community.
It’s part of my DNA.
Throughout my time in office, it has transformed from a hard-working industrial corridor to a lively and desirable community known for its vibrant neighborhoods, excellent schools, and well-maintained parks .
I wake up every day ready to fight for our families on the critical issues: Education: Ensuring every child, regardless of circumstance, has access to a high-quality education.
Safety: Working to make our neighborhoods safer by banning assault weapons and advocating for increased resources for law enforcement.
Healthcare: Guaranteeing access to care and allowing our seniors to age with dignity.
Human Rights: Leading the successful fight to keep abortion legal in Illinois and advocating for our LGBTQ+ community at every turn.
Representation means listening.
My office conducts a listening tour after every legislative session because your feedback is essential to ensuring our district thrives.
I am committed to earning your continued support by resolving outstanding issues and bringing essential resources to our communities.
I will always fight for you and make sure the 6th district’s voice is heard in Springfield.
