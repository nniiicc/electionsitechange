Jim grew up in Shoreview and, like his two kids, graduated from Mounds View High School.
He went on to earn degrees from the University of Wisconsin-Madison and Hamline University, where he also served as an adjunct professor of business and public administration.
He has served on the Mounds View School Board since 2019 and has championed many initiatives aimed at improving reading outcomes, providing mental health supports, innovative in-school vocational training, and college credit opportunities saving families more than $4 million in tuition costs.
Jim has been active in Rotary International, The Mounds View Schools Foundation, Mounds View Youth Hockey, North Suburban Soccer Association, Greater Twin Cities Youth Symphonies, the Hamline Business School Advisory Council, and St.
John’s in the Wilderness Church of White Bear Lake.
He and his wife, Channing, have two adult children.
