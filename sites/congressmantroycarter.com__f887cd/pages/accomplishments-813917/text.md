Since being elected, I have dedicated myself to delivering on the promises made to our community.
Here are some of the key successes that showcase our progress.
Smart Criminal Justice Reforms: Delivered tens of millions of dollars in violence prevention programs, nearly $2 million for our local law enforcement, and over $4 million for formerly incarcerated citizens returning to society.
Boosting Local Economy: Secured $300 million to improve the Port of New Orleans, creating jobs and boosting our economy.
Secured 1.3 billion dollars for Hurricane item recovery
Investing in Renewable Energy: Secured over $150 million in solar investments for Louisiana, promoting sustainable energy and job growth.
Rebuilding After Hurricane Ida: Brought home over $3 billion to help our people and businesses rebuild after Hurricane Ida.
Restoring Lincoln Beach: Secured millions of dollars to restore and renovate Lincoln Beach, transforming it into a vibrant community space for thousands of families to enjoy.
Secured 31.5 million dollars in Community Project funding
Advocated in support of the infrastructure law, which will bring at least 7 billion dollars to Louisiana
Official visits from the president, vice president, and over a dozen White House cabinet members and advisors
Supported the American Rescue Plan’s expanded Child Tax Credit which helped 85,000 families in his district
Closed over 800 constituent cases on issues with federal agencies, saving constituents over $500,000
Advocated for stronger EPA enforcement and monitoring
Introduced 9 bills and passed the Small Business Advocacy Improvements Act through the house
