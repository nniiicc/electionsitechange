How to Make Michigan Competitive?
LOWER TAXES
Cut property taxes and stop Wall Street from buying up homes and pricing Michigan families out.
LOWER HEALTHCARE BILLS
Require upfront prices, crack down on healthcare monopoly pricing, and end surprise medical bills.
LOWER UTILITY BILLS
Reverse $1 billion in rate hikes and stop unelected Lansing bureaucrats from approving higher bills.
FIX ROADS WITHOUT RAISING TAXES
Put every penny collected at the pump into roads and end corporate handouts.
PREPARE EVERY CHILD
Restore reading standards, expand skilled-trades education, and protect girls' sports.
PROTECT LOCAL COMMUNITIES
Restore local control and let communities reject massive data centers that threaten farmland and strain utilities.
