“Our struggle in Ohio isn’t between neighbors; it doesn’t matter your skin color, your sexual orientation, or your political affiliation, this is a class struggle between working people and powerful interests that have rigged the system against us.” - Tristan Rader
The divide in Ohio isn’t left vs. right — it’s the many vs. the moneyed few.
Ohio’s economy has been rewritten by and for the wealthy.
Corporations post record profits while families fall further behind.
Workers are told to “tighten their belts” while CEOs fly home on private jets.
This is not the natural order of things — it’s policy.
It’s power.
And it must change.
Healthcare is a human right, and Ohio should lead like it.
Healthcare in America is a marketplace designed to extract as much as possible from people at their most vulnerable.
It’s immoral.
No family should face bankruptcy, ration medicine, or skip care because an insurance company decided their health isn’t profitable enough.
We choose people over polluters — because a livable planet is non-negotiable.
Climate change is the defining struggle of our time.
Communities are breathing polluted air, Lake Erie is under threat, and utilities continue to buy influence while blocking clean energy that would save families money.
Tristan Rader is the State Representative for Cleveland’s Westside and Lakewood, a former at-large city councilman, and a lifelong progressive activist fighting for working people.
He’s married to Dr.
Caitlin Rader, a dedicated family physician who shares his commitment to service and community.
Together, they volunteer locally and work to lift up the neighborhoods they call home.
Tristan is also a clean-energy professional who has spent years helping Ohio families save money and gain control over their power.
Whether organizing neighbors, challenging corporate influence, or crafting policy at the Statehouse, Tristan has always stood firmly with everyday people — and he’s committed to building a future where all Ohioans can thrive.
