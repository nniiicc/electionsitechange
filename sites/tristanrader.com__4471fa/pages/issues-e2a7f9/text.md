Wages haven’t kept up with the cost of living, rent is through the roof, childcare is unaffordable, and working families are being squeezed from every direction while CEOs and corporations post record profits.
This didn’t happen by accident; it happened because the rules were rewritten to benefit the wealthy and well-connected.
An economy for all means rewriting those rules back in favor of the people who actually make this state run: the workers, parents, seniors, and small business owners fighting every day for a fair shot.
In an Ohio that works for everyone, not just the wealthy few: Workers have real power through strong unions, collective bargaining, and the right to stand together without fear or retaliation.
Housing, childcare, and education are treated as public priorities, not luxury goods available only to those who can afford them.
Public dollars serve the public good, instead of being siphoned off into corporate tax shelters, utility slush funds, and giveaways to the already powerful.
Billion-dollar corporations finally pay what they owe, instead of freeloading off the people who clean their floors, staff their stores, and keep their businesses running.
Every Ohioan can live with dignity, not just scrape by paycheck to paycheck in an economy designed to favor the top one percent.
Building an economy for all means standing up to the interests that have rigged the system, and standing with the working people who deserve better.
That’s the fight we’re taking on every day.

Healthcare for All Healthcare must be a human right, especially in the richest country in the world.
No Ohioan should delay care, accrue crushing debt, or watch a loved one suffer simply because coverage is unaffordable or out of reach.
A Vision for Universal Coverage Rep.
Tristan Rader is a lead sponsor of the Ohio Health Care Plan, a comprehensive proposal to establish universal health care coverage for every resident of Ohio.
This plan charts a path toward a system that ensures all Ohioans can access care, from preventive visits to emergency services, mental health support, vision and dental care, without fear of financial ruin.
The proposal reflects a belief that access to health care is fundamental to human dignity and well-being.
Protecting Families & Reducing Costs Under the Ohio Health Care Plan, the current maze of billing, surprise charges, copays, deductibles, and administrative complexity would be streamlined, aiming to reduce overall costs for families and businesses and eliminate barriers that leave people uninsured or underinsured.
The approach is rooted in the idea that when health care is accessible and affordable, Ohioans are healthier, families are more secure, and communities are stronger.
Legislative Leadership Tristan’s work on universal health coverage builds on decades of advocacy calling for a health system that is accessible, equitable, and affordable for all.
In the Statehouse, he continues pushing for reforms that protect Ohioans from medical debt, expand coverage options, and strengthen the health care safety net for working families, seniors, and people with pre-existing conditions.
Putting People Before Profits Rep.
Rader’s healthcare agenda rejects a system where coverage depends on employment, income, or luck.
Instead, it centers on patient needs, preventive care, and economic security.
By reducing bureaucratic waste and prioritizing patient-centered coverage, this approach aims to reinvest health dollars into care, not insurance overhead.
Tristan’s vision for healthcare is anchored in the principle that every Ohioan deserves quality care without fear, barriers, or financial hardship, a right, not a privilege.
A Planet for All Climate change and environmental degradation are among the defining challenges of our time, and Ohio must be a leader in protecting clean water, healthy air, vibrant public lands, and resilient communities for current and future generations.
Leadership on Energy & Environmental Policy As the Ranking Minority Member of the House Energy Committee and a member of the Natural Resources Committee, Rep.
Tristan Rader has played a pivotal role in shaping energy and environmental policy in the Ohio House.
He brings deep expertise and a people-first vision to energy debates, standing up for clean energy, accountability, and consumer protections.
Turning the Page on Coal Subsidies In just his first year in office, Tristan helped lead the charge to repeal the remaining $1 billion in coal-plant subsidies that were embedded in the scandal-tainted HB 6 era, ending a costly giveaway that forced utility customers to pay for outdated coal generation.
This victory marked a major step toward energy fairness and fiscal responsibility for Ohioans.
Expanding Clean & Community Energy Tristan was instrumental in advancing Ohio’s community solar program and broader clean energy legislation, laying the foundation for more accessible, affordable, and democratic energy options for residents and local businesses.
His work reflects a commitment to energy solutions that lower costs, create local jobs, and reduce carbon emissions.
Defending Water & Public Lands Beyond energy, Tristan has taken strong action to protect Ohio’s natural treasures from harmful extraction.
He has sponsored bills to ban oil and gas drilling under Lake Erie and state parks, safeguarding drinking water, wildlife habitat, recreation, and community health.
These efforts underscore his belief that natural resources belong to the people, not corporate interests.
Stopping Toxic Practices Tristan has also advanced legislation to prohibit the surface application of oil and gas waste brine on roads and public land, targeting a dangerous practice with documented risks to soil, water, and public health.
This policy work puts environmental safety and community well-being ahead of shortcuts that threaten our ecosystems.
Putting People & Planet First In the Ohio House, Tristan consistently stands with communities over corporate interests, advocating for responsible energy transitions, water protection, and the preservation of public lands.
His environmental leadership is grounded in practical policies that protect land, water, and people, ensuring that Ohio’s natural assets are preserved, not polluted, for the benefit of all.
Tristan’s approach to environmental policy is bold, evidence-based, and rooted in equity, building a safer, cleaner, and more sustainable Ohio for every generation.
Human Rights Healthcare, bodily autonomy, housing, and the right to live free from discrimination are fundamental human rights, not privileges to be granted or taken away.
In Ohio and across America, powerful interests often attempt to divide us by race, gender, sexual orientation, or identity.
As Lyndon B.
Johnson observed about how division is used politically, “If you can convince the lowest white man he’s better than the best colored man, he won’t notice you’re picking his pocket.” That insight holds today: when we turn our frustration on each other instead of standing together, the wealthy and well-connected win while everyday people lose ground.
Standing united for human rights is both moral and strategic.
Protecting Reproductive Rights & Bodily Autonomy Reproductive rights are central to human dignity and equality.
Ohio voters enshrined the right to abortion, contraception, fertility treatment, and miscarriage care in the state constitution with the passage of Issue 1 in 2023, reflecting a broad commitment to reproductive autonomy.
Tristan has been a staunch advocate for safeguarding and expanding reproductive health care, particularly for people who face barriers due to income, race, or geography.
He supports state-level protections that ensure reproductive decisions remain between individuals and their health care providers, and he works to eliminate policies that make reproductive care inaccessible to marginalized communities.
In 2025, Tristan joined with a colleague to introduce a symbolic bill, the “Conception Begins at Erection Act," designed to highlight the double standards in reproductive legislation by pointing out how laws targeting women’s reproductive choices rarely apply the same scrutiny to men’s bodies.
The effort was intended to spark public conversation about gender inequity in reproductive policy and the ongoing attacks on bodily autonomy.
Advancing LGBTQ+ Protections Tristan has taken legislative leadership to defend and expand civil rights protections for LGBTQ+ Ohioans.
He was a primary sponsor of legislation to explicitly prohibit discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity in employment, housing, and public accommodations.
He has also stood with House Democrats in public celebrations of Pride Month and in opposition to anti-LGBTQ+ legislation, affirming that LGBTQ+ equality is integral to human rights and community wellbeing.
Standing Against Manufactured Division In a political climate where some seek to use cultural issues to distract from concentrated wealth and power, Tristan emphasizes that human rights aren’t battleground slogans but essential protections that strengthen families and communities.
Whether fighting for reproductive autonomy, LGBTQ+ equality, or racial and economic justice, his focus remains on uniting people around shared values rather than allowing politicians or special interests to profit by turning us against each other.
Putting People Before Politics Tristan’s approach to human rights is grounded in unwavering support for dignity, equality, and freedom for all Ohioans.
He believes that laws should expand access to health care, safeguard bodily autonomy, and protect individuals from discrimination, not be used as tools to police bodies, limit choices, or pit one community against another.
Tristan’s human rights leadership reflects a core conviction: every Ohioan deserves the full protection of the law and the ability to live, love, and thrive without fear or stigma.
An Affordable Ohio The state must do more to make life truly affordable for Ohio families.
Property taxes were established in this country centuries ago as a way to tax wealth, initially rooted in the idea that land ownership signified economic power and substantial wealth.
Over time, especially after World War II and the widespread adoption of homeownership through mortgage lending, property has become the primary way working and middle-class families build financial security, grow generational wealth, and escape poverty.
Yet today, the property tax has shifted into a burdensome tax on work and family stability, weighing heaviest on those least able to afford it.
Property Tax Reform Rooted in Equity In Ohio, property taxes are among the oldest forms of taxation, dating back to the early 19th century.
They have traditionally funded essential local services like public schools, libraries, and emergency services.
But skyrocketing property values in recent years have pushed these taxes to unsustainable levels for many homeowners, especially those on fixed incomes, working families, and retirees.
What once was a tax tied to land value has increasingly become a tax on the middle class’ biggest asset: their home.
Why We Need a Fairer System The principle of taxation should be rooted in the ability to pay, not in penalizing people for building security and wealth through homeownership.
A fairer and more progressive system would ease the burden on working families by taxing massive wealth, corporations, and large stock holdings more appropriately, rather than letting property taxes disproportionately fall on families simply trying to stay in their homes.
Ohio’s current tax structure, shaped by decades of income tax cuts and limited revenue options, forces local governments to rely heavily on property taxes, placing an undue burden on homeowners while corporations and investors often avoid paying their fair share.
Progressive Solutions for Housing & Taxes Tristan supports comprehensive reforms that would: Fund meaningful property tax relief for low- and middle-income homeowners so that long-term residents aren’t pushed out of their communities by rising assessments.
Explore modern tax alternatives and structures that shift the burden from work and homeownership to wealth and capital gains — a move toward a system that reflects modern economic realities and promotes equity.
Support “circuit breaker” protections and targeted tax credits that limit property tax increases for families who can least afford them.
Advocate for a tax code that doesn’t tax people out of their homes but instead asks the largest corporations and richest individuals to contribute their fair share toward the public goods we all rely on.
Putting People First An affordable Ohio means tax policies that strengthen families, protect neighborhoods, and expand opportunity, not policies that force people to carry the weight of a system built for wealth extraction.
Tristan’s approach centers on building a tax code that supports working Ohioans, promotes generational wealth building for families, and ensures our communities remain vibrant and sustainable.
Tristan believes in a progressive, fair, and humane tax system, one that invests in people, not just preserves existing advantages for the wealthy.
