Tristan Rader A True Public Servant A Cleveland native, Tristan has dedicated his entire life to helping others succeed.
From neighborhood-level service to the Statehouse, he has focused on making government work for working people, not powerful special interests.
His approach is simple: listen first, lead with integrity, and fight every day for an Ohio that is more affordable, more sustainable, and more fair for everyone.
Public Service About Tristan Rader Tristan Rader is the State Representative for Ohio’s 13th House District, representing Cleveland’s West Side and Lakewood.
A lifelong resident of the Cleveland area, Tristan has dedicated his life and career to public service, community empowerment, economic justice, environmental sustainability, and human rights.
Early Life & School Tristan was born in Cleveland, Ohio, and grew up in Lorain and Cleveland, shaped by a family deeply rooted in service to others.
His mother spent her career as a nurse, and his father taught elementary school in Elyria, instilling in him a respect for working people and a belief that communities are strongest when people step up for one another.
That foundation shaped the choices Tristan made early on.
Right out of high school, he began a full-time career while attending night classes at Lorain County Community College, later going on to earn his bachelor’s and master’s degrees.
Along the way, he built a career in mission-driven nonprofits and management, focusing on practical, people-centered solutions and long-term impact rather than personal gain.
From Grassroots Organizing to Public Service Tristan’s public service began long before elected office.
Early in his career, he worked directly with community-based organizations, helping families connect to essential resources and advocating for state funding to support vulnerable Ohioans.
He worked in community outreach at the Greater Cleveland Food Bank, where he connected thousands of residents to food security programs and other supports.
And later, as the state director for Solar United Neighbors.
His leadership in grassroots and progressive politics includes working for labor unions and progressive political candidates, including Bernie Sanders' 2016 presidential campaign.
Tristan co-founded the Cuyahoga County Progressive Caucus, where he helped build a coalition committed to progressive values and community power.
In that role, he also supported campaigns for economic justice, environmental stewardship, and participatory democracy here in Northeast Ohio.
Local Government Leadership in Lakewood Tristan was elected and served for seven years on the Lakewood City Council, where he quickly became a leader known for delivering results that improved residents' everyday lives.
During his time on council, he: Raised the minimum wage for city employees to better reflect the cost of living and strengthen economic security for workers.
Championed Lakewood’s 100% clean energy commitment and authored the city’s first Climate Action Plan, setting the stage for long-term environmental leadership.
Expanded green space and new public parks, including support for transforming vacant lots into community assets.
Led reforms to eliminate lead paint exposure, expand tree canopy, and improve neighborhood safety and walkability.
Through these efforts, Tristan worked to ensure local government served the needs of residents, not special interests, and that government is an engine for opportunity, health, and inclusion.
Statewide Leadership & Clean Energy Advocacy Before his election to the Statehouse, Tristan served as Ohio Director for Solar United Neighbors, a leading nonprofit focused on expanding community access to clean energy.
In that role he helped thousands of Ohio families adopt rooftop and community solar, organized solar co-ops, and built cross-sector coalitions to advance equitable energy solutions.
Service in the Ohio House of Representatives Tristan was elected to the Ohio House of Representatives in 2024 and took office in 2025.
In Columbus, he continues to fight for progressive priorities that center people and communities, including economic justice, healthcare access, environmental stewardship, human rights, and consumer protections.
He serves on multiple committees, including Commerce and Labor, Energy, Natural Resources, and Government Oversight, and is the Ranking Minority Member of the House Energy Committee, where he plays a pivotal role shaping the state’s energy policy and defending Ohioans from regressive, fossil-fuel-driven giveaways.
Personal Life & Values Tristan lives in Lakewood with his wife, Dr.
Caitlin Rader, a family physician.
His lifelong connection to the community fuels his belief that government should work for people, not corporations or special interests.
Whether fighting for living wages, clean energy, affordable healthcare, or human rights, Tristan’s work is driven by a simple but powerful conviction: public policy must expand opportunity, protect dignity, and build a just, sustainable future for all Ohioans.
