This Privacy Policy applies to the Website and all products, services, and communications offered by Friends of Tristan Rader.
Information We Collect Automatically If you visit our Website to browse, read, or download information, your web browser automatically sends us (and we may retain) certain information, including: The Internet domain through which you access the Internet The Internet Protocol (IP) address of the computer or device you are using The type of browser software and operating system you are using The date and time you access our Website The Internet address of the website from which you linked directly to our Website We use this information only in aggregate form to help us maintain and improve the Website, such as determining the number of visitors to different sections of the site, ensuring the Website functions properly, and making it more accessible and useful.
We do not use this information to identify individual users, except for site security, compliance, or law-enforcement purposes.
We do not collect personally identifiable information through passive browsing unless you voluntarily provide it.
Information You Voluntarily Provide If you choose to identify yourself or provide personal information through online forms (such as signing up to volunteer, donate, receive updates, or contact the Campaign), we may collect and retain information including, but not limited to: Name Mailing address Email address Phone number Donation or volunteer information Any other information you voluntarily provide We use this information to respond to your requests, provide campaign updates, process contributions, coordinate volunteer activities, and otherwise communicate with you about the Campaign.
We may share your information with trusted third-party vendors and service providers (such as email platforms, donation processors, or compliance vendors) solely for purposes related to Campaign operations.
These parties are required to use your information only for authorized purposes.
If you indicate that you do not want your information shared with third parties beyond what is necessary to fulfill your request, we will honor that preference where feasible.
Law Enforcement and Legal Requirements If required by law, court order, or legal process, or if your communication relates to a law-enforcement matter, we may disclose information to appropriate authorities.
Data Retention We may retain collected information for as long as necessary to fulfill campaign, legal, compliance, or record-keeping obligations, unless a longer or shorter retention period is required by law.
Use of Cookies You may choose to set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent.
If you do so, some parts of the Website may not function properly.
Google Analytics We use Google Analytics to help understand how visitors use our Website.
Google Analytics collects information such as how often users visit the site, which pages they visit, and what other sites they visited prior to coming to our Website.
The information collected is used only to improve the Website.
Google Analytics collects your IP address on the date of your visit but does not collect your name or other personal identifying information.
We do not combine analytics data with personally identifiable information.
Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
We do not knowingly collect, use, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13.
The Website and Campaign communications are intended for individuals aged 13 and older.
Your Access to and Control Over Information You may opt out of future communications from the Campaign at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions included in our emails or by contacting us at: info@tristanrader.com Links to Other Websites Our Website may contain links to third-party websites.
Any personal information you provide on those sites is subject to the privacy policy of the third party, not this Policy.
We are not responsible for the content, privacy practices, or security of linked websites.
Security We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical precautions to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
Sensitive information (such as payment information) is encrypted and transmitted securely using industry-standard protocols.
While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.
Changes to This Privacy Policy Friends of Tristan Rader may update this Privacy Policy at any time.
When changes are made, we will update the “Last Updated” date at the bottom of this page.
We encourage users to review this Policy periodically to stay informed.
Your Acceptance of These Terms By using this Website, you signify your acceptance of this Privacy Policy.
If you do not agree to this Policy, please do not use the Website.
Continued use following changes constitutes acceptance of those changes.
