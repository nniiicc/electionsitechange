- Vince Jones-Dixon Multnomah County Commissioner “Rep.
Ruiz and I had meaningful conversations about the state of our city and communities, particularly the rise in youth violence.
In response, we developed the East Metro Outreach Prevention and Intervention (EMOPI) program, aimed at mentoring youth and guiding them towards success.
In 2021, Rep.
Ruiz championed a $2 million investment in this program, and under his leadership, the funding has now more than doubled to $5 million.
This expansion will extend the program's reach to the cities of Fairview, Wood Village, and Troutdale, as well as areas east of 82nd Avenue.
Rep.
Ruiz's dedication to public safety and youth empowerment has been instrumental in addressing these critical issues.”
