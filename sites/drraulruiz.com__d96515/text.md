Ruiz grew up in Coachella, CA and learned at an early age that the key to attaining the American Dream is hard work and a great education.
In the summer of 1990, Raul walked in the desert heat from business to business in the Coachella Valley asking them to invest in their community – by contributing to his education.
With each investment for college, he made a promise to come back home and serve his community as a physician.
