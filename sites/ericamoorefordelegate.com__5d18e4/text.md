Delegate Erica Moore represents District 15 in the West Virginia House of Delegates and serves as Assistant Majority Leader.
She’s a leader in the legislature and a tireless advocate for our communities.
“This is home, and there’s nowhere I’d rather be.
I’m fighting to keep it that way.” Erica raised her three sons here and knows that our small town way of life is irreplaceable.
Representing Wirt and Roane Counties Erica’s family has called this part of West Virginia home for decades.
Now, she’s working hard to keep this the best place in the world to raise the next generation.
