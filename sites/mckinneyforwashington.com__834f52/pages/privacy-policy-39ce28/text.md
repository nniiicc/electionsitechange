Privacy Policy
Amanda McKinney for Washington
Last Updated: 12/19/2025
This Privacy Policy explains how Amanda McKinney for Washington (“we,” “our,” or “the Campaign”) collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you visit our website or interact with our online services (the “Site”).
By using the Site, you agree to the practices described below.
1.
Information We Collect
1.1 Personal Information You Provide
We may collect information you voluntarily submit, including:
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Donation details (processed securely by third-party vendors)
- Volunteer or event registration information
- Communication preferences
1.2 Automatically Collected Information
When you visit the Site, we may collect:
- IP address
- Browser and device information
- Pages viewed and interactions taken
- Referring websites or links
1.3 Cookies and Tracking Technologies
We may use cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies to:
- Enhance website functionality
- Personalize user experience
- Analyze traffic and performance
You may disable cookies through your browser settings.
1.4 Text Messaging Opt-In Data
If you opt in to receive SMS/text messages from the Campaign, we collect your phone number and related preference data.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Communicate campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, and events
- Process donations and meet federal reporting requirements
- Respond to inquiries or requests
- Improve the Site and optimize campaign outreach
- Ensure security, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations
3.
How We Share Your Information
We may share information with:
- Trusted service providers (email platforms, SMS tools, donation processors, website hosting services)
- Legal authorities, if required to comply with laws or subpoenas
- The Federal Election Commission (FEC), when required for donation disclosure
We do not sell your personal information.
We do not share or sell text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related information, except as required by law.
4.
Donation Information
Donations are processed through secure third-party payment processors.
The Campaign does not store full credit card or banking information.
Federal law requires campaigns to publicly disclose certain donor information for contributions over applicable thresholds.
5.
Data Security
We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information.
However, no method of transmission or storage is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.
6.
Your Rights and Choices
You may:
- Opt out of emails by clicking “unsubscribe”
- Opt out of SMS by replying STOP
- Request updates or corrections to your information
For assistance, contact: contact@mckinneyforwashington.com
7.
Third-Party Links
Our Site may contain links to third-party websites.
We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.
8.
Children’s Privacy
We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.
If we become aware that such information was collected, we will remove it promptly.
9.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
The updated version will be posted with a new “Last Updated” date.
10.
Messaging Terms & Conditions
You agree to receive updates, donation asks, and informational messages from Steinmann for Congress.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
For help, reply HELP or email us at contact@mckinneyforwashington.com.
You can opt-out at any time by replying STOP.
