About the Candidate David Silverberg David Silverberg is a veteran journalist who has spent his career standing for decency, dignity, and democracy.
A permanent Florida resident since 2013, he is deeply rooted in the communities of Southwest Florida—from Naples and Marco Island to Immokalee, LaBelle, and Clewiston.
Beginning in 2018, David began writing as The Paradise Progressive , giving Southwest Floridians the political news, analysis, and commentary that local media wouldn't touch.
His blog has become an essential voice for the region's progressive community.
Before moving to Florida, David served as Founding Editor of Homeland Security Today —the leading publication providing insight to homeland security decision-makers in Washington, D.C.
He is the author of Congress for Dummies (2002) and Masters of Disaster: The Political and Leadership Lessons of America's Greatest Disasters ( 2010 ).
A seasoned communicator with deep knowledge of government, policy, and public affairs, David is ready to bring a journalist's integrity and a fighter's determination to the Florida Senate.

But the work doesn't end there.
David Silverberg is determined that nothing like Alligator Alcatraz will ever again stain our state, destroy the Everglades and utterly waste Floridians' hard-earned tax dollars.
