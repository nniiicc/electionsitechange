James cosponsored H3163, a law expanding worker’s compensation occupational presumptions for firefighters to officially include strokes, heart attacks, and respiratory diseases.
James’ work to provide cancer screenings for all firefighters in our state has picked up steam and garnered bipartisan support.
His bill providing these lifesaving screenings is expected to pass both chambers in early 2027.
James is also working with South Carolina companies to provide PFAS free equipment to our firefighter community.
These efforts led to James being selected as the PFFASC Legislator of the Year in 2026.
James has also worked with the Fraternal Order of Police to ensure that our most experienced officers who have retired can return to work without penalty as it relates to retirement benefits and cosponsored the HALO Act to ensure our First Responders can do their job safely without interference.
As a member of the Charleston County Volunteer Search and Rescue Team, and recipient of the Lifesaver of the Year award, James understands the challenges facing our first responder community.
