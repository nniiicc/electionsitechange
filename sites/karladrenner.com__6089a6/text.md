I will continue working to ensure that Georgia invests responsibly in both.
ENVIRONMENT AND RESPONSIBLE DEVELOPMENT Protecting Georgia’s natural resources has been a priority throughout my legislative career.
As our state grows, we also need to consider the impact of that growth on our communities, our environment, our water and our energy resources.
That includes careful oversight of large-scale developments such as data centers and ensuring that Georgia’s growth is responsible and sustainable.
ENERGY As a member of the House Energy, Utilities & Telecommunications Committee, I work on issues that directly affect Georgia’s energy future.
I support a resilient and sustainable energy system and policies that expand clean-energy opportunities while protecting Georgia consumers and communities.
PUBLIC AND COMMUNITY HEALTH Access to healthcare and strong public-health systems affect every community in Georgia.
Through my work on the House Public & Community Health Committee, I continue to address policies that affect the health, safety and quality of life of Georgians.
EQUALITY When I was first elected to the Georgia House in 2000, I became the first openly LGBTQ+ member of the Georgia General Assembly.
I have spent my career working for equal treatment under the law and speaking up when the rights and dignity of Georgians are at stake.
I believe government should serve all of us.
SERVING DEKALB COUNTY In addition to representing House District 85, I serve as Chair of the DeKalb County House Legislative Delegation.
That role gives me another opportunity to work with residents, community leaders, local governments and my fellow legislators on the issues affecting DeKalb County.
Listening to the people I represent is an essential part of my job.
I encourage residents to contact me, attend our public meetings and stay involved in the legislative process.

About Karla Karla lives in Avondale, is the mother of two beautiful twins, and was elected to the Georgia House of Representatives in 2000, where she is in her 10th term.
She sits on six legislative committees and sub-committees and also serves as the chair of the DeKalb County House of Representatives legislative delegation.
A native of Charleston, West Virginia, Karla has lived and studied in various regions throughout the U.S.
Karla earned a Bachelor of Science in Health Physics from West Virginia State College, a Master of Business Administration from Fontbonne University, and a Master of Science in Environmental Science from Southern Illinois University.
She was awarded a Ph.D. in Public Policy Analysis and Administration from St.
Louis University and completed her second Ph.D. curriculum in Occupational Safety & Health from LaSalle University.
Karla is also active in the policy process nationally, where she serves on the Policy Committee for the National Foundation for Women Legislators.
Recently, she was selected to participate in a two-year healthcare task force by the Conference of State Government.
At the regional level, she serves on the Energy and Environmental Public Policy Committee of the Southern Legislative Conference.
She is also a member of the Air and Waste Management Association, Health Physics Society, National Registry of Environmental Professionals, and the National Safety Council.
