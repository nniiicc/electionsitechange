ABOUT SPARGO
Meet Rick Spargo
Rick Spargo’s family has been involved in fighting for American democracy for over 250 years.
He has a passion to serve it’s in his DNA.
He had five ancestors who fought for the foundation of America in the American Revolution.
The 250th year of the signing of the Declaration of Independence was an obvious time for him getting involved.
You won’t find a candidate more patriotic, more dedicated to the ideals of the Founding Fathers and Life, Liberty and the pursuit of Happiness.
He believes in the Constitution as a whole and not just parts of it that are convenient for his purposes.
He strongly believes in common sense decisions, moderation, and compromise.
Spargo is a North Scottsdale resident who started a successful business 35 years ago.
He has lived in the district for 20 years.
He raised four children.
He is married.
He has raised thousands of dollars for a homeless veteran’s shelter.
Has been the State President of the Arizona Sons of the American Revolution.
A non-partisan educational and civic minded organization that supports youth programs and awards scholarships.
