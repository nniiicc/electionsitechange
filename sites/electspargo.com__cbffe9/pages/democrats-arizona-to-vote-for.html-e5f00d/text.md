How He Governs
Firm on Values, Open to Common Ground
What sets Spargo apart is how he governs.
He is firm on the values that matter to Democratic voters — investing in public education, protecting healthcare access, and safeguarding Arizona's water and environment for the next generation.
At the same time, he emphasizes compromise and common ground wherever it is possible.
In his words, Arizonans have more in common than the political noise suggests, and that is where real progress starts.
Spargo runs clean.
He declines traditional big-PAC fundraising because he wants to answer to voters, not special interests.
That is a deliberate choice, and it comes with a trade-off: a limited budget that depends on people, not power brokers.
It also means grassroots support genuinely matters.
Volunteering a few hours, sharing the campaign, or contributing a small amount does more here than in a race funded by outside money.
Scottsdale & LD3
A Democrat for Scottsdale, Fountain Hills, Cave Creek, and Carefree
Legislative District 3 covers most of Scottsdale along with Fountain Hills, Cave Creek, Carefree, Rio Verde, and nearby North Valley communities.
Democratic voters here sometimes assume their vote for the state legislature will not change much.
Spargo's campaign is built on the opposite idea: the Arizona House shapes water policy, school funding, healthcare access, and the cost of living, and every LD3 voter has a say in who represents them.
Because each Arizona legislative district elects two House members, LD3 voters can choose up to two House candidates.
