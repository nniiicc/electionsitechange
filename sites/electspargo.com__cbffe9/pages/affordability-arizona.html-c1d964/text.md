Why Affordability Is the Kitchen-Table Issue Utilities, rent or a mortgage, groceries, a trip to the doctor — the everyday math has gotten harder for Arizona families.

Spargo treats affordability as the central test of good policy.
When the basics become unaffordable, everything else — savings, small businesses, quality of life — feels the strain.
His focus is on the costs people actually notice, not the ones that only look good in a press release.

A Business Owner's Approach to Costs Spargo has lived the parts of the economy most candidates only describe.
Over 35 years in business he dealt with rising costs, labor shortages, contracts, and financing — the daily balancing act of keeping a company healthy while still paying people fairly.
He carries that same numbers-first instinct into public office.
To Spargo, being pro-business and pro-family are not opposites: when local small businesses can survive and grow, jobs stay local and the whole community gets stronger.
Close To Home What Affordability Means for District 3 Families Legislative District 3 spans Scottsdale, Fountain Hills, Cave Creek, Carefree, Rio Verde, Anthem, New River, and parts of Phoenix — communities where a stable utility bill, an affordable grocery run, and accessible healthcare shape everyday life.
Spargo's message to these families is straightforward: leaders should measure success by whether the basics stay within reach.
That means keeping essential costs manageable, defending healthcare access for seniors and working families, and protecting the local small businesses that make the district's economy work for everyone.

Spargo's platform focuses on those first: Utility bills — summer cooling makes electricity and water some of the largest monthly costs for Arizona households, which is why utilities sit at the top of his list.
Housing — rent and mortgage payments that take a growing share of family income across the Valley.
Groceries and everyday goods — including the price effects of tariffs on what families buy every week.
Healthcare — premiums, prescriptions, and protecting Medicare and Medicaid for seniors and working families.
Spargo is running for the Arizona House, where state budget, healthcare, and water decisions that affect these costs are made.
