Kyle Hilbert has spent his life working, leading, and delivering for Oklahoma.
Raised in the small town of Depew, Kyle learned early the value of hard work, faith, and community.
After serving as Student Government President at Oklahoma State University, he returned home to help grow his family’s small business—DoughBoyz Pizza and Equipment—serving rural communities across Oklahoma.
At just 22 years old, Kyle was elected to the Oklahoma House of Representatives.
Since then, he has built a reputation as a results-driven conservative, rising to key leadership roles including Vice Chair of Appropriations and Speaker Pro Tempore.
In 2025, Kyle became the youngest Speaker of the Oklahoma House in state history—trusted by his colleagues to lead, govern responsibly, and deliver results.
Today, Kyle is focused on keeping Oklahoma strong: lowering costs, strengthening schools, defending conservative values, and ensuring government works for the people—not the other way around.
Kyle and his wife Alexis live in Bristow with their three daughters, Addison, Dorothy, and Della.
They are active members of Foundation Church.
