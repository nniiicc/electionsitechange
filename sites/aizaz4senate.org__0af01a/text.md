See the Issues Meet Aizaz It's Time to Shaikh Things Up About Meet Aizaz Shaikh Air Force Veteran Magna Cum Laude Licensed Pilot Real Estate Broker Aizaz Shaikh is a native of Gwinnett County, GA, licensed real estate broker, and civic leader with a distinguished record across Real Estate, Education, Aviation, Logistics, and Public Service.
He graduated Magna Cum Laude from Georgia Gwinnett College and became a licensed pilot shortly after.
His most distinguished service was through the United States Air Force , where he served as an Air Transportation Journeyman.
He was nominated Airman of the Month, received the Air Force Academic Achievement Award, is a recipient of the Global War on Terrorism Service Medal and represented young Airmen as Vice-President of the Dobbins ARB's Airman Leadership Group.
He actively serves as a Substitute Teacher with Gwinnett County Public Schools and on his alma mater's Student Advisory Council.
Aizaz remains actively engaged in civic initiatives focused on Education Access, Leadership Development, and Aviation Advancement .
Platform The Issues That Matter Creating Affordable Housing Aizaz knows families are being squeezed by rising housing costs and will fight for practical solutions that make homeownership and affordable living attainable again.
Tax Savings for Families Hardworking families should keep more of what they earn.
Aizaz will push for tax relief that eases everyday costs and puts money back in your pocket.
Prioritizing Education & School Safety Aizaz will work to ensure every child learns in a safe, secure environment where parents can have peace of mind.
Helping Teachers As an educator, Aizaz understands the challenges teachers face and will support them with the resources they need to succeed in the classroom.
Supporting Small Businesses As a small business owner, Aizaz will champion policies that help local businesses grow, thrive, and create good-paying jobs in our community.
Integrity & Servant Leadership Service and integrity defined Aizaz's time in the United States Air Force—and they are the principles he will take to the State Senate.
The uniform may have changed, but his commitment to our families and taxpayers remains.
