The Mission Why This Matters Wyoming needs representatives who trust citizens more than bureaucracies.
As your representative, Ryan will defend the principles that built this state — individual liberty, limited government, personal responsibility, and local control — fighting to keep taxes low, protect constitutional rights, and stop unnecessary regulation from crushing opportunity for the working families, ranchers, small businesses, and energy producers of House District 11.
01 School Choice & Statewide Open Enrollment Every Wyoming family deserves the freedom to choose the school that best serves their child.
Statewide open enrollment removes arbitrary district boundaries and empowers parents to select from any public school in the state — whether it's closer to work, offers a specialized program, or simply provides a better fit for their student.
Remove district boundary restrictions for student enrollment Streamline the transfer and enrollment process for families Ensure funding follows the student to their chosen school Support transportation options so choice is accessible to all families 02 Property Rights & Government Overreach The right to own and use your property free from government intrusion is foundational to Wyoming's way of life.
Ryan will stand with landowners, families, farmers, ranchers, and small businesses against overreach — keeping decisions close to home and protecting the freedoms that government too often tries to erode.
Defend landowners from eminent domain abuse and excessive regulations Remove unnecessary regulations that burden families, farmers, ranchers, and small businesses Protect the rights of peaceful citizens from government overreach Keep decisions in local communities, not distant state or federal bureaucracies 03 Transparency & Accountability Taxpayers have a right to know how their government operates and how their money is spent.
Open, honest government builds trust and keeps those in power answerable to the people they serve.
Ryan will fight to bring sunlight to the legislative process and hold government accountable at every level.
Make budgets, contracts, and votes fully public Strengthen ethics rules and punish corruption Expand open-records laws and reduce secrecy Give taxpayers more oversight of government spending Core Values Guiding Principles Individual Liberty Government should trust the people — not control them.
