1 Step One Make Sure You Can Vote The General Election is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Wyoming does not offer online registration, so take two minutes now to confirm the Laramie County Clerk has you on the rolls at your current address.
If you have moved since the last election, this is the step that keeps your ballot in District 11.
1 Look up your registration.
The Clerk's ballot lookup shows whether you are registered, your polling place, and what is on your ballot.
2 Register or update if you need to.
Mail or bring the form to the County Clerk by October 19, or register at the polls on Election Day with an acceptable ID.
3 Pick how you will vote.
Vote early in person from October 6 through November 2, request an absentee ballot (any day except Election Day), or go to your polling place on November 3.
Look Up Your Ballot Register to Vote Polling Places Laramie County basics Registration deadline for the General Election: October 19, 2026.
Missed it?
Register at the polls on Election Day with an acceptable ID.
Early voting runs October 6 through November 2 at the Laramie County Governmental Complex atrium, 309 W 20th Street, 8 AM to 5 PM on weekdays.
Absentee ballots can be requested any day except Election Day and must reach the Clerk by 7 PM on November 3.
Polls are open on Election Day.
Bring your ID.
Wyoming asks for a party when you register.
It decides which primary you vote in; in November you can vote for any candidate.
