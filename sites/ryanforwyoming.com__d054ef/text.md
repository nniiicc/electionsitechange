Why This Race Matters
Stronger Representation
for Cheyenne
Wyoming needs representatives who trust citizens more than bureaucracies.
House District 11 deserves a voice that defends the principles that built this state — individual liberty, limited government, personal responsibility, and local control — at a time when federal overreach and runaway spending threaten the independence of working families, ranchers, small businesses, and energy producers.
Ryan will fight to keep taxes low, protect constitutional rights, and stop unnecessary regulation from crushing opportunity.
He will champion free markets, property rights, and transparent government, keeping decisions in the hands of the people closest to them — not distant politicians or unelected agencies.
District 11 deserves a clear alternative: a government that serves the people instead of controlling them.
The Candidate
Meet the Candidate
A K-12 substitute teacher in Laramie County School District 1, serving nearly every school in our district.
A veteran of the U.S.
Air Force and a current Field Artillery Officer in the Wyoming Army National Guard.
A wildland firefighter with Albany County Fire.
A former TV meteorologist on Wyoming News Now.
With a Master's degree in Environmental Policy and Management and a lifetime of hands-on service to country and community, Ryan Shollenberger brings the necessary discipline, unique education perspective, and deep dedication required to fight effectively for Cheyenne in the Wyoming House of Representatives.
