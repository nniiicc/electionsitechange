About Lori
Lori, a longtime educator, community leader, and advocate, is running for the Colorado Statehouse to fight for strong public education, modern infrastructure, and equitable opportunities for all.
With decades of experience and a passion for service, she’s ready to make a difference.
Lori is committed to investing in public education, expanding affordable housing, ensuring broadband access for all, addressing climate change, strengthening healthcare, and supporting working families.
Her vision includes equitable opportunities, bold climate action, and policies that uplift every Coloradan.
