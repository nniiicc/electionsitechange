Privacy Policy
Effective Date: July 21, 2025
Lori Goldstein For Colorade House District 29 (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your personal information through loriforcolorado.com, in compliance with applicable U.S. and Colorado privacy laws.
1.
Information We Collect
Information You Provide:
We collect personal information that you voluntarily submit, such as:
- Name, email address, phone number, mailing address
- Volunteer interests, donation details (processed via secure third-party services), or any messages you send via contact forms
Automatically Collected Information:
- IP address, browser type, device data, pages visited, and general location (non-identifiable), gathered via cookies and analytics tools
2.
How We Use Your Information
We may use your information to:
- Respond to questions, comments, and inquiries
- Send campaign updates, event notices, and fundraising appeals
- Manage volunteer sign-ups and communication
- Analyze site usage to improve performance
- Comply with campaign finance and other applicable laws
3.
Use of Third-Party Tools
We may use third-party services to support website functionality and outreach, including:
- Google Analytics: To measure traffic and usage patterns
- CRM or Email Platforms (e.g., HubSpot, Mailchimp): To manage supporter contact information and send emails or newsletters
These providers process your information on our behalf and under confidentiality agreements.
4.
Colorado Privacy Rights
Under the Colorado Privacy Act, Colorado residents may:
- Request access to personal information collected
- Request correction or deletion of personal data (where not required for legal compliance)
- Opt out of targeted communications
To exercise your rights, email lori@loriforcolorado.com with the subject line “Colorado Privacy Request.” We may need to verify your identity before responding.
5.
Cookies & Tracking
We use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Track page views and traffic trends
- Remember form entries or site preferences
- Support performance improvements
You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but doing so may affect your experience on the site.
6.
Data Sharing & Security
We do not sell your personal data.
We may share it:
- With vendors assisting in campaign operations (e.g., payment processors, mailing systems)
- As required by law (e.g., campaign finance reporting)
- To prevent fraud, abuse, or security threats
We use industry-standard measures (e.g., HTTPS, data encryption, limited access controls) to safeguard your data.
7.
Children’s Privacy
This site is not directed at children under age 16.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
If we learn that we have done so, we will promptly delete the information.
8.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy as needed.
The revised “Effective Date” above will reflect any changes.
Please check back periodically for updates.
9.
Contact Us
For questions or privacy-related requests, please contact:
