Meet Jordan Lopez
Jordan Lopez is an East Charlotte native and proud product of Charlotte-Mecklenburg Public Schools.
A proud force for good in his community with a strong call to service, Jordan will work in the legislature to be a voice for East Charlotte and to ensure that North Carolina is a strong community that is welcoming and accessible to all.
As Representative for NC House District 112, Jordan will fight for economic opportunity, better public education, infrastructure that works for everyone, investing in children and families, and protecting our democracy.
