Getting It Done
Murrell Smith is a leader shaped by faith, family, and the Sumter community that raised him.
He began his career as a public defender because he believed regular people deserved someone in their corner.
That commitment has guided his work in the House, where he has focused on cutting taxes, improving roads and infrastructure, fixing broken parts of government, and protecting the unborn.
His colleagues elected him Speaker because they know he does the hard work and tells the truth.
At his core, Murrell is a husband, a father, and a lifelong South Carolinian who believes public service only matters when it is rooted in the place you call home.
