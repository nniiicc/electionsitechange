Why Me?
I’m not a career politician—I’m a citizen who’s spent her life solving problems, leading teams, and helping people succeed.
I’ve seen firsthand how government can do better, and I believe public service should be about results, not re-election.
I want to bring practical, common-sense leadership to the Statehouse—leadership that focuses on solutions, accountability, and making sure government works for the people it serves.
Our state has had the same leadership for nearly three decades.
It’s time to open the door for new voices and fresh energy.
I believe in term limits because public office should be a responsibility, not a career.
With your support, I’ll bring an experienced hand, a clear vision, and a determination to fix what’s broken—so we can all move forward together.
