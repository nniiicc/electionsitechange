Home Why Me I Want Change Too Donate Contact Check Voter Registration Home Why Me I Want Change Too Donate Contact Check Voter Registration Higher Ed Leader I worked my way through college and left with very little debt because, at that time, the state paid for most of my education.
I earned a bachelor’s degree in Psychology and a Master's in Public Administration from the University of Houston.
I earned a doctoral degree in Higher Education Administration from the University of Texas at Austin.
I began my career in higher education, working the night shift at a college residence hall, and forty years later, I completed my career serving as a college President and Chancellor.
I work hard and get things done.
Now, I am an independent consultant working with college presidents and chancellors across the country to help them be successful.
And now, I am ready to work for you to help Indiana be successful!
Loves Living In Indiana Along the way, I spent 11 years working at IUPUI and fell in love with Indiana and the Hoosier way of life.
I have family and dear friends in Indiana.
I like our Hoosier values of fairness and hard work.
I like that Hoosiers are often modest, simply want to live a good life, and have a fundamental respect for others.
I like solving problems with sensible solutions that meet folks in the middle and working toward each of us living our best lives.
Loves Living in Brownsburg & Hendricks County In 2019, my spouse and I moved back to Indiana to Brownsburg and District 28.
We could have lived anywhere, and we chose Brownsburg, Indiana.
I want to work to keep what we all love about our community and to solve real problems that make a difference.
Proud to be a Hoosier!
