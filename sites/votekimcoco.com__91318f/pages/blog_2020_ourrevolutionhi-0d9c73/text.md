NEWS RELEASE ISSUED BY OUr Revolution Hawaii OUR REVOLUTION HAWAII ENDORSES KIM COCO IWAMOTO FOR STATE REPRESENTATIVE "Through your campaign you have demonstrated that you will be a courageous representative of the people, who willingly and enthusiastically fights for justice and the progressive issues that Our Revolution stands for, like the Green New Deal, increased funding for education, living wage, and health care for all.
Unfortunately too many elected officials forget that they were elected to represent the needs of the people rather than their corporate funders.
We know that you will represent the people of Hawaii very well with your commitment to protect and serve the people, community, and our aina.
We are impressed by your fearlessness to stand up for what is honest and fair over the current prevailing trend.
With our legislature heavily influenced by corporate greed, and our world on the verge of climate chaos, we need fearless leaders like you in our legislature, who have the courage to take the hard path for what is just over the easy path of what is popular." Our Revolution has worked tirelessly to educate voters about issues, get people involved in the political process, and work to organize and elect progressive candidates.
This a tremendous and honor for me to have their endorsement.
