PONO HAWAII INITIATIVE ENDORSES KIM COCO IWAMOTO FOR STATE REPRESENTATIVE
“We are undeniably at a pivotal point where action to slow and reverse these conditions is a moral obligation.
Our government institutions and business community are moving at a glacial pace to address the critical issues affecting Hawaii.
Or, they are moving us in the wrong direction.
It is with this urgency in mind that we are pleased and proud to endorse your candidacy for the Hawaii State House of Representatives, District 26.
Mahalo for all your work and commitment to your community and progressive ideals.”
I'm proud to be endorsed by the Pono Hawai`i Initiative, a group that is dedicated to putting people first and advancing a progressive vision for our future.
