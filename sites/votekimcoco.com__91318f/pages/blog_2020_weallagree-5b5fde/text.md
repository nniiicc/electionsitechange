HARADA July 17, 2020 – The last 26 years have brought great wealth to a few but undeniable hardship to so many more.
During these unprecedented times, we have an extraordinary opportunity to fundamentally improve our economy and lift all families.
The incumbent has been in the State House for 26 years.
What more can he do with a 14th term that he couldn’t do with his last 13?
Kakaako teacher Mrs.
Harada said it best, “Normally I vote for Scott, but after 26 years, I think we’re ready for enw leadership.” It’ s time for fundamental change.
New energy.
Greater transparency.
Steadfast accountability.
As your representative, I promise to stay engaged throughout the year, to listen, and share, so that I can be an advocate for you in every decision your government makes.
I humbly ask for your vote.

Join us and let your voice be heard at the August 8 election for State House Representative of District 26.
