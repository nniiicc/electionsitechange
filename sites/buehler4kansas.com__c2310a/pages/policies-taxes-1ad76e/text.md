Taxes and the Impact on Kansans
What is my stance on the taxes Kansans pay today?
I do not profess to be a “Tax Expert” on each and every tax levied by the State of Kansas.
As a homeowner though, I will work to get on the Taxation Committee in the Kansas House if elected as your State Representative, to become involved in, and work toward, reducing the tax burden on our citizens.
I say this after seeing my own property taxes seemingly climb every year without regard to the value of my home or the homes around me.
And we must also consider the flip-side of taxes which generate revenue for the state and that is SPENDING.
As your state representative, we will deliver a balanced budget every year, and work toward reducing spending levels associated with reduced taxation!
