The Issues That Matter US 23-71 Bypass One of my top priorities is stopping the proposed US 23-71 bypass.
Our homes, multi-generational farms, and open spaces are not “empty land” waiting to be developed—they are the backbone of our community and our economy.
Delaware County’s traffic problems should not be solved by destroying Morrow County’s way of life.
There are smarter, more efficient infrastructure alternatives that don’t involve seizing family land through eminent domain.
I am more than happy to work with neighboring districts to find technical solutions that work for them within their own borders.
#accountability Data Centers and Energy Costs As an IT professional with 20 years in the field, I understand the value of technology—but I also know when to call BS.
Right now, Ohio is handing out millions in tax breaks to massive data centers while they strain our electric grid and drive up water and energy bills for the rest of us.
I am calling for an end to these “data center tax holidays.” If these corporations want to tap into our resources, they should pay their fair share, not foot the bill to the families of District 87.
My plan to bring your utility bills down is trifold: Accountability: End the subsidies and ensure data centers pay for the infrastructure upgrades they require.
Energy Independence: We need to diversify our energy portfolio—including responsible wind and solar projects—to lower costs and increase local supply.
End New Data Center projects until they can prove that the facility won’t damage our environment, our health, and won’t cost the taxpayers money.

If elected, I will work toward practical, affordable solutions.
Property taxes fund our communities.

#fundourschools #fundlibraries #fundaddictionandmentalhealthfacilities Safe Housing for Vulnerable Adults My mom is a retired senior who lost her home because her landlord failed to maintain the property.
It became unsafe so she now lives with me.
Laws already exist to protect people in situations like this — but for many seniors, those protections are ineffective in the real world.
I want to close the loopholes that allow negligent landlords to avoid accountability and create a state-level Senior Housing Advocate — expanded to assist all vulnerable residents — so people can get help without needing a lawyer or fearing retaliation.
#seniorhousingadvocate Cheaper Prescription Drug Costs My mom has faced extensive health issues in recent years.
I help manage her medical bills and prescriptions, and I drive her to appointments.
Even with Medicare, her prescription costs can still reach hundreds of dollars every month.
Ohio has made some progress by requiring Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) to be licensed and disclose pricing practices.
But transparency alone does not save you money.
I support requiring PBMs to pass negotiated discounts directly to consumers at the pharmacy counter instead of pocketing that money.
I will do everything I can at the state level to help lower prescription drug costs for all Ohio families.
#lowerprescriptiondrugcost Fundamental Rights And I will continue to defend our fundamental rights—protecting women’s healthcare, supporting the LGBTQ+ community, helping those less fortunate, and ensuring safety from gun violence.
