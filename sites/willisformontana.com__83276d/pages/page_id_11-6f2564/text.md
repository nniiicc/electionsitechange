As a resident of Missoula for more than 40 years and a four term legislator, I am well acquainted with the issues facing our state and local community.
My experience as a small business owner, rancher, educator, wildland firefighter and community leader provides me the background necessary to be a strong, common sense voice on behalf of the voters in House District 98 and now for the voters in Senate District 49.
