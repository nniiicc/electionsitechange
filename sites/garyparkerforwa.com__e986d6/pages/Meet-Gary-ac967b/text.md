Gary Parker didn't spend his life preparing for politics.
He spent it solving problems.
01 Career A career built on solving real problems.
Over the course of his career, Gary has worked with teams across the United States and around the world in the technology sector — helping companies develop innovative solutions, manage complex systems, and deliver results in demanding environments.
Gary built his career working with global technology leaders including Intel and IBM , where he helped manage complex systems and deliver solutions in some of the most demanding environments in the world.
While at Intel, Gary's work earned him internal Recognition for Excellence — a prestigious honor awarded within the company to individuals who demonstrate exceptional leadership, innovation, and technical achievement.
Working in those high-performance environments required solving difficult problems, managing sophisticated technology, and collaborating with teams responsible for critical operations.
Those experiences taught Gary the importance of accountability, clear thinking, and disciplined problem-solving .
Gary loves people.
He believes it's his superpower.
02 Community Built in the community he loves.
Gary has always believed success means more than professional achievement.
It means using your experience to strengthen the community around you.
After years of working in global technology organizations, Gary and his family chose to invest in the Gig Harbor community they proudly call home.
They opened BBQ2U in Gig Harbor — creating a restaurant that quickly became more than a place to eat.
It became a gathering place where neighbors meet, families celebrate, and community groups come together.
Gary has used BBQ2U to support the community through fundraisers, charity events, and partnerships with local organizations that help schools, nonprofits, and families across the region.
The restaurant has also created local jobs and opportunities for members of the community.
For Gary, business success has always been about more than profit.
It begins with genuinely caring about the community around him.
It's about giving back to the community that has given him so much.
03 Why he's running Solutions, accountability, and common sense — back to Olympia.
Gary's work in technology gave him a deep understanding of how decisions made in government affect real people — the families working hard to get ahead, the local employers creating jobs in our communities, and the workers who depend on those opportunities.
Gary believes government should focus on creating the conditions where people and communities can succeed — not getting in their way.
That commitment to service is what ultimately led Gary to step forward and run for State Senate.
Gary isn't running to become a politician.
He's running to bring solutions, accountability, and common sense back to Olympia.
Our district deserves leadership focused on solutions, not politics.
