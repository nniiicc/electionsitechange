For 14 years, it’s been an honor to serve you and our neighbors as Newtown’s State Representative.
This is not a desk job, and certainly not a 9 to 5 commitment!
Done well, a great Representative carries your values, along with the overall values of the community, forward and acts upon them in the formation of state policy.
But, that's only one part of the job.
Just as important, and far more diverse is the work very few people ever get to see - the one to one Constituent Service work of taking care of people.
Some folks call it "being there when it counts".
Caring never stops, is not confined to regular business hours, and can never be taken for granted because no two situations are ever the same.
Many requires the utmost sensitivity and privacy.
Over my 14-years of service to Newtown, I have dropped what I'm doing thousands of times for matters that varied in as many ways as our town's population does.
I won't go into them here because each was personal.
Your vote in November keeps the caring in Newtown, along with the bipartisan legislative work on issues that matter to you, like lowering energy costs, saying NO to tax increases, protecting local control & finding every possible way to restore Connecticut's affordability.
Affordability matters - more so today than perhaps ever before .
Every day, we contend with the nation’s 3rd highest overall tax burden, 2nd highest electric bills, and runaway costs of housing, medical & insurance.
For 30-years, CT's majority party has used it's numbers to "nickel & dime" us with hidden taxes, Public Benefits abuse, fees, and compounding surcharges that add up faster than family earnings This must end and, in every session, I speak to Connecticut's growing unaffordability, and propose legislation to eliminate excessive spending, fees, and waste.
I’ve never voted to raise our taxes , toll your rides to work, shopping trips, or out-of-town family - nor do I vote for any budget that doesn't balance without sleight of hand.
Never have.
Never will.
You can count on me as your first line of defense against intensifying state efforts to take-away local control of zoning, education & emergency services.
CT has been a “Home Rule State” by legislative act for 70-years.
Letting the state bow to outside special-interests is a non-starter.
As a fiscal conservative , I am among the General Assembly’s strongest voices of reason in opposition to unfunded mandates, and in moderating nuisance taxes, fees, and spending without accountability.
Most of all, I believe in Newtown.
Kindness matters , as do integrity, and rolling-up one’s sleeves across aisles to find solutions that improve your quality of life, and create opportunity for our next generation.
You have my word.
May I have your vote? ** Thank you for reading this far.
