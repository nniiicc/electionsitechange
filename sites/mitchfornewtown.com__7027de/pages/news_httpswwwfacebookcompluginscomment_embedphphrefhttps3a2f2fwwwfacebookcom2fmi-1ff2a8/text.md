The Connecticut Business & Industry Association (CBIA) just announced its endorsement of incumbent Mitch Bolinsky for Newtown's State Representative.
In a statement, CBIA President, Joe Brennan endorsed Mitch among an elite group of Republican and Democrat lawmakers who "understand CT needs an environment where companies can compete, attract much-needed investment to our state, and drive economic growth and job creation." He concluded, "We urge voters to look beyond the rhetoric and vote for a candidate who will help make Connecticut a destination state—a place where companies want to invest and where people want to live, work, and raise a family."
November 6th, vote for Mitch Bolinsky.
