As a 14-year member of the Legislature's Education Committee, Mitch is committed to the fundamentals of a STEM education, focused on every child, regardless if they live in Newtown or any other ZIP Code.
He's been a leader in special education, believing that children who learn "differently" benefit for their entire lives with early detection and interventions before they fall behind.
A sampling of Mitch's work in Education includes: 2026, 2025 & 2024: A leading voice defending CT Homeschool families against State Department of Education and Special Interests efforts to eliminate parents' right to choose.
2025, 2024 & 2023: Prevented statewide effort by Special Interests to eliminate School Resource Officers (SROs) from CT schools.
Strongest voice in Hartford for school safety.
In 2024, secured a $3.5M Indoor Air Quality Grant for Newtown Public Schools.
2023 & 2024: Stopped special-interest cuts to Crisis Response Drills. (see related news item) In 2023, secured first $300,000 to facilitate expansion of local STEM-learning children's museum In 2022, Newtown Delegation secured $2.5MM grant for the Sandy Hook Permanent Memorial.
14-year Mandate-Fighter - Opponent of unfunded state mandates that limit your ability to choose what's best for your child, reduce instructional time, or unnecessarily burden educators.
In 2021, Co-founded CT Office of Dyslexia & Reading Disabilities within Dept. of Education & Autism Spectrum Disorder Unit within Social Services to support children who learn differently.
In 2021, Secured $6MM pandemic operating grant for nine CT Children's Museums. (including EverWonder) In 2021, Advocated to reopen schools with PPE protections, advocating with & for parent's rights to be part of decisions on instruction, curriculum, masking & vaccination.
In 2020, pivoted to full-focus on Constituent Service & troubleshooting Remote Learning as COVID locked us down, and state agencies stopped serving students and taxpayers.
Co-introduced PA 19-49, providing children with Autism Spectrum Disorder access to Medicaid covered transitional services, before "aging-out", closing gap between High School & adulthood.
Secured grants for public and private school safety upgrades.
(PA 19-52, & other sources) In 2017, reversed Governor's $4.89MM cut to Newtown Schools.
