I've never voted to raise your taxes , impose tolls, or slam you with fees that are nothing more than another way to tax you.
I never will.
It's funny that my opponent thinks that's a bad thing.
I call it accountability, especially in a state that's grown less & less affordable, and ranks 3rd on the list of states with greatest tax burdens in America.
Balanced budgets, accountability, and a government that works for the good of all its taxpayers, not just a privileged few, matters deeply to me.
I've proposed multiple, vetted, balanced no-tax-increase budgets that fully fund education, human services & infrastructure.
The one my colleagues and I proposed for FYs 2017-2018 introduced a set of "Fiscal Guardrails" that turned our state's 10-year run of ballooning deficit spending, large tax increases, record debt thru bonding, AND raiding of state employees' pension funds, into an 8-year run of state surpluses that allowed us pay down a chunk of that pension liabilities and high-percentage bonding.
What began as a "Republican Budget" and passed in a deadlocked legislature, was then VETOED by Governor Malloy, then, reintroduced in special session as the 2017 "Bipartisan Budget" If you do the math, you'll see that the surpluses commonly referred to by, now, Governor Lamont as CT's turnaround, actually came as a result of another governor's signing of a budget in 2017 that brought balance to CT's budget.
Not quite the message we hear in 2026 TV ads, is it?
You have a right to expect our state to plan, operate, and spend your tax dollars responsibly.
You deserve a representative in Hartford who will speak up, and is focused on moving our state forward, improving your quality of life, creating opportunity for the next generation - paid for with revenue generated by economic growth, driven by affordable policies that attract families, employers, and private investment.
This adds up to opportunities to live, learn, work, advance, earn a good living and thrive.
Higher taxes, and a government that mandates costly policy, like the one that created the shocking Public Benefits charge on our electric bills, began as a new way CT Democrats found to tax us in May, 2024.
The mandating of new costs in our electric bills bypassed public hearings, and was not voted upon by legislators.
It hit our utility bills in August 2024, executed by an Executive Order issued directly to the PURA Chief, who later resigned under pressure of an investigation.
One of my earliest mentors impressed upon me a fundamental lesson: "Your budget is your promise".
Keeping one's word, working with transparency, within your means, and not taking from Peter to pay Paul, matters.
I've proposed multiple, balanced, no-tax-increase state budgets that fully fund education, human services, infrastructure, and the humanities without relying on accounting tricks, or favoring special interests.
Our state government broke it's promises, working outside the 2024 & 2026 budgets.
In both years, 900 to 1,000 page "Appropriations Bills" came to the floor, as little as 30-minutes before being called for deliberation and votes.
Hiding inside were hundreds of never-before seen "repurposings" of taxpayer money in 2026, and of federal relief money in 2024 to pay for non-pandemic overspending, by way of federal pandemic relief funds intended to reimburse electric utilities for the revenue lost to the governor's 4-year shut-off moratorium with dollars intended to pay public benefits.
The result appeared on our August 1, 2024 electric bills.
