"As a leader of the state's Committee on Aging, and a 10-year family caregiver, the needs & concerns of seniors and their caregivers, matter!
But that's not enough to drive good policy and the legislation to implement it all the way to solid, bipartisan passage in todays political climate.
It takes a dedicated team with diverse perspectives & a sense of 'common purpose'.
We embody that". - Mitch In 2026, the Aging Committee continued to be the most non-partisan, most cohesive team at the Capitol, passing three major policy revision bills unanimously, or near unanimously.
In 2024, we took a 'Governor's Bill, and a "'Speaker's Bill' to create CT's signature 2024 legislation, raising the bar for Nursing Home care (PA 24-141), and advancing Aging-in-Place (PA 24-39) to a new level, making home care Medicare/Medicaid eligible, pending a federal waiver.
More affordable options matter to seniors & families.
Choices, including coverage for home-based care, matters to us." What does it mean for you, or the loved ones for whom you care?
AARP research indicates 3 of every 4 seniors would choose to stay in their homes, or with family.
We are making great progress implementing a Nationally Recognized, field-based Aging-In-Place program, and building a service & support network through five regional Area Agencies on Aging (AAAs).
Each is building capacity to connect seniors and caregivers to nursing-home level care in one's home, or a caregiver's home.
We are already hard at work improving access to more d-out, rural communities for 2027.
A-la-cart services and subscription plans covered by Medicare/Medicaid will provide real choice!
Skilled nursing, companion services or family-administered care in a home setting will become a matter of choice, pending expansion of a federal waiver.
Minor accommodations, like wheelchair ramps, widened thresholds, will qualify for consideration.
Consumer protections like, caregiver licensing, caregiver training and oversight being fine tuned.
