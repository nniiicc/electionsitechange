About Adonis
A Voice for the Working-Class Majority
I am not a career politician or a billionaire-backed insider.
I am a working-class American citizen who will work for you and fight for your rights.
I was born to a working-class immigrant family who strived for the American Dream, achieved it, and seen it become out of reach for the next generation.
I was raised in Ogden, UT, the oldest of 4 boys, to a conservative Catholic household where politics never trumped humanity.
My parents enrolled me in Catholic school, and going to the same school and seeing most of the same people from the age of 4 to 18 instilled a sense of togetherness and collective accomplishment in me.
After receiving a scholarship to attend Full Sail University near Orlando, Florida, I left home and became an independent adult.
In 2019, I attained my bachelor’s degree in film and my master’s degree in business.
After graduating, my friend, mom, and I packed up a Penske moving truck, moved to Los Angeles, and I began working as an office manager for a film and TV distribution company.
I was promoted to project coordinator in 2020.
All the while, I was stuck in Los Angeles during Covid paying $1,400 a month for a 500sqft apartment one block below McArthur Park.
Dealing with health issues in my 20s has strongly pushed me to advocate for Medicare for All.
I began to feel excruciating back pain—like a tennis ball stuck between my spine and shoulder blade.
Turns out I had two bulging discs.
Fortunately, I was under my parent’s insurance at the time, but the pain persisted even after treatment.
Unfortunately, after I turned 26 in 2024, I was kicked off my parent’s health insurance.
After moving back home in 2021, I started to prioritize family and the core elements that make life worth living over hustle culture.
Being stuck in a large city during Covid reminded me why I was working so hard: it was for my family.
I know I’m not alone in the struggle to stay financially afloat.
I spend my free time with my friends and family, playing sports, enjoying lake days, watching movies, hiking, and living a regular American life.
"When I fight for you, I’m fighting for my family.
Most of us in Utah’s 3rd Congressional District and the United States are working class, and my focus is our prosperity."
