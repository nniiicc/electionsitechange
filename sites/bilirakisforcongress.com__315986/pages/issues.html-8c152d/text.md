Lowering the Cost of Living
Groceries, housing, and the everyday basics cost more every week.
Families are squeezed by inflation on every necessity, workers watch tips and overtime get taxed away, and seniors stretch fixed incomes thinner every month.
Gus delivered the new $6,000 senior tax exemption, championed no tax on tips and no tax on overtime, and continues to push affordability legislation that puts money back in working families' pockets, including ending taxation on Social Security, lowering prescription drug costs for seniors, and his new homeowners insurance deduction.
