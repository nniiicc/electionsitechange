- Lowering costs for working families
- Fixing infrastructure and cutting bureaucracy

- Expanding housing opportunities
- Strengthening healthcare access
- Protecting public safety
- Creating policies that work for everyone

THE PATH & THE PLATFORM TO MOVE FORWARD
Focused on lowering costs, strengthening communities, and making Rhode Island a better place to live.
LOWER COSTS & TAX RELIEF
- Lower everyday costs for Rhode Island families
- Provide tax relief for working families and seniors
- Make housing more affordable and accessible — with policies that actually work for local communities
- Protect generational wealth and long-term family stability
ENERGY, AFFORDABILITY & RATEPAYER PROTECTION
- Lower energy costs for Rhode Island families
- Increase transparency and accountability for ratepayers
- Diversify our energy mix for long-term stability
- Explore next-generation nuclear as part of a balanced energy future
JOBS, SMALL BUSINESS & LOCAL INVESTMENT
- Cut red tape so businesses can invest in local communities
- Support small businesses and entrepreneurs
- Expand workforce training tied to real opportunities
- Strengthen pathways to good-paying jobs
CUT WASTE, FRAUD & GOVERNMENT INEFFICIENCY
- Eliminate wasteful government spending
- Crack down on fraud and misuse of taxpayer dollars
- Increase transparency and accountability
- Ensure resources reach families and communities
OCEANS & THE BLUE ECONOMY
- Build on what’s already working in Rhode Island’s ocean economy
- Support local marine trades, fishing, and small businesses
- Expand job opportunities tied to ocean-based industries
- Protect our coastline while encouraging responsible growth
HEALTHCARE & THE PHYSICIAN SHORTAGE
- Invest in our Community Health Centers — empower them to lead American healthcare forward.

- Reduce administrative burdens on providers
- Improve access to care across Rhode Island
- Keep healthcare focused on patients, not bureaucracy
EDUCATION THAT WORKS

- Support school choice and homeschooling options
- Ensure transparency for parents and communities
PARENTS & FAMILY VOICE

- Give parents a stronger voice in decisions

- Protect constitutional rights
