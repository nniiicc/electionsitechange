AN ECONOMY FOR EVERYONE ​ ​ It shouldn’t be this hard to get by.
Wages have stagnated for working families while both corporate profits and prices skyrocket.
The economy’s great if you’re a billionaire getting tax breaks from Trump, but not so much if you’re a hardworking Minnesotan.
Zack puts working Minnesotans first in everything he does , especially in the face of big-money interests at the Capitol.
He’s won fights against Big Pharma to lower the cost of life-saving prescriptions and against Big Tech to put social media safeguards in place for our kids.
He’s also passed economy-wide consumer protections, so Minnesotans are safer from scams and price gouging.
Zack was proud to support Minnesota's guarantee of paid family leave for every Minnesotan who works full time.
No one should lose a job because they have to take care of a new baby or a sick parent.
Zack also supports raising the minimum wage to make sure that no working family lives in poverty.
And Zack was a champion for the nation’s toughest law against wage theft.
Zack is committed to lowering costs because in the wealthiest country in the world, you shouldn’t have to choose between food on the table, paying your rent or mortgage, or getting the health care you need.
Our economy is rigged towards the largest, richest, out-of-state corporations, while our Main Street family-owned businesses struggle to compete.
Zack works to cut red tape and make it easier and more affordable to run the small businesses that serve our communities.
A HEALTHCARE SYSTEM THAT WORKS FOR YOU In America, we pay the most into our health care system, and still have the worst health outcomes in the developed world.
Zack is fighting to cut out the middlemen who inflate your costs, hold Big Pharma accountable, create a fair system where you can get the care your doctor says you need (not your insurance company), and ensure Minnesotans have a health care system that is reliable, accessible, and affordable.
Zack believes that all Minnesotans should be given the option of buying into MinnesotaCare.
Right now, only people who have limited income can sign up for MinnesotaCare, but the people who have MinnesotaCare are very happy with it.
And opening MinnesotaCare up to everyone will provide families with stability and save them money with no ongoing cost to taxpayers.
Middle class families should have the option of buying into MinnesotaCare if that is what makes the most sense in their individual situation.
Zack’s not scared of corporate lobbyists.
He’s passed bills that lowered the cost of live-saving medication, forced more transparency on Big Pharma, and cracked down on prescription drug price-gouging.
He’ll fight for a fair, working health care system, because all Minnesotans deserve the care they need without going bankrupt. ​ OUTCOME-DRIVEN EDUCATION ​Every Minnesota kid should be set up to succeed.
Every Minnesota parent should be confident that their kids are getting the highest-quality education possible, so they can grow up to support themselves and pursue their dreams.
Zack listens to students, parents, and educators about what they need when working on education policy.
Class sizes used to be smaller, with more individual attention for each student.
Extracurriculars used to be cheaper, so all kinds of families could support their kids playing sports or music.
Education used to be a desirable and rewarding field to work in, but wages and benefits have not kept up with any other sector, so many schools are understaffed.
And of course, there’s the endless screen time that has caused attention and mental health issues among our kids.
Zack fights for us at the State Capitol to fix all of these issues, because Minnesota’s kids deserve it.
He stands up to politicians who try to divert public money to private schools.
He focuses on evidence-based, outcome-driven policy to increase student achievement.
Zack is committed to: Universal, high-quality early education.
Investing in early education will benefit our children, build the workforce our state needs to succeed in the future, and relieve pressure on middle class families struggling with the ever rising cost of childcare.
Rigorous K-12 education.
Our students need high expectations, and our schools need the support to get the job done.
Despite increased funding in recent years, state funding for schools is still less per pupil than it was in 2003 when adjusting for inflation.
That is unacceptable.
And when a local school district tries to make up the difference by raising property taxes, it increases burdens on middle class families and retirees.
Varied and affordable higher education.
Not every kid wants or needs to go to college.
But we need to make sure that those who choose to go aren’t saddled with mountains of debt.
Zack supported legislation to ensure that families making under $80,000 can attend college debt free.
He also believes we need to do a better job supporting our vocational and technical schools.
Last, we need to do a better job connecting our high school students with all of the educational options open to them after graduation, from vocational schools and certificate programs, to community colleges, and our public and private colleges and universities.​​ As the parent of two public school students, and the husband of an Anoka-Hennepin public school teacher, Zack will continue to fight for you and your kids. ​ FIGHTING FRAUD ​Fraud, in any amount, is unacceptable.
Criminals and scammy businesses took advantage of our kindness in Minnesota.
They stole from taxpayers, kids with autism, and Minnesotans with disabilities.
Who steals from kids with autism?
It’s outrageous.
As a prosecutor, Zack supports cracking down on fraud and holding the people responsible accountable.
He has fought for tougher penalties, stronger oversight, and program controls to make sure this never happens again.
Zack voted to establish an independent Office of Inspector General to investigate fraud across state governments regardless of which political party is in charge.
Zack will continue that work, fighting for the harshest punishments possible for criminals, and supporting reforms to stop prevent before it happens.
Zack will also fight for a top to bottom audit of state government and work hard to strengthen public programs to make sure this kind of fraud never happens again.
We all deserve a government we can trust.
Zack will make sure your government is responsible, effective, efficient — and working for you.
PROTECTING OUR ENVIRONMENT ​We care about our beautiful nature in Minnesota.
Our way of life depends on our abundant natural resources.
Minnesotans from all walks of life love to enjoy our lakes and woods, to hunt, fish, camp, and hike with family.
Zack wants to make sure our kids, their kids, and their grandkids get to enjoy it the same way we do.
Zack fights for scientific, evidence-based approaches to curbing climate change.
This includes planting trees, investing in resilient infrastructure, and holding polluting corporations accountable.
Zack will fight to keep our Minnesota air and water clean from pollution and emissions, so Minnesotans can stay healthy.
Zack believes the Boundary Waters are priceless and must be protected.
That’s why he co-authored the bill that would prohibit sulfide-ore copper mining specifically near the Boundary Waters Canoes Area Wilderness (BWCA) and the headwaters of the Rainy River Watershed.
From lakes and streams to the Mississippi River, Minnesota’s most valuable natural resource is water.
Keeping our water free of pollutants is crucial not only to our agricultural and environmental success, but also because we all have a right to clean drinking water. ​​ Zack authored and passed Clean Energy First, a bipartisan bill to combat climate change by ensuring the next generation of electrical generation comes from clean sources whenever possible.
Minnesotans deserve clean air and water, a sustainable future, and lower energy bills.
PREVENTING GUN VIOLENCE ​Every year, the list of preventable tragedies grows longer.
Gun violence should not be a part of the everyday American experience.
As a working prosecutor, Zack’s seen the damage guns do when they get into the hands of the wrong people.
And as a father of two elementary school aged children, and the husband of a high school teacher, keeping our schools safe is deeply personal to him.
We’re the only country in the world where kids face this fear going to school.
In addition to our schools, Zack believes Minnesotans should be safe from gun violence in all aspects of their lives, whether at the movie theater, a grocery store, or a concert.
Everyday people should be safe doing everyday things.
Zack is firmly committed to taking meaningful action to address gun violence.
He started this work in 2023, passing background checks for all gun purchases and establishing “Red Flag” emergency laws, which have already saved lives.
Zack will stand up to the gun lobby.
He puts safety over special interests, and will continue to work on responsible gun violence prevention measures that will save lives.
Local Infrastructure ​Investing in our infrastructure isn’t always flashy, but it’s essential — so bridges don’t fall, potholes are filled, and wastewater systems are updated.
Anyone who works with Zack at the Capitol knows that he advocates hard for local infrastructure, roads, and bridges because that’s work that invests directly into our communities, while saving money in the long-term.
He also knows that local investment, beautiful parks, and more opportunity in our district not only increases our quality of life, it drives people to our community and helps our small businesses.
In our community, Zack has delivered by: Fixing Highway 10 Expanding the Coon Rapids Recycling Center Taking the first steps towards building a new river crossing between Dayton and Ramsey Rebuilding Mississippi Point Park in Champlin Rebuilding and expanding the facility that houses the business and nursing programs at Anoka Ramsey Community College Establishing the Anoka Social District Delivering funding for Hope for Youth and Havens for Heroes PUBLIC SAFETY ​As a prosecutor, Zack is deeply committed to making sure that our community is safe.
People love living in Anoka and Coon Rapids because of how peaceful and safe our cities are.
But to preserve that quality of life, we need to be proactive in addressing public safety concerns.
At the Capitol, Zack works with law enforcement to ensure they have the resources they need to keep us safe, are able to prevent crime before it happens, and are able to recruit and retain dedicated, quality peace officers that do tireless work for our community.
In the Legislature, Zack was chief author of the bill to end Minnesota’s marital rape exception.
Before Zack’s bill passed, Minnesota law prevented rapists from being prosecuted in certain cases if the victim was married to their rapist.
Zack was also proud to pass a bill to hold big pharmaceutical companies accountable for the opioid epidemic.
He secured new funding to hire probation officers with expertise on mental health issues.
Zack also passed bills to crack down on catalytic converter theft and to provide local police departments more resources to combat auto theft, which has led to a dramatic decrease in that crime across the state.
Data-driven, responsible public safety policy works.
Everyone in our community deserves to be safe from harm.
GETTING SENIORS WHAT THEY NEED ​Zack is committed to protecting Minnesota’s seniors.
They should be able to enjoy retirement with dignity, have sustainable budgets in the face of rising costs, and have excellent care.
In addition to fighting to bring down pharmaceutical costs, Zack advocates for quality, affordable senior housing, property tax cuts, and strong pensions.
Zack meets with seniors, hears their concerns, and advocates for what they need at the Capitol.
That’s why he fought to exempt social security from Minnesota income taxes.
As a result of tax cuts Zack fought for, seniors with an annual income of less than $85,000 per year ($104,000 for a married couple) no longer pay state taxes on social security - which includes the vast majority of seniors in our state.
It’s also why Minnesota has more protections against scammers who are most likely to rip off seniors than every state around us. ​ RESPONSIBLE BUDGETING ​Zack’s dad always told him “you get what you pay for.” Zack is a responsible, forward-looking leader that is committed to making sure we get more for our tax dollars, and that our tax system is fair for average people and small business owners.
He also believes that a state budget should be built thoughtfully, with every cut and spending adjustment made with the good of Minnesotans in mind.
Budgets in the past decades have been built to help the ultra-wealthy and large corporations, and it’s not right.
Zack fights for budgets that fund tangible improvements in Minnesotans’ lives, ensures a sustainable path forward, and is fairly funded by the people who profit most from our society.
Zack feels strongly that middle class and working class families are already paying their fair share in taxes, while prices rise and billionaires rake in record profits.
Zack fights to shift the burden from hardworking Minnesotans to the ultra-wealthy, out-of-state corporations (like AI and social media companies) that pay pennies compared to what they paid into our system pre-1980s.
WOMEN's RIGHTS ​ ​The Supreme Court's decision to overturn Roe v.
Wade reversed fifty years of settled law and marked the first time in American history that the Supreme Court took away a constitutional right it had previously recognized.
Zack strongly disagrees with the Court's decision and believes that women have the right to make their own decisions about their health care.
Zack supports preserving access to reproductive health care in Minnesota and opposes attempts to ban abortion.
Minnesota has taken strong steps to guarantee reproductive care, but access to that right shouldn't be kept behind high costs.
Zack authored legislation, now law, requiring that health insurance include abortion care.
Zack also believes that more should be done to advance women's rights.
He has voted for the Equal Rights Amendment, which would enshrine women's rights in the U.S.
