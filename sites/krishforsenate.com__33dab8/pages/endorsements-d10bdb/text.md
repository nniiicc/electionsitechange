Why we're standing with Krish.
Dr.
Krishnan (Krish) Subrahmanian is a beloved local physician and dedicated active community member who lives in Eden Prairie with his wife and three children.
Dr.
Krish serves as Chief Medical Officer at Hennepin Health and co-chairs the Early Childhood Caucus for the American Academy of Pediatrics Minnesota Chapter.
Like Senator Cwodzinski, Krish has had a life-long passion for service.
His early work included combating the HIV epidemic in sub-Saharan Africa and helping children with critical illness through the Baylor International Pediatric AIDS Initiative and SeriousFun camps.
In addition to being a celebrated physician, he is an educator, teaching at the University of Minnesota medical school, as well as a member of the Air National Guard, where he has served as an Air Force flight surgeon.
We are supporting Krish for office because we believe our community deserves leaders who bring people together, who listen first, speak with respect, and lead with heart.
The spirit that makes Eden Prairie and Minnetonka great places to raise a family is the same spirit that can guide our state forward: one of kindness, stewardship, and shared purpose.
Krish is the right choice for this moment because he has what it takes to give back and to help ensure that every Minnesotan has the opportunity to grow, thrive, and belong.
