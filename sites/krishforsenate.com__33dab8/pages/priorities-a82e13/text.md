On this page Health, safety & democracy Education & our kids' future The care economy & working families Environment & infrastructure 01 Health, safety & democracy When our neighbors are threatened, we all are.
That's why I'll fight to: Defend science-based public health 3 commitments + Support evidence-based medicine — including vaccines and other public health interventions.
Invest in workforce pipelines across nursing, EMS, and behavioral health.
Treat public health infrastructure as essential infrastructure.
Protect democracy itself 3 commitments + Ensure federal actions in Minnesota are subject to state oversight and judicial review.
Invest in election infrastructure and counter misinformation.
Uphold transparency for all law enforcement operating in Minnesota.
Defend reproductive freedom & healthcare 4 commitments + Advocate for a state Equal Rights Amendment protecting reproductive healthcare and LGBTQ+ rights.
Preserve the sacred relationship between patient and provider from political interference.
Expand access to preventive care for children, seniors, families, and rural communities.
Strengthen Minnesota's safety-net hospitals and clinics.
Protect Minnesotans from federal overreach 4 commitments + Require all law enforcement in Minnesota to display visible identification.
Prohibit immigration enforcement in hospitals, schools, and houses of worship without a judicial warrant.
Establish a community stabilization framework for families hit hardest by federal actions.
Ensure federal actions in Minnesota are subject to state oversight and judicial review.
02 Education & our kids' future Every child deserves the chance to succeed — teachers who see potential, not obstacles.
That's why I'll fight to: Strengthen public education 3 commitments + Fully fund public schools, special education, and early childhood programs.
Support safe, inclusive schools that partner with families from all backgrounds.
Expand school-based mental health services.
Protect kids from technology harms 3 commitments + Establish guardrails for AI deployment in schools and healthcare.
Implement age-appropriate restrictions on personal technology in K–12 classrooms.
Prevent algorithmic bias and protect privacy.
Address gun violence as a public health crisis 3 commitments + Invest in community-based violence prevention programs.
Promote safe firearm storage to reduce youth access.
Support background checks and extreme risk protection orders.
03 The care economy & working families Work should pay.
Families should thrive.
That's not complicated.
That's why I'll fight to: Support working families 3 commitments + Expand affordable childcare and early learning.
Support family caregivers through paid family and medical leave.
Invest in programs that allow families to care for aging loved ones with dignity.
Protect workers 4 commitments + Defend the right to organize and collectively bargain.
Enact protections for workers in the emerging AI economy.
Support pathways to family-sustaining jobs.
Ensure safe working conditions.
Preserve Medicaid 3 commitments + Strengthen Medicaid as the backbone of Minnesota's safety-net system.
Use data to address fraud while protecting patient access.
Improve care coordination and manage costs effectively.
04 Environment & infrastructure Clean air, clean water, safe roads — the basics aren't luxuries.
That's why I'll fight to: Protect our environment 3 commitments + Enforce science-based regulation of air and water quality.
Accelerate transition to clean energy while supporting workers in transition.
Invest in climate-resilient infrastructure.
Make government work 3 commitments + Build public dashboards tracking spending and outcomes.
Strengthen oversight to prevent waste and fraud.
Coordinate housing, health, transportation, and education policy toward measurable family outcomes.
