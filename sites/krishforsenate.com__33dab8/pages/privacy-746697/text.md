A short, plain-English rundown — How the Krish for Senate campaign collects and uses the information you share with us on this website.
Last updated · May 14, 2026 Introduction Krish for Senate ("the Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website and interact with our online forms.
By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
Information we collect Information you provide directly We collect personal information when you voluntarily submit it through one of the following forms on our website: Newsletter signup Email address Contact form Name Email address Message content Any other information you choose to include Volunteer signup form Name Email address Phone number (if provided) Location or availability (if provided) Any other information you choose to include Information collected automatically We use Squarespace Analytics, a built-in analytics service provided by Squarespace, Inc., to understand how visitors interact with our website.
This may include: Pages visited and time spent on each page Referring website or source Browser type and operating system General geographic location (country/region level) Device type How we use your information We use the information we collect for the following purposes: To send campaign newsletters and updates to subscribers To respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form To coordinate volunteer activities and follow up with interested volunteers To understand website traffic and improve user experience To comply with applicable federal and state campaign finance laws and reporting requirements How we share your information We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.
We may share your information in the following limited circumstances: Service providers.
With vendors who assist us in operating the website or conducting campaign activities (e.g., Squarespace), subject to confidentiality obligations.
Legal compliance.
When required by law, including compliance with Federal Election Commission (FEC) or applicable state campaign finance disclosure requirements.
Campaign operations.
With authorized campaign staff and volunteers who need access to carry out legitimate campaign activities.
Campaign finance disclosure Please be aware that contributions to political campaigns may be subject to disclosure requirements under federal and/or state law.
Information you provide in connection with any donation may be reported to the relevant regulatory authorities as required.
Data retention We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable law.
You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Your choices below).
Your choices You have the following rights with respect to your personal information: Unsubscribe.
You may opt out of campaign emails at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email we send you, or by contacting us directly.
Access or correction.
You may request access to or correction of the personal information we hold about you.
Deletion.
You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to any legal retention obligations.
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.
Children's privacy This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.
Third-party links Our website may contain links to third-party websites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies independently.
Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
We encourage you to review this page periodically.
