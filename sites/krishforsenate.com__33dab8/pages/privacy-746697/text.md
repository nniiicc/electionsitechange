Introduction
Krish for Senate ("the Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website and interact with our online forms.
By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
Information we collect

We collect personal information when you voluntarily submit it through one of the following forms on our website:
Newsletter signup
- Email address
Contact form
- Name
- Email address
- Message content
- Any other information you choose to include
Volunteer signup form
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number (if provided)

- Any other information you choose to include
Information collected automatically
We use Squarespace Analytics, a built-in analytics service provided by Squarespace, Inc., to understand how visitors interact with our website.
This may include:
- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Referring website or source
- Browser type and operating system
- General geographic location (country/region level)
- Device type
Squarespace Analytics uses cookies and similar tracking technologies.
For more information, please review Squarespace's Privacy Policy.
How we use your information
We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
- To send campaign newsletters and updates to subscribers
- To respond to inquiries submitted through the contact form
- To coordinate volunteer activities and follow up with interested volunteers
- To understand website traffic and improve user experience
- To comply with applicable federal and state campaign finance laws and reporting requirements
How we share your information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.
We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:
- Service providers.
With vendors who assist us in operating the website or conducting campaign activities (e.g., Squarespace), subject to confidentiality obligations.
- Legal compliance.
When required by law, including compliance with Federal Election Commission (FEC) or applicable state campaign finance disclosure requirements.
- Campaign operations.
With authorized campaign staff and volunteers who need access to carry out legitimate campaign activities.
Campaign finance disclosure
Please be aware that contributions to political campaigns may be subject to disclosure requirements under federal and/or state law.
Information you provide in connection with any donation may be reported to the relevant regulatory authorities as required.
Cookies
Our website, hosted on Squarespace, uses cookies to support analytics and basic site functionality.
You may be able to disable cookies through your browser settings, though doing so may affect your experience on the site.
Data retention
We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, or as required by applicable law.
You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Your choices below).
Your choices
You have the following rights with respect to your personal information:
- Unsubscribe.
You may opt out of campaign emails at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in any email we send you, or by contacting us directly.
- Access or correction.
You may request access to or correction of the personal information we hold about you.
- Deletion.
You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to any legal retention obligations.
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.
