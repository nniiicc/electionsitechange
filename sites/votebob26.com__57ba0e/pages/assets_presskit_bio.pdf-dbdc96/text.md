Bob Heitkamp is a familiar face across the prairies and small towns of North Dakota.
A lifelong entrepreneur who started his first business at just sixteen years old, Bob understands the grit and determination it takes to build something from the ground up.
After studying at NDSCS and Valley City State University, he went on to launch several successful local businesses, gaining firsthand experience with the regulatory hurdles and infrastructure needs that affect our local economy.
Today, he channels that same energy into his role as the Executive Director of Marketplace for Kids, where he helps the next generation of North Dakotans discover their own potential.
Bob is running for State Senate as a nonpartisan leader because he believes in common sense solutions over political labels.
He is focused on modernizing our aging infrastructure, protecting our vital agricultural programs, and ensuring our education system prepares students for the modern workforce.
Bob is not a career politician; he is a neighbor who wants to make a difference.
He is ready to bring his business mindset and community heart to Bismarck to ensure District 25 remains a place where families and businesses can thrive for generations to come.
