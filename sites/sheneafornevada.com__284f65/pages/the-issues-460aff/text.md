Her priorities reflect the everyday challenges facing Nevadans and her commitment to meaningful, community-driven progress.
01 A Strong and Resilient Economy Nevada’s future depends on a diversified economy that can withstand changes in tourism and hospitality.
Shenea will: N Support small businesses and local entrepreneurs N Expand opportunities in technology, clean energy, arts, and entertainment N Create career pathways in emerging industries to keep Nevada competitive and strong A stronger, more balanced economy means stability, good jobs, and opportunity for Nevada families.
02 Practical Education and Workforce Training Education is the foundation of economic success.
Shenea believes every Nevadan should have access to: N Quality K–12 education that prepares students for the future N Affordable career and technical education programs N Partnerships between schools and local industries to build clear paths to good jobs Her focus is on ensuring Nevada’s workforce is skilled, adaptable, and ready for 21st-century opportunities.
03 Safe and Healthy Communities Communities thrive when people feel safe and supported.
Shenea will: N Strengthen mental health services, especially for youth and veterans N Advocate for fair worker protections, including for gig and independent workers N Support local investments in parks, community spaces, and infrastructure that improve quality of life Strong communities are the backbone of Nevada’s success.
04 Supporting Worker Protections Nevadans deserve workplaces that respect their rights, safety, and dignity.
