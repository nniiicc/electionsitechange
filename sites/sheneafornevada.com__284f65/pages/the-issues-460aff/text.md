The Issues
Shenea's Solutions
Shenea’s campaign focuses on practical solutions that deliver for Nevada families.
Her priorities reflect the everyday challenges facing Nevadans and her commitment to meaningful, community-driven progress.
01
A Strong and Resilient Economy
Nevada’s future depends on a diversified economy that can withstand changes in tourism and hospitality.
Shenea will:
Support small businesses and local entrepreneurs
Expand opportunities in technology, clean energy, arts, and entertainment
Create career pathways in emerging industries to keep Nevada competitive and strong
A stronger, more balanced economy means stability, good jobs, and opportunity for Nevada families.
02
Practical Education and Workforce Training
Education is the foundation of economic success.
Shenea believes every Nevadan should have access to:
Quality K–12 education that prepares students for the future
Affordable career and technical education programs
Partnerships between schools and local industries to build clear paths to good jobs
Her focus is on ensuring Nevada’s workforce is skilled, adaptable, and ready for 21st-century opportunities.
03
Safe and Healthy Communities
Communities thrive when people feel safe and supported.
Shenea will:
Strengthen mental health services, especially for youth and veterans
Advocate for fair worker protections, including for gig and independent workers
Support local investments in parks, community spaces, and infrastructure that improve quality of life
Strong communities are the backbone of Nevada’s success.
04
Supporting Worker Protections
Nevadans deserve workplaces that respect their rights, safety, and dignity.
Shenea will fight for:
Fair wages and clear safety standards
Expanded protections for workers in all industries
Better access to benefits for all workers, regardless of classification
