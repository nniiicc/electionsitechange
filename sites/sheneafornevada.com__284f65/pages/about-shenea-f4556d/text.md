Home About Shenea The Issues Volunteer Contact Donate Select Page About Shenea Meet the candidate Shenea Booth is a dedicated community leader, athlete, and advocate for working families.
Born with a passion for performance and a drive to succeed, she rose to become a world champion acrogymnast and circus artist—a role that taught her leadership, resilience, and teamwork.
After years of performing around the world, Shenea returned to Nevada to focus on community building and public service.
Her experiences in athletics, arts, and community engagement have shaped her belief that Nevada’s future depends on inclusive opportunity, strong families, and a thriving economy that works for everyone.
Why She’s Running Shenea is running for the Nevada State Assembly because she believes District 22 deserves leadership that: Listens to Nevada families and workers Fights for economic stability and upward mobility Champions education, safety, and opportunity Strengthens communities from Henderson to Green Valley Ranch Her campaign is about real results, transparency, and accountability in state government.
