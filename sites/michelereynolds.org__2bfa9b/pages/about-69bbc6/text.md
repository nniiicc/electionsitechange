Who I Am
I am a lifelong resident of Ohio and have lived in the district for 25 years.
I am a Pastor’s wife, the proud mother of James and Noah, and the daughter and wife of military veterans.
I reside in Canal Winchester, Ohio with my family.
I am a third-generation business owner, a nonprofit CEO, a woman of faith, and a proud Buckeye!
WHAT I DO
I was elected to the Ohio State Senate in 2022, proudly representing Ohio Senate District 3.
My district includes Pickaway County, Madison County, and parts of Franklin County—a vibrant and diverse area that brings together urban, suburban, and rural communities.
As a lifelong Ohio resident, I have dedicated my career to serving local communities.
My journey includes serving as a Madison Township Trustee, where I gained valuable experience representing and advocating for my neighbors.
I hold degrees in Criminal Justice, Public Administration/Urban Studies, and Business, which have provided me with a well-rounded perspective in my work.
In 2026, I earned my Juris Doctor degree at the University of Dayton School of Law to further enhance my ability to serve Ohioans.
Since 2004, I have also been a licensed Realtor in Ohio, helping families and individuals find their homes across our great state.
