Why Erin Edwards?
Erin Edwards is a strong conservative leader committed to protecting Wyoming values and ensuring Wyoming remains a great place to raise our children and grandchildren. ​ Wyoming is God's Country—a place built by hardworking people, strong communities, and common-sense conservative principles.
Erin believes the next generation deserves the opportunity to build successful futures right here at home. ​ She will always put the families and communities of House District 41 ahead of political insiders and special interests, focusing on practical solutions that strengthen Wyoming's future while preserving our way of life.
Meet Erin Erin Edwards is a lifelong Wyoming resident who has spent her life serving others.
She's committed to practical, common-sense leadership rooted in listening first and acting responsibly.
Her focus is on serving neighbors—not political agendas—and making sure everyday voices in House District 41 are heard and respected. ​ She is a University of Wyoming Criminal Justice graduate and has served as a volunteer firefighter for 24 years. ​ During Operation Iraqi Freedom, Erin spent two years supporting American military personnel overseas as a contract firefighter for the Department of Defense.
She believes effective representation comes from staying grounded in the community, showing up consistently, and prioritizing solutions over partisanship.

Troops Operation Iraqi Freedom Focusing On Wyoming's Future Fiscal Responsibility & Accountability Erin believes the government should live within its means just like Wyoming families do.
That means prioritizing essential services, eliminating waste, and ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent transparently and effectively.
Erin supports regular review of programs to measure results, increase accountability, and make sure spending delivers real value to the public.
Education Strong schools are the foundation of Wyoming’s future.
Erin supports empowering parents, respecting teachers, and focusing on academic fundamentals like reading, writing, math, and critical thinking.
Local communities—not distant bureaucracies—should have a strong voice in shaping educational priorities that prepare students for careers, trades, and civic life.
Wyoming's Core Industries Wyoming depends on strong, stable core industries including energy, agriculture, tourism, and mineral production.
Erin supports policies that keep these sectors competitive, protect good-paying local jobs, and ensure responsible stewardship of our natural resources.
We should reduce unnecessary barriers while encouraging innovation and long-term economic strength.
A Culture of Life A culture that values life also means protecting the vulnerable.
That includes taking a firm stance against human trafficking, drug trafficking, and exploitation in all forms.
Erin supports stronger enforcement tools, great coordination among law enforcement agencies, and increased community awareness so that no victim is invisible and no trafficker operates without consequences.
Constitutional Rights Erin is committed to protecting the rights guaranteed by the U.S.
Constitution, including free speech, religious liberty, due process, and the right to bear arms.
These rights are not negotiable and must be defended consistently, even when it is unpopular.
Government’s role is to safeguard liberty—not restrict it.
Local Control Decisions should be made as close to the people as possible.
Local governments, school boards, and community leaders understand their needs better than distant state or federal agencies.
Erin supports restoring authority to local levels so residents have real influence over the policies that shape their daily lives.
I will represent House District 41 not a caucus, not special interests, and not political insiders.
House District 41 is my caucus.
