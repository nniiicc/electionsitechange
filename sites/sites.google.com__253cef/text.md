Willard Haley has lived in the 58th District of Missouri for 38 years.
He and his wife Jerri, of 36 years, have two children and two grandchildren.
Willard taught agriculture at Eldon High School for 25 years.
He then worked as the Eldon Career Center Director for seven years.
Mr.
Haley was able to balance these responsibilities all while maintain his agriculture endeavors.
Willard shares, "I have dedicated my life to serving others and it would be an honor to be selected as the representative of the 58th District of Missouri so that I may continue this work.
I am interested in being a voice for all constituents and would represent all citizens to the best of my ability if elected."
