Alexander For Colorado House District 60
Matt is a 4th generation Colorado native, successful small business owner of 20 years, and a dedicated father of three.
He has also been serving on the Canon City School board since 2023.
His experience as an entrepreneur, parent, and school board director, brings him the perfect skills to represent rural Coloradans in Denver.
Matt's campaign is rooted in restoring common-sense values to the state legislature.
He is fiercely, Pro-Life, Pro 2nd amendment, and passionate about protecting the rights and freedoms of Coloradans.
Matt also wants to ensure that public education is free from political ideologies and is preparing children for their best future.
Vote Matt Alexander for a safer, stronger Colorado!
Matt is passionate about Colorado, wildlife, ranches, water rights, education, but most of all he cares for her people.
Coloradans deserve good representation in Denver.
He will fight for your rights and freedoms, fighting to curb taxes disguised as fees and he will fight to keep your TABOR rights.
The power to decide the future of Colorado should be in the hands of the YOU, the people!
