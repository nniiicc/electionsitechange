Host a Lawn Sign
Show your support for Amáda by hosting a lawn sign!
How it Works
- Request a lawn sign (below).
Afterwards, donate what you can to cover the costs of producing the sign.
- A campaign volunteer will deliver the sign directly to your yard as soon as possible.
- When the campaign is over, volunteers will collect the sign and reuse it in the future.
DO NOT store the lawn sign or throw it away.
Lawn Sign Rules
Minnesotans are allowed to display candidate lawn signs from 46 days before the Primary Election to 10 days following the General Election (June 26, 2026 to November 13, 2026).
Lawn signs must only be displayed on residential property by the property owner or tenant.
If you received a lawn sign by mistake, or if your sign is broken or lost, please let us know, and we’ll fix the problem immediately.
