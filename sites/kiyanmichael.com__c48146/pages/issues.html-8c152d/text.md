One of the top issues facing Florida’s families this year has been the rising cost of property insurance.
It affects everyone from homeowners to renters and almost every consumer as small businesses have to raise the prices of their goods and services to pay for this expense.
There have been three main factors driving up the cost of insurance, and believe it or not, it is not storms.
The factors are fraud, litigation, and inflation.
At the state level, we have done what we can to put some reasonable legislation in place to stop fraud and frivolous lawsuits.
Unfortunately, Joe Biden’s inflation and federal price fixing have had a nationwide impact on the replacement costs for homes and apartments.
We must be patient and continue to look for ways to bring the cost of property insurance with the levers we have at the state level, and then we need to send Joe Biden and Kamala Harris into permanent retirement in November.
That will help ease inflation and should bring rates back down in Florida and even across the country.
