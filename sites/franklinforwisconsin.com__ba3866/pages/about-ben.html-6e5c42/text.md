| | ABOUT BEN Ben Franklin was born and raised here in the Midwest.
He enjoyed participating in athletics and volunteering in the community.
Shortly after graduating from High School, he continued volunteering in the community.
He served in various capacities in Relay for Life, Bowl for Kids Sake, Big Brothers Big Sisters, Boy Scouts of America, Church, and played in the community band “McNamara’s Band”.
Ben’s yearning for service continued after he married his wife Sharon.
With her unwavering support, much of their marriage was spent in the United States Air Force.
During this time, Ben progressed quickly through the ranks.
With service as a staple, Ben served thousands of hours in organizations and events outside of duty hours, earning him the Military Outstanding Voluntary Service Medal.
He also sharpened his leadership skills during his time in the Air Force and completed his Bachelor’s Degree.
Earning the degree was a proud moment for Ben as he was working full-time, attending school full-time, and had a family to help raise. |
Ben currently lives in De Pere, Wisconsin with his wife and two children.
As a small business owner and overseeing a multi-unit portfolio of restaurants across the state, he has firsthand experience in what it takes to create jobs, manage teams, and keep businesses strong.
He understands that economic stability is the foundation of a thriving community—and that a strong economy benefits everyone.
In addition to his business leadership, Ben has played a key role in teaching and facilitating a nationwide leadership development program for multi-unit managers in the restaurant industry.
He believes investing in people and developing future leaders is essential to building long-term strength in our workforce and our communities.
Ben’s commitment to service before self and his passion for helping others have driven him to continue serving in the State Legislature.
Drawing on his background in military service, business, and his experience in office, he is focused on delivering results and creating opportunities for the people of the 88th Assembly District.
As he campaigns for re-election, Ben looks forward to connecting with residents across Northeast Wisconsin.
As he’s proven in his first term, he is ready to listen, tackle challenges head-on, and work toward solutions that move our communities forward.
Your continued support is truly appreciated.
