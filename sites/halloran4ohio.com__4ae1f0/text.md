Kent Halloran is an Environmental Engineer with more than 30 years of experience designing and maintaining the infrastructure that communities depend on every day, specializing in stormwater and wastewater systems.
His interest in environmental stewardship began as a child after learning about the Cuyahoga River fire and attending one of the first Earth Day celebrations.
Those experiences sparked a lifelong commitment to protecting Ohio's natural resources, improving public infrastructure, and ensuring future generations inherit strong, healthy communities.
Throughout his career, Kent has focused on practical problem-solving, responsible planning, and finding solutions that balance economic growth with environmental responsibility.
As an engineer and former educator, he understands the importance of long-term thinking, fiscal responsibility, and investing wisely in the systems that support our quality of life.
Now, Kent is bringing that same problem-solving approach to public service.
He believes the people of Ohio House District 86 deserve leadership focused on accountability, affordability, strong schools, reliable infrastructure, and economic opportunity.
Rather than partisan politics, Kent wants to focus on delivering results and addressing the everyday challenges facing Ohio families.
Kent lives in Marion, Ohio, with his wife, Krista.
He looks forward to meeting voters throughout District 86, listening to their concerns, and working together to build a stronger future for our communities.
