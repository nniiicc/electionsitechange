Home News About Steve Priorities Endorsements Voter Info Volunteer Contribute Contact Governor Hochul Signs Legislation to Advance Climate Action and Protect Environment September 28, 2026 Governor Hochul today signed three pieces of legislation focused on clean energy upgrades, climate education and research, and environmental protection, including one establishing an electric landscaping equipment rebate program.
“I will never stop fighting to clean up the air we breathe,” Governor Hochul said.
“The rebate legislation I signed will encourage the purchase of cleaner and quieter battery-powered lawn mowers and other landscaping equipment that will benefit us all.” Assemblymember Steven Otis said, “Thank you to Governor Hochul for supporting small businesses and institutions in transitioning to emission-free equipment that will improve air quality, public health and make purchase of new equipment more affordable for local contractors.
