WHITE PLAINS, NY — An arts scene that is unique to Westchester just got a historic boost from the state.
ArtsWestchester in White Plains is set to receive a $5 million New York State budget allocation to modernize its landmarked headquarters and enhance cultural programming.
The organization described it as the largest single state investment in ArtsWestchester's history.
