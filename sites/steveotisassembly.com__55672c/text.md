Vote on Election Day, Tuesday, November 3rd or Via Early Voting (Saturday, October 24 through Sunday, November 1) Dear Friends, In the world we live in today, voting is more important than ever.
With so many of our beliefs of what democracy means at issue, voting is more important than ever.
My focus continues to be on providing tireless and effective service in addressing issues of importance to New York State, Westchester, and every community I represent.
I work closely with the municipal officials, school district advocates, local not-for-profits, and every individual resident on the Sound Shore.
That in-depth involvement helps me win results on issues affecting Westchester every year.
This year, victories on school aid, clean water funding, environmental and flood mitigation issues have benefited the Sound Shore.
My goal is effective service, detailed attention, and results that make a difference every year.
I also play a leadership role on EMS reform, support for digital equity and inclusion programs, and on fast moving science and technology issues including cybersecurity, AI governance, and data privacy.
Teamwork is an important part of my work in the Assembly.
If you live in the 91 st Assembly District, please support longtime governmental partners who are also on the ballot in November: State Senator Shelley Mayer, State Senator Nathalia Fernandez, and Congressman George Latimer.
I work closely with each of these colleagues on countless issues of importance to our communities.
I am running on the Democratic Party (Row A) and Working Families Party (Row D) lines in November.
Under NYS law, maintaining a party line on the ballot depends upon how many votes are cast for President or Governor each even-numbered year.
Currently, there are four such parties in New York.
Your vote for Kathy Hochul and Adrienne Adams for Governor and Lieutenant Governor on the WFP line (Row D) will assure the party continues to have a statewide line on the ballot along with the Democratic, Republican, and Conservative parties.
Thank you for your past support and your vote on November 3rd.
It is a privilege to serve the Sound Shore communities of Larchmont, Mamaroneck, New Rochelle, Port Chester, Rye, and Rye Brook.
My goal is always to win results on issues we care about, to make a difference for the communities and families I represent, and to break new ground on issues that require fresh thinking and new solutions.
Thank you for the ideas and comments that enhance my advocacy.
I will continue to devote my energy and devotion to service.
