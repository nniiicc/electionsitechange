Who is Dave Fournier?
Dave Fournier is a Northwest Ohio attorney, husband, father of three, lifelong resident of the community, and candidate for the Ohio House of Representatives in District 44.
Ohio House District 44
Clear, campaign-supplied information about Dave's background, priorities, and support for working people.
Dave is running to represent Ohio House District 44 in the Ohio House of Representatives.
Dave's campaign emphasizes affordability and real property-tax relief for homeowners; cleaning up corruption and sweetheart deals in Columbus; and lowering healthcare premiums and prescription drug costs while protecting access to care.
In his legal practice, Dave helps trustees of workers' health and retirement plans manage funds responsibly, avoid conflicts of interest, and put the people they serve first.
Dave supports collective bargaining, the right to organize, prevailing wage, responsible public contracting, and protecting benefits workers earned at the bargaining table.
He opposes so-called “right-to-work” schemes.
Dave has announced endorsements from United Auto Workers Region 2B; Teamsters Local 20; Ohio Education Association; Ohio Federation of Teachers; Toledo Firefighters IAFF Local 92; Communications Workers of America Local 4319; Plumbers and Pipefitters UA Local 50; Operative Plasterers and Cement Masons Local 886; AFSCME Ohio Council 8; Toledo Federation of Teachers; Ohio AFL-CIO; LiUNA Laborers' Local 500; Toledo Port Council; and Planned Parenthood Advocates of Ohio.
Dave opposes data center construction unless strict standards are met.
His plan calls for no tax breaks or tax incentives; significant payments to local tax funds; company-produced off-grid power; closed-loop cooling systems that use minimal water; company payment of all infrastructure costs; and ongoing independent environmental monitoring.
Dave says working families in District 44 are being squeezed by rising household costs, healthcare expenses, and deals in Columbus that favor powerful corporations and unaccountable private programs.
He is running to put working families first.
