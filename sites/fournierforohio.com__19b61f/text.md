Protecting our communities
No data center construction until strict standards are met—and taxpayers, ratepayers, and local resources are protected.
Absolutely no tax breaks or tax incentives.
Companies building data centers must make significant payments to local tax funds that benefit our schools, libraries, parks, and emergency services.
Data centers must produce their own power off the grid so they do not increase local electric costs.
They must also use closed-loop cooling systems that use minimal water.
All infrastructure costs must be paid by the company building the data center—not by local taxpayers.
Ongoing, independent environmental monitoring must ensure continued compliance with these standards.
