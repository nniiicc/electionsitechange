MEET RON LEADERSHIP DELIVERING RESULTS FOR OUR COMMUNITY Ron Mendive has lived the majority of his seventy-four years in North Idaho working in mining, logging, and the construction industry.
He is currently self-employed, operating a land service and excavating business.
He helped develop the technology that is used in a successful internationally marketed line of pellet stoves.
Ron grew up in the Silver Valley fishing, hunting, skiing and enjoying life in North Idaho.
Fifty-four years ago he met his wife, Sherlene, at North Idaho College.
Our dream was to have a small farm.
We purchased acreage south of Coeur D'Alene and built a log home where we raised our three children.
I will to continue to be your legislative representative who fights to "Secure the Blessings of Liberty for Ourselves and our Posterity." PICTURES OF RON © # by Don Richards.
