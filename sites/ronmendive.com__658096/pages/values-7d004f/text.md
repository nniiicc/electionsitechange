Our founders listed: Life, Liberty and Property rights as unalienable rights that are endowed upon men by their Creator.
They fought a revolution to secure those rights.
Fortunately, they gave us the right to vote so we can protect these God given rights without going to war.
We must exercise our right to vote and always be vigilant to preserve these incredible freedoms.
VALUES
Life
I believe that the founders included the pre-born in their definition of the unalienable right to life.
Pro-Life: I was privileged to be a member of the Idaho Legislature when we passed bill SB 1385 in the 2020 legislative session.
It stated that if Roe vs Wade was ever overturned this bill would be triggered to make abortion in Idaho illegal.
With the Supreme court decision to overturn Roe, I can now proudly state that elective abortions are illegal in Idaho.
Liberty
The first ten amendments to the U.
S.
Constitution (The Bill of Rights) were put in place to protect our Liberty.
If America is going to continue to be a great nation, we must continue to honor and utilize those rights.
Property
Pursuit of Happiness - I believe in personal property rights.
The ability to own private property is a GOD given right and the cornerstone of our Republic.
Government was instituted to protect such rights, not to overregulate or "take away" these rights.
Private property rights are a key indicator of our personal freedom.
Slaves are not allowed to own property.
