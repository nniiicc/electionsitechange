Mar 21, 2025
It was a very hectic but productive week in Nashville.
I was able to pass seven bills on through the committee process this week
including my biomarker bill, ambulance reimbursement and the “Protect Children from Child Exploitation Act”.
The Protect
Children from Child Exploitation Act closes a loophole in the legal system where currently Electronic Service Providers can tip off
individuals who have a subpoena against their phones.
When these folks get tipped off, they often delete all history which is
where the evidence is saved.
By closing this loophole, we will be able to prosecute the individuals faster and help protect the
children from these predators.
