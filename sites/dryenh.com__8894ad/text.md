Margaret Drye ABOUT MARGARET "I once read a short essay my mother wrote about her grandmother, who was an immigrant from Ireland.
Apparently, my great-grandmother loved baseball and politics - national, state, and local.
I never met my great-grandmother, but I definitely inherited her love of both baseball and politics.
So, I guess you could say that politics is in my blood..." Margaret Drye is a Republican member of the New Hampshire House from Plainfield, first elected in 2022.
She holds a mathematics degree from Mount Holyoke College (1980, cum laude) and has been a nationally registered emergency medical technician since 1979.
With her husband Rob, she has volunteered in local emergency medical services — the Cornish Rescue Squad — for more than four decades.
She completed Leadership New Hampshire in 1996.
She is a graduate and former board member of the Vesta Roy Excellence in Public Service Series.
She and Rob homeschooled their nine children.
DONATE Margaret Drye is a 46 year resident of Plainfield, NH.

Over 25 years as the Sullivan County 4-H club leader Over 50 years as a volunteer American Red Cross/American Heart Association CPR instructor She holds a current seat as Cemetery Trustee, presides over the Meriden Bird Club, serves as a Trustee of the Eastern States Exposition (The Big E), and is a Field Editor for Taste of Home magazine.
