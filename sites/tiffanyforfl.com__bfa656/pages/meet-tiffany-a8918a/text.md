Thank You, Tiffany Esposito was born and raised in Lee County and attended Lee County public schools prior to earning a bachelor’s degree in communications and a Master of Business Administration from FGCU.
After college Tiffany and her husband’s first home together was in Lehigh Acres.
Tiffany is currently the President and CEO of Southwest Florida Incorporated where she leads the way in promoting and protecting local businesses and working on solutions to confront the workforce shortage plaguing our economy.
Tiffany is running for the Florida House to ensure Florida never faces the mandates and shutdowns we have seen in liberal states across the country.
She recognizes our freedoms are under constant attack and is devoted to protecting our individual liberties from the overreaching arm of government.
As a conservative, we can count on Tiffany to fight for lower taxes, a fiscally responsible budget, and to protect and preserve our Constitutional rights.
Tiffany and her husband Danny Kittinger have been married for nearly 10 years and reside in San Carlos Park.
