Privacy Policy
Committee to Elect Conrad Cable (“the campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is dedicated to safeguarding your personal information.
When you share with our campaign, we handle the information with care and respect.
This page explains the type of information we collect, how it’s used, and how we keep it safe when you visit our website, donate, volunteer, or engage with the campaign.

What We Collect
The type of information we gather:
- Data you share with us: your name, address, email, phone number, and payment details when you donate, sign up, volunteer, or get in touch.
- Data that comes automatically: information about your device, IP address, browser type, and online activities (gathered by cookies and analytics software).
- Data from donations: contributions are processed securely by ActBlue or another trusted service.
We don’t keep your full payment details on file.
How We Use It
The information we collect will be used to:
- Update you on campaign news, events, and ways to get involved.
- Process contributions and meet all campaign finance reporting requirements.
- Answer your questions and respond to requests.
- Improve our website and make our outreach stronger.
Sharing of Information
We only share information when it’s necessary:
- With trusted vendors who help us run the campaign (like email or donation platforms).
- To follow the law.
The Federal Election Commission (FEC) requires us to report certain donor information.
If you give above the legal threshold, your name, address, occupation, and employer may be made public.
- To keep our website and systems secure.
We will never sell or rent your personal information.
Period.
Cookies and Tracking
Like most websites, we may use cookies to make the site work better and to understand how folks are using it.
You can turn cookies off in your browser settings, but some features may not work as well.
Your Choices
You may unsubscribe from our email updates at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our emails.
And if you ever have a question about your data, contact us.
We’re happy to help!
Keeping Your Information Safe
We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
The internet isn’t perfect, but we do everything we can to keep your information safe.
Updates to This Policy

Contact Us
