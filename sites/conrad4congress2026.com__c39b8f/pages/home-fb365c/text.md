Meet Conrad
Conrad is a first-generation farmer from north Louisiana who knows what it means to work hard and live by his word.
He’s running for Congress to fight for living wages, protect Medicare and Medicaid, and make sure no family in our state goes hungry.
Conrad can’t be bought by lobbyists or billionaires.
He’ll go to Washington to stand up for working people and root out corruption.
- Louisiana was built by hard-working people.
Today, families work harder than ever while big corporations make record profits.
Every worker deserves fair pay, the right to organize, and a voice on the job.
I’ll fight to raise wages, protect unions, and give power back to the people who do the work, not the corporations that exploit it.
- More than a third of people in our district rely on Medicaid.
Mike Johnson voted to cut it by almost a trillion dollars.
That is going to close hospitals and leave thousands without care.
That’s not reform.
It’s cruelty.
Healthcare is a human right and a practical necessity.
No one should be punished for getting sick.
I support Medicare for All, because healthcare should depend on your needs, not your income.
- Across Louisiana, families are stuck shopping lots of Dollar Generals but few stores with fresh food.
People on SNAP, WIC, or EBT often pay high prices for unhealthy food.
Local farmers miss out on selling to their own communities.
Fresh food is essential.
If big companies won’t provide it, the USDA should step in.
I will open public grocery stores in rural and low-income areas, stocked with fresh, local food that SNAP, WIC, and EBT recipients can buy.
This will help families, support farmers, protect the taxpayer from price gouging, and grow our local economies.

To have a government that works for us, we must root out the rot first.
