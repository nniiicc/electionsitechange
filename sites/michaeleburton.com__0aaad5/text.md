Standing Up For Seniors One of the issues Michael discovered as a community advocate was that the Affton area has many senior residents.
Seniors whose property tax increases are chasing them from their own homes.
Michael knows we have to provide property tax relief for our senior neighbors on fixed incomes.
In Jefferson City, one of his highest priorities will be keeping our senior neighbors in their homes, and he has a plan to make it happen.
Helping Our Small Businesses We are all living in unprecedented times, the damage the pandemic response has created in our economy and especially for our small businesses, needs a drastic response.
Michael knows its small businesses that employ the majority of American workers, and they make up the backbone of our economy.
Michael will make it a top priority in Jefferson City to help provide relief to our struggling small businesses to help them weather this storm.
Improving Access to Healthcare Now more than ever, we need to make sure that we make sure that everyone has access to healthcare.
We need to make health insurance more affordable.
We need to make sure that those who are unemployed have more options, and we need to make accessing testing and treatment for the COVID-19 virsus available to everyone no matter what.
