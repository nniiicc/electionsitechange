"I'm running because I want my children—and all children in Lewiston—to grow up in a community that is safe, affordable, and full of opportunity." Meet Janet Beaudoin Janet Beaudoin isn't a career politician.
She's a mother, a wife, and a dedicated property management professional who knows firsthand the challenges facing Lewiston families today.
Since 2021, Janet has served our community on the Lewiston School Committee representing Ward 2.
As a vital member of the School Finance, Curriculum, and Negotiations Committees, she has fought for accountability, transparency, and excellence in our schools.
Now, she's ready to take that same fight to Augusta.
Janet believes in common-sense governance: backing law enforcement, cutting wasteful spending, and ensuring that our community remains a place where families can afford to live and thrive.
