About Yvette
Yvette Benarroch is an US Air Force veteran who served our nation during Operation Desert Shield/Desert Storm.
She is a mom, small business owner, and grassroots conservative leader.
Yvette leads the Collier County chapter of Moms for Liberty, fighting to keep Radical Left ideology out of our schools.
Yvette was elected to the Florida House of Representatives in 2024.
Yvette is the co-owner of Affordable Landscaping Service & Design, LLC, a full-service landscaping company serving the Naples and Marco Island Area.
Additionally, Yvette is a licensed realtor.
She understands the importance of having a strong economy, good jobs, low taxes, excellent schools, and a Florida that is affordable for our families.
Yvette’s conservative values and the love and gratitude she has for the United States encouraged her to become more involved in politics.
Yvette served as Neighborhood Team Leader in SW Florida for both Trump Victory and Latinos for Trump 2020 campaign.
In 2022 she served as Neighborhood team leader for Governor DeSantis’s 2022 re-election campaign.
She served as Vice Chairwoman for the Collier County GOP from 2020-2022, was Past State Vice Chairwoman for the Republican National Hispanic Assembly of Florida, and past 2nd Vice President for the Florida Federation of Republican Women.
She currently lives in Marco Island, Florida, with her husband, two teenage sons, and 4 dogs.
She has a bachelor’s degree in Marketing from Interamerican University of Puerto Rico, a master’s degree in Marketing from Southern New Hampshire University.
