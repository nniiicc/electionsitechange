Get in Touch Spartanburg is My Priority Common Sense Leadership I'm Clemson Turregano, and I'm asking for your vote to represent SC District 33 in the House.
District 33 includes the communities of: Glendale, Pacolet, Clifton, Cross Anchor, Enoree, Moore, Roebuck and Camp Croft .
My focus is on building Spartanburg better to help our schools, businesses and families thrive.
I'm a father, a grandfather, a veteran, a business owner, a leadership coach and hold a PhD.
I have the leadership experience and the heart to support Spartanburg.
No drama.
No politics.
Just common sense decision-making you can get behind.
