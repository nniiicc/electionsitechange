Videos Why I'm Running Contact Donate Permitless Carry Where I Stand I have carried weapons in service to my country, and I know firsthand that firearms training is essential.
I am a trained rifle coach, and I have trained youths ranging from Boy Scouts to High Schoolers participating in shooting teams in the use of firearms.
I have also seen what happens when 18-year-olds are unsupervised with weapons.
It all-too-often ends in tears, broken hearts, and broken homes.
Permitless carry makes it easy for young people to access firearms.
It discourages training and the serious handling of firearms.
It puts women at greater risk of domestic violence, and law enforcement officers at greater risk of being shot in the line of duty.
Permitless carry is one of the most dangerous laws passed by Travis Moore and rubber-stamped by Conservative Columbia.
