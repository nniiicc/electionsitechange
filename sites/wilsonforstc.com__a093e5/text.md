About Travis “As your state representative, my priorities will be supporting Missouri’s families, helping business owners thrive, and upholding the American way of life.” Travis is committed to promoting conservative values, encouraging economic growth, and supporting American Freedoms!
When elected, he will not only concentrate on growing our economy but also looking out for Missouri’s families by promoting conservative values which help families prosper.
This includes: ​ Equipping business owners for success Ensuring continued parental input in Missouri’s education system Supporting law enforcement Standing up for the unborn Defending our second amendment rights ​ Furthermore, Travis believes it’s important that the American Freedoms our forefathers envisioned should not be infringed upon so that every Missourian can choose the best path in their own pursuit of happiness. ​ He and his wife, Kris, have been a part of the local community for decades and chose to raise their children, Ellyana, Lincoln, and Quincey right here in the City of Saint Charles.
He graduated from Francis Howell North High School and attended Saint Charles Community College before transferring to University of Missouri Saint Louis to complete his degree in Business Administration.
He even had the privilege of working in economic development for the City of Saint Charles for a large portion of his career. ​ Travis has also attained his real estate sales license and is a graduate of VISION Saint Charles County, Class of 2017.
He has been a proud member of a variety of economic development organizations including the Saint Charles County Association of Realtors, Missouri Economic Development Council, Heartland Regional Investment Fund, and the Hawthorn Foundation.
Additionally, as a volunteer, he has served both the Francis Howell School District and the school district where his children attend, Orchard Farm.
