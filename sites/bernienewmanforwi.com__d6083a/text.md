Why I am running
• To bring common sense and honesty back to government
• To make sure local voices are heard
• To be accessible and accountable to the people I represent
Key Issues
I’m proud to announce I’m running for Wisconsin State Assembly in District 58.
Like a lot of you, I’ve been frustrated watching decisions get made without real input from the people who actually live here, especially when it comes to large developments like data centers, solar, and wind projects.
It shouldn’t feel like there’s a “good old boys club” calling the shots.
Our communities deserve transparency, accountability, and a real seat at the table.
I’ve lived and worked in Washington County my whole life.
This isn’t about politics for me; it’s about doing what’s right for our communities.
If that resonates with you, I’d appreciate your support and your voice in this campaign.
