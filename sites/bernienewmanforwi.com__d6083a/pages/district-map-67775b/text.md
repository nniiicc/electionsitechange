Wisconsin’s 58th Assembly District
The 58th Assembly District covers communities in and around West Bend and portions of Washington County in southeastern Wisconsin.
The district includes a mix of city, village, and town areas, representing the people who live, work, and raise families across this part of the state.
Communities Included
The 58th Assembly District includes portions of:
- City of West Bend
- Village of Jackson
- Town of West Bend
- Town of Jackson
- Town of Polk
- Town of Trenton
- Surrounding rural areas within Washington County
