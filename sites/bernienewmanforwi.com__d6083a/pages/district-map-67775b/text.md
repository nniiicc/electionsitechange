The district includes a mix of city, village, and town areas, representing the people who live, work, and raise families across this part of the state.
Communities Included The 58th Assembly District includes portions of: City of West Bend Village of Jackson Town of West Bend Town of Jackson Town of Polk Town of Trenton Surrounding rural areas within Washington County (Always confirm boundaries using the official state map.)
