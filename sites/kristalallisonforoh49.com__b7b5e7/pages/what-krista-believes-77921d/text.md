All In for All People; Fighting for Us
Krista L.
Allison is running because Ohio families deserve a fighter in Columbus, someone who puts people first and never backs down when our communities are being shortchanged.
- Fighting for you means making sure seniors can age with dignity in the communities they helped build.

- Ensuring Ohio seniors are not left behind as costs rise and services shrink
- Programs that help seniors remain safely in their homes
- Affordable utilities, healthcare, and reliable transportation
- Fighting for you means standing up for students, parents, and educators.
Krista will fight for:
- Full and fair funding for public schools
- Competitive pay and classroom resources for teachers
- Mental health and wraparound services for students
- Fighting for you means supporting those who put their lives on the line for our communities.
Krista will fight for:
- Fair wages and benefits for firefighters and police
- Mental health resources for first responders
- Modern equipment and training
- Fighting for you means making everyday life more affordable for working families.
Krista will fight for:
- Relief from rising utility and housing costs
- Workforce development and job training opportunities
- Policies that protect workers’ rights and economic security
- Fighting for you means making it possible to build a future right here in Ohio.
Krista will fight for:
- Affordable housing options so young people can live, work, and stay in their communities
- Workforce development and job training that lead to good paying careers, not just jobs
- Accessible education and skills training for young adults, including trade programs, community colleges, and continuing education
- Fighting for you means investing in the places we call home.
Krista will fight for:
- Strong funding for local libraries and community services
- Infrastructure and neighborhood investment
- Support for local governments so communities aren’t forced to do more with less
- Fighting for you means making sure Ohio’s resources are used wisely and fairly.
Krista will fight for:
- Investments that directly benefit Ohio families
- An end to unnecessary corporate giveaways and special-interest deals
- Transparency in how taxpayer dollars are spent
- Fighting for you means holding government accountable to the people it serves.
Krista will fight for:
- Strong ethics and transparency standards
- Public input in major funding decisions
- A government that listens and delivers results
- Fighting for you means making sure District 49 is heard, respected, and never overlooked.
Krista will:
- Listen first and often by hosting regular community listening sessions
- Advocate with transparency through open and clear communication with constituents
