NAVY CAPTAIN, Retired For 25 years, Armen Kurdian served our nation in the U.S.
Navy, dedicating his career to protecting American interests and defending our values abroad.
His military service instilled in him a deep commitment to duty, honor, and country as well as a firm belief that there is nothing that can't be fixed with a calm assessment followed by a plan of action.
Throughout his career, Armen has worked to strengthen our communities by partnering with nonprofits, supporting veterans, and improving local government.
He understands the challenges facing working families because he's lived them.
Armen believes every American deserves the chance to own a home, feel safe in their neighborhood, and build a better future for their family.
These aren't just talking points—they're the values he's fought for throughout his life.

Meet the Family Armen and his family call Southern California home.
As a husband and father, he understands the everyday challenges families in the 49th District face—from the cost of living to the quality of local schools.

That personal experience drives Armen's commitment to making this community the best place to raise a family.
