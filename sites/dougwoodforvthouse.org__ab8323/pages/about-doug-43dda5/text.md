Meet Woody
I am a sixth-generation Vermonter who grew up in Chittenden County and attended the University of Vermont.
I have lived in Vermont my entire life and have been a homeowner in Colchester since 2001.
For more than 40 years, I’ve hunted, fished, and camped throughout our state, developing a deep appreciation for Vermont’s land, traditions, and rural communities.
Professionally, I spent 15 years working in the hospitality industry before building a 30+ year career in aviation.
From the years 1998 to 2002, I served as General Manager of an airline operating at Burlington International Airport.
Since 2014, I worked as Operations Foreman at the Leahy Burlington International Airport, retiring from the City of Burlington in 2025.
My career has been built on responsibility, teamwork, and keeping people safe—values I will bring to public service.
