HB1616 appropriates funds for one advanced life support ambulance to be based in Puna.
Unlike other similar bills, my version specifies that the advanced life support ambulance will be based in Puna.
This bill would help improve Emergency Medical response times and would help to address the growing health and safety needs of our district.
