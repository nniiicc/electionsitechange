Dominique Azzollini is a lifelong Pike County resident, real estate professional, and community advocate running for State Representative to make Pike and Wayne Counties affordable, healthy, and full of opportunity again.
Raised here and working two jobs to remain here, Dominique has firsthand experience with the very problems that her neighbors are facing.
Rising housing costs, lack of local healthcare, and limited job opportunities are pushing residents out of the community they love.
Dominique is running to change that by fighting for affordable living, real healthcare access, sustainable local jobs, and a future where young people, working families and retirees can build a life right here at home.
It’s time we elect a State Representative who is committed to solving our problems.
