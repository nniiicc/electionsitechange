Who is Dominique Azzollini?
A New Path Forward for Pike & Wayne Counties
I’m Dominique Azzollini, and I’m running for State Representative because our community deserves a future where people can afford to live, work, and raise a family… not just dream about it.
I’m not a career politician.
I’m a lifelong Pike County resident, a working-class young professional, and someone who is living through the challenges our district is facing and I refuse to accept that “this is just the way things are.”
Rooted in Pike County
I grew up here in Pike County, Pennsylvania, and graduated at the top of my class from Delaware Valley High School in 2018.
That same year, I attended Wilkes University’s Honors College, double-majoring in Neuroscience and Psychology, and working toward minors in Biology and Anthropology.
Throughout high school and college, I tutored my peers in various subjects and maintained a job in my free time to pay for my education.
However, like many young people in our area, I couldn't to afford to continue my education so I chose to return home.
My family couldn’t take on more debt with multiple children pursuing higher education, so I made the choice to step aside and allow my siblings to follow their own academic paths instead.
It wasn’t an easy decision but it shaped my understanding of affordability, access, and sacrifice in a very real way.
Building a Career and Serving My Community
In 2021, I started working for a local real estate company as an administrative assistant.
Once immersed in the local housing market, I began contemplating the idea of homeownership.
I started studying real estate to understand what it took to become a homeowner and I earned my Pennsylvania Real Estate License in 2023.
Thanks to both of the positions I hold within my company, I've become a vital resource for friends and neighbors navigating the complicated and expensive local housing market.
But this industry has also been tough to be a part of: I’ve witnessed many families get priced out of the area they love.
I’ve watched young professionals give up on the dream of buying a home.
And I’ve watched long-time residents leave because they simply can’t afford to stay.
Those moments stay with you.
They’re why I’m running.
Deeply Involved.
Deeply Invested.
I believe in showing up.
That’s how trust is built and how change happens.
I’m proud to be involved with:
- Delaware Valley Action Group (DVA!) – especially helping grow youth engagement
- Pike County Democratic Committee (PCDC)
- Pike County Divas
- Milford’s St.
Patrick’s Day Parade and other community events
- Various volunteer and civic outreach opportunities
In 2025, I stepped forward and ran for Dingman Township Auditor, earning 46% of the vote, a powerful sign that our community is ready for new voices and new leadership.
I work two jobs, and still carve out time to support the community because this place means everything to me.
I’m running because I want Pike and Wayne Counties to remain a place where people of all ages can stay, not a place they’re forced to leave.
