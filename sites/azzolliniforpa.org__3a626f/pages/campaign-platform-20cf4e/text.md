This issue is especially important when paired with the unsustainable housing costs in our area.
Too many of our friends and neighbors here in Pike and Wayne Counties are struggling to live and we need to fix this.
Housingshould not be a luxury.
Dominique will fight to: Expand affordable housing & support first-time homebuyers Raise Pennsylvania’s $7.25 minimum wage Keep data centers from proliferating in our communities and raising utility costs Create economic policies that lower the price of groceries Lower the Pennsylvania State Sales Tax Promote responsible and sustainable real estate development Protect long-time residents from being priced out of their community Everyone who works hard should be able to afford to live here and Dominique is committed to making that a reality.
We need practical, targeted steps to make living here possible again, especially for young families, workers, and retirees.
Healthcare Pike County is one of only nine counties in the state without a hospital , which means the 139th district does not having a hospital within its borders.
This is a problem.
A majority of employees within the district receive PA state insurance, however the closest physicians are in NY and NJ where that insurance is not accepted.
Residents with state insurance could have to travel an hour for basic medical care and up to 3 hours for specialized medical care.
This is unacceptable .
This puts lives at risk.
Dominique will focus on: Bringing primary care facilities and a hospital to the district Expanding rural healthcare investments Supporting transportation access for patients and retirees who must travel Reforming liability insurance to incentivize physicians to practice in PA Creating more opportunities for doctors to learn in PA Increasing access to mental health services Quality care should never be an hour away.
Economic Opportunity The largest employers within the 139th district are the schools, the government and the tourism industry.
An economy that is largely reliant on the government to supply work, is not a stable economy.
Many of our residents are employed outside of our district.
Too many.
Our residents commute out of state because there aren’t enough jobs within our district, let alone jobs that pay well.
This means we are losing talent, income, and our economy is becoming instable.
We need to bring jobs back to PA.
Dominique will work to: Expand broadband and infrastructure to support remote work and new employers Pass a Severance Tax on oil companies to increase state revenue Attract new businesses and support local entrepreneurs Legalize Marijuana in PA to create taxable revenue Promote job growth within tourism, environmental stewardship, and other emerging industries Fight against monopolies and price gouging to protect consumers Our community deserves an economy that works for us, not one we have to leave home to find.
Sustainable tourism Tourism is one of our district’s largest industries.
To maintain this source of income within our economy, we must protect what it depends on, our environment.
Dominique will champion: Protection of waterways, forests, and public lands Smart environmental policies and programs that bring revenue to our economy Strict ordinances that regulate data centers Investments that make outdoor recreation safe and accessible Expansion of tourism efforts while protecting our environment Our environment is our greatest asset and safeguarding it builds long-term prosperity.
Government Reform Government is supposed to work for the people that are represented by it, however, it is clear that our government is not working for us, even down to the state level.
Now is the time to fix it.
Dominique will work to: Eliminate monied interests in our state politics by supporting the PA State Gift Ban Set caps on campaign contributions Create term limits for elected officials End gerrymandering in Pennsylvania by creating an independent redistricting commission We deserve a government that not only functions properly, but also represents its citizens.
Change can happen if you vote for it.
