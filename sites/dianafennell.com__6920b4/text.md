Assistant Speaker Pro Tem, Maryland House of Delegates Delegate Diana M.
Fennell (Democrat) is a resilient public servant, dedicated advocate, and breast cancer survivor.
She began her political career in 1995 as a Council Member for Colmar Manor, later serving as Mayor from 2000 to 2010.
Elected to represent District 47-A in the Maryland House of Delegates in 2014, she secured a landslide re-election in 2022.
Delegate Fennell has championed key legislation, including Bill 166 to establish a state minimum wage, and MD - HB815 to improve cancer screening and healthcare equity.
Her work on MD - HB577 addresses housing discrimination, reflecting her commitment to social justice and inclusivity.
Now in her third term, she continues to strive for a brighter future for Maryland’s families, supported by her husband of 38 years, Jeffrey Fennell Sr., and their two children.
