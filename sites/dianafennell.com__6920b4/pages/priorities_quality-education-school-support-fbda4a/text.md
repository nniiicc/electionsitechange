Delegate Diana Fennell is deeply committed to enhancing the quality of education across Prince George's County.
She believes that every child deserves a solid educational foundation, and she actively advocates for increased funding to ensure schools have the resources they need.
By pushing for better infrastructure, modern facilities, and updated learning materials, she aims to create a positive environment where students can thrive academically.
Beyond the classroom, Delegate Fennell recognizes the value of extracurricular activities and after-school programs.
These programs play a crucial role in developing well-rounded individuals by offering students opportunities to explore their interests, build skills, and engage with their peers in a constructive way.
She advocates for expanding these programs to reach more students and provide support for working families.
Teacher development is also a key focus for Delegate Fennell.
She supports initiatives that invest in professional development, training, and fair compensation for educators.
By empowering teachers with the tools and resources they need, she seeks to enhance the overall quality of education and ensure that students receive the guidance and support necessary for their success.
