Building safe and inclusive communities is at the heart of Delegate Fennell's work.
She actively supports initiatives that address housing issues, ensuring that all residents have access to safe, affordable, and quality living conditions.
By advocating for policies that tackle housing discrimination and promote fair housing practices, she strives to create a more equitable community.
Social justice is a significant part of her agenda.
Delegate Fennell works on initiatives that combat systemic discrimination, promote equal rights, and support reentry programs for individuals transitioning back into society.
By focusing on reentry efforts, she aims to reduce recidivism rates and help individuals rebuild their lives, contributing positively to their communities.
Inclusivity is a core value for Delegate Fennell.
She believes that diversity is a strength and works to foster a sense of belonging across neighborhoods.
Through community engagement, educational campaigns, and support for cultural initiatives, she promotes understanding and unity, making Prince George’s County a welcoming place for everyone.
