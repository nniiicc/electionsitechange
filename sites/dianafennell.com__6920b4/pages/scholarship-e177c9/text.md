Eligibility Requirements:
To qualify for the Delegate Diana Fennell Scholarship, applicants must:
- Reside in Legislative District 47A – Check your district at mdelect.net
- File the FAFSA (Free Application for Federal Student Aid) by March 1, 2025
- Be enrolled or planning to enroll at an eligible Maryland college, university, or private career school
- Have a minimum GPA of 2.0
- Submit a 500-word essay explaining why they are the best candidate for the scholarship
Students enrolling in an out-of-state school may qualify if their program is unavailable in Maryland.
