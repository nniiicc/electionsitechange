She advocates for comprehensive healthcare policies that prioritize preventive care, helping individuals maintain their health and avoid costly medical interventions.
By supporting initiatives that expand access to essential services, she seeks to reduce health disparities across the community.
Mental health services are a critical component of her healthcare agenda.
Delegate Fennell understands that mental well-being is as important as physical health, and she advocates for increased funding and support for mental health programs.
By ensuring that mental health services are accessible and affordable, she aims to break the stigma around mental health and promote a more inclusive approach to healthcare.
Reproductive freedom is a key issue for Delegate Fennell, and she firmly believes in the right of individuals to make informed decisions about their health.
She supports policies that guarantee access to comprehensive reproductive healthcare, including family planning, prenatal care, and essential screenings.
Her advocacy ensures that all residents have the resources they need to make the best choices for themselves and their families.
