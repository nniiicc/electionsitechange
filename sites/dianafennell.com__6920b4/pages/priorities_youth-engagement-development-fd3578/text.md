She supports mentorship programs that connect youth with leaders and professionals, helping them build skills, confidence, and a sense of direction.
Through these programs, young people can explore career paths, gain valuable insights, and establish connections that will benefit them long-term.
Extracurricular activities and after-school programs play a crucial role in youth development.
Delegate Fennell advocates for expanding these opportunities, particularly in underserved areas.
By providing safe and engaging environments where young people can explore their interests, develop talents, and build social skills, she helps set the stage for their future success.
Leadership development is a key focus for Delegate Fennell.
She supports initiatives that encourage young people to take on leadership roles, both in their schools and communities.
By fostering a spirit of leadership and civic engagement, she aims to empower the next generation to be active, responsible, and successful citizens.
