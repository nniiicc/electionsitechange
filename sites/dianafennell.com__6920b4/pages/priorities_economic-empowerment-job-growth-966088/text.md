Economic empowerment is essential for a thriving community, and Delegate Fennell is dedicated to fostering job growth and economic stability in Prince George's County.
She champions policies that support small businesses, helping them grow and succeed.
By advocating for incentives, grants, and reduced bureaucratic hurdles, she aims to create a business-friendly environment that encourages entrepreneurship and innovation.
Attracting new industries and investment is another critical aspect of her economic vision.
Delegate Fennell actively works to bring diverse businesses to the area, focusing on sectors that offer high-quality job opportunities.
By building partnerships with local enterprises and larger corporations, she aims to create a vibrant local economy that benefits all residents.
Workforce development is a priority for Delegate Fennell, as she understands the need for a skilled and adaptable workforce.
She supports programs that provide residents with training, education, and skill-building opportunities, especially in emerging industries.
These initiatives ensure that people can access better job prospects and that the community remains competitive in a rapidly evolving economy.
