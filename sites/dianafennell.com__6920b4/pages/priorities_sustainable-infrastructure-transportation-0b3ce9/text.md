Delegate Fennell is committed to investing in sustainable infrastructure projects that improve the quality of life for residents.
She advocates for the development and maintenance of roads, bridges, and public transportation systems that are efficient, reliable, and safe.
These improvements not only make daily commutes easier but also stimulate local economies by facilitating better connectivity.
Environmental sustainability is a significant concern for Delegate Fennell.
She supports initiatives that promote green spaces, parks, and environmentally friendly practices.
By encouraging the use of clean energy, reducing carbon emissions, and protecting natural resources, she aims to create a healthier, more sustainable environment for future generations.
Public transportation is a vital service for many residents, and Delegate Fennell is dedicated to enhancing transit options across the county.
She advocates for expanded bus routes, improved metro services, and the development of bike-friendly infrastructure.
By making public transportation more accessible and efficient, she aims to reduce traffic congestion and provide residents with viable alternatives to car travel.
