"Wyoming needs a Secretary of State who will stand tall for all Wyoming interests." —BILL NOVOTNY, JOHNSON COUNTY COMMISSIONER ​ Wyoming Voters Wyoming County Commissioners Wyoming Organizations Peter Victor, Cody This is a post about the Wyoming Secretary of State primary.
If you are on the fence on who to vote for, I encourage you to spend a few minutes and read it.
I am a Wyomingite who lives in Cody.
Since 2021, my representative in the Wyoming State House has been Rachel Rodriguez-Williams.
And over the years, I have tried to get involved in the political process more, because I care deeply about democracy and making sure the voice of the people is heard.
I have attended town halls, gone to public comment sessions, and attended political gatherings of varying flavors.
And not once during those interactions has my elected representative treated me as a person worth listening to.
She laughs in the face of constituents she disagrees with.
She refuses to acknowledge when she is wrong.
She accuses anyone who dissents of being radical leftist outsiders.
I am not a radical leftist outsider.
I live in Cody, I went to school in Cody, I fell in love and settled down in Cody.
I'm not going to leave town just because someone, no matter how influential, is rude to me.
This is my home.
But because it is my home, I know we deserve so much better.
Rachel is not running for re-election in House District 50 this year, so I know that portion will get better almost by default.
But she is running for Secretary of State.
Obviously, after being brushed off by her for years, I was not keen to vote her into higher office.
So I started researching alternative candidates for Secretary of State.
Enter Robert Short, a Republican candidate from Douglas.
I first met Robert Short May 16th, at the Cody Politics in the Park event.
He was energetic, open, and smart as a whip.
He has clear goals on helping develop Wyoming's business sector within the scope of the Secretary of State's duties.
My wife and I both left there with an overwhelmingly positive first impression of him, both as a candidate and a human being.
Since then, we have each interacted with Robert Short a few times, and each time we have been incredibly impressed by his professionalism and approachable demeanor.
I helped out his campaign in Park County by distributing some signs in Powell, and we made a small donation of $25 to his campaign fund, because we believed it was an important race.
And for our support, we received a handwritten thank you note.
Our current representative, who was now shooting for higher office, never even gave us the time of day.
But her opponent from the other side of the state sent us a handwritten and heartfelt thank you card.
I'm not telling you how to vote based on politics.
I'm not going to change anyone's mind on social media with a long winded post about the Freedom Caucus.
But if you want to ensure the person you are voting for is a genuine and good person?
Who wants Wyoming to be better?
Who actually values the input of the citizenry?
Who embodies the Cowboy Code, and treats those around him well?
Then you really gotta vote for Robert Short.
He is a class act without compromising in experience or integrity.
Please make sure you either vote early or in in person on Primary Election Day, August 18th.
