My life and career is built on these principles and I live true to my word.

MY PROMISE TO VOTERS
I am dedicated to upholding the full range of Constitutional and statutory responsibilities charged to the Secretary of State.
The Office of Secretary of State is charged with a tremendous amount of responsibilities that, done well, keep Wyoming healthy and strong.
My experience, technical know-how, and passion for guarding and promoting Wyoming's interests will enable me to lead positively and effectively so all challenges are addressed with transparency, allowing Wyoming to prosper.
As a member of the State Lands and Investments Board (SLIB), I will be committed to the careful consideration of proposed uses for our state lands lands and their crucial relationship to fulfilling Wyoming's statutory obligations.
Additionally, I believe that treating all of these lands with thoughtful and evenhanded consideration is critical to achieve the constitutionally prescribed usage.
Utilizing these lands with the maximum benefit for future generations is key to fulfilling these obligations.
I take this responsibility to the Constitution and Wyoming statutory law seriously, and I am eager to gain your trust.

ELECTION INTEGRITY
The security of Wyoming’s elections is critical, and you can count on my commitment to ensuring they are conducted in a fair, honest, and accurate manner.
Fair, honest, and accurate elections are essential to the health and future of Wyoming.
As a former election judge, I have witnessed the hard work and dedication from the county clerks, their staff, and volunteers who run our elections.
They stake their reputations on the integrity and fairness of the process and outcome, and in Wyoming that means everything.
The volunteers who work to ensure honest and accurate elections are our mothers, fathers, sisters, brothers, aunts, uncles, cousins, grandparents, friends, and neighbors.
The commitment to strengthen trust in Wyoming’s elections at every level is of utmost importance to me.
By striving for constant improvement in our election processes—and being prepared to take swift and decisive action if missteps occur—we can achieve exceptional levels of trust in the outcome of elections at every level.
I am resolute in my commitment to overseeing Wyoming’s elections with great integrity.
COMPLIANCE
As Secretary of State, you can rest assured that all individuals and firms working in our state comply with our rules and laws—without exception.
As Secretary of State, I’ll protect Wyoming citizens from fraudulent investment schemes like cryptocurrency scams, ensure the firms and individuals working in Wyoming uphold our rules and state statutes, and educate our people about financial security and safety.
When rules are broken or enforced unfairly, trust is lost, families are hurt, and we all suffer.
It is the responsibility of the Secretary of State to investigate violations and refer criminal matters to the appropriate authorities when necessary.
I will tirelessly commit to ensuring compliance, because in Wyoming, we believe in being tough but fair.
