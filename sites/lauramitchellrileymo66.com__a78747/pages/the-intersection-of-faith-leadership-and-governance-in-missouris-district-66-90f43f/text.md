The Intersection of Faith, Leadership, and Governance in Missouri’s District 66
“When the Lord asks you to do something, it’s a privilege and an honor to do it for Him. ” These words resonate deeply for Laura Mitchell Riley, a Christian mother and professional counselor whose faith has been both her compass and catalyst on a surprising journey from personal calling to political candidacy in Missouri’s House District 66.
Understanding her story offers insight into the values steering her campaign and the vision she has for her community.
Understanding Faith-Based Leadership Missouri: Foundations and Motivations
For Laura Mitchell Riley, faith is not merely a private conviction but the foundation of her public life and leadership.
“I am a Christian, pro-life, a believer, and a mother,” she affirms.
Her decision to run for the Missouri House of Representatives, she says, is rooted in a divine calling.
It wasn’t a calculated political move but rather a heartfelt response to what she sees as a higher purpose.
Her educational journey, including a degree in liberal studies from St.
Louis University and a master's in professional counseling, has equipped her with tools to understand and serve people on a deeper level.
Yet it’s this spiritual prompting, more than any academic credential, that she credits as the driving force behind her campaign, underscoring how her values and faith directly influence her pursuit of public office.
Laura Riley, of District 66, explains, "When the Lord asks you to do something, it's a privilege and an honor to do it for Him.
That calling is what motivated me to run for office."

The Role of the Bible and Ministry in Shaping Political Values
Laura Riley's political philosophy is steeped in her understanding of biblical teachings and ministry principles.
She places a strong emphasis on integrity, truthfulness, and transparency, seeing these as not just personal virtues but crucial leadership qualities.
She states emphatically, “I don’t compromise the word of God.
If a policy goes against it, I cannot support it.
Faith must guide our governance. ” This unwavering commitment shapes her stance on key issues and the way she approaches public service, believing that political decisions must be filtered through the lens of faith to foster a just and moral society.

Addressing Education and Parental Rights Through a Faith Lens
One of the core areas where Laura Riley’s faith profoundly influences her policy stance is education.
She voices concern about what she perceives as harmful educational policies, especially those related to transgender issues and Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) programs in schools.
Riley argues that schools often proceed without parental knowledge—particularly regarding sensitive topics such as gender transition.
She believes that parental transparency and involvement are paramount: “Parents should know everything about what their children are being taught.
Schools must be transparent and accountable to parents.” Advocating strongly for school choice and homeschooling, Riley suggests these options empower families to ensure children learn values consistent with their faith and worldview.

Community Impact in District 66
District 66 is facing several challenges that Riley sees as opportunities for focused leadership.
Among them is a lack of recreational facilities and support services for veterans and seniors.
She notes that North County residents often have to leave their community to shop or find places for leisure, pointing to the closure of shopping centers like Jamestown Mall as emblematic of wider neglect.
Riley also highlights political ignorance—particularly an unawareness among many voters about party platforms—as detrimental to informed voting decisions.
Public safety and community cohesion are top priorities for her, as she wants to strengthen resources that help residents, especially vulnerable populations like seniors, feel safe and supported.
Leadership Qualities Rooted in Faith and Integrity
Laura Riley’s leadership style is deeply personal and faith-driven.
She describes her approach as one of leading by example, emphasizing the importance of walking the talk.
“People follow what they see, not just what they hear,” she reflects.
This means embodying the values she holds dear in every decision and interaction.
Facing criticism or political opposition, Riley demonstrates an openness to learning while remaining firmly anchored in her principles: “I listen to criticism if it resonates with my spirit and comes from those I trust.
But I won’t compromise on the word of God. ”
Riley shares, "To lead by example is to produce fruit in your walk.
People follow what they see, not just what they hear."
