Common Questions About Party Platforms and Informed Voting What is a political party platform and why is it important?
A political party platform is an official written document developed by a party's leadership — often at its convention — that outlines core beliefs, priorities, and solutions for major national issues.
These platforms serve as roadmaps for lawmakers and candidates, reflecting a party's stance far beyond campaign slogans.
How can Missouri voters access the platforms to make an informed decision?
Missouri voters can access the platforms of the Democratic, Republican, Independent, Libertarian, and Green parties through each party's official website.
Local libraries and civic engagement organizations also maintain summaries and full-text versions.
Do party platforms really impact how a party governs?
Yes.
While candidates may adapt their messaging for a campaign, their actual policy positions and legislative priorities are usually drawn from the party platform.
Platforms influence the bills introduced and the priorities pushed once the election is over.
