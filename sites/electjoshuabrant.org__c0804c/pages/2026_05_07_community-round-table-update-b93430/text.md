Brant on May 7, 2026 I want to share an important update regarding the Community Round Table events that were scheduled for this week.
After a great deal of consideration, I made the difficult decision to cancel both sessions.
A few unexpected challenges came together all at once: Health issues, complications with our promotional efforts, and an unplanned return home after living in a hotel for the past several weeks.
Each of these on their own would have been manageable, but combined, they prevented me from being able to host the kind of meaningful, well‑organized conversation our community deserves.
Thank you for your patience, understanding, and continued support.
I’m committed to making sure these events remain valuable spaces for open dialogue, and I’m looking forward to getting us back on track.
Next Community Round Table 📅 May 13th ⏰ 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM 📍 Faith West Community Center I hope to see you there as we continue building a stronger, more connected community together.
