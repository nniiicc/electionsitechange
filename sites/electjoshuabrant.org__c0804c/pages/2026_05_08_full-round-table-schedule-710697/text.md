Our campaign is excited to announce the Community Round Table Schedule for the upcoming weeks.
These events are designed to bring neighbors together, encourage open, honest conversations centered around the needs of our communities, and ensure every voice is heard.
No party talking points.
No pre‑packaged speeches.
Just real people, real concerns, and practical ideas for how we can ‘do better’ for our communities going forward.
People are tired of the division and toxicity in politics today.
Whether you care about schools, roads, public safety, small business, agriculture, or simply want a state senator who listens before acting and without judging, this is your space to be heard.
This is your time to speak up.
Each Round Table will focus on the issues that matter most to the people of our district.
Your participation is what makes these conversations meaningful.
What to expect:
• A welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment focused on real solutions.
• A listening‑first conversation.
• A chance to share what matters most to you.
• Updates on how an independent voice can serve our district.
• Light snacks & refreshments as a small gesture of gratitude for spending a part of your evening with me.
• No pressure opportunities to get involved by offering ballot-access signatures, volunteering, and future events.
These roundtables are part of my commitment to transparency, accessibility, and community‑driven problem‑solving.
Your voice shapes this campaign, and your experiences shape my priorities.
Bring your perspective.
Bring a neighbor.
Let’s ‘do better’ together.
