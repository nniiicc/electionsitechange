For example, medical or otherwise personal information of minors or other private citizens, security or safety reasons, protection of trade secrets, etc.
That being said, I’ll fight for greater transparency around WHY those agreements are necessary, and independent oversight as to HOW those agreements are conducted.
Agreements should only ever be entered into as necessary , should be minimal in scope, and meticulously justified to the public in every instance.

(More Information Coming Soon) Join the Discussion on Reddit Other Important Issues The First Amendment Freedom of expression is paramount, but with that comes certain responsibilities.
We don’t allow people to incite violence, threaten, or defame.
We also have laws to regulate commercial speech to protect people from fraud and misrepresentation.
Why then, does it seem to be perfectly legal for government officials to misrepresent and mislead?

Join the Discussion on Reddit LGBTQ Issues I believe in freedom, respect, and a live & let live philosophy.
It’s not the government’s business what consenting adults do in the privacy of their own home, and I strongly support equal rights.
Additionally, I view gender expression as being a form of free speech; covered by the First Amendment.
That being said, I don’t believe it’s reasonable to expect all of society to understand & conform to new ideas right away.
It’s just not human nature.
Instead, we should be patient with each other, make reasonable adjustments to make way for new concepts (such as organizing sports by weight class or other metrics instead of gender), and not attacking people for being slow to adopt.
