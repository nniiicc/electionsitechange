Joshua Brant, independent candidate for Indiana State Senate District 23, discusses data centers, why they’re being pushed at agricultural summits, how they affect communities, and the steps he’s taking to protect Hoosier communities.
Senate District 23 consists of the western half of Tippecanoe County, parts of Montgomery County, and all of Warren, Fountain, Vermillion, and Parke Counties.
