Independent Joshua Brant Secures Ballot Access in Senate District 23 Race
With District 23 already drawing national attention, Brant’s official ballot access adds a new dynamic to the November race.
Indiana – July 22, 2026 – Joshua Brant, candidate for Indiana State Senate District 23, announced today that the campaign has officially secured ballot access for the November 3rd election, confirming that voters will see Joshua Brant’s name on the ballot.
The milestone marks a major step forward for the campaign and reflects the work of supporters, volunteers, and community members who helped complete the ballot access process.
With ballot access now official, the campaign will continue building momentum around its message of government accountability and transparency, stronger education, and improved economic prosperity for all.
“This is an important moment for our campaign and for every voter who deserves a real choice on Election Day,” said Joshua Brant.
“I’m grateful to everyone who helped us reach this point, and I’m ready to take our message directly to voters across District 23.
This campaign is about putting people over partisan politics and today brings us one step closer to giving Hoosiers the genuine representation they deserve.”
In the weeks ahead, Joshua Brant’s campaign will focus on meeting voters, listening to community concerns, recruiting volunteers, and sharing a positive vision for District 23.
The campaign’s priorities include:
- Challenging insider politics and holding government accountable.
- Rebuilding Indiana’s struggling education system.
- Restructuring the tax code to lower the cost of living in Indiana.
“Ballot access is more than a paperwork milestone,” Brant added.
“It means voters will have the opportunity to weigh our ideas, compare our vision, and decide the future of District 23 for themselves.”
Voters who want to learn more, volunteer, request a yard sign, or support the campaign can visit https://electjoshuabrant.org/ or follow the campaign on social media.
About Joshua Brant
Joshua Brant is a Marine Corps Veteran, husband, father, writer, and business consultant running for Indiana State Senate District 23.
The campaign is focused on restoring genuine representation to government, strengthening education in Indiana, and making daily life more affordable for Hoosiers across the board.
