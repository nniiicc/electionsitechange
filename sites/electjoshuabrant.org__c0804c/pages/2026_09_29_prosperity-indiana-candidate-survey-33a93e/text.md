Brant on September 29, 2026 I’m not sure why it hasn’t occurred to me until now to post my answers to these candidate surveys, but I was really quite impressed with this one from Prosperity Indiana.
The questions really made me think, and a lot of the things they asked about were relevant to a broad spectrum of my proposals.
I think my vision for Indiana shines through the complexity of the policy language in this survey; hopefully giving readers a better idea of what my frameworks are meant to achieve.
Note: I’m aware that the survey starts with question seven.
The first six questions were just bio details like my name, email address, the district I’m running for, etc.
