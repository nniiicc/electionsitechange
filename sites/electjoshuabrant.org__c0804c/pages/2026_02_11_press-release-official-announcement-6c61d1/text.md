Joshua Brant Announces Independent Candidacy for Indiana State Senate District 23
Independent Leadership for a Brighter Tomorrow
About Joshua Brant
Joshua Brant, Independent candidate for Indiana State Senate District 23, is launching his campaign to bring much needed transparency and accountability to the state’s government.
With a background as a Marine Corps veteran and 20 years of combined leadership experience and education, Joshua is dedicated to promoting the values of Honor, Courage, & Commitment in public service.
As a lifelong Hoosier, Joshua has a deep understanding of the challenges faced by Indiana families.
His personal experiences have shaped his commitment to improving healthcare accessibility and affordability, as well as addressing the rising cost of living.
A Leader Forged by Experience
Joshua’s unique blend of military service, business acumen, and personal experience has equipped him to tackle the complex issues facing Indiana.
As he notes, “My childhood was chaotic, but it taught me to analyze problems and find solutions.
My time in the Marine Corps instilled in me the core values of Honor, Courage, & Commitment, which I believe are essential for effective leadership.”
Joshua is committed to fighting corruption and promoting transparency in government.
He believes that “We need comprehensive anti-corruption legislation at both the state and federal level to restore trust in our institutions.
It’s time for a new kind of leader who will put the people first.”
A Choice for Voters
In a crowded field that includes incumbent Republican Spencer Deery, Republican challenger Paula Copenhaver, and Democrat David Sanders, Joshua Brant offers a fresh alternative.
Unlike his opponents, Joshua is not beholden to party interests or special groups.
He is committed to representing the people of District 23, not just the powerful or well-connected.
The Stakes are High
This election is about more than just choosing a new representative – it’s about the future of Indiana.
With the cost-of-living skyrocketing and healthcare becoming increasingly unaffordable, the people of District 23 deserve a leader who will fight for their interests.
As Joshua says, “The status quo is not working.
It’s time for a new kind of leadership that will put the people first and work towards a functioning government so that future generations aren’t stuck cleaning up this mess.”
