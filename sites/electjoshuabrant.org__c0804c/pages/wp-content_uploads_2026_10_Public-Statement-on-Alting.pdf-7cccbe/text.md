FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Public Statement on Senator Ron Alting From the Campaign to Elect Joshua Brant Yesterday, Senator Ron Alting of District 22 acknowledged at least two incidents of shoplifting and attributed these actions to “concerning changes in [his] judgment and cognitive functioning.” He has announced that he will step back from active campaigning to seek medical care.
I want to begin by expressing my sincere hope that he receives the support and treatment he needs.
Public service is demanding, and no one is immune to health challenges.
I am offering this perspective not as a party advocate, but as an independent who has worked hard to remain neutral in a world of partisan politics, and as someone with deep personal ties to Senator Alting’s district.
It is the community where I grew up, where my family still lives, and where I first learned what responsible leadership looks like.
I care about the wellbeing of that district in a very real and personal way.
Accountability in government has been a central principle of my campaign.
Senator Alting’s acknowledgement that he is experiencing adverse changes in his judgement and cognitive functioning raises serious questions about his ability to carry out the responsibilities entrusted to him.
The people of District 22 deserve clarity, stability, and full confidence in their representation.
These are not abstract political ideals; they are practical necessities for effective governance.
For these reasons, I am calling on Senator Alting to make a full withdraw from the race.
Doing so would allow him to focus fully on his health and would give the district the certainty it needs moving forward.
This is not a partisan statement, nor is it made with any ill will.
It is simply an acknowledgment that public trust matters, and that stepping aside can sometimes be the most honorable and responsible choice.
I wish Senator Alting strength and healing as he addresses the challenges before him, and I hope the people of District 22 are able to move forward with confidence in the leadership that represents them.
Genuinely, Joshua Brant Candidate for Indiana State Senate District 23 Committee to Elect Joshua Brant joshuabrant@electjoshuabrant.org ###
