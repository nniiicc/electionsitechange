In 2014, Matt moved to New Hampshire for a fresh start, and it immediately became home.
After 30 years building a career in IT, he recently retired and now devotes his energy to the things he loves most: competitive shooting, gardening, and hiking the beautiful mountains of New Hampshire.
Matt brings hard-earned life experience, discipline, and common sense to the State House.
Now he's running for his second term in the New Hampshire House, ready to keep fighting for the people of Manchester.
