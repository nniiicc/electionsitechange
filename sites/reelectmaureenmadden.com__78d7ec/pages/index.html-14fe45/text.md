What Maureen Fights For
Your Priorities Are Her Priorities.
From groceries, gasoline, prescription drugs and healthcare, Maureen is working every day to assure affordability for Monroe County families.
Having worked to secure nearly $175 billion in K-12 education funding across Pennsylvania, she knows every child in Monroe County deserves a fully funded school.
Rated "A" by the Conservation Voters of PA and a "Friend of the Watershed", protecting our clean air, water, and the natural beauty of the Pocono Mountains is critical to Maureen.
As Chairwoman of the House Aging & Older Adult Services Committee, Maureen leads the fight to increase elder care quality, protect Medicaid, support caregivers.
Standing strong with unions and working people, Maureen believes a strong middle class is built by respecting the dignity of labor and protecting its right to organize.
A steadfast defender of women's reproductive freedom, Maureen will protect abortion access and IVF, and will keep fighting to get the government out of those decisions.
