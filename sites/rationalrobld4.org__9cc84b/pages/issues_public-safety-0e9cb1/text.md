Contribute Meet Rob Issues Endorsements News Public Safety COMING SPRING 2027 - PATH Crisis Relief & Sobering Center The PATH facility targets the root causes rather than just the criminal symptoms - giving officers, families and individuals a better option.
What it will offer: People will receive crisis stabilization, sobering services, mental health support, minor medical care, and connections to ongoing treatment.
Diversion Away from Jail & ERs: The facility will provide a meaningful alternative to emergency rooms and incarceration, while improving outcomes for individuals and families.
This means officers will have somewhere to take people in crisis other than jail or the hospital.
No-Refusal Policy The center will run under a 23-hour, no-refusal policy and feature a secure drop-off point for walk-ins.
This is huge for law enforcement — officers won't be turned away when they bring someone in, which has historically been a frustration with other facilities.
A community is only as strong as its most vulnerable members.
