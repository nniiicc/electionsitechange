Rooted Here
My wife Deb and I met in college in California, fell in love with the frontier spirit in Alaska, and built our life from the ground up — literally.
We hauled our own water, cooked on a wood stove, and raised four kids on the Kenai Peninsula before planting roots in the Spokane Valley in 1996.
Those years forged something in us: grit, flexibility, and the ability to find common ground with people from every background.
This February we'll celebrate 44 years of marriage.
Our kids — all University High School graduates — are raising their own families across Washington and Alaska.
