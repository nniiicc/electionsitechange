Endorsements
The Environment and Climate Council of of the Washington State Democratic Party is proud to endorse Rob Tupper for the 2026 general election.
To the Voters of Washington State Legislative District 4:
I have had the opportunity to see firsthand the kind of person Rob is, and what stands out most to me is his genuine commitment to helping people.
Rob is a problem solver and a hard worker who approaches challenges with thoughtfulness, determination, and a willingness to find solutions rather than simply focus on disagreements.
He understands that effective leadership isn't about whether someone has a particular political label.
It is about listening to people, understanding their concerns, and working together to make the community better.
I have personally seen how much Rob cares about people and their well-being, regardless of their political affiliation.
He treats people with compassion and respect, and his concern for others is something I have witnessed firsthand.
At a time when politics can seem increasingly divided, we need representatives who are willing to put people first.
Rob has the compassion, work ethic, and practical problem-solving skills to be that kind of representative.
He will listen, he will work hard, and he will strive to represent the entire community.
I truly believe anyone would be proud to say that Rob Tupper represents them in Olympia.
I encourage the voters of the 4th Legislative District to support Rob and give him the opportunity to serve our community.
Sincerely,
Jason B Jason Beatty
