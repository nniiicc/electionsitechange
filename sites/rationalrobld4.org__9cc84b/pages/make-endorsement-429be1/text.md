Thank you for endorsing me!
To help you write your endorsement, consider answering one or two of these:
- How do you know me, and for how long?
- Have you seen me handle a challenge or solve a problem?
What happened?
- What's one word or phrase you'd use to describe me, and why?
- Why do you think I'd be a good State Representative?
- What do you think our community needs most right now?
- Is there a moment that made you want to support my campaign?
There are no wrong answers, and shorter is totally fine!
