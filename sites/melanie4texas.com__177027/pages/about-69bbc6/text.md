Melanie Medley-Thomas for Texas House 103 Home About Issues Donate Contact About Melanie A life dedicated to service, faith, and community Colorado Roots Melanie grew up in a rural farming and ranching community in Colorado, where she learned the values of hard work, perseverance, and community.
Her upbringing instilled in her a deep appreciation for the land, family, and the importance of supporting one another.
Gold Star Sister As a Gold Star sister, Melanie understands the profound sacrifice made by military families.
This personal experience has shaped her commitment to honoring veterans and supporting those who serve our nation.
Faith-Centered Values Faith is the cornerstone of Melanie's life and leadership.
Her unwavering belief in doing what is right guides every decision she makes and fuels her dedication to serving others with integrity and compassion.
Education Melanie earned her degree in Psychology with a minor in Sociology from Oral Roberts University.
Her education provided her with a deep understanding of human behavior, community dynamics, and the importance of empathy in leadership.
Professional Career With a successful career in the health insurance industry in Dallas, Melanie has gained valuable experience in navigating complex systems and advocating for individuals and families.
Her professional background equips her with the skills to tackle challenging policy issues.
Athletic Background Melanie's athletic background taught her the importance of discipline, teamwork, and perseverance.
These qualities continue to drive her approach to leadership and her commitment to achieving results for Texas families.
Community Service Throughout her life, Melanie has been deeply committed to community service.
Whether volunteering, mentoring, or advocating for those in need, she believes in the power of giving back and making a positive impact in the lives of others.
A Leader for Texas Melanie Medley-Thomas brings a unique combination of personal experience, professional expertise, and unwavering values to her campaign.
She is committed to fighting for Texas families, protecting our freedoms, and building a brighter future for all Texans.
