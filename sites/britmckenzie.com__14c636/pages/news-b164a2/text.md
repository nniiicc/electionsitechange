Arkansas Education Legislation Summary - 94th General Assembly
ARKANSAS EDUCATION LEGISLATION SUMMARY 2023 HB1688-This bill amends public school funding.
It increases per-pupil funding from the current amount of $7,413 to $7,618 for the 2023-2024 school year.
The bill would increase the amount to $7,771 for the 2024-2025 school year.
