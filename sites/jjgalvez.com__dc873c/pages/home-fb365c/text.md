Get Involved The Plan I’M RUNNING for THE CALIFORNIA DREAM Meet JJ & Get Involved I came Here with big dreams and little certainty.
With hard work and determination, I built a life I’m proud of in California—arriving as an immigrant to attend college, earning my degree, building a career in technology consulting, getting married, and buying a home by age 30.
I know California is full of opportunity , but I see the challenges so many face—everything costs so much, especially housing, and we need to prepare our economy for a future shaped by innovation and new technologies.
I’m running for State Assembly because I believe California should be a place where people from every background can thrive .
As someone who has experienced both the promise and challenge of building a life here, I want to create a California for everyone—forward-looking and full of possibility .
More About JJ Donate to Make an Impact on our future Donate to Make an Impact on our future Donate to Make an Impact on our future Donate We need more unity and hope for tomorrow I live in Silverado Canyon, a small, beautiful community in the Santa Ana Mountains, where wildfires and floods are part of life.
What keeps me here is the people —neighbors who help each other, unify in the face of risk, and prove community is stronger than what divides us .
We need more of that in our state.
Even if we don’t always agree, we care about the same things— healthy communities , the value of hard work, and the opportunity to make a living, get what we need, and pursue our goals.
I’m running for State Assembly because I believe California has a golden opportunity to lead the way toward a future of hope and abundance .
It starts with recognizing we have challenges —including how expensive it is to live here and the risk of natural disasters like wildfires—and then getting to work to solve them .
