Roger in the News
Jefferson City News Tribune
The first of a two-part series profiling Roger Korenberg's Air Force career.
This article covers his journey from the Air Force Academy through transport aviation and into helicopter combat missions during the Vietnam War, including his classified special operations work inserting and recovering reconnaissance teams behind enemy lines.
The second part of the series covers Roger's post-Vietnam career, including his hand-selected classified assignment recovering de-orbited reconnaissance space capsules over the Pacific Ocean — what he calls “the best assignment of my career.”
More coverage will be added as the campaign progresses.
