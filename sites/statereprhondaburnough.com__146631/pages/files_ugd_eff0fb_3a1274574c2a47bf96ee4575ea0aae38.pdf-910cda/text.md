July 4, 2026 WHY CELEBRATE THE WOMEN WHO LEAD CLAYTON COUNTY?
For far too long, the contributions and achievements of women have been overlooked, minimized, or omitted from the stories that shape our nation’s history and popular culture.
Yet women have played an essential role in building and strengthening the United States.
We have nurtured families, supported our neighbors, led our communities, and helped create a better future for generations to come.
As our nation approaches its 250th anniversary, America250 provides a meaningful opportunity to celebrate the remarkable women who have shaped our communities and our country.
It is a time to honor our stories, recognize our accomplishments, and inspire future generations to take pride in their heritage and their potential.
Through this celebration, we recognize the women of Clayton County, our seven cities, and Clayton County Public Schools whose leadership, service, resilience, and dedication have made a lasting impact.
Their contributions have strengthened our communities and enriched the lives of countless individuals.
Let us proudly celebrate and honor the extraordinary women of Clayton County—who have broken barriers, opened doors, inspired change, and left an enduring legacy.
Their stories deserve to be told, their achievements deserve to be recognized, and their impact deserves to be celebrated.
Sincerely, Rhonda M.
