In her first legislative update, Representative Joanne Shofner shares highlights from her work at the Texas Capitol, including a visit to Fort Cavazos, filing HB 2532 to expand rural healthcare access, and participating in Chamber Days with local leaders.
She also recognized the Alabama-Coushatta Tribe and met with the Texas Farm Bureau to discuss agricultural priorities.
Joanne remains committed to representing the values of House District 11 through her committee work and continued advocacy for East Texans.
