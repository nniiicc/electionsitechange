Protect East Texas Water & Land
- Keep East Texas water in East Texas
- Protect rural land from out-of-control development
- Preserve our natural resources for future generations
East Texas water and land belong at the forefront of decisions that impact our region.
Joanne will fight to protect our natural resources, rural communities, and the property East Texas families have cared for for generations.
Property Tax Elimination
- Fight to eliminate property taxes
- Protect Texans from being taxed out of their homes and land
- Demand responsible government spending
Texans should not have to continually pay the government for the privilege of keeping property they already own.
Joanne will fight for a responsible path toward eliminating property taxes and keeping more money in the hands of Texas families.
Protect East Texas Affordability
- Fight rising costs for working families
- Keep East Texas affordable for future generations
- Oppose policies that unnecessarily increase the cost of living
East Texas should remain a place where families can afford to live, work, raise children, and retire.
Joanne will fight policies that drive up everyday costs and threaten the affordability of our rural communities.
Protect Property Rights
- Defend private property ownership
- Protect rural landowners from government overreach
- Ensure East Texans have a voice when development impacts their property
Joanne will stand with Texas property owners and fight government overreach that threatens the land, homes, farms, and livelihoods East Texans have worked hard to build.

- Prepare young Texans to build successful lives here at home
