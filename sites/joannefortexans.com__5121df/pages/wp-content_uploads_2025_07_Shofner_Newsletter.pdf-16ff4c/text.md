Texas State Representative, HD 11 UPDATES FROM JOANNE SHOFNER!
Greetings to the family of House District 11 from the Texas State Capitol.
The Texas Legislature has been hard at work in Austin on legislation to improve the lives of the people of the State of Texas and East Texas.
I am pleased to share with you this update on my time here as your State Representative in the Texas House of Representatives.
HOUSE BILL 3219 I was thrilled to introduce House Bill 3219, which would allow Texas pharmacists to dispense ivermectin without a prescription.
This proposal was heard by the House Public Health Committee on Monday, April 21st.
I am proud to support medical freedom and expand access for Texans.
READ MORE READ MORE HOUSE BILL 4429 This week, I had the honor of attending the layout of House Bill 4429, which I am also proud to joint-author.
This bill designates a portion of U.S.
Highway 281 in Blanco and Burnet Counties as the Muckleroy Memorial Highway—an enduring tribute to a legacy of service, commitment, and community impact.
READ MORE HOUSE BILL 6 On April 16th, the Texas House passed HB 6 – the Teacher’s Bill of Rights!
This legislation restores classroom authority to our educators, ensuring they have the tools and support needed to maintain order, safeguard student safety, and uphold academic integrity.
Proud to stand with our Texas teachers!
HOUSE BILL 2 PASSES!
The Texas House has officially passed HB 2 — a major step forward for public education in our state! ✅ Largest school funding and teacher pay raise bill in Texas history ✅ Strengthens public schools ✅ Supports our teachers ✅ Gives every student a chance to succeed After more than 12 hours of debate, this historic legislation passed — and I was proud to support it.
This is a victory for students, teachers, and communities across Texas.
Let’s keep putting Texas students and educators first!
