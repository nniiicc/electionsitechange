In this edition, Rep.
Joanne Shofner shares exciting legislative milestones, including laying out her first bill to improve university accreditation options and introducing a bill to expand pediatric subspecialty training in Texas.
She also celebrates the SFA Sylvans’ historic win at the Southern Forestry Conclave, recaps Governor Abbott’s signing of SB 2, and reflects on the fast-paced work of presiding over bill readings in the House.
