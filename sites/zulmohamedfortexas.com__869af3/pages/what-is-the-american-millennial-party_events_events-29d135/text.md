Bring your signs, Palestinian flags, keffiyehs, and your voice.
Whether you have been advocating for Palestinian rights for years or are joining a demonstration for the first time, everyone who supports peace, equality, and human rights is encouraged to participate.
Join Zul Mohamed for Senate and the American Millennial Party as we peacefully demonstrate, march, and call attention to the humanitarian crisis facing Palestinians.
Together, we can make our voices heard.
