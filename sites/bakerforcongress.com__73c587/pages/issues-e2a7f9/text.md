What You Told Me Matters Most To You
- A strong, stable, and affordable economy with good-paying jobs, better wages, and inflation kept in check, so you can afford housing, healthcare, groceries, and your everyday needs.
- More manufacturing and skilled-trade jobs here in Michigan, so you can build a good life close to home.
- Better access to mental healthcare and meaningful reform in physical healthcare, so you and your family can get the care you need.
- Education and improved literacy for our children, with education decisions made at the local level, strong parental involvement, and a meaningful federal role in supporting local school districts and our children.
- Safety at home and abroad, so you and your family can feel secure.
- A voice in responsible growth, including thoughtful decisions about energy, water, infrastructure, and a moratorium on data centers.
- Protection of our Great Lakes, environment, fisheries, and the communities that depend on them.
As Chair of the GLFC, I will continue to do this work, building partnerships and finding solutions to protect our Great Lakes for generations to come.
