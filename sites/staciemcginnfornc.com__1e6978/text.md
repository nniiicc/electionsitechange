MEET STACIE
The daughter of a West Point graduate, air force pilot and decorated veteran of three wars; and a mother who was one of the first women architecture graduates from Rice University, Stacie and her four siblings were raised to value education, hard work, family, honesty, love of God and country.
Part of a large, close family, Stacie is the favorite aunt to nine nieces and nephews and countless cousins.
As your next NC Senator, Stacie will:
- Continue to support parental rights in their children’s education, while improving overall student proficiency in reading and math, with an emphasis on early education.
- Support law enforcement.
Keep violent criminals, drug addicts and the mentally ill, who are a danger to themselves or others, off the streets.
- Support policies that have made North Carolina the best state for business, including low personal income and property taxes and reasonable regulations that attract well-paying jobs and make it easier to start new businesses.
- Balance the benefits of economic growth with the quality of life North Carolinians treasure: our small towns and leafy neighborhoods, Southern hospitality, and the natural beauty that surrounds us.
