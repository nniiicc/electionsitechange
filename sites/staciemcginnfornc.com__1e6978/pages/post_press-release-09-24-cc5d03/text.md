NEW AD: Stacie McGinn Calls Out Woodson Bradley's Rewriting of History Around Her Involvement in an Illegal Pyramid Scheme
CHARLOTTE, NC – Stacie McGinn, Republican candidate for Senate District 42, releases a new ad calling out her opponent Woodson Bradley’s rewriting of history around her involvement in Fortune Hi-Tech Marketing's pyramid scheme that defrauded over 350,000 consumers - including nearly 20,000 North Carolinians - of their life savings.
Woodson Bradley cannot be trusted.
Fortune Hi-Tech Marketing (FHTM) ran “one of the most prolific pyramid schemes” according to former Attorney General Jack Conway.
The firm was shut down by the Federal Trade Commission in 2013 with the help of former Attorney General Roy Cooper.
Woodson played a prominent role in the scheme as a Platinum Sales Manager, spokesperson, national trainer, and an elevated member in their ‘Ring of Honor.’
Stacie McGinn’s full statement:
“Woodson Bradley continues to weave a spider web of lies.
At FHTM, Woodson sold vulnerable people a bill of goods and lined her pockets.
Now, instead of taking responsibility, she absurdly claims she was ‘targeted’ by FHTM because she was a survivor of domestic abuse.
Every voter needs to know the truth about Woodson Bradley: She lacks the character and experience to lead as a state senator in Raleigh.”
