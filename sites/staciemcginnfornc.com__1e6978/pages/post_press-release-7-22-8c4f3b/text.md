A deeper dive into the numbers tells a disturbing story.
Roughly 80% of McGinn’s contributions received during the quarter were received from contributors within Mecklenburg County.
In contrast, only 19% can be said for Woodson Bradley.
In fact, about 41% of contributors to the Bradley campaign during the quarter were out-of-state, mostly from California.
Her campaign expenses show trips made to California during the quarter, presumably for fundraising purposes.
Read Stacie McGinn’s full statement below: “I am truly grateful for the tremendous support my campaign received from local contributors this quarter, to help fund our mission to bring common sense leadership to State Senate District 42.
Since my launch, I have remained focused on real solutions to the issues that North Carolinians care most about: lowering costs, increasing public safety, standing up for parental rights in education, while improving proficiency in math and reading in public schools, as well as policies that allow North Carolina’s economy to continue to thrive." Despite her claims of being ‘Team Normal,’ Woodson Bradley consistently sides with the most far-left voices in her party.
In 2024, California Governor Gavin Newsom canvassed for Nicole Sidman and Bradley here in the Charlotte area.
"This campaign report is just another example of how she’s out-of-step with the values and priorities that are most important to the voters of North Carolina and District 42.
We don’t need California progressives advising us how to run our state.” North Carolina’s State Senate District 42 – encompassing Charlotte and Mecklenburg County – was decided by a margin of 209 votes in 2024 and is expected to once again be one of the closest state legislative races in the state this fall.
