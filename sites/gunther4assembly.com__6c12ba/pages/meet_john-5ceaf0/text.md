Meet John
I’m John Gunther from the Hamlet of Shushan NY, Washington County; running for New York State Assembly District 114 against one of the state’s most entrenched Republicans who has done very little in the last six years to improve the lives of everyday people here in the North Country.
I was the founder and President of The Works, Inc. in Greenwich, NY.
I had manufacturing plants in both Washington and Warren Counties.
We employed hundreds of people over a span of 48 years.
I grew this start up business with loans from local banks, was never late on a payment, I never went bankrupt and never once stiffed a supplier or contractor.
I was one of the first borrowers from the Washington County Regional Local Development Corp. and the Lake George Lake Champlain Regional Planning board.
The Toy Works was the poster child for the board, meaning I repaid my loans and created all the jobs I promised.
My duties included all aspects of business: Product design, manufacturing, finance, legal, insurance, marketing, HR and negotiating licensing contracts with many companies including Marvel Comics, The Elvis Presley Estate, Random House Book Publisher and The Metropolitan Museum of Art to name a few.
I am your small business advocate.
With my real-world experience, I’m uniquely qualified to represent the hundreds of small businesses which make up the backbone of the economy here in the North Country.
There are many issues not being addressed by these incumbent Republicans like providing affordable health insurance to our employees, their failure to speak out against the tariffs on Canadian goods which has hurt tourism here and has increased the cost of raw materials and finished goods from our northern partners.
Conversely, Canada is importing far fewer New York State products.
They are looking at China as a more reliable trading partner than the United States.
Talk to your local dairy farmer who’s lost market share and getting paid $17.21/ hundred weight for their milk when their cost of manufacturing is $22/hundred weight.
We know we can’t depend on the Federal Government to come to our rescue and get things back to normal.
On a county and state level, I’ll work to devise a plan to attract back Canadian tourists.
I will work with our local businesses and chamber of commerce to incentivize our northern neighbors to cross that border and come south.
Growing small business means more jobs, more tax revenue for our towns and counties.
You must have income to pay your bills and we can’t depend on the federal government anymore; we need a strong small business culture to be successful.
I am running a grass roots campaign funded by small contributions from individual donors.
My campaign committee, gunther4assembly, has been approved by the New York State Board of Elections to participate in their matching funds program which was established to help independent candidates get elected who are not beholding to large corporations, their lobbyists and super PACS.
This Match Program levels the playing field.
