About Karen Varley
RURAL IOWA VALUES
Karen Varley grew up on her family’s Iowa dairy farm and built a career spanning agriculture, agronomy, law, and public service.
She and her husband, Warren, own a law practice in Stuart and have four grown children.
A dedicated community leader, Karen serves on several local boards and champions strong public schools, clean water, affordable health care and childcare, sustainable agriculture, housing, and economic growth for rural Iowa.
