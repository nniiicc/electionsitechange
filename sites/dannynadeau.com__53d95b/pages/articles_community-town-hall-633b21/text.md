Paul, MN — State Representative Danny Nadeau (R–Rogers) will host a community town hall meeting on Saturday, April 19 , from 2:00 to 3:30 p.m. at the Champlin Ice Forum – Large Meeting Room .
The event is open to the public and will offer constituents an opportunity to hear updates from the Capitol, share concerns, and discuss issues important to the community.
“Join me on Saturday afternoon, April 19th and let’s talk about what’s happening at the Capitol,” said Rep.
Nadeau.
“Meeting, listening and discussing issues important to us is a top priority and I always look forward to these meetings.” Event Details: What: Town Hall Meeting with Rep.
Danny Nadeau When: Saturday, April 19, 2025 | 2:00–3:30 p.m.
Where: Champlin Ice Forum – Large Meeting Room Address: 12165 Ensign Ave N, Champlin, MN 55316 No RSVP is necessary.
All are welcome to attend.
