Nadeau Takes Oath of Office, Sees Big Opportunity Ahead
St.
PAUL – State Rep.
Danny Nadeau appeared at the Capitol on Tuesday to be sworn in as a member of the Minnesota House of Representatives.
“We have a tremendous opportunity this session, but only if we’re determined to focus on people rather than politics and we remain committed to common sense, common honesty, and common decency,” said Nadeau.
This will be the second half of Nadeau’s first term representing District 34A.
He is a member of three committees this biennium and will serve as Vice Chair of the House Committee on Health Finance & Policy.
To get in touch with Rep.
Nadeau, you can contact him at 651-296-4315 or email rep.danny.nadeau@house.mn.gov.
Residents of District 34A can stay up to date by subscribing to his periodic email updates through his official state website: www.house.mn/06a
