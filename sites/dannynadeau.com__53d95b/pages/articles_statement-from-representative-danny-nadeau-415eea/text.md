RELEASE: Statement from Representative Danny Nadeau
Saturday, June 14, 2025
News Release
Statement from Representative Danny Nadeau
Rogers, MN – Representative Danny Nadeau (R-Rogers) released the following statement in response to the horrific shooting that claimed the lives of former Speaker Melissa Hortman and her husband Mark, and left Senator John Hoffman and his wife Yvette hospitalized:
“Like so many across Minnesota, I’m heartbroken and deeply shaken by the violence that occurred this morning.
My heart aches for my colleagues and their families, and I’m heartbroken for our world.
Melissa Hortman and John Hoffman were dedicated public servants who were passionate about serving their communities and the people of this state.
The consequences of threats, intimidation, and the violent rhetoric that’s accepted and encouraged in the political environment today are severe, sad, and unacceptable.
We must never forget that an attack on one lawmaker is an assault on each of us and the very foundation of our society.
I’m grateful for the first responders and law enforcement officers working to protect the public and locate the individual responsible for this act.
As we wait for more information, I urge all Minnesotans to set aside division and recommit to treating one another with decency and respect regardless of our differences”
