PAUL, MN, June 19, 2024 —The state’s leading small business political action committee, NFIB MN PAC, today endorsed Representative Danny Nadeau (Rogers) for re-election in House District 34A.
“Representative Danny Nadeau is a proven advocate for small business,” said John Reynolds, NFIB Minnesota State Director.
“The hardworking men and women on Main Street know that Danny always has their back.
Now more than ever, Main Street needs strong allies like Danny Nadeau in the Minnesota House.” State legislative endorsements are determined by the NFIB MN PAC Board, which is comprised of small business owners in the state.
Incumbent legislative endorsements are based on the 2023-24 NFIB MN Voting Record, which reflects how a lawmaker voted on priority issues for small business owners.
Lawmakers must have a score of 70 percent or better to qualify for endorsement.
Nadeau holds a 100 percent pro-small business voting record.
NFIB represents more than 10,000 small businesses in Minnesota.
The NFIB MN PAC endorsement is an important signal to voters across the state that a candidate will stand with small business.
