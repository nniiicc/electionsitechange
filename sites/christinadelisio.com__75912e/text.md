Support The Campaign Meet Christina Christina Delisio grew up in a family rooted in public service, learning early the values of responsibility, opportunity, and looking out for others.
After moving to Massachusetts as a child, she attended UMass Lowell, earning degrees in Political Science and History.
Christina built a career in environmental consulting, working on lead, asbestos, air quality, and site assessments, and later training inspectors nationwide for OSHA and the EPA.
She went on to earn her Juris Doctor from New England Law | Boston while raising three young children, then practiced law handling liability and property matters.
After returning to Massachusetts in 2014, Christina became deeply involved in her community—revitalizing the Manchester Essex Booster Club and serving on the Manchester Planning Board, where she has served as Vice Chair.
Christina is running for State Representative to bring practical experience, steady leadership, and a commitment to families, responsible planning, and public service to Beacon Hill.
