Meet Christina
My Priorities
Cape Ann is one of the most beautiful places in Massachusetts but it’s also becoming one of the hardest places to afford.
Families are being squeezed by rising costs.
Small businesses are struggling under fees and mandates.
Too many decisions are being made on Beacon Hill, without local input.
I’m running to change that.
AFFORDABILITY
Fighting rising electric fees, taxes, and costs that families didn’t vote for — and can’t escape.
ACCOUNTABILITY
Standing up for transparency, including advocating for a full audit of the Legislature.
LOCAL CONTROL
Protecting Cape Ann’s right to make decisions here — not from Beacon Hill.
