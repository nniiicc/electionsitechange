Danica Attends the 30th Annual Pocono Prayer Breakfast
On Tuesday, October 7, 2025, Danica attended the 30th Annual Pocono Prayer Breakfast at Kalahari Resorts and Conventions, joining hundreds of community members for a morning of prayer, reflection, and fellowship.
For more than three decades, the Pocono Prayer Breakfast has brought people together from across the region to unite in faith and community spirit.
While the event itself is non-political, it offers an important opportunity for local leaders and residents to come together around shared values such as faith, family, service, and love of country.
As a candidate for State Representative, Danica was honored to take part in this long-standing Pocono tradition.
She spent the morning connecting with community members, joining in prayer, and reflecting on how gatherings like this strengthen the bonds that hold our region together.
This year’s featured guest speaker was Mandy Harvey, a singer-songwriter and America’s Got Talent Golden Buzzer recipient.
Her inspiring story of perseverance and faith deeply moved the audience, reminding everyone of the power of resilience, hope, and belief in one another.
Danica shared her appreciation for the event’s message of unity and encouragement, noting how important it is for the community to come together in gratitude and shared purpose.
She left the breakfast feeling inspired by the compassion and dedication of those in attendance, and reaffirmed in her commitment to serve the people of the Poconos with integrity and care.
The Pocono Prayer Breakfast continues to be a cherished local tradition, bringing people of all backgrounds together to celebrate faith and the shared values that make the Pocono community strong.
