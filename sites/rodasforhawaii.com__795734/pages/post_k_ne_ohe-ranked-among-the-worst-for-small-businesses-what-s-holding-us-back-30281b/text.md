Featured Udpate: All Posts Community Engagement Government Transparency Local Kāneʻohe Issues Kāneʻohe Ranked Among the Worst For Small Businesses: What’s Holding Us Back?
'Ale Rodas May 12 1 min read For the last several years, we’ve seen small businesses come and go here in Kāneʻohe.
Recent data from WalletHub confirmed what our community has been feeling : Hawaii is one of the hardest places in the country to start and sustain a small business, and our very own Kāneʻohe ranks among the worst .
But what exactly is making it so difficult?
As reported in the WalletHub article, not only do high startup costs make opening a business unrealistic for many, but the looming red tape and permitting delays also slow the process and further drive up expenses.
Once a business actually opens its doors, scaling becomes another challenge.
Due to limited access to investors and a weak infrastructure , growing a business in Kaneohe is a real challenge many face.
As a result, small businesses are more vulnerable to closures .
However, this isn’t just about business owners.
When small businesses face difficulties, the entire community feels it .
Fewer local jobs, limited services, and fewer places that make Kaneohe feel like home are all the result of these struggles .
We have to ask: What specific actions are local leadership taking to support small businesses in Kāneʻohe?
How can we, as a community, come together to address these challenges?
We deserve leadership that actively supports local entrepreneurs, strengthens our economy , and keeps the heart of Kāneʻohe alive.
