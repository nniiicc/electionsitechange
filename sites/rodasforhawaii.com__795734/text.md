This is home.
This isn’t just where I live; it’s where I am raising my children, building relationships, and investing in the future.
Like many families, we chose here because of its deep sense of ʻohana, its respect for kupuna, and the way neighbors still look out for one another.
But lately, something feels off.
We see it in the growing challenges: families struggling, small businesses being pushed out, and a disconnect between the people and those meant to represent them.
I’m running because I believe leadership should feel close again.
Present, accountable, and rooted in the community it serves.

MY PRIORITIES
❤️ Protect Our Families
Strong families build strong communities.
I stand for parental rights, strengthening education options, advocating for our kupuna’s security and legacy, and addressing public safety and homelessness with real solutions.
🌿 Protect The Land
Protecting the land means maintaining our infrastructure, preserving the culture and natural resources, and taking a proactive approach to caring for our community.
More importantly, it means listening to residents before any big development or major changes to our landscape.
💰 Protect Our Every Dollar
Every tax dollar comes from a person who worked hard to earn it.
I stand for transparency, accountability, and responsible use of taxpayer dollars so our community sees real results.
