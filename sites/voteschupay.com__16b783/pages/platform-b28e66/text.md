THE ISSUES
Economic Affordability
Ensure economic affordability for hard-working Floridians and their families
- Reform home insurance legislation to reduce costs
- Stabilize electricity costs
- Extend healthcare benefits for seniors, working families, disabled citizens, and veterans
- Increase job training programs with a focus on labor shortage areas
- Expand new home buyer programs to allow more Floridians the opportunity to own
Safeguard Florida
Safeguard Florida by protecting our Constitutional rights, and supporting law enforcement and first responders
- Promote safety, security, and law enforcement for our citizens
- Propose/Support legislation to ensure voting rights, expand reproductive rights, and maintain marriage equality
- Protect our neighbors from unconstitutional federal law enforcement
- Employ common-sense gun control laws
Sustainable Future
Build a better, more sustainable future that accounts for all Floridians to live the American dream
- Ensure equity measures exist to address educational disparities, food insecurity, and healthcare access
- Improve our education system beginning with teacher wages
- Implement environmental resiliency measures to be better prepared for climate change events
- Protect our Florida wildlife and natural preserves, and expand conservation efforts
