GET INVOLVED
Here's an opportunity to do something that will impact the community.
Help Conrad make a difference.
There are many ways to volunteer, from walking door to door to hosting fundraising events.
Let us know how you can help:
BECOME A VOLUNTEER
Volunteering is the heart of our campaign and the most powerful way to make a real difference in our community.
From canvassing and phone outreach to event support and digital engagement, there is a place for everyone to get involved.
Together, we can create positive change by showing up, working together, and moving our community forward—one conversation at a time.
HOST A HOUSE PARTY
Hosting a house party is a simple and impactful way to support the campaign by bringing friends, neighbors, and community members together for meaningful conversation.
These gatherings create a welcoming space to share the candidate’s vision, ask questions, and build personal connections that strengthen our grassroots efforts.
Whether large or small, your home can become a powerful place for engagement and momentum.
MAKE A DONATION
Your donation helps fuel a grassroots campaign powered by people, not special interests.
Every contribution—no matter the size—supports voter outreach, community events, and the resources needed to share our message across the district.
By giving today, you are investing in a campaign committed to listening, serving, and building a stronger future for our community.
