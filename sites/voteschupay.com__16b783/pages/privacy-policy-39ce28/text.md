PRIVACY POLICY
Campaign for Conrad Schupay- Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 19, 2026
The Campaign for Conrad Schupay is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information you provide when visiting our website, signing up for updates, volunteering, or making a contribution.
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:
•Name
•Email address
•Phone number
•Mailing address or ZIP code
•Volunteer interests
•Donation and payment information (processed securely by a third-party vendor)
We may also automatically collect limited, non-identifying information such as browser type, device type, and pages visited to help improve our website and communications.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
•Communicate campaign updates, events, and opportunities
•Respond to inquiries and volunteer requests
•Process donations and comply with campaign finance laws
•Improve website functionality and outreach efforts
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
Text Messaging & Email Communications
Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.
Opt-in information will not be shared with third parties unless required by law.
By providing your phone number or email address, you consent to receive campaign communications.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in our messages or contacting us directly.
Donations
Online donations are processed through secure third-party payment processors.
We do not store your full credit card or banking information.
Donation information may be disclosed as required by Florida and federal campaign finance laws.
Sharing of Information
We may share information:
•With trusted vendors who assist with campaign operations
•When required by law, regulation, or legal process
All vendors are expected to maintain appropriate data security standards.
Cookies & Tracking
Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to enhance user experience and analyze website traffic.
You can adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies if you prefer.
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.
However, no online transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
Children’s Privacy
This website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Your Choices
You may request to update, correct, or remove your personal information by contacting us at: Conrad@VoteSchupay.com
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the Campaign for Conrad Schupay at Conrad@VoteSchupay.com
