Since earning your trust as State Representative in 2022, I’ve brought more state money into our district than any elected official in the last two decades.
I've delivered on my priorities to fund local healthcare, preserve farmland and open space, and support strong public schools.
When the Exeter fire burned more than 200 acres, it was a wake-up call.
The words of our fire chief stayed with me: Rhode Island’s forests are neglected.
In 2023 I got a bill passed, chaired a commission, brought in experts, and pushed hard to change a state budget with zero funding for forest management.
In addition to forest management provisions I fought for in the Green Bond, we've won $180,000 for 2 new forest rangers, and $50,000 for the first new forestry hoses since the 1980s.
