Boston, MA – Today, Team Healey-Driscoll launched a new, six-figure ad buy across broadcast, cable, digital, and streaming platforms.
The 30-second spot, “Choose,” highlights Governor Maura Healey’s vision for a Massachusetts where everyone can live safely, afford to raise a family, access top schools and healthcare, and have their freedoms protected – contrasted with Donald Trump and Mike Minogue’s harmful agenda of rising costs and hurting our communities.
The ad will run statewide across broadcast and cable, and on digital and streaming platforms.
