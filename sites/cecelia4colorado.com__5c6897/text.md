A Lifetime of Breaking Barriers.
Cecelia was the first.
She made sure she wasn’t the last. ★ FIRST IN HER FAMILY to attend college, graduate with honors, and attend law school. ★ FIRST MEXICAN-AMERICAN to become an appellate judge on the highest immigration court in the country. ★ FIRST LATINA to become a tenure-track professor to teach law in Colorado at the University of Denver. ★ PAVING THE WAY FOR OTHERS to enter, lead, and thrive in the spaces where she once had to be the first.
A New Era of Progress .
Cecelia has spent a lifetime opening doors.
Now she's using that experience to change what happens on the other side of them. ★ Justice that works for people.
Cecelia brings decades of experience in the courtroom and legal system to the Capitol, working to make Colorado’s justice system more fair, accurate, and accountable. ★ Safer communities.
Smarter solutions.
Cecelia believes public safety means protecting people while advancing practical solutions that address the challenges facing our communities. ★ Keeping Colorado affordable for families and businesses.
From the cost of raising a family to the cost of running a business, Cecelia is fighting to keep Colorado affordable and ensure everyone has the ability to thrive. ★ Progress that changes lives.
Because progress isn’t just about changing the law.
It’s about changing people’s lives.
