“As a dedicated nurse, volunteer, and community advocate, I will work to earn your trust and your vote for Illinois State Senator.” - Danielle Penman THIS NOVEMBER, ELECT A NURSE WHO WILL PUT PEOPLE BEFORE POLITICS.
As a labor-and-delivery nurse for 25 years, Danielle Penman has spent her career caring for families during some of life's most important moments.
She's seen firsthand how rising healthcare costs, higher insurance premiums, and limited access to care place unnecessary burdens on working families.
Danielle will work across party lines to lower healthcare costs, protect coverage for pre-existing conditions, safeguard access to IVF and fertility care and ensure quality healthcare remains close to home.
