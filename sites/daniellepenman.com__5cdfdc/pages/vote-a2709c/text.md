Vote By Mail Registered McHenry County voters may request a one-time Vote by Mail ballot or enroll in permanent Vote by Mail.
Ballots returned through the mail must be postmarked by Election Day and received no later than 14 days after Election Day.
Ballots can also be returned through authorized drop boxes or delivered to the election authority.
