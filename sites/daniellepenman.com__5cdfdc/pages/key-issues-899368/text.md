Key Issues
- Make Illinois Affordable • Provide property tax relief with commonsense reforms that reduce the burden on homeowners and make it easier for families to stay in the communities they love. • Lower the cost of living through policies that reduce energy costs, ease the burden of high utility bills, and make everyday life more affordable. • Balance the budget before asking taxpayers for more, ensuring government lives within its means and spends every dollar wisely.
- Expand Affordable, High-Quality Healthcare • Lower healthcare costs with a focus on reducing insurance premiums and prescription drug costs for Illinois families. • Protect women’s healthcare through stronger support for IVF and fertility care for mothers and babies while safeguarding coverage for pre-existing conditions. • Strengthen the healthcare workforce by recruiting and retaining doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals across Illinois.
- Put People Before Politics • Work across party lines to deliver practical solutions that lower costs, and improve the lives of Illinois families. • Demand honest government through greater transparency, stronger ethics, and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars. • Hold Politicians Accountable by opposing legislative pay raises until Illinois is on a sustainable fiscal path.
- Strengthen Public Safety • Support law enforcement by ensuring police officers have the resources, training, and support they need to protect our communities. • Restore cash bail through the repeal of key provisions of the SAFE-T Act, putting the rights of victims ahead of violent offenders. • Hold repeat violent offenders accountable with tougher penalties that keep dangerous criminals off our streets.
- Prepare Students for Successful Futures • Empower parents with more educational opportunities and a stronger voice in their children’s education. • Raise academic standards through greater accountability for student achievement and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars. • Expand career pathways so students have strong opportunities to pursue apprenticeships, vocational education, military service, or college. • Strengthen workforce training by building stronger partnerships between community colleges, union apprenticeship programs, local employers, and vocational schools.
