JENNIFER BOYLAN FOR RHODE ISLAND STATE REPRESENTATIVE, DISTRICT 66 RIVERSIDE / BARRINGTON
It’s truly been an honor to be a public servant for the people of House District 66, and I am excited to continue fighting for the issues that matter most to my community.
I am seeking re-election to the Rhode Island House of Representatives in the 2026 election.
Having served two terms in the Rhode Island General Assembly representing House District 66, I remain committed to continuing to work on behalf of the people of Riverside and Barrington.
During my time in office, I have been a strong advocate for protecting the environment, mitigating the effects of climate change, supporting common sense gun laws and reproductive freedom, and supporting families and small businesses.
