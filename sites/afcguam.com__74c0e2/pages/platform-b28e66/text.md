alicia's promise
Lowering Everyday Costs
Families feel the pressure of rising costs every single day — from groceries and housing to utilities and transportation.
Alicia will fight for practical solutions that help lower the cost of living, strengthen infrastructure, and reduce the policies that make life more expensive for island families.
Because people should not have to struggle just to afford the basics.
Making Housing More Attainable
Housing costs are pushing too many families into difficult choices.
Alicia supports expanding affordable housing, helping first-time homebuyers, and making sure federal housing programs actually work for local families.
A home should feel possible again.
Making Government Work Better
Too many people spend their time fighting broken systems instead of getting the help they need.
Long delays.
Complicated processes.
Services people cannot access when they need them most.
Alicia will push for better coordination, accountability, and investment so government programs work the way families need them to.
Because when systems fail, families pay the price.
Better Access to Healthcare and Childcare
Families should not have to wait months for care or struggle to find affordable childcare just to keep working.
Alicia will fight to improve healthcare access, strengthen local services, support healthcare workers, and expand childcare support for working families.
These are not luxuries.
They are basic needs.
Good Jobs and Real Opportunity
People should be able to build a future here at home.
Alicia supports workforce training, apprenticeships, vocational education, digital infrastructure improvement, and federal investments that lead to real jobs and long-term economic opportunity.
Opportunity should not depend on leaving your community behind.
Supporting Local Businesses
Small businesses keep our economy moving and create opportunity across our island.
Alicia will work to reduce unnecessary barriers, expand access to capital, and ensure local businesses have a fair opportunity to compete and grow.
When local businesses succeed, families succeed too.
Bringing Federal Resources Home
Guam deserves a stronger voice and a fair share of federal investment.
Alicia will fight to secure funding for infrastructure, healthcare, housing, energy, and economic development — while making sure those resources create real results for local families.
Because our community should never be treated as an afterthought.
Building a Stronger Future
A stronger future requires smart investments now:
Reliable infrastructure.
Affordable energy.
Better healthcare access.
Modern connectivity.
Safe communities.
Alicia believes we can build a future where families are not constantly struggling just to keep up.
