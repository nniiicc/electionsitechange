Key Campaign Issues
alicia's vision for guam
Lowering Everyday Costs
Families are working hard, but too many are still struggling to keep up with rising costs.
Alicia is focused on practical solutions that help make housing, utilities, and everyday life more affordable.
Making Life Easier for Families
When services fall short, families feel the impact most.
Alicia believes people deserve healthcare, childcare, and public services that are easier to access and work the way they should.
Creating Opportunity Close to Home
People should be able to build a future right here at home.
Alicia will support good-paying jobs, workforce training, and local businesses that strengthen our community and keep opportunity close to home.
Bringing Resources Home
Guam deserves a stronger voice and a fair share of federal investment.
Alicia will fight to ensure federal funding leads to real improvements in infrastructure, healthcare, housing, and community services people can actually feel in their daily lives.
Why Alicia is Running
Alicia is running because too many families are working harder than ever and still feeling like they’re falling behind.
She believes people deserve leaders who understand the everyday pressures families face — rising costs, limited access to services, and uncertainty about the future — and who will fight to make life work better for the community they call home.

meet alicia
Alicia knows the strength, resilience, and sacrifices of the people of Guam because she has spent years working closely with the community and hearing directly from families about the pressures they face every day.
She’s running for Congress to fight for practical solutions that help make life easier — lowering everyday costs, improving access to services, supporting local businesses, and creating more opportunity for families to build a future right here at home.
