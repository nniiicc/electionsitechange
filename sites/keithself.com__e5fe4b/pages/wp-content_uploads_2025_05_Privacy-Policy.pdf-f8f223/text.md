PRIVACY POLICY- Keith Self for Congress Welcome to the official website of Keith Self for Congress.
Privacy is important to us.
We understand that your right to privacy is important, especially online.
The only personal information collected is information you give us.
In an effort to grow our community online, we may ask you to submit information.

Some information must be collected when you make a contribution.
