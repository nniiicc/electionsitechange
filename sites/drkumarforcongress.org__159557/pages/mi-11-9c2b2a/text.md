ABOUT THE DISTRICT
Michigan’s 11th Congressional District covers much of Oakland County and includes a wide range of communities.
Among them are:
Auburn Hills, Berkley, Birmingham, Bloomfield Township, Bloomfield Hills, Clawson, Commerce Township, Farmington, Farmington Hills, Ferndale, Hazel Park, Huntington Woods, Lake Angelus, Madison Heights, Oak Park, Orchard Lake Village, Pleasant Ridge, Pontiac, Royal Oak, Royal Oak Township, Sylvan Lake, Troy, Walled Lake, Waterford, West Bloomfield Township, White Lake Township, Wixom, Wolverine Lake and Novi.
