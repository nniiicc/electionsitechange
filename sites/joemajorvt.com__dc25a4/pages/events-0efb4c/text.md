VT Primary Election Day
All polling locations in Vermont close at 7PM.
Contact your town clerk for additional information.
Planning to vote early?
Most but not all towns have drop boxes or a designated and secure location for voters to deposit their ballots.
One way to return your early/absentee ballot is to deposit it in a drop box in the town and district where you'd usually vote in person.
Download the statewide list of ballot drop boxes (PDF) to find where the drop box is in your town.
Polling Places: https://sos.vermont.gov/elections/voters/polling-places
VT Voter Portal: https://vote.vermont.gov/public/dashboard
