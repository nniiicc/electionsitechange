DONATE MEET ANN I'm Ann Shippy—a retired Air Force Colonel, long-time resident of Hilton Head, and I’ve spent a lifetime in service - both in uniform and in my community.
I served as the South Carolina Ambassador to the Women’s Memorial in Washington, DC, and here on Hilton Head, I’ve served as an HOA President, a Deacon, and now an Elder in my church, and as a volunteer with the Hilton Head Humane Association.
Service is in my DNA. ​ I served our country for 28 years, and my husband Doug—a retired Air Force fighter pilot—and I have built our lives around service and community.
As a retired Air Force officer myself, my professional experience has prepared me to lead with a mission-first mindset and accountability. ​ Commanding large organizations required planning, strategy, communication, and, most importantly, listening, trusting, and representing the people who depend on you. ​ District 123 is ready for fresh ideas, a new approach, and strong, accountable leadership that delivers real solutions for our community.
Your voice matters, and I WILL listen and act on your behalf.
You can count on representation with honesty, integrity, and open communication. ​ Your Issues Are My Issues.
