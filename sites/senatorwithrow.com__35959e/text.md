ISSUES
Adam Withrow's Platform
ENERGY
Let's get real.
Fossil fuels are obsolete.
Yes, we have them.
Yes, we will continue to use them, but they are finite resources, and the rate we use them up keeps increasing.
Building for the future based on technology from the past is beyond foolish.
The possibilities for the future are amazingly bright, and we must pave the way for a smooth transition starting today.
HEALTHCARE
The business of medicine is out of control.
Our health and well-being have taken the back seat to corporate profits.
We have the worst health outcomes per dollar spent, and the goal appears to be more about keeping us hooked than keeping us healthy.
It's time to re imagine our healthcare system and create one that works for everyone.
Healthcare for all would cost less than half of our defense budget.
ANTI-WAR, EVERY WAR
Do you know what the people living in every conflict zone in the world want to hear?
"The war is over.
We are at peace."
No one wants their home blown up over which flag flies where.
People just want to live in the place they call home.
We have too many things that work too well to be interrupting the progress of humanity with war.
Free trade and mutual global recognition of equality will accomplish what a billion pounds of bombs can not.
It is not in our best interest to prop up brutal regimes, nor is it in our interest to fund conflicts against our business competitors.
It is in our best interest to recognize that we are all equally and fully human.
We don't have time for this right now.
We cannot escape this planet if we mess this up, and it will be much more fun to preserve it for future generations.
