The Good Governance Board
Let's create a citizens' board to govern the government.
By the People...
-Establish a Board from throughout the state to keep track of our progress based on the following criteria:
*Participation
*Rule of Law
*Transparency
*Responsiveness
For the People.
*Consensus Orientation
*Equity and Inclusiveness
*Effectiveness and Efficiency
*Accountability
-Empower the board to make judgements and recommendations on the success or failure of our elected officials.
