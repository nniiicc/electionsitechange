ADAM WITHROW FOR SENATE
I am not a politician, I am a working-class man who has spent his entire life working just to be able to get back to work.
I've never been rich, at least not the money kind, anyway.
The life I've lived allows me to see how truly impoverished our civilization has become.
We've got classic poverty, and we've also got 'the new poverty.' The new poverty is embodied in those insatiable fools with more material wealth than ancient kings who still want more.
We used to call those people rich, but isn't true material wealth knowing you have enough?
I'm not interested in taking what they have, I'm interested in stopping them from taking what little we have left.
Money has become more powerful than the people who have it.
The undue influence of the dollar is leading us to ruin.
I understand that the challenges we face are not going to be solved overnight, but as your Senator, I will work tirelessly to advocate for policies that help create a better future for all of us.

Time For a Change
I'm sick of hearing about "working across the aisle." The biggest donors fund "both sides" of every election, and the policy outcomes are determined by those dollars.
A safe seat politician in a single party dominant district or state can act out a certain number of betrayals and keep their job.
Party zealots will bend over backwards to justify their actions.
Our current two-party system is a complete joke, and it is the laughing stock of the world.
Ever hear someone say that "serious people" think something about American politics?
We should stop taking these "serious people" seriously.

Be Involved, Stay Involved.
Your Voice, My Vote.
As a candidate for Senate, I believe that my responsibility is to represent the people, not special interests or party lines.
I mean that.
We can all see where that's leading us.
My goal is to listen to the voices of my constituents and take on the issues that matter most to them.
While I have a set of beliefs and values that drive me, I believe that my decisions on important votes should be influenced by the residents of our state in real time.

Are we really OK with that?
