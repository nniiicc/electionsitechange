Campaign Finance
Ever get the feeling that money has too much power in politics?
Are we really...
-Penalize those who receive substantive, material favors for their contributions.
Penalize those who do the favors, as well.
-Set limits for the raising and spending of money for campaigns.
-Prevent contributions from outside of the voting district or state.
So easily bought?
-Distinguish between a natural person and an artificial entity, i.e. a corporation.
Prevent artificial entities from participating.
-Create central, federal repository for candidate and platform info for all registered candidates at all phases of elections.
-Promote the idea that fundraising is detrimental to good governance.
