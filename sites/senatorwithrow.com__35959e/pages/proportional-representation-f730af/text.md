Proportional Representation
Our system is broken.
Only revolutionary change will fix it.
No more...
-Winner-take-all elections always leave people feeling burned.
-Single-member districts force us into winner take all.
-Voting for the lesser of two evils is still voting for evil.
-There should be no losing voters in elections, only losing candidates.
Wasted votes.
-The House of Representatives should be restructured into multi-member districts, where party representation is proportionate to the percentage of the vote they garner.
-Minor parties have major ideas, and they are passed over as 'wasted' votes.
Proportional representation allows you to vote FOR your favorite, not AGAINST the worst.
