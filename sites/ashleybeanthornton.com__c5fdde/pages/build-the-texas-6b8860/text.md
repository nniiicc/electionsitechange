Let’s Build the Texas We Want.
When I started this campaign, one of my friends said, “Talk about what you want, not just about what you don’t want.” I love that advice.
So, the first thing I did when I decided to run was to start listening.
What do you want your state representative to work on and care about?
What kind of Texas do you want to live in?
Thousands of bills will be filed in the upcoming legislative session.
As I think about how I will advocate for or against the bills that come before me — I plan to refer to this blueprint.
Will this bill help to build this kind of Texas?
I’m still listening.
If you have an issue that you are passionate about, I would love to hear what you have to say.
My email is Ashley@ashleybeanthornton.com.
Send me a note.
