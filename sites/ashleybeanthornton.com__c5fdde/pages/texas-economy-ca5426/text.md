The Texas Economy should work for ALL of us.
Texas has a booming economy — but is it booming for all of us?
Texas may be attracting new jobs — but do those jobs pay enough to support a family?
If we want our economy to keep growing, we need to reinvest a share of the benefits of that growth into the people who make it possible.
The people who live and work full-time in the state Governor Abbott calls “the Best Business Climate in America” should not be struggling to afford housing, healthcare, childcare, and everyday basics.
Texas can be business-friendly and worker-friendly.
It can be pro-growth and pro-family.
