Oregon veterans and their families have access to a wide range of state and federal resources, from health care and education to home loans, employment assistance, emergency financial support, and long-term care.
The resources below can help you find the right place to start.
Find a Veteran Service Officer Veteran Service Officers, or VSOs, help veterans and their families understand and access the benefits they have earned through military service.
They can assist with disability compensation and pension claims, health care benefits, education benefits, military records, survivor benefits, and other state and federal programs.
County Veteran Service Officers throughout Oregon are trained and certified through the Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs (ODVA).
ODVA also maintains resources for Tribal veterans, student veterans, and veterans seeking specialized services.
Find veteran services in your county: https://www.oregon.gov/odva/services/pages/default.aspx Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs 700 Summer St.
NE Salem, OR 97301 Phone: 800-692-9666 or 503-373-2085 Email: orvets.benefits@odva.oregon.gov Oregon Veterans' Emergency Financial Assistance The Oregon Veterans' Emergency Financial Assistance Program provides one-time emergency financial assistance to eligible veterans and their immediate family members who are experiencing an emergency financial need.
Assistance may be available for needs including: Emergency or temporary housing Rent or mortgage assistance Utilities and insurance Emergency home repairs Emergency medical or dental expenses Emergency transportation Payments are made directly to creditors rather than applicants.
Veterans seeking assistance should contact a Veteran Service Officer or veteran advocate for help applying.
Learn more about emergency financial assistance: https://www.oregon.gov/odva/agency-programs/grants/Pages/Emergency-Assistance-Grant.aspx Oregon Veteran Home Loan Program Oregon is one of only a handful of states with its own veteran home loan program.
Since 1945, the Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs has provided approximately $10 billion in low-interest home loans to more than 340,000 veterans.
The ODVA Home Loan Program is separate from the federal VA Home Loan Guaranty Program.
Eligible veterans may qualify for fixed-rate financing for an owner-occupied primary residence in Oregon.
Financing is available up to the applicable Fannie Mae loan limit, and eligible veterans may use the ODVA home loan benefit up to four times during their lifetime, with only one ODVA loan open at a time.
Learn more about Oregon Veteran Home Loans: https://www.oregon.gov/odva/home-loans/pages/default.aspx ODVA Home Loan Program Phone: 800-633-6826 or 503-373-2373 Email: orvetshomeloans@odva.oregon.gov Oregon Veterans' Homes Oregon operates two Veterans' Homes providing skilled nursing and long-term care for eligible veterans and family members.
Care at an Oregon Veterans' Home is an earned benefit available to qualifying veterans, their spouses, and eligible Gold Star parents.
Admission requires that skilled nursing care is medically necessary.

Specialized veteran employment services can help eligible veterans overcome barriers to employment, connect with employers, develop job-search strategies, and access training and career resources.
Veterans and eligible spouses may also qualify for priority of service for certain employment and training programs.
Find employment resources for veterans through WorkSource Oregon: https://www.worksourceoregon.org/ Education and GI Bill Benefits Veterans, service members, and eligible family members may qualify for federal education and training benefits, including the Post-9/11 GI Bill and other VA education programs.
Benefits may help cover college tuition, vocational or technical training, apprenticeships, licensing and certification tests, and other approved education and training opportunities.
Explore federal VA education benefits: https://www.va.gov/education/ Many Oregon colleges and universities also have veteran resource centers or designated staff who can help student veterans navigate benefits and campus resources.
Find Oregon campus veteran services: https://www.oregon.gov/odva/services/pages/campus-veteran-services.aspx VA Health Care Veterans who meet federal eligibility requirements may qualify for health care through the U.S.
Department of Veterans Affairs.
Eligibility depends on factors including military service, discharge status, service-connected disabilities, income, and other circumstances.
Eligibility has also expanded for many veterans exposed to burn pits, Agent Orange, and other toxic substances through the PACT Act.
Check your eligibility and apply for VA health care: https://www.va.gov/health-care/eligibility/ Manage Your VA Health Care Online Veterans enrolled in VA health care can use My HealtheVet on VA.gov to manage many aspects of their care online.
Depending on eligibility and availability, veterans can: Refill and manage VA prescriptions Schedule and manage appointments Send secure messages to their VA health care team Review medical records View lab and test results Order certain medical supplies Access My HealtheVet on VA.gov: https://www.va.gov/health-care/manage-health/ Federal Veterans Benefits The U.S.
Department of Veterans Affairs provides benefits and services covering health care, disability compensation, pensions, education and training, housing assistance, life insurance, careers and employment, survivor benefits, and burial and memorial services.
Explore federal veterans benefits: https://www.va.gov/ ODVA Conservatorship and Fiduciary Services The Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs provides professional fiduciary services for eligible veterans and family members who need assistance managing their financial affairs.
ODVA's Conservatorship Program can assist with managing income and assets, paying bills, corresponding with creditors, managing property and investments, and other financial responsibilities.
ODVA also provides representative payee services for eligible veterans.
Learn more about ODVA programs and fiduciary services: https://www.oregon.gov/odva/agency-programs/Pages/default.aspx 2026 Oregon Veteran Benefits Magazine The Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs publishes the Oregon Veteran Benefits Magazine, a comprehensive guide to state and federal benefits available to Oregon veterans and their families.
The 2026 Oregon Veteran Benefits Magazine includes current information about disability compensation, health care, education, employment, home loans, long-term care, behavioral health resources, Veteran Service Officers, and other programs.
View the current Veteran Benefits Magazine and other ODVA resources: https://www.oregon.gov/odva/ Need Help Finding the Right Resource?
You don't have to navigate these programs alone.
A trained Veteran Service Officer can help identify benefits you may qualify for and assist with the claims and application process.
Find veteran services near you: https://www.oregon.gov/odva/services/pages/default.aspx You can also contact the Oregon Department of Veterans' Affairs at 800-692-9666 or 503-373-2085 .
