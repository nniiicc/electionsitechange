Evans continues to provide experienced and effective leadership for Oregon, drawing on a lifetime of public service to strengthen our communities, improve public safety, prepare Oregon for emergencies and disasters, and expand opportunities for Oregonians.
He also serves in the following ways: As Co-Chair of the Joint Ways and Means Subcommittee on Public Safety , Paul helps guide critical state investments in public safety, the justice system, emergency response, and essential services.
His work on the Transportation Committee focuses on improving safety, strengthening Oregon's infrastructure, and ensuring our transportation system meets the needs of communities across the state.
A veteran himself, Paul brings decades of experience in public service and emergency preparedness to the Emergency Management and Veterans Committee , advocating for stronger disaster readiness and support for Oregon veterans and their families.
On the Higher Education Committee , Paul draws on his experience as an educator to support accessible education, workforce training, and pathways to good-paying careers.
Through his service on the Joint Ways and Means Committee and Emergency Board , Paul helps make responsible decisions about Oregon's budget and investments in communities throughout the state.
Priorities + Issues A Stronger Economy Local businesses are the backbone of our economy.
Our community needs to be a great place to run a business or find a job.
Paul will continue to oppose needless tax and fee increases, and he will fight to help small businesses thrive through support programs and cutting red tape.
Veterans As a veteran himself with more than 20 years in uniform, Paul knows what our veterans need when they return home.
Paul is committed to improving and providing resources for our servicemen and servicewomen.
His efforts led to a ballot measure that permanently sets aside 1.5% of all lottery revenue for veteran outreach and services.
Homelessness and Addiction Paul is committed to facing the homelessness and addiction crisis head on, and he understands this issue requires us to work together to find solutions that are unique to each community.
He will prioritize increased treatment for mental health and addiction support for community members battling addiction, and he is committed to making our streets safer for everyone by getting people into safe, stable housing.
Strengthening Schools Paul served on his local school board and is a teacher at Chemeketa Community College.
He understands how important it is to provide more training options to folks who are looking for a new career, and he is committed to creating more pathways to college and skills training programs that people can afford.
He will work to make smart investments that are proven to help lower class sizes, increase graduation rates, and expand summer programs.
Safer Communities A safe community is a thriving community.
As a former volunteer firefighter, Paul knows response times can be the difference between life and death, which is why he’s leading Oregon’s readiness effort and cutting bureaucratic red tape to lessen the impacts of natural disasters when they happen.
Paul will continue working to secure more resources for fire and emergency services and additional funding for police training and equipment.
Lowering Costs Paul is helping working families’ dollars stretch further.
He will continue to fight to make college more affordable, expand workforce pathways to higher paying jobs, and work to provide housing assistance to renters and landlords.
Abortion Access The Supreme Court’s decision to overturn Roe v.
Wade has made it more important than ever to protect the right to choose in Oregon.
Paul has voted time and again to protect access to abortion and is proud to be the only candidate in this race endorsed by Planned Parenthood PAC of Oregon.
