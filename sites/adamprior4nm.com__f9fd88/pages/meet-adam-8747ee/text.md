Read Adam’s Story Read Adam's Plan Candidate for NM House District 44 Corrales • East Rio Rancho • Northwest Albuquerque Meet Adam A Practical Voice for District 44 Adam Prior is a local general contractor, father, and neighbor—not a career politician.
He is running because District 44 deserves a representative who listens first, builds practical solutions, respects the budget, and delivers results with integrity.
Infrastructure & Accountability A Contractor’s Mindset for Infrastructure Adam meeting with first responders in District 44.
As a local general contractor, Adam knows what it takes to build, repair, and maintain things the right way.
He brings practical, blue-collar experience to decisions about budgets, capital projects, and public infrastructure.
In Santa Fe, Adam will treat tax dollars with the same strict accountability he applies to project budgets, fighting for District 44’s fair share of infrastructure funding to fix local roads, drainage, and flood-control systems.
Family & Community Why Adam Is Running Adam sees the same pressures his neighbors see: household costs rising faster than paychecks, roads and drainage that need attention, families worried about public safety, small businesses navigating unnecessary red tape, and parents asking for transparency and a meaningful voice in education.
He is running to bring a contractor's standard to public service: understand the problem, build a realistic plan, identify the cost, set a deadline, and remain accountable for the result.
