A Practical Voice for District 44
Adam Prior is a local general contractor, father, and neighbor—not a career politician.
He is running because District 44 deserves a representative who listens first, builds practical solutions, respects the budget, and delivers results with integrity.
A Contractor’s Mindset for Infrastructure
As a local general contractor, Adam knows what it takes to build, repair, and maintain things the right way.
He brings practical, blue-collar experience to decisions about budgets, capital projects, and public infrastructure.
In Santa Fe, Adam will treat tax dollars with the same strict accountability he applies to project budgets, fighting for District 44’s fair share of infrastructure funding to fix local roads, drainage, and flood-control systems.
Why Adam Is Running
Adam sees the same pressures his neighbors see: household costs rising faster than paychecks, roads and drainage that need attention, families worried about public safety, small businesses navigating unnecessary red tape, and parents asking for transparency and a meaningful voice in education.
He is running to bring a contractor's standard to public service: understand the problem, build a realistic plan, identify the cost, set a deadline, and remain accountable for the result.

Rooted in the Community
As a proud father raising his family in District 44, Adam experiences the same rising costs for groceries, gas, utilities, housing, and insurance as his neighbors.
He is running to ensure his children — and all children in our community — grow up in safe neighborhoods with access to high-quality, career-ready education.
Whether attending community events or listening to homeowners, first responders, educators, and small business owners across Corrales, East Rio Rancho, and Northwest Albuquerque, Adam is committed to protecting local values and keeping parents meaningfully involved in their children's education.
