Privacy Policy
Data Security & Transparency
Your Privacy is a Priority for Our Campaign.
This Privacy Policy describes how the Committee to Elect Adam Prior (“we,” "us," or “the Campaign”) collects, uses, and shares your information when you visit adamprior4nm.com.
1.
Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you sign up for our newsletter, volunteer, or make a donation.
This may include your name, email address, phone number, and zip code.
2.
SMS & Mobile Messaging
Text Messaging Opt-In
By providing your phone number, you consent to receive periodic campaign updates via automated text messages.
Msg & data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt-out at any time.
3.
Contributions & Third-Party Sharing
When you donate, we are required by New Mexico law to collect and report the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of individuals whose contributions exceed certain thresholds.
This information is a matter of public record.
We do not sell or lease your personal information.
We share data only with trusted campaign service vendors to carry out campaign transactions and legal reporting obligations.
4.
Use of Cookies
We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to analyze site traffic and improve your experience.
You can instruct your browser to refuse all cookies, though some portions of our site may not function properly as a result.
5.
Data Security
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information.
However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
6.
Your Rights
You may opt-out of receiving campaign emails or text messages at any time by following the "unsubscribe" or "STOP" instructions provided in those communications.
