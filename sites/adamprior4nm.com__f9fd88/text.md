Get to Know Him Meet Adam Prior Adam Prior is a local general contractor and father running to bring practical, results-driven leadership to Santa Fe.
He knows how to manage budgets, meet deadlines, solve infrastructure problems, and be accountable for the work.
Adam is focused on the everyday priorities of Corrales, East Rio Rancho, and Northwest Albuquerque: lower costs, safer neighborhoods, reliable roads and drainage, responsible use of tax dollars, and schools that are accountable to parents and families.
