Support Our Campaign Volunteer Voter Guide Caitlyn's Policy Priorities People-First Solutions Caitlyn Gegen will take an inclusive approach to all policies.
Select a priority below to read her full position.
01 Affordability I am committed to fighting for a livable minimum wage, expanding the stock of affordable housing, and advocating for universal healthcare.
I will fight for a future where every hardworking person in Georgia’s 9th Congressional District can live a life of dignity, where they have extra savings at the end of the month and are confident they will be able to buy their home.
02 Universal Healthcare Nobody should be dying, in the wealthiest country in the world, because they are unable to access the medical care they need.
I will fight for Medicare for All, because it will save about $1 trillion in healthcare costs per year, and it will save more than 100,000 lives every year.
03 The Epstein Class Pays Their Fair Share The Epstein Class needs to pay their fair share in taxes, which they are not currently doing .
If the government properly taxed the Epstein Class, we could use the extra funds to pay for social programs that would benefit everyone.
04 Hold the President Accountable Congress needs to take its power back and reinstitute the system of checks and balances it was meant to contribute to.
I will fight to help Congress to take back its war and taxation powers.
05 Stand Up to Flock, AI, and Data Centers Private companies with ALPRs are infringing on our rights, and tech companies are not acting responsibly.
We need representatives who understand the developing threats and are prepared to take action, such as creating a federal AI regulatory commission, working with other international actors, and fighting against the data centers that are taking our community resources.
06 Improve US World Standing The United States used to be a champion for human rights and a leader in global politics.
We are losing that reputation.
One way to regain it is by having a Congress that reins in the president’s power, so that the international community is not left to the whims of a new administration every 4-8 years.
07 Support Small Businesses Right now, big corporations are being prioritized, at the expense of smaller, locally-owned businesses.
That’s why I am committed to breaking up consolidation and monopolization in the marketplace, fully funding the small business administration, and lowering interest rates on small business loans.
08 Support Our Workers As someone who has helped unionize my workplace, I am familiar with the fear and hesitation workers feel when they try to demand better conditions.
That’s why I’m committed to fighting for the PRO Act and a livable minimum wage.
09 Abolish ICE ICE does not serve a unique purpose in the federal government, and its two main duties (enforcing immigration laws and investigating international crimes) could easily be absorbed by other federal agencies that do not routinely violate human rights.
This is not a call for open borders, but an acknowledgement that our current immigration enforcement system is threatening the safety of our communities, and we need to change.
10 Protect Human Rights I am committed to taking a proactive approach to protecting women’s rights, LGBTQIA+ rights, and the right to due process.
I will fight against discrimination in all forms, including in healthcare, in the workplace, in the military, and in the making of a family.
11 Protect Voting Rights The right to vote is fundamental to our democracy, but the Supreme Court has gutted the Voting Rights Act, removing the protections that guaranteed our right.
I am going to support legislation like the John R.
Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act, and fight to make Election Day a federal holiday.
12 Protect North Georgia’s Forests The current administration is not protecting our beautiful forests.
I am committed to fighting to make sure the US National Forest Service is fully funded and staffed, and to protecting the Roadless Rule, so that our forests are protected from intensive logging and road building.
13 Invest in North Georgia’s Rural Water Infrastructure North Georgia’s rural water infrastructure is outdated and in need of investment.
The degradation of the current system has made people go many days without water .
As your representative, I will fight for the funding we need to remove lead water pipes and ensure access to clean drinking water.
14 Improve Rail Safety Conditions I will fight to improve federal rail safety standards, to protect communities near rail lines.
This is especially important with the inland port coming to Gainesville.
15 Invest in Clean Energy in Georgia We are seeing the increasing impact of climate change, but it is not too late.
There is so much potential for clean energy investment in Georgia.
We are 5th in the country for solar energy, and investments in this sector will lead to decreased energy prices and increased energy independence .
16 Protect Women’s Rights Every woman should have the right to her own body, and should not have to travel out of state to get the care that she needs.
I will support legislation like the Women’s Health Protection Act, so that every woman can get the care she needs when she needs it.
I will also support legislation like the Black Maternal Health Momnibus Act, because maternal mortality rates are 2-3 times higher for Black women, and we need systemic change to address the issue.
17 Get Money Out of Politics The amount of money in politics right now is causing politicians to prioritize corporations over communities.
It has to stop.
That’s why I will advocate for campaign spending caps and legislation that overturns the Citizens United decision.
18 Support Our Rural Hospitals Our rural hospitals need increased investment so that they can keep serving our communities.
I will fight for universal healthcare because it will disproportionately benefit our rural hospitals, and I will fight to fully fund the federal programs that already benefit rural hospitals.
