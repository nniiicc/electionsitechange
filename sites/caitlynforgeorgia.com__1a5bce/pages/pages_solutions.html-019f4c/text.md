Support Our Campaign Volunteer Voter Guide Caitlyn's Policy Priorities People-First Solutions Caitlyn Gegen will take an inclusive approach to all policies.
Protecting Human Rights Every Georgian deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.
As your representative, I will fight to defend civil rights, bodily autonomy, LGBTQ+ rights, and voting rights.
I will start by overturning the Dobbs decision, abolishing ICE, and reinvigorating the voting rights act.
Advocate for Universal Healthcare I will fight for a universal healthcare system that lowers costs and improves services for Georgians.
Your health should not be dependent upon your income, and I will make sure we invest in the healthy future of our communities.
Expand Affordable Living Currently, our politicians are prioritizing the “Epstein class” over the working class.
I will tackle the cost of living with practical solutions on housing and fight to increase the minimum wage and break up monopolies, to return power and profits to our community.
Support Small Businesses As a member of Congress, I will invest in North Georgia’s communities by ending Trump’s tariffs and investing in renewable energy to decrease the cost of materials and energy while helping small business owners.
By breaking up monopolies, I will also create more opportunities for small businesses to flourish.
