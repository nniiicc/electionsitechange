Caitlyn Gegen For Congress Events Donate Volunteer About Caitlyn Gegen Caitlyn Gegen For Congress Georgia's 9th Congressional District Caitlyn Gegen is a proud "Double Dawg" holding a bachelor's and master's from the University of Georgia as well as a master's from the London School of Economics.
As a community organizer, she fought for women's rights and criminal justice reform.
She is running because she is committed to fighting for working families, protecting human rights, and building a stronger future for Georgia's 9th District.
