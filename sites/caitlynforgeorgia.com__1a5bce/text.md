Caitlyn Gegen For Congress Events Donate Volunteer About Caitlyn Gegen Caitlyn Gegen For Congress Georgia's 9th Congressional District Caitlyn Gegen is a proud "Double Dawg" holding a bachelor's and master's from the University of Georgia as well as a master's from the London School of Economics.
As a community organizer, she fought for women's rights and criminal justice reform.
She is running because she is committed to fighting for working families, protecting human rights, and building a stronger future for Georgia's 9th District.

Sign Up to Volunteer Values & Issues Solutions for northeast Georgia 9 Priorities Caitlyn Gegen stands for inclusion, fairness, and practical progress for all — explore all 18 of her policy priorities.
Affordability I am committed to fighting for a livable minimum wage, expanding the stock of affordable housing, and advocating for universal healthcare.
I will fight for a future where every hardworking person in Georgia’s 9th Congressional District can live a life of dignity, where they have extra savings at the end of the month and are confident they will be able to buy their home.
Universal Healthcare Nobody should be dying, in the wealthiest country in the world, because they are unable to access the medical care they need.
I will fight for Medicare for All, because it will save about $1 trillion in healthcare costs per year, and it will save more than 100,000 lives every year.
The Epstein Class Pays Their Fair Share The Epstein Class needs to pay their fair share in taxes, which they are not currently doing .
If the government properly taxed the Epstein Class, we could use the extra funds to pay for social programs that would benefit everyone.
Hold the President Accountable Congress needs to take its power back and reinstitute the system of checks and balances it was meant to contribute to.
I will fight to help Congress to take back its war and taxation powers.
