Insist on Stronger Gun Control Our public safety has been at risk for too long.
I believe every citizen has a right to feel safe anywhere they go, including school and church; I also believe responsible and law-abiding citizens have the right to purchase and use rifles and handguns for hunting or protection.
Assault weapons have no place in our communities.
Everyone deserves to feel safe and not fear the threat of another horrible and unnecessary mass shooting.
One more life lost is too many.
Educate, and Empower, the Next Generation Teachers in Minnesota schools are leaving the classroom and students continue to suffer learning loss post-pandemic.
Education is paramount to empowering the next generation of Minnesotans, but teachers cannot do it alone.
They need our support in children’s mental health and wellbeing, resourcing students with diverse learning needs, and keeping them safe in the classroom.
Preserve our Natural Resources Minnesota has stunning natural resources that it is important we preserve.
In my role, I am constantly reassessing the existing tension between economic development and environmental impacts, making bold choices to balance both.
I have advocated for infrastructure improvements to expand the electric grid and add electric charging stations across the state to reduce pollution.
Promote Local Businesses Small businesses are the backbone of our community, accounting for more than 95% of business in the state.
They are a key economic driver, employing thousands of community members and fostering a culture of innovation and growth for Minnesota.
Supporting these small businesses in their growth is paramount, including creating a favorable tax environment.
I initiated and led the effort to secure Plymouth’s first craft beer taproom, and I know it won’t be the last time I get to cut a giant ribbon.
Improve Transportation Even minor inconveniences when getting from place to place can cause trickle down effects on day-to-day life.
Prioritizing infrastructure improvements that reduce traffic congestion and elevate public transit will support healthier commutes for everyone.
Advocate for Better Health Care Health is deeply personal and important to protect.
Having worked as legal counsel for a nonprofit health insurance plan, I understand how hard it can be to get the services and treatment you need.
We cannot let Minnesotans suffer and skip vital treatments for fear of cost.
We can do better.
Improve the Criminal Justice System As a lawyer, I value fairness and justice.
I will always advocate for safety and support for citizens while also removing biases from sentencing decisions to eliminate disparity in our justice system.
This also includes investing in the police force and efficiency within the department.
