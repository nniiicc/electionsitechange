Office Services Meet Mark As a Pennsylvania state representative, Mark is committed to eliminating wasteful spending, reducing the size of state government, and continuing the fight to eliminate property taxes.
He currently serves as Chairman of the House Veterans Affairs and Emergency Preparedness Committee.
Prior to being elected, Mark worked as a Pennsylvania correctional officer, and an EMT with over 30 years of experience.
