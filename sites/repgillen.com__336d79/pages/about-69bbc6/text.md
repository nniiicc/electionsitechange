About Mark Gillen
State Representative Mark M.
Gillen began serving the people in the 128th Legislative District in 2011.
The district includes all or portions of the following municipalities: Amity, Birdsboro, Caernarvon, Cumru, Douglass, Exeter, New Morgan, Robeson and Union.
As a state representative, his top priorities are eliminating wasteful spending, reducing the size of state government and continuing the fight to eliminate property taxes.
Prior to being elected to the House, Mark was a Pennsylvania correctional officer and a former long-time Emergency Medical Technician.
Mark previously served as a Berks County jury commissioner and on Mohnton Borough Council.
He is a member of several civic organizations and clubs, including the Mohnton Fish and Game Protective Association and the Colonel Jacob Morgan Post 537 of the American Legion in Morgantown.
He graduated from Bucks County Community College and Bob Jones University and has a master’s degree from Kutztown University.
Mark currently serves as Chair of the House Veterans Affairs and Emergency Preparedness committee.
He and his wife, Kim, are blessed to have five children.
