Gillen to Host Dedication of PFC Travis C.
Zimmerman Memorial Bridge
July 2, 2026
WHAT: Rep.
Mark M.
Gillen (R-Berks) will host a ceremony to dedicate the PFC Travis C.
Zimmerman Memorial Bridge in Amity Township, Berks County.
The bridge designation was approved through Act 17 of 2025.
WHO: Rep.
Mark M.
Gillen; Rep.
David Maloney; Zimmerman family members, including Gail Camperson (mother), Betty and Lloyd Zimmerman (father); Pennsylvania National Guard Color Guard; and community members, veterans and invited guests.
WHEN: Tuesday, July 14, at noon.
WHERE: Bridge over Manatawny Creek near the intersection of PA Route 562 and Trumbore Lane, Amity Township, Berks County.
LIVE WEBSTREAMING: https://www.facebook.com/RepGillen/
Media contact: Will Jones, 717-260-6615, wjones@pahousegop.com
