About Cynthia
Cynthia Starke-Jones is a dedicated educator and community advocate who has spent over two decades serving the families of Georgia.
With a deep commitment to public service, she has been a tireless voice for affordable healthcare, strong public schools, and accessible housing.
As a former teacher, Cynthia understands firsthand the challenges facing our education system and is prepared to fight for common-sense solutions that put families first in the State House.
