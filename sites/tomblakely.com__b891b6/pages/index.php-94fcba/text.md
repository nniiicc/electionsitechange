The Bennington Banner, July 8
Here are my four rules for living.
So far these rules have worked well for me.
As your representative you can count on me to keep following them:
- Treat everyone fairly
- Always do my best
- Give back to the community
- Try to have fun
You can also sign up for my email newsletter on the Newsletters tab to see what I am up to, or send an email with questions or concerns.
I try to answer all emails I receive.
I will be announcing events in the newsletter, here and on my Facebook and Instagram pages.
Each will be an opportunity for you to get to know me better, and for me to meet you.
And please vote for me on Election Day.
I will be honored to serve you in the Vermont House of Representatives!
