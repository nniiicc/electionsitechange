The Bennington Banner, July 8
I'm not only running for office, I run.
The picture above is me crossing the finish line at the Battle Day 5K Road Race this year.
I try to run it every year, along with a couple of other races.
But mostly I just run.
You may see me heading down Elm Street towards downtown, or up to the Monument, or wherever the whim takes me.
If you do see me running around town, you may notice I have earbuds in.
Every wonder what I'm listening to?
Well, wonder no more!
I have pretty eclectic taste in music.
It ranges from pre-Renaissance dance music, to Classical, to 20th Century orchestral, to jazz, Rock, Metal, Grunge, Bluegrass, Blues and even a little Country and Folk.
Lately, I've been exploring Progressive Rock and especially Prog-Metal.
That would probably explain the ringing in my ears that isn't going to go away.
If you listen to the links here, keep the volume at a safe level.
And rock on!
Karnivool is a Prog-Metal band from Australia.
I like their music, and I really like their politics: fighting for the rights of Indigenous Peoples and against Facism.
Give a listen to All I Know and then ask yourself "Are you with me?
Are you of like mind?"
