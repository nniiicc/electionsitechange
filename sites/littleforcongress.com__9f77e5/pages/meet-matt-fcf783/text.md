Search site Submit search Menu Meet Matt Endorsements Key Issues About District 2 Get Involved For Media Yard Sign Donate Meet Matt Local guy I’m a local guy, born and raised in Lakeville, Minnesota.
I’ve lived in CD2 my whole life–I attended Parkview Elementary School (still a proud Panda!) and graduated from Rosemount High School (go Irish!).
I then received my BA in Political Science at the University of Minnesota-Morris (Cougars!) and my law degree from the University of Minnesota-Twin Cities (Ski-U-Mah!).
When I was a kid, I watched my Mom fight for years against our insurance company just to get the healthcare she needed.
I knew things should be different and that no one should have to fight just to receive care.
That experience pushed me to work hard, go to law school, and run for office.
Now, I own a law firm where I sue big insurance companies and fight for seniors and those with disabilities who have been neglected and abused.
Lakeville City Council After a year of teaching English abroad in Chile, I came home inspired to serve and was elected to the Lakeville City Council in 2010.
On the Council, I served on various committees and boards, including the Public Safety Committee, the Downtown Lakeville Business Association, the Municipal Legislative Coalition, and the Economic Development Commission.
Mayor of Lakeville In 2012, while still in law school, I ran for and was elected Mayor of Lakeville and was unopposed in my reelection in 2014.
Throughout my time in city hall, we focused on public safety, low taxes, job growth, and reducing long-term debt.
As Mayor, we built record amounts of housing and expanded the available housing types.
We stabilized property taxes and invested in public safety—I even offered to donate a portion of my salary to hire more support staff for the police department.
What was my proudest achievement as mayor?
Obviously, bringing Chipotle to my hometown!
Profile of Matt after being elected mayor at 27 Matt brings Chipotle to Lakeville State Senator In 2016, I was elected State Senator for District 58, representing Lakeville, Farmington, and southern Dakota County.
I focused on local support and balanced spending.
I authored and co-authored over 60 bipartisan bills, working with fellow legislators to support our communities and deliver the results working families needed.
I prevented cuts to veterans’ services, secured vital infrastructure funding for my district, and brought Metro Mobility to Lakeville so seniors and people with disabilities could access transportation.
I led the charge to ensure diabetics could afford the insulin they needed by passing the Alec Smith Insulin Affordability Act.
Big pharma pushed back against the bill, but I believe everyone should have access to healthcare– so I kept fighting until it was finally passed in 2020.
I’ve spent my career fighting for the underdogs, and I can’t wait to show up in Washington as a progressive fighter for CD2!
Small Business Owner & Family Time Since 2015, I’ve built my own law firm, fighting against big insurance companies for seniors and those with disabilities who have been hurt by negligence or abuse.
I live in New Market Township with my wife, Coco, my daughter, Poppy, three dogs, and a rabbit named Mochi.
In my rare free time, I enjoy running, basketball, snowboarding, hiking, spending time with my family, and recently, learning to play the banjo.
