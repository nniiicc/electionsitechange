Meet Congressional District 5
North Carolina’s 5th Congressional District starts out in the northwest portion of our state, where the beautiful Blue Ridge Mountains meet the foothills, and extends along the Virginia line until the Piedmont Triad, hooking down to scoop up Greensboro.
This district includes some of the most varied political terrain in the state and comprises the cities of Greensboro, Boone, Lenoir, Wilkesboro, Mt.
Airy, Reidsville, Sparta, Danbury, Jefferson, Taylorsville, Eden and Blowing Rock—alongside hundreds of other small communities where working North Carolinians are just looking for a fair shake.
Notable landmarks include Grandfather Mountain, the International Civil Rights Museum, Pisgah National Forest and portions of the Blue Ridge Parkway, Pilot Mountain, The Blowing Rock, amongst dozens of other historic sites.
This district also includes Appalachian State University, North Carolina A&T University, and the University of North Carolina at Greensboro.

Important Dates & Deadlines for Voting
| Sept. 6, 2024 | County boards of elections begin mailing absentee ballots to eligible voters who submitted an absentee ballot request form. |
| Oct. 11, 2024 | Voter registration deadline (5 p.m.).* |
| Oct. 17, 2024 | In-person early voting begins; same-day registration available. |
| Oct. 29, 2024 | Absentee ballot request deadline (5 p.m.).* |
| Nov. 2, 2024 | In-person early voting ends (3 p.m.). |
| Nov. 5, 2024 | General Election Day. |
| Nov. 5, 2024 | Absentee ballot return deadline (7:30 p.m.).* |
*Voter registration and absentee voting deadlines are different for military and overseas citizen voters.
