This district includes some of the most varied political terrain in the state and comprises the cities of Greensboro, Boone, Lenoir, Wilkesboro, Mt.
Airy, Reidsville, Sparta, Danbury, Jefferson, Taylorsville, Eden and Blowing Rock—alongside hundreds of other small communities where working North Carolinians are just looking for a fair shake.
Notable landmarks include Grandfather Mountain, the International Civil Rights Museum, Pisgah National Forest and portions of the Blue Ridge Parkway, Pilot Mountain, The Blowing Rock, amongst dozens of other historic sites.
This district also includes Appalachian State University, North Carolina A&T University, and the University of North Carolina at Greensboro.
NC-05 Fast Facts NC-05 is home to a total of six universities and three community colleges, as well as the Elon University School of Law.
Altogether, that adds up to more than 63,000 students!
Eight of NC-05’s ten counties are considered part of Appalachia according to the Appalachian Regional Commission.
NC-05 is rapidly growing and becoming more diverse as more North Carolinians of all kinds fall in love with the High Country, the Foothills, and the WNC way of life.
