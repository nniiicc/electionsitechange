| | I am running for North Dakota House from District 7 in the DEM-NPL Party, because I love our community and state and believe that we can make it more affordable and welcoming for all people.
I was endorsed as a candidate for ND House by the people of District 7 at the DEM-NPL Region 6 Convention on February 7, 2026.
Since February 7th, I have been consistently posting on social media about issues that effect the lives of the people of District 7 and our state.
I have heard the concerns of many people, and plan to walk our entire District in the coming months to make sure I hear the voice and concern of every person in our community. |
I believe the first step to improving the lives of the people in our community is to listen to their concerns, ask tough questions of our leaders, and advocate for a new way forward that will improve our lives while being fiscally responsible and sustainable for the long-term.
We can work together to make a better community for our children and ourselves in Bismarck and across North Dakota.
I moved to Bismarck in the fall of 2020 during the heart of the pandemic to work at Bismarck State College.
I have found my place and a home in this community, starting a thriving nonprofit music and performing arts organization called Dakota Pro Musica, and serve a number of non-profit organizations in Bismarck and the state as a board member.
I am the president of the Capital Curling Club board and the Standing Committee of the Episcopal Church of North Dakota.
