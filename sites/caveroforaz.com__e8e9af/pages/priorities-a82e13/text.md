DONATE PRIORITIES MY COMMITMENT Arizona's future depends on thoughtful leadership, responsible economic growth, and putting people before politics.
As your State Representative, I've worked every day to protect our freedoms, strengthen our economy, invest in our schools, support working families, and prepare Arizona for the challenges and opportunities ahead.
Together, we can continue building an Arizona that is more affordable, more innovative, more sustainable, and full of opportunity for every family.
A GOOD LIFE YOU CAN AFFORD.
Every Arizonan who works hard should be able to earn a living that supports their family, afford a safe place to call home, and build a secure future.
I will fight to strengthen our economy by raising wages, protecting workers' rights, supporting small businesses, expanding workforce opportunities, and attracting employers that provide family-sustaining jobs with good benefits.
Housing affordability is equally essential.
As the former Executive Director of the Arizona Fair Housing Center, I have seen firsthand how rising home prices, increasing rents, limited housing supply, and discriminatory housing practices have made it harder for families to thrive.
I support increasing the supply of affordable housing, protecting tenants and homeowners from discrimination, investing in homelessness prevention, and streamlining responsible housing development to reduce unnecessary delays.
By partnering with communities, businesses, and local governments, we can create an Arizona where hard work is rewarded, housing is within reach, and every family has the opportunity to achieve lasting economic security.
HARNESS INNOVATION & PROTECT ARIZONA.
Arizona is quickly becoming a national leader in artificial intelligence, semiconductor manufacturing, advanced technology, and data center development.
These industries can create high-quality jobs, attract new investment, and strengthen our economy—but innovation must be guided by accountability.
We should embrace emerging technologies while protecting Arizona's water, energy resources, taxpayers, and communities.
As your State Representative, I have been at the forefront of responsible technology policy.
In 2025, I introduced Arizona's first legislation to reform the state's data center tax incentive program, helping spark a statewide conversation about transparency, water consumption, energy demand, and taxpayer accountability.
That effort contributed to Arizona's three-year pause on new data center tax incentives, creating an opportunity to establish stronger standards that balance economic development with responsible resource management.
Artificial intelligence is reshaping healthcare, education, public safety, manufacturing, and government.
I believe Arizona should lead by adopting smart policies that encourage innovation while protecting privacy, strengthening cybersecurity, promoting ethical AI, and preparing our workforce for the jobs of the future.
As a member of the House Commerce and Science & Technology Committees, I will continue fighting for responsible AI, transparent government procurement, investments in STEM education and workforce training, and technology policies that ensure innovation benefits every Arizonan—not just a select few.
PROTECTING WORKERS LIVING WAGES.
As the daughter of a union family, I know that strong unions are the backbone of Arizona's middle class.
They have helped generations of workers earn fair wages, secure quality healthcare, retire with dignity, and provide better opportunities for their families.
I will always stand with working people by protecting the freedom to organize, supporting collective bargaining, defending prevailing wage standards, and opposing efforts that weaken workers' rights.
At the same time, we must prepare Arizona's workforce for a rapidly changing economy by investing in registered apprenticeships, career and technical education, workforce training, and partnerships between labor, educational institutions, and employers.
I believe Arizona should attract businesses that create high-quality, family-sustaining jobs while ensuring workers share in our state's economic success.
By strengthening organized labor and expanding pathways to good-paying careers, we can grow our economy, reduce inequality, and build a stronger future where every hardworking Arizonan has the opportunity to succeed.
STOP HATE.
I believe every Arizonan deserves equal opportunity, dignity, and protection under the law.
I will continue fighting to advance policies that protect civil rights, promote equal treatment, and eliminate discrimination based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or identity.
Arizona must confront systemic inequities while rejecting policies that encourage racial profiling, discrimination, or division.
I opposed measures like HCR 2060 because they risk repeating the costly mistakes of the past, undermining civil liberties, separating families, and exposing taxpayers to unnecessary legal costs.
Our state is strongest when every community is respected, protected, and empowered to succeed.
KEEPING FAMILIES TOGETHER.
Arizona's immigration policies should reflect our values of fairness, dignity, and public safety.
I believe families belong together and that every person deserves to be treated with respect and due process.
Public safety is strongest when residents trust law enforcement enough to report crimes, cooperate with investigations, and seek help without fear.
That is why I oppose policies that encourage racial profiling, separate families, or divert local law enforcement from addressing violent crime and other serious threats.
Instead, Arizona should invest in practical solutions that strengthen communities, including access to legal services, education, healthcare, workforce opportunities, and an earned pathway to citizenship.
We must focus law enforcement resources on combating violent crime, drug trafficking, and human trafficking while supporting community policing, victim services, and mental health programs.
By protecting civil rights, keeping families together, and building trust between communities and law enforcement, we can create a safer, stronger, and more welcoming Arizona for everyone.
PURE WATER & CLEAN AIR.
Arizona's future depends on protecting the natural resources that sustain our families, communities, and economy.
Water is our most valuable resource, and we must ensure responsible management as our state continues to grow.
I believe economic development and environmental stewardship can go hand in hand.
That means holding large water and energy users accountable, investing in water conservation, groundwater protection, recycling, and modern infrastructure, while expanding clean and reliable energy solutions.
We must also safeguard clean air, protect ratepayers and taxpayers, and ensure that future generations inherit a resilient Arizona.
I will continue advocating for transparent, sustainable policies that encourage responsible growth without compromising our water supply or quality of life.
By planning ahead and making smart investments today, we can strengthen our economy, preserve our environment, and ensure Arizona remains a great place to live, work, and raise a family for generations to come.
EDUCATION IS THE MOST POWERFUL TOOL.
As a mother of two daughters in primary school, I advocate for legislative action to halt the recurring assaults on public education.
We urgently need increased funding for daily maintenance, higher teacher and staff salaries, and enhanced capital funding to ensure every student has access to essential resources like textbooks and technology.
With the surge in mental health challenges exacerbated by the pandemic, it's imperative to boost education revenue.
I wholeheartedly endorse the idea of tuition-free community college for all Arizona residents and support the continuation of initiatives like the Arizona Teachers Academy to alleviate the financial burden on aspiring educators.
In 2022, 88.2% of school-age children attended public schools in the US, with even higher rates in Tucson (89.1%) and Arizona overall (90.2%).
With over 90% of Phoenix students enrolled in public schools, it's crucial to allocate funds effectively to support students, teachers, and schools.
Collaboration with state and local education authorities is essential to safely maintain open schools.
ESA SCAM.
Every child deserves access to a high-quality education, but Arizona's universal Empowerment Scholarship Account (ESA) program must be transparent, accountable, and fiscally responsible.
The rapid expansion of the program has diverted more than $1 billion from the state budget while providing limited oversight, creating significant financial pressure that threatens long-term investments in public education and other essential services.
I support strengthening accountability through rigorous financial audits, stronger fraud prevention, meaningful academic reporting, and greater transparency in how taxpayer dollars are spent.
We must ensure education funding is used effectively and delivers measurable results for Arizona students.
I will continue fighting to protect neighborhood public schools, responsibly manage taxpayer resources, and reform the ESA program so it serves students—not special interests.
Arizona can support educational opportunity while maintaining fiscal responsibility, transparency, and a strong public education system that benefits every family.
PROTECTING REPRODUCTIVE RIGHTS.
Support statutory and constitutional protections for reproductive freedom, including coverage of abortion services for those who depend on the government for their healthcare or coverage-including but not limited to Medicaid and Medicare beneficiaries, US service members and veterans.
Essential to prioritize policies that promote reproductive health equity, ensuring that all individuals, regardless of socioeconomic status or geographic location, can access the care they need.
The decisions should always be between the individual, their physician and healthcare professionals.
I strongly oppose “fake” health centers.
Comprehensive sex education that is age-appropriate, medically accurate, culturally appropriate, inclusive, and evidence-based.
Access to and coverage of comprehensive and holistic care throughout pregnancy and the postpartum period.
Support proposals to address rising maternal mortality and morbidity, including long standing health inequities that disproportionately harm Black and Native American communities.
HEALTH CARE IS A HUMAN RIGHT.
Just as I fought for every American veteran to have access to healthcare worldwide, I will continue the fight for every Arizonan to have quality, accessible and affordable health care.
I will work to protect reproductive rights, children's health care, and expand funding and access for behavioral and mental health.
Arizonan families should have healthcare coverage despite legal or financial barriers.
ELECTIONS BELONG TO THE PEOPLE.
Every eligible Arizonan deserves the freedom to vote easily, securely, and with confidence that their ballot will be counted.
I will continue fighting against efforts that make it harder for eligible voters to participate in our democracy while supporting policies that strengthen both election access and election integrity.
That includes protecting Arizona's popular Vote-by-Mail system, expanding early voting opportunities, supporting same-day voter registration with appropriate verification requirements, and ensuring election officials have the resources they need to administer safe, secure, and transparent elections.
Democracy works best when every eligible voter can make their voice heard, and I will oppose policies that create unnecessary barriers to participation or undermine public confidence in our elections.
Protecting the right to vote is fundamental to ensuring every Arizonan has a voice in shaping our state's future.
FIGHT FOR UNIVERSAL CHILDCARE.
No parent should have to choose between earning a paycheck and ensuring their child has a safe, nurturing place to learn and grow.
Childcare is not a luxury—it is essential infrastructure that strengthens families, supports our workforce, and prepares the next generation for success.
I believe Arizona should make the bold commitment to universal childcare, ensuring that every family has access to affordable, high-quality early childhood education regardless of income or ZIP code.
States like New Mexico have demonstrated that investing in universal childcare can increase workforce participation, improve educational outcomes, reduce childhood poverty, and strengthen local economies.
Arizona should learn from these successful models and build a childcare system that works for every family.
As a working mother of two daughters, I understand firsthand how difficult it can be to balance work and family responsibilities.
Families across Legislative District 11 are already stretched thin by rising housing costs, groceries, healthcare, and transportation.
Childcare costs should never be the reason a parent is forced to leave the workforce or postpone career opportunities.
During my time in the Legislature, I have supported investments that expanded childcare assistance, increased funding for after-school and summer programs, and helped more working families access quality care.
While these investments are important, they are only the beginning.
Arizona must move beyond temporary solutions and build a comprehensive, universal childcare system that supports children from birth through school age.
As your State Representative, I will continue fighting to: Establish a pathway toward universal childcare so every Arizona family can access affordable, high-quality care.
Establish a pathway toward universal childcare so every Arizona family can access affordable, high-quality care.
Increase wages, benefits, professional training, and career pathways for childcare professionals to strengthen and grow the workforce.
Expand early childhood education, after-school, and summer learning opportunities that help children thrive.
Support culturally responsive, inclusive, and high-quality childcare options that meet the needs of Arizona's diverse communities.
Partner with parents, childcare providers, educators, businesses, labor organizations, and community leaders to develop sustainable, long-term childcare solutions.
Universal childcare is an investment in Arizona's future.
It helps children succeed, allows parents to pursue careers, strengthens our economy, and creates healthier, more resilient communities.
I will continue working to make Arizona a state where every child has the opportunity to thrive and every family has the support they deserve.
CRIMINAL JUSTICE SYSTEM IS OUTDATED, FLAWED AND A TAXPAYER BURDEN.
Public safety and criminal justice reform are not competing priorities—they go hand in hand.
We can hold people accountable while building a justice system that is fair, effective, and focused on reducing crime, supporting victims, and preventing people from reoffending.
Arizona continues to spend billions on our criminal justice system while facing high incarceration rates and persistent challenges with recidivism, behavioral health, and substance use disorders.
We should invest in proven, evidence-based solutions that improve public safety, strengthen communities, and use taxpayer dollars wisely.
That includes expanding diversion programs, mental health and addiction treatment, educational and job training opportunities, and reentry services that help individuals successfully return to their communities.
Our schools should be places where students feel safe, supported, and ready to learn.
School Resource Officers play an important role in protecting students during emergencies, but they should not replace counselors, social workers, or mental health professionals, nor should routine school discipline unnecessarily funnel students into the criminal justice system.
Every child deserves access to supportive learning environments that help them succeed.
As your State Representative, I will continue advocating for smart, data-driven reforms that strengthen public safety while promoting fairness and accountability, including: Expanding diversion, behavioral health, and treatment programs for nonviolent offenses.
Supporting rehabilitation, education, workforce training, and reentry programs that reduce recidivism.
Ensuring School Resource Officers are focused on campus safety while increasing investments in counselors, social workers, and student mental health services.
Reviewing mandatory sentencing policies to ensure they are fair, effective, and proportionate.
Expanding earned-release opportunities for individuals who demonstrate rehabilitation and pose no threat to public safety.
Protecting youth by promoting age-appropriate services and rehabilitation rather than unnecessarily exposing them to the adult criminal justice system. ​ Supporting evidence-based policies for substance use disorders and continuing to evaluate criminal penalties to ensure they reflect current science and public safety priorities. ​Improving oversight, transparency, and accountability throughout Arizona's criminal justice system.
A safer Arizona is built not only through effective law enforcement, but also through prevention, rehabilitation, opportunity, and accountability.
By investing in people, supporting victims, and using evidence-based policies, we can create stronger communities, reduce crime, and build a justice system that works for everyone.
