About the Legislative Campaign
Our campaign is dedicated to building a brighter, more equitable future for our community.
We are seeking motivated, creative, and detail-oriented interns to support our communications team during this election cycle.
This role is ideal for students or early-career professionals interested in political communications, digital strategy, and hands-on campaign experience.
Responsibilities
As a Social Media & Editing Intern, you will:
- Support daily content creation across platforms (Instagram, TikTok, X/Twitter, Facebook, Threads, etc.)
- Edit short-form video content, including reels, clips, and rapid-response pieces
- Assist with copywriting for posts, ads, graphics, and storytelling content
- Manage social media scheduling and monitor engagement trends
- Help brainstorm digital concepts, messaging themes, and creative strategies
- Edit campaign materials such as newsletters, press releases, talking points, and website content
- Track social media analytics and suggest optimization improvements
- Rapid-response editing: turn around content quickly to respond to news cycles
- Contribute to digital organizing initiatives and online outreach
Qualifications
- Strong writing and editing skills with excellent attention to detail
- Proficiency with social media platforms and trending content styles
- Basic experience with video editing tools (CapCut, Premiere Pro, Final Cut, Canva, etc.)
- Ability to multitask and work well in fast-paced environments
- Creative mindset and willingness to pitch new ideas
- Interest in politics, community organizing, or public service
- Reliable, self-motivated, and able to meet deadlines
What You’ll Gain
- Hands-on campaign experience and a front-row seat to election strategy
- Portfolio-ready digital content and writing samples
- Mentorship from experienced campaign staff
- Networking opportunities across the political and communications fields
- Real-world experience in political storytelling, editing, and digital outreach
