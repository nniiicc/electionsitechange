PRIORITIES
A direct correlation exists between property values and quality public education.
Any attempts to defund our public schools by supporting vouchers for private schools and home schoolers weaken NH public education and will have a negative impact on NH communities.
Labor unions helped build a middle class in America, with workers collectively bargaining for better wages, benefits, and working conditions.
Right-to-Work and ‘transparent’ collective bargaining are designed to undermine unions and workers' rights, and they are wrong for NH.
As State Rep, Muriel will continue working for the next generation of New Hampshire.
In addition to being a strong voice for public education and labor, she will advocate for women and families, environmental justice, LGBTQ rights, expanding access to affordable and quality healthcare, childcare, and housing--these and other issues that will provide Granite Staters with greater opportunities and better lives.
Muriel believes that when we work together for the common good, our future is brighter.
