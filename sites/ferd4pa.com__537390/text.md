I'm Jeremy Ferderber and I am running for election to the Pennsylvania House of Representatives to represent District 28, which includes Bradford Woods, Marshall, Pine, Richland, West Deer, and (part of) Hampton Township.
The general election is on November 3rd, 2026.
My Values & Vision
As a father, a business leader, and an involved member of this community, I am running to protect our children's rights so that our daughters can have the same rights their mothers and grandmothers had, to enact realistic measures to keep our families safe, and to ensure our district remains a place where hard work is rewarded and communities thrive, regardless of skin color, accent, or political affiliation.
The future of Pennsylvania starts in our own backyard—and I am ready to serve.
Resilience In Resistance
What we are witnessing on a national scale currently is an unprecedented phenomenon that seems to have no guard rails nor end in sight.
Like most, I’ve grown sick and tired of being sick and tired about the current state of things, and rather than grow increasingly frustrated, I would like to be a part of the solution.
As the national political landscape seems to be crumbling around us, the importance of legislation at the state level has perhaps never been more relevant.
I want to safeguard the future that I will be leaving my children, and I want to ensure that the rights we have as Pennsylvanians are maintained and strengthened for generations to come.
Level-Headed Discourse
To live in a truly purple district and to be surrounded by folks on both sides of the aisle, it is evident that most of us see eye-to-eye on most things, albeit with some very basic fundamental differences.
I still think that there is hope to bring some level of civility back to the socio-political landscape, and there’s no better place to start that than at the local level.
We need a representative who will work to bring everyone together, while simultaneously speaking up against blatant injustice when it impacts our community.
Accountable Leadership
Restoring trust requires accountability.
Leaders must be willing to speak up when our community is affected—whether it’s addressing ICE raids in our own backyard, responding to threats against local officials, or ensuring that all residents feel protected and respected.
We need a representative who brings people together while also standing firm against injustice when it impacts our district.
My goal is to serve this community with honesty, steadiness, and a commitment to listening—because that’s how we move forward together.
Common-Sense Legislation
In our current day and age, it feels like things have flown off the rails.
It's time to reintroduce common sense and practicality back into politics.
Let's work together to find the obvious solutions to problems that we all face on both sides of the aisle.
