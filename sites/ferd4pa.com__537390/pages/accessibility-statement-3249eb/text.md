What web accessibility is
Web accessibility means that all visitors, including those with disabilities, can navigate our site with ease and enjoy the same experience as others.
We aim to achieve this through best practices and assistive technologies.
Accessibility adjustments on this site
Our website has been developed in accordance with WCAG 2.1 guidelines, ensuring accessibility at the AA level.
We have implemented features that accommodate assistive technologies, such as screen readers and keyboard navigation.
Our efforts include:
Utilizing accessibility tools to identify and rectify potential issues
Setting the primary language of the site
Organizing the content in a logical order
Creating clear heading structures across all pages
Providing alternative text for images
Ensuring color combinations meet the required contrast standards
Minimizing motion effects on the site
Making all multimedia content accessible
