Home About Issues Constituent Services Contact Follow Follow Follow Donate Home About Issues Constituent Services Contact Issues Election Integrity The chaos and confusion of the 2020 election underscored just how essential it is to protect the integrity of our elections.
Georgians deserve elections that are secure, transparent, and verifiable-and leaders who will show up, do the work, and stand firm when it matters.
I will fight for hand-marked paper ballots, strong chain-of-custody safeguards, and a process the public can trust.
That also means ending reliance on electronic voting systems and withdrawing from voter data-sharing organizations such as ERIC.
We cannot afford timid legislators who walk out when voters are counting on us to defend lawful elections-every time.
End the Woke Agenda Government and taxpayer-funded institutions should focus on core missions-public safety, education that prioritizes learning, and services delivered fairly and efficiently-not ideological activism.
I will push back on politically driven agendas that distract from results, undermine common sense, and erode community standards.
Georgia needs leadership that puts families, freedom, and fundamentals first.
Parental Rights and Protecting Children Parents-not bureaucrats-are the primary decision-makers for their children.
I will fight to safeguard parental rights and protect kids from irreversible, life-altering decisions pushed by activists and government systems.
That includes banning puberty blockers for minors and ensuring parents have full access to their children’s medical records, including school-related and provider-related documentation.
Families deserve transparency, consent, and the ability to advocate for their children without interference.
Tax Relief and Eliminating the State Income Tax Georgia families are working hard and feeling the pressure of rising costs.
I support bold, responsible tax relief-working toward the elimination of Georgia’s state income tax.
Government should live within its means, prioritize essentials, and return more money to the people who earned it.
Defend the 2nd Amendment The Second Amendment is non-negotiable.
I will continue fighting for strong Second Amendment protections and for the rights of law-abiding Georgians to defend themselves and their families.
I will oppose any effort to weaken constitutional freedoms under the guise of “safety.” Medical Freedom Medical decisions should be made by patients and their doctors-not politicians, bureaucrats, or corporate gatekeepers.
I will continue efforts on medical freedom, including policies that protect informed consent and preserve patient choice.
I also support making ivermectin available over the counter, so Georgians can access lawful medications without unnecessary barriers.
Protect the Unborn Human life-from conception to natural death-is a blessing given by God.
Every life has value, and I will fight to protect our most vulnerable.
Georgia should continue to be a state that defends life and supports mothers, families, and strong communities.
