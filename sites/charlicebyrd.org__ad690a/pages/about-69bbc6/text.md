Home About Issues Constituent Services Contact Follow Follow Follow Donate Home About Issues Constituent Services Contact About Charlice Charlice Byrd has been a conservative activist and community leader for years, donating her time and resources to advance limited, constitutional government and resources to conservative causes and candidates throughout the country and right here in our community.
In 2015, Charlice was recruited to join the paid staff of Donald J.
Trump for President.
Charlice has represented our community in the State House for over a decade.
She is a proven and outspoken conservative voice and an award-winning effective legislative leader.
Charlice played a key role in helping the Republican Party secure their first legislative majority in Georgia’s history since Reconstruction.
She now chairs the Georgia State House Freedom Caucus.
Charlice is a graduate of Southeastern Louisiana University where she earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in Elementary Education and spent the early portion of her career as a classroom educator.
Charlice and her husband Michael are longtime residents of Woodstock and members of First Baptist Church of Woodstock.
