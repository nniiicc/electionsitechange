Lowering Your Utility Bills
CenterPoint Energy, a Texas company, raised rates on District 78 families, and the Indiana Office of Utility Consumer Counselor found the increase went beyond what was actually justified.
When the legislature had the chance to rein that in, eleven separate consumer protection amendments to HB1002 came up for a vote.
Our representative voted no on every single one.
Sally will vote yes.
Fully Funded Local Schools
Sally taught middle school for fifteen years before she started training the next generation of teachers.
She knows what a classroom needs to work, and what it looks like when the money isn't there.
Indiana's voucher program just expanded again, with income caps removed and more than $190 million in new costs projected over the next two years, on top of what the state already spends.
Sally believes every one of those dollars should follow a kid into a classroom that has to take them.
