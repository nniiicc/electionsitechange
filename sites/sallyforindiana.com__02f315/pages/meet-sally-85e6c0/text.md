Meet Sally Busby: fifteen years a middle school teacher, now training the next generation of educators at the University of Evansville.

Sally Busby spent fifteen years teaching middle school before she started training the next generation of teachers at the University of Evansville.
She grew up in Memphis, and she came to District 78 when her husband's job brought them here.
She's spent her career showing up for kids, and she's spending this campaign the same way: on your porch, listening.
Join Team Sally Read Her Priorities Rooted in District 78 A career dedicated to public schools and community service A Career Built on Showing Up Rooted in District 78, Rooted in Public Education From the classroom to the kitchen table, Sally Busby's life has been shaped by the same instinct: pay attention, and do something about what you see. ★ Educator & Parent What Fifteen Years in the Classroom Taught Her Sally spent fifteen years teaching middle schoolers, and she's raising her own kids in District 78 now too.
She's seen firsthand how decisions made in Indianapolis end up on your kitchen table: a utility rate hike that eats into the grocery budget, a school funding formula that means bigger class sizes and fewer resources.
She doesn't need a briefing to understand what that costs a family.
She's lived it, and she's taught through it. ★ Special Needs Advocacy She's Navigated the System Herself Sally is the mother of two grown children who taught her, early on, what it takes to get a kid what they need.
Her son, now 22, has significant special needs and lives in a group home, a placement she researched, fought for, and helped build, one appointment and one form at a time.
Her daughter grew up managing dyslexia; this fall, she heads to Purdue.
Sally's doctorate in literacy research grew directly out of watching her own daughter learn differently.
It's not just a credential; it's where her advocacy started.
She knows exactly what it costs, in time, paperwork, and persistence, to get a kid what they're owed.
That's the fight she wants to keep having in Indianapolis. ★ Her Focus Show Up, Follow the Data, Get It Done Sally believes District 78 deserves a representative who's actually here, not just during election season.
On utilities, that means holding companies like CenterPoint accountable for rate hikes that don't add up, and supporting local energy options that keep more control (and more savings) with Hoosier communities.
