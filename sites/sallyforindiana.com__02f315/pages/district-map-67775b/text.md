Counties & Communities in District 78
District 78 represents working families across residential neighborhoods, suburban corridors, and Hoosier towns in Southwest Indiana.
Vanderburgh County
Includes eastern and northern portions of Vanderburgh County, encompassing parts of Evansville, residential neighborhoods, and growing suburban corridors around McCutchanville.
Warrick County
Extends into Warrick County, including the Ohio River community of Newburgh alongside family neighborhoods in Boonville, Chandler, and Elberfeld.
