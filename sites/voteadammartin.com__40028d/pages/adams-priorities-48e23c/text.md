Quality of Life District 72 is a diverse community, but not everyone experiences the same quality of life.
As your next state representative, I will work with local and state leaders to make sure every resident has the opportunity to live well and thrive.
Economy & Jobs Unless you work downtown, job opportunities can be limited.
As a successful business man, Adam will be focused on championing business-friendly policies and legislation that promotes economic growth and opportunities.
Taxes Lowering taxes means putting more money back into your pocket and making it easier for families to get ahead.
I support responsibly cutting the state income tax while protecting essential services like schools, public safety, and infrastructure.
We can do this by simplifying the tax code, reducing wasteful spending, and making government more efficient so taxpayers get better value without higher costs.
My goal is a fair, straightforward system that supports growth, attracts jobs, and helps every resident of District 72 thrive.
Education Adam wants to champion legislation that moves Oklahoma up from being ranked last in the nation for education.
He believes teachers play a critical role in preparing students to succeed and contribute to their communities.
Adam supports making Oklahoma a regional leader in teacher pay and creating incentives, like tax credits and tuition reimbursement, to attract and retain great educators.
By supporting our teachers, we can keep the best in our classrooms and build a stronger future for our students and our state.
Business Adam is a small business owner who understands the challenges of starting, running, and growing a business.
He believes Oklahoma should be a place where entrepreneurs and job creators can succeed without unnecessary barriers.
Adam will support policies that keep taxes low, reduce burdensome regulations, and make it easier to start and expand a business.
By creating a more business-friendly environment, he aims to attract new investment, support local businesses, and bring quality jobs to District 72.
Infrastructure Adam believes we must invest in our infrastructure before expecting new business growth.
Strong roads, utilities, and public services create the foundation for a thriving economy.
Adam knows that smart infrastructure investments attract jobs and support local communities.
As your state representative, he will work to strengthen District 72 by improving key transportation routes and making the most of our location to drive growth across the region.
Healthcare Adam believes every Oklahoman deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare.
Too many families face high costs and limited access.
As your next state representative, Adam will work to expand access, support local clinics, strengthen the healthcare workforce, and improve mental health services.
He is committed to lowering costs and building a healthier Oklahoma for every family.
Foreign Land Ownership Adam believes Oklahoma land should stay in the hands of those who live and work here.
He supports strengthening protections on foreign land ownership to safeguard our farms, resources, and national security.
As your next state representative, Adam will work to increase transparency, close loopholes, and ensure Oklahoma families and communities always come first.
