about
Zakir Choudhury, CPA, has built a career on integrity, public trust, and financial expertise.
Zakir Choudhury is a first-generation American, a licensed CPA, a parent, and a dedicated advocate for fairness, opportunity, and community-centered leadership.
As both a financial professional and a community organizer, Zakir has seen firsthand the struggles everyday New Yorkers face.
He understands how rising costs, limited resources, and declining public safety impact working families, immigrants, youth, women, and seniors.
These experiences drive his commitment to addressing the most urgent issues in our neighborhoods: strengthening public safety, making housing and healthcare more affordable, expanding opportunities for youth and women, improving education and job creation, supporting senior centers, and helping small businesses grow.

our manifesto
Public Safety
Every family deserves to feel safe at home and in their neighborhood.
I am committed to proven strategies that reduce crime, build trust, and ensure our communities thrive.
My Priorities:
- Expand community-based policing that strengthens neighborhood relationships
- Support violence-interruption and prevention programs for at-risk youth
- Improve lighting, security cameras, and safe corridors around schools and transit
- Create stronger partnerships between law enforcement and community groups
- Invest in mental-health response teams and crisis intervention services
Affordability
Housing and healthcare are basic human rights.
I will work tirelessly to lower costs and ensure every resident has access to the stability and care they need.
Affordable Housing:
- Increase the supply of affordable and supportive housing
- Strengthen tenant protections and prevent wrongful evictions
- Secure funding for critical repairs in public and rent-regulated housing
- Encourage responsible development that reflects community needs
Affordable Healthcare:
- Expand access to local clinics and urgent care services
- Lower prescription drug costs
- Improve mental health and addiction treatment resources
- Provide support for caregivers and families
Youth & Women Empowerment
Our community’s future depends on empowering youth and providing women with the resources and opportunities they deserve.
Youth Opportunities:
- More after-school programs in arts, sports, STEM, and trades
- Paid internships, apprenticeships, and mentorship programs
- Expanded youth centers and safe spaces
- Mental health and violence-prevention initiatives
Women’s Opportunities:
- Support and training for women entrepreneurs
- Enforce equal pay and workplace protections
- Expand access to childcare and maternal healthcare
- Leadership development and workforce training programs
Education & Jobs
Quality education and strong job opportunities are the foundation of a thriving community.
I will fight for schools, teachers, and economic programs that uplift every resident.
Education Improvements:
- Fully fund public schools and early childhood education
- Modernize classrooms with updated technology and facilities
- Provide more support for teachers, paraprofessionals, and staff
- Expand tutoring, special education, and enrichment programs
Driving Job Creation:
- Invest in workforce training for high-demand and emerging industries
- Partner with local employers to create career pipelines
- Support union jobs and fair wages
- Expand green-energy, infrastructure, and community-development projects
Senior Center
Our seniors built the foundation of our community.
They deserve respect, support, and access to services that help them live full, healthy lives.
My Commitments:
- Increase funding for senior centers, wellness programs, and transportation
- Expand access to healthcare and reduce prescription drug costs
- Strengthen home-care services so seniors can age in place
- Invest in social programs that reduce isolation
- Ensure safe, affordable, and accessible housing options
Small Businesses Growth
Small businesses keep our neighborhoods vibrant and strong.
I will work to create an environment where entrepreneurs can grow, hire locally, and succeed.
Supporting Local Businesses:
- Provide grants, low-interest loans, and technical assistance
- Reduce red tape and streamline the permitting process
- Support street vendors and local entrepreneurs
- Revitalize storefronts and improve commercial corridors
- Build partnerships with BIDs, chambers of commerce, and community organizations
