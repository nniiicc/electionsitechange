Why I’m Running
As a Certified Public Accountant, I have seen firsthand how responsible planning, strong oversight, and transparent budgeting can transform organizations and strengthen communities.
Our district deserves a representative who not only understands the numbers, but understands how to use them to deliver real results for the people.
I am running for the New York State Assembly to bring practical experience, financial discipline, and a steady, community-focused approach to Albany.
My goal is to ensure that every tax dollar is invested wisely, that state resources are allocated fairly, and that our government remains accountable to the residents it serves.
With a commitment to integrity, equity, and measurable progress, I want to help build a state government that works efficiently, listens to its people, and delivers meaningful improvements in the daily lives of families across District 87.
