Government Accountability Nevada belongs to the people who live and work here.
Tighe will challenge corporate influence, demand transparent spending, and hold political insiders accountable.
Your tax dollars should serve your community—not bankroll special deals for the well-connected.
Tighe will introduce legislation to ban corporate spending in Nevada politics and audit state spending.
Fixing Education Parents should choose the education that fits their child, and teachers should have the freedom to teach.
As a CCSD special education teacher, Tighe supports expanding school choice while cutting administrative bloat and putting resources into public school classrooms.
Decisions belong closer to students—with families, educators, and local School Organizational Teams.
Tighe will introduce legislation to expand school choice and give parents and teachers greater control over their schools.
Affordability Working Nevadans deserve the freedom to earn a living, build a business, afford a home, and access healthcare without unnecessary government interference.
Tighe supports ending corporate welfare, cutting burdensome regulations, reforming zoning and permitting laws to lower housing costs, and keeping Nevada income-tax free.
As a union member (CCEA), Tighe defends workers' freedom to organize, strike, and collectively bargain for better wages and working conditions.
He supports healthcare price transparency, expanding direct-pay options, and giving patients greater control over their care.
Tighe also opposes government-protected monopolies, particularly NV Energy, and supports opening Nevada's electricity market to competition.
Nevada's economy should serve working families and small businesses—not politically connected corporations.
Tighe will introduce a bill to break up NV Energy and the state government's toxic relationship.
Real Criminal Justice Reform Keep Nevadans safe while protecting their freedom.
Tighe supports decriminalizing victimless conduct, ending civil asset forfeiture, and focusing police resources on violent crime.
Government officials must answer for violating your rights, and people leaving prison deserve a real chance to rebuild their lives.
Tighe also opposes the public use of widespread license plate readers such as Flock or other kinds of illegal and unconstitutional surveillance.
Tighe will introduce legislation to release nonviolent prisoners from Nevada's Department of Corrections.
Defending the Guard Nevada’s Guard members should not be sent into Washington’s undeclared wars.
Tighe supports defending our communities against federal overreach and protecting your freedom to speak, protest, and boycott—including boycotts of foreign governments.
Nevadans should never be punished for peaceful political dissent.
Tighe will introduce Defend the Guard legislation to prevent Nevada Guard deployments into foreign hostilities without a congressional declaration of war.
Protecting Nevada's Land Tighe supports returning federally controlled lands to state, local, and tribal authorities.
Tighe opposes renewed nuclear testing, the Yucca Mountain nuclear waste repository, and the Thacker Pass lithium mine.
In District 21, he will fight to preserve Pittman Wash as a vital flood-control corridor, wildlife habitat, and community green space.
Tighe will introduce legislation to uphold tribal treaty rights such as the Treaty of Ruby Valley and to protect Nevada's natural landscapes.
