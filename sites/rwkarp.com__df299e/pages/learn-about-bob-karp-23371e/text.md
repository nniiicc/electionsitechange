Bob, a graduate of UCLA, has owned and operated a small manufacturing aerospace company, a software development company, and after retirement here in Arizona a Real Estate brokerage.
He moved to Cochise County in 2005 and lives in Sierra Vista.
He was president of the Southeast Arizona Association of Realtors in 2015 and served on the Sierra Vista Planning and Zoning Commission in 2017.
He is now running for State Senate in LD19 against Gail Griffin.
“The best way to create long term reform is to flip the state legislature from Republican control and extremism, to mainstream political values under Democratic Party control.” - Bob Karp upon filing to run in the Democratic Party Primary in 2024.
Bob’s mainstream values include defending public-school education, advocating fair tax policy, expanding state Medicaid access for working families, supporting the non-partisan fraud-free election system that has served this state well for decades from attacks by the national and state Republican party, advocating new and innovative solutions to our housing crisis, and working to ensure water rights for small farms and residential well owners, and stopping new mining operations that threaten groundwater sources and ecologically significant areas such as the Willcox playa.
