Bob Karp’s Ballot Pamphlet Biography
Bob Karp is a UCLA graduate, entrepreneur, and Cochise County resident since 2005.
In his 50 plus years in business, he has owned a small aerospace manufacturing company, a software development company and, after moving to Arizona, a real estate brokerage in Sierra Vista.
Bob has served as President of the Southeast Arizona Association of Realtors and on the Sierra Vista Planning and Zoning Commission.
He previously ran for the Arizona House of Representatives in 2018 and for the State Senate in 2020 and 2024.
Now, Bob is running for the Arizona State Senate to bring new leadership and mainstream problem-solving to Legislative District 19.
He believes it is time for a focus on public school education, water security, affordable healthcare, working families, and government reform.
