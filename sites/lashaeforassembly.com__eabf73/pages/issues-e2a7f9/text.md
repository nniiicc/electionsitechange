LaShae is Fighting for Us
Protecting Reproductive Freedom.
LaShae will ensure California stays on the leading edge of protecting a woman’s right to make her own health care decisions.
Prioritizing Equity in Education.
Close the opportunity gap and give all children a high-quality education regardless of their background or zip code.
Addressing the Homelessness Crisis.
Increase wrap-around services and help prevent homelessness in the first place so more families, seniors, veterans, and kids stay in their homes.
Delivering Environmental Justice and Protecting the Planet.
Build a more sustainable California by reducing greenhouse gas emissions, providing 100% clean energy, and ensuring that all communities have access to clean air, and water, and fight for stronger animal welfare, protect California's wildlife, and continue to make California a leader in the fight against climate change.
Delivering Real Affordable Housing and Equitable Transportation.
Fight for truly affordable housing at every income level so no family or worker struggles to put a roof over their heads and increase public transportation so only one form of transit is needed to get to a destination.
Expanding Health Care and Mental Health Care.
Ensure all families have access to affordable health care and increase mental health care services.
Creating a Stronger Economy.
Bring more good-paying jobs to our community, fight for better wages and working conditions, and deliver affordable child care now.
Protecting Families with Safer Communities.
Continue to lead against gun violence; get illegal guns off the streets, and champion legislation that addresses systemic issues contributing to gun violence.
