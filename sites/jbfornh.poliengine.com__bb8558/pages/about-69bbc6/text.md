A neighbor working for real solutions
Joseph wasn't raised with a silver spoon in his mouth.
He saw everyday how working-class people have to work to make ends meet.
"Watching my parents work long days, my dad as a truck driver and a father, my mom working, keeping a home and raising two boys.
I was learning firsthand the importance of hard work, but also how hard-working class American's have to struggle just to make ends meet."
Joseph worked his way through his 20's and 30's.
Engaging in a multitude of career paths.
Returning to college in his late 30's and received his Associate Degree in Business Management with a certificate in programing & development.
After school he continued working with fortune 500 companies and opened a small business,but soon turned back to helping those most in need...
For years, Joseph has dedicated himself to strengthening our communities through hands‑on service and compassionate outreach.
He has volunteered with the Shriners, helped raise funds for cancer research, and organized food collections for local pantries and shelters to support neighbors in need.
Joseph has also stood in solidarity with families impacted by ICE actions, speaking up for dignity, fairness, and the rights of all people.
His commitment to community led him, along with several neighbors, to establish the Monroe Democratic Town Committee — creating a local space for civic engagement and shared purpose.
In 2025, Joseph was elected to the Town of Monroe Budget Committee, continuing his dedication to responsible local leadership and fiscal transparency.
His passion for service isn’t a slogan; it’s a lived practice rooted in showing up, helping others, and building a New Hampshire where everyone is valued.
