Home About Issues Volunteer Donate Joseph Beaulieu for State Representative Home About Issues Volunteer Endorsements Issues Reducing Property Taxes The State must fulfill its constitutional and moral responsibility to fund public education fairly and equitably.
When the burden of school funding falls disproportionately on local property taxpayers, it deepens inequality between communities — forcing towns with fewer resources to struggle while wealthier areas thrive.
Every child, regardless of zip code, deserves access to quality education supported by the State’s fair share.
Giving $55 million in tax breaks to the ultra‑wealthy undermines that promise.
It diverts critical resources away from classrooms, teachers, and students who represent our future.
Investing in education strengthens our economy, our workforce, and our communities — while tax giveaways to the richest few only widen the gap and weaken the foundation of opportunity that public education provides.
Increasing Affordable Housing Increasing affordable housing is one of the smartest, most effective investments our state can make.
When families can afford to live in the communities where they work, our local businesses stay staffed, our schools stay strong, and our towns stay vibrant.
Affordable housing helps young people stay in the region, supports seniors who want to age in place, and gives working families the stability they need to build better lives.
It also attracts new employers who depend on a reliable workforce and strengthens the tax base without raising taxes.
By prioritizing housing that ordinary people can actually afford, we’re not just solving a crisis — we’re creating opportunity, boosting economic growth, and ensuring that every community in our state has the chance to thrive.
Increasing Career and Job opportunities Expanding career and job opportunities is one of the most powerful ways to strengthen our state.
When people can find good‑paying, stable work close to home, families stay rooted, young people choose to build their futures here, and local businesses thrive with a reliable workforce.
More opportunities mean higher household incomes, stronger local economies, and a broader tax base that reduces pressure on property taxpayers.
It also attracts new employers who see our region as a place where workers can grow, learn, and succeed.
By investing in job training, apprenticeships, small business development, and modern industries, we’re not just creating employment — we’re building long‑term economic resilience and giving every resident a fair shot at a better life.
Justice for Domestic Abuse Victims Ensuring justice for victims of domestic violence and abuse is essential to any society that claims to value fairness, dignity, and human rights.
When survivors are believed, protected, and supported, we affirm that every person deserves safety in their own home — the most basic expectation of a civilized community.
Failing to deliver justice allows cycles of harm to continue, destabilizes families, and weakens the social fabric that holds our towns together.
By strengthening protections, improving access to services, and holding abusers accountable, we not only defend the vulnerable — we build a community rooted in compassion, accountability, and respect.
A society that stands with survivors is a society that stands for justice.
