Monica Alponte is a new voice in the America First movement representing what Americans want: PEACE, LIBERTY, PROSPERITY, NATIONAL SOVEREIGNTY, and to STOP ENDLESS WARS!
Raised in a military family, she lived across the country before making Arizona her home 25 years ago.
This upbringing gave her deep respect for those who serve and a firsthand understanding of the costs of war, fueling her commitment to a non-interventionist foreign policy that prioritizes peace.
A retired business owner, writer, and former political TV talk show co-host, Monica parted ways with the uniparty to champion civil liberties and true individual sovereignty: "NOT LEFT, NOT RIGHT, JUST FREE."
For the past decade, she’s worked in local governance in HOA reform, leading homeowner advocacy, road safety, and animal rescue.
She's dealt with bureaucracy head-on, providing fiduciary oversight, drafting resolutions and amendments, serving on committees, approving and managing budgets—many of the same core duties required to serve in the U.S.
Congress.
