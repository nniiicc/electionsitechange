Public Safety & Support for Law Enforcement Safe communities are the foundation of a strong Alabama.
I believe in standing firmly with law enforcement and first responders who put their lives on the line to protect our families and neighborhoods.
As State Representative, I will oppose reckless policies that undermine public safety and instead support measures that give police, firefighters, and EMS professionals the resources, respect, and backing they deserve.
Economic Growth & Job Creation A strong economy creates opportunity for Alabamians and stability for communities.
I support responsible policies that attract manufacturing, grow small businesses, and bring good-paying jobs to District 70.
Strategic tax incentives should be used wisely to encourage investment while protecting taxpayers.
Economic growth strengthens families, expands opportunity, and ensures Alabama remains competitive for generations to come.
Education Excellence & Parental Rights Our children deserve an education that prepares them for success — not political agendas.
I support a strong focus on core academic subjects, transparency in the classroom, and respect for parents’ rights to know what their children are being taught.
Schools should educate, not indoctrinate.
By keeping classrooms focused on learning and accountability, we can ensure Alabama students are prepared for college, careers, and life.
Infrastructure & Community Investment Reliable infrastructure is essential to economic growth and quality of life.
I support smart investments in roads, bridges, utilities, and public infrastructure that keep communities connected and businesses moving.
Well-planned infrastructure improvements create jobs today while preparing District 70 for future growth.
Workforce Development & Skilled Trades Not every path to success runs through a four-year degree — and Alabama should support all career pathways.
I support expanding trade and workforce development programs in public schools and higher education.
By partnering with local industry professionals, we can train the next generation of skilled workers and strengthen Alabama’s manufacturing and innovation economy.
Alabama Values & Common Sense Leadership Alabama’s strength comes from its values: faith, family, hard work, personal responsibility, and respect for the rule of law.
These principles guide my approach to leadership.
As State Representative, I will work to ensure our laws reflect common sense, and strengthen families and communities — not expand bureaucracy or waste taxpayer dollars.
