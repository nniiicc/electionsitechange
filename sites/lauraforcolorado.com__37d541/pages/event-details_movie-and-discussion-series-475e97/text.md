About the event
Join me for the first part of a three-part movie series.
We will have a lively discussion after watching a thought-provoking film that delves into the subtle and often insidious ways in which indoctrination has infiltrated the lives of young people.
This film offers a compelling narrative that illustrates methods through which beliefs and ideologies are instilled in the impressionable minds of youth.
We will explore the forms of indoctrination portrayed, including societal pressures, educational influences, and media manipulation.
The discussion will allow us to analyze how these elements shape the perceptions and values of young individuals, often without their conscious awareness.
In addition to discussing the film's themes, I encourage participants to share their own experiences and perspectives on how indoctrination has manifested in their lives or the lives of those around them and most importantly, what we can do to correct these detrimental ideologies.
