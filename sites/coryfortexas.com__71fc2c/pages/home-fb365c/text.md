A native of Austin and a proud product of its public schools, he has dedicated his life to fighting for people’s constitutional rights and promoting integrity in government.
Judge Liu is the son of immigrants who lived through Communism and came to Texas to pursue the American Dream.
From top academic achievements at the University of Chicago and Harvard Law School to key roles serving Texas leaders, Judge Liu has built a distinguished career grounded in both legal excellence and community values.
As a current District Judge in Travis County, he applies his expertise in civil law with fairness, professionalism, and integrity—making him a trusted leader for the bench.
Meet Judge Liu Key Issues Ending Discrimination Judge Liu believes in equal justice under the law and has fought against discrimination, standing up for a colorblind Constitution that unites—not divides—Americans.
First Amendment Rights Judge Liu has a long track record of defending free speech and religious freedom.
He has worked alongside leading legal organizations to protect the first liberties protected by our Constitution’s First Amendment.
Law and Order Committed to public safety and justice, Judge Liu has stood up for the rights of victims and defended our men and women in uniform who put their lives on the line to protect our communities.
