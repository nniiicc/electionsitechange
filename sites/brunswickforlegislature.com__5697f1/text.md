ABOUT DAWSON
Dawson Brunswick lives in Columbus with his family, where they are proud to be raising their kids and putting down roots in the community they call home.
A Nebraska native, Dawson understands the values that make our state strong and has built his life around hard work, service, and supporting local families and businesses.
Dawson currently serves as President of the Columbus Area Chamber of Commerce, where he works closely with local employers, small businesses, and community leaders to grow the economy, create jobs, and strengthen opportunities across the region.
Through his work, he has developed a deep understanding of the challenges facing Nebraska families and the importance of practical, common-sense solutions.
Dawson is ready to take that experience to the Legislature to lower property taxes, support economic growth, and ensure Nebraska remains a place where families, businesses, and communities can thrive.
Kids.
I want Nebraska kids, including my two young daughters, to have the same opportunities that generations of Nebraskans had growing up: strong schools, safe communities, and a real chance to build a successful future.
We should keep politics out of kids’ lives as much as possible and focus on helping children learn, grow, and thrive.
Careers.
Promote job growth and upward mobility by backing small businesses, expanding skilled trades and career training, and creating opportunities that keep our workforce strong and our young people in Nebraska.
Running State Government Like a Business.
Apply common-sense, business-minded leadership to state government by cutting wasteful spending, improving efficiency, and holding government accountable so taxpayers know their dollars are being used responsibly.
Protecting Nebraska Values.
Stand strong for Nebraska values by defending constitutional rights, protecting the unborn, supporting law enforcement, and standing up for the families and principles that keep our communities safe and strong.
