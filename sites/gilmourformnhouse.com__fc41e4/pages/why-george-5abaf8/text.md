why george?
Minnesota deserves better than the anger, division, and political noise that have taken over our state.
For too long, we’ve been pushed into picking sides instead of actually solving real problems.
Meanwhile, hardworking Minnesotans are left dealing with rising costs, growing frustration, and a government that too often feels out of touch.
I’m running for Minnesota House because I believe Minnesota can do better.
Minnesota is filled with good people—families, workers, teachers, nurses, first responders, farmers, and business owners—who want many of the same things: safe communities, strong schools, affordable living, and the freedom to build a good life.
But right now, too much of our politics is focused on division instead of solutions.
I believe it’s time to restore common sense leadership and bring people together again.
My Priorities
Accountability in Government
Taxpayer dollars should be treated with respect.
We need transparency, integrity, and responsible budgeting to reduce waste, prevent fraud, and lower the burden on Minnesota families.
Safer Communities
Every Minnesotan deserves to feel safe.
Law enforcement should have the support they need to enforce the law, protect families, and keep our communities secure.
Parents’ Rights
Parents should have the primary voice in decisions about their children’s health, education, and future.
Unity and Respect
We may not agree on everything, but we can still work together.
Minnesota works best when we focus on shared values instead of political division.
Why I’m Running
Like many of you, I’ve spent years frustrated by the direction we’re headed.
Finally I reached a point where I knew frustration alone wasn’t enough.
It was time to step up.
I want to restore trust in government.
I want to protect your freedoms.
I want to help build a stronger, safer, more united Minnesota.
Together, we can move our state forward.
