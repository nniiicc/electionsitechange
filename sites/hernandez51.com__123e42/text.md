I'm Octavio Hernandez — candidate for FL State Representative District 51.
I immigrated here from Mexico.
While my origins are from Mexico, my roots are planted in Polk County, FL.
I grew up in Central Florida.
I'm a UCF alum with a B.A. in Political Science.
I proudly serve in Polk County schools as an Algebra Teacher and union member.
I decided to run to be a role model for my students.
I want to show them that this country is for the people by the people.
That any American citizen regardless of their origins has the constitutional right to make this country better for all.
Together, we can make Polk County a better place for everyone.
