MEET SUZANNE A Leader Who Understands Our Community Suzanne Ness is a business owner, community advocate, and State Representative dedicated to improving the lives of Illinois families.
Before serving in the legislature, Suzanne built her career helping businesses grow and solving problems for clients and communities.
That real-world experience shaped her approach to public service: listen first, understand the challenge, and work toward practical solutions.
As State Representative, Suzanne has remained accessible, responsive, and focused on delivering meaningful results for the people she serves.
