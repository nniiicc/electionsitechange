Prior to this, Suzanne served as a McHenry County Board Member for District 2 from 2018-2020.
In her role as State Representative, Suzanne has brought more than $34 million dollars to the district and has successfully sponsored and passed 14 bills, most signed into law.
She secured the remaining $12.5 million necessary for the Longmeadow Parkway Bridge, eliminating the need and therefore burden of tolling.
She consistently advocates for the needs of her constituents, and supports legislation that has made Illinois a leader in environmental sustainability, voting rights, and access to reproductive healthcare.
To learn more about her work in the Illinois General Assembly, visit the “Platform” page.
Suzanne’s professional and personal experience has prepared her well for a life in public service.
As a small business owner, she worked with companies to develop employee skills, improve morale, strategically plan for the future, and build value driven organizations.
She has helped build local business networking groups, and started a non-profit #ago that brought leadership development, coaching, and mentorship skills to local teens.
The program has continued to grow under her leadership and has become a community-building enterprise as teens then mentor elementary school kids and volunteer at a local daycare.
Learn More About Suzanne’s CAP AWARD Family serves as a “north star” in Suzanne’s life.
Suzanne is the oldest of 4 sisters, all of whom are active in the community.
She is the daughter of a trail-blazing community activist and talented musician.
Suzanne is a wife, mother to 3 adult children, mother-in-law, grandmother, and aunt to many nieces and nephews.
She hopes that her work will lead to a brighter future for all of them.
In her spare time, Suzanne enjoys running and training for marathons.
She has run 4 marathons, and is looking forward to competing in more!
She enjoys reading, volunteering, listening to music, and spending time with friends and family.
Suzanne grew up in Marengo and resides in Crystal Lake with her husband Robert.
Suzanne has a B.S. in Communications from Arizona State University; M.A. from University of Phoenix.
