What We’re Looking For:
Create a compelling, original ad or video supporting Governor Abbott, conservative leadership, Texas values, or the campaign’s vision for the future of Texas.
Submissions may include:
• AI-generated videos or visuals
• Original filmed content
• Motion graphics or edited videos
• Social-first campaign ads
• Humor, storytelling, testimonials, or persuasive messaging
We encourage creativity, authenticity, and strong storytelling.
To be eligible, submissions must meet the following requirements:
• 15–90 seconds preferred
• Maximum: 2 minutes
• MP4 or MOV preferred
• Vertical (9:16) for social media preferred
• Horizontal (16:9) accepted
• Minimum: 1080p resolution
Submission Requirements:

• Upload via the official submission form/QR code
• Include a short description of your concept
