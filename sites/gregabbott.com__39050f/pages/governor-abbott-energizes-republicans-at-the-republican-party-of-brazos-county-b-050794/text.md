The Governor emphasized the stark differences between the common sense policies Republican champion and the radical, socialist agenda Democrats continue to embrace.
Under Governor Abbott’s leadership, Texas has delivered historic property tax relief, increased teacher pay, secured the border, and passed the strongest bail reform laws in Texas history.
In contrast, Democrats continue to push for higher property taxes, allowing men to compete in women’s sports, and open border policies.
Governor Abbott urged Republicans to continue the fight to keep Texas affordable, secure, and grounded in Texas values.
Governor Abbott also highlighted Democrat Gina Hinojosa’s radical record of supporting policies that are out of step with Texas values.
“Gina Hinojosa has a radical record that is hostile to the safety, security, and prosperity of Texans,” said Governor Abbott.
“She has pushed for a state income tax, opposed commonsense election integrity measures, and fought to bring radical gender ideology into Texas classrooms.
