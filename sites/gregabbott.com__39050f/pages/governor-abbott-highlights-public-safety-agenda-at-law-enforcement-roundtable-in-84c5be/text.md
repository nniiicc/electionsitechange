“Texans want safe communities and secure borders,” said Governor Abbott.
“I have joined with law enforcement officers to keep Texas safe by creating the Repeat Offender Task Force, spearheading tougher bail for dangerous criminals, and cracking down on fentanyl findings at our border.
With enough fentanyl seized to kill every man, woman, and child in all of North America, this widespread fatal drug must be taken seriously.
We have now seen a 40% reduction in fentanyl related deaths, we are saving lives.” “Governor Abbott has been a true champion in the fight against fentanyl,” said Director of Soles Walking 4 Souls Kathy Drago.
“He has stood with families like ours, from launching Operation Lone Star and signing the One Pill Kills Initiative, and I am grateful that he has supported us at our Fentanyl Awareness Walk every year since we first invited him.
