During Hinojosa’s tenure on the AISD Board, district spending increased by more than $321 million – nearly 40% – while administrative payrolls grew nearly 15%.
Teacher pay, meanwhile, increased just 3.5%.
She may claim to be an advocate for teachers now, but Gina’s record is clear — she shoveled taxpayer money into the pockets of administrators, instead of excellence in the classroom.
“Gina Hinojosa has a curious record for someone who purports to care about public school teachers and Texas students.
On the AISD School Board, Hinojosa poured money into the pockets of administrative bureaucrats instead of teachers, raided the district’s savings account despite falling enrollment, and left teachers fighting for scraps.
If Gina Hinojosa can’t manage a school board, how can she run the eighth largest economy in the entire world?” — Catherine Frazier, Texans for Greg Abbott Communications Director Gina’s Hinojosa’s time on the AISD School Board proves she is grossly unfit to lead Texas: In a 2016 interview with KXAN , Gina Hinojosa said of her time on the AISD Board, “the buck stopped with me.” ( YouTube ) AISD’s spending grew significantly more than teacher pay.
During her tenure, Hinojosa squandered the school budget and increased AISD expenditures by over $321 million, or nearly 40%, even as student enrollment saw a steady decline.
A chunk of those expenditures went towards administrative payrolls, which increased by nearly 15% while Hinojosa was on the school board.
Conversely, over the same time period, teacher pay increased by only 3.5%.
