Texans for Greg Abbott Launches HypocriteHinojosa.com Highlighting Gina’s Progressive, Anti-Texas Record
AUSTIN – Texans for Greg Abbott today launched HypocriteHinojosa.com, a new website highlighting Gina Hinojosa’s radically progressive record in the Texas Legislature.
“Gina Hinojosa is out of step with Texans.
Her legislative record reveals an extreme agenda that includes prioritizing violent illegal immigrants and career criminals over hardworking Texans, voting for increasing property taxes 40 times, advocating for a state income tax in Texas, supporting a multi-million-dollar cut from law enforcement, and exposing children to life-altering sex change surgeries without access to care after the fact.
Voters deserve to know the truth about Gina Hinojosa’s record — not just the version she is selling on the campaign trail.”
— Catherine Frazier, Texans for Greg Abbott Communications Director
