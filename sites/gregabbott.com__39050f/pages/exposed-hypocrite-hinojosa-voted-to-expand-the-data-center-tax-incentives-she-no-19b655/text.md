“Gina Hinojosa is hiding her true record on data centers from Texas voters.
Her accusations are a confession: when Gina had the chance to stand up to data centers in the Legislature, she repeatedly voted to support their tax incentives while reducing the number of full-time jobs they were required to create to receive them.
Her signature campaign issue is built on denying her own record.
Texans deserve someone who will shoot straight, not a political performer who will say anything to get elected.” — Catherine Frazier, Texans for Greg Abbott Communications Director In 2017, Hinojosa voted for HB 4038, legislation that continued the same tax break that the 2015 Texas Legislature previously approved.

The bill ultimately died in the Senate.
