GINA HINOJOSA WANTS TO CENSOR THE FACTS ABOUT HER 40 VOTES TO RAISE PROPERTY TAXES
AUSTIN — Gina Hinojosa is desperate to hide her disastrous record of voting 40 times for higher taxes.
Last week, she sent cease-and-desist letters to Texas television stations demanding they pull a Texans for Greg Abbott (TGA) advertisement highlighting 40 votes she cast to increase property taxes.
Texans for Greg Abbott has sent a legal response to the television stations listing the 40 votes she took to raise property taxes (that are also listed in the advertisement).
Gina knows that voters who learn about her 40 votes to hike their taxes will never vote for her, but Governor Abbott will not let this desperate attempt at lawfare hide the truth from Texas voters.
“Gina Hinojosa is in full panic mode, desperately trying to hide the radical voting record that exposes just how far left she really is.
Now she is resorting to lawfare and legal intimidation to censor an ad that accurately exposes her long record of voting for higher taxes.
But no amount of threats can erase the facts: Gina voted 40 times to raise your taxes.”
— Catherine Frazier, Texans for Greg Abbott Communications Director
