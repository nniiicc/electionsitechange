“Socialist Democrats like Gina Hinojosa want to hike your property taxes, make you pay a new state income tax, and even tax you after you die.
In 2025, Gina had a chance to stand for common sense and failed.
She and other Democrats voted against banning death and inheritance taxes in Texas.
Her voting record makes one thing perfectly clear: Gina will tax you to death.” — Catherine Frazier, Texans for Greg Abbott Communications Director In 2025, Hinojosa voted against the constitutional amendment banning death taxes on inheritances.
(HJR 2, 89R) Gina Hinojosa has also advocated for a state income tax in Texas since 2016.
In a 2016 interview with KXAN , Gina Hinojosa said, “We rely so heavily in the state on property taxes, too heavily, in my opinion, because we don’t have, we don’t have the third leg of that stool … income taxes.” ( YouTube ) ​​​​​​​In an interview with First Unitarian Universalist Church of Austin she said that Texas is “missing” a state income tax and that it was the “third leg of the stool that we need.” ( YouTube ) As an AISD board member, Gina was also happy to raise property taxes making sure that every household in AISD had less money in their pockets when she left the board.
The total amount of AISD property taxes imposed increased by nearly $405 million, or 52%, during Hinojosa’s tenure on the AISD School Board. ( FY 2020 Budget Table 8 ) In her first year on the School Board, she voted to increase AISD property taxes on the average Austin home by nearly $140. ( FY 2018 Budget Table 20 ) Her second year, she voted to increase AISD property taxes again – by $230 on average. ( FY 2018 Budget Table 20 ) In year three, as AISD board president, she increased AISD property taxes yet again by over $170 on average. ( FY 2018 Budget Table 20 ) It gets worse.
In year four, Hinojosa voted to increase AISD property taxes by more than $330 on average. ( FY 2018 Budget Table 20 ) Over her four years on the School Board, Hinojosa voted to increase AISD property taxes on the average Austin home by nearly $900 total.
