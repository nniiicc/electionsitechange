She’s now straight-up lying to Texas voters in her recent claim to be against a state income tax.
Hinojosa said a state income tax ‘makes sense’ and then voted AGAINST the very constitutional amendment she trumpets in her new ad.
Tax Hiker Hinojosa can run from her record, but she can’t hide from it.” — Catherine Frazier, Texans for Greg Abbott Communications Director Gina Hinojosa has been advocating for a state income tax since 2016.
In a 2016 interview with KXAN , Gina Hinojosa said, “We rely so heavily in the state on property taxes, too heavily, in my opinion, because we don’t have … the third leg of that stool … income taxes.” ( YouTube ) In an interview with First Unitarian Universalist Church of Austin she said that Texas is “missing” a state income tax and that it was the “third leg of the stool that we need.” ( YouTube ) In 2019, Hinojosa voted against banning a state income tax in the Texas Constitution.
