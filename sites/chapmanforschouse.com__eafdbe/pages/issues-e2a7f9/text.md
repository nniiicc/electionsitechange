I'll always protect the commonsense, conservative values of the people of District 8. ​ Here are just a few of the things SC House Republicans have accomplished in my time.
I look forward to continuing our conservative work with your vote on June 11th!
LOWER TAXES The 2022 state budget keeps the legislature’s promise to continue to cut the state’s income tax.
It reduces the state income tax by $96 million in the second year of the income tax reduction plan.
It also budgets for an additional $134 million in property tax cuts.
In the past 30 years, the General Assembly has passed over $50 billion in tax cuts.
STOPPING CRIME I proudly sponsored the 2022 Trafficking in Fentanyl bill (signed by Governor), strengthening our state's laws against bringing this deadly drug into our state.
Additionally, I sponsored the Bond Reform bill (signed by Governor) which will help make South Carolina a safer place for us to all raise our families by stopping the revolving door for repeat criminals.
This bill creates sentence and bond enhancements for a person convicted of a violent crime while out on bond for a previous violent crime.
WORKFORCE READINESS In 2022, I voted for H.3726, the "SC Education and Workforce Development Act," which aimed to prepare 60% of South Carolinians with postsecondary degrees or industry credentials by 2030 by mandating measures such as preparing high school students for college-level math and reading, and utilizing technical colleges for workforce training.
RAISE TEACHER PAY & EXPAND SCHOOL CHOICE This past session, I voted to raise the minimum pay of K-12 public school teachers by $2,500.
Starting teachers will be paid $42,500 – a 33% increase in base pay over the past five years.
Additionally, I believe nothing is more important than keeping children safe.
The budget spends $27 million to fully fund a Safety Resource Officer (SRO) in every public school in the state.
The Education Scholarship Trust Fund will provide eligible elementary and secondary students with scholarships to cover education expenses at the school of their choice, funded directly from the state budget.
PROTECT LIFE The Legislature passed the “Heartbeat Bill” (signed by Governor) in 2022, reaffirming South Carolina’s commitment to safeguarding the rights of the most vulnerable among us.
The legislation ended South Carolina’s status as an abortion destination state.
We will keep working to protect life and the health and well-being of women with the personal sponsorship of the "Human Life Protection Act." SAFEGUARDING OUR FUTURE GENERATIONS & MOST VULNERABLE I proudly sponsored Gavin's Law in 2022 - a bill introduced by fellow freshman Rep.
