LOWER TAXES The 2025 state budget keeps the legislature’s promise to continue to cut the state’s income tax.
It reduces the state income tax by an additional $99 million in the third year of the income tax reduction plan.
Last year's budget, I voted to cut $96 million in income taxes and an additional $134 million in property tax cuts.
In the past 30 years, the General Assembly has passed over $50 billion in tax cuts.
PROTECTING OUR CONSTITUTIONAL RIGHTS, STOPPING CRIME, KEEPING OUR BORDERS SAFE, AND KEEPING CRIMINALS IN JAIL In 2024 , I Co-Sponsored our rights to Constitutional Carry law that is now law to protect our right to carry arms.
I have also Co-Sponsored legislation that recommits our constitutional rights to Keep and Bear Arms that will protect our rights to carrying weapons and weapons accessories.
During the term last year I proudly sponsored the 2022 Trafficking in Fentanyl bill (signed by Governor), strengthening our state's laws against bringing this deadly drug into our state.
Additionally, I sponsored the Bond Reform bill (signed by Governor) which will help make South Carolina a safer place for us to all raise our families by stopping the revolving door for repeat criminals.
This bill creates sentence and bond enhancements for a person convicted of a violent crime while out on bond for a previous violent crime.
WORKFORCE READINESS & CREATING JOBS During the current budget cycle, I have supported an $95 million to help prepare our workforce for jobs in the computer science, information technology, nursing, and, manufacturing industries.
During 2022, I voted for H.3726, the "SC Education and Workforce Development Act," which aimed to prepare 60% of South Carolinians with postsecondary degrees or industry credentials by 2030 by mandating measures such as preparing high school students for college-level math and reading, and utilizing technical colleges for workforce training.
All of these efforts focus on improving our workforce to have workers prepared to meet the needs of high quality employers in our state to improve the quality of life of our citizens.
RAISE TEACHER PAY & EXPAND SCHOOL CHOICE This year's budget includes $250 million in aid to class rooms and $115 million for instruction materials to increase teacher salaries and improve the resources to educate the children in our state.
The result of the budget support is raising the minimum pay of K-12 public school teachers by $2,500.
Starting teachers will be paid $45,000 – a 41% increase in base pay over the past six years.
Additionally, I believe nothing is more important than keeping children safe.
Last year's budget spent $27 million to fully fund a Safety Resource Officer (SRO) in every public school in the state.
This year we have included an additional $13 million to keep our schools safe.
The Education Scholarship Trust Fund will provide eligible elementary and secondary students with scholarships to cover education expenses at the school of their choice, funded directly from the state budget.
