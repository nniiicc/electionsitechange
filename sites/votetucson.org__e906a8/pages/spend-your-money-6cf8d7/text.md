We’re not taking your donations — but you’ve apparently got a few dollars you were willing to throw at a write-in candidate who once failed to recognize an astronaut in his own living room.
So here are better places for it, arranged from “right in front of you” to “the other side of the planet.” Everything here is deliberately non-partisan and non-religious .
No causes that’ll start a fight at Thanksgiving.
Just people, animals, and rest.
Pick a tier.
Open it.
Do one thing.
Yourself and the People You Love The most radical thing on this list.
You’re allowed to spend money on being a person.
Take your partner or a friend to a movie.
Go to The Loft Cinema Go to The Loft Cinema — it’s a Tucson nonprofit art house, it’s right here on Speedway, and it even runs on solar.
You’re buying a date night AND supporting a local institution. → loftcinema.org Buy a real dinner at a locally-owned restaurant.
Not a chain.
A place where the owner might be in the kitchen.
The money stays in town.
Get the thing you keep not buying for yourself.
The shoes, the book, the tool.
You know the one.
Pay for the date night you keep postponing.
Babysitter included.
Just rest.
Buy yourself an afternoon where you owe nobody anything.
That counts.
Someone Right Next to You No charity needed.
No website.
Just a person you can see.
Cover a struggling friend's bill the phone, the groceries, the car repair.
Quietly.
Leave a tip so big it ruins someone's bad day in the best way.
Pay for the stranger behind you in line who’s counting change.
Send money to that one person you know is barely holding on and would never ask.
Take a broke friend out your treat, no discussion.
Your Local Community (Tucson & District 6) Verified, local, secular.
These serve the actual district.
Community Food Bank of Southern Arizona feeds people across Cochise, Graham, Greenlee, Pima, and Santa Cruz counties (which is basically all of District 6).
Every dollar is meals. → communityfoodbank.org Humane Society of Southern Arizona local animal shelter and pet-food pantry that helps families keep their pets when money’s tight. → hssaz.org Fund a local classroom use DonorsChoose to pick a real public-school project in the Tucson area and pay for it directly. → donorschoose.org Your local library search “[your town] Friends of the Library” and donate.
Libraries are the last free, non-judgmental public space we’ve got.
A local food pantry or community fridge find the one nearest you and fill it.
Your Country Bigger reach, still secular, still non-partisan.
Feeding America the national food-bank network; routes your gift to wherever the need is highest. → feedingamerica.org DonorsChoose (anywhere in the U.S.) fund a classroom in a part of the country that needs it more than yours does. → donorschoose.org Direct Relief provides medical aid across all 50 states after disasters, with no regard to politics, religion, or ability to pay. → directrelief.org A disaster fund for wherever just got hit there’s always somewhere.
Give when it’s current and forgotten in three weeks.
Your state's 211 / community foundation local emergency assistance funds that quietly keep people housed.
The Whole World If you want to do the most good per dollar, this tier is where the evidence points — and we kept it clear of any current conflict so nobody has to argue about it.
GiveDirectly sends cash, with no strings attached, straight to people living in extreme poverty.
They decide what they need, not a middleman.
Rigorously studied, remarkably efficient. → givedirectly.org charity: water funds clean, safe drinking water in developing countries.
#% of public donations go to the work.
Secular, despite the name. → charitywater.org Direct Relief (global) medical supplies and aid in more than 90 countries, politically and religiously neutral. → directrelief.org GlobalGiving a platform where you pick the country and the project.
Want to help a specific place?
Browse, choose, give — your conscience, your call. → globalgiving.org A vetted charity of your own choosing check it first at charitynavigator.org or givewell.org so your money goes where it says it does.
