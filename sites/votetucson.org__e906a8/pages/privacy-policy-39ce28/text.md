The Platform How To Write Me In The Ledger Help Wanted About Contact Privacy Policy Privacy Policy Last updated: 6-13-2026 The short version: we collect almost nothing, we don’t track you, we don’t sell anything, and if you ever want us to delete what little we have, just ask.
The longer version, because honesty is sort of the whole point: What we collect The only information we collect is what you choose to hand us through our contact form: your name, your email address, your phone number (if you give it, it’s optional), what you said your message was about, the option you picked describing yourself, and whatever you wrote in the message box.
That’s it.
We don’t run analytics.
We don’t use tracking pixels.
We have not connected Google Analytics, Jetpack, or any other tool that watches what you do here.
We’re not interested in surveilling you.
We just want to read and answer your message.
Where it goes and how we store it When you submit the form, two things happen: a copy is saved in our website’s own database (using a plugin called Flamingo), and a notification is emailed to us so a real person can read it and write back.
The stored copies live on our web host’s server, and we’re the only ones who look at them.
What we do with it We read it.
We reply to it.
That’s the entire purpose.
We do not sell your information.
We don’t rent, trade, or hand it to advertisers, partly on principle and partly because we’re a tiny write-in campaign with no one to sell it to.
We won’t add you to any mailing list unless you specifically ask to be.
Cookies, logs, and the boring technical stuff Social media and embedded content Your information is yours You can ask us anytime to show you what we have on file or to delete it.
We’ll do it: no forms, no hoops.
Just email us (below).
Children This site is aimed at voters and isn’t directed at children.
We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
Changes If we change how any of this works, we’ll update this page and change the date at the top.
How to reach us Questions about your privacy, or want your data removed?
Email info@votetucson.org or admin@votetucson.org .
