A Message from Representative Fawn Pedalino Serving in the South Carolina House of Representatives has been one of the greatest honors of my life.
Over the past years, my commitment has remained the same: to be accessible, responsive, and helpful to the people I represent. ​ From the beginning, I believed constituent service should be one of the most important responsibilities of this office.
Whether you need help navigating a state agency, reporting a concern, asking a question, or simply trying to find the right place to start, my office is here to help. ​ That is why we have worked to make this website simple and user-friendly.
It is meant to give constituents an easy way to contact my office, report issues, and request assistance with state matters.
While we cannot guarantee a specific outcome, we will always do our best to help you receive a fair and timely response. ​ I am grateful for the trust you have placed in me, and I will continue working every day to serve Clarendon and Sumter counties with honesty, accessibility, and dedication. ​ Feedback is always welcome.
If you do not see a specific option that fits your needs, please visit the contact section of this website to reach out to my office. ​ Yours Respectfully, bottom of page
