As a veterinarian, educator, and engaged community servant,
I spend a lot of time talking to folks about
their unique goals, concerns, and interests.
Sometimes, those conversations venture into
complicated or even controversial terrain,
but you can count on me to speak
kindly, clearly, and with integrity every time.
I will never shy away
from talking to my neighbors
about things they care about.
If it matters to you,
it matters to me.
Is there something you’d like to ask me directly?
Is there a specific viewpoint you want to bring to my attention?
I want to hear from you.
