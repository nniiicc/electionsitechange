There is no question we need a secure border, and we need to actively prosecute and deport criminals.
We also need to guarantee the rights of law-abiding people who are in this country legally, and we need a rational policy for guest workers that allows the legal immigration needed to fill the demand generated by American farms and businesses.
As a result of the Trump Administration’s anti-immigrant agenda, the Immigration and Customs Enforcement Agency (ICE) will triple in size and is building a detention system larger than the U.S.
Bureau of Prisons.
The White House quota-driven deportation program has resulted in large numbers of legal immigrants, legal visa holders, and even American citizens, being seized in public without warrants by people in masks without uniforms, who are being detained or deported while being denied any of the due process guaranteed by our Constitution.
These unconstitutional actions are not making America safer, they are terrorizing citizens and non-citizens in every community in America.
In Congress I will support: Requiring federal law enforcement officers to wear their uniforms and show their faces.
Focusing immigration enforcement on keeping violent criminals off of our streets, not harassing legal immigrants while executing mass, quota-driven deportations.
Securing the border with better technology, ensuring safe and humane treatment for every person apprehended at the border, and investing in modernized ports of entry.
Reforming our guest worker programs so that farms, manufacturers, and service businesses can complement their labor force with legal immigrant workers.
Reforming the asylum process so that immigrants with legitimate fear for their lives or their families can enter America legally and have their case heard.
We need to hire more judges and officers to clear the asylum backlog, and ensure that legitimate asylum claims are heard and honored.
We need a smarter immigration policy that keeps our border secure and addresses criminal and drug-related activity, while allowing safe entry and humane treatment for legal immigrants to help meet the demands of American businesses and allow for a prosperous cross-border economy.
