NORTHERN WI.
Today, Clark for Wisconsin launched “Where’s Alfonso” - a new effort to draw the Trump-endorsed GOP contender for Wisconsin’s 7th district out of hiding and into living up to his commitment to debate Fred Clark.
Alfonso agreed to a debate with Clark on May 5, 2026.
As part of this new effort, Clark released the following video.
