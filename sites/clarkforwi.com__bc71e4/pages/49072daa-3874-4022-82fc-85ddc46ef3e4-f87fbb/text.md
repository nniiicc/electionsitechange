Privacy Policy
Fred Clark for Wisconsin “the Campaign” respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
Texting Programs
If you subscribe to any text programs that Fred Clark for Wisconsin makes available, the following terms apply:
- By subscribing to Fred Clark for Wisconsin updates or alerts, you consent to receive periodic updates or alerts by automatic text message.
Message frequency is no more than once per day.
Text STOP to stop.
For Help, text HELP or contact us via email.
Message and data rates may apply.
- You acknowledge and agree that you may opt-out of receiving further mobile messages via any opt-out keywords that are universally recognized, including, but not limited to, STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, or QUIT.
Information We Collect
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, ZIP code, and any other information you choose to share when you:
- Sign up for campaign updates
- Sign a petition
- Volunteer or RSVP for an event
- Make a donation
- Otherwise engage with the Campaign online
How We Use Your Information
We use the information you provide to:
- Communicate with you about the Campaign and issues you care about
- Share updates, news, and opportunities to get involved
- Request support, including donations and volunteer activity
- Improve our outreach and organizing efforts
Sharing of Information
The Campaign does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share information with trusted service providers who assist the Campaign with functions such as email delivery, website hosting, analytics, or compliance.
These providers are only permitted to use your information for Campaign-related purposes.
Your Choices
You may opt out of receiving email communications from the Campaign at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link included in our emails.
Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
Updates to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page.
