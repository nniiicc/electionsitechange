Who I Am
I’m a lifelong New Hampshire resident and have called Charlestown home for 32 years.
As a single mother raising four young children, I spent more than a decade living below the poverty line and relying on public assistance.
I know firsthand the challenges families face when every dollar matters.
After remarrying, I worked full-time, attended college, and helped raise five children.
Through hard work and determination, my husband and I paid off debt, rebuilt our credit, and achieved the dream of home ownership.
Today, as a grandmother, I see our children struggling with unaffordable housing, our schools cutting programs, and seniors being taxed out of their homes.
Families are being squeezed by rising costs for groceries, gas, healthcare, and everyday necessities.
I’m not a politician—I’m a grandmother who is deeply concerned about the future of New Hampshire.
This is not the state I grew up in.
I believe we need practical, sensible solutions that put families, seniors, and local communities first.
I don’t claim to have all the answers, but I will listen to those with expertise, work collaboratively, and support sensible policies that address these challenges one step at a time.
