About Taylor
Taylor Royal lives in Bennington with his wife, Katie, and their two children, Wells and Summer.
A Nebraska native, Taylor graduated from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln in just three years with a double major in accounting and finance.
He earned his Master of Science in Accounting from Southern Methodist University, as well as his CPA, before beginning his career at Deloitte in Dallas, specializing in international tax.
Today, Taylor is a Partner at Royal Wealth Partners, a local Private Client Group within Northwestern Mutual, serving clients in 47 states and managing over $1.5 billion in client assets.
Taylor is ready to bring his expertise in business and his experience as a working dad to the Legislature to cut taxes, create jobs, and make life more affordable for Nebraska families.
