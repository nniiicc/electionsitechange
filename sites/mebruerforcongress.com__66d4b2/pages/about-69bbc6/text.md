About Mebruer’s Policies And Values
Public policy should be guided by clear principles: responsible government, economic freedom, respect for human life, and the rule of law.
In recent decades, Washington has moved away from these principles, creating a system burdened by unsustainable debt, complex regulations, and policies that too often benefit special interests instead of the American people.
Government must remain disciplined and focused on its core responsibilities.
A strong economy requires sound fiscal policy, a simple and fair tax system, and regulations that allow businesses and workers to grow and succeed.
When government becomes overly complex or expands beyond its proper role, economic opportunity suffers and public trust declines.
A healthy society also depends on strong moral foundations and respect for the dignity of human life.
Public policy should recognize the value of the most vulnerable while supporting families and communities that form the backbone of American society.
Restoring fiscal discipline, simplifying the tax code, securing the border, protecting life, and reforming major federal programs are essential steps toward a more stable and prosperous future.
These policies focus on strengthening the American economy, restoring accountability in government, and ensuring that opportunity remains within reach for future generations.
Fiscal Responsibility
The long-term stability of the American economy depends on responsible fiscal policy.
For decades, the federal government has continued to spend far beyond its means, producing trillion-dollar deficits and a rapidly growing national debt.
While the effects of this borrowing may not be immediately visible, the reality is that an economy built on permanent deficit spending rests on weak foundations.
Rising debt will eventually crowd out investment, increase interest costs, and place a heavier burden on future generations.
Addressing this problem requires more than simply cutting small discretionary programs.
The vast majority of federal spending occurs through mandatory programs and structural commitments that continue to grow automatically each year.
Meaningful fiscal reform must focus on these long-term drivers of federal spending.
Michael Mebruer supports a comprehensive approach to restoring fiscal discipline.
His proposals focus on restructuring major entitlement programs to ensure their long-term sustainability, simplifying the federal tax code to promote economic growth, and reducing unnecessary federal bureaucracy.
By addressing the structural causes of deficit spending rather than relying on short-term fixes, these reforms aim to place the federal budget on a more stable path.
Restoring fiscal discipline is not simply an accounting exercise.
Responsible budgeting protects economic stability, encourages investment and innovation, and ensures that future generations inherit a stronger and more sustainable American economy.
Protection Of Life
The protection of innocent human life is one of the most fundamental responsibilities of a just society.
Michael Mebruer believes that every human life has inherent dignity and value, and that this principle must guide public policy.
Advances in science have made it increasingly clear that human life begins at conception, and that the unborn child is a distinct human being deserving of protection.
For decades, abortion has been treated in American politics as a matter of personal choice alone.
However, Michael believes that a free society must also recognize that freedom carries moral responsibilities.
A culture that fails to defend the most vulnerable ultimately weakens the moral foundations that sustain families and communities.
Michael supports policies that protect the unborn while also strengthening the institutions that support mothers, children, and families.
Protecting life should not only involve legal protections for the unborn, but also policies that make it easier for families to welcome children into the world and raise them in stable, supportive environments.
Following the Supreme Court’s decision to return abortion policy to the democratic process, the issue is once again being debated openly across the country.
Michael believes that elected leaders have a responsibility to defend the value of human life and work toward policies that reflect that principle.
At its core, the pro-life position affirms a simple but profound idea: every human life has worth.
A society that recognizes the dignity of the unborn demonstrates its commitment to protecting the most vulnerable and upholding the moral foundations upon which a healthy and compassionate nation depends.
Immigration
The federal government has a fundamental responsibility to secure the nation’s borders and enforce immigration law.
For too long, Washington has failed to meet this responsibility, allowing millions of individuals to enter and remain in the country illegally.
When immigration laws are not enforced, the rule of law is weakened and public confidence in government declines.
Michael Mebruer believes that the American dream and the opportunities it represents must be protected for American citizens and for those who follow the legal immigration process.
A functioning immigration system must be orderly, lawful, and fair.
Immigration should occur through legal channels, not through a system that rewards those who bypass the law.
Ending illegal immigration requires more than rhetoric about border security.
It requires removing the incentives that drive illegal entry in the first place.
One of the primary reasons individuals enter the country illegally is the ability to obtain employment.
Michael supports mandatory nationwide implementation of the E-Verify system so that employers can quickly and reliably confirm that workers are legally authorized to work in the United States.
When properly enforced, E-Verify makes the hiring of illegal labor extremely difficult.
Employers who knowingly hire illegal workers should face clear and meaningful penalties.
Businesses that follow the law should not have to compete with those who cut costs by ignoring immigration rules.
Enforcing workplace verification and employer accountability is essential to restoring integrity to the immigration system.
Combined with stronger border enforcement and consistent application of immigration law, these policies would significantly reduce illegal immigration while preserving a legal system that welcomes those who respect the laws of the United States.
Healthcare
America’s healthcare system faces growing challenges, particularly within the federal programs that serve millions of Americans.
Medicare and Medicaid represent some of the largest and fastest-growing areas of federal spending, and without meaningful reform, they will place increasing strain on both the federal budget and the long-term sustainability of the programs themselves.
Michael Mebruer believes healthcare policy should focus on improving efficiency, expanding patient choice, and ensuring that these programs remain financially sustainable for future generations.
Rather than relying on a system dominated by bureaucracy and complex regulations, healthcare reform should encourage competition, transparency, and innovation within the private sector.
Michael supports transitioning Medicare toward a system that gives seniors greater flexibility when choosing their healthcare coverage while maintaining strong protections for beneficiaries.
By allowing individuals to select from competing private plans, the system can create incentives for insurers to offer better coverage and lower costs while preserving access to care for retirees.
Medicaid also requires structural reform.
Michael supports restructuring the federal role in Medicaid to give states more flexibility while expanding access to private insurance options for low-income Americans.
By using market-based mechanisms and simplifying the program’s structure, Medicaid can provide coverage more efficiently while reducing unnecessary federal spending.
Healthcare reform should ultimately aim to empower patients rather than government bureaucracies.
By encouraging competition, simplifying federal programs, and focusing on long-term sustainability, these reforms would improve the quality of care while placing America’s healthcare system on a more stable path for the future.
Economic Growth and Tax Reform
A strong economy depends on policies that reward work, encourage investment, and allow businesses and workers to succeed without unnecessary government interference.
Michael Mebruer believes the current federal tax code has become overly complex, inefficient, and filled with special provisions that benefit well-connected interests rather than ordinary Americans.
Over the years, thousands of pages of deductions, credits, and exemptions have turned the tax system into a tool for political favoritism.
Large corporations and special interest groups often have the resources to navigate these loopholes, while working Americans are left facing a confusing system that is difficult to understand and costly to comply with.
Michael supports comprehensive tax reform that simplifies the tax code and reduces the overall tax burden on American workers.
A simpler tax structure makes the system more transparent, easier to follow, and harder for special interests to manipulate.
By eliminating unnecessary loopholes and reducing complexity, tax policy can focus on treating taxpayers more equally while encouraging economic growth.
Michael also believes that success should not be punished.
Tax policy should encourage productivity, innovation, and entrepreneurship rather than discouraging individuals from working harder or investing in new opportunities.
When Americans are allowed to keep more of what they earn, they are more likely to invest, start businesses, and create jobs.
A simpler and fairer tax system benefits the entire economy.
By reducing complexity, removing special-interest advantages, and promoting pro-growth policies, Michael’s tax reform proposals aim to strengthen the American economy and expand opportunity for working families across the country.
