Meet Nate
Nate Uldricks’ Northwest Indiana roots run strong.
Born to a working class family, Nate graduated from Portage High School and became a Purdue engineer.
Nate’s a proud father and husband, serves as Pine Township Board Chairman, and is a volunteer firefighter.
He previously served in management roles at the White House and U.S.
Department of Defense.
