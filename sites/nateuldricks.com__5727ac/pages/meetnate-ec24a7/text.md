Meet Nate
Nate Uldricks’ Northwest Indiana roots run strong.
Nate Uldricks is a 3rd-generation Indiana 4th District citizen who was born and raised in a working class, military family in Portage, IN.
Nate is a proud father and husband, serves as Pine Township Board Chairman, and is a volunteer firefighter.
He previously served in management roles at the White House and U.S.
Department of Defense where he led or was involved with major transformational efforts to cut waste and drive efficiencies that saved taxpayers billions of dollars.
Nate’s priorities as your State Senator will be building our manufacturing economy, lowering everyday costs for Indiana families, strengthening public safety, and protecting and preserving our dunes, lakes and farms.
Nate serves as a county leader as the Chair of the Pine Township Board.
Under his leadership the township has been very active and productive in modernizing and digitizing township operations, stewarding and growing township funds, improving transparency and community relations, strengthening public safety, and organizing a Wreaths Across America for the veterans laid to rest in the township cemetery.
Nate and his wife met on an airplane and they have one son who was born severely premature, weighing only one and a half pounds.
Nate is a graduate of Portage High School, and earned a BS in Industrial Engineering from Purdue University, MA in Government from Johns Hopkins University, and MBA from the University of Virginia.
