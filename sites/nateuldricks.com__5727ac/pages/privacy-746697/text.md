We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how you can manage your information.
This Privacy Policy applies only to information collected through our website(s) and digital platforms, and not to information collected offline or through other means.
Information We Collect User-Provided Information You may provide us with personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and other details when you: Sign up for updates Make a donation Volunteer or request information Contact us via forms, email, or text We may retain communications you send to us, including email messages and website submissions.
Third-Party Services Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help improve website functionality.
You may adjust your browser settings to refuse cookies, though some features of the site may not function properly.
We may also use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand website traffic and usage.
These tools collect information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, subject to their own privacy policies.
How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Operate and improve our website and campaign efforts Communicate with you about events, updates, and campaign news Process donations and maintain compliance with applicable laws Send text messages, emails, and other communications (with your consent) If you choose to provide any personal information, such as your mailing address or phone number, we may use that information to contact you.
By providing your contact information, you consent to receive communications from the Campaign.
You may opt out at any time using unsubscribe links or by contacting us directly.
We may retain your information for a reasonable period for legal, compliance, and archival purposes.
How We Share Your Information Personally Identifiable Information We do not sell or rent your personal information.
We may share your information with trusted vendors and service providers (such as payment processors or email platforms) solely to support campaign operations.
These parties are required to protect your information.
We may also disclose information if required by law or if necessary to: Comply with legal obligations Protect the rights and safety of the Campaign or others Prevent fraud or illegal activity Any information you voluntarily post publicly (e.g., comments or testimonials) may be visible to others.
How We Protect Your Information We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.
However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account information and communications.
Compromise of Personal Information In the event of a data breach, we will notify affected individuals as required by applicable law.
Your Choices You may: Opt out of email or text communications at any time Request access to or correction of your personal information Decline to provide personal information (though some services may be limited) To make requests, please contact us through the website or campaign contact information.
Children’s Privacy Our website is not intended for children under the age of 17.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 17.
If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information promptly.
Links to Other Websites Our website may contain links to third-party websites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.
Notification Procedures We may provide notices via email, website postings, text messages, or other means.
You may opt out of certain communications where permitted.
Text Messaging Policy Mobile Information will not be shared.
All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.
We do not sell or share your text messaging opt-in data or consent with third parties, except as required by law.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Message and data rates may apply.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Changes will be posted on this page and are effective immediately upon posting.
