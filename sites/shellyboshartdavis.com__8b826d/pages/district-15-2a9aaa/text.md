House District 15
I'm proud to have grown up in such an amazing place.
House District 15 includes a large portion of Linn County and a small part of Marion and Benton Counties.
From Millersburg to Tangent, from the outskirts of Corvallis to the edge of Lebanon with Albany in the middle, there is a wide range of issues that face our district.
I am proud to be your voice.
