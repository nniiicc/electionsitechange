Protecting Voter Access
Why This Matters
Every eligible voter in LD28 deserves a simple, secure, and reliable way to participate in our democracy.
When voting becomes harder, it’s not an accident — it’s a strategy.
Seniors, working families, and everyday Arizonans pay the price when politicians create barriers instead of removing them.
What’s at Stake
- Seniors in Sun City and Sun City West rely heavily on mail‑in ballots
- Working families depend on early voting and flexible options
- Automatic voter registration reduces errors and expands participation
- Restrictive policies silence the very people who keep our democracy alive
My Commitments
- Support automatic voter registration for all eligible Arizonans
- Protect vote‑by‑mail, early voting, and secure drop boxes
- Oppose any attempt to restrict access under the guise of “security”
- Strengthen ballot transparency and integrity without limiting access
- Ensure clear, accessible voting options for every LD28 resident
What This Means for LD28
Our district thrives when every voice is heard.
Protecting voter access isn’t a partisan issue — it’s a community issue.
I will fight to ensure that every voter, regardless of age, mobility, or schedule, can participate fully and confidently.
