Education: Stability, Skills, and a Future Arizona Can Count On
Why This Matters
Arizona’s education system is being pulled in two directions: families want stability, teachers want support, and employers want graduates with real‑world skills — yet the state continues to face uncertainty driven by unpredictable funding and an unregulated voucher system.
Many Arizonans have raised concerns that rapid voucher expansion has created budget instability and diverted resources away from public classrooms that serve the majority of students.
Planning for the future requires a system that is transparent, accountable, and built to prepare young people for a complicated world.
What’s at Stake
- Public schools face financial uncertainty when voucher costs fluctuate
- Families need reliable, stable school systems they can plan around
- Students must be prepared for a world shaped by technology, global competition, and rapid change
- Employers across Arizona are calling for graduates with durable, transferable skills
- Life‑skills education — financial literacy, communication, problem‑solving — is increasingly essential
- Communities depend on schools that are safe, well‑staffed, and focused on long‑term success
My Commitments
- Support efforts to rein in voucher expansion by requiring transparency, accountability, and predictable budgeting
- Promote durable skills education — critical thinking, communication, adaptability, and problem‑solving
- Strengthen life‑skills programs including financial literacy, digital literacy, and practical career preparation
- Advocate for stable, long‑term funding that allows schools to plan responsibly
- Encourage partnerships with community colleges, trade programs, and employers to prepare students for a complex economy
- Support teachers and staff with the resources they need to deliver high‑quality instruction
- Ensure that every Arizona student has access to a safe, supportive learning environment
What This Means for Arizona
A strong education system is the backbone of a strong state.
Arizona’s future depends on schools that are stable, transparent, and focused on preparing students for real life — not just test scores.
By promoting durable skills, life‑skills education, and responsible oversight of voucher spending, Arizona can build an education system that supports families, strengthens communities, and prepares the next generation for a rapidly changing world.
