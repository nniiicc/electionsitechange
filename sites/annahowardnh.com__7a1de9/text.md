Ward 1: Portsmouth & Newington Anna Howard for NH State Representative As a CPA, an MBA, and an educator, I understand how budgets, taxes, public policy, and institutions shape people’s daily lives.
I also understand that justice begins with economic justice.
A community cannot truly thrive when working families cannot afford housing, health care, education, child care, or a secure retirement.
I believe democracy depends on informed citizens, honest leadership, and active civic engagement.
I am running for State Representative because I want to bring both professional expertise and deeply held values to public service: integrity, compassion, accountability, fairness, and respect for every person.
Portsmouth and Newington are special communities, and I want to help protect what makes them strong while ensuring that opportunity, dignity, and a good quality of life remain within reach for generations to come.
