About Scott
Scott Slaugh is a fifth-generation Coloradan, Army Reserve Lieutenant Colonel, small business owner, and devoted husband and father currently serving Colorado State House District 64.
With deep roots in Northern Colorado communities like Johnstown, Milliken, Greeley, and Berthoud, Scott has built his life around service, hard work, faith, and conservative values.
Today, he is fighting at the State Capitol to protect constitutional rights, defend taxpayers, support veterans and families, and preserve Colorado’s small-town way of life.
