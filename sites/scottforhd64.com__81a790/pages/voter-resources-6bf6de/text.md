Please drop your ballot off or vote in-person in the county where you are registered to vote.
WHO CAN VOTE: Any voter in Colorado State Representative District 64 can vote for Scott Slaugh.
This includes most residents of the town of Berthoud, Johnstown, Milliken, Mead, and some residents of Greeley.
Learn more about the district here .
HOW TO VOTE: Ballots will begin arriving in mailboxes October 3, 2026.
If you do not receive one, contact your county clerk.
Every voter will receive a mail ballot.
Ballots can be returned either by mail or via any ballot drop-box in the county where you live.
You can vote in-person beginning October 19, 2026 (take your ballot with you if you vote in-person).
Note: Each person can return up to 10 ballots (including their own) for other people such as other voters living in the household, family or friends, and neighbors.
REGISTER TO VOTE: You can register to vote and vote up until 7pm on Election Day.
You can register online or in-person at a polling location.
UPDATE VOTER REGISTRATION: If you have moved recently, be sure to check that your voter registration is accurate and update it if needed.
You can update your voter registration even if a ballot was already mailed to your prior address as long a you did not cast that ballot.
You will be mailed a new ballot after your voter registration address is updated and the other ballot will be automatically voided.
