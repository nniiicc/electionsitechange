Colorado House District 64 Voter Resources
IMPORTANT: There are separate ballot drop-box and polling locations for Weld and Larimer counties.
Please drop your ballot off or vote in-person in the county where you are registered to vote.
Every voter will receive a mail ballot.
Ballots can be returned either by mail or via any ballot drop-box in the county where you live.
You can vote in-person beginning October 19, 2026 (take your ballot with you if you vote in-person).
Note: Each person can return up to 10 ballots (including their own) for other people such as other voters living in the household, family or friends, and neighbors.
