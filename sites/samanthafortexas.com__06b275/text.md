For years, she has served at the Texas Capitol, working alongside leaders like State Representative Donna Howard to champion issues that matter to everyday Texans — from strengthening our public schools and protecting access to healthcare, to defending workers' rights and making government more accountable to the people it serves.
As Chief of Staff to Rep.
Howard, Samantha has led the charge on legislation that invests in our teachers, supports working families, and ensures our tax dollars are used wisely.
Her work is rooted in listening to the people she serves, building consensus, and never forgetting who she's fighting for.
The daughter of immigrants, Samantha is a proud Latina and first-generation college graduate.
Because of her roots, she is dedicated to opening doors for others and shaping policy that reflects the diverse voices of Texas.
She knows the value of opportunity, perseverance, and community.
She believes every family in Austin deserves a fair shot — whether that means quality schools, affordable healthcare, safe neighborhoods, or the chance to build a secure future.
Now, Samantha is ready to bring her experience, passion, and commitment to the Texas House of Representatives.
With a fresh voice and a deep love for her community, she's running to be a strong, independent, and progressive advocate for all of Austin — someone who will listen, lead, and deliver results for every voice in District 50.
Together, we can build a better Texas for everyone.
