Stop the Tax Surge
The 2025 Legislature imposed the largest tax increase in Washington state history — nearly $12 billion in new taxes jammed through in a few weeks.
Starting October 1, 2025, the sales tax expands to services that have never been taxed: advertising, IT support, security, staffing, even driver's education.
More than 90,000 small businesses must now collect and remit sales tax on services that were always exempt.
Meanwhile, Democrats passed a 9.9% income tax and hiked the B&O tax on employers already barely breaking even.

In manufacturing, you don't waste a dollar.
In government, we shouldn't waste your money either."
