Endorsements
“It is my pleasure to announce that I am endorsing Doug Welton in his run for Utah House District 65.
In addition to his role as a public servant, Doug currently works as an educator.
His commitment to bringing up the next generation of independent thinkers is the kind of leadership Utah needs.
Doug isn’t one to shy away from tough subjects, and he never takes the facts at face value.
Instead, he spends extensive time asking questions and researching complex issues.
This allows for a better understanding of our citizens’ concerns.
After many years in various leadership roles, I am confident that Doug’s diverse service to our community makes him a strong candidate we can all rely on.
When America is at a precipice, conservative principles guide us back to prosperity.
Utah needs more leaders like Doug Welton.
I have no doubts that he will continue to serve with integrity and transparency.”
Congressman Burgess Owens
Utah County Sheriff Mike Smith
“As a strong advocate of law enforcement, Doug has always been there to support the officers and law enforcement agencies of Utah county.
Doug understands the need to build strong relationships with the community and keep those who bring harm to our communities and neighbors off the streets.

Payson Mayor Bill Wright
“I’m proud to endorse Doug Welton.
As Payson City’s Mayor it was my honor to work with him as a City Councilman.
I served with him for a total of four years and witnessed firsthand his dedication to the citizens of Payson.
I can say without any reservation that he is devoted to the citizens of this Great State of Utah and specifically the citizens of District 65.
Doug is willing to study the issues in depth.
He understands the needs and wants of our citizens and has an open-door policy.
As a Payson City Councilman he was not only willing to listen to all sides of an issue he routinely met with our citizens and maintained an active Facebook presence.
He kept our citizens informed of pending city council actions and plans.
Doug has always been very approachable on any issue, controversial or routine.
I feel very comfortable with Doug representing me and you, as a strong conservative voice in District 65.”
