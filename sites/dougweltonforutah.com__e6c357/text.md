Meet Doug
Doug Welton has been a committed member of our community.
He has served our students a High School teacher and award-winning debate coach, spent time understanding local government as a member of the Payson City Council, and now serves us a member of the Utah State House.

As our state representative, Doug has focused on delivering record-breaking conservative results.
From the largest teacher pay raise and income tax cut in Utah history, to reducing burdensome regulations, Doug delivers for our community.
