Constance Menzies is a dedicated community leader with a longstanding commitment to service, integrity, and advocacy.
She is running for State Assembly to deliver practical solutions, responsive leadership, and a stronger voice for the people of her district.

Her campaign is built on the belief that government should work for the people, not around them.
Constance is committed to bringing people together, strengthening neighborhoods, and helping create a future where every resident has the opportunity to thrive.
