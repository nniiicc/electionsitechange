Susan came to the United States from Laos as a three-year-old refugee, orphaned after her father died in the Vietnam War.
She grew up in poverty — and was lifted by the love of family, the kindness of community, and a belief that everyone deserves the chance to thrive.
In 2022 she became the first person of color to represent her district.
After two terms on the Brooklyn Park City Council (2016–2022) — including serving as Mayor Pro Tem — Susan now serves in the Minnesota Senate, where she's Vice Chair of the Capital Investment Committee and a member of the Housing & Homelessness Prevention and Transportation committees.
