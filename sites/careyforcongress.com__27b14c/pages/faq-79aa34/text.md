Find answers to some of the most common questions from voters about Mike's priorities, campaign, and ways to get involved.
Why is Mike Carey running for Congress?
Mike Carey is running to continue fighting for lower taxes, a stronger economy, secure borders, affordable energy, and safer communities while delivering results for Ohio's 15th Congressional District.
What are Mike Carey's top priorities?
Mike's priorities include making life more affordable, protecting Social Security, strengthening Ohio communities, supporting veterans and first responders, reducing government spending, and growing the American economy.
What district does Mike Carey represent?
Mike Carey proudly represents Ohio's 15th Congressional District, serving families, businesses, farmers, and communities throughout central and western Ohio.
What political party is Mike Carey?
Mike Carey is a Republican committed to conservative principles, fiscal responsibility, economic growth, and protecting individual freedoms.
How can I get involved with the campaign?
Supporters can volunteer, attend campaign events, display a yard sign, or make a contribution to help elect Mike Carey.
How can I stay up to date on the campaign?
Sign up for campaign updates and follow Mike Carey on social media for the latest news, events, and announcements.
How can I support Mike Carey's campaign?
You can support the campaign by volunteering, donating, sharing campaign updates, or encouraging friends and family to vote.
