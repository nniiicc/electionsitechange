Education
- Every South Dakotan should have access to high quality free public education.
- I will fight for drastic increases to public education funds to ensure we are attracting and keeping the best teachers to teach in this state.
- If public funds are going to private institutions, then those institutions need to have public oversight, just like their free public counterparts.
- If we want our children to succeed in school, we must invest in their success.
- I will continue to fight for free school meals at our public schools
- According to the National Library of Medicine, "The strongest evidence of an effect of food insecurity has been found in academic/cognitive outcomes and externalizing behaviors"
- According to No Kid Hungry, 14% and 17% of children are food insecure in Union and Clay County respectively.
