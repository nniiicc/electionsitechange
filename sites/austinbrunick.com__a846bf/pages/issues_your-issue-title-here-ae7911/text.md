Fair Labor Practices
- Abolish "Right-To-Work" and ensure businesses are properly punished for union busting.
- Establish a minimum living wage indexed to inflation, and eliminate subminimum wage exemptions
- Paid family medical leave (FMLA) for all workers
- Pass wage transparency laws that require employers to disclose salary ranges in job postings and prohibit retaliation against workers who discuss compensation.
- Ensure that the current workforce is protected against the implementation of AI as a replacement for the labor pool.
- Fight against the effort to dissolve the current child labor standards.
