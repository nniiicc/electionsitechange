I am proud to say I am a strong supporter of Medicare for All.

View more Fair Labor Practices For far too long South Dakotans have been working with limited worker protections and the threat of being fired without reason.
Right-to-Work was created with the specific purpose of busting unions and ensuring a "Sword of Damocles" of being fired was always present over the head of workers who were thinking about organizing their workplaces.
View more South Dakota's Tax Problem South Dakota's current tax system does not help the average South Dakotan.
South Dakota has the 6th most regressive tax system in the nation.
A regressive tax is a tax that takes a larger percentage of income from low-income earners than from high-income earners.
Its time we built a tax system that benefits South Dakotans and not the ultra-weathy using South Dakota as a tax haven.
