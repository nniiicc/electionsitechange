Skip navigation menu About Issues Volunteer Contact Donate About Issues Volunteer Contact Donate AI and Data Centers Education Healthcare Fair Labor Practices South Dakota's Tax Problem AI and Data Centers Data centers have become a hot button topic this year and for good reason.
Residents who live close to these data centers are reporting dizziness, nausea, anxiety, and more.
In rural areas where data centers are being built they are using natural-gas-powered turbines to generate the enormous amount of power needed to keep these data centers running.
The sound of these turbines can be heard many miles away.
The construction of data centers has been proven to be harmful as well.
In Morgan County, Georgia the water is not drinkable (More Perfect Union May 18, 2026) In Coweta County, Georgia Ansley Brown and her family were forced to sell the house she grew up in or risk having the land be taken via Eminent Domain because Georgia Power is planning to build a new transmission line to power data centers.
We must pass a state wide moratorium on all generative AI and crypto mining data centers until we learn what the long term effects these data centers are.
