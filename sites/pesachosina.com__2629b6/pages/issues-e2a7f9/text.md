Pesach Osina will fight to hold insurance and utility companies accountable, expand tax relief for middle-class families, and make childcare more affordable.
Read Pesach's Plan Improving Public Safety Every family deserves to feel safe in their neighborhood.
Pesach will support law enforcement, fight to hold repeat offenders accountable, strengthen laws addressing organized and repeat retail theft, and work to ensure small businesses, workers, and families are protected.
Read Pesach's Plan Fixing Transportation South Queens families spend too much time dealing with long and unreliable commutes.
Pesach will fight to protect the Cross Bay Bridge rebate, move QueensLink forward, and expand access to reduced transit fares for working and middle-class families.
Read Pesach's Plan Protecting Seniors & Retirees Protect pensions, address Tier 6, lower healthcare and prescription costs, strengthen property tax relief, and help seniors remain in their homes.
Read Pesach's Plan Supporting Veterans Bring veteran services directly into the district and make benefits, healthcare support, claims assistance, and other resources easier to access.
Read Pesach's Plan Resiliency & Emergency Preparedness Strengthen coastal protections, speed up resiliency projects, improve emergency planning, and ensure our communities receive the resources they need before, during, and after emergencies.
Read Pesach's Plan Responsible Housing Protect community input, support homeowners, oppose development that ignores local concerns, and help longtime residents afford to remain in their neighborhoods.
Read Pesach's Plan Fighting Hate Improve coordination and reporting of hate incidents and ensure victims have meaningful, accessible support.
Read Pesach's Plan Animal Protection Strengthen laws and enforcement addressing animal cruelty and hold offenders accountable.
