Protecting our Seniors and Retirees
Protecting Pensions & Fixing Tier 6
Retirement security shouldn’t be up for debate.
Pesach will:
- Fight to keep state pension systems fully funded and protected
- Work to fix Tier 6, which has made it harder for public workers to retire with security, by improving benefits and making the system fairer
- Support policies that strengthen long-term pension stability
People who serve our communities shouldn’t be penalized when it comes to retirement.
Lowering Healthcare & Prescription Costs
Healthcare costs are one of the biggest burdens for seniors.
Pesach will:
- Push to lower out-of-pocket costs, including premiums and co-pays
- Support efforts to bring down prescription drug prices
- Strengthen oversight of insurance providers to prevent excessive increases
No one should have to choose between their health and their savings.
Helping Seniors Stay in Their Homes
For many seniors, staying in their home is everything.
Pesach will:
- Expand and protect property tax relief programs like STAR and senior exemptions
- Advocate for additional relief for long-time homeowners facing rising costs
- Support programs that help seniors age in place safely
Seniors shouldn’t be priced out of the communities they built.
Protecting Seniors from Fraud & Abuse
Scams and financial abuse are a growing threat.
Pesach will:
- Strengthen protections against deed theft, title fraud, and financial scams
- Support better enforcement and faster response for victims
- Increase public awareness and prevention efforts
