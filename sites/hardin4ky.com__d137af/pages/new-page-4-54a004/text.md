As your candidate for State Representative in District 33, I believe that every decision, every dollar, and every vote should be open to the people it impacts.
Too often, communities like ours have been left in the dark while legislatures make choices without listening.
I’m committed to changing that.
Open Communication: I will keep constituents informed through regular updates, public forums, and accessible channels for feedback.
Responsible Stewardship: Every campaign dollar will be accounted for, with fundraising and spending reported clearly and honestly.
Legislative Accountability: I will stand by my votes and explain them, ensuring you know not just how I voted, but why.
Community First: Decisions will be guided by the needs of Jefferson, Oldham, and Shelby counties and not special interests or political insiders.
Transparency isn’t just a promise.
It’s a practice.
Accountability isn’t just a word.
It’s a responsibility.
Together, we can build a government that earns and keeps the trust of the people it serves.
