Folder: Volunteer Back Donations Donation Transparency Voter Pledge Form Get Involved Yard Signs Contact Donate View fullsize I was born in Louisville, Kentucky, and raised in Jeffersontown until my family moved to Oldham County, where I graduated from South Oldham High School.
I earned my BA in Government with an emphasis in Regional Analysis and Public Policy from Morehead State University and later pursued a master’s in public administration while raising a special needs child.
In 2016, I founded my first business, a Kentucky‑based gift company that designs and handcrafts items made from bourbon barrel staves and products featuring local photography.
In 2019, I launched my sock company, which began with Kentucky‑themed designs and has since expanded to offer products in 22 states, reaching customers across the U.S., Canada, and Europe.
As a mother, I enjoy spending time with my son on creative projects that encourage learning and growth.
I’m also an animal lover with three Boston terriers, including a rescue named Hamilton who joined our family in 2021.
I recently joined the board of Second Chances Wildlife and I’m working with some of the most dedicated people to ensure the recovery of Kentucky’s Wildlife.
As a business owner, I prioritize fair workplaces and employee well‑being.
I support labor rights and foster an environment where my team can contribute ideas and grow professionally.
My background in public policy, entrepreneurship, and community engagement shapes my commitment to helping build a future where individuals and families can thrive.
I've spent my life in the communities that make up District 33, as a student, a mother, a small business owner, and a community leader.
Like many families, I've seen firsthand the challenges of rising costs, overcrowded schools, and decisions being made without enough input from the people they affect.
I'm running because I believe government should listen to the people it serves, focus on practical solutions, and put our communities first.
Whether it's protecting neighborhoods from poorly planned development, supporting public education, or helping Kentucky families get ahead, I'll bring a commonsense approach and a willingness to work with anyone to get results.
District 33 deserves a representative who is accessible, accountable, and focused on the issues that matter most to the people who live here.
