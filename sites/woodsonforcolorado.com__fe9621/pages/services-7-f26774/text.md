Economic Development
Building destinations in Aurora that make us a true Twin City to Denver — through arts, culture, and community.
Invest in Arts & Cultural Infrastructure.
Protect and expand state investment in community-based arts and cultural spaces.
Arts and culture are powerful economic drivers that create jobs, attract tourism, and strengthen community identity.
As a State Representative, I will fight to:
- Secure state funding for historic and community arts venues like the Aurora Fox Arts Center
- Expand access to Colorado Creative Industries and cultural infrastructure grants
- Ensure investments prioritize local hiring, youth programming, and community access
Strong arts infrastructure strengthens small businesses, supports working artists, and brings long-term economic activity into our neighborhoods.
As Your Legislator, I Will:
- Support legislation that enables state funding for multi-use community facilities and event spaces
- Advance public-private partnerships tied to clear community benefit standards
- Establish tax incentives, similar to those used to attract major festivals like Sundance, to bring international film festivals to Aurora and boost local economic activity.
- Prioritize local workforce participation and small business inclusion in state-funded projects
- Ensure economic development policies promote equitable access and long-term community benefit
This approach ensures development strengthens community pride, creates jobs, and supports local economies — not just outside interests.
