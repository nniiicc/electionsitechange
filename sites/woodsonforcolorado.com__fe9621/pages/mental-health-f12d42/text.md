Building Colorado’s Mental Health Hub in Aurora
Aurora is uniquely positioned to become Colorado’s leading hub for mental health treatment — strengthening and expanding the state’s behavioral health system while complementing the work already underway in Pueblo.
With major medical campuses, a diverse population, and central access across the metro region, House District 42 is the right place for a state-of-the-art mental health facility that provides crisis stabilization, inpatient and long-term care, culturally informed services, and comprehensive wraparound support.
This vision recognizes Pueblo’s statewide behavioral health office as a critical partner, while establishing Aurora as the primary location for Colorado’s state mental health hospital, with Pueblo serving as a satellite facility.
Together, this model creates a coordinated, accessible system that better meets the needs of communities across the state.
A centralized mental health hub in Aurora is not just about infrastructure — it’s about dignity, access, and outcomes.
It’s about building a system that works for people in crisis and supports long-term recovery, while ensuring no community is left behind.
As Your Legislator, I Will:
- Advocate for Aurora to serve as Colorado’s primary state mental health hospital location, with Pueblo as a key satellite partner
- Secure state funding for crisis stabilization, inpatient, and long-term behavioral health care capacity
- Ensure culturally responsive, community-informed mental health services are built into facility design and care delivery
- Strengthen coordination between hospitals, community providers, and state agencies to improve access and outcomes
- Prioritize workforce investment to recruit and retain mental health professionals
- Center dignity, access, and long-term recovery in all behavioral health policy decisions
This approach builds a coordinated statewide system that meets people where they are, responds to crisis with care, and supports lasting recovery—while ensuring every community benefits.
