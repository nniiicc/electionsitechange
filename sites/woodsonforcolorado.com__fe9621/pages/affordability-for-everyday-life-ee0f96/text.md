Affordability for Everyday Life
Affordability in House District 42 is about more than housing costs — it’s about whether families can keep up with the everyday expenses of living, working, and raising a family in Aurora.
Too many residents are being squeezed by rising costs in housing, transportation, childcare, healthcare, and utilities, with little room to get ahead.
As your legislator, I will focus on practical, people-first solutions that reduce financial pressure, strengthen household stability, and help families stay rooted in the communities they call home.
As Your Legislator, I Will:
- Protect renters and homeowners from displacement by strengthening renter protections and preserving pathways to homeownership
- Address the impact of corporate ownership in residential neighborhoods to promote fair housing markets and community stability
- Build more entry-level homes people can actually afford by promoting high-quality manufactured housing — homes built efficiently and placed on permanent foundations
- Re-examine construction defect laws to responsibly reduce barriers to building condominiums and expand attainable homeownership options
- Reduce everyday costs by supporting utility affordability protections and consumer safeguards against excessive fees
- Expand affordable transportation options so families aren’t forced to rely on multiple vehicles to get to work, school, or healthcare
- Support workforce pathways, apprenticeships, and predictable scheduling to improve income stability
- Expand access to affordable childcare and family supports for working parents
- Lower healthcare and mental health costs by investing in prevention, community care, and crisis services
Affordability means security, stability, and opportunity.
My goal is to ensure Aurora remains a place where working families, seniors, and young people can afford to live, work, and build a future — not just get by.
Sarah Woodson
