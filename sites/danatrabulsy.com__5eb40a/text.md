St.
Lucie County has been my home for over 33 years.
My husband, Paul, and I have raised 4 children together, and are proud grandparents to 7 grandchildren.
I have worked for both corporate America and for myself as a small local business owner.

Throughout my life, I've focused on giving back to our community and supporting small businesses, our local economy, and the many organizations that serve the needs of our neighbors.
I ran for office six years ago on a platform that stressed the need for leaders who can effectively advocate for solutions to our challenges.
Leaders who understand that the success of our economy is tied to the health of our environment and that the most precious resource we can invest in is our children and their schools.
As your State Representative, that's exactly what I've focused on.
Over the past six years, I have been proud to support historic funding for our public schools and the preservation and restoration of our environment - all while balancing the budget, keeping taxes low, and growing our economy.
