HOME ABOUT PLAN for PA EVENTS VOLUNTEER CONTACT DONATE HOME ABOUT PLAN for PA EVENTS VOLUNTEER CONTACT DONATE ABOUT WENDY FINK Wendy Fink is a lifelong resident of York County with deep roots in the community she proudly serves.
She attended Red Lion Area School District and went on to earn a degree in Elementary Education from Millersville University.
As a mother and an educator, Wendy saw firsthand how policies pushed by career politicians in both parties too often put special interests ahead of Pennsylvania families and children.
That realization compelled her to step up, challenge the status quo, and run for office—determined to be a voice for everyday citizens, not the political establishment.
Since being elected, Wendy Fink has delivered a strong, principled conservative voice in Harrisburg.
She has consistently stood up to the radical liberal agenda and voted NO on legislation that threatens parental rights, public safety, and common-sense values.
Wendy remains committed to representing the people of the 94th District with integrity, accountability, and unwavering dedication to their beliefs.
