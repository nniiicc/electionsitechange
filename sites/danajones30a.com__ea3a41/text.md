DELEGATE DANA JONES
Delivering Results for Anne Arundel
Dana is District 30A’s voice in the Maryland General Assembly.
Described by the Capital Gazette as “one of the most prolific lawmakers” in the state, Delegate Dana Jones represents District 30A (Annapolis and Anne Arundel County).
Most recently, she led the charge battling back against Moms for Liberty as the lead sponsor of the Freedom to Read Act.
This historic legislation makes Maryland one of the first states in the Nation to protect the Freedom to Read and defend our literature and librarians from book bans!
