MARGARET ROSE HENRY For decades, I have had the privilege of serving the people of Delaware and witnessing firsthand what it takes to lead with integrity, compassion, and a tireless work ethic.
Today, I am enthusiastic about supporting Senator Nicole Poore.
In the years we served together in the Delaware State Senate, I saw Nicole’s dedication to her constituents and her unwavering commitment to our caucus.
Nicole understands the unique needs of our communities.
Her focus is always on making life better for everyone in our state.
She possesses the rare ability to listen with an open heart and then take decisive action in Dover.
Leadership is about loyalty, service, and a deep-seated belief in the potential of our neighbors.
Nicole Poore embodies these qualities every single day.
She has been a steadfast ally to her colleagues and a champion for her district, and I know she will continue to lead with the same passion and grace that she has shown throughout her career.
I am proud to stand with Nicole Poore, and I ask you to join me in supporting her continued service to the State of Delaware.
MAJOR GENERAL FRANCIS “FRANK” VAVALA (RET.) As an established Legislator, Senator Nicole Poore has demonstrated her character, leadership and commitment to her constituents in the 12th Senatorial District.
I know that we can all understand sacrifice, accountability and mission focused leadership, all values Senator Poore manifests every day.
I was blessed to have a 50 year career with the Military culminating with 18 years as the Adjutant General of the Delaware National Guard.
No one sacrifices more to keep us safe than members of our Armed Forces, Law Enforcement and our Emergency Response Community.
And no one has worked harder to support our heroes and their families, than my friend and your Senator, Nicole Poore.
She chairs our State’s Veterans Affairs Committee and was a former Senate Majority leader who was able to bring needed resources to the District.
Her record has been an outstanding example of public service of the highest degree validating your decision to elect her for the last 14 years.
In our State Capitol, Senator Poore has always been a leader on important legislation affecting all facets of Delaware life.
In the 12th District, you merely have to look around to witness her success in securing State funding for needed improvements, from parks to open space, to senior facilities and fire and paramedic services.
Senator Poore is there for her constituents!
She has always conducted her campaign forthrightly, concentrating on the issues.
She has never resorted to negative or personal attacks, that have unfortunately become all too commonplace in today’s world.
I admire you because all of you in the 12th District have made the right choice by electing a person who represents the best of our values.
Senator Nicole Poore has always fulfilled her duties as a public servant with honor and integrity.
She has earned my personal trust and I support her unequivocally.
I ask that you allow her to work on your behalf by voting for her in the upcoming primary.
