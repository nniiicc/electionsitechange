Ways to Get Involved
- Door Knocking & Canvassing – Help spread Senator Poore’s message by going door-to-door in the community.
Talk to voters, share important campaign updates, and help ensure every voice is heard.
- Phone Banking – Reach out to voters directly from the comfort of your home.
Phone banking is a great way to have meaningful conversations with constituents about the issues that matter most.
- Event Support – Join us at campaign events!
Whether it’s setting up, registering attendees, or sharing information, your help will ensure everything runs smoothly.
- Social Media Advocacy – Are you social media savvy?
Help amplify the campaign by sharing posts, engaging with supporters, and spreading the word online.
- Data Entry & Office Work – Behind-the-scenes work is essential!
Help keep the campaign organized by assisting with data entry, mailing lists, or other office tasks.
- Community Outreach & Voter Registration – Be a part of the effort to register new voters and engage with our local community.
This work is crucial for building support and ensuring high voter turnout.
- Host a House Party or Meet & Greet – Host an event in your home to introduce Senator Poore to friends and neighbors.
It’s a fun, personal way to build community support for the campaign!
