20 Years of SEED
Twenty years ago, Delaware launched the Student Excellence Equals Degree program (SEED), opening the door to college for countless students.
I sponsored SB 12 in 2021 to launch SEED+ – offering Delawareans the space and support needed to explore who they are, identify their strengths, and chart their future on their own timeline.
That flexibility is what makes this next chapter meaningful to our communities and workforce.
Being part of the legislation that expanded this program remains one of the greatest achievements of my career as a legislator, and I’m grateful to see the impact it makes every day.
I was honored to be featured on the Fall issue of the Delaware Tech Magazine with former Senator Harris McDowell and former Rep.
Nancy Wagner – the original sponsors of the SEED legislation.
Supporting Our Community
Everyone deserves to have access to warm, comforting meals — especially during the holidays when families gather together to celebrate the joys of the season.
Because of the incredible volunteers who showed up with huge hearts, more than 130 families went home with nutritious food and essential household supplies.
Teaming up with the Food Bank of Delaware, Councilman Brian Mattaway, and Speaker Melissa Minor-Brown for the Drive-Thru Mobile Food Pantry at Harvest Christian Academy reminded me why community matters so much.
I’m especially grateful to Pastor Ray and the Harvest Christian Academy community for welcoming us so openly.
And as always, the Food Bank of Delaware continues to inspire me with the way they show up for families across our state.

Tree Recycling
Don’t let your Christmas tree go to waste this year!
After the holiday season, hundreds of trees will make their way into our landfills where they will clog up space, instead of being repurposed for mulch, compost, and other useful material that is better and safer for our environment.
There are several places across New Castle County that will accept the disposal of your Christmas tree!

Paid Family and Medical Leave
Paid leave for Delaware workers begins on January 1, 2026.
Workers in our state deserve access to financial support as their life changes and major events occur.
We created the Delaware Paid Family and Medical Insurance Program so that workers can take paid leave to take care of their family, life, and other personal matters.
