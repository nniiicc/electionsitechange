One Month Left of the 153rd General Assembly!
June 13, 2026 admin Business & Economic Development/Policy , Economy , Health and Social Services , Health Care , News/Blog , Senator Poore 12th District Newsletter Comments are Closed 0 Friends and neighbors, As we head into the final weeks of the 153rd General Assembly, my colleagues and I are working hard to move important legislation across the finish line and continue advocating for the issues that matter most to Delaware families and communities.
Delaware’s regular legislative session runs each year from the second Tuesday in January through June 30.
If you’d like to follow along, you can watch Senate proceedings live or view recordings of past sessions.
You can also access daily Senate agendas , upcoming committee meetings, legislative schedules, and other legislative information online by clicking here .
As always, I value hearing from the people I serve.
Whether you have a question, concern, or an idea to share, please don’t hesitate to reach out.
You can contact me by email at nicole.poore@delaware.gov or by calling my Senate office at (302) 744-4164.
Thank you for the opportunity to serve you.
Sincerely, Nicole Poore Senator, District 12 News from Legislative Hall Our neighbors deserve a seat at the table when it comes to large-scale data center development in our communities.
Across the nation, these projects are being built on the backs of every day families who are left to shoulder the environmental and financial consequences, and that is why I introduced SB 312 .
As legislators, it is our responsibility to protect our residents, our environment, and the future we are leaving for the younger generation.
Economic development should never come at the expense of the people who call Delaware home, and it is my hope that this legislation cements those protections and expands transparency on these projects.
Thank you to Bay to Bay news for covering this legislation and the ongoing conversations being had across our state.
With the support of my colleagues in the House and Senate, I introduced and passed SCR 176 to recognize the importance of Diabetic Retinopathy screenings .
In April, the state of Delaware held its first-ever statewide screening event, expanding vision-care access for Delawareans living with diabetes.
The event was a success, and I had the pleasure of hosting two amazing optometrists at Legislative Hall for the passing of SCR 176.
Thank you to Dr.
Paula Ko , President of the Delaware Academy of Ophthalmology, and Dr.
Alissa Long , President of the Delaware Optometric Association for your advocacy and joining me in Dover.
For far too long, women’s menopause and perimenopause symptoms have consistently been dismissed and downplayed by medical professionals.
Rep.
Kam Smith and I introduced Senate Bill 319 because it is time for women’s pain to be heard, taken seriously, and supported through every stage of life.
SB 319 will bring Delaware into alignment with our neighboring states and make healthcare more accessible for Delawareans experiencing menopause and perimenopause symptoms.
I welcomed some very special students from Brandywine, Dover Air Base, and Cape Henlopen schools robotics team to join me in Legislative Hall.
These brilliant students all earned a chance to compete in the 2026 VEX Robotics World Championship last month to showcase their talent in building, coding, and problem-solving.
This competition is the largest of its kind, bringing the best and brightest students from over 60 countries together for a chance to become champions.Even to qualify for this competition takes tremendous talent, intelligence, and teamwork, and I am so proud to have hosted them in Dover to congratulate them on their success.
In District 12 and Beyond CELEBRATING PRIDE MONTH AT OLD NEW CASTLE The Delaware Sexuality and Gender Coalition (DSGC) held their first Pride Market pop-up in Old New Castle this month to kick off Pride Month!
This market brought together more than 100 vendors and businesses within the LGBTQ+ community to gather and celebrate this month dedicated to love, equality, and representation.
It was a joy to join neighbors and friends at this market to support our local businesses!
For more information on the DSGC, please visit their website here .
FORT DELAWARE TOUR WITH DNREC I was honored to join DNREC Secretary Greg Patterson at Fort Delaware as part of his “Amazing Park Race” journey across Delaware’s state parks.
Secretary Patterson set out to visit all 17 of Delaware’s beautiful state parks to learn more about their history, the communities they serve, and the important role they play throughout our state.
It was a pleasure to accompany him during his visit to Fort Delaware and share the rich history of this remarkable landmark, including its significance as Delaware’s second-oldest state park.
Fort Delaware is more than a historic site, it’s a source of pride for Delaware City and the surrounding communities.
Its rich history, educational opportunities, and tourism impact help connect visitors to Delaware’s past while supporting our local economy today.
Thank you, Secretary Patterson, for taking the time to experience and learn about one of Delaware’s treasured state parks.
DELAWARE CITY BICENTENNIAL CELEBRATION An amazing Delaware City Bicentennial celebration in the books!
Delaware City’s history is not just found in books or museums , it lives through its people.
Yesterday was a beautiful celebration of the past, present, and future of a town that continues to stand proud along the river it was built beside.
Delaware City has long been a gateway for commerce, transportation, and community for the families that live here.
We also had the honor of recognizing Polk Town, one of Delaware’s historically Black communities whose family and history have shaped the fabric of this community for generations.
I want to extend a heartfelt ‘thank you’ to the entire volunteer Bicentennial Committee, town council, community organizations, historians, and residents who worked tirelessly to make this celebration such a success.
And a very special recognition to Mary June, who has called Delaware City home for 89 years.
She is a living connection to the town’s story and spirit.
Your lifetime of memories and commitment to this community are truly something special.
CONGRATULATIONS DAVE MAJEWSKI I had the honor of recognizing Dave Majewski at his promotion ceremony as he is appointed to Career Fire Chief of the Goodwill Fire Company!
This is a historic accomplishment, marking this company’s first Career Fire Chief position, and it couldn’t have gone to someone more deserving.
David’s journey is a testament to dedication, service, and leadership.
As a third-generation member of Good Will and an active volunteer since 2008, he has spent nearly two decades answering the call to serve his community.
Through countless hours of training, emergency response, planning, and mentorship, he has helped shape the department into the strong organization it is today.
Congratulations, Chief Majewski and thank you for your years of service!
VISITING THE PORT PENN FIRE CO.
I also visited our local servicemen at the Port Penn Fire Co.
For over 70 years, this company has gathered local residents and volunteers to offer their protection for the residents of Port Penn.
Their commitment to public safety and community service is astounding, and I thank them for their time as we discussed pertinent topics on Port Penn and the surrounding neighborhoods.
Thank you Niko Vacula, Ed Sawicki, Alan Stuckert, Kevin Turner, Brian Dunkelberger, Steve DiVirgilio, Mark Willis, all of the volunteers and first responders who continue to answer the call and serve with pride.
RIVER ROAD READINESS CENTER GRAND OPENING After years of collaboration and advocacy, the 302nd Quartermaster Platoon of the Delaware Army National Guard finally has a proficient training facility for our servicemen and women!
The River Road Readiness Center was a joint venture between state and federal legislators to show support for our National Guard members and ensure they are equipped with the tools they need to successfully serve our country.
Thank you to the Bignell Watkins Hasser Architects, Century Engineering, and Whiting Turner for your years of work and attention to building this incredible facility, and to Gov.
Matt Meyer and Rep.
Melissa Minor-Brown for joining me at this celebration.
5K AT GLASGOW PARK For the last five years, the Delaware Athletic Trainer’s Association (DATA) has been hosting a 5k Coffee Run to fundraise for our state’s athletic trainers.
As a mom to high school athletes, I understand the necessity of our athletic trainers and the safety they bring to our state.
They are the ones present at every athletic event, game, and practice to ensure our children are protected from physical injuries at all times, and their roles are vital to our communities.
I was glad to continue my tradition of supporting DATA at their annual 5k and continue advocating for their association.
OLD NEW CASTLE DAY ANNUAL CELEBRATION Celebrating Old New Castle Day was a wonderful reminder of the history, charm, and community pride that make New Castle so special.
From the beautiful historic homes and gardens to the people who help preserve and share the story of this treasured town, it was a day filled with Delaware history and hometown spirit.
Thank you to everyone who helped make the day such a success!

Congratulations to Dr.
