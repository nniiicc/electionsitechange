Open Government Learn More Key Legislative Initiatives and Accomplishments Since first being elected to the Delaware State Senate, Nicole Poore has focused on one mission: fighting for working families, expanding opportunity, and delivering real results for Delaware .
Nicole’s Record of Results Expanded Free Education Opportunities – Championed Delaware’s SEED+ program, helping more Delawareans access tuition-free college and workforce training.
Raised Wages for Working Families – Supported increasing Delaware’s minimum wage and policies that help workers earn a fair paycheck.
Advanced Paid Family & Medical Leave – Helped make it possible for Delaware workers to care for loved ones without sacrificing their financial security.
Fought for Fair Pay for Workers with Disabilities – Led efforts to phase out subminimum wages and ensure greater workplace equity.
Protected Children from Predators – Sponsored legislation strengthening protections against sexual misconduct involving children.
Supported Veterans and First Responders – A longtime advocate for those who have served our nation and communities.
Invested in Delaware Communities – Helped secure funding for schools, public safety, infrastructure, parks, healthcare, and neighborhood improvements.
Proven Leadership.
Real Experience.
Nicole’s leadership has earned the trust of her colleagues and constituents alike.
She has served as Senate Majority Leader , Senate Majority Whip , Chair of the Senate Veterans Affairs Committee , Chair of the Bond Bill Committee , and Chair of the Sunset Committee .
She currently serves on several key committees responsible for healthcare, labor, public safety, business development, and capital investments across Delaware.
Fighting for Delaware’s Future Nicole Poore believes government should work for the people it serves.
That’s why she continues to focus on creating opportunity, strengthening communities, protecting children, supporting workers, and delivering the kind of results that improve everyday life for Delaware families.
