I wanted to pass along an announcement from the Delaware DMV:
Due to this week’s closure of the New Castle DMV location on Hessler Boulevard, there is high traffic and long lines surrounding the other DMV locations statewide today.
In response to this, the DMV has announced that all late fees will be waived for the remainder of December 2025 and January 2026.
Please visit myDMV.delaware.gov to complete eligible online services.
