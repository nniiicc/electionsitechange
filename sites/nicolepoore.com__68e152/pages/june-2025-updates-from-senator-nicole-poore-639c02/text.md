Sincerely, Nicole Poore Senator, District 12 Legislative Updates House Bill 1 (HB 1) As Chair of the Veterans Affairs Committee, I was proud to lead the charge on House Bill 1 in the Senate.
This is a game-changing bill that creates a Cabinet-level Office of Veterans Affairs here in Delaware – a long-overdue requirement for our vets.
This office will give our veterans the voice, support, and representation they’ve long deserved.
After years of advocacy by Rep.
Bill Carson and many others, we’re finally getting this across the finish line.
Thank you to my colleagues for standing with me and our vets.
I hope to bring this home with a unanimous vote in the House on Thursday!
If you are a vet or currently serving in our military, please join us in Legislative Hall on Thursday, June 26 to see this legislation pass in the Senate.
Senate Bill 174 (SB 174) For many of us, our pets are irreplaceable members of our families.
Sadly, too many animals find themselves in the hands of people who pose a serious risk to their welfare.
That’s why I’m proud to sponsor Senate Bill 174 , establishing an animal abuse offender list under the Office of Animal Welfare.
This legislation not only makes offenses of animal abuse public information – it also legally prohibits shelters from approving adoptions to people on the list.
House Bill 210 Introduced Earlier this month, I held a joint town hall meeting with Rep.
Minor-Brown , Delaware Department of Natural Resources and Environmental Control (DNREC) Secretary Greg Patterson , and the Delaware City Refinery General Manager Mike Capone to discuss the excess levels of sulfur dioxide that had been caused by a water leak in their boiler.
We wanted to come together to explain this issue, let us hear directly from our constituents, and inform everyone of our next steps collectively.
With the assistance of many legislators, including House sponsor Rep.
Lambert , we were able to introduce House Bill 210 , legislation that will create the Pollution Accountability Act.
This bill will increase penalties for environmental violations and directs shares of fines to the communities being affected by it.
HB 210 is a necessary step forward in protecting our environment and maintaining public health and safety.
By increasing the fine structure for our biggest polluters, we’re not only deterring future violations, but also directing revenue back into the neighborhoods most affected.
Around the 12th District Juneteenth Celebration I had the honor of speaking at this year’s Delaware City-Polktown Juneteenth Commemoration .
This is not my first year being invited to celebrate with the African Union Church Cemetery , and I am always so overjoyed to celebrate this important holiday with this community.
On Juneteenth, we honor the strength of those who waited for freedom—who built, prayed, sang, and hoped, even when justice was delayed.
Juneteenth is not just a date in history—it is a story of resilience.
It reminds us that freedom didn’t come all at once.
It came late.
And for many, it’s still coming.
As Maya Angelou said, “You may not control all the events that happen to you, but you can decide not to be reduced by them.” On this day, we remember.
We teach, we vote, we rise—because Juneteenth is not only about what happened in 1865, it’s about how we keep showing up for justice, for truth, and for each other.
May freedom continue to ring—louder, stronger, and with purpose.
