Every conversation, every phone call, every email, and every opportunity to help someone reminds me why I chose public service.

In District 12 and Beyond ART ON THE GREEN The New Castle Hundred Lions Club will be hosting ‘Art on the Green’ on September 26 at Battery Park.
This amazing tradition has been happening in our community for over 50 years – offering great chance for our small and local businesses and our neighbors to come together for a fun afternoon.
Join your neighbors for an afternoon of art, crafts, music, and creativity that allows you to enjoy our parks and surrounding neighborhoods.
The New Castle Hundred Lions are currently accepting applications for vendors to join them for Art on the Green.
You can find more information by visiting their website here .
Community Resources Each year, Easterseals hosts their annual Caregivers Conference in Dover that offers free support and resources to caregivers from across our state.
This year’s theme focuses on “Taking Care of You,” reminding caregivers to also prioritize their own well-being while also caring for others.
The conference held on August 26 will feature keynote speaker Heather Volkema, an expert in private-duty home care organization, and will be available both in-person and via Zoom.
For more information, visit their website here .
The Delaware Department of Transportation is now offering ‘Autism Warning’ road signs for families that may find peace in extra caution and security in their residential neighborhoods.
These signs are displayed to alert motorists to reduced speed limits, make them more vigilant towards children’s safety, and help avoid potential accidents.
To apply for a sign to be installed on a state-maintained road or permanent residence, please visit their website here .
Families should not have to search across multiple websites to understand Delaware’s public schools.
Public information should be easy to find, easy to understand, and clearly connected wherever people access it.
The Delaware Department of Education launched Explore Delaware Schools, a new public portal that brings together school information, attendance boundaries, available school profile and performance information, and interactive mapping tools into one place.
Explore Delaware Schools reflects the department’s broader commitment to making public information more transparent, more accessible, and easier to use.
Over time, information about Delaware schools has expanded across multiple reports, websites, and tools.
This new portal provides one front door where families and community members can begin their search with confidence.
You can explore the new portal by visiting their website here .
