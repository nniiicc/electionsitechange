News from Legislative Hall
My legislation to improve Delaware’s Equal Accommodation Laws, Senate Bill 198, unanimously passed the Senate earlier this spring.
Section 504 was the first piece of federal legislation to provide protections to Americans with disabilities, including physical accessibility measures to student supports — but we know that right now, federal protections are under attack.
This bill will help ensure that Delawareans with disabilities get broad and full protection under Delaware law.
Here is a list of other legislation that I prime sponsored that passed the Senate in March:
- HB 224 updates Delaware code to allow for physicians’ assistants to sign death certificate
- SB 248 addresses the formula used in the Workplace Safety Program to calculate insurance credits for businesses maintaining overall safety
- HB 222 modifies the state’s Nutrient Management Program fee structure
- SB 110, which passed both the Senate and the House unanimously, extends the physical therapy referral period from 30 to 90 days to ensure continuity of care
We had so many wonderful visitors in March!
From our friends in the disabilities advocacy community, to those raising awareness about rare diseases, to local artists and athletes, it’s always great to spend time with the people making Senate District 12 — and the State of Delaware — special.

Updates on the Proposed Starwood Data Center
Earlier this year, DNREC denied the permitting process for the proposed Starwood Data Center under the guidelines of the Coastal Zone Act.
Starwood appealed this decision, but the appeal was formally denied after a lengthy hearing earlier this month.
I’m sharing a clip from Bay to Bay that was published this morning, indicating that Starwood is still hoping to move forward with the project.
I will keep you informed as we learn more about the proposed data center.

The Faith Deanna Johnson Foundation has been a leader in advocating for improved gynecological health across Delaware.
Dwain and Karen Johnson started the foundation in honor of their daughter, Faith, who lost her battle with ovarian cancer in 2021.
Ovarian cancer is a silent killer.
Prevention depends heavily on early screenings, access to medical care, and increased awareness for Delawareans.
After many years of tireless work and advocacy, the Foundation has officially opened the Ovarian Cancer Awareness Center!
This center, “Faith of Deanna,” will expand their life-saving programs into communities across our state, and I am deeply grateful to have attended its grand opening earlier this spring.
