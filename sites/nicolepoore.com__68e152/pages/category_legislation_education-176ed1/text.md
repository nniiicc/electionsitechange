We have always been a leader in speaking for the voiceless and standing up for animals, and we continued that work today with the signing of SB 174.

It is an incredible honor to see this vision for Delaware’s veterans become a reality.

Reminder: Parents’ Rights in Education
During the 147th General Assembly, I introduced SB 299, legislation that gives parents the freedom to opt their children out of the DCAS standardized test.
As the parent of a child with a cognitive disability, I understand that every child learns differently — and that each one’s abilities and potential cannot be captured by a test score.
I wanted school to be a place that enriched my son’s life, not one that mesured his worth against academic benchmarks.
My son’s value was never defined by a DCAS Alt-1 score.
Our family understood his strengths, challenges, and what success looks like for him — not what a test says it should be.
But this legislation wasn’t written just for parents like me.
It also supports students who are still learning English and may struggle to understand test questions, or children who experience test anxiety and cannot perform their best under pressure.
SB 299 is about giving parents the right to decide what’s best for their child’s academix journey.
As we enter a new school year, let’s continue to remember that every child is unique, and that education should lift them up, not limit them.
If you are interested in opting your child out of DCAS testing, please talk with their school administrator and teacher.

I had a wonderful time joining April and John from the Pet Pawcast in Wilmington to talk about SB 174 and the importance of protecting animals here in Delaware.
As an owner of three beloved dogs, I know firsthand the unconditional love pets bring to our lives and the deep responsibility we share to care for them.
But that care shouldn’t stop with our own pets; it extends to every animal without a voice of their own.
SB 174 gives us the opportunity to be that voice.
It ensures that all animals are treated with the compassion they deserve and that those who harm them are held accountable.
Thank you to the Humane Animal Partners for inviting me onto your show to share why this work means so much – not only to me, but to our animals and shelters who have continued to advocate for this legislation.
