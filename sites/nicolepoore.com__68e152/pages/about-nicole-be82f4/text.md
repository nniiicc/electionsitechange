About Senator Nicole Poore
My name is Nicole Poore and I am a lifelong New Castle resident, wife, mother, advocate, and proud public servant who has dedicated my career to helping Delaware families navigate life’s challenges and opportunities.
Growing up in the community I now represent, I learned early the values of hard work, perseverance, and looking out for your neighbors.
Principles that continue to guide me every day in the Delaware State Senate.
Over the years, I have witnessed tremendous change throughout our community.
New neighborhoods have been built, businesses have opened and evolved, industries have transformed, and technology continues to reshape the world around us.
Today, we find ourselves discussing issues that would have seemed unimaginable just a few decades ago, including proposed data center projects in our own backyard.
While much has changed, what remains constant is the character of the people who call the 12th District home.
We are a community of hardworking individuals, strong families, dedicated first responders, small business owners, and neighbors who genuinely care about one another.
Like so many of the families I represent, everything I do is grounded in family.
My husband, Billy, and I have been married for more than 30 years and are proud parents of three children.
Today, our daughter has begun her legal career, and our youngest son has started his own business.
Our oldest son, Nicholas, continues to be one of my greatest sources of inspiration.
Nicholas was born nine weeks premature and diagnosed with cerebral palsy.
Since the day he entered our lives, our family has navigated the complexities of healthcare, insurance, education, and disability services.
Those experiences have given me a firsthand understanding of the challenges many families face and have strengthened my commitment to ensuring every Delawarean has the opportunity to live with dignity, independence, and purpose.
My journey as a mother, advocate, and public servant has shaped the work I do every day.
Whether I am fighting to improve healthcare access, supporting veterans and working families, strengthening workforce development opportunities, protecting consumers, or standing up for our local communities, my focus remains the same: finding practical solutions that improve people’s lives.
It has been the honor of a lifetime to represent my hometown in the Delaware State Senate.
I remain deeply grateful for the trust that residents have placed in me, and I will continue working every day to ensure that New Castle County remains a place where families can live, work, and thrive for generations to come.
I ask for your support as I seek re-election this fall, on September 15, 2026.
Loving Mother | Passionate Advocate | Experienced Problem Solver
Being a working mom to 3 kids has taught me some key strategies for being an effective legislator:
- “When money is tight, you have to work with what you’ve got.” – Like many families, we’ve had to make difficult decisions and prioritize what matters most.
That’s the mindset I bring to the Joint Capital Improvement Committee, where we carefully allocate funding for construction and infrastructure projects across Delaware while remaining mindful of taxpayers’ dollars.
- “There’s no such thing as other people’s children.” – Raising a family takes a village, and every child deserves the opportunity to succeed.
As legislators, we have a responsibility to look out for Delaware’s next generation as if they were our own.
Whether that means strengthening our schools, expanding opportunities, or ensuring families have the support they need.
- “Do what’s right, even when it’s not easy.” – Leadership isn’t about following the crowd—it’s about making thoughtful decisions and standing by them.
While I am proud to be a Democrat, I have always been an independent thinker.
I listen, I ask questions, and I make decisions based on what is best for the people I represent.
Some issues are bigger than politics, and I have never been afraid to put my constituents first.
In Legislative Hall, I have developed a reputation for being a relentless advocate for Delaware’s children, veterans, and people with disabilities.
