Status: Senate Health and Social Services Committee (as of 9/17/2013) HB-130 October 1, 2013 admin Criminal Law and Courts , Legislation 0 0 HB-130 – An Act to Amend Title 16 of the Delaware Code Relating to Regulation of Manufacture, Distribution and Dispensing of Controlled Substances Senate Sponsor[s]: Hall-Long Senate Co-sponsor[s]: Blevins, Bushweller, Ennis, Poore, Sokola, Venables Synopsis: Continuation of efforts to combat the manufacture of methamphetamine by authorizing a real-time electronic monitoring system for the purchase of over-the-counter drugs which can be distilled into ingredients for meth.
The bill will allow Delaware to join with 25 other states in the National Precursor Log Exchange which track interstate sales of drugs containing pseudoephedrine and block sales of the drug above set limits.
Status: Signed HB-35 October 1, 2013 admin Guns , Legislation 0 0 HB-35 – An Act to Amend Title 11 and Title 24 of the Delaware Code Relating to Criminal History Background Checks in Connection with the Sale or Transfer of Firearms Senate Sponsor[s]: McDowell Senate Co-sponsor[s]: Henry, Peterson, Poore, Sokola, Townsend Synopsis: Attacks the “Gun Show Loophole” for firearms sale by requiring federally licensed firearms dealers to conduct criminal background checks on most private firearms sales and maintain records of the checks as required by state and federal law.
Excluded are transfers among immediate family members, antique and certain antique firearm replicas, returns to an owner by a pawnbroker or transfers involving qualified active-duty and retired law enforcement personnel.
First offenses are Class A misdemeanors and subsequent offenses are G felonies.
Status: Signed SB-118 October 1, 2013 admin Health and Social Services , Legislation 0 0 SB-118 – An Act to Amend Title 24 of the Delaware Code Relating to Pharmacists Sponsor[s]: Poore, Hall-Long Co-sponsor[s]: Ennis, Henry Synopsis: Allows pharmacists to substitute FDA-approved interchangeable biosimilar products for biological products with safeguards and provides some liability protections.
To make a substitution, pharmacists must provide notice, make and maintain a label information/distribution record.
Records would be maintained for three years.

Status: Senate Ready List SB-40 October 1, 2013 admin Guns , Legislation 0 0 SB-40 – An Act to Amend Title 11 of the Delaware Code Relating to the Possession of Firearms During the Commission of a Felony Sponsor[s]: Ennis Co-Sponsor[s]: Henry, Marshall, Poore, Sokola, Townsend, Venables Synopsis: Gives prosecutors the option of seeking life prison terms under Delaware’s habitual offender law for the use of a firearm during the commission of a violent felony covered by the habitual offender law.
Status: Signed SB-16 October 1, 2013 admin Guns , Legislation 0 0 SB-16 – An Act to Amend Title 11, Chapter 5 of the Delaware Code Relating to the Mandatory Reporting of Lost or Stolen Firearms Sponsor[s]: Henry Co-sponsor[s]: Blevins, Peterson, Poore Synopsis: Mandates that gun owners report stolen or missing weapons to the State Police within 7 days of discovery.
