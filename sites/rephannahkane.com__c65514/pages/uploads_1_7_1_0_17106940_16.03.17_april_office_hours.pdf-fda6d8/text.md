Kane Holds April Office Hours in District SHREWSBURY- Representative Hannah Kane of the 11th Worcester District will be holding Office Hours in both Shrewsbury and Westborough during the Month of April.
Shrewsbury office hours will be held at the Shrewsbury Senior Center, 98 Maple Avenue, on April 29th from 10:30am-11:30am.
Westborough hours will be at the Westborough Senior Center, 4 Rogers Road, on April 29th from 12:00pm to 1:00pm.
Hannah looks forward to hearing her constituents’ questions and concerns.
If these hours are inconvenient, please call Representative Kane’s office at (617)-722-2430 to discuss your issue and/or concern.
