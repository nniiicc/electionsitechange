Kane Holds May Office Hours in District SHREWSBURY- Representative Hannah Kane of the 11th Worcester District will be holding Office Hours in both Shrewsbury and Westborough during the Month of May.
Shrewsbury office hours will be held at the Shrewsbury Library, 214 Lake Street, on May 27th from 1:30pm to 2:30pm.
Westborough hours will be at the Westborough Library, 55 W Main Street, on May 27th from 3:00pm to 4:00pm.
Hannah looks forward to hearing her constituents’ questions and concerns.
If these hours are inconvenient, please call Representative Kane’s office at (617)-7222430 to discuss your issue and/or concern.
