Kyle Clayton for State Senate
Candidate for Arizona State Senate in Legislative District 27
I am running to provide real solutions for Arizona’s Future.
My Priorities
Education: The Foundation for Arizona’s Future
Kyle believes that strong schools are the key to strong communities.
- Equal Funding: Address the imbalance between elementary and high school funding, so our kids get a strong start.
- Invest in Students: Raise per-pupil funding to prepare students for high-demand Arizona jobs.
- Early Learning: Fully fund full-day kindergarten and expand Pre-K.
- Accountability: Reform ESA vouchers to make sure they are transparent and results-driven.
Lowering Costs for Arizona Families
From energy bills to healthcare and housing, Arizona families deserve relief.
- Affordable Healthcare: Lower prescription costs and expand coverage for young adults.
- Energy Affordability: Invest in energy assistance and home efficiency upgrades.
- Childcare Solutions: Expand access, support working parents, and reward employers that help.
- Housing Affordability: Reform zoning to allow more homes and stop corporate bulk buying.
Fiscal Responsibility and Fairness
Kyle is committed to accountability and common-sense spending.
- Evaluate all tax incentives to ensure they deliver value for taxpayers.
- Require performance-based outcomes for companies receiving state benefits.
- Focus on smart investments — in education, families, and Arizona’s workforce.
