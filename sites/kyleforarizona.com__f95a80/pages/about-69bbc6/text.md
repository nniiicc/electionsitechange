Meet Kyle
Born and raised in Legislative District 27, Kyle Clayton is a lifelong Arizonan dedicated to building stronger, safer, and more affordable communities.
Today, he’s raising his own family in the district — with one child enrolled in the Washington Elementary School District (WESD) — and working every day to make Arizona a better place to live, learn, and grow.
As a full-time Realtor®, Kyle brings an analytical, data-driven approach to problem-solving and decision-making.
His professional experience has taught him the importance of transparency, integrity, and advocating tirelessly for others — principles he carries into his public service.
Since 2023, Kyle has served his community on the Washington Elementary School District Governing Board, where he currently leads as Board President.
In that role, he’s helped improve student attendance, championed continued investment in arts and athletics, and guided the district through a major leadership transition — always with a focus on students and families first.
Kyle’s commitment to community goes beyond the classroom.
As a member of the Shaw Butte Neighborhood Alliance, he’s helped secure more than $250,000 in local investments, planted over 300 trees, and organized events that bring neighbors together.
His track record proves he knows how to collaborate, build coalitions, and deliver results.
Kyle believes that leadership is about listening and service — not partisanship.
Whether it’s advocating for public education, lowering costs for working families, or ensuring fiscal responsibility, Kyle is ready to bring pragmatic, people-first leadership to the Arizona Senate.
