THE CHOICE IS YOURS
Nebraska voters deserve a clear, unfiltered look at where the candidates stand on the issues shaping our daily lives.
When you strip away the sound bites, party labels, and high-dollar TV ads, making an informed choice comes down to a simple question: who is actually working for you?
From agricultural policy and fiscal responsibility to personal privacy and healthcare, the contrast in priorities couldn’t be sharper.
Below is a direct side-by-side comparison highlighting where each candidate stands on the issues that matter most to Nebraska’s farms, families, and future.
Take a look at the facts and decide who truly represents working people—and who serves the status quo.
Agricultural Parity
Nebraska farmers deserve fair prices for their crops, not Wall Street profits.
Mike Flood talks about farm bills while family farms disappear.
I’m fighting for true parity pricing that ensures farmers receive the cost of production plus a fair profit—not government subsidies that favor corporate agriculture.
Medicare For All
Healthcare is a basic human need.
Flood voted against expanding healthcare access while rural hospitals close across Nebraska.
I support Medicare For All—comprehensive coverage that follows you from job to job, farm to town, covering prescriptions, dental, vision, and mental health.
No premiums.
No deductibles.
No bankruptcies.
Constitutional Due Process
Your rights aren’t negotiable.
While Flood supports expanding surveillance and government overreach, I stand for liberty first.
No warrantless searches.
No civil asset forfeiture without conviction.
No detention without due process.
The Constitution applies to everyone, equally, every time.
End Corporate Control
Family farms built Nebraska.
Now corporations dominate our markets, set our prices, and control our future.
I’ll break up agricultural monopolies, enforce antitrust laws, and ensure local farmers can compete.
Your land, your labor, your freedom.
Lower Taxes, Less Waste
Flood votes for massive spending bills then claims to be fiscally conservative.
Actions matter more than rhetoric.
Cut defense spending.
Stop exporting war.
Eliminate corporate welfare.
Let working families keep more of what they earn.
Government should live within its means.
Individual Liberty
Government should protect your freedom, not control your choices.
From farming practices to healthcare decisions, you know best.
Reduce unnecessary regulations.
Respect personal autonomy.
Trust Nebraskans to make their own decisions about their lives, families, and businesses.
