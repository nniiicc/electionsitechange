MONIQUE DOWARD FOR MINNESOTA STATE SENATE • DISTRICT 11 FORWARD INDEPENDENCE A STRONGER, SAFER, MORE AFFORDABLE DISTRICT 11 Accountable to District 11 communities—not a party caucus.
SAFE COMMUNITIES & ACCOUNTABLE LAW ENFORCEMENT Support law-enforcement staffing, training, mental-health response, fair enforcement, transparent complaint systems, and stronger partnerships with local communities.
RURAL HEALTHCARE THAT STAYS OPEN Create an early-warning system to protect vulnerable rural services before they close and help healthcare workers build long-term careers through childcare, housing, training, and service-based retention incentives.
LIBRARIES AS ESSENTIAL COMMUNITY HUBS Provide stable funding for rural libraries, broadband and technology access, workforce resources, literacy programs, and safe, welcoming spaces for every age.
STRONG PUBLIC SCHOOLS & RESPECTED EDUCATORS Increase classroom support, strengthen special education, improve school safety, retain educators and support staff, and reduce unfunded state mandates.
NO MEGA DATA CENTERS IN DISTRICT 11 Oppose projects that consume excessive water and electricity, shift costs to residents, or threaten farmland and community resources.
Local residents must have a real voice.
TRANSPORTATION & RURAL ECONOMIC GROWTH Restore funding and momentum for the Northern Lights Express, move the Duluth-to-Twin Cities passenger-rail project toward construction, and use better transportation connections to grow local businesses and familysustaining jobs.
AFFORDABLE, HEALTHY FOOD & STRONG LOCAL AGRICULTURE Lower household food costs by strengthening rural grocers, local farmers and food supply chains; expand access to fresh, nutritious food; and grow farm-to-school and community food partnerships.
MMIW/MMIR ACTION & MISSING-PERSON RECOVERY Strengthen prevention and response for Missing and Murdered Indigenous Women and Relatives, modernize statewide search coordination, improve alerts and case communication, and expand rural search-and-rescue resources.
UNION RIGHTS & FAIR WORKPLACES Defend collective bargaining, prevailing wages, safe staffing, workplace safety, retirement security, and the right of workers to organize without retaliation.
UNIVERSAL CHILDCARE PILOT FOR DISTRICT 11 Launch a state-funded pilot for children ages 6 weeks through 10 years with no family income cap, direct provider reimbursement, workforce expansion, and options for evening and overnight care.
FIREFIGHTERS, EMS & EMERGENCY READINESS Close retirement and health-benefit gaps for volunteer, paid-on-call, and EMS-only responders; modernize aging equipment; and provide fair, accessible grants for small rural departments.
CLEAN WATER, HONEST GOVERNMENT & ACCOUNTABILITY Protect drinking water and lawful utility service, strengthen fraud prevention and independent investigations, require transparent public records, and hold elected officials accountable when they knowingly break the law— regardless of party or position.
DIGNITY FOR SENIORS, VETERANS & PEOPLE WITH DISABILITIES Eliminate Minnesota state income tax on Social Security benefits for all seniors while protecting home- and community-based services, accessible transportation, caregiver support, veterans' resources, and independent living.
