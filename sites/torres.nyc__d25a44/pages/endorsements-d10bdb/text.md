Council of School Supervisors & Administrators
Leandra Feliz
Mother of Lesandro "Junior" Guzmán-Feliz
We write to express our strong support and endorsement of Congressman Ritchie Torres for his unwavering commitment to the residents of the New York City Housing Authority (NYCHA) and public housing communities across New York City.
Congressman Torres does not advocate for public housing from a distance; he grew up in NYCHA.
He understands firsthand the realities of mold, neglect, broken boilers, unreliable elevators, and the systemic disinvestment that has plagued public housing for decades.
His advocacy is personal and tireless.
In Congress, his commitment has only intensified.
As a member of the House Financial Services Committee and its Subcommittee on Housing and Insurance, Congressman Torres has continued rigorous oversight of NYCHA and federally assisted housing.
He has secured tens of millions of dollars for NYCHA developments across the Bronx, funding critical infrastructure upgrades, mold remediation, plumbing repairs, broadband expansion, playground improvements, and life-saving fire safety upgrades.
