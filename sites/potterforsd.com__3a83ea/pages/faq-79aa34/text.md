You can also vote early
- The deadline to register to vote is October 19, 2026, for the general election.
Registration forms must be printed, signed in original ink, and received by your County Auditor — no email or fax accepted.
- Any registered South Dakota voter can vote early or absentee — no excuse required.
Early and absentee voting for the November general election opens September 18, 2026.
Request your ballot through the SD Secretary of State's office or your County Auditor.
- Bring a valid photo ID: SD driver's license, U.S. passport, tribal photo ID, or current SD student ID.
No ID?
You can sign a personal identification affidavit at the polls.
- Check the South Dakota Voter Information Portal at vip.sdsos.gov — enter your info and it will show your polling location and sample ballot.
- 7:00 AM to 7:00 PM local time.
IMPORTANT: If you're in line by 7 PM, you are entitled to vote.
