Get Involved!
Winning State Senate District 7 will take our entire community — all 22 towns working together!
We’re recruiting volunteers for a variety of roles.
Whether you can give a little time or take on a larger role, there’s a place for you on our team.
Explore the opportunities and use the form below to sign up for one or more ways to get involved.
Join Us Host a House Party We’re looking for 20 supporters across the district to host fundraising house parties.
Bring together friends and neighbors, share ideas for New Hampshire’s future, and help build the resources needed for a winning campaign.
Interested?
Email our Campaign Manager at emma@rebeccafornh.com .
Organize a Community Conversation We’ve launched a series of Community Conversations in towns across the district to foster open, bipartisan dialogue on the issues that matter most.
We need volunteers to help organize locations, coordinate schedules, and spread the word in their communities.
Our goal is to conduct 22 of these Community Conversations in District 7 towns before November!
Attend a Community Event or Parade From pancake breakfasts and fish fries to parades and summer festivals — we want to be part of the events that bring our communities together.
Let us know about upcoming events in your town and how our campaign can participate.
We're happy to just show-up and meet community members, lend a hand at a community breakfast, walk together in a parade, or set-up a table at a festival or farmers market.
Write to your Local Newspaper Help us expand our reach through Op-Eds, Letters to the Editor, and social media.
Whether you enjoy writing or sharing content on platforms like Facebook and Instagram, your voice can help amplify our campaign and the issues facing our district.
Canvass your Community Neighbor-to-neighbor conversations are at the heart of a true grassroots campaign.
