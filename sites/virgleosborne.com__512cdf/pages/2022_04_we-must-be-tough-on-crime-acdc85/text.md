We Must Be Tough on Crime by virgleosborne Friend, An example of why we need to be tough on crime is the traffic stop that happened a couple weeks ago for a minor violation.
The stop transpired to be the uncovering and confiscation of an estimated 23 pounds of methamphetamine and 47,000 fentanyl pills.
Criminals negate the everyday responsibilities that Oregon’s citizens endure and enforcing traffic violations can be used as a device to ensure public safety.
I stand with law enforcement , and back the philosophy that you must be tough on crime, otherwise crime will be tough on you.
