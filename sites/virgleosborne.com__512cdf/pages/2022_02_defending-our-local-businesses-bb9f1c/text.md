Defending Our Local Businesses by virgleosborne Friend, Local businesses have undoubtedly faced hardship over the last year and a half.
With “non-essential” closures, occupancy limitations, and taxes – the need for a business advocate has never been greater, which is why I have dedicated time on the campaign trail to stop and listen to local businesses in the area.
Too often politicians speak too much and listen very little.
So far, I have spoken to the workers, managers, and owners of Nordic Veneer, Fabform Industries, Orenco, and North River Boats.
I learned about the challenges they face and how Oregon can help them flourish.
You can find these tours on my Facebook page below.
I always have and always will support Southern Oregon businesses.
They are the base for a strong local economy.
