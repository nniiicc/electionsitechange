An Introduction by virgleosborne Hello!
My name is Virgle Osborne, and I am running to represent YOU in the Oregon House of Representatives.
I am: Pro- Small Business Pro- Timber Industry Pro- Law Enforcement Pro- 2 nd Amendment Why I am Running I have deep roots here in Douglas County and I feel like current leadership is not representing the values of the people strong enough.
Too often Portland is running the show.
The ideology and views of one geographic area should not have as much say in the direction of our state as they do now – which is why we need a strong leader with common sense values to stand up against Portland extremists.
Looking Onward There is still about a year until the May 2022 Primary Election, but I wanted to introduce myself now.
In the meantime, feel free to check out my Facebook page and the rest of my website.
I am excited to be on this journey and I hope to meet you on the campaign trail!
