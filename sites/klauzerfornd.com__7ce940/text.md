MEET KYLAN
Committed to Family, Community, and Service
Kylan’s professional background is grounded in public safety, teamwork, and real-world experience.
He served nearly two decades with the Dickinson Police Department, where he held investigative and supervisory roles and served as a team leader on the regional SWAT team.
His work required preparation, calm decision-making, and collaboration — skills he brings to public policy.
Today, Kylan works for ONEOK and remains deeply involved in community organizations across Dickinson and western North Dakota.
His experience in both public service and the private sector gives him a balanced perspective on the challenges facing families, employers, and communities.
Kyland and his family are proud to call Dickinson and District 37 home.
