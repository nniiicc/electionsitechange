Lifelong Alaskan, husband, father, running for reelection to represent House District Nine
As a legislator, community leader, and entrepreneur, my guiding principle is always to help people, communities, and businesses improve and create their own future.
I believe that:
- Each generation of Alaskans should be good stewards of our resources, lands, and public finances.
- We should empower our communities to solve problems, create opportunities, and connect to cultural traditions.
- Every Alaskan should have access to opportunity and the ability to build the future they want.
- Alaskan innovation can create jobs, lower costs, and improve our economy by creating jobs and reducing costs.
- Solving Alaska’s problems requires leaders who will work across differences and make decisions grounded in evidence.
