Alaskan Opportunity Opportunity should be accessible to everyone, regardless of their background or location.
I am dedicated to creating policies that promote equality, support local and emerging businesses, and enhance community services.
These policies will be grounded in the uniqueness of Alaska and developed in consultation and cooperation with Alaskan native and tribal organizations to address the challenges of climate changes, sensitive remote arctic development and opportunities for future generations.
Inspiring Education As a university instructor and student advisor, father and a proud husband to a school librarian, I know firsthand the importance of education to the future of our state..
As your representative, I worked to increase student funding and fight the policies that have shuttered schools across the state.
I’ve supported reducing class sizes, providing key resources to our school teachers, and building an education system that empowers students to achieve their full potential.
Energy Security As Co-Chair of the House Energy Committee, I’ve leaned on my experience as a business leader, engineer, and entrepreneur to create innovative energy policies.
I expanded and improved efficiencies in the bulk fuel loan program, driving down energy costs; increased state investment for the most promising renewable energy projects; and worked with the utilities, resource developers and the RCA to help energy projects meet our needs for reliable and affordable energy.
I outlined a diversified energy strategy that would invest in future energy technologies, add $5 billion to our GDP, add up to 35,000 jobs, and contribute up to $400 million to our general fund to support schools and future economic development.
I want Alaska to lead the energy future with a balanced path of oil and gas production, in-state renewable generation, and new energy technologies.
The Economy of the Future I believe in the possibility of a resilient and innovative state economy that supports prosperity for all Alaskans, from healthy rural communities to vibrant urban centers.
This future depends on restoring a foundational partnership between public and private sectors.
While strong businesses create jobs and investments, quality public services can deliver a skilled workforce, healthy communities, and predictable business conditions.
I've worked to build that foundation by championing entrepreneurship and innovation, supporting workforce development, and creating the conditions for businesses to grow, develop products and services, and launch new ventures.
I don’t believe in managing decline - I believe in a fiscal plan that encourages Alaskan investment and restores confidence in public-private partnerships.
Local Infrastructure Infrastructure is the backbone of thriving communities and local economies.
For our district, infrastructure includes our highways, the bike trails in our parks, our rail lines, and our barge and rail services and ferry terminals.
Infrastructure also includes the resources available for disaster prevention and mitigation.
I will prioritize capital budget projects that will enhance transportation, keep us safe, and provide reliable utilities across the state.
Together, we can build a stronger, more connected community that will keep families in Alaska.
