Chris Wedum for MN House 50A Chris Wedum is a proven global leader in Human Resources and Talent Acquisition, known for transforming organizations, elevating people strategies, and building high‑performing teams that deliver measurable results.
With more than two decades of experience across healthcare, technology, retail, financial services, and real estate, Chris has earned a reputation as a decisive, human‑centered strategist who leads with clarity, compassion, and conviction.

Minnesota’s economy is strongest when people have access to good jobs, thriving businesses, and a government that protects taxpayer dollars instead of wasting them.
Over the last four years, Minnesotans have seen both gains and losses — and too often, fraud, mismanagement, and inefficiency have held our economy back.
My priority is restoring accountability, strengthening job growth, and ensuring Minnesota remains a place where families and businesses can rise together.
These issues matter to Chris and she will represent you in the MN House.
FRAUD Fraud is undeniable — and accountability is non‑negotiable.
For too long, fraud has drained resources from taxpayers, families, and the programs meant to support them.
Stopping fraud isn’t just a policy position — it’s a leadership imperative.
Every dollar must be protected, every system monitored, and every violation is addressed swiftly and decisively.
JOB GROWTH Workforce participation remains below pre‑pandemic levels, creating gaps in critical industries and slowing economic momentum.
Inflation and rising costs have strained families and employers, reducing consumer spending and limiting business expansion.
DECREASING TAX BURDEN Taxes are rising at a disproportionate rate.
It is not compassionate to overburden the middle class or retirees on fixed income.
It is not compassionate to continue to tax and spend without considering ways to make government more efficient and effective.
EFFECTIVE GOVERNMENT State government should serve people — not the other way around.
I will champion transparency, eliminate waste, and demand measurable results.
My leadership is rooted in accountability, operational excellence, and building systems that work.
PUBLIC SAFETY Every Minnesotan should feel safe where they live and work.
Safe communities are the foundation of strong communities.
I support law enforcement, fair judicial processes, and systems that hold people accountable.
EDUCATION QUALITY Statewide we have seen decreased education proficiency.
Every child deserves access to high‑quality education that prepares them for the future.
She will support strong schools, empowered teachers, and systems that put students first.
Chris brings her voice, her vision, and her values to the forefront.
She is running a campaign centered on people—on building workplaces, communities, and systems where individuals can thrive, contribute, and belong. ​ Her leadership is bold, inclusive, and future‑focused.
