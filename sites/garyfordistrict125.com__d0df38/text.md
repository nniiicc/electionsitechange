About
Gary richardson
Conservative businessman and former County Commissioner Gary Richardson is a proven conservative leader with an unmatched record of success.
Gary was elected to the Georgia State House to represent District 125 in a Special Election Runoff in March 2024.
He understands that our community needs trusted, conservative leadership to keep Columbia and McDuffie Counties safe and strong.
Gary previously served on the Columbia County Board of Commissioners for 8 years and was elected by his colleagues to serve as Vice-Chair five times.
On the Commission, he proudly voted to reduce taxes, fund law enforcement, and safeguard the traditional values that we hold dear.
Guided by his conservative principles, experience in the private sector, and a proven record of public service, Gary is delivering for the citizens of District 125.
With your vote, Gary will continue the fight to strengthen Georgia’s economy, promote small business, crack down on illegal immigration, protect our Georgia values, and keep up the fight to keep us safe and strong!
