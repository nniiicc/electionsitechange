He has been married for over 40 years to his wife, Jensine, a retired public educator from the North Kansas City School District; both are Oak Park graduates.
They raised four sons in the North Kansas City School District and now have four grandchildren attending North Kansas City Public Schools in the Staley area.
A retired electrician, Dan believes in hard work, strong neighborhoods, and leaving a Northland where families don’t just survive, they thrive.
His guiding belief is simple: the government works for the people again.
