Issues
Healthy Schools, Healthy Families
Dan believes strong public schools and accessible healthcare are essential to a healthy community.
He supports fully funding public education, expanding access to mental health care, and addressing the rising cost of health insurance that strains families and seniors.
A Fair Economy for Working Families
As an IBEW retiree, Dan supports policies that protect workers, promote fair wages, and expand affordable housing.
Dan opposes tax breaks for millionaires and billionaires and believes we should reward hard work while helping families build stable, secure lives.
Infrastructure & Community Investment
Dan believes infrastructure is more than investing in roads, and utilities, it's also investing in our emergency response programs and first responders.
This strengthens neighborhoods and creates dependable jobs.
Smart, responsible infrastructure investments help the Northland grow while keeping communities safe and connected.
