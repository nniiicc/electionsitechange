MERCH DONATE Privacy Policy for LoriForIdaho.com ​ Effective Date: June 11, 2026 Lori for Idaho ("we," "our," or "the campaign") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when visiting LoriForIdaho.com or interacting with the campaign online. ​ 1.
Information We Collect We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you: Sign up to receive campaign updates or volunteer.
Make a contribution through a donation platform such as ActBlue.
Complete a form on our website to request information, RSVP for an event, or get involved with the campaign.
This information may include: Name Email address Phone number Mailing address (if provided) Donation details processed securely through ActBlue We do not collect payment information directly.
All financial transactions are processed securely by ActBlue, and your credit card or bank information is subject to ActBlue's Privacy Policy.
2.
How We Use Your Information We use the information you provide to: Communicate with you about campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities.
Process and acknowledge contributions made through ActBlue.
Comply with applicable legal and campaign finance reporting requirements.
Improve our outreach and engagement with supporters.
You may opt out of receiving campaign communications at any time by following the unsubscribe link in our emails or contacting us directly.
3.
Sharing and Disclosure We do not sell or rent your personal information.
We may share limited information: With trusted vendors or service providers who assist with campaign operations, such as email communications, website services, or volunteer coordination.
When required by law, regulation, court order, or authorized election authorities.
Any third parties who receive information from us are required to protect that information and use it only for campaign-related purposes.
4.
Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
However, no online platform or data transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
5.
Links to Other Websites Our website may include links to third-party websites, including ActBlue and social media platforms.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those external websites.
6.
Children's Privacy Our website and campaign activities are intended for individuals aged 18 and older.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
7.
Updates to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
8.
