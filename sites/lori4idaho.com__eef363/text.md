Why I'm Running
My name is Lori Hickman, and I am running to be Idaho’s next Attorney General.
I never planned to run for office.
But over the last few years, I have watched the Attorney General’s Office drift away from its core mission.
Instead of serving as Idaho’s trusted legal advocate, the office has become increasingly consumed by politics, culture wars, and personal ambitions.
That’s not the tradition Idahoans deserve.
Attorneys General like Jim Jones, Larry EchoHawk, and Lawrence Wasden earned the trust of people across the political spectrum by following the law wherever it led, providing sound legal counsel, and putting the interests of Idaho first.
They understood that the Attorney General’s job is not to score political points — it is to serve the people of Idaho.
The Attorney General is the people’s lawyer.
That means protecting consumers, keeping communities safe, defending the Constitution, and providing honest, independent legal advice to state agencies and elected officials.
It means doing what the law requires, even when it is politically inconvenient.
Throughout my legal career — from a major law firm in New York City to Micron Technology to representing clients here in Idaho — I have built my reputation on integrity, professionalism, and sound legal judgment.
I am not running because politics is my career.
I am running because the law matters to me, Idaho matters to me, and this office is too important to leave on its current path.
As Attorney General, I will work every day to restore trust in the office, uphold the rule of law, and ensure that every Idahoan knows their Attorney General is working for them — not a political agenda.
I would be honored to earn your support.
