Endorsed by the WV CDL
Clark for WV
Apr 26
From the West Virginia Citizens Defense League:
"WVCDL is proud to endorse Wayne Clark for House of Delegates in the Republican primary.
WVCDL evaluates candidates based on their commitment to the rights of law-abiding West Virginians, using surveys and legislative histories when applicable.
We encourage all voters to learn more about these candidates and to participate in the upcoming primary elections if eligible."
