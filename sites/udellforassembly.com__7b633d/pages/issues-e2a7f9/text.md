ON THE ISSUES
Randy’s Blueprint for Wisconsin
EMPOWER WISCONSIN RESIDENTS
- Work to expand broadband & high-speed internet to rural and underserved areas of the state
- Continue the Child Care Counts program to provide child care access close to home and create a state Child Tax Credit
- Accept federal funds to expand Medicaid in Wisconsin
- Foster increased access to mental health resources
- Promote caps on insulin and prescription drug prices
PROTECTING OUR FREEDOMS
- Protect abortion and access to reproductive freedoms by repealing the 1849 abortion ban
- Codify the right to same-sex marriage in Wisconsin statutes
- Implement fair and nonpartisan redistricting to prevent future gerrymandering
- Ensure Wisconsin elections are free, accessible, and fair
ADVOCATE FOR LOCAL COMMUNITIES
- Pursue efforts to address climate change and preserve our environment
- Support public safety and local law enforcement, fire departments, and EMS districts
- Promote common sense gun control, including banning assault weapons and requiring universal background checks
- Invest the state’s surplus into a better shared revenue deal for Wisconsin municipalities
- Give small businesses the tools they need for success
- Re-authorize the Knowles-Nelson Stewardship Fund
- Push for long-awaited safety improvements on U.S.
Highway 14 in the town of Rutland
