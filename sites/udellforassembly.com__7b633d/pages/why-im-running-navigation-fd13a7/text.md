Why I’m Running
My deeply held belief, and my experience in local government, is that government works best when we bring people together to solve tough problems.
I want to continue bringing those values to the State Assembly.
When my husband Brad and I moved to Seminole Forest twenty-eight years ago, we fell in love with the Fitchburg area.
We cherish Fitchburg’s vibrant culture, the blend of urban and rural environments, our beautiful parks, and—above all—the caring and engaged residents who live here.
I am running for re-election to the Assembly in District 47, which includes most of Fitchburg, the southern half of McFarland, Stoughton, and all or parts of the towns of Albion, Dunn, Dunkirk, Pleasant Springs, and Rutland.
As a local elected official, I previously represented and advocated for urban, suburban, and rural constituents simultaneously.
District 47 needs an experienced voice that understands the unique needs of its urban, suburban, and rural constituents alike.
I served as one of two alderpersons representing District 4 on the Fitchburg City Council from 2020 to 2024.
During my tenure, I spent two years as Council President and three years chairing the Finance Committee.
From 2024 to 2026, I served as the Dane County Board Supervisor for District 33, which includes the southern two-thirds of Fitchburg.
I would like to continue using the perspective and experience I have gained from my time in local government and working in telecommunications to benefit all residents of our state.
We make progress when everyone has a voice.
As a District 4 alderperson and Dane County Board Supervisor, I have focused on bringing people together.
My style involves listening more than I talk - I consider all points of view and collaborate to find the best solutions.
I ask for your support to continue representing District 47 on Tuesday, November 3rd.
Let's keep moving District 47 and the state forward, together!
