I will represent people , not lobbyists or party bosses.
No culture war games — just common sense: You be you.
Don’t hurt people, don’t take their stuff, and live as you’d like .
Our district is made up of good people with many different ideas and priorities.
That’s a strength, not a weakness.
Representation should not be a tribal contest, nor should decisions come from lobbyists or party leadership.
I promise to represent the people of District 40 and listen to them.
I will not participate in the entertainment-driven outrage circus of culture wars.
Fiscal Responsibility Ohioans work hard.
Government should spend carefully, not bail out big corporations or tilt the playing field against small businesses.
State representatives must be careful stewards of Ohioans’ hard-earned money.
We should not bail out or subsidize large corporations, giving them unfair advantages over our locally owned small businesses.
Government should not pick winners and losers.
Protect the Ohio National Guard Support Defend the Guard : no overseas deployment without a congressional declaration of war.
Keep our Guard at home, ready to protect Ohio.
While Ohio has little power over foreign policy, we do have a responsibility to protect our Ohio National Guard.
I support Defend the Guard legislation, which would prohibit deploying Ohio Guard troops to overseas combat without a formal congressional declaration of war.
Since 2002, too many Guard members have been sent abroad instead of remaining ready to protect their neighbors here at home.
The misuse of our Guardsmen and Guardswomen who sign up to defend our communities and our nation is both unconstitutional and troubling.
Sending them into overseas combat zones without proper authorization, or activating them in other American states and cities for dystopian political purposes, is an abuse of their service and intentions.
Ohio should lead the way in restoring constitutional limits and ensuring our Guard serves where they’re meant to: protecting Ohio and her people.
Stand Up for Voters’ Decisions Respect the 2023 marijuana legalization vote.
Stop creating new ways to criminalize what Ohioans chose to legalize.
Defend the will of Ohio voters on marijuana legalization (2023 Issue 2).
The legislature should respect the result of that vote rather than seeking new ways to criminalize behavior that Ohioans have clearly chosen to legalize.
Fair Elections & Voter Freedom End gerrymandering with real, nonpartisan redistricting.
Open primaries so all voters have a voice.
Ranked-choice voting for better choices and less division.
Other Key Issues End Property Taxes – Support ballot initiative to abolish real property taxes.
End Qualified Immunity – Hold government officials to the same standards as everyone else.
Josh Umbaugh is running in Ohio House District 40, which includes includes Preble County, the western edge of Montgomery County including his hometown of Miamisburg, and the northern edge of Butler County including Seven Mile and Trenton.
