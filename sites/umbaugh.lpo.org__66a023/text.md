While Ohio has little power over foreign policy, we do have a responsibility to protect our Ohio National Guard.
I support Defend the Guard legislation, which would prohibit deploying Ohio Guard troops to overseas combat without a formal congressional declaration of war.
Since 2002, too many Guard members have been sent abroad instead of remaining ready to protect their neighbors here at home.
The misuse of our Guardsmen and Guardswomen who sign up to defend our communities and our nation is both unconstitutional and troubling.
Sending them into overseas combat zones without proper authorization, or activating them in other American states and cities for dystopian political purposes, is an abuse of their service and intentions.
Ohio should lead the way in restoring constitutional limits and ensuring our Guard serves where they’re meant to: protecting Ohio and her people.
