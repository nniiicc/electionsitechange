Ways You Can Help
Connect with Your Network | Talk with friends, neighbors, coworkers, and family.
Share why you believe in Michelle and invite others to learn more.
Personal conversations matter more than anything.
Share Online | Like, comment, and share campaign posts.
Help spread the word and keep the conversation going in your own voice.
Offer Your Skills | Good at organizing, writing, data, design, or logistics?
Let us know — we’d love to put your strengths to work.
Host a Gathering | Open your home, host a coffee, or bring people together in a way that feels comfortable to you.
These small moments build real momentum.
Help at Events | From community service projects to campaign events, extra hands make everything better.
Set-up, greeting, organizing — it all matters.
Text & Phone Outreach | Help us reach voters where they are.
Friendly texts and phone calls help share information, answer questions, and remind neighbors that this election matters.
Canvassing (Door-to-Door) | Join us out in the community.
Knock doors, listen, and connect with voters face-to-face.
We’ll train you and pair you with a team — you’re never on your own.
However you like to show up, there’s a place for you here.
