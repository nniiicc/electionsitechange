Action, Not Talk
We are already feeling the effects of climate change here in Connecticut—from stronger storms and flooding to rising energy and insurance costs.
This is no longer a distant issue.
It is impacting our homes, our budgets, and our future.
Michelle Embree Ku believes it is time to move beyond talk and take real, practical action that delivers results for families.
She will work to strengthen local infrastructure so our communities are better protected from flooding and severe weather.
That means securing state funding for stormwater management upgrades, improving drainage systems, and investing in resilient roads and bridges that can withstand extreme conditions.
Michelle will push for smart energy solutions that lower costs while reducing emissions.
She supports expanding access to clean, affordable energy options like solar, especially for working families who are often priced out of these programs.
She will advocate for policies that stabilize energy costs and reduce dependence on volatile fuel markets.
Protecting open space and natural resources will also be a priority.
Michelle will fight to preserve local land, waterways, and coastal areas—not just for environmental reasons, but because they are critical to our quality of life, property values, and long-term economic health.
She will also support practical recycling and waste reduction efforts that actually work for residents.
That means improving local systems, reducing confusion, and making it easier—not harder—for families to do the right thing.
Michelle understands that environmental policy must be both responsible and realistic.
Her approach focuses on solutions that protect taxpayers while delivering measurable results—not unfunded mandates or empty promises.
This is about safeguarding our community today and ensuring a stronger, more secure future for the next generation.
Michelle Embree Ku is ready to lead with action—and she will not back down.
