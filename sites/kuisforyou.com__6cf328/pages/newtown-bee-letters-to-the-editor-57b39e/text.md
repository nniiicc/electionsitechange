All In For Hayes, Blanchard, Ku, Kerton And Lamont In November
August 13, 2026
To The Editor:
The low turnout we recently witnessed in Connecticut’s primary should concern all of us — not because every voter has to support the same candidate or political party, but because participation in our democracy matters.
There is a lot at stake nationally as well as with who represents us in Hartford after the midterms.
Elections give us an opportunity have our voices heard and it is a wasted opportunity when only a small percentage of eligible voters participate and end up making decisions that affect all of us.
Democrats have excellent candidates who will do a great job representing us: Jahana Hayes for the 5th Congressional District; Rob Blanchard, who is ready for the open seat in the 28th State Senate District; and Michelle Embree Ku and Julie Kerton for the 106th and 107th House Districts respectively.
Last but not least, Ned Lamont for Governor.
All candidates have been reaching out to voters via door knocking and by making phone calls.
But we need more engagement and in order to educate the electorate as to why these candidates should represent us — we need to spread the word.
Talk to your family, friends, neighbors, and coworkers.
Remind people that their vote matters and encourage them to learn about the candidates and issues before Election Day.
Democracy works best when people participate.
Whether you vote by absentee ballot, vote early, or show up at the polls on Election Day, make a plan and make your voice heard.
Spread the word.
And most importantly, show up in November!
Every vote counts.
Alex Villamil
Sandy Hook

Supporting Ku And Blanchard
August 20, 2026
To The Editor:
I am writing to support the candidacies of Michelle Embree Ku and Rob Blanchard.
Michelle is running to represent Newtown in Connecticut’s 106th House of Representatives district.
Rob is running for Connecticut’s 28th State Senate District, which includes all of Newtown, Easton and Fairfield, along with parts of Westport and Weston.
Michelle’s 14 years of experience in local and state government and her deep connection to the people of Newtown make her uniquely qualified to represent us.
Like Michelle, Rob Blanchard has already served at both the local and state level.
Rob’s number one priority as a senator will be to reduce the cost of living in Connecticut.
One way he’ll do this is by working to pass legislation that would increase the base amount the state contributes to each student’s education, which has not changed since 2017.
Through his experience with state and local level budgeting, Rob knows that a portion of the state surplus can be redirected to reducing property taxes by increasing the state contribution to education.
This November, I’m voting for Michelle Embree Ku and Rob Blanchard.
I hope that you’ll join me, whether you’re a Democrat, Republican, Independent, or unaffiliated.
Erica Sullivan
