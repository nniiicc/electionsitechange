JAKE JOHANSSON CANDIDATE FLORIDA STATE SENATE DISTRICT 8 Approachable Leadership Strategic Vision Honesty & Integrity Retired US Navy Captain Donate Jake Johansson Candidate Florida State Senate District 8 Meet Jake Johansson: A Proven Leader Ready to Serve District 8 ​Jake Johansson is running for Florida State Senate District 8, representing most of Volusia County and the northern portion of Brevard.
With a lifetime dedicated to service, Jake is a leader who listens, collaborates, and takes action to address the challenges facing our communities. ​ After an impressive 35-year career in the U.S.
Navy, retiring as a Navy Captain, Jake transitioned his leadership skills to local government as Port Orange City Manager.
It was there he fell in love with the community and committed himself to helping it thrive.
As Volusia County's At-Large Council Member, Jake worked to attract better jobs, enhance quality of life, and build a brighter future for all. ​ Jake's commitment goes beyond his role as a leader—he's a trusted neighbor, an active problem solver, and a passionate advocate for the voices of District 8.
As a leadership consultant and a dedicated member of various boards and committees, Jake has worked tirelessly to strengthen our community.
He champions public health and safety, supports conservative, lean, and responsive government, and has fought for higher-paying jobs, greater transparency, and environmental stewardship.
Jake is here to make a difference for every resident in District 8. ​ Jake Johansson: Leadership you can trust, service you can count on.
