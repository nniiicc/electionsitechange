Strategic Vision
Leadership: A person of action, not rhetoric.
Will lead and make the hard decisions after listening to all sides of the issue.
Communication: Works hard to explain actions for all to understand.
Seeks input from citizens who are willing to identify issues and want what is best for the county.
Transparency: Will endeavor to be transparent regarding governmental actions and decisions.
Ethical Governance: Has never and will not be influenced by individuals or special interest at the expense of the citizens of the county.
