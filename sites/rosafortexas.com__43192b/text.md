Born and raised in Elsa, Oscar Rosa built a successful pharmacy business serving our communities before retiring to focus on faith and family.
Now, as a husband and father, he's running to represent the hardworking people of the Rio Grande Valley because it's time for change.
- Affordable Living: Cut property taxes, lower prescription drug costs, and make healthcare accessible for every family.
- Economic Growth: Create jobs by making Texas more business-friendly and supporting small businesses like the ones I ran.
- Strong Values: Champion education, traditional family values, and pro-life policies that honor the heart of South Texas.
- Secure Our Border: End the chaos of human trafficking, drug smuggling, and cartel violence that's destroying lives and families.
We'll push for state solutions to protect our communities.
"I don't want votes from just my party—I want them from anyone who believes in hard work, results over rhetoric, and putting our Valley first.
After years of the same old politics with no progress, let's deliver real leadership for District 35."
