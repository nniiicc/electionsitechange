My Why
Why have I decided to enter this race?
It’s time for me to lend my voice and stop the continuation by the left to expand their reach to take away and erode our freedoms.
During the height of the COVID fears, my dad was in rehab recovering from a fall.
My mother was prohibited from visiting him.
Separating them was detrimental to their health and happiness.
The picture below shows the efforts my sister and I did to give Mom the opportunity to at least see Dad.
We did have some giggles getting her, with her walker, over the rocks so she could peak through the window while they talked on the phone.
I was denied the opportunity to say goodbye to Dad.
My heart aches to this day.
Medical Freedom should never again be denied anyone for any reason.
