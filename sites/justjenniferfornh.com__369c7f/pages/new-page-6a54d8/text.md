Having extracurricular activities and programs is vital to developing successful caring adults along with the classroom work.
Best Buddies is one such program and every high school should have a chapter.
Thanks for leading the way in Carroll 2 Kennett HS!!
Go to their website New Hampshire - Best Buddies International and consider sponsoring a chapter and/or participating in one of their events.
Background: While serving as the PTSA President during my son’s junior and senior year of high school, a conversation led to the discussion of starting a Best Buddies Chapter.
It was an easy yes for me to engage with contacting the organization, finding a fabulous 1st Vice Principal to sponsor along with other teachers, engage the PTSA in providing funds for t-shirts, etc… See the growth of all students who are partnered with others and building beautiful relationships beyond the classroom still warms my soul thinking about.

In 2023, Dave Hausman of Big Dave’s Bagels & Deli teamed up with Kennett High School to bring Best Buddies New Hampshire to the Mount Washington Valley.
Since then, the Kennett High School Chapter has welcomed nearly 100 students to the organization.
Through the One-to-One Friendship Program, students with and without IDD have formed meaningful, lasting friendships.
Thanks to the Leadership Development Program, students with and without IDD have learned what it takes to become leaders, public speakers, and advocates for diversity and inclusion.
