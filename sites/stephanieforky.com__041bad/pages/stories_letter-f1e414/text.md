Coffee, and fortunately, she accepted the position with Big Brothers Big Sisters, where we worked together for several years.
Over time, our paths continued to cross, and my growing family even moved near hers in Oldham County.
There are many qualities I admire about Stephanie, but her commitment to showing up stands out the most.
She showed up for my children for Special Friends Day in preschool before my parents moved to Kentucky.
She showed up with advice about where to send my kids for summer camp and connected us with the St.
John’s community.
Even as she faced cancer, Stephanie remained steadfast—showing up for herself, her family, her friends, and her community.
She’ll bring that same dedication as the next Kentucky House District 59 Representative As a high school math teacher, Stephanie understands that Frankfort’s numbers aren’t working for Kentucky families.
Too many Oldham County families are left behind, and divisive politics have hurt us all.
Stephanie is committed to every family in District 59; she has the courage and compassion needed to make a difference.
When you vote, choose someone who will always show up for you—vote for Stephanie White.
