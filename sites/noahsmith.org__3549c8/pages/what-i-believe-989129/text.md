BOLD FAITH.
First and foremost, I am a firm believer in Jesus Christ.
Each day I wake up and strive to be a better Christian.
I am guided by my faith in Christ in all of my decisions, big or small.
As your State Representative, I will lead with that same conviction.
My faith guides my decisions, my viewpoints, and my life.
Christ is the center and the foundation of who I am.
LIMITED GOVERNMENT I am a firm believer in limited goverment with less bureaucracy and less regulations.
We weren't founded to be governed by endless red tape or bureaucrats.
SECOND AMENDMENT "Shall not be infringed" is pretty clear.
I am a gun owner, hunter, and avid outdoorsman.
I will always continue to support our Second Amendment Rights and empower our outdoorsmen.
PRO-LIFE I am a strong supporter of the Pro-Life movement and believe that life begins at conception.
The Bible tells us this plain and simple.
My faith in Jesus directs me to protect life as all people are made in the image of God.
TERM LIMITS We do not need career politicans.
We need to FIRE them.
I am a firm believer in term limits at all levels of goverment.
I am running to be a fresh, bold voice for District 36!
SUPPORT EDUCATION Parents have the fundamental right to choose where their kids get educated.
I believe a strong rural education system, where teachers teach and not indoctrinate, opens doors for our rural youth to succeed.
IMMIGRATION We must have a strong border and you must come LEGALLY!
We need to remove criminals, gang members, and abusers off our streets and deport them.
"A nation that cannot control its borders is not a nation" - Reagan LOWER TAXES Lower taxes means more money in the pockets of our consumers and neighbors.
Lower taxes creates a freer economy for people to spend their hard earned money where they want.
AMERICA FIRST America First with Tennessee Action!
We need the next generation of Republican leaders to put America First, not Last.
We need to invest in our country and state before others and fix our issues here at home.
VETERANS BENEFITS I owe my life and liberty to our nation's veterans.
We would not be a country today without them.
We must empower them with the resources they need and NOT CHARGE them to use their veterans benefits. ​Support Our Cause Together, we can send a clear message that we want new representation for us in Nashville.
