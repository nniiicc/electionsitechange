Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 8/1/2026
Bekki Brewer for Missouri House District 44 ("the Campaign," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting our website.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.
By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- ZIP code
- Employer and occupation (when required by campaign finance laws for contributions)
- Donation information
- Volunteer interests
- Information submitted through contact forms or event registrations
We also automatically collect certain technical information when you visit our website, including:
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Pages visited
- Time and date of your visit
- Referral website information
- General website usage statistics
How We Use Your Information
The Campaign may use your information to:
- Respond to your questions or requests.
- Send campaign news, updates, and event invitations.
- Recruit volunteers and coordinate campaign activities.
- Process donations and comply with applicable campaign finance reporting requirements.
- Improve our website and communications.
- Analyze website traffic and engagement.
- Notify you about elections, voting information, and campaign activities.
- Comply with federal, state, and local laws.
Contributions
If you make a contribution through our website, we may collect information required by Missouri law and other applicable campaign finance regulations, including your employer and occupation when required.
Contribution information may be reported to the appropriate government agencies as required by law.
Payment transactions are processed through secure third-party payment processors.
We do not store complete credit or debit card information on our servers.
Email Communications
If you subscribe to our mailing list, volunteer, attend an event, or otherwise provide your email address, you may receive campaign communications.
You may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link included in our emails or by contacting us directly.
Text Messaging
If you choose to provide your mobile phone number and opt in to receive text messages, you consent to receive recurring campaign communications by SMS.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
For assistance, reply HELP or contact the Campaign directly.
Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a contribution.
Cookies and Analytics
Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Remember your preferences.
- Improve website performance.
- Measure visitor engagement.
- Analyze website traffic through services such as Google Analytics or similar providers.
Most web browsers allow you to disable cookies through your browser settings.
Third-Party Services
We may use trusted third-party providers to operate portions of our campaign, including:
- Website hosting
- Email communications
- Online fundraising platforms
- Payment processors
- Volunteer management tools
- Event registration services
- Analytics providers
- Social media platforms
These providers maintain their own privacy policies governing the information they collect and process.
Sharing Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information only:
- With vendors and service providers working on behalf of the Campaign.
- As required by campaign finance reporting laws.
- To comply with legal obligations or court orders.
- To protect the rights, safety, or security of the Campaign or others.
Data Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect the information we collect.
While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.
External Links
This website may contain links to external websites.
The Campaign is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.
Children's Privacy
Our website is intended for individuals who are at least 13 years old.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Your Choices
You may request to:
- Update your contact information.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Remove yourself from our email communications.
- Ask questions about how your information is used.
Some information related to campaign contributions may be retained as required by law.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Updates will be posted on this page with a new Effective Date.
Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
