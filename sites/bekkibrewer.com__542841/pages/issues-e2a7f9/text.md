Working Families First
District 44 needs a voice that echos the everyday issues that we face across Boone County.
Talking about the struggles facing families shouldn't just stop at the dinner table, we need a representative who will carry these conversations to Jefferson City and make representation truly represent the people of Boone County.
Strong Public
Schools
I strongly believe that public dollars should stay in public schools.
Strengthening our public schools not only benefits local families, but it builds strong communities.
Deciding where to send your children for education is a choice for families to make, but allowing state subsidies to support private schooling further hinder the quality of public schools for those families and communities that rely on that education.
Representation,
NOT Control
The role of an elected official isn't to dictate how people should live their lives, but to listen, advocate, and ensure every voice in the district is heard.
I am committed to bringing the concerns of working families, farmers, small business owners, educators, and seniors to Jefferson City.
This means serving the community with transparency, accountability, and respect.
Our campaign is rooted in the idea that elected officials should be partners with their constituents, listening first and following up on promises.
Politicians should always answer to the people, and I am fighting to ensure that's the case in Missouri's 44th District.
Affordable Living
I believe every hardworking family in Boone County deserves the opportunity to build a secure future.
Expanding access to quality, affordable housing, lowering healthcare costs, supporting working parents prepare every child for success will be my focus in Jefferson City.
When families aren't stretched to the breaking point by rising costs, our communities and local economy are stronger.
As a Democrat running for Missouri House District 44, I believe that everyone who works hard should be able to afford a good life, have access to quality education, receive reliable healthcare, and earn a fair wage.
Personal Freedom & Access to Healthcare
It's simple.
Decisions regarding healthcare belong to patients, their families, and their doctors, NOT politicians.
Every resident deserves the freedom to make medical choices without government interference.
This goes for reproductive, preventive, mental health services, or treatment for chronic illnesses and more.
I'm committed to protecting these personal freedoms while expanding our access to affordable, high-quality healthcare.
Communities depend on accessible medical care, especially in our rural areas.
Healthcare is about dignity, freedom, and the ability to build a better future for ourselves and our families.
