Jamie Thompson
I'm State Representative Jamie Thompson and it is an honor to represent my community in Lansing.
I believe in servant leadership.
As a nurse, the ideals of compassion, empathy, toughness and fortitude are engrained in who I am.
I have taken those same ideals to Lansing where all too often they are lacking.
I am working for common sense solutions to the problems downriver families face on a daily basis.
I will continue to advocate for policies that create new and better jobs, give our kids a stronger education and keep our neighborhoods safe.
My Story
My name is State Rep.
Jamie Thompson, and I was first elected to the Michigan House of Representatives in November 2022.
I have been a fierce fighter for freedom, prosperity and hope and will work to defend families, communities and small businesses for a better tomorrow.
I understand Downriver communities are diverse areas that represent different backgrounds.
The region features working families, single-parent households, veterans, retirees and many small-business owners who all look to their elected officials to work on their behalf and provide effective, accountable leadership every day.
I am a life-long Christian, a mother of three and grandmother of three.
Family is incredibly important to Jamie.
Jamie and her husband are legal guardians to our three grandchildren after they tragically lost their 24-year-old daughter in a motorcycle accident in 2021.
Jamie is proud of her Native American heritage.
She is a member of the Mississaugas of Hiawatha First Nation and has legal standing.
