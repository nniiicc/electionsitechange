The system shut out more than 1 million Marylanders Maryland has approximately 4.3 million active registered voters.
Of those, about 2.2 million are registered Democrats, about 1,019,000 are registered Republicans, and about 1,004,000 are registered as unaffiliated.
Add roughly 80,000 more registered with minor parties, and more than one million Marylanders, nearly one in four, could not cast a ballot in Tuesday's primary.
They are taxpayers funding elections they cannot participate in.
Maryland Matters described the situation directly in its June 22 reporting: nearly one voter in four is barred from participating in Maryland's closed primaries.
That is the eligibility problem.
It is real, it is the most direct injustice in the current system, and it has a clean legislative fix.
