59% say Maryland is heading the wrong way The UMBC Institute of Politics released its statewide poll on April 1, 2026.
The headline finding: 59% of Marylanders now say the state is on the wrong track.
Only 30% say it is heading in the right direction.
The gap matters less than the trajectory.
In October 2025, just six months earlier, the same question split 48% wrong track to 40% right track.
In half a year, the wrong track number jumped 11 points.
The same poll asked Marylanders to rate the state's economic conditions.
76% said poor or fair.
Only 22% said good or excellent.
The previous October that number was 69%.
The trend is sharply down, across every demographic UMBC measured.
Mileah Kromer, the poll's director, put it bluntly: "Overwhelmingly, Marylanders are saying the cost of living across all these indicators is higher than it was last year.
They don't think things are going well.
