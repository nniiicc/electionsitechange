Information We Collect We collect information you voluntarily provide when you use our website, including when you join the campaign, volunteer, request a yard sign, donate, or contact the campaign.
This may include: First and last name Email address Phone number Residential address and ZIP code Employer and occupation (required for donations of $# or more under Maryland Election Law § 13-221(a)(3)) Party registration (optional) We also collect standard technical information automatically when you visit our site, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URL, through Google Analytics.
How We Use Your Information Information collected on this website is used solely for campaign purposes, including: Communicating with supporters about campaign events, voting deadlines, and updates Coordinating volunteer activities and neighborhood canvassing Delivering yard signs to requested addresses Processing campaign contributions in compliance with Maryland campaign finance law Complying with mandatory campaign finance disclosure requirements Maryland Campaign Finance Disclosure Under Maryland law, contributions of $500 or more require disclosure of the contributor's name, address, occupation, and employer.
This information is filed with the Maryland State Board of Elections and becomes public record.
Contributions of any amount may also be subject to disclosure requirements.
For more information, visit elections.maryland.gov .
Information Sharing We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.
Your information may be shared only in the following limited circumstances: With campaign vendors and service providers who assist with campaign operations, under confidentiality agreements As required by Maryland campaign finance law for mandatory disclosure filings As required by law or legal process Data Storage Voter contact information and form submissions are stored securely on AWS (Amazon Web Services) infrastructure.
Form submissions are also sent to info@vote4rehman.com for campaign use.
This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site.
Google Analytics collects anonymized data about page visits and user behavior.
You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on .
We also use Facebook Pixel to measure the effectiveness of campaign advertising on Facebook.
You can manage your Facebook ad preferences at facebook.com/ads/preferences .
Your Rights You may contact us at info@vote4rehman.com to request that your information be removed from our campaign contact list.
Note that information submitted as part of a legally filed campaign finance report cannot be removed as it is public record.
