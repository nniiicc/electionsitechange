Saif Rehman is an unaffiliated independent candidate for Maryland House of Delegates in District 9A, which covers western Howard County and northern Montgomery County.
The district has 18 election-day precincts, including 14 in Howard County and 4 in Montgomery County.
A business owner, CEO of NextGen Consulting Inc., and longtime community advocate, Dr.
Rehman has been involved in District 9A politics since 2018, running for Howard County Board of Education in 2018 and 2020, and for the District 9A delegate seat in 2022 under a party label.
Those experiences showed him how party structures in Annapolis prioritize the party over the district.
Running as an independent in the 2026 general election, Dr.
Rehman's campaign focuses on three core issues: making Maryland more affordable for working families — in the face of Annapolis-driven cost increases hitting District 9A families; strengthening education accountability — Howard County spends $21,512 per student per year, fourth highest in Maryland; and demanding fiscal responsibility after Annapolis closed a $3.3 billion deficit by reaching into family budgets.
District 9A elects two delegates to the Maryland House.
Dr.
Rehman is asking voters to use their second vote for an independent voice that answers only to them — not a party, not a caucus, not Annapolis leadership.
