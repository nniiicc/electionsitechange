The ISSUES THE ISSUES CLICK ON AN ISSUE BELOW TO SEE WHAT REBECCA STANDS FOR AND BELIEVES IN TAX CUTS Rebecca believes Arkansans work hard for their money and government should take less of it.
She has consistently voted to lower taxes and return dollars to families and small businesses.
Voted to cut Arkansas’ income tax by more than 25%, reducing the top rate to 3.7%.
Supported eliminating the state grocery tax.
Backed increasing the homestead tax credit from $300 to $675, lowering your property tax bill.
Voted to cut the used car tax.
These tax cuts are putting over $1 billion back into taxpayers’ pockets.
AFFORDABILITY Families across Arkansas continue to face higher prices and the lasting effects of inflation.
Rebecca is focused on policies that reduce the cost of living and let people keep more of what they earn.
Delivered meaningful tax relief on income, groceries, property, and used vehicles.
Fought to reduce the burden of taxes and regulation on families and small businesses.
Supports policies that put more money back in the pockets of hardworking Arkansans instead of growing government.
CRIME AND SAFETY Public safety is a top priority.
Rebecca supports policies that protect victims, hold violent offenders accountable, and back the men and women who keep our communities safe.
Voted for the Protect Arkansas Act, requiring violent felons to serve more of their sentences, strengthening parole supervision, and putting victims first.
Strongly supports law enforcement officers who put themselves in harm’s way every day.
Will continue fighting to increase minimum sentencing times, enhance victim notification, and improve corrections training.
Committed to tough-on-crime policies that keep Arkansans safe and criminals behind bars.
EDUCATION Rebecca supports parent-directed education and believes every child deserves access to a quality learning environment.
She is a strong advocate for the LEARNS Act and school choice.
Cosponsored the LEARNS Act, which expanded educational opportunity, raised teacher pay, and created Educational Freedom Accounts.
Supports school choice so parents can choose the best educational setting for their children, whether public, private, or homeschool.
Believes fair competition improves outcomes for all students.
Will continue pushing back against invasive liberal ideology in our schools and defending parental rights.
ILLEGAL IMMIGRATION Rebecca believes Arkansas must enforce the law, cooperate with federal authorities, and protect the integrity of our communities and public resources.
Sponsored legislation to prohibit cities and counties from issuing identification cards to individuals who cannot prove lawful presence in the United States.
Supports stronger cooperation between local law enforcement and federal immigration authorities.
Backs policies that prevent sanctuary practices and hold criminal illegal immigrants accountable.
Will continue fighting for secure borders and the rule of law.
