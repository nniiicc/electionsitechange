Religious Freedom
As the son of a minister, my character and life has been shaped by Church, Faith, and Religious Freedom.
I will strive to uphold those Constitutional rights for every resident of Randolph County and the people of our great state.
Property Rights
For over 20 years, I have been a Real Estate broker specializing in both Commercial and Residential listings.
I understand the planning, zoning, and economic developemt issues our District and State face.
I will put my experience to work and fight for better future for Randolph County and its residents.
Military
Both active duty and retired service members will be given highest honors and protection under my watch.
We must respect and honor the ones serving, the served, and those who have given their life for our country.
