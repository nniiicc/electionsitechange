Biggs was first elected to the North Carolina House in November of 2022.
However, Brian served on the Randolph County Board of Education from 2014 to 2022.

He also serves as Vice-Chair of the Appropriations Committee, and is a member of the Agriculture and Environment, Health, Transportation, and Joint Legislative Education Oversight Committees.
In his free time Brian enjoys exercise, sports, and spending time with his family and friends.
Brian and his wife Heather live in Trinity, with their two children Austin and Kylie.
