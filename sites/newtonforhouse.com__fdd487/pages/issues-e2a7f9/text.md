I understand the importance of responsible fiscal policy and am committed to ensuring that our state's finances are managed wisely and efficiently.
Investing in Infrastructure and Energy Security I have championed significant investments in our roads, bridges, and energy infrastructure, including supporting the South Carolina Energy Security Act.
My focus is on ensuring that our infrastructure meets the needs of our growing state while paving the way for a sustainable energy future.
I understand the importance of robust infrastructure and am dedicated to initiatives that promote long-term prosperity and resilience.
Championing Education and School Choice I believe education is the foundation of a prosperous future.
That's why I fought for the Education Scholarship Trust Fund (ESTF) and vigorously advocated for its expansion.
My goal is to empower parents with choices and to provide students with diverse educational opportunities.
I am dedicated to an education system that caters to the needs of every child, ensuring that our students have the necessary resources and support to thrive.
I have and will always support raising our hard working teachers' pay.
They need us to have their back and I am proud to have theirs.
Driving Economic Growth A strong economy is the backbone of a healthy community.
I have voted for measures aimed at promoting workforce development and economic prosperity.
My efforts range from supporting legislation that prepares our students for the workforce to fostering an environment where businesses can thrive.
I am at the forefront of initiatives to bolster our state's economy, and I remain committed to driving economic growth that benefits all South Carolinians.
Upholding Public Safety and Second Amendment Rights The safety of our communities and the protection of our constitutional rights are paramount.
I have stood firm on enhancing penalties for fentanyl trafficking and supported the Second Amendment Preservation Act.
Ensuring the safety and security of our citizens while upholding our constitutional rights remains a top priority in my legislative efforts.
Expanding Access to Quality Healthcare Ensuring access to affordable, quality healthcare is a cornerstone of my policy efforts.
I proudly supported the repeal of the Certificate of Need law, which has been a significant step in reducing healthcare costs and eliminating unnecessary barriers to care.
My dedication to healthcare does not end here; I am committed to advocating for policies and initiatives that further enhance healthcare accessibility for all my constituents.
Supporting Our Heroes Our veterans and first responders have made immense sacrifices for our freedom and safety.
I have always recognized the importance of honoring their service by securing funding for healthcare improvements and facilities.
Ensuring that our heroes receive the support they deserve is a cause close to my heart, and I will continue to advocate for their well-being.
