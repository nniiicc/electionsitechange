Brandon Newton, a devoted husband to Allison, a Nurse Practitioner, and proud father to their sons, embodies the values of community, leadership, and dedication.
His journey from earning degrees in business and history at USC Lancaster to an MBA from Winthrop University reflects not just an academic pursuit but a preparation for impactful service.
Brandon's leadership skills were evident early on as he served as student body president, laying the groundwork for his future in public service.
Elected to represent District 45 in the South Carolina House of Representatives since 2016, Brandon has tirelessly worked to address the key issues of education, healthcare, and economic development.
His commitment extends beyond legislative achievements; his active roles within the Republican Party and community boards like the Lancaster County Historical Commission and The Children's Council Board underscore his dedication to Lancaster's growth and prosperity.
Brandon’s re-election campaign is driven by a clear vision for Kershaw and Lancaster— communities where families thrive, businesses flourish, and education opens doors to opportunities.
With his wife and sons by his side, Brandon is more motivated than ever to continue his service, ensuring a brighter future for all in Lancaster and Kershaw Counties.
