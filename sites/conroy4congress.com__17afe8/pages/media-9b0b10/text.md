Additional Resources In Washington State, everyone votes by mail.
General election ballots are mailed to voters by Friday, October 16, 2026, and the deadline to return them is 8 PM on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Based on 2024 and 2022 results, nearly two-thirds of voters are expected to return their ballots by the final week of the election cycle.
Historically, older higher propensity partisan voters vote in the first week, whereas Independent and swing voters often wait until the last week.
