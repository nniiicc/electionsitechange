Carmela Conroy is a third-generation Eastern Washingtonian shaped by hard work, responsibility, and service, inspired by a family legacy that taught her to show up for her community.
After working her way through school, she returned home as a Spokane County deputy prosecutor, protecting families, standing up for victims, and learning that safety is personal, not partisan.
She later served in the U.S.
Foreign Service across Afghanistan, Japan, New Zealand, Norway, Pakistan, and Washington, D.C., leading teams in high-pressure environments, managing crises, and earning national recognition, including one of the CIA’s highest civilian awards.
Team Eastern Washington reflects her commitment to steady, principled leadership and real problem-solving, and she is running for Congress to honor the region’s traditions and help every community in Eastern Washington reach its full potential.
