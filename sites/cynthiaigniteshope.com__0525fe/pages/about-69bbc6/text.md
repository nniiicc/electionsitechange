We are strongest together." I know our district and see what it needs.
I'm a lifelong resident of Cordele in rural Southwest Georgia, and I'm proud to be a candidate for Georgia's State House District 148.
After earning a Master's degree in English Literature, I became a Master Teacher with a passion and track record of helping students achieve greatness, no matter what their walk of life.
My husband and I have been married for 45 years and are the founders and pastors of a wonderful ministry located in the heart of Cordele.
We have three thriving adult daughters and two grandchildren.
"I have a passion to ignite hope for Georgia's future, in our district and across the state." I chose a life of public service throughout my career.
I have a deep passion for serving others and advocating on their behalf.
I put that passion to work later in my life as a Crisp County municipal court clerk, helping others navigate through our local government structures to resolve their concerns.
"I am a person of ACTION, not just idle talk.
I will address the issues that matter the most." These are the real issues that matter most.
Reduced utility costs and properly zoned, responsibly built data centers.
Accessible, affordable healthcare along with wellness programs for seniors.
Stronger education outcomes for both students and teachers.
Job growth, economic development and enhanced public safety.
All achieved while honoring our precious land.
