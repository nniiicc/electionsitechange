Frank has taken his Northwest Indiana values seriously.
Before running for Congress, Frank served as the North Township Trustee for 15 years, and through his time in the Trustee office he knows the value of being a trusted resource and how to help people in the time of need.
Frank is currently serving in his third term in Congress on behalf of the people of Northwest Indiana where he’s fighting to protect women’s access to reproductive healthcare, growing the regional economy by advocating for good-paying jobs, and protecting the steel industry from bad actors around the globe.
To represent the strong labor and manufacturing industries of Northwest Indiana, Frank is honored to serve as Vice-Chairman of the Congressional Steel Caucus.
