And I feel that each issue I truly care about falls under the heading of "Affordability".
There is little that causes a feeling of insecurity as much as not knowing if you're going to be able to afford basic needs from month to month.
In America, about one in four households face this reality and are one illness, major repair, or injury away from financial ruin.
As your representative, I will work to help improve the standard of living for all Wisconsinites.
I will fight for changes in creating affordable wages, housing, and childcare.
We need to reduce the cost of groceries and energy.
We need to rework our tax code.
Finally, we need to reduce insurance costs.

Some immediate steps to improving Wisconsin's healthcare system would be to assist people with new reporting requirements, work to keep rural facilities open, promote cost transparency, and reduce administrative costs by eliminating the role of health insurers as middlemen through BadgerCare Plus for all.
We can also require human review instead of AI insurance denials, and we can eliminate the need for prior authorizations for chronic conditions.
Wisconsin needs to lead the way in making universal coverage in the United States a reality.

I will fight for our public schools by phasing out tax-funded educational vouchers, increasing funding to past levels, and changing or getting rid of the current revenue limit.

Additionally, they are a major part of our state's economy, contributing over $11 billion to our state's GDP and supporting close to 100,000 jobs.
Safe, clean drinking water is also essential for the well-being of Wisconsinites.
The Knowles-Nelson Stewardship program is a bipartisan commitment to land and water protection, and the shortsightedness of our GOP members has put this critical program in jeopardy.

Find out more Revamping Our Tax System Several changes can be made in our tax laws to increase working families' disposable income.
Changing the way in which we tax families is one way to help Wisconsinites feel financially secure.
We need to increase the earned income tax credit (EITC) at the state level and make it available for those without children.
Also, a child tax credit should be put in place and paid out monthly.
Sales tax, corporate tax for small businesses, and the school funding formula all need to be revamped to lower the tax burden on Wisconsinites.
Find out more Protecting Farming's Future I will fight to help farmers.
Family farms are under increasing stress.
Dozens of former Wisconsin Agriculture Department officials have written to Congress discussing the worsening problems facing Wisconsin farms.
Our farm families are witnessing a doubling of farm bankruptcies and experiencing difficulty remaining profitable.
The problems facing our farmers are related to the Trump Administration's tariffs and the resultant trade wars.
Their economic insecurity is exacerbated by higher input costs and rising operational insurance costs.
Additionally, many of our farmers face significantly higher health care costs due to the loss of subsidies to the ACA plans and the rising costs of health care.
I will work with our farmers to reduce these burdens by finding new markets, attacking insurance costs, and making health care more affordable.

AI, while having tremendous potential in helping solve complex problems and increasing productivity, will, in my opinion, be one of the most disruptive technologies ever developed, and potentially one of the most dangerous ones.
Safeguards need to be implemented at a national and international level, but there are things we can do at the state level as well.
