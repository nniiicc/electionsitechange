John Perryman
Pediatrician · Neighbor · Advocate for Families
I was born in Detroit and raised in Westland, Michigan, a small suburb of Detroit.
After attending college and medical school at the University of Michigan, I completed pediatrics training at Northwestern's Children's Memorial Hospital in Chicago.
I also received training in pediatric oncology at the University of Cincinnati.
I began working as a general pediatrician in 1993.
For the past 13 years, I have worked for MercyHealth in Rockford, where I served as chair of the Pediatrics Department and the Credentials Committee.
I have also worked for Physicians for a National Health Program, which is an organization that advocates for a national single-payer insurance program.
I still work part-time as a pediatrician for MercyHealth.
I live in Williams Bay with my wife Linda and am the proud father of Katie and Sean.
I am running for Assembly to ensure that the State of Wisconsin keeps the promise of America.
That promise is to make sure that if something unforeseen happens, we have a way to give you support and a hand up, to make sure you are not bankrupted by an illness, to help your kids get a good education in a safe environment, to restore good governance through a productive assembly, and to leave our children a thriving planet.
I have always been an advocate for families and will continue that advocacy in Madison.
I am proud to endorse Dr.
John Perryman for the Wisconsin State Assembly in District 31.
Wisconsin needs a representative who understands the challenges facing working families and will prioritize lowering costs, expanding access to quality healthcare, strengthening public schools, and promoting responsible economic growth.
Dr.
Perryman advocates for community involvement in decisions regarding large-scale data centers, emphasizing transparency and environmental oversight to hold developers accountable.
As a pediatrician, he understands how medical costs burden families and is committed to lowering healthcare expenses and ensuring access to care.
He champions fully funding public education to support our teachers and students, believing that families should never have to choose between healthcare, housing, or quality education.
