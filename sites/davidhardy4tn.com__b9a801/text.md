About David Hardy
TENNEESSEE BORN & RAISED
David Hardy is a Nashville native whose life and career have been shaped by a deep commitment to community, service, and respect.
Raised by parents who owned a local sporting goods store and worked closely with businesses throughout the area, David learned the values of hard work, leadership, kindness, and empathy from an early age.
Through his career in healthcare, he has developed a proven record of innovation and problem-solving, working to improve quality of life through better systems and technology.
Now running for TN State Senate - District 27, David is focused on bringing people together to build safer neighborhoods, support local businesses, and foster a culture of respect and collaboration.
He believes every voice deserves to be heard and is committed to leading with empathy, kindness, and respect.
