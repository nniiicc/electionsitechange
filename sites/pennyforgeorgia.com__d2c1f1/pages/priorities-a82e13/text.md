I Believe in Leadership That Delivers!
My vision is simple yet powerful: A Georgia for All of Us.
I believe!
It is about fairness for everyone, not restrictions that silence voices.
We should be expanding access to the ballot, not creating obstacles for Georgians who are eligible to vote.
I believe in a Georgia where every voice is respected, every eligible Georgian has the chance to be heard.
An office where public service works for all, not just the few.
I am running on a modern, pro-people platform rooted in fairness, access, and administrative excellence.
I am committed to ensuring that every Georgian, regardless of race, zip code, age, or political party can trust that their rights are protected and their voices heard.
Protecting Voting Rights & Restoring Public Trust in Elections
- Ensure free, fair, and accessible elections for all eligible Georgians, regardless of zip code, race, age, or political affiliation.
As a former judge and lawyer, I will advocate for clear, fair election rules that empower voters.
- Expand early voting sites and accessible voting centers across counties.
- Strengthen absentee ballot access with clear instructions and convenient drop boxes.
- Rebuild public confidence through transparent processes and voter education.
- Promote nonpartisan election integrity and protect election workers from threats and political pressure.
- Georgia’s elections should inspire confidence, not skepticism.
I will ensure systems are implemented and communicated clearly to every voter.
Empowering Small Businesses & Entrepreneurs
- Small businesses fuel Georgia’s economy, they deserve a Secretary of State who cuts red tape, not adds to it.
- Remove unnecessary hurdles for small business growth, especially in rural areas.
- Streamline business formation processes for faster startup to make it easier to start and grow a business in Georgia.
Offer responsive, plain-language guidance that demystifies regulations.
- Expand outreach & resources via mobile business fairs in counties.
- Launch a “Business Access Georgia” digital dashboard with live support, status tracking, and help for first-time entrepreneurs.
- Prioritize Georgia’s farmers and agricultural enterprises as small business stakeholders.
Modernizing Professional Licensing & Cutting Bureaucracy
- Georgia’s workforce depends on swift licensure, we must deliver that.
- Address licensing inefficiencies, reduce backlogs, and modernize application processes.
Georgia processes over 700,000 licenses across 64 professions.
Yet many face frustrating delays.
- Reduce wait times for license processing.
- Offer priority processing for critical professions including: nurses, teachers, contractors, and social workers.
- Introduce mobile-friendly license renewals for applications.
- Educate through community outreach to help Georgians understand and obtain credentials.
Safeguarding Seniors & Vulnerable Georgians from Fraud
- Our seniors deserve to live with peace, not fear.
- Use the office’s regulatory authority to investigate and prevent fraudulent licensing and elder exploitation.
- Partner with law enforcement and nonprofit coalitions to launch a Statewide Fraud Protection Campaign.
- Expand public education efforts around cybersecurity and scam prevention.
Transparency, Technology & Trust
- Make the Secretary of State’s office even more transparent and accountable through modern tools.
Trust isn’t given, it’s earned through openness.
- Prioritize cybersecurity and data protection in all areas of the Secretary of State’s digital infrastructure.
- Establish a public data portal that tracks performance metrics on elections, business filings, and licenses.
- Host community town halls and publish quarterly accountability reports to build public trust.
