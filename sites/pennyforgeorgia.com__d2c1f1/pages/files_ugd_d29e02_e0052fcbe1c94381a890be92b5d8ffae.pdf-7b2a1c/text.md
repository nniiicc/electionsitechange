Privacy Policy for Judge Penny Brown Reynolds Campaign The Judge Penny Brown Reynolds Campaign is committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding the personal information you share with us.
This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.
Information We Collect​ We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any other information submitted through forms, event sign-ups, donations, or communications with the campaign.
How We Use Your Information​ We use your information to communicate with you about the campaign, provide updates, organize volunteers, process donations, and share information about events, voting, and public issues relevant to the campaign.
Your information helps us better engage with supporters and voters.
Text Messaging and Mobile Information​ By opting in to receive text messages, you consent to receive campaign-related communications, including updates, reminders, and volunteer opportunities.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
Information Sharing​ We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share information with trusted service providers who assist in campaign operations, such as email or text messaging platforms, solely for the purpose of supporting campaign activities and in compliance with applicable laws.
We may also disclose information if required by law or to comply with legal processes.
Data Security​ We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.
While no system is completely secure, we work to ensure your information is handled responsibly.
Cookies and Website Use​ Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve user experience, analyze website traffic, and better understand how visitors interact with our content.
Your Choices​ You may opt out of email communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in our emails.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
You may also contact us to update or request removal of your personal information.
Children’s Privacy​ Our campaign does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13.
Updates to This Policy​ We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
