Why I'm Asking for Your Vote
As someone who has dedicated my life to serving and protecting others, I’m asking for your vote because I believe my experience can make a difference in our community.
Serving as a Lieutenant Commander in the NYPD taught me a lot about leadership, accountability, and the power of a committed team.
Now, I want to bring those values to Albany to fight for what’s right for Assembly District 11.
Our community deserves a representative who will prioritize the needs of our schools, seniors, and veterans.
I’ve seen firsthand how critical these resources are, and I’ll work to ensure AD 11 gets its fair share of funding and support.
I believe that every child should have access to a quality education, that our seniors should feel secure in their later years, and that our veterans are supported as they deserve.
I also know that affordability is a huge concern for many in Suffolk County.
Families are feeling the squeeze, and it’s becoming harder to make ends meet.
I’m committed to making life more affordable by fighting wasteful spending and ensuring taxpayer dollars are used wisely.
My goal is to make sure every dollar spent in Albany directly benefits our families and our community.
I believe in protecting our community from policies that put undue strain on our resources.
I want to see money directed to those who need it most—our schools, public safety, and essential community services—not to programs that don’t serve our residents.
If I have the privilege of representing you, I’ll be relentless in my pursuit of a safer, more supportive, and more affordable community for us all.
I’m asking for your support so we can make a positive impact in Assembly District 11 together.
