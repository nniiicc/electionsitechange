DONATE SUBSCRIBE ABOUT JOE CARDINALE Retired Lieutenant Commander of the NYPD and Owner/Operator of Blue Line Pilot Cars WHERE I COME FROM Joe Cardinale is a retired lieutenant commander of the NYPD and owner/operator of Blue Line Pilot Cars.
Born in Brooklyn and a resident of Amityville, NY, Joe has spent his life serving his community.
A THRIVING CAREER Joe Cardinale's lifelong commitment to public service has prepared him to take on the challenges facing NYS Assembly District 11.
He has spent his career protecting and serving the people of New York, and he is ready to bring that same dedication to Albany.
As an Assemblyman, Joe will fight to lower taxes, create jobs, and make our communities safer.
MISSION STATEMENT Experience, Leadership, Results Joe Cardinale is a Republican and Conservative candidate for NYS Assembly District 11.
His mission is to bring a fresh perspective and a wealth of experience to the political landscape of New York.
With his leadership and dedication to public service, Joe will work tirelessly to ensure that every resident of NYS Assembly District 11 has the opportunity to succeed.
