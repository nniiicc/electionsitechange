This Privacy Policy describes how Phillip Chen for State Assembly 2026 ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects personal information through our website and digital communications.
By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
Information We Collect
We collect information you voluntarily provide — such as your name, email address, mailing address, and phone number — as well as information collected automatically through cookies and analytics, including IP addresses and browsing behavior.
How We Use Your Information
We use collected information to communicate with you about campaign issues such as inflation and public safety, to send newsletters, and to extend event invitations.
We do not sell your personal information.
Cookies and Analytics
Our website uses cookies and third-party analytics tools to understand usage patterns and improve the user experience.
Your Communication Choices
You may unsubscribe from email updates at any time using the links provided in our messages, or opt out of SMS messages by replying "STOP."
Security
We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your personal information.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
Children's Privacy
Our website and communications are not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
California Resident Rights
California residents may request access to, inspection of, or deletion of their personal data by contacting phillip@phillipchen.org.
Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
