Representing the communities of Orange and San Bernardino counties.
The 59th Assembly District encompasses portions of Orange and San Bernardino counties and includes all or portions of the following cities:
- Anaheim Hills
- Brea
- Chino
- Chino Hills
- Fullerton
- North Tustin
- Orange
- Placentia
- Villa Park
- Yorba Linda
Constituents seeking assistance with state matters can reach the Assemblyman's official district office through the California State Assembly website.
