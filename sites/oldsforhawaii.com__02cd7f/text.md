Re-Elect Ikaika at a Glance About Ikaika District Resident for over 16 years Iraq War Combat Veteran B.S. and M.S. in Psychology Social Service Provider with a decade of experience working with the homeless in our district Proud Former HGEA, Unit 13 Union Member Lodge Le Progres de l'Oceanie F. & A.M Master Mason Veterans of Foreign Wars Post 8616 Member Kapahulu/Mo'ili'ili (KapMoi) Lions Club Lion Sons and Daughters of the 442nd RCT Member Board Member - Honolulu Community Action Program (HCAP) ​ What Ikaika Has Done Reduced Homelessness : Wrote and passed HB943 (Act 299) which created a statewide system to triage, treat, and redirect homeless individuals—especially those with mental health or substance use issues—away from jails and emergency rooms into coordinated healthcare and support services.
Increased Affordable Housing Access : Helped to pass HB703 (Act 282), which extended the state rent supplement program for our Kupuna.
Supported Education and Teachers : Helped pass bills expanding free school meals, improving student well-being, modernizing assessments and strengthening school infrastructure.
Ikaika also supported HB1890, providing annual teacher step increases and longevity pay to improve recruitment and retention.
