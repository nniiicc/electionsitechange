Kip Capley is a 7th generation Tennessean with a vision for our treasured, conservative values.
As your state representative for Tennessee’s 71st House District, he will be a strong, supporting voice for every person in his district.
If you live in Hardin, Maury, Wayne and Lawrence counties, he will take your concerns to Nashville and advocate for lowering taxes to reduce the burden of inflation on families while protecting the future of small businesses and entrepreneurs.
He will work tirelessly to reduce health care costs and ensure access to care is readily available.
Kip will fight to defend the life of the unborn and fully support law enforcement and first responders.
He will work to sponsor legislation to provide improved resources for educators and students.
He values your freedom and will defend your constitutional rights, especially the 2nd Amendment, and uphold the freedom that you deserve.
Vote for Kip Capley, Tennessee State Representative, 71st District.
