Rep.
Roy introduced a bill to study the pros and cons of the Pay It Forward model to pay for a college education.
Under this program it wouldn’t cost you anything to go to college while you there.
After graduation, students wouldn’t be thousands of dollars in debt, but they will still have to pay for their education.
The exact amount would depend on the graduates’ incomes.
A small percentage would be taken out of their paychecks over the next 20 or 30 years.
The bill was heard by the Joint Committee on Higher Education.
