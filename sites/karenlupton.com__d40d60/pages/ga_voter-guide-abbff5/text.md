Georgia’s State House District 83
House District 83 encompasses the southern half of Brookhaven and most of Chamblee.
Vote Early in Person
DeKalb County voters are able to cast their ballots up to 3 weeks before election day during the following times:
- Weekdays: 7am – 7pm
- Saturdays: 8am – 6pm
- Sundays: Noon – 5pm
You may vote at any early voting location within DeKalb County.

Vote by Mail
No excuse is required to have a ballot mailed to you.
Your ballot must be returned by 7:00pm on Election Day in order to be counted.
It is recommended to mail back your completed ballot as early as possible or drop it off at a drop box location.
Vote on Election Day
Election day polls are open from 7:00am to 7:00pm.
You can only vote at your own polling location on Election Day.
To find your polling location below, you will need to enter your name, birth date and county of residence.
