REGISTER Georgia’s State House District 83 House District 83 encompasses the southern half of Brookhaven and most of Chamblee.
Vote Early in Person DeKalb County voters are able to cast their ballots up to 3 weeks before election day during the following times: Weekdays: 7am – 7pm Saturdays: 8am – 6pm Sundays: Noon – 5pm You may vote at any early voting location within DeKalb County.

REGISTER Vote by Mail No excuse is required to have a ballot mailed to you.
Your ballot must be returned by 7:00pm on Election Day in order to be counted.
It is recommended to mail back your completed ballot as early as possible or drop it off at a drop box location.
