Policy Priorities
- Access to Healthcare Every New Mexican deserves access to high-quality, affordable healthcare.
Bills sponsored: 2023 HB 7 safeguarded access to reproductive and gender-affirming healthcare services (PASSED), 2023 HB 400 to take the first steps toward a Medicaid Forward program (PASSED), and 2024 HB 7 protected the Healthcare Affordability Fund (PASSED).
Next steps: Initiatives to increase affordable coverage (Medicaid Forward) and expand our healthcare workforce.
- Economic Development Our creative industries are the heart and soul of New Mexico, and represent untapped potential for economic growth and diversification.
Bills sponsored: 2023 HB 8 created the first-ever Creative Industries Division at the NM Environment Department, and funding for local grants.
(PASSED) Next steps: Build on a statewide plan to bring more funding to local communities for creative entrepreneurs and businesses
- Gun Violence Prevention Far too many families in New Mexico have been touched by the tragedy of violence involving firearms.
It will take a comprehensive approach to make our communities safer.
Bills Sponsored: 2023 SB 116, 2024 HB 127 Set the Age for purchase and possession of semi-automatic rifles to 21 years of age.
(FAILED) SB 5 prohibits firearms within 100 feet of polling places during elections (PASSED).
Next Steps: Reintroduce Set the Age, continue to pursue other solutions.
- Addressing Climate Change I am committed to protecting and restoring our air, land, water, and wildlife today and for future generations of New Mexicans.
Bills sponsored: HB 104 creates a statewide Public Health & Climate Program to help local communities identify and address the impact of climate change on community health.
(FAILED) HB 108 creates a local solar fund for local governments to invest in solar and energy storage infrastructure to speed our transition to clean energy and create resiliency centers.
(FAILED) Next steps: Lots more work to do here over the summer 2024 to advocate for funding both of these critical initiatives!
