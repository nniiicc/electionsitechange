House District 47
House District 47 is a beautiful and vibrant district within Santa Fe County.
HD 47 encompasses many communities including Rancho Viejo, Nava Adé, Oshara Village, Arroyo Hondo, Sol y Lomas, Foothills, De Vargas Heights, South Capitol, East Side, Museum Hill, northern Cañoncito, Glorieta, Cañada de los Alamos, South Capitol, parts of Downtown, Monte Sereno, areas north of Tano Road and Camino La Tierra, and part of Casa Solana.
