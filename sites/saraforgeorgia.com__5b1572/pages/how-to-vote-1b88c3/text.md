If you have any problems voting, please call the Democratic Party of Georgia Voter Protection Hotline: 1-888-730-5816 .
Early voting starts October 13th!
You may vote at any early polling location in Fulton County.
You must bring an ID to vote.
You may not use your phone in the polling booth, so check your ballot ahead of time on the Georgia My Voter Page and take notes!
