Jennifer Davis, a trailblazing entrepreneur and political outsider, is running for Illinois’ 8th Congressional District to protect Main Street America, put families first and protect the American Dream for future generations.
A proud wife and mother of ten, Jennifer co-founded Davisware with her husband Dan at just 17 years old, building both a family and a business from the ground up through sacrifice, hard work, and determination.
Raised in rural Wisconsin, Jennifer was taught the values of faith, perseverance, and community, all of which she will bring to her campaign for the United States Congress.
In Congress, she will address critical issues eroding opportunities in Illinois and beyond through a bold lens that squashes the status quo and brings true common sense.

A Life Built on Sacrifice, Innovation, and Family
At just 17, Jennifer and her husband, Dan, launched their first software venture—work that would ultimately evolve into Davisware, a pioneering technology company later headquartered in West Dundee, Illinois.
For more than 35 years, they bootstrapped the business with no outside capital until partnering with Serent Capital in 2019.
Along the way, they built technology that helped HVAC and commercial service companies automate, expand, and thrive—proving that innovation could empower the trades as much as it did Silicon Valley.
Davisware ultimately grew into a global leader in field service technology, supporting thousands of businesses worldwide.
The relentless journey of building a family business and a family demanded sacrifice but delivered profound rewards.
Jennifer and Dan’s commitment also fostered a tight-knit Davisware family, providing meaningful careers for hundreds of employees.
Her career is rooted in both trades and technology, and is uniquely relevant as government now shapes laws around emerging fields like AI, bitcoin, and blockchain.
This path sets Jennifer apart not only from Silicon Valley CEOs, but also from traditional politicians, giving her the experience, persistence, and innovative leadership to serve Illinois’ 8th District.
Jennifer’s best-selling book, Living Exponentially in Life and Business, showcases her blueprint for success, blending tactical empathy, joy, and resilience—qualities she now brings to her campaign for Congress.
After steering Davisware through the pandemic, she founded BExponential to empower women, guided by her Seven Principles of an Exponential Mindset, helping others pursue professional success without sacrificing joy, family, or purpose.
Both Jennifer and Dan grew up in farming families that were entrepreneurial at their core.
Their parents—and by extension, their children—were immersed in businesses ranging from automotive repair and sales to parts distribution, HVAC services, insurance, and even home demonstration goods like jewelry.
Their mothers, while raising 20 children collectively, still found ways to contribute—selling farm products, reading meters, and keeping households running through sheer determination.
These roots shaped Jennifer’s conviction that family and entrepreneurship are inseparable—and that opportunity flourishes when both are strong.
Jennifer’s vision for Congress is rooted in her proven record as a tech innovator and fierce advocate for families and communities.
She will champion parents’ rights, ensuring families have a voice in their children’s education, and she will protect the greatest asset our country has, our children.
Drawing on her entrepreneurial roots, she will cut red tape so small family businesses can flourish.
She is committed to equipping the next generation for success, protecting data from foreign threats, securing borders, and ensuring America leads in the responsible use of emerging technologies.
At a time when Congress faces a clear skills gap in areas like AI, digital currencies, and blockchain, her expertise, knowledge, and vision will close that gap.
What Jennifer brings to Congress is not only her record as an innovator and family advocate, but also her mission to inspire other political outsiders to engage—proving that everyday Americans can lead with courage, vision, and hope for the future.
As a political outsider, Jennifer rejects Washington’s inefficiencies.
Together, the Davis’ built a global tech company, ran a family business, raised ten children, and uplifted their community—living a life of shared sacrifice and purpose.
In doing so, they built their own American Dream, and Jennifer’s goal is to defend that opportunity so others can do the same.
From the long hours growing Davisware to mentoring employees and their families, Jennifer never shied from hard work.
That journey equips her to tackle challenges with a servant’s heart.
In Congress, she will deliver transparent, data-driven leadership that strengthens communities, empowers entrepreneurs, safeguards America’s security, and ensures Illinois’ 8th District has a prosperous and safe future.
