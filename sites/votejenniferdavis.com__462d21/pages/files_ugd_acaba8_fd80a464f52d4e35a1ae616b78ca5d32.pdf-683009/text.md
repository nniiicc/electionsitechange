2026 Internship Program Overview Committee Overview: We have several committees in our internship program.
Each committee will consist of a team ranging from 3 to 12 members.
Finance Committee - Opportunity to lead fundraising efforts and donor outreach.
Policy Point Committee - Research issues and create messaging that is consistent with the campaign.
Community Events Committee - Plan rallies, township meetings, meet & greets.
Field Team Committee - In charge of canvassing, tracking signs, and voter outreach.
Data and Outreach Committee - Phone banking, Data Organization, Produce Issue Research.
Communications Committee - Tracking and producing messaging for texts and emails.
Digital & Social Media Committee - Reshare content, create dialogue, contribute content.
Volunteer Recruitment Committee - Recruiting and Coordination.
Voter Support Committee - Helping people and listening to what matters most in their communities.
Coalition Building Committee - Coordinate coalition partners' participation in key campaign events.
GOTV Committee - Help voters get to the polls informed and ready to vote, and participate at the polls.
Research & Strategy - Opposition research, support the policy committee with contrast research and factual sourcing.
