Balancing Federal Debt
Updated: Aug 10, 2024
A balanced, responsible approach to federal debt considers both the need for fiscal sustainability and the importance of investing in the future of our country.
My stance on the federal debt emphasizes the importance of prudent fiscal management, economic growth, and strategic investments in critical areas such as education, healthcare, and infrastructure.
Reducing the federal debt should involve a combination of measures, including optimizing government spending, reforming tax policies to ensure fairness and efficiency, and fostering economic growth to increase revenues.
It's crucial to prioritize spending that yields long-term benefits for society, such as investments in education and sustainable energy with base load electrical policy.
We must move away from unsustainable subsidized energy policy 100%.
By taking a responsible approach to budgeting and focusing on policies that promote economic prosperity for all, we can address the challenges posed by the federal debt while laying the foundation for a stronger, more resilient future.
Let's work together to achieve fiscal health without compromising our commitment to the values and investments that drive our nation forward.
