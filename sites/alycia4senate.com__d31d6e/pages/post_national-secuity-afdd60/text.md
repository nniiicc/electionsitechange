Investing In National Security
National defense is a paramount duty of our government, ensuring the safety and security of our nation and its citizens.
My approach to national defense emphasizes a strong and ready military, strategic diplomacy, and robust intelligence capabilities to address both conventional threats and emerging challenges such as various types of warfare and global terrorism.
Investing in our military's readiness, modernizing our defense capabilities, and supporting our service members and their families are critical components of a comprehensive national defense strategy.
At the same time, it's essential to pursue diplomatic avenues and international conversarions to address global security issues and reduce the likelihood of conflict.
Strengthening our national defense also involves ensuring our domestic infrastructure and cybersecurity are resilient against attacks.
By fostering innovation, enhancing our intelligence capabilities, and working collaboratively with our allies, we can protect our national interests and maintain peace and stability in an increasingly complex world.
