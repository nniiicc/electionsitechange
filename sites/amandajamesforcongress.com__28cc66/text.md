Amanda Carter James is a lifelong Georgian and Cartersville native whose story reflects the grit, faith, and determination that define the people of Georgia's 11th District.
Born two months premature and weighing just four pounds, Amanda grew up legally blind in her left eye — an early challenge that taught her resilience and shaped her belief that every child deserves a fair chance to thrive.
The daughter of Carol Carter Garnigan and the late Wilson Carter, and stepdaughter of the late William "Butch" Garnigan, Amanda learned responsibility early.
She carried those lessons to Florida A&M University and later to Kennesaw State University, where she earned her B.S. in Political Science with a concentration in Public Service & Policy — completing her degree while raising three children.
She is running as a write-in candidate because she believes leadership should be rooted in people, not partisanship.
Her message is clear: The future does not belong to Red or Blue — the future belongs to YOU.

THE ISSUES Common-Sense Solutions for a Stronger Georgia Seven priorities grounded in compassion, accountability, and the everyday realities of Georgia's 11th District.
Affordability & Cost of Living Groceries, rent, mortgages, and utilities are rising faster than wages.
I'll fight for policies that lower everyday costs and let working families build stable, secure lives.
Healthcare & Prescription Costs Too many seniors spend entire Social Security checks on medication.
Healthcare should be accessible, transparent, and affordable — never a financial burden.
Mental Health — Especially for Children Our children face unprecedented anxiety, depression, and trauma.
We need stronger support in schools, better counseling access, and community resources that meet families where they are.
Government Accountability & Transparency Washington spends trillions with little oversight.
Taxpayers deserve clarity, honest budgeting, and reforms that put people before politics.
Community Safety & Support Safety is about strong communities, mental health resources, youth programs, and economic stability — balanced, community-centered approaches that protect families and build trust.
Education & Opportunity Every child deserves a fair chance: strong public schools, support for teachers, and clear pathways to college, trade programs, and good-paying jobs.
Independent Leadership Not owned by a party, a political machine, or special interests.
My commitment is to the people of Georgia's 11th District — all of them.
