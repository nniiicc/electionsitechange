Protecting Girls Sports Sanctity of Life Committed to protecting the most fragile members of our human community, Ed authored the Living Infants Fairness & Equality Act “LIFE Act”, which ensures that every human life is recognized under Georgia law by requiring fathers provide child support to pregnant mothers, providing full income tax deductions to expecting parents, and prohibiting abortion when a child’s heartbeat is detectable, subject to a number of exceptions supported by a majority of Georgians including cases of rape and cases in which the life of the mother is at risk.
In addition to being endorsed by the Georgia Life Alliance, Ed initially authored and helped pass Senate Bill 166 bill that removes crushing regulations to allow churches and non-profits to provide free homes to pregnant mothers.
Ed’s leadership helped pass restrictions on late term abortion in House Bill 954 and helped secure unanimous bipartisan passage of Senate Bill 77 , the Unborn Victims of Violence Act, in which Georgia became one of 30 states to prosecute violent crimes against a pregnant woman as crimes against two distinct persons.
