Our campaign is dedicated to ensuring every eligible voter has the information needed to participate in our democracy.
How to Vote STEP 01 Registration Check your voter registration status and register if you haven't already.
Ensure your information is up to date.
STEP 02 Voting Options Explore your options for Early Voting or cast your ballot on Election Day.
Choose the time and location that works best for you.
STEP 03 Required ID Bring proper identification if required by Law.
Being prepared ensures a smooth and non-partisan voting experience.
Find Your Poll Site Look up your exact poll site and view a sample ballot for the upcoming election.
FIND MY POLL SITE Important Voting Deadlines REGISTRATION DEADLINE Ensure you are registered to vote by this date to participate in the upcoming election cycle. ​ For the November 3, 2026 General Election: Last day to apply online or by mail is October 24, 2026 ​ Last day to apply in person at your county board of elections is November 2, 2026 ​ Put your ballot in the mail ensuring it receives a postmark no later than November 3, 2026 ​ Drop off your ballot to your county Board of Elections Office no later than November 3, 2026 by 9pm ​ Drop off your ballot to an early voting poll site in your county between October 24 and November 1, 2026 ​ Drop off your ballot to an election day poll site on November 3, 2026 no later than 9pm EARLY VOTING PERIOD Take advantage of flexible voting hours at designated early voting poll sites across the district. ​ Early Voting begins October 24 through November 1, 2026 ELECTION DAY Final day to cast your ballot at your assigned polling location.
Polls are open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
