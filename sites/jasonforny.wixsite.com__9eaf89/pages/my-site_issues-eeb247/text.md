FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
State Senate 27 Candidate Jason Murillo, along with State Assembly 74 Candidate Veronica Gonzalez, Unveils Multi-Billion Dollar Housing, Security, and Workforce Blueprint for New York
NEW YORK, NY — Jason Murillo, candidate for New York State Senate District 27, with his running mate Veronica Gonzalez candidate for New York State Assembly District 74 announced a sweeping package of infrastructure, housing, and workforce policies designed to fundamentally reshape public living and safety standards across the state.
Developed in collaboration with campaign policy coordinator Veronica Gonzalez, the comprehensive 2027 implementation platform introduces unprecedented investments into affordable housing, NYCHA security, and union apprenticeships.
At the center of Murillo’s platform is the "Reimagining Justice" initiative, a multi-billion-dollar adaptive reuse master plan to convert 16 closed New York State correctional facilities into gated, luxury-designed affordable housing complexes managed under the Section 8 Landlord Program.
The comprehensive financial projections for the project outline a $2.088 billion capital expenditure to develop 4,000 premium apartments, averaging 1,000 square feet each.
This large-scale capital investment will act as a major economic engine, projected to generate 14,400 construction jobs statewide over staggered 24-to-36 month build cycles.
The campaign is further expanding housing access through "Operation Shelter Rescue," proposing a 50-building development that will provide an additional 8,960 apartments supported by 24-hour MTA bus service from Dutchess County to NYC.
Recognizing the urgent need for enhanced safety in public housing, Murillo’s platform mandates the physical security gating of NYCHA complexes citywide.
To staff these secured entryways, the plan calls for the creation of a dedicated, 24-hour NYPD Security Guard Bureau.
This monumental infrastructure and personnel rollout demands an estimated initial capital investment of $73.48 million alongside a recurring annual operating budget of $531.06 million.
To sustain these upgraded facilities and bolster the local economy, the campaign introduced a robust series of employment training programs for implementation in 2027.
Highlighting this workforce initiative is a massive expansion of the MTA and NYCHA painter workforce, integrating union employees under Local District Council #9.
Additional job-creation measures include a NYCHA pest removal apprenticeship and a specialized locksmith training program projected to yield 680 new employees annually.
