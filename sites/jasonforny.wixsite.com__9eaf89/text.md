With a career spanning both community service, media, public service, and professional excellence, Jason is ready to bring conservative common sense solutions to District 27 to protect our streets and empower local families.
His vision for the State Senate is clear: accountability in Albany, support for our police, and a thriving economy for all.
Jason isn't just running for office; he’s fighting for the soul of the city he calls home.

ECONOMIC GROWTH

LOCAL GOVERNANCE
