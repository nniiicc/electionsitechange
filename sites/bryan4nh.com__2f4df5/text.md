I’m running because too many of today’s challenges are bigger than any one person can solve alone.” I’m not a politician.
I’m a Moultonborough resident, a father of a young daughter, a grateful husband, and an owner of a chaotic Golden Retriever named Jojo.
For more than ten years, I’ve worked as a licensed social worker, helping families navigate some of life’s toughest challenges.
Every day I hear the same concerns: the cost of living keeps rising, young families can’t afford to stay here, employers struggle to find workers, and too many people can’t access the mental health services they need.
My career has taught me that lasting solutions don’t come from shouting louder—they come from listening, finding common ground, and bringing people together.
Now I’m running to represent District 3 in the New Hampshire House, bringing that same approach to Concord — listening to his neighbors, finding common ground, and working for solutions that make life better for families in our communities.

A Representative Who Works.
Bryan Randolph has called Moultonborough home since 2013, where he lives with his wife, Lauren, and their daughter.
A licensed social worker specializing in anxiety and trauma, Bryan has spent more than a decade helping people navigate some of life’s toughest challenges.
That experience taught him something important: lasting solutions start with listening.
Now he’s running for State Representative to bring that same approach to Concord — listening to his neighbors, finding common ground, and working for solutions that make life better for families in our communities.
