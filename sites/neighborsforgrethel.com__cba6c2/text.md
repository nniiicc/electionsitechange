Ron Grethel
Ohio House District 62
On the Ballot - November 2026
Two parties shouldn’t be the only option.
I’m a husband, father of three daughters, Army National Guard veteran, and lifelong Ohioan rooted in Loveland, where I bought my childhood home to raise my family.
I served eight years as an Air Defense Artillery officer, including deployments to Iraq and Afghanistan.
Today I work in technology after building a career in the insurance industry.
What I’m Focused On
- Ranked-choice voting and more competitive elections
- Open primaries and fair district maps
- Making government more representative and accountable
- Supporting families through paid parental leave and affordable maternity care
- Fixing Clermont County’s infrastructure and planning responsibly for growth
- Expanding reliable, affordable energy while protecting taxpayers
- Cutting unnecessary bureaucracy and making state government work better
