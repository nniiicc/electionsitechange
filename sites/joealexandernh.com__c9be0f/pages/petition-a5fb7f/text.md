What’s Being Proposed
Hillsborough County is weighing county-owned land off Route 114 in Goffstown — between Saint Anselm Drive and Mast Road — as the site for a new, roughly $285 million jail to replace the aging Valley Street facility in Manchester.
The plan would bring a 356-cell, 485-bed correctional facility, plus a 70-bed community corrections center, into our town.
It is one of three options the county is studying, and the Goffstown Select Board is now reviewing it.
Read the coverage — New Hampshire Union Leader, July 15, 2026
Why This Matters
A decision this large should not be made behind closed doors.
Here is why residents across town are standing together in opposition.
Goffstown is a growing residential community of families, small businesses, and neighborhoods.
A county correctional facility belongs where it fits the surrounding land — not in the heart of our town.
The people who live here should have full transparency and a genuine seat at the table before any site is chosen — not a decision handed down after the fact.
Traffic, infrastructure, emergency services, and the long-term impact on property values and community character all fall on Goffstown taxpayers and families.
The county should exhaust every alternative that does not place this burden on a single town before ever asking Goffstown to carry it.
