Add My Name Why This Matters — Residents Signed What’s Being Proposed The county has its eye on Goffstown.
Hillsborough County is weighing county-owned land off Route 114 in Goffstown — between Saint Anselm Drive and Mast Road — as the site for a new, roughly $285 million jail to replace the aging Valley Street facility in Manchester.
The plan would bring a 356-cell, 485-bed correctional facility, plus a 70-bed community corrections center, into our town.
It is one of three options the county is studying, and the Goffstown Select Board is now reviewing it.
Read the coverage — New Hampshire Union Leader, July 15, 2026 Why This Matters Goffstown deserves a real say in its own future.
A decision this large should not be made behind closed doors.
Here is why residents across town are standing together in opposition.
The Wrong Place Goffstown is a growing residential community of families, small businesses, and neighborhoods.
A county correctional facility belongs where it fits the surrounding land — not in the heart of our town.
Residents Deserve a Voice The people who live here should have full transparency and a genuine seat at the table before any site is chosen — not a decision handed down after the fact.
Real Costs to Our Town Traffic, infrastructure, emergency services, and the long-term impact on property values and community character all fall on Goffstown taxpayers and families.
Better Options Exist The county should exhaust every alternative that does not place this burden on a single town before ever asking Goffstown to carry it.
