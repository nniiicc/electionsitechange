Thank you for your support,
District 49
I am honored to continue to serve the people of the 49th district as your representative in Springfield.
Over the course of this campaign and the last six years, you have graciously opened your doors to me and my team to talk about the issues that matter most to you.
We tackled tough subjects at your doors, and I will carry every single one of our conversations with me to Springfield as we work to make our communities safer; to cut down the cost of living; and to continue to get our state economy on strong financial footing.
I will continue to fight for your right to be your full, authentic selves; to make your own, personal decisions about your reproductive health care; to go to school free from the threat of gun violence; and to live in a peaceful and just world.
We have work to do here in Illinois to ensure that we are taking care of each other and our neighbors.
I am ready.
Together we will be bold and brave and stand up for the things in which we so deeply believe.
