District 49
Illinois house District 49
is in the far-west suburbs of Chicago, overlapping the western border of DuPage County, eastern border of Kane County, and the far-western tip of Cook County.
It includes parts or all of Elgin, South Elgin, Bartlett, Wayne, Geneva, West Chicago, Batavia, Winfield, Aurora, and Naperville.
If your home is near the boundries of any district, you should check your address specifically to verify your districts.
Verify your districts - Illinois State Board of Elections Map
You can (and should!) check your address with the Illinois State Board of Elections confirm which districts are yours.
Their interactive map can be found here.
After searching for your address, near the bottom of the search results will be “State Representative” which will list your current representative and your district number, followed by a link to their Illinois General Assembly member page.
