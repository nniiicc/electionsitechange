Donate Today:-) Welcome Representative Dr.
Imani Barnes, DrPH, is the Georgia State Representative for House District 86 and a nationally trained public health scientist.
A lifelong DeKalb County resident and proud parent, she champions policies that advance health equity, strengthen public education, and support working families.
Dr.
Barnes brings a data-driven, community-first approach to the Georgia General Assembly, focusing on childhood nutrition, mental health access, maternal health, and youth development.
She is committed to building healthier, more resilient communities across Georgia through evidence-based leadership and has won a national "Rising Star Award" in 2025 from Future Caucus for her leadership rooted in collaboration.

This initiative is essential for addressing food insecurity and ensuring that vulnerable populations, including those benefiting from Dekalb free school lunch programs, have access to necessary mental health resources.
