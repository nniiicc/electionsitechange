Home About Issues Endorsements News Volunteer Donate AMA honors Senator Briggs with Government Service Award February 12, 2025 The American Medical Association presented Tennessee State Senator Richard Briggs, M.D., with the prestigious AMA Award for Outstanding Government Service.
A retired cardiothoracic surgeon with 30 years of practice, an Army veteran, and a consensus builder, Senator Briggs has been a tireless leader in efforts to reduce firearm violence, expand insurance coverage, and address the opioid crisis.
His military record alone is extraordinary.
After earning his medical degree from the University of Kentucky and entering active Army service, he joined the Jarvik artificial heart transplant team, was recalled during Operation Desert Storm and awarded the Bronze Star for combat operations in southern Iraq, served in Afghanistan following 9/11, and deployed to Baghdad as the Senior Trauma Surgeon in Iraq.
All across 38 years of active and reserve military service.
In the legislature, Senator Briggs has championed Medicaid expansion in Tennessee since 2010, driven by his firsthand understanding of what insurance access means for patients.
Perhaps most telling is how colleagues across the aisle describe him.
AMA Board Chair Dr.
Michael Suk noted that people of all backgrounds and across the political spectrum consistently say the same thing about Senator Briggs: he’s collaborative, accessible, a good listener, and always open to authentic dialogue even in disagreement.
Senator Briggs is the rare leader who has served his country in uniform, healed patients in the operating room, and now fights for them in the halls of government.
This honor is richly earned.
