MEET JOHN CROISANT John Croisant is uniquely qualified to tackle the challenges facing our communities.
As a former teacher, coach, local businessman, and dedicated community volunteer, he understands the importance of strong leadership that puts people first.
Most importantly, John is a parent who knows that the future of our country depends on the opportunities we create for the next generation.
In Congress, he will fight for what is best for our families, our schools, and our economy.
As a parent, John knows that real solutions come from listening to families.
He will work to ensure that our government is accountable, transparent, and responsive to the people it serves.
John will stand up for policies that strengthen education, lower costs for working families, and create a brighter future for our children.
As a former teacher at Edison Prep, John knows firsthand how vital it is to invest in education.
He is committed to supporting teachers, students, and parents to ensure every child has access to a quality education—regardless of their zip code.
John’s experience as a coach has taught him the power of teamwork and perseverance.
In Congress, he will fight to protect funding for essential programs, including education, infrastructure, and job training, ensuring that every American has the tools to succeed.
John Croisant is ready to bring common-sense leadership to Washington.
He will fight for hardworking Oklahomans and ensure that our voices are heard.
Join John in the fight for a stronger future.
PRIORITIES As your next congressman, John will put the issues that matter to YOU front and center.
FIGHTING TO LOWER COSTS FOR OUR FAMILIES John Croisant is committed to making life more affordable for hardworking Oklahomans.
He will fight to lower the costs —because no family should have to choose between paying their bills and getting the care they need.
BUILDING A STRONG ECONOMY John Croisant believes that if you work hard and play by the rules, you should be able to build a good life for yourself and your family—own a home, send your kids to college, and retire with dignity.
Every Oklahoman, no matter their zip code, deserves access to these opportunities.
KEEPING OUR COMMUNITIES SAFE John Croisant is committed to making our neighborhoods safer by keeping illegal guns off our streets and strengthening the relationship between law enforcement and the communities they serve.
He believes in commonsense solutions that protect both public safety and individual rights.
