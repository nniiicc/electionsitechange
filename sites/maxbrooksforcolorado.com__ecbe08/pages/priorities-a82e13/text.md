My unwavering commitment extends to: Public Safety I will work continue to work with law enforcement to make sure they have the resources they need to fight crime and keep our community safe.
Education As a parent, I am a firm believer in the importance of parental rights.
I will continue to work to protect and empower parents in making decisions about their children's education and upbringing.
Economy I support the principle of small and limited government.
I will continue to oppose overregulation and fight to defend TABOR, lower taxes, and reform property taxes, while promoting individual liberty and economic freedom.
2nd Amendment I will continue to tirelessly defend our right to keep and bear arms.
As I represent our community in the Colorado House of Representatives, my vision for small government, opposition to overregulation, and core values will continue to guide my decisions and initiatives.
I am committed to fostering a thriving, free, and prosperous community by championing the policies that reflect our core values.
