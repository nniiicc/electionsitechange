Policy Priorities
GOALS FOR GEORGIA
- Pass legislation to lower income and administrative eligibility requirements, opening up vital coverage to thousands of working families and low-income adults.
- Implement strict state oversight and transparent rate-review processes to crack down on unjustified premium hikes and ensure insurance companies prioritize patient care.
- Create a dedicated state subsidy fund to bridge the financial gap left by expired federal ACA subsidies and keep marketplace plans affordable.
- Support reproductive freedom
AFFORDABLE HEALTHCARE
- Modernize the state funding formula to direct substantial investments toward schools serving high concentrations of children living in poverty.
- Enact safeguards to ensure that public education tax dollars go to public schools and oppose policies that weaken Georgia's public education system.

Her son was thriving at Spalding Drive Elementary in Sandy Springs.
When Fulton County announced the school would close, her family advocated to keep it open.
When the advocacy efforts failed and the school closed, she grieved the loss with other so many other families.
FULLY-FUNDED SCHOOLS
- Fight for mandatory safe storage legislation that requires firearms to be securely locked away, with specific protections and penalties involving children.
- Close remaining legal loopholes by requiring comprehensive background checks on all firearm purchases, including private transfers and gun shows.
- Establish Extreme Risk Protection Orders to allow courts to temporarily remove firearms from individuals demonstrating clear warning signs of danger.
SAFE COMMUNITIES
- Pass legislation to ban monopoly utility providers from raising rates to pad profits, tightening oversight to protect families from sudden rate spikes.
- Crack down on predatory corporate landlords and rent-gouging algorithms, increasing taxes on mass-purchased properties to prevent families from being priced out.
- Mandate strict resource-efficiency standards for data centers, ensuring corporations pay for infrastructure upgrades rather than straining community water and power resources.
