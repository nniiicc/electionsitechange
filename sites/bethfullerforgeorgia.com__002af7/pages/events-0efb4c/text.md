RoWR to The Door Canvass with Beth Fuller for House District 53
Join Roswell Women Rising and Beth Fuller, candidate for House District 53, on Thursday, October 22 for an evening of knocking doors for democracy!
We'll kick off at 6:00 PM in the Roswell Cultural Arts Center parking lot.
No canvassing experience needed.
We'll provide training and supplies, and we'll pair new volunteers with experienced canvassers.
Just bring a charged phone, comfortable shoes, and your voice!
Roswell Women Rising (RoWR) is 400+ local women building a strong, inclusive community through civic engagement, advocacy, and connection.

Everyone is welcome at this canvass, and if you like what you see, come roar with us!
