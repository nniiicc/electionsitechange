Soo Hong DEFENDING THE AMERICAN DREAM Soo's Story Soo Hong was born in Seoul, South Korea.
At 10 years old, her family legally immigrated to the United States in search of a better life and the American Dream.
She learned English with the help of amazing teachers who volunteered after school at Powers Ferry Elementary and went on to graduate from Georgia Tech and then Mercer Law School.
Now, she runs a successful legal practice with offices in Gwinnett and Marietta.
As our state representative, Soo is working hard each day to protect the American Dream by making Georgia safer, stronger, and more prosperous than ever before so that our future generations have the same opportunity she did. ﻿ As the daughter of immigrants, I’ve seen how sacrifice and hard work allow everyone to achieve the American Dream. ” On The Issues Rep.
Soo Hong is working every day to have a positive impact on the lives of all Georgians.
Lowering Taxes & the Cost of Living Soo has worked to cut income, and now the average family pays 55% less in taxes than they did in 2018.
She's also supported serious property tax reform that recently capped out of control assessments to the rate of inflation.
On top of this, Soo has also secured multiple income tax rebates for taxpayers totalling up to $2000 in the last few years alone.
She will continue to responsibly spend your tax dollars rather than making you pay more to the government.
Creating Jobs and Supporting Small Business Our economy is the engine for our state—and in the General Assembly, Soo has fought for policies that help job creators create more jobs and reduce red tape that make it harder to run a business.
She's also made it a top priority to ensure our students are prepared for good, high-paying, 21st Century jobs.
Investing in Public Safety A government’s first priority the safety of its citizens.
That’s why Soo has passed legislation to crack down on violent crime, gang activity, human trafficking and lawlessness in our state while investing in public safety and our law enforcement.
Quality, Affordable Healthcare for All Georgia is leading the way in innovative healthcare policy that gives providers and patients the power to make the best healthcare decisions for themselves and their families.
Soo knows we also need a safety net for struggling families.
That is why she's helped pass legislation for mothers in need, reformed our mental healthcare system, and supported resolutions to protect access to IVF treatments.
Defending Our Constitutional Rights As a first-generation immigrant, Soo has a deep appreciation for the Constitution and the rights it provides to us.
She will never waver in protecting your rights because they are what make America truly special.
