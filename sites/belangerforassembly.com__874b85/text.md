Legislative Priorities
Focusing on working families, small business, and taxpayers
Lower Taxes and Strong Schools:
As a husband and father of three kids, I see how rising costs are stretching our families thin.
I believe we can lower your property tax burden while also investing in our schools.
The State of Wisconsin has a significant budget surplus - let's work together to reduce property taxes for families, provide resources directly to the classroom, and build a strong future for the next generation.
Putting Communities First:
As demand for technology infrastructure continues to grow, it's important that we proceed responsibly.
That's why I support legislation preventing data centers from passing costs on to residents and taxpayers along with protecting our water resources from overuse.
Big tech needs to pay their own way, not put costs on the back of our communities, residents, and local governments.
Quality and Affordable Healthcare:
I believe in price transparency for healthcare, protecting those with pre-existing conditions, supporting postpartum care for mothers, and finding effective solutions to reduce costs while increasing access.
