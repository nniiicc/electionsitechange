About John
A Proven Record of Fiscal Leadership
John Belanger has spent his career solving problems and serving the Sheboygan community.
As a former alderman from 2012 to 2018, John has delivered real results - not just promises.
A UW-Eau Claire graduate with a degree in political science and history, John brings both the knowledge and the experience to be a strong independent voice for the people of the 26th District in Madison.
John and his wife Therese have been married 37 years and raised their three children - Matthew, Charlie, and Meghan - right here in Sheboygan.
