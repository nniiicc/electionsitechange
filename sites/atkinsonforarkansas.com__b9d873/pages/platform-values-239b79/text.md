We should be making investments that make northwest Arkansas safer, healthier, and more affordable.
All of these priorities have a good return on your investment, or ROI.
This is what getting your money's worth from your government looks like.
Safer
Keeping our communities safe means investing in people and infrastructure, not just prisons.
We need to bring economic opportunities to our communities, prioritize infrastructure that keeps up with growth, and support our first responders who put their lives on the line.
I support legislation that will…
- Keep our public schools safe and strong.
- Prioritize smart planning to alleviate traffic and keep roads safe.
- Increase line-of-duty death and disability benefits for first responders.
Healthier
By investing in mental health care, substance abuse treatment, and commonsense healthcare access, we can build stronger, healthier communities and lower the cost of healthcare for all Arkansans.
I support legislation that will…
- Expand access to mental health care.
- Prioritize substance abuse rehabilitation as an alternative to incarceration.
- Allow exceptions for rape and incest to the state’s extreme total abortion ban.
More Affordable
The cost of living in NWA is ever increasing with no relief in sight.
We need an economy that works for working people and that allows us to keep more of our hard-earned money.
I support legislation that will…
- Lower taxes and increase wages for working families.
- Make millionaires and billionaires pay their fair share.
- Give tax credits to developers of affordable housing.
- Invest in child care and health care in order to keep costs lower for families.
