Arkansas' 19th District
Arkansas House District 19 covers south and west Springdale, Johnson, and north Fayetteville.
This is one of the most competitive seats in the state, and Jamie’s running to win.
In HD19 you’ll find the best public schools in the state, the Botanical Gardens, Lake Fayetteville, and some of the fastest growing residential and commercial areas of our region.
HD19 deserves a representative who can keep up with the growth here and has solutions to the major issue that face the people that call this district home.
Beyond this, District 19 is home.
It is home for Jamie Atkinson and for more than 30,000 other Arkansans.
