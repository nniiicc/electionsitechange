Hudson and Litchfield
I am running because I believe New Hampshire is the best state in our nation, and I believe in our state’s core value: Live Free or Die.
But too many families in Litchfield and Hudson are feeling the pressure of rising costs that our state legislature can help relieve.
I've heard it directly from residents at town meetings, budget hearings, and while serving on the Budget Committee: property taxes are too high, driven largely by education funding issues and other local burdens.
I will bring my proven track record of fiscal oversight and community service to Concord to fight for real solutions.
