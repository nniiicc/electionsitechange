Primary August 18, 2026 General election Nov 3, 2026 Register to Vote Meet Sara I’m a life-long Alaskan and have lived in Juneau for over 37 years.
I spent three decades in education, teaching high school for 21 years.
I am proud to serve as your Democratic State House Representative, currently in my fourth term.
I love engaging in my community as a volunteer, working to support women, those less fortunate and the environment.
My husband Mark and I ran a small business selling locally caught fish for nearly two decades.
I love exploring the outdoors in our beautiful and wet Southeast rainforest.
I’m running for re-election to the State House because I love my community and our state.
This is a critical time for our state and Alaskans, as we face massive budget deficits, cuts to state services and a shrinking PFD.
I believe our bright future lies in a great education for our kids, good jobs for every Alaskan and safe, healthy, caring communities.
Together we can keep Alaska a great place to live, work, love and play.
I would be honored to be your State House Representative for House District 4 (Downtown Juneau, Thane, Lemon Creek, Douglas Island, Airport area, and the Mendenhall Valley); I look forward to earning your vote.
