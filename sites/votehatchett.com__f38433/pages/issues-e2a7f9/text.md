Home About Matt Issues Contact Donate Issues As Appropriations Chairman, Matt Hatchett has been a champion for our values — delivering real results on the issues that matter most to District 155.
Affordability Matt Hatchett is fighting to keep more money in your pocket.
He voted to eliminate Georgia's state income tax so families keep more of what they earn.
He championed property tax cuts and caps to protect homeowners from rising costs, and he secured significant teacher pay raises — investing in the people who invest in our children every day.
Public Safety Keeping our neighborhoods safe is non-negotiable.
Matt toughened penalties for gang activity and violent crime — zero tolerance for those who threaten our families.
He increased law enforcement pay, funded school safety improvements, and backed measures targeting human trafficking and fentanyl trafficking to protect communities across the district.
Healthcare Families in Laurens and Johnson Counties deserve quality care close to home.
Matt expanded rural healthcare funding, grew medical education programs to address the physician shortage, and supported Georgia's Medicaid waiver to extend coverage while protecting taxpayers.
He was instrumental in overhauling Georgia's mental health system and funded construction of the state's only mental health hospital.
Economic Development Good jobs don't happen by accident.
Matt funded workforce training through the Technical College System of Georgia so our workers are ready for the jobs of tomorrow.
He continued investing in infrastructure — better roads, bridges, and broadband — and secured regional economic development grants that bring new business and opportunity to every corner of our district.
