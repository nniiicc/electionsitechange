Home About Issues Volunteer Donate Today Privacy Policy Effective date: July 23, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how Cleveland for Indiana (“we,” “us,” or “the campaign”), the campaign committee for Chris Cleveland, candidate for Indiana State Representative in the 9th District, collects and uses information you give us on clevelandforindiana.com.
Information We Collect Information you give us.
When you sign up for updates, fill out the volunteer form, or RSVP to an event, we collect the information you enter — typically your name, email address, mobile phone number, ZIP code, any message you write, and the volunteer activities you pledge to.
Contribution information.
Donations are processed by WinRed, not by this website.
WinRed collects your payment information under its own privacy policy.
We receive the contributor information that federal and Indiana law require us to record.
How We Use Your Information We use the information you give us to send you campaign news and updates, to respond to your questions, to organize volunteers and events, to ask for contributions, and to comply with Indiana campaign finance reporting requirements.
Text Messages (SMS/MMS) If you provide your mobile phone number on one of our forms, you agree to receive text messages from Cleveland for Indiana — including messages sent using autodialed or automated methods — covering campaign updates, event invitations, and volunteer and fundraising requests.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.
You may also email us at contact@clevelandforindiana.com to be removed.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
No mobile information will be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
Mobile numbers are shared only with the service providers that help us deliver our messages, and those providers may not use the information for any other purpose.
How We Share Information We do not sell your personal information.
We share it only: With vendors and service providers who work on our behalf — for example, our form processor, email and text message providers, and analytics provider — and only so they can perform those services for us.
When required by law, including the contributor information that Indiana campaign finance law requires us to report publicly to the Indiana Election Division.
Your Choices Email: click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send.
Text messages: reply STOP to any message.
Everything else: email us and we will correct or delete the information we hold about you, except records we are legally required to keep.
Data Security and Retention We take reasonable steps to protect the information you give us, and we keep it only as long as we need it for the purposes described above or as the law requires.
No website or database is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Children This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
Changes to This Policy We may update this policy from time to time.
The effective date at the top will tell you when it was last changed.

Program Description Cleveland for Indiana sends text messages about the campaign of Chris Cleveland for Indiana State Representative, 9th District.
Messages include campaign news and updates, event invitations and reminders, volunteer requests, and requests for contributions.
Messages may be sent using autodialed or otherwise automated methods.
How to Opt In You join the program by entering your mobile phone number on a form on clevelandforindiana.com — for example, the volunteer form — and submitting it.
The disclosure shown with that form tells you what you are agreeing to.
You may also be added if you give your number to a campaign volunteer or staff member and agree to be contacted by text.
Providing a mobile number is optional; you can use our forms without one.
Message Frequency and Cost Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply, according to the plan you have with your wireless carrier.
Cleveland for Indiana does not charge you for these messages.
How to Opt Out Reply STOP to any message from us at any time to stop receiving texts.
You will receive one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, after which we will send no further messages unless you opt in again.
You may also email contact@clevelandforindiana.com to be removed.
How to Get Help Reply HELP to any message from us for assistance, or contact us at contact@clevelandforindiana.com .
Carriers and Delivery Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Delivery is subject to transmission limits and carrier conditions outside our control.
Supported carriers may change without notice.
Eligibility You must be at least 13 years old and either the subscriber of the mobile number you provide or authorized by the subscriber to enroll it.
If your mobile number changes or is reassigned, please tell us so we can update our records.
