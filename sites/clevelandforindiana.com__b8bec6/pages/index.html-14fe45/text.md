Chris was raised in a small town in a rural county.
His father was an auto mechanic and his mom a school
lunch lady.
He traveled dirt roads on the way to school.
He had a rifle and a dog.
He got his first paying job at age 12.
He delivered newspapers, shoveled walks, sold Christmas cards
door-to-door, and washed dishes in a restaurant.
He did well in school and got into Princeton as a scholarship kid.
After college he served as a congressional staffer during the Reagan administration.
He later became a
self-taught software engineer.
Chris is a business owner.
He starts software companies.
He and his teams have written and sold millions
of dollars worth of software, and his software has been used by millions of people in 12 countries.
