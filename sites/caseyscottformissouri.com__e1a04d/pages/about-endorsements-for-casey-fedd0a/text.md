Read Full Endorsement Letter from Missouri American Federation of Labor – Congress of Industrial Organizations (AFL-CIO) September 10, 2026 Dear Casey Scott, On behalf of hundreds of thousands of working Missourians and their families, the Missouri American Federation of Labor and Congress of Industrial Organizations is proud to formally announce that you have received our endorsement for your campaign for HD 2 in the November 3, 2026, General Election.
Protecting the rights of all workers, especially those with collective bargaining agreements, is necessary to provide upward mobility and quality of life for everyone in Missouri.
We believe you share these same values, and we believe you will work tirelessly to protect the rights of working people and stand and fight for their best interests when the time comes.
Whether it be standing up for working families in an affordability crisis, protecting the right to safe jobsites, or ensuring access to public education, we believe that we have a partner in you and can work together on these core values for years to come.
Because you stand with us in these endeavors, the Union workers represented by the Missouri AFL-CIO will stand with you on Election Day as we work to make Missouri work for everyone!
In Solidarity, President Jake Hummel Secretary-Treasurer Ryan McKenna Missouri AFL-CIO Executive Board About LuUNA !
