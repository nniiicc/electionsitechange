Running a campaign takes a lot more than just one person.
There are long hours, constant communication, planning, organizing, and a whole team of people working behind the scenes to make things happen.
It’s easy to only see one face attached to it, but campaigns are built on the effort, support, and dedication of many people working together toward the same goal.
If you feel called to be part of something bigger and my advocacy resonates with you, I encourage you to sign up and volunteer.
Every conversation, every helping hand, and every person willing to show up makes a real difference.
Our Team Audrey Snider Audrey brings a background in marketing and digital advocacy to Casey’s campaign.
A graduate of the University of Missouri with a degree in Marketing, she has experience in website development, social media strategy, public affairs, and advocacy.
She is passionate about using digital communications to connect with voters, increase civic engagement, and support leaders who are committed to fighting for rural communities and doing what’s right.
Digital Director Rose Waldeier A resident of Harrison County for 12 years, and raised in rural Iowa, Rose has a deep love for rural communities.
Skilled with being able to connect with groups both large and small, Rose is excited to energize local voters to participate in local politics.

She's excited to bring this skillset to Casey Scott's campaign for House District 2.
