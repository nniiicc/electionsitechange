Election Security Bills in the State Assembly 2023-24 Wisconsin State Representative Scott Krug April 2024 In 2023-2024 I chaired the Assembly Committee on Campaigns and Elections.
Election security is a huge and hugely-important issue.
It’s worth asking, “What did the State Assembly do this session to increase election security?” Below is a list of the main bills that I feel would have increased election security, that came before the Assembly Committee on Campaigns and Elections this session.
All these bills also appear in the other document I have placed on this site, which lists all the bills that passed out of my committee.
We had over 70 bills introduced and sent to our committee in 2023-2024.
Many of them would not have increased election security in the eyes of everyone.
Some did not really deal with election security at all; one example is 2023 AB 496 which would have required certain elected state officials to serve as election officials.
Would it have increased participation in polling by elected officials?
Maybe / probably.
But would it have increased actual election SECURITY?
Not really, not directly.
We did of course have some bills that would have increased election security.
We had nine such bills that I put in the list below.

I should mention a tenth bill, 2023 AB 567.
This is the “Monday process” bill that I worked hard to pass, and it DID pass in the Assembly but in the end we could not persuade the Senate to take it up.
Basically 2023 AB 567 would have allowed clerks to process (NOT count, just process) absentee votes the day BEFORE an election.
Currently this is not allowable under the law, and so absentee ballots are processed on Election Day, AFTER the in-person ballots.
The result has been that large numbers of ballots added to the counted results at the last moments of the count.
In 2018 and again in 2022 we saw absentee ballots in Dane and Milwaukee Counties swell the vote counts AND impact the statewide totals in the early-morning hours of the day after the election.
(Dane and Milwaukee Counties are our largest counties, so they have large numbers of absentee ballots; and on such things are conspiracy theories made.
On that basis I’d like to start doing Monday processing in Wisconsin and relieve people’s anxiety about the counting process.
I believe Monday processing would enhance election CREDIBILITY when people stop thinking that elections are being stolen at the last moment by fabricated ballots.
It’s worth doing.
2023 AB 567 was passed by the Assembly on a voice vote but the Senate neither voted on AB 567 nor acted on its companion 2023 SB 685.
