My job isn’t to tell you what to think.
My job is to make sure you have the information you need to make informed decisions about your community.
I’m running for State Representative…
because I believe our communities deserve a representative who listens, shows up, and works alongside the people they serve.
Like many families in our district, I’ve worried about rising costs, access to healthcare, education funding, and what the future will look like for our children and grandchildren.
I never planned to run for office.
I became involved because I saw members of my community searching for answers about issues that would impact their homes, their families, and their future.
I started helping people find information, understand the process, and make their voices heard.
Along the way, I realized that many residents felt disconnected from their elected officials and wanted someone who would be accessible, responsive, and willing to do the work.
As your State Representative, I will focus on protecting our communities, supporting working families, advocating for veterans and seniors, strengthening public education, and ensuring that growth happens responsibly and with community input.
This campaign is about people, not politics.
Together, we can build a stronger future for the Quiet Corner.
Affordability and Opportunity
People of all ages are feeling the impact of rising costs, from housing and healthcare to groceries and utilities.
I believe everyone should have the opportunity to build a secure future, whether they are just starting out, raising a family, or enjoying their retirement years.
Protecting Our Communities and Environment
The Quiet Corner’s natural beauty, rural character, and close-knit communities are worth preserving.
I support responsible growth that protects our environment, respects local voices, and ensures development strengthens our towns without sacrificing what makes them special.
A Voice for the Quiet Corner
Our communities deserve to be heard.
I will work to amplify the voices of residents across the district and advocate for investments in education, healthcare, infrastructure, and services that help people of all ages thrive, remain connected to their communities, and age in place with dignity.
