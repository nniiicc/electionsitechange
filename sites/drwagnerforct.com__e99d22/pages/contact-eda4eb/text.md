Why are you running for State Representative?
Because I believe our communities deserve a representative who listens, shows up, and works hard to deliver results for the people of the 51st District.
What communities are in the 51st District?
Killingly, Putnam, and Thompson.
What are your top priorities?
Lowering costs for families, supporting public education, improving access to healthcare, advocating for veterans and seniors, protecting natural resources, and encouraging responsible economic growth.
Do you take corporate PAC money?
No.
This campaign is powered by individuals and community support.
How can I help?
You can volunteer, host a meet-and-greet, display a lawn sign, make a donation, or help spread the word about the campaign.
