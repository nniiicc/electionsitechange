Town Hall; 29 March 2026 Article by Iowa State Daily Marshall County Launch; 26 Oct 2025 Story County Launch; 12 Oct 2025 Story County Fall Fundraiser 5 Oct 2025 Press Release ​26 Sep 2025 RHODES–Teresa Perin, a Democrat, announced plans today to run for the Iowa House in district 51.
Perin is currently a high school English teacher with the Marshalltown Community School District.
She recently retired from farming, but still lives on an acreage in rural Marshall County.
District 51 covers parts of Story and Marshall Counties.
“As a lifelong Iowan, I am running because we need innovative leadership who will make choices that benefit the people.
I’ve watched the policies implemented in the last several years take Iowa from the top of the nation in education to the middle of the pack.
I’ve watched the economy drop into negative growth.
The same policies from the same politicians will not bring us a better future.
I’m running because we need a fresh start for all Iowans.” “I want to be the candidate who will look past party lines and speak to the individuals to serve everyone.
It’s time to end the division and pull together to create the future we want for our state.” Perin is a proud graduate of West Marshall High School, located in district 51, and Iowa State University.
Her master’s degree is from Viterbo University.
She has a son and daughter-in-law who have blessed her with one granddaughter. ​ Perin can be reached at [email protected] .
