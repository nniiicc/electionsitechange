State Assembly campaigns typically rely on: Supporters often contribute to campaigns that reflect their values.

Matching funds amplify local donations:The New York State Matching Funds Program matches contributions of $5–$250 from district residents at a rate of up to 12‑to‑1.
The total amount an individual can have matched throughout the campaign is $1,050.
This program allows campaigns to spend more time engaging with voters rather than focusing solely on fundraising.
