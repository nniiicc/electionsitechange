Protecting Your Permanent Fund Dividend
The Permanent Fund Dividend belongs to the people of Alaska.
It’s not a government program — it’s your share of Alaska’s resource wealth.
I’ve consistently advocated that we follow the law and pay a full statutory PFD.
Just this year, when an amendment was offered to fund the full statutory PFD, I voted for it and spoke on the House floor urging my colleagues to do the same.
I’ll keep standing up for a full, fair, and predictable Permanent Fund Dividend for every eligible Alaskan.
