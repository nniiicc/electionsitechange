Where it goes
The blue-and-gold signs neighbors keep asking for, every single cycle.
Mail that lands in Badger and North Pole boxes before absentee ballots go out.
Radio, print and digital that carries the message district-wide in the final stretch.
Every dollar is local.
This is a grassroots campaign in a district of about 16,500 registered voters.
Small contributions from neighbors are what pay for it.
- Contributions to Mike Prax for State House are not tax deductible.
- Contributions must be made from your own personal funds, not from the funds of another person or entity, and not reimbursed by anyone.
- You must be a United States citizen or a lawfully admitted permanent resident.
- Corporations, labor unions, and other business entities may not contribute to Alaska candidate campaigns.
- Alaska law requires the campaign to report the name, address, principal occupation and employer of contributors.
Reports are filed with the Alaska Public Offices Commission.
