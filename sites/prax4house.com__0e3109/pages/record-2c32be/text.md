Seniority that works
Twelfth of forty
Since his appointment in 2020, Rep.
Prax has earned the trust of his colleagues and become one of the most senior members of the Alaska House — the 12th most senior of the 40.
In a forty-member body, seniority is what decides who sits on the committees where budgets are actually written and where bills either move or die.
That standing is not transferable.
A district that trades it away starts over at the back of the room.
