Step one Rank Mike Prax #1 Alaska uses ranked-choice voting in the general election.
You are not picking one name — you are putting the candidates in the order you like them.
It is simpler than it sounds: Fill in the oval for your first choice.
That is the only mark that has to be there.
You may rank a second and third choice if you want to.
You are never required to.
Ranking someone second cannot hurt your first choice.
Your second choice is only counted if your first choice is eliminated.
Three candidates are on the District 33 ballot this November.
Rank Mike Prax first, and the rest is up to you.
