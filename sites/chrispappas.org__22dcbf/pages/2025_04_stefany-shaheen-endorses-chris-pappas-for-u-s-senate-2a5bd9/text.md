Senate April 14, 2025 MANCHESTER, NH – Today, Stefany Shaheen, a business leader, advocate, and former Portsmouth city councilor and police commissioner, announced her support of Congressman Chris Pappas for the United States Senate.
“Our economy, our way of life, and the strength of our communities are on the line in this election.
That’s why I’m supporting Chris Pappas.
Chris is grounded in the people, places and values of New Hampshire, has a record of taking on the big fights, and he’s proven he can win tough races,” said Stefany Shaheen.
“Granite Staters trust Chris, and they know his record of looking out for them and putting New Hampshire first.
I am thrilled to endorse him in this critical race for the United States Senate and am ready to work together to win this fight for New Hampshire.” “I am deeply honored to receive my friend Stefany’s endorsement and could not be more grateful to have had her support and friendship for so many years,” said Congressman Pappas.
“Stefany is a tireless advocate and leader in New Hampshire, and her work has helped improve the lives and livelihoods of countless Granite Staters.
Whether working to keep communities like Portsmouth safe or advocating for critical or lifesaving health care research, Stefany always puts people first.
I’m grateful to have her on our team and part of the fight to stand up to this administration, self-dealing billionaires, and extreme politicians who are threatening our future and our ability to get things done for New Hampshire.” Following her endorsement, Stefany will join Chris on the campaign trail to continue to organize, grow support, and bring the message of this campaign to every corner of the Granite State.
