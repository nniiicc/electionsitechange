Chris Pappas Launches Campaign for U.S.
Senate
MANCHESTER, NH – Today, Congressman Chris Pappas, a small business owner and lifelong Granite Stater, announced his campaign for the United States Senate.
In a video released this morning, Pappas emphasized the tough fights he has taken on and how he has delivered for New Hampshire.
“I’m running for Senate because our economy, our democracy, and our way of life are on the line, and New Hampshire deserves a Senator who is grounded in the people, places, and values of this state,” said Chris Pappas.
“Granite Staters know my record of taking on the big fights and looking out for them – pushing tax cuts for working families and small businesses, taking on predatory companies and corporate polluters, and standing up to Big Pharma to lower drug costs.
Like Senator Shaheen, I’ll always put New Hampshire first.
You can count on me to lead the charge to confront this administration, self-dealing billionaires, and extreme politicians who threaten our future and our ability to get things done for New Hampshire.”
Pappas will kick off his campaign tonight in Manchester and then hit the road this weekend with events across the state.
Last month, Pappas completed a ten-county tour to listen and learn from Granite Staters as he considered entering the race for U.S.
Senate.
