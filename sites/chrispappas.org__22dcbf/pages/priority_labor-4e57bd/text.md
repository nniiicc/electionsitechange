Standing with Labor
Working people and organized labor built this country and created the greatest middle class the world has ever seen.
But we know that for far too long, political and economic power has been tilted towards billionaires and giant corporations, instead of working families.
Chris proudly stands with labor and working people across New Hampshire.
In Congress Chris is committed to ensuring that working people have a seat at the table and their voices are heard.
He helped introduce and fought to pass the PRO Act in the House, which would protect collective bargaining and hold corporations accountable for violating those rights.
When the Trump Administration stripped union rights from one million federal workers, Chris got to work, introducing bipartisan legislation and forcing a vote to restore those fundamental rights.
Chris is working to create a 21st-century workforce and supports efforts to expand job training and apprenticeships.
He also passed a bipartisan bill through the House to cut red tape and let veterans more easily access training in critical-need industries like trucking.
In the U.S Senate, Chris will continue to lead the fight to protect workers’ rights, strengthen the middle class, and secure safer working conditions, higher wages, and better benefits.
