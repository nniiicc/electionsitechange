Oceanside Councilmembers Endorse Jim Desmond for Congress
Peter Weiss and Rick Robinson Announce Their Support for Jim Desmond
OCEANSIDE, CA – Oceanside Councilmembers Peter Weiss and Rick Robinson have officially endorsed Jim Desmond for Congress, citing his strong leadership, dedication to public safety, and commitment to improving the quality of life for Oceanside residents.
"Jim Desmond has been a tireless advocate for Oceanside," said Councilmember Peter Weiss.
"From securing funding for public safety to standing up for our local businesses and infrastructure, Jim has always fought for our community.
His leadership is exactly what we need in Washington, and I am proud to endorse him for Congress."
Councilmember Rick Robinson echoed this sentiment, emphasizing Desmond's proven track record of delivering results.
"Jim has consistently put Oceanside first—whether it's pushing for solutions to restore our beaches, supporting our first responders, or ensuring our community has the resources it needs to thrive," Robinson said.
"We need a leader who listens, takes action, and gets things done.
Jim Desmond is that leader, and I am honored to support him."
Jim Desmond expressed his gratitude for the endorsements, highlighting his longstanding commitment to public safety, economic growth, and community well-being.
"I'm incredibly honored to have the support of Councilmembers Peter Weiss and Rick Robinson," said Jim Desmond.
"Oceanside is the largest city in the 49th District, and I’ve worked hard to ensure our law enforcement, businesses, and families have the resources they need to thrive.
I look forward to continuing that work in Congress and fighting for a better future for all of North County."
With the endorsement from Oceanside’s leadership, Desmond’s campaign gains even more momentum as he continues to focus on public safety, economic opportunity, and government accountability.
