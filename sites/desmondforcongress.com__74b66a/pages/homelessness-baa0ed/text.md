California alone accounts for 27% of the nation’s homeless population—a crisis that touches every community.
The California dream has become a nightmare, but it doesn’t have to stay that way.
We need real solutions, not endless excuses.
As your representative, I’ll push for common-sense policies to tackle homelessness at its core: Contact Us A Plan That Works for Everyone Get People Off the Streets and Into Treatment Fund treatment-first programs that address the root causes of homelessness: addiction and mental illness.
Stop enabling harmful behaviors and start offering real pathways to recovery through effective mental-health care and addiction treatment.
Federal Support for Local Communities Provide federal funding for cities and counties that move people from the streets into treatment, not just temporary shelters.
Expand resources to build treatment facilities and end the Medicaid rule that blocks funding for larger psychiatric hospitals.
Empower Medical Professionals, Not Bureaucrats Reduce legal barriers to getting people the care they need.
Give doctors and first responders the ability to intervene when lives are at risk, including involuntary care for those in crisis.
Demand transparency for dollars spent.
Tie federal dollars to real results: fewer people on the streets, more lives restored, and better community outcomes.
Donate Homelessness isn’t just a tragedy for those living on the streets—it’s a public safety issue, an economic drain, and a health crisis for our communities.
My plan ensures your tax dollars are spent wisely, helping people rebuild their lives and making our streets safer and cleaner.
It’s time for leadership that delivers solutions, not more empty promises.
Together, we can end this crisis and restore hope for those who need it most.
