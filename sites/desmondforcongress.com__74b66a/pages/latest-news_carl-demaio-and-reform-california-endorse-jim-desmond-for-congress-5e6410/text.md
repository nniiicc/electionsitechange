Backing from DeMaio and Reform California carries a big impact – as Desmond will now benefit from placement on the popular Reform California Voter Guide and receive the backing of the organization’s army of grassroots volunteers.
"Jim Desmond has been a strong voice for common sense in San Diego by standing up against costly and unfair tax hikes that hurt working families and fighting to restore public safety and secure our border,” DeMaio said.
“If we want to reform the broken system in Washington that only serves the special interests at the expense of our families, we need to elect Jim Desmond to be both our voice and our fighter in the 49th Congressional District,” DeMaio added.
Jim Desmond expressed his gratitude for the endorsement, saying: "I am honored to have the endorsement of Carl DeMaio and Reform California.
DeMaio and his army of volunteers and supporters at Reform California have been the ones leading the fights in California against higher taxes, to expose corruption and government waste, and to implement common-sense reforms of government at all levels.
I look forward to working alongside DeMaio and Reform California to protect taxpayers and bring real solutions to Washington." Jim Desmond has long been a champion for fiscal responsibility, public safety, and local control, and this endorsement further solidifies his standing as the candidate who voters can trust to fight for their values and interests in Congress.
DeMaio is a California state legislator representing District 75 (eastern and northern San Diego County) in the State Assembly and hosts a daily podcast on California politics that averages 1 million unique views a week.
DeMaio has built Reform California into one of the largest conservative organizations in the state – with over 600,000 subscribers, volunteers, and donors statewide.
