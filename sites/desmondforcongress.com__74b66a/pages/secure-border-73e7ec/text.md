Securing the Border
Provide Border Patrol agents with the tools and training they need to keep us safe.
Work with border states and local communities to address challenges.
A Legal Immigration System That Works
Launch a user-friendly online application system so immigrants can apply legally and transparently.
Streamline legal immigration for skilled workers, family reunifications, and humanitarian cases.
Prioritize areas of economic need, like agriculture, technology, and healthcare.
No Skipping The Line
Enforce existing immigration laws to stop illegal crossings and overstays.
Require asylum claims to be filed at U.S. embassies or designated centers abroad.
Remove incentives for illegal entry by creating real, legal options
