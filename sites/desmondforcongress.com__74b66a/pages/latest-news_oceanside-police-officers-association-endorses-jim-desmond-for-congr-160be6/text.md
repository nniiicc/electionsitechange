Joe Roy, Vice President of the Oceanside Police Officers’ Association, praised Desmond’s leadership, stating: “Jim Desmond has always stood up for public safety and has been a steadfast supporter of law enforcement.
He understands the challenges our officers face and has fought to ensure we have the resources, training, and support needed to protect our communities.
Jim has our full trust, and we know he will continue to be a champion for law enforcement and public safety in Congress.” Jim Desmond expressed his gratitude for the endorsement, saying: “I am incredibly honored to have the support of the Oceanside Police Officers’ Association.
Our law enforcement officers put their lives on the line every day to keep our families and communities safe, and they deserve leaders who have their backs.
In Congress, I will always fight to ensure our police departments are fully funded, equipped with the best tools and training, and given the respect they deserve.
Public safety will always be a top priority.” Jim Desmond has consistently worked to support law enforcement, oppose reckless policies that undermine public safety, and advocate for stronger measures to keep communities safe.
His commitment to standing with law enforcement makes him the clear choice for voters who prioritize security and safety in their neighborhoods.
