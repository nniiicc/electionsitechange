With nearly two-thirds of the 49th District residing in San Diego County, this endorsement sends a powerful message: Jim Desmond is the common-sense conservative leader who can win and flip this seat.
"Jim Desmond has spent his career delivering results for North County.
His proven record of fighting for businesses, prioritizing public safety, and standing up for working families makes him the right person to represent the 49th District in Washington," said Paula Whitsell , Chairwoman of the Republican Party of San Diego County.
"We’re proud to endorse Jim because he’s not just a candidate—he’s a leader.
He’s the one who can defeat Mike Levin and restore conservative leadership to this district." As a U.S.
Navy veteran, commercial airline captain, and current County Supervisor, Jim Desmond brings unmatched experience and credibility to the race.
His campaign has already earned support from law enforcement organizations, grassroots conservatives, and now the full weight of the Republican Party in San Diego County.
“I’m incredibly honored to receive the endorsement of the Republican Party of San Diego County,” said Jim Desmond .
“This endorsement is more than just symbolic—it’s a rallying point for conservatives across the district.
We need to clear the field, unite our efforts, and focus on the one goal that matters: defeating Mike Levin.
The 49th District is ready for a change—and I’m ready to deliver.” With Desmond’s endorsement, the Republican Party has sent a clear signal: it’s time to unify behind a battle-tested leader who has already represented much of the district and who has what it takes to win in November.
