"Jim Desmond has been a fighter and a leader when it comes to securing our border," said Manny Bayon, President of the NBPC San Diego Chapter.
"He has consistently stood against open-border policies that put our communities at risk and has worked tirelessly to ensure our Border Patrol agents have the resources and support they need.
Jim understands that a secure border is essential for public safety, and we need his leadership in Congress now more than ever." Jim Desmond has been a vocal advocate for strong border security, testifying before Congress on the crisis at the southern border and exposing the consequences of the Biden Administration’s failed immigration policies.
As San Diego County Supervisor, he has worked alongside law enforcement and federal agencies to combat human trafficking, drug smuggling, and illegal crossings.
"I am honored to have the endorsement of the National Border Patrol Council, San Diego Chapter, and our dedicated Border Patrol agents," said Jim Desmond.
"Every day, these men and women put their lives on the line to protect our country.
In Congress, I will continue to fight against reckless open-border policies and work to restore law and order to protect our communities." As a Congressional candidate, Jim Desmond is committed to strengthening border security, enforcing immigration laws, and ensuring law enforcement agencies have the resources they need to keep American families safe.
