Abdur-Rahim Hameed, President of National Black Contractors Endorse Jim Desmond for Congress
San Diego, CA—Abdur-Rahim Hameed, the president of the National Black Contractors Association, has personally endorsed Jim Desmond in his congressional campaign, recognizing his longstanding commitment to fair treatment, economic opportunity, and equal employment in the construction industry.
“Jim Desmond has always been a champion for fairness and opportunity when it comes to construction and economic development,” said Abdur-Rahim Hameed, President of the National Black Contractors Association.
“He understands the importance of ensuring that all workers—regardless of background—have a fair shot at employment and success.
His leadership has helped break down barriers, and we know he will continue that fight in Congress.”
Jim Desmond expressed his gratitude for the endorsement, emphasizing his commitment to fostering a complete and more prosperous workforce.
“I am incredibly honored to have the personal endorsement of Abdur-Rahim Hameed, President of the National Black Contractors Association,” said Jim Desmond.
“Whose dedication to uplifting communities and creating opportunities for all is truly inspiring.
I will continue to fight for policies that promote economic equality, fairness in hiring, and the success of businesses.
Together, we can build a stronger future for everyone.”
With the support of Abdur-Rahim Hameed, President of the National Black Contractors Association, Jim Desmond continues to gain momentum in his congressional campaign, reinforcing his commitment to economic development, equal opportunity, and job creation.
