This endorsement further solidifies Desmond as the clear choice for voters prioritizing public safety.
“I am incredibly honored to have the support of the Deputy Sheriffs’ Association of San Diego County,” said Jim Desmond.
“Our sheriffs put their lives on the line every day to keep our communities safe.
I will always fight to ensure they have the resources, support, and respect they deserve.
Public safety is not just a talking point for me—it is a commitment.” The Deputy Sheriffs’ Association President, Michael O’Deane, and the entire DSA Board of Directors have expressed strong support for Desmond’s candidacy.
“Jim Desmond has consistently been one of our biggest supporters.
He understands the challenges law enforcement faces and has always made public safety a top priority.
We are proud to endorse Jim because we know he will be a strong and effective voice for our deputies in Washington.” This significant endorsement from the DSASD continues the surge of support for Desmond’s campaign.
It highlights his long standing dedication to law enforcement and public safety, critical issues for voters in the 49th District.
Desmond continues to prioritize public safety as the foundation of his campaign, now having the endorsements of the Deputy Sheriffs’ Association, National Border Patrol Council, and Oceanside Police Officers Association, making him Law Enforcement’s Choice for Congress in the 49th.
