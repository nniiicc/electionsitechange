Endorsed by Roger VanVolkinburg
“It is with great pleasure that I am endorsing Rachelle Smit for State Representative.
Her dedication and devotion to faith and family as well as her experience in the family ag business will serve her well in Lansing.
Being an elected official as Martin Township’s Clerk also gives her an upper hand as to how local government needs to best serve their constituents.
I sincerely believe she will be our voice in Lansing for a smaller, more efficient government that actually serves the people rather than dictate them.
Please join me in voting for Rachelle Smit to be our next State Representative.”
