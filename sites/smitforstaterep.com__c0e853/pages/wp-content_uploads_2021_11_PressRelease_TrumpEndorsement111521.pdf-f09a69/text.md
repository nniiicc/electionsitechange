PRESS RELEASE: IMMEDIATE RELEASE RACHELLE SMIT RECEIVES PRESIDENT TRUMP’S ENDORSEMENT Trump Gives “Full Support” to Allegan County Candidate CONTACT: Amy Langeler, Campaign Manager (616) 334-5643 amy@differentisgood.com November 15, 2021-- President Donald Trump today gave his full support to Rachelle Smit, candidate for Michigan State Representative.
Smit is a Republican running for the 80th District, currently covering Allegan County, in the August 2022 Republican Primary and then the November 2022 general election.
Smit is a second-generation dairy farmer, twice-elected township clerk, running to represent the district in Lansing.
Smit is married to her high school sweetheart, and is a mother of three adopted boys.
“I am very honored to receive the endorsement and support of President Trump,” Smit said.
“His leadership worked to make America great, and it is my hope to go to Lansing to help make Michigan great.
I will work to achieve a Trump agenda of economic growth, good jobs, and sensible government.” Smit is a pro-Trump, pro-life, pro-farmer conservative who is committed to election integrity and reducing spending in the nearly $70 billion annual state budget.
Her campaign slogan is “Get Smit Done.” “I was asked to run, and to make sure we have strong leadership in Lansing.
I would be honored to serve my community and my neighbors in this role,” Smit said.
Smit was also a top fundraiser among Republican challengers this past quarter as seen in Secretary of State filings.
