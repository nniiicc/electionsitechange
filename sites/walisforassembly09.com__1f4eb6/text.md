Walis For NY Assembly 09
About Christopher Walis
Christopher Walis is running for NYS Assembly District 09.
A full-time Assistant Store Manager at a local grocery store at just 26 years old, he's learned the hard way that Long Island is no longer affordable.
With this position, he will make his district more affordable, so no Long Islander, young or old, is priced out of their home.
Key Positions
Affordable Housing
Several generations of Long Islanders are seeing no future here on Long Island.
A lack of affordable housing means that not only has the younger population moved away, but older generations are struggling with rising property taxes.
No matter what your background is, Long Island is increasingly becoming unlivable.
Human Rights Without Compromise
Safeguarding groups that have been continuously persecuted in our current times:
Protecting Trans Youth from being targeted by local school boards and elected officials.
Ensuring that Immigrant communities are safe from ICE
Environmental Preservation
Protecting Our Environment:
Ensuring the quality of our Ground Water From Toxic Plumes
Making sure local parks and preserves are kept clean.
Removing the toxic algal blooms found in South Shore waters.
