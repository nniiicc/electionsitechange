What makes Mary Ann the best choice for State Representative?
Mary Ann Perkins believes in government by the people and for the people - just like we were taught in school.
She knows that the people in power have a duty to respect the power of the people, and that's what she will do as your State Representative.
Meanwhile, the incumbent's voting record shows how out of touch he is: He brags about supporting education, but in reality he sends our tax dollars to private schools and he votes against funding our public schools and Dolly Parton's Imagination Library.
He voted to revoke voter-approved earned sick-leave for 700,000 working Missourians, even though voters in District 100 approved this measure by 63% in 2024.
He voted to put Amendments 4 and 5 on the August ballot -- both of which would have taken power from the people and put it into his own hands -- and both soundly defeated by the people all over the State.
He wants to ban abortion and that's how he votes.
Our State Representative ought to vote like we do, and in District 100, 62% of us voted to overturn the abortion ban in our State and protect abortion rights in our State Constitution.
This November, we have the opportunity to send a clear signal to smug incumbents who are only in it for themselves.
Vote for Mary Ann Perkins to defend your vote and ensure your voice is heard in Jefferson City.
