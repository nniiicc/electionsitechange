Meet
Jenny Leilani
Jenny Leilani Callison is an Army veteran, dedicated mother, and experienced public servant with a 14-year tenure at the California State Legislature, most recently as Chief Consultant for the Senate Military and Veterans Affairs Committee.
She is a Winters, CA native and a Dixon, CA resident.
Holding a Master’s Degree in Public Policy and Administration from California State University, Sacramento, she ran as an independent candidate for California State Assembly District 11 in 2022, advocating for education, mental health access, and maternal care improvements.
As a passionate community leader, Jenny coached roller derby and Junior Giants baseball, reflecting her deep commitment to creating opportunities and fostering growth for youth and families.
Why I run for
California State Assembly, District 11
I am running for office to make life in this state better and easier for the people who live here.
I served in the United States Army to help my country and my neighbors.
For 9 seasons, I contributed to my community and coached Junior Giants baseball.
For over 14 years, I worked for the California State Legislature.
My last job was with the California State Senate as Chief Consultant for the Military and Veterans Affairs Committee.
During those 14 years, I watched the state I was born and raised in change drastically.
Cost of living increased, the business environment became hostile, fires ravaged parts of the state, homeless spending led to a larger homeless population, loneliness and isolation created lasting impacts in lives.
Despite all of the technology and innovation, energy, water, and infrastructure are not more accessible or more affordable.
As your Assemblymember, I will work to pass effective legislation to increase the quality of our lives and reduce the cost of living.
I will support accountability and transparency in state funding, especially to programs that failed to produce desired outcomes.
I hope to open the regulatory door to a thriving local small business economy that employs from within the community.
With my experience in the Legislature and heart for service, I will be a positive influence in your life and in the lives of all Californians.
