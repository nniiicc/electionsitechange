The European languages are members of the same family.
Their separate existence is a myth.
For science, music, sport, etc, Europe uses the same vocabulary.
The languages only differ in their grammar, their pronunciation and their most common words.
Everyone realizes why a new common language would be desirable: one could refuse to pay expensive translators.
To achieve this, it would be necessary to have uniform grammar, pronunciation and more common words.
If several languages coalesce, the grammar of the resulting language is more simple and regular than that of the individual languages.
The new common language will be more simple and regular than the existing European languages.

Providing affordable housing helps prevent and reduce homelessness by ensuring that low-income individuals and families have stable places to live.
Affordable housing ensures that everyone, regardless of income, has access to safe and decent living conditions, reducing inequality and promoting fairness in society.
When people spend less on housing, they can invest more in education, healthcare, and local businesses, contributing to a stronger and more stable economy.
Safe, stable housing is linked to better physical and mental health outcomes, lowering healthcare costs and improving quality of life.
Affordable housing encourages diverse, mixed-income neighborhoods, reducing segregation and fostering stronger, more connected communities.
