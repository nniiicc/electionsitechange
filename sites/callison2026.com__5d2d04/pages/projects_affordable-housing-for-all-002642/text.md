Promotes Social Equity Affordable housing ensures that everyone, regardless of income, has access to safe and decent living conditions, reducing inequality and promoting fairness in society.
Boosts Economic Stability When people spend less on housing, they can invest more in education, healthcare, and local businesses, contributing to a stronger and more stable economy.
Improves Public Health Safe, stable housing is linked to better physical and mental health outcomes, lowering healthcare costs and improving quality of life.
Supports Inclusive Communities Affordable housing encourages diverse, mixed-income neighborhoods, reducing segregation and fostering stronger, more connected communities.
