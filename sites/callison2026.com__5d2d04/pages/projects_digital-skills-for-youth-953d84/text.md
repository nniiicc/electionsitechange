Boosts Employability By equipping youth with essential digital skills, the project prepares them for the modern job market, increasing their chances of employment.
Encourages Innovation Digital literacy empowers young people to create, innovate, and contribute to the tech-driven economy of the future.
Strengthens the Economy A digitally skilled workforce supports national economic growth by attracting investment and fostering entrepreneurship.
Promotes Social Inclusion It helps marginalized groups of youth become active participants in the digital world, reducing inequality and promoting civic engagement.
