Protects Vulnerable Road Users It prioritizes the safety of pedestrians, cyclists, and people with disabilities, who are often most at risk on unsafe streets.
Promotes Sustainable Transportation Safer streets encourage more people to walk, bike, or use public transit, reducing pollution and traffic congestion.
Supports Equity in Communities The project focuses on underserved areas where road safety has historically been neglected, helping to ensure fairness and access for all.
Boosts Local Economies Safer and more accessible streets attract more foot traffic and improve the quality of life, which can help local businesses thrive.
