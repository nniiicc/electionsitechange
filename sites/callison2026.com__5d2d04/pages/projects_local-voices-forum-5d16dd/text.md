Promotes Transparency The forum encourages open dialogue between citizens and authorities, fostering greater accountability in local governance.
Strengthens Democracy By involving people at the grassroots level, it deepens democratic practices and strengthens civic engagement.
Identifies Local Issues Early It helps uncover and address problems specific to communities before they escalate, enabling more responsive governance.
Builds Trust in Institutions Consistent and meaningful engagement through the forum can restore and reinforce public trust in political and governmental institutions.
