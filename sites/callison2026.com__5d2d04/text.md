I am not a career politician – I am a public servant and veteran with more than 14 years of experience in the State Legislature, most recently serving as Chief Consultant for the Senate Military and Veterans Affairs Committee.
As your Assemblymember, I will fight to lower the cost of living by reducing the gas tax, cutting tolls, streamlining permitting, and eliminating fees and taxes that burden families and small businesses.
I will protect our water and natural resources, support responsible fire recovery and rebuilding, and ensure we care for and preserve the land for future generations.
Our youth are the future.
As a volunteer Jr.
Giants Baseball coach for nine years, I saw firsthand how opportunity shapes young lives.
California ranks near the bottom in education; I will expand educational opportunities and school choice, defend parental rights, improve access to IEPs, and promote safer communities with stronger mental health services.
With over 33,000 veterans in District 11 and thousands of active-duty and reservists at Travis Air Force Base, I will ensure access to quality healthcare, housing, education, and employment.
Neighborhoods and communities can thrive when we reduce regulatory burdens and restore local control.
I am running as No Party Preference because I have always registered independent.
My allegiance is to the Constitution and the people of Assembly District 11.
I will put your priorities first – not any political party or special interest.
I respectfully ask for your vote.
