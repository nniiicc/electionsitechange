Privacy Policy | Text Message Terms Pledge Your Vote Yard Sign Press Click Here to Submit Meet Annalyse Annalyse Sarvinas is a mom, teacher, and community volunteer, READY to serve Topsham in the Maine House of Representatives.
When my husband and I bought our first home in 2014, we chose Topsham for many reasons.
Being an active, family-friendly town was at the top of the list.
We were raising two young boys on a single income, spending countless afternoons at the Topsham Public Library, weekends at Foreside Park, and evenings with the Scouts.
I earned my bachelor's degree in English and Secondary Education from the University of Southern Maine, graduating Magna Cum Laude in 2021.
Since then, I have worked in both public and private schools, teaching English Language Arts and helping students develop the reading and writing skills they will carry throughout their lives.
I am running for State House because the choices made in Augusta impact our everyday lives -- from the cost of living to the quality of our schools to maintaining safe neighborhoods.
Join the Campaign A Working Families Plan for Augusta The choices made in Augusta impact our everyday lives.
Here is what I will fight for when I go to work for you.
Tax Relief & Lower Costs Maine workers deserve to keep more of their paycheck.
Businesses deserve more liberty, so we have more good paying jobs.
I will fight to keep more money in your pockets and free businesses of our excessive tax and regulatory burden.
This will lower everyday costs.
Educational Reform As a teacher and mom, I know our children need a great education.
I also know first-hand we are lacking courage and willingness to embrace reform.
I will fight for tax credits, literacy-based reform, and earlier access to trades and other careers.
Free Market Healthcare Let's be honest, government healthcare is failing us.
We have to wait half a year to see our doctors and our hospitals are closing.
I will fight to end the Healthcare Certificate of Need (CON) that's allowing corporate health to block competition and innovation.
This will lower costs and attract new healthcare talent.
End Fraud We must end fraud of our hard-earned tax dollars.
Real people are being harmed.
Every tax dollar lost to fraud is not in our retirement community, classrooms, and assisting veterans.
It adds to inflation.
I will fight for transparency, but also demanding prosecution.
