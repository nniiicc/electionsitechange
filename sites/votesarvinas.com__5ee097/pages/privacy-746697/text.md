Annalyse Sarvinas Back to Campaign Site Committee to Elect Annalyse Sarvinas Privacy Policy Last updated September 10, 2026 The Committee to Elect Annalyse Sarvinas respects your privacy.
This policy explains the information collected through this website and how the Committee uses it.
Information We Collect When you sign up to support the campaign, we may collect the information you provide, including your name, email address, phone number, street address, town, campaign interests, and text-message consent choice.
How We Use Information The Committee uses supporter information to share campaign updates, organize volunteer opportunities, respond to requests, coordinate campaign activities, and communicate with supporters about the election.
We only use a phone number for campaign text messages when you have affirmatively opted in to receive them.
You do not need to consent to text messages to receive campaign information, volunteer, or make a donation.
How We Share Information The Committee does not sell your personal information.
We may share information with trusted service providers that help operate the campaign website, maintain campaign records, or deliver campaign communications, as well as when required by law.
Text messaging opt-in data, consent and related collected personal information will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, unless required by law.
Your Choices You may choose not to provide optional information on campaign forms.
To stop campaign text messages, reply STOP to any message.
For help with campaign text messages, reply HELP .
Security The Committee uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect supporter information.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Changes to This Policy The Committee may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
