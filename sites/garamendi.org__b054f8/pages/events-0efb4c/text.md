He received his B.A. in business from UC Berkeley where he was a Wheeler Scholar, second team All America offensive guard, two-time all Pacific Coast Conference team, and the 1964 West Coast heavy weight wrestling champion.
He also holds an MBA from Harvard Business School.
He and his wife Patti served in the Peace Corps in Ethiopia before returning to the U.S and settling down in Walnut Grove, where they raised six children.
They now have 13 grandchildren and continue to raise Angus cattle on their family ranch and grow pears in their Delta orchard in Walnut Grove.
