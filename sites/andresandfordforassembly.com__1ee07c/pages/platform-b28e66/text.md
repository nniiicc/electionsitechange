Human Workforce & Responsible AI Advancement Act Ensuring California leads in AI innovation while protecting the workers who power our economy.
Small Business Support Small Business Revolving Loan Fund (AB Proposal) A privately supported lending pool offering low-interest, fast-approval loans to businesses in neighborhoods impacted by crime and high costs.
Protecting Neighborhood Businesses — Commercial Tenant Protections Targeted, temporary protections for small commercial tenants against predatory rent increases.
Housing Affordability Lowering Housing Construction Costs Act – 98% Permit Fee Reduction (AB Proposal) Reducing single-family home building permit fees by 98% statewide and requiring 60-day permit decisions.
Corporate Housing Acquisition Tax — 30% One-Time Fee (AB Proposal) A 30% one-time tax on corporations purchasing residential properties to discourage speculative buying.
Anti-Trust Expansion for Housing (AB Proposal) Requiring 80% of newly built homes be sold to families, not corporations, and capping building permit fees.
Protecting Affordable Housing Act — Rent Cap (AB Proposal) Capping rent increases to the lower of 5% total or 2.5% plus inflation annually.
SB 423 Revision — Restoring Local Control (AB Proposal) Restoring city authority over affordable housing placement while mandating 30% affordable housing in all major developments.
Freezing Rent Increases & Protecting Families A temporary statewide freeze on residential rent increases to give families stability while California expands housing supply.
California State Land Bank Program A coordinated program to repurpose underutilized public land for affordable housing, community facilities, and small business space.
Vacant Homes to Housing Act Activating thousands of vacant housing units to place families in stable homes quickly and cost-effectively.
Affordable Housing Construction Accelerator Mandating 90-day review windows for infill housing projects and streamlining environmental compliance.
Healthcare Affordability California Universal Health Coverage Act (Billionaire Tax) Universal healthcare for every Californian — no premiums, no deductibles, no copays — funded by targeted taxes on extreme wealth.

Education & Youth Development Investing in our children and preparing the next generation for opportunity.

Universal Preschool Expansion Requiring every elementary school to maintain at least one free preschool classroom.
Small Class Size Act (AB Proposal) Limiting elementary school class sizes to 15–17 students per teacher.
