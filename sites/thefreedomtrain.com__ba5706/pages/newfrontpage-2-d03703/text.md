We Deserve Better We’ve Tolerated Ignorance Long Enough
A hundred years from now, all new people will wonder why we tolerated the ignorance and witlessness of Donald Trump, a man of zero moral or intellectual substance.
We deserve better than this.
We deserve better than the idiocy of the war with Iran, where every death – from American servicemen to Iranian school children- has been a slaughter.
We deserve better than the idiocy of Canada as the 51st state to his first-term blather that the Moon is part of Mars.
We deserve better than the status quo: the Republicans who enable Trump and the Democrats unable to muster substantive opposition.
We deserve common sense in these uncommon times.
It’s time for a second American Revolution.
It’s time to send me to Congress.
Thank you for caring,
