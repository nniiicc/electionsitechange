MEET REPRESENTATIVE MICHELLE McCANE Michelle McCane is a lifelong Tulsan, educator, and community advocate.
As the first woman to represent House District 72, she brings the voices of working families, students, and underserved communities into every room she enters — even if she has to bring the chair herself.

PUBLIC HEALTH & SAFETY HB1948 & HB1949 – Oklahoma Public Health and Safety Reform Act HB1019 – Creates a Rare Disease Advisory Council HB1018 – Bans unauthorized pelvic exams HB2012 – Supports harm-reduction services HB2705 – Requires law enforcement to update sexual assault victims about evidence ​Michelle supports legislation to protect the dignity and health of all Oklahomans, especially vulnerable groups.
