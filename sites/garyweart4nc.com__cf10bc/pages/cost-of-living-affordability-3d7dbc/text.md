Families across North Carolina Senate District 33 are struggling with the rising costs of everyday life.
From higher grocery bills to increasing property taxes, utility costs, and prescription drug prices, too many households are being stretched thin.
Gary Weart believes that government should work to ease these burdens and help working families thrive — not struggle to make ends meet.
North Carolina Priorities
Lower Grocery Costs
- Support policies that increase competition and reduce price pressures at the grocery store.
- Work with state partners to protect local food producers and supply chains so families can access healthy food without paying a premium.
Fair Property Taxes
- Advocate for property tax relief for long-time residents, seniors, and working families.
- Support transparency and accountability in local tax assessments.
- Encourage tools that help homeowners stay in their homes without being priced out by rising taxes.
Affordable Energy & Utilities
- Push for smarter energy policies that stabilize rate increases rather than burden families with unpredictable spikes.
- Back investments in energy efficiency and clean energy that lower long-term costs for households.
Prescription Drug Cost Relief
- Champion efforts to lower prescription drug prices through bulk purchasing and stronger consumer protections.
- Support measures that increase transparency in drug pricing so patients aren’t overcharged.
Every North Carolinian deserves the dignity of an affordable and secure life — where a paycheck can cover groceries, a roof over your head, safe transportation, and essential medicine.
Gary Weart will work in the state Senate to hold costs down, protect working families, and build an economy that lifts everyone up — not just those at the top.
