In North Carolina Senate District 33, too many people are forced to delay care, skip medications, or worry about medical bills because healthcare costs are simply too high.
Gary Weart believes healthcare should be a right—not a privilege.
Affordable Coverage for All Protect and strengthen access to affordable health insurance options.
Support efforts that reduce out-of-pocket costs for families, seniors, and working people.
Lower Prescription Drug Costs Advocate for policies that lower the cost of life-saving medications.
Support transparency in drug pricing so patients know what they are paying and why.
Protect Rural & Community Healthcare Defend local hospitals, clinics, and healthcare providers—especially in rural areas.
Expand access to primary care and preventative services close to home.
No family should have to choose between paying for healthcare and paying for groceries, rent, or utilities.
Affordable healthcare means healthier families, stronger workers, and safer communities.
Gary Weart will work in the state Senate to make sure healthcare works for people—not insurance companies or special interests.
“Healthcare should bring peace of mind, not financial fear.
