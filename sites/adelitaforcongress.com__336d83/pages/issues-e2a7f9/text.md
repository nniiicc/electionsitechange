Immigration & Border Security ​Protect immigrant communities and pursue comprehensive immigration reform. ​ Held the administration accountable through five oversight visits to ICE detention facilities in Arizona and Texas.
Supports protections for DACA recipients , including a pathway to citizenship.
Advocated for the release of people who do not belong in detention.
Sponsored legislation to protect immigrant veterans, end mandatory and family detention, phase out private detention facilities, strengthen due process, and increase accountability in immigration enforcement .
Veterans Visa and Protection Act ,The Dignity for Detained Immigrants Act,The Stop Excessive Force in Immigration Act , The Drain ICE Act, The Melt ICE Act, Protecting Sensitive Locations Act, H.R.
1589 - American Dream and Promise Act of 2025, The Stop Inhumane Conditions in ICE Detention Act Will continue holding officials and ICE officers accountable and push for comprehensive immigration reform with a pathway to legalization.
Economy & Cost of Living ​Lower costs, raise wages, and put more money in the pockets of working Arizona families. ​ Supports policies to lower the cost of gas, groceries, housing, utilities, healthcare, and other basic needs .
Opposes harmful tariffs and wasteful border militarization that can increase costs and hurt small businesses.
Supports expanding utility, internet, and health premium assistance for families.
Supports increasing the Earned Income Tax Credit and Child Tax Credit while asking the wealthy to pay their fair share.
Supports the Raise the Wage Act , including an initial $17 minimum wage tied to cost-of-living increases.
Supports the PRO Act and workers’ right to unionize and bargain collectively.
Supports corporate tax reforms that discourage outsourcing jobs and profits overseas.
Energy, Environment & Mining Put impacted communities first while investing in clean energy, climate resilience, water security, and good-paying jobs. ​ Supports a green jobs revolution and investments in solar, wind, battery projects, green infrastructure, and multimodal transportation.
Supports clean-energy incentives for Arizona households and streamlined development of clean-energy projects.
Will elevate the voices of rural, Latino, and Tribal communities in the transition to a clean-energy economy.
Supports greater accountability for corporations and utilities to protect Arizona ratepayers.
Supports modernizing the 1872 Mining Law , requiring royalties on minerals extracted from public lands.
Supports stronger environmental standards and meaningful consultation with Tribal Nations and impacted communities .
Water Protect Arizona’s water supply, communities, agriculture, and Tribal water rights. ​ Recognizes that climate change and drought continue to jeopardize the Colorado River and Arizona’s drinking water supply .
Opposes proposals that place the vast majority of long-term water cuts on Arizona.
Will continue fighting for CAP users in Arizona’s cities and towns that have reduced water use and made significant investments.
Will advocate for Yuma County agricultural producers and their role in America’s food supply.
Will continue standing with Arizona’s Tribal Nations and protecting their hard-fought water settlements.
Healthcare Protect healthcare coverage, lower costs, and expand access to quality care. ​ Supports protecting and expanding the Affordable Care Act and extending premium assistance.
Supports restoring federal Medicaid funding and repealing cuts that threaten coverage for 400,000 Arizonans.
Supports lowering prescription drug costs and protecting existing provisions that reduce medication expenses.
Supports increased investment in medical research and opposing cuts to the National Institutes of Health.
Supports reproductive freedom and access to medication abortion and contraceptives .
Supports protecting Medicaid, community health centers, hospitals, and essential services including behavioral health, cancer screenings, dental care, and prenatal care.
Education Strengthen public schools, support teachers, and ensure every student has access to a quality education. ​ Supports the Right to Read Act , investing in literacy instruction, school libraries, and librarians while protecting access to diverse reading materials.
Supports competitive teacher pay , mentorship and preparation programs, paid family medical leave, and childcare.
Supports establishing a competitive national minimum salary for first-year teachers .
Opposes dismantling the Department of Education and supports rebuilding and properly funding it.
Supports federal resources and partnerships for states and local schools, particularly chronically underfunded systems like Arizona’s.
Supports accountability for private and charter schools and protecting the rights of students and educators.
Veterans Protect veterans’ benefits, healthcare, and access to the services they earned. ​ Supports protecting immigrant veterans from deportation and creating a pathway for deported veterans to return to the United States.
Supports expanding VA healthcare and benefits for veterans exposed to PFAS and other toxic substances.
Supports expanded VA dental care and benefits for surviving spouses.
Supports fully implementing the PACT Act for toxic-exposed veterans .
Opposes cuts and privatization of the Department of Veterans Affairs .
Will fight for additional VA resources to reduce wait times and address benefit backlogs.
Supports expanding education, workforce training, reintegration programs, and protecting rural hospitals and VA clinics.
Housing & Homelessness Lower housing costs, expand affordable housing, and prevent displacement. ​ Supports expanding housing supply, modernizing federal housing programs, and making homeownership more accessible.
Supports d own payment assistance for first-generation homebuyers .
Supports stronger restrictions on large institutional investors buying neighborhoods and driving up home prices.
Supports guaranteed housing assistance for people experiencing homelessness, including veterans.
Supports fully funding and expanding Section 8 so families are not left on years-long waitlists.
Supports bringing federal housing funds back to Arizona and restoring fair-housing enforcement.
Supports protecting VA Home Loan programs .
Supports community-based approaches to homelessness that emphasize de-escalation, accountability, civil rights, mutual aid, and investment in public spaces and neighborhoods.
