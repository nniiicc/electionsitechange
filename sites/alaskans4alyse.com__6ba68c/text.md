MY STORY
I am a third-generation Alaskan and a proud mom of four.
I grew up in an Alaskan family that struggled with addiction, mental illness, and abuse, but I was determined to break the cycle.
My community and my school teachers lifted me up and gave me the tools and confidence I needed to persevere.
I served Alaska under Republican and Independent governors and led a non-partisan grassroots movement to restore education funding and improve our schools.
By bringing Republicans, Democrats, and Independents together, we were able to deliver real results for Alaskan families.
I am working to do the same for you in the State House representing District 14 in the Spenard, Rogers Park, and, Midtown Anchorage area.
In the 33rd Legislature, I served on the House Finance Committee keeping a keen eye on all bills with a fiscal note.
Ensuring Alaskans’ values are reflected in our budget will create the Alaska that keeps Alaskans here.
I am here to listen to and serve you.
