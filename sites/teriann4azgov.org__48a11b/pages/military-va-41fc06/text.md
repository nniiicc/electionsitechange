Arizona’s Veterans Deserve More Than Silence
Our military members, veterans, and their families have sacrificed so much for our freedom.
As Governor, I will make them a priority by listening to their concerns, advocating for better access to healthcare, mental health resources, employment opportunities, housing, and the respect they have earned through their service.
I also have to ask: Why don’t we hear more from Katie Hobbs and Andy Biggs about the daily challenges facing Arizona’s veterans?
They deserve more than recognition on holidays—they deserve consistent leadership and meaningful action every day.
Arizona, our veterans should never feel forgotten.
As your Governor, I will work to ensure their voices are heard and their sacrifices are honored with real support.
