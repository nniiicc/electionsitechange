Why do you want to be Governor?
Teri’s answer:
I have been witness and victim to the government and their agencies in Arizona and I am tired of the suffering.
I see things wrong in the state and I do not believe those in power today or running for office as Governor are dedicated, like I am, to fixing the problems.
I want to protect families, save Arizona from further harm from these agencies, fix the fraud, waste, and abuse happening, allow investigations into the state, protect the borders, protect our schools, and protect the constitution from further crumbling.
I will install good leaders into the agencies operating under the Governor and ensure operations are honorable and people are not being mistreated.
I will bring transparency between government and the people and not allow private funds set up, back door deals to be made, and investigate prior leaders for their actions.
Short answer: Save Arizona, Protect our Families, Investigate and clean up Fraud!
