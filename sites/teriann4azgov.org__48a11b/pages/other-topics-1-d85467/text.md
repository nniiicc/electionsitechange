Teri’s Commitment to Arizona as Her Authentic Self.
As a candidate for Governor of Arizona, my faith is an important part of who I am.
It guides my values, my character, and my commitment to serving others.
At the same time, I believe in serving all Arizonans fairly, respectfully, and impartially, regardless of their background, beliefs, or political affiliation.
I am not running to be someone I am not.
I am running as my authentic self—someone who understands the challenges facing Arizona because I live them alongside the people I seek to serve.
I believe voters deserve leaders who are genuine, transparent, and grounded in their principles.
I can share who I am openly and still unite people around common goals because this campaign is about Arizona and its future.
I am not a career politician.
I am one of the people, and I believe I have been called to serve at this moment in our state's history.
My commitment is simple: to lead with integrity, remain true to my values, and always put the people of Arizona first.

Teri Fights for Her Community!
As a candidate for Governor of Arizona, I am running because I believe our state needs meaningful change.
Too many Arizonans feel that government is no longer working for them, and I share their frustration.
As an Arizona native and a member of this community, I have seen firsthand the challenges families, workers, and small businesses face while confidence in our institutions continues to decline.
I believe government should serve the people, not special interests or political insiders.
That means greater transparency, accountability, and a renewed focus on the needs of everyday Arizonans.
My campaign is about restoring trust in government, putting citizens first, and ensuring that public officials are accountable to the people they represent.
Arizona deserves leadership that listens, acts with integrity, and fights for positive change.
I am committed to bringing that change and building a government that works for the people of Arizona.
