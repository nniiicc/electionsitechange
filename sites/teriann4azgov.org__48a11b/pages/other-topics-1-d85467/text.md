Big Money = Big Problems
As a candidate for Governor of Arizona, I believe that the influence of big money in politics has weakened public trust in government.
When politicians rely on large donations, endorsements, or support from corporations, special interests, and political action committees (PACs), there is a risk that those interests will be prioritized over the needs of everyday Arizonans.
That is why I do not accept PAC money or corporate contributions and never will.
My commitment is to remain accountable only to the people of Arizona, not to special interests seeking political influence.
As governor, my decisions will be guided by what is best for Arizona families, taxpayers, small businesses, and communities—not by the demands of major donors or political insiders.
Arizona deserves leadership that is independent, transparent, and focused on serving its citizens first.
Teri Ann Hourihan for AZ Governor | Leadership Means Taking Responsibility
As Governor, I will not spend my time blaming the federal government for Arizona’s problems.

Arizonans deserve a Governor who focuses on solving problems, building partnerships, and delivering results—not pointing fingers.
Leadership is about accountability.
Arizona deserves a Governor who is ready to do the work.
