Exposing corruption and fixing it all agencies, systems, and government sections in Arizona.
I will not hide it, I will show the public what is happening and my steps to fix it.
2.
Water and Power Sustainability: Arizona is suffering when it comes to increased cost to our utilities and sustainability of water plus growth in our economy.
I will work with experts and councils to ensure we act quickly to maintain water sources and sustainability forever without fail while still allowing for growth in our communities.
I will stand against increased rate hikes in utility companies when it is unnecessary and takes from tax payer pockets when the utility company is profiting millions.
2.
Healthcare Protection — Fraud, waste, and abuse in our governmental agencies in Arizona externally and investigate if internal fraud is happening within the system and agency and stop it by removing leaders from their positions and establishing a trustworthy leadership that does the job laid out for them — protect people and tax payer dollars.
Further, ensure good agencies and providers are getting paid to sustain business and patients are getting the care they need at all times from their providers of choice.
3.
Education System: Arizona is bottom ranking in the nation for their public schools.
I will work with legislators from around the nation’s top schools to help guide and install Arizona’s public school systems.
I will ensure that public schools are safe for all people and return to what is constitutionally safeguarded and protected.
4.
Rural Area Protection: I will protect rural communities and tribal nations from improper installation of windmills and solar panels across their land and our state that is ruled unnecessary.
I will work with communities around these issues and concerns to discuss ways to improve and create plans.
I will protect land from windfarms, solar farms, and billion dollar corporations coming in and destroying our land.
5.
I will stand against large companies and corporations from taking over our streets with unnecessary railroad systems and structures being built that costs our land, state, and people money, space, and time.
It is not okay to me that large corporate America takes advantage of us.
6.
Protect Families: Family courts across the nation are improperly removing kids’ custody from parent(s) without due process creating an influx in kids entering the foster care system or being outcast by one parent.
Families need to be protected and have rights, including due process and judges held accountable when they are not following laws and the judicial process.
