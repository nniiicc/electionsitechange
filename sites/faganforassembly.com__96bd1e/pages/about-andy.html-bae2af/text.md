Andy for NYS Assembly District 124
Together, We Can Do Better
I am running to represent NYS Assembly District 124.
I believe that the district needs a better advocate in Albany.
I will be a strong voice for the people of the district, especially the most vulnerable, local farms, and small businesses.
I want to improve communication and access to information and resources, as well as support affordable healthcare, childcare, and housing, foster community, the arts, and economic stability.
I will defend the dignity and human rights of all.
