The New York State Assembly is part of New York State Legislature, along with the New York State Senate.
The NYS Assembly is made up of 150 districts across the state, each represented by an Assemblyman or Assemblywoman.
The 124th NYS Assembly District
The 124th NYS Assembly includes all of Tioga County, much of Chemung County, and four towns in Broome County.
