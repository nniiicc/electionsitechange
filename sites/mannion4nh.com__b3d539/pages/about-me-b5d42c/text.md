In November of 2020, I was happy to proclaim myself a resident of Pelham, New Hampshire.
I escaped lockdowns of Massachusetts and vowed to ensure something like that could never happen again.
I hit the ground running, training to knock doors and spread the message of liberty.
I won the first political race of my life, coming in 2nd out of 6 candidates for 4 seats in the Republican primary of 2022 for State Rep.
Then had similar success in a race of 8 in the General.
I’ve been serving on the State-Federal Relations and Veterans Affairs committee in Concord, championing gun rights, veteran’s rights, and pushing back against the Federal government.
Work History Software Engineer (2012 – Today) Developing automation software for automotive camera assembly.
UMass Lowell (2008-2012) Graduated with Bachelors in Computer Engineering.
United States Marine Corps (2004 – 2008) Served as an 0311 Infantry Rifleman in 3/6.
Deployed twice in support of Operation Iraqi Freedom.
Honorably discharged in 2008.
