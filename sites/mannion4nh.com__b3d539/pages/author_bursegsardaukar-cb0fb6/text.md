Report Cards – 2026 HRA and RINO Report
I am pleased to report a House Republican Alliance (HRA) score of 98.3%!
The HRA scores votes based on constitutionality and alignment with the NH Republican Party platform.
And the annual Granite Grok’s “RINO Report” where the higher percentage equates to “more RINO” (Republican In Name Only).
Those that vote against the caucus more frequently, get a higher score.
My score for this last year was 1.9%!
