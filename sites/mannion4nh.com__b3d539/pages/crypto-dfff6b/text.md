I am a cryptocurrency enthusiast and support a free market for currencies.
With the US Federal Reserve printing away our purchasing power by inflating the dollar, it’s long past time to seek alternatives.
I would love to see New Hampshire become a crypto based economy, where Bitcoin, Etherium, and numerous others are accepted as easily as credit cards at all businesses in the state.
I will investigate incentives to drive this innovation from the state level, such as reducing business taxes on companies that do a significant portion of their transactions in crypto, or toll discounts when paying the same way.
