Read the Full Constitution (PDF) Preamble "We the people of Montana grateful to God for the quiet beauty of our state, the grandeur of our mountains, the vastness of our rolling plains, and desiring to improve the quality of life, equality of opportunity and to secure the blessings of liberty for this and future generations do ordain and establish this constitution." Ratified by the people of Montana, June 6, 1972 Articles of the Constitution I Article I: Compact with the United States Establishes the terms under which Montana was admitted to the Union under the Enabling Act of Congress approved February 22, 1889.
II Article II: Declaration of Rights Montana's bill of rights — 35 sections guaranteeing individual liberties, including popular sovereignty, inalienable rights, freedom of religion, speech, privacy, the right to bear arms, due process, and more.
III Article III: General Government Defines the three branches of government — legislative, executive, and judicial — and establishes the separation of powers.
Consists of 9 sections.
IV Article IV: Suffrage and Elections Establishes the right to vote for all citizens 18 and older, defines voter qualifications, election procedures, and ballot secrecy.
Consists of 8 sections.
V Article V: The Legislature Creates a bicameral legislature with a 50-member Senate and 100-member House of Representatives.
Defines legislative powers, procedures, and the initiative and referendum process.
Consists of 14 sections.
VI Article VI: The Executive Establishes the offices of Governor, Lieutenant Governor, Secretary of State, Attorney General, Superintendent of Public Instruction, and Auditor.
Defines executive powers and duties.
Consists of 15 sections.
VII Article VII: The Judiciary Creates the Supreme Court, district courts, justice courts, and other courts as provided by law.
Defines the judicial power and administration of courts.
Consists of 11 sections.
VIII Article VIII: Revenue and Finance Governs taxation, public debt, and the state budget process.
Requires taxes to be levied by general law for public purposes.
Limits state debt.
Consists of 17 sections.
IX Article IX: Environment and Natural Resources Declares that the state and each person shall maintain and improve a clean and healthful environment.
Governs water rights, natural resource management, and cultural preservation.
Consists of 7 sections.
X Article X: Education and Public Lands Establishes the public school system, the Board of Regents, and governance of public education.
Directs management of school trust lands.
Consists of 11 sections.
XI Article XI: Local Government Empowers counties, cities, and towns to govern themselves through self-government charters or general law.
Consists of 9 sections.
XII Article XII: Departments and Institutions Organizes state government into no more than 20 departments and governs the administration of state institutions.
Consists of 4 sections.
XIII Article XIII: General Provisions Covers miscellaneous provisions including the state seal, official oath, election of public officers, and provisions against public aid to sectarian schools.
Consists of 7 sections.
XIV Article XIV: Constitutional Revision Provides three methods for amending the constitution — citizen initiative, legislative referral, or constitutional convention.
A convention question must appear on the ballot every 20 years.
Consists of 11 sections.
Source: The Constitution of the State of Montana — Montana Judicial Branch.
