Barrington, Rhode Island —
BARRINGTON, RI — In response to today's report in GoLocalProv that Helena Foulkes' campaign has committed multiple campaign finance violations, Independent candidate for Governor Ken Block issued the following statement:
“This report should cause real concern for Rhode Island voters.
It shows that the Foulkes campaign has repeatedly violated our state’s campaign finance laws.
Even though she enjoys the advantages of being a retired corporate CEO with enormous wealth and a network of deep-pocketed out-of-state donors, her campaign has acted as though it's above the law when it comes to raising money.
“Any candidate can make a one-time mistake, but this number of violations over multiple years is more than just negligence – it is a troubling pattern of illegal behavior.
“The $2,000 maximum per contributor is a bright line.
Exceeding the maximum amount is illegal.
The Rhode Island Board of Elections should demand the immediate forfeiture of all illegal funds by the Foulkes campaign and, if necessary, levy penalties commensurate with the seriousness of the crime.”
