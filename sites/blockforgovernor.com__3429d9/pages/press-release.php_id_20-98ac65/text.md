★ November 3, 2026 Countdown to Election Day # Days # Hours # Minutes # Seconds Home About Issues Appearances Media & Press Ken I Be Honest Polls Donate Donate ← Media & Press Official Press Release ﻿Ken Block Calls Out "Helena's Hypocrisy" Following Attack Mailer and Fundraiser Ken Block, Independent candidate for Governor of Rhode Island, responded today to a news report that his Democratic opponent Helena Foulkes held a fundraiser hosted by an honorary Rhode Island chair of President Trump's 2020 re-election campaign just days after the state Democratic Party sent a mailer attacking Block for his previous run in the 2014 Republican gubernatorial primary.
Release Date October 13, 2026 Location Barrington, Rhode Island Media Contact michelle@blockforgovernor.com Barrington, Rhode Island — "Helena's hypocrisy is disappointing even by the standards of a partisan politician," said Block.
"Last week, her allies at the state party sent a mailer to Rhode Island voters criticizing my political affiliation from twelve years ago.
Then, just a couple of days later, she held a fundraiser hosted by a 2020 Trump for President state chair." As reported by GoLocalProv , Gerald Zarrella hosted a fundraiser this past weekend in support of the Foulkes for Governor campaign.
Alongside former State Representative Doreen Costa, Zarrella was named an honorary Rhode Island state chair of the 2020 Trump for President campaign.
Foulkes has made donations to Republican politicians in the past, including a $# personal donation to former Republican Senate Majority Leader Mitch McConnell in 2014.
In 2017, while Foulkes was President of CVS Pharmacy, the CVS Health PAC donated $35,000 to a joint fundraising committee of the Trump campaign and the RNC (Republican National Committee).
"Helena and her allies continue to engage in partisan attacks, even when they have no grounds to do so, in order to deflect attention away from the fact that Helena is closely tied to the political establishment and represents a continuation of the status quo," said Block.
