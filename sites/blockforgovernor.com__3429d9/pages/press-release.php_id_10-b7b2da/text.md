Release Date September 17, 2026 Location Barrington, Rhode Island Media Contact michelle@blockforgovernor.com Barrington, Rhode Island — BARRINGTON, RI — Independent candidate for Governor Ken Block today proposed new legislation, the Transparency in State Taxation Act, that would require identifiable taxes, fees, surcharges, and assessments that are passed on to consumers to be clearly disclosed on their bills.
“Rhode Islanders are being hammered by hidden taxes and fees imposed by our government,” said Block.
“This approach to raising revenue is wrong.
If people can see how many ways our government is taxing them, then maybe our government will change its ways.” Rhode Island’s own revenue records contain more than 200 state taxes, fees, licenses, permits, assessments, surcharges, and similar charges.
Some of these government charges are clearly disclosed while others are embedded in prices, folded into regulated rates, or buried within bills.
“This isn’t about whether every tax or fee is necessary,” Block said.
“It’s about honesty.
If the government makes you pay it, then the government should have to tell you about it.” The General Assembly provided an example this year.
Companion bills S2020 and H7465 proposed a new 0.25% assessment on property and casualty insurance premiums, including homeowners insurance, to fund fire-service training.
The legislation said the assessment would be “in addition to all other fees or taxes.” Then the bills specifically provided that the new assessment “shall not be passed onto policyholders as a specific line-item surcharge.” Both bills ultimately failed to pass.
“Think about that,” said Block.
“The legislation proposed a new government assessment on insurance premiums and then specifically required that this assessment not appear as a line-item surcharge on the policyholder’s bill.
