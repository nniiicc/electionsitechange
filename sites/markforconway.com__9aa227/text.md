Meet Mark Brannan Mark Brannan is a conservative you can trust , a neighbor you know , and a leader who will always put Arkansas first .
Mark Brannan’s life is a testament to the values that make Arkansas strong: faith in God Almighty , personal responsibility , limited government , and freedom secured by the rule of law .
Adopted by Esther and Frank Brannan Jr. in 1969, Mark’s story began in Conway, Arkansas, where his family’s roots stretch back to the late 1800s.
Raised in the heart of Conway at his family’s iconic small business, Frank Brannan’s Drive-In , Mark learned early what mattered to his community—lower taxes, the cost of living, fair wages, and personal liberty.
These conversations weren’t held in political boardrooms but across the front window of a bustling diner serving generations of Conway families.
After graduating from Conway High School in 1987, Mark attended the University of Central Arkansas, where he pledged Sigma Tau Gamma fraternity .
Answering the call to serve his country, Mark enlisted in the United States Navy .
In 2000, Mark returned home to manage the family business alongside his father and uncle.
It was during this time that Mark’s passion for defending small businesses caught the attention of Republican leadership.
In 2003, U.S.
House Speaker John Boehner asked Mark to conduct a survey of small business owners across Faulkner County.
The results informed key aspects of the Bush Tax Cuts , earning Mark the Ronald Reagan Gold Medal in 2004 from the National Republican Congressional Committee.
A lifelong Eagle Scout (1987), Mark has continually invested in the next generation, serving as a Scout leader and recruited as a District Executive with the Quapaw Area Council , and a mentor to families across ten Arkansas counties—helping parents and sons alike develop the leadership skills needed to guide our future.
Mark’s commitment to conservative principles is matched by his dedication to the Republican Party.
He serves as Faulkner County’s Representative to the State Republican Committee , sits on the Second Congressional District Executive Committee , and was named the 2024 John Nabholz Republican of the Year .
Mark and his wife Dana , college sweethearts from UCA, have been married since 1990.
Together, they raised two children— Morgan Wilkerson and Mitchell Brannan —and are the proud grandparents of three granddaughters.
Whether cheering from the bleachers at cheer competitions or visiting their son and daughter-in-law in San Diego, family remains the center of Mark’s life.
They are faithful members of Antioch Baptist Church in Conway.
