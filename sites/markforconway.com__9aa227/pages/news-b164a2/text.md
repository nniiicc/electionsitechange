Republican Mark Brannan Announces Bid for House District 56
“Conway’s concerns have always been my concerns.
That’s why I’m running.”
CONWAY, AR – Today, lifelong Republican, veteran, and small business advocate Mark Brannan announced his bid for the Arkansas State House of Representatives, District 56, which includes portions of Faulkner County.
Brannan, a proud native son of Conway with family roots dating back to the 1800s, has dedicated his life to service—through the U.S.
Navy, the Boy Scouts of America, and decades of involvement in local small business.
“I grew up working the front window of my family’s restaurant, Frank Brannan’s Drive-In, where my father and grandfather taught me the importance of listening to people, said Brannan.
“From gas prices to taxes, minimum wage to inflation—every issue came across that counter.
Conway’s concerns have always been my concerns.
That’s why I’m running.”
A graduate of Conway High School and Eagle Scout, Brannan served as a Hospital Corpsman in the United States Navy from February 1992 until October 1995.
He returned home to run Frank Brannan’s Drive-In, a longtime Conway staple.
In 2004, Brannan earned the Ronald Reagan Gold Medal from the National Republican Committee for his role in helping shape the Bush Tax Cuts through small business advocacy.
Brannan has also served in leadership roles within the Boy Scouts of America and is a current member of the Faulkner County Republican Committee, where he represents Faulkner County on the State Republican Committee and the Second Congressional District Executive Committee.
“As a Christian, husband, father, and grandfather, I want to protect the values that made Conway such a great place to grow up—faith, family, freedom, and—as someone adopted at birth—the sanctity of life,” Brannan added.
“Our future depends on principled leaders who will fight for those values in Little Rock.”
Brannan and his wife Dana, whom he met at the University of Central Arkansas, have been married since 1990.
They have two children and three granddaughters.
Mark and Dana are active members of Antioch Baptist Church in Conway.
