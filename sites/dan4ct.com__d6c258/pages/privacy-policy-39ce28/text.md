Privacy Policy
Dan for Groton New London collects only data provided by users to its form: name, and email, to stay in touch with voters.
Voters may opt out by replying to a text with the word "Stop" or by emailing danforgrotonnewlondon@gmail.com.
Data is shared only with third parties who need to use this information to carry out work directly on our behalf, and is protected by industry standard security measures.
Data is retained through the next November election, and conforms with applicable privacy legislation in Connecticut and the United States.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
