I am in full support of Hannah Pingree for governor of Maine.
I’ve known and admired Hannah for many years; I believe that she has the right combination of principled leadership skills, compassion, creativity and energy to enable our great state to realize its full potential over the next four and, hopefully, eight years.
As someone whose career included managing two Maine cities over a span of 30 years (first Calais and then 23 years in Augusta), I very much appreciate Hannah’s contributions as a legislator (including her time as speaker of the House) and more recently her leadership role in the Mills administration.
Her work championing, establishing and then leading the Office of Policy Innovation and the Future coordinated state agency efforts and important resources for Maine’s nearly 500 municipalities and 16 counties — something the prior administration had shortsightedly done away with when it abolished the State Planning Office.
Hannah is a native Mainer who’s done an admirable job of balancing the roles of spouse, mother, community leader and prominent public official.
She’s warm, genuine and wicked smart.
I hope folks will join me in voting for her on Nov.
3.
Rep.
Bill Bridgeo Augusta Spread The Word Let's Go!
