While negative ads crowd Maine’s airwaves, the spot gives voters a straightforward look at Hannah’s plans and priorities, and keeps her the only gubernatorial candidate to go on air.
The release comes as Hannah scored rave reviews for her first debate performance and maintains a double-digit lead in every public poll released.
WATCH THE AD The 30-second spot features Hannah’s two teenage children who ask viewers if they want to “take a timeout from all the attack ads” and introduce “our mom.” The ad highlights Hannah’s work to build housing Maine people can afford and her plan to build tens of thousands of new homes.
It also previews her priorities as governor: a new public health insurance option to reduce costs, and protecting Maine hospitals from Trump’s cuts.
“Mainers are tired of the negativity, and so are my kids,” said Hannah.
“I’m running to build more housing, lower health care costs, and protect our hospitals.
That’s the conversation Maine people want to have.” “Time Out” is part of Hannah’s media plan to reach Maine voters through the November 3 election.
Her campaign has reserved more than $1.8 million for eight weeks of broadcast television and cable advertising statewide, making her the only candidate in the race to place major broadcast airtime.
The reservation guarantees her message will be on Maine’s airwaves through Election Day, while her opponents have yet to go on air.
This third ad comes as Hannah has secured her place as the front-runner in the general election, fueled by strong grassroots support, a strong bipartisan coalition, record-breaking fundraising, high volunteer engagement, and a growing list of endorsements from elected officials, health care experts, fisherman, housing leaders and community advocates across Maine.
Hannah also earned strong reviews for her first debate performance.
Eight public polls have shown her with a commanding lead as early voting begins and heading into November.
