The visit comes as Hannah sent her own two kids back to Maine public schools on Tuesday and follows her recent endorsement from the Maine Education Association.
“Every year, I get to watch my own kids head back to Maine public schools, and every year I’m reminded how much we owe the teachers and staff who show up for them every single day,” said Hannah.
“Visiting South Portland High School today, I heard firsthand how much teachers and staff care about our kids.
My job as governor will be to make sure every student in this state has the same access to a great education.” As a graduate of Maine public schools, a former Speaker of the House, and former chair of her local school board, Hannah has made education a cornerstone of her campaign.
Her education plan , Building Maine’s Future Through Great Public Schools , focuses on six core priorities: supporting educators, expanding early childhood education and child care, accelerating literacy and math outcomes, strengthening career and technical education, modernizing school infrastructure, and developing a responsible approach to technology and AI in the classroom.
Among the plan’s signature proposals is universal public Pre-K, making free, full-day Pre-K available to every 4-year-old in Maine whose family wants it.
The plan also calls for expediting the increase in teacher starting pay to a minimum of $50,000, and a $100 million revolving loan fund to address school construction and repair needs, with a focus on rural and smaller districts.
