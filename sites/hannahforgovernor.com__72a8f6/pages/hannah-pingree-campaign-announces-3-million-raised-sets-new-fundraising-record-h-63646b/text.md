The campaign again has set a new record for the most funds any Democrat has raised in a Maine gubernatorial race during this reporting period and has $921,000 cash on hand as of today.
The announcement comes as Hannah just returned from a 3-day swing in Aroostook County, connecting with voters at grassroots events and touring small businesses and farms across the region in Presque Isle, Fort Fairfield and Mars Hill.
“Mainers deserve a governor who actually shows up, in every county, including the most rural,” said Hannah Pingree.
“I’ve been traveling this state since day one, and what I hear over and over is that people can’t afford housing, can’t afford health care, and they’re tired of waiting on Washington to fix it.
That’s what I’m focused on.
This kind of support, from every corner of Maine, tells me people are ready for a governor who will actually deliver, and I’m going to keep earning it all the way to November.” Since launching her campaign, Hannah has received support from all 16 counties and nearly 300 Maine towns, thousands of contributions reflecting the campaign’s statewide reach and growing support as Hannah continues to meet with workers, families, small business owners, and community leaders across Maine.
“Hannah’s momentum is clear,” said Emily Cain, Campaign Treasurer.
“Our campaign has more money, more donors, and more support in every county than Bobby Charles and Rick Bennett combined.
Hannah’s strength shows up in the polling, too.
Our opponents have no real statewide operation or base of support.
Voters from all sides are backing Hannah because they know she can govern and they believe she can win.
Right now the Republicans can’t say either.” Hannah Pingree’s campaign for governor has had record breaking fundraising, innovative policy announcements, packed events and Town Halls across Maine, countless endorsements and 400 event campaing stops and counting— alongside two public polls showing her with a commanding lead heading into November.
These milestones highlighted the growing excitement behind her candidacy and her vision to make Maine more affordable, expand opportunities for families, and deliver results that strengthen communities across the state.
A full campaign finance report will be filed with the Maine Ethics Commission by today, July 21st.
For more information on Hannah Pingree’s campaign, visit www.hannahforgovernor.com .
