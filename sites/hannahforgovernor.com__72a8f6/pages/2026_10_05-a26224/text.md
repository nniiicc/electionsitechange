Hannah Pingree is running for Governor because she believes in the Maine she grew up in—a place where everyone steps up and looks out for each other.
Today, Maine faces urgent challenges: a housing crisis, climate change, rising costs, and attacks on our rights and democracy.
Hannah is stepping up to fight back and build a stronger, fairer future for all Mainers.
