Two new public polls secure her overwhelming lead in the Maine gubernatorial race
MAINE – Hannah Pingree, former Speaker of the Maine House and Democratic nominee for governor, released her second television advertisement of the general election, titled “We Bought It” — remaining the only candidate in the gubernatorial race to go on air.
The release comes as Hannah wrapped up a weekend swing through Oxford, Waldo, and Androscoggin counties and maintains a double-digit lead in two new public polls.
The 30-second spot highlights Hannah’s work as Director of the Office of Policy Innovation and the Future on state policy that allowed residents of Blueberry Fields, a Brunswick manufactured home park, buy their park and turn it into a resident-owned cooperative.
The Cooperative Development Institute called the purchase the first deal of its kind in Maine.
“I moved here after my husband passed away.
When I found out that our park was for sale, I was afraid that some Wall Street people would come in, buy the park, and jack up our rates,” Blueberry Fields resident Celeste Y. says in the ad.
“We were able to purchase this park, to turn it into a cooperative, and be in control of our own destiny.”
“I really believe that Hannah Pingree is going to keep fighting for affordable housing for Maine,” Celeste says.
“We Bought It” is part of Hannah’s media plan to reach Maine voters through the November 3 election.
Her campaign has reserved more than $1.8 million for eight weeks of broadcast television and cable advertising statewide, making her the only candidate in the race to place major broadcast airtime.
The reservation guarantees her message will be on Maine’s airwaves through Election Day, while her opponents have yet to go on air.
This second ad comes as Hannah has secured her place as the front-runner in the general election, fueled by strong grassroots support, record-breaking fundraising, high volunteer engagement, and a growing list of endorsements from elected leaders, health care experts, and community advocates across Maine.
Six public polls have shown Hannah with a commanding lead heading into November.
