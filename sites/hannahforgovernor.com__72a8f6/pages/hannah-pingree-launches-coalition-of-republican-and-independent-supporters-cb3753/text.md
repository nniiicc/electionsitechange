GOP officials and legislators, Independent candidate for governor, and former Deputy Secretary of State are among the founding members of a new crossover coalition backing Hannah
PORTLAND — Hannah Pingree, the Democratic nominee for Governor of Maine, stood with a coalition of Republican and Independent Mainers backing her campaign at a press conference in Portland Wednesday, launching what organizers say will be an ongoing effort to organize crossover support for Hannah through November.
“This is the founding group of something we intend to build all the way to November.
Welcoming Republicans and Independents who are choosing to put Maine first and who are willing to say publicly that this election is bigger than the usual lines.
The problems facing Maine families land on all of us, which means solving them has to include all of us,” said Hannah.
“This campaign has always been about bringing people together to get things done, caring about your neighbors more than party – and that’s exactly what this coalition represents.”
Former candidate for Governor and Independent State Legislator Rep.
Ed Crockett was the first speaker and set the tone for the press conference, emphasizing that Maine’s challenges, from housing to healthcare, don’t stop at party lines.
He emphasized that the next governor needs to work with the Legislature to pass their agenda.
Hannah has built a reputation for working across the aisle to deliver results for Maine families and was elected unanimously as Speaker of the Maine House by Republicans, Democrats and Independents.
Among her key bipartisan achievements was championing the Kid-Safe Products Act to protect children from toxic chemicals in everyday products, a fight she carried to Washington to help secure national protections.
“I didn’t have to be here today.
I’m a Republican, and Hannah’s a Democrat.
But leadership matters more than party labels.
I served alongside Hannah about 20 years ago, and I watched her work, up close, for years.
She listens.
She works across the aisle.
That matters to me,” said former state legislator, Hon.
Don Marean of Hollis.
“I’m asking my fellow Independents to join me this November in supporting Hannah.
This is what it looks like when people put politics aside and come together for something bigger,” said former Deputy Secretary of State and Independent voter, Joann Bautista.

The event comes as Hannah continues to build momentum as the front-runner in the general election, fueled by strong grassroots support, record-breaking fundraising, high volunteer engagement, and a growing list of endorsements from elected leaders, health care experts, and community advocates across Maine.
Four public polls have shown Hannah with a commanding lead heading into November.
Hannah has also launched her first television ad of the general election, “Tough,” and is currently the only candidate for Governor on the air.
