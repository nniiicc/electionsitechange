The momentum reflects growing confidence among Maine’s most influential organizations and leaders that Hannah is best positioned to win in November and deliver for Maine families on day one.
Since winning the nomination as the Democratic candidate for governor, Hannah has continued to build a broad and growing coalition, earning new support from labor, health care advocates, and educators: EMILYs List Maine AFL-CIO Planned Parenthood of Northern New England Action Fund Maine Education Association National Women’s Political Caucus MSEA/SEIU Local 1989 GIFFORDS PAC Feminist Majority Human Rights Campaign Maine Gun Safety Coalition National Resources Defense Council Action Fund Maine People’s Alliance During the primary, Hannah built unmatched momentum with endorsements from state and local leaders, mayors, more than 120 current and former state legislators, Maine High School Democrats, Rachel’s Action Network, the Sierra Club, Vote Mama, Maine Youth Power and Maine Conservation Voters Action Fund.
She had also been named a “Gun Sense Candidate” by Mom’s Demand Action and a “Champion” by the Maine Gun Safety Coalition.
In addition to endorsements, the campaign has had record breaking fundraising, innovative policy announcements, packed events and Town Halls across Maine — alongside three public polls showing her with a commanding lead heading into November.
These milestones highlighted the growing excitement behind her candidacy and her vision to make Maine more affordable, expand opportunities for families, and deliver results that strengthen communities across the state.
