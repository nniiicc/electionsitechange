“Mainers deserve to hear directly from all of us about how we’d govern.
I know I’ll be focusing on how my Administration will bring down housing and health care costs and make life more affordable,” said Hannah.
“I’ve been traveling to every corner of this state listening to people and laying out real solutions, and I’ll keep doing that right up through Election Day — on the debate stage and everywhere else.” The three debates are: Newscenter Maine / Maine State Chamber of Commerce — Thursday, October 1 WGME CBS 13 / FOX 23 — Tuesday, October 13 WMTW Gubernatorial Debate — Wednesday, October 14 Maine Public / Maine Trust for Local News — Date to be determined The debates will come alongside an aggressive public and grassroots schedule for the summer and fall, with Hannah marching in parades and festivals, holding town halls, meeting with community groups, visiting communities and small businesses, and knocking doors with volunteers across Maine through Election Day, November 3.
