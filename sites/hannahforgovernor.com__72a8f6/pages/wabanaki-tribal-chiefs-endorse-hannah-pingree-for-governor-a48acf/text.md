During her years in public service, Hannah has partnered with Tribal leaders on economic opportunity, infrastructure, climate resilience, and public health.
As governor, she will build a strong government-to-government relationship to continue taking action on these issues.
More pictures and video available upon request Hannah supports full tribal sovereignty and will work with the Tribal Nations and the Legislature to pass legislation in her first 100 days in office.
She believes the Wabanaki Nations deserve the same rights and opportunities as every other federally recognized tribe.
“Sovereignty is not a favor from the state.
It is overdue.
Restoring it means Tribal Nations will have the same rights and autonomy as every other federally recognized Tribe.
It is an issue of fairness.
Getting this done will be a top priority for me,” said Hannah.
Statements of Support from Wabanaki Chiefs Chief Sheila McCormack, Mi’kmaq Nation: “Hannah Pingree has my full support to be the next Governor of Maine.
She has demonstrated a consistent commitment to working together with Tribal governments and has the experience and temperament to deliver on her campaign promise of Tribal Sovereignty within her first 100 days in office.” Chief William Nicholas, Passamaquoddy Tribe at Indian Township: “I’m proud to endorse Hannah Pingree for Governor of Maine.
She is a leader who listens first and works alongside people to get things done.
She has shown that she values real partnership, and I believe she will bring that same collaborative spirit to the governor’s office.” Chief Kirk Francis, Penobscot Nation: “Hannah is a proven leader with deep roots in Maine.
As Speaker of the House and as a community leader, she has a reputation for listening, bringing people together, and honoring her word.
I’m so pleased to support her for Governor.” Chief Clarissa Sabattis, Houlton Band of Maliseets: “Hannah Pingree does not back down when it matters for Maine families.
She is tough, she is fair, and she sees a future where tribal nations and the State of Maine thrive side by side.” Hannah released her third ad this week, “ Time Out ”, and has secured her place as the front-runner in the general election, fueled by strong grassroots support, a strong bipartisan coalition, record-breaking fundraising, high volunteer engagement, and a growing list of endorsements from elected officials, health care experts, fisherman, housing leaders and community advocates across Maine.
Hannah also earned strong reviews for her debate performances.
Eight public polls have shown her with a commanding lead as early voting begins and heading into November.
