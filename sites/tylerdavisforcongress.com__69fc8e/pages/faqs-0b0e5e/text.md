Davis on the ballot?
A: You can help by completing a candidate petition form and spreading the word!
This allows Tyler to qualify for the ballot without relying on large filing fees and ensures the campaign is powered by the community.
Q: How do I submit a completed petition?
A: You have two options to submit before May 11, 2026: Mail or deliver it directly to your county Supervisor of Elections (SOE) for verification (recommended) Send it to our campaign address (PO Box 6058, Pensacola, FL 32503), and we will submit it on your behalf with a confirmation of accepted/denied from the SOE Submitting directly to your county SOE is the fastest way to have your petition verified.
Q: What is my county’s Supervisor of Elections (SOE) information?
A: It depends based on your county.
If you can’t figure out which SOE to send to, please contact us and we’ll help direct you based on your county.
For Escambia county, please visit https://dos.elections.myflorida.com/supervisors/countyInfo.asp?county=ESC For Santa Rosa county, please visit https://dos.elections.myflorida.com/supervisors/countyInfo.asp?county=SAN For Okaloosa county, please visit https://dos.elections.myflorida.com/supervisors/countyInfo.asp?county=OKA For Walton county, please visit https://dos.elections.myflorida.com/supervisors/countyInfo.asp?county=WAL Q: Can I submit a petition if I don’t live in District 1?
A: No.
Only registered voters who live in Florida’s Congressional District 1 can submit valid petitions for this race.
This includes Escambia, Santa Rosa, Okaloosa, and parts of Walton county.
Q: Is there a deadline to submit petitions?
A: Yes.
All petitions must be received and verified by the Supervisor of Elections before the official deadline of 12:00PM EST (Noon), May 11, 2026.
We encourage you to submit as early as possible and no later than May 1, 2026.
Q: Can I print and mail my own petition?
A: Yes.
You can download, print, sign, and mail your petition directly to your county’s Supervisor of Elections, or use the pre-filled petition available below.
Please ensure that your signature matches the one used on your voter registration; otherwise, your petition may not be counted.
Blank petition: https://files.floridados.gov/media/693291/dsde104.pdf Please ensure that your filled petition includes your name as it appears on your voter registration, Tyler L.
Davis as the name to appear on the ballot, No party affiliation for the party demographic, and complete and sign with your appropriate information that matches your voter registration card.
NO ELECTRONIC SIGNATURES ARE ACCEPTED BY THE SOE IN FLORIDA.
Please print the physical copy to fill and sign your name and information on this form.
Q: How can I get involved with the campaign?
A: You can volunteer by helping with community events, petition collection, outreach, and more.
Visit our Contact us section to fill out the interest form to get started.
Q: Can I help collect petitions from others?
A: Yes.
We welcome volunteers to help gather petitions at events or within their communities.
Reach out and we’ll provide everything you need.
Q: How can I donate to the campaign?
A: Donations can be made securely through our website.
Every contribution helps support outreach, events, and campaign operations.
Q: Are there limits on donations?
A: Yes.
Campaign contributions are subject to federal election laws.
Details will be provided on our donation page.
Q: Where does my donation go?
A: Donations support campaign operations including community engagement, materials, outreach, and events across the Panhandle.
Q: How can I contact the campaign?
A: You can reach us through our Contact page or by emailing us directly.
We do our best to respond as quickly as possible.
Q: Can I request Tyler to attend an event?
A: Yes.
Please submit a request through our Contact page with event details, and our team will follow up.
