On September 15, 2026 at 5:30 PM, the community will gather at Gateway Church of Christ, located at 445 Creighton Rd, Pensacola, FL 32507, for a powerful and compassionate event focused on understanding the far‑reaching impact of addiction on families.
ASK – Awareness, Support, Knowledge – will feature lived‑experience speakers who have been personally affected by addiction.
Their stories will shed light on the emotional, relational, and practical challenges families face, while also offering hope, resilience, and pathways toward healing.
In addition to the speaker presentations, local community partners will be on-site to provide valuable resources, support services, and educational materials for individuals and families seeking guidance.
This event aims to strengthen awareness, foster connection, and empower the community with knowledge and support as we work together to address addiction and its effects on loved ones.
