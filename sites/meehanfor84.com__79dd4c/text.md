A lifelong Hoosier, honorably discharged from the Army National Guard, and working on elections for over a decade, I enjoy serving my community.
The cost of living is rising faster than wages, and families are feeling the strain.
I’m running for State Representative to lower everyday costs, support working families, and bring practical, bipartisan leadership back to the Statehouse.
I understand businesses need to succeed—but not on the backs of Hoosier families.
Right now, too many working people are struggling while large corporations receive tax breaks and special treatment.
Families are being priced out of their own communities, and childcare has become unaffordable or unavailable for many.
I’m running to put our community first—to focus on practical solutions that lower costs, strengthen our local economy, and ensure government works for the people it serves.
