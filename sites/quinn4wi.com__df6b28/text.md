23rd SENATE DISTRICT MEET ROMAINE A LIFELONG RESIDENT OF NORTHERN WISCONSIN Born and raised in northern rural Wisconsin, I attended St.
Joseph Catholic School in Rice Lake and graduated from Rice Lake High School.
During my senior year, I ran and was elected to the Rice Lake City Council.
After serving for a year on the council, I became the Mayor of Rice Lake at just 19 years old. ​ I’m proud to be an alum of UW - Eau Claire - Barron County, where I took classes while fulfilling my duties as Mayor.
After two years, I attended UW-Eau Claire and ultimately earned my bachelor’s degree in Political Science and Public Leadership from UW-Green Bay.
