MEET ROMAINE
A LIFELONG RESIDENT OF NORTHERN WISCONSIN
Born and raised in northern rural Wisconsin, I attended St.
Joseph Catholic School in Rice Lake and graduated from Rice Lake High School.
During my senior year, I ran and was elected to the Rice Lake City Council.
After serving for a year on the council, I became the Mayor of Rice Lake at just 19 years old.
I’m proud to be an alum of UW - Eau Claire - Barron County, where I took classes while fulfilling my duties as Mayor.
After two years, I attended UW-Eau Claire and ultimately earned my bachelor’s degree in Political Science and Public Leadership from UW-Green Bay.
Upon graduating in May of 2014, I returned to public service and launched my bid for the 75th Assembly District, which I held for 6 years.
In 2022 I was elected to the state senate, taking back a seat held by Democrats for 36 years.
My wife and I raise our 3 boys in the now 23rd state senate district.
I look forward to meeting you on the campaign trail and once again protecting our shared values in Madison!
