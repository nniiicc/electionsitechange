My Platform
The gridlock built into our representative government is not a flaw — it is a safeguard.
Separations of
power, checks and balances, and a legislature willing to stand against executive overreach are what keep
government answerable to the people.
I will fight to restore the legislature's rightful authority,
including proper oversight of emergency powers that governors have too often abused.
- Restore legislative oversight and approval of emergency powers
- Return to a part-time citizen legislature
- Protect states' rights and oppose federal overreach
Free and fair elections are the bedrock of our Constitutional Republic.
Michigan has 8.3 million
registered voters but only 7.9 million people of voting age — we must address this.
Our voter
roles are bloated!
- Voter ID requirements
- Regular audits of voter rolls
- Paper ballots and hand-counting
- Single-day voting with Election Day as a holiday
- Remove Michigan from ERIC membership
Strong families are the foundation of a thriving society.
I will promote policies that strengthen the
family and support parents.
- Promote policies that encourage traditional families
- Support parental rights in education and healthcare
- Advocate for faith-based community initiatives
More government means less you.
The freedoms guaranteed by the United States Constitution
are the foundation of our free society.
I will vigorously defend these rights against any
attempt to restrict individual liberties through legislation or executive overreach.
- Defend First Amendment rights in all public spaces
- Defend Second Amendment rights, and fight for constitutional carry in Michigan
- Support legislation that restores the balance between the three branches of government
Every dollar the government takes is a dollar out of your family's pocket.
Property
taxes in Michigan mean you are effectively renting your home from the government
— even after it is paid off.
That is not true ownership.
I will fight to eliminate property taxes
and reduce the income tax burden so that Michigan families and businesses can keep more of what
they earn.
- Eliminate personal property taxes — end renting your home from the government
- Reduce Michigan's income tax burden on families and small businesses
- Reduce burdensome regulations to encourage growth and innovation
- Promote American energy independence
Education should prepare students for success and instill civic responsibility.
Parents
— not government — should direct their children's education.
- Expand school choice options
- Focus on core academic skills and civic education
- Oppose critical race theory in schools
- Return to a curriculum that promotes unity and equal opportunity
About Jason
I am a Software Engineer by trade and have been actively involved in local and state politics for over five years.
My candidacy is built on a deep commitment to the principles that made America the greatest nation in history.
I have served as a Precinct Delegate for four years and have consistently advocated for transparency and accountability in our party and our politics.
I was an alternate delegate to the 2024 Republican National Convention.
I am also the Chairman of the Washtenaw County's GOP.
In all of these positions, I have been a vocal advocate for election integrity and upholding our Constitutional Republic.
That won't change when I get to Lansing — I will be a tireless advocate for the citizens of District 15.
I believe that professional politicians were never the aim of our founding fathers — it is time to return to a part-time citizen legislature.
Above all else, I am a man of faith.
Everything I do flows from my devotion to Jesus Christ, and from that devotion I derive the honesty, integrity, and grace I bring to public life.
