My Platform Preserving our Constitutional Republic ▼ The gridlock built into our representative government is not a flaw — it is a safeguard.
Separations of power, checks and balances, and a legislature willing to stand against executive overreach are what keep government answerable to the people.
I will fight to restore the legislature's rightful authority, including proper oversight of emergency powers that governors have too often abused.
Restore legislative oversight and approval of emergency powers Return to a part-time citizen legislature Protect states' rights and oppose federal overreach Ensuring Election Integrity ▼ Free and fair elections are the bedrock of our Constitutional Republic.
Michigan has 8.3 million registered voters but only 7.9 million people of voting age — we must address this.
Our voter roles are bloated!
Voter ID requirements Regular audits of voter rolls Paper ballots and hand-counting Single-day voting with Election Day as a holiday Remove Michigan from ERIC membership Upholding Traditional Family Values ▼ Strong families are the foundation of a thriving society.
I will promote policies that strengthen the family and support parents.
Promote policies that encourage traditional families Support parental rights in education and healthcare Advocate for faith-based community initiatives Protecting our Freedoms ▼ More government means less you.
The freedoms guaranteed by the United States Constitution are the foundation of our free society.
I will vigorously defend these rights against any attempt to restrict individual liberties through legislation or executive overreach.
Defend First Amendment rights in all public spaces Defend Second Amendment rights, and fight for constitutional carry in Michigan Support legislation that restores the balance between the three branches of government Economic Policy ▼ Every dollar the government takes is a dollar out of your family's pocket.
Property taxes in Michigan mean you are effectively renting your home from the government — even after it is paid off.
That is not true ownership.
I will fight to eliminate property taxes and reduce the income tax burden so that Michigan families and businesses can keep more of what they earn.
Eliminate personal property taxes — end renting your home from the government Reduce Michigan's income tax burden on families and small businesses Reduce burdensome regulations to encourage growth and innovation Promote American energy independence Education ▼ Education should prepare students for success and instill civic responsibility.
