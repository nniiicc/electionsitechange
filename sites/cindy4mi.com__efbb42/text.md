Cindy Ramirez is a proud West Michigan native, GRCC alumni, and the daughter of a migrant worker.
She knows what it means to work hard and fight for your community.
At Cindy 4 MI, we are dedicated to enhancing the growth of the community.
Cindy is running for State Representative in the 83rd District because she believes in empowering local communities and giving a voice to working families.
LEARN MORE ABOUT CINDY → Cindy's Priorities Fighting for the 83rd District Cindy is committed to the issues that matter most to the families of West Michigan.
Economic Growth Supporting local businesses and creating good-paying jobs for working families across the 83rd District.
Quality Education Investing in our schools so every child has access to quality education and opportunities to succeed.
Healthcare Access Fighting for affordable healthcare so no family has to choose between medical care and paying bills.
Public Safety Keeping our communities safe while building trust between law enforcement and the people they serve.
Community Empowerment Giving a voice to every resident and ensuring local communities have the resources they need to thrive.
Infrastructure Investing in roads, broadband, and clean water infrastructure to build a stronger West Michigan for the future.
