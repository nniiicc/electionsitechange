Meet Lisa Maria
A healthcare professional, emergency response leader, and proud Democrat committed to serving every family in District 112.
For years, Lisa has worked on the front lines of healthcare and emergency management, serving families in the moments that matter most.
Her experience has shaped her view of leadership: make thoughtful decisions under pressure, serve without ego, and be accountable to the people who depend on you.
Lisa stepped forward because every community deserves a real choice and steady representation.
She believes democracy works best when voters have options, accountability is real, and leaders show up for the people they serve.
As a Black woman running in a deeply conservative district, Lisa leads with strength, dignity, and respect for every voter.
Her campaign is rooted in service—not division—and in listening first, working hard, and putting people before politics.
