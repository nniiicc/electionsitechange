Here is the election timeline: PRECINCT CAUCUSES - February 3, 2026 Attendees heard from candidates, voted on potential party platforms, and selected delegates to attend the Senate District Convention.
SENATE DISTRICT 65 CONVENTION - March 29, 2026 at Humboldt High School Delegates and alternates selected through the precinct caucuses came together for the Senate District Convention, a full day of organizing, voting, and building momentum.
Throughout the day, delegates determined endorsements for State Senate (65) and State House (65A and 65B) races.
With strong support from delegates, Sebastian secured the DFL endorsement at this convention , a major milestone that positions the campaign with momentum heading into both the primary and general election.
PRIMARY ELECTION - August 11, 2026 The primary election determines which candidate will be on the ballot for the November general election for a given political party.
GENERAL ELECTION - November 3, 2026 The winner of the general election will become the next representative for Minnesota House seat 65B.
