What Sebastian will fight to do:
Provide Safe and Stable Housing
We all thrive when families have safe and stable homes.
As an attorney, Sebastian has fought for homeowners’ and renters’ rights for over a decade, advising people in mortgage foreclosures, defending people facing evictions, and suing landlords who refuse to make necessary repairs.
His experience in housing court has shown him that problem landlords are rarely local “mom and pop” owners, but instead out-of-state corporations with little concern for the communities where their properties exist.
Sebastian will lead efforts to:
- Pass a “right to repair” bill, allowing renters to hire licensed professionals for repairs and deduct the cost from their rent if landlords do not response to repair request within 14 days
- Enact standards on the responsiveness of management companies, and fine property owners when these standards are not met
- Increase the construction of affordable housing in Downtown, West Side, and in West St.
Paul
- Assist all renters, landlord, and homeowners to know their rights, responsibilities, and resources through in-person events, with special emphasis on immigrant communities
Strengthen Workers’ Rights
Minnesota’s economy runs on the people who show up everyday to teach our kids, care for our elders, build our houses, supply our stores, and run our businesses.
Sebastian will stand up for workers’ rights by:
- Ensuring that the minimum wage is a living wage
- Defending and strengthening Minnesota’s paid leave program
- Increasing protections for labor unions and workers’ right to collectively bargain
Reduce Gun Violence
The specter of gun violence causes stress, fear and division among us.
Minnesota has seen unprecedented attacks on public safety this past year, from the shooting at Annunciation Catholic School in Minneapolis, to the attacks on Minnesota House Speaker Melissa Hortman and others.
While respecting responsible gun owners, we can do much to save lives.
Sebastian will advocate for policies that:
- Ban assault weapons and high capacity magazines
- Require background checks on all gun sales
- Integrate mental-health crisis response teams into the 9-1-1 system
Defend Our Immigrant Neighbors
ICE’s ‘Operation Metro Surge’ has put our neighborhoods in fear.
Arresting and deporting law-abiding, tax-paying Minnesotans does not promote public safety -- it denigrates it.
Neighbors are not going to work, and their children are not going to school.
Sebastian has seen this firsthand, both as a trained constitutional observer for ICE raids and through mutual aid work supporting families at his children’s school and the school where his wife, Sarah, is an ESL teacher.
Sebastian will fight to:
- Protect the Constitutional Rights of all Minnesotans, including against unreasonable searches and seizures
- Bolster existing laws which limit cooperation with federal immigration officials
- Amend school regulations so that absences due to ‘fear of deportation’ do not count towards truancy totals
Protect Our Environment
Climate change is an issue that affects the entire planet.
The impacts of climate change are disproportionately felt by the working class and BIPOC communities.
We must work together to usher in the changes necessary to sustain and improve our environment.
Sebastian will advance policies that:
- Commit to 100% renewable energy by 2040
- Assist farmers in transitioning to more sustainable and diverse crops, and encourage urban farming and micro farming
- Increase funding to support public transportation, bicycles, and pedestrians
- Impose a moritorium on the consturction of data centers and study their impact.
These centers create jobs in the short term, but can have large negative environmental impacts with energy and water usage.
Promote Small Business
Small businesses are the lifeblood of their community- when they succeed, we all benefit.
Having started his own small businesses, Sebastian is aware of the bureaucratic and economic hurdles that small business owners face.
Sebastian is committed to:
- Increasing funding for programs that support small businesses
- Enacting renter protections for businesses who rent their space from commercial landlords
- Fighting to reduce the costs of childcare, which will help business owners and employees alike
LGBTQ+
Queer rights are human rights.
Period.
Minnesota has a proud status as a refuge for LGBTQ+ individuals facing discrimination and persecution in other states.
Sebastian is not afraid to speak his mind and call out any bigotry in the public discourse.
Sebastian will lead efforts to:
- Fight any effort to rollback LGBTQ+ protections
- Fight for the physical safety and dignity of trans people in schools, work, and out in public
- Support gender affirming care for trans individuals of all ages
