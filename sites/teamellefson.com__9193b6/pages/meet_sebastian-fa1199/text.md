Meet Sebastian Priorities Delegate Info Get Involved Events Endorsements Donate Meet Sebastian Sebastian Ellefson is a legal aid attorney working for the Volunteer Lawyers Network .
He fights for working class people everyday, representing and advising on wage theft, evictions, habitability issues, and more.
Sebastian was born and raised in the Twin Cities.
He earned a bachelor's degree from the University of Chicago, a master's degree in public policy from Loyola University Chicago, and a law degree from the University of Minnesota.
Sebastian is a co-founder of BallotReady , a civic engagement organization that creates free nonpartisan online voter guides.
Living on St.
Paul's West Side, Sebastian has been a volunteer and board member of the West Side Community Organization (WSCO) .
His wife, Sarah, works as an ESL teacher for St.
Paul Public Schools, and his two children attend St.
Paul Public Schools.
Sebastian is also a board member at Cherokee Park United Church, and is the proud troop leader to Girl Scout Troop 58484.
Sebastian is a progressive Democrat with roots in his community.
He has worked his entire career helping people- working for government, nonprofits, and cofounding his own public-interest company.
When he found out Maria Isa Pérez-Vega was not running for reelection in House seat 65B, he jumped at the chance to serve people in a larger way.
He will be ready on day one to pass legislation that help out all Minnesotans!
