Learn More Get Involved Chris Tipton's Public Service Chris has spent his entire adult life serving the people of southern West Virginia.
From emergency medicine to community service, coaching young athletes and caring for patients, Chris has always believed that leadership begins with service.
For over 30 years Chris has been associated with the Logan Emergency Ambulance Service Authority (LEASA) and the Buffalo Creek Volunteer Fire Department.
With LEASA, he first served as an EMT, then paramedic, and is now as a member of their board of directors.
With the Buffalo Creek Fire Department, he served as a volunteer firefighter and is now a member of their board of directors as well.
Chris is currently a physician assistant providing family medicine services at Southern West Virginia Health System’s Man Clinic.
