Learn More Get Involved Meet Chris Tipton Southern West Virginia is home.
For more than three decades, I have worked as a paramedic, Physician Assistant, volunteer firefighter, coach, and community leader.
I have had the privilege of helping people in their most difficult moments and caring for families throughout our region.
I come from a long line of coal miners, teachers, nurses, and veterans who believed in hard work, faith, and serving their neighbors.
Those values have shaped my life and continue to guide me today.
Southern West Virginia deserves leaders who understand our communities and who will stand up for the people who live and work here.
We must protect our coal and energy jobs, strengthen rural healthcare, support our first responders, and ensure our children have opportunities to build their futures right here at home.
I am not a career politician.
I am a husband, father, grandfather, paramedic, and healthcare provider who believes in serving others.
Now I am asking for the opportunity to continue that service in the West Virginia House of Delegates – District 33.
Together, we can protect the values that make southern West Virginia strong and build a brighter future for the next generation.
