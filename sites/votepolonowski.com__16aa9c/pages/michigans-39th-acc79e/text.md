The 39th House district in Michigan encompasses most of VanBuren County, 3 1/2 townships in southern Allegan County and the northeast corner of Berrien County.
It is home to about 94,000 residents and consists mostly of rural towns, villages and townships with the largest cities being Paw Paw and Hartford.
