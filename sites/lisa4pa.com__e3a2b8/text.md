RB-01 · Sunday Hunting SUBJECT: Rep.
Brian Smith (R) · PA House District 66 The Record On HB 1431, the Sunday hunting bill, Smith filed two amendments, A00932 and A00958 , that would have narrowed the bill.
Both amendments failed on the floor, 101 to 102.
The bill itself then passed the House 142 to 61 , and Governor Shapiro signed it as Act 36.
Suggested Message Sunday hunting passed 142 to 61.
Brian Smith spent his energy trying to water it down.
In a district full of hunters, our representative worked against the bill his own neighbors wanted.
