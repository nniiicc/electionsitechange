Kevin's Priorities
Protecting Reproductive Rights
Kevin is proud to have consistently received a 100% voting record from Planned Parenthood Federation of America and has long been a trusted ally for protecting reproductive freedoms.
The Supreme Court’s overturning of Roe V.
Wade was a disastrous reversal of the hard fought progress that reproductive advocates have accomplished.
In Congress, Kevin remains committed to fighting against any policy that further restricts access to reproductive health care and endangers women’s health.
Kevin is a proud cosponsor of the Women’s Health Protection Act, which would restore access to abortion throughout the United States.
He is also proud to cosponsor the EACH Act, which aims to repeal the Hyde Amendment, and ensure those enrolled in federal health care coverage like Medicaid, and federal employee health insurance plans, have access to abortion care.
Kevin was also an original cosponsor of the Right to Contraception Act, which aims to enshrine the right to access contraception and protect healthcare providers legal right to provide contraception.
