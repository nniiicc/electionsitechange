Kevin's Priorities
Fiscal Responsibility & Fair Tax Policy
We have a responsibility to govern with an eye toward the future, not just the next election cycle.
Our national debt continues to grow, driven in large part by irresponsible, unpaid-for tax cuts that disproportionately benefit the wealthiest Americans while leaving working families—and future generations—with the bill.
Fiscal responsibility means making tough, honest choices.
It means reforming the tax code so billionaires and large corporations pay their fair share, ending giveaways that add to the deficit, and prioritizing smart investments that strengthen the economy over the long term.
By restoring fairness to our tax system and committing to deficit reduction, we can move toward balanced budgets and a more stable fiscal future—an achievement last realized under Democratic leadership.
Sound budgeting isn’t partisan; it’s a generational obligation.
Responsible fiscal policy today is essential to protecting economic opportunity tomorrow.
