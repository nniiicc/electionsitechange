Its success - or failure - is dependent on each and every one of us.
America’s founders built the framework for a free and fair democratic government as a counter balance to corrupt autocratic kings.
This nation has faced monumental challenges since its inception.
But for the last 250 years, American democracy has been kept stable.
Until now.
Donald Trump’s political project is about division.
His goal is to divide our communities, divide our party, and divide our values, all so that he and his billionaire friends can get richer.
But this division is a distraction from the bonds that unite us.
The bonds that make our country so great.
While neighbors and friends may have differences in opinion, we all share a common unity in being Americans.
The values that we share and the respect we have for one another are far stronger than the differences in ideas we may have.
Authoritarianism can only win if we are divided.
In this moment, we must put our differences aside and band together for the fight that comes ahead.
We must do all that we can to strengthen our democracy in our local communities, and in our national politics.
When he first ran for Congress, Kevin promised that he would fight for our future.
We are in the fight of our life to protect the future of American democracy.
But with your support, Kevin will continue to fight back against authoritarianism in the courts, in our communities, and at the ballot box.
