AfforDable Housing
I support common-sense housing reform that expands affordable housing opportunities through smart zoning, responsible development, and housing assistance programs.
Healthcare Reform
I plan to suppport healthcare policy ensuring that all Arizonians have healthcare no matter your income, and that prescription drugs are affordable.
This is especially important for Arizona's children.
FOCUS on EDucation
We must commit to supporting public schools in Arizona.
We need to fully and sustainably fund the schools that are the choice for 90% of Arizona families.
Let's stop the teacher shortage by paying teachers what they deserve and providing classrooms the resources they need.
Water Management
We need to protect Arizona's limited water resources through smart growth, efficient use, and long term planning for the future.
Protecting Voter Rights
Mail in voting allows working families, the elderly and disabled to vote.
I will fight against voter suppression and support our constitutional right to vote.
Preserving Natural Resources
Preserving Arizona's natural resources and making sustainable decisions helps ensure that future generations will have they need to thrive.
