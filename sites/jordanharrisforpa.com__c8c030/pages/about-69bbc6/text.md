Harris stands as a pillar of transformative change, deeply committed to justice, education, and community empowerment.
Serving as the House Majority Appropriations Chairman, he represents the 186th Legislative District, embodying a vibrant mix of south and southwest Philadelphia communities.
Since his election in 2012, Jordan's journey has been marked by passion, leadership, and an unwavering vision for progress.
Jordan's ascent to prominence is filled with historic achievements.
Elected Whip for the Democratic Caucus in 2018, he later became the Democratic Appropriations Chair for the 2023-24 session, making history as the first African American in both roles.
As Chair of the Pennsylvania Legislative Black Caucus, he's dedicated to amplifying African American voices and concerns.
Leadership Journey Educational Background A lifelong south Philadelphian, Jordan's educational path—from John Bartram Motivation High School to Millersville University, where he obtained a bachelor’s degree in Government and Political Affairs—highlights his dedication to learning.
His master's in Education from Cabrini College and doctoral candidacy in Educational Leadership at Neumann University underscore his belief in education's transformative power.
Early Career and Advocacy Prior to politics, Jordan was deeply involved in youth development, serving as an educator, a Philadelphia Public School System employee, and Executive Director of Philadelphia's Youth Commission.
His commitment to nurturing young potential is evident in his early career choices Legislative Achievements A national figure in criminal justice reform, Jordan co-authored the landmark Clean Slate Law with Rep.
Sheryl Delozier, which has sealed over 40 million criminal records in Pennsylvania, inspiring similar legislation nationwide.
He also champions probation system reform, advocating for changes to reduce technical violation incidents and promote rehabilitation.
Recognition and Community Involvement Jordan's contributions have earned him widespread recognition, including being named one of “Philadelphia’s 150 Most Influential Philadelphians” by Philadelphia Magazine.
His leadership extends to community service, including his role as President of the Board at Lincoln Day Educational Center and memberships on several boards, showcasing his dedication to civic engagement.

Harris is more than a political figure; he is a visionary leader and advocate for justice and community betterment.
His path from local educator to a notable legislator embodies the impact of dedication, service, and leadership.
