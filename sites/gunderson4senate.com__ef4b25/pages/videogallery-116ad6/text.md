In this in-depth interview, we speak with Steve Gunderson, who is running for Montana Senate District 1 in 2026.
A long Time time Libby Resident, Army National Guard veteran, business owner for 35+ years, and a past Montana legislator, Steve discusses his background in natural resource advocacy, his legislative experience, and his priorities for the district.
WHAT YOU'LL LEARN: ✓ How Steve got started in the legislature and his journey from natural resource advocacy ✓ His 35+ years of business ownership and leadership experience ✓ Why he believes he's the best candidate for this seat ✓ His top three priorities: Jobs, Jobs, and Jobs ✓ His vision for natural resource extraction (mining, timber, logging) ✓ The Libby Exploration Project and permitting challenges ✓ How natural resource jobs create cascading economic benefits ✓ His experience as Chairman of Natural Resources Committee ✓ Challenges with forest management and wildfire prevention ✓ His perspective on housing, development, and zoning in Libby ✓ How he works across the aisle to pass bipartisan legislation ✓ His expertise in bail bonds and legislative expertise ✓ His closing thoughts on the future of Montana Senate District 1 KEY MESSAGES FROM STEVE: "Jobs, jobs, and jobs—if you don't have any jobs, nothing else really matters." "Lincoln County was the most prosperous county in Montana for a long time, and we can get back there." "We need to get back to our historic natural resource extraction economy." "It's not an I, it's not a me—it's 149 people you've got to bring into the fold." ABOUT MONTANA SENATE DISTRICT 1: Montana Senate District 1 represents Lincoln County and surrounding areas.
State senators work on legislation affecting the entire state, from natural resources and budget to property taxes and economic development.
