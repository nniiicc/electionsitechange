District 8 Voter Info
Are you wondering if you live in the Connecticut House of Representative’s 8th District?
- If you live in the towns of Coventry and Columbia you live in the 8th District.
- If you live in Bolton, Lebanon and Tolland, parts of your town are in the 8th District.
Use the Connecticut General Assembly Find Your Legislator Tool below to determine if you live in the 8th District.
- Use the button to the right to confirm your House of Representative District.
- You’ll be asked to enter your town, your street name, and house number.
- Select “Find” and you will be presented with a “Find Your Legislator” screen.
- If you live in the 8th District, you will see incumbent Tim Ackert, who is not running for re-election, listed as your “State Representative, District 008.”
