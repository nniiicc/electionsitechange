Meet Mike Issues News Volunteer Yard Signs Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Workers’ Rights & Strong Unions Workers’ Rights & Strong Unions Mike stands with union workers and believes that strong unions create a strong middle class .
He will fight against anti-worker policies like ‘Right to Work’ laws , which weaken workers' rights and lower wages.
Mike will protect the right to collective bargaining , ensure fair wages and safe working conditions , and support union apprenticeship programs to create pathways to stable careers.
He believes Ohio should reward companies that invest in their workforce rather than those that cut corners at the expense of workers.
Key Points: Oppose ‘Right to Work’ laws that weaken unions and lower wages.
Protect collective bargaining rights for fair pay and safe working conditions.
Support union apprenticeship programs to build a skilled workforce.
