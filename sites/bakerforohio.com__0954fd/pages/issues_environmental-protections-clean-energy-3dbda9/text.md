Environmental Protections & Clean Energy
Protecting Ohio’s air, water, and natural resources does not have to come at the expense of jobs.
Mike will hold corporate polluters accountable, invest in clean energy initiatives, and support energy efficiency programs that lower costs for families.
Key Points:
- Hold corporate polluters accountable to prevent environmental damage.
- Invest in clean energy sources like solar and wind to create jobs.
- Support energy efficiency programs to lower energy costs for families.
- Protect Ohio’s air, water, and farmland from industrial contamination.
