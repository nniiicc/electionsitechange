Mike will fight to protect Medicaid expansion , ensuring low-income and working families have access to affordable healthcare.
He will work to lower prescription drug prices by holding pharmaceutical companies accountable for price gouging and increasing pricing transparency.
Additionally, Mike is committed to expanding mental health services , especially for youth, veterans, and rural communities that lack access to proper care.

Lower prescription drug prices by increasing price transparency.
