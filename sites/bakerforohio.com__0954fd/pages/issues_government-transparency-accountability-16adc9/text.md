Government Transparency & Accountability
Trust in government starts with transparency and accessibility.
Mike is committed to open and honest governance, ensuring that elected officials work for the people—not special interests.
He will create a public tracker for constituent concerns, hold regular town halls and open office hours, and provide clear, public updates on legislation and policy decisions.
Voters deserve to know what’s happening at the Statehouse, and Mike will make sure they’re always informed.
Key Points:
- Establish a public tracker for constituent concerns and legislative updates.
- Hold regular town halls and office hours to hear directly from voters.
- Push for ethics reforms to reduce government corruption.
- Ensure taxpayer money is spent responsibly and transparently.
