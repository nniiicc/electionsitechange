Meet Mike Issues News Volunteer Yard Signs Contribute News / Statement on Issue 19: Elyria Schools Emergency Levy 6 Mar Thursday, 8:00 AM · 2025 Statement on Issue 19: Elyria Schools Emergency Levy As a proud member of the Elyria community and a strong advocate for quality public education, I fully support Issue 19 , the Elyria Schools Emergency Levy , on the upcoming May 6, 2025 ballot.
This levy is crucial for maintaining and enhancing the educational services that our children depend on.
What is Issue 19?
Issue 19 is an emergency operating levy designed to provide essential funding for the Elyria City School District.
This levy will support: Teacher and Staff Salaries: Ensuring that our schools can attract and retain qualified educators and support personnel.
Educational Programs: Maintaining a diverse range of programs that cater to the varying needs and interests of our students.
Classroom Resources: Providing up-to-date technology and materials necessary for effective teaching and learning.
Why is Your Support Important?
The passage of Issue 19 is vital for sustaining the quality of education in Elyria.
Without this funding, the district may face: Increased Class Sizes: Leading to less individualized attention for students.
Program Cuts: Reduction or elimination of programs that enrich our students' educational experiences.
Staff Reductions: Potential layoffs of teachers and support staff, adversely affecting student support and learning outcomes.
Fiscal Responsibility The Elyria City School District has demonstrated prudent financial management, as evidenced by: 20 Consecutive Clean Audit Awards: Recognizing the district's commitment to transparency and accountability.
No New Money Requests Since 2010: Highlighting the district's efforts to operate efficiently without additional taxpayer burdens.
Supporting Issue 19 is an investment in our community's future.
Quality education not only benefits our children but also enhances property values and attracts businesses to our area.
I encourage all Elyria residents to vote YES on Issue 19 on May 6, 2025, to ensure that our schools continue to provide the excellent education that our children deserve.
Together, we can uphold the standard of excellence in Elyria's schools and build a brighter future for our community.
