Education & Workforce Development
Every child deserves access to quality education regardless of their background.
Mike will fight to fully fund the Fair School Funding Plan so public schools have the resources they need, restore emergency and substitute levy tools that communities depend on, and prioritize neighborhood public schools over voucher programs.
He supports competitive teacher pay to attract and retain top educators and will work to expand career and technical education programs so students can graduate with real-world skills.
By investing in STEM, trade schools, and apprenticeship programs, Mike will help Ohio students prepare for well-paying jobs in the modern workforce.
Key Points:
- Fully fund the Fair School Funding Plan to ensure public schools are properly resourced.
- Restore emergency and substitute levy tools so communities can respond to urgent funding needs.
- Prioritize public schools over vouchers to strengthen neighborhood schools.
- Increase teacher pay to attract and retain top educators.
- Expand career and technical education programs to prepare students for high-demand jobs.
- Invest in STEM, trade schools, and apprenticeships to create more career opportunities.
