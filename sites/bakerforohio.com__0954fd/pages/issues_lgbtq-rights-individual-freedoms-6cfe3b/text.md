Meet Mike Issues News Volunteer Yard Signs Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ LGBTQ+ Rights & Individual Freedoms LGBTQ+ Rights & Individual Freedoms Every Ohioan deserves the freedom to live openly, safely, and authentically without fear of discrimination or political attacks.
Mike believes government should not be deciding who people can love, how they identify, or what healthcare decisions families make.
As politicians across the country increasingly target LGBTQ+ Ohioans, especially transgender youth, Mike will stand firmly against efforts to strip away rights, censor identities, or use vulnerable communities as political talking points.
Ohio should be a place where every person is treated with dignity and respect, no matter their sexual orientation or gender identity.
What Mike Will Fight For Protect LGBTQ+ Ohioans from discrimination in housing, healthcare, employment, and public accommodations.
Oppose attacks on transgender Ohioans and defend access to medically necessary healthcare.
Support safe and inclusive public schools where every student can learn without fear of bullying or censorship.
Protect marriage equality and personal privacy rights from government interference.
Expand access to mental health resources for LGBTQ+ youth and families.
Ensure Ohio laws reflect fairness, dignity, and equal protection for all people.
Why This Matters No one should be treated as less than because of who they are.
At a time when some politicians are trying to divide Ohioans for political gain, Mike believes our state moves forward by protecting freedoms, respecting differences, and standing up for every community.
