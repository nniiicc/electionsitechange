Lowering the Cost of Living & Housing Affordability
Ohioans are struggling with the rising costs of housing, food, and fuel, making it harder to afford a decent quality of life.
Mike will fight against corporate price gouging and hold companies accountable for unfairly inflating prices.
He will work to expand affordable housing initiatives, ensuring working families, seniors, and first-time homebuyers can find stable housing.
Additionally, Mike supports tax relief for middle-class families, so they can keep more of their hard-earned money.
Key Points:
- Stop corporate price gouging in food, fuel, and housing.
- Expand affordable housing programs for working families and seniors.
- Provide tax relief for middle-class families to ease financial burdens.
- Encourage responsible development that increases housing supply without displacing communities.
