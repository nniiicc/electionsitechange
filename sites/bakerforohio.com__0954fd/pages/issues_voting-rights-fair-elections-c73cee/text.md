Voting Rights & Fair Elections
Ohio’s elections should be fair, secure, and accessible to all eligible voters—not rigged by politicians.
Mike will oppose restrictive voter ID laws that make it harder for seniors, students, and working-class Ohioans to vote.
He will fight for fair district maps to eliminate gerrymandering and ensure elections are competitive.
Mike also supports expanding early voting and vote-by-mail options, making it easier for busy working families to cast their ballots without unnecessary barriers.
Key Points:
- Oppose restrictive voter ID laws that create barriers to voting.
- Support fair district maps to eliminate gerrymandering.
- Expand early voting and vote-by-mail options for voter accessibility.
- Ensure secure and fair elections without unnecessary restrictions.
