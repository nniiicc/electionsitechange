Meet Mike Issues News Volunteer Yard Signs Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Voting Rights & Fair Elections Voting Rights & Fair Elections Ohio’s elections should be fair, secure, and accessible to all eligible voters —not rigged by politicians.
Mike will oppose restrictive voter ID laws that make it harder for seniors, students, and working-class Ohioans to vote.
He will fight for fair district maps to eliminate gerrymandering and ensure elections are competitive.
Mike also supports expanding early voting and vote-by-mail options , making it easier for busy working families to cast their ballots without unnecessary barriers.
