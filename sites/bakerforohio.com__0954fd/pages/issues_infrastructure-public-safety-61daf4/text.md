Infrastructure & Public Safety
Ohio’s infrastructure is in dire need of upgrades.
Mike will push for critical road and bridge repairs, ensuring safe transportation for Ohioans while creating construction jobs.
He will fight for expanded broadband internet access, ensuring that rural and underserved communities can compete in the digital economy.
Mike will also work to support first responders, firefighters, and law enforcement by providing funding for training, mental health support, and updated equipment to keep communities safe.
Key Points:
- Invest in road and bridge repairs to improve safety and create jobs.
- Expand broadband internet access to underserved areas.
- Support first responders with better training, equipment, and mental health support.
- Ensure funding for public safety programs to keep communities safe.
