THE PEOPLE'S AGENDA
We the People need a government that works — and works for us.
Over months of traveling District 5, I’ve heard similar themes: South Carolinians are not happy with divisive politics, high costs, unreliable health care, attacks on public education, and above all—Congressional gridlock.
I’ve listened — and I believe we can find a path forward.
But, it requires real conversation, real commitment and real collaboration.
That is what I do best and that is what I am running to do.
In Washington, I will fight for real solutions to real problems facing South Carolinians every single day.
Did I miss something that matters to you?
If so, I want to hear about it.
