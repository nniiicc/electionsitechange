Accessibility General Alex Harper for Congress is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all people, including individuals with disabilities.
We strive to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards to ensure our website is inclusive and easy to navigate.
We are continuously working to enhance the accessibility and usability of our website and adhere to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, wherever possible.
These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor impairments.
Our efforts include: Regular reviews and updates to maintain accessibility best practices Using accessibility tools to assist with navigation Providing alternative text for images Ensuring proper color contrast and keyboard navigation Designing with screen readers in mind Feedback and Support If you experience any difficulty accessing content on our website or need assistance with any part of the site, please contact our team at info@dittmerforcongress.com.
