Every child deserves a great education, no matter their zip code.
That means investing in public schools, supporting teachers, modernizing classrooms, and making sure students have what they need to succeed — academically and emotionally.
South Carolina’s public education system consistently ranks in the bottom 10 nationally.
This is widely due to inadequate state funding and a state legislature more focused on culture wars than reading scores.
As a result, our kids are falling behind in math (#41), reading (#39), and college admissions testing (#47 in median ACT scores).
I’ll fight to fully fund our public schools, support educators, and keep political culture wars out of the classroom.
