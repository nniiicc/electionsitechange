Order at the Border & Fairness in the System
THE PEOPLE’S AGENDA
We the People insist on immigration policy rooted in law, order, and humanity.
I support serious immigration reform that strengthens border security, treats people with dignity, and attracts hardworking talent to the United States.
Our immigration system is broken — and political theater hasn’t fixed it.
I’ll work across party lines to secure the border, support our workforce, and restore constitutional law, order, and humanity to the system.
Priorities:
- Strengthen border security with a focus on system sustainability, lawful accountability, and human compassion
- Build a sustainable immigration system that minimizes disorder and overwhelm while maximizing national security and economic output
- Ensure due process and legal, warranted enforcement by U.S.
Immigration & Customs Enforcement
- Keep children with their families and ensure they have proper care if detained
- Create a fair path to citizenship for people who work hard and respect the rules
- Support South Carolina’s farms, businesses and workforce with simpler paths to legal status and citizenship for qualified workers
- Attract hardworking, law-abiding talent so we can continue to be a world leader in agriculture, technology, and the arts, and improve in healthcare and education
