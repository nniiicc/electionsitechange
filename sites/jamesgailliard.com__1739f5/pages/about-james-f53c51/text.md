James D.
Gailliard Political Leader Pastoral Leadership That Transforms Communities As Senior Pastor of Word Tabernacle Church, Pastor Gailliard has shepherded a ministry that now impacts thousands of families across 35 North Carolina counties and reaches internationally.
Under his leadership, the church transformed a former 110,000 sq. ft.
Home Depot into a thriving community hub—the largest building re-use project of its kind in North Carolina.
This represents more than renovation; it’s community reclamation at its finest.
Historic Legislative Leadership Pastor Gailliard made history as the first person of color elected to represent North Carolina House District 25, serving two terms with distinction.
His legislative work focused on the issues that matter most to working families: criminal justice reform, educational equity, and economic opportunity.
He brought both pastoral wisdom and policy expertise to Raleigh, serving on critical commissions including the Task Force for Racial Equity in Criminal Justice and the DRIVE Task Force for educational inclusion.
Commissioner of MyFutureNC Community Impact Beyond the Pulpit His commitment to systemic change extends through multiple leadership roles: Commissioner of MyFutureNC, co-developing North Carolina’s statewide educational attainment goals.
Founder and Chairman of The REACH Center, Inc.
Trustee of Elizabeth City State University.
