Donate Now Current Challenge: District 11 shows significant educational achievement gaps, with only 18% of Vance County adults having a two-year degree or higher.
School funding remains inadequate despite recent teacher pay increases.
Children in rural and under-resourced schools deserve the same opportunities as those in urban districts.
Our teachers deserve better pay, and our parents deserve more flexibility.
Policy Solutions: Support legislation ensuring full constitutional funding for public schools while maintaining quality school choice options.
Expand the NC Teaching Fellows Program.
Advocate for wraparound services including mental health support, nutrition programs, and after-school care.
Support universal pre-K expansion and community-based early childhood programs.
Champion student debt relief and increased Pell Grant funding for families in District 11, where 86% of Black students take out loans compared to 68% of white students.
Advocate for trauma-informed teacher and counselor-to-student ratio reforms.
Support quality educational options while ensuring public school funding remains strong, recognizing that 64% of African-American voters support expanded Opportunity Scholarships.
