Donate Now Current Challenge: District 11 faces severe housing affordability issues, with Vance County showing eviction filing rates over 20% – among the highest in the state.
Many low-income families sacrifice housing quality for affordability.
More than one-third of residents in parts of District 11 are rent-burdened.
Policy Solutions: Create an initiative providing down payment assistance, home ownership counseling, and support for first-time buyers.
Advocate for state funding to match federal community development block grants.
Support affordable housing development through public-private partnerships with faith communities.
Champion fair housing enforcement and anti-discrimination measures.
Create emergency rental assistance programs and eviction prevention services.
Partner with local churches and nonprofits to provide financial literacy education and home ownership preparation programs.
