Donate Now Current Challenge: Vance County faces crime costs of $519 per resident annually, with violent crime rates well above state averages.
The district needs comprehensive reform addressing both accountability and community restoration.
Policy Solutions: Advance the “Restoration and Reconciliation Act” building on recent NC criminal justice reforms (SB 300).
Support expansion of problem-solving courts, including drug courts and mental health courts.
Advocate for eliminating probation supervision fees and community service fees (currently pending in SB 529).
Create faith-based reentry programs partnering churches with formerly incarcerated individuals, providing mentorship, job placement, and housing assistance.
Support police-community partnerships while maintaining accountability through civilian oversight.
Champion debt-based driver’s license reform (HB 722) and eliminate barriers to employment from unpaid court debt (HB 980).
