Donate Now North Carolina Senate District 11 Why District 11, Why Now Our district stands at a crossroads: Economic Crossroads: Manufacturing jobs disappearing while new opportunities remain out of reach Young families unable to afford homes in communities where they grew up Seniors choosing between medication and meals Rural communities feeling left behind by economic growth As part of the North Carolina General Assembly, Senate District 11 plays a vital role in shaping statewide legislation and addressing local concerns such as education, healthcare, infrastructure, and economic development.
The district’s leadership has the opportunity to advocate for the needs of working families, small businesses, and underserved communities across the region.
Policy Positions Areas of concern Policy Positions Key priorities for District 11 include expanding workforce training, closing the digital divide, and advancing criminal justice reform.
The platform champions equitable healthcare, strong public schools, housing stability, and food security through initiatives like Homes and Hope and Abundant Life Nutrition.
