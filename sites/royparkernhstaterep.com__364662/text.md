About Roy Parker About Why Roy Parker is Running for NH State Rep About Why Roy Parker is Running for NH State Rep About Why Roy Parker is Running for NH State Rep I grew up in Nashua.
I moved to Texas and Colorado after college.
While I had fun in both places, I couldn't wait to get back to Nashua to raise a family.
Imagine the nightmare I came home to when the New Hampshire court system allowed my child to be involved in the eventual divorce.
Nobody wants children to be treated like this.
I want New Hampshire to be a place that other states envy.
There are many wonderful things about New Hampshire.
There are wonderful, hardworking people in NH.
But what message has a ruling like this sent to other states about New Hampshire and our values?
A terrible one.
That message needs to change.
I want New Hampshire to be a great place to live.
It's time there is an adherence to the rules.
It's time our rights and laws were protected.
They need to mean something.
No one should be able to steal them.
