Hi There!
I'M ANG NELSON
I’m an elementary school principal, a mom to two teenagers, and a lifelong Coon Rapids neighbor.
My roots here run deep, from biking to the Coon Rapids Dam and eating burgers at Shortstop as a kid to raising my own family just down the road from where my grandparents built their home in 1959.
I’m already in our community every day, in the grocery store, at high school sports, and at community events, listening to families about rising costs, burnout in our schools, and the challenges of making everything work.
I’ve spent my career in public education creating schools where students belong and are set up to succeed.
I’m running for the Minnesota State Senate because our community deserves a leader who listens, shows up, and puts people over politics.
LOWER COSTS
FOR FAMILIES
I’m focused on lowering everyday costs for working families like mine.
No one should ever have to choose between paying the bills, getting the care they need, and putting food on the table.
STRONG
PUBLIC SCHOOLS
I see every day how strong public schools hold our communities together.
I’ll fight for schools where every student belongs and is set up to succeed, and where our educators are supported.
SAFE AND FAIR COMMUNITIES
I believe safe and fair communities are built on trust, accountability, and care.
I’ll work to keep our communities safe while making sure rules are enforced fairly and resources
are used responsibly.
DIGNITY
FOR ALL
I believe everyone deserves dignity, safety, and respect at every stage of life.
I’ll stand up for seniors, people with disabilities, and communities that too often get left behind and overlooked.
