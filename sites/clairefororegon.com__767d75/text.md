About Claire
Claire Lynn is a seventh generation Oregonian who grew up on the North Fork of the Siuslaw River helping with her family’s logging business and cow‑calf operation.
From an early age, Claire learned the value of hard work and stewardship working during hay season, hunting and fishing with her dad, and watching her parents and grandparents balance timber work with community service.
Those deep roots took hold and Claire is known for her work ethic, deep convictions and ability to solve problems.
Claire’s commitment to her rural community is strong.
She has been a guardian with the South Willamette Valley Honor Flight and currently serves as a Court Appointed Special Advocate in Lane County.
After college, Claire took our rural values to both Salem and Washington, D.C. working in Republican legislative offices and on campaigns to defend natural‑resource jobs and bring common‑sense solutions to Oregon.
Today, Claire and her husband Brodey live outside of Elmira.
She is running for State Representative to ensure families like hers can continue to live, work and thrive on Oregon’s South-Central Coast.
