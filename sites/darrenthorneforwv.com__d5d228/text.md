Thorne for West Virginia Darren Thorne is a lifelong Hampshire County resident, farmer, and Christian conservative serving in the West Virginia State Senate.
Raised by a single mother, Darren learned the values of hard work, faith, and personal responsibility early in life.
A commercial poultry farmer and former owner operator truck driver, he entered public service to defend personal liberty, protect agriculture, and stand up for families.
Darren and his wife have been married for more than 32 years and raised their twins on their family farm.

Thorne on the Issues Agriculture & Food Safety Agriculture & Food Security Darren is a working farmer who understands firsthand that a strong food supply is essential to a healthy, independent nation.
He fights to protect family farms, defend property rights, and ensure West Virginia agriculture remains viable for future generations.
Cost of Living & Affordability West Virginia families are feeling the squeeze from rising costs, higher property values, and wages that haven’t kept pace especially in communities near the Virginia border.
Darren believes families should be able to afford to live, work, and raise their children in the communities they call home.
He supports commonsense policies that help keep jobs local, strengthen the workforce, and ensure working families aren’t priced out of their own counties.
Personal Liberty & Constitutional Government Personal Liberty & Constitutional Government Darren believes freedom is fragile and must be actively defended.
He has consistently stood against government overreach, supported limits on emergency powers, and voted to protect constitutional rights.
Every vote Darren casts is weighed against the Constitution and his deeply held values.
Medical Freedom & Informed Consent Medical Freedom & Informed Consent Darren supports medical freedom and informed consent, including religious and philosophical exemptions.
He is clear: West Virginians have the right to make healthcare decisions for themselves and their children without government coercion.
Promoting the Sanctity of Life Promoting the Sanctity of Life Darren is unapologetically pro-life.
He believes every life has value and has supported legislation to protect unborn children and oppose assisted suicide.
His faith guides his commitment to defending the most vulnerable.
Faith & Religious Freedom Faith & Religious Freedom Darren is a Christian conservative who believes religious liberty is foundational to a free society.
He will always defend the right of individuals and families to live according to their beliefs without interference from the government. ​ ​ Jobs & Economic Investment Jobs & Economic Investment Darren believes West Virginia’s future depends on growing good-paying jobs while protecting the communities that make the state unique.
