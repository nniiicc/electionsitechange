Key Dates & Deadlines
Make sure your voice is counted.
Keep these critical dates and deadlines in mind as you prepare to cast your ballot.
Voter Registration Deadline
Applications must be received by the Board of Elections no later than 10 days before an election to be eligible to vote.
Absentee / Early Mail Request
Online and mail applications must be received at least 10 days before the election.
In-person requests can be made up to the day before.
Early Voting Period
Early voting begins 10 days before Election Day and runs for 9 consecutive days, including weekends.
Election Day
Polls are open from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
You must vote at your assigned polling place.
