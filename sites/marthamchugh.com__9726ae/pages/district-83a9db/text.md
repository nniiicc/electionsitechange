The 46th New York State Senate District spans the heart of the Capital Region and the Mohawk Valley, bringing together a diverse tapestry of historic cities, growing suburbs, and rural communities.
From the state capital of Albany to the manufacturing legacy of Amsterdam, this district is defined by its industrious spirit, close-knit neighborhoods, and rich history.
But for too long, state government has lost touch with the everyday realities of the people who live here.
High taxes, rising energy bills, and public safety challenges have put unnecessary pressure on families and small businesses.
Marti McHugh is running to bring practical, focused leadership back to the Capitol, representing every corner of this district with the seriousness and accountability it deserves.

Albany County combines our state’s political core with vibrant suburban communities and historic towns.
As an Albany native raised in a family of eight, Marti knows these neighborhoods inside and out.
She understands that the decisions made in the Capitol must reflect the real needs of the people living just blocks away.
Key Local Concerns 1 Public Safety Albany County ranked fifth highest in New York for violent crime in 2023.
Marti is committed to supporting law enforcement and restoring common-sense bail policies to keep our neighborhoods safe.
2 Small Business Pressure Local shops and restaurants face rising costs, state mandates, and bureaucratic red tape.
Marti will fight for tax relief and regulatory reform to help small businesses thrive.
3 Youth Retention We must make Albany County an affordable, opportunity-rich place where our children can afford to stay, work, and raise families of their own.

Region-Wide Focus Addressing the concerns of every community.
Whether in our cities, suburbs, or rural towns, the challenges facing Capital Region families require practical, serious, and focused solutions.
Here is how Marti’s platform addresses the core issues in every corner of the 46th District.
Public Safety Reversing policies that weaken community safety, supporting local law enforcement, and addressing the opioid crisis with prevention and treatment.
Affordability Lowering the pressure on household budgets through targeted tax freezes, exempting seniors from property taxes, and controlling state spending.
Energy Costs Pausing state taxes, fees, and mandates that drive up utility and heating bills, and delivering a rebate check to every utility-paying resident.
Downtown Vitality Revitalizing historic main streets, supporting commercial corridors, and attracting new investments and tourism to our local communities.
Small Business Pressure Cutting red tape, easing state mandates, and lowering taxes to help local employers meet payroll and grow their businesses.
Youth Retention Creating high-quality opportunities and affordable living conditions so the next generation can build their lives, careers, and families here.
