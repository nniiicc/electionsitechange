ABOUT ME
Lindsay Gramlich for Indiana House District 39
I’m Lindsay Gramlich, and I’m running for Indiana House District 39.
As a mom, nonprofit leader, and active Carmel neighbor, I’m committed to serving the community that shaped me and the one where I’m raising my family today.
I’m ready to bring that commitment to the Statehouse.
Why I'm Running:
- I am running because I believe in the Constitution and in restoring the checks and balances our founders envisioned.
- I am running to ensure that every Hoosier has access to their fundamental human rights and basic needs.
- I am running for my daughter, for my friends, and for a future that looks forward—not backward—on women’s rights.
- I am running to bring humanity back into politics and to represent the best interests of District 39.
- I am Running for the People, Rooted in Community.
