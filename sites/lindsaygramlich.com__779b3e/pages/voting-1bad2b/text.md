Voting Information
Voter registration closes Monday, October 5th.
Register to vote or check your registration at IndianaVoters.com
Election Day
November 3rd
Polls will be open from 6:00 A.M. - 6:00 P.M.
Any person registered to vote by October 5th is eligible to do so on Election Day.
Bring your government-issued ID when you vote.
To find your polling place, go to IndianaVoters.com and click "Find Your Polling Place".
For more information about voting on Election Day, click here.

October 6th - November 2nd

Absentee Voting
Application Deadline: October 22nd
A registered voter in Hamilton County can vote absentee by mail
if they meet certain requirements listed here.
To apply for an absentee ballot, use the online absentee application at IndianaVoters.com, obtain an application in person at the Hamilton County Judicial Center (Suite 106), download the application here, or call the Hamilton County Election Office at 317-776-8476.
After your application has been processed, you will receive a paper ballot to review for your selection and a ballot card to record your vote.
Once you have voted your absentee ballot, you can return your ballot by mail or return it in person to the Hamilton County Election office.
Ballots, whether mail or hand-delivered, must be received by the Election Office on or before Election Day (November 3rd) at 6 PM.
