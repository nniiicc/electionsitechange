Lend A Hand
Too many families are struggling to put food on the table or access the basic resources they need to live with dignity.
This isn’t just an economic challenge — it’s a moral one.
A failure of leadership at the top to recognize our shared humanity and interdependence has weakened the social fabric that holds our communities together.
Elizabeth Moro believes that when we remember our connection to one another, we can rebuild the social infrastructure that sustains us — ensuring access to food, healthcare, and opportunity for all.
If you or someone you know is experiencing food insecurity, you can find your nearest food pantry here.
And if you’re able to lend a hand, please consider supporting one of these local organizations helping our neighbors in their time of need.
Together, we can restore a sense of care and responsibility to public life — and build a community where everyone has the support they need to thrive.
