This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information from supporters and visitors to our website.
1.
Information We Collect We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, which may include: Personal Identifiers: Name, mailing address, and email address.
Financial Information: Credit card details and transaction history (processed securely via our third-party payment processors) for campaign donations.
Mobile Information: Phone numbers provided specifically for SMS updates and alerts.
2.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Provide updates on the campaign and upcoming events.
Process and confirm your donations.
Respond to your inquiries or volunteer requests.
Send text messages to supporters who have opted in.
3.
Mobile Messaging & Third-Party Sharing We value your privacy and the security of your mobile information.
Mobile information will not be shared with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.
All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be sold or shared with any third parties.
4.
Data Security and Retention We take reasonable steps to protect your data from unauthorized access.
Your information is stored using secure, industry-standard platforms, which utilize encryption both in-transit and at-rest.
Access to supporter data is strictly limited to authorized campaign staff and volunteers.
We retain donor information as long as required by Pennsylvania and Federal campaign finance law.
5.
Your Choices and Opt-Out Email: You can unsubscribe from our email list at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any email.
SMS: You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us.
For assistance, reply HELP.
Data Requests: You may contact us at the address below to request access to or deletion of your personal data.
