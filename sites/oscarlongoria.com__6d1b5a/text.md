Happy Texas Tax-Free Weekend!
Starting today through Sunday at 11:59 PM (August 7-9th), Texas is waiving sales tax on clothing, shoes, backpacks, and school supplies priced under $100 — on every qualifying item, whether you shop in-store or online!
It’s a small break that can make a real difference for families gearing up for the new school year.
Take advantage of the savings, check off that school list, and let’s send our kids back with everything they need.
Wishing everyone in District 35 and Texas a great weekend of shopping and savings! #txlege #HD35 #puro956 #taxfreeweekend
