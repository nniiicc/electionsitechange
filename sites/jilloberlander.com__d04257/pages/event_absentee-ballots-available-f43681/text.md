Absentee ballots available October 2 All day If you want an absentee ballot, you must first complete an application online or in person at your Town Clerk’s Office.
Absentee ballots will be mailed to those who have completed an application starting October 2.
Starting October 2, you may walk into your Town Clerk’s office, request an absentee ballot in person, receive your ballot on the spot, then complete and return your ballot to the Town Clerk or place it in a secure official ballot drop box.
Completed absentee ballots must be received back by your Town Clerk’s Office by 8 pm on Election Day–November 3rd.
Find information about your Clerk’s Office and drop box locations here: Greenwich Town Clerk Stamford Town Clerk New Canaan Town Clerk A new Connecticut law provides all eligible voters with the option of casting their ballots in elections using absentee ballots .
