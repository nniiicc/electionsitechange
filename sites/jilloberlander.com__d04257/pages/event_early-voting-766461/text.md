Early voting
October 19 – November 1
Early voting is available October 19 through November 1.
Hours are 10 am to 6 pm daily — this includes two weekends.
There are extended hours on Tuesday, October 27 and Thursday, October 29 — 8 am to 8 pm.
If you are eligible, but not registered to vote, you can register at your early voting location on those early voting days and then vote.
Greenwich early voting location: Greenwich Town Hall, 101 Field Point Road
Stamford early voting location: Government Center, 888 Washington Blvd
New Canaan early voting location: New Canaan Town Hall, 77 Main Street
