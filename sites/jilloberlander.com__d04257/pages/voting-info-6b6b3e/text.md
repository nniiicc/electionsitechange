NOTE: Your Election Day voting location may be different Absentee Ballots New in 2026: Any eligible voter in CT may vote by “no-excuse” absentee ballot.
You may submit an online absentee ballot application starting September 19.
Absentee ballots for the November 3rd election will be mailed out staring October 2.
Starting October 2, you can also go into your town clerk’s office and fill out an absentee ballot application in person, receive your ballot on the spot, complete it and return it in person.
