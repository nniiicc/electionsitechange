Skip to content About Jill Voting Info Issues News Events Get Involved Donate Instagram Facebook YouTube LinkedIn People over politics Newsletter • October 15, 2026 Good governance isn’t partisan Lately, politics have become too much about picking a side.
That’s not how I approach public service.
I believe in listening, looking at the facts and making decisions based on what’s best for the people I represent — not governing from the extremes.
That’s how I led Greenwich’s finance board.
I worked across party lines and was unanimously elected by both parties to serve as chair.
As a leader, I don’t ask whether an idea comes from a Democrat or Republican.
I ask whether it makes sense and if it’s right for the town.
I’ll bring that same approach to Hartford.
Here’s what I believe is important: practicing fiscal discipline and keeping taxes low; supporting strong public schools; fostering a healthy business environment; protecting local zoning; and taking care of our neighbors.
I’m not running to represent the left or the right.
I’m running to represent you.
