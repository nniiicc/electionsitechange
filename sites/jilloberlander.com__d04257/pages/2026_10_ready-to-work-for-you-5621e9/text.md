I bring decades of experience solving complex problems as an attorney, along with a proven record of hands-on leadership in our community — on the Greenwich town legislature, the Board of Selectmen, and as chair of the Greenwich finance board.
I’ve worked across party lines to fund our schools and essential services, while keeping our property taxes low.
I know how government works — how budgets are built, how laws are written, and how to turn good ideas into achievable results.
And I also know that effective public service starts with listening, caring about the people you represent, and being willing to put in the work.
In Hartford, my priorities will be clear: tackle the cost of living, hold utilities accountable, support businesses and job creation, protect strong public schools and preserve the local voice in zoning and development decisions.
I will stand up for the fundamental rights and values Connecticut residents have said matter to them — including reproductive freedom, common-sense gun safety and access to the ballot.
I’ll bring an independent approach to this job.
I won’t judge an idea by which party proposed it; I’ve never operated like that.
I’ll look at the facts, listen to the people I represent, and focus on results for our district.
This community has been my home for more than 20 years.
It’s where my husband, Larry, and I raised our family, where I’ve volunteered and served, and where I’ve been fortunate to know so many extraordinary neighbors.
I care deeply about our district, and I want to do the work — every day — to make it an even better place to live, work and raise a family.
It would be an honor to bring my experience — as an attorney, public servant, neighbor and a mom — and put that to work for you in Hartford.
