Demonstrated Leadership
Proven
Jill is a proven leader, experienced public servant, and dedicated advocate for Connecticut families.
A Greenwich resident for 20 years, she has served as Selectperson, as the first Democratic chair of the town finance board, and on the town legislature.
Experienced
During her finance board tenure, Jill kept property taxes low — lower increases than the 10 years prior under Republican leadership — while responsibly supporting schools and town services.
Jill is an attorney and has spent her career working in government and public service.
Dedicated
Jill is running for state senate to bring fiscally responsible leadership to Hartford — protecting taxpayers, lowering the cost of living and growing our economy.
She’ll stand up for local control over zoning decisions and take on the challenges that hit families hardest, from rising healthcare costs to high utility rates.
