Privacy Policy
Last updated: 06/15/2026
This Privacy Policy explains how the Candace for Kansas campaign ("we," "us," or "the campaign") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website, sign up for updates, volunteer, or make a contribution.
Information we collect
We collect information you give us directly.
This includes your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any message you send us when you fill out a form, sign up for updates, volunteer, or contact the campaign.
When you make a contribution, federal and state law require us to collect and report certain information, including your name, mailing address, occupation, and employer.
Contributions are processed through our payment processor, ActBlue.
Your payment card details are handled by ActBlue and are not stored by the campaign.
We also collect limited technical information automatically when you visit the site, such as your device type, browser, pages viewed, and general location based on your IP address.
This helps us understand how people use the site and improve it.
How we use your information
We use your information to send you campaign updates, respond to your questions, organize volunteers and events, process and acknowledge contributions, comply with campaign finance and election laws, and improve our website and outreach.
Text messages (SMS)
If you give us your phone number and opt in, we may send you campaign text messages, including updates, event reminders, volunteer requests, and contribution asks.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any contribution or of volunteering.
You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
Reply HELP for help.
We honor opt-out requests promptly.
How we share your information
We do not sell your personal information.
We share information with service providers who help us run the campaign, such as our website host, email and texting platforms, and payment processor, only so they can perform services for us.
We may share information when required by law, including for campaign finance reporting to the Kansas Public Disclosure Commission, or in response to a lawful request.
Contributions made through ActBlue are also subject to ActBlue's Privacy Policy.
When you give through ActBlue, you can choose whether to share your contact information with the campaign.
We encourage you to review their policy.
Cookies and analytics
Our website may use cookies and similar tools to remember your preferences and measure site traffic.
You can set your browser to refuse cookies, though some parts of the site may not work as well.
Your choices
You can unsubscribe from emails using the link at the bottom of any message, opt out of texts by replying STOP, or contact us at candaceforkansas@gmail.com to ask us to update or remove your information.
We will do our best to honor reasonable requests, subject to recordkeeping we are legally required to maintain.
Children
Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
Changes to this policy
We may update this policy from time to time.
When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above.
