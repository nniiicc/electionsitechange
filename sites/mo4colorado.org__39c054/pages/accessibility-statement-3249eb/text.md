We at mo4colorado are dedicated to making our campaign site accessible to all citizens of Colorado State House District 64.
What web accessibility is
Web accessibility means that all visitors, including those with disabilities, can navigate and interact with our site effectively.
We aim to provide equal access to information and services for everyone.
Accessibility adjustments on this site
We have enhanced our site in line with WCAG 2.1 guidelines and have achieved compliance at the AA level.
Our site’s features are designed to work seamlessly with assistive technologies, including screen readers and keyboard navigation.
Some adjustments include:
Conducted an accessibility audit to identify and resolve potential issues
Defined the primary language of the site
Organized content in a logical order
Established clear heading structures for easy navigation
Provided alternative text for all images
Ensured color combinations meet accessibility contrast standards
Limited the use of motion to enhance usability
Made all multimedia content accessible

Accessibility arrangements in the organization
Our campaign offices are equipped with accessible features, including designated parking, ramps for wheelchair access, and assistive listening devices available during events.
We aim to ensure that all constituents can participate fully in the democratic process.
