Immigration Certain farms need year round guest workers - like a dairy.
The US does not allow this.
While we wait for Congress to act, let's take advantage of the pool of vetted migrants currently in the US - the 4 million migrants waiting for their asylum hearing.
Immigration Policy The US does not allow farmers to hire long-term migrant workers, who are essential for dairies and other farms.
A significant percentage of migrants would like to make money in the US for 5 years and then return to their home country and purchase a farm.
This is already happening outside of the legal system.
Let's take advantage of the 4 million asylum seekers waiting for their hearing - the grant rate is 25%, which means 3 million people will need to be deported. ​ The kindest method of deportation is self deportation.
Let's make this happen. ​ Give every asylum seeker (with no criminal record) an automatic agricultural work permit – if asylum is denied, allow them to work for 2 additional years so they have funds to go back home.
Allow the farmer to petition to renew these visas.
The migrant worker can then generate wealth and self-deport at any time as they will have funds to do so.
We have already spent significant sums vetting these people.
Let's not lose that value.
This kills several birds with one stone: It is a humane way to allow asylum seekers to stay in the US or to self-deport; It addresses the labor shortage for farmers in Michigan; It allows the US to maintain a stable population or to manage a slow and linear decline – we can grant them citizenship to keep the population wherever we want.
China can't do that - and they are already experiencing what will be a massive decline in population.
The US has a superpower over China in that people still want to come to the US.
Let's leverage that.
Self deportation saves the federal government deportation expenses.
