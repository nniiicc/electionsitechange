Effective August 13, 2026 • Campaign Website Policy 01 Introduction Our commitment to your privacy.
Richard Hayes for State Representative respects the privacy of visitors, supporters, volunteers and others who interact with this website.
This Privacy Policy explains the types of information the campaign may collect, how that information may be used and the choices available to you.
By using this website or submitting information through it, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.
02 Information Information we collect.
We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to the campaign, including: First and last name Email address Mobile telephone number Mailing or street address City and ZIP code Messages or questions submitted to the campaign Volunteer interests Yard sign requests Interest in campaign events, voter outreach or other campaign activities Email communication preferences Text message communication preferences Other information you choose to provide When you submit information through a website form, the campaign may also maintain information associated with the submission, such as the date and time it was received.
03 Use of Information How we use information.
Information collected through this website may be used to: Respond to questions, comments or requests Coordinate volunteers and campaign activities Fulfill yard sign requests Provide information about campaign events Communicate with supporters Send campaign news and updates when requested Send text message communications when a user has opted in Organize voter outreach and other campaign activities Maintain campaign records Protect the website against misuse, fraud or security threats Improve the website and campaign communications Comply with applicable laws or legal obligations ★ Your communication choices remain optional.
Submitting a general contact request, volunteering or requesting a yard sign does not by itself require you to agree to receive campaign email or text messages.
04 Email Email communications.
Visitors may choose to receive campaign news, updates, event information and other email communications from Richard Hayes for State Representative.
You may unsubscribe from campaign email communications at any time by using the unsubscribe instructions included in an email or by contacting the campaign.
Transactional or individual communications directly related to a request you submitted may still be sent when reasonably necessary to respond to that request.
05 Mobile Communications Text message communications.
If you voluntarily opt in to receive campaign text messages, the campaign may send recurring campaign communications to the mobile number you provide.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out of campaign text messages at any time by replying STOP .
You may reply HELP for assistance where supported by the applicable messaging service. ★ Consent to receive text messages is not required to contact the campaign, volunteer, request a yard sign, make a contribution or otherwise support Richard Hayes.
06 Campaign Forms Website form submissions.
Information submitted through campaign forms may be stored within the campaign's WordPress website and may also be delivered to authorized campaign personnel by email.
The campaign may retain form submissions and related records for campaign operations, historical recordkeeping, compliance purposes and other legitimate campaign needs.
Information may be deleted when the campaign determines that it is no longer reasonably necessary to retain it, subject to applicable legal or recordkeeping requirements.
Access to stored campaign information is intended to be limited to authorized personnel and service providers who require access for campaign purposes.
07 Email Delivery Brevo and email delivery services.
The campaign may use Brevo and related email-delivery services to assist with reliable delivery of website-generated and campaign email communications.
Information necessary to deliver an email may be processed by those services in connection with providing their services to the campaign.
Third-party service providers maintain their own privacy and data-processing practices.
08 Contributions Donations and WinRed.
Campaign contributions made through links on this website are processed by WinRed , a third-party fundraising platform.
When you select a donation link, you leave this website and interact directly with WinRed.
Payment information and other information provided during the contribution process are collected and processed by WinRed according to its own terms, privacy policies and applicable campaign-finance requirements.
Richard Hayes for State Representative does not process payment-card information directly through this website.
Information associated with a contribution may be provided to the campaign by WinRed as permitted or required for fundraising, campaign operations and campaign-finance reporting.
09 Website Technology Depending on the services enabled on the website, information collected automatically may include: Browser or device type General geographic information Referring website or source Pages viewed Approximate visit duration Interaction with website content Internet Protocol address or similar technical information Analytics providers may process this information according to their own privacy policies.
You may be able to limit cookies or similar technologies through your browser or device settings.
10 External Services Third-party websites and services.
This website may contain links to third-party websites, social media services, fundraising platforms, government resources, news sources or other external websites.
Richard Hayes for State Representative is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, accuracy or content of third-party websites.
Visiting an external website is subject to that site's own policies and terms.
11 Disclosure How information may be shared.
The campaign may share information with: Authorized campaign staff, consultants or volunteers Technology, hosting, email, communications or other service providers Vendors assisting with campaign operations Governmental or regulatory authorities when disclosure is required by law Other parties when reasonably necessary to protect the campaign, website, users or legal rights ★ Text Messaging Privacy.
We will not share text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, provided that the foregoing does not apply to sharing (1) with vendors, consultants and other service providers who need access to such information to carry out work on our behalf (and who will not use such information for their own purposes); (2) if we believe disclosure is required by any applicable law, rule, or regulation or to comply with law enforcement or legal process; and (3) if the user consents to our sharing of such information.
The campaign may also use information internally for legitimate political, campaign, fundraising and organizational purposes.
The campaign does not sell payment-card information collected through WinRed because such information is not processed directly through this website.
12 Security Data security.
The campaign takes reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information under its control.
No website, database, email system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Visitors should therefore use appropriate care when deciding what information to submit electronically.
13 Children Children's privacy.
This website is intended for a general audience and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If the campaign becomes aware that personal information from a child under 13 has been submitted without appropriate authorization, reasonable steps may be taken to remove it.
14 Your Choices Your privacy choices.
You may: Decline to provide optional information Decline email communications Decline text message communications Unsubscribe from campaign emails Reply STOP to campaign text messages Contact the campaign regarding information you previously submitted Certain information may need to be retained when required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate campaign records.
15 Updates Changes to this Privacy Policy.
This Privacy Policy may be updated as campaign practices, technology, service providers or legal requirements change.
When material changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
16 Contact Questions about this policy?
Questions about this Privacy Policy or the campaign's handling of information may be submitted through the Contact / Get Involved page on this website.
