About HD 57 A district shaped by Denton County's growth.
Texas House District 57 was created by the Texas Legislature following the 2020 Census as Denton County continued to experience significant population growth.
Today, HD 57 stretches across the heart of Denton County, bringing together established neighborhoods, growing communities and areas experiencing significant new development.
That growth creates new opportunities while increasing demands on roads, water, power and other essential infrastructure.
Richard Hayes has represented House District 57 since his election in 2022 and works to make sure the communities of the district have a voice in the Texas House.
Across Denton County Communities in HD 57 HD 57 includes all or portions of communities across Denton County.
