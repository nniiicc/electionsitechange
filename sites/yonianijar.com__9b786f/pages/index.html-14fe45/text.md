MEET YONI ANIJAR Yoni Anijar is a dedicated leader, passionate advocate, and seasoned problem-solver with a track record of success across multiple industries.
With a deep understanding of the challenges facing Florida’s families, businesses, and communities, Yoni is running for the Florida State House to bring a fresh, solutions-oriented approach to state government.
As a first-generation American , Yoni was raised by two Israeli immigrant business owners who built their own business from the ground up.
Growing up in an entrepreneurial household, Yoni saw firsthand the dedication, resilience, and sacrifices required to make a small business succeed.
From a young age, he worked side by side with his parents, learning the value of hard work , customer service , and community .
This early exposure to the challenges of entrepreneurship shaped his strong work ethic and instilled in him a deep sense of responsibility to honor the sacrifices his parents made to build a better life for their family.
Yoni’s academic journey further shaped his dedication to excellence.
He graduated with honors from the University of Florida, where he developed a strong foundation in leadership and critical thinking.
He then went on to earn his law degree from The George Washington University Law School, where he continued to excel academically and professionally, laying the groundwork for a successful legal career.
After law school, Yoni began his career at a prestigious large law firm in New York City, where he gained invaluable experience in corporate law, mergers and acquisitions, and complex negotiations.
His time at the firm equipped him with the skills to navigate high-stakes legal issues and work with top-tier clients in various sectors.
Yoni then transitioned to the tech industry, serving as in-house counsel for a leading technology company .
There, he played a pivotal role in managing complex legal challenges and driving strategic initiatives that supported business growth.
His experience at the tech company led to a position at a Fortune 500 corporation, where he continued to build expertise in business strategy, risk management, and corporate governance.
Yoni then went on to manage his own law firm, First Gen Law, PLC, specialising in corporate transactions for startups, technology, and media companies.
There, Yoni employed several attorneys and legal professionals while managing dozens of both US and international clientele.
As an entrepreneur and business owner , Yoni understands the challenges many small business owners face and will strive to support legislation to create an even more favourable landscape to attract new businesses and employers to Florida.
While building a successful career in the private sector, Yoni felt a strong call to serve and decided to transition into the U.S.
Army Reserve.
As a Captain and Judge Advocate General (JAG) , he uses his legal expertise to support soldiers and their families, helping them navigate the challenges of military law and deployment.
For Yoni, the decision to serve those who defend our nation has been a deeply rewarding and honorable chapter in his career.
Yoni’s diverse legal career has given him a unique perspective on how to create practical, effective policies that benefit both businesses and individuals.
He understands the complexities of balancing innovation, regulation, and economic growth, and he is committed to bringing this expertise to Florida’s government.

WHY YONI IS RUNNING Yoni’s decision to run for the State House comes from his belief that Florida deserves leadership focused on practical solutions, collaboration, and results.
He believes that our state can do better by putting partisanship aside and working together to address the critical issues facing Floridians today.
Whether it's improving education, lowering the cost of living, expanding job opportunities, or ensuring the safety and well-being of our communities, Yoni is committed to delivering forward-thinking policies that make a difference.
His vision for Florida is one where every family has the opportunity to thrive, where small businesses can grow and prosper, and where the government operates efficiently and transparently.
Yoni will fight for policies that promote economic development, protect our environment, and provide better access to healthcare and education for all Floridians.
Yoni’s mission is clear: to use his legal expertise, leadership, and military experience to ensure that the residents of Florida's 100th State House District continue to have a representative that fights for conservatives' priorities.
The choice for who represents this district should be of vital importance to each and every voter who went to the ballot last November in favor of the new leadership in Washington D.C.
Yoni Anijar is a proven leader who has spent his life serving his community and his country.
He is a political outsider who is ready to bring this same commitment to public service, coupled with his entrepreneurial background, to the Florida State House in order to ensure that the 100th District remains as Broward County's loudest conservative voice in Tallahassee.
With a track record of uniting people, solving complex problems, and working hard to deliver real results, Yoni is the candidate who will stand up for Florida’s future.
