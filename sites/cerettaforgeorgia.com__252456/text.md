Democrat for
Georgia's 12th District
I am Ceretta Smith.
I’ve dedicated my life to service, grounded in vision, and have fought hard for my constituents, with joy.
From serving nearly a decade as a U.S.
Army medic to fighting for working families as a federal employee, and national union leader, I’ve always believed leadership means showing up for people, not special interests.
For twenty-five years, I stood on the front lines for labor, civil, and human rights because every Georgian deserves dignity, fair pay, and opportunity, no matter their ZIP code.
My vision for our future is rooted in that same belief: that government should work for people, not the other way around.
As a city councilmember, I’ve turned those values into action championing our children, and holding local government accountable.
My joy comes from empowering others to lead and from helping communities thrive together.
With my experience in government, leadership, and caring for people, I understand how policy decisions in Washington impact us, our families, workers, and small businesses here at home, and I’m ready to fight for solutions that put our communities first.
These are my guiding values; Vision, Service, and Joy!
VISION
My vision is to secure what Georgia’s 12th District needs now—good jobs, strong schools, and healthy communities—while preparing for the future with investments in new industries, infrastructure, and opportunity for the next generation.
SERVICE
My record of service—as an Army medic, federal labor leader, and Grovetown Councilwoman—shows I deliver for people.
In Congress, I will secure resources that matter today, like fair wages and healthcare, while building long-term solutions that keep GA-12 and all citizens thriving.
JOY
I believe our communities need joyful warriors now more than ever—leaders who fight hard but also lift spirits, unite people, and spark hope.
I want to bring that every day, to show Georgia that progress can energize us, inspire action, and remind us what we can achieve together.
This is my platform, my priorities, for this campaign.
