Gale Mastrofrancesco for State Representative - 80th District FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE January 17, 2018 Contact: For more information Cell Phone (203) 525-3589 or galemastrofrancesco@gmail.com MASTROFRANCESCO ANNOUNCES CAMPAIGN FOR STATE REPRESENTATIVE (Wolcott, CT) - Gale Mastrofrancesco of Wolcott announced today that she will be running for State Representative in the 80th Assembly District, which covers Wolcott and part of Southington.
Mastrofrancesco has served on the Wolcott Town Council for the past eight years, six of them as Vice-Chair.
Incumbent State Representative Rob Sampson has officially filed a campaign committee for State Senate in the 16th District.
“I am proud and excited to announce my candidacy for the republican nomination for State Representative,” said Mastrofrancesco.
“Fiscal management and strong leadership are crucial in these difficult economic times.
I will represent the people of Wolcott and Southington with the same energy and tenacity that characterized my work on the Wolcott Town Council." Mastrofrancesco has experienced first-hand how the economics of state government has affected Connecticut towns.
“Due to the reckless spending and continued mismanagement of Governor Malloy and the Democratcontrolled legislature, towns like Southington and Wolcott have lost funding,” she said.
“Unless there is a dramatic change in course, this will hurt everyone in our community: senior citizens, children's education, businesses and jobs—and our property taxes.
“Our future is in jeopardy, as we lose taxpayers to other states because of the job-killing policies.
I have experience as a fiscal manager, and I know firsthand how to balance budgets, using common sense business strategies.
“Representative Sampson has served the voters of the 80th district with distinction and dedication,” said Mastrofrancesco.
“I want to continue his tradition of strong and effective leadership, willing to take a stand and fight for us.
I am excited about the opportunity to work as a team with Representative Sampson and his Republican colleagues as we take back Connecticut in November. “ Paid for by Gale Mastrofrancesco for State Rep.
Barbara Roberts, Treasurer.
