Joe Markley Southington On November 6, 2018, Republican Gale Mastrofrancesco was elected to the Connecticut House of Representatives from the 80 th District.
She won her election based on her record as being a fiscal conservative and a budget watch dog.
She adheres to the principal of common-sense budgeting, holding the line on frivolous spending and being fiscally responsible.
This is what we need more of in Hartford to get our financial house in order.
During her first two years as our State Representative, she has fought for the CT Taxpayer and business owners and voted against tax increases, bills and regulations that hamper business growth and infringe upon some of our personal freedoms.
She has made herself available to her constituents on a regular basis and is a strong supporter of Law Enforcement and Veteran groups.
I will be voting for Gale on November 3, 2020, and I hope you join me in letting Gale continue her work in Hartford and fight for our best interests and bring fiscal responsibility back to our state.
