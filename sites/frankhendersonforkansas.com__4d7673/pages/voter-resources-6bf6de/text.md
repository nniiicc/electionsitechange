Get Voter Assistance
The ACLU of Kansas runs the Kansas-based statewide, nonpartisan voter assistance program, Election Protection responding to issues called in to the 866-OUR-VOTE hotline.
If you have any questions or issues with registering to vote, voting early or by mail, or voting on Election Day, notify the hotline so a trained attorney or volunteer can assist or investigate.
Vote Your Union Values
Visit ImVotingUnion.com and enter your address to find the candidates on your ballot who have earned the endorsement of the Kansas AFL-CIO and local labor councils.
These candidates have been reviewed and recommended by working people because of their commitment to unions, workers’ rights, fair wages, safe workplaces, and strong communities.
