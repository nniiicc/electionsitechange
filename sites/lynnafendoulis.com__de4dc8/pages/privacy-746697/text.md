Privacy Policy
Effective June 1, 2026
Lynn Afendoulis for State Representative ("the Campaign," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you provide through our website, lynnafendoulis.com, and through related campaign communications.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following information when you choose to provide it to us:
- Name, email address, mailing address, and ZIP code
- Telephone number (mobile or landline), if you choose to provide it
- Volunteer preferences and areas of campaign support you wish to offer
- Donation information processed through our authorized payment partners
- Communication history with the Campaign, including text message and email exchanges
How We Use Your Information
We use the information you provide to:
- Respond to your inquiries and requests
- Coordinate volunteer activities you have agreed to support
- Send campaign updates, event invitations, and election information
- Process and acknowledge contributions to the Campaign
- Comply with Michigan campaign finance disclosure obligations
Text Messaging and SMS Communications
If you provide your telephone number and consent to receive text messages from the Campaign, you may receive SMS communications related to the campaign, including event reminders, election information, volunteer requests, and donation appeals.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency may vary.
You may opt out at any time by replying "STOP" to any text message you receive from us, or reply "HELP" for assistance.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
How We Share Your Information
The Campaign does not sell personal information collected through this website.
We may share information in the following limited circumstances:
- With service providers who help us operate the Campaign (for example, email and text messaging vendors, payment processors, and event-management platforms), subject to confidentiality obligations
- When required by Michigan campaign finance law, including disclosure of donor information meeting the reporting threshold to the Michigan Bureau of Elections
- When required by other applicable law, court order, or legal process
Donations
Contributions are processed through authorized payment partners.
Donor information meeting the reporting threshold under Michigan campaign finance law is publicly disclosed as required by the Michigan Campaign Finance Act.
Cookies and Website Analytics
Our website may use cookies and standard analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site.
You can control cookies through your browser settings.
Children's Privacy
This website is not directed to children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Your Choices
You may unsubscribe from email communications using the link in any email we send.
You may opt out of SMS communications by replying "STOP" to any message.
You may request that we update or remove your personal information from our records by contacting us at the address below.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
