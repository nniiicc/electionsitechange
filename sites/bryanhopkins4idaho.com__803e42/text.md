Register & Find Your Polling Location Click Here for Information Welcome to my campaign page and thanks for taking the time to research my background and qualifications for the State Representative for District 19A, one of the most diverse districts in our state.
This campaign is run on the same foundational principles I have run my life and my business: traditional values, common sense and good stewardship.
My professional background includes over three decades of experience as a CPA and Financial Planner.
I have also spent the better part of a decade working as an Adjunct College Professor.
Some of the top things on my list of objectives: 1.Keeping Idaho Idaho.
My kids and grandchildren live here and I want to make sure the Idaho way of life is maintained.
As a family we love the outdoors and I will work to make sure that is not compromised.
2.Our state has struggled with keeping a balanced budget and that task will not get easier with time.
With my background as a CPA/Financial Planner and College Professor I understand how to create a budget, how to analyze a budget and how to hold the people or organizations accountable when a budget does not work.
We are in desperate need of short-term as well as long-term planning in this area to make sure we are fiscally responsible.
3.As a college professor I understand the importance of education and the need for Idahoans to be competitive in whatever field they pursue.
Allocating resources to provide economically viable options to make sure that education suits the needs of the individual and as educators we are delivering students who are prepared to meet ever changing needs with skills that are relevant and current.
