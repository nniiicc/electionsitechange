In the Capitol In the 45th In uniform Adam Moore is proud to serve as the state representative for Kentucky’s 45th House District, bringing energy and focus to the job of moving our commonwealth forward.
Born and raised in Frankfort, Adam’s path has taken him from public and military service to small business ownership and community leadership.
After graduating from Murray State University, Adam worked for Kentucky legislators in Washington, D.C., before enlisting in the U.S.
Army Reserve.
His deployments to Africa and Afghanistan shaped his belief that servant leaders must step up and deliver for their communities.
When he returned home, Adam helped modernize technology systems in military hospitals as a project manager and built a small coaching business, helping athletes reach their health and fitness goals.
Today, Adam and his wife, Andrea, are raising their son, Remy, in Lexington’s Dogwood Trace neighborhood.
They want Kentucky to be a place where every family has the same opportunities to thrive.
That’s why Adam fights for hardworking families, strong public schools, affordable healthcare, and protecting the personal freedoms Kentuckians value.
Through his role in the legislature and as a member of Lexington’s Commission on Veterans Affairs, Adam stays focused on real issues—always working to build a stronger, more prosperous Kentucky for everyone.

Early voting access for caregivers Expanded absentee and early voting access for Kentuckians caring for a family member.
