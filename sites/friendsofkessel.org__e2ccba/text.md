A LIFETIME OF SERVICE TO NORTH DAKOTA
MEET SHAWN
Shawn Kessel is a proven leader with nearly 25 years of experience strengthening North Dakota communities.
A small business owner with experience in both the public and private sectors, Shawn built a career around service, problem-solving, and steady leadership.
Born in Dickinson, ND, and a graduate of Carrington High School, he spent much of his career in economic development in the cities of Dickinson and Wahpeton, as well as in the North Dakota Department of Commerce, before joining ROERS as Director of Business Development in Fargo where he and his wife Lori reside.
A person of faith and strong North Dakota values, Shawn is committed to helping families and communities thrive.
He is running for the North Dakota House because District 27 deserves a representative who listens, works hard, and delivers real results.
Shawn's Priorities
Shawn is running because District 27 deserves representation rooted in experience, integrity, and North Dakota values.
Because of his extensive service in North Dakota communities and the public and private sector, Shawn won’t need a long learning curve—he’ll contribute effectively from day one to advance priorities that strengthen North Dakota and District 27.
Advancing a diversified economy that creates opportunities for families and businesses, strengthens communities, and supports smart investments that reduce costs and ease the burden on individual taxpayers.
Leading strategic growth with practical solutions that lower the cost of living, support affordable housing and childcare, and help families move into District 27, put down roots, and contribute to strong, vibrant communities.
Putting people over politics by leading with North Dakota values, supporting individual liberties, listening with respect, and staying focused on delivering real results for District 27.
