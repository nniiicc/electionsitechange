About Grayson
Grayson Stone is a proud husband, devoted father, and a lifelong member of the Twin Falls community.
Raised in the Magic Valley, Grayson understands the values that make this region special — hard work, integrity, and neighbors helping neighbors.
His deep roots in the community have shaped his commitment to public service and his passion for building a stronger future for Idaho families.
Throughout his career, Grayson has worked closely with local entrepreneurs and business owners, helping support and grow businesses that create jobs and strengthen the local economy.
He believes that small businesses are the backbone of the Magic Valley, and he has spent years advocating for policies that allow them to succeed.
Grayson’s dedication to service led him to serve on the Twin Falls City Council, where he worked alongside community leaders to address the real needs of residents.
During his time on the council, he focused on responsible growth, supporting local businesses, improving infrastructure, and ensuring that Twin Falls remains a safe and welcoming place to live and raise a family.
As a leader, Grayson believes in listening first and working together to find practical solutions.
Rather than focusing on political division, he is committed to bringing people together to solve problems and create opportunities for the next generation.
In Boise, Grayson Stone will continue to put the people of the Magic Valley first.
His focus will be on protecting Idaho’s strong economy, supporting families, and ensuring that small businesses have the opportunity to grow and thrive.
Grayson believes that with the right leadership and common-sense solutions, Idaho can continue to be one of the best places in the country to live, work, and raise a family.
For Grayson, public service is about more than policy — it’s about community.
And he remains committed to working every day to ensure that the values and opportunities that make the Magic Valley special are protected for generations to come.
