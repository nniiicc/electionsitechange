KEEP Act · NEST Act · HOME Act · PAVE Act · PRICE Act STAY Act · SMALL Act · CHANCE Act · TRIM Act · PRUNE Act SHIELD Act · RISE Act · STOP Act · EQUAL Act · ROOTS Act BARE Act · TRUST Act · DRAW Act THE RISE ACT The RISE Act — Reintegration Into Society and Employment Second Chances — Put Prisoners to Work The goal of incarceration shouldn't just be punishment — it should be preparing people to come home and stay home.
Reive's initiative would create opportunities for incarcerated individuals to take on virtual jobs — real, skills-based work they can do from within the facility — and earn real wages deposited into a personal account.
When they walk out the door, they walk out with money in their pocket, job experience on their résumé, and a real shot at building a life.
No more being dropped on the street with nothing, but instead rewarding people for choosing to contribute back to society.
To help offset the cost to taxpayers, participants would contribute a small portion of their earnings — either $# or #% per day, whichever is greater — back to the facility or directly toward restitution for their victims.
Less recidivism.
More accountability.
Better outcomes for everyone.
