THE KEEP ACT
The KEEP Act — Keep Every Earned Penny
No Tax on Tips & Overtime — Make It Permanent
Wisconsin workers who earn tips and put in overtime hours deserve to keep more of what they earn.
Reive fully supports getting a tips and overtime tax exemption across the finish line in Wisconsin — and will be a strong vote to make it happen.
Reive's addition to the conversation is simple common sense: let's make it permanent.
A relief measure with an expiration date is a good start, but workers shouldn't have to wonder every few years whether they still get to keep what they earned.
Reive will advocate for removing any sunset clause so this becomes lasting law — not temporary relief that expires before it can make a real difference.
- Primary Goal — Pass a tips and overtime tax exemption into Wisconsin law
- Secondary Goal — Make it permanent with no sunset clause
- Stretch Goal — Push for full exemption of all overtime earnings at the 1.5x rate, not just the premium half
It's your money, and you know how to spend it better than the government.
