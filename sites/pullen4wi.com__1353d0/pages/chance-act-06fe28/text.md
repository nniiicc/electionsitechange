KEEP Act · NEST Act · HOME Act · PAVE Act · PRICE Act STAY Act · SMALL Act · CHANCE Act · TRIM Act · PRUNE Act SHIELD Act · RISE Act · STOP Act · EQUAL Act · ROOTS Act BARE Act · TRUST Act · DRAW Act THE CHANCE ACT The CHANCE Act — Creating Hire Avenues for New Careers and Employment Hire Local, Hire Heroes Wisconsin's businesses are the backbone of our communities — and some of our most deserving neighbors are being left behind.
Reive believes we can solve both problems at once by rewarding employers who take a chance on the people who need it most.
Veterans Hiring Tax Credit — Businesses that hire a veteran receive a meaningful state tax credit.
Veterans bring discipline, leadership, and real-world experience to any workplace.
Homeless Hiring Tax Credit — Businesses that hire someone experiencing homelessness receive an even larger tax credit.
A job is the single most effective path out of homelessness — more reliable than a shelter bed and more dignified than a government check.
Credits are earned over time — rewarding genuine retention, not just hiring: 2 Weeks Initial portion 90 Days Second portion 6 Months Third portion 12 Months Fourth portion 3 Years Bonus credit — long-term investment rewarded The 3-year bonus exists to prevent employers from recycling hires just to restart the credit clock.
The goal isn't a revolving door — it's a career.
