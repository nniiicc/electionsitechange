About Reive Reive Pullen is a United States Marine, Eagle Scout, and lifelong Wisconsinite running for the 52nd Assembly District.
He served in the Marine Corps Reserve with Fox Company, 2nd Battalion, 24th Marines out of Milwaukee, WI.
During his time in the reserves, Reive balanced military training and service with his education and career in public safety.
He earned his Associate Degree in Criminal Justice from Fox Valley Technical College while working in law enforcement as a Community Service Officer with the Appleton Police Department and later as a 911 Dispatcher for the Shawano County Sheriff's Office.
These experiences taught him how to stay calm under pressure, put service before self, and remain committed to protecting his community.
Reive’s dedication to service began before the Marines.
As an Eagle Scout, he learned the importance of leadership, integrity, and giving back to others.
Those values have guided him throughout his life and career.
Beyond his military and public safety service, Reive has built up a small business in the Fox Valley, operating a food, beverage, and coffee convenience service that supports local workplaces.
As a small business owner, he understands the challenges of navigating regulations, managing costs, and building something that serves others while supporting a family.
His experiences in the Marines, law enforcement, and entrepreneurship have shaped his belief in accountability, responsibility, and the importance of leadership that puts people first.
Reive is also active in civic life.
He is a member of the American Legion, where he continues to stand with and advocate for fellow veterans, and Appleton Concerned Taxpayers, where he advocates for fiscal responsibility and transparency in government.
He regularly takes part in community events, supports local causes, and is deeply invested in the future of Appleton and the surrounding Fox Valley.
Reive lives in Appleton with his wife and their dogs and cat.
Together, they are proud to call the 52nd District home and are committed to building their family’s future in the same community they love.
Campaign Focus Our campaign is built on Service, Business, and Results .
Reive is committed to putting neighbors first and ensuring government works for the people of Wisconsin’s 52nd Assembly District.
Key priorities include: Strong Schools: Support public education and career training so every student is prepared for success.
Lowering Costs for Families: Tackle rising expenses — from groceries and gas to healthcare — so families can keep more of what they earn.
Local Businesses: Cut red tape, reduce unnecessary taxes and fees, and create an environment where small businesses can grow and create jobs.
Fiscal Responsibility: Ensure taxpayer dollars are spent wisely, with a focus on efficiency and transparency in government.
Public Safety: Stand with law enforcement, first responders, and community safety initiatives to keep our neighborhoods secure — with a strong emphasis on protecting the vulnerable and ensuring child predators face full accountability.
Clean Air & Water: Keep our air clean and our water safe—now and for the next generation.
Veterans & Families: Advocate for veterans, military families, and community organizations that strengthen our district.
At its core, this campaign is about listening to neighbors, serving with integrity, and building a future where families and businesses can thrive in the Fox Valley .
