The Issues
Law and Order
• Increase support and funding for local police departments and Arizona DPS.
• Help President Trump and law enforcement to secure our border, effectively stopping the flow of illegal immigrants and lethal drugs into our state.
TAXES AND SPENDING
• Keep income taxes low to keep Arizona a small business-friendly environment.
• Cut wasteful programs to ensure funds are used appropriately and responsibly.
• Support future Governor Any Biggs’ goal to eliminate income taxes.
• Fight for property tax reform.
CHAMPION AMERICAN FREEDOM
• Pro 2nd Amendment: Shall Not Be Infringed.
• Pro-life, from conception until death.
• Pro election integrity.
• Pro free speech.
PROTECT OUR WATER
• Ensure all citizens have immediate access to clean, affordable water.
• Support long-term water agreements that ensure reliable water access, especially for rural and small communities.
• Invest in water reuse and conservation efforts.
ROADWAYS AND TRANSPORTATION
• Prioritize funding the repaving and repairing of worn-down roads.
• Invest in better signage, lighting, and the widening of lanes where needed.
EDUCATION
• Fight for school choice for all Arizonans.
• Improve literacy rates and educational opportunities for students without lowering statewide standards.
• Ensure schools have access to School Resource Officers and other safety procedures.
