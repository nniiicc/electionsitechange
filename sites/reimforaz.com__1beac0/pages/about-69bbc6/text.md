Cody Reim has called Arizona home for thirteen years.
He chose to invest his life into this state, starting his small business and having three of his four children in the district.
When his community was faced with a water crisis that forced his family to drastically cut down water usage- even being unable to take showers at home or clean their dishes- he stepped up, leading the public awareness effort.
In March 2026, Cody was appointed State Representative by the Maricopa County Board of Supervisors.
He would be honored to serve his community by continuing to be your State Representative.

For far too long, the Democratic Party has gotten away with taking advantage of everyday Americans.
If they get their way next November, they will pass legislation that will bring California policies to Arizona.
With Congressman Andy Biggs serving as our Governor, I hope to be a part of a Republican Party that isn’t complicit to Democrats, but plays offense against them.
