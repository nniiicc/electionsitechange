She believes in the South Dakota values that bring people together — hard work, personal responsibility, community, and respect for one another.
Her focus is on practical leadership that reflects the priorities of local residents, supports responsible economic opportunity, and preserves the freedoms that make South Dakota a great place to call home.
Families First Protecting Parental Rights Ensuring South Dakota children are raised with strong values, and that families—not government—make decisions about their upbringing and education.
Rural Economy Defending Small Businesses Pushing back against government overreach and burdensome regulations that threaten the backbone of our local economy.
South Dakota Values Fighting For Property Tax Reform Making homeownership attainable for young families and ensuring seniors aren’t taxed out of the homes they worked their whole lives to build.
Community Involvement Keeping SD Safe and Prosperous Supporting local law enforcement, fostering a business-friendly environment and keeping taxes low.
