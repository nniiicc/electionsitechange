A Vision For District 10
Kim’s campaign is centered on protecting individual freedoms, strengthening families, and ensuring the people of District 10 have a steady, thoughtful voice in Pierre.
She believes in the South Dakota values that bring people together — hard work, personal responsibility, community, and respect for one another.
Her focus is on practical leadership that reflects the priorities of local residents, supports responsible economic opportunity, and preserves the freedoms that make South Dakota a great place to call home.
Protecting Parental Rights
Ensuring South Dakota children are raised with strong values, and that families—not government—make decisions about their upbringing and education.
Defending Small Businesses
Pushing back against government overreach and burdensome regulations that threaten the backbone of our local economy.
Fighting For Property Tax Reform
Making homeownership attainable for young families and ensuring seniors aren’t taxed out of the homes they worked their whole lives to build.
Keeping SD Safe and Prosperous
Supporting local law enforcement, fostering a business-friendly environment and keeping taxes low.
