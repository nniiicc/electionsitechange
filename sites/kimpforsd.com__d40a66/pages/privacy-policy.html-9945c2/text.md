Privacy & Data Protection
Policy
Kim Petterson for South Dakota is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded.
This policy outlines how we handle your data when you interact with our website and campaign.
Effective Date: 04/13/2026
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you fill out forms, sign up for updates, make contributions, or contact the campaign.
This may include your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, and any additional information you choose to provide.
We may also automatically collect non-personal information such as browser type, device information, pages visited, and general usage data.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data
We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
How We Use Your Information
Information collected may be used to communicate with you, provide campaign updates, coordinate volunteer opportunities, process contributions, improve website performance, and comply with applicable laws and campaign regulations.
Text Messaging & TCPA Compliance
By providing your mobile number, you consent to receive recurring automated campaign messages, including updates, alerts, and volunteer opportunities.
Message frequency may vary.
- Message and data rates may apply
- You may opt out at any time by replying “STOP”
- Reply “HELP” for assistance
- Consent is not a condition of purchase or donation
Email Communications
By submitting your email, you agree to receive periodic campaign communications.
You may unsubscribe at any time using the link provided in our emails.
Donations & Legal Compliance
Contributions made through third-party platforms are subject to applicable campaign finance laws.
Information provided during the donation process may be used for compliance, reporting, and recordkeeping as required by law.
We do not store or process payment information directly on this website.
Sharing of Information
We do not sell or rent your personal information.
Information may be shared with trusted vendors and service providers who assist in campaign operations, or when required by law.
Cookies & Analytics
This website may use cookies and third-party tools such as analytics services to better understand user behavior, improve performance, and enhance user experience.
You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.
Data Security
We take reasonable precautions to protect your information.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Your Rights & Choices
You may opt out of communications at any time, request updates or corrections to your information, or ask for your data to be removed where applicable.
Third-Party Links
Our website may contain links to third-party platforms.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those external sites.
Updates to This Policy
This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.
Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
