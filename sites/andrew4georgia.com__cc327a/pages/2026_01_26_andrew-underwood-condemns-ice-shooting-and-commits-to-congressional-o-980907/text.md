Andrew Underwood Condemns ICE Shooting and Commits to Congressional Oversight of DHS
Rock Spring, GA. — Today, Libertarian Andrew Underwood declared ICE’s shooting of 37-year-old ICU nurse Alex Pretti as an inexcusable loss of life.
Underwood stands firm that Congress must hold DHS accountable for its justification on every shooting.
If elected, Underwood will support legislation for further ICE transparency and congressional oversight to ensure safety.
