Andrew Underwood Signs Pledge From U.S.
Term Limits Organization
Rock Spring, GA.— Andrew Underwood has officially signed his pledge to co-sponsor and vote for the U.S.
Term Limits Amendment of three two-year House terms and two six-year Senate terms.
He joins a small list of candidates in this election that are fully committed to keeping Congress accountable.
U.S.
Term Limits is a nonpartisan non-profit advocating for constitutional term-limits on federal lawmakers.
Attached is Andrew Underwood’s pledge, illustrating his commitment to structural change in the federal office.
