CONFIRMED: Libertarian Party of Georgia Selects Andrew Underwood as its Candidate for US House GA-14 Election
ROCK SPRING, Ga. — Today, the Libertarian Party of Georgia Executive Committee voted UNANIMOUSLY to endorse and select Andrew Underwood as its candidate for the special election to fill the unexpired term of Marjorie Taylor Greene, Congresswoman of Georgia Congressional District 14 in Northwest Georgia.
“I’m thankful for the Libertarian Party of Georgia’s endorsement.
With their help, I hope to reach out to many voters to provide a third option that is focused on productive conversation, actual liberty, and accomplishing freedom in our lifetime.
It’s time we tell Washington, D.C. that we’re hungry for liberty, and I’m here to serve it up for these voters.
— Andrew Underwood
As the party’s selected nominee, Underwood unlocks a number of resources and donors that will allow him to stand out from the Republican’s oversaturated field.
Today’s message is clear: Andrew is not a candidate in-name-only like others in this race.
