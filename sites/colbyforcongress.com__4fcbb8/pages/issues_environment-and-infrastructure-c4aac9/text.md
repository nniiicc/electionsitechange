Honor their service not only with words, but with action.
Our nation has a responsibility to keep the promises it makes to those who serve.
That means maintaining a strong and accountable VA, ensuring veterans can easily access the benefits they have earned, and treating every veteran with the dignity, respect, and gratitude they deserve.
Cut red tape and reduce delays at the VA.
Expand access to in-home and community-based care so veterans can receive care closer to home.
Improve mental health care, PTSD treatment, and suicide prevention services.
Fully honor the PACT Act and ensure veterans exposed to toxic substances receive the care and benefits they have earned.
Strengthen job training and career transition programs for veterans.
