Education & Strong Schools
Fund strong local public schools.
Pay teachers what the job is worth.
Keep education decisions with parents and local communities, not bureaucrats in Washington.
Ban social media companies from collecting data on children under 18.
Expand career and technical education, apprenticeships, and the skilled trades.
Not every student needs a four-year degree to build a good life.
Fully fund special education.
Strengthen Title I support for high-need schools.
Education is one of the greatest investments we can make in our future.
The strength of tomorrow depends on the education we provide our children today.
As someone who was homeschooled, I strongly support parents' right to choose the educational path they believe is best for their family.
At the same time, our public school teachers deserve better pay and greater support.
Every child deserves a quality education and a real opportunity to succeed.
