Economy
An honest day's work should be enough to build a good life.
Today, too many Americans are working as hard as ever and finding it harder to achieve the American Dream.
As economic power continues to consolidate into fewer hands, competition shrinks, small businesses struggle, and working families lose leverage.
I believe a strong economy is built from the ground up, with small businesses as its backbone.
We need fair markets where hard work, innovation, and entrepreneurship are rewarded, not monopolies that stifle competition and opportunity.
It's time to put our focus back on the real economy, where people build businesses, grow food, make things, and create something that lasts.
Cut the red tape that strangles small businesses and the skilled trades.
Stop punishing the people who build, make, and fix things.
Support American manufacturing and keep good-paying jobs here at home.
Support the small businesses that Main Street runs on.
Fund apprenticeships, trade schools, and job training.
Reward skilled work with wages people can live on.

Lower prescription drug costs.
Improve health outcomes, not just healthcare spending.
Cut unnecessary administrative costs and bureaucracy.

Keep education decisions with parents and local communities, not bureaucrats in Washington.

Not every student needs a four-year degree to build a good life.
Fully fund special education.
Strengthen Title I support for high-need schools.

Secure the border.
The chaos and fentanyl flow through a broken system.
Then make immigration fair.
Secure our southern border with modern technology, infrastructure, and adequate staffing.
Strengthen enforcement against drug trafficking, human trafficking, and criminal organizations.
Protect the dignity and human rights of every person while enforcing our nation's laws.
Modernize and speed up the legal immigration system.
Hold employers accountable for knowingly exploiting undocumented labor.
Provide efficient case processing so asylum claims and immigration cases are resolved quickly.
Focus enforcement resources on violent criminals and genuine public safety threats.
Work with local communities to develop practical immigration solutions rather than political theater.

Freedoms
Our Constitution exists to protect the freedoms of the American people, and I will always work to defend those rights.
That includes protecting the First Amendment, the freedom of speech, freedom of religion, the right to peacefully assemble, and the right to petition the government.
I also support and will defend the Second Amendment and the constitutional right of law-abiding Americans to keep and bear arms.
The rights and liberties guaranteed by our Constitution belong to every American, regardless of who they are, what they believe, or who they love.
It is not the government's job to tell law-abiding Americans how they should live their lives.
Government exists to protect our constitutional rights and liberties, not to take them away.
As your representative, I will work to uphold those freedoms equally and defend the constitutional rights of every American.
