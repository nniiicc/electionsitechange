Colby's Core Causes
Homeownership remains a cornerstone of the American dream and one of the surest paths to long-term financial stability and generational wealth.
It's time to bring that dream back within reach.
Healthcare should be about healing people, not maximizing profits.
No American should have to choose between getting the care they need and facing financial ruin.
Families shouldn't have to rely on GoFundMe to pay for medical treatment.
Most Americans recognize that big money has gained too much influence over our political system.
And we've all watched people in positions of power get away with things that the average American would be held accountable for.
We can restore trust in our government.
I believe in fair, incentive-based markets that reward hard work, innovation, and entrepreneurship.
But markets only work when there's plenty of opportunity and an honest day's work gives people a real chance to get ahead.
