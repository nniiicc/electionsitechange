Healthcare and Community Safety
Enacted laws I have authored and/or co-sponsored:
- Coverage of PTSD treatment for first responders
- Coverage of hearing aids for children who are deaf or hard of hearing
- Restoration of dental care to Medicaid
- Improved funding for emergency medical services
- Regulation to prevent vape sales to minors
- Reform of Idaho’s civil asset forfeiture law to better protect civil liberties
- Medicaid expansion
- Felony penalty for animal torture
