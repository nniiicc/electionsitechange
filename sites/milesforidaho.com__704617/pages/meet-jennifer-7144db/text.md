Jennifer's life and work in Southeast Idaho have shaped a practical, people-first approach to public service.
Here is the story of how she got here.
Southeast Idaho Roots Southeast Idaho Roots I grew up in Southeast Idaho, and this is home.
I care deeply about the families, schools, businesses, and communities that make this part of Idaho special.
I want the next generation to be able to build a life here, raise a family here, and find the education, work, healthcare, and opportunity they need to thrive.
Education Education Jennifer studied political science and social work at Idaho State University and later earned a Master of Social Work from Boise State University.
Her education and professional career have given her experience with both public policy and the real-life systems families depend on.
A Career Helping People A Career Helping People Working in behavioral health gave me the opportunity to work with people from many different backgrounds in our community.
I have seen firsthand how substance use, mental illness, poverty, intergenerational trauma, divorce, abuse, and other challenges affect families.
I have also seen programs such as drug court and mental-health court help people overcome barriers, strengthen families, and improve community safety.
Small-Business Experience Small-Business Experience Owning a small business has reinforced my belief that government should be limited, efficient, and focused on what it truly needs to do.
Small businesses need room to grow without unnecessary red tape, delays, and costs.
Why I Decided to Run Why I Decided to Run I believe government works best when reasonable people are willing to listen, work together, and focus on practical solutions.
I am running to help make District 29 a place where families feel safe, children have opportunities, businesses can grow, and our communities can thrive.
