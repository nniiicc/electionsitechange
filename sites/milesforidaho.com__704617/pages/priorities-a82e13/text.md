Parents should have choices in how their children are educated, and public schools must remain a priority.
Why It Matters to District 29 District 29 families depend on strong local schools, Idaho State University, and workforce training.
Programs like Idaho Launch have helped young Idahoans gain skills and stay here — and should be protected.
What I'll Work On Supporting schools, universities, and educators; protecting workforce training like Idaho Launch; and keeping parents involved in their children's education.
Priority Affordability & Responsible Taxes What I Believe Financial stability has always been a priority for Idaho.
Responsible tax policy protects taxpayers while ensuring the state can continue providing essential services.
Why It Matters to District 29 Affordability is a real concern for District 29 families.
Housing, groceries, and healthcare all cost more, and families feel it every month.
What I'll Work On Holding the line on taxes, looking for responsible relief, and making sure growth pays its own way so property taxpayers aren't left carrying the cost.
Priority Right-to-Work & Workers What I Believe Idaho's right-to-work law protects a worker's freedom to choose whether to join a union.
Workers and employers both do best when government stays out of the way.
Why It Matters to District 29 District 29 has a strong, hardworking community.
Protecting worker choice and a healthy job market keeps families rooted here.
What I'll Work On Defending right-to-work and supporting policies that grow good jobs and protect Idaho workers.
Priority Healthcare & Medicaid What I Believe Healthcare costs continue to rise, creating challenges for families and vulnerable populations.
Idaho must keep identifying reasonable solutions that ensure people can access the care they need while maintaining financial stability.
Why It Matters to District 29 Access to care — especially in rural Southeast Idaho — and support for a growing retirement population matter deeply in District 29.
What I'll Work On Supporting healthcare access, appropriate benefits for seniors, and responsible Medicaid policy that protects both patients and taxpayers.
Priority Public Safety & Behavioral Health Professional Insight Jennifer has worked directly in behavioral health and treatment courts — one of her strongest areas of professional expertise.
What I Believe Safe communities are essential to Idaho families.
Law enforcement officers, firefighters, and EMS personnel must be supported by strong community resources — crisis response teams, behavioral health services, court services, home-based services, and medical services.
Why It Matters to District 29 Jennifer has worked inside these systems.
In behavioral health, drug court, and mental-health court she has seen firsthand how treatment helps people overcome barriers, strengthen families, and improve community safety.
This is one of her strongest areas of professional expertise.
What I'll Work On Strengthening behavioral health and crisis response, supporting treatment courts, and making sure first responders have the tools they need to keep District 29 safe.
Priority Housing Affordability What I Believe Families need homes they can afford.
A healthy housing market and responsible planning help the next generation put down roots here.
Why It Matters to District 29 Growth has pushed housing costs up across District 29, and young families and seniors both feel the pressure.
What I'll Work On Supporting responsible growth, reducing unnecessary barriers to building, and keeping housing within reach for working families.
Priority Small Business & Economic Opportunity What I Believe Idaho values small businesses and the role they play in our communities.
Limiting unnecessary government overreach and using zero-based regulatory approaches helps businesses operate more efficiently, reduce costs, and grow.
Why It Matters to District 29 Jennifer owns a small business.
She understands how regulation, workforce availability, and costs affect employers right here in District 29.
What I'll Work On Cutting red tape, supporting small-business opportunity, and keeping local economies strong.
Priority Local Control What I Believe Government works best when decisions are made close to home.
Local communities know their own needs better than distant regulators do.
Why It Matters to District 29 District 29 communities should shape their own future rather than receive one-size-fits-all mandates from outside.
What I'll Work On Protecting local decision-making and pushing back on unnecessary state overreach.
Priority Water, Agriculture & Growth What I Believe Agriculture is a major contributor to Idaho's economy.
Protecting water resources and aquifers is critical to keeping agriculture strong for future generations.
Why It Matters to District 29 Water is the lifeblood of Southeast Idaho farms and communities.
Responsible planning for growth, infrastructure, and water matters here every day.
What I'll Work On Protecting water and aquifers, supporting agriculture, and planning responsibly for growth so costs don't fall on property taxpayers.
Priority Second Amendment & Local Decision-Making What I Believe Jennifer supports the Second Amendment and the rights of law-abiding Idahoans.
Why It Matters to District 29 Responsible gun ownership is part of Idaho life and culture.
What I'll Work On Protecting Second Amendment rights while respecting local decision-making.
Priority Public Lands What I Believe Idaho's public lands must remain in public hands — for hunting, fishing, hiking, camping, boating, skiing, and the wildlife that depend on them.
Why It Matters to District 29 Public lands and outdoor access are part of what makes Southeast Idaho home.
What I'll Work On Keeping public lands public, protecting wildlife habitat, and defending access to the outdoor way of life Idahoans value.
Priority Energy What I Believe Energy policy should support reliable, affordable power for Idaho families, farms, and businesses.
Why It Matters to District 29 Energy costs affect every family and business in District 29, and reliable power matters for rural communities.
What I'll Work On Supporting reliable, affordable energy and policies that keep costs stable while meeting Idaho's growing needs.
