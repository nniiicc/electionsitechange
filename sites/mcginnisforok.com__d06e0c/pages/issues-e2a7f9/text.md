Oklahoma can do better; it's time we look at evidence-based solutions to improve child outcomes and make Oklahoma a destination for teaching.
Let's give our students more options through programs like 4-H and FFA or trade programs that inspire the next generation.
Expand Mental Health Care It doesn't take a policy expert to tell you we need more Mental Healthcare options in Oklahoma.
I'm running to ensure that anyone, no matter who or where they are, can get the help they need.
Mental Healthcare is so important for keeping our communities and children safer.
Criminal Justice Reform Meredith McGinnis believes Oklahoma’s justice system should focus on safety, fairness, and second chances, not just punishment.
Punishment alone doesn’t make communities safer; accountability paired with rehabilitation does .
Protect Women and Children Meredith McGinnis believes every woman and child deserves to feel safe, heard, and protected.
She will fight to strengthen protections for survivors of domestic violence and sexual assault, ensure their voices are trusted, and expand access to critical support services.
