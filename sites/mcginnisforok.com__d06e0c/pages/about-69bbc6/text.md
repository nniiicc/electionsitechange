Meredith McGinnis is an attorney, community leader, and advocate dedicated to building a stronger, more connected Oklahoma.
Raised in Oklahoma, Meredith earned her Juris Doctor from the University of Oklahoma College of Law, where she served as President of the American Civil Liberties Union chapter and was recognized with the Justice Grimes Civil Libertarian Award.
She also holds a Bachelor of Arts from the University of Oklahoma, graduating with honors and participating in the N.E.W.
Leadership Program, which focuses on increasing women’s involvement in public service.
Meredith currently serves as Senior Corporate Counsel and Commercial Real Estate Advisor for a regional real estate company, where she leads litigation, negotiates complex contracts, and helps manage housing across Oklahoma and the surrounding region.
With over a decade of experience in law, she has handled everything from employment disputes to housing issues, giving her a deep, practical understanding of how policy impacts everyday Oklahomans.
She's ready to fight for us.
Meredith is a board member of Criminal Justice and Mercy Ministries, where she supports justice-involved individuals transitioning back into their communities.
She volunteers at Mabel Bassett Correctional Center, leads programming for children with incarcerated parents, and received the Oklahoma City Public Schools Foundation Volunteer of the Year Award for her organization in a high school food pantry She is also a boxing coach and competitor, using fitness as a tool to teach discipline, resilience, and emotional strength through her Boxing Empowerment and Emotional Management (BEEM) program.
Family First, Family Focused.
At the end of the day, Meredith’s most important job is being a mom to her three kids.
Whether it’s school drop-offs, busy evenings, or cheering them on in their activities, she’s living the same daily life as so many Oklahoma families.
That’s what grounds her.
It’s why she cares so deeply about strong schools, safe communities, and making sure every child has the support they need to thrive.
Meredith believes in a strong work ethic and is raising her boys to work alongside her on their farm, where they raise and breed primarily heritage-breed chickens and ducks.
For Meredith, this campaign is about building a better future for her kids and yours.
