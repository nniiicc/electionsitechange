Garcia Endorses Morales
United States Congresswoman of the 29th District endorsed Christina Morales for Texas House District 145.
The Congresswoman made her decision based on Morales's long history of community service leadership.
I know what kind of leader the hard-working people of this District deserve.
That is why I am endorsing my friend Democrat, Christina Morales, to continue the legacy of strong leadership in House District 145. -- U.S.

Sylvia Garcia
