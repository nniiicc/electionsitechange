All Posts News Search Morales Announces Campaign Team Morales for District 145 Dec 29, 2018 1 min read Christina Morales Announces Campaign Team for HD 145 Special Election Saturday, December 29, 2018 For Immediate Release --- Houston, TX – Third generation East End business owner, Christina Morales announces the campaign staff that will lead her campaign for House District 145.
Morales set out to assemble a staff that shared her desire to run a grassroots campaign focused on direct voter contact.
To construct and oversee the plan she has brought on General Consultant Jaime Mercado, and Lead Strategist Marc Campos.
The two local campaigners recently teamed up to lead Senator Carol Alvarado’s decisive victory.
To implement the plan, Linh Nguyen will lead the campaign as Campaign Manager.
Nguyen most recently served as the field director for Senator Alvarado’s team and was a Special Projects Director for the Beto O’Rourke campaign.
Nguyen brings over a decade of campaign experience from local races up through Presidential campaigns.
“I grew up in this community and this community raised my family for three generations, said Morales.
I wanted a team that knew the district the way I know the district and would be capable of delivering my messages to my community.
I plan to lead this team by demonstrating the work ethic I will expect from them.” ### Christina Morales is a third generation East End business owner and is a product of the Texas public school system.
Growing up, she was taught to return the generosity her community had shown her family.
Now she wants to continue the strong leadership District 145 enjoyed from another local girl that came up fighting for her neighborhood.
