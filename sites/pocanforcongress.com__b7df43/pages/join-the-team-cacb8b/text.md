The main responsibility of the role is the assembly, delivery, and installation of yard signs across Wisconsin’s 2nd Congressional District, which includes the counties of Dane, Green, Rock, Sauk, Iowa, and Lafayette.
Additional responsibilities may include reaching out to community members and campaign supporters to identify barn sign (4’x4’) and yard sign (2’x2’) locations and organize yard sign volunteers and calls.
Applicants must be able to begin immediately and work 30-40 hours per week through August 11th , with the potential to stay on through the fall.
Hours and schedule likely to be flexible after August 11th.
Compensation is $20.00 per hour.
A valid driver’s license is required.
Staging location for yard sign assembly and pick up will be in Madison.
Access to stable transportation not required but a plus.
Prior campaign experience is not a requirement.
