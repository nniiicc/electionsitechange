Ayanna Davis grew up in a single-parent home in Compton, California.
A mother and lifelong public servant, she has dedicated her life to lifting up our children and the next generation.
Davis previously served as a local classroom teacher and union member, as well as worked in early education as a public school principal at the Los Angeles Unified School District.
Now, Ayanna is running for Assembly District 65 to build more inclusive and just neighborhoods across our communities.
