AYANNA'S PRIORITIES
Expanding Healthcare Access
I believe healthcare is a fundamental right, not a privilege.
When I was in college, I lost my healthcare coverage prior to graduation.
To this day, too many Californians – especially those in underserved communities – face significant barriers to accessing timely, affordable, and high-quality healthcare.
In the Assembly, I will always fight to ensure every Californian can access the care they need without going into debt.
