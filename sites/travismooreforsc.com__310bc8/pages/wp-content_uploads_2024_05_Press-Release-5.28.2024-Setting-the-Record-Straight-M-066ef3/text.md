Setting the Record Straight: Campaign Ad Misrepresents Stellar Pro-Life Record Of Representative Travis Moore The following statement may be attributed to Lisa Van Riper, President, and Holly Gatling Executive Director.
COLUMBIA, S.C. – (Monday, May 28, 2024) A reprehensible attack which completely mischaracterizes the stellar pro-life record of Representative Travis Moore has come to the attention of South Carolina Citizens for Life.
We must set the record straight.
Travis Moore is 100 percent pro-life.
Representative Moore has a 100 percent pro-life voting record.
He returned a 100 percent pro-life survey to South Carolina Citizens for Life in which he upholds the SCCL position on every crucial pro-life issue that the South Carolina House of Representatives has established in the past and will consider in the future now that the U.S.
Supreme Court has overturned the lethal and unjust Roe v.
Wade decision.
In the contentious debate on the Fetal Heartbeat Act, Representative Moore worked hard to pass this law that protects both the unborn members of our human family whose heartbeat can be detected and their mothers.
The group misrepresenting Representative Moore’s pro-life record is an extremist organization that calls for putting women in jail for having an abortion.
The South Carolina Citizens for Life Action PAC has endorsed Representative Travis Moore for re-election to the South Carolina House of Representatives.
The PAC endorsement is based on Representative Moore’s stellar pro-life voting records and his commitment to supporting the lives of the unborn and their mothers.
In fighting for our country’s future generations, we are called to act with love and compassion as we seek fairness, justice, and liberty for unborn children and their mothers.
Representative Moore joins authentic prolife South Carolinians who love them both - the unborn babies and their mothers.
