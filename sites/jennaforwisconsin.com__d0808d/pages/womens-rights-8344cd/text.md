Women’s Rights
As a mother to a daughter and a daughter whose mother and grandmother fought for women’s rights, I am committed to protecting the rights they fought so hard to secure.
The Marquette University Law School poll confirms that the majority of Wisconsinites think abortion should be legal.
Our district deserves a State Senator who reflects those values.
Politicians do not belong in the exam room.
Medical decisions should be made by patients and their doctors, not politicians.
