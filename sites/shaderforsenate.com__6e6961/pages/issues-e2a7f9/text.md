High taxes stifle job creators and small businesses.
By lowering tax rates and offering incentives for equipment upgrades and workforce expansion, we can empower entrepreneurs to drive growth.
02 Invest in Infrastructure and Jobs Building on her creation of California’s first joint City-County infrastructure district in Placentia, which generated 5,000 jobs, Rhonda will support public-private partnerships to fund roads, utilities, and broadband in the 34th District.
These investments will drive economic growth and improve quality of life.
03 Eliminate Waste and Strengthen Oversight Rhonda will fight to root out inefficiency, fraud, and corruption in state agencies.
By implementing stronger oversight and reforming entitlement programs, we can preserve vital services for future generations while ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent wisely.
04 Lower Taxes to Boost Competitiveness High corporate and individual tax rates hinder job creation.
Rhonda will work to overhaul the tax code, reducing rates to a competitive 25%.
This will attract businesses, create jobs, and increase wages for hardworking families across the state.
05 Reduce Regulatory Burdens on Small Businesses Excessive regulations suffocate small businesses.
Rhonda will advocate for streamlined rules that ensure market stability without stifling growth.
By cutting red tape, we can empower local entrepreneurs to innovate, expand, and hire more workers.
Rhonda will support exceptions of benefit requirements for businesses with less than 50 employees.
06 Create the Business Owners of the Future Children are the future of our economy.
Every child deserves a quality education to ensure future employment.
Rhonda will support policies that let education funding follow the student, giving parents more options, including charter schools.
Emphasizing STEM education and parental involvement will equip students for success and give teachers greater flexibility.
07 Streamline State Licensing for Business Growth Cumbersome state licensing and enforcement boards hinder professionals and businesses with slow processes and excessive penalties.
Rhonda will push for comprehensive audits of these boards to boost efficiency, simplify licensing, and curb overzealous enforcement, fostering a business-friendly environment that supports growth and opportunity.
08 Make Healthcare More Affordable Rhonda will promote innovative healthcare solutions, such as allowing small businesses and individuals to pool resources for better insurance rates and purchase plans across state lines.
By reducing frivolous lawsuits, we can lower costs and improve access to care for all.
09 Protect Employers from Frivolous Lawsuits Rhonda will work towards creating a better environment to encourage employment by protecting employers from fraudulent employee claims.
Including PAGA reform and timelines for filing complaints.
10 Support Local Ownership Rhonda Shader will prioritize policies that help local entrepreneurs thrive.
She’ll support reduced permitting fees and revitalization incentives to breathe new life into aging commercial corridors.
By focusing on local ownership, we can keep dollars in the community and preserve the unique character of neighborhoods across the 34th District.
Rhonda's Priorities Sacramento is broken.
As a small business owner, councilwoman, and two-term mayor, I’ve delivered real results at home using fiscal discipline and common sense as my guiding principles.
I will fight relentlessly to make Senate District 34 more affordable, safer, and a better place to do business.
Public Safety Californians deserve to feel safe in their homes, neighborhoods, and businesses, but Sacramento policies have made that harder than ever.
Proposition 47, early release programs, and the dismantling of effective law enforcement tools have emboldened criminals and left communities to suffer the consequences.
Smash-and-grab robberies, retail theft, fentanyl on our streets, and rising violent crime are not inevitable, they are the result of policy choices made by politicians who have never had to live with them.
I will be a fierce advocate for restoring law and order in the 34th District.
I will fight to repeal the most damaging provisions of Prop 47, hold repeat offenders accountable with real consequences, and ensure prosecutors are working for victims, not criminals.
I will support fully funding and staffing our local law enforcement agencies, restore respect for the men and women in uniform who put their lives on the line every day, and oppose any effort to defund or demoralize our police.
Public safety is the foundation of everything else, you can't build a thriving economy, raise a family, or run a small business in a community that doesn't feel safe.
I will make sure Sacramento finally gets that message.
Affordability The affordability crisis didn't happen by accident.
It's the direct result of decades of excessive taxes, fees, and regulations piled onto everyday Californians by a Sacramento majority that has never had to worry about making ends meet.
Families across the 34th District are working harder than ever and falling further behind.
They're paying some of the highest gas prices, energy bills, and income taxes in the nation, and they're watching the dream of homeownership slip further out of reach with every passing year.
I'm going to Sacramento to fight for real relief, not promises, not studies, not task forces.
Real relief.
I will work to roll back the hidden fees and mandates driving up prices at the pump and on every utility bill, so families aren't forced to choose between filling their gas tank and keeping the lights on.
I will be a defender of Proposition 13, protecting the property tax limits that allow families and seniors to stay in the homes they've worked a lifetime to own.
I will push to cut taxes on the essentials, from prescription medicine to overtime pay, so that a hard day's work actually results in a paycheck that goes further.
And I will fight to reduce the state-imposed costs and bureaucratic red tape that make building and buying a home in California so expensive that young families are leaving the state rather than putting down roots here.
California is one of the wealthiest states in the world.
There is no reason its working families should be priced out of it.
I will make affordability a fight, not a talking point.
UNCOVER FRAUD, WASTE & ABUSE California taxpayers are among the most generous in the nation, and Sacramento has repaid them with decades of mismanagement, runaway spending, and outright fraud.
From the billions lost to EDD fraud during the pandemic, to homelessness programs that spend hundreds of thousands of dollars per person with nothing to show for it, to state agencies that can't account for where the money goes, Sacramento has made a habit of treating your tax dollars as an unlimited resource with no strings attached.
I believe that every dollar the government spends belongs to the people first.
As your State Senator, I will push for independent audits of the state's most bloated agencies, demand transparency in how homelessness, healthcare, and social service dollars are allocated, and hold bureaucrats accountable when funds go missing or programs fail to deliver results.
I will fight to strengthen the role of the State Auditor, close loopholes that allow waste to go undetected, and support real consequences, not just strongly worded reports, when fraud is found.
California doesn't have a revenue problem.
It has a spending problem.
I will go to Sacramento and say what too few politicians are willing to: the people of this state deserve to know where their money is going, and they deserve better than excuses.
Support for Small Businesses Small businesses are the backbone of the 34th District, the family-owned restaurants, neighborhood shops, contractors, and entrepreneurs who create jobs, invest in their communities, and keep dollars circulating locally.
But Sacramento has spent years making it harder, not easier, to run a small business in California.
Skyrocketing taxes, endless regulations, frivolous lawsuit exposure, and rising operating costs have driven businesses out of the state and pushed countless owners to the breaking point.
I know this firsthand.
As a small business owner myself, I have navigated the same maze of red tape, licensing burdens, and government overreach that my neighbors face every day.
I’m not learning about these challenges from a briefing book, I’ve lived them.
As State Senator, I will fight to cut taxes and reduce the regulatory burdens that strangle small businesses before they ever have a chance to grow.
I will push for meaningful PAGA reform to protect employers from predatory lawsuits, advocate for streamlined state licensing processes, and support exemptions from costly benefit mandates for businesses with fewer than 50 employees.
I will champion policies that make it easier to open a business, hire local workers, and compete, without Sacramento standing in the way at every turn.
When small businesses thrive, whole communities thrive.
I will make sure the 34th District is a place where local entrepreneurs can plant roots, build something, and succeed.
Homelessness Homelessness is an epidemic in California that we spend billions of dollars on every year with no sign of progress.
Rhonda supports empowering local governments and faith-based organizations to increase shelter capacity, while ensuring more accountability and transparency of state-level homelessness programs.
