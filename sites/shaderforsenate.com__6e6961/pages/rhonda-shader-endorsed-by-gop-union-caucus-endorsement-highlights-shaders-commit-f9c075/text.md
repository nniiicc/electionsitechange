RHONDA SHADER ENDORSED BY GOP UNION CAUCUS Endorsement Highlights Shader’s Commitment to Working Families
Placentia, CA – The GOP Union Caucus announced its endorsement of Rhonda Shader for State Senate District 34 citing her unwavering commitment to working families and her record of supporting policies that improve the lives of everyday Californians.
The endorsement adds to a growing and diverse coalition of support behind Shader’s campaign, reflecting the broad appeal of her pro-worker, pro-family agenda.
Throughout her career in public service, Shader has championed policies that strengthen the middle class, protect jobs, and ensure that hardworking Californians can thrive.
Her approach to governance that is rooted in fiscal responsibility, economic opportunity, and common sense has earned her support from across the political and professional spectrum.
“Rhonda Shader understands that strong families and strong communities are built on good jobs, fair wages, and an economy that works for everyone — not just those at the top.
The GOP Union Caucus is proud to endorse Rhonda Shader and looks forward to supporting her in this race,” said Rudy Melendez, Chairman of the GOP Union Caucus.
The GOP Union Caucus works to advance the interests of union workers within the Republican Party, advocating for policies that protect worker rights, promote job growth, and ensure that organized labor has a seat at the table in policy conversations.
The Caucus endorses candidates who demonstrate a genuine commitment to the concerns of working Americans regardless of party affiliation.

About Rhonda Shader
Rhonda Shader is the former Mayor of Placentia, a small business owner, and a dedicated community leader.
Throughout her career, she has championed policies that promote economic growth, public safety, and government accountability.
Shader is running for Senate District 34 to bring common-sense leadership to Sacramento and fight for policies that make California more affordable and livable for families and businesses.
Senate District 34 is an open seat that includes the cities of Santa Ana, Anaheim, Placentia, Fullerton, Garden Grove, Buena Park, La Habra, the City of Orange, and the unincorporated community of South Whittier.
