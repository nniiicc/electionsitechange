The issues Fiscal Responsibility As your next state senator representing the Brainerd Lakes and Grand Rapids Areas, Keri will work to ensure every tax dollar is spent wisely.
Minnesotans expect accountability and transparency, not bloated budgets and runaway spending.
Keri will fight to rein in wasteful government programs and make sure our hard-earned tax dollars are prioritized for what matters most to our district.
Sanctity of Life Keri Heintzeman believes in the fundamental value of every human life.
As a pro-life advocate, she will defend the rights of the unborn and work to promote a culture that respects life at all stages.
Keri supports compassionate policies that uplift mothers, strengthen families, and provide real support for those facing unplanned pregnancies.
Lowering Taxes Families and small businesses in our district are being crushed by high taxes — and they’re not seeing the return they deserve.
Keri Heintzeman is committed to easing that burden.
She’ll fight to lower income and property taxes, put an end to runaway government growth, and make Minnesota more affordable for everyone.
It's time the government starts living within its means — just like the rest of us.
Second Amendment The right to keep and bear arms is a constitutional freedom that Keri Heintzeman will fiercely protect.
She opposes efforts to chip away at lawful gun ownership and stands with responsible gun owners, hunters, and sportsmen across our region.
Keri will fight against radical gun control proposals and ensure your Second Amendment rights are never up for negotiation.
Combating Government Fraud Keri Heintzeman knows that every dollar lost to fraud is a dollar stolen from Minnesota taxpayers.
From fraudulent programs to mismanaged funds, waste and abuse have no place in state government.
Keri will push for strict oversight, stronger enforcement, and real accountability to root out fraud and ensure public funds are used with integrity and transparency.
Parental Rights As your senator, Keri will stand strong for parental rights and defend the personal freedoms that make our communities strong.
Keri is a fierce advocate for parental rights, believing that families should have a decisive voice in their children's education, health, and well-being.
