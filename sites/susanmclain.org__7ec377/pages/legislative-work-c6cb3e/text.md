She is a member of the Joint Ways and Means Subcommittee on Education, the House Committee on Agriculture, Land Use, Natural Resources and Water, and the Joint Transportation Oversight Committee.
During the Interim, when the Legislature is not in Session, Representative McLain serves as a member of the Joint Committee on Public Education Appropriation.
Representative McLain at Work Below are just a few highlights of Representative McLain advocating for the issues our community cares about: Law Enforcement Visibility and Accountability In 2026, Representative McLain was a Chief Sponsor of HB 4138, which restricted law enforcement agents from using face coverings to conceal their identity.
This bill passed and was signed by the Governor.
Freedom to Read In 2025, Representative McLain was a Chief Sponsor of SB 1098, which protected access to diverse stories and educational materials in our schools.
This bill passed and was signed by the Governor.
Including Community Voices in Land Use Policy Rep.
McLain believes that community members should be involved in land use decisions.
She encourages farmers to come to Salem and share their important perspectives.
Modern Infrastructure for the 21st Century Rep.
McLain is a leader on transportation and infrastructure policy.
She works with all stakeholders to make our system safer and more effective for every Oregonian.
Expanding Access to Healthcare In 2021, Representative McLain was a Chief Sponsor of Senate Bill 551, which provides for healthcare to part-time faculty members at Oregon’s colleges and universities.
The bill passed and was signed by the Governor.
