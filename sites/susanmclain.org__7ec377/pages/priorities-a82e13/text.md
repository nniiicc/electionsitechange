This year, she fought to hold ICE and law enforcement agents accountable, protect consumer privacy, and strengthen civil rights protections.
She is a proud advocate for reproductive freedom, immigrant justice, LGBTQ+ equality, gun safety, and voting rights.
Susan will always stand up for the freedoms of our community.
High-Quality Public Education As a teacher, Susan knows there is nothing more important than building strong schools for our children.
She was the Chief Sponsor of the 2024 Summer Learning bill, which established a statewide grant program to fund education & enrichment opportunities.
Susan will continue to fight for bold and sustainable investments in public education.
Affordable Communities Susan voted for the largest increase of the Earned Income Tax Credit in Oregon history to lower taxes for 200,000+ low and moderate income families.
She sponsored legislation to ban data centers from passing off energy costs to consumers and supported major investments in housing production to bring down prices.
She will continue to advocate for responsible economic development, accessible pathways to homeownership, and lower costs for Oregonian families.
Safe and Modern Infrastructure Susan is a leader on transportation policy in Oregon.
She has fought for increased public transit and bike-pedestrian facilities, safety improvements, and road maintenance that saves us from expensive repairs in the long run.
Susan has championed a new bridge across the Columbia River to keep travelers safe in the event of a major earthquake.
She knows Oregonians deserve a modern and robust transportation system that meets our 21st century needs.
Building Economic Opportunity Susan understands that growing small businesses and creating living wage jobs is key to Oregon’s economic prosperity.
She watched her late husband struggle with bureaucratic barriers while running his small business, and she is committed to making our economy work for all community members.
She has worked to cut red tape and expand opportunities for businesses to grow and succeed.
She will continue to fight for responsible economic development that balances the need for good jobs in high-tech and agriculture in our region.
Protect Our Environment and Responsible Land Use Economic development cannot come at the expense of Oregon’s environment.
As a Metro Councilor for 16 years, Susan worked to responsibly use our land and water resources.
She knows one of Oregon’s strengths is our commitment to smart growth.
Susan will continue to champion economic opportunities while protecting our farmlands, clean air, and water.
Protect Reproductive Freedoms Susan is a steadfast pro-choice candidate.
She sponsored legislation to protect funding for Planned Parenthood and strengthen patient privacy laws.
Susan believes every single human being should have the freedom to decide if and when they have children, based on what’s best for them and their family’s circumstances.
As a mother, grandmother, and legislator, this is a fundamental right she will always fight for.
