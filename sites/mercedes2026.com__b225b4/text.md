MERCEDES VAZQUEZ SIMMONS FOR NEW YORK STATE ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 137 Proudly Democrat Mercedes is the duly elected NYS Democratic Committee Member proudly representing NYS Assembly District 137 effective June 2024.
Learn more about the important role of your New York State Committee here: https://www.nydems.org/ MAP OF DISTRICT 137 VOTER LOOK-UP PORTAL LEADERSHIP THAT MATTERS My campaign is focused on three priorities that directly impact the quality of life of every family in our community: Affordability, Public Safety, and Education.
I will fight to make our community more affordable by addressing rising housing costs, utility bills, taxes, and the everyday expenses that are putting working families and seniors under increasing pressure.
I believe everyone deserves to feel safe in their home and neighborhood, which means supporting effective public safety strategies, strengthening relationships between law enforcement and the community, and investing in prevention and opportunities for our young people.
And I will fight for an education system that gives every child—regardless of their ZIP code—the opportunity to succeed, while ensuring teachers, parents, and students have the resources and support they need.
Affordable communities, safe neighborhoods, and strong schools are the foundation of a better future—and that is what I will work every day to deliver.
