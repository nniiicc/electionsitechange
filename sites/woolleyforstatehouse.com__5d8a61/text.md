Meet Gabe Woolley
OK House of Representatives - District 98
Gabe Woolley, a Republican and constitutional conservative, is a dedicated teacher from Broken Arrow, Oklahoma.
He believes that an elected position should embody the role of a servant to the people.
Gabe is committed to ensuring that legislation in Oklahoma is guided by the interests of the people, in strict accordance with the United States Constitution.
