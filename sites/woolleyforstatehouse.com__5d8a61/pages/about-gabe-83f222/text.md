Educator and Advocate Gabe Woolley is a dedicated educator with nine years of teaching experience in Tulsa, Oklahoma.
Beyond his role in the classroom, Gabe is a passionate advocate for family and parents' rights, working voluntarily to amplify the voices of Oklahomans through various media platforms.
Champion for Family Rights Gabe believes that the family unit is the cornerstone of society.
Through his work on documentaries and podcasts, he provides a platform for individuals across the state to share their stories and be heard.
His commitment to family and parental rights drives his advocacy and community engagement.
Commitment to Transparency and Accountability Throughout his career, Gabe has been committed to earning the trust of the community and holding himself accountable.
His transparency is evident in his documented journey, which he shares openly to build and maintain strong relationships with Oklahomans.
Media Appearances and Contributions Gabe's work and advocacy have been featured on various platforms, including NBC News, DeAnna Lorraine Shots Fired, Fox 23 News, The Oklahoma Lion Podcast, The Rescue Clayton Podcast, and the Saving Clayton documentary.
He continues to be a voice for the community through his upcoming projects.
Upcoming Work In partnership with Red River Creative Media, Gabe is involved in the upcoming documentary, ' Trans Oklahoma: The Stories of Oklahoma De-Transitioners .' This film explores the journeys of individuals who have left the LGBTQ lifestyle and returned to their roots, highlighting their paths to truth and self-discovery.
The documentary is set to be released soon.
