We’re witnessing unprecedented corruption, abuse of power, and an all-out assault on our Constitution.
Our government isn’t working.
And for too long, most Americans have felt like our economy isn’t working, either.
They’re right.
We need to defeat Trump and the MAGA movement.
But being against Trump isn’t enough.
We need to put forward a clear agenda – and deliver.
Here are some of my thoughts and priorities, from combatting corruption to bringing down costs.
Policy matters.
But to get any of this stuff done, we need members of Congress who are out there every day building the political movement to make it possible.
That’s why it’s so important to get new energy, new ideas, and new perspectives in Washington – right now.
Over the course of the campaign we’ll add to this page, and follow me on social to hear my latest views on policy issues.
