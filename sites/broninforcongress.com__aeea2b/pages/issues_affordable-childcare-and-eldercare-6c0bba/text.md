Affordable Childcare & Eldercare
Childcare and eldercare costs are forcing families into impossible choices.
These costs fall hardest on women and working families, and they hold back the entire economy by sidelining people who want to be working.
Meanwhile, caregivers themselves are underpaid and undervalued.
We need to treat care as essential and worthy of fair pay, especially as so many more Americans live longer into old age.
That means expanding access to affordable childcare and eldercare, raising wages for caregivers, and helping families pay for care without going broke.
Supporting care isn’t just about compassion.
It’s smart economic policy.
