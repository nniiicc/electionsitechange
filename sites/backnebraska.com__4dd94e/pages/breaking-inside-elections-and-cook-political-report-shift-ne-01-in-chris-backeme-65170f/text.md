The shifts come as multiple polls show Backemeyer taking the lead over incumbent Mike Flood.
A recent outside poll from Global Strategy Group shows Backemeyer ahead 49% to 45% head-to-head.
The lead holds when third-party candidates are included, with Backemeyer up one point.
In its analysis, Inside Elections wrote: “…[C]ontinued voter frustration over Trump’s tariff and agriculture policy, and high costs driven by the Iran War, have put this race in play.” Backemeyer, a former national security expert, released the following statement: “Nebraska families are working hard and still falling behind, while Mike Flood keeps voting for the tariffs and the war driving up their costs.
Families deserve a representative who puts them first, not one who rubber-stamps a failed agenda.
This campaign is about changing that, and we’re building the momentum to do it.” In August, the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee (DCCC) added NE-01 to its “Districts in Play” list, the first time the district has appeared on it in 20 years.
Backemeyer is on the air with a six-figure TV and digital ad buy highlighting Flood’s unrelenting support for reckless tariff policies that are raising prices and hurting farmers, and for the Iran War, which is raising fuel prices, adding $1 billion a day to the national debt, and putting the country at greater security risk.
