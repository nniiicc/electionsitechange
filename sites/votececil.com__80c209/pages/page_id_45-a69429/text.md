Putting Missourians First
I’m running for office to serve the people — not the political parties or special interests.
I believe in common sense, personal responsibility, and restoring honesty in government.
Every Missourian deserves a voice in their future, and it’s time to bring fairness, liberty, and integrity back to our state.
Tax ReformMissouri’s real estate and personal property tax system punishes hard-working families, homeowners, and small business owners.
I support eliminating personal property and real estate taxes and replacing them with a simple, flat sales tax that’s fair and transparent for everyone.
I also support establishing a modest utility service tax across essential services such as water, sewer, electric, phone, internet, and trash — a predictable, easy-to-understand system that funds essential services without punishing ownership.
Importantly, I support eliminating taxes, not creating more tax credits.
Too often, tax credits are handed out to corporations and insiders while regular Missourians get left behind.
Real reform means lowering the tax burden itself—not shifting it from one group to another.
Law Enforcement Accountability
Public trust in law enforcement is essential.
The many good officers who serve with honor deserve a system that supports accountability and professional standards.
I propose creating a Governor-appointed Commission on Law Enforcement to:Set statewide standards for law enforcement licensing.
Investigate public complaints against officers.
Suspend or revoke licenses where necessary.
Offer all citizens a fair, direct process for filing complaints.
This commission would help ensure officers are well-trained, ethical, and accountable—protecting both our communities and the integrity of law enforcement.
Children’s Bill of Rights
Missouri’s children deserve protection, stability, and love.
No child should be caught in a system that fails to keep them safe or separates them unnecessarily from caring family members.
I support establishing a Missouri Children’s Bill of Rights that guarantees:
Children’s right to safety and freedom from abuse.
Their right to maintain relationships with loving family members.
A requirement that all termination of parental rights cases be handled in Family Court, where judges understand the gravity of these decisions.
Children are not just case numbers.
They are lives worth protecting—and our policies should reflect that.
Government Accountability and Integrity
Government must be transparent and answerable to the people it serves.
Too many Missourians feel shut out of the process or ignored when something goes wrong.
I support creating a one-stop, public system where citizens can easily file complaints against state officials, employees, or members of the judiciary — and track the results.
This will help restore confidence in government by ensuring wrongdoings are addressed openly and consistently.
