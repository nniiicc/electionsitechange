FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE January 27, 2026 CECIL INCE FORMALLY FILES FOR MISSOURI STATE SENATE DISTRICT 30, VOWS DAY ONE REFORMS Springfield, MO – Cecil Ince filed with the Missouri Ethics Commission on January 22, 2026, officially launching his campaign for Missouri State Senate District 30 and pledging to champion Missouri families over politics.
Ince commits to introducing three bold bills on Day One if elected: • Accountability and Integrity in Law Enforcement Act: Creates independent review boards for police misconduct, requires body cameras with full data transparency, and protects whistleblowers plus citizens filming officers. • Missouri Children’s Bill of Rights: Ensures protection from abuse, sustained family relationships (barring inter-county transport without due process), access to education and medical care, with parental terminations handled in Family Court. • Missouri Education Freedom Act: Frees public education from state control, establishes autonomous county school districts, and grants locals complete authority over curriculum, funding, and policies.
"Incumbents promise reform—I'll deliver real solutions for Missouri families on Day One," Ince said.
