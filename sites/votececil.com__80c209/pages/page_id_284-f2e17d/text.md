Between now and Election Day, I’ll be organizing four cleanup days to keep our roads and sidewalks clean, safe, and welcoming for everyone.
This effort isn’t just about picking up litter—it’s about taking ownership of the spaces we share and showing pride in where we live.
I believe that strong communities start with people who care enough to take action.
Volunteers are always welcome!
If you’d like to join in one of our upcoming cleanup days or help support the effort in another way, please reach out by clicking HERE .
Let’s make our neighborhood shine together—one block at a time.
