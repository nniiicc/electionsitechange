FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Contact: Cecil Ince Phone: (417) 894-6720 Email: info@votececil.com Website: VoteCecil.com State Senate Candidate Cecil Ince Launches "Community in Action" Street Cleanup Series in Springfield SPRINGFIELD, MO — FEBRUARY 11, 2026 — Cecil Ince, Libertarian candidate for Missouri State Senate District 30, today announced the official adoption of Atlantic Street as part of a new community service initiative, "Community in Action." The candidate has committed to a series of structured cleanup days leading up to Election Day to ensure Springfield’s roads and sidewalks remain clean, safe, and welcoming for all residents.
The service project covers a half-mile stretch of Atlantic Street, spanning from North Campbell to North Washington.
Ince views the initiative as a cornerstone of his campaign's philosophy: that local leadership begins with personal responsibility and hands-on service.
"This effort isn’t just about picking up litter—it’s about taking ownership of the spaces we share and showing pride in where we live," said Ince.
"I believe that strong communities start with people who care enough to take action.
We are making our neighborhood shine together, one block at a time." Ince is calling for volunteers to join him in this effort.
All participants will be required to sign an Adopt-a-Street waiver before joining the crew.
The cleanup events are scheduled for Sunday afternoons, providing a consistent opportunity for neighbors to engage in local stewardship.
Cleanup Schedule (Sundays, 2:00 PM – 3:00 PM) • April 5 • May 24 • July 26 • September 27 Residents interested in volunteering or supporting the "Community in Action" initiative are encouraged to RSVP via email at info@votececil.com or visit VoteCecil.com for more information on the campaign and its commitment to a cleaner Springfield.
### About Cecil Ince: Cecil Ince is a Libertarian candidate for Missouri State Senate District 30.
His campaign focuses on community empowerment, individual liberty, and practical leadership that starts at the local level.
