English Español Congress Can Lead Congress has enormous power to improve people’s lives.
Too often, it chooses politics over solving problems.
Many of America’s biggest political battles now play out between the President and the courts.
A President acts.
A lawsuit follows.
A judge rules.
An appeal begins.
There is supposed to be a third player in the room: Congress.
Congress should be leading on the problems people worry about when they wake up in the morning: the cost of housing, healthcare, childcare, education, and everyday life.
It should also be preparing America for what comes next.
Artificial intelligence could transform our economy, improve healthcare and education, and make government work better.
It could also disrupt millions of jobs and create risks we are only beginning to understand.
AI isn’t a Republican issue.
It isn’t a Democratic issue.
It’s an American issue.
Congress doesn’t need to have all the answers.
It needs to ask the right questions, act before problems become crises, and work across party lines when the country needs it.
Congress can lead.
It should lead.
And I believe it can lead again.
