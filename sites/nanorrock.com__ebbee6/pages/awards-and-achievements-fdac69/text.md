Senator Nan Orrock knows how important local government is and the impact it has on their communities.
That is why, for her advocacy, she received the 2022 Champion of Georgia’s Cities Award from the Georgia Municipal Association!
Senator Orrock is proud of her work ensuring the passage of critical legislation for local issues, and she will continue to advocate for the local needs of Georgia as they arise.
She is honored to be part of the innovative, modern solutions for Georgia’s communities to thrive.
