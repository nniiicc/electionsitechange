Skip to content Proudly Serving Georgia House District 115 Menu Meet Bruce At the Capitol In the Community Contact Donate Meet Bruce A life-long resident of Walton County, Representative Bruce Williamson represents the citizens of District 112 in Walton County.
He was elected into the House of Representatives in 2010, and currently serves as the Majority Caucus Chairman.
He also serves on the Rules Committee, Governmental Affairs Committee, Banks and Banking Committee, Insurance Committee, Energy, Utilities and Technology Committee and the Ways & Means committee.
He chairs the Income Tax Subcommittee on Ways & Means.
