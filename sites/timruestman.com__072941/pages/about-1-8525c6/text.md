Meet Tim Ruestman
A Lifetime of Service, A Vision for the Future
Why is Tim the right choice?
Tim Ruestman has spent over three decades serving the people of Woodford County as Coroner.
Through moments of crisis and calm, he’s become a trusted leader known for his integrity, compassion, and accountability.
Born and raised in Central Illinois, Tim has deep roots in the 105th District.
He’s raised a family here, served in local government, and led the Republican Central Committee as chairman.
Now, he’s answering the call to serve at the state level, to represent rural communities that deserve a steady, local voice in Springfield.
