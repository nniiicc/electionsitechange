Meet Cece Harlin
Personal Life
I was born and raised in Wyandotte County and have spent my entire life in Kansas City, Kansas.
This community is not only where I live, but the place I proudly call home.
I attended Kansas City, Kansas Public Schools, and three of my children graduated from the same district.
I am also the parent of two adoptive children.
I am a proud grandparent of four, with a fifth grandchild expected in November; all have been born and raised in Kansas City, Kansas.
Family is at the heart of my life, and it is a privilege to care for both of my parents.
Professional Experience
I bring a broad background in healthcare, community service, advocacy, and operations, with a career rooted in serving others and meeting practical needs.
My professional experience includes direct patient care as a caregiver, certified dialysis technician, and phlebotomist, where I developed strengths in compassionate service, attention to detail, patient education, and adherence to safety standards.
I have also contributed to community-focused and administrative work through volunteer leadership, grant support, research, coalition-building, and public engagement, which have strengthened my communication, organization, and problem-solving skills.
In addition, my experience in warehouse operations and event support reflects reliability, teamwork, and the ability to perform effectively in fast-paced environments.
Together, these experiences reflect my strong work ethic, deep commitment to the community, and the practical leadership skills I believe are needed for public office.

Priorities
My advocacy is rooted in a commitment to accountability, fairness, and service to the people of Kansas City, Kansas.
My priorities focus on protecting taxpayer dollars through responsible development policies, improving educational outcomes by demanding stronger school accountability, and restoring public trust through transparent and consistent election practices.
Grounded in real-life experience and community service, I believe government should work for families, respond to community needs, and be held accountable for results.
Responsible Taxes
I believe taxpayer dollars must be managed with discipline, transparency, and clear public benefit.
I support a responsible approach to economic development in which tax incentives are granted only when developers can demonstrate measurable value for the community and are held accountable for delivering on those commitments.
I believe public resources should be protected and invested in ways that strengthen neighborhoods, schools, and working families.
School Accountability
I believe every child deserves a quality education and that public investment in schools must lead to measurable results.
When students are not succeeding, leaders must be willing to examine what is not working, take responsibility, and make meaningful improvements.
My focus on school accountability is rooted in the belief that strong leadership, clear expectations, and real outcomes are essential to protecting students’ futures.
Election Integrity
I believe public confidence in elections depends on transparency, consistency, and faithful adherence to the rules.
I support clear processes that help voters understand how elections are conducted and trust that outcomes are fair, regardless of the result.
My focus on election integrity is grounded in the belief that strong public trust encourages participation and strengthens democratic accountability.
