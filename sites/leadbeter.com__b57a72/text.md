Robert Leadbeter
PROVEN CONSERVATIVE FOR COLUMBIA COUNTY
Conservative Republican State Rep.
Robert Leadbeter was first elected in 2022.
He currently serves on
the House Insurance, Judiciary, Liquor Control, & Communications & Technology Committees.
He also
serves as the chairman of the PA House Freedom Caucus.
Prior to entering the legislature, Rep.
Leadbeter maintained a successful career as a small business owner
and senior executive for a Fortune Global 500 company.
He and his family live in Catawissa and are active members of New Covenant Church in Berwick.
