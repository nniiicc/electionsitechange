Beth Bosworth is a longtime educator and the current Chair of the MSAD 72 School Board.
She has spent more than 20 years working with students and families, both in the classroom and in the community.
Beth has worked as a public school art teacher and an out-of-school-time educator, and now leads experiential outdoor education programs with The Great Outdoor Company of Western Maine.
She lives in Fryeburg with her husband and two children.
