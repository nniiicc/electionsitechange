DISTRICT 11 CHALLENGES High state income taxes Unallocated surplus funds for schools and farmers Escalating fuel, energy, and food costs Division and mistrust between citizens and government Wisconsin's GOP majority has failed to manage the State's massive surpluses needed to support economic growth.
As a result, taxpayers, including those in the 11th District, face continuing school and municipal referendums, while surpluses sit idle.
As inflation continues to devalue the State's surplus, business owners and homeowners, as well as our farmers, are required to pay additional real estate taxes.
It is disingenuous to boast about surpluses when, in fact, local schools, taxpayers, businesses, and municipalities require referendums to fund basic needs.
Increasing property taxes is avoidable when surpluses are utilized as shared revenue.
As a Servant Leader, I value your insight for ideas that will support future growth across the 11th District.
Please know, I am uninterested in political infighting that turns neighbor against neighbor.
By working together, we can build and achieve a sound economic future.
I would be honored to represent you and your families in the 11th District and I respectfully ask for your support in the November 3, 2026 General Election .
