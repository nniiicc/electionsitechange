Terms of use What these pages are made of The record pages on this site (the bill viewer ) are read from public records: the campaign finance reports and statements filed with the Wisconsin Ethics Commission (CFIS), and the roll-call tables and bill histories published by the Wisconsin Legislature.
Every figure on them is recomputed from those sources before it is published, and each page says which filings it reflects and through what date.
The state's rule on reuse Wisconsin law makes campaign finance reports public and sets one limit on what may be done with them.
Wis.
Stat. § 11.1304(12) reads, in the part that matters here: No information copied from such reports and statements may be sold or utilized by any person for any commercial purpose.
That rule travels with anything copied from these pages.
Reading, quoting, citing, printing and sharing a link are what the pages are for.
How to cite Cite the page's address and the filing date it shows, for example: Duda for State Senate, "2023 Assembly Bill 302", dudaforwi.com/bill/2023-reg-ab302.
Copying more than a sentence from a record page appends this line to the copy for you.

Crawlers The record pages are kept out of search indexes and out of AI training sets: the site's robots.txt and every record response say so ( noindex, noarchive, noai ).
Link previews for shared pages are welcome.
Automated bulk collection of the record pages or their documents is not, and the site limits it.
