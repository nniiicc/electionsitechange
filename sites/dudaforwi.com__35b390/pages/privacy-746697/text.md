This is the website of Duda for State Senate, the campaign committee for Adam Duda, candidate for Wisconsin's 11th Senate District.
The site has no accounts and no sign-in.
This page says, in plain words, what it does with information.
What you send us Forms.
When you volunteer, ask for a yard sign, offer to host an event or write to the campaign, the details you type, such as your name, email, phone, address and message, are delivered to the campaign's email through Web3Forms, a form delivery service, and used to reply to you and to organize the campaign's work in the district.
The campaign does not sell or rent them.
Address lookup.
When you type an address into the district checker or the map, the campaign's server asks the U.S.
Census Bureau's geocoder for a map point for it, and, when the Census has none, OpenStreetMap's Nominatim or Google's geocoder.
Your browser then matches the point to the district boundaries.
The campaign keeps no list of the addresses looked up; the server notes the requesting connection only to limit abuse.
Donations are handled by ActBlue on its own site under its own privacy policy.
The campaign receives the contributor information that Wisconsin campaign finance law requires it to report.
What the site keeps on your device Who else sees traffic The site is served by Amazon Web Services and Cloudflare, which keep standard server logs (network addresses, pages, times) for security and operations under their own policies.
The campaign may record anonymous usage events, such as which pages and tools are opened, to improve the site; they are not tied to a name.
Sharing and outside links The share buttons open Facebook, Bluesky, WhatsApp, Reddit or your Messages app in their own sites or apps.
This site posts nothing on your behalf: you write and send the post there, under that service's policy.
The Facebook button opens Facebook's Share Dialog, which carries the campaign's Facebook app id; Facebook may record that you opened it under Meta's privacy policy.
The photos on this site come from the campaign's public Bluesky account.
Children The site is not directed at children under 13, and the campaign does not knowingly collect information from them.
Deleting what you gave us The site holds no accounts or profiles, and the campaign's Facebook app receives no data about you.
To have a message you sent through a form removed from the campaign's records, write to campaign@dudaforwi.com from the email address you used, and the campaign will delete it, except what Wisconsin law requires it to keep, such as contribution records.
These instructions also stand on a page of their own, /privacy/data-deletion .
Changes and contact Changes to this page are posted here with a new date.
