ABOUT BONNIE
I decided to run for Texas House because I was tired of seeing the incumbents run unopposed.
After trying unsuccessfully to get someone to run, I decided to put my name in the proverbial hat.
I am not a politician.
I am a business owner of almost 20 years and as such, I will bring that knowledge to the TX House.
My husband David and I (AKA Ms.
Bonnie & Mr.
David) have been active in the community for over 30 years.
After working in the hospitality industry, we took a leap of faith and started our own business, North Dallas Martial Arts.
Years later, we bought and gutted a house on Lake Palestine and turned it into a short term vacation rental, Paradise on Palestine.
We have volunteered in the local schools and scouts even long after our boys graduated.
During the summer, I teach swim lessons to all ages.
