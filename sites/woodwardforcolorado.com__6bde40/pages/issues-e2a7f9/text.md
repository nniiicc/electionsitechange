Home About Rob Issues Endorsements Contact Donate Issues Education ​​ As a father of three, improving education is one of my passions and will be a priority as a legislator.
Colorado deserves a quality education system that benefits students, parents, and teachers.
Students should be able to attend the schools that best suit their needs.
Parents should be the primary decision-makers for their children.
And teachers should be assured that education dollars are reaching their classrooms, not tied up in administrative growth.
Transportation Colorado ranks 46th in road quality nationwide, and regular maintenance is much more cost-effective than replacement.
Over $200 million in annual transportation fees are collected from Coloradans, yet a significant portion is directed toward bureaucratic growth, environmental mitigation, and public transit rather than road repair--as our highways continue to crumble.
Knowing that our transportation dollars should serve the needs of the entire state and that our economy relies on high-quality infrastructure, I will work to channel transportation funding back to roads. ​ Crime / Public Safety Colorado is ranked 4th-worst for public safety and is in the top ten for violent crime.
One of the core responsibilities of state government is to create safe living conditions.
As a senator, I commit to supporting law enforcement by voting for laws that put the needs of victims and families over offenders.
Colorado is one of only a few states that has seen a double-digit increase in fentanyl deaths over the past year as the the national rate has decreased by 21%.
In order to effectively address crime and public safety measures, we must toughen our drug distribution and possession laws.
Water ​ Water is vital in Colorado.
As our population continues to concentrate along the Front Range, the demands on our water supplies will also grow.
Conservation is undoubtedly an important element.
Beyond that, our only choices are to dry up farmland or store excess water during wet years.
We need forward-thinking leaders who can balance impacts while planning for the future.
I will support state policies that are informed by the work of our regional experts, free from partisan bias.  Affordability ​​ & the Economy Colorado was recently ranked as the third-most expensive place to live in the country.
The cost of everything from housing to energy to groceries is affected by Colorado’s burdensome regulatory environment.
I’ll work to lift regulations, repeal bad policies, and streamline processes in order to bring down costs.
Under one-party control, the Colorado legislature has exploded the size of government.

Far too many businesses have left the state because of bad Colorado policies.
A strong economy isn't possible without with a competitive business climate.
Energy Colorado’s energy needs have increased with population and the growth of high-demand industries.
We must ensure affordable and reliable energy supplies throughout the state, regardless of weather conditions.
Colorado is equipped to be a leader in clean, reliable, affordable domestic energy.
A strong energy economy creates jobs and reduces our reliance on foreign powers.
In my first term, I led the charge on classifying geothermal as a clean energy source.
Since then, nuclear has been reclassified as well.
Natural gas is cheap and plentiful in our state.
Wind and solar have lessened our reliance on coal but threaten affordability and are unreliable during extreme weather events.
State energy policy should be driven by Coloradans' needs, not political mandates.   Disaster Readiness Since it’s a matter of when, not if, the next natural or manmade disaster will strike, the state must plan accordingly.
I will work to ensure that emergency funds are available, that our insurance policies are just, that our physical infrastructure can withstand extremes, and that our communication systems maximize public safety.
Colorado is in the midst of a home insurance crisis driven by severe wildfires and hail, but made worse by legislative mandates.
Addressing this crisis on a legislative level will require a thorough understanding of the insurance industry.
Effective solutions should not lead to more bureaucratic growth.
2nd Amendment I will always defend our constitutional freedoms, including the right to bear arms.
