Results Over Rhetoric.
Most political websites start with a polished script and a list of empty promises.
That’s not me.
I’m Kathy Easton, and I’m running for State Representative in the 68th District because Illinois deserves better than “business as usual.” Illinois is broken.
We see it in our rising grocery bills, our property tax statements, and the growing concerns for our neighborhood safety.
For too long, career politicians have offered empty promises while the status quo remains the same.
I’m a wife, a mom, and a grandma who believes our community deserves better.
I’ve Been Where You Are My perspective wasn’t formed in some smoke-filled back room; it was built at kitchen tables and on shop floors.
I understand the challenges facing our district because I’ve lived them: The Paycheck-to-Paycheck Reality : I know what it’s like to sit down and wonder how the bills are going to get paid.
Which one can wait another month.
I’ve felt that weight, and I know that every tax dollar the state takes is a dollar taken from your family.
The Small Business Grind : I’ve owned a small business.
I know the pressure of being responsible for other people’s livelihoods.
When you’re the one making sure your team can provide for their families, you learn how to be resourceful, efficient, and fair.
A Veteran & Community Advocate : Whether I’m fighting for those who served our country or volunteering in our neighborhoods, I don’t just talk about service, I live it.
I continue to fight to ensure our heroes aren’t forgotten by the system they protected.
