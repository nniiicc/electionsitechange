HOW YOU CAN HELP?
Host a “Coffee with Kathy” : Invite a few neighbors over to talk about what’s actually happening in the 68th.
No scripts, just honest conversation.
Walk the Block : Help us get the word out by dropping off flyers in your neighborhood.
It’s the best way to meet the people we’re fighting for.
Yard Signs : Let your neighbors know that you’re voting for a public servant, not a politician.
Digital Crew : Help us share our “Sleeves Up” message on social media to break through the noise.
