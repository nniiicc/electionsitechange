THE “SLEEVES UP” PLAN
Common Sense.
Real Service.
No More Insanity.
They say the definition of insanity is doing the same thing over and over and expecting a different result.
When something is broken, you don’t
talk about it.
You fix it.
I plan to “Roll up my Sleeves” and get to work.
In the 68th District, we know the difference between talk and action.
For
too long, we’ve seen the Springfield Democratic machine, led by career politicians like Vella, prioritize rhetoric over results.
We are taxed out,
we feel less safe, and our voice isn’t being heard.
Here is how we get to work:
Affordability: Respecting Your Hard-Earned Paycheck
Illinois families are taxed out.
I will vote NO on tax hikes and YES to keeping more of your hard-earned money in your pocket.
- Stop the “Illinois Squeeze”: We need to freeze property taxes so seniors aren’t taxed out of their homes and young families can actually afford to buy one.
- Kitchen Table Budgeting: If you can’t spend more than you make, the state shouldn’t either.
I’ll fight for a balanced budget that treats your tax dollars with the respect they deserve.
Safety: Families First, Not Criminals
Safety is the foundation of a thriving community.
As a grandmother, I want our kids to play outside without us looking over our shoulders.
- Repeal the SAFE-T Act: This “revolving door” system has failed us.
I will lead the charge to repeal the SAFE-T Act and replace it with laws that prioritize the safety of our families and support our law enforcement officers, not the comfort of criminals.
- Backing the Badge: Our police deserve the training and tools to do their jobs effectively.
When we support our officers, we support our neighborhoods.
Education: Parents are the Primary Teachers
I believe with all my heart that you are your child’s first and most important teacher.
Somewhere along the way, the system forgot that.
- Parental Rights & Transparency: You deserve a seat at the table and the final say in what your child is taught.
No more “backroom” curriculum shifts.
- Protecting Girls’ Sports: As a long-time tennis coach, I’ve seen the sweat and heart our girls put into their sports.
To keep the playing field fair and safe, I believe girls’ sports must be reserved for biological girls.
I’ve been on the court, and I will stand up for our female athletes.
Veterans: : Honoring the Debt, We Owe
As a veteran advocate, I know that “thank you for your service” is a start, but it isn’t enough.
Our heroes shouldn’t have to fight a second war against bureaucracy when they come home.
- Cutting Red Tape: I will bring transparency to the Veterans Assistance Commission (VAC) and make sure
taxpayer dollars are used to directly support veterans, rather than excessive administrative overhead.
- Mental Health & Support: We need to ensure that local veteran resources are funded and accessible, so no one who wore the uniform ever feels forgotten by the state they protected.
