IT'S TIME FOR CHANGE ﻿Minnesota Deserves an Attorney General Who Fights for Its People — ﻿ Not Political Headlines Like many Minnesotans, I’m tired of watching our state become a national symbol of fraud and abuse while vulnerable people are left paying the price.
Too many Minnesotans feel like they’re being left without real protection.
Scammers and fraudsters are targeting seniors.
Families are paying more and getting less.
Small businesses and working people are forced to take on powerful corporations and government systems that too often believe nobody will stop them.
People like you and me are losing hard-earned money to fraudsters and feel like they have nowhere to turn.
At a time when Minnesota has faced massive fraud scandals that made national headlines, the current Attorney General has focused more on political battles than on protecting taxpayers and stopping corruption.
This has got to stop.
As a trial attorney, I’ve spent my career protecting businesses and people who lacked the resources to take on corporate giants.
I’ve taken on some of the world’s most powerful corporations and won.
I’ve secured courtroom victories worth tens of millions of dollars for individuals and small businesses that were told they didn’t stand a chance.
In one landmark case, I won a $110 million jury verdict for the inventor of the MRI machine and his small company after a corporate giant tried to crush them.
That’s not political theater.
That’s real accountability.
Minnesota doesn’t need another politician giving speeches about fighting for people.
We need an Attorney General who has actually done it and who will fight for Minnesotans every single day.
I’ve spent my career standing up for people who were outmatched, outspent, and ignored.
