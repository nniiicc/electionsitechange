As a fun part of this campaign, we will be featuring pets we encounter along the way, particularly Quendy's two spoiled rotten dogs, Max & Gracie along with a silver rubber chicken (Chicken Little) that hitched a ride back down south with Quendy while in New York City.
We also want to highlight Rescue pets and the people and organizations that care for and try to find homes for them.
To kick off 2026, we held a Facebook contest to choose some more pet representatives.
The winners will receive some "Quendy Campaign Swag" and will be featured here.
ALL of the contestants are winners in our book because they have the best owners who love and pamper them!
In a hard fought three round race, we ended with Earl and Dixie neck and neck.
We decided to have co-winners representing the dogs.
Earl is a 9 month old Brittany who lives in Bedford, Indiana with his family.
He loves to help with dishes by licking them before they go in the dishwasher, and he is an avid ice chaser.
He jumps on a piece of ice, shoots it across the floor, and chases it.
Earl also loves to hunt birds with his older stepbrother Odie, and honors every point.
Dixie is Border Collie & Australian Shepherd mix, and her birthday is February 24, 2021.
She is super smart and also super spoiled.
Her favorite snacks are Pringles and veggie straws.
Her favorite pastimes are chasing squirrels, announcing when deliveries come to her house, and riding in the truck.
True to her breed, she stares her owners down when it's bedtime, and herds them straight to bed where she has her own comfortable spot!
Brenda Kay ran away from her competition in both rounds of the Best Cat category.
Her birthday is March 26, 2024.

She is very excited about Quendy's campaign!
It just seems fitting that Sweet Pea, a feisty 9 month old chicken who is a great egg layer, won the 'Other' category.
