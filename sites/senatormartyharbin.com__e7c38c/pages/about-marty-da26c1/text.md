Georgia has been number one for business for five consecutive years.
We just passed the first reduction in personal income tax since 1934, and the first major overhaul of our adoption laws in decades.
This year, with help from the economic boost of President Trump’s tax cuts, we were able to fully restore state funding for public education for the first time since the Great Recession.
We need to continue to fund our public schools but also embrace innovation to make education better.
I support a focus on teaching students the skills they need to go on to college or technical school so that they can secure jobs.
We need to help our students be prepared for real life and help them succeed.
And we are making real progress in reducing government regulations on businesses.
As a small business owner myself, I know firsthand that getting government out of the way is usually the best thing to help businesses grow and create new jobs.

Thank you, Marty Harbin has served in the Georgia State Senate since 2015.
He graduated from Georgia State University in 1977 then started The Harbin Agency, growing the insurance and investments business from two employees to currently 22 team members.
He and his wife, Debbie, were married in 1978 and have lived in Tyrone for 40 years.
They have six children and nine grandchildren, with another precious one on the way.
They attended FBC Atlanta for 20 years where Marty was ordained as a deacon and taught Sunday School.
In 1996 they moved their membership to Fayetteville First Baptist Church where he serves as a deacon and continues to teach Sunday School.
He and Debbie support Southwest Christian Care, Fayette Pregnancy Resource Center, and other local and state ministries.
Harbin first worked in politics on Goldwater’s presidential campaign, then with the Reagan presidential campaign.
In 2008 he served on Huckabee’s Presidential Team100.
A well-known conservative leader, Marty gained broad support in conservative Republican circles for his bold speeches on reducing taxes and reining in an out of control federal government.
He has served in various leadership positions within the Republican Party including Fayette County District Representative, 3rd Vice Chair and 1st Vice Chair of the Third Congressional District.
In 2012, he served as a delegate to the National Republican Convention in Tampa.
