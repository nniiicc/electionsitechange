Protecting Our Students Sudden Cardiac Arrest (SCA) can strike without warning, and survival depends heavily on the rapid use of an Automated External Defibrillator (AED).
While Georgia has made strides in AED accessibility, it's time to take the next step: ensuring that 911 operators serving college and university campuses have a complete and up-todate map of all AED locations-so they can direct callers to the nearest lifesaving device instantly.
Here in Georgia, while AED owners must report their devices to EMS, colleges are not currently required to share AED locations with 911 dispatchers.
We can do better.
What we need now is the infrastructure to connect emergency callers with these lifesaving devices in real time-especially on campuses where students, faculty, and visitors may not know where to look.
