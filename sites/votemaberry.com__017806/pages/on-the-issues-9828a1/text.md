Our community is one of the fastest-growing in the nation, and our roads must keep pace.
Working with the Tennessee Department of Transportation (TDOT), I helped secure $68 million for the widening of Rossview Road , a project residents have waited on for years.
In addition, TDOT has allocated $296.3 million to widen Trenton Road (State Route 48) and $511 million to expand the I-24 corridor through Montgomery County.
I’m continuing to work closely with state and local leaders to ensure these projects move forward efficiently — reducing traffic, improving safety, and preparing our roads for future growth.
Border Security and Immigration I’ve consistently supported legislation that reinforces our southern border, strengthens law enforcement, and protects Tennessee communities from the costs of illegal immigration.
America is a nation built on the rule of law — and I’ve stood firm for that principle.
I’ve backed bills that de-incentivize illegal relocation to our state , ensure state resources are reserved for legal residents , and support our law enforcement officers who uphold border and immigration laws.
I’ll continue to fight for commonsense reforms that secure our borders and safeguard our families.
Education Education remains one of my deepest passions.
Having served on the Clarksville-Montgomery County School Board , I understand firsthand the needs of students, parents, and teachers.
In the legislature, I’ve been a leading advocate for parental rights , school safety , and academic excellence .
I sponsored legislation to expand education freedom and school choice , empowering parents to make the best decisions for their children’s future.
I will continue to fight for classrooms that focus on learning — not ideology — and ensure that teachers have the tools they need to help students thrive.
Healthcare Access When I first ran for office, I made a promise to bring new hospitals and better healthcare options to Clarksville — and we delivered.
Today, TriStar Clarksville Hospital and Saint Thomas Clarksville Hospital have both broken ground, a direct result of our persistent advocacy and partnership with state health officials and hospital networks.
I’ve also been a strong voice for reforming Tennessee’s outdated Certificate of Need (CON) laws , which restrict competition and drive up healthcare costs.
By removing unnecessary barriers, we’re making healthcare more accessible, affordable, and locally available for families in Montgomery County.
Fiscal Responsibility As your State Representative, I’ve voted to keep taxes low , cut government waste , and balance our state budget responsibly.
I believe in smaller government, smarter spending, and returning money to the taxpayers who earned it.
Tennessee continues to be one of the lowest-taxed and best-managed states in America, and I’ll keep working to protect that record of fiscal strength and accountability.
Second Amendment I’m a proud member of the NRA and Tennessee Firearms Association , and I’ve kept my word to defend our Second Amendment rights .
I’ve voted against every attempt to pass so-called “Red Flag” laws — government overreach that would allow the confiscation of firearms from law-abiding citizens without due process.
I’ll always stand up for the constitutional rights of responsible gun owners.
Public Safety As Clarksville continues to grow, public safety must remain a top priority.
I’ve supported legislation that enhances penalties for violent offenders , protects victims of crime , and provides new tools and mental health resources for our first responders .
I’ve also passed legislation expanding Veterans Treatment Courts and PTSD coverage for first responders , ensuring that those who protect us receive the care and support they deserve.
A safe community is a strong community — and I’ll continue working every day to keep our neighborhoods secure and our families protected.
