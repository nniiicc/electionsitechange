Representative Curtis Johnson has been that kind of leader — faithfully representing our district and our state as Deputy Speaker of the House and as a strong conservative voice for Clarksville-Montgomery County.
From his early service on the Clarksville City Council to his remarkable tenure in the Tennessee General Assembly , Representative Johnson has always led with humility, conviction, and an unwavering commitment to the people he serves.
His leadership helped transform our district from traditionally blue to proudly conservative — and his legacy of service will be felt for generations to come.
I had the privilege of sitting down with Representative Johnson in 2023 as he prayerfully considered his future in public service.
When he chose to retire, I knew that stepping forward to serve our district was a responsibility I could not take lightly.
Today, I am deeply honored and grateful to have earned the endorsement of Representative Curtis Johnson .
His trust and confidence mean a great deal to me, and I am committed to building on the strong foundation he has laid — continuing to champion smaller government, lower taxes, safer communities, and the conservative values that define Clarksville-Montgomery County.
Representative Johnson’s example reminds us that leadership is about service — and I’m committed to carrying that same spirit forward as we work together for the future of District 68.
