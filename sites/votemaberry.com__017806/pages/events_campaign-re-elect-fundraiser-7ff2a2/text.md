This will be an exciting evening to celebrate how far we’ve come — and to set our sights on the work still ahead.
Over the past two years, we’ve made tremendous progress for Montgomery County and Tennessee: Passed first in the nation, landmark legislation, the Dismantle DEI Act to restore merit-based principles in government and education.
Championed major infrastructure investments — including 68 million dollars in funding for the long-awaited improvements to Rossview Road.
Campaigned on bringing a new hospital to Clarksville, and now we have two new hospitals, Tri-Star Clarksville Hospital and Saint Thomas, breaking ground!
Led the way on education freedom, school safety, and parental rights — ensuring Tennessee students have every opportunity to succeed.
Strengthened support for our veterans, first responders, and families.
This campaign isn’t just about politics — it’s about people, principles, and the future of Clarksville-Montgomery County.
Come be part of the movement as we build on the momentum and continue serving our community with integrity and conservative leadership.
Special Guest: We will be shortly announcing exciting guests who will be joining us to share remarks!
Together, we’ll celebrate our victories, cast a bold vision for the future, and kick off our first re-election campaign with faith, family, and freedom at the center.
