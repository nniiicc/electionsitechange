Building a modern infrastructure
As conservative-minded freedom lovers have sought refuge here in Tennessee, our infrastructure hasn’t been able to keep up with our growth.
Sitting in traffic isn’t just frustrating, it’s keeping you from making it home in time for a family dinner or making it to your child’s soccer game.
But Marsha knows it’s more than just quality of life; in order to ensure Tennessee continues to lead the nation in economic growth, we must have the infrastructure to support that growth trajectory.
And infrastructure doesn’t just mean roads.
Lack of reliable internet access is not just an inconvenience; it holds Tennesseans back.
Without access to reliable internet, children are unable to complete their homework assignments, and our small businesses are unable to compete on a larger scale.
Marsha has long led the fight to bring broadband to rural communities.
As Tennessee’s next Governor, she’s committed to closing the digital divide.
