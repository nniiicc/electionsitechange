Marsha Will See To
Standing up for Tennessee farmers
Marsha went to college on a 4-H scholarship and knows how hard our Tennessee farmers work to put food on the table and power our economy.
Tennessee farmers are the backbone of Tennessee’s economy.
She’s deeply concerned by the rate at which Tennessee farmland is being developed and, as Governor, will work to protect our farms, especially when it comes to protecting our farmland from Communist China.
Agriculture and forestry are central to Tennessee’s economy, and she’ll be a fierce advocate for our farmers.
