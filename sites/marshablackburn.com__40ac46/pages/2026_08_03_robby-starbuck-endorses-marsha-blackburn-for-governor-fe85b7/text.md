In the interview, Starbuck said: “I want to endorse you.
I think you’re going to be a great governor for the state of Tennessee.
It’s a state I love immensely.
This is where we’ve raised our kids, and I think Tennessee needs leadership that is even more conservative than we have had in the past that is going to have their eye on these issues.
I think you’re going to do a great job.” “I appreciate it so much and am grateful for your endorsement and your support,” said Marsha Blackburn.
“You are exactly right; killing that state income tax taught Tennesseans a lot about ourselves.
We have the grit and determination to make things happen, and I think it also changed the direction and trajectory of our state.
We are an economic powerhouse.
We are the best place to live, to work, and to rear your children.
And we will be the best place for jobs for your kids and your grandkids to come home to.” Robby Starbuck’s endorsement adds to Marsha’s growing list of high-profile endorsements, following recent endorsements by Dr.
Arthur Laffer , Senator Bill Hagerty , and Dave Ramsey .
Marsha has also been endorsed by AFC Victory Fund , Tennessee Fraternal Order of Police , NRA , Congressman Matt Van Epps , Congressman Chuck Fleischmann , Congressman Tim Burchett , Honor our Veterans , T ennessee Chamber of Commerce & Industry , the Tennessee Professional Fire Fighters Association , and the Shelby County Deputy Sheriffs’ Association .
