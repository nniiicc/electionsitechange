Cutting taxes to bolster our economy
No one understands the importance of cutting taxes and keeping them low better than Marsha.
During her time in the State Senate, Marsha took on members of her own party and led the fight against the state income tax in Tennessee.
As a result, today, Tennessee is one of the least taxed states in the nation and is one of the leading states in economic growth.
Tennessee is proof that conservative values work.
That’s why entrepreneurs and businesses are flocking to the state.
As Governor, Marsha will continue her commitment to low-tax, pro-growth policies.
Marsha knows how difficult the Biden years were for Tennessee families.
She’ll help Tennesseans keep more of their hard-earned money and look for every opportunity to keep our economy growing for generations to come.
