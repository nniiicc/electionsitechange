In a video posted to social media , State Rep.
Tim Hicks said: “ As you look over my shoulder at the beautiful mountains, it’s no wonder that we have a lot of people wanting to move to the state of Tennessee.
But another big reason is because of Marsha Blackburn’s leadership from the state legislature, to Congress, then to the Senate, and now for governor.
Please help me and support Marsha Blackburn for the next governor of the great state of Tennessee.” “While serving in the State House, Tim Hicks has been a steadfast conservative voice for the common-sense principles that have shaped Tennessee,” said Marsha Blackburn.
“I’m honored to have the endorsement of an America First, Tennessee First leader.
Upper East Tennessee will always hold a special place in my heart, and I look forward to continuing our work together to make it even better!” For more information on Marsha’s campaign, visit marshablackburn.com .
