BRENTWOOD, TN— The Tennessee Fire Chiefs Association today endorsed Marsha Blackburn for Governor.
“Tennessee’s Fire Chiefs are trusted leaders who understand their communities’ needs and the challenges first responders face every day.
They run toward danger when others need help, and I’m deeply honored to receive their endorsement,” said Marsha Blackburn.
“These heroes deserve a governor who will always have their backs and make certain they have the support and resources they need to keep Tennesseans safe, and, as the next governor of Tennessee, that’s exactly what I will do.”
Marsha Blackburn has been endorsed by other law enforcement groups including the Tennessee Police Benevolent Association, Tennessee Fraternal Order of Police, Tennessee Professional Fire Fighters Association and the Shelby County Deputy Sheriffs’ Association.
For more information on Marsha’s campaign, visit marshablackburn.com.
