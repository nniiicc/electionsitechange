BRENTWOOD, TN— NFIB Tennessee PAC today endorsed Marsha Blackburn for Governor.
Speaking about the endorsement, NFIB State Director Jim Brown said: “Senator Blackburn’s pro-small business record speaks for itself.
She listens to small business owners and understands how decisions about taxes and regulations directly affect their ability to own, operate, and grow their businesses and serve our communities.
Senator Blackburn stood with small businesses first as a member of the Tennessee General Assembly and again as a member of Congress,” Brown said.
“Marsha Blackburn has a great track record supporting our state’s entrepreneurs, and we believe she will bring that same commitment to the governor’s office.”
“I’m so grateful for the confidence placed in me by small business owners who create jobs and strengthen our communities in Tennessee,” said Marsha Blackburn.
“These community leaders deserve a governor who understands the challenges of running a business and will fight to keep taxes low, cut unnecessary regulation, and protect the free market.
I’m honored to receive NFIB’s endorsement, and I will continue working to make certain entrepreneurs have the freedom and opportunity to grow and succeed in the Volunteer State.”
Marsha Blackburn has been endorsed by other groups, including the Tennessee Chamber of Commerce, AFC Victory Fund, the NRA, the Tennessee Fire Chiefs Association, the Tennessee Police Benevolent Association, the Tennessee Fraternal Order of Police, the Tennessee Professional Fire Fighters Association, and the Shelby County Deputy Sheriffs’ Association.
For more information on Marsha’s campaign, visit marshablackburn.com.
