State Representative Chris Todd (R-Jackson)
September 27, 2026
“…Marsha Blackburn has spent years sounding the alarm about the threat posed by Communist China and fighting to put America first.
“Campaign season is full of people telling voters what they want to hear.
Anyone can hire consultants, produce television commercials, and suddenly adopt the right talking points.
A record is harder to manufacture.
“When Tennesseans choose our next governor, we should look beyond the campaign advertising and ask who has actually been willing to stand up for Tennessee, protect American interests, and confront our dependence on Communist China.
“For me, that choice is clear.
“I’m voting for Marsha Blackburn because, time and again, she has had Tennessee’s back.”
