Marsha Will See To
Protecting Tennessee Elections And Supporting Party Registration
Tennessee is ranked #1 in the nation for election integrity, and that’s because we have long been a leader in making it easier to vote and harder to cheat.
Marsha supports going even further by closing our primaries, because she believes if you want to help choose the Republican Party’s nominees, you should openly declare yourself a Republican before election day.
As Governor, she will fight to implement party registration and do everything she can to keep Tennessee’s elections safe and secure.
