Speaking about the endorsement, Senator Hagerty said: “I’ve had the privilege of serving Tennesseans in the United States Senate alongside Marsha Blackburn; she is wholly committed to our conservative values, common sense, and President Trump’s America First agenda.
I know that, as governor, Marsha’s steadfast, conservative leadership in Tennessee will only grow.
I’m proud to endorse my friend, Marsha Blackburn, to be the next governor of the great state of Tennessee, and I hope you’ll join me in supporting her during early voting.” “In the Senate, Bill and I have stood together to protect our Tennessee values, and I’m grateful for his endorsement as I seek to serve Tennesseans in the governor’s office,” said Marsha Blackburn.
“Bill is a true conservative who has always kept the well-being of Tennesseans at the forefront of his work, and we’ll continue working together to deliver real results for Tennesseans.
Please get out and vote early.” Senator Hagerty’s endorsement adds to Marsha’s strong list of support, most recently from AFC Victory Fund , Tennessee Fraternal Order of Police , NRA , Congressman Matt Van Epps, Congressman Chuck Fleischmann , Congressman Tim Burchett, Honor our Veterans , Tennessee Chamber of Commerce & Industry , the Tennessee Professional Fire Fighters Association , and the Shelby County Deputy Sheriffs’ Association .
For more information on Marsha’s campaign, visit marshablackburn.com .
