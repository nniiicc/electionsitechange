Confronting Communist China
We cannot allow our Tennessee farmers to be put at risk because of Communist China’s quest for global dominance.
Marsha recognizes the existential threat Communist China poses to our American way of life, from preying on our children to buying up our farmland.
Marsha has led the fight to stop China from buying U.S. farmland and, as Governor, she will fight to protect Tennessee farmers from Communist China.
Marsha believes that the state of Tennessee should enact a complete ban on China having the ability to own any type of land in our state.
She wholeheartedly supports President Trump’s America First agenda, which includes trade deals that will protect Tennessee workers and consumers.
Standing up to China and standing for our farmers and businesses is conservative leadership.
