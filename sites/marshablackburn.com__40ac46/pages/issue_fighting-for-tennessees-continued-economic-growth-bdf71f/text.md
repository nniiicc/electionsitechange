Fighting for Tennessee’s Continued Economic Growth
Tennessee has seen immense growth over the past decade due to our proven conservative fiscal policies.
As a member of the Tennessee State Senate, Marsha led a successful campaign against the state income tax.
As Governor, Marsha will work to ensure we keep taxes and prices low and reduce unnecessary spending and regulations so more companies continue to relocate to Tennessee and bring great-paying jobs here to our state.
While working to attract that outside investment to bring additional jobs to our state, she’ll also support Tennessee-grown small businesses so that they expand right here at home.
Marsha will work with the business community to identify areas for improvement, like workforce development, and commit to filling those voids.
Making Tennessee America’s conservative leader will bring economic prosperity to all Tennesseans.
