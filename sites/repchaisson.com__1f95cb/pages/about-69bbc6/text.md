Chaisson
Candidate for Re-Election
Michael Chaisson is running for State Representative in the 1st Bristol District with a clear mandate to bring balance, common sense, and fiscal responsibility to Beacon Hill.
He proudly serves Foxboro, Mansfield, and Norton.
Chaisson's focus is on transparency, fiscal accountability, and, most importantly, advocating for the needs of the people he represents.
He is committed to being an effective leader who works tirelessly for every constituent in the district.
Whether navigating state agencies, addressing local concerns, or fighting for fair treatment on Beacon Hill, Michael Chaisson is accessible, honest, and dedicated to delivering real results for the 1st Bristol District.
