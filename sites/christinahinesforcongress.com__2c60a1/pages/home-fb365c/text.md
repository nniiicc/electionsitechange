“Macomb County is my home, and as a working mom of three young children, I know how hard it is to get by - paying for groceries, healthcare, childcare and more - and how important it is that we protect our children and honor our seniors.
I refuse to stand idly by while billionaires leave working families like ours in the lurch.
I am a Mom on a Mission fighting to lower costs, protect families, and fight corruption.”
- Christina Hines, candidate for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Michigan’s 10th Congressional District
About Christina:
Prosecutor and Mom on a Mission
- Christina’s story is one of hard work, service, and fighting for others.
These are the values she learned growing up in Macomb County in a hardworking middle-class family and in Warren Woods Public Schools.
These are the values she hopes to impart to her children that she and her husband, David, are raising right here in Macomb County.
These are the values she will bring with her to Congress.
- Christina attended the University of Michigan (Go Blue!) and earned her law degree from Wayne State University Law School.
Immediately after law school, she worked as a prosecutor, keeping our community safe, putting away murderers and predators, and advocating for victims of terrible crimes.
Throughout Christina’s career as a legal leader in Michigan, she has prosecuted hate crimes, cold case sexual assaults, homicide, torture, and human trafficking cases, from district courts to the Michigan Supreme Court.
- Christina is running for Congress because she believes it’s time to put the middle class first, to make sure that the American Dream isn’t just a dream but a reality for everyone—no matter who you are, where you come from, or how much money you make.
It’s time to rebuild an economy that works for *all* of us.
It’s time to fight to preserve the American dream for future generations.
A Mom on a Mission to:
- Christina is a working mom from Macomb who knows what it feels like to watch the bills pile up.
Groceries, gas, healthcare premiums — everything costs more, and we are struggling to keep up.
In Congress, Christina will fight to lower costs, take on the politicians and corporations that are taking advantage of working families, and fight for good-paying Michigan jobs.
She’ll work with anyone — Democrat, Republican, or Independent — to get it done.
- As a prosecutor, Christina spent her career protecting children and families and putting away dangerous people like pedophiles and murderers.
In Congress, she’ll fight for safer communities, world-class schools, and the resources law enforcement needs to prevent violent crime and protect kids and seniors online.
- Christina is a working mom from Macomb — not a career politician — and she believes D.C. should work for us, not corporations and special interests.
That’s why she’s the only candidate in this race refusing corporate PAC money.
In Congress, she’ll take on anyone driving up costs, ban members of Congress from trading stocks, and work with anyone — regardless of party — to clean up the corruption in Washington so that they finally start to deliver for working families.
