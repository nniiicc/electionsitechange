NOVEMBER 3RD GENERAL ELECTION FAQ
When is the next election in Michigan’s 10th Congressional District?
The Michigan 10th Congressional District General Election takes place on Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026.
When is the last day to register to vote in the November 3rd Election?
Online and mail-in voter registration forms must be submitted to your local clerk's office or postmarked by October 19th, 2026.
After that date, you may register in person at your city or township clerk's office until 8 p.m. on November 3rd, but you must have a document that proves your residency in the city or township where you are registering.
Acceptable proof includes a Michigan driver's license or ID card, a lease, paystub, or government check.
When are absentee ballots available?
Voters who requested an absentee ballot or are on the permanent absentee list will have ballots mailed to them beginning September 24th, 2026.
Voters can also go to their city or township clerk's office in person to request an absentee ballot starting on September 24th until 4 p.m. on November 2nd.
When must my mail-in ballot be received?
Your absentee ballot must be received at your city or township clerk's office or deposited in one of your city or township's ballot drop boxes by 8 p.m. on November 3rd, 2026.
We STRONGLY recommend that you mail your ballot back a minimum of 14 days before the election on Nov. 3 to guarentee its arrival by election day.
That means you mail your ballot any time before Tuesday, October 20th.
If you still have your ballot after October 20, do not mail it.
Bring it to your local dropbox or clerk’s office.

Do I need a photo ID to vote in person?
Voters are not required to possess a photo ID to vote in Michigan.
Voters without a photo ID may sign an affidavit and will be issued a ballot.
A photo ID presented at a polling place does not need to have the voter’s address on it.
The name on the identification card can be a shorter form of the voter’s full name.
Acceptable forms of photo ID include the following:
- Driver’s license or state ID card issued by Michigan or another state
- Federal, state, county or local government-issued photo ID
- U.S. passport
- Military photo ID card
- Educational institution ID
- Tribal photo ID card
- Local or county issued government ID
- Concealed Pistol license
What if I don’t have a photo ID?
If a voter does not have photo ID, or has forgotten to bring it, they can still cast a ballot by signing an affidavit stating that they are not in possession of a valid photo ID.
Once the affidavit is signed, they may cast a ballot, and it will be counted with all other ballots on Election Day.
