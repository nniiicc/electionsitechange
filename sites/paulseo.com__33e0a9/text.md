Meet Paul Paul Seo is a husband and father, U.S.
Army veteran, corruption prosecutor, and Mayor of Rancho Palos Verdes.
Raised by hardworking Korean immigrant parents who owned a local small business, Paul joined the Army before earning his law degree and building a career working to protect California communities.
He has successfully prosecuted violent crime, held corrupt politicians accountable for abusing the public's trust, and taken on corporations that price gouge consumers and exploit families during emergencies.
Now, Paul is running for State Assembly to crack down on corruption in Sacramento, lower the cost of living, keep communities safe, and make government work for working families.
That’s why Paul is supported by firefighters and first responders, nurses and healthcare workers, small business owners, frontline prosecutors, and working families across the South Bay.
