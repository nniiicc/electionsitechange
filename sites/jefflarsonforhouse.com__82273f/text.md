Priorities Putting the people of District 10B first.
01 Revitalize our rural economies.
We need our small cities and towns to be strong and support our rural neighbors.
That means economic incentives for small businesses and farmers.
We need to support beginning farmers, generational farming and farming innovation.
We need to cut costs and regulations for starting new businesses.
We need to reward innovation in our rural communities as much as we do in the big cities.
02 Address our broken healthcare system.
The cost of health care is out of control.
In our district poor healthcare access makes it even more expensive.
We need an affordable health plan for farmers and small business owners that brings local access to high-quality, comprehensive healthcare.
We need to find innovative ways to improve access to full service health care in small cities and towns.
03 Strengthen our Rural Schools.
Our rural schools operate at a significant financial disadvantage.
We need to create funding equity between rural, suburban and urban MN due to our smaller tax base.
The burden of unfunded mandates on rural schools are putting them at risk.
We need to level the playing field so our rural kids can continue to benefit from a strong public school education.
