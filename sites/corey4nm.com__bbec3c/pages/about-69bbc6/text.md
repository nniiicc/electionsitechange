Corey Zimmerman Corey Zimmerman has dedicated his life to serving the community and helping families through some of life’s most difficult moments.
Born in North Dakota and raised in the Midwest, Corey moved to Albuquerque in 2013 and understands the unique challenges and opportunities facing District 26.
After earning his Master’s Degree in Divinity, Corey worked tirelessly as a hospice chaplain and nonprofit director, serving people with compassion, integrity, and dedication.
Through that work, he saw firsthand the importance of strong communities, quality healthcare, and support for working families.
Corey advocates for policies that support small businesses, strengthen public safety, improve education, and make healthcare more accessible and affordable.
His commitment to education and healthcare reform comes from personal experience and his unwavering belief that lasting change begins at the community level.
Corey's Core Values Corey believes in integrity, transparency, and accountability as the foundation of effective leadership.
His dedication to fostering economic growth, improving educational systems, and ensuring equitable healthcare access drives his campaign.
Corey is committed to listening to the voices of District 26, prioritizing community needs, and implementing sustainable solutions that benefit all residents.
Corey's Vision for Public Service Driven by Purpose Corey is deeply motivated by a lifelong commitment to serving others and enhancing the quality of life in New Mexico.
His decision to run for office stems from a desire to bring about meaningful change and to ensure that the voices of District 26 are heard and respected.
With a focus on integrity and transparency, Corey aims to create policies that reflect the needs and aspirations of his community.
