How can I volunteer for the campaign?
You can volunteer by signing up on our website or contacting us directly via email or phone.
We welcome all forms of support, whether it’s canvassing, phone banking, or event organizing.
What are Corey's main campaign priorities?
Corey is focused on enhancing public safety, supporting small businesses, improving education, and ensuring accessible healthcare for all residents of District 26.
How can I donate to the campaign?
Donations can be made through our secure online portal on the campaign website.
Every contribution helps us reach more constituents and spread our message.
Where can I find upcoming campaign events?
Upcoming events are listed on our website’s events page.
You can also follow us on social media for the latest updates and announcements.
How do I contact the campaign for media inquiries?
For media inquiries, please email our communications team at media@coreyfornm.com or call our office during business hours.
What is Corey's stance on education reform?
Corey believes in strengthening our education system by increasing funding for public schools, supporting teachers, and ensuring equitable access to quality education for all students.
