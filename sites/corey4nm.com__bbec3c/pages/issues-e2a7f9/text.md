Supporting Small Businesses Building a Robust Local Economy Corey believes that small businesses are the backbone of District 26’s economy.
By providing targeted support and resources, we can ensure these enterprises not only survive but thrive, creating jobs and fostering innovation.
Corey advocates for reduced regulatory burdens, increased access to capital, and enhanced business education programs to empower entrepreneurs and stimulate local economic growth.
Enhancing Community Safety Creating Safe and Secure Neighborhoods Community safety is a top priority for Corey.
In District 26, residents deserve to feel secure in their homes and neighborhoods.
Corey proposes a collaborative approach involving local law enforcement, community leaders, and residents to address safety concerns.
By investing in community policing, improving street lighting, and supporting neighborhood watch programs, we can build a safer environment for all.
Health Care Challenges Access to Health Care in District 26 In District 26, access to health care remains a significant challenge for many families and individuals.
The lack of nearby medical facilities and specialists means that residents often have to travel long distances to receive necessary care.
This situation is particularly difficult for those without reliable transportation or flexible work schedules.
Additionally, the cost of health care continues to rise, making it increasingly unaffordable for many.
These barriers not only affect physical health but also contribute to stress and anxiety, impacting overall well-being.
Addressing these issues is crucial for ensuring that all residents have the opportunity to lead healthy, fulfilling lives.
Access to quality health care is a fundamental need that remains unmet for many in our community.
The shortage of health care providers and facilities in District 26 exacerbates this issue, leaving vulnerable populations without the care they need.
This gap in services disproportionately affects low-income families and individuals, who may delay or forgo necessary treatments due to cost or distance.
The impact of these challenges is far-reaching, affecting not just physical health but also economic stability and quality of life.
It is essential to address these barriers to ensure equitable health care access for all.
Proposed Solutions Corey's Vision for District 26 Corey is committed to revitalizing District 26 by implementing comprehensive solutions that support small businesses, enhance community safety, and improve health care access.
To support small businesses, Corey proposes reducing bureaucratic red tape and providing financial incentives for startups and local entrepreneurs.
By fostering a business-friendly environment, we can stimulate economic growth and create jobs within the community.
For community safety, Corey plans to increase funding for local law enforcement and community programs that focus on crime prevention and youth engagement.
By promoting collaboration between police and residents, we can build trust and create safer neighborhoods.
In terms of health care, Corey advocates for expanding access to affordable health services and increasing the number of health care providers in the area.
By addressing these critical issues, we can ensure a thriving and secure future for all residents of District 26.
