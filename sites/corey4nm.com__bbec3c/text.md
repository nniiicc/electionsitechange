Contact Select Page Empowering New Mexico's Vision for District 26 Corey's Core Initiatives Vibrant Communities Corey Zimmerman is committed to building communities that are inclusive, supportive, and resilient — where every resident feels valued, respected, and connected.
He believes strong neighborhoods are built through unity, public safety, opportunity, and a shared commitment to improving the quality of life for families across District 26.
Small Business Support Corey Zimmerman wants to empower local entrepreneurs in District 26 by giving small businesses the resources, support, and opportunity they need to succeed.
By strengthening local businesses, we can drive economic growth, create jobs, and encourage innovation right here in our community.
Ensuring Safety Corey Zimmerman is committed to prioritizing the safety and well-being of every community member through practical policies, strong community partnerships, and proactive engagement.
He believes safer neighborhoods are built when residents, local leaders, and law enforcement work together to strengthen communities, support families, and create a secure future for District 26.
Equitable Health Care Corey Zimmerman believes every family in District 26 deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare close to home.
He is committed to supporting policies that strengthen local healthcare services, improve access for seniors and working families, and ensure no one is left behind when it comes to the health and well-being of our community.
