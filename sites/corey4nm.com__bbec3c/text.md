Corey's Core Initiatives
Vibrant Communities
Corey Zimmerman is committed to building communities that are inclusive, supportive, and resilient — where every resident feels valued, respected, and connected.
He believes strong neighborhoods are built through unity, public safety, opportunity, and a shared commitment to improving the quality of life for families across District 26.
Small Business Support
Corey Zimmerman wants to empower local entrepreneurs in District 26 by giving small businesses the resources, support, and opportunity they need to succeed.
By strengthening local businesses, we can drive economic growth, create jobs, and encourage innovation right here in our community.
Ensuring Safety
Corey Zimmerman is committed to prioritizing the safety and well-being of every community member through practical policies, strong community partnerships, and proactive engagement.
He believes safer neighborhoods are built when residents, local leaders, and law enforcement work together to strengthen communities, support families, and create a secure future for District 26.
