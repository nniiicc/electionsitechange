Cinde Wirth Dr.
Cynthia “Cinde” Wirth is a seventh-generation Hoosier and resident of Bartholomew County, where she raised her family.
She taught high school biology and environmental science for more than a decade and inspired her students to take their STEM skills out of the classroom by creating a marine biology research-based study abroad program.
She served as a congressional fellow from 2018-19 in Washington, D.C., gaining experience as a policy writer while navigating bipartisan challenges.
A first-generation college graduate, Cinde earned her Ph.D. in 2025 from Ball State University, proving that learning is a lifelong commitment.
