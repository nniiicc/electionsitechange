Dr.
Cinde Wirth is focused on addressing the concerns of working-class Hoosiers.
She will take her classroom grit and working-class roots to D.C. to improve life in Indiana.
Learn more about her campaign priorities.
Support Working Families Working-class Hoosiers should be thriving, not just surviving.
Cinde will work for good-paying jobs, affordable housing, affordable childcare and equal access to benefits like sick time and paid family leave.
Learn More Strengthen Public Education Our children deserve equitable access to learning from the earliest age.
Cinde will support our professional teacher workforce and ensure our neighborhood preK-12 schools are a safe and welcoming place for students of all backgrounds and abilities.
Learn More Improve Access to Healthcare When it comes to our families, health and well being should never be a commodity traded to make profit.
Cinde will fight for accessible and affordable healthcare for all Hoosiers, because no one should ever fear seeking medical treatment because of job loss, an inability to pay or concerns of discrimination.
Learn More Protect the Environment We must take action immediately to ensure the Earth is hospitable for future generations.
Cinde will push for investments in clean forms of energy and renewable resources.
