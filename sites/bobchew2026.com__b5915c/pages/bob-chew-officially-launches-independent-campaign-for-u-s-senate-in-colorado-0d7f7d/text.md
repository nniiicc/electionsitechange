DENVER, Colo. – May 11, 2026 – Bob Chew, a Navy veteran, business leader, and political independent, today officially launched his campaign for the U.S.
Senate in Colorado on the Forward Party line, beginning with a $2 million personal investment in the race driven by his deep concern for the direction of the country and his belief that Colorado families deserve better candidates, more competition, and a real choice beyond the two-party status quo.
Watch his announcement video here.
“Washington is a circus.
Republicans and Democrats have failed us, and if we keep voting for them, I assure you they will continue to fail us,” said Bob Chew at a Highlands Ranch rally this past Saturday.
He continued: “The only way things change in this country is if we start electing people who are free from the control of party bosses, party mega-donors, and lobbyists.
I served my country, built a multi-million-dollar business, created hundreds of American jobs, and solved hard problems in the real world.
That is the kind of experience and independent leadership we need to take our country back from the career politicians and partisan gangsters.”
Bob will be focusing on the critical issues facing our country:
- Saving Social Security and Medicare
- Making daily living affordable
- Reducing our national debt
- Ensuring reliable energy and water supplies
- Restoring the American Dream for all generations
Bob Chew lives in Boulder, has two adult children, and is an avid outdoorsman.
