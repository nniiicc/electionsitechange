Senate FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE BOULDER, Colo.
(August 21, 2026) – Independent Veterans of America yesterday announced its endorsement of Bob Chew, a U.S.
Navy veteran and Forward Party nominee for U.S.
Senate in Colorado.
The endorsement recognizes Chew’s service, his commitment to putting country over party, and his campaign’s focus on offering Colorado voters a solutions-first alternative to the two-party status quo.
“Bob Chew took an oath as a Navy Ensign to support and defend the Constitution,” said IVA President and Founder Paul Rieckhoff.
“He still lives by that oath every day and he can’t watch anymore while the national debt balloons, Social Security and Medicare inch toward insolvency, and international threats grow — all because these problems are too hard for career politicians to touch.
Neither of his opponents is a veteran.
Bob is.
And he’s ready to serve again.” Chew, a former Naval submarine officer and longtime business leader, is running on a message of “real courage for real solutions.” His campaign emphasizes fiscal responsibility, protecting Social Security and Medicare for future generations, strengthening national security, and restoring a working Senate that can solve problems through compromise and accountability.
“I’m honored to have the support of Independent Veterans of America,” said Bob Chew.
“Veterans understand what it means to serve something bigger than ourselves.
I’m running because Coloradans are tired of extreme politics and career politicians who answer to party leaders instead of voters.
It is time to put an end to the dysfunction in Washington and send problem-solvers to the U.S.
Senate.” About Bob Chew Bob Chew is the Forward Party nominee for the U.S.
Senate in Colorado.
He served as an officer on a U.S.
Navy attack submarine, built a successful engineering and technical services company, created hundreds of American jobs with his business, and is running to bring fiscal seriousness and independent problem-solving to Washington.
About Independent Veterans of America Independent Veterans of America is a national organization dedicated to empowering independent and unaffiliated veterans to support and defend democracy, strengthen civic leadership, and build a more representative future for the country.
