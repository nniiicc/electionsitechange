(September 1, 2026) – Bob Chew, the Forward Party nominee for the U.S.
Senate from Colorado, released his second television advertisement, “They Brought Us Here.” The new ad argues that decades of two-party dysfunction have helped produce many of the problems confronting American families, and presents Chew as an independent problem solver focused on real, practical solutions.
The ad is part of Chew’s statewide $1M+ advertising buy on broadcast, cable, and digital media.
Watch the ad HERE .
“They Brought Us Here” draws a direct contrast between partisan gridlock in Washington and Chew’s results-oriented approach.
The ad’s message is simple: the same political system that has allowed urgent challenges to grow cannot be trusted to fix them without new leadership that prioritizes solutions over party loyalty.
“Republicans and Democrats have spent years blaming each other while the problems facing Americans have become harder and more expensive to solve,” said Bob Chew.
“I’m running to move us forward, with the courage to tell the truth, the independence to reject failed party politics, the freedom to reach across the aisle to both parties to forge compromise legislation that gets to the root causes of problems, and the experience to deliver real solutions for Colorado and the country.” About Bob Chew : Bob Chew is the Forward Party nominee for the U.S.
Senate in Colorado.
He served as an officer on a U.S.
Navy attack submarine, built a successful engineering and technical services company, created hundreds of American jobs with his business, and is running to bring fiscal seriousness and independent problem-solving to Washington.
