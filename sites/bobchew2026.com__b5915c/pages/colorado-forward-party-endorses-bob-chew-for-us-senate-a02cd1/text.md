Bob Chew is running as a Forward candidate for the United States Senate to address the difficult long-term problems that the two major political parties have refused to solve.
Bob grew up in a middle-class family in Cleveland, Ohio.
He earned his engineering degree while working to pay his own way, served as an officer on a nuclear submarine, and spent the last three decades building a global company from four employees to 700, then sold it to the employees via an ESOP.
He is not a career politician.
He is running because the problems facing Colorado and this country are real, urgent, and require someone willing to do something about them.
Bought by no one.
Working for you.
Bob will fight for a Colorado and a country where:
- The federal government lives within its means and delivers the services people depend on
- Every Coloradan has access to the education, opportunity, and resources to build a good life
- Those truly in need are supported without creating dependency or punishing hard work
- The United States leads the world with strength, integrity, and respect for our planet
Colorado has a choice.
Bob respects the decades of public service many politicians have dedicated to this state.
But a political resume doesn’t balance the budget, lower your grocery bill, or fix a Congress that is dysfunctional.
The problems we face demand solutions, not just “vote blue vs. red.” Bob Chew will deliver solutions that work.
