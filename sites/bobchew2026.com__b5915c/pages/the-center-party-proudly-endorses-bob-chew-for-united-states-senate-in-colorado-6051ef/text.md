At a time when partisan gridlock and political extremes are increasingly dividing the nation, Bob Chew emerges as a pragmatic, solutions-oriented leader dedicated to serving the majority in the middle.
Bob Chew, US Senate Candidate for Colorado The Center Party’s endorsement is rooted in Bob Chew’s unwavering commitment to the core principles of balance, compromise, and common sense.
After a thorough review of his platform and vision for Colorado, the Center Party recognizes that Chew perfectly embodies our Eight Pillars of reform: • Fiscal Responsibility: Chew understands that out-of-control spending threatens our nation’s future.
He is committed to reigning in the national debt and implementing sensible budgeting that protects taxpayer dollars without compromising essential services. • Government Efficiency: A firm believer in cutting red tape, Chew is dedicated to streamlining federal agencies, eliminating waste, and ensuring that government programs work for the people, not the bureaucracy. • Balanced Policy: Rejecting the extreme agendas of the far-left and far-right, Chew advocates for evidence-based legislation that finds common ground and delivers real results for everyday Americans.
Global Strength: Chew supports a strong, smart foreign policy that protects American interests abroad, strengthens alliances, and ensures our military remains the most capable in the world. • Fair Representation in Government: Recognizing the dysfunction of the current two-party system, Chew is an advocate for structural reforms that empower independent-minded voters and guarantee all citizens have a meaningful voice in Washington. • Equal Opportunity for All: True to his own life story of hard work and resilience, Chew is passionate about restoring the American Dream and ensuring that every Coloradan has the tools to succeed, regardless of their background. • Ensure American Jobs and Prosperity: Chew’s pro-growth agenda focuses on supporting small businesses, investing in future-focused industries, and keeping high paying jobs within our borders to secure long-term economic stability. • Protect Our Environment: Acknowledging the natural beauty and resources of Colorado, Chew champions practical, market-driven environmental protections that preserve our land and water while maintaining energy independence and economic growth.
“Americans are exhausted by the toxic division in Washington,” said CEO, Scott Brennan of the Center Party.
“Bob Chew represents the common-sense leadership that our country desperately needs.
He isn’t beholden to radical ideologies; he is committed to the people of Colorado.

Senate.” “The enthusiasm for my candidacy has been overwhelming.
Everywhere I go, people are so glad to know there is a viable candidate willing to break through the political dysfunction and offer real courage for real solutions,” said Bob Chew.
“I want to thank the Center Party for its vote of confidence and support.
Together, we can fix our political system and deliver real results for the American People!” The Center Party invites all moderate Republicans, moderate Democrats and Independents who feel unrepresented by the current system to join the movement for a better tomorrow.
