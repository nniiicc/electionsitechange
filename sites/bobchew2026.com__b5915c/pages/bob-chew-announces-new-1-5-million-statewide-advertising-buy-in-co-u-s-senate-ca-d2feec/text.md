(September 30, 2026) – Bob Chew, an independent Forward Party candidate for the U.S.
Senate from Colorado, today announced a new $1.5+ million statewide advertising buy designed to reach voters across Colorado through digital platforms, television, radio and a series of virtual town halls.
The campaign will use the coordinated media effort to introduce Chew to more Coloradans, communicate his priorities and create opportunities for voters around the state to engage directly with his candidacy.
TV advertisements will begin airing today and continue through Election Day.
“Coloradans deserve better than the same partisan politics and Washington dysfunction that have failed to solve the problems Americans face every day,” said Bob Chew.
“This investment will help us reach more Coloradans and explain how, unlike my opponents, I will work with anyone to deliver practical solutions to challenges like the affordability crisis.” The advertising buy includes: Digital advertising across major online and streaming platforms, with targeted outreach to voters throughout Colorado.
The first digital ad of the buy can be viewed HERE .
Television advertising statewide, including broadcast, cable and satellite.
The first TV ad of the buy can be viewed HERE .
Radio advertising reaching urban, suburban and rural communities throughout the state.
Virtual town halls giving Coloradans direct access to Chew for issue-focused discussions and live questions.
Dates, registration information and viewing details will be released through the campaign’s website and social media channels.
“This campaign is not about fitting Colorado into a partisan box,” Chew said.
“It is about bringing people together around ideas that work, treating voters with respect and restoring independent judgment to the U.S.
Senate.” About Bob Chew Bob Chew is the Forward Party nominee for the U.S.
Senate in Colorado.
He served as an officer on a U.S.
Navy attack submarine, built a successful engineering and technical services company, created hundreds of American jobs with his business, and is running to bring fiscal seriousness and independent problem-solving to Washington.
