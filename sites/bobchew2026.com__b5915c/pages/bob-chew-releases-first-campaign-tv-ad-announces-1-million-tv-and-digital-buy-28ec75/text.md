(August 5, 2026) – Bob Chew, Forward Party candidate for U.S.
Senate in Colorado, today released his first campaign television advertisement.
The $1 million-plus statewide buy will air on broadcast TV, cable TV, and digital platforms.
Watch the ad here .
Called “Ticked Off,” the ad introduces Bob Chew to Colorado voters as a U.S.
Navy veteran and successful businessman who has created hundreds of American jobs.
The ad begins airing today, marking a major new phase of Chew’s statewide campaign.
Bob Chew is running to break the failed two-party status quo, tackle the affordability crisis, restore fiscal responsibility in Washington, and put Colorado families first.
“Colorado deserves a senator who answers to voters, not party leaders, lobbyists, or Washington insiders,” said Chew.
“This ad speaks to the frustration hardworking Coloradans feel about a government that no longer works for them.” “For too long, Republicans and Democrats have asked voters to accept a broken status quo,” Chew added.
“I’m running to give Colorado something different: real courage, real solutions, and a senator who will tell the truth about the hard choices facing our country.” About Bob Chew Bob Chew is the Forward Party nominee for the U.S.
Senate in Colorado.
He served as an officer on a U.S.
Navy attack submarine, built a successful engineering and technical services company, created hundreds of American jobs with his business, and is running to bring fiscal seriousness and independent problem-solving to Washington.
