Meet Michanna
Michanna Tate is a resident of the City of Laurens.
She interacts with and gives back to the Laurens County community daily, through her position as Manager of KOT Pack and Ship, the only pack and ship store in Laurens County.
In addition, Michanna Tate is a seasoned legal professional and educator with over 13 years of experience in law and higher education.
Known for her diverse expertise in various areas of law, her strong leadership skills, and her commitment to educational excellence, Michanna has dedicated her career to serving clients and educating future professionals.
Michanna Tate’s academic journey began with a focus on Biology, earning her Bachelor of Science and Master of Science degrees in Biology from Howard University.
After gaining a solid foundation in the sciences, by working as a Molecular Microbiologist and a Science Professor, Michanna's career took a turn toward law.
She earned her Juris Doctor degree from Stetson University College of Law in 2011, solidifying her passion for legal practice.
With a legal career spanning more than a decade, Michanna has worked in various capacities, from private practice to governmental roles.
As Managing Attorney at Access Law LLC in Greenville, SC, she became known for her comprehensive legal services across multiple areas, including real estate litigation, estate planning, personal injury, and medical malpractice.
She has built a reputation for client-centered solutions and effective management.
In addition to her work in private practice, Michanna has held roles that contributed to public safety and the legal community.
As an Attorney for the South Carolina Department of Public Safety, she worked alongside the Highway Patrol and State Transport Police, handling prosecutions and offering legal advice.
She also facilitated training and worked as an Employee Advisor for the Office of Professional Responsibility, offering guidance during investigations.
In addition to her legal work, Michanna has made a significant impact in higher education, where she has taught and mentored students for more than a decade.
She has taught courses in Paralegal Studies, Biology, and Business at various institutions, to include Greenville Tech, USC Upstate, and Howard University.
Michanna’s teaching philosophy centers on providing real-world knowledge and fostering critical thinking, preparing students for successful careers in their chosen fields.
Michanna holds a variety of professional licenses and certifications that complement her legal and educational expertise.
She is a licensed attorney in South Carolina, having been admitted to the State Bar in 2012.
She is also licensed to practice in the U.S.
District Court for South Carolina and the U.S.
Court of Appeals for the 4th Circuit in Richmond, VA.
