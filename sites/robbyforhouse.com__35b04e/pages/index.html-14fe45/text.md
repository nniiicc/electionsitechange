Donate Today Election Day is Tuesday, November 3 Early voting October 19–31 · Check your registration Meet Robby Robbins Robby Robbins is from here.
He grew up in Reevesville, Walterboro, and St.
George, where his parents, Doug and Nancy, raised him on hard work and service.
His grandfathers showed him what public service looks like — Billy Moorer as a longtime magistrate, Jack Robbins as St.
George's police chief.
After law school, Robby came home in 1987 and opened a law practice in St.
George.
He helped start the Tri-County Regional Chamber of Commerce, then spent seven years as a prosecutor and Deputy Solicitor putting violent offenders behind bars.
In 2004, Governor Mark Sanford appointed him First Circuit Solicitor.
He went on to spend a decade on the Dorchester County Sales Tax Transportation Authority and six years on the South Carolina Department of Transportation Commission, serving as its chairman — work behind the road projects families in this district drive every day.
Robby and his wife, Mary Bryce, have two children, Bryce and Lena.
