Our recent representation in this seat have spectacularly failed in this duty.
This means that beyond doing that job competently which I vow to do, I must also help us to focus on learning the lessons offered to us by the calamitous failures we have invited into leadership.
These men, and sometimes women, have begun for us an experiment we should never have run.
We should, however, learn the lessons offered to us through these failures and work to prevent them moving forward.
I do not know if it is too late to reverse the damage done yet.
But never in our lifetimes have we had the opportunity to so boldly begin again laying new foundations for a country built on the guarantees of Life, Liberty, and the Pursuit of Happiness.
