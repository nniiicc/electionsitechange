Great question!
This can be confusing, especially since we just had a redistricting (redrawing of district boundaries).
Go to https://www.legmt.gov/districts/ to see a map and information about incumbent legislators.
You can check your voter registration status and register to vote in multiple ways; the Secretary of State's website is a great one stop shop: https://votemt.gov/voter-registration/
You cannot register online within 30 days of the next election, but you can register and vote at your county election office until 8pm on Election Day!
Check out https://www.catalystmt.org/voters !
They also have a lot of great resources to help people engage in their civic duty.
