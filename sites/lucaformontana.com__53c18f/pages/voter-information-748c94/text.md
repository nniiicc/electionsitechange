Do I live where I can vote for Luca?
Great question!
This can be confusing, especially since we just had a redistricting (redrawing of district boundaries).
Go to https://www.legmt.gov/districts/ to see a map and information about incumbent legislators.
Am I registered to vote?
How do I register?
You can check your voter registration status and register to vote in multiple ways; the Secretary of State's website is a great one stop shop: https://votemt.gov/voter-registration/ You cannot register online within # days of the next election, but you can register and vote at your county election office until 8pm on Election Day!
I have more questions about voting.
Check out https://www.catalystmt.org/voters !
They also have a lot of great resources to help people engage in their civic duty.
