Meet Precious
A Voice for District 51
Precious McKenzie has spent her career in education and fundraising for local non-profit organizations.
A Billings resident since 2011, Precious understands the challenges Montanans face — from rising housing and healthcare costs, to the challenges facing public schools and building an economy that works for everyone.
Precious wants to create a safe and secure Montana for all.
Precious has spent over a decade as a small business advocate and will work to ensure that small businesses can thrive.
“Montana families deserve a Representative who will work to uphold the Constitution and advocate for them.”
As an educator and parent, Precious understands the challenges teachers face.
She will work to support teachers and schools — so that all of Montana’s children have access to high-quality education to prepare them for the future.
Education is the key to future success.
Together, we are stronger.

Precious will support our public schools and make sure every student has the resources they need to thrive.
Healthcare is a right, not a privilege.
Precious supports protecting Medicaid expansion, lowering prescription drug costs, and ensuring rural Montanans have access to quality, affordable care close to home.
Montana’s economy should work for everyone.
Precious will champion livable wages, support small businesses, invest in workforce training, and create good-paying jobs right here in District 51.
Montana’s natural beauty is our greatest treasure.
Precious is committed to protecting clean air, clean water, and public lands.
Safe communities are built on trust and investment.
Precious supports first responders and will work hard so that they have the resources they need to keep everyone safe.
