MEET HIPOLITO
I was born and raised in Lindsay, California—a community that shaped my values, my work ethic, and my commitment to public service.
I am a proud graduate of the University of California, Davis, where I earned departmental honors in Science and Technology Studies, along with a minor in Management.
My commitment to serving the Central Valley began early.
I interned in the California State Senate, working on legislation that directly impacts our region.
As Mayor Pro Tem for the City of Lindsay, I also interned with the United States Conference of Mayors in Washington, D.C., where I collaborated with leaders from across the country to advocate for the equitable distribution of COVID-19 relief funding.
I furthered my policy training by earning a certificate in Public Policy through Harvard edX, an initiative of Harvard University.
I went on to serve four years on the Lindsay City Council, where I became the youngest mayor in California history.
During my time in office, I worked across party lines and served on numerous boards and committees focused on water quality, economic development, and government affairs.
I have represented the City of Lindsay at the state level, collaborating with local, state, and federal leaders to deliver results for our community.
My story is deeply personal.
I was raised by a single mother who worked in agriculture, and I help care for my brother with disabilities.
I come from a hardworking family, and those lived experiences have shaped my commitment to fight for families like mine—families who work hard, sacrifice, and deserve a fair shot.
I’m running to bring a new voice to District 33—one rooted in service, driven by results, and grounded in the real experiences of the people I seek to represent.
I am ready to get to work.
