Making a difference in the Texas State Senate requires a commitment to listening to constituents, understanding the diverse needs of our communities, and crafting legislation that drives meaningful progress.
It means championing policies that promote economic growth, public safety, quality education, and healthcare access, while maintaining fiscal responsibility.

Texas Economy
Texas is a land of vast opportunity, rich culture, and unmatched diversity, from the piney woods of East Texas to the deserts of West Texas, from the Gulf Coast to the Hill Country.
Our economy should reflect that greatness.
I believe in building a growing economy that benefits every Texan, not just the few.
That means reinvesting in our communities, supporting small businesses, expanding access to education and job training, and ensuring that every worker earns a living wage.
No Texan working fulltime should struggle to make ends meet.
I will fight for an increase in the minimum wage and advocate for policies that guarantee a living wage across the state.
When Texans thrive, our local economy thrives.
Together, we can build an economy that honors the hard work, ingenuity, and resilience of our people.
Let’s make Texas a place where prosperity is shared, opportunity is real, and no one is left behind.
Healthcare
Access to affordable healthcare is not a luxury, it’s a necessity.
Every Texan deserves quality care that’s close to home and within reach.
In rural communities, the lack of local healthcare options is a crisis we can no longer ignore.
I will fight to expand rural healthcare infrastructure, support local clinics, and ensure that no Texan has to drive hours just to see a doctor.
Women’s healthcare must be protected and prioritized.
From reproductive care to maternal health, we need policies that respect women’s autonomy and ensure access to comprehensive services.
It’s time to rethink our healthcare system.
I support exploring a hybrid model that blends the strengths of public and private healthcare, giving Texans more options, better coverage, and lower costs.
We can build a system that puts people over profits and health over bureaucracy.
Healthcare is a human right.
Let’s make it a reality for every Texan.
Public Education
Every child deserves access to a worldclass public education.
It's a strategic investment in the future of our communities, our economy, and our democracy.
To achieve this, we must prioritize the recruitment and retention of highly qualified, passionate teachers.
These educators are the backbone of our schools, shaping young minds and inspiring the next generation of leaders, innovators, and citizens.
Competitive salaries, professional development, and respect for their expertise are essential to attracting the best and brightest into the classroom.
Our children are not just students; they are our most precious resource.
Their potential is limitless, and our responsibility is to nurture it.
When we invest in public education, we invest in safer neighborhoods, stronger families, and a more equitable society.
Let’s commit to building a public education system that reflects the value we place on our children and the future they represent.
Because when we lift up our schools, we lift up everyone.
Universal Childcare
Universal childcare is not just a convenience; it's a necessity for today’s working families.
By making quality childcare accessible and affordable, we empower both parents to participate in the workforce, pursue career advancement, and contribute to the economy.
This support system enables families to build financial security, saving for college, retirement, emergencies, and even well-deserved vacations.
It also ensures that children are receiving quality nurturing, and early education that sets them up for success.
Investing in universal childcare is an investment in our future.
It strengthens families, boosts economic productivity, and creates a more equitable society for all.
