Meet John Stegner on his statewide ‘Do the Work’ Tour.
Hear his vision for Idaho, ask questions, and learn how you can help shape our state.
John Stegner believes leadership begins by showing up.
That's why he's traveling across Idaho on the Do the Work Tour—meeting people where they live, listening to their experiences, answering questions, and having real conversations about the future of our state.
Whether you've been following John's campaign for Governor from the beginning or you're simply curious to learn more, you're invited to join the conversation.
John is a lifelong Idahoan, former Idaho Supreme Court Justice, husband, father, and independent candidate for Governor.
Throughout his career, he’s made decisions based on the Constitution, the facts, and what’s right–not because of political pressure.
Families across our state are asking whether they can afford to stay in Idaho, build a career here, raise a family here, access healthcare here, and preserve the communities they love.
He’s running for Governor because those elected to listen and serve Idahoans aren’t showing up in the way they should.
John believes Idaho can do better—but only if we're willing to do the work together.
During this event, you'll hear John's vision for Idaho, have an opportunity to ask questions, and learn more about the campaign's plans for the months ahead.
You'll also discover ways to become involved—whether that's volunteering, helping organize in your community, hosting neighbors for a conversation, or simply staying connected as the campaign grows.
We hope you’ll join us in Grangeville or at another stop on the Do the Work Tour.
