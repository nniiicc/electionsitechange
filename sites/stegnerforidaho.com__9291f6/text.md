I’m John Stegner.
I’m a lifelong Idahoan, former Idaho Supreme Court Justice, and principled leader running for Governor because I believe Idaho can do better.
Why I’m Running Idaho is worth fighting for.
Across our state, families are asking whether they can afford to stay here, build a career, raise a family, and access the healthcare and opportunities they need.
For many, the answer is no.
Too many of our elected officials have stopped representing the people of Idaho.
They ignore the rule of law and pretend following the Constitution is optional.
I spent my entire career upholding the Constitution.
It’s a responsibility I carried as a judge and as a Justice on the Idaho Supreme Court, and why I am running for Governor.
I am not a career politician and I am not affiliated with a party.
I answer to the people.
Standing on Principle Throughout my career, I've made decisions based on the Constitution, the facts, and what I believed was right.
Not political pressure.
Sometimes that meant standing alone, apart from others.
But leadership isn't about doing what's easy.
It's about doing what's right.
As Governor, I'll bring that same commitment to serving the people of Idaho.
Meet John Focused on What Matters Affordability Families should be thriving in our state, not struggling to make ends meet.
Idahoans didn’t create the affordability crisis, but they deserve a governor focused on fixing it.
Healthcare Access Idaho families deserve access to quality healthcare.
We must address the challenges facing our healthcare system and strengthen access across our state.
Education & Opportunity Every Idaho family should have confidence that the next generation can build a future here and pursue opportunities close to home.
We have an obligation to properly fund our public schools.
Public Lands Retaining and maintaining public ownership of Idaho’s public lands isn’t optional; it’s the point of public lands.
Idaho isn’t for sale.
Water Rights Inaction over Idaho’s water shortage has Idaho families and farmers paying the price.
We need long-term solutions that ensure we protect this vital resource.
Data Centers Future growth should serve Idahoans, and if it doesn’t, it should go elsewhere.
Idaho families should get to decide whether or not data centers get built in their neighborhoods, not billion-dollar companies and their shareholders.
Constitutional Leadership Government exists to serve the people—not the other way around.
Upholding the Constitution and protecting individual rights must remain at the center of leadership.
Strong Communities Strong communities are built by strong families, local leadership, and a government that listens to the people it serves.
