Campaign Organizing Fellowship The Siffert for Assembly Fellowship is a hands-on leadership opportunity for students and young professionals interested in politics, public service, and community organizing.
Fellows will play a direct role in a competitive New York State Assembly campaign while gaining practical experience in grassroots political work.
This position is unpaid.
Over four months, fellows will work alongside campaign leadership to engage voters, build community relationships, and help shape a people-powered movement in District 66.
