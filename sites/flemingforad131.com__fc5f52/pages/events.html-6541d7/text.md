Billsboro will host a Meet-and-Greet for all candidates next Wednesday, March 11, 2026, from 6 to 7 PM at Billsboro Winery, 4760 W Lake Road, Geneva, NY.

- State Senator (NY-54): Michael Mills and Scott Comegys
