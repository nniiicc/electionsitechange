What I stand for and how I will represent the constituents of AD131
My top issues are:
Public school education, community engagement, immigration rights (pro-immigrant but definitely anti-ICE as a personal military machine for the current president of the United States), behavioral health, agricultural economics, public transportation in rural areas, and economic development.
On Day 1, I make a pledge to the constituents of AD131 to:
- Work with school administrators at school districts who don’t have adequate numbers of school psychologists, social workers, and guidance counselors to secure funding to hire and retain mental health professionals in public schools that need the funding.
- Ensure that there is funding to help finance public transportation systems and expansion initiatives in the AD131 region.
- Work with community advocacy groups to keep ICE agents at bay and to protect immigrants who live, work, and visit the AD131 region from ICE invasions.
- Work with the 2026 awardees of the clubhouse funding – East House (Monroe County) and Unity House (Cayuga County) – to ensure that interested constituents of AD131 have adequate access to these two new clubhouses. https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-announces-63-million-awarded-establish-clubhouse-programs
- Ensure there are adequate and accessible commercial establishments for people to buy everyday goods and services.
- Do an inventory of behavioral health facilities and programs in the area, identify gaps, and determine how to increase behavioral health supports in AD131, including peer support services, trainings, etc.
- Work with community advocates to close down toxic dumps and landfills – especially Seneca Meadow, Inc. – and consider alternatives such as Reduce, Reuse, Recycle, and Composting to cut down on waste and trash in AD131.
Make it financially unfeasible for New York City to keep shipping its trash to the Finger Lakes Region, but also work with the Mamdani administration to create its own waste management system.
- Work with constituents to increase community engagement using tools like in-person appearances, email, social media, and video conferencing platforms.
- Open a second satellite constituency office outside of the Geneva District Office and the Moravia Satellite Office that is accessible to ALL residents in AD131.
Mobile pop-up offices will also be created for constituents’ convenience.
- Work diligently with like-minded Assembly Members and Senators to pass the New York Health Act (Assembly A.1466/Senate S.3425) https://www.news10.com/news/new-york-health-act/, https://thehill.com/opinion/healthcare/5897530-universal-healthcare-reform-new-york/, https://www.nyhcampaign.org/aboutnyha, and https://www.nysenate.gov/sites/default/files/article/attachment/5024_jackson_nyha_brochure.pdf
