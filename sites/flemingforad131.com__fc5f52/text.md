What this campaign is all about
- Encourage and promote community engagement in-person, virtually, and hybrid with the input of as many people, institutions, organizations, and programs as possible.
- Restructuring the healthcare system so that constituents can afford to pay for healthcare coverage.
- Adequate and effective mental health and substance use recovery services and supports that are accessible to all constituents.
This including introducing the peer support model to constituents as a viable and cost-effect model option of recovery than the traditional model of behavioral health recovery.
- Protecting immigrants by severely limiting the influence and activities of the Immigration and Customs Enforcement agency in New York State and in AD131.
- Ensuring that local public transportation systems in AD131 serve the needs of ALL constituents and visitors who ride the buses and that these systems are long-term sustainable.
- Strengthening our local public school districts by providing resources to hire and retain school personnel, especially special education teachers, school psychologists, social workers, and guidance counselors.
- Protecting natural resources from pollution and destruction by closing down landfills and waste dump sites that are hazardous to human beings and pose threats to economic development and sustainability.
- Being visible and available to ALL constituents in AD 131.
