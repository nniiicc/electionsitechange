This community is not just where Kimberly Fisher lives—it's who she is.
For more than 37 years, Kimberly has called the 11th House District home.
She pays taxes here and faces the same challenges as her neighbors—high utility bills, costly car insurance, limited access to resources, and systems that too often forget the people they were built to serve.
Her family's roots run even deeper.
For over 90 years, generations of Fishers and Millers have lived, worked, worshipped, and served right here.
Detroit Public Schools shaped them.
Union jobs sustained them.
Faith anchored them.
Service defined them.
And now, Kimberly is stepping forward to give back to the place that raised her.
