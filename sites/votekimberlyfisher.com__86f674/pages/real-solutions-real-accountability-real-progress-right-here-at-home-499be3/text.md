Real Accountability.
Real Progress Right Here at Home.
April 17, 2026 • 2 min read Kimberly E.
Fisher's priorities are grounded in the everyday realities of the people she hopes to serve.
They come from real conversations, real concerns, and real experiences shared by residents across Detroit and Warren.
These are not distant policy ideas or empty campaign promises.
They are kitchen-table issues — the kind families discuss when utility bills keep rising, neighborhoods feel less safe, jobs feel uncertain, and basic infrastructure continues to be overlooked.
Kimberly has spent time listening to residents, walking alongside them, and understanding what matters most to parents, seniors, workers, and small-business owners throughout the district.
In many cases, she is not just hearing these concerns secondhand — she is living them too.
That is what makes her campaign different.
Kimberly is running because the people of this district deserve leadership that is responsive, accountable, and focused on results.
She believes residents should not have to fight to be seen, heard, or prioritized by the people elected to represent them.
From addressing rising utility costs and strengthening public safety to supporting economic opportunity and demanding better for neglected communities, Kimberly is committed to turning lived experiences into meaningful action.
Her campaign is rooted in the belief that progress starts at home — with leaders who know the community, understand its challenges, and are ready to do the work.
This is about real solutions.
Real accountability.
And real progress, right here at home.
