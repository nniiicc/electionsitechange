We serve constituents across House District 11, Michigan — including older adults and those using assistive technologies — and we take that responsibility seriously.
We are continuously working to apply relevant accessibility standards and improve the experience for every visitor.
This commitment reflects guidance from the U.S.
Department of Justice that online services and communications should be accessible to people with disabilities, and aligns with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) published by the World Wide Web Consortium.
Our Accessibility Goal We aim to conform as closely as possible to WCAG 2.2 Level AA — the current W3C standard — organized around content being perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.
Accessibility Features We Strive to Support This site is built with accessibility in mind.
We aim to provide: clear heading structure and readable page layouts meaningful alternative text for important images keyboard-accessible navigation and interactive elements sufficient color contrast across all page sections, including our campaign color palette descriptive links and buttons accessible forms with proper labels legible font sizing with scalable options to support older visitors and low-vision users responsive design that works across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices Ongoing Efforts Accessibility is an ongoing process.
We continually review content structure, color contrast, alternative text, keyboard navigation, link clarity, form usability, and compatibility with screen readers and other assistive technologies.
Need Assistance or Found a Barrier?
If you experience difficulty accessing any part of this website, encounter a barrier, or need information in an alternative format, please reach out and include the page URL and a brief description of the issue.
