My philosophy is rooted in cultivation: whatever we are blessed to acquire should be multiplied in the lives of others.
Anchored in Detroit's entrepreneurial and civic landscape, I have spent years serving as a connector between community, business, and government.
I believe deeply in vision paired with follow-through, and faith paired with action.
Leadership, to me, is about listening first and delivering consistently.
As the founder and owner of Lily's & Elise Tea House, Michigan's only Black woman-owned tea house, I created more than a business.
Located on Detroit's historic Avenue of Fashion, the tea house is a place of gathering, employment, mentorship, and healing.
Through this work, I understand firsthand the challenges small businesses and families face—rising utility costs, workforce shortages, and the need for smart, supportive public policy.
