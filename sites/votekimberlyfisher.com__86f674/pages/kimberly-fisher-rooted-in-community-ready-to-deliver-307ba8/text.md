During her time in Wayne County government, Kimberly served in a senior community engagement role where she helped lead countywide initiatives and supported neighborhood-centered problem-solving.
In that work, she partnered closely with residents, community organizations, and public leaders to build stronger relationships, uplift community voices, and move long-overdue projects forward.
That experience gave Kimberly a close-up view of how government works — and where it too often falls short.
It also strengthened her belief that public service must be rooted in accountability, responsiveness, and results for everyday people.
Kimberly's work has also extended beyond Wayne County.
Through collaboration with leaders across Macomb County, she has seen firsthand how families, jobs, transportation, and economic challenges do not stop at county borders.
That perspective has reinforced the need for thoughtful, connected leadership that reflects how people actually live.
Grounded in faith and guided by purpose, Kimberly is running to tackle the issues that matter most to working families: rising utility costs, housing stability, support for small businesses, and safer communities built through prevention and opportunity.
For Kimberly Fisher, this campaign is about more than winning a seat.
It is about building a movement for meaningful change, lasting progress, and renewed opportunity throughout the 11th House District.
She believes leadership should be present, principled, and productive.
It should show up, stay rooted in the community, and deliver real results.
And Kimberly Fisher is ready to do exactly that for the community that raised her.
