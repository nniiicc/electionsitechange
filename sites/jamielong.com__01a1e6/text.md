DONATE Jamie’s vision: A brighter future for all Minnesotans I believe that through the power of grassroots advocacy and progressive policy, we can combat the threat of climate change, safeguard democracy, strengthen economic stability, protect equal rights, and increase opportunities in education and healthcare for all Minnesotans.
Climate and Environment With bold action, we can reduce carbon energy usage, prioritize clean vehicles, and protect natural treasures, including the Boundary Waters.
Democracy From voting rights and voting power to defending Minnesotans against ICE, it’s time to safeguard our Constitutional rights.
Education and Childcare Let’s invest in universal early learning, prioritize equity and literacy in Minnesota’s schools, and work toward debt-free college education.
Gun Violence Prevention Gun violence is now the #1 cause of death in children and teens.
We owe it to our kids to ban weapons of war and high capacity magazines and keep them safe.
Housing Every Minnesotan deserves access to a dignified, affordable place to call home.
Healthcare Every Minnesotan, regardless of background or income, deserves affordable and comprehensive healthcare coverage.
Labor From raising the minimum wage, to offering paid leave for workers, to combating wage theft, it’s time for us to prioritize worker’s rights.
Criminal Justice Reform Let’s work toward a restorative criminal justice system with a goal of reducing crime and recidivism.
