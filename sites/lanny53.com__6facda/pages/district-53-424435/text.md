DISTRICT 53
ABOUT GEORGIA SENATE DISTRICT 53
Georgia Senate District 53 represents some of the most hardworking and close-knit communities in Northwest Georgia.
From small towns and rural farmland to growing local industries, District 53 is built on strong families, deep roots, and a commitment to community.
Lanny Thomas has lived in District 53 his entire life and is proud to call this region home.
He understands firsthand the challenges and opportunities facing our district because he has lived it.
COUNTIES & COMMUNITIES SERVED
Georgia Senate District 53 includes all or parts of the following counties:
- Chattooga County
- Walker County
- Dade County
- Catoosa County
- North Floyd County
These communities share common values — hard work, faith, family, and a belief that local voices should matter in state government.
WHAT MAKES DISTRICT 53 UNIQUE
District 53 is defined by:
- Strong agricultural traditions
- Manufacturing and textile industries
- Small businesses that anchor local economies
- Rural communities with limited infrastructure access
- Families who value education, safety, and local control
Our district faces different challenges than metro Atlanta and other metropolitans in Georgia.
The people deserves representation that understands rural Georgia.
They deserve someone who will represent them in Atlanta.
KEY ISSUES FACING DISTRICT 53
While every community is unique, voters across District 53 consistently care about:
- Economic growth and job stability
- Support for local farmers and businesses
- Rural broadband and infrastructure
- Access to healthcare and emergency services
- Strong public safety and law enforcement
- Protecting local schools and parental rights
Lanny’s priorities are shaped by decades of service in communities just like these.
