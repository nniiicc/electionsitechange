Vote by Mail (Absentee Ballot)
Registered Georgians have the option to vote by absentee ballot.
You must request an absentee ballot in order to receive one in the mail.
Absentee ballots are an option for registered voters who prefer not to vote in person.
You do not need an excuse to request an absentee ballot.
Your County Registrar's Office must receive your ballot before the polls close on Election Day to be counted.
