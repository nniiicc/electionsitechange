Six priorities driving Robert's campaign for Florida House District 37 — grounded in 20+ years of service to Seminole and Orange County families.
Tallahassee should work for the families of District 37 — not the other way around.
These are the fights Robert is running to win for every neighbor across Seminole and Orange counties.
Cost of Living An Affordable Central Florida Having raised a family on a teacher's salary, Robert knows firsthand how families are being squeezed on every side — rising housing costs, sky-high property insurance, tolls that keep climbing, and a tax burden that hits working families hardest.
Robert will fight to bring real relief back to Central Florida. ▸ Housing affordability and expanding responsible home ownership ▸ Property insurance reform — cracking down on carrier abuse ▸ Toll relief for daily commuters ▸ Tax relief that puts money back in family budgets Education Quality Schools for Every Student As a 20-year Orange County Public Schools educator serving families across Seminole and Orange counties, Robert has been in the trenches.
He knows what our teachers, students, and parents need — and it's not another one-size-fits-all mandate from Tallahassee.
He'll fight for schools that work for families. ▸ Real support and competitive pay for teachers ▸ Expanded vocational and career-tech pathways ▸ Focus on student achievement and literacy ▸ Educational choice that empowers parents Mental Health Mental Health & Recovery Support Mental health is the crisis behind almost every other crisis — from schools to public safety to homelessness.
Robert has spent his career walking beside students and families through it, and he'll bring that experience to the Florida House. ▸ Expanding access to mental health care ▸ Substance abuse prevention and recovery support ▸ Stronger family and youth services ▸ Coordinated crisis response for our communities Community & Growth Protecting Our Communities Seminole and Orange counties aren't just addresses — they're neighborhoods, ranches, parks, lakes and small towns that generations of families call home.
Robert will fight to keep this place worth calling home. ▸ Defend Orange and Seminole counties' rural boundaries ▸ Environmental and water conservation ▸ Smart growth that respects the community ▸ Infrastructure that keeps up with growth Public Safety Safer Streets.
