Robert Prater is the Republican candidate for Florida House of Representatives, District 37, on the November 3, 2026 ballot in Seminole and Orange Counties.
He is a husband, father, and grandfather who spent more than 20 years with Orange County Public Schools as a classroom teacher, Dean of Students, and educational leader.
What office is Robert Prater running for?
Robert Prater is running for Florida State Representative in House District 37, which covers parts of Seminole and Orange Counties.
The seat is open because Representative Susan Plasencia is retiring.
When is the election?
The general election is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Voters in District 37 can vote by mail, vote early, or vote at their polling place on Election Day.
Where does Robert Prater stand on the cost of living?
Having raised a family on a teacher's salary, Robert Prater knows how housing costs, property insurance, tolls, and taxes are squeezing Central Florida families.
He supports expanding housing affordability, reforming property insurance, toll relief, and tax relief for working families.
Where does Robert Prater stand on public schools?
As a 20 year Orange County Public Schools educator serving families across Seminole and Orange Counties, Robert Prater believes schools need practical support rather than mandates from Tallahassee.
He supports competitive teacher pay, expanded vocational and career technical options, stronger literacy and student achievement, and educational choice for parents.
Where does Robert Prater stand on mental health and substance abuse?
Robert Prater calls mental health the crisis behind almost every other crisis, from schools to public safety to homelessness.
He supports expanding access to mental health care, substance abuse prevention, stronger family services, and coordinated crisis response.
Where does Robert Prater stand on growth and rural boundaries?
Robert Prater supports defending Orange and Seminole Counties' rural boundaries and protecting the environment.
He favors smart growth that respects the character of existing neighborhoods and infrastructure that keeps pace with development.
Where does Robert Prater stand on public safety?
Robert Prater backs law enforcement and supports full funding for firefighters and EMS.
He supports tougher penalties for fentanyl trafficking and safer schools and neighborhoods.
Where does Robert Prater stand on family and faith?
Robert Prater supports parents' rights and common sense government grounded in faith and family.
He is committed to protecting seniors and veterans and to servant leadership that puts the community first.
Who has endorsed Robert Prater?
Robert Prater is endorsed by the Florida State Fraternal Order of Police, Associated Builders and Contractors Central Florida, BusinessForce, and the Central Florida Hotel and Lodging Association.
