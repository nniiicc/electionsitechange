Senate Witness Sign-Up Guide
To testify in favor or against a bill, you must sign up 48 business hours prior to the hearing of the bill 8am–6pm EST.
For example, a Wednesday bill hearing would require a Monday 8am-6pm sign up time.
HOW TO TESTIFY:
- All bill hearings will be held in person with a virtual oral testimony option (via Zoom) and streamed live on YouTube.
- Oral testimony sign-up and written testimony (must be a PDF document) are submitted using MyMGA on the Maryland General Assembly website found here: https://mgaleg.maryland.gov/mgawebsite/Account/Login.
If you submit written testimony, it does not have to match oral testimony, it should be shortened to 2 minutes.
- Sign in or create a MyMGA account online.
