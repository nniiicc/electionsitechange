Absentee Ballot Voting
Everyone is able to vote by mail in Connecticut.
You can request your absentee ballot online now: nedlamont.com/absentee
Completed absentee ballots must be returned to your local town clerk by 8:00 PM on Election Day.
They can be mailed, not postmarked, to arrive before or by Election Day or you can drop it off at your town’s secure ballot drop box.
Find your ballot drop box location here.
Early Voting - Oct. 19 - Nov. 1
Early voting is in person from Monday, October 19 - Sunday, November 1 from 10 AM to 6 PM each day.
There are extended voting hours on Tuesday, October 27 and Thursday, October 29 from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
