Fall Campaign Intern
Are you a motivated, reliable, and team-motivated high school or college student looking to learn about the electoral process and gain political campaign experience?
Governor Ned Lamont’s 2026 re-election campaign is seeking Fall Campaign Interns!
This role is ideal for those with little to no campaign experience interested in learning about the electoral process or are interested in pursuing a career in politics.
Applications will be accepted on a rolling basis until all available positions are filled.
The internship program will run from September 1st - November 6th, 2026.
Interns are expected to participate in their internships in the office and in the field - remote opportunities are not available.
Key Responsibilities
- Implement and execute the campaign’s field plan at the local level and regional level
- Participate in voter contact initiatives including canvassing, phone banking, texting, voter registration, and constituency mobilization, reporting to Field Organizers and Regional Organizing Directors
- Ensure data integrity
- Execute planned field events and support on-the-ground visibility efforts
- Staff and support campaign events, press conferences, and candidate appearances as assigned.
Qualifications
- Valid driver’s license and access to reliable transportation
- Availability to work weekends
- Strong Commitment to Democratic values
- Interest in politics, public policy, or electoral campaigns
- Ability to work in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment with attention to detail
- Strong organizational skills and willingness to take initiative
- Prior campaign, political, or communications knowledge is a plus but not required
Compensation & Benefits
- $18/hr for 10 hours of work per week
- First-Hand exposure to how statewide campaigns operate
How to Apply
- Applications will be accepted on a rolling basis, with internships beginning September 1st, 2026
