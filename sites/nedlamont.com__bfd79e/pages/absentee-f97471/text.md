Connecticut's Official Online Absentee Ballot Application Connecticut Online Absentee Ballot Request Portal Welcome to the Connecticut online absentee ballot request portal!
Any eligible voter can use this system to request an absentee ballot.
The portal opens 45 days before each regular primary and general election.
It opens on September 19, 2026, for the November 3, 2026, general election.
The portal also opens on a town-by-town basis for local special elections.
To receive your absentee ballot, please complete, sign, and submit an application online.
Please provide your name, date of birth, and town as it appears in the voter record in order to look up and verify your voter information.
Browser Notice: This portal may not function properly on certain versions of Apple Safari (Mac, iPhone, and iPad) .
If you experience loading issues or errors, please switch to other alternative like Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge .
