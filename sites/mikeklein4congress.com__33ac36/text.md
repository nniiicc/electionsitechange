Endorsed by See all endorsements Something is deeply wrong in America.
Everyone knows it.
Today’s America is defined by its divided, angry, and anxious population, locked in an endless blame game with the “other side”.
Our economy, our leadership and our country’s place in the world has diminished in ways most of us never imagined.
Here in North Florida the signs are everywhere.
You feel it at the grocery register, in the insurance premiums, at the gas pump, in the slow shock of what it costs just to keep a household running.
Mike Klein, carries a conviction that this is not who we are.
His story, and North Florida’s, is one rooted in something deeper than the dysfunctional polarization of our nation.
A fifth-generation Floridian raised in Gainesville in the 1970s, Mike’s childhood was all sun-scorched summers at Crescent Beach, Camp Crystal Lake, horse riding near Kanapaha, fantastic music, barbeque sandwiches at Chiapini’s, and long afternoons tubing the Ichetucknee.
No rules, no shoes, no reason to believe that society would ever feel this disconnected.
Values The same oath he took at 17 still drives him today, to defend the Constitution and serve the American people.
As he often says, his great-grandmother from Glen St.
Mary would expect nothing less.
His campaign is built on three values: Respect For people, for service, for each other, even in disagreement.
Resilience The strength to endure, rebuild, and move forward together.
Responsibility To lead with integrity, to be accountable, and to put people before politics.
Join The Movement Mike Klein’s campaign for Florida’s 3rd Congressional District is a movement built on principle, independence, and a genuine belief that the people of North Florida deserve a representative who fights for them, not for party bosses or corporate donors.
