CONTRIBUTE PILLAR 1 Defending Democracy Constitutional rights are not guaranteed — they must be actively defended by those sent to Washington.
Democracy is more than an election.
It is the daily work of protecting rights, ensuring accountability, and keeping power out of the hands of the corrupt and self-dealing.
Mike Klein believes that a functioning democracy requires structural reform — from the ballot box to the halls of Congress — and that every citizen deserves effective, fair representation.
Protect Constitutional Rights Defend the right to assemble and the right to vote — these are not partisan issues.
They are the foundation of a free society.
Transparency & Accountability Demand full transparency and accountability in government.
No stock trading.
Merit-based spending — not driven by special interests.
ICE Out — Respect Due Process Redirect resources to professional law enforcement.
Every person on American soil is entitled to due process under the law.
Ban Partisan Gerrymandering End the manipulation of district boundaries.
Voters must choose their representatives, not the other way around.
Money Out of Politics Crush corruption and self-dealing.
Ban corporate PACs and dark money that distort elections and undermine the public trust.
PILLAR 2 Strength Through Peace Real strength is not the willingness to start wars.
It is the wisdom to avoid them, and the courage to lead with integrity on the world stage.
America is strongest when it leads with diplomacy, acts within the law both at home and abroad, and channels its power into respect rather than fear.
Mike Klein believes that war must always be a last resort — never a first instinct — and that the men and women who serve deserve a government that supports them as more than political props.
Global Leadership with Integrity Champion diplomacy and principled engagement.
War is a last resort — not a tool of political convenience or executive ego.
Adhere to the Law Uphold domestic and international law without exception.
No President or administration is above the rule of law.
Support Troops & Veterans Service members are not political pawns.
Fully fund veteran care, mental health services, and transition support.
National Interest Foreign Policy Foreign policy must serve the American people — not special interests, defense contractors, or foreign lobbying groups.
Invest in Soft Power Rebuild America's global reputation and influence through cultural diplomacy, foreign aid accountability, and multilateral leadership.
PILLAR 3 Economic Resilience A resilient economy starts local — with food on the table, sustainable energy, and businesses that keep dollars circulating in communities.
Economic security is not a Wall Street metric.
It is measured in family farms that stay in production, local manufacturers that keep people employed, and young workers who have a real pathway to a skilled career.
Mike Klein’s vision for economic resilience is built from the ground up — investing in local supply chains, protecting workers, and ensuring that Social Security delivers for those who earned it.
Energy & Food Security Invest in local energy production and agricultural capacity so communities are never held hostage by foreign supply chains or corporate consolidation.
Localization - Farming to Manufacturing Build resilient local supply chains from farm to factory floor.
Keep production, jobs, and dollars circulating within communities.
Strengthen Local Businesses Enforce antitrust rules so small businesses can compete.
Provide technical assistance so entrepreneurs can adopt new tools and thrive.
Rescue Social Security Raise the cap on Social Security contributions so the wealthy pay their fair share.
Protect the retirement security that working Americans have earned.
Youth Apprenticeships Expand apprenticeship programs to connect young workers with 21st century businesses.
Education as a Strategic Imperative Investment in America’s future to provide access to a 21st century, AI-driven economy.
PILLAR 4 Community Renewal Communities should be thriving — not just surviving.
That requires investment, purpose, and the courage to show up for their neighbors.
When markets and technology leave communities behind, government’s job is not to replace them, it is to invest in them.
Community renewal means rebuilding the institutions and infrastructure that bind people together: broadband access, local healthcare, public spaces, and above all, a shared sense of civic purpose.
A modern Civilian Conservation Corps is the boldest and most powerful tool available for rebuilding America from the ground up.
Modern CCC Champion a modern Civilian Conservation Corps — putting Americans to work restoring public lands, building infrastructure, and revitalizing rural communities.
Good jobs with real purpose.
Local Infrastructure Investment Invest in housing, ag-tech, rural healthcare clinics, clean water systems, and broadband.
These are not luxuries — they are the foundations of a functioning community.
Community Cohesion & Civic Discourse Rebuild the social fabric through investment in shared spaces, local institutions, and programs that bring people together across divides.
Digital Skills Training Equip every community member with the digital literacy skills needed to participate in a 21st century economy — leaving no one behind.
Support Arts, Music & Creative Industries A thriving community has culture at its heart.
Support local artists, musicians, and creative entrepreneurs as essential contributors to community life.
PILLAR 5 American Dream 2.0 The American Dream is not dead.
It needs a new architecture — rooted in access, fairness, and the freedom to create something of their own.
The American Dream has always meant that one’s effort and ingenuity determine their future, not their zip code, their parents’ wealth, or the arbitrary power of a monopoly.
American Dream 2.0 updates that promise: affordable healthcare, a basic income that enables innovation, genuine entrepreneurial freedom, fair reward for hard work, and communities where people don’t just survive, they thrive.
Affordable, Accessible Healthcare Break the prescription drug monopoly.
Every American deserves access to affordable healthcare — not rationed by wealth or corporate pricing power.
Basic Income for All Families A basic income floor drives innovation and empowers workers navigating an AI economy, giving every family the dignity and security to build toward something better.
Entrepreneurial Freedom Remove barriers to starting and growing a business.
Enforce antitrust, protect small competitors, and create the conditions for genuine economic freedom.
Fair Reward for Hard Work Ensure that perseverance and effort are rewarded, through fair wages, worker protections, and an economy that doesn't rig the game in favor of the already powerful.
Join The Movement Mike Klein’s campaign for Florida’s 3rd Congressional District is a movement built on principle, independence, and a genuine belief that the people of North Florida deserve a representative who fights for them, not for party bosses or corporate donors.
From Defending Democracy to American Dream 2.0, every pillar of this platform is designed to make Florida’s 3rd district stronger, fairer, and more resilient for every family, farmer, veteran, and worker who calls it home.
