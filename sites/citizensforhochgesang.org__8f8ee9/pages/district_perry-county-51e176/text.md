The wheel tax squeeze
Tell City adopted Ordinance 1239, a municipal wheel tax and vehicle excise surtax, on August 3, 2026.
For city-registered vehicles, the municipal taxes replace the county taxes rather than stacking on top of them.
Nobody from the public spoke for or against the ordinance at the hearing.
I will not turn that silence into support or opposition.
Accountability means publishing the exact rates, comparing what residents will pay, identifying the roads the money will support, and actively finding the people affected before the vote.
The larger problem is the state's road-funding scheme: Indianapolis made local vehicle taxes the price of full Community Crossings support and, beginning in 2028, continued eligibility.
