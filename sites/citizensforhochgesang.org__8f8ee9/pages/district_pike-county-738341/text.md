What should follow coal at Petersburg?
AES is putting about 1.1 billion dollars into Pike County.
The plant's last two coal units are being converted to natural gas: Unit 3 went offline for its conversion in February 2026, Unit 4 was scheduled to follow in June 2026, and AES plans to end coal at Petersburg entirely in 2026, the first Indiana investor owned utility to do it.
A 200 megawatt battery installation has been in service since March 2025, and the Petersburg Energy Center, 250 megawatts of solar with battery storage, entered service in February 2026.
The plant has long been the county's largest taxpayer, and the school district lost about $300,000 as assets shifted.
Plant families, miners, and Pike Central parents live with this transition every day.
