Inside the Box Episode 1 data centers government transparency local government Indiana Senate District 48 Posey County Pike County Dubois County This is Episode 1 of Inside the Box , Brad Hochgesang’s source-linked series about issues shaping Indiana Senate District 48.
The first episode examines how data center projects can move behind nondisclosure agreements before the public learns their names, water demands, power needs, tax arrangements, or long-term costs.
It follows examples from New Carlisle, Morgan County, Posey County, Pike County, and Dubois County.
