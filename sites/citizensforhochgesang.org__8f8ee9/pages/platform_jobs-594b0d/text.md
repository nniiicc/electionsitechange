Jobs & Small Business
I'm a small business owner.
I know what it takes.
We need to keep young people here with real opportunity, not empty promises.
I run Ninja Mojo LLC.
I know what it's like to make payroll, navigate regulations, and compete for talent.
Small businesses are the backbone of every community in District 48.
But we're losing young people.
All five non-Dubois counties are declining in population.
Median ages are well above the state average.
When young families leave, they take their spending, their energy, and their future with them.
I'll fight for workforce training aligned with actual employer needs, reduced friction for small business permitting, and economic development that builds on what our communities already have — not imported mega-projects that benefit outside investors.
