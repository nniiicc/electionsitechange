Pike County Petersburg housing Premier Property Management announced plans for Prides Creek Apartments, a 64-unit market-rate development at 4225 North Buchta Drive in Petersburg.
Dubois County Free Press reported the announcement on September 15.
The plans call for 32 one-bedroom and 32 two-bedroom apartments.
A September 16 groundbreaking was scheduled, with construction expected to begin during September.
Rental rates and a projected completion date had not been finalized.
The announcement does not establish that construction has begun or that units will meet a particular affordability standard.
