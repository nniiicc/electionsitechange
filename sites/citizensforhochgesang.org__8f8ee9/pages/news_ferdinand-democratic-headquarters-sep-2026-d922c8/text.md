Dubois Democrats announce Ferdinand headquarters
The county party announced a headquarters at 1245 Main Street in Ferdinand for meetings, candidate events, and volunteer activities.
The Dubois County Democratic Party announced a new headquarters at 1245 Main Street in Ferdinand, at Main and 13th streets.
A September 14 announcement carried by Dubois County Free Press scheduled the opening celebration for September 16.
The announcement describes the restored property as a place for community gatherings, volunteer work, candidate events, and party operations.
It credits Keith Fritz with making the space available.
This summary reports the announcement; an opening-day recap and regular public hours have not yet been verified.
