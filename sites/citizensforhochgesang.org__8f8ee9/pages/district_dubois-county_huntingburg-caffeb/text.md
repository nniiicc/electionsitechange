AES is building the Crossvine solar and battery storage project between Huntingburg and Holland.
The battery system would be among the largest in Indiana, and it sits within about two miles of Holland Elementary and the Southridge schools.
Residents raised permit discrepancies and safety questions at public meetings through 2025 and 2026, and commissioners said the current ordinance gives the county little room to act.
Construction was scheduled to begin in March 2026, with operation targeted for late 2027.
