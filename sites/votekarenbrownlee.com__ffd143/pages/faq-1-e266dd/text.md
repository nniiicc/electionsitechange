Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.
Early voting begins October 6.
Here’s all your voting information:
- All eligible voters must be properly registered by October 5, 2026, to cast a ballot in the November Election.
If you have changed your name, recently moved, or have other issues to resolve, update your registration before the October 5 deadline.
- Anyone who is registered to vote in Ohio and resides in Hamilton County can come to the Board of Elections Office located at 4700 Smith Road, Cincinnati, Ohio 45212-9002 and cast their vote early.
- All registered, eligible Ohioans are able to vote by mail, without excuse.
Laws have changed regarding absentee and mail-in voting since 2025.
Make sure you’re prepared!
Mail-in ballots must now be received by your Board of Elections by 7:30 pm ON ELECTION DAY.
To ensure your vote arrives on time and is counted, you should do the following:
- Submit your ballot request here as soon as possible.
- Allow time for your Board of Elections to process your request.
- When you receive your ballot, complete and return it right away.
- You can return it via mail or to the Hamilton County Board of Elections Ballot Dropbox.
- Get out and vote!
Don’t forget the following for Election Day:
- Create, update, or check your registration following the steps above.
- Ensure you bring the proper form of ID.
More details here on the types of ID that are accepted at the polls.
- Polls are open 6:30 am -7:30 pm.
