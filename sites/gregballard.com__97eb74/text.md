Sadly, it’s common for Indiana Secretaries of State to endorse, fundraise for, and donate to candidates who appear on the very ballots they oversee.
Imagine running for office and seeing the official responsible for administering your election publicly backing your opponent.
That’s wrong.

Listen Wherever You Get Your Podcasts Get to know Greg Ballard, the former two-term Republican mayor of Indianapolis who is now running for Indiana Secretary of State as an independent.
In a series of interviews, he recounts his childhood in Indianapolis, time in the Marines (including the first Gulf War), his underdog campaign for mayor, and how he handled the biggest crises he faced in office.
Greg Ballard never planned to run for office.
After 23 years as a Marine, serving in war and peace, he returned home to Indianapolis with his wife Winnie to build a career in the private sector and raise their family.
He ran without financial backing or serious political connections.
He wasn’t part of the establishment.
He just believed Indiana’s capital city could do better...
