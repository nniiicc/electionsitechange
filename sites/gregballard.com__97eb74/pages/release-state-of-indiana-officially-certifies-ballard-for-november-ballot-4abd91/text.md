INDIANAPOLIS – The State of Indiana today notified the Greg Ballard campaign that he is officially certified to be on the November ballot in the Indiana Secretary of State race.
The former Indianapolis Mayor is running as an independent under the Lincoln Party banner.
Here is video of the Mayor learning the news.
“It’s good to officially be on the ballot.
It took a lot of hard work from people across Indiana to reach this historic moment,” said Ballard.
“We are pleasantly surprised at how quickly the certification happened, and I want to thank the Indiana Election Division staff for their time and effort in counting all our signatures.
It is truly special to be part of this movement.
See you on the trail.”
You can find the letter here from the State certifying 40,361 petition signatures to enable Ballard to be on November’s ballot.
