About Me
Dear Neighbor,
I am a fourth generation Central New Yorker running to be your next Senator because I have hope for our home.
I grew up here, right in Cicero.
I stayed local for school, graduating from Cicero-North Syracuse High School and Le Moyne College.
After graduation, I worked in the New York State Senate as a Legislative Director.
There, I saw first-hand how Albany’s failed policies have forced many of our friends and family members to leave home for better opportunities.
It is time for a new generation of leadership so our children, grandchildren and their children can grow up and succeed right here in Central New York.
I have an unwavering commitment and love for our community and would be honored with your vote.
James
