Donate Yard Signs Going The Extra Mile Tour Starting on June 8th I began my "Going The Extra Mile" Tour.
I walked over 150 miles through every village, town and city in State Senate District 50.
I believe walking where our neighbors live helped me connect with them on a deeper level.
I walked on their streets and visited their neighborhoods in order to hear directly what's happening with the people that lived there.
I'm so grateful for being able to meet so many different people along the way and getting to see in person how diverse this district really is.
The Tour may have ended but this is just the beginning of my campaign.
