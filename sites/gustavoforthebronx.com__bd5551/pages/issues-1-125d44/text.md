Gustavo also knows that having a strong relationship between our community and law enforcement is crucial to having safer streets and a safer community.
ACCOMPLISHMENTS: Enacted the strictest gun safety laws in the country and secured tens of millions of dollars for gun violence intervention programs, hospital-based gun violence prevention, and community services.
Coordinated with city agencies, law enforcement, and community-based organizations to triage neighborhood issues and quality of life concerns.
Cleaned up syringe litter with local syringe exchange programs to combat the spread of HIV and Hepatitis C while addressing the needs of community members with substance use disorder.
Secured $6 million to transform the Fulton Correctional Facility in Crotona Park into the most unique reentry facility to help formerly incarcerated Bronxites transition back to the community successfully.
Invested over $2 Million in revitalizing Washington Park in Belmont to provide youth in the neighborhood a safe place after the tragic murder of Lesandro "Junior" Guzman-Feliz.
