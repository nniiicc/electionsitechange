POLITICAL CANDIDATE RUBRIC Introduction: The right to vote is your constitutional right.
Exercising that constitutional right is your voice.
Those who you vote for should represent your voice and speak to issues important to you, your family and your community.
Deciding whom to vote for can be challenging.
This simple rubric was created to allow voters one way to think about: • Whether or not candidates speak to issues important to you, your family and your community; • Whether or not candidates understand issues important to you, your family and your community; and, • Whether or not candidates have a measureable plan on how they will address issues important to you, your family and your community so that you can hold the candidate accountable.
Instructions: 1.
Write down issues that are important to you, your family and your community based on your own thoughts in column 1.
2.
Write down the candidates’ last names in the first line of column 2 and 3.
3.
Learn about the candidate.
Don’t use social media as your sole source for information.
Use credible sources that are nonpartisan.
Rate the candidate in columns 2 and 3 under their names.
4.
After learning about and rating the candidate, why down your reason you rated the candidate that way and share this information with family/friends.
Rating: Poor = 1 point The candidate uses “buzz” words and makes passionate statements about what’s wrong but doesn’t truly understand the issue and doesn’t have a measurable plan.
Average = 2 points The candidate truly understands the issue and has a plan that can be done with assistance of others (legislators, common council, governor, mayor, county board, etc.).
