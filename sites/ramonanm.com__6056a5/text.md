A Leader New Mexico Can Trust
Ramona Goolsby came to New Mexico in 2019 for work and when the pandemic hit, she chose to stay.
While others retreated, she kept reporting to work as a Primary Care Provider for the Veterans Administration, caring for the men and women who had given so much for this country.
She had found her home.
Since settling in New Mexico, Ramona has thrown herself into public life.
She sponsored all six referendum bills in New Mexico and served as designated agent for HB7, SB13, and SB180.
She has partnered with nonprofits and advocacy organizations working on election integrity, constitutional rights, and community education across the state.
In November 2025, she was elected to the Ciudad Soil and Water Conservation District Board of Supervisors, where she champions urban stormwater capture, soil health initiatives, and strict fiscal accountability for public funds.
Ramona is a U.S.
Army Reserve veteran, a mother, and a grandmother — living a life rooted in service, stewardship, and responsibility.
