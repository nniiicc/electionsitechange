Assessment and Immediate Priorities: Days 1–30
- Full strategic review of every department in the Secretary of State’s office: current performance, immediate risks, and four-year goals.
- Comprehensive budget and contract audit to identify savings and root out inefficiency.
- Legislative session preparation through a complete review of relevant statutes and budget requests.
- Launch the New Mexico Voter Roll Cleanup Task Force: state-led, with no federal involvement, and monthly public progress reports.
The task force will focus on removing deceased voters, contacting voters who have moved, and verifying accurate addresses.
- Establish the Business Services Task Force to modernize business filings, searches, and customer service.
Implementation and Collaboration: Days 31–60
- Complete every administrative requirement from the legislative session, on time and in full.
- Hold statewide meetings with county clerks to hear directly from the people running our elections and act on what they say the state office needs to do better.
Transparency Milestones: Days 61–90
- Launch a public, searchable Election Data Portal so every New Mexican can access election reports, records, contracts, and statistics in real time.
Transparency is the foundation of voter confidence.
Enhanced Accountability Tools By Day 100
- Roll out improvements to the campaign finance and lobbying disclosure platform, making reporting faster, more accurate, and fully transparent to the public.
Public metrics, every step
Every commitment above will be tracked with measurable metrics; inactive and deceased voters removed, percentage of data made public, customer service response times, and more.
New Mexicans will see results, not just promises.
