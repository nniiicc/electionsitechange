WHAT BUSINESS SERVICES DOES The division maintains or administers business and corporate filings, UCC and commercial filings, agricultural liens, trademarks, notary commissions, apostilles and authentications, service of process, and other commercial records.
The SOS's online filing system also processes Athlete Agent Registrations.
ATHLETE AGENTS & THE NIL ECONOMY New Mexico's Uniform Athlete Agents Act requires registration in specified circumstances before an individual acts as an athlete agent.
An "agency contract" includes agreements authorizing someone to negotiate or solicit an endorsement contract for a student athlete.
The Secretary of State also maintains athlete-agent registration information, and applications are public records.
Registered agents must retain specified records for five years, which are subject to inspection by the Secretary of State.
