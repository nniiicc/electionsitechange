Meet Rudolf Haden - Your Neighbor and Patriot
Rudolf Haden is a passionate patriot who has dedicated his life to defending our nation!
Rudolf Haden has spent his life in service of our country.
Since retiring from the US Navy, he is committed once again to serving the people of Montana in the State House.
He is rooted in Montana's spirit and environment, understanding that our strength lies in our communities and the hardworking families that build them.
Rudolf’s values are forged in the belief that every citizen deserves a fair shot at success.
His commitment is personal.
He knows the pressure of rising costs and the struggle many face to keep their homes.
That’s why his platform focuses on real, tangible solutions to make Montana affordable again—ensuring that working families don’t just survive, but thrive in the state they love.
Whether he's listening to neighbors or developing policy through community networking, Rudolf is driven by a singular goal: to protect the Montana way of life for the next generation.
He stands for integrity, accountability, and a future where our children can afford to live, work, and grow right here.
COMMITTED TO REPRESENTING MONTANA'S FAMILIES
Our state is facing an affordability crisis, and our state government can do more to reduce this burden on the very people who build this state every day.
We do this by:
- Lowering costs and taxes for the Middle Class and Working Montanans.
- Opening streams of revenue that currently are limited to obsolete state programs.
- Improving goods inventories in the state, preventing unnecessary costs.
- Improving infrastructure to reduce the costs of transportation and business development.
Rudolf is driven by a powerful core conviction: every single American deserves a genuine opportunity to achieve their highest potential and their American Dream.
He epitomizes confidence and a tough work ethic that comes from strong values and morals.
Growing up in poverty, he never lost faith or gave up hope for a brighter future.
Family, Community and Faith drove him to develop his strengths, successfully face challenges, and build a legacy of leadership and service to his country.

He can empathize with all those who work hard and believe in our country, and wants to be THEIR CHAMPION IN HELENA!
Rudolf is optimistic we can maintain the Big Sky Country we all love, while keeping our kids in the state working jobs that sustain their families.
