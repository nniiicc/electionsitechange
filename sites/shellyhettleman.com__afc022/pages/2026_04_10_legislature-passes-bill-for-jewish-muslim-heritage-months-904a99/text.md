10, designating May as “Jewish Heritage Month” and January as “Muslim Heritage Month.” House Bill 661 is the culmination of a collaborative effort led by Del.
Sarah S.
Wolek (D-16th) of Montgomery County, Del.
Jared S.
Solomon (D-18th) also of Montgomery County, and Sen.
Shelly L.
Hettleman (D-11th) of Baltimore County.
Hettleman and Solomon are co-chairs of the Maryland Legislative Jewish Caucus.
The objective of House Bill 661 is to spotlight the contributions of Muslim and Jewish Marylanders while promoting celebratory and educational activities in communities across the state.
The bill now moves to Gov.
Wes Moore for his signature.
“At a time of world tension and strife, this bill provides an opportunity to celebrate Maryland’s Muslim and Jewish communities for their cultural contributions to our state and our nation,” said Hettleman, who led the bill in the Senate.
Solomon said the bill promotes dialogue and unity.
“Given the times we’re living in, working on this bill together — and ensuring it reflects an expression of unity — is essential,” he said.
“There is far more that unites us than divides us.
