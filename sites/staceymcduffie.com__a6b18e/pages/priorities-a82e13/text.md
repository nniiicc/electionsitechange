My priorities are shaped by my experiences as an advocate, business professional, veteran spouse, mother and active member of our community.
Government Accountability & Transparency Government works best when it is accessible, responsive and accountable to the people it serves.
Residents deserve transparency in how decisions are made and how taxpayer dollars are spent.
Strong Schools & Student Success Every student deserves access to safe classrooms, strong academics, supported teachers and opportunities that fit their individual needs.
As a mother of two daughters in public schools, I understand how important strong schools are to families and the future of our communities.
Affordability & Responsible Budgeting Families, seniors, workers and small businesses deserve responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars and practical solutions that help make everyday life more affordable.
Michigan families should be able to afford housing, insurance, utilities and everyday necessities without feeling left behind.
Veterans, Seniors & Working Families As the spouse of a retired military veteran, I understand the challenges military and veteran families face.
Veterans, seniors, caregivers and working families deserve leaders who understand their challenges and fight for practical solutions that strengthen financial security, access to services and quality of life.
Accessible & Responsive Representation Representation doesn’t matter if you can’t see it, touch it or reach it.
Constituents deserve a representative who is present, available and engaged.
I will prioritize communication, community engagement and making government easier to navigate.
