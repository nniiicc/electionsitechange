Shop A District Worth Showing Up For Minnesota’s 8th Congressional District covers approximately 31,240 square miles (31,239.6 square miles based on recent Census geography).
To put that in perspective: It’s the largest congressional district in Minnesota by land area.
It covers 21 counties in northern and central Minnesota, including Duluth, the Iron Range, the Boundary Waters Canoe Area Wilderness, and stretches down to the Twin Cities Exurbs.
At roughly 31,240 square miles , it’s larger than the entire state of South Carolina and is about 40% larger than New Jersey .
