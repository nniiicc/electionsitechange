Voter Concerns we’re Hearing About Accross Minnesota’s 8th District, Trina is listening to what people say and what they are concerned about.
Again and again, many of the same concerns come up: healthcare, good-paying jobs, housing, everyday costs, reliable broadband and help when times get tough.
Healthcare Close to Home Good-Paying Jobs Affordable Housing Affordable Healthcare Help When You Need it Jobs and Broadband VOTER CONCERN Keep Healthcare Close to Home Trina will protect rural clinics, hospitals and access to essential care.
Rural hospitals and clinics are the backbone of our communities, but many are struggling to maintain services and recruit permanent staff.
When local care disappears, families are left traveling farther for routine appointments, and communities lose more than healthcare—they lose local jobs and the people who help keep their towns thriving.
Protect rural hospitals and clinics by opposing cuts to Medicaid reimbursement rates.
Ensure a broad range of healthcare services stay close to home by investing in education and training, the healthcare workforce, and transportation.
Fight for universal health care so no family has to file for bankruptcy due to a catastrophic illness or diagnosis.
VOTER CONCERN Keep Good-Paying Jobs Close to Home Trina will help create stable, good-paying jobs and keep them here.
Healthcare costs are squeezing families from every direction, from monthly premiums and deductibles to prescription drugs and unexpected medical bills.
No one should have to put off seeing a doctor, skip a medication, or worry that getting sick could put their family in financial trouble.
Trina believes we can bring those costs down and make healthcare work better for the people who depend on it.
Vote to restore manufacturing incentives, especially the Advanced Manufacturing Production Credit that can help bring and keep good-paying manufacturing jobs in our district.
Fight to bring investment to CD8, by promoting our highly educated workforce and strong labor unions, securing funds for the infrastructure needed to move goods, and streamlining regulatory processes.
Fight to keep good jobs here.
Bring stability to markets by standing up to this Administration's whiplash tariffs and punitive slashing of stimulus programs and funding.
VOTER CONCERN Housing We Can Afford Trina will support more homes that working families, young people, and seniors can afford.
Everyone deserves the chance to find a home they can afford in the community they call home.
Trina will work to expand housing options, lower barriers to building new homes, and help ensure working families, young people, and seniors aren’t priced out of the communities where they live and work.
Understands housing and jobs go together.
Without a place to live, workers can't fill jobs and communities can't thrive.
Support the Neighborhood Homes Investment Act which helps communities build and rehabilitate more homes people can own.
Create tax credits for contractors who build new homes priced below local median prices and sell them directly to families who will use them as their primary residence.
VOTER CONCERN Healthcare We Can Afford Trina will work to lower premiums, prescription prices and out-of-pocket costs.
Strong communities need jobs that allow people to build a life without having to leave home to find opportunity.
Trina will support investments that strengthen local industries, create good-paying jobs, and give workers and small businesses the tools they need to grow and succeed right here in our communities.
Cap the cost of lifesaving drugs, including insulin, so families aren't forced to pay hundreds of dollars for a medicine people need to live.
Negotiate lower prescription drug prices.
Protect Medicare's ability to negotiate prices and push to bring down the cost of more prescription drugs.
Make the whole healthcare system more affordable.
Support hospital price transparency, prevent market monopolies with anti-trust laws, stronger oversight of prescription drug middlemen, and ultimately fight for universal health care.
VOTER CONCERN Help When You Need It Trina believes people who can work should work.
But you also pay into a system that is supposed to be there when you need it.
You can lose a job, get sick, have your hours cut or simply live somewhere there aren’t enough jobs.
A tough stretch shouldn’t mean you can’t put food on the table or see a doctor.
Trina will fight to keep that help available and make sure people are treated with dignity when they need it.
Keep food assistance within reach.
Give communities flexibility when there simply aren’t enough jobs and fight to reverse the new SNAP restrictions.
Protect people during a tough stretch.
Protect the safety net for veterans, young people just starting out and families facing unexpected hardship.
Protect healthcare when life changes.
Fight for universal healthcare so losing a job doesn’t mean losing your ability to see a doctor.
VOTER CONCERN Bring Opportunity Home Trina will expand reliable broadband so people can find good jobs and build their lives without leaving the communities they call home.
Broadband shouldn’t be a luxury in rural Minnesota.
It’s infrastructure.
It can connect someone to a good job hundreds of miles away, help a small business or farm reach new customers, and give young people another reason to stay.
We’ve seen the difference it can make right here in our district, where inadequate broadband has kept some people from being able to work remotely.
Invest in rural broadband.
Support the bipartisan Rural Broadband Modernization Act and continued investment in high-speed internet for communities the private market hasn’t reached.
Help young people build their lives here.
Make it possible to find opportunity without leaving the place they call home.
Bring jobs and businesses home.
Give workers, small businesses and farms better access to employers, customers and markets anywhere.
