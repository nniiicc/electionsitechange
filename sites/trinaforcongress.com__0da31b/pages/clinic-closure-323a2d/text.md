Statement from Trina Swanson, Candidate for Congress for MN-8 on the closure of Lake View Silver Bay Clinic:
“The closure of the Lake View Silver Bay Clinic would have a devastating impact on healthcare access on the North Shore.
Rural communities already face significant barriers to high-quality healthcare, and too often our rural communities suffer in pursuit of a health system’s bottom line.
I strongly oppose this closure, and urge Aspirus Health to reverse this decision to preserve local healthcare services in Silver Bay essential to ensuring that rural residents receive timely, equitable, and accessible care.”
