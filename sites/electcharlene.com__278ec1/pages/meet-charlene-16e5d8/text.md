Meet Charlene DONATE Volunteer Legislative Updates Representative Charlene Ward Johnson brings decades of leadership, advocacy, and public service to the Texas House of Representatives, where she proudly serves District 139.
A lifelong Democrat and devoted mother of two, Rep.
Ward Johnson is deeply committed to strengthening democracy and building inclusive communities where everyone has access to fair and equal opportunities.
Throughout her tenure, Rep.
Ward Johnson has championed collaboration with government and local leaders to advance safer neighborhoods, high-quality education, workforce development, livable wages, and accessible healthcare.
She has worked closely with organizations across Houston and District 139 to deliver essential resources and meaningful support to her constituents.
In Austin, she has stood firm against efforts to undermine Democratic values and remains dedicated to being a strong, principled voice for the people she represents.
Rep.
Ward Johnson brings more than 30 years of experience in Corporate America, where she consistently advocated for resources that strengthened local communities.
Her professional leadership has helped shape her results-driven approach to public service.
Her passion for community engagement extends well beyond her professional career.
In 2022, Charlene was elected to the Houston Community College Board of Trustees, where she has made significant contributions, including serving as Chair of the Academic and Student Affairs Committee and the External Relations Committee.
She has also served as Vice President of Greater Houston Frontiers, Chair of the Martin Luther King Jr.
Memorial Breakfast, President of her neighborhood Civic Club, President of the University of Houston Black Alumni, and a Board Member of the University of Houston Alumni Foundation Board, where she chaired the Public Affairs Committee.
In recognition of her service, she received the University of Houston Outstanding Volunteer Award in 2018.
Charlene is a longtime volunteer with the Houston Livestock Show and Rodeo Ticket Service Committee and is a proud member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc. and Top Ladies of Distinction, Humble Intercontinental Chapter.
In 1991, Rep Ward Johnson graduated from the University of Houston with a Bachelor's Degree in Technology.
