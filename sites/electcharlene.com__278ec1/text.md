State Representative Charlene Ward Johnson represents House District 139.
She was the first woman elected to serve in this role.
In the 89th Legislative Session, Rep Ward Johnson was appointed by Speaker Burrows to serve on the Higher Education and Culture, Reaction and Tourism Committees.
During the 89th Legislative Session, Rep Ward Johnson has worked hard to pass and sponsor meaningful legislation that impacted not only her constituents but all Texans.
She is dedicated to putting the community first, focusing on education, economic improvements, property tax relief, working families, veterans and seniors.
