Five pillars for District 29 Read the full platform and watch Darian walk through each issue. § 01 Top ↑ 01 Affordability Every month, Downriver families feel the same squeeze: rent went up, groceries went up, utility bills went up but paychecks didn't.
Affordability isn't a slogan, it's the single most urgent issue at every kitchen table in the 29th.
In Lansing, I'll fight for housing supply that actually serves Michiganders not out-of-state corporate landlords buying up entire blocks.
That means real incentives for owner-occupied homes, protection for working renters, and accountability on utility rate hikes that have outpaced inflation for a decade.
Groceries and gas aren't optional.
When monopoly pricing, hidden fees, or unfair markups hurt working families, the state has tools and a duty to push back.
I'll use them.
What I'll fight for Expand owner-occupied housing incentives in the 29th Cap and audit utility rate hikes for transparency Crack down on corporate single-family home buyouts Push for grocery and prescription price oversight Video Coming Soon Darian on Affordability Fund the Fight on This § 02 Top ↑ 02 Data Centers The surge of AI has driven corporations to push massive data centers into communities, raising concerns about noise, light, environmental impacts, and long-term effects.
Decisions of this scale should stay with the people who live there.
I believe communities should be able to vote via annual referendum to decide whether or not a data center is right for their community.
And if they choose, all work should be performed by union members.
Every proposed project should be judged on its merits, with transparency and genuine community input.
Until developers can prove a project truly serves the community's interests, the default should be no.
Not because change isn't welcome, but because communities deserve the right to decide their own future.
What I'll fight for Decisions of this scale stay with the people who live there Judge every project on its merits with transparency and genuine input Default to no until developers prove the project serves the community Protect communities from noise, light, and environmental harm Video Coming Soon Darian on Data Centers Fund the Fight on This § 03 Top ↑ 03 FLOCK // Where does it stop?
FLOCK camera networks, sold as 'public safety,' quietly log every license plate that passes and hand that data to out-of-state databases with little oversight, no warrant, and no sunset.
That's not safety.
That's a surveillance contract written without us.
Every trip down the block becomes a tracked event.
Once a surveillance network like this is installed, the question is never whether it stays the same, it's always: where does it stop?
More cameras, more data, more sharing with out-of-state databases, fewer warrants, less oversight.
Communities deserve to decide whether these systems belong in their neighborhoods at all, and to set real limits before the network grows beyond their control.
What I'll fight for Ban unchecked FLOCK camera and license-plate surveillance expansion Require local consent and a warrant before plate data is collected Mandate a sunset on every surveillance network so it must be re-approved Block sharing of resident data with out-of-state databases Video Coming Soon Darian on FLOCK // Where does it stop?
Fund the Fight on This § 04 Top ↑ 04 Education That Competes Other countries are out-educating us and we keep pretending it's fine.
It's not.
Our kids deserve a public education system that competes on the world stage, not one that defends the status quo.
That starts with the basics: a modern curriculum that takes math, science, civics, and trades seriously; teachers paid enough to stay; and classrooms resourced enough to teach.
It also means honest conversations about what's working and what isn't and the political backbone to act on the answers.
I'll push for funding formulas that follow students fairly, paid trades and tech pathways inside our public high schools, and serious investment in retaining the great teachers we already have in the 29th.
What I'll fight for Modernize curriculum and raise math/science standards Expand paid trades pathways inside public high schools Competitive teacher pay to retain talent Downriver Honest, transparent reporting on school outcomes Video Coming Soon Darian on Education That Competes Fund the Fight on This § 05 Top ↑ 05 Property Tax CUTS for Seniors If you spent your life working, raising a family, and paying off a home in the 29th you should not get taxed out of that home in your retirement years.
Full stop.
Property tax burdens on fixed-income seniors have become a quiet crisis Downriver.
Assessments climb, but Social Security checks don't.
Too many of our neighbors are choosing between staying in their home and paying for prescriptions.
I'll fight for a meaningful property tax reduction for senior homeowners, stronger homestead protections, and an honest review of assessment practices so retirees aren't priced out of the communities they built.

A State Rep fights for District 29 but silence on the national stage is how we got here.
These are the stands Darian will take for every American. § N1 Top ↑ N1 No More Fake Wars For 35 years I watched my old party cheerlead wars built on lies from weapons of mass destruction that never existed to forever wars that drained this country while working-class kids did the dying.
I won't be a quiet vote for the next one.
The military-industrial complex doesn't defend freedom it defends budgets.
Every 'emergency' authorization, every no-bid contract, every regime-change adventure costs American lives and American treasure while the same handful of defense contractors post record profits.
I'll oppose authorization for any war not tied to a genuine, documented threat to the United States, demand sunset clauses on every overseas deployment, and vote to audit the Pentagon the way we audit food stamps.
If we can track every dollar of a family's SNAP benefit, we can track every dollar of a Raytheon contract.
What I'll fight for Oppose authorization for wars built on intelligence we can't verify Sunset every overseas deployment no forever wars Audit the Pentagon budget line by line Ban stock ownership in defense contractors for members of Congress Video Coming Soon Darian on No More Fake Wars Fund the Fight on This § N2 Top ↑ N2 End Government Profiteering Public office was never meant to be a stock portfolio with a salary attached.
Members of Congress shouldn't be outperforming Wall Street in the very industries they regulate and they sure shouldn't be voting on contracts that pay their own donors.
The revolving door between Capitol Hill and K Street is the single biggest reason nothing changes.
Lobbyists write the bills, lawmakers pass them, then retire into seven-figure 'consulting' roles at the same firms.
Meanwhile, members trade stocks in industries they oversee defense, pharma, tech and somehow always beat the market.
I'll sponsor legislation to ban individual stock trading for members of Congress and their spouses, close the revolving door with a multi-year lobbying ban after leaving office, and require every bill to publish its corporate backers in plain language before a vote.
If you want to get rich, don't run for office.
What I'll fight for Ban individual stock trading for members of Congress and spouses Multi-year lobbying ban after leaving public office Publish every bill's corporate backers before the vote End no-bid federal contracts to campaign donors Video Coming Soon Darian on End Government Profiteering Fund the Fight on This § N3 Top ↑ N3 Health Care Is a Right Every other developed nation on earth has figured this out.
Their people don't go bankrupt from a cancer diagnosis.
Their insulin doesn't cost a week's wages.
Their families don't start GoFundMe pages to survive.
Only in America do we treat staying alive as a luxury product.
National health care isn't radical it's overdue.
A single-payer system that covers every American, negotiated drug prices, and an end to the insurance middlemen who add cost and delay at every step would save working families thousands a year and save lives.
I'll co-sponsor Medicare for All legislation, vote to let Medicare negotiate every drug price, and fight to cap out-of-pocket costs so no one chooses between a prescription and a mortgage payment.
The for-profit insurance model had its chance.
It failed.
What I'll fight for Co-sponsor and vote for Medicare for All Let Medicare negotiate every prescription drug price Cap out-of-pocket costs for working families End surprise billing and junk insurance plans Video Coming Soon Darian on Health Care Is a Right Fund the Fight on This § N4 Top ↑ N4 Bodily Autonomy for All Freedom doesn't stop at the doctor's door.
The same people who say they want government out of your life are the first to put government inside your body between you and your doctor, your pharmacist, and your own decisions.
Bodily autonomy is not a 'women's issue.' It's a human rights issue that touches every American reproductive freedom, medical decision-making, the right to refuse, the right to access gender-affirming care, and the right to make end-of-life choices with dignity.
I'll vote to codify reproductive freedom into federal law, protect access to medication abortion and contraception nationwide, support federal protections for gender-affirming care, and oppose any attempt to criminalize patients or doctors for private medical decisions.
Your body, your choice not the state's, not your employer's, and not a politician's.
