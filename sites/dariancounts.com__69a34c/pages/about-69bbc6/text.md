The Candidate · Downriver, MI Part I The Public Story Darian Counts has been a lifelong member of the Downriver community.
He completed his education at Woodhaven High School in 1984 and continued his studies at Henry Ford and Wayne County Community Colleges.
During the 1990s, he successfully launched businesses marketing mobile electronics and later, environmentally safe alternative products while simultaneously building a music career performing with some of America's favorite bands.
In 2008, Darian merged his love for music with his entrepreneurial spirit by founding Detroit Live Magazine , a monthly publication focused on showcasing the Michigan music scene.
As editor-in-chief, he guided the magazine's digital edition to reach music fans worldwide.
In 2015, he sold his interest in the magazine and accepted a position at Ford Motor.
Darian is also a former member of CWA Local 4100.
For 35 years Darian identified as a Republican and actively campaigned for Republican candidates as recently as 2004.
In 2007 he began to see the insincerity of the Republican party from the fake war based on the lie of weapons of mass destruction, to the billionaire bailouts after the 2008 collapse of the American economy.
In 2016, inspired by the candidacy of Bernie Sanders, Darian cast his first ballot for a Democrat.
Darian argues that the electoral system itself contributes to the problem: the significant role of money in political campaigns leads to a focus on fundraising rather than authentic community service.
As your next State Representative, Darian will be a guardian of the middle class fighting for community, affordability, and prosperity for Michigan families.
Part II The Long Version 01 A Child of the 70s Early stages on the mic Darian was raised as a child of the 70s nurtured to be independent and fearless.
After graduating from Woodhaven High School in 1984, he relocated to Dallas, Texas, where he began forging his career at 18, developing a work ethic that would sustain him for decades.
By the late 80s, Darian had made his way back to Michigan.
The realities of life taught him that education was crucial not only for financial achievement but also for personal growth and self-assurance.
02 The American Dream Following several years at a local mobile electronics store, Darian embraced the adage: find someone who has achieved the results you want and emulate their actions.
He replicated his previous employer's business model and established a thriving enterprise in record time.
In January 1993, he began collaborating with a company dedicated to addressing the chemicals and toxins that permeate our daily lives.
His five years as a leader and trainer deepened his appreciation for the American Dream.
03 A New Millennium The dawn of the new millennium ushered in parenthood and his first union role.
In 2001, Darian embarked on his journey with SBC Global and became a member of his first union, CWA 4100.
However, after seven years of a conventional 9-to-5 job, Darian felt the irresistible call of entrepreneurship.
He departed from SBC to pursue his next venture.
04 Detroit Live Magazine In 2008, Darian merged his passion for music and the arts into a monthly magazine that showcases Michigan artists and musicians, bringing their work to a global audience.
Detroit Live Magazine began as a modest local publication and, within eight years, elevated previously unknown Michigan artists into homes worldwide.
In 2014, he sold his stake and returned to his first love, the automotive industry assisting Downriver families in securing affordable transportation by selling Ford cars and trucks.
05 On Stage, Still A constant theme throughout Darian's diverse career has been his passion for musical performance.
During the 1990s, he performed alongside iconic artists including Vince Neil, Quiet Riot, and Candlebox.
In 2014, Darian transitioned into a tribute artist role taking on personas including David Lee Roth, Rob Zombie, Bob Seger, Huey Lewis, Santana, and AC/DC's original bad boy, Bon Scott.
Darian believes music and the arts are the foundation that strengthens communities, with significant impact on students' cognitive development.
