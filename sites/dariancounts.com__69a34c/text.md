Darian Counts has been a lifelong member of the Downriver community.
He graduated from Woodhaven High School in 1984 and studied at Henry Ford and Wayne County Community Colleges.
In the 1990s, he launched businesses marketing mobile electronics and environmentally safe alternative products while building a music career performing with some of America's favorite bands.
In 2008, Darian founded Detroit Live Magazine , a monthly publication showcasing the Michigan music scene.
As editor-in-chief, he guided its digital edition to reach fans worldwide.
In 2015, he sold his interest and accepted a position at Ford Motor.
He is also a former member of CWA Local 4100.
For 35 years Darian identified as a Republican.
In 2007, he began to see the insincerity of the party from the fake war based on weapons of mass destruction, to the billionaire bailouts after 2008.
In 2016, inspired by Bernie Sanders, Darian cast his first ballot for a Democrat.
