Sydney Gruters, a working mother of three and wife of RNC chairman Joe Gruters, recently served as Vice President of Advancement and Executive Director of the New College Foundation.
She previously worked for Congressman Greg Steube as District Director following his election in 2018.
A native Floridian, she was appointed by President Donald Trump in 2017 as State Director for Florida and the U.S.
Virgin Islands at the U.S.
Department of Agriculture, Rural Development.
She also spent more than a decade working in Washington, D.C., and Manatee County for Congressman Vern Buchanan.
