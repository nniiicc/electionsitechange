Kyle owns Club One Casino in Fresno, serves as president of the California Gaming Association, founded the Kirkland Foundation, and is the former chairman of Steinway Musical Instruments.
He has spent the last two decades solving industry and community problems and giving back to California’s Central Valley.

Track Record Results That Power This Campaign Decades of building businesses, protecting iconic institutions, and investing locally fuel Kyle's CA-21 campaign.
1984 Graduated Harvard magna cum laude in economics and joined Bain & Company to work on complex business problems.
1986 Arrived in California with $700, earned an MBA from Stanford, and pursued a career in finance in Los Angeles.
1995 Led the acquisition of Steinway & Sons, restoring an iconic American manufacturer and protecting its skilled workforce.
1996 Took Steinway public on the NYSE, expanding its global reach.
2008 Purchased Club One Casino in Fresno, investing millions in the Central Valley.
2013 Elected President of the California Gaming Association to advocate for the California cardroom industry with $5.6 billion of economic impact, over 20,000 jobs, and $500 million of tax revenue.
2016 Founded the Kirkland Foundation, rescuing 20,000+ at-risk animals across the Valley over the last ten years.
2019 Became Chair of the Fresno Chaffee Zoo Corporation board with 850,000 annual visitors and regional economic and educational impact.
2022 After a 15-month COVID shutdown, Club One Casino grew to 200+ employees, paid over $1M in annual city taxes, and contributed to area non-profits through the Club One Cares program.
2025 Launched the CA-21 congressional campaign to address cost of living, end government waste, and restore the American Dream for Central Valley residents.
