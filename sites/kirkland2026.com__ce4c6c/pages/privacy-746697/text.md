Your Rights and Choices 8.1 California Residents (CCPA) California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion (subject to legal requirements), and opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).

International Users This Website is intended for users in the United States.

Data Retention We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy and as required by law, including FEC record-keeping requirements.
