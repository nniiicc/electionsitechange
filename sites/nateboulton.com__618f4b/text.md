Menu Home More About Nate A leader in and for Our Community Recent News Stories Donate Links and Resources Contact Nate Boulton: A Proven, Trusted Leader Who Gets Results Nate Boulton is running for the Iowa House to represent District 39, which includes East Des Moines and Pleasant Hill.
As a two-term state senator, Nate Boulton represented eastern Polk County, including east Des Moines and Pleasant Hill, for eight years in the Iowa Senate.
During his two terms, he served as the Ranking Member of the Labor Committee, Ranking Member of the Judiciary Committee as well as an Assistant Democratic Leader and then Democratic Whip in 2024.
Nate, his wife Andrea, and their daughters Gwyn and Alys, live in the Grand View Park area of east Des Moines.
Outside the legislature, Nate is a practicing workers’ rights attorney who has represented individuals who have suffered workplace injuries or have been wrongfully terminated as well as serving as a lawyer for several labor unions in their representation of Iowa workers.
