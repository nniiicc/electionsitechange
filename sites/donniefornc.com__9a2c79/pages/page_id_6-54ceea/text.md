Home Issues Meet Donnie Donate Donnie on the Issues Donnie is a Christian, a conservative, and a Republican.
See how he stands on the issues below.
Pro Life Donnie is Pro-Life and will always fight for the unborn and the born.
He believes that the policies being proposed by the left that would kill newborn babies are evil and have no place in our society.
God grants the gift of life and that life should be protected.
2nd Amendment Donnie believes that without the 2nd amendment it is impossible to guarantee the freedom of this nation.
Economy Donnie believes in a free-market economy with lower taxes and less government regulations.
Growth in the economy will never stem from politicians, it will be from the hard-working men and women who are working toward a better future for them and their families.
Our economic policies must center around creating a business friendly environment where entrepreneurship can thrive.
Education Education works best when parents have the authority over their children’s education which is why Donnie supports school choice.
He will fight daily for our children to receive a quality education free from indocrintation.
Law Enforcement Simply put, Donnie backs the Blue, Veterans As a veteran himself, Donnie recognizes the importance of honoring those that have served.
He will work to ensure that North Carolina is the best state in the nation for our veterans.
Medical Freedom It is important to keep people safe.
It is also important to allow citizens to make decisions about their health with the advice of their doctor, without the government telling them what they have to do.
Election Integrity Donnie was in DC on January 6th.
He believes that without safe and secure elections we cannot have a thriving nation.
