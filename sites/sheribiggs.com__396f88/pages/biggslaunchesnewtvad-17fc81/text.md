Sheri Biggs Launches New TV Ad in SC03
Tuesday, April 9, 2024
ANDERSON, SC – Conservative Republican Sheri Biggs has launched the second television ad of her campaign for South Carolina's Third Congressional District.
The 60-second ad, entitled "Healing Our Nation," showcases Biggs, a board certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner and a Lieutenant Colonel in the Air National Guard, as a counselor to Uncle Sam and addresses many issues facing the nation today.
Using a cinematic approach, the ad focuses on the need to heal the nation from the broken Southern Border to the nation's growing debt, and from the failures of career politicians to the attacks on conservative values.
It also captures Biggs' commitment to result-focused leadership.
"There is no denying that our nation is in trouble today because Washington has a mental, fiscal and spiritual health problem," says Biggs.
"My campaign is focused on healing our nation with bold, conservative, servant leadership.
As the next Member of Congress, I will be mission-focused on securing our border, ensuring fiscal responsibility, draining the swamp, keeping the promises made to our veterans, and protecting our conservative values."
The ad can be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WYchrii8k7s
