HOME ABOUT PLATFORM GET INVOLVED NEWS DONATE 050124 - Mission Focused TV Ad “Border War” Focus of New Sheri Biggs TV Ad in South Carolina’s Third Congressional District Race Wednesday, May 1, 2024 ANDERSON, SC – Conservative Republican candidate for Congress Sheri Biggs has launched a new television ad in South Carolina’s Third Congressional District focusing on the crisis at the Southern Border, described in the ad as the “border war.” “Mission Focused,” the third television ad from Biggs’ campaign, details the harsh realities of the illegal immigration invasion, career politicians’ attempts to exploit the border for political photo opportunities, and the necessity to utilize Biggs’ military experience and mission-focused leadership to win the border war.
Full content of the ad is as follows: “Career politicians and candidates treat our Southern Border like a political photo op.
In reality, it’s a war zone.
Lieutenant Colonel Sheri Biggs understands that.
If we’re going to win the border war, we need Sheri’s military experience and mission-focused leadership to work with President Trump, stop the invasion, deport illegals, build the wall, and end government giveaways to illegals.
Lieutenant Colonel Sheri Biggs will get the job done.” Biggs – a Lieutenant Colonel in the Air National Guard, a nurse practitioner, and first-time candidate for office – expressed why the ad’s focus is so important today.
“Joe Biden’s immigration crisis will never be solved by career politicians and career candidates using the border as a political photo opportunity,” said Biggs.
“I am not a career politician, and I have never run for office before.
My neighbors in South Carolina’s Third Congressional District can rest assured that I will put my military experience and mission-focused leadership skills to work in offering solutions to the most critical issues facing our nation.” “Mission Focused” can be viewed at: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q1mjNT6Fc1o ### Sheri Biggs is a member of the Air National Guard.
