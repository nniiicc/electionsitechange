Governor Henry McMaster Endorses Sheri Biggs for Congress
Wednesday, April 24, 2024
ANDERSON, SC – South Carolina Governor Henry McMaster has endorsed Lieutenant Colonel Sheri Biggs in her race for South Carolina’s Third Congressional District.
In his endorsement of Biggs, Governor McMaster highlighted Biggs’ faith, military service, and commitment to work with President Trump on the most pressing issues facing the nation.
“I am proud to offer my enthusiastic endorsement of conservative Republican Lieutenant Colonel Sheri Biggs for Congress,”
said Gov.
McMaster.
“Sheri Biggs epitomizes the greatness of the people of South Carolina – an unwavering belief in God, a commitment to selfless service, and a true resolve to fight for our shared values.
While her distinguished military and healthcare careers are truly admirable, her passion to help heal our nation’s fiscal, mental, and spiritual health problems is exactly what we need representing us in Congress.
Sheri Biggs will work with President Trump to secure our border, protect tax dollars, defend our conservative values, keep the promises to our veterans, and drain the swamp.
I know Sheri Biggs will accomplish this platform because I have known Sheri and her husband, Bill, for many years.
Sheri and Bill Biggs have championed efforts to support veterans, law enforcement, and first responders and to advance the conservative cause in South Carolina and across the nation.
Like I did with President Trump in 2016, 2020, and this year – I humbly ask the great people of South Carolina to vote for the conservative fighter we need in Washington, Lieutenant Colonel Sheri Biggs for Congress.”
Biggs expressed her admiration of Governor McMaster’s fight for South Carolina and his work to deliver results in accepting the endorsement.
“Governor Henry McMaster has consistently fought for and delivered conservative results for the people of South Carolina,” said Biggs.
“Governor McMaster has taken tough stands for what is right – from being the first statewide official in the nation to endorse President Trump, to deploying National Guard troops to help secure the Southern Border, to defending the sanctity of life and protecting our Second Amendment here at home.
I am honored to earn Governor McMaster’s support and endorsement and will work tirelessly with President Trump, as he has, to champion the conservative cause in Congress.”
