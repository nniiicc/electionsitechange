Sheri Biggs Officially Files for SC’s 3rd Congressional District
Tuesday, March 26, 2024
ANDERSON, SC – Over Sheri Biggs – a dedicated servant leader with an extensive background in healthcare, military service, and community involvement – officially filed her candidacy paperwork to appear on the Republican ballot for South Carolina’s Third Congressional District.
Biggs, a nurse practitioner and Lieutenant Colonel in the United States Air National Guard, highlighted her conservative political outsider status upon filing.
“Career politicians and career bureaucrats have done nothing to fix the problems our nation faces today,” said Biggs.
“It is going to take conservative, political outsiders like President Trump and me to fight and tackle the toughest issues before our nation.
I stand ready to do just that and be the strong advocate for our conservative values, our freedoms, and our future.
I will continue to work hard to win this race and represent my neighbors with integrity, passion, and conviction as the next Member of Congress from South Carolina’s Third District.”
Today’s candidacy filing comes one week after the launch of the Veterans for Sheri Biggs Coalition and one month since the campaign launched its first television ad.
More information on Biggs, the Veterans for Sheri Biggs Coalition, and the campaign’s first television ad can be found at www.SheriBiggs.com.
