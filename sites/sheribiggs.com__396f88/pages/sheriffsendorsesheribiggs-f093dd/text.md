Seven Sheriffs in SC03 Endorse Sheri Biggs for Congress
Tuesday, June 18, 2024
ANDERSON, SC – Seven sheriffs from across South Carolina's Third Congressional District have endorsed Lieutenant Colonel Sheri Biggs in her bid for Congress.
The endorsements highlight Biggs’ integrity, unwavering support for law enforcement, and commitment to upholding conservative values.
Sheriffs endorsing Biggs are:
- Sheriff Ray Watson, Abbeville County
- Sheriff Chad McBride, Anderson County
- Sheriff Jody Rowland, Edgefield County
- Sheriff Clarke Stearns, McCormick County
- Sheriff Lee Foster, Newberry County
- Sheriff Mike Crenshaw, Oconee County
- Sheriff Josh Price, Saluda County
Edgefield County Sheriff Jody Rowland: "I am honored to be the first sheriff to endorse Sheri Biggs for Congress, and I am thrilled to see my fellow sheriffs joining me in standing with her.
Sheri Biggs' unwavering integrity, proven leadership, and steadfast commitment to conservative values make her the ideal candidate for South Carolina's Third Congressional District.
Her honorable background stands in stark contrast to her opponent, and her dedication to supporting law enforcement and upholding the rule of law is exactly what our district needs."
Anderson County Sheriff Chad McBride: "I proudly served in the military, and Sheri Biggs still wears the uniform today.
Sheri Biggs' service in uniform matters greatly to me, as does her service supporting the mental health of local veterans.
I trust Sheri Biggs and know she will fight for our veterans, work to secure the border, and support local law enforcement."
Oconee County Sheriff Mike Crenshaw: “As a Lieutenant Colonel in the Air National Guard and a committed nurse practitioner, Sheri Biggs has consistently demonstrated honesty, dedication, and commitment to service.
As a resident of my county, I can attest that Sheri Biggs has always been straightforward about her life experiences and positions on the issues, including her unwavering support for law enforcement.
Sheri respects the sacrifices our officers make every day and will always have our back.
With Sheri Biggs in Congress, we can trust that our values will be defended with honor and integrity.
I proudly endorse Sheri Biggs and ask you to vote for her in the runoff."
In accepting the sheriffs’ endorsements, Biggs expressed her appreciation for their service and reaffirmed her commitment to the rule of law.
"I am deeply honored to earn the trust of and receive the endorsements from these distinguished sheriffs," said Biggs.
"Their support underscores my commitment to uphold the rule of law, ensure our law enforcement officers have the resources they need to keep our communities safe, and represent our community with integrity.
Together, we will fight for the values that make South Carolina strong."
