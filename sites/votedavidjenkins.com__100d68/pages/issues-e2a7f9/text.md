As a farmer David knows what means to balance a budget, invest and grow his business.
David sees that common- sense isn’t common in government and he’s ready to continue the real and effective conservative agenda that our District needs.
He knows that if good citizens don’t get involved in our political system, our communities will suffer.
From delivering basic services to bringing jobs to our local economy, David will keep government in its place and focus on protecting the shared, traditional values of rural Georgia.
