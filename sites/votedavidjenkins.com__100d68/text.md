Home About Issues Contact Donate Who is David Jenkins?
David is a husband, a retired army officer, a farmer and serves his local community as an emergency air ambulance helicopter pilot.
In 2020, David Jenkins entered politics for his first-ever political race and landed a stunning blow to Democrats by defeating Georgia’s Democratic House Leader.
Since then, he has proudly served as a citizen legislator in the General Assembly representing House District 136.
Traditional conservative American values guide all that he does.
