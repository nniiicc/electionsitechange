Keith Mogel for Wisconsin State Assembly District 75 For too long, the Northern Assembly districts have been ignored by Madison.
It is time for a true voice to head to Madison to protect our interests.
I’m running for State Assembly because Northern Wisconsin deserves a leader who knows how to fight for the things we deserve: Funding for Rural Schools , Roads , and Healthcare , Local Authority for Local Issues , a Healthy Environment , and Affordable Cost of Living . → Why am I running?
Some of my fondest memories as a child were fishing with my grandfather, an Evangelical United Brethren minister, who taught me to question and to think freely, to care about your neighbor, and do what is right for everyone, regardless of how they looked, believed, or what they did.
I carried those lessons through 28 years as a Lieutenant Colonel in the Army and Wisconsin National Guard, and through my years in law enforcement and corrections.
I am running for the people of Burnett and Polk County, for the potential of the area with respect to its manufacturing, recreation, and farming, and the knowledge that Madison needs to be reminded that Rural Wisconsin and its People are Wisconsin’s Greatest Asset, and we cannot be ignored anymore by either party.
I am running to be your voice, no matter how you may have voted in the past.
