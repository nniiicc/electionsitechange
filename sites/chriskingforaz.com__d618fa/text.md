Education Friends, I’m running for State Senate because I believe the conservative principles of smaller, more limited government, secure borders and an unwavering defense of the constitution are vital to keeping our state a great place to live, work and raise a family.
As your next State Senator, my promise to you is that I’ll never apologize for or waver from my commitment to always stand up and fight for our shared conservative principles.
Unlike a lot of politicians who tell you one thing and do another, you will always know where I stand.
I am a conservative and with your vote for Legislative District 17 State Senator, I will represent you as a conservative in the Arizona State Senate.
Zero Tolerance for Illegal Immigration Under President Trump we have made real progress on securing Arizona’s border, but there is more work to be done.
Illegal immigration still has a real effect on public safety and quality of life in our state.
In the State Senate, I will fight for real solutions to crack down on illegal immigration in our state and remove those who are in our state illegally.
Smaller (Limited) Government Arizona is hundreds of millions of dollars in debt today because our liberal Democrat governor is all about growing the size and cost of state government.
I see things differently.
As your State Senator, I will fight for smaller, limited, more efficient government that spends your tax dollars wisely.
Protecting Taxpayers I believe you and your family know better than government how to spend your hard-earned money.
That’s why, as our next State Senator, I will stand up to and stop politicians who want to grow the size and scope of government and raise your taxes to pay for it.
Defending the Constitution I am committed to always defending the constitution.
In the State Senate, I will oppose any effort to infringe upon your constitutional rights.
This includes always standing up for your free speech rights, religious liberty, and your right to keep and bear arms.
Freedom of Educational Choice Parents know their children better than any bureaucrat in Phoenix ever could.
I firmly believe in parental choice in education.
Every Arizona family deserves the freedom to choose the best learning environment for their child—whether that’s their neighborhood public school, a charter school, a private or religious school, or homeschooling.
Freedom and Prosperity for Arizona Arizona is at its best when freedom and prosperity are the guiding principles of our state.
There will be no stronger advocate than I for policies that promote more freedom and more prosperity for Arizonans.
