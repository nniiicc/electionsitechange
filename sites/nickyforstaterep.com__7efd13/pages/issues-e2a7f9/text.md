A Common-Sense Plan for Harrisburg
Lower Taxes & Responsible Spending
Pennsylvania families are stretched thin.
Government must live within its means instead of demanding more from hardworking taxpayers.
Nicky Woods supports lowering taxes, reducing wasteful spending, and stopping Harrisburg from shifting costs onto homeowners through rising property taxes.
Parental Rights & Educational Choice
Parents—not government—should decide what is best for their children.
Nicky supports educational choice, strong academic standards, and removing unnecessary state barriers so families can choose public, private, charter, cyber, or homeschool education without being penalized.
Law & Order
Communities are safer when criminals are held accountable and law enforcement is supported—not second-guessed.
With more than 20 years in law enforcement, Nicky Woods will stand with police, strengthen public safety, and ensure victims’ rights come first.
Defending the Second Amendment
The right to keep and bear arms is a constitutional freedom—not a privilege.
Nicky will defend the Second Amendment and oppose policies that burden law-abiding citizens while failing to address violent criminals.
Limited Government
Pennsylvania government grew far beyond its proper role—especially during COVID—and too often still acts like it knows best.
Nicky supports limiting executive overreach, increasing transparency, and restoring accountability so citizens—not bureaucrats—make the decisions that shape their lives.
Supporting Small Business
Small businesses are the backbone of Pennsylvania’s economy.
Nicky will fight for fewer regulations, lower taxes, affordable energy, and a business climate where employers can hire, grow, and stay right here in Lancaster County.
