This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and handle personal information.
Information We Collect Friends of Nicky Woods may collect personal information, including names, email addresses, telephone numbers, mailing addresses, and other information voluntarily provided through our campaign website, campaign events, or direct communications.
We may also obtain voter contact information from political organizations, voter databases, and other legally available sources for campaign outreach and communication purposes.
How We Use Your Information Information collected or obtained by Friends of Nicky Woods may be used to: Respond to questions and inquiries.
Provide campaign updates and election information.
Communicate with voters and supporters through telephone calls, emails, and text messages, as permitted by applicable law.
Coordinate campaign events, volunteer activities, and community outreach.
Maintain campaign records and comply with applicable legal requirements. ​ Text Messaging and SMS Communications ​ Friends of Nicky Woods may communicate with voters and supporters through text messaging services, including peer-to-peer messaging platforms. ​ Text messages may include campaign information, election reminders, event announcements, volunteer opportunities, and other campaign-related communications. ​ Recipients may opt out of future text messages at any time by replying STOP.
Requests to stop receiving messages will be honored in accordance with applicable requirements. ​ Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply. ​ Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, except as required by law. ​ Information Sharing and Protection ​ Friends of Nicky Woods respects the privacy of individuals whose information is obtained through campaign activities and voter contact databases. ​ We do not sell, rent, or share voter contact lists with other political campaigns, committees, or organizations for their independent use. ​ We may provide information to trusted service providers, including our text messaging platform, solely as necessary to conduct campaign operations.
