Nicky Woods is the Republican candidate for State Representative in Pennsylvania’s 98th District.
She is a mother, small business owner, and former law enforcement officer with more than 20 years of experience protecting communities and serving the public.
Currently serving as Lancaster County Clerk of Courts, Nicky has delivered real results by streamlining operations, improving transparency, and saving taxpayers nearly $200,000.
Her record reflects a commitment to efficiency, accountability, and responsible stewardship of public resources.
Nicky is running to bring common-sense conservative leadership to Harrisburg — fighting for limited government, public safety, and putting families and taxpayers first.
