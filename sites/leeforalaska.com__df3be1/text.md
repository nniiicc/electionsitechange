I was born and raised right here in Eagle River.
I grew up on Hiland Road back when it was just a dirt road.
My family has multi-generational roots in this community.
I didn't move here to run for office, and I'm not using our district as a stepping stone.
Eagle River is my home.
It's where I built my small business, where I put in the hours volunteering, and where I want to ensure the next generation has the same incredible opportunities I had.
I believe in showing up.
While others are focused on national politics and partisan culture wars, I've spent years quietly doing the hard, necessary work on local boards, road service areas, and community councils.
That's the approach I'll bring to the State House: competent, grounded, and unmistakably local.
Real Priorities for District 23 Practical solutions over partisan rhetoric.
Focused on the issues that actually impact our daily lives in Eagle River.
Education First Our students and educators deserve full support.
As a member of the Eaglexit Education Committee, I know firsthand the challenges our schools face.
I will fight for adequate school funding, essential classroom resources, and deep support for the teachers who shape our future.
Strong schools build a strong community.
Roads & Public Safety Winter road clearing isn't a luxury; it's a critical safety issue.
With 3 years on the CBERRRSA board and running my own snow removal business, I understand the logistics of keeping Eagle River moving.
I will prioritize effective road maintenance, infrastructure, and robust public safety resources.
Economic Development As a small business owner, I know the obstacles entrepreneurs face.
We need to foster an environment where local businesses can thrive, create jobs, and build our local economy.
Smart infrastructure investment and removing unnecessary hurdles will help Eagle River prosper independently.
Fiscal Responsibility & the PFD I support paying Alaskans the full, statutory Permanent Fund Dividend they're owed.
Rather than cutting the PFD or raising taxes, I want to grow new revenue through responsible resource development, and expand healthcare options for our aging population by cutting the red tape that keeps new clinics and hospitals from opening here.
