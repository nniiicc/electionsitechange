This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you sign up to receive SMS/text messages from our campaign.
By providing your mobile phone number and opting in to receive text messages from us, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
Information We Collect - Your mobile phone number - Your name and basic contact information (if provided) - Your communication preferences and consent status - Message interaction details (e.g., delivery time, replies) - Any information you include in messages you send us How We Collect Information We may collect information when you: - Submit your phone number on our website or online forms - Sign up at events or in person - Text a keyword or number to join our SMS list - Provide your number to a campaign team member who adds you with your permission How We Use Your Information We use your information to: - Send you SMS messages you have opted in to receive - Provide campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer info, and donation opportunities - Respond to questions or messages you send - Improve outreach and comply with legal and carrier requirements Sharing of Information We do not sell, rent, or share your phone number or SMS opt‑in information with third parties for their marketing purposes.
We may share information only with trusted service providers who help operate our SMS program.
These providers may not use your information for their own marketing.
Text message opt‑in data and consent will not be sold, shared, or transferred to any third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.
Opting Out To stop receiving text messages: Reply STOP to any message Or contact us at: greg@gregdolezal.com You may receive a confirmation text when your request is processed.
Message Frequency & Rates Message frequency may vary.
Standard message and data rates may apply.
Data Security We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, though no method is 100% secure.
Data Retention We retain information only as long as needed for the campaign’s SMS program or legal requirements.
Changes to This Policy Updates may be made from time to time.
