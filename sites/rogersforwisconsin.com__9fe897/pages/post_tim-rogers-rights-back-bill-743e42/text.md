Tim Rogers Rights Back Bill
Nov 1, 2021
This bill will help integrate into society all ex-felons off paper who lost their rights.
You will automatically get your rights back after you serve your full sentence.
That would include the constitutional rights to bear arms, go hunting.
Rights to run for local and state office.
And in some states, your Right to Vote.
Tim Rogers is working with state officials to pass this bill in Wisconsin.
