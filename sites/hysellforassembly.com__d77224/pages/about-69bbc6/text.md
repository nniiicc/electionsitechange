He graduated from Pius XI high school in Milwaukee and Carroll University in Waukesha.
After college, Andrew lived and worked in Madison for the senate and assembly Democrats, first as an intern and then serving as a legislative aide.
At night, he organized phone banks to raise small dollar donations to support Wisconsin Democratic candidates.
Andrew started working at an early age, first with a paper route and later at McDonald’s and Denny’s.
To help pay his tuition, Andrew worked his way through college.
Later, he attended law school at night while working full time.
Andrew graduated from Georgetown Law and works as an attorney at Lawton & Cates, S.C.
Andrew Hysell is Committed to Protecting our Democracy Our country and our democracy are at-risk.
It is all of our responsibility to save it.
Andrew has worked tirelessly to support Wisconsin Democratic candidates and promote a progressive policy agenda.
It’s why he joined Senator Agard in 2020 to travel the state, raising money and volunteering for Democratic candidates to help “ Save the Veto ”.
It’s why he has been a strong supporter of Tony Evers and Democratic candidates for U.S.
Senate.
Andrew Campaigning With Senator Melissa Agard and Neal Plotkin Andrew And Board Members Of Reading Roadmap Working To Promote Early Childhood Literacy Andrew Hysell has Dedicated his Career to Helping Children Andrew has advocated for policies and programs to address and eliminate health and educational inequities facing at-risk children in the United States.
At the Campaign for Tobacco-Free Kids , he fought to pass ground-breaking legislation to protect children from the harms of tobacco.
He served as Save the Children’s Vice President, where he advocated for public education and public schools.
Andrew was the Director for the Campaign for Healthy Kids , and was a partner of First Lady Michelle Obama’s Let’s Move!
Campaign.
Today, he is a board member for Reading Roadmap , an education non-profit that works to close the literacy gap that exists among students from low income families.
