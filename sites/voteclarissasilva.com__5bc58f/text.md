MEET CLARISSA Justice Clarissa Silva has spent her career serving the people of South Texas through public service, legal advocacy, and judicial leadership.
Born and raised in Harlingen, Texas, she learned early the importance of hard work, personal responsibility, and serving others.
Today, she brings those same values to the Thirteenth Court of Appeals, where she is committed to fairness, integrity, and equal justice under law.
