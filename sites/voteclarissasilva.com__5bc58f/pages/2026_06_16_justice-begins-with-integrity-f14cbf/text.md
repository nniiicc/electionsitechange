Home Meet Clarissa Judicial Experience Join Team Silva News DONATE Justice Begins with Integrity June 16, 2026 Trust in the courts begins with trust in the judiciary.
Judges are asked to make decisions that can affect lives, families, businesses, and communities.
That responsibility requires more than knowledge of the law.
It requires character, discipline, humility, and integrity.
Justice Clarissa Silva’s life and career have been shaped by service.
Born and raised in Harlingen, she learned early the importance of helping others without expecting anything in return.
She became the first in her family to graduate from college and law school, then built a legal career focused on public service and the protection of vulnerable Texans.
Before joining the Thirteenth Court of Appeals, Justice Silva handled juvenile matters and cases involving abuse, neglect, and exploitation of the disabled and elderly.
She later represented the Texas Department of Family and Protective Services.
Those experiences reinforced the importance of preparation, fairness, and professionalism in every legal matter.
On the bench, integrity means taking every case seriously.
It means carefully reviewing the record, respecting the law, and making decisions grounded in reason rather than bias or outside pressure.
Justice Silva’s service reflects the belief that public trust must be earned every day.
Through fairness, professionalism, and respect for the rule of law, she continues to uphold the standard South Texas deserves from its judiciary.
