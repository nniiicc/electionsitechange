Justice Clarissa Silva brings more than eighteen years of legal and judicial experience to every case she reviews.
2007 Graduated from Thurgood Marshall School of Law at Texas Southern University and began a career dedicated to public service.
2008 Joined the Nueces County District Attorney’s Office, handling juvenile cases and matters involving abuse, neglect, and exploitation of vulnerable Texans.
2011 Received the Coalition for Crime Victims’ Rights Certificate of Appreciation for service and advocacy on behalf of victims.
2015 Earned Board Certification in Juvenile Law from the Texas Board of Legal Specialization.
2017 Joined the San Patricio County Attorney’s Office representing the Texas Department of Family and Protective Services.
2020 Elected Justice of the Thirteenth Court of Appeals.
2021-Present Reviews civil and criminal appeals from twenty South Texas counties and has authored more than 550 appellate opinions.
