Skip to content Mike Kirchner About OAS Paper Walk or Talk with Mike Kirchner About Mike Kirchner is a retired consultant with 30 years of experience evaluating mostly pension plans.
Mike is from Tiffin, Ohio and a graduate of Akron University with a degree in applied mathematics.
He then joined the Wyatt Company which was known as Towers Watson when he retired.
Mike lives in Highland Heights with Audrey and has two sons who live in Pittsburgh and Cincinnati.
His many hobbies include researching Social Security, catering events for family and friends, volunteering at the Cincinnati Open tennis tournament, running 3 sports pools and skiing.
During his travels, he climbed up Mount Elbert, Longs Peak, Mount Columbia, Guadalope Peak, Mount Washington, and Half Dome.
