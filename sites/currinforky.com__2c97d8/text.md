Meet Aaron Aaron Currin was born and raised in Kentucky’s 65th District.
He grew up here, went to school here, and built his career here.
After graduating from NKU and Chase Law, he chose a life of public service.
For 17 years, Aaron served as a public defender, standing up for people who needed someone in their corner.
Today, he’s a small-business trial attorney, still doing the work right here at home.
Aaron Currin knows this community because he’s lived it, and he’s ready to fight for the people who call it home.
