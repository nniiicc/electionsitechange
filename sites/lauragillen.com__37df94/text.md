Meet Laura Congresswoman Laura Gillen is the former Supervisor for the Town of Hempstead, a lawyer and a mother of four.
In 2024, Laura flipped one of the most competitive House of Representatives seats in the country by focusing on common-sense solutions to the problems that our families face today.
A third generation Long Islander, Laura grew up in Baldwin and is raising her family in Rockville Centre.
During her first term in Congress, Laura has worked across the aisle to help hard working families and has brought home millions of dollars of federal investment to Nassau County.
Laura is running for re-election in New York’s Fourth Congressional District because she’s willing to work with anyone to get things done for Long Island and improve life for all Americans.
