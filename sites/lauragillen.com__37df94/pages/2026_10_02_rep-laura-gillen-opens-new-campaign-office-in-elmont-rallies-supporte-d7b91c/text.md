FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE October 2, 2026 ELMONT, NY – On Friday, Congresswoman Laura Gillen (NY-04) joined volunteers and candidates in Elmont, New York to open a new campaign office ahead of the 2026 election.

Joylette Williams, to launch a community canvass and speak with voters.
