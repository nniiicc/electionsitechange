GILLEN RELEASES NEW TV AD DETAILING DRISCOLL’S COMPLETE ALLEGIANCE TO TRUMP “Driscoll calls Trump’s agenda “Wonderful,” even though it’s crushing Nassau County.” ROCKVILLE CENTRE, N.Y. – Today, Congresswoman Laura Gillen released a new ad, “Serve,” detailing Jeanine Driscoll’s 100% allegiance to President Trump and the economic policies he has implemented.
“Serve” is part of a multimillion-dollar ad campaign that will run through Election Day on broadcast, cable, streaming, and digital platforms.
The ad details how Trump’s agenda has caused gas prices to spike, healthcare costs to skyrocket and food prices to shoot up 33% with no relief in sight.
Long Islanders know that the numbers on every bill are a direct result of the policies that Driscoll praises.
“Nassau County families are getting crushed by rising costs for groceries, gas and healthcare,” said Congresswoman Gillen.
“That hasn’t stopped Jeanine Driscoll from pledging her total support for the hyper partisan agenda that is driving prices higher.
Long Island needs an independent leader in Washington, not a rubber stamp for Trump’s agenda.” Rep.
Gillen has cosponsored and introduced multiple bills to lower costs for commuting, car insurance, federal taxes and more.
Rep.
Gillen wrote the bill to permanently restore the full State and Local Tax (SALT) deduction so Long Islanders can keep more of their hard earned money in their pockets.
