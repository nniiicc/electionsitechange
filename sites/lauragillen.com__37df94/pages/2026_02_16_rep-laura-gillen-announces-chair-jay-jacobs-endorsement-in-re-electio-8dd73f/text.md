Laura Gillen Announces Chair Jay Jacobs Endorsement in Re-Election Campaign FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: February 16, 2026 CONTACT: press@lauragillen.com ROCKVILLE CENTRE, NY – Congresswoman Laura Gillen’s re-election campaign to represent New York’s Fourth Congressional District was endorsed by Nassau County Democratic Committee Chair and New York State Democratic Committee Chair Jay Jacobs.
The endorsement follows resounding support for Gillen’s campaign from House Democratic Leader Hakeem Jeffries, House Democratic Whip Katherine Clark, House Democratic Caucus Chairman Pete Aguilar, and Representative Gregory Meeks (NY-05).
“Laura Gillen has spent her career fighting corruption and working to make life better for Long Islanders, and there is no one better prepared to represent their needs and the Fourth Congressional District.
She has worked tirelessly to bring down costs for her constituents, make their communities safer, and deliver resources to Long Island.
I am proud to endorse her commonsense leadership and commitment to deliver a better future for Long Island’s working families,” said Nassau County Democratic Committee Chair and New York State Democratic Committee Chair Jay Jacobs.
