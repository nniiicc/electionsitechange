GILLEN RELEASES NEW TV AD TOUTING BIPARTISAN RECORD, DEDICATION TO DELIVERING FOR LONG ISLANDERS “I’m Laura Gillen and I’ll work with anyone if it lowers your costs and helps Nassau families.” ROCKVILLE CENTRE, N.Y. – Today, Congresswoman Laura Gillen released a new ad, Top Priority, highlighting her commitment to delivering for Nassau County families.
“Top Priority” is part of a multimillion-dollar ad campaign that will run through Election Day on broadcast, cable, streaming, and digital platforms.
“Nassau County families need a common sense leader,” said Congresswoman Gillen.
“I’ll continue to work with anyone to make life safer and more affordable on Long Island, bring more of our tax dollars back home and deliver real results for Nassau County.” Rep.
Gillen has a consistent record of working with leaders from across the political spectrum to find common ground and ways her constituents can benefit from legislation.
Rep.
Gillen earned an “A” grade from Bridge Grades, labeling her one of the most collaborative House members.
She is ranked the third most bipartisan Democrat in Congress.
Ad transcript: New York City is just a few stops away, but it can seem like another planet.
Look, I know you don’t want crazy, just someone who’ll deliver for Nassau County.
That’s why in Congress I’m working across the aisle to cut middle class taxes.
Going after the insurance companies ripping us off.
And taking on the special interests buying up homes and pricing families out.
I’m Laura Gillen and I’ll work with anyone if it lowers your costs and helps Nassau families.
That’s why I approve this message.
