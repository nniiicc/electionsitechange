I didn’t get into politics for headlines or titles.
I stepped up because I believe rural Minnesota deserves leaders who understand our way of life and aren’t afraid to stand up to government overreach.
I served for ten years in the Minnesota House of Representatives, and in 2022, I was honored to earn the trust of voters to serve in the Minnesota Senate.
Every day, my focus is simple: represent my district honestly, fight for conservative values, and protect the future for our kids and grandkids.
During this past legislative term, I fought to hold state government accountable and put northern Minnesota families first.
I stood up against waste, fraud, and unchecked government spending that has contributed to looming budget deficits, pushing for stronger oversight and transparency so taxpayers know where their money is going.
I opposed budgets that cut funding for nursing homes and long-term care facilities while prioritizing new government programs, and I worked to protect seniors and vulnerable Minnesotans who depend on reliable local care.
I also pushed back against higher fees and growing bureaucracy in environmental legislation, advocating for responsible DNR spending and common-sense permitting reforms that support jobs while protecting our natural resources.
In every debate, my focus was simple: fight for fiscal responsibility, protect our communities, and make sure rural Minnesota has a strong voice in St.
Paul.
