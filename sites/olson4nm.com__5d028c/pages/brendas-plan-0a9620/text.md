Accountable Leadership.
A focused plan for safer neighborhoods, responsible government, stronger infrastructure, and a better future for House District 28.
Putting District 28 First A Plan Built Around Results Families deserve more than political talking points.
They deserve a representative who will listen, study the issues, protect taxpayers, and pursue practical solutions that improve everyday life.
Brenda Olson will bring the discipline, accountability, and careful decision-making she developed through her service in the United States Air Force and the USDA Forest Service to the New Mexico Legislature.
Every decision should be measured by whether it makes District 28 safer, stronger, more affordable, and better prepared for the future.
Six Priorities Focused on the Issues That Matter 01 Public Safety Safer neighborhoods, support for victims, and accountability for violent offenders.
02 Government Accountability Transparent spending, independent oversight, and measurable results.
03 Affordability Responsible budgets, tax relief, and support for local jobs and businesses.
04 Water & Infrastructure Conservation, reliable roads, drainage, utilities, and responsible planning.
05 Healthcare Access Keeping qualified providers in New Mexico and expanding access to care.
06 Responsible Growth Local input, strong infrastructure, and growth that benefits residents.
01 Public Safety Safer Neighborhoods and Real Accountability Every family should feel safe at home, at school, and throughout the community.
Brenda supports a justice system that places victims and public safety first while ensuring serious and repeat offenders face meaningful consequences.
Support stronger consequences for repeat violent offenders.
Advance a Three Strikes approach for serious violent crimes, firearm offenses, and sexual abuse of minors.
Stand with victims and families seeking justice and accountability.
Support law enforcement, prosecutors, and first responders with the tools needed to protect the public.
02 Government Accountability Transparent Government That Respects Taxpayers New Mexicans deserve to know where their money goes and whether state programs are delivering results.
Brenda believes public spending must be transparent, measurable, and accountable.
Support a full, independent audit of state spending.
Identify waste, duplication, and programs that fail to deliver results.
Require clearer public reporting on how taxpayer money is used.
Promote responsible budgeting before creating new programs or obligations.
03 Affordability and Tax Relief Helping Families Keep More of What They Earn Rising costs place real pressure on working families, retirees, and small businesses.
Brenda will judge tax and spending proposals by whether they improve affordability and strengthen household financial security.
Protect taxpayers from unnecessary increases in the cost of government.
Review state spending before asking families to pay more.
Support returning a fair share of natural-resource revenue to New Mexico citizens.
Encourage local jobs, investment, and small-business growth.
04 Water and Infrastructure Protecting Water and Strengthening Essential Systems New Mexico’s water future requires long-term planning, modern infrastructure, and responsible conservation.
Brenda supports practical solutions that reduce waste, protect water quality, and prepare communities for future needs.
Upgrade drainage systems to reduce stormwater pollution and protect neighborhoods.
Promote non-potable water use and drought-resistant landscaping at public facilities.
Improve enforcement of existing rules against public water waste.
Support efficient irrigation, rainwater storage, and responsible conservation.
Prioritize roads, utilities, emergency services, and drainage infrastructure.
05 Healthcare Access Keeping Doctors and Expanding Access to Care New Mexico families need dependable access to qualified healthcare professionals.
Brenda supports reforms that protect patients while helping good providers remain and practice in our state.
Support a fair medical-claims process that protects legitimate patients.
Pursue reforms that reduce excessive malpractice costs without eliminating patient protections.
Expand medical training and residency opportunities in New Mexico.
Support loan-forgiveness and workforce incentives for providers serving local communities.
06 Responsible Growth and Local Control Growth That Strengthens Our Quality of Life Economic development should benefit the people who already live in the community.
Brenda believes major projects must be evaluated carefully, transparently, and with meaningful local input.
Determine who will pay for a project before commitments are made.
Evaluate effects on schools, healthcare, roads, traffic, water, utilities, and emergency services.
Measure whether projects create quality jobs and sustainable public revenue.
Require meaningful public consultation and compliance with local standards.
Ensure local communities help guide decisions involving land and natural resources.
Brenda’s Commitment A Strong Voice for House District 28 Brenda will approach every bill, budget, and policy decision with discipline, transparency, and a commitment to the people she represents.
Listen First Accessible Leadership Hear directly from residents and remain responsive to local concerns.
Study the Details Careful Decisions Review legislation thoroughly before casting a vote.
Protect Taxpayers Responsible Spending Demand transparency, value, and measurable results.
