PROTECT HOMEOWNERSHIP
AND NEIGHBORHOOD QUALITY
Protect Local Decision-Making
- Oppose one-size-fits-all housing mandates from St.
Paul that override local planning.
- Support the ability of cities like Woodbury to make development decisions that reflect community priorities.
- Ensure residents have meaningful opportunities to provide input on major development proposals.
- Advocate for transparency and public engagement throughout the planning process.
Keep Property Taxes Affordable
- Oppose state mandates that shift costs onto local governments and property taxpayers.
- Support responsible state budgeting to reduce pressure on local property tax increases.
- Review unfunded mandates that increase costs for cities, counties, and school districts.
- Advocate for property tax relief for homeowners, seniors, and families facing rising housing costs.
Support Responsible Growth
- Encourage housing policies that meet community needs while preserving quality of life.
- Support infrastructure investments to ensure roads, schools, parks, and public safety keep pace with growth.
- Promote balanced development that strengthens the tax base and attracts employers.
- Ensure growth decisions consider traffic, public safety, and community impacts.
Expand Opportunities for First-Time Homebuyers
- Support efforts to reduce unnecessary regulatory costs that increase housing prices.
- Expand access to first-time homebuyer assistance and financial education programs.
- Encourage development of realistic attainable housing.
- Promote policies that help the next generation achieve the dream of homeownership.
The people who know our community best are the people who live here.
I'll fight to protect local control, keep property taxes affordable, support responsible growth, and expand opportunities for homeownership.
As your State Representative, I will fight to keep housing affordable, protect local decision-making, and ensure families can continue to build their future in the community they call home.
