WATER QUALITY AND ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP Access to safe, reliable drinking water is not a partisan issue—it's a basic responsibility of government.
As your State Representative, I will work to secure the resources needed to protect our water supply while ensuring environmental policies remain practical, effective, and affordable for families. ​ Balance Environmental Goals with Affordability Evaluate environmental policies based on both environmental outcomes and economic impact.
Oppose mandates that dramatically increase utility bills or housing costs without clear public benefit.
Seek solutions that protect natural resources while maintaining Minnesota's affordability and competitiveness.
Ensure the needs of working families, seniors, and homeowners are considered when implementing environmental regulations. ​ Promote Practical Conservation Support conservation initiatives that produce measurable environmental benefits.
Encourage partnerships with local governments, businesses, and residents to protect natural resources.
Promote responsible stewardship of lakes, wetlands, parks, and open spaces.
Favor incentive-based approaches that encourage participation rather than excessive regulation. ​ Protect Drinking Water Infrastructure Support investments in water treatment facilities, pipelines, wells, and aging infrastructure.
Promote proactive maintenance to prevent costly emergency repairs.
Ensure state infrastructure funding is distributed fairly to growing communities like Woodbury.
Strengthen oversight to ensure taxpayer dollars are spent efficiently on critical infrastructure projects. ​ Secure State Funding for Long-Term Water Solutions Advocate for state funding to help communities address PFAS contamination and other emerging water quality challenges.
Ensure that local taxpayers are not left carrying the entire cost of state and federal environmental requirements.
Support long-term investments in water treatment technologies that provide lasting solutions.
Prioritize funding for communities facing significant infrastructure and remediation costs. ​ Woodbury families deserve clean, safe drinking water without being saddled with unaffordable costs.
I will fight for state resources to address long-term water challenges, invest in critical infrastructure, and pursue practical environmental solutions that protect both our natural resources and taxpayers.
