ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE
Every child deserves access to a high-quality education that prepares them for success in life.
As your State Representative, I will advocate for policies that put students, parents, and teachers first.
Focus on Academic Achievement
- Support legislation that prioritizes reading, writing, math, science, and civics.
- Advocate for evidence-based literacy programs to ensure every child can read proficiently by third grade.
- Promote regular measurement of student progress and transparent reporting of academic outcomes
Empower Parents
- Ensure parents have access to curriculum information and academic performance data.
- Support policies that strengthen communication between schools and families.
- Protect parents' role as the primary decision-makers in their children's education.
Expand Career and Technical Education
- Increase funding for career and technical education (CTE) programs.
- Strengthen partnerships among schools, local businesses, trade organizations, and community colleges.
- Create more pathways for students pursuing skilled trades, apprenticeships, military service, and workforce careers.
Promote Accountability and Local Control
- Support high academic standards and measurable results.
- Give local school districts greater flexibility to meet the needs of their students.
- Review state mandates to ensure schools can focus resources on student learning.
School Safety and Student Success
- Advocate for safe learning environments where students can focus on learning.
- Expand access to school counselors, mental health resources, and anti-bullying programs.
- Support policies that improve attendance, student engagement, and graduation rates.
Strong schools are one essential to maintaining the quality of life that attracts families to Woodbury.
I will work to protect and strengthen that tradition of excellence by focusing on academic achievement, parental involvement, teacher support, and preparing every student for future success.
