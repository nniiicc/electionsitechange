SAFE COMMUNITIES
SAFE SCHOOLS
Every family deserves to feel safe in their neighborhood, at school, and throughout the community.
As your State Representative, I will work to ensure that public safety remains a top priority while supporting proactive solutions that prevent crime and help those in crisis.
Strengthen School Safety
- Support funding for school safety improvements, including secure entrances, communication systems, and emergency preparedness training.
- Encourage strong partnerships between schools, law enforcement, and parents.
- Promote age-appropriate safety education and crisis response planning.
- Ensure schools have access to the resources needed to maintain a safe learning environment.
- Support initiatives that promote respect, responsibility, and positive school culture.
- Encourage parental involvement and transparency when addressing student safety concerns.
Support Local Law Enforcement
- Advocate for state funding that helps local police departments recruit, train, and retain qualified officers.
- Support grants for updated equipment, technology, and public safety resources.
- Promote strong partnerships among law enforcement, schools, and community organizations.
- Ensure first responders have the tools they need to keep our communities safe.
Hold Repeat Violent Offenders Accountable
- Support stronger penalties for repeat violent offenders who pose an ongoing threat to public safety.
- Advocate for policies that prioritize victims' rights and community safety.
- Work to reduce repeat offenses through effective supervision and accountability measures.
Expand Mental Health and Crisis Intervention Services
- Increase access to mental health services for children, families, and veterans.
- Support crisis response programs that connect individuals with treatment before situations escalate.
- Expand school-based mental health resources and early intervention programs.
Safe communities and safe schools are the foundation of a strong Woodbury.
I will support law enforcement, hold violent offenders accountable, and work to ensure every child can learn in a safe and supportive environment.
