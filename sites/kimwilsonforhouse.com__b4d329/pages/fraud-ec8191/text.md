FIGHT FRAUD
Families in Woodbury work hard for every dollar they earn.
They deserve a state government that respects those dollars, operates transparently, and delivers results.
I will fight for stronger oversight, greater transparency, and accountability at every level of state government.
Fight Fraud and Protect Taxpayer Dollars
- Increase penalties for individuals and organizations that defraud taxpayer-funded programs.
- Support stronger fraud detection and prevention systems across state agencies.
- Require agencies to report fraud investigations, recoveries, and corrective actions.
- Strengthen oversight and accountability for taxpayer-funded programs.
- Require measurable outcomes before expanding government programs.
- Ensure budget decisions reflect the needs of Minnesota families, not government growth for its own sake.
Improve Agency Performance
- Support regular performance audits of major state agencies.
- Require agencies to establish measurable goals and publicly report results.
- Identify and eliminate unnecessary bureaucracy.
My experience on the Woodbury City Council has taught me that public trust is earned through transparency, responsible budgeting, and accountability.
I will bring those same principles to
St.
Paul by championing a government that is efficient, transparent, and accountable to taxpayers.
