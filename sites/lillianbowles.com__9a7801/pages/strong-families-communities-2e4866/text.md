Working Families & Strong Communities
Strong families and communities are the foundation of a healthy, stable Utah.
That means making sure people feel safe where they live, can afford to stay in their homes, and have access to the resources they need to support their families.
It also means investing in the places where we live—our neighborhoods, schools, and local economies—and making sure people have a real voice in the decisions that shape their communities.
When families are supported and communities are connected, everything else works better.
Working Families:
Supporting every family with stability and opportunity.
Supporting every family with stability and opportunity means making sure people can afford to live, work, and plan for the future.
I will focus on lowering everyday costs, expanding access to healthcare and childcare, and creating real pathways to good-paying jobs so families can build secure, stable live
What I’ll Work On
Support Working Families
- Lower costs, expand access to childcare and healthcare, and create pathways to good-paying jobs.
Worker Voice & Fair Wages
- Protect workers, strengthen wages, and support the right to organize and be heard at work.
Economic Stability
- Help families stay financially secure and plan for the future.
Strong Communities:
Safe, connected communities where every voice matters.
Safe, connected communities where every voice matters don’t happen by accident—they’re built through smart investment and real engagement.
I will support public safety, modern infrastructure, and local economic growth while making sure residents have a meaningful voice in the decisions that shape their neighborhoods.
What I’ll Work On:
Public Safety & Infrastructure
- Invest in public safety and modern infrastructure—safe roads, reliable water, and well-maintained public spaces.
Community Involvement
- Prioritize transparency and community input so residents have a real voice in local decisions.
Local Investment
- Support smart local investment that strengthens neighborhoods and small businesses.
