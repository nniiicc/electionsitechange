Feb.
24, 2022 Minnesota Citizens Concerned for Life (MCCL), our state’s oldest and largest group dedicated solely to the protection of innocent human life, is recognizing several pro-life champions around the state who deserve our thanks.
And we’re happy to say that among those champions is state Senator Gene Dornink.
Our thousands of members and pro-lifers who receive our mailings in Gene's district probably already know of his 100% pro-life questionnaires and voting records.
Sen.
Dornink is committed in the ﬁght to protect the unborn from abortion, to end taxpayer funding of abortion, to protect women’s right to know about alternatives to abortion, and to provide women with support when they choose Life.
At the Capitol, Gene has been not just a vote, but also an important voice for the unborn.
I can’t think of a more honored legacy for a legislator than to be known for using his time at the Capitol to ﬁght for the most defenseless among us.
Our thanks to Sen.
Dornink for his strong and consistent stand for Life!
