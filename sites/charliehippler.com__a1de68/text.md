Meet Charlie My name is Charlie Hippler, and I'm running to represent Manchester, West Gardiner, and Hallowell in our state legislature.
I've called Manchester home my entire life.
I attended both Maranacook High School and Thomas College in Waterville.
Today, I live in Manchester and work in Hallowell as a commercial real estate broker.
I'm deeply invested in this community's future.
As the current Vice Chair of the Manchester Selectboard and Chair of the Manchester Sanitary District, I've put that same commitment to work for the people of Manchester.
Public service isn't new to me: it's something I strongly believe in.
I would be honored to earn your vote and carry our district's voice to the State Legislature.
