Unsubscribe
You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email, replying STOP to any text, or contacting us at erika@electerika.com.
FEC Disclosure
Federal and state law require political committees to report the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of individuals who contribute more than $200 in aggregate per election cycle.
This information becomes part of the public record.
