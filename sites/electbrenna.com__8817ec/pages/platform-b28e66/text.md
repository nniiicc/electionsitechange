Strong Public Schools Public funds belong in public schools Indiana’s public schools are the backbone of our communities.
They serve every student who walks through the door and anchor the towns and neighborhoods that make our state strong.
When we fully fund them, we invest in smaller class sizes, competitive teacher pay, and the support every student deserves.
Vouchers pull public dollars into private schools that aren’t required to serve all students or report how taxpayer money is spent.
That leaves public schools doing more with less - almost half a billion dollars went to vouchers in the 2024-2025 school year.
Public money should strengthen the schools that serve every child in every community.
Fully funding public education is how we build a stronger Indiana.
Accessible and Affordable Healthcare Indiana families deserve accessible, affordable healthcare , yet rising premiums, shrinking provider networks, and long wait times mean basic care is slipping out of reach.
The crisis is accelerating.
Early 2026 data shows nearly 60,000 fewer Hoosiers enrolled in ACA marketplace coverage than the year before — tens of thousands now without reliable insurance.
At the same time, hospitals and insurers operate with little transparency, prescription drug costs continue to climb without real accountability, and rural communities are losing clinics, forcing residents to travel long distances just to see a doctor.
Indiana’s public health funding ranks 48th in the nation , and the instability makes things worse.
County health departments saw major increases in 2024 and 2025, only to face cuts of more than 70% heading into 2026.
These wild swings make it nearly impossible to plan longterm outreach, maintain staffing, or meet community needs.
I support consistent, reliable funding so local health departments can do their jobs effectively.
I also support expanding access to contraception, maternity care, and ensuring women can make their own medical decisions without political interference.
Healthcare is necessity, not a luxury.
Hoosiers deserve fair pricing, real access, and a system that puts people first.
That’s how we build a healthier, stronger Indiana.

These facilities are exempt from sales tax on their massive utility usage while consuming nearly half of the state’s total power output .

Hoosiers deserve policies that put people first and ensure growth actually strengthens our communities.
Protecting Democracy A strong democracy depends on informed, engaged communities.
When people understand how government works, feel empowered to participate, and trust that their voices matter, our institutions become more responsive and effective.
I support accessible voter information , strong civic education , and community partnerships that encourage engagement so every Hoosier feels confident using their voice.
People deserve to know how decisions are made and how public resources are used.
I support transparent government practices , open communication with communities , and clear reporting on spending to strengthen trust in our institutions.
Protecting individual rights is essential to a functioning democracy.
I support strong civil liberty protections , fair treatment under the law , and policies that ensure every person can speak, worship, and live freely without discrimination or government overreach.
A healthy democracy respects both religious freedom and the constitutional boundary between faith and government.
I support policies that keep public institutions neutral while safeguarding every individual’s right to practice - or not practice - their faith.
Democracy grows from the ground up.
I support local civic leadership development , public forums that elevate community voices , and inclusive decision making so every person has a seat at the table.
