“Reproductive freedom, affordable housing, jobs, an end to homelessness and safe schools/neighborhoods.
These are the issues I champion on behalf of the East Bay and every Californian.” -Liz
My Priorities.
- Affordable Workplace and Family Housing “Too many people are getting priced out of the East Bay.
I continue to fight the system, in order to make housing affordable for teachers, nurses, and first responders.
My daughter and your future generations should be able to live in the communities we all love.”
- Expanding Jobs that pay a Living Wage Creation “I have a proven track record of placing hundreds of people into well-paying jobs—and I ’ll champion our small East Bay businesses, who make us stronger by creating great jobs in the communities we love.”
- Working to End Homelessness “Homelessness is a statewide and East Bay crisis.
Besides expanding affordable housing options and well-paying jobs, we must effectively address addiction challenges.
That’s why I’ve introduced legislation to address the devastating effects of fentanyl overdoses by requiring MediCal and private insurers to cover the cost of the life-saving medication Naloxone.”
- Community and School Neighborhood Safety “Every family deserves to live in a safe neighborhood.
I continue to advocate for neighborhood-based policing approaches where trained, accountable officers are working with the community to fight gun violence.
And we must secure our school sites so our children and teachers are safe”
