Kirschner launches his campaign at a moment when Gulf Coast families brace for the next insurance renewal notice, watch the weekly grocery total climb, and dread every new health care bill that lands in their mailbox.

A District That Reflects His Life
Kirschner enters the race with a proven track record of taking on and defeating powerful special interests and deep roots in this newly-drawn district.
No matter how this district was cobbled together, Kelly Kirschner has lived it.
His connection to the district is lifelong and personal.
Florida’s massive new Congressional District 16, stretches from St.
Petersburg through Sarasota and Manatee Counties, and beyond.
It is roughly twice the size of Rhode Island and is widely regarded as one of the most egregiously gerrymandered districts in the state.
Critics across the political spectrum have called it a symbol of everything that is broken in Tallahassee and in Washington D.C.
He has lived in two of its six counties, and worked in three of them.
Questions have been raised in the press about whether any candidate in this race truly knows St.
Petersburg?
Kirschner’s answer is his record — 14 years of sustained civic engagement in the city:
Eckerd College — Vice President and Dean, Executive and Continuing Education (13 years):
Kirschner led all of Eckerd College’s non-credit and executive education programs, building partnerships across the Tampa Bay business community and earning a reputation as one of the region’s foremost voices on workforce and lifelong learning.
St.
Petersburg Area Chamber of Commerce — Board Member:
A decade plus of service on the Chamber’s board, helping shape the city’s business climate and economic direction.
Foundation for a Healthy St.
Petersburg — Board Member (5 years):
Serving on the board of one of St.
Pete’s most important public health institutions, working to reduce health inequities across the city.
TigerBay Club of Pinellas County — Past President and board member:
Leadership of the region’s leading nonpartisan political forum, hosting candidates and elected officials from across the spectrum.
Outspent.

He Still Won.
Kirschner’s path to victory in this election will be built on a record of winning where he wasn’t supposed to.
In his first run for City Commission, he was outspent three-to-one by an incumbent backed by special interest money.
He won that race with 75% of the vote.
As neighborhood activist, Mayor, and Commissioner, he successfully took on powerful interests like Florida Power & Light, sweetheart marina deals and predatory developers.
And he led the charge to pass the strongest municipal campaign finance reform legislation in Florida’s history.
The over $100,000 raised in the campaign’s first week demonstrates this same grassroots momentum — a broad coalition of donors across the Gulf Coast believe this race can be won.
About Kelly Kirschner
A graduate of Georgetown University and a returned U.S.
Peace Corps Guatemala volunteer, Kirschner has spent the last fourteen years working and serving across the new District 16, from Sarasota to St.
Petersburg.
His work with the regional non-profit UnidosNow has helped over 1,000 first-generation students earn scholarships and attend the nation’s top universities without debt.
