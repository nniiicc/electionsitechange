PETERSBURG, FL — Democrat Kelly Kirschner leads Republican Sydney Gruters 40% to 39% in a new survey of Florida’s 16th Congressional District.
Download Poll Results (PDF) The live interviewer/text to web poll was conducted by SEA Research & Political Design from August 25 to 29 and surveyed 400 respondents across the newly drawn 16th Congressional District which spans from St.
Petersburg to Arcadia.
Republicans represent 47% of the universe, Democrats 34% and voters with no major party affiliation, 19%.
The margin of error was +/- 4.9%.

When Kelly Kirschner was Mayor, he stood up to Florida Power & Light, exposed bad deals for taxpayers and took on powerful special interests.
That’s what people want to see in their next Congressman, not another political insider looking for a payday,” said campaign spokesman, Charlie Goldstein.

Residents get the insurance bills, the unmanaged growth, the flood risk, and the higher utility rates.” The poll was conducted after the Republican and Democratic primaries where Sydney Gruters spent almost a half million dollars from her campaign and received over $2,000,000 of outside support.
That money does not appear to have helped her prospects in the general election against Kirschner.
Kirschner is leading in a district where Republicans carry a six-point advantage on the generic congressional ballot, 49% to 43%.
He is running roughly seven points ahead of the party brand because voters in FL-16 have concluded the system is rigged: corruption in government (41%) and the cost of living (40%) are the district’s top two concerns.
