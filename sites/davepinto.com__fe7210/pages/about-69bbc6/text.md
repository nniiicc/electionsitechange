Home About Dave Priorities Get Involved Volunteer Lawn Signs Endorsements Vote contribute About Dave Dave represents the Highland Park, Mac-Groveland, and Fort Road / West Seventh neighborhoods of St.
Paul in the Minnesota House.
A lifelong Minnesotan, he is a progressive champion for our community.
A Champion for Our Community As a felony prosecutor focused on crimes of gender violence, Dave keeps our community safe.
His policy work focuses on the well-being of children; on criminal legal reform and community safety; and on building an economy and society that allow everyone to thrive.
Education Dave grew up in Falcon Heights and graduated from Roseville Area High School and Harvard University.
He earned graduate degrees in law and management from the University of Virginia, where he received the highest award granted by the University’s honor society, recognizing service and contribution.
Public Service In his early career, Dave worked in the office of Congressman Bruce Vento, at the Clinton White House, and for Judge Diana Murphy, the first woman to be appointed a federal district judge in Minnesota.
He is a proud member of AFSCME Council 5.
Fighting for the Vulnerable Outside of the Legislature, Dave serves as a prosecutor in Ramsey County, where he has specialized in prosecuting domestic assault and sex trafficking.
He also directed statewide training and protocol development for Safe Harbor, Minnesota’s system to address sexual exploitation.
