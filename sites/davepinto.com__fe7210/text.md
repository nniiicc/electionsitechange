If you live in Minnesota, the first $75 ($150 per couple) that you contribute to a state campaign is reimbursed to you, under the Political Contribution Refund program.
It’s limited to one campaign of your choice per year, and it’s designed to help reduce the influence of big dollars on campaigns.
