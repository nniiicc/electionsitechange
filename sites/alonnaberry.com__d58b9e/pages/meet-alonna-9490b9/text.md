Alonna and the First State
Alonna Berry is a dedicated public servant and experienced leader who has spent her career working at the intersection of government, education, and community advocacy.
A resident of Sussex County, Alonna lives in Milton with her husband and their two sons.
She is running to remain in the Delaware House of Representatives to continue to be a strong, principled voice for District 20—focused on creating opportunity for every family to thrive, now and in the future.
Alonna comes from a long line of veterans and public servants.
Her father retired from the United States Air Force, and her husband is a U.S.
Army veteran.
She herself served two years as an AmeriCorps service member.
Since then, she has contributed her time and leadership to a range of nonprofit boards and local initiatives across Delaware, including the Milton Historical Society, Delaware Guidance Services, the Youth Philanthropy Board, and Next Generation South.
She currently serves as the Governor’s appointee to the Delaware Community Foundation board.
Alonna’s leadership and dedication have been recognized across Delaware.
She was honored with the MillSummit Spur Impact Award and the Sussex County Health Coalition Community Partner Award in 2021.
In 2020, she was named one of Delaware Business Times' 40 Under 40.
She received the Jefferson Award for Public Service for the Delmarva region, highlighting her commitment to building stronger, more connected communities through service and advocacy.
Alonna In Action
Alonna has made impactful changes in the year she has been in the House.
Her House bills reflect a deep care for accessible healthcare, public safety protocols, expansion for education services, and fair and well-compensated labor and work opportunities.
She has served as a key advocate for gun control, widening of rights for healthcare workers, moves to make rural healthcare affordable and available, and an advocate for increased school transportation and services for students with autism.
Her commitment to these issues will remain strong as she approaches re-election.
Alonna has been working hard in the House as well as on the ground in District 20.
Her visits include the Delaware Rural Health Conference, Prime Hook National Wildlife Refuge, and Delaware Together Day.
She cares deeply about interacting with the people and land she serves, and to meet successes and opportunities for improvement right where they are.
Before serving as state representative, she served in the office of Governor John Carney in family services, healthy, equity, and education.
She also leads a non-profit focusing on equity and criminal justice reform in Delaware to build our communities and keep us safe.
