About Me
Jim Butler for House of Delegates, 18th District
I am a Christian, husband, father, former United States Marine, small local business owner, and a recent member of the West Virginia House of Delegates.
I have two children, my son (21) who is in the US Naval Academy, and a daughter (18) who is an honor student and will graduate high school soon.
Our immediate family has lived on the same family farm in Gallipolis Ferry, West Virginia for over 30 years, a farm where my grandparents lived on since the 1940s.
