Aloha, I’m Jamie Wataru!
My Life of Service
Hawaiʻi Kai has been home since I was eight.
It’s where I grew up, raised my family, and learned what it means to show up for others.
My Roots and My Family
I attended Hahaione, Koko Head, Niu Valley, and Kaiser High School.
Regina and I have been married for 34 years and have five sons: Jonah, Justus, Josiah, Jace, and Joey.
For more than two decades, we’ve also welcomed medically complex infants into our home through foster care.
Caring for keiki is an important part of our family’s life.
A Career in the Trades
My 35-year plumbing career began with an apprenticeship through Plumbers Union Local 675.
Since 1998, I’ve worked as a journeyman plumber for the State of Hawaiʻi, maintaining infrastructure across Oʻahu.
I also serve as a Safety Representative for my fellow public employees.
Giving Back
Service has taken me from the job site to community boards, labor organizations, and Kaiser’s athletic fields.
Civic and board service
- Chair & Trustee, Hawaiʻi Employer-Union Health Benefits Trust Fund (EUTF)
- Board Member, Blood Bank of Hawaiʻi, Hawaiʻi Food Bank, and Goodwill Industries of Hawaiʻi
- Former Chair, Honolulu Fire Commission

Supporting Kaiser High School
- Football and powerlifting coach
- Volunteer sports announcer
- Kaiser High School Athletic Foundation supporter
- Ten years of concession stand fundraising for student-athletes
I’m running for Hawaiʻi State House, District 18, with a commitment to practical leadership, transparency, and listening to our community.
Why I’m running…
Rising costs, neighborhood safety, and slow government action affect daily life in our district.
I want to bring steady leadership to the Capitol, with clear priorities and follow-through.
My Priorities
- Public safety Support effective policing and clear consequences that protect residents and businesses.
- Accountable government Make public spending transparent, set clear timelines, and hold projects to their budgets.
- Protecting our natural landscapes Preserve shorelines and open spaces, enforce existing rules, and keep growth responsible.
