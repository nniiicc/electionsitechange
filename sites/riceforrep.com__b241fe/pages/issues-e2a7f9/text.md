WHERE TODD STANDS
BRING INVESTMENT TO DISTRICT 8
District 8 has a unique opportunity to drive innovation in Oklahoma.
Todd will encourage and welcome companies into our communities, because our people deserve great jobs and the ability to support their families.
Todd will fight to make District 8 the best place to live and turn our home into a leader across Oklahoma.
KEEP OUR JOBS IN OKLAHOMA
Oklahomans are losing their jobs as companies pack up and leave for states with better public policy.
Todd knows what it takes to operate a business and knows the impact that failing policy has on business owners and employees.
He will fight to keep the companies of District 8 right here at home by cutting regulations and creating a business-friendly Oklahoma.
STRENGTHEN OUR SCHOOLS
Our kids deserve an education that sets them up for successful futures, whatever those futures may be.
Todd will fight to give our schools the resources they need because our kids deserve access to the best education, regardless of whether the future is taking them to tech school or college.
PROTECT OUR VALUES
Todd has lived with the values of District 8 his entire life, and he is proud to support the Oklahoma way of life.
He will support our law enforcement officers, fight against government overreach, protect our unborn, and defend our constitutional rights from those who wish to take them away.
