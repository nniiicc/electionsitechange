TODD RICE FOR OKLAHOMA HOUSE DISTRICT 8
Todd Rice is a conservative business owner and lifelong resident of House District 8, running to bring limited government, fiscal responsibility, and pro-business leadership to Oklahoma.
As a small-business owner, he knows how policies impact real businesses and working families—and he’s ready to fight for a stronger, more competitive Oklahoma.
WHY I'M RUNNING
"Oklahoma families deserve leadership that listens, acts, and delivers.
I’m running because families are feeling the pressure of rising costs, small businesses need support to grow and compete, and communities deserve safer infrastructure and better opportunities.
I believe House District 8 needs a strong voice who will advocate for real solutions—not politics as usual."
- Economic Growth & Jobs:
Todd supports policies that encourage business development, attract investment, and create good-paying jobs across Oklahoma.
- Fiscal Responsibility & Local Investment:
Todd will advocate for improvements to roads, bridges, and local infrastructure to support long-term growth.
He is committed to responsible budgeting, cutting waste, and ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent efficiently.
- Public Focus & Education:
As a father of two children, Todd knows strong communities require safe neighborhoods.
Todd supports law enforcement and policies that protect Oklahoma families.
He believes in strengthening education while ensuring local control and accountability in schools.
