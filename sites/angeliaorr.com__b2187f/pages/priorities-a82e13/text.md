Real Property Tax Relief
Property taxes shouldn’t force families out of their homes.
That’s why Angelia Orr is delivering real, lasting relief for Texas homeowners—not just talk.
When Orr first took office in 2023, the homestead exemption was just $40,000.
In just two legislative sessions, she helped pass laws to raise it to $140,000—more than triple the original amount.
That’s real savings for Texas families.
Orr also supported landmark legislation that delivered $51 billion in property tax relief—the largest in Texas history.
She’s also addressing the high taxes small business owners face.
As a small business owner herself, Orr helped raise the personal property tax exemption for small businesses from $2,500 to $125,000.
For many of the local mom-and-pop shops that call our community home, that change could completely eliminate their personal property tax bill—freeing them up to grow, hire, and thrive.
While some politicians throw around the phrase “eliminate property taxes” as a talking point, Orr is taking action.
She co-authored a bill to study the responsible phase-out of property taxes—because she knows real solutions require real work.
Her approach is practical, grounded, and focused on results—not headlines.
She’s also fighting to rein in runaway appraisals, protect homestead rights, and stop local governments from using loopholes to raise taxes behind closed doors.

Strengthen Rural Healthcare
Angelia Orr is the leading voice for rural healthcare in the Texas Legislature.
As Chair of the Appropriations Subcommittee overseeing nearly one-third of the state budget for health and human services, she ensures House District 13 has a seat at the table when major funding decisions are made.
Texas leads the nation with 21 rural hospital closures over the past decade.
In many areas, pregnant women must drive hours to reach a hospital, putting their health and their babies’ at risk.
Orr is stepping up to the challenge.
She co-authored HB 3000, establishing a $90 million Rural Ambulance Grant Program to improve emergency medical services and save lives.
She also helped secure $100 million in HB 18 funding to stabilize rural hospitals and expand critical services like pediatric mental health, maternal care, telehealth, and staff training.
Additional budget wins include millions for Medicaid rural hospital rates, mobile women’s health units, stroke units, mental health outreach, and telepsychiatry.
Out of dozens of clinics statewide, only five mental health centers received targeted funding this session—and Orr secured it for one right here in House District 13.
Orr is delivering real results that protect rural Texans and their healthcare.

Secure Texas Elections
As a former elections judge, Angelia Orr understands firsthand the importance of fair, transparent, and secure elections.
She’s committed to protecting every legal vote and making sure Texans can trust the results.
Orr supports strong, commonsense reforms that improve election security and prevent fraud, including:
- HB 1661: Increases penalties for tampering with election supplies and interfering with voting, helping deter fraud and protect the process.
- SB 1470: Requires the Department of Public Safety to share data to keep the voter registration list accurate and up to date, preventing duplicate or invalid registrations.
- SB 1862: Improves interstate communication to stop illegal voting by people registered in multiple states.
- SB 2166: Ensures voting machines are thoroughly tested and verified for accuracy and security.
- SB 2753: Modernizes voting procedures by integrating early and election-day voting, speeding vote counts and increasing transparency.
- SJR 37: Proposes a constitutional amendment that clearly states only U.S. citizens can vote in Texas.
This stops cities or local governments from allowing illegal voting by non-citizens, protecting every Texas ballot.
- SB 985: Allows election officials to combine smaller precincts to run elections more efficiently.

For generations, families and farms depend on reliable water from aquifers and local systems to survive and thrive.

Secure The Power Grid
A reliable power grid is critical for Texas families, farms, and businesses—especially during storms and extreme heat.
Angelia Orr supports strong measures to improve grid reliability, protect infrastructure, and ensure Texans have affordable energy.
She backed key legislation including:
- SB 2148 to secure the electricity supply chain and prevent disruptions;
- HB 14 and SB 1535 to develop a skilled workforce for the growing nuclear energy industry;
