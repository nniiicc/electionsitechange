Meet Angelia Orr
Rural Texas' Proven Conservative Voice
Angelia Orr, proudly endorsed by President Donald Trump, Governor Greg Abbott, and Senator Ted Cruz, is serving in her second term as State Representative of House District 13.
She is a wife, mother, small business owner, and lifelong conservative rooted deeply in the values of rural Texas.
She and her husband, Will—whose family has farmed and ranched in Hill County since 1872—live in the home they built together, in the community where they raised their two children, run several family businesses, and worship at First Baptist Church Hillsboro.
