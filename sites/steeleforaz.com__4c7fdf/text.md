Meet Frank Steele
Frank Steele
Frank Steele is an Arizona native, U.S.
Army veteran, and longtime community leader who has dedicated his life to serving with integrity and common sense.
Grounded in a deep respect for Arizona families and the values that make our state strong, he believes public service should be rooted in accountability, fairness, and a commitment to results—not politics.
Now running for the Arizona State Senate in LD24, Frank is fighting for honest government, safe communities, and an economy that rewards hard work.
He supports lower taxes for working families and small businesses, protection for seniors on fixed incomes, strong public education with real accountability, and law enforcement that upholds both professionalism and community trust.
For Frank Steele, the mission is clear: put Arizona families first and lead with clarity, courage, and compassion.
What's At Stake
The Arizona State Senate decides whether your taxes go up, who controls the direction of our justice system, and whether Arizona passes common-sense policy — or folds to lobbyists and big interests.
Frank Steele is running to protect you, not the powerful.
Frank's Platform
The Arizona State Senate affects every family, every taxpayer, and every organization in this state — but most people don’t know much about it.
