Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 23 September 2026
Steele for Arizona (“we,” “our,” or “the Campaign”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information obtained through steeleforaz.com and related campaign communications.
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including but not limited to:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Information submitted through contact forms, sign-ups, event RSVPs, donations, or surveys
We may also automatically collect limited technical information such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, solely to improve website functionality and security.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Communicate campaign updates, news, and events
- Respond to inquiries and requests
- Provide information about Frank Steele’s campaign and positions
- Solicit volunteer participation or donations (where applicable)
- Send SMS or MMS messages in compliance with 10DLC messaging requirements
- Improve website performance and user experience
- Comply with applicable laws and regulations
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
SMS / Text Messaging (10DLC Compliance)
By providing your mobile phone number and opting in, you consent to receive campaign-related text messages from Frank Steele for Arizona.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
- Consent is not a condition of volunteering, purchase, or donation
- You may opt out at any time by replying STOP
- For help, reply HELP
All messaging is conducted in accordance with A2P 10DLC regulations and applicable carrier requirements.
Mobile information and SMS opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
SMS opt-in data and consent may be provided only to service providers necessary to deliver the messaging program, and not for their own marketing.
SMS enrollment requires a separate, optional opt-in.
Providing a phone number or submitting a volunteer form without selecting SMS consent does not subscribe you to recurring texts.
See our SMS Terms.
For assistance, email [email protected].
