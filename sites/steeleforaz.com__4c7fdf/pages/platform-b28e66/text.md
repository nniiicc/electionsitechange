Frank Steele's Platform
Helping Senior Citizens
I support protecting seniors on fixed incomes by eliminating or significantly reducing property taxes.
Save Taxpayer Money
I support lower income taxes so working families and small businesses can keep more of what they earn.
Great Education
I support Arizona’s leadership in school choice while strengthening public schools and holding all schools accountable for results.
Reliable Leadership
I believe government should be efficient, transparent, and focused on solving problems instead of creating them.
Safe Communities
I strongly support law enforcement while insisting on professionalism, accountability, and community trust.
