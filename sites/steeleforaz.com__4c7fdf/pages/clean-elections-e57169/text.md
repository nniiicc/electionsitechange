Many Arizonans don’t realize it, but there’s a way to run for office without taking a single penny from corporations, unions, or lobbyists.
It’s called the Citizens Clean Elections program , and it allows candidates like Frank Steele to run a truly grassroots, people-powered campaign .
Created by Arizona voters in 1998, the Clean Elections system provides public funding to candidates who agree to strict guidelines — including rejecting PAC money and collecting a specific number of $5 contributions from voters like you.
