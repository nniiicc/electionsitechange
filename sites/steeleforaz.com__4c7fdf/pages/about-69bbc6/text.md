Meet Frank Steele
I’m Frank Steele—an Arizona native, Army veteran, and longtime community leader who believes public service should be grounded in integrity, common sense, and respect for the people it serves.
I’m running for State Senate because Arizona families deserve honest government, safe communities, and an economy that rewards hard work.
I believe in the rule of law applied fairly and humanely, lower taxes so working families and small businesses can keep more of what they earn, and protecting seniors on fixed incomes from being taxed out of their homes.
Arizona is my home.
I support strong public education, parental involvement, and real accountability—because every child deserves a chance to succeed.
I strongly support law enforcement while also insisting on professionalism, accountability, and community trust.
My goal is simple: represent our district with clarity, courage, and compassion—always putting Arizona families first.
