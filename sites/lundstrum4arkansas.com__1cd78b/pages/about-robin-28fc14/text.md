Meet Arkansas State Representative Robin Lundstrum
Representative Robin Lundstrum is serving her second term in the Arkansas House of Representatives.
She represents District 87 which includes portions of Benton and Washington County.
During the 91st General Assembly, Rep.
Lundstrum serves on the Joint Committee on Energy, the committee for Insurance and Commerce, and the committee for Public Health Welfare and Labor.
“I grew up in Northwest Arkansas.
I went to school here, raised my family here and grew my business here.
Now I want to serve you in our state’s capitol.”
A little fact you may not know is the Little Rock Division of the FBI honored Robin Lundstrum for her work enhancing law enforcement partnerships in Northwest Arkansas and serving as an advocate for youth programs, she was presented the award at the FBI Headquarters in Washington, D.C. by the Director of the FBI.
Robin was selected as one of the top 2 legislators of 2015/2016 of the 90th General Assembly by the Arkansas State Lodge Fraternal Order of Police as Outstanding State Representative and she received the “Power of Courage Award” for outstanding service, untiring dedication and unyielding commitment to Christian Values in legislation from Family Council of Arkansas.
Representative Lundstrum earned a Doctorate in Health Science from the University of Arkansas.
She is currently a Property Manager at Cypress Investments LLC.
Representative Lundstrum is a member of the Springdale Rotary, Siloam Springs American Legion Auxiliary, FBI Citizens Academy Alumni Association Member, Siloam Springs Chamber, and Springdale Chamber.
She is a mother of two and resides in Springdale with her husband, Tom.
