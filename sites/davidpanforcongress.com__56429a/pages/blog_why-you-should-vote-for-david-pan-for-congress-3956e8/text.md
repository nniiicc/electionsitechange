We have a spiraling national debt, a looming fiscal cliff for Social Security and Medicare, a bloated federal bureaucracy, and a dysfunctional welfare system.
My name is David Pan, and I am running for Congress to solve these problems by phasing out our welfare and entitlement programs and returning the money back to the people as a universal basic income of $16,000 per year for everyone 21 and older.
My plan would eliminate the fraud and waste of our existing bureaucracy and save over $1 trillion per year.
It would also guarantee current Social Security and Medicare benefits for everyone 37 and older while giving our young people a new system that provides them the freedom and the responsibility to make their own futures.
Whether or not you agree with all the details of my plan, please support me if you want a serious discussion of the key issues that we are facing as a nation but no politicians are willing to address.
