Endorses David Pan for Congress in 2024 Nov 1 Written By David Pan School choice is the key issue that will create opportunity for our youth I am very proud to announce that Senator Gloria Romero (Ret.) has endorsed my campaign for Congress.
Romero stated, “David Pan is committed to fighting for school choice and parents’ rights so that all parents can have the possibility of choosing the best options for their children’s education.
We need his voice in Congress to help open up real possibilities for working families to improve their children’s lives.” As an educator, I understand what a difference a quality education can make in a young person’s life.
By creating a government monopoly on education in the public school system, we have trapped children in failing schools and left them without alternatives.
If we can establish charter schools as an alternative to neighborhood public schools, we are offering parents the ability to make choices about the best way to build their children’s future.
Offering school vouchers to allow all parents to pay for private schools would further provide their children with the same opportunities that are currently only reserved for the wealthy.
In this process, new opportunities would open up for both teachers and students to all thrive in alternative institutions.
Expanding the kinds of schools available to all children will improve education overall.
In fact, studies of Florida’s school voucher program have shown that children from all schools, including public schools, have improved their test scores in the new system.
Help me to create the best future for our children.
Please vote for me, tell your family and friends, and donate at DavidPanforCongress.com .
