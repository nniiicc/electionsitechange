My first priority is to reduce the size and scope of this bureaucracy by phasing out all of our welfare and entitlement programs.
The $4 trillion a year that we spend on these programs should be given back to the people.
My proposal would divide up this money equally to create a universal basic income of $16,000 per year for everyone 21 and over.

Setting aside the transition costs for maintaining our commitments to our retirees, under my plan we would save $800 billion per year as compared to our existing system, and we could use this money to balance our budget and reduce our national debt.
