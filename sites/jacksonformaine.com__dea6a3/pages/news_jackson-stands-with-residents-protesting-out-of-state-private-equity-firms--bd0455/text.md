PRESS RELEASE
Jackson Stands With Residents Protesting Out-Of-State Private Equity Firms Increasing Costs
Troy Jackson: “Private equity has been buying up our lives, making it harder for people in this country under Susan Collins' watch.”
PORTLAND, ME — Fifth-generation logger, former Maine Senate President, and Democratic Nominee for U.S.
Senate Troy Jackson joined Blueberry Ridge Mobile Home Park residents in Wells for their Fight for Our Homes conversation.
Jackson called attention to the unlivable conditions residents told him they are facing after out-of-state private equity firms bought out the park, raising rents and neglecting essential services to residents.
“They shouldn't be able to buy or sell anything until they fix the damn water,” said one resident, pointing to the greedy firms that have been allowed to swoop in and take advantage of Mainers under Susan Collins’ watch.
Jackson told residents that he would take on corporations that put profits ahead of the people who live in their communities.
