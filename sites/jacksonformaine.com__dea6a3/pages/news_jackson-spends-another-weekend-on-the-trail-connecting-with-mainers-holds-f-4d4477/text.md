The event was labeled as a “Solidarity with Seniors” town hall-style meeting, focused primarily on issues impacting seniors and a troubled health care system that has experienced deep service cuts in some parts of Maine.
Jackson took center stage to a rousing chorus of applause from an audience dominated by senior citizens.
“Working people – everyday people are not powerless,” Jackson told the crowd.
