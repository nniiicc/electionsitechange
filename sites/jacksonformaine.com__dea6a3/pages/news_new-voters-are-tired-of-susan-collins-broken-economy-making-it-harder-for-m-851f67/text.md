NEW: Voters Are Tired Of Susan Collins’ Broken Economy Making It Harder For Mainers To Get By
MAINE, USA — New reporting from the New York Times reveals Maine independent voters’ frustration with rising costs and worsening conditions under Susan Collins and Donald Trump’s leadership, making it harder and harder for everyday Mainers to get by.
The voters recall how life has changed during Collins’ 30 years in the U.S.
Senate, and express their excitement for change.
Voters like Andrew McRichards and Joe Reid point to Jackson’s commitment to protecting expanding health care access as their motivation to hit the polls in November, and are frustrated by Collins who enabled Trump's $1 trillion cuts to Medicaid.
Others, like Hannah Reid, cite Troy’s dedication to ending the rogue ICE agency that has terrorized Mainers — the same department that Susan Collins has voted to give $70 billion without guardrails, leading to the murder of Joan Sebastián Durán Guerrero in Biddeford earlier this year.
