Bombshell Investigation Surrounding Collins Operation Drawing Wave Of Local And National Scrutiny
Mainers are hearing all about how FBI anti-corruption agents sought broader investigation into alleged pay-to-play scheme involving Collins and major donor before probe died after Trump returned to office
MAINE, USA — Yesterday, a bombshell ProPublica investigation into an alleged pay-to-play scheme involving Senator Susan Collins and a major campaign donor has sparked coverage across Maine and the country, raising new questions about Collins’ relationship with the defense contractor at the center of the case and the federal funding her office helped secure for his company.
The investigation ultimately died after Donald Trump returned to office and dismantled the FBI anti-corruption team pursuing the case.
In less than 24 hours, the story spread from local television stations across Maine to some of the largest news organizations in the country, putting the allegations — and Collins’ response to them — under national scrutiny.
