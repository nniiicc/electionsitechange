Fox News Poll: Jackson Leads Collins As Majority Say She Votes With Trump Too Often
PORTLAND, ME — Fifth-generation logger, former Maine Senate President, and Democratic nominee for U.S.
Senate Troy Jackson is leading five-term incumbent Susan Collins by two points in a new Fox News poll of Maine voters — the latest survey to show Jackson ahead in one of the country’s most closely watched Senate races.
The poll, conducted August 6 – 10 among 1,000 registered voters, finds Jackson leading Collins 48% to 46%.
The survey also shows significant concern about Collins’ support for President Donald Trump’s agenda.
Fifty-five percent of Maine voters say they are very or extremely concerned that Collins votes with Trump too often, including majorities of independents, moderates, and rural voters.
Trump has a net favorability rating of -20 points in the state.
