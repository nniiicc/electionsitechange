Many in the Charleston area will continue celebrating today our 250th anniversary of the day the Battle of Sullivan's Island was won.
The story is legendary, of Patriots hunkering down in Fort Moultrie with a structure made of resilient Palmetto Tree wood in order to survive the attack of the British Navy.
Thankfully for our nation, they not only survived, but this was seen as a turning point in the Revolutionary War.

A Win for South Carolina Shrimpers!
Today, the South Carolina House adopted the Conference Committee Report for H.4248 , the Truth in Shrimp Labeling Act.
This legislation requires restaurants and food service establishments to disclose the country of origin of the shrimp they serve, helping prevent imported shrimp from being passed off as local.
It's a commonsense step that promotes transparency, protects consumers, and supports South Carolina's shrimping industry.
T he Buy American Iron & Steel Act This bill (H.4709) has officially passed the House UNANIMOUSLY!
A major win for American manufacturing, hardworking families, and South Carolina manufacturing.
This strong, bipartisan vote ensures taxpayer dollars prioritize American-made iron and steel, strengthening our economy and supporting the jobs that power our communities across our state .We’re putting America First — right here in South Carolina.
The Guarantee Banking Act (H.5538) is sent on to the Governor's desk!
No South Carolinian should lose access to banking services because of their political or religious beliefs.
The Guarantee Banking Act protects consumers and businesses by ensuring financial institutions cannot discriminate based on ideology.
If you do business in South Carolina, you should play by fair rules.
Article V Convention of States Faithful Delegate Override of the Governor's Veto H.355 is referred to as the "Faithful Delegate" bill because it requires guardrails in the event that South Carolina ever has to send a delegate to a Convention of States to rein in bad behavior by Congress.
Surprisingly, there are some conservatives who believe Article V of the Constitution is too dangerous because a Convention of States could become a "runaway convention" where the delegates try to throw out the Constitution altogether, or change things that we all know are extremely important to our Republic.
But the Constitution includes this provision specifically so states have the power to push Congress when it is not doing its job properly.
With this new law, a delegate from SC would have to stick strictly to the stated issues and not participate in any radical or "runaway" behavior to endanger the Constitution.
Even with this, there are people who say it is not possible to have guardrails for this process, but I believe they are wrong.
Congress has failed to act numerous times, especially regarding passing an actual budget, much less a truly balanced budget.
States must have the right to protect their citizens' interests when they are being ignored.
Failures Regarding Bills That Should Have Passed Two bills did not pass this week, and sadly, both were held up for purely political reasons.
I strongly believe we should always accomplish the people's work regardless of petty partisan or even internal party squabbles.
Because we still have to go back to Columbia at least one more time to finalize the Budget, we will have another opportunity to vote on both of the following bills, both of which passed the House solidly earlier in the year.
The first one is the HALO Bill, H.4763 , which requires a perimeter of 25 feet around law enforcement when they are trying to handle a dangerous situation, and a "reasonable perimeter" for medical personnel administering life-saving care.
The reason given for the change away from 25 feet for medical situations is that some events occur inside in tight places where 25 feet would not be possible.
The other bill that did not pass was H.4042 , which would allow for human-grade ivermectin to be able to purchased over the counter due to its numerous applications.
Both bills were delayed with procedural motions that never should have happened, in my opinion.
See below for the statement from Majority Leader Davey Hiott regarding this delay.
When internal politics gets in the way of doing what is right for the people of South Carolina, as with both of these bills, we must admit fully that this is wrong and do better.
Hopefully, in the next couple of weeks, we will get the chance to do that.

Around the District and the Lowcountry Carolina Day 2026 What a great day it was yesterday to celebrate the 1776 Patriot victory at the Battle of Sullivan's Island.
Because today, June 28th is the 250th anniversary of this major event, the festivities were more special than ever.
My husband Joe represented our family very well as both an active piper in the Charleston Pipe Band and a fairly recently confirmed member of the Sons of the American Revolution.
On top of the Pipe Band marching & playing, there was also a good-sized group from the British Royal Marines Band.
While the weather was sultry and hot, spirits were high as the various groups honored this historic day!
I would like to thank Park West for the beautiful display of American flags around each of their traffic circles.
It was so inspiring just to drive through the neighborhoods today with all the flags flying, including those lining the streets in front of people's homes.
As we approach the 250th birthday of our country on July 4th, it will be wonderful to see more of the patriotic decorations throughout our community.
