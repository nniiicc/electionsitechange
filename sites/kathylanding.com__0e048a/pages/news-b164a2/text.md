Heirs’ property owners now protected from some surprise tax bills under new South Carolina law
Sunday, July 12, 2026
Thank you to David Slade of the Post & Courier for sharing the story of how my bill, "Heirs Property Tax Relief," was created & passed into law.
This is a very simple bill with profound impacts.
But the work has just begun.
Now we have to get the word out to Settlement Communities all over our state that they can finally clear the title for their land without a major financial burden.
I will be meeting with representatives of the Settlement Communities East of the Cooper soon to learn more about the complicated process of clearing their title.
I have also spoken with Judge Irv Condon and plan to meet with him to see what can be done to simplify this process, including if there is new legislation needed.
Why is this so important?
Have you noticed that many Settlement Communities have mobile homes or trailers?
This is because if you are a descendant entitled to some of the acreage of Heirs Property, you have no legal rights to improve or use the land beyond a temporary dwelling (because these are categorized the same as vehicles).
Imagine you are a young couple living in Mount Pleasant in an apartment, or living elsewhere because it is too expensive to live here due to land prices.
One day you find out that your extended family has cleared the title to the land from your great-great-grandfather and you now have a quarter acre of land legally titled in your name!
How much is that worth?
I checked the range and it could be $200k - $650K depending on where it is.
You can use that land as collateral to get a construction loan & build a home for your family.
What happens when you build a home so you no longer have to pay rent & you have a valuable piece of land as collateral?
You put down roots & build generational wealth!
This problem has always been about bad policy that was making it extremely hard for families to clear their titles.
With this impediment gone, I envision many individuals and families now finally being able to move forward with the American Dream they have always heard about but might have thought was beyond their reach.
