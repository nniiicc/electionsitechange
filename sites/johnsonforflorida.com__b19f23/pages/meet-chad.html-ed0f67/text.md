As a 6th generation Floridian, I was born and raised in Alachua County, with family from Gilchrist and Marion counties.
I am a University of Florida graduate with a degree in Food & Resource Economics.
I was an IFAS Ambassador, served as a leader of the Alpha Gamma Rho Fraternity and was tapped into The Florida Blue Key.
In 1994, after marrying Angela Quincey, I moved to Levy County where we have resided and raised our four children.
I’m a former Levy County Commissioner, past president of the Levy County Farm Bureau and the Florida Auctioneers Association, and served as a director of the Dixie-Gilchrist-Levy Board of REALTORS.
Florida is prosperous because of free markets, limited government, and private property rights.
I’ll always defend these policies and fight to protect District 22’s quality of life, and our agriculture and natural resources.
