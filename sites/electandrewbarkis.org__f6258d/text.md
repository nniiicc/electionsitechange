Why I'm Running I am fighting to fix Washington by prioritizing the issues that matter most to your family: safety, affordability, and reliable infrastructure.
As your Ranking Member on the House Transportation Committee, I successfully helped secure $340 million in critical funding for road preservation and maintenance while introducing measures like HB 2181 to repair infrastructure damaged by severe winter weather without forcing drivers to pay higher gas taxes.
With nearly three decades of housing experience, I’ve championed common-sense solutions like HB 1345 to expand rural housing options and HB 2418 to hold the government accountable for permitting delays , all while blocking frequent, costly building code updates that drive up home prices.
I also stand as your primary defender against rising costs, having successfully blocked a billion-dollar grocery tax and $275 million in annual utility hikes while fighting the deficit spending that outpaces our tax revenue.
By stopping efforts to shorten sentences for serious crimes and maintaining accountability for registered offenders, I remain committed to restoring public safety and building a Washington where every community can thrive.
Our state faces many challenges.
