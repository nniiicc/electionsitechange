A True Voice: Chris’s Story
I’m a native Texan, a father, and a proud resident of Mansfield.
While I have lived in West, Central, and North Texas, my life partner Mike and I chose Mansfield as our home in 2019.
I am committed to preserving the quality of life that makes our community special.
My view of the world was shaped by an early challenge.
When I was a freshman in high school, I lost my father.
I watched my mother, a Registered Nurse, work incredibly hard to provide for us on a single income.
That experience showed me the resilience of Texas families, but it also showed me how much community matters.
The support we received from our neighbors in Fredericksburg made all the difference, and it instilled in me a lifelong drive to support others.
I carried that spirit of service into Angelo State University, where I earned my B.B.A. and served as a Program Director for the United Campus Ministries.
Working with students from various denominations taught me that we achieve more when we focus on what unites us rather than what divides us.
I spent my professional career at Verizon, serving 30 years in roles ranging from Purchasing to Systems Management.
Whether I was supervising teams, managing budgets, or implementing complex technical systems, my focus was always on efficiency and accountability.
I retired in 2024, and now I am ready to apply those decades of business experience to work for you.
A Philosophy of Radical Decency
When I decided to run for office, I did so knowing that winning was unlikely.
I ran anyway—because some moments call less for ambition than for responsibility.
Over the last sixty years, this country has made hard-fought progress toward treating people with greater fairness, dignity, and decency.
That progress was not inevitable, and it is not permanent.
I believe we are living in a moment when ground that was won through sacrifice is at risk of being quietly surrendered.
I am not running to inflame division or to posture for attention.
I am running because government has a moral obligation to protect the vulnerable, to tell the truth, and to use its power with restraint and humanity.
When laws are used to punish, exclude, or demean, it is the duty of citizens—and legislators—to speak plainly and act responsibly.
Win or lose, my purpose in this race is to raise the level of our civic conversation and to insist that decency is not weakness, compassion is not radicalism, and concern for our neighbors is not a partisan value.
It is a human one.
This campaign is my way of standing guard over what others before us struggled to build—and of refusing to be neutral when neutrality would mean letting it slip away.

Family Economic Security & Healthcare Reform
Relief through Resilience: Moving from Crisis to Care
- Proactive Mental Health & "The Roving Nurse" Model
- Reviving Community-Based Care: In the 80s and 90s, our local MHMR offices were proactive, not just reactive.
I am championing a return to the Home Health Care Network, where roving nurses and mental health professionals conduct wellness checks and medication management.
- Crisis Prevention: By meeting patients where they are—in their homes and communities—we ensure compliance with treatment protocols and identify needs before they escalate into a 911 call or a hospital ER visit.
- Ending the "Jail-as-a-Hospital" Pipeline
- Intermediary Care Facilities: For those in active crisis, we must replace the county jail cell with Intermediary Care Centers.
These facilities provide a bridge to recovery, allowing law enforcement to divert citizens to medical professionals rather than the criminal justice system.
- Medicaid Expansion for Texans
- It is time to bring our federal tax dollars home!
Expanding Medicaid in Texas will stabilize our local hospitals, lower insurance premiums for all of us, and provide the sustainable funding needed for mental health networks.
- Infrastructure & Workforce: The Foundation of Security
- Infrastructure for Commuters: Wasted fuel and time on I-35W and Hwy 287 is a "hidden tax." We will build the infrastructure that Mansfield, Burleson, and Cleburne and District 58 families deserve.
- Vocational & Trade Excellence: We will ensure that high-demand, high-paying jobs are accessible to every student, recognizing that a four-year degree is just one of many paths to a secure middle-class life.
- Property Tax & Utility Relief: We will continue the fight to lower the tax burden and ensure that energy and water remain affordable and reliable for every household.
