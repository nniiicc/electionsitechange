The Mental Health Resource Hub
As your candidate for State Representative, I believe our government’s first job is the safety and well-being of its citizens.
This hub is part of my commitment to bringing proactive, community-based mental health care back to Texas.
If you are struggling, please reach out to the professionals listed below.
District 58 Mental Health Resources
1.
Local 24/7 Crisis Support (Johnson & Somervell)
- Pecan Valley Centers Crisis Line: 1-800-772-5987
- The local authority for our district.
They provide a Mobile Crisis Outreach Team (MCOT) that can meet you at home, school, or work.
- Family Crisis Center of Johnson County: (817) 641-2332
- 24-hour support for those facing domestic or family violence.
2.
Specialized Support for Youth & LGBTQ+ Neighbors
- The Trevor Project: 1-866-488-7386 or text START to 678-678
- 24/7 crisis support specifically for LGBTQ youth.
- Trans Lifeline: 1-877-565-8860
- Peer support for trans and questioning people, run by trans people.
- LGBTQ SAVES (North Texas): lgbtqsaves.org
- A regional organization providing safe spaces and resources for youth.
3.
National Instant Connections
- 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: Call or Text 988
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
