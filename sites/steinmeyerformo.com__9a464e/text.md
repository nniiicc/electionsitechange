MEET MIKE Contact Donate Follow Community Support Mike’s supporters include individuals and organizations from across District 20, including labor and public safety unions.
They support Mike because he has shown the ability to work with legislators of both political parties to get important things done.
Like securing funding for workforce development and road projects in our district, and writing legislation to help attract more manufacturing jobs to our community.
Mike wrote and sponsored legislation that received overwhelming and rare bipartisan support in the Missouri House - including bills that raised awareness of rare pediatric diseases and established a pediatric disease task force to coordinate research initiatives.
Both bills passed with just one "no" vote.
PROUDLY ENDORSED BY Working For District 20 The needs of District 20 don’t fit neatly into one category.
Good jobs, strong schools, access to care, safe neighborhoods, and reliable local services all help our communities thrive.
Mike’s work in the Missouri House is shaped by conversations with the people who live and work in Independence, Sugar Creek, and the surrounding area.
From public educators and local city officials, to small business owners, labor union representatives, social service providers and families living across the District.
