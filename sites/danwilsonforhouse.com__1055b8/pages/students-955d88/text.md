Get Started Voting Can I vote in Minnesota?
You can vote in Minnesota if you are… A U.S. citizen At least 18 years old on Election Day (16 & 17 year olds can pre-register) A resident of Minnesota for 20 days Paying out-of-state tuition or having a driver's license from another state does not necessarily mean you can't vote in Minnesota.
More Info How do I register to vote?
You can register here or you can register on election day.
To register on election day, college students can bring their photo ID along with their name and address on a student housing list from the college as proof of residence.
Colleges and universities that enroll students accepting state financial aid must send election officials a student housing list.
This list includes students who live on-campus and those that have provided their current address to the school and live in the city where campus is located.
If you are on the list, show any form of photo ID approved for registering on election day to complete your registration.
What is my voting residence?
If you moved to Minnesota from another state and currently consider Minnesota your home, you can vote here even if you pay out-of-state tuition or have a driver’s license from another state.
