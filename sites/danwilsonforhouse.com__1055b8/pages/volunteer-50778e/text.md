Meet & Greet FAQs What is a Meet and Greet?
A meet and greet is a small, informal gathering where attendees can get to know Dan.
Dan will speak about his vision, listen to the interests and concerns of attendees and answer questions about his position on specific issues.
What do I have to do if I host?
Connect with us to pick a time.
Choose a place (could be your home, a park, a cafe/bar/restaurant, online… anywhere!
We want it to be comfortable/convenient for you and your guests).
Invite guests!
Our goal is for there to be between 5-15 guests at each meet and greet, but the number of invitees is up to the host.
How long is a Meet and Greet?
It’s up to you, typically they may be around 90 minutes, but you can schedule them for as long/short as you want.
Will you ask my guests for money at the Meet and Greet?
No.
The purpose for a Meet and Greet is to make connections and give your guests a chance to get to know Dan.
Dan may bring literature with his website, but he will not ask your guests for donations.
Do I have to tell my guests that I plan to support/vote for Dan?
This is up to you.
We want Dan to meet as many people with different views as possible.
If you want to hear more about Dan and his vision before you make up your mind, you can still host a Meet and Greet so that other folks you know have a chance to meet Dan and make an informed decision.
You can even invite other candidates for the seat if you wish!
