Meet Anthony Issues News Contact Events DiLorenzo Earns Turning Point Action Endorsement Portsmouth, NH — As first reported by WMUR, Republican congressional candidate in New Hampshire’s first district, Anthony DiLorenzo, announced the endorsement of Turning Point Action , a leading grassroots organization founded by Charlie Kirk and focused on mobilizing young conservative voters nationwide.
The endorsement builds momentum for DiLorenzo’s campaign centered on fiscal responsibility, support for law enforcement, and protecting the American Dream.
“Anthony DiLorenzo is the political outsider we need to give us the best shot at winning the Granite State’s crucial first district seat this fall,” said a Turning Point Action spokesperson.
“Turning Point Action is proud to support Anthony DiLorenzo for Congress.” “I am honored to have the support of Turning Point Action and the young Americans they represent,” said DiLorenzo.
“This is a movement to defend our values and secure a stronger future.
I have lived the American Dream, and I am running to protect it.” Turning Point Action’s backing strengthens DiLorenzo’s grassroots effort as voters look for a proven outsider focused on results, not politics.
