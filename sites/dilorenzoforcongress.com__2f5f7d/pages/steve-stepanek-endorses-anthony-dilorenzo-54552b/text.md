| Portsmouth, NH — Today, the Anthony DiLorenzo for Congress campaign is proud to announce the endorsement of Stephen Stepanek, former State Representative, former NHGOP Chairman, Co-Chair of President Trump’s 2016 campaign in New Hampshire, and Senior Advisor to President Trump’s 2024 campaign.
“Our campaign is building real momentum every day, and I’m honored to have Stephen Stepanek’s support,” said DiLorenzo.
“Stephen’s vision for our party changed New Hampshire for the better, and his endorsement is the latest sign that our party and our state are ready to rally around a candidate that puts results over rhetoric.
I’m running to preserve New Hampshire as the Land of Opportunity and bring common-sense, business-minded leadership to Washington.
I look forward to continuing to earn the support of all Granite Staters.” Stepanek underscored the importance of uniting New Hampshire Republicans behind Anthony’s campaign.
“It’s time for New Hampshire Republicans to rally around Anthony DiLorenzo.
Anthony is a world-class businessman, a pragmatic leader, and a proud Granite Stater.
At a time when our state is represented in Washington by out-of-touch Democrats, Anthony is a breath of fresh air.
I encourage all my fellow New Hampshire Republicans to join me in supporting him and help elect a member of Congress who puts Granite Staters first,” said Stepanek.
