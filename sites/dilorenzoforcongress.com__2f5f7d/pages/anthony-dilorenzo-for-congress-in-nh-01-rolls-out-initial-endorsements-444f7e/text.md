Portsmouth, NH — Anthony DiLorenzo’s campaign for Congress in New Hampshire’s First District today announced a broad and growing list of endorsements from state legislators, local officials, business leaders, and community figures from across NH-01.
These endorsements reflect the growing momentum behind DiLorenzo’s message of hard work, responsibility, and a commitment to protecting opportunity for future generations.
State Senator Tim Lang praised DiLorenzo’s life story and commitment to preserving the American Dream in New Hampshire.
“Anthony understands that opportunity does not come from government.
It comes from hard work.
He’s done the hard work, lived the American Dream, and is running to make sure it still exists in New Hampshire,” said State Senator Tim Lang.
State Senator Daryl Abbas underscored DiLorenzo’s results-driven approach.
“Anthony DiLorenzo is exactly the kind of leader New Hampshire needs in Congress.
He’s not a career politician — he’s a businessman who knows that results matter more than rhetoric.
While Washington talks, Anthony acts.
His resolve and his commitment to New Hampshire values are why I’m proud to endorse him for Congress,” said state Senator Daryl Abbas.
State Representative Tanya Donnelly highlighted that DiLorenzo’s business acumen equips him to deliver real results for Granite Staters in Congress.
“Anthony DiLorenzo sees opportunities where our current federal representation falls short.
With the proven ability to create and sustain businesses through all economic conditions, he has helped create jobs for thousands.
Anthony’s vision goes beyond being a businessman; he recognizes how government inefficiencies can lead to delays and missed opportunities for New Hampshire.
I am proud to support him because he will fight for our families, small businesses, and the New Hampshire way of life,” said State Representative Tanya Donnelly.
Chris Bright emphasized DiLorenzo’s values-driven leadership and focus on real-world solutions.
“I’m proud to endorse Anthony DiLorenzo because he lives the values that make New Hampshire exceptional: hard work, responsibility, and freedom,” said Chris Bright.
“Anthony is a proven problem-solver who will fight for our families, support small businesses, rein in spending, and protect the American Dream for every Granite Stater.”
DiLorenzo added, “I’m honored to have the support of so many Granite Staters who believe, as I do, that opportunity comes from hard work, not Washington.
Together, we can protect the values that make New Hampshire special and ensure the American Dream is still within reach for the next generation.”
