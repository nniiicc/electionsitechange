Meet Anthony Issues News Contact Events Anthony DiLorenzo Secures First National Endorsement Anthony DiLorenzo Secures First National Endorsement Portsmouth, NH – Today, U.S.
House Judiciary Committee Chairman, Rep.
Jim Jordan (R-OH), endorsed self-made businessman and Republican congressional candidate for New Hampshire’s First District, Anthony DiLorenzo.
“Anthony DiLorenzo is exactly the kind of leader we need in Congress.
He’s a self-made businessman who worked hard, took risks, created jobs, and built opportunities for others.
He has lived the American Dream and understands what makes this country great – faith, family, freedom, and hard work,” Chairman Jordan said.
“In Washington, he will fight for common sense and work to lower costs and raise wages for hardworking families.
I look forward to working with him to advance the America First agenda every single day.” DiLorenzo thanked Chairman Jordan for his endorsement and reemphasized his commitment to preserving the American Dream for Granite Staters.
“I’m honored to have Chairman Jordan’s endorsement.
He’s a proven fighter for working families, and I look forward to working with him to lower costs, raise wages, create jobs, and deliver real results for every Granite Stater,” said DiLorenzo.
Background: In 1983, Anthony DiLorenzo moved to New Hampshire to attend the University of New Hampshire.
After dropping out of school, Anthony started Key Auto Group with just $7,000 in savings.
Today, Key Auto Group is one of New Hampshire’s premier auto dealership chains.
In total, Anthony has started nearly 40 companies, employing 1,000 people.
As a Republican Candidate for Congress, Anthony is committed to doing what he has done his entire career: deliver results, create jobs, and help Granite Staters achieve the American Dream.
