Following the 2022 redistricting there are six precincts in the district.
You can register to vote, find your precinct, or request an absentee ballot below.
Alaskans can track their absentee ballot and sign up to receive email and text notifications as it is received and processed by the Division of Elections at http://alaska.ballottrax.net/voter/ Register or Update Information Registering to vote in Alaska is easy, but does require that an individual meet certain requirements.

All of this can be done online with the Alaska Department of Elections.

Voter Information Get Familiar with Denny's Priorities Priorities District 15 for Alaska's Future Alaska’s District 15 looks different today than a few years ago.
In 2022, the district was redrawn by the Alaska Redistricting Board.
Today, District 15 covers some of Anchorage’s oldest and newest neighborhoods, spanning 6 election precincts.
From homesteads dating to the earliest days of Anchorage, through the pipeline era explosion in the 1970’s, to today’s ongoing development of Anchorage’s newest neighborhoods in Westpark and Prominence Point, southwest Anchorage has been a great place to find recreational opportunities and peaceful places where Alaskans have settled to raise their families and build a bright future.
