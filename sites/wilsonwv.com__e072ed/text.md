U.S.
Military Veteran
Marshall Wilson served his country in uniform for over 20 years, prior-enlisted, he is now a retired combat veteran US Army Infantry Officer who knows sacrifice, discipline, and what it means to defend the Constitution, not just swear to it.
Wilson has served in various capacities, as a company commander and as a plans and operations officer.
Pastor & Community Leader
A shepherd to his community long before seeking office — grounded in faith, guided by principle, and accountable to something higher than Washington, D.C.
Marshall, fluent in the Spanish language, served as a church planter, taught Bible classes, and encouraged pastors and tribal communities in the Peruvian Amazon.
Scholar of National Security
After retiring from military service, Marshall earned his Masters in National Security because he takes the threats facing America seriously — and believes West Virginia deserves a Senator who does.
Prior to campaigning, he was on track to complete a Doctorate of Statecraft and Strategy to expand and deepen his understanding of our government's failures and to develop valid, principled, and feasible corrections to those failures.
State Legislator — WV House of Delegates, District 91 (formerly 60)
Marshall was elected by his district in 2016 and served in the WV Statehouse for four years.
During this period, he lead-sponsored 57 bills and sponsored 326 bills (full delegate record).
Continually advocating for Constitutional Rights, Marshall Wilson was the voice of reason speaking against unconstitutional mandates, lack of government transparency, misuse of taxpayer dollars, and government overreach.
He advocated for protection of the 2nd Amendment (and against Red Flag Laws), protection of children (requesting an audit of CPS), health freedom, and school choice, as well as an increase in teacher salaries.
Among the newsworthy events he took action on: requesting the impeachment of the Governor for unconstitutional use of authority (Petition for Writ of Mandamus).
