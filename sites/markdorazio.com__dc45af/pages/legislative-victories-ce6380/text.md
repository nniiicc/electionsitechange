Rep.
Mark Dorazio has spent a lifetime serving our community and the Republican Party – as a Precinct Chairman, as Chairman of the Republican Party of Bexar County, as State Republican Executive Committeeman for Senate District 25, and now as State Representative for Texas House District 122.
His track record as a principled and effective conservative is decades long, and Mark is proud to have delivered on the issues his constituents care about most.
Below you will find many of the Texas victories Mark delivered for his constituents in 2025:
Healthcare Costs
- SB 493 — Ended the Pharmacy “Gag Clause”
Mark Dorazio banned insurance companies from using secret contracts to stop pharmacists from telling you when a prescription is cheaper if paid in cash — putting that money back in your family’s budget instead of a corporation’s pocket.
- HB 1314 — Forced Hospitals to Show the Price First
Mark Dorazio required hospitals and clinics to tell patients what their care will cost before it happens — and banned them from padding the final bill above that price.
No more surprise bills you never agreed to.
- HB 2067 — Made Insurers Give Straight Answers
Mark Dorazio forced insurance companies to tell customers exactly why a policy was denied, canceled, or not renewed — arming Texans with what they need to fight back against unfair corporate decisions.
- SB 815 — Kept Doctors — Not Computers — in Charge
Mark Dorazio banned insurers from using artificial intelligence to deny or delay your medical care, putting treatment decisions back in the hands of a licensed physician instead of a piece of software built to say no.
- SB 213 — Stopped Coerced Insurance Bundling
Mark Dorazio stopped insurers from forcing Texans to bundle home and auto coverage just to keep a policy — freeing families to shop every policy for the best deal.
Taking on Big Insurance Companies
- SB 30 — Defeated Big Insurance’s Corporate Shield
Mark Dorazio defeated Big Insurance’s push to shield large corporations from paying the full cost of the harm they inflict on Texas families — forcing them to answer for the damage they cause.
- SB 39 — Blocked the Trucking-Industry Liability Shield
Mark Dorazio blocked legislation that would have made it harder for Texas families to hold trucking companies accountable when a driver causes a catastrophic crash — keeping the courthouse doors open to victims.
Lowering Property Taxes
- SB 4 — Raised the Homestead Exemption
- SB 23 — Delivered Bigger Relief for Seniors and Texans with Disabilities
Mark Dorazio increased property-tax relief for homeowners aged 65 and older and Texans living with a disability — protecting neighbors on fixed incomes from being taxed out of the homes they’ve owned for decades.
Public Education & Safe School Campuses
- HB 2 — Raised Teacher Pay and Funded Classrooms
Mark Dorazio coauthored the school-finance law that delivers permanent pay raises to Texas teachers and new funding for the nurses, counselors, and support staff who keep campuses running.
- HB 33 — Wrote the Uvalde Strong Act
Mark Dorazio coauthored the Uvalde Strong Act — named for the 21 lives lost at Robb Elementary — requiring emergency safety equipment on every Texas campus and mandatory active-shooter training for law enforcement.
- SB 571 — Ended “Pass-the-Trash” in Our Schools
Mark Dorazio ended the practice of quietly shuffling predatory school employees from one district to the next, requiring schools to report misconduct to the state.
- HB 1481 — Cleared Cell Phones Out of the Classroom
Mark Dorazio put an end to cell-phone distractions in Texas classrooms, keeping personal phones out of class during the school day so teachers can teach and kids can learn.
- HB 3629 — Barred Sex Offenders From School Boards
Mark Dorazio coauthored the law barring registered sex offenders from serving as school-district trustees — because convicted predators should never govern our children’s schools.
- HB 6 — Restored Order to the Classroom
Mark Dorazio coauthored the law giving teachers real authority to remove disruptive students from the room — restoring order so every child can learn.
Protecting Children
- HB 4623 — Gave Abuse Survivors the Right to Sue
Mark Dorazio gave victims the right to sue school districts that cover up sexual abuse by their employees — stripping away the legal immunity institutions once hid behind when a child was harmed.
- SB 12 — Put Parents Back in Charge
Mark Dorazio put parents back in charge of their children’s education — making sex education opt-in, strengthening parents’ say over school library materials, and refocusing classrooms on core academics.
Punishing Crimes Against Children
- HB 2306 / SB 1212 — Ended Parole for Child Traffickers
Mark Dorazio coauthored the law eliminating parole for those who traffic children and disabled Texans — and made all human trafficking a first-degree felony, so predators serve every day of their sentence.
Public Safety
- SJR 5 / Prop. 3 — Denied Bail to Violent Offenders
Mark Dorazio gave judges the power to deny bail to defendants charged with murder, rape, and child trafficking — keeping the most dangerous offenders off the street before trial.
Texas voters ratified it as Proposition 3.
- SB 9 — Toughened Bail for Repeat Offenders
Mark Dorazio required judges to review a defendant’s full criminal history before setting bail in felony cases — keeping dangerous repeat offenders behind bars while they await trial.
- SB 1 — Delivered a Helicopter to Bexar County’s Anti-Gang Unit
Mark Dorazio delivered $3 million to put a helicopter in the hands of the Bexar County Sheriff’s anti-gang unit — helping our deputies stay a step ahead of the violent gangs threatening our neighborhoods and families.
Pro-Life / Abortion
- SB 31 — Protected the Life of the Mother
Mark Dorazio guaranteed that Texas doctors can act immediately to save a mother’s life in a medical emergency — while still giving her unborn child every possible chance.
- HB 37 / SB 1233 — Passed Everly’s Law
Mark Dorazio cosponsored Everly’s Law, requiring doctors to inform families of every care option after a life-limiting prenatal diagnosis such as Trisomy 18 — and to make available the devices that give grieving parents time to say goodbye.
Housing Affordability
- SB 840 — Cleared the Way to Build
Mark Dorazio stopped cities from blocking new housing construction — expanding the supply of homes so first-time buyers and young families in our area can actually afford one.
- SB 15 — Made Room for Starter Homes
Mark Dorazio opened the door for smaller home lots across Texas cities — cutting the cost of building the starter homes that put first-time buyers and young families within reach of the market.
Cost of Living
- HB 9 — Cut Taxes on Local Small Business
Mark Dorazio coauthored the tax cut exempting local small businesses from the personal-property tax — lowering their costs, protecting the jobs they create, and helping hold down the prices they charge.
