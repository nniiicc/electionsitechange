Eddie is a Mountaineer by choice.

He has built his home here with his wife, Alana.
They are the proud parents of two wonderful kids who attend Mon County public schools.
When he has free time, you can find him walking the trails with his dogs, snowboarding,or tinkering with old cars.
Eddie is proud to call Mon County his home, and he is running for the house to make this a great place for all of us.
