Erin Huntley has spent nearly two decades raising her family in Central Florida with her husband, Eric.
Guided by faith, integrity, and strong conservative values, she brings real-world private-sector experience from Walt Disney Parks, JetBlue Airways, and DECA Inc.
Appointed by Governor Ron DeSantis to the Florida Virtual School Board of Trustees, Erin has championed conservative principles at every level.
Now she’s running for Florida House District 45 to expand school choice, strengthen public safety, promote fiscal discipline, and create opportunity for Florida families.
