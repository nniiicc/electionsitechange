I’m here to listen.
When people ask me why I'm running, my answer is simple: I'm concerned about where we are as a community.
Too often, politics divides us instead of bringing us together.
I believe we have far more in common than what separates us, and the best solutions come from listening to one another—not shouting past each other.
Our nation's motto, E Pluribus Unum —"Out of many, one"—reminds us that our strength has always come from people with different backgrounds, experiences, and perspectives working together toward a common purpose.
As your State Representative, I'll listen to everyone—Democrats, Republicans, and Independents alike—because every voice matters, and Salem deserves a representative who puts people before politics.
I am proud to be endorsed by: The fighting and division in our politics are hurting our communities.
Too often, we are encouraged to see each other as political enemies instead of neighbors and fellow human beings.
News has become opinion instead of fact, and rhetoric instead of truth.
While we are busy arguing with one another, many of the issues that affect our daily lives go unresolved.
At both the federal and state levels, government has too often lost sight of the people it is meant to serve.
We deserve representation that listens, cares, and most importantly, works for us—not against us.
I am not promising that I can fix every problem.
What I am promising is that I will do my best to represent everyone fairly, and to make decisions based on what is best for our community as a whole.
I believe good government is about serving the many, not catering to a select few, and always keeping the greater good in mind.
Fair representation is not a partisan idea; it is a constitutional right.
That is why I am running for State Representative.
I am not running to advance a political career, nor for personal gain.
I am running because I believe our community is strongest when government serves the people rather than divides them.
Salem is already a strong community, but I believe we can be even stronger when we focus on what unites us instead of what separates us.
If, over the next several months, I can help others in my community see their fellow human beings as people rather than political labels, I will consider this campaign a success.
Meaningful change begins with small steps, and small steps create a path forward.
I understand the responsibilities that come with serving as your State Representative.
What I want to understand better are the concerns, priorities, and experiences of the people I hope to represent.
Before I can effectively advocate for Salem, I need to hear directly from Salem residents.
That is why I am inviting anyone who would like to share their thoughts, concerns, or ideas to meet with me.
I am happy to meet at a location of your choice on any evening except Saturday, or during the day on Sundays.
Whether you support me, disagree with me, or are still undecided, your perspective matters.
I want to hear what is important to you, what concerns you have, and what you would like to see for the future of Salem.
Your voice matters.
