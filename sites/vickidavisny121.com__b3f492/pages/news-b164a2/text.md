The film industry is a significant economic driver in upstate New York.
Sometimes it’s hard-to-miss, such as when feature films are filmed on location in one of our beautiful towns, while some if it is quieter, behind-the-scenes work such as post-production, editing, special effects development, and more.
I was appalled when, in 2023, my opponent Joe Angelino sponsored a bill to repeal the New York State Film Tax production credit and the post-production credit, which are tax credits available to film and television production companies in New York.
The bill would have effectively ended these incentive programs.
Fortunately, the bill was not passed, but the threat remains as long as Angelino is in office.
With the incredible economic benefits to New Yorkers, both those employed in the industry and those who benefit from the industry’s presence (restaurants and retail, transportation services, etc.), it seems Angelino is out-of-touch with the 65,000+ jobs supported by New York State’s Film Tax Credits every year.
