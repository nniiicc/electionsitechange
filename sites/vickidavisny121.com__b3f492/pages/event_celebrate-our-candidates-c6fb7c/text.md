Celebrate Our Candidates August 25 @ 4:00 pm - 7:30 pm « Binghamton House Party Hamilton House Party » We will gather at Foothills Hops Farm Brewery on Tuesday, Aug.
25, to celebrate the candidates running for office this November.
It’s a great chance to meet and hear from the candidates themselves as we head into the crucial general election.
You can talk with: U.S.
Rep.
John Mannion, who is seeking re-election in New York’s 22nd Congressional district.
Phillip Fleming, who is running in NYS Assembly District 131.
Mandi Drake, seeking the NYS Senate seat in District 53.
Vicki Davis, candidate in NYS Assembly District 121.
Ihor Semko, who is running for NYS Assembly in the 122nd District.
Al Stirpe, seeking re-election in NYS Assembly District 127 There will be free admission at Foothills, along with live music, food truck, and a cash bar.
