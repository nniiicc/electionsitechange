I see what regulations, customs delays, and high prices do to families trying to build a home.
I'm running because both parties keep growing the debt and nobody takes the hard votes.
I'm asking you to vote for something, not against the other guy.
Issues Spending and the debt No automatic inflation raises for agencies.
Every expired program gets a recorded vote.
Congress passes its 12 spending bills on time, not one giant bill nobody reads.
If it's worth spending your money, it's worth a recorded vote.
Social Security In 2032 current law cuts every retiree's check.
I'd protect current seniors who depend on it and make Congress vote on real options for younger workers, in public, before the deadline.
Immigration Voters want less immigration.
Stop federal subsidies that pay people to come, close asylum loopholes, secure the border, deport criminals, no masked federal agents.
Housing Let people build.
Cut the rules and delays that drive up the cost of a home.
Surveillance and liberty Police should need a warrant to use camera data like Flock.
Government shouldn't make you sign a loyalty pledge to a foreign country to get a state contract.
Health care Lower prices by increasing supply and letting new technology compete.
No Medicare for All.
