Bobby Wilson for Congress Blog FAQ Frequently asked questions Who is Bobby Wilson?
Bobby Wilson is the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
House in Arkansas's 3rd Congressional District.
He's running to make Congress take the hard votes in public and to give voters a real choice beyond the two old parties.
Why is a Libertarian running?
Because voters deserve a real choice.
Libertarians stand for individual liberty, limited government, and fiscal responsibility.
Bobby is running to bring those principles to Congress and to push lawmakers to vote openly on the hard issues instead of dodging them.
What is the Libertarian Party of Arkansas?
The Libertarian Party of Arkansas (LPAR) is the state affiliate of the national Libertarian Party, working to elect candidates who support personal freedom and limited government.
Learn more at lpar.org .
Where can I watch the debate clips?
Follow the campaign on Facebook ( facebook.com/profile.php?id=61561046185031 ) and on X ( x.com/BobbyUScongress ), where debate clips are posted.
What's Bobby's position on Social Security?
Protect current seniors who depend on it, and make Congress vote in public on the options for younger workers.
How can I vote?
Election Day is November 3, 2026.
Check early voting dates and your polling place with your county clerk.
