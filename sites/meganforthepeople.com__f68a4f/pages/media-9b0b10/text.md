Brief Bio
Megan Woller is the Democratic nominee for Idaho House District 16A.
She currently serves as Executive Director of the Idaho Head Start Association.
In 2022, she was recognized by Idaho Business Review as one of Idaho's Accomplished Under 40.
A sixth-generation Idahoan on her father's side and a second-generation Mexican American on her mother's side, Megan was born in Nampa, raised in Emmett, and has called Boise home since 2011.
She comes from a hardworking family with roots in construction, mining, healthcare, and agriculture; experiences that shaped her commitment to expanding opportunity for all Idahoans.
She earned both her bachelor's degree in Health Education and Promotion and her Master of Public Administration from Boise State University.
Over the past 14 years, she has built a career serving Idaho communities through nonprofit leadership, public health, hunger relief, domestic violence prevention, substance abuse prevention, and community health initiatives.
Megan is a wife, mother, and involved in her local community.
When she's not working, she enjoys gardening, running, and performing music.
