Beau Bayh is a seventh-generation Hoosier who was raised to believe public service means actually serving the public — not your political party, and certainly not yourself.
For Beau, that meant joining the United States Marine Corps.
He served as an Infantry Officer, where he rose to the rank of Captain and graduated from the Scout Sniper Unit Leader’s Course.
As a seventh generation Hoosier and a U.S.
Marine Corps Infantry Officer who rose to the rank of Captain, Beau knows how to lead with integrity and transparency.
