INDIANA LEADERSHIP
Meet Beau
A Seventh-Generation Hoosier
Beau Bayh is a seventh-generation Hoosier who was raised in a family of public servants.
He believes public service means actually serving the public — not your political party, and certainly not yourself.
For Beau, that meant joining the United States Marine Corps.
He was sworn in at the Indiana War Memorial in Indianapolis.
In the Marines, Beau served as an Infantry Officer, where he rose to the rank of Captain and graduated from the Scout Sniper Unit Leader’s Course.
His military service took him around the world, including to Europe in support of America’s response to the Russian invasion of Ukraine.
Following his honorable discharge in 2023, Beau earned a law degree.
While in law school, he worked for Cummins Engines and Barnes & Thornburg LLP, both in Indianapolis.
Following graduation, he served as a Judicial Law Clerk in Bloomington for the Honorable David F.
Hamilton on the United States Court of Appeals for the Seventh Circuit.
Running Towards the Fight
Throughout his career, Beau has been guided by the lessons he learned as a Marine: When facing tough fights, you run toward them, not away from them.
That’s how he’ll approach the job of Secretary of State.
He’ll immediately clean up the corruption and wasteful spending that has brought so much shame to that office – and he’ll work with both parties to restore common sense to government.
Moreover, Beau will work to bring a better type of politics to our state government.
Too often, politicians at the state and national levels seek to divide Hoosiers.
In the units that Beau led, people came from different backgrounds, and they certainly didn’t vote the same way.
But they came together to achieve a common goal, a common mission.
Beau will bring that type of leadership experience to the Secretary of State’s office.
