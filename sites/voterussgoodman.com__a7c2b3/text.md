Meet Russ
As a 7th generation South Georgia farmer, my loyal roots run deep, and unfortunately, too many kids coming up today are moving away for better opportunities.
I want kids in Adel, Homerville, Valdosta, Quitman, and all across South Georgia to be able to stay, build a career, and raise their families.
I will continue to be a strong champion of policies that grow opportunities here and be a strong voice for our interests.
Together, we can keep South Georgia great.
