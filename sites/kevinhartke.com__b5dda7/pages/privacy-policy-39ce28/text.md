This Privacy Policy explains how Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you access or use our websites, mobile sites, and mobile applications that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Site”).
Changes to This Policy
Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 may change this Privacy Policy from time to time.
If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the date at the top of the policy and, in some cases, we may provide you with additional notice (such as adding a statement to our website homepage or sending you a notification).
We encourage you to review the Privacy Policy whenever you access the Site or otherwise interact with us to stay informed about our information practices and the choices available to you.
Collection of Information
Information You Provide to Us
Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 may collect information from you when you:
- Sign up for email updates
- Sign up for text message updates (SMS information is not rented, sold, or shared.)
- Take any other action on our Site
This information may include your name, address, telephone number, and/or email address.
We may obtain information about you from outside sources and add it to or combine it with the information we collect through this Site.
Information We Collect Automatically
Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 may also collect information automatically when you visit our Site, such as:
- IP address
- Browser type
- Operating system
- Pages visited
- Time spent on pages
This information is used to improve our Site and enhance your user experience.
Use of Information
Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 uses the information we collect to:
- Operate this Site
- Send you news and information about our campaign
- Solicit your participation in campaign events and activities
- Obtain and confirm RSVPs to campaign events and activities
Security and Data Retention
Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 takes reasonable measures to protect your information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy unless longer retention is required by law.
Your Rights
You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.
You may also opt-out of receiving communications from Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 by following the instructions in those communications.
Contact Information If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact Kevin Hartke for State Representative – District 13 at campaign@kevinhartke.com.
