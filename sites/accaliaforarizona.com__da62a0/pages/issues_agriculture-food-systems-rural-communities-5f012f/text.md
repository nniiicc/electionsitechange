Agriculture, Food Systems & Rural Communities
Supporting Arizona Agriculture
Arizona farmers and ranchers play a critical role in our economy and food supply, yet many small and mid-sized producers struggle to compete against large industrial operations and outside investment interests.
I support:
- Strengthening local and regional food systems
- Expanding opportunities for Arizona-grown products
- Supporting small and mid-sized farms
- Workforce development in agriculture and food industries
- Agricultural education and youth programs
- Policies that help keep farmland in production
Arizona should be supporting the people who feed our communities and contribute to our local economy.
Farmland Preservation & Smart Growth
As Arizona grows, agricultural land is increasingly being lost to unchecked development.
Once productive farmland is permanently developed, it is extremely difficult to recover.
I support:
- Responsible land-use planning
- Agricultural land preservation tools and incentives
- Smart growth policies that balance development with long-term sustainability
- Infrastructure planning that protects both rural communities and water resources
Growth should not come at the cost of permanently losing productive agricultural land and the communities tied to it.
Farm to School & Food Access
I strongly support expanding Farm to School programs and increasing connections between Arizona agriculture and public institutions.
These programs:
- Support local producers
- Improve access to fresh food for students
- Strengthen local economies
- Create educational opportunities around nutrition, agriculture, and sustainability
I also support:
- Universal free school meals
- School gardens and agricultural education programs
- Community-based nutrition initiatives
- Expanding healthy food access in underserved communities
Food security is not just about having enough food — it is about having access to healthy, reliable, and affordable food.
Water & Agricultural Sustainability
Arizona agriculture depends on long-term water security.
Farmers are often among the first groups affected by drought and groundwater depletion, especially in rural areas.
I support:
- Responsible groundwater management
- Investments in water-efficient agricultural technologies
- Long-term conservation planning
- Protecting rural communities from unsustainable water use by large-scale developments and outside interests
Arizona’s future depends on balancing growth, agriculture, and water sustainability responsibly.
Culinary, Trades & Workforce Pathways
Food systems are not just farms — they also include culinary careers, distribution, hospitality, food production, and skilled trades connected to the industry.
I support expanding:
- Culinary and hospitality Career & Technical Education programs
- Workforce training and certifications
- Community college partnerships
- Career pathways connected to Arizona industries and local economies
These programs create real opportunities for students while strengthening Arizona’s workforce and economy.
