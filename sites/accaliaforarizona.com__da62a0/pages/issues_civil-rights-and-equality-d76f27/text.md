Equal protection under the law should apply to everyone.
I also support: Protecting LGBTQ+ youth from discrimination and harassment Inclusive school environments where students feel safe and respected Equal access to healthcare and public services Protecting same-sex marriage and family rights Transgender Rights & Student Safety transgender people deserve the same dignity, safety, and equal protection under the law as anyone else.
I am a fierce supporter of transgender Arizonans and oppose policies that target or exclude them from public life.
I do not support policies that force schools to out students to parents against their will or deny students the ability to be referred to by their chosen names and pronouns.
I have personally seen the emotional harm these situations can cause, especially when students are placed in unsafe or unsupported environments.
Schools should prioritize student well-being, mental health, and safety while still maintaining reasonable communication and involvement with families whenever possible.
Privacy and inclusion can coexist.
Schools can invest in practical solutions like private changing areas, modernized facilities, and strong anti-harassment policies that ensure all students feel safe and respected.
I fully support transgender students being able to participate in school athletics and activities.
Sports are about more than competition, they are about teamwork, belonging, leadership, and community.
At the same time, I understand concerns around fairness in certain competitive situations.
I believe these issues should be handled thoughtfully through evidence-based athletic policies and governing bodies, not through blanket bans or political targeting of students.
No child should be turned into a political issue simply for wanting to participate in school and feel included.
Immigration & Human Dignity Immigration and Customs Enforcement serves an important role in border security and addressing legitimate public safety threats.
However, I believe ICE must remain accountable to the Constitution, the courts, and the rule of law.
People deserve due process, dignity, and humane treatment regardless of immigration status.
Strong communities are built when families are stable, children are supported, and people are treated like human beings instead of political talking points.
ICE should not function as a government “secret police” force, and enforcement agencies should never operate without transparency or accountability.
Officers should be clearly identifiable, follow proper legal procedures, obey court orders, and provide warrants when required.
I also do not believe federal immigration enforcement should be used as a political tool or unnecessarily deployed into local communities in ways that create fear and instability.
I also do not support the current immigration detention system as it exists today.
We are seeing growing reports of unsafe conditions, lack of medical care, denial of due process, and deaths occurring inside detention facilities at alarming rates.
According to ICE reporting and multiple independent investigations, 2025 was the deadliest year in ICE detention in more than two decades, with at least 31–32 deaths reported in custody.
In just the first few months of 2026, at least 17–18 more people had already died in ICE custody, putting the system on pace to surpass those numbers again.
Many of these facilities are operated by private prison contractors, and investigations have raised serious concerns about delayed medical treatment, unsafe conditions, lack of oversight, and failures to respond to medical emergencies.
Detention facilities should be subject to strict oversight, public transparency, independent inspections, and enforceable medical and human rights standards.
People should not be dying in government custody because of neglect, abuse, or lack of accountability.
We can support secure borders and enforce immigration law while still protecting due process, civil liberties, and basic human dignity.
