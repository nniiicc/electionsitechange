Affordability & Cost of Living Arizona is facing a growing affordability crisis.
Since 2019, housing costs have surged by roughly 60%, while wages and incomes have failed to keep pace.
Today, Arizona ranks among the least affordable states in the country, with rising costs impacting nearly every aspect of daily life — from rent and home prices to groceries, utilities, childcare, and healthcare.
The reality is simple: people are working harder than ever and still struggling just to stay afloat.
Arizona currently faces a housing shortage of well over 100,000 units, while median rent has climbed to around $1,600 per month and median home prices exceed $420,000.
Nearly half of Arizona renters are considered cost-burdened, meaning they spend more than 30% of their income on housing alone.
Many working families have been completely priced out of homeownership, and minimum wage workers would need to work unsustainable hours just to afford basic housing.
I believe rebuilding a strong middle class should be one of Arizona’s top priorities.
Read More Public Education Arizona students deserve fully funded public schools that families are proud to send their children to.
For too long, Arizona has underfunded education while expecting schools and teachers to do more with less.
When classrooms are overcrowded, teachers are underpaid, facilities are outdated, and extracurricular programs disappear, families naturally begin looking elsewhere.
I understand why parents want options — but I believe the long-term solution is rebuilding strong public schools and strong neighborhood communities.
Read More Healthcare Healthcare should not be a luxury.
No Arizona family should have to choose between going to the doctor, paying for medication, or keeping a roof over their head.
Yet across the state, families are delaying care, skipping prescriptions, or avoiding treatment altogether because of rising costs and limited access.
I believe healthcare is a fundamental part of a stable and functioning society.
Strong communities depend on people being healthy enough to work, learn, raise families, and participate fully in everyday life.
Read More Working Families, Labor Rights & Workforce Development Arizona’s workforce is the backbone of our economy, but for too long, working people, skilled trades, and local industries have been undervalued and overlooked.
We are facing growing workforce shortages across healthcare, education, infrastructure, agriculture, and the trades while many workers are experiencing burnout, instability, and limited opportunities for advancement.
At the same time, students are often pushed toward a one-size-fits-all path after graduation without enough investment in skilled trades, technical education, apprenticeships, and workforce development programs that lead directly into stable careers.
I believe Arizona needs to rebuild respect for working people by investing in workforce development, strengthening career pathways, supporting workers’ rights, and creating stronger connections between education, industry, and local communities.
A strong economy depends on having a skilled workforce, stable local industries, and real opportunities for people to build meaningful long-term careers here in Arizona.

Our state continues to grow rapidly, but development has outpaced long-term planning.
Communities are dealing with increasing pressure on water supplies, roads, schools, utilities, and public services while extreme heat and drought continue to intensify across the Southwest.
Arizona needs long-term planning that balances growth with sustainability, protects existing communities, and ensures future generations can continue to live and thrive here Read More Agriculture, Food Systems & Rural Communities Agriculture has always been a major part of Arizona’s identity and economy, but many farming communities are being squeezed by rapid development, water pressures, rising costs, and policies that often favor large corporations over local producers.
At the same time, Arizona imports much of its food despite having the ability to grow more locally and strengthen regional food systems.We should be investing in local agriculture, rural communities, and food systems that create long-term economic stability while improving food security and public health.
Read More Civil Rights and Equality Freedom means being able to live your life safely, authentically, and without unnecessary government interference in deeply personal decisions.
Every Arizonan deserves dignity, equal protection under the law, and the ability to participate fully in their community regardless of their identity, background, or circumstances.
Too much of today’s political climate is focused on dividing people and turning vulnerable communities into political talking points instead of addressing the real issues affecting everyday families.
The government should focus more on improving people’s quality of life and less on policing personal identity and private healthcare decisions.
Read More Responsible Gun Ownership & the Second Amendment Arizona has a strong culture of firearm ownership, hunting, recreation, and self-defense, and I believe law-abiding citizens have the right to protect themselves and their families.
I do not support restricting the rights of legal gun owners without cause, nor do I support broad firearm bans that primarily impact responsible citizens instead of addressing the root causes of violence.
At the same time, I support practical, common-sense safety measures that focus on preventing dangerous situations while respecting constitutional rights and due process.
I support: Universal background checks Closing dangerous loopholes in firearm sales Increased mental health intervention and crisis support systems Safe firearm storage education Suicide prevention initiatives Stronger enforcement against illegal firearm trafficking and violent offenders I also support age-appropriate firearm safety education initiatives in K–12 settings.
In a state where many families grow up around firearms through hunting, recreation, ranching, or self-defense, I believe responsible safety education is more effective than avoidance or fear-based approaches.
Read More Environment, Sustainability & Climate Resilience Arizona is already feeling the effects of extreme heat, drought, rapid growth, and environmental strain.
These are no longer distant future problems — they are affecting public health, water supplies, infrastructure, agriculture, and everyday quality of life right now.
We need practical, long-term planning that protects our ability to live, work, and grow sustainably in the desert.
Sustainability is not just about the environment — it is about whether future generations will still be able to afford to live here, raise families here, and rely on the resources and infrastructure our communities depend on.
