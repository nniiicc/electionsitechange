Responsible Gun Ownership & the Second Amendment
I support the Second Amendment and responsible gun ownership.
Arizona has a strong culture of firearm ownership, hunting, recreation, ranching, and self-defense, and I believe law-abiding citizens have the right to protect themselves and their families.
I do not support restricting the rights of legal gun owners without cause, nor do I support broad firearm bans that primarily impact responsible citizens instead of addressing the root causes of violence.
At the same time, I support practical, common-sense safety measures that focus on preventing dangerous situations while respecting constitutional rights and due process.
I support:
- Universal background checks
- Closing dangerous loopholes in firearm sales
- Increased mental health intervention and crisis support systems
- Safe firearm storage education
- Suicide prevention initiatives
- Stronger enforcement against illegal firearm trafficking and violent offenders
I also support age-appropriate firearm safety education initiatives in K–12 settings.
In a state where many families grow up around firearms through hunting, recreation, ranching, or self-defense, I believe responsible safety education is more effective than avoidance or fear-based approaches.
Due Process & Mental Health Intervention
I support carefully structured intervention measures in situations where there is a credible and immediate threat of violence, but any temporary restriction of firearm rights must include strong due process protections, judicial oversight, clear evidentiary standards, and pathways for restoring rights.
Mental health alone should not automatically strip someone of their constitutional rights, and policies should avoid stigmatizing people seeking treatment.
At the same time, documented violent threats, serious crisis situations, and certain involuntary commitments may warrant temporary intervention when handled through a transparent legal process.
I also support expanding free crisis intervention and firearm safety training for School Resource Officers, healthcare workers, school staff, and other frontline professionals who regularly interact with individuals in crisis.
School & Campus Safety
I support trained School Resource Officers in schools and believe campus safety should focus on prevention, relationship-building, mental health support, and rapid emergency response.
I believe trained school staff should have the option to participate in properly regulated firearm safety and response programs if districts choose to allow it, though this is an area that requires careful standards and oversight.
I also support lawful firearm rights for adults on college and university campuses consistent with Arizona law and constitutional protections.
Domestic Violence & Violent Offenders
I support restricting firearm access for individuals convicted of domestic violence offenses or individuals legally determined to pose a credible violent threat through due process.
Protecting constitutional rights and protecting victims of violence are not mutually exclusive, and public safety policies should focus on documented dangerous behavior rather than broad restrictions on responsible gun owners.
