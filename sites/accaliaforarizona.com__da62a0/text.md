WELCOME TO THE CAMPAIGN
Arizona is growing fast and many families feel like they’re being left behind.
Housing costs are rising, classrooms are overcrowded, water concerns are growing, and communities across our state are struggling to keep up with the cost of living.
I’m running for Arizona State Senate because I believe we can build something better, stronger schools, healthier communities, and an economy that works for working families, not just the corporations and special interests.
This campaign is powered by YOU and is focused on finding practical solutions to the problems facing working families in Arizona by investing in Arizona’s future from the ground up.
Together, we can:
• Strengthen Education by turning schools back into community hubs
• Support our local farmers and small businesses
• Protecting for Arizona’s water future
• Expand workforce development and career opportunities
• Improve affordability for families by pushing back against corporate domination
• and building communities where families can thrive
I’m proud to call Arizona my home, and I believe the future of our state should be shaped by the people who live, work, and raise their families here.
Thank you for being here with me and for being part of this movement.
It’s Arizona’s Future, Let’s Grow It Together.
— Jayme Accalia
