Our Team
Sai S. | Campaign Manager
Sai believes that investing in community resilience and bringing value to the lived experiences of all people are essential to build a prosperous and healthy future for the human species.
She has applied her public health, social engineering, harm reduction, and molecular engineering skills to develop innovative programming and systems transformation to meet a diverse spectrum of need.
These efforts are defined through her roles with program redesign and community provider at Vancouver BC’s supervised injection site; offering reproductive health education and screening for incarcerated women; combating family homelessness; and building a felony diversion program to help youth resolve their felonies through community alternatives to jail.
Her community experiences are informed by her BA in Cell and Molecular Biology with a minor in Chemistry, BA in Anthropology with a minor in Integrated Human Health, and Master in Public Health with a Genomics emphasis.

Alexxa W. | Content and Creative Director
Alexxa is a visual storyteller, photographer, content creator, and designer currently based in Kumasi, Ghana.
Over the last three years, she has been committed to building and connecting communities through communication, content creation and design.
This commitment informs her development and strategy for political designs, where she puts emphasis on interweaving personal narratives into politics, and creating authentic representations that drive campaigns.
She has equally started her own platform called Phenomxnal Womxn - where she shares stories that speak to the realities of women around the world.
Women and BIPOC communities have often been pushed to the peripheries of politics, art and history as a whole, which is why she strives to bridge these gaps in order to diversify and amplify the representations of people of color and ensure that marginalized communities have their voices heard.
