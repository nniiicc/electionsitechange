Born and raised in North Philadelphia, I was taught that faith, family, and hard work aren’t slogans, they’re how you survive and move forward.
Those values have guided me from the operating room to the neighborhoods I’ve served.
I haven’t forgotten where I come from, and I won’t forget who government is supposed to work for.
A FIGHTER FOR PEOPLE WHO SHOW UP AND DO THE WORK For more than 20 years, I’ve worked in operating rooms where there are no excuses and no room for failure.
I’ve also worked in our communities, helping families find healthcare, resources, and opportunity when they needed it most.
I know what it means to show up, do the job, and be accountable.
That’s the standard I’ll bring to Congress.
LEADERSHIP WITHOUT EXCUSES Philadelphia has had the same kind of leadership for years, and too many families are still struggling.
I’m not a career politician, and I’m not backed by political insiders.
I answer to the people.
Period.
That means telling the truth, making tough decisions, and focusing on results, not headlines.
BUILT FOR THIS MOMENT Families across our city feel it every day.
Higher costs.
Less safety.
Fewer opportunities.
And too often, no one in Washington seems to feel the urgency.
When government falls short, it’s working people who pay the price.
That’s why I’m running, not to talk, but to fix what isn’t working and fight for a city that works for you again.
