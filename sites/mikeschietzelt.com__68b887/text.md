2026 Campaign Priorities
Charting a course for growth, opportunity, and safety
Lowering the Cost of Living
Driving solutions that lower costs for families — because when expenses come down, opportunities rise for everyone.
Prioritizing Public Safety
Standing with victims and first responders — ensuring those who protect our neighborhoods have the tools, trust, and support to serve with integrity and impact, while upholding fairness and accountability for all.
Education and Opportunity
Strengthening our schools, supporting academic achievement, and expanding opportunity — giving every student the resources and preparation to take on whatever comes next: college, a career, or a calling.
Infrastructure and Local Growth
Partnering with local leaders to deliver modern infrastructure, guide economic growth, and shape a future where opportunity reaches every corner of our region.
