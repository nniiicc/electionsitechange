Together, we can make real change throughout the Commonwealth and secure our present and our future!
Affordability for All Affordability isn’t simply about lowering costs; it’s also about making sure you have more money to make choices about your life the way that you want.
Raise the Minimum Wage As a small business owner, I’ve both experienced and seen my fellow businesses struggle as our customers have to choose between supporting local businesses with awesome offerings and basic staples like groceries.
A higher wage floor will help get more funds into the hands of everyday Pennsylvanians so they have more options in their lives.
Create Affordable Housing The rising cost of living is most directly exhibited in rising housing costs across the Commonwealth.
Creating affordable options for both older and younger Pennsylvanians will lower the financial pressure on our citizens.
Justice for All Equality, freedom, and due process are at the heart of our systems of government, and Sean will work to make sure those important cornerstones are protected.
Legalize Adult-Use Marijuana As we have currently seen with dispensaries in the Pennsylvania, we can manage adult usage of marijuana with proper regulation.
We can open this freedom up to other adults, which is also a road to increased revenue for the state budget.
Properly Fund Public Schools A bedrock of equality is education for all, and ensuring that Pennsylvania’s public schools are equitably funded and the model of schooling for the nation is key to our success as a commonwealth.
Safety for All Knowing that they’ll be safe both in an emergency and regularly as you go about your life is vital for Pennsylvanians to thrive.
Bolster First Responders with Mental Health Professionals Not every call to 911 requires a armed professional, but they do require trained professionals.
We ask our police to do to much, which is why I propose funding and policy to add mental health professionals to first responder networks.
Adherence to Law and Court Orders I support law enforcement, which also means I support following legal opinions and court orders, and oppose working with any entity that flagrantly violates and ignores such orders.
Such actions make our Commonwealkth inherently less safe.
