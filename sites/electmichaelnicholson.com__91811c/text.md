A Pro-Maine Voice for House District 39
Michael Nicholson is a dedicated public servant and community advocate running to represent the hardworking citizens of Maine District 39.
With a lifelong commitment to integrity and fiscal responsibility, Michael is determined to tackle the rising cost of living by fighting for significant property tax reductions.
His campaign is built on the foundation of protecting Maine's future, ensuring our schools are safe environments focused on academic excellence, and safeguarding our vulnerable populations from fraud.
Michael believes in transparent leadership and town safety, bringing a common-sense approach to the State House to ensure every voice in our community is heard and respected.
