Any of the information we collect from you may be used in one of the following ways: Your information, whether public or private, will not be sold, exchanged, transferred, or given to any other company for any reason whatsoever, without your consent, other than for the express purpose of the intention described ont he page in which you gave the information.

However, non-personally identifiable visitor information may be provided to other parties for marketing, advertising, or other uses.
Online Privacy Protection Act Compliance We are in compliance with the requirements of COPPA (Childrens Online Privacy Protection Act), we do not collect any information from anyone under 13 years of age.

Online Privacy Policy Only This online privacy policy applies only to information collected through our website and not to information collected offline.
