Judy Ngying is a public school teacher, mother, grandmother, labor leader, organizer, and advocate who has called Deltona home since 1997.
Judy is in her 29th year as a public school teacher.
She is also a mother, grandmother, labor union leader, organizer, and longtime advocate for working families. ​ After moving to Florida in 1997, Judy made Deltona her home.
In 2001, while teaching chemistry at Pine Ridge High School, she met Sean.
Two years later, they were married. ​ Judy was part of the opening team at University High School in Orange City before leaving the district after defending her fellow teachers against contract violations and retaliation.
She found a new home in Seminole County Schools, where she has continued her career ever since. ​ Today, Judy teaches science at a public alternative school serving children who have been expelled or in trouble with the law. ​ She believes every child deserves a chance. ​ Regardless of their ZIP code or past, every child deserves what the Florida Constitution promises: a safe, secure, uniform, free, high-quality public education.
