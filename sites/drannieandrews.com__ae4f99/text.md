Why I’m Running
I’ve spent nearly twenty years caring for South Carolina’s children, from newborns with jaundice to teenagers struggling with mental health.
And if there’s one thing I’ve learned, it’s that our kids’ health and wellbeing depends on the world we build around them.
I’m running for the U.S.
Senate because families are struggling in ways that we should never accept as normal.
