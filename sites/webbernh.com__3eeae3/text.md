Latest News ABOUT KURT A life dedicated to serving the nation and community West Point graduate and retired Army officer with over 23 years of service.
Airborne, Ranger, Special Forces (Green Beret) qualified who served in Infantry & Special Forces units and had 2 tours teaching at West Point Volunteered to go back on active duty after 9/11 and served in Iraq in '05 advising the Iraqi Army Professor at Lakes Region Community College and New Hampshire Technical Institute from '01 to '17 Eagle Scout with over 25 years as an adult Scout leader A founder of the Patriot Resilient Leader Institute, which runs the Camp Resilience program that has hosted over 150 therapeutic retreats for military, veterans, first responders and their loved ones since 2014 that have served over 1400 participants.
A founder and President of the Board of Directors of the Lakes Region Veterans Coalition which works to connect veterans and reduce veteran suicide.
Served on the Gilford School Board from '06 to '15 to include 2 years as chair Active in the Gilford Rotary since '02 and President in '12-'13 NH State Coordinator for West Point Admissions Volunteer coach for the Gilford High School football team from '04 - '11 On the Gunstock Ski Patrol from '01 to '21 Married to Elisabeth for over 40 years with 2 sons, also Eagle Scouts and West Point graduates Political Philosophy I am a moderate who believes in fiscal responsibility and that the most important things for any member of any legislature are integrity and a willingness to compromise .
Without compromise, democracy doesn't work.
I understand, and in many cases sympathize with, the valid concerns of people at both ends of the political spectrum but don't agree with the more extreme positions on either side.
I am confident that I can be a very effective Senator for District 2 in the State Senate because of my integrity, willingness to work with members from both sides of the aisle, and ability to compromise.
Top Issues Affordable cost of living, particularly housing so that employees of local businesses can afford to live close to where they work.
Protecting our democracy at all levels from extremists and autocrats to ensure our government represents the interests of all our citizens, not just those of one party.
Providing an excellent public education for our children.
Protecting the climate and environment so that the communities in Senate District 2 remain great places to live and for tourists to visit.
