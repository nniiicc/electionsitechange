Home News Contact Us More Home News Contact Us Home News Contact Us NEWS All Posts Kurt Webber September 27, 2022 This is my first post to the News part of my campaign website.
The purpose of this is to discuss my political philosophy in more detail so you can determine whether you agree that I can bring reasonable, responsible representation as a NH State Senator representing District 2 representing the towns of Ashland, Belmont, Campton, Center Harbor, Gilford, Holderness, Laconia, Meredith, New Hampton, Sanbornton, Sandwich and Thornton.
The "About Kurt" section on the home page of this website lists all the ways that I have served our nation and community.
I can continue this by serving in the NH House.
I don't like labels but if I have to use them, I'd say that I am a fiscal conservative and a social moderate.
I know it sounds corny but I truly believe in and try to live up to the West Point motto of "Duty, Honor, Country", the ideals of Scouting and the Rotary motto of "Service Above Self".
I also understand, and in many cases sympathize with, the valid concerns of folks at both ends of the political spectrum but don't agree with the more extreme positions on either side.
Unfortunately, politicians in both parties feel they must appeal to their respective party bases to win their primaries and thus we end up being represented by folks who advocate for much more extreme policies than I think the majority of the population agrees with.
Thus, while I am running as a Democrat, I will be a moderate who will work with members from both parties and Independent to do what's best for Belknap Country and New Hampshire.
I strongly believe that integrity and a willingness to compromise is more important than loyalty to either party..
