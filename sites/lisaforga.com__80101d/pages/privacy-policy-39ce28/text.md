What is Personal Information?
"Personal Information" is information about a particular individual that specifically identifies that individual, or information about that individual, such as name, address, email address, telephone number, or credit or debit card information.
Personal information does not include "aggregate information,"
which is data that may be collected automatically or without reference to Personal Information about the use of the Sites.
The Privacy Policy does not restrict Lisaforga 2020's collection and use of aggregate information.

Cookies do not contain any Personal Information, and we will never track your Internet usage outside of the Sites.
