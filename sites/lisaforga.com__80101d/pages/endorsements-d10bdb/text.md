I am pleased to share that I have been awarded the 2026 Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate of Distinction, a meaningful recognition on National Gun Violence Awareness Day and #WearOrangeDay .
We all want to keep our families, our schools, and our communities safe from gun violence, which has become the number one cause of death for children and teens in the U.S.
We’re fighting for gun safety policies that are proven to save lives, including: ○ Background checks on all gun sales, to ensure dangerous individuals can’t buy a gun with no questions asked. ○ This session, I was the co-sponsor of HB 453 Georgia Red Flag Protective Order Act, and HB 452 to ensure that persons convicted of domestic abuse cannot have access to guns. ○ Laws encouraging or requiring secure storage of firearms to protect children and adults by preventing unintentional shootings, gun suicides, and gun theft. ○ Getting assault weapons — weapons of war designed to kill as many people as possible, as quickly as possible — off our streets.
No one law can stop all tragedies, but there are critical, commonsense steps — with strong bipartisan support — we can take now to keep guns out of dangerous hands.
