Wes Moore Launches New Ad Highlighting Bipartisan Support And Results Moore-Miller Campaign Continues Multi-Seven-Figure Ad Buy With New TV Ad The Moore-Miller for Maryland campaign has launched a statewide multi-seven-figure ad buy that includes a new tv ad focusing on his bipartisan support across the state and the results he’s delivered while in office.
“Marylanders are continuing to see the bipartisan work Governor Moore has accomplished to grow our economy, drop violent crime to record lows, deliver relief from skyrocketing energy bills, and build the best public schools possible,” said Senior Communications Adviser Carter Elliott, IV.
“As the campaign looks to November, Gov.
Moore continues to communicate directly with Marylanders about how he’s worked across the aisle to deliver real results for all of Maryland.” The Moore-Miller campaign just launched a new tv ad “Bringing Us Together,” highlighting support from Republican Mayor Travis Marion and Democratic Mayor Monique Ashton.
The two leaders discuss how for Governor Moore, ‘it’s not about party, it’s about Maryland,’ and when their communities needed him, he stepped up to help.
Here’s What Marylanders Are Watching: WATCH: “Bringing Us Together” Mayor Monique Ashton: I’m a Democrat.
Mayor Travis Marion: And, I’m a Republican.
Mayor Monique Ashton: We don’t agree on everything.
Mayor Travis Marion: That’s for sure.
But, we’re both supporting Wes Moore.
Mayor Monique Ashton: For Governor Moore it’s not about party, it’s about Maryland.
Mayor Travis Marion: When our town faced economic challenges, he was there for us every step of the way.
Mayor Monique Ashton: And, when federal workers were fired in my city, he was there for us too, day and night whenever we needed him.
Mayor Travis Marion: Wes Moore brings both sides together and lifts us up.
Mayor Monique Ashton: And, lord knows, we need more of that.
