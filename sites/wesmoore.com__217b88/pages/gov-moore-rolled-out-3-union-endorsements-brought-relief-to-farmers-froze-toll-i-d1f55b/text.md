Maryland Unions Endorse Wes Moore For Reelection This week, the Maryland State and DC AFL-CIO , International Longshoremen’s Association Local 333 , and Mid-Atlantic Pipe Trades Association announced their endorsement of Governor Wes Moore as he seeks reelection in 2026.

Moore Declared A State of Emergency Over Diesel Prices And Brought Relief To Farmers Gov.
Wes Moore declared a state of emergency as record diesel prices strain farmers, easing fuel and trucking rules to cut costs.

Moore Froze Toll Increases Statewide Maryland drivers are catching a break next year.
Gov.
Wes Moore announced Wednesday he instructed the Maryland Transportation Authority to pause any toll increases for 2027.
The increases were expected to offset financial losses from the Key Bridge collapse, but they are now expected to be pushed back until 2028.
Moore said the move will save Marylanders at least $50 million.
“Whether you’re crossing the Bay Bridge, I-95, or traveling through one of Baltimore’s tunnels, we’re working to give Maryland families like yours just a little extra breathing room,” the governor said in a video posted to social media.
“I know that times are tough, and when costs go up, government should be looking for ways to bring them down,” he continued.
“So we’re going to keep fighting to make Maryland more affordable and to deliver for the people who call Maryland home.” Gov.
Moore Cracked Down On Juvenile Car Thefts Maryland is launching pilot programs to crack down on young people who steal cars.
Maryland Governor Wes Moore ordered the creation of pilot programs in Montgomery County and Baltimore City designed to prevent kids from stealing cars and killing or seriously injuring people on roadways.

Moore Issued Directive To Strengthen Policies Against Campus Sexual Assault Maryland Governor Wes Moore has signed a directive instructing the state’s higher education agency to recommend actions to improve campus sexual assault policies across the state.
In the directive, signed by Moore Wednesday, the governor references recent “deeply troubling accounts” of students in other states coming forward to report sexual assault.
