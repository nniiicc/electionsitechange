Michigan’s 15th State Senate District lies within Washtenaw, Lenawee, and Monroe Counties.
The district includes portions of Ann Arbor and Milan, and Ann Arbor, Lodi, and Milan townships, and all of Augusta, Bridgewater, Clinton, Macon, Manchester, Pittsfield, Saline, Superior, Tecumseh, York, and Ypsilanti Townships, and the cities of Manchester, Saline, Tecumseh, and Ypsilanti.
