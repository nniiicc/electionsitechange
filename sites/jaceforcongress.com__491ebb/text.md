About Jace Yarbrough Texas Strong.

Jace Yarbrough is a seventh-generation Texan, Air Force veteran, and conservative constitutional lawyer running for Congress to defend traditional American values and the MAGA movement.
A graduate of UT Austin and Stanford Law School, he has built a legal career challenging progressive policies, including vaccine mandates, DEI initiatives, and religious discrimination.
Jace is a dedicated Christian who co-founded a classical Christian academy with his wife, Elizabeth.
His commitment to election integrity and his background as a military officer and father of five drive his mission to fight for constitutional freedoms and the America First Agenda in Washington.
