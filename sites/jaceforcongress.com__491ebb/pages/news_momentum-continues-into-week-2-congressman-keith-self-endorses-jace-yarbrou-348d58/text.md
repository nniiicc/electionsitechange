Endorsement
Rockwall, TX — Air Force Reserve officer, constitutional lawyer, and seventh-generation Texan Jace Yarbrough today announced the endorsement of conservative U.S.
Representative Keith Self.
Self has a strong record focused on border security, fiscal discipline, energy independence, election integrity, and defending constitutional rights.
“Jace Yarbrough is a seasoned warrior for the conservative cause,” said Congressman Keith Self.
“An Air Force officer and Constitutional lawyer, he’s taken on the far-left in court and won over and over on issues like vaccine mandates, and battling abortion providers – Jace never gives up a fight.
We need more conservatives like Jace to help advance President Trump’s America First priorities and push back against the radical left.
Please join me in working to elect Jace Yarbrough so he can fight for you in Congress.”
Self’s endorsement builds on a strong week of momentum for Yarbrough’s campaign since announcing his candidacy last week, including support from Texas state representatives, major Second Amendment and pro-life organizations, grassroots conservative leaders, and children and family advocacy groups across the district.
A husband and father of five, Yarbrough has spent his life defending faith, family, and the constitutional freedoms that define the Texas way of life.
A descendant of a veteran of the Texas Revolution, he continues to serve as an officer in the U.S.
Air Force Reserves and has built a legal career taking on liberal overreach in the courts.
Yarbrough is a constitutional lawyer.
He’s challenged religious discrimination, fought unconstitutional COVID vaccine mandates, opposed the misuse of taxpayer dollars for abortion travel, and defended First Amendment rights against government overreach.
His legal work has included successful efforts to protect first responders, parents, and children from unlawful mandates and a far-left, woke ideology.
Yarbrough earned degrees in Electrical Engineering and Government from the University of Texas at Austin and a law degree from Stanford Law School.
With his wife Elizabeth, he founded Saint Francis Academy, a classical Christian school rooted in faith and academic excellence.
The Yarbrough family is active in their local church and is raising five children.
