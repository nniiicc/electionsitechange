Anthony Pro-Life America in the Republican primary for Texas’s 32nd Congressional District.
With this endorsement, Yarbrough continues to consolidate support from leading pro-life organizations and conservative grassroots leaders across Texas and the country. ‍ In announcing their endorsement of Jace , SBA Pro-Life America praised his unwavering commitment to protecting life from conception to natural death and his proven record of taking on the abortion industry in court and in the legislature. ‍ ‍ “I am grateful to have earned the endorsement of Susan B.
Anthony Pro-Life America who understand that am unequivocally pro-life, from conception to natural death,” said Jace Yarbrough.
“My record of suing abortionists, fighting for pro-life legislation in Austin, and working to defund and dismantle the abortion industry is not just rhetoric, it is a record of action.
I will continue that fight in Congress and stand unapologetically for the sanctity of every human life.” ‍ SBA Pro-Life America’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition backing Yarbrough’s campaign, including President Donald J.
