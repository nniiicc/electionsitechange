Endorsement
Rockwall, TX — Air Force Reserve officer, constitutional lawyer, and seventh-generation Texan Jace Yarbrough today announced he has earned the endorsement of Susan B.
Anthony Pro-Life America in the Republican primary for Texas’s 32nd Congressional District.
With this endorsement, Yarbrough continues to consolidate support from leading pro-life organizations and conservative grassroots leaders across Texas and the country.
In announcing their endorsement of Jace, SBA Pro-Life America praised his unwavering commitment to protecting life from conception to natural death and his proven record of taking on the abortion industry in court and in the legislature.
“I am grateful to have earned the endorsement of Susan B.
Anthony Pro-Life America who understand that am unequivocally pro-life, from conception to natural death,” said Jace Yarbrough.
“My record of suing abortionists, fighting for pro-life legislation in Austin, and working to defund and dismantle the abortion industry is not just rhetoric, it is a record of action.
I will continue that fight in Congress and stand unapologetically for the sanctity of every human life.”
SBA Pro-Life America’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition backing Yarbrough’s campaign, including President Donald J.
Trump, Turning Point Action, Governor Greg Abbott, members of Congress, Texas state legislators, and a broad alliance of national, statewide, and local conservative grassroots, Second Amendment, pro-life, and family advocacy organizations.
About Jace
A husband and father of five, Yarbrough has spent his life defending faith, family, and the constitutional freedoms that define the Texas way of life.
A descendant of a veteran of the Texas Revolution, he continues to serve as an officer in the U.S.
Air Force Reserves and has built a legal career taking on progressive overreach in the courts.
Following his military service, Yarbrough became a constitutional lawyer, where he challenged religious discrimination, fought unconstitutional COVID vaccine mandates, opposed the misuse of taxpayer dollars for abortion travel, and defended First Amendment rights against government overreach.
His legal work has included successful efforts to protect first responders, parents, and children from unlawful mandates and a far-left, woke ideology.
Yarbrough earned degrees in Electrical Engineering and Government from the University of Texas at Austin and a law degree from Stanford Law School.
With his wife Elizabeth, he founded Saint Francis Academy, a classical Christian school rooted in faith and academic excellence.
The Yarbrough family is active in their local church and is raising five children.
Texas’s 32nd Congressional District encompasses parts of Northern and Eastern Texas, including Camp, Collin, Dallas, Hunt, Rains, Rockwall, Upshur, and Wood Counties.
