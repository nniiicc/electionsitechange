“Charlie Kirk built this incredible movement based on action, with the goal of saving Western Civilization.
He ultimately gave his life for it.
We must never forget what he did, and we must never give up as we carry on this fight.” ‍ Turning Point Action’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition backing Yarbrough’s campaign, including President Donald J.
