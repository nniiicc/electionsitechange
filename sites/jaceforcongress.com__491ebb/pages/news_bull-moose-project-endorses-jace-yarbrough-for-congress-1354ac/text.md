Endorsement
Rockwall, TX -- Air Force Reserve officer, constitutional lawyer, and seventh-generation Texan Jace Yarbrough today announced the endorsement of the Bull Moose Project, a conservative grassroots organization focused on advancing America First policies and electing proven conservative fighters.
“America is at a crossroads, and the choice couldn’t be clearer,” said Aiden Buzzetti, President of the Bull Moose Project.
“When radical left ideologues in Congress advance policies that put Americans last and threaten the rule of law, we need proven fighters in Congress who are willing to stand their ground and win.
Jace Yarbrough is the most conservative candidate in this race and the strongest voice for President Trump’s agenda.
He understands that illegal immigration is a direct threat to American workers, public safety, and our national sovereignty.
Jace not only believes in America First policies, he will fight to enact them in Congress and put the interests of the American people first.
That’s why the Bull Moose Project is proud to endorse Jace Yarbrough for Congress.”
The endorsement from the Bull Moose Project adds to the growing wave of conservative support behind Yarbrough’s campaign since its launch, including endorsements from members of Congress, Texas state representatives, Second Amendment and pro-life organizations, grassroots conservative leaders, and children and family advocacy groups across Northern and Eastern Texas.
A husband and father of five, Yarbrough has spent his life defending faith, family, and the constitutional freedoms that define the Texas way of life.
A descendant of a veteran of the Texas Revolution, he continues to serve as an officer in the U.S.
Air Force Reserves and has built a legal career taking on progressive overreach in the courts.
Following his military service, Yarbrough became a constitutional lawyer, where he challenged religious discrimination, fought unconstitutional COVID vaccine mandates, opposed the misuse of taxpayer dollars for abortion travel, and defended First Amendment rights against government overreach.
His legal work has included successful efforts to protect first responders, parents, and children from unlawful mandates and a far-left, woke ideology.
Yarbrough earned degrees in Electrical Engineering and Government from the University of Texas at Austin and a law degree from Stanford Law School.
With his wife Elizabeth, he founded Saint Francis Academy, a classical Christian school rooted in faith and academic excellence.
The Yarbrough family is active in their local church and is raising five children.
Texas’s 32nd Congressional District encompasses parts of Northern and Eastern Texas, including Camp, Collin, Dallas, Hunt, Rains, Rockwall, Upshur, and Wood Counties.
