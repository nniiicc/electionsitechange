With Scalise’s support, Yarbrough has now secured the backing of the top three Republican leaders in the U.S.
House: Speaker Mike Johnson, Majority Leader Steve Scalise, and Majority Whip Tom Emmer. ‍ “I am proud to endorse Jace Yarbrough for Texas's 32nd congressional district,” said House Majority Leader Steve Scalise.
“An Air Force veteran and constitutional lawyer, Jace has repeatedly defended our Constitution, taking on and defeating the Radical Left in court.
He's the Trump-endorsed candidate we need in Congress to continue fighting for faith, freedom, and President Trump's America First agenda.” ‍ Scalise’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition backing Yarbrough’s campaign, including President Donald J.
Trump, Turning Point Action, Governor Greg Abbott, members of Congress, Texas state legislators, and a broad alliance of national, statewide, and local conservative grassroots, Second Amendment, pro-life, and family advocacy organizations. ‍ Endorsement Tracker Susan B.
