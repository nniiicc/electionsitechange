Overview Economy & Industry Education District Issues House District 32 The border counties of Doña Ana, Hidalgo, and Luna — where desert meets determination.
New Mexico House District 32 stretches across the southern edge of the state, from the western bootheel through the historic mining towns and ranching communities that have defined the borderlands for generations.
It is one of the most geographically expansive districts in the Legislature, covering roughly 6,500 square miles of high desert, mountain ranges, and open range.
6,500 Square Miles 3 Counties 10+ Communities 1 State Rep Counties of District 32 Doña Ana County Hidalgo County Luna County Communities of District 32 Deming Luna County The district's largest community and Luna County seat.
A railroad and ranching town founded in 1881, Deming today is a crossroads of agriculture, transportation, and border trade — home to the historic Luna County Museum and the Great American Duck Race.
Columbus Luna County The southernmost incorporated village in New Mexico, sitting just three miles from the U.S.–Mexico border.
Known for the 1916 Pancho Villa raid and the historic Camp Furlong, Columbus is a gateway for cross border commerce and a tight knit community of ranchers and families.
Hurley Grant County Partial — near district boundary A former mining company town with a proud labor history, nestled in the foothills below the Chino Mine.
Bayard Grant County Partial — near district boundary A small mountain community with deep mining roots and sweeping views of the Pinos Altos Range.
Lordsburg Hidalgo County The Hidalgo County seat and a historic railroad and ranching hub on I-10.
Lordsburg anchors the western end of the district, serving ranching families, miners, and travelers crossing through the Bootheel.
Virden Hidalgo County A quiet farming community in the Gila River valley, sustained by irrigation and small scale agriculture for over a century.
Animas Hidalgo County A ranching and farming community near the Arizona line, surrounded by grassland and the Peloncillo Mountains.
Playas Hidalgo County A small unincorporated community and former company town built for copper smelting, now a research and training site with a fascinating history of reinvention.
Rodeo Hidalgo County A tiny community near the Arizona–New Mexico line, named for the nearby canyon that once hosted cattle roundups.
Remote, rugged, and rich in frontier character.
Antelope Wells Hidalgo County The southernmost port of entry in New Mexico, a remote border crossing in the heart of the Bootheel used primarily by ranchers and local travelers.
Geography & Landmarks Florida Mountains A rugged desert mountain range rising above Deming, home to bighorn sheep, pronghorn, and some of the district's most dramatic hiking terrain.
Peloncillo Mountains A volcanic range straddling the Arizona–New Mexico line, with sweeping vistas, deep canyons, and rich mineral history.
Mimbres River Valley An oasis of irrigated farmland and cottonwood groves threading through the high desert, sustaining agriculture for generations.
Continental Divide Trail The southern terminus of the iconic 3,100 mile trail passes through the Bootheel, drawing thru-hikers and outdoor enthusiasts from across the country.
