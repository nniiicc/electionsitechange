Subscribe Donate Meet Jenifer Jones A nurse, a public servant, and a daughter of the borderlands.
Jenifer Jones has spent her life caring for others.
First came a wide ranging career in nursing, and now she serves in the Roundhouse as the State Representative for House District 32.
From oncology floors and chemotherapy infusion to caring for medically fragile children and COVID survivors, she met patients in some of the hardest moments of their lives.
Since taking office in January 2023, she has served the border counties of Doña Ana, Hidalgo, and Luna with the same steady hands and clear priorities she brought to that work: meet people where they are, tell the truth, and fight for the communities too often overlooked in Santa Fe.
Now in her second term, Jenifer has earned a reputation as one of the Legislature's most credible voices on health care and rural New Mexico.
Leadership in the Roundhouse Jenifer serves as the Ranking Member of the House Health & Human Services Committee, the senior Republican guiding the committee that shapes nearly every health policy decision in the state.
It is a role she is uniquely qualified for.
As one of the few registered nurses in the Legislature, she brings real clinical experience to debates that too often happen without anyone in the room who has actually delivered care.
She also serves on the House Transportation, Public Works & Capital Improvements Committee, where she works to direct infrastructure investment toward the rural roads, water systems, and transportation assets that keep border communities connected and competitive.
Between sessions, Jenifer continues that work on the Interim Legislative Health & Human Services Committee, the Behavioral Health Subcommittee, and the Transportation Infrastructure Revenue Subcommittee, and she advises the Economic & Rural Development & Policy Committee.
A Career In Healthcare Jenifer is a registered nurse with decades of experience at every stage of life.
She earned her nursing degree from New Mexico Junior College and built much of her career with the University of Kansas Health System, along with work at Lovelace Health System and Gila Regional Medical Center, where she specialized in caring for medically fragile children.
She devoted years to oncology nursing, administering chemotherapy and walking alongside patients through their fight with cancer.
After returning home to New Mexico, she cared for COVID survivors.
Across rural and urban settings alike, she saw the gaps firsthand: long drives to the nearest specialist, chronic provider shortages, and too many patients falling through the cracks.
That experience is why health care remains at the center of everything she does in the Legislature.
Deep Roots in the Borderlands Jenifer is a third generation graduate of Deming High School and is deeply rooted in her community.
She treasures the diversity of the border region, the strength of its culture, and the rugged beauty of its desert mountains.
Public service is part of her family story.
Her campaign work began in her teen years, knocking on doors for her grandfather, who served as a Luna County Commissioner, and her mother, who served as Luna County Clerk.
Today she volunteers at the Luna County Museum, sharing the rich history of her hometown.
Family and Faith Jenifer lives in Deming with her husband, Mark, and their two rescue dogs.
A devoted animal lover and advocate, she is a strong supporter of rescue and animal welfare.
She is the proud mother of seven children, whom she homeschooled in their younger years, and the grateful grandmother of ten.
Representative Jones credits her Christian faith with shaping her view of community, service, and government.
She is committed to defending the rights guaranteed by the Constitutions of the United States and the State of New Mexico, and above all the God given rights to life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
When she isn't in Santa Fe or her district, you'll likely find Jenifer outdoors.
An avid hiker and backpacker, she has trekked to the top of the Florida Mountains above Deming and across the southern stretch of the Continental Divide Trail, capturing the beauty of New Mexico through her camera along the way.
Chip In Today Photo Gallery Volunteer for Team Jones Representative Jenifer Jones' Key Issues Jenifer brings a nurse's practicality to the Legislature: identify the problem, listen to the people closest to it, and fight for real solutions.
These are the priorities driving her work for House District 32.
1 Rural Health Care: No one in the Legislature understands rural health care like a nurse who has lived it.
Jenifer is the Ranking Member of the House Health & Human Services Committee, and she has made closing the rural care gap her signature cause.
She championed expanding the Rural Health Care Practitioner Tax Credit, lowering the service hour threshold so more doctors, nurses, and providers in Luna and Hidalgo counties qualify — a direct tool to recruit and keep medical professionals in the communities that need them most.
She fights for the hospitals, clinics, and providers that keep families healthy without a three hour drive.
2 Parental Rights: Jenifer believes parents are the first and most important voice in a child's life.
She has championed parental notification policies and fought to protect the fundamental right of parents to direct the upbringing, education, and healthcare of their children.
From supporting school choice to defending parental involvement in curriculum decisions, she stands with families against government overreach and ensures parents remain the primary decision makers in their children's lives.
3 A Stronger Border Economy: Jenifer believes the borderlands can be an engine of opportunity for the whole state.
She has worked to revitalize rural infrastructure, including a tax credit to incentivize private investment in the railways and transportation assets that move goods and create jobs across the border economy.
On the House Transportation Committee, she fights to bring infrastructure dollars home to the roads, water systems, and public works rural communities depend on.
4 Public Safety and the Second Amendment: With crime a growing concern across New Mexico, Jenifer believes law abiding citizens have the right to protect themselves and their families.
A lifetime NRA member, she is a consistent defender of Second Amendment rights in District 32 and statewide, and she supports keeping firearms out of the hands of those who break our laws.
5 Education and Opportunity for the Next Generation: Jenifer wants New Mexico's children to be equipped to thrive and choose to stay and build their lives here.
She sponsored legislation requiring a personal finance course for high school graduation, giving students practical skills to manage money, build credit, and plan for their futures.
She is committed to strong educational and economic opportunities so the next generation can flourish in the Land of Enchantment.
6 Life and Family: Guided by her faith and her experience as a nurse and mother, Jenifer is a consistent advocate for life and for families.
She has sponsored measures requiring medical care for infants who survive an abortion, parental notification before an abortion is performed on a minor, and common sense health and safety standards for women.
House District 32 House District 32 covers all of Luna County and Hidalgo County, along with parts of Doña Ana County including the Hatch community, stretching from the vast, beautiful bootheel of southwestern New Mexico up through the fertile Rio Grande valley.
It's a district defined by wide desert horizons, hardworking families, a proud border culture, and the kind of close knit communities where neighbors still look out for one another.
It's also a district that knows what it means to be overlooked.
Rural communities here face real challenges: a shortage of health care providers, aging infrastructure, and an economy that has too often been an afterthought in Santa Fe.
That's why Jenifer Jones ran for office, and why she shows up every session to make sure the borderlands have a strong, credible voice at the table.
Your Representative.
As a Deming native and third generation member of this community, Jenifer doesn't represent District 32 from a distance.
She lives the same long drives, the same rural realities, and the same deep love of place as the families she serves.
From her seat as Ranking Member on the Health & Human Services Committee to her work on transportation and infrastructure, every priority she carries to the Roundhouse comes back to one question: what's best for the people of the border?
Delivering for the Border Talk is cheap; results aren't.
Jenifer fights every session to bring our tax dollars back home, into the roads, water systems, public safety, and community projects that rural District 32 has gone too long without. $6.32 Million In Capital Outlay Funding Delivered Back to House District 32 since 2023 Representative Jenifer Jones believes your tax dollars should come home to work for you, not disappear into Santa Fe.
Since taking office, she has fought to bring $6.32 million in capital outlay funding back to the hardworking families of Doña Ana, Hidalgo, and Luna counties, real, tangible investment in the roads, water systems, public safety, and community facilities that rural New Mexicans depend on every day.
Jenifer doesn't believe in empty promises.
She believes in accountability, in making sure every dollar sent to Santa Fe is fought for, tracked, and returned to the people who earned it.
That's the kind of steady, results-driven leadership District 32 deserves, and it's what Jenifer delivers, session after session.
