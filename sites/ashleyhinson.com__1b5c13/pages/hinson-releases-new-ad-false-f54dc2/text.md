Marion, IA – Ashley Hinson launched a new ad for the general election, “False.” The ad is set to air on broadcast, streaming, and digital platforms across Iowa.
“Josh Turek is lying.
Ashley Hinson doesn’t own a single share of public stock, and she voted to ban members of Congress from trading stocks.
Josh Turek is just a big liberal who tells even bigger lies.”
Hinson’s record of cleaning up Washington and rooting out corruption:
- Hinson voted to ban members of Congress from trading stocks.
- Hinson and her husband sold all their public stocks shortly after she was sworn into office and do not trade stocks.
- Hinson introduced legislation to ban politicians from betting on and profiting off prediction markets.
- Hinson introduced bipartisan legislation to ban senior officials in the Executive Branch from lobbying after their public service.
