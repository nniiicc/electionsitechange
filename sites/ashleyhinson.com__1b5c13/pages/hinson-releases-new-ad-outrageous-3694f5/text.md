Marion, IA – Ashley Hinson’s campaign launched a new ad for the general election today, “Outrageous.” The ad is set to air on broadcast, streaming and digital platforms across Iowa.
“I don’t trade stocks, and no one else in Congress should either – that’s why I voted to ban it.
The best investment on Wall Street shouldn’t be a seat in Congress.”
Hinson’s record on cleaning up Washington and rooting out corruption:
- Hinson voted to ban members of Congress from trading stocks.
- Hinson and her husband sold all their public stocks and do not trade individual stocks.
- Hinson introduced legislation to ban politicians from betting on and profiting off prediction markets.
- Hinson introduced bipartisan legislation to ban senior officials in the Executive Branch from lobbying after their public service.
