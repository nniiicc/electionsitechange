Meet Ashley Issues Endorse Volunteer Fighting for Iowa Tour Contribute News & Updates Hinson to Launch “Fighting for Iowa” Statewide Bus Tour Oct 13, 2026 | Press Release Back to Updates Mason City, IA – Tomorrow, Ashley Hinson will launch her “Fighting for Iowa” statewide bus tour with supporters in Mason City.
Over the next three weeks, she will traverse the state sharing her commonsense message and warning Iowans that Josh Turek is too liberal for Iowa.
“I’m excited to hit the road on our ‘Fighting for Iowa’ bus tour to share our message of lowering costs and bringing a much-needed dose of Iowa common sense to D.C.,” said Ashley Hinson .
“From now until Election Day, we will be all over the state earning every vote and warning Iowans that Josh Turek is just too liberal to represent Iowa in the U.S.
Senate.
A liberal like Turek – who voted to give taxpayer-funded benefits to illegal immigrants and to let kids secretly change their gender at school – doesn’t share our Iowa values and should not be serving our state in the Senate.
I’ll be a bipartisan fighter for Iowa, and I’ll work with anyone to make life more affordable, take on big pharma and big health insurance, and deliver results for our families, farmers, and small businesses.” Over the course of Wednesday and Thursday on her “Fighting for Iowa” bus tour, Hinson will be in Mason City, Fort Dodge, Sioux City, Council Bluffs, Ankeny, Davenport, and Marion.
More stops will be announced soon.
A special guest will speak in Council Bluffs, too.
