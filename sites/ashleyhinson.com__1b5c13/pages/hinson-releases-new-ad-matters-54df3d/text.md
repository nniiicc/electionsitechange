Marion, IA – Ashley Hinson’s campaign launched a new ad for the general election today, “Matters,” with footage of Turek claiming Iowans don’t care that he skipped half of the votes in the Iowa legislature to move up the political ladder.
The ad is set to air statewide.
“Liberal Josh Turek skips work half the time, and thinks Iowans don’t care.
Now he wants a promotion?”
