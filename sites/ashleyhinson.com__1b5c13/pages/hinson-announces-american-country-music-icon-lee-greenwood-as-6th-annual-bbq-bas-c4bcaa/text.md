Marion, IA – Today, Congresswoman Ashley Hinson announced that American country music star, Lee Greenwood, will appear as a special guest at her 6th annual BBQ Bash in Clive on Saturday August, 29th.
“I’m thrilled to announce that American country music icon, Lee Greenwood, will be at my 6th annual BBQ Bash in Clive,” said Congresswoman Ashley Hinson.
“Lee embodies the best of the American spirit and has made us all proud to be Americans through his music.
This year’s BBQ Bash is going to be a blast with delicious foods, great friends, and patriotic music.”
“I’m excited to come back to Iowa to be with Iowa’s next U.S.
Senator, Ashley Hinson, for her BBQ Bash,” said Greenwood.
“I’m proud to be an American, and I know Iowans are, too.
It’s going to be a great day celebrating our country, patriotism, and the hardworking people who make Iowa so special.”
This year’s BBQ Bash will also feature a great slate of guest speakers, including Governor Kim Reynolds, Senator Joni Ernst, Senator Chuck Grassley, Congresswoman Mariannette Miller-Meeks, Congressman Zach Nunn, candidate for Iowa Governor Zach Lahn, candidate for Iowa’s 2nd Congressional District Joe Mitchell, and candidate for Iowa’s 4th Congressional District Chris McGowan.
Tickets can be purchased online by visiting AshleyHinson.com/BBQ.
