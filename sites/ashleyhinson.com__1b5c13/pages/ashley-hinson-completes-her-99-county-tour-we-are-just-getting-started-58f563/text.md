“It is my honor to follow in the footsteps of Senator Grassley by carrying on his tradition of visiting all 99 counties in Iowa.
Since last September, we’ve been putting some serious miles on the mom van to hear directly from Iowans about their priorities – lower healthcare costs, better childcare options, and standing up for Iowa agriculture.
