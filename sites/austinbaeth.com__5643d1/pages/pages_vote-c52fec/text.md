Early voting starts October 16!
Through November 4, vote in-person at the Polk County Election Office.
If voting by mail, absentee ballots must arrive by the time the polls close on Election Day, November 5.
Or you can cast your ballot on election day, which is Tuesday, November 5, from 7am-8pm.
For voter registration, absentee/early voting, and polling place locations, visit the Polk County Auditor Website.
