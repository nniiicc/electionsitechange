Meet Hillary
Congresswoman Hillary Scholten is a former DOJ attorney, a Christian, and a mother of two.
In 2022, Hillary made history by flipping a 50-year Republican stronghold and becoming the first Democratic woman to ever represent West Michigan in the United States House of Representatives.
She is laser-focused on advancing common-sense solutions for the most pressing problems facing us today.
During her first term in Congress, Hillary has proven herself as a strong advocate for West Michiganders and a leader among her peers.
Hillary is running for re-election in Michigan’s Third Congressional District because she’s got the experience and the vision to continue leading West Michigan forward, for all.
We’re building something new here in West Michigan.
A new political home for people on the right and the left, who are ready to put partisanship aside, and come together on the issues that matter.
That’s how we won in 2022, that’s how I’m leading in Congress, and that’s how we’re going to win again in 2024.
