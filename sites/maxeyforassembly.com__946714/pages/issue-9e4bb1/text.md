Taxes
During my years on the State Assembly, New Berlin Board of Education, and City Council, I have a proven track record of keeping property taxes as low as possible while maintaining and improving services.
I believe in a fair and simple tax code that rewards hardworking Americans.
Unfortunately, our current tax code penalizes success and the intact family
Pro-Life
I am Pro-Life for many reasons.
Here are just a few.
- As a Christian, I believe life begins at conception, and abortion should not be a convenient workaround to the sixth commandment.
- As an adopted child, I know firsthand that there are other options available.
- Our founding fathers included three rights in the Declaration of Independence: Life, Liberty, and the pursuit of Happiness.
They are listed in that order because each depends on the proceeding right.
Education
When my daughter started public school, I ran for the New Berlin Board of Education because I wanted to take an active role in my children's education.
I know most parents don't have the time to be that involved.
Therefore, Public School Districts must report accurate information on school finances, performance, and curriculum to provide parents and those who fund the schools with options to select the school that best serves their child's needs.
