Help Put Drew on the Ballot As an independent candidate, Drew needs your signature to appear on the November ballot.
This is the most important thing you can do right now — it costs nothing and takes just minutes.
Why Your Signature Matters Independent candidates in Pennsylvania must collect a required number of valid signatures from registered voters in the district just to get on the ballot.
Without enough signatures, Drew's name won't appear in November — no matter how strong the campaign is.
This is the ballot access petition process , and it only works with help from people like you.
Download Your County's Nomination Paper District 108 spans both Northumberland and Montour counties.
Download the correct form for the county where you are a registered voter.

Make sure you pick the form that matches the county where you are registered to vote.
Print double-sided on legal-sized paper (8.5" × 14") Use a standard home or office printer loaded with legal-sized (8.5" × 14") white paper .
Print double-sided, flipping on the long edge (also called "head to head").
Do not use letter-sized (8.5" × 11") paper — the form will not be valid.
Use black or blue ink only All signatures and entries must be made in black or blue ballpoint ink.
Do not use pencil or felt-tip markers.
Fill in your information legibly Print your full name, address, municipality, and date clearly.
Your address must match your voter registration exactly.
Sign in the signature column Sign your legal signature in the designated space.
Each signer must sign and date their own line — no one may sign on behalf of another person.
Get others to sign — but follow the rules You may gather additional signatures from friends, family, or neighbors — as long as they are registered voters in District 108 who live in the same county as the form.
Circulate the sheet to multiple signers if you'd like!
Complete the Statement of Circulator — only when you are done collecting on that sheet The back of the form includes a Statement of Circulator that must be filled out and signed by the person who collected the signatures.
Do not fill this in until you are finished collecting on that sheet — sheets do not need to be full before you complete it.
Each sheet gets its own Statement of Circulator.
Do not number the pages Leave any page number fields blank.
Page numbering is handled by the campaign when all sheets are assembled — writing in page numbers yourself can create problems.
Return the completed form to Drew by July 28, 2026 Mail or deliver the signed paper to the campaign as soon as possible — no later than July 28, 2026 .
See the return information below.
Important Rules — Please Read You must be a registered voter in Pennsylvania House District 108 to sign.
Your address on the form must exactly match your voter registration .
Use the correct county form — Northumberland voters use the Northumberland paper, Montour voters use the Montour paper.
Do not sign both county forms — one signature per person only.

Print double-sided, flip on long edge , on legal-sized (8.5" × 14") paper only — letter paper will not be accepted.
Do not number the pages — leave page number fields blank.
Complete the Statement of Circulator on the back only after you finish collecting on that sheet.
No white-out — if you make a mistake, draw a single line through that entry only.
Do not use correction fluid or tape.
If the error cannot be neatly crossed out, print a fresh copy.
