Register to Vote The deadline to register online or by mail for the August 18 special election is Monday, August 3, 2026 .
Register or update your registration in minutes at registertovote.ca.gov .
Missed the deadline?
You can still register and vote in person at any Alameda County vote center — including on Election Day — using same-day (conditional) voter registration.
Register Now Vote by Mail Every registered voter in California’s 14th District will automatically receive a vote-by-mail ballot, mailed starting the week of July 20, 2026 — no signup required.
Return your ballot by mail (no postage needed), at any official drop box, or at any vote center.
Mailed ballots must be postmarked by Election Day, Tuesday, August 18, 2026 .
Track your ballot at Where’s My Ballot?
Track Your Ballot Vote Early or In Person Alameda County vote centers open in the days before the election, where you can vote in person, get a replacement ballot, register same-day, or get help in multiple languages.
On Election Day, Tuesday, August 18, 2026 , vote centers are open from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Find vote center and ballot drop box locations at acvote.alamedacountyca.gov , or call the Alameda County Registrar of Voters at (510) 272-6973.
