Overview
Welcome to the official website or mobile application (the "Sites") of Tony Strickland for State Senate ("SSFCS").

By using these Sites, you agree that your use of the Sites is governed by this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service.
From time to time, we may update this Privacy Policy.

Your continued use of the Site affirms your agreement to any changes we make to this Privacy Policy.
Information Collected and How It Is Collected
Voluntary Information
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us including your name, e-mail address, postal address, phone number, mobile number, and geographic location.
You may provide this information when you request information, register, make a contribution, send us an email, sign up to receive updates, connect through a social feed, or fill out a form for other campaign-related purposes.
We may also collect demographic information such as gender, date of birth, occupation, employer name, and zip code.
When this information is linked to personally identifiable information, it will be treated as voluntary personal information.
In limited circumstances, we may collect payment information such as a credit card number where needed to complete a transaction.
By requesting information to your mobile phone, we may obtain your cell phone number, your carrier's name, and the date, time, and content of your messages, as well as other information that you provide.
We will use such information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
Automatically Generated Information
We may also collect non-personally identifiable information automatically while you are visiting the Site or elsewhere on the Internet when our advertisements are served.
This includes IP address, web pages visited before and after visiting the Site, date and time, device type, browser type, and clickstream data.
This data may be collected using cookies, pixels, or similar technologies to improve our Site and user experience.
All Site users remain anonymous unless they choose to give us their personal information.
You may elect not to allow us to collect and use this non-personally identifiable data as part of our Service by following the Opt Out procedures described below.
Cookies and Other Technologies
We may use cookies and other technologies to improve user experience and analyze traffic.
Cookies are small data files stored by your browser at the request of a website.
You may block, erase, or be warned of cookies by adjusting your browser settings.
Rejecting or removing cookies may affect your ability to enjoy full Site functionality.
This website uses third-party vendors such as Google Analytics to help analyze how users use the site.
These services collect anonymous traffic data for analysis purposes.
You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking at any time by visiting this Google page.
Information Use and Sharing
Use of Personal Information
If we receive your personal information, we will only use it for the purposes described where it is collected or otherwise described in this Privacy Policy.
We may share this information outside of SSFCS only as necessary to:

- Protect rights, property, or safety, or
- Engage authorized service providers for limited campaign-related purposes.
We may also aggregate your personal information with others' information and disclose it in aggregate form for analytics, marketing, or reporting purposes.
Use of Non-Personally Identifiable Information
SSFCS or its authorized vendors may use non-personally identifiable information to deliver more relevant advertising and content, measure campaign effectiveness, and optimize user experience.
Contests and Promotions
On our Site, you may wish to participate in contests and promotions that we may offer.
We will use the information you provide to administer such contests and communicate with you about related campaign updates or opportunities.
Participation is voluntary, and eligibility rules may apply.

Your California Privacy Rights
Under California law, residents may request a list of personal information disclosed to third parties for direct marketing purposes in the past year.
To make such a request, email eric@stricklandforsenate.com with "California Privacy Disclosure" in the subject line.
We will respond within 30 days.
Children
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
Donations
When you make a contribution to SSFCS, campaign finance laws require us to collect your name, address, occupation, employer, and donation amount.
This information may be publicly reported as required by law.
Credit card data is used only to process your contribution securely.
All contributions are final unless otherwise prohibited by law.
Job Applicants
Any personal information you provide when applying for a position with SSFCS will be used solely for evaluating your application and will not be shared except as required by law.
