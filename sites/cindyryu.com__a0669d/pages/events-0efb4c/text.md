Join us at the home of Ah Warner, who is hosting a Garden Chat for Danica and Cindy in Magnolia on Saturday, October 10th from 11:00 AM to 1:00 PM.
Cindy Ryu has represented the 32nd District in the State House since 2011.
Before that, she was Shoreline's first Korean American woman mayor.
She's a UW graduate and small business owner who raised her kids in the Shoreline and Edmonds school districts.
In Olympia, she has chaired the Housing and Community Development Committee and now leads the Technology, Economic Development and Veterans Committee.
She's running for State Senate to take on affordability, expand access to healthcare and fully fund our schools.
Danica Noble is a former Federal Trade Commission attorney and community advocate.
She has spent her career fighting for consumers, supporting public education and protecting children online.
She's running to bring that work home to Washington.
Her campaign is focused on strong schools, responsible technology and a fair economy for working families.
