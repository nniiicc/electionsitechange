Get to Know Rep.
Cindy Ryu Cindy fights for public schools, public transportation, environmental protection, consumer rights, and public safety.
She supports increased access to affordable housing & homeownership, and delivers practical solutions to make Washington State a better place to live, work, and raise a family.
A Lifelong Commitment to Her Community Cindy and her husband Cody have deep roots in their community.
Cindy immigrated to Washington State in 1969 from Korea via Brunei and Manila, growing up in rural Thurston and small town Lewis Counties.
They have lived and worked in, and raised their three children in north King and south Snohomish Counties since 1976.
Cindy received a Bachelor of Science in microbiology and an MBA in operations management from the University of Washington, Seattle.
Cindy chairs the Technology, Economic Development and Veterans Committee, with jurisdiction over AI, tourism, under-represented communities, disaster preparedness and response, community resilience, broadband, and telecommunications.
She also serves on Appropriations and Consumer Protection & Business committees.
Breaking Barriers and Leading the Charge While a Shoreline City Councilmember, Cindy served as Mayor, becoming the first Korean American woman mayor in America.
In Shoreline, she served as President of both the Shoreline Chamber of Commerce and its Dollars For Scholars Chapter, helped improve numerous public parks, build its first City Hall and create Shoreline’s Green Business Program.
In 2024-2025, Cindy served as President of Pacific Northwest Economic Region, bringing policy makers and industry together from 5 northwest states and 5 western Canadian provinces and territories.
Accomplishments Fully Funding Education Cindy has fought to increase funding for Washington’s public schools and she will continue working to ensure students have the resources they need to thrive.
Consumer Protection Cindy has championed bills to protect comsumers from unfair business practices, predatory lending, and preventing copper wire theft.
