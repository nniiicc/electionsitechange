I believe homeownership is a key part of the American Dream , and that everyone in our state deserves access to safe, affordable housing .
At the same time, we must protect renters .
That’s why I supported legislation to cap rent increases at 7% per year—providing stability for working families.
My opponent, Jesse Salomon, voted to allow rent hikes of up to 10% a year , a difference that could mean hundreds or even thousands of dollars more per year for renters!
In 2021, I introduced and helped pass HB 1070 , a law that allows local governments to quickly purchase existing buildings and convert them into affordable housing.
This created a faster, more efficient way to expand housing options without raising taxes.
In 2018, I introduced HB 2711 to increase housing supply by reducing development barriers , lowering costs , and encouraging faster construction in targeted areas.
I also introduced HB 1834 to expand access to homeownership , especially for first-time buyers .
