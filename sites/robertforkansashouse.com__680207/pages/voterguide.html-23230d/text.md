Voter Guide
Robert seek the votes of the people of District 18, the Kansas House district in which I am a candidate.
Still, I welcome endorsements from anyone in the world who likes that for which I stand: limited government, individual liberty, economic freedom and traditional values.
Voting is pretty simple, really.
Robert votes for the candidates who most closely share his viewpoint.
For voters in District 18, here are the people for whom Robert will happily vote and campaign.
- U.S.
Senator: Roger Marshall
- U.S.
Representative, 3rd District: Eric Jenkins
- Governor/Lieutenant Governor: Ty Masterson and Jeff Klemp
- Secretary of State: Pat Proctor
- Attorney General: Kris Kobach
- State Treasurer: Steven Johnson

- County Commissioner Chair: Karen Crnkovich
If you are reading this and do not live in House District 18 but live in Johnson County, Robert votes for all Republicans and No to retain any judge on the ballot, except for Supreme Court justice Caleb Stegall.
I vote Yes to retain him.

Huff.
All will support better policies than their opponents.
All believe in limited government, individual liberty, economic freedom and traditional values.
