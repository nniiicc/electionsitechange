Why I'm Running The American middle class is under attack.
Values that we have held sacred for generations are being trampled.
The country is increasingly for sale to the ultra-wealthy.
I believe that the states are our last bastion of defense against this assault on democracy and our social systems.
It will be the states that lead the charge in rebuilding a healthy, robust society where people can aspire and flourish.
I want to be a part of that process—and I want to do it for all Ohioans, not just those who vote for me.
The challenges we face don't belong to one side of the aisle, and neither do the solutions.
Real change takes collaboration, and I'm committed to doing the hard work of building it.
