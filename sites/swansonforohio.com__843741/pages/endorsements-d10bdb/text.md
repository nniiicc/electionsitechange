Tinkel Community Member VoteVets Action Fund My name is Tracy McKelvey, a lifelong resident of Williams County, and I am casting my vote for David W.
Swanson for State Representative.
I hope that you will join me.
I had the pleasure and privilege of working next to David Swanson for over 30 years at the medical device company he founded.
In our time working together, I witnessed first-hand David’s careful stewardship of the company’s finances, his mindful determination to do what was right for the company and its employees before himself, and the generosity and friendship he bestowed upon the people who worked for him, as well as countless others in the community.
David is a true and trusted leader in every sense of the word.
When it was time to turn the reins over to someone else, he was careful and deliberate about choosing a partner who would secure the company’s future, safeguard its employees and, most importantly, keep the business in Ohio.
Never one to rest, he now sets his eyes on Columbus armed with a lifetime of business experience and community engagement.
He will work tirelessly to protect voter rights and provide affordable healthcare to all Ohioans.
David has a wealth of knowledge about the health insurance industry and how they operate.
Far too many of my friends and family are working well beyond retirement age simply because they need health insurance.
We need advocates in Columbus working to correct that.
If that resonates with you, I strongly encourage you to support David W.
Swanson in his bid for State Representative.
Tracy McKelvey I am pleased to offer my strong personal endorsement of Dave Swanson as a candidate for the Ohio House of Representatives.
I had the privilege of working alongside Dave during my tenure as President of our local hospital board, where he served on both the general board and the finance committee.
In those roles, Dave consistently demonstrated thoughtful leadership, sound judgment, and a deep commitment to the long-term success and sustainability of our organization.
He approached complex financial and strategic decisions with clarity, discipline, and a collaborative mindset, qualities critical to public service.
Beyond his contributions to the hospital, Dave’s entrepreneurial success further speaks to his capabilities.
As the founder of Daavlin, he built a globally recognized phototherapy business from the ground up and successfully grew it into a respected leader in its field.
This achievement reflects not only business acumen but also innovation, perseverance, and the ability to create meaningful impact.
In addition to his success in the medical device industry, Dave also demonstrated his commitment to our local community by launching Zazarac’s Bar & Eatery.
This venture not only created jobs but also established a welcoming gathering place that has contributed to the area’s vibrancy.
It’s another example of Dave’s willingness to invest in and strengthen the community he calls home.
What sets Dave apart is his integrity and genuine dedication to the community.
He listens carefully, values diverse perspectives, and works diligently to find practical solutions.
I have no doubt that he will bring the same level of commitment, accountability, and result-oriented thinking to the Ohio House.
Dave Swanson has my full support and highest recommendation.
He is exactly the kind of leader we need: experienced, principled, and focused on delivering real results for the people he serves.
Chad D.
