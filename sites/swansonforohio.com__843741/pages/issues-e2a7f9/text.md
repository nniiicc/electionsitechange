Food
A significant number of our neighbors go to bed hungry.
Advances in agricultural technology coupled with creative governmental policies could turn northwest Ohio into a haven of nutritious, inexpensive food for our neighbors and beyond.
Northwest Ohio is an agricultural powerhouse.
It's important that we harness that power.
As long as we import food or transport it from distant states, tariffs, water scarcity, and the high cost of fuel will drive grocery prices even higher.
The solution lies in our own backyard.
