ISSUES & PRIORITIES
- Every Arizonan deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare — no matter their zip code.
Mike will:
- Keep rural hospitals open and fully staffed
- Expand telehealth and mobile clinics
- Protect mental-health care for veterans and families Because no one should have to drive 200 miles just to see a doctor.
- As an experienced educator, Mike knows what our schools need.
He’ll fight to:
- Fully fund public education and raise teacher pay
- Expand career and technical education (CTE) programs
- Respect parents’ roles in their children’s learning
- Modernize rural schools to prepare students for future-ready jobs
“Every child deserves a chance to learn, grow, and build a future right here in Arizona.”
- As a disabled combat veteran, Mike has lived the challenges of transitioning home.
He’ll:
- Increase access to affordable veteran housing
- Improve VA and state healthcare coordination
- Cut through red tape so Arizona’s heroes can live with dignity
“When veterans succeed, our whole community grows stronger.”
- Rural Arizona can lead the nation in clean energy — while protecting our land and water.
Mike supports:
- Expanding solar, wind, biomass, and nuclear energy
- Creating good-paying local jobs in clean industries
- Protecting Arizona’s water supply and open spaces
A sustainable future means jobs for today and a planet for tomorrow.
- Tribal nations have always been the stewards of this land.
Mike will:
- Strengthen government-to-government partnerships
- Advocate for tribal representation in state decision-making
- Expand education, healthcare, and infrastructure on tribal lands
Mike will make sure tribal voices lead Arizona’s future, not just react to it.
