MEET GINA Gina Mitten is a dedicated public servant who grew up in University City.
After living in Los Angeles for several years, she returned to St.
Louis and juggled work, single parenting, and education.
Her passion for her community led her to serve on the Richmond Heights City Council, and then she represented us for eight years in the Missouri House.
As a Democratic leader in the House, Gina championed ethics, environmental protection, education, and government accountability.
Gina has remained deeply involved in advocating for her community and for reproductive rights - and is now running to serve as our next State Senator for Senate District 4.
