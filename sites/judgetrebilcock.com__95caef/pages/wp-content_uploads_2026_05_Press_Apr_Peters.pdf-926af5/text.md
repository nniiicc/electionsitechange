SENATOR GARY PETERS ENDORSES JUDGE CHRISTOPHER M.
TREBILCOCK FOR MICHIGAN COURT OF APPEALS Royal Oak, MI – April 1, 2026 – Today, U.S.
Senator Gary Peters announced his endorsement of Court of Appeals Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock for the Michigan Court of Appeals, citing his extensive legal expertise, integrity, and lifelong commitment to supporting Michigan values.
“Over the years, I’ve had the privilege of getting to know Chris Trebilcock both professionally and personally,” said Senator Peters.
“I have long respected his sharp legal mind, his measured approach to complex issues, and his unwavering dedication to serving the public.
Chris brings integrity, experience, and a deep commitment to justice—qualities that make him exceptionally well-suited for the Michigan Court of Appeals.” Judge Trebilcock expressed gratitude for the endorsement, emphasizing his commitment to upholding the law and serving Michigan residents with fairness and impartiality.
“I am honored to have Senator Peters’ support,” said Trebilcock.
“His leadership and dedication to public service are an inspiration, and I remain committed to ensuring that every case is approached with, diligence, and respect for the law.” Judge Trebilcock continues to build broad support throughout District 2 as he seeks to serve the people of Michigan.
Since January 1, he has received the endorsement of U.S.
Senator Elissa Slotkin, Fmr.
Governors James Blanchard and Jennifer Granholm, Fmr.
Michigan Supreme Court Chief Justice Bridget McCormack, Oakland County Executive Dave Coulter, and Oakland County Prosecutor Karen McDonald, amongst others.
About Judge Trebilcock Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock was appointed to the Michigan Court of Appeals in April 2025 by Governor Gretchen Whitmer, bringing more than two decades of legal experience to the bench.
Before joining the Court, he served as an employment, labor, education, municipal, and election law attorney at two of Michigan’s most respected law firms—Clark Hill and Miller Canfield.
He previously worked in Washington, D.C. as an Attorney-Advisor at the U.S.
Department of Labor, supporting administrative law judges on complex federal cases.
Judge Trebilcock has been repeatedly recognized for his skill and integrity by Chambers USA, DBusiness, Crain’s Detroit Business, Michigan Lawyer’s Weekly, Best Lawyers in America, and Super Lawyers.
