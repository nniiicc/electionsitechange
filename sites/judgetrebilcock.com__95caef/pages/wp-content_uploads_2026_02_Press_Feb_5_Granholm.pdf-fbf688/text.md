GRANHOLM ENDORSES COURT OF APPEALS JUDGE CHRIS TREBILCOCK Royal Oak, MI – February 5, 2026 – Michigan Court of Appeals Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock today announced the endorsement of former Michigan Governor Jennifer M.
Granholm, citing a shared record of public service and commitment to Michigan’s future.
Governor Granholm previously appointed Judge Trebilcock to the Michigan Great Lakes Wind Council, where he contributed to the state’s efforts to responsibly develop renewable energy resources and protect Michigan’s Great Lakes.
“Judge Trebilcock has consistently demonstrated fairness, professionalism, and a deep respect for Michigan’s people and institutions,” Governor Granholm said.
“During his service on the Michigan Great Lakes Wind Council, he brought diligence and sound judgment to issues of great importance to our state.
I am pleased to offer my endorsement.” Judge Trebilcock expressed appreciation for Governor Granholm’s support and reflected on their shared commitment to public service.
Most recently Governor Granholm served as the 16th United States Secretary of Energy from 2021 to 2025.
She previously served as the 47th Governor of Michigan from 2003 to 2011 and as the 51st Attorney General of Michigan from 1999 to 2003, the first woman to hold either office.
“I am honored to receive Governor Granholm’s endorsement,” Judge Trebilcock said.
“Her leadership and vision have had a lasting impact on Michigan, and I was grateful for the opportunity to serve under her appointment on the GLWC.
Her confidence in my work means a great deal.” About Judge Trebilcock Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock was appointed to the Michigan Court of Appeals in April 2025 by Governor Gretchen Whitmer, bringing more than two decades of legal experience to the bench.
Before joining the Court, he served as an employment, labor, education, municipal, and election law attorney at two of Michigan’s most respected law firms—Clark Hill and Miller Canfield.
He previously worked in Washington, D.C. as an Attorney-Advisor at the U.S.
Department of Labor, supporting administrative law judges on complex federal cases.
Judge Trebilcock has been repeatedly recognized for his skill and integrity by Chambers USA, DBusiness, Crain’s Detroit Business, Michigan Lawyer’s Weekly, Best Lawyers in America, and Super Lawyers.
