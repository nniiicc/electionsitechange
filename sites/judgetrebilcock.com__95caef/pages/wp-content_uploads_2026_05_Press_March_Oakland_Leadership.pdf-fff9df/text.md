TREBILCOCK FOR MICHIGAN COURT OF APPEALS Royal Oak, MI – March 11, 2026 – A coalition of Oakland County’s top elected leaders today announced their united endorsement of Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock for the Michigan Court of Appeals, citing his integrity, legal expertise, and commitment to fair and impartial justice.
The endorsement brings together key county leaders: Oakland County Executive David Coulter, Prosecutor Karen McDonald, Board of Commissioners Chair David Woodward, County Treasurer Robert Wittenberg, and Water Resources Commissioner Jim Nash.
Together, they emphasized their shared confidence in Judge Trebilcock’s qualifications and leadership on the bench.
“Oakland County residents deserve judges who bring fairness, experience, and a deep respect for the rule of law to the courtroom,” said County Executive David Coulter.
“Judge Christopher Trebilcock has demonstrated those qualities throughout his career.
His record reflects a commitment to thoughtful, balanced decision-making, and I am proud to support him for the Michigan Court of Appeals.” Oakland County Prosecutor Karen McDonald also highlighted Trebilcock’s professionalism and dedication to justice.
“Judge Trebilcock understands the real-world impact of the law and the responsibility that comes with serving on the bench,” said Prosecutor Karen McDonald.
“He approaches every case with fairness, preparation, and respect for the people whose lives are affected by the legal system.
That kind of judicial temperament is exactly what we need on the Court of Appeals.” The endorsement signals strong institutional support from Oakland County’s leadership as Judge Trebilcock prepares for the upcoming election.
County leaders say they are committed not only to backing his candidacy but also to helping build the broad coalition necessary to secure victory.
“I am deeply grateful to County Executive Coulter, Prosecutor McDonald, Chair Woodward, Treasurer Wittenberg, and Commissioner Nash for their support,” said Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock.
“Their leadership and commitment to the people of Oakland County mean a great deal to me.

Trebilcock was appointed to the Michigan Court of Appeals in April 2025 by Governor Gretchen Whitmer, bringing more than two decades of legal experience to the bench.

He previously worked in Washington, D.C. as an Attorney-Advisor at the U.S.
Department of Labor, supporting administrative law judges on complex federal cases.
Judge Trebilcock has been repeatedly recognized for his skill and integrity by Chambers USA, DBusiness, Crain’s Detroit Business, Michigan Lawyer’s Weekly, Best Lawyers in America, and Super Lawyers.
