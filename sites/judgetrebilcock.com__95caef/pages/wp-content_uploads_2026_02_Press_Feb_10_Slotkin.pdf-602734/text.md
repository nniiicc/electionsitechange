SENATOR ELISSA SLOTKIN ENDORSES COURT OF APPEALS JUDGE CHRIS TREBILCOCK Royal Oak, MI – February 10, 2026 – U.S.
Senator Elissa Slotkin today announced her endorsement of Michigan Court of Appeals Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock, citing his record of fairness, integrity, and commitment to the rule of law.
“Throughout his 25-year legal career, Chris has tackled the consequential legal cases with consistent professionalism,” said Senator Slotkin.
“Judge Trebilcock’s experience, his respect for all parties, and his thoughtful approach to complex legal issues make him exactly the kind of jurist Michigan needs on the Court of Appeals.” Slotkin was elected to the Senate in November 2024 and sworn into office on January 3, 2025, succeeding Senator Debbie Stabenow.
She serves on key committees, including Armed Services, Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs, Agriculture, and Veterans’ Affairs.
Before her Senate service, Slotkin represented Michigan in the U.S.
House of Representatives from 2019 to 2025, flipping a long-held Republican seat and winning reelection twice.
“I am honored to receive Senator Slotkin’s endorsement,” said Judge Trebilcock.
“Her commitment to public service, preserving the foundations of our democracy, and to the people of Michigan is unrivaled, and I am grateful for her confidence in my work on the Court.
I remain committed to serving with independence, integrity, and fairness.” About Judge Trebilcock Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock was appointed to the Michigan Court of Appeals in April 2025 by Governor Gretchen Whitmer, bringing more than two decades of legal experience to the bench.
Before joining the Court, he served as an employment, labor, education, municipal, and election law attorney at two of Michigan’s most respected law firms—Clark Hill and Miller Canfield.
He previously worked in Washington, D.C. as an Attorney-Advisor at the U.S.
Department of Labor, supporting administrative law judges on complex federal cases.
Judge Trebilcock has been repeatedly recognized for his skill and integrity by Chambers USA, DBusiness, Crain’s Detroit Business, Michigan Lawyer’s Weekly, Best Lawyers in America, and Super Lawyers.
