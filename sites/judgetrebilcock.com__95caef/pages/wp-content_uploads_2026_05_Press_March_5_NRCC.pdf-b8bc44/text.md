Box 142 Royal Oak, MI 48068 media@judgetrebilcock.com FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE NORTHERN MIDWEST REGIONAL COUNCIL OF CARPENTERS ENDORSES JUDGE CHRISTOPHER TREBILCOCK FOR MICHIGAN COURT OF APPEALS Royal Oak, MI – March 5, 2026 – Court of Appeals Judge Christopher Trebilcock today announced that he has received the endorsement of the Northern Midwest Regional Council of Carpenters (NMRCC) in his campaign for the Michigan Court of Appeals, District 2.
“Judge Trebilcock’s demonstrated strength and longstanding support for the community, especially hardworking men and women like our members, is an important quality to have on the bench,” said Eli Isaguirre, Political and Legislative Director of the Northern Midwest Regional Council of Carpenters.
“The 36,000 members of the NMRCC are proud support Chris to ensure his continued service on the bench.” The dedicated members of the Northern Midwest Regional Council of Carpenters are committed to backing candidates who consistently advocate for the interests of working families across Michigan.
Through its endorsement process, the NMRCC supports leaders who reflect the values, priorities, and perspectives of hardworking men and women throughout the state.
“I am honored to have the endorsement of the Northern Midwest Regional Council of Carpenters,” said Judge Trebilcock.
“The men and women they represent are the backbone of Michigan’s economy.
I am proud of the trust they have placed in me and remain committed to serving with fairness, integrity, and respect for the rule of law.” Judge Trebilcock continues to build broad support throughout District 2 as he seeks to serve the people of Michigan with dedication, impartiality, and a strong commitment to justice.
About Judge Trebilcock Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock was appointed to the Michigan Court of Appeals in April 2025 by Governor Gretchen Whitmer, bringing more than two decades of legal experience to the bench.
Before joining the Court, he served as an employment, labor, education, municipal, and election law attorney at two of Michigan’s most respected law firms—Clark Hill and Miller Canfield.
He previously worked in Washington, D.C. as an Attorney-Advisor at the U.S.
Department of Labor, supporting administrative law judges on complex federal cases.
Judge Trebilcock has been repeatedly recognized for his skill and integrity by Chambers USA, DBusiness, Crain’s Detroit Business, Michigan Lawyer’s Weekly, Best Lawyers in America, and Super Lawyers.
