Box 142 Royal Oak, MI 48068 media@judgetrebilcock.com FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE FORMER GOVERNOR JAMES BLANCHARD ENDORSES JUDGE CHRIS TREBILCOCK FOR COURT OF APPEALS Royal Oak, MI – January 29, 2026 – Former Michigan Governor James J.
Blanchard today announced his endorsement of Michigan Court of Appeals Judge Christopher Trebilcock, citing the judge’s integrity, legal excellence, and longstanding commitment to public service.
“Judge Christopher Trebilcock exemplifies the highest standards of judicial temperament and legal scholarship,” Governor Blanchard said.
“Throughout his career, he has demonstrated a deep respect for the rule of law, fairness to all parties, and an unwavering commitment to justice.
Michigan is well served by judges like Christopher Trebilcock, and I am proud to offer him my full endorsement.” “I am honored to receive the endorsement of Governor Blanchard,” Judge Trebilcock said.
“His leadership and dedication to Michigan have set a lasting example, and I am grateful for his confidence in my service on the Court of Appeals.” Governor Blanchard served as Michigan’s 45th governor from 1983 to 1991, U.S.
Ambassador to Canada from 1993 to 1996, and remains a respected voice on issues of public service, law, and good governance.
His endorsement underscores the broad respect Judge Trebilcock has earned across Michigan’s legal and civic communities.
About Judge Trebilcock Judge Christopher M.
Trebilcock was appointed to the Michigan Court of Appeals in April 2025 by Governor Gretchen Whitmer, bringing more than two decades of legal experience to the bench.
Before joining the Court, he served as an employment, labor, education, municipal, and election law attorney at two of Michigan’s most respected law firms—Clark Hill and Miller Canfield.
He previously worked in Washington, D.C. as an Attorney-Advisor at the U.S.
Department of Labor, supporting administrative law judges on complex federal cases.
Judge Trebilcock has been repeatedly recognized for his skill and integrity by Chambers USA, DBusiness, Crain’s Detroit Business, Michigan Lawyer’s Weekly, Best Lawyers in America, and Super Lawyers.
