Box 142 Royal Oak, MI 48068 media@judgetrebilcock.com FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE OVER 50 CIVIC, LEGAL, AND BUSINESS LEADERS JOIN TOGETHER TO FORM FINANCE COMMITTEE TO ELECT COURT OF APPEALS JUDGE CHRIS TREBILCOCK Royal Oak, MI – January 13, 2026 – The campaign to elect Court of Appeals Judge Chris Trebilcock today announced the formation of its Finance Committee, bringing together a diverse group of civic, legal, and business leaders committed to supporting Judge Trebilcock’s continued service on the Michigan Court of Appeals.
The Finance Committee will play a central role in organizing fundraising efforts, engaging supporters across the state, and ensuring the campaign has the resources necessary to communicate Judge Trebilcock’s record of fairness, integrity, and dedication to the rule of law.
Rudy Hobbs, former State Representative and longtime community leader, is the Finance Chair.
“Judge Trebilcock has earned deep respect across Michigan for his thoughtful approach to the law and his unwavering commitment to justice.
I’m proud to chair this committee and help ensure voters learn more about his record and his service to the people of Michigan,” said Hobbs.
Judge Trebilcock expressed gratitude for the committee’s support, noting that the group reflects a broad coalition of individuals who value an experienced and principled judiciary.
“Having such a strong and engaged Finance Committee is an honor,” Judge Trebilcock said.

Trebilcock was appointed to the Michigan Court of Appeals in April 2025 by Governor Gretchen Whitmer, bringing more than two decades of legal experience to the bench.
Before joining the Court, he served as an employment, labor, education, municipal, and election law attorney at two of Michigan’s most respected law firms—Clark Hill and Miller Canfield.
He previously worked in Washington, D.C. as an Attorney-Advisor at the U.S.
Department of Labor, supporting administrative law judges on complex federal cases.
Judge Trebilcock has been repeatedly recognized for his skill and integrity by Chambers USA, DBusiness, Crain’s Detroit Business, Michigan Lawyer’s Weekly, Best Lawyers in America, and Super Lawyers.
