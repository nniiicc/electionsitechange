WILLIAM JOSEPH
FOR FL STATE REPRESENTATIVE District 88, Republican
“Solutions for a Smarter Future”
Husband, Father, Businessman, Patriot
As a resident of the District for over three decades, William Joseph, 55, combines his business acumen with public service, having served on three boards.
Joseph is married to Misoule Pierre Gilles and have three children raised and originated in the district, two daughters and one son.
A graduate of Oxford Academy and a licensed professional in investigations and as a fugitive agent, Joseph has a strong business background, having successfully owned and operated multiple businesses in the district.
In his public service capacity, Joseph focuses on aiding the homeless and the needy, and has been instrumental in his board roles within Lake Worth Beach.
His main priorities include fostering greater transparency and efficiency in municipal operations, creating a mobile response unit of social and emergency medical workers, improving District 88 and local recreational facilities, and making housing more affordable for residents, particularly first-time homebuyers.
