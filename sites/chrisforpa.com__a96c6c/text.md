Meet Chris Deluzio
Congressman Chris Deluzio is a Thornburg native, Iraq War veteran, and voting rights attorney.
After graduating from the U.S.
Naval Academy, Chris served as an officer in the Navy and deployed overseas multiple times.
As a lawyer, Chris worked to protect voting rights and our elections as the policy director at Pitt Cyber and previously at the Brennan Center for Justice.
