ABOUT AMY
Amy Hossain is running for Oklahoma Senate District 34 to bring fresh leadership, progressive values, and real change to the state.
As a mom, non-profit leader, and dedicated community advocate, she is committed to making a positive impact in the lives of all Oklahomans.
AMY'S PRIORITIES
Affordable Living and Housing
Every family deserves a safe, stable, and affordable place to call home.
Amy is committed to expanding access to affordable housing and lowering the cost of living so families can build lasting opportunity.
By investing in smart housing policies, supporting first-time homeowners, and ensuring fair rental practices, we can create communities where everyone can thrive.
When families have secure, affordable homes, they can focus on what truly matters-raising their children, building their futures, and strengthening the fabric of our communities.
Accessible, Affordable Healthcare
Healthcare means dignity and peace of mind, not being forced to choose between medicine and groceries.
Amy will work to make healthcare accessible, affordable, and fair to everyone.
This means fighting to lower prescription drug costs, expanding coverage for working families, and investing in primary and mental health services across Oklahoma.
Access to safe, comprehensive, quality education for our kids
Amy will fight to fully fund public schools so they have the resources needed to give every child a quality education.
She will work to make sure teacher salaries are competitive, recognizing that great teachers are key to strong communities.
Amy is committed to protecting our kids by supporting commonsense measures that keep schools safe and free from violence.
She believes every child deserves a safe and supportive learning environment that prepares them for a successful future.
