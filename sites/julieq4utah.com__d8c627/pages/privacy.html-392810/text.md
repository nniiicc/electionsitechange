Privacy Policy Effective Date: October 16, 2026 JulieQ4Utah ("the Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have — including with respect to text messages.
Information We Collect When you use our contact, volunteer, yard sign, or donation forms, we may collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, mailing address, and phone number.
If you make a donation, our payment processor also collects the billing information necessary to process that transaction.
How We Use Your Information We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, send you campaign updates and event reminders by email or text message (only if you've opted in), fulfill yard sign requests, coordinate volunteer activities, and process donations.
Text Messaging Program If you provide your phone number and check the text messaging opt-in box on our contact form, you consent to receive text messages from JulieQ4Utah, including campaign updates, event reminders, and get-out-the-vote messages, which may be sent using an autodialer.
Donations will not be solicited in these messages.
Message frequency varies — up to once per day through Election Day (November 3), and up to once per month thereafter.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message.
For help, reply HELP or Contact us on our website, for assistance.
Providing a phone number and opting in to text messages is never required to contact us, volunteer, request a yard sign, or donate.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Sharing of Information We do not share or sell your personal information, including your phone number and text messaging opt-in status, to any third parties.
We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or to service providers (such as our email, text messaging, and payment processing vendors) solely to help us operate this website and communicate with you on our behalf.
Your Choices You may unsubscribe from email updates using the link included in those emails, and from text messages by replying STOP as described above.
You may also contact us at any time to ask that we update or remove your information.
Terms & Conditions These Terms & Conditions apply to your use of this website and to our text messaging program.
Message types: campaign updates, event reminders, volunteer and yard sign coordination, and get-out-the-vote messages.
Donations will not be solicited.
Message frequency: up to once per day through Election Day (November 3), and up to once per month thereafter.
Message & data rates may apply.
Opt-out: reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages.
Help: reply HELP, or Contact us on our website, for assistance.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Contact Us If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our text messaging program, please Contact us on our website.
