Biography
I am proud to be the youngest candidate for State Representative in Missouri.
I'm an HVAC technician.
I graduated McCluer High School in Florissant, Missouri.
It is a part of the Ferguson-Florissant School District.
I became active in politics, like so many of us, in 2020.
My mission is about getting more people to participate- especially those who feel disenfranchised.
I started by attending grassroots organization meetings and have knocked over 1,250 doors for conservative candidates to better understand what voters need in our area of north county.
I made the decision to run for office to be the change we need in our state government and a viable, alternative candidate conservative Democrats and Republicans in Florissant and House District 75 can vote for.
