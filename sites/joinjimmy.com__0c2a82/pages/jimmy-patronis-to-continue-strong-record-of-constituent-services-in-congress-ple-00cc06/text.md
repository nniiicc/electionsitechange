In office, CFO Patronis has made it his top priority to serve constituents and he looks forward to continuing that in Congress.
As a member of Florida’s Cabinet, CFO Patronis led operations assisting thousands of Floridians with insurance claims, connecting policy holders with insurance companies, seeing their issue is resolved.
CFO Patronis plans to compliment Senator Rick Scott, voted best congressional office for constituent services, to deliver results for Floridians.
CFO Jimmy Patronis said , “I am a people person, that is why I am running for Congress, to assist and serve Floridians in the Panhandle.
For those who enjoy the traditional atmosphere of an in person meeting, I am pledging to reopen a district office in Pensacola.
It makes sense for the largest city in the Panhandle to have a dedicated location where they can go to get help and have their voices heard.
Florida is known in Washington for its constituent services and I will be the best advocate for the Panhandle in Congress.” The district office for CD-1 in Escambia county was closed in 2023 .
In addition to opening an Escambia office, CFO Patronis will prioritize mobile office hours and visiting Floridians in the district who may not be able to get to an office in person.
In Congress, CFO Patronis will work with President Trump and America First patriots to fix issues constituents are facing on the front end.
In addition to being endorsed by President Trump, Senator Rick Scott endorsed Jimmy Patronis in the CD-1 race.
The Special Primary Election Day is scheduled for January 28, 2025 and the Special General Election is slated for April 1, 2025.
