I am Lisa Cathro, an enthusiastic & energetic public school teacher, small business owner, church volunteer, and mom to 3 amazing kids!
I am running for WV House of Delegates District 96 because I am passionate about the people in our Berkeley County community, and I believe we deserve state government that actually listens to and works for the people!
I Will Work Across the Aisle to Get Things Done!
As a public school teacher, I have spent the last 13 years creating an environment that welcomes all voices & ensures collaboration to protect the learning of every person in my classroom.
As your WV House delegate, I will do the same for Berkeley County!
I will bring diverse voices together (Republicans, Democrats, & Independents) to find innovative solutions to our community problems.
TOGETHER WE WILL: Protect our personal freedoms, religious freedom, and constitutional rights Fully fund our Berkeley County public schools Support local small business & job market Protect our wild & wonderful outdoor spaces Celebrate diversity in our community Grow Berkeley County healthcare infrastructure Ensure access to food, clothing, & housing for all Demonstrate fiscal responsibility Protect our democracy & hold our elected officials accountable I Will Work to Fund our Social Programs While Also Cutting Government Spending As a small business owner, I know that every penny counts!
Sometimes we have to do without things we want (a new piece of equipment, a bigger office space, extra staff, etc.) in order to afford the things we need.
As your delegate, I am committed to putting our Berkeley County community needs first - and we have many!
My number #1 priority is fully funding our public schools.
I fervently believe that EDUCATION IS THE SOLUTION to every community issue: Better Jobs, Affordable Housing, Food Security, Addiction Recovery, Healthcare, etc.
In each of these areas, I believe we need to be "teaching each other to fish" not just handing out fish.
Of course, we should meet our community's immediate needs to prevent homelessness, food insecurity, etc., but these programs need to include long-term solutions and support through local workforce and educational opportunities.
In order to meet these objectives, there will be other areas of government that need to be cut.
I am 100% committed to balancing our budget and making sacrifices in other areas in order meet the most urgent needs of our communities (bring utility prices down, increasing wages & supporting small businesses, ensuring access to affordable housing & healthcare access, etc.).
I have spent much of my adult life working multiple jobs, driving beat-up cars, renting basement apartments for my family to live in, putting myself through college, buying classroom supplies for my students, eating ramen and peanut butter sandwiches - all while raising 3 children.
It has not been easy, and we have not always had everything we wanted, but I made sure everyone had everything they needed - I will do the same for Berkeley County!
Together, we will sacrifice the funding of the government programs we "like" and "want" in order to fund the government programs our community so desperately needs.
