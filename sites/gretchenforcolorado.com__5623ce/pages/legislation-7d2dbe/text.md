It’s a process, that is built step by step, through listening, collaboration, and action.
The legislation below reflect this process, measures I’ve sponsored and co-sponsored, many with bipartisan support.
Increasing Affordability We made it easier for artists to make a living and do business in Colorado( SB26-133 ).
We worked to ease the squeeze of rising costs by bringing more transparency to insurance decisions ( HB25-1002 ) and strengthening wage protections ( HB25-1001 ).
We’ve also supported pathways to more attainable housing ( HB25-1272 ) and worked to improve the affordability of private health insurance ( HB25B-1006 ).
Strengthening Our Mental Health System We established safeguards for how artificial intelligence is used in the delivery of psychotherapy ( HB26-1195 ).
We simplified the contracting and reporting process for healthcare providers so more people can obtain proper mental healthcare ( HB26-1116 and HB25-1124 ) and expanded treatment options for youth with severe depression ( HB25-1070 ).
We removed barriers like copays for those who can least afford them ( HB25-1026 ) and bolstered re-entry support for those exiting prison ( HB25-1129 ).
Advancing Human Rights We strengthened the law to prevent discrimination against rideshare passengers with service animals and wheelchairs ( HB26-1043 ).
We helped those with disabilities obtain meaningful employment ( HB25-1018 ), and have the opportunity to fully participate in their communities ( HB25-1017 ).
