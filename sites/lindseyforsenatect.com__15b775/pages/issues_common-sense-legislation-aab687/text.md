Meet Melissa Issues Events Volunteer Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Common Sense Legislation Common Sense Legislation Melissa believes that effective legislation should be clear, practical, and built around the needs of the people it serves.
Too often, policies coming out of Hartford are overly complicated, lack transparency, or create unintended consequences for local communities.
Residents deserve leadership that takes the time to get things right—not just get things passed.
Common-sense legislation means slowing down when necessary, asking the right questions, and making sure policies actually work in practice—not just on paper.
Melissa is committed to: Transparency and Accountability Ensuring that legislation is clearly communicated, publicly accessible, and open to meaningful input before decisions are made.
Practical, Results-Driven Policy Focusing on solutions that address real problems—whether it’s affordability, infrastructure, or public safety—without adding unnecessary complexity or red tape.
Protecting Local Communities Recognizing that one-size-fits-all policies don’t always work.
Melissa will advocate for legislation that respects the unique needs of towns across District 24.
Supporting Small Businesses and Working Families Reviewing regulations and policies to ensure they don’t place unnecessary burdens on the people driving Connecticut’s local economy.
Reducing Unintended Consequences Taking a thoughtful approach to legislation by considering long-term impacts—not just short-term political wins.
Independent and Thoughtful Leadership Melissa believes elected officials should evaluate each issue on its merits, ask questions, and make decisions based on what’s best for their communities—not simply follow a predetermined path.
