INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website:
Personal Information:
We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide to us, such as your name, email address, postal address, phone number, and any other information you submit through our website’s forms.

If you choose to opt in to receive text messages from us, we may collect your mobile phone number and related data required for text messaging services.

When you visit our website, we may automatically collect certain information about your device, browser, and usage patterns.

We may use the personal information you provide to us for the following purposes:

• To analyze and improve our website’s performance, content, and user experience.
• To comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights and agreements.

Your mobile phone number and related data collected for text messaging services will only be used to send you campaign-related text messages and updates for which you have provided consent.
SHARING OF INFORMATION
We will not sell or rent your personal information.

No mobile information will be shared by us with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared by us with any third parties.
Service providers may process information as necessary to provide services on our behalf, such as delivering campaign text messages, subject to applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.
