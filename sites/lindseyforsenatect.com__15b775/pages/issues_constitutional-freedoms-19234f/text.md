Meet Melissa Issues Events Volunteer Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Constitutional Freedoms Constitutional Freedoms Religious Liberty: Those in Hartford are pushing to undermine the Religious Freedom Restoration Act (RFRA) —the law that requires “strict scrutiny” for any government action that infringes on religious beliefs.
This means a law can only be justified if it is the least restrictive way to achieve a compelling government interest.
The state is currently in ongoing litigation over removing religious exemptions for vaccines in schools.
Instead of letting the courts decide, lawmakers are trying to change the law mid-case to influence the outcome.
If they can do this once, what stops them from doing it again and again?
Medical Autonomy: Legislation is being proposed to change vaccine requirements and apply them to all residents, not just children.
This could effectively create a state-mandated adult vaccine standard of care without calling it a mandate.
Questions arise: Could insurance companies deny coverage if you don’t follow these guidelines?
Could employers or doctors enforce compliance?
Could this extend to COVID, flu, or HPV vaccines?
Public Input: Thousands of Connecticut residents submitted testimony opposing these bills , while only a handful supported them.
Yet the Committee on Public Health cut off testimony and voted to support the bills anyway.
This is not how representative government is supposed to function.
The Core Question: What happened with the principles of “No Kings” and “My Body, My Choice” in Hartford today?
Residents’ rights should come first—our freedoms, our health decisions, and our voices must be respected.
Right to bear arms: Melissa supports the constitutional right to bear arms while recognizing the importance of responsible ownership and public safety.
Connecticut already has some of the strictest firearm laws in the country.
Rather than adding unnecessary layers of regulation, Melissa believes the focus should be on enforcing existing laws and ensuring they are applied fairly and effectively.
She supports: Responsible gun ownership Strong background checks already in place Enforcement of current laws over creating new burdens Parental Rights: Parents—not the state—have the ultimate responsibility for their children.
