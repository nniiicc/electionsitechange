Public Safety
Melissa believes that safe communities are the foundation of a strong Connecticut.
Residents deserve to feel secure in their homes, their neighborhoods, and their local businesses.
She supports law enforcement and recognizes the critical role officers play in keeping communities safe.
At the same time, public safety policy must be effective, fair, and focused on results—not politics.
In recent years, Connecticut has faced real concerns around repeat offenders, strained police resources, and policies that can make it harder for law enforcement to do their jobs effectively.
Melissa believes we need to refocus on common-sense solutions that prioritize both safety and accountability.
She supports:
- Enforcing existing laws consistently and effectively Ensuring that repeat offenders are held accountable and that laws already on the books are applied as intended.
- Supporting local law enforcement departments Making sure departments have the staffing, training, and resources they need to respond quickly and effectively.
- Addressing repeat offender policies Reviewing policies that allow individuals to cycle through the system without meaningful consequences, and working toward reforms that improve outcomes and public safety.
- Investing in community-based solutions Supporting programs that help prevent crime before it starts, including youth engagement and local partnerships.
- Improving communication between state and local agencies Ensuring towns like those in District 24 are not left dealing with the impacts of policies made in Hartford without proper support.
Melissa believes public safety should never be a partisan issue—it’s about protecting families, supporting communities, and making sure Connecticut remains a place where people feel safe to live, work, and raise a family.
