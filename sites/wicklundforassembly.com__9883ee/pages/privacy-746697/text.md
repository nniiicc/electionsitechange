This Privacy Policy explains how Wicklund for Assembly (the “campaign,” “we,” or “us”) collects and uses information you provide through our website at wicklundforassembly.com.
Information We Collect
When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include your name, email address, telephone number, ZIP code, and the contents of your message.
We use this information to respond to your inquiry, coordinate volunteer and campaign activities, and keep you informed about the campaign.
Text Messaging
If you provide your telephone number and check the consent box on our contact form, you agree to receive calls and text messages from the campaign, which may include requests for donations.
Message and data rates may apply, and message frequency may vary.
You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for assistance.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
How We Share Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with campaign staff, volunteers, and service providers who help us operate the campaign, and we may disclose information when required by law or to comply with campaign finance reporting obligations.
Your Choices
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
To request that we update or remove your information, contact us using the details below.
