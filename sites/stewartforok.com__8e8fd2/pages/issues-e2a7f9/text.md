Donate for Change Voting Rights Voting is the bedrock of our democracy and the right upon which all of our civil liberties rest.
We must protect and expand citizens' rights to vote in our elections.
Real change is accomplished by shining light on our diverse community’s many needs and bringing people together to take an active role in bringing that change to bear. ​ Ronald is passionate about expanding voter access in Oklahoma and making sure Tulsans have a say in who represents them at the Capitol.
Economic Development In this competitive economic environment, we need a representative who understands the needs of our working families.
We must fight to bring sustainable well-paying jobs to pair with our strong workforce.
Our small businesses are vital to our local economy, culture, and way of life.
So many Oklahomans work hard to provide quality goods and services for our community right here in District 73.
We need to protect our local family-owned businesses and startups from creeping nationalization.
If we’re going to build a brighter future for Oklahoma, we’ll need our small businesses at the table and thriving.
Ronald will always stand up for our local businesses and their workers.
Education Public education is the backbone of any strong community.
Oklahoma’s per-student spending is one of the lowest in the nation.
We need to change that.
Instead of taking vital funds away from public education, we need to ensure that our educators and their classrooms are funded sufficiently.
When our educators and public schools suffer, our children and our state suffer.
As your representative, Ronald will always support our schools.
Healthcare All Oklahomans deserve access to affordable and quality healthcare.
Over 500,000 Oklahomans still do not have health coverage.
Although we passed Medicaid expansion in 2020, there are still threats to reduce and restrict access to health care.
Currently, we rank among the lowest in the nation for uninsured adults and children.
In order to have a bright future, we need healthy Oklahomans who aren't forced to choose between going to the doctor and putting food on the table.
Ronald will work diligently to make sure we can get the medical treatment we need.
Public Safety All Oklahomans deserve protection and unwavering support, any time, anywhere, and our state’s rising crime rate is anything but reflective of the deserving and hardworking Oklahomans that live here.
Care and support should not only be given to affected citizens, but to the services that aid Oklahomans in times of need, and funding for critical services like law enforcement, emergency response, and public health should reflect this.
As an experienced firefighter, Ronald knows exactly how to coordinate efforts to ensure the most robust and efficient public safety services for all Oklahomans.
With 20 years of relevant experience, Ronald is the best person to get this job done.
