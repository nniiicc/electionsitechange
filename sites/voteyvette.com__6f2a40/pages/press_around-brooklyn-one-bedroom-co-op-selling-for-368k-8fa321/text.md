Yvette Clarke (D-Brownsville-Crown Heights-East Flatbush-Flatbush-Kensington) recently worked to bring two new COVID-19 testing sites to Brooklyn in partnership with AdvantageCare Physicians.
The newest testing sites will be located at 546 Eastern Parkway in Crown Heights and 1000 Church Ave. in Flatbush, within some of the hardest-hit neighborhoods in Brooklyn.
