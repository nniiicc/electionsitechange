Why I'm running for Re-Election
Aloha,
Thank you for giving me the honor of representing you as your State Representative.
I'm running for re-election to continue our efforts to better our community and our state.
As your Representative, we worked hard to build a stronger community in house district 24 with the Waikiki Citizen's Patrol, the neighborhood boards, community clean-ups, and multiple town halls and community events.
Bringing our community together has been a priority of mine as we continue to face a polarizing political environment and the divide continues to worsen.
That is why our motto continues to be: People Over Politics.
As your State Representative, I've authored and passed a number of bills that became law: noise detection cameras, youth homeless shelters, assisted community treatment, healthcare protections, and co-sponsored bills to protect condo owners, kupuna, and our most vulnerable people.
I've also fought for funding for our neighborhood schools, the Ala Wai Boat Harbor, Ala Wai Canal debris management, and projects to restore our beaches.
I could not have done all this alone, these accomplishments are the results of working with community stakeholders from the neighborhood boards, the Waikiki Community Center, Waikiki Community Police, the Waikiki Special Improvement District, and the Waikiki Business Improvement District.
If re-elected, I will continue to work with you, our neighbors and stakeholders to make our neighborhood stronger and more resilient.
I hope I can earn your trust and support.
Mahalo,
Adrian
