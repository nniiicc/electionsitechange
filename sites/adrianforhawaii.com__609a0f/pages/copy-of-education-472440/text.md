DONATE OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT WEBSITE < Go Back Education As a proud graduate of our public schools, I believe that our teachers and students deserve the best that we can offer.
When re-elected, I will continue to support: - Higher pay for teachers - Support for financial literacy for schools - Capital Improvement Projects for school infrastructure - Protect funds for education - Support and expand Science Technology Engineering Math (STEM) In 2026, the legislature passed automatic salary step increases for our teachers to improve teacher morale and retention rates.
I'm also a major of supporter pre-k education, and in 2024, Ala Wai Elementary School became the first school in our neighborhood to be part of the Ready Keiki program.
One of my favorite parts of being a legislator is class visits, and being able to talk to kids about the State government and to hear from them.
Our kids are our future and as a public school graduate I know the value of our education system.
