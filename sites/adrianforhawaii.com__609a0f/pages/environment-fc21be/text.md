DONATE OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT WEBSITE < Go Back Environment When it comes to the environment, I believe that we need to do everything we can to preserve and protect our natural resources and beauty.
These are some of the policy and principles that I support: - Ensuring that we meet our renewable energy goals, while taking steps to ease the cost of living in the transition - Capital improvement projects for shoreline restoration like Waikiki Beach to fight erosion - Disaster mitigation - A Green Fee Special Fund to ensure that those monies actually go towards environmental projects - Mitigation of noise pollution Protecting our environment is essential to our health and our economy.
Clean air, clean water, less noise, and sustainability is essential to our quality of life.
