Our Mission
Justin is committed to a Georgia where all residents prosper.
His platform focuses on fostering inclusivity, securing financial stability for families, and expanding access to quality healthcare for our citizens.
Crucially, he will champion our agricultural heritage by fighting for the protections and resources our local farmers need to thrive.
Justin is a principled advocate ready to defend the dignity of every worker.
Campaign Pillars
Economic Security
Building a Georgia where every family can thrive through living wages, fair tax policies, and direct investments in our local communities.
Health Equity
Ensuring quality, affordable healthcare services for every Georgian, focusing on expanding rural access and reducing prescription costs.
Workers Rights
Protecting the backbone of our economy with strong labor standards, collective bargaining rights, and workplace safety protections.
Inclusive Democracy
Championing equity and voting rights to ensure every Georgian's voice is heard, respected, and represented in the State Senate.
