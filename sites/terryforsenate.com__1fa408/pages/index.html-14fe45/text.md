Resources & Energy Serving the people of Rutland County Why I Care About Vermont's Future I love Vermont.
I was born here.
I grew up here.
I raised my family here.
My grandchildren call Vermont home.
I have worn the uniform of my country, served my community, and had the privilege of serving the people of Rutland County in the Vermont Senate.
But I do not believe public service is about holding office.
It is about leaving something better behind for those who come after us.
I want young families to build and buy homes here.
I want our children to find good jobs here.
I want seniors to afford to retire here.
I want Vermont's farms, forests, lakes, streams, and wildlife to remain the envy of the nation.
And I want government to work for the people — instead of the people working for government.
“Most of all, I want Vermont to have a vision.
Not a Republican vision.
Not a Democratic vision.
A Vermont vision — created by Vermonters.” That future will not be created by one legislator, one governor, or one election.
It will be created by Vermonters pulling together in the same harness toward a common goal.
