A PROMISE.
A trip to the grocery store costs more than it used to.
So does filling a prescription.
I know the middle class needs help.
Donald Trump gave billions in tax cuts to the very wealthy.
That's why I'm taking on Trump, fighting for middle-class tax cuts, investing in our communities, ending the tariffs that raise your prices, and opposing reckless wars that threaten our security and our economy.
I'm proud to work for the people of Western and Central Massachusetts.
As your Congressman, I've fought tirelessly for hard-working families and future generations by protecting Social Security and Medicare, lowering costs, standing up to Donald Trump's abuse of power, and making sure our region gets its fair share.
My promise to you remains the same: I will always have your back.
It is an honor to serve as your Congressman, and I would be grateful for the opportunity to earn your vote because I'm not done fighting.
