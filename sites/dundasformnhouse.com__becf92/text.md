We need Christoph Dundas for House District 23B!
Meet Christoph Dundas Christoph grew up in Wells, MN, where he learned the value of rural life and the strength that comes from working together.
As a career educator, he has worked to empower every student to find their voice and make a difference in the world.
Christoph's work as a teacher earned him the 2025 Austin Teacher of the Year award, and he was named as a finalist for 2025 Minnesota Teacher of the Year .
With a lifelong background in public service and a clear vision for our region, he is running for MN House to ensure that every voice in 23B is heard.
He believes in a government that works for the people, focusing on the real-world issues that matter most to our families and neighbors.
