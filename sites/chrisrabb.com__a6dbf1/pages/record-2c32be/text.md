Legislative Record Rep.
Rabb has introduced more than 100 bills and resolutions focused on equity, sustainability, and justice for working people – among them a bill to repeal Pennsylvania's death penalty, the Fair Share Tax Plan to create a fairer state tax system, and a fair funding formula for public schools.
He authored a bill to establish a statewide police misconduct database, which became law following his collaboration with Rep.
Summer Lee and others in a historic 2020 Capitol action demanding accountability in response to the murders of George Floyd, Breonna Taylor, and others.
Rep.
Rabb authored a bill to establish a first-of-its-kind First Chance Trust Fund, providing educational scholarships and grants for youth support services in communities with the highest incarceration rates, funded by a 1% tax on Department of Corrections contracts over $5 million and private donations.
He has also introduced legislation establishing microenterprise loans and specialty crop block grants.
He has championed bills that passed out of the House, including legislation to address deed fraud and the forced sale of inherited property – practices that siphon hundreds of millions in wealth from disproportionately Black and brown families – and legislation to hold electric utility companies accountable for untenable rate increases.
Rep.
Rabb brings broad expertise to his work and serves on the Agriculture & Rural Affairs, Commerce, Finance, and Judiciary Committees, chairing the Subcommittees on Local Business and Tax Modernization and Reform.
He has secured millions in state funding for community institutions across his district – including schools, churches, colleges, and museums – with the majority of that funding directed to historically underserved areas.
As a proud founding member of the PA Climate Caucus and House Progressive Caucus, a member of the LGBTQ Equality Caucus, and a board member of the National Caucus of Environmental Legislators, Rep.
Rabb has built critical coalitions throughout his ten years in Harrisburg.
He consistently elevates allies to carry legislation across the finish line, prioritizing meaningful change over personal credit.
Transformational Record A transformational record is the ideas advanced and the movements represented.
Bills serve two vital purposes: changing or creating law, and sparking critical public conversations that shift what is politically possible.
Rep.
Rabb has dedicated his career to introducing bold, necessary legislation — from ending the practice of charging children as adults and opening Pennsylvania's anti-democratic closed primary system, to fighting corporate monopolization of public news media.
From modernizing the criminal legal system to championing environmental justice and an affordable economy, Chris Rabb has consistently delivered the legislation working people need.
He has been a consistent voice for transformational policy — introducing bills in 2017 to prohibit Pennsylvania's collaboration with ICE, to enact a wealth tax, and to advance a decarbonized economy.
While his name did not appear on the final version of the police misconduct database bill, he introduced the first version and worked persistently to turn the idea into law.
Rep.
Rabb introduced legislation to move Pennsylvania to 100% renewable energy by 2050, authored the bill to end ICE collaboration in Pennsylvania in 2017, and introduced legislation to establish a living minimum wage and end the inequitable tipped minimum wage loophole.
He fought to preserve $1 billion in annual RGGI funding and has supported public transit initiatives across the state.
The ideas that movements demand matter — and Rep.
Rabb has spent his career introducing them.
Each piece of legislation builds the groundwork for future progress, and someday those bold ideas will become law as the next generation of legislators takes up the mantle.
His record reflects an unwavering commitment to advancing that work, even when the political moment isn't yet ready.
