Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez Endorses Chris Rabb in Philly PA-03 Congressional Race
AOC Endorsement of Rabb Only 3rd Congressional Candidate Backed in 2026, Highlights National Progressives Coalescing Around as Rabb Achieves Fundraising Surge, Wins Support of Ro Khanna, Pramila Jayapal, Ilhan Omar, Jamie Raskin, Congressional Progressive Caucus PAC Ahead of May 19 Primary
PHILADELPHIA – Today, State Rep.
Chris Rabb, a Congressional frontrunner in Pennsylvania’s Third District (PA-03), announced that U.S.
Representative Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (NY-14) is endorsing his campaign.
Ocasio-Cortez’s support of Rabb is only the third 2026 congressional race where she weighed in.
Most recently, she endorsed Analilia Mejia in New Jersey’s primary to fill the congressional seat vacated by New Jersey Gov.
Mikie Sherrill.
Mejia won this month.
Rabb’s support is rapidly growing in the past 10 days, winning endorsements from the Philadelphia Inquirer as well as 2028 Democratic presidential contender U.S.
Rep.
Ro Khanna — along with national progressive leaders U.S.
Reps.
Ilhan Omar (MN-05), Pramila Jayapal (WA-07), Summer Lee (PA-12), Jamie Raskin (MD-08), U.S.
Senator Chris Van Hollen (D-MD) and the Congressional Progressive Caucus PAC.
State Rep.
Chris Rabb: “Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez is the progressive standard bearer of this generation of Congress for a reason.
Her bold leadership for a multiracial working class America has resonated from Queens and the Bronx to Philly to every part of this nation.
I am beyond proud and humbled to receive the Congresswoman’s support, and look forward to standing together to fight for a better future for all of our communities and families.”
Rabb recently celebrated a fundraising surge with a blockbuster report showing $385,885 raised in Q1 of 2026, Rabb nearly doubled the fundraising totals of his opponents, amassing nearly 7 times more small-dollar donors and roughly 5 times more local donors compared to his opponents.
All while a super PAC props up his opponent, flooding the race with $2.3 million in ads to voters.
Rabb is building a fast-growing national progressive coalition, including this past week’s endorsement by the Congressional Progressive Caucus PAC (CPC PAC), along with recently announced support from , as well as Working Families Party which helped elect Zohran Mamdani, Justice Democrats which helped elect Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez, and grassroots groups like Philly DSA, OnePA, and Reclaim Philadelphia.
Rabb has also won the support of Philly progressive lawmakers as well, including State Sen.
Nikil Saval (SD-01), Rep.
Elizabeth Fiedler (HD-184), Rep.
Rick Krajewski (HD-188), and Councilmember Kendra Brooks and Councilmember Nic O’Rourke.
BACKGROUND
First elected in 2016, Rep.
Chris Rabb, is a 5-term state representative in the largest full-time state legislature in the U.S.
He proudly represents a majority Black, mixed-income district of nearly 70,000 people across 4 neighborhoods in northwest Philadelphia.
Rabb has championed bold ideas that once seemed impossible, some of which are now enshrined in law or become embraced statewide.
He has spent his career challenging corporate power, advancing social justice, and standing up for communities too often ignored by political insiders.
Now, he’s taking that fight to Capitol Hill to join other bold progressive public servants, amplify the voices of working families, and defy monied interests and establishment politics.
