Meet Zach
A Born Pennsylvanian
Zach graduated from the Bradford Area High School in McKean County and went on to study at the University of Pittsburgh at Bradford, followed by Saint Bonaventure University, earning his MBA in 2018.
Over the years, Zach's taken advantage of the opportunities that he's been presented with, but has also taken the opportunity to help others in the area by organizing clothing drives and computer donations for students in need in the local school district.
He's helped to organize more than 800 clothing items and several dozen computer donations to local students and their families.
Putting Down Roots
Bradford is home, and a few years back, he and his wife, Tayler, welcomed their daughter, Olivia, into the world.
With his daughter and so many nieces and nephews in the area, Zach is focused and passionate about working and fighting to make our shared home - District 67 - the best that it can be.
That means helping others, building up communities, and working to help our area grow and flourish.
There's So Much More to Do
Zach's running for PA State House because he knows that there's so much more work to be done.
Like many of us, he's watched decades of our main streets dying, communities, hospitals, and jobs leaving the area, and the snowballing issues that these problems cause.
He also recognizes that these issues aren't partisan.
They don't impact just Democrats or Republicans or Independents.
They impact all of us, and it's going to take all of us to fix it.
It's not going to be an easy job, but it is a worthwhile one.
There's work to be done, PA.
Let's get to it.
