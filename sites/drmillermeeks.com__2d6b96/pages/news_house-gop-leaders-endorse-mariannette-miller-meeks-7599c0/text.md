Speaker Johnson: “There is no better conservative fighter for Iowa’s First Congressional District”
DAVENPORT, IA – Today, House Republican leaders endorsed conservative Congresswoman Mariannette Miller-Meeks for re-election.
The endorsements reflect Miller-Meeks' record as a trusted conservative delivering results for Iowa's First Congressional District.
“I am honored to receive the support of House Republican leaders in my re-election as I fight for Iowa in Congress,” said Miller-Meeks.
“I look forward to continuing the fight with these conservative leaders to deliver for Iowa and defeat Joe Biden and Democrat extremists in November.”
See below for endorsement statements from Speaker Mike Johnson, Majority Leader Steve Scalise, Majority Whip Tom Emmer, Chief Deputy Whip Guy Reschenthaler, and Conference Chair Elise Stefanik.
Speaker of the House Mike Johnson (R-LA)
“There is no better conservative fighter for Iowa’s First Congressional District than Congresswoman Mariannette Miller-Meeks.
I am proud to endorse and serve beside Mariannette to deliver for the American people and help claw back disastrous policies from the Biden administration.”
House Majority Leader Steve Scalise (R-LA)
“I am proud to endorse my friend, Mariannette Miller-Meeks, in her re-election bid because America needs her in the fight for freedom against Democrat Socialists who want to destroy our country.”
House Majority Whip Tom Emmer (R-MN)
“Congresswoman Mariannette Miller-Meeks is a conservative leader here in Washington and a fierce advocate for Iowa’s First Congressional District.
Reelecting Congresswoman Miller-Meeks is crucial to House Republicans keeping our majority and delivering the results America desperately needs.
I am proud to endorse Mariannette and will do whatever it takes to help her win!”
Chief Deputy Whip Guy Reschenthaler (R-PA)
“Mariannette Miller-Meeks’ military service and career as a trusted medical professional provide a unique and necessary perspective to both the House Energy & Commerce Committee and the Republican conference as a whole.
“Mariannette’s work in Congress is pivotal for our efforts to reduce healthcare costs and ensure that veterans receive the care they deserve.
She is a staunch advocate for law and order, committed to securing our borders, reforming our healthcare system, and tirelessly championing the cause of farmers and ranchers in the Heartland of America.
Mariannette is an invaluable asset to Iowa and our country.
“I am proud to endorse her campaign and am dedicated to continuing our fight alongside her for the American people.”
House Republican Conference Chair Elise Stefanik (R-NY)
“Mariannette Miller-Meeks is a committed conservative delivering results.
She is a fierce fighter for Iowa in Congress to lower prescription drug prices and enhance the Hawkeye State’s energy leadership.
Democrats are threatened by Miller-Meeks’ distinguished background as a doctor and Army veteran.
I’m proud to endorse Dr.
Miller-Meeks for re-election as we need her in Congress to fight the radical policies of the Far Left.”
