Information Collected Voluntary Information: We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide including your name, email address, postal address, phone number, mobile number and geographic location.

In limited circumstances, we may collect payment information where needed to complete a transaction.

Automatically Generated Information: We may collect non-personally identifiable information generated automatically while you visit the Site, including IP address, web pages visited, date and time, device type, and interactions with our content.

Google Analytics collects information anonymously and will not associate your IP address with any other data held by Google.
