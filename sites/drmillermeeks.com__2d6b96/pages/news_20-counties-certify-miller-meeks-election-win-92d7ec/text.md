Davenport – Today, following Iowa's county canvass, all 20 counties in Iowa's First Congressional District certified their ballots confirming Congresswoman Mariannette Miller-Meeks' re-election.
The ballots now head to the Iowa Secretary of State.
“Today’s certification by all 20 countries in IA-01 further confirms what we already knew: Mariannette Miller-Meeks was re-elected to Congress for a third term.
The congresswoman’s lead and win are a mathematical certainty.
She will continue her work for all of Iowa and immediately get back to work to lower prices and inflation, secure the border, and fight to protect our farmers.”
