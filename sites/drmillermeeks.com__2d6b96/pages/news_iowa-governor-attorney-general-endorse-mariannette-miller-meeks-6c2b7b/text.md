“I was elected along with these two extraordinary Iowa conservatives in 2022, and I look forward to continuing to deliver results for Iowa and defeat Joe Biden and the radical Democrats in November.” Iowa Governor Reynolds and Iowa Attorney General Bird have a conservative track record to help and elevate all Iowans.
Now they are again supporting Congresswoman Miller-Meeks for re-election in Iowa's First Congressional District.
