Meet Bryan
Bryan Mouber is a lawyer, husband, and father of two, raising his family in District 28.
He's running for the Kansas House because the things that make this the place to raise kids didn't happen by accident, and they won't hold without someone willing to fight for them.
Kansas House District 28
District 28 stretches from Metcalf Avenue to State Line Road and from 123rd Street to 151st Street, containing parts of Leawood and Overland Park.
