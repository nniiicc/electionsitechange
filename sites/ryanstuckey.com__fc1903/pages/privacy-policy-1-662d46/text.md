Last Updated: September 29, 2026 Friends of Ryan Stuckey ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our campaign.
Information We Collect We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out a form, sign up to volunteer, request information, or communicate with us.
This information may include your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to communicate with you about our campaign, send updates, coordinate volunteer efforts, and respond to your inquiries.
SMS / Text Messaging Data and Consent If you provide your phone number, you consent to receive text messages from the Committee to Elect Ryan Stuckey.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency varies.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
For assistance, reply HELP.
Strictly Confidential: Mobile information and text message consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will be kept strictly confidential and will not be sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
Sharing of Other Information Excluding the mobile and text messaging data rules explicitly stated above, we may share other general information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting campaign activities, provided they agree to keep this information confidential.
We may also release information when required to comply with the law or protect our rights.
