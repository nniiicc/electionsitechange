About John Lowery for NC House
John Lowery is a lifelong Robeson County resident, an educator, and current Chairman of the Lumbee Tribe.
A graduate of UNC Pembroke and UNC-Chapel Hill, John has dedicated his career to public service — working to expand rural opportunity, strengthen education, and grow local jobs.
Now serving as our Representative in the North Carolina House, he’s committed to ensuring Robeson County’s values are represented in Raleigh through strong leadership, accountability, and conservative principles.
