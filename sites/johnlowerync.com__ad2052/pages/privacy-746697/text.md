PRIVACY POLICY & TERMS PRIVACY POLICY: Friends of John Lowery (“we,” “us”) is the candidate committee for John Lowery, candidate for the North Carolina House of Representatives, District 47.
Our address is 326 Sandlin Drive, Lumberton, NC 28358.
Information we collect.
We collect the information you give us, such as your name, email address, mobile phone number, and ZIP code, when you sign up on our website, contribute, or volunteer.
When you make a contribution, we also collect the information North Carolina campaign finance law requires.
How we use it.
We use your information to send campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer opportunities, voting information, and requests for contributions by email, phone, and text message.
We also use it to meet our legal reporting requirements.
Text messaging.
We will not share mobile information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
We will not distribute or sell your text messaging opt-in data, related personal information, or consent to any third party, except when legally required to do so.
Contributions.
North Carolina law requires us to report certain contributor information to the State Board of Elections, and those reports are public.
Opting out.
Reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts.
Click “unsubscribe” in any email to stop receiving emails.
We will not share text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, provided that the foregoing does not apply to sharing (1) with vendors, consultants and other service providers who need access to such information to carry out work on our behalf (and who will not use such information for their own purposes); (2) if we believe disclosure is required by any applicable law, rule, or regulation or to comply with law enforcement or legal process; and (3) if the user consents to our sharing of such information.
Contact.
Friends of John Lowery, 326 Sandlin Drive, Lumberton, NC 28358.

About John Lowery for NC House John Lowery is a lifelong Robeson County resident, an educator, and current Chairman of the Lumbee Tribe.
A graduate of UNC Pembroke and UNC-Chapel Hill, John has dedicated his career to public service — working to expand rural opportunity, strengthen education, and grow local jobs.
Now serving as our Representative in the North Carolina House, he’s committed to ensuring Robeson County’s values are represented in Raleigh through strong leadership, accountability, and conservative principles.
