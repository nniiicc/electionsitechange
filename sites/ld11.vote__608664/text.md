Josh Ayala is running for the Arizona State Senate to make sure Laveen, South Phoenix, and Guadalupe receive the same attention, services, and respect as every other community in Arizona.
Rooted in the district Josh grew up in the communities he hopes to represent.
As a mixed-race Arizonan raised in Laveen and South Phoenix, he understands how division can affect families, schools, neighborhoods, and opportunity.
A practical business background For more than two decades, Josh has worked in financial services, including 10 years owning a financial services company.
That experience shaped his focus on responsible decisions, family stability, and community opportunity.
Why safety matters Josh believes LD11 families deserve responsive public safety.
His own family experiences in South Phoenix and Ahwatukee made clear that every neighborhood should receive timely, serious service when residents call for help.
