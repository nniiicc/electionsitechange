Hi, I'm Bram Kleppner
and I am running for re-election to represent the South End of Burlington in the VT House.
Tiff Bluemle and I represented the Chittenden-13 district, which is roughly the part of Burlington south of Maple and Cliff Streets -- most of Wards 5 and 6.
We heard loud and clear that affordability (including affordable housing), property taxes, public safety (including addiction, mental illness, homelessness, and crime), climate change, and health care costs are most important to you, and we're working in the Statehouse to address these challenges.
I pledge to continue fighting for these problems if re-elected.
Please visit 'The Issues' for a look at our progress over the last two years and my goals for the term ahead.
