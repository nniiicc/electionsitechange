Priority Issues
We have a lot of work to do
Increase Affordable Housing
Affordable housing keeps communities strong by allowing workers, families, seniors, and young people to live where they work, reducing instability, supporting local businesses, and ensuring long-term economic and social vitality.
Ensure Accessible Healthcare
Accessible healthcare in Wisconsin, particularly rural areas, keeps families healthy, supports working people and seniors, reduces costly emergency care, strengthens the local economy, and ensures everyone can receive quality care close to home when they need it.
Fund Our Public Schools
Kevin knows that adequately funding our public schools strengthens our community, supports students with diverse needs, protects property values, and ensures every child has the opportunity to succeed and contribute to our shared future.
Promote Economic Growth
Kevin is fiscally conservative and socially liberal, opposing shifting tax burdens to working families or the middle class, supporting small businesses and their ability to drive local economies, and advocating for transparent, efficient government spending.
Supporting Our Veterans
Supporting disabled veterans honors service, fulfills moral obligations, reduces long-term healthcare and homelessness costs, strengthens workforce participation, and demonstrates Wisconsin’s commitment to those who sacrificed for public safety and freedom.
Protect Our Natural Resources
Wisconsin's natural resources are vital, and being good stewards of these God-given treasures ensures clean water, healthy land and wildlife, supports outdoor economies, and ensures future generations will enjoy them as much as we do today.
