VOLUNTEER About Kevin Knoke Husband, father, veteran, outdoorsman, and educator A resident of Hudson, WI, I grew up working summers on my uncle’s Iowa dairy farm, where I learned the value of hard work and service.
That sense of duty led me to the U.S.
Army, then to teaching, and now public service in the Wisconsin Assembly.
I'm married to my wife of 30 years, Jill, and am the proud father of two sons.
