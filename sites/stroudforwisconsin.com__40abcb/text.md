Stroud for Wisconsin
It has been an absolute honor to serve as your State Representative in the 73rd Assembly District.
During my first session, I did my best to learn as much as I could, communicated regularly, and worked as hard as possible to make state government work more effectively for the people in the Northland.
Despite being in the minority, I was able to accomplish a great deal this year.
I helped to secure free lead line replacement for homeowners in Superior; authored legislation to rein in data centers; and helped to pass healthcare expansion for new moms.
But there is so much more work to do to improve the lives of the people that I represent.
That’s why I’m running for a second term.
I hope that I can earn your support.
