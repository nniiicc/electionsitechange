Adam Baker stands strong On
Demanding a Lower Cost of Living
Hardworking families have tightened their belts for years.
It’s time Salem did the same.
- Respect the voters on taxes — This May, Oregonians voted overwhelmingly to reject Salem’s gas tax, payroll tax, and vehicle fee increases.
I’ll respect that vote: no new taxes without proving efficiency and results first.
- Fight runaway utility bills — Residential electric rates have jumped more than 50% in five years — six straight years of increases.
I’ll push for real ratepayer protection and stop shifting big users’ costs onto families and seniors.
- Keep tolls off I-205 — Clackamas County spoke loudly against tolling our daily commute, and I’ll make sure Salem never quietly brings it back.
- Demand accountability for every dollar — Audit before you ask.
Every agency should show measurable results before asking taxpayers for another dime.
