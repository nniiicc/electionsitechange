Here’s the Plan
HERE'S THE PLAN:
In Arizona, I’ve watched the same systemic problems: health crises, water shortages, crime, and broken immigration go unaddressed for too long.
I’m ready to bring real-world experience and innovative thinking to a system that desperately needs both.
Her campaign focuses on seven pillars of renewal:
- Strong Economy and American Dreams — Arizona isn’t just growing, it’s driving the next chapter of America’s economy.
From semiconductor production to aerospace, defense innovation, and logistics, we’re at the center of it all.
Leadership means staying bold and forward-focused.
In Congress, I’ll push to cut unnecessary regulations, strengthen pro-growth tax policies, expand American manufacturing, and tackle inflation head-on.
Our economy should work for the people who show up every day, not just large institutions or bureaucratic systems.
When we support working Americans, we create strong, stable jobs across the Valley and put our country on a more responsible fiscal path at the same time.
- Public Health & Wellness — Advancing “Make Arizona Healthy Again” initiative to strengthen access to preventive first healthcare and fix the bankrupt system.
The U.S. healthcare system spends more per capita than any other developed nation, yet struggles with chronic disease, access disparities, and rising costs.
Restructure discussions need to focus on shifting from reactive disease treatment to proactive health optimization.
We need to provide safety and shelters to protect vulnerable women and children through safe houses with enhanced security and survivor-led mentorship programs.
- Transparency in Government — Trust in government isn’t something we can talk about, it’s something we have to earn back through action.
Washington has operated behind closed doors for far too long, and it’s time to return power where it belongs: with the people.
I believe those elected to serve shouldn’t be using their position for personal financial gain.
That’s why I support clear rules that prevent members of Congress from trading individual stocks so decisions are made in the public’s best interest, not for private profit.
Transparency also means showing up and doing the job responsibly.
When critical funding decisions put Arizona families at risk, like the threat of missed paychecks or economic instability, I will always push for practical, bipartisan solutions that keep our government functioning and protect the people who depend on it.
Accountability.
Integrity.
Results.
That’s how we restore faith in leadership and build a government that truly serves its people.
- Education & Future Generations — Expand federal support for vocational education grants, promote skilled trades partnerships and modernize high school career-track programs.
We can’t fix education with a single policy or quick solution, we’ll fix it by realigning the entire system.
That means creating the right incentives so schools are rewarded for outcomes, not just compliance.
It means empowering parents with choice and giving teachers the support and respect they need to actually lead in the classroom.
And most importantly, it means reconnecting education to the real economy, so students graduate with skills, opportunities, and a clear path to a successful future.
- Immigration Revision — Building structured and secure pathways that respect both law and life with secure and orderly border management using policy that focuses on controlling entry while maintaining lawful trade and travel.
Securing our border, reversing failed open-border policies, and focusing on real, enforceable solutions.
That means prioritizing the removal of dangerous criminal offenders, fully supporting our law enforcement, and taking action to stop fentanyl before it reaches our communities.
Arizona families deserve to feel safe, and the men and women who protect us deserve unwavering support.
I’m committed to delivering both.
- Opioid Crisis Response & Unhomed — Strengthening prevention, rehabilitation, and mental health access with community outreach partnerships, extend federal grants for recovery centers and incentivizing sober house living.
District 04 keeps chasing the same problem instead of solving it—clearing streets without a real plan, only to see tents return within days or pop-up locations with no longterm stability.
It’s costly, ineffective, and ultimately inhumane.
The solution is a structured, results-driven approach: first, offer immediate alternatives through bridge housing like leased motels or apartments, along with options for family reunification.
No one is left without a path forward.
Then, restore the area by cleaning, repairing, and making it safe again.
Finally, maintain it with ongoing oversight and community-focused improvements to ensure the problem doesn’t return.
This isn’t about temporary fixes, it’s about lasting solutions.
- Law Enforcement & Fire Support — Ensuring those who protect us have the resources and respect they deserve.
Public safety systems depend on trained personnel, modern equipment, sustainable funding, mental health support, and strong community relationships.
Effective policy balances operational readiness with transparency and oversight.
- Data Centers & Responsible Growth — Arizona can lead in AI and technology without sacrificing our water, health, safety, or quality of life.
Data centers must be held to clear standards for water and energy use, noise, backup-generator emissions, and responsible placement away from homes and schools.
These facilities can require significant electricity and, depending on their cooling systems, substantial water resources, which deserves careful scrutiny in a desert state where every gallon matters.
I support greater transparency and accountability so communities know the true impact before projects are approved.
Innovation should strengthen Arizona, not drain the resources our families depend on.
America’s strength and prosperity are powered by energy and we have everything we need right here at home.
It’s time to fully develop our resources, from oil and natural gas to emerging energy innovations, and stop limiting our own potential.
True energy independence means lowering costs for families, creating high-paying jobs for Arizona workers, and strengthening our national security.
It means we’re no longer relying on unstable foreign sources or policies that drive prices up.
In Congress, I’ll work to remove unnecessary barriers, support responsible and renewable energy development, and invest in the infrastructure needed to keep America strong and self-reliant.
