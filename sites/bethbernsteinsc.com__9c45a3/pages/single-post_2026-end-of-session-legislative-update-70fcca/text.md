2026 END OF SESSION LEGISLATIVE UPDATE Jun 30 1 min read Dear Friends and Neighbors, My End-of-Session Legislative Update went out in the mail last week to households across House District 78, and I wanted to make sure it reached your inbox, too.
Every session, I come back to one question: Will this make life better for the families in our community?
This year, that meant protecting our children as they grow up online, helping renters and homeowners hold onto a stable place to live, standing up for public safety, and working to deliver real tax relief to working families.
You can read the full update here: https://acrobat.adobe.com/id/urn:aaid:sc:us:ad63b95f-62b9-4eb7-95ae-7cd9f42cc457 Also, please remember that with the election in November, it is a good time to confirm your registration at scvotes.gov and make your plan to vote.
As always, my door is open.
If something in this update matters to you or if I can ever be of help, please reach out.
Thank you for the privilege of representing you and our community at the Statehouse!.
Warmly, Rep.
