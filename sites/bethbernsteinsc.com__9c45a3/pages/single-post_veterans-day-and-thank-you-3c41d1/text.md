VETERANS DAY AND THANK YOU!
Nov 11, 2022 1 min read Dear Friends and Neighbors, Veterans Day is when we celebrate all those who selflessly serve our great country as members of the US Armed Forces.
Today, we stand united to honor their unwavering service in peacetime and war, here in this nation and throughout the world.
God bless them and their families!
As election results are being certified, I am humbled and honored to have the opportunity to represent my community (HD 78) for two more years in the SC House of Representatives.
I could not have accomplished this without the incredible support, guidance, and love from my family, friends, neighbors, and supporters like you!
I will continue fight for the issues most important to our community and state.
Thank you!
God bless America!
