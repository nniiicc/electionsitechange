Happy Thanksgiving!
Nov 26, 2020 1 min read Dear Friends and Neighbors, Thanksgiving is one of my most favorite holidays to celebrate.
It allows us the opportunity to spend time with extended family and reflect on things for which we are grateful.
Typically, I host a feast for around 25-30 family members.
Although due to COVID-19, we made the difficult decision to cancel it this year and have limited our meal to immediate family.
Nevertheless, the spirit of gratitude is still with us.
I am thankful for so many things.
Most importantly, I am grateful for my supportive friends and family, and as your elected state representative, I am thankful for the trust and confidence you have placed in me to represent you and our community at the State House.
No matter how you spend Thanksgiving today, I hope you are able to think about the things that have made you thankful.
From my family to yours, we hope you have a joyous and meaningful Thanksgiving!
