Self Scheduled Door Knocking Contact DONATE Workers Made America In a day and age where the working class needs support now more than ever, I’m here for you.
The DFL came out of a merger between the Democratic Party and the at the time dominant Farmer-Labor Party.
The Farmer-Labor Party was made by Farmers and laborers for the working class. and in the depths of the depression, saved the state from the ravages of the Great Depression.
I’m here to put the FL in DFL.
What Will We Do?
We will work to make sure that yearly raises that meet or exceed inflation are standard in every industry, not just the corporate world.
We will support unions and strengthen labor protections We will promote the establishment of cooperatives like Land O Lakes, Health Partners, and credit unions, as even when they are not worker Co-ops, they pay their employees better.
We will work to support and attract high-paying industries to the state.
We will create and support programs to help workers in declining and even dying industries find new careers with equal or higher pay.
We will support our schools and teachers to make sure that in this post-2020 world, our students have the tools they need to succeed in the workforce.
And We will continuously listen to workers.
Helping us find the solutions to bring back the middle class.
