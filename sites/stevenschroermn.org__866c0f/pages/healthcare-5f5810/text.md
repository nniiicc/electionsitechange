Self Scheduled Door Knocking Contact DONATE Healthcare Must Be Affordable For All Why Is Healthcare So Expensive Healthcare is so expensive in the US because we aren’t just paying for the services.
We are paying for the for-profit companies’ huge marketing budgets and the CEO’s Yach.
And with so many insurers, none of them have the weight to negotiate prices down as they do in Europe.
Is Universal Healthcare The Solution?
Yes!
Every other country in the wealthy world has this type of system.
Americans pay more for our health care and get worse outcomes than Europeans and Canadians.
Will Universal Healthcare Cost More?
No!
A single-payer system would save Americans money.
According to the National Institutes of Health , American households could save, on average, $2,369 per year.
How Do We Get There?
We can start here.
Minnesota can start our own single-payer system or even start with a public option.
It doesn’t have to be done nationally.
In Canada, it was the provinces, not the federal government, that created their Universal health care system.
