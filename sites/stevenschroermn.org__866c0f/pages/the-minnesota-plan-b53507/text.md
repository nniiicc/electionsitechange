Talking about the problems facing our democracy and some of the solutions.
Proportional Representation Through a combination of three different voting methods (RCV, STV, & Direct party vote) that have been tried and tested around the world and even here in the US for decades, we will make it so that every vote counts and you can vote for whoever you want without being punished by the system for making the “Wrong Choice”.
Parties will get the percentage of control they earn from the percentage of votes they receive.
No More Gerymandering.
No More Politicians Picking Their Voters .
If you are a republican in Minneapolis or a DFL’er in Greater Minnesota or a third-party voter, you will get representation for the first time in decades.
No More Big Money In Politics By regulating and, in some cases, eliminating corporate political spending and expanding and modernizing Minnesota's public campaign funding system, we will take our government back from the ultra-wealthy elite.
Mandatory Retirement Ages For Politicians Let’s leave till death do us part to our spouses, not our politicians.
