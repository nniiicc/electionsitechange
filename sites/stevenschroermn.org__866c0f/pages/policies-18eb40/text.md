Let’s Bring Back Some Hope For Our Future
And Here’s How
- Affordable Housing - End Homelessness Minnesota is over 100,000 housing units short.
The housing crisis needs to be addressed.
We are seeing soaring Rents, Home Prices, and Property Taxes.
Pricing out more and more Minnesotans of what should be the safest thing, Home.
- The Minnesota Plan - Fair Elections A plan for reform to strengthen and restore our democracy.
Ending the political games that have taken power away from the people, leading to gridlock, and a tragic increase in political violence.
Let's put the people back in the driver's seat.
- Supporting Workers - Better Pay Wages have been stagnant for decades.
We have seen the difference between the value workers provide their employers/community and their wages widen into a chasm.
Shrinking the middle class to the smallest it has been since the Great Depression.
- Affordable Healthcare Healthcare costs are out of control.
More and more of our healthcare money is going to profit margins and less and less to the health of Minnesotans.
We need single-payer healthcare like every other wealthy country.
