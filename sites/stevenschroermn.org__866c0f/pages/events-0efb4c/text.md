DFL SD57 Unit Convention
The DFL unit convention is where the party endorses its candidates ahead of the primary.
Delegates that are elected at their precinct caucuses will be sent to the convention to endorse candidates and resolutions, as well as elect delegates to both the congressional and state conventions.
