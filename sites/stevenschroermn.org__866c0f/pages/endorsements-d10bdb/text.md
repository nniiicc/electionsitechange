ENDORSEMENTS
DFL Endorsed!
Steven was endorsed at the SD57 DFL Convention to be the Democratic-Farmer-Labor Party Candidate for Senate District 57.
People
- Matt Little - DFL Candidate For The 2nd Congressional District & Former Lakeville Mayor “Steven is exactly the kind of person we need in the Minnesota State Senate.
He grew up in a working family that got hit hard by the 2008 recession, but, with Steven's help, they stayed tough and made it.
That's the kind of grit and determination he will bring to St Paul; fighting to get money and corruption out of politics, fighting for more housing, healthcare, voter reforms, lower prices, and higher wages.
That's an agenda everyone will support, and Steven will get it done.”
- Erin Preese - 2022 House District 57B DFL Candidate “Steven Schroer is running for the State Senate to make life better for all Minnesotans.
He is committed to expanding access to affordable healthcare and supporting living wages so working families can thrive—not just scrape by.
Steven also supports common sense gun safety measures that help keep our communities safe.
As a graduate of Lakeville Public Schools, he understands firsthand the value of a strong public education and will work to ensure every child has access to the same opportunities that helped him succeed right here in Lakeville.”
