Donate Here My name is Jocelyn and I am a candidate for the WV House of Delegates in District 69.
I have spent my career working in social services.
My career started in children and youth crisis stabilization.
I went on to work within the Title 19 IDD/DD Waiver doing service coordination and behavioral support.
Later, I found a calling to work with children and families in crisis who were judiciously involved .
Within that roll I provided treatment coordination, program support and facilitation, and advocated for the families in the school, community, and courts.
In doing this work I was provided first hand education in learning what impacts families, the barriers they face, and creativity in addressing those barriers.
I currently work with individuals who struggle with mental health challenges and substance use.
Having a background in various avenues of behavioral health has opened my eyes to the needs of the average WV family.
I have a passion to help build better support systems and better communities for a better WV.
