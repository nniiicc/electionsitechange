Log In About Venicia Venicia Considine has called Las Vegas home for 30 years.
As an attorney with Legal Aid Center of Southern Nevada, Venicia has defended individuals and families against unlawful foreclosures, predatory lenders, and fraud and has seen first hand the work we can do to provide economic stability and security for hardworking Nevada families.
Venicia was the first person in her family to graduate from college, earning her Bachelor’s and a law degree from UNLV.
Venicia and her husband, Kenn, live in Las Vegas with their dog, Nina.
