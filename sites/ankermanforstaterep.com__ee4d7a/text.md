Chantelle is a mom, attorney, and longtime Windsor resident running to represent Windsor and Windsor Locks in the Connecticut House of Representatives.
A lifelong Connecticut resident, Chantelle has called Windsor home for the past decade.
She earned her undergrad and law degrees from UConn, making her a proud “Double Husky.” Before her legal career, she worked for West Hartford Public Schools for over eight years.
Chantelle is committed to putting residents first and working to preserve the quality of life that makes our communities such special places to live, work, and raise a family.
Chantelle and her family have proudly called Windsor home for the past decade.
As both a parent raising children and someone whose older family members live in the community, she understands the challenges facing residents at every stage of life — from the rising costs confronting young families to the affordability, healthcare, tax, and independence concerns many seniors face.
