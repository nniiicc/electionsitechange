Make a Donation, Get Money Back — the PCR Program
When you donate to Liz’s campaign, you can be reimbursed through the Political Contribution Refund (PCR) program.
Minnesota will refund you up to $75 (or $150 per married couple) each year when you donate to an eligible campaign of your choice—like ours!
Upon donation, we will send you a special receipt you will need to submit, along with a form found here, for your refund.
