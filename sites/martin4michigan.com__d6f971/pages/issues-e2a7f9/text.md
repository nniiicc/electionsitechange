keep a strong voice in lansing
ISSUES
Energy
- Dave fought against PA 233 of 2023, which allowed state agencies to overrule local townships and force solar and wind energy installations in their community without their approval.
- Dave stands against “Green New Deal” energy mandates, and believes that increased adaptation of modern, clean nuclear and natural gas power plants is key to avoid using expensive energy from other states.
- Dave supports building the Line 5 pipeline tunnel.
This vital infrastructure would keep oil, propane, aviation fuel and other products flowing via the safest possible transportation methods, while protecting our Great Lakes.
- Dave knows that the free market should drive demand for electric vehicles, not artificial government mandates and unrealistic climate goals.
Dave supports technology advances that the majority of consumers actually want, such as the $1 Billion being invested into the new Internal Combustion Engine being produced in Genesee County.
Values
- Dave stands with women and girls against efforts to force them to compete with biological males in sports.
He also stands with families against the woke transgender ideology being pushed on our students and children in our schools.
- Dave is a leader in the fight to defend our Constitutional right to keep and bear arms, and to protect our 2nd Amendment Rights.
- Dave has been a strong advocate for the unborn, is endorsed by Michigan Right to Life, and has voted for a fetal tissue research ban (HB 5558 & 5559).
He also supported a state income tax exemption for expectant mothers (HB 4644).
Education
- Dave believes parents and local school districts should be in charge of controlling their curriculum – not bureaucrats in Washington or Lansing.
He supports reduced regulations on our classrooms to allow more local control over our schools.
- Dave has voted to follow through on the promise for equal funding for our classrooms and school districts (HB 4411).
David will continue to fight for our students to receive a quality education.
- Dave believes that we must encourage skilled trades training programs that prepare workers for today’s new careers.
When serving as the vice-chair of the Workforce, Trades & Talent Committee, Dave had a bill signed into law to ensure high school students are informed of the numerous career options available right here in Michigan (HB 4953).
law enforcement & Security
- Dave supports a secure border, not only to keep violent criminals and those who are national security risks out of our country, but also to check the increased flow of illegal drugs into our state.
Dave also has sponsored House Bill 5650, which would crack down on transnational gang crimes in our communities.
- Dave serves as chair of the House Natural Resources & Tourism Committee.
In this role, he is fighting against overreach by the DNR and EGLE to protect private property owners and hunters.
- Dave voted for increased investment in training, resources, and equipment for our law enforcement officers.
He also supported funding for additional school resource officers to more effectively protect our students and schools (HB 5522).
elections
- Our vote is our voice, and ensuring our vote counts is a crucial part of having confidence in our elections.
That’s why Dave voted for strengthening ID requirements when voting in-person or by absentee ballot (SB 285).
- Dave supported legislation to prohibit the Secretary of State from sending unrequested absentee ballot applications (SB 310) and voted for legislation to require a physical signature when requesting an absentee ballot (HB 5288).
- It is a problem that non-government groups can give money to specific government entities for elections.
Dave voted for a bill that would ban the use of third-party money (“Zuckerbucks”) for conducting elections (HB 5253).
GrowTh
- Dave has voted to eliminate state property taxes in Michigan (House Bills 5872-5879).
The bills would also repeal the real estate transfer tax and eliminate Michigan’s “pop-up tax,” which often causes taxable values to spike after a home is sold.
- Dave supports the job-creating efforts to further protect Enbridge Line 5.
He has fought against the proposed pipeline shutdown (HR 250), which would have a massive economic impact on Michigan’s consumers and businesses.
Veterans
- Dave is a US Air Force veteran and the former Veteran Services Coordinator at Mott Community College, where he helped student veterans and their families take advantage of educational benefits available to them.
Through Dave’s efforts, Mott is an award-recognized veteran-friendly school.
- As state representative, Dave has served on the Military, Veterans, and Homeland Security Committee, and works to continue to ensure our service members and their families get access to benefits they have earned by their dedication to our country.
Unemployment
- During the pandemic and following, David helped hundreds of laid-off workers cut through bureaucratic red tape to get their unemployment that they were rightfully due.
- With the dramatically increased volume of unemployment cases, there was widespread fraud and general incompetence at the Unemployment Insurance Agency, which led to delays for individuals applying for benefits.
That’s why David led in calling for a leadership change to address these issues (HR 175).
roads
- Road repairs continue to be a major issue facing Genesee County.
That’s why David voted in support of increased funding for our roads and bridges (HB 4410).
- With high gas prices, David voted to suspend Michigan’s 27 cent gas tax (HB 5570), and supports solutions to move Michigan towards increased energy independence.
He remains committed to finding innovative solutions and prioritizing road funding within the existing budget.
