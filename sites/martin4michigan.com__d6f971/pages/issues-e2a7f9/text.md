Energy Dave voted to require state energy regulators to prioritize reliable, cost-effective energy and greater transparency when utility companies seek rate increases (HB 5710, FY 2026-27 budget) Dave fought against PA 233 of 2023, which allowed state agencies to overrule local townships and force solar and wind energy installations in their community without their approval.
Dave stands against “Green New Deal” energy mandates, and believes that increased adaptation of modern, clean nuclear and natural gas power plants is key to avoid using expensive energy from other states.
He also voted to roll back costly energy mandates driving up electricity costs (HB 5711) Dave supports building the Line 5 pipeline tunnel.
This vital infrastructure would keep oil, propane, aviation fuel and other products flowing via the safest possible transportation methods, while protecting our Great Lakes.
Home Ownership Dave has voted to eliminate state property taxes in Michigan (House Bills 5872-5879).
The bills would also repeal the real estate transfer tax and eliminate Michigan’s “pop-up tax,” which often causes taxable values to spike after a home is sold.
Dave voted to keep Michigan families from being priced out of the housing market by prohibiting large corporations from buying up single-family homes in bulk (HB 6074) Dave voted for a new law to increase housing options for working families by creating a new housing tax credit that will help finance the construction of approximately 2,500 new housing units each year (HBs 5806-5807; PAs 30-31 of 2026) Dave voted for a new law that provides new tools to families facing tough times to help avoid foreclosure and stay in their homes (PA 79 of 2026) Dave voted to cut red tape within the state’s permitting processes and making it easier to build homes for working families (HBs 5932-5935) healthcare Dave voted to cap interest rates on medical debt and prohibit aggressive collection practices such as wage garnishment and home foreclosures tied to medical bills (HBs 5254-5255) Dave helped to save Medicaid by making tax reforms that saved billions in federal funding for Medicaid and investing more state resources to make sure the program continues to be available for vulnerable residents who count on it (HBs 4706, 4968, PA 25 of 2025) Dave voted to increase access to reliable healthcare by entering the state into numerous healthcare licensure compacts that allow more medical professionals to practice in Michigan (HBs 4032, 4101-4104, 4246, 4309, 4380, 4509, 4591, 5455; PA 6 of 2026) Veterans Dave is a US Air Force veteran and the former Veteran Services Coordinator at Mott Community College, where he helped student veterans and their families take advantage of educational benefits available to them.
Through Dave’s efforts, Mott is an award-recognized veteran-friendly school.
Dave helped lead a new state law recognizing Blue Star Mother’s Day each year (PA 69 of 2024) Dave voted to expand educational funding for children of servicemembers with the MI National Guard (HB 4813) Dave cosponsored legislation for skills gained in the military to transfer towards apprenticeship requirements (HB 4279) privacy Dave introduced legislation to protect private property rights by requiring a warrant for state regulators and law enforcement to come on your private property if no emergency circumstances are present (HB 6128).

That’s why he has previously voted to suspend Michigan’s 27 cent gas tax (HB 5570), and supports solutions to move Michigan towards increased energy independence.
He remains committed to finding innovative solutions and prioritizing road funding within the existing budget. law enforcement & Security Dave supports a secure border, not only to keep violent criminals and those who are national security risks out of our country, but also to check the increased flow of illegal drugs into our state.

Unemployment During the pandemic and years following, Dave helped hundreds of laid-off workers cut through bureaucratic red tape to get their unemployment that they were rightfully due.
With the dramatically increased volume of unemployment cases, there was widespread fraud and general incompetence at the Unemployment Insurance Agency, which led to delays for individuals applying for benefits.
That’s why Dave led in calling for a leadership change to address these issues (HR 175). elections Our vote is our voice, and ensuring our vote counts is a crucial part of having confidence in our elections.
That’s why Dave voted for strengthening ID requirements when voting in-person or by absentee ballot (SB 285).
Dave supported legislation to prohibit the Secretary of State from sending unrequested absentee ballot applications (SB 310) and voted for legislation to require a physical signature when requesting an absentee ballot (HB 5288).
It is a problem that non-government groups can give money to specific government entities for elections.
Dave voted for a bill that would ban the use of third-party money (“Zuckerbucks”) for conducting elections (HB 5253).
