Meet State Senator
Randy Maxwell
Randy Maxwell is a lifelong Hoosier who believes in hard work, family, and community.
Before serving in the Indiana Senate, Randy built a successful business right here in southeast Indiana — creating jobs, meeting payroll, and learning what it takes to make things work in the real world.
That experience taught him the value of fiscal responsibility, common sense, and keeping government out of the way of small businesses and families trying to get ahead.
Randy and his wife Robin have deep roots in this community.
They’ve raised their three children here, supported their local schools and churches, and seen firsthand how strong families and small towns are the heart of Indiana.
These values guide everything Randy does at the Statehouse.
100%
Pro-Family
Voting Record
as recognized by the Indiana Family Institute
Randy Maxwell on the Issues That Matter to Us
In the State Senate, Randy Maxwell upholds and defends the conservative principles
that define our communities — faith, family, freedom, and fiscal responsibility.
Keeping taxes low
Keeping taxes low so we can support our families and grow our businesses.
We earn our paychecks — we should keep as much of our money as possible.
Strengthening schools
Strengthening schools so students learn the fundamentals and are equipped to succeed.
No politics.
No indoctrination.
Supporting law enforcement
Supporting law enforcement so our neighborhoods are safe and remain great places to raise families.
Randy Maxwell always backs the blue.
Standing up for small towns
Standing up for small towns across southeast Indiana so they can grow and prosper.
Randy Maxwell won’t let our communities be forgotten.
Listening to us
Listening to us so we have a real, responsive voice in the State Senate.
Our priorities are Randy Maxwell’s priorities.
Randy Maxwell is proud to represent us, his neighbors,
and fight for the Hoosier values that make our communities strong.
