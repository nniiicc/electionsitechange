Cost of Living & Taxpayer Responsibility Inflation and rising utility, fuel, and grocery costs continue to impact Northern Michigan households.
Why It Matters Responsible budgeting and targeted relief help families stay ahead without burdening future generations.
Melissa Supports Responsible state budgeting Targeted tax relief for working families Energy affordability initiatives Small business tax stability Reducing unnecessary regulatory costs Strong families require responsible budgets.
Fixing MDOC Staffing & Retention Michigan is facing a staffing crisis inside the Department of Corrections.
Facilities are relying on excessive overtime, burning out experienced officers, and spending thousands training new hires who often leave within a few years.
Why It Matters When prisons are understaffed and overworked, it costs taxpayers more and makes facilities less safe.
Constant turnover drains resources, increases overtime spending, weakens institutional knowledge.
Public safety depends on stability, experience, and accountability.
Melissa Supports Retention incentives to keep experienced officer on the job Reducing excessive mandatory overtime Smarter workforce planning to stabilize staffing levels Accountability in overtime spending Investing in long-term career pathways within corrections Fix staffing.
Stop the waste.
Smarter Justice & Smarter Spending Michigan spends tens of thousands of dollars per year to incarcerate one person.
At the same time, families often struggle to access affordable childcare, mental health services, job training, and food support that could help prevent crises in the first place.
We must be smarter about where and when we invest taxpayer dollars.
Why It Matters If we can afford to spend $50,000 a year to house incarcerated individuals, we can afford to invest earlier in the tools that help families succeed, preventing crime where possible and stabilizing communities reduces long-term costs and strengthens public safety.
Melissa Supports ‍ ‍ Expanded access to mental health services Supporting paid vocational and skilled training Strengthening childcare and family support Targeting assistance toward families who need it most Making corrections spending more efficient and accountable Invest early.
Spend wisely.
Protect taxpayers and communities.

Strong Schools & Workforce Readiness Education decisions are often made far from the communities they impact.
Northern Michigan schools face unique workforce and funding challenges.
Why It Matters Strong schools attract families, support local economies, and prepare students for real world careers.

Affordable Housing & Responsible Growth Northern Michigan families are feeling the pressure of rising housing cost.
Workforce shortages, short term rental, and limited development have made it harder for working families to find stable, affordable housing.
Why It Matters When housing becomes unaffordable, workers leave, small businesses struggle to hire, and young families move away.
Stable housing is essential to economic growth and community strength.
Melissa Supports Incentives for workforce housing development Smart local zoning flexibility while respecting community character Expanding access to first-time homebuyer programs Responsible oversight of short-term rentals impacts Practical solutions that increase housing without raising taxes Housing that works for families, not just investors.
