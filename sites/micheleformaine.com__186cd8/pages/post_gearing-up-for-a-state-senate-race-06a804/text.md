What's on the ballot?
Questions 1 & 2 are Citizens Initiatives.
These reach the ballot through a process of signature collection
- Q1 would enact 27+ changes to Maine's election laws-fundamentally changing absentee voting here and disenfranchising many older, disabled, working and rural voters.
It's voter suppression- plain & simple.
Vote NO on 1.
- Q2 is an "Extreme Risk Protection Order" also known as a Red Flag law.
This would allow a family member to seek a court order to remove firearms if another family member is in crisis & is a risk to themselves /others.
Red Flag laws save lives.
Vote YES on 2.
