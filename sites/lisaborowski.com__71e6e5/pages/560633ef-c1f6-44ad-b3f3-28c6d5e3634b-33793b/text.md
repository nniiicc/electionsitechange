On the Issues
- Public Education Public education is what got Lisa involved in politics, and it will always be a top priority of hers as State Representative.
As a former Vice President of the Radnor Township School District and a parent of two public school graduates, Lisa has led the way to invest in our public schools.
- Common Sense Gun Reform Having worked for over three decades in healthcare in areas of Philadelphia where gun violence takes a disproportionate toll, Lisa has seen firsthand how gun violence shatters too many families and futures.
- Infrastructure As Commissioner, Lisa got her township roads paved when the state wouldn’t.
She improved stormwater management, secured $13.7 million in support of previously unfunded infrastructure projects, and made sure township services like trail maintenance and snow plowing had the necessary funding.
- Right to Choose Lisa is a proud pro-choice candidate who will always stand up for a person’s right to make their own healthcare decisions.
- Workers' Rights Lisa is the granddaughter of a union plumber who was able to become the first in his family to purchase a home thanks to good union wages.
- Access to the Ballot Box Nothing is more fundamental to our democracy than the right to vote, but today, it’s under attack.
Lisa supports expanding voting rights and making voting easier for all Pennsylvanians.
- Protecting the Environment Lisa is dedicated to fighting climate change, including a shift toward renewable energy sources.
- Public Safety As a mother, Lisa will always prioritize the safety of our community and is a proven advocate for our first responders.
