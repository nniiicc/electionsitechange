She knows that Pennsylvania is a union state, and she will never leave our workers behind.
As Radnor Township Commissioner, Lisa partnered with local labor to introduce and pass a Responsible Contracting Ordinance (RCO).
As State Representative, she has worked to secure a seat at the table for local unions on large construction projects.
She is supportive of a statewide RCO, legislation to protect safety and wages, and to cover off-site custom fabrication for public works projects.
