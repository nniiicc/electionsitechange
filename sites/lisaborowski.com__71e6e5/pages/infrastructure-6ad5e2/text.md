Infrastructure
As Commissioner, Lisa got her township roads paved when the state wouldn’t.
She improved stormwater management, secured $13.7 million in support of previously unfunded infrastructure projects, and made sure township services like trail maintenance and snow plowing had the necessary funding.
As State Representative, Lisa has brought back millions to the district for important projects which help offset local expenses.
From stormwater and sewer projects in Middletown, park improvements in Radnor, investment in historic preservation in Newtown, and open space enhancements in Edgmont, Lisa has worked to bring the needs of the 168th to the highest levels in Harrisburg resulting in over $10 million brought back to the district for infrastructure improvements since being sworn in in 2023..
