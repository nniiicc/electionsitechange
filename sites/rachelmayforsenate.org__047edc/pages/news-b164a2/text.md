May One of Most Productive State Lawmakers in 2025 Session
Auburn Citizen, Published, July 23, 2025
"May, D-Syracuse, sponsored 27 bills that were approved by the state Assembly and Senate....
The legislation sponsored by May that passed both houses includes the Freedom to Read Act, which aims to counter book bans, and the New York Open Water Data Act that would establish a platform to improve water data sharing."
