McGovern, M.D.
Community Sports & Recreation Building Address: 7318 Avenue F, Houston, TX 77011 On behalf of the East End Alliance and our community partners, I’m pleased to formally invite you to participate in the 2026 East End Candidate Forum on Saturday, October 17, 2026 , at the John P.
McGovern, M.D.
Community Sports & Recreation Building in Houston’s East End.
All candidates for participating offices have been invited to take part.
The forum will bring candidates appearing on the November 3 General Election ballot together with residents, neighborhood leaders, and community organizations from across Houston’s East End.
The East End area spans 29 voting precincts, representing more than 67,500 voting-age residents according to 2020 Census data.
Across the 2020, 2022, and 2024 General Elections, recorded turnout averaged approximately 21,000 ballots cast per election, based on historical data.
Available 2024 General Election records identify 44,977 Harris County registered voters within the forum area.
As a candidate for Governor , the forum will provide an opportunity to introduce yourself to East End residents, discuss issues affecting our neighborhoods, answer questions from the community, and engage directly with voters just two days before Early Voting begins.
The East End Alliance is organizing the forum with community partners from across the East End, including but not limited to: East End Communities Super Neighborhood 46-88 (Eastwood) Texas Democratic Women People for Polk Friends of Columbia Tap Save Bastrop Promenade Strong Towns Houston Progressive Caucus Confirmed candidates will also be included in our community outreach and social-media promotion leading up to the forum.
We hope you’ll join us for this opportunity to meet East End residents, hear directly from the community, and share your perspective as a candidate for Governor.
