Some politicians think jobs come from taxes.
They give your taxes to large corporations to entice them to grow their businesses in Texas, assuming this is the only source of jobs.
In doing so, the impact of growth is your burden, instead of the responsibility of those consuming more water and energy, putting more cars on the road, putting kids in schools, and increasing demand on public safety and emergency response.
This is not sustainable.
Free market sustainability means growth pays for itself.
It requires those who impact demand for resources to pay for them.
It welcomes growth and rewards those that cover all of their costs and succeed.
It recognizes that growth can originate from anyone with an idea that organically and locally grows their business
Texas leads the world in data centers, and more are popping up like Hill Country bluebonnets.
Texas will not be sustainable if the burden of growth falls solely on you.
Free market sustainability is the right policy for Texas.
