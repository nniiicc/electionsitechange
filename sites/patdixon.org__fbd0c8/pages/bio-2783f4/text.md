Socially liberal, fiscally conservative. (see www.LPTexas.org )… Born Feb 3, 1964 in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania; oldest of five children Attended Miami University in Oxford, Ohio.
Major in an engineering program for the pulp and paper industry.
Worked for Honeywell, Inc. in Phoenix, Arizona for ten years.
That’s me with my colleagues during development of the first digital communication link in the industry between an industrial automation system to nuclear sensors on a paper machine.
I was the manager of several large scale industrial projects and managed several engineering development projects with teams from across the world.
I have always had a love of the outdoors.
I love to visit beautiful outdoor locations.
Here I am on the Pacific Coast Trail in Washington state In Lago Vista I have led the volunteer effort to develop hiking trails in the Balcones Canyonlands Wildlife Refuge.
I am the president of the Central Texas TrailTamers.
The TrailTamers is a volunteer organization that builds and maintains hiking trails throughout Texas and in many other states.
I have led the development of trails at Doe Skin Ranch and Warbler Vista since its inception in 2001 (see Trail Tamers Balcones Canyonland Report for more details) I love fishing, tennis, basketball, and many other recreational activities.
