His deeply rooted values are evident in the way he offers real substance, not the typical political lip-service.
I have seen it in his tireless work for healthcare for all and in his push to ensure safe and secure housing for everyone, especially the most vulnerable.
John works with people who are voiceless in the political process, and in his own caring voice, speaks out with courage against the powers that oppress many of our neighbors.
