I am excited to support Senator John Marty for another term as our state senator.
John is a delightful, hardworking, honest, and moral individual.
He has the knowledge and the personality that are needed to lead effectively.
In addition, John always listens when constituents have questions or want to discuss a topic.
In such unprecedented times, John’s steady hand is what we need to lead our district and our state.
He is a progressive leader and champion around issues like healthcare, mental health, the environment, social justice, and education.
As a current nursing student, I appreciate John’s proposed Minnesota Health Plan which would provide high quality healthcare to every Minnesotan.
