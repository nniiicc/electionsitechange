Privacy Policy
Effective Date: September 1, 2026
Friends of Micah Aiu respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how Friends of Micah Aiu collects, uses, and protects information you provide through micahaiu.com, campaign forms, events, and communications.
Information We Collect
We may collect information that you choose to provide, including:
- Your name
- Email address
- Mobile phone number
- Mailing address
- Volunteer information
- Event registration information
- Information submitted through campaign forms
- Other information you choose to provide to the campaign
We may also collect basic technical information when you use our website, such as your browser type, device type, IP address, and information about how you use the website.
How We Use Information
Friends of Micah Aiu may use the information we collect to:
- Communicate with supporters
- Provide campaign updates
- Share information about events and volunteer opportunities
- Provide voter information and election reminders
- Respond to questions or requests
- Operate and improve the campaign website
- Maintain campaign records
- Comply with applicable laws and reporting requirements
Text Messaging Privacy
If you provide your mobile phone number and expressly consent to receive text messages, Friends of Micah Aiu may send you recurring informational SMS or MMS messages.
Messages may include campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, event notices, voter information, election reminders, and responses to supporter inquiries.
Donations will not be solicited through the SMS program.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
You may reply HELP for assistance.
Mobile information and text messaging opt in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
We may use service providers to help operate campaign communications and messaging systems.
Those providers may use information only as needed to perform services for Friends of Micah Aiu and may not use campaign mobile information for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
Sharing of Information
Friends of Micah Aiu may share information with vendors and service providers that help the campaign operate its website, communications, events, voter outreach, data systems, and other campaign activities.
We may also disclose information when required by law or when needed to comply with campaign finance, election, legal, or regulatory requirements.
Text messaging originator opt in data and consent are excluded from any sharing for third party marketing or promotional purposes.
Cookies and Website Data
The website may use cookies or similar technology to help the site function, understand website traffic, and improve the user experience.
Your browser may allow you to control or disable cookies.
Third Party Websites
Our website may contain links to websites operated by other organizations.
Friends of Micah Aiu is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.
Data Security
Friends of Micah Aiu takes reasonable steps to protect information collected through the campaign's website and communications systems.
No online system can guarantee complete security.
Your Choices
You may unsubscribe from campaign emails by using the unsubscribe link included in an email.
You may stop campaign text messages at any time by replying STOP.
You may contact the campaign to request that we update or correct information you previously provided.
Changes to This Policy
Friends of Micah Aiu may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
