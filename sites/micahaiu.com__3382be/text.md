Aloha, As a priority this legislative session, I focused on issues facing condominium owners including the cost of insurance, which is crucial for many in our district.
New legislators hardly ever get bills passed but a bill I introduced, SB 2600, passed.
SB 2600 simply allows condominium documents to be delivered via email if available.
This simple bill will save condo associations hundreds if not thousands on postage costs by not needing to mail out these documents.
The legislature also passed bills addressing rising maintenance fees, ensuring those on fixed incomes can age in place.
I am proud to have introduced and supported these measures that help our residents manage costs, making housing more affordable and sustainable in Hawaii.
My work is not finished.
