February 8, 2024 The Washington House of Representatives unanimously passed legislation designed to address aspects of the housing crisis by building more affordable workforce housing at a faster rate.
House Bill 1892, sponsored by Rep.
Mari Leavitt, D-University Place, would establish a workforce housing accelerator program to be administered by the Washington Housing Finance Commission.
The program would create a loan program to speed up affordable housing across the state.
