March 19, 2024 The governor has signed the Lucas Petty Act (House Bill 1956) to educate students about the dangers of fentanyl and opioids.
Opioids represent the largest percentage of overdose deaths in Washington, a staggering 68 percent.
Nationally, Washington has the second-highest percentage increase — 65 percent — in fentanyl poisoning deaths over a one-year period.
