Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 12, 2026
Behr for 57 respects the privacy of visitors to behrforillinois.com.
This policy explains what information may be collected through the website and how it may be used.
Information You Provide
If you contact the campaign or submit a contact or volunteer form, we may receive information you choose to provide such as your name, email address, phone number and message.
We use that information to respond to you and for campaign-related communication.
We do not sell or rent personal information.
Website Analytics
This website uses Google Analytics to help us understand how visitors use the site.
Google Analytics may collect information such as pages visited, session activity, approximate geographic location, browser and device information.
Google Analytics may also use first-party cookies such as the _ga cookie to distinguish visitors and sessions.
The site also uses Google Search Console to understand how the website appears and performs in Google search results.
Cookies
The website and services used by the website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and website measurement.
Visitors can control or delete cookies through their browser settings.
Contributions
Online contributions are processed by third-party payment services such as Anedot and WinRed.
Behrforillinois.com does not directly collect or store your credit card information.
Information submitted through those services is subject to their own privacy and security policies.
Anedot states that it does not sell or trade supporter data.
Service Providers
We may use third-party services for website hosting, analytics, email delivery, forms and contribution processing.
Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.
Retention
Information provided to the campaign may be retained as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, communication, recordkeeping and compliance with applicable law.
