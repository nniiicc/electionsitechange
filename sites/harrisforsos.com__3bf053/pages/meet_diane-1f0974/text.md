Meet Diane!
I’m Diane Harris, a conservative republican, born and raised with Midwestern values as the second child of a stay-at-home mother and a professional chauffeur for over 30 years.
We didn’t live beyond our means, and from a young age, I was taught the importance of integrity, kindness, helping others but working hard for the things needed to achieve.
Those values have guided me throughout my personal and religious life, public service, and professional career.
I attended Joliet Public Schools.
After high school, I began my career in public service by working in Joliet City Hall's Accounting Department.
I later attended Joliet Junior College, the nation’s first community college, earned an Associates, and college credits from USF, then on to earn a Bachelor of Science in Management from the University of Phoenix.
Career
I retired from ComEd after 32 years of dedicated service in the electric utility industry.
With many years as an IBEW Steward, I’ve represented workers and I’ll bring that same support to Springfield.
After retirement, I became an entrepreneur owning a ladies dress shop in downtown Joliet, a former licensed insurance agent, a long standing notary public, commissioned by the State of Illinois for over 10 years.
I am still active today certified licensed and bonded as Notary Signing Agent.
I’m also a substitute teacher in the local high school District 204, where I stay connected with our youth and community while remaining deeply involved in faith and service, attending church regularly.
Public Service & Local Leadership

- Newly elected Secretary of the Joliet Township Republicans

I’m also an active member of the local Chamber of Commerce and participate in many civic, business, religious and professional organizations throughout the state.
Personal Background
I’ve been a licensed Illinois driver since age 16, with a clean driving record, never suspended or revoked.
At age 19, I registered my first new vehicle and since numerous vehicle registrations, business licenses, notary commissions, family participates in Organ Donor program and I currently operate an LLC.
Proud mother of three adult children and one dog.

I believe in hard work, high ethical standards, and a servant-minded leadership.
Review the 885M spending budget for waste and fraud and bring relief to Veterans and Seniors on their vehicle registrations.
Everyone deserves a leader who will put the people first.
