About Diane Harris:
Conservative leader with proven customer service experience to better meet the needs of the DMV office.
Retired Liability Representative from ComEd and small business owner.
Currently elected as Joliet Junior College Trustee and 23rd Precinct Committee.
She’s also appointed to the Joliet Public Library.
Her experience with budgets, management, customer relations, and professionalism prepared her for the roll as Secretary of State.
She is ready and committed to serving all Illinoisans with excellence, integrity and will put the people first.
She has 3 adult children and one dog.
