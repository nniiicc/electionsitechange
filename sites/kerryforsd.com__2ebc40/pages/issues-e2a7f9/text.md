Fighting for Sioux Falls Families and South Dakota Values
Colonel Kerry Loudenslager has spent a lifetime defending freedom, leading under pressure, and serving others.
In the South Dakota House, his mission is simple:
Protect our freedoms.
Support our families.
Keep Sioux Falls strong and safe.
Defend
our Constitutional Values
Kerry swore an oath to defend the Constitution—and that commitment didn’t end with his military retirement.
In Pierre, he will:
- Protect the Second Amendment and the right of law-abiding citizens to defend themselves
- Defend religious liberty and the freedom to live out one’s faith
- Oppose government overreach that threatens individual freedom and privacy
- Stand firmly for free speech and constitutional rule of law
Freedom must never be negotiated away.
Keep
Sioux Falls Safe
Across America, communities have suffered when leaders weakened law enforcement and ignored rising crime.
Kerry will work to ensure Sioux Falls never follows that path.
His priorities include:
- Strong, consistent support for law enforcement
- Policies that prevent crime rather than excuse it
- Safe neighborhoods where families and businesses can thrive
- Opposition to radical policies that endanger public safety
Public safety is the first responsibility of government.
Protect
Families & Parental Rights
Strong families are the foundation of strong communities.
Kerry believes parents—not bureaucrats—should guide their children’s upbringing, education, and values.
He will:
- Defend parental rights in education and healthcare decisions
- Support policies that strengthen families and protect children
- Oppose radical agendas that conflict with South Dakota values
- Promote respect, responsibility, and opportunity for the next generation
When families are strong, South Dakota is strong.
Encourage
Responsible Growth & a Strong Economy
Sioux Falls is growing—and growth must be managed with common sense and fiscal responsibility.
Kerry supports:
- Low taxes and limited government spending
- Policies that encourage job creation and small business success
- Responsible development that protects quality of life
- Long-term planning that keeps Sioux Falls affordable and livable
Economic freedom creates opportunity for everyone.
Honor
Veterans & Service Members
After 27 years in uniform—and as part of a multi-generation military family—Kerry understands the sacrifices made by service members and their families.
He will work to:
- Protect and improve veterans’ services and benefits
- Support military families and National Guard members
- Ensure South Dakota remains one of the most veteran-friendly states in America
Those who served our nation deserve unwavering support at home.
Conservative Leadership You Can Trust
Faith • Family • Freedom • Service
And a lifelong commitment to doing what is right, not what is easy.
