Why I’m Running… Keep Sioux Falls Strong.
Safe.
Conservative.
Sioux Falls isn’t just where I live—it’s where my story began.
It’s where families build their future, where neighbors look out for one another, and where conservative, faith-centered values still matter.
But across America, we’ve seen great cities decline when leadership abandons common sense.
I’m running to ensure that never happens here .
I believe District 12 deserves leadership that: Z Defends constitutional freedoms and God-given rights Z Supports law enforcement and safe neighborhoods Z Protects families, faith, and parental rights Z Promotes responsible growth without radical agendas Preserving our community starts with stepping forward and I’m ready to serve once again.
