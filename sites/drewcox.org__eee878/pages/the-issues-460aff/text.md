The Issues
Every American deserves accessible, affordable, and reliable healthcare.
Drew is in support of a single-payer medicare for all healthcare system.
Corporate money in politics has caused detrimental damage to our democracy and representation.
We are pushing to overturn Citizens United and getting big money out of politics.
The state has an important role to play in improving our quality of life - but it needs to be kept within constitutional limits.
Drew will fight to protect the freedom of the American people and our civil rights.
Working people in the United States are not given the space or support they need to have children, recover from illness and injury, or take care of their mental, emotional and social health.
Drew is committed to an unapologetic pro-worker agenda to raise the minimum wage, support unions, and battle poverty.
America was founded upon the idea that people should be free to live their lives without excessive restrictions on their freedom from the government.
Drew is a strong believer that, when it comes to decisions about our bodies and our personal relationships, the government needs to respect our autonomy.
Home prices have skyrocketed in the past decade, becoming completely unattainable for the working class and America’s young people.
Drew will fight to establish affordable housing acts and provide relief to citizens.
U.S. involvement in foreign affairs should be humane and ethical.
Drew is strongly opposed to the current state of United States foreign policy, specifically regarding involvements in Gaza and the Middle East.
He is committed to pulling military aid from all nations committing violent acts, particularly the genocide in Gaza.
As a Marine veteran, Drew Cox will vote against authorizing any more needless, illegal wars and will fight to reign in the unilateral military action of the executive branch.
He will also fight to make sure that the veterans we have sent abroad to fight are supported and cared for by a well funded VA.
Drew will push for legislation to revive federal employment programs for the 21st century, ensuring employment for millions of Americans while revitalizing and modernizing America’s infrastructure.
AI data centers are the most present threat to resource security in the United States.
Drew will fight to implement a federal moratorium on AI data centers, as well as regulation requiring that all data centers use green energy to power their activities.
The ruling class has created a rigged and exploitative system of predatory lending, excessive interest rates, and harsh punishments for failures to pay within unrealistic deadlines.
Drew will advocate for legislation that limits the unfair credit interest rates and predatory loans that take advantage of the American people.
In the richest country in the world, American income inequality is at an all time high.
The ultra wealthy and mega corporations pay very little of their fair share through all kinds of tax loopholes.
Drew advocates for the implementation of a progressive tax system with higher tax brackets for the ultra wealthy, and taxation of major corporations to close all tax loopholes.
U.S.
House seats have been capped at 435 since 1929, but population growth has led to each member representing more people than intended.
Adding the number of seats to the House of Representatives is meant to grow committee depth, oversight, and policy expertise.
