My name is Drew Cox, and I’m running to represent Indiana’s Fourth District in Congress.
I’m a lifelong Hoosier and veteran raised by a farmer and public schoolteacher, and I’m committed to delivering the policies that serve Indiana’s residents instead of corporate interests.
Here in District 4, our elected officials have failed to represent working people.
Our campaign is fighting to change that.
We are putting young people and policies at the forefront of the campaign, and refusing to accept any corporate PAC donations to ensure that our interests lie entirely with the people.
Drew's Story CAMPAIGN PLATFORM We’ve set out to run a different type of Democratic campaign.
Instead of accepting corporate PAC dollars and serving the interests of billionaires, our campaign is committed to overturning Citizens United and getting big money out of politics.
Working class Americans deserve to have their needs met, and our current leaders in Washington aren’t delivering.
