Home Privacy Policy Home Privacy Policy Meet Representative Mark Gidley Mark Gidley proudly serves the people of Alabama House District 29, representing portions of Etowah and Calhoun counties.
Since being elected in 2022, he has worked to bring common-sense, values-based leadership to Montgomery — always focused on strengthening families, improving education, honoring military service, and protecting Alabama’s most vulnerable citizens.
A lifelong resident of Etowah County, Mark has built his public service on the same principles that have guided him as a pastor, husband, and community leader : faith, integrity, and accountability.
In the Legislature, he serves on the House Committees on Education Policy, Military and Veterans Affairs, and Commerce and Small Business , where he continues to champion policies that reflect the priorities of his district.
Key Legislative Achievements The Military Spouse Licensing Act Simplifies professional licensing for military spouses relocating to Alabama The Elder Abuse Accountability Act Prevents individuals convicted of elder abuse from profiting from their victims The School Chaplain Program Act Provides voluntary chaplain services to support educators and students Support for Historical Values Advocated for the display of the Ten Commandments in public schools The Assistant Principal Bill Added assistant principals to schools across the state Guided by his belief in transparent and principled leadership , Mark remains deeply committed to being accessible and responsive to the people who placed their trust in him.
He continues to work toward making Alabama a place where families, communities, and small businesses can thrive .
Mark and his wife, Kathy , have been married for more than 40 years.
Together, they have raised a family rooted in faith, service, and love for their community — values that continue to shape his vision for Alabama’s future.
