Issues Economy I will fight to repeal burdensome regulations that hinders business investment and stand against tax increases.
I will introduce Economic Enterprise Zone Reforms: The people should receive a 50% reduction in the sales tax for shopping in Enterprise Zones at the point of sale.
Education Residency Requirements Our public school systems, including Baltimore County Public Schools, must be held accountable to enforce the residency requirements of students.
Our classrooms are overcrowded and residency enforcement ensures that the resources intended for our public school system actually go to the students who live within the school system boundaries.
Secondary school Administrators should be required to teach at least one class in addition to their administrative duties.
For too long, the people running our schools have forgotten what it is like to teach in a classroom.
We must restore discipline in the classroom.
Disruptive students deprive educational opportunities from those who want to learn.
Students suspected or charged with a crime must have an alternative placement!
I support the expansion of the school resource officer program as well as deputizing teachers who are certified and trained with concealed carry permits.
We must protect our students.
Let the People decide.
An Elected School Superintendent from a pool of qualified candidates would restore trust and renew confidence in the future of our Baltimore County Public Schools.
Thanks to Delegate Joe Boteler for this creative solution.
School Choice I will fight to rescue our children from woke failing government run schools!
I support having educational dollars follow the child!
Parents should be able to decide which option is best for their family whether it be a private school, or even home school.
It’s your money!
I will vote against ANY tax increases and fight to repeal the Kirwan Blueprint to Bankruptcy.
I will sponsor legislation to protect girl’s/women’s sports from the discrimination of biological males invading their teams.
Support Education and Job Training Opportunities Insist that Learning and Recreation Activity Centers provide tutors.
I will continue to fight to build trade schools for our children.
Energy We need to end the green new scam madness and restore Maryland’s reliable energy portfolio including waste to energy, coal, and nuclear power.
We must repeal the fracking ban and bring good paying energy jobs to western Maryland.
I will stand against wasteful wind projects that threaten our environment and wildlife.
I will stand against eminent domain that threatens our family farms and fight to end unnecessary power line projects!
Annapolis’s failed war on reliable affordable energy must end.
Pro-Life I am a Pro-Life candidate for the Maryland State House of Delegates.
Life begins at conception, and should not end until natural death.
I will defend the Right to Life: I will always defend the right to life of the Pre-born and the Elderly.
This is why I am proposing that expectant Mothers be able to claim a child tax deduction with documentation of a pre-born heartbeat.
Defund Abortion Mills!
Public Safety I will fight to restore the Law Enforcement Officer’s Bill of Rights: HB 0670, which my progressive opponents voted for in 2021 (even overriding Gov.
Hogan’s veto), removed protections from our officers which caused a mass exodus from the force, and increased crime that is spiraling out of control!
I support elected leadership for our law enforcement agencies.
If you have the power to arrest, we should have the power to elect.
Sanctuary Cities We need justice for Kayla Hamilton and Rachel Morin who were murdered by illegal aliens!
Sanctuary policies in the state of Maryland protect criminal illegal aliens and endanger our community!
I will fight to defund pro-criminal illegal alien organizations like CASA!
Second Amendment When elected, I will uphold my oath to defend the Constitution and stand up to the gun grabbers in Annapolis.
This is a very dangerous time where politicians are pushing to defund the police.
Our 2nd Amendment right to keep and bear arms urgently needs to be protected in order to keep our families safe.
Term Limits I support term limits for legislators and executives that follow the Florida model where there is no lifetime limit on the number of times a Governor or legislator can be elected, but after the consecutive term limit is reached, the Governor or legislator must be out of that office for at least one election cycle before being eligible for re-election.
This approach will encourage more candidates to serve with new ideas, while preserving the voters with the option to re-elect a public servant who they truly love and admire in the future.
Let the Voters decide.
Trans Health Equity Act Repeal I support the repeal of the 2023 HB0283 Gender-Affirming Treatment (Trans Health Equity Act).
We must protect our children from predatory doctors who are pushing puberty blockers and life altering surgeries on our children.
