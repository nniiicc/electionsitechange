Term Limits
I support term limits for legislators and executives that follow the Florida model where there is no lifetime limit on the number of times a Governor or legislator can be elected, but after the consecutive term limit is reached, the Governor or legislator must be out of that office for at least one election cycle before being eligible for re-election.
This approach will encourage more candidates to serve with new ideas, while preserving the voters with the option to re-elect a public servant who they truly love and admire in the future.
Let the Voters decide.
