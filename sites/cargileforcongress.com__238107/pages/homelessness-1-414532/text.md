the data doesn’t lie

And it is usually the result of a list of unfortunate events, circumstances or choices made by, or forced upon an individual.
Because of this, there cannot be a “one size fits all” solution.
However, no matter how effective a treatment or training program may be, we wind up at the same place.

There is a direct correlation between the rise of Democratic leadership in California, homelessness and the loss businesses and employment opportunities.
