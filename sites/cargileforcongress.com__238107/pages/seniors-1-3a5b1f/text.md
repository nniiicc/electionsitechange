HEALTHCARE Like so many other facets of our society, the biggest roadblock to a robust healthcare system is government intrusion.
It should be a free market responding to the laws of supply and demand.
As our healthcare demands increase with each generation, the supply of healthcare professionals, equipment and medication must increase at an even greater rate to drive costs down.
Nothing drives prices down faster than competition.
No one has a “right” to healthcare, but we all need it!
Therefore, it should be affordable for all.
Right now, the market is being artificially inflated due to heavy industry regulation.
We need transparency and to remove the restraints on State-by-State competition (allowing inter-state access to all facets of, and suppliers in, the healthcare industry).
This is where the collective bargaining power of a union or co-op can be fully realized.
Finally, I would support an effort to provide real tax credits and incentives to those who choose this profession.
