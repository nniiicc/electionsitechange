Georgia Senate District 14
Georgia Senate District 14 is located in northern Metro Atlanta, entirely within Fulton County, covering parts of Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Roswell, Sandy Springs, and northeast Atlanta.
Major corporate headquarters are located here, including Mercedes Benz and UPS.
Democrat Josh McLaurin of Sandy Springs has represented the district since 2023, and is now running for Lieutenant Governor, opening the seat to new representation.
