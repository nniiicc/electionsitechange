The Issues
AFFORDABILITY
Kevin will tackle the affordability crisis head-on.
He is committed to protecting tax credits and strengthening renter stability to increase attainable housing and help essential workers live in the communities they serve.
Property tax and income tax should not be cut in order to benefit the wealthiest among us.
Kevin believes a fair economy is one centered on progressive tax policy.
HEALTHCARE
Healthcare should be a right, not a privilege.
Kevin has personally faced insurance gaps while fighting cancer, shaping his commitment to accessible, quality care for all Georgians, including women’s reproductive healthcare.
Kevin’s two daughters work in healthcare, witnessing firsthand the inequities and failings of our system.
Kevin will push to expand Medicaid in Georgia, reduce healthcare costs, and fight to end the abortion ban.
LEADERSHIP
Kevin’s professional, civic, and personal experience equip him to lead on day one.
He delivered results on the Georgia Department of Transportation Board and has advocated tirelessly on behalf of refugees and immigrants in Georgia.
He created jobs for hundreds of employees in the district, providing healthcare benefits to all.
Kevin will bring practical, results-driven experience to Georgia Senate District 14.
