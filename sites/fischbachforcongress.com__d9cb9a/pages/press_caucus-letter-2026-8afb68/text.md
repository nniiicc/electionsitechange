Caucus Letter 2026
February 3, 2026
Dear Fellow Conservatives,
Thank you for your time and participation at tonight’s Republican caucus.
It is an honor and a privilege to be your voice in our nation’s capital.
I am Michelle Fischbach, a proven conservative running to continue being your representative for the Seventh District in Congress.
Scott and I have been married for 38 years and live in the Paynesville area, where we raised our two children.
We are also the proud grandparents of seven.
We met on the campaign of Senator Rudy Boschwitz and have been active in the party and pro-life causes ever since.
As your voice in Washington, D.C, I serve you on the House Ways & Means and Rules committees.
I am also Co-Chair of the House Pro-Life caucus.
In this session of Congress, I have prioritized strong rural communities through protecting tax cuts, increasing funding for the border wall and ICE agents, and seeking fair trade deals for agriculture products.
Some of the many other important issues I’ve supported are requiring voter ID in federal elections, delisting the gray wolf, repealing a ban on mining, and, most recently, passing a bill to allow states to use TANF funds for pregnancy care centers.
I encourage you to volunteer to be a precinct delegate to your county convention.
We need your help on our winning team in western Minnesota.
In 2022, we ran an aggressive get-out-the-vote campaign to lift the republican ticket by nearly 7%.
In 2024, I earned more votes than Ilhan Omar had in Minneapolis.
This year, we plan to build on these successes and make western Minnesota into a Republican powerhouse that offsets the metro vote.
With so much on the line this year, failure is not an option.
Together, we must win in November as part of a united team to break the DFL stranglehold in Saint Paul.
I look forward to working with all of you again to not only return a conservative to Congress, but also help in the legislative districts that we need to flip the MN House and Senate.
Imagine the opportunities to chart a new direction for Minnesota with a republican trifecta!
Please contact me or my campaign anytime by the information found at the bottom of this letter.
Thank you for your support.
Let’s Make Minnesota Great Again!
And may God bless America!
