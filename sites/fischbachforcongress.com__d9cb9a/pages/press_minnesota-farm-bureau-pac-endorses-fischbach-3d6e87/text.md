Fischbach Litchfield, Minnesota – The Minn Farm Bureau Political Action Committee (MFB PAC) recently announced their endorsement for Congresswoman Michelle Fischbach, MN-07, in the upcoming November 2026 elections.
The announcement included a note of appreciation for her support of Farm Bureau policy and agriculture.
“One of the strengths of the Farm Bureau endorsement process is the direct connection it creates between candidates and our grassroots members,” said State PAC Committee Chair Delvin Durheim.
“The candidates we’ve endorsed have shown their commitment to support agriculture in our state.
We look forward to continuing to build those connections and working together on issues that matter to the future of our member’s livelihoods.” Rep.
Fischbach has been a fierce advocate for the priorities of western Minnesota’s farmers and producers, such as developing new markets for agriculture products, doubling the death tax exemption, preserving the 199A and 45Z production tax credits, and getting E-15 across the finish line in the House.
“I am honored to receive MFB PAC endorsement and am grateful for the partnership I have with the Farm Bureau,” reacted Fischbach.
“They are invaluable to help me connect directly with producers across western Minnesota for timely updates on their challenges and opportunities.
I am honored to receive the MFB PAC endorsement as a validation of the commitment we share to a prosperous agriculture community.” The MFB PAC previously endorsed Rep.
Fischbach in her 2022 and 2024 re-election campaigns.
