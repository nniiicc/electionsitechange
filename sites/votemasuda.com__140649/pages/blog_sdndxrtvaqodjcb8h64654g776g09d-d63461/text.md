The doors are open to anyone, regardless of party to come and ask questions.
I’ve also sent a direct invite to Tom McClintock.
Let's debate.
Any format, any moderator, hosted jointly by our campaigns or by a neutral organization in the district.
A newspaper, a chamber of commerce, a college, a farm bureau, or join us at any of our town halls.Take your pick.

I don't think that's a lot to ask.
