GOTV ~ Phone Bank for Masuda!
Wednesday, October 14 · 4:30 – 8:00pm Thank you for choosing to Phone Bank with MASUDA FOR CONGRESS!
By stepping up to make phone calls, you are making the single most powerful choice a supporter can make.
You aren't just joining a campaign; you are becoming the beating heart of this entire movement!
By using our online auto-dialer, you are maximizing our impact and helping us reach more people than traditional calling ever could.
We know that jumping onto a dialer takes focus, patience, and heart, and we are incredibly grateful for the time and passion you are investing in this community.
Phone Banking isn’t just about making calls; it’s about opening them to big ideas, real change, and genuine human connection.
As a Phone Banker, you are the voice, and the incredible energy of our campaign.
You’re the one turning political headlines into powerful, neighborhood conversations.
THANK YOU for your support!
