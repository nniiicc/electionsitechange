Representative David Silvers is widely recognized as one of Florida’s most effective Democratic legislators.
He was ﬁrst elected in 2016 and has since been re-elected in 2018, 2020, and 2022.
Throughout his tenure, Rep.
Silvers has consistently advocated for policies that improve the lives of Florida residents.
His legislative achievements include spearheading signiﬁcant mental health reforms for children, enhancing public education with career readiness programs, and advancing criminal justice reform.
In addition, he has worked to protect vulnerable investors, especially seniors, from ﬁnancial scams and exploitation.
Rep.
Silvers has also focused on incentivizing the development of affordable housing and supporting victims of domestic violence.
These efforts have further solidiﬁed his reputation as a dedicated and impactful public servant.
