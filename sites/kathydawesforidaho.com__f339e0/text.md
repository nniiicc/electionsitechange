Donate Kathy DAWES for Idaho HOUSE Seat 6B My Goal: “Bringing Idahoans Together” I hope to be part of the 2027-28 legislature that will honor and respect the dignity of all, maintain our freedoms and rights, trust our local governments, and make Idaho a better place for all Idahoans and their families.
My Priorities: Affordability - I support cutting Idaho’s 6% food tax and making our property and income taxes more fair.
Idaho has the highest grocery tax in the nation, property taxes are high, and Idaho's income tax cuts disproportionately benefit the wealthy while cutting vital services.
I want to make taxes fairer for working Idahoans and make the wealthy pay their fair share.
Freedom - I believe that ALL Idahoans should be able to live their best lives free from discrimination, they should be able to make decisions about their own healthcare (mental and physical), and women should have the freedom and privacy to make decisions about their own bodies, their reproductive healthcare, and family planning without legislators intervening.
Rights - I support voting rights, initiative/referendum rights and the rights of citizens to elect local leaders who make decisions in the best interest of their communities, free from state government overreach.
Education - I support public schools and will work to fulfill our constitutional duty "to establish and maintain a general, uniform and thorough system of public, free common schools." Environment - I support responsible stewardship of our parks, forests, and agricultural land, which our economy and communities rely on.
Idaho public land should stay in public hands.
About the Campaign Kathy Dawes is running for the Idaho House of Representatives, Seat 6B.
She is the Democratic candidate for the November 2026 election.
