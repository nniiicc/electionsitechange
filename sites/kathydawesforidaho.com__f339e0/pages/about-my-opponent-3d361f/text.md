Donate About My Opponent Brandon Mitchell’s Voting Record Representative Mitchell has served in the Idaho Legislature for the last 6 years (3 terms).
He has served as Co-Chair of the House Transportation and Defense Committee, which does not allow remote testimony in any hearings!
So, those of us in northern Idaho can only testify in person, which would mean a trip to Boise - totally impractical!
Since 2025, he has served on the Joint Finance Appropriations Committee (JFAC) that determines the state budget every year and votes in line with the GOP, cutting services and vital programs for our district, such as University of Idaho funding and affordable housing options.
