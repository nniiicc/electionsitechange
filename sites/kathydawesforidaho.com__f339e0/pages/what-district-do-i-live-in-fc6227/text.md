What District Do I Live In?
Idaho Legislative District 6, where Kathy is running, includes all of Latah and Lewis Counties, but only part of Nez Perce County.
If you live in Nez Perce County and aren't sure whether you are in District 6 or 7, search for my legislator at Idaho.gov.
Once you’re at Idaho.gov, enter your street address and zip code, and click on “Find My Legislator” to find your District number and current legislators.
