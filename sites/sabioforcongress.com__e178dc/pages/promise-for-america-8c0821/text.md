By workers.
By entrepreneurs.
By innovators.
By families who believed tomorrow could be better than today.
For generations, Americans believed that if you worked hard, played by the rules, and sacrificed for your family, opportunity would always be within reach.
Today, too many Americans feel that promise slipping away.
Costs keep rising.
Opportunity feels harder to reach.
Small businesses are struggling to survive.
Families are working harder while falling further behind.
Communities are divided while Washington offers more outrage than solutions.
I refuse to accept American decline.
I believe America’s greatest days are still ahead of us.
But only if we have the courage to build them together.
This is not a campaign built on slogans.
This is not political theater.
This is a governing blueprint.
Like the spirit behind the Contract with America, this is a public commitment to the American people.
If elected to Congress, I will introduce real legislation focused on: - lowering costs, - rebuilding opportunity, - restoring energy independence, - strengthening Main Street businesses, - protecting American families, - and renewing belief in the American Dream.
We do not need to manage America’s decline.
We need to restore the American Dream.
THE FIVE PILLARS OF THE PROMISE FOR AMERICA A commitment to lower cost, create opportunity, strengthen families, and secure America's future.
Energy Affordability & American Power Act Powering the next American economy through affordable, reliable, American-made energy.
American Jobs & Opportunity Act Building a new private-sector jobs ecosystem centered on manufacturing, innovation, and skilled trades., apprenticeships, and emerging technologies Small Business First Act Restoring Main Street opportunity, reducing barriers to success, and empowering entrepreneurs to grow and create jobs.
Safe Communities & Secure America Act Strengthening public safety, securing our borders, protecting our critical infrastructure, and enhancing national resilience.
American Health Freedom Plan Affordable Care.
More Choices.
Stronger Families.
Greater Freedom.
Together, these pillars form the foundation of the Promise for America.
WHY I AM RUNNING I am not a career politician.
I never imagined as a kid growing up in National City, California, that I would one day: - serve this country in the military, - protect national leaders, - work in national security, - lead complex operations, - or run for Congress.
I know what struggle looks like.
I know what sacrifice looks like.
And I know what opportunity can do when someone believes in you.
America changed my life.
I am running to help ensure the American Dream is still alive for the next generation.
I have served this country in: - the military, - federal service, - national security, - private-sector leadership, - and municipal government.
Not because I sought status.
But because I believe leadership is about service.
I believe America’s greatest days are still ahead of us.
But only if we choose to build them together.
My Commitment This campaign is about more than politics.
It is about restoring belief: - that hard work still matters, - that families can still prosper, - that small businesses can still grow, - that communities can still thrive, - and that the American Dream still belongs to the people.
America does not need more political theater.
America needs renewal.
The American Renewal begins now.
And together — we will build it.
