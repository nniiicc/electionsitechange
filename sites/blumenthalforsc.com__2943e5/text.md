Meet George
George Blumenthal has spent a lifetime watching what true public service looks like.
Raised in a family dedicated to community and leadership—his father served 12 years in the Maryland House of Delegates and his mother another 8—George knows the sacrifice and responsibility that come with holding office.
With a career built on education, experience, and integrity, he’s ready to bring that same commitment to South Carolina’s District 104.
Where George Stands
- Push for a South Carolina Homestead Act so families don’t lose their homes to creditors after hardship.
- Safeguard access to healthcare, equal pay, and propose adding an Equal Rights Amendment to the State Constitution.
- Use decades of insurance law experience to fight for fair homeowner’s coverage and lower premiums.
- Crack down on elder abuse and financial scams with stronger protections and dedicated enforcement.
- Support caps on excessive non-economic damages to keep insurance rates fair for everyone.
- Oppose unnecessary consolidation of county police and sheriff’s departments, ensuring better training and accountability.
