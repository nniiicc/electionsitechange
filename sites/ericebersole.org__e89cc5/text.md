Eric Ebersole Home Events Meet Eric District 44A Scholarships Newsletters Donate Contact Volunteer About Eric As a lifelong resident of District 44, Eric was raised by a family of teachers and is a product of Baltimore County Public Schools.
He attended the University of Maryland, graduating at the top of his class from the College of Education and receiving a Masters of Science in Math Education.
Returning to Catonsville, Eric and his wife, Tara, raised a family and both continued the family tradition of teaching.
Eric taught high school mathematics in our public schools for 35 years before becoming a Delegate.
During his time in Baltimore County, Eric has organized youth groups and service trips, instructed and educated students in school, and coordinated numerous community events.
Eric has committed himself to public service.
He taught the students of the district for 35 years and has served as a Delegate since 2015.
After 64 years in the district and state, Eric understands the challenges we face and the many opportunities that lie ahead – if we let Maryland lead.
