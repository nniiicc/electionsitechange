Best, Eric Ebersole Improving Child Care Bill Package Small Business Assistance Financial Help for Families To better financially support families both utilizing child care services and working in child care, we are working on HB995 and HB1100.
These bills allow families to immediately receive funds for child care from the state when they meet the proper criteria and create bonuses for newly hired employees in the child care businesses.
Together, they will help support low-income families and enable them to receive the care that their children need.
Child care centers are often run as small businesses, and HB089 and HB993 recognize this unique aspect of the industry by providing more funds to aid them.
HB089 creates a stabilization grant program and allows the Maryland State Department of Education (MSDE) to offer money to those child care centers in danger of closing in areas where they are most needed.
HB993 creates a revolving loan fund for child care centers to use on capital projects.
After the initial loans are paid back over the course of 5 years, the funds will be redistributed to a different child care center for a new capital project with no additional cost to the government.
Improving Child Care Access HB725 allows child care centers to specifically target the needs of children with developmental delays and disabilities by providing grants to them for individualized care.
The MSDE would provide these grants after reviewing the specific needs of the child to create a system that allows for money from the government to follow and support who are most in need.
Strengthening Cybersecurity Bill Package Modernizing Maryland's Technological Infrastructure In this package of bills, both HB1205 and HB1346 work together to improve and modernize our technological infrastructure.
These bills create a proper governance structure for cybersecurity by creating the Office of Security Management (OSM) and design an oversight commission consisting of experts in cybersecurity, legislators, and industry leaders.
Response to Cyber-Attacks Another bill in the Cybersecurity package, HB1202, better prepares state and local governments for potential cyber attacks.
By creating a "fusion center" to better share data across governmental entities, it ensures proper support to effectively respond to emergency incidents.
Family Budget Booster Tax Relief Package Work Opportunity Tax Credit The “Family Budget Boosters” tax relief package will help those who struggle to find work become more employable.
It includes the Work Opportunity Tax Credit , which provides a tax credit to businesses that hire and retain workers from underrepresented communities who are chronically unemployed.
This will help put unemployed Marylanders back to work.
Medical Product Sales Tax Exemptions Included in this package are HB364, HB492, and HB1151 which all cut the sales tax rate on health products that many Marylanders require.
Ranging on items from toothbrushes, blood pressure monitors, and diabetic care products, these bills will make basic medical necessities more affordable.
This will help ensure everyone has access to these products and will help to boost our public health.
Baby Product Sales Tax Exemptions The sales tax exemptions also stretch to many products related to families.
HB282 and HB288 feature tax cuts on diapers and additional baby products including car seats, baby bottles and bottle nipples to help save families money.
These measures will provide some financial relief to families with young children and help them continue to be able to afford the products that they need.
Preserving Our State Parks and Environment Patapsco Valley State Park Improvements Great Maryland Outdoors Act As a member of the Friends of Patapsco Valley State Park Board of Directors, I am always working to help improve the park that is key to our community.
This session, I am working to increase access and space for parking at the park, so all can continue to enjoy its beauty.
In my free time, I continue to volunteer to help the park personally as well.
HB727 (The Great Maryland Outdoors Act) provides a much needed focus on updating the infrastructure to Maryland Park Service and all of our state parks.
This bill will increase the number of full-time staff in the Maryland Park Service and address critical maintenance needs by updating park infrastructure.
I believe it will greatly improve the experience we can have in our state parks.
