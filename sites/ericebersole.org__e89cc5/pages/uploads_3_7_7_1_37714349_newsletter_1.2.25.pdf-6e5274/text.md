For the last three years I have been working on a piece of legislation that would require transportation employees to be trained in recognizing human trafficking and learning how to report it.
Labeled See Someone Save Someone Act, it passed in the house last year and this year I have a good Senate cross file.
But I am also proud of the fact that this year the bill is labeled as House Bill #1 (HB1) !
This bodes well and makes me proud.
But most importantly I hope the passage of this bill will help to take a bite out of human trafficking here in Maryland.
I hope that if transportation employees See Someone that needs help, they can Save Someone who needs help.
Baltimore County Public Schools A boundary line change under consideration for Arbutus and Catonsville Middle Schools will be the subject of a public information session scheduled for February 5, 2025.
The proposed boundary change, which would be effective for the 2025-26 school year, will result in Catonsville Middle receiving all students from Hillcrest Elementary School rather than the current feeder pattern that divides Hillcrest students between both Catonsville and Arbutus middle schools.
No changes will be made to any elementary school boundaries.
