District 44A
Maryland's legislative district 44A contains a section of southwest Baltimore County inlcuding Baltimore Highlands, Riverview, Lansdowne, Arbutus, Relay, Paradise, Academy Heights, and Catonsville.
It is a single member district for the House of Delegates.
