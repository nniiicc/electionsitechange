Where is House District 19B?
Minnesota House District 19B includes
parts of Waseca and Steele Counties, including:
Waseca, Owatonna and Medford
and the following townships:
Clinton Falls, Deerfield, Havana, Medford,
Meriden, Merton, Owatonna, St Mary, and Woodville
