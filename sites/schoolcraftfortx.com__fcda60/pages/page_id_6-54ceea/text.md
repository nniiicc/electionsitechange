Skip to content Home Meet Alan Priorities Endorsements Conservative Rankings Newsletters The Islamification of Texas Contact Contribute Subscribe State Representative Alan Schoolcraft is a devoted Christian, husband, father, and grandfather.
Representative Schoolcraft is an attorney and a successful business owner.
He is a United States Air Force veteran, a graduate of the University of Texas at Austin, and he graduated top of his class at Saint Mary’s University Law School in San Antonio.
He lives with his wife Joette in McQueeney, Texas.
Representative Schoolcraft grew up in Guadalupe County and he comes from a line of Republican activism.
Before Representative Schoolcraft’s own conservative service in the Texas House, his father served as the Chairman of the Guadalupe County Republican Party and his mother served on the State Republican Executive Committee for many years.
His mother also dedicated 17 years to the Schertz-Cibolo-Universal City ISD school board.
Representative Schoolcraft previously served in the Texas House for six-terms, from 1981 to 1993.
He returned to the Texas House in 2024 and he proudly represents Guadalupe and Gonzales counties.
Representative Schoolcraft is committed to representing House District 44 with strong conservative values in the Texas Legislature.
He currently serves on the Public Education Committee, Pensions, Investments & Financial Services Committee, and the Permanent Standing Subcommittee on Academic & Career-Oriented Education.
