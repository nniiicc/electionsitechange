Gilda Cobb-Hunter is a dedicated community activist and social worker who works across the aisle to improve the lives of everyone in Orangeburg County.
Gilda fights for the things that matter most to South Carolinians:
- Providing quality education for all
- Increasing state employees’ pay
- Advocating for children and families
South Carolina is one of the fastest growing states in the country but we have to make sure our rural communities don’t vanish, taking local jobs, schools and local healthcare away.
Did you know we lose approximately 35 acres of farmland a day due to development?
Gilda has worked tirelessly with both Democrats and Republicans to keep local jobs local.
She’s an outspoken advocate for workforce development programs that affect every aspect of the Orangeburg community.
Gilda’s work with Orangeburg-Calhoun Technical College ensures local businesses have the right students for the right jobs.
By focusing on increasing local jobs and improving local education, we can keep Orangeburg families close to home.
- Equal Rights Amendment
Gilda Cobb-Hunter supports the Equal Rights Amendment (ERA) which states that the rights guaranteed by the Constitution apply equally to all persons regardless of their sex.
- Affordable Healthcare
Ensuring that South Carolinians receive quality health services is of the highest priority to Rep.
Gilda Cobb-Hunter.
She is an outspoken proponent for accessible and affordable healthcare.
- Better State Employee Pay
Rep.
Gilda Cobb-Hunter supports competitive pay for South Carolina’s state employees.
The benefit to our communities will be a stronger economy and more stable workforce.
Gilda Cobb-Hunter is a Democratic member of the South Carolina House of Representatives, representing District 66 in Orangeburg County and living in Orangeburg.
