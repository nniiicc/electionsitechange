WHAT IS IMPORTANT FOR NORTH CAROLINIANS
Good Government… NC was the LAST state without a BUDGET!!
Good Government — Why it feels broken
We need to elevate the public discourse to restore our civil society.
Good government policymaking has always occurred somewhere in the middle ground, after compromise.
But that just doesn’t seem to happen anymore.
Old-fashioned statesmanship (and stateswomanship) feels like a lost art, but it doesn’t have to be that way.
WE NEED A HARD RESET.
Lead by example + compromise
It is essential that our leaders believe in the value of a hard-fought compromise.
I know that I believe in that method of governing, and I have always tried to lead by example.
That doesn’t mean we don’t disagree—often vehemently—but we talk about the issues with information and intelligence, and we forge a path forward for the benefit of ALL North Carolinians.
The budget problem
The Republican infighting is so bad that we are the LAST state in the union without a budget.
THAT is not good governance.
In fact, it’s malfeasance, in my opinion.
Strengthen our Economic Stability and Quality of Life
Raising the Minimum Wage
We must raise the minimum wage in an intentional manner that moves workers out of poverty, while giving businesses the capacity to flex and adapt.
Striking that balance can and must be the path forward.
Having a second or third job should be a choice, not a necessity.
Fair & Responsible Tax Structure
I support re-evaluating our tax structure so that it does not disproportionately affect those in lower income brackets.
We have lowered taxes in North Carolina, but we have allowed other fees and expenses (think the DMV) that mean poorer folks are actually worse off.
Eliminating the corporate tax is a terrible idea.
Nobody likes taxes, but everyone paying a fair share is necessary for government to function.
Affordable Workforce Housing
We must find a way to supplement affordable workforce housing, because firefighters, teachers, and civil service workers need to be able to live in the communities they serve.
Expanding Access to Community College
I support programs that will make our community college system more accessible to more students.
I would be interested in examining expanded grant programs, apprenticeship programs, and work/study programs.
Public Education….
Vouchers are killing high quality public education.
Teacher Pay & Per-Pupil Funding
North Carolina teachers are not being paid as professionals, and per-pupil funding is grossly low compared to other states.
The Voucher Program & Public School Funding
The voucher scheme favored by Republicans in leadership is siphoning roughly $800 million out of public school coffers and putting it into private schools.
Recent legislation has removed the income formula, meaning there is no limitation on who can access these voucher funds.
That means your multi-million-dollar neighbor is sending his or her children to private schools, and the taxpayers are paying for it.
That's not fair.
Student Mental Health & School Staffing
There are woefully few school counselors and even fewer school nurses, at a time when we know our children need more mental healthcare than ever.
Shifting Costs to Counties & Property Taxes
Well-intentioned county governments are now bearing the burden of Raleigh's failure to provide a sound, basic education for our children, as required by our North Carolina Constitution.
Our counties are straining to bridge the gap - and that means that your ad valorem taxes on your real estate are going up to cover this unnecessary cost shift coming from Washington and Raleigh to our counties.
Safer Communities
Assault Weapons Ban & Gun Safety Reform
We must ban assault weapons to protect our children and our police officers.
We know the ban works because we tried it and it saved lives.
Letting the ban expire was reckless and we must renew it immediately at the federal level.
I support common sense gun control including universal background checks, safe storage, and extreme risk protective orders as a good start.
Cannabis Reform
I believe that decriminalizing the personal use of small amounts of recreational cannabis is the right move at this time because we have too many people who have a stigmatized criminal record.
This limits their opportunities unnecessarily and it's time we move forward on this issue.
Medical cannabis should be available at a doctor's discretion.
If we can ease someone's suffering and relieve their pain, we should.
It would also reduce opioid dependence which is terribly important.
Ending Cash Bail for Non-Violent Offenders
I would end cash bail for non-violent offenders.
Lives can be ruined just because you are poor and the balance in your bank account shouldn't dictate your freedom.
Supporting & Reforming Law Enforcement
We need to fund and train the police to improve racial bias certainly, but we also need to support the police with additional personnel so that officers can focus on matters of policing instead of a myriad of social problems such as substance use disorder and metal illness.
Human Rights
Statewide Nondiscrimination Protections
I support passing statewide nondiscrimination legislation.
Addressing Structural Racism
I acknowledge that there remains profound structural racism in this country, and I support efforts to remedy this social ill.
Reproductive Freedom
I support the codification of Roe v.
Wade and believe that women should have the freedom to control their own bodies.
Immigration Reform & Civil Rights Protections
The Trump administration is committing civil rights violations through its use of ICE and Border Patrol to conduct warrantless searches and seizures of law-abiding immigrants.
This is hampering our local law enforcement, sowing distrust within communities, constitutes clear racial profiling, and violates our constitutional protections.
It is un-American in every way, and it must stop.
CONGRESS must address immigration reform in a holistic and comprehensive manner, rather than the piecemeal approach that has taken place since the 1960s.
Fund the Medicaid Rebase…. cost shifting from TRUMP threatens healthcare.
Medicaid Expansion & Healthcare Access
North Carolina was among the last - if not the last - state to expand Medicaid just a couple of years ago.
But after businesses, hospitals, and individuals continues to strongly advocate for expansion, it finally happened, and over 600,000 North Carolinians began receiving life-saving care they desperately needed.
Medicaid Funding & Budget Responsibility
AND NOW, without a budget, the Medicaid rebase is being underfunded - meaning that unless Republicans reconvene and hammer out a budget in 2026, many people will likely suffer cuts to essential services.
This is negligent governance, and it threatens the very health and lives of those who depend on Medicaid for their medications, doctor visits, and hospitalization.
It is unconscionable that people's very lives are being threatened because of the bickering and immaturity of Republican leadership in Raleigh.
Preserve our Natural Environment/Fight Climate Change
Protecting Water Quality & Preventing Pollution
It is time that we reimagine the discharge permitting process so that manufacturers must prove the safety of their processes before being allowed to use public trust resources.
We have suffered terrible water quality issues here in North Carolina (PFAS contamination), and while we struggle to find technologies to remove the accumulation of decades of these "forever chemicals", we have got to prevent contamination from happening in the first place.
It's called the precautionary principle, and it's time we embrace it before it's too late.
Plain and simply: polluters pay.
If you contaminate it, you pay for the clean-up.
Renewable Energy & Energy Independence
Renewable energy is an absolute necessity.
Investing in wind power and solar energy is the path to energy independence.
Electric Vehicles & Charging Infrastructure
I support the electrification of our governmental fleet, and I support the expansion of public charging stations to encourage consumers to buy EVs.
Climate Impacts on Coastal Communities
I am on the coast of North Carolina, and our hurricanes are becoming more frequent and more ferocious.
Even absent storm surge, we are seeing "fair-weather" flooding.
Floodplain Buyouts & Climate Adaptation
I support expanding floodplain buyout dollars because, until we turn the corner on rising temperatures, we are going to have to retreat from certain areas that are becoming uninhabitable.
Strengthen our Fragile Democracy
Ending Gerrymandering
It's time to end gerrymandering by establishing an independent commission charged with drawing our districts using mapmaking criteria as determined by the judiciary.
Campaign Finance Reform & Transparency
Money in politics is corrupting the process.
Campaign finance reform is essential to ensure that we are electing the best-qualified candidates - not those who can raise the most money.
I support caps on fundraising in order to level the playing field, and full transparency is likewise essential.
Protecting Voting Rights & Public Trust
Voting rights must be protected at all costs because voting is the premise upon which all else is built.
The " BIG LIFE" is incredible damaging because it undermines people's faith in the process.
Once that faith is sufficiently eroded. we will be in dangerous territory.
