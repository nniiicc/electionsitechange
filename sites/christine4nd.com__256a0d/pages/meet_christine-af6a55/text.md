Meet Christine About Christine Endorsements Better Together Meet Rosie Issues News Events Volunteer Contact Contribute Yard Signs Meet Christine Fargo, ND - North Dakota Republican Party District 11 endorsed Dr.
Christine O'Riley, Psy.D., March 31 st to be their senate candidate for the upcoming 2026 election.
If elected, O'Riley will serve a four-year term from 2027-2031.
O'Riley is a former military veteran who has worked in the ND rural mental health systems.
The psychologist hopes to bring her wealth of experiences to the legislature.
She received her clinical psychology doctorate from the Miami Institute of Psychology and a degree in canon law from Katholieke University Leuven, Belgium.
O'Riley served as an officer in the Army for over 20 years, and worked in the Department of Defense Health Care system for an additional ten years.
She has dedicated her professional life to the care of others.
O'Riley serves her local Catholic diocese, as well as other dioceses around the world, as a canon lawyer and clinical psychologist.
O'Riley is committed to addressing key issues such as the right to life for all ages, fiscal sanity, taxpayer relief, and prioritizing the success of future generations.
"I am deeply honored and humbled to receive the District 11 Republican endorsement for Senate," said O'Riley.
"North Dakota is a state with immense potential and resources.
I am committed to working hard to ensure its continued prosperity and well-being.
I am proud to call District 11 home and want to work to protect our citizens' human rights and quality of life." O'Riley has received widespread support from the membership of District 11, local organizations, and residents alike.
Dr.
Christine O'Riley has been married to Patrick O'Riley for 28 years and they have one daughter together Margaret O'Riley who is studying at NDSU.
