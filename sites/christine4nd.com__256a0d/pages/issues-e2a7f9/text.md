Meet Christine About Christine Endorsements Better Together Meet Rosie Issues News Events Volunteer Contact Contribute Yard Signs Issues After serving our great nation, my family and I chose to return to the home of my ancestors and “re-settle” in the beautiful state of North Dakota.
I am running to represent District 11 in the North Dakota Senate to ensure that the quality of life that attracted us to this area continues to be protected, as well as strengthened for my family, your family, and the future families who are proud to call North Dakota home.
It would be an honor and a privilege to serve my neighbors in the North Dakota Senate.
Build Successful Generations Build Successful Generations Success for North Dakotans begins at home.
As your State Senator, I will work to ensure our laws and programs support all of our citizens and enable them to establish a strong foundation for future success for themselves, their families, and the generations to come.
Some of the areas I will explore with my colleagues in the Senate include: North Dakotans must have a competitive edge.
Regional and state workforce solutions must enhance current and future generations by encouraging business and workforce development, strengthening recruitment efforts, committing to employee retention via good wages & benefits and advancement opportunities, while supporting coordination between educational and training systems that match employers’ needs.
Affordable housing options ought to begin with decreasing burdensome regulations, strengthening supply chains, as well as promoting careers in skilled construction trades to increase labor supply and reduce wait times and costs.
Burdensome regulations make up 25% of housing costs!
Maintaining North Dakota's valued livability, including a safe streets initiative to help reduce crime and to ensure it doesn't get a strong foothold in our communities (e.g., increase community police efforts, etc.).
Truth in sentencing legislation requiring that those convicted serve all or larger portions of their sentences is but one way to reduce crime.
I promise to work to empower parents to parent .
As the first educators of their children, I will defend parental rights and ensure our legislative efforts do not interfere with this important right.
Exploring quality education options that neither burden taxpayers nor benefit some students and not others.
Data centers and other projects can have an important role in giving North Dakotans a competitive edge by advancing our technological capabilities and enhancing the economy.
Nevertheless, large-scale projects such as data centers can stress local infrastructure, etc.
However, as with past industrial projects in ND, concerns can often be addressed through discussion, sensible legislation, and/or prudent regulation.
I want to hear your thoughts.
Caring For Our Neighbors A Right to Life for All Ages because...
"Every Life is Precious” The citizens of North Dakota have spoken time and time again that life matters here.
As your State Senator, I will work to ensure that our laws reflect the wishes of our community, where we value individuals and families by: Protecting life at every stage from conception to natural death Ensuring compassionate alternatives are available to help those struggling with mental health, addictions, and/or homelessness.
Empower area non-profit organizations that possess expertise and have the responsibility for delivering specialized services to meet the unique needs of those with mental health, addictions, and/or unstable living arrangements.
Non-profits must be outcome-driven and meet their objectives for delivering quality treatment services to help the people in their charge.
Right to Life encompasses more than talking about birth and death.
The journey of life may start with supportive pregnancy services and later require supportive family and/or palliative care types of support.
I will work to review practical options to break the cycles of complex issues and circumstances that sometimes keep members of our community from flourishing.
Taxpayer Relief Taxpayer Relief Every candidate talks about taxes, but I'm ready to roll up my sleeves and seriously address this important issue.
Excessive taxation has a significant impact on North Dakota families and businesses that work hard every day.
Every family in America must make decisions to make ends meet, and so must our government.
As your State Senator, I'll be investing in tax solutions that provide noticeable relief to our taxpayers, including: Working to abolish or significantly contain property taxes.
Limit special assessments via legislative guardrails.
Examine abolishing state income taxes.
With North Dakotans keeping more of their hard-earned money through tax relief, our families, businesses, and communities will be poised for generational success.
Common Sense Fiscal Sanity Common Sense Fiscal Sanity The people of North Dakota have a long tradition of working hard to support their families, their communities and the organizations they care about.
We must work with the North Dakota Legislature to ensure our hard-earned money is used wisely and contributes to the quality of life for our residents.
As your State Senator, I will work to ensure Common Sense Fiscal Sanity measures are in place and are enforced to ensure your tax dollars are used wisely and well-managed.
My priorities will include: Reviewing regulations and removing unnecessary statutes that hinder North Dakota businesses and homeowners.
Ensuring legislative guardrails are in place, and enforced to reduce waste, fraud and abuse.
Reviewing state investments in programs and policies to ensure they are delivering the intended results.
