Expect More… Service
Long before I enlisted in the Army, when I was 17 years old, pride in our nation was important to my family and me.
Growing up among men and women veterans, who bravely served our nation, participating in hometown Bicentennial celebrations, and honoring national traditions such as Veterans Day, Memorial Day, Flag Day, and the 4 th of July, inspired me to join national service.
National Service, aside from serving shoulder to shoulder with others, allows one to reach far beyond anything they thought possible or imagined doing.
For example, in the Army Medical Service Corps, many talented people come together as a team to perform difficult work under challenging circumstances.
Make an opportunity to meet the Veterans, North Dakota Guard members, and Active Military Service members in our community to learn how “duty, honor, and country” prepared them to meet the challenges of everyday life.
Expect More… Compassion
The importance of quality, affordable, and beneficial health and rehabilitation services cannot be understated.
As medical and psychological diagnostics and interventions continue to evolve, I believe healthcare must be ethical, compassionate, and always respectful of the sanctity of life.
As a clinical psychologist with specializations in neuropsychology and forensics, I strive to understand the unique circumstances of individuals and how their illness or injury affects their life and the lives of their loved ones.
Serious illnesses and injuries create vulnerabilities, even among the “strongest” of us.
As a clinician, I have been blessed to witness and be inspired by the hope and strength of patients and their
families facing complex and challenging circumstances over the years.
Expect More… Problem-Solving
Neither governments nor agencies make good parents or caretakers.
Whether pain and suffering stem from national, inner-city, local, or family circumstances, be confident that I am committed to enabling people to flourish.
I will challenge the state and local governments to empower people to move forward with their lives rather than encourage a life centered on government dependency and/or encouraging a life of “victimhood.”
Please share your ideas with me, because I seek to be your senator and represent you.
