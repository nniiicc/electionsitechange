If a candidate reaches out to NDFALA for support, there are three possible outcomes for that candidate: endorsement, approval, or neither.
The top level of NDFALA support is a formal endorsement.
If a candidate for a particular office is in agreement with NDFA values, they are eligible for a formal endorsement.
No more than one endorsement can be issued per position, per election.
If a candidate does not receive endorsement, they are still eligible for approval by NDFALA if they are closely aligned with NDFA values.
There is no limit on the number of candidates who may be approved for a particular position.
