On the Issues
When we support those hardest to reach, we reach everyone; when we lift the entire district, we lift the entire state.
🧠
Improving Behavioral Health Outcomes
Darya has been a driving force in improving Washington's behavioral health system.
She passed key legislation (HB 2099) that ensures people leaving state custody receive state IDs, removing a major barrier to re-entry.
She secured over $30 million in funding to tackle the behavioral health crisis, including $7 million for the CoLead Northgate program.
She was the lead negotiator for the House in addressing the Trueblood lawsuit.
Darya believes in proactive care—making sure people get the help they need before a crisis occurs.
🛡️
Increasing Public Safety
Darya knows that public safety is about more than just policing.
She's fought for early intervention programs, including $8.75 million for DESC's behavioral health response teams in King County.
She introduced HB 1994 creating tools for judges to meaningfully hold individuals accountable while getting them into treatment.
She is also leading HB 1445, which strengthens the Attorney General's power to investigate patterns of police misconduct.
❤️
Healthcare for ALL
Darya has been a relentless advocate for healthcare as a human right.
She introduced HB 1313 to expand access to Medicare.
She helped pass the My Health My Data bill and the Shield Law which protects people from out-of-state legal action following the overturning of Roe v.
Wade.
Her vision includes universal coverage, prioritizing patients over profits.
🗳️
Protecting Democracy
Darya is deeply committed to empowering more people to participate in our democracy.
She's introduced legislation for young people (HB 2192) and people with disabilities (HB 1475) to have access to tools and information to cast an independent vote with dignity.
She also introduced HB 1932 allowing local jurisdictions to determine if even year elections are appropriate for their communities.
🏠
Tackling the Housing Crisis
Darya has backed the Missing Middle Housing Bill (HB 1782) and secured $7 million for the CoLead Northgate program for transitional housing.
She is a strong proponent of rent stabilization (HB 2114) — the most important legislation to address housing instability and affordability.
🚆
Public Transportation & Environment
Darya has fought to expand the regional light rail system.
She helped pass funding for the Jose Marti Child Care center at Northgate, supports the I-5 lid in the University District, and is working to preserve vital bus lines in Lake City.
She's pushing for policies that expand clean, efficient transportation to tackle the climate crisis.
📚
Educational Opportunity for All
Darya has worked on bills to ensure students with disabilities and language barriers can succeed and secured funding for early childhood education facilities.
She is dedicated to advocating for better support and pay for teachers, ensuring every student has the opportunity to thrive.
🔫
Gun Responsibility
As the youngest member of the legislature and Vice Chair of Civil Rights and Judiciary, Darya has been a strong voice for gun safety.
Her approach is rooted in surviving a school lockdown during a mass shooting.
She supports comprehensive bans on assault rifles (HB 1240) and preventing bulk firearms purchases (HB 2054).
💰
Economic Relief
Darya led HB 2072, which strengthens penalties for antitrust violations.
She supported HB 1406 to create the Washington state wealth tax and HB 1628 to increase affordable housing through modified real estate excise tax.
She's committed to building an economy that works for everyone, not just the wealthy few.
