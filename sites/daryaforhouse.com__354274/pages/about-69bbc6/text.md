About Darya

Darya Farivar
Leadership & Record
A Voice for the People
Darya has been a leading voice in community safety, access to democracy, and healthcare reform.
As Vice Chair of Civil Rights and Judiciary, she’s advancing legislation to protect reproductive rights, regulate firearms, and address police accountability.
Current Roles
- State Representative, 46th District
- Vice Chair of Civil Rights and Judiciary
- Serves on Community Safety, Justice and Re-Entry, and Capital Budget
- Appointed to Language Access Work Group (DSHS)
- Joint Legislative Committee on Health Care Oversight
