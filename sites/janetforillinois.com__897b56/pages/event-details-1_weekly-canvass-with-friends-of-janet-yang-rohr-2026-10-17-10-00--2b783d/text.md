Join us to knock on doors for State Representative Janet Yang Rohr in District 41!
RSVP Time & Location Oct 17, 2026, 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM Friends of Janet Yang Rohr Office, 475 River Bend Rd, Suite 102a, Naperville, IL 60540, USA Other dates Sat, Oct 24, 10:00 AM Sat, Oct 31, 10:00 AM About the event Join us to knock on doors for State Representative Janet Yang Rohr in District 41!
We’re talking to voters right here in our community about what’s at stake this November, making sure our neighbors turn out to the polls.
These conversations are the most effective way to reach as many voters as we can.
Don't worry if you haven't canvassed before, we'll properly train you at the office before you go!

We'll provide you with lit, pens, clipboards, and anything else you need.
Then you'll head out into the District!
Logistics: Please wear a comfortable pair of shoes and a fully charged phone (we are out there rain or shine!) Feel free to bring a water bottle and a bag to walk with, we'll provide the rest!
Parking is available at the office, but for overflow, please park at Rotary Hill.
