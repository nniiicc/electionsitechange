Dear Friends,
Over the years--as a school board member, precinct committeeperson, and in my role as State Representative--I've been guided by a simple belief: Public service means listening to each other, working hard, and finding real solutions for families like yours.
Whether it was knocking on doors, collecting petitions, or banding together to support candidates who share our values, I’ve seen that real progress happens when we join forces.
I’ve been honored to work alongside so many of you to strengthen our Democratic Party and elect Democrats up and down the ballot.
That organizing work matters, because when we win elections, we get things done for our community.
I’m deeply committed to ensuring our party always stands for what matters most to the people we serve, like expanding access to affordable health care, lowering costs for families, and investing in public schools that give every child a chance to thrive.
For me, it’s about rolling up our sleeves and making a difference together.
I would be honored to continue working for you to build a future that reflects our shared hopes and values.
Thank you for considering me to serve as your State Central Committeeperson.
Your partnership and trust mean the world to me, and I look forward to all the good we can accomplish together.
Warmly,
Janet Yang Rohr
