Monica Mowdy for State Representative ("the Campaign") is committed to protecting your privacy.
This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or interact with our campaign.
We may collect the following types of information:
- Contact information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address when you sign up for updates, volunteer, request a yard sign, or contact us
- Donation information: Donations are processed through ActBlue, a third-party platform.
We receive your name, address, employer, and occupation as required by federal and state campaign finance law
- Website usage data: We may collect non-personally identifiable information such as browser type, pages visited, and referring website through analytics tools
- To send you campaign updates, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities
- To respond to your questions and requests
- To coordinate volunteer activities and yard sign delivery
- To comply with campaign finance reporting requirements
- To improve our website and campaign communications
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.
We may share information with campaign staff, volunteers, and service providers who help us operate the campaign.
Donor information is reported to the appropriate election commissions as required by law, and these reports are public records.
Our website may contain links to third-party websites such as ActBlue (for donations) and social media platforms.
These sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.
We encourage you to review their policies before providing personal information.
We take reasonable measures to protect the personal information we collect.
However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
- You can unsubscribe from our email communications at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email
- You can request that we remove your personal information from our records by contacting us
- You can choose not to provide personal information, though this may limit your ability to participate in certain campaign activities
We may update this privacy policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
