Accepted Forms of Photo ID Tennessee driver's license or photo ID issued by the Tennessee Department of Safety and Homeland Security United States passport U.S. military photo ID Tennessee handgun carry permit with photo Federal or Tennessee state government employee photo ID Don't have a photo ID?

Step 3: Vote Early or on Election Day Tennessee offers an early voting period.
You don't have to wait until Election Day!
Early Voting Early voting typically begins # days before an election and ends # days before Election Day.
You can vote at any early voting location in your county during this period.
Check your county election commission for exact dates and locations.
Election Day On Election Day, you must vote at your assigned polling location.
Polls are open from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM local time across Tennessee.
If you're in line by 8:00 PM, you have the right to vote.
