Campaign Event Neighbors Gather at the Pleasant Hill Meet & Greet Monica spent the afternoon with neighbors at the Pleasant Hill Community Room, sharing conversations over food and listening to what's on the minds of District 25 residents.
The gathering brought together longtime locals and new supporters for a candid back-and-forth about healthcare access, the cost of living, and the future of public schools in the Upper Cumberland.
"These are the conversations that matter," Monica said.
"When neighbors sit down together and talk honestly about what our communities need, that's where real representation begins." See upcoming events → Campaign Milestone Monica Mowdy Files for Tennessee House District 25 Monica Mowdy officially filed her candidacy for Tennessee House District 25 at the Cumberland County Election Commission.
"This district deserves a representative who is here, available, and fighting for you every single day," Monica said.
"I'm running because the people of the Upper Cumberland deserve better -- and together, we're going to build something different." Monica brings years of service as a public school educator, United Methodist Church pastor, and community advocate to the race, with a focus on healthcare access , public education , and government accountability .
Learn more about Monica's story → Community Advocacy Upper Cumberland Residents Advocate at the State Capitol Monica joined a group of community members from across the Upper Cumberland for a day of advocacy at the Tennessee State Capitol.
The group met with legislators to discuss healthcare access, broadband expansion, and education funding for rural communities.
"When our community shows up and speaks together, lawmakers listen," Monica said.
"This is exactly the kind of engagement I want to see more of -- people making their voices heard in Nashville." Get involved with the campaign → Campaign Update Campaign Officially on the Ballot in Putnam County The campaign completed ballot qualification in both Cumberland and Putnam County, ensuring voters across all of District 25 will have the chance to vote for Monica Mowdy.
The campaign continues to build momentum with growing grassroots support from community members throughout the district.
