Neighbors Gather at the Pleasant Hill Meet & Greet
Monica spent the afternoon with neighbors at the Pleasant Hill Community Room, sharing conversations over food and listening to what's on the minds of District 25 residents.
The gathering brought together longtime locals and new supporters for a candid back-and-forth about healthcare access, the cost of living, and the future of public schools in the Upper Cumberland.
"These are the conversations that matter," Monica said.
"When neighbors sit down together and talk honestly about what our communities need, that's where real representation begins."
