I never planned to run for office, let alone seek a higher one.
But as I look at what is happening across Colorado, I feel compelled to speak up and take action.
Too many families are being pushed out by the rising cost of living, making home ownership harder to reach and the future less secure.
Parents are increasingly concerned that our schools are not providing the kind of education they want for their children.
As a Westminster City Councilor, I have fought for lower taxes, stronger public safety, and responsible spending.
Many of you know me as a "Water Warrior" because I worked to keep water rates affordable and opposed costly projects that would burden taxpayers.
I am running for House District 29 to defend taxpayers, protect local control, and make sure the voices of everyday Coloradans are heard.
Putting People First with Common Sense I fought to lower Westminster water rates, which became some of the highest in the country.
Serving as your City Councilor has been an honor.
I care deeply about our community, and every decision I make is guided by what I believe is best for the people who live, work, and raise their families here—even when that has meant standing alone in a 6–1 vote.
What I Believe ✓ I believe in supporting homeownership so families can build stability and a future. ✓ I believe in standing with law enforcement and keeping our neighborhoods safe. ✓ I believe in upholding the Constitution and the Bill of Rights. ✓ I believe our schools should focus on strong fundamentals that prepare our children for success. ✓ I believe parents should have the primary role in raising and guiding their children. ✓ I believe small businesses are the heart of our community and should be supported, not overburdened. ✓ I believe government should live within its means and be respectful of your tax dollars. ✓ I believe in using smart technology to serve you more efficiently and responsibly.
Caring for Our Community I support following city codes and long-term plans that help our city grow thoughtfully and responsibly.
I believe local decisions should stay local, and I will continue to stand up for Home Rule so our community has a voice.
I believe in being a good steward of our beautiful city, its natural resources, and wildlife.
I supported keeping the WHOS dog park off-leash because that's what many residents asked for and enjoy.
I have been a strong advocate for those impacted by airport noise and environmental concerns.
I supported clear radio-active education signage by the bridge headed for the Greenway Trail to help protect residents and visitors and educate them of the history there.
Being a Responsible Steward of Your Money. • I voted against spending $4 million to upgrade a historic building for a private business when parking and broader community benefit were concerns. • I raised concerns and voted against the contract for the costly new water treatment plant project that provides less capacity while still requiring additional upgrades to the existing plant. • I believe major expenses like a $58 million courthouse with interest should have gone to the voters for approval. • I have not supported tax increases because I know how rising costs affect families.
At the End of the Day My focus is simple: Listen to you.
Care about this community.
And lead with steady, common-sense decisions that put people first.
