Disclaimer *The Adams 12 School District information was obtained from the Colorado Department of Education.
Lori Goldstein became school board president in 2022, so the comparison is from school year 2021/22 to school year 2025/26 (except for the change in operating spending, where the most recently concluded school year data was unavailable, so the change comparison is from school year 2021/22 to school year 2024/25).
The Adams 12 Accreditation Rating dropped to “Accredited with Improvement Plan” for school year 2025/26, which is the lowest it has ever been according to published data.
This data represents a snapshot at the time it was taken at the beginning of August 2026.
The annual additional property tax increase for homeowners within Adams 12 is due to the recent additional passed mill levy assessment (Ballot Issue 5B mill levy overide) and is the additional amount a homeowner would currently pay annually for an average home valued at $661,000 (according to county assessed value).
Individual Adams 12 homeowners would pay more or less than that amount depending on the assessed value of their home.
