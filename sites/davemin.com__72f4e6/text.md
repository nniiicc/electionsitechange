Congressman Dave Min is leading the effort to hold Donald Trump and his cronies accountable in Washington, DC.
And he’ll continue this fight, because that’s just how he’s wired.
The son of Korean immigrants and a product of California public schools, Dave has spent his career fighting to preserve and expand the American Dream he was able to live .
Dave and his younger brother Andrew are the proud product of California public schools.
Dave went on to attend the University of Pennsylvania’s Wharton School and College, and then earned his law degree at Harvard.
He began his career prosecuting Wall Street fraud as an Enforcement Attorney at the SEC , and later worked as a law professor at UC Irvine before being elected to the California State Senate in 2020.
In 2024, he was elected to the United States Congress to represent California’s 47th Congressional District in one of the closest races in the country.
Since being sworn into Congress in January 2025, Dave has been fearless and tireless in standing up to the lawlessness of the Trump administration and trying to defend the values of diversity, inclusion, and economic opportunity that have been so demonized by MAGA extremists.
He has been a loud and consistent voice in calling out the cruel and often criminal actions of ICE agents and in standing up against Trump’s illegal and reckless war in Iran.
And he has continued to advocate for economic policies to help working families.
Dave has also been a leader on tackling the rampant corruption in DC, serving as the chair of the Congressional CPC's Anti-Corruption Task Force, and sponsoring legislation to end stock trading by members of Congress and Trump Administration insiders.
On the House Oversight Committee, Dave forced the release of the Epstein files to and has been leading the charge to hold Trump and his cronies accountable, being among the first to call for the impeachments of Kristi Noem, Pam Bondi and Pete Hegseth.
Before he was leading the fight in Congress, Dave was a remarkably effective legislator, passing 28 bills into law as a State Senator, including first in the nation bills to increase the supply of workforce housing, secure women’s access to reproductive healthcare, expand protections for domestic violence survivors, ban the sale of guns on all state property, and address the causes of our climate crisis.
With the passage of Proposition 50 last year, California’s 47th Congressional District has been significantly changed.
While the new CA-47 continues to include the cities of Irvine and Laguna Beach and parts of Newport Beach, 60% of this district is new, including the cities of Aliso Viejo, Dana Point, Laguna Hills, Laguna Niguel, Laguna Woods, Lake Forest, and Tustin.
