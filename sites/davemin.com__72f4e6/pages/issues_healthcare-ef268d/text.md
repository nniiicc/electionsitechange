Issues
Healthcare
I hear from people across the district that their healthcare costs are too high.
That’s why I voted against cuts to funding for Medicaid and Medicare.
When Congressional Republicans voted to hike premiums for millions of Americans by eliminating enhanced premium tax credits, I spoke out against their plan and voted to protect the tax credits that Californians rely on to afford their care.
These cuts are devastating for folks in our community.
I have heard firsthand from working families who can’t afford to fill the prescriptions they need or visit the doctor, who lost coverage, or who are taking on medical debt to seek the treatment they need to survive.
It is absolutely unacceptable that our community is suffering because Donald Trump wanted to fund tax breaks for billionaires.
It is imperative that Democrats take back the House so that we can fight for affordable healthcare.
I believe that we must restore the enhanced premium tax credits and also essential funding to Medicaid and Medicare.
I will continue to oppose any effort to raise your healthcare costs.
