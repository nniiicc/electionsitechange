Dave Min is running to continue to represent California’s 47th Congressional District.
With the passage of Prop 50, this district will include the Cities of Irvine, Dana Point, Aliso Viejo, Laguna Beach, Laguna Hills, Lake Forest, Laguna Niguel, Tustin, and Laguna Woods as well as portions of Mission Viejo and Newport Beach.
Dave Min currently represents this area in Congress.
