Brittany Is Stepping Up.
Three years ago, Brittany began participating in a series of community conversations about why working families are falling behind.
Those conversations became part of The Deciders, a documentary about everyday people realizing the system isn’t working for them. ​ In conversations across Columbus County and Robeson County, from rural towns to tribal communities, Brittany kept hearing the same thing: wages are too low, costs are too high, and too many elected leaders have stopped listening. ​ People from Columbus County to Robeson County asked for meetings.
They asked for help.
