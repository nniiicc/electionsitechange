MEET BERNIE
New Leadership Delivering Results for Our Community
I'm an Italian Catholic who was raised in New York by parents who instilled in me the values of love and faith.
I am a retired public high school counselor from Iowa.
I live in Sun City with my lifelong partner.
We have owned our home since 2010 and have lived here full-time for the past 2 years.
Prior, I had visited the Lowcountry yearly since the mid-1980's and had owned a timeshare on HHI.
I am a lifelong Democrat and a lifelong member of the National Education Association.
Although most of my experience is from Iowa, I have observed very similar patterns in these 2 GOP-led states.
I believe that I can bring that knowledge and first-hand experience to help lift this community.
I had a unique experience living in Iowa, being first in the nation (until recently), and being part of a caucus state.
My membership in a “Truman Club” gave me interaction with most Democratic political candidates in intimate home settings.
I was a Caucus captain for Hillary Clinton and a Caucus secretary for the 2020 election.
Prior to Kamala Harris' campaign ending, the campaign sought my personal endorsement as an education and labor leader.
As a teacher union president, I have spoken up for students, teachers, and public educators with administrators, political candidates, and school board members.
My colleague and I ran a breakout session at the NEA conference in Chicago for other states that are potentially losing collective bargaining rights.
As a school board member, I drafted resolutions to keep public money in public schools and to support our LGBTQ+ youth.
I spoke against vouchers at a public hearing at the Iowa Capitol.
I used my platform to raise awareness of bills affecting the school system, including those that included topics of DEI, book banning, and guns in schools.
As an LWV Board member, I worked several tables to help register citizens to vote, including following a naturalization ceremony.
I am eager to share my faith and love with this community.
