ABOUT KEN MCCARTY
Known by Relationships, Not Just Recognition
Ken McCarty has spent decades serving the Eagle River, Chugiak, and Peter’s Creek community through leadership, counseling, education, healthcare, and public service.
As the owner of Discovery Cove Counseling and a former Trustee of the Alaska Mental Health Trust, Ken has worked directly with individuals and families facing real-life challenges.
His experience as a State House Representative for District 13 gave him firsthand insight into the importance of responsive, accountable leadership that listens to the people it serves.
Beyond public office, Ken has remained deeply involved in the community through local organizations, youth programs, aviation advocacy, healthcare initiatives, and volunteer service.
Whether helping improve community spaces, supporting local schools, or attending community council meetings, Ken believes leadership starts with being present and involved.
Ken is committed to protecting Alaska values, supporting local families and businesses, and ensuring Eagle River, Chugiak, and Peter’s Creek continue to have a strong voice and dependable representation.

WHY I’M RUNNING
Eagle River, Chugiak, & Peter’s Creek Deserve More
I’m running because our community deserves leadership that listens, responds, and stays connected to the people it represents.
Eagle River, Chugiak, and Peter’s Creek are built on strong families, hard work, independence, and neighbors who take care of one another.
I believe leadership should reflect those same values through honesty, accountability, and real community involvement.
My goal is simple: protect Alaska values, support local businesses and families, strengthen our economy, and help build a stronger future for the next generation.
Leadership is not about politics alone; it’s about relationships, service, and showing up for the community.
