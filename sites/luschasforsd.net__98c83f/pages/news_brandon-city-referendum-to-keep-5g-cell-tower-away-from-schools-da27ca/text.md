Home Meet Manuel Platform Scorecard News Endorsements Photos Contact News / Brandon City Referendum to Keep 5G Cell Tower Away from Schools 9 Sep Wednesday, 3:19 PM · 2026 Brandon City Referendum to Keep 5G Cell Tower Away from Schools Brandon has a Referendum on the November 3 rd , 2026 ballot to keep a 5G cell phone tower from being built near two schools.
A “Yes” vote keeps the zoning of the land as Natural Resources to prevent the construction of the 5G cell tower: The land in question is at the corner of Park Street and S Sioux Blvd, right across from Brandon Valley Intermediate School (BVIS) and Robert Bennis Elementary School (RBE): The reasons that I support voting “Yes” on this Referendum are: To prevent the exposure of over 1,200 children in RBE and BVIS to cell tower radiation all day long.
Childrens Health Defense has a good summary of the issues: "In a February 2019 U.S.
Senate hearing , senior telecom executives admitted they have not done any safety testing on 5G, nor do they plan to do any.
Currently, there are over two dozen cities in the U.S. and countless cities abroad that have 5G, yet there is no scientific evidence to support any claim of safety for it.
What related research does show is cause for alarm.
Thousands of independent studies indicate adverse health impacts from wireless radiation .
These range from cancer and sterility to DNA damage.
The government’s human exposure guidelines haven’t been updated in more than 20 years, while radiation from cell phones, cordless phones, WiFi and wireless baby monitors has increased exponentially in that time.
More than 10,000 peer-reviewed scientific studies conducted by independent researchers from around the world demonstrate the harmful biological effects of wireless radiation.
Because of their developmental stages, children are much more susceptible.
In addition, wireless radiation effects are cumulative, putting children at greater risk." There are no gaps in cell coverage in this area.
There is a cell tower visible from this location looking up on Maple St. already.
This is one of the busiest intersections in town during school drop off and pick up.
It is basically a 1-way egress for BVIS.
This location is in a flood zone.
Husets, Oakridge, and other private parties have declined a cell tower on their property, so why should we put one next to our kids' schools?
Construction of a cell tower at this location will reduce surrounding property values.
The history of this Referendum is a great example of the Brandon community coming together to push back against out-of-town corporate interests trying to extract wealth while sacrificing our community, culture, and quality of life.
Let’s review the history of how this Referendum got on the ballot.
The Brandon City Council approved the rezoning of this parcel on November 3 rd , 2025.
Seven families, from differing political parties, mobilized to work as a team.
These families had 20 days to gather 400 signatures of registered voters in order to put the rezoning decision on the ballot.
On November 19 th , the families received confirmation from the City of Brandon that they had enough valid signatures to put the rezoning decision on the ballot.
This was four days before the 20 day deadline.
On December 3 rd , the cell tower company challenged some of the petition sheets.
They found that a notary had put the wrong date on some of the petition sheets.
This would have invalidated about 55 signatures.
On December 5 th , Brandon’s city attorney filed a referendum with the courts to correct the notary dates that were in error.
On December 10 th , the judge ruled that the dates could be corrected and that the petition sheets in question are valid.
I would like to personally extend a big THANK YOU to the Brandon families who took time away from their jobs and kids to bring this issue to a public vote.
