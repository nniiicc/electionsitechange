Meet Manuel
I am running for one of the South Dakota State House of Representatives seats for District 2 to represent YOU, my constituents, not lobbyists, not special interests, not corporations, nor billionaires.
I am a Constitutional Conservative Republican.
I am honest, hard-working, transparent, and straightforward.
My actions are consistent with my words.
To understand why I want to represent District 2 in the South Dakota State House of Representatives, you have to understand my story.
So, bear with me.
I moved my family from Silicon Valley to Brandon in 2020 when we lost our freedoms due to the lock-downs in California.
Brandon was the only school district in the country that I could find at the time where my kids could go to school full time, in person, and without masks.
I left friends, colleagues, neighbors, and church behind to start a new life in South Dakota, a state I had only briefly visited once before.
I started my life over at age 49 to regain my freedoms.
This is why I want to represent the residents of District 2.
No South Dakotan should ever have to leave his or her community to find freedom elsewhere.
I understand exactly how freedoms erode over time.
I have been there; I don’t want to do that again.
I’ve seen how big money and big corporate interests take control of all levels of government, down to the local city council.
This is why I am self-funding my campaign (instead of buying my wife a new car!).
I pledge to recuse myself from any votes where I have a personal, financial, or professional conflict of interest.
Money in politics is not good for freedom.
I have also seen how social media is being designed and used to make people addicted and unproductive.
There is a reason why many Silicon Valley executives and engineers who have built and understand social media companies refuse to give their kids a smart phone.
Someone much smarter than me said that "if it's free, you are the product." This is why we will not use social media for this campaign.
Truthfully, I do not want to be a politician.
I’d much rather be in a tractor on the farm.
The problem is that the outgoing Legislators have been voting for big money corporate interests, and these votes are threatening to erode our freedoms.
Check out the Scorecard for records of these votes.
So, I asked all my conservative neighbors, friends and colleagues if they would run for House of Representatives, District 2.
Understandably, they all have jobs, families and commitments that don’t allow them to take 10 weeks off.
Two years ago the three District 2 incumbents only had one challenger.
This can happen again if someone does not step up.
That someone apparently needs to be me.
Not taking any donations and not having a presence on social media means this campaign will be a bit different.
I do not have a professional campaign manager writing, printing and publicizing my content.
I won’t have paid campaign workers.
My family is helping me run this on a shoestring budget (remember it’s my wife’s new car fund!).
Thank God that I have a loving, supportive family.
Back to me - I love the South Dakota “all the things” way of life - community, family, faith, freedom.
I have learned to hunt, shoot, fish, and farm.
I drive farm semi-trucks, and I repair large farm machinery.
I live a free and inspired life in South Dakota, and I want to protect it for my children.
The first year after moving to South Dakota we drove our car around with "Thank God for South Dakota" written on the back.
I was educated in Germany as an RF / Microwave electrical engineer.
I have worked on high speed digital communication hardware for the last 22 years.
I still work on hardware part-time remotely for a company based out of Silicon Valley.
The majority of my time I farm – corn, beans, oats, cattle, chickens, and turkeys.
I have been married to my wife Susan for 23 years.
We are blessed with two hard-working daughters.
Soraya is studying Metallurgical Engineering at South Dakota School of Mines.
Sabrina is a high school sophomore interested in Marine Biology.
On the weekend, I am most likely fixing a car, repairing a chicken coop, playing a board game with my family, or playing on my D-league soccer team.
If I ever get a spare moment, I enjoy a good German beer.
I am running for the State House of Representatives to represent YOU, not corporate interests.
I would love to hear from you to learn what is important to you - so we can all continue to live a free and inspired life in South Dakota: LuschasForSD@gmail.com, 605-888-8579.
