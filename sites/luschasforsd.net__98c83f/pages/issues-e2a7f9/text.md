Increase severance taxes on natural resources.
A severance tax is a tax imposed on companies or individuals when they "sever" or extract natural resources from the land.
The idea is: When a non-renewable resource like oil, gas, gravel, or timber is permanently removed, a portion of its value should go back to the public.
South Dakota already has some limited severance taxes (e.g. gold and precious metals, energy metals, sand/gravel/rock), but the rates are too low.
I do not support out-of-state multi-million dollar companies coming into South Dakota to extract our natural resources without paying their fair share of taxes.
