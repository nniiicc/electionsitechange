Mayor Brett Barker to run for House District 51
Nevada, IA – Mayor of Nevada and Story County Republican Chair Brett Barker has announced his candidacy for Iowa House District 51, currently represented by Representative Dave Deyoe, who has announced his upcoming retirement.
Mayor Barker and his wife Tracy live in Nevada where they are raising their four daughters.
Brett is a pharmacist at NuCara Pharmacy and Senior Policy Advisor for the Iowa Pharmacy Association.
He has served as the Republican Party of Story County Chair since 2015.
Mayor Barker’s experience in public service began in 2011 as a member of the Nevada City Council.
“I want to thank Representative Deyoe for his many years of service to Iowa and to our House District!” said Mayor Barker.
“I am running for the Iowa House because my depth of experience in economic development, healthcare, rural revitalization, and foster care will help our state enact policies where families can thrive and the next generation is proud to call home.”
For more information about Brett and his campaign visit www.barkerforiowa.com.
