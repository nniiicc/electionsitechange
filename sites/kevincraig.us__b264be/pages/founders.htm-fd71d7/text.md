Is "Civil Government" "Ordained" By God?
Romans 13:1 famously says, "The powers that be are ordained of God." There are two meanings of the word " ordained ." Providential ordination Prescriptive ordination The word "ordained" can mean "authorized," "condoned," or "commanded." The claim of this essay is that God never "ordained" "civil government" in this way.
The word "ordained" can refer to God's providential acts in history.
