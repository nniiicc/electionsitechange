What Does He Believe?
I believe in the promise of America .
I believe in the Constitution and am committed to protecting our God-given unalienable rights and principles of federalism, limited government, separation of powers, and the rule of law.
The major parties are more committed to their own power than these principles, and that's why I am unaffiliated with any party (like more and more voters) and running as an independent.
Representatives should live in the districts they represent , be intimately familiar with the needs of their constituents , and focus their efforts on addressing those needs .
What are His Priorities?
The cost of living is rising and incomes are not keeping up.
Restoring the American Dream for the average person is my highest priority.
Our immigration system is broken and ICE is out of control .
We need to reform the system, and redirect ICE on efforts on finding and deporting dangerous criminals.
The MAGA movement has been coopted by "intellectuals" who are critical of our founding principles .
We need to recommit to those principles, not reject them.
It's time for the Two-Party System to die .
