AZURE DUAN
Azure Duan is not a career politician.
She’s a mom, a small-business owner, and a proud American who sees danger in the direction the radicals want to take our country.
Having lived under one-party control, she knows firsthand what it looks like when the government divides people and tramples freedom.
She’s running for Georgia House District 50 to defend the principles that made her American Dream possible: safe streets, strong schools, and a government that works for the people.
AZURE
ON THE ISSUES
AFFORDABILITY AND COST OF LIVING
With a family of her own, Azure knows that costs are rising and the struggles families are facing.
She will stand for policies that bring real relief for Georgia families.
In the State House, she’ll:
- Provide property tax relief and state income tax relief
- Push back on fees and policies that drive up everyday costs
- Support negotiating with insurance companies to reduce premiums
SAFE STREETS & STRONG COMMUNITIES
Azure knows that nothing matters more than keeping our families safe.
She has stood with law enforcement, even raising funds for the Johns Creek Police Department when others turned their backs.
In the State House, she’ll:
- Back the blue with the training, staffing, and tools they need
- Keep violent offenders and repeat criminals off our streets
- Crack down on crime tied to illegal immigrations, protecting victims over criminals
PARENTS IN CHARGE OF EDUCATION
As a mom, Azure believes parents should decide what’s best for their children.
She will:
- Protect parental rights and transparency in schools Expand school choice with charter schools and vouchers so every child can succeed
- Keep classrooms focused on reading, math, science, and civics
- Defend fairness in girls’ sports and protect opportunities for young women
STANDING UP FOR SMALL BUSINESSES
Azure built her own business from the ground up.
She knows what red tape and over-taxation can do to entrepreneurs.
She will:
- Fight to eliminate double taxation like annual inventory taxes
- Cut unnecessary regulations that suffocate growth
- Promote a safe, pro-business environment so local jobs thrive
RESPONSIBLE SPENDING, NOT POLITICAL VANITY PROJECTS
Families balance their checkbooks and the government should too.
Azure will:
- Prioritize core services like public safety and infrastructure
- Oppose irresponsible spending
- Say no to tax hikes and ensuring accountability
