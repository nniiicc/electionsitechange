Carol Glanville is a lifelong resident of West Michigan who has built her career as an educator and fierce local advocate for West Michigan.
Since flipping a Republican stronghold in 2022, she has secured more than $75 million in state investments for her district supporting schools, fire stations, community centers, and programs like Meals on Wheels.
And she’s championed policies that improve the cost of living: eliminating the retirement tax, expanding the working families tax credit, and establishing universal Pre-K.
Democrats came within 400 votes of winning this seat in 2022, and Senate District 30 is considered Michigan’s top seat to flip in 2026.
Carol has a proven record of winning tough races.
Your support makes her victory that much more likely.
Carol is a former teacher and school administrator, so she knows the challenges students face and the tremendous work teachers put in—and they need more support.
While Republicans propose unprecedented school cuts, Carol has fought back to ensure classrooms have the resources they need.
The cost of living in West Michigan continues to go up.
For too many, income isn’t keeping up with bills.
Carol has pushed for changes that make a real difference, especially for working families and small businesses.
From medical debt relief to medical research, Carol has secured millions of dollars to make our home a leader in providing healthcare today and finding breakthroughs for tomorrow.
