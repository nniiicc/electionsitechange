Help for Struggling Families
- Stand with the neighbors still struggling to get back into their homes after the latest storms, and cut through the delays that keep families out of their own houses.
- Hold insurance companies accountable for excessive rate increases and crack down on the fraud that drives up premiums.
- Work to bring new insurance companies to Pinellas and increase competition to bring down premiums.
- Deliver real property-tax relief so no family gets priced out of a home over a tax bill.
- Address the permitting delays and red tape at every level of government to reduce the cost of homes and apartments built in Pinellas.
