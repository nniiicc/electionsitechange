These are Melissa’s six commitments to Pinellas families.
“What frustrates me about Tallahassee is that good ideas aren’t becoming great ideas — the intent gets lost in the way bills are written and implemented.” — Melissa Rutland That is why Pinellas needs a legislator who has actually done the work.
Melissa has spent over two decades writing, reading, and delivering complicated projects — so she knows the difference between a bill that only sounds good and a bill that makes a difference.
Help for Struggling Families Stand with the neighbors still struggling to get back into their homes after the latest storms, and cut through the delays that keep families out of their own houses.
Hold insurance companies accountable for excessive rate increases and crack down on the fraud that drives up premiums.
Work to bring new insurance companies to Pinellas and increase competition to bring down premiums.
Deliver real property-tax relief so no family gets priced out of a home over a tax bill.
Address the permitting delays and red tape at every level of government to reduce the cost of homes and apartments built in Pinellas.
End the Corruption and Restore Accountability Hold elected officials to a clear standard: public office is a public trust, never a path to personal gain.
Ban elected officials from using insider information to get rich at the public’s expense.
Require full disclosure when politicians do business with developers.
Support the audits and transparency to show taxpayers where every dollar goes.
Protect the Pinellas Way of Life Support thoughtful growth that respects the character and scale of our neighborhoods and puts the people who already live here first.
Ensure land-use decisions are grounded in the community and guided by local insight to protect our waterfront, parks, and arts culture that make Pinellas special.
Protect the Pinellas creative economy that supports jobs in arts and culture.
Support small businesses, attract companies that create high-paying jobs, and build a plentiful workforce of skilled workers.
Smart Infrastructure Modernize stormwater, drainage, roads, and utilities so neighborhoods are more resilient.
Prepare for the next 100 years instead of reacting to the last storm.
Make sure the laws passed in Tallahassee are properly written, funded, and implemented so they actually work here at home.
Strengthening Education Give every child a strong start, with early-reading support so kids are reading at grade level by third grade.
Fully fund public schools, with top pay and the best training for teachers to serve our students well.
Back parents with real choices and have real accountability.
Expand workforce training that responds to local employers’ needs and creates pathways to good-paying jobs here in Pinellas.
Clean Water, Resilient Coast Protect Tampa Bay and our beaches with real investment in clean water and modern stormwater.
Build a coast that stands up to the next storm for future generations.
Preserve the parks and waterfront that make Pinellas our home.
