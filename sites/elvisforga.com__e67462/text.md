02 Meet Elvis The son of immigrants, raised on faith, family, and hard work.
Elvis Casely comes from a background rooted in hard work, accountability, and service.
As the son of immigrants, he learned early that opportunity in America is real — but it has to be earned, protected, and passed on.
His career in sales and marketing put him face-to-face with everyday families and small businesses across Georgia, listening to their frustrations with a government that too often forgets them.
That experience is the foundation of this campaign.
