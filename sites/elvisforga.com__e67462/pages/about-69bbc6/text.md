Meet Elvis 01 Rooted in Family The son of immigrants, raised on faith, family, and hard work.
Elvis Casely comes from a background rooted in hard work, accountability, and service.
As the son of immigrants, he learned early that opportunity in America is real — but it has to be earned, protected, and passed on.
His career in sales and marketing put him face-to-face with everyday families and small businesses across Georgia, listening to their frustrations with a government that too often forgets them.
That experience is the foundation of this campaign.
02 Faith, Family & Hard Work A background rooted in hard work, accountability, and service.
He is running so his son — and every child in District 35 — can grow up in a Georgia where families are supported, ownership means ownership, and the people they elect actually do the job.
03 Professional Experience A career in sales and marketing, face-to-face with the families and small businesses of Georgia.
That experience taught Elvis to listen first — to hear people's real frustrations with a government that too often forgets them, and to turn those conversations into a plan.
04 Why Public Service?
Representation is a responsibility.
Too often, public office is treated like a title or a paycheck instead of a responsibility.
Representation means being present, listening to constituents, and doing the hard work every single day.
Elvis Casely is running to bring energy, transparency, and real engagement back to state government.
Why District 35?
For the next generation of Georgians.
Elvis wants his son to grow up in a Georgia where families are supported, opportunity is real, and elected leaders show up to do their jobs.
This campaign is about leaving the district stronger than we found it.
