The Problem Property taxes force families, seniors, and working Georgians to keep paying for something they already own, making it harder to build wealth and stay in their communities.
The Approach I support eliminating property taxes and replacing that revenue with fairer, more transparent alternatives that do not punish ownership.
By doing this, we can strengthen families, protect homeowners from being priced out, and make Georgia a place where ownership truly means ownership.
02 Income Tax Keep More Of What You Earn Eliminate Income Tax Hard work should be rewarded, not penalized.
The Problem Income taxes are taken directly from paychecks, limiting a family's ability to save, invest, and build a better future.
The Approach I support eliminating the state income tax so Georgians can keep more of what they earn and decide how to spend it.
When people have more money in their pockets, local businesses grow, jobs are created, and our economy becomes stronger from the ground up.
03 Education Parents Deserve Transparency Parental Rights & School Transparency Parents deserve to know what their children are being taught in school.
The Problem Families are too often left out of decisions about curriculum and classroom content, with limited access to what is actually being taught.
The Approach I support making school curriculum easy for parents to access, giving families reasonable options when content doesn't align with their values, and encouraging open communication between schools and parents.
Education works best when parents and teachers work together.
04 Criminal Justice Protect Our Children Toughest Penalties for Crimes Against Children Georgia must take a firm, uncompromising stance to protect its most vulnerable.
The Problem Those who commit the most severe acts of sexual abuse against a child forfeit their right to live in a civilized society, yet penalties do not always reflect the severity of these crimes.
The Approach I will fight to make the most horrific crimes against children punishable by death in the state of Georgia.
I will push for legislation that delivers real justice, prioritizes the safety of our children, and sends a clear message that these crimes will not be tolerated under any circumstances.
05 State Assistance Don't Penalize Families End the Marriage Penalty in State Assistance Getting married should never make life harder financially.
The Problem Right now, some assistance programs can reduce or eliminate benefits when couples get married — punishing families for making a commitment to each other.
The Approach I want to fix that so families are not punished for making a commitment to each other.
Strong families lead to stronger communities, better outcomes for kids, and greater long-term stability.
06 Consumer Rights Products Should Just Work End Forced Subscription Models When you buy something, it should work without extra monthly fees.
The Problem Too many companies now sell products like cars, printers, or appliances and then lock basic features behind subscriptions.
That's frustrating and unfair.
The Approach I support clear rules that protect consumers and make sure products work as promised, without surprise charges.
07 Digital Ownership If You Buy It, You Own It True Digital Ownership If a company says you “buy” a digital product, you should own it.
The Problem Movies, books, games, and other digital purchases shouldn't disappear later because of licensing changes or corporate decisions.
The Approach People deserve honesty and clarity when they spend their money, whether the product is physical or digital.
08 Health Reward Healthy Living Make Gym Memberships Tax-Deductible The tax code should support people who make the effort to take care of their health.
The Problem Staying active helps prevent serious health problems and lowers healthcare costs, yet the tax code does nothing to encourage it.
The Approach I want to make gym memberships, fitness classes, and exercise programs tax-deductible.
This is a simple way to encourage healthier habits and help families save money — not just on taxes, but on future health ailments they'll avoid by staying active.
