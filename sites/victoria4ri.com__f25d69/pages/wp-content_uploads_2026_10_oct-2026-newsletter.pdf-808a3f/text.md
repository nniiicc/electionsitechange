RSVP at victoria4ri.com/events Recap: Westerly Rail Advocates Happy Hour We had a great happy hour with the Westerly Rail Advocates to give updates on our efforts to bring more frequent, affordable service to Westerly Station.
RIDOT is awaiting the next round of Federal Railroad Administration grant funding to open up to apply for mini-high platforms at Westerly Station, and Rep.
Magaziner has put in an earmark request.
The vision is straightforward: better regional rail connecting southeastern Connecticut west toward New Haven and New York and east toward Westerly, Providence and Boston.
There will be many opportunities to make your voice heard: I encourage you to sign up for action alerts at westerlyrailadvocates.com In southern CT and RI, we already share an economy, a workforce and the Northeast Corridor.
Now we need rail service that reflects the region we already are. 🚆 🇺🇸 Wood-Pawcatuck Watershed Celebration @ Grey Sail There were many great conservation projects on display at Grey Sail including invasive species management.
Thanks in part to Senator Sosnowski’s legislation, RIDEM has begun treatment, along with a new plant survey and a boat greeter to educate visitors about preventing the spread of the invasive hydrilla.
The federal government has also allocated $500,000 for hydrilla treatment, building on a $250,000 SNEP grant for watershed protection.
Protecting Wordens Pond means protecting the headwater of the Pawcatuck River—and it’s great to see this work becoming reality.

LIHEAP: Low-Income Heating Assistance Program A lot of people I talked to are starting to fill up their tanks with heating oil for the winter, and we are definitely experiencing sticker shock.
Please share this with a neighbor, friend, or family member who might benefit.
Rhode Island’s Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) can help eligible households pay heating costs.
You may qualify if your household income is at or below 60% of Rhode Island’s median income for your household size.
You do not need to be receiving public assistance, and you can rent or own your home.
How to apply: The 2026–27 application period is now open.
Apply online or contact your local Community Action Agency for help with the application. 👉 Learn more and apply: https://dhs.ri.gov/programs-and-services/energyassistance-programs-heating/low-income-home-energy-assistance-1 View email in browser
