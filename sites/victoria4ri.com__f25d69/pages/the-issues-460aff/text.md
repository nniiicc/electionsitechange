Building A Community That Works For Everyone
We have unique challenges in our coastal towns (Westerly, Charlestown, South Kingstown).
First, we’re a coastal community in the time of sea level rise and stronger storms.
Second, we are seeing fewer families here and more out-of-towners who only come during the summer.
The cost of everything: housing, gas, food, childcare, and healthcare, is stretching many families’ budgets.
I will continue to work hard so that everyone who has roots here can afford to stay here.
Highlights from my work:
Protecting Access to the Shore
- Passed a law requiring oceanfront rentals to disclose Rhode Island’s public shoreline access rights
- Passed a law protecting against new parking reductions near rights-of-ways to the water
Climate & Environmental Leadership
- Passed the Act on Coasts to strengthen climate resilience planning
- Helped secure $5 million in additional Green Bond funding for flood and sea level rise projects
- Passed a law requiring 60 days’ notice before nonrenewal of homeowners’ insurance policies, giving homeowners more time to find coverage in a more difficult coastal insurance market
Mental Health & AI Safety
- Added Medicaid reimbursement for school psychologists and social workers
- Added the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline to middle and high school student IDs
- Led AI safety legislation requiring chatbot companions to include safeguards for users in crisis
Priorities for the Next Term
- Reform for-profit utilities like RI Energy so they don’t price-gouge customers on delivery costs
- Upgrade Westerly Station for more frequent, affordable transportation options
- Establish sustainable funding for community paramedicine to improve access to healthcare
The cost of everything: housing, gas, food, healthcare, childcare, is stretching many families’ budgets.
We need to tackle each of these issues in order to make sure that people with roots here can afford to stay here.
