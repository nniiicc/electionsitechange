At the urging of our local libraries, my colleagues and I presented a number of bills in Senate Education committee this week including: S-2514 - I'm sponsoring this bill to require fair contracts and prices for e-books and audiobooks sold to libraries.
Currently publishers are price-gouging libraries, making them pay up to 9x the retail price for an e-book for a limited number of uses before they have to buy it again at the inflated price.
I'm cosponsoring with Senator Mark McKenney: S-2281 Freedom to Read Encourages and protects the freedom of public libraries to acquire materials without limitations and prohibits materials being removed from public libraries due to partisan or doctrinal disapproval Libraries are the hub of many communities- a place where anyone regardless of background has free access to knowledge and community programs.
Glad to support our local libraries this week and every week.
Also pictures: RI State Senator Mark McKenney Seal Release at Blue Shutters Beach I look forward to Mystic Aquarium's seal rehabilitation & release every year at Blue Shutters Beach in Charlestown.
There has been a huge increase in the number of seals in Narragansett Bay & Block Island, signaling that wildlife protections are helping the population get back to its historic levels.
There are plenty of hazards at sea, though.
Here's what they said about the rescued seals: "Nugget" Nugget is a yearling male harp seal who stranded at Napatree Conservation Area in Westerly, RI.
The seal was minimally responsive and dehydrated and admitted to Mystic Aquarium’s Animal Rescue Clinic on February 4, 2024.
Now weighing around 31kg, Nugget has recovered well in rehabilitation and is ready for release!
"Shamrock" Shamrock is a juvenile female harbor seal who was initially reported stranded in Essex, MA.
Although she was active initially, the seal had a large, open wound on her left flank.
The seal was rescued on December 15, 2023 and was transferred to Mystic Aquarium’s Animal Rescue Clinic.
Shamrock’s wound has healed and she is now ready for release!
