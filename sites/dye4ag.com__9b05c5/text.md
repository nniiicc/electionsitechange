Read the Full Platform Michigan Patient Protection Plan When a Denial Kills, We Follow the Decision Michigan families should not have to fight an anonymous algorithm for medically necessary care.
As Attorney General, Jason Dye will establish a Patient Protection and Corporate Accountability Initiative to investigate dangerous denial practices, expose hidden financial incentives, challenge health-care monopolies, scrutinize nonprofit hospitals, protect medical whistleblowers, and follow life-and-death decisions from the denial code to the people and policies behind it.
He will also fight for legislation requiring two-hour emergency decisions, named and qualified human reviewers, complete algorithmic audit trails, meaningful civil remedies, and criminal accountability when a knowing or reckless deprivation of urgently necessary covered care causes death or catastrophic injury.
A denial is not automatically a crime.
But paperwork is not immunity.
