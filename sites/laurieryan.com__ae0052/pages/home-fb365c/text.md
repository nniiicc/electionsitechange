My 1st bill will be to prohibit any extra fees/taxes/charges for NY projects from being added to our utility bills which will eliminate approximately 55% cost burden to users.
My 2nd bill will be to equalize our individual state income tax brackets.
My 3rd bill will be to re-formulate property taxes and rename them service taxes.
My 4th bill will be to eliminate or drastically reduce school taxes for home owners 65 and older.
My 5th bill will to eliminate NYS tax on gas the first and 15th of every month.
My 6th bill will be to allow survivng spouses to collect their spouse's pension at 62 whether their spouse retired or not.
My 7th bill will be to prohibit solar installations on water and to prohibit the clearing of forests to install solar unless on private property excluding farmland with contingencies.
