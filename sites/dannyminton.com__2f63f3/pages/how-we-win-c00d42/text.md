The Math On paper, Texas's new 6th District looks safely Republican.
In a straight re-run of 2024 under the new lines, Trump and Cruz both carry it comfortably.
But seven converging factors — some structural, some environmental, some specific to this candidate and this cycle — give Danny Minton a realistic path to 51%.
None are decisive alone.
Together, they describe a district and a moment where the range of plausible outcomes is wider than the baseline number suggests.
Danny is on track to build the coalition of voters that delivers a victory in November.
