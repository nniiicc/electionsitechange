Who Can Vote by Mail?
In Texas, you can ask for a mail ballot if you:
- are 65 or older on Election Day
- are sick or disabled
- are expecting to give birth within three weeks before or after Election Day
- will be away from your county during early voting and on Election Day
- are civilly committed under Chapter 841 of the Texas Health and Safety Code
- are confined in jail, but otherwise eligible
Your application must be received by your county by Friday, October 23.

Common Questions
What if I’m in line when the polls close at 7 p.m.?
Stay in line.
If you are in line by 7 p.m., you can still vote.
What if my name isn’t on the list at my polling place?
Ask the poll worker to help you.
If they still can’t find you, you have the right to cast a provisional ballot.
Can I use my phone in the voting booth?
No.
Texas doesn’t allow phones or other wireless devices within 100 feet of voting stations.
You can bring printed or handwritten notes.
