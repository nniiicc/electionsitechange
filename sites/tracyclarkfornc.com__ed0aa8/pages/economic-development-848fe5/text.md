Guilford County’s population has increased by 8% over the past decade and 34% of residents are considered low-income (less than $62,400 for a family of four).
We have a 3.8% unemployment rate, higher than the 3.4% rate statewide.
(Source)
We must do more to support job creation across all sectors and of all entry levels for our county.
I am a huge supporter of recent large-scale investments to welcome companies such as HondaJet, Boom, JetZero, and Toyota.
At the same time, I’m a huge proponent of building up our entrepreneurial base with investments in hubs such as the Nussbaum Center for Entrepreneurship.
I have my MBA from Kenan-Flagler Business School.
My father started a wealth management company in 1980 that grew to 75 employees when I was on the board and helped to sell it in 2023.
Thus, I understand macro and microeconomics and the need for prudent financial decisions to strategically grow our economy.
HB92 is a full-circle moment for me as my family had a wealth management firm for 43 years here in North Carolina, and I oversaw marketing for the firm for 5 years prior to holding this seat.
Thus, I believe I am particularly competent in speaking out against this bill that allows our State Treasurer to invest up to 5% of our general fund and state pension funds in crypto!
It passed the House but thankfully got stuck in the Senate.
