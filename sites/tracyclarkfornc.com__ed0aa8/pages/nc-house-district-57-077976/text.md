Economic Development Healthcare Housing Public Education Contact/Support NC House District 57 NC House District 57 is shown in the map here and includes the northern areas of Greensboro in Guilford County.
The district cuts through Lake Brandt and Lake Townsend where I love to trail run on the weekends and includes Bryan Park where my husband and I love to golf.
We live in the Lake Jeanette area squarely in this district.
My family has lived here for the past six years and we love it here!
I strongly believe that I am the perfect person to represent this district since we are a young family with two children in public school in our district (Jesse Wharton Elementary School and plans to attend Mendenhall Middle School and Page High School).
My husband works at Cone Hospital, which is just outside of our district but a key employer of many in our district.
I will be a staunch supporter for key issues of those in my district.
Everyone in our district is impacted by our local economy and inflation.
We must protect our beautiful nearby lakes, parks and wildlife.
We must invest in and fast-track housing developments of all types to provide affordable housing options for all residents.
We need to solve the food deserts in the eastern side of my district, resulting in devastating food insecurity issues.
