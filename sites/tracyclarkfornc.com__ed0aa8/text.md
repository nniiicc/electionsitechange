In August 2024, I was elected by Guilford County Democratic Party leaders and appointed by Gov.
Cooper to finish my predecessor’s term and replace her on the ballot.
That November, I was proudly elected by an overwhelming 68% of voters - thank you to the 31,986 voters who put their trust and faith behind me!
I have worked tirelessly this biennium to represent ALL of my constituents and I am proudly unopposed this Midterm, thus will continue serving as your NC House Rep. for the 2026-2028 term!
Driven by Results: In my first legislative term, I: Served as co-chair of the freshmen caucus (12 members) Led the creation of HB250 , supporting active military business owners while deployed, which passed the NC House unanimously and signed into law within SB445: Regulatory Reform Act of 2026 .
Primary sponsored 35 bills (aka bills I led the creation/introduction) Cosponsored 290 bills (10+ ultimately became law) Helped secure $10M+ for Guilford County in the budget (GTCC; water/wastewater; Nussbaum; Folk Fest) Hosted five town halls, attended 100+ events/meetings/tours, and personally responded to 1k+ constituent emails Gave five floor speeches, eight press conferences, and 25+ media interviews Raised $# for my campaign and the NC House Democratic caucus so that we can hopefully regain the majority by 2030.
