Federal dollars limited to LEA-run public schools; traceable, non-transferable to non-LEA entities.
Promotion/retention Varies widely by state/district; social promotion common; minimal due process.
Promotion contingent on mastery; mandatory remediation plans, parent notice, appeals, and disparity monitoring.
Graduation pathways Often single academic track or fragmented CTE; credit loss when switching.
Three equal pathways (Academic/Technical/Artistic), credit portability matrices, counseling support to prevent delays.
Assessments High-stakes annual tests; instructional distortion and time drain.
Quarterly, low-burden evaluations to improve instruction; 2% time cap; validated alternatives allowed; bias review.
Grading practices Curves and quotas persist in places.
Bans curves that force failure/success independent of mastery.
Classroom feedback Irregular surveys; opaque handling of complaints.
360° reviews from students/parents/teachers/admin; anonymized public aggregates; response timelines.
Teacher compensation Opaque scales; uneven by district; slow alignment to market.
Market-benchmarked pay; public reporting; equity requirements; mediation within bargaining cycles.
Resource equity Property-tax dependence drives gaps; minimal national standards.
National minimum standards for facilities/tech/materials/extracurriculars; phased compliance over 7 years.
Enforcement Limited federal leverage; audits inconsistent; long-tail noncompliance.
OPEA audits; corrective action; court-approved, due-process receivership with limited scope, reviews, and exit criteria.
School meals Means-tested; stigma and administrative friction.
Universal, free, nutritionally complete meals; allergy/cultural protocols; no stigma; à la carte revenue restricted.
Professional development Inconsistent quality; unpaid time; limited mentorship.
Job-embedded PD, paid time, two-year mentorship, micro-credentials recognized with differentials and impact reporting.
Technology standards Patchwork devices/connectivity; weak sustainability.
Minimum tech standards; campus broadband + off-campus supports; Buy American, repairability, e-waste rules.
Digital literacy Uneven integration; limited AI/privacy education.
K–12 standards for media literacy, cybersecurity, data privacy, coding, and AI literacy.
Data privacy FERPA baseline; broad vendor variation.
Consent for non-instructional data, minimization, one-year retention limit, breach reporting, DPOs, vendor audits, ad/tracking bans.
Mental health High ratios; access gaps; ad hoc telehealth.
Staffing ratios (1:250 counselors, 1:500 psychologists) phased in; trauma-informed care; secure telehealth; FERPA/HIPAA compliance.
Curriculum transparency Limited public visibility; slow updates.
Publish curricula/syllabi online; 30-day update rule; evidence-based panels resolve disputes; beliefs discussed, facts intact.
Public dashboards Scattered reporting; hard to compare.
Quarterly, disaggregated dashboards on mastery, pay equity, resource parity, mental health access, digital literacy.
Teacher pipeline Shortages; costly preparation; mobility barriers.
Scholarships, paid residencies, loan forgiveness (5 years in high-need schools), housing support, credential reciprocity.
Special populations Fragmented supports; uneven outcomes.
Dedicated funding for EL, gifted, homeless, foster youth; civil rights outcome monitoring and corrective action.
Safety and climate Policy variance; uneven implementation.
Restorative justice, anti-bullying, trauma-informed discipline; bias-free civil rights alignment; facility safety inspections.
Facilities & transportation Deferred maintenance; diesel fleets; accessibility gaps.
Capital grants for HVAC, water, accessibility, modernization; cleaner buses; 7-year phased compliance.
Community schools Limited integration with health/social services.
Encouraged integration; federal partnerships; biennial impact evaluations.
Pilots & research Ad hoc; uneven evaluation.
Pilots authorized; IES longitudinal studies; 10-year reauthorization with independent evaluation.
Collective bargaining Contracts vary; alignment slow.
Existing CBAs respected; future CBAs align to benchmarks; mediation/arbitration when compliance is blocked.
Charter treatment Eligibility varies by program; transparency inconsistent.
LEA-run charters treated as public and eligible; independent charters not funded under this Act; equivalent transparency if funded elsewhere.
State waivers Wide discretion; mixed guardrails.
Innovation waivers allowed with equal-or-better equity/accountability, independent evaluation, 5-year sunset; core guardrails intact.
Rulemaking deadlines Often open-ended; delayed guidance.
Interim guidance at 180 days; final regs at 365; default standards apply if deadlines slip.
Interagency coordination Siloed USDA/FCC/HHS/FTC/EPA actions.
Council mandates joint guidance, shared data standards, annual public reports, and quarterly stakeholder meetings.
Legal durability Vulnerable to piecemeal challenges.
Severability clause; Spending Clause conditioning; due-process receivership; explicit preemption where needed.
Effective date Program-specific start times.
