To align the rapid growth of AI and data center infrastructure with measurable community benefits, enforceable energy efficiency standards, and transparent public reporting — ensuring that host communities see tangible returns in jobs, infrastructure, and environmental stewardship.
- Economic Context: AI workloads and hyperscale data centers are expanding rapidly, often locating in rural or semi‑urban areas.
- Community Concerns: High energy and water consumption, grid strain, limited local hiring, and minimal reinvestment.
- Policy Gap: Existing incentives focus on attraction, not long‑term benefit or transparency.
- Sponsor Qualifications: 25+ years in mission‑critical IT, manufacturing, and public‑sector systems; Top Secret clearance holder; experienced in uptime reporting, disaster recovery, and compliance auditing.
-
Community Benefit Agreements (CBAs)
- Mandatory for incentives or fast‑track permitting.
- ≥ 30% construction and ≥ 40% permanent staff from host county.
- Workforce training partnerships with local institutions.
- ≥ 0.5% gross revenue annually to a local community fund.
-
Energy Efficiency & Renewable Integration
- PUE ≤ 1.4 within 3 years; “Gold Tier” ≤ 1.2 at launch.
- ≥ 50% renewable within 5 years; ≥ 80% within 10 years.
- Waste‑heat recovery where feasible.
- On‑site energy storage to offset peak‑load spikes.
-
Infrastructure & Public Services Support
- Proportional contributions to roads, fiber, substations.
- Broadband spillover to local ISPs at cost.
- Emergency services funding proportionate to facility risk.
-
Transparency & Public Dashboards
- Quarterly updates on energy, hiring, investment, environmental metrics.
- Auditable by the Department of Commerce.
-
Incentives
- 5% tax credit for exceeding benchmarks by ≥ 10%.
- Fast‑track permitting for “Gold Tier” projects.
- Grant eligibility for R&D in AI cooling, storage, and integration.
-
Enforcement
- Fines up to $50,000/day for willful violations.
- Loss of incentives and public non‑compliance flag.
- Federal court enforceability of CBAs.
- Local Governments: Guaranteed jobs, infrastructure upgrades, and annual investment funds.
- Industry Operators: Predictable permitting and incentives; improved public perception.
- Utilities: Coordinated grid planning; reduced peak strain; renewable integration.
- Workforce Boards: Funded training pipelines into high‑paying tech jobs.
- Environmental Advocates: Enforceable efficiency and renewable targets; waste‑heat reuse.
- 0–6 Months: Rulemaking, CBA template, dashboard standards.
- 6–18 Months: First projects under new standards; initial audits.
- Year 3: PUE compliance check; renewable progress review.
- Year 5: Mid‑term evaluation; adjust benchmarks as needed.
