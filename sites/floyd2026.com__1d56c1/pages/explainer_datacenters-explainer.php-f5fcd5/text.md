The Community Data Benefit Act makes sure that when a data center sets up in Indiana, it: Hires local people for construction and permanent jobs.
Trains our workforce in high‑tech skills.
Invests in our community every year.
Runs on clean, efficient energy that doesn’t overload our grid.
Upgrades local infrastructure like roads, broadband, and emergency services.
Reports everything publicly so we can see the results.
Why I Wrote It I’ve spent 25+ years building and securing mission‑critical systems — including for Fort Knox and the U.S.
Census Bureau — and I’ve been vetted for and held a Top Secret clearance .
I know how to make technology work securely, efficiently, and transparently.
This bill is about making sure Indiana gets jobs, skills, and infrastructure — not just higher electric bills.
How It Works Community Benefit Agreements : Legally binding promises on jobs, training, and investment before a shovel hits the ground.
Energy Standards : Efficiency targets, renewable power, and waste‑heat reuse.
Public Dashboards : Quarterly updates on hiring, energy use, and community spending.
Incentives for the Best : Tax credits and fast‑track permitting for projects that go above and beyond.
Penalties for Breaking Promises : Fines, loss of incentives, and public non‑compliance flags.
What It Means for You More local jobs and training for high‑paying tech careers.
Better infrastructure paid for by the companies using it.
Cleaner energy and smarter grid use.
