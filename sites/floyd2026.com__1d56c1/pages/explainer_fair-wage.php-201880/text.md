Fair Wage for All Act
This bill ends the federal tip credit loophole and guarantees full minimum wage for every worker—no exceptions.
It also creates a support fund for rural businesses to help them transition without layoffs or closures.
Key Features
- 💵 Eliminates the federal tip credit—no more sub-minimum wages
- 🏘️ Creates a Rural Wage Transition Fund to support small businesses
- 📊 Transparent reporting on wage compliance and fund usage
- 🛠️ Includes technical assistance for payroll upgrades and compliance
- 🧑🌾 Prioritizes rural counties with high service-sector employment
Why It Matters
No worker should depend on unpredictable tips to survive.
This bill guarantees fair pay and protects rural employers from being left behind.
