Democratic Conference Retreat Brings Leaders Together
I had the opportunity to join my colleagues this weekend at the Democratic Caucus retreat at Sandia Resort and Casino.
It was a valuable time to connect, share ideas, and prepare for the important work ahead on behalf of New Mexicans.
Later in the evening, I attended the Speaker’s fundraiser dinner where we came together in support of strong leadership and a vision for a better future for our state.
I’m grateful for the chance to learn, collaborate, and continue building momentum for the upcoming session!
