The meetings can be attended in-person or online via Zoom.
No final decisions have been made concerning this proposal .
These public meetings will be critical to help guide any future decisions throughout this process.
Neighborhood-Focused Public Meeting* Friday, November 7 6:00 to 7:30 p.m.
New Mexico Farm & Ranch Heritage Museum 4100 Dripping Springs Road Las Cruces, N.M.
88011 ONLINE OPTION: Register to join via Zoom *The goal of this first meeting is to gather feedback from residents who live in neighborhoods in the direct vicinity of the proposed state trust land parcel, including Las Alturas, La Tierra Escondida, and Talavera.
General Public Meeting+ Monday, November 24 6:00 to 7:30 p.m.
New Mexico Farm & Ranch Heritage Museum 4100 Dripping Springs Road Las Cruces, N.M.
88011 ONLINE OPTION: Register to join via Zoom +The goal of this second meeting will be to gather feedback from the general public from communities throughout Las Cruces and Doña Ana County.
You are welcome to attend this meeting as well.
Please feel free to share information about these meetings with others in your community.
