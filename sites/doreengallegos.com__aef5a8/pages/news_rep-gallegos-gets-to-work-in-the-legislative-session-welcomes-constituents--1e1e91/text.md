Gallegos Gets to Work in the Legislative Session, Welcomes Constituents to the Round House Feb 3 Written By Adam Sommers Santa Fe, NM - With the 2025 legislative session underway, Representative Doreen Gallegos is hitting the ground running.
The Representative has already filed six bills aimed at improving the lives of New Mexicans, including HBIo, that will give New Mexico's Cannabis Control Division the resources it needs to ensure safe, legal, New Mexico-made products are being sold to consumers.
"I'm excited to be back in Santa Fe advocating for the communities in my district," said Rep.
Gallegos.
"My priority is always to ensure that the voices of my constituents are heard, and I encourage them to visit my office to share their thoughts and concerns." Rep.
Gallegos has welcomed many constituents to her office in the first days of the session, engaging in discussions about important issues facing the community.
She looks forward to continued collaboration with local leaders, advocates, and residents to make meaningful progress on legislative priorities.
