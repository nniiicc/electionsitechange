Home Meet Ed Issues Achievements Get Involved News Calendar Donate Contact Meet Ed Ed Orcutt has been serving SW Washington in the House of Representatives since 2002.
Since redistricting in 2012, he has been serving the citizens of the 20th district .
Ed served 5 years as the Ranking Minority Member of the Transportation Committee and serves on the Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee.
He is the Ranking Minority Member on the Finance Committee and has served as the Assistant Ranking/Ranking Minority Member of the Finance Committee since 2005.
An active volunteer even before he was elected to serve in the House of Representatives, Ed is a past president of the Lions Club and currently a member of the Kalama Lions.
He has served as past chairman of the Highlander Festival, a member of Columbia Theatre Board, and as a youth bowling coach.
After earning his B.S. in Forest Management from the University of Idaho, Ed has made his living as a forestry consultant since 1990 and owner of his own consulting company since 2009.
He is a leader in protecting our natural resource industries and quality of life.
Ed and his wife, Marcie, have been married since 1996 and make their home in Kalama, Washington.
