House District 50
District 50 is a culturally diverse region, spanning the southern part of Santa Fe county and a portion of Sandoval county.
While much of the area is rural, its population centers lie in communities like Eldorado, Edgewood, and Placitas.
Eldorado is known for its arts scene, outdoor trails, and close-knit neighborhoods, fostering a strong sense of community engagement.
Edgewood, situated at the threshold of mountains and plains, reflects New Mexico’s rural character, featuring a storied heritage of ranching and agriculture.
Placitas has its roots in historic Spanish land grants and thriving local traditions, offering a glimpse into the cultural history that defines so much of the region.
