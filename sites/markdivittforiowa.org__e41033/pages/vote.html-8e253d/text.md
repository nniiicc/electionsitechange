Key Dates Mark Your Calendar Oct 14 Early voting begins at your county auditor’s office.

Nov 2 Last day to vote early in person.
Nov 3 Election Day.
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
On Election Day you vote at your own precinct’s polling place.
Mailed ballots must reach your auditor by 8 p.m.

You can vote early at any time in your own county; you do not need a reason.
Before You Go What to Bring and What to Know Bring an ID Accepted IDs include an Iowa driver’s license, an Iowa non-operator ID, a U.S. passport, a U.S. military or veteran ID, a signed Iowa Voter ID Card, or a tribal ID.
No ID?
You can still cast a provisional ballot.
Vote by Mail Your county auditor must receive your request by 5 p.m. on October 19.
Your voted ballot must reach the auditor by 8 p.m. on Election Day.
Check your county’s drop-off options.
Not Registered?
Register online by October 19.
After that, Johnson County says you can still register on Election Day.
Check with your auditor for early voting.
