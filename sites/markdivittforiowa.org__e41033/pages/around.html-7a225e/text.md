Around District 91
Food pantries, senior meals, help with the heating bill, low-cost care for your pets — put together in one place, because good help doesn't do any good if you can't find it.
Please call before you go.
Hours, eligibility and locations change, and the people running these places are mostly volunteers.
A quick phone call saves you a trip.
Where we list hours, we've confirmed them by phone and noted when — but the organization is always the final word, not this page.
These organizations are listed as a service to our neighbors.
They have not endorsed any candidate, and none of them are connected to this campaign.
If you help run one of these and would like your listing changed or removed, just email info@markdivittforiowa.org and we'll take care of it right away.
Know Something We Missed?
This list is nowhere near complete, and it only gets better if people tell us what's missing.
If you know of a pantry, a clinic, a meal site, or a group doing good work in District 91 — or if something here is out of date — let us know and we'll check it out and add it.
Need Help Right Now?
Call 2-1-1.
It's free, confidential, available 24 hours a day, and staffed by people whose whole job is connecting Iowans to food, housing, heating, health care and crisis support in their own community.
If you're not sure where to start, start there.
You can also search their statewide directory online at 211iowa.org.
