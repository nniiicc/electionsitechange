Here at home
Water, roads, and the airport
The 2026 capital investment bill includes $3 million to redesign 332nd Street, a road a lot of us use every day and one that has needed safety improvements for years.
$3.9 million went to upgrading the St.
Cloud Regional Airport’s landing system and installing approach lights.
Not the most glamorous item on this list, but a safer airport is how a regional economy stays connected.
And $4 million went to improving St.
Cloud’s water treatment plant.
Clean, reliable water is the kind of thing you only notice when it stops working.
