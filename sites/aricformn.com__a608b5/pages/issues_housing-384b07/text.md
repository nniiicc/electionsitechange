Housing
Affordable housing
Sixty percent of the people in St.
Cloud live in economic distress — more than thirty percent of their income goes to housing.
Distress is the right word, because all it takes is one stretch of bad luck and almost two-thirds of our neighbors could experience homelessness.
The answer is complex.
We need more affordable options, but the problem goes deeper than supply — we need to reform how we fund and oversee both our housing and our help for those experiencing homelessness.
As our State Senator, I have
- Worked for capital investments to increase the supply of affordable housing and incentivize home ownership
- Advocated for our community and mediated conflicts between tenants and landlords
- Passed $40 million for the Greater MN Workforce Housing Program, which creates new workforce rental housing in small and medium-sized communities in Greater Minnesota
- Passed $1 billion for statewide construction of new housing
- Expanded rental aid and a comprehensive tenants’ bill of rights
- Passed emergency shelter grants and increased Local Homelessness Prevention Aid and housing aid targeted at Greater Minnesota
