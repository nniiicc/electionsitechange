ST.
CLOUD (St.
Cloud Times) —
When our university and businesses are connected we all do better, but for far too long SCSU and downtown St.
Cloud have been separated.
Recently one of my biggest accomplishments in the senate was signed into law: $1.2 billion in capital investment and infrastructure.
The bonding bill provides resources to help local communities with big projects that they likely couldn’t finance on their own.
I fought for us, and we were able to secure $12.4 million for important local projects.
