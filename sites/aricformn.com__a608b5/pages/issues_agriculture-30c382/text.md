I have made it a priority to visit farms across Minnesota and lift up the voices of those working to put food on all of our tables.
As our State Senator, I have Visited more than 100 Minnesota farms during my time chairing the Senate’s Agriculture, Veterans, Broadband, and Rural Development Committee Created the state’s first grain indemnity fund to protect producers if an elevator fails Increased the first-tier market value limit for ag homestead property by more than $1 million, providing an average property tax savings of $2,400 Expanded the Beginning Farmer Tax Credit in 2023 and removed the credit cap for 2026 Increased and expanded the state’s Sustainable Aviation Fuel tax credit Passed $85 million in emergency funding to the state’s food shelves Secured more than $5 million for Minnesota’s Farm-to-School Program Supported farmers fighting High Path Avian Influenza and funded innovations like turkey lasers Passed more than $1.5 million for downpayment assistance grants to address the barrier of land access for young farmers in the state Dedicated $3 million for payments to small and medium dairy operations to cover shortfalls in USDA’s Dairy Margin Coverage Program Visited more than 100 Minnesota farms during my time chairing the Senate’s Agriculture, Veterans, Broadband, and Rural Development Committee.
