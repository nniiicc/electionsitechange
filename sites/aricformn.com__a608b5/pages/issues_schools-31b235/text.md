No matter where we live or how much money we have, we all want our kids to have access to a quality education that gives them what they need to succeed in the classroom and in life.
I have worked to inspire more people to choose the teaching profession, and to support them as they do the work to become qualified.
As our State Senator, I have Supported more than $2 billion in school funding increases so we can lower class sizes and get more counselors in our schools — our young people need and deserve more adults in their lives to serve as mentors Passed the North Star Promise Act that is providing free in-state tuition to more than 53,000 Minnesotans Fought to support and improve Minnesota’s State Grant Program to help students afford college Passed new incentives to address Minnesota’s teacher shortage Passed and then strengthened the READ Act, new phonics-based curriculum to improve literacy instruction in our schools Supported stipends for student teachers to remove one financial barrier for those hoping to become teachers Authored the Hunger-Free Campus Act, because no one should have to choose between buying books and buying food Passed Universal Free School Meals for Minnesota kids, ensuring all kids have access to nutritious food at school By securing a quality education for all Minnesotans, we can build a strong future for generations to come.
