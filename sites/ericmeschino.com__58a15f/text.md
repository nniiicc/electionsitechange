Meet Eric
A lobsterman and small business owner fighting for our community
For the past 25 years, Eric Meschino has worked as a commercial lobsterman on the waters off the South Shore—following in the footsteps of his father, a dedicated educator and fisherman.
He knows firsthand the value of hard work, sustainable practices, and a deep respect for our natural resources.
A graduate of Boston College High School and the University of Massachusetts Boston, Eric brings experience from the financial sectors of New York and Philadelphia.
As a father of two, he is committed to responsible, effective leadership for the families of the 12th Plymouth District.

About the 12th Plymouth District
The 12th Plymouth District consists of the communities of Halifax (Precincts 1 & 2A), Kingston, Middleboro (Precincts 1 & 5A), Pembroke (Precinct 3), Plympton, and Plymouth (Precincts 1, 2, 3, 5, and 13).
Eric looks forward to serving the interests of all of these communities and ensuring every resident has a voice on Beacon Hill.
