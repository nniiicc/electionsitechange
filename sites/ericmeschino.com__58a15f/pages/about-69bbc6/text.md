Open main menu Home About Priorities Get Involved Contact Donate Donate Now Meet the Candidate About Eric Meschino A Lobsterman and Fiercely Independent Voice Welcome to the campaign of Eric Meschino, Republican candidate for State Representative in the 12th Plymouth District.
Representing the communities of Halifax (Precincts 1 & 2A), Kingston, Middleboro (Precincts 1 & 5A), Pembroke (Precinct 3), Plympton, and Plymouth (Precincts 1, 2, 3, 5, and 13), Eric is dedicated to serving the people of this district with commitment and integrity.
With a professional foundation rooted in the financial sectors of New York and Philadelphia, and a deep connection to our local coastline, Eric brings a wealth of diverse experience to his candidacy.
A graduate of Boston College High School and the University of Massachusetts Boston with a degree in Political Science, he has held key positions at leading firms, including Janney Montgomery Scott, Merrill Lynch, and Sands Brothers.
For the past 25 years, however, it has been Eric's work as a commercial lobsterman that best defines his commitment to our community.
Following in the footsteps of his father, a dedicated educator and fisherman, Eric has embraced the values of sustainable practices, hard work, and a deep respect for our natural resources.
As a father of two children who attended Plymouth public schools, he is passionate about supporting the prosperity and well-being of our district.

As a blue-collar worker and small business owner, he knows firsthand the value of hard work and perseverance, and he is committed to fighting for the American Dream—right here in the 12th Plymouth District—so every family has the opportunity to succeed and thrive.
Together, we can uphold the principles of hard work, integrity, and accountability that will ensure a thriving, sustainable future for all.
