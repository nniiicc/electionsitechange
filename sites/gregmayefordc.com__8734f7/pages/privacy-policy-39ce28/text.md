Privacy Policy Effective Date: July 23, 2026 Greg Maye for DC ("the Campaign," "we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when visiting www.gregmayefordc.com .
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.
Information We Collect We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including: Name Email address Mailing address Telephone number Any information you submit through forms on this website Information provided when making a contribution, volunteering, requesting information, or signing a petition We may also automatically collect limited technical information such as: IP address Browser type Device information Pages visited Date and time of your visit Cookies and similar technologies How We Use Your Information Information collected through this website may be used to: Respond to your inquiries Communicate campaign news and updates Process donations Process volunteer requests Process petition signatures Improve the website and visitor experience Maintain campaign records Comply with applicable federal and District of Columbia law Email Communications If you subscribe to receive campaign updates, you may receive periodic emails regarding campaign news, volunteer opportunities, events, fundraising, or other campaign-related information.
You may unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in campaign emails.
Text Messaging If you voluntarily opt in to receive campaign text messages, you consent to receive recurring automated or manual campaign-related text messages from Greg Maye for DC.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to cancel.
Reply HELP for assistance.
Your consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a contribution or supporting the campaign.
Sharing of Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information only: With trusted vendors performing services on behalf of the Campaign As required by law To comply with campaign finance reporting requirements To protect the rights or safety of the Campaign or the public Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Data Security We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information.
However, no website or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Third-Party Links This website may contain links to third-party websites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those websites.
Children's Privacy This website is not intended for children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Changes to This Privacy Policy This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.
Changes become effective immediately upon posting to this website.
The Effective Date at the top of this page will always indicate the latest revision.
