Accessibility Statement Effective Date: July 23, 2026 Greg Maye for DC is committed to making this website accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities.
We believe every visitor should be able to access campaign information, participate in the democratic process, and communicate with our campaign regardless of ability or technology.
Our Commitment We strive to make this website as accessible and user-friendly as possible by: Using clear and readable text.
Organizing content with meaningful headings.
Providing sufficient color contrast where practical.
Supporting keyboard navigation where available.
Adding alternative text to images whenever possible.
Working to improve compatibility with screen readers and other assistive technologies.
Ongoing Improvements Accessibility is an ongoing effort.
As we update this website, we will continue working to improve accessibility and usability for all visitors.
Need Assistance?
If you experience difficulty accessing any content on this website or need information in an alternative format, we encourage you to contact us.
Greg Maye for DC Campaign Committee Email: gregmayefordc@gmail.com Website: www.gregmayefordc.com Please include: The page or content you were trying to access.
A description of the issue.
The type of device or browser you were using (if known).
We will make reasonable efforts to respond promptly and provide the information in an accessible format whenever possible.
Feedback We welcome suggestions for improving the accessibility of this website.
Your feedback helps us continue making our campaign information available to everyone.
