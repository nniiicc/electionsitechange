How We Protect Your Information Effective Date: July 23, 2026 At Greg Maye for DC, we believe earning your trust means protecting the information you choose to share with us.
We collect only the information necessary to communicate with supporters, process petitions, coordinate volunteers, comply with campaign finance laws, and operate our campaign effectively.
We are committed to handling your information responsibly and with respect.
What We Do We work to: Collect only the information we reasonably need.
Use your information only for campaign-related purposes.
Protect your information using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards.
Limit access to personal information to authorized campaign personnel and trusted service providers.
Regularly review our practices to improve security and privacy.
What We Don't Do We do not: Sell your personal information.
Sell or share your mobile phone number for marketing purposes.
Share your text messaging consent with third parties for advertising.
Send unwanted messages or "spam." Collect more information than we need to support our campaign activities.
Your Choices You are always in control of your communications with us.
You may: Unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time.
Reply STOP to end text messages.
Contact us if you have questions about how your information is used.
Our Commitment We recognize that trust is earned.
Whether you choose to sign a petition, volunteer, attend an event, make a contribution, or simply learn more about our campaign, we appreciate your confidence in us and will continue working to protect the information you share.
Questions?
If you have questions about how we collect, use, or protect your information, please contact us at: Email: gregmayefordc@gmail.com Please also review our Privacy Policy for additional information about how personal information is collected, used, and protected.
