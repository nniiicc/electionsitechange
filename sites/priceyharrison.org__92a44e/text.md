I'm running for re-election in NC House District 61
After consultation with many colleagues, supporters, and friends, I have decided to run for re-election to NC House District 61 in 2026.
There is still much work to do to defend our democracy, environment, public education, women’s reproductive rights, social justice causes, and more.
It has been the honor of my life to serve the people of Guilford County and North Carolina for the past 20 years.
I am grateful for all of the support and encouragement I’ve received and look forward to the opportunity to continue to serve the citizens of North Carolina.
