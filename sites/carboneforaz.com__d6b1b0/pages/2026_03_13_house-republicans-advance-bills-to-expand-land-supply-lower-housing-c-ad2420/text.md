“Affordability is top of mind for Arizona families, especially when it comes to housing,” said Majority Leader Michael Carbone.
“As rents rise, home prices climb, and fewer attainable single-family options remain on the market, more Arizonans are being priced out of the communities they call home and further away from the dream of homeownership.
House Republicans are passing targeted reforms to reduce unnecessary government-driven costs, expand housing opportunities, and make it easier for Arizona families to buy a home of their own.”
