Email Privacy Policy
Effective Date: October 2025
Last Updated: October 2025
At Carbone for Arizona (“we,” “us,” or “our”), we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.
This Email Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your email information when you sign up to receive updates, newsletters, or communications from us through CarboneForAZ.com or related campaign platforms.
1.
Information We Collect
When you voluntarily provide your email address (for example, by signing up for updates, event information, or contributing to our campaign), we may collect the following:
- Your name and email address
- Optional details such as zip code or phone number (if you choose to provide them)
- Metadata related to your email interactions (such as open rates or link clicks) used solely to improve communication effectiveness
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Send campaign updates, newsletters, event invitations, and donation opportunities
- Respond to inquiries or requests you submit
- Improve our communication strategy and outreach efforts
- Comply with applicable laws, election regulations, and contribution reporting requirements
We do not sell, rent, or trade your email address or personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.
3.
Email Communications and Opt-Out
By subscribing to our communications, you consent to receive periodic emails from Carbone for Arizona.
You may unsubscribe at any time by clicking the “Unsubscribe” link provided in the footer of any campaign email, or by contacting us directly at info@carboneforaz.com.
Please allow up to 10 business days for your request to be processed.
4.
How We Protect Your Information
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
However, please note that no data transmission over the Internet or email system is entirely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
5.
Third-Party Services
We may use trusted third-party email platforms or donation processors (such as WinRed, Mailchimp, or similar) to send emails and manage lists.
These services are contractually obligated to protect your data and may only use it as necessary to provide the service on our behalf.
6.
Legal Compliance
Our campaign complies with all applicable U.S. and Arizona state laws, including the CAN-SPAM Act, campaign finance regulations, and data protection standards.
