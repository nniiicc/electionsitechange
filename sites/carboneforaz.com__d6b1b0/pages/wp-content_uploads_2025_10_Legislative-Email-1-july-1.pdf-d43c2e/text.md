L Education and Protection of Children Education is the foundation of our future, and it is imperative that we provide our children with the best possible start in life.
I​ am proud to have co-sponsored several bills aimed at enhancing educational opportunities and resources across our state: HB2032: Given the terrible tragedies that many school shootings around the country have brought us, we must make school safety a priority.
This bill protects students and teachers by ensuring that the blueprints and floor plans of our schools are not publicly accessible.
HB2310: Our children are our most precious resource, and it is our responsibility to protect them from harm.
These bills work towards creating a safer environment for our students and ensuring that predators who target minors are held accountable.
Healthcare Access to quality healthcare is essential for the well-being of every Arizonan.
I​ have been actively working on several healthcare initiatives to ensure that our communities have the resources they need.
HB2621: This bill addresses the rising costs of prescription drugs by allowing pharmacists to dispense cheaper alternative medications, with the patient’s consent, when the prescribed medication is not covered by their insurance.
HB2242: Mental health is just as important as physical health, and this bill aims to improve access to mental healthcare for Arizonans by requiring insurance companies to cover telemedicine services for mental health treatment.
I believe that these bills reflect our shared priorities for a better Arizona, and I am honored to have played a role in their creation.
