Some of them are: HB2622- Drug cartels: labeled terrorist organizations HB2821- Illegal Border Crossings a State Crime HCR2060- Illegal Entry from Foreign Nations Protecting Arizona Ensuring the safety and security of all Arizonans is a top priority in my legislative agenda.
It is crucial that we take proactive steps to protect our communities from various threats, ranging from criminal activities to foreign adversaries.
Keeping Arizona safe not only enhances the well-being of our residents but also boosts our state's economic vitality and quality of life.
HB2436: Also known as "Protection Procurement Act" aims to protect the state from potential national security threats posed by foreign adversaries.
It requires companies seeking to do business with the state to disclose any past or current business operations involving contracts with or services from a foreign adversary, as well as any subsidiaries or affiliates of the company.
Clean Elections I believe in the power of a fair and transparent electoral process, which is why I am proud to be a co-sponsor of several bills aimed at strengthening clean elections: HB2768: My co-sponsorship of this bill demonstrates my commitment to clean and fair elections, as it aims to strengthen the enforcement powers of the Arizona Clean Elections Commission.
HB 2785: This bill focuses on making the signature verification process more robust, military ballots making it to their destinations and back on time so military votes are counted, ensuring that our state electors are delivered on time by providing enough time for re-counts (if needed) and making sure that system log files are posted within a timely manner for accurate follow-up.
