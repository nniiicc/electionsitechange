Empowering Arizona Businesses One of my key efforts this session has been sponsoring HB 2450, a bill designed to simplify processes and reduce unnecessary burdens on small businesses.
“This bill ensures that Arizona’s unemployment system is responsive to economic realities while keeping the focus on getting people back to work,” said Majority Leader Carbone.
“Arizona is a state of opportunity, and we should always encourage workforce participation, not prolonged reliance on government benefits.
This is a responsible approach that strengthens our economy, protects taxpayers, and prioritizes those who truly need assistance.” “House Republicans are leading with strength and purpose, making sure government works for the people – not the other way around ,” Leader Carbone added.
“HB2450 is about keeping Arizona’s workforce engaged and ensuring our unemployment system serves as a safety net, not a substitute for employment.” This legislation reflects a balanced approach to supporting individuals in need while encouraging economic growth and personal responsibility.
By fostering a system that prioritizes workforce participation, HB2450 aims to create lasting solutions that empower Arizonans and strengthen the state's economy.
Honoring Veterans The passing of HB2672 underscores Arizona's commitment to honoring those who have selflessly served our nation.
By providing a full property tax exemption for 100% service-connected disabled veterans and their surviving spouses, this initiative reflects a profound recognition of their sacrifices.
My leadership in championing this bill showcases my dedication to Arizona’s veterans, ensuring they no longer bear the financial strain of property taxes on their homes.
This exemption not only offers meaningful relief but also affirms the state's role in safeguarding the well-being of those who have given so much for the country.
Under the new provisions of HB2672, Arizona eliminates existing barriers to property tax exemptions for disabled veterans, including income thresholds and assessment value caps.
By removing these restrictions, the bill ensures that veterans with severe service-connected injuries can focus on their recovery and quality of life without worry.
Extending this benefit to surviving spouses further strengthens the bill's impact, helping families of veterans remain in their homes and communities .
Arizona continues to lead with innovative policies that reinforce its core principles of economic security, low taxation, and unwavering support for those who have served in uniform.
With bipartisan backing, HB2672’s passage is a milestone in advancing the state’s mission to uplift its veteran community and their families.
Protecting the Vulnerable Last, but not least, Arizona House Speaker Steve Montenegro, Majority Whip Julie Willoughby, and Speaker Pro Tempore Neal Carter and I, Majority Leader, have teamed up to call out our Governor for her runaway spending and overburdening of our State Budget.
House Republicans are sounding the alarm over a critical funding crisis in Arizona’s Developmental Disabilities (DDD) program, laying the blame squarely on Governor Katie Hobbs’ administration.
A $122 million shortfall has placed one of the state’s most vital safety nets for vulnerable citizens at risk, raising serious concerns about fiscal mismanagement and accountability.
We have made it clear that while we are committed to ensuring the program continues to serve families in need, we cannot support a taxpayer-funded bailout without meaningful reforms to prevent future crises.
The situation is especially dire as the DDD program’s costs are set to double by Fiscal Year 2026, a trajectory that leaders have described as unsustainable.
Together, we are urging Governor Hobbs to engage in constructive dialogue and implement necessary reforms.
Arizona’s taxpayers and families deserve better.
As a Republican majority in the House of Representatives dealing with a Democrat Governor, we are underscoring the need for careful, bipartisan collaboration to restore stability to this essential program.
By addressing these critical concerns through carefully considered reforms, we have an opportunity to ensure the long-term sustainability of the DDD program while continuing to support the individuals and families who rely on it most.
This Press Release represents a necessary step toward fiscal responsibility and program efficiency, and we firmly believe that bipartisan collaboration will pave the way for a stronger, more reliable system for all Arizonans.
