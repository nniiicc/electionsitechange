Meet Jessica Kusner-Kalarchik A new voice for District 29 Donate I am Jessica Kusner-Kalarchik , and I am running to represent District 29 in the Alaska State House of Representatives.
Hello!
I am 51 years old and grew up primarily in Montana before joining the Army right out of high school.
My second enlistment as a paratrooper brought me to Alaska, and my continued military service kept bringing me back.
I recently retired after 31 years in the armed forces and multiple combat deployments.
Alongside my military career, I have spent a decade working as a Registered Nurse, specializing in medical-surgical care, the operating room, and serving as a Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner.
I reside on a rural property east of Palmer along the Glenn Highway and am the proud parent of two daughters and have five grandchildren.
Like many of you, I am frustrated with how our state government is currently operating.
I feel a strong calling to step up and enact real change.
I am running to provide the hardworking residents of District 29 with better leadership and a clear alternative at the ballot box.
