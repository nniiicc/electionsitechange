MEET JOEL MACE
Joel Mace is a lifelong Missourian and proud resident of Southwest Missouri.
As a detective for the Newton County Sheriff's Office, he works hard to keep our community safe from wrongdoers.
As a devoted husband and father of four, Mace wants to ensure that our rural values aren't eroded away by radical leftist agendas.
Mace serves as treasurer of the SWMO FOP Lodge #27, is active in the McDonald County Republican Club, and is a member of both the Missouri Firearms Coalition and the NRA.
He is also involved with the Missouri Cattlemen's Association in Newton and McDonald counties.
As state representative, Joel Mace will always uphold free speech and gun rights, defend life and liberty, and fight the "woke" agenda that is attempting to take over our nation.
With his career in law enforcement, Mace will always Back the Blue and ensure public safety remains a priority.
He will work to reduce government waste and cut burdensome taxes for hardworking families.
Mace will also advocate for Missouri students to receive the best possible education by increasing access to personalized options.
