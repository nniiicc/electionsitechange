Legal Assistance
About
Navigating the legal system can be intimidating, especially when facing critical issues like housing disputes, record expungement, workplace discrimination, or family law matters.
Access to affordable legal representation and information is vital for protecting your rights.
This guide connects District 40 and Maryland residents to trusted State, Federal, and Local legal services, public defenders, free legal clinics, and court self-help tools for the following legal fields:
- Consumer Rights, Scams, and Debt
- Criminal Defense and Record Expungement
- Domestic Violence
- Family Law
- Housing, Rent, and Eviction
- Immigration
IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER: By law, members of the Maryland General Assembly and their legislative staff, including the Office of Delegate Marlon Amprey, are prohibited from offering formal legal advice, recommending private attorneys, or intervening in active judicial proceedings or court cases.
