Equitable and Just Laws
Solutions for the Baltimore of Today and Tomorrow
Public Saftey and Health
- Strategically remove illegal guns from our communities and focus on violent offenders.
- Provide more mental health support to combat addiction and the cycle of violence produced by trauma.
- Equitably invest in prison reform to rehabilitate and educate all prisoners and reduce recidivism.
- Expand access to affordable health care for all.
- Preserve, protect and expand the right for women to choose and make abortion care more affordable and accessible.
Education
- Fully fund our public schools and provide more staffing and resources to support teachers.
- Expand access to career-focused trade schools and apprenticeship programs in high schools.
- Make college and professional schools affordable and accessible for all.
Transit and Housing
- Expand reliable public transportation options and restore the Red Line.
- Support Seniors and Older Adults with better transportation options and affordable and safe housing.
- Invest in more green public transportation options and charging stations.
- Create more sustainable affordable housing for families and seniors.
- Increase Black and Latino homeownership.
- Improve and expand diversity and equity in the procurement process.
Economic Development
- Develop and grow our small and medium-sized businesses to spur job growth.
- Invest in the expansion of more businesses owned by people of color and women.
- Restore or remove vacant homes to reduce blight and make communities safer.
- Equitably redevelop neighborhoods without forcibly removing legacy residents.
- Provide more access to fresh food and stop the expansion of food deserts.
