From healthcare costs and prescription assistance to home safety repairs, meal delivery, and property tax relief, a wide range of programs are available to support Baltimore’s seniors and their caregivers.
US Federal Resources Eldercare Locator A nation-wide service funded by the U.S.
Administration on Aging that connects older adults and caregivers with local support services.
Phone (800) 677-1116 Click here for more information.
Medicare Part D Extra Help Program A federal program that helps Medicare beneficiaries with limited income pay for Part D prescription plan premiums, deductibles, and co-pays.
Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
Maryland State Resources Maryland Access Point (MAP) Maryland’s official "no wrong door" resource network for older adults and individuals with disabilities.
Provide free one-on-one counseling on housing options, home care support, and long-term planning.
Phone: (844) 627-5465 Click here for more information.
Maryland Senior Prescription Drug Assistance Program (SPDAP) A subsidy program established by the Maryland General Assembly in 2005 to provide financial assistance to moderate-income Maryland residents who are eligible for Medicare and are enrolled in a prescription drug plan.
Provides up to $#–$# per month toward Medicare Part D or Medicare Advantage prescription drug plan premiums for moderate-income Maryland seniors.
Click here for more information.
Baltimore City Local Resources Baltimore City Division of Aging and CARE Services The local Area Agency on Aging, offering benefit screening, caregiver support groups, case management, and senior center programs.
Click here for more information.
Baltimore City Centers Community centers offering free wellness classes, computer literacy hubs, hot lunches, and social activities.
Click here for more information.
Civic Works Baltimore Provide home modifications such as grab bars, handrails and structural repairs that make houses more comfortable and aging-friendly for older adults.
Provide free, one-on-one, benefits assistance for Baltimore residents.
Phone: (410) 826-5955 (Press #2) Click here for more information.
HUBS (Housing Upgrades to Benefit Seniors) Baltimore coalition providing home repairs, weatherization, and mobility modifications (like stairlifts and ramp installation) to help older adults safely age in place at home.
Phone: (410) 396-3023 Click here for more information.
Maryand Transit Administration (MTA) MobilityLink Door-to-door paratransit service for eligible seniors and individuals with disabilities who cannot use fixed-route buses or light rail.
Phone: (410) 764-8181 Click here for more information.
MTA Senior Shuttle Reduced Fare Program Click here for more information.
