Food Assistance
About
Access to fresh, healthy, and affordable food is a fundamental right.
In District 40 and across Maryland, no individual or family should have to worry about where their next meal is coming from.
Navigating food assistance programs can sometimes feel overwhelming.
This resource guide is designed to help you quickly find emergency food support, apply for long-term food benefits, and access local nutrition programs at the Federal, State, and Local levels.
If you need help applying for state food assistance or are experiencing delays with your SNAP benefits, the Office of Delegate Marlon Amprey is here to assist you.
US Federal Resources
USDA Food Assistance Programs provides critical nutrition assistance through Food and Nutrition Administration (FNA) programs that include child nutrition programs, the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), and emergency food assistance among many other programs like the ones below:
- SUN Bucks (Summer EBT)
- Provides eligible school-aged children with grocery benefits during the summer months when school meals are not available.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- The Emergency Food Assistance Program (TEFAP)
- Provides emergency food and nutrition assistance at no cost to low-income Americans through local food banks and pantries.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Commodity Supplemental Food Program (CSFP/Senior Food Boxes)
- Works to improve the health of low-income elderly persons at least 60 years of age by supplementing their diets with nutritious USDA foods.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Federal Child and Adult Care Food Program (CACFP)
- Provides reimbursements for nutritious meals and snacks to eligible children and adults who are enrolled for care at participating child care centers, day care homes, and adult day care centers.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Feeding America
- The largest domestic hunger-relief and food-rescue organization in the United States.
- Click here to access their website for resources.
- For a list of other US food assistance programs, click here.
Maryland State Resources
The State of Maryland administers key food support programs.
The Office of Delegate Marlon Amprey has direct oversight with state agencies and can help advocate on your behalf if you run into issues with state-level applications.
- Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP / Food Stamps)
- Provides monthly funds on an EBT card to help low-income individuals and families buy food.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Maryland WIC (Women, Infants, and Children)
- Provides healthy food, nutrition counseling, and breastfeeding support for pregnant women, new mothers, infants, and children under age 5.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Maryland Farmers' Market Nutrition Program (FMNP)
- Provides checks/coupons to low-income seniors and WIC participants to buy fresh, locally grown fruits and vegetables at participating Maryland farmers' markets.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Maryland Market Money
- A state-matching program that doubles the spending power of SNAP, WIC, and Senior FMNP benefits at participating farmers' markets across Maryland.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Maryland Food Bank
- A non-profit organization leading the movement to end hunger in Maryland.
- Click here to access their website for resources.
Baltimore City Local Resources
- The Summer Food Service Program (SFSP)
- Provides free, nutritious breakfasts and lunches to kids in low-income areas during the Summer season.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Baltimore City Community Action Partnership (CAP) Centers
- Provide emergency food assistance, grocery boxes, and referrals for District 40 residents facing immediate crisis.
- Click here for more information and details on how to apply.
- Baltimore 2-1-1 Food Helpline
- Call 2-1-1 (or text your ZIP code to 898-211) 24/7 to speak with a specialist who can connect you with emergency food delivery or the closest open pantry.
