This policy explains what information we collect through martyjackley.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Information we collect We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, and any message you send us through our forms.
We also collect information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited, through standard web analytics.
If you volunteer, request a yard sign, or invite Marty to an event, that information is submitted to our campaign management system and used solely for campaign purposes.
SMS & text messaging If you opt in to receive text messages from the Campaign, you consent to receive recurring automated campaign updates, event reminders, volunteer requests, and fundraising messages at the mobile number you provide.
Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any donation or service.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply, based on your carrier and plan.
To opt out at any time, reply STOP to any message.
To get help, reply HELP or contact us at the email below.
After you text STOP, we will send one final confirmation message and then send no further texts unless you opt in again.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
We do not sell or share your mobile number with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
Email communications If you sign up for campaign email updates, every email includes an unsubscribe link.
You may opt out at any time.
How we use your information We use the information we collect to communicate with supporters, process volunteer and event requests, fulfill yard sign orders, respond to messages, and comply with federal and South Dakota campaign finance and election law.
Third-party services We use third-party vendors to operate donation processing (WinRed), forms and SMS/email delivery, and yard sign requests.
Those vendors process your information under their own privacy policies and only as needed to provide their service to the Campaign.
Your choices You may opt out of texts by replying STOP, unsubscribe from email at any time, and request that we delete your contact information by emailing us at the address below.
