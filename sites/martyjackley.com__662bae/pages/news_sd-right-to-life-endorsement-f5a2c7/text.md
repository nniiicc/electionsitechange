House of Representatives.
The organization named Jackley one of "SD's Top Three" pro-life candidates this election, alongside Governor Larry Rhoden and U.S.
Senator Mike Rounds.
From South Dakota Right to Life "Marty Jackley has been a long-time friend of SDRTL.
He has demonstrated his firm support of the Sanctity of Human Life.
In all of his many years of public service here in SD he has proven a firm commitment to protecting all innocents from conception to natural death.
This endorsement reflects Jackley's allegiance to strengthening a culture of life throughout South Dakota, the nation and soon to be in the U.S.
Congress." South Dakota Right to Life encourages voters to research their candidates and the issues, question them on their values, and vote pro-life.
