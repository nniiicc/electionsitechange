Jackley continues to build strong momentum and is the clear favorite in the race for Congress.

"I am the proven conservative in this race that has and will always place America and South Dakota first.
I will work with President Trump to strengthen our economy, secure our borders, rebuild our national defense, reduce the size of government, and cut taxes for hard-working Americans."
