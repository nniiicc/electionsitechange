About Issues Endorsements News Get Involved Contact DONATE DONATE PRESS RELEASE Jackley to Host Election Night Watch Party in Rapid City May 22, 2026 ← Back to News Rapid City: The Marty Jackley for Congress campaign announced today that Attorney General Marty Jackley will host a primary election night watch party at the Robbinsdale Entertainment Center in Rapid City on Tuesday, June 2, 2026.
Doors open at 5:30 PM.
The event is open to the public and to members of the press.
Jackley will be joined by family, supporters, and community members from across South Dakota as primary returns come in from across the state.
"I want to spend election night with the people who have made this campaign possible," said Jackley.
"Whether you have volunteered, put up a yard sign, or chipped in five dollars, this night is for you.
Bring your family and friends and come watch the results come in with us.
If you are following a different race on the ballot, you are welcome too." Food will be provided.
Family is welcome.
The event is open to anyone interested in watching the primary results, regardless of which race they are following on the ballot.
Members of the media are invited to attend.
Jackley will be available for one-on-one interviews throughout the evening.
To RSVP for media credentials, contact info@martyjackley.com.
