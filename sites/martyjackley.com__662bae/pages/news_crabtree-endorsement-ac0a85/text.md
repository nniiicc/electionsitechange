Pierre: Today Casey Crabtree announced he is dropping out of the race for U.S.
Congress and endorsed Marty Jackley.
I thank Senator Casey Crabtree for his endorsement.
Casey and I have been friends for nearly a decade, and that has never changed.
Casey has shown leadership for both his district and the people of our State, and I look forward to continuing to work with him on issues important to us both to make South Dakota even stronger.
Statement from Marty Jackley
