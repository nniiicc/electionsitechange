I have a husband and two kids in SD2 schools.
I’m part of the team that stocks the Costco Wholesale in Billings in the early mornings.
When I’m not at home, I’m probably out supporting my kids with their various activities or watching my husband perform in one of several local bands.
If it’s a Friday, I might be in line at an estate sale.
I have a passion for learning and have spent the last nine years serving the city of Billings on the Community Development Board.
Through that work I’ve been involved in major projects throughout Billings, including improving many public parks, bringing the first public restroom downtown, and affordable housing developments.
Though the CD board’s role is advisory, I’ve been able to add my thoughts and suggestions in ways that guided city staff’s priorities and decisions.
A vote for me is a vote to have your voice in Helena in 2027!
