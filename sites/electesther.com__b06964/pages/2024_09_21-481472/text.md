NASHVILLE, Tenn. – State Rep.
Esther Helton-Haynes, R-East Ridge, has been named an inaugural Defender of Medicine by the Tennessee Medical Association’s (TMA) Political Action Committee (PAC).
The recognition honors members of the General Assembly who have shown a commitment to creating a healthier Tennessee.
