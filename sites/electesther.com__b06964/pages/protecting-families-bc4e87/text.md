Esther has consistently demonstrated a commitment to advocating for policies that promote family values.
Esther has supported the “Protect the Tennessee Minors Act,” which mandates age-verification processes to prevent minors from accessing harmful online adult content.
As a pro-life advocate, she sponsored legislation aimed at protecting both the unborn and the life of the mother.
In addition to these efforts, she has backed initiatives that streamline the adoption process in Tennessee, reducing costs and simplifying procedures for families.
She also co-sponsored the “Right to Shop” Act, ensuring transparency by requiring insurance companies to publish the costs of medical procedures upfront, giving Tennesseans greater clarity in healthcare decisions.
In support of law enforcement, she co-sponsored the “Back the Blue Act” and the “Chris Wright Act,” both of which strengthen protections for officers and prioritize keeping dangerous criminals off the streets, ensuring safer communities across the state.
