THA Recognizes Rep.
Esther Helton-Haynes as a 2023 Hospital Hero
NASHVILLE (December 15, 2023) — The Tennessee Hospital Association (THA) honored Rep.
Esther Helton-Haynes (R-East Ridge) as a 2023 THA Hospital Hero for her dedication and work on behalf of hospitals in Tennessee.
