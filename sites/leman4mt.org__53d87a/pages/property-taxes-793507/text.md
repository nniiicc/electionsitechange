Industry used to carry a much bigger share of the property tax load.
Then the legislature started handing out tax breaks to favored businesses, and homeowners got stuck with the difference.
That’s crony capitalism, and it’s the government picking winners and losers.
It’s time to stop.
Make the favored industries pay their fair share, and cut the tax on the roof over your head.
