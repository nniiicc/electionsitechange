Campaign Update Chris Knoll Earns Dual Endorsement From Minnesota Firefighters and Police Officers 2026-07-16 · 3 min read · Public safety professionals are uniting behind Chris Knoll's campaign for Minnesota Senate District 21.
Chris Knoll Earns Dual Endorsement From Minnesota Firefighters and Police Officers Chris Knoll, candidate for Minnesota Senate District 21, has earned the endorsement of both the Minnesota Professional Fire Fighters and the Minnesota Police and Peace Officers Association, two of Minnesota's leading public safety organizations.
The dual endorsement reflects strong support from the men and women who protect Minnesota communities every day, from firefighters and paramedics to police and peace officers.
Earning the trust and support of both our firefighters and our police officers means everything to me.
These are the people who run toward danger to keep our families safe, and they deserve a partner in St.
Paul who will fight for them, their working conditions, and their retirement security.
I'm honored to have their support, and I will work every day to earn it.
Support From Minnesota Firefighters The Minnesota Professional Fire Fighters represent more than 2,500 firefighters, EMTs, paramedics, and dispatchers across 46 cities statewide as members of the International Association of Fire Fighters.
In its endorsement, the organization cited Knoll's understanding of the importance of protecting public safety professionals and strengthening the health and retirement security of its members, including preserving and strengthening pension protections.
Backing From Police and Peace Officers The Minnesota Police and Peace Officers Association has represented rank-and-file peace officers and retired members statewide since 1922.
The association endorsed Knoll without reservation, citing confidence in his leadership, character, and steadfast commitment to public safety.
The men and women who serve in communities throughout our state deeply appreciate Knoll's dedication to ensuring their safety and the safety of the citizens they protect.
Standing With First Responders Knoll said the endorsements underscore his commitment to standing with first responders on the issues that matter most to them, including public safety funding, working conditions, and retirement security.
As he campaigns to represent Senate District 21, Knoll said he will continue working to ensure the people who keep Minnesota communities safe have a strong partner and advocate in St.
Paul.
About Chris Knoll Chris Knoll is CEO of Minnewaska Community Health Services and a licensed Department of Human Services provider with firsthand experience navigating state government systems.
He is a Licensed Nursing Home Administrator, having completed rigorous state and federal testing along with BCA and FBI background checks, and he maintains his licensure through annual renewals, continuing education, and strict compliance standards.
Chris earned his Bachelor's degree in Public Administration from St.
Cloud State University and, while working full-time, earned his Master's degree in Public Administration from Minnesota State University, Mankato.
Across Senate District 21, individuals and businesses are feeling the effects of overregulation and government overreach.
Chris is committed to restoring balance, strengthening the local economy, and ensuring Minnesota remains competitive.
