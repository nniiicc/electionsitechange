Voter Information
From the Oregon Secretary of State:
May 19, 2026 Primary Election Voters' Pamphlets
The Secretary is committed to serving limited English proficient voters by translating voter information for special county, city, and district elections.
Translated voters’ pamphlets for the May 19, 2026 Primary Election will be available online on April 24, 2026.
Additional election information in English may also be available on county webpages.
