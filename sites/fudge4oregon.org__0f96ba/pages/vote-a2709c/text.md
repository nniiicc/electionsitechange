Make a plan to vote!
Use the information on this page to make sure you are ready to vote in Oregon’s upcoming statewide general election.
Key Dates Voter registration deadline : October 13 Counties begin mailing ballots to active voters in Oregon : October 14 Last recommended day to return ballots by mail : October 27 Last day to return ballots using a drop box : November 3 at 8 p.m.
View a printable version of all elections deadlines .
Voter Registration View Oregon voter qualification requirements and register to vote using the online registration page.
Check or update your voter registration or check the status of your ballot using My Vote .
Ballots are not mailed to voters with an inactive status.
If My Vote shows you are inactive, please update your registration or contact your county elections office .
Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026 Ballots will be mailed to voters in Oregon starting October 14.
Election Day is the last day for a voter to return their ballot.
The Secretary of State’s office strongly encourages voters to return their ballot via a drop box as soon as they are ready to vote.
County elections offices are open 7 am - 8 pm on Election Day.
View our official Ballot Drop Box Locator to find drop box hours.
