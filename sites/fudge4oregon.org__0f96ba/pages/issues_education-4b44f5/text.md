Meet Andrew Issues Endorsements News Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Education Education I support a Classroom First approach: Audit Oregon Department of Education (ODE) programs and administrative spending, consolidate or eliminate programs that cannot demonstrate improvements in student outcomes, reduce unnecessary mandates on teachers and districts, and redirect those resources toward teachers, instructional assistants, special education, career and technical education, classroom materials, and direct student instruction.
That includes reviewing ideological or politically driven programming at ODE.
Programs should be evaluated on whether they advance Oregon's core educational mission, not protected because they are politically fashionable.
And I want an Oregon Reading Reset.
Oregon already appropriated $117.2 million for early-literacy programs in the 2025–27 budget.
The next step should be demanding measurable results from that investment: universal K–2 literacy screening, prompt parent notification when a child falls behind, evidence-based reading instruction, high-dosage tutoring and intervention, literacy coaches supporting classroom teachers, and transparent school-level reporting of reading progress.
ODE itself already recognizes high-dosage tutoring and coaching as allowable literacy interventions.
