Meet Andrew Issues Endorsements News Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Affordability Affordability The cost of living in Oregon continues to rise, putting pressure on families, seniors, and small businesses.
Oregon families should not have to earn more every year simply to stay in the same place.
I will oppose new broad-based taxes and fees on working families and scrutinize regulations that increase the cost of housing, utilities, transportation, childcare, and everyday necessities.
I will bring my experience as a CPA and state budget professional to the Legislature to ask a basic question too often ignored in Salem: What are taxpayers getting for their money?
Oregon's State School Fund alone increased 11.4% to $11.4 billion for 2025–27.
Before asking families for more, state government should prioritize existing resources, eliminate ineffective programs, and measure results.
On housing, I support increasing supply by accelerating permitting, reducing unnecessary regulatory costs and delays, and making it easier to build starter homes and other housing working families can actually afford.
Oregon has already begun imposing permitting timelines, for example, SB 974 established a 120-day deadline for final engineering-plan review; I support building on reforms that reduce unnecessary time and expense.
My goal is simple: Oregon should be a place where working families can afford to build a life, not a place they are forced to leave.
