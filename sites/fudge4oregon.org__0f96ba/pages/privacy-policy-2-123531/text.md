This information will be used to inform you about online events, campaign activities, TV appearances, and other Fudge 4 Oregon news.

Some information must be collected when you make a contribution.
When you make contributions to Fudge 4 Oregon, federal law requires us to collect your name, mailing address, employer, and occupation.
We ask that children under 18 do not submit their information.
In accordance with the Federal Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act of 1998 (COPPA), Fudge 4 Oregon does not knowingly collect information from children.
