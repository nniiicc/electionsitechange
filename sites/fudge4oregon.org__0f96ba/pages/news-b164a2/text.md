FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Date: 6/28/26
Republican Nomination Secured — Now the Real Campaign Begins
I am honored and grateful to have earned the Republican nomination for Oregon House District 20.
Republican voters across our district delivered a decisive victory, awarding our campaign nearly two-thirds of all votes cast (excluding abstentions) — the most lopsided margin in contested Republican primaries for this district in recent memory.
This overwhelming result reflects a clear desire for accountable leadership, fiscal responsibility, and practical solutions to the challenges facing Oregon families.
To every volunteer who knocked on doors, made phone calls, displayed yard signs, contributed financially, or simply cast a vote of confidence in our campaign — thank you.
This nomination belongs to all of us.
However, the campaign is far from over.
Beginning immediately, my focus is on building the broadest possible coalition of voters who believe Oregon can do better.
I am actively seeking the cross-nominations of the Independent Party of Oregon and the Libertarian Party of Oregon while continuing to earn the support of Republicans, nonaffiliated voters, independents, and commonsense Democrats who want results instead of partisan politics.
The strong turnout in this year’s election demonstrates that voters are engaged and ready for change.
I fully expect a competitive race against the incumbent this November, and our campaign is moving now — not later — to build the organization, coalition, and momentum necessary to win.
The issues that unite us are bigger than party labels.
Families want Oregon to be affordable again.
Parents want classrooms that deliver results.
Communities want safer neighborhoods and real solutions to homelessness.
Taxpayers deserve accountability from their state government.
That is the campaign we will continue to run.
Over the coming months, you’ll see us expanding our outreach across House District 20, meeting with community organizations, earning additional endorsements, and speaking with voters from every political background.
Every conversation matters, and every vote will count.
The path to victory starts today, and with your continued support, I am confident we can bring a new voice and a new direction to Salem this November.
Thank you for your trust.
Now let’s finish what we’ve started.
