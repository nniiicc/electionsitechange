Meet Andrew Issues News Contribute Volunteer Home ❭ Issues ❭ Public Safety Public Safety Keeping people safe is one of government's most fundamental responsibilities.
I support strong law enforcement, effective prosecution of violent and repeat offenders, adequate resources for first responders, and practical policies that prevent avoidable tragedies.
That includes rail safety .
Oregon communities are crossed every day by freight trains carrying fuel, chemicals, and other hazardous materials.
I support requiring two trained crew members on trains , subject to appropriate federal exceptions.
Senate Bill 723 would have established that requirement in Oregon, and most freight trains already operate with two onboard crew members.
This should not be a partisan issue.
When an emergency happens on a train, a second trained crew member can provide an additional person to respond, communicate, secure the train, and assist first responders.
Yet this common-sense rail safety measure failed to advance from committee.
My opponent, Representative Paul Evans, opposed the bill, and the committee chair stated on the record that his opposition left the bill without enough votes to pass.
I would have voted yes .
Whether the issue is crime, dangerous drugs, emergency response, or rail safety, my approach is straightforward: support our first responders, hold offenders accountable, prevent avoidable emergencies, and put public safety ahead of politics.
