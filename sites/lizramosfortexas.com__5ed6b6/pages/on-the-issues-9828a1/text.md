Affordability Affordability is under attack from big government—but I'll fight back with common-sense conservative solutions to ease the burden on Texas families.
Cut taxes and waste: Slashes wasteful spending and taxes so families can afford groceries, gas, and utilities amid inflation.
Eliminate property taxes : Works to end property taxes, protecting seniors and families from being taxed out of their homes.
Fight overregulation : Opposes rules driving up prices while championing free markets for better jobs and higher pay.
Protect Parental Rights I believe parents—not bureaucrats—know what's best for their children.
I'll champion policies that restore your God-given authority over education, health, and upbringing.
Parents control education: Ends radical indoctrination in schools, empowering families to choose curricula, books, and values that reflect their beliefs.
No medical mandates on kids: Rejects forced vaccinations and gender experiments on minors, ensuring parents' consent for all healthcare decisions.
Family privacy first: Blocks government surveillance of home life, defending your right to raise children without state interference.
Protect Our Children I stand firmly in support of the sanctity of life and am dedicated to protecting the unborn, believing every life is precious and deserving of protection beginning at conception.
Protect the unborn : Champions policies that defend the most vulnerable, ensuring their rights are safeguarded from conception onward.
Support mothers : Builds a culture that values life by providing resources and encouragement for mothers and families in need.
Cherish all life : Leads with commitment so our community trusts that the unborn will always be protected and cherished.
Border Enforcement I'll repel invasion and deter illegal immigration with bold Texas-first enforcement to protect our families, jobs, and sovereignty.
Texas Homeland Security : Creates a dedicated Department of Homeland Security to block illegal entry, stop trafficking, and deport illegals to Mexico or their home countries.
Crack down on enablers : Imposes mandatory fines and jail time on individuals, corporations, non-profits, governments, and social media for aiding or inciting illegal entry.
Mandate E-Verify & end handouts : Requires all Texas employers to use E-Verify with harsh penalties, while cutting all subsidies, public services, in-state tuition, and school enrollment for illegals—except emergency care.
Community Safety First I'll restore law and order by backing our police, cracking down on crime, and protecting our neighborhoods from radical policies that endanger families.
Back the blue : Fully funds and empowers law enforcement to remove violent criminals from our streets, rejecting defund movements that weaken public safety.
Crack down on crime : Imposes tough sentences for fentanyl dealers, human traffickers, and repeat offenders, ensuring justice is swift and certain.
Secure neighborhoods : Ends sanctuary policies, supports armed school guards, and prioritizes community policing to keep families safe at home and work.
Faith, Family, Freedom I'll defend the timeless values that built Texas strong: our God-given rights, family-first policies, and freedom from government tyranny.
Uphold faith : Protects religious liberty, ensuring churches and believers can worship freely without big-government mandates silencing prayer or conscience.
Strengthen families : Champions pro-life laws, parental rights in education, and support for marriage and traditional values that keep families thriving.
Secure freedom : Fights overregulation, high taxes, and open borders to preserve individual liberty, Second Amendment rights, and Texas sovereignty for generations.
