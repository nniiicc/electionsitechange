Bohnak Announces Campaign for State Representative
MARQUETTE, Mich.
(WLUC) – A candidate running for the 109th state house district kicked off his campaign in Marquette Friday.
Karl Bohnak spoke to supporters at the Marquette Commons about what he would like to focus on if elected.
Bohnak, a Republican, is running to replace incumbent Democrat Jenn Hill in the 109th state house district.
Bohnak says he wants to be a voice for the U.P. in Lansing.
