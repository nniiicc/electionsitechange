Karl Bohnak hosts Terri Lynn Land and Pete Hoekstra for Meet and Greet
The Karl Bohnak for State Representative campaign hosted former Secretary of State and current Wayne State Board of Governors member Terri Lynn Land and current Michigan Republican Chairman Peter Hoekstra on Wednesday, May 29th at their campaign headquarters.
Over 50 people were in attendance to hear Karl and his guests speak on the state of affairs in Michigan and how Karl will offer an entirely different approach to serving in the Legislature which will better represent the hard working families of the 109th District.
“We need to put Peninsula over politics,” said Karl Bohnak.
“I have met people from all walks of life over my last 30 years working in the U.P. and I think most people just want to keep more of their hard earned money and reduce the amount of government control in their lives.
Lansing keeps trying to take more of the decision making power out of the home, out of our cities and out of our counties.
I think it’s time for some common sense leadership and I believe we need it now.”
