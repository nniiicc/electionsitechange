Privacy Policy
Effective Date: June 19, 2026
Lauren Schoenfeld for Wyoming respects your privacy.
This policy explains how information collected through laurenschoenfeld.com may be used.
Information We Collect
We may collect information you voluntarily provide through contact forms, volunteer sign-ups, yard-sign requests, email subscriptions, or other campaign inquiries.
This may include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and message.
The website may also automatically collect basic information such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited, referral source, and general location through cookies, analytics tools, and similar technologies.
How We Use Information
Information may be used to:
- Respond to inquiries and requests
- Share campaign news and updates
- Coordinate volunteers, events, and yard-sign requests
- Improve website performance and campaign communications
- Measure advertising and outreach efforts
- Meet legal, election, and campaign-finance requirements
Contributions and Third-Party Services
Campaign contributions may be processed through a third-party platform.
Information submitted through that platform is subject to its privacy policy.
Campaign-finance laws may require certain contributor information to be collected, retained, and publicly reported.
The website may also link to social media, email, analytics, advertising, and other third-party services.
Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.
Cookies
The website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functions, analyze traffic, and measure advertising performance.
You may adjust cookie settings through your browser, although some website features may not function properly.
Information Sharing
We may share information with trusted vendors that support campaign operations, including website hosting, email, analytics, advertising, volunteer management, and compliance services.
Information may also be disclosed when required by law or necessary to protect the campaign, website users, or others.
We do not sell personal information for monetary compensation.
Data Security and Retention
We use reasonable measures to protect personal information, but no website or electronic system is completely secure.
Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, recordkeeping, and legal requirements.
Children’s Privacy
This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
Your Choices
You may unsubscribe from campaign emails using the link provided in each message.
You may also contact the campaign to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you submitted, subject to applicable legal requirements.
Policy Updates
This policy may be updated periodically.
Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
