On May 22nd, 2026, in a post on Truth Social, the 45th and 47th President of the United States, Donald J.
Trump, endorsed Kenny Cody for Tennessee's State House District, stating:
"Kenny Cody has my Complete and Total Endorsement to be the next State Representative from Tennessee’s 11th House District – HE WILL NEVER LET YOU DOWN!
"
On August 17th, 2026, Vice President JD Vance endorsed Kenny Cody, the Republican nominee for House District 11, for State Representative.
He stated, " Kenny knows the working-class issues of Cocke, Jefferson, and Hamblen counties, and as a conservative voice for his home, he will ensure the people of the 11th District are properly heard in Nashville."
Diana Harshbarger is an American pharmacist, businesswoman, and U.S.
Congresswoman for Tennessee's 1st congressional district since 2021.
Her district spans from the Tri-Cities area all the way down to all of House District 11 in northeastern Tennessee.
She endorsed Kenny's campaign for the State House on April 3rd, 2026.
Jeremy Faison is the current State Representative for Tennessee's 11th House District and is Chairman of the House Republican Caucus.
He endorsed Kenny as his replacement on February 19th, 2026.
