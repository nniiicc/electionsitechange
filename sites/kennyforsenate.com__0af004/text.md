GET UPDATES Meet KENNY Facebook Instagram Kenneth “Kenny” Cianchette is a lifelong Maine businessman, coach, and community leader.
As a Mainer, he cherishes and exemplifies hard work, determination, grit, ingenuity, generosity, and compassion above all else.
After graduating from the University of Maine in 2010 with a degree in Economics, Kenny took jobs in operations of hospitality before taking on development projects across Southern Maine.
His stewardship of the 2014 Maine Project of the Year award by the Maine Association of General Contractors validates his expertise in managing and handling large scale budgets and projects, particularly in public infrastructure.
In 2018, Kenny purchased property in Windham, renovated it, and created the widely recognized Erik’s Church Bar and Grill out of a visionary concept.
Over the past 8 years, Kenny and his team at Erik’s Church have contributed and helped raise over $500,000 for local charities and organizations.
He has single handedly brought to the lakes region high caliber acts such as Joe Nichols, Kip Moore, Parker McCollum, Lauren Alaina, Kameron Marlowe, Priscilla Block, and many more artists.
His unique and highly successful business model has revolutionized the industry within New England, and his ability to maintain high morale for staff, patrons, artists, and vendors has been recognized by winning a litany of awards.
Despite the busy schedule, he also commits his free time to mentoring high school football players in his capacity as a defensive coordinator for the varsity level.
His reason for running for Senate is simply an act of appreciation for the great State of Maine that has provided ample opportunity for Kenny.
He feels it is necessary that he runs for the Senate in order to perform the duties of a Senator properly.
He understands that, above all, our community takes priority and that he will tirelessly work for the betterment of it.
He is committed to ensuring that Augusta policy is well discussed, designed, and implemented to ensure that all Mainers can become better off than they currently are.

KENNY'S PRIORITIES I love this district and the people in it.
This is where I live, built and run my business.
You can follow me on social media to learn more about me and why I am running for state Senate, but below you can find my core priorities as it relates to our community.
PROSPERITY Mainers are on a path to poverty, not prosperity.
To rectify this, our tax policies need to be adjusted accordingly, and our energy policies need to cut out the welfare given to developers and wealthy individuals.
Kenny has publicly State his desire to eliminate income taxes, drastically increase the homestead exemption, and eliminate policies to subsidize energy which creates much higher electricity costs.
Through a series of spending cuts, shifting of burdens, and accountability at the local level, all can be accomplished.
Maine’s best, past, present and future, deserve needed relief now.
MAINE VALUES We must protect our children, first and foremost.
DHHS has become too big to be run properly.
The Office of Child and Family Services must be removed from it, and given proper oversight.
Kenny will work with the next governor to ensure that this long overdue legislation gets passed, and that actions will be taken to address its failures.
Tim Nangle has been caught lying on his willingness to support this endeavor, so only Kenny can be entrusted to take the lead on fighting this most basic, yet most critical, issue.
ENVIRONMENT Our State’s beauty is unmatched.
We must preserve its natural system, allow people to enjoy it responsibly, and embrace economic growth.
Legislation that affects our environment must be carefully considered, and modified accordingly, to promote all three of those aspects.
Projects have been put into action lately that have failed to embrace all of those standards simultaneously.
