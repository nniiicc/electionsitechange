RECENT PRESS RELEASES ​ ​ For Immediate Release to Media of Communities West of Orlando 17 November 2025 Contact: 407-554-2120 Copy of Letter to Secretary of State Marco Rubio Concerning Ukraine-Russia War Candidate Groves, the 2026 Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Congress in the 11th Congressional District, which includes the communities west of Orlando, suggests to Secretary of State Marco Rubio that the first step toward a cease-fire between Russia and Ukraine should be via a multinational task force in a demilitarized zone.
With U.S. coordination, a multinational task force should be arranged among European non-NATO countries, e.g.
Ireland, Austria, Cyprus, and Malta, as well as possibly South Africa, Israel, and Armenia, each assigned to a specific sector of a demilitarized zone.
The non-NATO status of these countries prevents Russian fear about NATO.
The demilitarized zone should be demarcated on Ukrainian territory west of Luhansk, Donetsk, Zaporizhzhia, Kherson, and Crimea -- all occupied by Russian forces.
The mission of the task force should be prevention of further Russian incursion into Ukraine while enforcing a cease-fire between the opposing sides.
In addition to ground forces, the task force should be equipped with anti-aircraft capabilities to prevent drones being used by either side against the other.
While cessation of hostilities will stop further loss of life, it will not achieve permanent peace.
Peace can only be achieved by protracted negotiations, perhaps during the course of many years.
Ralph Groves is a U.S.
Army Veteran and served in Africa.
While in Africa, he witnessed French Foreign Legion troops enforcing a cease-fire zone zone in Cote d'Ivoire between rebels and government forces during the civil war in that country.
This provided an example of a third party preventing hostilities between opposing forces.
