Ralph Groves for Congress, 2026 A New Libertarian Candidate - Socially Liberal, Economically Conservative.
Vote for RALPH GROVES, the Libertarian Party candidate in Florida’s 11th Congressional District ​ I'm Ralph Groves, former Chairman of the Libertarian Party of Orange County.
As a Libertarian, I'm not a Democrat, not a Republican, not Left, not Right.
Instead, I'm a pragmatic Constitutionalist.
As a husband to an invalid wife and a father to a special-needs daughter, I am very dedicated to both Medicare and Social Security.
In addition, I'm pro-natalist, pro-human rights, and pro-2nd Amendment.
Further, as an Army veteran and Iraq War veteran, I'm very dedicated to issues pertaining to Florida's military personnel (including National Guard), and I favor National Guard troops to be kept at home.
My campaign will give the voters of the 11th Congressional District of Florida a third choice besides the Democrats and Republicans.
A few specifics: First: Medicare fraud.
I will work with Vice President JD Vance, who was appointed by President Trump, to tackle the fraud problem. ​ Second: Uphold the War Powers Act.
I will work with Congress to ensure that Presidents commit U.S. military forces overseas only for defense of the United States, and only for up to 60 days unless Congress authorizes an extended period of time.
Third: Promote a culture that respects life.
As a pragmatic Libertarian, not an ideologue, I am neither "pro-choice" or "pro-life" in a doctrinaire way.
I believe generally that life should be promoted.
Abortions should be available, especially for medical purpose and performed in hospitals, not in outside clinics.
