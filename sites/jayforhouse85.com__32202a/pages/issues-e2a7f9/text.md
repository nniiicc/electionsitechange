Improving Roads & Bridges
South Carolina’s rapid growth and mismanagement of the state budget has put real strain on our roads and bridges—and families feel it every day in traffic, delays, and safety concerns.
Jay believes infrastructure should be a top priority, not an afterthought.
That means directing existing tax dollars where they belong, fixing bottlenecks, improving safety, and making smart investments that keep up with growth—without raising taxes.
Our communities deserve roads and bridges that are safe, reliable, and built for the future.
Judicial Reform
South Carolinians deserve a judicial system that is fair, transparent, and accountable to the people—not controlled by the lawyer-legislator class.
Jay supports meaningful judicial reform to restore trust in our courts, including greater transparency in how judges are selected and ensuring the system reflects the will of the people—not the influence of the establishment.
Justice should be impartial, consistent, and applied equally—without favoritism or backroom deals.
Medical Freedom & Individual Liberty
Jay believes every South Carolinian has the right to make their own medical decisions without government interference.
Personal health choices should remain between patients and their doctors—not bureaucrats.
Standing Up to Federal Overreach
Washington continues to push policies that impact our economy, energy costs, and daily lives.
Jay will stand strong for South Carolina values and push back against federal overreach.
Putting Parents in Charge
Parents—not the government—are responsible for their children.
Jay supports full transparency in schools, removing inappropriate materials, and ensuring families have control over their child’s education.
Ensuring Election Integrity
South Carolinians deserve confidence in our elections.
Jay supports stronger safeguards, accurate voter rolls, and clear, transparent processes to ensure every legal vote counts.
Lowering the Cost of Living
Families are feeling the pressure of rising costs.
Jay supports tax relief, including reducing the gas tax burden, to help South Carolinians keep more of their hard-earned money.
Fiscal Accountability
Government should live within its means—just like families do.
Jay will continue fighting to eliminate wasteful spending and return surplus funds to taxpayers.
Protecting Life
Every life is a gift from God.
Jay is committed to protecting life at every stage and supporting policies that uphold the dignity and value of every human being.
Defending the 2nd Amendment
Jay is a strong defender of the Second Amendment and will continue to protect the constitutional right of South Carolinians to keep and bear arms.
Immigration & State Resources
South Carolina should welcome those who come here legally, work hard, and follow the rules.
But we cannot allow those here illegally to take advantage of taxpayer-funded resources meant for our citizens.
Our state’s schools, healthcare systems, and public services should prioritize South Carolinians first—not be strained by policies that ignore the rule of law.
Law & Order / Public Safety
Safe communities are the foundation of a strong state.
I support law enforcement, tougher penalties for repeat offenders, and policies that ensure our laws are actually enforced, not selectively ignored.
That includes cracking down on illegal activity tied to open-border policies and ensuring South Carolina is not a sanctuary for lawlessness.
