Meet Jay Jay's Journal On the Issues News Endorsements Make Endorsement Volunteer Contact Donate Meet Jay Jay Kilmartin is a successful small businessman, who operates two South Carolina Melting Pot franchise locations as well as The Cigar Militia Premium Cigar Store.
Jay uses his businesses as a ministry, and supports numerous charities - primarily Second Saturday - a ministry that serves local widows, disabled, and those in need.
Jay is a graduate of the University of South Florida with a degree in Public Communications.
Jay and his wife Elizabeth live in the Irmo area, and are very active within their community.
They have been married for over 30 years!
Elizabeth and Jay met at the Hyatt in Tampa and spent many years opening restaurants around the country.
They settled in South Carolina over #ago and chose to make this their home, finally opening their own restaurants and raising their family here.
Jay and Elizabeth have two children.
Ian and Rhys.
Ian is a Coast Guard veteran and works in HVAC locally.
Rhys is a graduate of Southeastern University.
The Kilmartin family attends Christian Life Church.
Jay has been involved in the America First movement throughout South Carolina and has been on the ground supporting President Trump and conservative causes.
His voting record in the South Carolina House has been consistently conservative!
Delivering on his promise, to bring conservative common sense to Columbia.
