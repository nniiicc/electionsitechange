Rep.
Lee Snodgrass, Assembly District 52
Lee has served in the Wisconsin State Assembly since 2021.
She advocates for child care as a workforce issue, affordable housing, safe communities, upholding voting rights & democracy, investing in strong public schools, supporting small business & labor rights, and advancing and protecting LGBTQ+ equality.
She has been recognized for her collaborative leadership as ranking member on the Campaigns & Elections committee and is a vocal voice for children and families throughout the state.
Lee is a locally-focused, community-driven representative who seeks bipartisan opportunities to improve lives for all Wisconsinites whenever possible.
Lee has lived in Appleton for 30 years where she raised her family and sought every opportunity to give back to the community.
