Meet
Vik Sandhu
Small Business Owner,
Father, and Husband.
Vik Sandhu holds a Master's degree in Nautical Science and was a former Merchant Navy Officer who worked for MTM tankers for ten years, gaining first-hand experience in leadership, discipline, and global operations.
He is married to Preet Sandhu, a United States Air Force veteran, and is a proud father to their son, Teja.
Vik and his family reside in Twinsburg, Ohio, where they are deeply rooted in the community.
As a successful small-business owner, Vik has consistently worked to strengthen local economies and create opportunities for others.
He previously served as Co-Chair of the Small Business Advisory Committee for Ohio's 14th Congressional District, where he helped improve operational efficiency and support the growth and success of small businesses across the district.
Vik served as the Fiscal Officer for Richfield Township, where he delivered measurable results for taxpayers.
During his tenure, he led the township's transition from the outdated OTAS accounting system to the Auditor of State–recommended UAN system, improving transparency, accuracy, and fiscal accountability.
Through a strategic and disciplined investment approach, he also increased township earnings by up to $70,000 per year by responsibly investing public funds in STAR Ohio, maximizing returns while protecting taxpayer dollars.
Vik Sandhu brings a rare combination of private-sector entrepreneurship and hands-on local government experience.
He is committed to applying this same results-driven mindset to public policy — making government more efficient, more affordable, and more accountable — so that future generations can achieve the American Dream.
Vik is running for State Representative in Ohio's 35th House District.
The 35th District includes portions of Geauga, Portage and Summit counties.
The
Issues
Affordability for Ohio Families
Vik is fighting to make life more affordable for all Ohioans by cutting wasteful government spending and applying smart, business-minded budgeting at the state level.
Tax Reform & Economic Growth
Vik supports a low-tax, pro-growth economic environment that empowers individuals, families, and small businesses to thrive.
His priorities include:
- Lowering the state income tax
- Reforming and capping property taxes
- Eliminating unnecessary and burdensome regulations on small business
Housing & Opportunity
Vik supports policies that expand affordable housing options for young families, first-time homebuyers, and seniors — ensuring Ohio remains a place where people can live, work, and retire with dignity.
Putting Kids First
Vik is fighting for safer neighborhoods, stronger schools, and a brighter future for all of our children and young families.
Vik's Approach to Data Centers
Vik Sandhu will oppose all data center construction unless they pay their own way.
Data centers should not receive massive sweetheart tax breaks and Ohio ratepayers should not be forced to foot the bill for their utility costs.
Additionally, Vik believes local residents should be able to say "NO" to data centers if they do not want one built in their community.
