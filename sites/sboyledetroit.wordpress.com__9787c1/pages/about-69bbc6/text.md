About The Candidate
Stephen Boyle has lived in the Metro Detroit area since 1971.
He received a BS, major Computer Science from Wayne State University 1983, and a MS, major Business Information Technology from Walsh College 2005.
His working journey includes 20 years of corporate computing, photography, graphics design, plant-based cooking, and consulting.
As a community activist in Detroit he’s dedicated time to research, writing, and blogging on Social Justice issues.
He became a Green Party Michigan member in 2014 and has ran for elected office multiple times.
In 2025 his interest is in City Council at Large, due to his wide network of contacts throughout the city.
You may have seen him speak at various city governance events on topics such as mass transit, environmentalism, and community health / safety / education.
