Skip to content Stephen Boyle for State Representative District 8 A Thriving Future Depends on Political Will and Access Stephen Boyle – Community Notes About Facebook Instagram About Stephen Boyle I’m known by some to be a bus rider, others as a cook.
I’ve been a community justice advocate, photographer, researcher, writer and organizer.
My formal background is in computing – BS Computer Science 1983 from Wayne State University; MS Business Information Technology 2005 from Walsh College.
I’m a systems thinker.
In my eyes we need more representatives with an agenda from common people impacted.
I’ve seen the frustration and resignation on so many faces around Detroit.
It’s time our communities around the city are brought up – giving people a purpose to rise and shine with pride in how they brought this city back to care for those that haven’t left it.
There’s been a focus on business districts and too many failures for those to improve life across the entire city for residents.
My political alignment is closest to US Green Party .
I’m currently running as a Candidate for State Representative District 8 of Michigan in 2026; and I’ve been a GPMI candidate for Wayne County Commissioner, District 5 (2024), Michigan State Representative District 3 (2020), and US Congress in Michigan’s 14th District (2014).
For more about the Michigan Green Party, click here .
Each day each of us has the ability to “ connect people with possibility “, how are you engaged?
Normal has never offered change or evolution, so why are we asked to be normal?
Be unpredictably bold as a free spirit encountering life.
Take the mistakes as ‘missed takes’ and learn from them.
I have a few websites and plenty of social network accounts.
I’ve found the current systems we have are being confronted by the interest of people to know how things have come to be the way they are, and not like things are great.
I’m outspoken enough to have lost access a few times.
