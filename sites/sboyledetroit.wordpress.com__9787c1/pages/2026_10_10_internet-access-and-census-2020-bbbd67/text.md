I’m updating this post from my campaign for State Representative District 3 in 2020.
We have the 2030 Census getting started soon.
Being informed helps you receive the help you desire.
Systemic Oppression The Census has a Hard To Count mapping website with a number of factors.
One of these is Internet Access and it drills down to the census tract level, this is something like by neighborhood as far as how large an area.
(Note: Census tracts are also now used for Auto and Home Insurance rate adjustments in Michigan.) We’ve got a problem when about 40% of our residents can’t access the internet personally.
Especially when vital services request filling out forms and sending emails regularly.
It simply can’t be considered a reliable way to interact within District 3.
When it is depended upon the condition of “Systemic Oppression” occurs.
People who have access get heard and those without are silenced and most often uninformed.
Internet Access With internet access at risk for 40% of District 3 this is a huge campaign issue.
This can be addressed first with community wifi access, then roll into the next stage of residential access based on extreme need.
It’s going to take bringing in a State Representative that will take on this issue and know how to get it implemented.
I’m asking for your vote!
A great post discussing the hard to count Census tracts is over at Detroitography: https://detroitography.com/2018/02/12/map-census-2020-detroit-hard-to-count-areas/ Get Counted The census delivers demographic information enabling communities counted to qualify for funding parks, libraries, fire fighters, schools, and access to much more.
Literally if you aren’t counted then you don’t exist, then your community is denied the ability to receive because the count just isn’t there to prove presence.
Census Interviews If you are approached for an interview (this ends September 30 at present) – please offer 5-10 minutes and get your information entered through the interviewer.
The Census Bureau was founded with confidentiality as a requirement.
You can respond ANONYMOUSLY, please work with the census worker to get the details on number of people, race, home ownership and ages gathered.
That information qualifies our district for assistance!
At present we’ve had areas of 30% self-return and only 1-4% answering follow up interviews.
There aren’t good statistics to qualify for receiving assistance.
When a census worker doesn’t find you home they will leave a Notice of Visit.
This gives you a temporary 2-day Census ID to use in filling out the interview ONLINE or by telephone 📞 844-330-2020.
Phone support is Sunday through Saturday 7:00am to 2:00am Eastern Time.
If you are in the 40% that don’t have internet access please MAKE THE PHONE CALL.
