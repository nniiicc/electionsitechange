Skip to content Stephen Boyle – Community Notes About Facebook Instagram Streetscape Projects I have a concern with Detroit’s “Streetscape” projects.
The improvements made along these thoroughfares has an impact on the tax assessments for residents nearby.
Engagement with the public however isn’t a strong suit within Planning.
The Dexter Streetscape is a well paved road through an area of housing that needs rescue.
Any plans for businesses to open along the road will take about a year to materialize on the vacant lots.
And yes nearby business will increase assessed value of nearby residents.
I believe there should have been a concentrated start on businesses along the road.
The Dexter Elmhurst Community Center needs to be reopened.
Children deserve a destination nearby for fun and learning.
The fact is planning has subjected the community to increased assessment without delivering the economic viability for them to be met.
I live near the planned Fenkell Business Corridor (between Livernois and Wyoming)… Planning held a public meeting in March 2025 asking for information from those interested in the progress.
There were about 100 people there giving information.
We asked for monthly followup… planning set a meeting for June with the public and another for “stakeholders”… now I’m wondering how did there become stakeholders seeing as the number of blight violations has been rapidly spread along Fenkell.
Most business fronts are closed up, mothballed.
There are at least 4 churches in that mile stretch which are open.
Several event venues.
One liquor store in the middle.
No groceries.
3 gas stations at the ends.
There’s not a lot of cleared land comparing to along Dexter between Davidon and Webb.
A few business fronts may need demolished due to neglect.
I think aiming at Planning to question the motives and plans of action is definitely needed.
It certainly doesn’t give any true community building vibes to me.
That’s typically when they have a pocket of support and that’s the stakeholders being worked with.
It seems too much like systems for cronyism rather than community.
Why doesn’t Planning actually have community conversations instead of presentations?
