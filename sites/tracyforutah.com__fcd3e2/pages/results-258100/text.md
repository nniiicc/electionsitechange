Tracy Miller is a Proven Leader Getting Real Results for Our Community!
In just one term, Tracy Miller has worked to expand affordability for families, lower taxes, reduce regulation, and protect our education funding.
HB 290 — Child Tax Credit Amendments (2026)
Expanded eligibility for Utah’s child tax credit, benefiting an estimated 12,000–20,000 working families
HB 379 — Child Care Provider Food Preparation Amendments (2026)
Reduced burdensome regulations for child care providers while maintaining appropriate food-safety standards.
HB 144 — School Community Council Amendments (2026)
Ensured parent-led School Community Councils have a proper role in making recommendations regarding safe technology utilization.
HB 184 — School Trust Land Amendments (2025)
Improved transparency and accountability for School LAND Trust funding.
HB 410 — Child Care Amendments (2025)
Required preschool teaching experience to count for educator relicensing and allowed limited development funds for child-care facilities.
HB 268 — Nonresident Online School Amendments (2025)
Reduced administrative burden for schools when a student attends another district’s online school.
HB 76 — Public Education Revisions (2025)
Made technical changes to fix several education provisions, creating greater flexibility and reducing duplicative requirements.
