While other school systems cave to Biden-era policies, DEI mandates, and teachers’ unions pushing far-left classroom agendas,… Read More → Issues and Concerns Public Safety & 2nd Amendment Doesn’t it make sense that we have safe communities — and strong rights?
I will ALWAYS support our law enforcement officers and first responders Empower police — don’t defund them Back the blue — no apologies Stop soft-on-crime policies Keep fentanyl and illegal drugs out of our neighborhoods Make it easier for law-abiding citizens to carry and protect themselves and loved ones Education & Parent Rights Doesn’t it make sense for parents to raise their children — not bureaucrats in Annapolis?
Local boards, not state politicians or unelected bodies, should run our local schools End political agendas and ideologies in the classroom Focus on academics — not activism More reading, math, science, history — less indoctrination Expand career and tech ed in high schools and community colleges Schools should NEVER keep secrets from parents Land Use & Local Control Doesn’t it make sense that we protect our farmland — and our freedom?
Fight the 70-mile MPRP transmission line that threatens rural land and property rights Stop state mandates that override local zoning decisions Keep Annapolis and out-of-state interests out of our land decisions Preserve our agricultural heritage and rural character Ensure responsible development of high-quality housing Taxes, Jobs & Less Government Doesn’t it make sense that our government should only spend what it can afford?
Cut income taxes and eliminate hidden fees Oppose tax hikes and tolls from Annapolis Support small businesses that create local jobs Slash red tape and reduce regulations Shrink bloated state agencies and government waste Taxpayers should keep more of their hard-earned money Girls’ Sports & Biological Reality Doesn’t it make sense that boys don’t belong in girls’ sports or private spaces?
There are only two genders — male and female Protect the privacy, dignity, and safety of girls and women Defend fair competition and scholarships for female athletes Address rising mental health concerns, not promote confusion Veterans & Military Families Doesn’t it make sense that we honor those who have served our nation?
Cut red tape for veterans seeking housing or jobs End state and local taxes on military pensions Eliminate property taxes for disabled veterans (70%+ rated) Guarantee quality care and support for military families Healthcare & Life Doesn’t it make sense that parents and doctors, not bureaucrats, decide care?
No mandates for masks or vaccines — ever again No irreversible medical procedures on children Protect life from conception No taxpayer funding for abortion Respect parental authority in all medical decisions Upcoming Events Notice There are no upcoming events.
