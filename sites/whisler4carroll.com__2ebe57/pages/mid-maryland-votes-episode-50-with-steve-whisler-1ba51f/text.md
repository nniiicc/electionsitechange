Mid Maryland Votes – Episode 50 with Steve Whisler
Carroll County is getting it right for our kids — and here’s why.
While other school systems cave to Biden-era policies, DEI mandates, and teachers’ unions pushing far-left classroom agendas, Carroll County said NO!
Their conservative school board and superintendent rejected political activism in the classroom and went back to what schools are actually for:
✅ Teaching kids to read, write, and think
✅ Discipline and accountability
✅ High expectations and real outcomes
❌ No ideological curriculum
❌ No union-driven politics
❌ No soft-discipline chaos
The result?
Carroll County students are outperforming their neighbors — academically, behaviorally, and in life readiness.
In Episode 50, we talk with Steve Whisler about how this happened, why it matters, and what other counties are too afraid to admit.
