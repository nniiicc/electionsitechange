About me
My roots run deep in the coastal communities of our district.
My Mom’s side of the family is from Cordova, and my husband’s family goes back 70 years in Kodiak.
I grew up commercial fishing in Prince William Sound, and was an educator in Kodiak for three decades.
We have been fortunate to have a representative that has used a bipartisan approach to deliver for our infrastructure, fisheries, schools, and ferries.
I intend to advocate in the legislature for our communities in the same way because I care deeply and know working together will bring the most benefits to our coastal families.
- Ensuring coastal communities continue to offer good jobs, whether that’s in fishing or the military
- Kids in this district get a great education
- We have reliable ferry service
- The people from here continue to see a future here, for themselves and their kids
