Issues & Platform
Family, community, and public service are the pillars of Michele's life and will be the foundation of her work in Annapolis.
Strengthening Communities
Supporting safe and welcoming neighborhoods and communities across District 37B for all — including our immigrant friends and neighbors.
Champion for Families, Youth, and Seniors
Supporting quality public schools, libraries, parks, higher education, child care, access to healthcare, and housing.
Creating Economic Opportunity
Empowering small local businesses, watermen, and farmers; supporting heritage tourism, leveraging Chesapeake College and the new hospital; and creating well paying jobs of the future.
Protecting our Environment For Future Generations
Countering climate change, and prioritizing clean air, clean water, flood protection, and public water access.
Supporting our Public Servants
Creating opportunities that enable our educators, healthcare workers, public safety workers, fire and rescue workers, and local government employees to live where they work.
