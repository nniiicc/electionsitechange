Johnson Candidate Maryland House of Delegates District 37B Talbot · Caroline · Dorchester · Wicomico Get Involved Donate Now Rooted in Maryland Michele is an experienced public servant and community leader with strong Maryland roots who will listen, engage, and deliver for the people and communities of District 37B.
20+ Years nonprofit leadership 10+ Years government experience 20+ Years volunteer & board service Putting Community and People First Family, community, and public service are the pillars of Michele's life and will be the foundation of her work in Annapolis.
Strengthening Communities Supporting safe and welcoming neighborhoods and communities across District 37B for all — including our immigrant friends and neighbors.
Champion for Families, Youth, and Seniors Supporting quality public schools, libraries, parks, higher education, child care, access to healthcare, and housing.
Creating Economic Opportunity Empowering small local businesses, watermen, and farmers; supporting heritage tourism, leveraging Chesapeake College and the new hospital; and creating well paying jobs of the future.
Protecting our Environment For Future Generations Countering climate change, and prioritizing clean air, clean water, flood protection, and public water access.
Supporting our Public Servants Creating opportunities that enable our educators, healthcare workers, public safety workers, fire and rescue workers, and local government employees to live where they work.
