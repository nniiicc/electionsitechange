Stay Informed, Stay Empowered, Stay South Dakota
In the past, the values of South Dakota’s lawmakers have been to please their donors rather than the people they have sworn to represent.
Being born and raised in South Dakota, I understand the values of traditions that we hold dear to our hearts.
I also understand, however, the growth we need to work towards as a state, both socially and economically.
Rather than engaging in big corporate greed, I want to encourage the small businesses, the helpful neighbors, and the betterment of Lawrence County.
