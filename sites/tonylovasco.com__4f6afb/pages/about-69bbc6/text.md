I'm a native Missourian and lifelong St.
Charles County resident.
I've been married to my wonderful wife Eva since 2014.
We met while both volunteering for the St.
Charles County Liberty Project, a nonpartisan political advocacy group that works to increase awareness of liberty issues in the County and surrounding areas.
I was previously elected to the Missouri House of Representatives in 2018, and represented District 64 until 2024.
I also served for 7 years as Township 11 Committeman on the St.
Charles County Republican Central Committee.
Additionally, I'm currently on the board of the Lewis & Clark Pachyderms, a local chapter of the National Federation of Pachyderms.
Since 2001, I've worked in sales, selling computer and related office equipment.
I also have experience in Linux software deployment and maintenance, and have maintained the Nexradix GNU/Linux distribution since its inception in 2008.
