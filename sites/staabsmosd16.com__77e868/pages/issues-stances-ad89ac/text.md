This campaign is rooted in a simple but essential principle: the people of Missouri deserve clear, honest, and unbiased information about the actions and decisions of their elected officials.
Transparent & Responsive Leadership Public service should be built on communication and accountability.
I believe elected officials have a responsibility not only to inform their constituents, but to actively listen.
Before casting any vote, a representative should first consider the will and needs of the people they serve—and act accordingly.
People-First Governance My guiding philosophy is that government exists to support its citizens, not to restrict them.
Tax revenue should be used responsibly to protect, strengthen, and uplift our communities.
Healthcare Freedom Healthcare decisions are deeply personal.
I believe those choices should be made between a patient and their doctor, with the involvement of family when desired—free from unnecessary government interference.
Workers’ Rights & Fair Treatment I stand firmly in support of workers across all industries.
Whether union or non-union, every employee deserves fair wages, reasonable working hours, and guaranteed breaks.
Strong communities are built on the dignity and stability of their workforce.
Responsible Leadership Over Special Interests Leadership should prioritize people—not large donors or corporate interests.
Every policy decision should be evaluated based on how it impacts the everyday lives of Missourians.
Protecting Our Future Missouri is home to remarkable natural beauty and resilient communities.
We must take care of our environment, support local economies, and invest in our people to ensure our state remains a place where families can thrive, businesses can grow, and future generations can succeed.
