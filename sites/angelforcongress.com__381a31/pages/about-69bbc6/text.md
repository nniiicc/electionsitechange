“The Experience to Lead and the Heart to Serve” I am Genevieve Angel, a dedicated Lakewood resident for nearly 30 years and a proud mother and wife who built her family right here in Southern California after moving from Manila, Philippines.
My foundation is built on education and action: BSN — University of Santo Tomas, 1992 MBA — AIU Los Angeles, 2007 MSN / Family Nurse Practitioner (Magna Cum Laude), 2025 2 years of Law — Pacific Coast University I don't hold degrees in journalism or political science, nor am I a career bureaucrat — I hold a degree in saving lives.
As a senior healthcare executive with extensive experience in hospitals, health plans, and community services, I am a proven leader dedicated to bringing actionable, positive change to our community.
