As of February 2026, the Los Angeles area experienced a 2.9% headline inflation rate.
Food prices rose 3.1%, while energy prices increased 0.2%.
Over five years, California grocery costs have risen 26%, and gasoline excise taxes are expected to increase further, compounding the pressure on families.
