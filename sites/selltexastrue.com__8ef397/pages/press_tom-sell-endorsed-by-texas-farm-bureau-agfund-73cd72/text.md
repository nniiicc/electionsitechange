Tom Sell Endorsed by Texas Farm Bureau AGFUND
Lubbock, TX — Tom Sell, candidate for Congress in Texas’ 19th Congressional District, expressed sincere gratitude for the endorsement of the Texas Farm Bureau AGFUND, the state’s largest grassroots organization — representing more than 480,000 member families including the farm and ranch families that embody America’s first and most foundational industry.
Texas Farm Bureau AGFUND President Russell Boening added, “We have worked closely with Tom on key issues affecting Texas agriculture, and he has consistently shown strong leadership, a clear understanding of agricultural policy, and genuine dedication to the farm and ranch families who work to provide a strong food and fiber supply.
His steady approach, integrity, and commitment to practical solutions make him well‑suited to serve in Congress, and we are proud to stand behind Tom as he seeks this important role.”
The endorsement highlights Sell’s depth and credibility built over his years of professional advocacy and stemming from his deep West Texas roots.
Sell has demonstrated his commitment to protecting agriculture, defending farmers, and fighting back against burdensome regulations that hurt rural communities.
“Our farm and ranch families who take on extraordinary risk to do this foundational work need a strong voice in Washington,” said Tom Sell.
“I’m honored to earn the support of the Texas Farm Bureau.
I understand firsthand the challenges our farmers and ranchers face, and I’ll always fight to protect their livelihoods and way of life.”
Sell noted his gratitude for similar endorsements from other great grassroots agricultural organizations including: Plains Cotton Growers, Inc., Texas and Southwestern Cattle Raisers Association Cattle Raisers PAC, Texas Corn Producers, National Sorghum Producers, Texas Wheat Producers, Rolling Plains Cotton Growers and the Southwest Council of Agribusiness.
