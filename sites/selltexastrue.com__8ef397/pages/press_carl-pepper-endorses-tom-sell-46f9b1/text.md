Lubbock, TX — Today, conservative candidate Tom Sell announced the endorsement of State Representative Carl Tepper, adding to the growing wave of support behind his campaign as it heads into the upcoming runoff election.
“We need someone with a record of achievement and Tom Sell is easily the choice in the race.
He has the expertise and experience to represent District 19; and indeed, he’s the most capable and ready to make contributions that will benefit the entire country,” said Rep.
Carl Tepper.
“I’m honored to have the support of Rep.
Tepper,” said Tom Sell.
“He’s a respected conservative leader, and I’m grateful to have him standing with our campaign as we work to unite voters and win this runoff.” The endorsement underscores Sell’s continued momentum as conservatives coalesce around his campaign.
Backed by grassroots energy and trusted leaders, Sell is emerging as the clear conservative choice in the race.
