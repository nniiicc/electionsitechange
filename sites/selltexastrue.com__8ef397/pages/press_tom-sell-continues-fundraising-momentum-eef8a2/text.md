Home About Press Issues Endorsements Volunteer Donate Early Voting: October 19-30 Election Day: November 3 ↵ Back to Press December 10, 2025 Press Release Tom Sell Endorsed by Texas Farm Bureau AGFUND Tom Sell, candidate for Congress in Texas’ 19th Congressional District, expressed sincere gratitude for the endorsement of the Texas Farm Bureau AGFUND, the state’s largest grassroots organization — representing more than 480,000 member families including the farm and ranch families that embody America’s first and most foundational industry.
Lubbock, TX — Tom Sell, conservative leader and candidate for Congress in Texas’s 19th District, today announced he has reached over $400,000 in financial support, strengthening the momentum behind his campaign.
Alongside the fundraising news, Sell also released an initial list of supporters and the members of his Campaign Ambassadors, local business and community leaders from across West Texas.
Sell said the continued outpouring of support shows the district is ready for a representative who will fight for West Texas values, promote America First agriculture, and bring steady, proven leadership to Congress.
