Cody Campbell Endorses Tom Sell for Congress in Texas' 19th District
Lubbock, TX — Today, congressional candidate Tom Sell announced the endorsement of prominent West Texas businessman and Texas Tech leader Cody Campbell, highlighting Sell’s deep ties to the region, commitment to Texas Tech, and dedication to the future of the South Plains.
Campbell, a fourth-generation Red Raider and native West Texan, praised Sell’s leadership and lifelong connection to the university and the region.
“As a fourth generation Red Raider and native West Texan, I know firsthand the importance and opportunities that Texas Tech provides to West Texas and to the entire state.
Tom Sell is also a dedicated, life-long Red Raider, and his commitment to the University and to our community is unparalleled.
Tom also understands the incredible potential to continue and even accelerate the incredible success our University has achieved and knows what it takes to ensure a prosperous future for the entire South Plains.
I can attest that Tom possesses the energy, enthusiasm, loyalty, and common-sense to fight for us, and I know that he commands great respect in the halls of Congress.
He will represent the 19th district with strength, resolve, and commitment to our shared values.
We can count on Tom to stand with President Trump, to protect our West Texas way of life, and to make Raider Nation proud!”
Sell said he is honored to earn Campbell’s support.
“Cody Campbell is a respected leader who has done so much for Texas Tech and for West Texas.
I’m grateful for his support and proud to stand alongside him as we fight for our shared values, protect our West Texas way of life, and deliver strong conservative leadership for the 19th District.”
Sell, a 5th-generation West Texan, rancher, and businessman, is running to bring conservative leadership and West Texas values to Washington.
