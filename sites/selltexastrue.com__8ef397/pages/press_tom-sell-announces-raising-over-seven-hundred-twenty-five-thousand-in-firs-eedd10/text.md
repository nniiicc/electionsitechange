Tom Sell Announces Raising Over $725,000 in First Quarter of Campaign
The impressive fundraising total reflects widespread enthusiasm from individual donors, conservatives, farmers, ranchers, and energy workers who believe in Sell’s commitment to fighting for West Texas values and delivering real results in Washington.
“I’m honored and energized by this outpouring of trust and support.
I love West Texas and will work to give our people, businesses and institutions the representation they deserve.” said Tom Sell.
“Voters want a conservative fighter who will secure the border, tackle inflation, unleash American energy, and stand up for the hardworking families who keep this country running.
I’m grateful for the overwhelming support we’re seeing across the district.”
The campaign enters the next phase of the race with significant financial strength, positioning Sell as a top contender in the race.
Sell, a fifth-generation West Texan, continues to campaign aggressively throughout the district, meeting with voters and community leaders while building a strong grassroots coalition focused on protecting West Texas jobs in agriculture and energy.
The campaign will continue to expand its voter outreach efforts, roll out additional endorsements, and build on this momentum in the coming weeks.
