| These past few weeks have been both busy and exhilarating out on the campaign trail!
On January 30th, we held an organizational meeting attended by over 50 volunteers and supporters.
It was great seeing so many old friends, but I was also excited to see so many new faces in the room.
At this meeting, we began our nomination paper drive to collect the signatures required to qualify for the ballot.
Last week, we officially submitted our first batch of signatures!
Thanks to the help of dozens of volunteers in Lexington and Woburn, we were able to exceed our signature collection goal.
We've also been very busy hosting coffees, meet & greets, and house parties across the district.
We have multiple public events scheduled every week and more are being added daily.
