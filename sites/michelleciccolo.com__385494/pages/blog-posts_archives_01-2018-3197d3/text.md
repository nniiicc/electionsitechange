1/16/2018 Comments Thank you, friends!
Last month, I told you about an aggressive goal we set for our campaign -- 100 contributions before the December 31st fundraising deadline.
Thanks to your incredible generosity, we surpassed our goal with over 214 donations!
I am so grateful and humbled by your support and belief in me.
Now, it's critical that we continue to build our grassroots organization.
I hope you'll join our team for our first organizational meeting.
Here are the details: Campaign Organizational Meeting Tuesday, January 30th 5:30 - 7:00pm Lexington Depot 13 Depot Square, Lexington All are welcome!
We'd love for you to come by to learn more about our campaign, talk about our shared vision for Lexington and Woburn, and get involved!
If you can't make it and would like to host a meet & greet, be listed as a public endorser, and volunteer in other ways, please click HERE.
We need everyone to sign up and share your support of our campaign.
I look forward to seeing you soon!
