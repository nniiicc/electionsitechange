A Small Businessman Jason grew up working in his family’s vending machine business.

Community Leader Jason is a member of the Moore Chamber of Commerce, Rotary International (where he led the annual Christmas gift drive) and formerly Earlywine Park’s YMCA Council.
He was a founding Board member of the Honor Flight Initiative of Oklahoma, which flies WWII vets to D.C. to visit/participate in war memorials and ceremonies.
