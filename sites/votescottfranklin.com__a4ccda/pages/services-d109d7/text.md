PROTECTING OUR AGRICULTURE
Agriculture is a cornerstone of Florida’s 18th Congressional District and a driving force behind our local economy.
The farmers, growers, and ranchers across our region feed our nation and the world, while supporting thousands of jobs and sustaining rural communities throughout Florida’s Heartland.
They are also among the best stewards of our land and water.
Florida producers continue to lead the way by implementing Best Management Practices and investing in new technologies to protect our natural resources while maintaining productivity.
But Florida agriculture faces real and growing challenges.
Recent freezes and severe weather have devastated citrus and other specialty crops, placing enormous strain on producers who are already operating on thin margins.
These events are not just setbacks, they are existential threats to family operations and the long term future of our domestic food supply.
A strong domestic food supply is critical to our national security and economic stability, especially in times of uncertainty.
That is why in Congress, I am working to ensure our agriculture industry remains strong, resilient, and globally competitive.
As Vice Chair of the Agriculture Appropriations Subcommittee, I am focused on delivering real support for our producers.
That includes advancing disaster assistance through block grants to ensure resources reach Florida farmers quickly and efficiently, supporting citrus recovery efforts, investing in research and disease mitigation, and pushing for policies that reduce unnecessary regulatory burdens.
I will continue fighting to ensure our farmers, growers, and ranchers have the tools they need to recover, rebuild, and succeed, and to keep Florida agriculture thriving for generations to come.
