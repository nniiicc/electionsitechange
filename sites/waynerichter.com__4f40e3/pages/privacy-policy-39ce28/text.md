Privacy Policy
Wayne Richter for Florida State House 85 is committed to protecting your privacy.
When you sign up to volunteer, donate, subscribe to updates, or contact the campaign, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and any information you choose to provide.
We use this information to:
- Communicate with you about the campaign
- Respond to your questions and requests
- Organize volunteer activities and events
- Send campaign updates and fundraising communications
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
Information may be shared with trusted service providers who assist with campaign operations, fundraising, communications, or legal compliance.
You may unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in our messages.
We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide through this website.
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like your information removed from our records, please contact the campaign through the contact information provided on this website.This applies to https://www.waynerichter.com/ only.
