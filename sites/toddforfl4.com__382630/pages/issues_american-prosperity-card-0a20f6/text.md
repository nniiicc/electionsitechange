The Problem
American families and businesses are quietly losing $600+ billion every year to hidden banking subsidies and low-yield accounts.
That money subsidizes big banks and foreign holders of our debt instead of flowing back to the people who earned it.
How the American Prosperity Card Works
- Free Treasury-backed debit card for every adult American (18+).
- Your money earns the full risk-free Treasury rate (~4.5% today) — paid monthly.
- Zero fees for you.
Use it anywhere debit is accepted.
- Capped swipe fees redirect savings into the American Prosperity Fund.
- Built on existing TreasuryDirect infrastructure — no new bureaucracy, no CBDC.
Real example: Average household balance of $5,000–$6,000 could mean $190–$270 extra per year — every year — with zero risk.
Why This Is So Much Bigger
- High MPC Feedback Loop: Spending bills and policy decisions create immediate, visible effects on your monthly yield and balance.
- Ends Fed Monopoly: Citizens and businesses become a direct transmission mechanism.
No more waiting for Fed meetings — instant feedback cools hot economies or bolsters weak ones.
- Transparency at Last: Wasteful spending hits your account.
Responsible governance increases your returns.
Small Businesses — and All Businesses — Win Big Too
Any business with an EIN (LLC, corporation, partnership, etc.) can use these Treasury-backed accounts.
Owners or accountants simply act as the authorized account manager.
Businesses lose billions to high swipe fees (2–3.5%) and near-zero interest on operating cash.
The APCA delivers near-zero fees and full Treasury yields.
The more businesses participate — regardless of size — the larger the Prosperity Fund grows for everyone.
No caps.
No means-testing.
More participation = more benefit for regular Americans.
What Happens to the Savings (~$215–260 Billion/Year at scale)
- 50% → Stronger Earned Income Tax Credit and Child Tax Credit for working families.
- 30% → Direct paydown of the national debt (priority: reduce foreign holdings).
- 20% → Administration (shrinks as adoption grows).
Every 1% uptake is projected to deliver roughly a $90–$100 annual boost to expanded EITC/CTC benefits for ~25–28 million working families — plus additional gains from business participation.
Built-In Safeguards
- Explicitly not a CBDC — heavy penalties for any attempt to convert it.
- Ironclad financial privacy — no surveillance, private right of action with big damages.
- Fully voluntary — opt in or opt out anytime.
- Fund protected by 2/3 congressional vote requirement to redirect.
