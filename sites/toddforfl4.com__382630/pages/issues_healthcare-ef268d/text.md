Volunteer Contact Home ❭ Issues ❭ Healthcare Healthcare Why are some Americans opposed to universal healthcare?
"They'd raise my taxes to pay for it"...
Well, the truth is you are paying that tax already in the form of premiums, co-pays, prescription drug costs.
We pay that tax by subsidizing the development of drugs and then allowing drug companies to keep all of the profits when they hit the market.
We pay that tax when someone without insurance goes to the hospital and we subsidize their care in our premiums.
We pay that tax when we don't go to see a doctor because we don't know if it is a $500 problem or a $5000 problem.
We are paying for it in the 40k+ Americans who die each year for lack of health care.
We are already paying for it, AND we are overpaying for it.
Why are politicians opposed to universal healthcare?
Maybe ask them...
I'd venture to say it is cold hard cash.
In the 2024 election almost $64,000 was contributed from the health industry here in FL-04 and across the country health industry contributions total hundreds of millions annually, influencing policy to protect profits over people.
Now ask yourself...
What are they buying with all that money?
Why are businesses so opposed to universal healthcare?
Businesses oppose it to tie workers to jobs and create leverage and gaps in coverage.
Large corporations leverage their scale to bargain for better plans and lower premiums, giving them an edge in attracting talent.
This job lock stifles worker mobility, preventing people from pursuing better opportunities or starting their own ventures—hurting the working class and economic dynamism in FL-04.
Have you ever had a job that you hated but stayed for the health insurance or turned down a job you would have loved because of their inferior health insurance?
Why should businesses embrace universal healthcare?
Small businesses lack the leverage of big corporations, often paying 25% higher premiums, which creates an unlevel playing field and stifles competition and growth.
Universal coverage would free them from these disparities, allowing focus on innovation and hiring while ensuring a healthier, more productive workforce.
