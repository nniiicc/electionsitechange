people to Carson City.

Also, next week we have November 11 th , Veterans Day.
On that day and every day, we need to
remember our veterans and the measure they gave for us in the United States to have the
blessings of freedom that we do have.
We should never forget that all gave some and some
gave all.
For almost 100 years we have celebrated November 11 th , first as Armistice Day to
honor the end of the Great War (World War 1) on the 11 th hour of the 11 th day of the 11 th
month, 1918 and then in the 1950s as Veterans Day.
