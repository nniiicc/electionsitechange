Kumar is the President and CEO of Frontload Group LLC, a holding company focused on investment and portfolio management.
Over the course of his career, he has successfully built and exited multiple companies and has advised organizations across a wide range of industries, including technology, media, energy, real estate, education, and food and beverage production.
His work has often involved identifying inefficiencies, improving operations, and removing barriers to growth.

Kumar is a regular contributor to financial news media, with a focus on educating individuals who may feel hesitant or intimidated when asking questions about finance and economics.

Kumar has a long-standing commitment to charitable and community-focused causes.
He serves on the Board of Directors of the Lupus Society of Illinois and the Gayle Sayers Foundation.
He previously served with Children’s Home & Aid (now Brightpoint) and The Chicago Lighthouse for the Visually Impaired.
He also founded The Same Day Foundation in honor of his late father and cousin, with a mission centered on improving the lives of children and families.

Kumar grew up in Milwaukee, Wisconsin and graduated cum laude from University School of Milwaukee.
