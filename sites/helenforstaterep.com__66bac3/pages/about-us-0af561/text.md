My Story
I was born in Shanghai, China, and came to America with my family as a high school student.
I graduated from Glasgow High School in Delaware, then earned my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Accountancy and Economics from Purdue University in West Lafayette, Indiana.
In 1998, I moved to Houston to begin my career with a Big 4 accounting firm—and I never left.
I instantly fell in love with Houston’s bright lights, warm weather, and limitless opportunity.
This city became my home, and I have never stopped working to make it better for all of us.
