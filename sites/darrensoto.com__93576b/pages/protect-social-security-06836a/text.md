Protect Social Security
Darren is fighting to protect Social Security for our seniors and future generations.
You pay into the program.
It’s your money, and it should be there when you need it.
If Congress continues to do nothing, there will be an estimated 25% cut to Social Security payments in 2032.
Darren is ready to act to stop these cuts.
That’s why he supports the Social Security 2100 Act and similar legislation that would scrap the income cap, currently at $184,500, and fully fund Social Security to 2100 and beyond.
