Stop Corruption
We have seen a disturbing rise in corruption in Washington: Billionaire ballrooms, pay-for-pardon schemes, and insider stock trading.
Darren continues to call out this corruption and vote to hold those responsible accountable.
We must bring balance back to Washington to ensure these scandals come to an end.
Darren also has supported legislation to stop stock trading by Members of Congress and the administration.
He has never personally owned any shares of stock and strongly believes that members should not trade while in office.
