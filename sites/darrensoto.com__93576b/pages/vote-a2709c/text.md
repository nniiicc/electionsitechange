HOW TO VOTE IN YOUR COUNTY?
Important Voting Dates
- Voter Registration Deadline: October 5, 2026
- Vote-by-Mail Request Deadline: October 22, 2026, by 5:00 p.m.
- Election Day: Tuesday, November 3, 2026 — polls are open from 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
- Vote-by-Mail Ballot Deadline: Please put your ballot in the mail by October 26, or drop it in a dropbox.
Completed ballots must be received by your county Supervisor of Elections by 7:00 p.m. on November 3, 2026.
Early voting dates vary by county.
