He has twice served on the Agriculture Committee during Farm Bill years.

Darren secured WRDA authorization for a new Taylor Creek Reservoir in South Osceola County.
This project will be critical for the water needs of Central Florida agriculture for years to come.
He has taken a hands-on approach, visiting and listening to local ranchers, growers, and farmers throughout the district over the years.
He will always have their backs, both at home and in Congress, to help them feed America.
