Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy explains how the Jamie Davis for U.S.
Senate Campaign Committee ("Campaign," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you access or use our websites, mobile sites, forms, and other online services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the "Site").
This Privacy Policy also applies to information collected in connection with SMS and MMS text messages sent by or on behalf of the Campaign.
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
If we make changes, we will revise the "Last Updated" date above and may provide additional notice where appropriate.
Collection of Information
Information You Provide to Us
We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us, including when you:
- Sign up for campaign updates;
- Opt in to receive text messages;
- Volunteer;
- Register for an event;
- Complete a form, survey, or petition;
- Make a contribution;
- Purchase campaign merchandise;
- Contact the Campaign; or
- Otherwise interact with us.
Information you provide may include:
- Name;
- Email address;
- Mobile or other phone number;
- Mailing address;
- ZIP code;
- Donation or transaction information;
- Volunteer and event information;
- Communications preferences; and
- Other information you choose to provide.
Payment information associated with contributions or purchases may be collected and processed directly by third-party fundraising or payment platforms.
Federal campaign finance law may require the Campaign to collect, maintain, and report certain contributor information, including, when applicable, a contributor's name, mailing address, occupation, employer, and contribution information.
Information Collected Automatically
When you use our Site, we and service providers operating on our behalf may automatically collect information such as:
- Browser and device type;
- Operating system;
- IP address;
- Pages viewed;
- Referring pages;
- Dates and times of access;
- Approximate location based on IP address;
- Transaction-related information; and
- Information collected through cookies and similar technologies.
We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate and improve the Site, understand usage, measure campaign communications, and support campaign outreach.
Information From Other Sources
We may receive information from lawful third-party and public sources, including public voter files, publicly available records, political or campaign data providers, and other data services.
Such information may include contact information, voter-related information, demographic or geographic information, and publicly available information.
A mobile phone number obtained from a voter file, public record, data provider, or other third-party source is not treated as consent to receive recurring SMS or MMS messages from the Campaign.
SMS messaging consent is collected separately through the Campaign's applicable opt-in process.
How We Use Information
We may use information to:
- Operate, maintain, and improve our Site;
- Communicate with supporters, volunteers, voters, donors, and other individuals;
- Provide campaign news and updates;
- Conduct voter outreach and get-out-the-vote activities;
- Administer events, volunteer activities, petitions, and surveys;
- Process and maintain contribution and transaction records;
- Respond to requests and inquiries;
- Analyze engagement with campaign communications;
- Protect our systems and prevent misuse;
- Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations; and
- Conduct other lawful campaign activities.
SMS and MMS Text Messaging
How SMS Consent Is Collected
Individuals may opt in to receive SMS or MMS messages from the Campaign by voluntarily submitting their mobile phone number through a Campaign website or other approved opt-in method and affirmatively agreeing to receive text messages.
SMS consent is separate from information obtained through voter files, publicly available records, data providers, or other third-party sources.
The Campaign does not treat possession of a mobile phone number from a voter file or other third-party source as SMS consent.
When you affirmatively opt in to the Campaign's messaging program, we may send recurring campaign-related text messages, including:
- Campaign news and updates;
- Election and voting information;
- Event information;
- Volunteer opportunities;
- Calls to action;
- Fundraising messages; and
- Other campaign communications consistent with your consent.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not a condition of making a contribution, purchasing merchandise, volunteering, or otherwise interacting with the Campaign.
Mobile Information and Messaging Consent
We do not sell, rent, share, or provide your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in information, or SMS messaging consent to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
SMS consent provided to the Jamie Davis for U.S.
Senate Campaign is specific to communications from or on behalf of the Campaign and is not sold, transferred, or provided to another organization so that organization may independently market or promote its products, services, causes, or communications.
Messaging Service Providers
The Campaign uses service providers, including messaging platforms such as CallHub and/or Twilio, to transmit, manage, and support Campaign text messages.
These providers may process your mobile phone number and messaging-related information solely as necessary to provide messaging services on behalf of the Campaign.
Providing information to a service provider for this limited operational purpose does not authorize that provider to use your SMS opt-in information or messaging consent for its own independent marketing or promotional purposes.
The protections for mobile information and SMS consent described in this section apply notwithstanding any other disclosure provision in this Privacy Policy.
Opting Out
You may opt out of Campaign text messages at any time by replying STOP.
After opting out, you may receive a final confirmation message.
You will not receive further messages from that messaging program unless you subsequently opt in again.
For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at info@jamieforlouisiana.com.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Contributions and ActBlue

Where lawful and appropriate, information other than SMS opt-in data or SMS messaging consent may be used in connection with these activities.
We do not provide SMS opt-in information or SMS messaging consent to advertising platforms, affiliates, data brokers, or other third parties for their marketing or promotional purposes.
SMS consent is not used as authorization to disclose an individual's mobile information to advertising platforms or other organizations for independent marketing or promotional purposes.
