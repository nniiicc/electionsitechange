Donate Kevin Burge is a U.S.
Marine Corps Veteran, husband, father, Texas Democrat running for Congress to fight for ordinary working families.
Kevin’s top priorities include affordable housing, restoring checks & balances, medicare for all, immigration reform, protecting civil liberties, tuition-free public college & trade education, restoring abortion rights, and building an economy that works for everyone — not billionaires.
With over 20 years of leadership experience working alongside Marines and patriots, including two deployments to Iraq, and one to Afghanistan, he is a proven, tested, and bold leader ready to defeat MAGA extremist Beth Van Duyne and focus on delivering a better future for all.
Together with his wife who immigrated to America from Honduras, and is a commissioned officer in the Texas Army National Guard, Kevin’s family resides in Tarrant County, where their son is proud and excited to attend a great neighborhood public school.
