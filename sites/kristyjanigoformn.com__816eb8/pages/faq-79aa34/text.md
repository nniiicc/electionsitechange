About And Background
Who is Kristy Janigo?
Kristy Janigo is a former Army National Guard servicemember, current Maple Grove City Council member, and candidate for Minnesota Senate District 37.
She has dedicated her life to public service through the military, local government, veterans advocacy, and helping Minnesotans access public services.
What communities are in Minnesota Senate District 37?
Minnesota Senate District 37 includes Corcoran, Greenfield, Independence, Loretto, Maple Grove, Maple Plain, and Medina.
What is Kristy’s military background?
Kristy joined the North Dakota Army National Guard at age 17 and served for six years after 9/11, reaching the rank of E-5 sergeant.
Her military service helped shape her commitment to veterans, public safety, and practical public service.
What is Kristy’s education and professional background?
Kristy is a graduate of public schools and land-grant universities.
She earned bachelor’s degrees from North Dakota State University and a Ph.D. in design and sustainability from the University of Minnesota.
She has worked in global manufacturing, higher education, local government, and veterans advocacy.
Platform And Legislative Priorities
What are Kristy Janigo’s main priorities for the State Senate?
Kristy’s priorities include bringing down costs for families, improving access to quality healthcare, strengthening public education, fighting fraud and rebuilding trust in government, developing economic security, promoting public safety, standing up for Minnesota veterans, and investing in local infrastructure.
What is Kristy’s plan to lower costs for families?
Kristy believes families are being pressed by the rising cost of housing, education, transportation, healthcare, and food.
She will focus on practical steps that reduce the cost of essential needs, support economic opportunity, and help families maintain a middle-class life.
Where does Kristy stand on healthcare?
Kristy believes healthcare should be accessible, affordable, and close to home.
She supports bringing down healthcare costs, keeping local clinics and hospitals open, reducing unreasonable delays, supporting caregivers, and making it easier for seniors, people with disabilities, veterans, and working families to get the care they need.
Where does Kristy stand on public education?
Kristy is the daughter of two teachers, a public school graduate, and a former higher education leader.
She supports strong public schools, career and technical education, special education, school mental health resources, and multiple paths after graduation, including trades, apprenticeships, college, military service, and direct entry into the workforce.
What is Kristy’s approach to local infrastructure?
As a city council member, Kristy understands the challenges cities face in delivering essential public services.
She supports stronger transit connections, reliable broadband, state support for roads, bridges, public buildings, water and sewer systems, and practical help for cities dealing with PFAS in drinking water.
What is Kristy’s approach to economic security?
Kristy believes Minnesotans need workplace opportunities that allow them to live with dignity, support their families, and pursue their purpose.
District 37 is home to major employers in med tech and other industries, and Kristy supports an environment where big businesses, small farms, entrepreneurs, and working people can thrive.
Integrity And Financial Oversight
What steps will Kristy take to fight fraud and protect taxpayer dollars?
Kristy believes fraud in government programs is unacceptable because it raises costs, abuses taxpayer trust, and harms people who rely on public support.
She supports swift investigation and prosecution of fraud, creating an independent Office of the Inspector General, regular in person site visit audits for state programs, stronger penalties for fraud, stronger whistleblower protections, thorough restitution processes for fraudsters, and banning politicians convicted of fraud in Minnesota from running for office again.
What experience does Kristy have with oversight and fraud prevention?
Kristy works in local government as a contract manager with a behavioral health services portfolio.
She conducts site visits and financial reviews and ensures public dollars go where they are supposed to go.
Her campaign ad also highlights her work protecting taxpayer dollars from waste and holding bad actors accountable.
Does Kristy have legislative experience?
Yes.
For three years, Kristy served as a volunteer legislative advocate for two statewide veterans nonprofits at the State Capitol.
She has drafted bill language, testified in committee hearings, worked with lawmakers of both parties, and helped move veterans legislation through the process with the successful passage into law of several bills to enhance veterans resources in Minnesota.
Veterans And Public Service
What has Kristy done for Minnesota veterans?
Kristy has worked directly with homeless and justice-involved veterans to help them access stable housing, food, income, education, benefits, and other resources.
She has also advocated at the State Capitol for veterans policy, including efforts to stop claim sharks, improve access to Veterans Treatment Courts, support women veterans, and protect veterans service organizations.
What veterans legislation has Kristy helped advance?
Kristy successfully helped pass four pieces of legislation supported by the Minnesota Association of County Veterans Service Officers during the 2024 and 2025 legislative sessions.
These included funding related to veteran food insecurity, grant funding for MACVSO technical assistance coordinators, changes related to community observances for the military community, and helping county veterans service officers access vital statistics documents for benefits work.
What awards or recognition has Kristy received for her public service?
Kristy received the Steven A.
Pihlaja Justice Partners Award from the Fourth Judicial District for her advocacy with Veterans Treatment Court participants’ veterans benefits claims.
She received the Minnesota Association of County Veterans Service Officers President’s Award in 2025 for her legislative work.
She was recognized at the Minnesota United Football Club August 10, 2025 halftime show, with the Eye of the Loon Award for deserving military service members from the community.
The nonprofit Bill’s Wish presented her with the Spotlight A Soldier Award at their 2025 Rise and Remember event as a leader in preventing veteran suicide and substance use.
Clarifying Questions about Kristy
Is Kristy Janigo focused only on veterans issues?
No.
Veterans advocacy is a major part of Kristy’s public service record, but her campaign priorities also include affordability, healthcare, education, fraud prevention, economic security, public safety, infrastructure, rights and freedoms, and environmental conservation.
Does Kristy support accountability in government spending?
Yes.
Kristy has made fighting fraud, rebuilding trust, and protecting taxpayer dollars central campaign themes and she is the only candidate in the race with experience preventing fraud in government programs.
She calls for stronger investigations, prosecution, penalties, whistleblower protections, and an independent Office of the Inspector General.
