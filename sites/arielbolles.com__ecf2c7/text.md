We live in a time of deep contradictions.
The national and international news makes me feel hopeless and helpless.
And , I feel more connected than ever to my friends and neighbors.
I see my tax dollars funding endless wars, enriching wealthy tax evaders, and imprisoning innocent people looking for a better life in our country.
And I’m inspired by people in Vermont and elsewhere speaking out and fighting back, on the streets and through legal action, policy and mutual aid.
I refuse to be complacent.
Here in Vermont, at a state and local level — whether in Town Meetings, Town Committee Zoom calls, PTO meetings, School Board meetings or the State House — I see Democracy that works, where every citizen and every vote can have an impact.
I see neighbors who are motivated and want to connect and be heard.
This is a moment that requires moral clarity, vision, and urgency.
Because I’m running for my kids, and everyone else’s kids.
To make Vermont better than ever.
