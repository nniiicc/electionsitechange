Taxes & Affordability: Empower Your Financial Decisions The state is increasing spending while delivering fewer results.
That money belongs in your pocket, not the legislature's ledger.
Your Choice: Keep more of your income and decide whether to save, invest, or spend it on what your family needs most.
Environment, Climate & Energy: Tailor Solutions to Your Needs Current mandates imposed by special interests threaten your property rights and heating bills.
Your Choice: Decide for yourself how to heat your home, what car to drive, and how to develop your land without fear of fines or lawsuits.
Healthcare: Access Personalized Care Options You shouldn't be restricted to a single insurance network that limits your options and drives up prices.
Your Choice: Select from multiple insurance providers, choose your own doctors, and access care that meets your specific health goals.
Workforce & Economy: Build a Future Where we all Can Prosper With high costs forcing families out of our state, the issue isn't just about wages—it's about whether you can afford to live here.
Your Choice: Raise your children in a community where housing is affordable, businesses are thriving, and there are real opportunities for everyone.
Education: Invest in a System That Delivers Results We are paying one of the highest per-pupil rates in the nation while seeing results decline.
You deserve better value for your school taxes.
Your Choice: Support an education system that prioritizes your child's future success and vocational skills over administrative overhead.
