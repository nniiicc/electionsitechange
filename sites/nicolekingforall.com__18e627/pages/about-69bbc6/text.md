Nicole King is running for Texas House District 3 because Montgomery County deserves a real choice—and leadership rooted in the everyday lives of the people who call it home.
The through line
People first.
Always.
Life has a way of showing us who we are in difficult moments.
When storms hit, systems break down, and families are left to navigate the aftermath, the true measure of a community is not found in a slogan.
It is found in the people who roll up their sleeves and show up.
At three years old, Nicole entered foster care.
Soon after, she was adopted into a loving blended family of ten children.
Growing up in that household taught her that strength is shared: you listen, you rely on one another, and you weather the hard seasons together.
That is the kind of public service she wants to bring to Austin—proactive, practical, and accountable to the neighbors who sent her there.
