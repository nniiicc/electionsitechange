Nicole King is running for Texas House District 3 to put people at the heart of every decision—and help build a Montgomery County that is more affordable, better prepared, and ready to move forward together.
Join the campaign See where Nicole stands → Advocate / Unifier / Leader Why Nicole A life shaped by community.
Placed into foster care at age three and adopted into a blended family of ten, Nicole learned early that nobody builds a strong community alone.
You build it by sharing the load, listening closely, and showing up when it matters.
That same belief guides her campaign for the Texas House: practical leadership that makes housing and everyday life more affordable, strengthens public schools, and prepares families before the next storm.
I value compassion and want to make sure that people who pay into the Texas economy can reap its benefits.
