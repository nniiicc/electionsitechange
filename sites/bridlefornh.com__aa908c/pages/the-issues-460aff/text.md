The issues that shape our future.
- Local Control & Home Rule As a Republican, Nicholas believes in the power of local governance.
He's worked across the aisle to empower Hampton's leaders and voters to make decisions about zoning, housing, and development that reflect our community's unique needs and values.
- Fiscal Responsibility & Taxpayer Protection Nicholas is a proud fiscal conservative.
On the Hampton Municipal Budget Committee and in the State House, he's fought to keep spending in check, oppose new taxes, and ensure Hampton taxpayers get the best value for their hard-earned dollars.
- Broadening the Seacoast Tax Base Nicholas knows that the key to reducing the tax burden on Hampton families is to grow our economic pie.
That's why he's championed policies to encourage business growth, attract new enterprises to the Seacoast, and create good-paying jobs for our residents.
By broadening our tax base, we can fund our priorities while keeping taxes low for everyone.
- Supporting Our Veterans Inspired by the service of his own family members, Nicholas is a staunch advocate for New Hampshire's veterans.
He's worked to ensure they have access to the benefits, services, and opportunities they've earned, regardless of party affiliation.
- Championing Hampton's Tourism Economy As the only candidate representing every corner of Hampton, Nicholas understands that our vitality is tied to the health of our tourism industry.
He's championed robust funding for tourism promotion, coastal infrastructure investments, and policies that support our small businesses and keep our economy thriving.
- Standing with First Responders As an emergency dispatcher and former President of the NH Emergency Dispatchers Association, Nicholas knows firsthand the challenges our first responders face.
In Concord, he's fought for the resources, training, and mental health support they need to keep all of Hampton safe, secure, and well-served.
- Preserving Our Shared Natural Resources Hampton's coastline, state parks, and natural beauty belong to all of us.
That's why Nicholas has been a steadfast shepherd of these resources, advocating for policies that protect our environment, preserve our open spaces, and ensure Hampton remains a great place to live, work, and raise a family for every generation to come.
- Comprehensive Mental Health & Substance Abuse Support The COVID-19 pandemic and opioid epidemic have deepened New Hampshire's mental health and addiction crises, touching families across the political spectrum.
Nicholas champions a holistic, compassionate approach spanning prevention, treatment, recovery, and criminal justice reform.
He's fought for increased funding and access to services for all Hampton residents in need, regardless of background.
By tackling these interconnected issues head-on, we can save lives, strengthen families, and keep our community healthy.

- Holding regular office hours and town halls to hear directly from Hampton residents
