About Tim Tim Anderson is a lifelong Nebraskan, husband, father of five, former volunteer firefighter, and farmer proudly serving as the Mayor of Sutton.
Born and raised in Clay County, Tim brings a track record of results and a Nebraska work ethic to everything he does.
What drives him is simple: faith in God, love for family, and a commitment to serving the great State of Nebraska.
As Mayor of Sutton, Tim: Declared emergency and led city-wide recovery after 2023 tornado Balanced the budget and increased community investment without raising taxes Launched participatory budgeting based on citizen input Developed new housing and infrastructure east of Sutton Promoted youth sports and community engagement through locally run programs Improved city finances through innovative fund management Tim knows that too often, politicians forget who they work for.
As a mayor, farmer, and father, he’s lived the challenges rural Nebraskans face—and he’s ready to deliver real solutions.
