Let’s Solve Parking
Parking is a hard problem.
Our homes often don’t have enough parking, which leads to congested on-street parking, conflicts amongst neighbors, and less space for sidewalks.
I’d like your help to understand who needs parking and who has extra parking.
Once we have this baseline information, I’ll work with stakeholders to build a public policy which allows this extra parking to be utilized by those who need it.
All responses are confidential and will not be published.
