Café LOUIE is a free, informal event where community members can meet with their state and local elected officials to discuss important issues.
All Metro and state elected officials are invited to participate, giving constituents an opportunity to ask questions, share concerns, and engage in meaningful conversations.
Anyone is welcome to attend as a guest to meet officials and join the discussion.
How Café LOUIE Works
Grab a cup of Heine Brothers coffee when you arrive.
Write down your question(s) on a provided notecard.
A trained facilitator will guide the discussion, ensuring officials have a chance to respond.
Meetings start at 9:00 a.m. and are held at Louisville Free Public Library locations.
