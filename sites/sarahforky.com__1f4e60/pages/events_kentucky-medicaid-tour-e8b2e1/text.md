The reality is that some will lose coverage.
Legislators need to hear directly from those who will be impacted, and individuals are invited to come and share their story of why Medicaid matters.
Everyone is invited to attend this free community event.
The House and Senate Democrats are hosting these events; however, Republican House and Senate members will be invited and encouraged to attend.
