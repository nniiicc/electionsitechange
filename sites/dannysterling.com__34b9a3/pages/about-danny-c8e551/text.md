Danny Sterling was born and raised in Noble, where his family owned a dairy farm.
He attended Noble Public Schools and graduated from Oklahoma State University in the top #% of his class.
He holds bachelor’s and master’s degrees in agriculture education from OSU.
Danny worked at Tecumseh Public Schools for 40 years, where he taught agriculture education and served as assistant principal and principal at Tecumseh High School.
He served for 17 years as co-director and manager of the Central District Livestock Show in Shawnee.
He also served as President of the Oklahoma Agriculture Education Teachers Association.
Active in the community, Danny has served on the Pottawatomie County Fair Board and the Shawnee Agri-Business Task Force.
An avid outdoorsman, Danny developed and maintains fish and wildlife habitats on his farm.
He also runs a small cattle operation on his Tecumseh homestead.
He is a member of the NRA.
Danny and his wife, Jenna, have four sons and six grandchildren.
They are members of Faith Christian Church, where they help sponsor missions, youth activities and camps.

Danny Sterling is a dedicated and respected leader in the Oklahoma House of Representatives.
He is Chairman of the A&B Judiciary Subcommittee, and Vice Chairman of the Common Education Committee.
