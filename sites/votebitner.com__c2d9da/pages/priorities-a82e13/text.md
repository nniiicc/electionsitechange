Fiscal Responsibility & Affordability As costs rise on everything from groceries to housing, my priority will be keeping taxes low and ensuring our state remains fiscally responsible.
I will insist that tax dollars are spent wisely and efficiently, and I will oppose unnecessary or irresponsible tax increases.
Conservative Values I believe in the principles of the Republican Party and the values that have made Utah strong.
Our state leads the nation because of our commitment to faith, strong families, personal responsibility, and service to others.
I will defend our constitutional freedoms, protect individual liberty, and stand up for Utah values at the Capitol.
Limited Government As your representative, I will work to keep government accountable and transparent, with decisions made as close to families and local communities as possible.
I believe government should respect your home, your business, and your way of life and stay out of the way whenever possible.
Strengthening Education A strong education system is essential to our children’s future.
I will advocate for high standards, parental involvement, and local control in education.
I will support parents and teachers in preparing students for the future without imposing a one-size-fits-all approach from the state or federal government.
Defending Our Water & Quality of Life I will work to support locally driven solutions that conserve water, protect the Great Salt Lake, preserve access to the outdoors, and help our communities grow responsibly while respecting private property rights.
