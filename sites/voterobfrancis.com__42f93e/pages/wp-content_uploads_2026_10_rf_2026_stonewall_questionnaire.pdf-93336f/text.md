Please describe in detail your experience with the LGBTQ+ Community.
What have you done to be a.) a vocal ally or b.) to fight for your fellow Pennsylvanians in the LGBTQ+ Community?
I am a gay person who was born and raised in southwestern Pennsylvania, and that has been a lifetime experience that shaped my views.
Experiencing firsthand from a young age the isolation, discrimination, and existential bigotry that comes with that, I also discovered individuality, profound and open love for people I may never know, and the insatiable need to see life made easier for people not only like me, but also those who are not.
An outspoken advocate for (and lifetime member of) the LGBTQIA+ community, I served as president of my community college’s Gay-Straight Alliance, and during my time as a counselor for survivors of domestic violence and sexual assault, one of the areas I specialized in was LGBTQIA+ and often had to fiercely advocate for the rights and privacy of my gay/lesbian and transgender clients.
I’ve done a number of mental health outreach and sexual literacy programs focused on LGBTQIA+ youth, and have been a longtime supporter of the HRC, The Trevor Project, and occasionally work with the president of out local PFLAG chapter (Greensburg).

5.
How do you intend to protect the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, its LGBTQ+ Community, and people peacefully protesting in support, from Federal over-reach?
I would support or introduce legislation that would expand hate crime statutes to include sexual orientation and gender identity, eliminate the “panic” defense (specifically regarding transgender and gay people), ban conversion therapy, amend name change laws to remove public notification and cut the red tape for changing on legal documentation.
I also strongly support The Fairness Act, codifying same-sex marriage, public education efforts to end stigmas against the LGBTQ+ community (particularly regarding ability to be parents, substance abuse, living with HIV, and other outdated prejudices), and would support pursuing legal action against the federal government when necessary.

How do you intend to advocate for equitable and respectful treatment for trans citizens and youth in (as applicable): a.
Healthcare, e.g., accessibility of gender affirming treatment I would support or introduce legislation modeled after the Transgender Health Care Access Act so that rural and other underserved areas are properly trained and funded to provide appropriate care.
I would block or oppose any attempts to limit gender affirming care or discriminatory healthcare restrictions that would disproportionately and negatively impact the lives of people within the LGBTQIA+ community and their families. b.
Fair jobs and housing Introduce or support legislation that protect individuals from discrimination based on gender identity/expression, remove barriers to changing names and gender identifiers on legal documentation, and block or oppose legislation that would cause harm or prejudicial treatment of transgender individuals, particularly youth.
I would support the action or formation of committees or commissions that are dedicated to the protection of LGBTQIA+ rights and holding accountable those who would violate them. c.
Education and the school system, e.g., the right to use gender affirming facilities, fair participation in school sports All students at any public school have the right to feel safe while freely expressing themselves.
I strongly support the use of gender affirming facilities and will always advocate for equitable treatment of transgender students in their education, health, and community participation.
I would advocate for funds and resource allocations that would allow our public education system to provide a more rounded understanding of gender identity/expression, support for the students and faculty who need it, and stand firmly behind the youths and families that these issues affect daily. d.
The justice system, the prison system, & LGBTQ+ safe rehabilitation I would introduce or support legislation that explicitly prohibits discrimination against individuals in the justice or prison systems (including misgendering or using non-preferred names or identifying pronouns, retaliatory action based on sexual orientation or gender identity/expression, or forcing medications, harmful therapeutic treatments, or isolation on LGBTQIA+ individuals), and ending mandatory prison sentences.
I would also provide oversight and site inspections/visits to local prisons, and regular checks for human rights violations. e.
The military, veterans affairs, and federal jobs I would coordinate with and advocate for state and local VA offices to ensure optimal and equitable care is being provided for all of our veterans.
I would also support House resolutions that reject discriminatory practices by the DOD, advocate regularly for the fair treatment of our National Guard and other service members, and work with advocacy groups and local National Guard and VA sites to ensure fair and equitable treatment of our veterans and the dispersing of their benefits. f.
Collecting Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity (SOGI) demographic data As long as SOGI data is voluntary, secured, and the purpose of collecting the data is to understand and address inequalities or disparities in our health, economic, judicial, housing, education, employment and other systems, I support it.
If, however, there are not clear limitations on what data is collected, why, how, and with whom it is shared, this creates a human rights issue.
I would support or introduce legislation and/or oversight to ensure data is collected ethically, kept private and secure, and utilized responsibly.
