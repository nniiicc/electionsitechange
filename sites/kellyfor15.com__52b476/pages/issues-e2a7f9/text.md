MIKE KELLY CONTINUES TO
Fight for lower healthcare costs
Mike Kelly is a relentless advocate for lowering healthcare costs, working to make medical care and prescription drugs more affordable for all.
His efforts are aimed at easing the financial burden on families and ensuring everyone has access to the healthcare they need.
Restoring access to skilled trades
Recognizing the importance of vocational education, Representative Mike Kelly is dedicated to restoring access to skilled trades training.
His initiatives support the development of job-ready skills, providing valuable opportunities for individuals to secure good jobs and drive economic growth.
Advocating for women
Mike Kelly is a staunch advocate for working women and women’s health.
He supports policies that ensure fair treatment in the workplace and safeguard access to essential healthcare services.
His dedication to advancing women’s rights and health reflects his commitment to equality and well-being for all.
