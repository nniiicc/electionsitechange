My Commitment
I am not going to agree with Democrats on everything.
I am not going to agree with Republicans on everything.
That is the point.
I am an Independent because I want the freedom to look at every issue, listen to the people I represent, and make the decision I believe is right.
Sometimes that will mean working with Democrats.
Sometimes it will mean working with Republicans.
Sometimes it will mean telling both parties they are wrong.
What will not change is who I work for.
The people of Illinois' Fourth District.
People over Party.
