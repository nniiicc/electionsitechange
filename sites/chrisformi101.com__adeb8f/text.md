Key Priorities Responsible, Fair, and Effective Three ideals that should be cornerstones of any government.
Unfortunately, this has not been the case for the communities of the 101st House District, or for rural Michigan, in Lansing, for a long time.
While some individuals may share some of the blame, most of it lies with the state government's complicated structures and the special interests that manipulate them.
The Michigan Democratic and Republican Parties have failed rural Michigan.
The MDP ignores us, and the RDP leadership takes advantage of us year after year.
It is time for this to change.
My campaign and policy priorities are centered on what I call "the backroads initiative." It is high time that the families, communities, and local governments along such byways have their issues and concerns addressed.
To guarantee this is the case, I am not seeking any endorsements or accepting campaign donations from any special interest groups (link to Chris's response letter below).
It is time for our needs, concerns, hopes, and ways of life to be addressed.
In Lansing, I will make the rural voice heard.
Quality of Life According to the latest Asset Limited, Income Constrained, Employed Report (ALICE) from the United Way Foundation, over 40% of Michigan households struggle to cover basic living costs, including housing, childcare, food, health care, transportation, and necessary technology.
In other words, many of our fellow Michiganders suffer from a chronically detrimental quality of life.
(This is reflected in the 101st District by county in the table below) According to the US Census Bureau, in 2024, 61,757 households were identified in the five counties in the table above.
Of those, 26,046 (42%) fell below the ALICE threshold.
In other words, approximately half of the people living in the 101st District struggle to make ends meet, thus preventing any financial security or economic mobility and negatively impacting their potential for a respectable "quality of life." Unfortunately, the usual governmental response to the various factors contributing to the underlying socio-economic conditions is too simplistic and unilateral.
As stated above, these are not mutually exclusive issues, and to improve the lives of those affected, lawmakers need to develop a more robust, comprehensive, multilateral approach that addresses several factors at once.
Think of it as a stool.
To raise the stool level, you must increase the length of all legs concurrently and incrementally; otherwise, the stool will fall.
Life, Liberty, and the pursuit of Happiness One of the most important foundational phrases ever written at the dawn of any nation.
Unfortunately, after two and a half centuries of struggle to fulfill such ideals, they seem to be at risk or nearly eliminated today.
As both Michiganders and Americans, we need to renew our vows to these ideals more than ever and do all we can to ensure that everyone, no matter who they are, can savor them, as is their inalienable right.
Central to my campaign is my commitment to working tirelessly to ensure and restore everyone's freedom of self-determination, privacy, and property security.
