MEET BRANDON
Brandon was born and raised in Maumelle.
Following graduation from Arkansas Baptist High School, Brandon attended the University of Arkansas at Fayetteville to study Biochemistry and spent 1 semester abroad in Dublin, Ireland where he would discover his passion for pharmacy.
Throughout pharmacy school, Brandon worked at Argenta Drug in North Little Rock, where he met his now wife and fellow pharmacist, Kaley.
Brandon and Kaley now co-own several independent community pharmacies across central and rural eastern Arkansas.
To date, they have helped to establish and continue the unique access to locally owned pharmacy care in 13 communities.
He and Kaley attend Central Hope Church in Little Rock and spend most of their free time trying to keep up with their beautiful 4 year-old daughter Savvy and her mini-aussie Gus.

Frequently asked questions
LOWER TAXES
As a small business owner, Brandon Achor knows strong economies empower Arkansas families.
As State Representative, he voted to slash the state income tax by over 25%, down to 3.7%, eliminate the state grocery tax and increased the Homestead Tax Credit by $375, putting Over One Billion Dollars back in taxpayers’ pockets.
In the State Senate, Achor will continue fighting for lower taxes, reduced regulations, and pro-business policies to create jobs and drive economic growth.
WELFARE TO WORK
As a steadfast conservative Republican, Brandon Achor champions policies that reduce welfare dependency and promote confident and competent self-reliance.
He supported laws requiring able-bodied adults in public housing to work or volunteer, responsibly reducing cash welfare time limits for work-eligible households to 12 months—among the lowest nationally—and requiring job-ready welfare applicants to seek work during the application process.
These reforms encourage workforce participation and protect taxpayer dollars.
In the State Senate, Achor will advance conservative policies that empower Arkansans through opportunity.
CRIMINAL JUSTICE
Having been robbed at knifepoint in his pharmacy with his wife and employees present, Brandon Achor has seen and felt the threat of violent crime and is deeply committed to public safety.
As a conservative Republican, he supported transformative legislation reforming Arkansas’s criminal justice system by strengthening parole processes, increasing minimum sentencing times, enhancing victim notification, and improving corrections training.
In the State Senate, Achor will fight for tough-on-crime policies that hold violent offenders accountable and prioritize victims’ rights.
His personal resolve ensures safer Arkansas communities..
EDUCATION
As a staunch conservative Republican, Brandon Achor is dedicated to empowering Arkansas families through educational freedom and excellence.
He proudly supported the LEARNS Act of 2023, a historic reform that expands school choice through Education Freedom Accounts, boosts teacher salaries, and enhances school safety measures.
In the State Senate, Achor will continue championing policies that prioritize parental rights, improve academic outcomes, and ensure every child has access to a quality education.
His conservative leadership drives bold reforms to strengthen Arkansas’s schools for future generations.
