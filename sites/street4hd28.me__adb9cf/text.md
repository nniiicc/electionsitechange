Born and raised in Nashville, he built his career around listening carefully, solving tough problems, and helping people through conflict.
After years of reporting on education funding and quality, Gordon earned his Ph.D. in clinical psychology and eventually settled in Maine with his wife, Lee, to raise their two daughters.
In Lincoln, he provides mental health care to adults, teens, and children, and was elected to the Town Council in November of 2023 with more votes than any other candidate.
Gordon now wants to take that same calm, commonsense approach to the state level—working to lower property taxes, grow local jobs, strengthen public schools, protect Maine’s natural environment, and help heal our political divide.
