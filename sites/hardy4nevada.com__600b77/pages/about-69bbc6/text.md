Meet Melissa
Fourth generation Nevadan.
Henderson small business owner.
Elected to the Assembly for District 22 four times.
Melissa was inspired at a young age by her parents.
Her father, Mel Close, served as Assembly Speaker and in the Nevada Senate.
Her mother, Connie, served as an aide to Senator Paul Laxalt.
From them she learned that public service is a way of giving back to the community and helping Nevada keep growing and prospering for future generations.
My goal is simple.
Work in a statesman like way to find solutions to the problems facing Nevada families.
Nevada roots
Melissa is a fourth generation Nevadan from the pioneer Stewart family that settled the Las Vegas valley in the early 1900s.
She graduated from Las Vegas High School and earned her Bachelors degree in Hotel Administration and Business Management from UNLV.
Family
Melissa and her husband Damon have been married for over thirty five years and have been blessed with two daughters, both of whom attended Clark County public schools and graduated from UNLV.
They have also had three adorable grandsons join their family now growing up in the Henderson community.
A working life in Henderson
Damon and Melissa obtained their real estate licenses in Nevada and bought and sold real estate as a team for over 15 years.
In 2005 they became small business owners when they opened a Port of Subs franchise in Henderson and ran it successfully for over fifteen years, consistently earning awards for customer service and sales productivity.
Service in the Assembly
In the Assembly, Melissa earned a reputation as a hard worker, a diligent advocate, and a fearless champion for healthcare access, quality education, public safety, and economic opportunity.
She has served four terms as the Assemblywoman for District 22 and looks forward to continuing her hard work and dedication to the Henderson community.
