Every candidate has a resume.
Guy Reschenthaler is a son of western Pennsylvania, born here and raised in the values and traditions that make our region great.
Guy graduated from Penn State’s Behrend campus and completed law school at Duquesne.
After law school, Guy fulfilled a lifelong dream and joined the US Navy Judge Advocate General Corps and volunteered for duty in Iraq.
In Baghdad, Guy made daily trips into the Red Zone and prosecuted nearly 100 terrorists, including some of the most dangerous terrorists in the Middle East.
Guy cross examined the Butcher of Fallujah in defense of 3 US Navy SEALS wrongfully prosecuted by the liberal Obama administration.
The SEALS were fully acquitted of these highly politicized charges, thanks in large part to the contributions of Lt.
Guy Reschenthaler.
Guy was elected to the State Senate in November 2015, winning a special election to represent the citizens of the 37th Senatorial District, which includes portions of Allegheny County and Washington County.
As a state senator, Guy is a leading conservative voice in the Republican caucus.
Guy is a fighter for cutting spending, lowering taxes, and eliminating job-killing regulations.
Guy Reschenthaler was born to lead and called to serve.
He kept our nation safe and he’ll take our values to Congress.
