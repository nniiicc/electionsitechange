My campaign is not about Nashville or big-city politics—it is about the heart of Tennessee, and the heart of Tennessee beats in all 95 counties .
I made a promise to lead as a true servant, and to do that, I must hear directly from every community.
That is why I am launching Drive 95 , a mission to visit every single corner of our great state between now and the November 3, 2026, election.
Why Drive 95 Matters As a Christian Independent and a 40-year veteran of Tennessee public education, I know that real solutions are found on the ground, not in a capital building.
I want to look you in the eye, listen to your concerns about Mental Health , Homelessness/Poverty , and Education , and other issues that face our state and discuss how we can bring New Results to our communities.
This is a low-budget, grassroots campaign relying on the spirit and hospitality of Tennesseans.
Every stop is a chance for fellowship, listening, and prayer.
