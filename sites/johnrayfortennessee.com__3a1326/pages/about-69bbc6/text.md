Throughout his tenure, he has served on several important House committees, including the health and commerce committees.
Since 2022, John Ray has also served in a leadership capacity as the chair of the Tennessee House Democratic Caucus.
On November 4, 2014, Nashville voters first elected John Ray to serve them as their state representative for District 55 in the Tennessee House of Representative.
Though it was significantly redrawn in 2022, District 55 is and has always been located wholly within Metropolitan Nashville and Davidson County throughout John Ray’s tenure.
He and his wife, Tamara, are the proud parents of John, Finn, Henry, and their dog, Walter.
John Ray practices law with his Nashville-based boutique civil litigation firm, and he is also a Rule 31 listed mediator.
In his free time, he coaches Little League, plays ice hockey, reads, attends his sons’ many sporting events, and spends as much time with his family as possible.
John Ray is a Fellow with the Nashville Bar Foundation, a former Barrister with the American Inns of Court, and a member of the Nashville and Tennessee Bar Associations.
He has enjoyed providing pro bono legal services to local artists and entrepreneurs through the Volunteer Lawyers & Professionals for the Arts, a program of the Arts & Business Council of Greater Nashville.
Prior to serving in elected office, John Ray served our community in multiple other capacities.
He remains an active ex officio member of Belmont-Hillsboro Neighbors, Inc., having served as its president.
He sits on the board of Friends of Sevier Park, Inc., a non-profit he co-founded with other neighborhood leaders.
As the former chairman of the board for Nashville RBI (Reviving Baseball in Inner-Cities), John Ray enjoyed the opportunity to combine two of his passions – baseball and public service.
He also served on the Metro Board of Fair Commissioners, as an appointee of Mayor Karl Dean, and the Tennessee Civil Service Commission, as an appointee of Gov.
Phil Bredesen.
Additionally, John Ray continues to represent his alma mater as a member of the Columbia Alumni Association of Nashville.
