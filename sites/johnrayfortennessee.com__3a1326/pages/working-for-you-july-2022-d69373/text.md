July 6, 2022
| I hope all of you had a wonderful and safe Fourth of July weekend.
I enjoyed seeing many of you while attending neighborhood celebrations in both the current and newly redrawn District 55, celebrating from Sylvan Park to Whitland to Cane Ridge.
Thank you to all of the volunteers who organized and executed these great family-friendly events.
CAMPAIGN 2022 Our campaign for re-election continues this coming weekend when I will be hosting two ice cream socials in Woodbine and Antioch on July 9th and 10th respectively.
If you reside in the new District 55, please come out and visit with me and other local officials and share your ideas and valuable opinions.
We will be handing out our new TeamJRC t-shirts to the first 100 attendees who register at each event.
If you would like to volunteer for either or both of the events, sign up here.
All volunteers will get a free TeamJRC t-shirt (and probably a hug from my mom).
SUPPORT Your financial support makes all the difference as I work to communicate with voters and stay in touch with the residents of District 55.
We have already delivered two pieces of mail to primary voters and purchased new campaign materials for use and distribution at events throughout the district.
We have four weeks until the primary election day and four months until Election Day, so much work remains.
Please consider donating today.
YARD SIGNS If you reside in the new District 55 and would like a yard sign, please email me.
SPREAD THE WORD If you have any friends or family who reside in the new District 55, please encourage them to reach out, sign up for my e-newsletter, and join the conversation on my various social media accounts (Facebook \| Twitter \| Instagram). |
