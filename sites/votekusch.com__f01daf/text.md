Matthias Kusch Matthias Kusch is a retired Hawaiʻi Fire Department Battalion Chief, farmer, and longtime East Hawaiʻi community advocate now serving House District 1.
Since taking office following the passing of Representative Mark Nakashima in 2024, he has organized and attended nearly 50 community meetings across the district — listening to residents and working to make Hawaiʻi Island more affordable for working families and kūpuna.
