Leonard for Delegate I was born and raised in Damascus MD, and moved to Rohrersville with my wife in 2018; we welcomed our wonderful son into this world in 2024.
An avid Boy Scout, I spent my younger years hiking, camping, and running around in the woods by my house.
Nowadays I ...still mostly do those same things, when I have the free time.
We live on a small flower farm, owned and operated by my spouse; I myself work as an electrical estimator.
I’m tired of politicians who only think about where they're going, and not where they came from.
I'm tired of every local issue only being expressed as it relates to national politics, as if tackling our own problems has no importance on its own, but only matters in how it affects a much grander political game.
That's not me.
I work for Maryland.
What happens in California or in Texas is not my job, and not my concern.
I am a Democrat; of the two major parties, theirs are the values that align closest to mine.
But it is not my job to work for the Democratic party; it is their job to work for me.
And for us.
Send me to Annapolis and I will see to it that they do.
