Issues As your Assemblyman for District 3 in Las Vegas, I'll fight for Nevada families with common-sense, liberty-minded solutions rooted in conservative principles.
We need less government interference, stronger individual rights, and policies that put America First.
Here's where I stand on the key issues facing our community.
Keeping our Communities Safe Nevada deserves safe streets where families can thrive without fear.
As a staunch supporter of law and order, I'll prioritize backing our police and ensuring criminals face swift justice — no more soft-on-crime policies that let repeat offenders walk free.
America was built on immigration, but not everybody who wants to come has our best interests at heart.
I support policies that secure our borders to stop the flow of drugs and human trafficking that plagues Las Vegas.
We have been heading in a wrong-headed direction for a long time and it's time to come to our senses.
I intend to: Fully fund law enforcement with competitive salaries and advanced training to attract top talent.
Crack down on fentanyl dealers and gangs with mandatory minimums and enhanced border security measures.
Promote community watch programs and Second Amendment rights for personal protection.
Oppose defund-the-police nonsense and bail reform that endangers our neighborhoods.
Together, we'll restore safety and freedom in our communities.
NO New Taxes Nevadans are taxed enough already — it's time to say "no more." I vow to oppose the government stealing from you to fund corruption and waste.
I'll oppose any tax hikes that burden working families and small businesses.
Our state thrives on low taxes; raising them kills jobs and drives people away.
I'll look for ways to cut waste and prioritize efficient government to fund essentials without picking pockets for pet boondoggles.
I intend to: Block all proposals for new sales, property, or income taxes in Nevada.
Audit state agencies to eliminate redundant programs and redirect savings to taxpayers.
Advocate for property tax caps to protect homeowners from skyrocketing assessments.
Support deregulation to unleash entrepreneurship, creating meaningful jobs to support people and families.
Oppose green energy mandates that indirectly tax consumers through higher utility bills.
Let's keep Nevada a tax haven where hard work pays off, not a funds-bloated bureaucracy.
Lowering the Cost of Living Skyrocketing prices for healthcare, gas, and rent are crushing Las Vegas families — government overregulation is to blame.
I'll advance free-market reforms to drive down costs, and oppose all the many wasteful big-government interventions that raise costs for Nevada's families.
I aim to put Nevada First by slashing red tape and fostering competition.
I intend to: Deregulate healthcare markets to allow cross-state insurance purchases and expand HSAs for consumer choice.
Fight federal mandates on energy that raise gas prices — promote Nevada's natural resources for affordable fuel.
Reform zoning laws to increase housing supply, making rent more affordable without subsidies.
Cut sales taxes on essentials like groceries and utilities to provide immediate relief.
Oppose price controls that stifle innovation; instead, incentivize private solutions for better outcomes.
By empowering markets over mandates, we'll make life affordable again for every Nevadan.
Educational Reform Our kids deserve better than failing public schools monopolized by unions.
I'll lead the charge to break up the Clark County School District bureaucracy and establish Education Savings Accounts (ESAs) for true school choice.
This libertarian approach empowers parents, not politicians, aligning with MAGA's focus on excellence and accountability.
No more one-size-fits-all indoctrination — let's put students first.
Dismantle the oversized CCSD into smaller, accountable districts for localized control.
Fund ESAs so parents can choose public, private, charter, or homeschool options with their tax dollars.
Nevada once led in this area, and we can again!
Expand Nevada's great charter schools program.
End Common Core and promote curricula focused on core skills, self-development, and vocational training.
Tie teacher pay to performance, rewarding excellence while protecting against union overreach.
Ban critical race theory and gender ideology in classrooms to keep education unbiased, effective, and not crazy-making for our children.
Empowering parents through choice will revolutionize Nevada's schools and secure our children's future.
Protecting Women's Rights and Fairness Ban biological males from women's sports.
End the insanity.
Enforce single-sex bathrooms and locker rooms in public facilities for privacy and safety.
Prohibit the transfer of male inmates to women's prisons, regardless of self-identification.
Support legislation defining sex as biological to prevent abuse of so-called gender laws.
Oppose taxpayer-funded gender transitions for minors, focusing resources on mental health support.
We'll safeguard women's spaces and uphold truth in Nevada — common sense over chaos.
