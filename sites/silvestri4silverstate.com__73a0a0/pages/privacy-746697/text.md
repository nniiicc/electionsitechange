Privacy Effective Date: February 25 th , 2025 Team Silvestri 4 Silver State respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, Silvestri4SilverState.com.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information.
1.
Information We Collect We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you: Make a donation Sign up for updates or newsletters Volunteer or contact the Campaign Complete forms on our Website Information collected may include: Name Mailing address Email address Phone number Employer and occupation (when required by campaign finance law) Any information you submit through contact fors Donation Information Donations made through our Website are processed by a third-party payment processor.
We do not directly collect or store full credit card numbers or payment details on our servers.
2.
How We Use Your Information We use collected information to: Process and record donations Comply with federal and state campaign finance laws Communicate campaign updates and events Respond to inquiries or requests Improve Website functionality Maintain internal records We may also use your information as required for campaign finance reporting and legal compliance.
3.
Third-Party Donation Processing Online donations are processed through Anedot.
When you make a donation: Your payment information is collected directly by the third-party processor.
Their privacy policy governs how payment data is handled.
We receive only the information necessary to record your contribution.
We encourage you to review the privacy policy of the payment processor.
4.
Information Sharing We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information: With service providers assisting campaign operations As required by election laws and public disclosure requirements When required by law or legal process To protect the rights and security of the Campaign Certain donor information may be publicly disclosed as required by campaign finance regulations.
Our Website may use cookies or analytics tools to: Understand visitor activity Improve user experience Monitor Website performance You may disable cookies through your browser settings.
6.
Data Security We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect your personal information.
However, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
7.
Email Communications If you subscribe to campaign communications, you may receive emails or messages from us.
You may unsubscribe at any time using the link included in our communications.
8.
Your Privacy Rights Depending on your state of residence, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including requesting access to or deletion of your data where permitted by law.
