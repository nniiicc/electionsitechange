Shared Parenting fails the House by one vote.
SB 172, the bill establishing a rebuttable presumption of shared parenting for fit parents, passed the Senate 20 to 13.
In the House it received 35 yes votes and 34 no votes, one vote short of the 36 needed to pass.
Both of District 32's seated representatives voted no.
