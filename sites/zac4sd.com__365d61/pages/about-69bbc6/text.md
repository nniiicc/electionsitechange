The Story
I grew up an Army brat.
My dad served, I did not.
We moved several times before my family settled in Cheyenne, Wyoming the summer before I started 7th grade.
By then I had already lived a dozen years and a handful of places.
Cheyenne is where I came of age.
I played college baseball back east, came home to Wyoming, graduated from the University of Wyoming with a Bachelor of Arts in History in 2004, and spent the next 13 years selling real estate in Cheyenne.
In 2018 a family court case brought my son to Rapid City.
I bought a house here that same year.
I was in Rapid City through COVID.
In 2022 I moved my life here for good.
That is eight years of South Dakota now, and it is home.
