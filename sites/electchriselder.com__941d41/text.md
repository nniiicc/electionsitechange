Chris Elder for State Representative in the 44th Legislative District Pos. 1
Meet Chris
Chris Elder is a 20-year Navy veteran of Naval Aviation and a resident of Snohomish County for over 30 years.
Together with his wife of over 20 years, he and their two children enjoy farming, boarding horses, and traveling throughout Washington State.
As a small business owner, real estate professional, and community volunteer, Chris will bring a practical, people-first perspective to Olympia—fighting for common-sense solutions that truly represent our district and state.
"I will bring my Navy leadership experience and business expertise to Olympia to ensure 'We the People' are truly represented in the Legislature.
I will fight for your priorities: safer communities, accountable spending, lower taxes, and real opportunities for thriving families with strong and safe communities.
I ask for your vote." — Chris Elder

Service to Country and Community
Chris is a graduate of the Navy’s Senior Enlisted Leadership Academy and recipient of the Military Outstanding Volunteer Service Medal.
Chris is a servant leader at heart.
He created the Mil2Vet podcast, which shares the transition stories of veterans and helps service members successfully navigate life after the military.
He strives to continue his service to others as your Representative in the 44th LD.
