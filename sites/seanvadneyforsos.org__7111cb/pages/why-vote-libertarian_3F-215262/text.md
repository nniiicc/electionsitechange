Voting Libertarian for Colorado Secretary of State comes down to one core idea: putting individual freedom, transparency, and fairness at the center of how elections and business systems operate.
First, a Libertarian approach prioritizes fair and accessible elections for every voter.
That means reducing unnecessary barriers, expanding secure voting options, and ensuring that every eligible Coloradan—regardless of background or circumstance—can cast a ballot independently and with confidence.
Rather than adding layers of bureaucracy, a Libertarian Secretary of State would focus on making the system simpler, clearer, and more transparent so people actually trust it.
Second, Libertarians emphasize limited government and maximized accountability.
The Secretary of State oversees elections and business registration, and those systems should work efficiently without overcomplication.
A Libertarian would push to streamline processes, cut red tape, and make information easily accessible to the public.
When government is easier to understand, it becomes easier to hold accountable.
Another key reason is support for small businesses and entrepreneurship.
In Colorado, starting a business should be straightforward and affordable.
A Libertarian Secretary of State would work to simplify filing requirements, reduce fees where possible, and modernize systems so entrepreneurs can focus on building their businesses instead of navigating complicated paperwork.
There’s also a strong focus on privacy and security.
Voting systems should protect both the integrity of elections and the personal data of voters.
Libertarians advocate for solutions that maintain security without unnecessary surveillance or intrusion.
Finally, voting Libertarian is often about breaking away from the two-party status quo.
It sends a message that voters want new ideas, less partisanship, and practical solutions instead of political gridlock.
A Libertarian Secretary of State would be less tied to party-driven agendas and more focused on administering fair systems for everyone.
In short, choosing a Libertarian for Colorado Secretary of State means supporting:
- Simpler, more accessible elections
- Transparent and accountable government
- Easier paths for starting and running a business
- Strong protections for privacy and voter confidence
- A move toward less partisan, more practical leadership
