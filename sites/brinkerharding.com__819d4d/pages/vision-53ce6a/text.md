Vision
This year, we will celebrate the 250th birthday of the greatest country on earth.
Next November, Americans will choose what we want our next 250 years to look like.
I am running for Congress because I believe America needs to look less like the failed policies of California and New York and more like its Heartland.
In Congress, I will fight to make America more affordable and make Congress accountable to the people it serves.
Affordability
- Grow our economy, increase wages, and keep inflation in check
- Pass year-round E-15 to help lower gas prices and support Nebraska agriculture
- Take on Big Pharma and Big Insurance to guarantee Americans pay the same low prices for prescription drugs as other developed countries
- Protect critical programs like Social Security, Medicaid, and Medicare for single moms, veterans, the elderly, and disabled – NOT illegal immigrants or able-bodied adults
- Make healthcare, including IVF, more accessible and affordable for Nebraska families
Accountability
- Support term limits, ban congressional stock trading, and halt congressional salaries during government shutdowns
- Promote body cameras and identifiable uniforms for ICE agents like we do with the Omaha Police
- Deport criminal illegal immigrants who commit violent crimes
- Pass nationwide Voter ID to ensure only American citizens can vote
- Stand up to career politicians, crack down on waste and fraud, and make government truly accountable to the people
