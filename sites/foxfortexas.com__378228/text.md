Merrie Fox for Texas House District 73 Protect what makes the Hill Country unique - strong communities, great schools, clean water, and stunning views.
Merrie Fox is a fierce advocate for protecting the Texas Hill Country and is currently running for Texas House District 73, representing Comal County and western Hays County.
She champions policies that reflect the values and priorities of local communities.
Strong public schools, local control over precious water resources, and the financial well-being of families are essential to preserving the region’s unique character and traditions.
Merrie believes that when families thrive and communities have a voice in managing proposed development, the Hill Country will remain a vibrant and beautiful place for generations to come.
