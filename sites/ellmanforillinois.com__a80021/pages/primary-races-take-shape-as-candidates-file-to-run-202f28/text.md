The holiday season has just hit its peak, but the next election season isn’t far behind.
Candidates for county, state and federal offices had a week to file their nominating petitions to secure their names on the March 20 primary ballot.
The deadline to file petitions was 5 p.m.
Dec. 4.
Voters will have the opportunity to elect candidates for Congress, governor, the Illinois General Assembly and various DuPage County seats.
