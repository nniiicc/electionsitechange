Senator Ellman believes state government should listen to working families, find practical solutions to complex problems, and be accountable to the people it serves.
She believes Illinois can meet its challenges by working together and focusing on the issues that matter most to communities across the state.
Building an Illinois where families and communities can thrive.
Senator Ellman’s vision for Illinois is one where families can afford to live and raise their children, everyone has access to quality health care, and Illinois’ natural resources are protected for future generations.
She will continue working to make Illinois more affordable for all families, protect access to health care and a woman’s right to choose, invest in public education, protect the environment, and strengthen consumer protections.
Practical solutions and accountable government.
Senator Ellman believes Illinois needs leaders who are willing to listen and focus on solving problems rather than scoring political points.
She believes state government should be transparent and accountable to the people it serves, and that every voice should have an opportunity to be heard.
Senator Ellman is proud of the progress made to strengthen communities, but there is still important work ahead.
She remains committed to delivering results for the people of the 21st Senate District and working with all communities to address the issues that matter most to them.
