Join us on Saturday June 16th for a volunteer kickoff to launch our campaign headquarters.
This campaign is about the people of the 21st district having a real voice in Springfield.
We deserve a State Senator who listens, solves problems, and is focused on building a stronger future for our families.
And the most important part of our campaign is YOU!
Come by to celebrate our new office and learn how you can help, sign up for a volunteer shift, get training on knocking doors and making calls, mingle and have snacks, and contribute to the campaign!
Laura Ellman will be speaking, as will Campaign Manager Meghan Hassett and Field Director Don Finch.
We will be training volunteers, collecting donations of office supplies, knocking doors, and making phone calls.
See you there!

11AM: Office Opening, Welcome from Laura

You can sign up to volunteer or drop off donations anytime from 11-4.
