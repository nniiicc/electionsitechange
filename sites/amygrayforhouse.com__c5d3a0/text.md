Get Involved My Vision Meet Amy Amy Gray brings a unique perspective to the State House, rooted in the heart of our community.
As a small business owner, wife, and mother, she understands the challenges of entrepreneurship, and as a hockey mom, she knows the value of hard work and family.
Together, these experiences drive her commitment to policies that support our local economy and strengthen our families.
The Vision For Michigan Small Business Growth Supporting local entrepreneurs through tax relief and streamlined regulations that empower our community's backbone.
Education Equity Investing in public schools and vocational training to ensure every student has the tools to succeed in a modern economy.
Workforce Innovation Modernizing infrastructure and supporting clean energy initiatives to create sustainable jobs for our future generation.
Standing With Michigan Workers Amy believes that when workers succeed, Michigan succeeds.
As a small business owner, she understands the importance of good jobs, fair wages, safe workplaces, and the ability of working families to build a secure future.
Amy is proud to stand with Michigan’s labor movement and believes workers deserve a seat at the table when decisions are made about their jobs, wages, benefits, and communities.
She will work to protect workers’ rights, support skilled trades and workforce development, and ensure Michigan’s economy works for the people who keep it moving every day.

Small Business Owner Running a small business has taught Amy that behind every paycheck is a person, a family, and a future.
She’s had to navigate the real cost of payroll, healthcare, and paying wages that truly respect her team’s work.
That experience shapes how she approaches policy.
Amy was raised in the Catholic faith, where she learned the value of strong families, personal responsibility, and the importance of loving thy neighbor.
She later gave back as a Sunday school teacher, helping instill those same principles in her community.
Her faith continues to shape her dedication to serving Macomb with integrity and compassion.
Family Values Respect for Law Enforcement Amy was raised in a law enforcement family rooted in service and sacrifice.
Her father served as a Chicago police officer and was shot in the line of duty before continuing his career as an FBI agent.
His courage and unwavering commitment to protecting others instilled in Amy a deep respect for the men and women who risk their lives every day to keep our communities safe.
As a dedicated hockey mom, Amy has taught her children that success comes from hard work, teamwork, and resilience.
The lessons learned at the rink—fairness, accountability, and pushing through challenges—extend far beyond the ice.
They are the same values that define everyday life in Macomb.
