Meet Joe Griffo Fighting to Make New York Affordable — And Delivering Results for Central New York Joe Griffo knows what families across Central New York and the Mohawk Valley are dealing with.
Higher energy bills.
Higher taxes.
Higher costs for just about everything.
Too many people are asking: “Why am I still in New York?” Joe Griffo is working to change that — standing up for taxpayers and advancing solutions that make it easier to live, work, and raise a family here.
A Record of Getting Things Done Joe Griffo doesn’t just talk about economic development — he has helped deliver some of the most important investments in the region.
He has played a key role in helping secure and support major projects that are creating jobs, strengthening communities, and rebuilding the local economy, including: The transformation of the former Griffiss Air Force Base into a hub for jobs and innovation Supported record investment in the AUD and Nexus Center The development of the new Wynn Hospital, ensuring access to modern healthcare for the region Semiconductor manufacturing expansion at Wolfspeed, bringing high-tech jobs to Central New York Continued growth and investment by Chobani, one of the region’s most important employers These aren’t promises — they’re results.
Respected across the aisle, Joe Griffo gets results — fighting to lower costs and helping the private sector deliver jobs and investment throughout the region.
Lowering Costs Starts with Fixing Energy Joe has been one of the leading voices pushing back against Albany policies that are driving up energy costs.
As former Chair of the Senate Energy & Telecommunications Committee, he has fought to: Oppose policies that increase utility bills on families and small businesses Support reliable, affordable energy that keeps the lights on and costs down Promote investment in infrastructure that strengthens the grid and supports growth Push for practical solutions instead of political mandates Joe understands a simple truth: If energy costs go up, everything costs more.
And right now, that’s exactly what families are experiencing.

Mayor.
