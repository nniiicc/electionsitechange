The Committee to Elect Angela Choberka is committed to safeguarding the privacy of our supporters and adheres to the highest standards of data protection.
This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect the personal information, including phone numbers and opt-in information, that we may collect during our political campaign and related activities.
