Get Involved Meet Kari Kari Dorry is a mom, teacher, union organizer, and community advocate who believes caring for one another is what makes strong communities possible.
As a special education teacher, she has spent her career supporting children and families, helping students succeed, and making sure every person feels seen, valued, and included.
As a parent, Kari understands the everyday challenges families face- balancing work and family life, finding affordable childcare, supporting schools, and simply trying to make ends meet.
Those experiences shape her commitment to practical, people-focused leadership.
Get Involved Kari is running because she believes it’s time to calm the chaos and focus on what truly matters: supporting families, strengthening schools, and building communities where people feel connected and supported.
She believes politics should bring people together to solve problems, not divide neighbors against one another.
