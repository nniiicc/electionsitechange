Wyoming through and through.
As the proud mom of a 20-year-old daughter and 18-year-old son attending Wyoming schools, Julie has spent much of the last two decades at sporting events, theater and choir concerts and helping with homework.
Now that her youngest is graduating from Kelly Walsh High School this May, Julie is ready to shift her focus to a new lens of public service.
Someday, Julie will retire in Wyoming, and she wants the future of our state to look like the Wyoming she grew up in – hardworking and neighborly.
