True freedom is the right to make your own decisions.
Our communities and economy are strongest in the hands of hard-working Wyoming people, so Julie believes in the smallest government possible without compromising healthcare, safety, and the working people.
Like many of us, Julie grew up hunting and practicing responsible gun ownership, and she is a defender of the Second Amendment.
Economically, she will support initiatives to promote small business success and bring more jobs into our state.
According to Julie, the less government involvement, the better.
“At the end of the day, you answer to God – not the government,” she says.
