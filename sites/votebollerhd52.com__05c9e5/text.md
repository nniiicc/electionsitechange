Why I am Running I’m not a politician—I’m someone who has lived the same challenges many of you face and is ready to bring practical solutions to Cheyenne.
I’ve spent my life serving this community, and I’m running to protect Wyoming’s future for the next generation.
Key Priorities Keep Wyoming Affordable As a pragmatic conservative, I believe in living within our means and working to keep property taxes stable and predictable, while reducing unnecessary regulatory burdens and supporting local jobs.
Strengthen Rural Healthcare As a nurse, I’ve seen firsthand how important access to care is.
I will work to support rural providers, strengthen the healthcare workforce, and help ensure our residents can receive care close to home.
Support Local Industry & Workforce I will support local industry and responsible growth on Wyoming’s terms by reducing regulatory burdens, encouraging job training and skilled trades, and backing the businesses that keep our communities strong.
Protect Local Control I believe government closest to the people is best.
I will work to keep decisions that impact our communities at the local level—not in Cheyenne or Washington.
Serve Our Community I’ve spent my life serving this community.
I will continue that work by supporting local efforts and focusing on practical solutions that help families and neighbors.
