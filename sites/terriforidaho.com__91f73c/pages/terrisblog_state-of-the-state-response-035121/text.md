Remember last year when he signed that $450 million tax gift to the wealthy?
He said he thought it was a bad idea.
He did it anyway.
If you want a contrast between how I would govern and how Mr.
Little leads, I will never pass a law that I know is a bad idea.
When I am governor of Idaho, I will lead in a way that prioritizes our children and young people and workers trying to make lives for themselves.
As this Legislature proceeds, I will listen to the people of Idaho.
I will support policies that keep rural hospitals, libraries, public schools and critical transportation infrastructure working for the people who the government is meant to serve.
I will call out bad policy and I won’t let corrupt politicians bully me, or bully any of us!
We are all in this together and it will take all of us to undo the damage that a reckless, scared governor has led us to.
Let me put it this way: I won’t lead like an outlaw who is just watching her back.
