It’s not chaos.
It’s beta testing.
The tyrants who led Iran made the world more dangerous and more cruel.
I am glad they are gone.
I also believe we are not safer today to have a president who starts wars without approval of the U.S.
Congress.
Trump’s outlandish decisions are no longer a mystery–it was never chaos; it was beta testing.
First, Trump takes outrageous action.
Second, he gauges the fallout.
Third, if not punished, he repeats the outrage in a greater way.
Think of all that Trump did leading up to this war of choice without congressional approval.
Bombed Venezuelan boats–no consequences.
“Double-tapped” (killed) survivors–no consequences.
Attacked Venezuela–no consequences.
And, he bombed Iran and declared them nuclear-free last year–no consequences.
So, he has started a war in the Middle East.
Trump’s beta-testing pattern is in full swing on our own soil.
He took over “blue” cities with federal forces.
His ICE agents violate our Constitutional rights.
His “War Department” clearly wants Artificial Intelligence to track all Americans.
And Trump has said he wants to federalize elections.

ICE agents remain masked.
Prisons are springing up.

You don’t need a vivid imagination to picture what outrage follows that.

Let’s work together to create the leadership Idaho, and our nation, needs.
