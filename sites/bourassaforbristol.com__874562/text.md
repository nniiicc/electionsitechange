Putting Bristol & Bridgewater First.
Marc Bourassa brings a unique blend of financial acumen and deep local engagement to public service.
His mission is to protect taxpayers while investing in the critical services and infrastructure that keep our communities strong.
With over three decades of professional experience as a financial executive, Marc has successfully managed millions of taxpayer dollars with accountability, precision, and efficiency.
He understands that every tax dollar spent is a responsibility to the families who earned it.
Here in Bristol and Bridgewater, Marc is active in local government.
He serves as the Elected Vice Chair of the Bristol Budget Committee , working hard to craft budgets that balance fiscal responsibility with community needs.
He is also an active member of the Bristol Planning Commission and the Bristol Energy Committee , ensuring our infrastructure is prepared for a vibrant and sustainable future.
Priorities Fighting For Our Families A balanced, common-sense approach to keeping New Hampshire affordable, strong, and prepared for the future.
Lower Property Taxes Marc is committed to easing the tax burden on Bridgewater and Bristol families.
He will protect local taxpayers by advocating for sensible state policies that support town-level relief.
Strong Public Schools Every child deserves access to a high-quality education.
Marc supports public schools with adequate funding to ensure strong resources, safe classrooms, and excellent teacher support.
Increased Affordability Rising costs are squeezing New Hampshire families.
Marc will work to lower essential everyday costs, including groceries, gas, home heating energy, and household utilities.
Affordable Housing & Healthcare Making sure our seniors, young professionals, and local workforce can afford to live here.
Marc advocates for accessible local healthcare and sensible housing opportunities.
Responsible Government Spending Marc believes state and local government should live within its means.
Using his 30+ years of financial experience, he will champion responsible budgeting and strict spending controls.
Protect Our Financial Future Smart choices today secure prosperity tomorrow.
Marc is dedicated to protecting taxpayer funds, avoiding wasteful projects, and keeping New Hampshire financially healthy.
