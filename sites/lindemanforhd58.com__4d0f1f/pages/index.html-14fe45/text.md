Meet Alec
I’m Alec Lindeman and I am running to represent House District 58 because I believe the best solutions start by listening to the people who live and work here.
I’ve called the Gunnison Valley home for the past 17 years.
Today, I serve on the Mount Crested Butte Town Council, where I work to advance thoughtful, practical policies grounded in understanding the issues, not gut feelings.
I am also the Executive Director of Crested Butte Mountain Theatre, where I’ve built partnerships, solved problems, and worked with neighbors to strengthen the community I call home.
I believe real leadership starts with listening, lending a hand, and bringing people together to get things done.
The Western Slope is made up of hard-working communities that care deeply about where they live.
Yet too many families are finding it harder to stay here.
Rising housing costs, limited access to healthcare and childcare, long-term drought, wildfire risks, and the everyday challenges of rural life require thoughtful leadership and practical solutions.
I am running to support agriculture and local businesses, protect Colorado’s water and public lands, strengthen rural communities, and help expand access to housing so the people who live and work here can continue to call the Western Slope home.
I believe the state government should listen to local communities, work across differences, and focus on practical solutions to improve everyday life.
You deserve a state representative that shows up, stays engaged, and works hard on your behalf.
That’s the kind of representative I’ll be.
By listening first, working together, and rolling up my sleeves, I believe we can build a stronger future for the Western Slope.
