A lifelong resident of Dennis with deep roots in Brewster and Yarmouth, Christopher Lambton is a trusted public servant, successful small business owner, and dedicated family man.
His leadership on the Dennis Selectboard and community initiatives demonstrates his unwavering commitment to enhancing the lives of all residents.
Christopher Lambton is a unifier.
His pragmatic leadership has brought together residents of all political affiliations to address pressing issues like housing, environmental preservation, and economic development.
Chris’s proven record of delivering results, coupled with his deep commitment to Brewster, Dennis, and Yarmouth, makes him a powerful advocate for the 1st Barnstable District and beyond.
