How to Vote Voting is how we shape our future.
From our schools to our roads to the opportunities available in our community.
Every voice matters, and every election counts.
I'm running for State Representative because I believe everyone deserves a seat at the table, and that starts with showing up to vote.
No matter who you support, I encourage you to get registered and make your voice heard this Election Day.
Ways to Vote There are three ways to vote this year – by mail, early in person, or in person on election day.
Mail Vote by Mail Voting by mail is easy – just request a ballot before August 25th and it will be mailed to you.
You can return it to your town or city clerk or mail it back – just make sure it is postmarked or dropped off on or before election day.
CLICK HERE to request ballot.
Mail Vote Early You can vote early in person seven days a week from August 22nd to August 28th.
CLICK HERE to find early voting hours.
Mail Vote at Polls You can still vote on election day at your regular polling place from 7am to 8pm.
CLICK HERE to find. your poll.
Voter Registration The voter registration deadline this year is August 22nd.
You can register to vote or update your address online.
