Ways to Vote
There are three ways to vote this year – by mail, early in person, or in person on election day.
Vote by Mail
Voting by mail is easy – just request a ballot before August 25th and it will be mailed to you.
You can return it to your town or city clerk or mail it back – just make sure it is postmarked or dropped off on or before election day.
