MISSION STATEMENT Leading.
Together.
Chris Lambton is running for State Representative to ensure Cape Cod remains a strong, sustainable, and connected community for generations to come.
Rooted in his lifelong commitment to Dennis and the Cape, Chris believes in protecting our natural resources, supporting local businesses, and making housing attainable for working families.
His mission is to bring common-sense leadership, collaboration, and a local voice to Beacon Hill — one that always puts the people and communities of Cape Cod first.
Priorities Affordable Housing Housing costs are rising, the year-round workforce is under pressure, and many longtime residents are facing challenges staying in the community.We need attainable housing for all.
Mental Health Awareness and Assistance Recognizing the importance of accessible mental health resources for youth, adults, veterans and seniors alike.
Accessible Healthcare Every resident should have access to high-quality, affordable healthcare when they need it.
He will work to expand access, reduce barriers to care, and ensure families can get the care they need without unnecessary financial strain.
Environment and Blue Economy Cape Cod relies on its waterways and coastlines not only for its vacation economy but also for its year around blue economy employing the fishing, shellfishing and eco-tourism industries Infrastructure and Municipal Services Reliable municipal services are vital to the quality of life in Dennis, Yarmouth, Brewster, and surrounding communities.
Residents rely on well-maintained roads, safe water and sewer systems, efficient public safety services, and responsive town management.
Public Safety Keeping our communities safe by supporting law enforcement, holding criminals accountable, and ensuring first responders have the resources they need.
Every family deserves to feel safe in their home, neighborhood, and community.
Education As a proud DY public school graduate, former public school teacher and parent of children attending public schools I know how important it is to invest in our schools for a strong future.
Local Economy Small businesses, seasonal tourism, and year-round services all play a role in supporting residents, creating jobs, and contributing to our community.
Arts and Culture The arts and culture are essential to the character and vitality of our communities.
We must support local artists, cultural organizations, and the creative economy that brings people together and strengthens our communities.
