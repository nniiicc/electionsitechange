About The Event
Wave & Walk Wednesdays
Join us every other Wednesday evening for a little visibility, a lot of conversation, and some good old-fashioned door knocking.
We’ll begin with a public standout, waving signs and showing the district that this campaign's momentum is growing.
From there, we’ll head into the nearby neighborhood to meet voters, drop off literature, and talk about why this election matters.
This is an easy, family-friendly way to get involved (playground nearby).
Come for the wave, stay for the walk, or join us for both.
Meeting location: 286 Station Ave, South Yarmouth, MA
Time: 5PM
