Tyler Herring is a Christian, husband, and dad running for State Senate to give hardworking Oklahoma families a stronger voice.
Tyler has spent the last 20 years in manufacturing and metal buildings, working his way up from entry-level jobs to becoming a project manager — solving problems, serving customers, and helping deliver real results.
He didn’t come up through politics.
He came up through work.
After high school, Tyler went straight into the workforce — not because it was easy, but because it was necessary.
He knows what it means to build a life the hard way, to make practical decisions, and to carry responsibility for a family.
That perspective is exactly what’s missing at the Capitol.
Tyler and his wife Brittany are raising their daughter with the same values that guide their life every day: faith, family, personal responsibility, and hard work.
He’s active in church, committed to his community, and ready to fight for the people who keep Oklahoma running.
Tyler is running because too many families feel like government has stopped listening to the people who pay the bills, follow the rules, and ask for nothing but fairness.
He’s running to change that.

LOWER TAXES.
PROTECT HOMEOWNERS.
Oklahoma families are already stretched thin.
Tyler believes the government should stop making it worse.
He’ll fight to protect homeowners from runaway property taxes, oppose wasteful spending, and support policies that keep more money in the pockets of the people who earned it.
Tyler knows what it feels like to watch costs keep climbing while families are expected to just absorb it.
He believes government should live within its means — just like everyone else has to.
SECURE THE BORDER.
ENFORCE THE LAW.
Tyler believes in law, order, and national sovereignty.
He supports secure borders, strong immigration enforcement, backing law enforcement, and keeping Oklahoma communities safe.
He believes the law should be enforced — and that elected leaders shouldn’t apologize for standing up for public safety.
That means supporting law enforcement, rejecting sanctuary-style policies, and putting the safety of Oklahoma citizens first.
GET SCHOOLS BACK TO BASICS.
Tyler believes schools should focus on what matters: reading, math, discipline, and preparing kids for life.
Parents deserve a bigger voice.
Teachers deserve classrooms where they can teach.
And students deserve schools that are focused on learning — not political distractions.
Tyler will fight for a back-to-basics education system that puts students first and restores common sense in the classroom.
PROTECT ELECTION INTEGRITY.
Tyler believes every legal vote should count — and only legal votes should count.
He supports voter ID, secure ballots, clean voter rolls, and election systems Oklahomans can trust.
Confidence in elections should not be controversial.
If people have to show ID to cash a check, board a plane, or buy certain products, they should have to show ID to vote.
DEFEND FAITH, FAMILY & OKLAHOMA VALUES.
Tyler believes Oklahoma works best when we protect the values that built it.
That means defending religious liberty, parental rights, the sanctity of life, the Second Amendment, and the right of families to raise their kids without government interference.
Tyler isn’t running to “manage decline.” He’s running to help preserve the kind of Oklahoma we still believe in.
BRING COMMON SENSE TO A GROWING DISTRICT Senate District 18 is growing fast — and government needs to keep up without losing what makes this area great.
Tyler will fight for better roads, smarter infrastructure, responsible growth, and practical leadership that protects quality of life for families who already live here.
He believes growth should benefit the people of this district — not leave them stuck with the traffic, costs, and consequences.
