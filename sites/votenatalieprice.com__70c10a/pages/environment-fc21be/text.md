Clean Water, Clean Air, Lower Bills
Michigan's environment and its ratepayers deserve better than they're getting.
- Enforce the strictest standards for data center development: renewable energy, ratepayer protections, local union labor, noise mitigation, and enforceable community and environmental agreements.
- Defend Michigan's landmark Clean Energy and Jobs Act from rollback and ensure the institutions responsible for implementing it, have the funding to actually do the job.
- Pass a Ratepayer Bill of Rights that ties utility rate increases to real improvements in reliability, stops ratepayer money from funding CEO bonuses and lobbying, and gives communities the freedom to build their own clean energy.
- Increase energy freedom through community solar and municipal energy options.
- Invest in home energy efficiency, especially in working-class communities.
- Protect Michigan's drinking water from PFAS and microplastic contamination through mandatory testing, site remediation, and extending the statute of limitations so polluters can't outlast the damage they caused.
- Raise Michigan's landfill tipping fees to stop the state from becoming a dumping ground for out-of-state waste.
- Force corporations to fund the cleanup of sites they've contaminated.
Polluters should pay, not taxpayers.
- Fund the Community and Worker Economic Transition Office to help laborers move into good-paying clean energy jobs.
