Safety starts with trust.
Michigan deserves leaders who will fight to build it and protect it.
- Pass comprehensive gun safety legislation, including waiting periods, high-capacity magazine and bump stock bans, age-21 purchase requirements, and a complete ban on ghost guns.
- End firearm manufacturer and retailer immunity so victims and families can hold the gun industry accountable.
- Fund community violence intervention programs that break the cycle of gun violence at its source.
- Keep guns out of schools, libraries, and government buildings.
- Restore local authority so communities can set their own gun safety standards.
- Ban immigration enforcement from schools, churches, hospitals, and courthouses.
- Pass the Drive SAFE bills so undocumented Michiganders can drive legally and safely.
- Restrict state and local law enforcement cooperation with federal immigration operations that tear families apart and undermine community trust.
- Require mandatory, fully funded accreditation for all Michigan police departments, including de-escalation and mental health crisis response training.
- Stop corporations from collecting, monetizing, and selling residents' personal data without their knowledge or consent.
- Eliminate cash bail so Michiganders aren't jailed before trial simply because they can't afford to pay.
