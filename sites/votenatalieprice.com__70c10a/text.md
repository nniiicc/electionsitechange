About Michigan’s New 10th Senate District
Approximately 270,000 people live in Michigan’s new 10th Senate District, which stretches across parts of Oakland and Macomb Counties.
The district includes all of Berkley, Center Line, Clawson, Ferndale, Hazel Park, Huntington Woods, Madison Heights, Oak Park, Pleasant Ridge, Royal Oak, Royal Oak Township, and part of Warren.
