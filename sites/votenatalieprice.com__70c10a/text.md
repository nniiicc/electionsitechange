Hi, I’m Natalie Price.
I’m a State Representative, former high school teacher, former Berkley City Councilmember, and a mom.
Now I’m running to represent our community in the Michigan Senate.
For the past four years, I’ve been a strong progressive voice in the Michigan House, and before that, I served on the Berkley City Council.
I have always worked to listen, lead, and fight for solutions that make people’s lives better.
I’m now ready to bring that same energy and commitment to the Michigan State Senate.
I’m asking for your vote so we can continue building a diverse, welcoming state where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
Let’s make a difference for our communities and futures—together.
