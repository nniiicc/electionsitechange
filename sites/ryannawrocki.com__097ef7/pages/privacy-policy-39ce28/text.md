Information We Collect
Information you provide voluntarily.
When you contact us, sign up for updates, volunteer, or request a yard sign, we may collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and the contents of your message.
Text messaging opt-in.
If you provide your phone number and opt in to receive text messages, we collect your phone number for the purpose of sending you campaign-related messages.
Information collected automatically.
Like most websites, we may automatically collect certain technical information when you visit the Site, including your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit, and similar usage data.
This information may be collected through cookies and similar technologies.
How We Use Your Information
- To respond to your messages, questions, and requests.
- To send you updates, newsletters, and information about the campaign, events, and ways to get involved.
- To coordinate volunteer activities you sign up for.
- To improve the Site and how it performs for visitors.
- To comply with applicable legal requirements.
Text Messaging
If you opt in to receive text messages from us, your phone number will be used solely to send you campaign-related messages, such as updates, event reminders, and calls to action.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
Phone numbers collected for text messaging will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We will not share, sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to third parties, except as described in this Privacy Policy or as required by law.

Please note that contribution records are subject to disclosure requirements under Maryland campaign finance law.

If we learn that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13 without parental consent, we will delete that information promptly.
