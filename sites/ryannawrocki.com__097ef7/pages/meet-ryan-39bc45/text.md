About Ryan
Rooted in Baltimore County.
Ryan was born and raised in Parkville and lives in Middle River with his wife, Dr.
Lauren Nawrocki, and their six children.
He’s a proud product of Baltimore County — and everything he does in Annapolis comes back to the place that raised him.
A graduate of Loyola Blakefield, Ryan earned a B.A. with honors from St.
Mary’s College of Maryland and a Master of Public Administration from Johns Hopkins University.
He is also a graduate of Leadership Baltimore County.
His career has been built around public service and communication.
He served as a Public Information Officer for the Maryland Aviation Administration — managing communications for BWI Thurgood Marshall and Martin State Airports — worked at LifeBridge Health, and served as a Communications Director in the U.S.
House of Representatives for several Members of Congress, including Congressman Andy Harris.
In 2016 he was named Senior Director of Communications and Marketing at the Maryland Transit Administration, work that earned him a place on The Daily Record’s VIP List of 40 leaders under 40.
Ryan is also a small-business owner — the owner of Green Relief Health, a wellness medical practice.
In 2022, the voters of District 7A elected Ryan to the Maryland House of Delegates, where his approach to the job is simple: show up, listen, and bring Eastern Baltimore County’s voice to Annapolis.

In the Community
Roots that run deep.
Ryan’s community involvement runs deep.
He serves on the Board of Directors for the Middle River Volunteer Fire & Rescue Company and has been a member of the Sons of the American Legion (Post No. 180 in Rosedale), the White Marsh–Cowenton Community Association, the Bird River Road Neighborhood Association, and the White Marsh Police & Community Relations Council.
