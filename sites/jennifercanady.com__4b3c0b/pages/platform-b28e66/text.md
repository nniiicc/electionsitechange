- Economic Growth
- Cutting taxes to help provide relief to those suffering at the pump and grocery store.
- Curbing abuses in our court system that drive up the cost of property insurance and make home affordability more difficult.
- Protecting Florida's position as the most fiscally sound state in the nation to keep our bond rating AAA and lower costs to taxpayers.
- Education
- Empowering parents to play a more direct role in their child's education - including having the ability to send their child to a school that best meets their needs.
- Investing resources directly into the classroom.
- Hardening our schools and classrooms and working with the Sheriffs Office to have well-trained Guardians.
- Quality of Life
- Giving our men and women in law enforcement the tools they need to keep us - and themselves - safe.
- Upgrading our infrastructure to ease traffic congestion and allow for greater economic activity.
- Protecting our environment and water quality to maintain our rich agricultural sector.
