Protecting Rural Healthcare
Strong healthcare systems are essential to strong rural communities.
Rusty Nelson is deeply concerned about the future of rural hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, and ambulance services throughout southwest Minnesota.
As a longtime EMT, Rusty has seen firsthand how critical local healthcare access is for families, seniors, farmers, and emergency responders.
With Medicaid cuts threatening healthcare services across rural Minnesota, Rusty believes state leaders - Republican, Democrat, and Independent must work together to protect access to care before communities face devastating losses.
Rusty believes every family deserves dependable healthcare close to home — not hours away.
"We need a cure for the damage caused by Medicaid cuts."
-- Rusty Nelson
Why This Matters
"Medicaid cuts threaten the future of our hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, and ambulance services.
We must act quickly to protect access to quality healthcare in rural Minnesota.
We can’t afford to lose our local hospitals, clinics, nursing homes, or ambulance services.
I will fight for sustainable funding and creative solutions so our communities don’t lose the care we depend on." --- Rusty Nelson
When rural healthcare services disappear, communities lose far more than buildings.
Families may be forced to drive long distances for emergency care.
Seniors may lose nearby nursing home options.
Ambulance response systems become stretched thinner.
Healthcare workers leave rural areas.
Small communities struggle to attract families, businesses, and future growth.
Every community deserves access to quality healthcare services that are stable, reliable, and sustainable for future generations.

He believes rural communities deserve leadership focused on common-sense solutions that help local businesses, farms, and families continue to thrive.
Rusty is committed to listening to the concerns of rural communities and advocating for policies that help communities remain strong, sustainable, and prepared for future generations.
