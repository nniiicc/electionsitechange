State Representative Jeff LaRe proudly serves the 73rd District, bringing with him an extensive background in business management, security and safety.
Jeff is the Executive Vice President of the Whitestone Group, a national asset protection and security services company.
He also brings expertise as a former Certified Peace Officer and Corrections Officer at the Fairfield County Sheriff’s Office.
In addition to his work, Jeff is a youth lacrosse coach and serves on the Pickerington Youth Lacrosse Board.
He is a triathlete and cyclocross competitor.
Jeff and his wife, Amy, have three children, and they enjoy living in Violet Township in Fairfield County.
