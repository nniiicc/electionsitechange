Constituent Services
As your State Senator, I am always here to help.
Please do not hesitate to contact me if I can ever be of service.
List of Services
-
Resources & Legislative Help: I am here to help the constituents and businesses of District 9, so please contact my office at xxx-xxx-xxxx or at clint.dixon@senate.ga.gov.
Please include your name, address, and number if you contact our office via email so that we can get in touch with you quickly and efficiently.
List Item 1
-
Letters of Recognition: I enjoy hearing about special events and accomplishments in my constituents’ lives, and I am happy to honor my constituents by writing letters or resolutions to recognize those special events.
So if you have a special event or accomplishment such as student achievement awards, sports awards, 50th wedding anniversaries, or other special achievements let me know.
Watch the Senate Live in Session or Committees: www.legis.ga.gov/Streaming/en-US/Channel/Senate List Item 2
-
School Visits and Student Engagement: Our students are our leaders of tomorrow, so it is very important to me that we educate them on the legislative process.
I am more than happy to visit local schools and talk with students about what I do and how the legislative process works.
If you would like to schedule a visit, please contact my office.
Flag flown at Capitol: Flags flown over our State Capitol make a great gift to recognize an individual or organization.
If you would like to receive a flag flown at our State Capitol, please call my district office for further information.
List Item 4
-
Resolution from the GA State Senate: We can also write a State Senate Resolution to honor, recognize and commend individuals, groups, schools, etc.
Examples include:
- Schools and churches in our community
- Citizens who have made a significant, positive impact on our community
- Students with a specific academic, athletic or extracurricular achievement
These Resolutions come with a State of Georgia seal and arrive in a protective, black folder suitable for presentations.
If you would like a Resolution from the State Senate, please contact me.
