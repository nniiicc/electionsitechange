Born and raised on her family’s farm in South Newbury, Molly learned two things early on: progress takes many hands, hard work and problem solving, and that everyone has the right to live with dignity and to have their basic needs met.
Over the last few years since serving as Lt.

Molly also became a mom and helps care for her mom, who battles multiple sclerosis, giving her an even clearer understanding of the caregiving and economic pressures facing working familIes.

MEET MOLLY Learn more MOLLY’S PRIORITIES Protecting the fundamental rights and freedoms of every Vermonter As Washington chaos and cruelty threaten basic freedoms, Vermont needs leaders willing to hold the line and fight back.
As Lieutenant Governor, Molly will help use every tool at Vermont’s disposal to protect Vermonters from attacks on fundamental rights, inhumane immigration policies, voter suppression, the harm caused by tariffs and other destructive actions coming from Washington.
Making life better for working families As a mom and caregiver, Molly understands the economic pressures facing working families—especially in an aging state like Vermont.
As Lieutenant Governor, Molly will continue to fight for affordable child care and elder care, universal paid family and medical leave, and urgent health care reforms that ensure affordable access for every Vermonter, no matter where they live.
Lowering costs and investing in Vermont’s future Even with one of the lowest unemployment rates in the country, Vermont has become one of the least affordable places to live.
As Lieutenant Governor, Molly will be laser-focused on what matters most: building more housing and lowering costs, strengthening public schools while tackling cost drivers, and making sure every Vermonter can access a good-paying job by supporting Vermont's businesses and expanding hands-on job training efforts.
