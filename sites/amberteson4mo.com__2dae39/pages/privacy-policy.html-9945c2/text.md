PRIVACY POLICY
How we handle information you share with the campaign.
Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 29, 2026
Friends of Amber Teson (“the Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website or interact with the Campaign, how we use it, and the choices you have.
By using this site, you agree to the practices described below.
Information We Collect
We collect information you choose to share with us, including when you sign up for updates, volunteer, attend an event, contact the Campaign, or make a contribution.
This may include your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, employer and occupation (required by law for certain contributions), and any message or comments you submit.
When you donate, payment information is collected and processed by our payment processor (ActBlue); the Campaign does not store your full payment card information on this site.
We may also automatically collect limited technical information when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring website.
This information helps us operate, secure, and improve the site.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to communicate with you about the Campaign, respond to your messages, organize volunteers and events, process and acknowledge contributions, comply with election and campaign-finance laws, and improve our outreach.
If you provide your email address or phone number, you may receive Campaign updates, invitations, and fundraising messages.
You can opt out at any time using the unsubscribe link in our emails or by replying STOP to text messages.
Information Sharing
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with trusted vendors and service providers who help us run the Campaign (for example, email, texting, hosting, and donation platforms), and we may share information with other candidates, committees, political organizations, and party committees who share our values.
We may also disclose information when required by law, including disclosures to the Missouri Ethics Commission or the Federal Election Commission, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of the Campaign, our supporters, or the public.
Cookies and Analytics
This site may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and understand how visitors use the site.
You can control cookies through your browser settings, though some features of the site may not work properly if cookies are disabled.
Children’s Privacy
This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove it.
Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect the information we collect.
However, no method of transmission or storage over the Internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Your Choices
You may update, correct, or request deletion of the information you have provided to the Campaign by contacting us using the information below.
You may also unsubscribe from emails or texts at any time.
Please note that we may need to retain certain records to comply with legal and reporting obligations.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
Your continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
Contact Us
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please reach out through our contact page.
