WHAT AMBER BELIEVES People should be able to build a good life here.
That means listening first, focusing on the everyday challenges families face, and bringing people together to deliver results.
01 Making Missouri More Affordable Support job growth and small businesses while lowering the pressure families feel from housing, groceries, childcare, and other everyday necessities.
02 Quality Healthcare Expand access to affordable care, protect coverage for working families, and make sure medical bills do not stand between Missourians and the care they need.
03 Investing in Public Education Fully support our public schools and local districts, and make sure teachers and school staff are paid fairly for the work they do.
04 Safe, Healthy Communities Support law enforcement and first responders while investing in mental health resources, community services, and prevention programs that help keep us safe.
05 Putting People Back into Politics Listen to District 64, work across party lines, and turn our neighbors’ concerns into real results—not just campaign promises.
BRINGING BACK BALANCE Different voices deserve a seat at the table.
One party holds a supermajority in the Missouri House.
When any one group has overwhelming control, too many voices can go unheard—and that does not reflect the full voice of our state.
I’m running to represent the people of District 64, listen to good ideas from both sides of the aisle, and work with anyone willing to put Missouri first.
I’m not asking every Republican to become a Democrat.
I’m asking for the chance to be a Democrat you can vote for: an independent-minded neighbor who will find common ground, get things done, and put people ahead of political parties.
That’s what Bringing Back Balance means to me.
Amber Teson Fighting for District 64.
