About Me.
It’s an honor and privilege to be serving our community as State Representative.
I grew up here and have lived in every part of the district from Fox Island to Bremerton, Gig Harbor to the Key Peninsula, and now in South Kitsap.
I graduated from Peninsula High where my mom teaches, and I worked through high school for my dad’s local small business before I went on to college.
As a lawyer, I’ve worked for the Northwest Justice Project where I served survivors of crimes like domestic violence and human trafficking.
I’ve also worked with Kitsap Legal Services in housing law serving working families struggling to make ends meet.
As the child of a public-school teacher, and a product of our schools, I believe wholeheartedly that quality education is the core to expanding opportunity for all.
I’m passionate about giving back to our community.
I volunteer with the Rotary Club, Foundation for Washington State Courts, Awesome Praise, Down Home Band on the Key Peninsula, and as a Peninsula High School Cross Country Coach, along with many others over the years.
I hope to earn your support and that you join the campaign trail!
