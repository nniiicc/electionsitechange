There are many ways to help!
- Canvass/Doorbell with us.
Canvassing is the most effective way to connect with voters.
Whether you knock on doors in your neighborhood, or during one of our group canvasses, this is an excellent way to show your support.
- Phone bank with us.
Phone banking can be done from anywhere!
Join us for a group phone bank or on your own time.
Remote training will be provided beforehand.
- Get a Yard Sign!
Show your Adison support by putting a yard sign in your yard.
- Write a Letter to the Editor.
Letters to the editor can be used to start a community conversation about an issue important to you.
- Send Texts for the Campaign!
Text banking involves individual outreach to voters through text messages, this contact can help prepare and motivate people to vote.
- Host a campaign event!
Help to organize an event in your neighborhood.
