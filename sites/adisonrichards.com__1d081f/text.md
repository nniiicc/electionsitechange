Donate Why I’m Running.
I’m Adison Richards, running to continue the work as your State Rep., and I’m running to deliver common sense solutions to homelessness, rising costs, public safety, protecting reproductive freedom, and creating opportunity for all.
We’re facing so many challenges with rising costs, especially at the pump, at the grocery store, in housing, health care, and childcare.
As a lawyer, I’ve worked in housing law with folks struggling to make ends meet and directly saw the effects of rising costs.
Having actively served crime victims for much of my legal career, I have a deep understanding of our public safety challenges and we have to do better.
I’ve fought for victims of domestic violence and human trafficking.
I promise to advocate for you in Olympia to create public safety for all.
We live in a district that is constantly overlooked and our Representatives have not done enough.
Our gas prices go up, our ferry service goes down, and our traffic problems aren’t getting any better.
We need more opportunities for our students to enter the trades and gain apprenticeships, which will create more pathways to good paying jobs and economic success.
Reproductive freedom is under attack across the country and Republicans in our state - including my opponent - want to take these rights away.
I promise to stand up for the right to choose.
We have a long journey ahead, but we can solve the challenges we face.
I hope to earn your support and that you join us on the campaign trail!
About.
I grew up here, went to school here, and have actively served here for years through my public service legal work or as a volunteer.
I have lived in every part of the district; from Gig Harbor to Purdy to the Key Peninsula to now being a homeowner in Kitsap County.
