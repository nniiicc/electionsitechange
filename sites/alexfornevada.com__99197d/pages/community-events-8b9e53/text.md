Community Access
This is something that is critical to my representation and my campaign.
In order for us to build a democracy that represents us, we have to build a community and engage TOGETHER.
We are not interested in serving corporations.
We need YOU to have access to your reps and feel like YOU have a stake in the system.
We are committed to investing in ways for you to be able to share your concerns as well as be able to come together with members of your community.
That’s why we will hold as many events to build as we can to BUILD that community instead of bougie events that cater to rich donors.
There will be town halls, live streams, voter events where we just have fun and hang, as well as fundraisers for important community causes that create positive results for our community.
Events that we have in the pipeline will be posted here on this page, and we will also invite you to these events.
Just provide us with your name and email!
