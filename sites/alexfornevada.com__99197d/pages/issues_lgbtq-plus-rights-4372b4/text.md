LGBTQIA+ Rights
Where I Stand
I am an ally of the LGBTQIA+ community.
I will do more than acknowledge their right to exist.
I believe marriage is between two people, regardless of sex or gender.
I believe in access to medically necessary care regardless of gender or sexuality.
I believe medically necessary access to medications and treatments that help individuals meet their physical and mental health goals should not be infringed upon.
What I’ll Do
- I will NOT stand for ANY restrictions of LGBTQIA+ rights in this state.
- I will support legislation that funds the creation of safe spaces for these communities.
I will support legislation that removes barriers to healthcare for those in these communities and shield members of our community from federal overreach.
- I will listen to voices from leaders of these communities to ensure that I am adequately representing their communities.
Why It Matters
This issue is not complicated.
Your rights are important, and we will make sure they will not be infringed upon.
Any threat on the rights of the LGBTQIA+ is a threat to all of our rights.
