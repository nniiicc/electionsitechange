Where I Stand
Las Vegas is one of the most diverse cities in the country.
Our service economy has greatly benefited from the contributions of immigrants who have made Las Vegas their home.
Donald Trump, ICE, and mass deportations are destroying our communities and service economy.
Our governor Joe Lombardo supports him and has used the National guard to support ICE operations in Nevada.
In Henderson, we have an ICE detention center.
We have to kick Joe out of office and we need to protect and shield our communities from ICE.
What I’ll Do
On the federal level, we need to Abolish ICE.
However, there are still things we can do locally to combat ICE.
In the Nevada legislature, I will:
- Call for the end of all ICE detention centers in Nevada
- Push for strong laws that shield our immigrant and communities of color from Trump’s immigration policies that refuse cooperation with ICE
- Push for policies that use our protective forces to PROTECT our communities and not cooperate with agencies that are ripping them apart.
Why It Matters
Legal and illegal immigrants have a constitutional right to due process, and if those can be violated, then so can citizens, so we must ensure we all have the right’s to due process.
We must protect our rights and our communities at all costs because the fear and hysteria are having real effects on all of us!
