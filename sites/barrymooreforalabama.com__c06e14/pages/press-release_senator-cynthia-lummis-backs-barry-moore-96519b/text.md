Enterprise, AL – U.S.
Senator Cynthia Lummis (R-WY), the leading pro-crypto voice in the United States Senate, has endorsed Barry Moore, citing his strong conservative record and leadership on digital assets and financial freedom.
“Barry Moore understands that innovation, not bureaucracy, is the future of American finance,” Senator Lummis said.
“He has been a consistent defender of sound money, blockchain innovation, and the right of Americans to control their own assets.
As one of the few members of Congress to personally own crypto assets, Barry understands that innovation will power America and Alabama’s future.
I’m proud to join President Trump in supporting Barry Moore, because he will be a powerful ally in the Senate for economic freedom and technological leadership.”
“Senator Lummis has led the fight in Washington to keep America at the forefront of financial innovation,” said Moore.
“Crypto and blockchain technology are about freedom, privacy, and opportunity — values that conservatives should defend.
As U.S.
Senator, I’ll work with Senator Lummis to stop regulatory abuse, protect innovators, and ensure the future of digital finance is built in America, not China.”
Senator Lummis has represented Wyoming in the U.S.
Senate since 2021 and is widely regarded as Congress’s foremost advocate for Bitcoin and digital assets, including as a founder of the Senate Financial Innovation Caucus.
