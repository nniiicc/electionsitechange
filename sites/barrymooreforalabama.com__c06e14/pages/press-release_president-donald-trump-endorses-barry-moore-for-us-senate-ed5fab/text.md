Enterprise, AL – President Donald J.
Trump gave Barry Moore his “Complete and Total Endorsement to be the next United States Senator from Alabama —BARRY WILL NEVER LET YOU DOWN!”
Barry Moore accepted the endorsement with the following statement:
“I am truly honored and thankful to receive the endorsement of President Donald Trump.
President Trump has always put America first, and I’m proud to have stood with him from the very beginning.
With his support, I’m ready to get to work on day one.
I’ll continue fighting for Alabama values, secure borders, a strong economy, and putting the President’s America First agenda into action.
Thank you!”
