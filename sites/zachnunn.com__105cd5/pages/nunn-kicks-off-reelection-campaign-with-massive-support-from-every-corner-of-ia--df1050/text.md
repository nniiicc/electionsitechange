Nunn Files for Reelection as Support Builds Across Urban and Rural Iowa
Des Moines, IA – Combat Aviator and U.S.
Representative for Iowa’s Third Congressional District, Zach Nunn officially filed for reelection in Iowa’s Third Congressional District on the first day candidates were eligible to do so.
Nunn enters the 2026 cycle with a broad coalition of grassroots support, strong momentum, and a clear record of delivering for Iowans.
Backed by thousands of farmers, veterans, small business owners, teachers, law enforcement officers, and Republicans and Democrats alike, Nunn’s campaign reflects a broad and growing coalition of Iowans united behind his mission to strengthen the economy, support those who served, and build a safer, more prosperous future for every hardworking family across Iowa’s Third District.
“This morning, I officially filed for reelection in Iowa’s Third Congressional District,” said Rep.
Zach Nunn.
“I’m grateful for the overwhelming encouragement we’ve received from families, small business owners, veterans, farmers, and community leaders across our district.
Iowans want commonsense leadership that delivers results, and that’s exactly what we’re going to keep fighting for.”
“Since taking office, Congressman Nunn has worked across party lines to deliver real results for Iowa families, cutting wasteful Washington spending, lowering costs at the kitchen table, defending Iowa agriculture, and ensuring our servicemembers and veterans receive the support they’ve earned,” said Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“Republicans, Democrats, and Independents alike are rallying behind Zach Nunn because he puts Iowa first.
He’s a proven leader who builds consensus, fights for results, and delivers for every corner of Iowa’s Third Congressional District.”
Since taking office, Nunn has delivered real results for Iowans, helping pass the largest tax cut in American history and making thousands of stops across all 21 counties in Iowa’s Third Congressional District.
