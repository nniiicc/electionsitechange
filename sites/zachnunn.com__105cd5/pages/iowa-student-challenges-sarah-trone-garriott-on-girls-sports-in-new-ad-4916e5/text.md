Representative Zach Nunn today released “Stand Up For Us,” the fourth television ad of the 2026 general election and the second to put Sarah Trone Garriott’s Iowa Senate record in front of Iowa voters.
The 60-second spot features an Iowa athlete describing what it takes to earn a spot on the field, and how Sarah Trone Garriott has voted to take that spot away.
“Zach Nunn is a father of five daughters.
When he voted to protect girls’ sports, he was a dad standing up for his kids and every other girl in Iowa,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy .
“Sarah Trone Garriott had the same opportunity twice, and voted no both times, including against protections for girls’ sports and locker rooms.
Now she’s running ads telling Iowa parents she agrees with Zach.
Nobody runs away from a record they’re proud of.
Trone Garriott is trying to rewrite hers because she knows Iowa voters won’t like what they see.” Background on the bills and roll calls in the ad: Girls’ sports (HF 2416) .
