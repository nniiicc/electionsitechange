Nunn Maintains Strong Financial Position Ahead of 2026 Election
Des Moines, IA – Congressman Zach Nunn, a combat aviator and Representative for Iowa’s Third Congressional District, announced more than $1.1 million raised across his campaign committees during the second quarter of 2026.
The campaign has also received contributions from all 21 counties in Iowa’s Third Congressional District, underscoring the broad grassroots support behind Nunn’s reelection effort as momentum continues to build in one of the nation’s most competitive races.
“I’m deeply grateful to the Iowans who continue to support our campaign and our mission to deliver results for families across Iowa,” said Nunn.
“As we work to deliver results on affordability, strengthen our economy, and keep our communities safe, this strong showing of support from Iowans demonstrates that our message is resonating as we build momentum to win in November.”
“The continued support and broad coalition behind Zach Nunn is built on his record of delivering bipartisan results that matter at Iowans’ kitchen tables,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“That’s made Zach one of the most effective members of Congress, and it’s why this campaign has the organization, resources, and momentum to win for hardworking Iowans in 2026.”
Details on Nunn’s Second Quarter Fundraising:
- Nunn brought in over $700,000 into his campaign committee, “Iowans for Zach Nunn.” Additionally, Nunn directed over $200,000 from “Team Nunn” into “Iowans for Zach Nunn” to reinforce general election efforts.
- Nunn secured over $425,000 through his joint fundraising committee (JFC), “Team Nunn.”
- Nunn added over $20,000 into his Leadership PAC, “Call to Service.”
