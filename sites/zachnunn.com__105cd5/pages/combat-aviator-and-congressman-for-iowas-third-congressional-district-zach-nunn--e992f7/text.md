With $1.6 Million Banked, Nunn is Poised for 2026 Showdown
Des Moines, IA – Congressman Zach Nunn, a combat aviator and Republican Representative for Iowa’s Third Congressional District, announced raising more than $700,000 across all campaign committees in the third quarter of 2025.
With more than $1.6 million cash on hand, Nunn heads into one of the nation’s most competitive House races with growing momentum and a battle-ready campaign.
“With Democrats naming Iowa’s Third District a top target in 2026, Zach Nunn’s strong fundraising sends a clear message: he’s battle-tested, well-prepared, and ready to defend the seat from far-left activists pushing an out-of-touch, radical agenda,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“Iowans trust Zach Nunn, and as one of the top ten most bipartisan members of Congress, he will continue to build a safer, more prosperous future for every hardworking Iowa family.”
