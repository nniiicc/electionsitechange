Representative Zach Nunn (IA-03) for reelection, citing his record of delivering for Iowa’s small businesses.
“Small businesses are where opportunity begins in Iowa.
Where a good idea and a lot of hard work can build something that grows our economy and creates good jobs close to home,” said Rep.
Zach Nunn .
“We delivered the largest tax cut in American history so small business owners can invest in their storefronts, working Iowans can keep more of every paycheck, and families can build a future in the towns they love.
I’m proud to have NFIB in our corner as we continue fighting to keep that opportunity alive on every Main Street in Iowa.” “Since his time in the state legislature, Rep.
Zach Nunn has been a true champion for small business,” said NFIB Political Director Louis Bertolotti .
“This Congress, he earned a 100% Voting Record on small business issues.
Most importantly, he helped ensure permanency for the 20% Small Business Tax Deduction.
Our members thank him for his tireless work to improve Iowa’s economic climate, and we are pleased to back him for re-election.” ### GET INVOLVED Zach Nunn is a member of the US Air Force Reserve.
