Des Moines, IA – Combat aviator and U.S.
Representative Zach Nunn today released “Iowa Comes First,” the fifth television ad of the 2026 general election.
The 30-second spot highlights Nunn’s record of putting Iowa ahead of party and working across the aisle, a record that earned him a ranking as the No. 3 most bipartisan member of the U.S.
House.
“Iowans trust Zach Nunn to put them first because his bipartisan record is built on delivering results for Iowa, not loyalty to any one political party,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“Whether it’s standing up for Iowa workers or lowering health care costs, Zach Nunn has never been afraid to put Iowa ahead of party.”
Zach Nunn was ranked the third most bipartisan member of the U.S.
House by Bridge Grades, a nonpartisan scorecard that measures how often members legislate across party lines.
The full rankings are HERE.
Zach has helped write and pass more than 25 bills into law under both President Biden and President Trump, including the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, where he wrote the rural housing title.
He has worked with both parties to advance year-round, nationwide E15 and bring down costs for working families.
