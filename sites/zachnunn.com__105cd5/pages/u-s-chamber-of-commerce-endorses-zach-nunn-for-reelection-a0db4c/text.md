Des Moines, IA – The U.S.
Chamber of Commerce, the largest business federation in the country and the leading voice for American employers and small businesses, today endorsed combat aviator and U.S.
Representative Zach Nunn (IA-03) for reelection, citing his record of delivering for Iowa’s workers, families, and small businesses.
“We delivered the largest working-class tax cut in history for the families, workers, and small businesses who make Iowa run, and I’ve fought to bring capital back to the heart of the heartland and cut the red tape Washington piles on our job creators,” said Rep.
Zach Nunn.
There’s more work to do, and I’ll keep fighting to keep more dollars in Iowa — because that’s how we build an economy where the next generation can start a business, buy a home, and raise a family right here in our communities.”
“The U.S.
Chamber is proud to endorse Congressman Zach Nunn for reelection in Iowa’s Third Congressional District.
He has been a strong advocate for pro-growth policies that strengthen the economy, support job creation, and help businesses of all sizes succeed,” said Tom Wickham, Vice President and Managing Director of the U.S.
Chamber’s Government Affairs team.
“We are pleased to support his campaign and look forward to building on our partnership in the next Congress.”
