Nunn Raises Over $900,000 into Campaign Committees in First Quarter, Most Raised Ever for a IA-03 Republican in First Quarter Non-Election Year
Des Moines, I.A. – Combat aviator Zach Nunn, Republican Congressman in Iowa’s Third Congressional District, announced the strongest fundraising performance in an off-year quarter for any GOP candidate for the seat in history.
“I am humbled and honored by the outpouring of support for our campaign to send a proven leader to Congress who will fight for a secure border, restore our economy and deliver commonsense solutions for our district,” Nunn said.
“Kelly and I want to thank all the supporters, friends and families who came together to demonstrate the strong base of support needed to continue delivering real results for Iowans across the Third Congressional District.”
“Washington liberals have set their sights on Iowa’s Third Congressional District, and they are prepared to pour hundreds of millions of dollars into deceptive, negative attacks to boost their slate of lousy candidates who support open borders, soft-on-crime policies, and reckless spending that fuels inflation,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“That is why hardworking Iowa families across the district are increasingly rallying around combat aviator Zach Nunn as the commonsense candidate who has established himself as the proven leader, proven fighter and proven winner for Iowans.”
Key Takeaways on Nunn’s First Quarter Fundraising Total:
- Nunn’s fundraising performance outpaced first quarter odd-year fundraising quarters for all previous GOP candidates in Iowa’s Third District in history.
- Nunn set a strong foundation for success with overwhelming support from Iowans and small dollar individuals, with over 6,700 of his campaign contributions coming from them.
