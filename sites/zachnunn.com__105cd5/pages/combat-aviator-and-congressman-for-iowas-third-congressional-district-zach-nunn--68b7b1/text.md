As part of a broader strategy to prepare for the general election, the campaign also executed a substantial transfer of resources from their joint fundraising committee, “Team Nunn,” into the campaign.
Further strengthening its position heading into November.
“I’m incredibly grateful to the Iowans who continue to stand with our campaign and invest in our mission to deliver real results across all 21 counties,” said Nunn.
“From lowering costs for families to keeping our communities safe, we’re focused on commonsense solutions that put Iowa first.
This support shows we have the momentum to keep fighting and winning for Iowans.” “This strong financial start to 2026 shows the broad support behind Zach Nunn and his record of delivering for all Iowans,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“That’s why he’s one of the top ten most bipartisan members of Congress.
While millions in outside spending pour into this race, we’re building the resources and operation needed to take our message directly to voters and win.” Details on Nunn’s First Quarter Fundraising: Nunn brought in over $835,000 into his campaign committee, “Iowans for Zach Nunn.” Additionally, Nunn directed $425,000 from “Team Nunn” into “Iowans for Zach Nunn” to reinforce general election efforts.
Nunn secured over $350,000 through his joint fundraising committee (JFC), “Team Nunn.” Nunn added over $20,000 into his Leadership PAC, “Call to Service.” Details on Nunn’s Campaign: Nunn received the largest number of nomination petition signatures than any other Congressional Candidate in Iowa, with more than three times the minimum to qualify.
Nunn continues to be consistently accessible to Iowans, holding hundreds of events in all 21 counties, producing dialogue that delivers real solutions for Iowa.
