Nunn Amasses War Chest Ahead of 2026 Showdown
Des Moines, IA – Congressman Zach Nunn, a combat aviator and Republican Representative for Iowa’s Third Congressional District, announced raising more than $3.5 million in 2025, including over $750,000 across all campaign committees in the fourth quarter of 2025.
With more than $2.3 million cash on hand, Nunn grows momentum heading into his third election for Congress in Iowa’s Third Congressional District.
“As Iowa’s Third District emerges as a top national target, Zach Nunn’s fundraising success reflects a campaign with the foundation to win in 2026,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“Iowans trust Zach Nunn because he listens, works across party lines, and delivers real results that help keep families safe and economically secure.”
