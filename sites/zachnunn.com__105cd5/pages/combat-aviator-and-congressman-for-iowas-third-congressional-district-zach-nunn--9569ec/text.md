Nunn Finished with Over $1.2 Million Cash on Hand in Campaign Committee
Des Moines, IA – Combat aviator Zach Nunn, Republican Congressman in Iowa’s Third Congressional District, announced raising over $1,100,000 across all campaign committees in the second quarter of 2025.
Nunn also finishes off the second quarter with over $1,200,000 cash on hand in his campaign account.
“I’m deeply grateful to everyone who rallied behind our campaign and chipped in their hard-earned dollars to build upon our mission to keep Iowa’s voice strong in Washington and deliver real results,” Nunn said.
“Together, we’re standing up for lower taxes, safer communities, and a more prosperous future for every Iowa family.
I’m ready to keep fighting for commonsense, bipartisan solutions and deliver for Iowa.”
“Left-wing extremists have made flipping Iowa’s Third Congressional District one of their top targets, and they’ll continue resorting to false attack ads to prop up their roster of weak candidates who support open borders, coddle criminals, and push a radical, inflation-driven agenda,” said Nunn Campaign Manager Brendan Duffy.
“Fortunately, Iowans see through the noise.
They know Zach Nunn is a proven fighter who doesn’t back down, and as one of the top ten most bipartisan members of Congress, he’ll continue delivering on his commitment to make Iowa stronger, safer, and more prosperous for every hardworking family.”
