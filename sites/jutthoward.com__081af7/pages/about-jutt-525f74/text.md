A Fighter for Conservative Values
Local businessman Jutt Howard has proudly been a resident of Carroll County since his early childhood.
Growing up in Sandhill, his father served as the minister of Bethany Christian Church and his mother worked as a teacher for the Carroll County school system.
Jutt is a graduate of Central High School and earned his Marketing degree from the University of West Georgia—all while running his own landscape business during college.
Today, Jutt is the Vice President of Business Operations at NG Turf, bringing over 20 years of agricultural experience to the role.
He also formerly served as President of the Board of Directors for the Georgia Crop Improvement Association (GCIA), helping to strengthen Georgia’s agricultural sector and ensuring farmers have access to top-quality resources.
In the Georgia State House, Jutt is a strong advocate for conservative values.
He has worked to lower taxes, enhance school safety, and protect girls’ sports across the state.
Jutt currently serves as a member of the following committees:
- Agriculture & Consumer Affairs
- Interstate Cooperation
- Natural Resources & Environment
Jutt and his wife, Natalie, have been married for 13 years and are raising their two children, Wyatt and Nellie, in Carroll County.
