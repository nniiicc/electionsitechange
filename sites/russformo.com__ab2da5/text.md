Russ Gosselin isn’t a career politician.
For years, he has worked directly with families facing real challenges — helping people find stable housing, meaningful work, stronger relationships, and a new purpose for their future.
“I believe leadership should focus on real-world solutions that help families build stable, successful lives.” Meet Russ Russ Gosselin is a builder—of businesses, of communities, and of pathways that help families move from surviving to thriving.
Throughout his career, Russ has worked across construction, community development, nonprofit, faith-based, media, and retail spaces, bringing people together to solve real problems facing families every day.
His work is driven by a simple belief: when the right support, relationships, and opportunities come together, lives can change.
Russ and his wife, Sharon, raised their two children in Colorado Springs, where they became deeply involved in neighborhood revitalization and community renewal efforts.
Those experiences shaped Russ’s conviction that lasting change doesn’t come from quick fixes, but from coordinated systems that treat people with dignity and help them move forward.
After his work with Convoy of Hope nationally, Russ founded Elevate Lives™ , a Springfield-based nonprofit that connects individuals and families with housing, mentorship, life skills, and community support.
Through Elevate Lives and the Elevate Center, Russ has helped build a collaborative model that brings together nonprofits, schools, employers, and faith-based partners to address root causes—not just symptoms—of poverty.
Russ enjoys spending time with his family, camping, hiking, and being outdoors.
More than anything, he is committed to building communities where families have the opportunity to thrive.
Russ Gosselin’s Priorities Helping Families Get Ahead Hard-working families deserve the chance to get ahead — not just get by.
Too many people are working harder than ever while still struggling with rising costs, housing pressure, and financial stress.
Russ supports practical solutions that: strengthen local businesses encourage job growth improve workforce opportunities support dependable infrastructure and help families build financial stability Because every family deserves the opportunity to move forward.
People don’t want handouts.
They want opportunity.
Better Futures for Our Kids Every child deserves the opportunity to succeed.
Strong schools, safe classrooms, and great teachers matter.
But preparing kids for the future also means helping them develop character, life skills, confidence, and real-world readiness.
Russ believes we should support: safe learning environments strong educational opportunities career and technical pathways mentorship and youth development and programs that help young people build successful futures right here in our community Because our kids are the future of Springfield.
We should prepare kids not just for tests — but for life.
Safer Neighborhoods Everyone deserves to feel safe where they live.
Safe neighborhoods happen when communities work together — law enforcement, schools, churches, businesses, parents, and neighborhood organizations all playing a role.
Russ believes we need practical partnerships that: reduce crime strengthen neighborhoods support addiction recovery create stability for families and help restore pride in our community Because safer neighborhoods create stronger communities.
People deserve to feel safe and proud of where they live.
