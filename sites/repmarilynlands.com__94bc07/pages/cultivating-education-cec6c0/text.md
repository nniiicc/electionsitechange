That is why I strongly support our investments in public education, because I see how it positively impacts individuals, families, and further strengthens our community.
I’m proud to say that my family went to public schools and I understand how that knowledge and experience served to unlock doors of opportunity for all of us.
That’s why we must safeguard and expand Alabama’s world class pre-kindergarten program, as well as ensure that every child also attends kindergarten.
We must give growing districts, like ours, the resources they need to build new schools and administrative support offices to keep up with new, incoming residents.
That includes making certain that there is adequate funding to help students with special needs.
While we increase resources to meet growing demand, we must also continue to support home rule, because what works for one district may not be the right fit for another.
Cultivating education goes hand-in-hand with growing economic opportunity.
That’s why I believe we need to work with local employers to re-engineer the way we engage in workforce development and training.
Today’s workforce is changing rapidly and we must embrace new ideas for learning and foster innovations in education to meet the needs of this fast-paced and dynamic economy.
As we look to cultivate education in Alabama, it means pruning away the distractions of contrived political controversy, and instead, nurturing edifying programs that have demonstrated measurable and evidence-based success.
Let’s pay teachers and retirees what they deserve.
Let’s create the type of effective learning environments that attract the very best and brightest educators to our state!
That’s how we can raise scores and close the educational achievement gap for Alabama students.
