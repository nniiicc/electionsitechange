Moving Forward
As we approach the 2025 Legislative Session, I have been active in the community by engaging with constituents and hearing your stories.
I will also be announcing town halls soon where we can meet and discuss the issues that are important to you.
So, please sign up for my newsletter and also check the Events page on this website to stay informed.
At the end of last session, in response to the critical need to expand women’s health care, I introduced HB494 which would provide presumptive eligibility for Medicaid to qualified mothers, as they await official confirmation, so that they may receive the critical maternal and prenatal care they need and deserve.
Often, confirmation of eligibility can take months and this common-sense measure would cut the red-tape and ensure that financially struggling mothers and their babies get critical health care services when it matters most.
I’m proud to say that my bill, HB494, has garnered bipartisan support and I will reintroduce it for this coming session.
The co-sponsors are: Representatives Rafferty, Ensler, Gray, Drummond, DuBose, Collins, Hulsey, Shaver, Daniels, Hollis, McClammy, Hall, Clarke, and McCampbell.
