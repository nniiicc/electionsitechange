On March 26, 2024, I was deeply humbled and honored to be elected to represent the people of House District 10 (my neighbors!) in the Alabama Legislature.
Serving House District 10 is a solemn responsibility and I will do my very best to ensure that your voice is heard on the important issues- the ones that matter most to you and our community.
Please subscribe to my newsletter and contact me so that I can best understand and represent your interests in Montgomery.
I want to hear your stories and your views.
This website will be updated to reflect the latest news from the legislature, information on issues that affect our community, and details about local events that may interest you.
Again, thank you for giving me the opportunity to serve my neighbors in House District 10.
-Rep.
Marilyn Lands
