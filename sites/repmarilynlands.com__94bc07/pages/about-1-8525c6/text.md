I say that because as a wife, a mother, and as a professional woman with a career, I understand many of the challenges and issues that are specific to our district because this is my home and my heart.
Here’s a little about my background and why I chose to run as a Representative for our district in the Alabama State House.
Professionally, I work as a Licensed Professional Counselor in a private practice and as a Supervising Counselor to help aspiring clinicians meet their requirements for state licensure.
Before entering private practice, I worked for over a decade in community mental health and I provided individual, family and group counseling for patients.
Over the years, I have also worked in the banking and aerospace industries.
I hold a Master of Science degree in Counseling Psychology from Alabama A&M University, as well as a Master of Science degree in Management and a Bachelor of Science degree in Business Administration from the University of Alabama in Huntsville.
I’m also a graduate of the Center for Action and Contemplation “Living School” (cac.org) and Leadership Huntsville/Madison County (member Class 5).
I’ve been honored to serve as past festival chair of Panoply of the Arts and a past board chair of The Arts Council and Leadership Huntsville/Madison County.
I am a person of deep personal faith and I am the author of A Day of Rest: A Primer on Keeping the Sabbath.
I am a member of the Episcopal Church of the Nativity and serve on the Commission for Spirituality in the diocese of Alabama.
I continue to work as a frequent lecturer and I conduct workshops on parenting, balanced living, stress management, sabbath keeping, mindfulness meditation, and coping with grief.
But, here’s why I ran for this seat and what I hope and believe we can accomplish together.
It’s simple really, I ran for this seat because I felt that the voices of my neighbors, especially women, were simply not being heard by politicians in Montgomery.
Too often, I see the government unnecessarily intruding into our personal lives and private decision-making.
I see little being done materially to improve access to affordable healthcare, mental health services, or women’s reproductive care.
I watched as our state not only took away a women’s right to choose, but also endangered IVF treatments for women wanting to start a family.
I see attacks on our libraries, public education, and even our voting rights.
I see a criminal justice system that needs massive overhaul and our immediate attention.
So, I decided to run to ensure that all of our voices were finally heard and, more importantly, to show that when we work together, we can make a positive, transformational, and measurable difference.
Especially on critical and life-changing issues as Creating Economic Opportunity, Cultivating Education, and Protecting Health Care and Reproductive Freedom.
Together, we really can make a difference.
Let’s keep sharing our stories and let’s keep working together to help Alabama reach its greatest potential.
