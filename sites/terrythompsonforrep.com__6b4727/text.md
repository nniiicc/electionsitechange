MEET TERRY
"I've worked hard to help create prosperity in Rural Missouri and I want to continue to make our communities a better place to work and live."
Terry Thompson is committed to saving Missouri's crumbling infrastructure.
Terry will work to expand workforce development including job training and business growth.
Terry will be a strong supporter of public education and wants to work to continue fully funding the foundation formula for K-12 education.
A married man of 40 years and proud father of two, Terry stands for family values.
Terry is a former small business owner and 40-year community banker in Lafayette County.
