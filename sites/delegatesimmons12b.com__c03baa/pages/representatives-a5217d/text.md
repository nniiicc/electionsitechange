District 12b Representatives Welcome to District 12B, where your voice matters!
At District 12B, we believe in the power of community engagement and the importance of connecting with our elected representatives.
Your input, ideas, and concerns shape the decisions we make and the policies we implement to serve our community better.
We invite you to connect with your elected representatives, share your thoughts, ask questions, and engage in meaningful dialogue about the issues that matter most to you.
Whether you have suggestions for improvement, want to learn more about local initiatives, or want to introduce yourself, we are here to listen.
There are several ways to connect with us: Town Hall Meetings : Join us for informative town hall meetings where you can engage directly with us and other community members.
Office Hours: Schedule a meeting to discuss specific concerns or ideas during our regular office hours.
Community Event s: Look out for us at various community events and gatherings throughout District 12B.
We welcome the opportunity to meet you in person.
Email and Phone : You can reach out to us via email or phone at any time.
We are here to listen and assist you with any questions or issues.
Social Media : Follow us on social media for updates, news, and opportunities to engage with us online. ​ Your participation is crucial to building a stronger, more vibrant community in District 12B.
We value your input and look forward to hearing from you.
Together, we can make a difference!
