Michael MacDonald isn’t a career politician — and he doesn’t think California’s problems are inevitable.
He’s spent his career inside local government, seeing what works, what doesn’t, and what happens when Sacramento’s decisions collide with the realities families and communities face every day.
He’s watched friends get priced out, felt the same rising costs, and grown frustrated with a politics that keeps asking Californians to pay more while accepting less.
Michael is running because we don’t have to manage decline — we can change it.
He brings the experience to understand government, the independence to challenge it, and the urgency of a new generation ready to address the causes, deliver results, and build what’s next.
