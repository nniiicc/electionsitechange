Andrew introduced House legislation and helped support the Senate companion bill requiring the state to provide valid identification to eligible people leaving the corrections system.
This is common-sense public safety.
When people have the basic tools they need to successfully reenter society, they are more likely to find stability and less likely to return to jail.
Andrew believes second chances should come with the basic tools people need to succeed.
