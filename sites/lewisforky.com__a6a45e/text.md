Meet Chris Lewis
Chris Lewis has served as State Representative for Kentucky’s 29th District since January 2025.
His full career has been dedicated to a better Louisville, beginning as a Field Representative for US Representative Anne Northup.
For over 20 years, Chris has advanced common-sense policy in Louisville Metro Government.
In the Kentucky House, Chris serves on the State Government Committee, the Local Government Committee, the Judiciary Committee, and the Small Business & Information Technology Committee.
His professional background includes:
- Deputy Director for the Louisville Metro Council Minority Caucus
- Legislative Assistant for Louisville Metro Council District 22
- Healthcare Services Professional
- REALTOR® with RE Solutions
- Former Small Business Owner
Chris is proud to be Pro-Life and believes in the sanctity of human life.
He is also a supporter of the 2nd Amendment and has fought to protect the rights of gun owners.
Chris is a strong supporter of law enforcement and is proud to be endorsed by the Kentucky Fraternal Order of Police.
He led the effort to bring gunshot location technology to high crime areas of Louisville, providing LMPD with a tool that has resulted in saving lives and helping solve crimes involving firearms.
Chris has had success helping to stop the creation of new tax burdens in Louisville, such as the tripling of health insurance premium taxes, and when he helped to stop increases in occupational taxes.
Both times, Chris was part of the solution to find efficiencies within our current budget without needing to increase taxes.
Chris worked to completely revamp Metro Government’s ethics code bringing additional transparency and accountability to our elected officials and government employees, and in Frankfort he carried that fight further as the primary sponsor of House Bill 607, bringing real oversight and accountability to Louisville Metro Government.
Married to his high school sweetheart, Linda, for 23 years, they share a commitment to their faith and have been actively involved members of Highview Baptist Church for nearly 20 years, where Chris has also served as a volunteer in prison ministry.
Together with his family, Chris has gone on several mission trips to Eastern Kentucky to help children in poverty-stricken areas.
Chris has a bachelor’s degree in Political Science from the University of Louisville, and Linda has degrees in Chemical Engineering and is a Senior Regulatory Manager at Brown-Forman.
Their children, Sydney [18] and Jackson [15] have both attended Whitefield Academy, where Chris served as a member of the board for several years.

IMPROVE EDUCATION
Fix JCPS, focus on students over systems, and equip our kids with career-ready skills.

Supporting our police officers to fight crime in Louisville.
