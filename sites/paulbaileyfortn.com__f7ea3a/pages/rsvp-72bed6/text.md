You’re invited to Senator Paul Bailey’s 5th Annual Cowboy Cookout!
September 19, 2026 | 5:00-8:00 pm | Duck Pond Manor
Join Senator Paul Bailey for a memorable Cowboy Cookout on Saturday, September 19, 2026, from 5:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. at the scenic Duck Pond Manor in Sparta, TN.

Admission is free, but we kindly ask that you RSVP by September 11, 2026, so we can be sure a plate is ready for you.
Come for the great food, stay for the entertainment, and enjoy the company of friends and neighbors in a beautiful setting.
Covered and tented areas will be available to ensure comfort in any weather.
