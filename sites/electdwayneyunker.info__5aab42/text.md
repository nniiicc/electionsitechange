Dwayne Yunker is a veteran, father, small-business-minded conservative, and Oregon State Representative for House District 3.
Raised with the values of hard work, faith, family, and service, Dwayne has spent his life standing up for his country, his community, and the freedoms that make America strong.
In Salem, Dwayne is fighting for lower taxes, safer communities, parental rights, Second Amendment freedom, government accountability, and the values of Josephine County.
Service & Duty My commitment to this country began early.
I enlisted in the U.S.
Navy during my junior year of high school, and that call to serve never left me.
After the Navy, I joined the United States Marine Corps, where I served more than a decade on active duty as an infantry Marine and later as a Marine Corps Drill Instructor.
Those years shaped me through discipline, courage, sacrifice, leadership, and an unshakable sense of mission.
When I gained custody of my eldest son, I made the decision to put family first and take on my most important duty: being a full-time father.
But my service to this country did not end there.
I later joined the Army National Guard, serving another 11 years and continuing my commitment to both country and community.
Today, I carry those same values into the Oregon State Legislature, where I serve with honor, integrity, and a firm commitment to protect the freedoms I once defended in uniform.
Why I’m Running I am running for re-election because Josephine County deserves a representative who will stand up to Salem and fight for the people back home.
Oregon families are facing higher costs, failing schools, rising crime, homelessness, and a state government that keeps demanding more while delivering less.
I believe taxpayers deserve accountability.
Parents deserve a voice.
Communities deserve safety.
Families deserve opportunity.
And our constitutional rights must be defended without apology.
I have served this country in uniform, served this community as a local citizen, and now serve House District 3 in the Oregon Legislature.
I am asking for your vote so I can continue fighting for Josephine County values in Salem.
