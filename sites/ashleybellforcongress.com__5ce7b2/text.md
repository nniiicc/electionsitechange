Election Day November 3 Early In-Person Voting October 15-31 Register Can same day register during early voting Voting Information Early voting locations are updated here.
During Early Voting (Oct 15-31), you can vote at any location in your county.
On Election Day, you have to vote at your assigned precinct.
If you miss early voting, you can still vote on Election Day, November 3.
Check the board of elections website (linked under each county here) for times.
If you're not registered, you can do same day registration during early voting and vote the same day.
Bring your photo ID with a valid, current address.
Same day registration is not available on Election Day.
