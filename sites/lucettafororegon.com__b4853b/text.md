It’s where I was raised, where I fell in love with my high school sweetheart, raised my family and decided to take a chance and start the first of many small businesses.
My story is one of community, connectivity, and unexpected chapters.
It’s a story that honed my leadership skills to prepare me for a role in our Oregon legislature.
In 2023, we considered leaving the state, but then I asked myself, “What if?” What if I could make a difference?
