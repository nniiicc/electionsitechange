Principles
Accountability
Elected officials must be directly accountable to the people who elect them — not to special interests, lobbyists, or outside influences.
The best representatives are those who regularly communicate with their constituents and faithfully represent their values and priorities.
I will always put the will of Utah voters first.
Education
A well-educated population is essential to a strong economy and a thriving society.
Utah’s Constitution recognizes education as a core responsibility of state government.
Every child deserves access to a quality education that prepares them for success in life, and we must strive to deliver excellent results for every Utah family.
Federalism
Government works best when it is closest to the people it serves.
Decisions should be made at the local and state level whenever possible, not by distant bureaucrats in Washington.
I will vigorously defend local control and push back against federal overreach that threatens our liberties and self-governance.
Free Market Capitalism
Free market capitalism, grounded in personal responsibility and voluntary exchange, is the greatest engine of prosperity and innovation ever created.
Excessive regulation and government interference stifle growth, discourage ingenuity, and raise costs for working families.
I will fight to reduce unnecessary regulations and expand economic freedom so Utah remains a place where businesses thrive and people can build better lives.
Individual Liberty
Our rights are God-given, not government-granted.
As an elected official, my sacred duty is to protect the individual liberties of every citizen.
I will strongly defend all of the rights guaranteed in the U.S.
Constitution’s Bill of Rights — especially freedom of speech, religion, the right to keep and bear arms, and due process.
Taxation
Taxation gives government the power to control behavior and pick winners and losers.
Government should only collect the taxes necessary to perform its essential functions.
Tax dollars belong to the people, not to politicians.
I will oppose using public money for special interest projects and work to keep taxes low so families and businesses can keep more of what they earn.
