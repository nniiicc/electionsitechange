We’re committed to raising teacher pay to competitive, livable levels, expanding resources for classroom materials, and increasing funding for support staff and student services.
By respecting and investing in those who teach our children, we ensure stronger learning environments, better student outcomes, and a more stable education system across the state.

Building Bridges Between Education and Industry Not every student needs a four-year degree to succeed—and Arizona’s workforce needs skilled professionals in every sector.
That’s why we’re increasing funding for vocational training programs and community colleges, especially in rural areas.
These programs provide practical skills and career pathways in fields like healthcare, construction, renewable energy, and advanced manufacturing—helping students secure good-paying jobs while fueling Arizona’s economic growth.
Expanding Career and Technical Education Water & Infrastructure Building a Resilient Arizona Arizona’s future depends on how we manage our most precious resource: water.
With prolonged drought and increasing demand, the time for action is now.
We’re developing a bold and comprehensive 100-year water strategy that secures long-term supply while protecting our natural ecosystems.
This plan will focus on diversifying our water sources through the development of new infrastructure such as water recycling and advanced treatment plants, responsibly exploring desalination, and increasing our reliance on reclaimed water.

Rebuilding Infrastructure for Safer, Stronger Communities Arizona’s roads, bridges, and public transportation systems are aging, and in many areas, they’re failing to meet the demands of our growing population.
Rural communities often face the brunt of these gaps, with unsafe roads and limited transit options.
Our infrastructure plan prioritizes investments in rebuilding and modernizing these critical systems across the entire state.

Modernizing for Sustainability and Resilience The Economy Building a Fair and Thriving Arizona Arizona families are being priced out of the housing market due to corporate investors buying up thousands of homes, driving prices sky-high and limiting supply.
We’re taking bold steps to stop this trend by cracking down on out-of-state investment firms, increasing transparency in real estate practices, and protecting homeownership opportunities for local residents.
At the same time, we’ll increase the construction of affordable and workforce housing through incentives, zoning reform, and public-private partnerships.
Every Arizona family deserves a chance to buy or rent a safe, stable, and affordable home—and we’re making that a reality.
Making Housing Affordable for Arizona Families Rising costs at the gas pump, grocery store, and utility bill are squeezing families across the state.
We’re fighting to put money back in your pocket by offering targeted gasoline rebates for working families and holding corporations accountable for unfair price gouging.
We’ll also invest in alternative fuel infrastructure, expand access to clean energy, and push for policies that reduce utility costs over time.
Economic relief shouldn’t just come during a crisis—it should be built into a smarter, more resilient system that protects working families all year round.
Lowering Everyday Costs for Working People Supporting Local Businesses and Entrepreneurs Small businesses are the backbone of Arizona’s economy—and we’re committed to helping them thrive.
Our plan includes expanding access to capital, cutting unnecessary red tape, and providing mentorship, training, and digital tools to help local entrepreneurs succeed.
We’ll prioritize rural economic development, support minority- and women-owned businesses, and encourage innovation by creating business incubators and startup hubs throughout the state.
When local businesses grow, they create good-paying jobs and build stronger, more self-sufficient communities.
