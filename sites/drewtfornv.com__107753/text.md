Issues
Public Safety
The safety of our families and neighborhoods must always come first.
I stand with law enforcement and victims of crime.
Safe communities allow families to thrive and businesses to grow.
Education
Families supported by strong schools are the foundation for our children’s future.
Parents should guide their children’s education.
I will fight for school choice, so every family can find the best fit for their child.
I will support our classroom teachers and ensure that taxpayer dollars go to learning, not wasteful overhead.
Economy
Nevadans deserve economic policies that let them control their future.
I will fight for family first policies that empower job creation, wage growth, and support the realities of affording life for growing families.
Energy
For too long Nevada has allowed California to regulate our energy and push their out of control policies into the wallets of Nevada’s families.
We must diversify our energy supply towards sources that are affordable.
