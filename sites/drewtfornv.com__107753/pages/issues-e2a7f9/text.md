Issues
Public Safety
The safety of our families and neighborhoods must always come first.
I will stand with law enforcement and victims of crime to oppose radical efforts to weaken justice for violent criminals, and make sure officers have the tools they need to keep our communities secure.
I know the importance of strength and consistency for cracking down on crime and protecting the rights of all law-abiding citizens.
Safe communities allow families to thrive and businesses to grow.
Economy
Nevadans deserve economic policies that let them control their future.
I will fight for family first policies that empower job creation, wage growth, and support the realities of life for growing families.
Through responsibly developing our natural resources and diversifying our industries, we can attract new opportunities and keep Nevada competitive for future generations.
I will fight to keep Nevada from having a state income tax and eliminating burdensome regulations that both hurt businesses and fail to provide a public benefit.
Education
Families supported by strong schools are the foundation for our children’s future.
Parents, not government or unions, should guide their children’s education.
I will fight for school choice, so every family can find the best fit for their child.
I will support all of our classroom teachers and ensure that taxpayer dollars go to learning, not wasteful overhead.
And I will oppose divisive political agendas in the classroom so our children can focus on learning the basics they need to succeed.
Nevada’s future depends on preparing the next generation with real skills, not political indoctrination.
Energy
Nevada’s dependence on California for our energy needs has left our families vulnerable to unnecessary costs and crushing regulations.
I am committed to a Nevada First energy policy that prioritizes supply diversity.
We must add additional pipeline capacity to secure fuel from across the country and move away from our reliance on a volatile neighbor.
By simultaneously fostering more energy production right here, we can lower the cost of living and provide the reliable, affordable power our industries and families need to flourish.
