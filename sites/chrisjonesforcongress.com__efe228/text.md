Meet Chris I grew up in Pine Bluff as the son of a minister, where I learned the values of service and community.
That path led me to science, but I never left Arkansas behind.
My work has always been about building bridges: connecting faith and science, rural and urban, young and old — and respecting our traditions while bringing new, innovative ideas for our future.
Along the way, I’ve helped our communities grow.
I’ve stood shoulder to shoulder with Arkansans, fighting for stronger schools, good-paying job opportunities, and high-quality accessible healthcare.
What drives me now is a simple vision: a future where every Arkansan belongs, and where the table of opportunity keeps expanding with room for us all.

Families want good jobs close to home, schools that prepare their kids for the future, healthcare they can count on, and a democracy that reflects their voices.
These goals are about building lasting opportunities and making sure every community has the tools to thrive.
Jobs & Local Economy We can build an economy that lifts every community.
Good jobs, fair wages, small business growth, high-quality internet access, and hospitals that stay open in rural towns.
That’s how we strengthen opportunity across the state.
Families & Health Every family deserves the chance to thrive.
Stronger schools, a full range of comprehensive maternal health support, addiction support and recovery, and food security must be part of the foundation for healthy communities.
Schools & Innovation Our government should invest in our people.
Early childhood education, hands-on apprenticeships, and training in both technology and the trades will prepare our children and our workforce for tomorrow.
Democracy for the People Democracy belongs to all of us.
Fair maps, secure and accessible elections, a government you can trust, and leaders who listen are the guardrails that protect our voice and our future.
