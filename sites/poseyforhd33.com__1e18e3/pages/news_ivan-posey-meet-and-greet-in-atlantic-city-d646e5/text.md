Ivan Posey Meet and Greet in Atlantic City
On Thursday, October 3rd, Ivan Posey will be in Atlantic City to meet you and answer your questions about his candidacy for the Wyoming Legislature HD 33.
This Meet and Greet will start at 6 PM and will be at the St.
Andrew’s Church Community Room, 50 E.
Forbes St., Atlantic City.
Ivan is running for the seat now held by Rep.
Sarah Penn.
In Wyoming (unlike more populated states) we have the opportunity to meet directly with our candidates and elected officials, no matter how small our community.
We look forward to a lively discussion.
