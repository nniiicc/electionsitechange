But what actually helped me make my mind up [about running] was reading the last legislative budget session and seeing that there were still people that are moderate and willing to work together, and that have the best interest of Wyoming in mind.
You know, I'm not only a tribal citizen or citizen of my tribe.
I'm also a citizen of the county and the state, and nationally.
As the original – I shouldn’t say Wyomingites, but we were here before the state was even in existence – I think I'd be bringing a little more and a little better representation, whether it's in health care, law enforcement or veterans issues.
I think it’s a big canvas with a lot to paint on.
