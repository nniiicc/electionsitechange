Ivan D.
Posey Statement on the House District 33 Election
We have won the election and assured Tribal and non-tribal representation from the district in the Wyoming Legislature.
I want to thank the many people who have expressed faith in me and supported my candidacy, including volunteers, donors, family, and friends.
I am so grateful and humbled by your support.
I look forward to representing all of the people of House District 33 in Cheyenne.
I will work with any other House member, Republican, Democrat, or Independent, to deliver results for my constituents.
I will approach my time in Cheyenne with a listening ear and the civility that befits the position and address the issues and concerns of the great people of the district.
Thanks to all for your support and God bless.
Let’s move forward together!
