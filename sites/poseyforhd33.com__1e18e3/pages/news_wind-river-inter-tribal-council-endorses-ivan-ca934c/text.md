Ivan D.
Posey as a candidate for House District 33, Wyoming State Legislature.
Mr.
Posey has the experience with Tribal, State and Federal governments, Education and Health institutions, the US Forest Service and VFW as well as other entities, all which would serve us all in the House District 33 and be a voice for the benefit of all our communities in our district.” The letter is signed by John St.
Clair, the Chairman of the Eastern Shoshone Business Council and Lloyd Goggles, the Chairman of the Northern Arapaho Business Council.
