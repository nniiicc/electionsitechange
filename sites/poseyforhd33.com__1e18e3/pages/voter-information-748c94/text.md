Voting Information
Election Day 2024— Tuesday, November 5th
Important Dates for Election 2024
- Now-November 4— Early Voting.
You can register and vote early in person at the Fremont County Clerk’s Election Office.
Fremont County Courthouse, 450 N. 2nd Street, Lander, Wyoming 82520.
The Election Office is open Monday-Friday from 8 AM until 5 PM.
- Tuesday, November 5— General Election Day
On Election Day, Tuesday, November 5th, you can register and vote at any of the Fremont County Vote Centers:
- Arapahoe Charter School Building, 445 Little Wind River Bottom Rd., Arapahoe
- Wyoming Indian High School Gym, 638 Blue Sky Highway, Ethete
- Ft.
Washakie School Gym, 90 Ft.
Washakie School Rd., Ft.
Washakie
- Kinnear Fire Hall, 11521 Highway 26, Kinnear
- Bob Carey Memorial Field House, Lander Valley High School, 350 Baldwin Creek Rd., Lander
- Fremont Center, Fremont County Fairgrounds, 1010 Fairgrounds Dr., Riverton
- Shoshoni Senior Citizens Center, 214 E. 2nd St, Shoshoni
- Dubois Fire Hall, 8 3rd St., Dubois
Vote Centers are open from 7 AM until 7 PM on Election Day
A Valid ID is Required to Vote!
Acceptable IDs:
- Wyoming Driver’s License or ID Card
- Tribal ID Card
- Valid United States Passport
- United States Military Card
- Driver’s License or ID Card from Another State
- University of Wyoming Student ID
- Wyoming Community College Student ID
- Wyoming Public School Student ID
- Valid Wyoming Concealed Firearm Permit
- Valid Medicare Insurance Card (no longer acceptable after 2029)
- Valid Medicaid Insurance Card (no longer acceptable after 2029)
