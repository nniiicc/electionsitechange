Ivan Will Represent All the People of House District 33.
Donate Ivan Posey, Candidate for Wyoming House District 33.
“I have served my country in the military, in public service to the Wind River Tribes, and through my career in natural resources and education.
I support effective government for the people and believe that the primary role of a government is providing for the people’s health, safety, education, and wellbeing.
An elected public servant’s role is ensuring these are supported AND equitably delivered to all the people, regardless of where they live or who they are.” Learn more about Ivan's Platform Born and Raised Here!
House District 33 needs real representation.
Ivan has lived in Fremont County his entire life, except for schooling at a boarding school in Oklahoma and service in the United States Army.
He has served in tribal government, worked for the USDA Forest Service, and is an educator at Central Wyoming College.
He knows the people of House District 33 because he is one.

Endorsed By: Wind River Inter-Tribal Council “Mr.
Posey has the experience with Tribal, State and Federal governments, Education and Health institutions, the US Forest Service and VFW as well as other entities, all which would serve us all in the House District 33 and be a voice for the benefit of all our communities in our district.” Wyoming Medical Political Action Committee WYOPAC endorsed- Medicine friendly candidate Wyoming Education Association Political Action Committee for Education Posey will “prioritize and protect our public schools and vote for what is best for students and education employees.” Wyoming Public Employees Association Political Action Committee Wyoming Realtors PAC Wyoming Stock Growers Association House District 33 Deserves… Experienced Leadership Ivan has served 21 years on the Eastern Shoshone Business Council, many of them as the Council Chair.
He served as the first Tribal Liaison for the Eastern Shoshone under Governor Dave Freudenthal.
Currently, he is Central Wyoming College’s Director of Tribal Education and the Institute of Tribal Learning.
Real Representation House District 33 is Wyoming’s only minority majority district.
Ivan understands the challenges facing members of both the Eastern Shoshone and Northern Arapaho tribes and will work to address them.
Through his work with the USDA Forest Service, Ivan has worked with the area’s outdoor recreationists and many of the ranching families that make up a sizeable portion of the district.
Ivan will work to represent all of House District 33.
Respectful, Civil Discussion Ivan will humbly approach the opportunity to represent the people of House District 33.
A self-styled conservative Democrat, Ivan will work for practical, commonsense solutions that meet the needs of all the people of the district.
