Issues change.
Values don’t.
My values shape how I approach public policy and the decisions I would make as your state representative.
They show you not only where I stand today, but how I will approach the challenges we haven’t seen yet.
These are the principles that guide me.
The People's Voice Must be Respected
Government belongs to the people.
When voters speak, their representatives should listen, even when they disagree with the outcome.
Everyone Deserves a Fair Shot
Government shouldn't guarantee outcomes, but the rules must not favor the wealthy, powerful, or well-connected.
Hard work should have a fair chance to pay off.
Government Answers to the People
Public officials work for the public.
They do NOT work for political parties, wealthy donors, corporations, or special interests.
Public Money is the People's Money
Tax dollars should be spent carefully, efficiently, and transparently for the public benefit.
Representatives must question each subsidy, giveaway, and policy that privatizes benefits while socializing costs.
Strong Public Schools Build Strong Communities
Education is our most important investment in Missouri's future.
Strong public schools give children opportunities, make communities stronger, and prepare them to participate in our democracy.
Good Healthcare Must Be Within Everyone's Reach
No one should face financial ruin because they get sick, and access to necessary medical care shouldn't depend upon wealth or circumstance.
Hard Work Should Be Rewarded
People who work for a living should be able to build a decent life.
Workers deserve fair pay, safe workplaces, the freedom to organize, and an economy that rewards productive work rather than merely accumulated wealth.
Rights Belong to Everyone
Individual liberty means nothing if it applies only to people we agree with.
Government must protect the rights and dignity of every person under the law.
Let's Solve Problems, Not Manufacture Enemies
Public service should be fair and practical.
Missouri has real problems to solve, and government works best when people with different beliefs can work together rather than treating every disagreement as a zero-sum game.
Checks and Balances
Concentrated power must never be tolerated.
Whether that power lies in our government, corporations, political parties, or wealthy special interests, we must demand and enforce transparency, accountability, and meaningful checks.
Facts Matter
Political or religious ideology must never determine the answer before the question has even been asked.
Our Obligation is to Our Future Generations
Decisions about education, infrastructure, natural resources, technology, debt, and economic development must always consider the Missouri we're leaving to the next generation.
