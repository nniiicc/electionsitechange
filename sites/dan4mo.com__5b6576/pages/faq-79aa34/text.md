Frequently Asked Questions What Missouri District am I in?
To learn which district you reside within, click here .
Enter your street address and city, and you'll be given your state Senatorial District, your State Representative District, and your U.S.
House District.
You'll also find the names of all your senators and representatives!
Am I registered to vote?
Please check your voter registration status periodically.
You can check your voter status by clicking here .
You'll need to enter your first and last name, your date of birth, the last four digits of your social, and your county.
If you are currently registered to vote, you'll see your name and address at the top of the page.
If you are not registered to vote, the resulting page will say that your name cannot be located, and it will provide links where you can go to register.
Where do I vote?
Follow the same link as given above by clicking here .
You'll need to enter your first and last name, your date of birth, the last four digits of your social, and your county.
If you are currently registered to vote AND if a polling place has been designated, you'll see the location on the resulting page.
A polling place may not have been designated at the time you click on the link, so you may not yet see your polling place.
If you're concerned that you still don't have a clear idea of where to vote, you can always call the Jefferson County Clerk at 636-797-5486.
