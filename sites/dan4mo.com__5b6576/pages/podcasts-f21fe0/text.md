Shades of Jade Podcast
The Shades of Jade Podcast is a right-leaning political podcast offering weekly summaries, interviews, and bonus episodes on true crime, hauntings, and more.
In this episode, host Sam Wooldrik talks with Dan about his ongoing campaign, the surprising level of voter engagement he's seeing on the ground, and why he's sticking with his provocative message that "Missouri is Rigged" - not in elections, but in systemic ways that favor the powerful.
Dan breaks down the chaotic redistricting battle playing out in Missouri, including petitions, court fights, and county-level pushback, while contrasting it with national political turmoil.
The discussion also covers a controversial ballot measure to eliminate the state income tax by massively expanding sales taxes on services (from haircuts and doctor visits to Netflix), Missouri's strong unemployment retraining programs, criminal justice reform stories involving long prison sentences and wrongful convictions, and even an innovative AI-based idea for preventing deer-vehicle collisions on highways.
Join us for a grounded, insightful episode full of real-talk on state-level politics and practical solutions.

Scuttlebutt Podcast
The Scuttlebutt podcast was born out of the desire to express ourselves openly.
They dance between serious and hilariously unserious, hopping on all the topical and tangential lily pads along the way.
They are a mix of personalities and backgrounds that cannot be described in a single category.
This recipe results in an honest, friendly, and, at times, controversial show, but almost always a fun one.
They often like to describe the podcast as a random meeting in a pub, at the smoke pit, or one of those long duty-hour-inspired settings; good vibes, loose lips, and who knows where the night will take us.
Listen in as the host, Hermes Auslander, and Dan discuss politics without fear of meandering into undiscovered territory.
