PRESS RELEASE
Tiffany Williams-Rice advances to the General Election
June 9, 2026
I am grateful for the support of voters, volunteers, supporters, and community members who helped make this milestone possible.
Following the June primary election, I have officially advanced to the November General Election for North Dakota House District 7.
This campaign has always been about listening to residents, building community connections, and focusing on the issues that matter most to North Dakota families, including affordability, healthcare access, education, housing, and economic opportunity.
As we move toward November, I look forward to continuing conversations across District 7 and earning the support of voters throughout our community.
Thank you to everyone who has supported this campaign and joined us on this journey.
