Affordable Healthcare
Every North Dakotan deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare close to home.
As a former healthcare leader and educator, I understand the challenges families face when healthcare costs continue to rise while access to providers becomes more limited.
I believe in strategic solutions that strengthen our healthcare system, improve access to care, and support the long-term health of our communities while being responsible stewards of taxpayer dollars.
I support efforts to:
- Strengthen and retain North Dakota's healthcare workforce.
- Protect and improve access to rural hospitals and community healthcare services.
- Expand access to primary care, behavioral health, and preventive services.
- Increase price transparency so patients can make informed healthcare decisions.
- Reduce unnecessary barriers that drive up healthcare costs for patients and providers.
- Encourage collaboration among healthcare providers, communities, and policymakers to improve health outcomes.
Healthy communities begin with accessible, affordable healthcare.
I'll work to ensure North Dakotans can receive the care they need, when they need it, close to home.
