Endorsement Announcement
Endorsed by The Collective PAC
June 2026
I am honored to announce that I have received the endorsement of The Collective PAC, a national organization dedicated to increasing Black political representation and supporting leaders committed to public service.
The experiences I've gained as an educator, former healthcare leader, and community advocate continue to guide my approach to listening, collaborating, and working toward solutions that support North Dakota families.
Receiving the endorsement of The Collective PAC is a meaningful recognition of those values and the work our campaign is doing to engage residents and strengthen our communities.
As I continue my campaign for North Dakota House District 7, I remain focused on the issues that matter most to North Dakota families, including affordability, healthcare access, quality education, housing, childcare, and workforce development.
I am grateful for the support of The Collective PAC and for the encouragement I continue to receive from residents throughout District 7.
I look forward to continuing conversations across our community and working toward practical solutions that strengthen opportunities for North Dakota families.
