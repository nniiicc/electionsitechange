The struggles we see on Bronx blocks — rent that keeps rising, families stretched thin, young people looking for a way up — are the struggles of New Yorkers everywhere.
That is where the work starts, and that is who it is for.

Seeing it up close is how we know what has to change — for the Bronx and for the whole state.

THE VISION FOR TOMORROW A New York that works for every New Yorker.
Homes you can afford — and keep Affordable housing, protection for tenants and homeowners, and an end to deed theft.
Safe, clean, thriving streets Neighborhoods where every family feels secure and proud to call home.
Young people who learn, earn and lead Strong schools, STEAM labs, the arts and child care — plus workforce pathways from the classroom to good jobs.
Good jobs and small business Union and vocational pathways, and the support local businesses need to grow.
Dignity for seniors and veterans Ending veterans' poverty and keeping our promises to those who built New York and those who served it.
Health care everyone can reach Affordable, preventive care close to home for every New Yorker.
MEET LANDON Rooted in New York — Working for New York.
Landon Dais is a husband, a father of two and a Highbridge homeowner who coaches his sons' baseball team.
Before serving in the Assembly, he worked in construction on the new Yankee Stadium, then built a law practice helping small businesses and nonprofits grow.
Morehouse, Columbia and Hofstra Law trained him; New York shaped him.
