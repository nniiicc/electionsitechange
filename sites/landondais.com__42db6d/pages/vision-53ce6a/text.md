NEW YORK FIRST.
01 · HOUSING Homes you can afford — and keep Housing is a right, not a luxury.
We will build more affordable homes, protect tenants, and stop deed theft so families who built our neighborhoods can stay in them.
02 · SAFE, CLEAN STREETS Neighborhoods we're proud to call home Every New Yorker deserves to feel safe on their block.
We will keep investing in community safety, clean streets and green spaces — and keep rolling up our sleeves alongside neighbors to get it done.
03 · YOUTH & EDUCATION Young people who learn, earn and lead Strong schools, STEAM and the arts, after-school programs and child care working families can count on.
And real workforce development — partnerships with unions, employers and training programs that create clear pathways from the classroom to good jobs for young people.
10 STEAM Labs $2 million to build them in schools across the district 04 · JOBS & SMALL BUSINESS Good jobs and a fair shot Union and vocational training pathways, and real support for the small businesses that power our economy and create local jobs.
05 · SENIORS & VETERANS Ending veterans' poverty No one who served this country should struggle to put food on the table or keep a roof overhead.
Landon's Campaign to End Veterans' Poverty fights for the housing, jobs, benefits and care veterans earned — and for dignity and security for the seniors who built New York.
06 · HEALTH CARE Care everyone can reach Affordable, preventive health care close to home, and standing with the nurses and health workers who care for our communities.
07 · EVERY NEW YORKER COUNTED If you're not counted, you're not heard The census decides our representation and our fair share of funding.
That is why Landon secured funding to build a dedicated state office focused on the census, and why he will keep fighting until every New Yorker — in every neighborhood — is counted.
A stronger tomorrow starts here.
