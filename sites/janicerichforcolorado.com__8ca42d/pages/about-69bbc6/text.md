About
Janice Rich served as Mesa County Treasurer from 2011 through 2018.
Prior to being elected Treasurer, Janice served two terms as Mesa County Clerk & Recorder.
Before being elected Clerk & Recorder, Janice operated her own secretarial and business support service for six years.
She is a Certified Professional Legal Secretary, has worked as a legal assistant for more than 20 years, and also served as Court Clerk in the U.S.
Magistrate’s office (Western Colorado).
