DONATE Log In Meet Julie Evans for Texas District 64 A Positive Change for Texas Connect with Julie About Julie Evans A Positive Change for Texas Right now, Texans need someone in Austin who is willing to listen, to engage with everyone from Runaway Bay to Krum.
Born and raised in Texas, Julie is proud of her Texas roots and has firmly planted them in the Denton soil for over 30 years.
When her husband of 36 years, Jack, left the Navy, they returned to Texas to raise their son, Nick, and two daughters, Maggie and Chelsea.
After graduating from high school and earning an Associate's degree in Journalism from Navarro Junior College, she attended UNT, where all three of their children would graduate with Bachelor's degrees.
She raised three children, spent decades volunteering in her community, dropped off her youngest daughter at high school, and then reentered the workforce.
She found her dream job, shaping the students' lives' in the Special Education classroom.
After retiring, she perfected the art of baking bread, growing vegetables, and spoiling their 2-year-old granddaughter, Nora.
In the spirit of both families' tradition of service, Julie now throws her hat in the ring to serve Texas.
Texans are facing obstacles that are deliberately put in our path by an overreaching, intrusive government, dark money, and billionaires who control it.
We need to raise the minimum wage, keep rural hospitals open, and address the unnecessary tariff war restricting the marketplace of our farmers.
Dedicated to protecting civil liberties and the Democratic process, Julie will be a champion for the rights of all Texans.
