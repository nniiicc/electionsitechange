- To process contributions and meet our reporting obligations.

- To comply with applicable law.
Federal disclosure of contributions
Contribution information cannot be kept private.
Federal law requires political committees to report to the Federal Election Commission the name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of any individual whose contributions aggregate in excess of $200 in an election cycle.
That information becomes part of the public record and is published by the FEC at fec.gov.
We are required to collect and report it and cannot treat it as confidential.
Email and text messages
If you provide your email address, you consent to receive email from the Committee.
You may unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email we send.
If you provide your mobile number and opt in, you consent to receive campaign-related text messages, which may be sent using automated technology.
Consent is not a condition of any contribution or purchase.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.
We do not share text-messaging opt-in data with third parties for their own marketing, except with vendors and service providers performing work on our behalf, or where disclosure is required by law.
How we share information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information in these circumstances:

With vendors who perform work for the campaign, such as email delivery, payment processing, data hosting, and analytics, and who are obligated to protect it.

With the Federal Election Commission or other authorities where required by law, and in response to lawful requests such as subpoenas or court orders.

Where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of the Committee or others.
