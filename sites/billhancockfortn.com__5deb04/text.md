Meet Bill Hancock Bill Hancock is a 6th-generation Tennessean and native Nashvillian, who grew up in West Meade.
The grandson and son of Metro school teachers, he learned the importance of a good education from a young age and earned a business degree from the University of Tennessee.
Bill built a successful business career in healthcare and real estate. ﻿ Active in his community and church, Bill is the proud parent of three wonderful children.
