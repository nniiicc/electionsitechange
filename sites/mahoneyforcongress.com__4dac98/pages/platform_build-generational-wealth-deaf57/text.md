05 · The Problem Economic mobility without ownership is fragile.
A family that rents can be pushed out by a market shift.
A family that owns can pass down stability across generations.
For too long, Philadelphia's wealth-building ladders have been broken — by predatory lending, by tangled titles, by neighborhood disinvestment. · Dennis's Plan Repair the wealth-building ladder.
Pass federal tangled-title reform so families can claim the homes their elders left them.
Fund home-repair grants so legacy homeowners can stay.
Invest in neighborhood small-business growth, especially Black- and immigrant-owned.
Strengthen the institutions — churches, CDCs, credit unions — that anchor wealth in place.
Dennis will fight to: Tangled title reform Home repair access Neighborhood small-business growth Pathways to homeownership Investment in churches, nonprofits, and community institutions Strong neighborhoods are built when families pass opportunity to the next generation. · How we pay for it Targeted tax credits and CRA modernization.
Modernize the Community Reinvestment Act to drive private capital into the neighborhoods that need it.
Pair with refundable homeowner tax credits and SBA expansion for legacy small businesses. · How you can help Become a neighborhood captain.
