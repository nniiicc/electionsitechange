03 · The Problem Opportunity only matters if you can reach it.
A job training program in Center City means nothing to a worker in West Philadelphia if the bus runs once an hour.
A college application portal is useless without reliable broadband at home.
ZIP code shouldn't decide whether opportunity is a real option. · Dennis's Plan Transit, broadband, and apprenticeships — together.
Push federal investment in SEPTA reliability and frequency on the routes that move working people.
Make broadband a universal-service utility with affordability protections.
Expand registered apprenticeships in the trades, healthcare, and advanced manufacturing.
Stand up neighborhood opportunity hubs — physical places where workforce, education, childcare, and benefits navigation happen in one trip.
Dennis will fight to: Expanded transit connectivity Digital access and broadband affordability Workforce training and apprenticeships Neighborhood opportunity hubs Investments connecting residents to growing industries Opportunity only matters if people can actually reach it. · How we pay for it Existing federal programs, used together.
