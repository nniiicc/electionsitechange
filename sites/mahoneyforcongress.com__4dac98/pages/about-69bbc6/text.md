A campaign for community.
Dennis Mahoney has spent his life showing up — for foster families, neighborhood markets, schools, and the people who make Philadelphia work.
Dennis Mahoney · Philadelphia Dennis Mahoney was raised on the idea that you serve before you ask.
Long before any campaign, he showed up — for foster families navigating an overwhelmed system, for parents trying to keep food on the table, for neighbors trying to keep a block together.
He has worked alongside coalitions supporting children in foster care, helped organize food markets for families in need, and stood with school districts working to give every child a real shot.
That work — quiet, ordinary, persistent — is the heart of this campaign.
Dennis believes Philadelphia is one of the greatest cities in the world: the birthplace of American democracy, a city of neighborhoods, resilience, faith, and grit.
He also believes too many families are working harder than ever while feeling further behind.
A raise should not cost a family its healthcare.
Overtime should not cost a parent childcare.
A senior should not lose the home their family built.
A young person should not have to leave Philadelphia to find a future.
His politics are not about outrage or cynicism.
They are about service, stability, and the quiet revolution of taking responsibility for the future, together.
