04 · The Problem A strong family is a public good.
Family stability is one of the strongest predictors of long-term economic mobility.
Yet too many federal policies treat families as line items rather than the foundation of a flourishing country. · Dennis's Plan Treat family stability as economic policy.
Restore and expand the Child Tax Credit.
Protect SNAP, WIC, and school meals from cuts.
Make childcare affordable through a combined federal-state structure.
Expand community-based healthcare access.
Strengthen utility affordability programs so no family chooses between heat and groceries.
Dennis will fight to: Restore and expand the Child Tax Credit Protect food assistance and school meals Increase childcare affordability Expand community-based healthcare access Strengthen utility affordability programs No family should have to navigate a crisis alone. · How we pay for it Investments in stability return to the economy.
Public investments in early childhood, child nutrition, and family stability are among the highest-return categories of federal spending.
The campaign will publish specific cost-and-return analyses, with their sources, as each proposal is finalized. · How you can help Sign on as a family advocate.
