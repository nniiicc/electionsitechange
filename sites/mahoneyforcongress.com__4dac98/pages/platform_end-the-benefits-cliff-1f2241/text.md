01 · The Problem A raise should not cost a family its healthcare.
Across Philadelphia, working families face a hidden tax: take a promotion, and you can lose more in benefits than you gain in pay.
A parent earning one more dollar can lose childcare.
A worker picking up overtime can lose Medicaid.
A senior accepting a small raise can lose food assistance.
This is the benefits cliff — and it punishes the exact upward mobility our economy depends on. · Dennis's Plan Phase benefits out gradually, not all at once.
Dennis will fight for federal legislation that smooths the transition from public benefits to economic independence — phasing benefits out gradually as income rises, not yanking them away the moment a family crosses an arbitrary line.
He will work with city and state partners to coordinate workforce programs, childcare subsidies, healthcare access, and housing assistance so a single life is treated as a single system — not five disconnected forms.
Dennis will fight to: Phase out benefits gradually instead of abruptly Protect families from healthcare affordability cliffs Coordinate workforce and support systems Modernize anti-poverty programs around upward mobility Work should always move families forward. · How we pay for it Designed to expand opportunity, not the deficit.
Smoothing the benefits cliff is intended to bring more workers into stable, full-time employment over time.
