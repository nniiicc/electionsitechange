Four chapters on Dennis Mahoney's work — and the campaign that comes from it.
01 01 A life of service A career built around showing up.
Dennis Mahoney has spent his life convening businesses, nonprofits, faith communities, and civic leaders to solve complex problems and widen the door of opportunity for vulnerable communities — overseas, across the United States, and in his own backyard of Philadelphia.
Wherever he serves, the throughline is the same: show up, build the coalition, deliver real results.
02 02 The work Real results for hurting people.
His career in disaster relief and long-term development shaped a single mindset — real results for hurting people.
In Philadelphia, Dennis builds cross-sector coalitions that keep kids out of the foster care system.
He brings neighborhood food markets to food deserts.
He works with the school district to surround students, teachers, and principals with community support.
03 03 Why this campaign A government that works like real life.
Too many people feel trapped between survival and stability — working hard and still struggling with rising housing costs, healthcare costs, transportation barriers, food insecurity, and systems that punish them for trying to move upward.
Our policies are too often designed in silos, while people experience life as one connected system.
Dennis is running to help build a government that rewards progress instead of punishing it — one that sees housing as workforce infrastructure, family stability as economic policy, and wealth-building as essential to neighborhood flourishing.
04 04 The vision A flourishing Philadelphia.
Philadelphia does not need more political theater.
It needs people willing to bring others together around practical solutions and a long-term vision — a city where families can afford to stay, young people can build futures, neighborhoods flourish, and institutions earn back the trust of the people they exist to serve.
The work ahead is bigger than any one politician or party.
