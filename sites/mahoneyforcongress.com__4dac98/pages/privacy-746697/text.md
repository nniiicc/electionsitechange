A plain-English summary of what we collect, how we protect it, and the promises we make about it.
Our promise: the information you share is used only for campaign communications and organizing.
We never sell, rent, or trade it, and we never hand it to outside organizations for their own use.
What we collect When you sign up on our site we collect your name, email address, ZIP code, phone number if you choose to share it, and the interests you select (campaign updates, volunteering, hosting an event, donating, or press).
We also record the date of your signup and which page you signed up from so we know where to follow up.
When you email or contact the campaign, we keep the message you send us.
If you donate, our payment processor collects the information required by law.
How we use it To send you campaign news, invite you to events near you, match you with volunteer opportunities that fit your schedule, and respond to what you asked us about.
That's it.
We do not use your information for advertising by other groups, and we do not sell or trade our lists.
Who can see it Only authorized campaign staff.
We also use a small number of trusted service providers who operate our email, event, and donation tools on our behalf — they may only process your information to provide those services to the campaign, and never for their own purposes.
How we protect it Your information travels over an encrypted connection and is stored in a secure, access-controlled database.
Staff access requires an individual account and sign-in, and only accounts explicitly granted access can view supporter records.
How long we keep it We keep your information for the life of the campaign and any period required by law, and we remove it when it is no longer needed for the purposes above or when you ask us to.
Your choices You can unsubscribe from email at any time using the link in any message.
To see, correct, or delete the information we hold about you, email info@mahoneyforcongress.com and we'll take care of it.
Text Messages and SMS Terms If you choose to sign up for texts, the Mahoney for Congress campaign will collect your mobile phone number and a record of your consent.
We use this information to send campaign updates, event reminders, volunteer opportunities, and fundraising messages, including autodialed messages.
Donations will be solicited.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Signing up for texts is optional.
You can unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP.
Reply HELP for help, or email info@mahoneyforcongress.com .
You can also request to stop receiving texts by emailing us.
We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS opt-in information with third parties for their marketing or fundraising purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Legal disclosures Federal law may require us to publicly disclose certain information about donors.
We follow all applicable election and campaign-finance rules.
This page will be updated as the campaign's policies and disclosures are finalized.
