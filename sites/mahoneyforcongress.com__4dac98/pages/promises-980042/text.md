Platform commitments 5 Pre-election, every commitment is "Committed." After the campaign begins legislating, we'll update each one — including the ones we couldn't keep, and why.
Filter All (5) End the Benefits Cliff Housing Is Economic Infrastructure Connect Every Neighborhood to Opportunity Strengthen Families and Community Stability Build Generational Wealth in Philadelphia 01 End the Benefits Cliff End the benefits cliff for working families.
Phase benefits out gradually as income rises, so a raise never costs a family its healthcare, childcare, or food assistance.
Read the pillar → Committed 02 Housing Is Economic Infrastructure Treat housing as the workforce infrastructure it is.
Expand affordable and attainable housing, preserve naturally affordable neighborhoods, support first-time homeownership, and strengthen anti-displacement strategies.
Read the pillar → Committed 03 Connect Every Neighborhood to Opportunity Connect every neighborhood to opportunity.
Expand transit connectivity, broadband affordability, workforce training and apprenticeships, and neighborhood opportunity hubs.
Read the pillar → Committed 04 Strengthen Families and Community Stability Strengthen families and community stability.
Restore and expand the Child Tax Credit.
Protect food assistance and school meals.
Increase childcare affordability and expand community-based healthcare access.
Read the pillar → Committed 05 Build Generational Wealth in Philadelphia Build generational wealth in Philadelphia.
Tangled-title reform, home-repair access, pathways to homeownership, and investment in neighborhood small businesses and community institutions.
