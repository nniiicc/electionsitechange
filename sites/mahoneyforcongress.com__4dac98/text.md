01 Dennis Mahoney · On the trail '26 01 About Dennis A life built around service.
“You have one life to give.” Dennis Mahoney has spent his life convening businesses, nonprofits, faith communities, and civic leaders to solve complex problems and widen the door of opportunity for vulnerable communities — overseas, across the United States, and in his own backyard of Philadelphia.
Wherever he serves, the throughline is the same: show up, build the coalition, deliver real results.
His career in disaster relief and long-term development shaped a single mindset — real results for hurting people.
In Philadelphia, Dennis builds cross-sector coalitions that keep kids out of the foster care system.
He brings neighborhood food markets to food deserts.
He works with the school district to surround students, teachers, and principals with community support.
Dennis believes that we all have a role to play in serving our city.
Locking arms together, Philadelphia's best days are ahead.
02 02 Why I'm Running A government that works like real life.
I am running because I believe Philadelphia can become the greatest large city in America to raise a family, build a business, and pursue opportunity.
Right now, too many people feel trapped between survival and stability — working hard and still struggling with rising housing costs, healthcare costs, transportation barriers, food insecurity, and systems that punish them for trying to move upward.
Our policies are too often designed in silos, while people experience life as one connected system.
If a family cannot afford housing near their job, cannot access reliable transportation, cannot keep healthcare while earning more, or cannot afford childcare while pursuing advancement — we have not built real economic mobility.
I am running to help build a government that rewards progress instead of punishing it.
A government that sees housing as workforce infrastructure, family stability as economic policy, and wealth-building as essential to neighborhood flourishing.
Leadership rooted in service.
Not performance.
Philadelphia does not need more political theater.
It needs people willing to bring others together around practical solutions and a long-term vision — a city where families can afford to stay, young people can build futures, neighborhoods flourish, and institutions earn back the trust of the people they exist to serve.
The work ahead is bigger than any one politician or party.
But together, we can build a flourishing Philadelphia.
03 03 Our Vision A Flourishing Philadelphia A bold vision · the next 40 years Service Opportunity Community Belonging The year is 2066.
Forty years ago, Philadelphia made a decision.
That decline was not inevitable.
That fear was not destiny.
That generational poverty could be disrupted.
That neighborhoods could flourish again.
We decided to serve our city together.
Investment in early childhood education, literacy, workforce development, the skilled trades, entrepreneurship, and AI transformed an entire generation.
Children who once lacked opportunity became scientists, teachers, entrepreneurs, builders, artists, engineers, and public servants.
Global business invested here because the workforce was talented, resilient, and diverse.
Families stayed because they believed their children could build meaningful lives here.
Population growth returned.
Neighborhood corridors came alive again.
Parks filled with families.
Main streets filled with local businesses.
Art and culture flourished.
Schools improved.
Thoughtful housing let longtime neighbors remain in the blocks they had called home for generations — and welcomed new ones in.
The benefits cliff was eliminated, and work led somewhere again.
Philadelphia became known not only as the birthplace of American democracy, but as the model for urban flourishing in the twenty-first century — the best large city to raise a family, to build a business, to belong to a community.
A city large enough for ambition.
Small enough that neighbors still knew neighbors.
Not through outrage.
Not through cynicism.
Through service — a quiet revolution of courage, kindness, and shared responsibility.
That future begins now.
It starts with you.
It starts with me.
It continues with us.
