Rural Energy Saving Program
It took some time, but Jim Clyburn’s Rural Energy Savings Program (RESP) became the law of the land in 2014.
RESP allows the nation’s Rural Electric Cooperatives to make loans to their members to increase the energy efficiency of their homes.
