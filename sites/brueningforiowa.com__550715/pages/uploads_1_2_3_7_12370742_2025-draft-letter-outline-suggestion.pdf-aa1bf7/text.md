I am writing to you to express my concerns about Pattison Sand Company’s permit request to dramatically increase water withdrawals and increase sources of water at its sand mining operation in near Clayton.
Topics you may want to include: 1) No explanations or questions were answered by either the DNR or Kyle Pattison at the public hearing April 22 regarding the permit modification request.
2) There was no prior testing or consultation with personal well owners or municipal wells who could see effects of increasing water withdrawals if allowed of up to 3.7 billion gallons of water per year.
3) There has been no study done to determine the impact of such an increase of water withdrawals to agriculture, tourism, water quality and quantity, and Mississippi River activities.
4) There was a lack of transparency in publishing necessary information about this request in Clayton County, only published in Calmar’s newspaper in Winneshiek Co.
5) The lack of response by the DNR to address existing concerns regarding problems with wells in the area near the mine.
6) The lack of follow-through on past statements by Kyle Pattison.
7) The lack of information about the required Reclamation fund.
8) The lack of clarification in permit language to address the repeated attempts by Pattison Sand to sell water to Western States.
9) The lack of discussion or knowledge about increased demands on water for new technology (AI), a carbon pipeline, new energy needs, and agriculture.
10) The lack of any DNR protections from sources of contamination to our water supply.
11) The lack of acknowledgement or concern over recent or prior depletion of the Jordan aquifer to replenish water levels, or the potential effects of a drought.
The permit, if approved, is for 5 years.
12) The lack of any explanation concerning responsibility for well interference expenses incurred by private well-owners. *The permit, if approved, is for 5 years.
