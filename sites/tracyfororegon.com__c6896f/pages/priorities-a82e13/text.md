Tracy Cramer: Fighting for Working Families
Working families in Marion County deserve bold, proven leadership.
From her roots in Gervais to building a business from the ground up to serving in the Oregon State Legislature, Tracy has always put her community before politics.
She’s focused on real solutions—not political games—to make life more affordable, safe, and full of opportunity for everyone in Senate District 11.
✓ Affordability & Tax Relief
Too many Oregon families are struggling to make ends meet, and state policies are making it worse.
Tracy believes the government should have to live within its means like families do.
That’s why Tracy opposed Tina Kotek’s $4.3 billion transportation tax and Virginia Stapleton’s costly payroll tax — both of which would have placed another burden on hardworking families.
As State Representative, Tracy supported tax cuts for middle-class families and fought costly regulations that drive up expenses.
Tracy will keep standing up to out-of-touch lawmakers who think the answer to every problem is making you pay more.
✓ Protecting Our Land & Communities
Tracy believes our land is our future.
That’s why she’s committed to protecting it from big corporations buying up farmland for massive data centers—threatening our water supply and driving up utility costs for our communities.
As your state senator, Tracy will introduce legislation to protect the right of communities like Woodburn, Keizer, Salem, Brooks, and Gervais to stop data centers from taking over our land and neighborhoods.
✓ Housing & Homelessness
Oregonians deserve safe, affordable places to live, and Tracy Cramer has a record of getting results on housing.
During her time as State Representative, Tracy voted for a historic bipartisan housing package that is expanding the housing supply, supporting agricultural workers, and helping homeless Oregonians transition into permanent housing.
Tracy also introduced a bill to turn shovel-ready land into homes working families can afford—incentivizing private investment and accountability instead of creating another government housing program.
While politicians keep spending taxpayer money without delivering results in places like Salem, Tracy is fighting for accountability and solutions that actually work.
✓ Education & Accountability
Too many parents feel their kids are falling through the cracks, even as education spending rises.
Tracy believes every child deserves a quality education, regardless of ZIP code or background.
That means funding schools responsibly while demanding accountability.
As State Representative, she fought to fully fund K-12 schools, expand school choice, and hold unelected bureaucrats accountable when they lowered graduation standards.
Tracy will keep pushing for schools to excel at teaching the basics, partner with parents, and prepare every child for success.
✓ Community Safety & Drug Reform
Every Oregonian deserves to feel safe in their home and neighborhood.
Tracy has taken action to address the drug and mental health crisis affecting too many Oregon communities.
She supported legislation to re-criminalize dangerous drugs while expanding access to treatment and mental health services.
Tracy believes in compassion, but also consequences.
That means giving law enforcement the tools and resources to keep us safe, and demanding accountability to taxpayers for the billions spent on programs that haven't delivered satisfactory results for the people they're meant to help.
