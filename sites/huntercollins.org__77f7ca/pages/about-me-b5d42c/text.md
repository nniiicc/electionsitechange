Skip to content Hunter Collins Menu Home Meet Hunter Get Involved Donate Meet Hunter A family man with a passion for helping others I am twenty-eight (28) years old and I’m proud to call southeast Indiana home.
I live in Aurora with my lovely wife Haylee and our beautiful daughter Johanna.
My family is my world, and I don’t know what I would do without them.
Giving back to my community has always been a central part of who I am.
My mother taught me early on that giving is better than receiving.
As a child, we would cook and deliver meals to elderly and infirm members of our church.
These experiences planted a lifelong desire to serve others.
As an adult, that passion led me to become deeply involved in organized labor.
Every day, I stand up to one of the world’s largest corporations, fighting for fair pay, better benefits, and safer working conditions — so that my fellow workers can have a real shot at the American Dream.
Mission & Vision Progress for the people, Not the powerful Hoosiers deserve better than political theater and bending the knee to big money interests.
I’m running for State Representative to bring real progress to District 68.
To be a voice for hard working families, and to focus on the issues that actually matter.
Things like strong local schools, addiction recovery and accessible healthcare, legalizing cannabis, and making your dollar go further in Indiana.
Mission Our mission is to restore trust in public leadership by fighting for fully funded public schools, affordable healthcare, economic dignity, modern infrastructure, and fair taxation — while rejecting political theater and standing firmly with the people over special interests.
We are building a movement powered by community, compassion, and common sense.
Vision We envision a southeast Indiana where every family has access to strong public schools, quality healthcare, economic opportunity, and safe communities.
A district where working people, not wealthy elites, shape our future.
Where infrastructure meets the needs of our towns, jobs pay a fair wage, and leaders are accountable to the people they serve.
Together, we will build a state where hard work is respected, families can thrive, and everyday Hoosiers come first.
Focusing on the issues that actually matter to everyday Hoosiers Hoosier Homes First - Addressing Indiana's Housing Crisis The Issue: Hoosiers are being priced out of their own communities.
Rents are rising faster than wages, large corporate landlords are snatching up homes, and hard-working families continue to live paycheck-to-paycheck just to keep a roof over their heads.
Whether you're in a small town or big city, housing is becoming more difficult to afford and harder to trust.
Families are living in run down, unsafe conditions, seniors are being pushed out of lifelong homes, and renters have little power to do anything.
Every Hoosier should have a safe, stable, and affordable place to live in their community.
Hunt er’s Plan: Phase out large corporate landlords over 5 years.
Hoosier homes should be for Hoosiers, not corporate greed Create a Tenant's Bill of Rights including "Just Cause" eviction protections, right to representation for eviction hearings, and creating a State Housing Ombudsman to investigate slumlords and ensure accountability Launch the Hoosier Homes Trust to build 100,000 affordable residences over 10 years.
Accomplishing this by creating a Public Housing Authority to build and maintain public, not for profit housing that is permanently affordable.
With rents capped at 30% of tenant's income.
Incentivize development of starter homes at a reasonable price, specifically in rural and small town communities Repeal Indiana’s outdated “First Step” program and replace it with Welcome Home Indiana , designed for first-time buyers and community heroes (veterans, firefighters, teachers, and active-duty military).
Offer 7% Down Payment Assistance (DPA) —usable for down payments, closing costs, or inspection-related repairs —forgiven after 10 years with no interest .
(Only applicable to primary residence) Strong Public Schools, Stronger Communities The Issue: Rural schools are underfunded, teachers are unsupported, and students lack equal opportunities.
Hunter’s Plan: Increase funding for rural public schools Fully fund special education Expand vocational training and community college access Oppose school voucher expansions that drain public resources Universal Pre-K and Affordable Childcare The Issue: Too many Hoosier kids are falling behind in reading and math, especially in rural and lower income areas where access to early education is limited.
At the same time, working families struggle to find affordable, quality childcare for younger kids.
Every parent deserves the opportunity to work and/or study without being overly burdened by childcare costs.
Likewise, every child deserves access to high quality early education.
Regardless of their household income or zip code.
Hunt er’s Plan: Fight for Universal Pre-K for every 4 year old in the state.
Giving them the head start they need to succeed Expand local childcare options for parents throughout the state Allow working parents with children under 4 years old to receive subsidies for childcare based on a sliding-scale Invest in expanding quality childcare in rural areas Affordable Healthcare & Addiction Recovery The Issue: Southeast Indiana faces an opioid crisis, and too many families lack access to basic or mental health care.
Hunter’s Plan: Cancel Hoosier's medical debt and bar hospitals operating in Indiana from reporting medical debt to credit bureaus or selling your debt to debt buyers Expand Medicaid eligibility & fight for a public healthcare option Fund local health clinics and addiction treatment programs Protect reproductive rights and keep medical decisions between patients and doctors Working Families First The Issue: Wages lag behind the cost of living, farms are struggling, and tax cuts benefit the wealthy over working families.
Hunter’s Plan: Raise the minimum wage to $15/hr Pass a refundable State Child Tax Credit Expand trade schools and apprentice programs Strengthen workers’ rights and unions by repealing right to work laws and banning captive audience meetings Ensure corporations and the wealthy pay their fair share Infrastructure That Works for Us The Issue: Roads, bridges, and utility systems in District 68 are failing from neglect.
Hunter’s Plan: Fund critical infrastructure upgrades Require cost-efficiency reviews for major projects Use state and federal grants to create local jobs and speed up development Ending Cannabis Prohibition The Issue: Indiana is surrounded by states with legal cannabis, losing revenue and wasting resources on outdated enforcement.
Hunter’s Plan: Legalize cannabis for medical and recreational use (21+) Tax sales at 8% and allow limited home cultivation with medical approval Expunge records for non-violent cannabis possession Lowering Taxes for Working-Class Hoosiers The Issue: High gas and vehicle taxes unfairly burden rural families and small businesses.
Hunt er’s Plan: Cap vehicle excise taxes Exempt one vehicle from the excise tax for working families Lower the state gas tax by 10¢ per gallon Finding Common Sense Solutions for All Hoosiers The Issue: Hoosiers feel like government isn't working for them.
To be fair, it isn't.
Our Statehouse is filled with political theater, big money interest, and deep partisan divide.
Hunter’s Plan: Find the common ground among us, and use common sense to get work done.
Refuse to engage in the political theater Work with both sides of the aisle to find solutions that work for the people of southeast Indiana without compromising our values.
