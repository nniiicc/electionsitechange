Fighting for the People, Not the Powerful
Hunter Collins
for State Representative
Hunter Collins is a proud resident of southeast Indiana – a fighter for workers, the son of a hardworking single mother, a husband, and a father to a beautiful girl named Johanna.
As a workplace organizer and community leader, Hunter has spent years fighting for better wages, stronger schools, and access to healthcare for working families.
Hunter isn’t a career politician — he’s your neighbor, a parent, and someone who believes southeast Indiana deserves better than partisan divide and political theater.
He’s running for State Representative because the people of District 68 deserve a voice who will actually listen and fight for them at the Statehouse.
