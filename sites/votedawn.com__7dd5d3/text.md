Meet Dawn Dawn White is a lifelong conservative Republican who has always believed in the values of faith, family, and community.
Dawn began her professional life as a kindergarten teacher in the Murfreesboro City School System at Black Fox Elementary, where she taught for seven years.
Outside the classroom, Dawn dedicated herself to serving the community she loves.
She became actively involved with organizations that tackle some of the greatest needs in Rutherford County, from helping families in crisis to supporting local charities and civic efforts.
Dawn White’s story is one of service, perseverance, and an unshakable dedication to the conservative principles that make Tennessee strong.
Read Dawn's Story Dawn's Priorities Dawn’s priorities reflect her unwavering commitment to conservative leadership and the values that keep Tennessee strong.
She proudly supports President Donald J.
Trump and the America First movement, believing Tennessee must stand with leaders who defend our freedoms, protect our communities, and put working families first.
Dawn is focused on continuing that mission by advancing policies that strengthen our state and safeguard the principles she has championed throughout her career.
A secure Tennessee begins with strong borders and respect for the rule of law.
Dawn has made fighting illegal immigration one of her top priorities, from casting the deciding vote against taxpayer-funded tuition for illegal immigrants to stopping Nashville’s sanctuary city proposal and sponsoring tougher laws that hold local officials accountable.
She is equally committed to strengthening education.
As a former teacher, Dawn prioritizes giving parents more choice in their children’s schooling, supporting great teachers, and ensuring Tennessee classrooms reflect the values of the families they serve.
Her priorities are clear: protect our communities, empower parents, stand with conservative leadership, and keep Tennessee moving forward.
