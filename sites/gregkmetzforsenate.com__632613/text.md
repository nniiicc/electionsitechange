Bullets, Babies & Borders
It isn’t just a slogan, it’s my promise to defend your rights, protect the innocent, and keep Montana strong.
ABOUT GREG KMETZ
- Bullets Folks in Montana know the value of guns and the responsibility that comes with it.
I’ve fought to keep those rights strong while I served in the House, and I aim to keep fighting to protect these rights in the Senate.
- Babies Some people talk about rights and forget the smallest ones among us.
Not me.
You measure a man by how he defends the helpless.
I’ve already stood for life in the Montana House, and I’ll keep that fight in the State Senate.
- Borders A nation without borders is like a ranch without a fence, wide open to danger.
Montana may sit far from the southern line, but the drugs and crime spilling through reach our small towns all the same.
That’s why I’ll fight in the Senate for strong borders, tough enforcement, and the safety of Montana families.
