ISSUES
Universal Healthcare is our Top Priority
Families are struggling and affordable, accessible healthcare is at the top of the list.
My priorities for House District 26 are to:
- Universal Healthcare: Pass universal healthcare that removes the health insurance industry, is run by the government, provides freedom of choice of doctor, and improves access to healthcare.
- Public Schools: Hiring and retaining the very best educators for early education, K-12, and higher education public schools.
- Cost of Living: Standing up to price gougers who are increasing the cost of everyday goods and services, from gas to housing to groceries to healthcare and prescription drugs.
- Immigration: Protect New Mexico's immigrant communities from ICE and federal immigration overreach.
