Democrat Eleanor Chavez: Fighting for Westside Families
I raised my four children in Westgate and have lived on the Westside since 1985.
I was a union organizer for over 40 years, organizing workers to improve their working conditions, benefits, and pay.
I have had the honor of representing Westside families in the State Legislature for eight years.
As I go door-to-door, I know that families continue to struggle with crime, healthcare costs and access, and the cost of living.
I hope you will join my campaign to bring real progress to Westside families.
If you have an idea to improve our community, I’d love to hear it.
You can always reach me on my cell at 505-401-4732 or by email at eleanorchavez4rep26@gmail.com.
Let’s get to work!
Eleanor
