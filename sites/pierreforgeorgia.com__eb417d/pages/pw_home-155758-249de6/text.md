Born in Newnan and raised in public housing by his single mother and grandparents, Pierre Whatley understands firsthand the challenges facing working families.
A proud product of the Coweta County School System, he has dedicated his career to public service, economic opportunity, and serving communities like the one that raised him.
Today, Pierre is running for the Georgia House because he believes District 70 deserves leadership that shows up, listens, and delivers.
