Leon Stavrinakis Files for Reelection
Mar 31, 2016
"It has been an incredible honor to represent District 119 in the S.C.
House of Representatives.
Last week, I filed for another term to continue my work fighting for traffic relief, better roads and schools, and more economic opportunity for every Charlestonian.
While I do not have primary opposition, I will have opposition in November.
So today I ask for your continued prayers and support as my family begins another campaign.
Please visit my new website, www.leonforhouse.com, and sign up for campaign updates and consider donating to my campaign if you are able.
Thank you for always having my back and I look forward to seeing you on the campaign trail."
-Leon
