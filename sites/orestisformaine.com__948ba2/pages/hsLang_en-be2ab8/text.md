About Alex
Alex Orestis is a longtime Eliot resident and former Chair of the Eliot Select Board, where he worked on budgets, infrastructure, and local services that directly affect families and seniors.
He’s running for the Maine House to bring practical, community-focused leadership to Augusta and ensure state decisions work for the people they impact.
My Priorities for District 150
Protect healthcare and seniors
Medicaid supports most nursing home beds in Maine.
I will fight to protect healthcare access for seniors and help communities prepare for federal funding changes.
Affordability and property taxes
Families are feeling the pressure from rising property taxes and the cost of living.
I will focus on practical solutions that keep Maine affordable without shifting the burden onto working families.
Strong local communities
Strong towns depend on steady leadership and smart planning.
I bring local government experience focused on problem-solving and long-term community investment.
