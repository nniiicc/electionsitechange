Fred Deutsch: Promises Made & Kept
Talk is cheap—results matter.
Here's what I've done to deliver for District 4, and the key votes I took that proves I’ve kept my word.
💰 Lowered the Tax Burden
I fought to keep more of your hard-earned money where it belongs—with families, seniors and small businesses.
- Voted for balanced budgets every year.1
- Backed South Dakota's largest sales-tax cut in history.2
- Voted to reduce school levies, directly easing property tax pressure on homeowners.3
🌾Protecting Farmers, Land and Property Rights
When big corporations and outside interests tried to push South Dakotans around, I stood with landowners.
- Stood against eminent domain abuse and fought to protect landowners from CO₂ pipeline overreach.4
- Voted to strengthen protections against foreign adversaries owning SD farmland.5
- Supported truth-in-meat labeling to protect ranchers and consumers.6
🎓Safe Strong Schools & Safe Communities
I have been a statewide leader in protecting kids, supporting parents, and giving law enforcement the tools to uphold public safety.
- Sponsored tougher penalties for child predators.7
- Made it a felony to intentionally disarm a law enforcement officer.8
- Passed laws to safeguard minors from medicalized gender interventions.9
- Strengthened penalties for eluding law enforcement, including dangerous vehicle pursuits.10
- Increased South Dakota Opportunity Scholarships to encourage high-achieving students to remain in SD for college/workforce and reward rigorous high school preparation.11
- 🚑Supporting Rural Healthcare
I led and backed efforts to expand rural access and modernize care.
Rural healthcare access close to home matters (people shouldn't have to drive hours to see their doctor).
- Sponsored reforms protecting rural healthcare workers from unfair non-compete contracts - to allow more to stay in our towns.12
- Gave families the right to electronic monitoring of loved ones in nursing homes and assisted living facilities during COVID to ensure access to care.13
- Supported appropriations to keep nursing homes open and staffed in rural South Dakota.
SB 155 (2022) — Long-term care provider aid; SB 59 (2022) and SB 52 (2024)—Appropriations including rural nursing home stabilization.14
- Funded Rural family medicine residency and workforce incentives to recruit doctors and nurses to underserved communities.15
🛡️Defending Life, Family, and Freedom
Unwavering conservative values rooted in faith and the Constitution.
My record is clear: I don't bend when it comes to protecting life, families, parental rights , and individual freedoms.
- 100% supporter of pro-life protections.
Prime Sponsor of the Born Alive Infant Protection Act.16
- Sponsored bills to protect children from harmful medical procedures.17
- Expanded Second Amendment freedoms.18
- Voted to strengthen constitutional integrity in elections.19
