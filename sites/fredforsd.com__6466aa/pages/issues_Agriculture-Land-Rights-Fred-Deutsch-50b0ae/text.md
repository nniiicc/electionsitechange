High input costs, fluctuating crop and livestock prices, and rising interest rates make it harder to stay profitable.
Droughts, floods, and harsh weather strain production and threaten rural stability.
At the same time, out-of-state corporations push eminent domain claims that undermine property rights and local control.
Add in labor shortages, aging infrastructure, limited market access, and rising property taxes, and family farms are being squeezed from every direction. 🛠️Fred’s Plan Keep family farms strong by making property tax rules fair, predictable, and tied to actual ag use.
Invest in ag research and innovation .
Guarantee local control in land-use decisions.
Stop eminent domain abuse —no private pipeline or corporation should seize South Dakota land against a family’s will. ✅Progress So Far Advocated for HB 1052 to prohibit CO₂ pipeline companies from using eminent domain against landowners.
Voted against SB 201 because it stripped counties of local control and opened the door for private companies to use eminent domain against South Dakota landowners.
Co-sponsored HB 1039 , improving fair assessment of ag land as non-cropland where appropriate.
Co-sponsored HB 1069 , banning foreign governments from purchasing South Dakota farmland. → See more on Promises Made & Kept . 💡Why This Matters Agriculture isn’t just South Dakota’s leading industry—it’s our identity, our economy, and our way of life.
When farmers and ranchers can’t survive, rural communities decline.
