Meet Fred Promises Kept Issues Photos Volunteer Vote Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ 🚑Rural Healthcare 🚑Rural Healthcare 🚨 The Problem In rural South Dakota, no family should have to drive hours for care, but that’s the reality in many areas.
Our hospitals face thin margins, heavy reliance on Medicare and Medicaid, and difficulty recruiting staff.
Fixed costs like ER coverage and imaging equipment don’t shrink just because patient volume is small.
When services like obstetrics or behavioral health shut down, families are left to travel farther and hospitals lose critical lifelines. 🛠️Fred’s Plan Protect rural hospitals.
Targeted support for rural facilities that keep doors open.
Expand telehealth.
Strengthen broadband and keep reimbursement fair so distance isn’t a barrier.
Support licensure reciprocity and cross-state telehealth partnerships so South Dakotans can see top specialists without leaving the state — and expand “e-consults” so rural clinics can coordinate care locally, reducing long drives for patients.
Grow the workforce.
Expand scholarships and loan repayment for doctors, nurses, and mental-health providers who commit to rural service.
U pgrade infrastructure.
Provide small grants so hospitals can modernize ERs, labs, and imaging without risky debt.
Utilize the federal Rural Health Transformation Program to secure new federal funding for South Dakota’s hospitals, mental health, and telehealth infrastructure. ✅Progress So Far I’ve worked to strengthen care close to home.
I supported expanding telehealth access, making it easier for rural families to see specialists without leaving town.
I backed scholarships and loan repayment programs for healthcare providers who commit to serving rural South Dakota.
I voted to protect funding for Critical Access Hospitals.
I also supported measures to expand access to life-saving medications like Narcan, giving first responders and families tools to fight overdoses. → See the full record on Promises Made & Kept 💡Why This Matters Healthcare isn’t just about treatment — it’s about dignity, security, and staying rooted in your community.
When rural families can get care close to home, when seniors can age in place, and when kids can see a doctor without hours of travel, our towns thrive.
