TO REGISTER TO VOTE:
If you are not yet registered to vote, the process is simple.
First, you need to print the Voter Registration Form from the South Dakota Secretary of State site.
You can use the link below.
Then fill out the form, sign it, and then submit it to your County Auditor.
ABSENTEE VOTING
Absentee voting opens September 18, 2026.
Download and print an Absentee Ballot Application (PDF) from the South Dakota Secretary of State's site and mail your original, signed application to your County Election Official.
If you would like more information, you can go to the SD Secretary of State's site - you will need to scroll down a bit.
You can also call them at 605-773-3537 to answer any questions.
