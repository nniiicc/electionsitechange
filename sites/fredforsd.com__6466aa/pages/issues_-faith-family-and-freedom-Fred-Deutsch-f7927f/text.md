✝️ Faith, Family, and Freedom
🚨 The Problem
South Dakota’s way of life is built on faith, strong families, and freedom.
Yet these values are under constant pressure — from Washington bureaucrats, activist judges, and cultural trends that push politics ahead of principle.
Too often, leaders bend to political convenience instead of standing firm on what matters most.
🛠️Fred’s Plan
- Defend religious liberty for all.
- Fight to protect every South Dakota child.
- Strengthen families and parental rights in education, health care, and decision-making.
- Safeguard constitutional freedoms, including the Second Amendment.
- Defend medical freedom so patients and doctors—not bureaucrats—make their own health decisions.
- Stand firm against federal overreach and outside agendas that threaten South Dakota values.
✅Progress So Far
- Sponsored legislation requiring doctors to provide lifesaving care to infants who survive abortion attempts.
- Consistently voted to protect the Second Amendment and defend law-abiding gun owners.
- Supported measures to reinforce parental rights and school transparency.
- Supported medical freedom legislation - the right of individuals to make autonomous decisions about their own healthcare, free from government coercion or interference.
Authored and Prime Sponsored HB 1008, to give employees a cause of action if an employer requires vaccination as a condition of employment; voted NO on SB28 to expand required immunizations for school entry; and was a co-sponsor of HB1080 to authorize terminally ill patients the medical freedom to access investigational treatments.

💡Why This Matters
Strong families and a strong South Dakota depend on the freedom for people to live according to their values.
That means protecting the right to worship, raise children, build a business, and make personal decisions without unnecessary government interference.
These aren’t abstract ideals—they’re the foundation of healthy communities and a healthy state.
When we protect these core freedoms together—faith, family, personal responsibility, and the ability to guide our own lives—we’re not just defending values; we're safeguarding our way of life for the next generation.
