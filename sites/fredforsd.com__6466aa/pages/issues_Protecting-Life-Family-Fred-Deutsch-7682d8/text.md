Even though South Dakota is one of the most pro-life states in the country, the unborn continue to face threats.
Abortion pills enter our state through mail-order providers in states with “shield laws.” These laws allow out-of-state providers to ship pills into our state, bypassing our state laws and local protections, and putting vulnerable mothers and unborn children at risk. 🛠️ Fred’s Plan Protect the unborn at every stage.
Strengthen laws against mail-order abortion pills.
Support mothers with resources and care. ️ Oppose bills that strip women of legal protection and expose them to prosecution for their own abortions. ✅ Progress So Far Sponsored legislation requiring doctors to provide care for infants who survive abortion attempts.
100% pro-life voting record.
Supported funding for programs that assist mothers, children, and families in need. → See the full record on Promises Made & Kept . 💡 Why It Matters Protecting life is about protecting the most vulnerable among us.
When we safeguard the unborn and support mothers, we uphold human dignity and ensure South Dakota remains a place where every child is welcomed and valued.
Closing abortion pill loopholes is essential to keeping our state truly pro-life.
Protecting life also means protecting women, and it means being honest about what actually works.
The most effective thing South Dakota can do to end abortion is cut off the supply chain of mail-order abortion pills flowing into our state.
Threatening women with life in prison, or worse, doesn't abolish abortion.
It drives the practice underground and scares women away from crisis pregnancy centers, the very places built to help them choose life.
A law that's true to life holds abortionists and traffickers accountable and welcomes mothers in for care.
It doesn't put women in prison.
