🌾 Support Agriculture & Protect Property Rights
The Problem
Agriculture is South Dakota’s backbone, but farmers and ranchers face mounting challenges.
High input costs, fluctuating crop and livestock prices, and rising interest rates make it harder to stay profitable.
Droughts, floods, and harsh weather strain production and threaten rural stability.
At the same time, out-of-state corporations push eminent domain claims that undermine property rights and local control.
Add in labor shortages, aging infrastructure, limited market access, and rising property taxes, and family farms are being squeezed from every direction.
Fred’s Plan
- Stop eminent domain abuse—no private pipeline or corporation should seize South Dakota land against a family’s will.
- Strengthen local control so counties and communities, not outside corporations, decide what happens on their land.
- Keep family farms strong by making property tax rules fair, predictable, and tied to actual ag use.
- Support rural development with strong infrastructure, better market access, and rural broadband expansion.
- Respect stewardship by ensuring conservation programs prioritize flexibility for producers, not one-size-fits-all mandates.
Progress So Far
- Advocated for HB 1052 to prohibit CO₂ pipeline companies from using eminent domain against landowners.
- Voted against SB 201 because it stripped counties of local control and opened the door for private companies to use eminent domain against South Dakota landowners.
- Co-sponsored HB 1039, improving fair assessment of ag land as non-cropland where appropriate.
- Co-sponsored HB 1069, banning foreign governments from purchasing South Dakota farmland.

Why This Matters
Agriculture isn’t just South Dakota’s leading industry—it’s our identity, our economy, and our way of life.
When farmers and ranchers can’t survive, rural communities decline.
By defending landowners, protecting property rights, and creating fair conditions for agriculture, we preserve not just livelihoods but the future of South Dakota.
