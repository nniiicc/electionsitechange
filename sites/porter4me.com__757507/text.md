Meet The Candidate Bernard "Ben" Porter If you happen to see a human swimming in January off the coast of Orrs Island, that's likely Bernard "Ben" Porter.
Ben swims year-round, enjoys snow biking, and practices Brazilian jiu-jitsu.
He is a retired Army Ranger who served our nation for 25 years and earned his MBA at Johns Hopkins University.
After spending 10 years at the U.S.
Treasury Department working in Cybersecurity, Ben returned to his roots in Maine.
Above all, he is a proud father of four, committed to ensuring the next generation of Mainers inherits a state that is safe, affordable, and thriving.
