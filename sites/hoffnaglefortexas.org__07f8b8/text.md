Quick Actions Full platform RSVP Volunteer Yard sign Email Facebook TEXAS HOUSE DISTRICT 100 JORDAN HOFFNAGLE FOR STATE HOUSE Where I stand Seven things I’ll fight for first.
The full platform — including the three bills I’ve already drafted — is on the issues page .
Cap Property Taxes Lock your property tax to what you paid for your home, for as long as you own it.
No more hikes because an appraiser decided the neighborhood got expensive.
It covers rentals too — when the tax bill goes up, so does the rent.
50/50 Custody Equal custody as the default starting point for two fit parents, with enforcement that actually has teeth.
Kids’ sports and activities protected in the order.
No child support obligation for parents earning $50,000 or less.
Real Careers, Not Just a Test Give juniors and seniors credit for real work — 10 hours a week or more in a licensed trade, construction, networking, data cabling, or computer science.
A diploma and a paycheck, not a diploma and a loan.
Data Centers in Corridors, Not Neighborhoods Build them where they belong — in designated corridors, generating their own power and sourcing their own water.
Not next door to your house, and not on the back of your electric bill.
Body Cameras On, Flock Cameras Off One year of mandatory body camera retention, a real process for residents to get the footage, and penalties for switching a camera off.
And a ban on Flock cameras and warrantless mass tracking.
Cameras should point at the government, not at you.
You Bought It, You Own It No digital locks on what you already paid for.
