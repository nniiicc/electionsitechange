Meet Adam Waukesha County Winning Media Volunteer DONATE KEEP WAUKESHA COUNTY WINNING For our home, our family, our way of life.
Affordable Winning Means People Can Afford To Stay In Their Homes.
The tax bill should still fit the house you already own--no more sticker shock from the county.
A county executive cannot rewrite the school share or the city share, and I will not pretend otherwise.
What I can do is hold the county’s piece of the bill and fight to keep the fees Waukesha already collects from being sent to Madison.
A family that has lived on the same block for nineteen years should not be the one that has to sell, and Seniors on fixed incomes should not be worried the next tax bill is going to drive them out of their homes.
Protected Winning Means You're Safe Where You Sleep, Work, and Shop.
When you call, a deputy should come.
The jail should have room, and the case should get charged.
Waukesha is not a county in trouble.
It is a county that cannot afford to get cheap on the one service people notice the night something goes wrong.
That protection is a budget choice, and it should not arrive with a new tax.
Calling Waukesha Home Winning Means You Can Raise A Family, Start A Business, & Call Waukesha County Home.
A family should be able to get the kids through school and still recognize the tax bill when it shows up.
A person should be able to open a shop without a rule from an office nobody voted for standing in the way.
If something costs real money, the people you can fire ought to vote on it in public.
Maintaining Infrastructure Winning Means The Roads Are Maintained.
They should be plowed in January and patched before they turn into a repair bill.
They should also be built before a new subdivision is already full of people sitting in traffic.
A road that fails is a tax you pay at the shop.
I would rather spend the money to keep it up.
A Growing County Winning Means Growth Pays For Growth.
The next subdivision and the next store should cover the load they add, rather than landing on the bill of the house that is already built.
Growth that is free for the developer and expensive for the neighbor is not winning.
It is handing someone else the cost.
A Thriving County Winning Means Your Kids Can Thrive At Every Stage & Stay Here.
That is a park when they are small, a first job when they are old enough, and a starter house that is not a rumor.
I want the twenty-eight-year-old who grew up in Brookfield or Pewaukee to see a way to live here, instead of a county that was only a fine place to be from.
