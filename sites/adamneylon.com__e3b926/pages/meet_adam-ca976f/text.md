Meet Adam
Waukesha County is where Adam built his life.
Adam Neylon came to Waukesha County to attend Carroll University and never looked back.
After graduating from Carroll in 2008 with a degree in political science, Adam chose to build his future here.
He started his career, launched a small business, married his wife Hannah, and together they are raising their three children in the community they call home.
For Adam, Waukesha County isn't simply the place he represents.
It's where he built his life, raised his family, and learned firsthand what makes our communities special.
That is why he is running to serve as the next Waukesha County Executive.
From Small Business to Public Service.
Adam knows that strong communities depend on people willing to take risks, build something of their own, and create opportunity for others.
Adam understands firsthand what it takes to build a business from the ground up.
An entrepreneur since his twenties, he founded The Neylon Group, a commercial window cleaning company, and later became a co-founder and partner of Starting Point Fitness.
His experience as a small business owner has shaped his approach to government: watch every dollar, eliminate unnecessary barriers, and create an environment where businesses and families can succeed.
Before running for office, Adam also served the people of Waukesha County as a member of Congressman Jim Sensenbrenner's staff and later worked as a policy advisor in the Wisconsin State Assembly.
In 2013, when an unexpected vacancy created a special election for the State Assembly, Adam stepped forward to serve.
He won that election and has continued earning the trust of voters ever since.
Today, Adam represents Wisconsin's 15th Assembly District and has spent more than a decade working on the issues that directly affect families, businesses, and taxpayers across Waukesha County.
A Record of Getting Results.
Adam has built his legislative career around a straightforward principle: government should work for the people it serves.
Throughout his time in the Assembly, he has taken on leadership roles focused on jobs and the economy, energy and utilities, health care, regulatory reform, and government accountability.
He has worked to reduce unnecessary government regulation, strengthen oversight of unelected agencies, protect individual privacy, and create opportunities for Wisconsin businesses and students.
Adam authored Wisconsin's first-in-the-nation REINS Act, landmark legislation that strengthened legislative oversight of unelected regulators and required legislative approval of administrative rules carrying $10 million or more in implementation and compliance costs.
He also authored legislation protecting Wisconsinites from having GPS tracking devices placed on their vehicles without their consent.
Adam helped establish grants supporting student participation in robotics programs and worked to give parents easier access to information about the performance of their children's schools.
As Chairman and now Co-Chair of the Assembly Committee for Review of Administrative Rules, Adam has continued working to ensure government agencies remain accountable to the people and their elected representatives.
For Adam, conservative government isn't simply about saying no.
It's about making government more accountable, more efficient, and more responsive to the people paying for it.
Ready to Lead Waukesha County.
Adam believes Waukesha County has succeeded because generations of residents and leaders made responsible decisions, protected taxpayers, encouraged economic opportunity, and built communities where people want to raise their families.
The next County Executive should protect that foundation while preparing Waukesha County for what comes next.
Adam is running to bring the same focus on fiscal responsibility, innovation, accountability, and individual liberty that has guided his work in the State Assembly to county government.
That means holding the line on spending and taxes.
Finding innovative ways to generate growth and make better use of county assets.
Investing responsibly in infrastructure.
Expanding opportunities for the next generation to afford a home here.
And protecting the privacy and individual freedoms of Waukesha County residents.
Adam isn't running to change what makes Waukesha County successful.
He's running to build on it.
More Than Politics.
When Adam isn't working, you'll find him spending time with Hannah and their three children.
A former Carroll University baseball player, Adam remains connected to the university and the community that gave him the opportunity to build his life here.
His faith, family, experience as a small business owner, and years of public service have shaped how he approaches leadership: listen first, be transparent, make responsible decisions, and never forget who government works for.
Waukesha County gave Adam the opportunity to build a future here.
Now he wants to make sure the next generation has that same opportunity.
