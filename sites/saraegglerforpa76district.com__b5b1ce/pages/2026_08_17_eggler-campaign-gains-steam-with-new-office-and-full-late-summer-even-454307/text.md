Skip to content Home About Press News Events Volunteer Register to Vote Donate News , Uncategorized Eggler Campaign Gains Steam with New Office and Full Late‑Summer Event Lineup August 17, 2026 LOCK HAVEN, PA — Sara Eggler, the non-partisan candidate for the 76th State House District, covering Clinton County & the majority of Union County is closing out a lively summer of community outreach with several campaign milestones on the calendar, including the opening of a new Clinton County Democratic headquarters and a run of public gatherings.
Four dates headline the campaign’s late-August plans: August 25: Campaign Office Official Opening Date, The Clinton County Democratic Party will open its new headquarters in the Save-A-Lot plaza at 120 E.
Walnut St. in Lock Haven.
The office is intended to be a steady home base for conversations with neighbors and community members.
“Having a physical office space is about much more than having doors and a computer,” Eggler said.
“It is a place where people can come together, build connections, and engage in the open dialogues our district deserves.” August 23 (12:00 PM – 2:00 PM): Free Hot Dog Lunch, Eggler and her team will hold a meet-and-greet at Triangle Park, with a free grilled hot dog lunch, for anyone who stops by.
Residents across the area are invited & encouraged to attend.
August 25 (5:00 PM – 8:00 PM): “Fund the Future!” Benefit Concert, The fundraiser will take place at the newly opened Haven Exchange, 104 E.
Main St., Lock Haven, and will include food, drinks, plus live music from local favorite The Echo & Sway.
Attendance is capped at 100 people on a first-come, first-served basis, and any remaining tickets will be available at the door.
Visit the event page or the campaign’s facebook page to secure your tickets, today!
Over the summer, Eggler has been showing up at local meetings, volunteering, and spending time listening to concerns raised by residents throughout the 76th District.
Opening the new office is also a meaningful step up in campaign operations as fall approaches.
Event details and volunteer information are available on the campaign’s events page: Website: https://saraegglerforpa76district.com/events/ .
