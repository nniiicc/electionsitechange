Vegas Chamber Announces Primary Endorsements for Nevada Legislature
The Vegas Chamber is releasing its initial Primary Election endorsements for candidates running for the Nevada Legislature.
Candidate endorsements are determined by the Vegas Chamber’s Government Affairs Committee, a 29‑member body representing a broad range of industries and employers.
For the 2026 Primary Election endorsements, Committee members interviewed more than 100 candidates.
The endorsements are based on each candidate’s policy positions, knowledge of business issues, voting record on the Chamber’s priority bills (if incumbents), understanding of issues impacting employers and the regional economy, as well as their willingness to work with the Vegas Chamber and the business community on a variety of issues.
These endorsement recommendations were approved through a formal vote of the Committee at its April meeting.
