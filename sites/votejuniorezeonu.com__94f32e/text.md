WHO IS JUNIOR?
Junior Ezeonu is of Nigerian descent and was born in Onitsha, Nigeria and moved to the state of Texas at the age of 2 years old.
Junior attended Bryant Elementary School, Barnett Junior High School and Bowie High School.
After graduation, he attended the University of Texas at Arlington where he graduated Summa Cum Laude, with a Bachelor of Arts degree in Political Science.
In June, 2021, at the age of twenty-two, he was elected to the Grand Prairie City Council after a run-off in which he defeated a twelve-year incumbent.
He became the council's youngest-ever elected member, as well as one of the only African-American members since the early 1990s.
In June, 2025, at age twenty-six, he became the youngest Mayor Pro Tem in the city's history.
While on the city council, Junior has helped lower the Grand Prairie property tax rate, raise the minimum wage for city employees to $15/hr for part time and $17.82/hr for full time employees, create an affordable transportation program for city residents, fund affordable housing programs, and address climate change.
He is running to bringing a fresh perspective and innovative solutions to tackle the challenges facing Texas House District 101.
This moment is calling for strong and bold leadership, and Junior is ready to stand at the front lines
for the community that made him the man he is today.
