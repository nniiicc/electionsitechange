Prescription Drug Prices : Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) are the middleman between pharmacies and drug manufacturers.
They are supposed to keep our drug prices low but through various practices, they have become their own money-making enterprise.
I will work for transparency in pricing and billing practices for our medicine.

Even the most fervent abortion opponent often believes in exceptions for the life of the mother.
What level of danger to the mother do we draw the line?
Who makes that decision?
I believe that it should be the mother.
The right to privacy, though not explicitly stated in our Bill of Rights, lives in many amendments within our country’s founding documents.
The legislature should not be looking over your shoulder in the doctor’s office.
