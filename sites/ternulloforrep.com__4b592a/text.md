STONEHAM, MA — Stephen G.
Ternullo, Republican candidate for State Representative in the 31st Middlesex District representing Stoneham and Winchester, announced today that he has received the endorsement of the National Federation of Independent Business Massachusetts PAC (NFIB MA PAC).
NFIB is the nation’s leading small-business advocacy association, representing small and independent businesses and advocating on issues affecting their ability to own, operate, and grow their businesses.
“I’m honored to receive the endorsement of the NFIB Massachusetts PAC,” said Ternullo.
“For more than 38 years, I’ve worked in the building trades.
I know what it means to work hard, earn a paycheck, support a family, and see firsthand how rising taxes, energy bills, and everyday costs affect the people who live and work in our communities.”
In its endorsement letter, NFIB MA PAC thanked Ternullo for his responses to its issue survey and for demonstrating his “support and understanding of the needs of thousands of Main Street small businesses and the millions of workers at small businesses in Massachusetts.”
Ternullo said the endorsement reinforces one of the central themes of his campaign: making
Massachusetts more affordable for working families and creating an environment where local businesses can succeed.
“Small businesses are a critical part of Stoneham and Winchester,” Ternullo said.
“They create jobs, support our neighborhoods, sponsor our youth programs, and help make our communities what they are.
But rising taxes, energy bills, and the cost of doing business are putting tremendous pressure on businesses and working families — and state mandates and policies are contributing to those rising costs.
Beacon Hill should be working to make Massachusetts more affordable, not making it more expensive to live, work, and do business here.”
Ternullo has made affordability, government accountability, energy costs, increased local aid, and greater transparency on Beacon Hill central issues in his campaign.
“Whether you own a small business, work for one, work in the trades, or are trying to raise a family or retire here, the cost of living in Massachusetts matters,” Ternullo said.
“We need to take a hard look at state mandates that drive up costs and ask a simple question: What is this costing the people we represent?”
Ternullo added, “I’m running to make sure the taxpayers, workers, seniors, families, and small businesses of Stoneham and Winchester have a strong voice on Beacon Hill.
We need greater accountability for how taxpayer dollars are spent and policies that make Massachusetts more affordable for the people who call it home.”

🔥 PRESS RELEASE — TERNULLO CAMPAIGN KICKS OFF WITH STRONG SUPPORT
Winchester, MA — Stephen Ternullo, a United States Army veteran, longtime community volunteer, and local taxpayer advocate, officially launched his campaign for State Representative last night — pledging to restore affordability, accountability, and common-sense leadership on Beacon Hill.
Even with bad weather, dozens of supporters turned out to hear Ternullo deliver a strong message about the direction of the Commonwealth.
“Our state is at a crossroads that is affecting our communities, our families, and our quality of life,” Ternullo told attendees.
“As a dad, husband, and taxpayer, I am deeply concerned about the future.”
Ternullo outlined what he sees as unsustainable spending priorities on Beacon Hill — particularly billions directed toward emergency shelter programs for migrants — while local aid remains flat and communities like Stoneham and Winchester struggle with repeated tax override battles.
“Stoneham is now facing its second Prop 2½ override in eight months,” Ternullo said.
“Our residents cannot afford more property taxes.
We need relief, not higher taxes.”
He called for refocusing state resources on residents, veterans, seniors, schools, and infrastructure — while amending the Right to Shelter law to prevent Massachusetts from becoming a “magnet state.”
“There is a solution,” Ternullo said.
“The funds being spent on the migrant crisis should have been invested in our veterans, our seniors, our schools, and our infrastructure.”
He also highlighted widespread frustration with a lack of transparency on Beacon Hill — noting that 72% of voters supported empowering the State Auditor to audit the Legislature, yet lawmakers have refused to allow it.
“That wasn’t a partisan message,” he said.
“It was the people demanding accountability.
It is our money — not theirs.”
Ternullo pledged to fight for fair education funding and to reduce the burden on taxpayers caused by unfunded mandates.
“Every child in Stoneham and Winchester deserves a world-class education without placing the full burden on local taxpayers,” he said.
He also emphasized his commitment to supporting veterans, first responders, teachers, small businesses, and working families.
“As an Army veteran, service is part of who I am,” he said.
“My time in uniform taught me responsibility, discipline, and a deep respect for community.”
Ternullo closed his remarks with a message of unity and purpose.
“Ultimately, I’m running because I care deeply about the people of Stoneham and Winchester.
I want our communities to remain places where families thrive, seniors stay in their homes, small businesses grow, and children succeed.
This isn’t about politics — it’s about protecting our future together.”
September 12th, 2025
Dear Residents of Stoneham and Winchester,
I am honored to announce my candidacy for State Representative for the 31st Middlesex District, proudly representing the communities of Stoneham and Winchester in the November 3rd, 2026, election.
My name is Stephen Ternullo—a longtime Stoneham resident, proud veteran of the United States Army, and dedicated community leader.
For years, I have served the town of Stoneham in a variety of roles.
I have a history of advocating against tax increases and have been a strong proponent of government efficiency and much needed transparency.
I am a member of the Stoneham Veterans Committee and serve as Quartermaster of VFW Post 620 in Stoneham, standing proudly with those who have served.
These experiences have deepened my understanding of many issues and have opened my eyes to how state policies directly impact communities.
This experience has strengthened my resolve to defend the shared values of Stoneham and Winchester.
Professionally, I specialize in the building trades, with expertise in educational facility oversight and maintenance.
Personally, I am blessed to share life with my wonderful wife, Katie.
Together, we are proud parents of four children: my son Stephen, my daughter Alyssa, and my stepdaughters, Emily and Samantha.
My decades of service as a Little League, youth hockey, and soccer coach, as well as my role as an elected board member for Stoneham Youth Hockey, reflect my belief that investing in our children is the cornerstone of any thriving community.
Our district faces significant challenges.
Skyrocketing costs of living make it increasingly difficult for families, seniors, and new residents to remain in Stoneham and Winchester.
From rising utilities and taxes to unaffordable housing the barriers to opportunity are out of reach for many.
Declining academic performance in our public schools is something that is deeply concerning to me because a quality public education is the pathway to financial independence and prosperity.
As a young father with small children, we were able to build a life here because it was affordable.
Today, that same dream is out of reach for too many.
I am running to change that trajectory and restore affordability, fairness, and opportunity for all.
As your State Representative, I pledge to:
• Secure adequate, fair funding and state resources so every child in Stoneham and Winchester has access to a world-class education.
• Relieve mandate burdens – Advocate for full state funding of mandates imposed on our schools, easing the tax burden on local residents.
• Support small businesses – Foster job creation and economic growth while reducing tax pressures on entrepreneurs.
• Expand access to healthcare – Ensure affordable healthcare, rehabilitation, and mental health services are available to all.
• Enhance public safety – Address crime and drug issues, especially in and around our schools.
• Lower utility costs – Fight for policies that make utilities more affordable for families and businesses.
• Honor voter voices – Support the overwhelming call to audit the state legislature for greater transparency and accountability.
• Prioritize veterans’ housing – Ensure that our veterans have safe, affordable housing as a top priority.
• Promote healthy eating in our schools – Expand access to free, nutritious school meals, partner with Massachusetts farms to bring fresh produce into cafeterias and reduce reliance on processed foods.
• Teach nutrition and healthy choices – Integrate nutrition education into school curricula, teaching children the importance of clean eating, healthy lifestyles, and making informed food choices that will benefit them for life.
My campaign is built on the shared values of fairness, affordability, opportunity, and community.
I am committed to listening to you, our neighbors, friends, and fellow residents, and working collaboratively to build a brighter future for the 31st Middlesex District.
