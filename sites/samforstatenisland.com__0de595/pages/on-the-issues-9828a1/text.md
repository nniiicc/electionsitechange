Fair Property Taxes
The current system assesses property values, which are often outdated and incorrect, leading to disparities in taxation.
Staten Island has a significant number of single and two-family homes that are evaluated at higher rates than larger multifamily buildings.
As a result, many Staten Island residents are paying more in property taxes than they should be, which is causing financial hardship for many families.
Additionally, the city’s property tax system is based on a formula that has not been updated in decades.
This outdated formula does not reflect current market conditions and property values, which means that many homeowners are paying too much in property taxes.
This is especially true in Staten Island, where many residents are struggling to make ends meet.
It is time for the city to address this issue and update the property tax system to make it more equitable for all New Yorkers.

Animal Rights
Shelters are overcrowded and underfunded, and they often resort to euthanizing animals as a means of controlling the population.
However, euthanizing healthy animals is cruel and unnecessary, and it goes against the very idea of animal rights.
By supporting animal rights, we can work towards finding humane alternatives to kill shelters and prevent the needless killing of innocent animals.
It is essential to support animal rights in this context because every animal deserves a chance at a happy and healthy life.
Many of the animals in kill shelters are abandoned or surrendered by their owners and are not given the chance to find a loving home.
By advocating for animal rights, we can raise awareness about the importance of responsible pet ownership, spaying and neutering, and adoption.
We can also support initiatives that provide funding and resources to shelters and promote adoption events to find these animals permanent homes.
Ultimately, by supporting animal rights, we can work towards ending the cycle of animal overpopulation and ensure that every animal is treated with the respect and compassion they deserve.
