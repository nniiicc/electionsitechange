My policy priorities
Fighting for East Tennessee
Our community deserves better.
These are some of the top policy priorities our campaign is focused on.
An Economy that Works for All
Working hard should get you ahead, not put you behind.
A successful future for our workforce, our economy, and our country means passing legislation that supports workers, families, and innovation, not just the already-wealthy.
Healthcare Focused on People, Not Profits
A healthcare system’s success should be measured by how many patients it heals, not how much profit it generates for its shareholders.
Quality healthcare should be affordable and accessible to everyone who needs it.
A Government that Actually Represents Us
We live in a representative democracy – yet so many of us don’t feel represented.
Because we aren’t.
The system is rigged because our elected officials are bought off by corporate interests.
If we are going to solve any of the crises we face, we have to fight corruption and fix what’s broken in our government.
Building Better Infrastructure
I may have a PhD in Civil Engineering, but you don’t need a doctoral degree to see that our infrastructure has not kept up with our community’s growth – and that is making everyone’s lives harder.
We send federal tax dollars to Washington.
We deserve a Representative who will fight to bring those dollars home and invest in our region.
