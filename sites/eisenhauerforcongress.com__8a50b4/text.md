WE ARE NOW QUALIFIED FOR THE NOVEMBER, 2026, GENERAL ELECTION BALLOT!
Together, we collected nearly 13,000 signatures as part of the State-mandated petition process, and press releases by the Secretary of State’s office have shown that more than enough signatures have now been verified or “accepted” as valid.
Thank you, Montana and the 2nd District!
The hurdle for ballot access was very high — once again, the 2025 Montana legislature made this even more difficult in a clear and intentional effort to preserve their own two-party duopoly, and to discourage independent or third-party candidates from engaging in the political process:
- For the 2026 election cycle, independent candidates for the House of Representatives (MT-02) needed to submit in excess of 7,274 valid signatures of current registered voters within the congressional district.
This is an exceptionally high hurdle; in comparison, the neighboring States of Idaho (500) and North Dakota (1000) require far fewer valid signatures for ballot access.
