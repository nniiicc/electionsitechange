Dr.
Michael Eisenhauer is honored to have earned the support of a broad coalition of Montana leaders, labor organizations, and veterans groups who share a commitment to independent, results-driven representation for the 2nd Congressional District.
These endorsements, from former Governors and Legislators across party lines, experienced public servants all, organized labor and those who have served our country — together reflect a growing recognition that Montanans need a voice in Congress focused on practical problem-solving, accountability, and putting the interests of working families, rural communities, and veterans first rather.
It’s not about the party bosses or partisan gridlock, anymore!
Organizational Endorsements
Montana AFL-CIO: The federation of unions representing working families across the state.
“…(We) proudly endorse Dr.
Mike Eisenhauer for Congress because he puts working families and common-sense solutions ahead of partisan politics.
As a cardiologist and retired Army Colonel, Mike will fight in Washington to lower healthcare costs, protect good-paying union jobs, and defend the rights of Montana workers.” — Montana AFL-CIO
Independent Veterans of America: Recognizing Dr.
Eisenhauer’s nearly 25 years of Army service as a physician and health care leader, including his Bronze Star for service in Iraq.
Big Sky 55+: The leading advocacy organization fighting to protect healthcare access, financial security, and consumer rights for older Montanans.
Forward Party: The Forward Party was born from a simple truth: America can do better than this.
Our political system isn’t broken by accident, it’s been manipulated to reward division, gridlock, and self-interest.
For decades, both major parties have used rules, primaries, and redistricting to protect their own power instead of serving the people.
“…Forward is committed to supporting leaders who have demonstrated a record of serving their communities and who are focused on practical governance rather than partisan politics.
Michael Eisenhauer’s campaign reflects the kind of independent leadership that we are working to support across the country.” — Lindsey Drath, CEO of the Forward Party
Montana State Conference of Electrical Workers (IBEW): The Montana State Conference of Electrical Workers (IBEW), representing more than 4,000 skilled electrical workers in locals across the state, has endorsed Dr.
Mike Eisenhauer for Montana’s 2nd Congressional District.
Chairman Dan DeBolt cited Eisenhauer’s service as an Army colonel and physician, his connection to Montana’s working families, and his focus on union jobs, responsible energy development, and practical solutions that turn economic growth into family-supporting work.
IBEW leaders say he will be a strong advocate for working men and women because he treats opportunity, infrastructure, and workforce development as one fight.
Montana Boilermakers Local 11: The officers and members of Boilermakers Local 11 have voted to endorse Dr.
Michael Eisenhauer.
Business Manager Joshua Mangold said the local is proud to stand behind the campaign because of its commitment to working families, organized labor, good-paying jobs, and opportunities for Montana workers.
