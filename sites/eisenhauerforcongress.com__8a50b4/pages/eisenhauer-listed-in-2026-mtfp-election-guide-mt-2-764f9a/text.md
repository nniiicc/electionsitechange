Helena, MT — Featured in the Montana Free Press 2026 Election Guide, Eisenhauer is positioning himself as a problem-solver who answers only to Montanans — not party bosses or coastal elites.
A Bronze Star recipient and longtime physician who has served patients across rural Montana, he is campaigning on affordability, protecting rural healthcare and hospitals, public lands, veterans’ issues, and breaking the two-party gridlock that he says is failing working families.
He has already gathered thousands of the signatures needed to qualify for the general election ballot.
