Billings, MT — For the first time in recent memory, independent candidates are running in all three of Montana’s federal races this year.
Great Falls cardiologist and retired Army Colonel Dr.
Mike Eisenhauer is the Independent candidate for the 2nd Congressional District.
Eisenhauer is campaigning to break the partisan gridlock in Washington and deliver real solutions for Montanans on affordability, rural healthcare, veterans’ issues, and public lands.
