Montana — In a candid Q&A with Montana Public Radio, Independent candidate Dr.
Mike Eisenhauer explains why he’s running: an unshackled representative can be a true problem-solver instead of adding to Washington’s gridlock.
The retired Army Colonel and cardiologist highlights his structured, evidence-based approach to issues, drawing from 24 years of military service and 35 years in medicine.
Top priorities for Montana’s 2nd District include affordability (housing, healthcare, fuel), protecting rural hospitals, supporting family agriculture, and a strong Right to Repair law.
He supports smart government reform without eliminating key agencies like the Department of Education, and favors balanced development of public lands and natural resources.
