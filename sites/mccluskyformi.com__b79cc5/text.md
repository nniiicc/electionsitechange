District 86
Michigan’s 86th House District is the most competitive State House district in West Michigan.
It encompasses parts of Allegan and Ottawa counties, including the City of Holland, most of Holland Township, and all of Park and Laketown Townships.
It has a population of approximately 91,386 residents.
