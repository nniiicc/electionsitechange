Strong communities need a strong employment-base that goes beyond state tax incentives.
It requires access to medical care, clean water, good roads, strong schools, affordable housing, quality daycare, safe recreation, and more.
Representatives in Des Moines are not trusting the people closest to the work, soil, or family and our communities are paying the bill.
