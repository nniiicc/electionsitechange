Below is a simple guide to voting in Arizona’s Legislative District 10.
Voters in Legislative District 10 choose two members of the Arizona House.
Check Your Registration Arizona voters can check or update their registration online through the Arizona Secretary of State .
Voting Options Early Voting: Available to registered voters Mail-In Voting: Ballots must be returned by the deadline In-Person Voting: Available on Election Day Important Dates October 5: Last day to register to vote October 7: Early voting begins and ballots are mailed October 23: Last day to request a ballot by mail October 27: Recommended date to mail your ballot back October 30: Last day to vote early in person November 3: Election Day , polls open 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.
For official election information, voters should visit the Arizona Secretary of State’s website or contact their county elections office.
