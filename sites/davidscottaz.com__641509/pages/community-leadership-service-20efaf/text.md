Education & Youth Education has been a consistent focus of David’s community work.
He has led early literacy efforts that bring together school districts, volunteers, and community partners to strengthen foundational learning.
He has also served on boards and committees focused on youth development, financial education, and preparing young people for the workforce.
Economic Development David’s work in economic development has focused on coordination and long-term planning through chambers of commerce and regional partnerships.
This includes efforts to support business growth, workforce alignment, infrastructure planning, and collaboration between cities, employers, and educators.
Faith & Service Faith has played an important role in David’s life and service.
Through church leadership and outreach, he has been involved in ongoing efforts focused on stewardship, care for others, and strengthening community connections.
