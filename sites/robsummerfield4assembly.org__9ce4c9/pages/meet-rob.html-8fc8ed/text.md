| Meet Rob Rob is a former small business owner who is seeking a fifth term in the Wisconsin State Assembly.
At an early age Rob began working in the hospitality industry.
That experience taught him the value of a hard day's work.
He also learned how to provide for others and deliver results.
Previously, Rob has helped operate the family business, Two Acres Supper Club.
He is also the former co-owner of Chippewa Valley Land Title.
He brings this background to his time in Madison and works every day to be a voice for economic growth and job creation.
In addition to his work and spending time with his family, Rob is also active in the community as a member of the Bloomer Chamber of Commerce, member of Good Shepard Lutheran Church, and as a youth sports coach.
Rob was born and raised in Bloomer and still resides there today with his wife Kim, and their three daughters. | |
