After a somber week of HIV/AIDS awareness event with rededicated of the AIDS memorial rock at Central Christian Church and showing of the documentary of How to Survive a Plague at the Great Falls Public Library, we invite you to a Party with a Purpose, with a live DJ, testing resources, silent auction and prizes!
Join members of our community while we remember those lost and are still affected with love and life.
An opportunity for our community to expand connections to make positive changes for the future.
Join us on Dec 7th from 7:00-10:00pm at Luna Coffee in Downtown Great Falls.
Sponsored by Independence Rock Coalition and the Great Falls LGBTQ+ Center, the gathering combines HIV awareness with health screenings, silent auction and prizes.
