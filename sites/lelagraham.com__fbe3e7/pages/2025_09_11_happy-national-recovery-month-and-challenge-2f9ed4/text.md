September 11, 2025 Happy September!
I love September in Montana.
I am taking full advantage of the weather and spending time getting my steps in by walking through my district.
My VA Doctor has highly encouraged me to get out and move more.
So let’s turn that advice into opportunity.
It is also National Recovery Month and with my upcoming Recovery Anniversary in October, my goal is to walk in the district everyday where I can touch base with voters and say Hello to all the dogs in the district.
Help support me with a recurring $# donation. https://secure.actblue.com/donate/recoveryrocks I will be posting my weekly mileage on my Campaign socials.
Thank you for all the ways you support me in this campaign.
