March 4, 2026 I am sorry that I missed you while I am out in the district talking with voters.
Like many people in Great Falls who are hard working just looking for an opportunity to succeed, it has been a great challenge to balance affording rent, groceries, and all other expenses that seem to keep growing; and the income not matching.
I am dedicated to the issues that are most important to my district and Montana.
It is now my job to continue to lead by example, stay actively involved in our community, and show you why electing me to the Montana legislature is important for Great Falls.
And with your help, I am as determined as ever to turn Great Falls into great opportunities.
