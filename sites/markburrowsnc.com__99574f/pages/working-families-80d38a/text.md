The Problem Nearly a fifth of our children live in poverty.
Many here pay more than 30% of their salaries for rent; roughly half have trouble paying their rent.
Our average weekly wages are much lower than the NC average – and costs are often higher.

Mark’s Plan Advocate for policies that promote a living wage right here.
Support state and local collaboration on affordable housing efforts Secure state resources for infrastructure and high-speed internet in WNC so we can keep the businesses we have – and attract new ones Why It Matters Everyone should be able to afford to live here in Western North Carolina, especially working class families.
Some common-sense policies to support the working class can make the difference between getting by and getting ahead.
