Skip to content Home About Platform Donate Home About Platform Economic Opportunity Education Environment Government Accountability Healthcare Working Families FAQ Contact Us Donate Menu Faqs What experience does Mark Burrows have to represent North Carolina House District 119?
Mark is passionate about helping working families, has deep roots in the community, and played a crucial role in recruiting and growing businesses in Transylvania County and Western North Carolina (WNC) for more than 30 years.
Mark has served on regional boards including AdvantageWest, Asheville Regional Housing Consortium, and Carolina West, and led the Brevard/Transylvania Chamber of Commerce.
Mark holds a Master of Environmental Management from Duke University and is a certified planner (AICP).
He is a long-time resident of Brevard who has raised his family in Western North Carolina.
Mark’s wife, Betsy Burrows was a public school teacher and professor at Brevard College for over forty years.
How will Mark Burrows make buying a house and everyday living expenses more affordable?
Mark believes people who work in Western North Carolina should be able to afford to live here.
Housing cost is the primary driver of the affordability issue.
He will co-sponsor a bill to provide local governments with flexibility to address their housing and construction needs.
This bill would also make it easier for counties and towns to build affordable housing for our teachers and essential workers.
Mark would amend House Bill 998 that reduced corporate income tax to zero by 2030 and caused North Carolina to lose billions of dollars in revenue.
Knowing that wages in the region lag the state average, Mark will support policies that increase wages beyond the federal minimum of $7.25 an hour.
Where does Mark Burrows stand on expanding healthcare access and protecting Medicaid?
Mark believes health care is a basic right and no one should have to choose between getting healthcare and putting food on the table.
In rural communities like District 119, local medical care is disappearing and too far away; many residents are uninsured, and a quarter to half of residents in Jackson, Swain, and Transylvania counties rely on Medicaid and risk losing coverage.
Mark will fight to protect Medicaid expansion, hold corporate healthcare services accountable, and lower medication costs.
What will Mark Burrows do to bring more jobs and economic development to our rural community?
Mark has a long career in growing economic opportunities and businesses in Western North Carolina.
He supports smart growth that protects the region’s extensive public lands and environment while balancing recreation, conservation, and job creation.
How will Mark Burrows make public education better for our kids and increase teachers’ pay?
Mark believes North Carolina once had one of the nation’s strongest public education systems, and we must invest in it again.
The state’s average teacher pay ranks 43rd in the nation – even after recent salary adjustments.
Our school facilities are also deteriorating.
Mark will support a statewide construction bond for K-12 schools.
Mark opposes shifting state funds to private schools that have no academic and basic educational outcomes accountability measures .
He also supports affordable early childhood education and more school based mental health services to help students cope with enormous stress and navigate a complicated world..
What does Mark Burrows think about the importance of infrastructure in our schools and community?
Mark believes investing in infrastructure is important and will keep our communities and schools prosperous and running smoothly.
Mark will work to secure state funding to build a more resilient infrastructure post-Hurricane Helene.
For schools, he will fight for funding to maintain school buildings and learning environments.
What are Mark Burrows’ views on the environment?
Mark believes District 119 is fortunate to have forests, preserves, parks, and the Blue Ridge Mountains which need robust protections.
Our district has more public lands than any other in North Carolina and is a source of pride and strength of our community.
Mark’s education, professional experience, and long history as a resident equip him to fight for policies that will protect us from adverse weather, reduce the state’s reliance on carbon-based fuels while diversifying the economy, and support policies that balance conservation, recreation, and local economic development.
What will Mark Burrows do to continue Hurricane Helene recovery efforts?
The Hurricane Helene recovery efforts have been hard for our district and slow at times.
Mark will work to keep the continued flow of FEMA money to rebuild our community.
What are Mark Burrows’ views on data centers?
Mark Burrows does not support data centers expanding into Western NC.
Mark is concerned about data centers’ potential strain on utilities, increased costs for working families, and potential harm to District 119’s mountains and rivers.
Mark also believes local governments, not the state, should have greater autonomy and control of their destiny in determining if data centers are right for their communities.
What does Mark Burrows think about the government’s responsibility to be transparent and accountable to constituents?
Mark believes the state government must be transparent and accountable to the people it serves.
He thinks legislators’ emails should be available to the public and calls for them to do their jobs and deliver an annual budget.
He also opposes extreme gerrymandering, saying voters should choose legislators rather than legislators choosing their voters.
About Mark Burrows Mark Burrows is a local leader, husband, and father who is running for NC House of Representatives to continue his thirty-year legacy of promoting economic planning and community development in Western NC.
