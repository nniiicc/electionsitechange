Agriculture
Iowa is a leader in agriculture not only in the United States but also around the world.
As one of the largest producers of corn, eggs, pork, and soybeans or local economies depend on the success of Iowa’s farming communities and families.
- As a state representative I have, and will continue to fight for the land rights of Iowa’s farmers.
- I will always support farming policy initiatives that support our small family farms, and I’ll continue to work and protect our legacy family farms from foreign companies.
- While serving in the Iowa House I have voted for and voiced my support for establishing new markets for Iowa farmers to sell their crops.
The elimination of meat packing facilities, Co-Ops, and local agriculture buyers puts Iowa farmers at risk.
Building Iowa's Local Economy
As a member of the Iowa House of Representatives I have supported legislation and polices that promote and support Iowa’s small business and local economies.
I believe that the as policy makers our role is to ensure that Iowa remains competitive in the worldwide marketplace, and that Iowa is full of opportunity for all Iowans.
My keys goals are to:
- Cut taxes and unnecessary government red tape.
- Support polices that promote strong job growth.
- Make sure that Iowa’s infrastructure is maintained, to allow Iowa businesses the opportunity to grow.
- Promote legislation that ensures Iowans access to a quality education and the job training skills they need.
Protecting Rural Healthcare
Rural Iowa has seen a significant decreases in rural healthcare access in recent years.
I have always and will always fight to make sure all Iowans has access to quality healthcare.
- In 2022 I sponsored and voted for the “Rural Emergency Hospital Bill” which eliminated the red tape and restrictions on standalone emergency and outpatient facilities.
- We need to promote access to telehealth programs, and support incentives to attract new
healthcare workers to rural Iowa communities.
- In 2023 I strongly supported legislation that would have equaled the playing field for Iowa rural pharmacies, giving them more power over their pricing which would not only allow them to stay in business, but to also pass those savings along to Iowa’s seniors.

Maintain Iowa Fiscal Health
As a legislator I believe in three key fiscal policy commitments:
- Never spend more than reoccurring revenue allows.
- Only support balance budget legislation, and continue the long-term smart fiscal policy that has been established.
- Cut taxes, spend wisely, and limit government waste whenever possible.
Protecting Iowa Natural Resources
I am committed to protecting and developing Iowa natural resources in a sustainable and environmentally conscious manner.
As an avid hunter and outdoorsman, I cherish the memories I’ve created with my family and I want to make sure future generations of Iowans have the same opportunities.
Looking after Iowa Veterans and their Families
As a veteran of the United State Navy, I understand the need to takes care of Iowa’s veterans and their families.
In 2023 I filed HF 2505 that was a comprehensive piece of legislation that provide millions of dollars of support for veterans and their families.
First, it provided immediate property tax relief for disabled veterans.
Second, it gave veterans and their families an opportunity to attend Iowa’s Universities at little to no cost.
