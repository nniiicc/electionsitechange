3, 2026 DONATE HERE About Ali Ali Simpson is a candidate for Tennessee State House District 32, which represents Roane County and Lenoir City.
She operates a small farm with her family in Ten Mile.
Ali’s Experience On the board of Southeast Tennessee Young Farmers Coalition: Ali Advocates for sustainable agriculture policy and resources for young and beginning farmers Americorps alum with Habitat for Humanity Former program coordinator with SCORE and the Small Business Administration (SBA), where she worked to establish programs that provide free education and resources to aspiring entrepreneurs.
Small farm operator : growing and selling veggies, wrasslin’ animals, wearing many (straw) hats.
Ali's experience in these roles has shown her that this country is abundant with folks who have the grit, drive, and know-how to lift up themselves and their communities.
With the right support, like access to affordable health care, a man in her community could start what would become a regional composting business from the back of his pickup truck.
With a weatherized home and a lower heating bill, a family she served at Habitat could save a little more for their future.
With a small grant, a farmer can produce fresh, healthy food for schools and food banks.
Ali’s Goals Fight for policies that improve the health, freedom and empowerment of the people.
Reverse the ongoing and shameless transfer of public wealth to corporations and special interests, and return that wealth to the hands of the people.
Platform Agriculture and the Preservation of Rural Life Farmland is disappearing at an alarming rate in Tennessee and with it, the green countryside that we love.
I will pursue an aggressive pro-agriculture agenda and will prioritize: Farmland Preservation Small Farm Profitability Right to Repair Water and Soil Health Initiatives Battling invasives such as spotted lanternfly and Asian carp Support for small and mid-size processors and commercial kitchens for value-added products Programs that purchase healthy food from local farms for schools Protections for workers in conditions such as extreme heat Healthy Communities Tennesseans deserve affordable and accessible healthcare.
I will fight to: Join our southern neighbors in accepting funding for the Medicaid expansion, which will help fund small and rural hospitals Support addiction treatment and prevention programs Fund programs to purchase food for schools from local farms Enable counties to better address issues such as domestic violence Advocate for programs that help older adults access healthcare by investing in rural transportation and mobile clinics Improve waste infrastructure and economy by passing the Tennessee Waste to Jobs Act.

Strong Public Schools With the passing of the school voucher scam, Tennessee public schools, especially those in rural counties, will be facing a crisis that will prevent kids from getting the best education.
In addition to opposing vouchers, I will: Support free breakfast and lunch for all Tennessee public school students Advocate for increasing teacher pay and pay for essential employees such as bus drivers and janitors Funding for special programs offering education in trades and the arts Read more here - Coming Soon!
Personal Freedom Many politicians in Nashville are salesmen.
They sell us the promise of freedom to get elected and then sell us out.
If elected, I will: Legalize and deregulate marijuana use and cultivation Support the Food Freedom Act Support Voting Rights Restoration for ex-felons Fight overreaching laws such as the Medical Ethics Defense Act, which empowers doctors to refuse to treat patients based on their beliefs—a dangerous policy when folks often cannot choose their doctors Protect the rights of Queer Folks!
Fight for the restoration of Abortion rights.
Abortion is healthcare and the rising maternal and infant mortality rates in our state prove it And, for the record, I support the 1st AND 2nd Amendment.

An Economy for all Tennesseeans For too long, our state has suffered from policies that favor corporate interests and profits over the people.
If elected, I will: Propose robust anti-lobbying legislation Propose an gradual increase of the minimum wage to $18/hr STOP the rapid pace of data center construction Ban algorithmic grocery pricing Advocate for affordable and accessible high-speed internet Support small businesses with resources and education services Fight for affordable, universal childcare Reduce or remove sales tax on groceries!
End Citizens United with States Rights!
The battle for a better future will not be won unless we take back control from corporations.
The Citizens United Supreme Court decision has proved disastrous for our politics and has supplanted our voices with the agenda of a wealthy few.
Tennessee has the power to fight back.
In summary, states have the power to define what corporations as an entity can do , regardless of where the corporation is headquartered—and that includes whether they can make political donations.
Montana has already stepped forward with a 2026 constitutional initiative to redefine corporate power.
If elected, I pledge to do everything I can to get this on the ballot.
