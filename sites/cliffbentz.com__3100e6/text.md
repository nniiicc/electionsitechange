The second of seven children, Cliff Bentz was raised by Kenneth and Anne Bentz on family ranches in Harney County.
His grandfather, Paul Stewart, had moved to Harney County in about 1916, acquiring a small ranch near Crane, then trading it for the Mann Lake Ranch, which in turn was traded for the Whitehorse Ranch.
The family sold the Whitehorse in 1960, and purchased the Actin Ranch some 15 miles north of Drewsey (in Harney County) in 1961.
