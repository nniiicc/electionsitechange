Who AM I?
I'm Tom Trost, a native Hudson resident running for the New Hampshire House of Representatives.
I grew up here in Hudson, where my sister Victoria and I were raised by our amazing parents, Ted and Krista.
More recently, I've worked for Governor Kelly Ayotte, studied politics at Saint Anselm College, and fought for legislation that will help our community.
Why aM I running?
I'm running because I know Hudson deserves representatives that will stand up for our values and put the needs of the public first.
The cost of living is rising, our education and healthcare systems are struggling, and political polarization has made civil disagreement rarer and rarer.
We can and we will overcome these challenges, but it will take hard work, humility, and courage.
New Hampshire is facing new challenges, and we need new leaders.
What district aM I running in?
I'm a Republican running to represent Hillsborough District 13, which covers all of Hudson.
