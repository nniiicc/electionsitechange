With 17 years of experience in the TV and film industry, Linda has played a pivotal role in shaping programming for the U.S.
Hispanic market.
As a proud founder of In Luz We Trust, Linda is committed to helping individuals overcome financial trauma and build lasting wealth.
Her acclaimed book, Wealth Warrior: 8 Steps for Communities of Color to Conquer the Stock Market, and her daily digestible content and workshops have provided invaluable financial education and resources to many.
Linda's achievements have earned her recognition from Latino Leaders Magazine and the City of Los Angeles.
Her contributions have been featured in prominent media outlets including Yahoo Finance, Forbes, and Univision.
Through her podcast Investies, she makes complex economic topics accessible and actionable for a broad audience.
Driven by her personal experiences and a deep understanding of economic and social issues, Linda is passionate about addressing key challenges in Texas.
These include advocating for equitable education funding, expanding healthcare access, supporting women's reproductive rights, and promoting infrastructure development.
Linda García’s commitment to her community and her expertise in economic empowerment are central to her vision for a better future for Texas.
