Garcia to Champion Financial and AI Innovation
AUSTIN, Texas - Speaker Dustin Burrows of the Texas House of Representatives has appointed State Representative Linda Garcia to the Pensions, Investments & Financial Services Committee and the Delivery of Government Efficiency Committee for the 89th Legislative Session.
With her extensive background in the financial literacy and growing leadership in artificial intelligence, Representative Garcia is ready to leverage her expertise to drive meaningful policy changes that benefit Texas communities.
As a member of the Pensions, Investments & Financial Services Committee, Representative Garcia will bring her deep understanding of financial markets and wealth-building strategies to the legislative process.
With over a decade of experience in financial education and investment analysis and advocacy, including her work with In Luz We Trust, Representative Garcia has dedicated her career to helping individuals overcome generational barriers and work towards financial independence.
She is committed to ensuring Texas’ pension systems remain robust and sustainable, while also advocating for policies that promote smart investments and financial security for all Texans.
Representative Garcia’s appointment to the Delivery of Government Efficiency Committee aligns with her passion for leveraging technology to improve governance.
As an expert in financial empowerment and a thought leader in emerging technologies, she is eager to work on policies that integrate artificial intelligence into government systems to enhance efficiency, transparency, and accessibility.
With AI increasingly shaping financial markets and public services.
Representative Garcia is well-positioned to help craft policies that ensure Texas remains at the forefront of responsible AI governance, balancing innovation with ethical safeguards.
“I am honored to serve on these committees and look forward to using my expertise to make a real difference for Texans,” said Representative Garcia.
“Financial security and technological advancement go hand in hand, and I’m committed to ensuring our state makes smart, forward-thinking investments in our pension systems and in the responsible use of AI to improve government services.
I want to thank Speaker Burrows for appointing me to these critical committees as we begin work in the 89th Legislative Session.”
Representative Garcia’s leadership in financial education, along with her commitment to modernizing government operations, positions her as a key advocate for policies that will empower Texans to ensure a more efficient, financially sound future for the state.
