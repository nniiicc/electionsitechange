Health Care For All:
Access to quality and affordable healthcare is a human right, and I am dedicated to continuing to advocate for healthcare reform that will lead to equitable access for people in Assembly District 31 and across New York State.
My office has trained over 150 community members in mental health first aid in collaboration with the NYC Department of Health of Mental Hygiene, and we are working to continue these efforts.
We will also continue the fight for the New York Health Act, collaborate with community organizations to advance initiatives that promote access to healthy foods, and partner with local healthcare experts, like the medical professionals at St.
John’s Episcopal Hospital, to provide regular updates about healthcare and overall wellness to the communities in our district.
