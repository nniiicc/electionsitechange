A Proven Conservative Voice for Northeast Missouri Greg Sharpe is a fifth-generation farmer from Ewing, Missouri, where his family has worked the same Northeast Missouri ground for more than a century.
Farming isn't a talking point for Greg — it's how he was raised, how he raised his own family, and how he still spends his time when he's not at the Capitol.
Greg currently serves as a State Representative, where he's built a record as a dependable conservative voice for rural Missouri — defending agriculture, fighting for lower taxes, standing strong on Second Amendment rights, and refusing to compromise on the protection of unborn life.
Agriculture has been Greg's life's work both on and off the farm.
He has served 15 years on the Missouri Soybean board, including a term as President, and has spent 16 years on the board of Missouri ALOT (Agriculture Leaders of Tomorrow), helping develop the next generation of rural leaders.
For 38 years, he has also served Northeast Missouri farmers as a Pioneer Hi-Bred Intl seed dealer — a relationship built one handshake, one field, and one harvest at a time.
Greg is a champion for Northeast Missouri and our rural way of life.
He's also a strong public school advocate who will defend our rural schools from the liberal, big-city special interests trying to take money from our kids and funnel it into the cities and suburbs.
Our children deserve the same opportunities as any student in the state — and Greg will fight to make sure they get them.
He's running for the State Senate because Northeast Missouri deserves a senator who actually knows what it takes to make a living out here.
Not a politician.
Not a lobbyist.
A farmer who shows up, listens, and votes the way the people who sent him to Jefferson City would vote themselves.
Greg is married to his lovely wife Theresa.
He is an amazing dad and grandpa.
When he isn't in the field or at the Capitol, you'll find him at a county fair, an FFA banquet, a high school ball game, or a small-town parade — anywhere the people of Northeast Missouri gather.
