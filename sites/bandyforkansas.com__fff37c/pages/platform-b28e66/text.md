I believe in freedom and opportunity.
Especially for the smallest among us: small people, small businesses, and small communities.
Rising Costs
Everyday costs for Kansans — groceries, the internet, furniture, and more — are increasingly too expensive.
The cost of raising children and aging with dignity has become unaffordable and inaccessible.
I will advance commonsense policies to lower costs and support families and seniors.
Economy & Workforce Stability
A strong Kansas economy depends on strong families.
When parents and caregivers can't find affordable child care, struggle to pay for diapers and food, or have to choose between their job and their baby's health, our whole workforce suffers.
When families who need it receive support early on, parents can stay in the workforce, businesses thrive, and communities grow stronger.
Health Care Access and Costs
Every Kansan deserves quality, affordable health care, from providers they trust, when and where they need it.
I will pass policies that will help realize that vision with lower prices and expanded access.
