Social Media Policy
Civility is expected from all commentors and those sending direct messages from any online or mail-based account to Löki Tobin for Alaska.
Use of profane language, slurs, innuendos, or hate speech or any imagery that conveys such will result in comments being hidden and flagged for further review by the platform and possible legal or civil action pursued.
Comments or imagery using any of the above language or making threats (either direct or implied) will be hidden.
Repeat offenders will be blocked from further communication.
