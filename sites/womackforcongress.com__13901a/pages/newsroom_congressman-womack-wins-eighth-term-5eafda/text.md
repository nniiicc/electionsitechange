Congressman Womack Wins Eighth Term
For Immediate Release:
November 5, 2024
ROGERS, Ark. – Representative Steve Womack (AR-3) today released the following statement after winning reelection to represent Arkansas’ Third Congressional District in the U.S.
House of Representatives for an eighth term:
“Conservative principles such as less government, lower taxes, pro-growth and pro-family policies, the rule of law, and a strong national defense are what I believe creates a better America for all.
Tonight, we see that Third District Arkansans agree with me.
I remain committed to fighting against the socialist wing of the Democrat party and restoring the American dream.
I work every day to deliver and improve the lives of Third District Arkansans.
Serving my state and country is the greatest honor of my life, and I will never take it for granted.
Thank you for your continued trust in me and making your voice heard.”
