Congressman Womack Casts Election Ballot
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
October 22, 2020
ROGERS, Ark. – Representative Steve Womack today released the following statement after casting his 2020 general election ballot:
“Every four years, we often say that this is the most important election of our lifetimes.
This year, I believe it really is.
We are at a true crossroads as a country, and there is a clear choice: freedom vs. socialism.
I am proud to cast my vote in support of protecting the America we know and love for generations to come.
A country that embraces hard work and individual responsibility, upholds the rule of law, unleashes opportunity, and stands for our God-given liberties.
Together, we will unite behind our conservative values to further build American strength, security, jobs, and freedom.
I look forward to the continued opportunity to serve you and fight for your best interests in Congress.”
