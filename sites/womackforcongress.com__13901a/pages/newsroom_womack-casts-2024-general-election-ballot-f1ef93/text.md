Congressman Womack Casts 2024 General Election Ballot
For Immediate Release:
November 1, 2024
ROGERS, Ark. – Congressman Steve Womack (AR-3) today released the following statement after casting his 2024 general election ballot:
"This may be the most consequential election of our lifetime.
America faces a critical choice—another four years of record-high prices of everyday goods, a dangerous open border, burdensome and costly regulations, and a struggling economy—or a return to a strong and prosperous America.
I’m proud to cast my vote in support of conservative values to make our country great again.
A nation built on freedom and the ability to pursue the American dream.
I look forward to gaining your trust to serve and fight for the best interest of Third District Arkansans."
