House of Representatives for a fifth term: "I’m humbled to have Third District Arkansans continue to put their faith in me as their representative.
My commitment to our shared conservative values of building a safer, stronger, prouder, and more prosperous nation is steadfast.
I will continue to represent you by working to uphold the principles of the Constitution and supporting conservative policies that safeguard taxpayer dollars, bolster our national defense, enhance economic opportunity, and protect individual liberty.
It is an honor to be your voice in Congress and represent the endless determination and strong entrepreneurial spirit our community embodies.
Thank you for your support, trust, and vote." ### J Foley Previous Previous Congressman Womack Files for Reelection
