Congressman Womack Wins Seventh Term
For Immediate Release:
November 8, 2022
ROGERS, Ark. – Representative Steve Womack (AR-3) today released the following statement after winning reelection to represent the Third District of Arkansas in the U.S.
House of Representatives for a seventh term:
“Tonight, Third District Arkansans made their voices and values heard.
The future they want is one of freedom, security, opportunity, and economic strength.
We are united behind reversing the failed Biden-Pelosi agenda.
The new House will require strong leadership and a targeted focus on curbing inflation, restoring U.S. energy independence, closing our open border, fiscal responsibility, and policies that put families first.
My duty to our country and the people of the Third District is an honor I will never take for granted.
Thank you, Arkansas, for your continued trust in me.”
