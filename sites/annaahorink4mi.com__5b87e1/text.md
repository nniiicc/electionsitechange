Anna’s husband helps keep our community safe as a police officer, and her kids go to Marquette public schools and play sports.

But most importantly – we need a leader who will put people over politics and fight for the U.P.
Anna isn’t a politician – she’s a wife, a mom, a healthcare provider, a neighbor, and she sure as hell won’t back down from a fight.
