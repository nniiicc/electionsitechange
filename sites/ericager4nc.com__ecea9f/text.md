Eric Ager is fighting for Buncombe County in the NC House.
Eric Ager believes we must expect more from our state legislature.
Raised on a working farm and a veteran of the US Navy, Eric learned the values of hard work, cooperation, and integrity from a young age.
In Raleigh, he believes we can prioritize the needs of working families, children, and veterans here in District 114 if we commit to our values, commit to each other, and commit to doing the hard work of governing.
