Budget/Spending
The former administration had lost its way with an economy that hurt so many of our families and neighbors.
As your Delegate, I remain focused on efficiency and effectiveness when spending our tax dollars.
I will always fight to stop the stealing of dedicated funds to balance the operating budget.
These funds are only to be used for the intended purpose our citizens supported.
That's why I was proud to work with Gov.
Hogan and vote for legislation that stopped the "redirecting" of transportation funds, Bay Restoration Funds and revenues from casinos that will help our schools.
