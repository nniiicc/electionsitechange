I have spent my life as a proud Maryland resident, committed husband, and devoted father.
For more than 29 years, my wife Erika & I have called Anne Arundel County home, raising our daughter, a 2016 graduate of Southern High School, in the same community we continue to serve.
As a former small‑business owner for over two decades, I understand firsthand how overregulation and unstable tax policy can hinder job creation and economic growth.
My business provided local jobs, strengthened the county’s economy, and shaped my belief that Maryland thrives when entrepreneurs and employers are empowered—not burdened.
In the House of Delegates, your family's economic prosperity remains one of my highest priorities.
I am focused on building a more business‑friendly Maryland by promoting a stable regulatory climate, reducing unnecessary barriers, and ensuring that local businesses have the infrastructure they need to grow and compete.
I also believe that Maryland’s children are its future heritage.
I am committed to strengthening the state’s education system, expanding long‑term career pathways, and ensuring that students and families have access to the opportunities necessary for lasting economic success.
"As your delegate, I am fully committed to protecting our way of life, family values, and the rural nature and beauty of our communities.
Preserving what makes our region special—its character, its heritage, and its quality of life—guides every decision I make."
Through strong partnerships with state and local leaders, I will continue to advance policies that support our families, local businesses, and the long‑term vitality of the communities I proudly represent.
