Opioid Epidemic
Unfortunately, Covid has taken some attention away from the opioid epidemic that threatens our communities.
Far too many of our friends, neighbors and family members have been taken from us by this epidemic.
That is why I have sponsored bills like HB 869 which has become law- This legislation requires the Department of Health, for the first time in this state’s history, to provide a publicly available list of state certified and accredited treatment centers for substance abusers.
This will help them, and their families make an informed decision on the resources they can utilize to help ensure a full and speedy recovery.
I believe that, together, we can end this plaque on our communities!
Taxes

Health Care
I continually sponsor and will always fight for legislation that would increase the annual state tax deduction toward the purchase of long term care insurance to $500.
Currently, Maryland only gives a one time $500 deduction for the purchase of long term care insurance.
Giving an annual deduction towards the purchase on long term care insurance would help to reduce the cost of healthcare and give many in our community, especially our elderly the ability to age at home.
This gives these vital members of our community and their families, much deserved peace of mind.

Jobs
As a local veteran business owner, I know first-hand how difficult it is to own and operate a small business in Maryland.
The liberal controlled legislature in Annapolis regularly puts forth legislation that increases costs, regulation and actively penalizes businesses.
As Delegate, I have worked hard and been successful at stopping legislation that would stifle small business growth and employment opportunities.
I will continue to work with local business owners, our partners at National Federation of Independent Business, MD Restaurant Assoc, MD Retailers Assoc and our State and Local Chambers of Commerce to develop legislation that provides incentives for business owners to grow and provide career long term jobs.
I will also work with the Board of Education to develop job training programs for our high school and community college students as these are springboards that can provide a direct career path to jobs right here in South County.
I will always fight to reduce the burden of government on our local business owners!

Education
Our children are our future heritage.
We must maintain a high focus on safety, classroom size, school construction budgets, maintenance programs and the voices and concerns of parents.
We must continue to strengthen a curriculum that better prepares our students for long term jobs once they have completed high school and or community college.

Keeping the safety of our students in mind, I have continually voted for any additional spending that would be spent directly on upgrading school safety and security equipment.

That's why I have continued to be a strong advocate for keeping Resource Officers in our schools.

Environment
The environment is imperative to our community.
The Chesapeake Bay is the largest estuary in our nation.
Unfortunately, the revenue derived from the Chesapeake Bay has been reduced to embarrassing levels for our state.
The state must continue to lead by example by cleaning and repairing its failing infrastructure and updating its pollution practices.
As your delegate in Annapolis, I was proud to support many legislative initiatives that did just that.
Voting for legislation that lock boxed Bay Restoration Funds and ensuring that those funds were prioritized for projects that enhanced water quality and clarity, were some of the first actions I took in Annapolis.
I have always said that the rural nature and beauty of South County is our gift to Maryland.
As your Delegate, I will fight to keep it that way!
