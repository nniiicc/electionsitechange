Environment
The environment is imperative to our community.
The Chesapeake Bay is the largest estuary in our nation.
In the last twenty years the revenue from the Chesapeake Bay has been reduced to embarrassing levels for our state.
The state must continue to lead by example by cleaning and repairing its failing pollution practices.
That's why I was proud to support legislative initiatives that ensured Bay Restoration Funds were prioritized to projects that enhanced water quality and clarity.
In South County, our rural beauty and nature are our gift to Maryland and I will fight to keep it that way!
