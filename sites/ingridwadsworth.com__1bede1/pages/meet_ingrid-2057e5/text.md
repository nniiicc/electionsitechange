Meet Ingrid
USA Today, in their article, May 29, 2026 about Grassroot Candidates wrote:
"Ingrid Wadsworth appears to be the ideal candidate for Democrats in the Panhandle."
I am running as the Democratic Candidate for Florida State House of Representatives District 3, which covers Santa Rosa County and the northern half of Okaloosa County on the Florida Panhandle.
New voice, new choice, for community over politics.
I am a retired executive, military widow, microbiologist, farmer, and now an author.
My two executive positions, over the past 25 years, required me to interact with Federal, State and local elected officials regularly and successfully, which gave me direct opportunities to build my unique skills in getting the job done.
As Deputy Director, I negotiated our initiatives “on the Hill” in Washington DC, meeting with House and Senate staffers.
As Executive Director, I pushed initiatives at the state levels and collaborated with all Federal and State agencies on the large-scale public construction projects we built.
And, as a USEPA employee, I participated directly in turning legislation into binding regulations in the CFR (Code of Federal Regulations).
I know how to do this stuff - and District 3 would benefit immensely from my 30 years of experience.
Additionally, having had a farm in Wisconsin, before moving here to the panhandle, supporting our local producers who support us, is of critical concern to me, especially since so much of Santa Rosa and Okaloosa Counties is rural.
I am a Navarre and Santa Rosa County resident by choice, not chance.
I love the panhandle for all its beauty and potential, and being among the military and veteran community - a value my late Air Force veteran husband demonstrated for me - feels like home and supporting this community will be part of my campaign commitment.
I am a 23 years+ Rotarian, now in Navarre Rotary, and an active member of Impact100, which supports non-profit work throughout our area.
This exposure has helped me see the needs in our communities, and especially now, affordability is particularly hitting families hard.
I want to know more, from you, so we can fix these unmet needs.
I'm running to represent everyone.
Both our US Constitution, and the Florida Constitution begin with the same 3 words: WE THE PEOPLE.
Isn’t it time that we elected a candidate that remembers that elected officials work for WE THE PEOPLE?
I am that candidate.
