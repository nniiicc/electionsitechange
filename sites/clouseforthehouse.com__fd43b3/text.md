Steve Clouse is a True Wiregrass Conservative
Steve Clouse, a lifelong resident of Ozark, Alabama, has dedicated more than three decades to serving the people of Dale and Houston Counties in the Alabama House of Representatives.
First elected in 1994 at the age of 38, Clouse has become one of the most experienced and respected legislators in the state, now serving his eighth four-year term.
Over the course of his 32-year tenure, Clouse has represented his home region with consistency, integrity, and a deep understanding of the needs of the Wiregrass.
Today, he stands as the Dean of the Wiregrass legislative delegation and is widely regarded as its steady leader and guiding voice.
Leadership and Legislative Impact
Representative Clouse currently serves as Chairman of the House Transportation, Utilities, and Infrastructure Committee.
He previously held one of the most influential positions in state government as Chairman of the House Ways and Means General Fund Committee, where he was responsible for crafting Alabama’s General Fund Budget for over a decade.
His leadership has helped shape critical investments across the region, including:
- Support for local organizations such as the Friends of Fort Rucker
- Funding for volunteer fire departments across Dale and Houston Counties
- Expanded access to healthcare through free breast and cervical screenings for underinsured women
- Transportation assistance for disabled veterans traveling to VA clinics
- Development of an Innovation Center in Dothan benefiting Wiregrass schools
- Establishment of a regional mental health crisis center
- Creation of a state forensics lab serving local law enforcement
- Renovation of the Alabama Aviation College in Ozark
A Foundation Built at Home
Steve Clouse is, at his core, a hometown leader.
Born and raised in Ozark, he built his life and career in the community he serves.
He worked alongside his father, Jimmy Clouse, in the family oil distribution business and remains deeply rooted in the traditions and values of Dale County.
A devoted member of First Methodist Church, Clouse and his wife, Diane, live on Main Street in Ozark.
Known for his accessibility, he has long maintained an open-door approach to public service — welcoming constituents and working personally to help solve their problems.
A Lifetime of Relationships and Trust
Clouse’s approach to leadership reflects the character of the Wiregrass itself — personal, principled, and grounded in relationships.
Known for his honesty and integrity, he has earned the trust of colleagues and constituents alike.
His long-standing friendships and deep ties to the community reflect a lifetime of shared experiences and service.
Whether working on policy in Montgomery or helping a constituent back home, Steve Clouse has remained committed to the people he represents.
A Legacy of Service
After more than three decades in office, Steve Clouse continues to be a driving force for progress in Southeast Alabama.
His experience, leadership, and unwavering dedication ensure that the Wiregrass has a strong and effective voice in the Alabama Legislature.
As he reaches this milestone year, his career stands as a testament to a life devoted to public service, community, and the enduring values of the people he represents.
