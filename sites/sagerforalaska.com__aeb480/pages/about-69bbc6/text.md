I’m Dan Sager and I'm running for State House District 18.
I’ve called Anchorage home for 13 years.
I recently married, and my wife and I just welcomed our first child in May.
My work as a Territory Manager for a building materials manufacturer has provided the privilege of traveling throughout this beautiful state.
I serve on the Housing, Homelessness and Neighborhood Development Commission and am active in the Government Hill Community Council.
I’m also a real estate investor and am heavily committed to helping Anchorage grow and thrive.
I strongly believe in individual liberty, limited government, and maximizing the potential of our state.
