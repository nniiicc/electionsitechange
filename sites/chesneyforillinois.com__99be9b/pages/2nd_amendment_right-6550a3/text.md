I am a hunter and a member of a shooting range.
I hold concealed weapons permits in both Arizona and Illinois and am a Life Member of the NRA.
I deeply understand and appreciate the rights and responsibilities outlined in the 2nd Amendment.
I firmly oppose any legislation that restricts 2nd Amendment rights.
I have never voted in favor of legislation that restricts these freedoms for lawful gun owners, and I vocalize my opposition when bills seek to infringe on these rights.
