Discussions focused on fraud, affordability, oversight of state programs, taxes, public safety, and community development.
Smith on Fraud “I combat fraud for a living,” Smith said.
“We can expand protections for whistleblowers, close policy loopholes that fraudsters exploit, and have stronger and more effective penalties when fraud does occur.” Smith also noted he worked as a citizen to draft fraud prevention legislation in 2025.
Smith on Affordability During the forum Smith highlighted affordability issues like housing, health care and childcare costs.
He discussed increasing housing supply by reducing “red tape,” supporting first-time buyers with grants or tax credits, lowering prescription drug costs and revisiting insurance prior-authorization rules to reduce premiums.
He also wants to consider targeted tax relief.
“There are real things we can do,” Smith said.
