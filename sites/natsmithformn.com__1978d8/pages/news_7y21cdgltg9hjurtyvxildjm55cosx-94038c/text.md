Nat Smith’s campaign is being built by working Minnesotans.
An average donation of just over $100 is proof that real people, not special interests, are powering this race.
That kind of support has the campaign well positioned to reach its fundraising goals and bring this district new leadership come November.
People are invested in Nat's vision for Senate District 33.
Outraising Karin Housley, a state senator of over ten years and former U.S.
Senate candidate, signals a strong grassroots campaign with the growing momentum needed to win.
"As a whistleblower attorney, I’ve spent my career fighting fraud and standing up for working families.
I’m grateful for the neighbors across the St.
Croix River Valley who are investing in this campaign because they believe we need a state senator who will put people first, make government work better, and do the hard work to deliver real results,” Nat Smith said.
“These fundraising numbers show our campaign continues to gain momentum because people are ready for a new, independent voice in the Minnesota Senate.
One focused on solving problems, protecting taxpayers, and putting our community ahead of partisan politics." Nat Smith is an attorney who has dedicated his career to combating fraud nationwide in order to ensure that Americans receive the best possible patient care.
Through his advocacy at the state capitol, Smith continues to focus on building safer communities, supporting working families, and delivering meaningful, common-sense reforms for Minnesotans.
