Nat Smith Secures DFL Endorsment at SD33 Convention
Nat Smith earned the DFL endorsement for Senate District 33 at the party’s district convention held Saturday, April 4th at Mahtomedi High School.
He secured the endorsement on the first ballot with unanimous support from delegates across the district.
“This race is about creating a brighter future for Minnesota and delivering real results.
Together we can make sure that people, not politics, are at the center of decision making,” said Nat Smith.
“I’m honored to have the support of DFL delegates and look forward to carrying our values forward to November.”
