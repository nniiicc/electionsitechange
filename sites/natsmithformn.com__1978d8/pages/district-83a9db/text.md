Minnesota’s Senate District 33
About our District
District 33 covers a large area of the St.
Croix River Valley, including Stillwater, Forest Lake, Bayport, Mahtomedi, Dellwood, Scanida, May Township, Marine on St.
Croix, Hugo, Willernie, Oak Park Heights, Stillwater Township, and surrounding communities in Washington County.
It’s home to William O’Brien State Park, along with a number of regional parks, trials, and lakes.
Minnesota's Strong Democratic Tradition
Civic pride is a way of life here in Minnesota — we love our state and know how important it is to make each and every voice heard in our democratic process.
That’s why The North Star State consistently has the highest voter turnout rates in the country.
In fact, our state has led the United States in voter turnout in the last two presidential elections, with a record-setting 79.96% of eligible voters in 2020 and 76.35% in 2024.
There’s a lot of work to ensure Minnesota’s elections stay safe and secure through investing in IT security and utilizing paper ballots.
For more information on election security and fairness, please visit the website of Minnesota’s Secretary of State.

Your Registration
You can register to vote on Election Day in Minnesota, but if you’d like to save time, you can register in advance.
Request an
Absentee Ballot
Minnesota allows you to vote early via an absentee ballot starting 46 days before Election Day.
You can request an absentee ballot online for federal, state, and municipal elections.

Polling Place
If you prefer to vote in-person, you can find your polling place through the Secretary of State’s website.
