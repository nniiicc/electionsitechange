A proud son of generations of coal miners, Josh knows firsthand the struggles and the strength of our rural communities.
Growing up in Ashford, a small, unincorporated coal community in Boone County, Josh watched as the "War on Coal" devastated local families.
He saw his own father injured in the mines and his family nearly lose everything.
These experiences didn't break him—they forged his resolve.
In 2020, Josh did what many said was impossible: he defeated a popular incumbent with decades of service in elected office to become the youngest elected official in county history and one of the youngest people ever elected to the West Virginia Legislature.
Why?
Because Boone County was ready for a voice that actually listened and shared their values.
Josh has since been re-elected twice, serving his third term in the House of Delegates and also serves as Chairman of the West Virginia Republican Party, making him the youngest State Party Chairman in the history of the Republican Party.
