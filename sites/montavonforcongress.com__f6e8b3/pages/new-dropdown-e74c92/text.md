meet matthew montavon
Bringing real-world experience, fiscal accountability, and common-sense leadership to Southwest Florida.
Why MAtthew?
Born in South Bend, Indiana, into a family of eleven, including his loving parents, Matthew learned the value of cooperation early.
Throughout his life, Matthew has worked tirelessly in service of his family and his community-- and will continue that service as your representative in Washington.
A BACKGROUND BUILT ON HARD WORK
- Since high school Matthew began working odd jobs, farmhand, janitor, messenger, and store clerk, to help support himself.
- He graduated with honors from the Catholic University of America with his Bachelor’s in Political Science and Spanish
- Continued his education at Indiana University, earning his Masters in Public Administration while completing two studies for the Indiana State government.
A LIFETIME OF PUBLIC SERVICE
- Matthew spent two years in the Peace Corps supporting rural agricultural communities in Central America.
- Provided critical support to migrant farm workers through the Illinois Migrant Council.
- 6 years working with regional planning councils in Martinsburg, WV and Lynchburg, VA, as well as the National Association of Regional Councils and the Government Finance Officers Association Research Center.
- 27 years with the UN Food and Agriculture Organization supporting programs and activities fighting hunger and improving food systems worldwide.
UNDERSTANDING HOW GOVERNMENT ACTUALLY WORKS
- Authored multiple publications and studies for improving public policy and services
- Extensive experience navigating local, state, and federal government interactions
- Years of urban and regional planning expertise working across West Virginia, Virginia and Washington, D.C.
- Authored comprehensive fiscal studies and plans for community development, block grant applications, and business directories.
AN ACTIVE MEMBER OF OUR COMMUNITY
- A resident of our district since the 1990s, Matthew has made Southwest Florida his home.
- Dedicated volunteer with AARP’s Tax-Aide program, Habitat for Humanity, and local hurricane relief groups.
- Co-founder of the Immigration Justice Coalition of Southwest Florida.
A LIFETIME DEMOCRAT WHO GETS THINGS DONE
- Matthew has worked to support democratic campaigns since before he could vote—starting with George McGovern.
- Promoted voter registration and participation among the American community overseas as chair of the Democrats Abroad Rome Chapter.
- President and active member of Sarasota Democratic Hispanic Caucus, building bridges and amplifying underrepresented voices in our community.
THE CANDIDATE WE NEED
Matthew brings what’s often missing in politics: genuine empathy born from experience, technical expertise in how government works, and a proven commitment to lifting up marginalized communities.
As our representative, Matthew will work endlessly to promote progressive policies and ensure that that the government works for the people-- not the other way around.
