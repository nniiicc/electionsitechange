This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

1) Information We Collect Information you provide: name, email address, phone number, mailing address, ZIP code, employer and occupation (as required by campaign finance laws), donation amounts, messages you send us, event RSVPs, petition signatures, and preferences.
Automatic data: IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, links clicked, referral URLs, approximate location, and other usage data collected via cookies, pixels, and similar technologies.
From third parties: voter or supporter files, social platforms, event partners, payment processors, and data providers that support our outreach and compliance.
We only receive what is permitted by law and their privacy policies.
2) How We Use Information We use information to: Operate and improve our website, communications, and supporter tools Process donations and comply with federal and state campaign-finance laws Send updates, event invites, advocacy calls to action, and fundraising appeals Personalize content and measure the effectiveness of our outreach and ads Detect, investigate, and prevent security incidents or misuse Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms 4) Email & Text Communications If you provide your email or mobile number, you consent to receive campaign communications, which may include automated emails and texts.
Message and data rates may apply; message frequency varies.
You can opt out at any time by using the unsubscribe link in emails or replying STOP to texts (HELP for help).
Opting out may not stop non-promotional messages (e.g., transaction receipts or legal notices).
Note: This section covers general consent and opt-out disclosures.
If you plan to run a full SMS program, we recommend adding separate “SMS Terms” on your site and linking them here.

We receive limited transaction details needed for receipts, record-keeping, fraud prevention, and legal compliance (including employer/occupation for required FEC reporting).
Contributions are subject to applicable campaign-finance laws and limitations.

Some information (such as donation records required by law) may be exempt.
10) Children’s Privacy Our site and outreach are intended for adults.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
