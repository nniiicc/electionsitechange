7th Worcester District | People First, Always
I grew up valuing hard work.
As a self-made entrepreneur and Selectman with over 6 years of local government experience, I've seen firsthand how rising costs squeeze working families.
That’s why I am running for State Representative — to lower everyday costs, champion small businesses, and bring the transparency we deserve.
People First, Always.
As your State Representative, Nick will fight to:
Make Massachusetts more affordable.
Nick will fight rising costs by holding utility companies accountable, expanding property-tax relief for seniors, and taking on policies that make housing, energy, and everyday life more expensive for working families.
Demand transparency.
Nick will fight tooth and nail to demand the legislative audit we overwhelmingly voted for in 2024, by a margin of over 72%!
Nick stands with the State Auditor's efforts and will ensure his office remains transparent and available.
Nick also supports the ballot measure requiring our legislators and the governor's office to comply with public records law (like everyone else).
Reform How We Fund Our Schools.
Nick will reform the Ch. 70 formula, so our schools get the state funding they need -without forcing communities to choose between cutting classrooms and raising property taxes.
Keep our communities safe.
Nick will work to put more officers on our streets, expand School Resource Officer programs, and ensure police, fire, and first responders have the staffing, training, and resources they need to keep our communities safe.
Champion Small Businesses.
As a small business owner, Nick knows firsthand what it takes to make payroll and keep the doors open.
He will cut unnecessary red tape, fight for affordable health insurance, expand access to capital, and make sure Beacon Hill works for Main Street.
