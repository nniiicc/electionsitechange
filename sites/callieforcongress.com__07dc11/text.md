Meet Callie
Callie was born and raised in Traverse City and Cheboygan.
After September 11, her high school sweetheart Matt enlisted in the U.S.
Marine Corps and served tours in Iraq and Afghanistan.
When Matt came home with combat injuries, Callie became his primary caretaker and advocate.
That work led her from the classroom to veterans advocacy, law school, and now a campaign focused on real solutions for working families across MI-01.
