Skip to content About Issues Contact About Issues Contact Donate Education Every single Arkansas child, regardless of their zip code, race, parents’ income, or disability status, deserves a world-class education, and today that starts with accountability for private schools and strategic investment in our public schools.
Accountability for public money given to private schools The Governor’s voucher system is actively funneling your tax dollars to private hands in our state.
The first priority should be accountability for every tax dollar being spent on education.
We have already seen waste, fraud, and even child abuse in the institutions receiving our education tax dollars.
This is unacceptable, and I intend to bring a voice for accountability in the current system.
I also support an income cap for recipients of vouchers, because the majority of recipients were already attending private schools.
This will reduce the reckless, endless spending on private education with our tax dollars, which is a concern I share with many legislators across the aisle.
Support for teachers to meet behavioral and mental health needs of students We have seen an increase in behavioral support needs in public classrooms over the last several years.
Public school teachers are being unreasonably expected to manage behaviors and meet mental health needs of students who are struggling, on TOP of educating effectively.
As a mental health professional serving kids and families in central Arkansas, I know how urgently our kids need these services.
I support increasing on-site behavior and mental health services for students, so that students can learn and teachers can get back to teaching.
Invest in special education and related services When students need extra help, our schools want to deliver, but the resources are not there.
The teacher shortage is at its worst in special education departments , and the students with the most need are affected the most by this and other issues.
We can solve this problem through targeted, strategic investment in special education and other services and supports for struggling students.
As a special education advocate who has worked with hundreds of families whose kids are entitled to these services, I am committed to bringing relief to teachers, students, and families who need it most.
