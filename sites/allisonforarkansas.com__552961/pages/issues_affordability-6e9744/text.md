With the right priorities, we can be a state where small businesses and working people thrive.
Suspend the state gas tax until the war ends Arkansans are paying the price of the President’s war in Iran every time we fill up our gas tank.
With no plan for the end of this war in Iran, and state leaders unwilling to provide any relief, hardworking people are struggling to make ends meet.
State government should protect us from the White House’s reckless foreign policy causing inflation and gas prices to soar.
I support suspending this tax burden in 90-day increments until the war is over and gas prices stabilize, because you need more money in your pocket now.
Other states are making this move for working families , and it’s time Arkansas does the same, especially going into holiday travel season.
Eliminate sales tax on family necessities Arkansans pay more in sales tax than nearly any state in the nation.
Eliminating the grocery tax was a good step toward relief for working Arkansans, but you deserve more.
Arkansas should also eliminate taxes on household family necessities to keep more money in your pocket when you budget each month.
We started with no taxes on groceries, and the same cut should be applied to other needs like over the counter medications, menstrual products, paper goods, and toiletries.
I am committed to solutions like this to keep money in your pocket and bring real relief to working families.
Lower Your Utility Bill My opponent sponsored the 2025 law that lets power companies raise your bill.
This bill allows you, the ratepayer, to foot the bill for infrastructure for data center development.
This legislation hurts ratepayers in a time when the cost of living is already out of control.
Using this law to increase your electricity bill was a decision made by members of both political parties, and it was the wrong choice for working Arkansas families.
If I’m elected as your State Senator, my priority will be what’s best for working families, not any party or special interest.
My opponent’s record shows his priorities are with data center builders and special interests, not working Arkansans who deserve relief in this economy.
Put Workers First I am a proudly pro-union candidate.
I support workers’ right to unionize to improve wages, benefits, and working conditions for themselves, allowing them to earn a living for their families.
It has never been more important to realize the strength in numbers that unions offer everyday people— that’s why I am proud to be endorsed by multiple unions in our state putting workers first.
Decades of propaganda and puppet politicians have prevented collective bargaining in Arkansas.
If elected, I am committed to advocating for workers’ right to unionize, including an end to so-called “right-to-work” laws in our state.
