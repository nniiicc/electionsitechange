Bring down the costs working families pay for healthcare Health insurance premiums costs increased 22% this year .
That’s the largest increase in the history of our state.
Paychecks aren’t keeping up with the cost of healthcare, and too many Arkansans are going without critical medical care because they can’t foot the bill.
Our state has a $655 million surplus; the Legislature can do more to bring down the price of coverage for Arkansans.
I’ll fight to bring costs down and push back when the state approves historic cost hikes.
Qualifying Arkansans keep coverage through traditional Medicaid Over 200,000 Arkansans could lose healthcare coverage on January 1, 2027 if the legislature doesn’t act.
The federal government has denied funding for our hybrid private/public model for delivery of coverage .
I support moving these Arkansans to the traditional Medicaid system to prevent any lapse in coverage.
While other options for keeping folks insured will be considered in the 2027 legislative session, this solution prioritizes patient coverage while lowering the cost of the program compared to the hybrid expansion we’ve had since 2013.
Expand postpartum coverage to 12 months (like the rest of the country) Arkansas has one of the worst maternal mortality rates in the country.
This is not only an embarrassment to our state— it’s an affront on our values of caring for mothers and babies.
Expanding postpartum coverage to the full 12 months for Medicaid recipients is an important step in improving our maternal mortality and helping mothers, babies, and families in Arkansas.
