My inspiration to run for re-election in Minnesota House District 7A is driven by my passion for Northern Minnesota and the Iron Range.
I am proud to call this area my 'hometown' and am humbled by this opportunity to run and represent the Citizens of 7A.
I currently serve the Citizens of House District 7A and it is the most humbling privilege of my life.
In the last couple of years I have become a loud and leading voice on the issues that matter to us here on the Range and Our Northland.
Growing jobs, economic expansion, education, and helping our communities thrive are the top issues I will focus and campaign on.
As a state, we need to break down the barrier between metro and Greater Minnesota.
Neither can thrive without the other and neither can survive without the other.
Growing our economy, strengthening our schools, and telling our story is what will bring our best days to reality.
Working together we can build a prosperous Northern Minnesota that retains our youth, builds our economy, and invests in our future.
I have been leading the effort to revitalize Our Northland and The Range to make it a place that can thrive for generations to come.
I have been utilizing my energy, passion, and resolve to lead our local efforts, while also balancing the needs of our great state, which in turn will build the best form of government that serves all the residents of Minnesota.
The Range and Northern Minnesota are the keys to the world of tomorrow.
Like the days of our past in building a nation, it is now our time once again.
The resources and people of our home can not only bring our best days to reality but our entire world.
I am asking for your support and trust to bring us there together.
