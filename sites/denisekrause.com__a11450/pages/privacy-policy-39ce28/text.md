This Privacy Policy describes how the Denise Krause Campaign (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and shares your information when you visit denisekrause.com.
1.
Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us—such as your name, email address, phone number, and ZIP code—when you sign up for our newsletter, volunteer, or make a contribution.
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use this information to send campaign updates, process donations via our third-party payment processor, and comply with Oregon campaign finance laws.
3.
Third-Party Sharing
We do not sell your personal information.
Data may be shared with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website or conducting campaign activities, provided they agree to keep this information confidential.
4.
Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
5.
Opt-Out
You may opt out of receiving communications from us at any time by clicking the "unsubscribe" link in our emails.
