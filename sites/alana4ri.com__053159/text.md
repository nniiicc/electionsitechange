Delivering Results,
Together
For the last six years, Senator Alana DiMario (D) has delivered results for District 36 by working alongside neighbors, advocates, and local leaders to turn community challenges into bills passed and budget dollars invested in solutions.
Her focus remains clear: practical solutions that improve quality of life and strengthen Rhode Island’s future.
This work is centered on three key priorities:
- Making Rhode Island more affordable: Raising wages, lowering utility costs, tax relief for seniors and working families, and expanding access to affordable housing and health care.
- Supporting Rhode Islanders at every stage: Strengthening child care, improving education, growing jobs, expanding access to mental health and primary care, and helping retirees age in place.
- Growing our local economy: Cutting red tape, making it easier for small businesses to succeed, and protecting our valuable natural resources.
About
Alana
At a time when people are looking for leaders who are willing to stand up for what’s right, Alana DiMario brings both conviction and accountability to her work.
First elected in 2020, she believes the future of Rhode Island depends on engaging people who too often feel shut out of the political process.
Alana works to make government more transparent, accessible, and responsive — so it delivers real results for the people it serves.
She lives in Narragansett with her husband and three children and is proud to represent Narragansett, North Kingstown, and New Shoreham in the Rhode Island Senate.
