March 2026 Petitions Article written by Mike Bannon for State Representative in the category Uncategorized Well friends, We fell short on signatures for the primary.
We were less than 50 signatures away from filing, but many doors had no answers.
But the battle is not over.
We still have the primary write-in process.
When you go to vote, write in Mike Bannon under the box for State Representative.
We are still in this fight to bring the people’s voice back to Harrisburg, but I need your help.
Please get out and vote on May 19th and write in Mike Bannon.
I am also looking for volunteers who want to help work the campaign.
If you are willing to help knock doors, spread the word, or support the effort in any way, please reach out.
Thank you to everyone who has supported this movement so far.
This campaign has always been about the people.
