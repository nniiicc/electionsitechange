Councilmember Julie Palakovich Carr called for a town hall meeting on diversity.
The public event is intended to be a forum to discuss ways for the city to be even more welcoming of its diverse population.
“This is our community; these are our neighbors and coworkers and children’s classmates,” she said of the city’s diverse population.
“It’s in our best interest if we can all be working together and hearing each other out respectfully and trying to make Rockville an even better place to live and work.”
