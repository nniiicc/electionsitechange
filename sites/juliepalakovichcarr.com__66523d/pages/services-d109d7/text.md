Need Assistance?
As one of your state elected officials, I am committed to providing superior constituent services.
If you have an issue or question about the state government, please reach out so that I can assist you.
Constituent Services
My office can assist with your requests involving state agencies.
And if you aren’t sure if your issue is a state problem, please reach out so that we can direct your question to the appropriate office.
Examples include:
- Difficulty in obtaining your unemployment benefits
- Problems with the Motor Vehicle Administration (MVA)
- Concerns about roadway conditions or safety
- Utility issues involving Pepco or WSSC
Scholarships
Each year, I award scholarship funds to residents of my district.
The scholarship is open to residents of Gaithersburg and Rockville who are full-time or part-time students at a community college, undergraduate, or graduate institution located in Maryland.
Students who attend school out of state may be eligible if they have a unique major waiver from the Maryland Higher Education Commission.
I offer two types of scholarships: 1) scholarships based on financial need, and 2) scholarships for displaced federal workers and contractors.
Learn more about both types of scholarships.
Additional scholarship opportunities are available from other organizations.
Sign up to receive information about my scholarship program.
