About Julie Legislation Accomplishments Endorsements News Contact Contribute Julie Palakovich Carr March 19, 2019 News Delegate Scholarship Funds Available Julie Palakovich Carr March 19, 2019 News Applications are now being accepted for the Delegate Julie Palakovich Carr scholarship.
The scholarship is open to residents of Maryland's 17th Legislative District (Gaithersburg and Rockville) who are full-time or part-time students at a community college, undergraduate, or graduate institution located in Maryland.
The scholarship will be awarded based on financial need.
Applications are due no later than April 30, 2019.
Please see the application for more details.
