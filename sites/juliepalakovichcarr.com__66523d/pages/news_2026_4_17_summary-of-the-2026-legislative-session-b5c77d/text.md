About Julie Legislation Accomplishments Endorsements News Contact Contribute Julie Palakovich Carr April 17, 2026 News Summary of the 2026 Legislative Session Julie Palakovich Carr April 17, 2026 News The 2026 legislative session of the Maryland General Assembly has concluded.
It’s an honor to serve as one of your state lawmakers in the Maryland General Assembly, especially during these incredibly challenging times.
I continue to be proud of the legislative work happening in Maryland, both in response to federal actions as well as the more routine business of our state.
Roughly 2,800 bills were introduced and 884 were passed by the legislature.
Nine of the bills I sponsored passed and one additional measure was included in the budget.
