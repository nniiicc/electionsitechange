Public Education is a Public Good
The purpose of compulsory free public education was to ensure that our economy had the skilled workforce needed to drive economic growth.
Funding public education is the core of a state’s mission in serving its citizens.
One of the most important factors young families consider when relocating is the quality of the school system.
If Iowa is to prosper and be a place that our citizens want to stay and be a destination for those seeking a better life, we must ensure that our schools not only attract and retain families, but that we graduate the workforce of tomorrow.
Iowa is failing our children and families.
Iowa has a budget surplus of over $1.8 billion!
A fraction of this money would move us toward creating world-class schools for our children.
We must be better
Those who are or want to start families are looking for a place that is not only welcoming to them but offers a higher quality of life:
