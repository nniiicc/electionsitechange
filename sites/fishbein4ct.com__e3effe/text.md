I am seeking to continue to represent the 90th General Assembly District which covers most of Wallingford, and all of Middlefield.
I am presently the House Ranking Member (meaning the House Republican Leader) of the Judiciary Committee, as well as a member of the Public Safety & Security Committee, and the Regulations Review Committee.
I am also a founding member of the Connecticut General Assembly Conservative Caucus, presently serve as its Chairman, and was previously its Vice-Chair for four years.
In addition to my state government service, I am also a member of the Wallingford Town Council, serving my ninth term.
In my professional life, I am a trial lawyer, practicing for 25 years in the areas of family law, domestic violence, civil and constitutional rights, as well as general civil litigation.
Throughout my government service, I am guided by the principles of smaller government, lower taxes, and less interference in the day-to-day affairs of our law-abiding citizens.
I believe that together, through these guiding principles, we can make Connecticut a more desirable place to live, work, and raise a family.
I hope you join me in support of those mutually desirable goals.
