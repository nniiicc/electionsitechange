2 Oct Friday, 10:51 PM · 2026 NEW LAWS TAKE EFFECT OCTOBER 1ST!
I wanted to let you know that several new state laws will go into effect on October 1st that may be important to you, your family or your business.
My priority remains protecting public safety, supporting our veterans, and keeping state government accountable to taxpayers.
Key legislative updates include: Enhanced Road Safety & Fines: Starting October 1, drivers face tougher penalties for certain violations.
Distracted driving is now expanded to drivers who watch videos on their phones, and drivers who violate storm travel bans face an increase of fines to $250.
Public Act 26-95 increases the penalty for a driver who is observed illegally passing a stopped school bus three or more times; they will now have their driver’s license suspended for six months.
Under a different bill (P.A.
26-120), distracted driving in a highway work zone will now be considered reckless driving, increased from being classified as a misdemeanor.
E-Bike Guidelines (P.A.
26-24): Establishes an exact 750-watt motor limit for e-bikes, restricts Class 2 bikes on natural-surface paths without local authorization, and excludes dirt bikes and ATVs from e-bike classifications.
Taking Care of Veterans (P.A.
26-35): Under the Hero Act, driver's license fees will be waived for any Department of Veterans Affairs verified veteran.
The bill also creates a Veterans Dental Care Program to help eligible veterans get certain dental services.
The bill sets an annual program cap of $3,000 per eligible veteran for covered services.
Consumer Privacy Safeguards (P.A.
26-64): Among other changes, this law prohibits a streaming video service from transmitting to a consumer commercial advertising audio that is louder than the video it accompanies, consistent with FCC regulations.

6 Aug Thursday, 10:33 PM · 2026 "Sales Tax FREE Week" is Coming Up!
Sales Tax Free Week - August 16-22, 2026!
I just wanted you to know that Connecticut's annual Sales Tax Free Week is back and this year will benefit even more families!
Beginning on the third Sunday of August and running until the following Saturday - this year it’s August 16 through August 22, 2026 - sales tax free week is timed in the run up to the start of the new school year when families are shopping for back-to-school clothing.
Starting this year, the price cap per item has increased, and new items now qualify as sales tax free.
Now, backpacks and cleated shoes will be sales tax free during this upcoming event!
This tax holiday applies to in-person purchases and purchases made online and applies to each eligible item costing under $300 (up from $100) regardless of how many of those items are sold to a customer on the same invoice.
Tax is calculated on the final sales price of each item after all reductions and coupons have been calculated.
You can find answers to frequently asked questions about the program on the Department of Revenue Services website.
