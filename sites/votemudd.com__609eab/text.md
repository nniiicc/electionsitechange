Meet Ben
Ben Mudd is a lifelong resident of Marion County, a pharmacist, and a small business owner who lives on the same family farm that’s been home to six generations.
With deep community roots and statewide healthcare leadership experience, Ben is running for State Senate to defend Kentucky values, strengthen small towns, and bring common-sense solutions to Frankfort.
