All her life, Lauren has championed issues central to her constituents in Legislative District 8: climate solutions, public education, affordable housing, voting rights, and LGBTQ+ and worker protections.
Lauren has been recognized as a national champion for climate action and collaborative governance.
As your State Senator, her advocacy for the people and against corporate interests earned her Progress Arizona’s “Rookie of the Year” and the 2nd-highest score in the Legislature.
When it comes to progressive values, Lauren doesn’t just talk the talk — she walks the walk.
She refuses to accept campaign contributions from registered lobbyists, corporate PACs (political action committees), utilities, and developers, allowing her to prioritize the needs of her constituents over the needs of billionaires.
