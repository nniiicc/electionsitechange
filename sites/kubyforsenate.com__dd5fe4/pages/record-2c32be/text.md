Worker, Housing, and Consumer Protections Climate change, unprecedented corporate power, and eroding healthcare access are squeezing working families.
I will not give up on everyday Arizonans seeking access to basic needs while billionaires continue business as usual.
Working families deserve stability, dignity, and protection.
These bills ensure that employers, corporate interests, and insurance companies aren't cutting corners and leaving working families behind.
SB 1608 - Eviction limit; heat; temperature settings Establishes tenant protections during extreme heat by prohibiting landlords from setting indoor temperatures above 82°F, allows renters to give 2-day notice (reduced from 5 days) to terminate their lease when landlords fail to fix AC, and prohibits evictions during heat waves because no Arizonans should be unsafe in their own home.
This bill is getting lots of attention !
SB 1464 – Family and Medical Leave Establishes a statewide paid family and medical leave insurance program for eligible Arizona workers so no one has to choose between their job and their family’s health.
SB 1465 – Omnibus Labor Bill Creates labor standards to protect against heat illness; Establishes a statewide paid family and medical leave insurance program; Right to Work repeal; Establishes apprenticeship pathways to occupational licensure.
SB 1607 - Fair Claims Accountability Act Protect patients’ rights to health insurance coverage by establishing a Healthcare Consumer Assistance Program to help consumers with denied claims, and by raising penalties on health insurance companies that systemically deny valid health insurance claims—putting patients over profits.
SB 1480 - Crime-Free Addendum Weakens Crime Free Addendums included in lease agreements to protect tenants from unjust evictions.
SB 1788 - Homeowners Insurance Bill of Rights Protects homeowners and renters from extraneous cancellations of their insurance and guarantees compensation from insurance in the case of emergency.
SB 1356 - community land trusts; study committee Creates a community land trust study committee for the purpose of identifying solutions for the barriers of developing community land trusts.

SB 1360 - ESAs; contract renewal; account closure Closes a student's ESA account after one year if the parent does not renew (currently, these accounts close after three years).
SB 1361 - ESAs; postsecondary education Requires an ESA account to be closed after the qualified student's high-school graduation.

SB 1606 – solar energy; balcony; requirements Decreases permitting barriers and provides protections for Arizonans using portable solar devices or "plug-in" solar devices so families can lower costs and take control of their energy future.
SB 1350 - outdoor-based therapy programs; grants Establishes "outdoor RX" Program with DHS to provide grants for outdoor-based therapy.
SB 1351 - energy measuring; reporting prohibition; repeal Repeals state preemption to enable cities and towns to require energy usage measuring and reporting for businesses and properties, if the city or town shall choose.
SB 1353 - monitoring program; fish contaminants Requires the Arizona Department of Environmental Quality (ADEQ) to monitor water quality through measuring pesticides, metals and other contaminants in sport fish once every three years.
SB 1354 - auxiliary containers; regulation; prohibition; repeal Repeals the state-wide ban on plastic bag ban.
SB 1357 - large electronics; recycling Requires a collection and recycling plan of any large electronic manufacturer upon registration with ADEQ; excludes small businesses.
Transparency and Government Corporations, developers, and utilities are taking advantage of public resources and infrastructure for private benefit, and people deserve to know the environmental impacts of projects in their community.
SCR 1053 - corporations; entities; political spending; prohibitions Prohibits political spending by corporations SB 1460 - agricultural improvement districts; mail ballots Requires SRP to notify and provide mail-in ballots to all eligible voters in SRP board elections.
SB 1508 - Utility Transparency Act Requires monopoly utilities to report expenditures on lobbying, advertising, and more that are paid with rate-payer monies because Arizonans have a right to know how their money’s being spent.
SB 1510 - nondisclosure agreements; information; prohibition Prohibits cities, counties, and the state from entering into NDAs that conceal a private entity’s (data center’s) water, energy use, emissions, or waste generation from the public.
SB 1509 - campaign finance reports; lobbyists Requires candidates to report contributions that are from lobbyists SCR 1016 – public utility commission Renames "Arizona Corporation Commission" to "Arizona Public Utility Commission" to better reflect its responsibility to the people—not corporations.
