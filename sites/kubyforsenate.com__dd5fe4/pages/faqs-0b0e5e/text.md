- I’m proud to reject any and all contributions from lobbyists, utilities, developers, and corporate special interests.
Arizona has a registered lobbyist database, where any person who received payment from entities lobbying for or against legislation must register.
These lobbyists are different from constituents asking their legislators to pass certain policies because they are specifically paid to represent oftentimes large-dollar entities and interests.
While I’m more than happy to meet with lobbyists to discuss policy, I don’t want to feel obligated to support their legislation.
That’s why I reject their contributions.
Utility companies also utilize contributions to candidates as an arm of their legislative outreach/agenda.
For the same reason, I reject their money as not to feel beholden to their interests.
Finally, corporate PACs are organizations (political action committees) established by corporations to push their policy interests.
While corporations cannot donate directly to candidates or PACs, these corporate PACs are established to funnel donations from members of the corporation to their political interests.
On the other hand, union/grassroots PACs are organizations not connected to the interests of corporations, but rather labor unions or grassroots organizations like the Sierra Club.
These PACs are established by the people and advocate for the people: worker protections, climate solutions, and other grassroots policies.
I’m proud to stand with the people and reject corporate PAC donations.
