The State Budget: Why I voted YES!
After a session of bad bills and long budget negotiations, I was energized to take positive action, fight alongside my Democratic colleagues for common-sense budget wins, and bring an additional $2M to my district in support of homelessness services (stay tuned).
I understand the importance of providing services for the unhoused; after college, I worked for Kathleen Kennedy (RFK’s daughter) to create a Homeless Hotline in Boston, making this win extremely meaningful for me.
I was proud to bring back wins for our district — take a look!
