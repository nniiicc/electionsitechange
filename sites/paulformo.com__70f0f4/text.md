I will always vote to fully fund our public schools and support Universal Pre-K to ensure every child in Missouri starts strong.
Also expand vocational "Innovation Pathways" that let high schoolers earn college credits in tech and healthcare and end the horrific practice of school lunch debt.
No Missourian should be one medical emergency away from bankruptcy.
I will fight for a "Patient's Bill of Rights" that caps out-of-pocket costs for essential medications like insulin and ensures primary care access for all.
Champion a state-level paid sick leave and increased minimum wage that the voters already approved so no Missourian has to choose between their paycheck and their health.
Protect the state income tax to not explode the deficit and burden working families and seniors with a sales and use tax hike.
Investing in safer roads, pedestrian infrastructure, expanding public transit options, and clean, sustainable energy.
These are more cost effective and will make our neighborhoods quieter, our air cleaner, and the whole state safer for everyone.
