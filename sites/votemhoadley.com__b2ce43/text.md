About Mike
Husband.
Father.
Army Veteran.
State Representative for Michigan’s 99th District.
Mike Hoadley has spent his career serving his community and delivering real results for Northern Michigan.
As a State Representative, he has focused on practical conservative solutions — protecting taxpayers, strengthening public safety, and supporting local jobs.
Mike believes government should be limited, accountable, and focused on the people it serves — not politics.
He has consistently stood for constitutional freedoms, individual responsibility, and policies that allow families, farmers, and small businesses to succeed.
A lifelong resident of Au Gres, Mike lives there with his wife, Mary, and their two children.
He understands the priorities of the 99th District because he lives them every day — and he continues to fight for the communities he calls home.
