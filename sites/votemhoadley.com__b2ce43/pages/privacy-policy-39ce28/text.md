DONATE PRIVACY POLICY October 28, 2023 ​ Overview ​ Welcome to the official website and mobile application (referred to as "the Sites") of the Committee to Elect Mike Hoadley. ​ This Privacy Policy delineates our procedures for gathering, utilizing, and disclosing your information when you interact with our Site and other Committee to Elect Mike Hoadley mobile apps displaying this policy.
By using these Sites, you consent to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service governing your use of the Sites.
We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically, so we recommend checking the Site for updates.
Changes will be indicated by the "last updated" date.
Your continued use of the Site affirms your acceptance of any modifications to this Privacy Policy. ​ Information Collection and Methods ​ Voluntary Information: We may collect personal information that you willingly provide, such as your name, email address, postal address, phone number, mobile number, and geographic location.
This data may be furnished when you request information, register, make a donation, send us an email, subscribe to email or text message updates, connect through social media, complete a form, or for other purposes.
We may also collect demographic information, such as gender, date of birth, occupation, employer name, and zip code.
In limited situations, we may collect payment information, like a credit card number, if necessary for a requested service or transaction.
Voluntary personal information does not encompass aggregate data, which refers to data about groups or categories of users with individual identifying information removed. ​ By requesting information via your mobile phone, we may obtain information such as your cell phone number, carrier name, and the date, time, and content of your messages in connection with our SMS service.
We will use this information in compliance with this Privacy Policy. ​ Automatically Generated Information: We may also gather non-personally identifiable information generated automatically during your visits to the Site or when our ads are displayed elsewhere on the internet.
This data may include your IP address, web pages visited before and after our Site, date and time of access, domain type, your mobile device's specifications, unique device ID, web pages viewed, links clicked within the Site, and interactions with our ads.
Various technologies, such as cookies and pixels, may be used to collect this information.
For instance, an IP address helps us determine the general geographical location of your device.
We utilize non-personally identifiable data to improve our Site. ​ Cookies and Other Technologies ​ Overview: We employ cookies and other technologies to gather specific information when your web browser accesses the Site or other websites within our network.
Cookies are small data pieces stored by your browser at the request of a website.
Although cookies enhance your Site experience, you have the option to block, delete, or receive notifications about their use.
When you watch videos on our Site, third parties may also place "flash cookies" on your computer.
Deleting or rejecting browser cookies may not remove flash cookies, necessitating a visit to https://www.adobe.com/products/flashplayer/security to disable them.
Refusing cookies could impact certain Site features. ​ Our website utilizes third-party vendors like Google Analytics to analyze user behavior.
Google Analytics uses cookies to collect Internet log data and visitor behavior in an anonymous form.
This information helps us assess website usage and create statistical reports for the Committee to Elect Mike Hoadley.
Google Analytics operates anonymously, never associating your IP address with other data held by Google.
We do not link any data collected from the site with personally identifiable information unless you explicitly submit that information through a form.
This analytics tool reports website trends without identifying individual visitors.
To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites you use, visit this Google page . ​ ​ Disabling Cookies: Your browser's "Help" section typically provides instructions on preventing cookie acceptance, receiving notifications of incoming cookies, and disabling cookies altogether.
Note that rejecting or blocking cookies may affect your ability to fully use our Site.
Web Beacons: Our Site pages and emails may include small electronic files known as web beacons, clear gifs, pixel tags, or single-pixel gifs.
These files allow us to count users who visit these pages, gather statistics about web page popularity, and verify system and server integrity. ​ SMS ​ When you opt-in to our service, we will send you an SMS message to confirm your signup. ​ You can cancel the SMS service anytime by texting "STOP." After you send "STOP," we will confirm your unsubscription.
Subsequently, you will not receive further SMS messages from us.
To rejoin, simply sign up as you did initially, and we will resume sending SMS messages to you.
If you forget the supported keywords, text "HELP," and we will respond with instructions on using our service and how to unsubscribe.
We can deliver messages to major mobile carriers, including AT&T, Verizon Wireless, Sprint, T-Mobile, MetroPCS, U.S.
Cellular, Alltel, Boost Mobile, Nextel, and Virgin Mobile.
Please note that carriers are not responsible for delayed or undelivered messages.
Message and data rates may apply, so it's advisable to contact your wireless provider for details.
