PANAMA CITY, FL — Today, with just five days to go until the August 18th Republican primary, Austin Rogers has surged into a clear, 10% lead in the race to represent Florida’s 2nd Congressional District.
A new publicly available poll shows Rogers leading the eight-person field at 25%, expanding a commanding lead over all competitors.
The results mark a dramatic multi-month rise for Rogers in polling—as voters across the Panhandle unite behind his faith, family, and freedom message, as well as his tough on China stance.
The polling surge demonstrates that Austin Rogers is continuing to consolidate the overwhelming majority of conservative voters seeking a principled, America First leader to succeed retiring Congressman Neal Dunn.
Key Takeaways from the Poll
- Rogers Holds Commanding 10-Point Lead: Austin Rogers leads the multi-candidate Republican primary field at 25%.
Rogers boasts the strongest brand in the race, sitting right-side up with 40% favorability to 17% unfavorability.
- Massive Campaign Momentum: Austin Rogers has pulled away from the pack in the final stretch.
Among voters who have recently seen, read, or heard news regarding the candidates, 40% report being more likely to support Rogers
Consolidating the Conservative Base
Rogers’ momentum has been bolstered by an unrivaled coalition, including backing from high-profile conservative leaders in the District and in Congress.
For example, Rogers was recently endorsed by House Judiciary Chairman Jim Jordan, House Majority Leader Steve Scalise, House Majority Whip Tom Emmer, Rep.
Greg Steube, Chief Deputy Whip Guy Reschanthaler, and Rep.
Derek Schmidt.
He has also garnered the most local support in the race, receiving endorsements from over 35 local elected officials and grassroots leaders across North Florida, including incoming Florida Senate Majority Leader, Jay Trumbull.
Regarding the polling momentum, campaign spokesman Michael Stwarka stated:
“With one week to go, the voters of Florida’s 2nd District are sending an unmistakable message: they want a proven America First candidate who shares their Christian values, will fight alongside President Donald Trump, and stand up to China and for the people of the Panhandle.
Austin Rogers is consolidating conservative voters across the district.
Austin has the momentum, the grassroots team, and the principled vision to deliver a decisive victory on August 18th.“
Austin Rogers added:
“I am profoundly grateful to the families, farmers, small business owners, and veterans across North Florida who have supported us.
The Panhandle doesn’t want a career politician or Establishment candidate—they want a fighter who will stand up to China, defend our constitutional rights, secure our borders, and get things done on day one.
We are going to keep running hard straight through Election Day.“
