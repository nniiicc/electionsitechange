Representative Greg Steube (FL-17).
Congressman Steube’s endorsement comes at a pivotal moment in the primary campaign, following recent polling that shows Rogers surging into first place and building strong momentum across the Panhandle.
Steube cited Austin’s unwavering commitment to conservative values, the Constitution, and working families in his decision to back his candidacy.
In endorsing Austin Rogers, Congressman Greg Steube stated: “I am honored to endorse Austin Rogers for Congress in Florida’s Second Congressional District.
Austin is a strong conservative who understands that serving in Congress means standing up for the Constitution, defending our nation, and fighting for the hardworking families who make Florida strong.
He will stand with President Donald J.
Trump, support our military and veterans, protect our farmers and small businesses, and never back down from the America First principles we share.” “There is no doubt in my mind that Austin has the character, conviction, and leadership needed to deliver real results for North Florida.” In response to the endorsement, Austin Rogers issued the following statement: “Today, I’m honored to announce the endorsement of Congressman Greg Steube.
I am deeply grateful for his support and strong conservative leadership for Florida in Congress.
I look forward to working alongside him and Republicans in Congress to support President Donald Trump and advance America First principles that will benefit all North Florida residents.” Paid for by Rogers for Florida Privacy Policy Contact: info@rogersforflorida.com Connect
