PANAMA CITY, FL — Today, Team Rogers announced a substantial broadcast advertising campaign to support their campaign to represent Florida’s 2nd District.
This ad, which will run across each market of the district, highlights Rogers’ district roots, his deeply guiding faith, and his commitment to President Trump’s America First Agenda.
You can watch the ad here.
“We’re excited for voters of Florida’s 2nd Congressional District to see what we know; Austin’s faith, local roots, and steadfast backing of the MAGA agenda proves he is the most reliable conservative in this race.
Austin will give President Trump strong backup and will provide a strong America First voice for the voters of the Panhandle in Congress.” said Rogers for Florida Spokesperson Michael Stwarka.
