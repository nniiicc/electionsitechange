The endorsement from Rep.
Emmer builds on a wave of decisive momentum for Rogers following recent polling that shows Rogers surging into first place among the FL-02 primary field, having the most local endorsers of any candidate in the race, and a new endorsement over the weekend from Rep.
Greg Steube.
Rep.
Emmer pointed to Rogers’ extensive experience working alongside conservative leaders in the U.S.
Senate as a primary reason he is prepared to hit the ground running in the House.
In endorsing Austin Rogers, Rep.
Tom Emmer stated: “Austin Rogers is the America First conservative we need in Congress.
His experience serving as Chief Counsel to Senators Lindsey Graham and Rick Scott has prepared him to hit the ground running and advance President Trump’s agenda from day one.
Austin is a devoted husband, father, and man of faith who understands that public service is about fighting for the hardworking families, individuals, and small businesses that make North Florida and the Panhandle great.
He will stand up to China and has the conservative values and leadership our Republican majority needs to keep America on the right track.
I’m proud to give Austin Rogers my complete and total support for Florida’s 2nd Congressional District.” In response to the endorsement, Austin Rogers issued the following statement: “Thank you, Rep.
Emmer.
I am deeply honored to have your support and endorsement.
Tom Emmer is a proven conservative leader who knows what it takes to pass critical legislation and keep our House majority focused on delivering results.
I am looking forward to working with Whip Emmer and our conservative colleagues in Congress to advance America First policies, stand up for North Florida, and support President Trump’s agenda on day one.” Paid for by Rogers for Florida Privacy Policy Contact: info@rogersforflorida.com Connect
