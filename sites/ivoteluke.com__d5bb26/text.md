Donate to the Campaign Donate to the Campaign Donate to the Campaign meet Luke Luke Del Castillo is a community builder, nonprofit leader, filmmaker, and candidate for State Representative in Michigan’s 92nd House District.
Luke has spent his career helping people bring ideas to life - whether supporting small businesses, strengthening downtowns, creating opportunities for artists, or building connections across local communities.
As the former Director of Place-Based Businesses at the Middle Michigan Development Corporation, Luke worked closely with entrepreneurs, local leaders, and community partners to support rural downtowns and small businesses across the region.
He also serves as President of the Royal Starr Arts Institute, a nonprofit dedicated to supporting Michigan’s film and arts community, and serves on the board of the Broadway Theatre in Mount Pleasant.
Through this work, Luke has seen how creativity, small businesses, strong local partnerships, and community pride can help places grow and thrive.
Luke is running because he believes Lansing needs leaders who listen, show up, and turn community concerns into real action.
His campaign is focused on making life more affordable, supporting working families, strengthening local economies, expanding access to childcare, investing in skilled trades and education, and making sure rural communities are not left behind.
At his core, Luke believes public service is about people.
It is about meeting neighbors where they are, understanding what they are going through, and fighting for practical solutions that help families, workers, small businesses, and communities build a better future.
