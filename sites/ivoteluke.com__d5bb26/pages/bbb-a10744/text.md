Bonfires, Board Games & Brews is a simple way for neighbors to gather, meet Luke, talk about the issues that matter, and help build a people-powered campaign across the 92nd District.
Whether you want to host something in your backyard, around a kitchen table, or at a local brewery, we will help make it easy.
Host a bonfire Bring people together in a relaxed, welcoming space.
A bonfire is a great way to invite friends, neighbors, and family to meet Luke, ask questions, and talk about what our communities need.
This does not need to be fancy.
A fire, a few chairs, and people who care about their community is more than enough.
