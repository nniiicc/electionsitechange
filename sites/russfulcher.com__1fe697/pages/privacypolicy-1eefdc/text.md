PRIVACY POLICY
Introduction
The Russ Fulcher for Idaho campaign (referred to as "the Campaign") values the privacy of our supporters and website visitors.
This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to protect it when you visit our website or contribute to our campaign.
Security
The servers that house the Russ Fulcher for Idaho website are maintained with high-security standards to safeguard the information in our databases effectively.
We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.
Contributions
When you contribute online to the Campaign, the transaction is processed using encrypted code on a secure donation site, hosted on a secure and dedicated web server.
The personal information requested during the donation process is the same as what we would request for donations through the mail.
Importantly, we do not retain records of contributors’ credit card numbers.
Personal Information
We respect your privacy and ensure that any personal information you provide voluntarily, such as your email address, is collected with your knowledge.
We will not sell or share your information with third parties.
This site does not collect personal information about you without your consent.
When you visit our site, we automatically collect certain information, including:
- The name of the domain from which you access the Internet (e.g., yahoo.com).
- The date and time you access our site.
- The Internet address of the website from which you linked directly to our site or the Internet address of the computer used to link to our site.
This information is used solely for site management purposes.
Notice
Unless you choose to provide such information, we do not collect or maintain personal information about you when you visit our site.
If you send us an email message or complete a web form containing personal information, we collect and store the personal information you choose to provide, such as your mailing address, email address, and the content of any request for information or comments you may have.
Use of Information
If you choose to provide personal information, such as your mailing address or phone number, we may use that information to contact you regarding campaign updates, events, and other relevant information.
Cookies
What is a Cookie?
A cookie is a small piece of data that a website asks your browser to store on your computer or mobile device.
The cookie allows the website to “remember” you without containing any of your personal information.
Most Internet browsers support cookies; however, users can set their browsers to decline certain types of cookies or specific cookies.
Users can also delete cookies at any time by clearing their browser cookies.
Why Do We Use Cookies?
We use cookies to learn how you interact with our content and to improve your experience when visiting our website.
Types of Cookies We Use
We use both first-party and third-party cookies on this website:
- First-party cookies: Cookies issued from the Russ Fulcher for Congress website domain.
- Third-party cookies: Cookies managed by other parties, such as Google and Facebook.
Session Cookies
Session cookies are temporary cookies used to remember you during your visit to the website, which expire when you close your web browser.
Cookies for Advertising Purposes
Cookies and ad technologies such as web beacons, pixels, and anonymous ad network tags help us serve relevant ads to you more effectively.
They also help us evaluate performance reporting for advertisers.
Pixels enable us to understand and improve the delivery of ads to you and know when certain ads have been shown to you.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
The Campaign reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time.
Any changes will be posted on this page, and we will notify you of significant changes via email or through a notice on our website.
