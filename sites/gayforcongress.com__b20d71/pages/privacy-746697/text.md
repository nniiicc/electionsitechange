GAY VALIMONT FOR FLORIDA PRIVACY POLICY
This policy (“Privacy Policy”) governs the use of this website (“Site”), as well as social media accounts and other communication platforms operated by Gay Valimont for Florida (the “Campaign”).
Gay Valimont for Florida makes every effort to ensure the privacy and security of information that you share with us online.
We also encourage you to take steps to protect your personal information, such as safeguarding your passwords and logging off after submitting information.
INFORMATION COLLECTION AND USE
While using our Site, we may ask you to provide certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you (“Personal Information”).
Personal Information may include, but is not limited to:
• Name
• Email address
• Phone number
• Mailing address
• Employer and occupation (as required for federal campaign finance compliance)
• Information used to identify your social media accounts if you choose to share this information
Please do not send or disclose sensitive Personal Information, such as Social Security numbers, government identification numbers, financial account numbers outside of secure donation processing, information related to race or ethnicity, religious beliefs, health information, biometric data, or criminal history.
How We Use the Information You Provide
We may use the information we collect to:
• Provide, maintain, and improve our Site
• Process donations and transactions and send confirmations and legally required receipts
• Comply with federal election laws and reporting requirements
• Send campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, event information, and fundraising communications
• Send text messages (which may be subject to fees from your wireless carrier)
• Respond to your emails, questions, comments, and requests
• Solicit volunteers, donations, and support for Gay Valimont for Congress
• Connect you with other volunteers and supporters
• Conduct internal operations such as compliance review, fraud prevention, security monitoring, analytics, and improving campaign outreach
• Carry out any other purpose disclosed at the time the information was collected
HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION
We may share your information as follows:
• With vendors, consultants, compliance professionals, digital platforms, payment processors, and service providers who need access to perform services on our behalf
• With political committees, organizations, or groups that share similar viewpoints, principles, or objectives
• With organizations that facilitate communications among like-minded campaigns and causes
• As required by federal law, including public reporting of certain donor information to the Federal Election Commission
• In response to lawful requests, subpoenas, court orders, or other legal processes
• When we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect supporters, investigate fraud or abuse, or comply with applicable law
We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties, except with vendors who provide services on our behalf and are contractually obligated not to use such information for their own purposes, or if required by law.
LOG DATA
We may collect information that your browser sends when you visit our Site (“Log Data”).
This may include your IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, time and date of your visit, time spent on pages, and other analytics data.
COOKIES
Our Site may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience and collect analytics data.
You may configure your browser to refuse cookies or notify you when cookies are being used.
Some features of the Site may not function properly without cookies.
SERVICE PROVIDERS
We may employ third-party companies and individuals to facilitate our Site, process donations, analyze website usage, manage communications, and provide other campaign-related services.
These providers have access to Personal Information only to perform services on our behalf and are obligated not to use or disclose it for other purposes.
SECURITY
We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your Personal Information.
However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
YOUR CHOICES
You may opt out of receiving campaign emails by using the unsubscribe link included in each message.
Even if you opt out of email communications, we may still send important administrative or transactional messages.
You may opt out of receiving text messages by following the opt-out instructions in the message.
To update or request access to your Personal Information, please contact us at: contact@gayforcongress.com
LINKS TO OTHER SITES
Our Site may contain links to third-party websites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.
We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
CHILDREN’S PRIVACY
This Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 18.
We do not knowingly collect Personal Information from children under 18.
If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it promptly.
COMPLIANCE WITH LAW
We may disclose Personal Information as required by law, regulation, subpoena, or other legal process.
CHANGES TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page and are effective upon posting.
We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
