Morning Coffee with Todd Studer Mar 16 Written By Angela Griner On this episode of Morning Coffee, Todd Studer sits down with Eric Armstrong, a candidate for the Iowa Legislature in District 20, which includes Carter Lake and much of Council Bluffs.
Armstrong shares his background in business, why he decided to run for office, and what he learned while helping pass legislation at the Iowa Statehouse.
The conversation focuses on economic development, affordability, and the importance of listening directly to the people of the district.
