I want to Hear From You
This campaign is about the people of Utah and the future we deserve.
Our elected officials should be fighting for us—not operating behind closed doors or answering to special interests.
Local politics shape our daily lives more than anything else, yet too often they’re hidden, inaccessible, and controlled by insiders.
Many Utahns don’t know who represents them or what decisions are being made in their name.
That’s a problem.
Local government should be loud, transparent, and accountable.
It should be driven by real people, real needs, and real results.
I believe the public deserves honest leadership and open communication.
That’s why I want to hear directly from you.
Email me—whether it’s to share an issue you care about, offer feedback, or just say hello.
Your voice matters, and it should help shape the future of our community.
