Rich McCormick qualifies for reelection for District 7
The Forsyth County News reports that Congressman Rich McCormick has qualified
for reelection in Georgia's 7th Congressional District, which includes all of
Forsyth, Dawson, and Lumpkin counties, along with portions of east Cherokee,
north Fulton, and west Hall counties.
