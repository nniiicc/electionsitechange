Rich McCormick Dr.
Rich McCormick is a Marine helicopter pilot and Navy commander, an emergency room physician trained at Morehouse School of Medicine, and the U.S.
Representative for Georgia's 7th District.
Raised by a single mother, he worked his way through school, served in combat zones including Afghanistan, and cared for Georgia families on the front lines of the ER before bringing that same service-first mindset to Congress.
