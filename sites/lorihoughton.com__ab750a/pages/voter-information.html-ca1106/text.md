Important 2026 Vermont Dates
Primary Election - Tuesday August 11th, 2026 - Early voting starts June 27th, 2026
General Election - Tuesday November 3rd, 2026 - Early voting starts September 21st, 2026
Not Registered to Vote Yet?
Click REGISTER TO VOTE to visit the Vermont Secretary of State's office
How to Vote Absentee?
Primary Election - absentee ballots will be available not later than June 27th, 2026 and can be requested with the City Clerk or online.
Ballots will not be automatically mailed.
General Election - Ballots will automatically be mailed to all active registered voters, not later than October 1, 2026.
If you do not receive a ballot please contact the City Clerk's Office at 2 Lincoln St.
Where to Vote?
- In person voting for both primary and general elections at the City of Essex Junction polling location - Champlain Valley Exposition - from 7am to 7pm
- Prior to primary and general voting day ballots can be dropped in the ballot box at our municipal office located at 2 Lincoln St
- Returned by mail.
Please remember to give enough mail delivery time for the ballot to be received prior to election day
Important Information
- Follow the directions on the ballot and remember to sign the envelope!
- Remember you can vote for TWO Chittenden 22 State Representatives on your General Election ballot.
- You can check the status of your mail in ballot here: https://mvp.vermont.gov/
- You can register to vote right up until each Election Day.
