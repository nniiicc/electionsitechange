Fair Tax for All
In the pursuit of true fiscal equity that echoes the American promise of opportunity for every citizen, Jonathan Chavez champions the FairTax proposal as the cornerstone of “Fair Tax For All” in Georgia’s 13th Congressional District—a bold replacement for the convoluted federal income, payroll, corporate, and estate taxes with a simple 23% national sales tax on new goods and services, abolishing the IRS and its burdensome four-million-word code once and for all.
This system levels the playing field by treating every purchaser equally at the register, while delivering a monthly prebate rebate to all legal households—enough to cover taxes on essentials up to the poverty level, ensuring low-income families pay zero net tax and turning regressive burdens into progressive relief that rewards thrift and penalizes only lavish spending.
Workers pocket their full paychecks without withholdings, seniors enjoy tax-free Social Security and pensions, and businesses flourish free from hidden compliance costs, sparking economic growth, job creation, and wage hikes that lift every Georgian from bustling urban centers to rural heartlands.
Under Jonathan’s leadership, the FairTax isn’t just reform—it’s a reclamation of fairness, where the wealthy shoulder more through their choices, the middle class thrives unencumbered, and the vulnerable are shielded, forging a prosperous future where tax freedom empowers us all to build the lives we deserve.
