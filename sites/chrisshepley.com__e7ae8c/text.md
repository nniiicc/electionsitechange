About Chris Shepley
Chris Shepley is a dedicated public servant and community leader currently serving as a member of the Andover School Committee.
With a proven track record of effective leadership and commitment to conservative values, Chris is ready to bring real change to the Massachusetts State House.
As a lifelong Massachusetts resident and member of the 18th Essex District community, Chris understands the challenges facing our region.
He's committed to fighting for lower taxes, better schools, safer communities, and protecting the freedoms that make Massachusetts great.
