13, 2026 Media Contact: Nate Jackson (903-638-1874) [email protected] Colorado Fraternal Order of Police Endorses Phil Weiser for Governor Fraternal Order of Police (FOP) Highlights Phil’s Work Supporting Law Enforcement Training, Recruitment, and Mental Health Resources DENVER, CO – Today, Democratic nominee for governor of Colorado Phil Weiser announced that he has been endorsed by the Colorado Fraternal Order of Police (FOP), Colorado’s largest law enforcement organization representing more than 10,000 sworn peace officers dedicated to protecting the health and safety of communities across the state.
In its endorsement, the FOP praised Phil for being a critical partner in advancing effective, evidence-based public safety policies; overseeing the first major redesign of Colorado’s law enforcement academy training in over 40 years; championing additional funding for officer recruitment and retention; and working to increase funding for the Peace Officer Behavioral Health Grant Program to support officers exposed to traumatic events.
“Attorney General Weiser’s record of service, integrity, and commitment to public safety has earned our endorsement,” Micheal Deedon, President of the Colorado State Lodge Fraternal Order of Police, said .
“AG Weiser understands the realities facing frontline peace officers, and he values their experience and expertise.
As governor, he will continue to support robust public safety resources and effective policies that serve the interests of all Coloradans.
The FOP is confident that he will continue to be accessible, thoughtful, and collaborative as he works to keep Colorado safe.” “I’m honored to receive the endorsement of the Colorado Fraternal Order of Police, whose members put their lives on the line every day to keep our communities safe,” Phil Weiser said .
“Throughout my time as Attorney General, I’ve worked closely with law enforcement leaders and officers to ensure they have the modern training, mental health support, and resources they need to serve effectively and safely.
As governor, I look forward to continuing our strong partnership to keep Colorado safe, support our first responders, and build trust in communities across our state.” For more information about Phil’s campaign for governor, visit https://philforcolorado.com/ .
