Rob Swearingen for Assembly Home About Rob Issues District CONTRIBUTE Contact About Rob Rob is a former small business owner who is seeking an eighth term in the Wisconsin State Assembly.
A lifelong resident of Rhinelander, Rob attended Rhinelander Catholic Central as a youth and is a1981 graduate of Rhinelander High School.
He and his wife Amy have been married over 30 years and have raised two daughters.
Rob and Amy previously owned The Al-Gen Dinner Club in Rhinelander, where they enjoyed the restaurant business for 30 years.
In addition, Rob is a past president of his industry trade association and represented close to 5000 members across Wisconsin.
He held that position and was elected by the membership in 2007.
