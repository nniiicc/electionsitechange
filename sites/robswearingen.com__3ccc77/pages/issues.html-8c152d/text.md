Issues
| During his time in the legislature, Rob has worked to lower taxes, promote tourism, and address the issues facing rural schools around the state.
He remains dedicated to helping and serving the greater Northwoods.
Here are several issues that Rob will continue to work on: Taking Action on Affordability Increasing costs are squeezing family budgets which is why Rob worked across the aisle to enact $1.5 billion in meaningful tax relief for Wisconsin families and seniors.
He lowered income taxes, eliminated taxes on retirement income, made utility bills tax-exempt, and voted to eliminate taxes on tips and overtime to help you deal with the increasing cost of living. | Fighting for Rural Wisconsin Born and raised in Oneida County, Rob understands the challenges facing our community.
He voted to increase funding for rural roads and supported innovative solutions to improve access to healthcare and emergency services.
Rob also supported a budget that increased state revenue sharing to local governments as our communities deal with rising costs.
Prioritizing Clean Water and the Environment Wisconsin is fortunate to have an abundance of freshwater.
This vital resource not only powers our economy but also provides a clean, reliable source of drinking water to all Wisconsinites.
Rob worked across the aisle to secure the necessary resources to combat PFAS pollution in our water supply.
These funds will help municipalities and landowners identify, contain, and clean up dangerous chemicals that threaten access to clean water across the state. |
