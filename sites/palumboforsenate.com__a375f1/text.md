About
Anthony Palumbo
Senator Anthony Palumbo is working to make Long Island safer and more affordable for families, businesses, and seniors.
He’s led the fight against cashless bail and other radical policies that have made the state less safe and the job of law enforcement more dangerous.
As Senator, he will continue to lead the effort to reduce crime, strengthen our local economy and enhance the quality of life on Long Island.
As a lifelong resident of Suffolk County, Senator Anthony Palumbo is dedicated to protecting and serving our communities.
