OKLAHOMA CITY, OK — President Donald J.
Trump has officially endorsed T.W.
Shannon for Lieutenant Governor of Oklahoma, giving Shannon his “Complete and Total Endorsement” and praising him as an America First leader who will deliver results for the state.
In a statement posted to Truth Social, President Trump highlighted Shannon’s long-standing support for the America First movement and his record of conservative leadership:
“It is my Great Honor to endorse T.W.
Shannon, an America First Patriot… T.W. has proven he has the Courage and Wisdom to deliver strong results for the incredible people of his State and Nation… He has truly been with us from the beginning!”
President Trump also emphasized his overwhelming support in Oklahoma, noting he won all 77 counties in 2016, 2020, and 2024, and made clear Shannon is the candidate to carry that momentum forward.
“T.W.
Shannon has my Complete and Total Endorsement… HE WILL NEVER LET YOU DOWN!”
Shannon’s background includes serving as the youngest Speaker of the Oklahoma House of Representatives, National Chair of Black Voices for Trump, and as a senior appointee in the Trump Administration at the U.S.
Department of Agriculture.
“I’m deeply honored to have President Trump’s Complete and Total Endorsement,” Shannon said.
“President Trump transformed our country by fighting for the American people and putting America First.
I was proud to stand with him then, and I’ll continue that fight for Oklahoma as your next Lieutenant Governor.”
Shannon said his campaign will focus on advancing the same priorities outlined in President Trump’s endorsement, including growing the economy, cutting taxes and regulations, supporting farmers and ranchers, securing the border, strengthening law enforcement, and defending the Second Amendment.
“Oklahoma is Trump country — and we need leaders who will stand with him and deliver results,” Shannon said.
“That’s exactly what I’ve done, and that’s exactly what I’ll do as Lieutenant Governor.”
President Trump’s endorsement is expected to energize grassroots conservatives across Oklahoma and solidify Shannon’s position as the leading candidate in the race.
