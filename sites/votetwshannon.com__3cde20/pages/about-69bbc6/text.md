T.W.
Shannon is a sixth-generation Oklahoman whose life has been shaped by faith, family, hard work, and service.
Raised in Lawton, T.W. learned early that opportunity comes with responsibility—and that leadership means helping others succeed.
Those values have guided him throughout his career in public service and business.
A Fighter Who Delivers Results T.W. made history as the first African-American and Chickasaw to serve as Speaker of the Oklahoma House.
His experience extends far beyond the State Capitol. ✓ Youngest Speaker of the Oklahoma House ✓ Oklahoma Transportation Commissioner ✓ Former Community Bank CEO ✓ Senior Advisor in President Trump’s Administration From government to business, T.W. has built a record of leadership focused on opportunity, accountability, and results.
Putting Oklahoma First T.W. believes Oklahoma should be the best place in America to work, build a business, raise a family, and pursue opportunity.
As Lieutenant Governor, his priorities include: ✓ Growing Oklahoma’s economy and creating opportunity ✓ Attracting new jobs and investment ✓ Cutting unnecessary government red tape ✓ Supporting Oklahoma businesses, farmers, and energy producers ✓ Promoting Oklahoma across the country and around the world T.W. will bring his experience in business and government to the job and work to keep Oklahoma moving forward.
THE FUTURE OF OKLAHOMA T.W. believes Oklahoma’s best days are ahead.
Our state has the people, resources, industries, and values to compete with anyone.
T.W. wants to build on those strengths, expand opportunity, and make sure the next generation can build an even better life right here in Oklahoma.
That means a stronger economy, responsible government, thriving communities, and more opportunities for Oklahoma families.
