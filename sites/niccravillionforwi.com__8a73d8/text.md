Republican for Wisconsin State Senate, District 1
— NIC CRAVILLION
Nic Cravillion is a Northeast Wisconsin native, Army combat veteran, small business owner, and public servant running for Wisconsin State Senate to continue Working for Wisconsin.
Born and raised on his family farm in New Franken, Nic learned the values of hard work, faith, responsibility, and service at an early age.
After serving in the United States Army and deploying to Afghanistan in support of Operation Enduring Freedom, he returned home to continue serving the community he loves.
Today, with more than 15 years of military service, Nic continues to serve in the Wisconsin Army National Guard.
For more than five years, Nic has served as District Director for State Senator André Jacque, working alongside local governments, farmers, veterans, businesses, and families throughout the First Senate District.
That experience has prepared him to step into the State Senate ready to serve on day one.
Rooted in a seventh-generation family homestead and raising his family in Northeast Wisconsin, Nic is committed to protecting the values, freedoms, and opportunities that make this region a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
