A message from Bill Moskal, candidate for Florida House District 80 What District 80 deserves, in specifics.
No slogans.
No category-level promises.
Two priorities, with named bills, named line items, and a record of how Bill intends to vote. № 01 Growth we can influence, infrastructure we can control A decade of growth lifted property values, then handed District 80 the traffic, the flooding, and the insurance bills while developers moved on.
Roads, schools, water systems, and storm protection should lead development, not chase it.
The same pressure that overwhelms our infrastructure is what fouls the Caloosahatchee and leaves the barrier islands exposed to the next storm.
Growth should work for the people who live here, not just the people who build here.
Read the position № 02 Medicare, Social Security, and Health Care Costs Everyone Can Actually Afford District 80 residents are getting squeezed from every direction: Medicare and Social Security under federal pressure, prescription drug costs climbing year over year, elder care options thinning, and working families priced out of basic coverage.
Bill spent forty years inside the hospital systems that make these decisions.
He knows where the costs are manufactured and where they can be stopped.
