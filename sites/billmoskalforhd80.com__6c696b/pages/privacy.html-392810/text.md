We built this site to help neighbors get involved, not to track them.
The only personal information we collect is what you choose to give us through the volunteer form.
We do not use advertising trackers, we do not build profiles of visitors, and we do not sell or rent anyone's information.
Ever.
What we collect
When you fill out the volunteer form, you give us your name, email address, ZIP code, and how you would like to help.
That is the only personal information this site asks for, and you provide it on purpose.
If you donate, your payment is handled entirely by ActBlue on their own secure site.
We never see or store your card details.
ActBlue collects the information federal and state law requires for political contributions, and their handling of it is governed by ActBlue's own privacy policy.
How we use it
- To reach out to you about volunteering, events, and door-knocking in your part of the district.
- To keep you updated on the campaign if you ask us to.
- To meet the recordkeeping rules that apply to political campaigns in Florida.
We do not use your information for anything else, and we do not share it with outside groups for their own purposes.
Cookies and analytics
This site uses a privacy-friendly analytics tool that counts page visits without setting cookies, without tracking you across other websites, and without collecting anything that identifies you personally.
It tells us which pages people find useful.
It cannot tell us who you are.
That is why you will not see a cookie pop-up on this site.
We do not run advertising or tracking cookies, so there is nothing for you to consent to or opt out of.
How we protect it
Volunteer submissions are stored in a private, access-controlled campaign account that only authorized campaign staff can reach.
We keep your information only as long as the campaign needs it, and we take reasonable steps to keep it secure.
Your choices
You can ask us at any time to see what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it.
If you no longer want to hear from the campaign, tell us and we will remove you from our lists.
To make any of these requests, email us at the address below.
