Vibrant Communities Where Seniors Can Age in Place
Our seniors deserve the dignity of aging in place, with the support they need to remain in the communities they helped build.
I will work to expand access to affordable home care and strengthen services that allow seniors to stay connected to their families and neighborhoods.
I will also support job training, technology access, and age-inclusive employment policies that value older New Yorkers, while working to combat age discrimination and ensure retirees receive the full benefits they have earned.
