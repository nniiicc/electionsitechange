Affordable, High-Quality Healthcare
Healthcare is too expensive, and too many New Yorkers are forced to choose between getting care and making ends meet.
I will work to expand access to affordable, high-quality healthcare across the state, reduce out-of-pocket costs, and close coverage gaps so that every New Yorker can get the care they need.
I will also fight to lower prescription drug costs by increasing transparency and accountability in pricing practices, ensuring that seniors, people with chronic conditions, and working families are not priced out of essential medications.
Strengthening our healthcare workforce and expanding emergency medical capacity will also be a priority, so that all New Yorkers can receive timely, reliable, and life-saving care when they need it most.
