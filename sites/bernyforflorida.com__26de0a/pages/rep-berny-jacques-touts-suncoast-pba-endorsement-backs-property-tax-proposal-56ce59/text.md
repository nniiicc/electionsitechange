Berny Jacques touts SunCoast PBA endorsement, backs property tax proposal July 21, 2026 | by Kennedy Owens on Florida Voice TAMPA, Fla. — State Rep.
Berny Jacques defended Florida’s law enforcement policies, highlighted a recent endorsement from a local police organization and argued that local governments should prioritize public safety spending if voters approve a proposed property tax overhaul.
Appearing on Florida’s Voice Radio with Drew Steele, the Republican lawmaker discussed his campaign for House District 59, recent legislative efforts supporting law enforcement and an upcoming campaign event ahead of the November general election.
Jacques said his campaign recently held a “Super Saturday” door-knocking event and announced that he had received the endorsement of the Suncoast Police Benevolent Association.
Jacques, a former state prosecutor, said the endorsement reflected both his legal background and his legislative record on public safety.
“I worked with all of these agencies that make up the Suncoast PBA and enforcing our laws in the trenches, in the courtrooms, to make sure the bad guys that they’re apprehending on the streets are put away for a very long time,” Jacques said.
