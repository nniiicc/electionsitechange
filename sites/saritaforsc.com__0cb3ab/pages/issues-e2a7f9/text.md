Home About The Issues Endorsements Newsletter Contact Home About The Issues Endorsements Newsletter Contact Donate DONATE The Issues Fiscal Responsibility South Carolinians work hard for every dollar they earn—and the government should respect that.
I am committed to eliminating wasteful spending, cutting the budget, and moving South Carolina to a 0% income tax.
Families deserve to keep more of what they earn, and politicians in Columbia must learn to live within their means instead of growing government at the expense of taxpayers.
Improving Roads & Infrastructure For too long, Columbia has raised taxes while our roads crumble.
I support the Freedom Caucus plan that ensures every penny of the state gas tax goes directly to fixing and maintaining our roads - not siphoned off for pet projects or bureaucracy.
Infrastructure is a core function of government, and I will make sure South Carolina drivers finally see their tax dollars at work on the roads they use every day.
Pro-Life I believe every child is a precious gift from God.
I will always defend the unborn and work to protect innocent life at every stage.
South Carolina must be a place where life is cherished, families are supported, and our laws reflect the value we place on the most vulnerable.
Second Amendment The right to keep and bear arms is non-negotiable.
I strongly oppose any attempt to chip away at the Second Amendment.
Law-abiding citizens have the right to defend themselves, their families, and their property.
I will always stand with South Carolina’s gun owners against federal overreach and liberal gun-control schemes.
Education & School Choice Parents, not bureaucrats, should decide what’s best for their children’s education.
I fully support school choice, empowering families with the ability to choose public, private, charter, or homeschool options.
I also oppose divisive political agendas (like DEI) being pushed in our schools.
Our classrooms should focus on excellence, not indoctrination.
Closing the Primaries Republicans should choose Republican candidates.
It’s that simple.
I support closing South Carolina’s primaries so that only registered Republicans can participate in selecting our nominees.
This ensures that the Republican Party reflects the values of its actual members, not those of Democrats or independents trying to sway our elections.
Immigration South Carolina must never become a sanctuary state.
Illegal immigration threatens jobs, wages, and community safety.
I will fight to enforce our immigration laws, oppose taxpayer benefits for those here illegally, and work to ensure our state stands with law and order—not open borders.
The fight for our values is only just beginning.
