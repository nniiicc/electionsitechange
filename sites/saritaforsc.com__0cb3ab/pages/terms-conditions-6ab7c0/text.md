Sarita Edgerton is a devoted wife, mom of four, Realtor, and conservative fighter proudly representing District 34 in the South Carolina House.
Known as “The Hammer,” she earned her nickname by hammering down on fellow legislators who try to pull fast ones on taxpayers.
Sarita is committed to cutting wasteful spending, eliminating the state income tax, and making sure our roads are finally fixed the right way.
She is a strong defender of life, the Second Amendment, and the rights of parents to choose the best education for their children.
