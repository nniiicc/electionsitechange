Candidate for Governor of Florida
Frank J.
Russo
A Florida-born businessman, entrepreneur, insurance executive, developer, humanitarian, father, and faith-guided leader.
Why this leadership matters
Experience built around responsibility.
Frank brings more than 30 years of insurance and executive experience to a campaign focused on affordability, accountability, and the daily realities facing Florida families.
He has built successful organizations, managed teams of more than 500 people, and worked across business, housing, development, payroll, and operational leadership.
His test for government is direct: does it strengthen families, protect children, and secure Florida’s future?
He believes profitability and principle can coexist when leadership places people first.
