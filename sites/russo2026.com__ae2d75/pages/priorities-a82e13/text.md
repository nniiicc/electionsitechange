AFFORDABILITY Make Florida Affordable Again Florida’s success should be measured by whether the people who already live here can afford to stay here.
Families don’t experience insurance, housing, property taxes and everyday costs as separate problems – they all come out of the same paycheck.
Frank’s affordability agenda takes on those pressures together – with practical plans to lower costs, protect homeowners, expand opportunity and invest in the people who make Florida work.
Florida 9.9 – Invest in Florida’s future through a proposed 9.9% consumption tax only on spending above $1 million, with the first $1 million exempt and the resulting revenue protected in public trust for housing, teachers, healthcare, skilled trades and the future of work.
This replaces other taxes that currently take money while it’s being made; Frank’s plan harnesses money only when it is spent.
Property Insurance Reform – Put homeowners first with a more affordable, accountable and transparent insurance system – backed by Frank’s more than 30 years of experience in the industry.
Housing Affordability & Accountability – Build more housing working Floridians can afford while demanding measurable results when taxpayer dollars and public incentives are involved.
Property Tax Relief – Help Floridians keep more of what they earn, pursue additional relief for homeowners and hold government accountable for how property tax dollars are spent.
