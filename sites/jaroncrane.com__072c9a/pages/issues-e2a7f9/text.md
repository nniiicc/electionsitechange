Where Jaron Stands
Not just talk.
Real legislation that protects Idaho families.
ISSUE 01
Ending human trafficking
In 2023, Jaron carried legislation strengthening Idaho's human trafficking law by defining "pimping" and making the law more victim-focused.
In 2024, he authored a complete 42-page rewrite of the state's trafficking code.
The rewrite made the code consistent and victim-focused throughout, defined labor trafficking, clarified the responsibilities of the Attorney General's office, and gave law enforcement the tools they need to investigate and charge perpetrators of this heinous crime.
ISSUE 02
Securing our border
Jaron worked with local law enforcement and the Attorney General's office to find a way for Idaho to step up where the federal government has not.
He drafted legislation modeled on Texas Senate Bill 4 and moved it through the House.
He will keep working with Senate counterparts to move this and similar legislation forward, giving Idaho the ability to address illegal immigration head-on.
ISSUE 03
Protecting our children
Jaron carried legislation in 2023 and 2024 to protect children from material deemed harmful to minors in taxpayer-funded school and community libraries.
It took several attempts, but he persevered, and Idaho now has that protection on the books.
In 2026, he passed a law that is the first of its kind in the country and has since been copied by other state legislatures across the country.
This law protects children against the harms of addiction created by social media companies.
After this law went into effect Idaho has since won a settlement against META.
Idaho will receive over $127.7 million from META as part of a larger $17.1 billion multi-state settlement.
This is being compared to the 1998 Master Tobacco Settlement Agreement.
