Meet Jaron Crane
HIS STORY
A lifelong Idahoan, born and raised in Nampa
Ronald "Jaron" Crane is a third-generation Idahoan, born and raised in Nampa and the youngest of six children of retired State Treasurer Ron G. and Cheryl A.
Crane.
He attended Nampa Christian Schools from kindergarten through 12th grade, then headed to Bible College in Hobe Sound, Florida.
He transferred to Ohio Christian University in a basketball scholarship where he earned an Associates Degree in Business Management while playing basketball and golf.
FAITH, FAMILY AND BUSINESS
His heart beats for Faith, Family and Freedom
He grew up in the family business, Crane Alarm Service, working alongside his Dad from a young age.
After returning home, he went full time in 2014, expanding the company into seven surrounding states.
In 2020, he and his brother, Brent, bought American Fire Protection making Crane Alarm Service one of only a few companies in the state that could offer a complete one stop shop in the industry.
In 2022, the brothers purchased Crane Alarm Service from their parents.
