Privacy Policy
Last updated: September 23, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Jaron Crane for Idaho ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you.
This Policy applies to information we collect when you use JaronCrane.com and any other websites, mobile sites, applications, and online services that link to this Policy (collectively, the "Site").
We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time.
If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the date at the top of this Policy and, in some cases, may provide additional notice.
We encourage you to review this Policy whenever you use the Site to stay informed about our information practices.
Mobile Messaging and SMS Privacy
If you provide your mobile number and opt in, you consent to receive recurring automated text messages from Jaron Crane for Idaho, such as campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer opportunities, and requests for support or donations.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any donation or purchase.
- ✓ Message frequency varies.
- ✓ Message and data rates may apply.
- ✓ Reply HELP for help, or STOP to cancel at any time.
After you text STOP, we may send one message confirming your opt-out.
- ✓ Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except as required by law.
Personal information collected through the SMS program will not be shared, sold, or rented to third parties for their own marketing purposes.
Collection of Information
Information You Provide to Us
We collect information you provide directly to us.
For example, we may collect information when you make a donation, sign up to receive email or text updates, fill out a form, volunteer, request information, or otherwise communicate with us.
The types of information we collect may include your name, email address, postal address, phone number, mobile number, employer and occupation (as required for certain contributions), payment or credit card information, and any other information you choose to provide.
Information We Collect Automatically
When you access or use the Site, we automatically collect certain information, including:
- Log Information: the type of browser you use, access times, pages viewed, your IP address, and the page you visited before navigating to our Site.
- Device Information: the computer or mobile device you use, including the hardware model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- Cookies and Similar Technologies: we use cookies and web beacons (tracking pixels) to operate and improve the Site, understand which areas and features are popular, count visits, and measure the effectiveness of our communications, including whether an email has been opened or acted upon.
Information We Collect From Other Sources
We may obtain information from other sources, such as voter files, public records, and vendors, and combine it with information we collect through the Site.
Use of Information
We may use the information we collect to:
- ✓ Provide, maintain, and improve the Site.
- ✓ Process and complete donations and other transactions, and send related confirmations and receipts.
- ✓ Send you updates, newsletters, event information, and other messages about the campaign and issues we think will interest you.
- ✓ Solicit volunteers, donations, and support.
- ✓ Respond to your emails, questions, comments, and requests.
- ✓ Send technical notices, security alerts, and administrative messages.
- ✓ Monitor and analyze trends, usage, and activity in connection with the Site.
- ✓ Carry out any other purpose for which the information was collected.
This Site is based in the United States and the information we collect is governed by U.S. law.
By using the Site or providing information to us, you consent to the processing and transfer of information to the United States.
Sharing of Information
We may share information about you as follows:
- With vendors, consultants, service providers, and volunteers who need access to carry out work on our behalf.
- With organizations, groups, or causes that share similar viewpoints, principles, or objectives, except as limited below for mobile and SMS data.
- In response to a legal request, subpoena, warrant, court order, or other legal process, or as required by applicable law.
- To protect the rights, property, and safety of the campaign, our supporters, or others, including to prevent fraud or unlawful activity.
- In connection with any merger, reorganization, or transfer of our operations.
- With your consent or at your direction.
We may also share aggregated or anonymized information that does not directly identify you.
As stated above, mobile opt-in data and information collected through the SMS program are not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
Analytics and Advertising
We may allow third parties, such as Google, Meta (Facebook), and other advertising and analytics providers, to use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to collect information about your use of the Site and other sites over time.
This information may include your IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, time spent on pages, links clicked, and conversion information, and may be used to analyze usage, measure and target advertising, and better understand your interests.
This Policy does not cover the practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to review their privacy policies.
Campaign Finance Disclosure
Certain contribution information, including a donor's name, city, state, ZIP code, employer, occupation, and contribution amount, may be required to be collected and publicly reported to the Idaho Secretary of State or other authorities as required by campaign finance law.
That reporting is required by law and is separate from the marketing practices described in this Policy.
Security
We take reasonable measures to help protect information about you from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction.
No security measures are perfect, and we cannot guarantee the security of information transmitted to or from the Site.
Your Choices
Cookies.
Most web browsers accept cookies by default.
You can usually set your browser to remove or reject cookies, though this may affect the availability and functionality of the Site.
Email and Text Messages.
You may opt out of receiving campaign emails by following the unsubscribe instructions in those emails.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message.
If you opt out, we may still send you non-marketing messages, such as those about a transaction you initiated.
Children's Privacy
The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
