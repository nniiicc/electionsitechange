P Pillar 01 Preservation Protecting our natural environment, heritage, and multi-generational family properties through structural fail-safes.
01 The Virgin Islands Environmental Accountability and Commercial Waste Tracking Act The Goal Engineer a systemic deterrent against exploitation of our natural resources and hold violators strictly accountable for illegal dumping in territorial parks and marine environments.
The Mechanism Build a closed-loop enforcement architecture that follows hazardous commercial goods from purchase through lawful disposal and converts every transfer into an auditable record.
Chain of Custody Require commercial purchasers of bulk hazardous goods to maintain disposal logs linked to product barcodes and lot numbers, removing anonymity from environmental crimes.
Statutory Response Clock Set enforceable timelines for DPNR to acknowledge reports, test affected sites, publish findings, and begin remediation.
Strict Liability Apply enhanced penalties for dumping in protected conservation zones and ring-fence the proceeds for environmental surveillance and remediation technology.
Whistleblower Shield Protect residents and workers who report hazards so investigations follow physical evidence instead of targeting the person who raised the alarm.
R Pillar 02 Respect Ensuring transparency, strict government accountability, and dignified care for elders and vulnerable populations.
01 The Central Federal Grants Division Mandate The Goal Cure the territory's chronic project-delivery failures so awarded federal dollars become completed public infrastructure instead of expiring balances.
The Mechanism Execute a statutory administrative reorganization that centralizes grant accounting, reimbursement, compliance, and closeout inside the Office of Management and Budget.
Central Control Plane Create a Central Federal Grants Division within OMB with authority to standardize workflows, monitor deadlines, and intervene when an agency falls behind.
Public Digital Tracker Publish the award amount, obligated amount, money spent, project stage, next milestone, responsible agency, and schedule risk for every federal infrastructure grant.
Oversight Loop Create a Legislative Oversight Council and require quarterly performance reports to the Committee on Finance, including corrective-action plans for delayed projects.
O Pillar 03 Opportunity Modernizing the economy, supporting local entrepreneurs, and creating load-bearing pathways to homeownership and financial relief.
01 The Digital Economy & Banking Modernization Act The Goal End the financial isolation of the Virgin Islands and give local entrepreneurs the same digital tools available to mainland competitors.
The Mechanism Overhaul the territorial banking code to define and attract regulated FinTech operations, streamline chartering, widen payment-processing access, and force real competition into a concentrated banking market.
Modern Chartering Create a clear, time-bound licensing pathway for qualified digital financial institutions with strong consumer, cybersecurity, and reserve safeguards.
Payment Access Remove territorial barriers that keep businesses from integrating processors such as Stripe and Square, while preserving anti-fraud and consumer-protection controls.
Competitive Pressure Break the operational grip of local banking monopolies by giving residents and businesses lawful alternatives and forcing incumbent institutions to modernize.
02 The First-Time Homebuyer & Local Builder Initiative The Goal Combat the Brain Drain by making homeownership attainable for young professionals and anchoring generational wealth inside the territory.
The Mechanism Expand territory-funded first-time homebuyer grants and build a fast-track, reduced-fee permitting lane reserved for qualified local residential builders.
Buyer Capital Increase grant capacity and establish transparent eligibility, award, and closing timelines so approved buyers can compete for available homes.
Builder Fast Track Create one coordinated permit pathway with defined review deadlines, reduced fees, and a single accountable project manager for local residential construction.
Housing Supply Tie faster approvals to construction milestones and occupancy delivery, converting public support into more homes rather than stalled applications.
P Pillar 04 Preparation Future-proofing our infrastructure with disruptive engineering and building systems that remain functional under island conditions.
01 The Virgin Islands Plug and Play Solar Act The Goal Deliver immediate, decentralized financial relief to working families while building grid resilience outside WAPA's monopoly.
The Mechanism Authorize certified micro-tier solar kits that residents can mount on galleries and connect to standard outlets.
Prohibit WAPA from imposing interconnection studies, prior approvals, or standby fees on compliant systems.
Zero Export Mandate Require integrated smart shunt technology that prevents power from feeding into the WAPA grid and protects residents from billing errors.
Marine-Grade Durability Require NEMA 4X and IP68-rated hardware engineered against salt-air corrosion, water intrusion, and tropical electrical degradation.
Blackout Resilience Prioritize hybrid systems with battery backup capable of sustaining essential loads such as refrigerators and medical devices during outages.
Built-In Enforcement Require UL 1741 and UL 3700 safety certification at the point of retail sale, removing DPNR inspectors from the deployment path.
The Political Edge More than 52 percent of Virgin Islanders rent their homes.
Portable solar gives renters direct access to lower energy costs, nullifies HOA and landlord aesthetic bans for compliant systems, and turns resilient clean energy into a direct anti-poverty tool.
