I'm Rudi Hodge, born and raised right here in the Virgin Islands.
As an attorney, small business owner, and civic leader, I've spent my career working to solve real problems for our community.
I'm running for the 37th Legislature because I believe the Virgin Islands deserves leadership that combines fresh ideas with a deep respect for our home.
As an elected Delegate to the 6th Constitutional Convention and Vice Chair of the Human Rights Standing Committee, I'm helping draft a modern constitution to replace the 1954 Revised Organic Act.
This is the opportunity of a generation to shape our territory's future, and I'm honored to be a lead author in that effort.
I'm a barred attorney in the Virgin Islands and New York, with experience in corporate governance and digital rights.
I also bring hands-on technical skills in system administration and emerging technologies that can help modernize how our government serves its people.
Community service is central to who I am.
I serve as Secretary of the Board of Directors for the Family Resource Center (FRC), helping ensure governance for an agency that provides shelter to victims of crime and domestic violence.
I'm ready to bring that same dedication to the Senate.

TIER 1 Stabilization Foundational policies to stop the immediate bleeding in our most critical sectors: energy and healthcare.
Energy Generation & Transmission Bifurcation Act Restructure WAPA by legally separating power generation from transmission and distribution to force accountability, modernize the grid, and drive down utility costs.
Healthcare Revenue "Lockbox" Act Legally ring-fence healthcare-generated revenues so funds intended for our hospitals and healthcare infrastructure cannot be diverted to the general fund.
TIER 2 Accountability Initiatives that demand Radical Sunlight in government, focusing on transparency and restoring public trust.
Procurement Modernization & Speed Act (Title 31 Reform) Overhaul Title 31 of the V.I.
Code to streamline archaic procurement processes, speeding up infrastructure projects while maintaining strict, transparent oversight.
Civil Justice & Historical Truth Resolution Execute a comprehensive public audit of the Epstein settlements to ensure absolute transparency regarding the dispersal, management, and utilization of those funds.
TIER 3 Future-Proofing Policies designed to position the Virgin Islands as a forward-thinking, tech-forward jurisdiction.
Tamper-Proof Government Act Use modern blockchain technology so that once a government contract is signed or a dollar is spent, the public record can never be altered, hidden, or erased by corrupt officials.
V.I.
Digital Asset & DAO Recognition Act Legally recognize Decentralized Autonomous Organizations (DAOs) and establish a safe, regulated sandbox for digital assets to attract new, tech-based revenue streams to the territory.
