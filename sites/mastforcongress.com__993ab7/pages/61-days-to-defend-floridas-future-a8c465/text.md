The primary is behind us.
Now the real mission begins.
This election is about whether we keep moving forward with cleaner water, safer communities, stronger support for veterans, lower taxes, secure borders, and an America First agenda – or hand the wheel back to the same tired politics that put Washington first.
I have never believed public service is about holding a title.
It is about showing up, doing the work, and delivering results for the people you serve.
I have done exactly that.
I secured new accountability for every drop of Lake Okeechobee water sent into the St.
Lucie River and put the EAA Reservoir’s mission into law: end harmful discharges.
I wrote and passed the Medal of Sacrifice Act so fallen law enforcement officers and first responders receive the permanent national honor they deserve.
I passed tax relief that lets working families keep more of what they earn.
Those are not talking points.
They are promises kept.
But progress does not protect itself.
Every gain we have made can be weakened, reversed, or delayed if good people sit this election out.
Florida’s General Election is Tuesday, November 3.
Check your registration.
Talk to your family.
Remind your neighbors.
Make a plan now , because Election Day will be here before we know it.
I know what is at stake.
I also know what this community can accomplish when patriots stand together.
Sixty-one days is enough time to organize, build momentum, and defend Florida’s future.
Let’s get to work.
