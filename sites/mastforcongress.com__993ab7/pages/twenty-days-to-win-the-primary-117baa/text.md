That is not a far-off political date.
It is the next mission in front of us.
I have always believed public service is about showing up, doing the work, and putting people before politics.
That’s why I have fought to protect our waterways, stand with veterans, defend law enforcement, lower costs for working families, secure our border, and make sure Florida families have a voice that does not back down.
We have made real progress.
We have pushed to send clean water south, protect the St.
Lucie River, and hold the Army Corps accountable for decisions that affect our estuaries.
We have fought for veterans care, protected critical programs, and opened the kind of local support that helps veterans get answers when they need them.
We have stood up for the men and women who keep our communities safe.
We have worked to cut waste, lower taxes, and help families keep more of what they earn.
But none of that work continues by accident.
The Primary Election is Tuesday, August 18.
Vote-by-mail requests are due Thursday, August 6 at 5:00 PM.
Early voting begins Saturday, August 8 in Palm Beach, Martin, and St.
Lucie counties.
If you are voting by mail, request your ballot now.
If you are voting early, make your plan.
If you are voting on Election Day, know where you are going and remind your family, friends, and neighbors to do the same.
I am not on the ballot for the primary because I am running unopposed, but this campaign is not about one person.
It is about clean water, safe streets, veterans who earned better than excuses, families who are tired of paying more, and a country that is still worth fighting for.
Twenty days is not much time.
But it is enough time to organize, talk to voters, and make sure every patriot who cares about Florida’s future shows up.
Help Republicans in our community finish strong and keep fighting for the community we love.
