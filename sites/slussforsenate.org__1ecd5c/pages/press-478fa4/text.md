Bio
Jared Sluss is a candidate for Alabama State Senate, District 7.
A UAB public health graduate, he has spent eight years working in affordable housing and neighborhood development — as a small business owner and at nonprofits.
He is running to make healthcare and housing affordable for Alabama families.
Jared Sluss graduated from UAB with a degree in public health and has spent eight years working in affordable housing and neighborhood development — as a small business owner and at nonprofits.
He is running for Alabama State Senate, District 7, because loving your neighbor isn't a slogan — it's a responsibility that must be reflected in policy.
His campaign focuses on healthcare price transparency, housing affordability, and making tax dollars work for Alabama families.
