Meet Richard
Richard Goldman lives in Dresden with his wife Linda Hermans and their dog Leo.
He is a retired attorney who spent his career practicing bankruptcy, environmental law, and government and administrative law out of Augusta.
He and Linda have three grown children: Rachel, Ben, and Wendy.
For decades, Richard helped families, small businesses, and towns navigate the legal system.
He saw the difference between a government that actually works for the people who live under it and one that does not.
That experience is why he is running for the Maine House.
