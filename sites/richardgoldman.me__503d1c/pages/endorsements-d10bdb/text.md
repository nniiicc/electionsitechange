Why these endorsements matter
None of these groups hand out endorsements casually.
Most of them require a written questionnaire, an interview, or both, and they compare a candidate’s answers against their members’ priorities.
Taken together, they say something simple about this campaign: Richard will show up for the people who do the work in Chelsea, Dresden, Pittston, and Randolph.
Richard is running as a Maine Clean Elections candidate, which means this campaign is funded by $5 qualifying contributions from registered voters in the district rather than by large private donors.
