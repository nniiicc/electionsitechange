MSEA-SEIU Local 1989 represents state employees, university and community college workers, judicial branch staff, and other public and private sector workers across Maine.
The union awarded its endorsement after reviewing Richard’s answers to its legislative candidate questionnaire.

Thank you for your support for working families and the essential services our members provide.
The people who plow the roads, staff the courthouses, teach in our public universities, and keep state services running live in Chelsea, Dresden, Pittston, and Randolph.
