Planned Parenthood Maine Action Fund PAC (PPMEAF PAC) has endorsed Richard Goldman for State Representative in House District 53.
Planned Parenthood Maine Action Fund and PAC are the political arms of Planned Parenthood of Northern New England, a three-state affiliate with health centers in Biddeford, Portland, Sanford, and Topsham that serves more than 35,000 patients a year.
For many people in rural Maine, a Planned Parenthood health center is not a political symbol.
It is the place they go for a cancer screening, birth control, an STI test, or a routine exam.
Richard will vote to protect access to reproductive health care and to keep those decisions between patients and their providers.
Access to care is the through line of his health care platform.
