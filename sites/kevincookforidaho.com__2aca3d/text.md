Top 3 Issues Kevin Cook is concerned about: ISSUE 1 I am passionate about Education.
I want to give Idaho students greater educational opportunities.
My dream is to fill every Idaho job with Idaho students, who have the education, skills, and training to succeed.
ISSUE 2 Agriculture is a major contributor to the economics of Idaho.
I want to further international exposure for Idaho’s farm products.
The proper management of our surface water and aquifer is crucial.
ISSUE 3 I believe in limiting the growth of government and only passing laws and creating new regulations when absolutely necessary.
I believe the free market thrives when governments reduce regulations and allow entrepreneurs to implement the accepted principles of capitalism.
Issues Important to Kevin Education I want to ensure students have the tools necessary to be prepared for an increasingly competitive job market.
I support the ability our students have to earn college credits while in high school and will support these accomplishments and look for and listen to other educational opportunities.
Pro-Life I believe that life begins at conception and that new life is sacred.
I am endorsed by the most respected pro-life organization in the state, Idaho Chooses life.
Property Taxes As a homeowner, I understand the burden of ever-increasing property taxes.
I feel there is a solution to our property tax burdens and I am dedicated to finding it.
Small Business Small businesses are the lifeblood of Idaho.
I will work to keep government regulation minimized to help business flourish.
Agriculture Agriculture is a major contributor to the economics of Idaho.
I want to further international exposure for Idaho’s farm products.
The proper management of our surface water and aquifer is crucial.
I have the backing of Idaho’s most respected farming organization, the Idaho Farm Bureau.
Government - Limit Regulations and Laws I believe in limiting the growth of government and only passing laws and creating new regulations when absolutely necessary.
I believe the free market thrives when governments reduce regulations and allow entrepreneurs to implement the accepted principles of capitalism.
Second Amendment As a gun owner, I have and will strongly defend the 2nd Amendment.
I am also a member of the National Rifle Association.
