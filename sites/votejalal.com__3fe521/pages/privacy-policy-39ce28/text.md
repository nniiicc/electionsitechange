Donations and Third-Party Payment Processing All campaign donations are processed directly through ActBlue.

We use standard tracking technologies to understand how visitors interact with our website and to support advertising efforts.
These may include: Google Analytics Meta Pixel Google Ads These tools may collect information such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the site.
This data is used in aggregate to improve our messaging and outreach.
We do not knowingly use cookies for sensitive personal profiling.
You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.
Sharing of Information We may share your information with trusted third parties solely for campaign-related purposes, including: Campaign consultants and vendors Email and SMS/MMS communication platforms Digital advertising platforms The Michigan Democratic Party or affiliated political organizations, as permitted by law We do not sell your personal information.

Communications Opt-Out You may unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time using the link provided in our messages.
To opt out of SMS/MMS communications, follow the instructions included in the message or contact us directly.
Children’s Privacy This website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If you believe information has been collected from a child under 13, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.
Data Security and Retention We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including operating the Campaign communicating with supporters, processing contributions, complying with legal and regulatory obligations.
Certain information may be retained for longer periods as required by applicable federal, state, or local election laws, campaign finance regulations, tax laws, and recordkeeping requirements.
We use industry standard reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information.
However, no online system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.
Where retention is not necessary for campaign operations or legal compliance, we will take reasonable steps to delete or otherwise dispose of personal information in accordance with internal policies and applicable law.
