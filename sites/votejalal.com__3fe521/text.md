Meet Jalal
Jalal Abdallah is a lifelong Dearborn resident, attorney, husband, and father running to represent Michigan’s 15th House District.
The son of immigrant small-business owners, he attended Dearborn Public Schools, earned his degree from the University of Michigan–Dearborn, and built his legal career serving families through public defense and private practice.
Jalal is running to bring Dearborn and Dearborn Heights’ priorities to Lansing, working with local leaders to deliver practical results for the community he’s proud to call home.

Leadership that listens first
Jalal is running to make state government work better for the people of Dearborn and Dearborn Heights.
He believes leadership starts with listening locally, investing in strong neighborhoods, and making smart, practical decisions that protect working families.
Jalal is focused on strengthening public services, supporting local businesses, and ensuring families have access to opportunity so this district remains a place where people can build, stay, and call home.
