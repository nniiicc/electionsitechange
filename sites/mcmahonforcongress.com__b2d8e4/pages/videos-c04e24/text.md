top of page Log In About Patty On the Issues Videos Get involved Videos Some moments in history call for more of the same.
Others call for something different.
Patty McMahon believes America is at one of those moments.
In these videos, she shares why she believes the challenges facing our country require a different kind of representative in Congress.
What makes me qualified for Congress What qualifies someone to represent you in Congress?
Patty McMahon shares how 35 years of federal service shaped her understanding of Congress, constituent service, government accountability, and fiscal responsibility.
She explains why she believes a representative's job is to serve the people, not a political party, and why her independent approach benefits Colorado's 6th District.
Why Endless Wars Must End In this video, Patty McMahon explores what America loses when war becomes routine.
Drawing on history, she reflects on Vietnam, the fall of the Berlin Wall, and the lessons they offer about freedom, family, and the human cost of conflict.
She explains why she supports a strong national defense while opposing wars of choice, why she believes economic leadership and diplomacy are more powerful than military aggression, and why America's political divisions have become their own form of endless war.
Above all, Patty argues that the United States must remember what it was founded to protect: People.
Not political parties.
Not military power.
And, not the economy.
Separation of Powers Political parties have done more than divide America.
The Constitution separated legislative, executive, and judicial power to prevent any one interest from controlling the entire government.
But Democrats and Republicans built an alternate, extraconstitutional structure that runs through all three branches, allowing party allegiance to reconnect the powers the Constitution deliberately separated.
There is no Democratic or Republican branch of government.
But there is a partisan structure capable of controlling all three.
Let's end it.
Foreign Policy, Alliances & Leadership How should the United States respond when its allies disagree with military action?
Drawing on her 35 years of federal experience, Patty uses a workplace analogy to explain why disagreement does not necessarily mean disloyalty.
She argues that allies should not be criticized for declining to join a conflict they never agreed to enter. ​ The video explores the importance of respecting the sovereignty and decisions of other nations, maintaining strong alliances even when disagreements arise, and focusing criticism on the source of a conflict rather than on partners who choose a different course.
Patty discusses why effective leadership requires respecting boundaries, understanding differing perspectives, and preserving long-term relationships with America's allies.
Break the Cycle What happens when voters stop believing they have only two choices?
In this video, Patty compares America's two-party system to an unhealthy relationship, one in which voters are convinced they need the parties more than the parties need them.
She argues that the real power belongs to the voters and that Congress won't change until citizens demand better Patty explains why she is running for a single term, why she refuses to spend her time in office fundraising for reelection, and why she believes it's time to restore accountability, fiscal responsibility, and Congress's focus on serving the American people instead of political parties.
The Real Power of the "Wasted" Vote The “spoiler theory” and “wasted vote” hypothesis are lies.
Third-party and independent candidates fill a political void that give voters a reason to show up.
And when our voters show up, they usually vote down ballot too.
That’s the real political power of the “wasted” vote.
Education shouldn't end at high school In this video, Patty McMahon introduces OPEN America (Online Public Education Network), her proposal to transform the Department of Education into a nationwide online learning platform that makes college courses, technical training, and workforce education available to every American.
By using existing technology and partnering with accredited colleges and trade schools, OPEN America would allow students to earn transferable credits, reduce the cost of higher education, and continue learning throughout their lives.
Patty explains why she believes investing in education is one of the smartest investments America can make and why the country's next great innovation should be making lifelong learning accessible to everyone.
Leadership Without Division Can America become stronger without tearing each other down?
In this video, Patty explains why criticizing a leader's behavior is not the same as hating the person.
She shares her belief that America is strongest when its leaders inspire, unite, and elevate people rather than divide them through insults and personal attacks. ​ Patty discusses how the United States is viewed abroad, why words matter in leadership, and why making America "great" begins with treating one another with dignity and respect.
She argues that Americans, regardless of political party, are one national family and that the path forward is through encouragement, accountability, and unity rather than name-calling.
Repealing the 19th Amendment Is About Power Not Voting For a long time, Patty dismissed the movement to repeal the 19th Amendment as trolling.
She doesn’t anymore. ​ Some advocates argue that women vote incorrectly, are too easily manipulated, or support the wrong policies.
But liberty does not require people to make choices others approve of.
It requires defending their freedom to make choices others may absolutely hate. ​ The 19th Amendment does not give women permission to vote.
It tells government that a citizen’s right to vote cannot be denied because of sex. ​ No one has to agree with women, like how they vote, or approve of their choices. ​ But women’s rights are not conditional upon anyone’s approval. ​ Liberty belongs to all of us.
Open the Debate Stage Colorado voters should have the opportunity to hear from all the candidates applying for the job. ​ In this video, Patty challenges 9NEWS’s debate criteria, including the 15% polling threshold for Libertarian gubernatorial candidate Eric Mulder, and asks a simple question: How is a candidate supposed to reach that threshold when Libertarians often aren’t included in the polls? ​ Debates shouldn’t decide which candidate's voters are allowed to seriously consider.
They should give voters the opportunity to hear the candidates, compare their qualifications, and decide for themselves. ​ Colorado’s unaffiliated voters now make up the largest share of the electorate.
It’s time our political debates made room for voices outside the two major parties. ​ Put everybody on the stage.
Let the voters decide.

He’s the Speaker of the House.

Let’s talk about his response.
Justice for George and all workers at the World Trade Center Recovery and Cleanup Site Stitch incoming, because I want everyone to know about George Allen.
George was a federal employee who volunteered for a temporary assignment at ground zero after 9-11 at the World Trade Center Recovery and Cleanup site.
When George returned to Colorado, he got cancer.
He believed it was caused by the chemicals and toxic air that he was exposed to while there.
The federal workers’ compensation system kept denying the connection and gave him the runaround until he died.
This release of 170,000 pages of documents revealing that government officials misled the public is enraging.
I want justice for George and everyone like him because this government hide and seek bullshit that kills people must end.
