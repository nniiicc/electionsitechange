Empowering Local Education Education reform must start with protecting rural schools.
That means strengthening teacher support, stabilizing funding, and ensuring public dollars stay in public classrooms.
As your state representative, Tiffany will fight for all Oklahoma students and repeal the voucher program that is weakening rural districts.
According to the Oklahoma Policy Institute, "In school year 2024-25 , the program distributed $248.5 million in credits to 39,229 students, of whom just 4,071 (10.4 percent) were previously enrolled in public schools.
Of the 39,229 students benefiting from the credit, nearly three-quarters (72 percent) had household incomes of $75,000 or higher.
The homeschool was credit was claimed by 2,376 households in the amount of $3.3M." Oklahoma is robbing rural schools to give tax credits to the wealthy.
Healthcare Rural healthcare demands real investment and smart policy—supporting local hospitals, offering incentives for providers to serve rural communities, and improving emergency response times.
When rural hospitals and clinics close, families pay the price.
Protecting access to care in District 12 is not optional—lives depend on it.” ​ According to the American Hospital Association, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act (HR 1) would lead to a reduction of $50.4 billion in federal Medicaid spending on rural hospitals over ten years.
