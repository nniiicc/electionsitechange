Representative Daryl Campbell proudly serves as the FL State Representative of House District 99, advocating for the vibrant communities of Fort Lauderdale, Lauderhill, Lauderdale Lakes, Wilton Manors, and Plantation.
In his first term in the Florida Legislature, Representative Campbell sat on pivotal committees such as Healthcare Appropriations, Justice Appropriations, Civil Justice, Ways and Means, and Energy, Communication, and Cybersecurity.
Representative Campbell firmly believes in cultivating a purpose-driven mindset, viewing obstacles as opportunities to effect meaningful change.

His journey to the Legislature springs from his profound experience as a social worker.
As a Licensed Clinical Social Worker and Certified Addiction Therapist, Representative Campbell has dedicated himself to counseling individuals grappling with mental health challenges.
During his tenure in the legislature, Representative Campbell has empowered individuals to embrace their authentic selves, transcended barriers of race, gender, and socioeconomic status.
He has secured $10.4 million in appropriations and successfully passed bills addressing the needs of persons with sickle cell disease, as well as enhancing job opportunities for formerly homeless individuals, guiding them towards realizing their limitless potential.
Equipped with a diverse array of skills refined through delivering constituent services, building relationships with governmental bodies and community leaders, and possessing a profound comprehension of local concerns, Representative Campbell approaches his role with a solution focused perspective of a social worker.
