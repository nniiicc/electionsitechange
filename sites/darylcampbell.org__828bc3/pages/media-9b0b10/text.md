Second-generation Jamaican wins Florida House seat
By: Lester Hinds | The Gleaner | January 15, 2022
Daryl Campbell, whose parents are Jamaican, is the newest member of the Florida State House of Representatives.
Campbell emerged victorious in Tuesday’s special election held to fill the seat formerly held by Bobby DuBose.
DuBose had given up the seat to run in the special congressional election to fill the vacant seat.
Just over 12, 400 voted in the election or nine per cent of the eligible voters on the voters’ roll for the House District 94 seat.
Of that, Campbell received 40 per cent of the vote in a four-person field, or just over 4, 900 votes.
