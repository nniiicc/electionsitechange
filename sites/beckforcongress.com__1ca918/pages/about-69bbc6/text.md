My story is a Wisconsinite’s story: I come from working-class roots—strong union values, a belief in fairness & I‘m committed to making sure every family has a fair shot.
That’s the fight I’ll take to Washington.
I was born & raised in Milwaukee, in the heart of working-class neighborhoods.
My family taught me that hard work, integrity, and community mean everything.
My mom worked for Milwaukee Public Schools and was active in the teachers union.
My dad worked at Briggs & Stratton as a proud union member.
My grandfather and uncles were leaders in Milwaukee’s labor movement, helping build union power in the #s and ’#s.
Their fight for fairness is in my blood.
I’ve carried that legacy forward as a union member myself, and I’m proud to see working people standing together again today.
Like so many Wisconsinites, I’ve worked my way through different jobs and careers.
I waited tables, earned my degree while working in hospitality management, and later retrained in healthcare as an MRI technologist.
I later joined GE Healthcare in Waukesha, where I led product development while raising a family and earning my MBA at Cardinal Stritch.
I’ve lived most of my life in the 5th District.
Today, I call West Bend home.
I’m running because our district (YOU) deserves a representative who comes from us—not from boardrooms or Washington politics.
Like you, I am someone who knows what it means to punch a clock, pay union dues, raise a family, and feel the pressure of rising costs, every day.
I know where I come from.
I know who I’m fighting for.
I listen before I lead; I follow through — and will, improve Wisconsinites’ livelihoods from Washington.
