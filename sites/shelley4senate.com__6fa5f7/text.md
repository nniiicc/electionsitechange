Who Is Shelley?
Shelley has been honored to serve as the State Senator for New York’s 37th Senate District since 2018, and is running for re-election in the 37th Senate District.
Shelley is a proud public servant, committed to always providing her constituents with attention and care.
We have faced – and will continue to face – many serious challenges, and local leadership matters more than ever because it’s what impacts daily life the most.
Shelley will continue to push for policies that help our communities and improve the lives of all New Yorkers.
She will continue to prioritize providing quality education for every child, no matter their zip code; addressing climate change, unemployment assistance, or small business support; fighting for workers; and listening to the concerns of her constituents.

Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 20236
Flip your ballot and VOTE YES on Prop 1/New York Equal Rights Amendment
You can learn more from the New Yorkers for Equal Rights Coalition website.
BALLOT LANGUAGE:
Proposal Number One, An Amendment – Amendment to Protect Against Unequal Treatment
This proposal would protect against unequal treatment based on ethnicity, national origin, age, disability, and sex, including sexual orientation, gender identity and pregnancy.
It also protects against unequal treatment based on reproductive healthcare and autonomy.
A “YES” vote puts these protections in the New York State Constitution.
A “NO” vote leaves these protections out of the State Constitution.
REGISTER TO VOTE
Voter registration applications must be received by October 26, 2024.
If you want to check your voter restoration status, register online, or print out a voter registration application you can do so from the NYS Board of Elections website.
VOTE EARLY IN-PERSON
Early voting is from Saturday, October 26, 2024 to Sunday, November 3, 2024.
For a list of locations and hours in Westchester click here.
VOTE BY MAIL
The deadline to request an early mail ballot and absentee ballot is October 26, 2024.
Completed ballots must be postmarked by November 5, 2024, hand delivered to an early voting location, the Board of Elections office in Westchester (148 Martine Avenue, White Plains, NY 10602), or delivered to a poll site by 9 PM on November 5.
To request an early mail or absentee ballot use the Online Ballot Application Portal linked here.
VOTE ON ELECTION DAY
If you do not vote early or by mail, please vote on Election Day, Tuesday, November 5 from 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM.
You can find your ballot and polling place by filling out this form.
