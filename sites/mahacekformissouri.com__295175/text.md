Services Bob Mahacek is a lifelong St.
Louis County resident, successful small business owner, and dedicated community volunteer running for State Representative in District 95.
With decades of experience navigating the challenges of a small business, Bob is committed to bringing fiscal conservatism and commonsense solutions to the state capitol. ​​ His platform focuses on delivering tangible results for Missouri families, including cutting property taxes, fixing the broken reassessment system, and streamlining government waste.
As a staunch supporter of law enforcement, Bob is dedicated to reducing violent crime and "Backing the Blue." ​ Bob’s deep roots in the community are reflected in his extensive service record, from volunteering in prison rehabilitation and supporting Our Little Haven to his weekly work with a dog therapy team at Delmar Gardens.
Bob is running to restore civility to the Missouri House and ensure a brighter future for the community he has always called home.
