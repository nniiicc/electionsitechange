| Rancho Cucamonga, CA – Today, the campaign for lifelong educator and Etiwanda School Boardmember Robert Garcia announced securing the endorsement of the California Legislative Progressive Caucus.
Founded in 2017, the California Legislative Progressive Caucus is a values-based group of legislators who fight for economic justice, civil rights, safe communities, and environmental justice.
This latest endorsement solidifies lifelong educator and Etiwanda School District Board Member Robert Garcia as the clear choice to champion progressive values and deliver results for the people of the 50th Assembly District.
Garcia has also received the endorsement of Assembly Majority Leader Emeritus Eloise Gómez Reyes, the California Federation of Teachers (CFT), California School Employees Association (CSEA), and dozens of local leaders across the district.
Garcia stated, “Earning the endorsement of the California Legislative Progressive Caucus is a huge honor for me.
The Inland Empire deserves a champion who is committed to delivering on the issues that matter most — fighting warehouse pollution, creating good-paying union jobs, and expanding access to quality education, housing, and healthcare.
I look forward to working with members of the Progressive Caucus to uphold our shared values and strengthen our communities in the Inland Empire.” |
