Successfully earning more than 87% of the Delegate vote, Garcia is the clear Democratic choice for the 50th Assembly District
| Rancho Cucamonga, CA – Today, the campaign for lifelong educator and Etiwanda School Board Member Robert Garcia announced earning the endorsement of the California Democratic Party’s delegates in the 50th Assembly District during the pre-endorsement caucus over the weekend.
Of the delegates who voted, more than 87% voted to support Robert Garcia for the California Democratic Party’s endorsement.
The California Democratic Party’s endorsement for Robert Garcia will be placed on the consent calendar at November’s convention where the delegates will vote to make it official.A son of immigrants, lifelong educator, and current Etiwanda School Board Member, Robert Garcia is also endorsed by the California Federation of Teachers (CFT), California School Employees Association (CSEA), the California Latino Legislative Caucus, the California Legislative Progressive Caucus, and dozens of local leaders across the district, including County Supervisor Joe Baca, Colton Mayor Frank Navarro, Fontana City Councilmember Jesse Sandoval, and dozens more.Garcia stated, “As an educator, Etiwanda School Board Member, and elected delegate myself, I’m deeply honored to earn more than 87% of the California Democratic Party’s AD-50 Delegates during the pre-endorsement caucus.
I’m proud to be a Democrat who is running for Assembly to deliver results for the Inland Empire and build on the progress led by Assemblymember Eloise Reyes.
Thank you to the Delegates for their work to protect democracy and uphold our values.
In the State Assembly, I look forward to continue working with our community leaders and Democratic Party delegates to prioritize service to the people of the 50th Assembly District.”Democratic Party Delegates made the following statements:Assembly Majority Leader Emeritus Eloise Reyes: “Robert has dedicated his life to fighting for communities in need and supporting residents of this district.
