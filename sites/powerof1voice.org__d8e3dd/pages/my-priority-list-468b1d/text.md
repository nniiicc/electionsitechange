502-994-4566 Power of One Voice About the Candidate My Priority List Support the Movement My Priority List Jobs & Economic Growth for District 43 A thriving district starts with good jobs, strong businesses, and an economy that works for every family.
Here is what Lisa Holliday Harris will fight for in Frankfort.
Priority 01 Attract New Businesses Partner with state and local agencies to recruit employers to District 43 — bringing quality jobs and investment directly into our neighborhoods.
Priority 02 Support Small Businesses Cut red tape, expand access to small-business loans, and create a mentorship pipeline so local entrepreneurs can start, grow, and stay in our community.
Priority 03 Workforce Development Invest in job-training programs, apprenticeships, and community college partnerships that equip residents with the skills employers are hiring for today.
Priority 04 Raise Household Income Advocate for policies that increase wages, reduce the cost of living, and ensure working families can build real financial security.
Priority 05 Revitalize Underserved Areas Direct economic development dollars to long-neglected corridors in District 43 — turning vacant lots into opportunity and blighted blocks into thriving commercial zones.
Priority 06 Public-Private Partnerships Forge strategic alliances between government, universities, and the private sector to fund infrastructure, innovation hubs, and job-creation initiatives.
