Meet Dr.
Lisa Holliday Harris
Lisa Holliday Harris is a veteran spouse and write -in candidate who is a dedicated leader committed to honest and transparent governance.
A Portland resident and business owner with real-world experience representing District 43 of the Kentucky State House of Representatives, will be a great honor.
She is no outsider; Lisa lives within a blueprint designed for change and is an advocate for safer neighborhoods, stronger families, and economic revitalization.
Her role as a nonprofit leader and transportation professional is dedicated and results-driven.
Kentucky families need problems solved, not representation that will cause additional problems.
Lisa will bring accountability, transparency, and responsive constituent service to Frankfort.
Her vision and plan are to eradicate Democratic Socialist ideologies and represent every resident with integrity.
District 43 deserves a representative who is accessible, accountable, and committed to listening to every constituent.
Lisa Holliday Harris believes effective leadership begins with understanding why history matters.
As of 2026, this district remains heavily democratic in voter registration.
Available data show approximately 15.2% are Republicans and 5.2% are independents, although registration numbers do not necessarily predict individual voting behavior.
Why this history matters to this campaign: Democrat political leadership has raised significant questions about what measurable improvements the people of District 43 should be able to point to.
Not a lot of improvement; " Things have gotten worse," from constituents that I've spoken with.
