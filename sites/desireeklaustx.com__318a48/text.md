Meet Desirée Desiree is a life-long Baytown native.
Growing up in HD-128 and attending schools in Highlands & Baytown, the community has taught her strong work ethic, keeping the faith when times get tough, and to always give a helping hand whenever you can.
She knows what it’s like to work as a college student making lattes at the local Starbucks to becoming a full-time working Mom in a 9-5 job.
Desiree learned to pivot by building her own small business.
She knows what it’s like to live paycheck to paycheck.
When the daycare bill is more than your mortgage and when a health scare emergency gives you sticker shock.
All while having to pay for insurance out of pocket expenses.
Learn More Education & Experience Desirée joined the workforce like many college students looking to earn a living while getting her education.
Throughout her workforce journey, she worked in a variety of fields: Retail/Food Service Marketing Customer Service Home Health Industry Former Licensed Insurance Agent Digital Media Producer ( HCC, Jolt Action, Sesh Co Working, Latino Entrepreneurs Week, Black Entrepreneurs Week) Desiree has worked towards certifications and mentorship from HCC, which helped her build her own business from the ground up.
She also created opportunities for small business events, e.g. markets, workshops and networking.
Desiree has become a resource for local leadership and resources available to support the small business community.
