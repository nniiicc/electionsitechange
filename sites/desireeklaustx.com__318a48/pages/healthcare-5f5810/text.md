Expand Care.
Put Families First.
Healthcare should not be a luxury.
Families in District 128 deserve affordable prescriptions, access to quality care close to home, stronger mental health support, and a healthcare system that treats people with dignity instead of forcing them to choose between their health and their bills.
Priority: Healthcare Access District: TX House 128 Last updated: Chip In Volunteer On This Page Why It Matters What’s Broken My Plan Community Care Take Action Core message Families should be able to get the care they need without fear of crushing bills, delayed treatment, or impossible choices between medicine, rent, and groceries.
Why This Matters Healthcare affects every part of family life.
It affects whether a parent can keep working, whether a child gets care early, whether a senior can afford medication, and whether everyday stress turns into a crisis because care came too late or cost too much.
“No family should have to choose between getting better and staying afloat.” What’s Broken Prescription costs remain too high for too many families.
Many people delay care because they are worried about the bill.
Neighborhood access to clinics, preventive care, and mental health support is not strong enough.
Families are often left navigating complicated systems without clear support.
Working people can have coverage on paper and still struggle to afford actual care.
What Families Feel Skipping appointments because they cannot miss work or pay another bill.
Paying too much for prescriptions or cutting pills to make them last.
Waiting too long for help with mental health, maternal health, or preventive care.
Trying to figure out insurance, billing, and referrals while already under stress.
Desirée’s Plan I’m fighting for healthcare that is affordable, local, and easier for families to access before a problem becomes a crisis.
What I’ll Fight For Lower prescription costs so families can afford the medicine they need.
Stronger access to neighborhood clinics and community-based care.
More preventive care so problems are caught early and treated sooner.
Mental health support that is available, timely, and treated like real healthcare.
Maternal and family health support for women, parents, and children.
Protection from surprise costs and coverage gaps that destabilize working families.
What I Oppose A healthcare system built around confusion instead of care.
Sky-high medication costs that punish people for being sick.
Underinvestment in local access that forces people to wait, travel, or go without.
Policies that leave families one emergency away from financial crisis.
What This Looks Like in HD-128 Healthcare access in our district should mean more than just having a facility somewhere on a map.
It should mean families can actually get in, get seen, afford treatment, and stay connected to care over time.
That means strengthening local care options, improving support for working families, and making sure healthcare policy reflects real life — especially for parents, seniors, caregivers, and people managing chronic conditions.
Community-First Care More local access to basic and preventive care.
Better support for women’s health and family health.
Stronger mental health resources in everyday community settings.
Policies that reduce stress instead of adding more bureaucracy.
My Commitment I’m running because families in District 128 deserve a representative who understands that healthcare is not separate from economic security, family stability, or dignity.
I will fight for practical solutions that lower costs, expand access, and put people first.
I’m not bought, I’m building.
Help us build a healthier future for working families, seniors, parents, and children in District 128.
