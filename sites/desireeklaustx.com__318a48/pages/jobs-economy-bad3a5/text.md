Local Opportunity.
A Stronger District 128.
Families in District 128 deserve an economy that works for working people.
That means good-paying jobs, stronger support for small businesses, practical workforce training, and infrastructure that helps our communities grow without leaving local families behind.
Priority: Jobs & Small Business District: TX House 128 Last updated: Chip In Volunteer On This Page Why It Matters What’s Broken My Plan Small Business Take Action Core message Economic growth should not just look good on paper.
It should mean better jobs, stronger wages, more support for local businesses, and more opportunity for the people who already live and work here.
Why This Matters The economy is not just about headlines.
It is about whether families can afford groceries, whether parents can find stable work close to home, whether small businesses can survive, and whether young people can see a future for themselves right here in District 128.
“An economy that works should reward work, create opportunity, and help local families build something real.” What’s Broken Too many working families are doing everything right and still struggling to keep up.
Small businesses face rising costs, barriers to capital, and too little support.
Workforce pipelines do not always connect people to the jobs actually available in our region.
Infrastructure needs can slow growth, hurt safety, and make daily life more expensive and stressful.
Economic decisions are too often made without enough input from local workers, families, and entrepreneurs.
What Families and Workers Feel Long commutes that take time away from family.
Wages that do not stretch far enough for housing, healthcare, and childcare.
Local entrepreneurs carrying all the risk with too little institutional support.
Young adults looking for a path forward but not always seeing one nearby.
Desirée’s Plan I’m fighting for an economy rooted in local opportunity, practical investment, and respect for the people who keep this district moving.
What I’ll Fight For Workforce training tied to real jobs so residents can step into high-demand careers.
Support for small businesses through access to capital, coaching, and community partnerships.
Infrastructure investment that improves mobility, safety, and long-term local growth.
Stronger local hiring pipelines that help residents benefit from regional economic activity.
Economic opportunity for young people through job readiness, technical training, and mentorship.
Policies that value workers and strengthen family stability.
What I Oppose Trickle-down politics that leave working families waiting for relief that never comes.
Neglecting small businesses while giving the biggest players the most attention.
Growth without community benefit where residents see the burden but not the opportunity.
Economic policy disconnected from everyday life for working families.
Support Small Business, Support Community Small businesses are not just part of the economy — they are part of the identity of our communities.
When local entrepreneurs succeed, they create jobs, invest back into neighborhoods, and keep our communities connected.
As a small business owner and community builder, I know firsthand that local businesses need visibility, support, and real opportunity — not just speeches about entrepreneurship.
What This Looks Like in HD-128 More support for neighborhood businesses trying to grow.
More partnerships between education, industry, and workforce training.
Better coordination to connect people with jobs close to home.
Economic development that includes the people who already live here.
My Commitment I’m running to help build an economy where families can stay, grow, work, and thrive right here in District 128.
That means investing in people, not just press releases — and making sure local opportunity reaches local families.
I’m not bought, I’m building.
Help us build an economy that works for working families, small businesses, and future generations in District 128.
