Serving the Community
Family is in everything that I do.
For years, Desirée has amplified local entrepreneurs and small businesses, connecting families with opportunity and resources.
She knows that when small businesses succeed, families succeed—and thriving families build thriving cities.
She has mentored entrepreneurs, partnered with nonprofits, and built coalitions across District 128 to tackle health, wealth, and education challenges.
Now, she’s ready to take that same grit and grassroots experience to the Capitol—because District 128 deserves leadership that puts people first.
Why I’m Running
District 128 deserves a representative who will fight for job growth, strong schools and real opportunities for working families and small businesses.
- Educate: Invest in our public schools, workforce training, and community colleges.
- Elevate: Grow small-business support, reduce red tape, and expand access to capital.
- 128: Put our district first—Baytown, Crosby, Highlands, Deer Park, La Porte, Pasadena, Huffman, and Morgan’s Point.
