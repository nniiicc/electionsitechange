Every child in District 128 deserves a safe, fully funded public school where they can learn, grow, and feel supported.
Our public schools should not be underfunded, overcrowded, or pushed aside while politicians hand public dollars to private interests.
Priority: Public Education District: TX House 128 Last updated: Chip In Volunteer On This Page Why It Matters What’s Broken My Plan Local Impact Take Action Core message Texas should invest in neighborhood public schools, student safety, teacher retention, and mental health support — not voucher schemes that drain resources from our classrooms.
Why This Matters Strong schools are the foundation of strong communities.
When schools are safe, well-funded, and equipped to serve students, families are stronger, teachers stay in the profession, and local communities thrive.
“A child should never have to wonder if they are safe at school, and a teacher should never have to do more with less while politicians call it reform.” What’s Broken Public schools are expected to do more while receiving fewer resources.
Teachers are leaving because of burnout, low pay, and lack of support.
Students and parents are too often left out of conversations about school safety.
Politicians are pushing voucher schemes instead of fixing the schools most families actually use.
Mental health and student support systems are stretched too thin.
What Families Feel Parents paying out of pocket for school supplies, fees, meals, and activities.
Teachers buying classroom materials with their own money.
Students carrying the weight of unsafe conditions, stress, and inconsistent support.
Communities being told to settle for less while state leaders play politics with education.
Desirée’s Plan I’m fighting for practical, people-first solutions that strengthen public education and protect students.
What I’ll Fight For Fully funded public schools so districts can meet student needs without constant cuts.
More classroom support including counselors, intervention staff, and student services.
Teacher recruitment and retention through better pay, respect, and real support.
Student safety systems that work with accountability, transparency, and prevention.
Mental health access so students are not left waiting for help.
Real family engagement so parents and community members have a seat at the table.
What I Oppose Voucher programs that divert money away from neighborhood public schools.
Budget games that leave schools uncertain year after year.
Policies without accountability that sound good in headlines but fail students in practice.
Ignoring student voices when it comes to safety, support, and school climate.
What This Looks Like in HD-128 In our district, school issues are not abstract.
Families live them every day — from supply costs and overcrowding to concerns about student safety and whether teachers have the tools they need.
The state cannot keep asking communities to carry the burden while withholding support.
District 128 deserves a representative who will listen to parents, stand with educators, and fight for schools that serve every student — not just the families who can afford alternatives.
Community-First Approach This campaign believes solutions should be built with the people most affected: students, parents, educators, counselors, and community partners.
Listen locally.
Fight at the state level.
Bring resources back home.
Keep students at the center.
My Commitment I’m not running to manage decline.
I’m running to help rebuild trust in public education, strengthen the schools our families rely on, and make sure every student in District 128 has the opportunity to succeed in a safe and supportive environment.
I’m not bought, I’m building.
Help us build a stronger future for students, teachers, and families in District 128.
