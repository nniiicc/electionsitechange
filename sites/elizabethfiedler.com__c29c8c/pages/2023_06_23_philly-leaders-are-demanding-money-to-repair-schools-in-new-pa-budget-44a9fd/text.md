June 23, 2023
As negotiations over the state budget heat up, Philadelphia lawmakers and officials say funds to fix the city’s aging, toxic schools rank as a top priority.
At a news conference Friday at South Philadelphia High School, the school district’s superintendent, City Council members and union leaders joined State Rep.
Elizabeth Fiedler (D., Philadelphia) in support of a proposal to pump hundreds of millions of dollars into school facilities.
