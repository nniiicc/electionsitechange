Philadelphia, May 3, 2022 – Pennsylvania State Representative Elizabeth Fiedler on Tuesday announced endorsements from Planned Parenthood and EMILY’s List in her bid for re-election.
“EMILY’s List is excited to endorse Elizabeth Fiedler to represent Pennsylvania’s 184th House District,” said Sarah Curmi, Vice President of State and Local Campaigns at EMILY’s List.
“Rep.
Fiedler’s dedication to expanding health care, investing in education, and preserving the environment have greatly improved the lives of Pennsylvanians.
She is the best choice for the residents of Pennsylvania House District 184.”
The Planned Parenthood Pennsylvania PAC (PP PA PAC) endorses candidates who demonstrate a commitment to protecting and expanding sexual and reproductive health.
“With the Supreme Court reportedly planning to overturn Roe and Casey, it’s clear that abortion is on the ballot in 2022.
I will continue to protect the reproductive rights of women and all people,” said Rep.
Fiedler, a member of the PA State House Health Committee.
“Abortion is legal in the U.S., and the Supreme Court should not be inserting themselves between a pregnant person and their doctor.”
State Representative Elizabeth Fiedler is running for re-election on the May 17, 2022 Democratic Primary ballot.
