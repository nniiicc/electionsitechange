Machesney Park, IL 61115 Subject: Sheriff’s Star Legislative Endorsement Thank you for your interest in receiving the Sheriffs’ Star legislative endorsement.
It is now my distinct privilege to announce that you have been selected as a recipient of this award for 2022.
The Sheriffs of Illinois PAC is a bipartisan and voluntary political program and fund.
This program offers support to politicians who support Sheriffs, and all of law enforcement, in their effort to protect our citizens and communities.
We certainly appreciate your willingness to participate in a ride along with a Sheriff’s Office and the suggestions provided on programming.
This endorsement is on behalf of the Sheriffs of Illinois PAC, rather than individual Sheriffs.
If you are interested in receiving a specific Sheriff’s support, you should contact them directly.
While we have continued to experience a number of challenges across Illinois, we hope you know that Sheriffs will continue to perform their duties in protecting and enhancing the lives of the citizens they represent.
We, however, cannot do this alone.
As a recipient of this award, we certainly hope that we can count on you in supporting our efforts in the General Assembly and our communities.
The Sheriffs of Illinois PAC thanks you for your support and offers congratulations for being a recipient of this award!
We have included an award certificate for your use.
At a later date, we will also be issuing a press release providing a list of all award recipients, as well as an announcement on our Facebook page.
We look forward to working closely with you moving forward.
Thank you for your service and please contact Jim Kaitschuk at 217-753-2372 with any questions.
Sincerely, Dwight Baird, Chairman Kendall County Sheriff
