She graduated from Arlington Heights High School and continued her studies at Weatherford College, Tarleton State University, and East Texas State University at Commerce.
Lola served in the U.S.
Army National Guard and spent 31 years working for the Santa Fe Railroad, where she also held leadership roles in the United Transportation Union advocating for workers.
Raised in a civically engaged family, Lola has always believed in serving her community.
Today, she is running to focus on the issues that matter most to Tarrant County: strengthening public education, protecting our water and environment, and improving access to healthcare for families.

Protecting Democracy & Voting Rights Our democracy works best when every voice is heard and every vote counts.
Michell supports expanding access to the ballot box, protecting against voter suppression, and ensuring fair district maps.
Gun Safety Michell supports commonsense gun safety measures, such as universal background checks and keeping dangerous weapons out of the hands of criminals, while respecting the rights of responsible gun owners.
Climate Action & Clean Energy The climate crisis demands urgent action.
Michell supports transitioning to clean, renewable energy, creating green jobs, protecting clean air and water, and safeguarding our communities from extreme weather.
Affordable Housing Everyone deserves a safe and affordable place to live.
Michell supports expanding affordable housing, cracking down on predatory landlords, and helping first-time homebuyers achieve the American dream.
Good-Paying Jobs and Economic Fairness Michelle is committed to building an economy that works for everyone, not just the wealthy few.
That means supporting small businesses, raising the minimum wage, investing in job training, and protecting workers’ rights to organize.
Strong Public Schools Every child deserves a great education, no matter their zip code.
Michelle supports increased funding for public schools, higher pay for teachers, universal pre-K, and affordable pathways to college or vocational training.
Healthcare for All Michelle believes that access to affordable, quality healthcare is a right, not a privilege.
They support lowering prescription drug costs, protecting Medicare and Medicaid, and expanding coverage so that no family is forced to choose between paying bills and getting the care they need.
