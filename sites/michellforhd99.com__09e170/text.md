Michelle Winder is a lifelong Fort Worth native and candidate for Texas House District 99.
She proudly served in the U.S.
Army National Guard and built a career in public service, including work with the Tarrant County Sheriff’s Department, General Dynamics, and 31 years with the Santa Fe Railroad before retiring in 2024.
Lola is focused on three key priorities for Tarrant County families: • Protecting public education and supporting our public schools • Safeguarding our water and environment • Expanding access to quality healthcare
