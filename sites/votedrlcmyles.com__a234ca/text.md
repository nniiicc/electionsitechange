About Dr.
LC Myles
Dr.
LC Myles Jr. is truly an exceptional individual with a rich history of service and leadership.
His background as a distinguished public servant, rooted in hard work and Christian values, has shaped his remarkable career dedicated to serving his country and community.
From his early days as the son of a Mississippi Sharecropper, Dr.
Myles learned the importance of dedication and service.
His 21-year career in the U.S.
Army, which included a combat tour in Vietnam where he was awarded the Bronze Star Medal, showcases his commitment to serving his country with honor and distinction.
After retiring from the military as a 1st Sergeant, Dr.
Myles continued his service in a second career with General Dynamics Corp. as a Systems Engineer/IT Manager.
His experience in administrative and management roles honed his skills in team building, financial management, and strategic planning.
As a servant leader, Dr.
Myles prioritizes putting others first and fostering a culture of service and collaboration.
His extensive experience in leadership roles, along with his dedication to the principles of servant leadership, exemplifies his commitment to ensuring the success of those he serves.
Dr.
Myles's involvement in community activism, political advocacy, and volunteer work further demonstrates his unwavering commitment to improving his community and holding elected officials accountable.
His impressive list of accolades, including the President Joseph R.
Biden, Jr.
Lifetime Achievement Award and the President Barack Obama Lifetime Achievement Award, reflect his outstanding contributions to his community and country.
With a deep understanding of the Democratic Party at various levels, Dr.
Myles brings a wealth of knowledge and experience to his advocacy for the values and principles he holds dear.
His academic achievements, including bachelor’s and master’s degrees, as well as honorary doctorates, highlight his commitment to continuous growth and service to others.
Overall, Dr.
LC Myles Jr. is a formidable force in the political arena and a beacon of hope for those he represents.
His dedication to his community, unwavering commitment to excellence, and proven track record of leadership truly make him a remarkable individual deserving of recognition and respect.
