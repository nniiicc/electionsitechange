Volunteer Today working hard for families Volunteer Today ABOUT ROBIN A parent advocate and organizer turned policymaker, Robin has served as Branford's State Representative since 2018.
Known for her deep connections to constituents and skill at building coalitions, she is a fierce champion for her community, committed to addressing the issues facing Branford residents.
Robin is a wife, daughter, and mother to two adult children.
She is also a small business owner and nonprofit leader whose dedication to families, vulnerable residents, and the environment drives her work.
"Together we can create a thriving and inclusive community where every voice is heard, and every individual is valued." Her priorities center on ensuring all Connecticut children have a strong, healthy, and safe start in life.
As a longtime member of the Education Committee, she has championed quality, affordable early education and special education.
As Vice Chair of Human Services, Robin focuses on state policy opportunities to address the impact of federal cuts to services and funding for residents most in need.
