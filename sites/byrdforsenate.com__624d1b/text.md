Giving Wyoming a Voice
For too long, Washington has offered Wyoming a false choice: two parties more focused on climbing the ranks than delivering for the people who sent them there.
James Byrd has spent his career in the Wyoming legislature proving there's another way, working across the aisle to get real results, without ever answering to national interests.
Bring Down
Gas Prices
Wyoming shouldn't have to choose between filling up the tank and paying the bills.
James Byrd will fight to lower energy costs by backing domestic production, cutting red tape, and ending the wasteful foreign wars driving prices up.
Protect Public Lands
Wyoming's public lands are our heritage, our economy, and our way of life.
James Byrd will fight to keep them public and open for hunting, fishing, grazing, and recreation, not sold off to the highest bidder.
Make Healthcare Affordable
No one in Wyoming should go bankrupt because they got sick.
James Byrd will push to lower drug costs, protect rural hospitals, and make insurance actually work for the people paying into it.
