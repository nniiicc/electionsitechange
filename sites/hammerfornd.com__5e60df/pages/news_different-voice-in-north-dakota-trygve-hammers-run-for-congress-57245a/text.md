DIFFERENT VOICE IN NORTH DAKOTA: TRYGVE HAMMER’S RUN FOR CONGRESS
March 20, 2026
On this episode of Open Range, Gary Emineth sits down with North Dakota Democratic candidate for U.S.
House, Trygve Hammer, for a powerful and eye-opening conversation.
Hammer shares his roots growing up in Velva, North Dakota, giving viewers a grounded, small-town perspective that shapes his values today.
He dives into his time serving in the military after graduating from the U.S.
Naval Academy, highlighting leadership, discipline, and service to country.
Hammer also discusses his experience as a teacher and his work in the Bakken, offering a real-world understanding of education, energy, and the challenges facing North Dakota families.
His unique background—from rural upbringing to military service to the oil fields—brings a different voice and perspective to the political race.
This episode delivers insight into the issues, the candidate, and what’s at stake for North Dakota voters.
