Molly McKee-Seabrook is a lifelong Democrat, running for State Delegate in Maryland’s 22nd District.
She is a mom, a problem solver and an experienced state policy advocate.
Molly and her husband are proud Prince George’s County Public School parents and are raising their three children in Hyattsville, where Molly grew up.
Families
Working families deserve a society that actually works for them.
Families need access to childcare and aftercare, quality education, affordable housing, and healthcare, including reproductive care.
All of our neighbors should be able to live, work, and play in District 22 while feeling welcome and safe.
Investments in parks, community centers, public transportation, bike shares, and complete streets projects help make that a reality.
Molly feels strongly that protecting our environment and finding innovative solutions to meet our state’s climate goals are some of the most important issues facing Maryland and future generations.
