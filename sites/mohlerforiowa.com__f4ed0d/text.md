Three things every family in this district needs, whether you vote red or blue.
1 Water Pay farmers to lead on clean water.
The Iowa Healthy Waters Act: a tax credit for every acre under a real nutrient management plan, zero-percent loans for the precision equipment that already reduces runoff, and "Batch & Build" wetlands and saturated buffers.
Iowa has the second-highest cancer rate in the country, and researchers are studying whether nitrates in our water are part of the reason.
We should not have to wait for the final answer to clean up our water.
2 Schools Stop shipping our school money out of the district.
Iowa will spend over $315 million on private-school vouchers in the 2026-27 school year, with the income cap removed.
In the program's first year, two-thirds of voucher students were already in private school.
Those dollars leave our public districts, which still have to keep the lights on.
Cap and sunset the vouchers, restore the Area Education Agencies, and fully fund our public schools; three years running, the legislature hasn't even kept up with inflation.
Call it what it is: our tax dollars paying private tuition at schools our kids can't reach.
3 Healthcare Hospitals and clinics that stay open.
In February 2026, MercyOne closed its Ottumwa clinic and pointed patients to Centerville.
Now Centerville is losing care too: River Hills closes its clinic there on July 31, 2026.
Every closure means another hour behind the wheel for a checkup.
Pay nurses and doctors to plant roots here on multi-year contracts, and hold the managed-care companies accountable for every rural clinic they starve.
Monroe County Hospital caught my son's Type 1 Diabetes in the middle of the night.
That's what a hospital close to home is worth.
Also on my list Right to Repair, In Statute The Iowa House passed a right-to-repair bill in April 2026; the Senate let it die.
Until it's law, the only thing standing between a farmer and a tractor he can't fix is a non-binding handshake.
Codify it: protect IP, protect manufacturers, and give farmers an enforceable standard at three a.m. in the middle of harvest.
Tools for Schools Put welders, lathes, and diagnostic equipment back in our high schools.
AI is not going to plumb a hospital or run a CNC machine anytime soon.
We need plumbers, electricians, and machinists, and the trades deserve fair wages and an apprenticeship pipeline worth the body.
Homes You Can Afford Out-of-state investment firms now own nearly half of Iowa's manufactured-home lots, and they have raised lot rents on fixed-income residents by as much as #%.
Tie lot rent to inflation, triple the Homestead Tax Credit, and freeze property taxes for our seniors.
