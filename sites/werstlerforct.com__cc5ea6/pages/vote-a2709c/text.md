Early voting is at your town's early voting location, which is often not your usual polling place.
You can only vote early in the town where you're registered.
2.
Vote by absentee ballot New for 2026, any Connecticut voter can vote absentee with no excuse needed.
Request yours by Monday, Nov 2 .
Return it by mail, to your town's official drop box outside Town Hall, or in person to your Town Clerk.
Mailed ballots must arrive by 8pm on Election Day, so mail it early or use the drop box.
Forgot to sign the envelope?
Connecticut now lets you fix (cure) a missing signature so your ballot still counts.

Missed it?
You can still vote.
Connecticut has Same-Day Registration every day of early voting and on Election Day.
During early voting, register and vote at your town's early voting location.
On Election Day, go to your town's Same-Day Registration location (usually Town Hall; call your registrar to confirm), not your polling place.
Bring ID and proof of address.
Arrive early, because same-day registration takes longer.
Check if I'm registered At the polls What to bring Connecticut voters are asked for ID.
Any of these works: a photo ID, a Social Security card, or a pre-printed item showing your name and address (like a utility bill, bank statement or paycheck).
If you have no ID, you can still vote by signing a statement at the polls.
First-time voters who registered by mail may need photo ID or a document showing their address.
