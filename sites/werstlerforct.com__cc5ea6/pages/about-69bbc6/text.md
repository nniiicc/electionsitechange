Home About Platform Endorsements VOLUNTEER Meet the Candidate About Ethan Ethan Werstler is a candidate for State Senator of the 35th District, which includes Ashford, Chaplin, Coventry, Eastford, Ellington, Hampton, Stafford, Thompson, Tolland, Union, Vernon, Willington, and Woodstock.
Ethan is a third-generation Woodstock resident and has lived in town his whole life.
An alumnus of Woodstock Academy, Ethan earned a bachelor's degree in Political Science and Communications from the University of Connecticut.
Ethan is a former associate at a premier government relations firm in Hartford, where he developed a deep understanding of the state legislature.
Ethan has a passion for mentoring young people to be future leaders.
As a small business owner, he serves as a Public Speaking and Leadership coach to thousands of high school students from around the world.
As a volunteer at Woodstock Middle School's Public Speaking club, he is a youth leadership coach.
Ethan is also a Member of the Roseland Park Board of Trustees in his hometown of Woodstock.
Ethan brings unique experience to this campaign.
He has served as the Field Organizer for Joe Courtney for Congress, where he built grassroots support coalitions across Northeastern Connecticut by recruiting, training, and managing hundreds of volunteers for dozens of phone-banking, texting, voter-registration, and canvassing events.
In 2021, Ethan was elected to represent 23,000 undergraduate students across all five campuses as a full voting and speaking member of the UConn Board of Trustees.
In his role as Vice President of the UConn Undergraduate Student Body, he oversaw the logistical operations of the Undergraduate Student Government, including hiring and supervising 60+ paid employees, managing an almost $3 million budget, and addressing a diverse range of professional and interpersonal challenges facing large organizations.
Ethan is also a founder of "Husky Market", a food insecurity initiative that has provided more than $460,000 worth of grocery store gift cards to thousands of food-insecure students at UConn.
This successful initiative has now expanded into a collaboration with CT Foodshare and is available on all UConn campuses statewide.
For his efforts, he was awarded the prestigious Cohen Student Leadership Scholarship for demonstrating a significant and active commitment to eliminating bigotry, prejudice, and discrimination and to fostering tolerance and understanding.
Ethan Werstler is not a conventional candidate for elected office.
Focused less on party allegiance and more on the needs of this district, a Senator Werstler will work tirelessly in Hartford for our communities.
