Jimmy Dore Joins 9/11 at 25 NYC Forum With NSA Whistleblowers
September 8, 2026
Jimmy Dore will join us in Manhattan on Sunday, September 13, as a headliner for The Endless War Turned on You, a live public forum marking 25 years since 9/11.
He joins former NSA officials and whistleblowers William Binney, Thomas Drake, and J.
Kirk Wiebe, along with Jose Vega, veterans, organizers, commentators, and independent voices.
Together, the speakers will examine the wars, surveillance powers, and permanent state of emergency built in the shadow of 9/11—and what accountability requires now.
The forum took place in Manhattan on September 13, 2026.
