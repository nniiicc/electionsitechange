She currently serves as Vice-Chair of the Interim Employee Benefits Program Committee and is a member on the Interim Health Care, Retirement, and IT Committees.
Representative Stemen was appointed to his legislative seat in 2021 to replace Rep.
Tom Beadle who was elected State Treasurer.
He currently serves on the Interim Retirement Committee.
“I am extremely grateful for the opportunity to serve our state and the citizens of District 27 and hope to earn your support in 2022,” said Representative Stemen.
Senator Roers added, “Representing the residents of District 27 has been an honor and a privilege.
