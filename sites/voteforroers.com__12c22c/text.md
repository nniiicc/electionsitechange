MEET KRISTIN
Kristin Roers was born in Lyon County, Minnesota, and raised in Sioux Falls, South Dakota.
She holds a BS in Economics and a BS in Nursing from South Dakota State University, and an MS in Nursing and Healthcare Systems Administration from the University of Minnesota.
A nursing and clinical services consultant by profession, Kristin brings real-world experience in health care, economics, and public policy.
She was first elected to the North Dakota Senate for District 27 in Fargo and has served since 2018.
LEADERSHIP & IMPACT
Kristin has earned respect for her leadership in the Senate, holding key roles including:
- Chair, State & Local Government Committee
- Membership on Human Services, Health Care, Employee Benefits, and Rural Health Transformation committees across sessions.
- Elected Majority Caucus Leader by her peers in the Senate, showcasing her ability to build coalitions and drive policy.
In 2025, Kristin was honored with North Dakota’s Election Hero Award for her commitment to election integrity.
Her hands-on experience as an election worker at all levels, combined with her legislative advocacy, underscores her belief in free, fair, and secure elections.
Kristin has also received the Legislative Vision Award from the Greater North Dakota Chamber, recognizing her role in defending pro-growth policies and regulatory responsibility.
