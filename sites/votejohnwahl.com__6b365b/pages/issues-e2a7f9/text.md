Issues Important to Alabamians
Where John Stands
John Wahl is a proven conservative fighter who doesn’t just talk about Alabama values — he’s got a proven track-record.
As Chairman of the Alabama Republican Party, John led the charge for conservative values, pushed back against the radical left, and championed the principles that make America great.
He stood with President Trump when it mattered most, and has built a reputation for action, not just words.
Now, he’s ready to take that same fight to the office of Lt.
Governor — to stand for you, your family, and never back down.
Defending Your Rights and Freedom
Our liberties don’t come from government — they come from our Creator.
John Wahl believes the Constitution exists to protect our God-given rights, not to give bureaucrats more power over our lives.
Whether it’s your freedom of speech, freedom of religion, or your right to live without government overreach, John will always defend the foundational principles that made this nation great.
He will apologetically fight to ensure that government serves the people, not the other way around.
Economy and Jobs
Alabama’s working families deserve economic opportunities, not government roadblocks.
John Wahl supports pro-growth policies that reduce red tape, attract new businesses, and empower entrepreneurs.
He believes in creating a climate where small businesses can thrive and workers can earn a good living.
John will fight for job training programs, workforce development, and support energy independence to help Alabama compete — and win — in the national economy.
Tax Relief for Alabama Citizens
It’s time for Government at all levels to cut wasteful spending, balance their budgets, and put the people first again.
With the cost living rising and family’s struggling to make ends meet, John supports meaningful tax relief that will put more money back in the hands of hardworking Alabamians.
As Chairman of the Alabama Republican Party, he helped lead the charge to cut the state’s grocery tax — saving working families millions each year and proving what conservative leadership can do.
Now is the time for Alabama to follow Donald Trump’s lead allow We The People to keep more of your hard-earned money where it belongs — in your pocket.
Securing the Border
A nation without borders is not a nation.
Illegal immigration strains our communities, endangers public safety, and undermines the rule of law.
John Wahl strongly supports President Trump’s plan to secure the southern border, finish the wall, and stop illegal immigration once and for all.
He will work to ensure Alabama is never a sanctuary for criminal activity and that our state stands strong for law and order.
Election Integrity
John Wahl has been a leading voice for secure and honest elections in Alabama.
Under his leadership, the Alabama Republican Party has championed strong post-election audit legislation, demanded accountability for voting machines, and worked to restore public trust in our elections.
As Lt.
Governor, he will work to ensure that every legal vote is counted accurately, and that Alabama voters can trust their election process to be secure, transparent, and fair.
Improving Education
Education should be about academic excellence, not political indoctrination.
John Wahl believes in returning control of our schools to parents and local teachers where it belongs.
He supports school choice, so families can decide what’s best for their children – not government bureaucrats.
John believes it’s time to get red-tap out of the classroom and supports Donald Trump’s efforts to abolish the Federal Department of Education.
He’s committed to getting back to the basics: reading, writing, math, history, and civics — not critical race theory, gender ideology, or social activism.
John will fight to make sure Alabama classrooms are places of learning, not political battlegrounds.
Defending the Second Amendment
John Wahl is a lifelong defender of the Second Amendment.
He believes the right to bear arms is essential to liberty and security — not just for hunting or sport, but as a constitutional safeguard.
John will oppose any attempt to infringe on your rights and support legislation that strengthens and protects your ability to defend yourself and your family.
Standing Up for Life
John is proudly and unapologetically pro-life.
He believes that every child — born and unborn — is a precious gift from God.
He supports policies that protect the sanctity of life, support expecting mothers, and promote adoption as a life-affirming option.
As Lt.
Governor, John will continue to be a voice for the voiceless and a defender of the unborn.
Protecting Children from Woke Policies
Our children deserve innocence, not indoctrination.
John Wahl is committed to keeping radical, left-wing ideology out of Alabama classrooms and public institutions.
He will oppose efforts to introduce gender confusion, racial division, and sexualized content to our youth.
John will fight to ensure that schools and public policy reflect the values of Alabama families — not the agenda of Washington activists.
Standing for Alabama’s Farmers
Agriculture is the backbone of Alabama.
Raised on a family farm and now a farmer himself, John Wahl knows firsthand the hard work, sacrifice, and values that define Alabama’s agricultural communities.
He understands the challenges our farmers face, and he’ll be a tireless advocate for their needs at every level of Government.
Supporting Veterans
Freedom isn’t free — and we owe a debt of gratitude to those who served.
John Wahl is deeply committed to Alabama’s veterans.
He will work to ensure they receive the healthcare, education benefits, job opportunities, and respect they’ve earned.
John believes in cutting VA red tape, expanding services, and honoring our heroes not just with words, but with action.
Medical Freedom
No government should dictate your personal medical decisions.
John Wahl believes in informed consent, individual liberty, and protecting your right to choose what’s best for your health and your family.
He stood strong against unconstitutional mandates during the COVID 19 pandemic and will continue fighting to keep government out of your doctor’s office and your private choices.
Crime and Public Safety
Safe communities are the foundation of a strong society.
John Wahl supports our law enforcement officers and believes in holding criminals accountable.
He will work to make every one of our communities is safer, and expand resources for local police and first responders.
John also supports measures to combat human trafficking and protect our most vulnerable citizens.
John Wahl doesn’t just talk about Alabama values — he lives them.
With a proven track record of leadership, he’s ready to keep fighting for the people of Alabama as your next Lt.
Governor.
