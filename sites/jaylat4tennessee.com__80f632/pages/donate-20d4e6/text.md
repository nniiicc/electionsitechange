Frequently Asked Questions Is my donation tax-deductible?
Political contributions to Jayla Thomas for Tennessee State Representative are not tax-deductible as charitable donations for federal or state income tax purposes.
Where does my donation go?
Every contribution directly supports Jayla Thomas's grassroots campaign in District 55.
Donations help fund: Voter outreach and community engagement Campaign mailers and digital communications Events and town halls Yard signs and campaign materials Operational and compliance expenses Your support ensures we can connect with voters and share Jayla's vision for accountable, results-driven leadership.
Can I volunteer or get more involved?
Yes — and we'd love to have you involved.
This campaign is powered by community members who want real solutions and real leadership.
You can: Volunteer at events Help with voter outreach Host a neighborhood meet-and-greet Display a yard sign Join the digital team Visit our Volunteer page or contact us to get started.
Do you accept in-kind donations?
Yes.
We welcome in-kind contributions such as: Event space Printing Professional services Supplies or equipment All in-kind contributions must comply with Tennessee campaign finance regulations and will be properly reported.
Please contact the campaign before providing in-kind support to ensure compliance.
Can I donate in honor or in memory of someone?
Yes.
You may indicate in the donation notes if your contribution is in honor or in memory of someone.
If you would like the campaign to notify the honoree or their family, please include contact details. paid for by The Friends of Jayla Thomas Committee.
By donating, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old, making this contribution with your own funds, and not receiving reimbursement from any other person or entity.
Contributions are subject to Tennessee campaign finance laws.
