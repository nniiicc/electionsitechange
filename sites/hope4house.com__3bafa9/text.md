Hope Beard People over Politics Georgia State House 123 DONATE About Hope I am proud to be a lifelong Georgian and a graduate of Lincoln County High School, Paine College, and Troy University.
As a licensed Professional Counselor in both Georgia and Florida, I bring over a decade of expertise in behavioral health, mental wellness, consultation, and advocacy.
My professional experience, combined with my deep roots in this community, uniquely positions me to serve as your next State Representative for District 123.
As the founder and owner of a successful mental health practice in Lincolnton, I live by the motto: “Let Us Help You Put the Pieces of Your Life’s Puzzle Together.” This philosophy guides my work every day—not only in counseling individuals and families, but also in creating solutions that uplift and empower our entire community.
I’m also the proud founder of the House of Hope Wellness Center, a new initiative focused on youth development and mental wellness.
As a passionate small business owner, a devoted mother, and a longtime community leader, I understand firsthand the challenges our families, youth, and working professionals face.
My service extends beyond my profession.
I’m an active member of First Baptist Church of Lincolnton, where I support the church’s mission of service and mentor in its youth development programs.
I’m committed to building bridges that bring people together and strengthening the bonds that make District 123 a place we are all proud to call home.
I am running for the Georgia State House because our district deserves bold, compassionate, and effective leadership.

Medicaid Expansion Mental Health Georgia is one of just ten states that still hasn’t expanded Medicaid and it’s costing us lives, jobs, and rural hospitals.
Expanding Medicaid would give over 400,000 Georgians access to care and bring billions of federal dollars back into our state.
This isn’t about politics.
It’s about common sense, economic growth, and doing right by our people.
Too many Georgians are struggling in silence, not because they don’t want help, but because they can’t afford it or can’t access it.
I’m committed to increasing mental health funding, expanding community-based services, and ensuring every school has the resources to support students and staff alike.
Mental health is health and it's time we treat it that way.
