ICYMI: Josh Turek Launches Bipartisan “Educators for Turek” Coalition
Des Moines, IA – Yesterday, Josh Turek continued his “Team Iowa Tour” with the launch of “Educators for Turek,” a bipartisan coalition of Iowa educators who are committed to sending Josh to the U.S.
Senate because of his deep commitment to public education and public school employees.
Josh is building a campaign that can win, with a grassroots coalition of working class people from across Iowa and across the political spectrum.

WHO: Turek picks up new endorsement
- Democratic Senate candidate Josh Turek was also on the campaign trail today, picking up a new endorsement.
- “We want to make sure that every single American kid, but every single child from Iowa has access to a world-class education.
You shouldn’t have to win the genetic lottery to have access to a world-class education in this country.
Urban or rural, rich or poor, every single kid deserves a world-class education.”
- The Iowa State Education Association is supporting Turek to replace Joni Ernst in the Senate.
KCCI: Turek delivers keynote speech
- Turek talked about his plans for Iowa’s public schools.
He was the keynote speaker at the Iowa State Education Association’s summer leadership conference.
- He says Iowa needs to prioritize public education, arguing public funds should stay in public schools rather than vouchers.
- Turek believes it could bring more people to our state.
- “Families were moving here because they wanted to be educated.
This is certainly one of the very best places that we can start: prioritizing our public education and our post-secondary education.
If we do that, young people will come here, put down roots and want to stay here.”
The Gazette: Josh Turek campaign announces support from Iowa educators
- Iowa Democratic U.S.
Senate candidate Josh Turek on Friday announced the support of state educators in his campaign’s “Educators for Turek” coalition.
- Turek announced the support after speaking at the annual meeting of the Iowa State Education Association, the statewide teachers union.
- The Turek campaign said the educators are supporting Turek “because of his deep commitment to public education and the people who keep our public education system running.”
