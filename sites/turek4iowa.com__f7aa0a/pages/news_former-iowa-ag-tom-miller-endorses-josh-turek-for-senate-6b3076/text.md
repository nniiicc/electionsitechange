Former Iowa AG Tom Miller Endorses Josh Turek for Senate
Council Bluffs, IA – Today, former Iowa Attorney General Tom Miller endorsed Josh Turek for U.S.
Senate in Iowa.
Miller, who served as Attorney General for more than 40 years, said Turek “has a strong commitment to making sure doors are open for every Iowan.”
The endorsement follows the endorsements of more than 20 current and former Iowa elected officials last week.
