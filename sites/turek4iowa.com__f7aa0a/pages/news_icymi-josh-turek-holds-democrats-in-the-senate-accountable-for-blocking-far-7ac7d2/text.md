ICYMI: Josh Turek Holds Democrats in the Senate Accountable for Blocking Farm Bill
Josh puts Iowans first – always
Des Moines, IA – Last week, U.S.
Senate candidate Josh Turek condemned Democrats in the Senate for stalling the passage of the Farm Bill.
Josh has long said that Congress shouldn’t be allowed to adjourn until they pass a Farm Bill.
Since then, Iowans have been hearing all about how Josh will always put Iowans first, even when it means breaking with his party.
Missed it?
See below for the highlights.
The Des Moines Register: Josh Turek blasts Senate Democrats for blocking Farm Bill in committee
- Josh Turek, Iowa’s Democratic U.S.
Senate nominee, is blasting Senate Democrats for voting down a new Farm Bill in the Senate Agriculture Committee.
- Senate Democrats blocked the legislation in an Aug. 6 vote as they sought to secure a two-year delay in state cost-sharing requirements for the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) that will kick in as a result of Republicans’ One Big, Beautiful Bill.
- Republicans offered a one-year delay, but lacked the votes to pass the measure without the support of at least one Democrat on the committee.
- “Iowa farmers have been waiting three years for an overdue Farm Bill, and today Democrats in the Senate failed them by letting the perfect be the enemy of the good,” Turek said in a statement first provided to the Des Moines Register.
- “To be clear: I would have voted in support of this Farm Bill,” Turek said.
“This is just another example of a fundamentally broken Washington, and as Iowa’s next U.S. senator, I’ll work with anyone to pass a damn Farm Bill — and I’ll make it so Congress can’t recess without passing one.
Our farmers deserve no less.”
- The Agriculture Committee’s 10-11 vote on the Farm Bill means that the legislation, which hasn’t been updated since 2018, won’t be considered until the Senate returns after its August recess.
The committee’s chair, Sen.
John Boozman of Arkansas, said he will reconvene for a new vote in September.
