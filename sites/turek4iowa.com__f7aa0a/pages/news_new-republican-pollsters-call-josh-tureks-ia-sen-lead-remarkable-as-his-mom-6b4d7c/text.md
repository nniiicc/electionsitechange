NEW: Republican Pollsters Call Josh Turek’s IA-SEN Lead “Remarkable” as His Momentum Grows One Month Out from Election Day
Rasmussen Reports: “Turek’s lead is remarkable in a state Donald Trump won by a 13-point margin in 2024.”
GOP Pollster: “[T]he warning signs are clear: Iowa Republicans seem ambivalent, independents prefer Turek, and even [Hinson’s] supporters say they aren’t sure.”
Des Moines, IA – New polling by Fox News and Rasmussen Reports in the last 24 hours show Josh Turek leading Ashley Hinson by four points – including in a sample that voted for Trump by 14 points, even more than his 2024 margin of victory.
The state of the race for Hinson a month out from election day: “But the warning signs are clear: Iowa Republicans seem ambivalent, independents prefer Turek, and even her supporters say they aren’t sure,” said GOP pollster Daron Shaw, who works on the Fox News Poll with Democrat Chris Anderson.
What’s more: Josh continues to lead with critical voting blocks that will decide this race.
- Fox: “Independents favor Turek by two-to-one.”
- Rasmussen: “Among moderate voters, Turek leads by a 34-point margin, 63% to 29%.”
Hinson’s negative favorability continues to drag her down – even with her own party, as Josh picks up support from 14% of Republicans.
Nearly 4 in 10 respondents say their view of Hinson is “strongly” unfavorable.
What’s more: Josh has more enthusiastic support as “more of Turek’s backers are certain of their choice […] and twice as many Hinson supporters say they might change their mind.”
