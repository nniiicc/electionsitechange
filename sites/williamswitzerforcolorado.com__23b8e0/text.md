I’m William Switzer, and I am not a career politician.
I am a builder and a connector.
I have spent my life solving real-world problems and scaling operations from the ground up, and I know that our state is in dire need of a rebrand: one that represents the people who live here, not one that favors the political class.
My mission is simple: It is to strip power from the insiders and restore common-sense, bipartisan leadership to our government.

This will limit and eliminate the passing of extreme and damaging public policy coming from our statehouse.
This is exactly what the establishment is afraid of: the formation of a coalition dedicated to legislate for and protect the people of Colorado.
Colorado, we need an adult in the room.
Together we can bring forward a new era of leadership.
No more empty promises, hidden agendas, or leaders tied to special interest groups.
I will restore a direct line of trust and accountability back to the people.
I'm not running to join their club.
I'm running to break it.
Let me earn your vote.
