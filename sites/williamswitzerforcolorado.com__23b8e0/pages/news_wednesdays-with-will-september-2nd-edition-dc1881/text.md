Part of being a good neighbor is being involved in your community.
While one might argue there are many ways to be involved, I believe participating in local politics is perhaps one of the most overlooked ways.
Whether you’re tired of politics, overwhelmed by the extremes, or simply just can’t seem to care, our day-to-day experiences are often shaped through local politics.
We are governed by legislation, and the decisions made by those we elect directly impact our quality of life.
It’s hard to disagree that education is power.
One of the best ways to remain informed is from receiving information directly from the source.
I’ll admit, in politics the source is not often forthcoming in information.
There is a divide where parties and opponents offer solutions, though they don’t quite seem to solve current problems we face as a community.
This is why events like political debates, town halls, and public forums exist.
I often attend and recommend you do so as well.
They are the best way to see and hear from the options you’ll see on your ballot this fall, and make a decision about who to cast your vote for to represent you.
I am proud to announce my opponent and I have come to terms with a Forum of our own.
I personally want to invite you.

Reed Street.
Lakewood, Colorado 80232].
Those in attendance will be able to submit questions to the moderator.
Both of us will be answering those questions.
Your voice matters.
I want to hear directly from you, and as I promised from day one, I will listen to your priorities, and work to offer tangible solutions.
I recommend you come early, because you don’t want to miss this chance to get informed and be involved.
