Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute News / Wednesdays With Will (August 26th Edition) 26 Aug Wednesday, 8:50 PM · 2026 Wednesdays With Will (August 26th Edition) Original Vision.
Zero Echoes.
My expertise is in the business world.
I am specifically well credited for my involvement with startups.
From day one, I brought this strategy into my political campaign.
As an independent a mere 3 days before filing to run for this office, I saw the Republican Party as my vehicle for the freedom to be genuine and directly focus on putting people above politics.
So many candidates are tied to special interests, or even the promise of political party wins, over the will of the actual people they would represent.
I believe that is one of the biggest problems in politics.
As our campaign has grown and resonated with HD30 communities, we have watched many other campaigns with struggling identities copy and mirror our successes on both sides of the political aisle here in Colorado.
That is not unity; it is theft.
I am reminded of the old saying, “Imitation is the sincerest form of flattery." And while that may be permissible on paper or with a product, we are talking about representing people's lives here.
Candidates and elected leaders should be pioneers, not empty shells or simple echoes.
I have to admit, this is one of the biggest frustrations I have as a candidate.
Politics should not be this way.
Public servants should be educated, solution-based problem solvers, not status-seeking or naive.
I often rally against the lack of originality when I speak at events, and either it’s deflected or met with me being labeled the 'anti-tech and anti-AI candidate'.
I am the anti recycled message candidate.
I believe in original vision and zero echoes.
From parroted campaign speaker points to clearly AI drafted stances and websites full of empty promises, I ask where are the risk takers and the true problem solvers?
I see few and far between at least in Colorado's political sphere.
I sympathize with the voters when they constantly express whether these are really their choices.
The voters want solutions.
Not another political slogan or tagline.
Stop cutting corners.
We all see it.
We see it on your websites, we see it in your speeches, and we see it when questioned on actual issues.
If you cut corners on your campaign, it can only be assumed that when in office, you will do the same.
Counterfeit pioneers can’t guide Colorado forward.
True Colorado leadership is earned on the trail by charting the course.
Not copying the map.
I want to thank you all for the support with my campaign.
As it continues to grow, we are staying loyal to the original vision.
There will be zero echoing here.
We are going to flip the HD30 seat for the future, and return it back to the people.
That is how we Move Colorado Forward.
Together.
