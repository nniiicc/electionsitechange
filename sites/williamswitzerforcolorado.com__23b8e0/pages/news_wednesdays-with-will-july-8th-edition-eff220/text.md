Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute News / Wednesdays With Will (July 8th Edition) 8 Jul Wednesday, 10:00 AM · 2026 Wednesdays With Will (July 8th Edition) Ok, Colorado, let's talk about the primary.
I recently returned from Washington, D.C.
250 years of our great nation is something we should celebrate.
150 years of Colorado history is also a celebration.
As an American and a fellow Coloradan, I am proud of our nation, our state, and my fellow neighbors here in the communities of HD30.
Lakewood and Edgewater, what we saw in this primary cycle should raise some concern no matter what side of the political aisle you align with.
Let me break it down with what I am seeing and how my campaign will respond.
Our founding fathers did not expect us to agree.
They expected us to endure.
We are facing a crossroads here in Colorado.
This is not about who is specifically in our White House or governor's mansion.
Voters are frustrated with the process and a system that continues to put elected representatives' self-interests above the interests of who they are supposed to represent.
Across the state, we watched a clear trend emerge.
The Democrat Party is increasingly being pulled towards a socialist platform, represented by insurgent victories in key districts that favor radical structural change over steady practical governance.
Simultaneously, we are witnessing a collapse in energy on the right as large numbers of the Republican base stayed home, failing to participate in the democratic process.
Putting both of these issues together results in what we will see this fall.
The extremes dominate the conversation.
When this happens, the people of Colorado lose.
We should not be asked to choose between an agenda that seeks to dismantle our existing systems and a vision that feels increasingly disconnected from the practical realities of our daily lives.
What is before us is a political landscape that no longer looks like us.
Colorado does not need more polarization.
Our state needs adults in the room.
We need voices that understand that true progress is found in the middle.
This is where common sense, fiscal responsibility, and individual liberty meet.
My campaign has always been a platform that encourages this, and our messaging will not change as we see the arena move more and more towards polarization as each party seems to be adopting more extreme and fringe policies and talking points.
We will continue stepping up to offer a different way forward.
I believe in fixing the issues that matter most without being held to the rigid fear tactics of the left or the right.
I am committed to representing the vast, silent majority of Coloradans who are tired of the shouting matches and want to see actual results.
My campaign answers to the people, not to special interest groups or party bosses who prioritize keeping their seats over keeping their promises.
My promise remains clear and simple.
Practical solutions, not ideology.
A common ground agenda and accountability.
Colorado has a history of being a small l (libertarian) state.
Once a place where independent thinking was a badge of honor; now it is a badge of courage.
Let my campaign become a home to all: the disenfranchised, the alternatives, the moderates, and those who want to actually Move Colorado Forward, not just be the loudest in the rooms.
We have to reclaim this spirit as the general election is on the horizon.
If you are tired of being caught in the crossfire of the two growing extremes, join us.
Let us bring balance back to Colorado.
