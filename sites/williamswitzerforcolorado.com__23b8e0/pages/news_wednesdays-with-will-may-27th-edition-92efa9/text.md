Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute News / Wednesdays With Will (May 27th Edition) 27 May Wednesday, 5:00 PM · 2026 Wednesdays With Will (May 27th Edition) Rings and Responsibilities (My First Job) We’re almost to June and Colorado’s temperatures are definitely reflecting that now.
With the added pressure, it reminds me of my first job as a freshman in high school.
I worked at a tuxedo and jewelry store doing rentals and sales.
In that industry there are major expectations.
As a freshman, I was the youngest employee there.
However, that didn’t mean that I received special treatment.
I was treated the same as all the other salesman.
I didn’t know much about the lifestyle of my clients - those with far more money than I had.
So, I had to step up to the occasion and be the best I could possibly be to ensure their time in the shop reflected the service they expected.
This required goal-setting and discipline.
I’m grateful for this early practice, as it has impacted so many areas of my life today.
As school lets out, summer jobs begin for some, the workload for many parents increases as children are now home, and the uptick in business that summer presents takes root.
It is vital that we all take a moment to breathe and recognize these added challenges also present opportunities.
May we not let this summer simply pass us by.
There are a number of ways to get involved in your community this summer.
I’d like to invite you to a very specific one.

That’s right.
