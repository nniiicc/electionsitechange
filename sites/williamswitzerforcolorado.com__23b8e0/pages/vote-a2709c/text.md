Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute Voter Information Participating in voting is our civic duty.
Casting our vote each election cycle is one of the most important ways to have our voices heard.
Involvement in this process is how we encourage conversations and enact change at the national, state, and local levels of our government.
It is your right, as an American citizen, to choose your representation, ultimately protecting your way of life here in Colorado.
Please take time to make sure to register to vote, verify/update your voter information, and/or request your Colorado State Ballot.
Colorado's Election Day is Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026.
