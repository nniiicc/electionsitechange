Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute The Restoring Fiscal Responsibility Act Coloradans and their families are the masters of the budget.
We save when times are tough, we plan for the future, and we live within our means.
Why shouldn't our state government be held to the same standard?
For too long, politicians in Colorado have treated our tax dollars like an unlimited slush fund.
I will represent my district and our state with the Coloradan first standard.
This ideal has the full intent of restoring fiscal sanity to the Capital through commonsense everyday spending.
I will defend TABOR at all costs and build upon it.
The Taxpayers Bill of Rights is the last line of defense for our wallets.
I will fight to protect your right to vote on any tax increase and ensure that when the state collects more than it needs, that money is returned to you, not spent on expanding bureaucracy or added to another general fund to be used in any means.
I support full independent audits.
We need a top to bottom review of our state agencies.
If a program isn't producing measurable results, we shouldn't be funding it.
I will call for independent, performance-based audits to limit waste, fraud, and mismanagement.
Tied to my Legislative Clarity proposal, I would end the omnibus spending.
There has to be a Legislative Clarity standard established.
No more hiding multi-million dollar spending projects in thousands of pages of dense, unreadable legislation.
Every dollar must be transparent, debated, and voted on in plain sight.
I would lead a legislative measure attaching each tax dollar to be tied to a real-time tracking system.
This would regain the trust of voters and would hold legislators accountable for the spending of tax dollars.
