Zealous Zookeepers (A Child’s Business Venture)
The time is now upon us when troves of children are let out of school, and summer officially begins.
As we usher in summertime, I’m reminded of my school days of youth and the excitement that accompanied the feeling of freedom.
As a young boy, I couldn’t wait for the end of the school year.
It meant summer vacations, long days outside, and endless time with friends.
By high school, summer had taken on a different meaning as I started my first job, but it’s those earlier memories that I still cherish most.
One memory stands out above the rest: the summer my siblings, neighbors, and I decided to create a community zoo using our family pets and the occasional feral critter we found around the property.
Looking back, it may have been one of my first business ventures, though certainly not the most well-thought-out.
We didn’t charge admission, but we printed tickets, built makeshift enclosures, and organized exhibits featuring turtles, snakes, fish, and any other animals we could safely manage.
My brother wanted to be the “Crocodile Hunter” tour guide, while even at a young age, I found myself focused on managing logistics and operations.
Our family was known as the home that would take in just about any animal that crossed our path.
At one point, we had seven dogs, five cats, three horses, and even a guinea hen.
We were fortunate to have the space and resources not just to care for them, but to care for them well.
As a kid, I may not have appreciated the responsibility that came with cleaning stables, feeding animals, and keeping schedules organized.
But as an adult, I realize those experiences helped shape the disciplined and organized life I lead today.
Looking back now, I see summer wasn’t just about freedom; it was about growth.
I learned how to balance creativity with responsibility, fun with structure, and ambition with practicality.
Even those early “business ventures” taught me valuable lessons about leadership, teamwork, and knowing when to adapt when things became unmanageable.
That same mindset is what I hope to bring to House District 30: practical leadership, creative thinking, and a fresh approach to serving our community.
I’m excited to officially announce our campaign kickoff event at the end of this month.
This won’t be your traditional political gathering.
Instead, it will reflect the kind of leadership and community-centered approach our campaign hopes to bring to HD 30, welcoming, energetic, and focused on people first.
Join us as we kick off both summer and our campaign on Saturday, May 30th, at TROPITAI DELIGHTS.
Consider this your official invitation, even if we haven’t met yet.
Come enjoy an afternoon of conversation, community, and celebration for the future of our district.
Hope to see you there next week!
— William Switzer
