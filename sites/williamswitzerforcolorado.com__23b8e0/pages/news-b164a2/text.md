Read more 12 Aug Wednesday, 7:15 PM · 2026 Wednesday With Will (August 12th Edition) Top 3 Commonly Asked Questions: What political party do you belong to?
I am on the ballot this fall as a Republican.
I was Unaffiliated three days before filing, and as a result of changing my voting status, almost had to get a lawyer involved in order to run.
Running as a Republican guaranteed ballot access and prevented a three-way race.
I believe political parties should be vehicles, not a candidate's entire ethos.
I will work for you, the people, not the party.
Here is my Colorado Tracer account.
You can see all my donations have directly come from individual donors abiding under the legal limitations.
My campaign has taken ZERO money from any political party.
My campaign has taken ZERO money from groups or special interests.
Compared to my opponent and other Colorado candidates or elected leaders, my campaign and our movement is truly a grassroots one.
Do you really think Colorado can change?
What is the point of voting when both sides have terrible choices?
Yes.
I am optimistic if we get the right local, down-ballot leaders in office, it can, but our window is closing.
Let's face it, we are seeing a civil war unfold on the left between the Democratic Socialists of America (DSAs) and the Democratic establishment.
Alternatively, on the right side, the GOP's state party is a public mess.
Many are stuck in echo chambers, parroting speaking points and forgetting to offer real solutions.
I predict we have two election cycles left before our window for commonsense leadership in Colorado closes completely.
Colorado can change when presented with feasible policies, not empty promises.
Political leaders and candidates on both sides are failing to measure up.
They say the same thing: they'll solve problems.
But press them or ask them to explain and they fall apart because they do not have plans.
This is why I have released actual solutions to some of the problems fellow Coloradans have expressed.
I am continuing to add more policies as well to my website under my issues tab.
Like so many of you I believe talk is cheap, so I encourage you to check them out.
How would you rebuild trust and accountability?
I would champion transparency on every level.
Many of my proposed legislations both add and create a new standard of accountability and transparency for Coloradans.
My voting record will follow the people's majority, not always just the Republican.
On every vote and decision made, I would publicly come online to discuss it more in-depth.
I truly intend on flipping this HD seat for the future, not a political party.
I commit to being accessible to those I would represent.
I would host monthly town halls and open office hours.
You deserve a seat at the table, and your representation should be made available for you to communicate openly with.
Something has to change.
I know the political system.
I do not want to be part of their "club".
If you give me the opportunity, I will go in there and take a hammer to it.
The era of the protected political class is over.
Representatives answer to the people, and that starts with accessibility.
