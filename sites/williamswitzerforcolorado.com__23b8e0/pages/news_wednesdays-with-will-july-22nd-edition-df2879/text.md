Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute News / Wednesdays With Will (July 22nd Edition) 22 Jul Wednesday, 10:00 AM · 2026 Wednesdays With Will (July 22nd Edition) With all the talk of unity, there is an elephant in the room that must be addressed.
Primary season is over.
Like many campaign cycles before, both parties have sounded the call for unity from within.
But I challenge you with this: What are you unifying for?
Is it simply against the opposition or is it more than that?
In 103 days, the future of Colorado could drastically change.
It is no secret the Democratic Socialists of America (DSAs) walked away with major, decisive wins in their primary, arguably pushing the Democratic Party even further left.
It is time for the Republican Party to move beyond talking, or even messaging.
I fear our party will simply move further right.
That is not the solution or what is best for Coloradans.
The voters deserve better.
Let me be clear, these races were won with three simple tactics: a message of perceived connection, an infrastructure of active volunteers, and the financial backing to push these campaigns through the finish line.
My warning is the primary was just the beginning.
If Republicans want a chance to compete in the election, it is not only necessary to match the energy of the DSAs, but go above and beyond.
It is time to step up.
We have to outwork our opposition.
We have to get voters to the polls.
And we have to have winnable solutions because talk is cheap.
Last night I spoke at an event where I urged fellow Republican and Unaffiliated candidates who fall under the banner of freedom and liberty to put forward tangible solutions.
I also announced the publication on my solutions.
On Monday, I released a policy that addressed the housing crisis Lakewood and Edgewater face.
Not only am I educated in public policy, but I have hands-on experience in shaping policies over the years.
This experience means on day one I will not start working; I will have already been working to represent and legislate according to the needs and desires of my constituents with transparent policies to propose.
I am not like the numerous candidates you have heard from thus far.
I will not continue to deliver rallying speaker points.
Rather, I present to you legitimate solutions.
I live in Lakewood.
This past special election has my district on high alert.
We have to reevaluate our leadership in HD30.
We can not expect to Move Colorado Forward if we do not work to solve the problems of our own neighbors.
I look forward to continue this as your next State Representative for Edgewater and Lakewood this fall.
