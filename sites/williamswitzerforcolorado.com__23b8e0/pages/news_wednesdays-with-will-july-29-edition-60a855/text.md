My campaign hinges on the notion to Move Colorado Forward, and to do so, this means being active and engaged in my own backyard.
I have attended multiple City Council and Ward meetings.
Many of you reading this came to say hello or met me for the first time at one of those meetings.
Every person I’ve talked with agrees on this: Get involved.
Volunteer for a candidate.
Attend local government meetings.
Email Council members your concerns and show up to hold them accountable for answers.
The time for talk is behind us.
It’s time for action.
I will personally be in attendance at Lakewood’s joint Ward 2 and Ward 3 meeting next Wednesday, August 5th.
I’d love to meet you - no matter what side of politics you’re on and have a conversation, as neighbors, about the future of our incredible city.
It might surprise you to find that we agree on more than you think, and together we can find a way to Move Colorado Forward.
