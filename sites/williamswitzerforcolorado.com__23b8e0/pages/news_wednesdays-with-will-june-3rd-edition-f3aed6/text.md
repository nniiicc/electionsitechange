Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute News / Wednesdays With Will (June 3rd edition) 3 Jun Wednesday, 7:00 PM · 2026 Wednesdays With Will (June 3rd edition) One of the biggest misconceptions about newcomers to politics is that they lack an understanding of public policy and how government works.
In my case, that couldn't be further from the truth.
I hold a Master's Degree in Public Policy and have worked on campaigns at the local, state, and national levels.
That experience means I will be ready on day one to begin delivering on my campaign promises, bringing greater transparency to government, and fighting for meaningful change in our Edgewater and Lakewood communities.
My education taught me how legislation is drafted, how bipartisan coalitions are built, and how effective public policy is created through results not rhetoric.
I've put those lessons into practice by helping candidates develop policy platforms and campaign strategies focused on solving problems rather than scoring political points.
My first campaign experience was helping elect a statewide official who, much like me, came from the business world rather than a political career.
At the time, voters wanted change not another out-of-touch politician.
Our team worked tirelessly, emerging from a crowded primary field to earn the nomination and ultimately win the general election.
Four years later, after a successful first term and a record of delivering results, voters rewarded that leadership with an overwhelming reelection victory.
That experience taught me one of the most important lessons in public service: elected officials must represent the people they serve, not themselves.
Your personal beliefs matter far less than the priorities of your constituents.
If I am elected to represent House District 30, I will represent everyone in Edgewater and Lakewood including those who may not vote for me.
This campaign is bigger than political parties.
It's about putting our communities first.
I am not backed by special interests, nor do I view this office as a stepping stone to higher office.
I am running because I believe public service should once again be about service.
Like many of you, I am tired of politicians making promises they never intend to keep.
As your neighbor, I want to restore accountability and remind those in government who they ultimately work for: the people.
Following that first successful campaign, I worked on a local race that increased voter turnout and brought newcomers into the political process.
From there, I joined a national campaign as a state field organizer where I gained valuable experience managing large-scale operations and learned the same principle applies at every level of government: listening to the people matters.
Today, I am no longer working behind the scenes.
I have stepped forward because our community deserves a representative who listens before legislating, puts people over politics, and is focused on moving Colorado forward not simply further left or further right.
To everyone who attended our campaign launch party, thank you.
We saw an incredible turnout from people of all political backgrounds.
What stood out most was the return of something our politics desperately needs: conversation.
Despite what social media and political echo chambers often suggest, we have far more in common than what divides us.
I also want to thank the many volunteers who signed up to support our campaign.
Your energy and commitment are helping build a grassroots movement focused on common sense, accountability, and our community.
If you'd like to join us, I encourage you to sign up for one of our volunteer opportunities.
Together, we can bring new leadership to House District 30 and build a stronger future for the communities of Edgewater and Lakewood.
Let’s flip the HD30 seat for the future.
Together, we can Move Colorado Forward.
