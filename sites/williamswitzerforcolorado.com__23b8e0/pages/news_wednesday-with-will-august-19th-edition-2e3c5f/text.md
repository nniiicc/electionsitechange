Home My Story Public Policy Goals News Connect Get Involved Join an Event Contribute News / Wednesday With Will (August 19th Edition) 19 Aug Wednesday, 8:00 PM · 2026 Wednesday With Will (August 19th Edition) I have always loved August.
Everyone is usually slowing down, soaking in the final days of summer or rushing to cash in some last minute PTO before the kids transition back to school.
This bittersweet season of life ushers in important change and growth, and relishes the notion of living life with your loved ones.
In the business world it is the month that really can set you apart from the pack.
If you really lock in and hold, your discipline starts to show.
This month, I’ve been releasing my public policies to further encourage growth and stability not just for HD30, but for Coloradans across the state.
I recently released my education act.
The education system as a whole needs support.
Legislators must listen to those on the frontlines and be the connection that teachers and parents are seeking for what is best for the students.
Teachers, educators, families, I assure you, with a wide range of varying educational experience as a student under my belt, and a wife who has worked as an educator and curriculum developer for almost a decade, I have your back.
The day to day efforts of those in the field are too often overlooked and under appreciated.
I see you and I am committed to listening.
My goals are to support you in your careers, assist parents, and ultimately ensure each child here in HD30, and our state, gets the educational process best suited for them and their families.
We can fix this if all of us work together.
I will lead that charge.
