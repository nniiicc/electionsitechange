Coosa County Sheriff Michael Howell Endorses Katherine Robertson for Alabama Attorney General
Birmingham, AL – Coosa County Sheriff Michael Howell today announced his endorsement of Katherine Robertson in her campaign for Alabama Attorney General.
Sheriff Howell, a lifelong resident of Coosa County and dedicated law enforcement leader, praised Robertson's commitment to public safety, respect for the rule of law, and strong support for those who serve on the front lines.
"As Sheriff of Coosa County, my first responsibility is protecting the citizens I serve.
That mission requires strong partnerships with leaders who respect the rule of law and support law enforcement.
That’s why I’m proud to endorse Katherine Robertson for Attorney General.
Our state needs an Attorney General who will stand up for victims and ensure our laws are enforced fairly and effectively.
I’m confident Katherine Robertson has the leadership and dedication to serve Alabama and be a strong partner to law enforcement across the state," Sheriff Howell stated.
Robertson, currently serving as Chief Counsel to Alabama Attorney General Steve Marshall, has built a reputation as a staunch defender of conservative values, victims' rights, and law enforcement priorities.
Her extensive experience in the Attorney General's Office positions her as a proven leader ready to tackle the challenges facing Alabama on day one.
"I am grateful for Sheriff Michael Howell's endorsement and for his unwavering dedication to keeping Coosa County and all of Alabama safe," said Katherine Robertson.
"Sheriff Howell's service exemplifies the courage and commitment that our state relies on every day.
I look forward to continuing to partner with Sheriff Howell and law enforcement leaders across Alabama to protect our communities, uphold the rule of law, and ensure justice for victims."
This endorsement is the latest in a groundswell of law enforcement support of Robertson, which she credits to her deep connection and longstanding partnership among the Alabama men and women behind the badge.
