Tuscaloosa County Sheriff Ron Abernathy Endorses Katherine Robertson for Alabama Attorney General
Birmingham, AL – Today, Katherine Robertson, Conservative Republican candidate for Alabama Attorney General, announced the endorsement of Tuscaloosa County Sheriff Ron Abernathy.
Robertson made the announcement on Tuscaloosa’s 95.3FM The Bear’s “Steve and DC Show.”
Sheriff Abernathy, a respected law enforcement leader who has served the Tuscaloosa County Sheriff's Office since 1988 and as Sheriff since his first election in 2015, touted Robertson's extensive record of supporting law enforcement, fighting criminal enterprises across Alabama, and standing up for the rights of victims.
His endorsement is the latest addition to Robertson's impressive backing from sheriffs and law enforcement professionals across the state.
"Katherine Robertson has been on the front lines of Alabama's toughest battles against federal overreach, violent crime, and policies that undermine law enforcement," said Sheriff Ron Abernathy.
"As Chief Counsel to Attorney General Steve Marshall, she has consistently stood with law enforcement to keep our communities safe and uphold the rule of law.
Alabama needs a strong, conservative Attorney General who will continue that fight without apology, and Katherine is that leader.
I am proud to endorse her and encourage all Alabamians to support her campaign."
Having served in the Attorney General’s office for the last ten years as Chief Counsel, Robertson’s record of leading efforts to combat illegal immigration, protecting women's sports, defending free speech, and taking down career criminals, is proving to clearly set her apart from the field in the race.
"Sheriff Abernathy is a dedicated public servant who has spent decades protecting the people of Tuscaloosa County, and I could not be more excited to have his support in this race" said Katherine Robertson.
"Public servants like Sheriff Abernathy are the backbone of law enforcement in Alabama, and they know I will always have their back in the fight against crime and for public safety."
