She’s the daughter of Joy and Kincey Green and a graduate of Auburn University and the University of Alabama School of Law. ​ Katherine began her public service in Washington as clerk and counsel to Senator Jeff Sessions on the U.S.
Senate Judiciary Committee — standing up for the Constitution and America First principles.
