Campaign contributions help political campaigns cover the practical costs of communicating with voters and operating a campaign.
Depending on the campaign, contributions may help fund: Community Outreach -- Connecting with residents and sharing information about campaign positions and priorities.
Voter Communications -- Producing and distributing mail, digital communications, and other informational materials.
Campaign Events -- Supporting community meetings, town halls, and other opportunities for residents to engage with the campaign.
Digital Outreach -- Maintaining the campaign website and communicating through digital platforms.
Advertising -- Paying for television, radio, print, digital, and other campaign advertising.
Campaign Operations -- Covering administrative expenses, printing, technology, and other costs associated with running a campaign.
Your Contribution Matters Every campaign operates on a budget, and contributions provide financial resources that can be used for campaign activities throughout an election cycle.
Maryland campaign-finance laws govern who may contribute, how much may be contributed, and how campaigns must report contributions and expenditures.
