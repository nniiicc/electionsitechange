Public Safety
Focus on community safety, support for law enforcement, and policies that hold individuals accountable for criminal behavior.
Emphasize collaboration between law enforcement, residents, and community organizations to address crime and improve neighborhood safety.
Limited Government
Advocate for a government that remains accountable to taxpayers and respects individual rights.
This includes examining government spending, reducing unnecessary regulation, and ensuring that state government decisions remain transparent and responsive to the people it serves.
Economic Growth
Support policies intended to encourage private-sector investment, strengthen small businesses, and expand employment opportunities in Washington County.
A strong local economy can help residents build careers, support their families, and keep businesses investing in the community.
Education
Support parents having a meaningful voice in their children's education while promoting strong academic standards and educational opportunities.
As an educator, Thiam's background also provides experience with the challenges facing Maryland's schools and students.
As a current special education teacher in Washington County Public Schools, Brenda believes in the power of education and its importance for a viable society.
Brenda supports school choice and advocates for parents to decide where their student attends school; especially if the child is in a failing school and the tax dollars must follow the student.
She also supports the B.O.O.S.T. program, which stands for Broadening Options and Opportunities for Students Today.
Scholarship funding is available for low income students and families in grades K-12 to attend eligible non-public or private schools in Maryland.
Family Values
Focus on policies affecting families, parental involvement, personal responsibility, and opportunities for children to succeed.
This includes recognizing the importance of stable families and strong community institutions.
Opioid & Substance Abuse
Address substance abuse as both a public-safety and community issue.
Efforts can include prevention, education, treatment resources, and accountability while working with families, schools, healthcare providers, law enforcement, and community organizations.
Between June 2025-July 2026, Washington County has had 50 total deaths.
One death due to opioids or fentanyl is too many, and we will work collaboratively with our local partners to drive down these numbers.
Responsible Taxation
Examine the impact of state taxes and regulations on working families, homeowners, and small businesses.
Support responsible budgeting and careful consideration of how state revenues are collected and spent.
With the increase in MVA fees; 3% IT tax increase for businesses; emissions fee increase, to name a few, Marylanders are suffering and the legislature can effect positive change.
Government Accountability
Promote transparency in government and responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
Residents should be able to understand how decisions are made, how public funds are being spent, and how elected officials are held accountable.
With recent audits across several state agencies, 1.2 billion dollars is unaccounted for, and taxpayers should be concerned.
Findings in the audits have occurred year after year, and an Inspector General would help mitigate this problem.
