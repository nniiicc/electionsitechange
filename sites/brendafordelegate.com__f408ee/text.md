I'm Brenda Thiam, and I'm running for State Delegate in Hagerstown, Maryland, because I believe our community deserves strong, experienced leadership that puts people first.
I will fight for common sense solutions that create opportunity, support our families, and protect the values that make our community strong.
Experienced Leadership.
Stronger Communities.
Brenda Thiam understands that effective leadership starts with listening.
As a former Maryland State Delegate, educator, and longtime advocate for individuals with disabilities and their families, Brenda has dedicated her career to serving others.
Her experience in education, community service, and state government has given her a firsthand understanding of the challenges facing families throughout Hagerstown and Washington County.
Brenda believes District 2B deserves a strong voice in Annapolis - someone who will stand up for public safety, responsible government, economic opportunity, quality education, and the families who make our community strong.
