Texas House District 133
Located in west Harris County, House District 133 includes parts of Houston along with the communities of Bunker Hill Village, Hedwig Village, Hunters Creek Village, and Piney Point Village.
The district sits at the heart of the Energy Corridor and serves as a hub for both global industry and local families.
HD133 is defined by a unique mix of economic strength, established neighborhoods, and critical infrastructure.
Residents here value strong schools, safe communities, and a business climate that supports both major employers and small businesses.
Some of the more notable features of HD133 include:
- Buffalo Bayou & Terry Hershey Park trail system
- The Energy Corridor and major employment centers
- CityCentre and Town & Country Village
- Memorial City District (Mall & Medical Center)
- HISD and Spring Branch ISD schools
- I-10 / Katy Freeway and Westheimer corridors
This balance of economic opportunity and community stability is what makes HD133 worth fighting for.
