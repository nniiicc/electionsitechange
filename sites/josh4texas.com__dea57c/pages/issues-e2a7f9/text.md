The Issues

Josh Wallenstein wants the Texas government to work for all Texans, especially those who live in HD133.
This means focusing on core matters that impact everyone.
Fight Corruption
Josh Wallenstein has spent more than two decades as an anti-corruption attorney fighting fraud, misconduct, and abuses of power.
He believes that our government is working for them, not for politically connected insiders or special interests.
In the Texas House, Josh will fight for stronger ethics rules, greater transparency, and tougher safeguards against conflicts of interest.
End Pay-to-Play Politics
Government contracts and public policy should never be rewards for political donors or well-connected insiders.
Josh supports stronger transparency and ethics rules to prevent campaign contributions, personal relationships, or political influence from determining who benefits from taxpayer dollars.
Texans deserve to know that decisions are being made based on what is best for their communities, not who wrote the biggest campaign check.
Crack Down on No-Bid Contracts
Taxpayer dollars should be spent openly, competitively, and responsibly.
Texas procurement rules generally call for competitive bidding whenever possible, but state law also allows other procurement methods in certain circumstances.
Josh supports strengthening competitive bidding requirements, increasing transparency around contracts awarded without competition, and requiring greater scrutiny when taxpayer-funded contracts are handed out without an open bidding process.
No company should get special treatment simply because it has the right political connections.
Ban Stock Trading with Campaign Funds
Campaign contributions should be used for legitimate campaign and officeholder purposes, not to build investment portfolios.
Current Texas ethics rules expressly recognize that political contributions can be held in investments such as stocks and bonds, and candidates and officeholders must report certain investments purchased with political contributions.
Josh supports banning candidates and officeholders from using campaign funds to buy, sell, or trade individual stocks and other securities.
Political contributions should not become investment accounts for politicians.
Campaign dollars should be used for their intended political purpose, with clear rules that reduce opportunities for conflicts of interest and financial self-dealing.
Require Accountability for Data Centers
Data centers can bring investment and jobs to Texas, but they also place enormous demands on our electric grid, water supply, and local infrastructure.
Josh supports stronger transparency and oversight so communities understand the costs and impacts before major projects are approved.
Local residents should have a real opportunity to weigh in on where data centers are built, how much water and electricity they use, and what tax incentives they receive.
Big corporations should not get special deals behind closed doors while families and small businesses are left paying higher costs.
Make Government Work for Texans
For Josh, fighting corruption is about more than ethics rules.
It is about making government work better and making sure taxpayer dollars actually serve the public.
When contracts are handed out based on connections, campaign money becomes an investment portfolio, or special interests have more influence than ordinary Texans, trust in government suffers.
Josh will bring the same approach he has taken throughout his career as an anti-corruption attorney to Austin: follow the facts, follow the money, hold powerful interests accountable, and make sure the same rules apply to everyone.
Business Efficiency
Texas’s reputation as the Business Capital of the World is at risk.
Unfortunately, our leaders have lost sight of their pro-business roots.
As a small business owner, Josh has seen firsthand how our current anti-capitalist, anti-free market policies have raised taxes and increased red tape – making it harder for small businesses to compete.
Josh will pursue win-win policies that reduce barriers, cut waste, and lower costs so both employees and employers succeed.
Smart Energy Policy
Texas’s energy industry is the backbone of our economy, and Josh will fight to protect good-paying oil and gas jobs while also ensuring the industry remains competitive in a changing global market.
He believes smart policy can safeguard these jobs, support workers, and keep energy affordable without raising costs for families.
Accessibility
Families in HD 133 live next to world-class hospitals, but too many can’t afford to access them.
Rising premiums and out-of-pocket costs are squeezing families because our legislature has failed to act.
Increase Coverage
Because of the failure of state leadership, Texas has the highest uninsured rate in the country.
When uninsured patients can’t pay their medical bills, those costs get shifted to everyone else through higher premiums and hospital charges.
That means we are all paying more for less.
Simple Solutions
Josh believes smart policy can reduce the uninsured rate, expand access, and lower costs at the same time — ensuring every family can afford quality care in their own backyard.
Our leaders have blocked reforms and allowed costs to spiral; Josh will deliver solutions that improve care while protecting taxpayers.
