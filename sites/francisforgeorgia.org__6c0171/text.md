Francis Putting The People First Who Am I?
“I’m Darius L.
Francis, a U.S.
Army veteran, entrepreneur, and community advocate running to represent District 115 in Dekalb County and Henry County.
I was raised in South Miami by my grandmother in low-income housing.
Growing up with hard work, discipline, and the ability to do more with less shaped my perspective on the world.
I believe everyone deserves genuine choices in representation—leaders who can echo their concerns and fight for them in the places where decisions are made, rather than just voices that talk at them.
I served as a Sergeant in the United States Army, completing two combat tours in Afghanistan.
This experience instilled in me the values of leadership under pressure, accountability, and the importance of protecting the people you serve.
After my military service, I transitioned into entrepreneurship and community advocacy, engaging with individuals from diverse backgrounds to listen to their struggles, goals, and frustrations with a system that often feels disconnected.
Why I’m Running?
“South Dekalb and Henry County are experiencing rapid growth.
This can either be a blessing or a burden, depending on who bears the cost.
I’m running to ensure that responsible growth in our communities benefits residents, not just developers; that homeowners aren’t priced out of their own neighborhoods; that our infrastructure, schools, and public safety measures keep pace with the growth; and that the needs of seniors and working families are respected and not overlooked.
I believe that government should be accountable, practical, and deeply rooted in the real lives of the people it represents.
My goal is straightforward: to be a voice you recognize, a leader you can reach, and a representative who shows up and gets the important work done.
The Issues Responsible Growth & Development What’s happening: Large-scale development proposals in Dekalb County and Henry County can bring jobs and opportunity but also strain roads, schools, water, and community character.
My stance: Support property rights and responsible growth through effective community advocacy.
It is essential to require transparent impact reviews to ensure accountability.
Additionally, I advocate for impact fees so developers contribute to funding roads, schools, and public safety.
Local Control & De-Annexation What it means: Communities in Dekalb County and Henry County shouldn’t be trapped in bureaucratic gridlock over who controls property, zoning, and planning.
My stance: Support community advocacy to keep decision-making close to the people, prevent overreach, and create clear, fair timelines that prioritize public safety.
Additionally, we should encourage collaboration between the county, city, and residents to ensure responsible growth.
Property Taxes & Strong Schools Property Taxes & Strong Schools Property Taxes & Strong Schools The reality in Dekalb County and Henry County is that families are stretched, and schools are growing rapidly.
We need responsible budgeting and outcomes that align with our investment to ensure public safety and promote responsible growth in our communities.
My stance: Support community advocacy for property tax protections and transparency, allocate funds into classrooms rather than bureaucracy, and expand high-quality options such as career pathways, charters, and targeted support while strengthening public schools.
Transportation & Infrastructure Property Taxes & Strong Schools Property Taxes & Strong Schools What residents of Dekalb County and Henry County feel: congestion, road wear, and growth pressure.
My stance on these issues is rooted in community advocacy for targeted road and traffic fixes that enhance public safety and provide much-needed relief.
We must ensure that responsible growth contributes to necessary upgrades, while also opposing wasteful spending and prioritizing initiatives that genuinely benefit both residents and commerce.
Public Safety & Crime My Stance I support community advocacy for well-trained, well-equipped law enforcement in Dekalb County and Henry County that prioritizes public safety.
We must expand victim support and utilize effective tools, including evaluating technology like FLOCK cameras, while ensuring clear privacy standards.
It’s essential to focus on repeat violent offenders and implement prevention strategies that foster responsible growth and keep youth from cycling into crime, promoting healthier neighborhoods.
"We don’t have to choose between safety and accountability.
We need both." PRIORITIES FOR SENIORS & FAMILIES Healthcare Access & Affordability for Seniors and Working Families Healthcare is often too expensive and confusing.
My focus is practical: to help people in Dekalb County and Henry County access the programs they already qualify for, reduce red tape, and expand real access points for care, especially for seniors.
Through community advocacy, we can ensure that both public safety and responsible growth are prioritized in our healthcare initiatives.
Help seniors lower costs (Medicare counseling, prescription cost help, caregiver support) Expand access locally (clinics, telehealth, mental health access) Cut the paperwork barriers so eligible families can actually get coverage.
