Senator Fahy Announces $50,000 Grant for Rotterdam-Carman Little League for Updated Turf and New Batting Cages
Senator Patricia Fahy announced that $50,000 in capital funds were secured through her office for Rotterdam-Carman Little League (RC Little League).
Formerly Rotterdam and Carman Little Leagues, the groups merged in 2014 to form RC Little League.
Carman Little League dates back decades in the Capital Region and had a famous Little League World Series run in 1977.
Just last year, the RC Little League 10U All-Star team took home 4th place at the 2025 New York State Little League 8-10-year-old Championship Tournament.
