Platform
An Illinois for All
My platform is built on community empowerment, justice, and sustainability.
I’m committed to policies that uplift working families, the working poor, and low-income communities — groups that have been ignored for far too long — while protecting our environment and ensuring every Illinoisan has access to the resources they need to thrive.
Community-Centered Governance
Power back to the people through transparency, accountability, and government that serves communities — not corporate interests.
Justice & Human Rights for All
A commitment to criminal justice reform, divestment from war and oppression, reparations, immigrant and refugee protections, and equitable treatment for every resident.
Sustainable
Futures
Environmental stewardship, climate justice, public health, and high-quality education that prepares and protects future generations.
