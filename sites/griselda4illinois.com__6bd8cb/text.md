My name is Griselda Romero and I’m running for Governor to return power to the people and rebuild a system rooted in community, justice, and integrity.
About Griselda
In the News
What I Stand For
I’m running for Governor to put people first.
Illinoisans deserve leadership that empowers communities, invests in our future, and prioritizes the well-being of residents — not just corporations or the wealthy few.
Our state deserves a leader who delivers results, acts with integrity, and works with constituents to build a system that truly benefits everyone.
Together, we can create a government that serves as a steward, helping communities thrive and bridging the gaps that have held us back.
Join me in building a new system — by the people, for the people — where equity, justice, economic opportunity, and environmental stewardship guide every decision.
Illinois can lead the nation in transformation, and together, we will make it happen.
