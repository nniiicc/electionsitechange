Carl Ingwell is running for Utah House District 67 because he believes every family deserves a representative who listens to their concerns and fights for their interests in the State Legislature.
With deep roots in the community, Carl understands the challenges facing Utah families—from education funding to healthcare access to protecting our beautiful public lands.
Environmentalist Protecting Utah's lands Community Leader Active in local initiatives Key Issues What Carl will fight for in the State House Education Investing in our schools and teachers to give every Utah child the opportunity to succeed.
Supporting fair funding and smaller class sizes.
Healthcare Access Working to expand healthcare access and lower costs for Utah families.
No one should go bankrupt because they got sick.
Public Lands Protecting Utah's beautiful public lands for future generations while supporting responsible recreation and conservation.
Economy & Jobs Supporting small businesses and creating good-paying jobs.
Building an economy that works for everyone, not just the wealthy.
Affordable Housing Addressing Utah's housing crisis with smart solutions that help families afford to live in the communities they love.
Water & Environment Protecting Utah's water resources and addressing air quality issues to ensure a healthy environment for all.
