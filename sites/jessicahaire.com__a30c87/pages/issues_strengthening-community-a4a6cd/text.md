Communities thrive when elected officials are active participants in fostering trust, opportunity, and making sure people feel both supported and invested in where they live.
That starts with practical foundations: reliable schools, safe neighborhoods, and personal investment.
I have seen firsthand the importance of strengthening communities through my volunteer service with Court Appointed Special Advocates (CASA) and work as a former commissioner on the Anne Arundel County Commission for Women.
As a Court Appointed Special Advocate, I volunteer for children in foster care, fighting to find them a permanent home and an opportunity to thrive.
Every child deserves support, care, and a path forward.
On the Commission for Women, I worked with community leaders to expand opportunity and enrich the lives of women and families in Anne Arundel County.
Together we can help District 30 build a strong future.
