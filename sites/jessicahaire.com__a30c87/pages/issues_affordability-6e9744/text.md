Affordability
Fighting to lower costs and make Maryland more affordable for working families and small businesses.
Marylanders are working harder than ever, but too many of us are feeling the squeeze from high taxes, rising fees, higher gas prices, higher grocery prices, and unaffordable utility bills.
I've heard from countless neighbors, parents, teachers, and public safety officers that the cost of living in our community is unsustainable.
You deserve better.
We need elected officials in Annapolis who are willing to chart a different course - one that facilitates innovation, entrepreneurship, and fosters an environment where families and small businesses thrive.
As the next Senator for District 30, I am committed to making YOUR life more affordable by providing solutions, not lip-service.
I'll work to find innovative, flexible mechanisms to address escalating fees and energy bills, putting more money back in your pockets.
I'll push for smarter budgeting, prioritizing essential public services and long term investments in quality of life improvements, while preserving critical services for our vulnerable populations, our seniors, and those with disabilities.
