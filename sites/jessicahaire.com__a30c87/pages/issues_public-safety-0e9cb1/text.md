Public Safety
Building safe communities through honest partnerships between law enforcement and residents.
Families shouldn't have to worry about whether their children can walk to school safely, small businesses shouldn't fear vandalism, and neighborhoods shouldn't feel unheard when crime rises.
I believe strong, safe communities are built through honest partnerships between law enforcement, local government, and the people we serve.
Residents of District 30 feel the impact of repeat violent offenders, property crime, and substance misuse.
These issues aren't solved by slogans or empty promises - they require common-sense strategies, accountability, and a commitment to both supporting our officers and addressing the needs of those affected.
As State Senator, I will work every day to ensure our families, neighborhoods, and small businesses are protected and respected.
That means strengthening penalties for repeat violent offenders while investing in victim services and support for survivors, so those who have been harmed by crime have access to counseling, restitution, and justice without delay.
I will continue to work with law enforcement, community leaders, and residents to find real solutions that protect the residents and businesses of District 30, while ensuring victims and survivors receive the justice and care they deserve.
