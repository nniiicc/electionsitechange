Bipartisan Leadership.
Practical Results.
As a member of the Anne Arundel County Council, I worked across party lines to advance legislation addressing real challenges facing families, homeowners, veterans, first responders, and the environment.
As the lead sponsor of more bipartisan legislation that became law than any other member of the County Council, I have a record of working across party lines to deliver results.
From protecting the Chesapeake Bay to strengthening the county’s financial position, my record reflects a commitment to bipartisan leadership and improving the quality of life in our communities.
Here are a few examples of my bipartisan legislative record:
Protecting the Chesapeake Bay and Improving Quality of Life
Leading efforts to address failing septic systems
I was the lead sponsor of bipartisan legislation establishing a program to help convert failing septic systems to public sewer.
Failing septic systems contribute to nitrogen pollution in the Chesapeake Bay and can negatively affect the quality of life for homeowners.
These landmark pieces of legislation helped protect the Chesapeake Bay and save taxpayer dollars.
Supporting Those Who Serve
Property tax credits for service-disabled veterans
I was the lead sponsor of bipartisan legislation to establish property tax credits for service-disabled veterans, helping recognize the sacrifices made by those who served our country.
Property tax credits for public safety officers
I was the lead sponsor of bipartisan legislation establishing property tax credits for public safety officers, supporting the men and women who help keep Anne Arundel County communities safe.
Removing barriers to property tax credits
I was the lead sponsor of bipartisan legislation removing the fee for individuals applying for property tax credits, helping make the application process more accessible.
Strengthening County Finances
Raising the revenue reserve fund limit
I was the lead sponsor of bipartisan legislation raising the county's revenue reserve fund limit (the equivalent of the county’s “Rainy Day Fund”).
The measure helped strengthen Anne Arundel County's financial position and contributed to an improvement in its bond rating.
Responsible financial management requires planning for the future, protecting taxpayers, and ensuring local government has the resources to meet its obligations.
This legislation received unanimous cosponsorship from my colleagues on the Anne Arundel County Council.
Encouraging Innovation and Economic Opportunity
Maximizing public-private partnerships
I was the lead sponsor of bipartisan legislation requiring the county to maximize public-private partnerships ‘when in the best interest of the county and community.
Public-private partnerships can create opportunities to deliver services and infrastructure more efficiently while leveraging private-sector resources and expertise.
Protecting Wildlife and Natural Resources
Allowing bird sanctuaries in Anne Arundel County
I was the lead sponsor of bipartisan legislation allowing bird sanctuaries in Anne Arundel County, supporting wildlife protection and conservation efforts.
