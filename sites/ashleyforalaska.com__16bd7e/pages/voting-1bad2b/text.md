Our District, House District 35
House District 35 is located in the western part of the Fairbanks North Star Borough.
It includes the communities of Ester, Rosie Creek, Chena Pump, Geist, the University of Alaska Fairbanks main campus, and more.
It consists of eight voting precincts each with their own polling place where voters may cast their vote on election day.

Rank Choice Voting
In 2020, the voters of Alaska passed Ballot Measure 2.
This established the open primary and rank choice voting system.
In the open primary voters pick one candidate, and the top four candidates in a race proceed to the general election.
In the general election voters rank the candidates in order of preference, but it is important to note that voters are not required to rank multiple candidates.
This video produced by the Alaska Division of Elections can help explain rank choice voting and what it means for Alaska’s elections.
More information about rank choice voting can be found on the Division of Elections website.
