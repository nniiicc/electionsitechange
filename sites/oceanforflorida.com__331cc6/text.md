MEET OCEAN FITTS
& KNOW WHY HE IS IN THIS FIGHT
Before he ever ran for office, Ocean was already showing up.
As a policy thinker 🧠
Cutting state revenue with no plan to replace it means fewer firefighters, police officers, and librarians, and it pushes the bill onto families later at a higher price.
Ocean judges every policy by what it really costs Manatee County over time.
As an advocate 🗣️
When his best friend's body gave out after years of physical work, Ocean helped them apply for disability benefits, only to find out they didn't have enough work credits to qualify.
How does that make sense?
Ocean is running so Florida families stop hitting walls like that one.
Raised by a family of public servants 🏛️
Ocean grew up in a family shaped by service.
His grandmother worked in social work, his mother in a university library, and his father as an attorney.
His grandfather, Jere, served as the base doctor at the Navy’s AUTEC facility, while his great-grandfather, John, was a military pilot who flew in the Berlin Airlift before becoming a teacher at Northeast High School in St.
Petersburg, the same school Ocean and his mother would later attend.
From an early age, Ocean saw how service, education, and community institutions can make a difference in people’s lives.
