Calling All Volunteers 📣
Let’s Work Together
District 71 is going to be won by neighbors talking to neighbors.
When you sign up, you join a team of Manatee County residents who believe this community deserves a representative who shows up for it.
Here is what to expect when you sign up:
- ⚓ Personal Outreach
- A member of the campaign team will contact you to learn about your interests and availability.
- 🗳️ Direct Action
- Knock on doors across Bradenton, Palmetto, and Ellenton, make calls or send texts from home, register voters, assist at events, or talk with voters in your own social circle.
- 📚 Full Support
- You will receive full training and support from the campaign, making first-time volunteers as welcome as seasoned campaign veterans.
- 🤝 Grassroots Growth
- Campaigns grow one conversation at a time.
If you know someone who cares about District 71, invite them to join you.
- 💙 Flexible Roles
- Whether you have 15 minutes a month or want to lead neighborhood volunteers, there is a meaningful place for you on this team.
Ready to make some waves?
Sign up today and let's get to work.
