Pamela Salaam is a lifelong Wilmington resident, community leader, and Democratic candidate for State Representative in the 16th District.
Raised in Southbridge, she has spent decades serving her community through advocacy, service, and leadership.
This campaign is powered by community.
Your support helps fund outreach, advocacy, and on-the-ground work that keeps people informed, engaged, and heard across the 16th District.
Every contribution, big or small, helps us continue fighting for environmental justice, safer communities, and stronger support for children and families right here at home.
This campaign is rooted in service, not politics as usual.
Pamela has spent years working directly with her community, listening, showing up, and addressing real issues that impact families across the 16th District.
Protecting neighborhoods from toxic exposure, industrial pollution, flooding, and unsafe conditions while addressing food deserts and health risks.
Reducing gun violence, supporting trauma-informed care, and expanding suicide prevention efforts to keep families, children, and seniors safe.
Advocating for accessible mental health services, substance abuse recovery support, and compassionate care for individuals and families in need.
Fighting for education, food security, and community resources that support families by creating stability, protecting dignity, and expanding access to opportunity.
Ensuring residents have a voice in decisions that affect their neighborhoods through transparency, accountability, and active civic engagement.
Supporting efforts that address homelessness, rent stabilization, and basic needs so families can remain safely housed in their communities.
Pamela brings decades of real-world experience serving her community through health care, recovery support, nonprofit leadership, and neighborhood advocacy.
Her work is grounded in honesty, accountability, and a lifelong commitment to helping people where they are.
This campaign is supported by community leaders, neighbors, advocates, and families who believe in honest leadership, accountability, and putting people first in the 16th District.
