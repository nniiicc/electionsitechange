Bobby Charles Releases New Ad: “Augusta Scandals” -- Calls on Maine to Open the Books, Investigate Crimes, and Reject More of the Same
AUGUSTA, Maine -- Republican gubernatorial candidate Bobby Charles today released a new digital ad filmed on the grounds of the Maine State House, arguing that Democrat Hannah Pingree represents continuity with the Janet Mills era — not a change of direction.
Shot against yellow caution tape stretched across the State House lawn, the 48-second spot frames Augusta as a “crime scene” of career-insider politics.
On-screen text and Charles’s narration hit three themes:
Continuity with Mills.
The ad states that “Hannah Pingree ran Janet Mills policy” and that she is “the same Augusta with a new name on the door.”
Family and government money.
Graphics assert that “Pingree’s husband got government money.”
Accountability.
Charles, a former federal official, calls to “open the books, investigate the crimes, [and] hold Maine accountable.” The ad ends on “It’s time for a change,” with the campaign slogan “Cut crime, cut taxes” and a paid-for-by disclaimer from the Bobby Charles for Maine Governor Campaign Committee.
Charles appears on camera in a Navy cap and American-flag jacket patch, holding a roll of caution tape as a visual stand-in for unfinished business in Augusta.
“This race is not a rebrand.
It is a choice,” the campaign said in releasing the video.
“Maine families are paying the price for high crime, high taxes, and a capital that protects itself.
We will open the books and hold people accountable.”
The ad is paid for by the Bobby Charles for Maine Governor Campaign Committee.
