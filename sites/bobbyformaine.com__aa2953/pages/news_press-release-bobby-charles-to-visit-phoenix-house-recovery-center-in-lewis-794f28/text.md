PRESS RELEASE: Bobby Charles to Visit Phoenix House Recovery Center in Lewiston on July 31, at 11 a.m.
Bobby Charles to Visit Phoenix House Recovery Center in Lewiston, Meet with Local Leaders on Addiction and Recovery
Republican candidate for governor to tour treatment facility, hear from Lewiston community members during addiction and recovery tour week
WHAT: Bobby Charles, Republican candidate for governor, will visit Phoenix House, a Lewiston addiction treatment center, as part of his campaign's addiction and recovery tour.
Charles will tour press-accessible areas of the facility and be available for brief remarks and questions.
He will also meet separately with area residents and local officials to discuss the impact of the addiction crisis on the Lewiston community.
WHO: Bobby Charles, candidate for governor
WHEN: Tomorrow, 7/31, 11 a.m.
WHERE: Phoenix House, 87 Bartlett St, Lewiston (contact Dale upon arrival)
RSVP: Media wishing to attend must RSVP directly to Dale Crafts, campaign manager, at dale@bobbyformaine.com or 207-320-8534.
Access will be limited to confirmed press.
Out of respect for patients and staff, filming or photographing individuals in treatment is strictly prohibited without explicit consent, and press will be restricted to designated areas of the facility.
