PRESS RELEASE: Bobby Charles for Governor Campaign to Hold Public Town Hall Meetings Across Maine Starting Thursday in Brewer
Confirmed Town Halls to be Held in Brewer, Caribou and Lewiston, More to be Added
AUGUSTA, ME — Today the Bobby Charles for Governor campaign announced it will hold a series of open town hall meetings across Maine, with the first confirmed event this Thursday in Brewer.
These events are open to the public and to all media.
The current schedule is:
Bangor Town Hall
Thursday, Sept. 3 -- 5:30pm
Jeff’s Catering
Caribou Town Hall with Austin Theriault
Thursday, Sept. 10 – 5:30pm
VFW Post 9389
Lewiston Town Hall with Gov.
Chris Christie
Wednesday, Sept. 23 – 5:30pm
Clarion Hotel
Please note: A Southern Maine Town Hall meeting will be scheduled also, with the date and location to be provided soon.
