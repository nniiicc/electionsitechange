I believe he will: Lower the tax burden.
Make energy more affordable and reliable.
Reduce unnecessary government spending.
Make it easier to build housing.
Help small businesses stay in Maine. ‍ I think Bobby Charles will be a governor who knows how to manage. ‍ Every Mainer deserves to feel safe in their home, on their street and in their community.

I think he will address addiction, protect victims and hold repeat offenders accountable. ‍ Another key issue is energy independence for Maine.
