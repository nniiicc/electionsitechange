How to Vote Every vote in Kansas House District 3 counts.
Here is when and where you can vote in Crawford County, what to bring, and how to find your polling place.
Photo ID Required Kansas law requires a photo ID to vote in person.
A Pittsburg State University student ID can be used.
Other accepted IDs include a Kansas or other state driver's license or ID card, military ID, tribal ID, government employee ID, a concealed carry license, and a public assistance ID card.
If your ID has an expiration date, it must be current.
Voters age 65 or older may use an expired photo ID.

Voting by Mail If you are voting by mail, apply as soon as you can.
Applications are due October 27, and ballot mailing begins October 14.
Complete your ballot carefully, and sign and date the envelope .
Return it promptly to a drop box or an active polling place if possible.
USPS service may be delayed.
Finding Your Polling Place On Election Day you must vote at your assigned polling location.
Look yours up on Kansas Voter View, the Secretary of State's voter lookup tool.
Go to myvoteinfo.voteks.org/VoterView .
In the polling place section, enter your residential address.
Select View Locations to see your polling station for upcoming elections.
You can also confirm your registration on the same page.
Enter your first name, last name and date of birth to check your address, party, mail ballot status and polling place.
