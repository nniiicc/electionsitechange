Meet Me Monday
While walking in our community is nothing new for me (though my doctor may disagree), I will be posting the locations and times of some of those walks in commercial areas so that anyone that wants to meet me has the opportunity to find me.
These will usually be on Mondays, but I will try to throw in some other days when I can.
You are also welcome to come up to me anytime you see me out.
Keep in mind, these locations are very loosley defined and orbit around the defind location by several blocks.
Because I value your privacy, I do not do all the door knocking, texting, calling, junk mailing, etc. of other candidates.
Additionally, my publicized walks will be in areas that are primarily commercial in nature to avoid disturbing our residential neighborhoods.
Although I do not take any contributions to my campaign, I am sure some of our local retailers, restaurants and service providers near my walks would love the chance to earn your business.
If you have a business in the area that I am in and would like me to stop in, please let me know.
Also, if I appear to be missing an area, please let me know.
If this format appears popular, and you choose me to represent you in the Senate, I would like to keep it going throughout my term as well.
I can’t represent you well if you can’t find me.
See you soon, Ray
