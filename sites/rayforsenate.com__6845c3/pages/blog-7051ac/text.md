9/16/2026
“AI will strip us naked, sexually assault us and kill us all within 10 years.” At least that’s what some of our political leaders want you to believe in their newest fear mongering election tactic.
“We must pass laws now to take political control from these horrible engineers creating this monster.”
The truth is that AI is a powerful tool that allows individuals to get access to information and viewpoints that the politicians do not want them to have and take action on that knowledge in ways they may not previously have had the skills for.
As with all tools, the information can be accurate or inaccurate, it can be biased, it can be friendly or mean.
None of that has changed since the first person put pen to paper.
In fact, that’s what AI really is, print for the 21st century with some skill enhancement thrown in.
What’s new is AI’s ability to create new information pulled from various sources and combined in such a way that no person had yet thought of.
It also has the ability to act on that information either by direction of or in the absence of human intervention.
As AI, like many politicians, has no moral compass; this can be either a positive or negative outcome depending on the viewpoint, and one cannot be legislated away without removing the other.
I am far more concerned about the natural intelligence that is actively trying to harm us now.
Scammers trying to steal our wealth (and I don’t just mean politicians), hackers trying to disrupt our water systems, politicians trying to use laws against companies as a back door route to eliminating our natural rights and violent repeat offenders that keep being let back into society.
Let’s work on preventing harm by these real threats of today, and that will in itself address the potential threats of AI in the future.
