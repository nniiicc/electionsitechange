Priorities Rooted
in Everyday Life
From the condition of local schools to whether seniors can stay in their homes, the choices made in Albany show up quickly in people’s daily lives.
John’s priorities are shaped by what residents experience firsthand — and by what will make it easier to raise a family, grow older, and feel secure in the community.
- John’s priorities are guided by one goal: making the 80th District a place where families can build stable lives, seniors can age with dignity, and neighborhoods continue to grow stronger.
Looking ahead, he is focused on:
- Expanding after-school programs, supporting school facilities, and strengthening pathways from school to higher education and careers.
- Defending Mitchell-Lama, supporting homeowners and tenants, and investing in building safety and housing preservation.
- Investing in prevention, intervention, and community-based services that address root causes and improve quality of life.
- Expanding access to services, upgrading facilities, and strengthening programs that help seniors remain active and connected.
- Maintaining parks, improving streetscapes, and supporting neighborhood business districts that keep communities vibrant.
- Improving coordination with agencies and reducing delays that keep residents waiting for answers and services.
