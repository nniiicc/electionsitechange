80th district
From residential blocks to busy corridors, the 80th District is made up of communities with strong identities and deep roots.
John Zaccaro Jr. believes every neighborhood deserves real attention and real investment — not just promises, but projects and services that make a difference in daily life.
Across the district, he has worked to bring resources home and turn funding into visible improvements.
allerton
Investing in sanitation, pedestrian safety, and community facilities along active residential and commercial corridors.
little yemen
Supporting local small businesses, community organizations, and public space improvements in a vibrant cultural corridor that is an important part of the district’s identity.
morris park
Supporting parks and playgrounds, senior accessibility, and neighborhood business corridors, while addressing safety and infrastructure near busy intersections.
norwood
Protecting affordable housing, improving street safety, and supporting small businesses while investing in cleaner streets and stronger community institutions.
pelham gardens
Focused on housing preservation, building safety, and quality-of-life improvements for longtime residents and families.
pelham parkway
Strengthening schools, youth programs, park conditions, and pedestrian safety for a family-centered community.
van nest
Improving street conditions, traffic safety, and public spaces while supporting local institutions that serve the heart of the neighborhood.
