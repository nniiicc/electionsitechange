As Assemblyman for the 80th District, John Zaccaro Jr. works to keep neighborhoods strong, make government easier to navigate, and ensure resources reach the people who need them.
In just three years, he has helped deliver millions in funding across the district — and he is focused on keeping that progress going.
this is home. and the work matters.
Every neighborhood in the 80th District has its own character, but people are facing many of the same pressures — rising costs, busy lives, and systems that do not always move fast enough when help is needed.
John understands that frustration because it is part of his own story.
He was raised in the Bronx by a single mother and experienced housing insecurity growing up.
He knows what it feels like to wait for answers and to need support that does not always come easily.
That is why he approaches this job the way he does: stay accessible, solve problems directly, and make government easier to navigate, not harder.
