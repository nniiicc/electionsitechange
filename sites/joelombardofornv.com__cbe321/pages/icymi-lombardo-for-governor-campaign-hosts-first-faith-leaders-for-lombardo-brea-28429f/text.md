Faith Leaders for Lombardo events were created to bring together faith leaders and their congregations for fellowship and community in support of Sheriff Joe Lombardo.
Pastor Matt Teis of Liberty Baptist Church spoke at the breakfast, sharing how he first met Lombardo through the success of the community policing initiative in his community.
“I first met Joe Lombardo through his work and efforts towards community policing.
As undersheriff, Joe championed the community policing initiative at Metro, which reduced crime in our neighborhood by 75%,” said Pastor Teis .
“As I got to know Joe more, his heart for service, integrity, and humility were made clear to me.
It’s an honor to support Joe for governor, and I am confident that he will be a force for good in our community.” “During my tenure at Metro, I’ve worked to partner with faith leaders to make our communities safer and stronger.
As I run for governor, I know how critical it is to listen to our faith leaders and learn from them: they are the backbone of our communities and their input, support, and guidance is invaluable,” said Sheriff Lombardo .
