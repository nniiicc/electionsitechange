ICYMI: Lombardo Joins “Fox & Friends”
To Discuss His Campaign and Record-Breaking Fundraising
January 14, 2022
LAS VEGAS, NV – Today, Republican gubernatorial candidate and Clark County Sheriff Joe Lombardo appeared on “Fox & Friends” to discuss his record-setting $3.1 million fundraising haul.
In the interview with host Ainsley Earhardt, Lombardo explained why he was inspired to run for governor, saying, “We’re under single-party rule.
We’re lacking in economic diversity.
Our education system is constantly rated at the bottom…support for law and order is waning in the state.
And I believe I have the vision and the leadership skills to change all of that.” In addition to discussing his candidacy and the outpouring of financial support for his campaign, Lombardo also discussed his background in crisis management, 33 years in law enforcement, and his family.
