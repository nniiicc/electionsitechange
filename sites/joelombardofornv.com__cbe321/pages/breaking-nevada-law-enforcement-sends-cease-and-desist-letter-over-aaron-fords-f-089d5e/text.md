BREAKING: Nevada Law Enforcement Sends Cease-and-Desist Letter Over Aaron Ford's False Endorsement Ad
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
August 8, 2026
Contact: press@joelombardofornv.com
LAS VEGAS, NV – Today, it was revealed that Aaron Ford's latest campaign ad reuses footage from his previous campaigns for attorney general and features individuals wearing the logo and badge of the Nevada Association of Public Safety Officers (NAPSO) and the Las Vegas Police Protective Association (LVPPA) – neither of which authorized the use of their names, logos, or likenesses in the 2026 gubernatorial race.
The advertisement also recycles language that referred to claims made during the 2018 attorney general campaign, creating the false impression that those statements and endorsements apply to Ford's campaign for governor today.
As a result, multiple law enforcement organizations have issued cease-and-desist letters demanding the advertisement be removed.
"The Las Vegas Police Protective Association did not authorize any individual to wear or display the LVPPA logo or badge in Aaron Ford's campaign advertisement, nor did we authorize our organization's name or likeness to be used to imply an endorsement in the 2026 race for Governor,” said John Abel, Director of Governmental Affairs at the LVPPA.
“We have issued a cease-and-desist letter to the Ford campaign demanding that this misleading advertisement be removed.
Furthermore, our position is clear: the Las Vegas Police Protective Association proudly supports Governor Joe Lombardo for reelection."
"It is despicable that Aaron Ford would mislead Nevada voters by falsely portraying support from Nevada's law enforcement community that he simply does not have.
Rather than earning these endorsements, he chose to recycle old footage from a different campaign and create a false impression in an attempt to deceive voters,” said Halee Dobbins, Lombardo for Governor Spokesperson."Governor Joe Lombardo is proud to have earned the endorsement of major law enforcement organizations in Nevada through decades of standing with the men and women who wear the badge and wearing the badge himself – not by misleading the public."
Governor Lombardo has been endorsed by the Carson City Sheriff’s Supervisory Association, Las Vegas Metro Police Managers & Supervisors Association, Las Vegas Police Protective Association (LVPPA), Peace Officer Research Association of Nevada (PORAN), Police Officers Association of CCSD (POACCSD), Public Safety Alliance of Nevada (PSAN), Washoe County Sheriff Deputies Association (WCSDA), Reno Police Protective Association, Sparks Police Protective Association, Douglas County Sheriff Protective Association, LVPOA, Washoe County School Police Officer’s Association, and the Nevada Police Union (NPU).
In addition to his endorsements from major law enforcement organizations, Governor Lombardo has been endorsed by Carson City Sheriff Kenneth Furlong, Churchill County Sheriff Lee Orozco, Clark County Sheriff Kevin McMahill, Douglas County Sheriff Daniel Coverley, Elko County Sheriff Aitor Narvaiza, Eureka County Sheriff Miles Umina, Humboldt County Sheriff Angel Cardenas, Lander County Sheriff Ron Unger, Lincoln County Sheriff Derek Foremaster, Lyon County Sheriff Brad Pope, Pershing County Sheriff Jerry Allen, Storey County Sheriff Mike Cullen, Washoe County Sheriff Darin Balaam, and White Pine County Sheriff Scott Henroid.
