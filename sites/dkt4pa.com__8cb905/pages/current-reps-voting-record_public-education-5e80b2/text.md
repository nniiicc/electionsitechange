Background A court ruled the school funding system unconstitutional, and required the state to fix the deficit.
Mako has voted against the fix.
In February 2023 , the Commonwealth Court of Pennsylvania ruled school funding unconstitutional.
Local property taxes drive the gap: wealthier districts raise more, poorer districts cannot, and students are left with fewer resources, from educational programs to athletics, music, art and after-school programs.
The fix was left to the House of Representatives.
Three funding votes have reached the floor since that ruling.
Mako voted no on each one.
Source: repmako.com Zach Mako has represented HD-183 for nearly ten years .
It is his job to keep the public schools of HD-183 from being left behind.
Yet he voted against House Bill 2370 and the budgets that carried the new classroom funding.
Instead, he continuously supports charter schools, which: channel public money away from public schools — unlike private schools, which are privately funded are permitted to discriminate against students are not required to prepare students adequately for future success Mako also voted that charter schools do not need to educate students for 180 days like public schools (under HB-1422 ).
