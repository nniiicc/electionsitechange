Comparison of Zach Mako and Deirdre Kamber Todd on minimum wage, AI data centers, public education, and grants for fire departments Issue R Zach Mako , Republican D Deirdre Kamber Todd , Democrat Minimum Wage Opposes mandated state increases.
Voted against the state House bills aimed at progressively raising Pennsylvania’s minimum wage floor.
Full Voting Record on Minimum Wage Supports raising the minimum wage.
Strongly advocates for boosting the state wage floor to protect workers’ rights and support families facing rising inflation.
AI Data Centers Voted “No” on multiple regulatory bills introduced in the Pennsylvania State House designed to manage, restrict, or overlay environmental and grid mandates on new data center infrastructure.
Also voted to allow Secrecy/NDA Agreements for data center companies.
Full Voting Record on AI Data Centers Advocates for strict zoning and transparency.
Pledges to limit AI data center development strictly to designated industrial and warehouse areas.
Demands rigorous, ongoing community impact standards.
Public Education Voted “No” to supporting public schools in favor of charter schools, which are funding by diverting public school monies and additional property taxes.
Full Voting Record on Public Education Advocates for increased funding reinvestment.
Prioritizes robust state reinvestment into public school systems to ensure equitable resources, student safety, and comprehensive academic support.
Grants for Fire Departments Voted “No” on House Bill 2446, the $30 million Fire Company Transformational Grant Program for equipment, stations, staffing, and recruitment.
The House passed the bill 105–96.
Full Voting Record on Grants for Fire Departments Advocates for the promotion of grant opportunities for first responders, boroughs and township, community growth, and home safety improvements.
Plans to create public grant teams to assist with grant identification, writing, and maintenance.
A staunch supporter of first responders for decades through legal assistance and fundraising, she seeks to strengthen our volunteer fire departments through grant writing opportunities.
