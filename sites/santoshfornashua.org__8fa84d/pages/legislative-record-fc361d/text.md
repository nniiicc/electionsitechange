General Election: Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Legislative Record Home Legislative Record Rep.
Santosh Salvi has represented Nashua Ward 5 (Hillsborough District 9) in the New Hampshire House since November, 2024.
He serves on the House Committee on Commerce and Consumer Affairs Bills Sponsored / Co‑Sponsored by Representative Santosh Salvi: More Details Committee on Commerce and Consumer Affairs: The New Hampshire House Committee on Commerce and Consumer Affairs oversees banking and insurance regulations and creates economic development programs, while protecting consumers from unfair business practices, fraud, or unsafe products.
The Committee also specifically manages the licensing of businesses to sell liquor.
Since joining the Committee on Commerce and Consumer Affairs in 2025, Representative Santosh Salvi has consistently supported measures that foster economic growth while prioritizing consumer trust for all New Hampshire residents.
Most recently, he co-sponsored legislation to regulate the use of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare insurance decisions, ensuring greater transparency and protecting patients from automated claim denials.
