AS YOUR STATE REPRESENTATIVE, CARL FIGHTS TO
- Secure the Border The Biden Administration has created a border crisis.
If the federal government won't do its job, Texas must step up to secure the border and cut off the magnets that encourage illegal immigration.
- Preserve the Second Amendment As a proud gun owner, I believe strongly in our right to keep and bare arms.
In Austin, I will fight to ensure our gun rights are never infringed.
- Protect Unborn Life I am 100% pro-life and will support all efforts to protect the unborn and stop our taxpayer dollars from funding Planned Parenthood and other abortion providers.
- Back the Blue I will fight to stop leftist efforts to defund the police and ensure our law enforcement have adequate resources to keep our communities and schools safe.
- Election Integrity In Texas, it must be easy to vote, but hard to cheat.
We must continue working to secure our elections to ensure the principle of one person, one vote.
- Tax and Spending We do not have a revenue problem, we have a spending problem.
I will fight to lower our property taxes and limit spending to population growth plus inflation.
