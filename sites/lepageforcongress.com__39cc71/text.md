Meet Paul Paul LePage knows what it is like to struggle.
Born into a broken home, Paul’s father handed him a 50-cent coin and told him to tell the doctors he fell down the stairs as he laid battered in a Lewiston hospital.
11-year-old Paul LePage took that coin, and left the hospital.
He never returned home.
Homeless, finding work and a place to sleep day-to-day, it was a horse stable worker who told him, “If it is to be, it is up to me.” Paul took that phrase to heart, finished high school, earned a college and graduate degree, and went on to become one of New England’s most successful businesspeople, creating jobs and growing the economy.
He was elected, and re-elected Mayor as a Republican in one of Maine’s most Democratic cities and then served as Maine’s Governor, creating jobs, growing the economy, protecting Maine’s senior citizens, and always looking out for taxpayers.
Now, he’s running for Congress to bring no-nonsense leadership to Washington.
Because Mainers deserve a straight talker who always puts them over politics.
