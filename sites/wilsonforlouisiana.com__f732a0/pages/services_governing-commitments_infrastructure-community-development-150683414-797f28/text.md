Home Agenda About Contact Home Agenda About Contact Infrastructure & Community Development Get Involved Infrastructure and community development are vital to creating a thriving society in which my community can flourish.
Recognizing robust infrastructure as a key driver of economic growth and high-quality living for all residents, I focus on advocating for much-needed improvements in water and sewer systems, roads, bridges, drainage, housing, broadband, and more.
Addressing these crucial areas, I aim to eliminate the longstanding infrastructure deficits that have hindered growth in Louisiana's 5th Congressional District.
With a strong personal commitment, stemming from my practical experience in construction and project management, I understand that infrastructure is not just a theoretical idea—it's the tangible projects that influence daily lives.
By diligently securing funding and ensuring its responsible allocation, I provide both accountability and transparency in every community development initiative I support.
My approach fosters trust, encourages lasting partnerships, and emphasizes public collaboration.
For you, this translates into safer roads, cleaner drinking water, more secure housing solutions, and reliable broadband access that connects you to a world of opportunities.
Indeed, you deserve a community where public facilities meet diverse needs, where growth is sustainable, and where each development project creates measurable, positive change for you and your family.
I listen closely to the needs of the Lincoln community, as your input steers my efforts.
By partnering with me, you invest in our shared vision and play a significant role in shaping the future.
Together, we diligently plan, efficiently execute, and eagerly celebrate the progress we achieve.
Join me in this journey towards realizing a stronger, more interconnected future that benefits all of us.
