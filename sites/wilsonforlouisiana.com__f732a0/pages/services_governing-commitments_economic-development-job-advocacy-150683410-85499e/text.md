Home Agenda About Contact Home Agenda About Contact Economic Development & Job Advocacy Get Involved Economic Development & Job Advocacy is my plan to stop watching jobs leave Louisiana's 5th Congressional District and start building the kind of economy that keeps families rooted in place.
As a general contractor and business owner, I know firsthand what employers look for when they decide where to invest: stable infrastructure, a trained workforce, predictable rules, and local leadership that understands budgets and timelines.
This effort has several practical focuses.
First, I will actively recruit new employers in manufacturing, logistics, agriculture-related processing, healthcare, and technology.
That means sitting across the table from site selectors and business leaders, speaking their language, and laying out why this district is ready for investment.
Second, I will fight for small business support, because many of the best jobs are created by neighbors who open shops, farms, and local companies.
I will work to expand access to capital, simplify federal programs, and connect entrepreneurs with mentoring and workforce training partnerships through community colleges, technical schools, and apprenticeship programs.
Third, I will link economic development directly to workforce training.
Good-paying jobs require skills, and too often those skills are taught somewhere else.
I plan to push for federal funding that ties training dollars to real hiring commitments from local employers, so that a student who starts a welding, nursing, CDL, or tech program sees a clear path to a job right here at home.
Finally, I will insist on measuring results.
Economic Development & Job Advocacy will be tracked through clear metrics: employers recruited, jobs created, average wage increases, and training slots opened.
Residents will be able to see not just promises, but progress they can count in paychecks, business openings, and fewer young people needing to move away to find opportunity.
