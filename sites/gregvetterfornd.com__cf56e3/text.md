Meet Greg
Greg’s career has been built on responsibility, accountability, and long-term success.
Whether in banking, higher education, or board leadership, he has earned trust by listening, working with others, and delivering outcomes.
Throughout his career, he has served in several leadership positions, including:
- Former Bank Examiner for the State of North Dakota
- Senior leadership roles at Bravera Bank and American State Bank & Trust
- Board Member, Bravera Bank
- Former Board Member for the North Dakota Bankers Association and Independent Community Banks of North Dakota
- Former Executive VP and Chief of Staff at the University of Mary
He’s spent decades working in the private sector—helping organizations grow, creating opportunities, and ensuring financial stability.
That experience is exactly what North Dakota needs in the Legislature.
Greg has long been a dedicated community leader, generously giving his time, skills, and expertise to organizations that serve North Dakota families.
In 2017, he served as Chair of the Board for the Missouri Slope Areawide United Way and has been deeply involved with the Women’s Care Center in Bismarck.
One of Greg’s greatest passions is advocating for individuals with disabilities.
He served for many years as Chair of the Great American Bike Race, helping grow the organization’s annual support from $200,000 to $400,000.
He is also a strong supporter of CPABLE, which works to improve the lives of children with cerebral palsy and other childhood-onset neurological conditions.
Greg and his wife are deeply involved in their parish and the Diocese.
As proud parents and grandparents, they are committed to keeping North Dakota a strong, welcoming place for families—today and for generations to come.
I am running for State Representative to use my experience and work for commonsense, limited government, policies that have made our state a leader in economic prosperity and freedom.
Throughout my leadership roles in business and in our community, I have demonstrated the ability to work as part of collaborative and respectful teams serving those who entrusted me with that responsibility.
I will take that same results focused mindset to the Legislature.”
-Greg Vetter, Candidate for North Dakota House
