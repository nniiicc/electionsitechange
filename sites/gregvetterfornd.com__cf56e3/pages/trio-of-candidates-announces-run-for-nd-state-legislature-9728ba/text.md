Meet Greg Priorities News Get Involved Vote Contact Donate News & Updates Trio of candidates announces run for ND state legislature Feb 5, 2026 | In the News BISMARCK, ND ( KXNET ) — Today, a few candidates told a group of people in Bismarck why they should be the Republican nominee in the upcoming primary in June.
Governor Kelly Armstrong appeared at a Bismarck cafe earlier today to campaign for incumbent State Senator Michelle Axtman, alongside State House candidates Greg Vetter and Steve Sauter.
They are running as a slate in district seven, representing Bismarck.
“This isn’t the beginning or the end of anything, this is the beginning of a process,” Governor Armstrong said.
“This goes through the convention and primary and general election and the process works its way all the way through.
I don’t know if anybody saw what happened in the special election in Texas last week.
Anybody who takes anything for granted in politics right now is making a dangerous, dangerous mistake.” Following the governor’s speech, Senator Axtman and the other candidates all spoke briefly.
The trio is opposed by a slate of candidates led by former North Dakota State Representative Rick Becker.
Sauter and Vetter are first time political candidates while Axtman was elected to the State Senate in 2022.
