Canino for Torrington 2026 operates LetsGoCanino.com.
This policy explains how the campaign handles information submitted through this website.
Effective September 22, 2026.
Our contact, volunteer, and yard sign forms collect the details you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, address, volunteer interests, and message.
We use these details to answer your request, coordinate campaign activities, and deliver requested yard signs.
Website submissions are stored in the campaign’s website system.
Providing a phone number alone does not enroll you in marketing texts.
Text enrollment requires your affirmative selection of the separate SMS consent checkbox.
We do not sell, rent, or share mobile numbers or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional use.
SMS opt-in records are excluded from any broader permission to share campaign information.
Service providers may process information solely to operate the campaign’s messaging service, subject to confidentiality and use restrictions.
Information may also be disclosed when legally required.
You may submit a contact, volunteer, or yard sign request without agreeing to marketing texts.
To stop campaign texts, reply STOP.
For messaging assistance, reply HELP.
Contact the campaign at 860-294-6918 with questions about your information, to correct submitted details, or to request removal from campaign contact lists, subject to applicable recordkeeping requirements.
External websites linked from this site have their own privacy practices.
Changes to this policy will be posted here with an updated effective date.
