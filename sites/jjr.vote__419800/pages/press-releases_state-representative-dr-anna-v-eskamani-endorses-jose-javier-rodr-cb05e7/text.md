Orlando, FL - Today, Florida State Representative Dr.
Anna V.
Eskamani announced her endorsement of José Javier Rodríguez in his campaign for Florida Attorney General, citing his commitment to lowering costs for families, holding powerful corporations accountable, and defending Floridians’ constitutional rights.
“José Javier Rodríguez is exactly the kind of Attorney General Florida needs right now, someone who will put people first.
Floridians are facing an affordability crisis, and they need an Attorney General focused on protecting consumers, holding corporations accountable, and defending our constitutional rights, not taking them away,” said Rep.
Eskamani.
"When it comes to corporate accountability, José Javier is the person who will stand up to powerful interests and put Floridians first.
I’m proud to endorse him for Attorney General.”
A leading voice in the Florida Legislature, Rep.
Anna V.
Eskamani has been a fearless advocate for working families, reproductive freedom, and government accountability.
Throughout her time in office, she has championed policies that lower costs, expand opportunity, and protect the rights of everyday Floridians.
“I’m honored to have Representative Eskamani’s support,” said Rodríguez.
“We served together in the Legislature and fought side by side for working families and everyday Floridians.
She has always been a fearless advocate for accountability and fairness, and I’m proud to have her support in this campaign.”
Rep.
Eskamani’s endorsement adds to the growing momentum behind Rodríguez’s campaign as he continues building a broad coalition of leaders committed to bringing accountability and new leadership to Florida’s Attorney General office.
