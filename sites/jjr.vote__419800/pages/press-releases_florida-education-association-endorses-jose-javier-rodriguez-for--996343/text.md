EN ES Home About Priorities Media Endorsements Get involved Donate FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE July 23, 2026 Contact: press@jjr.vote , 786-683-8781 EN ES Florida Education Association Endorses José Javier Rodríguez for Florida Attorney General Tallahassee, FL - Today, the Florida Education Association — representing more than 120,000 teachers, education staff professionals, higher education faculty, and other public education workers across Florida — endorsed José Javier Rodríguez for Florida Attorney General, citing his record of standing up for public education.
"José Javier Rodríguez has been a strong advocate for Florida's educators, support professionals, students, and neighborhood public schools," said Andrew Spar, President of the Florida Education Association.
"He understands the importance of protecting public education and standing up for the teachers and staff who serve Florida's students every day.
We are proud to endorse him for Attorney General." Rodríguez, a workers' rights attorney, former state senator, and former U.S.
Assistant Secretary of Labor, is running to restore the Attorney General's office as an advocate for Floridians focused on tackling crime, costs, and corruption.
“I am not only an advocate for our public schools, I am a product of them, and I know the work Florida's educators and school staff do every day to shape the future of our state," said José Javier Rodríguez.
"I am honored to once again have the support of Florida's educators.
Thank you to the Florida Education Association for standing with our campaign.
Florida's public schools, educators, staff, and students deserve leaders who stand up for public education.
I have always done that, and I will continue to do so as Attorney General." The Florida Education Association endorsement adds to growing support for Rodríguez’s campaign from labor organizations, elected officials, community leaders, and advocates across Florida.
