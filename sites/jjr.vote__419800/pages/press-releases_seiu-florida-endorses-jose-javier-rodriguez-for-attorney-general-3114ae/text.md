Fort Lauderdale, FL – Today, the Service Employees International Union (SEIU) Florida State Council officially endorsed José Javier Rodríguez for Attorney General of Florida during a press conference with workers and union members in South Florida.
SEIU Florida leaders and workers highlighted Rodríguez’s longstanding commitment to protecting workers, defending consumers, and holding powerful interests accountable, as well as his years of experience fighting for working families both as a workers’ attorney and in the Florida Legislature.
“Florida’s workers are tired - tired of a state government led by Ron DeSantis and James Uthmeier that has consistently failed working families and made life harder for workers across our state.
That’s why SEIU Florida is proud to endorse José Javier Rodríguez for Attorney General,” said Helene O’Brien, Vice President for SEIU 32BJ and Florida District Director.
“JJR has spent years fighting for workers, standing up for consumers, and believing in the rule of law.
He understands that government should work to improve people’s lives, not make them harder.
Florida workers deserve an honest Attorney General who will stand with working families, not powerful interests.
That’s why we’re proud to stand with José Javier Rodríguez.”
“During years as a workers’ attorney, I fought for people who were simply seeking fair wages, dignity, and respect in the workplace.
And during my time in the Florida Legislature, I worked to stand up for working families, protect consumers, and take on powerful interests that too often put profits ahead of people.
I’ve seen firsthand how hard Florida families work just to stay afloat while costs keep rising,” said José Javier Rodríguez.
“Floridians deserve an Attorney General who will stand up for workers and consumers, hold corporations accountable, and make sure the law works for everyday people, not just the wealthy and well-connected.
I’m honored to once again receive the endorsement of SEIU and stand alongside workers who are fighting for a fairer Florida.”
The SEIU Florida endorsement adds to growing support for Rodríguez’s campaign and underscores his focus on lowering costs, fighting corruption, and protecting Florida families.
