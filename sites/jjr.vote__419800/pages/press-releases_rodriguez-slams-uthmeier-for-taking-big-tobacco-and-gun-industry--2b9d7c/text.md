Miami, FL - Following new reporting detailing major contributions from Big Tobacco and a gun manufacturer to Attorney General James Uthmeier’s campaign, Democratic candidate for Attorney General José Javier Rodríguez criticized Uthmeier for prioritizing powerful special interests over Florida families.
Recent campaign finance filings show Uthmeier’s political committee received large contributions from corporate interests, including a tobacco company and a gun manufacturer that has pushed to limit accountability in the courts.
“While Florida families are struggling to keep up with rising costs, James Uthmeier is bankrolling his campaign with checks from Big Tobacco and a gun manufacturer looking to avoid accountability.
That tells you everything you need to know about who he’s working for, and why Floridians aren’t seeing relief,” said José Javier Rodríguez.
“Floridians deserve an Attorney General who stands up to corporate abuse, not one funded by it.
As Attorney General, I’ll be the people’s lawyer - focused on lowering costs, protecting consumers, and holding powerful interests accountable.”
Rodríguez, a workers’ rights attorney, former state senator, and former Assistant Secretary at the U.S.
Department of Labor, is running on a platform focused on lowering costs, cracking down on corruption, and ensuring the Attorney General’s office works for the people, not special interests.
