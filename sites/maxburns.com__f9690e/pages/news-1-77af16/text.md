This video by Senator Burns discusses: Rules changes Richmond Delegation Inauguration of State Officials Upcoming Legislation Legislative Day 1 - Swearing in for the 2023 - 2024 Session State Senator Max Burns was sworn-in for a 2nd term to the Georgia General Assembly on 1/9/2023.
Georgia State Senate District 23 is made up of Burke, Columbia (portions), Emanuel, Glascock, Jefferson, Jenkins, McDuffie, Richmond (portions), Screven, Taliferro, and Warren counties.
“I am committed to serving the citizens of the 23rd State Senate District and that commitment continues with the new 2023-2024 General Assembly Session.
We must support and protect Georgia’s economy, education, religious freedoms, and election integrity.
I look forward to continuing to serve our communities in the 23rd District and provide leadership for Georgia in the State Senate.” Picture contains Senator Burns & Family (wife: Lora) and Justice Bethel of the Supreme Court of Georgia.
