Steve Cohen MAKING SAN DIEGO AFFORDABLE Housing costs have pushed working families to the breaking point.
Groceries take a bigger bite out of every paycheck.
Steve Cohen will fight for real solutions: expanding housing supply, cracking down on corporate price manipulation, and ensuring that economic policy works for the people who actually live here.
HEALTHCARE THAT WORKS FOR PATIENTS Too many San Diegans skip prescriptions, delay checkups, or avoid the emergency room because they can't afford the bill.
Steve Cohen will work to lower prescription drug costs, protect coverage for pre-existing conditions, and hold insurance companies accountable for putting profits over patients.
PROTECTING OUR SENIORS San Diego's seniors built this community.
They deserve to retire with dignity — not spend their final years choosing between medication and rent.
Steve Cohen will defend Social Security and Medicare from cuts and ensure that the generation that invested in San Diego isn't abandoned by Washington.
FISCAL ACCOUNTABILITY Steve Cohen spent his career investigating where the money goes — and who benefits.
In Congress, he'll bring that same scrutiny to the federal budget: demanding transparency in government spending, opposing wasteful earmarks, and ensuring your tax dollars reach San Diego families.
