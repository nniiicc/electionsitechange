Cohen for Congress (“we”, “us”, “our”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Information We Collect
We collect information you provide directly to us, including:
- Name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and other contact information
- Payment information when you make a contribution (processed by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers)
- Demographic and political information you voluntarily share
- Communications between you and our campaign
How We Use Information
- Communicate with you about the campaign, events, and opportunities to get involved
- Process contributions and comply with FEC and state campaign-finance reporting requirements
- Improve our website and outreach
- Comply with applicable law
Text Messaging (SMS)
If you opt in to receive text messages from Cohen for Congress, message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency may vary.
Messaging may include requests for donation.
You may reply “STOP” at any time to opt out, or “HELP” for help.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
Sharing of Information
We may share information with service providers acting on our behalf (such as email service providers, payment processors, and text-messaging vendors) under contracts that limit their use of the information.
We may also disclose information when required by law or by FEC / state reporting obligations.
We do not sell your personal information.
Your Choices
- Email: Unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.
- Text messages: Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
- Access and correction: Request access to or correction of your information by contacting us through our website.
Children’s Privacy
This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The Effective Date above reflects the most recent revision.
