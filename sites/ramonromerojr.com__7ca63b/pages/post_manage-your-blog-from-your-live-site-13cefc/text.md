Representative Romero Files For Re-Election For House District 90
Admin
Nov 13, 2021
"Today, I filed for reelection as State Representative of House District 90.
So much has changed in the past four sessions I have served as Representative of HD 90, especially in the past year--the House composition, an unprecedented pandemic, a brutal winter storm, and of course the redrawing of our District map.
Through it all, my resolve to represent the best interests of this district, the home I have known my whole life, has remained steadfast.
That's why I chose today, my 48th birthday, as the day to file for reelection.
I hope you, my neighbors, will place your confidence in me once again and allow me to spend another year as your advocate in Austin."
- State Representative Ramon Romero
