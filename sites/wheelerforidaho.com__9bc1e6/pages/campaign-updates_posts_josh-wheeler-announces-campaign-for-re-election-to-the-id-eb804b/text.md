Wheeler, who has served in the Idaho Legislature since 2022, announced his candidacy with a renewed commitment to the values and priorities of his district.
"I am honored to have had the opportunity to represent the people of District 35 in the Idaho House of Representatives," Wheeler said.
"Over the past legislative sessions, I have worked hard to be a thoughtful, principled voice for our community, and I am excited to continue that work." Wheeler highlighted several key areas of focus for his re-election campaign, including fiscal responsibility, supporting Idaho families, and protecting individual liberties.
"Idaho is at a crossroads," Wheeler continued.
"We need experienced, dedicated leaders who will stand firm on conservative principles while also being willing to work across the aisle to get things done for our state.
I believe my record demonstrates that commitment." During his time in office, Wheeler has been an advocate for limited government, lower taxes, and policies that support economic growth in Idaho.
He has also championed efforts to improve transparency in government and increase civic engagement among Idahoans.
"The people of District 35 deserve a representative who listens, who shows up, and who fights for their interests," Wheeler said.
"That is what I have done, and that is what I will continue to do if re-elected." Wheeler encouraged residents of District 35 to get involved in the upcoming election and to reach out with any questions or concerns.
"This campaign is about the people of District 35," Wheeler added.
"I want to hear from you — your ideas, your concerns, and your vision for Idaho's future.
Together, we can continue to move our state forward." For more information about Josh Wheeler's campaign, please contact [email protected] .
