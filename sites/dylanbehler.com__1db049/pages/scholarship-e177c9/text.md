Delegate Scholarship
I was able to afford to go to college as a first-generation college student, in part, because I received a Delegate Scholarship - so it is such an honor to be able to award a Delegate Scholarship to college students and soon-to-be college students!
Scholarship program is open to all District 30A residents who attend or plan to attend a college or graduate institution.
To be eligible, you must currently attend or plan to attend a Maryland college, university, or private career school.
(Private career schools must have the Maryland Higher Education Commission’s approval to operate and be accredited by a national accrediting association approved by the US.
Department of Education.
You may also attend a nursing school if the Commission has approved the curriculum.)
You must complete your Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) or a MSFAA (for students ineligible to complete the FAFSA - this is also called the MHEC One-App) BEFORE completing this application.
Please upload the Student Aid Report in the education information section of this application.
If you have not yet filed your FAFSA application, visit http://studentaid.gov to apply.
Only the student applying for the scholarship should complete this form.
To be considered, applications must be fully completed on or before May 1.
If you have any questions, please call our office at 410-841-3045 or email dylan.behler@house.maryland.gov.
