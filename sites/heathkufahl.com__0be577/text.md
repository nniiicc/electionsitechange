With a heart for faith, family and freedom, Heath will bring his proven leadership skills to the Capitol.
In the State House, he'll work to advance the values that have Made America Great, values like limited government, personal responsibility, and biblical truth.
Teacher and Entrepreneur
An Oklahoma Baptist University graduate, Heath has over 25 years of experience teaching and coaching in OKC area high schools.
Heath now serves as the National Sales Director and part owner of Echo, a technology company focused on real time video and replays.
Echo's sports clients have included OU, Duke, Harvard, Clemson and more than 150 other Division I schools.
Military clients include the U.S.
Navy Seals/Special Naval Warfare Operations and the U.S.
Naval Academy.
Heath is one of the state's most successful high school volleyball coaches with over 500 career wins and four state titles.
Heath has lived in Moore/south OKC for many years.
He's served on the Board of Community Directors for the Oklahoma City Dodgers/Comets baseball team and partnered with organizations like MetroTech, OKC Parks & Recreation, the OKC Zoo, and local chambers of commerce to train teams in leadership and communication.
Heath and his wife, Lacey, live in Moore.
Between them they have 8 kids and one grandchild.
Lacey works part-time for WatersEdge, providing financial services to churches.
Heath attends Southern Hills Baptist Church, where he's taught Sunday school for nearly 15 years to young married adults.
Heath will support the right to life and stand up for the Christian conservative values this country was founded upon, protecting the most vulnerable among us.
Heath strongly supports our constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
Heath will hold the line against tax and regulation increases that burden hardworking Oklahomans.
Heath will fight illegal immigration and work to secure our borders.
Heath backs strong law enforcement to keep our community safe.
Heath believes in limited government, personal responsibility, and biblical truth—the values that have Made America Great.
