Get Involved Learn More About Heath Faithful Conservative, Proven Leader Teacher and Entrepreneur An Oklahoma Baptist University graduate, Heath has over 25 years of experience teaching and coaching in OKC area high schools.
Heath now serves as the National Sales Director and part owner of Echo, a technology company focused on real time video and replays.
Echo's sports clients have included OU, Duke, Harvard, Clemson and more than 150 other Division I schools.
Military clients include the U.S.
Navy Seals/Special Naval Warfare Operations and the U.S.
Naval Academy.
Championship Coach Heath is one of the state's most successful high school volleyball coaches with over 500 career wins and four state titles.
Community Involvement Heath has lived in Moore/south OKC for many years.
He's served on the Board of Community Directors for the Oklahoma City Dodgers/Comets baseball team and partnered with organizations like MetroTech, OKC Parks & Recreation, the OKC Zoo, and local chambers of commerce to train teams in leadership and communication.
Husband, Father and Grandfather Heath and his wife, Lacey, live in Moore.
Between them they have 8 kids and one grandchild.
Lacey works part-time for WatersEdge, providing financial services to churches.
Man of Faith Heath attends Southern Hills Baptist Church, where he's taught Sunday school for nearly 15 years to young married adults.
