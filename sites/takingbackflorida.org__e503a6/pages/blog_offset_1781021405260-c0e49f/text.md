Jason Bellamy-Fults Announces Candidacy for Senate District 6.
Local civic leader and working class advocate Jason Bellamy-Fults is officially announcing his candidacy for Florida’s 6th Senate District.
Bellamy-Fults’ candidacy launches amidst affordability crises in Florida.
He promises to push for higher wages and lower cost of living for working Floridians and to fight Tallahassee corruption and continuing attacks on local control.
