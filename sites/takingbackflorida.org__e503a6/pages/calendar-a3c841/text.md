United for Democracy
Join us for a community rally and candidate showcase!
We're bringing neighbors together for an afternoon that mixes civic energy with festival style fun.
Meet the candidates running to represent our community, hear their visions for the future of Florida, and enjoy a lively atmosphere filled with music, conversation, and connection.
We'll have live bands, food, light carnival games, and plenty of space for families, supporters, and first-time voters to get involved.
Whether you're coming to learn more, cheer on your favorite candidate, or just enjoy a community-centered afternoon, this rally is built for everyone.
We'll have candidates for the Florida House, Senate, and local school board.
Bring a friend, bring the kids, and bring your voice.
This is how we build momentum.
This is what democracy looks like!
