During my first term in the Senate, I have chief-authored over 30 bills which have been signed in to law, making life better for the folks in District 41, and all of Minnesota.
Here are a few highlights:
Amara’s Law – SF 834
Amara’s Law was passed in 2023 and contains the strictest bans on intentionally-added PFAS in consumer products in the nation.
It not only bans the addition of PFAS to things like cookware, dental floss, cosmetics, and children’s products, it put reporting requirements in place so consumers know what they’re buying.
I received international attention due to my work on this bill!
Not only was I interviewed for a Japanese documentary on my work to fight PFAS in Minnesota, I met with an Australian Member of Parliament who flew all the way to Minnesota to learn more about my work.
I have also testified before legislative committees in Massachusetts and New South Wales, Australia, regarding my work on Amara’s Law.
Fighting Fentanyl Package – SF 2659, SF 1687
I have been a crusader for keeping our communities safe from fentanyl from day one.
We have seen this deadly poison devastate our communities and, after hearing the heartbreaking stories of constituents lost to fentanyl poisoning, I decided enough was enough.
In 2023 I enacted a law increasing the penalty for distributing fentanyl to the same level as heroin, recognizing the incredible danger of this drug.
And in 2025, I enacted a law allowing high school students to self-carry the life-saving drug, Naloxone, on school grounds.
I am committed to continuing to fight for legislation to keep fentanyl out of neighborhoods, homes, schools, and communities.
Eligibility Expansion for Public Safety Officer Survivor Benefits – SF 1989, SF 4667
Families of our public safety partners often struggled to obtain Line-of-Duty-Death Benefits that were due to them after losing a loved one on the job.
I made this process easier by passing legislation in 2023 that recognized PTSD as a line-of-duty death for police, fire, and EMS, and in 2026 passing legislation recognizing death from certain cancers, as well as COVID, as presumed line-of-duty deaths.
I am proud to support my public safety partners.
Payday Lending – SF 1635
In 2023, I cracked down on predatory payday lenders, capping the interest they are allowed to charge at 36%.
Before then, these loans often caught folks in a debt trap, causing borrowers to take out one loan after another just to meet the obligations of the prior loans.
The average borrower had a staggering NINE payday loans, and paid interest exceeding 400%!
I was able to put these predators out of business in Minnesota and save consumers hundreds of thousands of dollars!
Wage Theft Enforcement Provisions – SF 1988
In 2023, during my first year in office, I took on the pervasive issue of wage theft and enacted a law to protect workers and to hold general contractors accountable.
Up until this time, general contractors often turned a blind eye to the questionable employment practices of their subs, and allowed wage theft to run rampant in the construction industry.
I cracked down on this practice and held general contractors responsible if their subs engaged in wage theft.
Since the enactment of this law, we have seen a dramatic decrease in reported wage theft, and it has held everyone to a higher standard when it comes to hiring practices in the construction industry!
Forever Green SF 1220
Forever Green is an initiative of the University of Minnesota to develop sustainable and economically viable cover crops to help improve soil and water health.
Farmers know how important cover crops are to maintain soil health and integrity, but they traditionally have not been viable economic options.
Forever Green is changing that with the development of perennial, winter annual, and native woody crops such as Kernza, winter camelina, pennycress, and winter durum.
I have authored legislation year after year to support the Forever Green program and ensure that sustainable farming is also economically sound.
Increasing Penalty for Assault of a Firefighter or EMS Worker – SF 1329
As a paramedic, I know that EMS workers are increasingly being assaulted and injured by violent behavior on the job.
After a harrowing attack on HCMC paramedics in 2025, I authored a bill to increase the penalty for assaulting a firefighter or EMS worker.
When our frontline first responders are out every day keeping us safe, I am proud to have their backs at the Capitol.
Supporting Mental Health in the Construction Industry – SF 2744
Supporting mental heath is a priority for me.
But I also know that in some industries, it can be difficult to speak up when you’re not OK.
I am working to change that.
I enacted a law in 2025 providing support, funding, and resources for the construction industry to allow workers to speak up and be connected with immediate help when they need it.
Modeled after laws passed in the fire service, this law focuses on changing the culture of mental health in the construction industry as much as providing resources.
My law is an important step in ensuring the mental health of all Minnesotans is supported no matter what industry you’re in.
Abandoned Boats – SF 2735
When I was contacted by a constituent with concerns of an abandoned boat which had been polluting the beautiful St.
Croix River for months, I rolled up my sleeves and got to work.
I worked across the border with the Mayor of Hudson, WI to develop a bill to combat this pervasive problem.
Did you know that folks routinely abandon boats not just in waterways, but in State Parks and along state highways?
These boats are expensive to get rid of and agencies often did not have the resources to do so.
My bill authorized the DNR to not only remove these environmental hazards, but to assess the costs of removal back against the owner!
