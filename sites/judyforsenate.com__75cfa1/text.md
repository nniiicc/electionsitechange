Judy Seeberger Gets The Job Done
Meet Judy
I have spent my life dedicated to service and helping others.
I am a public school teacher, a paramedic, a firefighter, a lawyer, a military wife, and the mom of two amazing young men.
I understand the dedication and sacrifice that comes with public service.
I am also not afraid to stand up for what’s right – even if it is uncomfortable or comes at personal cost.
Since being elected in 2022, I have been committed getting results.
I have enacted legislation to support public safety, to enhance consumer protections, to protect our environment, and to make life better for all Minnesotans.
I have also demonstrated that I can do what’s right for the people of my district – pushing back when needed, and standing strong in the face of special interests.
My commitment to you is to continue to be a voice for balance and accountability.
