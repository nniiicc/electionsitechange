Meet Eric
Eric Toney is dedicated to the safety and security of Wisconsin families.
A tough and proven prosecutor, Eric has successfully prosecuted crimes as varied as human trafficking, homicide, sexual assault, domestic violence, drug trafficking, financial crimes, and racketeering.
Eric served as President of the Wisconsin District Attorneys Association, helping build coalitions across the criminal justice system and both parties to deliver real results for Wisconsin, and was named Wisconsin’s 2024 District Attorney of the Year.
As Wisconsin’s next Attorney General, Eric will restore the Department of Justice’s mission to fight crime, support law enforcement, protect families, and enforce the law.
Why I’m Running
For more than a decade, I’ve served as Fond du Lac County District Attorney – leading efforts to protect our communities, stand up for victims, and hold violent criminals accountable.
Then, two years ago at age 39, I underwent open-heart surgery.
By the grace of God – and years of running – my heart held on.
That clarity led me here: if I could still make a difference for Wisconsin, I would.
I’m running for Attorney General to put that experience to work for you, because Wisconsin deserves an Attorney General who puts public safety before politics, defends the law without fear or favor, and stands up for every Wisconsinite.
Our Department of Justice has amazing frontline staff, but after nearly eight years of broken promises and political spin from the Attorney General, it’s time for change.
It’s time to restore integrity, accountability, and leadership to an office that has lost its way – and make Wisconsin safer for every family in every community.
