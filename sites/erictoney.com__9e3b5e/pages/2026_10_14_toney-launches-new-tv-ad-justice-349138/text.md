14, 2026 CONTACT: info@erictoney.com Toney Launches New TV Ad “Justice” Ad to air statewide on broadcast, cable, and digital FOND DU LAC, WI – On Wednesday, Eric Toney launched his first television ad “Justice.” The ad, airing statewide on broadcast, cable, and digital, features sheriffs who have seen the consequences of Josh Kaul’s eight years as Attorney General, including historic crime lab delays that have denied justice for victims.
The ad features testimonials from each of the five sheriffs featured: “We depend on Wisconsin’s crime labs to help deliver justice for victims.
Josh Kaul promised to fix the backlog.” Sheriff Ryan Waldschmidt, Fond du Lac County “Josh Kaul promised to fix the backlog.
Instead, average DNA testing times are up 60%.” Sheriff Eric Severson, Waukesha County “Even with more resources, better technology, Kaul is still taking twice as long to complete many tests.” Sheriff Brent York, Adams County “Then Kaul changed the reporting, delayed the release of the numbers, and hid his failure from public view.” Sheriff David Zoerner, Kenosha County “Josh Kaul has failed Wisconsin, and victims deserve better.” Sheriff Christy Knowles, Ozaukee County Narrator: Eight years of broken promises is enough.
