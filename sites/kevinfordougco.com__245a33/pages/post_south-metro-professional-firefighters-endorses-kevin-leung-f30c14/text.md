South Metro Professional Firefighters Endorses Kevin Leung
We’re proud to share an important campaign update: South Metro Professional Firefighters Local 2086 has endorsed Kevin Leung for State Senate District 30 which include Highlands Ranch, Lone Tree, The Village at Castle Pines, Castle Pines, Sterling Ranch, and Roxborough Park.
This endorsement is especially meaningful to Kevin.
Since being elected to the South Metro Fire Rescue Board of Directors in 2022, Kevin has worked alongside the firefighters and first responders who serve and protect our communities every day.
In announcing its endorsement, Local 2086 highlighted Kevin’s commitment to public safety and his support for the men and women of South Metro Fire Rescue, noting that he has stood “shoulder-to-shoulder” with firefighters in supporting the resources and policies they need to serve our community.
Kevin is deeply honored to have the support of South Metro Professional Firefighters Local 2086 and the firefighters who put their lives on the line to keep our communities safe.
🗳️ Vote Kevin Leung for Colorado State Senate District 30 this November 3.
