All Posts Search South Metro Professional Firefighters Endorses Kevin Leung kevinfordougco Sep 25 1 min read We’re proud to share an important campaign update: South Metro Professional Firefighters Local 2086 has endorsed Kevin Leung for State Senate District 30 which include Highlands Ranch, Lone Tree, The Village at Castle Pines, Castle Pines, Sterling Ranch, and Roxborough Park.
This endorsement is especially meaningful to Kevin.
Since being elected to the South Metro Fire Rescue Board of Directors in 2022, Kevin has worked alongside the firefighters and first responders who serve and protect our communities every day.
In announcing its endorsement, Local 2086 highlighted Kevin’s commitment to public safety and his support for the men and women of South Metro Fire Rescue, noting that he has stood “shoulder-to-shoulder” with firefighters in supporting the resources and policies they need to serve our community.
Kevin is deeply honored to have the support of South Metro Professional Firefighters Local 2086 and the firefighters who put their lives on the line to keep our communities safe. 🗳️ Vote Kevin Leung for Colorado State Senate District 30 this November 3.

Kevin Leung is a community leader, small business owner, and education advocate running for Colorado State Senate District 30.
After 36 years in Douglas County, Kevin has served as a Douglas County School Board Director and South Metro Fire Rescue Board member.
His priorities include affordable housing for local workers, strong public schools, responsible growth that protects water and resources, and a better quality of life for seniors.
Kevin’s campaign focuses on collaboration and results—bringing people together across party lines to solve problems.
