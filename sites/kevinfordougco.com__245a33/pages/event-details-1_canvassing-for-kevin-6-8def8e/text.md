Kevin Leung is a community leader, small business owner, and education advocate running for Colorado State Senate District 30.
After 36 years in Douglas County, Kevin has served as a Douglas County School Board Director and South Metro Fire Rescue Board member.
His priorities include affordable housing for local workers, strong public schools, responsible growth that protects water and resources, and a better quality of life for seniors.
Kevin’s campaign focuses on collaboration and results—bringing people together across party lines to solve problems.
