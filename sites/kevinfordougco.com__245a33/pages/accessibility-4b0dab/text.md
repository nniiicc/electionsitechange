Accessibility
Kevin Leung for State Senate District 30 is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all visitors, including individuals with disabilities.
We strive to make our website accessible and user-friendly in accordance with applicable accessibility standards.
We are continually working to improve the accessibility of our website to ensure that all individuals have equal access to information about our campaign.
Accessibility Features
Our website is designed to:
- Be navigable by keyboard
- Use readable fonts and sufficient color contrast
- Include descriptive links and alternative text where applicable
- Support screen reader compatibility where possible
Ongoing Efforts
We are committed to improving accessibility and regularly review our website to identify areas for enhancement.
