MY PROMISE TO YOU
I’m not running to be another politician.
I’m running to be the same neighbor I’ve always been—someone who shows up, does the work, and gets results.
At a school board forum, someone once told me I didn’t belong because I didn’t speak English.
I could have walked away.
Instead, I stayed—and kept serving.
That’s the kind of leader I’ll be in the State Senate: facing challenges with grace, working across party lines, and never forgetting that every decision affects real families with real dreams.
In the State Senate, I’ll bring the same approach that helped us pass a historic $250 million school bond and secure $40 million annually for teacher pay: listen with compassion, act with courage, and serve with heart.
An immigrant from Hong Kong with a thick accent could never have imagined he’d one day have the chance to serve Douglas County in the State Senate.
But that’s the magic of this place we call home.
And I’m running to keep that magic alive—for the teacher who needs affordable housing, the hardworking family worried about putting food on the table, the senior who needs healthcare, and the newcomer with an accent chasing their American dream.
This isn’t just a campaign.
It’s our chance to write the next chapter of Colorado’s success story—together.
