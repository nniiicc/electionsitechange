Volunteer
This campaign isn’t powered by special interests—it’s powered by people like you
Whether you're knocking on doors, making phone calls, sharing our message online, or helping behind the scenes, you are the heart of this campaign.
Kevin believes in community-first leadership, and that starts with a team of passionate, engaged neighbors who are ready to make a difference.
No matter your experience or schedule, there’s a way for you to get involved.
Why Volunteer?
- Make a Local Impact: Help elect a leader who listens and delivers results.
- Build Community: Join a team of like-minded neighbors and changemakers.
- Use Your Talents: From tech to talking, your skills matter here.
- Have Fun While Doing Good: Campaigning is hard work—but it’s also energizing, meaningful, and memorable.
Ways You Can Help
✅ Door-to-Door Canvassing
Help us connect directly with voters in your neighborhood.
Training provided!
✅ Phone Banking or Texting
Talk to voters from the comfort of your home.
Flexible and powerful.
✅ Event Support
Help set up, greet guests, and spread the word at campaign events and town halls.
✅ Social Media Outreach
Share campaign updates, stories, and key messages online.
✅ Office & Admin Help
Assist with data entry, mailers, or behind-the-scenes organizing.
✅ Host a Meet & Greet
Invite your friends, neighbors, or faith group to learn about Kevin’s vision.
