Privacy Policy
Kevin Leung for State Senate District 30
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Kevin Leung for State Senate District 30 (“Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you interact with our website, sign up for text message updates, donate, volunteer, or otherwise engage with our campaign.
Information We Collect
When you opt in to receive text messages or engage with our campaign, we may collect:
- Your name
- Your mobile phone number
- Your email address (if provided)
- Mailing address (if provided)
- Donation information (processed securely through third-party platforms)
- Interaction data related to campaign communications
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Send political campaign updates
- Provide volunteer and event information
- Share voting reminders and important notices
- Respond to your inquiries
- Process donations
- Improve campaign communications
Message frequency may vary based on your interactions, upcoming events, or campaign updates.
Text Messaging Terms
By providing your mobile number and opting in, you consent to receive recurring political text messages from Kevin Leung for State Senate District 30.
- Message & data rates may apply.
- Message frequency varies.
- Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.
- Reply HELP for assistance.
- You may also contact us at: kevin@kevinfordougco.com
Consent to receive text messages is not required as a condition of donation.
Information Sharing
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.
Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.
We may share information with:
- Trusted service providers who assist in campaign operations
- Compliance vendors
- Payment processors
- Text messaging platforms
All third parties are required to maintain the confidentiality of your information.
Data Security
We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information.
However, no system can guarantee absolute security.
Your Choices
You may:
- Unsubscribe from email communications via the unsubscribe link
- Opt out of text messages by replying STOP
- Contact us to request removal of your information
