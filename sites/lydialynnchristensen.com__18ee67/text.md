Become a Constitutional Captain today. ★ Libertarian • Michigan • 2026 Meet the Candidate Meet Lydia Lydia Christensen is an American politician from Michigan and a candidate for the United States Senate in 2026.
She is Michigan's Unchained Voice.
Her family is from Ironwood, MI, but she was born in Ashland, Wisconsin, helicoptered across the border because the UP lacked the hospital infrastructure to deliver her safely.
She has been fighting for the Upper Peninsula ever since returning home.
As the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Senate, Lydia speaks for all Michiganders free from left-right party constraints.
Her campaign is about unchaining opportunity, protecting clean water, and building unity across our state.

Learn More Repealing the Patriot Act: Defending Civil Liberties Most of the Patriot Act's surveillance powers already expired in 2020.
Two provisions are still active: sneak-and-peek warrants that delay notifying you of a search, and National Security Letters that compel your records without a judge's approval.
Lydia will push to end both.
