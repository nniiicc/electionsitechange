Read More Opt-In, Not Out Save Medicaid poster, public domain/CC0 Medicaid for All: Real Choice, Real Coverage Medicaid for All is opt-in coverage for citizens and legal residents.
All medically necessary care is covered, no diagnosis or category required.
Premiums are capped by income, free below a set threshold, and priced using Medicare-negotiated rates.
Full enrollment within three years, backed by real outreach funding so people know it exists.
Funded by drug price negotiation, administrative savings, spending cuts, and House-originated legislation closing corporate and wealth tax loopholes, not by taxing workers, and structured as mandatory spending so it can't lapse through inaction the way ACA subsidies just did.

Read More Food and Drug Administration logo, public domain Restoring Food Safety The FDA budget was cut from $3.6B (2024) to $2.9B proposed (2026).
Small farm exemptions already exist in FSMA: $25K de minimis (fully exempt), $500K qualified exempt (direct-to-consumer).
Private auditors are already accredited by FDA: SQF, BRC, FSSC 22000.
Compliance costs: small farms 6-7% of sales vs.
0.33% for large farms.
Lydia will rebuild inspection capacity, let large operations use accredited private auditors with FDA oversight, keep small farms and direct-to-consumer sales out of the compliance trap, and hold the system accountable for actual safety outcomes, not paperwork.
