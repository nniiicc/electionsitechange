Medium bio (2–3 sentences): Lydia Christensen is the Libertarian Party candidate for Michigan's open U.S.
Senate seat in 2026.
Born into a family from Ironwood, MI, she was inspired by the Upper Peninsula's waterfalls and natural beauty to fight for Michigan's environment, economy, and individual liberty.
She built this entire campaign herself - website, platform, and grassroots organizing - without establishment backing or corporate donors.
Full bio: Lydia Christensen is running for U.S.
Senate in Michigan as the Libertarian Party nominee, operating under the motto "Unchain Michigan." She believes that both major parties have failed the state, using Michigan as a bargaining chip rather than fighting for its people.
Lydia's family roots are in Ironwood, MI - in the heart of the Upper Peninsula - and her campaign priorities reflect that heritage: protecting the Great Lakes, expanding economic opportunity in rural and UP communities, advancing LGBTQ+ rights and social equity, and championing digital rights and responsible cryptocurrency policy.
She has no corporate PAC backing and built this campaign - including its website and legislative tracking tools - entirely herself.
As the Libertarian nominee, Lydia has automatic ballot access; her Constitutional Captains program now has volunteers knocking the 12,000–15,000 doors needed to win by Election Day, November 3, 2026.
