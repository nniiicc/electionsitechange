FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE July 4, 2026 IRONWOOD, Mich., July 4, 2026.
On the 250th anniversary of the Declaration of Independence, Lydia Christensen, candidate for U.S.
Senate, has been confirmed as the Libertarian Party of Michigan's nominee for the November 3, 2026 general election.
The Libertarian Party of Michigan certified her nomination following the state convention held June 28 in Troy.
Christensen has run her campaign under the banner "Unchaining Michigan" for more than a year, building a platform centered on civil liberties and economic populism.
Her positions include repeal of the Patriot Act and FISA Section 702, removal of automated license plate reader surveillance networks, a Digital Bill of Rights that enumerates a person's personal identifying information as their own property, and Medicaid for All.
"Two hundred fifty years ago, people risked everything over the idea that government needs consent and government needs limits," said Christensen.
"Both major parties have had thirty years to produce meaningful anti-surveillance legislation, and both have consistently voted against the Constitution's interests instead.
Section 702 of FISA still lets the government search Americans' communications without a warrant in 2026, and neither party has fixed it.
That is the actual measure of whether this country still believes what it said in 1776.
Not a slogan.
A warrant requirement.
My name is going to be on the ballot in November so Michigan voters have someone running who will hold that line instead of just quoting the Declaration on the Fourth of July and voting the other way in December." Christensen is a member of the Kiowa-Sioux Nation and has connected her campaign's civil liberties platform to tribal sovereignty and federal treaty obligations.
She lives in Ironwood, in Michigan's Upper Peninsula.
Christensen's committee, Lydia Christensen for United States Senate, is registered with the Federal Election Commission under Committee ID C00903575.
