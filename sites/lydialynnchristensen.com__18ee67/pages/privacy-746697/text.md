This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.
1.
Information We Collect Information you provide directly When you use our contact forms, volunteer signup, petition, or newsletter, we may collect: Full name Email address Phone number Mailing address and ZIP code Michigan county Your stated volunteer interests or message FEC-required information Federal law requires campaigns to collect and report the name, address, employer, and occupation of anyone who contributes more than $200 in an election cycle.
This information is reported to the Federal Election Commission and becomes part of the public record.
Automatically collected information When you visit this site, we automatically collect: Google Analytics 4 (GA4): Pages visited, session duration, general geographic region, browser type, and traffic source.
This data is anonymized and aggregated.
Facebook/Meta Pixel: Page views and certain conversion events to measure the effectiveness of campaign outreach.
See Meta's Privacy Policy for details.
2.
How We Use Your Information To respond to your messages and inquiries To send campaign updates, press releases, and event announcements if you opt in To coordinate volunteer activities and petition drives To process and report donations as required by federal law To understand how people use our website so we can improve it We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.
3.
Third-Party Services We use the following third-party services to operate this site.
Each has its own privacy policy: Donorbox - secure donation processing EmailJS - form submission email delivery Google Analytics 4 - site analytics (data processed by Google) Meta Pixel - campaign outreach analytics (data processed by Meta) 4.
Data Retention We retain contact and volunteer information for the duration of the campaign and as required by FEC recordkeeping rules (generally three years after the election).
You may request deletion of your information at any time, subject to our legal obligations.
5.
Your Rights You have the right to: Request a copy of the information we hold about you Request correction of inaccurate information Opt out of campaign communications at any time by replying "UNSUBSCRIBE" to any email Disable Google Analytics tracking by installing the GA Opt-out Browser Add-on 6.
Security We use industry-standard security practices to protect your data.
Donation processing is handled entirely by Donorbox and is never stored on our servers.
7.
Contact Questions about this policy?
Contact us at lydialynnchristensen.com/#/contact or email senator@lydialynnchristensen.com .
