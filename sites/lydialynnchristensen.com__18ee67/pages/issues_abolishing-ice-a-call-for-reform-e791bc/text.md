Constitution and human rights.
From the separation of families at the border to the indiscriminate detention of immigrants, ICE has shown a disregard for American values of justice and equality.
Lydia will advocate for the abolition of ICE, as the agency has demonstrated a pattern of abuse, corruption, and human rights violations that cannot be reformed.
She will push for a complete dismantling of ICE and its powers, seeking instead a new, humane approach to immigration enforcement—one that prioritizes the protection of individuals' rights, due process, and decency.
