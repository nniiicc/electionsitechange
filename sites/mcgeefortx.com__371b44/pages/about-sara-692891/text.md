About Sara
I am not just a candidate; I am a lifelong Texan, born in Dallas and raised in Katy since I was three.
I walked the halls of Katy High School and earned my Bachelor’s in Radiologic Sciences from Midwestern State University in Wichita Falls.
For 22 years, I’ve dedicated my career to healthcare, currently serving as the Director of Radiology at a Houston hospital.
I am also a proud mother to two children, and our chocolate lab, CJ.
Having called House District 132 home for 41 years, this place isn’t just where I live; it’s where I’ve grown up, where I’ve built my life, and where I’ve raised my children.
The people here aren’t just constituents; they are my neighbors, my friends, and my extended family.
I am running for office because I believe passionately that our collective voice, the voice of this community, deserves to be heard loud and clear in the Texas State Legislature.
Let’s ensure our district is represented by someone who truly understands and lives the life of a Texan.
