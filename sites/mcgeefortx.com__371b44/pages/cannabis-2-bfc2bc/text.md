Let the people have their plants.
Cannabis has been studied extensively, and the benefits are innumerable.
Here are some of the benefits that legalization could bring to our state: Tax revenue: States like Colorado have reported millions in income from cannabis sales, which could be used in Texas to fund public services, education or infrastructure.
Job creation: The cannabis industry would create jobs in cultivation, retail, distribution and related sectors.
Regulation for safety: Legal cannabis can be regulated for quality, ensuring that customers have access to safe, uncontaminated products.
Medical benefits: Full legalization could expand on the limited medical use available now, potentially aiding those with conditions like epilepsy, chronic pain, PTSD and more.
Reduction in opioid use: Some studies suggest that legal access to cannabis might decrease opioid use and related deaths by offering an alternative for pain management.
Border security: Legalization could decrease the illicit cannabis trade across our southern border, reducing the influence of cartels and improving border security.
