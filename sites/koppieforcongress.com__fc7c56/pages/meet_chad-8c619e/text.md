Meet Chad
Chad is the Republican nominee for Illinois' 7th Congressional District.
He had three terms on the Kane Co.
Regional Board of Schools, and served as the board president.
In 1998, Chad was the only conservative who ran for Illinois governor, in the Republican primary.
In 2020, Chad ran for Congress in Illinois' 8th District.
He was on the ballot for the U.S.
Senate in 2016 as a write-in candidate; 2008 for the Constitution Party; 1996 for the U.S.
Taxpayers Party; and 1992 for the Conservative Party of Illinois.
Chad was the vice-chairman of the Illinois Center Right Coalition, 2007-'09.
The ICRC was a conservative, statewide group that was founded in 2003.
The ICRC steering committee included about 40 leaders including Jim Oberweis, Pat O'Malley, Steve Rauschenberger, Norm Hill, Mike Psak, Jon Zahm, Rich Evans, and Cody Holt.
