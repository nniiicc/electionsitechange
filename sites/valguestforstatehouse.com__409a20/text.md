Val Guest has called the Grand Strand home for more than 30 years.
As an attorney, former prosecutor, and State Representative, Val brings decades of experience and a commitment to protecting the community he calls home.
In the State House, Val is working to keep our communities safe, protect taxpayers, promote accountable government, and address the challenges that come with Horry County's continued growth.
