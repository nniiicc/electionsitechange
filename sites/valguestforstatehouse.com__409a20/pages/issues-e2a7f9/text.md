Issues
Public Safety
As a former prosecutor and member of the House Judiciary Committee, Val understands the importance of strong laws, effective law enforcement, and safe communities.
Val supported pay increases for state law enforcement and $24.5 million to strengthen the Palmetto 800 communications system used by law enforcement, firefighters, and first responders.
He remains committed to supporting those who protect our communities and keeping Horry County families safe.
Growth & Infrastructure
Horry County is one of South Carolina's fastest-growing areas, and Val believes our infrastructure must keep pace while protecting the quality of life that makes the Grand Strand special.
Val supported $344 million for roads and bridges to improve infrastructure, address congestion, and move important transportation projects forward.
He will continue working to ensure the needs of Horry County are heard as South Carolina plans for continued growth.
Economy & Tax Relief
Val believes government should respect the taxpayers who fund it and create an environment where families and businesses can thrive.
Val helped deliver $422 million in new tax relief, including reducing South Carolina's top income tax rate from 6% to 5.21% and increasing the Homestead Exemption for eligible seniors from $50,000 to $75,000.
He will continue working to keep South Carolina affordable, competitive, and fiscally responsible.
Education
Strong schools are essential to preparing the next generation and maintaining South Carolina's continued economic success.
Val supported raising the minimum starting teacher salary to $50,500, adding another $2,000 to the teacher salary schedule, and making new investments in classroom materials, school safety, buses, and school facilities.
He remains committed to supporting students, teachers, and parents while preparing South Carolina's workforce for the future.
