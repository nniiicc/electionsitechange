I understand what working families are going through right now because I experienced the struggles firsthand.

That’s why my husband and I started Barrington Children’s Charities, to help struggling families in our community.
For over 14 years we have provided meals for children struggling with food insecurity at home, brought mobile dental units to underserved communities, and even provided glasses and mental health services.
My top priority is making things more affordable for working families who are struggling by lowering costs for things like health care and prescription drugs, utilities, housing, education, groceries, gas, and childcare.
Everyone agrees it is too expensive to live in Illinois right now.
It’s time we put aside the political agendas and partisan games and focused on helping working families.
I will also fight to keep our communities safe for families and children.
