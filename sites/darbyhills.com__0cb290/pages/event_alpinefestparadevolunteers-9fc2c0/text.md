Volunteers Needed: Walk with Senator Darby Hills in the Lake Zurich Alpine Fest Parade July 19 @ 10:30 am - 12:30 pm « Internship Opportunity Day of Action » Volunteers Needed: Walk with Senator Darby Hills in the Lake Zurich Alpine Fest Parade Senator Darby Hills is looking for volunteers to join her campaign team at this year’s Lake Zurich Alpine Fest Parade.
The parade is a great opportunity to connect with neighbors, meet local families, and help spread Senator Hills’ message throughout the community.
Whether you’re a longtime supporter or looking to get involved for the first time, we’d love to have you walk alongside Senator Hills.
Event Details Event: Lake Zurich Alpine Fest Parade Date: Sunday, July 19 Time: 11:00 a.m.
Parade Kick-Off Location: Lake Zurich Volunteers will walk the parade route with Senator Hills, greet residents, and hand out campaign materials.
Comfortable walking shoes are recommended.
If you’re interested in participating or have any questions, please contact Campaign Manager Jon Nelson by texting 630-677-5168 or emailing [email protected] .
We hope you’ll join us as we celebrate one of Lake Zurich’s favorite community traditions and continue building momentum across the 26th Senate District.
