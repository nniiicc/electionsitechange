Fall Internship
August 25 - November 3
Team Darby is growing!
Senator Darby Hills has loved working alongside the talented interns who have been part of Team Darby, and this fall, she’s excited to expand the team and welcome a new group of motivated students.
This is more than just an internship.
It’s an opportunity to get hands-on experience in politics and public service, work directly on issues impacting Illinois families, build professional skills and be part of a team making a difference in the community.
Students interested in politics, government, communications, public policy or grassroots organizing are encouraged to apply.
Fall Internship Details:
• $20/hour
• Up to 20 hours per week
• Academic credit available
• Hands-on experience with Team Darby
