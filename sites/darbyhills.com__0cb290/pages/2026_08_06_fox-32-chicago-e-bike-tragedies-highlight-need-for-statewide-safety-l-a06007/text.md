Volunteer Opportunities Contact Voter Info Contribute Contact FOX 32 Chicago: E-Bike Tragedies Highlight Need for Statewide Safety Legislation August 6, 2026 Darby Hills recently spoke with FOX 32 Chicago about two heartbreaking tragedies involving Illinois children: a 16-year-old Chicago student who lost his life in an e-scooter crash and another child who was seriously injured in an e-bike crash. https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/crystal-lake-e-bike-crash https://www.fox32chicago.com/news/chicago-student-killed-e-scooter-crash-remembered-his-kindness-big-dreams The tragedies are raising new concerns about the safety of high-powered e-bikes and e-scooters, some of which can reach speeds of up to 50 miles per hour.
As Senator Hills explained, mayors, local officials and police chiefs have been raising concerns about these vehicles and the challenges law enforcement faces under current Illinois law.
That is why Senator Hills co-sponsored legislation to establish statewide safety standards for high-powered e-bikes and e-scooters.
The legislation would give law enforcement clear authority to address unsafe riding and create consistent safety standards across Illinois.
This is not about banning e-bikes or e-scooters.
It is about commonsense regulations that protect children, families and communities while giving law enforcement the tools they need.
