Volunteer Opportunities Contact Voter Info Contribute Contact On ABC 7 Eyewitness News: Senator Hills Highlights Need for Statewide E-Bike Safety Standards August 4, 2026 Darby Hills recently joined ABC 7 Eyewitness News with Tanja Babich to discuss efforts to improve safety for e-bike, scooter and other micromobility device users across Illinois.
Hills co-sponsored SB 3484 in response to growing concerns from parents, local communities and law enforcement about the rapid increase in electric bikes and scooters and the lack of consistent statewide standards governing their use.
As these devices have become more common for transportation and recreation, Hills has pushed for clearer rules that prioritize safety while still allowing families and riders to enjoy the benefits of micromobility.
The legislation establishes statewide standards for e-bikes and other electric micromobility devices, creating clearer expectations for riders and helping communities and law enforcement better understand how different types of devices should be operated.
The goal is to provide consistency across Illinois rather than leaving residents to navigate a patchwork of different rules from one community to another.
The issue has taken on greater urgency following several serious crashes involving young riders.
Hills has emphasized the importance of making sure Illinois’ laws keep pace with the technology being used on Illinois roads, trails and sidewalks and that families have the information they need to make safe decisions.
