CONTACT (828) 400-4812 TO REQUEST YOURS Home About Issues Disaster Recovery Economy Education Healthcare Public Safety Small Businesses Volunteer News Events Contact Public Safety & Justice Keeping Our Communities Safe and Our Courts Fair Experience That Strengthens Safety and Trust Spending over thirty years as a District Court Judge, Danny Davis understands what it takes to keep communities safe while ensuring fairness and respect for everyone involved.
He knows the challenges law enforcement faces and the importance of courts free from political interference.
The Problem Law enforcement and courts are strained.
Political interference is eroding judicial independence.
Communities need clear laws and consistent application of them.
People need to trust the justice system to protect their rights.
Danny’s Plan Support law enforcement with resources and practical policies.
Advocate for clear legal standards and consistent application of the law.
Uphold judicial independence and oppose political interference.
Strengthen services for people who need supervision, treatment, or support.
Why it matters Safety depends on trust.
Trust in the law, trust in the courts, and trust that decisions are made fairly.
Danny’s decades of experience give him the insight and integrity to strengthen that trust across WNC.
