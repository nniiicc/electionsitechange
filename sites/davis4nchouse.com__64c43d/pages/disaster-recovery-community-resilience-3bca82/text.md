CONTACT (828) 400-4812 TO REQUEST YOURS Home About Issues Disaster Recovery Economy Education Healthcare Public Safety Small Businesses Volunteer News Events Contact Disaster Recovery Helping Western North Carolina Heal and Rebuild Rebuilding Stronger, Together Hurricane Helene left deep scars on Haywood and Madison counties.
Homes were destroyed, businesses were shuttered, and many families are still displaced.
Danny knows recovery must move faster, and must continue long after the headlines fade.
The Problem Many families still cannot return home.
Small businesses remain damaged or closed.
State support has been too slow and too limited.
Future storms will continue to threaten WNC communities.
Danny’s Plan Fight for full funding to rebuild homes, businesses, and infrastructure.
Support long-term recovery programs, not short-term fixes.
Promote resilience planning so WNC is better protected from future storms.
Ensure rural communities are not forgotten in state allocations.
Why it matters For many families, the damage from Helene didn’t end when the waters receded.
The loss, the disruption, and the constant worry about the next storm remain.
Danny will work tirelessly to ensure Western North Carolina isn’t left waiting or forgotten as we rebuild stronger and protect our communities for the future.
