A Career of service
Danny Davis has spent more than three decades serving the people of Western North Carolina.
First as an attorney, then as a District Court Judge for 33 years, and later as Chairman of the Haywood County Board of Elections.
His life’s work has centered on fairness, integrity, and truly listening to the people he serves.

More Than a Judge.
This Is His Why.
For decades, Danny sat with people at some of the hardest moments of their lives: families breaking apart, young people losing their direction, victims seeking justice, and neighbors struggling to rebuild.
Those experiences shaped him.
They taught him that every person has a story, and every decision has a real human impact.
They also strengthened his belief that public service means listening first, acting with compassion, and fighting for fairness; not politics.
That’s why he’s running for North Carolina House 118.
A Life Rooted in This Community
Danny was raised here, in Western North Carolina, by a family of working people and learned the value of hard work early; stocking warehouses, cleaning buildings, and working trade jobs before becoming an attorney.
He understands the challenges facing working families because he’s lived them.
He chose to raise his own family here, serve here, and dedicate his career to helping the people of this district find fairness, stability, and strength in difficult times.
That deep connection to his community guides everything he does.
33 Years on the Bench
Danny handled thousands of cases with fairness and consistency, always respecting the people whose lives were impacted.
Guided by Faith and Service
Danny’s life is shaped by faith, family, and a commitment to lifting others up through steady leadership.
Problem Solver in Crisis

Trusted Community Leader

Election Integrity Advocate

What Drives Him Forward
Danny believes leadership is about responsibility.
To listen, to understand, and to do what is right even when it’s difficult.
After a lifetime of working with families, children, and neighbors in crisis, he’s running for the NC House with one goal: to deliver real, practical solutions that strengthen the future of Western North Carolina.
