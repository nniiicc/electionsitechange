Housing costs are soaring, wages have barely moved in years, and childcare expenses are pushing parents to the brink.
Danny Davis believes our economy should work for the people who make it run, not just those at the top.
The Problem Housing costs have risen far faster than wages.
Rent increases are squeezing young families.
Childcare often costs as much as a mortgage payment.
The minimum wage has been stuck since 2009.
Tax policies reward wealth, not work.
Danny’s Plan Expand childcare access so parents can work without going into debt.
Push for wages that reflect today’s cost of living.
Fight for tax fairness that benefits working families and small businesses.
Support policies that help young people afford to stay in WNC.
Support housing strategies that increase affordability and stability.
Why it matters If we want our region to thrive, we must ensure that people who grow up here can afford to live here.
Danny is committed to a future where working people, teachers, nurses, tradesmen, service workers, young families, can build a life right here in Western North Carolina.
