But across WNC, families struggle with high costs, limited access, and long wait times for basic care, mental health treatment, and substance abuse services.
Danny has seen these struggles firsthand, and he’s ready to push for real solutions.
The Problem Healthcare costs continue rising with no relief in sight.
Mental health and addiction services are stretched thin.
Families face long drives or waits for basic healthcare.
Privatization has left vulnerable people without support.
Danny’s Plan Expand access to affordable healthcare and mental health treatment.
Push back against harmful privatization that leaves families behind.
Support funding for substance abuse programs and recovery efforts.
Strengthen rural healthcare so people in WNC get care close to home.
Why it matters Affordable, accessible healthcare is essential for strong families, strong communities, and a strong economy.
Danny will work to ensure no one in Western North Carolina is left without the care they need.
