CONTACT (828) 400-4812 TO REQUEST YOURS Home About Issues Disaster Recovery Economy Education Healthcare Public Safety Small Businesses Volunteer News Events Contact Privacy Policy Last Updated: 12/22/2025 This Privacy Policy explains how information about you is collected, used, and disclosed by Davis For NC House 118 (the “Campaign,” “we,” or “us”).
This Privacy Policy applies to information we collect when you use our website, mobile sites, email communications, text messages, social media pages, and other online services (collectively, the “Sites”).
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
If changes are made, the “Last Updated” date above will reflect the revision.
We encourage you to review this page periodically.
Information We Collect Information You Provide to Us We collect information you provide directly to us.
For example, we collect information when you: -Fill out a form on our Sites -Sign up for email or text message updates -Request information -Sign up as a volunteer -RSVP for events -Make a donation -Communicate with us by email or through social media -Otherwise interact with the Campaign The types of information we may collect include your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, employer, occupation, social media handles, and any other information you choose to provide.
Campaign Contribution Information North Carolina election laws may require us to collect certain personal information from donors.
This may include your name, mailing address, employer, and occupation , and to disclose certain contribution information as required by law.
Information We Collect Automatically When you access or use our Sites, we may automatically collect information, including: Log Information: Browser type, access times, pages viewed, IP address, and referring URLs Device Information: Information about the device you use to access the Sites, including operating system and browser Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to improve site functionality, understand usage, and measure campaign effectiveness You can learn more about cookies and how to control them in the “Your Choices” section below.
Information From Other Sources We may receive information about you from third-party platforms, including social media services, if you interact with our content or connect with us through those platforms, consistent with your privacy settings on those services.
How We Use Information We may use the information we collect to: -Provide, operate, and improve our Sites -Respond to inquiries and communicate with you -Send campaign updates, newsletters, and information about events and issues -Process donations and provide receipts or confirmations -Recruit volunteers and supporters -Comply with applicable election, reporting, and legal requirements -Analyze trends and measure campaign engagement -Personalize content and communications The Campaign is based in the United States, and the information we collect is governed by U.S. law.
Sharing of Information We may share information about you: -With vendors and service providers who perform services on our behalf (such as website hosting, email delivery, donation processing, or analytics) -As required by North Carolina election law or other applicable laws -To comply with legal obligations or lawful requests by public authorities -To protect the rights, property, or safety of the Campaign, its supporters, or others -With your consent or at your direction We may also share aggregated or anonymized information that does not identify you personally.
We do not sell your personal information.
Phone numbers collected for text messaging purposes will not be shared with third parties except as necessary to provide messaging services or as required by law.
Online Petitions and Public Actions If you sign an online petition or submit public comments, you understand that your name, city, state, and any comments provided may be publicly visible and shared with community leaders or the media.
Social Media Features Our Sites may include social sharing features or links to third-party platforms.
Your interactions with these features are governed by the privacy policies of the companies that provide them.
Analytics and Advertising We may use third-party analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or similar services) to understand how users engage with our Sites.
These providers may use cookies or similar technologies to collect information about your use of our Sites.
You can learn more about interest-based advertising and opt-out options at: www.aboutads.info/choices Security We take reasonable measures to help protect your personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
Your Choices Cookies Most browsers accept cookies by default.
You can adjust your browser settings to remove or reject cookies, though doing so may affect certain features of our Sites.
Email and Text Communications You may opt out of receiving promotional emails by following the unsubscribe instructions included in those emails.
To stop receiving text messages, reply STOP.
We may send a confirmation message after you opt out.
Text messaging opt-in data will not be shared with third parties, except as necessary to provide messaging services or comply with applicable laws.
