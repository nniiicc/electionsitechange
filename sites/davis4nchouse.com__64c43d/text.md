After decades on the District Court bench, Danny Davis has seen how policy failures land hardest on working families: higher costs, unsafe housing, strained schools, and broken healthcare systems.
Now Danny’s stepping off the bench and into the legislature to fight for real solutions.
Lower Costs.
Better Wages.
A Stronger Middle Class.
Families can’t keep up; not with housing, groceries, childcare, or energy bills rising faster than paychecks.
Danny Davis will prioritize policies that raise wages, reduce household costs, and rebuild the middle class.
Affordable Care
That Actually Works.
People shouldn’t skip medications or doctor visits because they can’t afford them.
Danny Davis will fight to lower healthcare costs and expand access; especially for working families and seniors.
