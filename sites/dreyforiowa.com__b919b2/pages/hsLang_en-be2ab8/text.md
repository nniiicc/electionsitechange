How old are you?
I turn 38 this year and think the perspective of a young (ish) mom is needed at the capitol.
We have the opportunity to make the state capitol look more like the people represented there and even though I'm considered "young" by political standards, I am ready to make the changes we need to attract more families to our state.
What experience do you bring to the table?
I’ve had a lot of jobs, from child welfare to financial services and I’ve seen policy failures up close.
I’m the founder of Moms for Iowa , a grassroots initiative making public policy accessible for busy Iowa parents, and I’ve served on local boards and statewide political committees.
All of these experiences have solidified one thing about me - I am on this earth to be of service to others.
So you get elected; what comes next?
I will prioritize clear communication via a variety of channels so you always know what’s going on at the capitol and how new legislation will affect you.
Helping my future constituents is my top priority, so if you ever need a hand, all you have to do is ask.
