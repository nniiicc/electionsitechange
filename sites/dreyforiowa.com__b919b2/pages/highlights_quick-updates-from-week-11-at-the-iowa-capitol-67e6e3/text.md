Request Your Absentee Ballot
The June 2 primaries are just around the corner, and you can now request your absentee ballot from the Iowa Secretary of State’s office.
The deadline to request your ballot is May 18 at 5:00 p.m.
Ballots must be returned by June 2.

150 years of UNI:
This week, the Iowa Senate adopted SR 111, a resolution recognizing and honoring the 150th anniversary of the University of Northern Iowa.
UNI began as the Iowa State Teachers College and has grown into one of our proud Regents universities, alongside the University of Iowa and Iowa State University.
