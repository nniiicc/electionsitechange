Jamie’s Work Bringing Home Grant Awards Rep.
Flick is incredibly proud to support our district in Harrisburg.
To date, projects in the 83rd District have been awarded $8.4 million dollars in grant funding .
This includes: Over $3.4 million dollars to the Williamsport Sanitary Authority which is estimated to save ratepayers 20% over the next two years Nearly $500K to Gregg Township Municipal Authority in Union County $500K to Lycoming County to update a section of the levee in South Williamsport Borough, protecting 644 structures and nearly 60% of the Borough’s land area, while making it compliant with federal regulations. $1.5 million through the Pennsylvania Department of Transportation’s (PennDOT) “Green Light-Go” program to update signal equipment at Little League Boulevard and Hepburn Street, and Market Street (SR 2023) and Little League Boulevard in the City of Williamsport, and for updated signal equipment along Market Street (Route 15) in South Williamsport Borough. $100K for the Lycoming County Coroner’s new building.
Supporting Commonsense Legislation Since taking office, Rep.
Jamie Flick has introduced several bills that will achieve the following: Eliminate Emissions Testing for Lycoming County (and similar sized counties), Provide Line of Duty Death Benefits for Coroners and Deputy Coroners, Change Requirements for Temporary Substitute Teachers (From 4-Year Degree to 2-Year Degree), Ensure Fairness for Children in Custody Disputes by Enacting Shared Parenting Law.
In addition, he also supports proposed legislation: Strengthen voter ID, Reduce the size of our state government, Reduce property taxes for veterans and senior citizens, Create Supreme Court Term Limits.

Finding Common Ground & Meet Me In The Middle Caucus.
Not all of us are going to agree on every issue.
And that’s okay – it’s part of human nature.
While many politicians refuse to listen or even talk to colleagues across the aisle, I believe we’re paid good money to do our jobs for all constituents – and that means rolling up our sleeves and finding common ground for causes like: Strengthening our mental health system, Increasing access for people and children with legitimate disabilities, And finding ways to better support our kids.
That’s why I helped form the Meet Me In The Middle Caucus.
We don’t need to agree on everything, but we can agree that some things are above politics.
Taking Personal Responsibility Personally, I don’t think people should be paid before a job is done.
That’s why I was the only state representatives who asked for my paycheck to be withheld until our state budget was fully passed (166 days).
Additionally, because I believe in smaller government, I have opted not to receive the pension, 401k plan or per diem and meal reimbursement benefits available to those who hold this office.
I also don’t believe in receiving money from businesses, special interest groups, super PACS or lobbyists.
I am the only representative that I know of that does not take money from any of the above.
That means I speak from the heart, vote for my district, and sleep well at night.
