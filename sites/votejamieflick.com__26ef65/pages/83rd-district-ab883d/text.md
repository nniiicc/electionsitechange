We are home to Little League Baseball, New Trail Brewing Company, Lycoming Engines, PMF Industries, Lycoming College, Pennsylvania College of Technology, The Community Arts Center, UPMC Williamsport, Allenwood Correctional Complex, Clyde Peeling’s Reptileland, the annual 9/11 Memorial Ride, and so much more.
This district is notable for the many farmers, teachers, business owners, doctors, service providers and homemakers who make our community a great place to live, work and raise families.
