Florida’s 2026 data center law says a water management district cannot permit a large-scale data center’s water use if it is harmful to the water resources of the area.
The Suwannee River Water Management District is one of the boards that makes that call.
That is the same board Merrillee has been sitting in front of for twenty years.
Who is Merrillee She did not come to this from politics.
She came to it from the river.
Merrillee Malwitz-Jipson co-owns Rum 138 in Fort White — a canoe and kayak outfitter, art gallery and cafe a few hundred yards from the Santa Fe River.
Watching that river change is what pulled her into two decades of water advocacy, long before anyone put her name on a ballot.
