You deserve to know exactly what I support—and what I oppose—before you ever cast a vote.
I’m not running for politics-as-usual.
I’m running to protect constitutional rights, restore public safety, strengthen our economy, and demand real accountability from government.
Below you’ll find clear positions, practical priorities, and the outcomes I’ll fight for.
If you agree, I’d be honored to earn your support.
If you have questions, I will answer them directly.
I am a conservative, and I will never hide what I believe.
But representation does not stop at a party line.
If you live in District 40, I want to know what is affecting your family, your business, your land, your school, and your community—whether you are Republican, Democrat, Independent, or none of the above.
My job is to listen, identify problems, gather the best ideas available, and determine what government can responsibly do to help solve them.
I believe prosperity begins with people, not government.
When families have greater financial freedom, they can save, invest, build businesses, raise families, pursue ideas, and chase dreams.
Strong people build strong businesses.
Strong businesses build strong communities.
Strong communities create a stronger New Mexico.
Government should provide the foundation—constitutional protection, public safety, education, infrastructure, responsible stewardship, and accountable administration—then give people room to build.
I do not need you to agree with me on everything.
I need you to tell me what matters.
Your politics may tell me how you vote.
Your problems tell me how I can serve you.
Find out what I oppose What I stand For When I say I'm a Republican I mean something simple: The Constitution comes first—always.
I believe in limited, accountable government, secure elections, and equal rights under the law.
I will defend the freedoms our families depend on—faith, speech, property rights, and the right to protect your home.
I believe the government’s job is to protect public safety, build the basics that keep a society strong (infrastructure and education), defend the nation, and then get out of the way so hardworking people can live free, raise kids, and build a life.
Why I'm a Republican I believe our rights are God-given and protected by the Constitution—not granted by politicians.
Government should be strong where it must be (public safety, national defense, secure elections, core infrastructure) and restrained everywhere else.
In House District 40, I will fight for families, lawful self-defense, parental rights, property rights, and honest stewardship of our land and taxpayer dollars.
ELECTIONS & ACCOUNTABILITY Do you support Voter ID?
Yes.
Voting rights are sacred, and election integrity protects every lawful citizen’s vote.
I support Voter ID because verifying eligibility strengthens confidence that elections are legitimate, fair, and secure—regardless of party.
Do you support “one issue, one bill”?
Yes.
I support one bill, one problem.
No more omnibus bills stuffed with unrelated policies and backroom deals.
Legislators should have to vote clearly on each major issue.
How will you stay accountable to constituents?
I will implement a clear response standard for constituent requests (target: within 72 hours ) with a tracking system to ensure people are heard and contacted back.
This office is accountable to the people—period.
If we can’t respond quickly, we’re not doing our job.
What would you change about the state petition portal experience?
I support reforms so voters can see more complete options when engaging with the petition process and aren’t nudged into party-line screens.
People should be able to support the candidate they believe is best, not the one a system makes easiest to find.
CRIME & PUBLIC SAFETY What will you do about violent and repeat offenders being released?
We must stop the revolving door.
Violent offenders and repeat offenders should not be released repeatedly at the expense of public safety.
Accountability must be real.
Strong Prosecution Strong prosecution of violent crime, including fentanyl trafficking and crimes against children, and support victims with stronger services and faster, more certain justice.
Victim First Policies Backing law enforcement with tools that work: coordinated policing, real consequences, and victim-first policy.
SCHOOL SAFETY What is the School Security Reserve Program?
I support creating a School Security Reserve Program : vetted, trained, authorized volunteers (including qualified parents) who complete intensive training and operate in coordination with local law enforcement and school administration .
The purpose is deterrence and rapid response—not politics.
Layered school security Controlled entry, communication plans, drills, and coordinated response—built for prevention and rapid action.
Our children deserve the highest level of protection we can responsibly provide.
We should explore lawful partnerships that increase trained, coordinated presence at major events—without turning schools into political battlegrounds.
Zero Tolerance for threats Zero tolerance for threats and violence, with clear consequences and rapid intervention.
SECOND AMENDMENT Shall Not Be Infringed Where do you stand on firearm ownership and lawful carry?
I support lawful ownership and carry for responsible citizens who are not violent felons.
I support training, safety, and personal responsibility—and I oppose policies that punish lawful people while criminals ignore the rules.
I will always focus enforcement on violent criminals and prohibited possessors—not on responsible citizens.
What about restoring rights for non-violent felons?
I support a case-by-case pathway for certain non-violent offenders after they have completed their sentence and proven rehabilitation through a rigorous process.
Violent felons should not have access.
Non-violent cases deserve careful review, not blanket lifetime punishment.
Do you support repealing broad “sensitive places” restrictions?
Yes.
I oppose broad “sensitive places” policies that disarm lawful citizens while criminals ignore the rules.
These policies create soft targets and punish responsible people.
SUPPRESSORS (SOUND SUPPRESSION) This is a Health and Safety issue.
Where do you stand on suppressors?
I support modernizing federal law so suppressors are treated as noise reduction and safety equipment , not as “firearms” under federal classification.
A suppressor cannot fire a round—its purpose is hearing protection and safety for hunters, shooters, ranges, landowners, and law enforcement.
Suppressors reduce noise—they do not make firearms “silent.” I will advocate for federal reform and support state-level policies that encourage hearing protection and responsible use.
Criminal Misuse Clear, consistent rules that punish criminal misuse while not burdening lawful citizens with outdated regulations.
Suppressor Encouragement Encouraging suppressor use for hearing protection and safer shooting environments for hunters, ranges, landowners, wildlife, and law enforcement.
TAXES, JOBS, AND GROWTH What’s your position on New Mexico’s Gross Receipts Tax (GRT)?
I support eliminating or radically overhauling GRT.
It punishes business activity and raises prices for everyone.
New Mexico should be a top-tier state to start and grow a business—growth will replace revenue through jobs, investment, and long-term expansion.
Do you support a Farm & Ranch Growth Package?
Yes.
I support a practical growth package that helps producers expand—targeted incentives, infrastructure support, and reduced burdens so more land can be put to productive use and more local food can be produced.
What about greenhouse and community garden initiatives?
I support programs that expand local food production—especially when tied to measurable outcomes like supplying shelters, clinics, and care facilities with healthy food.
Victory gardens, native vegetation restoration, seed banks, and tree-planting initiatives—these should be encouraged and supported by the state.
LAND ACCESS, FORESTS, AND WATER What do you want to do with state lands?
State lands should be protected, not neglected.
I support restoring access for recreation, firewood collection, and maintenance—plus road and forest work that improves public safety and emergency response capability.
Where do you stand on forest management?
Forest management needs a real overhaul: thinning, fuel reduction, access roads, and practical wildfire prevention.
Neglect turns forests into tinderboxes and threatens homes, lives, and watersheds.
What’s your position on cloud seeding / weather modification?
I support full transparency, strict oversight, and real accountability .
If it’s happening, the public deserves to know who authorized it, what is being used, and what independent review says about risks and outcomes.
No secrecy.
Until there is full transparency, an independent review, and clear public authorization, it should not be allowed in New Mexico.
What about water?
Water is New Mexico’s defining issue.
I will dive in immediately, rely on technical experts, and pursue practical solutions: infrastructure, watershed health, storage, conservation, and fair allocation that reflects New Mexico realities.
HEALTHCARE WASTE & GOVERNMENT EFFICIENCY Waste and broken systems hurt the people who need help most.
What needs to change about Medicare waste and administrative failures?
The system should help people who truly qualify—and it should stop wasting money through errors and broken processes.
I support auditing, modernization, and accountability so benefits go to those who need them, when they need them.
Transparency and Oversight Transparency in government spending: clear reporting, measurable outcomes, and consequences for failure.
Simplification Cutting red tape so seniors, families, and providers can get answers without years of delays.
LAW ENFORCEMENT I stand with all Law Enforcement!
Do you support more training and resources for law enforcement?
Yes.
Always.
Our officers deserve the best.
I support dedicated funding for specialized training, scenario training, and officer safety resources.
I back the blue, and I will fight to support those who protect us.
Officer and public safety must come first.
Everyone deserves this.
Competitive Pay To be the best and have the best, you have to offer the best, through competitive pay, retention incentives, and hazard compensation for officers doing high-risk work.
Wellness/Retention Mental health resources, peer support, benefits, and healthier schedules so good officers can stay strong and stay on the job.
PARENTS’ RIGHTS & EDUCATION Do you support resources for homeschooling parents?
Yes.
Parents who homeschool still pay taxes and still deserve access to support services and resources.
Education is one of the most important responsibilities we have—our children deserve excellence.
Right to Know Parents’ right to know what’s being taught and to have a real voice in curriculum, materials, and programs.
Right to Choose School choice and multiple pathways: public, charter, private, homeschool, and vocational—based on what works for each child.
VETERANS Veterans kept their promise to this country.
We must keep ours to them.
What’s your plan for veteran housing and community renewal?
I support turning tax-foreclosed and neglected properties that are on county books into opportunities for veterans—paired with structured community service, property rehabilitation, and a path to ownership.
It strengthens neighborhoods while honoring those who served.
Access Overhaul Faster, easier access to benefits and services—especially mental health support and employment transition resources.
Veteran Preference Preference for veteran hiring and local partnerships that restore stability, dignity, and purpose after service.
