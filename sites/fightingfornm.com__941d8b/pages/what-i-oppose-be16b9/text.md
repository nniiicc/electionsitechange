This page is here for one reason: clarity.
You deserve to know what I will not support before you ever cast a vote.
I believe in justice, accountability, and the protection of the innocent—and I believe your representative should be reachable, responsive, and answerable to the people.
I oppose systems that make elections harder to verify and easier to question.
Every lawful voter deserves confidence that elections are secure, legitimate, and protected.
I oppose “everything-in-one” bills that hide unrelated policies, bury accountability, and force representatives into false choices.
One issue, one bill.
I oppose policies that repeatedly release dangerous offenders back into our communities until someone gets hurt.
Public safety must come first.
I oppose broad “sensitive places” restrictions and policies that punish responsible gun owners instead of targeting violent criminals and prohibited possessors.
A suppressor is a safety and hearing-protection tool—not a weapon that can fire a round.
I oppose outdated policy that regulates suppressors like operational firearms.
I oppose security approaches that rely on luck and response time alone.
I support layered school safety, including structured, trained programs coordinated with law enforcement.
I oppose tax policies that choke small businesses and slow growth.
New Mexico should reward work, investment, and job creation—not punish it.
I oppose policies that restrict access, ignore maintenance, and allow forests to become dangerous fuel loads.
Stewardship means management, not neglect.
I oppose any activity that lacks transparency, oversight, and public accountability.
If it’s happening, New Mexicans deserve clear answers and clear rules.
I oppose systems that trap people in red tape, fail to correct eligibility errors, and waste taxpayer money.
Programs should serve those who truly qualify—efficiently and responsibly.
I oppose policies that shut parents out and accept failure as normal.
Our children deserve high standards, real accountability, and multiple education pathways.
I oppose lip service without results.
Veterans deserve real support—housing options, services, and solutions that restore stability and purpose.
I believe there is no worse evil in the world.
These crimes should carry serious, certain consequences, and we must protect children first—always.
Protecting children is non-negotiable.
It’s unacceptable, and it must end.
I oppose officials neglecting their duties, their constituents, and their oath.
Public service is a responsibility, not a title.
I oppose using government for personal gain.
Transparency and ethical standards should be enforced.
I oppose forcing counties and communities to implement programs without the funding to execute them.
I’m running to protect what works, fix what’s broken, and deliver measurable results for NM-40—rooted in justice, accountability, and protecting the innocent.
