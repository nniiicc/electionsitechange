This move comes at a time when gerrymandering has twisted the will of the people into a political weapon, rigging districts so voices get silenced instead of heard.
On top of that, the citizen petition process.
One of the last tools Missourians got to hold power accountable is also under attack, sabotaged to keep everyday folks from shaping their own democracy.
This special session is a reminder that our fight ain’t just about laws, it’s about whether the people of Missouri still have the power to choose their own future.
