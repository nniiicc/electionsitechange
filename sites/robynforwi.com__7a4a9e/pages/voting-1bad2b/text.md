Registration Deadlines
From myvote.wi.org
Deadline to Register by Mail
October 14, 2026 Your voter registration form, along with proof of residence must be postmarked to your municipal clerk no later than October 14, 2026.
Find your Municipal Clerk’s Contact Information here.
Deadline to Register to vote online.
October 14, 2026 Voters who are able to match their name, date of birth, Wisconsin Driver License or State ID number, and address with the Wisconsin DMV can register to vote online through October 14, 2026.
Deadline to Register in Your Municipal Clerk’s Office
October 30, 2026 @ 5:00 p.m.
Voters may register in-person in their municipal clerk’s office during the clerk’s business hours until 5:00 p.m. on October 30, 2026.
Find your Municipal Clerk’s Contact Information here.
Register to Vote at Your Polling Place
November 3, 2026 from 7:00 a.m.-8:00 p.m.
Voters can register to vote at their polling place on Election Day, November 3, 2026.
Find your Polling Place here.
Absentee Voting
From myvote.wi.org
Deadline to Request an Absentee Ballot – Regular and Overseas Voters
October 29, 2026 @ 5:00 p.m.
If you are a regular or a Overseas Voters, your absentee ballot request must be received by your municipal clerk no later than 5:00 p.m. on October 29, 2026.
Deadline to Request an Absentee Ballot – Indefinitely Confined and Military
October 30, 2026 @ 5:00 p.m.
If a voter is indefinitely confined, or military and not away from home, your absentee ballot request must be received by your municipal clerk no later than 5:00 p.m. on October 30, 2026.
In-Person Absentee Voting period
October 20, 2026, possibly through November 1, 2026 Voters can possibly request and vote an absentee ballot in-person in their municipal clerk’s office through November 1, 2026.
Office hours vary by municipality.
Some municipal offices may not offer additional in-person absentee hours Check MyVote or contact your municipal clerk for absentee voting hours.
