This summer, when my son and I were writing postcards for Robyn’s campaign, he naturally had some questions.
While complicated answers about politics swirled through my head, the answers that came out of my mouth were simple.
Because Robyn stands for love, and kids understand love the most.
I told him- Robyn loves people, and she wants them all to have good schools like you do, good doctors like you do, safe neighborhoods like you do, clean water like you do.
Some people don’t have those things.
And that is not ok.
Robyn is working very very hard to try to change that.
I’m voting for Robyn because I really do believe that LOVE WINS.
I am proud of the way she leads with love and has tenaciously represented the values of our community.
I can’t wait to see what she accomplishes in her next term. -Kelly
