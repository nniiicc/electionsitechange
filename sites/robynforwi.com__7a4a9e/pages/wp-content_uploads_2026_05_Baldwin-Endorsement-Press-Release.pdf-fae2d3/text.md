Tammy Baldwin Endorses Rep.
Robyn Vining for State Senate WAUWATOSA, WI — Rep.
Robyn Vining is proud to announce that she has received the endorsement of Tammy Baldwin, United States Senator from Wisconsin, in her race for state Senate.
The endorsement demonstrates the two leaders’ commitment to creating solutions on issues important to the entirety of the Badger State, including affordable healthcare, quality public education and support for small businesses–and making life more affordable for Wisconsinites.
Rep.
Robyn Vining and Senator Tammy Baldwin released the following statements: U.S.
Senator Tammy Baldwin: “I am proud to endorse Rep.
Robyn Vining for State Senate,” Senator Tammy Baldwin said.
“Robyn Vining has fought in the Assembly for 8 years for affordable healthcare, quality public education, reproductive freedom, childcare, mental healthcare, small businesses, and much more.
Her love for people, and the policies that serve them, will lead us to flip the most flippable seat in the state Senate.
When we secure the Democratic majority in the legislature, we can build a Wisconsin that works for everyone.” Representative Robyn Vining: “I am honored to receive U.S.
Senator Tammy Baldwin’s endorsement in my race for state Senate.
Whether it’s fighting for access to quality, affordable healthcare; working to support small businesses; or protecting our democracy, I appreciate Senator Baldwin’s leadership, partnership and support to move Wisconsin forward, together!” ### The 5th Senate district includes Wauwatosa, Brookfield, Elm Grove, West Allis, Milwaukee, Waukesha, and Pewaukee
