Roe v.
Wade
After the fall of Roe v.
Wade, I joined my Democratic colleagues to introduce the Restore Roe Act to repeal Wisconsin’s archaic 1849 criminal abortion ban and enshrine the protections of Roe into state law.
My Democratic colleagues and I introduced the Abortion Rights Preservation Act this legislative session.
This bill repeals the 1849 criminal abortion ban and repeals a number of restrictions that prevent access to healthcare and puts women’s lives in danger.
Latest Republican Abortion Bans
Rather than take up the Abortion Rights Preservation Act, Republican lawmakers introduced more abortion bans.
And this is what happens with a Republican majority: Wisconsin women have less access to healthcare.
Mifepristone Access
Mifepristone is a safe and effective medication that has been FDA approved for decades.
A Trump-appointed judge overturned the approval of Mifepristone, an attempt to ban one of two FDA approved abortion medications.
I, alongside legislators from 49 states, wrote a letter in protest of this attack of reproductive freedom.
