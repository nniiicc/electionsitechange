Before moving to the district in 2017, I really only followed national politics.
But this community is different from any other place I’ve lived.
This community is engaged, and I quickly learned about the local politicians.
At that time I had been concerned with gun laws and loved the common sense gun reforms that Robyn was for.
I was excited for what Robyn could do when she was elected in 2018.
Since then so much has changed and priorities have shifted.
And that whole time Robyn has always been at the forefront of the changes we needed.
Calling for equality for all and full support of diversity and inclusion.
Quickly supporting masks and safety measures when the pandemic hit.
She always has her priorities straight. – Trevor
