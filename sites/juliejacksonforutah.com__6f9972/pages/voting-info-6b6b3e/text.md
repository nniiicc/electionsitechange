A thank you from
Julie Jackson
It will be an honor to represent you in the Utah State Legislature.
Thank you for participating in Utah’s primary election and the democratic process.
Thank you to my opponent, Erin, for running a positive and respectful campaign.
She has a strong record of working on important issues, and I look forward to opportunities to work together in serving our community.
I’m deeply grateful to the hundreds of people who contributed to the success of this campaign.
Your support has meant so much to me.
You showed me once again what is possible when people come together around a shared purpose.
When each of us helps, even in small ways, it can add up to something great.
I ran because I believe we can solve Utah’s toughest challenges when we listen to each other, work together, and invest wisely in our future.
I am a product of my community’s investment in me, and I’m honored by the trust you have given me.
Together, we can do real good for our community and for the state of Utah.
