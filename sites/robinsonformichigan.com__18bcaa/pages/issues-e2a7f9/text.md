Fixing the Roads While Protecting your Wallet _________________________________ Cleaning Up the Red Run River: Representative Robinson is committed to tackling the long-standing problems of the Red Run River, from erosion and odor to repeated flooding that has burdened families across the district.
He is working closely with EGLE and Macomb County Public Works to develop real solutions that protect neighborhoods, safeguard property, and improve water quality.
By holding Oakland County accountable for its role and championing stronger protections, Robinson is working to ensure that residents finally see some positive results.
Under the FY26, Ron also secured funding for the construction of a new odor control facility to address persistent sanitary sewer odor along the 15 Mile Road and ITC corridors, while also helping preserve critical sewer infrastructure, finally providing relief for an issue that has been ongoing for decades.
