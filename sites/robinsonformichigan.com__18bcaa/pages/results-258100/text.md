Wins for Veterans and Police Rep.
Robinson is also proud to deliver real results for those who’ve served our country.
As Chair of the Veterans Subcommittee on Appropriations, he’s helped secure major investments to support Michigan’s veterans and first responders, including $4 million for a grant program to renovate VFW halls, American Legions, and other veteran nonprofits, and $250,000 for Helmets to Hardhats to help veterans transition into skilled trades careers.
He also backed funding for the National Guard Benefit Fund to assist guardsmen with tuition, childcare, and healthcare, and delivered $250,000 in new police equipment for the Utica Police Department.
