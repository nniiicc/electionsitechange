Rooted in Community, Ready to Lead
As the grandson of Italian immigrants, I learned early that community is everything.
Since moving to Trumbull in 2005, I have lived that value — coaching Rec Basketball, leading Odyssey of the Mind, and serving as president of the Nichols Improvement Association.
Elected to the Town Council in 2015, I have spent five terms delivering real results: major infrastructure upgrades to parks and roads, expanded athletic fields for youth and schools, restoration of the Police Pension, and maintenance of Trumbull's top-tier AA++ bond rating — all while keeping budgets balanced and fighting for the funding Trumbull families deserve.
For nearly twenty years, I have worked as a social worker at Trumbull High School, supporting hundreds of students and families through some of their most difficult moments.
In 2024, I earned my Doctorate in Social Work — a lifelong goal that reflects my deep commitment to education, service, and the well-being of this community.
That same commitment now drives my campaign for State Representative: to bring Trumbull's values to Hartford and make a lasting, positive impact for every resident I serve.
My Priorities
- Fully Fund Public Education & Reduce the Local Tax Burden — Increase state investment in K–12 so towns aren't forced to over-rely on property taxes.
Fix the Education Cost Sharing formula to be fairer for suburban communities, and eliminate unfunded state mandates.
- Improve Affordability & Grow the Tax Base — Deliver property tax relief for middle-income homeowners and seniors, encourage smart economic development (small business growth, workforce training, redevelopment of vacant properties), and support responsible housing options — growing the grand list rather than raising taxes.
- Support Seniors & Help Residents Age in Place — Expand home modification grants, in-home care access, and caregiver tax credits so seniors can stay in their homes safely and affordably without being priced out of the communities they built.
