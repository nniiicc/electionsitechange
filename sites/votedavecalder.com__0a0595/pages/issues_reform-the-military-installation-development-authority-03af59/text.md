Dave Calder Will Work to
Pause Data Centers
Utah needs a pause on building new data centers until we develop a comprehensive process to ensure water is conserved and Utahns are not unfairly burdened by energy costs.
Data centers should not be subsidized by tax breaks that shift the load to residents.
I will work to reform the unelected Military Infrastructure and Development Authority (MIDA) which was misused by state leaders to bypass Box Elder County residents for the Stratos Hyperscale Data Center.
MIDA was established to help the Air Force at Hill Air Force Base obtain much needed facilities to replace dilapidated World War II era buildings, but MIDA was later hijacked by state leaders to implement backroom deals with developers well outside the scope of its intended authority.
Legislation is needed to remove MIDA's taxing authority and restrict it to its original mission to support the military.
