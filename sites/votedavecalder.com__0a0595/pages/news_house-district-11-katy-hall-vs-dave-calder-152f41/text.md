PRESS RELEASE
"The Money Looks Nothing Alike"
Corporate PACs make up 90% of Calder Opponent Campaign Funding
Shauna Miller of the Weber County Hive breaks down the funding sources for Hall and Calder in the House District 11 race: "Hall’s campaign has drawn roughly 90 percent of its funding from PACs and committees, with individual donors making up about 10 percent.
Calder’s funding runs almost the opposite direction: about 83 percent from individual donors, 15 percent self-funded or from family, and just 2 percent from party or PAC sources."
