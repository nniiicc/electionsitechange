PRESS RELEASE
Utah Democratic Party Convention 24-25 April
"On April 24 & 25, 2026, delegates from across Utah will gather at Jordan High School in Sandy for the Utah Democratic Party Nominating Convention.
At this convention, delegates will nominate Democratic candidates for federal and multi-county offices, including congressional races.
This is a critical step in the election process and ensures our candidates are chosen by grassroots Democrats from every corner of the state."
