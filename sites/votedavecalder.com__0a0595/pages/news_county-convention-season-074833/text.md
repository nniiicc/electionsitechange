Calder Speaks at the Davis County Convention
On Saturday, April 11, Dave spoke at the Davis Democratic Party convention.
The convention decided county races and selected delegates to represent county parties at the state convention.
Dave previously spoke at the Weber county convention on April 2.
The Utah State Democratic Party convention will be held on April 25th at the Jordan High School, with a training day the day before on the 24th at the Salt Lake Community College, Taylorsville campus.
