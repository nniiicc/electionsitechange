Dave Calder believes in
Healthcare Access for All
- "About 10 percent of Utahns have Medicaid, which is the lowest rate in the nation." Every Utahn should have quality medical care regardless of employer, medical history or income.
I support protecting Medicaid expansions that cover gaps in care that would be debilitating for citizens and I will pursue legislation that restores ensures eligibility requirements help reach the approximately 55,000 Utahns currently without any insurance.
In the longer term I support pathways that expand Medicare for all.
This is doable and cost effective.
