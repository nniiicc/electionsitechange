Calder Calls on Utah's Congressional Delegation to begin Impeachment and Removal of Donald Trump
In a video released on TikTok on April 7th, Dave Calder stated that Donald Trump's threat to destroy the Iranian people is a war crime under the Geneva convention and the United States Military regulations.
"This is an impeachable offense yet the entire Utah congressional delegation sits on the sidelines.
The congressional delegation needs to exercise their Article I constitutional powers and impeach and remove Donald Trump from the presidency."
