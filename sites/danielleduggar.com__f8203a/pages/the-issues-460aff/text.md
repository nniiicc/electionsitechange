The Issues
Cutting Government Waste And Lowering Taxes
Montgomery has shown a consistent pattern of wasting taxpayer dollars on the pet projects of politicians.
Every year, millions of dollars are spent on budget items that don't reflect the priorities of voters.
Instead of growing government, we should prioritize increasing government efficiency and lowering taxes.
Protecting Children from Woke Policies & Defending Parental Rights
Our schools should teach reading, writing, and arithmetic, not radical ideologies.
I’ve worked with grassroots organizations to fight woke policies and protect kids from unlawful discrimination.
I’ll take that fight to the Capitol.
Parents deserve a say in what their children are taught, and kids deserve a classroom focused on education — not activism.
In Montgomery, I’ll work to restore common sense and keep politics out of our schools.
Strengthening Medical Freedom
No one should lose their job, education, or rights over vaccine status or religious beliefs.
I’ve spent years tracking legislation nationwide—Alabama must lead in protecting informed consent.
Medical freedom and religious liberty are foundational American values, not negotiable privileges.
I’ll fight to pass strong protections so no Alabamian is punished for making personal health decisions or standing by their faith.
Preserving Our 2A Rights
Last session, lawmakers restricted our 2nd Amendment rights (SB 116 & SB 119).
I’ll stand firm against any attempt to infringe on our right to keep and bear arms.
The Constitution is clear, and so am I: the right to self-defense shall not be infringed.
I’ll defend our 2A rights without compromise — because freedom isn’t up for negotiation.
Ensuring Responsible Community Growth & Planning
As our county grows, we need responsible planning that strengthens schools, supports farms, and invests in infrastructure—not just more development for profit’s sake.
Growth should serve our communities, not overwhelm them.
I’ll work to ensure new development respects our rural values, protects natural resources, and actually improves quality of life for local families.
