top of page Home Donations About Danielle Issues Endorsements Resources Social/Media Get Involved Donate about danielle Danielle Duggar is not a career politician and is not afraid to take a stand on issues that matter to you.
As a wife and mother of three children whose husband works as an engineer, she understands what is really important for us here in South Alabama. ​ The Duggars have called Baldwin County home for almost 20 years and grew up in the South with the values that matter most to us.
Their children have received public, private, and home education; thus, Danielle understands the importance of individual freedom in educational choice.
She and her husband, Wesley, have been closely involved in their children’s education and believe that parents should set the agenda for the values taught in school. ​ As a Christian, Danielle is committed to the sanctity of human life for both the unborn and our elderly.
As a constitutionalist, Danielle supports your Second Amendment rights to keep and bear arms and opposes the subtle attempts to restrict law-abiding citizens from protecting themselves and their families in exercising those rights.
Danielle supports honest government and limited government, including limiting the government’s ability to mandate medical procedures, medications, or vaccinations for you or your children.
Moreover, she is opposed to the expansion of government and the increase in taxes that come with it. ​ Danielle works as a Legislative Analyst for a national nonprofit organization and teaches English and Reading ACT prep.
She loves reading and gardening with her family and is an active member of Faith, Family, Fellowship Baptist Church in Spanish Fort.
Danielle also has a Master of Arts degree in American History from Norwich University.
Because of her education and understanding of our nation’s history, she has a deep appreciation for what makes Alabama a great state to live in and raise a family.
The Biblical principles she holds shape her worldview and her opinion of the powers that government should and should not have.
A vote for Danielle is a vote for freedom, liberty, and true conservatism.
