He is a Republican fighter with conservative principles who fights for families, calls out corruption, promotes the economy as well as the future of our region and defends the Constitution.
A DEVOTED PUBLIC SERVANT Soon after graduating from the University of Albany, Mark began a career in public service with a focus on statewide policy and desire to improve life for residents of Upstate New York.
Mark joined the staff of state Senator Patty Ritchie in 2011 where his passion for constituent services first emerged.
In 2015, Mark was elected to the Watertown City Council, a nonpartisan council.
Mark was elected to the NYS Assembly in 2018.
While serving the people of the NYS 116th Assembly District, which he referred to as the "Front yard of America", he helped to cultivate an environment for job creation and committed to investing in the aging infrastructure while protecting our precious rivers and lakes.
He remains a strong proponent of policy that empowers teachers and gives students the opportunity to be successful in job preparation training at the secondary and collegiate levels. ​ In January of 2023 he began his journey as Senator of Senate District 49, which includes all of Jefferson, Lewis, Fulton, Hamilton and parts of Herkimer, St.
Lawrence and Oswego counties.
He continued his mission to serve the people of the 49th Senate District with unrelenting passion and to carry a message that builds faith in our republic and inspires hope in New York's future.
Mark is the ranking Republican member on the Elections Committee and on Cities 2.
Mark is a member of the following: Committee on Banks, Committee on Energy and Telecommunications, Committee on Internet and Technology, Committee on Housing Construction and Community Development and Committee on Legislative Commission on Rural Resources.
In addition to his dedication to the Empire State, Mark Walczyk has proudly served in the armed forces as a commissioned engineer officer in the United States Army Reserve since 2012. ​ Mark Walczyk is a member of the US Army Reserve.
Use of his military rank, job titles, and photographs in uniform does not imply endorsement by the Department of the Army or Department of Defense .
A COMMITTED COMMUNITY MEMBER Mark attends New Life Christian Church.
He and his wife, Jessica, live in Sackets Harbor with their sons Atticus and Byron, their daughter Willetta and their dog Alfred. ​ He is a member of the Watertown noontime Rotary Club, the NRA, American Legion Post 61, the Italian American Civic Association, Friends of the MAC Nature Center, the Frederic Remington Art Museum, and a number of other organizations.
NEW YORK STATE'S 49th Senate District Established in 2022, the 49th New York State Senate District is geographically the largest Senate District in the State.
The district stretches from the magnificent St.
Lawrence River on the United States-Canada border through the Adirondacks, and into the Mohawk Valley .
The 49th New York State Senate District consists of: all of Fulton, Hamilton, Jefferson, Lewis, and parts of Herkimer, Oswego, and St.
