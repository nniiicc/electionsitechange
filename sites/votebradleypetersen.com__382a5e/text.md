Hardworking, faithful, and proud of our rural heritage.
Bradley Petersen is running to protect the freedoms that make our state strong and to ensure Wisconsin remains a place where families can farm, build businesses, worship freely, and raise their children according to their values.
Bradley Petersen is a lifelong Kewaskum-area resident and proud conservative Republican running for Wisconsin's 59th Assembly District.
Raised by a single mother on a family dairy farm, he learned the values of faith, hard work, and personal responsibility from an early age.
Those values have never left him.
Having grown up in agriculture, Bradley knows firsthand that farming is the backbone of our communities.
He is committed to defending family farms, protecting property rights, and pushing back against the burdensome regulations hurting rural Wisconsin.
Bradley serves on the Kewaskum School Board as its Policy Chairman and Vice-President, where he fights for parental rights, academic excellence, and keeping political agendas out of the classroom.
As Chair of the Town of Scott Planning Commission, he has been a consistent advocate for limited government and local control, while working to reduce the overregulation that burdens farmers and small business owners.
Bradley is a small property owner who feels the weight of every tax increase.
Grounded in his faith as an elder at Immanuel Lutheran Church, he is unapologetically pro-life with no exceptions, unwavering on the Second Amendment, and fully committed to backing our law enforcement officers.
Bradley Petersen is not a career politician.
He is one of us, and he is running to cut taxes, defend our freedoms, and fight every single day for the families in Washington, Ozaukee, Sheboygan, Fond du Lac, and Dodge counties.
