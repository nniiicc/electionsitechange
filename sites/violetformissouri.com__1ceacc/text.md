top of page MEET TERRI VIOLET ​ As an effective Legislator, Terri Violet successfully sponsored HB 1268, HB 1826 and HB 1827.
Terri is a United States Navy veteran and a retired Alderman for the City of St.
Peters, MO.
As a founding member of the Mid America Veterans Museum and a former member of the St.
Peter's Veterans Memorial Commission, Terri is well-known for her advocacy for those who selflessly served in the US Military.
Terri Violet is a proud wife, mother and grandmother. ​ With a conviction for conservative values, Violet is committed to defending the First and Second Amendments, protecting unborn life, and ensuring a quality education for all students.
She knows that families and local businesses are struggling from a turbulent economy and will fight to keep taxes low.
Violet is also determined to bring greater support for veterans across the state.
Violet will appear on the 2026 ballot for House of Representatives, District 104 in August.
