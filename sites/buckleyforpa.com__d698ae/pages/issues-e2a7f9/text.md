The Issues
Protecting Taxpayers
Kathy Buckley will be a fiscally conservative leader and will focus on controlling spending, cutting government waste, and preventing tax increases.
She will oppose any proposed tax increases and fight tirelessly to eliminate unfunded mandates.
Make Healthcare Accessible & Affordable
Hospital closures and a lack of local doctors are making life too hard for too many.
Kathy will work to attract new providers to our community, support innovation that improves access, and increase competition to reduce costs.
Supporting Law Enforcement
Law Enforcement officers put their lives on the line every day to make our communities, neighborhoods, and families safer.
Kathy Buckley supports fully funding our police and opposes any effort by the radical left to defund the police.
Job Creation
Our ability to pursue the American Dream in Pennsylvania is vanishing.
High taxes, unfunded mandates, and job-crushing regulations have hurt the creation of family-sustaining jobs.
Kathy Buckley will support all pro-growth policies that help to create a job-friendly environment that helps job creators.
Protecting Senior Citizens
Kathy prioritizes seniors' welfare by advocating for sustainable Social Security and Medicare benefits, expanding healthcare access, and supporting financial assistance programs.
She emphasizes pension protection, elder abuse prevention, age-friendly housing, transportation services, and nutrition programs, ensuring seniors' well-being and quality of life are safeguarded.
Making Schools Stronger
Kathy understands the vital role of education in shaping children's future success.
As Representative, she pledges to improve education daily.
Voters can trust Kathy to boost education funding to unprecedented levels, enhance school safety through increased grants, and support both public and choice education initiatives.
She advocates for reducing standardized testing to prioritize real skills teaching.
More Jobs, Better Pay
Kathy understands the importance of bringing high-paying jobs to DelCo families.
As State Representative, she will champion supporting career and technical education for sought-after skills.
Additionally, she advocates for fostering a responsible energy industry for sustainable jobs.
Kathy also stands up for workers' rights and preserving earned benefits while enhancing Pennsylvania's appeal to job creators for retention and new opportunities.
Safe & Secure Elections
Kathy wants to make sure it’s easy to vote and hard to cheat.
She will ensure the rules are applied fairly to everyone, clean voter rolls to prevent mistakes, and make sure our system is secure.
SANCTUARY COUNTY
I firmly oppose the support for Delaware County, to remain a sanctuary county.
Sanctuary policies undermine the rule of law by providing safe havens for undocumented immigrants, often shielding individuals who have committed crimes from deportation.
Sanctuary policies not only encourage illegal immigration but also burden local law enforcement agencies and strain public resources.
Furthermore, sanctuary jurisdictions risk jeopardizing public safety by impeding cooperation between local and federal authorities in addressing immigration enforcement.
It is essential to uphold and enforce immigration laws to protect our communities and ensure fairness for legal immigrants who have followed proper procedures.
Therefore, endorsing sanctuary status for Delaware County is contrary to my principles and I will support any legislation to overturn sanctuary status throughout our Commonwealth.
Illegal aliens can negatively impact union workers as well.
One significant concern is the undercutting of wages.
With undocumented immigrants willing to work for lower pay, employers may exploit this situation to drive down wages for all workers, including unionized ones.
Moreover, the presence of undocumented workers can lead to increased competition for jobs, making it harder for union members to secure employment and negotiate fair wages and benefits.
Overall, the employment of illegal aliens can erode the hard-won gains of union workers, perpetuating a cycle of economic insecurity and unfair labor practices.
