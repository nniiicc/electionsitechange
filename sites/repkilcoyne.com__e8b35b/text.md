Meg is the State Representative for the 12th Worcester District in the Massachusetts House of Representatives.
She proudly represents Berlin, Boylston, Clinton, Lancaster, Precinct 4 of Northborough, and Sterling.
Meg is the FIRST female Representative of the 12th Worcester District.
She was first elected in 2020, and is now serving her third term.
Prior to her election, she worked in the State House for 10+ years, centering her professional life around fighting for our towns to help enhance the quality of life for the residents of the District.
