Representative Kilcoyne’s office offers a comprehensive and educational internship program for Undergraduate and Graduate students.

Summer 2025 Internship Application Fall 2024 Intern Savannah Thompson Savvy is a Junior at Tufts University studying International Relations and Cognitive and Brain Sciences.
While not a native Massachusettsan, Savvy grew up outside of DC and was consequently exposed to politics and public policy from a young age.
On campus, she is a Senator for the Class of 2026, a Mock Trial team captain, and a Tufts Boxing Club instructor.
As a student, Savvy is interested in the intersection between domestic and foreign policy.
She is eager to apply her learnings in this internship towards her greater academic and career goals.
When she’s not working or studying, Savvy enjoys Taskmaster YouTube videos, new recipes, and game nights with friends.
Summer 2024 Intern Evie Lockwood Mullaney Evie is a rising Sophomore at Connecticut College pursuing a double major in Government and Psychology while minoring in Hispanic Studies.
Native to Needham, Massachusetts, she is passionate about comprehensive and affordable healthcare, as well as services for our veterans.
On campus, Evie is a member of the Women’s Volleyball Team, the college’s newspaper, and assists in the Child Development Lab School.
Her coursework includes “Human Rights, Media, and Activism”, International Politics, and Social Psychology.
Following her undergraduate degree, Evie would love to work in the State or Federal Government.
Outside of work, she enjoys acapella, hiking, baking, and reading a good book!
She is most looking forward to experiencing the House of Representative’s legislative day-to-day, as well as learning more from a team of young women in politics.
