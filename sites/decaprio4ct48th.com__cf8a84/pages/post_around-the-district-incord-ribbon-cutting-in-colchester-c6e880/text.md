Around the District: InCord Ribbon Cutting in Colchester
When a business expands locally, it is always a good thing!
I was happy to join Colchester First Selectman Bernie Dennler, along with countless members of the Colchester who attended the grand opening of InCord Custom Netting Solution.
The new facility is 30,000 square feet and features office space, a shipping and receiving department, an innovative warehouse, and everything their employees need under one roof.
InCord is a supplier of custom lifting and rigging solutions, nets for playgrounds, construction sites, and sports netting.
It is wonderful to see our local businesses thrive here in eastern Connecticut!
I was happy to attend, and I look forward to celebrating the accomplishments of businesses across our area.
For more on InCord Custom Netting Solutions, you can visit their website at www.incord.com.
