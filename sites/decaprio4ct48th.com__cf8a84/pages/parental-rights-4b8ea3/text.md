MARK DECAPRIO
STATE REPRESENTATIVE—48TH DISTRICT
Parental Rights
I strongly advocate for parental rights, firmly believing that parents, not the government or state, should determine what is best for their minor children.
Parents must always be heard when engaging with the board of education staff or attending school board meetings.
Children are a gift, and parents possess the deepest understanding of their needs and aspirations.
Our son, Sam, exemplifies this belief as he pursues his doctorate in cello music performance, sharing his talents and knowledge with both beginner and advanced students.
