Personal Message from Mark DeCaprio
State Representative, 48th District, seeking re-election
It has been a true privilege to serve the residents of Bozrah, Colchester, Franklin, and Lebanon as your State Representative.
I care deeply about maintaining the unique character of our towns and protecting the local voices that make our communities strong.
That’s why I will continue to support legislation that supports our local values and oppose legislation that interferes with local decision-making or imposes unnecessary mandates on our communities.
Affordability remains one of the most pressing issues facing Connecticut families.
Whether you are raising a family, running a small business, or planning for retirement, the cost-of-living here matters.
I am committed to keeping more of your hard-earned money in your pocket and working toward a Connecticut where people can afford to live—and retire—right here at home.
As I seek re-election, my commitment remains the same: to make Connecticut more affordable, to support initiatives that will lower Connecticut's electric rates, use taxpayer dollars responsibly & wisely, and hold government accountable—while standing firmly against unnecessary government mandates.
My goal is simple: to help individuals wherever I can, work to strengthen our communities, and support a brighter future for our children and grandchildren.
With thoughtful leadership and continued collaboration, I believe Connecticut can move forward in a positive and sustainable way.
I respectfully ask for your support by contributing to my campaign, so we can continue this important work together.
-- Mark DeCaprio, State Representative, 48th House District
