Meet Karlie Easley
Karlie was born in Pocatello, ID, and is a fourth-generation Idahoan.
She has called the Treasure Valley home since she was in middle school, and has called District 22 home for much of her adult life.
Karlie is dedicated to bringing balanced, common-sense leadership to District 22.
She is focused on the everyday lives of Idaho families—prioritizing strong schools, accessible healthcare, and a government that works for everyone.

Real Solutions for Idaho Healthcare
As a nurse who has dedicated her entire career to Idaho’s healthcare system, Karlie understands what our communities need: healthcare that is both accessible and affordable.
Yet instead of focusing on Idaho families, too many legislators are consumed by partisan rhetoric.
The result is real and alarming—physicians are leaving our state at record rates, even as our population continues to grow.
To meet this moment, we must actively partner with organizations that can help us recruit and retain the providers our communities depend on.
Idahoans made their priorities clear when they overwhelmingly voted to expand Medicaid.
Still, some elected officials continue working to undermine that decision.
At the same time, fiscally irresponsible policies have shifted the burden onto everyday Idahoans—forcing families to go without care while prioritizing tax breaks for the wealthiest.
When people lack access to affordable healthcare, the consequences ripple across families, workplaces, and the broader community.
Karlie is committed to cutting through the noise and advancing practical, commonsense solutions.
Idaho needs a healthcare system that works for its people—one that prioritizes patients over politics and delivers real results for the communities it serves.
