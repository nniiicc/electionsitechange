Rep.
Dr.
Jasmine Clark, Candidate for Georgia's 13th Congressional District, Releases Statement on the shooting at Fort Stewart
See Below for a statement from Rep.
Dr Jasmine Clark on the shooting at Fort Stewart:
“I am heartbroken to see that yet another mass shooting has occurred in Georgia.
I am praying for the five soldiers that were injured in this horrible shooting, as well as their families and the entire Fort Stewart community.
I am grateful for the swift actions from law enforcement and first responders who acted swiftly to secure the base and save lives.
Our soldiers and Army personnel have put themselves on the line to do a dangerous and honorable job of defending our homeland.
They deserve the highest level of safety while on their base and out in their community–and today, that didn’t happen.
This kind of violence has no place anywhere in our country .”
