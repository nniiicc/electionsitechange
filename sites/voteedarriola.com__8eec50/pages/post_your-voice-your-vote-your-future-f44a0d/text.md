Your Voice.
Your Vote.
Your Future.
Every election is an opportunity to shape the future of the Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands.
Whether you're voting for the first time or have participated in every election, one of the most important things you can do is make sure you're registered and that your voter information is up to date.
If you're eligible to vote but haven't registered yet, don't wait until the last minute.
Register early so you're ready to make your voice heard on Election Day.
Already Registered?
Take a few minutes to:
- Confirm that you are registered to vote.
- Verify that your name and address are correct.
- Update your registration if you've moved, changed your name, or need to update your voter information.
First-Time Voter?
Voting is one of the most meaningful ways to participate in our democracy.
If you're eligible to vote, registering is your first step toward having a say in the future of our islands, our economy, our schools, and our community.
Where Can I Register or Learn More?
The Commonwealth Election Commission provides official voter registration forms, election information, polling locations, and important deadlines.
Visit the official Commonwealth Election Commission website:www.votecnmi.gov.mp
There you'll find:
- Voter registration forms
- Election announcements and public notices
- Candidate information
- Polling place information
- Important election dates and deadlines
Contact edarriolacnmi@gmail.com if you need assistance registering or have any questions.
No matter who you support, participating in our elections makes our Commonwealth stronger.
Encourage your family, friends, and neighbors to register, verify their information, and vote.
