Today, I serve as Vice President of Data Analytics for Micronesia Benefit Administrators, where I work with business leaders to improve healthcare programs, financial performance, and operational efficiency.
Throughout my career, I've helped organizations make informed decisions by analyzing complex data, improving operations, negotiating with partners, and developing long-term strategies.
Those same skills are exactly what our government needs today.
As your representative, I will focus on: Restoring trust and accountability in government.
Growing our economy by supporting local businesses and attracting new investment.
Improving disaster preparedness and speeding recovery after storms.
Fighting for better infrastructure, reliable utilities, and stronger public services.
Creating more opportunities so our children can build successful futures here in the CNMI.
I believe public service is about listening, being accessible, and making decisions based on facts—not politics.
My commitment is simple: work hard, be transparent, and always put the people of Precinct 3 first.
My Priorities Jobs and Economic Growth Growing our economy starts with supporting entrepreneurs, attracting investment, expanding private-sector employment, and creating opportunities that allow our young people to build their future here instead of leaving the islands.
Roads & Infrastructure Our infrastructure should reflect our pride.
I'll advocate for better road maintenance, reliable utilities, safer neighborhoods, and smarter long-term investments that strengthen our communities.
Revive Tourism I'll work to strengthen partnerships between government and the private sector, improve visitor experiences, expand marketing efforts, and support new tourism opportunities that benefit local businesses.
Disaster Readiness Typhoon Sinlaku reminded us how vulnerable we are.
We need stronger infrastructure, improved emergency planning, better communication, resilient utilities, and faster recovery systems that put families first.
Support Local Business Small businesses create jobs.
Government should simplify permits, reduce unnecessary bureaucracy, encourage entrepreneurship, and ensure local businesses have opportunities to compete and grow.
Honest Leadership Government belongs to the people.
I believe in transparency, accountability, responsible spending , and making decisions based on facts—not politics.
