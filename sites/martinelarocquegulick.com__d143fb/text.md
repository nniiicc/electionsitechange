Martine Larocque Gulick
Vermont State Senator
I am proud to be a two-term Vermont State Senator serving on the Senate Finance Committee and as Vice-Chair of Senate Health and Welfare.
I have served as Co-Chair of the School District Redistricting Task Force and the State Aid for School Construction Working Group and I am a member of the Judicial Retention Committee and the Green Mountain Care Board Nominating Committee.
I am also a legislative appointee serving on the Building Bright Futures Advisory Council and the Vermont Citizen’s Advisory Committee on Lake Champlain’s Future.
Prior to serving in the Senate, I was a high school teacher who taught French and English and served as Library Director at Essex High School/Center for Technology Essex.
I also served on the Burlington School Board for eight years.
I am proud to represent the Chittenden Central District which includes Burlington, Winooski, The City of Essex Junction, Essex Town and Colchester.
I was born in Japan where my father served in the Air Force and have lived in Canada, France, Germany and the Czech Republic.
My family moved to Vermont in 1972 and I have lived in Burlington’s New North End for 30 years.
