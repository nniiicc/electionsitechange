The session may have ended, but there is still so much to learn about the legislation we supported this session!
Join us on June 30th from 6 – 6:45 PM as we unpack the end of the legislative session and look ahead at the work happening across the state in the lead-up to the midterm elections.
This month, we are highlighting our work to ban Paraquat, a pesticide that is heavily linked to Parkinson’s disease.
This session, the legislature passed a bill into law that will ban its use in Vermont.
We’ll be joined by Senator Martine Gulick and VPIRG Executive Director Paul Burns to discuss this critical legislation, its enactment, and how Vermonters will benefit from this ban.
We’ll also be discussing upcoming opportunities to engage with your local and statewide communities, and how you can continue to have an impact on legislation when the Legislature is no longer in session.
