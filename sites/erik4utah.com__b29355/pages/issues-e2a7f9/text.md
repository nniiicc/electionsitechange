Local Voices.
Local Decisions.
I’ve served our community on the West Point City Council and as Mayor.
That experience shaped my belief that the best decisions are made closest to the people they affect.
Too often, state government forgets that Utah is not one community—it’s many.
Clinton, West Point, Clearfield, and Sunset each have different needs, different challenges, and different priorities.
My job as a state representative is not to dictate from the Capitol, but to carry local voices to it.
