If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the effective date at the top of the policy and, in some cases, we may provide additional notice, such as adding a statement to our homepage or sending an email notification.
We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy whenever you access the Site to stay informed about our information practices and the ways you can help protect your privacy.
COLLECTION OF INFORMATION

We collect information you provide directly to us.
For example, we may collect information when you make a donation, request information, fill out a form, send us an email, sign up to receive updates, register for an event, apply for or inquire about a job or internship, make a purchase, or otherwise communicate with us.

When you access or use our Site, we automatically collect information about you, including log information, device information, and data collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve the Site and measure campaign effectiveness.

We may use information about you to provide and improve the Site, process donations and event registrations, communicate campaign updates, solicit support, analyze engagement, and carry out any other purpose for which the information was collected.

By using the Site, you consent to the processing and transfer of information within the United States.
