As a lifelong Berks County resident, educator, and community advocate, Andy understands the everyday challenges facing working families, seniors, and small businesses because he has lived them alongside his neighbors.
His priorities are shaped by listening to residents, showing up in the community, and delivering practical solutions that protect our quality of life.
This platform reflects a commitment to responsible leadership, accountability, and putting people over politics - focused on real results, not rhetoric.
Education: Rebuilding Through Schools Our schools are more than classrooms—they're community hubs.
I believe that investing in education is investing in our future.
Modernize School Infrastructure – Build Pride, Reduce Taxes I’ll advocate for funding to renovate aging school buildings across our school districts.
Pride in where our students learn needs to be a priority again.
Assisting our schools with projects reduces local tax burdens and promotes pride, making it a desirable place for future homeowners to live and kids to learn.
Future-Focused Job Training I’ll promote vocational school training with increased time spent on trades in public schools.
I’ll partner with businesses to ensure student readiness and job opportunities.
Provide students with the opportunities to graduate high school with micro credentials; kids can use for real jobs.
Eliminate State Standardized Testing State testing has hindered student preparedness for real-world applications.
We’ve seen enough!
It’s time to say goodbye to standardized testing and instead earn certificates that demonstrate competency for jobs and higher education.
Community-Centered Food Access I’ll partner with local farms to provide fresh food to families in need, reducing taxpayer burden and promoting our local farms.
Fair Funding for All Schools I’ll fight for equitable state education funding to ensure every child has the resources to succeed.
Restructure School Format I’ll explore year-round schooling.
Our kids are falling behind other countries across the globe.
They need more time in education to catch up—and in time, lead!
Fiscal Responsibility & Energy Independence Pennsylvania’s natural resources can fuel our economy—responsibly and sustainably.
We’re a leader in energy production, and we can do even more.
With increased energy production and upgrades, we’ll keep prices lower.
Maximize Responsible Energy Production (REPs) I’ll support balanced development of timber, natural gas, oil, coal, solar, and hydro.
MAX REPs!
I’ll back policies that reduce energy costs and diversify our energy portfolio.
Fair Tax Code I’ll advocate for proportional taxation that eases the burden on working families and the elderly.
Secure Independent and Reliable Energy Energy demands are skyrocketing.
I’ll invest in our century-old power grid and promote public school education for training in the energy trades.
I’ll develop apprenticeships that partner with energy companies.
Timely, Transparent Budgets I’ll push for on-time state budgets that prioritize community needs.
Reward and Recruit through Lower Income Taxes I’ll propose a 1% reduction in State Income Tax for First Responders to help recruit and retain these vital professionals.
Reduce Waste in Harrisburg It’s time for common-sense fiscal stewardship.
It’s your money—not Harrisburg’s.
Eliminate State Emission Tax on New Car Purchases Emission testing on brand-new vehicles is a tax.
Over 96% of new cars pass emissions.
Manufacturers can do better without consumers paying a tax on emissions.
Fighting for Our Municipalities I’m committed to keeping water and electricity utility prices down.
I will work tirelessly to ensure fair rates, prevent unnecessary hikes, and protect families from rising costs.
Affordable utilities mean stronger communities!
Public Safety & First Responders Our first responders are the backbone of public safety.
My plan honors their service and strengthens their support.
Tax Relief for First Responders I’ll propose income tax reductions for volunteer and paid responders to reward and attract new people.
Let’s give those who protect and help us a break.
Education Incentives I’ll expand tax credits for volunteers pursuing higher education.
Funding for Equipment & Training I’ll ensure rural and underserved departments have the tools they need.
Local Tax Credits I’ll promote real estate tax incentives for active volunteers to encourage retention.
Community Growth District 128 is rich in heritage, talent, and opportunity.
My vision is to elevate our region through smart, inclusive growth.
Infrastructure Investment I’ll improve roads, bridges, and public transit to connect communities.
Support Local Business & Agriculture I’ll empower small businesses and farms that feed and employ our neighbors.
Sustainable Development I’ll plan for growth that respects our rural character and prepares for the future.
Champion for ALL I’ll ensure every voice is heard and valued in Harrisburg.
