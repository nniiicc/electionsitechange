Healthcare allows each of us to live our best lives.
Each individual, community, and state should have the care they deserve to prevent diseases and respond to public health emergencies.
Arizonans, Republicans and Democrats, for decades have supported this premise through the expansion of Medicaid.
More than two million Arizonans receive healthcare through Arizona’s Medicaid.
Read More Water Conservation Water is critical to life here in the desert.
With the flow of the Colorado River declining, it is essential that we develop a long-term plan to secure our water supply at the state level.
In the face of threats to our water supply, corporations are building massive data centers across Arizona that are draining our precious water resources, as well as driving up our energy costs.
I am committed to protecting Arizona’s water future for generations to come, which means ensuring communities are consulted before new data center projects are built.
We should manage our own water and power needs before we approve new data centers, and we should not have to pay higher water and electricity bills to subsidize these projects.
As your state legislator, I will answer to Arizonan families, not utility companies and Big Tech shareholders.
Public Education Public education is the foundation of American democracy.
Education not only creates dreams.
It makes them possible.
I am outraged by the decades-long assault on public education in Arizona, and I am committed to putting an end to it.
Before I moved to Arizona, I spearheaded the Read California campaign to promote reading among youth.
Read More Democracy America was founded on the self-evident truth that all people are created equal.
These values are aspirational.
We can only achieve them if we promote, protect, and defend civil rights for every citizen and resident in the nation.
To enshrine equal justice under the law, everyone must have the opportunity to participate in our democracy.
Dangerously, in a cynical attempt to strip power from the people, current MAGA-Republicans have undermined trust in free and fair elections.

We must secure our border and respect the humanity and civil liberties of immigrants.
When I have had the opportunity to help new citizens register to vote I am always humbled by those who choose American citizenship with pride and stake their future and the future of their family with the generations of immigrants that preceded them.
