This policy explains how we collect and use information through www.deborahhowardaz.com and our campaign communications.
Information We Collect We collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, mailing address, telephone number, and the contents of messages or forms you submit.
When you sign up for text messages, we also collect your opt-in preference and consent record.
Our website may collect technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, through cookies or similar technologies.
How We Use Information We use information to respond to inquiries, fulfill requests, coordinate volunteers and events, provide campaign updates, request contributions, and maintain our website.
We also use information to manage communication preferences and meet applicable legal obligations.
Telephone Calls and Text Messages Providing a telephone number is optional.
You may submit our contact form without providing a telephone number or consenting to calls or text messages.
By providing your telephone number and checking the optional consent checkbox, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Deborah Howard for State Representative – District 27.
Messages may include campaign updates, event information, volunteer opportunities, and requests for donations.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt out of text messages.
Reply HELP for help.
You may also contact us through our contact page for assistance or to request that we stop calling you.
Consent is not a condition of making a donation or purchase.
Text Messaging Privacy Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with or sold to any third parties, except as required by law.
We will not share your mobile telephone number with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
Other Information Sharing For information other than the protected text messaging information described above, we may work with service providers to host our website, process contributions, manage campaign records, and perform other campaign services.
These providers may process information needed to perform those services on our behalf.
We may disclose information when required by law, including applicable campaign finance reporting requirements.
These disclosures may make certain contributor information publicly available.
Cookies and Third Party Websites Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to support website functions and understand website usage.
You can manage cookies through your browser settings.
Our website links to third-party services, including donation platforms.
Information you submit directly to those services is governed by their privacy policies.
Please review those policies before providing information.
Your Choices You may request an update or correction to information you have provided or ask us to stop sending campaign communications by using our contact page.
You may unsubscribe from campaign emails using the unsubscribe link included in those emails.
For text messages, reply STOP.
We may retain information needed to comply with legal obligations or honor your opt-out request.
Changes to This Policy We may update this policy.
Any revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
