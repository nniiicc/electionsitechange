Tim Hanigan is a South Dakotan, engineer, and business leader who believes strong communities are built through hard work, local control, and personal responsibility.
Tim currently serves as CEO of the Aberdeen Development Corporation, where he works daily to attract new businesses, support local employers, and strengthen Aberdeen's long-term economic future.
He is also the owner of Prairie Tool Company, a precision manufacturing business founded in 1989 that serves customers across the medical, agricultural, consumer, and industrial sectors.
Tim understands firsthand what it takes to meet payroll, invest in growth, and compete in a global economy.
Before his time at the ADC and Prairie Tool, Tim spent more than a decade with Banner Engineering in manufacturing and operations leadership roles, overseeing large teams, managing multi-plant operations, and driving improvements in safety, quality, and customer service.
His background as a mechanical engineer and operations executive shapes his practical, results-driven approach to problem-solving.
Deeply committed to community service, Tim has served on numerous local and regional boards, including as Chair of the Aberdeen Development Corporation Board, President of the SD Manufacturing & Technology Solutions Advisory Board, Vice President of the Aberdeen Hardball Association, and a member of the Sanford Health Advisory Board.
He also founded the Aberdeen Sharks youth baseball organization, helping expand opportunities for young athletes.
Tim earned a Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering from South Dakota State University and an MBA in Operations and Supply Chain Management from Southern New Hampshire University.
He and his family call Aberdeen home, where he is passionate about keeping South Dakota a place where families can thrive, businesses can grow, and decisions are made close to home.
