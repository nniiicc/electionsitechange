On The Issues Stephanie Gricius is a conservative Republican leader who fights for strong infrastructure, responsible growth, public safety, and more so families can thrive in safe, well connected neighborhoods.
She champions fiscal responsibility, demands limited government, and enforces taxpayer accountability to keep government working for the people of Utah.
Stephanie defends freedom, safeguards Utah County values, and works to preserve Utah’s natural beauty while driving economic opportunities for families and small businesses.
Above all, she listens, responds, and delivers results for the people of District 50.
