Why I’m running for re-election
There is more work to be done.
Since beginning my service in January 2023, I have helped secure 2.1 BILLION dollars in transportation funding for the district, cut taxes, passed common sense, first in the nation legislation for medical freedom, protected individuals and families, passed occupational licensing reform, and so much more.
I look forward to continuing to serve you!
On The Issues
Stephanie Gricius is a conservative Republican leader who fights for strong infrastructure, responsible growth, public safety, and more so families can thrive in safe, well connected neighborhoods.
She champions fiscal responsibility, demands limited government, and enforces taxpayer accountability to keep government working for the people of Utah.
Stephanie defends freedom, safeguards Utah County values, and works to preserve Utah’s natural beauty while driving economic opportunities for families and small businesses.
Above all, she listens, responds, and delivers results for the people of District 50.
