FABER ANNOUNCES FOR ATTORNEY GENERAL
CELINA, OH [Jan 27, 2025]—In a video released today, Keith Faber announced his candidacy to be Ohio’s next Attorney General.
In it, he pledged to protect Ohio families and defend our Constitution.
Faber said his Attorney General’s office will focus on public safety and will actively support Ohio police and prosecutors.
