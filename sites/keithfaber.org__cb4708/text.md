Faber said his Attorney General’s office will focus on public safety and will actively support Ohio police and prosecutors.
MEET KEITH After more than two decades of private sector experience, public service leadership, and civic engagement Keith Faber was elected Ohio’s 33rd Auditor of State.
Throughout his career, he has been a leader in the effort to move Ohio forward by advocating for a more efficient, effective, and transparent government in Columbus.
Keith has a successful private law practice in his hometown of Celina, where he lives with his wife Andrea and their two children Adam and Brooke.
LEARN MORE THE RECORD Keith Faber has led a team that has delivered more than 170 convictions on 384 separate criminal charges against corrupt public officials.
His team has issued 291 findings for recovery, totaling more than $29 million in taxpayer dollars.
In addition, his team discovered approximately $6.9 billion in fraud and overpayments within Ohio’s unemployment compensation system.
