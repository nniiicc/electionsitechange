THE VALUE OF HARD WORK His career is the compilation of public service, private sector experience, and civic leadership.
As a former President of the Ohio Senate, he championed fiscal responsibility by reducing state spending, cutting taxes, and increasing government transparency.
Now serving as Ohio’s 33rd Auditor of State, Keith continues to protect taxpayer dollars by holding state and local governments accountable and ensuring efficiency and transparency at every level.
MOVING OHIO FORWARD Keith is a steadfast defender of the Ohio Constitution and the freedoms it guarantees.
He has fought to protect the sanctity of life, uphold religious liberty, and preserve the Second Amendment rights of Ohioans.
His commitment to protecting families extends to safeguarding neighborhoods from crime, tackling the drug epidemic, and supporting law enforcement.

In his bid for Ohio Attorney General, Keith is focused on protecting families, defending constitutional rights, and ensuring that every Ohioan has the opportunity to thrive in a safe community.
