Keith will be a strong advocate for Ohio consumers by protecting them from fraud, scams, and unfair business practices.
He will increase enforcement against predatory companies and cybercriminals that target vulnerable Ohioans, especially seniors who face financial exploitation.
He will hold businesses accountable for deceptive practices, promote transparency, enforce strict penalties on bad actors, and educate the public about their rights.
His vision will create a fair and safe marketplace where families and small businesses can thrive.
