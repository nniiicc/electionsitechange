Privacy Policy
At Brian Rasel for Pennsylvania House of Representatives, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the safety of any personal information you provide to us.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you visit our website, donate, or engage with our campaign.
By using our website, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.
1.
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information from you when you:
- Sign up for email updates
- Make a donation
- Volunteer for the campaign
- Complete a survey or poll
- Engage with us on social media
- Use our website and associated services
- The types of personal information we collect may include:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Payment information (for donations)
- IP address and browser information (collected automatically)
2.
How We Use Your Information
We use your personal information for the following purposes:
- To communicate with you about campaign news, events, and updates
- To process your donations and comply with campaign finance laws
- To respond to your inquiries or requests
- To engage volunteers and support campaign activities
- To analyze website traffic and improve our services
- To target advertisements and outreach through digital platforms
- We will not sell or rent your personal information to any third parties.
3.
Sharing Your Information
We may share your personal information with trusted third parties to help run the campaign effectively.
These may include:
- Payment processors for secure donation processing
- Email service providers for communication
- Data analytics services to understand website usage
- Advertising platforms for outreach and marketing efforts
- Vendors and consultants assisting in campaign operations
- We ensure that any third parties involved are committed to protecting your privacy and use your data only for the purposes described in this policy.
4.
Donor Information
In accordance with federal and state campaign finance laws, we are required to
report certain donor information.
Donations over a certain amount may be publicly
disclosed, including the donor’s name, address, and employer.
We take appropriate
measures to protect your payment information, and all transactions are processed
securely.
5.
Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Access the personal information we have collected from you
- Request corrections to your personal information
- Opt out of campaign communications at any time
- Request the deletion of your personal information, subject to our legal obligations
- To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.
6.
Data Retention
We will retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the
purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law.
Donor information may be
retained for compliance with campaign finance reporting requirements.
7.
Security of Your Information
We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information
from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
However, please
note that no data transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot
guarantee the absolute security of your data.
8.
Cookies and Tracking
Our website uses cookies and other tracking technologies to enhance your
experience, analyze website traffic, and deliver personalized content.
You may
adjust your browser settings to decline cookies, but this may affect the
functionality of certain features of the website.
9.
Children’s Privacy
Our website and campaign services are not intended for children under the age of
13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
If we become aware
that we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, we will
take steps to delete the information as soon as possible.
10.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on
this page, and we will notify you of significant changes via email or website
notification.
11.
International Data Transfers
If you are accessing our website from outside the United States, please note that
your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data
protection laws may differ from those in your country.
12.
Contact Us

rights concerning your personal information, please contact us at
