Gallivan represents the 59 th Senate District, which includes all or portions of Erie, Wyoming, Livingston and Monroe counties.
He was first elected to the Senate in November 2010 and overwhelmingly re-elected in 2012, 2014, 2016 and 2018.
He serves as Assistant Minority Leader for Floor Operations and is the Ranking Member of the Senate Committee of Health.
He is also a member of the committees on Crime Victims, Crime and Correction; Transportation, Labor and Codes.
