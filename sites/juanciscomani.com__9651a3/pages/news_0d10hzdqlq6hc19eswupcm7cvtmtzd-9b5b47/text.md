New Ad: “Effective” Highlights Ciscomani’s Record of Delivering Results for Arizona
Sahuarita Mayor Tom Murphy Highlights Ciscomani’s Record as Arizona’s Most Effective Member of Congress
TUCSON - Ciscomani for Congress launched a new television ad, “Effective”, featuring Sahuarita Mayor Tom Murphy and highlighting Congressman Juan Ciscomani’s proven record of delivering results for Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District.
Ciscomani has been ranked Arizona’s most effective member of Congress and most bipartisan member, building a record of working across the aisle and turning Arizona priorities into real results.
First by the Center for Effective Lawmaking and most recently by Bridge Grades.
As a result of focusing on results and delivering for his district, Ciscomani tops both rankings for Arizona in both the House and the Senate.
“Juan Ciscomani doesn’t just talk about getting things done — he delivers,” said Sahuarita Mayor Tom Murphy.
“He’s been ranked the most effective member of Congress from Arizona twice, and we see that effectiveness right here at home.
Juan has delivered tax cuts for seniors and working families and record funding for critical local projects that strengthen our communities.
That’s the kind of leadership Southern Arizona needs.”
Since taking office, Ciscomani has focused on delivering tangible results for the people he represents:
- Tax relief for seniors and hardworking Arizonans
- No tax on tips
- Over $60 million in federal investments for critical infrastructure, public safety, water, transportation, and other community priorities across AZ-06
- Increased funding for Border Patrol
“Results matter,” said Congressman Juan Ciscomani.
“I promised to work with anyone to deliver for our district, and that’s exactly what I’ve done.
From cutting taxes for seniors and working people to securing record investments for our communities, I’ll keep fighting every day to make sure Southern Arizona has a strong and effective voice in Congress.”
“Effective” will air across Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District.
