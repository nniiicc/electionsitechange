NEW AD: “IT’S PERSONAL” CISCOMANI FIGHTS BACK AGAINST MENDOZA’S DANGEROUS AGENDA
TUCSON - Congressman Juan Ciscomani’s campaign today released a new 30-second ad titled “It’s Personal,” drawing a sharp contrast between Ciscomani’s commonsense record and JoAnna Mendoza’s extreme agenda.
As the son of immigrants who came to America in search of freedom, opportunity, and safety, Ciscomani knows those values cannot be taken for granted, but JoAnna Mendoza’s agenda puts them at risk.
At a time when Arizona families are already struggling to make ends meet, Mendoza supports higher taxes that would make life even less affordable and backs the dangerous push to defund the police.
These policies threaten the prosperity and safety of families, small businesses, and communities across Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District.
“JoAnna Mendoza’s agenda would make life harder, less affordable, and less safe for the people of AZ-06,” said Congressman Juan Ciscomani.
“She is pushing an ideology that is completely out of touch with our district and lacks any common sense.
My family came to America in search of freedom, opportunity, and safety.
Mendoza’s extreme agenda threatens all three, and that makes this race personal.
I will continue rejecting the extremes on both sides of the aisle and serving as a common sense voice for Southeastern Arizona in Washington.”
Ciscomani has spent the past three years delivering bipartisan results for AZ-06, earning recognition as one of the most effective members of Congress.
Rejecting the extremes on both sides isn't just good policy– it's common sense.
And it's how Juan Ciscomani will protect the freedom, opportunity, and safety that define the American Dream, so future generations in AZ-06 can experience it just like Juan's family did.
