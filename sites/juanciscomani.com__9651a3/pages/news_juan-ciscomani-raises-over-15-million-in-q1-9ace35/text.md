Juan Ciscomani Raises Over $1.5 Million in Q1
Ciscomani Heads Into 2026 Cycle With Large War Chest
This fundraising haul puts Congressman Ciscomani in a strong position to defend Arizona’s 6th congressional district in 2026.
Congressman Ciscomani’s first quarter fundraising comes from resources raised directly into Ciscomani for Congress, Defending the American Dream PAC (Ciscomani’s leadership PAC), and Ciscomani Victory Fund.
“I am humbled and honored to the thousands who have donated to our campaign and believe in our team,” said Ciscomani.
“This sign of strength shows the confidence our district and state has in our mission and in what we continue to deliver on for Arizona’s 6th Congressional District.
I remain focused on delivering results on the issues my constituents care about.”
In 2024, Ciscomani defended his congressional district and won with an expanded margin and vote total from his 2022 victory.
He won by 2.5% and 10,822 votes in 2024.
After flipping this district from blue to red in 2022, he won by 1.4% and 5,232 votes.
Ciscomani has deep support throughout his congressional district, spanning portions of 5 Arizona counties.
And Ciscomani was recently ranked the most effective member of Arizona’s congressional delegation in both the U.S.
House and U.S.
Senate throughout the 118th Congress.
He is running for a third term to continue representing Arizona’s 6th congressional district.
