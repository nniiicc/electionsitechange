The endorsement underscores Ciscomani’s effective leadership and relentless advocacy for stronger border security—an issue that remains vital to protecting Arizona communities, securing the nation’s borders, and supporting the men and women on the front lines of law enforcement.
“We've had the pleasure of working with Congressman Ciscomani for years, dating back to his work with Governor Doug Ducey on southern Arizona border operations, long before he ever ran for Congress,” said Hector Andujo, President of the National Border Patrol Council. “ Over the years, Juan has proven that he'll show up, listen, and deliver results.
During the government shutdown, he even withheld his own pay to stand with Border Patrol agents who weren't getting paid through no fault of their own.
That's why we know we can count on Juan to stand with us when it matters most.
We're proud to endorse him for re-election to a third term.” The endorsement comes as Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District remains one of the nation’s most closely watched House races, where border security remains a top issue for voters.
Ciscomani, who immigrated to Tucson from Mexico at age five and became the first naturalized U.S. citizen from Mexico elected to Congress from Arizona, has built a record of working directly with Border Patrol agents to advance practical, on-the-ground solutions that secure the border and support the communities who live with its realities every day.
As Vice Chair of the Homeland Security Appropriations Subcommittee, and the only member of Arizona’s congressional delegation from either party or chamber to serve on the Appropriations Committee, Ciscomani has delivered resources for the agents who patrol Arizona’s Sixth District’s border communities.
He led passage of the Pay Our Homeland Defenders Act to guarantee Border Patrol agents and DHS employees continue receiving pay during government funding lapses, and helped secure additional agents, retention bonuses, and equipment funding through the Working Families Tax Cuts Act.
He has also introduced the FLASH Act to strengthen public safety on federal lands along the southern border, co-introduced bipartisan legislation to establish Joint Operations Centers coordinating federal, state, local, and tribal law enforcement at the border, and cosponsored legislation to combat fentanyl trafficking and strengthen border interdiction efforts.
“Maintaining a secure border is one of my highest responsibilities as the representative of a border district, and it's something I work on every single day I'm in Congress,” said Congressman Juan Ciscomani.
“I'm honored to once again earn the trust and support of the National Border Patrol Council.
This is the third campaign they've had my back, and I don't take that lightly.
I've spent countless hours with Border Patrol agents in the field, from the middle of the night to the hottest part of the day, listening to what they face and what they need to succeed.
Every visit leaves me with an even greater appreciation for their service.
I'll continue fighting to ensure they have the resources, tools, and support they need to protect our communities and keep our nation secure.” The National Border Patrol Council’s endorsement adds to Ciscomani’s growing coalition of law enforcement, local leaders, and community advocates backing his re-election campaign.
It reflects his record of delivering results on one of the issues that matters most to Southern Arizona families.
