"The Facts" Ad Launched By Ciscomani
TUCSON – Congressman Juan Ciscomani’s campaign has released a new ad titled "The Facts," airing across Arizona’s 6th Congressional District to set the record straight against Kirsten Engel's repeated lies and misinformation about his stance on abortion.
Engel's campaign, once again, is resorting to deceitful tactics, attempting to mislead voters with the same false narrative that Arizonans rejected last cycle.
In the ad, Rep.
Ciscomani personally addresses Engel's blatant falsehoods, including her claim that Ciscomani supports a complete abortion ban with no exceptions– a statement that is simply untrue.
Congressman Ciscomani has always been clear: he opposes a federal abortion ban and supports exceptions for cases of rape, incest, and protecting the life of the mother.
These facts haven’t changed, despite Engel’s relentless attempts to distort them.
"Kirsten Engel has proven, yet again, that she’s willing to lie to Arizonans to advance her campaign," said Congressman Ciscomani.
"She tried this misinformation strategy last election, and it didn’t work.
It won’t work this time either because Arizonans know better and they are smarter than Engel likes to give them credit for.
I’ve always been clear: I oppose a federal abortion ban and support exceptions for rape, incest, and of course the life of the mother."
The "The Facts" ad not only exposes Engel’s false claims but also underscores Congressman Ciscomani’s commitment to honesty and transparency with the voters of Arizona’s 6th Congressional District.
Engel’s deceptive tactics have no place in Arizona, and the Ciscomani campaign is committed to holding her accountable for misleading voters.
