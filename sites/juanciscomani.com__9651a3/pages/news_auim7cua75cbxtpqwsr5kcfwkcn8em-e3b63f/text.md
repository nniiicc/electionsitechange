A local small business owner speaks directly to the community, describing the economic hardships that families face daily and underscoring why Juan Ciscomani is the leader who understands and fights for them.
As someone raising his own family here, Juan Ciscomani is a member of the community.
He understands what it’s like to build a life in this community, working hard for a better future.
As Arizona’s most bipartisan member of Congress, he’s brought over $60 million in federal funding back to the district.
He is working to improve infrastructure, secure the border, support law enforcement, and make life more affordable for everyday families.
He has consistently delivered for Arizonans, working across the aisle to solve the real issues facing the state.
The ad captures the stark contrast between Congressman Ciscomani’s dedication to his community and Kirsten Engel’s dangerous, out-of-touch record.
Engel has consistently supported policies that have hurt the very families she claims to want to represent—policies like higher taxes, inflation-driving government spending, and dangerous calls to defund the police.
While families across the district struggle with rising prices and threats from an unsecured border, Engel’s record shows her disconnect from these critical issues.
She lives outside the district and continues to support extreme policies that fail to address the real needs of our community.
“Arizona’s 6th Congressional District is my home.
The families here are my neighbors, and I know firsthand the struggles they face every day,” said Congressman Ciscomani.
“Whether it’s keeping our border secure, lowering the cost of living, or supporting small businesses, I’m fighting for the people who make this community great.
Engel’s policies are disastrous, plain and simple.
Just like Engel herself, her agenda is out-of-touch and has no place in the district.” Ciscomani’s proven leadership stands in stark contrast to Engel’s radical, out-of-touch record.
While Engel continues to push policies that hurt Arizonans– higher taxes, higher prices, and defunding the police, Ciscomani has demonstrated time and again that he is the leader Arizona’s 6th Congressional District can count on.
This November, voters have a clear choice between common-sense solutions and Engel’s failed policies.
