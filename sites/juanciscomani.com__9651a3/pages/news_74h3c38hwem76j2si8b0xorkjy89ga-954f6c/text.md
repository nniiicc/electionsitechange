The ad was released in English and Spanish .
As a proud immigrant, Juan learned to value hard work, sacrifice, and never to take opportunities for granted.
His dad drove a bus, his mom cleaned houses, and Juan flipped burgers as his family worked to build a better future for themselves in pursuit of the American Dream.
He worked maintenance jobs to pay his pay through Pima Community College and the University of Arizona. " The opportunity to pursue our American Dream changed my life, but it's not just my story – it's the story of countless families across the country who simply want an opportunity to build a better future," said Congressman Juan Ciscomani .
"Families are working too hard to watch Washington politicians put themselves first.
Arizonans deserve leaders who never forget where they came from, why they're there in the first place, and who they work for.
I don't work for Washington.
I work for you.” Twenty years ago this year - as our nation celebrates his 250th anniversary - Juan became a United States citizen.
Now, he serves in Congress with a mission to restore the same American Dream that changed his life and ensure Arizona families have that same shot at opportunity.
People across Arizona's Sixth Congressional District are working hard every day simply trying to make ends meet and create more opportunities for their children.
They deserve leaders who are fighting for them, not politicians who use public office to enrich themselves.
That's why Juan stood up to both parties and sponsored legislation to ban insider trading by members of Congress.
He believes public office should be about service, and that members of Congress should never be allowed to profit from information unavailable to the American people.
