"The Tucson Business Council supports leaders from both parties who share our commitment to the people of Tucson, and Juan Ciscomani is one of them,” said John Smalley.
“As Arizona's only member on the House Appropriations Committee, Juan has brought more than $60 million back to Southern Arizona, and you can see the results.
He secured $4.2 million to replace aging Tucson Police vehicles, making our streets safer for families and businesses, and $2.7 million to expand career training at Pima JTED, building the skilled workforce our employers need.
That's how you grow businesses of all sizes and create good jobs, and it's why we're proud to endorse him." “I’m honored to earn the support of the Tucson Business Council,” said Congressman Juan Ciscomani.
“Small businesses are the backbone of Arizona’s economy, and investing in their success means more jobs, stronger communities, and greater opportunity across AZ-06.
That’s why I fought to deliver tax relief for our local businesses, giving them the certainty and resources to grow, hire, and invest right here at home.
I will keep working across the aisle to make Arizona’s Sixth District the best place to start and grow a business.” As Arizona’s only member of the House Appropriations Committee, Ciscomani has secured more than $60 million for community projects across Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District.
These investments are strengthening public safety, expanding workforce training, improving critical infrastructure, and creating new opportunities throughout Southeastern Arizona.
The Tucson Business Council joins a growing coalition of business organizations, labor groups, law enforcement, and local leaders from across the political spectrum supporting Ciscomani’s campaign for reelection.
