Ciscomani’s story is the American Dream.
He immigrated with his family from Mexico to the United States, became a U.S. citizen, and made history as the first naturalized citizen from Mexico elected to Congress from Arizona.
That journey shaped how he approaches the job, with a relentless focus on delivering results for the people of Arizona’s Sixth District so they can pursue their own American Dream.
That work ethic and record of results is why nonpartisan groups have ranked him the most effective member of Arizona’s congressional delegation and one of the most collaborative and bipartisan lawmakers in Congress.
He has fought to put more money back in the pockets of the people who earned it, delivering no tax on tips, no tax on overtime, and Social Security tax relief for Arizona seniors.
He also voted to make the 2017 tax cuts permanent and helped 89,330 small and pass-through businesses across Arizona’s Sixth District receive a combined $169.8 million increase in their qualified business income deduction, giving local employers more room to grow and invest.
Ciscomani has delivered for Arizona’s 6th congressional district: More than $61 million directly back to all five counties in the district for roads, water, health care, schools, and public safety Secured a new mission at Fort Huachuca which is expected to generate $3 billion in regional economic impact Authored and led the bipartisan Senator Elizabeth Dole 21st Century Veterans Healthcare and Benefits Improvement Act Delivered millions back to the district for local law enforcement and emergency services Ciscomani has brought that same independent approach to cleaning up Washington.
He has fought to ban members of Congress from trading individual stocks and using public office for personal gain.
He works for AZ-06, not Washington.
And his independently ranked bipartisan record proves he is not a rubber stamp for anyone.
“This country gave my family a chance at the American Dream.
Nowhere else on Earth could you hear a story like mine.
I came here as an immigrant, my dad drove buses, and today I have the honor of serving in Congress,” said Congressman Juan Ciscomani.
“I want every family in Arizona’s 6th congressional district to have that same opportunity to work hard, get ahead, and pursue their own dream.
That starts with putting more money back in the pockets of working people, making life more affordable, supporting small businesses, and creating opportunities right here at home.
That is what I will continue fighting for in Washington because I work for our community.” “Directly From Me” asks voters to cut through the campaign noise and look at the record, because Juan Ciscomani delivers for Arizona’s Sixth District.
