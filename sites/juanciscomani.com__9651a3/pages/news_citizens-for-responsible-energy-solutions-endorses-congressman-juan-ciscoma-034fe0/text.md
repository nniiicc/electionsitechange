Ciscomani is one of just 22 House Republicans nationwide and the only member of Arizona's delegation to earn a spot on this initial slate, which recognizes lawmakers who have championed an all-of-the-above approach to American energy security.
“Arizona sits at the center of America's energy and mineral future, and I've made it my mission to cut the red tape holding back our domestic supply chains,” said Congressman Juan Ciscomani.
“From leading the Critical Mineral Consistency Act to securing new investment in Arizona copper, I'm proud to fight for an all-of-the-above energy strategy that creates jobs here at home and reduces our reliance on foreign adversaries.
I'm honored to earn this endorsement from Citizens for Responsible Energy Solutions.” In announcing the slate, CRES founding president and board chair James Dozier said , “To continue advancing an American energy dominance agenda, Congress needs leaders committed to bolstering all forms of energy and their related supply chains through durable, pragmatic policy solutions.
CRES is proud to endorse this slate of hardworking policymakers who have demonstrated their leadership and applauds their efforts to promote stable, affordable, and cleaner American energy.” The endorsement reflects Ciscomani's record as one of Congress's leading voices on domestic energy and mineral security, an issue with direct stakes for Southern Arizona's economy.
He authored the Critical Mineral Consistency Act, which passed the House with bipartisan support and aligns federal lists of critical minerals to speed up permitting for copper and other resources essential to America's energy future.
He also co-led the Streamlining Home Installation of New Energies (SHINE) Act, bipartisan legislation to cut red tape and speed up local permitting for residential solar and battery storage.
He applauded the $1 billion Ivanhoe Santa Cruz Copper Project investment near Casa Grande and $180 million in mining education grants, including at the University of Arizona, home to a state that produces nearly 70 percent of the nation's domestic copper supply.
Ciscomani also supported the Energy and Water Development Appropriations Act, working to secure Arizona's priorities and strengthen the domestic supply chains that reduce reliance on China.
The CRES endorsement adds to Ciscomani's growing coalition of bipartisan, business, and community support heading into November, and reflects his record of delivering practical, results-driven solutions.
