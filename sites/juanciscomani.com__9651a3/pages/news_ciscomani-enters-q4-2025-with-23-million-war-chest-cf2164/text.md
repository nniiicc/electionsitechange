Ciscomani Enters Q4 2025 with $2.3 Million War Chest
TUCSON - Congressman Juan Ciscomani announced today that his re-election campaign enters the final quarter of 2025 with more than $2.3 million cash on hand.
“I’m incredibly grateful for the outpouring of support from Arizonans who believe in our mission to deliver results, not rhetoric,” said Ciscomani.
“With more than $2.3 million cash on hand, it sends a clear message: Southern Arizona is fired up to keep this seat red and focused on commonsense solutions.
While others are busy playing politics, we’re building momentum, delivering wins, and earning the trust of the people we serve.”
After raising more than $600,000 in the third quarter of 2025, Ciscomani is significantly outpacing his previous cycle’s trajectory.
This growing financial strength follows Ciscomani’s back-to-back wins in a highly competitive district.
After flipping the seat in 2022 by 1.4 percent and 5,232 votes, Ciscomani expanded his margin of victory in 2024, winning re-election by 2.5 percent and more than 10,800 votes.
Support for Congressman Ciscomani runs deep across the district, which spans portions of five Arizona counties.
He was recently ranked the most effective member of Arizona’s congressional delegation in both the U.S.
House and U.S.
Senate throughout the 118th Congress and currently serves on the House Appropriations and Veterans’ Affairs Committees.
He is running for a third term to continue representing Arizona’s 6th Congressional District and delivering on issues that matter most to Arizonans.
To learn more, visit www.juanciscomani.com.
