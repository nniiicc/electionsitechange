This fundraising haul firmly positions Ciscomani in the lead to fight and protect Arizona’s 6th congressional district in 2026.
“I’m grateful to the thousands who have invested in our campaign and believe in our mission,” said Congressman Ciscomani.
“This strong support is a clear sign of trust and confidence in the work we’re doing and the results we continue to deliver for Arizona’s 6th district.
I remain focused on getting things done for the people I represent.” This impressive fundraising haul follows Congressman Ciscomani’s more than $7 million raised during the 2024 cycle.
In 2024, he successfully defended Arizona’s 6th congressional district, expanding both his margin and vote total from his 2022 victory.
After flipping the district from blue to red in 2022 with a 1.4% margin and 5,232 votes, he widened his lead in 2024, winning by 2.5% and 10,822 votes.
Ciscomani has deep support throughout his congressional district, spanning portions of 5 Arizona counties.
And Ciscomani was recently ranked the most effective member of Arizona’s congressional delegation in both the U.S.
House and U.S.
Senate throughout the 118th congress.
Ciscomani serves on the House Appropriations and Veterans’ Affairs committees.
He is running for a third term to continue representing Arizona’s 6th congressional district.
To learn more, visit www.juanciscomani.com .
