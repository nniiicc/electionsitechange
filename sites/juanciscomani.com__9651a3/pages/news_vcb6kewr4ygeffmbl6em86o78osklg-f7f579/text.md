CISCOMANI CAMPAIGN HAULS IN $1.1 MILLION, BUILDS WAR CHEST OF $3.8 MILLION
Massive Early Advantage Underscores Strength in One of the Nation’s Most Competitive Races
The campaign’s position has surged well ahead of its pace in the last cycle.
Today, the campaign holds over $1.4 million more in the bank, reflecting expanding support across Arizona’s Sixth District and beyond.
“This campaign is building real momentum because we’re delivering real results,” said Congressman Ciscomani.
“I’m grateful for the strong support from across Southern Arizona.
Voters here want leadership that shows up, listens, and gets things done — and that’s exactly what I’ve focused on every day.”
Ciscomani’s growing war chest reflects both strong support and a campaign committed to smart, targeted investments — ensuring resources are deployed effectively to communicate his record and win in a highly competitive environment.
From border security and national defense to water reliability, veterans’ services, and economic growth, Ciscomani’s record is driving increasing support from both grassroots donors and national backers — fueling a campaign that continues to build strength quarter after quarter.
“This isn’t just a strong start — it’s a campaign that’s building, growing, and moving forward,” Ciscomani added.
“We’ve put in the work, we’re earning people’s trust, and we’re in a strong position as we head into the next phase.”
Ciscomani represents Arizona’s 6th Congressional District and serves on the House Appropriations Committee and the House Veterans’ Affairs Committee.
