All three pointed to Ciscomani's work delivering direct federal investment to their communities, funding critical projects that benefit Huachuca City, Casa Grande, and Pima.
“It has been a pleasure to work alongside Juan to preliminarily secure funding for our fire department to address an aging fire truck that will now be replaced with a new ladder truck that has been enhanced for fire suppression and other needs that will enable our brave firefighters to meet the needs of our community,” said Huachuca City Mayor Johann Wallace.
“Juan has proven he is willing to go the extra mile to accommodate all needs for communities across his district.
As Mayor of Huachuca City, I hope everyone can join me in supporting Juan for reelection” “The Town of Pima has a warrior in Congress that we can count on to deliver results for us with Juan Ciscomani!” said Town of Pima Mayor Brian Paull.
“He delivered critical funding for a road project leading to our high school, ensuring our parents, students, and staff have the ability to safely commute to the school.
He has never shied away from addressing important issues that affect us, delivering solutions — not rhetoric.
I am proud to stand by Juan for reelection, and I look forward to working with him after he is reelected!” “Over the years, Juan has proven to be a leader who delivers for Casa Grande,” said Former Casa Grande Mayor Craig McFarland.
“Securing millions of dollars for schools, roads, and water projects.
Juan has always stood with the people of Casa Grande to ensure they have the environment they need to work and raise a family.
He has my trust and unwavering support for reelection.” In Huachuca City, Ciscomani has preliminarily secured $1.3 million in Fiscal Year 2027 Community Project Funding for the town's fire department.
The funding will help replace an aging fire engine with a quint ladder truck, enhancing fire suppression, rescue, and emergency response capabilities in Huachuca City and beyond through mutual-aid resourcing among departments.
In Casa Grande, Ciscomani has helped secure $3.79 million for the Thornton Road Widening Project, a critical roadway serving major employers, along with $2.73 million for Burris Road improvements.
He also delivered $1 million for Casa Grande Elementary School's Community Center Revitalization and $1.8 million for the Casa Grande Union High School District's affordable workforce housing construction for staff.
Likewise, in Pima, Ciscomani secured $1 million in Fiscal Year 2026 Community Project Funding for the Town's High School Road project, delivering critical infrastructure improvements for the community.
“Whether it's a new fire truck for Huachuca City, road improvements in Pima, or investments in Casa Grande’s schools, I'm proud to deliver real results for every town that makes up this district,” said Congressman Juan Ciscomani.
“Rural communities like these are too often overlooked in Washington.
I'm grateful for the trust and support of all three of these community leaders, and I'll keep fighting to make sure they get the resources they need.” Ciscomani has now secured endorsements from nine mayors and eleven county supervisors across Arizona's Sixth Congressional District, from both sides of the aisle.
This growing coalition of local leaders has applauded Ciscomani's record of turning federal funding into results for their communities.
As AZ-06 remains one of the nation's most competitive House races, Ciscomani's campaign continues to build support at every level of local leadership ahead of November.
