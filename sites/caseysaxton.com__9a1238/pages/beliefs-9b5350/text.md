Casey's Beliefs
My campaign motto is Freedom and Opportunity for All - and that's my mission.
I support policies that bring freedom and opportunity.
I've listed my core beliefs below so you'll know, if elected, what type of principles and belief system I'll approach my legislative service with.
Freedom Principles
- I believe in freedom of speech, freedom of religion, freedom of expression, freedom of the press, freedom of assembly, the free exchange of ideas, and the free flow of information.
- I believe in fiscal responsibility, living within our means, and in limited and transparent government that operates with checks and balances.
- I believe in the right to keep and bear arms, the right to self-defense, the right to privacy, the right of due process, the right to own property, and in equal justice under the law.
- I believe in service to our community, loyalty to our country, and in helping those most in need.
Opportunity Principles
- I believe all human beings are created morally and socially equal.
- I believe life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness are the God-given birthright of all people.
- I believe the values of liberty, equality, and justice must always guide our state and country.
- I believe in building safe, strong, and vibrant communities.
- I believe in hard work, personal responsibility, self-reliance, and that individuals are ultimately responsible for their own welfare and destiny.
Prosperity Principles
- I believe in free enterprise, free markets, free and fair trade, limited regulation, low taxes, energy independence, and in economic prosperity for our country and its people.
- I believe in creating and sustaining an environment where builders, creators, entrepreneurs, farmers, inventors, makers, manufacturers, and producers of all kinds can thrive.
- I believe in a rigorous, merit-based K-12 public education for children and in school choice for families who need alternative options.
- I believe in scientific discovery, investment in research, and technological advancement that improves lives.
- I believe in responsibly using our natural resources and in balancing their use with the conservation of our natural environment.
