Privacy Policy
The Casey Saxton for State House campaign is committed to protecting the privacy of those accessing its website and services, as well as those who receive digital communications from the campaign via text or email.
This policy describes how information collected from website users is collected and used.
Section 1: Website Visitor Information
Those visiting the campaign website of Casey Saxton for State House, caseysaxton.com, automatically transmit specific information via their web connection to our website analytics platform.
The collection of this information is an industry standard.
Information includes, but may not be limited to:
- Date & Time of Visit
- Duration of Visit
- IP Address & Domain
- Operating System
- Referring Website (the website you visited before visiting this website, such as Google.com or a social media platform)
- Web Browser
- Webpages Viewed
This information is used to improve web content, services and accessibility.
None of the information collected identifies users individually, giving users an important level of privacy.
Information may be subject to disclosure by applicable state or federal law.
Section 2: Collection of Voluntary User Information
The Casey Saxton for State House campaign does not collect personal information through caseysaxton.com unless users voluntarily provide specific information through an application, contact email, contact form, online form, sign-up form, survey, etc.
Often, this information is required if the user would like to access a specific service or to participate in a specific program or event.
The Casey Saxton for State House campaign uses several third-party platforms, where this information may also be stored, to provide various services or communicate with you.
The Casey Saxton for State House campaign reserves the right to communicate with all individuals who voluntarily provide their information on the campaign’s website or other third-party platforms used by the campaign.
This includes communicating via email, text message, phone call, and standard mail for any reason.
Voluntary user information is kept confidential unless subject to disclosure by applicable state or federal law.
Section 3: Cookies & Retargeting
Cookies are text files generated by a web server and stored on a visitor’s computer.
Cookies allow a web server to determine if a specific webpage has previously been visited and when the last visit occurred.
The use of cookies or similar technology is an industry standard.
The Casey Saxton for State House campaign may use the functionality of cookies to show users custom digital ads based on their actions on the campaign website.
As an example, someone who visits caseysaxton.com may see an ad on Facebook from Casey Saxton using a practice known as “retargeting.”
Section 4: Email and Text Communications
The Casey Saxton for State House campaign may routinely communicate by email or text message with registered voters in House District 37 and others who sign up or otherwise engage with the campaign.
Those who receive email or text communications may unsubscribe at any time.
Those who unsubscribe will be removed from future text or email communications.
The Casey Saxton for State House campaign may use email addresses or phone numbers to show content to users of social media platforms, such as Facebook, Instagram, Twitter or LinkedIn when matched (by the social media platform) to a specific user profile on those platforms.
Section 5: Information Retention
Information collected through the Casey Saxton for State House website, caseysaxton.com, or other third-party services used by the campaign will be retained or disposed of pursuant to all applicable state or federal law.
