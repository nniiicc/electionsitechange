I'm running for the Utah House of Representatives in District 37 to advocate for the interests of the citizens of Kearns , Taylorsville and West Jordan .
I'm a common sense conservative committed to being a strong voice for the West Side.
Why am I running?
I truly believe that we can deliver more for our community.
It's no secret that the West Side sometimes gets the short end of the stick.
Our community matters, so I'm running to make sure we have a seat at the decision-making table in the Utah House of Representatives.
View Policy Priorities Get Involved HOME & COMMUNITY I am a resident of Kearns and work in West Jordan.
I spend the majority of my time right here on the West Side of Salt Lake County.
These days, you'll normally find me somewhere in between the cities of Kearns , Taylorsville , West Jordan , and West Valley City .
I've lived on the West Side for the last nine years and was able to buy my first home in Kearns in early 2025 after nearly a decade of saving up a decent down payment.
I live in the Hoffman Heights / West Kearns neighborhood.
Like many of my neighbors, my home was built in the 1950s on part of the former Camp Kearns . ​ ​ My main focus, if elected, will be to advocate for the interests of the communities that I serve.

CAREER & WORK My work background is primarily in public affairs and communications.
I've worked for the last nearly nine years to help make government more transparent and responsive to the needs of citizens. ​ I'm currently in my fourth year working as District Director for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Utah's 4th Congressional District.
In this role, I've helped Congressman Burgess Owens advocate for the interests of Utahns and supported his efforts that have brought in nearly $20 million for infrastructure and public safety projects in the cities of Kearns, Taylorsville, West Jordan and West Valley City. ​ This role has given me a front row seat to learn about many issues that Utahns face and the needs our community has. ​ Prior to my current job, I worked for Riverton City for five years as Director of Communications.
In this role, I led efforts to make city services and information accessible online, and ensured citizens were informed about city issues in a timely manner. ​ ​ I've also worked as a Marketing Manager for Utah State University Extension and held several part-time roles over the course of my life, including: public relations and marketing assistant, recreation manager, a packing/shipping laborer, and farm hand in my early years working for my dad on his farm.
