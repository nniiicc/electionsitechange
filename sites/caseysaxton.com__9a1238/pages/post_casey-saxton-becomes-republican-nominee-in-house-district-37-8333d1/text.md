Casey Saxton Becomes Republican Nominee in House District 37
KEARNS, UTAH - Casey Saxton became the official Republican candidate for House District 37 at the Salt Lake County Republican Party Nominating Convention on April 18, 2026.
"The stage for the November election is set and voters have an important decision to make," said Casey Saxton.
"I look forward to getting out in the weeks and months ahead and visiting with my neighbors about their concerns, needs and priorities.
We have a lot of work to do for our community." Saxton, a Kearns resident, first declared his candidacy back in January.
District 37 includes portions of Kearns, Taylorsville and West Jordan.
"I've said this before, but I truly believe there is opportunity to deliver more for our community," said Saxton.
"Our community needs someone at the decision making table advocating for our interests.
If my neighbors elect me this year, I'll work hard to give our community a voice at the state legislature." Those interested in learning more about Casey Saxton's campaign can visit caseysaxton.com.
