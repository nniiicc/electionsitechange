My wife and I live in Searsport, where we’ve built our home.

I’m running for State Representative to bring a practical, common-sense approach back to Augusta—focused on supporting seniors, improving healthcare access, cutting wasteful spending, and standing up for small businesses and working families.
In Augusta, I’ll do what I’ve always done: Get to work and get the job done.
What Matters Most Cutting Wasteful Spending & Providing Tax Relief I believe we need to eliminate wasteful government spending and find responsible ways to deliver tax relief for working Mainers.
Supporting Small Businesses & Jobs As someone who has spent a career creating jobs and managing businesses, I will advocate for small businesses and the workers who depend on them.
Supporting Seniors I will support policies that protect and strengthen the well-being of Maine’s senior citizens and ensure they have the resources they need to live with dignity.
Healthcare Access & Quality I will focus on improving access to affordable, high-quality healthcare, especially in rural areas where services can be harder to reach.
