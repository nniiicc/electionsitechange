№ I.
William is running as a Republican candidate for Congress to fix our broken political system and restore bipartisanship.
He is driven by his faith in God and his belief that Republicans and Democrats can come together to move our country forward.
Born and raised in a military family, William served in the U.S.
Marine Corps and was medically retired as a First Lieutenant.
He went on to serve as a prosecutor in Pensacola and Jacksonville, where he pursued justice for victims and enforced the law.
William believes the working class should take the country back from political insiders, Washington elites, and corrupt politicians.
No more extremism.
No more excuses.
Just an honest representative — accountable to the people of Florida's 5th, and answerable to no one else.
