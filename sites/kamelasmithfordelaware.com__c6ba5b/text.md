Vote September 10
Kamela “Kam” Smith is a progressive Democrat running to represent Delaware’s
15th district.
Kam has dedicated her life to helping members of our community.
She is a wife, an author, a mother in a blended family, and a woman of faith.
Kam’s 25 year career in mental health care has inspired her to confront the barriers she sees facing our community in Bear, New Castle, and St.
Georges, and fight to make sure everyone in our community has the support and resources to thrive.
"I am persuaded by the interests of those I serve, and not the interests that serve me."
