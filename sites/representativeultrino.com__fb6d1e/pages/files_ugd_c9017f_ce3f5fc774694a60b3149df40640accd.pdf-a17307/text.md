You must present your claim in writing within 2 years of date of loss. • You can either file a claim through the Attorney General's Office or present your own claim in writing to the Secretary of the Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs.
1.
If you present your own claim: 1.
You must present your claim in writing and include description of what happened, when, where, and what you seek in compensation, as well as your address and contact number.
Supporting documents and photographs are useful in addressing your claim more quickly.
2.
Mail your claim to: Rebecca L.
Tepper, Secretary Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs 100 Cambridge Street, Suite 900 Boston, MA 02114 2.
If you file a claim through the Attorney General: 1.
You can download and fill out a presentment claim form HERE 2.
Mail a copy of your completed form and any supporting documents (including bills/estimate for property damage) to: Office of Attorney General Andrea Joy Campbell Trial Division One Ashburton Place, 18th Floor Boston, MA 02108 3.
You can also call the Attorney General's Trial Division at (617) 963-2350 with any questions. • DCR will issue a formal recommendation of settlement or denial within 6 months from the date your claim 6 is presented.
