Issues & Priorities
Healthcare
Healthcare is a basic human right.
In every community across the Commonwealth, families should be able to count on accessible, affordable, and compassionate care when they need it most.
Steve supports expanding access for working families, seniors, and those that need help the most while ensuring equal access to healthcare for all.
Economic Development
Expanding access to capital, technical assistance, and workforce resources for small businesses ensures that economic opportunity reaches every corner of the Commonwealth.
When local businesses thrive, our communities thrive.
Steve has been a supporter of Malden's local economy for decades and continue to advocate for grants and economic initiatives to ensuring equal access to resources and opportunities for small businesses.
Education
Public education is the foundation of opportunity.
When we invest in our schools, we invest in our children, our workforce, and the long-term strength of our communities.
Steve strongly believes we must continue to invest in our educators and students to guarantee that everyone has access to a great education and equal opportunities in the future.
Social Justice
Communities of color have endured generations of discrimination and structural barriers that have limited access to opportunity.
Building a more just Commonwealth means acknowledging these disparities and advancing policies that promote fairness, equity, and meaningful opportunity for every resident.
Every person in our Commonwealth has the right to the same opportunities, resources, and assistance.
Steve believes that the only way forward is to ensure that low-income communities, immigrants and people of color no longer disproportionately face barriers and being denied opportunities in MA.
