Malden's Second Lieutenant Kanwar Singh Recognized for Leadership and Service
I am incredibly proud of Malden's very own Second Lieutenant Kanwar Singh for his dedication to our country and his tireless advocacy for supporting diversity both in our community, and across the entire US Army.
Kanwar, thank you for your service, and thank you for your tenacious advocacy to ensure that all Sikhs can serve our country in keeping with their faith.
A special thanks as well to Senator Jason Lewis for continuing to elevate his story!
