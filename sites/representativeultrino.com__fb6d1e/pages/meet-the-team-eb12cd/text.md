Meet The Team Staff Director Claudia Chung Claudia joined this office in May 2021.
She focuses on policy, legislation, and stakeholder relations in the office. ​ A recent graduate from the University of Pennsylvania, Claudia has experience in the private, public, and non-profit sectors.
Prior to joining Rep.
Ultrino's staff, she worked as a digital communications intern for Mayor Jim Kenney's office in Philadelphia and as a human rights education and fundraising intern at Amnesty International.
Growing up in both Hong Kong and the States led her to develop a deep interest in the racial diversity within politics and how to develop legislation that can help people in urban communities thrive.
She speaks English, Mandarin, and French as well as her native Cantonese.
In her downtime, she enjoys trying to recreate recipes from the latest season of The Great British Bakeoff, going to the beach with her puppy Ted, rewatching Parks and Recreation, and playing new games on her Nintendo Switch.
