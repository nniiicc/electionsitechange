Linden & Town Line Brook Flooding Resources Since late 2021, my office has received dozens of calls from residents about severe flooding along Delta Terrace and other residents living in neighborhoods near other storm drain structures. ​ My office is actively pursuing different options to address the root causes of this neighborhood flooding issue.
