Donna Dye Sholk #ago 2 min read Early voting starts this Saturday, October 24, and runs nine days through Sunday, November 1.
You can vote at any early voting site in your county, no matter where you live in it.
No reason needed, no line to speak of.
If you can go early, go early.
Niagara County early voting sites Cornell Cooperative Extension, Niagara County Fairgrounds, 4487 Lake Ave, Lockport Newfane Community Center, 2737 Main St, Newfane Wheatfield Community Center, 2790 Church Rd, North Tonawanda Wendelville Fire Co., 7340 Campbell Blvd, North Tonawanda Niagara Falls Train Station (Amtrak), 825 Depot Ave W, Niagara Falls Niagara County hours: Saturday and Sunday, October 24 and 25, 9 AM to 5 PM.
Monday and Tuesday, October 26 and 27, noon to 8 PM.
Wednesday October 28 through Sunday November 1, 9 AM to 5 PM.
Questions: Niagara County Board of Elections, 716-438-4040.
Erie County early voting sites closest to the district Our Lady of Peace Church, Uriel Hall, 10950 Main St, Clarence Akron Village Hall, 21 Main St, Akron Depew Municipal Building, 85 Manitou St, Depew Alden Municipal Building, 13336 Broadway, Alden Erie County hours: Saturday and Sunday, October 24 and 25, 9 AM to 5 PM.
Monday October 26 through Friday October 30, noon to 9 PM.
Saturday and Sunday, October 31 and November 1, 9 AM to 5 PM.
Erie County voters can use any of the county's sites.
Questions: Erie County Board of Elections, 716-858-8891.
Not registered yet?
Saturday is also the last day.
Your registration has to reach your county Board of Elections by October 24.
Register online, or go to the Board in person.
Everything you need is on our Vote page: votedonnadyesholk.com/vote The last two weeks.
This race will be decided by who shows up, and by who reminds their neighbors to show up.
If you can give us a Saturday morning or a couple of evenings, we will put you to work knocking doors or making calls.
Sign up here: votedonnadyesholk.com/get-involved Election Day is Tuesday, November 3.
Polls are open 6 AM to 9 PM.
On Election Day you vote at your assigned polling place, which may not be your early voting site.
Find yours at voterlookup.elections.ny.gov Thank you for being part of this.
