top of page Vote Donna Dye Sholk New York State Assembly About Priorities Get Involved Vote Contact Events Blog DONATE SUBSCRIBE Hi, I'm Donna WHERE I COME FROM I've called Lockport home my entire life.
I went to school here, raised my family here, and built my life here alongside neighbors who work hard and care deeply about this community.
I know what it means to be part of a place - to have a stake in whether it thrives.
That's not something you can fake, and it's not something I take for granted.
A THRIVING CAREER I spent over 40 years in business accounting and finance, most recently as the controller of the Niagara Falls Olin plant.
I'm a certified management accountant - someone who understands not just the numbers, but what they mean and what to do about them.
I've also walked the halls of the State House as part of a coalition addressing energy policy, so I'm no stranger to how legislation works and what it takes to move the needle.
MISSION STATEMENT Equality Meets Opportunity What we need right now are not career politicians - we need problem solvers.
People who show up not for the title, but because they believe things can be better.
I've spent 30 years in this community fostering children without homes, helping neighbors get meals, and advocating for people who needed someone in their corner.
Running for the 144th is an extension of that same work.
If elected, I will represent every resident of this district with respect and dignity - and I will fight to bring the resources and attention this region deserves.
