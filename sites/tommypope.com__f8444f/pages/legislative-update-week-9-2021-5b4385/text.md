This legislation has two goals: one, to maintain the integrity of each and every vote cast, and two, to ensure elections are run consistently across the state.
After the elections of 2020, it became clear that there were discrepancies in the way each county handled votes.
Simply put, we learned that counties were employing inconsistent processes, which is unacceptable.
Upon ratification, this legislation would task the State Election Commission with ensuring there is a uniformity to guarantee free and fair elections.
I supported this bill and it passed by a vote of 84-36.
Protecting the Right to Worship When Needed the Most The Religious Freedom Act is legislation that reinforces our First Amendment Right to worship, even during a State of Emergency.
It deems religious services essential and protects our ability to worship in times of crisis when we need it most.
I voted in favor of this bill, which passed second reading by a vote of 86-29.
COVID-19 Updates: ONE MILLION SOUTH CAROLINIANS VACCINATED This week, our state began the transition into Phase 1B, which makes a new group of individuals eligible for the COVID-19 vaccination.
This group includes people 55 and older, teachers, law enforcement personnel, store clerks, and other at-risk individuals.
Visit https://scdhec.gov/covid19/covid-19-vaccine-allocation for more information about how to qualify.
You can visit https://scdhec.gov/covid19/covid-19-testing-locations to find a testing location and schedule an appointment near you. ***As of March 11 th , 1,244,565 doses of the vaccine are in South Carolinian arms.
For more COVID-19 statistics, visit https://scdhec.gov/covid19/covid-19-vaccination-dashboard .*** Other Legislative News Barbershop on Wheels: This week, the House passed a law that allows mobile barbershops to operate upon acquisition of a permit.
This opens a new avenue of business, which is important considering the hardships faced by the personal services industry during shutdowns.
This passed by a vote of 85-29.
Homeschooled Children Can Participate in Interscholastic Sports: This bill, proposed by Rep.
Allison as a joint resolution allows for more homeschool students to be eligible to participate in public school interscholastic activities, such as sports.
I voted in favor of this bill, which then passed by a vote of 104-1.
Unclaimed Money Might Be Waiting For You Millions of dollars are transferred to the Unclaimed Property Program each year by companies who cannot find the rightful owners.
This week, The SC Treasurer’s Office announced that there are more than 600,000 new properties that have been reported to the Unclaimed Property Program.
This includes things like money in dormant bank accounts, uncashed checks, and forgotten deposits.
Search for unclaimed property on the State Treasurer’s website to see if you have money waiting on you: https://treasurer.sc.gov/what-we-do/for-citizens/unclaimed-property-program/ .
