CONTRIBUTION LIMITS Candidates may not accept more than the following amounts from individuals during an election cycle: · $3500 for statewide candidates or · $1000 for local candidates.
These figures are based on a cumulative total for the election cycle.
Candidates may not accept more than the following amounts from political parties during an election cycle: · $50,000 for statewide candidates or · $5,000 for local candidates.
These figures are based on a cumulative total for the election cycle.
Contribution limits apply to each primary, general, and runoff election.
One contribution limit applies to candidates who are unopposed during an election cycle.
