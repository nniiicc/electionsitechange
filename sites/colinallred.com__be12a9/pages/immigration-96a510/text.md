Families are tracking ICE activity on Facebook to warn each other.
Pulgas sit empty, businesses have lost their workers, and communities feel the Texas they love slipping away.
This is a crisis, which is why Colin will fight to abolish ICE and build a just immigration system in Congress.
America is a nation of immigrants, and our diversity has always been our strength.
But right now, the federal government's immigration enforcement is out of control – tearing families apart, imprisoning and deporting people without due process, and funneling billions of taxpayer dollars to private corporations that profit from cruelty.
Colin's connection to this issue is personal.

Immigration isn't a wedge issue for Colin.

It is a policy problem, and he intends to fix it.
Section I: Overhaul the Immigration Enforcement System Trump is using ICE as a political weapon.
Under Trump's direction, ICE is raiding churches, schools, and workplaces.
They are detaining people without due process, and rushing tens of thousands of new agents into the field after slashing training from five months to six weeks.
Local sheriffs and police chiefs are sounding the alarm: ICE's tactics are undermining legitimate policing and creating a public safety emergency.
Abolish ICE and Transfer its Functions • Pass legislation to abolish ICE and transfer its functions where they belong: criminal investigations to the FBI and DEA; visa enforcement to a new civil agency with transparent standards and robust civil rights protections; and border management to CBP with meaningful Congressional oversight.
Guarantee Due Process and the Right to Counsel • ICE is routinely violating the constitutional rights of citizens, legal residents, and undocumented immigrants alike, arresting without warrants, denying counsel, and detaining tens of thousands indefinitely far from family or legal support.
These are constitutional violations that cannot be tolerated. • Pass the Fair Day in Court for Kids Act to guarantee legal representation for unaccompanied children and expand access to counsel for all individuals in removal proceedings. • End indefinite detention and require bond hearings within 48 hours.
Every person on American soil deserves their day in court with real legal representation, and due process must mean something again. • The Trump administration has already fired more than 113 immigration judges, many for ruling against the government.
It is Congress's duty to pass legislation to transfer immigration judges into an independent Article I court to prevent political interference in their decisions.
Section II: Hold Bad Actors Accountable Under the Trump administration, bad actors across the private sector and administration have built a cruel mass enforcement system that needs to be dismantled.
Colin will expose them, regulate them, and hold them accountable, including individual agents who break the law, violate civil rights, or abuse their power.
Shut Down the For-Profit Immigration Enforcement Industry • A group of corporations have built a lucrative business model around mass enforcement.
GEO Group, CoreCivic, and other private prison companies receive billions annually from ICE detention contracts and lobby aggressively for more detention beds and harsher enforcement because their profits depend on it.
The scale of that profit motive came into sharp relief in 2025, when GEO Group executives told investors on an earnings call that it had been the most successful year for new business in the company's history, as detention facilities across the country filled beyond capacity. • Companies like Palantir hold lucrative federal contracts to provide ICE with data analytics and surveillance tools that aggregate location data, financial records, and personal information to hunt down immigrants, including people with no criminal record. • No agency that can detain and use force should be policing itself, and neither should the contractors getting paid to do the same work.
The End For-Profit Private Prisons Act must be passed to phase out these federal contracts entirely. • Federal immigration contractors need real oversight.
That means stronger whistleblower protections and an independent Inspector General with jurisdiction over all DHS contractors and mandatory public reporting. • Pass the Transparency in Government Contracting Act to require full public disclosure of contracts between federal immigration agencies and private technology companies, and prohibit the use of predictive analytics for civil immigration enforcement without explicit Congressional authorization.
Section III: Build a System That Actually Works Abolishing a broken system is not enough.
Colin is fighting for comprehensive immigration reform that creates legal pathways, protects the people already woven into our communities, and manages the border with both order and humanity.
Create a Real Path to Citizenship • Pass the Dream and Promise Act to provide a pathway to citizenship for Dreamers and TPS holders who already call America home, raise children here, pay taxes, and have known no other country.
Enact comprehensive immigration reform extending that same opportunity to undocumented immigrants who meet residency and background requirements. • Establish a clear pathway to legal status for undocumented immigrants who demonstrate continuous residency, a clean criminal record, and community ties through employment, family, or tax history. • Modernize the legal immigration system to reduce backlogs and reunite families faster.
Manage the Border with Order and Humanity • Closing ports of entry and banning asylum has not reduced migration.
It has made it more dangerous and more chaotic.
Senate Republicans killed a serious bipartisan border bill before it could get a vote.
Texas paid the price for that. • We must restore full asylum protections, end Title 42-style expulsions and blanket asylum bans, and reinstate the CBP One appointment system as the primary mechanism for orderly processing at ports of entry. • Pass the bipartisan Senate border bill's investment framework to hire additional asylum officers, immigration judges, and CBP personnel and allow asylum pre-screening at U.S. consulates and UNHCR-partnered sites south of the border so people with legitimate claims can schedule appointments in advance. • Tie border management to real processing capacity and work with Central and South American partners to address the root causes of migration.
Reform the Visa System to Reflect our Values and Strengthen our Economy • Outdated visa caps leave industries chronically short-staffed, while employers use immigration status as leverage to suppress wages, bench workers without pay, and silence those who report violations.
Trump's $100,000 H-1B fee prices out small businesses and hospitals while stripping protections from H-2A and H-2B holders. • Congress must establish a portable visa system that ties work authorization to the worker, not the employer, to increase wage competition and end employer exploitation. • Repeal the $100,000 H-1B fee, expand worker visa caps to meet documented labor demand, and create an Essential Industries Visa for construction, food processing, elder care, and hospitality.
Any expansion must include robust labor protections and a clear path to permanent residency. • Mandate that green card sponsorship cannot be weaponized as a condition of employment, and establish independent whistleblower protections allowing H-1B workers to report violations without risking their visa status. • Eliminate the per-country green card backlog that traps skilled workers in decades-long queues.
Pass the Equal Access to Green Cards for Legal Employment Act to distribute employment-based green cards first-come, first-served regardless of national origin.
