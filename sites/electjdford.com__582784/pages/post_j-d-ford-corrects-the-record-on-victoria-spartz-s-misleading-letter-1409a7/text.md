Ford corrects the record on Victoria Spartz’s misleading letter JD Ford Sep 21 1 min read Carmel, IN - J.D.
Ford released a response to Victoria Spartz’s letter, which contains misleading information about her stock trades.
The responses outlines: Spartz’s financial disclosures and transaction reports indicate that she traded stocks while in Congress Her financial disclosures indicate that she traded airline stocks while in congress She does not deny that she has benefited from high dollar trading of individual stocks worth between $450,000 and $1,000,000 regulated by Congress Spartz apparently used her campaign funds to pay expenses related to an investment property Read the full response here.
“Congresswoman Spartz denying her stock trades and contributing them to her spouse is not an acceptable excuse for benefiting from her position in Congress.
This is an insult to voters,” said Ford.
“Hoosiers are struggling with the cost of groceries, utilities and gas, while politicians like Victoria Spartz continue to work for donors and special interests to pad their bank accounts.
This is exactly why I’m running – to fight corruption in Washington D.C.” ### About J.D.: J.D.
Ford is running for Congress in Indiana's 5th Congressional District.
He grew up in a working-class family as the son of a union truck driver and an assisted living administrator, with grandparents who served as local officials.
J.D. learned early that public service is about people, not power.
He served two terms in the Indiana State Senate, where he fought to lower the cost of lifesaving medication, strengthen public education, and ensure every Hoosier gets a fair shot.
He's running to bring a working-class perspective to Washington and make life more affordable for every Hoosier.
