Privacy Policy
Your Data Privacy:
The J.D.
Ford for Congress campaign is committed to protecting your privacy.
This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you interact with our website.
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, and donation details.
We may also collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited to help improve our website.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Process donations and comply with campaign finance laws.
- Communicate with you via SMS and other methods about the campaign, events, soliciting donations, and opportunities to get involved.
- Respond to inquiries and coordinate volunteers.
- Improve our website and outreach efforts.
- We will not sell your data to third-parties for marketing purposes.
Donations
All online contributions are processed securely through ActBlue or other trusted third-party payment processors.
We do not store your financial information on our servers.
Information Sharing
We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information, including SMS opt-in data, with third parties for marketing purposes.
We may share information with vendors and service providers who assist in campaign operations, provided they use the data solely for campaign purposes and not for their own use.
Text Messaging Consent
By providing your phone number, you consent to receive SMS text messages from J.D.
Ford for Congress, including updates, donation asks, and volunteer opportunities.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.
Communications & Opting Out
You may opt out of campaign emails or text messages at any time by using the unsubscribe link or replying “STOP” to text messages.
The above excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, which information will not be shared with any third parties, provided that the foregoing does not apply to sharing (1) with vendors, consultants and other service providers who need access to such information to carry out work on our behalf (and who will not use such information for their own purposes); and (2) if we believe disclosure is required by any applicable law, rule, or regulation or to comply with law enforcement or legal process.
Cookies & Analytics
Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand how visitors use our site and to improve performance.
You may disable cookies through your browser settings.
Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
Updates to this policy
This policy may be updated from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page.
