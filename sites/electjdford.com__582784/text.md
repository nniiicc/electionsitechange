Ford is the 2026 Democratic candidate for Congress in Indiana's 5th Congressional District, representing Hamilton, Delaware, Madison, Grant, Howard, and Tipton counties.
Learn about his plans to lower healthcare costs, support working families, bring a strong Democratic check in Congress, and make life more affordable for Hoosiers.
Affordability The cost of groceries, housing, energy, and healthcare have exploded, but our paychecks haven’t.
Corporations are raising prices while political leaders focus on power instead of your bills.
In Congress, I’ll fight to cap healthcare and prescription costs and push for corporate accountability to lower your monthly expenses.
Accountability Washington is making too many unchecked decisions that affect our lives without any real oversight.
We can’t afford to let this lawless behavior go on—we need a strong Democratic check in Congress to hold these leaders accountable.
I’ll fight to bring federal agencies out of the shadows and ensure no one is above the law.
