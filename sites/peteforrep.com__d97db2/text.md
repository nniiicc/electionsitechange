Meet Pete
VeteranDad of 3City Councilor
After service, I juggled multiple jobs to pay my way through school to earn a Bachelor's and a Doctorate of Dental Medicine from Boston University.
Today, I’m a proud Small Business Owner, Dad, and a City Councilor.
I was born to a working class family, my dad was a firefighter in Lynn who struggled with alcoholism and my mother was a factory worker at GE.
At the height of the war in Afghanistan and Iraq, I enlisted in the U.S.
Navy because I felt a duty to this country.
While most politicians are in it for themselves, I’ve spent my life serving others whether it be protecting our nation or caring for families in my dental practice.
I’m running for State Representative because you need a leader that is accessible, brings people together, and never stops working hard for you.
I humbly ask for your vote on November 3rd, 2026.
