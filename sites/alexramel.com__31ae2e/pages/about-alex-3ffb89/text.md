About Me I’m a single father, a community organizer, and an unflinching Democrat.
I have served in the Washington State House of Representatives since 2020.
As your representative, I strive to carry forward the core values that have kept me engaged in politics and public policy for two decades: building healthy and inclusive communities, planning for the future our children deserve, and working together for a more just and inclusive Washington State.
CuRRENT ROLE • House Democratic Caucus Whip • Committee Membership: Energy & Environment, Transportation, Finance, and Rules • Ex Officio Member of the Washington State Building Code Council • Executive Board of the House Democrats Campaign Committee Past Experience • Board Member of Kulshan Community Land Trust, including three years as President • Author of Bellingham's first Local Climate Action Plan • Founder of the Community Energy Challenge • Energy and Policy Director for Sustainable Connections • Whatcom Wins Co-Chair • 2013 Environmental Hero Award, RE Sources for Sustainable Communities • Climate Policy Advisor for Stand.earth • Whatcom Climate Impacts Advisory Committee What I stand for on key issues: Climate change I am committed to a rapid and just transition to a clean energy economy.
Affordable Housing We are in a housing crisis.
We need more homes that people can afford, and fair rules that keep them affordable.
Healthcare for all Healthcare is a basic human right and we must work steadily to make sure everyone in our community has the care they deserve.
Preventing gun violence It's time for common sense policies that will save lives. building the economy Our economy should put people first: living wages, a more progressive tax code, and opportunities for everyone.
Standing up to The Federal Government Washington State should do everything we can to protect all of our neighbors from the chaos, corruption, and violence of this administration, and to hold those responsible accountable.
