Environment
The Issue:
Climate change is real, it is serious, and it requires real, serious solutions.
I am proud to be a legislator who focuses on improving our environment, including clean air, clean water, more green space, and improved measures to manage flooding and difficulties with stormwater drainage.
I believe we can set standards for the environment we all want and deserve, move in new directions for developing energy sources that work for our communities, and empower our localities to decide what is best for them as we move to a diverse and cleaner energy future.
As a member of the State House, I have worked to ensure we address climate change and empower citizens to determine what is best for them in their communities.
