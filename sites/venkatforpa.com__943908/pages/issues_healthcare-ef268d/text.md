I believe we need to make our healthcare system more accessible and affordable, enacting medical debt relief, reducing copayments and deductibles so that insurance actually works for families, keeping our parents and grandparents independent and in their homes, expanding resources for treatment of addiction and mental illness, and ensuring that our ERs will be able to care for all of us in times of need.
