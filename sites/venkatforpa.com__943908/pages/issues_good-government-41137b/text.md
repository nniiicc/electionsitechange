Good Government
The Issue:
For too long, our politicians have focused on how to make elections work for them rather than having voters decide what they want for themselves, their families, and their communities.
I believe voting should be easy and secure and that our elections should reflect the will of the electorate, not ideologues that would change voting standards to make it harder for all the people’s voices to be heard.
