Public Safety
The Issue:
As an ER doctor, I’ve seen how stretched EMS and ambulance services have become and how hard our police and fire departments are working to keep our communities secure, while struggling to recruit and retain personnel.
As our State Representative, I fight every day to make sure that the vital public services on which we all depend, including our police, fire departments, and EMS, are fully funded so they can be there for all our communities.
