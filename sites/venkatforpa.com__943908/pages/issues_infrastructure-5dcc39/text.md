Infrastructure
The Issue:
Our portion of Allegheny County has seen significant growth over the last decade, but Pennsylvania’s commitments to building and maintaining infrastructure have not kept pace.
In Harrisburg, I will continue to prioritize bringing funding from recent federal legislation to upgrade our local roads, bridges, sewer systems, and other critical pieces of local infrastructure.
Rebuilding our communities will improve quality of life and create badly needed construction and trades jobs in the region.
