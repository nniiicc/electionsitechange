Young Kim Leading Calvert Overall and with Key Voting Blocs Press Release February 10, 2026 Internal Poll Shows a Strong Path to Victory for Rep.
Kim who has a $2 Million Cash-on-Hand Advantage over Calvert Anaheim, CA – Today, the Young Kim for Congress campaign released an internal poll showing Rep.

“Young Kim is a proven America First fighter, and voters trust her record of standing up to Gavin Newsom and the radical woke agenda,” said George Nassar, a partner at Public Opinion Strategies.

Kim leads not only among Republicans but also among the highest-propensity voters (4 of 4 voters) and seniors.
Among Republicans who view both Young Kim and Ken Calvert favorably, Kim leads 54%-39%.
Kim recently added half a million dollars to her media buy, raising the total reservation to $3.75 million, the 2nd largest Republican ad reservation in the entire country.

Kim outraised Calvert by over $500,000 in the same period and currently holds a $2 million cash-on-hand advantage.
Congresswoman Young Kim currently represents California’s 40th Congressional District that encompasses portions of Orange, Riverside, and San Bernardino counties.
Kim currently represents over 175,000 registered voters and over 42% of high-propensity Republican voters in the new 40th district.
She serves on the House Financial Services Committee, House Foreign Affairs Committee, and House China Select Committee.
