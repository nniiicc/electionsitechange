Anaheim, CA – In case you missed it, Politico today is reporting on a recent poll showing the race between Young Kim and 34-year incumbent Ken Calvert has essentially been reset since the primary and is now a dead heat.
The polling memo reported by Politico can be viewed HERE.
From Politico Morning Score:
FIRST IN SCORE — CA-40: A super PAC backing Rep.
Young Kim (R-Calif.) circulated a polling memo to donors this week, obtained by Score, showing Kim statistically deadlocked with Rep.
Ken Calvert (R-Calif.).
Calvert came in with 33 percent, with Kim at 32 percent and 18 percent undecided, though Kim’s support went up significantly after a message test (July 8-12, MoE +/- 4.9 percent).
The memo tells donors that “Kim is in a position to win if she has the financial backing needed not only to fully define herself,” and it notes that the redrawn CA-40 includes more of Calvert’s old district than Kim’s.
