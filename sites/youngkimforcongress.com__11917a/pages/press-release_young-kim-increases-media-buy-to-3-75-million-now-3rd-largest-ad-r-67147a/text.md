Kim Adds Half a Million Dollars to Media Buy; Maintains a $2 Million Cash Advantage Over Ken Calvert
Anaheim, CA- Today, the Young Kim for Congress campaign announced a major expansion of its media buy, adding $500,000 in new broadcast and cable advertising.
The increase brings the campaign’s total media reservation for California’s June primary election to $3.75 million.
The buy, which is scheduled to begin in early April 2026, includes more than $3.25 million committed to broadcast and cable, with an additional $500,000 dedicated to streaming platforms.
