Victoria Martz · Indiana House District 55 FROM RIPLEY COUNTY, FOR RIPLEY COUNTY A Hoosier upbringing.
Victoria grew up between Milan and Batesville with five siblings.
Her family’s Christian values, hard work, and care for one another helped shape her commitment to community.
Homeschooled until eighth grade, she learned by raising animals and growing produce—asparagus, strawberries, potatoes, tomatoes, and peppers—on the family farm before attending school in Batesville.
A voice for others.
Inspired by her grandfather—a medical malpractice attorney who always stood up for regular people hurt by big companies—Victoria pursued a career helping people navigate the justice system.
She graduated from IU Law School, passed the bar in 2019, and became a criminal defense lawyer representing young people and adults.
Home is where it starts.
Today, Victoria lives in Batesville, where she’s raising her child.
You might see her at a farmers market, a community event, or a gardening shop.
She welcomes the chance to meet her neighbors and hear what matters to them.
