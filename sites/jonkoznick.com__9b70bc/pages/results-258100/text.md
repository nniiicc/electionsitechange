ADDRESSING COVID & HELPING SMALL BUSINESSES
To address the health concerns caused by COVID, Jon helped fund testing centers and lead the fight to get schools, businesses and churches open.
Jon’s efforts also included acquiring $4 million for Lakeville and $52 Million for Dakota County to assist in the needs and help our small business community with losses caused by imposed shutdowns.
He is a co author of a number of bills to end similar "emergency" orders used in 2020, and the "Never Again Bill".
In response to COVID orders closing restaurants and limiting dining
room capacity, Jon authored and brought together a bipartisan
coalition to permit our restaurants make additional revenue by selling
wine and beer to-go.
WORKING FOR SAFE COMMUNITIES
Jon is endorsed by the MN Police & Peace Officers Association AND MN Professional Firefighters for his work with law enforcement to keep our neighborhoods, schools and businesses safe.
Jon has authored ground breaking legislation on transit safety and championed the Community Safety & Security Act to enhance local public safety programs and increase law enforcement outreach efforts.
Jon was also the chief author to provide ground breaking Equine Mental Health therapy for Police and Fire first responders.
This program at Canterbury Park utilizes retired race horses in a clinically approved methods that has served a unfortunate demand in the face of the sad Burnsville tragedy and some horrific Lakeville incidents.
The program has proven to literally save lives in metro area departments.
CARING FOR OUR SENIORS
Jon knows senior citizens have faced many challenges over the past year.
He has worked to protect nursing homes from harmful cuts, voted to reduce the tax on social security benefits and was responsible for getting Metro Mobility service to Lakeville for disabled seniors and seniors who aren’t able to drive.
Jon authored the bill that finally delivered service to Lakeville and saved the Met Council an estimated $8 million a year to pay for the Lakeville service.
LOWERING TAXES
For the first time in 20 years Minnesotans will see a reduction in
their income tax rates as the second income tax rate was reduced
from 7.05% to 6.80%.
This is part of a Koznick bill to lower income
taxes for working families.
