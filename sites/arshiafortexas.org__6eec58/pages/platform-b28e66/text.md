Freeze The Rent
Texans continue to suffer at the helm of inflated housing costs.
For too long bad landlords have squeezed tenants for higher rents.
A rent freeze is needed across the board to help everyday Texans get by.
We need pricing controls, more market regulation, new development and social housing to support our communities in building brighter futures together.
Cheaper Groceries
As the federal government continues to cut food stamps and subsidies, it’s time for Texas to pick up the slack.
It’s time for Texans to support Texans and keep food affordable.
We need to take action to bring down the cost of groceries for every day Texans.
That looks like state-funded grocery stores, further investments in our food production sectors, state expansion of subsidized food programs, and more emphasis on sustainability.
Universal Childcare
Texas families work hard enough to keep the lights on and put food on the table.
It’s time for Texas to step up to help working parents by providing universal childcare, implementing universal and extended paid parental leave to bring families closer together and set our young Texans on a path to success.

End Mass Incarceration
Every year, the Texas Department of Corrections spends 2.5 billion dollars to keep people behind bars.
Despite this massive budget, state prisons have a 30.7% recidivism rate.
Y’all, the numbers aren’t adding up.
Instead of pouring billions into a system that consistently fails, the hundreds of thousands of taxpayer dollars spent yearly on each individual imprisoned, are better spent on rehabilitation over incarceration.
Community Mental Health Support
A mental health approach in schools and policing promotes early intervention and de-escalation, addressing underlying issues before they become crises.
We can improve policing by implementing mental health specialists in the Texas Department of Public Safety, limiting usage of police forces to violent crime, ending qualified immunity for law enforcement misconduct, and holding our public servants accountable.
Texans deserve specialized care to keep our communities safe and flourishing.
Invest in mental health workers on transit to prevent crises and protect riders.
Criminal Justice Reform
Every day, our fellow Texans face harm under our current system.
We need to implement speedier justice by allocating more funding to improve court processes.
To give all Texans a fair shot in our justice system, we must end the bail system, which harms those most economically disadvantaged in our society.
Additionally, we need to make sure our justice system works diligently to end wrongful convictions.
