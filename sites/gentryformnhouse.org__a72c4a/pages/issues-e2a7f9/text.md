With costs on the rise in all aspects of life, people simply cannot afford any kind of preventative care, much less health insurance.
With insurance companies looking to turn the greatest profit, we need to put health decisions back in the hands of doctors and their patients.
I know that to achieve free health care for all there will be many steps to get there, so I propose that we start with making sure our children are covered first, from ages newborn all the way up to 26.
This will help give them the healthcare they need without the added costs that come with copays and insurance prices through parents work.
At the same time healthcare doesn’t just cover a trip to the doctors, it should also cover sight, hearing, dental, and mental health as well.
We should not have several different insurances when it is all healthcare.
As well as making sure everything is covered, I believe we should put a cap on drug prices.
For too long insurance prices have continued to go up, as well as drug prices.
Low-income families continue to struggle while pharmaceutical companies continue to make more and more money.
Healthcare We need to increase funding for our education system, which will also increase funding for our disability programs.
The goal is to find this funding without putting extra strain on taxpayers.
For example, we can tell corporations to pay what they owe.
It's time we prioritize people over corporate profits.
Prioritizing people also means prioritizing teachers.
No teacher should have to work multiple jobs to make ends meet.
Teachers shouldn't have to spend their own money on school supplies needed throughout the year.
This November you can find a constitutional amendment on the ballot that does just this.
It is proposing increasing the permanent school fund payout from 2.5% to 4.5%.
This will not increase your taxes.
For more information you can check out 2026 Proposed Constitutional Amendment – What You Need to Know | League of Women Voters of MN .
Education I fully support the Second Amendment.
That being said, we need common-sense gun laws/red flag laws.
As a truck driver, I had to get a federal background check and undergo fingerprinting in order to haul hazardous materials.
We could use the same system for purchasing firearms.
I would also like to see purchasers undergo proper training before they purchase the firearms they intend to buy.
I am asking that we take a proper safety handling course and spend some time at a range before a purchase.
The #1 cause of death for children is gun violence.
As a gun owner myself and someone who has the training on how to properly store and use them myself, I know that protection is important.
I stand up for our 2nd amendment rights, but I also stand up for the protection of children and those that are most vulnerable when it comes to gun violence.
Gun Reform Our ongoing conflict with Iran is making it clear that we should increase our efforts toward renewable energy.
Our reliance on oil for energy is costing the average taxpayer too much each year, and costs are increasing.
We also need to protect our other resources, such as lakes and rivers.
With the constant pollution, I fear that water from these areas will become too tainted to drink from, not just for us, but for the wildlife as well.
Illinois passed a rewilding law last year that went into effect, granting more power to the DNR, to implement rewilding into its conservation strategy.
(Taken from https://rewilding.org/let-illinois-amaze-us-rewilding-law-in-the-prairie-state/ ) We should be following their example.
Environmental Protections I support ranked choice voting local options legislation for 2026 that would give all Minnesota counties, cities, towns, and school boards the opportunity to use RCV if they choose, and provide consistent standards for implementation.
RCV has been tested and successful in Minnesota cities and across the country.
More local jurisdictions should have the opportunity to use it.
Ranked Choice Voting Data Centers & Ai Oversight Minnesota's natural resources and communities deserve protection from unchecked industrial expansion - and that includes hyper-scale and AI data centers.
That is why I support a complete AI data center ban.
Right now, we do not know the full extent of the environmental damage caused by these types of data centers nor the impact they will have on communities.
It would be irresponsible to even consider approving the construction or conversion of data centers of any kind without meaningful safeguards in place.
Until proper regulations are determined and implemented, a statewide moratorium of no less than 2 years should be enacted.
This will give lawmakers and regulators the time needed to study the consequences and craft responsible policy.
This pause will give us the chance to implement stronger regulations on existing data centers and clear regulations governing the use of AI.
We cannot allow our water, land, and communities to bear the costs of an industry that has yet to be held accountable.
Government accountability Elected officials should be held to a higher standard when it comes to upholding the law.
No member of government should get a slap on the wrist when they break the law.
Everyone, whether an elected official or not, should be held accountable for their actions, be it through the government itself or the people they represent.
Repeat offenders should no longer be able to hold office.
Fraud has been a large talking point lately, each accusation should be investigated thoroughly.
We have the newly established Office of Inspector General, this office should receive the full support of both the house and senate and given any tools and resources they need to do their job.
Which is rooting out fraud in every corner of the state.
Small and Local Business Small and local businesses are the backbone of any community.
We need to support them in becoming successful.
This will have to be a bit of give and take by giving tax breaks to small and new businesses and increasing taxes of big corporations.
Elk River is home to roughly 2500 small businesses and each of those employ on average around 11 employees.
We need to strive to increase minimum wage so those employees can have livable wages while at the same time bringing cost down so employers do not struggle to pay those livable wages.
According to Elk River Economic and Development Business Resource this area thrives on small businesses.
And that’s just Elk River; Otsego is home to several small businesses as well and they deserve to have the means to thrive too.
By giving small businesses, a leg up then we can work towards increasing their long-term success.
Housing Affordable housing is an issue that is not just statewide but country wide.
Everyone should have the opportunity to achieve homeownership.
And those that do not wish to own a home should also be able to rent without predatory increases and on one income, as well as not cost more than 30% of someone’s income.
To lower costs, we can build more and smaller starter homes as well as renovate older homes to reduce the shortage.
We could also look into using older abandoned buildings and bring them up to code to convert them into low-cost apartments.
Corporations and real estate investment companies should be limited on how many single families homes they can own.
They should also be required to sell those homes after 5-10 years.
Energy Sources I support lifting the moratorium on nuclear energy.
We have 2 existing plants in Minnesota that are currently producing over 30% of the state’s current energy needs.
The Xcel Energy plant is over 50 years old and with current technology I believe that nuclear energy is much safer and more reliable.
I do still believe that we should invest in solar, wind, and hydroelectricity.
We should make strides to move away from the Sherco power plant as it is far too outdated.
