Who Represents You State Representative, District 30B: Paul Novotny (R–Elk River) State Senator, District 30: Eric Lucero (R) District by the Numbers Population: approximately 45,800 residents For context, the average Minnesota House district represents about 42,600 people, so 30B is slightly larger than a typical district.
