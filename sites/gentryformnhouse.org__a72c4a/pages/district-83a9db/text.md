A Look At Your District
Minnesota House District 30B
This page is a quick reference for the communities that make up Legislative District 30B: where it sits, who represents it, and a few facts about the people who call it home.
Communities in District 30B
- Nowthen (Anoka County), population 4,581
- Oak Grove Precinct 2 only (Anoka County), population 9,577
- Elk River (Sherburne County), population 28,317
- Otsego Precincts 3B, 4B, and 5B only (Wright County), population 23,790
Note: some of these cities and townships are split between multiple legislative districts — only the precincts listed above fall within 30B.
Who Represents You
- State Representative, District 30B: Paul Novotny (R–Elk River)
- State Senator, District 30: Eric Lucero (R)
District by the Numbers
- Population: approximately 45,800 residents
- For context, the average Minnesota House district represents about 42,600 people, so 30B is slightly larger than a typical district.
School Districts Serving 30B
This is one area where the district isn't neatly split along one line, coverage overlaps two districts
- Independent School District 728 (Elk River Area Schools) serves Elk River, Otsego, and Nowthen
- Anoka-Hennepin School District 11 also serves parts of Nowthen and Oak Grove
