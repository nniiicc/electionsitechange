Keenan Gentry and Jen Bloomquist are partnering together for an event in Nowthen.
Both Jen and Keenan cover Oak Grove with districts 30B and 31B.
Come out to the park with us as we celebrate moving into the fall season.
Take a break and enjoy the crisp air while attending a family friendly event.
You will be able to find us at the Prairie Park Pavillion.
What to expect:
Pumpkin painting
Pumpkin carving
Yard games
Sweet treats and candy
Warm beverages
Face Painting
Photo Booth
We will provide everything but if you have your own pumpkins, you are more than welcome to bring them along.
Hope to see you there.
