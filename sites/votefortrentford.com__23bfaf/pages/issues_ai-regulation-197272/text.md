AI & Innovation
Smart regulation that protects Alabamians while embracing innovation
Artificial intelligence is transforming how we work, learn, and live.
Alabama needs thoughtful policies that protect our citizens while ensuring we don't get left behind in the innovation economy.
We need smart regulation, not fear-based bans or unchecked corporate power.
- Protect Alabamians: Ensure AI systems used in employment, healthcare, and government services are transparent, fair, and accountable
- Embrace Innovation: Support AI development and adoption that creates jobs and economic opportunity in Alabama
- Prepare for Future Jobs: Integrate AI literacy into education and workforce training so Alabamians can compete in the 21st-century economy
- Balance Protection with Progress: Thoughtful policies that prevent harm without stifling innovation that could benefit our communities
- Surveillance Cameras: Ban surveillance cameras like Flock cameras
- Surveillance Pricing: Ban surveillance pricing so companies can't manipulate your grocery bill based on purchase history and other information
"Smart regulation of artificial intelligence that protects Alabamians while embracing innovation.
We need thoughtful policies that prepare us for the future."
AI is already affecting jobs in manufacturing, healthcare, and agriculture—key industries in District 7.
We can either shape how AI impacts our communities, or let others decide for us.
This isn't about being pro-tech or anti-tech.
It's about being pro-Alabama.
We need policies that ensure AI serves our communities, creates opportunities, and protects workers and families.
