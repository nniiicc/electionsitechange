Alabama House of Representatives District 7
"Find out if you can vote for Trent Ford"
Alabama House of Representatives District 7 covers rural northern Alabama, including all of Lawrence County and specific portions of Morgan and Colbert counties.
The district has about 46,370 residents across 833 square miles.
Important: This district does NOT include the city of Decatur or other major urban centers.
It's primarily rural and small-town communities.
