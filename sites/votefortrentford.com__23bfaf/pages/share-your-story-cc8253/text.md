Why Your Story Matters Most politicians talk at you.
I want to listen to you.
The challenges facing families in Lawrence, Morgan, and Colbert County aren't abstract policy debates—they're real struggles with real consequences.
When I understand what's actually happening in your life—how high power bills are cutting into your grocery budget, how the grocery tax makes every trip to the store harder, how rural hospital closures mean driving hours for care—I can fight for solutions that actually work.
That's what solutocratic governance means: solutions based on real problems, not party talking points.
