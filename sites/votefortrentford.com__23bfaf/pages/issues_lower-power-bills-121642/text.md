Lower Power Bills
Expand nuclear and solar energy to increase clean power supply and drive costs down
High power bills are crushing Alabama families.
We need practical solutions that increase supply, reduce costs, and prepare us for the future.
- Expand nuclear energy to increase clean power supply and drive costs down
- Explore solar energy options to diversify our energy portfolio
- Support advanced nuclear projects coming to the Tennessee Valley
- Alabama should lead in clean, affordable energy that powers our economy and reduces costs for families
"Every policy I support answers one question: Does this make life better or more affordable for Alabamians, workers, and families in Lawrence, Morgan, and Colbert County?
Lower power bills through smart energy policy—that's a yes."
