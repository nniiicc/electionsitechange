Serving Raleigh County in the West Virginia House of Delegates In January 2026 , Rev.
John K.
Jordan was appointed by Governor Patrick Morrisey to represent the 42nd District in the West Virginia House of Delegates , serving the people of Raleigh County .
Originally from Parkersburg, West Virginia , Delegate Jordan brings decades of leadership, service, and community involvement to the legislature.
Since 1998 , he has served as Lead Pastor of Calvary Assembly of God in Beckley , guiding his congregation and supporting families throughout the region.
Beyond the church, he serves as Assistant Superintendent of the Appalachian Ministry Network and as a General Presbyter for the General Council of the Assemblies of God .
Since 2000 , he has also served the community as Chaplain at Raleigh General Hospital , offering spiritual care and comfort to patients and their families during some of life’s most difficult moments.
Delegate Jordan earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from the University of the Cumberlands and a Master of Science in Church Leadership from Assemblies of God Theological Seminary .
He and his wife, Stephanie , are the proud parents of three children: Nathaniel, Lydia, and Thaddaeus .
