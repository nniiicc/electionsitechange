Why I'm Running
Bringing Change to Frederick
I am running to bring change to Frederick County and the State of Maryland.
We need a government that is responsive to its citizens.
I oppose what ICE is doing and I oppose participation in 287g.
We need to expand affordable health care in Frederick County while protecting the patients freedom to choose their care.
We need the infrastructure funding to address the growth which has occurred in Frederick County, and the incumbents have failed to do that.
