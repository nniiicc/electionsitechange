DONATE All Posts Press Releases In the News Letters to the Editor Search Letter to the Editor: Falconer earns voter’s support for state House Oct 15, 2024 1 min read Three attributes have more than earned my vote for Alex Falconer for state House in District 49A in the November election: Commitment – Beyond his commitment as the father of three young children, Alex is a volunteer coach of the Minnetonka High School cross-country and Nordic ski teams.
He’s committed to protect the environment through his job with the Campaign to Save the Boundary Waters, a position that has educated him about how things work at the state Capitol.
Balance – Alex is endorsed by labor groups because he supports workers and their rights, but he also understands the needs of business, growing up in a small business household.
He watched his father rebuild the Red Wing Stoneware Company and learned firsthand the importance of hard work, responsibility, and supporting local economies.
Personal style – Alex is easy to be around.
He hasn’t let his many accomplishments swell his head.
I think it’s this humility that will make him a welcome voice of compromise and problem-solving in St.
Paul.
For these reasons and many more, I urge my neighbors to vote for Alex Falconer, someone who will work for a state government that works for us all.
