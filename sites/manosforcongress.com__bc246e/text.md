As someone that began his public efforts as an advocate and volunteer, Steve takes pride in listening to the needs of our residents, and ensuring their voices are heard.
For Steve, this is a deeply personal mission to improve the lives of the people of our community.
His personal, professional, and public life all reflect this truth.
Steve has been a part of a team of leaders that has brought significant improvement at the city level through brave initiatives and fiscal discipline.
He knows government and how to achieve tangible results.
As an individual that grew up in the Inland Empire and has a proven track record of improving his hometown, Steve has a special passion for representing the Inland Empire in our nation's Capitol and a unique loyalty to its residents.
He is dedicated to restoring the promise of the American Dream.

"There are many challenges facing our country today.
We need representation at the national level that understands the needs of our community.
As your next Congressmen I will seek policies that protect this nation from all threats - both foreign and domestic - ensure parental rights are never threatened, remove the regulatory barriers that increase the cost of housing and basic needs, and will always act with dignity and in the best interests of our citizens.
"We must immediately fund freeway repairs along the 215 freeway, along with increased transportation capacity for our growing region.
Our Veterans need our continued support.
We need legislation that limits government overreach at all levels of government.
We need legislators that care more about getting things done over appearing on social media.
"Our greatest need is affordable, market-rate housing for our young and young-at-heart.
We need to remove the barriers to housing that have made that basic asset attractive to giant hedge funds.
These should be homes, not just investment vehicles.
"I have real world solutions for real world problems that touch every American, not just a small minority.
It has been an honor to make incredibly positive change in my hometown... it is time to take on bigger, more difficult challenges.
For that reason and with the urging of my family and friends, I humbly seek the opportunity to work for you." - Steve Manos
