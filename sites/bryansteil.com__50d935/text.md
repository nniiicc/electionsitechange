Steil’s Vision for America
Strengthening Our Economy
Families and workers haven’t been able to afford the things they need.
Steil is working to lower costs by cutting wasteful government spending, unleashing American energy, and reducing regulatory costs.
Securing Our Communities
We must continue working to secure our border.
Steil has worked to stop the open border policies that allowed the flow of illicit drugs, like deadly fentanyl, into our country.
He’s collaborating with local law enforcement to ensure our communities are safe and secure.
Protecting Our Seniors
Steil strongly supports Social Security and Medicare.
He is committed to protecting these vital programs for older Americans and those who rely on them.
