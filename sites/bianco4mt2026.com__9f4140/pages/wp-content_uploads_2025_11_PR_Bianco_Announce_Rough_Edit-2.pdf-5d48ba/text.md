FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE November 21, 2025 CONTACT: Ashley Bianco C: 406.201.6895 Bianco Launches Campaign for Montana House District 1 FORTINE, MONTANA – Michelle Bianco, a longtime Lincoln County advocate and community leader, today announced her candidacy for the Montana House of Representatives in District 1: “While the Democrat Party continues to terrorize Lincoln County with reckless environmental lawsuits that destroy livelihoods and drive families from their homes, I’m stepping up to fight back,” said Michelle Bianco.
“There is a battle happening right here in Lincoln County, and I plan on protecting our way of life, defending our jobs, and standing up for the men and women who built this county.
I’m running to bring back mining, revive logging, and stop the endless property tax hikes that are crushing rural Montana families.” Bianco, a proven conservative advocate, has fought for Montana jobs, challenged wasteful spending, and exposed the dangers of environmental lawsuits.
As a local leader, she has served on the Lincoln County Library Board, launched Citizens to Protect Lincoln County, and organized forums for the community, connecting residents with the DNRC, NRCS, and other state resources.
Her leadership has also been felt beyond county lines.
As a voice for Montana in Helena and Washington, D.C., Michelle has testified before state committees, drafted legislation to address secondhand drug exposure, spoke before congressional leaders, and worked with state decisionmakers to secure PILT, SRS, and forest management support.
“Lincoln County deserves a representative who doesn’t back down when bureaucrats and environmental extremists try to shut down our mills, strangle our industries, or tax our property out from under us,” Bianco added.
“I’ll bring a dose of much-needed common sense to Helena, defend our resource-based economy, and fight for policies that put Lincoln County first.” Bianco, President of the Republican Women’s Club for Lincoln County, is a lifelong educator of military veterans and a small-business owner.
Together with her husband, Tom, a U.S.
Army veteran, she has dedicated her life to serving others.
With Montana roots that stretch back to Butte’s first High Ore miners, Michelle is a proven fighter for Lincoln County.
She concluded, “Each day we wait, environmental extremists and liberal elites chip away at Lincoln County’s future.
It’s past time to send a proven fighter who gets results to Helena who will stand with us – not against our interests.” ### bianco4mt2026.com
