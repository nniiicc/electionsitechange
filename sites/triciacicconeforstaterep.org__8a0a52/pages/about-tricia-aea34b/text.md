Meet Tricia
Tricia Ciccone is a mom, neighbor, community leader and longtime Norwalk resident who has dedicated her career to serving children, families, and the community she proudly calls home.
Tricia raised her family in Norwalk and understands firsthand the opportunities and challenges facing local families.
As a parent whose children attended Norwalk Public Schools, she knows the importance of strong schools, safe neighborhoods, affordable living and a community where families can thrive.
Professionally, Tricia has spent years working with children and families through educational and after-school programs, overseeing services that support more than 1,300 students across multiple locations.
Her experience managing budgets, leading teams and building partnerships has reinforced her belief that government should be accountable, transparent and focused on delivering results.
Beyond her professional work, Tricia has been actively involved in the community through volunteer service, local organizations, youth programs and civic engagement.
She believes that the best solutions come from listening to residents and working together to address the issues that matter most.
Today, Tricia is running for State Representative because she wants to be a strong voice for local families in Hartford.
She is committed to lowering costs for working families, supporting quality education, improving public safety, promoting economic opportunity and ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly.
As a mom, neighbor and community advocate, Tricia understands what makes Norwalk special and is committed to preserving and strengthening that quality of life for future generations.
"I raised my family here, I serve my community here and I am committed to fighting for the families and neighborhoods that make Norwalk such a special place to call home."
