Congresswoman Monica De La Cruz announced a $6 million Economic Development Administration grant to the City of McAllen to expand infrastructure at its industrial park, a project officials expect will attract private investment and create close to 500 jobs.
The award is paired with $34 million in private investment, and background released with the announcement noted she has helped secure more than $50 million in federal resources since taking office and has requested more than $40 million for projects across the district for fiscal year 2027.
She appeared alongside Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick to make the announcement.
