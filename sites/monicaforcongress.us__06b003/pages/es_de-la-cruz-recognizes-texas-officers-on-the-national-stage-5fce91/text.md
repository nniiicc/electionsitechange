She recognized DPS Troopers Juan Zuniga of McAllen and Roman Gonzalez of Edinburg, both members of the elite Texas DPS Honor Guard, noting that Gonzalez served as a Marine in Iraq and earned the Combat Action Ribbon before his 17 years as a trooper.
She honored Edinburg Police Chief Jaime Ayala, who won the Texas Police Chiefs Association’s Mike Gentry Executive Leadership Award and whose department has been named the Valley’s favorite law enforcement agency three years running.
She closed with Sergeant Joshua Roque of the Jim Wells County Sheriff’s Office, an Alice native who rose from correctional officer to patrol sergeant and SWAT team member.
