Five bills that Congresswoman Monica De La Cruz introduced became law as provisions of the 21st Century ROAD to Housing Act, aimed at helping Texas families pursue homeownership, expanding housing support for veterans, and increasing the national housing supply.
The provisions include the Housing Affordability Act, which updates multifamily loan limits for modern costs; the VA Home Loan Awareness Act, which makes sure veterans and their families know they qualify for a VA benefit when they apply for a home loan; an interagency coordination requirement directing HUD, USDA, and the VA to work together to maximize housing programs; and the Housing Supply Expansion Act.
“After years of work across both chambers, I am thrilled to see this bill become law,” she said.
