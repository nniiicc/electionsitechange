Monica’s deep community roots and desire to serve inspired her to run for U.S.
Congress in TX-15.
She knows that District 15 is full of vibrant, hard-working individuals from the border of Mexico to the heart of Texas.
Monica lives in the lower region of District 15 in Edinburg and has even made a second home in Seguin in order to better understand the needs of her entire district.
Monica fights for the citizens of District 15 on the national stage in Congress as The People’s Voice.
