Meet Richard Aungier
I was born in a military family, my father being a WWII and Korean War veteran.
I grew up on several military bases, including one in Japan, where all my friends and neighbors were Japanese.
I learned to appreciate foreign culture and ways of life at an early age.
I graduated from Portland State University with a BS degree in Social Sciences.
Interested in travelling, I made my way to Alaska where I worked on the Alaska Oil Pipeline Project as an air traffic advisor and weather observer.
With my connection to far eastern culture early in life, I decided to return to Japan where I taught English for four years.
From there, I then moved to South Korea to continue to teach English and other academic skills for another year.
Back in the US, I worked as an owner operator of a small trucking business, travelling across the lower forty-eight states and Canada.
I received my commercial airplane pilot's license during this time as well.
Later on, I also had the opportunity to work as a tour guide and driver in Fairbanks, AK for the Princess Cruise Lines.
I have been a resident of the Bozeman community for the past twelve years and have worked with several of the long-standing businesses here, including First Student Transportation and Montana Whitewater Rafting.

Support policies that help working families afford to live in Gallatin County.

Senate District 31
About Montana Senate District 31
Senate District 31 is located in Gallatin County and includes the city of Bozeman, one of the fastest-growing communities in Montana.
The district encompasses a diverse mix of urban neighborhoods, suburban areas, and rural landscapes.
Home to Montana State University and a thriving local economy, District 31 represents a dynamic community focused on education, economic opportunity, housing affordability, and preserving the quality of life that makes Bozeman special.
