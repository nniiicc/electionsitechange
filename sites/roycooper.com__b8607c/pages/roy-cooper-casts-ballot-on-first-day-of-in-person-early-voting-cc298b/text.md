“I’m proud to have voted early and I encourage my fellow North Carolinians to make a plan to do the same,” said Roy Cooper.
“So much is at stake in this election.
Costs are rising across the board and everyday people in our state are hurting.
My opponent, Michael Whatley, is a Washington DC Insider and Big Oil and utility lobbyist who is running to boost his own stock portfolio and help his billionaire buddies get even further ahead while working families struggle.
I’m ready to take some North Carolina common sense to Washington and work with anyone I can to make stuff cost less.” North Carolinians can vote early in the general election in-person from now until 3 PM on Saturday, October 31.
