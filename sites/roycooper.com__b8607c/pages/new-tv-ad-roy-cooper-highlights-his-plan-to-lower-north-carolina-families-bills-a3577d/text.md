“Roy Cooper knows that big corporations and Washington DC insiders like Michael Whatley are enriching themselves while your bills skyrocket,” said Cooper for North Carolina campaign manager Jeff Allen .
“Whatley spent his career lobbying for companies like Duke Energy as they hiked rates, but Roy is running to make stuff cost less, and in Washington DC, he will take on the big corporations making life more expensive for hardworking families.” The ad will run on broadcast television, streaming and digital platforms statewide as part of a seven-figure buy.
Read a transcript of “ Bills ” below: Roy Cooper: You ever get a bill and think, how did this get so high?
Health insurance, electric bills.
Companies get more while you get less.
I’m Roy Cooper.
In the Senate, I’ll fight insurance companies that send you big bills you don’t expect and deny care a doctor says you need.
Take on utility companies jacking up your rates and work to stop this war and big oil companies driving up gas prices.
I approve this message because we’ve had enough of corporate lobbyists and greedy politicians in Washington.
It’s time for a change.
Roy has been laser focused on lowering costs – you can read all of his plans to lower high prices for working families at RoyCooper.com/CostLess/ .
As Governor, Roy assembled a bipartisan coalition to bring affordable health care to hundreds of thousands of working people through Medicaid expansion, lowering costs for everyone.
Roy’s Make Stuff Cost Less plans ban insurance companies from sending people big bills they don’t expect.
Roy will work to lower utility bills by ensuring that corporations – not you – pay for energy used, including making sure data centers pay the full cost of all the power they use and preventing that cost from being passed on to families.
Michael Whatley is running for Senate to continue to enrich himself, while North Carolinians are dealing with an affordability crisis: Whatley led the charge with Washington Republicans to double the cost of health insurance premiums while he personally profited .
As Duke Energy threatens further rate hikes on North Carolina families, Whatley – who has over $400,000 invested in Duke Energy and would see “boost[ed] profits” – has refused to take a stance.
Whatley continuously pushed for the Iran war with no exit plan, which spiked his Big Oil stocks by up to $219,660 at the expense of North Carolina families who paid hundreds more at the pump and farmers who are struggling having to pay record diesel prices because of the Iran War.
