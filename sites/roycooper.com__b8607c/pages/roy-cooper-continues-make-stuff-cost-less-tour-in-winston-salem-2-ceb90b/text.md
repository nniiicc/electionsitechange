Senate to make life more affordable for North Carolina families.
“As hardworking families in Winston-Salem and around the state pay more at the gas pump and on their energy bills, Washington DC insider Michael Whatley profits off his oil and utility stocks,” said Roy Cooper .
“As a career Big Oil and utility company lobbyist, Michael Whatley has always put his bottom line first.
I am running to make stuff cost less and will work with anyone in Washington to deliver for North Carolina families.” “For four generations my family has grown tobacco in the Piedmont Triad but because of the chaos coming out of Washington DC, times have never been tougher,” said Rockingham County and fourth generation farmer John Ashe .
“From diesel to tariffs, Washington DC insider Michael Whatley’s policies are hurting my family.
Roy Cooper knows this chaos just isn’t right.
In the U.S.
Senate, I know Roy will fight to make stuff cost less for hardworking farmers and families.” “I love my community and right now, it is hurting as prices skyrocket,” said Winston-Salem realtor McKenzie Ziegler .
“We see it at the grocery store, at the gas pump, on the utility bill.
Washington DC insider Michael Whatley is profiting at our expense – and we just can’t trust him to fight for us.
As Governor, Roy Cooper delivered for working families.
We need him in the U.S.
Senate to continue to deliver for us, to continue fighting to make life more affordable.” On the Make Stuff Cost Less tour, Roy released his plans to lower the cost of food and groceries , health care , utilities , child care and housing .
