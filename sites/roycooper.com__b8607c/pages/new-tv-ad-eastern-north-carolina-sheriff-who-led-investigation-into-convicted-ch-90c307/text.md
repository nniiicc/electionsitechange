“Harvey Lee West was a convicted child sex predator who spent six years in prison for his crimes and yet, Michael Whatley knowingly championed him and gave West a top political role with access to children,” said Cooper for North Carolina Campaign Manager Jeff Allen .
“Whatley is out for himself and will stop at nothing to benefit himself.
Roy Cooper is the only candidate in this race who has put violent criminals, rapists and child sex offenders behind bars and kept them there.” On the campaign trail, Whatley continues to embrace predators, campaigning with a convicted child rapist , members of a cult accused of sexually abusing children and at a church that protected a child sex abuser.
The ad will run on broadcast television, streaming and digital platforms statewide as part of a seven-figure buy.
Read a transcript of “Led” below.
Roy Cooper: I’m Roy Cooper and I approve this message.
Former Beaufort County Sheriff Alan Jordan: I was the sheriff in Beaufort County for 16 years.
I led the investigation into Harvey Lee West.
He spent six years in prison for sexually abusing kids.
He’s a monster, but Michael Whatley – he put him in a senior leadership role anyway, even though he knew he was a convicted sex offender.
Whatley even let West attend events with little kids.
I put predators like Harvey Lee West in jail.
Michael Whatley put one on his team.
Michael Whatley has a disturbing pattern of campaigning with and elevating child sex predators, including: Appointing Harvey West, who spent six years in prison for sexually abusing three minors to NCGOP leadership.
Campaigning with Daimon King, a registered lifetime sex offender who spent years in prison on two separate occasions for child sex crimes, including rape and incest.
Attributing his rise in politics to the Rutherford County GOP, which has been “taken over” by the Word of Faith Fellowship – a cult that has been accused of physically and sexually abusing children.
Campaigning at Victory Baptist Church, which reportedly covered up for and protected an employee who was indicted for sexually abusing children for over a decade.
