Protecting Democracy
- HB 664 Judge Andrew F.
Wilkinson Judicial Security Act protects judges and other protected individuals by allowing them to have their personal information removed from the Internet and request sensitive information not be published online.
- HB 333 Election Law - Election Disinformation and Improper Influence Related to Voting requires the State Board of Elections to maintain a portal on its website where the public can report election disinformation and periodically review the submissions and refer any relevant submissions to the State Prosecutor.
- HB 585 Protecting Election Officials Act of 2024 prohibits someone from threatening an election official, or their family member, in connection with their work in elections.
- SJ 1 Affirming the Federal Equal Rights Amendment resolves that the Maryland General Assembly affirms the federal Equal Rights Amendment is the 28th Amendment to the Constitution.
The resolution urges the Biden Administration to publish the federal Equal Rights Amendment as the 28th Amendment to the U.S.
Constitution and the U.S.
Congress to pass a joint resolution affirmation.
