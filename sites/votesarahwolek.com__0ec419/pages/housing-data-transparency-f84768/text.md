It also allows for municipalities to voluntarily report this information.
MDP will then make this information centrally and easily accessible to all on an interactive and searchable website.
Stakeholders (residents, advocates, builders, etc.) can then better understand Maryland's housing needs and be equipped to propose informed, data-driven solutions that can be implemented with a broad coalition of support.
