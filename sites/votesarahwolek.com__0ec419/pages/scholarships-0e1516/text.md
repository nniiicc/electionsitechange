To be eligible for Delegate Wolek’s Legislative Scholarship, you must: Live in the 16th Legislative District.
You may confirm that your permanent residence is in District 16 here .
Have completed the FAFSA .
Attend or have been accepted by a Maryland college or university, graduate school, or private career (or vocational) school either full or part-time.
OR attend or have been accepted by an out-of-state school and have been approved for a "unique major" not offered by ANY other Maryland institution as determined by MHEC To apply, complete this application . ( Allow 8 weeks .) Questions?
Email Sarah.Wolek@house.maryland.gov .
