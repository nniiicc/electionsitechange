Legislative Scholarship
As a Delegate, Sarah Wolek is able to award scholarships to District 16 residents pursuing further education and is excited to support the academic goals of D16 residents.
To be eligible for Delegate Wolek’s Legislative Scholarship, you must:
- Live in the 16th Legislative District.
You may confirm that your permanent residence is in District 16 here.
- Have completed the FAFSA.
- Attend or have been accepted by a Maryland college or university, graduate school, or private career (or vocational) school either full or part-time.
OR attend or have been accepted by an out-of-state school and have been approved for a "unique major" not offered by ANY other Maryland institution as determined by MHEC
To apply, complete this application.
(Allow 8 weeks.)
