Cosponsored and Supported Legislation
Read below to learn more about some of the notable bills I cosponsored and voted for that became law.
- Federal Workforce Protections Maryland and the federal government are intrinsically linked.
Reductions in force, DOGE, and priority changes have impacted Marylanders.
We must protect our residents and their families.
- Economic and Workforce Development Improving processes, supporting businesses of all sizes, and strengthening worker rights and benefits are all essential to our state’s economic development.
- Health Care Health care is a human right.
Increasing access to health care, protecting reproductive rights, and lowering prescription drug prices ensure Marylanders have access to the care they need to maintain and improve their health.
- Environment and Energy As energy demands increase, we cannot overlook the environmental impact that meeting those needs may have.
Being good environmental stewards is the only way to ensure earth’s future for our children.
- Transportation Transportation infrastructure is essential to ensuring people get where they need to be safely.
We must continue improving our transit systems to ensure access and safety for pedestrians, cyclists, drivers, and metro and bus riders.
- Protecting Democracy Everyone who lives in Maryland deserves equal dignity and opportunities.
Especially when our basic rights are being infringed upon, we must do everything we can to maintain the integrity of our democracy.
- Education Our children are our future and their education is of utmost importance.
Investing in our school systems and early childhood education sets our young people up for a life of success.
- Housing We need housing that fits the needs of our communities - affordable and energy efficient.
- Immigration Our diversity is one of our greatest assets.
At a time when immigrants are being vilified, it is more important than ever to protect our community members.
- Gun Safety We have to make common sense gun reforms because no one should live in fear of gun violence.
- Consumer Protection We must protect consumers from unfair, deceptive, and fraudulent business practices while maintaining a competitive marketplace.
