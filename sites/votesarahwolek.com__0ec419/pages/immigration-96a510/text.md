Immigration
- HB 1222 Public Safety - Immigration Enforcement (Maryland Values Act) requires federal law enforcement officers to notify certain individuals of federal immigration actions at sensitive locations and prohibits those sensitive locations from allowing certain federal personnel to enter spaces not accessible to the general public.
The bill also requires the Attorney General to publish guidelines regarding immigration enforcement at sensitive locations.
- SB 608 Criminal Procedure - U Nonimmigrant Status Petitions alters the requirements for certification of a U Nonimmigrant Status Petition in a way that aims to improve safeguards for undocumented immigrants who are victims of crime and are helpful in the investigation and prosecution of criminal activity.
