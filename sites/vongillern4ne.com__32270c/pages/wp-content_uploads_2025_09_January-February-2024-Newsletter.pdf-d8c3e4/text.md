Brad von Gillern Below is a list of the bills I introduced this year and a summary of what each does.
LB807 makes Nebraska a state that only requires a single mounted license plate while reducing the fee from $50 to $5.
The bill is also resourceful in reducing waste during this time of an aluminum shortage.
LB1023 introduced this session as an important measure for research and development in the state.
The Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) that temporarily eliminated the factory tax by creating the immediate cost recovery for investments in business machinery, equipment, and software.
This bill extends that act.
Not only does this help established Nebraska companies but is critical to startups that have a short period to recover costs and have returns on their investments.
LB1134 When the Tax Equalization and Review Commission (TERC) has determined that a property should be valued at a higher amount, the taxpayer may owe additional taxes.
LB1134 would give the taxpayer a 30-day grace period to pay the additional taxes before interest starts to accrue.
LB1172 would place an eight week reporting period requirement on the State Cancer Registry Database.
Currently requests from researchers are taking over a year to process.
This is unacceptable and is putting valuable research funding and findings at risk.
LB1177 clarifies the intent of the legislature in adopting the existing exemption from paying a documentary stamp tax for certain types of real estate transactions that occur between and among family members when actual consideration is not exchanged.
These are typically agricultural properties.
LB803 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act to add joint entities formed under the Interlocal Cooperation Act that include a city, village, or county, and limits the transfer of sales tax funds if the facility is a sports complex to the effective date of the Act.
LB1241 mandates any increase in property value will be offset by an equal reduction in levy by local taxing authorities.
The idea is to eliminate the “windfall” effect of rising property values that unfairly burden taxpayers.
LB1295 aims to establish a data match system between the Department of Revenue and financial institutions, facilitating the identification of tax debtors.
LB1307 mandates the Director of Insurance to identify any required health benefit exceeding the essential health benefits outlined in federal law so the increase in cost to the state may be clearly understood by all parties.
LB1308 aims to eliminate the sales tax exemption for business to business accounting services and agricultural repair parts.
LR286CA offers a Constitutional amendment that would be placed on the ballot to prohibit the State of Nebraska from contracting with or investing in companies that have active business operations with any foreign terrorist organization or state sponsor of terrorism.
