​Hello Neighbors,​ ​This week features part two of the bills I have introduced for this session.
Altogether I have​ ​introduced 13 bills.
Here is the summary of the last seven.
This list includes my priority bill​ ​LB805.
In addition to that summary I have shared why that bill is important and why I have​ ​made it my priority designation for this year.​ ​LB491 In 2005 the Nebraska Legislature passed the Nebraska Advantage Research and​ ​Development Act.
The program sunsetted in 2022.
LB491 would renew the program, and modify​ ​e-verify compliance issues, and remove the 20 year cap on eligibility for the program.​ ​LB492 The Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) temporarily eliminated the factory tax by creating the​ ​immediate cost recovery for investments in business machinery and equipment.
This provision​ ​expired under federal tax law beginning January 1, 2023, reverting Nebraska businesses to​ ​amortizing these costs, effectively raising taxes on critical investments needed to grow​ ​Nebraska’s economy and invest in our workforce.
LB492 will allow this provision to remain in​ ​place.
This is not a tax cut or increase as it will remain in the state tax baseline.
However, it will​ ​allow for Nebraska to remain competitive for new capital investments.​ ​LB803 modifies and clarifies the ownership structure of the Sports Arena Financing Act.
It​ ​permits interlocal agencies to receive turnback tax appropriations from the state.​ ​LB804 would cut the top corporate income tax rate to 5.84% by taxable years beginning or​ ​deemed to begin on or after January 1, 2023.
This is one of two bills I introduced on behalf of​ ​Governor Pillen.​ ​LB805 intends to promote character development in the state educational system by providing​ ​youth organizations as listed in Federal Code Title 36, Subtitle II, Part B access to public​ ​schools.
I have made this my personal priority bill for the session because I have seen certain​ ​youth organizations being unfairly treated.
Nearly 100,000 Nebraska students are being denied​ ​information access to organizations like the Boy Scouts.
This bill would simply allow​ ​organizations like Big Brothers and Big Sisters, Girl Scouts, and FFA to have the opportunity for​ ​a one time informational access into our schools during non curriculum time each year.
These​ ​groups foster character development and have a longstanding tradition as being cornerstones in​ ​our communities.​ ​LB806 would cut the top individual income tax rate to 5.84% by taxable years beginning or​ ​deemed to begin on or after January 1, 2023.
This is the second bill I introduced on behalf of​ ​Governor Pillen.​ ​LB807 will make one single rear mounted license plate the requirement for all registered​ ​vehicles in the state of Nebraska.
The concept is to conserve aluminum during a time of a​ ​shortage, cut down on costs, and eliminate a headache for automobile owners.​
