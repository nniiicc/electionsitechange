Brad von Gillern​ ​Below is a list of bills I introduced in 2024 that are moving through the legislative process:​ ​LB1023 introduced this session as an important measure for research and development in the state.
The Tax​ ​Cuts and Jobs Act (TCJA) that temporarily eliminated the factory tax by creating the immediate cost recovery​ ​for investments in business machinery, equipment, and software.
This bill extends that act.
Not only does​ ​this help established Nebraska companies but is critical to startups that have a short period to recover costs​ ​and have returns on their investments.
This bill is my Priority Bill for 2024 and will be a powerful economic​ ​incentive for businesses in our state.​ ​LB1134​​When the Tax Equalization and Review Commission​​(TERC) has determined that a property should​ ​be valued at a higher amount, the taxpayer may owe additional taxes.
LB1134 would give the taxpayer a​ ​30-day grace period to pay the additional taxes before interest starts to accrue.
The bill is currently on​ ​General File.​ ​LB1172 would place an eight week reporting period requirement on the State Cancer Registry Database.​ ​There’s been delays in recent years with data being released to researchers.
The delays are putting valuable​ ​research funding and findings at risk.
This past week I gathered DHHS CEO Dr.
Steve Corsi, data experts​ ​from DHHS, along with representatives from UNMC and Creighton to identify the issues slowing down the​ ​registry and find solutions to these problems.
We made some short term goals that can be implemented this​ ​year and also drafted an interim study for long term goals for DHHS and the registry.​ ​LB1177 clarifies the intent of the legislature in adopting the existing exemption from paying a documentary​ ​stamp tax for certain types of real estate transactions that occur between and among family members when​ ​actual consideration is not exchanged.
These are typically agricultural properties.
This bill has been placed​ ​on General File with a Speaker Consent designation.
A Consent Calendar bill “fast tracks” the progress of​ ​the bill as it’s deemed legislation without opposition.​ ​LB1197 amends the Sports Arena Facility Financing Assistance Act to add joint entities formed under the​ ​Interlocal Cooperation Act that include a city, village, or county, and limits the transfer of sales tax funds if the​ ​facility is a sports complex to the effective date of the Act.
This will be a powerful development tool for​ ​Nebraska communities.
At the hearing for the bill Nebraska Volleyball Coach John Cook submitted a letter​ ​stating the importance of this bill as Nebraska’s capacity for youth sports is lower than neighboring states​ ​and this is a quality of life issue we need to address.​ ​LB1241 mandates any increase in property value will be offset by an equal reduction in levy by local taxing​ ​authorities.
The idea is to eliminate the “windfall” effect of rising property values that unfairly burden​ ​taxpayers.​ ​LR286CA offers a Constitutional amendment that would be placed on the ballot to prohibit the State of​ ​Nebraska from contracting with or investing in companies that have active business operations with any​ ​foreign terrorist organization or state sponsor of terrorism.
Last fall I issued a resolution signed by 39 other​ ​State Senators condemning Hamas and declaring Nebraska’s support for Israel.
I believe it’s important we​ ​send a message that such evil will not be tolerated.​ ​Check out my Facebook updates for all the latest on these bills and others I have introduced.
