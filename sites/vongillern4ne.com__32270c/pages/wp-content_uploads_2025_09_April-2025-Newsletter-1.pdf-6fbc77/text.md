This April, I also joined my colleagues to speak up on your behalf against a push to expand gambling in its most predatory form in Nebraska through the legalization of online sports betting.
As a result of the efforts of a number of other senators, we successfully headed off a proposal that would have allowed harmful online gambling schemes into our state.
While speaking on the floor, I presented facts demonstrating the very real and immense social costs of this type of gambling where it has been approved in other states.
As chair of the Revenue Committee, I have also had the responsibility to advance several major committee bills through the Legislature process, including: 1.
LB 649 - Designed to draw high-paying defense-contractor jobs to Nebraska in coming years.
Given the currently-shifting regulatory and cost-cutting climate in Washington, my colleagues and I are striving to make Nebraska a more attractive place for these industries to do business.
2.
LB 650 - Designed to pare back recently-enacted and under-used tax incentive programs in order to boost revenue to the state and help balance our budget.
These bills have engendered lively and productive debate concerning our state's fiscal and budgetary strategies.
The Revenue Committee and I are confident that these bills are important and will take Nebraska in the right direction, especially considering the most recent downward adjustment to our revenue forecasts reported last week.
