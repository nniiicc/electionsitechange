​Hello Neighbors,​ ​The legislature reached day 35 of the session this week.
Currently progress is slow as the body​ ​is being filibustered by several Democrat senators.
Until the filibuster is over a number of​ ​important issues won’t be discussed.
I am hopeful we’ll soon be able to move on to debate​ ​involving important issues to you and the rest of the state.
Some of those issues include bills I​ ​have introduced.
For this week’s update I wanted to provide you with a summary of the​ ​legislation I have introduced in 2023.
I’ll cover these bills in two parts this week, and in next​ ​week’s update.​ ​LB205 creates the Government Neutrality in Contracting Act.
The purpose of the Act is to​ ​increase efficiency and level the playing field for purchasing goods and services by government​ ​units for project bids.
The hearing for this bill was in Government Committee was on February​ ​10th.​ ​LB206 seeks to allow partnerships filing Nebraska partnership tax returns to make an election to​ ​pay Nebraska income tax directly when an amended return is filed, rather than “passing​ ​through” the tax liability to its partners.
Under Nebraska’s current law, amended income tax​ ​returns would require a K-1N form for each partnership to be forwarded out to partners.
In turn,​ ​partners that are “flowthrough” entities would amend their returns and send out additional​ ​K-1Ns.
This bill is to simply streamline a filing process.
The hearing was January 25th in the​ ​Revenue Committee.
The bill is now on General File.​ ​LB207 was heard in Banking, Commerce, and Insurance Committee on January 23rd.
The bill​ ​has since been placed on General File passing to Select File by a vote of 33 for, 0 against, and​ ​2 present not voting.
The bill clarifies that a sale of property by a trustee pursuant to a power of​ ​sale under a trust deed may be conducted, in any public building in which county offices are​ ​located.
There have been some disputes across the state involving such sales when county​ ​offices are undergoing improvements and have temporarily relocated to a different location.​ ​LB317 will provide county assessors more flexibility with property inspections.
It would make​ ​possible the action of virtual assessments.
The intent is to provide savings to counties and​ ​taxpayers by cutting unnecessary inspection time and travel costs.
The bill was heard in the​ ​Revenue Committee on February 16th.​ ​LB489 defines employment status of individuals engaged in the Marketplace Network Platform.​ ​These are workers engaged in services utilizing tech applications such as Uber and DoorDash.​ ​The bill specifies the nature of their employment as independent contractors.
This bill has a​ ​hearing date on March 13th in the Business and Labor Committee.​ ​LB490 appropriates federal funds of $5,000,000 for fiscal years 2023-24 and 2024-25.
These​ ​funds are to be used for post COVID 19 research, post COVID 19 clinics, expanded mental​ ​health services for care of COVID 19 patients, and educational materials related to COVID 19.​ ​The hearing on LB490 will be in Appropriations on March 24th.​
