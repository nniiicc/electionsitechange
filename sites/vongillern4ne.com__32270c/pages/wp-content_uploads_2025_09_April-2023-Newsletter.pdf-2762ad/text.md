Below is a list of some of the bills I introduced this year.​ ​LB805 - Promotes character development in the state educational system by providing the youth​ ​organizations listed in Federal Code Title 36, Subtitle II, Part B access to schools.
These groups include​ ​the Girl Scouts, Boy Scouts, Big Brothers and Big Sisters, TeamMates, FFA, and Mentor Nebraska.
I​ ​designated LB805 as my priority bill for this session.
I have worked closely with the Nebraska School​ ​Board Association, our local schools, and other organizations to find a solution that works best for all​ ​parties and allows these non profit organizations to make a positive impact with our youth and​ ​communities.
The bill is on General File and waiting for the first round of debate.​ ​LB754 - This bill is the Revenue Committee bill.
I introduced LB804 and LB806 on behalf of Governor​ ​Pillen.
These two bills were then amended into LB754.
It will cut the corporate income tax rate to 3.99%​ ​by taxable years beginning or deemed to begin on or after January 1, 2023.
It will make the individual​ ​income tax rate 5.84%.
A third bill I introduced, LB492, is also part of this package.
LB492 addresses the​ ​Tax Cuts and Jobs Act that temporarily eliminated the factory tax by creating the immediate cost recovery​ ​for investments in business machinery and equipment.
This bill renews the expired program preserving​ ​and creating high paying jobs.
LB754 is now on Select File, which is the second round of debate.​ ​LB490 - This bill will help hospitals like Madonna Rehabilitation Hospital continue to provide adequate​ ​care for those who are suffering from “long Covid” symptoms.
Currently the bill is still in committee.​
