Born and raised in Nebraska, Brad von Gillern has been a business leader in Omaha for decades.
In addition to serving as past President and CEO of Lueder Construction, he has worked in the community on over a dozen non-profit boards and is a leader in his church.
He is known throughout Omaha for his leadership, deep commitment to his endeavors, and transparency – traits that have served Nebraskans well in the Legislature.
Brad has been married to his high school sweetheart, Mary, since 1981.
They have four children and seven grandchildren.
