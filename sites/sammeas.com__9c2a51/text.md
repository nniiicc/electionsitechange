His journey from Cambodian refugee to successful American businessman and community leader shaped his unwavering commitment to serve.
1970s-1980s From Cambodia to America Born in Kandal Province, Cambodia, Sam lost his father to the Khmer Rouge and was separated from his mother and sisters during the war.
He fled to Thailand's Khao-I-Dang refugee camp before being sponsored by Catholic Charities USA and welcomed to America in 1986.
1980s-2000s Education & Career Sam graduated from Virginia Tech in 1996 with a degree in finance.
He worked at State Street Corporation, BlackRock, and Fidelity.
He owns a successful Real Estate Brokerage business and Tax Accountant Business in Lowell, Massachusetts.
His business experience taught him the challenges facing families and entrepreneurs.
2000s-Present Community Leadership Sam has been deeply involved in numerous community organizations throughout the district, including serving on the board of directors of the North Suffolk Mental Health Association.
He has dedicated himself to supporting cultural organizations, mental health services, and community wellbeing.
2025 Historic Candidacy Sam made history as the first Cambodian-American to run for U.S.
Congress.
He has consistently fought for the values that made America a beacon of hope: freedom, opportunity, and the promise that with hard work, anyone can succeed.
