The foreign policy we’re seeing isn’t “America First” – it’s playing directly into China and Russia’s hands while hurting our credibility at home and abroad.
Instead of defending our allies and strengthening NATO, we’re weakening our alliances all over the globe with insults and knee-jerk foreign policy decisions based on ego and social media instead of reality and solid intelligence.
And we’re spending billions of dollars on pointless foreign wars when we have many of our own problems to solve right here at home.
When democracy is attacked anywhere, it threatens democracy everywhere.
We need to show countries like China and Russia that we won’t support their attempts to take over other nations.
That means supporting Ukraine, protecting Taiwan’s sovereignty, and deescalating this War of Choice in Iran.
It also means we need to call out our own country’s actions.
I oppose illegal and unnecessary wars.
What’s happened in Iran and with the boats coming from Venezuela – and what this administration is hinting at with possible military action in Greenland and Cuba – is a dangerous abuse of power and violates international law.
Bare minimum, we need congressional oversight and our international allies to agree before we go to war.
