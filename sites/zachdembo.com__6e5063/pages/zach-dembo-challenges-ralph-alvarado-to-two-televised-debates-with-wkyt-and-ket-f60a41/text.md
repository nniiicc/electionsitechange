The challenge comes as multiple public polls show the race is locked in a dead heat.
Zach Dembo has formally accepted debate invitations from WKYT hosted by the League of Women Voters at the University of Kentucky’s Worsham Theatre, and by KET hosted out of their studio in Lexington, both of which are taking place in the 6th congressional district.
“I’ve spent my life showing up for Kentuckians, whether it was during my commission in the Navy, working alongside Governor Beshear, or prosecuting violent criminals.
Now I’m calling on my opponent Ralph Alvarado to show up for two televised debates, because voters deserve to see us side-by-side and learn the choice they have in this election,” said Zach Dembo .
“Kentuckians should hear directly from us on the affordability crisis crushing Kentucky families, this senseless war in Iran causing us all pain at the pump, our plans to make healthcare more affordable, and how we’ll root out the corruption plaguing Washington.
I’ll be there because I’ll always show up for Kentuckians – the only question is whether or not my opponent can say the same.” With less than # days to go, Dembo’s campaign is surging.
Despite Trump winning the district by 15 points two years before, three polls show the race neck-and-neck between Alvarado and Dembo.
The Dembo campaign awaits Alvarado’s response.
