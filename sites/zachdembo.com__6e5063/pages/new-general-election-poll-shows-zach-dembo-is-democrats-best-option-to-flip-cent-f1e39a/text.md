Zach is already tied with presumptive Republican Ralph Alvarado and beating Ryan Dotson by 5 points in the initial vote tally.
Once voters learn more about both candidates, Dembo beats both Republicans by the same margin: 46 to 39.
Former state representative Cherlynn Stevenson would lose in a direct match up to Alvarado by two points after voters heard positive profiles on both candidates.
“I got into this race to represent people of all backgrounds from Central Kentucky who have long been failed by politicians on both sides of the aisle,” says Zach Dembo.
“Voters are ready for a political outsider with experience that prepares them to fight the corruption out of D.C. head on from day one.
It’s been humbling to see how many folks are ready for a new face and new message from a first-time candidate who’s new to politics.” The poll also highlights Zach’s appeal as a veteran and prosecutor over a lifetime politician like Alvarado.
A majority of independent voters indicate they are more likely to vote for Zach based on his service to the country.
The poll was not commissioned by the campaign or the campaign’s polling firm and was fielded between April 24-25, 2026.

### Democratic candidate Zach Dembo is a ninth generation Kentuckian raised in Lexington, Kentucky.
From participating in the Teach For America program and serving as a United States Navy Judge Advocate to working as a federal prosecutor in the U.S.
Department of Justice’s Civil Rights Division and United States Attorney’s Office, Zach has dedicated his life towards public service and fighting for people, regardless of political party.
After seeing firsthand the corruption and politicization of the Department of Justice, he made the difficult decision to resign and step up to run to bring Central Kentucky’s voice to Congress.
