Skip to content Menu X-twitter Threads Instagram Facebook Youtube DONATE Endorsed by Governor Andy Beshear, former Lexington mayor Jim Gray, veterans, and teachers Commonsense Regulation for AI and Data Centers Right now, data centers are being built without enough community input or environmental oversight, and they’re exhausting our power grid in ways that drive up energy bills for working families.
Some are also polluting clean air and water and destroying fertile farmland.
That’s not progress – that’s passing the costs of corporate expansion onto the rest of us.
If a community doesn’t want a data center in their backyard, they should not be forced to have one, and state, local, and county leaders should be responsive to their community’s desires.
The first job of any elected official is to listen.
In Congress, I’ll push for legislation that requires transparency and local input in data center siting decisions, holds companies accountable for their environmental impact, and makes sure Big Tech can’t offload rising energy costs onto Kentucky families.
I also support commonsense AI safeguards to protect children interacting with chat bots and make sure the newest models don’t pose major risks to our cybersecurity.
I believe AI has real potential to improve lives – but it also comes with real risks and shouldn’t involve giving Big Tech a blank check.
AI should work for all of us, not just for the companies building it.
And as AI companies make billions, that prosperity has to be shared with the local communities without harming the community’s health or environment, or they should not be allowed in.
We should know if massive layoffs or displacements are happening because of AI and corporations should have to give notice before replacing employees with AI.
If this replacement happens, big tech and the companies that profit should be required to pay into a fund that helps retrain workers who lose their jobs to AI so workers don’t lose their livelihoods as our economy transitions into the AI age.
