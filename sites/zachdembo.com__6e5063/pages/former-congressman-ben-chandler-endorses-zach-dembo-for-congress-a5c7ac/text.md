Rep.
Chandler’s announcement comes the day after POLITICO Playbook published a PPP poll memo showing Zach Dembo as the best Democratic candidate in the race to defeat the Republicans.
Zach was joined by other endorsers Wednesday, including former Kentucky Transportation Secretary Jim Gray, former Kentucky Supreme Court Justice Mary Noble, former United States Attorney and Justice Cabinet Secretary Kerry Harvey, former Louisville Congressman Mike Ward, Vice President of the Kentucky Education Association Jessica Hiler, among others.
“In every role that Zach Dembo has been in, he has led with decency, character, and competence, all of which are needed to serve Kentucky in Congress,” said former Congressman Ben Chandler.
“Today, I’m coming out in full support of Zach Dembo because I firmly believe that Zach Dembo has the best shot to win the Sixth Congressional District and return a Democrat to represent Central Kentucky for the first time since I held it in 2012.
I typically don’t endorse, but I believe in Zach, and flipping the house and giving Central Kentucky the representation they deserve in Congress is too important to sit this one out and stay on the sidelines.” In addition to today’s announcement, Zach recently announced support from the Lexington Herald-Leader, former Transportation Secretary and mayor of Lexington Jim Gray, and the National Education Association.
Zach and his supporters have knocked on doors and made phone calls to thousands of Kentuckians across every county in the 6th district.
VoteVets, an organization that supports Democratic veterans, recently announced a $175,000 digital and cable buy to support the campaign.
In response to the news, Zach Dembo said the following: “As the last Democrat to hold this seat, Ben Chandler knows what it takes to win in Central Kentucky, and I’ve valued his guidance and friendship through my first run for office.
He’s been an invaluable mentor and trusted voice throughout this process, and I’m so proud he believes in the movement we’ve built from the ground up.
I am deeply honored to have his support.
With Ben in our corner, I know we have what it takes to win this primary in less than a week and return decency and common sense to how Central Kentucky is represented in D.C.” Ben Chandler served as the Congressman from Kentucky’s 6th congressional district from 2004 to 2013.
He previously served as the Attorney General of Kentucky from 1996 to 2004 and the State Auditor of Kentucky from 1992 through 1996.
