Inside Elections – Kentucky 6: New Poll Fuels Democratic Optimism By Inshara Ali July 10, 2026 “GOP former state Sen.
Ralph Alvarado led Democratic military veteran Zach Dembo 42-39 percent in a June 24-28 survey… with 19 percent either undecided or voting for a third-party candidate.
Alvarado had higher name ID, but also higher negatives.
He had a 21 percent positive/20 percent negative rating compared to 22 percent positive/12 percent negative for Dembo.
According to the memo, President Donald Trump is underwater by 7 points in a district where he finished ahead of Vice President Kamala Harris by 15 points in 2024 and by 11 points over Joe Biden in 2020.
In contrast, Democratic Gov.
Andy Beshear had a 58 percent favorable/36 percent unfavorable rating in the poll.” “Alvarado… ran as GOP Gov.
Matt Bevin’s running mate for lieutenant governor in 2019; the pair finished behind Beshear by 11 points in the 6th District en route to a statewide loss.
Alvarado later moved to Tennessee to serve as the state health department’s commissioner under GOP Gov.
Bill Lee and returned to Kentucky in 2025 to run for this seat.
Dembo, a former federal prosecutor and Navy JAG officer, is running his first campaign for office.
He has the backing of Beshear, for whom Dembo served as a policy advisor and legislative director… The University of Michigan Law graduate is making gains in the fundraising race with $625,000 raised in the second quarter, with nearly $500,000 cash on hand, according to the campaign.” “Overall, if Republicans are having to worry about defending Kentucky’s 6th, then the only question is how big the Democratic House majority will be next year.” Read more here and view the polling memo here .
ABOUT ZACH: Former prosecutor Zach Dembo is a ninth-generation Kentuckian raised in Lexington, Kentucky.
From participating in the Teach For America program and serving as a United States Navy Judge Advocate, to serving Governor Andy Beshear as his policy advisor and legislative director, to fighting crime as federal prosecutor in both D.C. and Lexington, Zach has dedicated his life to public service and fighting for people, regardless of political party.
After seeing firsthand the corruption and politicization of the Department of Justice, he made the difficult decision to resign and step up to run to bring Central Kentucky’s voice to Congress.
Since the primary election, Dembo has seen a surge in momentum, securing the endorsements of key groups and political figures, including Governor Andy Beshear, the AFL-CIO, Next 50, Council for a Livable World, and others.
