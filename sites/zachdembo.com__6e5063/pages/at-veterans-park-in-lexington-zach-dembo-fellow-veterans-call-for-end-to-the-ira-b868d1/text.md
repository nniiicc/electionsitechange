Meanwhile, Dembo’s opponent Ralph Alvarado hasn’t said a word aside from his previous statement that “he fully supports the President’s decisive action” that is raising costs on Kentucky families, ignoring the Dembo campaign’s multiple calls for him to denounce it.
In addition to his remarks at the presser earlier, Zach Dembo put out the following statement: “Families in Central Kentucky and our brave service members are paying the price of the war in Iran,” said former Navy JAG and congressional candidate Zach Dembo.
“People in this district do not want another forever war in the Middle East, especially one that has dragged on for more than seven months with no end in sight.
As this conflict continues, gas, diesel, and fertilizer costs have skyrocketed, putting even more pressure on working families.
Meanwhile, billionaires are profiting from the war.
It is yet another example of a corrupt Washington that puts wealthy special interests ahead of the hardworking Americans who are left footing the bill.” Zach stood with fellow veterans to call for an end to the Iran war as momentum continues to build behind his campaign.
Multiple public polls show that the race for Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District is locked in a dead heat, and non-partisan race raters have shifted the race in his direction citing the strength of his candidacy.
