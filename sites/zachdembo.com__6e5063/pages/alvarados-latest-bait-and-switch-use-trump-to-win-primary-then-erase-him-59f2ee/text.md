Republican Nominee Scrubs Mentions of President From Website as Polls Show Him Tied with Democrat Zach Dembo
LEXINGTON, KY – Today, NBC News reported that Ralph Alvarado, the Republican nominee for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district, has scrubbed his campaign website as he scrambles to try and paper over his real record.
NBC News reports that “Ralph Alvarado once called himself an ‘America First fighter’ and a ‘vocal supporter’ of Trump on his site” – but has now wiped away every single mention of Trump.
Throughout his career in politics – and most recently ahead of the contested May 2026 Republican primary for the district – Ralph Alvarado ran his campaign closely aligned with President Trump, touting his endorsement and taking positions that include fully backing Trump’s war in Iran and tariffs.
While Trump won KY-06 by 15 points in 2024, Alvarado’s campaign’s website overhaul comes as three public polls show a tied race and the president underwater in the district.
Alvarado, who has run for office as a Republican six times in Kentucky – including statewide as disgraced Governor Matt Bevin’s running mate in 2019 – has changed key messaging on his website from “America First Fighter,” to “Ralph Alvarado – Independent Leadership for Kentucky.”

“My opponent is a classic politician.

NBC News: Trump-backed House candidate removes mentions of the president from his website
By Sahil Kapur | August 25, 2026
- Ralph Alvarado is running with Donald Trump’s endorsement in a House district Kentucky that the president carried by double digits in 2024.
But since winning the Republican primary in May, he has removed a series of pro-Trump messages and a photo of them standing together from his campaign website, according to an NBC News review.
- Previously, the homepage of Alvarado’s site trumpeted his support for Trump, painting himself as an “AMERICA FIRST FIGHTER” in the state’s 6th Congressional District
- “President Trump needs more allies in Congress to deliver on his bold vision to Make America Great Again.
With your help, I will be that fighter,” Alvarado, a physician and former state senator, wrote on his website.
- Further down, the website displayed a photo of Alvarado and Trump together, with both of them smiling and the president flashing a thumbs-up.
Alvarado won the GOP primary on May 19 for the seat being vacated by Rep.
Andy Barr, a Republican, as he runs for the Senate.
- But since then, Alvarado’s message has shifted.
- Where Alvarado’s website used to call him an “AMERICA FIRST FIGHTER,” it now says he would offer “INDEPENDENT LEADERSHIP” for Kentucky, according to an earlier version of the website captured by the Internet Archive.
- The caption that promised he’d deliver on Trump’s MAGA vision has been replaced by this paragraph: “Healthcare isn’t a talking point to me — it’s been my life’s work.
In Congress, I’ll work with both parties to fight for affordable healthcare, lower costs, and practical solutions that help Kentucky families.”
- The photo of Alvarado and Trump together is now gone, replaced by a shot of just Alvarado.
- An earlier biography on the campaign website labeled Alvarado “one of President Trump’s earliest supporters” and touted his 2016 distinction as “the only Latino” to speak in support of Trump at that year’s Republican convention.
It described Alvarado as “a national leader in the America First movement.”
- Now, all five mentions of Trump that used to be on Alvarado’s homepage have been deleted.
His revised website makes no reference to the president or to “America First” or the “Make America Great Again” movement.
- A publicly archived version of Alvarado’s website shows that he still had the mentions and photo with Trump up through at least June 9, after he won the primary, meaning the changes were made after the general election campaign had begun.
- Alvarado’s campaign didn’t return messages seeking comment.
- The pivot comes amid a difficult political environment for Republicans as the midterm elections quickly approach.
Trump’s approval ratings have sagged nationally as voters have soured on his handling of the economy and the Iran war.
- Alvarado is running in a district that Trump carried by 15 points in 2024, according to an NBC News Decision Desk analysis.
- Trump gave his “Complete and Total Endorsement” to Alvarado for the House seat just ahead of the May 19 primary, saying on Truth Social that he is an “America First Patriot” and a “true friend to MAGA.”
- Alvarado will face Democrat Zach Dembo, a former federal prosecutor and Navy JAG officer, in the general election.
Last week, the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee added Dembo to its “red to blue” list, a signal to donors and party activists that it believes the race is winnable and worth investing in as Democrats vie for a House majority.
