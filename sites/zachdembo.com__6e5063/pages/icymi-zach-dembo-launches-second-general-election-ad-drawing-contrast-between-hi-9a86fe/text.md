WATCH “ STOOD BY ” HERE.
TRANSCRIPT: VO: Zach Dembo served his country in the Navy, and put violent criminals behind bars at the Justice Department.
Ralph Alvarado?
He served Matt Bevin as his running mate.
Backed his attacks on seniors, teachers, and Medicaid, and even stood with Bevin when he pardoned hundreds of criminals, including a child rapist.
And that’s the difference.
Zach Dembo put the bad guys in jail.
Ralph Alvarado stood by while they walked out. – At the Department of Justice, Zach Dembo served as a prosecutor fighting to get justice for victims of crime, getting tough convictions and helping deport violent undocumented immigrants to keep all of us safe in Central Kentucky.
Meanwhile, his opponent Ralph Alvarado stood behind his former running mate, disgraced ex-Governor Matt Bevin, even when he pardoned criminals.
Alvarado was also named as the beneficiary of a Medicare fraud scheme and served as the Medical Director of nursing homes that were sued multiple times for medical malpractice and wrongful death while he headed the Tennessee’s Department of Health.
“Time and time again, Ralph Alvarado has put himself first at the expense of Central Kentuckians.
While Zach Dembo put service over self as a Navy veteran and federal prosecutor, Alvarado stood by when his former running mate Matt Bevin pardoned convicted criminals and attacked Medicaid and seniors,” said Olivia Dupree, Zach Dembo’s campaign manager.
“That’s the choice that Central Kentuckians have in this race: a servant leader in Zach Dembo, or a corrupt career politician in Ralph Alvarado who is willing to stand by and sell us out if he thinks it’s beneficial to himself.” ### Let's Go!
