Dembo is a former prosecutor who has locked up violent criminals; Alvarado is attached at the hip to behind-bars Bevin
Lexington, KY – Yesterday, former Governor Matt Bevin was booked into jail after being arrested in Bourbon County.
Bevin – who Ralph Alvarado ran alongside as his 2019 gubernatorial running mate – was charged with contempt of court and slander resistance to the order during a dispute over a child support case, after repeatedly ignoring court orders to show up for court.
In response to the news, former federal prosecutor Zach Dembo released the following statement:
“In public service, character and the company you keep matters, and Ralph Alvarado’s running mate Matt Bevin has been arrested after violating the law.
As a former federal prosecutor, I held those in positions of power accountable to the fullest extent of the law.
“Alvarado, however, has shown a consistent disregard for the law like his running mate.
He has been implicated in a federal fraud case.
He passed laws designed to benefit his own bank account.
And now, his running mate, who had previously pardoned a murderer and child rapist while Alvarado said nothing, spent the night behind bars.
“Central Kentucky’s choice isn’t Democrat versus Republican.
It’s between a ninth generation Kentuckian who volunteered for the Navy and prosecuted federal crimes, and a Californian/Tennessean who views public office as a way to benefit himself and his jailbird running mate.
I have faith about what commonsense decision my fellow Kentuckians will make in two months.”
