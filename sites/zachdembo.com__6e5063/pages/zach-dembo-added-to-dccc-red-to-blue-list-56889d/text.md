Latest sign of momentum behind Dembo’s campaign comes as public polling shows the race tied up
LEXINGTON, KY – This morning, the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee (DCCC) added ninth generation Kentuckian, Navy JAG veteran, former federal prosecutor, and Democratic nominee for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district Zach Dembo to their Red to Blue designation, officially adding his name to the priority list of candidates who they believe can flip the House from Red to Blue.
Red to Blue is a highly competitive and battle-tested DCCC program that arms top-tier candidates with organizational and fundraising support to help them continue building winning campaigns.
Additionally, through Red to Blue the DCCC provides strategic guidance, staff resources, candidate trainings, fundraising support, and more.
This news comes as momentum surges for Team Dembo.
Despite Donald Trump winning the seat two years before by double digits, three public polls show a statistical tie in Kentucky’s 6th congressional district, with Trump 7 points underwater in Central Kentucky.
This seat hasn’t been added to the DCCC’s Red to Blue designation in several years, but Dembo – in addition to the tied polling – has clinched several major endorsements and has nearly $100,000 more on hand than his opponent as of the last public fundraising filing.
In response to the news, Zach Dembo released the following statement:
“People are struggling, and it’s because career politicians are more concerned with how they can line their own pockets and curry favor with corporate interests than help working people.
We’re running a campaign that speaks to the frustration people feel and offers real solutions that’ll help – from making health care accessible for everyone, reversing the disastrous tariffs and Medicaid cuts, and tackling corruption head on – and that’s how we’ll win in November.
It’s great the national party is taking notice, but I’m staying focused on helping working families here in Central Kentucky.”
