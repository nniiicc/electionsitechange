Lexington, KY – This morning, Serve America just announced that they have endorsed Zach Dembo for the Democratic nomination in Kentucky’s open 6th congressional district.
Serve America is an organization that supports Democratic public servants who run for office started by Rep.
Seth Moulton (MA-06).
They join a growing coalition of organizations and officials who support Dembo’s bid for Congress, including the Lexington Herald-Leader, the National Education Association, VoteVets, former Lexington Mayor and Transportation Secretary Jim Gray, and the last Democratic representative in KY-06 Ben Chandler.
“To take back the House and stop the chaos in Washington, we have to win across America,” said Rep.
Seth Moulten, founder of Serve America.
“I’ve spent my career arguing that we need a new generation of leaders in Congress who are willing to get things done, not cling to politics as usual or the status quo.
Zach is that candidate.”
In response to the news, Zach Dembo said the following:
“I’m truly humbled by Serve America’s endorsement, Congressman Moulton’s kind words and the incredible outpouring of support we’ve received these past few days.
As a fellow veteran, Seth built his career on the idea that public service is a calling, and that’s exactly what’s driving this campaign.
I’m grateful to have a fellow veteran like him in our corner, and I’m more energized than ever heading into the primary and to flip the Sixth come November.”
