Cook shifted their rating of the race from Solid Republican to Likely Republican, writing in part: “With Barr leaving his seat to run for Senate, however, Democrats have an outside chance at flipping this seat.
New polling commissioned by Democratic nominee Zach Dembo shows him down three points to GOP nominee Ralph Alvarado, 39%-42%.
Dembo winning about 40% of the vote is quite believable — but the question is whether or not he can win over enough undecided voters.
Dembo, a former federal prosecutor and Navy veteran, has demonstrated that he’s capable of running a strong campaign: he defeated a well-established former elected official in the primary.
And despite having a more competitive primary, he has slightly more cash on hand , $500,000 to Alvarado’s $400,000.
National Democratic operatives have been fairly bullish on this race from the beginning of the cycle, and House Majority PAC reserved $670,000 in ad time in this district.
Though Alvarado, a former state senator, remains the clear favorite, this could turn into a competitive race. ” Recent polling shows the race at a statistical tie .
Dembo has more cash on hand than his Republican opponent Ralph Alvarado and the seat is open for the first time in 20 years, giving Democrats a huge opportunity to send an independent and common sense Congressman to D.C.
In response to the news, Zach Dembo released the following statement: “We are running a competitive campaign in this open seat against a historically unpopular politician – and it’s getting noticed.
People across Central Kentucky are hurting right now as every part of life becomes more and more unaffordable, and they need someone who’s going to show up, prosecute the case against corruption, and fight for them regardless of what the polls, parties, or career politicians say.
That’s what this campaign has been about since day one, and it’s what we’re going to keep doing all the way through November.” Let's Go!
