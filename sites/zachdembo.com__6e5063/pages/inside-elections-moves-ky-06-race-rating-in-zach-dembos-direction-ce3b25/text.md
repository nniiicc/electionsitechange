Inside Elections shifted their rating from Solid Republican to Likely Republican, writing that: “A DCCC Analytics poll conducted July 29-30 found Democrat Zach Dembo and Republican Ralph Alvarado tied at 47 percent… [I]n this political environment, he’s making the race interesting.” This news comes off the heels of the release of public polling showing the race is tied, and after the DCCC included Zach Dembo in their coveted Red to Blue slate.
Dembo also has more cash on hand than his Republican opponent Ralph Alvarado as of the last public filing.
With the seat open for the first time in 20 years, Democrats have a huge opportunity to send an independent and common sense Congressman to D.C.
In response to the news, Zach Dembo released the following statement: “Folks all across Central Kentucky are struggling, and they’re looking for an independent voice and new leadership in Washington.
My opponent has only been in politics for two reasons: to line his own pockets and do his own party’s bidding.
We deserve better.
Voters want someone who will lower their costs, prosecute the case against corruption, and make healthcare accessible and affordable for everyone.
That’s what this race is about, and that’s what I intend to do regardless of what the polls, parties, or career politicians say.” Let's Go!
