The ratings shift comes as multiple public polls now show Zach Dembo taking a lead over opponent Ralph Alvarado.
Cook writes in part in their analysis : “Polling from both parties shows Democratic prosecutor Zach Dembo and former Republican state Sen.
Ralph Alvarado neck-and-neck — and some surveys even show Dembo in the lead.
“Bevin picked Alvarado as his running mate, and Democrats are now airing ads accusing Alvarado of being complicit when Bevin pardoned hundreds of criminals on his way out of office.
Bevin’s successor, Democratic Gov.
Andy Beshear, has performed quite well in this Lexington-based district, winning it by 20 points in 2023.” Cook’s rate change follows a series of updates from other national rate raters, citing the enthusiasm behind Zach and his competitive campaign.
The race raters also cited the lack of enthusiasm Republican voters have for Alvarado’s campaign and overall concerns with his close association with disgraced ex-Governor Matt Bevin.
Zach Dembo, former federal prosecutor and Navy veteran, released the following statement: “Our path to victory keeps getting clearer because this race is a contrast between public service and corruption.
People in Central Kentucky are tired of career politicians and know that if we want people in our government actually focused on bringing down costs, we have to send someone different to Washington.
“Political pundits are starting to realize what folks here in Central Kentucky have known for a while: this race is a toss-up.
Momentum is on our side and I will continue working to earn every vote so that we can shake up a broken Washington once we win.” ### Let's Go!
