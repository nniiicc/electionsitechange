Alvarado “fully support[s] and applaud[s]” Iran War LEXINGTON, KY – Today, 200 days since the start of this reckless war in Iran that has cost American servicemembers’ lives and raised prices for Kentucky families, Democratic nominee for Congress and Navy veteran Zach Dembo once again called for his opponent, Ralph Alvarado, to denounce this war that no one asked for.
Dembo was quick to condemn the war when it launched in February and has continued to vocally oppose the war as rising gas and diesel prices crush Kentucky farmers and families who are paying more as a result.
Yesterday, the nonpartisan Congressional Budget Office released its findings on the cost of combat operations against Iran, estimating that the conflict has cost American taxpayers $38 billion, depleted U.S. weapons stockpiles, including critical interceptors for air defenses, and contributed to higher than expected inflation.
Alvarado did not respond to Dembo’s call last month to denounce the war in Iran and has since been quiet on the matter after his previous statement saying: “I fully support and applaud” the decision to go to war in Iran on February 28.
Navy Veteran Zach Dembo released the following statement: “Central Kentuckians voted for no new wars two years ago, but this corrupt government has betrayed us, and now we’re stuck picking up the tab, which just hit $38 billion.
“All of us feel the pain at the grocery store and the gas pump, and – with diesel at a record high right when they need fuel the most as harvest ends – this war is devastating Kentucky’s farmers and cattlemen.
“Now, 200 long days later, our strategic objectives aren’t met, Iran controls the Strait of Hormuz, American lives – including several Kentuckians – have been lost, and my opponent won’t say a word.
This is the cost of corruption, and we deserve leadership that fights for us rather than shipping our hard-earned tax dollars overseas.” ### Let's Go!
