Lexington, KY – This afternoon, Kentucky Governor Andy Beshear has endorsed Zach Dembo for Congress.
“I got to work closely with Zach Dembo when he was in my administration and I assure you he’s the kind of person that will lead with conviction, character, and competence,” Governor Beshear said.
“As Kentuckians struggle with the rising cost of living, we need more public servants who will put people over their party and focus on getting things done for the great folks of our Commonwealth, and I know he’s the type of nominee that can flip this seat.”
Governor Beshear joins several in the growing coalition of Kentucky Democrats to support Zach Dembo’s bid for Congress, including Congressman Morgan McGarvey, former representatives Mike Ward and Ben Chandler, former Lexington mayor and Kentucky Transportation Secretary Jim Gray, and former KY-06 candidate Erin Petrey.
In response, Zach Dembo, Democratic nominee for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district, released the following statement:
“I’m so humbled to have the support of my former boss, Governor Andy Beshear, and I’m looking forward to collaborating with him to flip this seat,” said Zach Dembo, Democratic candidate for Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District.
“I often say this on the campaign trail but I think we win by following Andy’s example, focusing on the issues that matter, and finding real solutions that tackle the issues that get costs down and fight the rampant corruption we’re seeing in government.”
