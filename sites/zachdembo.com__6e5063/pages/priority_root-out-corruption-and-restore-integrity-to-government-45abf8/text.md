I resigned from my position as a federal prosecutor because I couldn’t in good conscience stay on as this administration turned the Department of Justice into the president’s personal law firm.
Since this administration took power, we’ve seen unprecedented corruption: a president accepting a $400 million private jet from a repressive monarchy, donors buying White House access through crypto coins, and weaponizing the justice system to go after the president’s political enemies and not the criminals.
But it’s not just this administration – Congress has been lining their own pockets for decades while failing the people who elected them.
We need real reform: ban stock trading for members of Congress so they can’t use non-public information to profit on Wall Street, impose a lifetime lobbying ban for former members, and get rid of dark money in politics.
Big business and the ultra-wealthy shouldn’t be able to buy influence in Washington.
We also need to take steps to make the Department of Justice completely independent from the whims of this president.
The enforcement of our nation’s laws should be apolitical, and we need to make sure Congress makes that independence the law by moving the Department of Justice out of the executive branch and protecting the Attorney General and his or her subordinates from the political vicissitudes of an increasingly chaotic White House.
I believe the Attorney General should be appointed for six year terms, and he or she should only be removable for cause.
I spent my career investigating and fighting corruption as a federal prosecutor.
In Congress, I’ll shine a light on corruption through proper oversight and accountability, and I’ll work to restore people’s faith that the government works for them, not the rich and powerful.
