Lucille comes from seven generations of farmers, with her children still working on Kentucky farms, so she knows firsthand that people are hurting.
Everything from gas, diesel, groceries, and healthcare have become more expensive over the past two years, and corrupt Washington politicians aren’t doing anything about it.
Zach’s listening to his grandma and is focusing on helping working families who are struggling to get by in Central Kentucky as Ralph Alvarado campaigns with the status quo politicians that brought us into this mess.

“Unlike his opponent, Zach Dembo is a public servant focused on helping Central Kentucky, not a partisan puppet,” said Olivia Dupree, Zach Dembo’s campaign manager.
“As everything from gas to groceries gets more expensive in the face of this administration’s actions and Congress’ cowardice, Zach will keep folks like his grandma, who spent her life farming, in mind and work with anyone to bring these costs down.
Ralph Alvarado, on the other hand, represents the same status quo that’s jacking up all of our costs and won’t lift a finger to keep working families.” Let's Go!
