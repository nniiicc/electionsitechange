According to the AAA , average gas prices in Kentucky spiked more than 23% from $3.11 to $4.05 as of Tuesday April 7.
Democratic frontrunner for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district Zach Dembo has been outspoken about the effects of the War of Choice in Iran and Donald Trump’s budget on people’s pocketbooks and our legislative priorities, pulling from his background as a Navy JAG veteran and descendent of nine generations of Kentucky farmers.
In response, Zach Dembo, Democratic candidate for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district and only veteran in the race, released the following statement: “People were struggling before.
Now, Kentuckians are already feeling the effects of this war at the gas pump, but I’m sorry to say it could get worse.
From helium fueling our semiconductors – the technology behind every piece of tech we have – to the fertilizer growing our crops and keeping our farmers in business, this war in Iran will have lasting reverberations across our economy.
While I hope the temporary cease fire means an end to putting our soldiers and sailors in harm’s way, we need to go farther and minimize the danger – economic, physical, or otherwise – of our troops, people, and pursue peace.” #### Democratic candidate Zach Dembo is a ninth generation Kentuckian raised in Lexington, Kentucky.
From participating in the Teach for America program and serving as a United States Navy Judge Advocate to working as a federal prosecutor in the U.S.
Department of Justice Civil Rights Division, Zach has dedicated his life towards public service and fighting for people, regardless of political party.
After seeing firsthand the corruption and politicization of the Department of Justice, he made the difficult decision to resign and step up to run to bring Central Kentucky’s voice to Congress.
