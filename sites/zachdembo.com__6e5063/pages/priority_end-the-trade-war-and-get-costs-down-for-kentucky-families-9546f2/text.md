Central Kentuckians are struggling to make ends meet.
Working families can’t afford groceries, and the middle class is getting squeezed while politicians in Washington do nothing about it.
Small businesses are the backbone of Central Kentucky, and they’re getting crushed by tariffs that are just taxes on our businesses and consumers.
Since this trade war started, we’ve seen retaliatory tariffs hit our markets, exports drop sharply, bourbon distillers shutter shops, and our farmers unable to sell their crops.
Plus, prices continue to skyrocket, with staples like beef and coffee reaching record highs and burdening hardworking families’ grocery budgets.
They’re also costing Kentucky families an extra $1300 per year, and have already been ruled unconstitutional by the Supreme Court.
My family has been farming in Kentucky since the mid-1700s.
Our farmers feed this country, but they’re getting left behind.
Tariffs are wrecking the soybean, cattle, and corn crops grown here in Central Kentucky.
We need to protect our farmers by protecting crop insurance, expanding water resources, reforming the tax code to protect family farms, and ensuring fair and open trade.
In Congress, I’ll fight to end this senseless trade war, support free and open trade that helps our farmers and distillers compete globally, and make sure Main Street can compete with Wall Street to lower prices for us all.
