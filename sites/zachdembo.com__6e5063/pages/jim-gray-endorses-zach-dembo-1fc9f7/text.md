Former Lexington Mayor and KY Transportation Sec. says Zach is ‘our best shot’ at flipping the Sixth Congressional District blue
Lexington, KY – Wednesday morning, Jim Gray endorsed and announced his full support for Zach Dembo for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district.
“I’m endorsing Zach because Central Kentuckians deserve a representative who will intentionally focus on our needs – and can build the coalition to win in November,” Jim Gray stated.
“Through our work with Governor Beshear, I saw firsthand that Zach has a public servant’s heart.
He’s genuine, thoughtful, responsible, and will do what’s right for Kentuckians in Congress, regardless of party lines,” Gray continued.
“It’s well beyond time we had someone represent Kentucky in Congress that’s dedicated to public service, and Zach is the only candidate running in either party who will intentionally focus on the people of Kentucky and not get distracted.
And for Democrats in May, voting for Zach is without any doubt our best possible chance of flipping the 6th district in November.”
With Gray’s endorsement and support, momentum is growing and the race now has a clear frontrunner – Zach Dembo.
In addition to today’s announcement, Zach recently announced the non-partisan endorsement of Foreign Policy for America and fundraising results that lead all candidates – Democratic and Republican.
Zach and his supporters have knocked on doors and made phone calls to thousands of Kentuckians across every county in the 6th district.
And Zach’s campaign is the only Democrat sharing his message on TV, digital, and cable.
In response to the news, Zach Dembo said the following:
“Jim Gray is one of the finest public servants we’ve known in Central Kentucky.
From his historic service to my hometown of Lexington as Vice-Mayor and Mayor, to his amazing work as Secretary of Kentucky’s Transportation Cabinet, Jim has exemplified the kind of public service I aspire to pursue.
I had the privilege of getting to know Jim in 2020 when we both joined Team Kentucky working for Governor Andy Beshear, and what struck me most was how much he loves this Commonwealth and the people he worked for: you.
His endorsement means the world to me.
Jim, thank you.
Less than four weeks to go, and the coalition is growing.
Let’s finish strong and win in May and November.”
Jim Gray was the Secretary of the Kentucky Transportation Cabinet for Governor Andy Beshear, and served as Mayor of Lexington from 2011 to 2018.
