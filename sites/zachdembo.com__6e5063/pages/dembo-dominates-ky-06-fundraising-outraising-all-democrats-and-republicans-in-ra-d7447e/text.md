These numbers send a clear message: Central Kentucky is ready for new leadership,” Zach Dembo, frontrunner for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district.

“In less than five weeks, it all comes together.

Thank you to every single donor who was a part of making this happen.” #### Democratic candidate Zach Dembo is a ninth generation Kentuckian raised in Lexington, Kentucky.
From participating in the Teach for America program and serving as a United States Navy Judge Advocate to working as a federal prosecutor in the U.S.
Department of Justice Civil Rights Division, Zach has dedicated his life towards public service and fighting for people, regardless of political party.
After seeing firsthand the corruption and politicization of the Department of Justice, he made the difficult decision to resign and step up to run to bring Central Kentucky’s voice to Congress.
