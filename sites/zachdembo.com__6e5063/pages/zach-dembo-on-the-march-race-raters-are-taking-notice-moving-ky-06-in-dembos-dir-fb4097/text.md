Dembo set to post $1.35M+ for Q3 fundraising as polls show him in a lead and race raters move the race from “Likely Republican” to “Lean Republican”
Lexington, KY – Last week, nonpartisan organization Inside Elections updated their rating for Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District from “Likely Republican” to “Lean Republican,” moving the likelihood of winning in Zach Dembo’s direction in light of the worsening enviroment for Republicans, including Alvarado scrubbing his website of all mentions of Donald Trump.
That’s not all: The Kentucky Lantern has also reported on polling that show Dembo is in a decisive 5 point lead in the four-way vote, 42-37, with 9% of the electorate undecided.
Olivia Dupree, Zach Dembo’s campaign manager, made the following statement:
“Ralph Alvarado was Matt Bevin’s right-hand man.
The two are birds of a feather, represent the corruption in our politics that has let costs skyrocket these past two years, and we’re in the lead because the voters are taking notice.
“Alvarado and his Washington backers are spending big, but too scared to release their polling because they know voters are fed up with career politicians who treat office as a way to enrich themselves, not helping working people.
But not only that, voters are motivated by a real public servant like Zach Dembo who understands that the system is rigged against Central Kentucky families and is willing to work to fix it.”
