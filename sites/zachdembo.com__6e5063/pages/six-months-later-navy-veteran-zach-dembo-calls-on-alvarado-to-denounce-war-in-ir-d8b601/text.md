LEXINGTON, KY – Today, on the six month anniversary of the reckless war in Iran that has cost American servicemembers’ lives and raised prices for Kentucky families, Democratic nominee for Congress and Navy veteran Zach Dembo called for his opponent, Ralph Alvarado, to denounce this war that no one asked for.
Dembo was quick to condemn the war when it launched in February and has continued to vocally oppose the war as rising gas and diesel prices crush Kentucky farmers and families who are paying more as a result.
Navy Veteran Zach Dembo released the following statement:
“Instead of spending our money to benefit Central Kentucky, we’ve spent billions on a reckless war no one asked for that doesn’t do a damn thing for Kentucky.
Meanwhile, my opponent continues to support this cost-spiking war.
Six months in, our strategic objectives aren’t met, Iran controls the Strait of Hormuz, American lives – including several Kentuckians – have been lost, and all of us are paying the price at the gas pump and the grocery store.
This is the cost of corruption, and we deserve leadership that would fight for us rather than the corporations, defense contractors, or warmongering foreign policy establishment.”
Dembo’s opponent Ralph Alvarado has previously said “he fully supports the President’s decisive action” in the war that is raising costs on Kentucky families.
