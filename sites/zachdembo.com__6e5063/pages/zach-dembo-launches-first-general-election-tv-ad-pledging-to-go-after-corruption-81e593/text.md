A ninth-generation Kentuckian, Zach Dembo dedicated his career to public service and justice as a teacher in rural classrooms, Navy veteran, and federal prosecutor until he stepped down from the Department of Justice in opposition to the corruption he saw in Washington.
“Zach Dembo has dedicated his career to keeping his community safe and serving his country-,” said Olivia Dupree, Zach Dembo’s campaign manager.
