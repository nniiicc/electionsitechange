Six-figure buy spotlights Zach’s service to country over party as a Navy veteran and federal prosecutor as polls show a tied race in KY-06
Lexington, KY – Today, Navy veteran and former prosecutor Zach Dembo announced a six-figure TV and digital ad buy focused on how he’ll put country over party in order to fight the corruption in Washington, lower costs, and make sure Central Kentuckians have access to quality, affordable healthcare.
A ninth-generation Kentuckian, Zach Dembo dedicated his career to public service and justice as a teacher in rural classrooms, Navy veteran, and federal prosecutor until he stepped down from the Department of Justice in opposition to the corruption he saw in Washington.
“Zach Dembo has dedicated his career to keeping his community safe and serving his country-,” said Olivia Dupree, Zach Dembo’s campaign manager.
“This campaign will make it clear to voters the choice they have in November — between a public servant who will take the crises facing our country seriously and work to root out corruption, or a career politician like Ralph Alvarado who will use Congress to continue to enrich himself and cater to a corrupt system”
