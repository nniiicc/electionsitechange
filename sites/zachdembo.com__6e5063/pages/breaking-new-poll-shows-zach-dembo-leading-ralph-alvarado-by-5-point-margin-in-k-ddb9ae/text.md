Multiple public polls continue to show momentum building behind Dembo
Lexington, KY – Today, Navy veteran and 9th generation Kentuckian Zach Dembo released a poll showing he leads his opponent Ralph Alvarado by five points.
The poll finds Dembo ahead of Alvarado 42% to 37%, with 11% voting third party.
In a head-to-head match up, Dembo bests Alvarado 46% to 43%, with the rest of the electorate undecided.

This latest poll comes on the heels of Sabato’s Crystal Ball and Inside Elections both moving the likelihood of Zach winning this race in his direction from “Likely Republican” to “Lean Republican.”
Zach is a ninth generation Kentuckian, a former official in Governor Andy Beshear’s administration, and will be an independent voice for Central Kentucky in Washington.
Alvarado is a career politician who has used his political power to benefit his own businesses, enrich himself, and support the most craven in Kentucky politics, including disgraced ex-Governor Matt Bevin.
Zach Dembo, former federal prosecutor and Navy veteran, released the following statement:
“I’ve fought corruption my whole career.
The corruption in Washington has led Democratic and Republican politicians alike to sit on their hands when it comes to lowering costs for working Americans.
“This recent poll shows what we’ve known for a while: Central Kentucky families are fed up with a system they know is rigged against them.
They also know that Ralph Alvarado would be more of the same corruption that’s screwing them over.
“We’ve got more work to do with a month left until the election—and it’s all gas, no breaks.”
