The campaign now has nearly $500,000 cash on hand.
The campaign’s growing warchest despite spending down in a primary election ensures that Dembo’s campaign will have the resources it needs to mount a competitive general election against career politician, Bevin running mate, and recent Tennessee transplant Ralph Alvarado for this open seat.
In response to the news, Zach Dembo released the following statement: “As an outsider, not a career politician, I’m humbled every day by the support and enthusiasm pouring into this campaign.
I’m no stranger to being an underdog – we’ve already beaten the odds once in May, and with your help, we’ll do it again in November.
That’s thanks to every supporter who’s called a voter, knocked a door, or chipped in to help us spread our message and support our mission to root out corruption in Washington and lower costs for Kentucky families.” ABOUT ZACH: Former prosecutor Zach Dembo is a ninth-generation Kentuckian raised in Lexington, Kentucky.
From participating in the Teach For America program and serving as a United States Navy Judge Advocate, to serving Governor Andy Beshear as his policy advisor and legislative director, to fighting crime as federal prosecutor in both D.C. and Lexington, Zach has dedicated his life to public service and fighting for people, regardless of political party.
After seeing firsthand the corruption and politicization of the Department of Justice, he made the difficult decision to resign and step up to run to bring Central Kentucky’s voice to Congress.
Since the primary election, Dembo has seen a surge in momentum, securing the endorsements of key groups and political figures, including Governor Andy Beshear, the AFL-CIO, Next 50, Council for a Livable World, and others.
