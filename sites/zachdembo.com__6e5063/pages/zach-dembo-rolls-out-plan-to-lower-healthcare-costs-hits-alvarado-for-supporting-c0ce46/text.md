PARIS, KY – Today, Navy veteran and candidate for Congress Zach Dembo, former Kentucky Governor Steve Beshear, health care professionals, and Central Kentuckians held a press conference in Bourbon County, where Dembo outlined his plan to lower healthcare costs.
Dembo also highlighted the importance of Medicaid funding in Kentucky, especially in cities like Paris with a rural hospital currently at risk of shutting down due to Medicaid cuts Alvarado supports.
“Medicaid is under attack in Congress, and we’re all going to pay the price with higher healthcare costs because politicians like Ralph Alvarado got their way and slashed our benefits to fund tax cuts for their billionaire donors,” said Dembo .
“In Congress, I’ll work to make healthcare affordable for everyone by extending health care tax credits that lower costs, protecting Medicaid funding, capping out-of-pocket expenses, getting the government to negotiate fair drug prices, and fighting for a public option to expand health care access.” While Alvarado falsely claims his policies will “lower costs,” he’s personally profited off of the waste, fraud, and abuse in our health care system.
In addition to standing behind disgraced former Governor Matt Bevin’s sweeping Medicaid cuts, Alvarado was implicated in a Medicare fraud scheme and wrote legislation making it harder to sue for medical malpractice when he was the Medical Director of several low-rated nursing homes.
He oversaw five 1-star nursing homes with poor ratings that have racked up $400,000 in fines over three years for the abuse, neglect, and wrongful death of patients.
