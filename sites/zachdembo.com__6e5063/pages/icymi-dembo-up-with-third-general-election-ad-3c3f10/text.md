Lexington, KY – On Friday, former federal prosecutor and candidate for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district Zach Dembo released his third ad of the general election, highlighting Zach’s agenda to combat corruption and lower the cost of living.

The light-hearted positive spot calls attention to Zach Dembo’s core platform: fighting corruption and tackling the affordability crisis head on.
Affordability has been a major theme of Dembo’s campaign since day one, and Dembo has been outspoken about reversing the Medicaid cuts one-third of Kentuckians depend on, getting rid of sweeping tariffs that are hurting Kentucky’s industries, and ending the disastrous war in Iran, which has spiked diesel prices to a record high in the state.
The contrast couldn’t be more clear on corruption: The Dembo campaign released a sweeping anti-corruption platform that calls for lifetime ban on lobbying, enacting term limits for members of Congress, and banning insider stock trading for members of Congress.

Zach Dembo, former federal prosecutor and Navy veteran, released the following statement:
“Too often, political advertising is all doom and gloom.
From the start, I knew I wanted to run this campaign a little differently, cut through all the noise in a fun way, and get our message out there.
We’re still at it, introducing ourselves to every voter we can in the district and staying focused on the issues that matter most to Central Kentuckians – lowering costs and fighting corruption.”
