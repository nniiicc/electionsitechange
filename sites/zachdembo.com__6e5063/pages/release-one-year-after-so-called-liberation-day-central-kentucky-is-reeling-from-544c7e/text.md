Lexington, KY – Today is the one year anniversary of Donald Trump’s sweeping tariffs, which have raised prices on Central Kentucky’s families and hurt our industries.
Just this week, the Lexington Herald Leader published that American whiskey exports are down $250M, with Kentucky bourbon distillers sitting on record inventory of more than 16 million barrels.
Kentucky farmers are watching their markets disappear, and our families are paying more for everything.
Zach Dembo, ninth generation Kentuckian from a long line of farmers and candidate for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district, released the following statement (See more here):
“Andy Barr claims these tariffs are helping us.
The reality is Central Kentucky’s families are struggling to make ends meet, and this trade war is doing nothing to help them.
Tariffs are hiking taxes on our households and hurting our industries, especially our distillers, manufacturers, and farmers.
We need fighters for us and our industries in Congress, and one of the first things I would do is make sure Congress exercises its power and stops this needless trade war that’s hurting all of us.”
