Lexington, KY – Today, the National Education Association (NEA) has officially announced their endorsement of Democratic candidate Zach Dembo ahead of the May 19th primary.
The NEA represents teachers and public employees in schools all over the country and is the largest union in America.
Kentucky’s NEA affiliate, the Kentucky Education Association, represents over 44,000 certified educators and classified education support professionals, and is the largest labor union in Kentucky.
The NEA’s endorsement continues Dembo’s surging momentum in the primary, which includes endorsements from former Lexington mayor and Transportation Cabinet Secretary Jim Gray, former Justice and Public Safety Cabinet Secretary and Supreme Court Justice Mary Noble, VoteVets, With Honor, New Politics, and Foreign Policy for America..
In response to the news, Zach Dembo, former teacher and candidate for Congress, said the following:
“I’m so humbled to be receiving this endorsement from the National Educational Association, a powerhouse in the fight to fully fund our public schools, provide our students with world-class education, and make sure our teachers can live and retire with dignity.
I’ve enjoyed working alongside our amazing partners at the Kentucky Education Association and the local chapters in Fayette, Madison, and throughout our district, and look forward to continuing our fight together for teachers, students, and education in the Sixth Congressional District.
In Congress, I’ll always be a tireless advocate for teachers, students, and working families, and I’ll stand with labor unions like the KEA”.
Zach’s first job out of college was as an eighth grade language arts teacher in the rural Mississippi Delta, where he learned firsthand the difficult and amazing work teachers do in classrooms across America every single day.
He has been vocal in his fight for better education in Kentucky.
His experience as the only candidate who taught in an American classroom, as well as his vocal criticism of this administration’s dismantling of the Department of Education, helped him earn the NEA’s endorsement
NEA President Becky Pringle said the following:
“Zach Dembo is a champion for America’s students and Kentucky working families.
As a former teacher, Zach knows the importance of ensuring every student – no matter their background, race, or ZIP code – has access to amazing public schools.
He will fight to expand learning opportunities for our students and to prevent diverting funding from the public schools that 90 percent of our students attend into unaccountable and discriminatory private schools through vouchers.
He will stay focused on delivering lower costs for working families while getting things done to help students, educators, and communities across Kentucky.
The National Education Association is proud to recommend Zach Dembo in his campaign for Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District.”
