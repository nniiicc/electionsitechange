Lexington, KY – The questions keep piling up for Ralph Alvarado.
A wave of bad press has raised questions about Alvarado’s record of corruption and his willingness to put himself ahead of the people of Central Kentucky.
Now, with less than 50 days until Election Day, Alvarado continues to dodge the tough questions on issues voters care about.
Here’s a list of things Alvarado owes Central Kentuckians an answer on:
- Is he still “honored and humbled” to have been the running mate of disgraced ex-Governor Matt Bevin AFTER his war on teachers and cuts to Medicaid?
- Does he still believe “the vision [Bevin] has had for this state needs to be carried on and carried forward” after the disgraced ex-Governor’s time behind bars?
- Why did he write unconstitutional laws that benefitted his business?
- Why did he abandon his constituents for a promotion in Tennessee?
- Once he tucked tail for Tennessee, why did he THEN keep side gigs at low-rated nursing homes that have been sued for wrongful death?
- Why has he doubled down and stood by sweeping Medicaid cuts that threaten a third of Kentuckians’ health care coverage?
- Does he regret supporting the trade war that has severely hurt Kentucky’s bourbon and horsing industries?
- Does he regret praising the War in Iran as Kentuckians and farmers deal with all-time high diesel prices during harvest season?
- Why is Alvarado profiting off of data centers while several counties in KY-06 oppose them outright?
- Will he sell the hundreds of thousands to millions in individual stocks he owns if he becomes Central Kentucky’s representative or will he continue to profit off of them in Congress?
- Will he condemn the illegal use of AI in misleading ads supporting him or is he complicit in deceiving Central Kentuckians yet again?
- Why did he scrub his website of all mentions of Donald Trump despite hailing the “Triple Trump” endorsement in the primary?
Zach Dembo, Democratic nominee for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district, released the following statement:
“Public service means showing up, listening, and answering the tough questions.
Time after time, my opponent has done the opposite.”
“He won’t answer for leaving Kentuckians for a job in Tennessee, why he has overseen nursing homes that have been sued at least 13 times since 2023, why he stood alongside disgraced former Governor Matt Bevin, or why he stands by the Iran war that is jacking up gas and diesel prices and Medicaid cuts that will hurt all of us.”
“Voters deserve answers, and Central Kentuckians deserve better.”
