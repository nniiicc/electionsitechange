FACT CHECK: We won’t let him.
Alvarado was complicit in the worst of the disgraced ex-Governor’s actions.
Lexington, KY – Today, the Republican Party of Kentucky and Ralph Alvarado released a statement claiming Matt Bevin’s running mate was against the controversial pardons.
The only problem is no one can find evidence he said anything.
Don’t take our word for it.
Multiple outlets have reported that there is no evidence of Alvarado directly saying anything to condemn the pardons at the time.
As an elected official – and especially as Matt Bevin’s former running mate – Ralph Alvarado had an unique obligation to make his views on these outrageous pardons plain and public, yet unlike other Republicans in the state legislature, there’s no evidence of him speaking up publicly then.
It was only after he felt the political blowback of our ad that Alvarado finally spoke out just two weeks ago, which was six years too late.
When Louisville Public Media asked Alvarado for any evidence to support their claim that Alvarado “opposed [Bevin’s pardons] publicly” at the time, the outlet “received no response.” Now Alvarado points to unverified claims from fellow politicians who want to see him elected, asking us to take their word for it that Alvarado was against it.
Alvarado can say that now, but the fact remains that when Kentuckians needed courage from him, he was nowhere to be found.
Here’s what there is evidence of: Reporting proves Alvarado did maintain a relationship with the disgraced ex-Governor as recently as 2022, when he was theorizing about a potential third Bevin run.
Olivia Dupree, Zach Dembo’s campaign manager, released the following statement:
“If Ralph Alvarado was so outraged by the pardons, why didn’t he say anything publicly at the time and why did he maintain a relationship with the disgraced ex-Governor as recently as 2022, when he was apparently bouncing ideas off of Ralph for a third run for Governor?
It’s because he was complicit in these pardons and the rest of Matt Bevin’s disastrous record as his running mate, from his sweeping Medicaid cuts, war on teachers, and yes, his politically-motivated pardons, including of a child rapist.
Alvarado has a record of trying to re-write history – just like how he wiped Donald Trump from his website when it was politically convenient for him – and this campaign will continue to hold him accountable for his actions.”
