LEXINGTON, KY – Earlier today, President Trump ordered the military to conduct large-scale military operations with the intended aim to eliminate Iran’s nuclear program – the same nuclear program the President claimed was “completely and totally obliterated” by his earlier attack on Iran last year.
At this time, Tehran has retaliated, firing missiles at Israel, U.S. bases in the Middle East, and numerous other nations in the Middle East that took no part in the military action.
In response to the news, Zach Dembo, Navy JAG veteran, released the following statement:
“Once again, this administration has turned its back on the promises it made to the American people to keep us out of unnecessary foreign wars and focus on the problems we have here at home.
As a Navy veteran and former federal prosecutor, I know what it means to serve this country.
We owe it to our troops and the American people to make sure that war is a last resort, and that any such actions are taken with a full explanation to the American people and approval from their representatives in Congress.
This isn’t ‘America First’ – it’s dangerous and reckless.
This unauthorized military attack does nothing to help the people of Central Kentucky.
It does nothing to control the sky-high costs we’re experiencing here, or the chaos that this administration has unleashed in our streets.
We need long-term solutions in the Middle East, not reckless wars that put American lives at risk and needlessly escalate the already formidable tensions.
This war was completely of the President’s choosing – this did not have to happen now, nor did it have to happen without any Congressional approval.
He has made no attempt to explain his goals or the endgame to the American people, and whatever chaos ensues will be attributable to his recklessness.
Congress should hold this President accountable by passing the bipartisan Congressional War Powers Resolutions introduced by Senator Tim Kaine and Rand Paul and Congressmen Tom Massie and Ro Khanna.
And if they won’t, we need to hold him accountable in November.”
