Multiple public polls show the race is all tied up
Lexington, KY – Today, UVA Center for Politics’ Sabato’s Crystal Ball updated their rating for Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District from “Likely Republican” to “Lean Republican,” moving the likelihood of winning in Zach Dembo’s direction.
The ratings shift comes as multiple public polls show the race is locked in a dead heat.
This rate change affirms what has been true for a while: Central Kentucky wants someone in Washington who is there to serve the people, not their stock portfolio, and who will stand up to corruption in both parties.
Zach Dembo, former federal prosecutor and Navy veteran, released the following statement:
“What we’re seeing on the ground here in Kentucky, a place my family has called home for nine generations, is that folks are tired of a system where people go to Washington to get rich and forget about the people they’re supposed to represent.
“The culture of corruption that exists in both parties has led to inaction on the issues that matter most to people.
From tariffs that have crushed small businesses and cut farmers off from their trading partners to the war in Iran that has spiked gas, diesel, and fertilizer costs, leaders in Washington sit on their hands if it means they’re getting richer while everyday folks foot the bill.
“This race has moved in our direction because folks believe in our message: ban congressional stock trading, end the Iran war, roll back these reckless tariffs, and lower costs for working families.
“People want change here in Central Kentucky.
In November, we’re going to deliver that change.”
