Lexington, KY – Earlier today, prosecutor and Democratic nominee for Kentucky’s 6th congressional district Zach Dembo unveiled a sweeping anti-corruption platform in a packed room of union members, law enforcement, and supporters of his campaign.
The platform addresses some of the root causes of rising costs for working families: a government more focused on serving corporations and special interest donors than the American people and politicians more interested in lining their own pockets than lowering costs.
Dembo pledged to take on corruption wherever he sees it – regardless of political party.
In addition to his remarks at the anti-corruption presser earlier, Zach Dembo put out the following statement:
“As a JAG officer, US Attorney and Senior Advisor to Governor Andy Beshear, serving our country and serving Kentucky have always been my north stars.
Service before self has been central to my life, but what we have now is a Congress that has become consumed by a culture of corruption.
That corruption has cost our families, driven up our prices and enriched politicians more interested in their own bottom lines than us.
I am running to end that corruption by banning corporate PAC money, implementing term limits and barring Members of Congress from becoming lobbyists, so Washington finally works for Kentucky, not itself.”
“Right now, the cost of everyday life keeps going up.
Healthcare, groceries, and gas are putting more pressure on families, while big corporations continue to post record profits,” said Jeremy Jenkins, Business Manager of the Laborers’ Local 189 chapter based in Lexington, who spoke at the event.
“We deserve affordable healthcare, affordable housing, fair wages, and a government that puts working people first… That is why Zach Dembo matters.
And that is why we have to keep fighting for a government that works for all of us, not just those at the top.”
“By shielding negligent medical providers from liability and making it more difficult for injured Kentuckians to receive just compensation, I believe that Dr.
Ralph Alvarado violated a physician’s most sacred oath: to do no harm,” said Wesley Delk, Mt.
Sterling resident and victim of medical malpractice.
“Ralph Alvarado and his corrupt, self-dealing politicians have no place representing the 6th District of Kentucky in Congress.
Zach Dembo, with his clear track record of integrity, is clearly the right choice.”
Zach unveiled his anti-corruption agenda as momentum continues to build behind his campaign.
Multiple public polls show that the race for Kentucky’s 6th Congressional District is locked in a dead heat, and non-partisan race raters have shifted the race in his direction citing the strength of his candidacy.
