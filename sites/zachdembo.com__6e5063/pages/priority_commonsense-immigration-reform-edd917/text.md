ICE is out of control right now; one need look no further than their killings of two Americans in broad daylight.
The firing of Kristi Noem was long overdue and in no way addresses the underlying problems with that overfunded and undersupervised agency.
As a former federal prosecutor, I will always be for immigration enforcement against violent criminals and a secure border, but what we’re seeing right now is ICE inspiring fear rather than public safety and killing taxpayers instead of serving them.
We must hold ICE to the same standards as all law enforcement: comprehensive training (not just 47 days), body cameras for transparency, no hiding identities behind masks, and a focus on actual violent criminals – not law-abiding people in courthouses, schools, or churches.
We can have both security and humanity.
We also need immigration reform that secures our border while treating people with dignity and creating a path to citizenship for those already here working hard and contributing to our communities.
