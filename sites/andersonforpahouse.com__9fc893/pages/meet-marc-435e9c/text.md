MEET MARC
Two years ago, after encouragement from students, wise counsel from friends, and much prayer, Marc made the decision to leave behind his teaching career and pursue a seat in the Pennsylvania House of Representatives.
That decision kicked off a campaign that changed his family’s life.
The campaign was one of the most challenging seasons of his life and he could not have done it without the overwhelming support of the community that he and his wife have called home for nearly 30 years.
Since taking office one year ago, Marc has been frequently humbled by the trust that the voters of the 92nd District have placed in a history teacher from Northern High School.
Though challenging at times, Marc has never stopped fighting for the people of Northern York County to ensure that their voice is heard in the halls of our State Capitol.
Marc considers his most important titles to be husband to Cindy, dad to Ben and Maggie, and "pap" to Hayes.
