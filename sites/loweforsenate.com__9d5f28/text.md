Dr.
J Adam Lowe is the current state senator representing the counties of Bradley, McMinn, Meigs, and Rhea in Tennessee.
Since his election, Senator Lowe has carried and supported legislation supporting conservative government.
The American Conservative Union praised him for "conservative achievement" and he has championed the Trump agenda in the state.
Further, Senator Lowe is known for his legislation battling distributors of fentanyl, catalyzing education reform, and promoting state sovereignty.
Senator Lowe serves on the committees for state & local government, education, pensions, and is the vice chairman of agriculture & energy.
He is also the NCLS appointee to serve on the national committee for energy.
He works as a professor of public policy, author, and businessman.
His wife Rachel is a public school educator and they have four children.
