Results You Can See.
Utah’s strength begins at home — with strong families, faith, and community.
I’ll defend our freedoms, keep government accountable, and focus on practical solutions that keep Utah the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
Core Values Family & Faith Life Fiscal Economy Education Safety Liberty & 2A Air & Water Ethics Guiding Principles Core Values Strong families and tight-knit communities are the foundation of Utah’s success.
I’ll protect families, defend freedom, and keep government accountable and limited.
Live within our means; fund only what truly serves the public.
Lead with honesty, transparency, and respect — listen first, unite where possible.
Honor the Constitution’s promise of opportunity, not guarantees.
Utah’s Cornerstone Family & Faith Every major challenge is easier to solve with strong families and vibrant faith communities.
Champion parental rights and individual responsibility.
Protect freedom of religion for every Utahn.
Promote policies that strengthen home and community.
Most Fundamental Right Defending Life Life is sacred and deserves protection at every stage.
Stand for the unborn; oppose taxpayer-funded abortion.
Support compassionate, accessible adoption.
Do Less, Do It Better Fiscal Responsibility & Tax Stewardship Fiscal discipline is a moral obligation to future generations.
Balanced budgets and opposition to unnecessary spending.
Simpler, stable taxes that reward work and investment.
Keep taxes low for families and small businesses.
Opportunity for All Economic Growth & Opportunity Utah thrives on free enterprise, innovation, and personal responsibility — not favoritism.
Strengthen the workforce and support small business growth.
Invest wisely in infrastructure to keep pace with growth.
Oppose cronyism and government favoritism.
Parents First Education Excellence Every child deserves the chance to succeed, with parents in the driver’s seat.
Empower families to choose public, private, charter, or home school.
High standards, real accountability, and a focus on the basics.
Strengthen civics: rights, responsibilities, and constitutional literacy.
Safe Communities Public Safety & Rule of Law Safe neighborhoods require well-trained, well-supported first responders and a fair, firm justice system.
Back police, firefighters, and first responders with tools and respect.
Prioritize victim rights and community trust.
Rights & Responsibility Individual Liberty & the Second Amendment I will always defend the right to keep and bear arms for law-abiding citizens — with a culture of safe, responsible ownership.
Stewardship Clean Air, Clean Water, and a Healthy Utah Protecting Utah’s natural beauty requires balanced, local solutions — not heavy-handed mandates from Washington.
Practical conservation for air, water, and open space.
Growth planning that respects families and small businesses.
Public Trust Ethics & Accountability Elected officials work for the people.
I’ll set a high bar — and expect the same from everyone in public service.
Transparency in process and spending.
Honesty, humility, and open communication.
