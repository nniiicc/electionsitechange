Taxes & the Economy Amid rising inflation, high fuel prices, and heavy property taxes, Bruce's directive is clear: drastically reduce wasteful government spending and lower property taxes.
Bruce has helped lower taxes every year since he was elected, opposed every tax increase proposal, and pledged never to raise taxes.
Promises kept.
Defending Your Constitutional Freedoms Bruce stands at the front line defending our rights.
On free speech, he believes every voice must be able to speak without fear of censorship.
As a man of deep faith, he is unwavering on religious liberty.
As an attorney, Bruce volunteered his legal representation at no cost in Freedom of Speech, Freedom of Religion, and Defending Women's Sports cases in federal court, defending the rights in our Constitution and Bill of Rights and the inalienable rights endowed by our Creator.
A Life Member of the NRA, he is committed to preserving and defending the Second Amendment against every encroachment.
Public Safety Bruce is a law-and-order legislator, a staunch ally to those who keep our communities safe and an enemy to criminals.
His stance against defund-the-police is clear and uncompromising.
Endorsed by the Fraternal Order of Police, he has worked to crack down on fentanyl traffickers and eradicate the threats that jeopardize Idaho families.
He also restored the firing squad as an execution method in Idaho.
Sanctity of Life Bruce's commitment to the pro-life cause runs deep, grounded in respect for the intrinsic value of every life, from conception to the last breath taken.
In his first year as a legislator, he passed a law that ended all state funding, directly and indirectly, for abortion.
He served seven years on the Nampa Lifeline Crisis Pregnancy Center Board of Directors.
His endorsement from Idaho Chooses Life underscores a vision where every stage of life is safeguarded with compassion and dignity.
Protecting Our Children Our children are the most vulnerable members of society, and they deserve legislators who will fight for them.
Bruce carried into law some of the harshest criminal penalties in the nation for those who intentionally harm children.
He outlawed child sex changes, sending doctors who perform them to prison, and put those who use or deal in child pornography, including AI-generated material, behind bars.
He carried the law imposing the death penalty for child molesters in the most extreme cases.
His dedication is evident in the Vulnerable Child Protection Act, which he introduced and passed to safeguard Idaho's children from irreversible harm.
