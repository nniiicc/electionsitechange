Taxes and The Economy Amidst the pressing concerns of mounting inflation, skyrocketing fuel prices, and oppressive property taxes, decisive action isn't just desired—it's essential.
Bruce Skaug remains steadfast in his commitment to confront these challenges with unwavering determination.
Central to his strategy for Idaho is a clear-cut directive: drastically reduce wasteful government spending and substantially lower property taxes.
By judiciously utilizing every taxpayer dollar and alleviating the tax pressure on our residents, Bruce seeks to cultivate an environment primed for growth and financial stability.
This mission goes beyond mere fiscal policies; it's about building an Idaho where every resident can afford to live, work, and raise a family.

Defending Your Constitutional Freedoms In an era where our constitutional freedoms are increasingly challenged, Bruce Skaug stands at the forefront, resolute in defending these rights against radical opposition.
Free Speech: The heartbeat of our democracy lies in the power of free speech.
Bruce firmly believes in the importance of safeguarding our right to express ourselves without the chilling specter of censorship or retaliation.
It's imperative that all voices, regardless of their views, can articulate their beliefs and opinions without fear, while also recognizing the responsibility that comes with such freedoms.
Religious Liberty: As a man of deep faith, Bruce profoundly understands the indispensable value of religious liberties.
Our right to practice and uphold our religious beliefs without persecution or constraint is paramount, and Bruce is unwavering in his dedication to protect this foundational right.
Second Amendment: Our constitutional right to bear arms is more than just a clause; it's a reflection of our nation's history and values.
With a strong endorsement from the NRA, Bruce is committed to preserving and defending our Second Amendment rights against any encroachments.

Public Safety Bruce Skaug has consistently emerged as a beacon of unwavering support for law enforcement.
He's not just a supporter; Bruce is a law and order legislator, which makes him an enemy to criminals and a staunch ally to those dedicated to preserving peace in our communities.
His stance against movements like "defund the police" is clear and uncompromising, understanding that these approaches threaten the very fabric of our safety.
Bruce believes in constructive solutions and stands firmly against ideologies that weaken our law enforcement and put our communities at risk.
Endorsed by the Fraternal Order of Police, his commitment to public safety isn't merely in words.
This legislative session, he has been tirelessly working on initiatives to crack down on fentanyl traffickers, further solidifying his dedication to eradicating the threats that jeopardize the well-being of Idaho's residents.
He also carried the bill into law that sets mandatory minimum sentences for child molesters and death penalty by firing squad in extreme cases.
