ROOTED IN IDAHO Five generations, one home state Bruce is a fifth-generation Idahoan, drawing strength from ancestors who first homesteaded in Canyon and Boise Counties.
Born in Pocatello and raised in Jerome, he has called Nampa home since the early #s.
He and his wife Debbie have been married since 1986.
Together they raised six children and have been blessed with fourteen grandchildren.
Education A.A., College of Southern Idaho.
B.S., University of Idaho.
J.D., University of Idaho College of Law.
Career Founder of Skaug Law; retired attorney and former Ada County deputy prosecutor.
Faith & Family Husband, father of six, grandfather of fourteen, and active in his church.
IN THE STATEHOUSE Leadership that delivers Now in his third term, Bruce made an impact from day one.
In his freshman year he turned more than a dozen bills into signed law, an achievement that may have set a record for a first-term representative.
Across his years in the legislature he has carried more than 70 bills into law.
Today he chairs the influential House Judiciary, Rules and Administration Committee and serves on Local Government and State Affairs.
These roles put him at the center of the fight to uphold conservative principles and safeguard Idahoan values.
A servant's heart Before the legislature, Bruce spent six years on the Nampa City Council, where his leadership helped earn Nampa recognition as one of the best-run cities in the country.
He has served on numerous nonprofit boards, and his deep faith has led him to active roles within his church.
Away from the capitol, Bruce is a lover of Idaho history and its pristine landscapes, happiest on his ATV or working with a chainsaw and ax.
A Life Member of the NRA, he is also an avid sportsman.
Milestones 2014-2020 Nampa City Council Six years of service, including as council president, as Nampa earns national recognition for how well it's run.
2020 Elected to the Idaho House Sends a record number of freshman bills to the governor's desk in his first session.
2023 Vulnerable Child Protection Act Authors and passes landmark legislation protecting Idaho's children.
2026 Committee Chairman Leads Judiciary, Rules & Administration while running for a fourth term.
