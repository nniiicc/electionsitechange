Overview This Privacy Policy explains how Skaug for Idaho (the campaign, we, us, or our) collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with skaugforidaho.com and our related digital properties, communications, and forms.
By using our website or providing your information to us, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
Mobile Messaging (SMS) If you opt in to receive text messages from Skaug for Idaho, you consent to receive automated campaign updates, event invitations, and volunteer and donation requests at the mobile number you provide.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or contribution.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier's plan.
You can reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or reply HELP for assistance.
We do not sell or share your mobile opt-in information or SMS consent with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
Information We Collect Information you provide When you make a contribution, sign up to volunteer, request a yard sign, subscribe to updates, or contact the campaign, we may collect your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, employer and occupation, and payment information.
Federal and state law requires us to collect employer and occupation details for certain contributions.
Information collected automatically Information from other sources We may obtain information about you from voter files, public records, and third-party vendors, and we may combine that information with the information we collect through our website and forms.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to process and acknowledge contributions, send campaign communications, invite you to events, solicit support and volunteers, respond to your inquiries, comply with legal obligations, and analyze and improve the performance of our website and outreach.
How We Share Your Information We may share information with vendors and service providers who perform work on our behalf, with organizations and candidates who share our goals, and as required or permitted by law.
As noted above, we do not share mobile opt-in information or SMS consent with third parties for their marketing purposes.
Analytics and Advertising Campaign Finance Disclosures Idaho law requires the public reporting of certain contributor information.
Your name, city and state, employer, occupation, and contribution amount may be disclosed to the appropriate election authorities and made available to the public as required by law.
Data Security We take reasonable measures to help protect the information we collect from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access.
However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Your Choices You can set your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent, though some parts of the site may not function properly if you do.
You can unsubscribe from our emails using the link in any message, and you can reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving SMS communications.
Children's Privacy Our website is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we will revise the date at the top of this page.
We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we handle your information.
