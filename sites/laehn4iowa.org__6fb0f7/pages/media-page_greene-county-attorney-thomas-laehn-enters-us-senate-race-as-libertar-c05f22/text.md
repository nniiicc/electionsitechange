Senate race as Libertarian Oct 27 Written By Kara Glenn Greene County Attorney Thomas Laehn is running for the U.S.
Senate as a Libertarian.
He's the first and only Libertarian to be elected to public office in Iowa’s history.
Laehn said Iowans are ready for something besides the two-party system.
As an intern in Sen.
Chuck Grassley’s office more than two decades ago, Laehn recalled when a staffer took him to the Senate floor.
What he saw shocked him.
Rather than a room full of senators debating and deliberating, Laehn said he saw just one senator talking to a camera.
