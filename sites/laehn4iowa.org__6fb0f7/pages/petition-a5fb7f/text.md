Download the Petition Now How to Fill Out the Petition 1.
Print the petition form Each petition page has room for up to 15 signatures.
2.
Write in the county name Before collecting signatures, fill in the blank for the county on the petition page.
Each petition page may contain signatures from only one county .
3.
Collect signatures from eligible voters Ask friends, family, neighbors, coworkers, and others in your network to sign, as long as they are eligible voters living in the county listed on that page.
4.
Make sure each signer completes all required information Each signer should fill out: their signature their Iowa residential street address their city the date 5.
Mail completed petitions back to the campaign Please return signed petitions to: Laehn Committee PO Box 103 Jefferson, IA 50129 Where to Send Completed Petitions Mail completed petitions to: Laehn Committee PO Box 103 Jefferson, IA 50129 Questions?
