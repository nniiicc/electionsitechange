Thomas Laehn, Iowa Libertarian, Elected As Greene County Attorney
Dr.
Thomas Laehn Elected as Greene County Attorney, first Libertarian partisan office holder in Iowa.
The Libertarian Party of Iowa obtained its first victory in a race for a partisan elective office.
On November 6, Dr.
Thomas Laehn, Libertarian of Jefferson, was elected by the people of Greene County as their new County Attorney.
