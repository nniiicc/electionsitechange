SB 496, a Bill I carried in the House, has been signed by the Governor.
It expands the criteria for a home to be certified as historic.
It also extends the sunsets for the tax credits for rehabilitation of historic homes and structures to December 31, 2029, and for the tax credits for rural zone revitalization to December 31, 2032.
It was sponsored by Chairman Max Burns, and signed by Chairman Chuck Hufstetler, Chairman Frank Ginn, Chairman Chuck Payne, Chairman Rick Williams, and Sen.
Harold Jones II.
Rep.
Ron Stephens and Rep.
Penny Houston were also instrumental.
