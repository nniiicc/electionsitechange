“As someone who deeply respects the sovereignty and heritage of Oklahoma’s Tribal Nations, I fully support their inherent right to self-govern.
This includes the right to manage their own lands, resources, and traditional practices such as hunting and fishing without interference.
Tribal self-governance is not just a matter of law — it’s a matter of respect, history, and partnership.
Our Tribal Nations have been strong stewards of Oklahoma’s natural resources for generations, and their leadership continues to benefit our entire state.
In Congress, I will stand firmly for policies that honor our treaties, strengthen tribal sovereignty, and protect the cultural and economic vitality of Native communities throughout Oklahoma’s Second District.”
— Brandon Wade
Candidate for U.S.
Congress, Oklahoma’s 2nd District
