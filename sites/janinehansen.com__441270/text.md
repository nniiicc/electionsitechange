Governor BIOGRAPHY CONTACT The Lt.
Governor serves as the President of the Nevada State Senate during regular and special sessions of the Nevada Legislature.
I have been an advocate for families and taxpayers at the Nevada Legislature my entire adult life.
I know the process and will use my influence to protect families, taxpayers and citizens and oppose the expansion and intrusion of state government into our lives.
The Lt.
Governor chairs the Nevada Commission on Tourism and the Advisory Board on Outdoor Recreation.
As a native Nevadan I have an understanding of the critical importance of tourism to Nevada’s economy.
The livelihood of many Nevada families depends on tourism.
Expanding our tourism, especially outdoor recreational opportunities, will particularly help our rural counties and our economy as a whole.
The Lt.
Governor is a member of the Board of Economic Development.
We have witnessed many people leaving California in the last few years.
Many of them come to live in Nevada.
We have no state income tax and other taxes are lower than California.
It is important to keep our taxes low.
I would advocate that lowering Nevada’s taxes would increase our economic growth and prosperity.
When citizens and businesses keep more of their own money, they buy more, they invest more, they create more jobs and opportunities for all Nevadans.
The Lt.
Governor is a member of the Nevada Homeland Security Commission.
The Commission advises the Governor and the Legislature on preventing and responding to terrorism and related emergencies.
Under the Biden administration there was a 3000% increase of terrorists coming across our border.
Most of them have not been deported.
Nevadans must be vigilant in keeping our citizens and communities safe, while protecting our fundamental Constitutional rights as citizens. ​ Independent American Party of Nevada Nevada Families for Freedom bottom of page
