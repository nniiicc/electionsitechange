6 Years Serving on the First Court of Appeals 300+ Written opinions authored on the merits Thousands Of appellate matters considered A Career Dedicated to Public Service Julie Countiss has dedicated much of her professional life to public service.
Before joining the appellate bench, she served as an Assistant County Attorney with the Harris County Attorney’s Office, where she represented Harris County in state and federal civil litigation and advised public officials on compliance with local, state, and federal law.
Her commitment to serving others began years earlier as a Teach for America teacher in the South Bronx.
That experience helped shape her lifelong belief in public service, community involvement, and the importance of strengthening the institutions and communities we rely on.
