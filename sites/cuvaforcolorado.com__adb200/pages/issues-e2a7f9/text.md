Lower Cost of Living
Colorado families and seniors are being squeezed by rising costs for housing, groceries, energy, and everyday essentials.
I will fight to lower the cost of living by cutting taxes, reducing unnecessary fees, eliminating wasteful government spending, and rolling back regulations that drive up costs.
We must pursue policies that make it easier to live, work, raise a family, and achieve the Colorado dream.
Strengthen Public Safety
Every family deserves to feel safe in their home, at work, and in their neighborhood.
I will work hard to protect our community by supporting law enforcement and holding criminals accountable by prioritizing the prosecution of violent and repeat offenders.
By ensuring our police have the resources they need to fight violent crime, we can restore safe communities and protect the quality of life Coloradans deserve.
Protecting Your Freedom to Thrive
Colorado's future depends on preserving the freedoms that allow individuals, families, and businesses to succeed.
I will defend TABOR, protect taxpayer refunds, and oppose efforts to grow the government at the expense of hardworking Coloradans.
By reducing red tape, protecting our individual liberties, and promoting a strong business climate, we can create greater opportunity, foster a business-friendly environment, strengthen our economy, and ensure every Coloradan has the opportunity and freedom to thrive.
