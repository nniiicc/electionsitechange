Follow Us Meet Lawrence Priorities Donate Privacy Policy Effective September 17, 2026 Friends of Lawrence Almagno (“the campaign,” “we,” “us”) runs lawrencealmagno.com in support of Lawrence Almagno’s campaign for Rhode Island State Representative, District 27.
This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.
What we collect What you give us on our sign-up and volunteer forms: your name, email address, ZIP code and mobile number.
What you tell us when we talk with you at your door, by phone, by text or by email.
Public voter registration information that Rhode Island makes available to candidates and campaigns.
Donations are processed by our payment processor, Anedot.
We don’t receive or store your card number.
How we use it To contact you about the campaign: updates, events, volunteer opportunities, voting information such as mail ballot deadlines and polling places, fundraising requests, and answers to your questions.
Who we share it with Service providers who help us send email and text messages and run our website, only so they can do that work for us.
When the law requires it, including campaign finance reports filed with the Rhode Island Board of Elections.
We don’t sell or rent your personal information.
Text messaging and your mobile number No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
This excludes the service providers that deliver our text messages.
Cookies and advertising Our website uses the Meta (Facebook) pixel, which sets cookies and tells Meta when you visit our site so we can measure and target campaign ads.
You can limit this through your Facebook ad settings or your browser’s cookie settings.
Your choices Email: click “Unsubscribe” at the bottom of any email.
Text: reply STOP to any text message.
To ask us to update or stop using your information, email LA@LawrenceAlmagno.com or call 401-373-8625 .
