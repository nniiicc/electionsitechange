Join the Campaign
Strong campaigns are built by neighbors who care about their community and choose to get involved.
If you're interested in volunteering, I would be grateful to have you alongside us.

Join a Community Canvass
Our weekend community canvasses are one of the best ways to get involved.
You'll receive a brief training, meet other volunteers, and spend a couple of hours talking with neighbors across East Cobb.
Whether it's your first time volunteering or you've worked on campaigns before, you'll be welcomed, supported, and part of a team that's working together for our community.
