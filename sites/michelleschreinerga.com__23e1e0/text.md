Practical Policies for
Georgia Families
Moving Georgia forward means focusing on issues that shape everyday life, including:
Strong Schools That Support Every Child
Families should not feel forced to leave public schools to get their children the support they need.
Whether it’s learning differences or other specialized needs, our schools must be equipped to serve students well.
When parents are paying significant property taxes, they deserve confidence that their public schools can meet their children’s needs.
Support for Longtime Residents and Caregivers
Families in our district feel the strain of navigating healthcare for aging parents.
They are also supporting loved ones with specialized care needs.
We need practical solutions that support aging with dignity and help families manage the responsibilities and costs of care.
Safe and Stable Communities
Our community deserves leadership that prioritizes safety, stability, and responsible governance.
We can protect what works and address what needs improvement.
Decisions should be grounded in data and accountability.
