The typical all-or-nothing party approach (where you're expected to agree 100% or be the enemy) is driving good people apart.
I believe Sylvania, the 44th District, and Northwest Ohio need an independent voice for real progress in Columbus.
As a third-generation architect in a small family business and Sylvania City Council member, I’ve spent my life building our community, not a political career .
I have seen incredible results when political game-playing and ego are set aside to find solutions, and I want to do the same at the statehouse.
The two parties are stuck grasping for power while major issues affecting families go unaddressed and get worse.
As an independent who answers directly to the district , I can help break the stalemate and get things done.
I am a candidate to give District 44 an independent voice for better government in Ohio, through practical solutions rather than partisan gridlock.
I Believe: We are more alike than we are different, and we can achieve results on common ground.
Political power grabs, partisan gridlock, and big money are undermining District 44’s best interests.
People are stretched thin and need the cost of essentials like groceries, healthcare, and utilities to be lower.
Keeping Ohio a good place for business, creating new jobs, and growing Ohio’s economy benefits us all.
Public education is valuable and important, and needs to be properly funded.
A direct highway from Northwest Ohio to Columbus unlocks economic opportunities.
We need to work hard to return tax dollars to the 44th District for local government capital improvement projects.
Follow “Live and Let Live” rather than letting culture wars distract us from addressing shared issues.
The Local Government Fund needs to be restored and tax dollars shared.
Reduce government spending and uphold fiscal responsibility for long-term quality of life.
