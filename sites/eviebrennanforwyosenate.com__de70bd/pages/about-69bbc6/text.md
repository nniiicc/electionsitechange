top of page EVIE BRENNAN REPUBLICAN Contribute Home About Issues Connect News Senator District 31 Meet Evie Brennan Senator Evie Brennan is a consistent conservative whose desire is to preserve Wyoming for her children and their generation like her grandparents preserved Wyoming for her.
Evie is a third generation Wyoming native whose great-grandfather homesteaded in Wyoming.
Married with four children, she and her husband are raising a new Wyoming generation. ​ ​ Evie Brennan graduated from Cheyenne East High School in 2000.
She attended the University of Wyoming where she graduated with a Bachelors of Science in Nursing in 2004. ​ ​ Evie has worked as a nurse in Cheyenne since graduating college.
For the past 12 years she has worked in the ICU.
This experience has prepared Evie to work hard, think critically and defend the freedoms of Wyoming. ​ Evie has served on the Joint Transportation Committee.
She is currently a member of the Joint Education Committee, The select committee on School Finance-Recalibration and is the Senate Chairman for Labor, Health and Social Services. ​ ​ ​ PAID FOR BY BRENNAN FOR SENATE.
