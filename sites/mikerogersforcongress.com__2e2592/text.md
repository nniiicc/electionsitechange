Mike Rogers, a Conservative Republican, is a sixth generation East Alabamian and the son of a retired textile worker and fireman.
He and his wife Beth have been married for over 40 years and have three children.
They live in Calhoun County, Alabama, and attend Saks Baptist Church.
“I want to thank the constituents of Alabama’s third district for their continued faith in me to represent them in Washington.
I’d also like to thank President Trump for his early and strong endorsement.
I am very grateful for his trust in me to champion and help pass his peace through strength agenda in Washington.
Throughout the next two years we must complete what we started under President Trump.
We must close our borders, protect the unborn, unleash American energy, and grow our economy!”
