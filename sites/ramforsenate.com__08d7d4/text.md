Meet Ram
A Progressive Champion for Working Families.
Ram has dedicated his career to advocating for the people of Illinois.
As a union organizer, he fought for home care for seniors, child care for working families, and Medicaid for those in need.
Before that, he worked setting up job fairs and helping folks enroll in Obamacare.
Ram has taken that passion to Springfield; to be the outspoken, Progressive champion the people of the 8th District deserve.
