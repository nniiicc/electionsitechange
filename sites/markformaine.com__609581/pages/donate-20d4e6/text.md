As a Clean Elections candidate, Mark receives no money from PACs, corporations, or special interests and is running a citizen-funded campaign.
Mark needs 535 contributions of just $5 from voters in Senate District 33 to receive the full funding necessary to wage a strong and competitive campaign.
It’s easy to contribute to Mark’s campaign: Go to www.maine.gov/cleanelections , enter your information, select Mark's name, and follow the steps until the process is completed.
Couples who want to contribute can use the same credit card but must log on and enter information separately.
