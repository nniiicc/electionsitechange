A fifth-generation Floridian.
A person of deep faith, David Jolly believes everyone deserves a shot at work, wages, and wealth, and that their rights should be protected and their dignity celebrated, regardless of the color of their skin, where they were born, who they love, or who they worship.
David is a former Member of Congress, attorney, and nationally recognized television analyst who launched his bid for Governor in June of 2025.
Since announcing his candidacy, David has traveled every corner of the state, hosting hundreds of town halls and community forums focused on improving the state's economy.
Born into public service.
Gwen Graham was born in Miami Lakes and raised in a family built around public service.
A proud product of Florida's public schools and a mother of three, she spent her early career fighting for teachers and students, from the PTA to the Leon County school district.
In Congress, she represented Florida's 2nd District and worked together with David to protect our coastline and keep oil drilling out of the Gulf.
Now they're running side by side for a Florida that works for everyone.
