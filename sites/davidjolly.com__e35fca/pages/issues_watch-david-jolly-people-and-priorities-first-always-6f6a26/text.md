Related questions Why does David Jolly say he was never a good enough Republican?
David Jolly supported positions on marriage equality, climate science, gun control, and campaign finance reform that didn't align with Republican orthodoxy.
See the video at 0:00 for his full explanation.
What does it mean to stand in the middle of the road politically?
Refusing to toe a party line often means facing criticism from both sides.
In his congressional career, David Jolly found that neither major party fully embraced someone unwilling to adopt their entire platform.
He calls it the axiom: if you stand in the middle of the road, you get run over.
How do most people practice their politics differently from how parties operate?
Most people don't live their lives pinpointing where on the left-right spectrum their solutions fall.
They ask whether something works for their family, their community.
Politics should follow that same instinct: solve the most number of problems for the most number of people.
