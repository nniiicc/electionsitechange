[5] Questions Floridians are asking Q.
When is the Florida governor election?
Election Day is Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Early voting runs statewide October 24 through October 31, and counties may add extra days.
Polls are open 7 am to 7 pm local time on Election Day.
Q.
When is the deadline to register to vote in Florida?
October 5, 2026.
You can register or check your registration through the Florida Division of Elections at registertovoteflorida.gov, or through your county Supervisor of Elections.
Q.
How do I vote by mail in Florida?
Request a vote-by-mail ballot from your county Supervisor of Elections by 5 pm local time on October 22, 2026.
Under current Florida law a request covers one election cycle, so even if you voted by mail before, confirm your request is on file for 2026.
Q.
Who won the Florida governor primaries?
In the August 18, 2026 primaries, David Jolly won the Democratic nomination and Byron Donalds won the Republican nomination.
Q.
Why isn't Ron DeSantis running?
Florida governors are limited to two consecutive terms.
Governor DeSantis completes his second term in January 2027, so the office is open.
Q.
Who is David Jolly's running mate?
Gwen Graham, a former Member of Congress from Florida's 2nd District and a longtime public schools advocate, is the Democratic nominee for Lieutenant Governor.
