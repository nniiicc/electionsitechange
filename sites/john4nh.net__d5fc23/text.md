Christine Walters My Story John Suiter is a dedicated family man, proud to be happily married and the father of four children.
His commitment to his community is evident in his service as a Selectman for the town of Mason, where he works tirelessly to ensure that the voices of local residents are heard and respected.
John is a Disabled Iraqi War Veteran, and his service to our country has instilled in him a deep sense of duty and patriotism.
This same sense of duty drives his unwavering stance on key issues that affect our community and state.
John is a strong advocate for parental rights, believing that parents should have the primary say in their children’s education and upbringing.
He is also a staunch supporter of the 2nd Amendment, firmly believing in the right of individuals to bear arms and protect their families and property.
A fiscal conservative, John believes that taxes need to be cut and reduced to alleviate the burden on hardworking families and businesses.
He is committed to fighting for policies that promote economic freedom and personal responsibility.
For John, “Live Free or Die” is far more than just a motto on a license plate; it is a guiding principle that underscores his commitment to liberty and the fundamental rights of every citizen.
John is ready to continue serving his community with the same dedication and integrity that has defined his life and career.
