Long Bio (150 words) Amanda Matchett is running for Minnesota House because she believes government should work for people.
She is a nationally recognized attorney with more than 15 years of experience, currently serving as Vice President of Legal for a technology company.
Amanda is also an educator who teaches law students how to negotiate, advocate, and lead.
Through her leadership in the Minnesota Association of Black Lawyers, Amanda co-founded programs that expand access to the legal profession.
She earned her Executive MBA from Northwestern University’s Kellogg School of Management, where she focused on technology, economics, and responsible innovation.
Amanda lives in Blaine with her fiancé and their rescue dog and cat.
She is the DFL-endorsed candidate for Minnesota House District 32A.
