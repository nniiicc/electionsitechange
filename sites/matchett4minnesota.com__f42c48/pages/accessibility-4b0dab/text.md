Our Commitment
Amanda Matchett for Minnesota is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for voters of all abilities.
We are continuously working to improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards.
Standards Followed
This site targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance.
Our development practices include semantic HTML structure, keyboard navigation support, descriptive alternative text on images, sufficient color contrast, and respect for reduced-motion preferences.
Limitations and Alternatives
Despite our best efforts, some content on this site may not yet be fully accessible.
If you encounter an issue, we want to hear from you so we can resolve it and make the information you need available in another format.
