Effective Date: April 26, 2026 Amanda Matchett for Minnesota House (“Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information in connection with our campaign activities, website, and communications.
1.
Information We Collect Information You Provide Name Email address Phone number Mailing address Employer and occupation (required for certain political contributions) Donation information Volunteer and event participation details Any other information you submit through forms or communications Automatically Collected Information IP address Device and browser type Pages visited and interactions 2.
How We Use Your Information Send campaign updates, fundraising requests, and event information Process donations and maintain contribution records Comply with federal, state, and local campaign finance laws Organize volunteers and campaign activities Improve website functionality and outreach Respond to inquiries 3.
Political Contributions & Legal Compliance All contributions are subject to applicable campaign finance laws and regulations.
Minnesota Campaign Finance Requirements We are required to collect and report certain donor information, including name, address, employer, and occupation for contributions above applicable thresholds Contributions may be publicly disclosed as required by the Minnesota Campaign Finance and Public Disclosure Board Contributions are not tax-deductible Political Contribution Refund (PCR) Program Minnesota residents may be eligible for a refund: Up to $75 per individual Up to $150 per couple Refunds are issued by the State of Minnesota and are not tax credits.
4.
ActBlue Contributions We may use ActBlue to process online donations.
Your payment information is processed securely by ActBlue ActBlue may collect and store your information according to its own privacy policy We receive donor information necessary for compliance and campaign operations We encourage you to review ActBlue’s privacy practices when making a donation.
5.
Text Messaging and Email Communications If you opt in: You agree to receive recurring automated campaign messages via SMS and email Message frequency varies Message and data rates may apply Opt-out options: Text STOP to unsubscribe from SMS Click unsubscribe in any email Consent to receive communications is not a condition of making a contribution.
6.
Sharing of Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share your information with: Service Providers Email and SMS platforms Payment processors (including ActBlue) Website and analytics providers Legal and Regulatory Authorities As required by campaign finance laws In response to lawful requests or legal obligations Campaign Staff and Volunteers As necessary to operate campaign activities 8.
Data Security We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your information.
However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
9.
Data Retention We retain your information as necessary to fulfill campaign purposes, comply with legal reporting requirements, and maintain campaign and financial records.
10.
Your Rights and Choices Opt out of communications at any time Request corrections to your information Contact us with questions about your data 11.
Third-Party Links Our website may include links to third-party platforms (such as ActBlue).
We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
12.
Children’s Privacy This website is not intended for individuals under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
13.
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy at any time.
Updates will be posted with a revised effective date.
Your continued use of campaign services constitutes acceptance of those changes.
