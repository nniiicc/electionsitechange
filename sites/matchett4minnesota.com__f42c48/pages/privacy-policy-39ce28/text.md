Information We Collect

- Donation information

- Any other information you submit through forms or communications
Automatically Collected Information

2.
How We Use Your Information
- Send campaign updates, fundraising requests, and event information

- Comply with federal, state, and local campaign finance laws
- Organize volunteers and campaign activities
- Improve website functionality and outreach
- Respond to inquiries
3.
Political Contributions & Legal Compliance
All contributions are subject to applicable campaign finance laws and regulations.
Minnesota Campaign Finance Requirements
- We are required to collect and report certain donor information, including name, address, employer, and occupation for contributions above applicable thresholds
- Contributions may be publicly disclosed as required by the Minnesota Campaign Finance and Public Disclosure Board
- Contributions are not tax-deductible
Political Contribution Refund (PCR) Program
Minnesota residents may be eligible for a refund:
- Up to $75 per individual
- Up to $150 per couple
Refunds are issued by the State of Minnesota and are not tax credits.

ActBlue Contributions
