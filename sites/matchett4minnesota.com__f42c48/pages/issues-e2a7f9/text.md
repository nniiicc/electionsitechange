The Platform
Priorities
Three priorities that reflect what families across the North Metro need most.
- Stability Strong communities depend on stable institutions, safe neighborhoods, and leaders who protect constitutional rights.
- Protect democratic institutions
- Support strong public schools
- Promote safe communities
Policy Priority Support election voting protections like Ranked Choice Voting.
- Affordability Rising costs are putting pressure on almost everyone.
Housing, healthcare, and childcare should be within reach.
- Housing affordability
- Manufactured housing protections
- Healthcare and childcare costs
Policy Priority Support the Manufactured Home Residents Bill of Rights.
- Opportunity The North Metro should remain a place where people can build a future, start businesses, and raise the next generation.
- Workforce development
- Small business growth
- Infrastructure investment
Policy Priority Secure continued investment in Highway 65 improvements.
