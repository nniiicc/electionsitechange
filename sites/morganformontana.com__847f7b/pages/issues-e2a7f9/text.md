Defend the Right to Bear Arms
Montanans have a long tradition of responsible gun ownership, and the right to keep and bear arms is protected in our state constitution.
I'll defend that right for hunters, ranchers, sport shooters, and families protecting their homes.
Rural Healthcare Access
Powell and Granite counties depend on small hospitals and clinics (Deer Lodge, Philipsburg, Drummond) that are one bad budget year from closing.
Position: protect Medicaid reimbursement rates for rural providers, support telehealth expansion, and fight for EMS/ambulance funding in low-population areas.
This is a life-and-death issue that doesn't split by party in a place where the nearest ER may be 40 minutes away.
Public Lands & Access
This district's economy and identity are tied to hunting, fishing, and public land access (national forest, BLM land, historic mining land).
Position: oppose transfer or sale of public lands to private or federal hands that would restrict access; support responsible multiple-use management that keeps land open for hunters, anglers, ranchers grazing permits, and recreation businesses.
Strong Education
We need to strengthen public schools and get students trained earlier with trade school school partnerships, so they can enter the job market right after high school without carrying the burden of student loans.
More specialized training in STEM curriculum such as cybersecurity, programming, agentic artificial intelligence and machine learning.
Protect Medicaid
Medicaid keeps Montana families, seniors, and rural hospitals going.
When coverage is cut, people delay care, clinics lose funding, and small-town hospitals struggle to stay open.
In the Legislature, I'll fight to protect Medicaid coverage and oppose cuts that put care out of reach for the people in our district.
Ban Kratom
Kratom products are sold today in gas stations and convenience stores across Montana, often as drinks or pills, with no age limits and no rules about what's in them.
Highly concentrated versions act on the brain like opioids, and families in our communities are paying the price.
I'll vote to ban kratom in Montana and protect our kids and neighbors from another addictive drug on store shelves.
Invest in Geothermal Energy
Montana sits on real geothermal potential, and it's an energy source that runs around the clock, needs no fuel, and takes up very little land.
Drilling technology has improved fast, and much of the skill needed to tap underground heat is the same work Montana's oil and gas and mining workers already do.
I'll support smart, responsible geothermal development in Montana, so we can create good local jobs, keep energy costs stable, and put our own resources to work for our own communities.
Yes on the Montana Plan (I-194)
Big money has taken over too much of our politics.
I-194, known as the Montana Plan, would stop corporations and other artificial entities from spending money to influence elections.
It has drawn support from people across the political spectrum.
I'm voting yes, and I'm asking you to join me, so that Montanans, not deep-pocketed interests, decide our elections.
Protect Veterans
Those who served our country shouldn't have to fight for the care and benefits they earned.
I'll work to make sure Montana veterans have access to health care, housing, job opportunities, and the support they need when they come home.
