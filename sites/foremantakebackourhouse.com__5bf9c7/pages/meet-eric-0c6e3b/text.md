Eric Michael Foreman is a lifelong Nebraskan, working-class family man, and Libertarian candidate for the United States House of Representatives in Nebraska's 2nd Congressional District.
Raised in Lincoln, planted his roots in Omaha.
Eric understands the challenges facing ordinary Americans because he has lived them.
He has spent his career working multiple jobs to make ends meet.
From kitchens to contsruction.
Now in property management and maintenance.
Solving problems, managing responsibilities, and helping and serving people from all walks of life.
He knows what it means to earn a paycheck, balance a budget, raise a family, and navigate the realities of an economy that increasingly feels stacked against hardworking Americans.
As a follower of Christ, Eric believes every person is created with inherent dignity and deserves the opportunity to pursue a better life through hard work, personal responsibility, and freedom.
His political philosophy is grounded in the principles that made America exceptional: individual liberty, limited government, fiscal responsibility, strong communities, and equal justice for all.
Eric is running for Congress because he believes Nebraska deserves real representation.
He believes Washington has become disconnected from the people it serves and that both major parties have contributed to growing debt, rising costs, and increasing division.
He is committed to bringing a practical, common-sense approach to Congress focused on lowering the cost of living, restoring accountability, protecting constitutional rights, strengthening communities, and putting the interests of Nebraskans ahead of politics.
Above all, Eric believes the promise of America is worth preserving for the next generation.
His campaign is built around a simple idea: Life, Liberty, the Pursuit of Happiness, with Justice for All.
