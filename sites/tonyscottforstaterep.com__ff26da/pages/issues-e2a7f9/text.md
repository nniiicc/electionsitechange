Key Issues Facing Monroe, Easton and Trumbull Voters
Taxes – Connecticut continues to be one of the highest taxed states in the country.
NPR reported earlier this year that Connecticut residents have the 5th highest tax burden in the United States.
Tax and spend Democrats in Hartford have been running Connecticut too long and will keep taking your hard-working dollars to spend on more bloated government programs.
In the latest budget passed in June, Republicans stood strong and worked with the Governor to lower taxes for many residents.
We need someone up there to fight for no new taxes and tax decreases.
I have been and am willing to continue to do just that.
Municipality Funding – Since being elected, I have helped bring in millions of dollars in grants and other types of funding for Monroe, Easton & Trumbull.
We need to continue to have a voice in Hartford that will fight for towns as hard as others do for the cities.
Just because our towns are more financially prudent and smaller in size, does not make them less deserving.
Our towns are in need of funding for Education, Public Safety and Infrastructure.
Local Control – Towns across Connecticut are under attack from legislators in Hartford and activists from the cities to take away local zoning decisions from towns and instead install a one-size-fits-all approach to zoning regulations.
We need housing of all different kinds and sizes, but the market will dictate where and when that gets built.
If a large apartment complex does not fit the downtown of a town, they should not be worried about getting sued or having no say because some legislature from New Haven or Norwalk or Stamford says so.
As the Ranking Member of the Housing Committee, I can promise I will stand up for our towns and how we want to have a say as to how they will be developed.
Education – Everyone understands the importance of educating our children, the future leaders of Connecticut, but we need to continue to keep pushing to make it easier for communities to provide this essential right.
Elements like teacher pensions and arbitration need to be looked at to not hinder cities and towns from providing the best education at affordable costs.
Safety & Security – One of the biggest issues I hear about in my district is worry about safety.
Crime is up in so many ways (like car break-ins) but police have been handcuffed to not act as the criminals many times are not help accountable and won’t be prosecuted.
The Clean Slate law just took into effect which wipes some felonies and misdemeanors from criminal’s records.
So even when they do the crime and found guilty, they ultimately are not help accountable.
What about the victims?
This is the direction the state of Connecticut is heading in where criminal’s rights are more important than victim’s rights.
