How do I register to vote in person?
You’ll need valid identification, such as an Idaho driver’s license or identification card and proof of residency (for example, a utility bill).
Register to vote in person on ELECTION DAY, November 8th, at your polling location .
Or register in person: Bonner County: Through November 4 at the Elections Office, Bonner County Administration Building , on the corner of Highway 2 and Division, Sandpoint.
