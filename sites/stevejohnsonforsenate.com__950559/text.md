Independent for North Idaho Families
I’m Steve Johnson.
I am an Independent Candidate running for Idaho State Senate.
I’m a farmer, home-builder, small business owner, and semi-retired teacher.
I’m married to Marguerite, my childhood sweetheart, and my adulthood sweetheart.
We have four amazing children and nine wonderful grandchildren.
My mom and dad bought our family farm in Sagle in 1957 and it’s been Home ever since.
My main goal as a state senator is to improve the daily lives of Idaho citizens.
I believe affordability issues are top priority.
We all know the cost of living continues to increase, including gas, food, utilities, housing, childcare and healthcare.
My focus will be on programs that directly benefit North Idaho families.
– Steve Johnson
