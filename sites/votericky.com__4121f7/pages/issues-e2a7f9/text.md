- Problem:
- The "starter home" has become a myth, with housing prices doubling, tripling, or even quadrupling in the past decade, pushing homeownership out of reach for young families.
- MAGA Republicans blame frivolous spending (like avocado toast and lattes) while ignoring the real issues: limited housing supply, excessive building restrictions, and outdated zoning laws.
- These policies force developers to expand into natural green spaces rather than building efficiently in existing communities.
- Without action, we risk creating car-dependent, traffic-choked suburbs instead of strong, walkable neighborhoods.
Solution:
- Encourage attainable housing construction to increase supply and lower costs.
- Reform restrictive zoning laws to allow for smarter, denser development with local input.
- Subsidize infill housing (building on unused urban land) to revitalize communities without sprawl.
- Invest in transit-friendly neighborhoods to reduce traffic and pollution.
Why It Matters:
Homeownership is a pathway to stability and wealth-building but right now, it’s slipping away.
Smart housing policy means stronger communities, less sprawl, and a fair shot for the next generation.
"We need homes our families can afford—not more excuses.
I’ll fight to cut red tape, lower costs, and build communities that work for everyone." - Ricky Rivard
