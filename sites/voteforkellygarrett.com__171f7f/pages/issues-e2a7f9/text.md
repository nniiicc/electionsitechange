The Issues Kelly Will Fight For
Kelly Garrett is fighting for a Michigan where people can live with dignity, stability, and real opportunity.
She understands that families are being stretched thin and that far too many are forced to make impossible choices just to get by.
From the cost of healthcare and education to keeping the lights and heat on, Kelly believes government should work for the people who do the work every day.
She is committed to strengthening schools, ensuring cities receive fair funding, standing up for workers, keeping communities safe, and protecting our democracy.
Her focus is simple and unwavering.
No family should struggle this hard to survive, and every community deserves the resources it needs to thrive.
Greater investment in public education
Strong schools are the foundation of strong communities.
I support fair and adequate funding for public education, smaller class sizes, support for educators, and safe learning environments so every child has the opportunity to succeed.
Affordable and accessible healthcare
Everyone deserves access to quality healthcare without fear of crushing costs.
I will fight to lower healthcare expenses, protect coverage, and expand access so families do not have to choose between seeing a doctor and paying their bills.
Fair Revenue Sharing for Cities
Cities need reliable funding to maintain roads, water systems, public safety, and essential services.
I will advocate for fair revenue sharing so communities receive the state support they are owed and can plan for the future with confidence.
Advocacy for Workers
People who work hard should be able to make ends meet.
I will stand up for fair wages, safe workplaces, and policies that respect the dignity of work so families can put food on the table, keep the lights and heat on, and remain secure in their homes.
Keeping Our Communities Safe
Safe neighborhoods matter to everyone.
I support strong, well resourced, and accountable public safety while also investing in prevention and community stability so families can feel secure where they live.
Protecting Our Democracy
Our democracy works best when every voice is heard and every vote counts.
I will defend voting rights, support fair and transparent elections, and protect the integrity of our democratic process.
