WV Deputy Sheriff’s Association endorse Drennan
December 20, 2023
I’m absolutely thrilled to share that I’ve just received my first endorsement from the WV Deputy Sheriff’s Association!
I am beyond honored and grateful for the Association’s belief in my candidacy.
From the very beginning, I instilled in my two boys the importance of expressing gratitude to our officers and military for their dedicated service.
It’s not just a commitment for me, but a family tradition.
My brother-in-law, a veteran deputy and Captain with the Kanawha County Sheriff’s Department, exemplifies the values of service and protection that I hold dear.
I’m deeply moved by the support and confidence shown by the WV Deputy Sheriff’s Association.
Let’s continue working together to bring positive change to our community!
