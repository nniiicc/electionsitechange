Sarah Drennan attends Healthcare Providers Legislative Reception
February 10, 2024
On February 8th, 2024, I had the privilege of attending the Healthcare Providers Legislative Reception, a pivotal event demonstrating our commitment to healthcare in West Virginia.
This gathering, convened by esteemed organizations such as the WV Hospital Association, the WV Behavioral Healthcare Providers Association, The Health Care Association, and the WV State Medical Association, facilitated meaningful dialogue between healthcare professionals and elected officials.
As a nurse dedicated to serving our communities within a hospital environment, I am acutely aware of the significant contributions made by hospitals and healthcare providers across our state.
From nursing homes to behavioral health facilities, from physicians to hospital staff, these entities collectively employ hundreds of thousands of individuals, enriching the fabric of West Virginia.
Participating in this event was both enlightening and humbling.
It reinforced my appreciation for the tireless efforts of those in the healthcare sector and highlighted the critical importance of legislative support in advancing our healthcare initiatives.
Looking ahead, I am hopeful for the opportunity to attend future Healthcare Providers Legislative Receptions as a duly elected House member representing the 20th district.
With your unwavering support, I am confident that together, we can advocate for policies that prioritize the well-being of our communities and ensure equitable access to quality healthcare for all West Virginians.
