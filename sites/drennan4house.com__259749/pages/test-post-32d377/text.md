Sarah Drennan
House of Delegates
Today, accompanied by the boys and Mark, we went to the Capitol to officially file my candidacy for the House of Delegates in the 20th district.
It was a truly special experience – meeting current Delegates, standing behind the podium for a photo, and engaging with House leadership.
The conversations centered around the issues facing House members and the state of West Virginia.
My key takeaway is a commitment to serve with dignity and honor, voting according to my conscience and the will of my constituents.
I left with a strong sense that, together, we can make a meaningful difference in Putnam County and the entire state.
A piece of advice from a current Delegate and state-wide candidate resonated with me: “Let people know who you are and why you’re running in 25 words or less.” So, here it is: Hello, I’m Sarah Drennan, running for House of Delegates 20th District.
I aim to shape a West Virginia where our children choose to build their families!
I would appreciate your help and support!
