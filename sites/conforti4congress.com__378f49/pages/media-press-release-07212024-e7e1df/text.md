“With the Democrat Party in disarray over the lack of leadership from Joe Biden, and his recent

IL-06 has been of interest to Republicans, both locally and nationally, since Sean Casten unseated
former Congressman Peter Roskam in 2018.
The district, which includes large parts of DuPage
County, also brought in the moderate terrain of the Southwest Side of the City of Chicago and
surrounding Cook County suburbs in the 2021 redistricting process.

Driven by her passion to help others, Niki has been involved with numerous philanthropic efforts
through her church, as well as with IOCC (International Orthodox Christian Charities), and
