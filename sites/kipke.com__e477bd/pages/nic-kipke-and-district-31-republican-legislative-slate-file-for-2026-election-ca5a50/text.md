This announcement follows the decision of Senator Bryan Simonaire not to seek reelection after two decades of service to the district.
Today, Delegate Kipke joined Delegate Brian Chisholm, Delegate LaToya Caldwell-Nkongolo, and Mike Jacobs as they filed to run for the Maryland House of Delegates.
Together, the four candidates are campaigning as a team slate called Republicans for the Future , seeking the support of Republican voters in the upcoming Republican Primary Election on Tuesday, June 23, 2026.
