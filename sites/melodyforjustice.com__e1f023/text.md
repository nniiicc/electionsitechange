Meet Justice Melody
Born and raised in Spokane County, Colleen grew up in a family of public servants.
Her father was an elementary school teacher and principal, and her mother was a nurse who worked at the Department of Health.
Colleen worked through college waiting tables, and then attended law school with a commitment to serving her community as a lawyer.
Colleen has dedicated her career to protecting the rights of Washingtonians.
She built the Civil Rights Division of the Washington State Attorney General’s Office from the ground-up, turning it from a startup into a nationally recognized powerhouse that protects state residents from harassment, discrimination, and violence no matter their gender, race, age, or disability.
She has taken on Fortune 100 companies for unfair business practices and held the Federal Government accountable for violating the rights of Washingtonians.
From trial courts in every corner of our state to the U.S.
Supreme Court, Colleen has stood firm for the rule of law and all of our rights.

Endorsements
