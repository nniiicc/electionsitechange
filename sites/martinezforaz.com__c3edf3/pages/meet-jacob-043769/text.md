Raised in Mesa and a proud graduate of Mesa Public Schools and ASU Law, Jacob understands firsthand how strong classrooms and supportive educators can shape a young person’s future.
In addition to his local roots, Jacob serves in the U.S.
Navy Reserve, where he has strengthened his commitment to service, leadership, and accountability.
That same commitment drives his work in the community, from advocating for students to helping neighbors navigate some of the area’s most pressing challenges.
Jacob is running to fight for stronger public schools, expanded economic opportunity, and policies that uplift working families across Mesa, Tempe, and the surrounding communities.
He believes LD 9 deserves a representative who listens, shows up, and works every day to deliver real results.
