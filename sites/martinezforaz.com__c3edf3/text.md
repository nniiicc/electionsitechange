Meet Jacob
A lifelong resident of Mesa and proud graduate of Mesa Public Schools and ASU Law, Jacob has dedicated his life to service—in the courtroom, in the community, and in the United States Navy.
From advocating for students to standing on the frontlines of some of our most pressing issues, Jacob has always fought for the people of LD9.
As your next State Representative, he’ll bring commonsense leadership rooted in accountability, fairness, and opportunity.
About LD9
Legislative District (LD) 9 encompasses West and Downtown Mesa and a small portion of eastern Tempe.
