During the Stark County Fair in September 2026, candidate Nick Morris was asked to be the judge of the Beyond Barriers Livestock Show, a livestock event for youth with special needs.
Nick was not just flattered to be asked, he announced it was his “favorite thing at the Stark County Fair.”
The event was featured on Jordan Miller News, where Jordan Miller called it, “One of the most heartwarming moments I’ve experienced at the Stark County Fair,” on his Facebook news feed.
“Today I had the chance to attend the Beyond Barriers Livestock Show, a special program that gives individuals with disabilities the opportunity to step into the show ring and experience what it’s like to exhibit an animal at the fair,” wrote Miller.
“Each participant is paired with an experienced mentor who helps them work with an animal before they enter the ring alongside a judge.
And, most importantly, every exhibitor gets their moment to be recognized in the winner’s circle.”
“Watching the smiles, excitement and pure pride on their faces today was something special,” Miller wrote.
“This is about so much more than showing livestock.
It’s about inclusion, confidence, friendship and giving everyone an opportunity to experience a part of the fair they might not otherwise get to experience.”
