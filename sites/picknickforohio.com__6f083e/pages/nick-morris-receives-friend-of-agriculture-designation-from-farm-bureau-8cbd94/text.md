Nick Morris has been designated a “Friend of Agriculture” by The Stark County Farm Bureau!
The Bureau’s Agriculture for Good Government Political Action Committee sends local representatives to interview candidates before choosing which candidates to endorse with this title.
Our campaign is proud that Nick has been recognized for who he is: a community leader who cares deeply about agriculture, farmers and rural communities.
“Over the course of my career I’ve studied the history of agriculture and fought for its future, and both have been a joy,” said Morris.
“Fifteen years ago our family made the decision to welcome agriculture into our home life, starting with a dozen laying hens.
Those hens kicked off quite a run of experiences, including hogs, lambs and goats.
From filling our own freezer and feeding our friends to hosting a diverse collection of 4-H animals today, our journey has been real and I wouldn’t change a thing … well maybe stronger fences for our first goats.
This Friend of Agriculture recognition from the Farm Bureau is very meaningful, both personally and professionally.”
