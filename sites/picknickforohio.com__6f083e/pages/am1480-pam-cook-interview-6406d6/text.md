Nick Morris and his wife, Sarah Morris, were interviewed on Pam Cook’s show on 1480 WHBC.
Nick emphasized his preparedness for the job, having the skills it takes to succeed as a lawmaker and leader at the Statehouse, and the work ethic to represent the district well.
Speaking about the retirement of Ohio Statehouse Rep.
Scott Oelslager, Pam Cook said, “There’s a guy I knew for a long time, who was a worker.”
“The people who elected him would certainly be looking for the same thing,” Cook said.
Nick Morris recounted being asked to run for the seat and saying, “I’m not going to run against Scott Oelslager!
He’s an institution.”
“And he does the job, well!” added Sarah Morris.
Nick said when he learned Oeslager was not running for re-election, he realized now was the time to run for office.
“I have a lot of respect for Scott as a leader,” said Morris.
“I’m happy to pick up the good things he’s got going and has developed in this district and carry those forth as well.”
