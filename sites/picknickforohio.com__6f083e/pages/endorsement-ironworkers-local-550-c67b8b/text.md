When Nick Morris decided to run for the Ohio Statehouse seat representing Stark County, his first community conversation was with the Ironworkers local 550 leadership to understand the concerns of the members and more broadly talk about what labor unions value.
From that first meeting to today, the Ironworkers have been there for this campaign.
We appreciate the open and honest conversations and we are proud to share that Nick Morris has received their endorsement.
“Working hard so working families can invest in themselves for the future” is a key piece of Nick’s mission and he looks forward to continuing to work together.
Thank you Ironworkers 550.
