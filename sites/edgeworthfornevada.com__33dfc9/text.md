why i'm running
As the only practicing physician serving in the Nevada Assembly, I see firsthand the challenges facing Nevada families, seniors, small businesses, and healthcare providers.
I ran for the Legislature to bring practical solutions—not politics—to Carson City and to fight for safer communities, better healthcare, and a stronger Nevada economy.
ABOUT REBECCA
Born at Nellis Air Force Base and trained at UMC, Rebecca has spent her career caring for Nevada patients and teaching future healthcare professionals.
As a physician, educator, and legislator, she is committed to protecting public safety, expanding access to healthcare, supporting small businesses, and keeping Nevada a place where families can thrive.
