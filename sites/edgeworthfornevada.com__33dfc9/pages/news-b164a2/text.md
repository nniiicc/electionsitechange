October Bringing Our Community Together One of the things I have been most focused on is making sure people in our district have opportunities to meet me, ask questions, and get to know the person behind the name on the ballot.
September was filled with some incredible events!
I hosted a movie night in the park with the Mountain’s Edge Master Association where we watched Top Gun!.
We had a Taco Truck event at Somerset Park in Southern Highlands, a happy hour at Spaghetty Western, one of our local institutions, and our first-ever event at Bowlz LV, where I got to meet an entirely new group of constituents while enjoying some delicious spam musubi. ​ Read All August ​ Sons of the Revolution ​ The Nevada Constitution makes the Legislature a part-time citizen legislature, and I believe that's one of its greatest strengths.
The best legislators stay connected to the people they serve by continuing to live, work, and serve in their communities.
When people ask what I do for a living, I tell them I'm a physician and a professor.
Both roles are deeply rewarding.
This month I taught medical students about caring for older adults, a field that has always been close to my heart.
I also brought students into the community to care for underserved patients, because I believe future healthcare providers learn just as much by serving people where they are as they do in the classroom.
My work as a physician helps shape my work in Carson City Read All June June was a very busy month!

Read All September A Few Months to Go!
Election Day, is almost here!
As we get closer to the next legislative session, I’ve already started working on legislation, with a major focus on something I care deeply about: expanding access to quality, affordable healthcare for Nevadans.
I’ve been meeting with doctors, nurses, hospital administrators, healthcare educators, coalitions, and people throughout our healthcare system to hear what we’re doing well and should expand, and where there are gaps we need to address.
I want to bring real-world experience and practical solutions to Carson City, not just make promises. ​ ​ Read All July Staying Busy While I didn't have a primary election, there wasn't much time to slow down.
Now that the primaries are behind us, we know what's ahead in the general election, and the real work begins.
Outside of the Legislature, I stayed busy in my work as a physician and professor, teaching the next generation of healthcare providers.
Summer is one of the toughest times of the year for those experiencing homelessness, and our team continued providing care to men, women, and children across the valley.
By bringing healthcare directly to people in need, we can often prevent unnecessary emergency room visits.
I had the opportunity to visit The Courtyard, SHARE Village, the Catholic Worker, and several other community locations.
