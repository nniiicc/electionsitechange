ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 35 SEPTEMBER NEWSLETTER A Few Months to Go!
Election Day, is almost here!
As we get closer to the next legislative session, I’ve already started working on legislation, with a major focus on something I care deeply about: expanding access to quality, affordable healthcare for Nevadans.
I’ve been meeting with doctors, nurses, hospital administrators, healthcare educators, coalitions, and people throughout our healthcare system to hear what we’re doing well and should expand, and where there are gaps we need to address.
I want to bring real-world experience and practical solutions to Carson City, not just make promises.
A Strong Economy and Better Healthcare Go Hand in Hand Supporting Nevada businesses is also a major part of what I believe in.
I truly believe that a healthier Nevada will also be a stronger Nevada economically.
I’m proud to be supported by organizations including the Las Vegas and Henderson Chambers of Commerce, Nevada Mining, Nevada REALTORS®, and Nevada Gaming.
I want Nevada to be a place where businesses can grow, and people can build a good life.
I’ve also been hearing a lot of discussion and rumors about legislators considering tax increases.
I want to be very clear: I oppose raising taxes on Nevada families and businesses!
Before asking Nevadans for more of their hard-earned money, I believe we need greater transparency and accountability about how the money we already have is being spent.
Healthcare Isn’t Just Politics for Me.
It’s My Life.
Of course, I have to squeeze all of this legislative work around my actual jobs as a physician and professor!
As many of you know, I continue to work in the clinic, caring primarily for underserved Nevadans.
A lot of people talk about improving healthcare and making it more affordable.
For me, this isn’t a talking point.
It is something I see and work on every day.
The same is true when we talk about expanding Nevada’s healthcare workforce.
I’ve spent my entire career teaching and preparing the next generation of healthcare professionals.
For me, this isn’t about vague promises.
Healthcare, education, and serving our community are what I have dedicated my life to.
Working for Nevada During the Interim We wrapped up both of my interim legislative committees this month.
The Health and Human Services Committee submitted 15 bill recommendations covering issues including maternal and fetal health, children in foster care, access to mental healthcare, and other important priorities.
We won’t see the final details and bill language until the legislative session, but I’m encouraged by the work being done and the ideas being developed to help Nevadans.
We also wrapped up the work of my other interim committee, Seniors, Veterans and Disabled Persons.
One meeting included some heartbreaking testimony about the way veterans have been treated in the past.
I hope that by bringing these stories into the light and having honest conversations, we can acknowledge where we have fallen short and make sure we do better.
A Special Weekend with Nevada Physicians This month also brought the annual Nevada State Medical Association meeting at the M Resort.
Fun fact: I have a gigantic billboard right before the St.
Rose exit, so everyone heading to the meeting got to see my giant head!
The weekend included a legislative mixer where candidates had the opportunity to meet physicians from around Nevada, as well as a beautiful Gala dinner celebrating outstanding leaders in our community.
It was wonderful to celebrate Governor Joe Lombardo and his commitment to making healthcare a priority for Nevada.
And then I received an incredible honor of my own: I was named Community Physician of the Year.
That recognition means so much to me, especially because I know how many physicians across Nevada are giving their time and energy every day to improve our communities.
As a member of NSMA, I was also able to participate in the weekend’s discussions about upcoming resolutions and potential legislative priorities.
I’m grateful for the opportunity to be a voice for physicians and for the patients we serve.
Out in the Community This month was also full of opportunities to spend time with constituents and supporters.
We held our first Super Walk, and I was so grateful to see a group of dedicated supporters come out on a Saturday morning to knock doors and talk with families in the community.
It was HOT , but the time flew by!
We also held our monthly coffee at Aware Coffee, and it was wonderful to see the group continuing to grow.
We had nurses, teachers, a psychologist, college students, retirees, and many others join us.
Hearing directly from you about what is happening in your lives is one of my favorite parts of serving in the Assembly.
We also held our second Happy Hour at Spaghetty Western, where dozens of people came out to talk.
We discussed education, the affordability of groceries and housing, and, of course, healthcare.
I’m so grateful for every conversation, every question, and every person who takes the time to share their thoughts with me.
