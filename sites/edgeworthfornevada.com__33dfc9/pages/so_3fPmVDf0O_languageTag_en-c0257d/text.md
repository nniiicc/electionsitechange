ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 35 NEWSLETTER Hello friends, and happy New Year!
As we head into a new year, things are already moving fast!
Work, policy, and politics are in full swing.
I’ve set ambitious goals for the months ahead: staying connected with our community, developing thoughtful and impactful legislation, and of course, earning your trust and support to be re-elected so I can continue serving you.
Even outside of session, the work never slows down.
Interim committees are underway, and I’m excited to be actively involved during this critical time.
I’m proud to serve on the Health and Human Services Committee as well as the Senior, Veterans, and Disabled Interim Committee.
Interim is our opportunity to monitor how policies are working, identifying gaps, and laying the groundwork for real solutions.
This is where meaningful progress happens, even in the “off season.” This month, I’ve been meeting with community partners to continue the work I started last session.
One highlight was the honor of meeting with Nathan Adelson and Dr.
Oz to discuss improving care for our most vulnerable neighbors.
Hospice care, when done right, is truly a blessing for families, and I remain deeply committed to improving end-of-life care.
My bill, AB161, was an important step forward, and I’m encouraged to see this issue gaining national attention.
Likewise, AB315, which focused on reducing Medicaid provider fraud, is also being recognized beyond Nevada.
These two issues are especially close to my heart.
My work on healthcare workforce development continues as well.
Nevada faces critical shortages across many healthcare professions, and we must act boldly.
As a professor, I’m fortunate to work hands-on in the classroom and in clinical settings to help train the next generation of healthcare professionals.
Together with students and colleagues, we also serve people in our community who lack access to care.
Improving Nevada’s healthcare system will take all of us working together.
I’m truly inspired by the community leaders I continue to meet.
These leaders share a commitment to strengthening education and healthcare across our state.
I’m deeply honored to be endorsed by our Governor, who has made both of these priorities for Nevada’s future.
I hope you’ll join me at one of my monthly Coffee with Constituents events, but please don’t hesitate to reach out anytime with questions, ideas, or concerns.
