ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 35 MARCH NEWSLETTER February may have been a short month, but it was absolutely packed both for me and for my campaign!
One of the greatest privileges of serving is connecting with the people who make our community so special.
From my monthly Coffee with Constituents gatherings to thoughtful conversations with leaders who shape our neighborhoods, I’m constantly inspired.
I had the honor of meeting with our Enterprise Area Command LVMPD officers, who shared valuable insight into practical changes we can make to keep our community safer.
As legislators, we also have the opportunity to see firsthand the incredible work being done across Las Vegas.
I toured Foster Kinship and Child Haven and witnessed the life-changing impact they have on vulnerable children and families.
I connected with outstanding business leaders at the Vegas Chamber and Latin Chamber, and spent time with engaged grassroots advocates at events like the Log Cabin Republicans and the CCRP Lincoln Day Dinner.
There is nothing more energizing than being surrounded by people who are passionate about making Nevada stronger.
A true highlight of the month was visiting The Boring Company’s Vegas Loop.
As a Las Vegas native, I never could have imagined the innovative tunnel system being built beneath our city.
Seeing how quickly and seamlessly you can travel under the Strip in zero-emission vehicles that take you directly to your destination genuinely feels like stepping into the future.
Even while serving as a part-time legislator, my work in healthcare remains central to who I am.
Expanding access to care and training the next generation of providers are deeply personal missions.
This month, we partnered with incredible organizations including Share Village, the Guatemalan Consulate, The Courtyard, Catholic Charities, the Catholic Worker, HELP of Southern Nevada, The City of North Las Vegas, and Volunteers in Medicine of Southern Nevada to serve those who need it most.
Caring for our medically fragile and underserved neighbors continues to guide my priorities.
February also brought a beautiful celebration of our community’s diversity with several Lunar New Year events including the ACDC Gala, the ACC Gala, and a vibrant festival at La Vang Church.
It was wonderful to gather with friends, enjoy incredible food, and celebrate traditions filled with color and joy.
Here’s hoping the Year of the Horse brings health and prosperity to all!
Many people ask how Nevada legislators accomplish so much in just 120 days every other year.
The truth is that, the work continues year-round through interim committees.
I proudly serve on the Health and Human Services Committee and the Seniors, Veterans, and Disabled Committee.
These are exactly where my heart and professional experience align.
March will be especially exciting as candidates officially file to run.
I will proudly file for re-election, and I am deeply grateful for your continued support.
Please join me for Coffee with Constituents on March 21st!
