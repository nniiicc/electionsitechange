REBECCA ON THE ISSUES Rebecca serves in the Nevada Assembly to be a strong, consistent voice for the people she represents.
From strengthening healthcare and education to supporting safe communities and economic opportunity, she is committed to fighting for real solutions that reflect the priorities of Nevada families.
EDUCATION Rebecca is an Assistant Professor and teaches in higher education.
She wants teachers to have the resources they need, money to make its way into the classroom, and for parents and children to have more access to a quality education.
PUBLIC SAFETY Rebecca has dedicated her life to helping Nevadans and knows fewer things are more important than a safe and secure community.
She supports law enforcement and the public servants who work so hard to keep our community safe.
SUPPORTING SMALL BUSINESS Rebecca understands businesses are the backbone of Nevada.
She wants Nevada to have the best climate in the country when it comes to doing business and one that innovators want to be a part of.
She believes businesses need less regulation and taxation in order to truly thrive.
HEALTHCARE Rebecca has dedicated her life to lifting up healthcare for Nevadans, working to expand access, improve quality, and support the patients and providers who keep our communities strong.
She is committed to delivering real solutions that make care more affordable, more accessible, and more effective for every family across our state DEMANDING ACCOUNTABILITY Rebecca wants to make sure that the government and our officials are accountable to you.
She believes we need more transparency in government and will demand it in Carson City.
PROTECTING CIVIL LIBERTIES Rebecca is dedicated to fighting for our individual liberties and protecting our individual freedoms.
She believes in our Constitution and wants every citizen's right protected.
