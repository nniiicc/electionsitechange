ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 35 NEWSLETTER Friends, As I look back on the past year, I’m filled with gratitude and excitement.
Completing both my first regular session and special session in the Assembly was an incredible milestone.
Along the way, I’ve had the privilege of meeting inspiring community leaders dedicated stakeholders, and passionate constituents who pour their hearts into making Nevada a better place every single day.
Your commitment motivates me more than you know.
December was truly a whirlwind, filled with meaningful holiday events and moments of reflection.
Participating in Hanukkah celebrations was especially powerful this year, coming at a time of rising antisemitism around the world.
I was also grateful for the opportunity to celebrate the Christmas season with friends and colleagues.
The holidays reminded me of what matters most.
I also made time in December to connect directly with you.
If you haven’t joined me yet for Coffee with Constituents, I hope you will!
These conversations are one of my favorite parts of the job!
I love hearing what’s important to you, and yes, the coffee is ALWAYS on me.
Join me Saturday, January 24th, from 10:00 a.m.–12:00 p.m. at Aware Coffee on South Rainbow.
I’ll also be out and about at many more events in the coming months, and I look forward to meeting even more of you.
December is always a meaningful time for community outreach, particularly supporting underserved populations by proving free medical care.
I also had the opportunity to do a ride-along with the LVMPD Homeless Outreach Team (HOT).
Seeing firsthand the difficult work they do was eye-opening and heartbreaking.
Visiting tunnels and parks underscored the urgent need to continue addressing homelessness with empathy and action.
As we look ahead to 2026, I’m energized by what’s to come.
I’m already gathering ideas for important legislation I hope to bring forward if I’m fortunate enough to be re-elected.
And yes!
I am running for re-election this November, and I would be honored to have your support.
If you’re interested in volunteering or contributing to the campaign, please reach out.
Door-knocking will be an all-hands-on-deck effort, but there are lots of ways you can contribute.
Thank you, as always, for the trust you place in me.
