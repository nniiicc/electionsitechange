ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 35 OCTOBER NEWSLETTER Friends, we are at the Final Stretch!
The push is on as we head into the final stretch of the election, but honestly, it feels like I’ve never stopped campaigning.
There is always another community leader to meet, another conversation to have, another part of our state to learn about, and, most importantly, more constituents to listen to.
That is what I love about this work.
Being your Assemblywoman means being out in the community, hearing directly from the people I represent, and understanding what matters most to you and your families.
Why Healthcare Will Always Be Personal to Me My work teaching and providing medical care in our community never stops, and I wouldn’t have it any other way.
When you work with so many underserved populations across Nevada, you are constantly reminded of why the work we do in the Legislature matters.
The laws we pass directly affect how doctors care for their patients and the care that Nevadans receive.
Access to healthcare will continue to be a challenge for our state, but we are making progress.
We are educating and training more healthcare professionals, including physicians, nurses, physical therapists, and so many others.
Nevada still has a significant shortage of healthcare professionals, and I know firsthand how important it is that we continue building that pipeline.
As a physician, professor, and legislator, this is an issue I will continue to fight for.
Serving You Means Showing Up For more than three years, I have been out in the community hosting monthly coffees, holiday celebrations, garage sales, movie nights in the park, and other opportunities for neighbors to come together.
I believe that as your representative, it is incredibly important for me to connect with you.
And this is not something I only do during election season.
I’ve never stopped doing it !
Of course, one of the best ways to meet people is to go directly into our neighborhood and knock on doors.
Sometimes people answer, sometimes they don’t.
But the conversations I’ve had at the doorstep have been some of the most meaningful.
I’ve talked with neighbors about public safety, healthcare, affordability, and the everyday issues that affect their families.
What those conversations have shown me is that people care deeply about our community.
They want Nevada to be a place where their families can thrive, and they want their elected representatives to listen.
Those are the issues we are working on in the Legislature, and they are the issues I look forward to continuing to work on.
Bringing Our Community Together One of the things I have been most focused on is making sure people in our district have opportunities to meet me, ask questions, and get to know the person behind the name on the ballot.
September was filled with some incredible events!
I hosted a movie night in the park with the Mountain’s Edge Master Association where we watched Top Gun!.
We had a Taco Truck event at Somerset Park in Southern Highlands, a happy hour at Spaghetty Western, one of our local institutions, and our first-ever event at Bowlz LV, where I got to meet an entirely new group of constituents while enjoying some delicious spam musubi.
These events have allowed me to meet hundreds of people in our district, hear what matters to them, and, hopefully, make sure they know exactly who they are voting for.
And we have so much more coming up!
