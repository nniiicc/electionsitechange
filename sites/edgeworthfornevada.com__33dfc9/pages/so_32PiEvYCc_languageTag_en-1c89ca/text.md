ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 35 NEWSLETTER Friends, November was such a busy month!
Just as you think the year is starting to wind down, the politics heats back up!
The month started the RJC annual summit right here in Las Vegas.
It was a great opportunity to connect with old and new friends from all over the US.
While these organizations often focus more on national elected positions, Nevada politics is very important because we are a swing state.
Local politics matter!
My work in the community continues to take quite a bit of my time, but I love it.
I work with various organizations to provide free medical care to underserved populations in our community.
One of the biggest events is Project Homeless Connect which is run by the Nevada Homeless Alliance and I’ve been the medical chair for the last 10 years.
This year we helped hundreds of men, women and children to get access to free medical care.
The event brings together many healthcare providers in the community, and our help is certainly appreciated.
Probably the most exciting thing I did this month was going to Special Session in Carson City.
Special session is called by the Governor and serves to address urgent or emergency needs between regular sessions.
We worked on a wide variety of topics including a crime bill, a healthcare bill, and a bill to help with SNAP benefits.
It was a very rigorous schedule, starting early and ending well into the night and through the weekend.
In the end, the Governor signed some solid legislation addressing serious concerns to help the state of Nevada.
As we wrap up the year and head into the holiday season, I hope you and your family enjoy time together.
As always, I want to hear from you!
Please reach out anytime with your questions, ideas, or thoughts about how we can make Assembly District 35 even better.
