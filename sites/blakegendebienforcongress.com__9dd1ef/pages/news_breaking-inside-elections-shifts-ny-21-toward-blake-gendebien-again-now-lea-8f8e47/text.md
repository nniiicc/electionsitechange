The forecaster first moved the seat off its “Solid Republican” list on October 1 , and now rates it alongside the most competitive Republican-held seats in the country.
In its analysis, Inside Elections writes that “this is a real race despite the typically Republican DNA of the North Country district,” pointing to the recent Siena poll showing Blake Gendebien ahead of Anthony Constantino 46% to 42% in a district Donald Trump carried with 62% of the vote.
Diesel in the Watertown-Fort Drum area is above $6.50 a gallon, up from under $4 #ago.
The trade war with Canada is hurting border businesses and farm exporters, with U.S. farm debt hitting a record $605 billion.
And rural hospitals are already cutting services before most of the Medicaid cuts have even taken effect.
Blake Gendebien, a second-generation dairy farmer, has built his campaign around lowering those costs and protecting rural healthcare.
Two independent polls this fall have shown him leading.
Constantino, meanwhile, has spent the final weeks of the race on stunts outside the district instead of answers for the people in it.
“I’ve spent my life on a dairy farm.
I know what six dollar diesel does to a family budget, what a trade war does to a milk check, and what it means when the nearest hospital cuts another service.
Anthony Constantino has cheered on every one of those policies and hasn’t offered a single idea to fix them.
Republicans, Democrats, and independents here want lower costs, a hospital close to home, and a representative who shows up for them.
That’s why this race keeps moving,” said Blake Gendebien.
### Blake and his wife Carmen have raised their three sons on Twin Mill Farms in Lisbon, milking 500 cows and farming 1200 acres.
Blake and Carmen also started The Jules of Life Foundation, which provides resources and support to North Country families battling pediatric cancer.
For the past 10 years, Blake has served as Vice Chair of the Cooperative Board of Agri-Mark, representing the interests of farmers from across the region.
He is a former member of the Lisbon Central School Board and was a longtime junior varsity basketball coach.
Blake is committed to being a bipartisan leader who will lower costs, save our rural healthcare, and end the corruption in Washington.
