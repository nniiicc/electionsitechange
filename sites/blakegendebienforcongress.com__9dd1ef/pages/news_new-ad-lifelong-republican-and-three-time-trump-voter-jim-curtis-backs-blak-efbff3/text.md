In the 30-second spot, Curtis describes Blake as a hard worker and a dairy farmer born and raised in the North Country, calling him “an old-school conservative Democrat” who wants to work together and get things done.
“We don’t agree on everything, but he’s one of us,” Curtis says.
“And I know Blake will always do right by upstate families.” Curtis is far from alone.
The independent Times Union/WPTZ-NBC5/WAMC/Siena poll released October 9 shows Blake leading Anthony Constantino 46% to 42% among likely voters, with 21% of Republicans already backing Blake.
While Blake has consolidated 85% of Democrats, Constantino is holding just 65% of Republicans in a district where Republicans make up nearly half of likely voters.
Nearly a third of Republicans view Constantino unfavorably, independents favor Blake 51% to 30%, and 58% of all likely voters say Constantino is running a negative campaign.
Rather than unite Republicans, Constantino has spent this race fighting them.
He threatened to run as an independent when the Conservative Party backed State Senator Dan Stec, whom he branded a “Trump hater.” He waged a bitter primary against Assemblyman Robert Smullen, who had the support of the state Republican Party and every Republican in New York’s congressional delegation, and sent Smullen a letter accusing him of defamation.
“Jim and I don’t see eye to eye on every issue, and that’s exactly the point,” said Blake Gendebien.
“Up here, we’ve always been able to disagree and still sit down at the same table to get things done.
I’m running to lower costs, clean up Washington, and be a voice for every Northern New York family, whether they’re Republican, Democrat, or independent.

### Blake and his wife Carmen have raised their three sons on Twin Mill Farms in Lisbon, milking 500 cows and farming 1200 acres.
Blake and Carmen also started The Jules of Life Foundation, which provides resources and support to North Country families battling pediatric cancer.
For the past 10 years, Blake has served as Vice Chair of the Cooperative Board of Agri-Mark, representing the interests of farmers from across the region.
He is a former member of the Lisbon Central School Board and was a longtime junior varsity basketball coach.
Blake is committed to being a bipartisan leader who will lower costs, save our rural healthcare, and end the corruption in Washington.
