Customs and Border Protection.
Perry’s service is well known in the North Country.
When he retired in 2018, then-Congresswoman Elise Stefanik honored him in the Congressional Record, thanking him on behalf of NY-21 for his dedication to his community and country.
As an Air Interdiction Agent with the Plattsburgh Air Unit, Perry patrolled the northern border from the air, supported officers on the ground, and took part in the 2015 manhunt for the Dannemora prison escapees.
He also trained the next generation of pilots as an instructor, and served four years in the Marine Corps before joining CBP.
In the ads, Perry draws on two decades of firsthand experience to argue that both parties have failed on immigration, and that Blake, a dairy farmer who deals with the broken system every day, will work across the aisle to fix it.
In the radio ad, Perry, a Marine himself, also responds to Anthony Constantino calling a decorated Marine a “f***ing coward.” “I saw firsthand what a mess our immigration system is, and how politicians from both parties are to blame,” said Gerhardt Perry.

Blake and Carmen also started The Jules of Life Foundation, which provides resources and support to North Country families battling pediatric cancer.
For the past 10 years, Blake has served as Vice Chair of the Cooperative Board of Agri-Mark, representing the interests of farmers from across the region.
He is a former member of the Lisbon Central School Board and was a longtime junior varsity basketball coach.
Blake is committed to being a bipartisan leader who will lower costs, save our rural healthcare, and end the corruption in Washington.
