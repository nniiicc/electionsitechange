A Dedicated Public Servant
Who Never Backs Down from a Tough Fight
When corporate greed closed Nashoba Valley Medical Center, Margaret Scarsdale fought back and delivered.
After personally bringing prospective bidders to the table, Margaret knew firsthand the damage that Steward Health Care had done to Nashoba Valley Medical Center, carving out services and failing to maintain key infrastructure.
When it was clear that no bidders for the hospital would emerge because of this, Margaret pivoted her efforts, co-chairing the Emergency, Outpatient, and Healthcare Services Subcommittee.
The work of the sub-committee, and Margaret’s continued advocacy, helped spur UMass Memorial’s investment in a satellite emergency facility in Groton.
While the new facility is being built, Margaret championed the effort to secure
$5 million in new state aid for our first responders to compensate our towns for longer EMS travel times and rising costs.
Now, Margaret is leading the fight on Beacon Hill to set right the broken school funding formula, which has shortchanged the First Middlesex District for decades.
She brought together a first-of-its-kind statewide education funding reform coalition, which achieved a critical win in the FY2027 state budget to reopen and adjust the school funding formula and enabled year-over-year state aid increases for all six towns in her District.
A Proven Leader who Shares Your Priorities
On the issues that matter most, Margaret Scarsdale has been our champion on Beacon Hill:
School Funding: Margaret launched the first statewide coalition to fix the broken state education funding formula, resulting in year-over-year increases in state aid and renewed momentum to make structural change.
Cost of Living: Margaret proudly voted YES for tax relief for working families, seniors, and small businesses, and continues to advocate for legislation to make our communities more affordable.
Affordable Housing: Margaret was proud to support legislation to make record investments in affordable housing, cut through red tape, and promote and preserve housing across Massachusetts.
Climate Change: Margaret has continued to champion funding for green projects in our towns, initiatives that reduce pollution, and create new green economy jobs to help us leave this planet a better place for our children.
Economic Development: Margaret has sponsored legislation aimed at making our communities more attractive to businesses, including through modernizing our employment laws and enabling new tax credits.
Healthcare: Margaret believes everyone deserves access to affordable healthcare and medications.
She is also pro-choice and believes healthcare decisions should be made between you and your doctor, not the government.
First Responders: Margaret led the fight to secure $5M for local fire and EMS after the closure of Nashoba Valley Medical Center and has consistently voted in favor of bills that benefit frontline workers.
Legislative Transparency: Margaret supports legislative transparency and audits.
She is committed to providing timely, effective constituent services, regularly attends community events across her district, and is a full-time representative.
