Opal Lee was born on October 7, 1926, in Marshall, Texas, during a time of deep racial segregation.
2 1939 Juneteenth Violence At age 12, a white mob destroyed her family's home in Fort Worth on Juneteenth, leaving an indelible mark on her understanding of freedom.
3 2016 The Walk Begins At age 89, Ms.
Opal began walking 2.5 miles a day (symbolizing the 2.5 years it took for enslaved Texans to learn of their freedom) to advocate for a Juneteenth federal holiday.
Dione engineered the 'Opal's Walk 2 DC' campaign that launched a national movement that garnered 1.5 million signatures on the online petition for holiday recognition.
4 2021 Juneteenth Becomes Law President Biden signed the Juneteenth National Independence Day Act, making June 19 a federal holiday.
