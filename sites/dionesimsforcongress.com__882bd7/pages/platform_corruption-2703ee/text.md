Harris Endorses Dione Sims for Congress Read the official announcement Back to the Platform Issues Freedom from corruption Fighting Corruption and Restoring Accountability in Washington Position brief Fighting Corruption and Restoring Accountability in Washington I am running for Congress because public office must once again mean public service—not personal profit.
The American people deserve a government that works for them, not a system where elected officials, political appointees, or other powerful individuals can abuse their positions to enrich themselves, manipulate markets, reward family and friends, or provide special access to well-connected insiders.
In Congress, I will fight for aggressive oversight and meaningful accountability whenever credible evidence suggests corruption, fraud, conflicts of interest, insider dealing, market manipulation, self-enrichment, or abuse of governmental power.
I will support lawful congressional investigations that follow the facts and the evidence—regardless of political party, wealth, title, or personal connections.
Where wrongdoing is uncovered, I will support appropriate referrals to independent law-enforcement, regulatory, and ethics authorities so allegations can be investigated through the proper legal process and individuals can be held accountable when the evidence establishes violations of the law.
I will also fight for stronger transparency and ethics requirements, including meaningful financial disclosures and safeguards designed to prevent public officials from using confidential government information or the power of their offices for private financial advantage.
Accountability cannot be partisan.
If a Democrat abuses public office, investigate it.
If a Republican abuses public office, investigate it.
If a powerful donor, corporate executive, political insider, family member, or friend improperly benefits from government power, follow the evidence wherever it leads.
Washington should never operate under one set of rules for ordinary Americans and another for the wealthy and politically connected.
Nobody is above the law.
Nobody should be able to purchase access to our government.
And nobody entrusted with public power should be permitted to turn that power into a private business opportunity.
As your representative, I will work to restore something fundamental to American democracy: the principle that elected officials work for the people—and that public power must always be exercised in the public interest.
