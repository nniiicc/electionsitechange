What Is TX-25?
TX-25 is 1 of 38 US House Districts.
People who live here vote for the same member of Congress.
If you live in TX-25, Dione Sims could represent you.
2025 TX-25 District Map View Interactive Redistricting Map Current District Boundaries TX-25 stretches from Arlington and Fort Worth in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex through rural counties east of Abilene .
The district includes parts of Tarrant County (including Arlington, home to AT&T Stadium and Six Flags Over Texas) and several rural counties between the Metroplex and Abilene.
Redistricting Changes (2025) Following the 2025 redistricting special sessions, Texas redrew congressional district boundaries.
The redistricting redefined TX-25 to encompass the northern boundary of the current TX-33 .
What changed: The district was reshaped to include less of southeastern Arlington and more of southern Mid-cities area, while maintaining connections to rural counties.
Note: District boundaries are subject to ongoing Supreme Court legal review.
The map shown reflects the current boundaries in effect as of Summer 2025.
