Harris Endorses Dione Sims for Congress Read the official announcement Back to the Platform Issues Freedom to live healthy Healthcare Should Protect Your Health — Not Threaten Your Financial Future Position brief AFFORDABLE HEALTHCARE FOR EVERY TEXAN Healthcare Should Protect Your Health — Not Threaten Your Financial Future Texas is an economic powerhouse, but when it comes to healthcare access, too many Texas families are being left behind.
In 2024, Texas had the highest uninsured rate in the nation at 16.7%, compared with 8.2% nationally.
Among working-age Texans ages 19–64, the uninsured rate was even higher at 21.6% — also the highest in the country.
More than 5 million Texans were uninsured.
For Dione Sims, those numbers represent working parents, small-business owners, children, caregivers, veterans, rural families and neighbors who should not have to choose between paying a medical bill and paying their mortgage, rent, groceries or utilities.
Dione Sims believes access to affordable healthcare is fundamental to the freedom to pursue the American Dream.
The Dione Sims Healthcare Affordability Plan As a member of Congress, Dione Sims will work across federal, state and local government to pursue practical reforms that expand coverage while lowering the actual cost of healthcare.
1.
Close the Texas Healthcare Coverage Gap Texas remains among the states that have not adopted the Affordable Care Act's Medicaid expansion.
Under the ACA framework, Medicaid expansion can cover eligible low-income adults, with the federal government providing an enhanced matching rate for the expansion population.
Dione will advocate for Texas to capture available federal healthcare resources instead of allowing Texans' federal tax dollars to support healthcare expansion elsewhere while millions of Texans remain uninsured.
As a federal legislator, she will support policies that strengthen incentives for Texas to close its coverage gap while protecting taxpayers and demanding measurable results from healthcare programs.
2.
Make Private Health Insurance More Affordable Healthcare reform must also help working and middle-class Texans who earn too much to qualify for Medicaid but are struggling with premiums, deductibles and out-of-pocket expenses.
The enhanced Affordable Care Act premium tax credits expired at the end of 2025, reducing financial assistance for many Marketplace consumers in 2026.
Dione will support targeted federal assistance that makes Marketplace coverage more affordable for working families while pursuing reforms that address the underlying cost of healthcare rather than simply shifting those costs to taxpayers.
Her objective is straightforward: more competition, more transparency, lower costs and greater consumer choice.
3.
Lower Prescription Drug Costs No Texan should have to ration medication because of its price.
Dione will support strengthening Medicare's ability to negotiate the prices of high-cost prescription drugs, increasing competition from safe generic and biosimilar medicines, improving transparency across the prescription-drug supply chain and holding pharmaceutical companies and intermediaries accountable for practices that unnecessarily increase consumer costs.
Medicare's first negotiated prices took effect in 2026.
CMS estimated that people with Medicare Part D would save approximately $1.5 billion from the first group of negotiated drugs under the standard benefit design.
Dione wants Congress to build on approaches that demonstrably reduce costs while preserving American medical innovation.
4.
Protect Rural and Community Healthcare Healthcare access means little if the nearest doctor, hospital, maternity unit or specialist is an hour away.
Dione will fight for federal investment in rural hospitals, community health centers, telehealth, mental-health services, maternal healthcare and workforce programs that help underserved Texas communities recruit and retain physicians, nurses and other healthcare professionals.
The goal is not simply more insurance cards.
The goal is access to actual healthcare.
5.
Put Patients and Families Ahead of the Healthcare Bureaucracy Dione supports greater transparency so Texans can better understand what healthcare will cost before receiving a bill.
She will support efforts to strengthen hospital and insurer price transparency, reduce unnecessary administrative costs, address surprise medical expenses and make it easier for consumers to compare healthcare options.
Healthcare policy should be designed around the patient — not around bureaucracy.
HOW DO WE PAY FOR IT?
Dione Sims believes expanding healthcare must be accompanied by fiscal responsibility.
With the federal government already facing substantial deficits, Washington cannot simply write another blank check.
Her approach is to redirect resources, leverage federal matching funds, reduce unnecessary healthcare spending and demand greater value for every taxpayer dollar.
For Medicaid expansion populations, federal law provides an enhanced federal matching rate, meaning the federal government bears the substantial majority of qualifying expansion costs.
Dione will also support responsible financing strategies that include: Capturing federal healthcare funding available to Texas while requiring strong fiscal oversight and measurable outcomes.
Reducing waste, fraud and unnecessary administrative spending across federal healthcare programs.
Expanding effective prescription-drug competition and negotiation where it produces documented savings.
Closing unjustified tax loopholes and ensuring that major corporations and extremely wealthy taxpayers meet their lawful obligations rather than shifting the burden to working families.
Investing in preventive and primary care where evidence demonstrates that earlier treatment can reduce more expensive emergency and acute care.
Requiring transparency and accountability so taxpayers can see where healthcare dollars are going and what results they are producing.
Dione will insist that major healthcare legislation identify its costs and financing before asking taxpayers to support it.
THE BOTTOM LINE Texas should not lead America in the number of people without health insurance.
We can do better.
Dione Sims' healthcare agenda is built around a simple principle: Protect the patient.
Strengthen the family.
Lower the cost.
Expand access.
Spend taxpayer dollars responsibly.
This isn't about creating a bigger government healthcare bureaucracy.
It is about building a smarter healthcare system where a child's healthcare, a parent's prescription or an unexpected diagnosis does not become a family's financial crisis.
Affordable healthcare is part of economic freedom — and every Texas family deserves the opportunity to pursue the American Dream without living one medical emergency away from financial hardship.
