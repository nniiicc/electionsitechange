Harris Endorses Dione Sims for Congress Read the official announcement Back to the Platform Issues Freedom to work, provide & build a future Dione Sims’ Plan for Affordable, Accessible Childcare for Texas Families Position brief THE FREEDOM TO WORK, PROVIDE & BUILD A FUTURE Dione Sims’ Plan for Affordable, Accessible Childcare for Texas Families Every Texas parent should have the freedom to work, build a career, start a business, or pursue an education without childcare costs consuming the family budget.
For millions of working families, childcare is not optional—it is part of the basic cost of going to work.
Yet Texas families are being asked to absorb childcare expenses that can rival housing, transportation, and even college tuition.
Dione Sims believes we can strengthen Texas families and the Texas economy at the same time by making quality childcare more affordable, more accessible, and more responsive to the realities of working parents.
THE CHILDCARE AFFORDABILITY CHALLENGE IN TEXAS The numbers demonstrate why action is necessary.
According to Child Care Aware of America, center-based childcare in Texas averages approximately $11,349 per year for an infant and $10,921 for a toddler.
A family with an infant and a four-year-old can face approximately $21,574 per year in center-based childcare expenses.
For a single-parent household earning the Texas median for single-parent families, center-based infant care can consume approximately 30% of household income.
Cost is only part of the problem.
Availability is becoming a serious economic challenge.
A 2026 CHILDREN AT RISK analysis found that approximately 441,000 low-income Texas children under age six with working parents—76% of that population—live in subsidized childcare deserts, where demand is at least three times greater than the available supply.
Four out of five also live in areas without adequate access to higher-quality Texas Rising Star childcare.
This is particularly damaging to working-class and rural families.
When parents cannot find childcare, they may have to reduce their working hours, turn down promotions, change jobs, leave the workforce, or rely on arrangements that may not provide the stability their families need.
Affordable childcare is therefore not only family policy.
It is workforce policy, economic-development policy, and an affordability issue.
THE SIMS CHILDCARE FREEDOM PLAN 1.
Make Childcare More Affordable for Working Families Dione Sims will support expanding childcare assistance for working- and middle-class families so that childcare expenses do not consume an unreasonable percentage of household income.
Assistance should be structured on a sliding scale: families with the greatest financial need receive the greatest support, while working families above traditional assistance thresholds can receive more limited help.
The objective is simple: Work should always pay—and accepting a better job or earning a raise should not suddenly cause a family to lose all childcare assistance.
2.
Expand the Supply of Childcare Texas cannot solve the childcare crisis by helping families pay for childcare that does not exist.
Sims will support federal-state partnerships that help qualified childcare providers expand capacity, particularly in rural communities and childcare deserts.
That can include competitive grants and financing for licensed providers to expand classrooms, renovate facilities, meet safety requirements, and open childcare operations in communities with demonstrated shortages.
The campaign will also support responsible expansion of licensed home-based childcare providers, which can be particularly important in rural Texas.
3.
Expand Employer-Supported Childcare Government does not have to solve this problem alone.
Sims will encourage Texas employers to become part of the solution by expanding incentives for businesses that provide: On-site childcare; Childcare stipends or reimbursements; Partnerships with nearby childcare centers; Reserved childcare slots for employees; and Shared childcare facilities among multiple small businesses.
Federal law already provides an employer-provided childcare tax credit, and beginning in tax year 2026 the maximum credit increased to $500,000 annually—or $600,000 for eligible small businesses—with enhanced reimbursement percentages for qualifying expenditures.
Sims would work to increase awareness and utilization of these incentives among Texas employers.
4.
Support Parents Who Work Nights and Weekends The modern Texas economy does not operate from 9:00 to 5:00.
Nurses, police officers, firefighters, manufacturing employees, hospitality workers, retail employees, transportation workers, first responders and many other Texans work evenings, overnight shifts and weekends.
Sims will support incentives for qualified childcare providers that expand evening, weekend, early-morning and nontraditional-hour childcare, particularly in communities with large populations of shift workers.
5.
Strengthen Early Head Start, Head Start and Pre-K Partnerships Texas should better coordinate existing federal, state, local and school-based resources.
Sims will advocate for protecting and strengthening Early Head Start and Head Start, while encouraging partnerships among childcare centers, school districts, community organizations and employers.
The goal is to eliminate unnecessary duplication and make every existing childcare dollar work harder for Texas families.
6.
Protect Quality While Expanding Access Affordable childcare must also be safe childcare.
Sims supports maintaining appropriate licensing, health, safety and background-check requirements while expanding access to high-quality providers, including Texas Rising Star programs.
Parents deserve both affordability and peace of mind.
HOW WILL WE PAY FOR IT?
Dione Sims believes Texans deserve more than promises.
Every major proposal should come with a responsible funding strategy.
The Sims plan would use a public-private financing model rather than placing the entire cost on Texas taxpayers.
First, protect and maximize existing federal childcare funding.
Texas already receives federal childcare resources.
Sims will fight to ensure Texas receives its fair share and that federal dollars reach working families and communities with the greatest shortages.
Second, strengthen childcare tax relief for working families.
Sims will support federal tax policies that allow working families to keep more of their own money when paying legitimate childcare expenses.
Third, leverage employers and the private sector.
Federal incentives can encourage employers to invest directly in childcare rather than requiring government to finance every childcare slot.
Fourth, prioritize existing programs that produce measurable results.
Federal childcare investments should be transparent and accountable.
Programs should be evaluated based on affordability, availability, quality, workforce participation and measurable outcomes.
Fifth, use targeted investment rather than universal subsidies for wealthy households.
Public resources should be concentrated on working-, middle-class and economically vulnerable families for whom childcare costs create a genuine barrier to employment.
This approach allows government, employers, childcare providers and families to share responsibility rather than creating an unlimited new government entitlement.
CHILDCARE IS ECONOMIC INFRASTRUCTURE Texas invests in roads because Texans need roads to get to work.
We invest in electricity because businesses cannot operate without power.
And we must recognize another reality of the modern economy: Working parents cannot work when they have nowhere safe to leave their children.
Affordable childcare helps parents remain employed.
It helps businesses recruit and retain workers.
It gives parents greater freedom to pursue promotions, education and entrepreneurship.
And it gives children access to safe environments where they can learn and develop.
DIONE SIMS’ COMMITMENT Freedom means more than telling Texans they are free.
It means creating the conditions that allow people to use that freedom.
A mother should have the freedom to pursue a career.
A father should have the freedom to accept a better job.
A single parent should have the freedom to work without surrendering a third of their income for childcare.
A small-business owner should have the freedom to grow a company while raising a family.
And a child should have the opportunity to begin life in a safe, nurturing environment.
Dione Sims will fight for a Texas where raising a family and earning a living are not competing ambitions—and where affordable childcare gives working Texans greater freedom to pursue the American Dream.
