Howard Launches Conservative Independent Write-In Campaign for Texas House District 124 Texas Conservative Party Endorses Faith-Led, Community-Focused Candidate Committed to Accountable Government and Measurable Results CONTACT Team L.D.
Howard Campaign | 210-559-0701 ldhoward@teamldhowardfortexas.com | teamldhowardfortexas.com SAN ANTONIO, Texas - Retired U.S.
Air Force Veteran, community advocate, minister, and servant leader L.D.
Howard has launched a campaign as a Conservative Independent write-in candidate for Texas House District 124.
L.D.
Howard has received the official endorsement of the Texas Conservative Party.
After confronting Texas' demanding independent ballot-access process, L.D.
Howard chose to continue as a declared write-in candidate rather than abandon the residents and priorities that brought the campaign into the race.
The campaign is constitutionally grounded, faith-led, and independent of two-party control.
"My decision to continue as a write-in candidate is rooted in the principles that have guided my life: faith, service, perseverance, accountability, and respect for all people," L.D.
Howard said.
"This campaign belongs to the people of District 124.
I will not abandon their voices simply because the path became more difficult." The campaign motto is: Faith.
Service.
People First.
Grounded in Matthew 19:26, "With God all things are possible," the campaign focuses on practical solutions, transparent government, and results residents can see and measure.
A Conservative Definition of Sustainability For L.D.
Howard, sustainability means strengthening families, businesses, and neighborhoods through self-reliance, responsible stewardship, local ownership, and solutions that reduce, not increase, dependence on government.
The "Love in Action for Sustainability" platform turns compassion into accountable action that helps people build lasting stability.
District Priorities and Public Commitments Accountable Representation: Hold quarterly town halls across District 124, publish a district accountability report, and maintain reliable constituent casework with clear response standards.
Mental Health and Wellness: Strengthen community-based crisis response, early intervention, and partnerships among schools, churches, nonprofits, healthcare providers, and first responders.
Seniors and Veterans: Improve constituent casework and coordination with the Texas Veterans Commission, local Veteran organizations, caregiver networks, and appropriate federal partners.
Small Business and Jobs: Connect entrepreneurs with state resources, procurement training, workforce pipelines, apprenticeships, and opportunities that strengthen neighborhood commercial corridors.
Housing and Accessibility: Increase housing supply, protect property rights, hold negligent landlords accountable, expand responsible pathways to homeownership, and strengthen accessibility compliance.
Youth and Education: Advance mentorship, literacy, life-skills education, career and technical training, apprenticeships, school safety, and targeted action addressing chronic absenteeism.
A Clear Choice for Write-In Voters To vote for L.D.
Howard, voters must select the write-in option and enter L.D.
Howard as the candidate for Texas House District 124.
Voters should follow the instructions provided on their specific voting system.
"I humbly ask for your prayers, your support, and your write-in vote," L.D.
Howard said.
"District 124 does not need another list of promises.
It needs leadership that listens, acts, measures the results, and remains accountable to the people.
With faith as our foundation and community as our purpose, we can build a stronger District 124 together." About L.D.
Howard L.D.
Howard is a retired U.S.
Air Force Veteran and retired Department of Veterans Affairs servant leader, as well as a clinical mental-health professional, Texas-licensed real estate broker, entrepreneur, ordained minister, nonprofit leader, wife, mother, grandmother, and longtime District 124 community member.
L.D.
Howard holds a master's degree in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a bachelor's degree in Business Management and Marketing.
This leadership is grounded in faith, constitutional government, personal responsibility, compassion, and service to the whole person.
