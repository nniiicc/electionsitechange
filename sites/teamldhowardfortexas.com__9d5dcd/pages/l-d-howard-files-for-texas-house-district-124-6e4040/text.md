Howard Files for Texas House District 124, Championing "People Over Politics"
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE November 10, 2025
Contact: Team L.D.
Howard Campaign coms@teamldhowardfortexas.com
AUSTIN, Texas – L.D.
Howard, a U.S.
Air Force retiree and recent retiree from the U.S.
Department of Veterans Affairs, officially submitted her candidate application today to run as an Independent for the Texas State House of Representatives, District 124.
Howard, accompanied by her husband Brian, traveled to Austin to file the application, formalizing her commitment to bringing servant leadership and a new, non-partisan voice to the Texas legislature.
As an Independent, Howard is running on a platform centered on unity and community-focused solutions.
"I am running as an independent because I believe in placing people over politics," said Howard.
"My campaign is founded on the Christian values I was raised on – the scriptures that teach us to love our neighbors, serve the vulnerable, and act with integrity.
My motto is 'Faith, Service, and People First,' and my theme is 'Showing Love in Action with Sustainability.' This isn't about a political party; it's about our community."
Howard’s career is defined by service.
She dedicated over two decades to federal service in the United States Air Force and with the VA’s South Texas Veterans Healthcare System, advocating for countless service members and their families.
A multi-passionate entrepreneur, Howard is also the founder of several businesses and non-profits dedicated to providing holistic support to underserved communities, veterans, unwed mothers, youth, and seniors.
She holds a Master’s degree in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and a Bachelor’s in Business Administration and Marketing, blending deep empathy with a strategic understanding of community and economic needs.
Howard’s platform for the 2026 Texas House District 124 electionis a direct reflection of her life’s work, focusing on:
- Mental Health & Wellness: Expanding access to mental health care and crisis support.
- Housing & Homelessness: Championing affordable housing initiatives and developing long-term solutions for the unhoused.
- Seniors & Veterans: Protecting seniors with stronger healthcare and advocating for veterans' access to medical care, job training, and housing support.
- Economic Development: Empowering small businesses and attracting sustainable industries to strengthen the local workforce.
- Youth Empowerment & Education: Investing in mentorship, life skills, and vocational training.
L.D.
Howard is a proud daughter, sister, wife, mother of six, and "Nana" to nine grandchildren.
