Howard to Host Valentine’s Day "Love in Action" Meet & Greet for District 124 On Saturday, February 14, L.D.
Howard, a U.S.
Air Force veteran and Independent candidate for Texas House District 124, will host a community Meet & Greet at Exquisite Elegance.
Following the recent filing deadline for the 2026 election cycle, L.D.
Howard is centering this campaign on a "People Over Politics" platform, emphasizing clinical mental health expertise and sustainable economic development for the West Side and Greater Lackland.
WHAT: Community Meet & Greet with L.D.
Howard , Independent for HD 124.
WHO: L.D.
Howard , local veterans, small business owners, and residents of District 124.
WHEN: Saturday, February 14, 2026, from 1:00 PM to 4:00 PM.
WHERE: Exquisite Elegance, 7141 W US HWY 90 #115, San Antonio, TX 78227.
WHY: To discuss a "humanity-first" legislative agenda that addresses the district's $20 billion economic potential alongside its urgent "childcare deserts" and mental health resource gaps.
