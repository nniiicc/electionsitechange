Howard FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE DATE: November 17, 2025 CONTACT: Team L.D.
Howard Campaign EMAIL: coms@teamldhowardfortexas.com SAN ANTONIO, TX —L.D.
Howard, a U.S.
Air Force retiree and recent retiree from the U.S.
Department of Veterans Affairs, and an Independent Candidate for State Representative, District 124 , will host a "Tea & Breakfast" community event to directly engage with voters.
The event is a direct reflection of her commitment to "Showing Love in Action with Sustainability" and placing "People Over Politics." This forum provides residents of District 124 a space to learn more about Ms.
Howard and the campaign, ask questions, voice concerns, share ideas, and meet the team.
The discussion will focus on State issues and the campaign's humanity-first solutions to community problems.
Event Details WHAT: Tea & Breakfast w/ Team L.D.
Howard WHO: L.D.
Howard, Independent Candidate for TX House District 124 WHEN: Saturday, December 13th, 9:00 AM – 11:00 AM WHERE: Exquisite Elegance, 7141 W US HWY 90 #115, San Antonio, TX 78227 Export to Sheets Resources and key campaign takeaways will be available on site for attendees.
Key Campaign Themes & Commitments Ms.
Howard’s platform is a direct reflection of her life’s work, focusing on core community needs under the banner of humanity-first solutions.
Core Issues: Transparency & Accountability Mental Health and Wellness Accessibility (ADA Compliance) Housing and Homelessness Youth Empowerment and Education Support for Seniors and Veterans Law Enforcement and Safety Specific Commitments: Transparency: Publish finance reports, hold listening sessions, and maintain an open-door policy.
Health: Expand mental health awareness and secure funding for counselors in schools.
Accessibility: Audit community places for Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) compliance.
Housing: Advocate for affordable housing and tenant protections.
Community: Expand mentorship programs for youth, host wellness workshops for seniors and veterans, and advocate for community policing and de-escalation training.
