Meet Anissa
Dr.
Anissa Monique Jones, DC, is a proud Macon native, healthcare professional, entrepreneur, and public servant who represents the citizens of Georgia House District 143, serving communities across Macon-Bibb and Houston Counties.
For more than two decades, Dr.
Jones has dedicated her life to improving the health and well-being of families throughout Middle Georgia.
She made history as the first African-American chiropractor in Macon, and for more than 27 years she has served the community as Clinical Director of Total Fuze Spine and Pain Center, helping thousands of patients restore mobility, manage pain, and live healthier lives.
Dr.
Jones earned her Bachelor of Science in Biology from Fort Valley State University in 1994, followed by her Doctor of Chiropractic degree from Life University in 1998.
Committed to expanding her leadership and business expertise, she later earned a Master of Business Administration in International Business from Wesleyan College in 2005.
Her professional accomplishments have been recognized through honors including the Heritage Registry of Who’s Who and the Foxington Registry of Who’s Who Executives and Business Professionals.
In 2017, she broke another barrier when she became the first African American and first woman to serve as President of District 14 of the Georgia Chiropractic Association, advocating for quality healthcare and advancing the chiropractic profession across the region.
Dr.
Jones’ commitment to service extends beyond healthcare into civic leadership.
In 2018, she was elected to represent District 1 on the Macon Water Authority Board.
After being sworn in on January 3, 2019, she quickly earned the trust of her colleagues and was appointed Chair of the Personnel Committee while serving on several critical boards and committees, including Macon Soils, Macon Water Alliance, the Engineering Committee, the Pension and Trustee Board, and the Finance Committee.
She later served as Vice Chair of the Macon Water Authority Board, helping guide responsible governance and infrastructure planning for the community.
Throughout her career, Dr.
Jones has remained deeply involved in community development and civic engagement.
She currently serves as a Director of the Georgia Chiropractic Association and is a 32-year member of Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, Incorporated.
She is also a Leadership Macon alumnus (Class of 2004) and was recognized as a 2018 Middle Georgia Regional Champion.
Dr.
Jones has served on numerous boards dedicated to economic development and community revitalization, including NewTown Macon, the NewTown Loans Board, the Rosa Jackson Advisory Board, and the Northeast High School Advisory Board.
She also served as Past President of the Main Street Macon Board, an organization committed to revitalizing Macon’s historic downtown district.
Her passion for community service is reflected in her advocacy work as well.
Dr.
Jones serves as an Advocate with Citizen Advocacy and is a member of Sisterhood, an organization dedicated to promoting HIV/AIDS awareness and testing throughout Middle Georgia.
A strong supporter of youth development, Dr.
Jones sponsors several adolescent and teen sports teams, helping provide uniforms and financial support so young people can participate in athletics and mentorship opportunities.
In honor of her grandmother, she founded the Lois Taylor Institute of Excellence, a nonprofit organization that empowers students ages 8–18 to pursue careers in technology, real estate, entrepreneurship, and leadership.
Through the organization, she also hosts an annual Christmas food and toy drive that serves more than 1,500 families each year, ensuring that children and families in the community are supported during the holiday season.
As a member of the Georgia House of Representatives, Dr.
Jones is committed to working with local leaders, educators, healthcare providers, and businesses to strengthen infrastructure, expand healthcare access, improve public safety, and create economic opportunities—especially for young people and working families.
Dr.
Jones is honored to serve the people of Macon-Bibb and Houston Counties and remains dedicated to building stronger communities and greater opportunity across the State of Georgia.
