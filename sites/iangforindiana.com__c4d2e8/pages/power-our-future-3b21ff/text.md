Power Our Future
Wouldn’t it be nice to never have to see another plow or spend the whole winter washing salt off your car?
This is our proposal to make that a reality using practical, local engineering.
By pairing Vertical Axis Wind Turbines with basalt thermal storage, we’re laying out a roadmap for a community-scale microgrid.
It’s a vision for our future that prioritizes road safety, lowers home energy costs, and secures our energy independence.
We’re ready to get to work—let’s talk about how we can build a future that’s built to last.
