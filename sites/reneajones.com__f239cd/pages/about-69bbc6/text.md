Meet Renea A native East Tennesseean, Renea has balanced the trials of farming, running a business, and raising a family.
She is the President of Jones & Church Farms, a legacy business founded in 1975, renowned for delivering premium, farm-fresh tomatoes to supermarkets and restaurants across the nation.
When she is not at the farm or in the office, she enjoys spending time with her children and grandchildren.
Her Conservative Values Renea’s Christian faith is the foundation for her principles and conservative beliefs.
She is unapologetically conservative and believes that the government should take a limited role in the lives of East Tennesseans.
She is 100% Pro-Life and Pro Second Amendment.
A Committed Servant For over a decade, Renea dedicated herself to serving as a member of the Unicoi County School Board and later as the President of the Tennessee School Board Association.
During her tenure, she collaborated closely with legislators to bolster support for rural school districts.
Renea has also held numerous leadership positions with the Tennessee Farm Bureau, protecting the folks who put food on the table and ensuring they’re able to work without the burden of overreaching regulations.
