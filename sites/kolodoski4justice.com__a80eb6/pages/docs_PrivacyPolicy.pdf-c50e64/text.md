Privacy Policy Effective Date: 10/11/2026 1.
Introduction This Privacy Policy outlines the practices and policies of Matthew Kolodoski Campaign ("we," "us," or "our") regarding the collection, use, and protection of personal information provided by users ("you" or "users") of our website kolodoski4justice.com.
2.
Collection of Personal Information We collect personal information from users when: • • • You voluntarily provide it to us when using our website or services.
You submit your phone number to receive text messages from our toll-free number.
You submit your email address to receive emails from us.
3.
Use of Personal Information We use the personal information you provide to send you campaign updates, donation asks, and informational messages from Matthew Kolodoski Campaign.
4.
Mobile Information Sharing No mobile opt-in information will be shared with third parties.
5.
Toll-Free SMS Program Terms As a sender of text messages via a toll-free number, we adhere to the following principles: • Consent to receive text messages is collected through a clearly labeled, unchecked checkbox separate from any other consent (including email consent) and is not a condition of submission. • • • • The phone-number entry field is accompanied by clear disclosure of the message program, message frequency ("Msg frequency may vary"), the statement "Msg & data rates may apply," and links to this Privacy Policy.
Privacy policies and terms and conditions are displayed next to the phone-number entry fields.
End-user information is protected and is not shared or sold to third parties for marketing, lead generation, or analytics purposes.
Opt-in and opt-out procedures are clearly outlined in this Privacy Policy.
6.
Messaging Terms and Conditions By submitting your phone number and opting-in, you agree to receive updates, donation asks, and informational messages from Matthew Kolodoski Campaign.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
For help, reply HELP or email us at mkolodoski@gettelaw.com.
You can opt-out at any time by replying STOP.
7.
Opting Out and Help You can opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from our toll-free number.
After you send STOP, we will send you a confirmation message and you will not receive additional text messages from us unless you opt in again.
For assistance, reply HELP to any message, or contact us using the information in Section 10.
8.
Disclosure of Personal Information We do not share or sell end-user information, including mobile opt-in or consent status, to third parties or affiliates for any marketing, lead generation, or analytics purposes.
9.
Changes to this Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The revised policy will be posted on our website with the effective date.
10.
Contact Us If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact us at mkolodoski@gettelaw.com.
