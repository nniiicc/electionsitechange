Building a Healthy, Worker-Centered Economy and an Environment Supportive of Small Businesses
Stop pitting workers against small businesses.
Neither of them is the problem.
The real problem is record-high profits for the record the largest corporations and wealthy individuals leading a new Gilded Age.
I will fight back by:
- Regulating the gig economy directly regardless of whether gig workers are traditionally considered employees.
- Adopting stiffer penalties for misclassification employees as independent contractors.
- Support the Worker Protection Act and support further measures to make it easier for workers to unionize and combat retaliation at the state level as federal agencies fail us.
- Fight for living wages, including for tipped workers.
- Provide tax incentives, subsidies, and technical support to small businesses to reduce their costs.
- Impose a graduated tax or fees on large, highly profitable corporations to fund and support local small businesses.
