Supporting Neighbors Against Deportation
The Trump Administration has been all too willing to scapegoat immigrants to fuel a crisis in this country and justify authoritarianism, threatening our rights, our communities, and our values.
But I will stand up to him and protect our neighbors and a multi-cultural vision of America by:
- Advocating for the abolishment of ICE at the federal level.
- Ensuring every resident in our state has access to essential services and that their data cannot be shared with the federal government.
- Limit cooperation between our local police and federal immigration agents.
- Funding legal assistance for immigrants in deportation proceedings.
