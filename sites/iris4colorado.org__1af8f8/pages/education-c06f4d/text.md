Improving Educational Outcomes
Colorado public schools are chronically underfunded, ranking 48th out of 50 in the country for per-pupil spending.
We need to support our children and educators like they deserve to be treated.
- Reducing the growth and encouraging more regulation of charter schools.
- Increasing investment in public education by raising the salaries for educators and reducing class sizes.
- Ensuring educational opportunities during the summer for students and their families to reduce learning and achievement gaps.
- Repealing TABOR so we can adequately fund our public schools.
- Increase debt relief for teachers and other educational professionals who go into service for our students.
