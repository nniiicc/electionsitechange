Protecting Civil and Constitutional Rights and Bodily Autonomy
I have been on the frontlines of fighting the Trump Administration’s assault on our rights, and as an elected representative will continue that fight, including:
- Protecting our First Amendment Rights whether it is our right to protest and criticize unfair practices and abuses or our intellectual freedom in libraries and schools.
- Preventing racial, ethnic, and other forms of profiling, ensuring due process through our state laws.
- Protecting bodily autonomy by ensuring access to abortion, birth control and IVF, and gender affirming care even if it means direct funding from the state, enforcing our anti-discrimination laws, and further strengthening our shield laws to protect providers.
- Protecting Privacy and prohibiting artificial intelligence algorithms from discriminating in employment, housing, credit practices, health care, and so many other ways AI now impacts our lives.
- Create through legislation a way to hold federal agents civilly accountable for violating our federal constitutional rights.
