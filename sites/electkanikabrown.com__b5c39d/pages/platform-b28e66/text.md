Healthcare Access
No one should have to choose between buying groceries or their prescription pills, but this is a sad reality for many people in Forsyth County and North Carolina.
First and foremost, we must increase healthcare access to hundreds of thousands of North Carolinian families by expanding Medicaid.
That is a sensible and fiscally responsible decision that will positively impact our communities.
We also must take on the crisis we’re seeing in rural counties where hospitals and birthing centers are closing, leaving tens of thousands of people without critical care.
You deserve access to quality healthcare, no matter where you live.
