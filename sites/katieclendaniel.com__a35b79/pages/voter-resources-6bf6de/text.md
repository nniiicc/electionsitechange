Find answers to common questions about casting your ballot in Maryland.
How do I return my mail ballot?
Follow the instructions included with your ballot and sign the required oath.
If you mail your ballot, it must be postmarked on or before November 3.
A ballot placed in a drop box or delivered in person must arrive by 8 p.m. on November 3.
You cannot return a voted ballot online, by email, or by fax.
Read the mail voting instructions.
Can I still register to vote?
Eligible Maryland residents can register during early voting at an early voting center in their county, or on Election Day at their assigned polling place.
Bring a document proving where you live, such as an MVA license or ID, paycheck, bank statement, or utility bill.
See registration and address guidance.
Where should I go for voting help?
Your local board of elections can help with registration, ballot questions, and voting arrangements.
Find your county board of elections.
