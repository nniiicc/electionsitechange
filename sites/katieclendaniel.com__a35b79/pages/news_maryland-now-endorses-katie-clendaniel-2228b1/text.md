Maryland NOW supports candidates working to advance equality and opportunity across Maryland.
“This group of strong women promotes legislative priorities focused on women’s economic security, safety, and independence.
Especially important to me are issues that relate to how women can better build financial security, access to reproductive health (access to women's healthcare) and stronger protections for women and children experiencing domestic violence, sexual violence, stalking, coercive control and financial abuse” Clendaniel says, commending the good work Maryland NOW has dedicated their committee to.
The organization joins a growing list of groups supporting Clendaniel’s campaign.
With the support of Maryland NOW, Clendaniel and her team will continue working towards election day on November 3rd.
