Donate Privacy Policy Effective Date: August 14, 2026 About This Policy This Privacy Policy explains how Friends of Kathleen Clendaniel collects, uses, and shares information submitted through this website.
In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to Friends of Kathleen Clendaniel.
Information We Collect We may collect information that you choose to provide, including your first and last name, email address, message, volunteer interests, and any other information you submit through a campaign form.
Our website platform and service providers may also collect technical information such as your browser type, device information, Internet Protocol address, pages viewed, referring website, and information collected through cookies or similar technologies.
Donations Campaign contributions are processed through ActBlue rather than directly through this website.
Information submitted on ActBlue is governed by the ActBlue Privacy Policy and ActBlue’s other applicable terms.
How We Use Information We may use information to respond to questions and requests, send campaign news and updates to people who choose to receive them, coordinate volunteers and events, operate and improve the website, comply with campaign finance requirements and other applicable laws, and protect the rights, safety, and operations of the campaign and others.
How Information May Be Shared We may share information with vendors and service providers that help us operate the website, communicate with supporters, manage campaign activities, process contributions, or comply with legal obligations.
We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a government authority, or when reasonably necessary to protect the campaign, website visitors, or others.
Mobile Messaging Mobile information, including text messaging opt in data and consent, will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
This information may be shared only with service providers necessary to operate and deliver the messaging program.
Message frequency may vary, up to one message per week.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP.
For help, reply HELP or contact info@katieclendaniel.com .
Message and data rates may apply.
Your Choices You may unsubscribe from campaign emails by using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting us at info@katieclendaniel.com .
You may request that we review or update information you previously submitted by contacting us.
Third Party Websites This website includes links to third party websites, including ActBlue, social media platforms, and government election resources.
We are not responsible for the privacy or security practices of those websites.
Their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.
Security We use reasonable measures to protect information under our control.
No website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Please do not send sensitive personal or financial information by email.
Children’s Privacy This website is not directed to children under thirteen, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under thirteen.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Changes become effective when the revised policy is posted on this website.
Contact Us Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to info@katieclendaniel.com or 410-205-4770.
