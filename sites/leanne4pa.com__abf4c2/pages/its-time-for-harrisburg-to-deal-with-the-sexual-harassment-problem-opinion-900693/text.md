The #MeToo movement has been instrumental in raising awareness of the pervasiveness of sexual harassment and sexual assault.
It has highlighted the unfortunate reality that no industry or community is immune to this epidemic and brought to the light the lack of formal processes for victims to report claims of workplace harassment and assault and employers to effectively address them.
This has not just been true in Hollywood or private industry — it has also been true in Pennsylvania’s State Capitol.
