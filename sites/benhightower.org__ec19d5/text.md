Now, I'm answering a new call: to serve our community and fix what's broken in Annapolis.
Our community deserves leadership that actually solves problems we face everyday— not just ignores them to focus on self interests.
I'm running because I believe District 9 families and businesses deserve better, and I know how to get results.
This isn't about party politics—it's about bringing common-sense leadership back to Maryland.

Together, we can build a Maryland that works for everyone.
