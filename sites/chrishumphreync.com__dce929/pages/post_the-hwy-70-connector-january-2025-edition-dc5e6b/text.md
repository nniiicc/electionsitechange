APPLICATIONS OPEN: NC HOUSE PAGE PROGRAM Do you know a high school student interested in government and leadership?
The NC House Page Program offers a unique, four-day experience to learn about the legislative process and assist in the daily operations of the General Assembly.
Open to students in grades 10-12, this program provides a firsthand look at how laws are made.
Learn more and apply online at www.ncleg.gov/House/PageProgram .
LENOIR COUNTY FAIR EARNS STATEWIDE RECOGNITION Congratulations to the Lenoir County Fair for earning the Innovation Award at the NC Agricultural Fairs Conference!
Their creative display featuring bug catchers in the field highlights the hard work and ingenuity of our community.
A special thanks to Bonnie Faulkner and everyone involved in making this achievement possible.
We're proud to see Lenoir County shining on the statewide stage!

About Representative Chris Humphrey An Eagle Scout who was born and raised in Eastern North Carolina, Chris Humphrey has always been deeply invested in the community, possessing a strong work ethic and a passion for public service from a young age.
Humphrey was first elected to the N.C.
House of Representatives in 2018.
While in office, he has been an advocate for defending the 2nd Amendment, upholding Voter ID laws, safeguarding parental rights, and protecting our state from the harmful effects of flooding and other natural disasters.
