APPLICATIONS OPEN: NC HOUSE PAGE PROGRAM
Do you know a high school student interested in government and leadership?
The NC House Page Program offers a unique, four-day experience to learn about the legislative process and assist in the daily operations of the General Assembly.
Open to students in grades 10-12, this program provides a firsthand look at how laws are made.
Learn more and apply online at www.ncleg.gov/House/PageProgram.
LENOIR COUNTY FAIR EARNS STATEWIDE RECOGNITION
Congratulations to the Lenoir County Fair for earning the Innovation Award at the NC Agricultural Fairs Conference!
Their creative display featuring bug catchers in the field highlights the hard work and ingenuity of our community.
A special thanks to Bonnie Faulkner and everyone involved in making this achievement possible.
We're proud to see Lenoir County shining on the statewide stage!
JONES COUNTY POSTER CONTEST

This is a wonderful chance to showcase your creativity and learn more about conservation.
📅 Deadline: All submissions are due by March 14th, 2025.
Don’t miss this opportunity to participate in an engaging and educational experience.
For details on how to get involved, reach out to the Jones County Soil & Water Conservation District: blaire.king@jonescountync.gov.
