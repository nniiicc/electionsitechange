NEW EMERGENCY SERVICES FACILITIES IN JONES COUNTY
We recently celebrated the opening of two vital emergency facilities in Jones County — a new EMS station in Pollocksville and a renovated Emergency Operations Center in Trenton.
Thanks to support from the NC General Assembly and the USDA, these investments will cut response times, expand 24/7 coverage, and ensure our first responders have the tools they need to protect lives across our rural communities.
These facilities will serve as the heartbeat of emergency response — from hurricanes and floods to any crisis that comes our way.
This is where lives are protected through preparation, coordination, and teamwork.

NC HOUSE PASSES BUDGET PAY PLAN
In October, the NC House unanimously passed our Budget Pay Plan — an investment in our state’s workforce:
✅ 8%+ raises for teachers — with the highest starting pay in the Southeast
✅ 13% average raises for local law enforcement
✅ 2.5% raises for state employees
✅ COLA bonuses for retired public servants
✅ Restored degree pay for teachers with advanced education
This plan delivers meaningful resources to those who serve and protect our state — and I’m proud to have supported it.

BIG JOBS NEWS FOR GREENE AND LENOIR COUNTIES
I’m excited to share that three major companies have announced over $33 million in investment and 154 new jobs for Lenoir and Greene counties!
✅ Electrolux expanding in Kinston – 74 jobs
✅ Cardon Rehabilitation bringing HQ to Kinston – 20 jobs
✅ Judco Manufacturing moving HQ to Snow Hill – 60 jobs
