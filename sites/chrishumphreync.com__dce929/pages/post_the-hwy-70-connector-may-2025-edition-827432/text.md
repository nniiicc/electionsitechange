NC HOUSE BUDGET HIGHLIGHTS
The NC House recently passed our version of the 2025 Appropriations Act with overwhelming bipartisan support — 93 to 20.
I proudly voted YES on a budget that reflects our values and delivers for North Carolina:
✅ $50K starting teacher pay — investing in the future of our children
✅ No tax on tips — relief for hardworking service workers
✅ Back-to-school tax holiday — support for parents and students
✅ $450M+ to rebuild Western NC — committed to flood resilience & recovery
❌ $10M cut from DEI bureaucracy — prioritizing merit and accountability
This is smart, conservative leadership with real bipartisan backing.
North Carolina families deserve nothing less.
NO TAX ON TIPS!
In Eastern North Carolina, we know the value of hard work — and that includes the folks in our service industry who go above and beyond every day.
That’s why I’m proud our NC House Budget includes a “NO TAX ON TIPS” provision — exempting the first $5,000 in tip income from state tax.
I’ll always support hardworking North Carolinians who are doing everything they can to make a better life for themselves and their family.
SAVING NC FAMILIES MORE MONEY
Our NC House budget also includes an increase to the standard deduction — an additional $500 for individual filers and $1,000 for married couples.
I remain committed to supporting policies that strengthen our economy and ease financial burdens on working families across North Carolina — allowing them to keep more of their hard-earned income.
BACK TO SCHOOL TAX-FREE HOLIDAY
I’m proud to support a spending plan for our state that puts families first! 🎒📚
In the NC House Budget, we’re bringing back the Tax-Free Weekend starting August 2026.
That means more savings for parents and students on school supplies, clothes, and other essentials.
This is a common-sense move that helps hardworking North Carolinians keep more of their money.
NC HOUSE CROSSOVER HIGHLIGHTS
We've officially made it through the crossover deadline at the North Carolina General Assembly — a key milestone where bills must pass their originating chamber to remain alive for the remainder of session.
I’m proud to report that several important bills made it through the House, reflecting our commitment to public safety, family values, government accountability, and common-sense reforms.
From supporting our firefighters and veterans to protecting kids online and cracking down on fraud, these measures are about delivering real results for the people of North Carolina.
