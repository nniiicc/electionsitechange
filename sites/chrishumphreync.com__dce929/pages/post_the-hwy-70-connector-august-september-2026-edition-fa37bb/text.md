ADDRESSING TRAFFIC THAT'S BEING RE-ROUTED THROUGH RURAL ROADS IN ENC My office has been hearing from residents and local farmers about a troubling increase in speeding, crashes, near misses, and unfamiliar traffic on roads in Lenoir County.
That’s why I sent a letter to Transportation and Agriculture officials, along with Google Maps , Apple Maps , and Waze — asking them to take a closer look at whether GPS systems are re-routing drivers onto roads that weren’t designed for heavy through-traffic.
These roads are used every day by families, school buses, farmers and slow-moving farm equipment.
We need to work together to identify solutions that protect rural residents in North Carolina and across the country.
While the letter highlighted several specific roads as examples, the concerns raised are not limited to those roads.
The letter was intended to address the broader issue of GPS navigation directing traffic onto rural roads across our communities that were not designed to handle increased through-traffic.
I’m grateful for the opportunity to discuss these concerns in an interview with WITN (video linked below).
The goal isn’t to close roads — it’s to make sure navigation technology accounts for the realities of rural roads and helps keep our families, farmers, and drivers safe.
