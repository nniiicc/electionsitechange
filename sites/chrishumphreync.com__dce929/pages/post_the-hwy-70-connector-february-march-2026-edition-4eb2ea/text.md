GREENE COUNTY OPENS NEW PLAYGROUND AT RECREATION COMPLEX
Greene County recently celebrated the opening of its first inclusive playground at the Recreation Complex in Snow Hill.
Funded by a $250,000 grant from Trillium Health Resources, the playground features wheelchair-accessible surfaces, sensory-friendly equipment, and a liberty swing.
This is a great example of how community partnerships can expand opportunities for families and strengthen quality of life in Eastern NC.

VETERANS FARM PROJECT IN JONES COUNTY
During a recent Jones County Board of Commissioners meeting, county leaders heard an update on the Veterans Farm Project, a program designed to help veterans transition into agriculture while supporting mental health and personal well-being.
The program provides hands-on farming experience, training, and structure to help veterans build skills, purpose, and community as they move into civilian life.
For more information, contact the Jones County Veterans Services Office at 252-448-1121.

JONES COUNTY BROADBAND EXPANSION
Jones County continues to make progress expanding high-speed internet through the CAB program with Brightspeed.
Construction is underway in multiple areas, with 27 locations now able to access fiber service.
Once complete, the project will bring reliable broadband to approximately 192 locations countywide, focusing on unserved and underserved areas.
