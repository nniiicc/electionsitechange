THE HWY 70 CONNECTOR: February-March 2026 Edition Mar 1 4 min read Dear Neighbor, It’s been a full and productive month across Lenoir, Greene, and Jones counties, and I’ve been grateful for the opportunity to stay engaged with so many of you at local events, community meetings and celebrations.
From breaking ground on new economic development projects to celebrating achievements in our schools and recognizing the leaders who make Eastern North Carolina strong — there’s a lot of positive momentum happening across our district.
I remain focused on creating opportunity, strengthening rural communities, and making sure our region continues to move forward towards prosperity.
Thank you for the continued trust you place in me to represent you in the NC House.
As always, if my office can ever be a resource for you and your family, please don’t hesitate to reach out.
At your service, Chris Humphrey State Representative GROUNDBREAKING IN JONES COUNTY I was proud to join local leaders in Trenton for the groundbreaking of Jones County’s new 20,000-square-foot industrial shell building.
This investment will help recruit new businesses, create jobs for our residents, and strengthen Eastern North Carolina’s economy.
Projects like this give folks in our communities the opportunity to build their careers right here at home.
The future is bright for Jones County!
STATE AUDITOR DAVE BOLIEK VISITS ROTARY CLUB OF KINSTON It was a pleasure to have Auditor Dave Boliek and his team from the NC Office of the State Auditor join us for the Kinston Rotary Club meeting at King’s BBQ.
We appreciated the update on the important work being done to ensure accountability and transparency in state government.
GREENE COUNTY OPENS NEW PLAYGROUND AT RECREATION COMPLEX Greene County recently celebrated the opening of its first inclusive playground at the Recreation Complex in Snow Hill.
Funded by a $# grant from Trillium Health Resources, the playground features wheelchair-accessible surfaces, sensory-friendly equipment, and a liberty swing.
This is a great example of how community partnerships can expand opportunities for families and strengthen quality of life in Eastern NC.

VETERANS FARM PROJECT IN JONES COUNTY During a recent Jones County Board of Commissioners meeting, county leaders heard an update on the Veterans Farm Project, a program designed to help veterans transition into agriculture while supporting mental health and personal well-being.
The program provides hands-on farming experience, training, and structure to help veterans build skills, purpose, and community as they move into civilian life.
For more information, contact the Jones County Veterans Services Office at 252-448-1121.

READ MORE >>> JONES COUNTY BROADBAND EXPANSION Jones County continues to make progress expanding high-speed internet through the CAB program with Brightspeed.
Construction is underway in multiple areas, with 27 locations now able to access fiber service.
Once complete, the project will bring reliable broadband to approximately 192 locations countywide, focusing on unserved and underserved areas.

About Representative Chris Humphrey An Eagle Scout who was born and raised in Eastern North Carolina, Chris Humphrey has always been deeply invested in the community, possessing a strong work ethic and a passion for public service from a young age.
Humphrey was first elected to the N.C.
House of Representatives in 2018.
While in office, he has been an advocate for defending the 2nd Amendment, upholding Voter ID laws, safeguarding parental rights, and protecting our state from the harmful effects of flooding and other natural disasters.
