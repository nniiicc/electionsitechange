NC HOUSE PASSES "IRYNA'S LAW"
Our communities deserve peace of mind knowing repeat violent offenders won’t slip through the cracks.
That's why I voted YES to pass Iryna’s Law — because I believe in accountability and justice.
This new law protects victims, keeps dangerous criminals behind bars, and delivers enhanced public safety for every North Carolinian.
