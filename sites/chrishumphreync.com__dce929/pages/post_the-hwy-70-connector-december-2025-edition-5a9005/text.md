As your state representative, I remain committed to strengthening education in eNC.
A big win for Greene County families!
FAMILY REBUILDING HISTORIC HOUSE IN JONES COUNTY
A piece of Jones County history is coming back to life!
The David Green House, built in 1832 just outside Trenton, is being lovingly restored by sixth great-grandson David Cole and his wife, Morgan.
After decades of vacancy, they're preserving original features like the hardwood floors, fireplaces, and trim work while carefully updating it for modern living.
Their dedication is a powerful reminder of the importance of honoring our past while building for the future.
Congratulations to the Cole family on this incredible labor of love!
