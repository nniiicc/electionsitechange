Chris Humphrey STATE FUNDING FOR NEW WEST GREENE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL I’m proud to announce that Greene County is receiving $42 million in state funding to replace West Greene Elementary School — addressing increased enrollment and ensuring our students have access to a safe, modern, and accessible learning environment.
As your state representative, I remain committed to strengthening education in eNC.
A big win for Greene County families!
FAMILY REBUILDING HISTORIC HOUSE IN JONES COUNTY A piece of Jones County history is coming back to life!
The David Green House, built in 1832 just outside Trenton, is being lovingly restored by sixth great-grandson David Cole and his wife, Morgan.
After decades of vacancy, they're preserving original features like the hardwood floors, fireplaces, and trim work while carefully updating it for modern living.
Their dedication is a powerful reminder of the importance of honoring our past while building for the future.
Congratulations to the Cole family on this incredible labor of love!

About Representative Chris Humphrey An Eagle Scout who was born and raised in Eastern North Carolina, Chris Humphrey has always been deeply invested in the community, possessing a strong work ethic and a passion for public service from a young age.
Humphrey was first elected to the N.C.
House of Representatives in 2018.
While in office, he has been an advocate for defending the 2nd Amendment, upholding Voter ID laws, safeguarding parental rights, and protecting our state from the harmful effects of flooding and other natural disasters.
