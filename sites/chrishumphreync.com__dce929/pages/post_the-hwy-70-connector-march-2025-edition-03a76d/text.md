MORE DISASTER RECOVERY FUNDING
More hurricane relief is on the way!
This week, the NC House passed — and the Governor signed into law — a $528 million recovery package to help western NC rebuild after Hurricane Helene.
This funding will support farmers, repair roads and bridges, rebuild homes, and get small businesses back on their feet.
House Bill 47 also includes $217 million to help eastern NC residents who were impacted by Hurricanes Matthew and Florence to get back into homes.
I’m proud to stand with my colleagues to deliver real help to those who need it most.

TRAFFIC UPDATE FOR JONES COUNTY
Heads up, drivers!
The intersection at N.C.
Highway 41 & Wyse Fork/Ten Mile Fork Road is now a four-way stop.
This change comes after NCDOT studies found the area prone to accidents, making this a cost-effective way to improve safety.
Please be alert and drive safely as everyone adjusts to the new traffic pattern.
