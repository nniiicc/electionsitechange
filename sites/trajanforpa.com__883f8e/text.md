As a 4th generation resident of Indiana County, Trajan Jones has spent his life dedicated to the improvement of the place that shaped him.
From his early days in the Indiana School District to his professional career in community development, Trajan's journey is one of service, empathy, and a tireless commitment to the people in his community.
As an emerging professional in the urban planning field, Trajan understands the complexities of local policy.
He has bridged gaps between residents and local government, fighting for transparent leadership and sustainable growth.
His qualifications aren't just titles — they are the real solutions he's brought to our neighborhoods.
At the center of Trajan's platform are core values that resonate with Indiana County families: integrity, accessibility, and resilience.
He isn't just running for office; he's running to ensure every voice is heard in the halls of the PA House.

05
Raise Wages & Paid Sick Leave
PA's minimum wage has been frozen at $7.25 since 2009 — a 30% pay cut after inflation.
Workers deserve a living wage and the right to stay home when they're sick without losing a paycheck.
Learn More
- Fight for a $15 minimum wage — 82% of PA voters support a raise
- Pass paid sick leave legislation — PA has zero protections, 21 states do
- Protect union rights and fight wage theft against working families
- Support workforce training and apprenticeship programs in Indiana County
- Close loopholes that let corporations exploit part-time and gig workers
$7.25 since 2009 — every neighboring state has raised theirs
06
Reproductive Freedom
Healthcare decisions belong to you, your family, and your doctor — not politicians in Harrisburg. 63% of Pennsylvanians agree: protect the right to choose.
Learn More
- Protect access to reproductive healthcare and oppose any abortion bans
- Fight efforts to amend the PA Constitution to strip reproductive rights
- Expand Medicaid coverage for maternal and reproductive health services
- Support access to contraception, IVF, and family planning resources
- Stand against extreme politicians who put ideology over women's health
63% of PA supports reproductive freedom
07
Data Centers That Answer To Us
The country's largest gas-fired power plant is being built in our district to run AI data centers.
The construction jobs are real.
So are the questions about our water, our air, and our electric bills.
Indiana County deserves straight answers on both.
Learn More
- Require public disclosure of how much water a facility will draw before permits are approved
- Independent air monitoring in Homer City, Center Township and neighboring communities, with results posted publicly
- Protect Indiana County households from rate increases driven by data center demand
- Enforceable local hiring and apprenticeship commitments so the permanent jobs go to people who live here
- Make sure host communities keep a real share of the tax benefit, not just the impact
4.4 gigawatts under construction in Indiana County
