Endorsements from Local Leaders
I am honored to have the support of several local leaders who have dedicated years to serving Canton and Avon.
Tim LeGeyt - Former State Representative, 17th District — Canton & Avon
“I am offering my recommendation for Andrew Ziemba in his bid to win the 17th District House seat this November.
Andrew is a sincere, informed, caring and intelligent candidate for election to the House of Representatives.
He is dedicated to supporting the concerns and preferences of the people in Canton and Avon.
He knows his District and has campaigned thoroughly throughout the District to connect with the residents of the 17th District.
He is informed about the issues and policies that affect the residents of his District and will work energetically to represent them.
He has a strong understanding of what makes good government and will work with the other members of the House to effect that end.
He is a eager listener and has an open mind about the issues and how to create solutions that will benefit the citizens of the district and the State of Connecticut.
I strongly recommend Andrew for election to the 17th District House seat and urge you to support him with your vote!!
Connecticut needs thoughtful and caring legislators like Andrew Ziemba!”
