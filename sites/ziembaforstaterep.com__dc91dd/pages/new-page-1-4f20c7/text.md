He grew up in Canton, graduated from Canton High School in 2005, and continues to call the community home with his wife and family.
A parishioner at Immaculate Conception Parish in New Hartford, Andrew's faith, family, and commitment to service are at the center of his life.
Civility, compassion, and patriotism guide his daily decisions and his dedication to strengthening the community he loves.
As a residential solar consultant, Andrew's work takes him door-to-door throughout Connecticut, from the neighborhoods of Avon and Litchfield to communities in Hartford and Bridgeport.
Along the way, he has built friendships and learned from people of all backgrounds and walks of life.
These experiences have reinforced his belief in humility, gratitude, and treating every person with dignity and respect.
As the son of a Polish immigrant who came to America seeking opportunity and freedom, Andrew has a deep appreciation for the American dream and the values that make our country strong.
Outside of work, Andrew enjoys spending time with his family, weightlifting, and exploring Connecticut's walking trails throughout the Farmington Valley.
He and his wife enjoy cooking together, and he proudly supports her growing cottage bakery business, which she launched in 2025.
Andrew also maintains extensive collections of clothing, shoes, and books, reflecting his years operating an online haberdashery and bookstore.
His passion for meeting new people and building relationships naturally drew him to both sales and public service.
