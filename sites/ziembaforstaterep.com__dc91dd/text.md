Canton is home.

My allegiance is to the Constitution and to the people — not to a party, not to Hartford, and not to Washington.
I’m running to bring people together regardless of party affiliation and restore the sensible middle.
The voters demand it and I’ll deliver it.
Canton and Avon send a fortune to the state, but education aid to Canton and Avon has been flat for more than a decade while unfunded mandates pile on.
This has created a property tax crisis and it must be reversed.
Canton and Avon should not subsidize the rest of the state while seniors and hard working families here struggle.

Local zoning keeps getting overridden.
Our debt per capita is outrageous and climbing.
We cannot tax and borrow our way to prosperity.

They haven’t.
