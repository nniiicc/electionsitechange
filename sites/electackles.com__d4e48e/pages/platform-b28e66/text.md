BETTER YOUTH OPPORTUNITIES
In rural areas, there is a pressing concern regarding the limited opportunities available for youth, both in terms of recreational facilities and services.
This dearth not only impacts the positive development of young individuals but also contributes to potential issues such as juvenile delinquency.
Addressing this challenge requires a multifaceted approach that involves creating and enhancing recreational facilities, fostering partnerships with non-profit organizations, and optimizing school services to effectively use the time when children are both in and out of school.
Better Youth Opportunities is OUR FIGHT!
