Janene Ackles-Dublin is a proud mother, wife, and long-time resident of District 43, where she has built both her family and her life’s purpose—serving the community she loves.
Born in Maryland and raised in a family rooted in service,
Her father served in World War I, World War II, and Vietnam.
He began military service in the US Marine Corps and retired from the US Army.
He completed a second career in the post office.
Her mother was an educator and trailblazer.
She graduated from Shaw University and completed a career as an elementary school teacher.
She was the first Black Girl Scout to integrate Girl Scout camps in North Carolina at Camp Mary Atkinson.
Janene’s passion for civic engagement began early.
She discovered her love for politics while working with the Close Up Foundation in Alexandria, VA, and later on the 1991 mayoral campaign of Charlene Drew Jarvis.
That early spark set the tone for a lifetime of advocacy, leadership, and community-focused work.
Professionally, Janene is a highly accomplished grant writer and the founder of Grantastic Consulting, a firm that has secured millions of dollars for nonprofit organizations across the country.
Her journey into grant writing began with a simple request from a local pastor—one successful grant turned into a thriving career with over 200 organizations served.
Janene has built a reputation for excellence, hosting her first grant writing seminar in 2004, publishing Non-Profit 501C-3, Easy as 1-2-3, and helping churches, community groups, youth programs, shelters, and grassroots organizations access the funding they need to survive and grow.
Her work is deeply personal, shaped by years of perseverance, single motherhood, and a commitment to lifting others through opportunity and education.
In addition to running Grantastic Consulting, Janene is the President and Founder of the Cumberland County Community Watch Coalition, where she works daily to strengthen neighborhoods and build safer communities.
She also serves as a Board Member of both the Vision Resource Center (VRC)—supporting visually impaired residents—and the Jai 6 Youth Foundation, advocating for youth empowerment and community-based support.
Janene is a proud member of the Triple Nickels and the founder of “DisruptHERS,” a rapidly growing group of women rooted in action, not talk—women determined to create real change for families across Cumberland County.
Over the past year, she has also volunteered her grant writing services to nonprofits throughout Cumberland County, helping secure critical funding for programs, shelters, youth initiatives, and community development efforts.
Janene’s priorities for District 43 reflect the values she has lived by her entire life: comprehensive healthcare for every citizen, transformative education reforms—especially in rural communities, strong rural economic initiatives that create real job opportunities, clean soil and water for every family, and safe neighborhoods for all.
As a wife, the mother of a blended family of eight adult children, a grandmother, and a longtime community champion affectionately known as “Momma J,” she understands the everyday challenges and hopes of District 43 because she has lived them too.
With deep roots, proven leadership, and a true heart for service, Janene Ackles asks for your vote—and your partnership—in bringing positive, meaningful change to North Carolina House District 43
