ABOUT STEVE
Steve Feldman
As your congressman, my goal is to leave our children — and children everywhere — a fiscally sound country and a less violent culture.
I believe the best way to achieve those goals is to promote individual liberty and international peace, build on our foundation of shared values, and seek reasonable compromise.
Bringing this fresh perspective to Congress, I will work across divides to shrink government, reduce federal debt, and let people live their lives as they wish, as long as they do no harm to others.
As a physician and medical scientist who works with insurers, patient advocates, pharmaceutical companies, and other physicians to improve healthcare delivery, I have been rated one of the world’s leading experts in dermatology.
I have lived in North Carolina for more than 40 years and received my medical training at Duke University and the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill.
My wife Leora and I have been married for 40 years.
We have two sons, one a sportswriter and the other a graduate student studying statistics.
