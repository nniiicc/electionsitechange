About Debra
Representative Debra Lekanoff’s extensive career in public service spans more than three decades, during which she has faithfully served the 40th Legislative District, Washington State, Tribal communities, and served in federal roles under both Democratic and Republican administrations.
Her dedication to enacting policies, regulations, and fiscal investments that positively impact communities is commendable.
Why Re-Election?
My commitment to continue to serve in the Washington State Legislature comes from the dedication to a life of serving the people, giving all that is required to provide a future for generations to come.
I am dedicated to representing the 40th Legislative District and all Washingtonians, and I will continue to demonstrate a deep understanding of the challenges and opportunities that come with the responsibility of public service.
I will continue to provide my unwavering focus on serving those in need, including children, marginalized cultures, individuals striving for the American dream amidst adversity, industries working to create sustainable jobs, and the brave public safety officers and first responders who put their lives on the line every day, showcases your genuine dedication to improving the lives of all members of the community.
I am willing to face these challenges head-on with a steadfast commitment to advocate for those who are struggling with addiction and mental health issues and will do this with compassion and determination to create a brighter future for all Washingtonians.
I will give my unwavering commitment to serving in the Washington State Legislature as this will be testament of dedication to fostering positive change and uplifting those who are most in need.
My selfless devotion is a foundational part of who I am, and I will continue to demonstrate a genuine spirit of leadership and a strong commitment to creating a more inclusive and supportive society for every individual.
My hope is to make a significant impact on improving the lives of those you represent, and I will work tirelessly and give all my efforts to advocate for the community reflect my deep-rooted passion for building a better future for all.
Through these reflections, I have found clarity in my purpose.
Each time I have questioned my path, the answer has been clear – I am called to serve, and I am exactly where I need to be.
My commitment to the 40th District remains steadfast, and I believe it is our time to rise into leadership not just locally, but across the state.
To truly thrive as a community, we must unite our tribal, local, state, and federal governments, working collaboratively with a deep understanding of fiduciary and judiciary responsibilities to create healthy, safe, and prosperous communities throughout our state.
I seek re-election to the Washington State Legislature because I am driven by a deep sense of duty to serve and make a meaningful difference in the lives of those I represent.
I am committed to bringing people together, fostering cooperation, and advocating for policies that benefit all Washingtonians.
By continuing in this role, I aim to build upon the progress made and contribute to creating a better future for our district and our state.
