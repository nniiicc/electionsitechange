An Update from Representative Lekanoff
Dear Friends,
This week, I made the decision to step down from serving on the board of Earth Justice to focus and prioritize my time with the community I serve right here in the 40th, including my wonderful daughter Emma.
With salmon eggs in one hand and seeds in the other, it is an honor to be a part of this environmentalist community around me from our fields to our Orcas.
Thank you to Earth Justice for the opportunity to work and learn alongside our lawyers for Mother Earth.
“Stay Safe, Stay Healthy”
Rep.
Debra Lekanoff
