About Jon
Jon Nelson is a lifelong resident of Lake Preston, where he and his wife, Cathy, have built a life rooted in faith, family, and agriculture.
For more than four decades, Jon has farmed and raised cattle while helping grow a multigenerational family operation alongside their son and his family.
Agriculture is not just Jon’s livelihood — it is his way of life.
His leadership in South Dakota agriculture spans decades.
Jon currently serves as District 6 Director for the South Dakota Soybean Association and has served in multiple statewide commodity leadership roles.
As a graduate and former class president of South Dakota Ag and Rural Leadership, he has remained committed to developing the next generation of rural leaders through continued service on the SDARL Foundation Board.
Jon also served 12 years on the Lake Preston School Board, including multiple years as board chair, where he worked to support students, families, and strong local schools.
A lifelong member of North Preston Lutheran Church, Jon continues to serve his community in leadership roles, including as Chair of the Cemetery Association.
Jon’s life has always been about service, stewardship, and strengthening the community he calls home.
Why I’m Running
I’m running for the South Dakota House of Representatives in District 8 because I believe our rural way of life is worth protecting and strengthening for the future.
As a lifelong farmer and cattle feeder, I understand the challenges facing agriculture and the importance of protecting landowner rights.
Agriculture is the backbone of our state, and we need leadership that will stand up for it.
I also believe strong rural communities are essential.
That means supporting opportunities for growth, good jobs, strong schools, and ensuring families can build a future right here at home.
After a lifetime of farming, community involvement, and service—including time on the Lake Preston School Board and in agricultural leadership—I feel a responsibility to give back and represent the people of District 8.
As a husband, father, and grandfather of four, my goal is simple: to help keep South Dakota a place where the next generation has the opportunity to live, work, and prosper here if they choose.
I would be honored to have your support.
