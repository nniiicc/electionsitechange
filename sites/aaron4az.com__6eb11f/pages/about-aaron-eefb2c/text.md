Meet Aaron
An Entrepreneur, Problem Solver, and Bipartisan Leader.
A Career Built on Results
Born and raised in Phoenix, Aaron Lieberman isn't a career politician.
He's an entrepreneur and business leader who has spent his career building organizations that solve problems and improve people's lives.
After graduating from Yale, Aaron helped launch Jumpstart, a national early literacy nonprofit, and later co-founded Acelero Learning.
Under his leadership, Acelero grew into a nationally recognized company that created more than 2,000 good jobs and helped nearly one million preschoolers get ready for school.
Today, Aaron is the founder and CEO of Buzze, an Arizona company expanding access to electric vehicle charging in apartment communities and other multifamily housing.
Throughout his career, Aaron has built organizations, created jobs, and delivered practical solutions to real challenges.
Bringing That Experience to Public Service
Aaron brought that same results-oriented approach to the Arizona Legislature, where he worked across party lines to strengthen public education, expand economic opportunity, improve healthcare, and make state government work better for Arizona families.
His experience also taught him that the partisanship, name-calling, and extremism on both sides are tearing our state apart.
Arizona deserves leaders who focus less on political fights and more on solving problems.
Aaron believes no party has a monopoly on good ideas.
He'll work with anyone, regardless of party, to do what's right for Arizona and deliver results that make life better for Arizona families.
