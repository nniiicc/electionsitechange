Why I’m Running
Arizona deserves leaders who solve problems, not score political points.
An Arizona native, entrepreneur, bipartisan leader, and former state representative, Aaron has built businesses, created jobs, and delivered results.
He's running to bring that same practical leadership to the Arizona State Senate.
Priorities:
- Lower the cost of housing, healthcare, childcare, and everyday essentials so Arizona families can get ahead, not just get by.
- Ensure every child has access to an excellent education and every student is prepared for success, from early childhood through college or career training.
- Create more good-paying jobs by supporting entrepreneurs, small businesses, workforce development, and Arizona's growing innovation economy
- Strengthen Arizona's roads, water security, energy, and other critical infrastructure to support a growing economy and a high quality of life.
- Support law enforcement, invest in crime prevention and mental health, and ensure every Arizona family feels safe in their community.
