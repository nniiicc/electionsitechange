Every session, we get stronger in our community and in Columbia.
Keeping that strength is why I continue to run.
Together we can keep up the great work we are doing in the name of justice and equality for South Carolina.
I will always be standing tall for district 82, and come June 14th, and I hope you stand tall with me.
As the first African American to be elected to the Aiken city and county councils, I am a veteran civil servant with decades of experience.
Throughout my time in our community, I have been a champion for all South Carolinians.
Help me continue to champion education, rural development, and job creation in the statehouse; so we can keep our movement driving.
Representative Clyburn in our community I have fought for education, affordable healthcare, housing, senior citizens, jobs, public safety, and a better quality of life for Aiken, Edgefield, and Saluda Counties - NOT just by TALKING - but by BRINGING MONEY to this area in support of these causes.
MY record speaks for itself.
I have always appreciated the love and support you have given me over the years, and I am honored that you have given me the chance to fight for the things that matter to you most.
As a Christian and a family man, I am asking for your continued support on June 14th and the opportunity to continue fighting for you.
