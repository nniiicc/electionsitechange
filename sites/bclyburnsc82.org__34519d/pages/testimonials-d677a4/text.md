He was elected Executive Committeeman, and I was elected President of Aiken Precinct #4.
With our commitment to the task and the help of the people in the community, we developed that precinct into one of the top-performing precincts in voter turnout in Aiken.
Timeline - 2 In 1973, Bill was elected to the Aiken City Council in an at-large election.
He served in that office with distinction and dedication from 1973 to 1981.
Timeline - 3 Bill was then elected to the Aiken City Council, where he served from 1981 to 1986 with the same degree of dedication he is known for, that serves the people.
Timeline - 4 In 1986, South Carolina Governor Richard Riley appointed him to serve on the South Carolina Workers Compensation Commission.
This commission has statewide jurisdiction.
He served in the position from 1986 to 1994.
Timeline - 5 Bill was elected to the South Carolina House of Representative District 82 in 1995.
The district covers a portion of Aiken, Edgefield, and Saluda Counties.
He has served this district in an honorable manner from 1995 to the present.
The people of the community are pleased with his service.
Timeline - 6 Through Bill's leadership, $500,000.00 has been awarded to the counties under the Rural Infrastructure Authority, $250,000.00 to the African American Art and Culture Center, and $6,000,000.00 to Rural Health Center.
Timeline - 7 The South Carolina General Assembly presented Bill with an award for his idea of placing reflectors in the center of rural streets and roads.
This suggestion saves lives and SCDOT over $4,000,000.00 annually.
This community needs Bill Clyburn in the General Assembly!!
He is willing to serve, so please join us in the June 14, 2022, primary election.
VOTE FOR A PROVEN AND TRUSTED LEADER, VOTE FOR WILLIAM (BILL) CLYBURN!!
