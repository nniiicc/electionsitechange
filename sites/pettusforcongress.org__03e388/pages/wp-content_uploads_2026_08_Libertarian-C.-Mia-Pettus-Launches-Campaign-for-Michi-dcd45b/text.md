Mia Pettus Launches Campaign for Michigan’s 8th Congressional District – Independent Political Report Pettus first announced her candidacy earlier this month in a statement she later provided to Independent Political Report.
In it, Pettus said she is entering the race to draw attention to issues she feels are not being appropriately addressed by the political establishment, as well as to offer Michigan voters in the 8th District another choice on the ballot.
“Public office is a public trust,” Pettus stated in an included press release.
“I am running to represent the people of Michigan with integrity, transparency, accountability, and fidelity to the Constitution —not to become part of the political establishment.” Though she didn’t announce her bid until earlier this month, Pettus had already earned the nomination of the Libertarian Party of Michigan at its June 28 state convention in Troy.
She is part of a large slate of candidates fielded by Michigan Libertarians this cycle—with 30 earning the party’s support at the state convention alone—and one of seven Libertarians currently running for Congress in the state.
According to her campaign website, Pettus previously worked as a certified police officer in Philadelphia and later as a civilian federal police officer for the Department of Defense.
She says those experiences, along with later efforts to challenge government policies and procedures through administrative processes, helped shape her interest in due process, oversight, and the way public institutions exercise their authority.
That background has also influenced her work outside of law enforcement.
