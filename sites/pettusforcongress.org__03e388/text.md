Mia Pettus for Congress Michigan’s 8th District Restore Constitutional Government, Restore Trust, Restore Liberty Meet Mia Carissima Mia Pettus is a Libertarian candidate for the United States House of Representatives representing Michigan’s 8th Congressional District.
A former City of Philadelphia Police Officer, former Department of Defense Civilian Federal Police Officer, iindependent researcher, publisher of PALS Report on Substack, and digital creator, Mia Pettus brings experience in public service, constitutional research, government accountability, and advocacy for individual rights.
Her campaign focuses on restoring trust in government, protecting constitutional freedoms, strengthening transparency, supporting Michigan families, and ensuring public institutions remain accountable to the people they serve.I envision a government that faithfully follows the Constitution, respects individual liberty, protects taxpayer resources, and remains accountable to the people.
I believe our nation is strongest when government operates transparently, exercises only those powers granted by the Constitution, and serves citizens fairly regardless of political affiliation, race, religion, or background.
Together, we can restore trust, strengthen our communities, protect individual rights, and ensure that government once again works for the American people—not the other way around.
