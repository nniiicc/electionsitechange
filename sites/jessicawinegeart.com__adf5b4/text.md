Jessica Winegeart for Senate Home About Endorsements Platform Contact Get Involved Learn More Proudly Endorsed By About Jessica Calumet Wife and Mom Jessica and her husband, Erik, have deep roots in western Oklahoma.
They live with their two daughters in Calumet on the farm that Jessica's family has owned for almost 100 years.
Jessica attended Calumet High School; Erik was an all-state wrestler at Hinton.
Erik proudly served our country in the United States Air Force.
Today, he works as an oilfield pipeline inspector.
Hay Broker Jessica has a lifetime in agriculture.
She grew up working alongside her father in his trucking and hay sales operation.
Shortly after college, Jessica worked as a seed and fertilizer salesman.
In 2012, she joined her father in the family hay brokering business.
She took over the business in 2013 and has led it ever since.
School Board Member Jessica is deeply committed to education and the future of our rural communities.
For 5 years she served on the Calumet School Board, supporting better pay for teachers, discipline and high standards for our students, and absolute transparency and accountability with our tax dollars.
Traditional Conservative Values Right to Life A lifelong Republican, Jessica will support the right to life and protect the most vulnerable among us.
2nd Amendment Rights Jessica strongly supports our constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
Hold the Line on Taxes Jessica will fight against unnecessary taxes and regulations that burden hardworking Oklahomans.
Investment in Education Better investment in our schools, supporting teachers and ensuring high standards for students.
Infrastructure & Public Safety Strong support for better roads and public safety to keep our communities safe and connected.
Rural Heritage Strongly supporting our rural heritage and small-town way of life that makes Oklahoma great.
