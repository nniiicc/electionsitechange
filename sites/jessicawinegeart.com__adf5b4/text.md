Jessica and her husband, Erik, have deep roots in western Oklahoma.
They live with their two daughters in Calumet on the farm that Jessica's family has owned for almost 100 years.
Jessica attended Calumet High School; Erik was an all-state wrestler at Hinton.
Erik proudly served our country in the United States Air Force.
Today, he works as an oilfield pipeline inspector.
Jessica has a lifetime in agriculture.
She grew up working alongside her father in his trucking and hay sales operation.
Shortly after college, Jessica worked as a seed and fertilizer salesman.
In 2012, she joined her father in the family hay brokering business.
She took over the business in 2013 and has led it ever since.
Jessica is deeply committed to education and the future of our rural communities.
For 5 years she served on the Calumet School Board, supporting better pay for teachers, discipline and high standards for our students, and absolute transparency and accountability with our tax dollars.
A lifelong Republican, Jessica will support the right to life and protect the most vulnerable among us.
Jessica strongly supports our constitutional right to keep and bear arms.
Jessica will fight against unnecessary taxes and regulations that burden hardworking Oklahomans.
Better investment in our schools, supporting teachers and ensuring high standards for students.
Strong support for better roads and public safety to keep our communities safe and connected.
Strongly supporting our rural heritage and small-town way of life that makes Oklahoma great.
