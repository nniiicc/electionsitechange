Chris Gallant Letter to the Editor Guidelines How to Write a Letter to the Editor for Chris Gallant Step 1: Find the submission email Visit your local newspaper’s website and locate the email address or online form for submitting Letters to the Editor.
Step 2: Check the word limit Review the newspaper’s submission guidelines.
Many publications limit letters to 150–200 words.
Step 3: Write your letter Explain why you support Chris Gallant or highlight an issue that matters to you.
Use the tips below to make your letter personal and compelling.
Step 4: Submit your letter Paste the letter directly into the body of your email unless the newspaper provides different instructions.
Step 5: Include your contact information Provide your full name, address, and phone number if requested.
Newspapers use this information to verify your identity, but your phone number will not be published.
Step 6: Submit to local publications Consider smaller community newspapers as well as larger regional publications.
Local papers may offer a better chance of publication.
Tips for Writing Your Letter ●​ Focus on one topic.
You might write about Chris’s record of service, his plan to lower costs, his commitment to protecting Long Island’s environment, or another issue that matters to you. ●​ Keep it clear and concise.
You may have only 150–200 words, so make every sentence count and follow the publication’s word limit. ●​ Use your own voice.
You do not need to sound like an academic or policy expert.
Write as a thoughtful Long Island voter who cares about the future of your community. ●​ Make it personal.
Explain who you are, where you live, and how the issue affects you, your family, or your neighbors. ●​ Connect your story to Chris.
Explain why your experience has led you to support Chris Gallant and why you believe he is the right person to represent New York’s 1st Congressional District. ●​ End with a clear message.
Encourage other voters to learn more about Chris, get involved, or support him in the election.
Sample LTE Gallant Gets It To the Editor: It was just a month or two ago that Nick Lalota voted against a war powers resolution that could have brought an end to the war in Iran and brought our soldiers home.
As service members are killed and the cost of energy soars through the roof, it’s hard to understand why Nick Lalota continues to sanction this war.
Chris Gallant understands what it means to serve this country.
He has served for practically half his life.
At 17, he enlisted in the Army.
He’s a Black Hawk helicopter pilot and he has served two tours in the Middle East.
His own fiance is currently deployed.
Chris knows what it means to fight for America, yet in stark contrast to Lalota, he has taken a truly principled position on this conflict.
I was heartened to hear Chris say that he would vote to bring an end to the war in Iran.
He recognizes that this is a conflict we’ve been drawn into with no clear objectives or exit strategy.
He knows that with each day this war drags on, the risk to American service members increases and energy costs here at home will continue to skyrocket.
Chris is not a politician.
He’s one of us – feeling the weight of this war each and everyday.
We have an opportunity to send a leader with an admirable record of service to Congress.
Gallant has my vote.
