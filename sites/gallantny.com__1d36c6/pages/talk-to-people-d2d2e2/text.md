Talk to People
Relational organizing is when you organize within your community by talking to friends, family and others in your social network!
Data has shown that a conversation between people who know and trust each other is at least 2.5x more effective than a conversation between strangers.
What that means is you can play a significant role in helping us turn out low-propensity or undecided voters just by talking with people you know!
To start, we recommend mapping your personal networks.
Think of folks who are politically engaged or disengaged, or ones you think could be persuadable voters.
Don’t overthink; keep the conversation simple!
