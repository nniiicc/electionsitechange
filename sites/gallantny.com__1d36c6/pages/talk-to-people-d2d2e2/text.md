Data has shown that a conversation between people who know and trust each other is at least 2.5x more effective than a conversation between strangers.
What that means is you can play a significant role in helping us turn out low-propensity or undecided voters just by talking with people you know!
To start, we recommend mapping your personal networks.
Think of folks who are politically engaged or disengaged, or ones you think could be persuadable voters.
Don’t overthink; keep the conversation simple!
HOW TO TALK ABOUT THE NOVEMBER 3RD ELECTION A quick guide for conversations with friends, neighbors, and family about the NY-01 Midterm Election between Chris Gallant & Nick LaLota.
START WITH WHAT MATTERS: Lower costs.
Reliable leadership.
A representative who shows up and puts Long Island first.
Keep the Conversation Simple 1.
Ask: “What issue matters most to you right now?” Listen first and connect their concern to Chris’s record or Mission Plan.
2.
Share: “I’m supporting Chris because he has spent his life serving, he understands Long Island, and he will put people ahead of party politics.” 3.
Contrast: “Nick LaLota has had his chance.
His votes have cut health care and food assistance, prolonged an unauthorized war, and he has not been accessible to the people he represents.” 4.
Make a plan: Ask: “Are you registered to vote, and will you vote early, by mail, or on Election Day?” Then help them take the next step.
