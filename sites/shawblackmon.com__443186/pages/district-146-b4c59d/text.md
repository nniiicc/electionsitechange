District Information Shaw is proud to serve the community where he was born and raised.
Attending public school, while growing up in the area gives Representative Blackmon a unique perspective of the district, which is comprised of multiple communities within Houston County.
Shaw is familiar with our longstanding traditions, but has also experienced the growth, change and development of our district, giving him further insight as to handle the issues he is presented while serving.
Representative Blackmon is proud to work for his family-owned small business which currently helps to support over 40 families.
