Ben Keathley explains how over 300,000 Missourians are taking advantage of the senior property tax freeze across 90 different counties.
"These last four years, as your state representative, I have many accomplishments I'm proud of.
But one I'm most proud of is the senior property tax freeze.
Passed not once but twice.
The original measure in 2023 and the expansion bill in 2024.
Since we passed the expansion, over 300,000 Missourians are taking advantage of the senior property tax freeze across 90 different counties.
We are saving taxpayers hundreds of millions of dollars every year between that and income tax elimination on Social Security benefits and public pensions.
Seniors have benefited under these programs.
