About Noelle
Long before being elected to the State House, Noelle was a wife, mother, small business owner, and community advocate.
Her approach to public service is rooted in the values that have guided her throughout her life: faith, family, freedom, and a belief that government should remain accountable to the people.
That legacy has shaped Noelle’s commitment to honoring those who have served our country and defending the constitutional principles of freedom and individual liberty.
Noelle and her husband have been married for almost 30 years and are the proud parents of two grown children.
Together, they built a successful accounting and consulting firm serving clients nationwide, giving Noelle firsthand experience with the challenges facing small businesses and working families.
Her involvement in the local faith community has strengthened her belief in the important role families, churches, and community institutions play in building strong communities and preparing the next generation to thrive.
As President and Co-Founder of Protect Student Health Georgia and through her work with the Child & Parental Rights Campaign, Noelle became a leading advocate for parental rights.
She has also been active in the Republican Party, taught biblical citizenship throughout Georgia, and served in numerous roles supporting election integrity and Georgia’s election process.
Her grassroots involvement eventually led her to the State Capitol and ultimately to the Georgia House of Representatives.
Today, Noelle has the privilege of representing the community she calls home.
It is a responsibility she carries with the same values that first drew her to public service.
Whether she’s under the Gold Dome or back home in Henry County, Noelle believes the job starts with listening, staying connected, and never forgetting who she was elected to represent.
“It is an honor to represent the community I call home.
I will continue working every day to protect our values, strengthen Henry County, and make sure the people of District 81 have a strong voice under the Gold Dome.”
