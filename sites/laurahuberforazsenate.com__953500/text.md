Priorities That Put People First
From water security to mental health, these are the issues that define our community's future.
Water Security
Arizona cannot afford to wait for the next crisis to act.
We need to protect the water we have, develop responsible new supplies, and make sure growth does not come at the expense of our farms, communities, or future generations.
Education
Ensure adequate funding, safe learning environments, competitive compensation, and the tools teachers need to succeed.
Healthcare
Expand access to care, maternal and mental health services, telehealth, and pandemic preparedness.
Environmental Protection
Protect Arizona's air, land, and natural resources through responsible policy, conservation programs, and sustainable growth that benefits communities across the state.
A Counselor, a Farmer's Daughter, and Your Future State Senator
I come from five generations of farmers and ranchers.
I grew up on wildlife refuges surrounded by open skies, winding rivers, and people who understood how deeply connected we are to the land and to each other.
That same sense of duty and compassion guided me into a career in mental health, where I've sat with people in their darkest moments and seen how powerful it can be when government actually works for the people it serves.
