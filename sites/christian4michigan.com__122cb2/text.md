Michigan's 5th District Christian Vukasovich for Congress Common Sense • Community • Caring Election Day Nov 3, 2026 Join Our Movement Meet Christian Meet Christian Vukasovich Born and raised in Michigan, Christian's values are rooted in his experience where hard work is valued, and communities take care of one another.
A son of an immigrant family on his father's side that came to America to build a better life, and descended from a long line of Michigan ministers on his mother's side, Christian was raised to believe in faith, fairness, and service to others.
He worked his way through school as a bricklayer, learning the value of an honest day's work.
Christian is a husband, a father, and a teacher.
He has dedicated his career to helping students build brighter futures for themselves and their families.
As a union organizer, he has experience standing shoulder to shoulder with workers fighting for fair wages, dignity, and respect.
Christian believes that Washington should work for working people.
Congress needs a strong voice fighting to keep the American Dream alive for every man, woman, and child.
