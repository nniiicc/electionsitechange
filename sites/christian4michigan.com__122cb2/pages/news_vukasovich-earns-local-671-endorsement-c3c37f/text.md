Vukasovich Earns Local 671 Endorsement MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich announced today that his campaign has received the endorsement of Plumbers, Pipefitters, HVAC & Welders Local 671.
“I am proud to have the support of the skilled tradespeople of Local 671,” Vukasovich said.
“I know what a hard day’s work looks like.
I spent years working as a bricklayer while earning my degrees, and I know that a good union job can provide a family with the stability and dignity to build a future.” Vukasovich is running to lower costs, raise wages, strengthen unions and invest in the infrastructure and manufacturing that create good-paying jobs across southern Michigan.
His campaign has emphasized prevailing wages, project labor agreements and ensuring that public investments create opportunities for local workers.
“Southern Michigan does not need more politicians making promises to working people while sending our tax dollars to corporations,” Vukasovich said.
“We need to put people to work building the roads, bridges, schools, factories and infrastructure our communities need.
I will fight for union jobs and make sure working people have a seat at the table.” The endorsement adds Local 671 to a growing coalition of labor, community and progressive organizations supporting Vukasovich’s campaign for Congress.
