Media Contact: Jae Slaby, Campaign Manager Jae@christian4michigan.com September 6, 2026 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Vukasovich Calls for Accountability Over $27 Billion Federal Corporate Portfolio MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich is calling for greater congressional oversight of the federal government’s growing portfolio of corporate investments, which has reached approximately $27.7 billion across 39 deals, according to the Council on Foreign Relations.
The investments span critical industries including semiconductors, steel, energy and mining.
They include a 10% stake in Intel, a golden share in U.S.
Steel and equity positions in companies including MP Materials and Lithium Americas.
Vukasovich said taxpayers deserve greater transparency about how these investments are made and what protections are in place to ensure public dollars serve the public interest.
“When working families ask Washington to help lower their costs or raise their wages, they’re told the government should stay out of the economy.
But when Washington spends billions of taxpayer dollars acquiring stakes in private corporations, we need to have an honest conversation about what role the government should play.” Vukasovich also pointed to the administration’s 35% stake in North American Blue Energy Partners, a venture with rights to develop Venezuelan oil fields, as another example of the federal government taking a direct ownership role in private enterprise.
“Government should work for the people who pay the bills, not play venture capitalist with their money,” Vukasovich said.
“Congress needs to provide real oversight and make sure Michigan families, not politically connected corporations, are the ones who benefit from our economic policies.” ### Open attached PDF Christian Vukasovich Running for U.S.
