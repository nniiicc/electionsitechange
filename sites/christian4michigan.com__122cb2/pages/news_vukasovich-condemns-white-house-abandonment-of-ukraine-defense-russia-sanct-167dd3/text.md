Vukasovich Condemns White House Abandonment of Ukraine Defense, Russia Sanctions MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich condemned recent White House decisions involving Ukraine’s satellite defenses and U.S. sanctions on Russia, warning that erratic leadership and backroom deals put national security and human lives at risk.
The controversies include restrictions on Ukraine’s Starshield satellite network, which disrupted critical drone operations, followed by a decision to waive sanctions a bipartisan Congress had overwhelmingly approved to increase pressure on Russia.
“People deserve a foreign policy built on principles, not backroom deals,” Vukasovich said.
“You cannot claim to stand up to Russian aggression, sign bipartisan sanctions into law, and then waive them to secure a short-term political or commercial deal.
That undermines our credibility, puts our allies at risk, and sends the wrong message to our adversaries.” Vukasovich called for stronger congressional oversight and greater transparency in decisions affecting national security and civilian lives.
“Our government should be accountable to the American people, not driven by the whims of billionaires, corporate interests, or politicians looking to avoid public scrutiny,” Vukasovich said.
