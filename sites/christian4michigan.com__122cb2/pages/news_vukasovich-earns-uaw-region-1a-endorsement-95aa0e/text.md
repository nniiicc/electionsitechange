Vukasovich Earns UAW Region 1A Endorsement MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich announced today that his campaign has received the endorsement of UAW Region 1A in Michigan’s 5th Congressional District.
UAW Region 1A represents approximately 150,000 active and retired members across Monroe, Washtenaw and Wayne counties and communities extending to the Ohio border.
“I am honored to have the support of UAW Region 1A,” Vukasovich said.
“I come from a working-class family, worked as a bricklayer while earning my degrees, and organized my own union local.
I know that when workers have a seat at the table, families and communities are stronger.” Vukasovich is running to lower costs, raise wages, strengthen collective bargaining, support good-paying union jobs and rebuild American manufacturing.
His campaign has emphasized putting working people ahead of corporate lobbyists and wealthy donors.
“Working people should not have to choose between a good paycheck and a decent life,” Vukasovich said.
“I will take the fight for good jobs, affordable health care and stronger unions to Washington.
This campaign is about making sure working people have a voice at the table—and I am proud to have UAW members standing with us.” The endorsement adds UAW Region 1A to a growing list of labor organizations supporting Vukasovich’s campaign for Congress.
