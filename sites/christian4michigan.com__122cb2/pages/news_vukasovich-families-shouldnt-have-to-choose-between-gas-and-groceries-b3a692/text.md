Vukasovich: Families Shouldn’t Have to Choose Between Gas and Groceries MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich is challenging Rep.
Tim Walberg (R-Mich.) after Walberg said during an appearance on JTV’s The Bart Hawley Show that long-term energy security may require Americans to make sacrifices.
“Choosing between fuel and groceries isn’t a sacrifice.
It’s a crisis,” Vukasovich said.
“Families across southern Michigan are already making impossible choices about what bills they can afford to pay.
They need a representative who understands what that feels like and is focused on lowering costs, not telling working people to simply accept them.” Vukasovich said rising gas prices are adding to the pressure families face from higher utility bills, health care costs and other household expenses.
“People work hard, pay their bills and play by the rules,” Vukasovich said.
“They shouldn’t have to choose between getting to work and putting food on the table.
Washington should be working to make life more affordable for working families.” Vukasovich will discuss his plan to lower costs and protect household budgets at a candidate forum Tuesday, Sept.
29, at 6 p.m. at the Tecumseh District Library.
Vukasovich has confirmed his attendance.
Walberg has not yet accepted the invitation.
