30, 2026 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Vukasovich Demands Campus Sexual Assault Reform MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich is calling on Congress to tie federal funding to campus sexual-assault protections, pass the bipartisan Campus Accountability and Safety Act (CASA Act) and restore stronger Title IX safeguards, arguing that colleges should not be able to protect their reputations at the expense of student safety.
The renewed scrutiny of a sexual assault case at Cornell University is the latest example, Vukasovich said, of a broader national problem: institutions can face powerful incentives to minimize allegations, protect their standing and avoid scrutiny rather than put survivors first.
“Students should never have to wonder whether the institution they trust will protect them or protect itself,” Vukasovich said.
“There cannot be one set of rules for ordinary people and another for wealthy students, athletes or prestigious universities.
The law must apply equally.” Vukasovich is calling on Congress to pass CASA, require timely and transparent reporting of campus sexual violence, strengthen federal enforcement of Title IX and condition federal funding on meaningful compliance with student-safety standards.
He also criticized President Donald Trump’s rollback of stronger Title IX protections, arguing that weakening federal safeguards puts survivors at greater risk and gives institutions less incentive to address sexual violence openly.
“We cannot end campus rape culture by making it harder for survivors to come forward,” Vukasovich said.
“Congress needs to set a clear standard: protect students, report sexual violence transparently and enforce the law — or face consequences for failing to do so.” ### Open attached PDF Christian Vukasovich Running for U.S.
