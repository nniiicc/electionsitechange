Vukasovich Earns Michigan Education Association Endorsement MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich announced today that his campaign has received the endorsement of the Michigan Education Association (MEA), representing educators and education professionals across Michigan.
“I am honored to have the support of the Michigan Education Association,” Vukasovich said.
“I know firsthand that strong public schools are the foundation of strong communities and a strong economy.
Our educators deserve respect, fair wages, and the resources they need to give every student the opportunity to succeed.” Vukasovich is running to lower costs, raise wages, expand access to health care, strengthen workers’ rights and invest in communities across Michigan’s 5th Congressional District.
His campaign has emphasized putting people ahead of corporate lobbyists and wealthy donors and ensuring that working families have a voice in Washington.
“Teachers and school employees should not have to choose between serving their students and making ends meet,” Vukasovich said.
“We need to invest in public education, support the people who make our schools work, and make sure every child—regardless of where they live or how much their parents earn—has a fair shot at a good future.
I am proud to have the MEA standing with our campaign.” The endorsement adds the Michigan Education Association to a growing coalition of labor, community, and progressive organizations supporting Vukasovich’s campaign for Congress.
