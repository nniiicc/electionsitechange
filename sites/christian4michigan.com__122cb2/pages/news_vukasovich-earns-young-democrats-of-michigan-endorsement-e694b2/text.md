Vukasovich Earns Young Democrats of Michigan Endorsement MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich announced today that his campaign has received the endorsement of the Young Democrats of Michigan.
“I am proud to have the support of young Democrats across Michigan,” Vukasovich said.
“Young people are working hard, earning degrees, starting families and building careers, but too many are being told they should settle for higher costs and fewer opportunities.
We can do better.” Vukasovich is running to lower costs, raise wages, protect workers’ rights and build an economy that gives young people a fair shot at a good life in southern Michigan.
His campaign has emphasized affordable health care, good-paying union jobs, affordable housing, strong public schools and investments in infrastructure and manufacturing.
“Too many young people are working full time and still struggling to afford a home, pay for health care or build a future,” Vukasovich said.
“We need an economy that works for people who work for a living — not one that asks another generation to pay more while corporations and wealthy donors get more.
I will fight for an economy where hard work pays off and every young person has a real opportunity to build a future here in Michigan.” The endorsement adds the Young Democrats of Michigan to a growing coalition of labor, community and progressive organizations supporting Vukasovich’s campaign for Congress.
