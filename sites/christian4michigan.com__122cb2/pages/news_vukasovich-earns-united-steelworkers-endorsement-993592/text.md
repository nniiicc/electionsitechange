Vukasovich Earns United Steelworkers Endorsement MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich announced today that his campaign has received the endorsement of the United Steelworkers (USW), one of North America’s largest industrial unions.
“I am honored to have the support of the United Steelworkers,” Vukasovich said.
“I have spent my life working alongside people who understand that a good job is about more than a paycheck.
It is about having the security to raise a family, build a future and contribute to your community.” Vukasovich is running to lower costs, raise wages, strengthen unions, support good-paying manufacturing jobs and rebuild American industry.
His campaign has called for trade policies that protect American workers, stronger collective bargaining rights and investments that bring quality jobs back to communities across southern Michigan.
“America should be making things again, and the people who make them should be able to build a good life doing it,” Vukasovich said.
“We cannot rebuild American manufacturing by leaving workers behind.
I will fight for good union jobs, fair trade and an economy that puts working people ahead of corporate lobbyists and wealthy donors.” The endorsement adds the United Steelworkers to a growing coalition of labor, community and progressive organizations supporting Vukasovich’s campaign for Congress.
