Vukasovich: Michigan Families Deserve Lower Costs, Not More Tariffs MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich is calling for action to lower costs for Michigan families following reports that Michigan households face an estimated $5,619 annual tariff burden.
At the same time, 35.4% of children in Niles Township live below the poverty line.
“Working families are already struggling to pay the rent, buy groceries and keep the lights on,” Vukasovich said.
“They cannot afford another hidden tax on everything they buy.
And when more than one in three children in a community like Niles Township live in poverty, Washington should be focused on lowering costs and raising wages, not making life more expensive.” Vukasovich said Congress must strengthen antitrust enforcement, protect good-paying jobs and hold corporations accountable for excessive prices.
He also pointed to the closure of Sturgis Hospital as another example of the growing economic and health care pressures facing rural communities.
“I’m not taking corporate PAC or dark money because my priority is simple: people over special interests,” Vukasovich said.
