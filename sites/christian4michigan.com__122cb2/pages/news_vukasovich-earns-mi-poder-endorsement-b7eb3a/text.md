Vukasovich Earns MI Poder Endorsement MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich announced today that his campaign has received the endorsement of MI Poder, a Michigan organization dedicated to building political power and advancing economic opportunity for Latinx communities across the state.
“I am honored to have the support of MI Poder,” Vukasovich said.
“Our campaign is about making sure working people have a voice in Washington and that government works for the families and communities it represents.” Vukasovich is running to lower costs, raise wages, expand access to health care, strengthen workers’ rights and invest in communities across Michigan’s 5th Congressional District.
His campaign has emphasized putting people ahead of corporate lobbyists and wealthy donors and ensuring that every community has a seat at the table.
“Whether you are a factory worker, a small-business owner, a farmer or a parent trying to make ends meet, you deserve a government that listens and delivers,” Vukasovich said.
“I will fight for an economy that works for working families, stronger communities and a democracy where everyone’s voice matters.
I am grateful to have MI Poder standing with us.” The endorsement adds MI Poder to a growing coalition of labor, community and progressive organizations supporting Vukasovich’s campaign for Congress.
