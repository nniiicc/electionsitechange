Vukasovich Invites CD-5 Residents to Meet the Candidates in Tecumseh TECUMSEH, Mich. — Voters in Michigan’s 5th Congressional District will have an opportunity to hear directly from the candidates for U.S.
House on Sept.
29 at the Tecumseh District Library.
Incumbent Rep.
Tim Walberg and Democratic nominee Christian Vukasovich have been invited to participate in the forum, which will give residents an opportunity to hear the candidates discuss their priorities and answer questions from the community.
The event begins at 5:30 p.m. at the Tecumseh District Library, 215 N.
Ottawa St.
“This district is facing real challenges, and people deserve the opportunity to hear directly from the candidates who want to represent them in Washington,” said Vukasovich.
“I’m looking forward to meeting with residents, listening to their concerns and discussing how we can lower costs, strengthen our communities and make sure working people have a seat at the table.” Vukasovich, an educator, researcher and former union organizer, is challenging Walberg, who currently represents Michigan’s 5th Congressional District in the U.S.
House.
The forum will focus on the issues facing residents across southern Michigan, with attendees encouraged to bring their questions and concerns.
Light refreshments will be served.
WHAT: CD-5 Meet the Candidates Forum WHEN: Tuesday, Sept.
29, 2026, at 5:30 p.m.
WHERE: Tecumseh District Library, 215 N.
Ottawa St., Tecumseh, Mich.
Vukasovich’s campaign has stated that Walberg’s attendance has not yet been confirmed.
