Attacks on a Free Press Are Attacks on Democracy MONROE, Mich. — Democratic congressional candidate Christian Vukasovich is calling on Rep.
Tim Walberg (R-Mich.) to defend the First Amendment following the White House decision to bar CNN, MS NOW and Politico journalists and deactivate their press credentials.
The move has drawn criticism from journalism organizations and raised constitutional concerns over the government restricting press access based on coverage it dislikes.
“Free and independent journalism is not a privilege granted by whoever occupies the White House.
It is essential to a functioning democracy,” Vukasovich said.
“When government officials decide which reporters are allowed to ask questions and which stories can be covered, every American loses access to the information they need to hold their government accountable.” Vukasovich said elected officials have a responsibility to uphold constitutional protections even when they disagree with the press.
“Working families deserve a government that answers their questions, not one that shuts the door when the questions get uncomfortable,” Vukasovich said.
“Tim Walberg should tell the people of the 5th District where he stands.
The Constitution should never take a back seat to party politics.” Vukasovich will discuss protecting constitutional freedoms and restoring government accountability at a candidate forum Tuesday, Sept.
29, at 6 p.m. at the Tecumseh District Library.
Vukasovich has confirmed his attendance.
Walberg has not yet accepted the invitation.
