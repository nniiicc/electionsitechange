I’ve spent the last three years talking with people across this district — Democrats, Republicans, independents, and plenty of people who are tired of politics altogether.
We don’t agree on everything.
But nearly everywhere I go, I hear the same frustrations: healthcare costs too much.
Property taxes keep climbing.
Schools are being asked to do more with less.
Small towns and rural communities feel forgotten.
These problems did not happen by accident.
They are the result of choices made in Madison.
And after years of the same leadership, it is fair to ask:
What has actually gotten better?
I’m not asking you to agree with me on everything.
I’m asking for the chance to earn your vote.
