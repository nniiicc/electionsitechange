The Budget The budget process began in 2025, when state agencies submitted their budget requests to the governor.

The prior biennial budget 2.
The funding needed to maintain current service levels (the “standard budget”) 3.

That bill is introduced simultaneously in the House and the Senate.
At that point, legislators begin debating the bill, offering amendments, and working toward passage in their respective chambers.
Ultimately, the House and the Senate passed their versions of the budget bill, coming within $170 million of each other (out of $11 billion).
The final budget was resolved by the Joint Conference Committee in a matter of hours.
After the last two budgets increased state spending by 24%, the final budget for FY27-28 limited the spending increase to 0.8%.
Local Funding We stabilized the funding of Direct Distributions from the state with a formula to provide predictable funding levels that counties and municipalities can depend on in the future.
The result for the FY27-28 budget is that local governments will receive an increase in funding from the state.
