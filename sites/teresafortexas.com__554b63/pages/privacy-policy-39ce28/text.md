Teresa Johnson - Hernandez Privacy Policy Privacy Policy Privacy Policy Effective Date: July 3, 2026 Welcome to TeresaforTexas.com (“Site”).
We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share your information when you visit our website, sign up to volunteer, contact the campaign, opt in to receive communications, or make donations through third-party platforms.
1.
Information We Collect We collect the following types of information: a.
Information You Provide Directly Volunteer Sign-Up: When you sign up to volunteer, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, address, and any other information you voluntarily provide.
Contact Forms: If you contact us using a website form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, message contents, and any other information you choose to provide.
SMS/Text Message Opt-In: If you choose to provide your mobile phone number and check the SMS consent box, we may collect your mobile number and your consent to receive campaign-related calls and text messages. b.
Automatically Collected Information IP address Browser type and version Pages visited and time spent on the Site Device type 2.
Use of Information We use the information we collect to: Register and communicate with volunteers Respond to inquiries and provide campaign updates Send campaign-related email, phone, and text communications when permitted Provide information about campaign events, volunteer opportunities, donation opportunities, voter outreach, and campaign updates Improve the Site’s functionality and performance Comply with applicable campaign laws and regulations 3.
Donations and Fundraising TeresaforTexas.com does not collect or store your financial information directly.
All donations are processed through CashApp , a third-party donation platform.
When you click on a donation link, you will be redirected to CashApp ’s website.
Please note: CashApp may collect financial and personal information from you to process your donation.
CashApp has its own privacy policy and terms of use, which govern your use of its services.
We encourage you to review CashApp ’s privacy practices before submitting personal information on its platform.
4.
Sharing of Information We do not sell or rent your personal information.
We may share your information only in limited circumstances, including: With campaign staff or trusted service providers who help us operate our campaign and the Site As required by law or to comply with campaign finance regulations To protect the rights, property, or safety of the campaign, its volunteers, supporters, or the public 5.
Mobile Information and SMS Consent No mobile information will be shared by us with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared by us with any third parties.
SMS opt-in information, including your mobile number and consent status, will not be sold, rented, or shared.
If you opt in to receive text messages from Dr.
Teresa Johnson-Hernandez for Texas State Representative, your mobile number and SMS consent will be used only for campaign-related communications.
6.
Email and Text Communications If you provide your email address or phone number, you may receive campaign-related updates and communications.
Text messages may include campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, event reminders, donation reminders, voter outreach, and other campaign-related communications.
By providing your mobile number and opting in, you are consenting to receive calls and texts, including autodialed and automated calls and texts, from Dr.
Teresa Johnson-Hernandez for Texas State Representative.

7.
Data Security We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
However, no method of data transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
8.
Children’s Privacy This Site is not directed to individuals under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
9.
Your Rights and Choices You may: Request access to or correction of your personal information Request to be removed from our contact list Withdraw your consent to receive communications Opt out of text messages by replying STOP To make any of these requests, please contact us at: info@teresafortexas.com 10.
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
