Teresa Johnson Hernandez Announces Campaign for Texas House District 42 LAREDO, Texas — Dr.
Teresa Johnson Hernandez will officially announce her candidacy for the Texas House of Representatives, District 42, on Thursday, November 13, 2025, from 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM on the steps of the Webb County Courthouse, 1000 Houston Street, Laredo, Texas 78040.
Dr.
Hernandez, a respected entrepreneur, community advocate, and lifelong Webb County residents, enters the race with a commitment to improving local schools, expanding healthcare access, supporting small businesses, and strengthening South Texas’s voice in Austin.
“Our community deserves strong representation focused on opportunity, accountability, and the future of South Texas,” said Dr.
Hernandez.
“I’m running to ensure our values are heard — and our families are supported — in every decision made at the State Capitol.” The campaign announcement will feature remarks from Dr.
Hernandez, community supporters, and local leaders, followed by media Q&A; at 11:45 AM.
Members of the public and press are invited to attend.
Event Details: Who: Dr.
Teresa Johnson Hernandez, Candidate for Texas House District 42 What: Campaign Announcement & Remarks When: Thursday, November 13, 2025 – 11:00 AM to 12:00 PM (CT) Where: Steps of the Webb County Courthouse, 1000 Houston St., Laredo, TX 78040 Visuals: Courthouse backdrop, supporters, community leaders Languages: Interviews available in English and Spanish.
Parking: Public lots and street parking available.
About Dr.
Teresa Johnson Hernandez Dr.
Teresa Johnson Hernandez is a lifelong educator and civic leader with a proven record of service in Laredo and Webb County.
Her platform emphasizes education reform, healthcare accessibility, economic growth, and government transparency — values rooted in decades of dedication to her students and community.
