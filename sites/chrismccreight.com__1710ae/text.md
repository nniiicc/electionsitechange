MEET CHRIS
Meet Chris
Chris McCreight is a former small business owner who currently works as a Senior Advisor to Councilmember Kayla Santosuosso (previously Chief of Staff for 8 years to former Councilmember Justin Brannan) and is a Democratic District Leader for Bay Ridge, Dyker Heights, Coney Island, Brighton Beach and Sea Gate.
Prior to working for the City Council, Chris worked as a Communications and Policy Director in the New York State Assembly.
