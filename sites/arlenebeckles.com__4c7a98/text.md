Proven Results.
Unwavering Leadership for District 96
- Building on my work to address healthcare disparities, I'm committed to ensuring that every District 96 resident has access to high-quality, affordable care — not just coverage on paper, but real, meaningful access to the doctors, specialists, and services they need.
I'll continue pushing for Medicaid expansion to close the coverage gaps still affecting thousands in our district, champion community health centers that serve as frontline access points in underserved areas, and advocate for mental health parity so that behavioral health is treated with the same urgency as physical health.
I'll also fight to expand access to preventative care and specialized treatment, because catching health challenges early shouldn't be a privilege reserved for the wealthy.
Every District 96 resident deserves quality healthcare — regardless of their zip code, income, or background.
- Over my tenure in the State House, I've been a consistent champion for tenant protections, rental affordability, and accessible homeownership.
As housing costs continue to squeeze District 96 families — from renters facing steep increases to first-time buyers locked out of the market — I'm doubling down on these commitments.
I'll push for state-level rent stabilization measures that shield renters from sudden, unmanageable hikes, strengthen anti-discrimination enforcement so every resident is treated fairly, expand funding for first-time homebuyer programs, and advance rental assistance initiatives that keep families stably housed.
Because every District 96 resident deserves a safe, affordable place to call home — in the community they've helped build.
- Reproductive freedom is under unprecedented attack — and District 96 deserves a proven, fearless champion who has stood firm and will never back down.
Throughout my tenure, I've been on the front lines defending access to comprehensive reproductive healthcare, and I'm not done fighting.
I'll continue to push back against every restrictive, politically motivated piece of legislation that seeks to strip away our rights and insert government into deeply personal medical decisions.
I'll work to expand reproductive healthcare access across our district, protect and preserve the liberties that belong to every resident, and ensure that healthcare decisions remain exactly where they belong — between patients and their doctors, not politicians.
Our bodies, our rights, our future — and I will defend them all.
- A thriving District 96 economy starts with getting people where they need to go — reliably, affordably, and safely.
Transportation isn't just infrastructure; it's the backbone of economic opportunity.
When residents can move freely and efficiently, they can reach jobs, access services, grow businesses, and invest in our community.
That's why I'll continue championing increased transit funding, expanded and improved bus routes that serve our neighborhoods, and smart infrastructure investments that make walking and biking safe and viable options for everyone.
I'll work with regional and state partners to tackle traffic congestion, attract economic development along transit corridors, and ensure that District 96 families — especially working families — are never left behind because of a lack of mobility.
Greater access to transit means greater access to opportunity, and I'm committed to making both a reality for every corner of our district.
- Strong schools are the foundation of a strong District 96 — and securing their future is one of my highest priorities.
As a consistent and proven advocate for public education funding, I know that what we invest in our classrooms today determines the opportunities our children have tomorrow.
I'll continue fighting for adequate per-pupil funding that reflects the true cost of educating every child, modern school facilities that inspire learning rather than hinder it, and resources that empower our teachers to do their best work.
But securing our schools' future means more than budgets — it means protecting public education from efforts to defund and destabilize it, ensuring our schools remain cornerstones of this community for generations to come.
Our children deserve schools built for 21st-century learning, with robust support for students with special needs, expanded career and technical education pathways that open doors to real careers, and wraparound services that meet the whole child — academically, emotionally, and socially.
When we secure our schools, we secure our future — and I will never stop fighting to do exactly that.
Your trust has been my honor.
Your priorities are my mission.
Let's continue building the Georgia we all deserve—together.
