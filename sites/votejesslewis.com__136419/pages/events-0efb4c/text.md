Robbinsdale Door Knock with Jess Lewis
Join DFLers and Jess Lewis on the doors!
Real conversations are how we build community and win elections.
No experience needed—we’ll provide a brief training and support.
Please bring your water bottle and cell phone.
If possible, please download the Minivan app in advance on your phone - we’ll give you a list of doors to knock right on the app, or let us know in advance if you will need a paper list.
Parents with kids in strollers are absolutely welcome, too!
Every conversation helps move us closer to our goal: getting out the vote.
Come be part of it 💙
