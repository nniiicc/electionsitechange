Become an Election Judge!
Requirements: Be an eligible voter in Minnesota.
Be able to read, write and speak English.
Not be the spouse, parent, child, or sibling of any Election Judge serving in the same ward or of any candidate at that election.
Not be a candidate at that election.
Payroll & Training: All Election Judges are paid by direct deposit.
The direct deposit form will be provided to Election Judges upon confirmation of their training.
Election Judges are paid $13 per hour including paid training.
Training is approximately 2 hours and can be done either online or in person.
Judges must complete training in order to serve.
Dates for training are to be determined and will be communicated via email closer to the elections.
