In 2002, at the age of 74, Coretta Scott King was interviewed by noted photographer Joyce Tenneson.
The photo that accompanies her words shows an older woman full of dignity and strength.
Mrs.
King stated, “When you age, you become wiser in so many ways.
Experience has shown you the potential of the human spirit.
Committing to do what is right, what is just, and what is good will bring you fulfillment.” There are so many ways to do what is right, what is just, and what is good.
Truly, these actions can bring fulfillment.
Mrs.
King was a wise woman, indeed.
I am so grateful to meet so many people who are working so hard to do what is right, what is just, and what is good, and I am trying to do those things, too.
I look forward to seeing you and hearing from you about all that you are doing – and may it bring you fulfillment, as well.
For Everything there is a Season Tomorrow is the last day to register to vote or to change your registration if you have moved.
Check to see if your registration is current by searching your name and date of birth on the Oregon Secretary of State’s MyVote website .
You can update your voter registration online, or you can do so in person at your County Clerk’s office in Salem for Marion County (555 Court Street NE, 2nd Floor, Salem, OR 97301) or in Dallas for Polk County (850 Main Street, #201, Dallas, OR 97338).
Ballots are Being Mailed on October 14th Wednesday, October 14th is the day that ballots are being sent out to registered voters.
If you do not receive yours by the 21st, please check with your County Clerk’s office.
