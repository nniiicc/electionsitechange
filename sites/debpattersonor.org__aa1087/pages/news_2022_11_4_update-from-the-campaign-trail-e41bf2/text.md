Home About Deb Priorities Endorsements Get Involved Español Donate Home About Deb Priorities Endorsements Get Involved Español Donate Update From the Campaign Trail With only one week left on the campaign trail, I wanted to write you a quick email to give you an update on our campaign.
I have been out knocking on doors every day connecting with voters across our district.
Altogether, our team has knocked well over 30,000 doors and has made over 2,500 phone calls, talking with more than 7,750 voters in total.
This is an incredible feat and since we have exceeded our goal of 30,000 doors, we are increasing our goal to hit 40,000 doors in total before Election Day on November 8th.
Know that in this race it is critical to have to have face to face conversations with voters.
Thank you to everyone who has already volunteered and special thanks to Senator Jeff Merkley for stopping by the campaign office and supporting my campaign.
If you are able to volunteer an hour or two in the next 13 days to either phone banking or canvassing our neighbors, it would go a long way.
Thank you!
