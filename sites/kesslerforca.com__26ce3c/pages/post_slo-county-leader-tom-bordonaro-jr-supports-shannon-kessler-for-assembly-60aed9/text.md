SLO County Leader Tom Bordonaro Jr.
Supports Shannon Kessler for Assembly
Shannon Kessler for California Assembly District 30 is honored to announce the endorsement of San Luis Obispo County Assessor Tom Bordonaro Jr.
Experience and accountability
Bordonaro’s public service on the Central Coast reflects the value of local experience and accountable leadership.
Kessler is running to bring practical, community-focused problem solving to Sacramento.
A community-centered campaign
Kessler is a Central Coast business owner and children’s advocate.
Her priorities include housing affordability, public safety, parental involvement in education, small-business growth, and transparent government.
