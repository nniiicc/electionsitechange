top of page Shannon Kessler CA Assembly — District 30 Support the campaign Vote About Priorities Endorsements Support Join the Team Events Endorse Shannon News Blog All Posts Press Releases Campaign Updates Announcements Search Women Leaders Endorse Shannon Kessler for Assembly Adriana Gonzalez Jan 27 2 min read Updated: Feb 24 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Contact: Adriana Gonzalez at info@kesslerforca.com “Kessler for Assembly Gains Momentum into the New Year” Woman Leaders Support Shannon Kessler for Assembly ( Arroyo Grande, CA ) This week Shannon Kessler for the 30 th Assembly District received the endorsements of two of the most respected Woman Elected officials from San Luis Obispo County.
Former San Luis Obispo County Supervisors Lynn Compton and Debbie Arnold announced their support for Shannon Kessler for Assembly.
“Shannon has our full support and endorsement.
We could not be more excited to see a more passionate and caring candidate in Shannon.
Shannon cares about her community and our children, she will fight for young woman’s rights, our environment and affordable housing.
She has the experience to hit the ground running,” said Lynn Compton, Former San Luis Obispo County Supervisor.
“When I met Shannon; I know she had our communities’ best interest at heart and would make an excellent State Assemblywoman.
Shannon has a big heart and vision to bring common sense policies to Sacramento.
I could not be prouder to endorse and support Shannon for Assembly,” said Debbie Arnold, Former San Luis Obispo County Supervisor.
Shannon Kessler is a Central Coast business owner and community volunteer running to bring common sense and fairness to Sacramento.
She is the founder of Save Girls’ Sports Central Coast, where she has become a leading voice protecting fairness and safety for girls across California.
Shannon also founded Culture Impact to empower citizens to engage locally and prepare them for civic volunteerism.
She serves on the board of the San Luis Obispo County Republican Party, educated at Pepperdine School of Public Policy, and co-owns a local family construction company and real estate company.
A lifelong livestock supporter and former agriculture leader, Shannon loves to surf and ride horses in her spare time.
Shannon has been married for 37 years.
They have 2 children and are expecting their first grandchild in April this year.
They and have been living in their Arroyo Grande area for 36 years.
