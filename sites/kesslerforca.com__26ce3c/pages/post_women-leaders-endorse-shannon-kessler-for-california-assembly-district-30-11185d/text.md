Women Leaders Endorse Shannon Kessler for California Assembly District 30
Former San Luis Obispo County Supervisors Lynn Compton and Debbie Arnold have announced their support for Shannon Kessler for California Assembly District 30.
Leadership grounded in community
The endorsements recognize Kessler’s community involvement, experience in business and public service, and commitment to families across the Central Coast.
Priorities for District 30
Kessler’s platform addresses affordable housing, public safety, education, women’s sports, environmental stewardship, and accountable government.
