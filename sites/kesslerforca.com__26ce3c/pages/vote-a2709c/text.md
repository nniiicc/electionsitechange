General Election: Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
Polls are open 7 a.m. to 8 p.m.
Key dates from the California Secretary of State:
October 5: County elections officials begin mailing ballots on or before this date.
Secure ballot drop boxes open by this date.
October 19: Last day to register to vote.
After this deadline, same-day voter registration is available at your county elections office or a voting location, up to and including Election Day.
October 24: Vote centers open for early in-person voting in Voter's Choice Act counties, which include Santa Cruz County.
October 31: In-person early voting locations are open throughout California.
November 3: Election Day.
Last day to vote in person or return your ballot by 8 p.m.
Ballots returned by mail must be postmarked on or before Election Day.
Hours and locations vary by county.
