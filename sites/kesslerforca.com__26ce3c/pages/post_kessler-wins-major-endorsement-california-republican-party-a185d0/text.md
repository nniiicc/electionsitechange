“Kessler Wins Major Endorsement” California Republican Party
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
Contact: Adriana Gonzalez at info@kesslerforca.com
(Arroyo Grande, CA) — Shannon Kessler, candidate for California’s 30th Assembly District, has received the endorsement of the California Republican Party.
“The California Republican Party is proud to endorse Shannon Kessler for State Assembly.
Shannon’s common-sense solutions and vision for California will be a refreshing addition to Sacramento.
We need more leaders like Shannon who will fight for public safety, fewer regulations, and real results in our education system,” said Corrin Rankin, Chairwoman of the California Republican Party.
“I am honored to receive the official endorsement of the California Republican Party.
I am committed to bringing common-sense solutions to Sacramento and delivering real results for California families,” said Shannon Kessler, candidate for the 30th Assembly District.
Shannon Kessler is a Central Coast small business owner and advocate committed to delivering practical solutions and accountable leadership in Sacramento.
Kessler’s campaign centers on empowering parents, expanding housing opportunities, and cutting red tape for small businesses across the Central Coast.
She is committed to advancing practical solutions that protect individual rights and strengthen local communities.
