Dr.
Karen McCormick is a veterinarian, previous small business owner of a veterinary hospital, a mom and a strong Democrat.
She is passionate about our state and working for policies that strengthen our community.
In the General Assembly she advocates for hardworking families: for affordability of health care, housing and childcare, for education funding, for immigrants, LGBTQ and reproductive rights, for economic opportunity for all, for climate and energy resiliency and for the future of our natural resources.
Karen is currently the State Representative for House District 11 which encompasses most of Longmont (see map) Being term-limited she is running for re-election to her last term at the General Assembly in 2026 Rep.
