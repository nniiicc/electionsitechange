His background equips him to thoughtfully interpret and uphold the law at the state’s highest judicial level.
Justice Hawkins deeply respects the trust Ohioans have placed in him.
He understands the responsibility that accompanies this overwhelming mandate and is honored by the opportunity to serve.

MEET JUSTICE HAWKINS
A Strong Conservative for Ohio's Supreme Court
Justice Dan Hawkins was born and raised in Columbus, Ohio.
He is a proud graduate of St.
Francis DeSales High School and earned his Bachelor of Science in Criminal Justice from Bowling Green State University.
After completing his undergraduate studies, Justice Hawkins returned to his hometown to pursue his legal education at The Ohio State University, where he earned his law degree.
Upon passing the bar, Justice Hawkins began his legal career as an Assistant Prosecuting Attorney with the Franklin County Prosecutor’s Office.
Over 13 years, he distinguished himself as a trial prosecutor, including a decade as Director of the office’s highly regarded Special Victims Unit.
This unit focuses on prosecuting violent crimes against women and children, such as murders, sexual assaults, child abuse, human trafficking, and cases involving internet predators.
Justice Hawkins has an impressive record, having taken more than 100 cases to trial.
In July 2013, Justice Hawkins was appointed to the Environmental Division of the Franklin County Municipal Court to fill a vacancy.
He was later elected to complete the term.
During his time on the Environmental Court, he tackled issues related to nuisance properties throughout Franklin County, including abandoned buildings and drug houses that attracted criminal activity.
Justice Hawkins introduced innovative solutions, such as a neighborhood clean-up program that engaged individuals with court-ordered community service hours to help beautify the community.
In November 2018, Justice Hawkins was elected to the Franklin County Court of Common Pleas.
During his time there, he earned high ratings from the Columbus Bar Association and recognition from law enforcement agencies and community organizations alike.
Dan Hawkins' career in the courtroom led him to run for the Ohio Supreme Court in 2024.
Justice Hawkins achieved an historic victory in that election winning by a margin of over 540,000 votes, earning the trust of nearly three million voters to serve all Ohioans.
Outside the courtroom, Justice Hawkins and his wife, Amy, have been married since 2006.
They reside on the north side of Columbus with their three children.
Amy works with her family's business in Plain City, Ohio.
The Hawkins family are long-time members of St.
Paul the Apostle Catholic Church in Westerville, Ohio, where they remain active in their faith community.
