That’s what I’ve been trying to do since being elected to serve in the State Senate in 2018.
I’m proud of the work we’ve done—and the legislation we’ve passed—to fight for working families across the Keystone State.
But there’s still work to be done, and I’m not finished working for the people of the 38th Senatorial District.
Read more about Lindsey Priorities A Healthy, Safe, and Prosperous Community Protecting Working Families Having grown up in a union household, I am a lifelong advocate for working families.
We must invest in roads and bridges, require companies to pay family-sustaining wages, and protect workers’ ability to collectively bargain.
Education As Minority Chair of the State Senate Education Committee, I’ve fought to invest in our schools so students and educators have the resources they need to succeed.
Students are our region’s economic, civic, and artistic future—it is critical that we support them to make that future successful.
Healthcare Proactively and equitably combatting the impacts and further spread of the COVID-19 pandemic remains a priority.
Healthcare is a human right and all Pennsylvanians should have high-quality and affordable access.
Taxes I support a Fair Share Tax Plan that would close the structural deficit and provide revenues necessary to meet the needs of the state, while sparing low- and middle-income residents from shouldering the tax burden.
Women's Rights Women's rights are human rights.
We must continue to advocate for a more equitable workforce with equal pay for equal work, and fight back against any attempt to restrict women’s right to choose.
