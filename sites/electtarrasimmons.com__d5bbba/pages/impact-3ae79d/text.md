Tarra brings the experiences, concerns, and ideas of the people she represents with her to Olympia.
Her work has helped secure investments, advance legislation, and address the everyday challenges facing families and communities across the 23rd Legislative District.
From transportation and education to healthcare, small businesses, and community safety, Tarra has worked across issues to make government work better for the people it serves.
Contact Tarra Affordability & Housing Millionaires Tax | SB 6346 · 2026 Tarra fought through nearly 25 hours of debate to help pass the Millionaires Tax.
The measure affects only the highest earners while creating funding for programs that benefit working families, including free school meals and childcare.
Working Families Tax Credit | 2026 Supported an expansion of the Working Families Tax Credit reaching 460,000 more Washingtonians, putting money directly back into the hands of low- and middle-income families.
Small Business Relief | 2026 Supported B&O tax relief that means 70% of Washington businesses will pay zero B&O tax.
Non-Compete Agreements | ESHB 1155 · 2026 Supported legislation banning non-compete agreements, giving workers greater freedom to pursue better opportunities in their fields.
Affordable Housing & Reentry | 2SHB 1818 · 2022 Authored legislation supporting reentry and rehabilitation, recognizing stable housing as a foundation for successful reentry.
Social Housing Authorities | EHB 1687 · 2026 Supported legislation creating social housing authorities.
Covenant Homeownership Program | 2023 & 2025 Supported a pathway to homeownership for families historically shut out by redlining and discriminatory lending.

Housing & Shelter Investments Tarra secured state funding for projects across Kitsap County.
Her work helped bring affordable housing, shelter, medical respite, and healthcare resources to communities throughout the district, including $1 million for Saint Vincent de Paul Housing & Shelter and $1.5 million for Peninsula Community Health Services.

Healthcare Investments in Kitsap County Tarra helped secure millions in state funding for local healthcare.
Investments include $18.48 million for a new substance use disorder clinic in Poulsbo, $4.5 million for the Suquamish On-Reservation Health Service Center, and funding for Peninsula Community Health Services and Kitsap Mental Health Services.
