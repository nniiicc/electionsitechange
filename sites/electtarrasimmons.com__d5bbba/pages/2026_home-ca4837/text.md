Millionaires Tax | SB 6346 · 2026
Tarra fought through nearly 25 hours of debate to help pass the Millionaires Tax.
The measure affects only the highest earners while creating funding for programs that benefit working families, including free school meals and childcare.

Housing & Shelter Investments
Tarra secured state funding for projects across Kitsap County.
