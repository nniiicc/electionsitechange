Privacy Policy

By using our website, you agree to the terms described below.
INFORMATION WE COLLECT
We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address, when you sign up, contact us, volunteer, or contribute.
We also collect technical information automatically, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and general usage data, through cookies and similar technologies.
If you contribute online, we collect the payment details needed to process your donation; we do not store your credit card information under any circumstances.

Contact and donation forms on this site are hosted through NGP VAN, a third-party campaign platform.

HOW WE SHARE INFORMATION
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.
Information collected for text message opt-ins is never shared with third parties for marketing.
We may share information with service providers who help us operate the campaign, or when required by law.
DATA SECURITY
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures, including secure servers and restricted access, to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
YOUR CHOICES
You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP to any text you receive from us.
You can unsubscribe from emails using the link at the bottom of any email we send.
You can also contact us to ask what information we have about you or to request that it be corrected or deleted.
