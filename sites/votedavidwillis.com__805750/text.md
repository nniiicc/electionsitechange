Working for Here.
David and his wife Shannon chose Union County more than two decades ago for the same reasons so many families do — great schools, low taxes, and wonderful people.
They're raising their three children in our public schools , worshiping at Calvary Church, and building a small business that serves local families every day.
After graduating from Appalachian State and spending over 15 years in manufacturing, banking, and finance, David built a successful preschool business — experience he now puts to work protecting taxpayers and growing opportunity for every family in North Carolina.

Laws.
Plenty of politicians file bills.
David Willis passes them.
Eight bills he authored as a primary sponsor have been signed into North Carolina law — for veterans, families, students, and taxpayers.
SL 2021-12 Wounded Heroes Day Authored the law creating an official state day honoring North Carolina's combat-wounded veterans.
2021 • Sole Primary Sponsor SL 2021-144 Foster Parents' Bill of Rights Landmark protections and a clear voice for the foster families who open their homes to vulnerable children.
2021 SL 2022-5 Tax Relief for Farm & Fishing Families Extended filing deadlines so farmers and fishermen aren't penalized by circumstances beyond their control.
2022 SL 2023-80 Mental Health Licensure Fairness Fair practice standards that help more licensed counselors serve North Carolina communities.
2023 SL 2023-107 Charter School Omnibus Expanded flexibility and accountability to grow high-quality public school options for families.
2023 SL 2023-110 Charter School Review Board Created an independent board to approve quality charter schools — enacted over the Governor's veto.
2023 SL 2023-125 Principal Licensure Reform Smarter licensure pathways to put strong, proven leaders at the front of North Carolina schools.
2023 SL 2025-17 UNC Tuition Discounts Lower-cost pathways to a UNC System degree — making college more affordable for NC students.
