I am not a career politician, but I do believe our community deserves a local candidate who represents their values in South Dakota.
Like many of us in District 10, I’ve become increasingly concerned that state governance doesn’t truly represent the voters in our State.
My mission is to change this by representing the best interest of the people of District 10 with integrity, compassion and hard work.
The voters of District 10 elected me in 2022 and if re-elected in 2026 I will continue to respect the great responsibility entrusted to me and better the lives of all South Dakotans.
