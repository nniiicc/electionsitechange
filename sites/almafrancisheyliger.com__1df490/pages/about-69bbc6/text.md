SENATOR ALMA FRANCIS HEYLIGER Background A Life of Service to the Virgin Islands Senator Alma Francis Heyliger was born on October 12, 1973, in St.
Thomas, United States Virgin Islands.
A proud product of the Virgin Islands public school system, she graduated from Charlotte Amalie High School and has dedicated her adult life to the upliftment of her community.
Serving in her third term in the Legislature of the Virgin Islands, Senator Francis Heyliger represents the 36th Legislature's commitment to accountability, transparency, and genuine service.
She has served in the 34th, 35th, and 36th Legislatures, building a record of legislative achievement that spans water safety, economic empowerment, government accountability, and cultural preservation.
Beyond her legislative work, Senator Francis Heyliger is a multifaceted creative force.
She is the owner of Radio One AM 1000, making her one of only 18 African American women to own a radio station in the United States.
She is also an author, having published her debut novel Fallen Petals of the Orion in 2004, as well as a songwriter, music producer, and real estate developer.
Senator Francis Heyliger believes deeply that through open discussion and genuine engagement, solutions can always be found.
Her legislative philosophy centers on the idea that every Virgin Islander deserves access to clean water, economic opportunity, and a government that is accountable to the people it serves.
In the 36th Legislature, Senator Francis Heyliger serves on the Rules and Judiciary Committee, the Economic Development, Agriculture and Planning Committee, the Health, Hospitals and Human Services Committee, the Government Operations, Consumer Protection and Veterans Affairs Committee, the Culture, Youth, Aging, Sports and Parks Committee, and the Committee of the Whole.
