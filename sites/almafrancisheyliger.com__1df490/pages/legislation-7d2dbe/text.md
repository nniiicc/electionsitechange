Official Report 100-Day Legislative Report Senator Francis Heyliger's comprehensive report on her legislative priorities and accomplishments in the first 100 days of the 36th Legislature.
View on Legislature Site Legislation Passed and Championed What Senator Francis Heyliger Has Delivered Public Health Passed 2023 Bills 35-0200 & 35-0201 Water Safety and Ratepayer Relief for St.
Croix Provided six months of water bill relief to St.
Croix ratepayers and mandated annual lead and copper testing for all water systems territory-wide, protecting public health across the Virgin Islands.
Economic Empowerment Passed 2022 Unclaimed Property Database Act Territory's First Online Unclaimed Property Database Created usviclaims.unclaimedproperty.com, the first online unclaimed property database in the USVI, giving Virgin Islanders access to over $50 million in government-held assets.
Education & Culture Passed 2021 Virgin Islands History Education Act Mandatory VI History in Schools Co-sponsored legislation mandating the teaching of Virgin Islands history in public schools, ensuring future generations understand and celebrate their rich cultural heritage.
Government Accountability Passed 2022 Government Liability Reform Raised Government Liability Cap Fought to raise the government liability cap from $25,000 to $150,000, providing meaningful recourse for Virgin Islanders harmed by government negligence.
Government Accountability Passed 2023 Separation of Powers Reform Legislative Independence Act Co-sponsored legislation to strengthen the separation of powers between the Legislature and the Executive Branch, protecting democratic governance in the Virgin Islands.
Government Accountability Championed 2024 Attorney General Election Act Elected Attorney General Championed legislation to make the Attorney General an elected position, ensuring the territory's top law enforcement officer is directly accountable to the people rather than appointed by the Governor.
Legislative Achievement Passed 2014 Freshman Sole Sponsor Legislation Unanimous Freshman Legislation As a freshman senator, advanced legislation as the sole sponsor that passed unanimously, demonstrating her ability to build consensus and deliver results from her very first term.
Disaster Recovery Passed 2018 Community Relief Package Post-Hurricane Community Recovery Sponsored and supported multiple pieces of legislation to aid community recovery following the devastating 2017 hurricane season, helping residents rebuild their lives.
Energy & Infrastructure Passed 2024 WAPA Board Restructuring Act Restructured the WAPA Governing Board Co-sponsored landmark legislation to restructure the Virgin Islands Water and Power Authority (WAPA) governing board, bringing in stronger expertise and accountability.
The VI Supreme Court upheld the Legislature's authority to restructure the board in October 2024, rejecting Governor Bryan's constitutional challenge.
Energy & Infrastructure Active 2026 WAPA Oversight & Accountability WAPA Consumer Protection and Rate Transparency Actively leading legislative oversight of WAPA's chronic service interruptions and aging infrastructure.
Convened the Committee of the Whole in April 2026 to compel WAPA executives to testify on outage causes, reliability plans, and customer communication failures — holding the utility publicly accountable.
Stood Against What Senator Francis Heyliger Has Opposed Fiscal Responsibility Unaccountable Government Spending Consistently opposed legislation that lacked transparency or accountability measures, demanding that taxpayer dollars be spent responsibly and with full public disclosure.
Environment Weakening of Environmental Protections Stood against proposals that would have reduced environmental safeguards for the Virgin Islands' natural resources, including its pristine waters and coral reefs.
Education Cuts to Public Education Funding Fought against budget proposals that would have reduced funding for public schools and educational programs, defending the right of every Virgin Islands child to a quality education.
Government Accountability Consolidation of Executive Power Opposed measures that would have concentrated excessive power in the Executive Branch at the expense of legislative oversight and the separation of powers.
Energy & Infrastructure WAPA's Lack of Accountability to Ratepayers Sent a formal letter to WAPA calling their actions 'gross negligence' and urging the utility to do right by customers.
Consistently challenged WAPA leadership on rate increases, service failures, and the need for transparent communication with the public they serve.
