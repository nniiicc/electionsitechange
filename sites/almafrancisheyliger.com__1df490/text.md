View Full Legislative Record About the Senator Born and Raised on St.
Thomas Born on October 12, 1973, Senator Alma Francis Heyliger has dedicated her life to public service, community upliftment, and fighting for the rights of every Virgin Islander.
A proud Charlotte Amalie High School alumna, she has served three consecutive terms in the Legislature.
Beyond the Legislature, she is the owner of Radio One AM 1000, an author, songwriter, producer, and real estate developer who believes that through discussion, solutions can be found.
