Issues
LOWER THE COST OF LIVING
Cut property taxes, lower insurance premiums, and fight for better-paying jobs and affordable housing for working families.
DATA CENTER REFORM
End tax incentives for data centers, make them pay for their own water and electrical costs, and allow local communities to decide how close they can be built to residential communities.
STRONG SCHOOLS & GOOD JOBS
Support funding needed to improve education, increase career training and apprenticeship programs so students are prepared for the work force.
KEEPING TEXAS FAMILIES SAFE
Keep violent, repeat criminals behind bars, and give law enforcement the equipment and training they need to stop drugs and human trafficking.
ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT
Fight for low taxes, less regulations, and the predictable legal environment that makes Texas the envy of the nation and leading job creator in America.
PROTECT WOMEN'S SPORTS & PRIVATE SPACES
Keep men out of women’s sports, bathrooms and locker rooms because the privacy, safety and dignity of women and girls is not negotiable.
SECOND AMENDMENT
Protect Texan’s right to keep and bear arms.
SANCTITY OF LIFE
Be a voice for the voiceless protecting innocent life and remove barriers so the private sector can provide resources to women facing crisis pregnancies.
