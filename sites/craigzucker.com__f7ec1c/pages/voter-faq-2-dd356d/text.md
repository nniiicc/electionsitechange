To vote in the 2026 primary election, you must be registered to vote in Maryland.
Up until Tuesday, October 13, 2026, voters may:
- Register to vote
- Update their name or address
- Change their political party affiliation
If you miss the October 13 deadline, you may still register or update your registration during early voting or on Election Day.
Learn more about voter registration and check your registration!
Voters may cast their ballot in one of three ways: in-person on election day, in-person during early voting period, and by mail.
