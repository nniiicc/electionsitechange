Accomplishments Matt Lesser fights for his district Matt has brought back over $400 million to our district in the past two years.
Matt secured funding for Macdonough School renovation at the highest reimbursement rate in state history - winning $42 million - to meet young learners' needs and spare local taxpayers from additional property tax (2025) Matt has championed historic education funding increases—$4 million more per year into our schools, for a total of $109 million coming back to Middletown schools over the next two years.
Matt has secured funding in the past two years Middletown projects and organizations including MacDonough school transportation, Middlesex Hospital's ambulance service, the Middlesex Chamber of Commerce rehabilitation, Middletown's emergency services dock, the YMCA overhaul, Oddfellows Playhouse, the GIFTS Girls reading program run through the Middlesex NAACP, upgrades to the Middletown Senior Center, and upgrades to the Community Health Center.
In the midst of federal cuts to nutrition programs, Matt is ensuring seniors stay healthy through securing funding for Middletown and Cromwell Meals on Wheels and meals at the senior center. ​ To ensure that Connecticut residents keep food on their tables, Matt is fighting for our state to offset federal cuts to SNAP.
