Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 3-1-2026
The campaign of Indiana Thompson for the 67th District of Wisconsin (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how your information is collected and used.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website.
Information We Collect
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us, including:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address (if provided)
Any information submitted through contact forms or donation forms
We may also collect non-personal information automatically, such as:
- Browser type
- Device type
- Pages visited
- Time spent on the website
How We Use Your Information
We use the information we collect to:
- Respond to inquiries or requests
- Provide campaign updates and information
- Process donations (via third-party platforms such as ActBlue)
- Communicate with supporters and volunteers
- Improve our website and outreach efforts
Email Communication
By providing your email address, you consent to receive emails from Indiana Thompson for Wisconsin, including:
- Campaign updates
- Event announcements
- Volunteer opportunities
- Fundraising communications
You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in our emails.
Text Message (SMS) & Phone Communication
By providing your phone number, you consent to receive calls and text messages from Indiana Thompson for Wisconsin, which may include:
- Campaign updates
- Event reminders
- Volunteer opportunities
- Donation requests
Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier.
You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying:
STOP to unsubscribe
HELP for assistance
You may also contact us directly to be removed from our communication lists.
Sharing of Information
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share your information with trusted third parties only as necessary to operate the campaign, including:
- Donation processing platforms (e.g., ActBlue)
- Email and communication service providers
- Website hosting and analytics providers
These third parties are required to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide.
Cookies and Tracking Technologies
Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to:
- Improve user experience
- Analyze website traffic
- Understand how visitors interact with the site
You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings.
Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
However, no online system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Third-Party Links
Our website may contain links to third-party websites (such as donation platforms).
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites and encourage you to review their policies.
Your Rights
You may request to:
- Access the personal information we have about you
- Correct or update your information
- Request removal from our communication lists
To do so, please contact us using the information below.
