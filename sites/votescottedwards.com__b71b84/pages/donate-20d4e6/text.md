Donation FAQs
If you have questions about giving, these answers cover the basics.
For anything else, please reach out through the contact form below.
Is my contribution secure?
Yes.
We use reputable payment processing and follow standard security practices to protect your information.
Are contributions refundable?
Contributions are generally not refundable.
If you believe an error occurred, please contact the campaign promptly so we can review.
Can I donate by check?
Yes.
If you prefer to donate by check, please mail your contribution to: P.O.
Box 4475, Rollingbay, WA 98061.
Will I receive a receipt?
Yes.
You will receive a confirmation for your records after your contribution is processed.
Are there contribution limits or rules?
Contributions are capped at $4,800 per individual or $9,600 for a married couple for the 2026 election cycle.
Please note that these contributions are not tax-deductible.
Can I donate in honor of someone?
Yes.
Please include a note in the comment section with your contribution or contact us so we can record your dedication appropriately.
