Sarah Sanders and every Arkansas statewide official → Menu Home About Tom Issues News Volunteer Contact Donate Press Release Maggie’s List Endorses Senator Cotton for His Leadership in Congress, Military Service, and Support of Republican Women October 15, 2026 Contact: James@TomCotton.com Today, Maggie’s List, which helps elect conservative women across the country, has endorsed Senator Tom Cotton for re-election to the U.S.
Senate.
In part, Maggie’s List wrote: “Maggie’s List is proud to count U.S.
Sen.
Tom Cotton of Arkansas among our Congressional Partners supporting the mission to elect more conservative women to federal office.
Before entering Congress, Sen.
Cotton served nearly five years on active duty in the U.S.
Army, including combat tours in Iraq and Afghanistan.
He later represented Arkansas in the U.S.
House before being elected to the Senate.
Today, Sen.
Cotton serves as Chairman of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence and as a member of the Armed Services, Energy and Natural Resources, and Joint Economic Committees.
He also serves as Chairman of the Senate Republican Conference.
Maggie’s List appreciates Sen.
Cotton’s partnership and his support for increasing the number of conservative women serving in federal office.” “I’m grateful for the support of Maggie’s List, and I’m proud to support their work electing conservative women.
Our country needs strong, conservative leaders to fight the radical left, secure our border, and keep America strong.
I look forward to continuing our partnership to elect more of them,” said Senator Tom Cotton.
