Press Release
Senator Cotton has outraised his Democratic opponent each quarter and now has more than ten times as much cash on hand as his opponent.
“The Cotton campaign is thrilled to announce more than $10 million cash on hand across accounts with $827,000 raised this quarter,” said Campaign Manager Breanne Davis.
“Senator Cotton has received contributions from all 75 counties and tens of thousands of Arkansans and is in a strong position to win and keep fighting for Arkansas’s conservative values.”
