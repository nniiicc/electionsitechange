Cotton Accepts Salem Media Invitation to Debate
LITTLE ROCK, Ark. — U.S.
Sen.
Tom Cotton has accepted an invitation from Salem Media and an Arkansas television network to debate his Democratic opponent in Arkansas.
“Senator Cotton looks forward to showing Arkansans the clear choice in this election between a conservative Republican and a liberal Democrat,” said Cotton campaign spokesman James Arnold.
“Senator Cotton has secured the border, cut taxes for Arkansas families, fought crime and drug trafficking, and brought billions in defense investments to Arkansas.
Liberal Democrat Hallie Shoffner wants to defund ICE, impose an energy tax on Arkansas families, and has a long record of donating to Kamala Harris and socialist Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez.
We look forward to the debate.”
