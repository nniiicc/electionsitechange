Immigration & Our Sovereignty
America is a nation of laws, and a country without a border is not a country.
Senator Tom Cotton has been one of the strongest voices for securing the border and restoring American sovereignty.
He backed President Trump’s border enforcement policies, fought to end catch-and-release, and pushed to finish the wall.
He voted to pass the Laken Riley Act to deport criminal illegal aliens.
He has consistently voted to deport criminal illegal aliens and crack down on drug and human trafficking that harms Arkansas communities.
Cotton authored the RAISE Act to fix our broken legal immigration system by ending chain migration and putting American workers first.
His position is simple: the right amount of illegal immigration is zero, and Arkansas families should be protected from illegal immigration.
