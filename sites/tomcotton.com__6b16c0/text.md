After 9/11, Tom joined the Army as an Infantry Officer and served combat tours in Iraq and Afghanistan, with the 101st Airborne and at Arlington National Cemetery.
His decorations include the Bronze Star Medal, Combat Infantry Badge, and Ranger Tab.
