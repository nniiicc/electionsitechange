SAMUEL’S PRIORITIES
Samuel Vilchez Santiago is running to put people over special interests and expand opportunities for the community that raised him.
His priorities are shaped by lived experience, growing up right here, seeing what works, and fighting to make sure every family has a real chance to get ahead.
Here’s what he’s fighting for:
- Housing, health care, and insurance costs are spiraling out of control.
Samuel will work to:
- Expand Medicaid and lower prescription drug prices
- Put a stop to unaccountable data centers that increase our cost of living
- Lower property insurance costs by holding insurers accountable and expanding My Safe Florida Home grants
- Fully fund housing affordability programs and restore local control to cities and counties
- Samuel is a proud graduate of Title I schools in our district.
Samuel will work to:
- Fully fund public education
- Pay teachers fairly and support them in the classroom
- Expand access to dual enrollment, career and technical programs
- Improve oversight of voucher programs
- Stop the politicization of our public colleges and universities
- East Orlando is home to vibrant immigrant communities who deserve safety, dignity and opportunity, not fear.
Samuel will work to:
- Block state cooperation with mass deportation efforts
- Restore in-state tuition for Dreamers and TPS holders
- Expand access to professional licenses
- Recognize immigrants as essential to Florida’s economy
- Repeal anti-immigrant laws like SB1718 and the TRUMP Act
- After escaping authoritarianism as a child and later organizing campaigns across Florida to defend voting rights, Samuel will work to:
- Expand early voting and vote-by-mail access
- Fight voter suppression and election subversion
- Protect local election administrators from partisan attacks
- Oppose illegal, partisan gerrymandering
- Advance civil rights
