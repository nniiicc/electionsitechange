Why I’m Running
East Orlando Deserves a Champion Who’s Been in the Fight
Samuel Vilchez Santiago grew up in East Orlando, the son of political refugees who came to Florida seeking safety and opportunity.
He struggled early on by relying on Medicaid, food stamps, and the support of public schools but this community gave him a shot.
After graduating as Valedictorian at Colonial High School, he went on to earn honors degrees from Princeton University and the University of Oxford.
Today, Samuel is a nonprofit leader, immigrant-rights advocate, and Chair of the Orange County Democratic Party.
He's running to represent District 43 because he knows what it's like to grow up here, has spent his life fighting for the people who call it home, and wants every family in our community to have opportunities he has had to get ahead
