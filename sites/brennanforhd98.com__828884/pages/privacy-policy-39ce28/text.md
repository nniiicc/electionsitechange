Home About Policy Priorities Donate/Support Events & Blog Cate Brennan for Texas House District 98 Privacy Policy Privacy Policy Cate Brennan for Texas House District 98 Effective Date: 12-15-2025 Cate Brennan for Texas House District 98 (“the Campaign,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal information.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share information obtained through our website, communications, and campaign activities.
Information We Collect We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including but not limited to: Name Email address Mailing address Phone number Donation information (processed securely by third-party payment processors) Volunteer interests and availability We may also collect limited, non-personal information automatically when you visit our website, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, to help us improve our website and communications.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Communicate with you about the campaign, events, and updates Process donations and comply with campaign finance and reporting laws Coordinate volunteer activities and grassroots outreach Respond to inquiries and requests Improve website functionality and user experience Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements Your information is used solely for legitimate campaign purposes.
Donations and Payment Processing All online donations are processed through secure, third-party payment processors.
The Campaign does not store or have access to your full financial information, such as credit card numbers or bank account details.
Donation information may be disclosed as required by Texas Ethics Commission and other applicable campaign finance laws.
Sharing of Information The Campaign does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share information only: With trusted vendors or service providers who assist with campaign operations (such as email platforms or donation processors), under strict confidentiality agreements When required by law, regulation, or court order To comply with campaign finance, election, or ethics reporting requirements Communications and Opt-Ou t By providing your contact information, you may receive emails, text messages, or other communications from the Campaign.
You may opt out of email communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link included in our emails.
To opt out of text messages, follow the instructions provided in the message.
Data Security We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
While no system can be guaranteed to be 100% secure, we strive to use industry-standard practices to safeguard your data.
Children’s Privacy This website is not intended for children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
External Links Our website may contain links to external websites not operated by the Campaign.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites.
We encourage you to review their privacy policies.
C hanges to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in law, technology, or campaign practices.
Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
