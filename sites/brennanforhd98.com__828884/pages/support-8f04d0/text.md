Donate: Invest in a Better Texas
Running a competitive campaign in HD 98 requires financial resources, and your contribution helps us purchase yard signs, door hangers, text messages and even gas money.
Cate Brennan is committed to a people-powered campaign that puts families first—not corporate special interests.
Your donation helps:
- Protect public schools from privatization and demand accountability
- Defend personal freedoms and civil rights
- Fight for affordable healthcare, housing, and childcare
- Promote transparency, fair elections, and ethical government
Every contribution matters, whether it’s $10 or $250.
Together, we can build a campaign that reflects our true Texas values of respect, diversity and personal freedoms.
Volunteer: Be Part of the Movement
Volunteers are the heart of this campaign.
Whether you have a few hours a month or want to be deeply involved, there is a place for you on Team Cate.
Volunteer opportunities include:
- Talking with voters at the door or by phone
- Helping at community events and block walks
- Assisting with data entry, outreach, and logistics
- Hosting or helping organize meet-and-greet events
- Supporting voter registration and turnout efforts
No prior political experience is needed—just a commitment to positive change and respect for our community.
Training and support are provided every step of the way.
