Donate KELLY HOUGH SOUTH CAROLINA STATE REPRESENTATIVE DISTRICT 114 Meet Kelly !
As a proud South Carolina native, I’ve experienced firsthand the long-standing challenges our state continues to face.
I’ve lived the education gaps, the financial pressures, the healthcare barriers, and the ongoing conversations around women’s rights and civil liberties.
Challenges that have shaped our communities for generations.
I’m a nurse, a mother, a wife, a daughter, and yes, a proud dog mom.
I understand what families in South Carolina and District 114 are balancing every single day.
As an alumna of Midlands Technical College, Trident Technical College, the College of Charleston, and the University of South Carolina, my education background spans healthcare, business, psychology, gender studies, communications, film, and real-world public service and community experience.
I’m ready to bring that perspective to the State House.
I was called to nursing because service has always been at the center of who I am.
For years, I have served South Carolina families at their most vulnerable moments.
During COVID, I stepped into leadership roles when our healthcare system was stretched beyond capacity.
Supporting patients, guiding teams, and helping families navigate fear and uncertainty.
Public service isn’t new to me.
I've also served as a hospice nurse.
Mentoring families through their most vulnerable and scared moments, reinforcing my commitment to compassionate, patient-centered and as a South Carolina public servant.
It’s time for action, responsible growth, strengthening our economy, investing in infrastructure, protecting healthcare access, supporting education, and standing up for women and civil liberties.
Our state deserves leadership shaped by experience, grounded in community, and driven by action.
I want to improve the state of South Carolina by "Leading change.
Together".
Leading Change.
