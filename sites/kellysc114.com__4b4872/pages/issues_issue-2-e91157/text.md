Investment in Infrastructure
Smart Infrastructure That Supports Growth
• Targeted funding to relieve high-traffic issues
• Coordinated road planning before approving major developments
• Strategic widening and intersection redesign.
• Investment in alternative routes, bike access, and walkability.
• Exploring responsible mass transit options for high-growth areas
Traffic along our area steals hours from hardworking citizens who simply want to get home.
The current infrastructure wasn’t built for this pace of growth, and now families are paying the price in time and stress.
Every minute sitting in traffic is a minute away from your family.
Every delayed road project is a hidden tax on working families.
Growth should improve our economy.
Not trap us in traffic.
In South Carolina and District 114, we deserve infrastructure that keeps up with our success.
Not one that punishes it.
Smart infrastructure investment is economic policy.
Flood Planning
Right now we often:
- Wait for flooding
- Declare emergency repairs
- Quick fix it or wait it out
- Repeat
Instead, we should:
- Push for long-term flood resilience planning
- Coordinate across counties (Dorchester, Charleston, Berkeley)
- Leverage federal mitigation grants more effectively
- Preventative planning
- Smarter spending.
Not wasting more tax dollars on repeat fixes
Fixing flood damage after the fact is more expensive than preventing it in the first place.
I pledge to work hard on our infrastructure as SC State Representative and help our communities flourish responsibly.
