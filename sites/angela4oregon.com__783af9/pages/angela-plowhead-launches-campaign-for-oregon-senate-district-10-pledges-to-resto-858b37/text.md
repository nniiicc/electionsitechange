A service of Burnett Media Group 13 January 2026 Angela Plowhead Launches Campaign for Oregon Senate District 10, Pledges to Restore Accountability and Common-Sense Leadership FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE April 8, 2025 Salem, OR Dr.
Angela Plowhead, U.S.
Air Force veteran, clinical psychologist, and small business owner, has announced her candidacy for Oregon State Senate District 10.
Running to bring transparency and practical solutions back to Salem, Plowhead is focused on delivering safer communities, stronger schools, and a more responsible government.
Oregonians are being silenced while their concerns are ignored, said Plowhead.
Senator Deb Patterson has repeatedly shut down public input on extreme policies and pushed dangerous price-capping schemes that threaten access to healthcare.
District 10 deserves a leader who listens, respects public voices, and puts people before politics.� Plowhead draws inspiration from the legacy of the late Senator Jackie Winters, who once represented the district with principle and poise.
Jackie led with integrity and a deep respect for the people she served.
I will work every day to honor that legacy with honest, effective leadership.� Key Priorities of Angela Plowhead Campaign: Public Safety & Community Support: Say no to policies that weaken public safety.
Say yes to supporting law enforcement, investing in community-based solutions, and ensuring safe, thriving neighborhoods for everyone.
Education & Family Engagement: Say no to one-size-fits-all education mandates.
Say yes to higher standards, open communication between schools and families, and ensuring every student has a path to succeed.
Healthcare Access & Affordability: Say no to price-setting schemes that limit access and slow innovation.
Say yes to thoughtful reforms, innovation, and smart deregulation that make care more affordable and responsive to patients.
Economic Growth & Jobs: Say no to red tape and policies that hold back small businesses.
Say yes to practical reforms that encourage growth, support local employers, and create more opportunities in every community.
Plowhead’s campaign has already earned endorsements from leaders across Oregon.
Connie Whelchel, Vice Chair of the Oregon Republican Party, stated, Angela is principled, capable, and ready to fight for the people.
She’s the leadership change Salem needs.� With a background in service, business, and advocacy, Plowhead is prepared to bring real accountability back to the State Capitol.
