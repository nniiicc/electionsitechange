While she was in the Navy, Rebecca earned her M.B.A. from the University of Pennsylvania’s The Wharton School, and went on to receive an Inc.
Magazine “Best in Business” award in 2024 – so she knows that Congress needs to do everything possible to create a 21st-century economy that puts New Jersey workers and families first.
It’s time we put the rhetoric and the fighting behind us, and bring together public-private partnerships that create good-paying jobs and allow our children, and our children’s children, to succeed.
In Congress, Rebecca will: Ensure New Jersey is a national leader in clean energy, biotech, AI development, and high-tech jobs, instead of cutting and rescinding funding intended to move our tech sector forward.
Fund job training and apprenticeship programs so young people across New Jersey are ready for good paying jobs that don’t need a college degree.
Implement a commonsense and predictable trade policy that allows families, businesses, and foreign allies alike to build a better future together.
Support workers’ rights and unions to ensure the right to organize and strengthen fair wage and labor laws to improve job security in New Jersey.
Modernize our air traffic control system and invest in other key infrastructure upgrades, including the Gateway Tunnel, to ensure one-seat rides and reduced traffic congestion.
