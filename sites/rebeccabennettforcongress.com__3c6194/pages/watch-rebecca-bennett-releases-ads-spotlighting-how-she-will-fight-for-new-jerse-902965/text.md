Both ads feature local moms who are voting for Rebecca because of her commitment to lowering grocery and healthcare costs, and fighting back against the tariffs that are crushing New Jersey’s economy.
They highlight how Rebecca is raising her kids in the district and will fight to build strong communities while ensuring the next generation has the opportunity to succeed.
The ads are backed by an initial six-figure investment and will run on digital.
