SMART-TD Union Endorses Rebecca Bennett for Congress
Cites Bennett’s Focus on Gateway and One Seat Ride
SOMERVILLE, NJ — Today, the Sheet Metal Air Rail and Transportation Workers Union (SMART-TD) announced that they are endorsing Rebecca Bennett for Congress because of her commitment to supporting labor unions and focus on improving transit throughout New Jersey.
“SMART-TD is proud to endorse Rebecca Bennett as she runs to represent New Jersey’s Seventh District.
Rebecca is committed to working together to complete the Gateway Tunnel, and after decades of waiting, deliver a one-seat ride to New Jerseyans along the Raritan Valley Rail Line.
She will also stand up against the attacks against collective bargaining to make sure that our rail workers here in New Jersey have a safe workplace, fair wages, and the right to join a labor union,” said Jared Cassity, National Safety & Legislative Director for SMART TD.
“I am grateful for the support of SMART-TD as we work to build a transportation system that actually works for New Jerseyans.
I commuted on NJ TRANSIT and know firsthand how an unreliable train schedule makes it impossible to know if you’ll be back in time to pick up your kids from daycare, coach soccer, or just make it home for dinner.
I am excited to work with SMART-TD to build the Gateway Tunnel because it’s long past time to bring one-seat ride to New Jersey’s seventh district,” said Rebecca Bennett.
Rebecca Bennett, Democratic Nominee for Congress in NJ-07, is a former U.S.
Navy helicopter pilot, officer in the Air National Guard, healthcare leader, and mom of two.
Rebecca served in the Navy for over a decade as a helicopter pilot and aircraft commander.
After serving her country for over fifteen years in the military and making waves in healthcare innovation, Rebecca is running to unseat Tom Kean, Jr. to build a better future for our community.
As a mom to two young girls, Rebecca understands first-hand the challenges of balancing work & family while navigating inflation and the rising cost of living.
