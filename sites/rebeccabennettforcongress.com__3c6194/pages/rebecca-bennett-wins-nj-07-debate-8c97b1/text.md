RAHWAY, NJ — Tonight, Rebecca Bennett and Congressman Tom Kean Jr. took the stage for their one and only debate of the campaign.
Rebecca outlined her vision for the future of our district, including how she’ll lower costs, get corruption out of Washington, and put country over party.
Meanwhile, Tom Kean Jr. failed to answer for his record of refusing to show up for New Jerseyans and putting his stock portfolio, party bosses, and corporate donors first.
“New Jerseyans who tuned in to tonight’s debate saw two visions for the future of our district and our country.
Rebecca laid out her plans to lower the cost of living and make it possible for New Jersey families to live the American Dream while cracking down on the corruption in Washington that’s driving up prices.
Meanwhile, Tom Kean Jr. defended the status quo, including his votes to raise healthcare, energy, and gas prices, while failing to answer for his multi-million dollar stock portfolio.
This November, New Jerseyans are ready for change, which is why they’ll cast their ballots for Rebecca Bennett,” said Om Savargaonkar, Campaign Manager, Rebecca Bennett for Congress.
