SOMERVILLE, NJ — Today, Rebecca Bennett released her plan for how she will fix New Jersey’s aging infrastructure in Congress.
Rebecca’s plan outlines how she will stop the games and finish the Gateway Tunnel, support bus lines, modernize roads and bridges, and expand air traffic controller pipelines to improve air travel.
“I’m committed to fixing New Jersey’s aging infrastructure because I’ve lived it as a commuter and a mom.
I know how unreliable commutes mean missed family dinners, soccer practices, and daycare pickups,” said Rebecca Bennett.
“In Congress, I’ll fight to make sure we’re getting every cent we’re owed in order to modernize New Jersey’s roads, rails, buses, and airports.
Investing in our infrastructure will bring good-paying jobs to New Jerseyans, boost our economy, and improve the quality of life for all of us.”
