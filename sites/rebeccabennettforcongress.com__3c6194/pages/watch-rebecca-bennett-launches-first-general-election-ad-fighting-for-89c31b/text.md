Rebecca pledges to take on corruption in both parties
SOMERVILLE, NJ — Today, former Navy helicopter pilot and mom of two Rebecca Bennett launched her first ad of the general election, “Fighting For.” The ad highlights how Rebecca learned to put service over self in the Navy, and how she’ll bring that same type of leadership to Congress to tackle the culture of corruption in Washington that is costing New Jersey families.
In the ad, Rebecca focuses on her commitment to cleaning up corruption in both parties, highlighting the fact that she does not take corporate PAC money and has divested all of her individual stocks.
The ad is backed by an initial six-figure investment and will run on broadcast and digital.
