From childcare to groceries, it feels like the price of everything has gone up.
Rebecca’s family knows what it’s like to deal with rising prices, and she knows that families across our district are feeling it too.
It’s time for both parties to work together to make life in New Jersey more affordable for all of us.
Things aren’t working the way they have in the past, and Rebecca’s ready to go to Congress to deliver real results for all of us.
In Congress, Rebecca will: Lead efforts to cut red tape, increase housing supply and construction, and make housing more affordable for buyers and renters.
Fully restore the SALT deduction so that New Jersey taxpayers are no longer double taxed on their hard-earned income.
Lower energy costs across New Jersey by investing in clean, reliable, and domestic energy sources, so that families pay less and our communities are more energy resilient.
Pass paid family leave, because parents should be able to balance work with supporting their kids and loved ones.
Fully fund elder care across New Jersey and support our caregivers, because New Jersey seniors deserve respect and dignity without breaking the bank.
