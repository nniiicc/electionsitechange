SOMERVILLE, NJ — In case you missed it, Congressman Tom Kean Jr. was featured in a New York Times article about members of Congress who have continued to trade stocks and betray the trust of voters.
“Congressman Tom Kean Jr. has spent the past four years trading stocks while voting to drive prices up for all of us.
My husband and I already divested all of our individual stocks because I want New Jerseyans to know that I practice like I preach and will always put our district first,” said Rebecca Bennett.
Let’s not forget: Tom Kean Jr. first ran on a promise to bring ‘the highest levels of ethics and transparency’ to Washington.
Instead, he’s become one of the most prolific stock traders in Congress — even trading stocks while missing months of work.
