That’s why she has a plan to ensure no one pays more than 7% of income for childcare.

Healthcare Costs, Medicaid, and Prescription Drugs After serving in the armed forces for over 15 years, Rebecca Bennett built a career in healthcare to increase access and improve patient outcomes in women’s health, oncology, obesity, and gastrointestinal care.
Tom Kean Jr. is claiming that Bennett “made a fortune by denying people healthcare and putting profits over patients.” The fact is that Rebecca never worked for a health insurance company.
She worked to expand access to care, including women’s healthcare.
In congress she’ll support measures to lower the cost of prescription drugs and prevent insurers from denying necessary care their doctors prescribe.
Stock Trading Rebecca Bennett owns no individual stocks, because Americans shouldn’t have to wonder if their representative is looking out for them or their own stock portfolio.
Her husband also divested his individual stocks as well.
Accessibility Rebecca Bennett is meeting voters where they are, appearing at over 150 events since launching her campaign and sitting down for interviews with local outlets like NJ Spotlight News and News 12 and national outlets like CNN and MS NOW.
