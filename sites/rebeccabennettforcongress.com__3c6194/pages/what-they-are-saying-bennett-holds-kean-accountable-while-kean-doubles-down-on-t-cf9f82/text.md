SOMERVILLE, NJ — Last night, Rebecca Bennett and Tom Kean Jr. faced off in the one and only debate of the campaign.
While Rebecca laid out her vision for lowering costs and rooting corruption out of Washington, Tom Kean Jr. doubled down on his support for Trump’s tariffs and even gave a thumbs up to the war in Iran.
“Last night, Rebecca spoke directly to voters about her vision for the future of our country, and laid out her plans to lower costs, get corruption out of Washington, and put country over party.
Meanwhile, Tom Kean Jr. stood on stage cheering on Trump’s tariff policies and his war with Iran that are making costs soar for New Jerseyans while failing to answer for his stock portfolio,” said Carly Jones, Communications Director, Rebecca Bennett for Congress.
