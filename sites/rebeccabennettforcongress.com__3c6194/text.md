As a mom to two young girls, Rebecca understands first-hand the challenges of balancing work & family while navigating inflation and the rising cost of living.
So Rebecca is stepping up to bring a new kind of leadership to Washington.
She’s not interested in scoring political points—she simply wants to make the country a better place for her family and yours.
