On the Issues
Environment
The beauty of New Hampshire’s rivers, lakes, mountains, coastline and wildlife are some of its greatest assets.
Yet, climate change, pollution and habitat loss threaten these precious resources more than ever.
I will fight to conserve New Hampshire’s natural gifts, for example by supporting responsible energy and sustainable development policies.
Inflation
With higher prices following the pandemic recovery, the economy is on everyone’s minds.
State policies play an important role in making New Hampshire an affordable place to live and raise a family.
I will fight for fair school funding, accessible childcare and increased housing availability to help keep core costs down.
Reproductive Rights
Education
Public education is the cornerstone of an informed and productive society.
I will endeavor to ensure access to quality education for every student.
To meet this objective, all schools and educators must have the resources necessary for each individual’s success.
Public money should be spent on public schools, and I oppose voucher schemes that inappropriately fund religious and private schools.
Mental Health
Mental health issues are worsening across New Hampshire, with a record number of children impacted.
While important improvements have been made across the state in recent years, these must be continuously prioritized and reinforced to guarantee patients receive the care they need.
The state must fulfill its responsibilities to promptly treat mental health patients and eliminate their languishing in emergency rooms.
Equality
The right of equality for all individuals is the foundation of the New Hampshire constitution.
I will work to protect these rights, including the freedom to make private medical decisions, the right to love who you choose and the right to vote in free and fair elections, to ensure dignity and respect for all.
