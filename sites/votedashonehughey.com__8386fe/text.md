Congress Meet Da'Shone An entrepreneur, educator, and builder — not a politician.
Da’Shone Hughey is an entrepreneur, educator, author, and technology innovator dedicated to building pathways into the future economy.
His work centers on artificial intelligence, robotics, workforce training, and education reform.
Running as an Independent under the banner “We The People,” he brings a builder’s mindset to public service — focused on execution, outcomes, and long-term opportunity for New Jersey’s 8th District.

Five priorities that reflect both the needs of today and the demands of tomorrow.

Workforce Development Direct pathways from training to employment in high-demand fields.
Grow Local Wealth Investing in entrepreneurship, small business, and community-based development.

The Issues That Matter Practical solutions for the future economy.
1 Education for the AI Era Expanding access to technology education, digital literacy, robotics, and AI learning so young people are ready to compete and lead.
2 Workforce Development Building direct pathways from education to employment in fields like robotics, HVAC, data infrastructure, advanced manufacturing, and technology services.
3 Small Business & Entrepreneurship Supporting entrepreneurs with access to capital, technical assistance, and public-private partnerships that help them grow and hire.
4 Economic Opportunity Fighting for policies that create opportunity in underserved neighborhoods and strengthen the middle class.
5 Community Innovation Investing in future-focused industries and training systems to make our communities stronger and more resilient.
Campaign Trail The Listening to the People Tour Before asking for your vote, Da’shone Hughey is taking the time to listen.

The Listening to the People Tour is about more than conversation.
