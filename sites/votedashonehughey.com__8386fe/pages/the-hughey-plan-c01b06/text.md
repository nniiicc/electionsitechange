Across Hudson County, hardworking families continue to face rising housing costs, increasing everyday expenses, aging infrastructure, and an economy that is changing faster than many communities can adapt.
The Hughey Plan offers a different path forward—one rooted in accountability, innovation, and measurable results.
It is a comprehensive governing agenda designed to improve quality of life, strengthen our local economy, and restore public confidence that government can once again work for the people.
If elected to Congress, Da’Shone Hughey will pursue an ambitious legislative agenda focused on addressing the issues that matter most to the residents of New Jersey’s 8th Congressional District.
From expanding affordable housing and lowering the cost of living to supporting small businesses, investing in modern infrastructure, and preparing workers for the opportunities of an AI-powered economy, every proposal is designed with one goal in mind: creating lasting opportunity for every community.
Together, these initiatives form a long-term strategy to make New Jersey’s 8th Congressional District a national leader in economic growth, workforce development, innovation, public safety, and community investment.
The Hughey Plan is built on a simple belief: elected officials should be accountable to the people they serve.
Government should deliver solutions—not excuses—and every taxpayer deserves leadership that puts citizens ahead of political insiders, lobbyists, and special interests.
Together, we can build a Hudson County where families can afford to thrive, businesses can grow, neighborhoods are safer, and every resident has the opportunity to succeed It is time to give government back to the people.

Congress The Three Signature Acts The Apprenticeship Growth & Small Business Act America has millions of good-paying jobs that don't require a four-year degree, yet too many workers cannot access them and too many small businesses cannot afford to train them.
This legislation will: Expand federal apprenticeship tax credits for small businesses.
Provide additional incentives for employers investing in distressed communities.
Increase apprenticeship opportunities in Artificial Intelligence, Robotics, Cybersecurity, Healthcare, Manufacturing, Construction, and the Skilled Trades.
Results More apprenticeships Higher wages Stronger small businesses More local investment The Second Chance Apprenticeship Act Everyone deserves the opportunity to rebuild their life through meaningful work.
This legislation creates a federal Department of Labor grant program that connects returning citizens with registered apprenticeships while providing transportation assistance, work equipment, mentoring, housing support, and career coaching.
Results Lower recidivism Stronger families More skilled workers Safer communities The Transit-Linked Affordable Housing & Apprenticeship Act Housing and transportation should work together—not against working families.
This legislation expands incentives for affordable housing built near transit while requiring apprenticeship opportunities during construction.
By connecting housing production with workforce development, we create homes and careers at the same time.
Results More affordable housing Lower commuting costs Faster housing development Thousands of new construction careers HOW WE WILL TRANSFORM HUDSON COUNTY These three bills are the engine that powers our Five-Point Plan.
