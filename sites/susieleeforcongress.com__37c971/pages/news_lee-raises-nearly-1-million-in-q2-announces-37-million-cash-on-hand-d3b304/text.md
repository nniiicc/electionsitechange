Most Money Raised at this Point Than in Any of Lee’s Previous Cycles
Lee’s fundraising total brings her cash on hand to $3,689,228 — the most money her campaign has ever had on hand and nearly $700,000 more cash on hand than she had going into the third quarter in 2024.
This was fueled by grassroots support from over 15,000 individual donations this cycle with a $10 median donation.
“Southern Nevadans know Susie Lee is an independent problem solver who is fighting to put money back in the pockets of hardworking families,” said Greg Lademann, Campaign Manager for Susie Lee for Congress.
“As the cost of gas, groceries, housing, and utilities skyrocket under the Trump Administration, Susie continues to work to lower costs and make life more affordable for southern Nevadans.”
Susie Lee represents Nevada’s Third Congressional District in Congress, where she has been named one of the most bipartisan Members of Congress and one of the most independent voices in Washington.
Before running for Congress, Susie started a comprehensive homeless shelter to help the most vulnerable members of our community, and she led the leading statewide dropout prevention program, Communities in Schools of Nevada.
