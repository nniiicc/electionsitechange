Crime Prevention & Justice — Safe Harbor Asset Protection Protect victims with due process.
The Florida Safe Harbor Asset Protection Act is built around a simple principle: people who follow the law should not bear the financial burden created by those accused of serious crimes.
Tough-on-crime with constitutional safeguards, The Safe Harbor Act ensures justice is served fairly, while Florida businesses continue operating, workers stay employed, and public resources get focused where they are needed most.
Stops criminal bad actors from gaming Florida's court system; even from Washington, DC or Tallahassee.
Tough-on-crime operations focusing on violent crimes, abuse, trafficking, racketeering and organized criminal enterprises.
Assets tied to indicted people for serious crimes are preserved so victims, taxpayers, and communities are not left paying the financial costs when offenders attempt to delay courts, or hide and move their money.
Protects constitutional rights and due process.
Benefits people found not guilty when indicted for crimes they didn't commit.
Reduces taxpayer costs and government waste.
Aids the courts in pursuing justice, instead of assets.
Generates revenue for the State without raising taxes and creates new Florida jobs.
Expected $10 - $50 million annually in taxpayer savings and new state revenue.
Estimated 2,500 Florida jobs preserved or created annually.
