Insurance — FIFA (Florida Insurance Fairness Act) 2026 Insurance fairness.
The Florida Insurance Fairness Act (FIFA) creates a “certification to operate” model for insurance companies operating in Florida to comply with: Advertising standard full-coverage plans for every line they service (home, auto, health, dental, etc.).
No exclusions for previous health conditions, floods, hurricanes, etc.
Removing hidden gaps in coverage and unintentional underinsurance for Florida families.
Premium increases limited to inflation-based metrics (CPI-U).
Price transparency.
Enforcing through the Office of Insurance Regulation (OIR) with the ability to fine, suspend & remove non-compliant insurance companies.
Insurers unwilling to operate fairly may leave the market.
