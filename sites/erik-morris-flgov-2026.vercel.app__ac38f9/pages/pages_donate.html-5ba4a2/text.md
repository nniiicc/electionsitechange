Make a Contribution
Contributions to Florida gubernatorial campaigns are limited to $3,000 per election (Primary and General are separate elections).
All contributions are publicly reported in accordance with Florida law.
Donations should be sent via Zelle.
The information below is required for financial records.
Information is sent privately to our donation email and will not be resold.
Rather than using ActBlue, WinRed, and similar campaign financial schemes, our donations are person-to-person.
Avoiding these money machines allows our focus to be on policies and people.
Of course, if you feel inclined to donate to our cause we appreciate it!
This campaign is low-cost intentionally, donations will go towards advertisements, signs and shirts with any remaining funds going back to Florida charities and communities.
More important than your donation, please share our website and ideas with your Florida friends and family.
Only together can our message get out throughout the state and let us succeed!
Thank you for your support!
