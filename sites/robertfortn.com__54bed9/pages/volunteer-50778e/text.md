VOLUNTEERS
We are looking for enthusiastic volunteers who are ready to make a difference.
We are recruiting volunteers that can help with spreading our message, bringing awareness to Robert’s campaign, and making local connections in their community.
If you are interested in this role, see the Campaign Volunteer description below.
We have 95 counties to visit before November.
We will rely on volunteer County Representatives that will find the most impactful way to reach their communities and set up events for Robert’s ALL95 visits.
County Representatives will recruit volunteers in their county and will be the first point of contact for most of the campaign volunteers in their area.
Read below for more information.
We will be recruiting volunteer Regional Coordinators for West Tennessee, Middle Tennessee, and East Tennessee.
Regional Coordinators will be the first point of contact for their corresponding county representatives.
See the description below if you are interested in this role.
Training will be provided online or in-person (if the volunteer is in northeast Tennessee).
We are on a mission to boost community engagement and empower voices across our state.
Your involvement can help us bridge the gap between the people and the policymakers.
