Had a great time supporting local artists, musicians, crafters, farmers, and small business owners at Arts in the Park in Morristown today!
Something I've noticed during my travels across Tennessee: this state has some serious creative expression!
We have huge venues, giant fairs, and events that draw thousands of people.
Those absolutely have their place.
However, in my opinion, these local events are some of the very best showcases of Tennessee.
The conversations are organic.
They're real.
You meet the people actually creating, growing, building, and selling the things that make their communities unique.
