#ago 1 min read Updated: #ago The first stage is complete!
More than 400 signs delivered across Tennessee.
Six days of forums, podcasts, emails, questionnaires, sign placing, and conversations.
A lot of miles - and so many people who made every one worthwhile.
We have two weeks of early voting ahead, a brief pause, and then Election Day on November 3.
We’re pushing to the very end!
A HUGE thank you to Anthony, Miree, Cindy, Meredith, Brian C., Brian B., Justin, Kendra, Lara, Paul, Liliana, Michael, Ray, and Gary!
Getting these signs across the state in just a few days took a community.
Every stop you made and every moment you gave helped make this possible.
When you vote, please know there is a real community behind the name on that ballot.
People showing up, pitching in, and believing we deserve a government that listens and serves us equally.
My name is on the ballot.
However, this effort belongs to all of us - and so should our government.
Unity through Community.
AROOO!
