I will also be there to meet with members of the public.
All are invited to attend!
We would love to see our community be present to voice their concerns and to become informed on various candidates that are on the August 6th ballot for Washington County, Tennessee.
FREE STORE: We will have (gently used and new) donated items including kid's clothing and books, pet toys, human and pet food, and more.
All items are free for anyone in need.
Anyone can also donate the following supplies between the hours of 11 am until 3:30 pm on July 25th. -Non-perishable food items that have not expired for humans, dogs, cats, farm animals, and other companion animals -Gently used and new clothing -Gently used and new books -Hygiene products (toothpaste, menstrual products, soap, etc.) -New household items -First aid supplies -Pet supplies (cat litter, new water or food dishes, dog or cat beds, etc.) DONATION DROP-OFF LOCATIONS: All Tails Vet (both Jonesborough and Johnson City clinics) will have bins for pet or human food donations.
If you are a local store or organization and would like to host a drop-off bin for donations, please reach out.
EVENT LOCATION: Parking lot at 395 Forest Circle, Jonesborough, TN 37659 .
We will have tables outside in front of All Tails Vet Clinic in Jonesborough across from Taco Bell.
Come see us on the 25th!
CANDIDATES: Tri-cities residents that are running for local, state, and federal positions, including ones that are on the August 6th ballot for Washington County, will be present from 12 pm until 4 pm to meet and hear from with the public.
Come meet Ashley Cavendar, Mary Grace Wooten, Luke Carter, Rodger Broadwater, Kitty Allen, Amanda Bacon, and Robert Jones.
This is not a public forum.
The candidates will be available to introduce themselves and for one-on-one conversations.
See our Facebook Event page for the candidate's Facebook links.
