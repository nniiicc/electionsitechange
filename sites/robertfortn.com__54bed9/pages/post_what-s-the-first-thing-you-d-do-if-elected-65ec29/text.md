#ago 1 min read I hear this question a lot.
After traveling Tennessee throughout the campaign, my answer has changed: A WORKING tour of all 95 counties.
I’ve seen with my own eyes how divided we’ve become — and how obvious it is which communities get attention and which get passed over due to politics.
Too many public servants seem to stop showing up where they think they can’t win votes.
The people living there still have needs.
Still pay taxes.
Still deserve representation.
They are not enemies!
Your ZIP code and your ballot should never determine whether your senator shows up for you.
We should all be sick of politics that turns neighbors into enemies and treats entire communities as lost causes.
Every county will get meaningful time with me — to tell me what’s working, what’s broken, and what they need from the federal government.
Then comes the responsibility to act and follow through.
No victory lap.
No grand speeches.
Just work.
I’m running to serve Tennessee.
All of it.
Representation should walk beside you.
