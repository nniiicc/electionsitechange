Infrastructure & Smart Growth
Like it or not, Blount County and Tennessee are growing.
We cannot afford to keep outgrowing our roads, schools, hospitals, and public safety resources.
We must make infrastructure upgrades that support the growth that has already occurred while ensuring that all future growth is smart and responsible.
Strong infrastructure is the foundation of an unmatched quality of life for our citizens.
Good roads, clean water, reliable waste facilities, and expanding access to high-speed internet ensures that our communities will remain strong and sustainable for generations to come.
