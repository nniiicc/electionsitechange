Fiscal Responsiblity
Like many, I am tired of the expanding debt at various levels of government.
I am committed to using hard earned tax dollars in a way that honors the historic conservative value of fiscal responsibility.
Overall, Tennessee has done an excellent job in being fiscally responsible.
However, we must remain vigilant in identifying fraud, waste, and abuse in budgets and government operations while still providing for the needs of our state.
I am against raising taxes and would like to see the individual tax burden decreased.
