Steve Meskers
Connecticut State Representative
150th District
I’m running for reelection in 2026 to continue serving as your state representative for the 150th district.
It’s been the honor of a lifetime to serve as your state representative for the past eight years.
I’ve worked collegiately in the chamber to bring worthwhile legislation to the table, particularly in the areas of COMMERCE and FINANCE.
If re-elected as your State Rep, I plan to continue my work as chair of the Commerce Committee to build on this progress and work to grow the economy of this state.
You can count on me for my steadfast support of expanding voting rights and protecting all civil rights.
Decisions at a state level matter now more than ever.
I have forged friendships in Hartford which give Greenwich a strong voice at the heart of policy decisions.
More importantly to me, I am committed to serving the residents of Greenwich.
Your Democratic house delegation has brought back significant money for our schools, our children, our elderly and our most vulnerable residents.
Please call or text me anytime.
Share your ideas and concerns.
I love hearing from my constituents and I’m always available.
No problem is too trivial to bring to my attention.
If I can help you, nothing makes me happier.
