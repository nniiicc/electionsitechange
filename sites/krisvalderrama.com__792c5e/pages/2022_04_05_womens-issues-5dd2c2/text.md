- Women’s Issues
Women make up 51.6 percent of Maryland’s citizenry.
Most women work and many are the sole
support of their families.
Yet, women still face workplace discrimination.
Which is why I successfully
sponsored legislation to prevent sexual harassment, provide equal pay for equal work and outlawed
pregnancy discrimination for state employees.
Women deserve to be treated fairly.
I am fighting for my
daughters, and for yours as well.
