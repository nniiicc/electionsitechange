Meet Chris A proven approach My service in the legislature will focus on solutions that are proven, consistent, and protect individual liberties.
That’s how I served on the West Jordan City Council—and it’s how I’ll serve in the Utah State House.
With every vote, I’ll ask hard questions to protect your liberty and your wallet: Is this a problem that needs to be solved by the government?
Does the problem need to be addressed by the State, or can it be better addressed by a level of government closer to the people affected (city, county, school, etc.)?
Will legislation simplify or complicate the problem?
Are individual rights being protected or restricted?
Is the cost something I could justify to a retiree on a fixed income?
Ultimately, is this the right thing to do?
