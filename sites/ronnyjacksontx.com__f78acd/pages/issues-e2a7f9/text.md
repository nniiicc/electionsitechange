Safeguarding the Second Amendment
Our Second Amendment right shall never be infringed and I will continue to fight every day to defend our right to keep and bear arms.
I am a proud member of the NRA and have been a gun owner my entire life.
In Congress, I have successfully removed any “Red Flag Laws” from the NDAA, introduced legislation to prohibit local governments from requiring insurance, taxes or user fees, and proudly voted in favor of the One Big Beautiful Bill to safeguard Second Amendment rights by removing tax and registration requirements for firearm silencers and eliminating silencers from the National Firearms Act.

Fighting for Texas Agriculture
Texas’ 13th District is the top-producing agricultural district in the state, and I am committed to making sure our farmers and ranchers have the tools and policies in place they need to thrive.
I am proud that two of my key provisions were included in the One Big Beautiful Bill: the Foreign Animal Disease Prevention, Surveillance, and Rapid Response Act, which strengthens the USDA’s premier animal health programs, and the LIP Enhancement Act, which provides critical support to livestock producers affected by the 2024 Panhandle wildfires so they can rebuild their herds.
I will always fight to protect and advance agriculture, not just in the 13th District, but across the nation.
