House District 11 deserves a representative who listens, works hard, and puts this community first.
Britney Tennant is running because she believes public service means representing everyone, not just the people who already agree with her.
Here's where she stands.
Britney is running... ...to fight for the Wyoming she grew up in - generous, independent, hardworking, and supportive.
Cowboy never meant conformity.
People didn't ask what you were; they waited to see who you were. ...to protect what she knows is the best of Wyoming: our legacies of opportunity and equality, our public lands and animals, and our spirit of independence and determination. ...to champion the causes that matter most to Wyoming's people — affordability, quality public education, equal economic opportunity, and conservation and protection of public lands and animals.
This election is a choice about who we are and who we will become: will we shape our own future, or will we let someone else dictate it to us?
Britney's Key Issues 01 Voice & Local Representation Wyoming rejected extremism in the primary.
But one-party rule still leaves too many voices out.
Britney has spent more than twenty years building coalitions and working across differences to get things done in Cheyenne.
In the legislature, she'll bring that same listen-first leadership — representing all of House District 11.
Without Britney, important issues like women’s rights, healthcare, good jobs, and protecting public schools and public lands won’t get the hearing they deserve.
02 Prosperity & Affordability Wyoming families are working hard and still falling behind.
Housing, healthcare, and childcare all cost more than a paycheck can keep up with, and too many parents can't find childcare that lets them work in the first place.
As data centers and new growth reshape our community, that growth has to strengthen the local services families depend on, not strain them.
Britney is a small-business founder and nonprofit CEO who has spent her career balancing budgets and building partnerships that deliver real results for working people — she knows what it actually takes for families and local businesses to get ahead, not just get by.
03 Education & Healthcare A strong Wyoming starts with strong public schools, from our neighborhood classrooms to the University of Wyoming — and Wyoming's new school voucher program threatens to drain public dollars away from the schools that need them most.
Healthcare costs are pricing too many families out of the care they need, including basic maternal care close to home.
Wyoming's Constitution protects our right to healthcare, including a woman's right to choose, and that freedom is worth defending.
Britney is a product of Cheyenne's public schools, and so is her son — she knows firsthand what they make possible, which is why she opposes the voucher program entirely and will fight to keep public dollars in public schools, not diverted out of them.
Her career leading Cheyenne nonprofits and building healthcare partnerships gives her a practical, informed voice for closing the healthcare gaps families face today.
04 Public Lands & Wildlife Wyoming's public lands and wildlife are part of who we are.
Protecting them for the next generation means treating stewardship as a responsibility, not an afterthought — and that same responsibility carries over to how we treat the animals in our care, wild and domestic alike.
Public lands and wildlife should never be sold off for short-term gain, and neglect or cruelty toward animals is never something this community should look away from. ​ Britney was raised here, and she's raising her own family here — protecting Wyoming's public lands and wildlife for her kids, and everyone else's, is personal.
It's also professional: she's spent her career leading Wyoming's animal welfare organizations, from founding Black Dog Animal Rescue to now serving as CEO of the Cheyenne Animal Shelter.
Whether you're a Democrat, a Republican, an Independent, or somewhere in between, Britney is working to earn your trust.
