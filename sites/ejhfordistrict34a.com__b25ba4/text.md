Why I’m running
- The rising cost of living is the number one issue facing families in Harford County.
From energy bills to groceries to housing, too many people feel like they are falling behind.
I am focused on practical solutions that increase transparency, reduce unnecessary costs, and ensure that state policies prioritize working families.
I believe that government should be focused on helping people afford to live and stay in their communities, not making it harder.
- Harford County residents value the character of their communities, and I believe growth should be thoughtful and responsible, not rushed or uncontrolled.
At the same time, many of our young residents and families are being priced out of the housing market.
I support a balanced approach that protects communities while improving affordability by reducing unnecessary permitting delays, encouraging appropriate development in the right areas, and supporting first-time homebuyers.
My focus is on maintaining quality of life while ensuring future generations can stay in the county.
- A strong local economy is key to opportunity and long-term stability.
I support policies that encourage business growth, reduce unnecessary regulatory burdens, and strengthen the environment for small businesses.
I believe in working with local economic development leaders to attract and retain jobs in Harford County so residents have access to good-paying careers close to home.
My goal is to expand opportunity while keeping the county a great place to live and work.
