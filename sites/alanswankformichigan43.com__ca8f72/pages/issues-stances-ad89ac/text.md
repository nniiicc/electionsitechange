Championing the right to repair
The right to repair is about empowering consumers and local businesses.
It ensures that individuals and independent repair shops can fix their own products, from farm equipment to electronics, without relying solely on manufacturers.
This fosters competition, reduces waste, saves money for Michigan families and businesses, and supports local economies.
We believe in your right to repair what you own.
Advocating for LGBTQA+ rights
Every individual deserves to live with dignity, respect, and equal protection under the law.
We stand firmly for the rights and well-being of the LGBTQA+ community in Michigan.
This means fighting for comprehensive non-discrimination protections, access to healthcare, and creating a truly inclusive environment where everyone feels safe, valued, and has the opportunity to thrive.
