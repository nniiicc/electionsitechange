Meet PEPPER Contribute Priorities Photos Contact Endorsements My focus: Quality Education Affordable Energy Responsible Budgeting I believe: Education: We need to provide a quality education for all students in Alaska.
I will work toward finding ways to recruit and retain quality teachers, reduce class sizes, and provide students with the knowledge and skills to succeed.
Alaska must focus on preparing students for good paying jobs.
We need to put value back into a diploma.
Students who graduate in Alaska need to have skills, not just seat-time.
I will work to establish requirements in U.S. history, civics, and reading proficiency.
Energy: I will work to lower the cost of energy so Alaska families can keep more of what they earn and businesses can grow and create jobs.
I will work for energy security by exploring wind, solar, hydro-electric, geothermal, and the natural gas pipeline.
Alaska needs to be in charge of our own future, not a multi-national corporation intent only on profit.
State Budgeting: Our budget should reflect our priorities.
I will work to ensure public dollars are spent on programs that help working families and strengthen our communities.
Government funding should not be based on the price of oil.
I will push for more stable sources of funding, allowing for a full payment of the PFD.
