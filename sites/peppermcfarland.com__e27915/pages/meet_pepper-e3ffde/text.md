Meet PEPPER
Why are you running?
As a resident of district 33 for 16 years I have watched Mike Prax vote the party line.
I got tired of waiting for someone who represents my values to run, so I’m running.
North Pole deserves a reasonable choice.
I am that choice.
Why are you running undeclared?
I have been voting undeclared all my voting life because I don’t belong in any of the boxes.
I am fiscally conservative - let’s not spend taxpayers money unless we know exactly who it’s going to help and have some form of accountability - but socially, I’m more concerned about how we care for people - we need to support elders, veterans and children.
By picking a box I signal that I am subscribing to all that that box subscribes to, and I don’t.
I think independently, and make decisions on my values and if elected I will take your values into account as well.
What makes you qualified?
What makes me qualified is that I am a listener and a thinker.
I don’t just believe what I’m told; I do research, I talk to people on all sides, and I consider the benefits and consequences of decisions before making them.
Twenty-three years as a teacher has made me good at handling situations in a personable and diplomatic way without giving up my standards.
Many years as a bartender made me a good listener and a quick thinker.
Running a small business has allowed me to think about decisions from an economical perspective.
My bachelor’s major was history with minors in economics and political science.
My Master’s was secondary education.
There is a lot more to me than just being a teacher.
