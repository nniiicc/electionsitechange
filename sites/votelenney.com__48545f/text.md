April 2026: The war's still on April 2025: The golden age 2024 letter (PDF) ↗ Session of 2026 · Letter dated April 2026 The report nobody else will send you DATE: April 2026 FROM: Senator Brian Lenney South Nampa Friday, 9:33 a.m.
Dear Fellow Idahoan, We just finished 81 days in Boise.
And I want to give you the real account of what happened, not the version that makes everyone look good.
So, a few wins first.
I personally carried the bill that stripped teachers' union dues off the taxpayer dime.
Got it passed.
American law for American courts (I pushed that through in the Senate)?
Now signed into law.
And my voter affidavit reform that actually puts teeth into ID voter verification at the polls is now law.
These weren't wins that fell into my lap.
I put my name on the legislation and dragged them through to the finish line.
We also landed some hits on the money side.
Idaho's sitting on a $1.6 billion surplus and the instinct in that building was to spend it.
Every surplus is an invitation to grow government, and there are plenty of people in Boise who answered that invitation enthusiastically.
I didn't.
We pushed through rescissions and budget cuts that clawed back agency spending (and gutted millions from the fed-funded Minnesota-style daycare program too).
But I want to be straight with you about what all of this actually is.
Cutting agencies and clawing back program dollars is treating symptoms.
The disease is a spending culture in Boise that assumes every surplus belongs to the government first.
I held the line where I could but I'm not in the majority the way I'd need to be to actually win that war.
So we won some battles this session.
But the war is still very much going.
And nothing proves that more than the biggest fight we could have had this session, the one we didn't have.
Not really, anyway.
I'm talking about illegal immigration.
It's not just a "federal issue." It touches everyone in Idaho.
Your neighbor who can't find construction work.
The school that can't figure out who's actually enrolled.
The wage that won't go up because someone will always undercut it with labor that doesn't cost what labor should cost.
I personally went in with several enforcement bills and they ALL got killed or refused a hearing before most Idahoans ever heard they existed.
Not by Democrats.
But by our own Republican supermajority.
That's where we're at as a state.
And you know what I keep coming back to?
Somebody profits from this.
Somebody profits when enforcement is weak and workers are cheap and nobody asks questions.
And those somebodies were in that building, quiet and patient and effective, the whole session.
The Idaho Sheriff's Association showed up to help kill the ICE cooperation bill I co-sponsored (and there were several more bills they testified AGAINST).
I saw committee chairmen make bills disappear into their desks.
And I watched every single bill we had die or got no hearing.
Yes, in "Republican Idaho." So while most legislators from safe Republican districts will send you an end-of-session letter that lists all the wins and call it a day...
I'm not going to do that.
You're getting this letter because you deserve to know what the cheap labor lobby still has on the Idaho statehouse.
Their stranglehold didn't loosen this session (if anything, now I know exactly how tight it is).
What you get from having me in that building is simple.
Someone who names it.
Someone whose name is actually on the enforcement bills (not just the press releases).
And someone who isn't going to tell you we had a "great session" when the most important lever we had didn't get pulled.
That's the report from Boise.
For Idaho, Brian Lenney Senator Brian Lenney Idaho State Senate, District 13 P.S.
Most people in Idaho have no idea any of this happened.
You do now.
That matters more than you think.
Session of 2025 · Letter dated April 2025 Trump's golden age is here, Idaho is ruby red & America is back!
DATE: April 2025 FROM: Brian Lenney 23 miles west of Boise 12:43 P.M.
Hey there, As your Senator from Nampa, I'm thrilled to report that the 2025 legislative session was a roaring success for making Idaho great again.
We scored massive America/Idaho First wins, and I had an absolute blast fighting for the conservative values District 13 holds dear.
So here's the high-level scoop on some of our victories—and a bit of bad news we'll crush next time.
First, the bad...
Illegal immigration remains a thorn in our side... ➔ We couldn't get a hearing for my E-Verify bill (which those who hire illegal aliens HATE) or my bill mandating Idaho law enforcement pursue 287G status (which lets them be "deputized" by ICE). ➔ A bill to outlaw transporting and harboring illegals (H335) got killed in the House and in a weird twist of events, Rep.
Stephanie Mickelson, an active legislator, was caught employing a violent illegal alien criminal during an ICE raid on her farm! ➔ Not to mention Governor Little's weak executive order on illegal aliens was just fluff with no teeth.
Needless to say, there's A LOT of work to be done here.
Now, to the good...
We passed H93, a school choice tax credit, empowering parents over bureaucrats.
And we passed a bill saying parents now have to opt in to sex ed, not opt out (H239)... common sense, right?
I co-sponsored a bill preventing the type of indecent exposure we saw at "Nampa PRIDE" last year (H270) which was signed into law.
We passed a bill to keep men out of women's locker rooms (H264), mandated the death penalty for pedophiles (H380) by firing squad (H37), no less.
I'm also proud to say that health freedom soared...
My bill to forever ban mask mandates (H32) was signed into law My bill to pull back power from Idaho's public health districts (S1031) was signed into law My bill to strip Health & Welfare bureaucrats of their overreach after a $15 million child care budget overrun (H90) was signed into law My bill to make DHW bureau chiefs fireable (S1052) was signed into law And that's just the tip of the iceberg!
We also... ★ Fixed vaccine exemptions to favor parents (S1179) ★ Made Ivermectin available over the counter (S1211) ★ Passed the "Medical Ethics Defense Act" (H59) ★ Shifted rules to statutes to take power from the unelected bureaucrats (we did this a lot) ★ Passed "The Medicaid Reform and Cost Containment Act" (H345) But wait, there's more!
My bill combating lawfare and protecting free speech (S1001) was signed into law, we slashed $# million-plus in taxes, raised the grocery tax credit so you get to keep more of your money (H231), and deleted heaps of pages of outdated codes and regs.
I also sponsored several transparency and accountability bills (H312, H215, H222, H113, H358) to keep the government lean and mean.
This session mirrored the America First and MAHA movements as we saw many big wins for liberty, family, and Idaho values.
There's more to do, but I'm proud of what we achieved.
I had a blast making Idaho a better place to live, work, and raise a family and I hope you're as fired up as I am.
For liberty, Brian Lenney Nampa's Conservative Senator blforidaho@gmail.com P.S.
