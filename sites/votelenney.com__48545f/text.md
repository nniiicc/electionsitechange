Hey there,
As your Senator from Nampa, I'm thrilled to report that the 2025 legislative session was a roaring success for making Idaho great again.
We scored massive America/Idaho First wins, and I had an absolute blast fighting for the conservative values District 13 holds dear.
So here's the high-level scoop on some of our victories—and a bit of bad news we'll crush next time.
First, the bad...

Now, to the good...
We passed H93, a school choice tax credit, empowering parents over bureaucrats.
And we passed a bill saying parents now have to opt in to sex ed, not opt out (H239)... common sense, right?
I co-sponsored a bill preventing the type of indecent exposure we saw at "Nampa PRIDE" last year (H270) which was signed into law.
We passed a bill to keep men out of women's locker rooms (H264), mandated the death penalty for pedophiles (H380) by firing squad (H37), no less.
I'm also proud to say that health freedom soared...
- My bill to forever ban mask mandates (H32) was signed into law
- My bill to pull back power from Idaho's public health districts (S1031) was signed into law
- My bill to strip Health & Welfare bureaucrats of their overreach after a $15 million child care budget overrun (H90) was signed into law
- My bill to make DHW bureau chiefs fireable (S1052) was signed into law
And that's just the tip of the iceberg!
We also...
- ★ Fixed vaccine exemptions to favor parents (S1179)
- ★ Made Ivermectin available over the counter (S1211)
- ★ Passed the "Medical Ethics Defense Act" (H59)
- ★ Shifted rules to statutes to take power from the unelected bureaucrats (we did this a lot)
- ★ Passed "The Medicaid Reform and Cost Containment Act" (H345)
But wait, there's more!
My bill combating lawfare and protecting free speech (S1001) was signed into law, we slashed $400 million-plus in taxes, raised the grocery tax credit so you get to keep more of your money (H231), and deleted heaps of pages of outdated codes and regs.
I also sponsored several transparency and accountability bills (H312, H215, H222, H113, H358) to keep the government lean and mean.
This session mirrored the America First and MAHA movements as we saw many big wins for liberty, family, and Idaho values.
There's more to do, but I'm proud of what we achieved.
I had a blast making Idaho a better place to live, work, and raise a family and I hope you're as fired up as I am.
