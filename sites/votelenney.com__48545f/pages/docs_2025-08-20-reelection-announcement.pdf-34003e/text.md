“I’m Just Getting Started.” Senator Brian Lenney Announces 2026 Reelection for Idaho State Senate NEWS PROVIDED BY Brian Lenney August 20, 2025 Nampa, Idaho – Senator Brian Lenney, a proven conservative leader, announces his re-election campaign for the Idaho State Senate in the Republican Primary on May 20, 2026.​ ​ Recognized as one of Idaho’s most effective senators, Lenney earned the 2025 CPAC Award for Conservative Excellence and a top ranking from Club for Growth for driving economic growth.
His 2025 legislative victories in education, health freedom, and limited government solidify his commitment to The Gem State.​ ​ “Idahoans need a senator who delivers results, not excuses,” said Lenney.
“My 2025 record proves I fight for freedom, parental rights, and the American worker.
Illegal immigration threatens our communities, and I’ll keep pushing to protect our way of life and put Idahoans first.”​ ​ In just three sessions, Senator Lenney has amassed an impressive record of legislative victories, establishing himself as a formidable force and a leading voice in Idaho conservatism.
In his most recent 2025 legislative session, Senator Lenney secured key victories for freedom and accountability.
His notable achievements include spearheading Idaho’s first anti-lawfare bill (S1001) to protect free speech, banning mask mandates (H32), limiting public health district powers (S1031), and ensuring Health & Welfare bureaucratic accountability (S1052), to name just a few of his significant wins.​ ​ "I'm fighting to protect Idaho's values and secure our future," Lenney declared.
"I've empowered parents, curbed government overreach, and tackled disastrous covid-era policies.
Now, we must defend American sovereignty and put Idaho first with unyielding resolve."​ ​ Lenney’s campaign will zero in on curbing illegal immigration, expanding parental rights, and protecting Idaho from woke nanny-state policies.
To learn more about Lenney visit www.votelenney.com.
