From: Brian Lenney Nampa, Idaho Senator, District 13 3:13 pm Dear Friend, Milton Friedman said: “If you put the federal government in charge of the Sahara Desert, in 5 years there'd be a shortage of sand.” That statement resonates deeply with me.
Here in Idaho, and frankly across America, the real power doesn't lie in the distant halls of Washington D.C., but right here in our states.
The truth is, Washington has lost its way.
In that vast machinery, a single person seems incapable of making a genuine difference.
But here?
In the states?
This is where one person can truly stand out and drive change.
As your representative for Nampa in the State Senate, I've dedicated myself to championing the rights and freedoms of our community.
Since you've entrusted me with District 13, my mission has been to ensure our laws reflect the interests of Idahoans themselves, not those of faceless corporations.
In this letter, I want to share with you some of the victories we've achieved together during the 2024 legislative session.
These achievements are a testament to our shared commitment to keeping Idaho a place where individual rights are paramount, and where the government serves the people, not the other way around.
This year, we made significant strides in enhancing the safety and wellbeing of Idahoans.
Believe me, if I could, I'd dive into each one in detail: ➔ We empowered our police and legal teams with greater authority in sentencing fentanyl traffickers, taking a firm stand against this deadly crisis...
(H406) (Over, please)... ➔ We revolutionized how we handle our state's budget.
For the first time in decades, we're breaking it down piece by piece, moving away from the all-or-nothing approach (this change ensures more thoughtful and effective use of our resources)... ➔ I was the deciding vote to kill a bill that would’ve made it nearly impossible for parents to remove obscene content from libraries (S1289).
We also killed a bill that would have granted Big Pharma legal immunity from the consequences of their products (S1245)! ➔ I led the charge against international threats by sponsoring a bill that stops foreign entities, specifically China, from stealing American genetic information (H670)... ➔ I also took a proactive stance against the adoption of Socialist "Universal Basic Income" programs in Idaho's cities and counties, ensuring our state remains a beacon of individual responsibility and free-market capitalism (H572a)... ➔ And all session long, I fought like hell for parental rights in education, health care, and every other area of life where the government is trying to encroach on parents...
Of course, those are just a few things I fought for this year (there are hundreds more).
As a member of the Idaho Freedom Caucus, I couldn't be more proud to represent Nampa in the Statehouse.
I hope to hear from you or see you around this Summer!
