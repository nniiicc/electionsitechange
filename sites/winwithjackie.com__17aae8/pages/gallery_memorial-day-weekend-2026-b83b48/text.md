It means having the choice to show up and march through the rain to honor the men and women who gave everything so we could live in a country where showing up is still our choice.
And today, I was proud to see our community make that choice.
With umbrellas in hand and flags held high, we stood together to remember the cost of the freedoms we too often take for granted.
It was an absolute honor to march with the Town of Marlborough Republican Committee in the Marlboro-Milton Memorial Day parade and stand alongside a community that still understands the meaning of this day.
So now I ask: What does Memorial Day mean to you?
