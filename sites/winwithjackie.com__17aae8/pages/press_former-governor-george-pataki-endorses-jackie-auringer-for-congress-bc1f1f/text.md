“New York needs a new generation of leaders who are connected to their communities, know how jobs are created, and believe public service means putting people ahead of politics,” said Governor Pataki.
He added, “Jackie Auringer is deeply connected to the Hudson Valley and the people who call it home.
Coming from a family business, she understands firsthand the challenges businesses face every day from rising costs and taxes to unnecessary government red tape.
“I know in Congress, Jackie will bring the energy, common sense and a commitment to service that our country needs.
She will always put the people of the Hudson Valley ahead of politics, and I am proud to endorse Jackie Auringer for Congress,” said Pataki.
Auringer said Pataki’s 12 years as governor demonstrated the kind of practical, people-first leadership she believes is needed in Washington.
“Governor Pataki showed for 12 years what common sense and servant leadership can accomplish,” said Auringer.
“He governed with principle, brought people together and never lost sight of who he worked for.
I’m incredibly honored to have his endorsement and proud to carry those same values into this campaign.” Auringer said her campaign remains focused on lowering costs for families, expanding economic opportunity, supporting businesses and restoring accountability to government.
“Our region deserves a representative who is rooted here, understands the challenges people face, and will always put the people of this district ahead of politics,” Auringer said.
“That is the kind of representative I intend to be.” ### About Jackie Auringer for Congress Jackie Auringer is running for Congress because the community she grew up in deserves a stronger voice in Washington, focused on restoring affordability, strengthening local economies, and fighting for the families and small businesses that make the Hudson Valley thrive.
