Weekends Weekdays Evenings Anytime Join the Team Common Questions Do I need any experience to volunteer?
Not at all.
We train everyone before their first shift.
Whether this is your first time getting involved in a campaign or you are a seasoned activist, there is a place for you on this team.
How much time do I need to commit?
Any amount helps.
Some volunteers give a few hours on a single Saturday.
Others are with us every week.
You set the pace and we will match you with opportunities that fit your schedule.
Can I volunteer from home?
Yes!
Phone banking, data entry, and social media ambassador work are all done remotely.
You never have to leave your house to make a real difference.
What if I am nervous about talking to strangers?
That is completely normal.
We provide scripts, training, and pair newer volunteers with experienced ones.
Most people find that after the first few conversations, it starts to feel natural.
What happens after I sign up?
A campaign organizer will reach out within 48 hours to welcome you to the team and share your first opportunity.
You are never just a name on a list here.
Can I bring a friend or family member?
Please do!
Volunteering with someone you know makes it more fun and more effective.
Just have them fill out the sign-up form as well so we have their info.
