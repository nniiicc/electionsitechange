Clinton Community Day
Had a great time at Clinton Community Day yesterday.
The Hudson Valley is worth fighting for and days like these remind us why!

It was great spending the day with Gregory Campus, candidate for NYS Assembly in the 106th District.
Thank you to the Town of Clinton Republican Committee for having us and to everyone who stopped by to say hello!!
