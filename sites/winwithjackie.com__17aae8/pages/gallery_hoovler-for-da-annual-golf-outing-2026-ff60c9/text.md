Hoovler for DA Annual Golf Outing
Had the pleasure of attending the 14th Annual David M.
Hoovler Golf Outing to support our Orange County District Attorney David Hoovler.
I loved seeing everyone come out for our DA, from former Yankees and West Point graduates to veterans, union members, business owners and community leaders.
I even had the privilege of playing Vanna White during the raffle.
It's heartwarming to see people from all walks of life come together for a common cause.
No matter who you are or what you do, we all play a part in giving back to our community.
