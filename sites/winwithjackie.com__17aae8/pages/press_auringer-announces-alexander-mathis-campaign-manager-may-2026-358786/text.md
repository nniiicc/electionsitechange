Mathis joins the Auringer campaign after serving as Deputy Campaign Manager for Congressman Mike Lawler's campaign, where he helped oversee day-to-day campaign operations, strategy, and coordination across field, communications, finance, and political teams.
He previously worked as a Field Director for the New York Republican State Committee, managing field operations across Rockland, Westchester, Putnam, and Dutchess counties.
"Congressman Mike Lawler runs one of the best political operations in the country, and Alex's experience on that team is exactly the kind of talent and discipline we want on this campaign," said Jackie Auringer.
"He understands the Hudson Valley, he knows how to organize, and he knows what it takes to connect with voters and deliver results.
I'm thrilled to have him leading our campaign as we work together to save the Hudson Valley." Mathis also has experience in election administration, having served as a Program Administrator with the Westchester County Board of Elections, and serves in the New York Army National Guard.
"Jackie Auringer was born, raised, and works right here in the Hudson Valley, and that matters," said Alexander Mathis.
"She understands what families are facing because this is her home, too.
Jackie is running to fight for affordability, restore common sense and protect the quality of life that makes the Hudson Valley special.
I'm proud to join her campaign and help build the team needed to win this race and save the Hudson Valley." Auringer's campaign will focus on restoring affordability, opportunity, strengthening public safety and giving Hudson Valley families a voice in Washington.
### About Jackie Auringer for Congress Jackie Auringer is running for Congress because the community she grew up in deserves a stronger voice in Washington, focused on restoring affordability, strengthening local economies, and fighting for the families and small businesses that make the Hudson Valley thrive.
