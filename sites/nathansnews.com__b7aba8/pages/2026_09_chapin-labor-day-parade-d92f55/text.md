Skip to content Nathan Ballentine People, not Politics About Issues Nathan’s News Get Involved Contact Donate September 8, 2026 Chapin Labor Day Parade (video) Everyone loves a parade!
This year’s crowd seemed even larger than ever.
I’ve been walking the route (and Okra Strut Parade in Irmo) many years!
Unless I’m out of town, I’m there!
Was great to see so many organizations, students, and community groups get a chance to be visible to the community during a weekend of events (even if some events had to be canceled due to weather).
As usual, many candidates walked the route, shook hands, and handed out candy!
I was glad to walk with the Republican candidates for Governor, Education, Agriculture, and Attorney General.
I also got to meet Democratic candidates for Congress.
Labor Day seems to be the unofficial kickoff to campaign-season around the country – and that’s the case for my campaign as well.
Soon you’ll see my signs around town and see me and volunteers walking your neighborhood and listening to what matters most to you and your family!
I’m asking for your support again this fall and hope you’ll get out and vote early to avoid the lines on Election Day!
