Skip to content Nathan Ballentine People, not Politics About Issues Nathan’s News Get Involved Contact Donate August 14, 2026 Eye-sore in Irmo will be removed soon! (video) This is why I love hearing from y’all!
Seems like each month when I send out my monthly COMMUNITY UDPATE EMAIL, I get the most feedback – and I love it!
Like you, I drive down Woodrow Street and used to notice the railroad ties stacked up.
After months (years?) I guess we get used to it and don’t pay it any attention.
A constituent in Friarsgate asked about them and so I headed over to grab dinner one night at Lizard’s Thicket, noticed the ties still there, and thought “has anyone called the railroads to have these removed?” I pulled over.
Made a call.
And soon this eye-sore will be removed.
I was told before Halloween, they will be gone!
That’s a nice treat – and hopefully not a trick! 🙂 September’s COMMUNITY UPDATE EMAIL will go out in a few weeks!
Look forward to more suggestions and questions like these around our community.
As your state representative, I’ve got many agency heads on speed dial and eager to help when we need them!
