Skip to content Nathan Ballentine People, not Politics About Issues Nathan’s News Get Involved Contact Donate April 20, 2011 House Takes Furlough It’s our second furlough week this year in the House and I wanted to give a quick glimpse at my “paying job” at Wells Fargo Home Mortgage .
As I mention in the video, I don’t know how my colleagues from across the state manage their jobs while we’re in session here in Columbia but I do hear the challenges they face.
Fortunately, my office is just a few blocks away and obviously my work is mainly in the Midlands with local realtors and our local bankers.
The balance between “paying jobs” and public service is difficult and it’s one of the reasons I support a shorter session for the General Assembly.
