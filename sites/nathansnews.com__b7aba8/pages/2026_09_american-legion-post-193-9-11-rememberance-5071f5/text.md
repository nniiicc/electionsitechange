PATRIOT DAY – WE WILL NEVER FORGET
This Friday, September 11, 2026, at 8:30 a.m., Chapin American Legion Post 193 invites our members, families, friends, and community to join us for a special Patriot Day Ceremony at our new Post home.
This year marks the 25th anniversary of the terrorist attacks of September 11, 2001—a day that forever changed our nation.
We will gather to remember the nearly 3,000 innocent lives lost, honor the firefighters, law enforcement officers, EMS personnel, and other first responders who answered the call, and recognize the service members who served and sacrificed in the military operations that followed 9/11.
During our ceremony, we will remember the significant moments of that morning, toll the bell in remembrance, and lower our Nation’s flag to half-staff.
Friday, September 11, 2026
⏰ 8:30 a.m.
📍 Chapin American Legion Post 193
230 Amicks Ferry Road, Chapin, SC
We invite our entire community to stand with us as we remember those we lost and honor those who answered our Nation’s call.
Twenty-five years later, our promise remains the same:
🇺🇸 WE WILL NEVER FORGET.
