Skip to content Nathan Ballentine People, not Politics About Issues Nathan’s News Get Involved Contact Donate September 24, 2026 21st Annual Irmo Community Prayer Breakfast – Jack’s Pack What a wonderful, impactful prayer breakfast this morning at Gateway Baptist Church!
There wasn’t a dry eye in the building after Ned Timmerman shared his family’s and his son’s journey and faith.
If you aren’t familiar with Jack Timmerman (their oldest son), please make time now and click here to read about his life .
I had not heard of Batten’s Disesase until Ned called me a few years go and it’s the Timmerman’s hope and I my hope to others will learn about this disease and ultimately one day a cure may be found.
If you are able and willing to help Jack’s Pack Foundation, please consider doing so!
Seeing many in our community come together to pray, support and offer help to this family is truly God moving in our community.
It was wonderful to see so many first time-attendees this morning as well.
We hope to see more next year when Union United Methodist church will host the event!
