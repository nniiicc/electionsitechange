I waited until this week to start delivering my yard signs and look forward to visiting with you at your doorstep.
Volunteers have joined me greeting voters and we will continue the next several weeks as I look forward to earning your vote again and representing you in Columbia.
Like always, my signs are placed personally by me and with the permission of the property owner and I will personally remove them the day after the election.
This year we have several candidates on the ballot: from statewide offices (Governor/LtGovernor, etc) to US House/Senate to our local races for State House and School Board and a few county offices as well.
The election is November 3rd at your normal polling locaiton.
Early voting is from October 19th – October 31st
Monday – Saturday, 8:30 am – 6:00pm
