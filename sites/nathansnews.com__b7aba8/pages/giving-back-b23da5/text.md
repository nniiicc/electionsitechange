Dear Friends, When I campaigned to be your State Representative in 2004, I did so to put your interest first and to be a true public servant.
Because we have all seen or heard of elected officials abusing their position for private gain, I committed to return my legislative salary to worthy causes and organizations that truly make a difference in the lives of others and our communities.
I hope and plan to continue to return all that I can in the coming years and am humbled to be able to return so much during my first term.
Below are some of the organizations that are making a difference.
If you have a request, please bring it to my attention and I will do all that I can with funds that are available.
