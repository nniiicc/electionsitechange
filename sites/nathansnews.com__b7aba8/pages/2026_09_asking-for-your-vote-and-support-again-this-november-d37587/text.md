Skip to content Nathan Ballentine People, not Politics About Issues Nathan’s News Get Involved Contact Donate September 1, 2026 Asking for your support and vote this November!
I’m asking for your vote to continue serving the Irmo-Chapin community in the South Carolina State House.
For years, I’ve had the honor of serving you and your family in Columbia where experience matters.
I’ve built the relationships, earned the seniority, and gained the perspective necessary to make sure Irmo and Chapin have a strong voice when decisions are being made at the state level.
South Carolina is growing, and with that growth comes an important responsibility: making sure families can afford to live, work, and raise their families here.
I’ve remained focused on being a fiscal conservative and keeping affordability top of mind — supporting policies like eliminating the state sales tax on groceries, lowering income tax rates, increasing the Homestead Exemption for senior citizens, and helping keep college tuition frozen for the past seven years.
These aren’t just numbers on a budget sheet; they are real dollars back in the pockets of South Carolina families.
But I’m not running on my past.
I’m running on what we can accomplish next.
There’s still work to do, and I believe Irmo and Chapin deserve a representative with the experience, relationships, and determination to get it done.
A common-sense conservative.
That’s me.
I’m proud of my record and endorsements.
I’m proud of the trust you’ve placed in me.
And I’d be honored to have your vote to continue serving our community.
Experience matters.
You deserve a voice that gets things done.
Let’s keep moving our state and our community forward.
People, Not Politics, continues to be my focus just like it was when our community first elected me!
Serving you is the honor of a lifetime and one I don’t take it lightly.
Thank you for your continued support, advice, prayers and your vote!
