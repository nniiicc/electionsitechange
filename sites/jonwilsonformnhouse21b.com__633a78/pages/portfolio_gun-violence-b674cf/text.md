Core Principle
I do not believe guns are the root cause of fun violence, and I do not support restrictions that violate the 2nd Amendment.
Law-abiding gun owners are not the problem.
Violence is driven by breakdowns in mental health, warning systems, accountability and prevention — and that’s where we must focus.
Propose
1.
Threat Assessment Teams (Proven and Non-Political)
2.
Strengthen School-Based mental health support
3.
Close the Accountability Gap
4.
School Safety that Works
5.
Community-Based Violence Prevention
6.
Responsible Gun Ownership (Without Mandates)
