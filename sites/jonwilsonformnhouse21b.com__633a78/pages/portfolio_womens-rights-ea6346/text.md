The government should protect the ability of families and doctors to make difficult healthcare choices.
Propose 1.
Protect Choice within the law: enshrine access to safe, legal abortion as they discuss with their supporting group of people 2.
Support Family-Centered Reproductive Health: fund parenting classes, childcare support and family health clinics 3.
Safeguard Conscientious Medical Care: ensure hospitals and doctors can make medical decisions without political interference 4.
Public Education and Prevention: fund community programs to reduce unplanned pregnancies 5.
