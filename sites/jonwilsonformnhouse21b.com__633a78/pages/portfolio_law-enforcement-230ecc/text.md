Support and Strengthen Rural Law Enforcement: Targeted rural staffing grants for counties and small cities 2.

Police Training and Professional Development: expand scenario-based training – emphasize de-escalation and situational awareness for officer safety 5.
Law Enforcement Careers and Pipeline: high school pathways – paid cadet and internship programs 6.
