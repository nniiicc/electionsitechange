Sign Up Today: Marty's Old School Halo Tournament Close About Priorities Join Store Contact Yard Sign Donate Follow Frequently Asked Questions Everything you need to know before the event Where should I stay?
Red Rock Resort and Casino offers on site lodging.
There are additional lodging options nearby and throughout the Vegas Valley.
Where do I pick up my tickets?
Tickets will be picked up at Red Rock Resort and Casino on the day of the event.
More specifics will be sent to attendees via email as the event approaches.
Attendees must bring a valid photo ID to verify their identity.
What is the equipment for the tournament?
The tournament will be played on xbox360s with wired controllers and each participant will have their own monitor.
Can I bring any of my own equipment?
To ensure competitive fairness, the only equipment participants are permitted to bring are their own headset.
Headsets must be compatible with the original xbox360 controller.
Can I bring Halo gear to have signed?
Absolutely!
Marty O’Donnell, Jaime Griesemer, Hardy LeBel, and Tim Dadabo will be around all day to sign autographs and take pictures at no additional charge.
We may limit the number of items an individual may have signed if demand is overwhelming.
What will I be doing if I’m not playing in the tournament or get eliminated?
There will be speeches, panel discussions, and the ability to watch the tournament on the big screens.
Additionally, there is a social gaming corner featuring 16 player LAN split screen available for all attendees to play throughout the event.
Will there be food and drink provided?
There will be a cash bar at the main event on Saturday, October 10th.
Red Rock is home to many restaurants and attendees are encouraged to eat at one of them at their leisure.
Tournament participants will be given a box lunch to ensure that the tournament runs in a timely manner.
The VIP event on October 9th will feature complimentary food and beverage.
Do I have to stay the entire time?
Once you receive your ticket, you can enter and leave the ballroom at your leisure.
Tournament participants will receive a tentative schedule the day of and will be expected to be in the vicinity at all times to play their scheduled matches. still have questions?
