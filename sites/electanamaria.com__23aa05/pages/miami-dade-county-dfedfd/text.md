There is no place Ana Maria would rather call home or raise two children with her husband than South Florida.
Miami-Dade is a place of opportunity, culture, entrepreneurship, and hardworking families, but too many residents are feeling squeezed by rising costs.
Ana Maria’s priority has been, and will continue to be, representing residents and addressing the issues that affect everyday life.
That means working to make housing more affordable, cutting taxes and fees where possible, lowering homeowner’s insurance premiums, keeping communities safe, investing in schools, and supporting a strong local economy.
As your State Representative and State Senator, Ana Maria has supported record funding for Florida’s public school system, empowered parents to make decisions about their children’s education, and championed more affordable, accessible healthcare.
She has fought to keep taxes low, reduce unnecessary regulations on small businesses, and help local employers create good-paying jobs.
In Miami-Dade, the affordability crisis is one of the most urgent challenges facing families, seniors, renters, homeowners, and small businesses.
Ana Maria is focused on practical solutions that put more money back in residents’ pockets: tax relief, property insurance reform, stronger consumer protections, responsible budgeting, and policies that make it easier to build and preserve affordable and workforce housing.

Expand telehealth options for seniors, working families, and vulnerable residents.
