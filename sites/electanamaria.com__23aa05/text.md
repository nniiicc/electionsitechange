Meet Ana Maria Rodriguez
Ana Maria Rodriguez has spent her career advocating for the people of South Florida as a public servant, community leader, and working mother.
She is focused on the issues families talk about around the kitchen table every day: making life more affordable, cutting taxes, lowering homeowner’s insurance premiums, improving schools, expanding access to quality healthcare, and protecting the communities that make Miami-Dade County and Monroe County special.
Ana Maria believes South Florida’s best days are ahead, and she is committed to working across communities to improve the quality of life, strengthen the economy, and put more money back in the pockets of residents.
