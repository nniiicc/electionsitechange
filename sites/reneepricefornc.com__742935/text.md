Good Trouble Lives On
Now is the time to get into more good trouble and necessary trouble for the survival of morality, decency, and humanity.
Across North Carolina, people are rising up and speaking out in opposition to unconscionable legislative acts — in essence, the crimes being perpetrated against our democracy and our freedom.
Collectively, we must strive to preserve and advance the founding principles of our nation, as well as the human and civil rights for which our foremothers and forefathers fought so valiantly.
