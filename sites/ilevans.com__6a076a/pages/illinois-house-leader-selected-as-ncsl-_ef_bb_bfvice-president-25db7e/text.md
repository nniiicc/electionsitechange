Evans, Jr. as their next Vice President, who will subsequently become the President-Elect, then President.
Within the Illinois House of Representatives, Rep.
Evans serves as Assistant Majority Leader and Chairman of the Labor & Commerce Committee.
NCSL is bi-partisan non-political policy focused organization.
Leader Evans has been actively involved in NCSL since 2017, serving on both the State and Local Taxation Taskforce (SALT) and the Executive Committee.
