A third-generation Billings resident, I have proudly raised four children through Billings Public Schools and spent over three decades serving the community that shaped my family.
My leadership includes serving as President of the MetraPark Advisory Board, membership on the Citizen’s Police Advisory Board and Downtown Rotary, and coaching basketball at both Senior and Central High Schools.
Our platform.
- Lower Taxes & Fiscal Discipline – Reducing the tax burden on families and small businesses and opposing wasteful government spending.
- Defending Constitutional Rights – Protecting Second Amendment rights and ensuring law-abiding citizens can own and carry firearms without unnecessary interference.
- Property Rights & Limited Government – Standing firm against government overreach and defending private property.
- Parental Rights in Education – Ensuring parents have the primary voice in their children’s education and opposing policies that sideline families.
