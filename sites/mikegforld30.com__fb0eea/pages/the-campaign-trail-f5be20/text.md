It was great to attend Quartsite and Bouse for their candidate forums the communities had many of the same issues and concerns as most but we discussed unique issues to each community.
As a supporter of rural Arizona I understand and promise to support our way of life and keep the great state of Maricopa from controlling and dictating what is best for our district.
Thank you to Colorado River Republican Women’s group, Pastor Steve and Praise Chapel, Larry Tunforss with his great sense of humor, and Pam Smith for putting on last nights forum in Bullhead City.
It was great the community could have all LD-30 candidates in the same room to ask questions and see the vast differences between us.
I will keep my promise and work hard to continue protecting our communities water resources and keep control in local hands!
It was clear water, education, and infrastructure were the main priorities to the locals as many other communities feel the same way.
With the experience I have received over the last 5 years as a volunteer working in legislature trying to get bills passed and common sense laws passed I will hit the ground running to take care of our communities.
I will never forget where I came from and the original goal of representing your best interests.
Also I will always do my best to be available for you to voice your concerns to ensure you have a voice at the capital.
July 21st is the primary election date and I would be honored to have your vote.
God Bless
Mike Gannuscio
