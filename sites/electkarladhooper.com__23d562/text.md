Welcome to the official website of Karla Daniels Hooper, your candidate for Georgia State Representative, District 113.
For the past 22 years, Karla has been an integral part of the Covington community, actively engaging in local initiatives and serving on esteemed boards such as the Newton County Comprehensive Planning Commission and Newton County Animal Services Board.
Her passion for positive change is evident in her efforts to preserve the integrity of our neighborhoods, from leading petitions against unwanted commercial developments to drafting visionary plans for sustainable growth.
Karla's campaign is driven by a vision of transformation and resilience, advocating for safer neighborhoods, enhanced access to quality food and dining, controlled community development, expanded parks and recreation, and the beautification of our streets and neighborhoods.
She believes in the power of collective action and is committed to turning words into plans, plans into action, and action into tangible results that uplift every resident of District 113.
Join Karla Daniels Hooper in her mission to bring about real change for Newton County.
Together, we can shape a future that honors our values, nurtures our families, and celebrates the potential of our community.
Thank you for your support.
