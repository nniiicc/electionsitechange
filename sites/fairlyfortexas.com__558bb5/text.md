MEET CAROLINE FAIRLY
Faith.
Family.
Conservative leadership.
The cornerstone values of the Texas panhandle have always grounded conservative Republican Caroline Fairly.
Growing up in Amarillo, Caroline and her four siblings were raised with faith in God, hearts for service, and respect for the founding principles that made our nation great.
At Abilene Christian University, she served as Class President and led multiple ventures in the business school’s entrepreneurship program, Wildcat Ventures.
After earning her degree in Business Marketing, Caroline went to work for Republican Ronny Jackson’s campaign for Congress, and then went on with him to Washington to serve his congressional constituents.
Seeing D.C.’s dysfunction up close, she eventually decided to return home to Amarillo to make a difference in the panhandle.
Caroline took a job as a medical negotiator at The OccuNet Company, working to make medical treatment more affordable for Texas families.
Today, she trains professionals at Occunet in the art of complex negotiations, which will prove useful in Austin.
Caroline’s faith and love of service drive her volunteer work with several local ministries and programs, including the Hope Choice Pregnancy Center and Mentoring Programs.
There, she has witnessed firsthand the blessings that unfold when a mother chooses life.
She also mentors West Texas A&M University students at the 1330 Campus Ministry and is active in her church.
Caroline’s conservative values are rooted in her faith: a belief that we are best when serving others, and that those values must inform the debates, decisions and issues we face.
She believes we need to send leaders to Austin who are selfless, God-fearing and bold, and conservative as West Texas itself.
Caroline Fairly will fight to preserve what has made the Texas panhandle an amazing place to call home—a place where we still love our flag, where opportunity abounds, and where values and family still matter.
On the issues
The Conservative Issues Caroline will Fight For
Standing Strong on Constitutional Values
Freedom is non-negotiable, specifically when it comes to our right to practice our religion, own guns, educate our children, and work and live without government control or interference.
There is a powerful social narrative that is trying to chip away at these rights, and many politicians have fallen for it.
Caroline will never waver on our inalienable freedoms which she believes are God-given.
Securing our Border
The federal government has failed to secure our Texas border, and every town in the 87th district is affected by the flow of drugs, guns and people who broke the law in coming here.
Countless women and children are being enslaved and abused by the sophisticated cartels who want to rule our border.
Caroline will fight for additional manpower, innovation, and technology to stop the flow of illegal drugs and weapons, and to defend our sovereignty.
Protecting the Innocence of Children
Caroline will fight to ensure our students are well-educated, never indoctrinated, and that their innocence is protected.
She won’t tolerate radical gender ideology, or inappropriate materials in Texas classrooms and libraries.
Empowering Parents
Parents will always know better than the state what their child needs to be healthy and successful.
Caroline will fight for Texas parents’ rights to make the best education decisions for their own children.
Fighting for the Right to Life
Guided by her faith, Caroline firmly believes that life, born or unborn, must be protected.
Through her work with Hope Choice Pregnancy Center, she has seen firsthand the blessings of expectant mothers choosing life.
Caroline will continue to support and help young mothers, and fight to ensure that Texas continues to be the most pro-life state in the nation.
