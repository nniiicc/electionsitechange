INFORMATION WE COLLECT We may collect information that you provide, including your name, email address, mailing address, mobile phone number, volunteer interests, and the content of messages you send to us.
Payment details submitted through a donation service are processed by that service under its own privacy policy.
We do not store full payment card details on this website.
HOW WE USE INFORMATION We may use information to send campaign updates, volunteer opportunities, event notices, voter information, and election reminders.
We may also use it to respond to questions, operate and secure our website, improve our communications, and comply with legal duties.
Donations will not be solicited through our SMS program.
HOW WE PROTECT AND SHARE INFORMATION Friends of Darius Kila maintains strict privacy policies, ensuring that personal information of our users and members is not sold, rented, released, or traded to others without prior consent or a legal obligation.
Personal information includes name, email address, phone number, and other contact information.
We may provide information to vendors that perform services for us, such as website hosting, form processing, analytics, and message delivery.
Those vendors may use the information only to provide services to us and must protect it.
We may also disclose information when required by law or when needed to protect the Committee, our website, or other people.
Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.
Text messaging opt in data and consent will not be sold, rented, or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
SMS OPT OUT If you are receiving text messages from us and wish to stop receiving them, simply respond with “STOP” to the number from which you received the message.
Once we receive your message, you will no longer receive further text messages from us.
