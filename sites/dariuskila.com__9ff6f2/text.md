ABOUT DARIUS
MEET REPRESENTATIVE DARIUS KILA
Born and raised on the Waiʻanae Coast, Representative Darius Kila understands firsthand the challenges West Oʻahu families face every day.
Since being elected to represent House District 44 (Honokai Hale, Nānākuli, Māʻili) in 2022, he has remained focused on creating meaningful, lasting change that reflects the voices and needs of his community.
As one of seven children, he grew up in a family that never had the opportunity to own a home.
He knows what it means to work hard and still struggle to find stability in the place you love.
Those experiences drive his advocacy for housing local families can truly afford, safer roads, stronger schools, and more opportunities for keiki to succeed here at home.
Darius pursued higher education at Lane College in Tennessee, Fullerton College in California, and Southern Oregon University — broadening his perspective while reinforcing his commitment to return home and serve the community that raised him.
“I was born and raised here on the Waiʻanae Coast, and this community has shaped who I am.
The lessons I learned growing up — about hard work, resilience, and looking out for one another — continue to guide me every day in public service.
There is still more to do.
I remain committed to building a stronger, more stable future for West Oʻahu — one where our keiki and kūpuna have opportunities, our families can afford to stay, and our communities continue to thrive.”
– Darius Kila

Worked with DHHL to reduce the Native Hawaiian Homestead Waitlist.
PUBLIC SAFETY & ROADS
Advanced Legislation to update vehicle identification, enforcement laws, and clearer towing practices.

Campaign Priorities
Rooted In Service.
Focused On What Matters.
Representative Kila’s campaign is grounded in a simple commitment: to continue serving West Oʻahu by focusing on the everyday challenges our families face and delivering real, practical solutions.
01
Safer Roads, Safer Communitues
For many West Oʻahu families, getting to work, school, or home safely isn’t always guaranteed.
Darius is committed to improving traffic safety through smarter infrastructure investments, better planning, and continued advocacy for resources that keep our roads safe and accessible for everyone.
02
MAKING WEST OʻAHU MORE AFFORDABLE
The rising cost of living continues to put pressure on local families.
Darius will continue working to support policies that make Hawaiʻi more affordable — focusing on housing, economic opportunity, and ensuring that working families have a fair shot at building a future here at home.
03
ADVANCING OPPORTUNITIES FOR NATIVE HAWAIIANS
As one of the leading Native Hawaiian voices in the Hawaiʻi State Legislature, Representative Kila understands the responsibility to advocate for the lāhui at every level — from protecting ʻike Hawaiʻi to reducing the DHHL waitlist and getting more families into homes.
04
STRONGER COMMUNITIES, BETTER QUALITY OF LIFE
At its core, this work is about improving quality of life — making sure our communities are safe, our keiki have opportunities, and our neighborhoods have the resources they need to thrive.
From investing in schools and community spaces to strengthening public safety and access to services.
