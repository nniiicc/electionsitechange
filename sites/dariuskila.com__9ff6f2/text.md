Since being elected to represent House District 44 (Honokai Hale, Nānākuli, Māʻili) in 2022, he has remained focused on creating meaningful, lasting change that reflects the voices and needs of his community.
As one of seven children, he grew up in a family that never had the opportunity to own a home.
He knows what it means to work hard and still struggle to find stability in the place you love.
Those experiences drive his advocacy for housing local families can truly afford, safer roads, stronger schools, and more opportunities for keiki to succeed here at home.
Darius pursued higher education at Lane College in Tennessee, Fullerton College in California, and Southern Oregon University — broadening his perspective while reinforcing his commitment to return home and serve the community that raised him.

Focused On What Matters.
Representative Kila’s campaign is grounded in a simple commitment: to continue serving West Oʻahu by focusing on the everyday challenges our families face and delivering real, practical solutions.
01 Safer Roads, Safer Communitues For many West Oʻahu families, getting to work, school, or home safely isn’t always guaranteed.
Darius is committed to improving traffic safety through smarter infrastructure investments, better planning, and continued advocacy for resources that keep our roads safe and accessible for everyone.
02 MAKING WEST OʻAHU MORE AFFORDABLE The rising cost of living continues to put pressure on local families.
Darius will continue working to support policies that make Hawaiʻi more affordable — focusing on housing, economic opportunity, and ensuring that working families have a fair shot at building a future here at home.
03 ADVANCING OPPORTUNITIES FOR NATIVE HAWAIIANS As one of the leading Native Hawaiian voices in the Hawaiʻi State Legislature, Representative Kila understands the responsibility to advocate for the lāhui at every level — from protecting ʻike Hawaiʻi to reducing the DHHL waitlist and getting more families into homes.
04 STRONGER COMMUNITIES, BETTER QUALITY OF LIFE At its core, this work is about improving quality of life — making sure our communities are safe, our keiki have opportunities, and our neighborhoods have the resources they need to thrive.
