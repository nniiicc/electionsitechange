Speaker Tim Moore Raises $1 Million in First Month of Congressional Campaign
December 6, 2023
“The outpouring of financial support our campaign has received in just a few short weeks is astonishing.
I’m honored by the response and momentum we’ve seen in the race so far.
We will not slow down; we’re just getting started,” said Speaker Moore.
“Because of Speaker Moore’s hard work, the campaign has surpassed their fundraising goal for the entire year.
With three major events still on the calendar, I expect the campaign will set a new state fundraising record for a first-time congressional candidate,” said Paul Shumaker, Tim Moore’s chief political advisor.
Moore resides in Kings Mountain.
He is the father of two sons, McRae and Wilson.
