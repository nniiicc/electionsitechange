“Today’s filing marks the official start of our campaign to restore true conservative leadership to the great people of NC-14.
I’m honored by the outpouring of support my candidacy has already received from patriots who are ready for a change in Washington.
They deserve a fighter in Congress with a proven record of delivering a conservative agenda for the people of North Carolina, and I cannot wait to be their voice in the People’s House,” said Speaker Moore.
Moore resides in Kings Mountain.
He is the father of two sons, McRae and Wilson.
