North Carolina Troopers Association Endorses Tim Moore for U.S.
Congress
December 8, 2023
Kings Mountain, N.C. — Today, The North Carolina Troopers Association announced their endorsement of Speaker Tim Moore for the U.S.
House of Representatives.
“Tim Moore has a proven track record of serving the citizens of North Carolina with pride and integrity.
Under Speaker Moore’s leadership, our state has seen unprecedented economic growth and passing of crucial legislation to help the citizens of North Carolina we strongly believe he can do the same in Congress.
Speaker Moore has a long history of supporting Law Enforcement by working to pass legislation that make our communities safe and protected,” said NCTA President Ben Kral.
“I am honored to accept this endorsement from the North Carolina Troopers Association.
The brave men and women of our state’s law enforcement, including the dedicated troopers who safeguard our communities, deserve our unwavering support.
I am grateful for the Association’s endorsement and promise to never waver in my support of law enforcement when I am elected to Congress,” said Speaker Tim Moore.
The North Carolina Troopers Association’s full press release may be found HERE.
Moore resides in Kings Mountain.
He is the father of two sons, McRae and Wilson.
