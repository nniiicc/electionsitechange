House of Representatives.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Republican District Attorney’s serving in our communities here in the 14th congressional district .
Their steadfast commitment to public safety and the rule of law is critical to the safety and security of our citizens and I am grateful for their support,” said Speaker Tim Moore.
Moore resides in Kings Mountain.
He is the father of two sons, McRae and Wilson.
