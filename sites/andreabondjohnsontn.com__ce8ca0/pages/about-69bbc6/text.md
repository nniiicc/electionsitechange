Donate Andrea Bond Johnson: A Voice for District 80 Andrea Bond Johnson is a lifelong advocate, community leader, business owner, wife, mother, and public servant who believes government should work for the people it serves.
As a candidate for the Tennessee House of Representatives, District 80, Andrea is running to bring practical leadership, accountability, and a strong commitment to the families, workers, seniors, farmers, educators, and small businesses that make our communities strong.
A Life Built on Tennessee Values Born and raised with the values of faith, hard work, and service, Andrea has spent her life helping others solve problems and build opportunities.
As the owner of a successful insurance agency, she understands the challenges families and businesses face every day—from rising costs and access to healthcare to economic uncertainty and the need for quality education.
A Proven Record of Community Leadership Andrea’s record of service extends far beyond her professional career.
She has dedicated countless hours to civic engagement, economic development, community improvement, and advocacy efforts throughout West Tennessee.
Her leadership has always been grounded in listening to people, bringing diverse voices together, and finding common-sense solutions that move communities forward.
Committed to Strengthening Our Communities Guided by a desire to serve, Andrea has worked alongside countless volunteers and community leaders to address issues affecting families, strengthen neighborhoods, and expand opportunities for future generations .
Her commitment to service reflects a belief that real leadership begins with showing up, listening, and taking action.
As State Representative, Andrea will focus on the issues that matter most to District 80: Lowering the cost of living for working families Expanding access to quality healthcare Supporting public education and workforce development Growing economic opportunities in rural and underserved communities Strengthening public safety and community well-being Ensuring government remains accountable to the people Andrea believes that leadership is not about politics—it’s about people.
She is committed to being accessible, transparent, and responsive to every community in District 80, including Haywood County, Hardeman County, and the Madison County communities she seeks to represent.
