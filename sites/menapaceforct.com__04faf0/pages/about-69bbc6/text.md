ABOUT NICK Nick Menapace, a lifelong resident of Connecticut and local educator.
Public service is something that Nick has chosen throughout his life: from being an Eagle Scout and in spending the last ten years teaching in diverse communities throughout the state.
Nick ran for State Representative in 2022 and has continued to stay involved in Southeast Connecticut.
It's the culmination of this experience, specifically in working with Connecticut students and families, that has motivated Nick to take his passion for civic engagement to represent East Lyme, Salem, and Montville in Hartford.
Nick and his wife Stephanie, an engineer, live in East Lyme with their dog Minnie that they both love dearly.
