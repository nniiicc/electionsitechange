We The People: Celebrating the Alamance Piecemakers Quilt Guild at Burlington City Hall
If you visit the Burlington City Hall foyer this month, you will see a remarkable piece of local artistry: the USA 250th Anniversary "We The People" Quilt crafted by the talented members of the Alamance Piecemakers Quilt Guild.
Growing up with a mother who is a quilter, I’ve seen firsthand the countless hours, meticulous precision, and sheer patience poured into every stitch of a project like this.
But what makes this showcase truly special goes beyond the vibrant patchwork on the front—it is the living story recorded on the back.
A Tapestry of Lived Experiences
When you look at the back of the quilt, you will find personal inscriptions, inspiring quotes, and a map listing all the different states where these quilters have lived throughout their lives.
This display serves as a powerful reminder of how our community thrives:
- Welcoming New Perspectives: Alamance County is continually enriched by people bringing distinct talents, backgrounds, and histories together from across the nation and the globe.
- Building Community Together: Whether your family has lived here for generations or you chose to make Burlington your home like I did, every neighbor contributes a vital thread to our shared civic fabric.
- Honoring Local Artistry: Community guilds preserve traditional craftsmanship while fostering intergenerational connections that keep our civic spaces vibrant.
Thank you to the Alamance Piecemakers Quilt Guild for sharing your time and creativity with our city!
