Ian Baltutis
Democratic Candidate for NC House 63
Ian Baltutis is a Master City Planner and entrepreneur with over a decade of public service to Alamance County.
From 2015 to 2021, Ian served as Burlington's youngest-ever mayor for three terms.
He led downtown revitalization, expanded parks, and launched the LINK Transit regional bus system.
After his time as mayor, he earned a Master's in City and Regional Planning from UNC-Chapel Hill so he could bring real technical expertise to the toughest budget, housing, and infrastructure challenges.
Today he serves on the Burlington City Council.
Ian has a unique combination of executive experience, specialized technical expertise, and a steadfast commitment to community-led governance.
Ian's leadership philosophy is rooted in a nuanced understanding of civic engagement.
He believes that true representation goes beyond the ballot box -- it requires a government that actively removes barriers to participation and empowers residents to co-design the future of their neighborhoods.
Ian is dedicated to supporting affordable communities, accountable government, and a North Carolina where we all belong.
Electing Ian means sending to Raleigh a hands-on, common-sense leader who is fiscally sound, strategically minded, and committed to making sure every voice is heard.
Committed to Community.
Outside of City Hall, Ian puts his values into practice as the owner of Persnickety Books, Alamance County's only independent bookstore and a vibrant community gathering place.
As part of his efforts to revitalize main streets, Ian was a key founder of Burlington Beer Works, a cooperatively owned brewery and restaurant in downtown Burlington, NC.
Ian is a pilot with the North Carolina Civil Air Patrol, the official civilian auxiliary of the U.S.
Air Force, and an Eagle Scout.
He is restoring a 1912 home and serves on the board of Preservation Alamance, which seeks to protect and celebrate the architectural and historical heritage of Alamance County.
Ian also co-founded a product design and manufacturing company right out of his Elon University dorm room.
The Vibration Solution, LLC specializes in ultra-soft Sorbothane polyurethane components for vibration isolation, impact reduction, and equipment protection.
He lives in Burlington with his partner and their family of pets.
