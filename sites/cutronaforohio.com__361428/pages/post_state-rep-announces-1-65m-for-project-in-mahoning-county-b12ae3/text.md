State Rep. announces $1.65M for project in Mahoning County
Team Cutrona
Jun 7, 2024
COLUMBUS, Ohio (WKBN) – State Representative Al Cutrona (R-Canfield) announced Tuesday that $1,651,370 has been awarded for a project in Mahoning County.
The funds will go towards construction at the Ohio State Penitentiary in Youngstown.
“These funds will help update facilities and ensure they operate in a healthy and safe manner,” said Cutrona.
“Investing in these renovations now saves taxpayer dollars and prevents larger, costlier problems down the road.”
The State Controlling Board approved the funding at a meeting on Monday.
The next meeting is scheduled for June 17.
