MICHAEL RULLI
Congressman Michael Rulli was born and raised in the Mahoning Valley.
He understands the struggles Ohio families are facing and what hard work means.
Over 100 years ago, Michael's family founded Rulli Brothers Market, which he and his brother still run today.
In 2018 he was elected to the Ohio State Senate as a voice for our values, prior to that he served as Leetonia School Board President, where Michael led the school system out of financial chaos without raising taxes!
