Everything you need to make your voting plan for the November 3, 2026 General Election, all in one place. 🗳️ Vote Early In South Dakota, early voting is officially called in-person absentee voting .
You do not need to wait until Election Day to cast your ballot.
Address: Pennington County Auditor’s Office County Administration Building 900 Concourse Drive, Suite 130 Rapid City, SD 57703 Hours: Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Bring a valid photo ID.
If you do not have one, you may sign a personal identification affidavit and vote a regular ballot.
Questions?
Call the Pennington County Auditor’s Office at 605-394-2153 .
Learn More About Early Voting → ✅ Make Sure You’re Registered The voter registration deadline for the November 3 General Election is October 19, 2026 .
Already registered?
It never hurts to double-check.
South Dakota’s Voter Information Portal lets you confirm your registration information and make sure everything is ready before you vote.
Need to register or update your name, address, or other information?
Complete a South Dakota voter registration form and return it to your county auditor.
Important for new voters: South Dakota changed its voter registration requirements in 2026.
New registrants must provide acceptable proof of U.S. citizenship to be registered for a full ballot.
Check My Voter Registration → Register to Vote → 👀 See What’s on Your Ballot Want to know what you’ll be voting on before you get to the voting booth?
Use South Dakota’s Voter Information Portal to look up your personalized sample ballot for the General Election.
Your ballot is based on where you are registered to vote, so this is the best way to see the races and questions that will appear on your ballot.
The portal can also show your polling place and let you track the status of an absentee ballot.
