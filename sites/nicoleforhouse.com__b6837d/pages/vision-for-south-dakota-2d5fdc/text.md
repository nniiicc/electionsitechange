Vision for South Dakota
I believe South Dakota can protect what makes our state special while building a stronger future for the people who live here.
That takes listening, doing the work, and finding practical solutions that can actually move forward.
In Pierre, I have worked to protect living organ donors, strengthen protections for survivors of abuse and trafficking, and create sensible pathways for local businesses to innovate.
I bring that same practical approach to the priorities that matter most to District 32.
Strong Schools and Strong Families
As an educator, I know strong communities begin with strong public schools.
I am working for competitive teacher pay, safe and modern learning spaces, and smarter ways for school districts to finance major projects without placing unnecessary pressure on property taxpayers.
I also support affordable child care, lower everyday costs, and practical policies that help parents work, children thrive, and families build a future in South Dakota.
Protecting the Black Hills
The Black Hills are our home, our water source, and a foundation of our economy and way of life.
I support stronger mining accountability, responsible development, and long-term protections for our land and water.
We can support jobs and economic growth without sacrificing the resources future generations will depend on.
Local Opportunity and Small Businesses
South Dakota should be a place where people can afford to stay, start a business, and build a life.
I support small businesses, local entrepreneurs, workforce development, and community-led solutions to housing and infrastructure challenges.
Government should set clear, responsible standards while giving people room to solve problems, invest in their communities, and create opportunity.
Accountable, Balanced Government
Good government requires transparency, competition, and leaders who are willing to listen.
I believe South Dakota is stronger when no party or politician is allowed to take voters for granted.
I will continue working across differences, asking hard questions, and bringing an independent voice from the classroom to the Capitol.
Practical Leadership.
Real Progress.
I do not believe every problem has an easy answer.
But I do believe progress is possible when we focus on the work instead of the noise.
My priorities are clear: strengthen schools and families, protect the Black Hills, support local opportunity, and make state government more accountable.
That is the future I am working toward for District 32 and for South Dakota.
