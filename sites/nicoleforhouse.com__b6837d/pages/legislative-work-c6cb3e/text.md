See Voting Record → Three bills were signed into law, two resolutions were adopted, and four additional proposals helped move important conversations forward.
Signed Into Law HB 1101: Living Donor Protection Act Signed into law Protects living organ donors from long-term insurance discrimination.
South Dakotans who make the extraordinary decision to donate an organ should not face unfair consequences when seeking life, disability, or long-term care insurance.
View Official Bill → HB 1299: Natural Pools for Small Lodging Establishments Signed into law Allows qualifying small lodging establishments to use alternative pool filtration systems while maintaining appropriate permitting, water-quality, inspection, and public-safety standards.
View Official Bill → HB 1300: Clemency Notice Reform for Survivors Signed into law Creates a safer clemency process for qualifying survivors of human trafficking and domestic abuse when publishing their personal information could put them at risk, while preserving required notification for victims.
View Official Bill → Resolutions Adopted HCR 6003: Black Hills Watershed Collaborative Recognition Adopted Recognizes the importance of collaborative watershed restoration efforts in the Black Hills and the work being done to improve water resilience, restore landscapes, and reduce wildfire risk.
View Official Bill → SCR 605: Monarch and Pollinator Habitat Support Adopted Supports voluntary efforts to protect monarch butterflies and other pollinators while recognizing the important role that agriculture, landowners, and responsible land management play in conservation.
View Official Bill → Work To Continue HB 1204: School Construction Loan Fund Did not advance in Joint Appropriations Proposed a zero-interest loan fund to help school districts finance construction projects and reduce their reliance on local property-tax opt-outs.
Although it did not advance, the proposal continues to inform conversations about school infrastructure and property taxes.
View Official Bill → HB 1205: Education Funding Formula Adjustment Tabled in Joint Appropriations Would have increased school funding each year by either 3 percent or the rate of inflation, whichever was greater.
The proposal highlighted the need to better understand and refine how South Dakota’s funding formula responds to rising costs.
View Official Bill → HB 1273: Revision of Mining Statutes Did not advance in committee Proposed updates to mining oversight in the Black Hills, with a focus on greater clarity in state law and the long-term protection of water resources.
Future efforts may focus on more targeted changes involving resources such as limestone and lithium.
View Official Bill → SB 7: Gold Severance Tax Adjustment Did not advance in committee Proposed updating the tax imposed on gold extracted in South Dakota to better reflect current economic conditions and the value of the state’s natural resources.
