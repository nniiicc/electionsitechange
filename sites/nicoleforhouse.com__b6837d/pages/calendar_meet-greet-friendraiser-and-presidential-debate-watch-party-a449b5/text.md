Event Details
You're invited to join us for an evening of community, conversation, and politics at our "Meet & Greet Friendraiser and Presidential Debate Watch Party." This event offers a wonderful opportunity to connect with fellow community members, enjoy some great food and drinks, and engage in meaningful discussions.
The evening will begin at 5:00 PM with our Meet & Greet "Friendraiser," where you'll have the chance to mingle with local candidates and other guests.
This portion of the event will run until 7:00 PM, providing ample time to make new connections and strengthen existing ones.
At 7:00 PM, we'll transition into our Presidential Debate Viewing Party.
Settle in with a drink and some food as we watch the debate together and share our thoughts on the candidates and issues.
The viewing party will conclude around 8:30 PM.
The event will be hosted at the home of former South Dakota Senator Tom Katus, located at 821 Upper Pines Dr. in Rapid City.
To get there, simply take Kansas City Street west from downtown until it ends and turns into a gravel road.
Continue up the hill, and you'll find 821 Upper Pines at the very end, the last house on the road.
We hope you'll join us for what promises to be an enjoyable and engaging evening!
