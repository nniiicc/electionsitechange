Why should I contact my representatives?
I read every email from my constituents, and I believe other legislators do too, so let us know where you stand and how it impacts South Dakota—if one of them represents your district, be sure to call that out.

Do legislators actually read my email?
I read every email I receive and do my best to respond to everyone—I believe most legislators do the same.
Take Horse Dentistry, for example.
We were flooded with emails, and it had a real impact.
It caught us off guard, and suddenly, everyone was talking about it.
When the Governor proposed defunding SDPB, constituents spoke up loud and clear.
When enough people raise their voices, it truly makes a difference!
What should I write? ✅ Include the bill number in the subject line to ensure your message is easily identified. ✅ Make it personal —explain in your own words how the bill affects South Dakota and why it matters to you. ✅ State your connection —if you’re a constituent, mention that.
Lawmakers pay close attention to messages from people in their district. ✅ Keep it clear, respectful, and direct —a concise, well-reasoned message is more likely to be read and considered.
