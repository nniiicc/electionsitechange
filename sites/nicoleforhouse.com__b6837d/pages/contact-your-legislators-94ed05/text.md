- ✅ Include the bill number in the subject line to ensure your message is easily identified.
✅ Make it personal—explain in your own words how the bill affects South Dakota and why it matters to you.
✅ State your connection—if you’re a constituent, mention that.
Lawmakers pay close attention to messages from people in their district.
✅ Keep it clear, respectful, and direct—a concise, well-reasoned message is more likely to be read and considered.
