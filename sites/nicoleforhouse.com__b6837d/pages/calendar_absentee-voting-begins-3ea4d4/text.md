Voting Absentee in Pennington County A new absentee ballot application must be completed each calendar year .
Vote by mail: Complete a South Dakota Absentee Ballot Application and return the original, signed application to: Pennington County Auditor PO Box 6160 Rapid City, SD 57709-6160 Your original signature and date are required.
Electronic signatures are not accepted.
