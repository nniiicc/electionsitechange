Absentee voting begins today for South Dakota’s November 3, 2026 General Election.
If you plan to vote before Election Day, absentee ballots are now available through your county election official.
Voting Absentee in Pennington County
A new absentee ballot application must be completed each calendar year.
Vote by mail: Complete a South Dakota Absentee Ballot Application and return the original, signed application to:
Pennington County Auditor
PO Box 6160
Rapid City, SD 57709-6160
Your original signature and date are required.
Electronic signatures are not accepted.

Special absentee voting options are available for military members, their dependents, and U.S. citizens living overseas.
Already Requested a Ballot?
You can check the status of your absentee ballot request through South Dakota’s Voter Information Portal.
