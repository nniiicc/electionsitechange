Event Details
Come meet Nicole, candidate for the South Dakota House of Representatives in District 32, at a "Meet the Candidate" gathering on Tuesday, September 17th, from 5:30pm to 7:30pm.
This event will be hosted at 2 Saint Patrick St in Rapid City.
It’s a great opportunity to get to know Nicole, hear her vision for our community, and ask any questions you might have—all in a relaxed, welcoming setting.
Whether you’re a longtime supporter or just curious, everyone is invited to join.
Nicole brings over ten years of experience as a teacher and currently mentors educators across South Dakota and Wyoming.
She’s eager to listen, share her ideas, and connect with you personally.
Snacks will be provided, so feel free to stop by, enjoy some good conversation, and be part of the discussion about our community’s future!
RSVP is appreciated but not required.
