In-person early voting begins Friday, September 18th, for South Dakota’s November 3, 2026 General Election.
In South Dakota, early voting is officially conducted as in-person absentee voting.
Registered voters can go to their county auditor’s office, request an absentee ballot, complete it, and vote in person before Election Day.
Early Voting in Pennington County
Beginning September 18, voters can cast an absentee ballot in person at:
Pennington County Auditor’s Office
County Administration Building
900 Concourse Drive, Suite 130
Rapid City, SD 57703
Hours: Monday through Friday, 7:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
Bring a valid photo ID.
If you do not have a photo ID, South Dakota provides the option to sign a personal identification affidavit and vote a regular ballot.
For questions about absentee or in-person early voting, contact the Pennington County Auditor’s Office at 605-394-2153.
Prefer to Vote by Mail?
You can also request an absentee ballot and have it mailed to you.
A new absentee ballot application must be completed each calendar year.
