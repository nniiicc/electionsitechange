South Dakota Needs Justice Reform, Not Just Another Prison
South Dakota needs justice reform, not just another prison.
I never supported the new prison plan, but our current facility is inhumane and overcrowded.
Now that the funding has failed, we must fix the sentencing laws that put so many people there in the first place.
I’m backing SB 83 to prioritize treatment over incarceration for nonviolent drug offenses—because we can’t wait for the system to collapse before taking action.
