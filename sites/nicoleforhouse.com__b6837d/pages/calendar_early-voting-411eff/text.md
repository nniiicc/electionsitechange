Early voting is now open in Rapid City!
Early voting is now open in Rapid City! 🗳️ Swing by the Pennington County Administration building at 130 Kansas City St., Suite 230, to cast your vote.
It’s super easy—just bring your ID and follow the signs inside.
Voting hours are Monday through Thursday, 7 a.m. to 6 p.m.
(closed on holidays).
Need help checking your voter registration, finding your polling place for Tuesday, November 5th, or getting registered to vote?
We’ve got you covered at https://www.nicoleforhouse.com/voting-resources to make sure your vote counts!
