Accessibility Statement
Effective Date: 07.01.2025
Christy MacPherson Campaign is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities.
We are continually working to improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.
What We’re Doing:
- Striving for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
- Ensuring alt text for images, logical heading structure, and high contrast
- Testing keyboard navigation across sections
- Regular reviews and updates of accessibility features
