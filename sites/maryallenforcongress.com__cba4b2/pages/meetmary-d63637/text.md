Meet Mary
Mary has called Indiana home for more than 30 years.
After leaving for military training, she returned to Evansville to raise her three daughters in the community she loves.
Today, she and her husband, Greg, live in downtown Evansville, where they enjoy time with their two grandchildren.
Mary currently serves as an At-Large City Councilor, where she serves as Vice President.
She serves on the County's Solid Waste Board and the board of the Evansville Metropolitan Planning Organization.
Mary has been a small business owner for more than 15 years.
She owned Sixth and Zero until November of 2025.
And she and her husband Greg co-own Virginia Street Properties, where they have rehabbed several homes to support neighborhood improvements.
Service has always been at the heart of Mary’s life.
She has invested decades in strengthening her community—serving 10 years in a nonprofit urban ministry, creating a neighborhood garden, and investing in mental health.
Today, she chairs the board of the Enterprise Zone, working to revitalize distressed communities.
Mary is running for Congress to bring people together, solve real problems, and make sure the next generation has more opportunities—not fewer.
