Based in Pasadena, Texas, he has spent more than a decade building businesses, creating jobs, and investing in projects that strengthen local families and neighborhoods.
As the owner of GS Advanced Industry, David has led large-scale construction and industrial projects, tenant improvements, and international import-export initiatives, earning a reputation for reliability, vision, and hands-on leadership.
Raised with strong family values, David believes that success is measured not only by business achievements but by the positive impact made on others.
Throughout his career he has partnered with workers, contractors, and community members from all backgrounds, always emphasizing respect, opportunity, and integrity.
His approach to business reflects his personal philosophy: treat people fairly, keep your word, and build something that lasts beyond yourself.
David has been deeply involved in civic life in Pasadena and across Texas.
He has worked alongside local leaders, supported community programs, and advocated for policies that protect families, encourage economic growth, and defend Texas values.
His commitment to public service led him to step forward as a candidate for public office, driven by the belief that everyday citizens deserve a strong voice and practical solutions from leaders who understand real-world challenges.
Above all, David is a father and family man who strives to lead by example.
He speaks often about perseverance, faith, and the responsibility to help the next generation succeed.
Whether in business, community service, or personal life, he is guided by a simple principle: work hard, serve others, and leave things better than you found them.
