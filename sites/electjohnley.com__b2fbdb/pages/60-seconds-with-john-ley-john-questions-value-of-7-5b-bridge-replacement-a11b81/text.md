Home News Latest News 60 Seconds With John Ley About Me Donate 60 Seconds With John Ley Issues 60 Seconds with John Ley • John Questions Value of $7.5B Bridge Replacement September 29, 2024 by John Ley In a recent discussion at the Tualatin Chamber of Commerce’s Multi Chamber Transportation Round Table, John Ley raised a critical question about the I-5 Interstate Bridge Replacement Project.
The $7.5 billion plan intends to replace the current 3-lane bridge with another 3-lane bridge, which will still be congested on the day it opens.
Ley points out that by 2045, morning commute times from Salmon Creek to the Fremont Bridge are predicted to double, and half of the rush-hour traffic will be stuck moving at 0-20 mph.
Ley calls out Oregon Rep.
Susan McLain for providing a political answer filled with half-truths about improvements, highlighting that the Rose Quarter is currently stalled and will likely offer minimal relief.
Where’s the value in such a costly project when it won’t solve the core issue of traffic congestion?
Transcript: Good morning, John Ley, Clark County.
