Peter Abbarno explained why Mittelman’s experience and practical approach make her a strong fit for downtown Vancouver Washington state Rep.
Peter Abbarno praised Sarah Mittelman’s campaign for the 49th Legislative District during a Friday appearance on KELA/KMNT Radio’s Let’s Talk About It .
“Sarah Mittelman is the perfect candidate for the 49th,” Abbarno said.
“She fits the district to a tee.” Mittelman, a board-certified psychiatric mental health nurse practitioner with more than 20 years of health care experience, is running for the Washington House of Representatives in the 49th Legislative District, Position 1.
Her campaign is focused on affordability, public safety, behavioral health, transportation and greater accountability in government.
Abbarno highlighted Mittelman while discussing the work of Cascade PAC, an organization supporting candidates in races throughout Washington.
He argued that successful candidates must genuinely understand and reflect the communities they hope to represent.
The politics of downtown Vancouver, he noted, are different from those of the 20th Legislative District, which Abbarno represents.
Applying the same political checklist to every district is not a winning strategy.
Instead, Abbarno said political organizations should ask whether a candidate fits the district, can earn the trust of its voters and will make a positive contribution to the Legislature.
For Abbarno, Mittelman checks those boxes.
He described her as someone who would be good for both the voters of the 49th District and the Washington Legislature.
Abbarno also explained that Cascade PAC works with candidates at the city, county and state levels, helping promising leaders communicate with voters through grassroots outreach, radio, digital media and direct mail.
