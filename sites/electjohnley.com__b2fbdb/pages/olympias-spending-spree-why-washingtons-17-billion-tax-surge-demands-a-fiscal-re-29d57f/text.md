Home News Latest News 60 Seconds With John Ley About Me Donate Issues Olympia’s Spending Spree: Why Washington’s $17 Billion Tax Surge Demands a Fiscal Reset October 7, 2026 by John Ley Every family and small business in Washington knows the basic rule of budgeting: you cannot spend money you do not have.
When household costs rise, families prioritize essentials, cut back on luxuries, and stretch every dollar.
Unfortunately, state government in Olympia operates under a completely different set of rules.
Over the past two years alone, Olympia Democrats have increased taxes on Washington citizens and businesses by a staggering $17 billion.
Over the last four to six years, state spending has expanded by $15 billion to fund new government programs and policy mandates.
State spending has grown three times faster than average household income.
Washington does not have a revenue problem—it has a spending problem.
The $17 Billion Reality Check When Olympia levies $17 billion in tax hikes, every resident feels the pain: ● At the Gas Pump & Grocery Aisle: Energy regulations, carbon taxes, and payroll fees filter directly into higher retail prices. ● On Small Businesses: Main Street employers face escalating taxes and regulatory compliance costs, squeezing profit margins and stalling wage growth. ● On Family Budgets: Paychecks are stretched thinner as state mandates drive up the baseline cost of living across the 18th Legislative District and statewide.
12,000 New Bureaucrats: Exploding the State Payroll Where is all this revenue going?
Beyond funding new programs, state government has undergone an unprecedented administrative expansion.
Over the last four to five years, the executive branch under the governor’s administration has added over 12,000 new state employees.
Adding 12,000 personnel creates a permanent, compounding financial commitment.
Salaries, pensions, healthcare, and administrative overhead add hundreds of millions—and ultimately billions—to the state’s baseline budget every single year.
While access to public services is important, government cannot expand beyond the ability of taxpayers to foot the bill.
A Common-Sense Plan: Cap Budget Growth, Protect Working Families To restore fiscal balance, Representative John Ley is championing a common-sense approach to state budgeting: 1.
Cap State Budget Growth: Tie spending increases directly to median household income growth and population metrics so government never grows faster than the families paying for it.
2.
Audit State Programs: Conduct rigorous performance reviews to eliminate redundant administrative positions and streamline state operations.
3.
Provide Tax Relief: Stop continuous tax hikes and return excess revenue to working families and job creators.
The Choice This November Washingtonians are facing an affordability crisis driven directly by decisions made in Olympia.
The path forward requires accountability, restraint, and leadership willing to stand up against unchecked fiscal expansion.
“You can’t afford the demands of Olympia Democrats.
You can’t afford your tax bill.
Your employers can’t afford it… As much as we would love unlimited government services, you can’t afford the bill that goes with them.
I’m fighting for common sense spending in Olympia.” — WA State Representative John Ley (LD18) Help protect your paycheck and bring fiscal accountability back to our state capital.
