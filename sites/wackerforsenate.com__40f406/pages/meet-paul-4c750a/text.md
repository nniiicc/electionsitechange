He was born and raised in north central Iowa.
As a fourth-generation Iowan, Paul’s family has deep ties to Iowa’s rural landscape and our agricultural foundations. ​ Paul farmed by Klemme for eight years.
He attended North Iowa Community College and Iowa State University.
Recently retired, Paul worked for UPS over thirty years.
In addition to his work at UPS, Paul served as Vice President of Do Right Inc. for thirty plus years.
Do Right Inc provides sound and lighting for Christian Events.

He is committed to preserving what makes Iowa a truly unique and remarkable place to call home. ​ Paul is asking for your vote to be the next Senator for Iowa Senate District 25.
