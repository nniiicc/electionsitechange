Issues
Strengthen Jobs & the Economy
Chris believes Michigan’s future depends on creating an economy that rewards hard work and innovation.
Too often, government overregulation and high taxes drive jobs out of our state.
Chris supports cutting red tape, lowering the tax burden, and making Michigan a place where small businesses and manufacturers can grow and hire local workers.
Strong families depend on strong paychecks, and Chris will always fight for policies that put Michigan workers first, attract new industries, and keep our best talent here at home.
A healthy economy is built by the people, not government.
Supporting the 2nd Amendment
Chris supports the Second Amendment as a fundamental right.
Law-abiding citizens should be able to own firearms for self-defense, hunting, and protection against government overreach.
While Chris believes in enforcing existing laws to keep guns out of the hands of criminals, he opposes new restrictions that punish responsible gun owners for the actions of criminals.
100% Pro-Life
Chris is pro-life because every child has the right to life.
Chris believes in protecting the unborn while also promoting resources for mothers and families, so no woman feels abortion is her only option.
These beliefs are rooted in valuing both life and compassion, standing for the most vulnerable among us.
Fix Our Roads
Fixing Michigan’s roads is not just about spending more money—it’s about spending money wisely.
For too long, Lansing has raised taxes and promised results, yet our roads are still crumbling.
Chris supports directing funds strictly to infrastructure, cutting wasteful projects, and demanding accountability so every dollar goes where it’s needed.
We should prioritize Michigan workers and Michigan companies for road projects, ensuring quality that lasts.
Safe and reliable roads are a necessity for families, farmers, and businesses, and Chris will always fight to make sure taxpayers get results without being asked to pay more every year.
Education
A strong education system begins with a solid foundation in early learning.
Every child deserves the chance to build core skills in reading, writing, and math without being weighed down by political agendas or distractions in the classroom.
Teachers should be free to teach, and students should be free to learn in an environment focused on academics, discipline, and respect.
Chris supports empowering parents with a greater voice in their child’s education and ensuring taxpayer dollars go directly to strengthening classrooms, not bloated bureaucracy.
The earlier we invest in real learning, the stronger Michigan’s future will be.
Tough on Crime
Chris supports law enforcement with a no-nonsense, tough-on-crime approach.
He opposes ineffective catch-and-release policies that allow repeat offenders to walk free and threaten public safety.
Chris stands with police, prosecutors, and communities to ensure laws are enforced and justice is served — because keeping families safe is non-negotiable.
