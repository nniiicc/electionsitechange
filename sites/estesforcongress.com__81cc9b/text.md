Meet Ron
our kansas champion
Congressman Ron Estes is a fifth-generation Kansan representing Kansas’ Fourth District in the U.S.
House of Representatives and serves on the House Committee on Ways and Means.
A champion for tax reform, free and fair trade, retirement security, and lowering the price of health care.
Ron has helped pass landmark tax reform, rolled back burdensome regulations, increased support for veterans and our military, defended Wichita’s aviation industry, and helped lead efforts to secure the U.S.-Mexico-Canada Agreement vital for agriculture and manufacturing in Kansas and throughout the country.
With a background in engineering and business and prior service as Sedgwick County Treasurer and Kansas State Treasurer, Ron implemented efficiencies that returned over $1.5 million to taxpayers, oversaw more than $24 billion in public funds, enacted important pension reforms, and continues to be recognized nationally for his conservative leadership.
Ron, his wife Susan, and their three children live in Wichita, where he returns home each week.
