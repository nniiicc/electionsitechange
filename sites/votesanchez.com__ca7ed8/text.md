About Dennis
A Life Built on Faith, Family & Hard Work
Raised in a Christian family in Castroville, Dennis lost his father at a young age and was taught early to step up, work hard, and take responsibility.
By age twelve he was already working, learning that serving others, especially in their most difficult moments, is a duty grounded in dignity, compassion, and respect.
Dennis is a Boy Scouts of America Eagle Scout and a Salinas Police Explorer Scout.
He was also a member of the Independent Order of Odd Fellows, standing watch at the cemetery.
Dennis trained in law enforcement with the Salinas Police Department, built a career in construction and private security, and became a small business owner and regional manager with over 30 years of experience.
A father of five, much of it as a single dad, Dennis understands sacrifice, perseverance, and the importance of strong families.
"Sacramento needs common sense, accountability, and real-life experience.
That's what I'll bring to the State Assembly."
