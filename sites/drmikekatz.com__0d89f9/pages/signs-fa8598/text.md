In a state where most voters have never heard the name of the candidate challenging an incumbent, a yard sign in your front lawn does what no email or social post can: it puts Dr.
Katz’s name in front of your neighbors, on the route they drive every single day.
The earlier signs go up, the more they compound.
Awareness Every drive-by sighting builds name recognition with voters who’d otherwise never hear it.
Repetition is how Delaware learns who’s on the ballot.
Social Proof Your sign tells the people on your street where you stand — and quietly gives them permission to do the same.
One sign on a block usually means three by next week.
Early Momentum The signs that go up now do the most work.
They establish that a real campaign is underway and turn skeptical conversations into “I think he can win this.” Dr.
