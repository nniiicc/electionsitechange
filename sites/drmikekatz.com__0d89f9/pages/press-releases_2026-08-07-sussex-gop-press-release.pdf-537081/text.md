SENATE NEWS RELEASE F O R I M M E D I AT E R E L E A S E August 7, 2026 MEDIA CONTACT Hunter Dworsky hunter@drmikekatz.com drmikekatz.com Sussex County GOP Executive Committee Endorses Dr.
Mike Katz for U.S.
Senate County Republican leaders rally behind the Delaware GOP’s convention-endorsed candidate ahead of the September 15 primary.
GEORGETOWN, DE — The Sussex County Republican Party has endorsed Dr.
Mike Katz for the United States Senate, encouraging Republicans across Sussex County to support the party’s endorsed candidate in the September 15 Republican Primary and again in the November General Election.
The endorsement follows Dr.
Katz’s overwhelming support at the 2026 Delaware Republican State Convention, where he earned the backing of 82 percent of convention delegates.
The Sussex County Republican Party joins Republican leaders and grassroots conservatives across Delaware in supporting the Delaware Republican Party’s endorsed candidate for the United States Senate.
Sussex County Republican Committee Chairman Tom Molnar issued the following statement: “Our Executive Committee has taken the time to get to know Dr.
Mike Katz personally.
We have looked at his record, talked with him about the issues facing Delaware, and carefully considered who is best prepared to represent our state in the United States Senate.
The Sussex County Republican Party does not make endorsements lightly.
After getting to know Mike and carefully evaluating his record, we have complete confidence in his integrity, honesty, professionalism, conservative principles, and unwavering commitment to serve the people of Delaware.
Character matters.
Trust matters.
We believe Dr.
Mike Katz has both.
Throughout his career as a physician, business leader, and former Delaware State Senator, Mike has demonstrated that leadership is about service—not self—and that people always come before politics.
He has earned our trust, and we believe he has earned the trust of Republicans throughout Delaware.
Mike understands Sussex County.
He respects our farmers, watermen, small businesses, veterans, law enforcement officers, sportsmen, faith communities, and the hardworking families who make this county such a special place to live.
We know he will be a strong advocate for Sussex County and will fight to protect our values, our economy, and our way of life.
Our goal is straightforward.
We want to elect a United States Senator who will represent Delaware with integrity, fight for Sussex County, defeat Chris Coons, and help Republicans compete and win across our state.
We believe Dr.
Mike Katz is that candidate, and we are proud to give him our full support.” Dr.
Katz expressed his appreciation for the endorsement: “I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of the Sussex County Republican Party.
I sincerely thank Chairman Molnar, the Executive Committee, and Republicans throughout Sussex County for their confidence, encouragement, and support.
For thirty years, Delaware has been home for Trish and me, where we raised our family, built our careers, and dedicated our lives to serving others.
I will never take the trust of the people of Sussex County for granted.
If elected to the United States Senate, I will work every day to serve the people of Delaware with integrity, humility, and accountability.
The people of Sussex County deserve a Senator who will listen, who will be present, and who will never forget who sent him to Washington.
I will fight to protect Sussex County’s agricultural heritage, support our small businesses, strengthen public safety, secure our borders, defend our constitutional freedoms, pursue affordable and reliable energy, and ensure Sussex County always has a strong voice in Washington.
Together, we can unite Republicans, earn the trust of voters across Delaware, defeat Chris Coons in November, and move our state in a stronger direction.” The Sussex County Republican Party encourages every registered Republican to make a plan to vote for Dr.
Mike Katz during Delaware’s Early Voting, beginning September 2, or on Primary Election Day, September 15.
Following the primary, Republicans should come together, unite behind the Republican nominee, and work to defeat Chris Coons and elect Republicans up and down the ballot on November 3.
