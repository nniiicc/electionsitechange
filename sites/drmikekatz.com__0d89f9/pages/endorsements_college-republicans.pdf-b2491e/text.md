June 5, 2026 Endorsement of Dr.
Mike Katz for U.S, Senate On behalf of the Executive Board of the College Republicans at the University of Delaware our organization proudly endorses Dr.
Mike Katz for US Senate.
Dr.
Katz has spent the last three decades working towards a better Delaware.
From the ICU to the state legislature, Dr.
Katz has served Delaware courageously with diligence – and he will continue to do the same in the United States Senate.
Dr.
Katz’s motto of putting “people over politics” is exactly the solution our country desperately needs in the current moment.
As Washington elites continually attempt to block President Trump’s common sense agenda, our nation and our state desperately need leaders that will ﬁght for the interests of Delaware, not the interests of the political elites.
The race comes down to one motto: people over politics.
A vote for Katz is a vote for “people over politics.” A vote for Coons is a vote for continued domination of the lobbyist class over Delaware politics.
We need fearless leaders with experience in winning for Delaware, both within and outside the political sphere, serving our great state.
Dr.
Mike Katz is exactly the man to ﬁll this role and we are grateful for his courage and leadership.
Tim Schugsta President, College Republicans at the University of Delaware Ira Hurwitz Chairman, Delaware College Republicans
