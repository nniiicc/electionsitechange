The Platform — Eleven Planks 1.
Economy & Cost of Living Reining in inflation and restoring fiscal discipline.
Halt wasteful federal spending.
Cut red tape for small businesses.
Lower energy and housing costs for Delaware families.
Oppose new taxes on working families.
Protect seniors on fixed incomes from having their savings eroded by inflation.
2.
Healthcare Common Ground — no family’s health depends on their zip code.
Lower prescription drug costs while protecting the U.S. innovation pipeline.
Fund rural care in Kent and Sussex counties.
Push back on some of the region’s highest health insurance premiums.
Shift federal incentives from treating illness to preventing it.
3.
Protecting Delaware Jobs and Keeping Business Here Reversing regulatory flight and restoring our competitive edge.
Lock in pro-growth federal taxes.
Cut the red tape choking small business.
Bring critical manufacturing home — semiconductors, pharmaceuticals, defense.
Oppose federal changes that push Delaware employers to other states.
4.
Education Every child’s future is determined by their potential, not their address.
Expand early childhood investment.
Align curricula with modern workforce needs.
Close the zip-code gap in outcomes across all three counties.
5.
Military, Veterans & National Security Keep America’s promise to those who defend our freedom.
Rebuild American military strength; modernize forces; strengthen recruiting and retention.
Cut VA red tape so veterans spend less time fighting bureaucracy for earned benefits.
Strengthen cybersecurity and missile defense.
Stand with allies and up to adversaries.
