SB 80 → SS 1
Dr.
Katz originally developed the business plan for DHIN in 1999–2000 during an International Executive MBA at Georgetown University.

Healthcare
SB 90
In response to the horrific Kermit Gosnell scandal — where dangerous, unregulated practices were exposed at a Wilmington clinic — Dr.
Katz used his medical expertise to lead the fight for patient safety in Delaware.
Working alongside the Medical Society of Delaware, he authored and introduced SB 90 to mandate strict accreditation rules for any facility performing invasive medical procedures, and to ban licensed medical personnel from working in unaccredited clinics.
The bill did not fund or expand abortion; it was a patient-safety bill designed to hold clinics to the highest medical standards.
When a near-identical bill (HB 144) was later pushed through by other lawmakers, Dr.
Katz opposed it, arguing that it watered down key protections.
Opposing lawmakers publicly complained that Dr.
Katz's legislation "skewed too much in favor of his profession" and protected doctors too heavily.
