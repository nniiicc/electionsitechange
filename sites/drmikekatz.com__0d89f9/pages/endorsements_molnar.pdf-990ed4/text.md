From the Desk of Chairman Molnar Sussex County Republican Party Dear Delegate, About two years ago, I sat down with Dr.
Mike Katz when he was running for the United States Senate as an Independent.
I remember that conversation because it stayed with me.
After hearing him out, I told him something simple.
I said to Mike, if you ever run as a Republican, I will be the first person in line to support you.
Today, I’m here because that moment came.
And I’m proud to stand here and endorse Dr.
Mike Katz for the United States Senate!
Dr.
Katz is a physician.
He has spent his career making decisions that affect people’s lives in real time.
He has saved lives.
He understands healthcare from the inside, and that matters when we’re talking about making it work better for families here in Delaware.
We also know our education system needs work.
Our students deserve real opportunities, not just promises.
That means stronger schools, better direction, and pathways that actually lead somewhere.
For me, this part hits close to home.
I’ve spent over fifty years as a vocational instructor.
I’ve seen what happens when someone gets the chance to learn a skill and build something for themselves.
Right now, those programs are full.
Day and night.
That tells you everything you need to know.
We should be expanding those opportunities and starting earlier so more young people can step into the trades and build a future.
Delaware runs on people who work.
Small businesses.
Agriculture.
Tourism.
And right now, too many of them are being squeezed by regulations that don’t make sense.
We need someone in Washington who understands how things actually run.
Someone who understands what it takes to hire people, to manage risk, and to keep something going.
Dr.
Katz understands that.
He also understands our responsibility to veterans.
His plan to bring an advanced medical center to Southern Delaware and work with partners across the region is something that would make a real difference.
We also need to be honest about energy.
We need reliability.
We need consistency.
Dr.
Katz supports nuclear energy with the right safeguards because we need solutions that can actually meet demand.
And housing is a real issue.
Right now, getting into a home in Delaware is not easy.
Costs are high, and it is putting pressure on families trying to get started.
He supports giving first-time buyers a real shot and addressing the cost side so we can bring some balance back.
Now I want to say something that I know people are thinking about.
Dr.
Katz has run as a Democrat.
He has run as an Independent.
And now he is running as a Republican.
To me, that says he took the time to figure out where he stands, and like Ronald Reagan, he chose to be here.
That matters.
He has served as a State Senator.
He knows this state.
He knows the people here.
And he understands what is at stake.
Two years ago, I told him I would be first in line.
Tonight, I am.
And I ask you to join me in supporting Dr.
Mike Katz for the United States Senate Tom Molnar Thank you Tom Molnar Chairman, Sussex County Republican Committee
