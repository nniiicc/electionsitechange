The Challenge
Delaware families are paying more for groceries, energy, and housing than they were five years ago.
Small businesses are struggling under federal regulations written for lobbyists, not entrepreneurs.
Our rural communities still lack reliable healthcare access.
And for fifteen years, Delaware’s senator has been reliably present in Washington, reliably voting with his party, reliably collecting endorsements, while Delaware’s real problems go unaddressed.
Dr.
Katz has spent his career diagnosing problems and delivering results: in the ICU, in the boardroom, and in the legislature.
Delaware doesn’t need another career politician.
It needs someone who has actually run something, built something, and fought for something.
“The political class has been kicking the can down the road.
I’m building a coalition with Republicans, Independents, and disenchanted Democrats to defeat the incumbent Chris Coons on November 3rd.”
The Role
▶︎
Advance policies that improve lives and bring real resources back to Delaware—not talking points or headlines, but measurable results.
▲
Tackle the issues that matter most: healthcare access, economic growth, jobs, energy costs, and affordability—with the same discipline and leadership required in critical care.
★
Put Delaware families ahead of party politics and special interests.
Every vote in Washington should answer one question: How does this help Delaware?
“That is how I have approached every role I have held, and how I will serve in Washington.”
The Platform
Real solutions from a doctor who has spent his career fighting for patients — now ready to fight for Delaware families.
Halt wasteful spending, cut red tape, and lower energy and housing costs for Delaware families.
Lower drug costs, fix rural care gaps, and shift the system from treating illness to preventing it.
Cut federal red tape, lock in pro-growth taxes, and bring critical manufacturing home.
Expand early childhood, align education with the modern workforce, and close the zip-code gap.
A strong military, honored veterans, a modern VA, and a nation that stands with allies and up to adversaries.
Back the blue, defend the Constitution, and protect Bill of Rights freedoms.
Dismantle two-tiered justice, stop federal censorship, and rein in the regulatory state.
Finish the wall, interdict fentanyl, and restore federal accountability at the border.
Pass the SAVE Act, audit voter rolls, and verify every ballot is cast by a citizen.
Reject red-flag confiscation, block federal firearm registries, and defend due process.
Defend Title IX, preserve women's sports as a competitive category for biological females, and protect athlete safety.
