About the Book
In her first book and memoir, Lily traces her remarkable and poignant journey between two worlds, weaving her personal story together with the sweeping political and social transformations that shaped the lives of a generation.
Beginning with her childhood in China, she reflects on the trauma and upheaval of the Cultural Revolution, followed by her experiences as a young immigrant adapting to life in 90s America.
Her story unfolds alongside China’s rapid modernization and her growing exposure to American democracy, civic life, and political participation.
Through her story, Lily highlights the vital role Asian Americans play in American public life while examining how their voices and experiences have often been overlooked.
She demonstrates how immigrants bring important perspectives to conversations about democracy, immigration, identity, and civic engagement.
