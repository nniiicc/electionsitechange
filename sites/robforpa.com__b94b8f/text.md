As a 19 year-old, Rob took the reins of his grandfather’s union electrical contracting company and expanded the business across the Commonwealth while tripling the number of good-paying union jobs.
He dedicated his career to revitalizing our community, helping local entrepreneurs, and creating jobs for Pennsylvania families.
Rob brought that same entrepreneurial and problem solving spirit to Congress where he’s consistently delivered results for Northeastern Pennsylvania.
