Pittston, PA – Teamsters General President Sean M.
O’Brien visited Pennsylvania’s Eighth Congressional District today alongside Congressman Rob Bresnahan, underscoring their shared commitment to delivering results for working people and celebrating recent victories for Teamsters members in Northeastern Pennsylvania.
During the visit, O’Brien, Teamsters General Secretary-Treasurer Fred Zuckerman, Rep.
Bresnahan, and Teamsters Local 229 Secretary-Treasurer Mike Kovaleski toured the Bazooka Ring Pop facility in Moosic, where more than 60 Teamsters recently ratified a new three-year contract that includes a 15 percent wage increase, lower health care costs, pension improvements, and stronger workplace protections.
They also made stops at TForce Freight in Moosic, a UPS Facility in Taylor, and LT Verrastro Inc. in Old Forge.
“Rob Bresnahan is a strong ally on the issues that matter to Teamsters, from fighting to get the Faster Labor Contracts Act across the finish line to strengthening workplace protections and investing in railway safety.
We know we can count on him to deliver results for working people,” said Teamsters General President Sean M.
O’Brien.
“Pennsylvania’s workers deserve leaders who they can depend on to stand up and fight for the issues that matter to them,” said Rep.
Bresnahan.
“I’m proud to work alongside the Teamsters to strengthen protections for workers, support collective bargaining, and fight for policies that benefit the hardworking men and women who are the backbone of Northeastern Pennsylvania’s economy.”
In June of this year, The Pennsylvania Conference of Teamsters endorsed Congressman Bresnahan’s campaign for reelection.
