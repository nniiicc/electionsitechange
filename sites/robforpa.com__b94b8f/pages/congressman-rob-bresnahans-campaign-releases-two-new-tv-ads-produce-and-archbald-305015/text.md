The new ads spotlight Bresnahan’s work to lower costs, support NEPA farmers and protect local communities from out-of-state corporations.
Pittston, PA — Rob for PA today released two new TV ads, “Produce” and “Archbald,” highlighting Congressman Rob Bresnahan’s work in Congress to lower costs, support Northeastern Pennsylvania’s farmers, and keep decisions about local communities in local hands.
“PRODUCE”: BRESNAHAN’S LOCAL FARMERS FEEDING OUR COMMUNITIES ACT
“Produce” highlights Bresnahan’s Local Farmers Feeding our Communities Act, bipartisan legislation included in this year’s Farm Bill (H.R. 7567) that connects local farmers directly with community food programs.
The ad shows that paying farmers for their surplus produce means fresher food at a lower cost for NEPA families.
“ARCHBALD”: BRESNAHAN’S LOCAL CONTROL PROTECTION ACT
“Archbald” is named for the Lackawanna County borough that has fought off more proposed data center campuses than any other municipality in Pennsylvania.
The ad highlights Bresnahan’s Local Control Protection Act (H.R. 9262), which requires local approval before a data center can be built and shields communities from being sued by billion-dollar developers when they say no.
Together, “Produce” and “Archbald” underscore the same message: Rob Bresnahan is fighting in Washington for Northeastern Pennsylvania to ensure we lower costs, support our farmers who feed Northeastern Pennsylvania and support the communities working to protect their own backyards.
