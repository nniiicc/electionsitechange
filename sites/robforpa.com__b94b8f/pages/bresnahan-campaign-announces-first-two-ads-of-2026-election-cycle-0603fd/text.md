Pittston, PA – Congressman Rob Bresnahan’s campaign released two new ads today highlighting his work for Northeastern Pennsylvania.
In “Keep Caring,” NEPA nurse Lisa talks about Rep.
Bresnahan’s “relentless” pursuit to keep multiple hospitals in Northeastern Pennsylvania open to provide care to thousands of patients and save jobs for health care workers.
“On Call” features NEPA Fire Chief Tony, who highlights Rep.
Bresnahan’s consistent reliability and the new equipment he’s provided for firefighters out of his own pocket because he donates his entire congressional salary back to the district.
Both ads emphasize the dedication Rep.
Bresnahan has to Northeastern Pennsylvanians and his prioritization of local issues that make a big impact on the people he represents.
“Congressman Bresnahan is a steadfast champion for Northeastern Pennsylvanians and has spent his first term in Congress fighting to achieve real, meaningful results for the region.
These ads underscore the partnership Rep.
Bresnahan has struck with the people of this district and the hard work and commitment he brings to Congress to deliver on the promises he has made them.” – Bresnahan campaign spokesperson Samantha Bullock
For more information visit www.RobForPA.com.
