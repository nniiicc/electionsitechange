In a joint letter, the presidents of all four unions praised Bresnahan as a “consistent and steadfast advocate for the U.S. maritime industry” and pledged their unions’ full support heading into November.
The unions credited Bresnahan for cosponsoring the bipartisan SHIPS for America Act, legislation designed to revitalize the U.S.-flag fleet and help American mariners and vessel operators compete in global shipping.
They also pointed to his support for full funding of the Maritime Security Program and his work ensuring enforcement of the Jones Act and U.S.-flag cargo preference requirements, policies the unions say are essential to protecting American maritime jobs and maintaining the manpower base the country needs “in time of war or other international emergency.” The full endorsement letter can be found HERE .
“The licensed and unlicensed merchant mariners of AMO, MM&P, MEBA, and SIU keep our country and our economy running, and they deserve a partner in Washington who understands that” said Congressman Rob Bresnahan.
“I will always stand up for working people and am grateful for the support of our merchant mariners.” The four maritime unions join a large and growing coalition of labor organizations supporting Bresnahan’s re-election, including: Pennsylvania Laborers’ District Council (Endorsement letter HERE ) Amalgamated Transit Union (Endorsement announcement HERE ) American Federation of Government Employees (Endorsement announcement HERE ) American Federation of Government Employees District 3 (Endorsement letter HERE ) Brotherhood of Railroad Signalmen (Endorsement letter HERE ) International Brotherhood of Boilermakers, Iron Ship Builders, Blacksmiths, Forgers and Helpers Local 13 (Endorsement Letter HERE ) International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 163 (Endorsement letter HERE ) International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 1319 (Endorsement letter HERE ) International Union of Operating Engineers Local 542 (Endorsement letter HERE ) Pennsylvania Conference of Teamsters (Endorsement letter HERE ) For more information visit www.RobForPA.com .
