Rob Bresnahan today announced the launch of the “Women for Rob” Coalition, a growing network of more than 80 women across all five counties in the 8 th Congressional District supporting his re-election campaign and his record of delivering results for Northeastern Pennsylvania families.
The coalition will be co-chaired by Monroe County Republican Women Chair and Republican State Committee Member Shelli Bird and Mayfield Borough Council President Diana Campbell.
This announcement reflects the results-driven leadership Rep.
Bresnahan was elected to deliver and the growing confidence women across the district have in his commitment to addressing the everyday challenges facing families throughout the region.
Since taking office, Bresnahan has worked to strengthen access to quality healthcare, protect critical hospital services, support working families, and advocate for investments that better the lives of all Northeastern Pennsylvanians.
Monroe County Republican Women Chair, public school teacher, and mother of two Shelli Bird : “Working women and families across Northeastern Pennsylvania need leaders who understand the challenges they face every day.
While Paige Cognetti cut paid maternity leave for Scranton city employees, Rep.
Bresnahan has focused on supporting working families, lowering costs, and creating opportunities for the next generation.” Mayfield Borough Council President Diana Campbell: “Rep.
Bresnahan’s work to secure funding for the Moses Taylor Hospital Women’s Imaging and Breast Care Center is just one small example of the ways he has been a champion for greater investments in women’s health services.
Bresnahan is a tireless advocate for the health and well-being of our communities, and he has consistently fought to deliver access to high-quality health care.” Rep.
Bresnahan : “I am honored to have the support of such strong female leaders in our communities like Diana Campbell and Shelli Bird.
The work I do in Congress is about delivering for the people who sent me there.
Across Northeastern Pennsylvania, women are the backbone of our families, our workplaces, and our communities.
They are raising children, caring for loved ones, running businesses, volunteering, and helping keep our region strong.
Diana, Shelli, and the members of this coalition represent the very best of Northeastern Pennsylvania, and I’m proud to have them as partners in our fight to keep moving our communities forward.” Shelli Bird, Diana Campbell, and 80 other members of Women for Rob invite other women in the 8th Congressional District to join the coalition.
