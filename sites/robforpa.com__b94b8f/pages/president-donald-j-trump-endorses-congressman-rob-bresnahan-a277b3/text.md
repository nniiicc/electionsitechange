Trump Endorses Congressman Rob Bresnahan November 3, 2025 Pittston, PA – President Donald J.
Trump has endorsed Congressman Rob Bresnahan for re-election in Pennsylvania’s 8th Congressional District, praising him as a strong conservative leader and successful businessman who is fighting to create jobs, cut taxes, stop wasteful spending, secure the border, and protect the Second Amendment.
“I am honored to have earned the support of President Trump and am proud to partner with him to advance an agenda that puts the people of Northeastern Pennsylvania first,” said Congressman Rob Bresnahan.
“I look forward to working with the President to continue fighting for a stronger economy, affordable energy, and a secure border with safer communities.” Bresnahan Campaign Manager Peter Brath said the endorsement highlights the clear contrast in the race with far-left Scranton Mayor Paige Cognetti.
“President Trump won this district by nearly 10 points because voters here rejected the policies of Joe Biden and Kamala Harris that Paige Cognetti embraces with open arms,” said Rob Bresnahan Campaign Manager Peter Brath.
“While Rob Bresnahan is fighting for the people of Northeastern Pennsylvania, Paige Cognetti is doubling down on the same failed, extreme policies including the government shutdown that is hurting working families across our region, all over her insistence on giving free healthcare to illegal immigrants.
The people of Northeastern Pennsylvania deserve a representative who will fight for them, not the far-left extremists who have taken over the Democrat Party.” Learn more at www.RobForPA.com .
