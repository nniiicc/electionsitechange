Law Enforcement Officers of Southern Luzerne County Represented by Christina-Doherty FOP Lodge 84 Endorse Congressman Rob Bresnahan for Reelection
Pittston, PA – Congressman Rob Bresnahan today announced that he has earned the endorsement of Christina-Doherty Fraternal Order of Police Lodge #84, representing law enforcement officers across southern Luzerne County.
Rep.
Bresnahan has now earned the endorsement of seven FOP Lodges in Pennsylvania’s 8th district, as well as the Pennsylvania State Troopers Association.
The endorsement follows Bresnahan’s recent address to the lodge, where he reaffirmed his commitment to supporting law enforcement across Pennsylvania’s 8th Congressional District.
“Throughout his tenure in Congress, Rob Bresnahan has proven himself to be an unwavering champion for public safety and officer welfare.
From fighting to secure crucial federal funding for local departments to advocating for legislation that protects those who wear the badge, his leadership has provided our officers with the confidence and resources needed to execute their duties safely and effectively,” said FOP Lodge 84 President, Michael Bogart.
“He truly understands the unique challenges facing modern law enforcement, and his steadfast commitment to our mission does not go unnoticed.” The full endorsement letter can be viewed HERE.
“Every day, the officers of FOP Christina-Doherty Lodge #84 put on the badge and answer the call, whatever it takes to keep southern Luzerne County safe,” said Congressman Rob Bresnahan.
“That kind of courage deserves a partner in Washington who will fight for the resources and respect they’ve earned.
I’m proud to have their support, and I won’t let them down.”
