The endorsements reflect a shared commitment to public safety and underscore Rep.
Bresnahan’s support for the brave men and women who work every day to keep Pennsylvania communities safe.
The full letter can be found HERE .
“I am deeply honored to receive the endorsement of the Pennsylvania State Troopers Association and Fraternal Order of Police Lodges 38, 43, and 46,” said Congressman Bresnahan.
“These men and women are heroes who show up and sacrifice every single day to protect our families and communities, and I am deeply appreciative to have earned their trust and support.
I will continue fighting in Congress to ensure our law enforcement officers have the resources, tools, and backing they need to do their jobs safely and effectively.” For more information visit www.RobForPA.com .
