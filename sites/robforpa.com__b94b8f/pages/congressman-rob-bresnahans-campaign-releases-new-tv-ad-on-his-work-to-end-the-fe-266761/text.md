Pittston, PA – The Rob Bresnahan for Congress campaign today released a new TV ad, Rain Tax, highlighting Congressman Rob Bresnahan’s fight to end the federal Rain Tax mandate that drained the wallets of homeowners and small business owners in Northeastern Pennsylvania for over a decade.
Rain Tax highlights how Bresnahan delivered on his fight to end the federal Rain Tax mandate, standing beside President Trump as he signed an executive order officially rescinding the decade-old rule.
For homeowners and small business owners in Northeastern Pennsylvania, ending the rain tax would mean no more mandatory quarterly stormwater fees, no more threat of fines for local municipalities working to comply, and more money staying in the pockets of NEPA families instead of going toward an unsuccessful cleanup project over 200 miles away.
Rain Tax underscores the same message Bresnahan has delivered since he first announced he would run for Congress in 2023: that he will fight in Washington to lower costs and put money back in the pockets of NEPA families.
While Rob Bresnahan was working to end the federal Rain Tax mandate, Paige Cognetti has spent her time raising money with the person responsible for the mandate and refusing to say whether she supports the movement in Pennsylvania to end the Rain Tax.
