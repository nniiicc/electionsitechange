In its endorsement letter, Local 507 pointed to Bresnahan’s continued support for the firefighters serving the City of Hazleton, including his efforts to help address a critical staffing shortage.
The union specifically highlighted Bresnahan’s recent visit to its firehouse, where he met directly with members to hear their concerns, as well as his advocacy surrounding the department’s recent federal grant applications.
“Your willingness to advocate on behalf of our Local, including your support surrounding our recent federal grant applications, demonstrates your commitment to addressing the public safety needs of the communities you represent and the firefighters who serve them,” wrote IAFF Local 507 President Shawn McArdle in the union’s endorsement letter.
The full endorsement letter can be read HERE .
“When someone calls 911, they expect help to be there.
The men and women of the Hazleton City Fire Department put their lives on the line every day to answer those calls, and they deserve the staffing, equipment, and resources necessary to do their jobs safely,” said Congressman Rob Bresnahan .
“I’ve had the opportunity to sit down with these firefighters, hear their concerns firsthand, and work alongside them to address their needs.
