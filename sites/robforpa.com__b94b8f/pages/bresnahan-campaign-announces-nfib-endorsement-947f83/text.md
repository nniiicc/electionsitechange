Pittston, PA – Congressman Rob Bresnahan today announced the National Federation of Independent Business (NFIB) has endorsed his reelection campaign for Pennsylvania’s 8th Congressional District.
The endorsement highlights his record in Congress of delivering wins for Main Street and small businesses in Northeastern Pennsylvania.
Bresnahan has delivered a number of wins for small businesses during his first term in Congress, including the Bresnahan-led Small Business Cybersecurity Assistance Evaluation Act of 2026, which the House passed unanimously in June.
“Rep.
Bresnahan has proven himself to be a strong friend to small business during his first term in Congress.
He helped deliver one of the greatest wins for Main Street by voting to make the 20% Small Business Deduction permanent, NFIB’s top priority for the 119th Congress.
We are proud to endorse him for re-election.” – Louis Bertolotti, NFIB Political Director.
“I’ve made payroll.
I’ve hired people.
I know what it means to sign the front of a check, not just the back,” said Bresnahan.
“Small businesses are truly the foundation of Northeastern Pennsylvania’s economy, and in Congress I’m going to keep fighting to cut the red tape and taxes that make it harder for them to succeed.”
Bresnahan became CFO of his family’s business, Kuharchik Construction, at 19 years old before later serving as CEO, growing the company’s footprint across Pennsylvania and supporting more than 150 working families.
He went on to found RPB Ventures, investing in the revitalization of downtown Pittston and creating opportunities for local entrepreneurs.
In Congress, he serves on the House Small Business Committee, where he brings a business owner’s mindset to Washington.
For more information visit www.RobForPA.com.
