Montana Tax Solutions While some tax is necessary to fund the responsibilities of the government of the state of Montana, there are ways to reduce the burden on Montana residents.
I will work with other legislators to come up with solutions to bring high rates down, while continuing to provide necessary services which have increasing costs.
Protect Montana's Rights The needs of Montanans are often different from the needs of more urban areas of our country.
Laws made in Washington often do not make sense in our rural setting.
We need to be vigilent to protect our rights to make laws that make sense to Montanans and protect our way of life.
I am working with other states' legislators to share ideas and information to protect Montana from Federal overreach.
Growing Montana's Economy We need to continue to work on building small business opportunites through economic development.
As a small business owner, myself, for most of my adult life, I know the challenges of running a small business in this time of increasingly challenging environment for small businesses.
Small business has been the heart and soul of our economy for so many years and that is changing and forcing small businesses out.
Too much bureaucracy forces small businesses out.
Secure Our Elections With the fear that our elections are vulnerable to outside interference, we need to be sure we have solid policies in place to prevent any tampering with our elections.
In the past I worked with a citizen group, along with a bi-partisan legislative workgroup, the Secretary of State's Office, Commisioner of Political Practice (COPP), election clerks and Montana Association of Counties (MACO) to look into how our process works, and make recommendations to increase voter confidence in our elections.
Pro-Life I believe in contraception before conception.
Human life is precious.
While this is a very personal decision, we as a state should set firm guardrails around the issue of abortion.
We need to focus our energy on helping support young families in these times when family values are under attack.
Health Care and Insurance Costs Much of the cost of healthcare is due to the Healthcare Industry's unbridled ability to overcharge for drugs, treatments, insurance, and procedures in a way that leaves the consumer being faced with costs that make no sense.
The average person has to spend too much time trying to sort it all out.
Corporate healthcare is now dictating to doctors how to treat their patients.
Doctors have to be allowed to use their training and expertise and decide what is best for their patients.
We need to ensure patient care comes before corporate profits.
Secure Montana Borders I have worked with the Department of Justice and the Department of Labor to beef up our laws to cut back on human trafficking.
We are hearing reports of human labor trafficking, which is often linked to drug and child and sex trafficking.
Montana is not immune to the issues facing our Southern states.
It has just been slower getting here.
Education Quality basic education is the key to having a quality workforce in today's world.
Children must be able to read well and have good writing skills, as well as a solid understanding of math concepts and skills.
The loss of learning opportunities during the last couple of years is a challenge to our schools and students.
As members of the Montana Legislature we need to support schools in overcoming these challenges.
Higher education options must include Trade Schools, Apprenticeships, College or University learning as well as distance learning programs.

Energy policies that make sense for Montana.
Mandatory all electric does not make sense for most of Montana.
We must allow for responsible use of a variety of energy sources, including responsible coal mining and gas and oil exploration.
With electricity demands on the rise, we must have the ability to run generation plants when hydro-electric, wind and solar cannot meet the ever-increasing needs for electricity.
We need to continue efforts to explore adding hydro-electric plants to existing dams, geothermal possibilities, and pursuing new and safe technologies in nuclear energy.
Diversification will increase reliability.

2nd Amendment Rights I would resist pressure to further erode 2nd Amendment Rights.
As we look at other countries around the world, it becomes apparent what our forefathers had in mind when including this in the Constitution.
An unarmed citizenry is easily at the mercy of an aggressor.
While I respect the concerns of those who have been victims of senseless shootings in this country, the solution is not to take the guns out of the hands of law-abiding citizens.
