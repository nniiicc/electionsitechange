Lisa Keller
Candidate for State House District 13
Hi!
I’m Lisa Keller, and I’m running to represent Alaska House District 13.
I was born and raised in Anchorage, and for more than thirty years, I’ve called District 13 home — as a coach, small business owner, mother, and grandmother.
I’ve dedicated my life to helping people set goals, overcome challenges, and reach their potential.
These are the values I’ll carry with me to Juneau.
I’ve had the privilege of working for both Representatives Andy Josephson & Alyse Galvin in Juneau, learning firsthand how thoughtful, hardworking legislators make a difference for our community.
I'm proud to continue his legacy of practical, accessible, and people-focused leadership.
Our community deserves a representative who listens, brings people together, and gets results.
I’m running to strengthen public safety, support our teachers, schools, and students, and build a sustainable fiscal future for Alaska — so our families and businesses can thrive.
Please contact me if you have any questions about my campaign.
My Priorities
Public Safety
Lisa Keller is committed to keeping our community safe by strengthening first responders, focusing on prevention, and making sure public safety solutions are practical, accountable, and rooted in community trust.
Education
Lisa Keller believes every child deserves a strong public education—one that supports teachers, prepares students for real-world opportunities, and ensures schools have the resources they need to help every student succeed.
Fiscal Responsibility
Fiscal responsibility means being careful with taxpayer dollars, making smart, transparent budget decisions, and prioritizing investments that deliver real results for families and the community.
