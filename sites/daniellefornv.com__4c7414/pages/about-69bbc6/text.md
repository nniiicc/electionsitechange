top of page Home About Priorities Contact Menu Close Donate Meet Danielle Gallant Danielle is a wife, mother, and small business owner.
She holds a Nevada Broker license and is a partner of Guardian Realty Investment and Property Management with her husband Paul.
Danielle has been doing full time Real Estate and Property Management for over a decade and also worked in the construction industry, as co - owner in Gallant Construction.
Prior to joining her husband in the family construction business, she worked as a Marriage and Family Therapist.
She holds a B.A. in Psychology and a Masters in Human Development and Family Studies from Auburn University.
Danielle and Paul have been married for 16 years and have 2 boys.
They moved from the Bay Area because Danielle ' s family needed help.
Danielle and Paul felt the only way they could help support their entire family was to leave California. ​ So they closed down their Construction business in California and risked starting from scratch for the sake of family.
This scary move with only uncertainty became the best move their family ever could have made.
Nevada was the right move and they are proud to now call it home. ​ With Nevada ’ s no state income tax, affordable housing and family focused values, Danielle and her family have thrived along with their businesses in just a short time.
Trying to get their business to the point it is now would have taken them three times as long had they stayed in California.
Danielle is grateful for the State of Nevada and wants to ensure that we continue to offer that same level of hope and opportunity to anyone else who wants to call Nevada home.
She feels strongly that we need better legislation with less interference so that Nevada can get back to offering the same opportunity her family was given #ago.
