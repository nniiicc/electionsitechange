With Nevada’s no state income tax, affordable housing and family focused values, Danielle and her family have thrived along with their businesses in just a short time.
Trying to get their business to the point it is now would have taken them three times as long had they stayed in California.
Danielle is grateful for the State of Nevada and wants to ensure that we continue to offer that same level of hope and opportunity to anyone else who wants to call Nevada home.
She feels strongly that we need better legislation with less interference so that Nevada can get back to offering the same opportunity her family was given 8 years ago.
She is fighting to protect and preserve those same opportunities for future generations to come.
Her goal is to ensure that the great state of Nevada continues to offer a better and easier life for everyone.
She will continually advocate for policies that move Nevada forward.
