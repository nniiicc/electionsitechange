I was proud to support Connecticut’s bipartisan balanced state budget that maintains the largest middle-class tax cut in state history.
The budget generated surpluses used to continue to pay off long-term debt while also increasing funding to municipalities across the state by $300 million.
All of the towns in the 8th district benefited from the increase in resources from the state.
Every extra dollar in funding the state provides is one less dollar that a municipality has to collect from its residents in property taxes.
- Increased K-12 regular education funding more than $4.6 million
- Provided $1.4 million in grants for special ed
- Provided more than $3 million in grants to support municipal budgets
- Increased funding for the Town Aid Road Program by $1.6
The federal government has spent the last two years cutting countless programs that our residents rely on.
We created a $550 million dollar Emergency Response Fund from our surplus to backill the cuts to:
- Food assistance for seniors and families
- Medicaid and the Affordable Care Act
- Higher education loans for nurses, teachers, and social workers
- Dairy industry and farmers across Connecticut
- Reproductive healthcare
- Mental health supports for children
Established new protections for Connecticut residents’ personal data.
Connecticut isn’t waiting for Washington to keep residents safe.
- Allows consumers to delete their data from databroker websites
- Bans the sale or sharing of precise geolocation data
- Prohibits businesses using facial recognition in public spaces from sharing data
Protecting Families from AI
- Requires chatbot operators to detect signs of suicidal ideation and connect users with mental health resources
- Requires disclosure when AI is used in hiring
- Increases protections from discrimination by AI
Creates a new AI Academy and workforce training partnerships to ensure working families thrive in an AI Economy
Connecticut continues to lead the nation in providing services, support, and benefits for the men and women who served our country.
As co-chair of the Veterans and Military Affairs Committee I was proud to lead the passage of a comprehensive veterans’ package that expands dental coverage, amplifies programs to train veterans and service members for careers in the trades, extends grace periods for returning service members, strengthens protections against predatory claims agents, and dedicates funding to restoring neglected veterans’ graves across the state.
