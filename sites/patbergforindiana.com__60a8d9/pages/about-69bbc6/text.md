Meet Logan Patberg Hi, I’m Logan Patberg—a lifelong resident of Southwestern Indiana and a neighbor who cares.
I grew up attending public schools in the EVSC, and today, I’m proud to be a teacher in that same district.
My fiancé is also an educator, and our son attends public school in the MSD of North Posey district.
I’m committed to reinvesting in our schools and supporting thriving communities — no matter their size.
Outside of the classroom, I serve on the board of directors for the Evansville Teachers Association.
I’m a proud union member, and I will continue to advocate for collective bargaining rights and stand strong in support of labor unions.
I’m not a career politician.
I’m a teacher, a union member, a parent, and a Hoosier who understands the struggles many families face.
I believe in listening, adapting, and working across the aisle to solve real problems.
Snapshot of District 76 District 76 is situated in Southwestern Indiana covering parts of Vanderburgh and Posey County.
The district includes portions of the cities of Evansville and Mount Vernon also covering the towns of New Harmony and Poseyville.
