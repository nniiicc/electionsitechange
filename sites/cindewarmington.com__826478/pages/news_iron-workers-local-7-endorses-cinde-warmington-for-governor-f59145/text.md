Iron Workers Local 7 Endorses Cinde Warmington For Governor
MANCHESTER, NH — Today, Iron Workers Local 7 endorsed Cinde Warmington for Governor.
Local 7, representing more than 3,600 ironworkers across New Hampshire, Maine, Vermont, and Massachusetts, highlighted Cinde’s commitment to fighting for working families.
“As Cinde proved to us during her time on the Executive Council, she’s not afraid to leave it all on the field to make sure workers receive fair wages for fair work,” said Matt Glavin, Iron Workers Local 7 Business Agent.
“As our next governor, Cinde will make every decision through the eyes of working families, and we’re proud to endorse her campaign.”
“It is an honor to have the support of Iron Workers Local 7 as we fight to deliver a better, more affordable New Hampshire,” said Cinde Warmington.
“Unions built our middle class and are the foundation of an economy that works for working people.
I’ll always have their backs in the governor’s office.”
Alongside Iron Workers Local 7, Cinde’s campaign for governor has been endorsed by other members of organized labor across New Hampshire, including American Postal Workers Union Local #230, Teamsters Local 633, and the American Federation of Teachers-New Hampshire.
