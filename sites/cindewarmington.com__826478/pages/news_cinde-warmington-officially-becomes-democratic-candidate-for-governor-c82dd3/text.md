Cinde Warmington Officially Becomes Democratic Candidate for Governor
MANCHESTER, NH — Less than a day after officially becoming the Democratic candidate for governor following the close of New Hampshire's candidate filing period, Cinde Warmington opened her campaign headquarters in Manchester over the weekend, joined by supporters ready to help deliver a better, more affordable future for Granite Staters.
“Between now and November, we’re going to be fighting back every single day to take back our state for the working people who built it,” said Cinde Warmington.
“Everywhere I go, I meet Granite Staters who just can’t afford two more years of Kelly Ayotte.
This fall, we have a real opportunity to make life better for working families, level the playing field so that everyone has what they need to get ahead, and give people hope again.
We can do so much better — and we will.”
“House Democrats have been fighting for our shared values — public education, public safety, housing, child care, and making life more affordable for Granite Staters.
But every day, we’re looking for leadership that we just haven’t seen from Kelly Ayotte,” said House Democratic Caucus Chair Matt Wilhelm.
“New Hampshire voters are ready to bring balance back to the State House — and that means electing Cinde Warmington, a proven champion for working families, to the corner office.”
