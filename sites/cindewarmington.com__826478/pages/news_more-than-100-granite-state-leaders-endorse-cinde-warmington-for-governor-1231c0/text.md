KEY ENDORSEMENTS
More than 100 Granite State Leaders Endorse Cinde Warmington for Governor
Over 100 leaders from every corner of New Hampshire stand with Cinde as she challenges Kelly Ayotte
CONCORD – Less than 24 hours after Cinde Warmington announced she is running for Governor, more than 100 leaders across the Granite State endorsed Cinde — including over 80 current and former elected officials, as well as local officeholders, from every county across the state.
“This unmatched rush of support — from leaders in every county across the state — speaks to the strength and depth of our campaign, and our commitment to putting the needs of working Granite Staters front and center,” said Cinde Warmington, candidate for Governor.
“While Kelly Ayotte may have MAGA millionaires bankrolling her, we have a growing grassroots coalition of Granite Staters who are committed to bringing down skyrocketing costs and taking back the Governor’s office for the people of New Hampshire.”
