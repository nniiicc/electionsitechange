Keeping the NC Lottery Accountable.
We'll bring in 1 billion additional dollars for public schools!
Whether you play the lottery or not, people do.
If you like it or hate it, the proceeds are SUPPOSED to go to our kids!
While everyone is begging for education dollars and blaming it on Republicans or Democrats, I have a plan to bring in over a billion dollars to education over 5 years just by making them do what they’re supposed to already be doing!
They say their goal is 38%, but they fall too short for my comfort level at 16 PERCENT!!!!
We can require accountability of the CEO (yes, there is one), and the board so that they have MANDATORY PERCENTAGE spending on education like was intended.
In 2025, the North Carolina Education Lottery generated approximately $6.6 BILLION in sales.
Here is where that money went: 75.9% – Player Prizes: $5 BILLION 16.2% – Education: $1.07 BILLION 4.1% – Retailer Commissions: $270.6 MILLION 3.8% – Operating Expenses: $252.5 MILLION The online gambling generated approximately $2.6 BILLION in sales, but paid out roughly 87% in prizes.
For every $100 spent, $87 went back to players, leaving just $13 before expenses.
North Carolina law establishes a 38% “guideline” for lottery revenue going to education, yet we’re sitting at approximately 16%.
At $6.6 billion in annual sales: 16% = $1.06 BILLION for education 38% = $2.51 BILLION for education That’s a difference of approximately $1.45 BILLION annually.
We can’t change the percentages 22% over night, but we CAN require accountability from the CEO of the NC lottery, Mark Michalko and the Chairman of the State Lottery Commissioner, Bryan Starnes.
There is a board of 9 Commissioners appointed by the governor, state house and senate, the treasurer, and auditor.
I suggest a bill to require a 2% increase year-over-year to a percentage both possible and enforceable.
If they can not meet these requirements, then they get removed.
It’s quite simple.
The 38% may be unrealistic.
Having an advertised goal and falling too darn short versus ACTUAL benefits to students are two different things.
One is propaganda, the other is education funding.
