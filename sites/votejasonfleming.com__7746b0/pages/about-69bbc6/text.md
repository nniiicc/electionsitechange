Jason Fleming is a proud veteran, entrepreneur, and community leader based in the Black Hills region of South Dakota.
After 21 years of dedicated service in the United States Air Force, Jason brought the same discipline, integrity, and commitment to excellence into the civilian world — building a legacy of hard work both in business and in his community.
Jason is the owner of two veteran-owned businesses, Veteran Roofing and Veteran Painting, providing quality craftsmanship and trusted service to homeowners and businesses throughout the region.
Beyond his entrepreneurial pursuits, Jason is deeply invested in the growth and safety of his community.
He serves as Board President of the Box Elder Fire District Board and holds board positions with the Rapid City Summer Nights Board and the Box Elder Economic Development Board.
He is also a proud member of American Legion Post 315.
Jason’s commitment to local business and regional growth is reflected in his active memberships with the Box Elder Chamber of Commerce, the Belle Fourche Chamber of Commerce, the Spearfish Chamber of Commerce, and the Black Hills Home Builders Association.
When he’s not leading in the boardroom or on the job site, Jason is most at home with his family.
He and his wife Andrea are the proud parents of three children — sons Isiah and Ian, and daughter Lena.
Jason also serves as a committee member for the Douglas School System’s Portrait of a Graduate initiative, helping shape the future for the next generation of students in his community.
Jason Fleming is a man of service — to his country, his community, and his family.
