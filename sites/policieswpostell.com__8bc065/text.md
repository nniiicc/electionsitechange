Meet Quentin Fighting for Addressing PFAS Contamination and Community Health Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) contamination has become a major public-health and environmental concern in Whitfield County.
Recent legislation — including House Bills 211 and 1212 and Senate Bill 577 — has raised questions in the community about accountability for industrial pollution and long-term impacts on residents’ health.
If elected, my goal is to work toward repealing or revising policies that limit residents’ ability to seek restitution for documented harm.
I will also advocate for large-scale water remediation solutions, such as reverse-osmosis filtration systems similar to those implemented in nearby communities, to help ensure safe drinking water for more than 60,000 local residents now and for future generations.
Responsible Management of AI Development and Local Resources Proposed artificial-intelligence (AI) data or “super” centers planned for Dalton — expected as early as July 2026 — raise important concerns about water usage, infrastructure strain, and equitable taxation.
These facilities may place significant demand on local water systems that are already under pressure from contamination and population needs.
My priority is to ensure that any major industrial or technology development in Whitfield County is accompanied by transparent agreements that protect residents’ access to clean water and require companies to contribute fairly to local tax revenue, infrastructure upgrades, and long-term economic benefit for the community.
Immigration Enforcement Policies and Due Process Protections Participation in federal immigration enforcement programs, including those authorized under policies such as the ICE program that collaborates with local jails, has generated strong debate about local law-enforcement roles, civil liberties, and community trust.
Concerns have been raised about potential wrongful detentions and the broader impact on families and local economic stability.
My goal is to review and, where appropriate, advocate for withdrawing local agencies from agreements that expand immigration enforcement beyond core public-safety functions.
I will work toward policies that reinforce due-process protections, maintain local accountability, and ensure that community policing efforts prioritize fairness, transparency, and public confidence.
