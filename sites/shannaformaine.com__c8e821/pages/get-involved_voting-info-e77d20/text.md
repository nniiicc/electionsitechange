How To Register to Vote
In Maine, you may register to vote up to and including the day of an Election.
If you plan to vote Absentee or plan to register anywhere other than your Municipal Clerk's office, the date in which you must register by may be sooner than election day.
Click here to visit the State of Maine's website on voter registration.
Where and When to Vote
In Maine, you may vote Absentee (by mail before an election), or In-Person on the day of the election, or early at your Municipal Clerk's office.
Elections in Lewiston have multiple polling locations based on which Ward you live in.

Where to Get More Information
You should always aim to recieve election information from official sources.
We recommend reviewing the voting informtion available on the Maine Secretary of State's website or your local Municipal Clerk's office.
