What Comes Next?
AI is changing fast.
The future is still ours to shape.
Artificial intelligence is already changing how we learn, work, create, communicate, and do business – and the pace of change is accelerating.
But the future of AI isn’t something that should be left only to technology companies, policymakers, or experts.
We all have a role in shaping it.
Join us for Brews & Banter: AI Is Here.
What Comes Next?
This engaging, interactive community conversation features Gray Cox, author of Smarter Planet or Wiser Earth , educator, signer/songwriter, and researcher in philosophy, peace studies, and artificial intelligence at College of the Atlantic .
Gray will introduce some of the big ideas behind AI, explain what is changing in the current landscape, and help us explore the opportunities, risks, and choices ahead.
Then we’ll open the conversation: How is AI affecting our lives?
What should responsible use look like?
Who gets to make the rules?
And how can students, workers, businesses, and community members help shape its future?
No technical background is needed!
Just bring your curiosity and a willingness to join the conversation.
Tuesday, September 15 Doors open at 4:30 p.m. | Conversation begins at 5 p.m.
