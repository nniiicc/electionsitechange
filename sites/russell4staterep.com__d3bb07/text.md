Davida’s Priorities
Making Life More Affordable
Families should not have to choose between rent, utilities, and healthcare.
- Fight for affordable housing and rent stabilization
- Lower utility costs and protect families from excessive bills
- Expand access to affordable healthcare, including mental health services
Investing in Quality education
Every child deserves a strong start and a fair shot.
- Increase funding for public schools and classroom resources
- Expand after-school programs, tutoring, and mentorship
- Support vocational and career training tied to good-paying jobs
Building Safe, Thriving Communities
Safety starts with trust and opportunity.
- Support public safety strategies that work with communities
- Promote community policing and transparency
- Invest in neighborhood revitalization that attracts families and businesses
Creating Good Jobs That Strengthen Families
Work should pay and opportunity should be local.
- Support economic development and fair wages
- Expand workforce training for today’s job market
- Help small businesses grow through smart investments and support
