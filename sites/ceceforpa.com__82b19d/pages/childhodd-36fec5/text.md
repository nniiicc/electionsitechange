But right now, childcare costs in Pennsylvania are out of control, often higher than rent or a mortgage.
Families are struggling, and our economy suffers when parents—especially women—are pushed out of the workforce.I believe childcare is a right, not a privilege.
As your State Representative, I will fight to: ​ Make childcare affordable for every family by capping costs at no more than 7% of income and expanding state subsidies.
Invest in early learning by fully funding Pre-K Counts, Head Start, and birth-to-three programs so all children can thrive.
Support childcare workers with fair wages, benefits, and training so the people caring for our kids can support their own families.
Expand access and options by funding community-based programs, evening and weekend care, and inclusive services for children with disabilities.
Fund childcare fairly by closing tax loopholes and making big corporations and the ultra-wealthy pay their fair share.
