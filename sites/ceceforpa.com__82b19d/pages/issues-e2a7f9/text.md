JUSTICE &
PUBLIC SAFETY
Everyone deserves to feel safe in their home, their neighborhood, and their community.
But safety is about more than policing—it’s about strong neighborhoods, good jobs, quality schools, affordable housing, and trust between residents and those who serve them.
In Pennsylvania, our criminal justice system is too often unequal, over-punitive, and ineffective.
As your State Representative, I will fight for policies that keep us safe while protecting everyone’s rights and dignity.
HOUSING
Everyone deserves a safe, stable place to live—
no exceptions.
People in the 22nd District are being priced out of their neighborhoods, tenants are facing rising rents and evictions, and affordable housing is harder to find every year.
Housing is not a luxury—it’s a basic human right.
I’m running for State Representative to make sure every person in District 22 has access to affordable, dignified housing.
EDUCATION
Every child—no matter their ZIP code, family income, or background—deserves a world-class education in a safe, well-resourced public school.
In Pennsylvania, our public schools have been underfunded for decades, and the state’s own courts have declared the current system unconstitutional.
The time for half-measures is over.
WORKER'S RIGHTS
Pennsylvania’s economy should work for everyone—not just the wealthy and well-connected.
Too many working people are stuck in jobs that don’t pay a living wage, provide unstable hours, or deny basic benefits.
As your State Representative, I will fight to make sure every worker has the dignity, security, and respect they deserve.
HEALTHCARE
FOR ALL
Health care is a human right, not a privilege.
No one should have to choose between paying for medicine and paying for rent, yet too many Pennsylvanians are uninsured, underinsured, or drowning in medical debt.
Our health care system should put people over profits and guarantee that everyone—no matter their income, ZIP code, or job—can get the care they need.
CHILDCARE
FOR ALL
No parent should have to choose between earning a paycheck and ensuring their child is safe and cared for.
In Pennsylvania, too many working families are paying more for child care than their rent or mortgage—and too many providers are underpaid and under-supported.
Child care is not a luxury; it’s essential infrastructure for working families and our economy.
LGBTQ RIGHTS
Everyone deserves to live free from discrimination, harassment, and violence—no matter who they are or who they love.
Pennsylvania still lacks comprehensive protections for LGBTQ+ people, leaving too many at risk of losing their job, housing, or access to services simply because of their identity.
As your State Representative, I will work to ensure full equality under the law and a safe, welcoming Commonwealth for everyone.
CLIMATE
The climate crisis is here—and it’s already impacting Pennsylvania with extreme weather, flooding, poor air quality, and rising energy costs.
We have a moral obligation to act now, not only to protect our environment but to ensure that the transition to a clean energy economy benefits working families, not just corporations.
Climate justice means fighting pollution and environmental destruction while tackling economic inequality head-on.
Communities like Allentown and Salisbury Township know best what their residents need to thrive.
Local governments should have the power to pass laws and policies that reflect the values and priorities of their communities—not be overridden by one-size-fits-all mandates from Harrisburg or special interests.
LOCAL CONTROL
REPRODUCTIVE
RIGHTS
Our communities are stronger when people have the freedom and resources to decide if, when, and how to grow their families.
I will always stand with those fighting to protect reproductive rights and ensure Pennsylvania remains a safe place for care.
CAMPAIGN
FINANCE REFORM
Elected leaders should work for the people — not for the wealthy few and corporate special interests.
But right now, big money has too much influence in Harrisburg and in politics across the country.
Everyday Pennsylvanians deserve a government that listens to their voices, not the highest bidder.
