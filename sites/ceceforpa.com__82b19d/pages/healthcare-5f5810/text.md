Healthcare for All
- Support universal, affordable healthcare coverage for all Pennsylvanians.
- Protect and expand Medicaid so families don’t lose life-saving coverage.
- Invest in local health centers, mental health services, and substance use treatment programs.
- Cap the cost of essential medications like insulin.
- Hold Big Pharma accountable for price gouging.
- Expand affordable dental, vision, and hearing care.
- Stop corporate hospital closures that abandon urban and working-class neighborhoods.
- Ensure hospitals and healthcare providers put communities first, not corporate shareholders.
- End surprise billing and increase transparency in healthcare costs.
- Address health inequities that impact Black, Brown, LGBTQ+, and low-income communities.
- Strengthen public health with investments in preventative care.
- Tackle the root causes of poor health: housing, clean air and water, childcare, and education.
