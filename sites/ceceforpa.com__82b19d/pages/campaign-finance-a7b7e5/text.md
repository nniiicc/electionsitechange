Campaign Finance Reform
I don't take corporate or developer money.
I supported finance reform in Allentown and will keep fighting in Harrisburg.
I will always be accountable to the people of the 22nd District — never to corporate lobbyists or special interests.
Our campaign finance system must be reformed so that our government truly represents us all.
As State Representative, I will fight to:
- Ban corporate contributions to candidates and close loopholes that allow dark money to flow into our elections.
- Strengthen transparency and disclosure laws, so voters know exactly who is funding political campaigns.
- Establish fair contribution limits, to level the playing field and reduce the outsized influence of wealthy donors.
- Support public financing of elections, so everyday people — not just the wealthy or well-connected — can run for office.
- Empower small-dollar donors, encouraging grassroots campaigns that reflect the will of the people, not corporate PACs.
