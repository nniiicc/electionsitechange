EDUCATOR.
ARTIST.
Sarah Senff is an advocate, educator, and artist who hails from southern Ohio.
She uses a variety of methods to develop and implement programming for community issues.
She is particularly interested in community empowerment, civic and health education, and the medical humanities.
As an experienced facilitator of difficult dialogues, Sarah understands the complexities of the problems communities face, and works to support connection across difference.
She specializes in work focusing on health equity, particularly around intersectional and inclusively-defined women’s issues, disability, and resilience.
She has a passion for educating the public and health professionals alike.
In her free time, she is a consummate baker who enjoys hiking, reading, board games, and running… okay, walking half marathons in order to raise funds for St.
Jude Children’s Research Hospital.
