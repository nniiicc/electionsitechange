What are the three pillars of conservative community development?
Our approach to community development relies on three essential pillars: prosperity, preservation, and protection.
We foster prosperity by advocating for policies that stimulate local businesses, attract new investments, and create sustainable jobs.
We focus on preservation by defending the core constitutional rights and values that make our nation exceptional, ensuring our liberties are never impeded by government actions.
Finally, we prioritize protection by securing our borders, supporting law enforcement through initiatives like backing the blue, and remaining proactive against socialist ideologies.
Together, these pillars form a comprehensive strategy to empower families and ensure a safe, thriving future.
How can community advocacy programs protect local families and children?
We are deeply committed to protecting families through proactive legislation and community empowerment.
Our platform highlights crucial initiatives like the Alianna Act, designed as preventative legislation to safeguard our youth, and the SAVE SA Act, which focuses on building safe communities, reforming education, and cultivating vibrant businesses.
We believe in providing families with the resources they need to thrive, ensuring that safety and empowerment start at the local level.
By supporting our law enforcement and advocating for family-first educational and healthcare reforms, we work to build a reliable foundation where our children can inherit a secure, free, and prosperous nation.
What motivated the decision to run for public office?
Our decision to run for public office was solidified after witnessing key congressional votes that failed to denounce the devastating impacts of socialism.
Having experienced the harsh realities of a socialist regime firsthand during a family trip to Cuba, we saw the lack of basic necessities, the restriction of career choices, and the loss of personal freedoms.
We refuse to sit back while our communities inherit a weakened system built on failed ideologies.
We are driven by a lifetime of service—as a soldier, critical care nurse, and attorney—to protect our constitutional rights and secure a bright future.
How does faith guide leadership and community service efforts?
We believe that true leadership is demonstrated through actions, not just words.
Faith, family, and freedom form the bedrock of our campaign, guiding our commitment to justice, mercy, and stewardship.

By putting our faith into action, we aim to inspire others to engage in service and kindness, strengthening the moral fabric of our nation.
