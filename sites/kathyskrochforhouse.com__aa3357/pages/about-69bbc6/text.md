Biography Rep.
Kathy Skroch Representative Kathy Skroch is a lifelong farmer and rancher whose story is rooted in North Dakota soil and North Dakota values.
For 51 years, she has shared her life with her husband, Michael, building a strong, faith-filled family together.
They have seven grown children, six children-in-law, and eighteen grandchildren, and Kathy considers them her greatest blessing and her constant reminder of why strong communities and a strong state matter.
Kathy's life has been defined by hard work and service.
In addition to farming and ranching, she has managed a small business at Vinnie's Mud Bog, driven a school bus to safely get children to and from school, and cared for patients as a CNA and restorative nurse.
She has been deeply involved in her church and community, always stepping up where she is needed.
Her neighbors first sent her to the North Dakota House of Representatives in 2016 and reelected her in 2020.
After redistricting, she ran again in 2022.
In 2025, following the passing of Representative Cynthia Schreiber-Beck, Kathy was appointed to return to the legislature.
Today, she is running for a full term in 2026 so she can continue being a strong voice for her community.
In the legislature, Kathy has served on the Agriculture, Agriculture and Water Management, and Human Services Committees and has contributed to efforts addressing mental illness and guardianship.
She brings a farmer's practicality, a caregiver's compassion, and a mom and grandma's determination to protect the next generation.
Kathy's conservative principles guide her work: she is pro-life, pro-family, and pro-freedom.
She stands up for property rights and water rights, fights for property tax relief, and defends the common-sense North Dakota values that make our communities strong.
Above all, she is a leader who listens — taking the time to hear from the people she represents and working hard to turn their concerns into real solutions.
