Bills & Sponsorships Legislation That Delivered Results HB 1511 Medical Privacy & Stopping Vaccine Passports I promised to protect your civil liberties and property rights.
That promise led to HB 1511, which enshrined your right to medical privacy and stopped vaccine passports dead in their tracks.
HB 1340 Strengthening Property Rights Through HB 1340, I made it easier for those who have fallen on hard times to keep their home, strengthening your property rights where previously your property could be easily seized by the government.
HB 1323 Removing the Mask Mandate With HB 1323, I helped remove the mask mandate and put the decision back into your community's hands.
HB 1408 Reinstating Rural Highway Department Shops I co-sponsored HB 1408, which reinstated five of Richland County's ND Highway Department shops that were scheduled to be closed, improving snow removal in rural areas and protecting our schools, employers, emergency personnel, and business owners in our small towns.
SB 2345 Funding Rural Hospitals & EMS By helping pass SB 2345, I secured funding for our rural hospitals and rural emergency medical care so that our communities can maintain access to healthcare.
HB 1505 County Roads, Bridges & Prairie Dog Funding HB 1505 increased funding for county and township roads and bridges, and boosted Prairie Dog funding to non-oil producing counties and townships.
HB 1285 Expanding Abandoned Infant Immunity (Prime Sponsor) As the prime sponsor of HB 1285, I expanded the Abandoned Infant Immunity statute, providing for a training program and additional locations to safely deliver a child, protecting children from abuse and helping place them in safe, loving homes.
Additionally, I sponsored a bill which mandated bus driver safety training for drivers of mini-buses.
HB 1365 Fast-Tracking Mental Health Treatment (Prime Sponsor) Through HB 1365, which I also prime sponsored, families and individuals received relief, and treatment is now fast-tracked through medical providers to avoid delays in care.
HB 1181 Fitness to Proceed for Mentally Ill Defendants (Prime Sponsor) As the prime sponsor of HB 1181, I ensured that individuals who break laws while suffering from mental illness are found fit to proceed before appearing before the court.
