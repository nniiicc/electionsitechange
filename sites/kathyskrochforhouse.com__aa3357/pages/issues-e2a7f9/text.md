Kathy Skroch for ND House Home About Kathy Issues Proven Record Contact Donate Where I Stand Issues That Matter to District 25 Real solutions grounded in North Dakota common sense — pro-life, pro-family, pro-freedom, and pro-property rights.
Honest, Transparent, and Accountable Leadership North Dakotans want transparency and accountability in government—no matter their party.
I support making state finances and campaign disclosures as easy as possible to access.
I support strengthening open-records practices, and ensuring ethics rules are fair, clear, and consistently applied.
Integrity is not a partisan issue: every public servant should be honest, accessible, and answerable to the people back home.
Lower Taxes and Responsible Spending Families, farmers, and small businesses are already squeezed by inflation and rising cost of living.
Higher taxes only make it harder to thrive in North Dakota.
I support lowering the tax burden wherever possible, while prioritizing essential services and cutting wasteful spending.
Every dollar the government spends comes from a North Dakota worker or retiree, and it should be treated with respect.
We must continue our property tax relief, but we must truly reform out of control property tax valuations.
Quality Education with North Dakota Values I support strong neighborhood schools, vocational education, and community colleges that equip students with skills needed in our farms, shops, and main-street businesses.
This includes making sure the state government fully funds North Dakota's K-12 school system, just as our state constitution requires.
Parents and local school boards – not distant bureaucracies in Washington, D.C. – should have the final say on what is taught in the classroom.
Keeping Rural Hospitals and Emergency Care Open No family should have to drive hours in a blizzard to reach life-saving care.
I will continue working to support rural clinics, hospitals, and ambulance services so people can get help close to home.
Strong local healthcare keeps our communities healthy, supports our seniors, and gives young families confidence to put down roots here.
I'm committed to helping to ensure recent federal Rural Healthcare Transformation Funds are used effectively and wisely to enhance delivery of care.
Strong Rural Infrastructure and Safe Roads Safe roads, plowed highways, and sound bridges are lifelines for school buses, ambulances, and farm trucks across our state.
I support fair, responsible funding for rural infrastructure so our communities are not left behind larger cities.
When winter storms hit or crops need to move, North Dakotans must be able to count on their roads and local shops.
Protecting Property, Water, and Our Way of Life In North Dakota, property is more than a line on a map—it is family history, a small business, or a farm built over generations.
Projects like carbon pipelines, massive factory-style dairies, and new transmission lines should never trample the rights of local landowners or threaten their water.
In District 25, families are facing real pressure from outside interests that want to force projects through our fields, near our homes, and over our aquifers.
I will fight to ensure government cannot easily seize homes or land, stand against the abusive use of eminent domain, and make sure regulations do not crush responsible landowners.
Protecting property rights and water resources keeps our small towns strong, safeguards private wells and family farms, and preserves our North Dakota way of life for the next generation.
Protecting Medical Freedom and Privacy North Dakota families—not bureaucrats, big hospitals, or out-of-state interests—should decide what is best for their health.
I support policies that protect medical privacy, oppose heavy-handed mandates, and respect personal responsibility and common sense.
Our state must remain a place where your medical choices stay between you, your family, and your doctor.
Caring for Vulnerable Children and Families North Dakota should always be a place where every child is valued and protected.
I back policies that expand safe options for infants in danger, strengthen protections against abuse, and connect struggling families with resources that keep kids in safe, loving homes.
Standing up for the most vulnerable reflects both our values and our responsibilities as neighbors.
Mental Health, Justice, and Public Safety When someone breaks the law while struggling with serious mental health issues, we need a system that protects the public but also recognizes their condition.
I support efforts to ensure people are properly evaluated and get appropriate treatment while still holding them responsible for their actions.
We must address the problem of repeat offenders with stricter sentencing.
North Dakotans deserve a justice system that is firm, fair, and grounded in common sense.
Keeping Local Control in Our Communities Whether it is school policies, health decisions, or community standards, local leaders and parents are closest to the people and know what works here.
I will push back against one-size-fits-all mandates from Washington or Bismarck that ignore the realities of rural North Dakota.
Local control keeps government accountable and responsive to neighbors, not special interests.
Kathy Skroch North Dakota House of Representatives, District 25.
An experienced leader who listens.
