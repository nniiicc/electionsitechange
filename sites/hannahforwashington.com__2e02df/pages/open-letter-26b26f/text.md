To the Voters of the 43rd Legislative District (Capitol Hill, First Hill, Downtown, Belltown, University District) and beyond:
We are progressive community organizers and leaders standing up to the wealthiest and most powerful institutions in the world.
We fight every day to build more affordable housing, to create a transit-rich and walkable city, to defend queer people, trans people, and immigrants from fascist attacks, and to make Washington a place we can afford to build good lives in.
We are used to our work being undercut by establishment institutions and wealthy interests that benefit from the status quo.
What we are not used to is The Stranger’s endorsements upholding the same tired messaging of those establishment institutions.
That’s why their primary endorsements this year, especially in the 43rd Legislative District State Senate race, cut so deep.
Hannah Sabio-Howell is a proven progressive leader and seasoned public servant in our community.
She is a forceful champion for a more just, equitable, and progressive Washington who we have worked alongside for nearly a decade.
She is a queer woman of color with deep ties to our district and our state taking on a 20-year incumbent, and we trust her and back her for a reason.
Hannah worked for years in the state House of Representatives as a legislative aide and in the state Senate as a communications specialist, where she worked on successful efforts to tax the rich like the Capital Gains Tax, make healthcare more affordable with an expansion of postpartum Medicaid, and bring everyday people into the legislative process.
There, she also helped pave a path to the historic unionization of Washington’s legislative staff, organizing with her coworkers to end their exclusion from collective bargaining rights.
Hannah then went on to be a leader in a broad labor coalition including domestic workers, service workers, and farm workers across the state, where she helped build the grassroots movement that won the Domestic Workers Bill of Rights.
She has organized shoulder-to shoulder with food delivery gig workers to take on giant corporations like DoorDash to defend fair wages, and won.
She has been a key part of redefining what fair workplaces look like and what all workers deserve.
The local media establishment lauds the “institutional seasoning” of the 20-year incumbent, and dismisses Hannah’s legislative and organizing experience, while failing to acknowledge the grassroots coalition pressure that made progressive wins possible.
We don’t need editorial boards telling us who is allowed to lead and when, or framing grassroots momentum a liability.
We certainly don’t abide by a political analysis that encourages progressive young leaders to move to communities that aren’t theirs just to run for office.
Our experiences deserve to be honored, our movements deserve to be respected, and our voices will be heard at the ballot box, just like we’re being heard across the country in win after win where progressive challengers are beating incumbents from the left.
That’s why Hannah has earned the sole endorsements of the 43rd District Democrats, King County Democrats, Working Families Party, The Urbanist, Seattle Gay News, and Seattle’s prominent progressive voices, like Mayor Katie Wilson, Rep.
Shaun Scott, Councilmember Alexis Mercedes Rinck, and Commissioner Toshiko Hasegawa.
They know that a real progressive affordability agenda is only possible when we have organizers on the inside.
We hope you will join us in voting for Hannah Sabio-Howell for State Senate by August 4.
