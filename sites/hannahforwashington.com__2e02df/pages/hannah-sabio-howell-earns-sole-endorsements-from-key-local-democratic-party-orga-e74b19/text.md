The district spans neighborhoods such as Capitol Hill, First Hill, the University District, Fremont, Belltown, Downtown, Montlake, Eastlake and Laurelhurst, and is widely known as one of the most progressive districts in the country.
She is challenging Senate Majority Leader Jamie Pedersen, a 20-year incumbent.
Since launching March 9, the campaign has raised over $82,000 in individual donor dollars without taking a dime of corporate PAC money.
The campaign has also engaged over 85 volunteers, most of whom are under 30 or new to electoral politics.
With nearly 5,000 doors knocked, on top of the over 800 signatures from registered 43rd district voters gathered in April to waive the filing fee, Sabio-Howell has reached almost 6,000 voters with her message of an affordable future for Washington.
“Now is not the moment for more of the same,” said Alexis Mercedes Rinck, Seattle Citywide Councilmember, speaking in favor of Sabio-Howell’s 43rd Democrats endorsement at the meeting last Tuesday, June 16.
“Now is the moment for working people, and Hannah Sabio-Howell is the person who will fight for us.
Hannah is the kind of leader who knows that to make the change we need in our communities, the city and state need to lock arms and lock in.
I’m proud to endorse Hannah because of her work ethic, her integrity and her commitment to a Washington that works for all of us.
She is exactly the kind of leader we need in Olympia.” At the King County Democrats endorsement meeting on June 23, several members spoke in favor of a sole endorsement for Sabio-Howell.
What people are saying: “I stand in strong support of [the 43rd District Democrats’] endorsement of Hannah Sabio-Howell.
