Please save the date and stay tuned for a link to RSVP.
We’re leaving politics-as-usual in the past and doing things differently with our campaign, so our kickoff will be a celebration of the artistry, vibrancy, and diversity of our one-of-a-kind community.
There will be drag, live music from local artists, a live DJ, local photographers, merch, and maybe a surprise or two.
If you can help us with the set-up, any amount you can chip in helps SO much and means the world to me.

That’s over $52k’s worth of fuel for our people-powered campaign to get our message out to voters that a better, more affordable future is possible when we elect fighters who will go to the mat for that vision.
Raising over $52k makes for an incredible first month that no one thought possible.
By doing so, we’ve demonstrated loud and clear that we are all ready for change and it’s our time.
But… we are still up against a 20-year incumbent who takes thousands of dollars in corporate money, who’s starting this race with a whopping $187,000.
While we’ve seen over and over again that progressive young leaders can get outspent 10:1 and still win, we know our campaign needs to keep our foot on the gas from now to November.
Can you donate now to help us raise at least $30k in the month of April?
We’ve taken on a big fight, and it’s a fight worth having.
We need you in it with us to win.
