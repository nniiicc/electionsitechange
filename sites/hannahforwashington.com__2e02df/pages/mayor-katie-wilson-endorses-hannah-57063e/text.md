Here’s what the mayor said about the three candidates she endorsed, Hannah being one of them: “When I ran for office as a first-time candidate last year, I was propelled by a sense of urgency to tackle the problems I saw my neighbors facing in their daily lives.
Ever-rising costs of housing, childcare, and food.
More and more people sleeping unsheltered on our streets and in our parks.
All against a backdrop of historic wealth inequality and a cruel and hostile federal administration.
I stepped up because I believed that we could do better.
I see that same energy in three candidates who are running to represent parts of Seattle in the Washington State Legislature.
I’m proud to endorse Jaelynn Scott for State House LD 37, Position 2; Hannah Sabio-Howell for State Senate LD 43 ; and Ron Davis for State House LD 46, Position 1.
These candidates have more than good ideas and good values.
They’ve been doing the hard work — as community leaders, as organizers, as advocates — each in their own way, for many years.
They’re smart, creative, pragmatic, and progressive. … Hannah Sabio-Howell is, like me, a renter in the 43rd, where three in four residents now rent their homes.
She’s built her career organizing for stronger labor standards and workers’ rights.
I know she’ll be an effective and energetic fighter for the most progressive district in Washington state. … We’re at an inflection point in our city and our region.
Voters are feeling a warranted frustration with the status quo, and I believe these leaders can help us to chart the way forward.” – Mayor Katie Wilson, Mayor of Seattle And as for us, we are incredibly honored to have earned Mayor Wilson’s endorsement.
She ran on a platform about making Seattle a city that working people can afford to build good lives in, and that’s exactly our campaign’s vision too.
As renters, as organizers, we know what the crisis of affordability feels like because we live it and we know what it takes to fight against the ultra-wealthy interests that created it.
We’re so proud to be among the courageous community leaders that Mayor Wilson trusts to build an affordable future alongside her.
Thank you for all the people-power, money, and time you’ve contributed to our grassroots campaign already.
We wouldn’t be able to fight this fight without you.
