🔥 Primary Election Recap 🔥
From the launch of our campaign on March 9 to the day of the August 4 primary, we did something extraordinary.
Here’s a look at the movement we’re building by the numbers:
- 1,500+ signatures gathered to get on the ballot (820+ of which waived the fee)
- 15,000+ doors knocked
- 25,000+ direct voter contacts made
- $105,000+ in grassroots dollars raised, without taking a single dime of corporate or outside PAC spending
- 120+ individual volunteers backing our campaign, whether through knocking doors, phonebanking, writing thank-you notes, and more
- 800,000+ impressions across Instagram, TikTok, and Bluesky
🧐 Results Analysis 🧐
We’ll keep it real with you.
The primary election results were not what we hoped for.
Early returns had us winning 18% of the vote, and after a week of ballot-counting, we’re coming in closer to 25%.
But we are extremely proud of winning over 8,735 votes from voters in the Fighting 43rd despite being outspent 5:1 by our opponent, who used 20 years of corporate contributions and hundreds of thousands in outside PAC spending in his favor to try to drown us out.
