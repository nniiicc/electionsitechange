These past eight years I have had the honor of serving as a member of the Hiawatha City Council.
My priority on the council has always been to improve the lives of our residents and to make the city of the Hiawatha the best place it can to live, work, and play.
I am running for the Iowa House of Representatives because we have a government in Des Moines that wants your vote, but not your opinion.
I am running because you deserve a government that works for you – one that works to develops new, high paying jobs into the state, restores Iowa’s prestige with the nation’s best schools, and enables you to live the American Dream.
Iowans deserve a government that isn’t held hostage by out of state dark money groups, that doesn’t dictate their health choices, and that understands Liberty and Justice for all is not optional.
I ask for your support and your vote.
Thank you.
~ Aime
