A lifelong resident of Saint Louis Park, John still lives in the home his grandfather built, a testament to his deep roots in the community he hopes to represent in Washington.
Throughout his life, John has made tough calls in high-stakes environments.
Now, he is stepping up once more to serve his neighbors, his city, and his country.
Born to a hardworking middle-class family, John learned early the values of compassion, faith, service, and hard work.
His grandfather, a volunteer firefighter in Golden Valley, sparked John’s passion for public service by taking him on visits to the fire station as a child.
Those visits planted the seed for a lifelong career spent protecting Minnesotans.
A devoted husband to Connie since 1988, a proud father of two, and a recent grandfather, John’s life is firmly grounded in faith, family, and public service.
While pursuing a degree in Criminal Justice from the University of St.
Thomas, John interned with the Edina Police and rode along with officers in Saint Louis Park.
His dedication to service led him to the Minnesota State Patrol Academy in 1981.
As a State Trooper, John patrolled the highways of Hennepin County and also worked as a safety consultant for the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.
His leadership and trustworthiness earned him assignments on the Governor’s security detail and training opportunities with the FBI and U.S.
Secret Service.
