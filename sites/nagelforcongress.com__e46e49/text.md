Those visits planted the seed for a lifelong career spent protecting Minnesotans. ‍ A devoted husband to Connie since 1988, a proud father of two, and a recent grandfather, John’s life is firmly grounded in faith, family, and public service. ‍ While pursuing a degree in Criminal Justice from the University of St.

His dedication to service led him to the Minnesota State Patrol Academy in 1981.
As a State Trooper, John patrolled the highways of Hennepin County and also worked as a safety consultant for the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration.
His leadership and trustworthiness earned him assignments on the Governor’s security detail and training opportunities with the FBI and U.S.
Secret Service.

John rose to the rank of Sergeant at the State Patrol Training Academy, where he oversaw traffic safety programs and served as a resource for schools across Minnesota.
He helped train first responders through Minnesotans for Safe Driving and played a key role in expanding public safety education statewide.
His service earned him the Minnesota State Patrol’s Meritorious Service Award, Employee of the Year honors, and multiple community service awards for his outreach to children’s hospitals and shelters.
John also showed extraordinary courage in the field.
He once climbed onto a freeway sign to rescue a suicidal individual, and another time used a PIT maneuver to stop a fleeing bank robbery suspect during a high-speed pursuit.
After retiring as a Field Lieutenant in 2011, John never stopped serving.
He taught defensive driving and college courses on terrorism and world governments, while also serving on the security team for the Minnesota Twins.
He also became a Minnesota DNR Youth Firearm Instructor, teaching safety, ethics, and survival skills to the next generation of outdoorsmen.
