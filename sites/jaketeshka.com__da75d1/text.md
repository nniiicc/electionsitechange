Get Involved Meet Jake Jake Teshka State Representative Indiana House District 7 Faith · Family · Community About Jake A Neighbor, Not Just a Politician A lifelong Michiana resident, Jake Teshka lives the values he legislates — faith, family, and community service.
Jake earned his B.A. in Political Science from St.
Joseph's College and his Master of Public Affairs from IU South Bend.
Before the Statehouse, he served South Bend's 5th District on the Common Council — then brought that ground-level experience to Indianapolis in 2021.
Today, Jake lives in North Liberty with his wife and two children.
He works in business development, serves on various local nonprofit boards and attends The Vineyard Church in Mishawaka.

Championing Literacy Jake authored HEA 1558 (2023) and was the House sponsor of SEA 1 (2024) which mandated Science of Reading instruction across the state, held teacher prep programs accountable for ensuring teachers are properly trained in literacy, and delivered significant support to schools to supplement their literacy work.
As a result, 2025 saw the single largest increase in IREAD scores in state history and Indiana jumped to 6th in the nation in both 4th and 8th grade reading.
Education Fighting for Working People As the author of HEA 1343 (2023), HB 1038 (2024), and House sponsor of SEA 34 (2024), Jake has fought to ensure that state government is not standing between Hoosier workers and their right to earn a good living.
He has also promoted employee ownership as a way to save the small businesses that are the lifeblood of our communities and provide long term benefit to employees.
Economic Freedom Protecting Our Children Whether it is protecting kids from Big Tech (SB 199 [2026]), curbing woke ideology in schools (HB 1346 [2023]), ensuring age verification on adult websites (HB 1063 [2024]), or protecting reasonable childhood independence (HEA 1035 [2026]), Jake has tirelessly advocated for bolstering the safety of Hoosier kids and safeguarding their innocence.
Reclaiming Childhood Guarding Taxpayer Dollars As Chairman of the House Financial Institutions Committee, Jake has been involved in ensuring that taxpayer dollars are not being used in ways that harm Hoosiers.
Through direct legislation (HEA 1084 [2024], HEA 1008 [2023]) and behind the scenes work, he has fought to uphold the state's laws that forbid investment in companies owned by foreign adversaries, those who push ideological agendas over ROI, and Big Banks that undermine our constitutional rights.
