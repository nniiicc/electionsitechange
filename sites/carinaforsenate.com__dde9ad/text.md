Hi, I’m Carina Santa Maria When I talk with families around their kitchen tables, I hear the same thing again and again: people are doing everything right, but it’s still getting harder to make ends meet.
A mom and advocate bringing real-life experience—and real solutions—to Springfield.
The stock market may be up, but grocery bills, property taxes, childcare, and healthcare costs are squeezing working families.
Parents are worried about safety, struggling to keep up, and wondering if their kids will ever be able to afford to live in the communities they grew up in.
I’m running to take your stories and your concerns to Springfield—to fight for real solutions that make life more affordable, our streets safer, and our state more compassionate Because Illinois works best when government actually works for the people it serves.
What I’m Fighting For An Economy That Works for All of Us Safe, Thriving Communities Compassionate Leadership Accountable Government Smart housing, real public safety, and strong neighborhoods.
Policies that reflect real life—not Wall Street.
Ethical, transparent leadership that respects local voices.
Care‑first solutions for families, youth, and vulnerable communities.
