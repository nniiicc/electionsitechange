Officially Endorsed by Wendy Dunnington —Village of Arlington Heights Trustee
“I am proud to endorse Carina Santa Maria for Illinois State Senate.
I have had the privilege of serving alongside Carina as an Arlington Heights Village Trustee and have seen firsthand her strong commitment to our community.
Time and again, I have been impressed by her insight, work ethic, and integrity as an elected official.
Carina thoughtfully considers residents’ concerns, input from local businesses, and staff recommendations when making difficult decisions, always grounded in her values and her professional experience as a local nonprofit executive.
I am confident she will bring this same thoughtful, principled approach to serving families across Illinois.
Please join me in voting for Carina Santa Maria in November!”
-Wendy Dunnington
Village of Arlington Heights Trustee
