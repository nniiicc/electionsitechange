Officially Endorsed by Equality Illinois
I am honored to be endorsed by Equality Illinois for State Senate in the 27th District.
Equality Illinois is a leading voice for LGBTQ+ equality, dignity, and inclusion across our state, and their endorsement reflects shared values I have carried throughout my career in public service: protecting civil rights, standing up for marginalized communities, and ensuring that everyone—no matter who they are or who they love—can live safely and authentically.
I believe Illinois is strongest when our laws reflect fairness, compassion, and respect for all people.
That means defending LGBTQ+ rights, opposing discrimination in all its forms, and creating policies that affirm every individual’s humanity.
I’m grateful for the trust and support of Equality Illinois, and I look forward to continuing the work of building a more inclusive, just, and equitable Illinois—together.
