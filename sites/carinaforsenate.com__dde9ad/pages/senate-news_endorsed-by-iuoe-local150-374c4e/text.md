Officially Endorsed by International Union of Operating Engineers Local 150
I’m honored to be endorsed by International Union of Operating Engineers Local 150, one of the leading labor unions representing skilled workers who build and maintain the infrastructure our communities rely on every day.
IUOE Local 150 represents hardworking men and women across Illinois who keep our roads, bridges, utilities, and construction projects moving forward.
Their work strengthens our economy and creates good-paying jobs throughout the state.
In their endorsement, the union emphasized the importance of leadership that stands up for working people and invests in strong infrastructure:
“Hardworking Illinoisans are counting on determined leaders to stand up for their rights and ensure that we have a safe infrastructure that is a strength rather than a weakness when it comes to economic activity and job creation.”
I share that commitment.
Supporting workers’ rights, strengthening our infrastructure, and ensuring that economic growth benefits working families are essential priorities for Illinois.
I’m grateful for the support of IUOE Local 150 and proud to stand alongside the skilled workers who help build and power our communities every day.
