Greg and I have had the opportunity to serve alongside one another, ask tough questions, and make decisions that directly impact the community we both call home.
There’s something especially meaningful about earning the support of someone who has seen your work up close.
Thank you, Greg, for your support, your service to Arlington Heights, and your commitment to our community.
I’m proud to have you in my corner. ________________________- “ I am proud to endorse Carina Santa Maria for State Senate in Illinois’ 27th District.
Having worked beside her on the Village Board in Arlington Heights, I know that Carina brings a deeply grounded, community first perspective to public service.
Through her work in non-profit leadership, public health, and local advocacy, she has consistently demonstrated that effective policy starts with listening to the people.
She understands the practical challenges facing our district: supporting small businesses and sustainable economic development, investing in infrastructure, and ensuring our communities remain safe, inclusive, and well-supported.
The 27th Senate District deserves a leader who will be an accessible, responsive, and relentless voice in Springfield.
Carina Santa-Maria has proven time and again that she puts community first, and she is precisely the kind of thoughtful leader we need representing us at the Capitol.
I strongly encourage you to join me in supporting Carina Santa Maria for State Senate. ” - Greg Zyck Arlington Heights Village Trustee Be a part of the change you want to see in our communities.
