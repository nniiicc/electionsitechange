Officially Endorsed by Chicago Fire Fighters Union Local 2
I’m honored to be endorsed by the Chicago Fire Fighters Union Local 2 for the 2026 Primary Election.
Chicago Fire Fighters Union Local 2 represents thousands of firefighters and paramedics who serve communities across the Chicago area with courage, professionalism, and dedication.
Their work protects families, strengthens public safety, and ensures that help is there when our communities need it most.
In their letter, the union shared that the Executive Board of Local 2 voted to endorse my candidacy and will be recommending me to their members and supporters in the upcoming primary election.
I’m deeply grateful for their support and proud to stand alongside the firefighters and emergency personnel who dedicate their lives to protecting others.
I will continue working to support policies that strengthen public safety, respect the work of first responders, and ensure our communities have the resources they need to stay safe.
