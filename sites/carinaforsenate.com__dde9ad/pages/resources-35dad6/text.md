Community Resources
If you or your family need support, help is available.
As your voice in Springfield, Carina believes every resident of District 27 deserves access to care, stability, and dignity — no matter what they're going through.
Below you'll find trusted organizations, crisis lines, and community resources serving Arlington Heights, Mount Prospect, Rolling Meadows, Palatine, Prospect Heights, Schaumburg, and surrounding communities.
If you're in crisis right now, please scroll to the Emergency & Crisis Support section first.
