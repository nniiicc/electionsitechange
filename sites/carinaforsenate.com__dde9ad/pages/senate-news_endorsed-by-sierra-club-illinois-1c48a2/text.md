Officially Endorsed by Sierra Club Illinois
I’m honored to be endorsed by Sierra Club Illinois, the Illinois chapter of America’s oldest and largest grassroots environmental organization.
This endorsement reflects our shared commitment to protecting our environment, advancing clean energy solutions, and defending public health for families across Illinois.
For decades, Sierra Club Illinois has mobilized communities to take on the climate crisis, fight for clean air and water, and build a future where every Illinois community has access to a healthy environment.
I’m proud to stand with them in the fight to:
- champion clean energy and job-creating climate solutions,
- safeguard the health of our neighborhoods from pollution and environmental harms,
- and advance policies that protect our natural resources for future generations.
Their support strengthens my resolve to lead with bold climate action and environmental justice at the center of our work in Springfield.
