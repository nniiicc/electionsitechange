Officially Endorsed by Arlington Heights Mayor Jim Tinaglia
I’m incredibly honored to have the support of Mayor Jim Tinaglia, someone I’m proud to serve alongside on the Arlington Heights Village Board.
There’s something especially meaningful about earning the trust of someone who has seen you do the work up close.
Local government has taught me that public service is about showing up prepared, listening to people, asking the hard questions, and finding common ground - even when we don’t always start in the same place.
Mayor Tinaglia and I share a deep love for Arlington Heights and a belief that community should always come first.
I’m grateful for his partnership, his trust, and his confidence in the kind of leadership I’ll bring to Springfield while representing the families of the 27th District.
Thank you, Mayor.
It means a lot. ❤️
“I’ve had the opportunity to work alongside Carina on the Arlington Heights Village Board, and I’ve seen firsthand how seriously she takes the responsibility of public service.
Carina listens to residents, to her colleagues, and to people who may see an issue differently than she does.
She asks thoughtful questions, does her homework, and looks for ways to bring people together around solutions that strengthen our community.
We have built a strong partnership rooted in our shared commitment to Arlington Heights, and I know Carina will bring that same collaborative, community-first approach to representing the 27th District in the Illinois Senate.”
-Jim Tinaglia
Mayor of Arlington Heights
