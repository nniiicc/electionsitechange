I’ve spent this campaign walking.
I’ve walked door to door, block to block, through parades and farmers markets, and I’ve had a lot of conversations on a lot of front porches.
It feels right to finish that walk together.
Meet me at the campaign office on Miner Street, and we’ll stroll over to Arlington Heights Village Hall to cast our ballots early.
It’s a short walk with good company, and you’ll have your vote in a full week before Election Day.
Bring your partner, your neighbor, your stroller, your dog, or the friend who keeps saying they’ll get around to it.
Expanded early voting starts October 19, so there’s no reason to wait until November 3 and hope the lines are short.
Let’s make the plan and do it together.
