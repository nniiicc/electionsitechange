Parents are worried about safety, struggling with childcare, and wondering if their kids will ever be able to afford to live in the communities they grew up in.

Around the District: Q+A Why are you running for State Senate?
Because our communities deserve a representative who understands the system and the people it’s meant to serve .
I’ve done that work my whole career—and I’m ready to take it to the next level.
What sets you apart from other candidates?
I bring a rare blend of hands-on experience and policy-level leadership.
I’ve worked in the trenches and in legislative chambers.
I don’t just talk about change—I make it happen.
Where do you see District 27 in four years?
Thriving—with stronger community services, more affordable housing, accessible mental health care, and smarter development that balances progress with sustainability.
What is the biggest challenge facing the district?
Affordability.
We need solutions that tackle rising costs—without sacrificing essential services or leaving families behind.
I’ll bring creative funding strategies and cross-sector partnerships to the table.
What three things do you love most about your community?
The sense of connection, the diversity of experiences, and the shared belief in building a better future.
Is there a diversity gap in the district?
How would you address it?
Yes.
I would work to break down language and cultural barriers, expand inclusive representation, and invest in community engagement efforts that reflect the full richness of our district.
