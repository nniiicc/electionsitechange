Cleary Wins District 35 Republican Primary
Bismarck Republican advances to the general election this fall
Bismarck, ND - Sean Cleary, a Bismarck Republican, has secured the Republican primary nomination for the State Senate in District 35.
“I am grateful for all of my supporters and everyone who voted in this primary election,” Cleary said.
“I look forwa
rd to earning the support from voters across the district this fall.”
Cleary works for the Theodore Roosevelt Medora Foundation in Bismarck.
He is a former staff member for US Senator John Hoeven and Governor Doug Burgum.
Cleary is an alumnus of the University of Mary and earned a master’s degree in applied economics from the University of North Dakota.
Cleary was born and raised in Bismarck, where he now lives with his wife Amy and daughter Audrey.
