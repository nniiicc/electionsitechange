what it means to be a
Clean Elections candidate
Running as a clean elections candidate allows me to focus on what matters most: knocking on doors, listening to you, and finding real solutions for our community.
It puts people before big money and ensures our campaign is about ideas, not fundraising.
To qualify for public funds through the Maine Clean Election Act, I must show community support by collecting a minimum number of contributions of $5 from registered voters in the district.
Every contribution counts and demonstrates that our campaign is powered by the people!
