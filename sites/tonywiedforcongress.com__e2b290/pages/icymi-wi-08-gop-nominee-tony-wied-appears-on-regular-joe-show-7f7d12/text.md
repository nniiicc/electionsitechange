Wied discussed his plan to go to Washington to lower prices, secure the border, and reign in out-of-control government spending.
He also blasted his opponent for being out of touch with WI-08 voters.
“My opponent has actively campaigned with those who call to abolish ICE and defund the police,” Wied stated, pointing to Kristin Lyerly’s recent campaign stops with extreme liberal Rep.
Mark Pocan and the Democrat Socialists of America.
“That is just too far left for the people of this district,” Wied added.
Wied also discussed his small business background and how he will take that experience to Washington to solve problems for the voters of WI-08.
“Voters need to look at both of our records and what they will find is that I am the right choice for this district,” said Wied.
