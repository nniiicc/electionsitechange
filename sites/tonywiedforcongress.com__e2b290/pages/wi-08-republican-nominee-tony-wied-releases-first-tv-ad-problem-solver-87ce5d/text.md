“I spent decades creating jobs right here in Northeast Wisconsin and one thing I learned is real success doesn’t come from winning arguments, it comes from solving problems,” said Wied.
Wied’s ad goes on to focus on his commitment to securing our southern border, protecting senior’s Social Security and Medicare benefits, and ensuring Wisconsin farmers continue to thrive.
“WI-08 voters deserve to be represented by someone with real world experience and a history of solving problems.
Unlike his opponent, who would rather spend her time attacking people, Tony is focused on getting to work for the people of Northeast Wisconsin,” said Wied Campaign Manager Aidan Strongreen.
