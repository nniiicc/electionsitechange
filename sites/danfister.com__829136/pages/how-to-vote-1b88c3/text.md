Here are three ways to cast your ballot in the General Election for the 56th District.
Three Ways to Vote “ Every vote counts.
Make your voice heard — vote for real representation in the 56th District. ” Option 1 — Absentee: Request your ballot from GoVoteKY.com.
Mail it back or drop it off before Election Day.
Be sure to sign both envelopes.
Option 2 — Early In-Person: Check your county clerk for dates and hours.
Locations: Woodford County Courthouse, 103 S Main Street, Versailles, KY 40383 · Jessamine County Clerk's Office, 101 N Main St, Nicholasville, KY 40356 · Franklin County Election HQ, 317 West 2nd Street, Frankfort, KY 40601.
Option 3 — Election Day: Vote at your assigned precinct.
Polls open 6am–6pm.
For more information, visit elect.ky.gov or contact your county clerk's office in Woodford, Franklin, or Jessamine County.
