Education
Every child deserves access to a high-quality public education, regardless of their ZIP code.
Strong public schools create opportunity, strengthen communities, and help prepare the next generation for success.
Priorities:
- Fully fund Wisconsin's public schools.
- Increase special education reimbursement and support services.
- Provide free, healthy school meals for all students.
- Invest in educators, classrooms, and student success.
- Protect public education from privatization and disinvestment.
