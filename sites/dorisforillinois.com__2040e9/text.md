ABOUT DORIS TURNER
REPRESENTING THE 48TH DISTRICT
With more than 20 years of experience with the Illinois Department of Public Health, Doris has worked to expand affordable, exceptional healthcare for all residents, especially those in rural and low-income communities.
She believes in the importance of constituent services and taking a one-on-one approach with the people she proudly serves.
Whether it be agriculture, tourism and hospitality, or education, you can always count on Doris to fight for our values.
