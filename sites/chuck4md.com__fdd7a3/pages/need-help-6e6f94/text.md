Mary’s: Feed St.
Mary’s is a group working to alleviate food insecurity throughout St.
Mary’s County.
Their vision is a county free from hunger.
They have resources on local food banks, donation sites, and food-related resources such as a printable pantry map.
Go to Feed St.
Mary's Secondary Education Community College Promise Scholarship: This is a last-dollar scholarship that is available for the 2025-2026 academic year to students who enroll at a Maryland community college.
Eligible students can receive up to $5,000 to cover any remaining tuition and mandatory fee expenses after federal or state financial aid and grants have been applied.
Those who qualify are students whose families earn less than $150,000 a year and or less than $100,000 in a single-parent household.
