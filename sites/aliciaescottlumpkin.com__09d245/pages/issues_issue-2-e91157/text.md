Alicia believes in
Protecting Access to Healthcare
- Work to expand the healthcare network.
- Advocate for increased access to affordable and preventative care.
- Protect Medicare and Medicaid from harmful cuts and ensure seniors, people with disabilities, and low-income families keep the care they've earned.
- Protect reproductive rights and ensure patients can make their own health decisions.
- Address disparities in care, particularly the maternal health crisis affecting Black women.
- Prioritize the protection of healthcare access and benefits for veterans and their families.
