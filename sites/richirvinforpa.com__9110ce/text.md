Rich Irvin was elected to the Pennsylvania House of Representatives in 2014.
Prior to being elected to the House, he served for 18 years as the Huntingdon County Treasurer.
Rich has earned a reputation as one of the most conservative members in the state legislature.
He has supported legislation that protects the unborn, defends our 2nd Amendment rights and halts the government overreach by liberal politicians and activists.
Rich currently serves as a member on the following House committees: Agriculture and Rural Affairs, Insurance, Labor and Industry and Local Government.
Rich is a graduate of Juniata Valley High School and earned a Bachelor of Science degree in accounting from Indiana University of Pennsylvania.
He is a longtime member and past president of the Huntingdon County United Way.
Rich is also a board member of the Center for Community Action, a human service agency serving Huntingdon, Bedford and Fulton counties.
Rich resides in Spruce Creek Township with his wife, Jaime, and their children Brent, Emma, Bruce and James.
