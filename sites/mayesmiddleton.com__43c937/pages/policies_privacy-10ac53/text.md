INTRODUCTION
Mayes Middleton for Attorney General (“Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our campaign website, use our mobile application, sign up for our mailing list, make a donation, volunteer, or otherwise interact with our campaign.
Please read this Privacy Policy carefully.
By accessing or using our services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to be bound by all terms of this Privacy Policy.
If you do not agree with our policies, please do not access or use our services.
INFORMATION WE COLLECT
Personal Information We may collect the following types of personal information:
- Name, email address, postal address, phone number
- Donation information, including credit card details and billing information
- Employment information
- Political preferences and affiliations
- Volunteer availability and skills
Non-Personal Information When you interact with our services, we may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage patterns, including:
- Device information (e.g., IP address, browser type, operating system)
- Usage details (e.g., pages visited, time spent on pages, click patterns)
- Location information (with your consent)
HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
We may use the information we collect for various purposes, including:
- Communicating with you about the campaign
- Processing donations
- Organizing volunteer activities
- Analyzing and improving our services
- Complying with applicable laws, including campaign finance laws
- Targeting political advertisements and sending campaign updates
- Conducting polls and surveys
- Preventing fraud and enhancing security
SHARING OF YOUR INFORMATION
We may share your information with:
- Campaign staff, consultants, and volunteers
- Service providers and vendors who assist with campaign operations
- Other political organizations and committees with aligned interests (with your consent)
- Legal and regulatory authorities, when required by law
We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties for commercial purposes.
TEXT MESSAGE PROGRAM
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
If you opt into our text message program:
- Message frequency varies (typically up to 3 messages per week)
- Standard message and data rates may apply
- You can opt out at any time by texting STOP
- Text HELP for assistance
- Consent is not required for contributions or purchases
DATA SECURITY
We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.
However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
YOUR CHOICES
You have certain rights regarding your personal information:
Opt out of receiving communications Access, correct, or delete your personal information Withdraw consent where we rely on consent as the legal basis for processing Object to certain uses of your information

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete that information promptly.
