Defending Wyoming’s Values
As your representative, I'll keep listening, keep working, and keep fighting for the practical, conservative values that have made Wyoming the best place on earth to live, work, and raise our families.
Our Shared Values That Guide Me
Strong Families
Families are the foundation of Wyoming.
I'll champion parental rights, protect family values, and ensure parents always have the final say in their children's upbringing and education.
Lower Taxes
I believe Wyoming taxpayers deserve relief whenever it can be provided responsibly.
I'll continue fighting for lower taxes while making sure we maintain the strong financial foundation that keeps Wyoming's communities viable and strong.
Safe & Viable Communities
Strong, safe communities require the resources to support law enforcement, fire, and EMS while maintaining a reliable infrastructure, and providing services that families count on every day.
2nd Amendment Champion
I will always defend the Second Amendment – a right that shall not be infringed – and our Wyoming way of life.
Pro Business
Business and industry are the backbone of our economy.
I'll continue to review and reduce burdensome regulations, and create an environment that attracts industry and business large and small to our state.
Healthcare & Insurance
Wyoming is a CMS frontier state for health care delivery due to our small population and large geographic area.
Strong communities depend on affordable, accessible healthcare, and people shouldn't have to postpone care because they can't afford insurance.
I currently chair a task force committed to resolving these problems.
