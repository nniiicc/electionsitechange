Meet Sam Forstag
Candidate for Montana's First District
Sam is a smokejumper and union leader — a working Montanan who understands the struggle of breaking your back trying to get ahead and still barely getting by.
Sam was raised by a mom who picked up extra nursing shifts and a dad who became a public school teacher, and they made just enough to get by.
That childhood taught Sam the value of hard work.
It also taught him just how hard it can be for working people to get by in an economy that too often doesn’t work for them.
After working his way through school at the University of Montana, he went to work for the Forest Service, where he climbed the ranks to become a smokejumper – a firefighter who parachutes into remote areas to stop fires before they grow.
Between fire seasons, Sam never stopped showing up for Montanans, working as an organizer across Montana and an advocate at the state legislature, where he fought for public education, libraries, healthcare and constitutional freedoms.
When Elon Musk tried to gut the Forest Service and public land agencies, Ryan Zinke didn’t stand up for Montanans — but Sam did.
As Vice President of his union’s local, Sam organized against Musk’s cuts and rallied Montanans around the real-life impacts of Congress’ inaction.
Year after year, Montanans keep seeing the cost of their groceries, housing, healthcare and childcare go up.
Sam’s running for Congress because he knows it shouldn’t be this hard to afford the basics in Montana.
It’s time working families in Montana had one of their own fighting for them in Congress.

OUR PLATFORM
Every Montanan should be able to work a good-paying job that allows them to afford housing, healthcare, childcare, and a good retirement.
That’s not the case anymore for so many Montanans, but it can be.
Right now in this country, we deal with basic needs like housing and healthcare the same way that we deal with forest management and wildfire – we wait until it’s an absolute crisis, and then we pay for it in the most expensive, least effective way possible.
Whether that’s a 60 year-old woman sitting in the emergency room because she couldn’t afford a primary care visit, or a wildfire burning into a subdivision because our leaders lacked the courage to invest in our public lands up front.
We can spend our resources more wisely and solve the biggest problems facing our country by taking action now.
For so many of us in Montana, the cost of just living here has reached crisis levels – but we’ve solved crises like these before, and we can solve them again by investing in affordable housing & childcare, reforming our broken healthcare system, and making corporations and the ultra-rich carry their own weight just like the rest of us.
For years now, national leaders in both parties have left working people in Montana behind.
It’s time for a new generation of leadership to step up and make Congress finally work for working people.
