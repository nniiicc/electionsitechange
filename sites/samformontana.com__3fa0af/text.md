Play Video Who Is Sam Forstag?
A Smokejumper and Union Leader Sam grew up with a mom who picked up extra nursing shifts and a dad who taught in public schools.
They made just enough to get by, and that taught him what it takes for working people to stay afloat in an economy built for someone else.
He worked his way through the University of Montana, then joined the Forest Service and became a smokejumper — a firefighter who parachutes into remote country to stop fires before they grow.
Between fire seasons, he organized across the state and fought at the legislature for public schools, libraries, healthcare, and civil liberties.
Meet Sam Where Sam Stands Build a country that works for working people.
Here’s how: Make Housing Work for Working People Montanans are being priced out of the towns we work in.
Sam will make federal housing investments that match the crisis, reform outdated zoning, ban hedge funds from buying up our homes, and restore the first-time homebuyer credit.
Read The Plan Make Healthcare Work for Working People Private insurance leaves too many of us paying more for worse coverage.
Sam will give every Montanan the choice of free Medicare for working people, expand it to cover dental, vision, and hearing, and let Medicare negotiate all drug prices.
Read The Plan Get Money Out Of Politics Unlimited money in politics keeps working people out of Congress and makes life less affordable under both parties.
Sam will overturn Citizens United, lower contribution limits, ban members of Congress from trading stocks, and stop former members from becoming lobbyists.
