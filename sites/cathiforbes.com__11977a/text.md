CATHI FORBES
GETS IT DONE.
For more than 20 years, Cathi Forbes has successfully advocated for our community.
She led the recent campaign to rebuild Towson High School, and an earlier fight to build West Towson Elementary and improve conditions at Rodgers Forge, Stoneleigh and Dumbarton.
She has secured critical state funding to help Towson-based non-profits serve people with developmental disabilities, unhoused people and food insecure families.
And as Towson’s representative in the Maryland House of Delegates, Cathi continues to vote like an advocate for education, the environment, health care and social justice.
