Hello Gilbert I am running for the Arizona State Senate in LD14 to bring practical, solutions-focused leadership to the Legislature.
I believe Arizona’s future depends on our ability to work together, solve problems, and make smart decisions today.
My priorities include strengthening Arizona's economy, rejuvenating public education, promoting responsible energy production, and protecting Arizona’s water resources so families, businesses, and communities can thrive.
I am committed to listening, serving, and helping build a stronger, more united future for Arizona.
About Stephanie Walsh Stephanie is an engineer, nonprofit leader, and entrepreneur from Gilbert, Arizona.
She is the founder and former owner/CEO of Walsh Engineering Services, a full-service engineering firm that quickly grew to 100+ employees under her leadership.
After selling the company, Stephanie shifted her focus toward public service, energy policy, and community impact. ​ Stephanie has served on several nonprofit and advisory boards, including the Department of Energy’s BOOST Energy Innovation Board, the Partnership for Science and Technology, the Museum of Idaho, and Habitat for Humanity.
She is the founder of the Idaho Sustainability and Energy Coalition, which promotes practical clean energy solutions, leads Idaho’s geothermal energy consortium, supports Tribal energy sovereignty initiatives, and works to advance reliable, affordable, and environmentally responsible energy development. ​ Stephanie and her husband, Stuart, are the proud parents of two adult children: a 25-year-old mechanical engineer and a 22-year-old environmental science student at Arizona State University.
