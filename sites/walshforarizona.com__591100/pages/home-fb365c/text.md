ABOUT STEPHANIE
Bringing Experience to Serve You
As a dedicated advocate for the people of Legislative District 14, I am committed to bringing positive progress and long-term success for Arizona.
I am an engineer with over 30 years of experience in energy, business, and creating practical solutions to difficult problems.
I'm ready to bring my experience and work ethic to the Arizona Senate.
Today's issues around water, our local economy, and jobs require real solutions that address competing needs.
Tomorrow's emerging challenges will be even greater--they will require strategic plans and strong decisions that will lift up everyone in our community.
I will fight for financial accountability and transparency, and for quality of life--from water rates to energy prices to quality education.
My team and supporters are driven by a passion to see progress, not division.
To see ethics and community service as the foundation for political service.
To move forward in unity and service for all constituents--not for personal gain or influenced by divisive politics.
By anchoring our actions with our core values of respect, honesty, and cooperation, my team will help create a better future for Arizona.
One that respects our past and our future, in cooperation our neighbors.
