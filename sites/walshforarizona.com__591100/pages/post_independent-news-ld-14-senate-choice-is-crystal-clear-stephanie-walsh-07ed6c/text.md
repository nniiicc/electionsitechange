The status of the Colorado River is getting dire, and I’ve seen it with my own eyes while driving to San Diego.
Energy and water costs continue to escalate, hurting middle-class Arizonans.
Our ballots are being delivered this week, and we Gilbert residents have a real opportunity this election.
We have a candidate for the State Senate in Legislative District 14 who has a lifetime of experience and expertise finding solutions to these critical problems.
Stephanie Walsh is an engineer who built an energy company of over 100 employees working on solving the exact issues we are dealing with.
By electing her to our state Legislature, we can help not just Gilbert, but Arizona as well.
I’m imploring voters, regardless of their party affiliation, to look at the two candidates, not just the letter next to their name.
Stephanie is running against Andy Biggs’ 26-year-old daughter, who has no experience or qualifications.
She’s running strictly on her father’s name recognition and his coattails.
She’s on the record stating that women should not hold public office.
Our choice is crystal clear.
Let’s send a real problem solver, moderate, brilliant woman to our state Senate.
Vote for Stephanie Walsh.
