Melody Cunningham believes that we must put patients before profits.
She believes that healthcare is a fundamental right and that the government should not interfere with private medical decisions between physicians, patients and families.
She believes that a woman has a right to reproductive freedom and privacy as enshrined in the Montana Constitution.
She recognized that LR-131 would harm infants and families.
Through her strong advocacy and voice, she helped to defeat it.
She is pictured here on a home visit with a hospice patient for whom coming to the hospital was a challenge.
Affordable housing Safe and affordable housing is a right for all.
Melody believes that this is a value that all Montanans hold dear.
Due to dramatic property tax increases and rising housing costs, there is a severe shortage of homes that community members in all areas of Montana can afford to buy and rent.
In the 2025 session, we were able to make meaningful changes in property taxes and thus affordable housing.
If re-elected, she will continue this fight to further protect our hardworking Montanans and for fair and affordable housing and property taxes for all.
Public education As a former teacher, a first-generation college grad and retired member of MFPE, Melody believes that public education is the great equalizer and the backbone of democracy.
She was advantaged by amazing public institutions through high school and for medical school.
She opposes use of taxpayer dollars for funding of private charter schools that have no oversight of school boards.
She will work hard to protect public education and equitable pay and benefits for our educators, staff and union members who are essential to our schools and our children.
Climate and environment Melody realizes that protection of our environment promotes both resiliency and our local economies.
She is a stalwart supporter of continuing to develop clean energy technology and believes that the only way to ensure the future of the planet for our children and grandchildren is to transition to sustainable energy sources.
She is a steward of her trees, wildlife and property and has earned a degree in Climate Change and Health.
Clean energy technology exists and could not only protect the environment but stimulate the economy.
Rural businesses in Montana have been awarded funds to become more resilient and energy efficient.
LGBTQ+ rights Dr.
Cunningham believes that the government should not interfere in healthcare decisions thoughtfully made by parents, patients and medical teams.
Melody believes that her voice and knowledge of the intimate patient, parent and healthcare team decisions made in the privacy of the office, along with standard of care practices, will continue to be an important asset to Montanans.
Seniors Melody has navigated senior issues with her 91-year old mother here in Missoula.
She is committed to being a leader in ensuring that Montana seniors have access to high-quality healthcare, that the state fully funds provider rates for residential care facilities, and supports local aging councils.
Melody is also committed to bolstering affordability so that seniors can remain in their homes amid rising property taxes and cost of living.
She is pictured here with one of her mother's neighbors.
Veterans As the daughter of a Purple Heart recipient, Melody looks forward to continuing to support Montana veterans.
From the provision of mental health care, especially for suicide prevention, to property tax relief, Melody believes that the State Legislature has a critical role in serving the people and families who have given their lives, health and heart to protect our state and country.
