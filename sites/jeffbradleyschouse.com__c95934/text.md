Jeff Bradley, a Hilton Head Island resident since 1979, has combined his financial expertise and deep community involvement to serve as a highly effective leader in the South Carolina House of Representatives.
Representing District 123, which now includes Hilton Head Island, Daufuskie Island, and parts of Jasper County, Bradley has led with a focus on education, regulatory reform, and public infrastructure.
As Chairman of the House Regulations and Administrative Procedures Committee and Vice Chairman of the Education and Public Works Committee, Bradley has championed initiatives that enhance the quality of life for his constituents.
His commitment to community service, including founding GED Camp Hilton Head and serving on multiple local boards, makes him a trusted advocate for the people he represents.
Re-elect Jeff Bradley for proven leadership that delivers real results.
