Accomplishments
Heather is fighting for freedom and to hold the powerful to account.
During her first two terms in the SC House, she has helped pass legislation to create jobs in Columbia, provide free school meals to kids, and to promote outdoor recreation.
Heather has also used her power to take on members of both parties by pushing for accountability in government.

Accountability for the Comptroller General
Heather helped force an incompetent comptroller general from office after he lost track of $3.5 Billion.
Heather’s budget amendment to reduce his salary to $1 passed with overwhelming bi-partisan support.
Then he resigned.
Protecting Women
To protect SC women, Heather passed an amendment to prevent women who miscarry a pregnancy at home from being jailed.
This is an important safeguard following the enactment of the Republican legislature’s disastrous six-week abortion ban.
Oversight for Richland One School District
Heather believes in public education—public schools gave her the opportunity to go to college, start a business, and serve in the SC House.
But too many children in Richland One are being left behind, and the school district continues to struggle to recruit and retain teachers.
Heather stood with teachers, parents, and students last year over the school district’s poorly planned, mid-year teacher reassignments.
She also asked the SC Office of the Inspector General (OIG) to examine Richland One’s spending on a $31 million facility.
That probe revealed the school district violated procurement and permitting laws in pursuing the project.

Richland Mall Redevelopment
Demolition of the Richland Mall on Forest Drive is underway thanks to budget funding Heather helped secure to redevelop this important property into a long-overdue commercial hub for our district.
Hunting & Fishing in South Carolina
Heather serves as the Co-Chair of the South Carolina Legislative Sportsman caucus and has helped pass legislation that will help promote hunting and fishing to SC’s women and girls so every South Carolinian can enjoy our state’s beauty and abundant natural resources.
Her bill to protect public land for hunting has passed the House, sub and full committee in the Senate and is expected to become law this legislative session.
