Focused on Your Priorities
Protect Civil Rights & Liberties
Cole will fight to safeguard and expand the civil rights and liberties of all people against modern threats.
That includes all federal government overreach or corporate surveillance that infringes on people’s data privacy, free speech, due process, voting rights, and individual freedom to make decisions about your life.
Build an Economy for the American Dream
Cole will fight to lower healthcare costs with more transparency in medical billing, health insurance premiums, and prescription drug costs.
Cole will also work to make housing more affordable by reducing property tax burdens and addressing out-of-state private equity.
He will also combat fraud and financial mismanagement at the Capitol with better oversight of taxpayer dollars.
Responsibly Steward Natural Resources
Cole will fight for clean water and to conserve the natural beauty of the scenic St.
Croix River Valley and surrounding area.
Cole will also work to ensure that everyone has access to clean water, and he will defend Minnesota’s air, water, and land against pollution, contamination, and emissions.
Educate and Equip the Next Generation
Cole will fight to adequately fund education, retain good teachers, keep kids safe in schools, and foster high literacy rates.
Cole will also work to ensure education is preparing students to achieve their goals, especially in light of new challenges that technology and artificial intelligence pose to critical thinking and children’s mental health.
