Meet Cole Born and raised in the St.
Croix Valley, Cole Birkeland is an attorney, devoted husband, and active member of our community who understands the challenges we face.
For several years, Cole and his wife, Shelby, have called Lake Elmo home.
As a board member of a local community service organization, Cole works hands-on to serve his neighbors and strengthen the community we all share.
Professionally, Cole has a unique understanding of how government is supposed to work for the people.
As an attorney, he has served as legal counsel to various local governments, working on the frontlines to ensure government furthers the public interest within its lawful authority.
Today, he represents working people, fighting to protect their rights and hard-earned retirement savings against wrongdoers—a commitment to fairness and fiscal responsibility he will bring to the Capitol.
