Wisconsin currently has no statewide framework for data centers, leaving local governments to navigate trillion-dollar tech companies on their own.
I believe we need a real solution that reflects concerns I’ve heard in community meetings across the district.
That includes protecting ratepayers from rising utility costs, ensuring responsible water use, safeguarding our environment, addressing noise and light pollution, setting clear labor standards, and banning nondisclosure agreements that keep the public in the dark.
I am ready to support real, enforceable standards that protect Wisconsinites.
