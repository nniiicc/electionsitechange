During my time on the Beloit City Council and in the State Assembly, I have seen the good that government can do when it focuses on people, not politics.
I believe in local control, strong public schools, safe communities, and an economy that works for working families.
I’m running to continue delivering practical results for Beloit and southern Wisconsin and to ensure our communities have a real voice in Madison.
Making Progress Where it Matters Most Local government is where real work gets done, and I know families are feeling the pressure of rising costs, especially property taxes.
In 2023, I fought to pass a historic increase in shared revenue funding after years of stagnant state support for local communities.
That was an important step, but there is still more to do.
Local governments continue to face rising costs, and families are still feeling the burden on their property taxes.
We need to keep strengthening support for cities, towns, villages, and counties so essential services can be maintained without placing the weight on homeowners.
I believe local leaders know their communities best.
As your representative, I will continue working to give our communities the flexibility and resources they need to reduce reliance on property taxes while funding public safety, infrastructure, and the programs families rely on every day.
Supporting Our Kids and Schools Every child must get the support they need to succeed.
I have worked with kids facing a variety of challenges and have seen firsthand the barriers that can hold them back.
That is why I am committed to increasing special education funding, supporting our public schools, and reducing the need for referendums.
Our kids deserve the resources and opportunities to thrive, and I am running to help them and their families succeed.
