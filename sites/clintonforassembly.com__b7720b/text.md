Local government is where real work gets done, and I know families are feeling the pressure of rising costs, especially property taxes.
In 2023, I fought to pass a historic increase in shared revenue funding after years of stagnant state support for local communities.
That was an important step, but there is still more to do.
Local governments continue to face rising costs, and families are still feeling the burden on their property taxes.
We need to keep strengthening support for cities, towns, villages, and counties so essential services can be maintained without placing the weight on homeowners.
I believe local leaders know their communities best.
As your representative, I will continue working to give our communities the flexibility and resources they need to reduce reliance on property taxes while funding public safety, infrastructure, and the programs families rely on every day.
