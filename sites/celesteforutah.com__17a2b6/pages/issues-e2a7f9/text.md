She knows what it means to work for a paycheck and make ends meet.
It has been one of her top priorities to slow inflation and make sure your paycheck stretches as far as possible.
Celeste recently passed legislation that delivered the largest tax cut in American history.
This legislation increased take-home pay by $10,000 per year for the average family, eliminated taxes on tips and overtime, and cut taxes for 88 percent of seniors on Social Security.
SECURING OUR BORDER Celeste Maloy is committed to securing America’s border.
She voted to fully fund President Trump’s border wall and provide funding and technology to our Border Patrol agents so they can stop the flow of drugs and human trafficking across our southern border.
PROTECTING RELIGIOUS FREEDOMS Celeste is a woman of faith and holds religious freedom near and dear to her heart.
She knows that Utahns do, too.
She will always fight for Utahn’s right to worship and practice their faith as they see fit.
STOPPING OUT OF CONTROL SPENDING AND INFLATION Utahns need financial stability.
Runaway government spending fuels inflation and weakens our economy.
Congresswoman Maloy has made fiscal responsibility a top priority.
She has voted to reduce bureaucracy and eliminate wasteful spending, saving taxpayers $9 billion each year.
On the House Appropriations Committee, she remains vigilant in eliminating wasteful spending.
DEFENDING THE 2ND AMENDMENT Celeste knows the 2nd Amendment is clear – the right of the people to keep and bear Arms, shall not be infringed.
She will fight the gun grabbers in Washington DC and defend our constitutional rights.
STANDING UP FOR OUR PRO-LIFE VALUES Celeste believes all human life is sacred and will stand up for the unborn and our pro-life values in Congress.
UNLEASHING AMERICAN ENERGY Celeste has voted to undo burdensome regulations that have stifled domestic energy production and driven up costs.
She voted to bolster U.S. energy production and affordability by expanding and protecting American refining capacity.
She will continue fighting for American energy independence and affordability.
PUTTING UTAH IN CONTROL OF OUR LANDS AND RESOURCES Utahns should have more control over the resources they depend on to make a living.
Celeste knows Federal bureaucrats in Washington DC don’t understand how to manage our lands and resources better than we do.
In Congress, she has been fighting to make sure Utahns have more control over the land and resources they depend on to make a living because she knows no one cares more about protecting and preserving our lands and natural resources than the farmers and ranchers who live and work on them.
MAINTAINING A STRONG NATIONAL DEFENSE & DEFENDING UTAH’S MILITARY INSTALLATIONS AND ASSETS Celeste is a strong supporter of our military and national defense.
On the House Appropriations Committee, she has worked to pass funding that benefits our military and Hill Air Force Base while ensuring these dollars are spent responsibly.
She will continue fighting to keep America strong and our service members properly equipped.
