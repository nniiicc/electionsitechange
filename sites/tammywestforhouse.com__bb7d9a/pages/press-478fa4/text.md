State Representative Tammy West represents House District 84 in the Oklahoma House of Representatives.
Before joining the House, she served on the Putnam City Board of Education, including as its president in 2013.
A graduate of Central State University (now the University of Central Oklahoma) with a degree in business management, she owned and operated Wellness Plus, a small business, for more than 20 years.
She has lived in the district she represents for more than 40 years.
