How information is collected, used, and protected across the campaign website.
Effective Date: 01/07/2026
Austin Peterson For Senate (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through our website.
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- ZIP code
- Donation information
- Any information submitted through contact or volunteer forms
We may also collect non-personal information such as browser type, IP address, device information, and website usage data to improve site functionality.
We use the information we collect to:
- Communicate with you about the campaign
- Respond to inquiries
- Process donations
- Coordinate volunteer activities
- Send campaign updates via email or text message
- Improve website performance and user experience
- Comply with applicable laws and campaign finance regulations
By providing your phone number, you consent to receive text messages from Austin Peterson For Senate.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency may vary.
You may opt out at any time by replying “STOP” to any message.
Reply “HELP” for assistance.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or donation.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.
We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in operating our website, processing donations, or communicating with supporters, provided they agree to keep this information confidential.
We may also disclose information as required by law or campaign finance reporting requirements
We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your personal information.
However, no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure.
Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to enhance user experience and analyze website traffic.
You may adjust your browser settings to disable cookies.
Our website contains links to third-party (external) websites.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those websites nor whether they require a subscription or account/login to view content.
Our website is not intended for individuals under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
