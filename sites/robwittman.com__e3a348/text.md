About Rob
Rob is focused on delivering results for Virginia’s First District which stretches from the Northern Neck to parts of Henrico and Chesterfield Counties to the Historic Triangle of Jamestown, Yorktown, and Williamsburg.
You can find Rob driving back and forth from his home in Montross to DC when Congress is in session and traveling throughout the district to meet and listen to you, his neighbors, on how best to serve you.
Rob is committed to getting things done.
From rebuilding our military to increasing access to broadband, to making sure our children have a 21st-century education, he is constantly working for the First District.
