These methods allow you to skip the long lines on election day and avoid any roadblocks that could prevent you from making it to your voting precinct on time.
Please see below for helpful information about casting your ballot!

Receiving and Casting Your Absentee Ballot 1.
Absentee ballots will be mailed # days before election day and arrive in your mailbox.
2.
You will need to fill out your ballot and return it in one of three ways: By Mail- Your ballot must be postmarked on or before election day and received by your general registrar’s office by noon on the third day following the election.
In Person- You must return your ballot to your local general registrar’s office by 7:00 p.m. on Election Day.
Dropbox or Satellite Location- You may also return your absentee ballot to a drop-off or satellite location.
For more information, review the absentee instructions provided in your absentee ballot mailing.
Voting Early In-Person Early in-person voting will begin on September 18th and end on October 31st.
During the early voting window, you can vote at your local registrar’s office or a designated satellite voting location.
Note: When at the registrar’s office or satellite voting location, provide your name and address and present an acceptable form of identification or sign an ID Confirmation Statement.
Failure to provide acceptable identification will necessitate the signing of an ID Confirmation Statement, or alternatively, a provisional ballot will be offered to you.
You have until noon the following Friday to complete the ID Confirmation Statement.
