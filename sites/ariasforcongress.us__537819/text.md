A foundation of resilience, raised in the Douglas Housing Projects in Manhattan, Victor learned early the value of hard work and education.
He earned his degree from Hamilton College and his Juris Doctorate from SUNY Buffalo Law School before making Florida his home in 1991.
For over 35 years, he has advocated for justice as Owner and Managing Attorney of Arias Law Firm — representing working families in personal injury, workers' compensation, and school law.
He isn't a career politician.
He's running to cut the red tape for veterans, protect Medicare for seniors, and defend the water quality that drives our economy.
Married to his wife Angelette for 32 years, Victor and his family are rooted in Southwest Florida.
Their twins, Ariana and Victor, are each attending a university.
A member of Covenant Presbyterian Church since 1999, Victor has served as an Elder and on the Board of Trustees.
