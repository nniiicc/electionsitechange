The endorsement reflects the organization's confidence that Arias will be a strong advocate for the barrier island communities of FL-19 — fighting to protect clean water, restore the Caloosahatchee, and defend the natural environment that defines life on Sanibel and Captiva.
The primary election is scheduled for August 18, 2026.
