This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or interact with our campaign.
Please read this policy carefully.
By using our website or providing us with your information, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.
1.
Information We Collect We collect information you provide directly to us when you: • Sign up for campaign updates or newsletters • Submit a volunteer interest form • Make a donation through our ActBlue donation page • Contact us via email or phone • Interact with our website The types of information we may collect include your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, and any other information you choose to provide.
When you make a donation, payment processing is handled by ActBlue and is subject to their privacy policy.
2.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: • Send you campaign updates, event invitations, and announcements • Send recurring automated SMS messages related to voter communication and campaign announcements (if you have opted in) • Respond to your comments and questions • Coordinate volunteer activities • Comply with applicable laws and regulations, including Federal Election Commission (FEC) reporting requirements • Analyze and improve our outreach efforts 3.
Text Message (SMS) Communications By opting in to SMS communications, you agree to receive recurring automated text messages from Victor Arias for Congress.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
To opt out of SMS messages at any time, reply STOP to any message you receive from us.
For help, reply HELP or contact us at victor@ariasforcongress.us.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Opting out of SMS will not affect your ability to receive email communications if you have separately signed up for those.
4.
Email Communications By providing your email address, you agree to receive campaign-related emails from Victor Arias for Congress.
You may unsubscribe from email communications at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link included at the bottom of every email, or by contacting us directly at victor@ariasforcongress.us.
5.
Sharing of Information We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.
We may share your information in the following limited circumstances: • With service providers who assist us in operating our website and conducting campaign activities, subject to confidentiality obligations • With ActBlue and other payment processors for donation processing • As required by law, legal process, or government request • To protect the rights, property, or safety of our campaign, our supporters, or others • With your consent or at your direction We may share aggregated, anonymized data that does not identify any individual for analytical purposes.
6.
Federal Election Commission Disclosure As a federally regulated political committee, Victor Arias for Congress is required to collect and disclose certain donor information to the Federal Election Commission (FEC) for contributions exceeding applicable thresholds.
This information becomes part of the public record as required by federal law.
For more information about FEC disclosure requirements, visit fec.gov.

Children's Privacy Our website and services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
