Issues About Events Press Volunteer Donate EN ES HT EN ES HT Back to Press Room For Immediate Release Victor Arias Candidate for FL-19 Qualifies via Petition May 16, 2026 · CAPE CORAL, FL On Tuesday, May 12, 2026, the Supervisor of Elections certified that Victor Arias, Democratic candidate for Congressional District 19, officially qualified for the ballot by petition.
Since January 2026, when Arias began collecting signed petitions, 2,714 have been officially verified and posted by the Supervisor of Elections office, well above the number required to qualify for the open congressional seat formerly held by Rep.
Byron Donalds.
"We visited every corner of this district and connected with countless voters, regardless of party affiliation," said Arias.
"These signatures represent a desire for leadership focused on the real issues affecting our community, not partisan divisiveness.
People understand that when we work together, we can accomplish so much." An attorney, Arias has lived and worked in Southwest Florida for nearly three decades.
Throughout his career, he has advocated for working families and contributed to the district through civic and community leadership, including Leadership Florida.
Arias says his campaign is focused on cutting VA red tape for veterans, protecting Medicare and Social Security for seniors, expanding economic opportunity, investing in infrastructure and education, protecting the environment, and clean water, and restoring accountability to Washington.
"Residents are worried about affordability and the future of this region," Arias said.
"They deserve someone representing them in Washington who understands this district, loves this district, and who's been fighting for this community here.
For more than 27 years, I've been committed to District 19, and I'm prepared to fight for every family in it." Arias and his wife, Angelette, have two children who graduated from Florida SouthWestern State College.
The primary election is planned for the end of May.
