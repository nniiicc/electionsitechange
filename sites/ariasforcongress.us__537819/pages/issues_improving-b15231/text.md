In Southwest Florida, clean water isn't just an environmental issue — it's an economic lifeline.
Red tide events cost our region $164 million or more per event in lost tourism revenue, shuttered businesses, and declining property values.
The Caloosahatchee River, our Gulf beaches, the Everglades — these aren't abstract conservation topics.
They are the reason people visit, move to, and stay in FL-19.
Victor has lived in the region for over 27 years.
He's seen the damage — the dead fish lining Fort Myers Beach, the green slime on the Caloosahatchee, the "Do Not Swim" signs that cost our tourism industry millions.
He's running to fight for federal investment in water quality at the source, not just cleanup after the damage is done.
"Our Gulf beaches, our waterways, our Everglades — they aren't abstract conservation topics.
They're our livelihood, our home, and our legacy." — Victor Arias Victor's Commitments Fight for full federal funding of Everglades restoration and Lake Okeechobee discharge reform.
Support research funding to combat red tide and protect our $3.2 billion tourism economy.
Support affordable housing initiatives so workers in our hospitality and service industries can live where they work.
Lower grocery prices and cost of living by addressing supply chain bottlenecks and corporate price gouging.
