The endorsement reflects the DECF's confidence that Arias is the strongest voice for environmental protection and clean water policy in Southwest Florida.
The DECF, which has endorsed candidates in statewide races and a majority of U.S.
Congressional and Florida state legislative races, selected Arias based on his long-standing commitment to the issues that define life in FL-19: clean water, Everglades restoration, fighting red tide at the source, and protecting the Gulf Coast economy that depends on a healthy environment.
The DECF engages its membership in phone banking, texting, email outreach, and door-knocking on behalf of endorsed candidates.
Their endorsement of Arias signals a commitment to mobilize environmental voters across Florida in support of his campaign ahead of the August 18 primary.
The primary election is scheduled for August 18, 2026.
About the Democratic Environmental Caucus of Florida: The DECF is a statewide organization dedicated to electing Democrats who champion environmental protection, clean water, and climate action across Florida.
