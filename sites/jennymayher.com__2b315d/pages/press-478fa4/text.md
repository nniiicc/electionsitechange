The public is invited.
A Democrat and longtime Midcoast resident, Mayher currently serves as Director of Communications at Lincoln Academy.
She previously worked as a children’s librarian at Skidompha Library and helped lead a successful grassroots effort to limit large-scale retail development in Damariscotta.
“I’m running to protect what makes this place special while ensuring every family has access to affordable health care, housing, and childcare,” Mayher said.
The event will include seasonally-appropriate refreshments (pie for pi day on 3.14) and a table where people can paint their own signs.
More information about Mayher, the campaign, and the event on March 14 is available at www.jennymayher.com .

LCN article published 11/2021 Our Town Organizers Reflect on Size-Cap Vote 10 Years Later Jenny Mayher can still remember how she felt standing in the upstairs room of the Damariscotta River Grill on March 21, 2006.

If the vote passed, Wal-Mart’s plan to build a 186,800-square-foot supercenter on Route 1 would no longer be possible.
