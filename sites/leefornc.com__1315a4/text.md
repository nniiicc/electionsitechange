Lowering Cost Michael supports lower income taxes, a larger standard deduction, responsible budgets, and more competition in health care so families can keep more of what they earn in the state CNBC has repeatedly named America's Top State for Business.
Strengthening Education Michael's focus is student outcomes: strong early literacy, competency-based pathways like SparkNC, real career training for every student, and competitive pay for the teachers who deliver results.
Making health care more affordable and accessible Michael worked across party lines to expand health coverage for working families, and he is removing outdated barriers so more providers can serve patients close to home.
Investing in infrastructure and quality of life Roads, water and sewer, drainage, schools, the airport, and coastal resilience all shape daily life.
Michael is focused on planning and paying for the infrastructure a growing New Hanover County needs.
Protecting clean water and the coast Michael has been a primary sponsor of Water Safety Act bills in every term he has served and continues to push for PFAS testing, treatment, research, and removal.
Keeping communities safe Michael supports law enforcement, first responders, school safety, and clear consequences for violent crime while investing in the people who protect our communities.
