The Issues
Bring Back Manufacturing!
We will unleash American ingenuity to bring jobs home.
By investing in next-gen biofuels and regenerative materials, expanding trade schools, and building skilled workers up, we can create supply chains that start and end in the USA.
This allows expansion into other fields such as building cheaper farm equipment and renewable energy.
We will ensure these new jobs pay a living wage by protecting the right to organize and join a union.
I will have more extensive details about my plans to bring manufacturing back later.
- Made In The USA
- Bring jobs back
- Pro union
A National Mission For Energy Independence
Energy independence shouldn't mean choosing between a healthy planet and a healthy bank account, and it shouldn't mean relying on foreign oil.
By harnessing American-made solar, wind, and other innovative technologies, we can produce abundant, cheap, and clean power right here at home.
This drives down utility bills for families, creates high-paying jobs, and conserves our planet for future generations.
We should not only push for greener energies, but we should hold companies accountable for knowingly polluting our planet and dumping chemicals in our rivers and lakes.
- Freedom from foreign oil
- Cheap, reliable, American made energy
- CNC & Manufacturing Act (Details to come)
Protecting The 2nd Amendment AND Our Schools
The right to bear arms comes with a responsibility to ensure public safety.
We can protect our children without eroding our freedoms by enacting common-sense reforms.
Raising the purchasing age for firearms, ensuring all sales require a background check, closing loopholes that allow domestic violence abusers to access such weapons, and expanding mental healthcare.
Responsible gun owners know that safe communities and 2A rights are NOT mutually exclusive.
I'm not here to offer empty thoughts and prayers, I'm here to offer action and solutions.
- Protect the 2nd, not the status quo
- An armed society is a responsible society
- Fund mental healthcare, secure our safety
