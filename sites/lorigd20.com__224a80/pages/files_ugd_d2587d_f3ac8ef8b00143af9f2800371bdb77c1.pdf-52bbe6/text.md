Heating and Electric Bill Assistance The Maryland Energy Assistance Program (MEAP) and the Electric Universal Service Program (EUSP) assist eligible low-income customers with their heating and electric bills, even if they are currently without electric service.
Who is eligible?   Households with a heating/electric bill in their name; AND Households whose heating/electric bill is included in their rent or condominium fee If your heating/electric bill is included in your rent or condo fee, your benefit payment will be forwarded directly to the landlord or condominium association after they’ve completed the agreement that any benefit payment received on behalf of your household will be used to reduce your rent by that benefit amount.
Income Eligibility: Two key factors that determine eligibility for these programs are the size of the household and the total amount of pre-tax money that all of the members of the household made in the last 30 days.
