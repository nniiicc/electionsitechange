Help with Health Care Costs
Lorig has long prioritized consumer protections, especially around the high cost of health care.
Thanks to her legislation Maryland now ranks #1 in the country for having the strongest consumer protections for people with medical debt according to medicaldebtpolicyscorecard.org.
