#ago 3 min read Promoting Advanced Transmission Technologies Wouldn’t it be great to get more electricity from our existing power lines?
It would mean we wouldn’t need to build as many new power plants and lines to meet rising demand for electricity.
Turns out we can by using advanced transmission technologies (ATTs) and “grid-enhancing technologies” (GETs).
What are ATTs and GETs?
Advanced transmission technologies (ATTs) are a suite of tools that can quickly and cost-effectively increase the capacity of the existing electrical grid without building new transmission lines.
These typically encompass grid-enhancing technologies (GETs) –– hardware and software solutions that can be deployed on the existing system and essentially act as energy efficiency for the grid –– and advanced conductors.
By increasing and optimizing the capacity of lines already in place, the grid can transmit more electricity without the lengthy planning and permitting process required for new transmission lines.
Adding ATTs to lines being constructed, ensures that all new lines being built are as efficient as possible, thus decreasing the need for more lines and ensuring the best use of rate-payer dollars.
While construction of new transmission lines will still be needed to support projected increased demand, ATTs ensure maximized efficiency from current and future grid investments.

Progress on Foraging Opportunities in Maryland Ensuring opportunities for responsible food foraging is an important part of supporting a resilient and equitable food system.
Food foraging creates opportunities to access fresh, nutritious, seasonal foods grown in the wild.
When done responsibly, it is sustainable and connects us with our ecosystems in a delightful way!
During the previous two Sessions, I have introduced legislation to allow foraging opportunities on Maryland Department of Natural Resources (DNR) land, which is currently prohibited.
I am grateful to many of you for your advocacy efforts and testimony.
While the legislation has not passed, it has given me a chance to engage with DNR to discuss the value and importance of foraging.
And it worked!
DNR Secretary Josh Kurtz recently sent me a letter indicating that as a result of our efforts: “Following a thorough review, the Department has decided to update its regulations to permit members of the public to gather natural items on state land for personal consumption, at the Department’s discretion.
I believe this update will foster a deeper connection to Maryland's natural resources while ensuring sustainable management practices remain in place.” This means that DNR will change their regulations to allow foraging, even without having to pass a bill.
Until these new regulations are in place, I will continue to work closely with DNR and will let you know when we can go foraging together!
