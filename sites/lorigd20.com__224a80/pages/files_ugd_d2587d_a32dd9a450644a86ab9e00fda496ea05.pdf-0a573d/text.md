CASH Campaign of Maryland is a non-profit organization that promotes economic advancement for low-to-moderate income individuals and families in Baltimore and across Maryland.
CASH accomplishes its mission through operating direct service programs, building organizational and field capacity, and leading policy and advocacy initiatives to strengthen family economic stability.
