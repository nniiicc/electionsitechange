Consequently, in early July, I filed paperwork with the Indiana Election Division to register as a Write-in Candidate for the Fourth Congressional District.
How to Vote for a Write-in When you present your identification at the polling location, I recommend that you inform the individual checking your ID that you would like to vote for a Write-in Candidate.
Ask them if there are any special provisions needed for you to do this.
I recently received a response from the Indiana Election Division and he told me that there are two main options at polling locations: [1] Direct Record Electronic (DRE) Voting Machines.
For each office you will have an option to select “Write-in Candidate”.
Select this option and it will let you type the name of the Write-in Candidate. [2] Paper Election Card Again, there should be an option to select “Write-in Candidate”.
Check this option and print the name of the candidate in the space provided (NOTE: you will need a pen to do this).
