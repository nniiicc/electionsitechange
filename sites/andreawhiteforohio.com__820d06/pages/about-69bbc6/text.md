Meet Andrea White
A Lifetime of Service to Our Community
Andrea has lived and raised her family in Kettering for most of her adult life.
She is a graduate of Centerville High School and Wright State University and lives with her husband, John.
Before joining the Ohio House, Andrea served as Clerk of Court for Kettering Municipal Court, representing Kettering, Centerville, Washington Township and Moraine.
Her campaign biography says she worked with her team to modernize processes, improve services, reduce costs and return millions in revenues and savings to taxpayers.
Andrea also spent more than 15 years as a small business owner, providing communications and process-improvement services to businesses and nonprofit organizations.
Her private- sector background also includes work in health care, services for Ohioans with developmental disabilities and hospital communications.
Deep Roots in Community Service
Long before serving in the Legislature, Andrea worked on community initiatives addressing youth drug and alcohol prevention, addiction, hunger, criminal justice and positive youth development.
She has worked closely with the South Suburban Coalition, Partners for Healthy Youth, Montgomery County Overdose Action Team, Montgomery County Drug-Free Coalition and Montgomery County Prevention Coalition, along with more than a decade helping organize a summer program connecting young people in an under-resourced neighborhood with food and mentors.

A Message from Andrea:
It has been my honor to serve as your State Representative these past six years.
I have spent most of my adult life working to help build a positive future for our children, our families, our businesses and our communities.
What drives me is my belief that every person -- no matter where you’re born, who your parents are or what zip code you live in -- has the right to be treated with dignity and have access to opportunities and resources to thrive and grow to their fullest God-given potential.
Over the past few years, our communities and our state have faced enormous challenges.
Now, more than ever, we need experienced problem solvers and compassionate, common-sense leadership to bring your voice and get things done for our communities.
At the Statehouse, I’ve been an independent voice for our district – listening and leaning in to address issues head-on.
The health, safety, education and economic security of our children, our families and our workforce has been my top priority.
