Pierce is a father, an award-winning educator, a historian, and a dedicated advocate who has been a champion for his community.
As your Representative for NC House District 27 in the legislature, he’s making sure that everyone in northeastern NC can have the voice in our state’s government that they deserve.
In Raleigh, Rodney strives to be a bold, committed leader who will usher in a new era of Democratic representation in the district.
Rodney is fighting to fully fund our public schools via the Leandro Plan, defend democracy by protecting the right to vote, support workers’ rights and a living wage for working people, and advocate for affordable healthcare, housing, and equitable economic development.
