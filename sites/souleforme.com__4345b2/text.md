Get Involved Meet Eryn About Eryn Soule Deep Roots in Lewiston.
A Commitment to Maine.
Eryn Soule is a dedicated resident of Lewiston who understands firsthand the challenges our community faces every day.
Running as an Independent, Eryn refuses to be bound by party lines.
Instead, she is bound only to the people she aims to serve.
She believes that the best solutions come from collaboration, common sense, and a genuine willingness to listen.
Whether it's ensuring our schools have the resources they need, fighting for affordable housing, or protecting Maine's natural beauty, Eryn is ready to bring a fresh, independent perspective to the State Senate.
For her, public safety is the top priority.
She'll work tirelessly to ensure our first responders have every resource they need to keep us safe, while keeping our streets and neighborhoods clean to preserve the beautiful state we all call home.
Eryn believes we must approach the addiction crisis with deep compassion for those struggling and a firm commitment to long-term recovery.
She advocates for prioritizing investment in treatment beds and comprehensive support services to provide a real path to wellness.
Her goal is to ensure every neighbor has the help they need while keeping our shared community spaces safe and clean for everyone.
As someone who is not a career politician, Eryn is focused solely on delivering practical, community-focused results for her neighbors.
"We need leaders who answer to their neighbors, not political parties.
It's time for practical solutions that work for Lewiston and for Maine." — Eryn Soule The Platform Priorities for Our Community As an Independent, Eryn looks at the issues, not the politics.
Here is where she stands on the challenges that matter most to Lewiston and Maine.
Public Safety Making public safety the top priority by ensuring our first responders have every resource they need to keep us safe, while keeping our streets and neighborhoods clean and secure.
Stronger Schools Fully funding our public schools to support our teachers and give our students the tools they need to succeed in a modern economy.
Economic Growth Supporting small businesses in Lewiston and across Maine by cutting unnecessary red tape and investing in workforce training programs.
Mental Health & Addiction Recovery Approaching the addiction crisis with compassion and a firm commitment to recovery—investing in treatment beds and support services so every neighbor has a real path to wellness.
Affordable Housing Tackling the housing crisis head-on through smart zoning reforms and incentives for developing attainable housing for working families.
Healthcare Access Ensuring every Mainer has access to affordable, high-quality healthcare without going bankrupt.
We need to protect rural hospitals and expand mental health services.
