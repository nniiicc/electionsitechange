Fires Resources All Posts Spokane Area Fires juliaforoffice Aug 4 1 min read Hello Spokane, First I want to say, thank you to every beautiful person out there doing something to help, if you are a Firefighter or National Guard member, if you are a First Responder, Medical Personnel, Therapist or you take care of animals.
Thank you!
If you are an ordinary citizen helping at any of the organizations, if you are helping with kids, or supplies, if you have opened your home or your RV, or your land to our community.
Thank you!
If you are sending prayers or good vibes, or blessings; whatever.
Thank you!
We are showing our true hearts during this tragedy and I want each and every one of you to hear me when I say.
Thank you!
We see you, we hear you, we appreciate you.
For those that are coming to my pages for Primary election response, I want to say that I am cautiously optimistic that we will continue on to the General election.
The ballots are still being counted, and this is a close race, nothing is certain.
I want to express my faith and confidence in Washington’s election process, and; as I've said all along, the voters deserve to have their voices heard and I want to make sure the system has had time to assure that.
I'll see you all on the other side either way.
Fingers crossed.
