Fires Resources All Posts A Victory for You! juliaforoffice Aug 14 1 min read Updated: Aug 30 Hello Spokane.
Wow, the last 13 days have been surreal.
From marching in the Hillyard Hijinks parade to evacuating our home and then being so incredibly blessed to come home and find our place intact.
I know there are so many that didn't have that happy ending, and please know that we are all here and wanting to help those who have lost things, big or small, your loss is valid and we want to help.
I'm coming in here today to say I am so grateful to all of you who voted for me.
I also want to extend a heartfelt thank you to each and every person who helped me on this road so far; we have more work to do.
Thank you is not enough but it's all I have.
I am humbled by your voices.
You have chosen me to move on to the general election and to be your champion against the opposition.
I will continue to make myself Accessible to all and Accountable to YOU.
Let's take this energy all the way to Olympia and make sure Eastern Washington is represented and heard.
In future weeks I'll take what you have told me you want and further refine our policy priorities and make sure we have clear objectives and a plan that we can take all the way to Olympia.
Thank you again so much for your votes.
