Fires Resources All Posts UPCOMING: Julia at White Cane Day on October 15 at 11 AM juliaforoffice #ago 1 min read What: White Cane Day When: October 15 at 11 AM Where: Red Wagon at Riverfront Park On October 15, we celebrate White Cane Awareness Day.
We celebrate the right of blind people to move freely and independently with cane or guide dog, which is affirmed by White Cane laws in all fifty states.
For blind people, the white cane is an essential tool that gives us the ability to achieve a full and independent life.
It allows us to move freely and safely from place to place—whether it's at work, at school, or around our neighborhoods.
