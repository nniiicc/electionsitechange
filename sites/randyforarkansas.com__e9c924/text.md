Randy Torres is President/CEO of the Siloam Springs Chamber of Commerce and serves as the Community Development Officer for Generations Bank.
He has experience in commercial lending, treasury management and private banking.
Prior to being a Chamber Executive and a banker, his experience includes owning and operating businesses in the service, manufacturing, ranching and real estate industries.
He has also worked in the areas of hospitality, teaching, higher education, ministry and human resources.
Randy obtained a B.S. degree in Animal Science from Texas A&M University in 1993, a Master’s degree in Ministry in 2003 followed by a Master’s in Business Administration in 2012, both from John Brown University.
Randy has devoted many years to leadership and currently serves on the following state legislative committees: Aging, Children and Youth, and Legislative Affairs, Public Transportation, and Joint-Energy.
Locally and regionally, the Northwest Arkansas Council, Western Benton County Partnership and Community Clinic NWA.
He was the recipient of the 2017 Outstanding Civic Leadership Award by the Siloam Springs Chamber of Commerce.
Randy and Molly have been married 32 years and have two children, daughter Allison Torres Gooderl, wife of Robert and mother of Graham and Eden, and son Christian Torres, husband of Taylor.
