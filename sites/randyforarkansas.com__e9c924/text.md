ABOUT MEET RANDY Rep.
Randy Torres is President/CEO of the Siloam Springs Chamber of Commerce and serves as the Community Development Officer for Generations Bank.
He has experience in commercial lending, treasury management and private banking.
Prior to being a Chamber Executive and a banker, his experience includes owning and operating businesses in the service, manufacturing, ranching and real estate industries.
He has also worked in the areas of hospitality, teaching, higher education, ministry and human resources.
Randy obtained a B.S. degree in Animal Science from Texas A&M University in 1993, a Master’s degree in Ministry in 2003 followed by a Master’s in Business Administration in 2012, both from John Brown University.
Randy has devoted many years to leadership and currently serves on the following state legislative committees: Aging, Children and Youth, and Legislative Affairs, Public Transportation, and Joint-Energy.
Locally and regionally, the Northwest Arkansas Council, Western Benton County Partnership and Community Clinic NWA.
He was the recipient of the 2017 Outstanding Civic Leadership Award by the Siloam Springs Chamber of Commerce.
Randy and Molly have been married 32 years and have two children, daughter Allison Torres Gooderl, wife of Robert and mother of Graham and Eden, and son Christian Torres, husband of Taylor.
THE ISSUES THE ISSUES THE ISSUES SMALL BUSINESSES Arkansas is powered by small business.
As such, we need to keep them running and thriving.
Small businesses are our major job creators.
By cutting back oppressive regulations and lowering taxes, we can keep our communities growing.
I will push for a lessened regulatory environment, one that fosters innovation and leads to prosperity for Arkansas families.
SECOND AMENDMENT It doesn’t get any clearer than the Constitution – “A well-regulated Militia, being necessary to the security of a free State, the right of the people to keep and bear Arms shall not be infringed .” I believe the Second Amendment guards against crime and provides protection for our families.
I will fight to make sure your right to protect your family and property from those seeking to harm them remains intact.
FIERCE ADVOCATE FOR THE UNBORN Our nation's foundation rests on the principle that all individuals are equal and possess certain rights, including the right to life , liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
I am unwaveringly pro-life, and I am dedicated to persistently advocating for the rights of the unborn.
EDUCATION Each child deserves a high-quality, parent-directed education system that nurtures top talent and consequently promotes ongoing job and economic growth.
I firmly believe in providing our children with a solid education and supporting fair competition.
LAW AND ORDER I support our law enforcement agencies.
Hiring is difficult right now due to a noble and necessary profession being criminalized by the mainstream media.
Governor Sarah Huckabee Sanders is leading the way to make our cities safer, eliminating fentanyl from the streets, and supporting the law enforcement profession with better benefits and pay.
I will support those efforts.
FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY Inflation and government spending are out of control.
Elected officials bear the duty of effectively managing taxpayers' money, and it is time for our government to refocus its efforts on serving the people's best interests.
Government should be limited, and I will work to ensure tax dollars are spent wisely.
