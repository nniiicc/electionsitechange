Third-Generation Farmer Small Business Owner Father of Nine Married 25 Years School Board — 12 Years National Young Farmer Award ND State Advisory Board ND Dry Pea & Lentil Council Sacred Heart Parish Council Meet Kevin A farmer, a dad, and your neighbor in District 29.
Kevin Wolsky is a third-generation farmer and small business owner from Carrington.
He and his wife Jen have been married for 25 years and together they're raising nine children.
When you're feeding a family of eleven, you learn pretty quick what things cost — and you learn to stretch every dollar.
With nine kids in the house, Kevin and Jen deal with the same things every family in District 29 deals with: making sure the schools are good, making sure there's a doctor nearby, and hoping their kids will want to stay and build their lives right here instead of moving away.
Kevin has spent his career giving back to this community.
He served on the Carrington High School Board for 12 years, sat on the North Dakota State Advisory Board, and currently serves as vice chairman of the ND Dry Pea and Lentil Council.
He's a member of the Parish Council Board at Sacred Heart Catholic Church in Carrington and was honored with the National Young Farmer Award in 2016.
Kevin is running because he believes the people of District 29 — from Carrington to Jamestown to every small town in between — deserve a representative who actually lives this life alongside them.
Not someone who talks about rural issues from Bismarck, but someone who's out in the field, at the school board meeting, and in the pew on Sunday.
"I'm not a career politician — I'm a dad, a farmer, and a neighbor.
I know what it's like to sit at the kitchen table and worry about property taxes, school funding, and whether the next generation will stay in North Dakota.
That's why I'm running." — Kevin Wolsky Kevin drove bus to Bismarck to pick up students from the FBLA convention — because showing up for the next generation is just what you do.
