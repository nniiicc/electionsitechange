Voters Not Politicians - ProVoter Designation
Receiving the Pro-Voter designation from Voters Not Politicians reflects my commitment to putting people ahead of politics.
As a retired Air Force officer, I spent my career serving under the principle that leaders are accountable to the people they serve.
That same principle should guide our government.
I support fair elections, transparent government, and strong disclosure requirements so voters know who is funding political campaigns.
I believe we must reduce the influence of corporate special interests and dark money so elected officials are accountable to constituents—not donors.
