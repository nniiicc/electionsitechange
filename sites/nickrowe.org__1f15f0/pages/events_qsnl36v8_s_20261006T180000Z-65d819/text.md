About this event
This get-out-the-vote FREE celebration is bringing together college students, young voters, community organizations, Democratic groups and friends from across Kalamazoo for a night built around connection, information and getting involved.
And yes, we’re making a night of it!

WAYS TO GET INVOLVED

Senate candidate Abdul El-Sayed.
