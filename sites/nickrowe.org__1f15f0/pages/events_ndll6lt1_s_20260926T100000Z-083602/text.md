Join dozens of elected officials, candidates, and staff members from across Michigan as they come together to help elect Nick Rowe as your next State Representative and strengthen our Get Out the Vote effort across the district.
Never knocked doors before?
No problem.
Training will be provided before we head out, and we’ll pair new volunteers with an experienced teammate so everyone feels comfortable and ready to go.
We’ll meet at the Gull Lake High School parking lot at 10:00 AM and head into the community to knock doors, meet neighbors, and have meaningful conversations about the issues that matter most to our district.
Wear your walking shoes and bring water, sunscreen, and a couple of friends.
More than anything, come ready to have some fun, meet new people, build friendships, and strengthen the bonds that connect our communities.
Canvassing is about more than politics.
