Meet Nick Issues News Volunteer Events Photos Contribute News / Michigan's Education Association Endorsement 26 Aug Wednesday, 12:11 PM · 2026 Michigan's Education Association Endorsement Michigan must make education a real budget priority.
That means protecting classroom funding, fully supporting special education, ensuring rural schools aren't left behind simply because of their ZIP code, and investing in the teachers, aides, counselors, bus drivers, and support professionals who keep our schools running every day.
We also need to make education a career people can build a life around.
That means competitive pay, strong benefits, and a retirement system that allows educators and school employees to finish a lifetime of service with security and dignity.
If we want great people in our classrooms, we have to show them that Michigan values their work not just today, but throughout their entire career.
That is why I am incredibly proud to have earned the endorsement of the Michigan Education Association.
Their members are doing the work every day, educating our children, supporting families, and holding our communities together.
