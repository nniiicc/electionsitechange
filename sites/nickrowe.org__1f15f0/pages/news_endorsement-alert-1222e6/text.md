I’m proud to have earned the endorsement of the Committee to Protect Health Care.
My approach to health care comes back to a simple leadership principle I learned as a military commander: take care of your people.
When people know their leaders have their backs, they can focus on the mission, support one another, and build a stronger team.
That same principle should guide how we govern.
Putting people first means protecting access to quality, affordable health care and supporting the doctors, nurses, caregivers, and health professionals who keep our communities running.
A healthy community depends on a strong health care workforce—and a strong economy depends on people being healthy enough to work, raise their families, and contribute to their communities.
I’m grateful for the Committee to Protect Health Care’s support, and I’ll continue fighting for a health care system that puts people before politics and patients before profits.
