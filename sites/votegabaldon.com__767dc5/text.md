Key Issues Rosanna's legislative priorities focus on the fundamental needs of Arizona families and communities.
Public Education Every child deserves access to a strong public education that prepares them for the future.
She is committed to supporting teachers, investing in classrooms, and making sure schools have the resources they need.
Her focus is on fairness and opportunity, so that every student, from kindergarten to college, can succeed. • Secure stable state funding to provide schools with the resources needed for quality education across Arizona. • Improve teacher pay and strengthen workforce support to attract and keep talented educators in our classrooms. • Ensure smaller class sizes and safe learning environments so students can focus on learning. • Expand pathways through community colleges, trade schools, and universities to prepare students for good jobs and strong careers.
Water Sustainability Arizona has faced drought for more than two decades and securing our water future is essential to protecting families and our economy.
Rosanna is committed to finding bipartisan solutions that balance growth with conservation, ensuring Arizona remains strong. • Advance long-term water security planning to protect Arizona's future. • Support bipartisan drought contingency measures that safeguard our State. • Develop sustainable water supplies that balance growth with conservation. • Promote water policies that strengthen both economic and environmental sustainability.
Economic Development Strong infrastructure is the foundation of economic opportunity.
Rosanna supports investments in roads, highways, and community development.
She is focused on rural areas, where infrastructure improvements create jobs and open the door to new opportunities. • Invest in road and highway rehabilitation to keep Arizona moving safely and efficiently. • Strengthen rural infrastructure to support families and small businesses. • Promote economic development initiatives that create jobs and attract investment. • Ensure livable wages that strengthen our communities.
Public Health & Safety Rosanna believes in hands-on leadership and community involvement, listening to people directly to bring forward solutions that reflect common sense and compassion.
Transparent Government Rosanna is committed to following through on commitments and bringing the voices of Southern Arizona to the State Capitol with honesty and heart.
