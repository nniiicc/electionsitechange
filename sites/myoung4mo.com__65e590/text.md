Meet Michael Young
Michael Young is an Independence businessman, a devoted husband and father, and a proud grandfather running for State Representative to bring common sense to Jefferson City.
As a first-time candidate, Michael is committed to putting people over partisan point-scoring and ensuring local voices are no longer ignored by career politicians.
With over two decades in Independence, Michael’s dedication to his community is reflected in his service on the Independence Planning Commission, the Resource Health Board, and the FIFA World Cup Advisory Committee.
His experience as a local business owner provides him with a unique understanding of the economic pressures facing Missouri families.
Michael’s platform centers on upholding constitutional freedoms, promoting public safety, and holding politicians accountable.
He believes in seeking common ground to protect local values and ensure a prosperous future for the next generation.
Michael is ready to roll up his sleeves and provide the fresh representation District 21 deserves.
