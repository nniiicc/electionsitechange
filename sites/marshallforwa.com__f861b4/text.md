Matt Marshall is a state legislator, physician assistant, and veteran who has spent his career serving others in medicine, the military, education, and public office.
He represents Washington’s 2nd Legislative District in the State House and brings a rare combination of medical, military, legal expertise, and local government experience to Olympia.
Matt served in the United States Army from 2005 to 2021, rising to the rank of Captain and serving overseas as a Battalion Surgeon in demanding operational environments.
After military service, he continued caring for patients as a board-certified physician assistant with experience in orthopedic surgery, urgent care, emergency, and critical care medicine.
Matt also served on the Eatonville School Board from 2019 to 2023, where he worked through complex legal and policy issues while standing up for parents and local control.
He earned his Juris Doctor from Seattle University School of Law, his Master of Physician Assistant Studies and Bachelor of Science from the University of Nebraska Medical Center, and his Associate of Science from George Washington University.
Matt lives in Eatonville, Washington, and remains deeply committed to the small-town communities, families, and values of the 2nd District.
He is focused on making Washington more affordable, restoring accountability in government, protecting public safety, defending constitutional rights, and expanding choice in education and healthcare.
Education Juris Doctor — Seattle University School of Law Master of Physician Assistant Studies — University of Nebraska Medical Center B.S. — University of Nebraska Medical Center A.S. — George Washington University Where Matt Stands Matt’s priorities for the 2nd District Seven commitments rooted in affordability, accountability, and freedom — informed by a career in medicine, the military, and local government.
01 Affordability & Tax Relief Repeal Olympia’s ever-expanding taxes, cap runaway spending, and put guardrails on agencies that treat taxpayers like an endless ATM.
Olympia has made Washington too expensive for working families, small businesses, and retirees.
Matt is fighting to repeal Olympia’s ever-expanding taxes, cap runaway spending, and put guardrails on state agencies that treat taxpayers like an endless ATM.
He supports restoring advisory votes on tax increases and giving them real teeth by tying them to automatic referenda when voters reject new taxes.
Matt believes every dollar government spends should be transparent, traceable, and tied to clear outcomes so taxpayers know what they are getting for their money.
02 Accountability for Public Spending & Bargaining Bring public-money negotiations into the open, end legislative privilege, and support term limits for career politicians.
Matt believes the problem in Olympia is not just how much government spends, but how little accountability there is for the results.
Programs grow, budgets swell, and yet homelessness, addiction, and learning loss get worse, not better.
He supports bringing every negotiation involving public money, including public-sector union bargaining, under the Open Public Meetings Act so taxpayers can see who is asking for what and why.
Matt also backs ending legislative privilege that shields lawmakers from public records, and he supports term limits to stop career politicians from treating office as a lifetime job.
03 Public Safety & the Rule of Law Restore consequences for criminals, back law enforcement, and roll back policies that put repeat offenders back on the street.
Washington’s weak-on-crime policies have led to rising crime, repeat offenders, and communities that no longer feel safe in their own neighborhoods.
Matt wants to restore consequences for criminals, support law enforcement with the staffing and tools they need, and roll back policies that put prolific offenders back on the street instead of behind bars.
He believes in a justice system that is fair, transparent, and accountable: one that protects victims, backs officers who do their jobs with integrity, and sends a clear message that crime has real consequences.
04 Healthcare Choice & Lower Costs Cut red tape, increase price transparency, and protect the doctor-patient relationship from government interference.
As a practicing physician assistant, Matt has watched healthcare costs soar while patient choice shrinks under layers of bureaucracy, red tape, and top-down mandates.
His priority is to cut unnecessary regulations, reduce paperwork burdens, and empower patients and their clinicians to make decisions without interference from bureaucrats and insurers.
He opposes healthcare mandates that take decisions away from patients and their clinicians, and he strongly supports every patient’s right to choose the care, coverage, and treatment path that best fits their needs and values.
Matt’s priority is to lower costs by reducing unnecessary regulations, increasing price transparency, expanding competition, and protecting the doctor-patient relationship from government interference.
He believes healthcare works best when patients have more freedom, more information, and more control, not less.
05 Parents’ Rights & Education Reform Curriculum transparency, parental consent on sensitive topics, and school choice that follows students, not systems.
Matt believes parents should be the primary decision-makers in their children’s education and that schools must be open and accountable to families.
He supports curriculum transparency, meaningful notification and consent on sensitive topics, and clear avenues for parents to address concerns when schools ignore their rights.
He backs reform that follows students, not systems, including robust school choice and voucher models that let parents choose public, private, charter, or home-school options that best fit their children.
Matt wants an education system that is focused on reading, writing, math, and real-world skills, not ideological agendas.
06 Protecting Small Towns, Farms & Local Control Reduce red tape, streamline permits, and keep decisions close to the people — not distant bureaucracies.
The 2nd District is built on small-town heritage, agriculture, timber, and small business.
Matt is committed to reducing red tape, streamlining permits, and restoring local control so communities are not strangled by one-size-fits-all rules from Olympia.
He supports policies that keep family farms viable, protect working forests, and respect property rights while still taking care of the land and water we all share.
For Matt, decisions should be made as close to the people as possible, not by distant bureaucracies.
07 Constitutional Rights & Limited Government An unwavering defender of free speech, due process, the right to bear arms, and religious liberty.
Matt believes the Washington and U.S.
Constitutions are the best safeguards for liberty ever written.
He is an unwavering defender of free speech, due process, the right to bear arms, religious liberty, and the right of citizens to hold their government accountable.
He will continue to oppose laws that erode constitutional rights and to fight for a limited government that knows its boundaries, respects individual freedom, and serves the people rather than ruling over them.
