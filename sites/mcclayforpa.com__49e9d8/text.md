Release September 3, 2026 State Representative Candidate Jessica McClay Commends Bear Creek Township for Avoiding Data Center Expansion Proposal WILKES-BARRE, PA – Jessica McClay, Democratic candidate for Pennsylvania State House, is speaking out about the recent industrial zoning vote in Bear Creek Township, which falls within the 121st District.
McClay attended the public hearing on Monday, August 31, where she listened to more than thirty community members testify against the ordinance that would have increased the size of Bear Creek’s industrial zone to accommodate data center construction.
“Hearing the voices of this community come together in such a powerful way was inspiring, but it should also be a serious wake-up call for our legislators,” said Jessica McClay.
“As State Representative I will always stand with the people of the 121st, and I will vote to rein in the data center expansion that hurts our environment and puts financial strain on families already struggling to get by.” All five Bear Creek Township Supervisors claimed they would not consider the ordinance during the August 31 meeting.
On Thursday, September 3, the motion to expand the industrial zone died before it was brought for a vote.
“As a lifelong resident of Northeastern Pennsylvania, I’ve seen the damage that reckless development can inflict on our communities firsthand,” said McClay.
“Growth here has to be responsible and beneficial to more than a corporation’s bottom line, and these big companies coming in must be made to answer to the people first.
