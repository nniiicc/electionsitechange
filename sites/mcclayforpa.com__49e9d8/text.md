Jessica McClay for PA ("the Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.
Information We Collect
When you sign up to volunteer, send a message through our contact form, subscribe to updates, or otherwise interact with us, we may collect your name, email address, mailing address, phone number, and any message content you share.
Donations are processed by ActBlue, an independent third party.
Payment and donor information you submit on ActBlue is handled under ActBlue's Privacy Policy, not this one.
Federal and state law may require us to publicly report certain donor information.
Like most websites, we automatically collect limited technical information when you visit — such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and pages viewed — to help us operate the site and understand how visitors use it.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information you provide to communicate with you about the campaign, send updates and event invitations, organize volunteers, conduct fundraising, and engage in get-out-the-vote efforts.
We may also use aggregated, non-identifying data to improve our outreach and the website itself.
Information Sharing
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with service providers who help us run the campaign (such as email platforms, hosting providers, and analytics tools) under appropriate confidentiality obligations, and with the Pennsylvania Democratic Party and affiliated Democratic campaigns and committees who share our values and goals.
We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Campaign, our supporters, or others.
Cookies and Analytics
We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences and to measure site performance.
You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features of the site may not work as well without them.
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect the information we collect.
No internet transmission is ever completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
Children's Privacy
This website is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.
Your Choices
You may opt out of campaign emails at any time using the unsubscribe link in any message we send you, or by emailing us.
To request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, contact us at the address below.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we'll update the effective date at the top of this page.
Continued use of the site after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
