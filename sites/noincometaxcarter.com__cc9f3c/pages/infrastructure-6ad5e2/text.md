Our electrical companies are searching for ways to increase their capacity as demands continually increase across our electrical systems.
Solar is not meeting the needs in WA state with our long cloudy winters alongside the high price of installation and upkeep.
Nuclear possibilities along with increased natural gas and upgrading our 100% clean hydro power would all go a long way to making the usage demand of electric cars and winter heating viable.
This is the type of activity government was formed to empower.
Making electricity available to keep our people safe, warm and working is the job for the state.
We can do better!
