The page also follows an audit of the police department's license plate readers, a petition against a dollar store at 1355 MacArthur Boulevard, data centers in West San Leandro and fire station earthquake safety.
San Leandro has worked hard to serve all of its residents.
Let's explore the topics that are still open and think through how we can support the city, so that everyone here can keep living in the place they want to call home.

Business license tax measure
The business license tax.
The city spent up to $283,000 on polling and an outside consulting firm to prepare an update to the business license tax, which would have consolidated 30 classifications into 9 and quadrupled the warehousing rate to $4.50 per $1,000 of gross receipts, a business's total sales before expenses.
The city's measure page named 3 November 2026 as a possible ballot date, while the Tri-City Voice reported on 14 July 2026 that council voted to work toward the November 2028 ballot.
The city's ballot information page lists no business license measure for 2026.
Worth watching: the council minutes confirming the timing.
