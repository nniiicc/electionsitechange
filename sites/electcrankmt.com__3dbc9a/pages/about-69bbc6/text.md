Home About On The Issues Get Involved More Home About On The Issues Get Involved DONATE TODAY Home About On The Issues Get Involved DONATE TODAY ABOUT BOB CRANK COMMITTED TO SERVING YOU Originally from the Midwest, I graduated high school in St.
Louis, Missouri before joining the U.S.
Army Reserves.
From 2003–2004, I deployed to Iraq in support of Operation Enduring Freedom and Operation Iraqi Freedom, serving in the Psychological Operations Command attached to the 101st Airborne Division and concluding my service as a Team Chief (Sergeant).
After returning home, I attended Missouri Baptist University on a partial athletic scholarship and competed on the men’s volleyball team while earning a Bachelor of Science degree in Sports Medicine and Sport Management.
My passion for athletics led to a nearly 20-year career as a collegiate volleyball coach at NCAA Division I, II, and III universities.
In 2021, I came to Montana to work as an outdoor adventure guide, leading trips throughout the region surrounding Yellowstone National Park.
While guiding in Paradise Valley, I built strong relationships with a local ranch family and continued returning to Park County each season to help on ranches and guide elk hunts.
Over time, Montana became home.
In September 2023, I was honored to begin serving the community as a Police Officer with the City of Livingston.
Today, I work as a Community Resource Officer and remain committed to public service.
When I’m not working, I enjoy helping on a ranch in Paradise Valley, guiding dude rides, and exploring Montana’s outdoors with my dog Dolly.
I currently live in Emigrant and am proud to call Park County home.
