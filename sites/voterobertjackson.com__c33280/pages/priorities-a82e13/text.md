The work ahead is focused on strengthening the foundations that allow families and communities to thrive:
• Making New York more affordable for working families
• Protecting tenants and expanding access to housing
• Fully funding public education and expanding opportunities for our youth
• Strengthening workers’ rights and economic security
• Protecting civil rights and immigrant communities
• Investing in safer neighborhoods and stronger public institutions
• Building a more resilient and sustainable future
These priorities reflect the values that have guided my work from the beginning: fairness, accountability, and a belief that government must remain rooted in the communities it serves.
