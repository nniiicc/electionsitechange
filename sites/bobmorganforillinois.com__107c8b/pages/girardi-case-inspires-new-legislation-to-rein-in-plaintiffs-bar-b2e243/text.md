As I noted when the bill advanced, most modern bus manufacturers are already moving in this direction: “This is really the standard for every new bus today that is being manufactured … So, this really should not be any difficulty for school districts.” Key points: The requirement applies only to new buses built after 2031 — existing buses are grandfathered in.
The legislation passed with strong bipartisan support: 83-27 in the House and 39-13 in the Senate.
