Illinois Enacts Law Requiring Seat Belts on New School Buses
Governor JB Pritzker has signed a new law requiring that all new school buses manufactured in Illinois be equipped with lap-and-shoulder (three-point) seat belts, starting in 2031.
As I noted when the bill advanced, most modern bus manufacturers are already moving in this direction:
“This is really the standard for every new bus today that is being manufactured … So, this really should not be any difficulty for school districts.”
Key points:
- The requirement applies only to new buses built after 2031 — existing buses are grandfathered in.
- The legislation passed with strong bipartisan support: 83-27 in the House and 39-13 in the Senate.
- Supporters say this is a commonsense step to protect children and bring Illinois in line with evolving national safety standards.
