Learning from his older brother with the genetic disorder, Fragile X Syndrome, Bob has advocated for disability rights issues, in Springfield and Washington D.C.
He has helped pass legislation protecting against abuse of seniors and adults with disabilities and increased funding for direct service personnel helping adults with disabilities.
