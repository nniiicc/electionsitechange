Home About Issues News Contact Volunteer Donate & Support Home About News Donate Contact Volunteer Civil Rights Bob has spent his life fighting for civil rights for all and has made this a top priority in his work as State Representative.
Throughout his personal and professional life, he has dedicated himself to helping others.
He has been there for those in need of a voice and been a champion for people with disabilities, immigrant families, seniors, LGBTQ communities, women, and the economically challenged.
Bob looks forward to continuing to champion these issues in Springfield.
LGBTQ Rights Bob supports full equality and inclusion for all LGBTQ people, including marriage equality, access to affirming medical care, safe and affirming schools for LGBTQ youth, anti-violence initiatives, and protections against discrimination.

A woman’s choice should be between herself and her doctor, not dictated by politicians.
Read More Disability Rights Bob is committed to protecting the rights of and securing opportunities for people with disabilities.
Learning from his older brother with the genetic disorder, Fragile X Syndrome, Bob has advocated for disability rights issues, in Springfield and Washington D.C.
