TRACY'S PRIORITIES
1.
COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT
A government that listens, includes, and delivers.
- Expand resident involvement in decision-making
- Host regular community forums
- Partner with local organizations
- Promote transparency and accountability
2.
HUMAN RIGHTS & EQUITY
Fairness, dignity, and justice for all.
- Advance equitable policies for every community
- Address systemic inequalities
- Support criminal justice reform and reentry
- Stand against all forms of discrimination
3.
EDUCATION
Stronger schools for every student.
- Tackle absenteeism and barriers to attendance
- Invest in student support and resources
- Expand life skills and practical learning programs
- Ensure safe, inclusive learning environments
4.
WORKING FAMILIES
Supporting families to thrive.
- Increase access to affordable childcare
- Strengthen after-school programs
- Support working parents with flexible solutions
- Expand access to essential services
5.
YOUTH & OPPORTUNITY
Investing in the next generation.
- Grow mentorship and youth programs
- Expand workforce and career training
- Create safe spaces for youth engagement
- Empower young people to lead
6.
ENVIRONMENT
Protecting the places we call home.
- Preserve green spaces, parks, and natural resources
- Improve air and water quality across our communities
- Support climate resilience and flood mitigation efforts
- Promote environmental education and green workforce opportunities
