03 🌿Nature Coast & Local Economy
Our waters are who we are.
I will protect them.
The problem
- 7 of 8 Springs Coast springs exceed safe nitrate levels SWFWMD
- Septic and fertilizer runoff dumps 5 million lbs of nitrogen into springs every year DEP
- Weeki Wachee choking on algae from water overuse and runoff — threatening the $800M+ Nature Coast economy Visit Florida
- The Water Management District board that oversees our springs isn't elected by the people who drink this water — all nine members are appointed by the Governor, with no vote from Nature Coast residents SWFWMD / FL Statute 373
- Once a project is permitted, regulators simply assume it's protecting the water — no one checks.
Builders keep adding septic tanks near Weeki Wachee and Homosassa, even as the springs choke on algae Tampa Bay Times, Oct. 2025
- Data centers are creeping into Florida, straining water and power supplies we can't spare
Tony's solution
- Incentives for native ground covers — use 60% less water and cut nitrogen runoff by 85% UC Davis / UCS
- Moratorium on new development threatening our springs and waterways
- Full transparency on every dollar invested — no backroom deals, no polluter carve-outs
- Make Water Management District board members elected, not appointed — so the people who drink this water have a say in who protects it
- Oppose any further hyperscale data centers in Florida
