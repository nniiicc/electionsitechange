01 💰Affordability & Costs I will fight the costs squeezing District 53 families.
The problem Everyday goods and groceries continue to take a bigger bite out of family budgets.
High gas prices make getting to work and running a household more expensive.
Homeowners insurance remains unaffordable for too many Florida families.
Rent and housing costs consume too much of working families' income, while Tallahassee has preempted local governments from regulating rents and many landlord-tenant issues.
Too many Florida children face food insecurity while the state continues to forego SUN Bucks federal summer food assistance Tony's solution Propose targeted tax holidays on essential household goods to provide direct relief.
Propose temporary fuel-tax relief when gas prices spike.
Reform Citizens Insurance to prioritize affordability, transparency, responsible depopulation, and meaningful mitigation savings.
Revisit Tallahassee's preemption of local rental policy and give communities appropriate tools to address their own housing affordability problems, while expanding attainable workforce housing.
Bring SUN Bucks to Florida , giving eligible children summer grocery assistance and bringing federal food-assistance dollars back to Florida families.
