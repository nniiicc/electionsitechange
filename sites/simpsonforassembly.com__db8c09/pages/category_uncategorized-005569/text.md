By Lake George Mirror Staff
Matt Simpson, the Republican candidate for the Lake George region’s seat in the New York State Assembly, has announced that he has secured the endorsement of more than 175 elected village, town, and county officials from throughout the district.
Warren, Essex and several towns in Washington and Saratoga Counties comprise the 114th Assembly District, which is currently represented by Republican Dan Stec.
“These men and women have been entrusted by tens of thousands of voters from the small towns and villages with the North Country’s future.
I look forward to putting their invaluable insights to work in Albany and fighting to make sure our voices are heard.”
Simpson says his terms as president of the Association of Adirondack Towns and Villages, as a member of the Adirondack Park Local Government Review Board and of the Warren County Board of Supervisors, have made him familiar with many of the issues that will cross his desk as a legislator representing the Adirondacks.
Among them: declining school enrollments and aging, shrinking populations (although the population of Simpson’s own town has increased, thanks, probably, to retirees making their second homes their year-round residences).
Simpson says two priorities are protecting the Park’s woods and waters and growing small town economies with businesses not unlike Brant Lake’s Hub, a café – performance space – wine, craft beer and cider bar-cum-bicycle repair shop, whose owner has also helped develop and promote northern Warren County’s natural and recreational resources.
Those serving in the Lake George watershed endorsing Simpson include: Lake George Town officials Dennis Dickinson, Vinny Crocitto, Marisa Muratori, Nancy Stannard; Lake George Village officials Bob Blais, John Earl, Raymond Perry; Town of Hague officials Steve Ramant, Council Member, Martin Fitzgerald II, Donald Smith; Bolton officials Ron Conover, Robert MacEwan, Susan Wilson, Tim Coon, Wanda Cleavland, Jodi Pettys, and William Sherman.

Matt Simpson honored by the Adirondack Lakes Alliance
BRANT LAKE | The Adirondack Lakes Alliance has honored Town of Horicon Supervisor Matt Simpson for his half-dozen years of advocacy work towards protecting the environmental quality of lakes and rivers in the Adirondacks.
The award was presented Aug. 1 at the Alliance’s annual symposium, held at Paul Smith’s College.
Simpson was honored for distinguished stewardship of area waterways and their surroundings, as well as outstanding service to Adirondack communities.
