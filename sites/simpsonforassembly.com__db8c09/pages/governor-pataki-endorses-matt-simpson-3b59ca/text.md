News Contact Search for: Search Governor Pataki endorses Matt Simpson news October 28, 2020 0 62 Search for: Search Search Search for: Search I’m honored to receive an endorsement from Governor Pataki.
“Matt Simpson is going to be a terrific Assemblyman and I am proud to endorse him.
Matt has lived in our district all his life and he knows our challenges.
He’s a self-made guy who built a successful small business and raised his family here.
As a local official, Matt has earned respect from people of all political parties.
He is an accomplished leader on critical issues like expanding broadband in the North Country and protecting our natural resources in a smart way that will allow our local economy to grow.
Matt knows what he’s doing, and his competence is important in times like these.
I hope you’ll join me in supporting Matt Simpson for Assembly.
He’s exactly the type of leader we need in Albany and I look forward to him being our representative,” said Governor Pataki.
