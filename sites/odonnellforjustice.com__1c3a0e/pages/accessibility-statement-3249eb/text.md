Accessibility Statement This statement reflects our ongoing commitment to making our website accessible to everyone.
We strive to ensure that all individuals, including those with disabilities, can access and enjoy our website.
WHAT WEB ACCESSIBILITY IS Web accessibility is about designing and developing websites so that people with disabilities can use them.
An accessible site provides an inclusive user experience, allowing all users to navigate, understand, and interact with the content.
ACCESSIBILITY ADJUSTMENTS ON THIS SITE We have designed this site in accordance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) [2.0 / 2.1 / 2.2] to ensure it is accessible to everyone.
Our website is compatible with assistive technologies, such as screen readers and keyboard navigation.
To achieve this, we have: • Utilized the Accessibility Wizard to identify and address potential accessibility issues • Established a clear content order and heading structure for improved navigation • Provided descriptive alternative text for images • Implemented color combinations that meet required contrast ratios • Minimized the use of motion to prevent distraction • Ensured accessibility of all multimedia and documents on the site DECLARATION OF PARTIAL COMPLIANCE WITH THE STANDARD DUE TO THIRD-PARTY CONTENT [ONLY ADD IF RELEVANT] Certain pages on our site may contain third-party content that could impact overall accessibility.
We are actively working to address any issues and ensure full compliance with accessibility standards on these pages.
ACCESSIBILITY ARRANGEMENTS IN THE ORGANIZATION [ONLY ADD IF RELEVANT] As an online campaign, our accessibility arrangements primarily focus on ensuring the inclusivity of our digital platforms.
We are dedicated to addressing any accessibility concerns and enhancing the overall user experience for all visitors.
REQUESTS, ISSUES, AND SUGGESTIONS We welcome feedback on the accessibility of our website.
