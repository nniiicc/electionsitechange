On to 2026!
I’m proud to represent the 43rd District—and I’m running for re-election in 2026 to keep fighting for what matters.
I’ll keep defending women’s reproductive freedom, protecting seniors, expanding access to affordable healthcare, creating good-paying jobs, and standing strong with working families.
I will never back down from opposing Donald Trump’s dangerous agenda to dismantle Medicaid and Social Security, weaken our democracy, and harm our most vulnerable neighbors.
Illinois must remain a beacon of fairness, freedom, and opportunity—and I’ll be a tireless voice for justice, dignity, and a brighter future for all.
Let’s keep moving forward—together.
I have been honored to represent the 43rd District in Springfield and I am running for re-election to continue advancing the values we share.
