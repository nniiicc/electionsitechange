Harold Hutchison Candidate for Oregon Senate District 15 Facebook Email X Substack Who I am I am 72 and now retired.
I have lived in Oregon since mid-1963, arriving at age 8 in Pendleton.
Liz and I married in 1981, then in 1990 we moved our children to Beaverton.
In 1992 we moved again, to Forest Grove, and still live in our first house here.
We have 5 children and 4 grandchildren.
I am Conservative, believe that Christ is our risen Savior, and ardently believe that these United States are special and I strive to see they remain that way.

I am done sitting on my hands as people with a far different view of what our society should look like are elevated by default to positions of authority.
The Democrat Party has grown far out of touch with the good people of this state, and it is time to replace the madness with some common sense.
