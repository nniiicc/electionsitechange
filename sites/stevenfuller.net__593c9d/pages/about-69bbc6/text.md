Hi there!
I’m Steven Fuller, and this is who I am.
Military brat, and then joined the Army at 17.
Served for 14 years as an Infantryman with three combat tours in Iraq starting in OIF 1.
In between I did three years as a Drill sergeant.
I left the military in December 2011 and went to college for a few years.
Bounced around the country living all over before settling here in Colorado in 2017.
I fell in love with the natural beauty, culture, and life here in El Paso county.
Started Wackadoo Brewing over six years ago as the only brewery in America to ever start in a VFW and the only brewery in America that donates 100% of our profits every month to charity.
We currently work with 21 different nonprofits, almost all of them Veteran nonprofits.
During my years with the brewery we have helped so many people in this community and have been a part of countless organizations.
I have never paid myself a dime, I do this to help others, especially Veterans and families.
I recently married the love of my life Renee and adopted her daughter, now my daughter as well.
I am the 2025 Veteran Entrepreneur of the year for Colorado.
I believe people should come first, profit and personal gain are not a thought for me as long as I can pay the bills and help others.
I am running for congress in District 5 as an Independent.
