Priorities
Lower Property Taxes
I am committed to helping Shakopee families keep more of their hard‑earned money by advocating for responsible tax policies that reduce financial burdens.
Strong Schools and Quality Education
I am dedicated to strengthening Shakopee’s schools and ensuring every student has access to a high-quality education.
I will empower parents, teachers, and local school leaders to make decisions that best serve their students.
Supporting Law Enforcement and Public Safety
I am committed to keeping Shakopee safe by supporting the law enforcement officers who protect our community every day.
With over 30 years of experience in law enforcement, I understand firsthand the tools, training, and resources officers need to do their jobs effectively.
Tackling the Rampant Fraud in Our Community
I am dedicated to protecting Shakopee families by taking action against rampant fraud and abuse.
I will tackle this issue head-on, by implementing stronger oversight, accountability, and enforcement measures to stop fraud before it happens.
Affordable Housing and Childcare
I am committed to ensuring Shakopee families have access to affordable housing and quality childcare.
I support commonsense policies that lower costs, cut red tape, and make it easier for working families to find safe, affordable places to live.
I also advocate for expanding access to reliable, high-quality childcare options so parents can pursue careers and provide for their families without added financial strain.
Reducing the Political Divide
I am committed to bringing people together by focusing on practical, community-driven solutions that unite Shakopee.
I believe that progress comes from listening to different perspectives and working across party lines to find common ground.
