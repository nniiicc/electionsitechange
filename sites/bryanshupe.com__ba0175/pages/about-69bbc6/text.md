Who I Am A Lifelong Delawarean Bryan Shupe is a husband, father, and lifelong Delawarean who has built his career serving his community and creating opportunities for others.
He has spent over 15 years building small businesses, creating jobs, and investing in Milford.
Today, Bryan serves as your State Representative, focused on lowering costs, protecting families, and holding government accountable.
