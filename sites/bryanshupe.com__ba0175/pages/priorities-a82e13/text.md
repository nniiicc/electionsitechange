36 th District Home About Priorities News Voting Get Involved Donate State Representative · Bryan Shupe My Legislative Priorities Delaware families deserve to know exactly what is happening in Legislative Hall and where their elected officials stand.
As your State Representative, I am fighting to lower costs, protect taxpayers, defend parental rights, strengthen public safety, support small businesses, protect election integrity, and hold government accountable.
This page is a resource for you to review the legislation I am working on, track the issues that matter most, and see how I am standing up for our community in Dover.
On This Page Lower Costs & Responsible Spending Better & Safer Schools Affordable Healthcare Local Control & Property Rights Stronger Families Clean Water & Natural Resources First Responders & Veterans Lower Costs & Responsible Spending HB 126 — Overtime Tax Relief Sponsored legislation to eliminate Delaware state income tax on overtime earnings for full-time hourly employees working more than 40 hours per week.
HB 233 — Personal Income Tax Relief Proposed reducing personal income tax rates so Delaware taxpayers can keep more of what they earn.
HB 246 — Property Tax Protection Sponsored legislation addressing the authority for school districts to increase property-tax revenue by up to 10% following a general reassessment.
The latest substitute would remove the 10% provision while permitting limited increases when a district demonstrates a projected revenue loss.
HB 429 — Constitutional Spending Limits Proposed constitutional limits on state spending following six years in which state spending increased approximately 42% while revenue increased approximately 22%.
HR 21 — Financial Responsibility Training for Legislators Sponsored a House resolution requiring representatives who do not serve on the Joint Finance Committee or Joint Committee on Capital Improvement to complete financial-responsibility training.
Better & Safer Schools HB 29 — School Performance Transparency Created an online school-performance portal to give parents easier access to information about how Delaware schools are performing.
HB 69 — Supporting Struggling Readers Provided additional literacy intervention and support for students struggling to read.
HB 32 — Safer Schools Creates a state funding mechanism to help schools employ school resource officers and constables.
Statewide Early Childhood Literacy Program Created an early-childhood literacy program working with Delaware libraries and Read Aloud Delaware to provide families with resources that encourage reading and learning at home.
Affordable Healthcare HB 318 — Healthcare Competition Sponsored legislation seeking to eliminate Delaware’s Certificate of Need system with the stated goal of increasing competition among healthcare providers.
HB 160 — Telehealth Supported expanding access to healthcare through telehealth services.
SS 1 for SB 65 — FAST Program Supported tuition assistance of up to $10,000 for eligible Delawareans pursuing education and training for in-demand careers, including healthcare.
Local Control & Property Rights HB 297 — Holding Developers Accountable Sponsored legislation requiring developers to provide financial security to help ensure required subdivision road improvements are completed.
SS 2 for SB 23 — Local Land-Use Authority Opposed legislation concerning state and local requirements for housing development, including provisions affecting local land-use decision-making.
Protecting Private Property — Annette Billings Stood alongside a Milford homeowner during the city’s effort to acquire her private property through eminent domain.
Stronger Families HB 75 — Protecting Survivors of Childhood Sexual Abuse Sponsored legislation addressing civil claims involving survivors of childhood sexual abuse and institutions accused of concealing abuse.
HB 113 — Pensions and Crimes Against Children Sponsored legislation to deny the state-funded portion of a pension to education employees convicted of specified crimes against children committed in connection with their employment.
Memorial Park Designed park improvements and helped secure funding for a new playground and pickleball courts.
Addiction & Recovery Worked to increase support for organizations such as Adult & Teen Challenge and Love INC that provide addiction recovery and other services intended to help individuals and families achieve greater stability and independence.
Clean Water & Natural Resources Drinking Water Purification Program Created a water-purification initiative with state drinking-water officials to assist families dealing with contaminated private wells.
HB 370 — Buyer Property Protection Act Proposed requiring recent water-quality information to be provided before the sale of residential property.
First Responders & Veterans HB 230 — Fire, EMS & Paramedic Funding Supported the allocation of millions of dollars in grant funding benefiting local fire companies, ambulance services, and paramedics.
HB 236 — EMT Pension Benefits Supported a proposal to expand eligibility under the police and firefighters’ pension system to include qualifying EMTs.
HB 1 — Delaware Department of Veterans Affairs Voted to establish the Delaware Department of Veterans Affairs to coordinate services and help veterans access benefits and resources.
HB 160 — Delaware National Guard Education Benefits Supported legislation enacted into law reforming post-secondary education benefits for eligible Delaware National Guard members to make those benefits more accessible.
SB 219 — Military Pension Tax Relief Supported increasing Delaware’s state income-tax exclusion for qualifying military retirement income from $12,500 to $25,000, with the increase phased in.
