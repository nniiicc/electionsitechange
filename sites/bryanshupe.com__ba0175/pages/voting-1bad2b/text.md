Ten Days of In-Person Voting Early Voting Delaware offers # days of in-person early voting before the General Election.
Voters may cast their ballot at any designated early-voting location within the county where they live.
For the 2026 General Election, early voting is scheduled for: October 22–24 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m.
October 25 No early voting October 26–27 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m.

Official Early-Voting Locations & Schedule Voting by Mail Absentee Voting Delaware voters who qualify under state law may vote by absentee ballot.
Eligible voters can request an absentee ballot through the Delaware Department of Elections.
For the November 3 General Election, October 30 is the deadline for the Department of Elections to mail absentee ballots, and November 2 at noon is the deadline for the Department to issue absentee ballots.
Completed absentee ballots must be received by the voter’s county Department of Elections office by 8:00 p.m. on Election Day, November 3.
For complete eligibility requirements and the most current absentee-voting information, visit the Delaware Department of Elections.
