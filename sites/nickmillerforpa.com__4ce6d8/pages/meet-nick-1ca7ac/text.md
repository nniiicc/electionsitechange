Senator Nick Miller represents the 14th Senatorial District, serving communities across Lehigh and Northampton Counties.
A fifth-generation Allentown resident, Nick was raised by a dedicated public servant who taught him the importance of giving back.
Following his graduation from Penn State University, he built his career as a Project Manager and later launched his own business.
He began his public service on the Allentown School Board, where he helped direct $34 million in federal funds to modernize school buildings and improve learning environments.
During his time serving Allentown’s 17,000 students on the School Board, he also earned two master’s degrees from the University of Pennsylvania in public administration and law.
Elected in 2022 at age 27, Miller was the youngest Pennsylvanian elected to the state senate in 135 years.
He was elected chair of the Pennsylvania Senate Policy Committee in 2024, becoming the youngest member in history to hold a leadership position in the state Senate.
Since his 2022 election to the Pennsylvania Senate, Nick has championed historic investments in public education, boosted small businesses and family-sustaining jobs, and backed initiatives that make our communities safer.
He understands the challenges families and local employers face and is committed to expanding economic opportunity and strengthening communities for the long term.
