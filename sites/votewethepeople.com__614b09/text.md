Home The Farmer Servant Movement We’re done with empty promises.
Oklahoma deserves leaders who show their plan, follow the people’s will, and protect constitutional rights.
I’m Robert Brooks.
I’m building a movement that gives every Oklahoman a real seat at the table.
The Problem Career politicians keep getting promoted while Oklahoma ranks 50th in education.
Turnpikes expand, taxes rise, and lobbyists win while small towns, small businesses, and teachers get left behind.
Candidates talk in slogans.
They rarely show how they’ll deliver.
Why Now Every other candidate in this race has already held office.
They had the power to make things better and didn’t.
If we want different results, we need a different model of leadership.
The Solution: The People’s App A governor who asks the people first before signing bills, issuing pardons, or proposing policy.
Real-time input: Every registered voter can weigh in.
Transparent decisions: The governor commits to follow the people’s majority.
Accountability: Public dashboards show what the people decided and what action was taken.
How It Works You receive clear, plain-language summaries of bills and decisions.
You vote securely in the app.
The results are published.
The governor signs or vetoes according to the people’s will and shows the receipts.
What We Stand For Parents, teachers, and students first, measurable gains in education, not talking points.
Safer communities through common-sense policy shaped by the people closest to the problem.
Respect for property rights, farmers, ranchers, small business owners, first responders, and working families.
Zero tolerance for pay-to-play politics.
Call to Action If you demand elected officials who only do the will of the people, who observe your constitutional rights, and who listen to the people, join me.
