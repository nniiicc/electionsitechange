We The People of Oklahoma
Preamble
By entering my information below and affixing my digital signature, I solemnly pledge that I will support and vote only for candidates who publicly commit to the following terms.
I further affirm that I will not cast my vote for any candidate who refuses these commitments.
The Oath
1.
Direct Will of the People
I will only vote for a governor who promises to carry out the majority will of Oklahomans through a direct communication channel such as the People’s App.
2.
Rights Observed
I will only vote for candidates who publicly promise to observe and protect the constitutional rights of the People at all times.
3.
Always Listen
I will only vote for candidates who pledge to listen and remain connected to the People by using open, ongoing forums such as the People’s Table, and who commit never to become insulated from the public.
Binding Intent (Public Pledge)
By submitting this form, I affirm that I will only elect, support, volunteer for, or otherwise assist candidates and initiatives that uphold these promises.
Otherwise, I will not vote for that candidate.
