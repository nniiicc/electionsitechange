It’s a WE Thing, Not a ME Thing.
For too long, Oklahoma government has been driven by political parties, special interests, and career politicians instead of the people they were elected to serve.
The Farmer Servant Movement is built on one simple belief: Government belongs to the people.
These are our Four Promises to every Oklahoman.
Promise One: Let the People Decide
The people deserve a voice before major decisions are made.
Through the People’s App, registered Oklahoma voters will have the opportunity to weigh in on major legislation, significant state projects, appointments, pardons, clemency decisions, and other important issues affecting our state.
Government should listen to the people—not just during elections, but every day.
Our Promise: We will ask the people before making major decisions whenever practical and lawful.
Promise Two: Let the People See
Trust begins with transparency.
Through the People’s Table, Oklahomans will have greater access to government information, public records, audits, proposals, reports, and the decision-making process.
Government should never operate behind closed doors when it can operate in the open.
Our Promise: We will make your government more transparent and accountable to the people.
Promise Three: Protect the Rights of Every Oklahoman
The Governor’s first responsibility is to protect the constitutional rights and freedoms of every citizen.
Those rights do not belong to one political party, one religion, one group, or one community.
They belong to every Oklahoman.
Our Promise: We will defend the constitutional and civil rights of every person fairly and equally.
Promise Four: Weed Out Corruption, Waste, Fraud, and Abuse
Every dollar wasted by government is a dollar taken from the people.
Our administration will aggressively identify corruption, eliminate unnecessary government waste, expose fraud, investigate abuse of public office, and hold public officials accountable regardless of political party.
When government operates more efficiently, taxpayers should benefit.
Our Promise: We will work to return savings to the people through lower taxes, debt reduction, better services, or other fiscally responsible solutions that strengthen Oklahoma.
Our Commitment to Oklahoma
- Let the People Decide
- Let the People See
- Protect the Rights of Every Oklahoman
- Weed Out Corruption, Waste, Fraud, and Abuse
These are not campaign slogans.
They are the standard by which we ask the people to judge our administration.
Thank You
Thank you for taking the time to learn about the Farmer Servant Movement.
Whether you agree with every proposal or not, we appreciate your willingness to become an informed voter.
Together, we can build a government that listens to the people, operates in the open, protects the rights of every Oklahoman, and never stops fighting corruption.
Every movement begins with one person choosing to get involved.
We’d be honored to have you join ours.
