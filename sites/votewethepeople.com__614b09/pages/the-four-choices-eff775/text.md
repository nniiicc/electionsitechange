When your political team disappoints you, what comes first: defending the team or demanding accountability?
Read all four.
Ask yourself honestly where you stand.
A My team comes first.
The right.
“I see Donald Trump as the leader who can save the country.
I welcome Mike Mazzei aligning himself with Trump.
Even when I acknowledge false claims, like immigrants eating pets in Springfield, I still trust his judgment on bigger decisions, including war.” Would I excuse the same behavior from a Democrat?
B My team comes first.
The left.
“I support the No Kings movement and believe leaders must answer to the people.
But I accept my party’s decision to nominate Kamala Harris after Biden withdrew, without a new competitive presidential primary for voters to choose his replacement.” Would I accept the same process from Republicans?
C I’m in the middle.
I pick a likely winner.
“I don’t feel at home with either extreme.
I may prefer another candidate’s ideas, but I usually choose a major-party candidate because I believe that person has a better chance of winning.” Am I choosing the government I want?
D I’ve had enough.
Earn my vote.
“I am willing to consider any candidate who makes a public covenant with the people before Election Day: a clear platform, specific commitments, stated goals, and ways to hold that candidate accountable.” Show me what you will do.
