Home → The Farmer Servant Movement The Farmer Servant Movement Who The Farmer-Servant Movement is made up of ordinary, everyday citizens, what I call Farmer-Servant citizens.
These are people from every walk of life who are sick and tired of government lies, political games, and being ignored by those they elect.
We’re not lobbyists, elites, or party insiders.
We’re parents, teachers, farmers, veterans, small-business owners, and workers who are willing to make three solemn promises: 1.
To only do the will of the people through the People’s App.
2.
To make sure that every decision is constitutionally sound.
3.
To always listen to the people.
What The Farmer-Servant Movement is a grassroots call to action for average citizens to reclaim government from corrupt and disconnected politicians.
It’s a movement for truth, transparency, and accountability where every citizen can have a voice in every major decision through direct input on the People’s App.
Farmer-Servants believe in a government that is once again of, for, and by the people, not by lobbyists, donors, or party bosses.
Where The movement began right here in Oklahoma, but it’s an idea meant for every American community.
It’s born out of frustration with decades of political lies, from broken promises to Native American tribes before statehood, to the deceit of the land runs, to politicians’ ongoing false promises about temporary turnpikes and “education-only” taxes.
Wherever people have been lied to, ignored, or silenced, that’s where the Farmer-Servant Movement belongs.
When This movement didn’t just start today, its roots reach all the way back to the founding of our nation.
You see, the framers of our state and federal constitutions were the original Farmer-Servants.
They designed a system where power was meant to flow upward, from the people to their representatives.
Back then, the framers didn’t have smartphones, Wi-Fi, or modern apps.
The only way they could gather the will of the people was by sending out delegates, men who would travel across colonies and territories to collect the voice of the people.
But today, we have modern technology that allows us to do the same thing instantly.
Through tools like the People’s App, we can collect feedback, votes, and ideas in real time.
There’s no excuse anymore for politicians to hide from the people, ignore questions, or claim they don’t know what the public wants.
The Farmer-Servant Movement simply restores the original vision of the founders, using the technology of today.
Why Because the people have had enough.
We’re tired of politicians who say one thing and do another, who promise transparency but hide behind closed doors, who talk about “the people” but never listen to them.
The Farmer-Servant Movement exists to end that cycle.
We’re standing up for the simple idea that government should be as honest, humble, and hardworking as the people it represents.
This isn’t a new revolution, it’s a return to the original one.
How We do it by using technology and truth.
Through the People’s App, we give every citizen a direct say in the decisions that shape their lives.
We use open communication — online and in person, to confront and expose corrupt politicians.
We ask hard questions.
We demand written guarantees.
We insist that every plan be constitutional, transparent, and accountable to the people.
And if the politicians won’t listen?
Then we’ll elect Farmer-Servants ourselves, citizens who will take back the Governor’s seat and restore power where it belongs: with We the People.
