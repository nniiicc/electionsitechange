Home → The Farmer Servant’s Prayer The Farmer Servant’s Prayer Heavenly Father, I come before You today as a humble servant of the people, a Farmer Servant, seeking the same courage Your Son Jesus showed when He walked into the temples, when He confronted the governments and the Pharisees of His day, and when He turned over the tables of corruption without fear.
Lord, walk with me now as You walked with Him then.
Let Your Spirit remove the scales from the eyes of this state and this nation, so the truth can be seen, spoken, and defended.
Give me a heart that is bold but not bitter, courageous but not cruel, firm in the fight but humble in the mission.
Let every step I take be guided by Your wisdom, every word I speak be wrapped in Your truth, and every action I make be covered by Your protection.
Jesus, You exposed the injustice of Your time— not for power, not for politics, but for the people.
Let me walk that same path.
Let me stand against modern-day Pharisees, against corruption, greed, and those who abuse the authority that belongs to the people and ultimately to You.
Strengthen me as I confront what needs confronting.
Shield me as I reveal what needs revealing.
Uphold me as I fight for the Constitution, for justice, for the forgotten, for the people of Oklahoma, and for every ordinary person who deserves truth and fairness.
And Lord, remind me always that this battle is Yours first.
Let me be only the vessel, Your voice, Your hands, Your servant in this season.
Bless this movement, bless this mission, and bless every soul who walks beside me.
May Your light expose every shadow.
May Your justice break every chain.
May Your truth prevail over every lie.
In Jesus’ mighty name I pray, Amen.
