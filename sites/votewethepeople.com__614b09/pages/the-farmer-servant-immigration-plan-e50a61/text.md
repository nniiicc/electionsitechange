Enforce the Law.
Treat People with Dignity.
Immigration has become one of the most divisive issues in America.
For too long, politicians have used it as a political weapon instead of working toward practical solutions.
The Farmer Servant Immigration Plan is built on a simple belief: A nation has both the right and the responsibility to secure its borders, enforce its laws fairly, and treat every person with dignity.
This plan seeks to protect Oklahoma communities, support law enforcement, strengthen the economy, and create a lawful, orderly immigration system.
It is inspired by approaches taken by leaders from both political parties who supported stronger border security while also creating legal pathways for people willing to comply with the law.
Presidents including Ronald Reagan signed major immigration legislation, and President Bill Clinton also emphasized border security and immigration enforcement during his administration.
The Farmer Servant Plan draws on lessons from those approaches while recognizing today’s legal and economic realities.
Step 1: Secure the Border Every nation has the right to know who is entering its borders.
The Farmer Servant Plan supports strong border security, adequate staffing, modern technology, and cooperation among federal, state, local, and tribal law enforcement agencies.
A secure border is the foundation of an orderly immigration system.
Step 2: Enforce Existing Laws Consistently Immigration laws should be enforced consistently and fairly.
Government should apply the law equally while respecting constitutional rights and due process.
Predictable enforcement builds confidence in the system.
Step 3: Prioritize Public Safety Law enforcement resources should focus first on individuals who present the greatest risk to public safety.
Priority should be given to those involved in: Violent crime Human trafficking Drug trafficking Organized criminal activity Terrorism-related offenses Repeat serious offenders Protecting Oklahoma families must remain the highest priority.
Step 4: Create a Voluntary Registration Process Individuals who are in the country without legal status and are not considered a threat to public safety should have the opportunity to voluntarily identify themselves and begin addressing their immigration status through legal processes established by federal law.
Participation would include, where authorized by law: Identity verification Background checks Documentation review Cooperation with immigration authorities Compliance with applicable legal requirements The goal is to encourage lawful compliance rather than driving people further into the shadows.
Step 5: Support Legal Immigration America has long benefited from lawful immigration.
The Farmer Servant Plan supports an immigration system that is: Orderly Predictable Efficient Fair Focused on following the law Those who follow the legal process should be treated with respect.
Step 6: Protect Oklahoma Workers Immigration policy should also protect Oklahoma workers and employers.
The plan supports enforcing employment laws, discouraging exploitation, and promoting a level playing field for businesses that follow the rules.
No employer should gain an unfair advantage by violating the law.
Step 7: Work with the Federal Government Immigration is primarily a federal responsibility.
Oklahoma should cooperate with federal authorities while focusing state resources on protecting public safety, supporting local communities, and enforcing state law.
Step 8: Respect Human Dignity Every person should be treated with dignity and respect.
Enforcing immigration laws and treating people humanely are not contradictory goals.
Government can uphold the law while recognizing the humanity of every individual.
Step 9: Encourage Citizenship Through the Legal Process People who wish to become Americans should be encouraged to pursue lawful pathways provided under federal law.
Citizenship carries extraordinary rights and responsibilities.
The legal process should be respected and strengthened.
Step 10: Reject Political Division Immigration should not be used to divide Americans.
The goal should not be scoring political points.
The goal should be creating policies that are lawful, practical, compassionate, and focused on public safety.
The Goal The Farmer Servant Immigration Plan seeks to move beyond political slogans and toward practical solutions.
It recognizes that Oklahoma needs secure borders, consistent enforcement, respect for the rule of law, and compassion for people.
Rather than embracing extremes, this plan follows a balanced approach inspired by leaders from different political traditions who believed border security and lawful opportunities could exist together.
The goal is an immigration system that protects Oklahoma communities, respects the Constitution, supports legal immigration, and reflects both justice and compassion.
Because strong borders and human dignity are not opposing values.
They are both essential to a nation governed by the rule of law.
