Home → Paid Time Off Paid Time Off THE PEOPLE’S VOLUNTARY PAID TIME OFF PLAN We propose a voluntary program that helps Oklahoma small business owners fund paid sick leave and vacation time for their employees.
No business will be required to participate.
HOW IT WORKS Participating employers make regular contributions into dedicated paid-leave accounts.
Employees earn sick and vacation hours under the benefit package their employer selects.
When an employee takes covered time off, the employer draws against its funded account to pay the employee through normal payroll.
This spreads the cost throughout the year and gives businesses a practical way to budget for employee benefits.
The program will draw on Oklahoma’s state employee approach to earning and tracking leave, adapted for participating small businesses.
PUT THE MONEY TO WORK While funds await use, they will be managed under conservative investment rules designed to protect principal and keep money available for leave payments.
Investment earnings are not guaranteed.
Employee benefits, required reserves, and reasonable operating costs come first.
Only available net earnings will be considered for additional uses.
THE PEOPLE DECIDE WHERE THE EARNINGS GO We will present five options on the People’s App : Employer Contribution Credits: Return value to participating small businesses by reducing their future deposits.
Additional Employee Leave: Fund extra paid sick or vacation hours for participating workers.
Emergency Illness Assistance: Help eligible employees facing serious illness after their earned leave runs out.
Small Business Startup Assistance: Help qualifying businesses begin providing paid time off through matching contributions.
Temporary Staffing Support: Help participating businesses pay for replacement workers during covered absences.
The people will review the costs, benefits, and financial limits of these options and help decide how available earnings should benefit Oklahoma’s workers and small businesses.
OUR PATH TO DELIVERY We will use that public input to craft proposed legislation with small business owners, employees, and financial and employee-benefits professionals, then submit it to the Oklahoma Legislature.
The proposal will establish protected accounts, clear withdrawal rules, investment safeguards, transparent administration, and independent audits.
The program will launch after the necessary legislation and operating rules are approved.
A VOLUNTARY, PRACTICAL APPROACH Voluntary participation.
Employer-funded paid leave.
Protected benefits.
Public input on how available earnings are used.
It’s a WE thing, not a ME thing.
