Contract with Ridgefield
On July 9th, 2026, we did something historic...
Our campaign was the first ever in the state of Connecticut to sign a binding contract with my constituents.
If I fail to comply with any of my core campaign promises while in office, I forfeit my right to run for reelection.
This document was notarized at town hall, and an additional copy has been left behind with the town clerk (available for public inspection).
As a candidate, I believe that if you can hire me, then you can fire me.
Attached alongside is a scanned photocopy of this very contract.
For Ridgefield.
