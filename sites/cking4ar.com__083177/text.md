"Courtney brings a deep commitment to her community, a strong connection to
Arkansas's natural heritage, and a passion for public service that reflects the values
of District 25.
A lifelong Arkansan, originally from Fort Smith and now rooted in
Northwest Arkansas, Courtney has lived and worked in the district for more than two decades.
As a mother of three, multimedia artist, environmental educator, and
certified Art Therapy Practitioner, Courtney has dedicated her career to serving
others and fostering connections between people, community, and the outdoors.
Her experience spans classrooms and outdoor learning environments across
Arkansas, from Fort Smith and Fayetteville to the Ozark Highlands Trail, and
includes serving as a facility manager at Hobbs State Park, Arkansas's largest state park.
Courtney understands the importance of protecting Arkansas's natural resources
while building healthy, thriving communities.
She will bring her creativity,
leadership, and commitment to public service to the State Capitol, providing the
thoughtful and forward-looking representation that District 25 deserves."
