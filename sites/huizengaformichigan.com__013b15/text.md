Meet Bill
Bill is a lifelong Ottawa County resident, small business owner, and proven leader fighting for Southwest Michigan in Congress.
Before serving in Washington, Bill built and ran a local business, giving him firsthand experience with the challenges facing employers and workers across the region.
In Congress, he has earned a reputation for fiscal discipline, government accountability, and standing firm on the issues that matter most.
Bill and his wife Natalie raised their five children in Southwest Michigan and remain deeply rooted in the community they serve.
On the Issues
Bill Huizenga doesn’t just talk; he delivers real results for the people and communities of Southwest Michigan.
From the lakeshore to Kalamazoo and Battle Creek, this region depends on strong manufacturing, agriculture, and small business growth.
Bill has made it his mission to protect that economic foundation and ensure families here can afford to live, work, and raise the next generation.
