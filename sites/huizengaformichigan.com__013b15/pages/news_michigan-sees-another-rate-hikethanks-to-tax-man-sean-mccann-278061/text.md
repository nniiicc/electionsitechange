HOLLAND, MI – Michigan families will see yet another surcharge added to their monthly energy bills, according the state’s utility regulators, all thanks to Tax Man Sean McCann.
Reminder: McCann voted in 2024 to let the commission jack up this exact surcharge on Michigan families.
And that’s after he already voted for extreme state-mandated rate hikes that studies say will double electric rates for Michigan families and force families to pay $228 more a month for electricity.
“Thanks to Senator Sean McCann, Michigan families are opening their mail and seeing utility bills that look more like a car payment,” said Huizenga spokesman Calvin Moore.
“Families are already struggling to make ends meet with skyrocketing gas, grocery and housing costs, the very last thing they need is Tax Man Sean McCann making their energy bills more expensive too.”
