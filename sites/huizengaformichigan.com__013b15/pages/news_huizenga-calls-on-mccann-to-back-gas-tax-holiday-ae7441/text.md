Donate About Issues News Join Donate News Huizenga Calls on McCann to Back Gas Tax Holiday September 30, 2026 Back to News HOLLAND, MI – Congressman Bill Huizenga today called on Senator Sean McCann to support suspending Michigan’s gas tax after the Detroit News reported the “Michigan House will vote on gas tax pause if Senate sends the bill.” Huizenga has been calling for a suspension of both the federal and state gas taxes since May as Michigan families struggle with high prices at the pump.
Michigan drivers currently pay 52.4 cents per gallon in state gas taxes alone.
Now that there is a clear path to getting relief to Michigan families, the only question is whether Sean McCann will support it.
“Apparently everyone in Michigan can agree on giving drivers a break at the pump except Sean McCann.
I called for suspending the gas tax months ago.
There’s bipartisan support for it today, and the House Speaker is ready to vote.
Michigan families don’t need another roundtable or phony speech from Sean McCann about affordability, they need him to get to Lansing and do his job and suspend the gas tax to bring families relief at the pump right now.” Reminder: Sean McCann voted to raise Michgian’s gas tax to the highest of anyone in the Midwest.
