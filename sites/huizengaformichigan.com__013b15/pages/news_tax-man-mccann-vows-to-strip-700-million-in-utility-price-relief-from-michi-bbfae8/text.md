HOLLAND, MI. – According to WZZM 13, Michigan families will see $700 million in savings on their energy bills thanks to a new program finalized by the Department of Energy this past weekend, courtesy of the One Big Beautiful Bill Act.
But Tax Man Sean McCann promised to repeal the One Big Beautiful Bill as high first priority if he makes it to Congress, meaning his first act of business would be raising energy bills $700 million across the state.
“Tax Man McCann has already said his first priority in Congress would be to raise Michigan electric bills by over $700 million,” said Huizenga campaign spokesman Calvin Moore.
“Tax Man McCann is so extreme that his entire campaign platform is finding new ways to make life even more expensive for working families.”
