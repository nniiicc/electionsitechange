Donate About Issues News Join Donate News ICYMI: Detroit News Endorses Bill Huizenga for Reelection September 22, 2026 Back to News HOLLAND, MI – The Detroit News endorsed Congressman Bill Huizenga for reelection in Michigan’s Fourth Congressional District, pointing to his record of delivering for Michigan, protecting the Great Lakes and working to lower taxes and regulations.
The editorial also pointed to Huizenga being named the most effective member of Michigan’s congressional delegation by the Center for Effective Lawmaking, for his record of bringing people together – of both parties – to get things done for Southwest Michigan.
From The Detroit News: “Huizenga, a conservative, is a strong advocate for free markets and has worked to lower taxes and regulations.
His institutional knowledge and influence are of great benefit to Michigan….
Bill Huizenga should be returned to Congress.
He gets our endorsement.” Read the full endorsement here.
