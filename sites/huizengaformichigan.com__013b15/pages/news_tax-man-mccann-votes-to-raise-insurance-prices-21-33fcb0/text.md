HOLLAND, MI. – Just when Michiganders thought rising prices couldn’t get any more taxing, “Tax Man McCann” voted today to pass reckless legislation that would increase insurance costs for Michigan families by up to 21%!
Today, Sean McCann voted for S.B. 245 which, according to studies, would cost Michigan families and small businesses $2.4 billion to $4.7 billion annually and result in an average 21% spike across auto, home, life insurance and more.
“Today’s vote shows again exactly why people all over Michigan are calling him Tax Man McCann: Sean simply cannot stop himself from voting to make life more expensive for families,” said Calvin Moore, Huizenga campaign spokesman.
“Families are already finding it hard to get by and yet McCann voted to jack up insurance rates for cars, homes and more by 21%.
For families looking to drive down costs, the worst choice they can make is Sean McCann for Congress.”
