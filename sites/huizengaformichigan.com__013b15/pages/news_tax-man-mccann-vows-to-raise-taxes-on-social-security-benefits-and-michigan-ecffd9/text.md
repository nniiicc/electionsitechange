HOLLAND, MI – Bill Huizenga’s campaign released the following statement after Sean McCann vowed to raise taxes $1,320 on Social Security benefits at a town hall yesterday.
Speaking to a crowd and joined by Hillary Scholten and Jamie Raskin, McCann said “we have to reverse it” when talking about the historic tax cuts signed into law last year.
Under McCann’s plan…
- Seniors would lose a $6,000 deduction for their social security benefits, meaning they’d be forced to pay roughly $1,320 more in taxes on the benefits they earned.
- McCann would hit more than just seniors.
The average family would face $3,131 tax increase under his plan, while stripping $173 million from rural healthcare in Michigan.
“Southwest Michigan seniors are struggling already, but ‘Tax Man’ McCann promised to make things even harder by taxing their Social Security benefits and robbing seniors of the benefits they earned,” said Huizenga campaign spokesman Calvin Moore.
“Raising taxes on working families and seniors while ripping $173 million from rural hospitals shows voters exactly why they can’t afford to ’Tax Man’ Sean McCann in Congress.”
