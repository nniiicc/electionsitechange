HOLLAND, MI — Today, Huizenga for Congress announced the launch of two new television advertisements across Michigan’s 4th Congressional District.
The first spot, titled “Gravel,” highlights Huizenga’s background growing up in his family’s third-generation sand and gravel business.
The ad showcases his work to hold Congress accountable through the No Budget, No Pay Act, his ranking as the most effective Representative in Michigan by the Center for Effective Lawmaking, and his bipartisan record of lowering prescription drug costs and helping families keep more of their paychecks.
The second spot, titled “Certain,” highlights ‘Tax Man’ Sean McCann’s record of raising costs for working families while taking care of himself.
The ad details McCann backing higher fees on water and garbage, supporting higher income, property, and sales taxes, and pushing to raise gas taxes and energy bills—all while voting to give himself a 22% pay increase.

“Southwest Michigan families are tired of politicians who make excuses instead of delivering results,” said Calvin Moore, Huizenga campaign spokesman.
“While Bill Huizenga is working across the aisle to help families keep more of their hard-earned paychecks and lower drug costs, Sean McCann’s answer to every problem is higher taxes and fees for working families and fat pay raise for himself.”
