HOLLAND, MI – In a TV ad launched today, Democrat Sean McCann boasts that he has spent his career standing up to “greedy landlords.” But records reviewed by the New York Post reveal McCann let his own rental properties pile up dangerous safety violations and fire hazards while pocketing over $50k a year in rental income.
“Sean McCann is nothing short of a complete and total fraud,” said Calvin Moore, Huizenga campaign spokesman.
“Sean McCann wants to talk tough on TV about taking on ‘greedy landlords’ but city records show he might be one of the greediest in town.
Sean pocketed over $50,000 in rental income while forcing residents to live with dangerous safety issues in his own rental units.
Whether its raising his own pay on the Kalamazoo City Commission or taking advantage of tenants, the only thing Sean McCann cares about is lining his own pockets.”
