HOLLAND, MI – The Huizenga for Congress campaign is wishing Sean McCann a happy birthday today with a song written just for him.
Today, Huizenga for Congress released the country hit “Happy Birthday, Tax Man Sean,” a musical tribute to McCann’s record of higher taxes, higher fees, higher utility costs and a 22% pay increase for himself.
“We wanted to get Sean McCann something special for his birthday, but Michigan taxpayers have already given him enough already.
Between higher taxes and a 22% pay increase, this year Tax Man Sean will have to settle for a song.” – Calvin Moore, Huizenga campaign spokesman.
