About Issues Media Volunteer Donate Together we can uplift working families Jobs & Economy Rising costs for essentials like food and housing are stretching household budgets and making it harder for working families to stay afloat.
Too many people are working full time and struggling to cover the basics.
Cinthia believes that a healthy economy should reward hard work with fair pay that keeps up with the cost of living.
That means standing with workers to fight corporate greed and pushing back against policies that value profits over people.
Cinthia is dedicated to expanding access to stable, good-paying jobs for Nevadans and ensuring our state remains a place where families can build a prosperous future.
Housing/Rental Cost Nevadans are struggling to make rent and many are at risk of displacement or eviction due to rising housing costs.
Cinthia believes that everyone deserves a safe, stable place to live.
She knows that our communities are stronger when housing is accessible and affordable for people at all income levels.
That’s why Cinthia is committed to addressing Nevada’s housing crisis by lowering costs, strengthening renter protections, and holding greedy corporate landlords accountable.
Health Care Access to quality healthcare should not depend on income, zip code, or background.
Nevada families deserve care that is affordable, timely, and reliable.
Strengthening Nevada’s healthcare system means expanding access and improving quality.
Cinthia is fighting for affordable healthcare so that Nevadans are never forced to choose between medical care and paying for basic necessities.
Public Safety Safe communities are the foundation of a strong and thriving Nevada.
Public safety requires collaboration, trust, and adequate resources for those who serve on the front lines.
For Cinthia, this means investing in training, staffing, and equipment for first responders to ensure they can do their jobs effectively while building stronger relationships with the communities they serve.
Education Every child deserves a high-quality education, no matter where they live or their family’s circumstances.
Strengthening Nevada’s school means supporting educators, reducing overcrowded classrooms, and ensuring students have access to the resources they need to succeed.
At the legislature, Cinthia will fight for investments in teacher pay, student support services, and nutrition programs to create learning environments where students can focus, grow, and reach their full potential.
Gun Violence Preventing gun violence saves lives and helps create safer neighborhoods for everyone.
Cinthia knows it is possible to respect constitutional rights while also implementing common-sense measures that improve public safety.
Cinthia is sharply focused on passing policies like comprehensive background checks that keep firearms out of the hands of those who pose a danger to others.
Reproductive Rights Cinthia firmly believes that healthcare decisions should be made by individuals, not politicians.
Protecting reproductive freedom means safeguarding access to abortion, contraception, and fertility care without stigma or interference.
Comprehensive reproductive healthcare is essential and ensuring these services remain accessible to women and families in Nevada is top priority for Cinthia.
Environment Everyone deserves clean air to breathe and safe water to drink, regardless of where they live.
For Cinthia, achieving a clean and healthy environment means taking decisive action to reduce pollution, protect natural resources, and prioritize investments in clean energy.
By focusing on sustainability and environmental responsibility, Nevada can protect its communities while building a stronger future for generations to come.
