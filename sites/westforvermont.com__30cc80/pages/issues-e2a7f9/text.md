That means protecting what works, fixing what doesn’t, and judging every decision by a simple question: How does this make life better for Vermonters?
My priorities are rooted in quality of life — affordable homes, strong schools, healthy communities, good jobs, safe neighborhoods, responsive government, and a Vermont that remains a place people can choose to call home for generations to come.
Housing Housing won’t solve every challenge Vermont faces, but it has to be part of almost every solution.
We need more homes of different sizes and types, built in places where communities can support growth and where Vermonters can actually afford to live.
Learn More Cost of Living A good life in Vermont should be affordable.
We need to take property taxes and everyday household costs seriously, be more disciplined about government spending, and stop solving every challenge by simply passing the cost onto families.
Learn More Rural Vermont Rural Vermont should be a place where people can build a future, not just remember a past.
State policy should recognize the realities of small towns and give rural communities the resources and authority they need to shape their own future.
Learn More Environment & Climate Protecting Vermont means protecting both the place we love and the people who live here.
We can reduce pollution, prepare for climate impacts, protect our working lands and natural resources, and still make sure environmental policy respects affordability and local decision-making.
Learn More Education Every Vermont child deserves a great education, no matter their ZIP code.
We should improve affordability without weakening our schools, protect and strengthen school choice and town tuitioning, and make sure communities have a real voice in the future of local education.
Learn More Healthy Communities Strong communities don’t just respond to crises.
They help prevent them.
Vermonters should be able to access primary care, mental health care, substance-use treatment, prevention, and community support close to home.
Learn More Good Government & Democracy Government should be open, understandable, and accountable to the people it serves.
That means transparency, strong ethics, respect for privacy and civil liberties, meaningful local input, and a willingness to fix what isn’t working.
Learn More Public Safety Safe communities require both strong emergency response and strong prevention.
We should support effective police, fire, and EMS services while addressing the mental health, substance-use, and community challenges that can prevent crises before they happen.
