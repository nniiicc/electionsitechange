Thomas West Named a 2026 Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate
Updated: Jul 31
MANCHESTER, VT — July 28, 2026 — Thomas West, Democratic candidate for Vermont House representing the Bennington-4 district, has been recognized as a 2026 Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate.
The distinction is awarded to candidates who demonstrate a commitment to promoting evidence-based policies that help reduce gun violence while supporting safer communities.
“I’m honored to be recognized as a 2026 Gun Sense Candidate by Moms Demand Action,” said West.
“Keeping our communities safe is one of the most important responsibilities of public service.
Vermonters deserve thoughtful, common-sense approaches that help prevent gun violence, protect our children, support responsible gun ownership, and respect the traditions that are important to many families across our state.”
As a father of four, Army veteran, and community leader, West believes public safety requires collaboration, prevention, and policies that are grounded in evidence and focused on saving lives.
About Thomas West
Thomas West is an Army veteran, husband, father, and community leader running for Vermont State Representative.
He serves on the Manchester Planning Commission, as a Manchester Justice of the Peace, and on the Southwest Vermont Regional Technical School District Board.
He lives in Manchester with his husband and their four children.
About moms demand action
Moms Demand Action is a grassroots movement working to end gun violence through education, advocacy, and the promotion of evidence-based public safety policies.
