About The Event
Join Thomas West and Rob Hunter for the Bennington-4 State Representative Candidate Forum on Monday, October 5.
Thomas and Rob will take part alongside the other candidates for the district in a nonpartisan discussion about the issues affecting Arlington, Manchester, Sandgate, and Sunderland and the work ahead in the next legislative session.
The forum is presented by GNAT-TV in collaboration with The Manchester Journal and Manchester Community Library.
