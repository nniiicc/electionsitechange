Organization Endorsements
Proud to have the support of organizations that share our commitment to Vermont families, service, and community.
Planned Parenthood Vermont Action Fund IE
Planned Parenthood Vermont Action Fund IE PAC is a nonpartisan organization that endorses candidates like Thomas who are committed to protecting reproductive freedom, expanding access to affordable health care, advancing LGBTQIA+ equality, and defending the right of every Vermonter to make their own health care decisions.
VPIRG
VPIRG Votes’ mission is to support and elect public interest champions who will promote and protect the health of Vermont’s people, environment, and locally-based economy.
Vermont Conservation Voters
Vermont Conservation Voters is a non-partisan organization that endorses candidates committed to protecting Vermont’s environment, public health, and communities while advancing justice and strengthening democracy.
For new candidates, VCV’s endorsement process includes a detailed questionnaire examining their environmental priorities, values, and positions on issues facing Vermont.
Vermont NEA
Vermont-NEA, the union representing thousands of educators across Vermont, has recommended Thomas West for State Representative.
Vermont-NEA recommends candidates based on their positions on public education, support for educators and students, and issues affecting working families and Vermont communities.
VoteVets
VoteVets is proud to support veterans like Thomas who are stepping up to continue their service beyond the uniform.
At a time when our communities and our democracy face real challenges, veterans bring proven leadership, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to the Constitution and the country.
Moms Demand Action
Thomas West has been recognized as a Moms Demand Action Gun Sense Candidate, a nonpartisan distinction awarded to candidates who have demonstrated a commitment to supporting common-sense gun safety policies and safer communities.
This recognition is not an endorsement, but it signals that Thomas will govern with gun safety in mind if elected.
