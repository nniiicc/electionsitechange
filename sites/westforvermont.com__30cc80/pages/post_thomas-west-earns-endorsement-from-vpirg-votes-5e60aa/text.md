VPIRG Votes supports candidates who demonstrate a commitment to building a more affordable, sustainable, and equitable Vermont through practical public policy and community-focused leadership.
“I’m honored to receive the endorsement of VPIRG Votes,” said West.
“Our campaign is focused on making Vermont the best place in America to raise a family.
That means tackling the housing shortage, lowering costs for working families, strengthening public education, protecting our environment, and ensuring every community has the opportunity to thrive.
I’m grateful for VPIRG Votes’ confidence in our campaign and look forward to working together to move Vermont forward.” West’s campaign has centered on addressing the affordability crisis facing Vermont families by increasing access to housing, supporting strong public schools, expanding economic opportunity, and preserving the natural resources that make Vermont such a special place to call home.
About Thomas West Thomas West is an Army veteran, husband, father, and community leader running for Vermont State Representative.
He serves on the Manchester Planning Commission, as a Manchester Justice of the Peace, and on the Southwest Vermont Regional Technical School District Board.
He lives in Manchester with his husband and their four children.
