Matthew Mobilia, seasoned trial lawyer and local business owner, with a deep-rooted commitment to Staten Island, is running for public office to bring common-sense leadership to his community.
His campaign focuses on four core issues that reflect the needs and values of the residents.
First, Mobilia is dedicated to ensuring safe communities through stronger community policing, better training for law enforcement, and collaborative initiatives with community leaders.
His comprehensive approach aims to create neighborhoods where everyone feels secure.
Boosting local economies is another key focus of Mobilia's campaign.
Recognizing that small businesses are the backbone of the community, he will advocate for policies that provide financial support, reduce unnecessary regulations, and create a thriving environment for local entrepreneurs.
By revitalizing the local economy, Mobilia aims to ensure prosperity for all residents.
Additionally, he believes in investing in local infrastructure, enhancing public services, and supporting educational initiatives to build a neighborhood that offers opportunities and a high quality of life for every resident.
Mobilia is also a staunch advocate for civil and human rights, fighting against discrimination and injustice.
He promotes equality, ensures fair treatment, and defends the rights of all individuals to build a more inclusive and just community.
His campaign slogan, "Common Sense Leadership," embodies his practical and transparent approach to governance, always prioritizing the community's best interests.
Mobilia believes in the importance of working across the aisle and reaching compromises on legislation and ideas to make the community better.
Join Matthew Mobilia in his journey to make Staten Island a safer, more prosperous, and more equitable place for all.
