Privacy Policy
Last updated September 21, 2026
Friends of Anna Scharf (“Friends of Anna Scharf,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, how and why we use it, circumstances in which it may be shared, and the choices you may have regarding your information.
This Privacy Policy applies when you visit voteannascharf.com, sign up for campaign communications, communicate with Friends of Anna Scharf, make a contribution, volunteer, attend campaign events, or otherwise interact with the campaign.
1.
Information We Collect
The information we collect depends on how you interact with Friends of Anna Scharf.
Information You Provide to Us
We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:
- your name;
- email address;
- telephone or mobile number;
- mailing address;
- communication preferences;
- volunteer or event information;
- information included in messages, forms, surveys, or other communications you submit to us;
- contribution and transaction information; and
- other information you choose to provide to the campaign.
When required by campaign finance law, we may also collect information necessary to satisfy campaign finance reporting and recordkeeping requirements, such as occupation, employer, contribution information, and/or other legally required information.
We may receive information when you interact with us through other campaign activities or services, including campaign events, volunteer activities, contribution platforms, social media, voter-contact activities, or other lawful sources.
Information Collected Automatically
When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically by the website or services used to operate it.
Depending on the technology in use, this may include:
- Internet Protocol (IP) address;
- browser and device type;
- operating system;
- referring website or page;
- pages viewed and links selected;
- approximate geographic information derived from an IP address;
- dates and times of website visits; and
- other diagnostic, security, or website usage information.
This information may be collected through server logs, cookies, analytics tools, pixels, or similar technologies.
2.
How We Use Information
Friends of Anna Scharf may use personal information to:
- communicate with supporters, volunteers, voters, constituents, and members of the public;
- provide campaign, candidate, policy, election, and event information;
- send campaign emails or text messages when permitted;
- recruit and coordinate volunteers;
- organize events and campaign activities;
- encourage voter participation and civic engagement;
- respond to questions, requests, and other communications;
- process and maintain records concerning political contributions;
- comply with campaign finance, election, tax, accounting, and other legal obligations;
- maintain and improve our website and communications;
- understand how people interact with our website and campaign;
- protect the security and integrity of our website, communications, records, and systems;
- prevent fraud, misuse, or other unlawful activity; and
- carry out other lawful activities related to the campaign and political committee.
We may also use information for another purpose with your consent or when otherwise permitted by law.
3.
Text Messaging and Mobile Information
If you affirmatively opt in to receive text messages from Friends of Anna Scharf, we may send campaign updates, event information, volunteer opportunities, get-out-the-vote reminders, election information, fundraising communications, and other campaign-related messages.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time.
Reply HELP for help.
Consent to receive text messages is voluntary and is not a condition of making a contribution, volunteering, or otherwise supporting Friends of Anna Scharf.
No mobile information will be shared by us with third parties/affiliates for marketing/promotional purposes.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
We may use service providers, including Rumble and telecommunications providers, to deliver and support our text messaging program.
These providers may process information as necessary to provide services on behalf of Friends of Anna Scharf; however, text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared for third-party marketing or promotional purposes.
You may withdraw your consent to receive text messages at any time by replying STOP.
4.
Email Communications
If you provide your email address or subscribe to campaign emails, Friends of Anna Scharf may send you campaign news, election information, event announcements, volunteer opportunities, fundraising communications, and other campaign-related information.
You may unsubscribe from campaign marketing emails using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in those communications.
Even after you unsubscribe from promotional communications, we may retain information when reasonably necessary for recordkeeping, compliance, honoring your communication preferences, or other lawful purposes.
5.
Cookies, Analytics, and Similar Technologies
Our website is hosted and operated using Squarespace and may use cookies and similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, security, performance, and understanding how visitors use the site.
These technologies may collect information such as your IP address, browser or device type, pages visited, referring website, and the date and time of your visit.
Our website also uses services such as reCAPTCHA to help detect and prevent spam, fraud, and automated abuse.
Third-party services incorporated into the website may use their own cookies or similar technologies.
Information collected directly by those services is governed by their respective privacy practices.
Most browsers allow you to limit, block, or delete cookies through your browser settings.
Disabling certain cookies may affect how some portions of the website function.
6.
When and With Whom We Share Information
Friends of Anna Scharf may disclose personal information when reasonably necessary to operate the campaign or carry out lawful campaign activities.
This may include sharing information with categories of recipients such as:
- website hosting and technology providers;
- email and communications platforms;
- campaign management and voter-contact services;
- consultants and contractors working on behalf of the campaign;
- analytics and security providers;
- contribution and payment processors;
- accounting, compliance, and professional service providers;
- governmental agencies when required for campaign finance or other legal reporting; and
- other service providers performing functions on behalf of Friends of Anna Scharf.
We may also disclose information when required or permitted by law, regulation, subpoena, court order, governmental request, campaign finance reporting requirement, or other legal process, or when reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property, safety, or security of Friends of Anna Scharf or others.
Friends of Anna Scharf does not routinely sell or provide personal information to third parties for their independent commercial marketing or promotional purposes.
We may, however, transfer, exchange, or otherwise disclose certain campaign-related information in connection with legitimate campaign or political activities, the winding down or reorganization of the committee, satisfaction of campaign obligations, or another lawful purpose, subject to applicable law and any privacy choices or rights available to you.
The provisions above do not alter the additional protections applicable to mobile information and SMS consent described in Section 3.
SMS opt-in information and consent will not be sold or transferred with other campaign data for third-party marketing or promotional purposes.
7.
Contributions and Campaign Finance Disclosure
Political committees are subject to campaign finance laws that may require Friends of Anna Scharf to collect, maintain, and publicly report certain information about contributors and contributions.
Information that Friends of Anna Scharf is legally required to report may become part of a public record and cannot necessarily be deleted, withheld, or kept confidential at your request.
Online contributions are processed through Anedot, a third-party contribution and payment-processing service.
Information you provide when making a contribution may therefore be collected and processed by Anedot in accordance with its own privacy practices.
Friends of Anna Scharf does not receive or retain complete payment-card information processed by Anedot.
8.
Data Retention
We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected or subsequently lawfully used.
The appropriate retention period may depend on the nature of the information, our continuing relationship with you, campaign operational needs, legal and regulatory requirements, campaign finance recordkeeping requirements, security considerations, and our need to establish or defend legal rights.
When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete, anonymize, aggregate, or otherwise securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.
Information may remain for a period of time in backups, archives, compliance records, suppression lists, or other systems where immediate deletion is impractical or where continued retention is necessary to comply with law or honor your preferences.
9.
How We Protect Information
Friends of Anna Scharf takes reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures intended to protect personal information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.
No website, database, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Accordingly, we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or maintained by us.
10.
Your Choices and Privacy Rights
You may contact Friends of Anna Scharf to ask questions about personal information we maintain about you or to request that we correct, update, or delete information.
Depending on applicable law and the circumstances of the request, you may have legal rights concerning access to, correction of, deletion of, or use of your personal information, as well as rights to opt out of certain uses, transfers, sales, targeted advertising, or profiling.
We will consider and respond to privacy requests in accordance with applicable law.
Some requests may be subject to exceptions, including where retention or disclosure is required by campaign finance, election, tax, accounting, recordkeeping, security, or other laws.
We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity before providing access to personal information or acting on certain requests.
Any information collected for verification will be used for that purpose and handled appropriately.
You may also:
- unsubscribe from campaign marketing emails using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in the message;
- stop campaign text messages by replying STOP;
- adjust your browser settings to limit or delete cookies; and
- contact us regarding other communication or privacy preferences.
Where applicable law requires us to recognize a legally valid universal opt-out preference signal, we will honor that signal as required by law.
11.
Children's Privacy
Friends of Anna Scharf's website and campaign communications are intended for voters, and are not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly use the website to solicit personal information from children under 13.
We will handle information concerning minors in accordance with applicable law, including any restrictions governing the sale, targeted advertising, profiling, or processing of children's personal information.
