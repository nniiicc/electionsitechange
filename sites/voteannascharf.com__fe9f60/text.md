REPRESENTING YOU
Hi, I'm Anna Scharf.
I’m your neighbor, a local farmer, an involved parent, and your State Representative.
I have deep roots in our district, and like you, I care about making it a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
From serving on the Perrydale School Board and budget committee, enjoying many years on the Polk County Fair board, being an active member of the Polk County Farm Bureau, and a Trustee on the OSU 4-H Foundation, I have a history of showing up and doing the work that is needed to be done to make a difference in our community.
I have worked in the legislature doing policy and unpaid advocacy work, and now as your State Representative.
I have seen first-hand the dysfunction of polarizing partisanship.
I want you to know that I am committed to serving you, and the whole of House District 23.
I believe our community is served best when we have a seat at the table and work together to find good solutions.
You have my word that I will listen to you, show up for you, and carry your voice to Salem.
If we haven’t met, I am looking forward to seeing you at one of the many community events throughout the year.
In the meantime, if you have any questions, concerns, or ideas, please do not hesitate to reach out to my office.
I am here for you, and I hope you will be there for me.
Please take a moment to sign up for my newsletter and follow me on Facebook so we can stay in touch.
