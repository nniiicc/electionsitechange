For the Women Who Hold Everything Together
Tiffany Alston is a mother, a wife, an attorney, a community servant, and the only Black woman running for State Senate in Prince George's County.
She has delivered real results for the women and families of Maryland.
She will continue delivering in the Senate.
THE SEAT IS OPEN.
THE MOMENT IS NOW.
In 1990, Gloria Lawlah made history as the first woman, Black or white, elected to the Maryland State Senate from Prince George's County.
She broke that barrier so that women in our community would never again be without a voice in Annapolis.
Senator Joanne Benson has carried that torch.
Fighting for the women, families, and seniors of our region with grace and determination.
With her departure, that voice — a woman's voice, a Black woman's voice — is at risk of going silent.
I am the only woman in this race.
And I refuse to let that happen.
If we want to ensure that Prince George's County continues to have a woman fighting for women's issues in Annapolis (healthcare, childcare, housing, safety, education), then the work starts right now, and I need your help.
Gloria Lawlah opened the door.
Joanne Benson held it open.
It is our job, together, to make sure it never closes.
This isn't just about electing a candidate.
It is about protecting a seat at the table that women fought too hard, for too long, to ever give up.
