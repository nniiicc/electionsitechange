Peña Lauren's Plan to Protecting Children 1.
Champion the SAFE NIGHTS Act Lauren's signature legislation targets the trafficking networks operating along I-35 and through Austin's communities — increasing penalties, expanding victim services, and funding law enforcement task forces specifically focused on child trafficking.
2.
Restore parental rights in education Parents have the right to know what their children are being taught and what medical decisions are being made on their behalf.
Lauren will vote for every federal parental rights bill and oppose any policy that excludes parents from their children's lives.
3.
Secure the border to stop trafficking An open border is a trafficker's best friend.
Lauren will vote to fully fund border security, complete the border wall, and end the catch-and-release policies that allow traffickers to operate with impunity.
4.
Increase penalties for child predators Lauren will push for mandatory minimum sentences for child trafficking, child exploitation, and child abuse — and oppose any effort to reduce sentences or eliminate accountability for those who harm children.
5.
Fund survivor support programs Survivors of trafficking and abuse need real support — housing, counseling, job training, and legal assistance.
Lauren will fight for federal funding for survivor services that actually work, not bureaucratic programs that fail the people they're supposed to help.
