Peña is the Republican nominee for the United States House of Representatives in Texas Congressional District 37.
She won the Republican primary run-off on May 26, 2026, and faces Democrat Greg Casar in the November 3, 2026 general election.
A lifelong Texan who grew up in Austin since childhood, Lauren is a human trafficking survivor, mother of six, and constitutional conservative who has witnessed firsthand how left-wing governance destroys communities.
Her campaign slogan — "Street to the Hill" — reflects her journey from public housing and trafficking survival to running for the United States Congress.
Lauren has proposed two pieces of federal legislation: the SAFE NIGHTS Act , which targets human trafficking in nightlife and hospitality venues, and the SODA Act , which eliminates congressional immunity for sexual misconduct.
Both bills are drawn directly from her personal experience as a survivor.
She is running on a platform of border security, lower energy costs, parental rights, medical freedom, veterans' support, and government accountability — including demanding the release of the Epstein files and exposing Congressional sexual misconduct.
