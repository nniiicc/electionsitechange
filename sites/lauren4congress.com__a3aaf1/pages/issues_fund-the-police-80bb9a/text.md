In 2020, then-Austin City Councilmember Greg Casar championed the "People's Budget" — a plan that cut the Austin Police Department's budget by $150 million, eliminated 150 officer positions, and redirected funds to social programs.
The result was predictable: violent crime surged, response times increased, and Austin's reputation as a safe city was shattered.
By 2022, Austin's homicide rate had reached a 30-year high.
Car thefts, burglaries, and assaults all increased significantly.
The officers who remained on the force were overworked, demoralized, and leaving for other departments.
Austin went from a city where families felt safe to a city where families were asking whether to stay.
Casar has never apologized for this.
He has never acknowledged the harm his policies caused.
Instead, he took his defund-the-police ideology to Congress, where he has voted against every measure to support law enforcement and secure the border.
