Meet Zak
Working for Warwick
Zak Pereira was born and raised in a working-class Rhode Island family that taught him the value of being present with family and working hard.
Watching his mother struggle to navigate the healthcare system showed him how difficult it has become for people to access the services they should be able to count on.
The same belief in access guides his work at the University of Rhode Island where he helps military veterans earn college credit for the skills and experience gained serving our country.
Zak is running to represent House District 22 to keep Warwick safe, make life more affordable, and ensure every family has access to the opportunities and services they should be able to count on.

Key Issues
Affordability
No one should be forced out of our community because they cannot find affordable housing, timely medical care or keep up with rising costs.
Working families and retirees should be able to afford to buy a home, pay their bills, and build a future here in Warwick.
Safety
Warwick is one of the safest communities in the country, and I want to keep it that way.
That means supporting our police officers, firefighters, and first responders, and making smart investments in public safety infrastructure.
It also means ensuring that the places that make our community special, from Conimicut Point to Oakland Beach, remain safe for families to enjoy for generations to come.
Opportunity
Every child deserves access to a high-quality public education, with pathways to success through college or career and technical education.
Veterans deserve meaningful economic opportunities after
their service.
Opportunity shouldn't depend on who you know or where you were born.
