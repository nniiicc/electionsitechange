Get Involved
I’m not about slogans or spectacle.
I’m about people—neighbors who care about their communities and are willing to show up for one another.
The work is done best when it stays local and when people are involved in ways that make sense for their lives.
Volunteer
If you’re interested in helping, there are practical ways to get involved:
- Talking with neighbors
- Helping at local events
- Staying connected and sharing information
Every effort helps keep this work grounded in the community.

Get a Yard Sign
If you’d like to show your support with a yard sign, let us know.
It’s a simple way to help spread the word locally.
You can access them by connecting with me or let us know your address below and we will try to get one to you soon!
Additional Support
My goal is to serve as a public servant and represent District 16 with care and integrity.
I rely on neighbors and community members to help share my values and let others know where I stand.
If you’d like to help financially, I welcome you to reach out directly.
Most importantly, I would appreciate your vote and your support in sharing my message.
