On the Issues
Serving the people of District 16 and South Dakota means listening carefully and staying focused on what affects everyday life here at home.
The responsibility is to represent the people who live and work in these communities, not outside interests or political agendas.
Where I Stand
My approach to the issues comes from a lifetime of faith and service—in uniform, in agriculture, as a coach, and in the community.
01
Agriculture
Agriculture is the backbone of our communities.
I support:
- Protecting family farms and ranches
- Supporting the next generation of producers
- Reducing unnecessary regulations
- Strong support for property rights and local control
Strong agriculture helps keep rural communities strong.
02
Veterans & First Responders
Those who serve deserve practical support.
I work to:
- Improve access to veteran services
- Support first responders and their families
- Ensure programs are effective and accountable
03
Families & Communities
Strong families and involved communities keep South Dakota strong.
That means:
- Supporting youth programs and schools
- Respecting local values
- Keeping decision-making close to home
