I’ve always been drawn to serving my community and my country.
Over the years, that service has taken many forms—military service, agriculture boards, coaching youth, community involvement, and now public office.
My faith, family, and community have shaped my belief that service means putting others first and doing what’s right, even when it’s not easy. ​ I’m running to bring service-driven, common-sense leadership to Pierre and to stay accountable to the people I represent.
