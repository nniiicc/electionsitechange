Regina English Dr.
Regina English was born and raised in Battle Creek, Michigan, and is a dedicated advocate, wife, mother of five, grandmother of 12, and founder of YES MAAM, a scholarship pageant system for African American youth.
As the first Black woman to represent El Paso County in the Colorado General Assembly for House District 17, she champions educational equity, criminal justice reform, mental health awareness, and environmental sustainability.
Her diverse accomplishments include founding the Be You-Be U nonprofit and serving as President of the Colorado Alliance of Black School Educators, while remaining deeply committed to empowering her community and fostering positive change.​ Learn More We have so much work to do at the capitol.
