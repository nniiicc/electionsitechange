Criminal Justice Reform & Public Safety
Criminal Justice Reform
I am committed to rigorously addressing and dismantling ineffective systems within the incarceration framework, advocating for the humane treatment of individuals in custody.
My goal is to ensure that all incarcerated persons are treated with dignity and respect, and are not subjected to conditions that undermine their health and well-being.
Additionally, I will work to eliminate punitive practices that penalize individuals for their refusal to engage in exploitative labor, ensuring that both men and women can maintain meaningful connections with their loved ones without facing unjust consequences.
Public Safety
I am dedicated to advancing public safety through the enhancement of road infrastructure and travel experiences.
I will advocate for comprehensive measures to ensure that our roads are safe and well-maintained, reducing accidents and promoting efficient travel.
Additionally, I am committed to fostering secure neighborhoods by supporting initiatives that enhance community safety and address local concerns effectively.
My focus is on creating environments where all residents can travel and live with confidence and peace of mind.
