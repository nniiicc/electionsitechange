Colorado Leaders TRUST Dr.
English!
These respected leaders and organizations are endorsing Dr.
Regina English for re-election to the Colorado House of Representatives because of her proven leadership, dedication to educational equity, and commitment to uplifting communities.
Dr.
English has been a powerful voice in advocating for criminal justice reform, mental health awareness, and policies that support families and students.
With her deep roots in the community and relentless focus on creating opportunities for all, these endorsements highlight her track record of delivering positive change and her vision for a brighter future for Colorado.
