Reproductive Rights Reproductive Rights I firmly believe in the fundamental right of women to have autonomy over their own bodies.
Reproductive rights are a crucial aspect of this autonomy, and government should not interfere with personal decisions regarding reproductive health.
I am committed to advocating for policies that ensure women have the freedom to make their own choices without legislative interference, safeguarding their right to make decisions about their bodies with dignity and respect.
