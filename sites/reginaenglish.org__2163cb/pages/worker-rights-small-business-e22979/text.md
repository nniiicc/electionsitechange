Workers Rights & Small Business Workers Rights I am committed to supporting and advancing workers' rights, including the vital role of labor unions.
I will advocate for fair labor practices, safe working conditions, and equitable compensation to ensure that all workers are treated with dignity and respect.
My focus includes bolstering support for unions as essential advocates for workers' interests, strengthening their ability to negotiate effectively and protect their members' rights.
Through these efforts, I aim to promote a work environment that supports both individual employees and collective labor movements.
Small Business I am dedicated to supporting small businesses, which are vital to our local economy.
I will focus on creating opportunities for growth by simplifying regulations, increasing access to capital, and providing targeted relief.
Small businesses face unique challenges, and I am committed to addressing these with tailored solutions that foster their success.
By removing barriers and offering the necessary support, we can ensure that our small businesses not only survive but thrive, driving economic prosperity for our community.
Together, we can build a stronger, more resilient local economy.
