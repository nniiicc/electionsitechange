Education & Mental Health Education I am committed to ensuring that every student receives a high-quality education, with schools that are safe, well-funded, and equipped to provide all students with the opportunity to succeed.
By advocating for robust funding, we can support essential resources and programs that enhance learning environments.
Ensuring safety in schools is paramount to fostering an atmosphere where students can thrive without fear.
My dedication is to guarantee that every child has access to the tools, support, and opportunities necessary for their success, paving the way for a brighter future for all students no matter their color or zip code.
Mental Health I am committed to prioritizing mental health by advocating for enhanced access to and opportunities for comprehensive care.
This includes addressing mental health needs within the criminal justice system to ensure humane treatment and support for incarcerated individuals.
Additionally, I will work to integrate mental health resources in schools and make programming accessible for our youth population, ensuring that students receive the necessary support to thrive academically and emotionally.
By focusing on these areas, I aim to foster a system that provides equitable mental health care and supports well-being across all sectors.
