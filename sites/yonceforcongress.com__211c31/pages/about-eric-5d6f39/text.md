Meet Eric Yonce.
A working-class, people-first voice for Florida's Sixth District.
A lifetime in our community Rooted here.
Ready to serve.
Eric was born in Florida, raised in the Sixth District, and has always called this community home.
Growing up in Citra and graduating from North Marion High School shaped his connection to the people and places of the district.
His father served as an Ocala police officer, while his mother leads the local Realtor Association and works to make homeownership more accessible.
Their example taught Eric the importance of hard work, public service, and standing up for neighbors.
Appalachian Trail thru-hike · Class of 2022 Built for the long haul 2,000+ miles.
One enduring purpose.
“In 2022, I completed a 2,000-plus-mile thru-hike of the Appalachian Trail.
That journey tested my endurance and reinforced my belief in perseverance, purpose, and resilience.
That same mindset led me to start my own small business renovating vintage Airstream travel trailers.” — Eric Yonce 01 Protecting Florida Eric has seen responsible, profit-driven development threaten the coastlines, natural landscapes, and wildlife that make Florida special.
He believes growth must respect the communities and resources already here.
02 Working-family perspective After more than twenty years managing big-box retail stores across the district, Eric understands the pressures facing employees, customers, and families working hard to get ahead.
03 Small-business perspective That same determination led Eric to start a small business renovating vintage Airstream travel trailers.
He understands what it means to build something, solve problems, and be accountable for the result.
Showing up for the communities that make Florida's Sixth strong.
