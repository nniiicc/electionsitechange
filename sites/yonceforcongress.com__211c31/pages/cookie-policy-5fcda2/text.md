What Cookies Are Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website.
They can help websites function, remember choices, understand how visitors use pages, and measure communications.
How We Use Cookies Essential Cookies Essential technologies support basic site operation, security, accessibility, and your cookie preference.
These cannot be disabled through our cookie banner because the site may not function correctly without them.
Analytics and Performance If analytics tools are enabled, they may help us understand aggregate site traffic, page performance, and campaign communications.
Optional analytics will only be enabled after you select “Accept all.” The current site does not load optional analytics tools.
Third-Party Services Links to services such as ActBlue, volunteer forms, and social media platforms lead to third-party websites.
Those services may use their own cookies under their own policies.
Your choices on this site do not control cookies placed directly by third parties after you visit them.
