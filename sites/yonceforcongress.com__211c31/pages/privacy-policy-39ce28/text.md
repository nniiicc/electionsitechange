Last Updated: October 9, 2023 This Privacy Policy explains how Eric Yonce for U.S.
House of Representatives (“Committee,” “we,” or “us”) collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you access or use our websites, mobile sites, and mobile application that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Site”).
You can contact us by emailing Eric@yonceforcongress.com .
We may change this Privacy Policy from time to time.
If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the date at the top of the policy and, in some cases, we may provide you with additional notice.
We encourage you to review the Privacy Policy whenever you access the Site or otherwise interact with us.
Collection of Information Information You Provide to Us We collect information you provide directly to us.
For example, we collect information when you sign up to receive email or SMS updates, request information, fill out a form, sign a petition, sign up as a volunteer, sign up for an event, make a donation or purchase, send us an email, or otherwise communicate with us.
The types of information we may collect include your name, contact information (such as email address, postal address, and phone number), credit card and other payment information (although payment information may be collected directly by a third-party processor), and any other information you choose to provide.
In addition, we may be required to collect certain personal information from donors.
For example, the disclosure of name, mailing address, occupation, and employer of all individuals whose donations to the Committee exceed $# per election cycle may be required.
Automatically Collected Information When you access or use our Site, we automatically collect information about you, including: Log and Usage Information: Information related to your access to and use of the Site, including browser type, app version, access times, pages viewed, IP address, and the page visited before navigating to our Site.
Device Information: Information about the computer or mobile device you use, including hardware model, operating system and version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
Transactional Information: If you make a donation or purchase, information about the transaction, such as donation amount, purchase price, product details, and date and location of the transaction.
Location Information: We may derive the approximate location of your device, such as from your IP address.
Information We Collect from Other Sources We and our service providers may obtain information about you from other sources and combine it with information collected on our Site.
These sources may include mailing list providers, publicly available sources, and third parties such as ActBlue when you make a donation for our benefit.
Use of Information We may use the information we collect to: Provide, maintain, and improve our Site; Provide requested information, process donations and transactions, and send related information; Send technical notices, updates, security alerts, support, and administrative messages; Respond to communications, request feedback or support, and contact you about participation with the Committee; Communicate about issues, events, activities, volunteer opportunities, donations, and support for the Committee; Provide news, remind you to vote and register to vote, and assist with registration information and polling locations; Comply with legal and financial obligations, including Federal election law requirements; Monitor and analyze trends, personalize the Site, and provide content or advertising based on preferences and activity; Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent transactions and other illegal activities; and Carry out any other purpose described when the information was collected.
Campaign and Donation Text Messages Sign up online or via mobile to receive campaign and donation messages from our campaign.
Message and data rates may apply.
Message frequency varies.
Unsubscribe at any time by replying STOP .
We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, except as required by law.
For program support, email us using the contact information at the top of this document.
Email is not an acceptable method of opting out of the program.
Sharing of Information We may share information about you: With vendors, service providers, volunteers, and consultants who need access to carry out work on our behalf; To report donation information required in public disclosures, including information about individuals whose contributions exceed $# in an election cycle; In response to lawful requests or as required by applicable law or legal process; When reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful claims, subpoenas, warrants, or court orders; To protect the rights, property, and safety of the Committee, its employees, volunteers, constituents, or others; In connection with a reorganization, successor organization, asset transfer, financing, or similar transaction; and With your consent or at your direction.
We may also share aggregated or de-identified information that cannot reasonably be used to identify you.
Links to Other Websites The Site may contain links to third-party websites or provide access to tools and features powered by third-party vendors.
Any personal information you provide through those services is provided directly to that third party and is subject to that third party’s privacy policy, not this Privacy Policy.
Online Petitions If you sign an online petition, you understand that the petition is public information and that we may make the petition and your name, city, state, and comments publicly available.
We may also provide petitions or compilations of them to national, state, or local leaders or to the press.
Social Sharing Features The Site may offer social sharing features and integrated tools.
Your use of these features enables sharing with your friends or the public depending on your privacy settings for the relevant social media site.
Advertising and Analytics Services Provided by Others This information may be used to analyze and track data, determine the popularity of content, provide measurement services, deliver targeted advertising and content, and better understand online activity.
We may use tools such as Google Analytics and Facebook Pixel.
You may review interest-based advertising choices at aboutads.info/choices .
Transfer of Information to the U.S.
The Committee is based in the United States, is directed to U.S. residents, and processes and stores information in the U.S.
If you are located outside the U.S., we and our service providers may transfer, store, or access your information in jurisdictions that may not provide equivalent levels of data protection as your home jurisdiction.
Your Choices Cookies Promotional Communications You may opt out of promotional emails from the Committee by following the instructions in those emails.
If you opt out, we may still send non-promotional emails, such as those about your account or our ongoing business relations.
Mobile Push Notifications and Alerts With your consent, we may send promotional and non-promotional push notifications or alerts to your mobile device.
You can deactivate these messages at any time by changing the notification settings on your device.
