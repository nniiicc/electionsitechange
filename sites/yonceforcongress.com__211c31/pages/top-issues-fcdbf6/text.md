Practical solutions rooted in freedom, fairness, and a stronger future for Florida’s Sixth District.
Eric's priorities Real challenges.
Clear positions.
Eric brings a working-class, people-first perspective to Congress.
His priorities focus on lowering pressure on families, protecting personal freedom, investing in our communities, and making government work for the people it represents.
01 Cost of Living Florida families should be able to afford a home, healthcare, insurance, and everyday essentials. + Expand affordable and workforce housing while protecting renters and homeowners.
Stabilize insurance through resilience investments and federal reinsurance.
Fight price gouging, enforce antitrust laws, and ban congressional stock trading.
02 Economic Development Economic growth should reward work, support local businesses, and expand opportunity. + Help small businesses grow and revitalize vacant properties.
Create apprenticeships and reskilling programs for an economy reshaped by technology.
Address homelessness through coordinated housing, food, health, and workforce support.
03 Workers' Rights A fair day’s work should provide fair pay, safe conditions, and a stable life. + Protect organizing rights and strengthen unions.
Support wages, schedules, hours, and benefits that reflect the real cost of living.
Create safeguards so AI and automation strengthen workers instead of leaving them behind.
04 Medicare & Social Security The benefits working people earn must remain strong for today’s seniors and future generations. + Ask the wealthiest Americans to contribute a fairer share.
Negotiate prescription prices and strengthen meaningful cost-of-living adjustments.
Protect benefits while modernizing systems and cracking down on fraud.
05 Human Sovereignty Every person deserves control over their body, beliefs, healthcare, and future. + Protect reproductive freedom and personal medical privacy.
Keep healthcare decisions between patients and their providers.
Defend freedom of religion—and freedom from government-imposed religion.
06 Foreign Policy American resources should strengthen security without fueling endless conflicts. + Prioritize diplomacy, partnerships, and genuine strategic threats.
Reduce military intervention when the United States is not directly threatened.
Condition foreign assistance on international law and human rights.
07 Immigration A secure, fair immigration system can protect communities while respecting human dignity. + Use modern technology and proper staffing to secure borders.
Focus enforcement on real public-safety threats, not contributing families and students.
Create clear pathways to citizenship through lasting bipartisan reform.
08 Responsible Gun Ownership Constitutional rights and sensible safeguards can coexist. + Require safety training and competency standards for new purchases.
Strengthen background checks for histories of serious violence or dangerous crises.
Keep firearms away from genuine threats while respecting responsible owners.
# Education Every student deserves safe, well-funded schools focused on learning—not political agendas. + Raise teacher pay and help rural schools recruit and retain staff.
Fully support special-needs students, unhoused children, and English learners.
Expand vocational, technical, and STEM pathways tied to local careers.
10 Environment & Resilience Protecting Florida’s natural systems also protects homes, infrastructure, and insurance affordability. + Discourage reckless development in floodplains, wetlands, and vulnerable coastal zones.
Expand resilience grants, stormwater projects, and green infrastructure.
Give local communities a stronger voice in responsible, science-based growth.
11 First Responders & Veterans Those who protect and serve deserve resources, healthcare, and opportunity—not just gratitude. + Fund lifesaving equipment and mental-health support for first responders.
Expand VA care, transitional housing, and suicide-prevention services.
Build apprenticeships, job training, and civilian career pathways for veterans.
