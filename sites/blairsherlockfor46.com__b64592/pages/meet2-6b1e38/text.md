Meet Issues Volunteer Donate Meet Issues Volunteer Donate Meet Diane Meet Diane Diane is originally from Brookfield, Illinois and moving into the Villa Park community over #ago.
I believe our politics can be better.
When we put people above special interests, when we listen to the community and work for them, we can and will see change.
Prior to being elected to her first term as Representative for the 46th District in 2022, Diane had her own law practice that focused on real estate and commercial law.
Diane and her husband, a Teamster who works at the airport, decided to raise their family in the Villa Park community.
Diane's son is attending graduate school and her daughter is a 5th grade school teacher at the Wisconsin School for the Deaf and also in graduate school.
In addition to her legislative work, Diane has always had a passion for community and helping others.
Her involvement can be seen throughout the district.
As representative she has: Created and presented a series of seminars for families of children with special needs Hosted informational seminars on media literacy, consumer fraud, recycling, native gardening and solar energy Hosted Senior health fairs, annual Juneteenth celebrations and Geekpalooza Established "Unraveled" a group of knitters and crocheter's that have created and donated over 7500 hats, gloves, scarves and wash clothes for the less fortunate Created connections between non-profits to more effectively and efficiently provide services Held health and senior health fairs Brought funds in to the district for lead pipe replacement, ADA playground equipment, Recreation facilities and equipment, sidewalk, road and sewer replacement, infrastructure, and mental health services.
