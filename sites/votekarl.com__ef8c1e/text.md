Karl Gillespie
For NC House District 120
I am running for re-election for NC House 120th district that includes Cherokee, Clay, Graham, and Macon Counties.
Serving in the NC House has been an honor, I will do my best to represent the interests of all the citizens in the far western counties of NC
Vision, Issues, & Values
I have filed for reelection to the North Carolina House 120th district.
I am a fifth-generation Maconian and am very proud to be from Western North Carolina.
In 1999, I started National Communications, Inc.
(NCI) with one technician.
Since then, NCI has grown to become a regional company providing the installation and maintenance of all types of low voltage communications systems.
I have been actively involved in our community as a past member of the Macon County Board of Commissioners, past member of the Southwestern Community College Board of Trustees, past President of the North Carolina Cattlemen’s Association, and past President of the Macon County Farm Bureau Board of Directors.
I am a member of First United Methodist Church.
I am married to Janet Taylor Gillespie, who is also a Macon County native, and have one son, Logan.
In just my second term as a Member of the North Carolina House of Representatives, I was elected by my colleagues to a leadership position as House Majority Whip, I am Chairman of the Appropriations-Agriculture, Natural and Economic Resources Committee, Chairman of Agriculture and Environment, Vice Chairman of Appropriations, Member of Emergency Management and Disaster Recovery, Member of Energy and Public Utilities, Member of Federal Relations and American Indian Affairs, Member of Health, Vice Chairman of House Select Committee on Helene Recovery, Member of House Select Committee on Redistricting, Member of Rules, Calendar, and Operations of the House, Member Wildlife Resources, Co-Chair of Agriculture and Forestry Awareness Study Commission, Ex Officio of Environmental Review Commission, Co-Chair Joint Legislative Oversight Committee on Agriculture and Natural and Economic Resources, Member of Governmental Operations, Subcommittee on Hurricane Response and Recovery.
Facing the Issues
I share this with you for two reasons.
First is to let you know where I stand on some important issues.
Second, I would like to ask you for your vote on November 3rd.
I will bring the knowledge I have gained as a County Commissioner and a NC House member, as well as over thirty years of business experience and I commit to you that I will continue apply the same conservative principles to every item that comes before me as a member of the NC House, as I have in my business.
Thank you for your consideration.
Broadband: One of the biggest challenges facing our rural counties is the lack of affordable broadband.
Because of COVID-19 our schools have initiated some remote learning.
This is extremely challenging where in some communities, up to 40 percent of the households do not have access to broadband.

We should work toward the development of good and decent jobs that allow citizens to better themselves and improve their lives.

About Karl
Hello, my name is Karl Gillespie.
I have filed for reelection NC House District 120 that includes Cherokee, Clay, Graham, and Macon Counties.
I am a fifth-generation Maconian and am very proud to be from Western North Carolina.
In 1999, I started National Communications, Inc.
(NCI) with one technician.
Since then NCI has grown to become a regional company providing installation and maintenance of all types of low voltage communications systems.
I am currently serving as a Macon County Commissioner, liaison to several other boards, and am a past board of Trustee member at Southwestern Community College.
I live on our family farm where we have registered black Angus cattle and I am on the local Farm Bureau Board.
I am a member of First United Methodist Church.
I am married to Janet Taylor Gillespie, who is also a Macon County native, we have one son, Logan.
I look forward to the opportunity to represent the citizens of Clay, Cherokee, Graham, and Macon Counties and I respectfully request your vote on November 3rd.
Thank you.
