Colorado contribution limits.
State law allows $225 per person per election for a state house candidate, and the primary and the general count as separate elections.
That means $450 is the most any one person may give across the full cycle.
Because the primary has already been held, a donor who has not given yet can give the whole $450 at once.
A small donor committee may give $3,100.
Contributions from corporations and labor organizations are prohibited, cash contributions cannot exceed $100, and every contributor must be a United States citizen.
