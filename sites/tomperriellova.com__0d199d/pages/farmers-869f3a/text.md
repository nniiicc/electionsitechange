End Monopoly Abuses of Farmers
Most farmers in the 5th District raise beef cattle, and four meatpackers control most of what they're paid.
Two of those four are foreign-owned.
Tom will:
• Pass the PRIME Act, a bipartisan proposal to support the right of local processors to sell locally and avoid the hegemony of the big packers
• Require utility monopolies to purchase at market rate distributed power produced by farmers from manure management and other diversification programs.
• Support “Right to Repair” reforms that ensure farmers truly own the equipment they buy, including their right to fix their own equipment, instead of being chained to the corporate seller for the life of the product and subject to their delays.
• Enforce the Packers and Stockyards Act and other antitrust laws against packers who rig the market
