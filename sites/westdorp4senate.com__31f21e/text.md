About Lara Westdorp
Lara is a small business-owner, mom, wife, and lifelong advocate for wild spaces, clean water, and individual rights.
Having grown up in a small town surrounded by family farms, much like her grandparents' farm, she and her husband Andrew chose Frederick County in to raise their family.
Since then, Lara has consistently been involved in the Frederick County community, from sitting on local and state boards and commissions and assisting with local campaigns, to traffic mitigation efforts in her neighborhood.
