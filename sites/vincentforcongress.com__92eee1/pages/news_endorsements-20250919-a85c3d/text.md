PRESS RELEASE
JOHN VINCENT ENDORSES CANDIDATES IN KEY RACES
George Blumenthal, KathyRedwine, and Sylvia Wright
MYRTLE BEACH, SC - John Vincent, candidate for South Carolina's 7th Congressional District, today announced his endorsement of three exceptional candidates running in key state races: George Blumenthal for SC House District 104, Kathy Redwine for House District 108, and Sylvia Wright for State Superintendent of Education.
Vincent said, "George and Kathy have really stepped up to challenge incumbents who have never been challenged.
They are both exceptionally qualified and will do an exceptional job when elected.
Sylvia, also exceptionally qualified, is the hardest working candidate anywhere.
Nothing shows how hard she will work for the education of our children more than how hard she is working to WIN."
Vincent praised Blumenthal for his commitment to bringing fresh leadership to District 104.
Blumenthal's background in community service and dedication to addressing local infrastructure needs align perfectly with Vincent's platform of fighting for families and common-sense solutions.
Blumenenthal is a seasoned attorney with a robust background in litigation, including civil and criminal law.
Kathy Redwine earned Vincent's enthusiastic support for her bold vision for House District 108.
Her professional experience and deep understanding of the district's health care challenges make her an ideal advocate for working families in the state legislature.
In endorsing Sylvia Wright for State Superintendent of Education, Vincent highlighted her tireless dedication to improving South Carolina's educational system.
Wright's comprehensive plan to support teachers and enhance educational opportunities for all students resonates with Vincent's commitment to investing in the future of South Carolina families.
"We cannot win without people like these willing to put life on hold to do what must be done.
Please support them, as you are supporting me," Vincent added.
Vincent's campaign continues to focus on fighting for families, supporting veterans, and addressing government dysfunction.
His endorsement of these candidates reflects his commitment to building a stronger South Carolina through dedicated public service at all levels of government.
