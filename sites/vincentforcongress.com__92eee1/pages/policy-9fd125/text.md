OUR ISSUES
Time to Put People Over Politics
The basic needs of this district have been ignored for too long.
Working families, family farmers, and small business owners alike are being left behind.
Our campaign is unified by four clear commitments, and each one comes straight from what I hear across the Great 8.
This election is a job performance review, and the people of the 7th District are the boss.
Here is the work I am asking you to hire me to do, for every family from the shores of Myrtle Beach to the farms of the Pee Dee.
Every priority below comes straight from what I say on the trail and what our neighbors are living through right now.
End the War in Iran
Work with all members of Congress to pass the War Powers Resolution so Congress can reel in this unconstitutional war
End the Trade Wars Making Life More Expensive
Work with all members of Congress to end global tariffs.
These two unnecessary actions are fully supported by my opponent and have led to a crushing environment for farmers, small business owners, and the 99% of residents of the GREAT 8 not making so much money that none of this matters.
Fully Fund Social Security
Introduce legislation to remove the Social Security income cap.
As of today, we have less than 6 years before we will be facing massive cuts to Social Security and then in turn, Medicare and Medicaid.
Removing the income cap will add 30 years of solvency to Social Security.
Protect and Provide for Veterans
Push to ensure the Major Richard Star Act H.R. 2102, Caring for Survivors Act H.R. 680, and the Supporting Families in Need Act H.R. 585 are passed.
This Representative will not give lip service to Veterans; he will champion Veterans.
Introduce Legislation to Address Healthcare and Deficit Spending
Put the final touches on two plans we have created and then introduce legislation: one, the ALL American Healthcare Act; the other, the Fiscal Responsibility and Debt Stabilization Act.
One will provide low-cost insurance for every American who chooses it, without eliminating private insurance and without adding one dollar to the debt.
The other puts strict accountability measures in place through a 6-part plan to bring federal spending under control.
