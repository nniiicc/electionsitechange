info@poindexterforcongress.com On the Issues The Economy and Cost of Living Working families are paying more while corporations post record profits.

Workers’ Rights & Unions Every worker deserves the freedom to organize without intimidation.
Strong unions mean safer workplaces, fair pay, and stronger communities.
Healthcare That Makes Sense Healthcare shouldn’t disappear because a job does.
The system is confusing by design, and working families pay the price.
Made in America We shouldn’t reward companies for shipping jobs overseas.
If corporations want the benefits of this country, they should invest in American workers and communities.
Fighting for Workers because he is a worker Brian Poindexter understands these issues because as a card carrying union member and a member of the working class, he's worked many of the blue-collar jobs residents of Ohio's 7th Congressional District have trying to make ends meet, dealing with kitchen table issues, and working hard to raise a young family.
