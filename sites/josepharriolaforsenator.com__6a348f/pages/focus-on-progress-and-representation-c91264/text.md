FOCUS ON PROGRESS AND REPRESENTATION I know things might not happen right away, but I’m committed to working with everyone to find solutions that will work for our community in the long run.
My main goal is to make sure we have laws that are practical and easy to understand, and that will actually help our island.
Guam deserves progress we can feel—and representation we can trust.
Let’s build a government that listens, learns, and leads with the people.
Beware the sitting politician who campaigns on fixing what they already had the power to change.
If they had the votes, the budget, and the microphone—but chose silence or delay—then their promises now are not plans, they’re confessions.
Accountability means asking: Why now?
Why not then?
