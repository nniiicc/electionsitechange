Voter Registration for the Primary is open until August 3rd.
After August 3rd, you can register and vote early at the Fremont County Elections Office.
On Election Day, August 18th, you can go to any Vote Center and register and vote.
Bring your Drivers License to register to vote.
Other forms of ID may be accepted.
For more Voter Registration information: https://sos.wyo.gov/Elections/State/RegisteringToVote.aspx
You can vote in person between July 21st and August 17th at the Fremont County Election Office at 450 North 2nd Street, Room 120 in Lander.
They are open from 8-5 each weekday.
On Election Day, August 18th, 2026 you can vote at any Fremont County Vote Center.

You can request an absentee ballot at any time during the calendar year in which the election is held, but not on the day of the election.
Absentee voting begins on July 21st, 2026.
To request an absentee ballot, contact the Fremont County Clerk's Office.
You will need to provide your full name, residence address, mailing address, and date of birth.
Remember to ensure that your absentee ballot is returned to your County Clerk’s office by 7 p.m. on Election Day.
