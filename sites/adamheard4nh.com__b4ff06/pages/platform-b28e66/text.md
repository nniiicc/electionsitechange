PLATFORM "I come from a caring family that has been involved with public service for as long as I can remember.
I want to work for you.
We need responsible, well-run government that makes the most efficient use of your tax dollars to provide services that our community needs." LIST OF ISSUES Education I believe every child has a right to an adequate education.
I believe public education is a bedrock of our society and is one of the elements that has made New Hampshire great.
Food Security I believe that no American should go hungry.
I am particularly concerned about the high percentage of children that suffer from food insecurity.
The Environment and Climate Change I believe in science.
I believe that the burning of fossil fuels creates greenhouse gasses that trap heat in our atmosphere which is leading to global warming and climate change.
I believe that the change to our climate is causing significant problems to our society through forest fires, flooding, storms and sea level rise.
I believe that these effects will only get worse in the future.
Quite likely significantly worse.
I believe that we need to address this issue and the government created solutions are the only way to do this.
Housing I have long believed that no-one in our society should go without a roof over their head.
I believe that adequate, affordable housing is a real issue in our state. ​ I have supported the Concord Coalition to End Homelessness and the Family Promise Program both financially and through volunteerism.
Healthcare I believe that every American should have access to at least a basic provision of healthcare.
