Meet Katelynn
Katelynn Jordan grew up in McDowell County.
Today she's a full-time student at Fairmont State University — triple-majoring in Criminal Justice, Sociology, and Political Science with honors — and pursuing a career in law so she can put it to work for the people she's known her whole life.
She's running because, like so many voters here, she felt her county wasn't being advocated for.
The more of West Virginia she saw, the clearer it became that McDowell can be represented better.
She has seen the injustice her whole life: families without clean water, sky-high electric bills, and too few jobs to stay for.
Politicians have counted McDowell out for years.
The people here haven't gone anywhere.
They raise their families, run their businesses, and look after their neighbors.
McDowell deserves a fair shot, and someone in Charleston who knows the place firsthand.
Katelynn is running because McDowell is worth showing up for.

Protecting Our Care
Welch Community Hospital is a cornerstone of healthcare in southern West Virginia.
It serves not only McDowell County but residents from neighboring counties who often have few other options for receiving timely medical care.
I believe the hospital should remain focused on serving the public interest, and any proposal involving privatization or major operational changes must be evaluated with extreme caution.
Across the country, rural hospitals have struggled after ownership changes, service reductions, or closures.
Our community cannot afford to gamble with access to healthcare.
I am also concerned by reports that local officials and community stakeholders may not have been fully included in discussions about the hospital’s future.
Decisions of this magnitude require transparency, accountability, and public input.
— Katelynn Jordan
