Welch Community Hospital is a cornerstone of care in southern West Virginia.
It serves not just McDowell County, but families from neighboring counties who often have nowhere else to turn for timely medical care.
That's exactly why any talk of privatization or major operational changes has to be handled with extreme caution.
Across the country, rural hospitals have struggled after ownership changes, service cuts, and closures.
Our community cannot afford to gamble with access to healthcare.
I'm also troubled by reports that local officials and community stakeholders may not have been fully included in conversations about the hospital's future.
Decisions this big demand transparency, accountability, and public input — not a process that happens behind closed doors.
