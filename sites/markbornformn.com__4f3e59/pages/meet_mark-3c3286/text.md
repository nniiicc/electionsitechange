Meet Mark Volunteer Contribute Contact A lifelong Minnesotan, I learned the value of a hard day’s work on a Carver County dairy farm, where 'full-time' meant seven days a week not 40 hours.
That foundation of discipline led me to the U.S.
Air Force where I proudly served for eight years.
While in the service, I expanded my education and qualifications by attending Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University.
Following my service, I spent over two decades ensuring our state’s energy reliability at Xcel Energy’s Prairie Island Nuclear Plant.
As a former small business owner, who navigated the immense challenges of the COVID-19 era, I’ve seen firsthand how government decisions impact the kitchen table.
I am running to join a Republican team dedicated to the 'repair and renovation' of our state.
Minnesota needs common-sense leadership that delivers results, and I am ready to get to work.
