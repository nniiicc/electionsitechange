They provide a framework to prevent workplace abuse, harassment, and discrimination based on factors such as race, gender, or age.
Safe Working Conditions The rights of workers include the right to a safe and healthy working environment.
Labor laws and regulations establish standards to protect employees from hazardous conditions and provide guidelines for workplace safety practices.
Fair Wages & Benefits Labor rights advocate for fair wages that provide workers with a decent standard of living.
These rights also encompass the provision of benefits such as healthcare, paid leave, retirement plans, and other forms of social protection.
Collective Bargaining The right to organize and engage in collective bargaining allows workers to negotiate with employers for better working conditions, wages, and benefits.
This empowers employees to have a voice in decision-making processes that directly affect their lives and livelihoods.
Social & Economic Equality Labor and workers’ rights contribute to social and economic equality by ensuring that all workers receive fair treatment and opportunities for advancement.
They help mitigate income inequality and promote a more inclusive society.
Human Dignity Protecting labor and workers’ rights recognizes the inherent dignity of every individual.
It acknowledges that work is not merely a means of survival but also an avenue for personal growth, self-expression, and fulfillment.
