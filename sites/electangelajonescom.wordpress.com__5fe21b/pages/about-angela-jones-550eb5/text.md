Hello!
My name is Angela Jones and I’m a resident and homeowner here in Southwest Michigan where I graduated high school, attended college, and now live with my husband.
I’ve had the pleasure of watching my children grow up here.
I actively participate and volunteer in the community that I proudly call home.
I’ve been a small business owner since I began my photography business in 2009 in what is arguably one of the most heavily saturated industries in the world.
I turned my business into a successful career focusing on people (literally) and helping them overcome self confidence issues.
I created a thriving multi-six figure career and supported my family, a testament to my hard work ethic and ability to get things done.
I found passion in my work until the current economic disaster hit and ripped the rug out from under me.
I’m now watching my friends and their businesses close around me, hearing the anger and hurt in their voices.
I feel that same fear of what happens when we can’t pay our bills.
I feel frustrated at the skyrocketing cost of living.
And I know what it feels like not to be heard and feel gaslit by those in positions of power that are failing their constituents.
I see how partisan politics are tearing our communities apart and I want to put a stop to that and cross the divide to reunite and heal our communities.
I could sit back and hope for someone else to step up and do the hard work , or I could take the initiative and try to make a positive difference in the lives of those around me.
I firmly believe the vast majority of us want the same thing: to have a good life and a better future.
I want to rebuild people’s faith in our government and make sure they know they are heard.
I want to support our individual autonomy and protect our constitutional freedoms.
I want to ensure our government is doing its job and working for the people, not special interests or partisan party agendas.
I want to see transparency, and a government that runs efficiently and is easily understood.
A business contract, by law, has to be easily understandable by the average reader but our lawmakers are crafting laws that are incredibly difficult to understand to the point that even their fellow lawmakers need help to decipher what is in them.
By writing bills in language that is easily understood by the average reader, it would make it more difficult to confuse our lawmakers and voters, and create a more efficient and effective government.
I strongly believe in the importance of single issue bills to prevent any party from including unrelated matters to manipulate votes.
Additionally, I am a staunch supporter of term limits and campaign finance reform across all levels of government.
These measures are critical in eradicating bribery and corruption from our political system, ensuring that our government truly represents and works for the people.
Our current incumbent has made decisions that are concerning.
He voted to extend his own term limits and opposed legislation aimed at increasing government transparency and limiting the influence of special interest groups.
He has continued to vote for policies that would give the government more control over people while limiting regulations that protect people.
These actions do not align with the principles of fighting government corruption and limiting government overreach.
It is clear that this Representative is not effectively representing the desires of his constituents.
The majority of Michiganders strongly support term limits and have concerns about corruption in our government.
Term limits play an important role in promoting government accountability, fostering fresh ideas and perspectives, and reducing corruption and abuse of power.
They are essential in ensuring that our government remains dedicated to serving the people.
On the other hand, extending term limits only benefits incumbents and special interest groups, rather than the general public.
It allows politicians to exploit their positions for personal gain, undermining the true purpose of our government.
I firmly believe this will take all of us coming together at the ballot and showing our government we want a change.
Your vote is a representation of your voice and it should matter.
I want to make sure it does matter.
I humbly ask you to cast your vote for me this November and give me the opportunity to show you what I can do for you and our communities.
