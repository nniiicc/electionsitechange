Labor and workers’ rights are a fundamental aspect of my campaign.
They encompass the principles of fair and equitable treatment for all workers, ensuring their well-being, safety, and dignity in the workplace.
I strongly believe that every individual deserves the right to fair wages, reasonable working hours, and a safe working environment.
Protecting our agriculture is of utmost importance to my campaign.
Agriculture is not only a vital industry that provides food and sustenance for our population, but it also plays a crucial role in our economy, environment, and rural communities.
Equity in Healthcare, Education, and Economic Security is a fundamental principle that is central to my campaign.
It is important to prioritize equity because it ensures fairness and justice for all individuals, regardless of their background, socio-economic status, or identity.
