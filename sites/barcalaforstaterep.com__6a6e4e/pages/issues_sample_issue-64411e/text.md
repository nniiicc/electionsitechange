Housing
Affordable, safe housing is a critical issue for families across North Alabama.
Everyone deserves a stable place to call home, yet many in our communities struggle with rising costs, limited options, and aging infrastructure.
We must work to expand affordable housing opportunities, support responsible development that preserves community character, and remove barriers that make it harder for working families to secure quality homes.
By partnering with local governments and developers, we can create sustainable housing solutions that strengthen neighborhoods and ensure every family has a chance to thrive
