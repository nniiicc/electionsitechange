Healthcare
Quality healthcare should be within reach for every family.
We need to expand access to affordable, high-quality medical services.
This is especially true in rural areas where providers and specialists are often scarce.
That means supporting local clinics and hospitals, strengthening mental health and addiction care, reducing barriers to care, and ensuring transparency in healthcare costs.
By prioritizing patient-centered solutions and working with providers and communities, we can build a healthier Alabama where everyone has the care they need and deserve
