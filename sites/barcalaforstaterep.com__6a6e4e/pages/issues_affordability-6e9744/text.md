Affordability
Affordability is a major issue in Alabama because many residents face rising costs for health care, housing, food, and utilities while wages remain relatively low.
A key part of the debate is the state’s decision not to expand Medicaid, which leaves many working adults without affordable health insurance and puts financial pressure on rural hospitals.
In growing areas such as Limestone County, higher home prices and rent are making it harder for first-time buyers and low-income families to keep up, while in rural communities limited job opportunities and long travel distances for services add to the strain.
As a leader, I will ensure we work together to enact meaningful legislation to bring these rising costs down.
