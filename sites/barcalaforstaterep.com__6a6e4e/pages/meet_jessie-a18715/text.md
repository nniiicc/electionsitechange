Meet Jessie Issues Volunteer Contribute Events Endorsements SHOP Meet Jessie My name is Jessie Barcala, and I’ve spent more than two decades serving this country and protecting the people who call it home.
My wife and I grew up in Alabama, graduated high school here, and although we’ve lived around the world, we have always considered Alabama home.
We’ve lived in this District for the past six years, where we’ve continued building our lives and investing in our community.
I recently retired after 25 years of government service and now own a business providing security consultation and training.
Most recently, I served as a Transportation Security Specialist in Huntsville, working alongside agencies such as the FBI, ATF, and Department of Homeland Security to identify threats and strengthen transportation security.
I began my public service as an Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) Technician in the United States Air Force, deploying in support of Operations Iraqi Freedom and Enduring Freedom and leading counter-IED missions in Iraq and Afghanistan.
Throughout my career with the military, TSA, and FBI, I led teams, conducted sensitive investigations, developed national policy, and worked with partners at every level of government to deliver real-world results.
I’m running as the Democratic candidate for the Alabama House of Representatives because Alabama needs leaders who are willing to serve, not posture.
The same principles that guided my career—discipline, integrity, accountability, and common-sense problem solving—are the principles I will bring to Montgomery.
I’m committed to public safety, responsible government, economic opportunity, and standing up for the families of District 5, with one simple goal: to serve with honesty, work hard, and always put Alabama first.
