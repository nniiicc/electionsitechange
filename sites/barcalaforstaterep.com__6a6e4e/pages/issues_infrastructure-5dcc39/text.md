Infrastructure
Strong infrastructure is essential to the prosperity and safety of North Alabama.
Although we are currently behind our expected population increase, we have to invest in modernizing our roads, bridges, broadband access, and water systems so they meet the needs of today’s families and businesses.
Improving infrastructure not only enhances quality of life, but it also attracts jobs, supports economic growth, and ensures our communities are prepared for the future.
Through smart planning, responsible funding, and open communication with residents, we can build and maintain reliable infrastructure.
