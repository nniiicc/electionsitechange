Veterans' Services and Mental Health
As a veteran, I know all too well the struggles of our military.
Veterans deserve more than our gratitude, they deserve real support.
We must strengthen access to quality veteran services, with a strong focus on mental health care, suicide prevention, and timely treatment for PTSD and substance use.
That means better coordination between state agencies, local providers, and the VA, expanding community-based resources, and ensuring veterans and their families can easily navigate the system.
When we invest in veterans’ mental health and well-being, we honor their service and build a stronger, healthier Alabama for everyone.
