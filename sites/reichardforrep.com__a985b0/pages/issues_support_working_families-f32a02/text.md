Home Meet Chris Issues News Volunteer Contribute Events Home ❭ Issues ❭ Support Working Families Support Working Families The working class is falling behind, and MO-08—Missouri's poorest and most rural district—is feeling the pressure hardest.
Families here can't afford more costs piled on top of farming challenges, healthcare accessibility or outdated infrastructure.
As your representative, from Day 1 I'll fight to turn that around: Back pro-labor bills like the PRO Act to protect lower- and middle-class families by strengthening worker rights against employers, banning union-busting tactics, and ensuring fair bargaining for better pay and safer conditions.
Address trade, tariffs, and cuts from DOGE and the 'Big, Beautiful Bill' : Recent shake-ups have backfired in our rural district.
Tariffs and soured trade deals added ~$2,400/yr to average family costs (up to $4,000+ for farming families hit by export losses and higher inputs).
Add impacts from DOGE workforce slashes and the 'Big Beautiful Bill' provisions, and families face another ~$1,500/year in higher utilities, health, and food costs.
We need fair trade that protects American workers, small businesses and farmers—not policies that hurt them.
Secure affordable healthcare access —so when emergencies strike, help is there, not hours away or out of reach due to closures and gaps in rural care —whether it’s Medicare-For-All or Medicaid support in the fight to keep rural facilities open.
Don't let labels like "progressive" divide us.
Progressive issues are working-class issues: stronger unions, lower family costs, and politicians who put people first.
