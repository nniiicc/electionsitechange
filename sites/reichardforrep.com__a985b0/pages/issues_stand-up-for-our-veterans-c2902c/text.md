Stand Up For Our Veterans
Our veterans served for us—and now their benefits are under attack.
In Missouri's 8th District, where too many rural veterans wait too long for care, we can't let that stand.
As a disabled Iraq War veteran, I know the sacrifice firsthand.
Pending legislation and actions deserve full support to deliver what was promised:
- The Major Richard Star Act — Ensuring combat-injured veterans receive both their earned military retirement and VA disability benefits, without offsets that force impossible choices after great sacrifice.
- The Full Cost of War Act — Requiring Congress to authorize VA funding for veterans' care before any declaration of war or use of military force—because we must account for the human and financial toll upfront.
- Restoring cut VA positions — Reversing reductions in an already overworked, understaffed VA system so veterans get the timely, quality care they deserve—not longer wait times or backlogs.
*As a product of forever wars, I support diplomacy first and foremost.
