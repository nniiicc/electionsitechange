Meet Chris
Chris is a proud blue-collar union worker, disabled Army veteran and life-long fighter for working families.
Born in rural Tennessee and raised in High Ridge, Missouri, Chris enlisted in the Army at 18.
Just six short months later, he was boots-on-ground in Iraq—providing critical communications support during some of the war's toughest moments—including the Second Battle of Fallujah and the first free Iraqi elections in Mahmudiya.
His service from 2004 to 2012 included three deployments and numerous awards citing leadership, accountability, and fidelity.
He swore an oath to defend the Constitution, and he has never forgotten it.
After leaving the military, Chris stayed in the communications construction field.
He first worked at a non-union, Right-to-Work shop in Houston, Texas, where he saw firsthand the harsh realities of shady employment practices and weak worker protections.
That experience drove him home to Missouri.
He settled in Arnold and proudly joined IBEW Local 1, where he continues working as a union electrician and communications technician today.
Chris has always stood up for what’s right.
Whether on the battlefield, the job site, or in the streets, he has supported civil rights movements through peaceful protest — the very freedoms protected by the Constitution he defended in uniform.

Chris is running for Congress in Missouri’s 8th District because he knows what it’s like to live paycheck to paycheck, to fight for fair wages and benefits, and to come home changed from war.
He’s not a career politician — he’s a working-class veteran who still shows up every day with tools in hand and a promise to serve the people, not the powerful.
