Healthcare & Education Traveling across Missouri's 8th District the two issues that came up the most were Healthcare and Education.
Dozens of Missouri's rural hospitals are at extreme risk of closing.
One third of Missouri's public schools have moved to 4-day school weeks.
Both are severely underfunded.
The worst part -- there are dozens of bills stalled in Congress that would fix these issues including: Medicare-For-All and several stepping stone bills to get us there without shocking the system.
Breaking up medical monopolies where a single company owns everything from the insurance to medication and even physicians leaving zero room for negotiation.
Funding rural healthcare including hospitals and clinics.
Ending private education vouchers systems that have been taking resources away from public education.
Creating fair wages and incentives to retain our educators.
Support Working Families The working class is falling behind, and MO-08—Missouri's poorest and most rural district—is feeling the pressure hardest.
Families here can't afford more costs piled on top of farming challenges, healthcare accessibility or outdated infrastructure.
Back pro-labor bills like the PRO Act to protect lower- and middle-class families by strengthening worker rights against employers, banning union-busting tactics, and ensuring fair bargaining for better pay and safer conditions.
Address trade, tariffs, and cuts from DOGE and the 'Big, Beautiful Bill' : Recent shake-ups have backfired in our rural district.
Tariffs and soured trade deals added ~$2,400/yr to average family costs (up to $4,000+ for farming families hit by export losses and higher inputs).
Add impacts from DOGE workforce slashes and the 'Big Beautiful Bill' provisions, and families face another ~$1,500/year in higher utilities, health, and food costs.
We need fair trade that protects American workers, small businesses and farmers—not policies that hurt them.
Don't let labels like "progressive" divide us.
Progressive issues are working-class issues: stronger unions, lower family costs, and politicians who put people first.
