Home Meet Chris Issues News Volunteer Contribute Events Home ❭ Issues ❭ Healthcare & Education Healthcare & Education Traveling across Missouri's 8th District the two issues that came up the most were Healthcare and Education.
Dozens of Missouri's rural hospitals are at extreme risk of closing.
One third of Missouri's public schools have moved to 4-day school weeks.
Both are severely underfunded.
The worst part -- there are dozens of bills stalled in Congress that would fix these issues including: Medicare-For-All and several stepping stone bills to get us there without shocking the system.
Breaking up medical monopolies where a single company owns everything from the insurance to medication and even physicians leaving zero room for negotiation.
Funding rural healthcare including hospitals and clinics.
Ending private education vouchers systems that have been taking resources away from public education.
Creating fair wages and incentives to retain our educators.
