With over three decades of legal experience, Cynthia Curtin stands out as an exceptional candidate for Judge of the Court of Appeals.
She has judicial experience combined with extensive legal experience.
Her prior legal background included jury trials, mediation, arbitration, negotiation, appellate argument, motion practice—equipping her with the well-rounded perspective and judgment needed to serve the community from the bench.
- Judicial experience
- Magistrate, Summit County Domestic Relations Court
- Former judge of the Stow Municipal Court
- Over 35 years of litigation experience
Throughout her legal career she represented: individuals, small businesses, large corporations, national retailers, and insurance companies—offering a broad and balanced perspective of legal experience.
Cynthia Curtin began her legal career at Keller, Scully & Williams in Cleveland, where she developed her trial advocacy skills.
In 2003, she and her husband, G.
Michael Curtin, Esq., co-founded their own litigation firm in Akron.
She became managing partner in 2009, and under her leadership, the firm became a certified woman-owned business.
Cynthia and Michael practiced together until his unexpected passing in 2017.
She continued the firm until 2023, when she joined Perez Morris in Cleveland.

- While raising her family in Hudson, Cynthia served on the Hudson Planning Commission overseeing the expansion of Hudson’s downtown development.

- Cynthia Curtin has lived in Hudson for nearly 50 years.
She graduated from Hudson High School where she was president of her senior class and a member of the National Honor Society.
Prior to moving to Hudson, she lived and attended school in Cali, Colombia when her father was transferred to Cali for business.
She learned to speak Spanish and traveled throughout Central and South America learning about the Latin culture.
She credits her parents for their guidance, advice and teaching her to live with integrity, strength and thoughtfulness.
Cynthia met her future husband, G.
Michael Curtin, a prominent trial attorney originally from New Jersey, when the two worked together at Keller, Scully & Williams in Cleveland.
The two had an immediate attraction.
They married and settled in Hudson where they raised their two children, a daughter and a son.
Their children each went to college in Virginia and have remained there to start their careers.
Their daughter, also a litigation attorney, is married and works in a law firm.
Their son, a software engineer, is also married and employed by a Fortune 500 financial company.
Michael unexpectedly passed away at the end of 2017.
Not only did his passing leave a huge void in the Curtin family but also in the local legal community.
Cynthia quickly rose to the challenge of single handedly leading the law firm and her family.
