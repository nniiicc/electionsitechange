Celebrate Labor Day with Cynthia Curtin at the 2026 Barberton Labor Day Parade!
Come out for one of Barberton’s annual traditions featuring colorful floats, marching bands, antique vehicles, and plenty of community spirit.
This year’s parade returns to Barberton’s historic route and steps off at 10:00 AM on Monday, September 7.
Monday, September 7 | 10:00 AM
Barberton, Ohio
The parade begins at the BCF Sports Complex, travels along Wooster Road and through Barberton, and concludes at St.
Augustine Catholic Church.
Bring your family, wave hello to Cynthia, and join us for a Labor Day morning celebrating the hardworking people and community of Barberton.
The parade is free to attend and takes place rain or shine.
