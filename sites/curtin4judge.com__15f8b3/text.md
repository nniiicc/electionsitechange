Proven | Fair | Respected Magistrate Cynthia Curtin Cynthia Curtin is a former Judge of the Stow Municipal Court.
She was appointed by Gov.
DeWine in June 2025 to complete an unexpired term which ended on December 31, 2025.
While on the bench, Curtin handled a docket of approximately 500 cases per month, overseeing misdemeanor crimes, traffic offenses, and civil cases valued under $15,000.
She is currently serving as a Magistrate at the Summit County Domestic Relations Court.
Her pre-judicial career involved over 35 years of extensive litigation experience involving — jury trials, appellate argument, negotiation, motion practice, mediation, and arbitration —equipping her with the well-rounded perspective needed to apply the law with wisdom and compassion to serve as judge.
She now seeks a seat on the Ninth District Court of Appeals to continue serving the community with fairness, integrity, and knowledge of the law.
She believes in upholding the U.S.
Constitution and the Ohio Constitution.
Being a judge is a capstone to her legal career, not a stepping stone.
