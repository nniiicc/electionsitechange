Why I’m Running
I’m running for the North Carolina Senate because too many people feel unseen, unheard, and left to carry the weight of a system that no longer works for them.
I’ve spent my life listening—to people on the job, in uniform, in classrooms, in church basements, and in living rooms across Gaston County.
As a Navy veteran, I learned the value of service and responsibility.
As a documentary filmmaker, I learned how to pay attention—how to sit with people long enough to understand what’s really happening beneath the surface.
What I hear, again and again, is the same truth: working families are doing everything right and still falling behind.
Seniors worry about healthcare and retirement.
Veterans are thanked for their service but too often forgotten after.
Young people want opportunity without having to leave home.
And communities are tired of decisions being made without them.
I’m not running to play politics.
I’m running to bring people back into the conversation—to make sure Gaston County has a strong, steady voice in Raleigh that puts people before party and solutions before slogans.
This campaign is about fairness, dignity, and accountability.
It’s about protecting what people have earned, investing in what comes next, and making sure government works for the people it’s supposed to serve.
I believe leadership starts with listening, and progress comes from showing up.
That’s what I’ve done my whole life—and that’s what I’ll do in the North Carolina Senate.
What I'll Work On
Good-Paying Jobs & Workforce Training
Gaston County deserves jobs that pay enough to live on and training that leads somewhere real.
I believe in investing in skills, apprenticeships, and workforce programs that prepare people for today’s economy—not yesterday’s promises.
When people can earn a decent living close to home, families stay strong and communities grow.
Protecting Social Security, Medicare & Medicaid
These are not handouts.
They are promises made to workers, veterans, and seniors who paid in over a lifetime.
I will oppose any effort to cut or privatize Social Security, Medicare, or Medicaid and fight to make sure our elders can age with dignity and security.
Affordable Healthcare
No one should have to choose between paying rent and filling a prescription.
I support expanding access to affordable healthcare so working families, seniors, and rural communities can get the care they need when they need it—without fear of financial ruin.
Communities Over Corporate Interests
Development should serve the people who live here, not outside investors looking to cash out and leave.
I will stand with local communities to protect neighborhoods, infrastructure, and quality of life, and push back against deals that put profits over people.
Strong Public Schools
Public education is the backbone of opportunity.
We need to respect teachers, support students, and make sure schools prepare young people for college, careers, and life.
Investing in education is not optional—it’s how we build a future that works for everyone.
Fair Taxes for Working Families
The system is tilted, and working people feel it every day.
I believe in fair taxation so nurses, teachers, veterans, and small business owners aren’t carrying the load while the wealthy and well-connected get special breaks.
Everyone should pay their fair share—no loopholes, no favoritism.
Supporting Veterans & Seniors
Service should be honored with action, not just words.
Veterans and seniors deserve reliable healthcare, housing stability, and access to the benefits they’ve earned.
I will always fight to make sure those who served and those who built this state are not left behind.
Common-Sense Leadership in Raleigh
North Carolina doesn’t need more noise—it needs leaders who listen, work across differences, and get things done.
I’m running to bring common sense, accountability, and people-first leadership to Raleigh, focused on results instead of party politics.
