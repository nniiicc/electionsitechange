Home Meet Steve The Focus OUR Community Community 1 Community 2 Community 3 Community 4 Yard Signs Make Endorsement News Hey Everybody, I'm Steve Steve Rutherford is a son of Gaston County, shaped by work, service, and story.
A South Point High School graduate, he went on to earn his bachelor’s degree from Southern Illinois University, studied executive leadership at the Tuck School of Business at Dartmouth, and pursued graduate work at HEC Paris.
His education didn’t just happen in classrooms—it happened in lived experience, across borders, cultures, and communities.
Steve is a 20-year U.S.
Navy veteran who served mainly in the European theater, traveling to dozens of countries and earning distinction as one of the few enlisted sailors trusted to con a naval vessel during underway replenishment, leading a small team of sailors on special operations exploring the use of new technology in remote areas and other operations.
That kind of responsibility doesn’t come from rank alone—it comes from judgment, calm under pressure, and accountability.
Steve also served as president of the local NAACP chapter in Charleston, SC, where he worked across lines of difference to address education, economic opportunity, and equal treatment under the law—focused not on division, but on dignity and results.
After military service, Steve became an entrepreneur and, just as importantly, a documentary filmmaker.
Through his lens, he has committed himself to telling stories that too often go untold—stories of historical significance, working people, Black and Brown communities, and those pushed to the margins by systems designed to overlook them.
He does not chase spectacle.
He documents truth.
His filmmaking is an act of witness, preservation, and resistance against forgetting.
It has made him, at heart, a historian of the present moment.
That same instinct—to see clearly and refuse silence—has guided his civic life.
Steve has always worked to help people rise while calling out governments and power structures that keep people down.
He has answered the call for humanity when it mattered, whether in uniform, behind a camera, or standing shoulder to shoulder with neighbors.
He was part of a grassroots coalition that successfully stopped a major development that threatened his community, proving that organized people can still outmatch organized money.
Steve is a family man—married to his wife Dawne, they have a blended family of three daughters and one son, six grandchildren—and he and his wife live in the very house he grew up in, which they restored.
Roots matter to him.
Place matters.
People matter.
He believes deeply in education as a living, evolving tool for liberation and constantly encourages others to explore new learning opportunities to better their lives.
For Steve Rutherford, progress is not abstract.
It is personal.
It is local.
And it is long overdue.
