Representative for the 12th Bristol District
Reasonable ~ Responsible ~ Respectful
Hello, my name is Norman Orrall and I am the State Representative for the 12th Bristol District.
My family has enjoyed five generations of living, working, and serving in the communities that make up the 12th Bristol District.
The residents of the 12th Bristol District have become accustomed to principled leadership in the Massachusetts Legislature and I believe my experience in local government and as a professional engineer in the private and public sectors give me the unique qualifications to serve you as your next 12th Bristol State Representative.
Helping the people and businesses in the 12th Bristol is my motivation as we face many social, economic, and infrastructural challenges.
The communities of Berkley, Lakeville, Middleborough, and East Taunton have a strong history of education, helping others, community involvement, and hard work.
I will work hard to earn your trust and your vote.
I want to be your voice for reasonable, responsible, and respectful state government so that our communities can thrive.
