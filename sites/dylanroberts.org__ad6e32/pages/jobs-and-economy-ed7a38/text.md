Colorado’s high country is an amazing place to live but we need to do more to make sure our rural and mountain communities are also places where hardworking individuals can find a good job, raise a family, and start a business.
At the Capitol, I led our state’s economic recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic, created programs to support and jump-start rural small businesses, and crafted legislation to ensure hardworking individuals have access to good-paying jobs.
You can count on me to continue fighting to:
- Sponsor bills to lower taxes and fees for small businesses
- Create incentives to bring 21st century jobs to Colorado
- Ensure our communities have the tools they need to support and retain local workforce with housing, child care, and more
- Support economic development opportunities for communities experiencing energy transitions
