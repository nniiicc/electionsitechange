People want to live in our corner of the state to enjoy its natural beauty, open their businesses, and raise their families but we face immense challenges.
If we do not have a strong voice at the Capitol, our communities will not have their needs addressed.
In his time in the legislature, Dylan Roberts has proven to be a dedicated and effective advocate for our region’s unique challenges and opportunities.
Over the next several years, it will be up to the leaders of our state to do big things.
We must: Tackle the rising cost-of-living in our region including the cost of housing and child care.
Ensure that all Coloradans have affordable health care options, lower prescription drug costs, and access to mental health care.
Create and maintain good-paying jobs for Colorado’s mountain and rural communities.
Preserve what makes our state great by protecting our water, agriculture, and environment.
Invest in a strong education system that fully prioritizes and funds the needs of students and teacher Build and maintain the infrastructure that supports our people and our businesses, including safe roads and reliable broadband.
