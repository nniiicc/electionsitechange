Committed to keeping NH tax-free and a beautiful place to live
I’m immensely pleased to announce my candidacy as a Republican candidate for State Representative, from Merrimack County, District 5 (Andover, Danbury, Hill, Salisbury, and Webster).
Deeply touched by random strangers thanking me for my public service, it has been a great privilege to serve in this capacity for the past two years, and I seek to (again) earn your vote in the 2026 General Election, to be held on November 3rd.
My Priorities
Low Taxes
I will work to keep state costs down so you keep more of your paycheck.
I will strongly oppose any sales or income tax.
Protecting Your Rights
I will defend all of our Constitutional freedoms, including 2nd Amendment.
Voting
Voting will be based on ‘reasonableness’; what a majority of constituents want, and not upon what I personally believe or feel.
