Moving to rural New Hampshire in 2006 has blessed me with the gift of almost unlimited gardening space, and where plants grow unlike anything I’ve ever seen in Virginia.
Monstrous glacial erratics are everywhere, each with its own personality!
The backyard is like a wild zoo, with a constant parade of furry visitors walking along an ancient game trail.
I derive so much pleasure and inspiration from my own backyard, that I don’t feel compelled to seek it elsewhere!
