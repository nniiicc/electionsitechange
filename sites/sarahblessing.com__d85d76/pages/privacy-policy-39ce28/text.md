Privacy Policy
How we handle your information.
This website is maintained by the campaign committee for Sarah Blessing, Democratic candidate for Indiana State Representative in House District 70.
Contributions
Contributions to this campaign are not tax deductible.
Under Indiana law, political committees are required to report certain contributor information — including name, mailing address, occupation, and employer — once contributions exceed specified thresholds.
Required reports are filed with the appropriate election authority.
Online contributions
Online contributions are processed by third-party payment processors that handle payment information securely.
Payment details are not retained by this website.
District lookup tool
The "Am I in District 70?" address checker converts the address you type into map coordinates using Photon, a third-party geocoding service operated by komoot.
Your address is sent to that service to perform the lookup, subject to its own privacy practices; this website never saves, stores, or shares the addresses you enter, and no address is associated with your identity.
If you prefer not to use a third-party lookup, you can check your districts directly at indianavoters.in.gov.
Analytics
This website uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure traffic.
Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies and does not fingerprint or track individual visitors across sites.
The data collected is limited to anonymous, aggregated metrics — page views, visitor counts, referring sites, and general browser and country information — used solely to help us understand how this site is used.
Accessibility
We are committed to making this website accessible to all visitors.
If you experience any difficulty accessing content on this site, please contact us at me@sarahblessing.com and we will do our best to provide the information you need in an accessible format.
Contact
Questions about this policy can be sent to me@sarahblessing.com.
