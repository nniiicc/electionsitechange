Sarah is the Democratic nominee for House District 70 — make sure you're registered and ready for the November 3 general election.
Key dates · General election When to vote Sarah won the May 5 Democratic primary.
The general election that decides District 70 is Tuesday, November 3, 2026 .
Register by October 5 Registration deadline for the November election.
Miss this date and you can't vote in the general.
Early voting October 6 – November 2 Vote early in person at your county's designated early-voting sites — locations and hours are set by each county clerk and listed at indianavoters.in.gov .
Early voting ends at noon the day before Election Day.
See sites and hours for Clark, Floyd, and Harrison counties.
Vote by mail Apply by October 22 Indiana requires a reason to vote by mail — such as being 65 or older, having a disability, or being away from your county all 12 hours polls are open.
Apply at indianavoters.in.gov by 11:59 p.m.
October 22.
Your ballot must reach your county election board by 6 p.m. on Election Day.
Election day November 3 Polls open 6 a.m. – 6 p.m. local time.
