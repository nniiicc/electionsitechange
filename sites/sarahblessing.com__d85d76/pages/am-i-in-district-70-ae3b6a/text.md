What's in District 70 Towns, ZIP codes, and counties Indiana House District 70 is one of 100 districts in the Indiana House of Representatives.
It covers a chunk of Southern Indiana between the Ohio River and Salem, with the seat in Corydon — the state's first capital.
Counties: District 70 includes parts of Harrison County, Washington County, and Floyd County.
The majority of the district sits in Harrison County.
Towns in or partly in District 70: Corydon (47112), Palmyra (47164), Greenville (47124), New Salisbury (47161), Crandall (47114), Lanesville (47136), Elizabeth (47117), Laconia (47135), Mauckport (47142), Central, and Milltown (47145).
ZIP codes covered (partial or full): 47112, 47114, 47117, 47122, 47124, 47135, 47136, 47142, 47145, 47161, 47164, plus pieces of 47165 and 47167 in the northern Washington County portion.
Several of these ZIP codes cross the district line — that's why the address lookup above does an actual point-in-boundary check rather than a ZIP match.
