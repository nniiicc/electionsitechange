Home About Misam The Plan Press New Pillars Get Involved Donate Pillar 03 Back to The Plan Healthcare Affordability Reform Healthcare should bring peace of mind.
But for too many families, it brings financial fear.
One of the main reasons premiums rise is because rare catastrophic medical cases create enormous costs that are pushed onto the entire insurance market.
A reinsurance model helps stabilize that system.
In simple terms, the state and insurers share responsibility for extremely high-cost cases so those rare situations do not force premiums higher for everyone else.
Key Goals Establish a state reinsurance structure Lower insurance premiums by ~40% Stabilize the healthcare market Encourage preventative care The Human Impact Imagine a hardworking family paying for health insurance every month.
They do everything right — but the cost of rare extreme medical claims keeps driving premiums higher.
A smart reinsurance system helps prevent those rare cases from raising costs for everyone else.
The result is simple: Lower premiums.
More stability.
Less financial stress for families.
Let's utilize the TN Citizen wealth fund creatively to assist in creating a modernized healthcare system that works effectively, makes sense and is extremely affordable.
