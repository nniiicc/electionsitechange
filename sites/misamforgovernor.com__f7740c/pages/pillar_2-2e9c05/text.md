Home About Misam The Plan Press New Pillars Get Involved Donate Pillar 02 Back to The Plan Citizen Wealth & Tennessee Dividend Fund Let's make TN less dependent on Federal Dollars and more self-sufficient Most Americans are taught how to work for income.
Very few are taught how to build wealth.
The Tennessee Dividend Fund is designed to help change that.
Instead of only collecting taxes and spending them year after year, Tennessee should also build long-term financial assets that strengthen the state and its citizens.
The Tennessee Dividend Fund will be built to grow over time through disciplined investment strategies connected to the future blockchain economy.
Those returns can then support programs that help citizens build real stability.
Key Goals Establish a Tennessee Dividend Fund on Blockchain Invest in future-focused assets Support education, housing, and financial opportunity Reduce long-term tax pressure through smart asset growth The Human Impact Imagine a young student who works hard in school but does not come from family wealth.
The Tennessee Dividend Fund should help make sure that student can pursue education, trade training, or entrepreneurship without feeling trapped by debt before life even begins.
Or imagine a young couple working hard and saving for their first home.
Ingenious programs supported by the fund will help make ownership possible again.
The Future of TN should belong to the people who live here, not distant corporate landlords.
We will tackle this rental epidemic by opening new creative doors to homeownership and making it easier for Tennesseans to build real equity in their communities.
Renters just pour money away.
Owners begin building wealth.
This pillar is about helping Tennessee families move from surviving… to building.
