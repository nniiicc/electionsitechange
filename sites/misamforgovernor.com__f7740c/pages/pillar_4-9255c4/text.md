Home About Misam The Plan Press New Pillars Get Involved Donate Pillar 04 Back to The Plan The Tennessee Skills Guarantee Every student should leave school with something real in their hands.
A diploma is important.
But a real skill can change a life.
The Tennessee Skills Guarantee ensures every student graduates with at least one practical economic skill they can rely on.
Key Goals Financial literacy education Trade skill training Technology and AI education Apprenticeships and hands-on learning The Human Impact If life goes exactly according to plan, wonderful.
But life does not always follow the script.
If a young person knows electrical work, carpentry, coding, agriculture, digital design, or financial management, they always have a way to stand back up and provide for themselves.
If TN ensures that every student leaves high school with at least one marketable skill, it dramatically reduces the chance that someone becomes trapped in poverty.
The key is choosing skills that are: in constant demand, learnable in 1–2 years, portable, not easily replaced by automation, and able to generate income quickly.
Education should give every citizen that safety net.
Let's rethink our education system so that it is not just serving the tuition fees for universities.
As TN we need to provide our kids with the correct common-sense tools and skills to destroy poverty.
