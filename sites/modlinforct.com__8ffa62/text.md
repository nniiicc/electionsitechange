Volunteer for the Campaign Meet Scott Modlin Endorsed by the Independent Party of Connecticut The Realist Ethos: Stepping Up As a fifth-generation Brooklynite, I was raised with a simple rule: When you see something wrong, you don't stand by.
You step up.
My wife, Hillary, and I chose Trumbull to raise our two children because we believe in the pillars of family, safety, and prosperity.
I am running for State Senate to ensure our neighborhoods remain the premier places where families choose to plant roots and thrive.
A Career of Advocacy & Results I am a builder and an advocate, not a career politician.
My professional life has been defined by standing with people during their most difficult moments: 20 Years as a Licensed Public Adjuster: I specialize in resolving complex differences with insurance companies to ensure fair settlements for homeowners and small businesses.
Proven Crisis Management: During Hurricane Sandy, I traveled 550 miles through closed borders to secure the home of a neonatal doctor while he remained at Lenox Hill Hospital saving infants evacuated from NYU.
Supporting Our Heroes: I have spent decades providing pro bono advocacy for police, fire, and safety personnel—the people who protect us deserve someone who understands the fine print.
Building from the Ground Up I understand the mechanics of growth because I've lived them.
I helped build Global Scenic Services in Bridgeport from the ground up—a premier manufacturing facility that recently completed iconic projects for Universal's Epic Universe.
I know what a "shovel-ready" project looks like and how to foster public-private partnerships that actually produce results.
Legacy of Service & Cooperation Service is our family tradition.
With a father who served in the army, and a grandfather who served in WWII, and a great uncle who was a Gold Star hero, I understand the weight of loyalty and service to one's country and community.
I learned the art of collaboration from my mother, a veteran educator and union representative who worked with a Republican governor to help coin the American Children with Disabilities Act.
In my campaign, we don't just "fight"—we cooperate to accomplish goals.
Modernizing the Foundation: My Vision for District 22 The leadership in Hartford is operating on "2013 math," using an outdated funding model that leaves our taxpayers to bridge a massive deficit.
My platform focuses on: Direct to District (D2D) Funding: Reforming the state general fund so that philanthropic investments and state resources go directly to our local schools in Trumbull, Monroe, and Bridgeport.
Educational Fundamentals: Protecting an environment where students focus on core academics without the influence of personal opinions.
Economic Realism: Bringing a common-sense, results-oriented perspective to Hartford to ensure a safer, more prosperous future.
As we approach our nation's 250th anniversary, it's time to stop talking about the gaps and start filling them.
I'm ready to get to work.
