Meet Jeff Priorities Endorsements Gallery District 31 Volunteer Donate Meet Jeff Priorities Endorsements Gallery District 31 Volunteer Re-elect Senator Jeff Smith the 31st state Senate District of Wisconsin Donate Media Toolkit Senator Jeff Smith is a lifelong western Wisconsinite.
Jeff has dedicated his life to public service working at every level from PTA President and rural Town Chair to state Senator.
He is committed to securing a future for rural Wisconsin with affordable broadband, access to high quality health care, strong public schools, and a clean environment.
Read all about Jeff’s priorities here .
Jeff is a former small business owner, growing a father-son window cleaning business in the Chippewa Valley to a 20-person operation.
He has lived in the rural Town of Brunswick for over 30 years on a small farm with his wife, Sue, a lifelong Eau Claire public school teacher and union member.
His two daughters attended Eau Claire public schools and one went on to graduate from the University of Wisconsin.
