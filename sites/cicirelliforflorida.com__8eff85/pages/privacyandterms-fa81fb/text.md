Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy explains how Cicirelli for Florida (the campaign, "we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit cicirelliforflorida.com or sign up for updates.
Information we collect
When you submit a form, we collect what you provide: your name, email address, phone number, and any other details you share.
We also collect basic, anonymous usage data (page views and button clicks) through a cookieless analytics tool that does not personally identify you.
How we use your information
Text messaging (SMS) program
If you provide your mobile number and check the consent box, you agree to receive recurring text messages from Cicirelli for Florida, including donation asks, voter contact, and informational messages.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe, or HELP for help.
We do not sell your information.
No mobile information or text-messaging opt-in data will be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Opt-in data may be shared only with service providers (such as our texting platform) strictly to operate the messaging program on our behalf.
Sharing of information
We may share your name and email with other aligned local Democratic candidates and committees to support down-ballot races.
As stated above, your mobile phone number and SMS opt-in are never shared with third parties for their marketing.
We may disclose information where required by law or by campaign-finance reporting obligations.
