Education and Workforce I will continue to support career and technical education so that Wisconsin can provide its businesses with a well-educated and highly skilled labor force.
We must work to strengthen and preserve our manufacturing and agriculture sectors in the 39th District and across Wisconsin.
Agriculture Agriculture is the cornerstone of Wisconsin's economy and culture.
Whether you are talking about the dairy, crop, or livestock industries, we must fight to protect and preserve their presence in Wisconsin.
Safe and Fair Elections Wisconsin must keep working to ensure free, fair, and safe elections.
As your Representative in Madison I will continue to support legislation that will help bolster the integrity of our elections while keeping the right to vote strictly to US/Wisconsin residents.
Tourism Tourism is a major driver of the 39th District's economy.
We must continue to invest in tourism to highlight all that our district has to offer and why it is such a great place to visit!
Protect Life As your Representative in Madison I will be a leader in protecting our most innocent and vulnerable.
Second Amendment I will defend your right to keep and bear arms.
I will fight any gun control legislation that may come to the forefront.
As your Representative in Madison, I promise to uphold the Constitution while preserving our State's longstanding tradition of hunting and shooting sports.
