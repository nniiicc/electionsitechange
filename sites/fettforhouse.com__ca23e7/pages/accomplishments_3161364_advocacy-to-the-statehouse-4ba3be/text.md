Debate on SF 496 - Parental Rights
Mothers call for more parental say in restricting 'obscene' books in schools - Iowa Capital Dispatch
During the 2023 legislative session, discussions with parents, teachers, school administration and legislators created bills that would protect students.
This was an ongoing process during the session.
I was part of a House Oversight committee to testify on the process books can be challenged at the local level and how broken the process was.
Based on feedback, we were able assist law makers into crafting new policy to make the process more transparent and fair.
