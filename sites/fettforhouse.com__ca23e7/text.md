WHO AM I?
I grew up in the Chicago suburbs and came to Iowa to attend Drake University, where I earned my bachelor’s degree in journalism and mass communications.
After graduation, I married my husband, Josh, and together we’ve made Iowa our home for the past 25 years — spending the last 15 in Carlisle, where we raised our two daughters, Sophia and Cecelia.
Our family has continued to grow, and we are now proud grandparents.
We also enjoy raising and training our three German Shepherds and tending to our small hobby farm outside city limits.
I’ve spent more than 25 years working in media and marketing, including leading the Iowa Newspaper Association’s print and digital division.
Today, I use that experience as a marketing and communications consultant, partnering with nonprofit organizations across the state.
Josh, who was born and raised in western Iowa, works as an electrician.
We are active members of Holy Cross Lutheran Church and have a passion for supporting youth and strengthening our community.
My public service began at the grassroots level when I served on the Carlisle School Board.
That experience deepened my commitment to protecting parental rights and strengthening family‑focused policy.
As a Moms for Liberty leader, I’ve been active in advocacy efforts to empower parents, uphold strong families, and expand education options across Iowa.
Working with organizations that support school choice helped me see firsthand how policy directly impacts families and children.
Serving on the school board motivated me to become more involved in the legislative process.
I saw clearly that working directly with lawmakers is how we create strong, effective policy.
Standing with parents when it matters most is part of my purpose — and it’s what ultimately led me to run for the Iowa House.
After serving my community and standing with parents at the local level, I felt called to take that work to the Statehouse.
That path led me to run for the Iowa House, where I recently finished my first term.
In the Legislature, I serve as Vice Chair of the Education Committee and also serve on the Agriculture, Government Oversight, and Judiciary Committees, as well as the Education Appropriations Subcommittee.
My focus remains rooted in the strength of Iowa families and the success of Iowa children.
I believe that when we focus on the strength of our families and the skills of our children, we can build a better future for Iowa — one grounded in opportunity, responsibility, and the values that make our state home.
