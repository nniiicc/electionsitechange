The Oklahoma Farm Bureau is a leading cornerstone in the fight to protect Oklahoma’s farmers and ranchers.
Their efforts to give a voice to the countless Oklahomans who are engaged in the world of Agriculture, is critical to preserving our state’s proud heritage and way of life.
“I am truly honored to have the support of the Oklahoma Farm Bureau in this race for Attorney General,” Echols said.
“In the legislature, I was dedicated to making sure rural Oklahoma’s voice was heard at the Capitol, and as your Attorney General, I will continue to work with, and fight for, our hardworking Ag producers.” “Jon Echols has been a longstanding friend to the agriculture community here in Oklahoma,” Oklahoma Farm Bureau President Stacy Simunek said in a statement. “ We know Jon will stand with Oklahoma’s agricultural producers to ensure that rural Oklahoma’s way of life is protected, and we are proud to stand with him.
He has our full endorsement to be our next Attorney General. ” With the endorsement of the Oklahoma Farm Bureau, Echols continues to build a strong coalition heading into November.
Jon Echols is a business leader, battle-tested attorney, and dedicated family man.
He is running for Attorney General on a simple platform of making Oklahoma safer, freer, and stronger.
