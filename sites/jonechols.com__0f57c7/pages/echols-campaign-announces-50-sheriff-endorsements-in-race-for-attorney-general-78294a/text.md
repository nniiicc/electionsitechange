Oklahoma City, OK – Today, the campaign for Oklahoma Attorney General candidate Jon Echols announced a historic milestone, rolling out a total of 50 Sheriff endorsements.
The announcement comes as the Echols Campaign has continued to show strong grassroots and fundraising momentum.
With the support of 50 Sheriffs as well as the endorsement of the Oklahoma Fraternal Order of Police, Echols is clearly positioned as the public safety candidate in the race for Attorney General.
Anthony Louie – Seminole County Sheriff
BJ Hedgecock – Pushmataha County Sheriff
Bruce Shirey – McCurtain County Sheriff
Donald Yow – Marshall County Sheriff
Frankie McClendon – Pittsburg County Sheriff
Jason Smith – Coal County Sheriff
Kevin Garrett – Lincoln County Sheriff
Landy Offolter – McClain County Sheriff
Ray Thomas – Delaware County Sheriff
Terry Park – Choctaw County Sheriff
Trever Teague – Hughes County Sheriff
With the 11 new endorsements, Echols has been endorsed by 50 current Sheriffs in Oklahoma.
The entire list of endorsements can be viewed at jonechols.com
“Having 50 Sheriffs endorse Jon says a lot about the trust and respect he has earned across Oklahoma.
These law enforcement leaders know Jon’s commitment to public safety is genuine and proven.” said Campaign Manager Isaac Hadam.
“These Sheriffs are leading the charge every day to protect our communities, and their trust means the world to me,” stated Echols.
“I will work with them every single day to attack the scourge of fentanyl in our state and root out illegal Chinese marijuana grows.
Together, we will keep Oklahomans safe.”
Jon Echols is the former Majority Floor Leader of the Oklahoma House of Representatives.
Holding the title for 8 years he is the longest serving Floor Leader in Oklahoma history.
Jon bas a proven track record of conservative leadership and is running for Attorney General to make Oklahoma safer, freer and stronger.
To learn more about his campaign, visit jonechols.com
