Dr.
Kelly Forbes is a lifelong Oklahoman, raised in Skiatook and Broken Arrow.
He now lives in Oklahoma City with his partner, Dr.
Chris Culver.
His roots in service run deep, both his parents and grandparents served in the Army, with his “Papa” dedicating 44 years to the Oklahoma National Guard.
As a child, Kelly lived across the street from his grandparents’ cattle feed store in Skiatook.
He watched his Papa and Keke build a small business, Kelly B’s Plastics, from the ground up.
His grandparents, who helped raise him, instilled in him the Oklahoma values that continue to guide him today: hard work, strong community, and lending a hand to those in need.
Their sacrifice and determination paved the way for Kelly to become the first in his family to graduate from college.
He earned his degree in Spanish from Oklahoma City University, then a Master’s in Bilingual Education and Administrative Leadership from the University of Central Oklahoma.
He continued his education with a Doctorate in Administration and Leadership from Southern Nazarene University.
A fluent Spanish speaker, Kelly has served as a classroom teacher, in school administration, as a federal programs director, director of dual language education, and an adjunct professor.
He has worked in rural, suburban, and urban school districts, supporting students, parents, and teachers.
Kelly’s longstanding involvement in the arts has taken him around the world and developed his creative perspective and broad understanding of how tourism, culture, and hospitality can drive Oklahoma’s economy.
Kelly has followed his family’s entrepreneurial spirit, and is now the owner of Kelly B’s Consulting, where he helps schools and communities navigate the complexities of education and government bureaucracy.
His wide range of experiences as a leader, small business owner, and in the arts provides him with opportunities to connect with diverse communities and gives him a strong understanding of how together we can help Oklahoma’s economy grow.
Kelly and his partner Chris are actively involved in their local communities volunteering at events, supporting charities, and mentoring new business owners.
Kelly has also served on several boards including service as the former president of the Oklahoma Association for Bilingual Education.
He currently serves on the board of directors of Palomar, Oklahoma City’s Family Justice Center, where he helps survivors of domestic violence and elder abuse get the support they need to rebuild their lives.
