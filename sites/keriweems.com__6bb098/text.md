Meet Keri
Keri is a committed community advocate, business leader, and problem‑solver who believes public service begins with listening.
Raised with a deep respect for hard work, responsibility, and neighbor‑to‑neighbor support, she has built her career around bringing people together.
Keri’s professional background spans leadership roles in both the private and nonprofit sectors, where she has earned a reputation for integrity, collaboration, and a steady hand in complex situations.
She has worked closely with local organizations, small businesses, and families to strengthen economic opportunity, expand access to essential services, and support initiatives that improve quality of life across the community.
Her commitment to public service is rooted in a simple belief: government should work for the people it serves.
Keri champions transparent decision‑making, responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars, and policies that empower individuals, families, and local businesses to thrive.
She is known for her ability to cut through noise, focus on facts, and build coalitions that get things done.
Outside of her professional work, Keri is a dedicated spouse, parent, and volunteer.
Keri Weems is running for office to bring principled leadership, common‑sense solutions, and a renewed spirit of accountability to public service.
She is ready to work every day to ensure her community remains a place where people can continue to build their future.
