Whether you vote early, by mail, or on Election Day, make a plan now and make sure your voter registration is up to date.
Not sure if you live in Virginia’s 11th Congressional District?
Check your voter registration, congressional district, polling place, and other voter information through the Virginia Department of Elections. ↳ Check Your Registration & Find Your District ↳ Find your Polling Location Register to Vote The deadline to register to vote or update your registration for the November election is Friday, October 23rd, 2026.
Online registration is available until 11:59 PM , while in-person registration closes at 5:00 PM .
You should also check your registration if you have recently moved, changed your name, or are unsure whether your information is current .
Missed the deadline?
Virginia also offers Same Day Registration through Election Day.
Voters who register after the October 23rd deadline will cast a provisional ballot. ↳ Register to Vote in Virginia ↳ Check or Update Your Registration Vote Early Early in-person voting begins Friday, September 18th and continues through Saturday, October 31st at 5:00 PM .
You do not need a reason to vote early.
In Fairfax County , you can vote weekdays at the Fairfax County Government Center (8 AM – 4:30 PM), the North County Governmental Center (1 PM – 7PM), or the Mt.
Vernon Governmental Center (1 PM – 7 PM) beginning on September 18th.
On Saturday , September 19th, you can vote at any of the three locations 9 AM – 5 PM.
On Sunday , October 18th, you can vote at any of the three locations 12 PM – 5 PM.
An additional 13 voting locations will open beginng on October 22nd. ↳ Find Early Voting Hours & Locations in Fairfax County In Fairfax City , you can vote at Fairfax City Hall from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM on weekdays starting September 18th, and on October 18th, 24th, 25th and 31st. ↳ Find Early Voting Hours & Locations Location in Fairfax City Vote by Mail The deadline to request a ballot by mail is Friday, October 23rd at 5:00 PM .
Any registered Virginia voter can vote absentee by mail.
You do not need to provide a reason.
If you plan to vote by mail, request your ballot early and return it as soon as possible. ↳ Request a Mail-In Ballot ↳ Track Your Ballot What’s on your Ballot?
Virginia voters will elect a statewide U.S.
Senator , their member of the U.S.
House of Representatives , and vote on three proposed amendments to the Virginia Constitution .
Your ballot may also include local candidates and ballot questions depending on where you live.
If you live in Virginia’s 11th Congressional District, you can vote to reelect Congressman James Walkinshaw.
Three Constitutional Amendments Every Virginia voter will have three proposed constitutional amendments on their ballot.
James supports voting YES on all three.
Question 1: Protect Reproductive Freedom — VOTE YES Would amend Virginia’s Constitution to protect the right to make personal decisions about reproductive health care, including contraception, fertility care, pregnancy, miscarriage care, childbirth, and abortion. ↳ Learn more about the Reproductive Freedom Amendment Question 2: Protect Marriage Equality — VOTE YES Would remove Virginia’s constitutional ban on same-sex marriage, affirm the right of two adults to marry regardless of sex, gender, or race, and require legally valid marriages to be treated equally under Virginia law. ↳ Learn more about the Marriage Equality Amendment Question 3: Protect the Right to Vote — VOTE YES Would establish a fundamental right to vote in Virginia, restoring voting rights to people convicted of a felony once they are released from incarceration. ↳ Learn more about the Voting Rights Restoration Amendment Local Sample Ballots Your exact ballot depends on where you live.
