Public Safety
Enhancing community safety is essential to the quality of life for individuals and families.
This means supporting law enforcement with the resources, training, and tools they need to effectively serve and protect, while also investing in proactive crime prevention programs that address the root causes of crime.
In District 164, public safety is a shared responsibility that requires strong partnerships between residents, community leaders, and public safety agencies.
Building trust, improving communication, and fostering collaboration are key to creating safer neighborhoods.
By supporting youth programs, expanding community outreach initiatives, and encouraging accountability and transparency, we can work toward long-term solutions that reduce crime and strengthen our communities.
Every resident deserves to feel safe where they live, work, and raise their families, and together we can create an environment where safety and opportunity go hand in hand.
