From groceries and gas to housing, childcare, and healthcare, rising expenses are placing added pressure on household budgets.
In District 164, families are feeling these challenges firsthand, and it’s important that we focus on practical solutions that meet the needs of our local communities.
By promoting policies that reduce costs, support fair and livable wages, and create opportunities for financial growth, we can help families regain stability and peace of mind.
Strengthening economic conditions, expanding access to resources, and encouraging smart, sustainable solutions will ensure that residents of District 164—and beyond—are not just getting by, but truly able to thrive.
