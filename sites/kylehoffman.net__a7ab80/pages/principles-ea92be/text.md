Principles / Issues
- Create opportunities for private business growth through decreased regulations.
- Minimize the growth of government spending while still funding necessary services that only government can provide.
- Create a culture of life that values all life.
- Maintain a low tax burden on our Kansas families and businesses.
- Providing quality public education of our youth in our rural areas while allowing for parental choice.
- Support the business of agriculture in our state, the biggest industry in Kansas.
