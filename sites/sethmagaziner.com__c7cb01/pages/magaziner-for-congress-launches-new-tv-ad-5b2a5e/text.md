October 8, 2024
WARWICK, RI – Today, Congressman Seth Magaziner launched a new television advertisement highlighting his work in Congress on behalf of working Rhode Islanders.
“In Congress, I’m taking on the special interests to help working people.
I fought to crack down on price gouging by the oil companies and lower food and medicine costs, and to protect Social Security, Medicare, and a woman’s right to choose,” said Congressman Magaziner in the ad.
“Public officials should serve the people, not themselves.”
The 30-second ad, “Working for Us,” can be watched here.
Election Day is Tuesday, November 5.
Magaziner is seeking Rhode Islanders’ support for a second term in the U.S.
House of Representatives.
