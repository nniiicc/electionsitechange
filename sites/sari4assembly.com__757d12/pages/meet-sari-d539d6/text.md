MEET SARI
A Lifetime of Dedication to Community and Leadership
Sari Domingues is a proud native of Santa Maria, California, where she was born and raised.
Her journey through life has been marked by a strong sense of community, a passion for education, and a commitment to public service.
Sari's early years were spent attending St.
Mary's Elementary/Junior High School and later, Santa Maria High School, where she laid the foundation for her lifelong dedication to her hometown.
Sari's ties to Santa Maria run deep, as her parents and grandparents were all born in the region.
Her father hailed from Nipomo, her mother from Guadalupe, her grandfather from Pozo, and her grandmother from Ballard.
This rich heritage has instilled in her a profound appreciation for the diversity and vibrancy of District 37.
Sari's memories of growing up in Santa Maria is filled with the joys of a safe and supportive community.
She recalls fondly her upbringing alongside her ten brothers and sisters.
With a deep desire to recreate the safe and vibrant atmosphere of her youth and to play her part in restoring California's responsible governance, Sari Domingues is committed to making a difference in District 37.
Her dedication to the principles of Servant Leadership, her extensive experience in public service, and her love for her community make her a formidable candidate poised to bring positive change to the district and the state as a whole.
