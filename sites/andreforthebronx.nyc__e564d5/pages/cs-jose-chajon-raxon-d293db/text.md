“On August 12, Jose Chajon-Raxon became the third person to die while detained at Delaney Hall.
More than 50 people have died in ICE custody since Trump took office.
“ICE has $85 billion at its disposal to detain our neighbors in horrific conditions.
Defund and abolish ICE.
