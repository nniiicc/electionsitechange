Representative Juan Carlos Porras, the youngest elected official in Florida, remains dedicated to serving Miami-Dade County.
Porras voted to lower taxes and keep our families safe during his first term as our State Representative.
His key accomplishments include the successful passage of two of the most significant HOA reform bills in the state's history.
These bills established the Homeowners' Bill of Rights, ensuring accountability and transparency within HOA boards.
Additionally, Porras played a crucial role in approving the state's budget, which allocated funds for school choice programs, toll relief, and domestic security.
Born and raised in the community as the proud son of Cuban exiles, Juan Carlos Porras is grateful for his parents' sacrifices which enabled him to become the first college graduate in his family.
As a lifelong resident of District 119 and a small business owner, Juan Carlos values principles like freedom, liberty, and economic independence.
He is committed to advocating for lower property insurance rates, cutting taxes to benefit residents financially, and ensuring transparency within HOA boards.
