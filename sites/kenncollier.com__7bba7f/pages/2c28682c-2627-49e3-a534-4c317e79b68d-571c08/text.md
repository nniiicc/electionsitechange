This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have when you interact with our website, forms, and communications.
What We Collect When you visit our website or complete a form, we may collect information you provide, including: Name Email address Phone number Zip code and/or address (if provided) Volunteer interests and other form responses We may also collect standard website data such as IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referral source.
How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Respond to your requests and messages Send campaign updates, event information, and volunteer opportunities Process donations (if applicable) Improve our website and communications Comply with legal and reporting requirements Text Messages (SMS) and Email Communications If you provide your phone number, you may receive campaign-related text messages from Kenn Collier for Georgia, including updates, event notices, donation requests, volunteer opportunities, and voting reminders.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
You can opt out at any time by replying STOP .
For help, reply HELP .
If you provide your email address, you may receive campaign emails.
You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in our emails.
Sharing Your Information We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the campaign (such as website, email, texting, analytics, and donation processing services).
These providers are only permitted to use your information to perform services for the Campaign.
We may also disclose information if required by law.
We may use cookies and similar technologies to understand how people use our website and to improve performance.
You can adjust cookie settings in your browser.
Donations If you donate, payment processing is handled by our donation processor.
We do not store your full payment details on our website.
Donation information may be used for campaign compliance, recordkeeping, and required reporting.
Your Choices You may: Opt out of texts by replying STOP Unsubscribe from emails using the unsubscribe link Request updates or deletion of your information where applicable by contacting us Data Security We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.
Contact Us If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: Email: campaign@kenncollier.com Website: kenncollier.com Office Located at 2947 Legion Way.
