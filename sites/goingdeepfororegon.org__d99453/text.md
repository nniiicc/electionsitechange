About Me
Tom Lum Forest
Hi - thank you for being here.
I'm happy to meet you!
Running to represent Oregon House District 31, I'm an MIT grad with a science degree in management.
I'm a software engineer, living rurally three miles up a gravel road at 1,300'.
I graduated from Hillsboro High School when it the only high school in town, and have deep family and personal connections to Oregon going back to 1880.
I'm also a wilderness hike leader, a singer/guitarist, and have been on the Banks School District Budget Committee since 2012.
I'm running against a pro-data center, pro-ICE, anti-choice multimillionaire.
I'm here to listen to you and collaborate to get where we want to go from where we are, to do the most good for the most people.
