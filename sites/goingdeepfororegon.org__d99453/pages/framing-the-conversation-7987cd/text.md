TIRED: LEFT & RIGHT WIRED: PROGRESSIVE & REACTIONARY The use of hands as a scale for political orientation is ultimately from Revolutionary France in the late 1700s, referring to where in the National Assembly (the legislature) people sat.
Revolutionaries sat on the left and Royalists sat on the right.
I don’t use left/right for two reasons.
One is that in one sense it implies a neutrality, claiming a false equivalence.
The other is that “right” also mean “correct,” and “left” also means “abandoned.” Those overtones are not helpful to us.
I prefer saying “progressive” and “reactionary.” “Make America Great Again” is far from conservative.
It is the very definition of reactionary, taking America back in time to a mythical and idealized past.
Reactionary also describes the thoughtlessness in the GOP’s actions and its raw, counterproductive emotional outbursts.
