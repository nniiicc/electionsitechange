Willamette Week interview, Spring
From their email to me:
“Our editorial board consists of Anthony Effinger, Joanna Hou, Aaron Mesh, Sophie Peel and Andrew Schwartz.
Several editorial board members will be present for each interview.
“Our interviews are conducted as group interviews [i.e., with my primary opponent, Rebecca Schalager].
We ask that candidates be respectful to each other and not interrupt, though we encourage a robust conversation.
We don’t guarantee equal time, but will offer everyone an opportunity to make their case and explain their positions.
“The interviews are filmed, and will be shown on our website the same day our endorsements are published.
Candidates are allowed to record the interviews themselves if they wish.”
