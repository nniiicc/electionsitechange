Why I'm Running

An important part of my life in software is relevant to legislating.
Quoting my Meet Candidate page, the Agile software development process I used for many years starts with talking to all the stakeholders and understanding the presenting problem and its underlying issues, giving everyone a chance to make sure they are heard.
There was certainly a lot of politics and conflict among the stakeholders at my clients like Intel, PGE, and Thermo Fisher to negotiate!
Next, we prioritize the issues, estimate the level of effort to address each aspect, and re-prioritize accordingly.
We typically have daily team meetings, weekly stakeholder meetings, and continuous online updates.
I’d like to apply this process to developing legislation to help you.
In fact, I’ll write a web app so we can do that in real time.

The technique is nice, but the passion is the point.
While I’ve been a geek all my life and can be very Spock, I also take inspiration from Carlos Santana, whose music goes from a whisper to a scream and everything in between.
