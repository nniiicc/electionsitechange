Fall Voter's Pamphlet Statement
This Statement will go into the Voters Pamphlet booklet mailed to all registered voters in early October.
Occupation: Software Engineer
Occupational Background: Full-stack Software Engineer at PGE, Thermofisher, and Intel, designing and building collaborative, custom, in-house web applications
Educational Background: Hillsboro High School; Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Bachelor of Science, Management
Prior Governmental Experience: Banks School District Budget Committee, since 2012
I’ve seen communities in Northwest Oregon struggle to balance the legacy of their agricultural and natural resource economies with needs from new industries and more people.
There’s room for both here, not letting the perfect be the enemy of the better.
I’ll bring people together to collaborate and seek solutions that work for everyone.
My family began moving to Oregon in 1880, with my great-grandparents and my great-granduncle Simon Benson.
Benson transformed the local logging industry, replacing oxen and railroads with Steam Donkeys and Cigar Rafts (AKA tractors and barges).
He donated his timber wealth for Benson Bubblers, Benson Polytechnic School, and Multnomah Falls.
An Army brat, I live rurally (gravel road, limited Internet, septic tank, well).
I went to Hillsboro High when it was the only high school in town and Hillsboro had just 16,000 people.
I will work to protect us from Federal overreach like ICE, vote-by-mail suppression, and tax breaks for the rich.
Incumbent multimillionaire and Koch Brothers beneficiary Darcey Edwards sponsored GOP’s out-of-touch initiatives like bills to undermine medical judgment and women’s freedom managing pregnancies (HB4087); gut environmental protections (HB3103); and raise utility rates and give hundreds of millions of dollars in tax breaks for data centers and big tech in North Plains (SB1586).
I’m a big “No” to all that.
As a legislator, I will be a workhorse, not a show horse.
I won’t sponsor legislation or spend my time in committees yelling at the clouds, virtue signaling, or arguing with the referees.
I’ll work with my 59 colleagues to focus on what we can do for you – for more jobs, for fully funded education, for rural transportation, and for your happiness.
