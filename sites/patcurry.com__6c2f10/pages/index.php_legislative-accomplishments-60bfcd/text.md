State Representative Pat Curry helped deliver one of the strongest property tax relief packages in Texas history.
The new budget dedicates $51 billion—15% of the entire state budget—to lowering property taxes, an increase of $11.2 billion over last biennium.
Rep.
Curry’s work protects the $100,000 homestead exemption, maintains 10.6¢ in existing rate compression, and adds another 6.8¢ of new compression to further buy down local tax rates.
Pat Curry also coauthored SB 17, protecting Texas land, agriculture, and critical infrastructure from acquisition by governments of hostile nations.
This legislation is a strong step toward defending our state’s sovereignty and ensuring the security of generations to come.
Pat Curry supported key legislation to strengthen Texas’ border security.
Senate Bill 8 enhances information-sharing, communication, and joint operations between state and federal agencies, ensuring Texas has a more unified and effective response to securing the border.
He also backed Senate Bill 36, creating a new Homeland Security Division within the Texas Department of Public Safety.
This division centralizes the state’s homeland security efforts—improving threat analysis, emergency response coordination, intelligence gathering, and support for local law enforcement.
Together, these measures reinforce Texas’ commitment to stronger border protection and statewide security.
Representative Curry voted to end the practice of letting Democrats serve as committee chairs in the Texas House.
This vote ensured that leadership matched the values of the voters.
By supporting this change, Rep.
Curry helped clear the way for Republicans to deliver historic conservative wins like stronger border security, real property tax relief, and protections for Texas families and schools.
This vote made sure the House could focus on getting results for the people of Texas.
Representative Curry supported House Bill 2, a bipartisan House and Senate plan that delivers a historic $8.5 billion boost for Texas public schools.
HB 2 includes $1.3 billion to cover rising education costs, $4.2 billion for record teacher and staff pay raises, and $2.2 billion for special education, Pre-K, early learning, school safety, and career and technical education.
The bill also dedicates $800 million to support growing and rural school districts.
Together, these investments strengthen classrooms, support educators, and ensure every Texas student has the opportunity to succeed.
Pat supported Senate Bill 10, landmark legislation from the 89th Legislature that ensures the Ten Commandments are displayed prominently in Texas public school classrooms.
SB 10 reflects a commitment to preserving Texas’ foundational values and recognizing the historical and moral principles that have shaped our state and nation.
By reinstating this long-standing tradition, Rep.
Curry helped affirm Texas’ dedication to faith, heritage, and the values that strengthen our communities.
Pat is a leader in the Texas House in strengthening government accountability and rooting out waste.
He authored and passed House Bill 4214, which creates a centralized database for public information request contacts, making it easier than ever for Texans to access public records.
He also passed House Bill 2715, giving citizens a clear and uniform process to petition for the removal of county-level officials who betray the public trust.
Additionally, Pat passed House Bill 4748, a commonsense reform that ensures taxpayer dollars are used wisely by allowing state agencies to select the procurement method that offers the best value for Texans.
As a member of the House Transportation Committee, Pat stood up for Texas drivers and market freedom by passing House Bill 2440, which ensures Texans can continue to choose combustion engine vehicles.
The bill prevents government overreach from mandating electric vehicles or banning gas-powered engines — because markets, not mandates, should drive technological change.
Pat is committed to keeping Texans safe.
He passed House Bill 3611 to empower local governments to crack down on illegal “bandit signs,” freeing up taxpayer resources for more pressing community needs.
He also sponsored Senate Bill 1598, giving law enforcement access to Carfax data to help solve kidnapping and human trafficking cases and protect vulnerable Texans.
Pat secured $5 million for Heart of Texas Crisis Stabilization and Inpatient Services, expanding mental health resources for Central Texas families.
He also delivered $10 million for the expansion of the Texas Ranger Hall of Fame, strengthening Waco’s cultural heritage and local economy.
Pat has been a consistent voice for fiscal responsibility and government efficiency.
Through his work on the House Committee on Delivery of Government Efficiency (DOGE) and the House Committee on Transportation, he focused this session on transparency, taxpayer protection, and public safety — always putting the needs of his constituents first.
