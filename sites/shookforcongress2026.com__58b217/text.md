Policy & Issues Get Involved Steven Shook for US House of Representatives, Texas CD-26 Steven Shook MSN, RN, AGACNP-BC is the Democratic nominee for Texas's 26th district in the U.S.
House of Representatives with a strong commitment to restoring the balance back to democracy in the United States Congress.
Steven has been providing care to the critically ill and injured since 2009.
He holds degrees from Tarrant County College, Texas Tech University (graduating Summa Cum Laude), and the University of Texas at Arlington.
Born and raised in Fort Worth, Steven is proud to come from a family of dedicated healthcare providers and small business owners.
His passion for excellent healthcare in the DFW metroplex fuels his Texas congressional campaign as he seeks to restore our nation's democracy to health.
As a future U.S.
Representative for district 26, Steven plans to create jobs, protect Social Security and Medicare benefits, ensure healthcare is affordable and accessible for everyone, support local businesses, defend educational opportunities for all children, address systemic inequity in our society, improve local infrastructure, and amplify the voices of those who feel unheard in our democracy.

Lower prescription drug prices by expanding Medicare price negotiation to all insurance plans.
End direct-to-consumer drug advertising that drives up costs without improving health.
Cap out-of-pocket medication costs and protect coverage for pre-existing conditions.
Strengthen rural hospitals and protect emergency and trauma care access.
Expand mental health services and crisis intervention, especially in rural communities.
Bring front-line healthcare experience to Congress, decisions guided by patient outcomes, not lobbyists.
Lowering the Cost of Living Healthcare Affordability & Access Healthcare Affordability & Access Reduce healthcare, energy, and education costs that squeeze working families.
Support targeted tax relief for middle-class families, not giveaways to billionaires.
Crack down on price-gouging and corporate practices that inflate everyday costs.
Protect SNAP benefits and ensure families aren’t punished during economic hardship.
Energy & Grid Reliability Healthcare Affordability & Access Education & Property Tax Relief Bring federal funding and incentives home to North Texas for grid modernization, weatherization, and energy storage.
Advocate for national reliability standards and research that help states plan for extreme weather and demand spikes.
Ensure AI data centers and large server farms help pay for the grid upgrades they require and do not harm our communities and natural land.
Partner with local leaders and emergency managers to secure grants, technical assistance, and backup power for critical infrastructure.
Education & Property Tax Relief Public Safety & Gun Violence Prevention Education & Property Tax Relief Shift school funding away from over-reliance on property taxes and toward smart state and federal investment.
Protect public K-12 education and oppose efforts to drain funds into unaccountable voucher programs.
Invest in workforce readiness, career training, and affordable higher education pathways.
Support educators and school staff, not political culture wars in classrooms.
Social Security & Retirement Security Public Safety & Gun Violence Prevention Public Safety & Gun Violence Prevention Protect Social Security and Medicare from cuts or privatization.
Oppose raising the retirement age.
Strengthen long-term solvency by requiring the wealthiest Americans to pay their fair share.
Honor the promise made to seniors who paid into the system their entire working lives.
Public Safety & Gun Violence Prevention Public Safety & Gun Violence Prevention Public Safety & Gun Violence Prevention Invest in advanced training for local police and first responders, including de-escalation, crisis intervention, mental health response, and emergency medical care.
Close loopholes that allow guns to be sold without background checks.
Support red-flag laws to keep firearms away from those who pose an imminent danger.
Expand access to mental health care as a core public safety strategy.
Focus on evidence-based solutions that save lives, not political talking points.
