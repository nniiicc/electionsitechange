Minnesota offers a unique incentive for small-dollar donors through the Department of Revenue.
- If you contribute to a qualified state candidate or political party, you can apply for a full refund of your donation.
- Refund Limits: Up to $75 per person or $150 for married couples per calendar year.
- Donate any amount with a max up to $1,000 per person.
Refund applies to MN residents only.
How does it work?
- Make your contribution to the Andrea Zupancich for Senate
- Wait for your official EP-3 receipt with included application to arrive by mail within about 30 days
-
Send in your receipt and completed application to:
Minnesota Department of Revenue
Political Contribution Refund
600 N.
Robert St.
St.
Paul, MN 55146-1800
- Receive your refund!
