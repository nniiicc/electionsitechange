About Andrea I'm Andrea Zupancich, mother of four, long-time northern Minnesotan, and candidate for Minnesota Senate District 3.

I am running to be your next state senator to bring integrity and values to St.
Paul.
District 3 needs a strong leader, and that is what I am.
I will bring forward my experience as a mother, wife, mayor, and small business owner to effectively represent you and our way of life in northern Minnesota.

We proudly built our livelihood and raised our children here, in northern Minnesota.
In addition to our businesses, I have humbly served the City of Babbitt as mayor, now in my 10th term, and served two years on the city council prior to that.
I also serve on the St.
Louis County Board of Commission and Planning and Zoning for the last four years.
I remain immersed and vested in this district, and now I am asking for your support as I run for state senate.

Paul and in Washington D.C.
