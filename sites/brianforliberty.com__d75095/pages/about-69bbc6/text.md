Serving People, Not Power
Brian is a Navy veteran, husband, and father who is done with a political system that answers to money instead of people.
He lives in Williamston, South Carolina, and he’s running for one reason: to prove that representation does not have to be bought.
No PACs.
No corporations.
No special interests.
Not even small donations.
Brian’s campaign refuses every dollar—because politicians funded by political machines inevitably serve them.
This campaign exists to expose that excuse and end it.
After nine years in the U.S.
Navy, Brian learned duty, leadership, and accountability.
He became a libertarian because freedom works, government overreach doesn’t, and Americans deserve to be trusted to live their own lives.
Less control.
More responsibility.
No apologies.
