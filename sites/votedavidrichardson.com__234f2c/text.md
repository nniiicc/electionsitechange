Short bio David Richardson is a Republican candidate for Arizona State House in Legislative District 12.
Raised in Phoenix on food assistance, he became the first in his family to earn a college degree, graduating debt-free from ASU on a full-ride scholarship.
An engineer, he has founded three Arizona bioscience and healthcare companies and was selected by the U.S.
State Department as a Global Innovation Fellow.
He lives in Chandler with his wife and three children.
About David Born and raised in Phoenix, David grew up in a rented guest house on food assistance.
He couldn't read until his mother taught him at the kitchen table and moved him to a school that fit him, a decision he credits for everything that followed.
He earned a full-ride scholarship to ASU, worked full time through college, and graduated debt-free as the first in his family with a degree.
After engineering roles in medical devices and at ASU, he launched his first company in 2016 to build tools for personalized cancer treatment.
During the pandemic, he pivoted his team to rapid-response diagnostic testing for local health organizations, businesses, and assisted living facilities.
Today he runs healthcare ventures, partners with local researchers on new treatments for cancer and untreatable infections, and mentors Arizona entrepreneurs.
