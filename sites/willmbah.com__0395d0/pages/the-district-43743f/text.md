The 34th Middlesex District
Will is running to serve the people of Medford and Somerville in the 34th Middlesex District.
The district covers South Medford, West Somerville, and portions of Winter Hill, Gilman Square, and Ten Hills in Somerville.
Official areas in the district:
- Medford: Ward 4, Ward 5, Ward 7 (Precincts 1 & 2A), Ward 8 (Precinct 2)
- Somerville: Ward 4 (Precincts 1, 2, 3, & 4), Ward 6 (Precinct 3A), Ward 7
