Helping students achieve their educational goals, no matter their background, is one of the most rewarding parts of being your elected official.
To qualify for my scholarship: Your permanent address must be in the 16th Legislative District.
(Please go to http://mdelect.net/ to confirm.) You must attend or have been accepted by a Maryland college or university, graduate school, or private career (or vocational) school either full or part time.
You may attend or have been accepted by an out-of-state school and have been approved for a "unique major" not offered by ANY other Maryland institution as determined by MHEC by completing their unique major.
(Allow 8 weeks.) You must complete the FAFSA by the required date.
You need to show demonstrated financial need.
You are required to complete and submit your application (available HERE Spring 2026) by June 1, 2026.
You may also apply for additional District 16 Legislative Scholarships by contacting: Delegate Marc Korman, Marc.Korman@house.maryland.gov Delegate Sarah Wolek, Sarah.Wolek@house.maryland.gov Delegate Teresa Woorman, Teresa.Woorman@house.maryland.gov If you do not live in D16, contact your district legislators to apply for their scholarships.
