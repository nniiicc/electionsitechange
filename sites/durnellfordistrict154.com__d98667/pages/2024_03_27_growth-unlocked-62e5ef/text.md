Many Hands Make Light Work!
Published by Lisa Durnell on March 27, 2024 The official campaign account, Committee to Elect Lisa Durnell, has now been established!
Donations can be made by check at any West Plains Bank location.
Please tell them you want to make a contribution to the “Committee to Elect Lisa Durnell” account.
Your name and address will need to be on the check and donation form(s) for regulatory compliance.
You can also give online right now by clicking HERE and completing the form on the giving page.
This page has the option for you to cover the card fees, as well, so your entire donation will go to the campaign if you would feel led to opt in for that!
If you do not have easy access to a local West Plains Bank branch or do not feel comfortable giving online, you can also send checks to: Committee to Elect Lisa Durnell, 3384 County Road 3780, Willow Springs, MO 65793.
Any amount of contribution is appreciated and truly effective to accomplish our goals.
Without everybody working together, we can not accomplish the needed tasks to best serve our region, state, and country!
Maximum contributions per person per state house election is regulated to not more than $2,000.
I truly appreciate the support I have received so far!!
Thank you all for your faith in the process and in my campaign!
