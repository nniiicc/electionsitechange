Key Cases
These are some selected appellate cases where Michael served as an attorney or an administrative law judge:
- Harris v.
N.C.
Dep't of Pub.
Safety, 252 N.C.
App. 94, 798 S.E.2d 127 (2017); affirmed, 370 N.C. 386, 808 S.E.2d 142 (2017) (Judge John Tyson).
In the Court of Appeals and Supreme Court, Michael wins a wrongful dismissal action for a correctional officer accused of striking an inmate.
Harris is cited in 90 subsequent decisions.
- Whitehurst v.
East Carolina Univ., 257 N.C.
App. 938, 811 S.E.2d 626 (2018) (Judge Valerie Zachary).
Michael wins a wrongful dismissal action for an ECU police sergeant subjected to a politically motivated firing.
Whitehurst is cited in 28 subsequent decisions.
- Wetherington v.
NC Dep't of Pub.
Safety, 270 N.C.
App. 161, 840 S.E.2d 812 (2020) (Judge Donna Stroud).
Michael assists veteran police attorney J.
Michael McGuinness in winning an eleven-year legal battle over the wrongful dismissal of a Highway Patrol member accused of lying over the loss of his hat.
- Locklear v.
N.C.
Dep't of Pub.
Safety, 289 N.C.
App. 268, 887 S.E.2d 501 (2023) (Judge Julee Flood).
Court of Appeals unanimously affirms Michael’s Final Decision overturning the dismissal of a Highway Patrol member for the employer’s failure to consider legal factors mandated by the Supreme Court in public employment cases.
- WR Imaging, LLC v.
N.C.
HHS, 290 N.C.
App. 367, 891 S.E.2d 497 (2023) (Judge R.
Christopher Dillon) Court of Appeals unanimously affirms Michael’s Final Decision in a case brought under the North Carolina Certificate of Need (CON) law.
Michael’s Final Decisions in two other CON law cases were unanimously affirmed by the Court of Appeals, most recently on July 2, 2025.
