Compassion in Action: Supporting Families Every Day
Nicholas was the calm during one of the most challenging transitions our family has faced for our child with intellectual disabilities.
He helped guide our family with integrity, compassion, and respect while finding suitable accommodations and helping integrate meaningful life skills into our son’s everyday routine.
Nicholas attended every meeting alongside us to ensure our child received the best education possible — one that truly supports his future and individual needs.
His dedication to children and families in our community goes far beyond his professional role.
In addition to his work within the intellectual disability's community, Nicholas also volunteers as a leader in Cub Scouts, where my other children have had the opportunity to learn from him.
He instills pride, integrity, compassion, encouragement, and leadership in every child he works with.
Throughout the time I have known Nick, he has always been the first person to offer a helping hand, a listening ear, and thoughtful solutions.
He plays a vital role in our small community, and I believe he would do a phenomenal job serving as State Representative.
