top of page Menu Close Home News & Events Get Involved Donate All Posts Community Bingo Challenge: "Explore Local" nicholasdilorenzo8 Jun 11 1 min read What makes Bradford, Fairlee, and West Fairlee special isn't just our beautiful scenery, historic buildings, or local businesses?
It's the people who call these towns home.
The Community Bingo Challenge was created to encourage neighbors, families, friends, and community groups to get out, explore, and reconnect with one another.
In a time when many of us are busier than ever, this challenge provides a fun and simple way to discover local landmarks, support small businesses, meet new people, and celebrate everything that makes our corner of Vermont unique.
This challenge is about more than completing a bingo card.
It's about strengthening community connections, supporting local businesses, encouraging outdoor activity, and building pride in the places we call home.
Every stop represents a piece of our shared community story.
Together, we can create stronger connections, support one another, and celebrate the best of Bradford, Fairlee, and West Fairlee.
We invite everyone to participate, share their photos, and help make this a community-wide celebration.
