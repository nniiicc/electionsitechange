top of page Menu Close Home News & Events Get Involved Donate All Posts Community Leaders Stand with Nicholas DiLorenzo nicholasdilorenzo8 Jul 16 1 min read I am honored to receive the endorsement of Meroa "Peep" Benjamin , a respected member of the Bradford Selectboard and a dedicated public servant who has spent years working to strengthen our community.
Peep's endorsement reflects a shared belief that the best solutions come when local leaders, residents, and state representatives work together.
Our communities deserve a representative who listens, communicates openly, and advocates for practical solutions that improve everyday life for Vermonters.
Throughout this campaign, I've made it a priority to meet with residents, community organizations, and local officials across Bradford, Fairlee, and West Fairlee.
Those conversations have reinforced that, despite our differences, we all want safe communities, affordable living, strong schools, accessible mental health care, and opportunities that allow families to thrive.
I am grateful for Peep's confidence and support.
Together, we can bring local voices back to Montpelier and build a stronger future for Orange-2 and all of Vermont.
