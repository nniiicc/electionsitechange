This campaign has never been about politics as usual.
It’s about hard work, common sense, and making sure rural communities and working families still have a strong voice.
I love this state!
Vermont is where we raise our families, support our neighbors, and build our future.
But too many people feel ignored, overtaxed, and left behind.
I’m running because I believe Vermonters deserve leadership that listens , shows up , and fights for the people who keep this state moving every single day.
Over the coming months, I’ll be working hard — knocking on doors, listening to concerns, and earning every vote.
I want to hear directly from the people of this state about what matters most to them and what kind of future they want for their families.
This campaign is about bringing practical solutions, accountability, and a renewed sense of pride back to Vermont.
I’m grateful for the support so far, and I’m ready to keep working hard every day to earn the trust of the voters.
Thank you, and let’s move Vermont forward together.
