Meet Nicholas
For those of you I haven’t met yet, my name is Nicholas DiLorenzo, and I will be working to win your vote to represent towns in Vermont's Upper Valley area in Montpelier.
I believe our rural communities deserve a stronger and more positive voice in Montpelier!
Like many of you, I love this part of Vermont.
We look out for our neighbors.
We value hard work.
And we believe that if you live within your means, government should too.
Right now, too many Vermonters are squeezed.
Property taxes are rising faster than incomes.
Housing is harder to find.
Energy costs are going up.
And decisions made in Montpelier often feel disconnected from the realities of small towns and rural life.
Why I Am Running
I’m not running to make headlines.
I’m running to do the work!
I believe:
- Common sense should come before politics
- Government should live within its means
- Local voices matter
I am running as a common sense Republican to represent Vermont's Orange-2 House District in the Vermont Legislature, which includes the towns of Bradford, Fairlee and West Fairlee.
As your representative, I will listen first, work across the aisle, and always put the interests of our community ahead of party or ideology.
What I Believe...
An Affordable Vermont
Vermont has become too expensive for the average working Vermonter.
We need common-sense, reduced government spending and lower taxes.
Safe Communities for Our Kids
Drugs, theft, and serious crime once were rare in Vermont.
The public deserves strong law enforcement and judicial accountability.
Real Education
Our school spending is one of the highest in the country, yet achievement continues to fall.
We need to teach our children life fundamentals in our schools, not the woke ideologies of recent years.
Growth For All
Suffocating regulations and costs harm small businesses - the foundation of our economy.
Economic growth means good paying jobs for our children so they can afford to stay in Vermont.
