Infrastructure Reliable infrastructure keeps communities connected and local economies moving.
Maintaining roads, bridges, transit, and public utilities is essential for public safety, economic growth, and quality of life—especially for seniors, workers, and small businesses who rely on them every day.
Safe & Strong Schools As a mother of four children in our public schools—and a graduate of public schools myself—I understand how central education is to a child’s future.
When students have a strong educational foundation, they are better prepared to reach their full potential.
I believe every child in our district deserves access to a high-quality public education.
As your representative, I will advocate for strong and reliable funding for our schools, support job training and workforce programs aligned with today’s economy, and work to expand affordable pathways to higher education.
I am also committed to working with parents, educators, and community leaders to identify practical solutions that make our schools safer for students and staff.
Crime Prevention & Response As a partner to our local police department, I understand that public safety is essential to strong, thriving communities.
When the safety of our children, families, and neighbors is prioritized, communities can grow and succeed.
We must continue to support local law enforcement and ensure officers have the resources, training, and tools they need to do their jobs effectively and safely.
At the same time, we should focus on prevention and early intervention—especially for young people.
That means reducing unnecessary school suspensions, expanding access to mental health counselors in schools and communities, and supporting restorative approaches that help address issues before they escalate.
As your state representative, I will work to fund programs that strengthen community-based support systems while continuing to fully support and invest in our police departments.
Climate Change Our communities are facing real environmental and economic challenges—but also real opportunities.
With smart, forward-looking energy policies, our region can remain competitive, create good-paying jobs, and protect the health of our families.
As a representative in Harrisburg, I will work to expand clean and reliable energy options, support workers through a responsible energy transition, and encourage innovation that strengthens the Allegheny Valley’s economy.
Strategic investments in public transportation and modern infrastructure can lower costs for families, reduce congestion, and improve quality of life.
Energy policy should put communities first—promoting transparency, accountability, and long-term economic stability while ensuring Pennsylvania remains an energy leader for generations to come.
Women's Rights We are all entitled to basic human rights - to be educated, to earn an equal wage, to vote, to own property, to live free from violence and discrimination, to have access to good healthcare, and to be able to make decisions about our bodies.
As your state representative, I will be a strong advocate for women’s rights.
Gun Ownership The right to own firearms is protected, and with that right comes responsibility.
Law-abiding gun owners and hunters are an important part of our outdoor heritage and play a key role in wildlife conservation.
As a representative, I will support responsible gun ownership and policies that promote public safety, including background checks and reasonable waiting periods.
I also support modernizing Pennsylvania’s hunting laws—such as expanding Sunday hunting—to better reflect today’s work schedules and ensure continued access to outdoor traditions.
Protecting our wild spaces while respecting individual rights is the balanced path forward.
