Health Care Water and Energy Public Schools Mental Health Public Safety Health Care and Reproductive Freedom Protect women's reproductive rights, including access to IVF, which the Republican platform seeks to make illegal in Texas.
Expand Medicaid.
Keep politicians out of private medical decisions between patients, families, and doctors.
Lower prescription drug costs for Texas families by capping costs and negotiating through government health plans.
Water, Energy, and Responsible Growth Hold high-demand industries, including data centers, accountable for their impact on water, air, infrastructure, and electricity demand.
Tax billionaire corporations appropriately and make sure they provide good, local, union jobs when they are allowed to operate in Texas.
Public Schools Keep public dollars in public schools.
Stop voucher schemes that drain money from neighborhood public schools.
Invest in universal pre-K.
Fully fund special education.
Mental Health Care and Diversion Courts Expand access to mental health care across Texas.
Support mental health diversion courts.
Invest in crisis intervention, substance-use treatment, and community-based mental health services.
Public Safety and Protecting the Military Hold federal agencies accountable for their actions in our communities, including ICE.
Protect our military, including the Texas National Guard, from having to perform federal policing activities and from being deployed in overseas conflicts.
