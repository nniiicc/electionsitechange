ABOUT MOSHE
Moshe Landman is running as a Green Party candidate for the United States Congress in Maryland's 6th Congressional District.
Moshe grew up in Wheaton, Maryland, and is the son of Rabbi Reuben Landman, who led Congregation Har Tzeon-Agudath Achim and was the President of the Rabbinical Assembly of Greater Washington (he performed a prayer for President Barack Obama).
Moshe attended the Hebrew Academy of Greater Washington in Silver Spring, where many of his classmates have gone on to become attorneys, doctors, and prominent members of American society.
After high school, Moshe traveled to Israel to study for a few years.
Moshe returned to the U.S. and attended Yeshiva University in New York City, where he graduated with a Bachelor of Arts with honors as a History major in 2000 (focusing on American, European, and Jewish history).
Moshe went on to complete a Master of Business Administration with a concentration in Information Systems Management at the evening program of George Mason University Costello College of Business in Fairfax, Virginia, in 2003.
Subsequently, he was admitted to and attended Georgetown University Law Center in Washington D.C., where he graduated with a Juris Doctor in 2006.
Moshe is a licensed attorney and registered member of the New York State Bar (membership #5368303), a former Maryland Realtor with Long & Foster, a former NMLS residential mortgage loan originator (formerly licensed in 8 states), and a commercial mortgage broker.
In 2025, he was admitted to the United States Immigration Court - Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR ID: XX918451).
Moshe has worked in the fields of information technology, finance, business, automotive, real estate, and law.
Moshe is the founder and President of Maryland Metal, a precious metal & rare coin dealer, helping clients preserve wealth and reminding them about real money and true values!
In 2025, he founded Maryland Medicinals, a holistic wellness company specializing in expertly crafted herbal blends, natural products, and traditional medical solutions.
Moshe is also an attorney at and founder of Landman Law, a national law firm comprised of skilled attorneys across America who are dedicated servants of the law, officers of the court, and fierce advocates for the firm's clients.
Landman Law is committed to addressing personal and communal injustices in American society, protecting citizens' rights, resolving disputes, restoring the environment, and ensuring victims receive fair compensation, helping them regain their well-being.
Additionally, Moshe has published four books, available on Amazon (see below).
Moshe is a registered voter with the Green Party of Maryland.
Moshe's interest in the Green Party and environmental issues began as a child.
His mother, Gila Landman, may she rest in peace, was a strong advocate in Maryland for the environment.
She always had a large garden on the home lawn and was an early promoter of recycling in the Jewish community.
She attended and took Moshe and his siblings to events to support farms, the Chesapeake Bay, and the University of Maryland environmental programs.
At the Georgetown University Law Center, Moshe gained a greater appreciation for environmental law.
He studied the history of the Chesapeake Bay Foundation, Maryland laws as they pertain to the environment, and federal programs that assist states in improving their environments.
He now follows the good works of the Georgetown Climate Center.
While a Realtor, Moshe joined the Montgomery County Forest Conservation Advisory Committee.
As a voting member, Moshe worked on reforestation, carbon credits, state parks, forest lands, and the requirements for developers to plant trees in new developments or offset tree loss in other areas.
In 2022, Moshe ran as the Green Party candidate for the Maryland State Senate in District 39 against the Democratic majority leader.
In 2024, Moshe was appointed as a Maryland Green Party State Delegate to the 2024 Virtual Annual National Meeting & Presidential Nominating Convention.
Moshe also ran for United States Congress to represent Maryland's 6th Congressional District in 2024, but the Green Party primary election ended in an exact tie, insufficient to allow his campaign to challenge the established red and blue parties.
In addition to environmental issues, Moshe's campaign is focused on alleviating poverty, solving our housing crisis, improving our healthcare system, making our justice system work fairly for all residents, maintaining fiscal responsibility, improving our world-class education system, and achieving net-zero clean energy independence.
Moshe's campaign is driven by a passion to improve the lives of his fellow Maryland residents.
His upbringing and education have instilled in him a strong sense of service, emphasizing the importance of community involvement to make his city, state, and nation a better place for everyone.
