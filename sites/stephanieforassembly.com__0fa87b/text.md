Building a Stronger Future for Wisconsin Families Through Honest Leadership and Community-Focused Action
As the Democratic candidate for Wisconsin Assembly District 70, Stephanie will listen, learning what matters most to voters.
Once in Madison, she will fight for changes that address those priorities.
Stephanie Stuve-Bodeen’s lifelong connection to rural Wisconsin shapes her commitment to honest leadership, public service, and community advocacy.
Her campaign focuses on supporting working families, strengthening public education, protecting the environment, and creating opportunities that help local communities thrive.
Stephanie believes Wisconsin families deserve leadership that listens, responds, and works toward practical solutions that improve everyday life across the district.
Stephanie’s campaign is grounded in hometown values, accountability, and open communication.
The goal is simple: represent the people of District 70 with integrity, compassion, and a commitment to meaningful progress.
