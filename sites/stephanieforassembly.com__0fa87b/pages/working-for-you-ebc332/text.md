Stephanie’s Priorities for Wisconsin Assembly District 70 Stephanie is focused on building a stronger future for Wisconsin families by addressing affordability, healthcare access, education, veterans’ support, environmental protection, and sustainable economic growth.
Building Stronger Communities Across Wisconsin Affordability Working families should not have to struggle to keep a roof over their heads.
Seniors on fixed incomes should not have to choose between medications and food.
Stephanie supports raising the minimum wage and lowering income and property taxes to help ease financial pressure on families.
Healthcare Stephanie advocates for the full expansion of Medicaid to close the coverage gap affecting working families, farmers, small business owners, and self-employed individuals.
She also supports lowering prescription drug costs, reducing insurance premiums, reforming Chapter 51, and expanding access to mental health resources.
Education Support for Veterans Public schools are the backbone of local communities, and students deserve a quality education regardless of zip code.
Stephanie supports fully funding public schools and providing universal school lunch programs so schools are not forced to rely on repeated referendums while property taxes continue rising.
Support for Veterans Support for Veterans As the daughter of an Air Force veteran and wife of a Navy veteran, supporting veterans and military families is deeply personal to Stephanie.
She supports programs focused on healthcare, housing affordability, mental health support, and employment retraining opportunities.
Environment & Agriculture Stephanie supports the full reinstatement of the Knowles-Nelson Stewardship Fund to help protect water quality, fisheries, and outdoor recreation opportunities.
As someone who grew up on a farm, Stephanie also supports programs that help family farmers address climate-related agricultural challenges.
Connect With Stephanie’s Campaign Sustainable Funding Sources Stephanie believes building a better future for Wisconsin requires fair and sustainable funding solutions.
Stephanie supports creating a higher income tax bracket for the wealthy, closing corporate tax loopholes, and legalizing cannabis to generate additional state revenue.
