Issues
1.
Affordability
Because Kentuckians deserve a better quality of life.
- Create better jobs, lower household costs, and expand access to affordable housing.
2.
Healthcare
Because a healthy Kentucky is a stronger Kentucky.
- Expand mental health care access, strengthen maternal health, and protect Medicaid.
3.
Public Safety
Because Kentuckians deserve to live a life free from fear.
- Address gun violence with real solutions.
- Restore voting rights to returning citizens.
- Tackle poverty at its roots, not by criminalizing it.
4.
Jobs
Because a strong workforce builds hope for generations to come.
- Protect the right to organize.
- Expand childcare and transportation so families can work.
- Fight for living wages that keep pace with the cost of living.
