A Future Built on Values
No family should have to rely on credit cards to buy groceries, keep the lights on, or pay for an emergency.
Yet that has become reality for too many families as the cost of everyday necessities continues to rise faster than their paychecks.
Years of reckless government spending and inflationary policies, supported by Democratic leadership, have made everyday life more expensive, forcing many families to rely on credit cards to make ends meet.
I will support temporarily capping credit card interest rates at 10 percent during times of economic hardship.
I will reduce inflation through responsible fiscal policies and promote competition that benefits families instead of large financial institutions.
Working people deserve the opportunity to build financial security, not spend years trying to escape debt.
Hard work should provide stability, not constant financial stress.
Across South Jersey, families are paying more for groceries, gasoline, utilities, housing, insurance, and nearly every other household expense while struggling to keep up.
Current leadership in Washington continues to spend beyond its means while New Jersey families are forced to live within theirs.
Inflation, excessive regulations, and failed economic policies have increased the cost of living and made it harder for working families to save, invest, or plan for the future.
My priority will be to reduce unnecessary federal spending, support affordable, reliable American energy, encourage economic growth, cut burdensome regulations, and protect tax relief for working families.
The goal is simple: help families keep more of what they earn and make South Jersey a place where opportunity is once again within reach.
Every young person deserves the opportunity to build a successful future.
Whether they choose college, a trade, military service, or entrepreneurship, every path should be respected, and every career should be valued.
For too long, Washington has encouraged a one-size-fits-all approach while overlooking career and technical education, apprenticeships, and workforce development.
At the same time, the rising cost of housing has made it harder for young adults to remain in New Jersey and build a future close to their families.
I will support expanding apprenticeships, strengthening vocational education, encouraging workforce development, and promoting policies that make homeownership more attainable.
Our state should create opportunities that encourage the next generation to build successful careers and strong families right here in South Jersey.
I come from the trades.
I know what it means to work with your hands, show up early, and earn a living building something real.
The trades built the middle class in South Jersey, but Washington ignored that path and pushed college at any cost.
That created massive debt, labor shortages, and higher construction costs.
College should be an option not a trap.
In Congress I will support vocational training apprenticeships workforce development tied to real jobs American manufacturing and infrastructure that uses American labor.
I will fight regulations that drive up housing and construction costs and make it harder for working people to succeed.
Confidence in our elections is essential to preserving our Constitutional Republic.
Every legal vote should count, every eligible voter should have the opportunity to vote, and every citizen should have confidence that elections are conducted fairly, securely, and transparently.
Some leaders in Washington and Trenton continue to oppose commonsense election safeguards that would strengthen public confidence without preventing any eligible citizen from voting.
Protecting our elections should never be viewed as a partisan issue.
It is about protecting the voice of every legal voter and ensuring the integrity of the process.
I will support requiring proof of citizenship to vote in federal elections, requiring voter identification, improving the accuracy of voter rolls, strengthening mail in ballot security, and increasing transparency throughout the election process.
Every New Jersey voter deserves confidence that their vote is protected and counted fairly.
A nation without secure borders cannot remain secure for long.
Although significant progress has been made at the federal level to regain control of our border, New Jersey taxpayers are still dealing with the consequences of years of failed immigration policies and sanctuary policies that continue to strain public resources.
Current leadership in Trenton continues to support policies that limit cooperation with federal immigration authorities while asking New Jersey taxpayers to shoulder the financial burden.
We can support legal immigration while also enforcing our laws, protecting public safety, and ensuring taxpayer funded benefits are reserved for those who are legally entitled to receive them.
I will support secure borders, enforce existing immigration laws, strengthen cooperation between federal and local law enforcement, improve the legal immigration process, and ensure taxpayer dollars are used to serve American citizens first.
New Jersey families deserve safe communities, secure borders, and leadership that puts their interests first.

Across New Jersey, many parents have become increasingly concerned that political and ideological agendas are replacing academic excellence in our classrooms.
Government should never stand between parents and their children.
Education should prepare students to think critically, develop practical skills, and become responsible citizens, not promote political activism.

Strong families and strong schools are the foundation of strong communities.
Every family deserves to feel safe in their home, their neighborhood, and their community.

I will support our law enforcement officers, strengthen penalties for violent criminals and drug traffickers, combat the fentanyl epidemic, and improve coordination between local, state, and federal agencies to keep our communities safe.

The answer to ideas we disagree with is more open discussion, not censorship.

Our Constitutional Republic depends on an informed public and the freedom to speak without fear.
