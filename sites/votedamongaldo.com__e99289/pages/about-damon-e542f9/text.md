I am a working husband, father, and tradesman who believes that South Jersey deserves better than what we have been given.
Throughout my life, I have worked in construction and the trades, building real projects and earning a living just like most families in this district.
I understand the importance of hard work and taking responsibility.
I know what it means to balance a household budget, face rising costs, and worry about the future that our children will inherit.
I decided to get involved in public service because I watched New Jersey become more expensive, less accountable, and more disconnected from the people who actually live here.
Families are working harder than ever yet falling further behind.
Young people cannot afford to buy homes.
Parents are losing their voice.
Government keeps growing but life keeps getting harder.
After my last run for Congress I did not walk away.
I listened.
I learned.
And I built something of my own.
I started Americas Pulse Network and the Jersey Sure podcast because too many important conversations were being ignored or controlled.
Everyday people did not have a platform to speak honestly about what was happening in their communities.
I wanted to create a space where real issues could be discussed openly without corporate media narratives or political pressure.
That experience confirmed what I already believed.
The problem is not a lack of ideas or hard work.
The problem is a political system that protects itself instead of the people it is supposed to serve.
I am running for Congress again because South Jersey needs a representative who understands real work, real costs, and real consequences.
Someone who will fight for affordability, accountability, public safety, parental rights, and free speech and who will put American citizens first.
I am not running to climb a political ladder.
I am running to challenge a system that has lost touch with the people it represents.
South Jersey deserves leadership that works as hard as its people do.
That is why I am running for Congress.
