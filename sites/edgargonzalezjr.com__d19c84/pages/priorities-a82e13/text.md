As your state representative, Edgar has prioritized:
- Expanding access to affordable healthcare for patients with preexisting conditions, lowering prescription drug costs, and fighting big insurance companies that raise rates for no reason
- Keeping our communities safe by voting to ban the possession and sale of assault weapons and funding community-based violence prevention programs
- Protecting the fundamental reproductive rights of Illinois women by voting for pro-choice legislation and repealing outdated laws
- Increasing funding for public schools, allocating millions of dollars towards MAP grants for students pursuing higher education, and expanding vocational education and skills training
PRIORITIES
Together, we have delivered real results for our community by:
- Fighting housing insecurity by helping homeowners and renters apply for mortgage and rental assistance and sponsoring legislation to lower property taxes for working families
- Creating more good-paying jobs for working families by introducing and passing proworker bills into law and voting to pass paid leave for 1.5 million Illinois workers
- Supporting small businesses by helping businesses apply for and get awarded almost $1,000,000 in Back to Business grants programs
- Bringing almost $3,000,000 for community projects across the district, including building a community center in Brighton Park, renovation of a community center in Little Village, construction of green spaces across the Southwest Side, and repaving roadways and alleys
PRIORIDADES
Como su representante estatal, Edgar ha priorizado:
- Ampliar el acceso a atención medica asequible para pacientes con condiciones preexistentes, reducir los costos de los medicamentos recetados y luchar contra las grandes com pan fas de seguros que aumentan las tarifas sin motivo alguno
- Mantener seguras a nuestras comunidades votando para prohibir la posesión y venta de armas de asalto y financiando programas comunitarios de prevención de la violencia

- Aumentar los fondos para las escuelas publicas, asignar millones de dólares a subvenciones MAP para estudiantes de colegio y ampliar la educación vocacional y la formación profesional
Juntos, hemos logrado resultados reales para nuestra comunidad al:
- Luchar contra la inseguridad de vivienda ayudando a los propietarios e inquilinos a solicitar asistencia hipotecaria y de alquiler y patrocinando legislación para reducir los impuestos a la propiedad para las familias trabajadoras
