MERY’S STORY
As a mother of four, Mery understands firsthand the challenges and aspirations of families in our community.
Her years of community engagement have given her unique insights into the issues that matter most - from quality education and public safety to the importance of creating opportunities for the next generation.
It is this deep connection to our community that fuels her passion to fight for policies that will secure a brighter future for all families in our district.
Mery's commitment goes beyond rhetoric; it is a personal mission to ensure that every family in District 102 has the chance to thrive and succeed.
With over two decades of experience as an accomplished attorney, Mery has honed her skills in advocacy, negotiation, and problem-solving.
Her legal background equips her with a deep understanding of how laws impact our daily lives, making her uniquely qualified to craft effective legislation in Tallahassee.
BRINGING A NEW PERSPECTIVE AND A RESULTS-DRIVEN ATTITUDE TO TALLAHASSEE
Mery's commitment to our community extends far beyond her professional life.
As a small business owner of multiple successful ventures, including the beloved Vicky Bakery franchise in Weston/Sunrise, she has firsthand experience with the challenges and opportunities facing local entrepreneurs.
This perspective will be invaluable in creating policies that support and nurture small businesses - the backbone of our local economy.
Now, Mery is ready to take her passion for service to the next level.
She's running for the Florida House of Representatives to be a voice for families, a champion for small businesses, and a tireless advocate for the betterment of District 102.
With her unique blend of professional expertise and community insight, Mery is ready to tackle the complex challenges facing our state.
As our representative in Tallahassee, she'll bring the same dedication, work ethic, and commitment to results that have defined her career and her life of service.
With Mery, we're not just electing a representative; we're choosing a neighbor, a parent, and a proven leader who will fight tirelessly for the values and priorities of our district.
“We can't sit by while hardworking families struggle with sky-high insurance premiums, watch their paychecks shrink due to inflation, worry about crime in their neighborhoods, or wonder if their kids are getting the education they need.
It's time for action, and I'm ready to be your voice and your champion in Tallahassee.” Mery
