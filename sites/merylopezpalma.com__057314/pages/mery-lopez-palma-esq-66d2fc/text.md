Political Experience
Mery Lopez-Palma is a passionate Republican involved in the party since high school.
Her family's escape from Cuba and their journey to the US inspired her to become a Republican and fight for the freedoms granted by the US Constitution.
She has served the Republican Party in various roles, including as a delegate in the 2000 Presidential race, Vice President of the Republican club at the University of Central Florida, and a Florida lawyer.
Mery's passion for the law and the Constitution has motivated her to educate children about their constitutional rights through her community involvement with 917society.org.
She is married to Armando Palma, and they have four children, who are all active in community affairs with her.
Mery is a Florida Founder of 917society.org, where she collaborates with a team of volunteers to distribute constitutions to schools during constitutional week.
She planned and hosted the first Broward fundraiser for 917society.org, raising money to print and distribute constitutions to 8th-grade students in the county.
Mery has also served as the Southwest Ranches Precinct Committeewomen for the Broward Republican Party for 2.5 years and is the Chairwomen of all events for the party.
She also serves as the CFC Christian Family Coalition Broward Director, which puts together a Christian voter guide after a candidate interview process lasting months and delivers it to over 150 churches in Broward.
Mery has helped over 25 candidates in Broward through in-kind contributions, financial contributions, legal pro-bono consultation, food donations, drink donations, volunteering as Master of Ceremonies, meet and greets, donating silent auction items, door knocking, Led Truck ads, telephone calls, raising money for candidates, and many other candidate engagement and development efforts.
Professional Experience
Mery Lopez-Palma, Esq. is a visionary entrepreneur with over 22 years of experience as the founder and CEO of the Law Offices of Mery Lopez-Palma.
She also owns the Vicky Bakery Franchise in Weston/Sunrise, FL, and is the CEO of Palma Vida Foods & Mery's Tropical Fruit Spreads.
With her exceptional management skills, she has successfully overseen all aspects of the operations of her law firm and other profitable companies.
Throughout her career, she has gained extensive experience in property acquisition, lender negotiations, evictions, HR hiring, PR, and other areas crucial for her company's growth.
She is a respected community leader serving South Florida since 1999.
Her dedication to excellence and commitment to serving the community have earned her a reputation as a trusted and reliable regional business leader.
