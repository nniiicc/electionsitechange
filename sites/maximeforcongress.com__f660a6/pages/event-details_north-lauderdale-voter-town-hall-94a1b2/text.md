Kedner Maxime will attend the upcoming North Lauderdale Voter Town Hall and Mayoral Candidate Forum as part of his continued commitment to listening, learning, and staying connected to the communities of District 20.
The event will bring together local residents, mayoral candidates, the League of Women Voters, and representatives from the Broward County Supervisor of Elections for an important conversation about civic participation, local leadership, and the issues affecting North Lauderdale families.
While this is a mayoral forum and not a congressional candidate event, Dr.
Maxime believes strong representation begins by showing up, listening to residents, and building relationships across every level of government.
Dr.
Maxime encourages District 20 residents to attend, become informed, meet their local leaders, and make their voices heard.
Our democracy is strongest when our communities participate.
