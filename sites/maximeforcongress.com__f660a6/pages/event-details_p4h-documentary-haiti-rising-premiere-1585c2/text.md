Maxime attended the world premiere of P4H Documentary: Haiti Rising at the Little Haiti Cultural Complex in Miami, joining community members, educators, and families for an evening honoring fifteen years of grassroots transformation in Haiti.
The documentary tells the story of Dr.
Bertrhude Albert and Dr.
Priscilla Zelaya, co-founders of a movement that has trained more than seventeen thousand educators and reached over five hundred fifty thousand students across one hundred sixty partner schools in Haiti.
The film highlights a narrative built on sustainable, locally led progress driven by Haitian leaders, teachers, and families.
The evening included a red carpet welcome, the full documentary screening, and a live question and answer session with the founders.
Dr.
Maxime joined the audience in celebrating the students, teachers, and community leaders whose work is reshaping opportunity across Haiti.
