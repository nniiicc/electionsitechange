Donation FAQ
How do I donate to the campaign?
You can give securely online through our platform by clicking the 'Donate Now' button below.
We appreciate your generous contribution to our mission.
Is my donation tax-deductible?
No, contributions to federal political campaigns are not considered tax-deductible for federal income tax purposes.
Please consult with a tax professional for more specific advice.
Are there limits to how much I can contribute?
Yes, federal law limits individual donations to $3,500 per election.
By choosing to give, you help us reach more voters across Florida's District 20.
Who is eligible to make a donation?
Donations are to be made by individuals or eligible LLCs.
Contributions from corporations are not permitted.
