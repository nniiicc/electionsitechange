On the Issues
The gap that's holding District 20 back
Working District 20 families are earning paychecks — and too few are ever building anything with them.
Rent goes to someone else's asset.
Retirement savings feel out of reach.
And the wealth that gets passed from one generation to the next never quite gets started.
That gap between working hard and getting ahead is why so many families feel stuck no matter how many hours they put in.
