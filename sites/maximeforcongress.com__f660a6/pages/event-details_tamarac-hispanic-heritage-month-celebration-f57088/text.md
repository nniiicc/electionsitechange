Kedner Maxime will join residents and families from across Tamarac for the City's annual Hispanic Heritage Month Celebration on Friday, September 18.
Hispanic Heritage Month is an opportunity to recognize the history, culture, traditions, and contributions of Hispanic and Latino communities that help make South Florida such a vibrant place to live.
The free community celebration will feature live Latin music, cultural performances, dancing, local food vendors, and entertainment for the entire family.
Dr.
Maxime looks forward to spending the evening with Tamarac families, supporting local businesses and vendors, and celebrating the cultures and traditions that make our communities stronger.
District 20 is home to people from many different backgrounds and cultures, and that diversity is one of our greatest strengths.
Come celebrate our neighbors, our cultures, and the community we share.
