Maxime joined neighbors, local leaders, and families in Fort Lauderdale's Historic Sistrunk neighborhood for Sounds on Sistrunk, a free live concert and block party held at Provident Park.
The evening brought together Vice Mayor Robert McKinzie, Fort Lauderdale Commissioner Pam Beasley-Pittman, and the Broward County Cultural Division to celebrate the music, culture, and creative spirit of the Sistrunk corridor.
Performances from local South Florida artists Ogneiljay and Black Nimbus filled the park with soul and rhythm as families gathered for an evening under the summer sky.
Dr.
Maxime used the opportunity to connect directly with residents of District 20, hearing firsthand about the pride and priorities of one of Fort Lauderdale's most historic neighborhoods.
The event also spotlighted the small businesses that make Sistrunk distinctive, with attendees encouraged to support local favorites before and after the show.
