Kedner Maxime will be participating in a Nonpartisan Candidates Forum hosted by John Knox Village on Friday, September 25, 2026, from 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM at the Cultural Arts Center.
This forum gives residents of District 20 the opportunity to hear directly from candidates appearing on the ballot and to engage in informed civic participation within their community.
Each participating candidate, including Dr.
Maxime, will be given equal speaking time of approximately four minutes and equal opportunity to address attendees.
Following the formal program, Dr.
Maxime looks forward to staying for informal conversation with residents and guests who would like to learn more about his campaign and his vision for District 20.
We encourage all residents of John Knox Village and the surrounding community to attend and take part in this nonpartisan opportunity to get to know the candidates on this year's ballot.
