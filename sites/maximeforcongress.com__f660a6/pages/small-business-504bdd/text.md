Small Business
On the Issues
Why small business is District 20's backbone
The corner store.
The family restaurant.
The two-truck landscaping outfit.
The immigrant-owned bakery that anchors the neighborhood.
Small businesses are how District 20 builds — how families move from working for someone else to owning their own future.
But too many entrepreneurs are drowning in paperwork, shut out of capital, and losing ground to national chains that get every break in the book.

He knows exactly what breaks a small business and what saves one.
