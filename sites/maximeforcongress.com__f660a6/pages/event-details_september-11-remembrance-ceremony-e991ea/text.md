About the event
Dr.
Kedner Maxime will join members of the Sunrise community for the September 11 Remembrance Ceremony on Friday, September 11.
This gathering is a time to honor the lives lost on September 11, 2001, remember the courage of first responders and ordinary Americans who acted selflessly that day, and stand with the families whose lives were forever changed.
Dr.
Maxime will attend simply as a member of the community, joining neighbors in reflection, remembrance, and gratitude for those who served and sacrificed.
On this day, we remember the lives lost, honor the heroes, and reflect on the strength and unity our nation showed in the face of tragedy.
We will never forget.
