Jobs & Economic Security
On the Issues
Why this fight matters
Too many District 20 workers are stuck in jobs that don't pay enough to live on.
Too many workers who want more can't afford the training to get there.
And too many young people watch good jobs go somewhere else because the pipeline between our schools and our employers isn't connected.
Economic security isn't a slogan — it's whether you can pay rent, save for retirement, and take a day off when you're sick.
