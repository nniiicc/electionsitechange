On the Issues
Why this fight matters
Dr.
Maxime spent 25 years teaching Sunday school in Broward County churches.
He knows the difference a great teacher makes in a child's life — and he knows what it costs a community when our schools are underfunded and our teachers underpaid.
In District 20, too many young people, especially in underserved communities, aren't getting the education and training they need to build the future they deserve.
Solutions
What Dr.
Maxime will do in Congress
Every child deserves a real shot — not one that depends on their zip code.
