Why this fight is personal
Dr.
Maxime taught Sunday school in Broward County churches for 25 years.
He knows District 20's young people — their talent, their dreams, and the walls too many of them run into.
Right now, too many students are graduating with debt that dictates the rest of their lives.
Too many young workers are locked out of trades they'd thrive in.
And too many families are watching their kids leave South Florida because opportunity keeps moving somewhere else.
