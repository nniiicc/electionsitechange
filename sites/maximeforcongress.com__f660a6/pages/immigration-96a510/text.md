On the Issues A different kind of voice on immigration For more than 30 years, Dr.
Maxime has worked with immigrant families across South Florida — as an accountant, a U.S. citizenship service consultant, and a pastor.
He has sat across the table from families navigating a complex system, helped them understand their rights, and helped them become proud American citizens.
He knows this issue not from a talking point, but from a lifetime of walking with people through it.
What's Broken Now ​ The system isn't working for anyone.
