Kedner Maxime Kedner Maxime Sep 14 1 min read Some of the best conversations happen in the barber's chair.
That's the idea behind our new Barbershop Series — real talk, real cuts, at barbershops across District 20.
Dr.
Kedner Maxime sat down for the first stop of the series at Petit Beau Barber Lounge, for a real conversation about why he's running to represent Florida's District 20.
Why I'm Running While getting a haircut, I got straight to the point.
I'm not a career politician, and I'm asking the people of District 20 to give me the chance to represent them in Washington, because their struggle is my struggle.
I feel the weight of the cost of living right now, same as everyone else.
Too many families in this district are living paycheck to paycheck, and they deserve a quality of life that matches the work they put in every day.
If elected, I will fight to create and pass policy that actually helps people get there.
How to Get Involved Toward the end of the conversation, I was asked where people can go to learn more, donate, or volunteer.
The answer is simple: maximeforcongress.com.
That's where you'll find everything, including links to every part of this campaign.
More Stops Coming Petit Beau Barber Lounge is just the first stop.
Over the coming weeks, we'll be pulling up chairs at barbershops across District 20's 14 cities, from Oakland Park to Pompano to Tamarac and beyond, to keep having these conversations in the neighborhoods where they matter most.
More stops are coming across District 20 — follow along so you don't miss them.
