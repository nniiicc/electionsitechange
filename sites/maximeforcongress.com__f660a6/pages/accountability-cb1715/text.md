On the Issues
Why this matters
Too many politicians get elected, disappear to Washington, and only come home to campaign.
Voters can't easily see how the money is being spent.
They can't easily see how their representative is voting on the things that matter.
And they can't easily reach the person who is supposed to be working for them.
That's not democracy — that's absence.
