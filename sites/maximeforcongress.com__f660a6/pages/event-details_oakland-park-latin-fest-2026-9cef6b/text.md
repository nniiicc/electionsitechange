About the event
Dr.
Maxime is looking forward to joining the City of Oakland Park for Latin Fest 2026, a free community celebration of Latin American culture, music, and food at Jaco Pastorius Park.
The evening will feature live entertainment from Salsabor All Stars, DJ Chris, and the Florida Elite Event Dancers, along with food and drinks from a lineup of local vendors serving Mexican, Argentine, and Colombian favorites.
Family activities will include a Sensory Village designed for children with sensory needs and a Kid's Kitchen offering hands-on cooking fun for young festivalgoers.
Admission is free and open to the public.
