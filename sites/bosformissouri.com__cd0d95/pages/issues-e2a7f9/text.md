Education
I believe every student deserves access to a strong, well-funded education, no matter their zip code.
That means investing more in our public schools, especially in rural and underserved communities that have been left behind for too long.
I support increasing teacher pay to attract and retain quality educators, improving classroom resources, and expanding opportunities for students through career and technical programs.
I also believe we should reduce the financial burden of higher education by making public college more affordable and exploring pathways toward tuition-free undergraduate programs.
Education isn’t just an expense, it’s an investment in our future, our workforce, and our communities.

Affordability
I believe every individual deserves a fair shot at stability and prosperity.
That means addressing the rising cost of everyday essentials like housing, childcare, healthcare, groceries, and energy.
I support policies that strengthen wages, lower out-of-pocket costs, and make it easier for families/individuals to get ahead, not just get by.
I’m committed to fighting for affordable housing, more accessible childcare options, and fair pricing practices that protect families/individuals from being priced out of their own communities.
When working class missourians have breathing room in their budgets, our entire economy and community grow stronger.

Small farms growth
I believe small farms are the backbone of our rural communities and deserve strong support and protection.
I’m committed to policies that help family farms grow and compete by expanding access to fair markets, improving infrastructure, and protecting them from being pushed out by large corporate operations.
That means standing up against monopolistic practices, ensuring fair pricing, and preserving farmland for future generations.
I also support investing in local agriculture programs and creating more opportunities for small farmers to sell directly within their communities.
When small farms succeed, our local economies, food systems, and way of life stay strong.
