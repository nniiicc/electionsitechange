Meet Bradley Issues News Volunteer Contribute Meet Bradley My name is Bradley Bos, and I’m running to represent Missouri’s 157th District because I believe our communities deserve leadership that listens and works for the people.
I’m a husband, a coach, and a working Missourian who understands the everyday challenges families face.
My wife recently graduated from nursing school, and watching her dedication to helping others has only strengthened my belief that serving our community is one of the most important things we can do.
For years, I’ve been involved in my community—whether it’s coaching youth sports, working with young people, or helping bring people together around the issues that matter most.
Those experiences have shown me that our communities are strongest when we support one another and when leaders are willing to stand beside the people they represent.
I’m running for office because I believe our district deserves someone who will fight for working families, farmers, small businesses, and the next generation.
My campaign is built around the idea of “Missouri First”—putting the needs of our communities ahead of politics and focusing on practical solutions that make life better for the people who live here.
This campaign isn’t about politics as usual.
It’s about making sure the voices of the people in District 157 are heard loud and clear in Jefferson City.
