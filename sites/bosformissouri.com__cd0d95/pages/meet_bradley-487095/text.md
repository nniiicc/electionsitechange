Meet Bradley Issues News Volunteer Contribute Meet Bradley Bos I'm a working-class Missourian who decided it was time to step up.
My name is Bradley Bos, and I'm running to represent Missouri's 157th House District because I believe our communities deserve a voice that understands what everyday people are going through.
I'm a husband, a father of three, a school bus driver, a baseball coach, and someone who has spent years working hard to provide for my family.
I've spent a decade working in manufacturing, including time as a machine-shop supervisor and senior operator.
I know what it means to work hard, stretch a paycheck, and worry about whether you're doing enough to give your family a better future.
I also know what it's like to struggle.
I grew up knowing what it meant to need a little help to get by.
My family relied on assistance at times, and those experiences shaped the person I am today.
They taught me that nobody should be ashamed of struggling and that a little help at the right time can make a world of difference.
That's a big part of why I'm running.
I'm running because our kids deserve fully funded public schools and teachers who have the resources they need.
Working families deserve fair wages and an economy that doesn't leave rural Missouri behind.
Our farmers, small businesses, and local communities deserve a state government that listens to them instead of just looking out for the people with the most money and influence.
But this campaign is about more than policies and political parties.
It's about people.
It's about making sure our neighbors have a fair shot, our children have opportunities we might not have had, and the people making decisions in Jefferson City understand the lives of the people those decisions affect.
I'm not asking anyone to agree with me on everything.
I'm asking people to look at where I come from, what I stand for, and whether I'm willing to listen and fight for them.
I believe good ideas can come from Democrats, Republicans, and independents alike.
Our communities should always come before party politics.
I didn't get into this race because I wanted a political title.
I got into it because I believe we can do better, and I refuse to believe rural Missouri has to settle for less.
I'm a neighbor, a working dad, and an activist who believes in putting community first.
I'm Bradley Bos, and I'm running to be your voice in Jefferson City.
