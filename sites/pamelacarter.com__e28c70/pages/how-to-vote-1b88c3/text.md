LD4 voting guide
How to vote in Legislative District 4.
Arizona votes early and by mail.
Here are the dates, the ID rules, and the official tools — all in one place.
Key dates
Your 2026 election calendar
- Monday, October 5 Voter registration deadline Last day to register or update your address for this election.
- Wednesday, October 7 Early ballots mail Ballots go out to voters on the Active Early Voting List.
Early in-person voting opens.
- Friday, October 23 Last day to request a mailed replacement ballot After this, request a ballot in person at a vote center.
- Tuesday, October 27 Mail your ballot back by this date Maricopa County's recommended deadline so your ballot arrives in time.
- Friday, October 30 Early in-person voting ends Last day to vote early at a Maricopa County vote center.
- Tuesday, November 3 Election Day Vote centers open 6 a.m. to 7 p.m.
Ballots must be received by 7 p.m.
All dates are for the 2026 Arizona general election and are shown in Arizona time.
Confirm details with Maricopa County Elections before you vote.
