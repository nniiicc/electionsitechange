Chapter One Four generations under the Arizona sun.
My great-grandfather immigrated from Mexico to work the copper mines near Globe — and became a legal U.S. citizen.
That story shaped me: legal immigration, hard work, and the dignity that comes from earning your place.
I was raised in Arcadia, in the heart of Phoenix.
I went to Arizona schools, learned Arizona values, and never forgot what it took for my family to build here.
Chapter Two Building a business.
Building a national voice.
I founded and ran a sports medicine and performance company in Scottsdale — a real business with payroll, contracts, and a reputation to protect.
Leading that operation taught me how to balance a budget, manage people, and deliver results under pressure.
That foundation led to Get in Shape with Pamela Carter, a national television program I hosted, produced, and directed for 18 years on TBN — more than 2,000 episodes.
I interviewed authors, political leaders, and pastors from across the country.
The experience sharpened my ability to communicate clearly, listen carefully, and lead with conviction.
Chapter Three Faith first.
Family always.
I hold a Master's degree in Communications and Biblical Studies.
I'm an ordained minister under Window of Heaven International.
I serve on the board of Help 4 Kidz, reaching at-risk youth here in Arizona.
Faith isn't a footnote on a campaign biography for me.
It's the ground I stand on, the way I treat people, and the reason I stand up for parental rights, religious freedom, and the protection of children online.
