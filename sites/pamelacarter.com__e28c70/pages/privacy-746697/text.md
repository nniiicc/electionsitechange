Privacy policy.
Pamela4AZ (“the campaign”) respects your privacy.
This policy explains what we collect through pamelacarter.com and how we use it.
Information we collect
We collect only what you give us: your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, ZIP code, volunteer preferences, and any message you send through our forms.
How we use it
We use your information to respond to you, coordinate volunteering, deliver yard signs, send campaign updates you asked for, and comply with campaign finance reporting requirements.
Text and email consent
If you opt in to calls or text messages, message and data rates may apply and message frequency may vary.
Messaging may include requests for donations.
Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.
You can unsubscribe from campaign email at any time using the link in any message.
Donations
Contributions are processed by Anedot.
Payment card details are entered on Anedot's secure checkout and are never stored by this website.
Contributions to a political campaign are not tax-deductible, and required contributor information is reported as Arizona law requires.
Sharing
We do not sell your personal information.
We share it only with campaign staff, volunteers, and vendors who help us operate the campaign, and where required by law.
