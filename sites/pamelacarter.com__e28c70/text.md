As a Christian, I believe we are each called to use our gifts in the service of others.
In my first term I hit the ground running — protecting deployed service members, championing Arizona's nuclear energy future, and sponsoring Cade's Law to hold accountable those who coerce minors toward suicide online.
Despite a divided government, I earned the Vice Chair seat on Public Safety & Law Enforcement — and used it to advance safe communities, parental rights, low taxes, secure elections, and local control.
I don't believe government has all the answers, but I do believe elected leaders should show up, listen, and get the small things right — because for the family waiting on a permit, the veteran navigating benefits, or the small-business owner watching the next regulation, one bureaucratic delay is one too many.
