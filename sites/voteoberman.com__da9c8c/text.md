My Values What I Will Do In the Indiana House Stop GIVING DATA CENTERS A BLANK CHECK I believe in no handouts for huge corporations.
No backroom deals.
No higher utility bills for working families.
No projects that put our land, water, and communities at risk.
The world's largest technology companies are pushing to build massive data centers across Indiana, often with generous tax breaks for the corporations, enormous energy demands, and very little transparency or long-term benefit for the communities expected to host them.
This community deserves leaders who are willing to ask hard questions, not politicians who rubber-stamp corporate giveaways at the expense of working families and call it progress.
Strengthen Safe & Thriving Communities I wore a badge to protect children — now I want to use the law to do the same.
As Indiana State Representative, I will introduce legislation to elevate crimes against children to felonies and limit plea bargains.
Rein In Utility Costs Families in Northwest Indiana are being priced out of their own homes by skyrocketing utility bills.
What I will want to happen: Stronger oversight of NIPSCO and freeze utility rate increases, which have skyrocketed up to nearly 20% since 2023 Transparency and accountability in billing Make data centers pay their share and not pass costs on to communities Champion Small Businesses We need to level the playing field so small businesses in Northwest Indiana can operate as easily as large corporations do.
Our neighbors' businesses deserve fair taxes, reasonable regulations, and access to resources.
Support Public Safety & First Responders Public safety starts with taking care of the people who take care of us.
My law enforcement experience gives me firsthand insight into what first responders actually need: Pay first responders wages that allow them to live in the communities they serve, commensurate with the service they selflessly provide Ongoing, proactive mental health care—not just after tragedy Modern training and tools to meet rapidly changing challenges Protect Public Schools & Respect Teachers Indiana succeeds when public education is strong and teachers are respected and supported.
Make Indiana attractive for new graduates and experienced teachers alike by paying a living, competitive wage Oppose voucher programs that drain funding from public education Public schools mean we have elected school boards and local control Defend Strong Unions & The Middle Class Northwest Indiana was built on hard work and skilled labor.
My family history in organized labor shapes my commitment to standing with working people.
Protect collective bargaining rights Support fair wages, safe workplaces, and job security Ensure future generations have access to union careers Support the Movement Tax revenues from the big industry in NWI are sent down state to fund the rest of Indiana.
Meanwhile, Indianapolis politicians are using our hard-earned tax dollars for luxury vehicles, while defunding our police, fire, municipalities, libraries, and schools.
