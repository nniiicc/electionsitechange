Donate Here Home Policy Agenda Contact Home Policy Agenda Contact More Home Policy Agenda Contact Biography Jeff Cooley is a proud father, husband, and Quality Engineer who has lived in Michigan HD-35 his entire life.
A graduate of North Adams - Jerome Public Schools, he understands the needs of rural communities.
After speaking with many residents in the district, he has consistently heard that people are tired of the noise in Lansing and are looking for real problem-solvers in the legislature.
With over 30 years of experience on the factory floor, Cooley has built a career around identifying what is broken and fixing it for good, and he is committed to bringing that same approach to Lansing.
As your next State Representative, Cooley will work to expand access to health care, lower costs for everyday Michiganders, and protect the right to vote for all citizens, regardless of political preference.
The campaign will operate without accepting contributions from corporate PACs or utility companies such as DTE.
