Support Our Mission Get Involved Meet Hilary.
Hilary Trudell has spent her career serving communities, bringing people together, and standing up for our rights and freedoms.
She has worked across the country to train community leaders, teach future public servants, and lead organizations that help people work together.
Through it all, Hilary has learned that we make real progress when we listen to each other, find common ground, and have the courage to stand up for what matters. ​ For Hilary, East Tennessee is home.
She and her family chose to return here to raise their son near the mountains, communities, and people they love.
Now, as a mom and candidate for Tennessee House District 8, Hilary is thinking about the Tennessee her son—and all of our kids—will inherit.
She’s running to protect our public schools, support working families, and make sure everyone has a voice in the decisions that affect our lives.
At a time when politics too often divides us, Hilary believes we need leaders who will show up, listen, and work across our differences to build a better future for all of us.
Key Issues For Our Kids We are committed to pushing vouchers OUT of Blount County.
They are a misuse of taxpayer's hard earned cash and deplete funds from our rural communities.
Instead of sending millions of tax-payer dollars to private institutions, we should be investing in our public schools.
Our mission is to ensure that every student in District 8 has access to a high-quality, public education.

For Our Community Data centers are being built in communities before anyone has time to regulate them.
This puts our jobs, environment, and resources at great risk.
Everyone who lives here deserves a say in how we grow.
We will let politicians in Nashville know that Blount County is not for sale but working to stop the development of all new data centers is paused until stricter regulations - including job protection - are in place.

For Our Future Tennessee is one of only 13 states with a tax on groceries.
Our grocery tax rate is the 6th highest in the nation.
This puts the heaviest burden on low-income households that spend a higher portion of their income on taxes in Tennessee than any other group.
We all deserve to be able to feed our families.
We will work to repeal the grocery tax, putting more money back in the pockets of Blount County families so they can do more than make ends meet—they can thrive.
