November 14, 2017
I am pleased to allocate $2,000 of my Council Office’s Fiscal Year 2018 Discretionary TOT funds to support the Athenaeum.
The funding would support an Organizational Support Program by funding Ritmos Mestizos.
A music and dance series of three events designed to celebrate Latino and Chicano culture.
I respectfully request that a total of $2,000 be transferred from Council District 8 TOT Fund: 2000205, Cost Center: 1414111219, GL: 512059 and be used to augment the existing contract between the City and Athenaeum.
If you have any questions or need further information, please contact Gabriela Dominguez on my staff at 619-236-6688.
