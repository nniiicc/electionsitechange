Committee members requested the City Attorney address questions related to meet and confer obligations.
In his response (attached), the City Attorney attached a draft ordinance covering unrepresented employees and stated that " ... we see no legal impediment to this moving forward to City Council for adoption in February as an ordinance and reinforcement as a voter-approved proposition in June to ensure that it cannot be amended or rescinded in the future without voter approval." I would request that this ordinance be docketed for City Council consideration in February.
