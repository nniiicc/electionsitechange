October 13, 2017
I am pleased to allocate $2,500 of my Council Office's Fiscal Year 2018 Discretionary TOT funds to support Mainly Mozart Inc.
The funding would fund an Organizational Support Program by supporting Mainly Mozart mission to enrich the lives of music lovers by connecting them to the innovations of Mozart and the Masters.
I respectfully request that a total of $2,500 be transferred from Council District 8 TOT Fund: 2000205, Cost Center: 1414111219, GL: 512059 and be used to augment the existing contract between the City and Mainly Mozart Inc.
If you have any questions or need further information, please contact Gabriela Dominguez on my staff at 619-236-6688.
