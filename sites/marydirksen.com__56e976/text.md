About Mary
Shaped by decades of public service, I’m running for State Senate to restore balance and accountability to Oregon’s 19th Senate District.
After earning dual degrees from Portland State University, I spent 24 years serving Oregonians—most recently as a Senior Policy Analyst.
My leadership spans human trafficking advisory panels, the Tualatin Budget Committee, and a nonprofit board supporting foster youth.
I volunteered for 15 years at Coffee Creek Correctional Facility and am a certified Master Gardener.
I met my husband on a mission trip to Cambodia, and together we explore the world with our children, nurturing curiosity and compassion.
My campaign is rooted in service, faith, and a deep commitment to a better Oregon.
My Priorities
- Public Safety & Victims’ Rights Families deserve to feel safe in their homes, neighborhoods, and communities.
I will support law enforcement, strengthen consequences for repeat offenders, and fight for policies that put victims first.
I believe Oregon needs accountability and appropriate remedies to address crime, addiction, and hazards on our streets.
- Accountability & Lower Taxes Government should serve people—not burden them.
I will fight to rein in wasteful spending, demand transparency for every tax dollar, and pursue meaningful tax relief.
My own family felt the squeeze of record-high taxes in 2024, and I know Oregon families need relief now.
- Education & Parental Involvement Our students deserve better outcomes, stronger attendance, and higher standards.
I will push for curriculum transparency, increased parental involvement, and a renewed focus on foundational learning.
We must prioritize student success above politics.
- Economic Growth & Cost of Living The cost of living and doing business in Oregon has become unsustainable.
I will champion tax relief, and support for small businesses so Oregon can become a place where companies stop leaving and start choosing again.
A strong economy begins with empowering the people who drive it.
