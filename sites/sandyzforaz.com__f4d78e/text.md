Get in Touch Candidate for LD1 State Representative for Arizona Sandy is a 30-year resident of Arizona and has lived in the Prescott Area for 9 years.
Sandy is a grandmother, Veteran, Bible study facilitator, Distinguished Toastmaster, candidate for office, widow, and non-MAGA Christian.
She comes from a rural area of Colorado.
So, she understands the rural lifestyle.
She has an Associate Degree in Electronics and a Bachelor's Degree in Bible and Theology.
Her faith defines who she is.
She has a real love for the poor and disenfranchised because that's what her faith demands.
What do I stand for?
As a Veteran I understand the importance of good benefits for our Veterans.
They served us now it's time for us to serve them.
There's a shortage of affordable housing in Arizona.
We needs sensible solutions to the rising cost of living.
We all need affordable healthcare.
Healthcare costs are rising and someone needs to fight so everyone can afford to go to the doctor.
Our schools are under attack.
Our kids, no matter what sex, income status, or disabilities.
Children deserve the best education where they are safe.
Salaries are not keeping up with rising costs.
It's important that we have good jobs in Arizona.
As we get older we need a dignified retirement where we don't have to worry about whether we will eat or afford our medications.
