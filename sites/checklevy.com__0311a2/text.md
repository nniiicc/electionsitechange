Learn More MEET VELERIA Experienced Leadership Veleria M.
Levy is a nonprofit leader, health equity advocate, and community servant who believes public office begins with one simple practice: you lead best when you're accountable to the people you serve.
Her campaign for NC House District 99 is built on a Circle of Service — showing up, listening, delivering, and inviting the community to "Check Levy" when something isn't right.
She believes leadership is a shared responsibility, and strong representation requires transparency, humility, and community partnership. ​ As a mother, legal scholar, and longtime voice for Black and Brown communities across North Carolina, Veleria has dedicated her career to helping families navigate systems that too often overlook them.
She has led statewide health equity work, strengthened community organizations, and built coalitions grounded in dignity, access, and opportunity.
