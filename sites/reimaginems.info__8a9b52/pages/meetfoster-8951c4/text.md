Bennie Foster is a Jackson native, educator, mentor, and community advocate.
A former student-athlete and SWAC Track & Field Champion at Jackson State University, Bennie holds a Bachelors degree from Jackson State and a Masters Degree in Education from Strayer University.
As a builder of community transformation, Bennie brings over 38 years of experience in educational administration, business consulting, personal development, and local program leadership.
Together with his wife, and the ReIMAGINE Mississippi team he works directly across the Mississippi 2nd District to establish sustainable support systems that empower families, strengthen communities, and build real pathways out of poverty.
Running as an Independent, Bennie is committed to deliverable outcomes over partisan gridlock.
Bennie Foster is the catalyst behind numerous organizations and business initiatives that transcend traditional charity.
He builds sustainable pathways out of poverty, dismantles the root causes of crime and violence, and fosters economic ecosystems that empower individuals to thrive.
He understands that true progress is not about handouts, but about hand-ups – providing the tools, resources, support, and opportunities for communities to realize their full potential.
Bennie Foster didn't just observe the challenges facing Mississippi's 2nd Congressional District; he immersed himself in them.
He listened, learned, and acted, transforming firsthand experience into a mandate for change.
He is not a politician seeking office, but a community leader answering a call to service because the constituents’ voices have not been heard for decades.
Bennie's Foster's accolades – Top 50 Mississippi Men in Business, Numerous Servant/ Leader Awards and recognition from Oakley Youth Development Center, and Job Corps – are not mere awards; they are markers of his unwavering commitment to service and impact.
His mentorship is not just guidance; it's a catalyst for transformation, igniting potential in youth and adults alike, both locally and globally.
Bennie Foster is not offering empty promises; he is presenting a proven track record of tangible results, results over rhetoric.
He is not seeking power; he is seeking partnerships.
He is not just a candidate; he is a visionary leader, a bridge between communities, and the new champion for Mississippi's 2nd Congressional District.
He is ready to bring his experience, his passion, resilience and resolve with his unwavering dedication to the halls of U.S.
Congress representing MS02, ensuring that every voice is heard and every community is empowered as MS02 choose results over rhetoric at the polls.
