Meet Vicky Wors
Originally from Texas, I have called Missouri home for 37 years and have lived in Franklin County for the past seven years.
I am a semi-retired business consultant specializing in workforce development and management.
I hold a Bachelor of Business Administration from the University of North Texas and a Master of Science from Lindenwood University in St.
Charles, Missouri.
Throughout my 35-year career, I worked for major organizations including Emerson, Coca-Cola, Schlumberger, The Earthgrains Company, and Anheuser-Busch.
I later transitioned into consulting, serving small and midsized organizations throughout the St.
Louis area.
During my years working with unionized organizations, I successfully negotiated more than 80 labor agreements without a single work stoppage or unfair labor practice claim.
I worked to ensure employees had safe working environments while developing competitive compensation and benefits programs for both salaried and hourly employees.
I also built strong communication strategies designed to improve working relationships throughout organizations.
After semi-retiring, I relocated to Union, Missouri, in 2019.
I have been a member of of several civic organizations, including the Washington Elks Lodge and the Washington Lions Club.
I enjoy golf and pickleball and attend the Presbyterian Church of Washington.
I was widowed several years ago when my husband, Jim, passed away from cancer.
