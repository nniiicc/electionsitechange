Reform California is leading the fight to qualify and pass the Voter ID Initiative on the 2026 ballot.
At this free event, you’ll learn exactly how you can help make sure every vote cast in California is legal and secure.
- What is the California Voter ID Initiative?
This initiative will require voters to present valid government-issued photo identification when voting in person and to include a verified identification number when voting by mail.
- Why does it matter?
It will help prevent voter fraud, ensure only eligible citizens vote, and increase public trust in election results.
- What will the town hall cover?
- The exact language of the proposed measure
- The timeline to collect signatures and qualify it for the 2026 ballot
- Volunteer opportunities to help gather signatures
- Ways to contribute financially to the campaign
