Mayor Jerry Dyer Endorses David Tangipa for State Assembly
RELEASED January 10, 2023
Contact: david@davidtangipaforassembly.com
(559) 425-6520
FRESNO - Today, David Tangipa announced that Fresno Mayor Jerry Dyer endorsed his candidacy for Assembly District 8.
“David Tangipa has the experience, energy, and tenacity to address California’s homeless, affordable housing, and public safety crises,” said Mayor Dyer.
“He will be a strong representative for Fresno in the State Assembly, and I’m honored to join Jim Patterson, Lisa Smittcamp, Sheriff Zanoni, Sheriff Mims, and many others to support his candidacy.”
