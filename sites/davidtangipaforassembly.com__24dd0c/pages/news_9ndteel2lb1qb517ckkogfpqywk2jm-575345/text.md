The Fresno Deputy Sheriff’s Association and California Correctional Peace Officers Association join Fresno County District Attorney Lisa Smittcamp, Fresno County Sheriff John Zanoni, Fresno County Sheriff Margaret Mims (ret.), Calaveras County Sheriff Rick DiBasilio, and Inyo County Sheriff Jeff Hollowell (ret.) in making Tangipa the clear public safety choice in this race.
“We are proud to support David Tangipa for Assembly District 8.
