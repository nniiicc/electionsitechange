Fresno Deputy Sheriff’s Association and California Correctional Peace Officers Association Endorse David Tangipa
RELEASED October 12th, 2023
Contact: david@davidtangipaforassembly.com
(559) 425-6520
FRESNO - Today, David Tangipa announced that the Fresno Deputy Sheriff’s Association and the California Correctional Peace Officers Association endorsed his bid for State Assembly.
The Fresno Deputy Sheriff’s Association and California Correctional Peace Officers Association join Fresno County District Attorney Lisa Smittcamp, Fresno County Sheriff John Zanoni, Fresno County Sheriff Margaret Mims (ret.), Calaveras County Sheriff Rick DiBasilio, and Inyo County Sheriff Jeff Hollowell (ret.) in making Tangipa the clear public safety choice in this race.
“We are proud to support David Tangipa for Assembly District 8.
David is the public safety candidate in this race and will fight for common sense solutions to empower law enforcement and keep Californians safe.” - Fresno Deputy Sheriff’s Association
