Not Just Talk, Real Results.
Brandon Davis is one of us.
He’s a father, small business owner, and volunteer who has spent years working to make our community stronger.
Brandon serves on the CCSD Attendance Zone Advisory Commission and local school associations, volunteers with youth programs and his church, and leads a Las Vegas-based digital marketing business that helps other Nevada businesses grow.
He’s running for Assembly District 34 because Nevada families deserve leaders who understand the challenges of everyday life like rising costs, struggling schools, and healthcare that’s out of reach for far too many.
He founded a nonprofit to protect your tax dollars, and has lived through every cost increase you’ve felt.
He’ll bring common sense to Carson City and fight to lower the costs of living, healthcare, and housing, strengthen education, and create good-paying jobs.
It’s time we have representatives who actually fight for the people who live here.
Brandon shows up.
He gets things done.
He’s the leader Nevada Assembly District 34 needs.
