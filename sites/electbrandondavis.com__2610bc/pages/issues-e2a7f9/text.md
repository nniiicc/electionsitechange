Real results.
Built around what working Nevada families need.
Lowering the Cost of Living Cut hidden fees and surprise costs.
Give Nevada families real relief on the bills they pay every week.
Groceries, gas, rent, insurance, utilities.
It all adds up fast, and your paycheck does not stretch like it used to.
My wife and I are raising two kids while running small businesses.
We feel the same pressure you do.
The answer is not more taxes or another tone-deaf policy out of Carson City.
The answer is concrete relief: cut hidden fees and surprise costs, build fuel infrastructure that lowers gas prices and reduces our dependence on California, and push smart reforms that bring everyday costs down across the board.
Affordability is about dignity.
Every Nevadan should be able to work, save, and live without feeling one surprise bill away from crisis.
STRENGTHENING EDUCATION Get dollars into classrooms, recruit and keep good teachers, lower class sizes, and give parents real options.
Nevada ranks near the bottom nationally in student achievement.
As a Representative on the Clark County School District Attendance Zone Advisory Commission, an elected member of our elementary school’s Strategic Organizational Team, and an active PTA volunteer, I see the cracks up close.
I am a public school parent.
I want public schools to work.
That starts with getting more dollars into classrooms by capping the growth of non-classroom positions, recruiting and retaining the teachers we have, and bringing class sizes back down.
It also means giving parents real options when their kids need something different, which is why I support expanding education savings accounts and opportunity scholarships.
We owe our kids both a strong public education and the flexibility to choose what works.
Every child deserves a real shot at a quality education.
Quality Affordable Health Care Lower prescription drug costs, end surprise billing, transparent prices, and more providers in-state.
I have experienced the health care system as a husband, a father, and a small business owner.
Working families in Nevada do not need a brief from Carson City to tell them what is broken.
They live it every month: prescription costs that keep climbing, surprise bills that show up weeks after a procedure, hospitals that will not tell you what something costs until after they have done it, and not enough doctors and nurses in the state to meet demand.
The fix is access, transparency, and competition.
Lower prescription drug costs.
End surprise billing.
Require transparent pricing.
Build Nevada’s medical education pipeline so we train more doctors and nurses here and keep them serving the communities they grew up in.
Expand direct primary care, grow access to Health Savings Accounts, grow telehealth in rural and underserved areas, and grow the Private Mutual Health Insurance market to lower insurance premiums.
GROWING NEVADA’S ECONOMY Cut red tape, lower taxes, and back working Nevadans and the small businesses that hire them.
Nevada’s economy should work for the people who actually keep it running: working families, entrepreneurs, and small business owners.
Not the politically connected.
As a CEO who has built, bought, and sold businesses for over a decade, I know what it takes to create jobs that pay, and I know how much harder Carson City makes it for the small businesses that create them.
We will reduce the tax burden on small business, streamline licensing, expand the Regulatory Sandbox to diversify our economy, and back the kind of practical reforms that let people who do the work keep more of what they earn.
Nevada should be the best place in the country to build a business and raise a family.
We are going to fight for that.
Making Housing Affordable Faster permitting, smart zoning reform, and a real path from renting to owning.
I have been a renter.
I have worked two jobs to cover rent in my twenties.
I have also become a homeowner, and I know what it means to build equity and care about your neighborhood.
Nevada’s housing market is broken.
First-time buyers are priced out, renters cannot keep up, and property owners are getting squeezed.
We will support responsible zoning reform, faster permitting, and incentives for smart development that respects existing communities.
We will protect homeowners from rising costs and create real pathways for working Nevadans to move from renting to owning.
Housing should be a ladder, not a dead end.
