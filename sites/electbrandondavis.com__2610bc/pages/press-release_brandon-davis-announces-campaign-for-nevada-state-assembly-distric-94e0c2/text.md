Local Business Owner, Father, and Community Leader Vows to Bring Common-Sense Solutions Back to Carson City
Las Vegas, NV – Well-known community leader and business owner Brandon Davis announced his candidacy for Nevada Assembly District 34 as a Republican today, pledging to bring results that matter to the State Legislature.
Davis, who came within striking distance of flipping the seat in 2024, says he’s running because Nevada families deserve more than political theater and empty promises.
“This race isn’t about partisan games or being some figurehead, it’s about results for the people of Nevada,” said Davis.
“I’m tired of watching state legislators push the same failing policies and ideologies every session, requiring our Governor to break veto records.
It’s time we have representatives who actually fight for the people who live here.”
Davis brings a rare mix of private-sector success and public service to the table.
He’s the CEO of a successful Las Vegas-based digital marketing agency and represents District E on the Clark County School District’s Attendance Zone Advisory Commission (AZAC).
He also volunteers with local nonprofits and founded a Nevada-centered watchdog group focused on exposing government waste.
Davis is running against incumbent Hanadi Nadeem, who he says, “failed to deliver results in the 2025 Legislative session, prioritized virtue-signaling bills over the serious issues Nevadans are facing, and has completely ghosted the community she was elected to represent.”
His campaign is focused on five key priorities: growing Nevada’s economy and supporting small businesses, lowering the cost of living for working families, and strengthening education with more accountability and better options for parents.
He’s also pushing for practical, affordable health care solutions and creating more attainable housing to make it easier for families and seniors to afford a place to live.
These aren’t talking points, they’re real problems Brandon’s lived, and real solutions he’s ready to fight for.
