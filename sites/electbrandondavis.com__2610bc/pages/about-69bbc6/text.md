Brandon Davis is a husband, a father, and a Las Vegas small business owner running for Nevada State Assembly District 34 because the working families of this district deserve a representative who shows up and lives what they live.
Brandon was born into a working-class family of factory workers and raised by a single mother.
He learned early that no one is coming to save you.
You work hard.
You look out for your neighbors.
You fight for what matters.
That grit has guided him ever since.
Brandon is the founder and CEO of MNGR, a Las Vegas-based digital marketing agency he launched in 2015.
Over the past decade he has helped thousands of small businesses across Nevada and the country grow and thrive while navigating the same red tape that keeps so many others stuck.
He signs payroll every two weeks.
He knows what it costs to keep a small business open in this state.
Brandon is also a husband to Rebecca and the father of two children in elementary school.
He has been a renter, working two jobs to cover rent in his twenties, and he is now a homeowner who knows what it took to get there.
As an appointed representative of District E on the Clark County School District Attendance Zone Advisory Commission, an elected member of his children’s elementary school Strategic Organizational Team, and an active PTA volunteer, Brandon is a public-school parent who sees the cracks in our education system up close and is doing something about it.
His community work runs deep.
Brandon is a volunteer youth sports coach, a former Vice President of Fund Development at Assisting Lives in Las Vegas, the largest 100% volunteer-run charity in Southern Nevada, and the founder and president of DOGE NEVADA, a Nevada nonprofit protecting taxpayers, pushing accountability, and bringing transparency to how the state spends our money.
He is a proud Christian and a follower of Jesus Christ who volunteers at his church.
Now, Brandon is running to make Nevada more affordable, more accountable, and more responsive to working families.
He is focused on lowering the cost of living, expanding access to quality affordable health care, strengthening education, making housing attainable, and growing a Nevada economy that rewards hard work, not connections.
He is not a career politician.
He is a neighbor, a parent, and a leader who believes that with honesty, hard work, and common sense, Nevada’s best days are still ahead.
