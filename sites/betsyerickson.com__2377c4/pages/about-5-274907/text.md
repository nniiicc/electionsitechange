Hi, I'm Betsy!
I am a lifelong Wyomingite.
I was born and raised in Casper, Wyoming.
I graduated from Natrona County High School in 2008 and from the University of Wyoming at Casper College with a BA in English Literature in 2013.
Shortly after graduating from university, my husband and I got married at Fort Caspar.
I earned my MA in Education from St.
Catherine's University in 2018.
My husband and I have two wonderful children, a dog and a cat.
We enjoy attending community events as well as spending time in nature.
We especially enjoy fishing and camping around Wyoming.
I'm not a lifelong politician.
I've been a teacher, a parent, a volunteer, a neighbor, and a community advocate.
Public service isn't something I discovered when I decided to run for office—it's something I've been doing for years.
I decided to run for office because I believe that it is time for the next generation to step in to help build the future of Wyoming.
My Work and Volunteer Experience
- I worked as a preschool teacher for most of ten years.
Two of those ten years I ran my own preschool business in my home.
- I currently work as a digital communications coordinator for WyoUnited Education Project.
- I volunteer with Meals on Wheels.
- I am the board secretary for WyoUnited.
- I was the Parent Teacher Co-op President of Woods Learning Center.
- I serve on the Natrona County Historic Preservation Commission.
- I am a member of The CUSP (Casper Unity and Solidarity Project).
