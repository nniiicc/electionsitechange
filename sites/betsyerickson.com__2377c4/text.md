Betsy Erickson
Building Wyoming's Future.
A lifelong Wyomingite.
An educator.
A mom.
A neighbor.
A community volunteer, and a candidate for House District 37.
I’m running because I believe Wyoming deserves leadership rooted in honesty, hard work, integrity, and a genuine commitment to our communities.
I love Wyoming, and I believe we can protect what makes it special while building a future where our kids and grandkids can thrive.
What I Believe:
- Communities work best when we look out for one another and invest in them.
- Our public lands, our wildlife, and outdoor heritage belong to all of us.
I will fight to protect them for the next generation.
- Government works best when people can trust their representatives to tell the truth, listen, and keep their word.
- Hardworking people built Wyoming, and will continue to do so.
- Our public schools are the foundation of strong communities and a strong Wyoming economy.
- Wyomingites should have the freedom to make personal decisions without unnecessary interference from the government.
