Putting North Little Rock and Sherwood First
In March, the people of North Little Rock and Sherwood elected me to represent our community at the State Capitol.
It has been the honor of my life.

Fighting for a Future You Can Afford
I’m focused on the costs families feel every day—not political distractions that do nothing to improve their lives.
North Little Rock and Sherwood are my priorities:
- I’m working to:
- Cut taxes for working Arkansans
- Provide meaningful property tax relief for homeowners
- Expand quality childcare and pre-K so children can learn and parents can work
- Pursue practical solutions as the costs of groceries, housing, utilities, and insurance continue to rise
- My professional career has been dedicated to strengthening Arkansas’s healthcare workforce.
In the legislature, I’m working to:
- Prevent clinics and hospitals from closing
- Recruit more healthcare professionals to Arkansas
- Encourage health insurance providers to compete for your business
- Expand access to affordable preventive and long-term care
- Protect families from rising healthcare and insurance costs
- The seniors in our community spent their lives building the places we call home.
They deserve security, quality care, and the ability to age with dignity.
Earlier this year, state leaders moved to end a program that helps prevent senior hunger.
After hearing from people across our community, I worked with colleagues to get the program reinstated.
That is the kind of focused advocacy District 70 deserves.
I’m continuing to work for:
- Property tax relief that helps seniors remain in their homes
- Incentives to attract quality, affordable long-term care providers
- Better access to preventive care and aging-at-home services
- Tax credits for in-home caregivers and adults caring for aging parents
- Partnerships that help seniors and families manage rising grocery and utility costs
