Campaign Kick-off May 3 May 21 Written By Brooke Dillon Brooke Dillon is running for the Ohio House of Representatives (District 77) on a strong progressive platform -- and I would love for you to meet her!
She's kicking off her campaign on Sunday, May 3 from 2-4pm at Secrest Arboretum - Miller Pavilion.
More info and the map of where to park and how to get there is attached!
It's an open house and candidate meet & greet with snacks and drinks, set among the lovely plants of the arboretum.
It would be great to see you there!
