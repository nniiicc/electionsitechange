Brooke Dillon Endorsed by Ohio Federation of Teachers Jul 20 Written By Brooke Dillon The Ohio Federation of Teachers has endorsed Brooke Dillon in her campaign for election to the Ohio House of Representatives, District 77, on behalf of 20,000 active and retired members.
Our support for her candidacy is based on your dedication to public schools, higher education, libraries, and social workers: “We need people like you in office fighting for the working people of Ohio.” See also the Ohio Federation of Teachers’ list of endorsements .
