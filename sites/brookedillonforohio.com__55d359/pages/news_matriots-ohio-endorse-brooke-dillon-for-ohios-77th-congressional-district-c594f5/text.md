Matriots Ohio endorse Brooke Dillon for Ohio’s 77th congressional district
Matriots Ohio endorsed Brooke Dillon for Ohio’s 77th district, naming her as a hard-working, outstanding candidate who has demonstrated that she shares their values and is dedicated to promoting an economy in which Ohio women can thrive and prosper.
Matriots Ohio is a nonprofit, values-based organization dedicated to advancing women’s equal participation in all aspects of political life.
The Matriots was founded in 2017 by Ohio women in response to the significant underrepresentation of women in Ohio politics.
