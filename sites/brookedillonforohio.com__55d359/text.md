Brooke Dillon endorsed by the Ohio Federation of Teachers Who I am I’ve lived in the rolling hills of northeast Ohio farm country my whole life – and I want to help do what’s best for my community.
Growing up in Litchfield where my dad was a Teamster truck driver, and starting a job at 14 myself, I learned the value of hard work.
I will take those same working class values to the State House in Columbus if I’m elected.
I know Wayne County like the back of my hand.
I graduated from OSU-Agricultural Technical Institute in Equine Sciences, so I know the early mornings and tough work that goes on at the 2,084 farms in Wayne County.
And I know how hard it is for working families to afford basics like healthcare, transportation, and education.
I’ve also worked on causes that make our community stronger, healthier, and a better place to live.
I believe in the power of citizens to shape their government and I think that elected officials should be accountable to their constituents.
I’ll never take PAC money because the voice of the people should prevail over special interests.
