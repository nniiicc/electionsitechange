Senate candidate and District Attorney Scott Colom responded to a new video showing Cindy Hyde-Smith avoiding answering a serious question about Nolan Wells’ death.
Hyde-Smith has a history of avoiding questions from reporters and Mississippians.
“It’s hard to watch this video.
A reporter asked Cindy Hyde-Smith a serious question about Nolan Wells’ tragic passing, and she chose to run away and call security.
At the very least, she could have acknowledged Nolan’s family and offered her condolences for their tremendous loss.
“For everyone who knew Nolan and loved him, this is deeply disappointing.
No matter your politics, there should always be some basic humanity when talking about someone who lost his life — especially from an elected official.
Mississippi deserves better.” Earlier this week, Colom called for the release of the autopsy report and full investigative file.
