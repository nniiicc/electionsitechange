He is running for U.S.
Senate because he believes Mississippi matters, and that the people who work hard here deserve a senator who works for them.
Lower Costs Scott believes a family that works hard should be able to afford groceries, gas and the light bill.
Right now tariffs are taxing everything Mississippians buy, and a war nobody voted on is driving up what we pay at the pump.
The Constitution gives Congress the say on both.
Cindy Hyde-Smith gave Mississippi’s voice away.
In the Senate, Scott will: Vote to take back Congress’s power over tariffs Require a vote of Congress before America goes to war, because Mississippi’s service members and their families deserve that much Fight for a seat on the Agriculture Committee, so Mississippi farmers have a senator who actually answers to them – not Washington Raise Wages Scott believes work should pay fairly.
If you put in a full day, you should be able to build a life in the community where you work.
But Mississippi has the lowest household income and the highest poverty rate of any state, and too many of our neighbors work full time and still come up short at the end of the month.
The federal minimum wage has been $7.25 since 2009.
Cindy Hyde-Smith has been in Washington for eight years, and it has not moved a penny.
In the Senate, Scott will: Fight to raise the minimum wage and tie it to the cost of living, so workers never wait years and years for a raise again Work to end the federal income tax for Mississippians making under $75,000, starting with teachers and law enforcement officers Save our Healthcare and Hospitals Scott believes nobody should have to choose between seeing a doctor and paying the rent, and no family should have to drive an hour to reach an emergency room.
Cindy Hyde-Smith voted to cut about $11 billion from Mississippi’s Medicaid to pay for a billionaire tax break.
Medicaid pays for more than half the births in this state, and eight rural hospitals are at risk of closing.
She also voted against the Affordable Care Act (ACA) tax credits that kept health insurance affordable.
Today 70,000 fewer Mississippians have marketplace coverage than #ago.
In the Senate, Scott will: Vote to cancel the Medicaid cuts to help keep rural Mississippi’s emergency rooms and delivery rooms open Restore the ACA tax credits that lower insurance premiums Restore and strengthen eliminated federal ACA funding to incentivize Mississippi to finally expand Medicaid coverage Make Big Tech Pay Its Own Way Scott believes Mississippi should welcome investment, and that everybody here pays their own way, including the richest companies in the world.
Data centers are already on Mississippi power bills: about $10.60 a month for the average Entergy household, according to one independent analysis.
Cindy Hyde-Smith’s answer is a bill that does not make one company pay one dime.
It asks states to consider it, gives them two years to think about it, and writes a voluntary pledge into law.
A pledge is not protection: if a company breaks it, nothing happens and Mississippians keep paying.
Meanwhile, the AI industry’s super PAC is spending to keep her in office.
In the Senate, Scott will: Push for a pause on new data center construction until the companies agree to pay for their own power and water, show the deal before it is signed, and hire Mississippi workers in writing Make sure a real person answers when AI makes decisions about our lives Protect the right of Mississippi communities to have the final say on whether data centers come into their communities Clean up Washington Scott believes public office is a trust, not a business.
Washington has forgotten that.
Politicians trade stocks in the industries they oversee, the biggest donors get the first call, and the well-connected get richer while everybody else pays more.
Cindy Hyde-Smith is part of it.
She billed more than $10,000 in Las Vegas hotels and meals to her campaign, and she takes money from fertilizer companies under federal investigation for price-fixing.
Mississippi pays for it at the pump, at the pharmacy, at the co-op and at the checkout.
In the Senate, Scott will: Ban members of Congress, their spouses and their dependent children from trading stocks, with no loopholes Bar senators from taking money from companies under federal investigation Fund real ethics enforcement and give the Senate an ethics office with teeth Work to overturn Citizens United so senators answer to voters instead of donors Keep showing up.
Scott has held more than 50 town halls.
Cindy Hyde-Smith has not held a public town hall in more than six years Keep Mississippi Safe Scott believes everyone deserves to feel safe at home, and that nobody is above the law.
He has spent ten years as a prosecutor.
He won 94% of his trials, secured convictions in more than 15 high-profile murder cases and prosecuted a sheriff from his own party.
Cindy Hyde-Smith voted against forcing the Epstein files into the open.
In the Senate, Scott will: Back law enforcement with the pay and tools to do the job Work to secure the border and enforce our immigration laws Hold the powerful to the same law as everyone else Let's Go!
