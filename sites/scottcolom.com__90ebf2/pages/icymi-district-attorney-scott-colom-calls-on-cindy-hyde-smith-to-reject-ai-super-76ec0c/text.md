Jackson, MS — In case you missed it, after calling for an immediate pause on data center construction in Mississippi, U.S.

That’s why 63% of voters oppose a new data center being built in their community.
“Cindy says families should come first.
So, why won’t she protect Mississippi families from paying for data center power upgrades?
Maybe it has something to do with the pro-AI data center super PAC spending millions to help her campaign that she pretended to not know anything about,” said District Attorney Colom.
“Cindy, now is your chance to turn down the money and put Mississippi first.”
While Hyde-Smith refuses to back legislation to protect Mississippi consumers from cost hikes related to data centers, Colom released his plan this week to protect Mississippi consumers and workers.
