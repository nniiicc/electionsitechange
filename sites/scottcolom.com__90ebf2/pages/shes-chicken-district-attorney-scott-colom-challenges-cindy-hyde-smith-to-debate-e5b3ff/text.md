Columbus, MS — Today, outside the WCBI TV station, U.S.
Senate candidate and District Attorney Scott Colom challenged incumbent Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith to debate him in Columbus, Mississippi hosted by an outlet of her choosing.
On Tuesday in Jackson, Colom accepted invitations from two Mississippi TV stations to debate and sent Hyde-Smith’s campaign a letter asking her to respond by Friday.
He also offered to debate her at the outlet of her choice.
Hyde-Smith has not accepted.
