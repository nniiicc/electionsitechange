Mississippi Today: Pete Buttigieg to campaign for Scott Colom in Mississippi [10/13/2026] Former U.S.
Transportation Secretary Pete Buttigieg will campaign for Scott Colom, Mississippi’s Democratic Senate nominee, in cities along the Gulf Coast on Oct.

Buttigieg, in a statement, said that he is looking forward to joining Colom on the campaign trail and “seeing him elected as the next U.S.
Senator from Mississippi.” “Scott Colom is the kind of leader we need in the U.S.
Senate: rooted in his community and ready to work with anyone to deliver results,” Buttigieg said.
