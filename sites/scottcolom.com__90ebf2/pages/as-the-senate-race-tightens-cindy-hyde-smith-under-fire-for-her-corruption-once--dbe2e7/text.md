Senate candidate and District Attorney Scott Colom launched a new microsite – whereiscindyhydesmith.com – to highlight incumbent Senator Hyde-Smith’s rampant corruption and record of voting to raise costs for Mississippi families.
Mississippi voters across the political spectrum continue to sour on Hyde-Smith’s terrible record, which is why recent polling shows the race in a dead heat.
“Mississippi families are sick of Cindy Hyde-Smith betraying our state.
We deserve a leader who will fight for us, but instead, we have an absent and corrupt Senator who spends campaign funds for her personal Las Vegas vacations and votes to cut Medicaid to pay for tax breaks to billionaires,” said District Attorney Colom.
“This race is in a dead heat because unlike Cindy Hyde-Smith, I’m not afraid to talk to voters.
Voters across the political spectrum know that when I am in the Senate, my top priorities will be to lower costs for families, save our healthcare and hospitals, and bring good jobs home.” Colom’s ongoing “Where’s Cindy?” campaign highlights Hyde-Smith’s pattern of avoiding Mississippians and public events because she cannot defend her record of voting to cut Medicaid and SNAP while giving tax handouts to billionaires, blocking the release of the Epstein files, and billing more than $10,000 in Las Vegas hotel stays and meals to her campaign while Mississippi families struggle to make ends meet.
Just last week, instead of being in Mississippi talking to Mississippians who are feeling the burden of rising costs, Cindy Hyde-Smith was in Dallas with the same people who are responsible for making life unaffordable.
