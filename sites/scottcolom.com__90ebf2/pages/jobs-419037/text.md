Location Hybrid remote in Columbus, Mississippi Job Description The Scott Colom for US Senate campaign is looking to hire a full-time Finance Associate.
The campaign is seeking hard-working, self-motivated individuals to join its team through November 2026.
This is an at-will position tied to the duration of the campaign.
Responsibilities: Work with the finance team to meet fundraising goals.
Plan, develop, and execute multiple in-state and national fundraising events.
Conduct donor research to identify and analyze new prospective donors Manage the call time process, including organizing prospects in Pingdex, coordinating staffing for the candidate, documenting key takeaways from donor engagement, leading donor follow-up, and maintaining accurate pledge tracking and reporting Conduct data maintenance projects Support and coordinate Call Time Qualifications: 1-2 years campaign finance experience preferred Ability to meet deadlines, work independently, and manage multiple projects simultaneously Excellent written and verbal communication skills when reaching donors by phone, text, and email.
Highly organized and detail-oriented Able to work in fast paced environments Access to a car (and a valid driver’s license), cell phone, and a laptop.
Some in-state travel may be required.
Ability to work non-traditional hours as needed Proficiency with Google Suite, NGP, Canva and Pingdex preferred This is a full-time, salaried position, with compensation based on experience.
