From the Delta to the Gulf Coast to the suburbs of DeSoto County, the local leaders closest to Washington’s choices say Colom is the senator Mississippi needs
Mayoral endorsements build on the 33 state legislators already backing Colom, bringing his coalition of former and current elected officials across Mississippi to more than 50
District Attorney Colom: “Mayors see up close what it costs Mississippi when Washington turns its back on working people and families.
That’s why I’m humbled that mayors from every corner of our state are standing with this campaign”
Columbus, MS — Today, District Attorney and U.S.
Senate candidate Scott Colom announced the endorsement of 17 mayors from communities across Mississippi, along with former Tupelo Mayor Jason Shelton, growing the coalition of current and former elected officials backing his campaign to more than 50 statewide.
The mayors join 33 state legislators who have already endorsed Colom, along with U.S.
Congressman Bennie Thompson, as Colom continues prosecuting the case against incumbent Republican Senator Cindy Hyde-Smith’s track record of corruption and negligence.
The endorsers lead towns and cities from the Mississippi Delta to the Gulf Coast, from the Hills of northeast Mississippi to the suburbs of DeSoto County.
They include Greenville Mayor Errick Simmons, who leads the largest city in the Delta, and Stephen Jones, the mayor of Colom’s hometown of Columbus.
As the officials closest to the people they serve, mayors are often the first call when a rural hospital is at risk, when a work site closes down, or when families fall behind; these mayors say Colom understands what those choices cost Mississippi families because he has spent his career fighting for them.
“I am proud to support Scott Colom for the U.S.
Senate.
He brings a strong commitment to public service, a clear vision for our future, and the integrity we need in leadership right now.
I believe Scott Colom will be a powerful voice for our state, and I’m honored to stand with him in this campaign,” said Metcalfe Mayor Shaquita Allen.
“Mayors see up close what it costs Mississippi when Washington turns its back on working people and families.
That’s why I’m humbled that mayors from every corner of our state are standing with this campaign.
We share the same belief: that every Mississippi family deserves quality health care and hospitals, good-paying jobs close to home, and leaders who put this state first, not themselves or their rich friends.
Together, we are going to make sure Washington remembers that Mississippi matters,” said District Attorney Scott Colom.
The mayoral endorsements add to a growing wave of support that already includes Congressman Bennie Thompson, the Mississippi AFL-CIO, the Congressional Black Caucus PAC, and 33 state legislators representing every region of the state.
Colom has spent the campaign traveling Mississippi for town halls and community conversations, a sharp contrast with Senator Hyde-Smith, who has largely stayed out of public view in the state and has avoided answering Mississippians’ questions about her record.
