ABOUT KELLY State Representative Kelly T.
Keisling has served the 38th House District in the Tennessee General Assembly since 2010.
Currently, he serves as chairman of the State Government Committee along with being a member of the following committees:​​ ​ ​ Calendar & Rules Committee Finance, Ways, and Means Committee Government Operations Committee Appropriations Subcommittee Corrections Subcommittee Departments & Agencies Subcommittee Joint Commerce, Labor, Transportation and Agriculture Public Service Subcommittee In addition to his legislative duties, he serves on the Board of Directors of the Children’s Center of the Cumberlands; President, Keisling Insurance; member of the following Chamber of Commerce’s: Macon, Clay, Pickett, Fentress and Scott Counties; member, Tennessee State Employees Association.
Representative Keisling and wife, Janet, have been married 51 years and are the proud parents of three adult children, seven wonderful grandchildren, and two beautiful great-grandchildren.
They reside in Byrdstown and are members of Etter Baptist Church.
