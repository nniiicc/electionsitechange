All Posts Search Tennessee Right to Life PAC Endorses Representative Kelly Keisling for State House Based on Record of Shared Commitment and Dedication to Pro-Life Cause State Rep.
Kelly Keisling Jul 3 1 min read The state's oldest and largest pro-life organization has endorsed Representative Kelly Keisling for TN House District 38, representing Clay, Macon, Pickett, Scott and part of Fentress Counties.
Citing Keisling’s demonstrated commitment to defending Tennessee's vulnerable unborn children and their mothers, the organization underscored their support.
“Representative Keisling represents Tennessee's pro-life values and will be a consistent voice for those who can't speak for themselves," said Roger Kane, president of Tennessee Right to Life Political Action Committee.
“Tennesseans have made great strides in restoring protections for women and their unborn children, and Representative Kelly Keisling has been a big part of that.” During his time in office, Representative Keisling has used his voice and his vote to keep Tennessee one of the strongest pro-life states in the nation.
He understands that our work is not over and that it will take bold leadership to keep Tennessee free from legalized abortion and to stop dangerous abortion pills from being sent into our state.
Pro-life legislators in Tennessee understand that it takes vigilance and courage to confront the attacks on human life in all its ages, stages and conditions.
Pro-life constituents can confidently rely on them to make sure Tennessee law continues to reflect Tennessee values.
Tennessee Right to Life is grateful that Representative Keisling is running for the right reasons.
We strongly believe that his record of support for the sanctity of human life makes him qualified to speak for those who have no other voice, and the Political Action Committee fully endorses his campaign in District 38.
We urge every pro-life voter in District 38 to work and vote for Representative Kelly Keisling’s re-election.
