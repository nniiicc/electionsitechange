Member - House of Representatives, Florida
For over 25 years, Ryan Chamberlin has empowered thousands of entrepreneurs and business leaders nationwide. in May 2023, he was elected to the Florida House of Representatives and is passionately working through legislation and advocacy to safeguard individual freedoms, promote economic prosperity, and uphold conservative values.
Ryan has authored and co-authored 5 books on entrepreneurship, leadership and marketing and now provides expert guidance in business development, compliance management, and strategic advisory services.
His proven track record includes consulting with companies of various sizes, helping them enhance profitability, align with regulatory standards, and foster robust, productive organizational cultures.
His extensive experience also includes co-founding and serving as CEO of the True Patriot Network (TPN), a conservative social media and news platform championing strong American values.
Ryan lives in Central Florida with his wife of 31 years, and 4 Adult sons.
