Priorities ECONOMIC RECOVERY & DIVERSIFICATION Continue supporting West Maui’s recovery while strengthening local businesses, encouraging innovation and expanding opportunities that create a more resilient and diversified economy.
AFFORDABLE HOUSING & COST OF LIVING Support practical housing solutions that help local families stay on Maui, while working with community partners to address homelessness, mental health and long-term affordability challenges.
INFRASTRUCTURE Invest in roads, water systems, technology and community infrastructure to support our growing needs, improve quality of life and strengthen our future.
EDUCATION & WORKFORCE DEVELOPMENT Expand opportunities in early education, STEAM programs, vocational training and workforce development so we can prepare our next generation and keep local talent here at home.
CLEAN ENERGY Support responsible renewable energy initiatives that strengthen energy independence, create local jobs and protect our environment for future generations.
AGRICULTURE & FOOD SECURITY Strengthen food security by supporting local agriculture and increasing access to land, training and resources that help grow and sustain Maui's food systems.
RESPONSIBLE TOURISM & LOCAL ECONOMY Support a visitor industry that respects our community, strengthens local businesses and helps sustain the jobs and opportunities our families depend on, while continuing to diversify our economy for the future.
ENVIRONMENTAL STEWARDSHIP Protecting our land, water and natural resources helps preserve the quality of life that makes Maui special and ensures future generations can enjoy and benefit from them.
