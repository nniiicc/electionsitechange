TOGETHER WE CAN BUILD SOMETHING GREATER
The greatest outcomes aren’t achieved alone—they’re built side by side as a community.
By bringing people together, finding common ground and focusing on practical solutions, we can address the challenges facing West Maui and create new opportunities for our families and future generations.
Priorities
- Continue supporting West Maui’s recovery while strengthening local businesses, encouraging innovation and expanding opportunities that create a more resilient and diversified economy.
- Support practical housing solutions that help local families stay on Maui, while working with community partners to address homelessness, mental health and long-term affordability challenges.
- Invest in roads, water systems, technology and community infrastructure to support our growing needs, improve quality of life and strengthen our future.
- Expand opportunities in early education, STEAM programs, vocational training and workforce development so we can prepare our next generation and keep local talent here at home.
- Support responsible renewable energy initiatives that strengthen energy independence, create local jobs and protect our environment for future generations.
- Strengthen food security by supporting local agriculture and increasing access to land, training and resources that help grow and sustain Maui's food systems.
- Support a visitor industry that respects our community, strengthens local businesses and helps sustain the jobs and opportunities our families depend on, while continuing to diversify our economy for the future.
- Protecting our land, water and natural resources helps preserve the quality of life that makes Maui special and ensures future generations can enjoy and benefit from them.
