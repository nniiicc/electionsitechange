WHY I AM RUNNING
I’m running because our district needs money.
Period.
I want to deliver results people can actually see and feel in their communities.
With 351 cities and towns all competing for the same state resources we need to get creative and look outside of the state coffers to fund our district's needs.
We have been ignored for too long while our infrastructure continues to degrade.
We can’t rely on the state or federal government to save us.
We have to save ourselves.
Our towns were built by people who fixed what needed fixing — shipbuilders, quarrymen, tradespeople, teachers, caregivers, and volunteers.
That spirit still defines the 5th Plymouth District.
When elected, I’ll bring that same practical, collaborative approach to the State House — focused on solutions, following through, and steady progress.
