02
ECONOMICS OF SURVIVAL
The "micro-economy" inside your wallet is under siege.
Jason’s "Competence-First" economic plan focuses on:
- Wage Indexing: Phasing in a minimum wage tied to the Consumer Price Index (CPI) so workers can afford to live where they work.
- Rent Fairness: Preventing predatory price gouging by corporate institutional investors.
- Defeating the Property Tax Trap: Opposing the abolition of property taxes that would create a $400 million hole in Cherokee schools and force a doubling of the sales tax.

Our children are navigating a digital world designed to exploit them.

- Safety by Design Standards (SB 488): Holding Big Tech accountable for predatory algorithms and harmful AI.
