Mark Cochran is a proven conservative leader committed to the success of his district and the State of Tennessee.
He grew up feeding cattle on a sixth-generation family farm and bagging groceries in his father's store.
Through these experiences, Mark learned the value of hard work and the importance of serving his community.
His time working in two family businesses also allowed Mark to witness firsthand what is possible when Tennesseans are free to pursue their dreams.
That is why he wants to ensure Tennessee remains a place where people are free to pursue God's purpose for their lives unencumbered by government interference.
During his time in the General Assembly, Mark has fought to make Tennessee the most conservative state in the nation.
He has helped lead the way on important issues like keeping men out of women’s sports and banning transgender surgeries for minors.
He has co-sponsored nearly $1 billion in tax cuts for Tennesseans and fought to protect religious liberty and the lives of the unborn.
Mark has a history of delivering conservative victories and received the Award for Conservative Excellence from the American Conservative Union Foundation.
Let’s send Mark back to Nashville to continue the fight on behalf of McMinn and Monroe Counties.
