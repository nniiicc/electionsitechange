Feature in Atlanta Magazine – Atlanta’s 500 most powerful leaders
I was recently featured in Atlanta magazine of Atlanta’s 500 most powerful leaders.
Senator Sheikh Rahman, a Democrat, is the first immigrant and first Asian American to serve in the Georgia State Senate.
He is also the first Muslim Georgia Legislator.
Elected in 2018, he represents the 5th Senate District in the heart of Gwinnett.
Rahman is the chairman of the Gwinnett Senate Delegation and the secretary of the Urban Affairs Committee.
He also serves on the Agriculture and Consumer Affairs, Economic Development and Tourism, Government Oversight, and Special Judiciary committees.
Rahman grew up in Bangladesh, where his country was devastated by the Bangladesh Liberation War of Independence in 1971.
At 13, Rahman was kidnapped at gunpoint as a prisoner of war.
He came to America in 1981, becoming a citizen in 1995.
