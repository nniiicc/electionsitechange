From the Sidelines to the Senate Floor
Recently we have our end of season cheer banquet.
We get to remember the season and honor our athletes.
Coaching youth sports has taught me more about leadership than any seminar or
policy paper ever could.
You see teamwork.
You see frustration.
You see growth.
But most importantly, you see what happens when people listen to each other.
That’s the same mindset I want to take to Lansing — helping our communities work together again.
Greg
