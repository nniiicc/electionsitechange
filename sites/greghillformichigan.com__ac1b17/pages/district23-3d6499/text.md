Michigan's 23rd District
A Community Worth Fighting For.
From farms to lakes to growing neighborhoods and the best downtowns—
this is home.
A community built on hard work, hometown pride, and a whole lot of heart.
District 23 is one of the most unique places in Michigan — a stretch of farms, lakes, forests, and growing neighborhoods.
From the small towns of Addison and Dryden, to the busy sports fields in Clarkston and Lake Orion, to the lake communities in White Lake and Highland, this district is full of people who show up for each other.
These aren’t just lines on a map.
These are the places where we raise our kids, run small businesses, worship, coach, volunteer, and build our lives.
This is home — and it deserves leadership that listens.
What Makes Our District Special
🏡 Hometown Character
Family-owned shops, historic downtowns, and neighbors who actually know each other.
🌲 Lakes, Trails & the Outdoors
From Highland Recreation to the trails in Addison Oaks — we live where people vacation.
🏫 Strong Schools & Youth Programs
Clarkston, Oxford, Lake Orion, Brandon, Waterford, Holly, Huron Valley — our schools are the backbone of our communities.
🚜 Working Farms & Open Land
Family farms are part of our identity — and our future.
🏘️ Growing Communities
More families are choosing to plant roots here.
We need thoughtful leadership to keep our quality of life strong.
