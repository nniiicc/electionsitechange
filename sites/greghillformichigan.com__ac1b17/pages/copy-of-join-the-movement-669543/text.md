Voices of the 23rd
This campaign is built from the community up — not from political insiders or party leaders.
Across our district, I’ve heard from parents, workers, teachers, small business owners, retirees, students, and neighbors who all share something in common: they want to be heard again.
This page is a place to lift up those voices.
Not polished statements.
Not political talking points.
Just real people sharing what matters to them, why they support this campaign, and what kind of future they want for our community.
Voices from the Community
Common themes from the community:
- Cost of living
- Supporting working families
- Less division
- Better opportunities for future generations
- Government accountability
- Strong public schools
