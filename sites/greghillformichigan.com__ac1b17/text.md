Michigan gave my family incredible opportunities.
I want to make sure the next generation has even more.
I’m running for State Senate because I believe in something simple: When leaders listen, communities get stronger.
That’s why this campaign isn’t about me — it’s about us: the parents, students, farmers, teachers, front line workers, first responders, health care workers, and small business owners who make this district home.
I’m here to work with you, learn from you, and fight for the things that matter most to your family.
WHAT WE’RE FIGHTING FOR Lowering Costs & Making Life More Affordable A hard day's work should be enough to build a good life.
Families shouldn't have to choose between groceries, healthcare, keeping the lights on, and saving for tomorrow.
Strong Schools & Real Opportunity Every kid deserves the chance to find their path and build a future right here in Michigan.
That means great teachers, strong classrooms, skilled trades, college, and opportunities that match every student's strengths.
Power to the People Your voice should matter more than your connections or your checkbook.
Government should answer to the people it serves — not lobbyists, political parties, or the biggest donors.
Lifting Up Michigan's Working & Middle Class Michigan should be a place where working hard still gets you ahead.
Where local businesses can grow, family farms can survive, workers can build a career, and the next generation doesn't have to leave Michigan to find opportunity.
