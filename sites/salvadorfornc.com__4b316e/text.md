Senator DeAndrea Salvador, Democratic Incumbent for NC’s 39th Senate District and a 5th-generation Charlottean, is running to deliver for the community that instilled the values that strongly direct her life today.
DeAndrea’s Priorities:
Affordable Healthcare
Ensure that healthcare is affordable and accessible.
Educational Opportunity
Refocus our attention to academic excellence and resolving systemic barriers that stymie the path to college and careers.
Advancing Infrastructure
Accelerate economic mobility in our area as investments translate to jobs.
