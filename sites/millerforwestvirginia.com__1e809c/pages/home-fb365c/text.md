Learn More About Chris Chris Miller isn't a career politician.
He's a businessman, employing 500 West Virginians, who believes government should solve problems, not create them.
Chris got his first job delivering newspapers when he was ten years old.
By fifteen, he was working on a bison farm.
Later, he started in the family car business by washing cars before eventually leading and expanding the business.
Business teaches you a few simple lessons.
If you don't solve problems, customers leave.
If you waste money, you won't stay in business very long.
Chris believes government should be held to those same standards.
Like many West Virginians, Chris has faced personal challenges.
After battling addiction, he has been sober since 2004.
That experience strengthened his faith, reinforced the importance of family, and taught him the value of second chances.
Today, Chris lives in his hometown of Huntington with his wife, Cassie, their three children, and two puppies.
Raising a family in West Virginia gives him a personal stake in the future of the state and the community he calls home.
He sees too many young people leaving West Virginia to build their futures somewhere else.
He sees roads and bridges that have been neglected for too long.
He sees neighborhoods that continue to flood after every heavy rain.
He sees families struggling with rising costs while government keeps asking for more.
And he sees a government in Charleston that too often forgets who it works for.
That's why Chris is running.
Not to become another politician.
To put West Virginia first every day by building the infrastructure West Virginia deserves, keeping West Virginians in West Virginia, preparing our kids for success, and cleaning up Charleston.
