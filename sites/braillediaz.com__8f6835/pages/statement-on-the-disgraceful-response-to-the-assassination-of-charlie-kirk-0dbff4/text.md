See videos below Rather than treating this tragedy with the solemnity and respect it demands, MSNBC chose to use their platform to smear and bash Kirk even as his family, friends, and countless Americans mourn his loss.
Such conduct is beneath any network that claims to serve the public trust, and it speaks to a moral bankruptcy that corrodes our nation’s discourse.
Even more disturbing was the disgraceful display in Congress, where Democratic members audibly shouted “no” when a Republican colleague requested a moment of silence and prayer for Charlie Kirk.
This shocking rejection of a simple act of human decency reveals a dangerous level of partisanship and hatred.
In moments like these, we should be united in grief and respect, not divided by cruelty and contempt.
Finally, we must condemn in the strongest possible terms those on the left who are openly celebrating Charlie Kirk’s death on social media and in public forums.
To revel in the murder of a fellow American is abhorrent, inhuman, and incompatible with the values of any civilized society.
Violence must never be excused, justified, or glorified.
The assassination of Charlie Kirk is a national tragedy.
The disgraceful reactions from certain media outlets, elected officials, and segments of the left only deepen the wound inflicted on our country.
America must reject this descent into hatred and reaffirm that radical leftist political violence and the celebration of death have no place in our Republic.
