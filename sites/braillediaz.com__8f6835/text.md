Priorities Affordability / Fighting Inflation Provide relief for oppressive inflation, reduce high utility bills, and cut taxes.
Cap annual increases in State spending, cut burdensome regulations, and reduce the size of government.
Provide tax relief for families with young children; increase childcare availability and flexibility.
Common Sense, NY-Based Healthcare Work together with nearby states to create a regional health insurance marketplace to increase options for New Yorkers.
Enhance outreach and expand opportunities to get more New Yorkers insured.
Allow New Yorkers to save for health costs through a New York Health Savings Program.
Holding Criminals Accountable Keep criminals off the street by fixing our broken bail and discovery laws.
Reform our parole system – authorize judges to use discretion when making bail decisions.
Provide support for substance abuse treatment services; hold drug dealers accountable.
Additional Issues Volunteer Fire and EMS Attract / retain volunteer first responders with tuition forgiveness plans, provide college credits, and increasing early-on training opportunities.
Punish targeting first responders with violence / harassment; protect volunteer fire/EMS from fentanyl exposure.
Ride Safe, Ride Secure Ensure our public transportation systems are safe by enhancing penalties, increasing law enforcement presence on public transit, and creating funding opportunities to increase transit protections.
Work Force Development Strengthen/expand NYS youth training programs; ensure certified apprenticeship programs receive needed support.
Create State-supported local sector partnerships to create a pipeline of prepared workers in every region of New York State.
