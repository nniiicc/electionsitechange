Ann Orcutt
Ipswich
I’m Ann Orcutt, a native of Ipswich, owner of AnnTiques on South Main Street, and founder of the Ipswich Business Roundtable.
I’m so pleased to endorse Dan Kelly for State Representative.
Since Dan and Lori moved to Ipswich five years ago, he has utterly embraced the town, and shown his concern for all of us in so many ways.
He cares, he listens, and will be a wonderful representative of all the people in the towns that make up his district.
