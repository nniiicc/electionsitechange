The Lehigh Valley is growing, and our transportation system needs to catch up.
Traffic on Routes 22, 33, and 78 is eating into family time and slowing down local businesses.
As a union guy, I know how precious those few hours after work are.
I’ll work to make travel easier and more reliable.
I’ll build on the work we’ve already done to expand the Lehigh Valley International Airport, including expanding parking and runways to build capacity.
There was a time the Valley was connected to New York and Philadelphia via passenger rail.
Thousands rode the train every day into the city for work.
I’ll work hard to both support SEPTA and reconnect our region to those hubs.
Specifically, there’s no reason the Lehigh Valley shouldn’t be connected to Philadelphia by passenger rail.
SEPTA still owns the right-of-way between Lansdale and Bethlehem, which could be double-tracked and electrified with the right investment.
Investing in transportation will help families, strengthen our economy, and keep the region moving forward.
