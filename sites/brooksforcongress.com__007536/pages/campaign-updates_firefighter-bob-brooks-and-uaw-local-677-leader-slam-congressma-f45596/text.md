Nazareth, PA – Today, Congressman Ryan Mackenzie will speak alongside Donald Trump in the Lehigh Valley as they continue to unleash a cost of living crisis, rip away access to health care, support a reckless war in Iran that has cost billions of dollars and raised gas prices, and screw over hardworking Pennsylvanians.
Ahead of Mackenzie’s latest appearance with Trump, firefighter Bob Brooks and UAW Local 677 President Mike Shupp issued the following statements:
“Career politician Ryan Mackenzie is no friend to working people.
He is a liar who campaigned on a promise to lower costs but has instead voted in lockstep with his boss Donald Trump to jack up prices, rip away health care, and spend billions of dollars on a needless war overseas,” said firefighter Bob Brooks.
“No speech from Mackenzie can change the fact that his time in Congress has been an absolute disaster for the hardworking people of the Lehigh Valley.
It’s time we send one of our own, a working person who understands the struggles we face, to fight for us in Congress.”
“For more than a decade, Ryan Mackenzie has consistently voted against the interests of working people — in both Harrisburg and Washington,” said UAW Local 677 President Michael Shupp.
“His record shows a pattern of siding with corporate priorities over the needs of our communities — including sponsoring ‘right to work’ bills 15 times, voting against the Faster Labor Contracts Act, and passing drastic cuts to Medicaid.
To this day, he has offered no explanation for how he plans to close the loopholes in the USMCA that continue to undermine American jobs and weaken labor standards.”
Bob spent 20 years as a firefighter in the City of Bethlehem.
He’s held nearly every position on the truck and every post in his union, from shift rep to president of the Pennsylvania Professional Fire Fighters Association, where he now represents over 8,000 firefighters across the Commonwealth.
Among Bob’s proudest victories is helping lead the fight to pass mental health coverage for firefighters struggling with PTSI — a ten-year battle he refused to give up on.
Bob was raised primarily by a single mom who worked as a bartender to keep food on the table.
By the time he turned 30, Bob had already held more jobs than most people do in a lifetime: dishwasher, short-order cook, bartender, landscaper, warehouse worker, pizza delivery driver.
He moved to Pennsylvania more than 30 years ago and never looked back.
Bob also runs Brooks Lawn Care, a small family-operated snow removal and lawn care business he started in 2013.
Whether plowing driveways at 4 a.m. or mowing lawns after a firefighting shift, Bob has never stopped hustling to provide for his family.
When he’s not working, you’ll find Bob on the baseball field, where he’s coached youth and varsity baseball for over two decades.
Or maybe you’ll spot his tattoo — a bulldog in a fire helmet — which says just about everything you need to know about his grit, his loyalty, and his love for the job.
Bob’s never had a silver spoon — just calloused hands, a deep sense of duty, and an unshakeable commitment to do what’s right.
In Washington, Bob won’t only talk about fighting for working people — he’ll live it every day, just like he always has.
