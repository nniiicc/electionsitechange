Nazareth, PA – Today, firefighter Bob Brooks – Democratic candidate for Congress in Pennsylvania’s 7th Congressional District – received the formal endorsement of former Secretary Pete Buttigieg.
One of the most influential voices in Democratic politics today, the backing of Secretary Buttigieg adds significant momentum to Brooks’ campaign – which already has the endorsement of leaders including Governor Josh Shapiro – and further cements his position as the leading Democratic contender to challenge vulnerable Republican Congressman Ryan Mackenzie.
“People are seeking leaders who understand their lives and fight for their needs,” said Secretary Buttigieg.
“Bob’s experience as a firefighter, union leader, and snowplow driver gives him a real understanding of what working families are facing.
He understands the urgency of lowering costs because he’s lived it – working long hours, juggling jobs, and fighting for a paycheck that actually covers the basics.
It’s a perspective Washington needs more of, and I’m proud to endorse him.”
“Pete’s endorsement means a great deal,” said Bob Brooks.
“Pete gets that things aren’t working the way they should for working families, and that our party needs new leadership grounded in real life experience to fix it.
He’s focused on listening to new voices and making government work for everyday people at a time when too many feel shut out and left behind.
It’s an honor to have him on board as we fight to build a Congress that looks like and works for the people it serves.”

Bob’s unifying background and career of service to the Lehigh Valley stands in stark contrast to career politician Ryan Mackenzie, who has sold out working families during his first months in Congress.
Mackenzie cast a decisive vote in Congress for House Republicans’ budget that cuts more than $1 trillion from Medicaid and could kick more than 26,000 people in PA-07 off their health insurance while jacking up health care premiums, all so that he could give more tax cuts to billionaires.
Mackenzie’s vote will eliminate food assistance for hungry children and families, jeopardize rural hospitals across Pennsylvania, jack up electricity bills, and add trillions to our national deficit.
In Congress, Bob will be a champion for the working class and fight to lower costs and expand access to health care for Lehigh Valley families.
Bob spent 20 years as a firefighter in the City of Bethlehem.
He’s held nearly every position on the truck and every post in his union, from shift rep to president of the Pennsylvania Professional Fire Fighters Association, where he now represents over 8,000 firefighters across the Commonwealth.
Among Bob’s proudest victories is helping lead the fight to pass mental health coverage for firefighters struggling with PTSI — a ten-year battle he refused to give up on.
Bob was raised primarily by a single mom who worked as a bartender to keep food on the table.
By the time he turned 30, Bob had already held more jobs than most people do in a lifetime: dishwasher, short-order cook, bartender, landscaper, warehouse worker, pizza delivery driver.
He moved to Pennsylvania more than 30 years ago and never looked back.
Bob also runs Brooks Lawn Care, a small family-operated snow removal and lawn care business he started in 2013.
Whether plowing driveways at 4 a.m. or mowing lawns after a firefighting shift, Bob has never stopped hustling to provide for his family.
When he’s not working, you’ll find Bob on the baseball field, where he’s coached youth and varsity baseball for over two decades.
Or maybe you’ll spot his tattoo — a bulldog in a fire helmet — which says just about everything you need to know about his grit, his loyalty, and his love for the job.
Bob’s never had a silver spoon — just calloused hands, a deep sense of duty, and an unshakeable commitment to do what’s right.
In Washington, Bob won’t only talk about fighting for working people — he’ll live it every day, just like he always has.
