Republicans spent nearly $2M to try to stop Bob from facing Mackenzie because they know they will lose in November Bethlehem, PA – A new poll of likely voters in Pennsylvania’s 7th Congressional District shows firefighter Bob Brooks leading Congressman Ryan Mackenzie.
Brooks leads Mackenzie 47%–43% , putting the firefighter clearly ahead in one of the most closely watched House races in the country and demonstrating just how underwater Mackenzie is with Lehigh Valley voters.
The poll also finds that the political environment in the Lehigh Valley has shifted sharply since 2024: President Trump, who carried the district by three percentage points, is now underwater with PA-07 voters (44% favorable / 51% unfavorable).
“Lehigh Valley voters are rallying around firefighter Bob Brooks because they know he’s lived the challenges working families are facing and will fight to clean up a broken and corrupt Washington when he gets to Congress,” said Campaign Manager Jenna Kaufman.
“This poll shows that momentum continues to build behind Bob, and we will take our message to every corner of Pennsylvania’s 7th District to earn every vote and win in November.” Key findings include: Brooks leads Mackenzie by 4 points.
Brooks is up by 4 points in the current vote (47% Brooks / 43% Mackenzie), a significant advantage against a sitting incumbent this far from Election Day.
Trump is underwater in a district he carried by 3 points.
Voters in the sample recall backing Trump over Kamala Harris by a single point in 2024 (47% – 46%), but his standing has collapsed since: his net favorability now sits at -7 (44% favorable / 51% unfavorable).
Mackenzie, who has voted in lockstep with the Trump agenda, is tied to a brand that Lehigh Valley voters are rejecting.
The poll was conducted by GBAO Research from June 29-July 2, 2026, among 500 likely voters in PA-07.
These findings come as PA-07 remains one of the most fiercely contested seats in the country.
The Lehigh Valley-based district is widely seen as one of the purest swing seats in America, and Cook Political Report, Inside Elections, and Sabato’s Crystal Ball all rate the race a “Toss Up.” Mackenzie flipped the seat in 2024 with just over 50% of the vote.
Now the freshman congressman faces reelection in a dramatically less favorable environment and has a record of voting to raise prices and rip away health care from his own constituents.
Share About Bob Brooks Bob spent 20 years as a firefighter in the City of Bethlehem.
He’s held nearly every position on the truck and every post in his union, from shift rep to president of the Pennsylvania Professional Fire Fighters Association, where he now represents over 8,000 firefighters across the Commonwealth.
Among Bob’s proudest victories is helping lead the fight to pass mental health coverage for firefighters struggling with PTSI — a ten-year battle he refused to give up on.
Bob was raised primarily by a single mom who worked as a bartender to keep food on the table.
By the time he turned 30, Bob had already held more jobs than most people do in a lifetime: dishwasher, short-order cook, bartender, landscaper, warehouse worker, pizza delivery driver.
He moved to Pennsylvania more than #ago and never looked back.
Bob also runs Brooks Lawn Care, a small family-operated snow removal and lawn care business he started in 2013.
Whether plowing driveways at 4 a.m. or mowing lawns after a firefighting shift, Bob has never stopped hustling to provide for his family.
When he’s not working, you’ll find Bob on the baseball field, where he’s coached youth and varsity baseball for over two decades.
Or maybe you’ll spot his tattoo — a bulldog in a fire helmet — which says just about everything you need to know about his grit, his loyalty, and his love for the job.
Bob’s never had a silver spoon — just calloused hands, a deep sense of duty, and an unshakeable commitment to do what’s right.
In Washington, Bob won’t only talk about fighting for working people — he’ll live it every day, just like he always has.
