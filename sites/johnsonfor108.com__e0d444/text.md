About Priorities Volunteer Contact Donate Larry Johnson for State Representative A Common-Sense Leader for the Upper Peninsula Larry Johnson is a lifelong Yooper, husband, father, and community leader dedicated to strengthening Michigan’s Upper Peninsula.
Born and raised in Wilson, Larry learned early the value of hard work and integrity, lessons that have guided him through every stage of his career and public service.
Today, Larry lives in Spalding with his wife of 18 years and their three daughters.
He has proudly served as a Menominee County Commissioner since 2019, where he’s earned a reputation for fiscal responsibility, balanced budgets, and putting people before politics.
As Finance Chair, Larry has ensured county budgets consistently end in the black.
He also leads as Chair of the Public Health Delta & Menominee Counties Board, steering the department through major challenges, including the COVID-19 pandemic.
Outside of government, Larry gives back through his work with the Powers-Spalding Lions Club, where he’s helped launch numerous local service projects supporting youth, schools, and families.
With a blue-collar background and decades of hands-on experience, from repairing electrical systems to managing industrial projects, Larry brings a practical, no-nonsense approach to problem-solving. ‍ His campaign for State Representative is rooted in one simple belief: the Upper Peninsula deserves strong, steady, and sensible leadership.
What Larry Johnson Is Fighting For Putting the U.P.
First Larry Johnson’s priorities come directly from conversations with families, workers, and business owners across the 108th District.
His focus is on solutions that strengthen our communities and protect our way of life.
Mental Health Care The U.P. needs access to quality mental health care close to home.
Larry will champion the creation of an inpatient mental health facility in the Upper Peninsula and build partnerships with neighboring states to expand treatment options.
Fiscal Responsibility & Government Oversight As Finance Chair of the Menominee County Commission, Larry has proven his ability to balance budgets and cut waste.
In Lansing, he’ll push for accountability and transparency in state government, ensuring taxpayer dollars are spent wisely.
Economic Growth & Jobs Larry believes in growing the U.P. economy from the ground up.
He’ll work to support small businesses, empower entrepreneurs, and attract good-paying jobs that keep families here and strengthen our communities.
Regulatory Reform Government should work for the people, not against them.
Larry will advocate for streamlined regulations and efficient government processes that make it easier for businesses to grow and for citizens to get things done.
Protecting Our Way of Life As a lifelong resident of the U.P., Larry understands the unique challenges and opportunities that define our region.
He’ll continue fighting for policies that preserve our natural resources, expand access to health care, and keep the U.P. strong for future generations.
