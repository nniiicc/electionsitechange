Cameron Parish, Louisiana, has slightly higher crime rates than the national average.
Cameron Parish has an overall crime rate of 61.58 offenses per 1,000 residents per year, placing it in the 24th percentile for safety among US counties.
Cameron Parish is safer than 24% of US counties (CrimeGrade.org).
Violent Crime
In Cameron Parish, violent crimes include assault, robbery, rape, and murder.
The violent crime rate is 5.994 per 1,000 residents, earning it a C-, indicating that it is slightly higher than the national average for violent crime.
According to CrimeGrade.org, the likelihood of becoming a victim of violent crime in Cameron Parish varies from 1 in 47 in the east to 1 in 342 in the north.

Crime Distribution
Crime is not distributed equitably within the parish.
The northern section of Cameron Parish is generally seen as safer, whilst the southwest areas report higher crime.
Higher visitor traffic in retail locations can lead to increased crime reporting, creating a distorted sense of crime rates (CrimeGrade.org).
Addressing the crime issue in Cameron Parish could benefit from a multifaceted approach:
- Community Policing: Strengthening the relationship between law enforcement and the community can help in crime prevention and increase trust in the police.
- Improved Surveillance: Increasing the use of surveillance cameras in high-crime areas, particularly around retail and public spaces, can deter criminal activities and aid in investigations.
- Social Programs: Implementing community programs focused on education, employment, and youth engagement can help address the root causes of crime.
- Home Security: Encouraging residents to adopt home security systems can reduce the likelihood of property crimes.
Homes without security systems are 300% more likely to be robbed (CrimeGrade.org).
- Data-Driven Policing: Utilizing crime data to allocate police resources more effectively to high-crime areas and times can enhance the efficiency of law enforcement efforts.
By adopting these strategies, Cameron Parish can work towards reducing its crime rates and improving overall safety for its residents.
