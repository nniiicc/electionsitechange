Acadia Parish, Louisiana, experiences substantial criminal difficulties, with rates that exceed national standards.
Acadia Parish’s violent crime rate is 34.7, which includes serious acts including murder, rape, assault, and robbery.
This rate is significantly higher than the national average of 22.7%, indicating a more serious issue with violent crime in the area.
Additionally, Acadia Parish has a property crime rate of 44.8.
This category encompasses crimes such as burglary, theft, and vehicle theft.
Again, this figure is higher than the national average of 35.4 percent, implying that property crimes are more common in Acadia Parish than elsewhere in the United States.
These statistics highlight the urgent need for effective crime prevention and community safety measures in Acadia Parish to address these elevated crime rates.
- Violent Crime:
- Murder: The rate of murder in Acadia Parish contributes to the high violent crime statistic.
This category includes intentional homicide.
- Rape: Sexual assault cases are a significant concern, reflecting a higher incidence rate than national figures.
- Assault: Physical assaults, including aggravated assault, contribute to the higher violent crime rate.
- Robbery: Incidents of robbery, involving the taking of property by force or threat, are also elevated.
- Property Crime:
- Burglary: Break-ins and unauthorized entry into homes and businesses are frequent in Acadia Parish.
- Theft: General theft, including shoplifting and larceny, is prevalent.
- Vehicle Theft: The stealing of vehicles is a notable issue, adding to the property crime rate.
