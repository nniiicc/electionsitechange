Louisiana’s 3rd District is a vibrant and diverse region comprising several unique parishes, each with its own rich history, culture, and community.
As a Democratic candidate for this district, my commitment is to understand and serve the needs of each parish.
Below is a brief overview of the parishes in the 3rd District, with links to more detailed information about each one.
Rich Cajun Heritage Acadia Parish is renowned for its Cajun culture, rooted in the Acadian settlers.
Key economic activities include rice farming, celebrated annually at the International Rice Festival in Crowley.
Learn more about Acadia Parish
Economic and Cultural Center Calcasieu Parish, home to the city of Lake Charles, is a hub for industry, commerce, and culture in Southwest Louisiana.
The parish boasts a strong economy with significant contributions from petrochemical plants, gaming, and tourism.
Learn more about Calcasieu Parish
Natural Beauty and Wildlife Cameron Parish is known for its stunning coastal landscapes and abundant wildlife.
The parish’s economy is driven by fishing, agriculture, and energy production, making it a unique blend of natural beauty and industrial strength.
Learn more about Cameron Parish
Cultural Melting Pot Iberia Parish blends French, Spanish, and Creole cultures.
The parish’s economy thrives on oil and gas, agriculture, and seafood, supported by institutions like the University of Louisiana at Lafayette.
Learn more about Iberia Parish
Agricultural Heartland Jeff Davis Parish is a key agricultural region, known for its rice and crawfish farming.
The parish also hosts various festivals celebrating its rich cultural heritage and agricultural contributions.
Learn more about Jeff Davis Parish
Cultural and Economic Hub Lafayette Parish is the heart of Acadiana, famous for its vibrant festivals.
Its diverse economy includes healthcare, technology, and education, anchored by the University of Louisiana at Lafayette.
Learn more about Lafayette Parish
Historical and Natural Beauty St.
Martin Parish is known for its historical significance and scenic landscapes.
The economy is driven by agriculture, particularly sugarcane and crawfish farming, and tourism.
Learn more about St.
Martin Parish
Agricultural Excellence Vermilion Parish is a leader in agriculture and seafood, known for its fertile lands and vibrant community events.
The town of Abbeville is celebrated as the “Seafood Capital of Louisiana.”
Learn more about Vermilion Parish
Louisiana’s 3rd District is a tapestry of vibrant parishes, each with unique contributions.
By focusing on economic development, cultural preservation, and community engagement, we can build a brighter future for all.
