Raised in local schools PS11, IS125, and Queens Technical High School Brandon went on to graduate from Queens College (CUNY).
His story reflects the working families of Queens: hardworking, determined, and ready for opportunity.
As a former community liaison with the NYC Department of Small Business Services, Brandon helped local businesses grow.
At Queens College, he led career development efforts to help students secure jobs, internships, and real world skills.
He’s also worked across grassroots campaigns, building bridges across communities and political backgrounds.
