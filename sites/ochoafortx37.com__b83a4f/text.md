ABOUT OZZIE
A LIFETIME OF SERVING VALLEY FAMILIES
Ozzie Ochoa has spent his life serving the Rio Grande Valley.
A lifelong Harlingen resident, Ozzie grew up on a small family farm and graduated from Harlingen High School South.
He went on to serve our community as a teacher, coach, principal, and Director of Special Education.
As a husband and father, Ozzie understands the challenges families face.
He wants the Valley to remain a place where people can build a life, raise a family, and give their children every opportunity to succeed.
Now, he’s running for State Representative to bring that same commitment to service and practical leadership to the Texas House.
VALLEY PRIORITIES
Building a stronger Rio Grande Valley starts by listening.
Across House District 37, people are asking for relief from rising costs, better access to healthcare, strong public schools, and practical solutions to the challenges facing our communities.
Ozzie believes our government should truly be "of the people, by the people, for the people." He will put those concerns first and represent the people of District 37 above politics.
