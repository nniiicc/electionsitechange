Doctor Nick Fierro ​ ​​ Independent State Senate District 10 Candidate Home Issues About Nick Donate& Contact Nick graduated from Mesa Community College with an Associate in Arts.
He then pursued a bachelor in Exercise Science from Brigham Young University, and a Doctor of Physical Therapy from Rocky Mountain University of Health Professions.
As he pursued his degrees he became concerned about the political climate evolving in Arizona.
Nick ran for office in 2022 seeking to bring bipartisan legislation to District 10.
He focused on state issues transpiring in Arizona such as: education, homelessness, and the economy.
In conjunction with his political activism, Nick strives to participate in community organizations and has served with The Mesa Welcome Center, Books for a Better World, the East Valley Hispanic Institute, and the Health Care Board of Advisors for Skyline High School.
Nick was born in Rochester, New York and moved to Arizona when he was 7 years old.
