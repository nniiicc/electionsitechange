Meet Katie
Katie Kramer is a lifelong Alabamian, working mother, and community leader running for Alabama State Senate 8 to bring real-world experience to Montgomery and deliver practical solutions that put Alabama families first.
A nationally ranked, seven-time national champion swimmer, Katie began her career teaching swim lessons at 13 and went on to lead large-scale public safety and aquatics programs across Alabama.
She managed multi-million-dollar operations, oversaw public facilities serving underserved communities, and built a career centered on safety, accountability, and leadership.
Katie’s passion for civic service led her to work in local economic development and community revitalization, partnering with business owners, managing public events, and helping strengthen downtowns and local economies.
Katie and her husband Jeff settled in DeKalb County, where they raised their family.
While raising her daughters, Katie led nonprofit efforts supporting food security for children and assistance programs for families in need.
Today, Katie serves as VP of Operations at a metal manufacturing plant in Northeast Alabama, where she has fought for higher wages, pay equity, safer workplaces, and expanded opportunities for women.
She has helped open doors for women in nontraditional roles and strengthened safety standards on the plant floor.
Katie is also a trusted public safety advocate, working with suicide prevention organizations, certifying lifeguards, and training foster parents, workers, churches, and businesses in CPR, First Aid, AED, and water safety.
Katie and Jeff have been married since 2009 and are proud parents and grandparents.
She is running for State Senate to fight for safer communities, stronger local economies, and a future where every Alabama family can thrive.
