Meet Katie Issues Volunteer Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Infrastructure Infrastructure Katie believes investing in strong infrastructure is key to building thriving communities and expanding economic opportunity across Alabama.
She supports increasing investment in roads and transportation improvements, expanding access to reliable broadband internet, and modernizing water and sewer systems so that families, businesses, and local communities have the resources they need to succeed.
In many smaller communities, state investment in infrastructure can make the difference between economic stagnation and growth.
By strengthening essential infrastructure, Katie believes Alabama can attract new businesses, support local employers, and improve quality of life for residents.
