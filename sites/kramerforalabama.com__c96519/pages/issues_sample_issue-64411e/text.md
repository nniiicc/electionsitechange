Meet Katie Issues Volunteer Contribute Home ❭ Issues ❭ Healthcare for All Healthcare for All Katie Kramer believes healthcare should be accessible and affordable for every Alabamian.
She supports expanding Medicaid and introducing legislation that closes the healthcare coverage gap so more women and working families can access the care they need.
Katie is also committed to strengthening women’s healthcare, including improving access to fertility care and ensuring women have affordable options when planning and growing their families.
Katie believes mental health care must be treated with the same urgency and importance as physical health.
She supports expanding access to mental health services across Alabama and will work to introduce legislation that protects employees seeking mental health treatment.
Katie will also advocate for policies that encourage employers to offer mental health care as part of workplace benefits, ensuring people can seek help without risking their job or financial stability.
