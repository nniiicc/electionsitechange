Issues
Committed to fighting for Alabama families, Katie Kramer believes every community deserves access to quality healthcare, good-paying jobs, and safe neighborhoods.
She is dedicated to building a stronger future for our state while protecting the values and natural beauty that make Alabama the Beautiful our home.
Healthcare for All
Katie Kramer believes healthcare should be accessible and affordable for every Alabamian.
She supports expanding Medicaid and advancing solutions that close the healthcare coverage gap so more women and working families can get the care they need.
Katie will also work to lower prescription drug costs and support healthcare programs that make quality care affordable for all.

Workforce Development
Alabama’s workforce potential is one of our greatest strengths, and we must invest in the people who keep our state moving forward.
Katie supports expanding job training, apprenticeships, and technical education so more Alabamians can access high-quality careers without unnecessary barriers.
By aligning education, industry, and opportunity, we can grow our economy and keep talent here at home.

Infrastructure
Katie believes strong infrastructure is essential to growing Alabama’s communities and supporting local businesses.
She supports increased investment in roads and transportation improvements, expanding broadband internet access, and modernizing water and sewer systems.
In many smaller communities, these investments directly impact economic opportunity and quality of life.
Katie is committed to ensuring communities have the infrastructure they need to grow, attract jobs, and support families.

Affordability
Families across Alabama are feeling the strain of rising prices, from groceries and utility bills to the cost of finding an affordable place to live.
Working families shouldn’t have to choose between paying the power bill, putting food on the table, or saving for the future.
As your State Senator, Katie will support policies that help lower everyday costs, increase housing affordability, and provide responsible tax relief for working families.
Alabama’s economy should work for the people who power it.
