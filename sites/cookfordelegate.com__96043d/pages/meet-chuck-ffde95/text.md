DONATE MEET CHUCK Working for working people ​I'm Chuck Cook.
I'm a Veteran, a Husband, and a Father.
I'm running to be your next Delegate in District 33B - Anne Arundel County ​ I grew up in a single-income working class family, and my parents worked hard to get into the middle class.
But, a strong work ethic, alone, didn't get them there.
Policy choices made by elected officials did.
In the past 40 years, our policy makers made the wrong choice to side with big corporations and big money interests over small businesses, workers, and their families.
Now, most of us are living paycheck-to-paycheck on two incomes, while single-income families are nearly unheard of.
I want to represent you in the General Assembly because I believe in the dignity of hard work and want to honor workers, small business owners, and their families in central Anne Arundel County.
From Davidsonville to Crofton; from Crownsville to Severna Park, and everywhere in between, parents are getting up every day to go into work, put food on the tables for their families, and they deserve a Delegate that will work to make housing, food, energy, and transportation more affordable and accessible.
Working families and small business owners make this county, state, and country possible, and it's far past time we had a Delegate that valued them and what they do, every day. ​ Join me in this fight.
I could use your ideas, your input, and your help. ​ - Chuck HOME MEET CHUCK THE ISSUES GET INVOLVED More Use tab to navigate through the menu items.
