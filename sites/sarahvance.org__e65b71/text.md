Sarah Vance
does what you elect her to do!
Representative Vance
holds pivotal roles in the Alaska State House,
serving as the Chair of the House Judiciary Committee and the House Special Committee on Fisheries.
Additionally, she serves as the Vice-Chair of the House Transportation Committee and contributes her expertise to the House Special Committee on Tribal Affairs and the Legislative Budget & Audit Committee.
Sarah is also a member of the Joint Legislative Seafood Industry Task Force, an ex-officio board member of the Alaska Seafood Marketing Institute, a member of the Alaska Criminal Justice Data Analysis Commission and an ex-officio member of the Governor’s Council on Human & Sex Trafficking, which is one of her top criminal justice issues.
Rooted in Homer,
where she is raising her family alongside her husband Jeff, Sarah’s connection to her hometown runs deep, having grown up just around the corner.
Her journey into public service was shaped by her early experiences in Alaska’s fishing and tourism industries, alongside invaluable lessons learned from her family business.
