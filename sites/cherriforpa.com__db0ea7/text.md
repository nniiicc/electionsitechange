One of Us Meet Cherri One of Us.
Fighting for Us.
A lifelong Western Pennsylvanian raised in a middle-class, union household.
Both parents were proud United Steelworkers members.
Cherri learned early that dignity comes from hard work, perseverance, and looking out for one another.
A proud member of the Pittsburgh Federation of Teachers, Local 400.
A Calling to Care A career nurse spanning occupational health, geriatrics, psychiatry, and health informatics.
Today she serves as a certified school nurse , ensuring children are healthy and ready to learn.
RN-CSN, MSN Education & Policy Holds a Doctorate in Education from the University of Pittsburgh .
Twice selected for the prestigious Salk Fellowship , developing deep expertise in how Medicaid affects working families and seniors.
EdD, Salk Fellow Why She's Running Families are squeezed by rising costs.
Cherri believes government should make life more affordable, not harder .
She'll fight to lower healthcare costs, defend union jobs, and deliver real relief.
