Meet Cherri
A lifelong Western Pennsylvanian raised in a middle-class, union household.
Both parents were proud United Steelworkers members.
Cherri learned early that dignity comes from hard work, perseverance, and looking out for one another.
A proud member of the Pittsburgh Federation of Teachers, Local 400.
A career nurse spanning occupational health, geriatrics, psychiatry, and health informatics.
Today she serves as a certified school nurse, ensuring children are healthy and ready to learn.
RN-CSN, MSN
Holds a Doctorate in Education from the University of Pittsburgh.
Twice selected for the prestigious Salk Fellowship, developing deep expertise in how Medicaid affects working families and seniors.
EdD, Salk Fellow

Union Jobs
Protecting collective bargaining rights and the freedom of workers to organize.
Good union jobs build strong communities.

- Support prevailing wage laws on public projects
- Fight against right-to-work legislation that weakens unions
- Protect pensions and retirement security for workers
Cherri's family: 3 generations of USWA members
02
Healthcare
No family should choose between paying bills and seeing a doctor.
Fighting to lower costs and protect access for everyone.
- Cap insulin and prescription drug costs
- Protect and expand Medicaid for working families
- Ban insurance coverage caps and protect pre-existing conditions
- Invest in rural healthcare access and mental health services
Cherri: RN with decades of clinical experience
03
Affordability
From groceries to housing to utilities, families are squeezed.
Government should make life more affordable, not harder.
- Raise the minimum wage from $7.25 to a living wage
- Pass paid sick leave and family leave legislation
- Property tax relief for seniors and working families
- Lower utility costs and fight price gouging
PA minimum wage: frozen at $7.25 since 2009
04
Public Education
Every child deserves a quality public education.
Cherri will fight for fully funded schools, fair teacher pay, and the resources students need to thrive.
- Fully fund public schools through fair funding formulas
- Support competitive teacher pay to recruit and retain great educators
- Invest in school nurses, counselors, and student mental health
- Hold cyber charter schools accountable for student outcomes
- Expand workforce training and career-readiness programs
Cherri: EdD from Pitt & certified school nurse
