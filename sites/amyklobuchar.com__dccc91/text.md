Amy Klobuchar is running for governor, with her running mate Ben Schierer, because Minnesotans deserve to have their work recognized and rewarded.
She’s running for every Minnesotan who wants to buy their first house; for every parent who wants a better world for their kids; for everyone who wants affordable health care.
As governor, Amy will focus on lowering costs for families, getting results for Minnesotans, rooting out fraud, and making the government accountable to the people of our state.
Amy Klobuchar has always been guided by the values she learned growing up in Minnesota — the granddaughter of an iron ore miner and the daughter of a newspaperman and a second-grade teacher.
She’s never shied away from taking on tough fights, whether that’s working to guarantee 48-hour hospital stays for new moms and babies, prosecuting fraud and corruption when she ran the biggest prosecutor’s office in the state, or taking on pharmaceutical companies, Big Tech, and Trump’s tariffs in the Senate.
She’s been ranked #1 for authoring bipartisan bills and #3 for passing bills into law.
Amy has worked to deliver results for Minnesotans, and that’s exactly what she’ll do as governor.
