ELIZABETH BELIEVES IN A
GOVERNMENT THAT
WORKS FOR THE PEOPLE
Elizabeth believes public policy should be practical, honest and accountable.
Government should protect working families, invest in the future, tell the truth, spend responsibly and use evidence instead of ideology.
That means getting big money out of politics, rebuilding public trust, modernizing public systems and making sure every federal dollar serves people, not corporations, monopolies or political insiders.
What Elizabeth Will Do
embrace Truth, Media Literacy and Public Trust
- Fight for media literacy education, stronger local journalism, transparency around political funding and accountability for misinformation that threatens public health, elections and community safety.
- Support strong, transparent oversight of artificial intelligence and emerging technology to protect privacy, reduce bias, prevent manipulated content and ensure workers are not left behind as the economy changes.
- Why it matters: Innovation should improve lives, not replace accountability.
Addiction, Mental Health and Harm Reduction
- Support evidence-based treatment, medication-assisted therapy, naloxone access, peer support and diversion programs for nonviolent offenses
- Support establishing mental healthcare inside jails, prisons and detention facilities.
- Support strong, transparent oversight of artificial intelligence and emerging technology to protect privacy, reduce bias, prevent manipulated content and ensure workers are not left behind as the economy changes.
- Why it matters: Addiction should be treated as a healthcare crisis, not a moral failure.
Responsible Budgeting and Fair Taxes
- Support closing tax loopholes for the wealthy and corporations,
- Lowering healthcare costs
- Cutting wasteful spending and protecting Social Security, Medicare, Medicaid and core services working families rely on.
- Why it matters: These programs are a promise made to the American People.
Rural and Small-Town Renewal
- From a rural farm town in Ohio, Elizabeth will fight for rural clinics, broadband, water infrastructure, local schools, small farms and workforce training
- Support investments that strengthen Main Street instead of Wall Street.
- Why it matters: Our rural areas are full of hardworking people who deserve the opportunity to thrive.
Elizabeth’s campaign is rooted in one simple promise: people first.
That means healthcare over insurance profits, families over corporate greed, truth over disinformation, public schools over privatization, clean water over special interests and democracy over dark money.
In Congress, Elizabeth will support practical, evidence-based policies that help working people thrive, protect civil rights, strengthen communities and rebuild trust in government.
