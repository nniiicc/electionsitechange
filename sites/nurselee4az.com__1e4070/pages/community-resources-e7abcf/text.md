Whether you are trying to navigate the complexities of Medicare, looking for a safe place to live, or seeking reproductive care, you shouldn't have to fight a mountain of bureaucracy just to find the help you need.
Elizabeth is running for Congress to be your advocate in Washington, but her commitment to serving the people of Gilbert, Chandler, Queen Creek, and Apache Junction starts right here at home.
This page is a curated collection of local, state, and federal resources designed to help our neighbors find stability, health, and opportunity.
Knowledge is power so please use these resources to advocate for yourself and your family.
Healthcare & Medicare AHCCCS (Arizona’s Medicaid): The primary portal for low-income individuals and families to apply for health coverage.
HealtheArizonaPlus.gov Medicare Assistance (SHIP): The State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) offers free, unbiased counseling for Medicare beneficiaries.
DES Medicare Assistance Community Health Centers: Valleywise Health: Provides comprehensive care regardless of ability to pay.
ValleywiseHealth.org Adelante Healthcare: Local clinics in Mesa and Chandler offering family and pediatric care.
AdelanteHealthcare.com Healthcare Marketplace: The official federal marketplace for ACA plans.
Healthcare.gov Reproductive Health, Abortion & Fertility Planned Parenthood Arizona: Information on reproductive healthcare services, including cancer screenings, birth control, and abortion access updates.
PlannedParenthood.org/Arizona Arizona Family Health Partnership: Connects residents to low-cost or no-cost sexual and reproductive health services statewide.
ArizonaFamilyHealth.org Fertility Resources: RESOLVE: The National Infertility Association provides support groups and insurance advocacy information for Arizonans.
Resolve.org Abortion Fund of Arizona: Financial and logistical support for those seeking abortion care.
AbortionFundAZ.org Affordable Housing & Utility Assistance AZ Housing Coalition: A central hub for finding emergency shelter and affordable housing programs.
AZHousingCoalition.org Community Action Agencies: These agencies provide emergency rental assistance and utility help (LIHEAP).
Maricopa County Human Services: Maricopa.gov/HumanServices Pinal County Community Action: Pinal.gov/CommunityAction Save Our Home AZ: Assistance for Arizona homeowners facing foreclosure.
Education & Youth Resources Arizona Department of Education (ADE): Resources for K-12 students, including school report cards and special education services.
Azed.gov/Parents First Things First: Early childhood education and health resources for children ages 0–5.
FirstThingsFirst.org AZ529 Plan: The state-sponsored college savings plan to help families save for future education costs.
AZ529.gov College Depot: Free college planning and scholarship resources through the Phoenix Public Library system.
PhoenixPublicLibrary.org/CollegeDepot General Community Support 2-1-1 Arizona: The quickest way for constituents to find local food banks, clothing, and crisis intervention.
Dial: 2-1-1 or visit 211Arizona.org St.
Mary’s Food Bank: Serving the East Valley with emergency food boxes.
FirstFoodBank.org Arizona Self Help: An easy "pre-screener" tool to see which of the 40+ state programs a family might be eligible for.
