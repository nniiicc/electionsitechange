Elizabeth believes that protecting the right to vote is not a partisan issue , it is the bedrock of representative government .
That means safeguarding access to the ballot, ensuring election integrity without creating unnecessary barriers, and restoring trust through transparency and accountability.
When people can participate fully and confidently in our elections, our government works better, reflects our communities more accurately, and remains accountable to the people it serves.
What Elizabeth Will Do Expand Voting Access + Protect Voting Rights Champion the John Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act to restore and strengthen protections against racial discrimination in voting.
Protect and expand early voting and vote-by-mail, building on Arizona's longstanding voting traditions.
Oppose discriminatory voter ID requirements and unnecessary barriers that suppress voter participation.
Make Election Day a national holiday so working Americans don't have to choose between earning a paycheck and exercising their right to vote.
Strengthen federal oversight and accountability in jurisdictions with documented histories of voting discrimination.
Ensure voting is accessible to all eligible Americans, including voters with disabilities, rural communities, and historically marginalized populations.
Why it matters: The right to vote is the foundation of our democracy, not a partisan privilege.
We honor Congressman John Lewis's legacy by ensuring every eligible American can make their voice heard and their vote count.
Democracy works best when every voice has an opportunity to be heard.
End Citizens United + Get Big Money Out of Politics Support a constitutional amendment to overturn Citizens United Require transparency in campaign donations Limit dark money influence Why it matters: Your vote shouldn't be drowned out by billionaire Super PACs.
Protect Election Integrity Defend election workers from harassment and threats Strengthen cybersecurity for voting systems Combat disinformation campaigns Why it matters: Elizabeth believes in facts, not conspiracy theories.
Elections are decided by voters, not lies.
