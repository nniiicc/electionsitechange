elizabeth believes in
PROTECTING DEMOCRACY +
VOTING RIGHTS
A functioning democracy depends on free, fair, and accessible elections, and right now those foundations are under real strain.
Elizabeth believes that protecting the right to vote is not a partisan issue, it is the bedrock of representative government.
That means safeguarding access to the ballot, ensuring election integrity without creating unnecessary barriers, and restoring trust through transparency and accountability.
When people can participate fully and confidently in our elections, our government works better, reflects our communities more accurately, and remains accountable to the people it serves.
What Elizabeth Will Do
Expand Voting Access
- Support early voting and vote-by-mail (which Arizona already does well!)
- Oppose voter ID laws designed to suppress turnout
- Make Election Day a national holiday
- Restore the Voting Rights Act
- Why it matters: Democracy works best when everyone participates.
Voting should be easy, safe, and accessible.
End Citizens United and Get Big Money Out of Politics
- Support a constitutional amendment to overturn Citizens United
- Require transparency in campaign donations
- Limit dark money influence
- Why it matters: Your vote shouldn't be drowned out by billionaire Super PACs.
Protect Election Integrity
- Defend election workers from harassment and threats
- Strengthen cybersecurity for voting systems
- Combat disinformation campaigns
- Why it matters: Elizabeth believes in facts, not conspiracy theories.
Elections are decided by voters, not lies.
