Rep.
Dave Rogers has spent years fighting to make housing more affordable, stable, and accessible for Massachusetts families.
Long before the housing crisis reached today’s level, Dave was a leader in advancing solutions that protect renters and expand opportunity.
He was the first legislator to propose giving cities and towns the local option to adopt rent control if their residents chose to do so, empowering communities with another tool to address rising housing costs.
Dave created the Office of Fair Housing to strengthen enforcement of fair housing laws and expand access to safe, equitable housing opportunities.
He also secured an additional $1 million for the Affordable Housing Trust to support the creation of new homes.
His commitment to making housing more affordable and fair earned him the Robert L.
Terrell Fair Housing Award from the Citizens’ Housing and Planning Association (CHAPA).
Dave has been a leading advocate for access to counsel for tenants facing eviction, because no family should lose their home simply because they cannot afford legal representation.
He has secured increased state funding to address homelessness and supported investments that help seniors, working families, and young people remain in the communities they call home.
As a champion of the Affordable Homes Act , Dave helped deliver the largest investment in housing in Massachusetts history.
The law funds the construction and preservation of tens of thousands of homes , expands assistance for first-time homebuyers , strengthens public and affordable housing, legalizes accessory dwelling units statewide, and gives communities new tools to increase housing production.
Throughout his career, Dave has believed that solving the housing crisis requires both building more homes and protecting the people who live in them.
He will continue fighting to ensure every Massachusetts resident has access to a safe, stable, and affordable place to call home.
FIGHTING FOR PUBLIC EDUCATION As Chair of the Higher Education Committee , Rep.
Dave Rogers has led the fight to make higher education more affordable, accessible, and responsive to the needs of Massachusetts students and families.
He secured historic increases in financial aid and state scholarship funds, strengthened support for public colleges and universities, and championed investments to modernize campuses, improve learning environments, and ensure students have the facilities they need to succeed.
Dave had long championed making community college tuition-free, and as Chair of the Higher Education Committee, he launched the Free Community College Program , making community college tuition-free for Massachusetts residents and expanding access to higher education across the Commonwealth.
By removing a major financial barrier, this historic investment has opened the doors of opportunity to thousands of students, workers, and parents who may have otherwise been unable to pursue a degree, gain new skills, or advance their careers.
Under Dave’s leadership, community college enrollment has increased by nearly 70 percent , creating unprecedented new opportunities for students while helping build the skilled workforce Massachusetts needs to compete in the future economy.
Years prior, Dave rallied in support of the “ Student Opportunity Act ”.
Signed into law in 2019, this sweeping education reform law invested $1.5 billion in Massachusetts' public schools, strengthening classrooms across the Commonwealth by expanding student support services, increasing resources for high-need districts, and giving schools the tools they need to empower every students to seize greater opportunities and builds a stronger future for Massachusetts.
Dave believes that a student’s future should not be determined by their ability to afford tuition.
By expanding access, investing in campuses, and strengthening financial aid, Dave is helping ensure that every Massachusetts resident has the opportunity to earn a high-quality education and build a better future.

Climate & Environmental Protection Protecting our environment has always been a deeply held commitment for Rep.
Dave Rogers.
With professional experience in the clean energy sector and at the U.S.
Environmental Protection Agency , Dave has dedicated his career to advancing practical solutions that protect our ecosystems, combat climate change, and build a cleaner economy.
Environmental stewardship is also a family value, Dave’s sister, Kathleen Rogers, has served as President of EarthDay.org for more than two decades, helping lead the global Earth Day movement.
Dave has made environmental leadership a cornerstone of his work in the Legislature.
Endorsed by the Environmental League of Massachusetts for his strong environmental record, he has championed policies to expand renewable energy, increase the Renewable Portfolio Standard, accelerate the development of offshore wind, and reduce greenhouse gas emissions across the Commonwealth.
Dave has also secured thousands in funding for environmental protection and clean energy initiatives across the district, including state funding for clean energy upgrades at Arlington's Hardy Elementary School , environmental restoration and landscaping improvements along the Alewife Brook Parkway in North Cambridge, and numerous other investments that have made Belmont, Arlington, and Cambridge more sustainable and resilient.
Dave believes that protecting our environment and growing our economy go hand in hand, and he will continue fighting for a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable future for the next generation.
Transportation & the MBTA Just like most members of our community, Dave has endured the painstaking process that is our morning and afternoon commutes.
He believes and successfully has fought for comprehensive solutions to our State’s transportation challenges, including a long-term revenue solution for the MBTA.
As a member of the MBTA Caucus, solving our transportation issues has been one of his highest priorities and just recently, he supported the passage of a major tax increase package to benefit transportation infrastructure, including additional funding to the T.
This bill, which was passed overwhelmingly in the House, will raise corporate taxes to invest those funds in transportation.
If the Governor and the federal government will not lead, Dave will and has, by taking tough votes to invest new revenue in our transportation network.
Dave also realizes that a vibrant transportation system is an environmental issue as every commuter on the T is one less car on the road and few carbon emissions.
By 2022/2023, the Red Line will be virtually a brand new system, with new cars, track and an improved electrical grid and that is in part due to the steady advocacy of Dave.
Dave is seeking another term as your Representative because he feels our State has unfinished business with regard to controlling healthcare costs.
Having previously served on the Joint Committee on Health Care Financing, he has continually supported expanded health insurance rights, and has formed the necessary relationships and skills to find solutions to this vital issue.
He has co-sponsored single-payer legislation, as well as introduced the “Cover All Kids” legislation, which would give health care rights to undocumented children.
Everyone deserves the right to make their own choices for their body, and Dave will continue to fight to ensure that everyone receives the proper coverage they need, no matter their choice.
Dave is proud to have supported the Advancing Contraception Coverage and Economic Security in our State, or ACCESS Bill.
He is particularly pleased to have co-sponsored this bill, which goes even further than the Affordable Care Act, which allowed health insurance companies to choose one form of contraception to cover within each of the 18 categories.
The ACCESS bill requires coverage of nearly all FDA-approved contraceptives, with no cost-sharing with consumers.
With this legislation, most Massachusetts women will be protected from whatever action the Trump administration takes on repealing birth control coverage.
