Thank You Illinois’ 5 th District!
This week I was privileged to be re-elected by Illinois’ 5 th District with 66% of the vote.
I am extremely grateful for your confidence in me and am looking forward to my continued service in the U.S.
House of Representatives.
As we move into the 113 th Congress, I will continue to lead the fight to make government work for our district by calling for more reform, transparency and accountability to government.
I will continue to work towards creating new jobs, building a stronger economy, improving access to health care and ensuring equal rights for all.
Again, thank you for all of your support.
It is an honor to serve you!
