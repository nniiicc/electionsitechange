This Privacy Policy explains how Amanda for MN Senate (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through this website.
1.
Information We Collect
We only collect personal information that users voluntarily provide through the website, including:
- Contact form submissions (such as name, email address, and any message content)
- Phone number if voluntarily provided for communication via call or text
We do not automatically collect personal data through analytics tools, cookies, advertising pixels, or tracking technologies.
2.
How We Use Information
We use personal information solely for campaign-related purposes, including:
- Responding to inquiries and messages

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information with outside parties for marketing or commercial purposes.
3.
Data Sharing and Disclosure
We may share personal information only in the following limited situations:
- With service providers who support website hosting or communication on our behalf and are obligated to protect the data

We do not share data with advertisers, analytics providers, or unrelated third parties.
4.
Data Security
We take reasonable administrative and technical steps to protect submitted personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
However, no transmission over the internet is fully secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
5.
Children’s Privacy
This website is not directed toward children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those third-party sites.
