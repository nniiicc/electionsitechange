A good life should still be within reach.
Residents of Woodbury and south Maplewood are working hard, raising kids, planning head, and building their future here.
But, they’re still feeling stretched.
My work is about protecting the things that make a good life possible:
- strong schools
- clean water and air
- accessible, quality healthcare
- supportive communities
- economic security
- opportunities for the next generation
Because Minnesota should be a place where people can thrive at every stage of life.
