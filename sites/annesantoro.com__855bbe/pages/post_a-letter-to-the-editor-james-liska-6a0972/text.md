A Letter to the Editor-James Liska
I have had the pleasure of spending time with Anne Santoro and Lee Elci during events in the East Lyme area.
Anne is well educated, hardworking, and puts her heart into the tasks she undertakes.
I have been a longtime listener and fan of Lee’s radio program and have met him at several local events.
I appreciate his willingness to listen, gather information, and make decisions that put ideas into action.
We share values and a connection to southern Connecticut.
In a community where we want the best for our families and neighbors, I believe Anne Santoro and Lee Elci are the best leaders to have.
My time with Anne and Lee has given me an appreciation for their strengths and dedicated involvement in our community.
James Liska
East Lyme, CT
Published in the CT Examiner
