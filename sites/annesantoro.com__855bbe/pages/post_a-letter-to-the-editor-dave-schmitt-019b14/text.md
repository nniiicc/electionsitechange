top of page Home Meet Anne News Contact Photos Get Involved All Posts Search A Letter to the Editor-Dave Schmitt Sep 28 1 min read To the Editor: I have observed Anne Santoro as a public official in East Lyme and gotten to know her as an active member of our community.
Anne will be a very effective legislator for the 37th District and Connecticut.
She has the knowledge, experience, and work ethic to address the failed policies that have damaged our state’s finances and economy.
From her service as Deputy First Selectman and member of the Board of Finance, and from her work as a municipal and business attorney, Anne has a broad-based understanding of municipal government and business which will be of great value in working for the residents of East Lyme, Salem, and Montville.
Anne also has the personal skills required to be a legislator.
She will listen, research, and work with others to find solutions to our state’s problems.
Knowing Anne, she will represent the 37th district with dignity and respect while working tirelessly to improve the lives of all Connecticut residents.
A vote for Anne is a vote for a better Connecticut.
It’s time for change.
