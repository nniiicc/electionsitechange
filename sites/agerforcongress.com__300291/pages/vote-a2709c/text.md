North Carolina’s 11th District
The 2026 election for North Carolina’s 11th Congressional District will be held on Tuesday, November 3rd, 2026.
The district covers the southwestern portion of North Carolina, including the counties of Avery, Buncombe, Cherokee, Clay, Graham, Haywood, Henderson, Jackson, Macon, Madison, McDowell, Mitchell, Swain, Transylvania, Yancey, and the northwestern portions of Polk.
If you aren’t sure whether or not you live in District 11, you can find your address on the North Carolina General Assembly’s interactive website.
