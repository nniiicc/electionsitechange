The district covers the southwestern portion of North Carolina, including the counties of Avery, Buncombe, Cherokee, Clay, Graham, Haywood, Henderson, Jackson, Macon, Madison, McDowell, Mitchell, Swain, Transylvania, Yancey, and the northwestern portions of Polk.
If you aren’t sure whether or not you live in District 11, you can find your address on the North Carolina General Assembly’s interactive website.

Frequently Asked Questions How can I check where or if I’m currently registered to vote?
You can check your voter registration by looking up your name on the North Carolina Board of Elections website .
How can I update my voter registration or register to vote?
The easiest way to update your voter registration or register for the first time is to use the NC DMV’s online portal, available here .
However, you can also register by mailing a voter registration application to your county’s Board of Elections or in-person during early voting.
The voter registration deadline for the general election is October 9th at 5:00 PM, but you can still do a same-day registration when you go to vote early in-person.
Learn more about registering to vote here .
How can I find my polling place for election day?
You can find your election day polling place by looking up your address on the NC State Board of Elections polling place search tool, located at vt.NCSBE.gov/PPLkup .
If you’re planning to vote early in-person, you can vote at any open polling place in the county where you’re registered.
Look up polling places in your county at vt.NCSBE.gov/EVsite .
What do I do if I know I can’t make it to the polls on election day?
Election Day is November 3, 2026!
If you know that you won’t be able to make it to the polls on that date, or if you just want to get voting out of the way, North Carolina offers two ways of voting absentee.
You can vote absentee-by-mail by requesting a mail-in ballot beginning in early September, or you can vote early in-person at any early voting site in your county between October 15-31, 2026.
You can learn more about voting absentee-by-mail here , and you can look up locations for voting early in-person here .
What do I need to know about Voter ID?
All voters will be asked to show a photo ID in order to vote in the 2026 election.
This can include your North Carolina Driver’s License, a state ID from NCDMV, your US Passport, a free photo ID card issued by the State Board of Elections , certain college or university student or employee ID cards , a military or veterans ID card, a tribal enrollment card with photo, or another photo ID issued by a US or North Carolina government agency.
If you are unable to show a photo ID, you will be asked to fill out a provisional ballot and fill out a photo ID exception form explaining your situation.
Absentee-by-mail voters must include a photocopy of an acceptable ID when returning their ballot.
Learn more about voter ID requirements at the State Board of Elections website.
