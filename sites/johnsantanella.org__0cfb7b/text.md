Meet Democrat John Santanella
Born and raised in Enfield, Democrat John Santanella graduated from Enfield High and Boston College before starting his career in marketing and product development for Milton Bradley, Nickelodeon, and Playskool.
Today he owns several successful businesses—allowing him to give back fulltime to our community.
John is a trusted voice for our Democratic values, with a proven record of delivering for our children, seniors, and families.
