After her appointment to the Ohio Senate in January of 2025, Jane proudly represents the 29th District, encompassing most of Stark County, including Canton, Alliance, Canal Fulton, Louisville, Massillon, and North Canton.
Jane learned the value of hard work, integrity, and perseverance from a young age.
These Midwestern values have guided her throughout her life—whether in her education, career, or public service.
She earned her undergraduate degree from Harvard University before obtaining her Juris Doctor from American University Washington College of Law.
After earning her J.D., Jane began her career as a lawyer before transitioning into politics.
She served as a magistrate and law clerk in Stark County’s court system before being appointed vice chairman of the Stark County Republican Party in 2010.
In 2017, Jane made history as the first woman ever elected Chair of the Ohio Republican Party.
Jane worked tirelessly to unite conservatives, strengthen the party, and deliver victories up and down the ballot.
She led a dynamic grassroots movement that engaged voters across Ohio, advocating for policies that supported law enforcement, safeguarded constitutional rights, and put Ohio’s families first.
Under her leadership, Ohio solidified its position as a stronghold for conservative values, advancing policies that promoted job growth, lower taxes, and a stronger economy.
As a wife, mother, and lifelong advocate for conservative principles, Jane knows firsthand the challenges that families face.
Whether it’s skyrocketing inflation, government overreach, or attacks on our values, she is committed to fighting for policies that strengthen our economy, create jobs, and keep Ohio families safe and prosperous.
