Accountability as a Moral Obligation
Scripture and moral philosophy alike insist that those entrusted with power must
be held to higher standards.
In public life, that means rejecting a system where:
- Corporate lobbyists wield more influence than constituents.
- Dark money floods elections without accountability.
- Lawmakers regulate industries in which they financially benefit.
- Ethical failures are excused while marginalized communities are blamed.
This is not a partisan critique—it is a moral one.
