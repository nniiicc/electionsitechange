Haynes believes in
A New Vision for Equitable Development
Development should not displace communities — it should strengthen them.
As a representative, I will advocate for:
- Federal investment in historically under-resourced neighborhoods.
- Support for minority-owned and locally rooted small businesses.
- Infrastructure funding that prioritizes public transit, broadband access, and climate-resilient construction.
- Community-centered economic planning that ensures residents benefit from growth.
- Development must be measured by who thrives--not by who is pushed
