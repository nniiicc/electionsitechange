Faith-Grounded Accountability: Ethical Governance That Delivers Justice
My commitment to Government Accountability is rooted in faith--but not in proselytizing or imposing belief.
It is rooted in a justice ethic that insists power must answer to the people it serves.
Across traditions and worldviews, there is a shared moral understanding that leadership without accountability becomes exploitation, and authority without ethics becomes abuse.
That conviction shapes how I will govern.
Faith, for me, demands honesty over hype, responsibility over rhetoric, ethical values over ethnic attacks and scapegoating, and justice over division.
It requires naming corruption clearly--and refusing to distract from it through fear, name-calling, and political gaslighting.
