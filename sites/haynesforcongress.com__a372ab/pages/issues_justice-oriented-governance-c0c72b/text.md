Justice-Oriented Governance
In Congress, my faith values will shape how I govern--not who I govern.
They will guide me to:
- Center the voices of those most impacted by injustice
- Hold powerful interests accountable
- Craft policies that repair harm rather than deepen it.
- Build coalitions across lines of belief, race, class, and party.
Justice is not about belief enforcement—it is about public responsibility.
