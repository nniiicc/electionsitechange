From the Outside to the Inside
My work challenging environmental racism has always been rooted in a simple
truth: zip code should not determine life expectancy.
In Congress, I will:
- Strengthen environmental justice provisions in federal law.
- Ensure communities most impacted by pollution are first in line for remediation and clean energy investments.
- Hold corporations accountable for environmental harm.
- Support a just transition to green jobs that provide living wages and local hiring.
- Environmental justice is public health, civil rights, and economic justice--combined.
