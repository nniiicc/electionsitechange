Policies for Educational Justice
I will champion:
- Equitable federal funding formulas that prioritize high-need districts.
- Increased investment in early childhood education.
- Resources for mental health services, counselors, and social workers in schools.
- Fair pay and support for educators.
- Pathways to higher education, vocational training, and workforce development
- without crushing debt.
Education policy must reflect the belief that every child, regardless of race, income,
or zip code, deserves excellence.
