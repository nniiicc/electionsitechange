Congressional Priorities for Economic Security
In Congress, I will fight for policies that:
- Cap interest rates nationally and close loopholes that allow predatory lenders to
- exploit vulnerable borrowers.
- Strengthen consumer protections and enforce fair lending laws.
- Expand Access to affordable housing, childcare, healthcare, and paid family leave.
- Support wage growth tied to the real cost of living, not outdated benchmarks.
- Invest in wealth-building opportunities, including home ownership, small business development, and student debt relief.
- Economic security must be treated as a public good — not a privilege reserved for those with generational wealth.
