ECONOMIC SECURITY
Economic security is not merely about having just enough to survive—it is about
whether families can cover their daily expenses, save for retirement and their children’s
futures, and manage setbacks without being pushed into crisis.
The True Cost of
Economic Security, developed by the Urban Institute, reminds us that real-life costs and
resources for housing, food, health care, childcare, transportation, and savings and debt
repayment must be included in measuring economic security.
In District 30, as in too
many districts across the country, families are one emergency away from financial
collapse.
That reality is compounded by predatory industries that profit from
desperation.
