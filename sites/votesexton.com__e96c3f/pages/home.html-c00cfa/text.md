Meet Tom
Tom, a retired USAF Colonel, Senior Leader Educator, and retired Major Construction Project Vice President, brings robust leadership, breadth of experience, and conservative values to the MN State House race.
His global experience as a combat Special Operations helicopter pilot and Combat Rescue Squadron Commander reflects his steadfast commitment to our Nation and our great State of Minnesota.
Tom prioritizes fiscal responsibility and economic growth for southern Minnesota.
Actively involved in the community, Tom volunteers on two different planning commissions, serves as Chairman of LakeFest (community Independence Day celebration), and actively participates in the American Legion Honor Guard, VFW, Knights of Columbus, and the Lakes Association.
After 37 years of wedlock, Tom and Jeanne celebrate their marriage each and every day!
