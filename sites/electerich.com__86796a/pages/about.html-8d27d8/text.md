In my time, I have been a dishwasher, factory laborer, janitor, and construction worker, to name a few.
I found a trade at age 16 as a field archaeologist, which led to a career in cultural resource management, studying and writing about Nevada history and archaeology.
I ran a small business producing interpretive signs, books, and pamphlets which shared the results of these studies with the public.
My wife Meg and I are 30-plus year residents of Silver City, on the Comstock in Lyon County.
We built a house here, raised our daughter, and continue to be proud, contributing members of our community.
I’ve served for eleven years on our town Citizen Advisory Board, where I’m currently Chair. ​ ​ Family I am the luckiest man in the world, I find myself part of an amazing family: my wife and companion of 46 years, my daughters, my grandchildren, and their life partners.
They remind me every day that I would repeat any of life’s wrong turns and misfortunes a thousand times over because they all led me here, with them, now .​ My Vision Nevada should be a place where everyone has the opportunity to make a good living working at a job they love.
Opportunity is the key, and taking advantage of opportunity requires: Good education.
Good health, and good health care.
An affordable economy, meaning: Wages stay ahead of the cost of living.
Housing is available and affordable.
You can keep food on the table, gas in the car, buy soccer shoes for the kids, and still have time and money to invest in yourself—improve your job skills, study for your real estate license, or make the down payment on that food truck and start being your own boss.
Education I support full funding for education, including Career and Technical Education and apprenticeship programs.
Nevada must modify the Pupil Centered Funding Plan to recognize and compensate rural school districts for the unique challenges they face.
Health and Health Care No one succeeds in life without their health and the available and affordable care that sustains it.
No one should be denied health care, including mental health, just because they don’t have the money.
I support full funding for programs which step in when private insurance or government plans, such as the Affordable Care Act or Medicaid, fall short.
An Affordable Economy The affordability crisis squeezing Nevada families is making it impossible to ever get ahead.
I will support legislation that creates better jobs with higher wages, while also supporting measures that keep costs down.
In District 39, paying for a place to live is eating up household budgets.
We need to encourage and increase housing construction, including fully funding the Nevada Housing Access and Attainability Act and the State Infrastructure Bank.
I also support incentivizing local governments to change planning and zoning that places undue restrictions on multi-family housing, and we must pursue opportunities to make suitable federal lands available for residential development.
Finally, I will fight to stop private equity funds from manipulating supply and shutting ordinary Nevadans out of the housing market.

It was a job I loved, although there were plenty of times in between when I worked at whatever I could find: factory labor, night janitor, dishwasher, construction, just to name a few.
I know what it is like to work at everything from a minimum wage job to various trades where your skill and effort were respected and rewarded.
I know what it is like to earn a comfortable professional living, and I’ve also experienced the satisfaction of having my own business and being my own boss.
I know that progress toward a better job is the key to a better life, and I will fight to make sure your state government stands with you as you make that journey.
