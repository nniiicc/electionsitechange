Mark Proctor has been named a 2026 Gun Sense Candidate by Moms Demand Action in recognition of his commitment to preventing gun violence and building safer communities.
After more than 25 years in law enforcement, Mark understands that effective public safety requires both experienced leadership and common-sense policies that help prevent tragedy before it happens.
Throughout his career, he dedicated himself to protecting Tennessee families, and he will bring that same commitment to the State House.
