Mark Proctor believes in
Women’s Healthcare
I trust women and families to make their own healthcare decisions.
Politicians should not be inserting themselves into deeply personal choices.
Tennessee ranks near the bottom nationally, around 46th, in women’s healthcare and mental health.
That is not where we should be.
Instead of restricting care, we should focus on improving it, expanding access, supporting women and families, and making sure people can get the care they need when they need it.
I support protecting a woman’s right to choose and ensuring access to quality, affordable healthcare.
We should be working to make healthcare better and more accessible-not limiting care.
