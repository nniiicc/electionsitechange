Mark Proctor's Stance on
Immigration & ICE
We need a system that is fair, lawful, and humane.
I do not support policies that create fear in our communities or separate families.
Local law enforcement should be focused on public safety, not used in ways that erode trust between officers and the communities they serve.
When people are afraid to call the police, it makes us all less safe.
Public safety depends on trust, and we should not undermine that trust or waste resources that could be used to better protect our communities.
