State Representative Antonio Parkinson is a decorated U.S.
Marine veteran, retired fire lieutenant, author, and four-time Tennessee Legislator of the Year, currently serving his eighth term in the Tennessee House of Representatives for District 98, representing Memphis and Shelby County.
His legislative leadership has driven landmark reforms in criminal justice, education, and workplace protections.
Notable achievements include the Healthy Workplace Act to combat workplace bullying, Kimberlee’s Law mandating full sentencing for aggravated rape convictions, and the Neighborhood Protection Act empowering communities against crime.
Beyond the legislature, Parkinson is a dynamic speaker and community advocate.
He founded initiatives like ABetterMemphis.com, the Block Party for Peace, and leads efforts to reduce infant mortality through the Fresh Starts Community Baby Shower.
As former Chair of the Tennessee Black Caucus of State Legislators and Democratic Caucus Vice Chairman, he brings a powerful voice to issues of health, education, public safety, and ways to put tax payer's dollars back into their households.
Parkinson’s compelling personal journey—from overcoming homelessness to becoming a respected public servant—resonates with audiences seeking authentic leadership and actionable insights.
His expertise and passion make him a sought-after speaker for conferences, media interviews, and civic events.
For interviews and speaking requests, visit tnrepparkinson.com or contact Ken Jobe at 901-277-0606.
