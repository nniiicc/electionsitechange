How do I vote for Valerie?
My name is Valerie Jones Giltner.
I am excited to announce my candidacy for State Representative as the Republican nominee for the 37th Representative District.
My commitment to you is to maintain the hard work ethic and community service for the people of the 37th District that they have been accustomed to for decades.
About Valerie
I am a retired critical care nurse, mother, wife, and involved member of our community.
I am a product of our Delaware Public Schools, having graduated from Sussex Central High School.
I am past President of the Delmarva Christian High School Parent – Teacher – Fellowship Board, and a lead fundraiser for Delaware Elite Basketball.
I have participated in numerous Food Bank of Delaware and Habitat for Humanity outreach efforts, and have many years of volunteer service with the Felton/Harrington Little League.
