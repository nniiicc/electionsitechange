Committed to being your strong voice in Dover How do I vote for Valerie?
If you are a resident of the 37th Representative District, you can vote for Valerie one of three different ways: Early Voting begins Friday December 8th.
Dates and Times for Early Voting: Friday, December 8 from 11:00 am – 7:00 pm Monday, December 11 – Thursday, December 14 from 11:00 am – 7:00 pm Friday, December 15 – Tuesday, December 19 from 7:00 am – 7:00 pm Early voting location: Department of Elections Warehouse 542 S.
Bedford St.
Georgetown, DE 19947 Absentee Voting: You may vote by absentee ballot if you are unable to appear at your assigned polling place on Special Election Day for a reason permitted by State law.
Eligible absentee voters may request an absentee ballot.
The last day absentee ballots requested will be mailed is Saturday, December 16, 2023 .
Your voted absentee ballot must be returned to the Department of Elections, Sussex County Office at 119 N.
Race St., Georgetown, DE 19947 by 8PM Special Election Day, December 21, 2023 .
Absentee Ballots cannot be returned to the Special Election Early Voting Site or Polling Places.
Voting In-Person: Polls are open from 7AM – 8PM on Election Day.
Your assigned Polling Place is listed on your Polling Place Card .
You may call the Department of Elections, Sussex County office at (302) 856-5367 or visit ivote.de.gov or gis.elections.delaware.gov to find your polling place.
