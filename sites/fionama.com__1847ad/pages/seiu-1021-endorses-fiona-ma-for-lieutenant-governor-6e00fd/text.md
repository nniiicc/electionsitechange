SEIU 1021 has endorsed Fiona Ma for California Lieutenant Governor in the November 3 General Election.
SEIU 1021 represents public and nonprofit workers across Northern California and says its election endorsements are made by members who want candidates who will stand with workers, protect strong contracts and support working standards.
Fiona is grateful for the support of SEIU 1021 members and for the broader labor coalition behind her campaign.
She has consistently emphasized that California’s economy is strongest when working people have a voice, good jobs and a fair opportunity to build a secure future.
