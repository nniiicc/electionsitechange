Fiona Ma served as keynote speaker at the Latino Coalition of San Benito County’s 29th Annual Visionary Women’s Luncheon in Hollister.
The event honored Ann Marie Sayers, Lucy Rodriguez and Rachel Perez for their leadership, service, cultural preservation and commitment to education.
The luncheon also supports the Latino Coalition Scholarship Fund, which helps local students continue their education after high school.
Fiona spoke about the importance of women’s leadership, community service and creating pathways to opportunity for the next generation.
The event brought together community leaders, elected officials, educators, business leaders and local families from across San Benito County.
