Fiona Ma is honored to earn the endorsement of the California Medical Association (CMA) , representing more than 50,000 physicians and medical students across California.
CMA President René Bravo, M.D., highlighted Fiona’s record of supporting healthcare financing and expanding access to care, calling her a pragmatic and effective leader at a time when California’s healthcare system faces significant financial pressures.
“California’s doctors and health care professionals are essential to the health and strength of our communities,” Fiona said.
“They deserve the workforce, resources and support needed to provide quality care to every Californian.” Read the California Medical Association’s full endorsement announcement.
