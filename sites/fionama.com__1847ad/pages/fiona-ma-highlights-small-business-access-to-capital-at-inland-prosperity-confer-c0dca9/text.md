Fiona Ma joined women entrepreneurs and regional business leaders at the 2026 Inland Prosperity Conference in Riverside for a discussion on small-business growth and access to capital.
Her session, “Main Street Momentum,” focused on what entrepreneurs need to become ready, resilient and bankable — including stronger connections to lenders, public programs, technical assistance and customers.
The conference brought together entrepreneurs, funders, lenders, public agencies and business-support organizations from across the Inland Empire and Coachella Valley.
Fiona began her public-service career advocating for small businesses and continues to make entrepreneurship and economic opportunity central to her work.
