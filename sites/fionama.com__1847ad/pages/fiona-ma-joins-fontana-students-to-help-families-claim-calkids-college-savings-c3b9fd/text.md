Fiona Ma joined students, educators and local leaders at Fontana High School to help eligible students claim their CalKIDS college savings accounts.
Students may be eligible for $500 to $1,500 through CalKIDS, with funds available for use at accredited colleges, universities, vocational schools and professional schools nationwide.
Fiona thanked San Bernardino County Superintendent Ted Alejandre, ScholarShare Investment Board Executive Director Cassandra DiBenedetto, Fontana Unified Superintendent Miki Rene Inbody, Principal Joe Malatesta, Fontana Mayor Acquanetta Warren, counselors and students who helped families connect with the program.
