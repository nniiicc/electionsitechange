Fiona Ma is proud to earn the endorsement of Congressman Robert Garcia in her campaign for California Lieutenant Governor.
Robert has been a tireless advocate for working families, affordable housing, infrastructure investment, and equality.
From his leadership as Mayor of Long Beach to his service in Congress, he has consistently fought to improve the lives of Californians.
“Robert has always been a principled leader who knows how to bring people together and get results,” said Fiona Ma.
“I am grateful for his friendship, confidence, and support.
Together, we will continue working to make California more affordable, create economic opportunity, and deliver for communities across our state.”
Join us on November 3, 2026, as we continue building a stronger, more affordable California for everyone.
