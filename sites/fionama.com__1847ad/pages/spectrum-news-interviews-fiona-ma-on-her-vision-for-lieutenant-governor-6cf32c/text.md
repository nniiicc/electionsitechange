Fiona Ma joined Spectrum News’ Inside the Issues for a conversation about her campaign and vision for the office of California Lieutenant Governor.
In the interview, Fiona discussed the experience she would bring to the job as California State Treasurer and a former member of the State Assembly, Board of Equalization and San Francisco Board of Supervisors.
Her campaign has focused on affordability, higher education, workforce development, economic opportunity and using the Lieutenant Governor’s statewide responsibilities more actively to help communities across California.
