Fiona Ma is grateful to receive the endorsement of Lancaster City Councilmember Cedric White for her campaign to become California’s next Lieutenant Governor.
Cedric’s life is a remarkable example of leadership through service.
From excelling on the football field in the NFL, to dedicating more than 25 years as a teacher and probation officer, to creating jobs as a successful business owner, and mentoring at-risk youth through the AV Community Uplift Foundation, he has never stopped giving back.
