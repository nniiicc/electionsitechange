California’s Official Voter Information Guide now includes Fiona Ma’s candidate statement for Lieutenant Governor.
Fiona’s statement focuses on affordability and the rising costs of housing, groceries, gas, insurance and healthcare.
She also points to the challenges facing small businesses and the need to reduce unnecessary red tape while keeping California economically competitive.
Fiona highlights her background as a Certified Public Accountant and her experience managing California’s finances as State Treasurer.
