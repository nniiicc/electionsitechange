Former Los Angeles City Councilmember Joe Buscaino has endorsed Fiona Ma for California Lieutenant Governor.
Fiona thanked Buscaino for his years of public service, including his time with the Los Angeles Police Department and his service representing Council District 15.
During his time on the City Council, Buscaino focused on the LA Waterfront, Port of Los Angeles operations and jobs, homelessness, and youth engagement through community policing.
