California has done it—and then some!
California State Treasurer Fiona Ma and her team opened the portal this morning and already 11,000+ people have signed up for the new Disneyland-themed specialty license plate to support California’s children’s hospitals.
They only needed 7,500 to move forward—thank you for showing up in a BIG way!
Through the California Health Facilities Financing Authority (CHFFA), which Fiona Ma proudly chairs, and in collaboration with Disneyland, they’re turning everyday moments into meaningful impact—funding critical programs and services for children and families across California.
