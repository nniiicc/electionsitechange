Fiona Ma’s 58 Counties.
1 California. series continues to highlight communities, businesses, institutions and local priorities across the state.
Recent installments have included Riverside County, where Fiona filmed in downtown Riverside and at Tio’s Tacos, along with new videos from Calaveras, Tehama, Colusa, Amador and El Dorado counties.
Each stop is part of Fiona’s commitment to show up, listen and understand how California’s challenges look different from one region to the next.
