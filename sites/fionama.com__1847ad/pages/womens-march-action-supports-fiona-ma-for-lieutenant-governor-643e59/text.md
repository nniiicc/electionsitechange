Fiona Ma is honored to be listed among the 2026 candidates supported by Women’s March Action .
Women’s March Action supports candidates who meet its criteria on reproductive freedom, gender equality and the rights of women.
The organization explicitly distinguishes this support from a formal endorsement and provides resources to candidates who align with those priorities.
Fiona has spent her career supporting women’s leadership, reproductive healthcare, economic opportunity and protections for survivors of domestic violence.
She is grateful to stand alongside leaders working to expand representation and opportunity for women across California.
