Fiona Ma met with members of California State Retirees in San Diego to discuss retirement security, healthcare and the affordability pressures facing retired public employees.
FOX 5 San Diego and KUSI News covered the visit in a segment titled “California State Treasurer Addresses CA Retirees.”
Fiona has long worked on retirement security as California State Treasurer and as a member of boards overseeing major public pension systems.
She said retirees deserve stability, responsible stewardship and confidence that the benefits they earned will be protected.
