LAist and CalMatters have published their 2026 guide to California’s Lieutenant Governor race , profiling Democrat Fiona Ma and Republican Gloria Romero ahead of the November 3 General Election.
The guide explains the responsibilities of the office, including serving on California’s higher-education governing boards, the State Lands Commission and other statewide bodies, and compares the candidates’ backgrounds, endorsements and campaign finances.
Fiona’s profile highlights her experience as California State Treasurer and her previous service on the Board of Equalization, in the State Assembly and on the San Francisco Board of Supervisors.
