Democratic Lieutenant Governors Association (DLGA) Executive Director Kevin Holst released the following statement congratulating Fiona Ma on her victory in the primary for California Lieutenant Governor:
“Fiona Ma has spent more than two decades delivering for California’s working families as a legislator, a member of the Board of Equalization, and State Treasurer, where she has managed more than $3 trillion in annual payments and overseen a portfolio of more than $124 billion on behalf of California taxpayers.
She knows how to make government work for working people.
