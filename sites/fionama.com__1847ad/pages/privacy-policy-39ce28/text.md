Privacy Policy

- The date and time you accessed FionaMaForTreasurer.com.
- The IP address from which you accessed FionaMaForTreasurer.com.
- The type of browser software used to view FionaMaForTreasurer.com.
When you visit the FionaMaForTreasurer.com website, personally identifying information is not collected, unless you choose to provide such information.

Submitted information is collected and used for campaign related purposes related to Fiona Ma’s campaign.
We also work closely with Democratic candidates, parties and allied organizations and may share some basic contact information with them, and they may contact you.
Personal and financial information is required when making a contribution to Fiona Ma’s campaign.
This information is used to process your contribution or merchandise request.
All contributions made through FionaMaForTreasurer.com are regulated by California election law.
