The North Carolina House Democratic Caucus selected Rep.
Robert Reives to serve another two-year term as House Democratic Leader, through 2024.
“I appreciate this vote of confidence in my leadership from the House Democratic Caucus,” Leader Reives said.
“I look forward to working with our newly elected members and everyone who was re-elected to continue our work making North Carolina the best it can be.
Over the past two years as Leader, we have accomplished a lot for North Carolina, including sustaining all of Governor Cooper’s vetoes.”
