AMERICA FIRST, ALWAYS.
Scott Petersen is a lifelong conservative , Nebraska business owner , and proven Republican leader who believes government should serve the people, not political insiders or outside interests. ​ He is running to restore confidence that our Secretary of State is fulfilling the constitutional duties of the office.
He will work hard to deliver secure , accurate elections with full transparency and verifiable results.
He also believes we can streamline and modernize our business services division to make it easier to do business in Nebraska . ​ Scott believes the office must return to its core mission: constitutional governance , transparent and publicly verifiable elections , and responsible stewardship of taxpayer resources . ​ Scott and his wonderful wife Ann live in Omaha.
He is committed to bringing disciplined, America First leadership and putting Nebraska First in everything he does. ​ With decades of real-world experience and a record of conservative leadership, Scott brings the discipline and perspective needed to restore confidence that Nebraska can "Play it Right ."
