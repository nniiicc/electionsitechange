Scott Petersen is a lifelong conservative, Nebraska business owner, and proven leader who believes our state works best when government is accountable, constitutional, and firmly in the hands of the people, not bureaucrats, global interests, or political insiders.
For more than two decades, Scott built and ran a successful Nebraskabased business, creating jobs, meeting payroll, and navigating the same state systems every day Nebraskans rely on.
That real-world experience shaped his belief that government should serve citizens efficiently, transparently, and without unnecessary interference.
He understands that when records are accurate, rules are clear, and systems function properly, families and businesses thrive.
Scott has spent years earning the trust of Nebraska Republicans through consistent leadership and service.
He currently serves as Vice Chairman of Nebraska’s Second Congressional District, is a member of the Nebraska Republican Party Executive Committee and previously led as Chairman of the Douglas County Republican Party.
In 2024, Scott was honored to represent Nebraska as a National Delegate to the Republican National Convention, proudly casting his vote for President Donald J.
Trump and standing shoulder-to-shoulder with the America First movement.
Scott is not new to the responsibility of safeguarding Nebraska’s institutions.
He served on the Nebraska Republican Party’s select legislative subcommittee on election integrity, helping develop reform frameworks adopted by party leadership in 2022.
That work reinforced his core belief: trust in government is not demanded, it is earned through transparency, accountability, and adherence to the law.
As Secretary of State, Scott Petersen will focus on doing the job the right way.
His plan prioritizes constitutional governance, Nebraska sovereignty, and practical reforms that make state services work better for the people they serve.
He supports secure, transparent, and publicly verifiable elections, streamlined business and records services, and firm resistance to federal overreach, while keeping Nebraska values front and center.
Scott believes the Secretary of State’s office is stewardship, not a political trophy.
It exists to protect elections, preserve records, defend the Constitution, promote our trade interests, and ensure Nebraska remains free, fair, and accountable to its citizens.
Scott Petersen is running for Secretary of State to put Nebraska first, restore confidence in our institutions, and ensure the rule of law – not special interests – serves as the guide for every decision.
