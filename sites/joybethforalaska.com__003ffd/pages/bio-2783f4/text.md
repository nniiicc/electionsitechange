She earned a Bachelor of Science in Biology, Associates in Municipal Fire Science, and an Associates in Emergency Medical Services from the University of Alaska Fairbanks throughout her career.
In 2003, she became the first female firefighter ever hired at Fairbanks Fire Department.
Promoting through Driver and Captain ranks to Battalion Chief, she left employment at the City of Fairbanks to pursue her passion educating young firefighters at the University Fire Department.
She continues to teach student firefighters the tricks of the trade, to implement cost-saving measures, and to support other women and minorities entering the fire service.
The department now employs a workforce of over 1/3 women.
She spends her free time pursuing a variety of recreational hobbies including fishing and hunting with family, fly-tying, adventuring with her pups, and motorcycling, and she strength trains regularly for health and career longevity.
