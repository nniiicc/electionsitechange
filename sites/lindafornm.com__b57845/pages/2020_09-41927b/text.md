BIG NEWS!
Congresswoman Deb Haaland endorsed my campaign for State Representative for House District 45!
I'm honored to have the endorsement of Rep.
Deb Haaland.
She has many titles - organizer, trailblazer, community leader - and now Congresswoman.
Since being elected, she's working every day to be our voice at the table.
She's fighting to bring our priorities to the attention of her colleagues in Congress.
This in addition to our recent endorsement from Run for Something which supports young progressives running for their community.
Now I'm eager to stand for all of my neighbors on our side of Santa Fe in the Roundhouse.
Our community has been disproportionately impacted by COVID-19 and our families are worried about their health, families, and wallets.
When the legislature returns in January, I'm ready to stand for policies that are going to protect working families.
