QUANTÁ CREWS BACK TO THE HOUSE!
I’m honored to serve every resident of our district, no matter your background.
My focus is on building an informed, engaged community through open communication, because trust is key to progress.
My platform tackles the issues that matter most to us.
With my lived experiences and policy work, I bring a necessary perspective to the legislature.
Every day, democracy is tested, and we need strong advocates to stand firm and protect our freedoms.
I'm committed to being that voice for all of us.
HOUSING SECURITY Housing security is a top priority... everyone deserves a safe, stable home.
I’m committed to expanding affordable housing, preventing homelessness, and investing in sustainable development.
By tackling the root causes of the housing crisis and promoting fair housing, we can ensure every resident has the chance to thrive.
VOTING RIGHTS Protecting and expanding voting rights is crucial to our democracy.
Every eligible voter deserves easy, equitable access to the ballot.
I’m committed to fighting voter suppression and pushing for policies like automatic voter registration for those released from prison.
By promoting voter education, increasing access to polling locations, and supporting early voting and mail-in ballots, we can ensure fair elections and empower every citizen to make their voice heard.
QUALITY EDUCATION Every child deserves access to quality education.
I will continue to fight for funding and resources to ensure our schools are equipped to provide a world-class education.
GUN VIOLENCE PREVENTION Preventing gun violence is key to keeping our communities safe.
I support common-sense gun laws that protect families while respecting responsible ownership.
This means universal background checks, banning assault weapons, and closing dangerous loopholes.
Together, we can create a safer environment where our children can thrive without the fear of gun violence.
CRIMINAL JUSTICE REFORM Criminal justice reform is critical for a fair and equitable system.
I’m focused on reducing mass incarceration, ending cash bail, and promoting restorative justice.
By advocating for alternatives to incarceration and prioritizing rehabilitation over punishment, we can create a more just, compassionate society that respects everyone’s dignity and rights.
ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE I am fighting to ensure clean air and water for generations.
We have been gifted with this amazing state and should be the very best stewards of this beautful place we all call home.
That incluses responsibly taking care of it including wildlife and resources.
REPRODUCTIVE FREEDOM Reproductive freedom is a fundamental right that must be protected.
I am committed to ensuring that everyone has access to comprehensive reproductive healthcare, including contraception, family planning, and safe, legal abortion services.
Decisions about reproductive health should be made by individuals and their healthcare providers, free from political interference.
By safeguarding these rights, we empower people to make the best choices for themselves and their families.
HEALTHCARE EQUITY Healthcare is a right, not a privilege.
I am dedicated to making healthcare more affordable and accessible for every family in our district.
I also believe in the right to privacy and seperation of church and state.
ECONOMIC OPPORTUNITY I believe in creating jobs, expanding skilled trades training & apprenticeships and supporting small businesses.
Let’s build a local economy that works for everyone, with fair wages and opportunities for growth.
