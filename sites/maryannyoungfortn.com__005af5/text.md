Meet Mary Ann Young Murfreesboro has been home for nearly 30 years.
I have built a life in Murfreesboro, raising 3 sons here, putting down deep roots with my husband whose family is from Rutherford County.
Watching Murfreesboro Grow That growth is part of why I am running.
My path into local politics started not in a campaign office, but in my own neighborhood, when concerns over warehouse development and annexation pushed me to get involved and speak up for the community's future.
That same instinct, to show up and fight for the people in our community, has carried me into opposing the Middle Point Landfill expansion and advocating for safer roads in our area.
Professional Career I have 28 years of experience in fintech and B2B payments.
Previously served as Director of Digital Customer Experience Product Manager at Corpay.
I hold a Certified Scrum Product Owner (CSPO) certification and a Human Centered Design certification from LUMA Institute.
I have lead large-scale projects, including a platform migration of more than 100,000 users.
Why Run?
That same problem-solving mindset, listening first, then building real solutions, is what I will bring to the Tennessee House of Representatives.
I am running for District 37 because I believe in Young Leadership that delivers Stronger Community , leadership focused on responsible growth, better infrastructure and protecting education funding for the families who call this district home.
