What I Stand For Melissa Elder for OK Senate I’m running for the Oklahoma State Senate because I believe our community deserves leadership that listens to the people and delivers results.
I was born here and have raised my family in Oklahoma, and like so many other Oklahomans I value authenticity and generosity.
These are the values that define who we are.
Throughout my career as a social worker and licensed mental health provider, I’ve worked to listen to what people need and bring them together to solve problems.
I’ve worked alongside and seen firsthand the dedication of teachers, healthcare professionals, small business owners, and working families who help keep our communities strong every day.
I want to make sure they have the support they need to keep doing that important work.
It’s time to focus on what really matters: strong public schools, healthy families, and an economy that works for everyone.
Why I’m Running I’ve seen firsthand how teachers, small business owners, and healthcare workers strengthen our communities every day.
But, to do what they do, they need support from leaders to create opportunities and laws that support their work, their health, and their families.
My campaign is about leading and partnering with the people of Oklahoma to find solutions that benefit everyone.
District 24 Coverage Area Melissa Elder proudly represents Oklahoma Senate District 24, serving communities across Moore, SW OKC and North Norman.
