Serving Our Veterans & Seniors
Serving our veterans and seniors is a fundamental expression of gratitude, respect, and recognition for their invaluable contributions to our nation.
As a society, we owe a debt of gratitude to those who have served our country in the military and to our seniors who have dedicated their lives to building our communities and shaping our collective future.
We must ensure our veterans and seniors have access to high-quality affordable healthcare services, including preventative care, mental health support and long-term care to address their specific needs and preserve their quality of life.
As important, it is essential to provide them with the support they need to achieve economic security, including access to affordable housing, job training, and financial assistance programs, to ensure that they can live with dignity and independence.
