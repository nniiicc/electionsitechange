Third Reader Votes A "third reader" vote is the final vote that is taken on a bill in the House.
Each bill receives three readings on the floor in each chamber before it becomes law.
The first reading is when the bill is introduced and referred to the appropriate committee.
The second reading is when a bill comes back to the floor after being amended and passed out of its respective committee (not all bills get this far).
This is also when the bill can be amended on the floor by all members of the chamber.
The third reading for "final passage" is when the final version of the bill with all amendments is voted on before making its way to the Senate, or the Governor if the bill started out in the Senate.
As your elected representative, I believe in transparency and letting my constituents know how I vote on every bill that comes to the Senate floor.
