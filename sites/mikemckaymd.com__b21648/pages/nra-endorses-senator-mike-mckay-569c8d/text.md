The political bosses in Annapolis and Baltimore want to take away the guns of law-abiding Marylanders, and Senator McKay is leading the charge to stop them.
In Western Maryland, the Second Amendment is not an abstract debate.
It is the rifle handed down from a grandfather, the opening day that empties classrooms and job sites across Allegany, Garrett, and Washington Counties, and the right to protect your family in places where help can be a long way off.
“I am grateful to the NRA for this endorsement, and to every gun owner in Western Maryland who has stood with me,” McKay said.
“The folks in Annapolis and Baltimore who want to take your guns are not going to quit, and neither am I.
