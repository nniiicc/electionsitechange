Georges Creek Task Force Action Report Funding and Policy Intent February 28, 2026 Dear Chair Guzzone, Vice Chair Rosapepe, and Members of the Committee, I’m writing to express my strong support for the proposed budget intent language advancing the recommendations of the Georges Creek Task Force Action Report to mitigate future flooding impacts in Allegany County.
This measure provides a coordinated and proactive framework directing the Maryland Department of Emergency Management, Maryland Department of the Environment, and Maryland Department of Transportation to work collaboratively to strengthen flood resilience, improve infrastructure protections, and enhance emergency response capacity.
By prioritizing policy revisions, legislative proposals, and funding allocations, the proposal reflects a comprehensive approach to safeguarding communities and preventing future losses in the Georges Creek watershed.
The devastating flooding that occurred in May 2025 caused significant damage to homes, businesses, and critical public infrastructure, underscoring the urgent need for sustained State action.
The Task Force recommendations address key recovery and mitigation priorities, including reimbursement for damages not covered by the Federal Emergency Management Agency, enhanced coordination with federal partners, investment in floodproofing and relocation of vulnerable infrastructure, and the development of long-term flood mitigation strategies such as voluntary property buyouts and hydraulic studies.
Additionally, the proposal strengthens local and regional capacity through training, infrastructure investment, stream maintenance, and environmental protection measures that balance public safety with responsible stewardship.
The Georges Creek Task Force Action Report Funding and Policy Intent further supports the provision requiring ongoing accountability and transparency through regular reporting by the Western Maryland delegation on flood mitigation progress.
This oversight ensures continued focus on implementation and reinforces the State’s commitment to protecting residents, supporting economic stability, and improving disaster preparedness.
For these reasons, I respectfully request favorable consideration of this intent language as a critical step toward building long-term resilience and protecting the health, safety, and welfare of communities impacted by flooding in the Georges Creek region.
Sincerely, Senator Mike McKay Representing the Appalachia Region of Maryland Serving Garrett, Allegany, and Washington Counties
