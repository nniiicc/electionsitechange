8th Annual Mike McKay Golf Tournament
September 11 @ 10:00 am - 3:00 pm
Grab your foursome and play a round for Western Maryland.
Senator Mike McKay’s 8th Annual Golf Tournament tees off with a 10am shotgun start on Friday, September 11 at Rocky Gap in Flintstone.
Play: $425 per 4-person team, or $125 per individual golfer.
Sponsor the day: Red Level $500, White Level $750, Blue Level $1,000.
Hole and underwriting sponsorships are also available.
Register by August 31.
Reserve your spot online, or email your golfers’ names to bud@willetts.com or call Bud Willetts at 301-697-8790.
Maryland contribution limits apply: individuals 18+, PACs, and businesses may give up to $6,000 per 4-year cycle (Jan. 1, 2023 – Dec. 31, 2026); married couples up to $12,000.
