Stay Connected, Sign up for Email Updates Email Follow Vince on Social Dear Neighbor, The Central Valley is my lifelong home.
I was born and raised here, and today my wife and I are raising our son here.
I am proud of my roots, and it is the honor of a lifetime to represent our community in the United States Congress and fight for our way of life, each and every day.
Our state and nation face critical issues that demand real solutions.
In Congress, I am working to lower taxes, eliminate crushing regulations, address the high cost of living, support law enforcement, end illegal immigration, and work with local job creators to help our economy grow and our families prosper.
When I was elected, I promised never to lose sight of our shared values— faith, family, and patriotism.
I stand by that commitment and consider it an honor to uphold those values in our nation’s capital.
Thank you for the opportunity to serve you.
