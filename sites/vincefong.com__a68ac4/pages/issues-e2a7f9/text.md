On the Issues
Energy Independence
America should never have to rely on foreign adversaries to power our economy.
We are blessed with abundant natural resources, and we must unleash responsible domestic energy production to lower costs for families and small businesses.
Energy independence strengthens our national security, creates good-paying jobs, and ensures California and the nation remain competitive.
We can protect our environment while also developing reliable, affordable energy here at home.
Water and Agriculture
Water is the lifeblood of the Central Valley and the backbone of our agricultural economy.
Our farmers feed the nation and the world, and they deserve reliable water infrastructure that reflects science, not politics.
We must modernize storage, improve water conveyance, and stop policies that send water out to sea while our communities suffer.
Supporting agriculture means supporting jobs, food security, and the economic strength of our region.
Aerospace and National Defense
Our aerospace and defense industries are critical to both our local economy and our national security.
Our military installations play a vital role in advancing innovation, testing, and military readiness.
We must ensure our armed forces have the tools and technology they need to deter threats and protect our freedoms.
Investing in aerospace leadership strengthens America’s position in the world and supports high-skilled jobs at home.
Immigration and Border Security
A secure border is fundamental to national sovereignty and public safety.
We need an immigration system that is orderly, lawful, and fair—but that begins with enforcing existing laws and stopping the flow of illegal crossings.
Border security protects our communities, prevents human trafficking and drug smuggling, and upholds the rule of law.
We can support legal immigration while ensuring our border is secure and our laws are respected.
Protecting the Second Amendment
The Second Amendment is a constitutional right that safeguards the freedoms of law-abiding Americans.
Responsible gun owners should not be punished for the actions of criminals.
We must focus on enforcing existing laws and holding violent offenders accountable, rather than restricting the rights of citizens who follow the law.
Protecting constitutional freedoms remains a cornerstone of our republic.
