Meet Jessica Killin
A proud fourth-generation Coloradan, Jessica is a devoted mother, Army veteran, and public servant running for Congress to restore competence, courage, and character to Washington.
Raised in Colorado Springs by public school educators, she learned the value of hard work and public service.
She served as a Captain and paratrooper in the U.S.
Army, leading soldiers overseas and participating in peacekeeping missions.
She has since dedicated her career to improving the livelihoods of all Americans, including over a decade at USAA where she worked to protect military families during the 2008 financial crisis, streamline VA home loans, and help servicemembers avoid predatory lenders.
She then returned to public service in leadership roles on Capitol Hill and at the White House.
Her time in Washington showed her the urgent need for change in both parties.
Now, Jessica is ready to bring her patriotism, her proven tenacity, and her independent voice, to deliver solutions that improve the lives of Coloradans.
