Iron Range mining taxes drive the Iron Range Resources and Rehabilitation Board, and is responsible for millions in economic development each and every year in the Taconite Assistance Area.
Properly managing the resources of our Range is of the highest priority.
Jobs & Training Jobs & Training Jobs & Training The Iron Range is abundant in job opportunities unavailable elsewhere in the nation.
Our community benefits from local career path programs taught in our schools and the advanced technical programs provided in places like the Minnesota North College system.
We bolster the viability of the whole region when we can help connect people in our community to the right training and the right, suitable and gainful careers.
Prioritizing the creation of employment opportunities while providing employers with high-quality job candidates is a key focus.
Taxes Jobs & Training Energy Minnesota has long been coined as the “Land of 10,000 taxes”.
This was proven in the 2023 legislative session, when Minnesota was found to have approximately 17.5 billion dollars in surplus money collected from taxpayers.
Despite the record surplus, Democrats raised taxes by more than $10 billion that will hit every corner of our state and make everything we purchase more expensive.
Working families and social security recipients deserve meaningful and permanent tax relief.
Energy Second Amendment Energy In 2023, The Minnesota Legislature passed bills that will force us into more expensive and less reliable power situations across the state.
Local utility management agencies have confirmed that the technology to implement their plans not only doesn’t exist but will wildly increase the cost to consumers to double or more current costs.
I support a balanced and diverse approach to energy, with an emphasis on what is affordable for all Minnesotans.
