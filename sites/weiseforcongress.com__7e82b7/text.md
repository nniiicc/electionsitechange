It was never meant to be this way.
The status quo created these problems, and it will not solve them.
Washington needs a change in leadership, a new generation that is willing to go against the political establishment, defend our constitutional freedoms, and fight for the liberties of all people.
I am prepared to be that change.
I am running to represent the people of Southwest Ohio - not only AIPAC donors or MAGA supporters.

Priorities Make America Affordable Again Life is becoming increasingly unaffordable for Ohioans.
Reckless government spending has fueled inflation, driving up the cost of groceries, housing, healthcare, childcare, and higher education.
Nathan supports responsible spending reforms and policies that make homeownership more attainable, helping families keep more of what they earn and achieve long-term financial stability.
Stop Government Mass Surveillance Government mass surveillance is dangerous.
Not only is it a violation of the Fourth Amendment and individual privacy rights, but it also creates cybersecurity risks, undermines personal autonomy, and increases the potential for abuse by law enforcement and others with access to sensitive information.
Nathan believes that Americans should be free from unwarranted government monitoring and that constitutional protections against unreasonable searches and surveillance must be upheld.
End the Wars War has not only cost the United States trillions of dollars, but it has also been a major driver of anti-American sentiment around the world.
Nathan supports ending ongoing foreign wars, commits to rejecting future unjust wars, and believes the United States should reduce the number of its active military bases abroad.
In his view, there is no reason for the United States to serve as the world police.
Immigration Reform Nathan supports an Ellis Island-style immigration policy that makes entry into the United States easier and provides a simple path to permanent residency.
He supports eliminating the militarization of border security and immigration enforcement, believing that force should not be used against individuals who are not violent.
Abolish ICE and replacing it with nothing.
