PROVEN LEADERSHIP IN PUBLIC SAFETY
Since taking office as Sheriff in 2016, I have led both the Corrections and Field Services divisions with a commitment to innovation, accountability, and public safety.
My team and I have implemented programs and policies that enhance community protection, improve rehabilitation efforts, and strengthen partnerships across Livingston County.
Here are just a few of our key accomplishments:
- Solving a 40-Year-Old Homicide: Bringing long-overdue closure to a grieving family and demonstrating our relentless pursuit of justice.
- Combating the Opioid Crisis: Established a Vivitrol Program in partnership with Livingston County Community Mental Health and implemented a Medically Assisted Treatment (MAT) Program to support opioid users upon release from jail.
- Enhancing Jail Security: Introduced two trained K9 units to detect contraband and improve facility safety.
- Supporting Schools & Youth: Partnered with L.E.S.A. to address truancy and literacy issues, while also increasing the number of School Resource Officers to ensure safer learning environments.
- Strengthening Community Outreach: Created a Community Outreach Unit to collaborate with schools, nonprofits, and service providers, working together to address local challenges.
- Achieving Statewide Accreditation: Led a full policy and procedure overhaul in our Field Services division, making us the first Sheriff’s Office in Michigan to be accredited by the Michigan Law Enforcement Accreditation Commission.
- Cutting Costs for Taxpayers: Partnered with Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan to reduce healthcare costs for inmates requiring off-site medical treatment.
- Leading Statewide Training Initiatives: Hosted Michigan’s first-ever mock riot training exercise, bringing together local, state, and federal agencies to prepare for large-scale emergency situations.
These achievements would not have been possible without my highly dedicated and motivated staff.
I encourage you to visit the Livingston County Sheriff’s Office website for more details, including our annual reports that showcase the full scope of our efforts to keep our community safe.
