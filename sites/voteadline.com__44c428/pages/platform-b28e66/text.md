A story about Adline's pre-filed disabled voter bill aired in December 2025 on WSFA_TV : Rep.
Adline Clarke (D-Mobile) said disabled voters are at a disadvantage at the ballot box under current state law.
She said House Bill 45 is aimed at bridging that gap.
At the bottom of Alabama’s absentee ballot, there is a section that says “only the voter may hand this application to the absentee election manager” or “the voter may also forward this application through the mail.” “That is problematic for a whole lot of people,” said Rep.
Clarke.
She said the rules are not doable for Alabamians with severe mobility challenges or who are bedridden.
HB 45 would allow a disabled person to designate a person to handle their ballot for them.
“We just simply need to remove or amend that language so that disabled persons are free to have someone act on their behalf,” she said.
