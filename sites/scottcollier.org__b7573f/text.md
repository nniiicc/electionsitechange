Here's a sample: Why are you running for office?
I want to work with the citizens of District 7 to turn the tide on crime, gangs, educational issues and the out of control drug problems that have greatly diminished the standard of living in many of our communities for far too long.
Moreover, the ongoing issues with our juvenile and criminal justice systems are alarming as well as the growing economic and illegal immigration problems we face.
Another priority is putting an end to food deserts in our communities.
Yet another priority is to continue working with members of the Law Enforcement Community and groups that support our officers because when we do, our communities are safer and more tranquil environments to live, work and play.

Many thanks to everyone who voted to make me your Congressional candidate again in 2024.
I also want to extend my thanks to everyone who has supported my efforts as a community advocate and a disability advocate for over a decade.
My supporters mean the world to me and to win OUR “WE THE PEOPLE RULE AMERICA” grassroots campaign your support is needed more than ever!
In 2014 and 2018 I ran for the 6th district state senate seat as an independent.
I also ran for the 2nd district congressional seat in 2020 as a Republican and won the District 7 nomination in 2022.
However, I am not a politician--I am an extremely frustrated American who wants to be an agent of positive change for our district and the American people!
I have never taken a dime from anyone for any of my campaigns and never will.
Money is a huge problem in our election process and that needs to change.
I want voters to vote for me because of the things I have done to improve our communities as well as our beautiful and amazing nation, not how much money I raise.
I want to focus on solving important issues and taking care of my constituents' needs rather than hitting them up for some of their hard-earned-pay!
If I am blessed enough to win in November I will strive to provide the best constituent service residents have ever seen in our district.
My staff and I will work very closely with the communities to improve the quality of life of all residents and to ensure future generations have a community they can proudly call their home.
