Why are you running for office?
I want to work with the citizens of District 7 to turn the tide on crime, gangs, educational issues and the out of control drug problems that have greatly diminished the standard of living in many of our communities for far too long.
Moreover, the ongoing issues with our juvenile and criminal justice systems are alarming as well as the growing economic and illegal immigration problems we face.
Another priority is putting an end to food deserts in our communities.
Yet another priority is to continue working with members of the Law Enforcement Community and groups that support our officers because when we do, our communities are safer and more tranquil environments to live, work and play.
