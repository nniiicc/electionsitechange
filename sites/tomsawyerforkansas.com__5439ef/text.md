About Tom Sawyer
State Representative Tom Sawyer is dedicated to serving the people of Wichita's 95th District with integrity and commitment.
Tom Sawyer is a Native Wichitan.
He was born at St.
Joes Hospital in south Wichita.
He grew up watching Major Astro, going to Joyland and the NBC tournament.
He graduated from Wichita State with a BBA in Accounting.
He is active in our community.
As your Kansas State Representative, he strives to ensure that your voice is heard and that your concerns are addressed.
He is passionate about community engagement, transparent communication, and advocating for policies that promote the well-being and prosperity of our district.
Tom Sawyer believes in working together to build a stronger, more vibrant community.
Through Community Outreach, Media Relations, and Public Policy Advocacy, we aim to create positive change and foster a sense of unity within the 95th District.
Join us in our mission to make Wichita a better place for everyone.
