My Approach
Collaborative
I believe in the power of collaboration to create exceptional public policy.
As your representative, I seek to immerse myself in the vision and goals of my constituents and work closely with my community, of all walks of life, to make that vision a reality.
I believe the work of the representative should not only reflect the values and strengths of our district, but also to set the standard on improving the quality of life in the area, and what it truly means to be a representative in modern day state policy making.
Facts and Data Driven
I believe in checks and balances.
I believe in a studied approach.
I think when we take a peer-reviewed, data-based approach, we can measure our starting points, our efforts and ultimately ensure the final result aligns with the vision we set forth.
We are the SHOW ME STATE after all.
Our data collection in MO is important and it reflects the unique strengths and challenges in District 127 and Missouri as a whole, since 1821.
Good data. from our Census to our annual reports from various state departments, takes a while to gather and report, and then to study and research, so we are usually years behind what is happening in real time.
These reports guide us to direct our efforts in bridging the gaps in Rural Missouri and help us measure our failures and successes.
We should always consider the data for our state on any given proposal brought forth by legislators and let that have a larger effect in our decisions and votes moving forward.
When our State Auditor says we will have to make budget cuts next year or we won’t balance; it doesn’t seem like the right time to cut 64% of our state working budget by phasing out the income tax with NO plan in place.
To make it a rush vote to the voters in the primary, where voter turnout is known to be significantly lower, seems irresponsible.
This is a great example of a conversation that needs more facts, data, and research.
Missourians deserve to see the plan and facts before agreeing to anything.
