Andy Beshear in April 2025, Judge Will Moynahan brings a lifetime of public service and legal experience to the Kentucky Court of Appeals.
On the bench, Judge Moynahan approaches each case with fairness, impartiality, and the combat-tested judgment he’s built over decades of service.
Now, he’s running to be elected to his seat and continue serving Kentucky.
Will’s life of public service began as a Naval Aviator.
He flew 40 combat missions in the F-18 Hornet as part of Operation Iraqi Freedom, and continues to serve in the Navy Reserve.
He was promoted to the rank of Captain in 2020.
Aboard the USS John C.
Stennis in 2004 Getting soaked by his family after his last instructor flight with Training Squadron Nine.
After serving his country, Will pursued a legal career, clerking for the U.S.
Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces and for a Judge on the Sixth Circuit U.S.
Court of Appeals before joining the U.S.
Department of Justice.
As an Assistant U.S.
Attorney, Will went after criminals trying to rip off consumers and taxpayers through price-fixing, bid-rigging, and fraud.
He resigned from the DOJ in January 2025, and was appointed in April 2025 to the bench by Gov.
Andy Beshear Judge Moynahan holds a bachelor’s degree from Harvard University, a Masters in Strategic Studies from the U.S.
Army War College, and received his Juris Doctor with high honors from George Washington University.
He grew up in London, Ky. and was the valedictorian of his class at South Laurel High School.
Visiting Cumberland Falls in 2024 Will & Elizabeth, with Caitlin (l) and Madeline (r).
Will and his wife Elizabeth, a pharmacist, are raising their two daughters, aged 13 and 10, in Lexington.
The girls attend Fayette County public schools, and Will has been an active parent volunteer at Rosa Parks Elementary, serving as PTA treasurer and on the School-Based Decision Making Council.
Will and Elizabeth are active members of the First United Methodist Church of Lexington.
