Andy Beshear appointed Will to the Kentucky Court of Appeals and he was sworn in by Justice Pamela Goodwine in April 2025.
The 5th Appellate district covers Fayette, Madison, Franklin, Scott, Jessamine, Woodford, Clark, and Bourbon Counties.
Military Service Will has served 26 years as an aviator in the U.S.
Navy and Navy Reserve.
He flew 40 combat missions as an F-18 Hornet pilot during Operation Iraqi Freedom, and was promoted to the rank of Captain (O-6) in 2020.
He currently commands the reserve component of Carrier Strike Group Five.
Experience Decade as a federal prosecutor (2014-2025) Law Clerk, U.S.
Court of Appeals for the Armed Forces (2010-2013) Law Clerk, U.S.
Sixth Circuit Court of Appeals (2013 – 2014) Education BA, economics, Harvard College (magna cum laude) JD, George Washington U.
Law School (with high honors) Masters in Strategic Studies, U.S.
Army War College, (Distinguished Graduate) Kentucky’s 5th Appellate District ROLE OF THE COURT OF APPEALS The Kentucky Court of Appeals, with a few exceptions, reviews most cases appealed from Kentucky Circuit Courts.
Cases are not retried at the appellate level.
Instead, the original trial record is reviewed, with attorneys presenting the legal issues to the Court of Appeals for a decision.
Fourteen judges, two elected from each of the seven appellate districts, serve for eight-year terms.
Judicial candidates run in non-partisan elections.
Court of Appeals judges are divided into panels of three to review and decide cases, with the majority deciding the outcome.
The panels do not sit permanently in one location, but move around the state to hear appeals.
