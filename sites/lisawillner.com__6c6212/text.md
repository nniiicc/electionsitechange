Volunteer Dear Neighbor, I’m honored to represent the 35th District, and proud of my continued record of standing up for working people.
I believe that partisan power and corporate greed too often rule Frankfort.
I proudly stand up to both.
My focus remains on finding commonsense answers that help real people address real challenges.
We have an affordability problem - families simply can't afford basic needs like housing, groceries, medicines, and childcare.
However, big corporations continue to ask the Kentucky Legislature for more and more tax breaks - even when they are making record profits.
It's wrong and I call them out.
I continue to write, support, and sponsor legislation to address the growing affordability crisis and I will continue to push for closing loopholes on corporate greed.
And as the only licensed mental health professional in the Kentucky Legislature, I bring a unique voice.
We must address the growing mental health challenges that face our Commonwealth.
I have had some recent successes but there is still much work to be done.
I will continue to work with anyone on behalf of the hard-working people throughout my district.
The only promises I make are: 1) to never compromise my values and 2) to always work hard on behalf of our district.
Please reach out if I can be of help.
