What I'm Focusing On
I am running for State House Representative for District 29 to provide practical, community-focused leadership on the issues that matter most to residents.
My campaign is centered on strengthening public education, improving affordability for working families, and supporting safe, stable communities.
I believe every student deserves access to a high-quality education, and I support policies that invest in teachers, expand resources for students, and ensure schools are equipped to meet diverse learning needs.
A strong education system is essential to long-term economic growth and opportunity.
Addressing affordability is also a top priority.
Many families are facing rising costs in housing, healthcare, and everyday expenses.
I am committed to advancing responsible solutions that help reduce financial strain, support small businesses, and create pathways to economic stability and upward mobility.
In addition, I support efforts to maintain safe communities by ensuring law enforcement has the resources they need while also promoting accountability and trust between agencies and the people they serve.
My goal is to represent the interests of District 29 with transparency, collaboration, and a focus on policies that deliver measurable results for the community.
