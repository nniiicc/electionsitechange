Serving People Is My Mission
For most of my life, I've been focused on one mission: serving people.
Whether helping families through life's challenges as a pastor, creating jobs as a business owner, or representing our community in Concord — I've always believed leadership is about putting people first.
New Hampshire is a special place because of its hardworking citizens, strong communities, and independent spirit.
It's a state where neighbors still help neighbors, and where common sense matters more than politics.
Those are the values that have guided my life and my public service.
During my previous terms in the State House, I worked to protect taxpayers, support local businesses, defend individual freedoms, and strengthen the communities that make New Hampshire such a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
I approached every issue with a simple question: "How will this affect the people I serve?"
As a pastor, I've sat with people during their greatest joys and toughest struggles.
As a business owner, I've experienced the challenges and rewards of building for a better tomorrow.
These experiences gave me a real-world perspective I brought to Concord — and will bring again as your representative.
I'm running because I believe public service is about more than politics.
It's about people.
I would be honored to earn your support and the opportunity to serve you once again.
We need leaders who will listen — who understand everyday citizens and work hard to build a stronger future for the next generation.
— Allen Cook
The Fight For Brentwood
Get Big Government Out Of The Way
I will work tirelessly to hold government accountable by removing the red tape that hinders the progress and freedom of private citizens.
Property Tax Relief
I will work to lessen — and even alleviate — property taxes, particularly for our elderly neighbors who deserve to stay in the homes they've worked a lifetime to build.
Lower Fuel Prices
I will work to unleash our natural energy resources through expansion and production, lowering costs for hardworking Granite State families.
School Choice For All
Every child deserves the opportunity to succeed.
I will work to support and expand school choice so parents — not bureaucrats — decide what's best for their kids.
Keep New Hampshire Free
New Hampshire is strong because of its people — hardworking citizens who value common sense over politics and freedom over red tape.
We need to preserve what makes our state unique while building a brighter future for the next generation.
That's the promise I'll carry to Concord.
