Stand With Allen Meet Allen Meet Allen Cook Serving People Is My Mission Pastor Business Owner Former State Rep Community Leader For most of my life, I've been focused on one mission: serving people.
Whether helping families through life's challenges as a pastor, creating jobs as a business owner, or representing our community in Concord — I've always believed leadership is about putting people first.
New Hampshire is a special place because of its hardworking citizens, strong communities, and independent spirit.
It's a state where neighbors still help neighbors, and where common sense matters more than politics.
Those are the values that have guided my life and my public service.
During my previous terms in the State House, I worked to protect taxpayers, support local businesses, defend individual freedoms, and strengthen the communities that make New Hampshire such a great place to live, work, and raise a family.
I approached every issue with a simple question: "How will this affect the people I serve?" As a pastor, I've sat with people during their greatest joys and toughest struggles.
As a business owner, I've experienced the challenges and rewards of building for a better tomorrow.
These experiences gave me a real-world perspective I brought to Concord — and will bring again as your representative.
I'm running because I believe public service is about more than politics.
It's about people.
I would be honored to earn your support and the opportunity to serve you once again.
We need leaders who will listen — who understand everyday citizens and work hard to build a stronger future for the next generation. — Allen Cook Where Allen Stands The Fight For Brentwood 01 Get Big Government Out Of The Way I will work tirelessly to hold government accountable by removing the red tape that hinders the progress and freedom of private citizens.
