Building Strong Communities
Strong communities are built by investing in the places where families live, learn, and gather.
I have worked to secure funding for projects that improve everyday life throughout House District 22, including:
• East Mountain High School science, technology, engineering, and mathematics expansion.
• East Mountain Baseball Complex improvements.
• Estancia Valley Classical Academy technology improvements.
• Los Vecinos Community Center.
• Tijeras Senior Center.
• Tijeras Village Hall improvements.
• Elena Gallegos Open Space improvements.
• Historic Campos Sanchez Stone House preservation.
• Local parks, recreation facilities, and community spaces.
Building strong communities means investing in education, recreation, public spaces, and the quality of life that makes our district such a special place to call home.
