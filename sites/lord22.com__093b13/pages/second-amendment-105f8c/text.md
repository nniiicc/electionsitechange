My major accomplishments in past sessions included fighting and defeating unconstitutional anti-2nd Amendment bills that punish legal gun owners.
Such measures will never stop violent criminals from being criminals.
For this reason, I fought against the following legislation: HB 68 – Created a new fourth-degree felony penalty for anyone with a firearm within 1,000 feet of a school zone, and anyone under the age of 19 with a firearm.
This would have included a lawful adult with a firearm in their car or walking through a school zone on the sidewalk.
We convinced the bill sponsor to remove these portions of the bill, preventing law-abiding minors and adults from being turned into felons.
HB 156 – A pipeline-to-prison bill designating all firearms with a magazine capacity larger than 15 rounds as a “large-capacity” magazine and making simple possession of such magazines a felony under New Mexico law.
This bill would have turned thousands of law-abiding New Mexicans into felons overnight.
I debated this bill and convinced Democrats to vote no, successfully killing the bill in committee.
SB 17 – I was able to stop the most egregious firearm bill in the history of New Mexico.
This bill would have not only banned all gas-operated firearms, but it also would have heavily punished local gun dealers.
