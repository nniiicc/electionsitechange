She and her husband, Thor, have built their lives in District 22, where they enjoy riding motorcycles, supporting local businesses, hiking New Mexico’s beautiful public lands, and spending time with their rescue dogs.
Stefani believes the best representation begins with listening.
Whether attending community meetings, hiking local trails, supporting first responders, visiting schools, or working alongside local officials, she believes that understanding a community starts with being present in it.
Since taking office, Stefani has focused on delivering practical results, bringing millions of dollars in investments back to District 22, protecting local water resources, improving public safety, expanding healthcare access, supporting education, and preserving the rural character that makes our communities unique.
Her philosophy is simple: Put the community first.
Listen first.
Work hard.
Deliver results.
