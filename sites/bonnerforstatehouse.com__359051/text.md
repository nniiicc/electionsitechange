Meet Josh
Josh Bonner is a dedicated public servant, businessman, and military officer.
Elected in 2016 to represent the citizens of Fayette and Coweta Counties in the Georgia House of Representatives, Josh has been committed to serving his community with integrity and passion.
In the Georgia House, Josh has been a champion for our Veteran community.
He serves as Chairman of the Defense and Veterans Affairs Committee, which handles legislation and oversight related to Georgia's Department of Defense, military personnel, and organizations providing service to military veterans.
He also serves as a member of the Appropriations, Economic Development and Tourism, Creative Arts and Entertainment, Human Relations and Aging, and the Industry and Labor Committees, demonstrating his broad range of interests and expertise in legislative matters.
