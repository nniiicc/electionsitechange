MEET ANNE
Hey, y’all!
Thanks for taking time to learn more about my campaign for re-election as Kentucky State Representative for the 76th District.
I’ve just completed my first year in the General Assembly, and I’m eager to serve a second term.
In my first year, I filed key pieces of legislation like Pre-K for All and numerous bills to protect reproductive freedom while fighting hard against bills that would harm Kentuckians.
I partner with local elected officials to support work happening within the City of Lexington and advocate with Kentucky’s federal delegation to fight against extremist policies coming from Washington.
I proudly served on the following committees:
- Agriculture: promoting Kentucky agriculture and advocating for policies that support Kentucky farmers.
- Licensing, Occupations, and Administrative Regulations: overseeing regulated industries such as horse racing, bourbon, cosmetology and many others.
- State Government: dealing with a wide variety of issues related to state governance, including state employees, Capitol grounds, and even LGBTQ+ issues.
- Transportation: addressing all aspects of Kentucky’s transportation network, including disaster recovery, aviation, and the state road fund.
In addition to these legislative committees, I am currently the chair of the Fundraising Committee for the Kentucky House Democratic Caucus Campaign Committee, where I work to advance Democratic policies and candidates across the Commonwealth.
As a graduate of Emerge Kentucky, I support future female candidates as the Treasurer of the Board and active alumna of the program.
Outside of my legislative service, I am a mother of two daughters, Maggie and Lucy.
I am actively engaged with our community as a former non-profit executive, and I love to celebrate the good things in Lexington.
I currently sit on the Lexington Habitat for Humanity, working toward the goal of housing for all.
I invite you to join me by learning more about my platform, volunteering for the campaign, donating to help spread the word, and most importantly, by voting for me on May 19, 2026.
It has been an incredible honor to fight for you in Frankfort, and I hope that I can count on your support to send me back for my second term in 2026!
