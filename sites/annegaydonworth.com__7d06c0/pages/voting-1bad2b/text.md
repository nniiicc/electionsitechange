What to Bring, Other Info to Know Bring a photo ID to your polling location.
If you’re voting on Election Day, you cannot be turned away if you are in line by 6:00pm.
If you aren’t sure of your voter registration status, visit govoteky.com.
Vote Restoration Information In 2019, Governor Beshear restored the right to vote for nearly 170,000 Kentuckians.Unfortunately, many people are unaware they have gotten their right back.
To find out if your rights have been restored, visit civilrightsrestoration.ky.gov.
If you are eligible to vote, you can check your registration status and/or register at govoteky.com to vote in the general election in November.
