I am a resident of St Johnsbury, a retired faculty member of St Johnsbury Academy, a mother of three grown children, two of whom have taught in the Vermont school districts, a grandmother, a volunteer for local organizations, a voter, and your neighbor.
All of us have watched property taxes rise, food prices and health care costs rise, fuel and heating oil and home prices rise.
The issues behind these increases are complex and some of them are beyond our control but their effects are community wide.
What is in our control is how we vote in the next election.
I have read the various candidates’ platforms, listened to them, met a few.
What I want this time around is a willingness to try new approaches with the recognition that many of our concerns are bigger than just Vermont but that the impact is local.
I will vote for Amanda Cochrane as the Democratic candidate for the Vermont House of Representatives.
She identifies the issues affecting the Northeast Kingdom; more importantly she sees how they are interconnected – that healthcare costs impact our school system which is funded by property taxes which increase the cost of housing which contributes to homelessness which comes back to healthcare – on and on and on.
The challenge here is considerable – I vote for change.
Mary Shepley
St Johnsbury, Vt.
