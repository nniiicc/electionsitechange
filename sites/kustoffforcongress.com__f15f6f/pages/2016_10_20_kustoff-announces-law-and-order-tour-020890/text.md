Toggle navigation Home About Issues News Volunteer Contact Donate Kustoff Announces Law and Order Tour October 20, 2016 Tour will stop in Brownsville, Jackson, and Trenton GERMANTOWN, TN – The Republican nominee for Tennessee’s 8th Congressional District, David Kustoff, announced he will be traveling across the district to discuss West Tennessee’s safety and security with local lawmakers.
“As I continue to travel this district, it is important I listen to folks, discuss the concerns they have, answer their questions, and talk with them about our safety and security,” Kustoff said.
“As the former United States Attorney for West Tennessee, I know we must fight back against radical Islamic terrorism, secure our borders, and support our police officer.
My goal is to make sure we keep West Tennessee safe, secure, and prosperous.” The following are the details of his Law and Order Tour: Kustoff for Congress Law and Order Tour October 21, 2016 TIME: 9:00AM – 10:00AM EVENT: Haywood County ADDRESS: 100 S Dupree St, Brownsville, TN 38012 TIME: 12:00PM – 1:00PM EVENT: Madison County ADDRESS: 546 E College St, Jackson, TN 38301 TIME: 2:00PM – 3:00PM EVENT: Gibson County ADDRESS: 301 N College St, Trenton, TN 38382 David Kustoff’s roots run deep in West Tennessee.
He was born and raised in Shelby County and is a graduate of the University of Memphis.
Appointed U.S.
Attorney for the Western District of Tennessee by President Bush in 2006, Kustoff set his sights on cleaning up government and public corruption and fighting violent crime and drugs.
He oversaw the majority of the Tennessee Waltz trials that sent Senator John Ford and 12 other defendants to federal prison.
David and his wife of 14 years, Roberta, have two children Maggie and Jake.
