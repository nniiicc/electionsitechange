“I know what it’s like to fight the establishment and take on corrupt politicians,” said Governor Huckabee.
“And I know what it’s like to stand on principle while those around you run.
That’s why I’m supporting and campaigning for David.
He’s pro-life, pro-gun and pro-family.” “I’m honored and humbled by the endorsement of Governor Huckabee,” said David Kustoff.
“As Governor Huckabee and I campaign across the district next week I’m looking forward to sharing my conservative message with voters.” The Kustoff for Congress Campaign Tour Schedule: Wednesday, July 27 8:30AM Dave’s on the Square 107 West Market Street Dyersburg, TN 11:30AM Brooks Shaw’s Old Country Store 56 Casey Jones Lane Jackson, TN 3:30PM Kustoff for Congress Headquarters 5635 Poplar Avenue Memphis, TN David Kustoff’s roots run deep in West Tennessee.
He was born and raised in Shelby County and is a graduate of the University of Memphis.
Appointed U.S.
Attorney for the Western District of Tennessee by President Bush in 2006, Kustoff set his sights on cleaning up government and public corruption and fighting violent crime and drugs.
He oversaw the majority of the Tennessee Waltz trials that sent Senator John Ford and 12 other defendants to federal prison.
David and his wife of 14 years, Roberta, have two children Maggie and Jake.
