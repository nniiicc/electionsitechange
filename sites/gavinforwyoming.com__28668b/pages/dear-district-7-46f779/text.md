Dear District 7
Dear District 7
Friends and Neighbors,
I’m proud to announce my campaign to represent our district.
As someone who was born and raised here, this community shaped who I am.
It taught me the value of hard work, looking out for your neighbors, and building a future worth staying for.
Through my work in local government, I’ve seen firsthand the challenges facing Wyoming families: rising costs, housing affordability, workforce shortages, and too many young people feeling like they have to leave home to build a life for themselves.
I’m running because I want young people to see a future in Wyoming, not an exit plan.
I believe we can build that future by investing in job training and apprenticeships, supporting businesses that invest back into our communities, addressing affordability, and making practical decisions that strengthen Wyoming for the next generation.
This campaign is about keeping our people here, taking care of the families and seniors who built our communities, and ensuring Wyoming remains a place where people can afford to live, work, and thrive.
I would be honored to earn your support because Wyoming is home.
Sincerely,
Gavin Lobmeyer
