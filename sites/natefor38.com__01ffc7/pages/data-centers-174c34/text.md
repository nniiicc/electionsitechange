The massive amounts of water required by data centers in 2025 spurred my candidacy.
I have since watched the City of Kokomo blind annex 750 acres into the city and rezoned it Industrial to make way for a data center.
At the time, they wouldn't disclose the project, proceeding despite public outcry.
You know it's time for big change when the government listens to hours of testimony and refuses to act on it.
Flock cameras and data centers are examples of how the State just does whatever it wants.
We need representation that listens and executes the people's plan!
