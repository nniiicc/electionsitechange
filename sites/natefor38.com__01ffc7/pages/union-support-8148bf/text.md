Unions have secured vital worker rights, including the eight-hour workday, weekends, and safer conditions, along with fair wages and benefits.
These achievements have not only improved individual lives but set standards across the workforce.
Right-to-work laws have weakened unions by allowing non-paying members to benefit from their efforts, reducing financial resources and bargaining power.
This has led to stagnant wages and poorer working conditions.
To ensure unions continue providing fair treatment and economic equality, repealing these laws is essential.
Strong unions mean a strong workforce, benefiting everyone.
