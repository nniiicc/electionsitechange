I was born in Tipton, and grew up in the country spending summers in Windfall with my grandparents.
They were farmers who taught me the value of hard work, commitment, and taking care of what's yours.
After 20 years building a career with some of the biggest brands in the country, my heart never left home.
I'm a dad.
I've lived real life, and it taught me patience, perspective, and doing right by your kids no matter what.
I believe every child growing up in a small town deserves access to a quality education.
Our communities deserve thoughtful and strategic economic development that prioritizes small businesses and entrepreneurs, and we must ensure rural health care is accessible and affordable for everyone.
If you believe in focusing on practical solutions that benefit our community, you're in the right place!
