top of page About Keith Why I'm Running Contribute Serving for 22 years in the United States Army, I learned a thing or two about getting the job done no matter what. - Keith About Keith Keith is a U.S.
Army veteran with over 30 years of service.
He joined the Army Reserve in 1981, completed Basic Combat Training at Fort Jackson, SC, and Combat Medic Training at Fort Sam Houston, TX.
In 1982, he entered active duty and trained as an Infantryman at Fort Benning, GA. ​ His career included leadership roles across the 9th, 1st Armored, 4th, and 25th Infantry Divisions as an Anti-Armor Specialist, Squad Leader, Platoon Sergeant, First Sergeant, Drill Sergeant, Recruiter, and instructor.
He finished his service as Senior Military Instructor in the ROTC program at the University of Wisconsin-La Crosse. ​ Keith and his wife Charlene have been married 37 years.
They met at Fort Carson, Colorado while serving and are proud parents of two and grandparents of five (with one on the way). ​ After retiring, Keith worked as a Warehouse/Plant Manager, Accountant, ROTC Scholarship Officer at UW-La Crosse, and Director of Military Aligned Students at Viterbo University.
He holds a BS in Accounting from UW-La Crosse and an MBA from Viterbo.
