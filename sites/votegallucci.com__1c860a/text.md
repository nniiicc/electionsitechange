Solutions Over Slogans.
A Voice for Real Change in Maryland Senate District 32 Join Us Our Mission Justin Gallucci isn't a career politician.
He's a father, a Realtor, and a community leader who has spent his career solving real-world problems in industries that touch every Maryland family: clean water, energy, healthcare, and housing.
From managing hazardous materials and regulatory affairs at Kuehne Chemical, to running one of Tesla's largest energy facilities, to advancing innovation at United Therapeutics.
Justin has learned how safety, efficiency, and smart practice can improve lives without unnecessary red tape, monetary bloat, and over-regulation.
Meanwhile, Annapolis has failed families.
First-time homebuyers are priced out and pushed into government-subsidized high-density complexes instead of being given a real path to homeownership.
Maryland families pay some of the highest energy costs in the country while politicians chase failing green schemes.
Our district is the epicenter of human trafficking because Baltimore leaders refuse to confront crime.
Schools are weighed down with bloated contracts that take away from teachers and students.
Insurance premiums are out of control, and instead of fixing the system, Annapolis caters to special interests while raising taxes and fees to cover bloated spending, and we're still in a deficit.
Justin is running because Maryland deserves better.
He serves part-time in the Maryland General Assembly as a Legislative Assistant, helps families as a Realtor with Berkshire Hathaway, and leads PoliticoGPT, an AI platform built to bring transparency and accountability to government.
His priorities reflect what he sees every day: housing families can afford, reliable and affordable energy, schools that deliver results, safer communities protected from trafficking and opioids, and fiscal accountability in Annapolis.
Faith and family are the foundation of Justin's life.
He attends Lighthouse Church in Glen Burnie every weekend, and his daughter Chloe is his reason for running.
For her and for all of Maryland's children, Justin is committed to building a future where families can thrive, neighborhoods are safe, and opportunity is real.
