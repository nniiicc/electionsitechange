I stand for LOWER TAXES
I am a major supporter of lower taxes, especially lower property taxes!
When people attain the ultimate goal of paying off their primary residence, they should own it free and clear and not continue to be burdened by property taxes.
I will keep working for property tax relief!
Here are bills I have sponsored over the years:
2026
- HB 961 – Property Tax Elimination – This would eliminate ALL property taxes on a primary place of residence.
The loss of property taxes off the payrolls would be equal to approximately $932,000,000.
This loss would be replaced with a sales tax increase of 1.75 percent.
2025
- HB 40 – $253M lowered individual & corporate income tax rates from 5.695% to 5.3%.
- HB 304 – $100M shifted from state revenue to a property tax reduction fund & school facility fund.
- HB 231 – $50M increased the annual grocery tax credit to $155 per person to offset sales tax on food.
2024
- HB 521: provided over $1 billion dollars in property tax relief by doing the following:
- Income tax reduction: from 5.8 to 5.695. this is roughly $60-$65 million in income tax relief.
- Property tax direct relief: $75 million was taken and run through the formula set up in 2023 with HB292.
This resulted in approximately $75 million in property tax relief on top of the property tax relief that was completed this past year.
- School facilities fund: $125 million of general fund money was taken to be able to bond on for the next 10 years.
This amounts to $1 Billion.
This will be distributed to all the school districts for facility needs.
As the Governor has said, this certainly results in property tax relief.
But it’s also a substantial investment in education.
It helps to reduce the likelihood of additional property tax increases through bonds & levies.
2023
- HB 292: $355M provides 3 years of homeowner property tax relief
- Year 1: School district facilities fund = $135 million
- Year 1: Homeowners Property Tax Account = $190 million
- Year 1: Property Tax Relief to All Property Owners = $50 million
- HB 236: $50M in additional property tax relief
2022
- HB 436: $350M tax rebate; individual & corporate tax rate reduced from 6.5% to 6%
- HB 1: $500M tax rebate; individual & corporate tax rate reduced from 6% to 5.8% (special session)

I stand for Better Healthcare options
The fight for better healthcare for everyone is ongoing.
We need to take a more innovative approach to customizing our health insurance plans according to our needs.
- Just as we customize our home and car insurance, we should be able to do the same for health insurance.
Increased personalized health insurance options will make rates more competitive and patient-friendly, which will lead to decreased insurance premiums.
- Currently, only "in network" doctors can be used (which is silly!).
All doctors, especially our own doctor, should be accessible.
- Medical procedures, whether from hospitals or doctors, should be honest and transparent up front.
We have a right to see what each exam and procedure will cost before we receive the health services.
These three simple changes will dramatically improve the quality of our healthcare and lower our healthcare costs.
Healthcare paid for by taxpayers should be guarded against fraud and abuse.
This is why I supported HB 913 which implemented work requirements for Medicaid recipients.
This was passed and signed by the governor.
