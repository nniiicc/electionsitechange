I’m Ali Dieng, a New North End neighbor, father, and former three-term Burlington City Councilor.
I founded the Burlington School District Parent University to help families connect with our schools and helped found the Vermont New American Advisory Council so more residents could have a voice in decisions that affect them.
I serve on the board of Community Health Centers and previously served on the board of CVOEO.
Through my work at Building Bright Futures, I help strengthen the system of care and well-being for children and families across Vermont.
I know our neighborhood’s strengths and the pressures families face.
In the Vermont House, I’ll bring that experience to the work of making our neighborhood stronger and addressing housing, childcare, health care, public schools, and community safety.

Our Priorities: Education : Support sustainable school funding, expand access to early childhood education, and make state and community colleges more affordable while strengthening workforce training.
Universal Health Care : Expand access to affordable, high-quality health care, including mental health services, while reducing prescription drug costs.
Affordable Housing: Increase the supply of affordable housing, address homelessness, and support policies that make homeownership and renting more attainable.
Workforce Development : Address workforce shortages and demographic challenges by retaining young Vermonters, attracting workers, and creating opportunities for families to succeed.
I am seeking your support to help build a more affordable, healthy, and prosperous Vermont where every resident has the opportunity to thrive.
