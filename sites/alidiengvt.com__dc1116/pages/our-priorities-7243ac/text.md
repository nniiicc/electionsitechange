My name is Ali Dieng, and I am proud to announce my candidacy for the Vermont House of Representatives representing Chittenden-18.
Nearly 20 years ago, I came to the Green Mountain State believing in the promise that hard work, service, and community can change lives.
My story is the story of many New Americans who came here not just seeking opportunity, but seeking a chance to contribute.
Vermont welcomed me, and I have dedicated myself to giving back through public service, community leadership, and advocacy.
As a husband, father of two young girls, former Burlington City Councilor, board member of the Community Health Center, educator, and community advocate, I have seen firsthand the challenges Vermonters face every day.
Rising housing costs, workforce shortages, an aging population, and increasing health care expenses are making it harder for families and businesses to thrive.
I am running because Vermont deserves leadership that is accessible, transparent, and accountable.
Leadership that listens before it acts.
Leadership that works collaboratively to build solutions that strengthen our communities and improve quality of life for everyone.
My priorities include:
• Expanding affordable housing opportunities for working families and seniors
• Strengthening Vermont’s workforce and supporting economic opportunity
• Improving access to affordable, high-quality health care and mental health services
• Supporting strong public schools and educational opportunities for all children
• Promoting smart growth, public safety, and sustainable economic development
• Addressing affordability and the rising cost of living facing Vermont families
• Building stronger, more engaged communities where every voice is heard
This campaign is about bringing people together around common goals and shared values.
It is about ensuring that every Vermonter has the opportunity to succeed, regardless of their background, income, or ZIP code.
Together, we can build a Vermont that is more affordable, healthier, more inclusive, and better prepared for the future.
I invite you to join our campaign, share your ideas, volunteer, host a neighborhood conversation, and help us create a people-powered movement for Chittenden-18.
Together, we can be a voice for working families and build a stronger future for Vermont.
