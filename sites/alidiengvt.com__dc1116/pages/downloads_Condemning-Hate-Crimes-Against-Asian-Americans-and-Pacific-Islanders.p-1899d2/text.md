Resolution Relating to RESOLUTION________ Sponsor(s): Dieng Introduced: ____________________ Referred to: ____________________ ______________________________ Action: ________________________ That WHEREAS, ​the Declaration of Independence defines the United States of America as a democracy based on the unalienable rights of life, liberty and the pursuit of happiness and government by the consent of the people, and the 14th Amendment instilled equality of the races into the US Constitution; and WHEREAS, ​Stop Asian Americans & Pacific Islanders (AAPI) Hate, a coalition that tracks the prevalence of anti-Asian hate crimes, reported over 2,800 incidences of hate within this past year around the country.
However, these numbers are anticipated to be much higher due to a large number of hate crimes that go unreported; and WHEREAS, ​since the beginning of the coronavirus pandemic, rates of hate crimes against Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders, including verbal, physical, and psychological abuse, have increased exponentially.
In major U.S. cities, the rate of anti-Asian hate crimes has increased by nearly 150%​; and WHEREAS, ​according to the City of Burlington 2019 Equity Report, Asian American and Pacific Islander Burlingtonians represent 6.3 percent of our demographic data, the second largest demographic after whites; and WHEREAS​, according to the Burlington School District 2020 Annual Report data, Asian Americans and Pacific Islander students represent 11% of our student demographic data; and WHEREAS​, the increased rate of anti-Asian hate crimes affect community members of all ages and are often directed at the most vulnerable populations such as older Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders, AAPI women, and AAPI youths; and Page 2 Resolution Relating to​ CONDEMNING HATE CRIMES AGAINST ASIAN AMERICANS & PACIFIC ISLANDERS WHEREAS, ​Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders contribute meaningfully to the greatness of our city and state.
They are business owners, professionals, entrepreneurs, our neighbors and coworkers, and they contribute to our city and state’s cultural and religious fabric; and WHEREAS, On Thursday July 15​th​ 2020, Mayor Miro Weinberger, the Burlington City Council and community leaders gathered to declare Racism a public health emergency in the city of Burlington, with more than 30 organizations pledging to take action to fight racism in the community; and WHEREAS,​ racism and violence against Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders are deeply ingrained in American history.
Centuries of discrimination have been exacerbated by racist anti-Asian rhetoric espoused by the previous federal Administration throughout the coronavirus pandemic; and WHEREAS,​ according to a recent statement from President Biden, “The Federal Government must recognize that it has played a role in furthering these xenophobic sentiments through the actions of political leaders, including references to the COVID-19 pandemic by the geographic location of its origin.
Such statements have stoked unfounded fears, and perpetuated stigma about Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders have contributed to increasing rates of bullying, harassment, and hate crimes against AAPI persons;” and WHEREAS, this increase in racism is leaving a traumatic impact on Asian Americans and Pacific Islander youth in our city and state.
It is critical to acknowledge and condemn the targeting of hate, particularly for the next generation of AAPI or any minority group, to move forward and create a more equitable and welcoming city of Burlington, where all members of our society can pursue the American dream without living in fear; NOW, THEREFORE, BE IT RESOLVED that the Burlington City Council condemns any type of hate crime against Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders (AAPI) and stands behind our community members of AAPI descent; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the Burlington City Council respectfully tasks the Parks, Arts and Culture Committee to coordinate with the Fletcher Free Library and Burlington City Arts to better promote the month of May as Asian History Month to highlight the contributions of Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders in our city; and BE IT FURTHER RESOLVED that the City Of Burlington strive to make our city a safe, welcoming and equitable place for all.
