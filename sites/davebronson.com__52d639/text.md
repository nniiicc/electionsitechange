Dave and Josh’s Policies Economy Develop Alaska’s abundant natural resources — oil, gas, mining, critical minerals, and timber — to create jobs, power America, and strengthen our state’s finances, without raising taxes.
Learn More Infrastructure Invest in roads, bridges, and ports so businesses can thrive and families can prosper, following Dave’s record of delivering the largest state appropriation in history for the Port of Alaska.
Learn More Housing Secure control of Alaska’s land and put it to work for families, ensuring housing is attainable and communities can grow.
Learn More Education End bloated bureaucracy and political agendas in schools; focus on reading, writing, and math; reward great teachers; and restore parents’ rights.
Learn More Permanent Fund Dividend The Permanent Fund Dividend belongs to the people of Alaska.
It was created so every Alaskan can share fairly in our state’s resource wealth and not to be treated as a political football or used to paper over budget gaps.
I support a full statutory PFD, but how we achieve that will take work.
Learn More Government Reform Alaskans deserve a government that works for them, not one that works to protect itself.
For too long, decisions have been made behind closed doors, accountability has been blurred, and bureaucracy has grown while trust has eroded.
My approach to government reform is straightforward: transparency, accountability, and results.
