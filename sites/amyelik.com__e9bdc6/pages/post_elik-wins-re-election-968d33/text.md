Elik Wins Re-Election
Nov 5, 2024
Updated: Jun 25, 2025
State Representative Amy Elik was re-elected by the voters of the 111th district with 61.5% of the vote.
"I am incredibly honored and grateful to the people of the 111th district for placing their trust in me once again," said Elik.
"To every supporter, volunteer, friend, and family member who stood by us—thank you.
I’m excited to keep fighting to lower the cost of living, bring fiscal responsibility to state government, and hold the majority accountable.
Let’s get to work!"
Elik's third term will begin on January 8, 2025.
