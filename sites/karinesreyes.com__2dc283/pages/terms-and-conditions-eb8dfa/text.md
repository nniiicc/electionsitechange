Message Frequency Message frequency varies based on campaign activity and election cycles.

Opt-Out Instructions You may opt out of receiving text messages at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us.

After opting out, you will no longer receive SMS/MMS messages from this Program unless you opt in again.
No further messages will be sent after your opt-out is processed.
