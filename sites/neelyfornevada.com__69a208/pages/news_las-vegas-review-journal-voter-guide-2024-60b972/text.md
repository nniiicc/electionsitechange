Her experience with the public school system, both as a mother and an advocate, has fueled her determination to fight for better schools and safer environments for all children.
Known for her “mama bear” spirit, Erica is fiercely protective of her community, tirelessly working to create positive change.
Her character is defined by her compassion, authenticity, and courage to stand up for what is right.
Whether fostering children, coaching youth soccer, or engaging in local issues, Erica is committed to making a difference and fighting for a better future for Nevada’s families, schools, and businesses.
She brings a blend of practical experience, a deep love for her community, and a bold vision for a stronger, more prosperous Nevada.
Experience Erica’s professional experience began after high school, when she worked in accounting alongside professionals in property management and real estate investment.
After moving to Las Vegas, she continued working in accounting at a CPA firm, where she assisted with year-end tax returns and learned the importance of accuracy, responsibility, and meeting important deadlines.
She later worked at Station Casinos, giving her firsthand experience in an industry that is deeply connected to Las Vegas and the people who keep our city running.
Erica is also a small-business owner, education director, foster and adoptive parent, youth soccer coach, and author of Fighting for Sophia.
Her experience was built where everyday life happens, at work, around the kitchen table, on the soccer field, in the classroom, and throughout her community.
She has worked alongside parents, supported children, built businesses, and spoken up for families when it mattered most.
Those experiences have shaped her into a strong, practical leader who listens, follows through, and never forgets the people she serves.
Education Erica Neely has a diverse background that reflects both real-world experience and a strong commitment to lifelong learning.
