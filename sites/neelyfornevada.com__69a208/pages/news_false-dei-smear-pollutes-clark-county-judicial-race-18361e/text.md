(Chuck Muth ) – District Court Judge Tina Talem, Department 14, is a woman.
And yes, unlike some Democrats, we know what a woman is.
She’s also Indian-American – same as Kamala Harris and Nikki Haley.
What she is NOT is a DEI hire.
Talim was appointed back in June by Nevada Gov.
Joe Lombardo, who noted that Talim “has nearly two decades of experience working in the Clark County District Attorney’s Office and her expertise will be a valuable asset to the Eighth Judicial District Court.” And as reported by the Las Vegas Review-Journal a few days ago… “Talim graduated from Pepperdine University’s law school in 2004 and moved to Las Vegas afterward.
She worked in civil law for a year before she was hired at the Clark County district attorney’s office, where she spent about 19 years as a prosecutor.
Toward the end of her time as a prosecutor, she oversaw drug-trafficking cases and was in charge of the team that began charging people with murder in connection with fentanyl overdoses.” So, yes, she EARNED the appointment and deserves to be elected to a full term on the bench on November 5 th .
But that’s not how her opponent sees it.
Alan Lefebvre – who’s been accused of homophobia and bigotry for columns he’s written in the past – is accusing Judge Talim of being a “DEI” candidate, referring to the practice of hiring, elevating, or appointing a person based on the “identity politics” of Diversity, Equity & Inclusion.
“I don’t have those DEI credentials,” Lefebvre told the RJ.
“I’m a person who seeks judgeship on the basis of merit.” Sorry, Charlie, that’s sheep-dip.
It’s wrong to give someone preference based on race, gender, sexual orientation, national heritage, etc.
But it’s equally wrong to falsely besmirch someone who HAS earned their position regardless of race, gender, sexual orientation, national heritage, etc.
I’d already endorsed Judge Talim for the November 5th election before reading about Mr.
Lefebvre’s sliming of her.
Now I’m doubling down.
Vote Judge Tina Talim for District Court Department 14.
Meanwhile, back at the ranch… The Las Vegas Review-Journal announced several endorsements of State Assembly candidates this morning.
They include… Lisa Cole for AD4, April Arndt for AD21, Annette Dawson Owens for AD29, Rebecca Edgeworth for AD35, David Brog for AD37, and Rafael Arroyo for AD 41…all of whom have also been endorsed by Gov.
Joe Lombardo.
And speaking of endorsements… The Latin Chamber of Commerce – the most pro-small business Chamber in the state – released its endorsements a couple days ago.
And it endorsed all but one of Gov.
Lombardo’s endorsed GOP legislative candidates.
The exception was Erica Neely in Assembly District 9, who’s running against Democrat Assembly Speaker Steve Yeager.
But get this… It also didn’t endorse Yeager!
And I understand he’s furious about it.
The Latin Chamber handled this one exactly right, even at the risk of p*ssing off a powerful and vengeful Democrat leader.
Yeager is clearly not a champion of small business but does wield considerable clout in the Legislature.
So staying neutral and letting the chips fall where they may was the wise and prudent course.
But make no mistake: The Latin Chamber’s decision NOT to endorse Yeager – especially since politically it would have been the easy way out – speaks volumes.
Without a doubt, it was a vote of “no confidence.” Voters in AD9 should take note and vote for Erica Neely.
