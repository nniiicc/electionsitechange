They told me to just write short meaningless statements on issues without taking any firm positions or writing anything that someone might not agree with.
They told me to write like this one from my opponent’s “Issues” page on crime: “I will work with law enforcement, first responders, and community leaders to ensure our public safety professionals have the resources they need.” Come on.
That could have been written by any candidate, in any state, running for any office, in any year.
It tells you absolutely nothing.
Now – and I know this is a “radical” idea – I think you deserve to know more about the person asking to represent you in Carson City than just their name on a ballot.
So instead of giving you the usual list of short, content-free talking points written by some “professional” consultant… I wrote a book!
It’s called The Kitchen Table Agenda: One Mom’s Answers for the Problems Politicians Ignore.
And it’s exactly what it sounds like – me, at my own kitchen table, telling you the unvarnished truth about what I believe and why.
It’s an e-book… so it’s free and you can download and read it at your convenience.
Here’s what you’ll discover: My background and family .
I want you know why I fight for this district the way I do, not just that I say I will.
If you’ve ever felt like nobody in Carson City understands what your family is up against, you’ll understand why I ran.
A few of our favorite things .
Because the person voting on your behalf shouldn’t be a mystery.
You’ll know me the way you know your own neighbors – including my favorite movies and songs!
Where I stand on the issues .
No hedging.
No “I’ll fight for” without saying what that means.
You’ll know exactly what you’re voting for, so there’s no surprise waiting for you after Election Day.
Why I’d represent you and your family better than my opponent .
Laid out plainly, so you can judge for yourself instead of just taking my word for it.

The political “experts” told me my opponent will take things from this book and twist them into attack mailers.
I know that.
I wrote it anyway.
Because you deserve to know exactly who I am and exactly where I stand before you vote rather than find out after I’m already in Carson City.
And here’s something else you should know: You won’t agree with me on every single position in this book.
I’d actually be a little worried if you did.
Think about your own kitchen table.
The people sitting around it don’t agree on everything.
But they still trust each other because they know each other.
That’s not weakness.
That’s what a real family looks like.
I’m asking you to trust me the same way.
Not because we’ll agree on every issue, but because you’ll always know exactly where I stand and you’ll never have to wonder if I’m telling you the truth.
Which is a major problem when it comes to my opponent… which I talk a little about in the book.
One More Thing: This Isn’t About Me Again, I know the risk of sharing all this with you.
And I understand that it might cost me the race, just as the “experts” have warned.
But I’m OK with that… because this race isn’t about me.
It’s about who the majority of voters in Assembly District 9 want to represent them in Carson City next year.
That’s what elections are all about.
And if the majority of you prefer my opponent, guess what?
I can live with that.
I’ll still be buying milk at the Von’s on Warm Springs and Durango.
I’ll still be coaching my daughter’s youth soccer team with Summerlin AYSO Region 1258 at Community Park near Town Center and Sahara.
I’ll still be having family picnics at our neighborhood parks, like Oak Leaf Park and Mesa Park.
I’ll still be going to the movies with my family at AMC Town Square or catching a movie at the Suncoast.
Because this is my home, too.
I’ve lived in this community for almost 20 years.
And I’m not going anywhere… win, lose, or draw.
