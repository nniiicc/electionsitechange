Sue Lee Loy
For Hawaiʻi State House - District 2
Sue Lee Loy’s commitment to serving the people of Hawaiʻi Island runs deep in her veins.
In 2016, Sue Lee Loy was elected to represent Hilo - her home, the neighborhood she grew up in and the neighborhood she calls home today - on the Hawaiʻi County Council.
In recognition of her sensible policy and real results, Sue was re-elected in 2018, 2020, and in 2022 – a full four terms of service.
Now Sue is seeking to continue her public service at the Hawaiʻi State House of Representatives.
