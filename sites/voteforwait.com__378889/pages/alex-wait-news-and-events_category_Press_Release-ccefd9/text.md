Alex Wait Announces Run for Indiana State House to Help Families Afford to Thrive
Alex Wait announced his campaign for Indiana State House, saying families are working harder while falling behind.
A dad who moved home to raise his daughter, Wait is focused on practical help for families, investing in kids’ futures, and standing up to politicians selling people out.
He says Hoosiers deserve steady leadership that listens, does the homework, and puts communities before politics.
