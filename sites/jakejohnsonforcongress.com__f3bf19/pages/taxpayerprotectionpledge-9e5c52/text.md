Taxpayer Protection Pledge
I’ve spent 20 years working for a paycheck, and I hate seeing my tax dollars go to endless wars, rampant fraud, and corporate giveaways.
I’m upset that my congressman, Brad Finstad, voted for a bill that gave massive tax breaks to his donors, cut healthcare funding in rural Minnesota, and increased the national debt by over $4 trillion.
We need new leadership.
Here’s how I’ll make the math add up for working families again.
1.
Get the fraud money back
- Require cuts to government spending and taxes that are equal to the amount that was stolen, so that we get our money back.
- Require mandatory prison time for everyone who broke the law in connection to these massive fraud cases.
- Reinstate truly independent watchdogs in Washington to find and root out waste, fraud, and abuse.
2.
Stop the runaway national debt
- Vote against raising the debt ceiling until the budget is balanced.
- Run a serious effort to find and cut wasteful spending and inefficiency in the long term, unlike the failed effort that Elon Musk led.
- Modernize government tech to reduce spending and make government work better.
3.
Make the tax code fair for working families
- Undo Finstad’s tax giveaways for billionaires and big corporations.
- Close corporate loopholes that allow American companies to outsource jobs and hide those profits overseas.
- Close loopholes like the Carried Interest Loophole that allow hedge fund billionaires to dodge paying taxes the rest of us pay.
4.
Stop politicians from getting rich on our dime
- Ban elected officials and their spouses from trading and owning individual stocks and from betting on prediction markets.
- Don’t pay Congress when the government is shut down.
- Ban the use of taxpayer dollars for first class flights.
5.
Prevent corporations from buying politicians
- Take no money from corporate PACs.
- Work to overturn Citizens United.
- Ban politicians from ever becoming lobbyists or foreign agents.
6.
Make representatives actually represent us
- Put term limits on elected officials.
- Require representatives to hold at least one in-person town hall per term.
(And to be clear: I’ll hold 21 of them every term.)
- Require the FEC to conduct timely investigations and impose meaningful penalties for campaign finance violations.
7.
Stop spending on giveaways and wars
- Require research grants and federal contracts to be awarded based on merit and performance, not politics or favoritism.
- Oppose taxpayer-funded bailouts for foreign countries like Argentina.
- End the Iran War, and focus on bringing costs down at home.
