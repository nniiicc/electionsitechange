Energy Costs & Affordability
The state’s own energy agency — NYSERDA — admitted in February 2026 that the CLCPA mandates could cost upstate families over $4,100 a year and raise gas prices by more than $2 a gallon.
Small businesses could face $7,000 in added annual costs.
These mandates are unworkable and unaffordable.
Gary supports repealing them and building an all-of-the-above energy strategy that keeps the lights on and costs down for working families in West Seneca and Lackawanna.
Term Limits
Gary believes in term limits at every level of government.
As Town Supervisor, he held a referendum in West Seneca — voters passed it overwhelmingly, and Gary is now subject to those limits himself.
In Albany, he will fight for a constitutional amendment to bring term limits to state legislators and put it to a statewide vote.
The voters should decide — not the career politicians who benefit from staying forever.
Gary made that a reality in West Seneca, and he’ll fight to do the same in New York.
