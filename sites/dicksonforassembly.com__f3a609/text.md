No platitudes.
Here is where Gary stands and why.
Energy Costs & Affordability The state’s own energy agency — NYSERDA — admitted in February 2026 that the CLCPA mandates could cost upstate families over $4,100 a year and raise gas prices by more than $2 a gallon.
Small businesses could face $7,000 in added annual costs.
These mandates are unworkable and unaffordable.
Gary supports repealing them and building an all-of-the-above energy strategy that keeps the lights on and costs down for working families in West Seneca and Lackawanna.
Term Limits Gary believes in term limits at every level of government.
As Town Supervisor, he held a referendum in West Seneca — voters passed it overwhelmingly, and Gary is now subject to those limits himself.
In Albany, he will fight for a constitutional amendment to bring term limits to state legislators and put it to a statewide vote.
The voters should decide — not the career politicians who benefit from staying forever.
Gary made that a reality in West Seneca, and he’ll fight to do the same in New York.
Fiscal Accountability Five years.
Taxpayers’ town taxes did not go up for 5 years in a row.
Gary controlled tax increases through a combination of cost cutting, economic development, conservative budgeting, and prudent use of federal assistance funds.
As a result, financial reserves were increased and the town’s credit rating was upgraded.
He understands that every dollar that government spends is a dollar from someone’s pocket.
Albany needs that kind of discipline, not unfunded mandates and projects that don’t deliver for Western New York.
Stop the fraud!
Stop the wasteful spending!
Gary answers to you, not to Albany.
Public Safety Gary spent 27 years fighting violent crime, organized crime, terrorism, cybercrime, and foreign spies.
He understands accountability for public safety from the inside, and knows that law enforcement works best when agencies at all levels work together.
Albany doesn’t need more press releases on this issue.
It needs someone who has actually done the work.
Gary has done the work.
It’s time for new leadership in Albany!
