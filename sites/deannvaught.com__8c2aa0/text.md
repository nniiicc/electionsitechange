She represents District 87 which includes Little River County, Sevier County, and a portion of Howard County including the city of Dierks.
Representative Vaught graduated from Southern Arkansas University with a Bachelor of Science degree in Agriculture Business and is currently a farmer in Horatio.
She is a member of Farm Bureau and Pork Producers.
She is a mother of three, and resides in Horatio with her husband, Jon.
