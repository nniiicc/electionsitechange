State Senate Meriden, Middletown, Middlefield, Cheshire DONATE VOLUNTEER Your hometown leader.
The communities in the 13th Senate District are engrained in Jack.
He is raising his family in Meriden, where he dedicates every single day to improving his neighbors' quality of life.
For two terms, he has served Cheshire as a trusted leader.
Growing up, he volunteered his summers in Middlefield at the John J.
Nerden Camp.
And when his family first emigrated to America from Sicily for a better life, they settled in Middletown.
This Senate district is defined by its people-- sincere, hardworking, and willing to go above and beyond for neighbors and loved ones.
As a working dad, Jack remains focused on instilling these same values in our next generation, including his kids.
And as long as he represents these communities in Hartford, Jack will have your back.
A principled legislator.
Jack is a dynamic, versatile, and forward-thinking state lawmaker.
An state representative for Meriden and Cheshire in the Connecticut House of Representatives, Jack listens to and speaks up for those of us who need it most.
In Hartford, Jack has quickly established a reputation as a hard worker for his constituents and a trusted voice on the Judiciary Committee.
Collaborating alongside his colleagues in Meriden, Cheshire, and other communities across Connecticut, Jack has secured invaluable funding for our schools, nonprofits, first responders, seniors, students, and many other community stakeholders.
He has also championed legislation that has reduced government costs, strengthened outcomes for middle and working class families, and expanded due process for all Connecticut residents.
These are chaotic times.
We need experienced leadership with a fresh perspective.
Jack delivers both.
His voice is the one we need for Meriden, Cheshire, Middletown, and Middlefield.
An experienced attorney.
Jack is a graduate of the University of Notre Dame, Georgetown University, and Boston College Law School.
He practices law with Shipman & Goodwin LLP, a full service law firm in downtown Hartford.
He offers deep experience advising elected officials, contractors, homeowners, developers, municipalities, small businesses, stockbrokers, and a wide range of other clients.
His areas of practice include real estate litigation, municipal law, and commercial litigation.
He has successfully argued in front of a wide variety of authorities, including trial courts, administrative boards, and the Connecticut Appellate Court.
Jack has leveraged his extensive legal knowledge to make valuable contributions to the Judiciary and General Law Committees and spearhead some of the most complex legislation to reach the House floor.
