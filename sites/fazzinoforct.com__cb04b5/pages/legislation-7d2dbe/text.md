Delivering reliable (and reasonable) utilities.
It's no secret that Connecticut has some of the highest utility costs nationwide.
This is our biggest challenge as a state.
Jack sees investing in grid infrastructure, increasing electric supply, shaving back unnecessary public benefits, and supporting principled utilities regulation as the hallmarks of Connecticut utilities reform.
In 2025, Jack co-sponsored P.A. 25-173: An Act Concerning Energy Affordability, Access, and Accountability, a bipartisan effort to address Connecticut's skyrocketing electric rates.
This proposal will reallocate approximately $800 million in public benefits over the next three years.
It will also permit securitizing storm recovery costs, offsetting the immediate impact on ratepayers and businesses for these costs.
The legislation also allocates a meaningful portion of funding for nuclear energy, in an effort to reduce supply costs, the biggest driver of utility costs statewide.
As your State Senator, Jack will fight to hold utilities companies accountable, champion meaningful choice in public utilities, and find creative solutions to our ongoing energy crisis.
