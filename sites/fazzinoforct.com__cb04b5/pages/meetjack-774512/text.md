Jack's story begins with his grandfather, Giuseppe ("Joe") Fazzino, who left a small farming town in Sicily for a better life in Middletown, Connecticut.
Like so many other hardworking immigrants who have made Connecticut the strong state it is today, Jack's grandfather built a life for himself and his family from the ground up.
After graduating from Middletown High School, he attended classes at Wesleyan, worked nights as a security guard, and, ultimately, became the union president of I.A.M.
Local 1249 in New Britain.
Joe then passed down this immigrant work ethic to the next generation.
Jack's parents built a successful small business and raised their three kids in the Hanging Hills of Connecticut.
Growing up, Jack took classes at Valentin Karate, played basketball at the Boys & Girls Club on Lincoln Street, and spent many Christmas seasons at Hubbard Park.
Summers were filled with volunteer days at the John J.
Nerden Camp in Middlefield and walks around Hanover Pond with his childhood dog.
Now, Jack has worked to instill this same immigrant work ethic in the next generation.
He is raising his family on the east side of Meriden.
His wife, a physician, is building a robust family practice in Middletown.
For generations, his family has prioritized giving back.
And for years now, Jack has done just this for the communities that raised him.
At just 19 years old, Jack took his first job with the Connecticut General Assembly.
Over the next ten years, his experiences in public leadership have been rewarding, consistent, and diverse.
In law school, he clerked for a federal judge in Boston, published writings on a commercial law journal, and dedicated 300 hours of pro bono service to tenants facing eviction.
He has experience advising local governments as a municipal attorney, and serving as an elected official in local government.
At just 31 years old, he is among the youngest legislators currently serving in the Connecticut General Assembly.
Despite his age, he has brought diverse skills and principled conviction to Hartford, which could only develop after several years of public service.
Challenging times call for decisive leadership.
Jack has proven time and again to be a steady hand for the folks he represents.
People hire Jack to solve their problems.
He has successfully prevented his clients from losing their homes to eviction and foreclosure.
He has helped vindicate Ponzi Scheme victims after they've lost their life savings.
He has advised small businesses as they navigate financial distress.
He has represented towns facing tough legal questions.
He has successfully argued before a three-judge panel at the Connecticut Appellate Court.
We need leaders with common sense who can think critically and who bring real world experience to their lawmaking.
In the same vein, Jack brings a constituent-centered approach to his work.
In three campaigns, Jack has knocked roughly 15,000 doors.
He has helped seniors heat their homes.
He has guided them through difficult statutory questions on issues like special education, insurance, and environmental remediation.
He has served as an effective facilitator between state decision-makers, local officials, and his constituents.
He has successfully represented one of the most diverse house districts in Connecticut.
Jack's primary goal is to bring more young families to Connecticut.
He believes in Connecticut because he knows firsthand what this state can do for its young people.
Jack is an avid runner and twice-participant in the Meriden Markham Airport's 5K Run the Runway, the best showcase of the South Meriden Neighborhood.
He loves to teach people about civic engagement, offering lectures on the legislative process to community organizations across the district, including the Meriden Rotary Club.
He also loves to participate in community cleanups, notably those hosted by Ball Headz Inc., Meriden Rotary, and various land trusts.
He has spent significant time at senior centers across his district, building crucial relationships with those who have lived the longest in our communities.
He has gone on ride-alongs with the Meriden Police Department to get a firsthand understanding of the challenges they experience.
The challenge and beauty of this Senate district is its diversity.
As a state representative, Jack already represents just about every possible kind of neighborhood-- from multi-family high rise apartments on East Main Street, to dense middle class neighborhoods on Diamond Hill, to open farmland in Cheshire.
Active participation in our communities is the only way to truly understand their value.
