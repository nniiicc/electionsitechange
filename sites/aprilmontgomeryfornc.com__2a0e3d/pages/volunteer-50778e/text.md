Ways to Get Involved You don’t need political experience — just a willingness to listen, share ideas, and help connect us with the community.
Put a Sign in Your Yard: Show your support with a lawn sign!
Visible, simple, and powerful — signs help build momentum and show others that change is growing in your neighborhood.
Request a sign!
We’ll deliver it to your door.
Canvass & Connect: Knock on doors or make calls to introduce April, listen to concerns, and invite neighbors to get involved.
We’ll train you — all you need is a friendly voice and a pair of comfortable shoes.
Text & Phone Bank: Help us reach thousands of voters with a message from one neighbor to another.
Join us in person or from home — we’ll provide the tools and talking points.
Host a Sunday Scoop: Open your porch, driveway, or local park for a casual ice cream social with April.
We’ll bring the scoops — you bring the neighbors.
