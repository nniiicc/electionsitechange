Brian Lambert FOR CONGRESS About Brian Why Libertarian Issues Updates Volunteer Contact Donate Where I Stand Fiscal Responsibility Washington doesn't have a revenue problem—it has a spending problem.
Every dollar the federal government wastes is a dollar taken from hardworking Americans or borrowed from future generations.
America's Greatest National Security Threat America's national debt has become the single greatest long-term threat to our national security.
A nation buried beneath trillions of dollars in debt cannot remain economically strong, strategically independent, or leave future generations the country they deserve.
Every dollar Washington borrows today is a bill sent to our children tomorrow.
We cannot continue spending money we do not have while expecting future Americans to bear the burden.
Fiscal responsibility is not simply about balancing numbers on a ledger.
It is about preserving America's independence, protecting our economic future, and honoring our obligation to leave the next generation a stronger nation than the one we inherited.
Why This Matters The national debt continues to grow while Congress spends money it doesn't have.
Inflation has quietly reduced the purchasing power of every American family.
Government should live within its means just like the people it represents.
Families balance budgets every day.
Washington should be expected to do the same.
Pass a Balanced Budget Congress should be required to live within its means just as American families do.
Confront the National Debt America's national debt has become the single greatest long-term threat to our national security and the future prosperity of our children.
End Reckless Spending Washington must stop spending money it doesn't have and adding to the national debt.
Stop Inflationary Money Printing Printing trillions of dollars devalues every paycheck and every retirement account.
Audit Federal Agencies Every federal agency should be accountable for how it spends taxpayer dollars.
Eliminate Unconstitutional Bureaucracies Agencies that exceed the powers granted by the Constitution should be reduced or eliminated.
Hold Congress Accountable Members of Congress should be held responsible for the nation's fiscal health, not rewarded for reckless spending.
Protect Future Generations Restore fiscal responsibility so our children inherit opportunity instead of crushing debt.
The Bottom Line Fiscal responsibility isn't just about balancing numbers on a spreadsheet.
It's about honoring the trust taxpayers place in their government.
Every dollar Washington spends was earned by someone who worked for it.
We can restore accountability, reduce waste, and build a government that lives within its means.
That's how we protect both our economy and the future of our children.
