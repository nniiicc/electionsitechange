Energy Independence • Free Markets • National Security Why It Matters Affordable, reliable energy powers every part of the American economy.
Families, farmers, manufacturers, and small businesses all depend on access to abundant and dependable energy.
Energy policy is more than an economic issue—it is a national security issue.
A nation that depends on foreign adversaries for critical energy resources weakens its own independence.
America has the resources, innovation, and workforce to meet our own energy needs while continuing to develop new technologies for the future.
Government should encourage innovation and responsible development, not pick winners and losers through mandates and subsidies.
Where I Stand I support an all-of-the-above energy strategy that allows American producers to compete and innovate in a free market.
I support responsible domestic production of oil, natural gas, nuclear power, and renewable energy where it makes economic sense.
Energy decisions should be driven by consumers, innovation, and sound science—not political ideology.
America's goal should be energy security, affordable prices, and reduced dependence on foreign governments.
My Commitments Promote Energy Independence America should produce the energy it needs whenever possible.
Strengthen National Security Reliable domestic energy reduces dependence on foreign adversaries.
Support Free Markets Competition and innovation—not government mandates—should guide energy policy.
Encourage Innovation New technologies should compete on their merits and benefit consumers.
Reduce Regulatory Burdens Unnecessary regulations should not stand in the way of responsible energy production.
Protect Affordability Energy policy should keep costs reasonable for American families and businesses.
Bottom Line America is strongest when it can power its homes, businesses, and military with reliable, affordable energy produced here at home.
