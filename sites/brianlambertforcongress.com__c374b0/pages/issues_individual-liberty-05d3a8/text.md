Brian Lambert FOR CONGRESS About Brian Why Libertarian Issues Updates Volunteer Contact Donate Where I Stand Individual Liberty Every American is born with rights.
Government does not create those rights, and it should not take them away.
Why This Matters Liberty is not a gift from government.
It is the birthright of every American.
Free speech, privacy, property rights, due process, religious liberty, and the right to keep and bear arms are not optional.
They are protected by the Constitution and must be defended without apology.
When government treats freedom like a permission slip, it stops serving the people and starts ruling over them.
Defend the Bill of Rights The Bill of Rights is not a list of government privileges.
It is a boundary government must not cross.
Protect Free Speech Americans should never fear government punishment for speaking their minds or challenging those in power.
Protect Religious Liberty Government has no authority to dictate matters of conscience, faith, or belief.
Defend the Second Amendment The right to keep and bear arms is a constitutional right and must be protected.
Protect Privacy and Property Americans have the right to be secure in their homes, property, personal data, and private lives.
Oppose Government Overreach Personal decisions should remain with individuals and families, not federal bureaucrats.
The Bottom Line Liberty is not negotiable.
It does not belong to the government, and it should never depend on which party is in power.
Freedom is not Left or Right.
