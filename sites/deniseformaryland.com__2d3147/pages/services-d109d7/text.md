CITATIONS & MEMORIALS
You may request a citation from my office for the following:
- Birthdays, 60 years or older
- Wedding anniversaries, 25 years or more
- Retirement & years of service, 20 years of more
- Eagle Scout rank or Girl Scout Gold Award
- Other special awards & acknowledgements
Send request to my office with the full name of recipient, date needed, short summary of the occasion, and if you would like me to formally present it.

BOND REQUESTS

The number and type of projects funded by the General Assembly is limited and extremely competitive.
