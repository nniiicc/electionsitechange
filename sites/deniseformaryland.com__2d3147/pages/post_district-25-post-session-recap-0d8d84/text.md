All Posts News Article Blog Press Release Search DISTRICT 25 POST 2025 LEGISLATIVE SESSION RECAP Denise Roberts May 5, 2025 1 min read Please join the District 25 Leadership Team, which includes the esteemed Senator Nick Charles , Delegates Denise Roberts , Kent Roberson , and Karen Toles , for an important and informative event—the Post 2025 Legislative Session Recap.
This event is scheduled for Wednesday, May 14, 2005, at 6:00 p.m. sharp.
The Townhall will take place at Second Baptist Church SW, located at 5501 Silver Hill Road, District Heights, Maryland.
During this event, residents of the District 25 area will be updated on a variety of legislative changes that are poised to impact the lives of Prince Georgians and Marylanders alike.
These updates will cover key issues that have been addressed in the recent legislative session, including education reform, healthcare initiatives, public safety measures, and economic development strategies.
This recap is particularly crucial as it will provide insights into how these legislative changes will affect local communities, families, and businesses.
Attendees will not only gain valuable information but also have the chance to engage directly with their elected officials.
There will be a segment dedicated to questions and answers, allowing residents to voice their concerns and seek clarification on matters that are important to them.
The District 25 Leadership Team is committed to transparency and community involvement, and this event represents an essential opportunity for constituents to stay informed and actively participate in the democratic process.
We encourage all residents to mark their calendars for this pivotal event and to come prepared to listen, learn, and contribute to the discussions that shape our community’s future.
Your voice matters, and together we can work towards a better understanding of the legislative landscape that affects our daily lives.
