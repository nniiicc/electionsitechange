END OF SESSION LETTER April 7, 2025 Hello Neighbors! ​ As the 447th Legislative Session of the Maryland General Assembly ends, I want to extend my heartfelt thanks for the continued honor of representing you and the residents of District 25. ​ Despite the challenges of a tight budget year, we made meaningful progress on the issues that matter most— reserved funding for Developmental Disabilities Administration (DDA), public safety, education, healthcare access, and environmental protection.
I’m proud to share a few key wins we achieved together. ​ Working in partnership with my dedicated colleagues—Senator Nick Charles, Delegate Karen Toles, and Delegate Kent Roberson—we remained focused on delivering results for our community. ✅ LEGISLATIVE HIGHLIGHTS ​ ​ 👶 Foster Care Support (HB1057/SB0833) Private providers of treatment foster care homes will now receive childcare stipends—ensuring fair and equitable support across Maryland's foster care system. 📄 Read More ​ 🚯 Illegal Dumping Enforcement (HB1527/SB0525) Residents in Prince George’s County can now submit video evidence of illegal dumping.
If that footage results in a conviction, the submitter will receive 50% of the collected fine. 📄 Read More ​ 🛍️ Organized Retail Theft Prevention (HB0179) We’ve strengthened laws to target repeat and large-scale retail theft, giving courts the authority to classify them as organized retail crimes and apply tougher penalties. 📄 Read More ​ 🏥 HEALTH & EDUCATION ACHIEVEMENTS ​ ​ 💻 Preserve Telehealth Access Act (HB869) Telehealth insurance coverage is now permanent—removing barriers like travel and scheduling conflicts to make healthcare more accessible. 📄 Read More ​ 📘 Excellence in Maryland Public Schools Act (HB504) This bill protects funding for low-income students, English language learners, and community schools, while investing in teacher recruitment and professional development. 📄 Read More ​ 🏥 Maryland Health Insurance Coverage Commission (HB718) This new commission will monitor changes to federal health policies and ensure that affordable healthcare remains accessible in our state. 📄 Read More ​ 📍 BOND INITIATIVES: Local Investments Secured for District 25 ​ ​ We’re proud to have secured critical funding for important community projects, including: American Legion Post No.
