KEY ISSUES
SMALL AND MINORITY BUSINESSES
Supporting the work that small businesses do for the betterment of our economy by expanding the "Jobs First Act", designed to create equitable opportunities for small minority and black-owned businesses to secure resources.
EDUCATION
Education is the foundation from which you can create your own success and achieve financial stability.
Education gives us a refined knowledge of the world around us.
HEALTHCARE SERVICES
A healthy community is the key to a successful community.
We support quality healthcare, healthy lifestyles, and holistic wellness and partner with health-focused organizations to facilitate access to quality healthcare options.
Women's healthcare, especially, will be a key focus because women have unique challenges when it comes to healthcare.
I am committed to developing programs that will address these needs.
AUTISM
Working to improve the lives of autistic individuals and their families through community-building, advocacy, and education.
SENIOR SERVICES
Our senior citizens possess insurmountable wealth and experience and we strive to uplift them and serve as an ally for the issues that affect their quality of life.
We work to promote the independence and enhance the dignity of Prince George’s County older adults, persons with disabilities, and their families through a community-based system of services, opportunities, benefits, and protections.
PUBLIC SAFETY
Denise strives to improve the quality of life in Prince George’s County by promoting safety, advocating for the highest quality of emergency and medical services, and community outreach programs.
