1st Year Delegate Denise Roberts Among 13 Howard University Alumni Honored at 2024 Bison in Annapolis Reception
On Thursday, Jan. 11, nearly 100 people, including President Ben Vinson III, Ph.D., Cabinet members and student leaders, gathered at the historic Governor Calvert House in downtown Annapolis to celebrate Bison making an impact in Annapolis.
Awardees included first-year Delegate Denise Roberts, class of 1995 Howard Alumni.
The inaugural event was held last January on the heels of Gov.
