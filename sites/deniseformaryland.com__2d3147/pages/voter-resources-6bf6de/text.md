VOTER RESOURCES
Our goal is to equip you with the necessary tools to participate in both the primary and general elections.
Here, you'll discover vital tools, guides, and resources to assist you in navigating the voting process, from registration to polling places.
Remember, your strength is in your right to vote.
ELECTION INFORMATION
EARLY VOTING
Vote early and avoid the crowds!
You can cast your ballot at select locations in your county without an appointment.
Just show up at a time that suits you.
Find your voting location today!
Early voting is October 22, 2026, through October 29, 2026
VOTE BY MAIL
Registered voters can easily vote by mail.
Request your ballot online, by mail, fax, or at an election office.
After filling it out, return it to a drop box by Election Day or mail it back, ensuring it's postmarked by November 3, 2026.
ELECTION DAY
Vote in person on November 3, 2026, until 8 PM.
Polls will be open statewide, and we have everything you need for confident voting, including same-day registration.
REGISTER TO VOTE
Registering to vote and choosing your party affiliation takes just a minute.
You can register online, by mail, or in person at select locations using your driver's license, ID card, or the last four digits of your social number.
REGISTER ONLINE
This website allows you to:
- Register to vote in Maryland elections
- Request a mail-in
- Update your registration for name or address changes, or if you wish to change your political party.
To make updates, please verify your identity by entering the necessary information.
REGISTER BY MAIL
To register online, you need a valid Maryland ID or driver's license.
- If you're outside the U.S. a military member, use the last four digits of your social security number.
- Your signature will be based on the ID or social security number provided.
- If you prefer not register online or lack ID, complete your application here, print it, sign it, and mail it to your local board of elections.
IN-PERSON REGISTRATION
You can register to vote in person at local election boards, State Board of Elections, Department of Health offices,VA offices, and public colleges, among others.
Registration is also available during early voting and on election day with a list of early voting centers provided later.
