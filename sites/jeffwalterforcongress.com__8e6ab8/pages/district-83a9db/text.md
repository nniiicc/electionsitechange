Illinois’ 11th Congressional District
Illinois’ 11th Congressional District is a dynamic and growing region that stretches across parts of eight counties - Boone, Cook, Dekalb, DuPage, Kane, Lake, McHenry, and Will - and brings together some of the state’s most vibrant and pleasant communities.
Anchored by major population centers like Naperville and Aurora, the district is home to a diverse electorate representative of the whole state.
With its blend of farmland, suburban neighborhoods, historic downtowns, and high-tech corridors, the 11th District represents the heart of modern Illinois—where small businesses can thrive, families can put down roots, and the American Dream can succeed.
