Rural Jobs & Local Economies My No.
1 priority is job growth in our district.
This is why I will support new and creative local private- sector initiatives designed to create employment across Assembly District 1.
At the same time, I will bring new job training initiatives to local communities most in need.
Small & Medium Business I have a plan to cut red tape and expand access to capital for local entrepreneurs so they can capitalize on future-proof industries like timber harvesting and innovative new outdoor recreation opportunities.
Our Main Streets are the backbone of this district, and they deserve a champion in Sacramento.
Land & Water Preservation To stimulate job growth, enable sustainable forest clearing, and prevent future wildfires, I'm proposing a set of regulatory reforms that will promote the expansion of engineered wood production in our district sourced from small-diameter logs collected during forest thinning operations.
Rising Costs & Affordability Too many of our neighbors are a single ambulance ride away from financial ruin.
As your Assembly member, I will launch a North State medical debt relief fund that partners with local hospitals and donors to buy up families' unpaid medical bills and erase them for good.
Wildfire Resilience & Lower Utility Bills I refuse to take campaign contributions from public utilities, so I will fight to prevent them from raising rates to cover the costs of their mistakes.
In addition, reducing exposure to wildfire risk through forest management and defensible space programs is central to my platform.
About Dianna Rooted in Northern California I am a 5th generation Californian and a proud resident of Mount Shasta.
My career has been defined by a deep sense of service and accountability.
As a Foreign Affairs Officer at the U.S.
Department of State and a field-based aid specialist, I have served in some of the world's most challenging environments — from Asia to the Middle East — strengthening democratic processes, protecting human rights, and pressing governments to deliver for their people.
Today, I am bringing that same dedication and grit to the North State.
We face critical challenges: rising costs, a lack of rural jobs that pay a living wage, policies that impede our small businesses, and the urgent need to protect our land and water.
I am running for Assembly to ensure that North State communities have a strong and effective representative in Sacramento — one who will work with anyone, across the political spectrum, to achieve real results for our district.
