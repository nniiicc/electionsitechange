Meet Bryan My name is Bryan Cohn and I am running for NC House District 32 Father I decided to run for public office because of my three young kids.
I am running to ensure a better future for my kids and yours in our community and throughout North Carolina.
Public Servant In 2021, I became a public servant because I believe everyone deserves leaders who understand it’s our job to listen to you and fight for you, not for themselves or special interests.
That's why I was elected to represent District 32, and why I'm running to continue representing it.
Leader From my professional work in agriculture and as a small business owner to my service as an elected official, I’ve worked with diverse voices across our state and country to get things done for communities and working families.
