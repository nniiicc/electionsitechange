Meet Bryan Cohn
State Representative Bryan Cohn serves Granville and Vance counties in the North Carolina House.
Bryan is a small-business owner, husband, and father of three.
He and his wife are raising their family in Oxford, where he also owns and operates a local business that pays a living wage and creates jobs in the community.
Bryan believes public office should be measured by results, accountability, and whether government is making life better for the people it represents.
He understands the pressures facing working families and small businesses because he deals with them every day.
Delivering for Granville and Vance Counties
Since taking office, Bryan has focused on protecting local resources, strengthening public infrastructure, defending access to the ballot, and standing up for workers and public employees.
- Fought to protect local control of public water resources and opposed efforts to shift the costs and risks of regional growth onto Granville and Vance counties.
- Challenged political interference in local election administration, demanded transparency, and fought to protect fair access to early voting.
- Supported stronger pay and protections for workers, including teachers, law enforcement officers, firefighters, state employees, and the people who keep local government running.
