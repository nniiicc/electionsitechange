Jeremy 4 TN operates an SMS messaging program to communicate with supporters who have opted in to receive text messages.
Messages may include campaign updates, event invitations, volunteer coordination, and other campaign-related information based on the topics you selected when subscribing.
This program is operated using Twilio as our messaging service provider.
Your participation is entirely voluntary.
You may opt in to receive SMS messages from Jeremy 4 TN through any of the following methods:
- Website subscribe form - Complete the subscription form at jeremy4tn.com/subscribe and check the SMS opt-in box for your selected topic preferences (Campaign Updates, Event Invites, and/or Volunteer Opportunities).
- Donation page - Check the SMS opt-in box displayed below the Phone Number field on our Anedot donation page at secure.anedot.com/jeremy-4-tn/signs.
Opting in is not required to complete a donation.
- Keyword opt-in - Text JOIN to our campaign number from your mobile device.
This method is used at live campaign events.
You will receive a confirmation message immediately upon opting in.
By opting in through any of these methods, you confirm that you are the owner or authorized user of the mobile number provided, that you are 18 years of age or older, and that you consent to receive recurring automated text messages from Jeremy 4 TN at that number.
Consent is not a condition of any purchase, donation, or other activity.
Reply STOP to any message at any time.
You will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent to that number.
You may also email [email protected] with your name, mobile number, and a request to be removed.
Reply HELP to any message, or contact us at [email protected].
