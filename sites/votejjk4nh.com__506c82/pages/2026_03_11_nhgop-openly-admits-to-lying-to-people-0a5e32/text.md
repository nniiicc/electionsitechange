Even less does it happen when they’re talking to a new organization.
Yet, Jason Osborne, the House Majority Leader of the NH House of Representatives, came out and said this and more in remarks to the Libertarian Party of New Hampshire event in Concord last weekend.
“Sometimes you have to go on the 6 o’clock news and have to say with a straight face something that you don’t actually believe,” Osborne said.
Jason Osborne Referring to himself as a reformed radical, Jason Osborne went on to further explain his thought process and ideology, including items such as: “Your vote is just a tool.” – also known as ‘the ends justifies the means’, “A well-organized coalition voting for imperfect candidates — flawed candidates, candidates you may not even like at all” – also known as ‘party over state’.
“Sometimes you have to go on the 6 o’clock news and have to say with a straight face something that you don’t actually believe,” – lying…this is just called lying.
Speaker Osborne’s presence at the event is even more surprising considering the Libertarian Party of New Hampshire recently stated that it was ‘perfectly permissible to kill’ former Executive Councilor for income tax plan , seemingly aligning to this line of thinking.
I would really love to know what WMUR thinks about Speaker Osborne admitting to lying on air.
My first thoughts here are that I’ve never understood the premise of ‘party over state’ or ‘party over country’.
I understand there can be general differences between individuals, but someone who is representing a populace, I feel, shouldn’t be restricted to just their party.
If I were to be elected a representative, I would work to represent ALL people of Strafford District 6.
We all need to buy groceries, we all drive on the same roads, we all pay taxes; we ALL share the same human experience, albeit in different shoes and situations.
And, if I thought I was flawed, I wouldn’t run.
I don’t know if that’s something politicians are supposed to say.
The whole reason I’m looking to run is because I’m upset with what has been happening.
The confusion on repealed laws, needing to pass amendments on bills controlled by a super majority because they were written incorrectly, or when you have town officials saying they’re trying to pass laws to force one political party to move out….it just doesn’t make sense to me.
I’m running to restore common sense legislation back to Concord.
