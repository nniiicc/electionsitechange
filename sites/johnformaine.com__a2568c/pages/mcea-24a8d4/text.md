How to Donate to a Maine Clean Elections Act Candidate
Step 1: Go to http://maine.gov/cleanelections
Step 2: Click “Contribute”
Step 3: Complete fields as follows:
- Municipality (select Sanford)
- Residential Address (your address)
- Street number (i.e.
“1” for 1 Main Street)
- Steet Name: start typing and your street will show up (i.e.
Main St.) Be sure to check that you selected the right street type (St, Rd.
Ave.)
- Unit/Apartment Number (if applicable)
- Voter Name (Your name)
- Enter your name as your registered to vote
- Click Continue
Step 4: Check the box next to John Henkelman any other Maine Clean Elections Act candidates.
Step 5: Sign the electronic signature.
Then click Continue to Payment
Step 6: Complete payment information.
