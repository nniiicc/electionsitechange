Maine Education Association Endorses Educator and Scientist John Henkelman for State House District 143
SANFORD, Maine — The Maine Education Association (MEA)—representing thousands of teachers, higher education faculty, education support professionals, and school staff across the state—has officially endorsed candidate John Henkelman for the Maine House of Representatives in District 143.
The endorsement highlights Henkelman’s background as an educator, analytical scientist, and community advocate, as well as his commitment to strengthening public education, expanding early childhood learning, and delivering data-driven solutions for Sanford families.
Standing Up for Students, Educators, and Working Families
As an adjunct educator at the University of New England and a former analytical development scientist in biotech, Henkelman brings a strong commitment to public education, fact-based leadership, and workforce development to the race.
His legislative priorities align directly with core educational and community needs in Maine, including:
- Childcare & Early Learning for all Mainers: Expanding state-level subsidies, tax credits, and provider slots to support working parents and ensure children enter school prepared to succeed.
- Mental Health & Student Support: Growing Maine’s mental health workforce and expanding community-based crisis response to relieve pressure on schools and emergency services.
- Supporting Educators & Public Schools: Investing in the public education workforce, ensuring fair compensation and resources, and fostering strong partnerships between schools and local communities.
"Public education is the foundation of a strong community and a thriving economy," said Henkelman.
"As an educator and a scientist, I have seen firsthand how access to quality education changes lives.
I am deeply honored to earn the endorsement of the Maine Education Association.
In Augusta, I will work tirelessly to support our teachers and support staff, invest in early childhood education, and ensure every student in Sanford has the tools they need to succeed."
Fact-Based Leadership Powered by the Community
Henkelman’s campaign is funded entirely through the Maine Clean Election Act (MCEA) system, relying solely on $5 qualifying contributions from District 143 residents to ensure complete accountability to neighbors rather than special interest groups or PACs.
In addition to his teaching and scientific experience, Henkelman serves on the Sanford Recreation Advisory Board, contributes to the Sanford Springvale News, and is a Registered Maine Guide committed to preserving public access to Maine’s outdoors.

Maine AFL-CIO Endorses Slate of Candidates for Sanford and Springvale House Districts
SANFORD, ME - Candidates Rebecca Andrews (House District 141) and John Henkelman (House District 143) announced that they have received the full endorsement of the Maine AFL-CIO for their campaigns to represent the people of Sanford and Springvale in the 133rd Maine Legislature.
The Maine AFL-CIO, representing over 40,000 working Mainers across the state, grants endorsements to candidates who demonstrate a steadfast commitment to supporting working families, protecting union rights, advocating for fair wages, and addressing the rising cost of living.
With this unified slate of endorsements, working people in Sanford and Springvale have strong champions in all three local House districts.
"I am deeply honored to earn the trust and endorsement of the Maine AFL-CIO," said Rebecca Andrews, candidate for House District 141.
"Working families are the backbone of our community.
In Augusta, I will fight to protect workplace safety, secure fair wages, and ensure every worker in Sanford, Springvale, Shapleigh, and Newfield has the opportunity to thrive."
"The labor movement built Maine's middle class, and it is a privilege to stand with the working people of the Maine AFL-CIO," said John Henkelman, candidate for House District 143.
"In the 133rd Legislature, I will work tirelessly to champion policies that put working people first, strengthen our local workforce, and keep Sanford affordable for families."
The Maine AFL-CIO endorsement process involves a comprehensive evaluation of candidate questionnaires, voting records, and interviews regarding labor, economic, and workplace policy matters.
Learn more at https://maineaflcio.org/news/endorsements.
The general election will take place on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.

Protect Sanford Residents: Critical Rent Stabilization Vote Approaching City Council
Updated (01 June 2026):
Here is a link to the finial Mobile Home Park Lot Rent Stabilization Ordinance
The Sanford City Council is preparing to vote on a proposed rent stabilization ordinance that aims to protect local residents from destabilizing housing costs.
To ensure the public is fully informed and engaged, I have created a centralized online resource containing all official documents currently under consideration by city leaders that can be found here: https://www.johnformaine.com/sanford-rent-stabilization.
As housing costs continue to rise across the region, this ordinance represents a critical step toward ensuring housing security for working families, seniors, and vulnerable populations within Sanford.
By establishing fair and predictable limits on rent adjustments, the measure will help prevent displacement and foster long-term community stability.
Like many things in Maine, action starts local and this is the first step in protecting mobile home park residents across the state.
To that end I applaud the work that Sanford City Council and Manager have done on this first of it’s kind in the Pine Tree State.
Securing stable housing is a fundamental necessity for the well-being of our community.
This proposed ordinance provides balanced protections that keep families in their homes and keep Sanford strong.
I urge every resident to examine the documents before city council, understand the positive impact of this policy, and make their voices heard at this weeks city council meeting.
This upcoming vote provides an opportunity for Sanford residents to advocate for proactive solutions to local housing challenges.
Community members are encouraged to review the materials, attend the City Council meeting, and contact their local representatives to express support for the ordinance.
For more information, to view the official city council documents, or to learn how to participate in the upcoming public meeting, please visit https://www.johnformaine.com/sanford-rent-stabilization.
