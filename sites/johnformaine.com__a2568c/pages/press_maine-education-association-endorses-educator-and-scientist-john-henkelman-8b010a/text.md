Maine Education Association Endorses Educator and Scientist John Henkelman for State House District 143
SANFORD, Maine — The Maine Education Association (MEA)—representing thousands of teachers, higher education faculty, education support professionals, and school staff across the state—has officially endorsed candidate John Henkelman for the Maine House of Representatives in District 143.
The endorsement highlights Henkelman’s background as an educator, analytical scientist, and community advocate, as well as his commitment to strengthening public education, expanding early childhood learning, and delivering data-driven solutions for Sanford families.
Standing Up for Students, Educators, and Working Families
As an adjunct educator at the University of New England and a former analytical development scientist in biotech, Henkelman brings a strong commitment to public education, fact-based leadership, and workforce development to the race.
His legislative priorities align directly with core educational and community needs in Maine, including:
- Childcare & Early Learning for all Mainers: Expanding state-level subsidies, tax credits, and provider slots to support working parents and ensure children enter school prepared to succeed.
- Mental Health & Student Support: Growing Maine’s mental health workforce and expanding community-based crisis response to relieve pressure on schools and emergency services.
- Supporting Educators & Public Schools: Investing in the public education workforce, ensuring fair compensation and resources, and fostering strong partnerships between schools and local communities.
"Public education is the foundation of a strong community and a thriving economy," said Henkelman.
"As an educator and a scientist, I have seen firsthand how access to quality education changes lives.
I am deeply honored to earn the endorsement of the Maine Education Association.
In Augusta, I will work tirelessly to support our teachers and support staff, invest in early childhood education, and ensure every student in Sanford has the tools they need to succeed."
Fact-Based Leadership Powered by the Community
Henkelman’s campaign is funded entirely through the Maine Clean Election Act (MCEA) system, relying solely on $5 qualifying contributions from District 143 residents to ensure complete accountability to neighbors rather than special interest groups or PACs.
In addition to his teaching and scientific experience, Henkelman serves on the Sanford Recreation Advisory Board, contributes to the Sanford Springvale News, and is a Registered Maine Guide committed to preserving public access to Maine’s outdoors.
