Elect your neighbor
Running for office was never part of my plan.
But over time, I watched the neighborhood I love become harder to live in and harder to recognize.
I’m Alina Bonsell, your neighbor.
I’ve called Manhattan’s East Side home for more than 20 years.
I’ve worked in healthcare, run my own business, and raised my two sons here.
Our neighborhood's neglect didn’t happen overnight.
It happened slowly, under leadership that looked the other way while the rest of us were expected to accept less.
We pay some of the highest taxes in the country.
Yet, our streets don’t feel safe, businesses are struggling to survive, homelessness and mental illness continue without solutions that work, and too many families are trapped in a broken court system.
For far too long, our elected officials and our government have not been doing their jobs.
Enough neglect.
Elect me, your neighbor, to the New York State Senate so I can be your voice in Albany.

The cost of living is up.

If we vote the same way, we get the same result.
Another term for Liz Krueger means more of what the last 24 years have brought: higher taxes on working families and retirees, a socialist agenda that underfunds our police and puts repeat offenders back on the street, shelters opened in our neighborhoods without warning or a say, and a senator who's endorsed the very policies driving this district in the wrong direction.
I will bring down the cost of living, oppose new taxes on working families and retirees, and fight the Albany spending that's driving prices up.

I will end the surprises in our neighborhoods with a law that requires real notice and community input before any shelter or major housing decision moves forward.
