Cost of Living
Right now, families across Pennsylvania are feeling the squeeze of rising costs and stagnant wages.
We need to get things in place to ease the burden on families.
I want to begin by immediately looking into how the PUC decides whether or not to approve a rate increase.
The fact is when utility companies continue to post profits to shareholders year-over-year, they should not be granted rate increases.
We should not bear the burden of infrastructure improvements, it should be the responsibility of the utility company to manage it’s finances better so infrastructure is improved meaningfully, before it becomes “aging infrastructure”.
I also want to pass legislation to protect families from AI-adjusted price fixing based on algorithms that determine where you stand financially.
While this is not something that has occurred in the district, I believe in being proactive vs reactive.
We should not constantly be chasing to catch up with technology, we should be aware of where it is going.
Finally, I want to ensure that the existing data centers in the district are forced to pay their fair share.
Rapid expansion into our communities has left many feeling the burden of data center construction and operation.
We need to make sure that their costs are not passed onto us.
Data Centers
Data centers have become a central topic across Pennsylvania, with the rapid push to expand into our communities.
To start, I support a moratorium on data centers.
I think it’s important that the townships have time to learn and understand the impact of data centers in their community.
We must make sure that they are held accountable and pay their fair share.
We cannot let them destroy our communities, drain our resources, and drive up our cost of living.

Protecting Children
Protecting children from harmful online content needs to become more of a priority especially with advancements taking place everyday.
Given the reach and anonymity the Internet allows, I think it’s important to make sure our children and youth aren’t being exploited.
I want to pass legislation that would require companies to keep our children and community safe.
When companies build services with the idea of children in mind, it is important that they are held to strict privacy standards to make sure our children’s data is kept safe, and that the interactions they have online are appropriate and with other children, not adults.
I will work on legislation that will require stricter protections in online gaming, such as separating adult players from children players in online games like Roblox.
Further, I will work on legislation that would require age limits on things like social media.
We need to start working on protecting our children from exploitation and online dangers that have become so easy to encounter.
