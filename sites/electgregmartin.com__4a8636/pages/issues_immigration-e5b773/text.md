Immigration
The federal government has failed the American people at the southern border since Joe Biden took office.
Over 7 million illegal aliens have crossed into our country and it affects Tennessee in a great way.
Tennesseans have seen an increase in crime and human and drug trafficking, and Tennessee taxpayers had to pick up the bill.
I voted in the general assembly for three laws:
- Give the Governor the greater authority to send the National Guard to the southern border.
- When local law enforcement detains someone in the state of Tennessee the federal government must be informed.
- The punishment for crimes broken by illegal aliens will be enhanced.
