Regional Organizing Director Location: Locations across Texas Reports to: Organizing Director Position Type: Full-time Start date: ASAP Salary: $6,000 per month The Gina Hinojosa for Governor campaign is seeking Regional Organizing Directors to lead the campaign’s regional persuasion and grassroots organizing program.
This role is responsible for developing a robust volunteer leadership structure, executing direct voter contact programs, and representing the campaign across regional political and community spaces.
To apply, please send a resume and cover letter to info@ginafortexas.com with the subject line “Application for Regional Organizing Director”.
Key Responsibilities ●​ Organizing & Persuasion Program ○​ Design and execute regional persuasion and voter contact programs including canvassing, phone banking, relational organizing, and community outreach. ○​ Ensure the campaign meets ambitious voter contact, persuasion, and volunteer recruitment goals. ○​ Maintain strong data integrity and reporting for all organizing activities. ●​ Volunteer Leadership Development ○​ Recruit, train, and develop volunteer leaders capable of managing teams and organizing independently. ○​ Build a scalable leadership ladder that grows volunteers into team leaders, captains, and regional leaders. ○​ Facilitate regular trainings, organizing meetings, and volunteer mobilization events. ●​ Regional Strategy & Operations ○​ Coordinate with allied organizations to maximize turnout and persuasion efforts. ○​ Work closely with campaign leadership to develop regional organizing plans and priorities. ○​ Monitor progress toward organizing goals and implement systems for accountability. ○​ Support campaign events, rallies, and regional campaign activities.
Qualifications ●​ At least two years campaign organizing experience. ●​ Experience managing volunteers or staff organizers. ●​ Strong communication and relationship-building skills. ●​ Familiarity with voter contact tools and organizing databases. ●​ Highly organized, detail-oriented, and able to manage multiple priorities simultaneously ●​ Comfortable working in a fast-paced, high-pressure campaign environment ●​ Willingness to perform all levels of organizing work—from door-to-door canvassing to leading strategy discussions at regional tables. ●​ Willingness to travel extensively and work evenings and weekends as required
