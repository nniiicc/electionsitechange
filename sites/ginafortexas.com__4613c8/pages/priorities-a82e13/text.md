Securing the American Dream
The American Dream is simple: it’s the chance to work hard, get ahead, and build a better life for your family.
But for too many Texans, it feels out of reach.
As Governor, Gina Hinojosa will have a short, simple agenda, because the plan should be as plain as the promise: save our public schools, put money in your pocket, and end Greg Abbott’s corruption – so every Texan can live their American Dream
