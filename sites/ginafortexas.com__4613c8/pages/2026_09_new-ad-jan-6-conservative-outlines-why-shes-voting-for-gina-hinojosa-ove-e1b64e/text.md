NEW AD: Jan. 6 Conservative Outlines Why She’s Voting for Gina Hinojosa Over Greg Abbott
Austin, TX – Today, Democratic nominee for Texas Governor Gina Hinojosa released a new campaign ad featuring Lynn Davenport, an education activist and self-proclaimed “staunch conservative” who attended the January 6th rally, outlining why she’s voting for Hinojosa over Greg Abbott.
In the six-figure ad buy, which will run statewide beginning this weekend, Lynn calls out Abbott’s decades of putting billionaires and corporations first and making working Texans foot the bill for his corruption.
From her time in the legislature fighting alongside MAGA moms to defeat vouchers in 2023 to now, Hinojosa has built a broad coalition by meeting Texans in their communities and responding to their needs, whether it’s about saving public schools, lowering costs, or ending the data center madness.
That’s why she’s leading Abbott in back-to-back public polls – including winning over 9% of Republican voters – and why she’ll win this November.

Greg Abbott is messing with Texas.
I was at the January 6th rally.
I’m a staunch conservative, but I cannot support Governor Greg Abbott.
Greg Abbott is bought and paid for by the billionaires and the corporations – those are his donors; that’s who he listens to.
No more.
