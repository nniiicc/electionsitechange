NEW: Hinojosa Continues Latino Voter Outreach With Spanish Town Hall, New Ad
HOUSTON, TX – Yesterday, Democratic nominee for Texas Governor Gina Hinojosa hosted a Spanish town hall in Houston.
Moderated by Carlos Eduardo Espina, Hinojosa spoke directly with hundreds of Spanish-speaking voters as part of the campaign’s broader push to reach Latino voters across Texas.
Today, Hinojosa is building on that outreach with the release of a new bilingual ad, “Trabajar.”
The town hall and new ad come as Hinojosa consolidates her support among Latino voters.
Recent public polling shows that Hinojosa is leading Greg Abbott by 4 points – the first time Abbott has been down in a gubernatorial poll – and winning Latino voters by 33 points, up from an earlier 27-point lead.
This growing support reflects her focus on key issues for Latino communities, including the cost of living and healthcare access.
