From UH Rainbow baseball to community leader, Markus Owens brings a lifetime of teamwork, public service, and dedication to Hawaiʻi Senate District 20 as a Democrat.
A former University of Hawaiʻi Rainbow baseball player and San Francisco Giants minor leaguer, Markus understands what it takes to build a team, overcome challenges, and work together toward a common goal.
That same commitment to teamwork has guided his career and service to Hawaiʻi.
Markus spent 33 years serving the people of Hawaiʻi through both the State of Hawaiʻi and the City and County of Honolulu , gaining extensive experience in government, public relations, permitting, water, wastewater, and environmental issues.
For more than 30 years, he has also been a familiar voice in Hawaiʻi sports as a broadcaster and trusted communicator.
Today, as a Coldwell Banker Realty REALTOR® , Markus helps local families navigate Hawaiʻi’s challenging housing market.
He lives in ʻEwa Beach with his wife, Mary, and understands firsthand the issues facing families and working people in the community.
Now, Markus is ready to bring that same dedication, communication skills, and collaborative spirit to the State Senate.
As a Democrat, he will work with fellow legislators and community leaders to pass meaningful legislation, secure funding, and deliver real results for ʻEwa Beach and Senate District 20 families.
Markus Owens is ready to work, ready to deliver, and ready to serve.
Our Main Issues There are many serious challenges facing our community.
Here’s what we’re fighting for in ‘Ewa Beach.
01 Community Safety With homicides up 80% in West O’ahu and shootings in family areas, our community deserves better.
Markus will fight for fully staffing HPD District 8, supporting community policing, and working with law enforcement to keep our families safe.
02 Smart Growth & Housing As a realtor, Markus sees daily how working families struggle with $719K median homes and $2,527 monthly rents.
We need SMART development—affordable housing with proper infrastructure like sewers, roads, and schools.
No more “monster” developments that destroy neighborhood character.
03 Traffic & Infrastructure 39-minute commutes.
Roads turned into dangerous “drag strips.” Delayed projects like Keaunui Drive Extension.
Markus will prioritize traffic safety, complete critical infrastructure projects, and ensure our growth includes the roads and services we need.
04 Education & Youth From youth baseball to supporting our schools, Markus is committed to giving our keiki the opportunities they deserve.
Quality education, safe schools, and positive alternatives to crime are investments in our community’s future.

8, 2026, primary election, earning 3,716 votes — 100% of the Democratic vote .

Owens now turns his focus toward the General Election and a campaign centered on delivering practical results for the people of District 20.

The General Election will be held November 3, 2026 , when voters will choose between Owens and the republican nominee.
