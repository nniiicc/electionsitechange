AFL-CIO Endorsement
The AFL-CIO (American Federation of Labor and Congress of Industrial Organizations), who is the largest federation of labor unions in the United States and represents nearly 15 million working people across dozens of national and international unions, endorses Markus Owens for the 2026 Hawaii General Election for State Senate District 20.
HGEA Endorsement
The Hawaii Government Employees Association, who has grown to become the state’s largest labor union, with 37,000 members across nine bargaining units, happily endorses Markus Owens for State Senate District 20 in the 2026 November General Election.

Planned Parenthood Alliance Advocates
Planned Parenthood is a leading provider and advocate for accessible, affordable sexual and reproductive health care, education, and reliable health information.
Through its network of nonprofit health centers, community programs, and online resources, Planned Parenthood serves millions of patients with compassionate, respectful, and nonjudgmental care.
Its services include primary and preventive health care, with a strong focus on ensuring that people—regardless of gender or income—can access the care they need.
Planned Parenthood Federation of America supports independent affiliates, while the Planned Parenthood Action Fund separately focuses on advocacy, education, grassroots organizing, and voter engagement.
Together, these organizations work to promote equitable access to health care and empower people to make informed decisions about their health and well-being.
