Raised in Topsham, VT, Aiden has lived in our district his whole life.
A statewide leader and community member at heart, he is a staunch supporter of the rural towns he grew up in, and will prioritize the improvement of everyday Vermonters’ lives.
Aiden supports: - Tax breaks for families, veterans, and our seniors; - Supporting farmers/small business development; - Leading with people over party; - Protecting the rights of ALL Vermonters; - Building affordable housing - Tackling a broken healthcare system - And More!
