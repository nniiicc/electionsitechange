Born and raised in Central Montana, Shane Klakken represents House District 37 — serving communities across Petroleum and Fergus Counties.
With over 31 years of military and public service, Shane brings firsthand experience in leadership, accountability, and dedication to the people of Montana.
As a lifelong advocate for civic engagement, Shane has taught U.S.
Constitution classes and continues to encourage Montanans to take an active role in preserving their freedoms.
Now retired from federal service, he manages his family ranch in Grass Range and remains committed to protecting Montana’s rural way of life.
