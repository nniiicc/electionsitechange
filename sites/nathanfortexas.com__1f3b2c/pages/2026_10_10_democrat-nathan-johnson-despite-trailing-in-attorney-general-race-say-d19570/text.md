Skip to content Facebook X-twitter Instagram Menu Contribute WFAA Democrat Nathan Johnson, despite trailing in Attorney General race, says he’s right where he wants to be October 10, 2026 DALLAS — As we close in on early voting and election day, state Senator Nathan Johnson, the Democratic nominee for Texas Attorney General, says voters are still trying to get to know him and his opponent, Republican state Senator Mayes Middleton.
Johnson expects to define both candidates and gain new supporters with a new seven-figure ad buy now airing across the state.
“It is to close that gap and get on the other side of it.
I want to win this race by three points,” Johnson told us on Inside Texas Politics.
“There’s a lot of undecideds in this election.
Polling and estimates are between anywhere from 8% to 15% undecided.
In 2018 (when he first ran for state Senate), I picked up more than three quarters of the undecided vote.
I plan to do the same thing.” Johnson’s ad will air statewide between now and Election Day.
And he tells us he’ll release a second ad any day now.
