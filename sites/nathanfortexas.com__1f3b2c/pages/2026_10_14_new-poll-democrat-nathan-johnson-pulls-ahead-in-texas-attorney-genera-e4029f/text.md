Skip to content Facebook X-twitter Instagram Menu Contribute Press Release New Poll: Democrat Nathan Johnson Pulls Ahead In Texas Attorney General Race October 14, 2026 Johnson takes 48% to his opponent’s 47% among likely voters in nonpartisan Texas Politics Project poll (Dallas, Texas) — Nathan Johnson has taken the lead in the race for Texas Attorney General, according to the new University of Texas / Texas Politics Project survey, which puts him at 48% to his Republican opponent’s 47% among likely voters.
In August, the same pollsters had Johnson down five with one in five voters still undecided.
Those voters have now made up their minds, and they broke toward Johnson by better than two to one.
52% of voters say Texas has gone off on the wrong track, and corruption, a key issue for Johnson, is ranked as the second most important problem facing the state.
“People are tired of the Attorney General‘s office being run as a political show.
They want someone serious.
Middleton pledges to continue the show, with the same tired tricks, the same tired rhetoric.
I pledge to restore the office to its constitutional purposes,” said Nathan Johnson, Democratic candidate for Attorney General.
Johnson’s anti-corruption plan, That’s My Lawyer, would increase scrutiny of state contracting, employ conflict-of-interest review across agencies, and restore the office’s focus on consumer protection.
