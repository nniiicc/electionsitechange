The Democratic candidate for Texas Attorney General launched a seven-figure television ad buy on Monday amid signs of the party’s momentum in the red Lone Star State.
Nathan Johnson, a Democratic state senator, argues in the ad that “Texas could use a good lawyer” to replace current Attorney General Ken Paxton (R), the scandal-clad prosecutor who’s running in the state’s high-profile Senate race.
The 30-second spot focuses on a May interview with KHOU in which candidate Mayes Middleton, a Republican state senator and oil and gas executive, said he did not think the office had become too political.
“After 12 years of Ken Paxton in office, we need an Attorney General who will put the rule of law over politics and the public good over a personal agenda,” Johnson says in the ad, which is running statewide through Election Day, according to a release from the Democrat’s campaign.
Polling has been sparse in the race, but the latest numbers indicate the race for the typically red seat could be closer than expected.
