Skip to content Facebook X-twitter Instagram Menu Contribute CNBC Democratic Texas AG candidate vows to probe Deloitte’s role in voter registration delay October 2, 2026 Nathan Johnson, a Texas state senator and the Democratic candidate for attorney general, announced that, if elected, he plans to investigate Deloitte and other state vendor contracts in the wake of a software error that resulted in a failure to process voter registrations for what he and a Republican state lawmaker say were at least 178,000 Texans.
Johnson, in an exclusive interview with CNBC ahead of the Friday announcement, blamed Texas Republicans for pretending to shrink the state government and outsourcing essential duties to contractors.
Deloitte allegedly installed a software update on the Department of Public Safety’s servers in 2025 that led to the error, which was only caught in September with a little over a month to go before the 2026 midterm election .
If he becomes attorney general, Johnson said he wants to review the process for outsourcing state functions, as well as the individual contracts themselves.
