DALLAS — Nathan Johnson, the Democratic nominee for Texas Attorney General, is going on TV today with a major ad buy, two weeks before early voting begins.
“Twelve years of Ken Paxton turned the Attorney General’s office into a political operation, a private law firm for the far right,” said Johnson.
“Take the politics out and the office starts working again.
No political favors, no special treatment, no pay to play.
Just a lawyer doing the job for 31-million clients.”
