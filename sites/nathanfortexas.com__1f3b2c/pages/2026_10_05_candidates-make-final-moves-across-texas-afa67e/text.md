Nathan Johnson is the latest candidate to unveil a political ad in the closing weeks of an election cycle going down to the wire in Texas
With less than a month until Election Day, candidates across Texas are taking their last steps until they meet the lone star electorate.
Many recent polls are showing tight races for statewide office, with some even favoring Democrats.
On Monday, October 5 (which is also the last day to register to vote in Texas), Attorney General candidate Nathan Johnson released a general election ad: a seven-figure buy that will run statewide until Election Day.
Johnson’s ad contrasts his approach to the office from his Republican opponent Mayes Middleton, who has said the 2020 election was stolen and is supported by Ken Paxton.
“Take the politics out and the office starts working again,” says Johnson in his ad.
“No political favors, no special treatment, no pay to play, just a lawyer doing the job for 31 million Texans.”
