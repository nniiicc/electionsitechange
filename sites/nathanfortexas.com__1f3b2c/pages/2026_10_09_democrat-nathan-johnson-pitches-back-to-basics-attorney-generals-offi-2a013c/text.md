Eight years later, Johnson is trying again, this time on a statewide stage, as he vies to be Texas’ next attorney general.
It’s an uphill battle: The last time a Democrat was elected as Texas’ top lawyer, Forrest Gump was in movie theaters.
In the years since, national conservatives have come to rely on the state agency as a launchpad for partisan legal battles, and Johnson’s opponent, fellow state Sen.
