We don’t.
I’m running to be Texas’s next Attorney General in order to change that.
Why I'm Running Get Involved A Champion for Texas Values Senator Nathan Johnson is a Texas State Senator and veteran litigator and mediator.
He’s written roughly 433 bills and passed 134 since 2019 spanning water, energy, education, healthcare, criminal justice, and more.
He enters the Attorney General’s race with deep policy and legal expertise and a reputation for defending constitutional principles, demanding government transparency and fairness in the law, and tackling hard problems regardless of who stands in the way.
