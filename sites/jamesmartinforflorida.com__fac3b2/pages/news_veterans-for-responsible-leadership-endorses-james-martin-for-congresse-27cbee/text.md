PRESS RELEASE
Veterans for Responsible Leadership Endorses James Martin for Congress
HOBE SOUND — Today, James Martin’s campaign announced that Veterans for Responsible Leadership (VFRL) has officially endorsed his campaign for Florida’s 21st Congressional District.
VFRL is a nonpartisan 527 organization committed to supporting principled candidates across the nation who bring integrity, leadership, and real-world expertise to elected office.
“Veterans for Responsible Leadership is proud to endorse James Martin for Congress in Florida’s 21st District.
James’s exemplary service in the U.S.
Coast Guard brings the tested leadership and expertise our country desperately needs.
He represents a new generation ready to deliver real results for the American people and our national security.
James is a proven leader who gets things done,” said VFRL Executive Director Scott Peoples.
As a proud U.S.
Coast Guard veteran, I’m honored to have the support of Veterans for Responsible Leadership,” said James Martin.
Veterans know what it means to serve something bigger than ourselves and to get the mission done.
That’s exactly what Florida’s 21st District needs—a representative who will deliver real results instead of empty rhetoric.
I’m ready to bring that commitment to the halls of Congress.”
VFRL’s endorsement follows recent backing from Former Mayor Eric Jablin and Former Mayor David Levy, two former City of Palm Beach Gardens leaders whose support underscores Martin’s growing momentum among community leaders across the district.
