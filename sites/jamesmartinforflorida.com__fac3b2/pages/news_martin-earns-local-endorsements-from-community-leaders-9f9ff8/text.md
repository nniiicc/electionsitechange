PRESS RELEASE
Martin Earns Local Endorsements from Community Leaders
HOBE SOUND, FL — James Martin, Democratic candidate for Florida’s 21st Congressional District, today announced the endorsement of two respected local leaders: Former Mayor Eric Jablin and Former Mayor David Levy.
Their support reflects growing momentum behind Martin’s bid to unseat Brian Mast and bring new leadership to the Treasure Coast and Palm Beach County communities that make up FL-21.
Together, these former City of Palm Beach Gardens mayors represent a community integral to Florida’s 21st congressional district, underscoring Martin’s rising support among officials who have served closest to the day-to-day needs of residents.
“I’m honored to earn the support of these respected former mayors,” said James Martin.
“We are building a grassroots movement powered by the people who make this community so special.
As the great nephew of former Palm Beach Gardens Mayor Bob Diamond, these endorsements carry deep personal meaning for me and my family.
With these leaders joining our coalition, I know we can deliver a better future for every family in Florida’s 21st Congressional District.”
Joint Statement:
“James Martin understands what Floridians are facing as a fourth-generation Floridian, a father raising his child here, and a Coast Guard veteran who has served this country.
James will fight to lower the cost of living, fix the Treasure Coast’s clean water crisis, and strengthen the economy for everyone—and he has the policymaking experience at the Federal level to deliver.
We trust James to be a strong partner and advocate, and we look forward to working with him to tackle some of Florida’s most pressing issues.”
