Brian Mast Oct 2 2025 PRESS RELEASE Coast Guard Veteran James Martin Raises Over $150K in First 24 hours Hobe Sound, FL – James Martin, a fourth generation Floridian, U.S.
Coast Guard veteran, and national security expert, just entered the race for Florida’s 21st Congressional District, where he hopes to unseat five-term Republican incumbent Brian Mast.
He’s making quick headway; Martin raised more than $150,000 within the first 24 hours of running, his campaign said “This incredible early support shows that Floridians are ready for new leadership grounded in service, accountability, and results,” said Martin.
“From day one, our campaign has been about putting people first.
We’re building a movement that unites folks from all walks of life–regardless of political party–to fight for lower costs, cleaner water, and a strong future for all Floridians.” Read the article here About James Martin The son of a small business owner and public school teacher, James Martin is rooted in working class values that define the Treasure Coast.
A former lieutenant commander, Martin spent nearly a decade on active-duty in the U.S.
Coast Guard, commanding two cutters in Fort Pierce and leading successful missions against drug trafficking, human smuggling, and transnational criminal networks.
He also served as the second in command of a Coast Guard cutter deployed to Bahrain.
Over his career his Coast Guard teams seized over 200 tons of narcotics worth $3.5 billion, bringing hundreds of traffickers to justice.
Most recently, Martin served as Chief of Staff for National Security Affairs and Executive Secretary of the Office of the Vice President, where he worked across two administrations, leading teams of national security professionals from across the U.S. government and providing high-level counsel on defense and homeland security issues.
James holds a commission as a lieutenant commander in the U.S.
Coast Guard Reserve and is a proud life member of the Society of the Cincinnati, the nation’s oldest patriotic organization composed of direct descendants of Continental Army and Navy officers who served in the American War for Independence.
James carries forward a deep legacy of defending freedom and putting country before self.
Now he’s continuing his commitment to public service in Congress, where he will work on behalf of all Florida families with the same character and dedication he brought to his military career.
