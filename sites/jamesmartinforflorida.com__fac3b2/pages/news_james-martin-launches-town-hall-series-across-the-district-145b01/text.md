Brian Mast Sep 30 2026 PRESS RELEASE James Martin Launches Town Hall Series Across the District HOBE SOUND, FL – Today, James Martin is launching a town hall tour of Florida's 21st Congressional District to reach voters across Palm Beach County, Martin County, and St.
Lucie County.
Martin, the Democratic nominee, said, "MAGA incumbent Brian Mast has held this seat for nearly a decade and the number one complaint I hear from voters on the campaign trail is that Rep.
Mast does not hold town halls often enough." "I think it's important to show voters that I want to hear from them and that I will hold quarterly town halls if elected.
Voters deserve to have their voice heard and my campaign is traveling the district to meet people where they are." Martin added, "Another thing I hear frequently on the trail is that voters are clamoring for a debate between me and Brian Mast.
I challenged him to three debates, but Rep.
Mast has not agreed to it—despite the Congress being out of session until mid-November.
These town halls will give voters a clear split screen between a one party rubber stamp and real representation."
