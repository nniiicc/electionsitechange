How to vote
- Register to vote through the Alabama Secretary of State
- Check your registration and find your polling place
- Alabama requires a photo ID at the polls.
A driver's license, passport, state ID, military or tribal ID, or an Alabama college or government employer ID all work.
If you don't have any of those, Alabama will issue you a free photo voter ID.
Your county Board of Registrars can make you one.
- Register by Monday, October 19, 2026.
That is the deadline for the November 3 general election.
- Voting absentee: Alabama requires a reason to vote absentee, and the application lists the qualifying reasons.
The surest route is in person.
Take your application to the Madison County Absentee Election Manager by Thursday, October 29, 2026.
By mail, it has to arrive by Tuesday, October 27.
Before you return the ballot, the affidavit needs a notary or two witnesses to sign it, or it can't be counted.
Hand-deliver the ballot by 5 p.m.
Monday, November 2, or mail it so it arrives by noon on Election Day.
