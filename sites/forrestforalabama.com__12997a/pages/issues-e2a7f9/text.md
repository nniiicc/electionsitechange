How these connect
The healthcare consolidation that drives premiums up is the same dynamic that compresses non-healthcare wages.
The wage compression that makes housing less affordable is the same that pushes child care out of reach.
The infrastructure deficit that lengthens commutes is the same that reduces the geographic pool from which schools and hospitals can hire.
And the accountability gap is what allows each of these to drift, quarter after quarter, with no public record of the drift.
Fix one and the others get easier.
