Forrest is a UAB-trained engineer and serial entrepreneur who founded his first startup at age 19.
From 3D-printed medical devices to custom-fit respirator masks for frontline healthcare workers during COVID-19, he has spent his career solving real problems for real people.
He has worked in Huntsville since 2022, and today he builds manufacturing and data systems for a Huntsville defense and engineering company.

Our Values
Forrest Satterfield is guided by six core values that shape his decisions as a candidate and will continue to do so as a representative.
These values have also been the foundation of every company he has built.
You deserve transparency about how your tax dollars are spent.
Forrest is committed to creating public dashboards and open records, updated quarterly in plain language, so our community can easily audit government activities.
Forrest is dedicated to spending time not only in Montgomery but working within the district: hosting town halls, conducting listening sessions, and drafting bills before they are filed.
The same drive that led to founding a medical-device company at nineteen is applied here: understanding problems from within, not from an outsider's perspective.
This approach shapes our campaign's focus on healthcare, housing, infrastructure, education, and child care.
Our community runs on working families, rural neighbors, and people the system stopped noticing a long time ago.
Forrest is building a campaign that listens to them first, not last.
This campaign is funded by neighbors, not corporate PACs.
