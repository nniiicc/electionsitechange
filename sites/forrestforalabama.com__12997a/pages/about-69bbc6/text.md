Forrest is a UAB-trained engineer and serial entrepreneur who founded his first startup at age 19.
From 3D-printed medical devices to custom-fit respirator masks for frontline healthcare workers during COVID-19, he has spent his career solving real problems for real people.
He has worked in Huntsville since 2022, and today he builds manufacturing and data systems for a Huntsville defense and engineering company.

Throughout my career, I've focused on creating meaningful impact, addressing issues others tend to overlook, and being present during critical moments.
In the business world, leaders provide stakeholders with quarterly reports, detailing achievements, ongoing projects, and investment allocations.
I plan to apply the same rigorous approach in Montgomery by delivering quarterly public updates, ensuring District 21 always knows precisely what their representative is doing for them.
Founded Satterfield Technologies at Age 19 Described by UAB's Inquiro as the first startup company of the Collat School of Business Innovation Lab.
Forrest founded it in 2014 as a UAB sophomore, building a team and developing affordable, 3D-printed medical devices, setting the foundation for a career of entrepreneurial problem-solving.
UAB MakerSpace Co-Founder As a University Innovation Fellow, a program run by Stanford's Hasso Plattner Institute of Design, Forrest was one of four students who launched the UAB MakerSpace and helped grow it into a team of eighteen.
The campus prototyping space opened in Sterne Library in February 2016.
He didn't wait for the institution to build it.
Blazer Innovation Challenge Winner Won first place and $5,000 in UAB's inaugural Blazer Innovation Challenge for a 3D-printing manufacturing system for prosthetic and orthotic braces, making life-changing technology accessible to people who couldn't otherwise afford it.
Answered the PPE Shortage Through Obex Health, Forrest pivoted his 3D-printing infrastructure into custom-fit reusable masks built around replaceable N95 filters for frontline healthcare workers when national supply chains failed.
Obex sold hundreds of them, most going to healthcare providers nationwide.
Selected as one of four Reignite Alabama winners, a $37,500 award for COVID-response innovation.
He didn't wait for someone else to solve it.
Founded Cicada Forrest co-founded Cicada (cicada.now) to build civic infrastructure for the 21st century, tools that help citizens read state legislation (cicada.guide), surface community priorities (cicada.buzz), and run for office without wealthy donors (cicada.run).
