Who we are This Privacy Policy describes how Forrest Via Satterfield, candidate for Alabama House District 21 (referred to in this policy as "the Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you visit forrestforalabama.com (the "Site"), interact with our communications, contribute to the Campaign, sign up to volunteer, or otherwise engage with us.
The Campaign is a political committee registered with the Alabama Secretary of State under the Alabama Fair Campaign Practices Act.
Our registered address is 740 SW Constellation Pl Dr, Apt.
202, Huntsville, Alabama 35801.
If you have questions about this policy or how we handle your information, contact us at candidate@forrestforalabama.com or by mail at the address above.
2.
Information we collect We collect information in three ways: information you give us directly, information collected automatically when you use the Site, and information we receive from third parties.
Information you give us directly includes: Name, postal address, email address, phone number, and employer or occupation when you contribute, sign up for updates, RSVP for events, or volunteer.
Payment information when you contribute.
Payment card numbers are processed by our payment processor and are not stored on our systems.
Any message, comment, or other content you submit through forms on the Site or in reply to our communications.
Demographic and contact information you choose to share, such as your ZIP code or precinct.
Information collected automatically when you visit the Site includes: Information from third parties may include: Contribution and registration data from our payment processor.
Voter file data licensed from public records and political data vendors, which may include name, address, party registration, and voter history.
Information from social media platforms when you interact with our pages or content.
3.
Why we collect it We use your information to: Process contributions and comply with campaign finance law.
Send you campaign updates, fundraising appeals, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities through email, text, and direct mail.
Reach voters with information about the Campaign and the candidate's positions.
Recruit and coordinate volunteers.
Maintain and improve the Site.
Respond to your inquiries.
Comply with legal obligations and protect the rights and safety of the Campaign and others.
4.
Reporting to government agencies Federal and Alabama state law require political campaigns to disclose certain information about contributors.
When you contribute to the Campaign, your name, address, employer, occupation, contribution amount, and contribution date may be reported to the Alabama Secretary of State and made part of the public record under the Alabama Fair Campaign Practices Act.
Reporting thresholds and requirements are set by law and may change.
We have no discretion over what information must be reported.
By contributing, you acknowledge that this information will become a matter of public record.
5.
How we share information We do not sell, rent, or exchange your personal information with other political committees, campaigns, or organizations.
We share information only in the following circumstances: Service providers.
We share information with companies that perform services for the Campaign, such as payment processors, email and text-message platforms, hosting providers, mail vendors, and consultants.
These providers are bound by contract to use the information only for the services they provide to us.
Government agencies.
As described in Section 4, contribution information is reported to government agencies as required by campaign finance law.
Legal requirements.
We may disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or court order, or when we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of the Campaign, our supporters, or others.
Business transfers.
If the Campaign winds down or transfers assets to a successor committee, your information may be transferred along with those assets.
6.
Email communications If you give us your email address, we may send you campaign updates, fundraising appeals, event invitations, and volunteer requests.
Every email we send includes a one-click unsubscribe link.
You can also unsubscribe by replying to any email with the word "UNSUBSCRIBE" or by contacting us at candidate@forrestforalabama.com .
Federal law (the CAN-SPAM Act) provides certain exemptions for political messages, but we honor unsubscribe requests regardless.
7.
Text message (SMS) communications If you provide your phone number through our website, a paper sign-up, an event registration, or a contribution form, you consent to receive text messages from the Campaign.
Messages may include campaign updates, fundraising appeals, event reminders, volunteer requests, and get-out-the-vote messages.
Message frequency varies.
You may receive several messages per week, particularly close to election dates and fundraising deadlines.
Message and data rates may apply.
The Campaign does not charge for these messages, but your wireless carrier may charge standard messaging rates depending on your plan.
Opt out.
You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message we send.
After you reply STOP, we will send one confirmation message and you will not receive further messages unless you opt back in.
Help.
Reply HELP to any of our text messages for assistance, or contact us at candidate@forrestforalabama.com .
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
We may use third-party analytics services to understand how visitors use the Site and to improve our communications.
These services may collect information about your visit and combine it with information from other sources.
The Site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals.
9.
Children The Site is not directed to children under the age of 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it.
If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us at candidate@forrestforalabama.com .
10.
Your choices and rights Regardless of where you live, you may: Unsubscribe from email at any time using the link in our messages.
Opt out of text messages by replying STOP.
Request that we update or correct the information we hold about you.
Request that we delete the information we hold about you, subject to legal retention requirements (we cannot delete records that campaign finance law requires us to maintain).
To make any of these requests, contact us at candidate@forrestforalabama.com or by mail at the address in Section 1.
If you live in a state with a data-rights law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, or others), you may have additional rights, including the right to access the categories and specific pieces of personal information we hold about you, the right to know whether we have shared your information, and the right not to face discrimination for exercising these rights.
To exercise any of these rights, contact us as described above.
11.
Data security We use commercially reasonable physical, technical, and administrative safeguards to protect the information we collect.
No system can be guaranteed against breach.
If you have reason to believe your information has been compromised, contact us immediately.
12.
Third-party links The Site may contain links to other websites, including ActBlue, social media platforms, news outlets, and partner organizations.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.
Read their privacy policies before providing information.
13.
Changes to this policy We may update this policy from time to time.
When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
Material changes will be communicated through the Site or by email to subscribers.
