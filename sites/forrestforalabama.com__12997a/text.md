A working plan for North Alabama.
From breaking the healthcare monopoly to protecting homes from Wall Street, fixing US 231, fully funding public schools, and making state government open its books.
Forrest has a plan for every family in our district.
Bring competition back to North Alabama healthcare.
One system now controls every general hospital in and around Huntsville.
Forrest supports reviewing the antitrust immunity that enabled consolidation, reforming Certificate of Need rules, requiring public financial disclosure, and applying public ethics and competitive-bid rules to health care authorities.

Protect new homebuyers and keep homes in family hands.
Alabama law lacks requirements for builder warranties and liability insurance.
Forrest proposes a statutory warranty, mandatory insurance, and protections for family homes and seniors.

Fix US 231 and give communities local control.
Thousands commute daily on US 231, a state road often neglected.
Forrest aims to give local control back to communities, reducing commute times and empowering residents.

Compete for teachers and prepare students for North Alabama jobs.

Fix a child-care market that is failing parents and workers.
Forrest supports a refundable child-care credit, a wage floor for child-care workers, universal Pre-K, and simpler licensing that protects children without blocking responsible providers.
