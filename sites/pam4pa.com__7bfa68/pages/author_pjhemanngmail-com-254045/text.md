Meet Pam Hemann
Pam Hemann is a lifelong Pennsylvanian, a passionate advocate for rural communities, and a fierce defender of working families.
With deep roots in the 110th District, Pam understands the everyday challenges facing our region—from disappearing rural healthcare and school funding shortfalls to job insecurity and aging infrastructure.
Pam built her career in nonprofit leadership and community service, always putting people before politics.
She has worked to expand access to education, connect local businesses with resources, and fight for healthcare that meets the needs of seniors, veterans, and low-income families.
Now, she’s running for State Representative to give our rural communities a voice in Harrisburg.
Pam believes in government that listens, leads with compassion, and actually gets things done.
She’s not a career politician—she’s a neighbor, a problem-solver, and a woman ready to work for the people.
