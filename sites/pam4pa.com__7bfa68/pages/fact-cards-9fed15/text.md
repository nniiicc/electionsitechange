Fact Card 01
Minimum Wage
Pennsylvania’s minimum wage is still $7.25 an hour.
Tina Pickett voted no
- Rep.
Tina Pickett voted NO on HB 1549, which passed the Pennsylvania House to raise the minimum wage.
- That vote supported keeping Pennsylvania’s minimum wage at $7.25.
- Working families deserve better.
Where I stand
- People who work full time should not live in poverty.
- Hard work deserves a fair wage.
- I support raising Pennsylvania’s minimum wage with the cost of living.
- I’ll fight for working families.
