My Top Priorities
It's no secret that we have an affordability crisis in America.
Housing, food, education, healthcare, you name it.
As a young man who grew up in Middle Tennessee, I witnessed myself how a lack of courage from government leads to worse conditions for those who have to work for a living.
After rising grocery prices, hospital shutdowns, the reckless development of our rural communities, and a shrinking wage, I decided enough was enough.
When you give me your support, you aren't supporting a career politician or a millionaire developer who has no idea what it's like to live and work in Tennessee, you're supporting someone who wants everyone to get a helping hand when they need it.
Talk to me about what you care about.
