Policy Internship
Responsibilities
Policy interns will be responsible for researching, reviewing, and discussing broad policies and specific policy points.
They will work directly with campaign staff to help shape the broader vision of the campaign.
Qualifications
Applicants will demonstrate experience in or knowledge of policy research, creating policy briefs, and creating infographics.
Applicants must also be able to use word processors, email, and presentation software effectively.
Email questions to Campaign@OwenFarnsworth.com
