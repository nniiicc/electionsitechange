Meet Steve Get Involved Meet Steve Biography Steve Dodson is a Roswell business owner, community leader, and advocate for working families who believes New Mexico needs more real-world experience in the Legislature.
For nearly two decades, Steve has served as Principal of Dodson Wholesale Lumber Co., where he manages every aspect of the business, from payroll and hiring to regulatory compliance and capital investments.
Like many small business owners in New Mexico, Steve has seen firsthand how burdensome regulations and a complicated tax system make it harder for businesses to grow and create jobs.
Before returning to Roswell to lead his family’s company, Steve built a career in enterprise technology.
As a Senior Consultant with Thermo Fisher Scientific, he implemented laboratory technology systems and worked as a programmer and database administrator for Fortune 500 companies across North America.
Earlier in his career, he served as a classified web developer supporting missile defense programs at Schriever Air Force Base, holding a U.S.
Department of Defense Secret clearance.
Steve earned his degree in Business and Management Information Systems from Oklahoma State University, combining business knowledge with technical expertise that continues to guide his practical approach to solving problems.
Steve has long been active in the Roswell community.
He has served on the boards of the Roswell-Chaves County Economic Development Corporation, the Rio Grande Foundation, the Roswell Homeless Coalition, and the New Mexico Shooting Sports Association, and is a graduate of Leadership Roswell and Leadership New Mexico.
Steve is married to his wife, Callie.
They were married in 2006, and together, they are raising four children.
Steve and Callie served as licensed foster parents for several years and adopted a daughter.
Their experience working with foster children gave them a firsthand understanding of both the dedication of many CYFD workers and the serious reforms needed to better protect vulnerable children.
A strong supporter of youth development, Steve coached Little League and USSSA baseball for seven years and served as President of the Noon Optimist Little League Board of Directors.
He is also a certified 4-H shotgun and rifle instructor and remains active in youth shooting sports.
Steve believes New Mexico deserves leadership grounded in real-world experience—leaders who understand how businesses operate, how families struggle, and how government policies affect everyday life.
In the State House, he will work to strengthen New Mexico’s economy, protect constitutional freedoms, and build a better future for the next generation.

Defending the Second Amendment Steve Dodson is a strong and unapologetic supporter of the Second Amendment.
For Steve, the right to keep and bear arms is not theoretical—it is a normal part of life, family, and community in southeastern New Mexico.
He is a certified 4-H shotgun and rifle instructor who has spent years teaching young people firearm safety and responsible marksmanship.
His own children compete in scholastic shooting sports.
Steve believes firearm ownership should be normalized, not stigmatized.
In the Legislature, Steve will: Oppose any legislation that restricts the rights of law-abiding gun owners Protect the rights of hunters, sportsmen, and competitive shooters Promote firearm safety education and youth shooting sports Push back against attempts by politicians in Santa Fe to erode constitutional freedoms Fixing New Mexico’s Broken Tax System New Mexico’s tax system is one of the biggest obstacles to economic growth in our state.
The Gross Receipts Tax (GRT) pyramids taxes at multiple stages of production, raising prices for consumers and making it harder for small businesses to operate and grow.
It is one of the most damaging features of New Mexico’s tax structure.
As a small business owner responsible for payroll, taxes, and regulatory compliance, Steve Dodson understands exactly how these policies hurt working families and entrepreneurs.
Steve believes it is time for serious tax reform, including: Reforming or eliminating the Gross Receipts Tax and it’s many carve-outs Simplifying the tax code so businesses can grow and invest with confidence Reducing regulatory and tax burdens that discourage job creation Making New Mexico competitive with neighboring states Santa Fe needs more legislators who have actually run a business and understand how policies affect job creators.
Supporting New Mexico’s Energy Industry Oil and gas production is the backbone of New Mexico’s economy and provides thousands of high-paying jobs across the state.
Unfortunately, politicians in Santa Fe too often treat the energy industry as a problem rather than the economic engine that funds our schools, roads, and public safety.
Steve Dodson believes New Mexico should be leading the nation in responsible energy production, not driving the industry away.
In the Legislature, Steve will: Defend oil and gas jobs from harmful regulations Support responsible energy development that strengthens local economies Push back against policies that threaten New Mexico’s energy independence Ensure that energy-producing communities receive fair representation in Santa Fe When energy succeeds, New Mexico succeeds.
Making New Mexico a Place Where Businesses Can Thrive Too many businesses avoid New Mexico because of excessive regulation, slow permitting, and unpredictable government mandates.
Steve Dodson has spent years working to strengthen the business climate through his service with the Roswell-Chaves County Economic Development Corporation.
He understands what it takes to attract investment and grow local economies.
In the Legislature, Steve will work to: Cut unnecessary regulations that burden small businesses Streamline permitting and licensing processes Encourage entrepreneurship and new investment Expand opportunities for rural communities New Mexico should be a place where businesses want to invest, grow, and create jobs.
Restoring Accountability in State Government New Mexicans deserve a government that works efficiently, responsibly, and transparently.
Too often, decisions made in Santa Fe create unintended consequences for families, businesses, and local communities.
Steve Dodson believes government should focus on its core responsibilities, operate with accountability, and respect the people it serves.
He will work to: Demand transparency and accountability from state agencies Ensure taxpayer dollars are used responsibly Bring practical, real-world experience to legislative decision-making New Mexico needs fewer political insiders and more citizen legislators who understand how policies affect everyday life.
