State Funds Secured by Delegation are Officially Approved for Carroll County On Wednesday, October 7 th , the Maryland Board of Public Works considered and approved $1,905,000 to replace building systems and a building envelope on the main campus of Carroll Community College.
These systems include heating and cooling equipment, the fire alarm system, and the building envelope of Building A.
These replacements will address deferred maintenance issues, improve student comfort, and reduce operating costs.
Ultimately, these repairs will result in increased life-safety protection, additional expansion capability, uniform communication and alarms, improved maintenance and troubleshooting, and more reliable systems.
The Carroll County Delegation, working alongside Carroll Community College, were responsible for securing this key funding through the Capital Budget process during the 2026 Legislative Session.
National Prescription Drug Take Back Day – October 24 th National Prescription Drug Take Back Day is on October 24, providing Maryland residents with an opportunity to safely dispose of unwanted and expired medications.
More information about this day can be found by clicking here.
For residents who need a disposal option before or after the event, MyOldMeds.com offers free, year-round resources for safe medication disposal.
Through the MyOldMeds locator , residents can find 425 secure medication disposal sites across Maryland.
Many of these sites are located in places people already visit regularly, such as pharmacies, grocery stores, and medical clinics.
MyOldMeds.com also has a Maryland resource page that provides information on safely managing medications at home and properly preparing them for disposal.
