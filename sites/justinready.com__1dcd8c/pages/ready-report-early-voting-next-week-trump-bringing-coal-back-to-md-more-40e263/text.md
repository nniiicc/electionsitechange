Trump Administration Restoring Reliable Energy for Maryland Earlier this week, we received great news that, under direction of President Trump, the federal government is directing $85 million in Federal Defense Production Act funding to restart the AES Warrior Run coal-fired power plant in Cumberland, MD.
This is a meaningful step toward addressing Maryland’s energy supply crisis.
The Senate Republican Caucus released the following press release with more details: Carroll County Farm Museum Free for Military Personnel and their Family Carroll County Government has announced that the Carroll County Farm Museum will join museums nationwide in the 2026 Blue Star Museums initiative, a program that provides free admission to currently serving U.S. military personnel, including National Guard and Reserve, and their families this summer.
The 2026 program will begin on Armed Forces Day, Saturday, May 16, 2026, and end on Labor Day, Monday, September 7, 2026.
Learn more and find the list of participating museums at arts.gov/BlueStarMuseums .
