Early Voting Just Three Weeks Away & Upcoming Election Deadlines Early voting will begin on Thursday, October 22nd, and continue through Thursday, October 29th.
Voting hours are from 7 am to 8 pm (just like election day), including Saturday and Sunday.
Typically, Saturday and Sunday are the least busy days to vote.
Carroll County has 3 early voting centers located at: Westminster Senior Center – 125 Stoner Ave, Westminster South Carroll Senior Center – 5928 Mineral Hill Rd, Sykesville Charles Carroll Community Center – 3719 Littlestown Pike, Westminster October 13 th is the advanced deadline to register to vote, as well as the deadline for a registered voter to request a voter registration list.
New requests will not be taken until registration reopens.
Election day is Tuesday, November 3rd.
Voting hours are from 7 am to 8 pm (just like early voting).
Voters who want to vote on election day should go to their assigned polling place.
Voters can find their polling place by clicking here.
National Prescription Drug Take Back Day – October 24 th National Prescription Drug Take Back Day is on October 24, providing Maryland residents with an opportunity to safely dispose of unwanted and expired medications.
More information about this day can be found by clicking here.
For residents who need a disposal option before or after the event, MyOldMeds.com offers free, year-round resources for safe medication disposal.
Through the MyOldMeds locator , residents can find 425 secure medication disposal sites across Maryland.
Many of these sites are located in places people already visit regularly, such as pharmacies, grocery stores, and medical clinics.
MyOldMeds.com also has a Maryland resource page that provides information on safely managing medications at home and properly preparing them for disposal.
