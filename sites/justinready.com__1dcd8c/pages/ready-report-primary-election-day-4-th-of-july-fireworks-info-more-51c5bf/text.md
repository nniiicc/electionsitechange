Finally, a number of people have asked me about who I support for Governor.
I decided to vote for Ed Hale .
Let me tell you why.
His background as a successful businessman who is running on a very strong platform of lowering taxes, getting our energy production back up and prices down, cutting waste and fraud in state government and securing our elections are good.
There’s other candidates running with good platforms that are good people – but the fact is, we have to have someone who can prosecute the case against Wes Moore’s failures and put together the kind of campaign that it takes.
I believe he gives us the best chance to give Wes Moore a real race in the general election.
Maryland cannot afford more of the same.
We need a Governor focused on Maryland families, working-class taxpayers, small businesses, public safety, and the long-term strength of our economy.
If Ed Hale wins the primary, we have a real opportunity to compete in the Fall General Election and get Maryland turned around.

Traffic Alert – 4 th of July Fireworks at Carroll County Farm Museum The Carroll County Farm Museum will host the July 4 th Celebration and fireworks on Saturday, July 4 th, with a rain date of Sunday, July 5 th .
For more information about attending this great family event, click here .
In order to facilitate safe and orderly traffic flow to and from the event, the following roadways will be closed to all traffic beginning at 8:30 pm and will remain closed until all the traffic has cleared at the conclusion of the fireworks: Center Street – From MD 140 to MD 32 Gist Road – From MD 32 to Kate Wagner Road Kate Wagner Road – From MD 32 to MD 27 Hook Road – From MD 32 to the High School Entrance Smith Avenue – From MD 32 to Gist Road All traffic exiting the Farm Museum grounds will use Center Street from the Farm Museum to Maryland 140.
All traffic exiting the Agricultural Center will use Gist Road to Kate Wagner Road to MD 27 or MD 32/MD 97.
Access to the Carroll Hospital Emergency Room shall be made from the intersection of Gist Road and Maryland 32.
Law Enforcement will be at this intersection to assist people needing access to the emergency room.
Carroll County Farm Museum Free for Military Personnel and their Family Carroll County Government has announced that the Carroll County Farm Museum will join museums nationwide in the 2026 Blue Star Museums initiative, a program that provides free admission to currently serving U.S. military personnel, including National Guard and Reserve, and their families this summer.
The 2026 program will begin on Armed Forces Day, Saturday, May 16, 2026, and end on Labor Day, Monday, September 7, 2026.
Learn more and find the list of participating museums at arts.gov/BlueStarMuseums .
