Open House for MD 97 – April 17 The Maryland Department of Transportation State Highway Administration is planning potential improvements along MD 97 (Littlestown Pike) between MD 140 and MD 496 (Bachmans Valley Road).
There will be an open house on Wednesday, April 17 th , from 5 – 7 pm , in the Winters Mill High School cafeteria .
Citizens are encouraged to attend to view preliminary concepts for the MD 97 corridor and participate in a new survey that will also be posted online the day of the public open house.
There will also be an opportunity to give feedback and receive answers to questions from study team members.
