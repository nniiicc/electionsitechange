They are joined over 740 other state legislators from around the country including several from Maryland.
The SAVE Act, introduced by Texas Representative Chip Roy, amends the National Voter Registration Act (NVRA), to require states to obtain documentary proof of U.S. citizenship and identity – in person – when registering an individual to vote in a federal election.
The SAVE Act also directs states to implement an alternative process for individuals without proper documentary proof but are U.S. citizens.
Finally, the act empowers citizens to bring civil suits forward against election officials who fail to obtain documentary proof of U.S. citizenship.
This is basic common sense, only citizens should be voting in our elections.
