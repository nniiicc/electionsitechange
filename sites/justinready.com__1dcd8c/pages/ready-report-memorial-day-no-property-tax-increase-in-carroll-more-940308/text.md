Vehicle Registration Fees to Increase in 2025 One of the biggest fights in Annapolis this year was over the Budget.
Republicans fought hard to keep any tax or fee increases from passing, however against my strong opposition , Democrats forced through a number of different vehicle registration fee increases, including ones based on weight of vehicles as well as raising the overall fee.
These funds DO go largely toward the Transportation Trust Fund which has been facing shortfalls but that is largely because we are saddling primarily drivers with most of the cost while heavily subsidizing mass transit systems at over 90%.
I had legislation to try to change this ( SB 841 ) but it did not move.
I will continue to work for fairness in transportation funding and to eliminate the unjust automatic gas tax hikes we have every July 1 st .
However – I did want you to have the specific fee chart so you could be aware .
Earlier this week, the Motor Vehicle Administration announced the new rates for vehicle registration, which are based on vehicle class and weight.

Good News – Carroll Co.
Budget Approved – Proposed Property Tax Increase Eliminated!
Over the past few weeks the Carroll County Commissioners have been debating final passage of the county’s FY2025 Operating Budget.
The preliminary budget included a 2 cent property tax increase, which would have been the first tax hike in many years.
This was partly due to costs that the state government has forced down on counties that I have tried to stop or ease, including the Kirwan Education Blueprint mandate.
Thankfully the commissioners, with Commissioner Joe Vigliotti (District 1) leading the charge, worked out additional savings and maneuvers in the budget to be able to eliminate this property tax hike.
Also important – they restrained overall budget growth to just .4% so that is also good news.
You can read the county government’s press release on the FY2025 budget by clicking here .
