ICYMI: Good News – Carroll Co.
Budget Approved – Proposed Property Tax Increase Eliminated!
Over the past few weeks the Carroll County Commissioners have been debating final passage of the county’s FY2025 Operating Budget.
The preliminary budget included a 2 cent property tax increase, which would have been the first tax hike in many years.
This was partly due to costs that the state government has forced down on counties that I have tried to stop or ease, including the Kirwan Education Blueprint mandate.
Thankfully the commissioners, with Commissioner Joe Vigliotti (District 1) leading the charge, worked out additional savings and maneuvers in the budget to be able to eliminate this property tax hike.
Also important – they restrained overall budget growth to just .4% so that is also good news.
