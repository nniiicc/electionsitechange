Moore Climate Executive Order:
Last week, Governor Moore signed a far-reaching executive order involving emissions and climate – it requires government agencies to come out with a plan to regulate all energy in homes by 2035, which would involve getting rid of gas stoves, lays groundwork for miles traveled taxes and “clean tolls” on roads etc.
All this would have essentially zero impact on the overall climate in even our region much less the world but of course we know that’s not really the point.
