Explore the Platform Meet Tom Meet Tom Rooted in Community Tom Rockwell is a husband, father, stepfather, Quaker minister, and church leader living in Richmond.
At 34 years old, Tom describes himself as one of the youngest people running for office in Indiana in 2026.
Born in Michigan and raised in the Midwest, Tom says his experience in ministry has centred on supporting churches, helping communities navigate difficult change, and working alongside pastors and local leaders.
As a father to a three-year-old daughter and stepfather to three stepchildren, he says he is running with the future of the next generation in mind.
Why I'm Running A Different Approach to Government Tom's plaform identifies housing, schools, jobs, addiction, local control and the influence of wealthy interests as central concerns.
Tom says Richmond has been his home for six years and describes his political outlook as rooted in his experience of Midwestern communities.
Tom's stated objective is to build communities where working people can afford a home, schools and libraries are supported, wages are fair, young people can build a future, and government listens to local residents.

View priorities → Just Economy Education Public Safety Rural Investment Technology Healthcare Democracy A Just Economy Tom believes in an economy in which working people have access to fair wages, safe housing, affordable child care, good schools and health care.
Protect Working People Protect workers' rights to organise and bargain collectively.
Support fair wages, safe working conditions and reliable schedules.
Expand apprenticeship and job-training programmes.
Raise the minimum wage so full-time workers are not living in poverty.
Address wage theft and worker misclassification.
Support small and medium-sized local businesses.
Housing Build more high-quality affordable housing.
Repair and refurbish existing homes.
Hold slumlords accountable.
Protect renters from unsafe housing and abusive evictions.
Support first-time homebuyers.
Public Schools, College & Vocational Training Fully fund public schools.
Raise teacher pay and support school staff.
Expand vocational programmes and apprenticeships.
Expand community-college and job-training opportunities.
Provide additional financial support for higher education and vocational training.
Safe Communities & Public Safety Support law enforcement, firefighters, EMTs and first responders.
Improve emergency response times.
Invest in mental-health crisis response.
Expand addiction treatment and recovery support.
Support domestic-violence prevention and survivor services.
Invest in housing, jobs, schools, youth programmes and healthcare.
Support community-based violence prevention and youth mentoring.
Rural & Small-City Investment Tom believes Wayne and Henry Counties and other small cities and rural communities require investment.
Invest in downtown revitalisation and local businesses.
Support local manufacturers and family farms.
Expand rural healthcare and mental-health access.
Protect rural hospitals and emergency medical services.
Repair roads, bridges, sidewalks, water systems and broadband.
Support affordable housing and home-repair programmes.
Expand vocational education and apprenticeships.
Support farmers markets and local food systems.
Responsible AI & Technology Protect workers from job loss, surveillance and exploitation related to AI.
Require transparency when AI is used in major public systems.
Ensure productivity gains benefit workers and communities.
Invest in worker training for new technologies.
Healthcare as a Human Right Tom believes healthcare, mental-health treatment, addiction recovery, maternal health, elder care and integrative care are major priorities.
Universal Healthcare Expand healthcare coverage.
Expand access to rural and local healthcare providers.
Invest in preventive care.
Reduce administrative waste and healthcare costs.
Mental Health & Addiction Expand access to mental-health treatment.
Increase crisis-response and suicide-prevention resources.
Expand addiction treatment and recovery programmes.
Support recovery housing and peer support.
Maternal Health Expand prenatal, birth and postpartum care.
Support regulated midwifery and doula services.
Improve insurance coverage for maternal services.
Invest in community-based maternal healthcare.
Real Democracy Tom's platform focuses on campaign finance, fair representation, constituent access and local control.
Money in Politics Increase transparency around campaign donations and political spending.
Address corporate PAC and lobbyist influence.
Strengthen ethics requirements.
Fair Districts End partisan gerrymandering.
Create a politically neutral redistricting commission.
Require transparent maps and public hearings.
Government Accountability Regular public online town halls.
Public Zoom sessions during the legislative session.
Clear explanations for major votes.
More transparent constituent services.
Local Control Protect local control over schools, libraries and public safety.
Oppose state preemption that prevents local problem-solving.
Give local governments more flexibility.
Require the state to listen to local communities.
