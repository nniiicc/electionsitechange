Due to repairs to the Tilton Driver Services facility Commercial Driver License (CDL) testing lot, the CDL testing course will be temporarily closed for CDL pre-trip, skills and road-testing procedures.
The course is expected to reopen Tuesday, May 9, 2023.
No other facility services will be impacted by this project.
In the meantime, CDL customers who need to schedule pre-trip, skills and road tests may schedule an appointment at one of the facilities below by visiting www.ilsos.gov.
Rantoul Driver Services facility at 421 S Murray Rd in Rantoul (Class B only)
Charleston Driver Services facility at 1010 E Street in Charleston (Class A & B)
