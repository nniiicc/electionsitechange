Trump Gives Bergman Complete and Total Endorsement
Former President Donald J.
Trump endorsed Congressman Jack Bergman for U.S.
Congress.
President Trump stated, "Congressman Jack Bergman is a tireless advocate for the people of Michigan's 1st Congressional District.
A retired Lieutenant General in the U.S.
Marine Corps, Jack bravely served our Country in combat, and he is doing an outstanding job in Congress.
He is working hard to Secure the Border, Strengthen our Military, Care for our Veterans, Defend the Second Amendment, and Give a Voice to the Working Men and Women of Michigan.
Jack Bergman has my Complete and Total Endorsement!"
