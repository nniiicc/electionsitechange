Despite a surplus, Madison continues to reduce and eliminate healthcare protections for working families.
If elected, I will work to increase and protect access to affordable, high-quality healthcare, reproductive rights, and mental health services for Wisconsinites.
Education As an educator, I know how important Wisconsin's public schools are.
I saw firsthand how our legislature fails our kids and communities by consistently underfunding public schools As your representative, I will fight for increased funding for public schools and property tax relief.
I will work to establish policies that support our hard working teachers.
Get Things Done I believe we have more in common than divides us, and as your representative I will listen, work across differences, and bring our shared, common‑sense values to make Madison work better for all of us.
Wisconsin families deserve nothing less!
Lower Cost of Living We’re all experiencing rising prices in everyday living expenses like groceries, gas and electricity, housing, insurance, and childcare.
In the Assembly, I will work across the aisle to lower the cost of living so you can keep your hard-earned money in your pockets and all families can flourish.
Data Centers We must stop tax breaks for AI data centers and require them to pay for the water and electricity they use.
We need permanent protections and real guardrails that prohibit passing costs onto ratepayers and pollution of our air, water, and land.
I’m ready to roll up my sleeves and get to work to make sure our neighbors, pocketbooks and our environment are safe from any harms that may come from unregulated AI and make sure our communities have a seat at those tables.
Raise Wages Wages are not keeping up with the cost of living.
Americans are struggling with day-to-day expenses like rent and groceries.
I promise to work hard to bring costs down by holding corporations who game the system accountable.
We need to raise wages and lower costs.
Hard-working Americans should be able to live, work and play and be able to give their kids a better life.
