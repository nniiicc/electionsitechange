Family Stability, Maternal Health, and Childcare
Celeste understands how quickly families can fall into crisis.
Housing and food insecurity doesn’t happen in isolation — it affects everything.
When rent becomes impossible, when a family loses their home, when a mother can’t access care, when childcare costs more than a paycheck, stability slips away fast.
In the State Assembly, Celeste is continuing to fight for real solutions that keep families stable through programs like emergency rental assistance, eviction prevention, affordable housing development, and supportive services like CalFresh.
Families need more than survival – they deserve support to build a foundation.
In the State Assembly, Celeste will push to expand maternal health access so that no mother is left without care during pregnancy or postpartum, and no family is forced to navigate one of life’s most vulnerable moments alone.
She knows that childcare is not a luxury — it’s what allows parents to work, children to learn, and families to stay afloat.
That’s why she is committed to strengthening childcare systems, supporting providers, and expanding access to affordable, reliable care for working families across the Northeast Valley.
