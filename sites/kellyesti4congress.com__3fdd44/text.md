Policy Priorities
Military / Veteran Services
Georgia’s 8th District has a strong and well‑known military connection, especially with Robins Air Force Base in Warner Robins, one of the largest employers in the region.
Many families in Warner Robins, Macon, and Valdosta have deep ties to military service, whether through active duty, the reserves, civilian defense work, or long‑standing family traditions of serving our country.
As a Navy veteran, I understand the challenges service members face during and after their time in uniform.
Veterans in our district rely on federal programs like VA health care, disability benefits, education support, and housing assistance.
Access to these services can vary depending on where someone lives, especially in rural communities where medical facilities and transportation options may be limited.
Ensuring that veterans in every part of the district can reach the care and resources they earned is an important responsibility.
Supporting military families also means paying attention to the needs of spouses and children.
Frequent moves can affect employment, schooling, and access to consistent medical care.
Stable housing, reliable health services, and strong community networks make a meaningful difference for families who relocate often or transition back into civilian life.
As a member of Congress, part of my job would be to oversee the federal agencies that support veterans and military communities.
That includes monitoring how programs are delivered, identifying gaps in access, and making sure national policies reflect the needs of a district with a large and active military presence.
My goal is to ensure that service members, veterans, and their families receive the support they have earned through their service.
