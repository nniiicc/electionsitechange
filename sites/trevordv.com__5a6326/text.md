ABOUT TREVOR
Trevor De Vries is a lifelong St.
John resident, husband, father of four, and trusted advisor to businesses and labor leaders.
He’s not a career politician.
He’s a husband, dad, and neighbor who decided to step up because he cares deeply about the future of his hometown, Northwest Indiana, and the state he loves.
Guided by his core values of Faith, Family, and Freedom, he is committed to protecting parental rights, cutting government waste, and putting Hoosiers First.
Trevor isn’t a politician; he’s a servant-leader determined to give families a stronger, safer, and more prosperous future.
An Indiana First Conservative, Trevor is ready to represent the people in District 1 with conviction, courage, common sense and a servant’s heart.
