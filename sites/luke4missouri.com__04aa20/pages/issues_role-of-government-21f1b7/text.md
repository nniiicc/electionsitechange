We the people are the government.
A government is just a collection of people who have decided to work together.
We pay taxes into a collective account to fund programs to help us.
A good government’s duty is to the people who elected it and the main goal should be to facilitate prosperity for as many citizens as possible.
The United States is now an Oligarchy.
More and more money and power are being consolidated everyday in the hands of a few.
Money and power become easier to get with more money and power, so the problem is accelerating.
According to Merriam-Webster, an oligarchy is defined as government by the few, specifically a system where a small group exercises control, often for corrupt or selfish purposes.
There needs to be a balance between government and private business, neither can meet all the needs of Missouri alone.
Private business should provide as many services as possible with oversight of government to protect citizens against unfair business practices.
The government has a role to provide essential services when private business cannot adequately meet the needs of the community.
This includes services like the fire department, police department and public schools.
I believe healthcare is also an essential service and currently the private system is failing most of us.
