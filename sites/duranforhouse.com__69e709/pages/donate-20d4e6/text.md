Minnesota residents can contribute and get it refunded in just a few weeks!
Thanks to the Minnesota Political Contribution Refund (PCR) program, Minnesotans can have ONE $75/person or $150/married couple FULLY REFUNDED by the state annually.
The process is simple: you donate, I send you a receipt and a short form to fill out, you mail it in, and a couple of weeks later, you’ll get your refund from the state
1) Donate $75/person or $150/couple.
2)Duran will mail you a receipt and refund form.
You must send both of them to the Department of Revenue.
3) Receive your refund in a few short weeks from the MN Department of Revenue.
Maximum - $2,000 per couple
Maximum Political Contribution Refund per Calendar Year - $150 per couple
Maximum - $1,000 per person
Maximum Political Contribution Refund per Calendar Year - $75 per person
