He drove a forklift, loaded trucks, and learned firsthand the value of hard work, responsibility, and perseverance. ​ For more than fourteen years, Chris has dedicated his career to helping families navigate some of the most difficult moments of their lives.
As a parent defense attorney, he has worked alongside parents, children, case managers, and service providers to help families overcome challenges and move forward. ​ Chris believes the government should create opportunity, strengthen communities, and help families succeed. ​ His life story is proof that when opportunity exists, people can achieve extraordinary things.
Chris' Dream For All Georgians WHY CHRIS IS RUNNING Chris is running for Congress because he believes the government should work for working people.
Too many families are doing everything right and still struggling to get ahead.
They are working hard, raising families, paying bills, and contributing to their communities, yet they feel like the American Dream is becoming harder to reach.
For fourteen years, Chris has fought for families facing difficult circumstances.
He has seen how decisions made in government affect people in their everyday lives.
We need leaders who understand the challenges families face because they have lived them.
We need leaders who focus on solutions instead of division and results instead of politics.
Chris is running to expand opportunity, lower costs, strengthen our communities, and ensure every family has a fair chance to succeed.
