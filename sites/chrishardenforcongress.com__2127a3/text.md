Let's do something different together ​ For too long, politics has focused on division instead of solutions and partisanship instead of people. ​ Chris Harden is running for Congress because he believes the government should work for working people.
He believes every family deserves the opportunity to succeed, every community deserves to be heard, and every person deserves leadership that puts results ahead of politics.
Dream CHRIS' VISION Chris believes the government should work for the people it serves.
The American Dream is built on the promise that hard work, responsibility, and determination create opportunity.
Today, too many families feel that promise slipping further out of reach.
The cost of housing, healthcare, childcare, and everyday necessities continues to rise while many families are working harder than ever just to keep up.
Chris Harden is running for Congress because he believes every family deserves the opportunity to build a better future.
Chris' vision is a community where families can afford to live and thrive, small businesses can grow, and every child has access to the opportunities they need to succeed.
Chris believes in responsible government, fiscal accountability, strong public infrastructure, quality healthcare, and an economy that rewards hard work.
Chris also believes leadership should be measured by results, not rhetoric.
Congress should focus on solving problems, creating opportunity, and helping families succeed.
Together, we can build a future where opportunity remains within reach and where the American Dream is available to every generation that follows.
CHRIS' PRIORITIES Eleven Priorities for a Stronger 11th Chris Harden is running to put the needs of Georgia’s 11th District first.
His priorities are rooted in the everyday concerns of working families, small businesses, veterans, students, and seniors across Northwest Georgia.
Healthcare and Reproductive Freedom Every family deserves access to quality, affordable healthcare and the freedom to make personal medical decisions without political interference.
Chris believes reproductive freedom is a fundamental right.
Women in Georgia’s 11th District should have access to the full range of reproductive healthcare, including contraception, fertility treatment, maternal and prenatal care, and abortion care.
These are personal medical decisions that should remain between a patient and their doctor, not politicians in Washington or the state legislature.
Families throughout Northwest Georgia rely on Affordable Care Act premium tax credits to make health insurance more affordable.
Chris will fight to restore and expand those credits so families are not hit with higher premiums because Congress fails to act.
He will work to protect and strengthen Medicare and Medicaid, cap out-of-pocket costs for life-saving medications, expand access to mental health services, and improve rural access to care.
Residents of Jasper, Calhoun, Cartersville, and surrounding communities should not have to travel hours to see a specialist.
As an attorney who has spent more than 14 years helping families navigate difficult challenges, Chris understands that healthcare decisions can shape a family’s financial security and future.
Healthcare is not a privilege.
It is essential to the stability of our families and communities.
Deliver a Tax Cut for the Middle Class Working families should not pay a higher effective tax rate than multinational corporations.
Chris will fight for middle-class tax relief while ensuring that large corporations and the wealthiest Americans contribute their fair share.
Tax policy should help families pay for childcare, housing, healthcare, education, and retirement.
It should not simply increase returns for shareholders who are already doing well.
The benefits of economic growth should reach the people who work for it.
Get Corporate Money Out of Politics Washington should answer to voters, not corporate PACs.
Chris has pledged not to accept corporate PAC money.
He supports greater transparency, stronger enforcement of campaign finance laws, and closing loopholes that allow unlimited spending to overwhelm the voices of everyday voters.
Public accountability should not depend on wealth, political connections, or convenience.
When Congress had an opportunity in July 2025 to force a vote on releasing the Epstein files, Barry Loudermilk chose not to vote.
Elected officials should be accountable to the people they represent, especially when difficult questions demand clear answers.
Chris believes voters deserve representatives who work for their communities, not for the highest bidder.
Lower Costs for Families Families across Georgia’s 11th District are feeling the pressure of rising costs.
Housing, healthcare, groceries, childcare, and utility bills are taking a larger share of household budgets, making it harder for hardworking people to save, plan ahead, and get ahead.
In communities from Adairsville to Fairmount, people do not measure the economy by charts in Washington.
They measure it by what is left in the bank account at the end of the month.
Chris will support policies that lower everyday costs, strengthen economic security, and help families keep more of what they earn.
He will support trade policies that protect Georgia jobs and agriculture without driving up prices for consumers, and he supports raising the minimum wage so that a full day’s work can support a stable life.
Grow Small Businesses and Local Economies Small businesses are central to the local economies of Georgia’s 11th District.
They create jobs, support local organizations, and keep dollars circulating in our communities.
Chris supports expanding access to capital for small businesses, reducing unnecessary barriers for entrepreneurs, encouraging innovation, and creating conditions that allow local businesses to grow, hire, and invest.
Washington should make it easier for people to start and sustain a business, not stack the deck in favor of corporations with teams of lobbyists and legal departments.
Protect Our Environment and Public Health We have a responsibility to protect the land, water, and communities that future generations will inherit.
Chris supports clean air, clean water, responsible growth, and practical investments that protect public health while supporting local jobs and economic opportunity.
He believes residents deserve a meaningful voice in decisions that affect their neighborhoods, health, environment, and quality of life.
Economic growth should not come at the expense of safe drinking water, healthy communities, or Georgia’s natural resources.
Expand Opportunity Through Education and Workforce Development Every child deserves a quality public education, and every adult deserves a real path to economic opportunity.
Chris supports strong public schools, career and technical education, apprenticeship programs, workforce training, and affordable routes to higher education.
Whether someone wants to attend college, learn a trade, start a business, or build skills for a new career, they should be able to access the training they need without taking on unmanageable debt.
Having worked full time while putting himself through school, Chris understands the value of opportunity and the determination it takes to build a better future.
Ban Partisan Gerrymandering Politicians should not choose their voters.
Voters should choose their representatives.
Chris supports independent redistricting commissions that draw fair maps, respect real communities, and create more competitive elections.
District lines should not be manipulated to protect political power or silence voters.
Fair representation is essential to a government that people can trust.
Crack Down on Price Gouging New technology should help consumers, not give corporations new ways to exploit them.
Companies are increasingly using artificial intelligence and personal data to change prices in real time.
Chris believes families deserve transparency and protection when they shop for essential goods and services.
He will fight to prohibit AI-driven surveillance pricing for essential goods such as groceries and support stronger federal enforcement against corporate price gouging.
Families should be able to trust that they are being charged a fair price, not a price tailored to what a company thinks they can afford.
Stand Up for Veterans and Military Families Georgia’s 11th District is home to thousands of veterans, active-duty service members, and military families.
They have served our country and deserve a representative who will fight for them in return.
Chris will work to expand access to VA healthcare and reduce wait times, particularly for rural veterans who may otherwise need to travel long distances for care.
He supports increased investment in veteran mental-health services and suicide-prevention programs, stronger job training and career pathways for those transitioning from military service, and full protection of the benefits veterans and their families have earned.
Supporting veterans means more than offering thanks.
It means making sure they can access the healthcare, opportunity, and support they were promised.
Invest in Infrastructure, Housing, and Clean Energy As Georgia’s 11th District continues to grow, our infrastructure must keep pace.
Chris supports investments in roads, bridges, water systems, broadband, and traffic improvements that help communities function and support local economic growth.
He also supports modernizing the electric grid and expanding clean-energy development that can lower long-term energy costs, create good jobs, and strengthen American competitiveness.
Chris will work to increase the supply of housing that younger Georgians need to build their lives and that older Georgians need to retire with security and dignity.
Families across the district are also raising legitimate concerns about rapid and unchecked data-center development.
Washington needs to take those concerns seriously.
Working families should not pay higher utility bills to subsidize special treatment for large data centers.
Chris believes data centers built in the district should meet clear standards for protecting water supplies and the environment, using local labor where possible, and planning responsibly for their energy needs.
Ratepayers should not be left covering the cost.
Healthcare That Puts Patients Over Profits Healthcare should be a right, not a privilege.
No one in Cobb, Cherokee, Bartow, Pickens, or Gordon County should delay seeing a doctor because they are worried about the bill, and no family should go bankrupt because someone gets sick.
Millions of Americans, including families here in Northwest Georgia, rely on Affordable Care Act premium tax credits to keep coverage affordable.
Chris Harden will fight to permanently restore and expand those credits so families are not blindsided by higher premiums when Congress fails to act.
Harden will also push to cap out-of-pocket costs for life-saving medications and expand rural access to care, so residents of Jasper, Calhoun, and Cartersville are not forced to drive hours to see a specialist.
A single illness should never threaten a family’s financial security.
A Tax Cut for Our Middle Class Working families should not pay a higher effective tax rate than multinational corporations.
Harden will fight for a middle-class tax cut while ensuring that large corporations and the ultra-wealthy pay their fair share.
Tax relief should reach the families who need it, not simply add to the returns of shareholders who already have plenty.
Get Corporate Money Out of Politics Washington should answer to voters, not corporate PACs.
Harden has pledged not to take a dime of corporate PAC money.
He will fight for greater transparency, stronger enforcement of existing campaign finance laws, and the closure of loopholes that allow unlimited spending to drown out everyday voters.
Elected officials should be accountable to their constituents, not to whoever writes the biggest check.
Lower Costs, Raise Wages Families across this district should not pay more at the checkout line because of Washington’s trade policy failures.
Wages here have not kept pace with the cost of living.
Families in Adairsville and Fairmount measure the economy by what is left in the bank account at the end of the month, not by a chart on a screen in Washington.
Harden will fight for smart trade policies that protect Georgia jobs and agriculture without punishing consumers.
He also supports raising the minimum wage so a full day’s work pays for a full day’s living.
Ban Congressional Stock Trading Members of Congress should never be able to profit from information the public does not have or from votes they cast themselves.
Public service should never become a pathway to personal enrichment.
Harden supports a full ban on stock trading for members of Congress, federal judges, senior Executive Branch officials, and their immediate families.
Demand Real Transparency in Washington The American people deserve the truth, and accountability should not depend on wealth or political connections.
When Congress had the opportunity in July 2025 to force a vote on releasing the Epstein files, Barry Loudermilk chose not to vote.
Harden believes transparency is not optional.
He will push for it consistently, not only when it is politically convenient.
Clean Up Congress Serving in Congress should be the highest form of public service, not a career or a stepping stone to a lucrative lobbying contract.
Harden supports a lifetime ban on former members of Congress lobbying the federal government.
That would help break the pipeline between elected office and special interests.
He also supports an age limit of 80 for members of Congress.
A healthy democracy depends on new ideas, fresh leadership, and meaningful opportunities for the next generation to serve.
Public office should never become a lifetime entitlement.
Ban Partisan Gerrymandering Politicians should not get to pick their voters.
Voters should pick their politicians.
Harden supports independent redistricting commissions that draw fair maps and create more competitive elections.
District lines should reflect real communities, not political convenience.
Crack Down on Price Gouging New technology should help consumers, not exploit them.
As corporations increasingly use AI and personal data to adjust prices in real time, real accountability is needed.
Harden will fight to ban AI-driven surveillance pricing on essential goods such as groceries and strengthen federal enforcement against corporate price gouging.
Families deserve to know they are paying a fair price.
Responsible Oversight of Data Center Development Families in this district are increasingly concerned about unchecked data center development, and it is a conversation Washington is not having.
Working families should not subsidize special treatment for data centers through higher utility bills.
Data centers built here should meet strict standards for protecting the environment and water supply, employing local labor, and generating their own power.
Ratepayers should not be left footing the bill.
Invest in Clean Energy and 21st-Century Infrastructure This is about more than protecting the planet.
It is about lowering energy costs, creating good-paying jobs, and making sure America leads rather than falls behind.
Barry Loudermilk voted for the 2025 reconciliation bill that rolled back Inflation Reduction Act clean-energy tax credits and incentives.
Those policies were helping families lower utility bills and helping workers secure good-paying jobs in one of the world’s fastest-growing industries.
Harden will fight to expand clean energy, modernize the electric grid, and invest in the roads, bridges, and infrastructure this district depends on.
He will also push to build the housing younger Georgians need to start their lives and older Georgians need to retire comfortably.
Lowering Costs for Families Families across Georgia’s 11th District are feeling the strain of rising costs.
From housing and healthcare to groceries and childcare, too many hardworking people are finding it harder to get ahead.
Chris will support policies that lower costs, strengthen economic security, and help families keep more of what they earn while building a future they can afford.

He supports protecting access to contraception, fertility treatments, maternal healthcare, and abortion care, and believes these deeply personal decisions should remain between patients and their healthcare providers, not politicians.
He will work to protect and strengthen Medicare and Medicaid, lower prescription drug costs, expand access to mental health services, and ensure families can access quality healthcare regardless of where they live or how much they earn.
As an attorney who has spent more than fourteen years helping families navigate some of life’s most difficult challenges, Chris understands that healthcare decisions can have lasting impacts on a family’s future.
He believes the government should empower people to make the best decisions for themselves and their loved ones while ensuring every community has access to the care and resources they need.
Healthcare is not a privilege.
It is essential to the health, stability, and economic security of our families, our communities, and our future.
Growing Small Businesses and Local Economies Small businesses are the backbone of our local economy.
Chris supports expanding access to capital, reducing unnecessary barriers for entrepreneurs, supporting innovation, and creating an environment where businesses can grow, hire, and invest in their communities Protecting Our Environment and Public Health We have a responsibility to leave future generations a healthier and more sustainable community.
Chris supports clean air, clean water, responsible growth, and investments that protect public health while creating economic opportunity.
He believes communities deserve a voice in decisions that impact their health, environment, and quality of life.
Investing in Infrastructure As Georgia’s 11th District continues to grow, we must invest in the infrastructure that keeps our communities moving.
Chris supports improving roads, addressing traffic congestion, expanding broadband access, modernizing water systems, and making strategic investments that support economic growth and improve quality of life.
Expanding Opportunity Through Education and Workforce Development Every child deserves access to a quality education, and every adult deserves pathways to economic opportunity.
Chris supports strong public schools, career and technical education, apprenticeship programs, workforce training, and affordable pathways to higher education.
Having worked his way through school while working full time, Chris understands firsthand the importance of creating opportunities that help people succeed.

Across our communities, families are working hard, raising children, supporting local businesses, volunteering in schools, serving in their churches, and investing in the future of their neighborhoods.
They are teachers, healthcare workers, veterans, first responders, entrepreneurs, tradespeople, and community leaders who make this district a great place to live.
Our district continues to grow because people see opportunity here.
Families are putting down roots, businesses are expanding, and communities are investing in the future.
But growth also brings challenges.

People want safe communities, strong schools, affordable healthcare, reliable infrastructure, and economic opportunities that allow them to build a good life.
The people of Georgia’s 11th District are not looking for someone to speak for them.
