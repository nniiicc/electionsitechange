We are a nation built on the rule of law, and that includes strong border security.
Under the current Administration, the southern border has been secured and must remain so to prevent illegal immigration and protect national security.
Existing federal laws should be enforced consistently, including the deportation of immigrants who commit crimes.
We also need reforms to visa programs so businesses can access needed workers while ensuring visa holders return home when their visas expire rather than remaining in the United States unlawfully.
