Day One Priorities Flagship Bills The six bills that matter most to me — chosen because they deliver real results for District 30A and bring people together across the aisle.
Expand Drug Price Caps 01 Extend the $25/month copay cap beyond insulin to medications for diabetes, heart disease, asthma, and cancer maintenance.
A pocketbook win that unites families across the aisle .
Insurance Claims Transparency 02 Require all insurers to publicly report claim denial rates , reasons, appeal outcomes, and use of AI in claims processing — published on a searchable public dashboard.
Transparency with teeth.
Upgrade the State Spending Dashboard 03 Mandate that all state and local agencies publish spending data within 5 business days , with district-level filtering so you can see exactly where your tax dollars go.
Accountability in real time.
Predictable School Funding 04 Set an inflation-indexed formula floor with a standard one-page levy transparency report — so districts can plan without referendum roulette and voters see exactly where property tax dollars go.
Bridges tax-sensitive and pro-education voters.
My Minnesota Citizens Portal 05 Build a single online portal where Minnesotans can access services across all state agencies, check eligibility for programs, and track applications — with in-person service centers in every region.
Government that works for you.
Satellite Health Clinics 06 Fund new health clinics in fast-growing suburban communities with primary care, behavioral health, and prenatal services — so families don't have to drive 30 minutes for a checkup.
Healthcare closer to home. !
