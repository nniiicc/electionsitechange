Look up your exact district at the Minnesota Secretary of State pollfinder.
How do I register to vote?
Register online at mnvotes.gov with a Minnesota driver's license or ID, or register at your polling place on Election Day with proof of residence.
Where do I vote?
Find your polling place at mnvotes.gov/pollfinder — enter your address and your assigned location and hours will appear.
Can I vote early or by mail?
Yes.
