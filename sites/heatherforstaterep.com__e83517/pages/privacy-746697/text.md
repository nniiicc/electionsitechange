Privacy Policy
Stafford for 131 collects only data provided by users to its form: name, email, and mobile phone number, to stay in touch with voters.
Voters may opt out by replying to a text with the word "Stop" or by emailing heather@heatherforstaterep.com.
Data is shared only with third parties who need to use this information to carry out work directly on our behalf, and is protected by industry standard security measures.
Data is retained through the next November election, and conforms with applicable privacy legislation in Connecticut and the United States.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Terms and Conditions: You agree to receive informational messages (event reminders, campaign notifications, etc.) from Stafford for 131.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
For help, reply HELP or email us at heather@heatherforstaterep.com.
You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.
