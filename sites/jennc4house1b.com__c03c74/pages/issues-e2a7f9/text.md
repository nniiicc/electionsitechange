The issues facing District 1B are not political talking points to me, they’re real life experiences that impact families, workers, parents, and communities every single day.
My priorities are rooted in the conversations I’ve had, the work I’ve done, and the challenges I’ve personally experienced.

I’m committed to practical solutions that make everyday life more affordable while helping people build long-term stability.
