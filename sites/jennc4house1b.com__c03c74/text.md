Our priorities
High Cost of Living
Families across our communities are working hard and still struggling to keep up.
I believe we need practical solutions that make everyday life more affordable, from housing and childcare to groceries, utilities, and property taxes.
Making sure people are heard
Too many people feel ignored by the systems and leaders that are supposed to represent them.
I believe leadership starts with listening, showing up, and making sure everyday voices have a real seat at the table.
Mental, Dental, & Physical Healthcare
Healthcare should include the whole person.
That means expanding access to mental health support, affordable dental care, addiction recovery resources, and quality physical healthcare, especially in rural communities where services are often limited.
Public Safety & Responsible Gun Ownership
We can support constitutional rights while also raising the standards for gun safety and community protection.
I believe in practical, responsible approaches that keep firearms out of dangerous situations while respecting lawful gun owners and rural traditions.
