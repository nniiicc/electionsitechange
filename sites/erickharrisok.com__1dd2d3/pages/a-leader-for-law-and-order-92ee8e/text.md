Principle 1: Support for Law Enforcement As a conservative, Erick believes we must provide law enforcement with the resources, pay, training and respect they deserve.
Principle 2: Crime Prevention We need to invest in proven, common sense policing measures and proactive strategies to keep our neighborhoods and schools safe.
Principle 3: Tough on Crime Criminals who pose a threat to our community must face appropriate consequences.
Principle 4: Self Defense The rights of law-abiding citizens to protect themselves and their families must not be infringed upon.
Principle 5: Mental Health and Substance Abuse Many individuals in the criminal justice system need access to proper treatment and support to break the cycle of crime.
