Like Ronald Reagan and many Oklahomans, Erick was raised in a Democratic household.
But he realized that his conservative principles aligned with the values of the Republican Party.
As your state representative, you can count on Erick to bring conservative, limited government to the state House.
He is Christian, pro-life, pro-family, pro-business, and opposes radical attempts to redefine gender.
Erick will always fight for law and order, and support education, parental rights and liberty.
