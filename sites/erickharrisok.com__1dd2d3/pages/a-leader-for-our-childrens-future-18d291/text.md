As the son and grandson of educators and a former adjunct professor at the University of Central Oklahoma, education is more than just policy for Erick; it’s a personal commitment.
Principle 1: Local Control As a conservative, Erick believes the parents, teachers and school boards in our community know what’s best for our kids.
Principle 2: School Choice Every child is unique, and parents should have the freedom to choose the best educational path for their kids.
Principle 3: Knowledge & Skills Our students should be proficient in reading, writing and arithmetic – not Critical Race Theory and transgender ideology.
Principle 4: Accountability We must have high standards and regular assessments to measure student progress, as well as the effectiveness of teachers and administrators.
Principle 5: Education Funding We need to invest in our educational infrastructure to ensure a safe and effective learning environment.
It’s also vital that we pay our teachers competitive salaries.
Principle 6: Support Public Schools As a strong believer in the value of a public-school education, Erick will work to strengthen public schools.
