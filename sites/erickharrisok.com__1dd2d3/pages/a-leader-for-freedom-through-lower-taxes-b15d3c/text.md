Erick believes Oklahomans are better equipped than the government to determine how to spend their hard-earned money.
Principle 1: Lower Taxes Erick wants to reduce the tax burden on Oklahomans and help them keep more of their earnings.
Principle 2: Fiscal Responsibility We must ensure that every taxpayer dollar is used wisely and eliminate wasteful government spending.
Principle 3: Economic Growth By creating a business-friendly environment, we can attract more companies and jobs to our state, ensuring a brighter future for all Oklahomans.
