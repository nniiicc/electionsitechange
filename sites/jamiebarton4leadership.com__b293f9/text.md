FOR PENNSYLVANIA'S 124TH DISTRICT
Like many of you, Jamie is invested in this community because it is his home.
As our State Representative, Jamie will fight to make the government work for us by focusing on job growth, education, reducing taxes and wasteful spending, eliminating property taxes and revitalizing the PA Energy industry.
Jamie knows what it takes to make a business run efficiently and that is the experience he will take with him to Harrisburg to fight for us.
Jamie is a lifelong member of this community, who shares our values.
He’s spent the past 32 years as the Vice President of Jack Rich, Inc., a successful energy business in our community.
He has been a business leader, sitting on the boards of some of our region’s leading energy groups and helping to create jobs in our community.
Jamie is also a community leader, serving as a lay minister in his church and as a mentor and leader in several community groups.
He was named the 2017 Citizen of the Year by the Orwigsburg Lions Club and the 2021 Businessman of the Year by the Schuylkill County Chamber of commerce.
