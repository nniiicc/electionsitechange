The conversation covered a range of important issues impacting California, as well as Mr.
Gates’ campaign platform and perspective on statewide policy priorities.
The meeting provided a valuable opportunity for dialogue on leadership, public service, and the direction of California’s future.
“It was an insightful evening,” Hoelter noted, reflecting on the discussion.
Engagements like these continue to reinforce Charles Hoelter’s commitment to staying informed, building relationships with leaders across the state, and actively participating in conversations that shape the future of California.
