Patrick Haddon is committed to preserving our community’s values while driving meaningful reforms for a better future.
With a proven record of leadership and dedication, he is the right choice to represent us and ensure our voices are heard.
Cutting Taxes Delivered more than $1 billion dollars to taxpayers by lowering the income tax rate and providing a property tax credit on owner occupied homes.
Less money going to Columbia means a smaller government.
Protecting Agricultural Lands Establishes the Working Agricultural Lands Preservation Program and Fund to identify and help provide permanent protection to significant working farmland properties using selection criteria.This legislation will aid in the stopping of destruction of farm land and turning it into development.
Protecting Life Enacted the Fetal Heartbeat Bill, upholding the sanctity of life and affirming South Carolina’s commitment to its future generations.
Protecting Our Children Passed legislation banning transgender surgeries for minors and safeguarding boys’ and girls’ sports.
Strengthening Public Safety Backed law enforcement and first responders by giving them a pay raise of more than $28 million dollars and funded a school resource officer in every public school.
Closed the ‘Revolving Door’ for criminals who commit additional crimes while out on bond.
Led the fight to strengthen penalties for possession and distribution of fentanyl.
Protecting your 2nd amendment Rights Eliminated state government intrusion by enacting the Second Amendment Preservation Act that safeguards our constitutional right to have firearms.
Advancing Educational excellence & Choice Expanded school choice for parents and allocated $230 million to raise teacher salaries, with an additional $20 million for school safety.
Investing in Our Roads and Bridges Committed $200 million towards strengthening our bridges and allocated significant funds for comprehensive infrastructure improvements, prioritizing roads, bridges, and energy security for a future-ready South Carolina.
Supporting Veterans Honored our Veterans with more than $10 million to help maintain and manage Veterans Homes while offering grants to support military communities and boosted healthcare and facilities for our heroes.
Securing our Borders Created the Illegal Immigration Enforcement Unit within SLED to secure our borders and communities.
