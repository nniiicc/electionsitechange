(864)320-1946 Mission & Vision Home > My Vision & Mission Building a Stronger Community Dedicated to Values, Security, and Progress As your candidate for the House of Representatives, I am committed to a mission centered around preserving the values that make our community strong and resilient.
My campaign is dedicated to keeping taxes low, ensuring that hardworking families can keep more of their income and that businesses can thrive and create jobs.
I will work tirelessly to stop illegal immigration, securing our borders to maintain safety and order.
Protecting our agricultural lands is also a priority, as it is crucial to safeguarding our economy and way of life.
Furthermore, I am unwavering in my commitment to fight for the protection of the unborn, advocating for pro-life policies that reflect our shared values.
I am also focused on reforming state government to make it more efficient and responsive to the needs of our citizens.
Fixing our roads by reforming the Department of Transportation is critical to improving our infrastructure and ensuring safe and reliable transportation for all.
I will protect our children by supporting policies that ensure their safety and well-being.
Additionally, I am a staunch supporter of our veterans, advocating for the resources and recognition they deserve.
