Have your ID ready when you cast your ballot Voter ID is required in Nebraska.
Your ID must have your photo and name.
Your ID CAN be expired.
Your ID CAN have an out of date address.
For more information, visit the Nebraska Secretary of State website .
Election Day November 3rd Vote in-person at your polling place or the Douglas County Election Commission .
Must have your ID to vote.
