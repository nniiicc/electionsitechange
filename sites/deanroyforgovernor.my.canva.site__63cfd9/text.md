Home Platform Dean Roy For Governor Dean Roy, a born and raised Vermonter, is running to become the youngest governor in the United States.
Roy is running with the Freedom and Unity party, which he independently founded.
Dean Roy, born and raised in Stowe, Vermont, enjoys Nordic skiing and running in the winter and summer.
He enjoys making maple syrup with his family in the spring.
Dean was a former VT page, and is a political fanatic.
He also enjoys Formula One, and is a Fernado Alonso fan.
Dean Roy About the Freedom and Unity Party The freedom and Unity party, founded in 2025 by Dean Roy, is the youngest and fastest growing political party in Vermont.
Why’s he actually doing this?
Dean was always politically active.
But he really wanted to get involved after his time as a Vermont Page during his 8 th grade school year.
Then, during his 8 th grade graduation, his teacher told him if he’s ever running for governor, that he will be his campaign manager.
That teacher might not be managing Roy’s campaign, but still, Roy is trying to make a difference and is running for Governor of Vermont.
Who is he politically?
Dean was partly inspired to run after witnessing the raise of more left wing politicians around America.
Dean wants to bring anti authoritarian, left wing politics into his potential leadership in Vermont.
Dean combines strict anti authoritarianism, through both a radical state wide ban on ALPRs and facial recognition technology, and by putting the freedom of all Vermonters first in his campaign.
He combines this with an economic focused campaign, including caping networth at 1 billion dollars, to radically reduce economic inequality that is ravaging our state and Country.
Along with this, Dean supports a public option, and later universal healthcare, along with worker rights and gradually increased minimum wage.
