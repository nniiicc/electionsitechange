Learn More About Scott $1.4 Billion in Tax Cuts for Utah Families For the past five years, I have worked to provide meaningful savings for Utah families.
As the cost of living increases, I will continue to look for ways to save you money and keep the tax burden low.
Standing Against Judicial Overreach As our congressional districts have come under threat due to judicial overreach and outside liberal groups, I have been working to keep our districts conservative.
I have been working to ensure Utah’s elected representatives, not unelected judges, decide how our districts are drawn.
Putting Rural Realities into Law As a 3rd generation rancher and farmer, I have made it a priority to ensure that our district’s voice and realities are understood in the Utah Legislature.
To that end, I have passed laws that support our agricultural communities and ensure our concerns are represented in the legislature.
Protecting Our Water Rights I have worked to pass bills that protect water rights for Utah farmers, businesses, and our local communities.
I have also worked to pass legislation that will support the Great Salt Lake while protecting existing water rights.
Ending (DEI) in Utah Institutions I worked to end DEI in our public institutions because I believe in giving the job or position to the most qualified person.
Now, when our public institutions are hiring, they will hire based on merit, not skin color, gender, or sexual orientation.
