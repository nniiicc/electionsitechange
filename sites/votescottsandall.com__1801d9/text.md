I’m Scott Sandall and as lifelong resident, rancher, conservative, and business owner in Box Elder County, I love our special area of the state.
With my history, serving in the Utah Senate, Utah House of Representatives, and the Utah Farm Bureau State Board of Directors, I believe that I'll be able to build upon the legacy of hard work and prosperity in Northern Utah
My commitment is to continue to represent the interests of you and your family at the Utah State Legislature.
- For the past five years, I have worked to provide meaningful savings for Utah families.
As the cost of living increases, I will continue to look for ways to save you money and keep the tax burden low.
- As our congressional districts have come under threat due to judicial overreach and outside liberal groups, I have been working to keep our districts conservative.
I have been working to ensure Utah’s elected representatives, not unelected judges, decide how our districts are drawn.
- As a 3rd generation rancher and farmer, I have made it a priority to ensure that our district’s voice and realities are understood in the Utah Legislature.
To that end, I have passed laws that support our agricultural communities and ensure our concerns are represented in the legislature.
- I have worked to pass bills that protect water rights for Utah farmers, businesses, and our local communities.
I have also worked to pass legislation that will support the Great Salt Lake while protecting existing water rights.
- I worked to end DEI in our public institutions because I believe in giving the job or position to the most qualified person.
Now, when our public institutions are hiring, they will hire based on merit, not skin color, gender, or sexual orientation.
