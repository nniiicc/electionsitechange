MEET NIKKI TORRES
Nikki Torres | 8th Legislative District
Senator Nikki Torres has called the Tri-Cities home for over 40 years.
Her deep roots in our community and her extensive experience in the private sector uniquely qualify her to lead as a common-sense voice for the Mighty 8th.
Business Expertise & Economic Leadership
Before her service in the State Senate, Nikki spent several years in Commercial Lending at Gesa Credit Union, where she led business development and expanded the institution’s commercial footprint across Eastern Washington.
By working directly with small business owners, large enterprises, and non-profits, Nikki gained a firsthand understanding of the diverse needs of our local economy.
This professional experience solidified her firm belief that the government should empower, not hinder, the private sector.
While working full-time in lending, Nikki earned her MBA, sharpening the fiscal expertise she now uses to fight government overreach and protect the interests of Washington’s job creators.
A Proven Track Record of Community Service
Nikki’s commitment to our region extends far beyond her professional career.
As a former Pasco City Council Member and Past President of the Tri-Cities Hispanic Chamber of Commerce, she has worked tirelessly to boost economic development and provide technical training to help local businesses flourish.
Her leadership spans across vital sectors, including:
- Healthcare & Safety: Board Member for Tri-Cities Community Health and the Kennewick Police Department Foundation.
- Regional Growth: Member of the Tri-city Hispanic Chamber of Commerce and the AWB Grassroots Alliance.
- Families & Education: Appointee to the Washington Childcare Collaborative Taskforce.
Family First
Above all, Nikki is a devoted mother of two daughters and a proud grandmother of three.
Whether she is in Olympia or here at home, her focus remains the same: ensuring a safe, affordable, and prosperous future for the next generation of Washingtonians.
