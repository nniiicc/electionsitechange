As described in the above panel, the state of Oregon taxes business gross receipts, or in other words, sales.
The state has what is called the CAT tax, which is short for Corporate Activity Tax.
Oregonians pay more for the things they buy because the CAT tax is passed by businesses onto Oregon consumers, hidden in the sale prices of things Oregonians buy.
Businesses normally pass along the CAT tax to Oregon consumers without it appearing as a separate line-item tax, as would be the case with a typical sales tax.
