State Representative District 122 Menu About Fellowship Capitol Corner Constituents Priorities Volunteer Donate About Fellowship Capitol Corner Constituents Priorities Volunteer Donate Fellowship Spotlight: Zarah Punjwani Posted December 8, 2020 By Mennah Abdelwahab Fellowship Spotlight Zarah Punjwani is a second-year marketing and international business major with a minor in French.
After graduation, she hopes to work in the tech industry as a marketing director or creative brand manager.
As a Public Relations fellow, Zarah aims to gain experience, memories and lifelong friends.
Before joining the fellowship, Zarah served as a marketing intern for the Aga Khan Foundation, which hosts youth summer programs overseas in Kenya, India and Pakistan.
Currently, she interns with The Folklore, an online retailer specializing in African designers selling in the U.S. market, and is a member of the American Red Cross.
Zarah has traveled to over 16 countries. ©# Spencer Frye State House 122.
