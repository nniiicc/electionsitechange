Meet Brett Friend of the Taxpayer and a Straight-Talking Leader Brett Miller has spent his career in service to others, and he brings that same commitment to representing the 41st District in Harrisburg.
Before entering public office, Brett spent 26 years as a public school guidance counselor, helping thousands of Lancaster County students find their path.
He first put that service to work locally as an East Hempfield Township Supervisor, where his peers elected him board chairman for 4 years and where he guided the township from a fiscal deficit to a healthy surplus without raising taxes or cutting services.
A resident of East Hempfield for more than 35 years, Brett lives with his wife, Jennifer, a public school elementary teacher.
Voters in the 41st District know Brett Miller as a straight-talking, reform-minded leader who shows up, listens, and gets things done.
