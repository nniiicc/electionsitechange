She has worked tirelessly to promote our values as caucus chair of the Montgomery County Delegation.
She serves on the Environment and Transportation Committee and is treasurer of the Women Legislators of Maryland.
Prior to being elected as our delegate, she was chair of the Montgomery County Democrats and led a foundation that helped young women fight for workers’ rights and social justice.
