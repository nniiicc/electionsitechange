Toggle navigation Home Meet Julie Volunteer/Contact Events Gallery District Map Donate Meet Julie Home Meet Julie I was born in Madison and raised in Verona, WI, and built much of my career in Madison, where I worked in administration for a large tax and accounting firm.
In 2013 my husband and I made Sauk County our home, my parents grew up and graduated from Sauk Prairie and I knew this would be the perfect place to settle and call home for ourselves with a strong community, small town, values, and the quality of life it offers.
Since then, I’ve been proud to give back—through volunteer service, customer service at a local insurance agency, and by being the kind of neighbor who shows up when others need help.
As a mother, grandmother, and lifelong Wisconsinite, I believe in protecting the values that make our community strong.
I’m running to ensure Sauk County remains a place where families can thrive today and for generations to come.
Friends of Helmer for Assembly, Jerry Helmer, Treasurer.
