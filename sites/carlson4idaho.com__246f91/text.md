Idaho Senator, District 7
ALL IN FOR IDAHO!
I am Cindy Carlson, your District 7 Senator, a dedicated conservative, and a passionate advocate for protecting Idaho’s values.
I bring both personal and professional experience to my role in public service.
For four years, I’ve worked hard to ensure that conservative voices are heard in the legislature, passing key legislation and standing firm on issues that matter most to District 7 and the State of Idaho.
During this time, I have worked hard on legislation with the intent to protect parental rights in education, provide parental choice in education, improve Civics education requirements in our schools and protect kids from social media addiction.
As a member of the JFAC (Joint Finance and Appropriations Committee) our responsibility is to balance the state budget.
My main priority will continue to be transparency and common sense regarding Idaho’s revenue and spending.
I’m committed to continuing this work and I am ALL IN FOR IDAHO!
Issues
As a True Conservative, I will always…
- Fight for God’s Values.
- Fight for our rights to freedom of religion, of speech, and to assemble, as recognized by the First Amendment.
- Fight for our right to Keep and Bear Arms as recognized by the Second Amendment.
- Fight for common sense in governing and spending.
- Fight to protect the children of Idaho from being indoctrinated and for their right to have their own opinions.
- Fight to ban Critical Race Theory education in all of our schools.
- Fight for the lives of the unborn and elderly.
- Fight against vaccine mandates and passports.
- Fight for the right to keep our medical decisions private.
