Nancy Mannion, a former emergency nurse and international healthcare leader, is running for Pennsylvania’s 11th Congressional District.
For over 40 years, her integrity and astute decision-making have had a profound, positive, impact on people during their most challenging moments.
Nancy is visible in the district – she’s held eight town halls in PA-11 since February – and has shown up, ready to listen and learn at multiple public events in Lancaster and York counties.
She is ready to act decisively for the residents she will represent because politics transcends party lines when people need problems solved.
Nancy is deeply concerned about the welfare of her community and will fight for affordable healthcare, strong public education, workforce development and to protect voting rights, as well as other issues.
Nancy will bring the qualities that framed her career in healthcare – integrity, accountability, compassion and clear thinking under pressure – to Washington D.C.
