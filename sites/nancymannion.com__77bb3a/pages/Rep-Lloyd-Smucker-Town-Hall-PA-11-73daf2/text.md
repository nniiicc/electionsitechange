How long has it been since Lloyd held an in person town hall?
Its about ACCOUNTABILITY Smucker said that he has no plans to hold an in-person town hall.
“I’m not interested in a room full of people yelling.” -Smucker Why assume people will yell – unless you think they’re mad at you?
He hasn’t held an open in-person town hall in over a decade. ( source ) During the 2025 government shutdown, Smucker’s PA-11 “town hall” was a phone call: register online, submit a question, hope the staff chooses it.
That’s not openness.
It’s message control.
That’s been PA-11’s version of accountability for a decade.
People weren’t yelling.
They were asking: When paychecks, disaster aid, and loans would arrive.
Whether VA visits, WIC, SNAP, and school meals would be disrupted.
How Washington’s choices were hitting PA-11 families.
Those are accountability questions.
Dodging them is dodging the job.
If you asked me whether a shutdown would hit your paycheck, farm, benefits, or kid’s lunch, I’d say: Yes.
Then I’d ask what’s keeping you up at night, and we’d start there.
That’s listening: not a form, not a screened call – a conversation.
When elected, I’ll hold open town halls across PA-11 and answer your questions – even when the answer is hard.
That’s the job.
A representative who hides from constituents isn’t doing the job.
PA-11 has lived with that for a decade.
In 2026, we can change it.
