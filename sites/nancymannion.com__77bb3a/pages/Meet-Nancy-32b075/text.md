A Life of Service
Nancy Mannion is an accomplished registered nurse and healthcare leader.
She’s spent decades caring for people experiencing health emergencies, listening and acting to improve, and sometimes save, lives.
No stranger to high-stakes situations, Nancy built her career around thinking clearly under pressure, solving complex problems quickly, and delivering compassionate care.
For Nancy, integrity and accountability are core values.
As importantly, she has the strength, know-how, and experience to advocate for what working people need to survive and thrive.
She’ll bring those skills to her role as a U.S.
Congresswoman.
Her priorities include creating affordable, accessible healthcare programs and building a ladder to opportunity through strong education and workforce development programs.
In addition, recognizing that Lancaster and York counties are home to some of the most fertile farmland in our nation, Nancy is committed to helping farmers achieve economic success.
The residents of PA’s 11th District deserve a representative who will funnel tax dollars back into Lancaster and York to provide affordable healthcare, housing, and education and ensure that children and seniors are safe, have enough to eat, and a chance to thrive.
Nancy will work tirelessly for YOU, not the billionaires looking to increase their stock portfolios.
More about Nancy
Nancy’s life of service began in childhood.
Led by her parents’ example, she volunteered at church and completed service activities as a Girl Scout.
Her engagement in community service continues as an adult—she is Rotarian, church volunteer, and leader on multiple non-profit boards.
Nancy’s nursing education began with a Registered Nurse diploma.
She then earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing, Master's, and Doctorate degrees.
She has substantial management and governance experience from her positions in hospitals, health systems, and associations, where she honed her executive leadership skills.
Nancy served on numerous boards in the community and healthcare sectors, including as president of the Emergency Nurses Association, chair of the Academy of Emergency Nursing, and a Trustee of the Emergency Nurses Association Foundation.
Nancy is the immediate Past-Chair of the Nurses on Boards Coalition, which promotes nurses’ influence on community health through board service.
Nancy will bring extensive international experience to Congress.
She spent decades providing humanitarian medical care in South America, Asia, and Africa.
She has been on teams that collaborate with locals, people serving in missions, and the now-decimated USAID.
As an international healthcare consultant, she brought current clinical standards to Europe, Asia, and the Middle East, while educating healthcare leaders and governments on best practices in leadership and governance.
As a small business owner, Nancy understands firsthand the challenges and uncertainties of a rapidly changing marketplace.
She supports simplifying the tax code, investing in training and education programs, and expanding broadband so small businesses can thrive in the digital economy.
Nancy has been recognized as a Fellow by the American Academy of Nursing and the Emergency Nursing Association for her expertise and years of service to the profession.
Her writing has been published extensively in books and journals, and she is an in-demand speaker worldwide.
At home, Nancy and her husband enjoy gardening and are the proud pet parents of two dogs, Mr.
Finn and Joey.
