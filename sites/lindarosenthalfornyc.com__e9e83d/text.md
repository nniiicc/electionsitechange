Passing Groundbreaking Legislation Assemblymember Linda Rosenthal has written landmark legislation and she’s led the fight to pass it.
She is the author of 70 laws, including the recently passed Child Victims Act which will finally help New Yorkers who were sexually abused as children seek justice.
She’s also written first-in-the-nation legislation to protect pets by banning cat declawing.
Protecting Tenants, Supporting Our Public Schools No one in the state legislature has fought harder to protect tenants than Linda Rosenthal.
She sponsored the package of tough new rent laws that eliminates vacancy decontrol and cracks down on abusive landlords.
She’s been just as forceful a voice for public education—leading efforts for a new middle school in her West Side district and demanding full funding so that students in every zip code have high quality schools.
Moving New York Forward Whether it’s passing the Reproductive Health Act to protect a woman’s right to choose, making college affordable for immigrant children through the New York Dream Act, or passing the Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act to tackle global warming head on, Linda Rosenthal continues to push New York forward and make the state the progressive leader it should be.
