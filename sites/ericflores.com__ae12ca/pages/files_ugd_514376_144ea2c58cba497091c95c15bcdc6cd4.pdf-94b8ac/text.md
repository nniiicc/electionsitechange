Rather than serving as the independent voice South Texas was promised, Gonzalez aligned himself with the same far left politicians driving the national Democratic agenda, figures like Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez and Jasmine Crockett, politicians who are wildly out of touch with South Texas values and the everyday realities facing families along the border.
South Texas believes in secure borders.
South Texas believes in the rule of law.
South Texas believes American elections should be decided by American citizens.
The SAVE Act simply ensures that only eligible United States citizens vote in federal elections.
That is not extreme.
That is basic accountability.
Yet Vicente Gonzalez voted no.
