FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE August 10, 2026 Contact: press@ericflores.com Matthew Brownfield (214) 566-8589 ERIC FLORES ANNOUNCES ENDORSEMENT FROM NUECES COUNTY TAX ASSESSOR-COLLECTOR KEVIN KIESCHNICK CORPUS CHRISTI, TX — Republican congressional nominee Eric Flores today announced the endorsement of Nueces County Tax Assessor-Collector Kevin Kieschnick, recognizing the support of one of Nueces County’s longestserving and most respected public servants.
For the past 15 years, Kieschnick has faithfully served the people of Nueces County, earning a reputation as a responsible steward of taxpayer dollars and a leader committed to accountability, transparency, and efficient government.
His dedication to public service and longstanding involvement in the community have made him a trusted figure throughout the Coastal Bend.
“I am honored to receive Kevin Kieschnick’s endorsement,” said Flores.
“Kevin has devoted his career to serving the people of Nueces County with integrity and fiscal responsibility.
His support means a great deal to our campaign, and I look forward to fighting for the taxpayers and families of South Texas and the Coastal Bend in Congress.” Kieschnick’s endorsement adds to the growing coalition of local elected officials, law enforcement leaders, and respected community leaders supporting Flores’ campaign for Congress.
As momentum continues to build across Texas’ 34th Congressional District, Flores remains committed to advancing policies that strengthen the economy, secure the border, improve infrastructure, and create greater opportunity for the people of South Texas and the Coastal Bend.
