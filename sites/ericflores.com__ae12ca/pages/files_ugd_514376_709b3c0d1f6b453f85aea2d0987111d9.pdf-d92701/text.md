FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE August 26, 2026 Contact: press@ericflores.com Matthew Brownfield (214) 566-8589 CHAIRMAN TODD HUNTER ENDORSES ERIC FLORES AS LOCAL SUPPORT CONTINUES TO GROW CORPUS CHRISTI, TX — State Representative and Texas House Calendars Committee Chairman Todd Hunter has endorsed Eric Flores for Congress, joining a growing coalition of elected officials across Nueces County backing Flores to represent Texas’ 34th Congressional District.
“Eric Flores understands the Coastal Bend and the issues that matter to our area," Chairman Hunter said.
"He has the experience, work ethic and commitment to be a strong voice for South Texas in Congress.
I’m glad to support Eric and look forward to working with him to deliver results for the Coastal Bend and the entire region.” “Chairman Hunter knows what it takes to deliver for the Coastal Bend, and I’m honored to have his support,” Flores said.
“Across Nueces County, elected officials are coming together because they know we need a Congressman who will show up, work with our local leaders, and fight to solve the problems facing our communities.
Vicente Gonzalez has had years to be that partner, and our communities deserve better." Chairman Hunter's endorsement adds to growing momentum for Flores across Nueces County, where local leaders are rallying around a shared goal: electing a representative who will put the Coastal Bend first and work alongside them to deliver results.
"This campaign is building a coalition around putting South Texas and the Coastal Bend first," Flores said.
