FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE June 18, 2026 Contact: press@ericflores.com Matthew Brownfield (214) 566-8589 ERIC FLORES BRINGS USDA LEADERSHIP TO CORPUS CHRISTI TO HEAR DIRECTLY FROM SOUTH TEXAS FARMERS AND RANCHERS CORPUS CHRISTI, TX — Republican congressional candidate Eric Flores recently brought senior representatives from the U.S.
Department of Agriculture to Corpus Christi for a roundtable discussion with South Texas farmers and ranchers, ensuring local producers had a direct line to federal decision-makers.
Flores helped facilitate the meeting through relationships he has built in Washington and said the goal was simple: make sure South Texas has a seat at the table.
“Our farmers and ranchers work every day to put food on America’s tables, and they deserve leaders who will fight for them,” said Flores.
“Instead of waiting for Washington to notice South Texas, I brought Washington to South Texas.
That’s the kind of leadership our communities deserve.” Producers raised concerns ranging from water shortages and rising costs to livestock issues and the future of family farming and ranching operations.
“Agriculture isn’t just an industry here — it’s our way of life,” Flores said.
“I will always stand with the men and women who feed, fuel, and clothe this country.” Eric Flores is the Republican nominee for Congress in Texas’ 34th Congressional District.
