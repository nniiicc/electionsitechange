FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE June 19, 2026 Contact: press@ericflores.com Matthew Brownfield (214) 566-8589 VICENTE GONZALEZ ATTACKS CONSTITUENTS, JOKES ABOUT DEPORTATION SAN BENITO, TX — Eric Flores today criticized Congressman Vicente Gonzalez after screenshots surfaced showing Gonzalez attacking constituents online, engaging in personal insults, and joking about deportation.
“South Texans deserve a congressman who spends his time fighting for jobs, infrastructure, and opportunity, not attacking the very people he represents online,” said Eric Flores.
“Leadership means serving your constituents, even when they disagree with you.
Instead of fighting for South Texas, Vicente Gonzalez is picking fights with South Texans.
TX-34 deserves better.” The screenshots, which are being shared publicly by the Flores campaign, show a pattern of behavior that voters have come to expect from a congressman more focused on online arguments than delivering results for the people he represents.
Flores, a former federal prosecutor and former infantry officer, is running for Congress to bring accountability, economic growth, and stronger leadership to Texas' 34th Congressional District.
