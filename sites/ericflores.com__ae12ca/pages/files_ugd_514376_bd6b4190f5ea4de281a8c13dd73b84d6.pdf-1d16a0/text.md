FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE July 16, 2026 Contact: press@ericflores.com Matthew Brownfield (214) 566-8589 TX-34 VETERANS RALLY BEHIND ERIC FLORES WITH LAUNCH OF VETERANS FOR ERIC HARLINGEN, TX — Following the launch of Veterans for Eric, veterans from across Texas’ 34th Congressional District are coming together to support Eric Flores for Congress.
Flores, a current infantry captain in the Texas Army National Guard, said he is encouraged by the strong response the coalition has received since launching.
"Veterans have spent their lives serving our country, yet too many still struggle to access the care, benefits, and resources they have earned," said Flores.
"That ends when I am elected to Congress.
I will be their strongest advocate and make sure veterans across South Texas and the Coastal Bend always have someone fighting for them in Washington." Local veteran Orlando Sims praised Flores’ leadership and commitment to South Texas.
"Eric Flores is a strong Republican champion who understands Texas values," said Sims.
"Eric Flores has my full faith and confidence in his ability to serve Texas Congressional District 34.
I am proud to support him to be our next congressman." Flores’ former command sergeant major, Brian Gonzales, also spoke about the leadership he witnessed while serving alongside him.
"I had the privilege of serving alongside Eric during Operation Guardian Support and multiple Defense Support of Civil Authorities missions," said Gonzales.
"I witnessed firsthand his leadership, integrity, and dedication to serving others.
Eric continues to serve our nation in the military, and I know he'll bring that same commitment to Congress as he fights for the Coastal Bend and South Texas." Veterans for Eric will continue bringing together veterans and military families who believe TX-34 deserves a representative who understands service, sacrifice, and the responsibility of leadership.
