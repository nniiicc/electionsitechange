Mike knows what it’s like to work hard every day and still see the dream of buying a home slipping out of reach for working families.

Paul.
Mike is running to change that; he's bringing real, working-class perspective to the capital.

His campaign is dedicated to lifting up those who lift up Minnesota: our nurses, educators, farmers, laborers, veterans, and social service workers.
