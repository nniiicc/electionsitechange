Gerald Boursiquot
Gerald Boursiquot (bor-si-co) was born in Rockland County, New York, to hardworking Haitian immigrants.
From a young age, his parents taught him courage, leadership, service, and the value of an honest day’s work.
Gerald proudly served our country in the armed forces for 22 years, 10 in the Navy, and 12 in the Air Force.
In the field, he fearlessly guided 18 of his fellow Airmen through some of the world’s most dangerous places.
He made tough calls to protect their lives and defend our Constitution.
Gerald Boursiquot was a member of the U.S.
Air Force and the U.S.
Navy.
Use of his military rank, job titles, and photographs in uniform does not imply endorsement by the Department of the Air Force, the Department of the Navy, or the Department of Defense.
Gerald has lived in Fallbrook for over a decade.
He is an active and dedicated member of many North and East County communities, volunteering to support veterans and military families on Camp Pendleton and speaking out on the issues closest to our hearts.
While incumbent Assembly member Carl DeMaio has failed to bring any real solutions to the issues facing our district, Gerald is fighting to build a better future for all of us.
He is a veteran, former union member, friendly neighbor, loving father, and lifelong public servant running to build a California that works for everyone.
