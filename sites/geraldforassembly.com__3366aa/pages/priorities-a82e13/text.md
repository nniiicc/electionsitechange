Priorities
Government is not about clout or selfish power games.
It's about leadership.
It’s about creating real solutions when we need them, and Gerald is up to the task.
As our Assemblymember, Gerald will:
EXPAND HOUSING ACCESS
Housing is a human right.
But after hanging up my uniform, I didn’t have a home for 9 months.
I’m working so no one else goes without a roof over their head — Gerald
- Proposing and supporting laws to help us raise enough money to cover new and existing affordable housing projects without straining our local, county, or state
budgets.
- Working local organizations to deliver affordable housing projects for our veterans, young people, farmworkers, seniors, and working families.
- Providing affordable home insurance and wildfire mitigation options to protect us from future incidents like the Monte, Border 2, and Henderson Fires earlier this year.
- Incentivizing local governments to build more projects that meet housing needs while preserving North and East County’s charm and character.
- Removing red tape to make permitting easier for affordable housing developers.
- Investing in new affordable housing projects and repurposing old buildings for housing.
- Incentivizing multi-family housing development and diversifying existing housing options where it makes sense.
TACKLE THE COST OF LIVING
As a proud member of a union family and a former union auto worker, I know that all working people deserve a real chance to thrive.
That's why I'm fighting to help our hardworking families stay afloat in these difficult times — Gerald
- Keeping our fire and rescue services running through state and community partner funding sources while keeping our tax dollars in our pockets.
- Requiring more oversight and smarter spending in local school district budgets.
- Protecting working families’ right to organize for fair wages and working conditions.
- Proposing laws to reduce our ballooning health costs and promote transparency in our hospital bills.
- Partnering with SANDAG to bring rural families affordable, cost-effective local transportation options such as neighborhood shuttles.
- Expanding tax relief for veterans, unhoused folks, and working families by proposing smart changes to our tax code.
CHAMPION HEALTHCARE FOR ALL (CalCare)
"Healthcare is a fundamental human right, not a luxury.
After serving our country, I know that no working family, veteran, or senior should ever have to choose between putting food on the table or paying for a lifesaving prescription." — Gerald
- The Ultimate Cost-of-Living Solution: Healthcare expenses are one of the single largest drivers of household debt.
CalCare eliminates skyrocketing health insurance premiums, deductibles, copays, and out-of-pocket expenses, putting thousands of dollars back into the pockets of working families every year.
- Revitalizing Business & Driving Economic Growth: High corporate healthcare costs and surging employee insurance premiums are driving small businesses to the brink and forcing companies out of state.
By unburdening employers from paying for bloated private health insurance plans, CalCare lowers operating costs for small businesses, levels the playing field, and invites new business investment back to California.
- Ending Corporate Price-Gouging: Replaces wasteful, profit-driven health insurance middlemen with a streamlined, state-run single-payer system.
We keep our healthcare dollars right here in our communities, investing in frontline nurses, doctors, and hospitals rather than insurance CEO bonuses.
- Comprehensive Care for Every Community: Guarantees full medical, dental, vision, mental health, and prescription drug coverage for all residents across East and North County—ensuring our veterans, rural families, and seniors get the care they need without network barriers.

Now, I’m
running to keep that dream alive.”
- Securing funding for more job centers in North and East County to bring opportunities closer to home.
- Providing incentives to encourage small and large businesses to invest in our district.
