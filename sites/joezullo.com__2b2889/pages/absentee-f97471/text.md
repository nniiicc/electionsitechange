VOTE BY ABSENTEE BALLOT Can't make it to the polls?
You can vote by absentee ballot!
Voting by absentee ballot is easy and convenient.
Anyone eligible to vote on Election Day is eligible to vote by absentee ballot.
WHEN: October 2, 2026 to November 2, 2026 HOW: 1.
Request an absentee ballot application (you can request one using the form on this page).
2.
Fill our your absentee ballot application and return it to town hall (either by mail or hand deliver the application).
3.
When you receive your absentee ballot, complete the ballot according to the directions on the ballot.
4.
Return the ballot to Town Hall by mail no later than November 2, 2026.
