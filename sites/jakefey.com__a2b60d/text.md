A NOTE FROM REP.
FEY: It is my honor to represent you in the State House of Representatives.
I am a lifelong progressive that believes that our state, country and the planet face important challenges that must be addressed.
For the past 10 years, I have fought for legislation to ensure a stronger future for local families, businesses, and especially for our kids.
My accomplishments include investments in accessible, affordable green transit, in affordable housing construction, in behavioral health services, and in groundbreaking carbon reduction and clean energy.
With your support, I will continue fighting for urgent climate action, working to combat homelessness, investing in efficient and effective transportation, supporting living wage jobs and affordable higher education, and protecting everyone’s right to healthcare – including reproductive and gender affirming care.
