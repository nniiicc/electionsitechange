Selected for 2026 Run Club
'm honored to share that our campaign has been selected for 603 Forward's 2026 Run Club, a statewide candidate development program supporting New Hampshire legislative candidates.
Our campaign was one of only two State Representative campaigns from Carroll County selected for the 2026 cohort.
I'm excited to bring what I learn back to our campaign as we continue building a strong grassroots effort across Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro.
