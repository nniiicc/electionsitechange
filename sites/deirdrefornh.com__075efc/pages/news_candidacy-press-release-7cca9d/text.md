Deirdre McEachern Announces Candidacy for NH State Rep in Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro
June 3, 2026 - Deirdre McEachern, a business leader, entrepreneur, author, educator, and community volunteer, today announced her candidacy for New Hampshire State Representative representing Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro (Carroll County District 6).
McEachern said she is running to bring a renewed focus on thoughtful problem-solving, collaboration, and responsive local leadership.
