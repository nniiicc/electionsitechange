Jul 10 Written By Rebecca Henry July 4, 2026 - Our campaign was proud to take part in Wolfeboro's Fourth of July Parade as our community celebrated America's 250th anniversary .
Nearly 20 volunteers marched alongside Deirdre in campaign T-shirts behind a pickup truck decorated with American flags and campaign banners.
Volunteers handed out candy to parade-goers and bubble wands to children along the route.
The parade was a joyful celebration of our nation's history and our community.
