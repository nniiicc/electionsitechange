After 11 hours at the polls, Deirdre finished the day with 1,045 votes between the two towns.
She even received a write-in vote on the Republican ballot in Tuftonboro!
Based on the Primary Day totals, her support would have garnered her one of District 6’s two seats in the general election.
That support has grown one conversation at a time: at public events and house parties, while knocking on doors, and over countless cups of coffee on Main Street and beyond.
Thank you to every hearty volunteer who held a sign, greeted voters and spent the day with us at the polls.
We are deeply grateful for your time and dedication.
Now, let’s carry this momentum through November 3, just seven and a half weeks away!
