Frequently Asked Questions What office is Deirdre running for?
Deirdre McEachern is running for the New Hampshire House of Representatives in Carroll County, District 6, which includes Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro.
Why is Deirdre running?
Deirdre is running because she believes Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro deserve a representative who listens, brings practical solutions and works constructively on local concerns.
Deirdre is fighting for strong schools, affordable communities, expanded childcare programs, accessible healthcare, protected natural resources like our lakes, and responsive leadership that puts the people of her district first.
What experience would Deirdre bring to the State House?
Deirdre brings more than 25 years of New Hampshire entrepreneurship, extensive educational experience as a pre-K through grade 8 Head of School, advanced training in conflict resolution, and a long history of community service.
She would put those skills to work listening to constituents, evaluating legislation, and creating practical solutions.
What has Deirdre contributed to the local community?
Deirdre is an active member of the Wolfeboro/Tuftonboro Lions Club, serves on the Wolfeboro Festival of Trees Committee, and volunteers on the Development Committee of the Wentworth Watershed Association.
She is also an avid reader and loves the LRNC Book Club!
Deirdre is active in her faith community and serves on the Board of Directors for the Star Island Corporation jointly owned by the UUA and UCC churches.
During the campaign, she has spent time meeting residents, visiting local businesses, attending community events, and listening to people throughout Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro.
What party is Deirdre running with?
Deirdre is the Democratic Candidate for State Representative in Carroll District 6.
She is committed to representing the people of Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro, including residents who belong to another party or no party at all.
Her decisions will be guided by the needs of the district, the facts before the legislature, and the effect a proposal would have on local residents.
Would Deirdre vote with her party on every issue?
Deirdre will evaluate legislation on its merits and consider how it would affect Wolfeboro, Tuftonboro, and New Hampshire as a whole.
She will listen to residents and explain her votes.
Which organizations and community leaders have endorsed Deirdre?
Deirdre is honored to have earned endorsements from American Federation of Teachers NH,The Sierra Club, EMILYs List, New Hampshire Rights & Democracy, the New Hampshire Action Fund, the Sierra Club NH, NH Youth Movement, Move the Goalposts NH, US Congressman Ro Khanna and 350NH Action among others.
A complete list appears on the campaign's endorsements page at Endorsements — Deirdre McEachern for NH State Rep.
What issues is Deirdre most focused on?
Deirdre is running to protect strong public schools while easing the burden on local property taxpayers.
She supports locally driven housing solutions for workers, families, and seniors, while preserving local control.
She is also focused on protecting the district’s lakes, land, and rural character and strengthening local businesses and essential infrastructure.
What is Deirdre's position on public education?
Deirdre believes strong public schools are essential to strong communities.
She supports giving educators the resources they need, strengthening partnerships between families and schools, and keeping the focus on effective teaching and student outcomes.
She also believes the state must meet its constitutional responsibility for education so local property taxpayers do not carry an unfair share of the cost.
She believes public dollars should remain in public school budgets - she does not support private school vouchers.

How would Deirdre address affordability and property taxes?
Deirdre would address affordability by pushing the state to stop reneging on its obligations and fund its responsibilities to schools and towns, easing pressure on local property taxpayers.
She opposes a broad-based sales or income tax.
She supports locally driven housing solutions that help workers, families, and seniors afford to remain in Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro.
She also supports energy programs to focus on renewable energy and reduce expensive fossil fuel dependence for residents What is Deirdre's position on housing?
Deirdre supports locally driven efforts to expand housing that workers, young families, and seniors can afford.
She believes communities can create more housing while respecting property rights, using existing infrastructure wisely, and keeping important land-use decisions in local hands.
She also believes the Housing Champions program needs to be fully reinstated (and funded) by the legislature.
