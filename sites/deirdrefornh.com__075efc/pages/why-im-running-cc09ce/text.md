Throughout my career—in business, education, coaching, and community leadership—I've seen what can happen when people listen to one another, work together, and stay focused on solving problems.
I've also been disappointed to see too many lawmakers become more focused on political ideology than on the people they were elected to serve.
Too often, the conversations that matter most to local families get pushed aside.
Meanwhile, Granite Staters are being asked to shoulder more of the burden while too many policies benefit powerful interests instead of strengthening our communities.
We need leadership that puts people first, protects what makes New Hampshire special, and focuses on practical solutions that improve everyday life.
I'm running because I believe District 6 deserves a representative who will listen, work across differences, and never lose sight of who sent them to Concord.
I'll always be accessible, thoughtful, and committed to doing what's best for Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro—not what's best for politics.
Together, we can strengthen our public schools, support local businesses, protect our lakes and forests, make New Hampshire more affordable for working families, and preserve the qualities that make our communities such wonderful places to live.
I'd be honored to earn your trust—and your vote.
The weathered pilings along our shoreline remind us of what makes our communities strong.
Each stands on its own, but bound together, they are strong enough to weather the storms and steady enough to meet changing waters.
That same strength comes from neighbors working together—listening to one another, finding common ground, and remaining firmly anchored in the values we share.
