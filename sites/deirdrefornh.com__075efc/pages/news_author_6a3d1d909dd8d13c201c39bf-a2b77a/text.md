Supporting Deirdre McEachern for state representative
Carolyn Sundquist of Melvin Village, a nine-year member and former chair of the Tuftonboro Selectboard, published a letter in the Laconia Daily Sun (Sept. 21, 2026) endorsing Deirdre McEachern for state representative.
Sundquist cites McEachern's background as an entrepreneur, former Head of School, author, and community leader, along with her conflict-resolution and mediation experience, as strong preparation for the role.
She specifically praises McEachern's active campaign presence, coffee gatherings, house parties, door-to-door canvassing, as evidence of genuine engagement with Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro residents, and closes by urging fellow residents to learn more about McEachern's candidacy.
