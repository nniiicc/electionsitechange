Deirdre McEachern Named to ‘Move The Goalposts' First Endorsement List
Deirdre McEachern just landed on New Hampshire political analyst Steve Marchand's first round of House endorsements for 2026.
Marchand's organization, Move The Goalposts, is backing about 50 candidates this cycle and Deirdre McEachern made the initial cut of ten.
Marchand called Deirdre a very good match for the district, pointing to her background in education, as an entrepreneur, and in local civic life.
Deirdre is the right candidate at the right moment for Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro!
