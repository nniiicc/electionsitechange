American Federation of Teachers Endorses Deirdre McEachern for State Rep
We are honored to share that Deirdre’s campaign has been endorsed by the American Federation of Teachers!
As a dedicated advocate for public education, Deirdre is truly grateful to receive their support.
From Ryan Richmond, the Vice President of AFT-NH:
Congratulations!
On behalf of the American Federation of Teachers-New Hampshire, I'm excited to let you know that we have voted to endorse your campaign for the 2026 election.
We're proud to stand with candidates who understand that investing in public education and the people who make our communities work is an investment in New Hampshire's future.
Congratulations again, and thank you for your commitment to serving the people of New Hampshire.
We look forward to working with you throughout the campaign.
