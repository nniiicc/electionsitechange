Deirdre McEachern Endorsed by EMILY's List
Deirdre McEachern has been endorsed by EMILY's List, the nation's largest resource dedicated to electing Democratic women to office.
The endorsement, announced September 9, names Deirdre as one of 16 Democratic women running in New Hampshire's most critical State House flip opportunities this cycle, a recognition that Carroll 6 is a seat Democrats can and will win this November.
EMILY's List President Jessica Mackler called this year's slate of candidates "bold leaders" standing at the center of the fight to protect working families and build Democratic power across New Hampshire.
This is more than an endorsement.
It's a vote of confidence from one of the most influential organizations in Democratic politics, and it puts real momentum behind Deirdre's campaign heading into the general election.
