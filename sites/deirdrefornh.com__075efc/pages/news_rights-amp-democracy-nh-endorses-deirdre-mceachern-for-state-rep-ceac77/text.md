Rights & Democracy NH Endorses Deirdre McEachern for State Rep
I’m honored to receive the endorsement of Rights & Democracy, an organization working to protect fundamental rights, strengthen democracy, and build thriving communities across New Hampshire.
Their support brings valuable organizing experience and grassroots energy to our campaign as we work to ensure that Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro have thoughtful, principled representation in Concord.
I’m proud to stand alongside candidates committed to listening to their communities, defending our shared values, and building a stronger future for all Granite Staters.
