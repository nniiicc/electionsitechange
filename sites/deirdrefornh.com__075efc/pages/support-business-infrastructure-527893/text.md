They create jobs, serve residents and visitors, support local organizations, and contribute to the character and vitality of our towns.
When they succeed, our entire community benefits.
But businesses cannot thrive without the fundamentals that allow people to live and work here.
Safe and reliable roads, high-speed broadband, public safety, emergency response, accessible healthcare, childcare, and elder services are not separate from economic development—they are essential to it.
Our seasonal economy brings tremendous opportunity, but it also presents challenges.
Strengthening the infrastructure and services people depend on will help existing businesses succeed, encourage responsible economic growth, and create more stable, year-round opportunities.
As an entrepreneur and former business leader, I understand the challenges involved in building an organization, managing limited resources, solving problems, and adapting to changing circumstances.
I will listen to local employers, workers, residents, and municipal leaders and work across the aisle to advance practical solutions that strengthen both our economy and our quality of life.
Strong local businesses and dependable infrastructure go hand in hand.
Supporting both will help Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro remain vibrant places to live, work, raise a family, retire, and build a future.
As your State Representative, I will work to: Support locally owned businesses and policies that encourage responsible economic growth and more year-round employment opportunities.
Listen to small-business owners and address unnecessary state-level obstacles that make it harder to operate, grow, and create jobs.
Advocate for safe, well-maintained roads, bridges, and other infrastructure essential to residents, employers, workers, and visitors.
Support local police, fire departments, emergency medical services, and other first responders who keep our communities safe.
Protect access to healthcare, childcare, and elder services so families can work, seniors can remain in their communities, and employers can attract and retain workers.
Help towns and local organizations secure state resources for infrastructure and essential-service improvements.
Bring business, municipal, and community leaders together to identify shared needs and develop practical solutions that can earn bipartisan support.
The weathered pilings along our shoreline remind us of what makes our communities strong.
Each stands on its own, but bound together, they are strong enough to weather the storms and steady enough to meet changing waters.
That same strength comes from neighbors working together—listening to one another, finding common ground, and remaining firmly anchored in the values we share.
