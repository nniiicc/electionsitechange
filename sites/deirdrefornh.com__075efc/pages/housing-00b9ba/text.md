Young families should have the opportunity to put down roots, and seniors should be able to remain close to the friends, services, and communities they know.
Yet too many local workers struggle to find housing within reach, young families are priced out, and longtime residents have few options when they are ready to downsize.
This affects more than individual households—it makes it harder for schools, healthcare providers, public safety departments, and local businesses to attract and retain the people they need.
I support locally driven efforts to create more housing people can afford while respecting property rights, using existing infrastructure wisely, and keeping important land-use decisions in local hands.
There is no single solution for every town.
Progress will require listening to residents, working with municipalities and local employers, and finding practical approaches suited to Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro.
My experience in business, education, conflict resolution, and community leadership has prepared me to bring people with different perspectives together, negotiate thoughtfully, and work across the aisle toward balanced solutions.
As your State Representative, I will work to: Support housing options that meet the needs of local workers, young families, and seniors.
Keep important planning and land-use decisions in local hands rather than imposing one-size-fits-all mandates from Concord.
Respect private property rights and the distinct character and needs of Wolfeboro and Tuftonboro.
Encourage thoughtful development in areas where roads, water, and other infrastructure can support it.
Help towns access state resources and incentives for locally supported housing initiatives.
Work with municipalities, employers, housing organizations, and residents to identify practical solutions that strengthen our communities for the long term.
The weathered pilings along our shoreline remind us of what makes our communities strong.
Each stands on its own, but bound together, they are strong enough to weather the storms and steady enough to meet changing waters.
That same strength comes from neighbors working together—listening to one another, finding common ground, and remaining firmly anchored in the values we share.
