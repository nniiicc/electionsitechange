Deirdre McEachern is an entrepreneur, educator, and community leader who believes government should bring people together to solve problems and strengthen local communities.
A first-generation American and graduate of public schools, she worked her way through college before building a successful career in technology, co-founding a 25+ year New Hampshire business, and later serving as a Master Certified Career and Small Business Coach, Adjunct Professor of Sociology, and Head of School.
She holds a Master's degree in Conflict Resolution from Trinity College Dublin and is a NH-trained Peacekeeper.
Deeply committed to her community, Deirdre is running as a Democrat for State Representative to bring practical leadership to Concord.

Strong public schools
Protect excellent public schools, strengthen constructive partnerships between families and educators, and ensure resources support effective teaching and strong student outcomes.
Require the state to meet its constitutional responsibility for education so our schools can thrive without placing an unfair burden on local property taxpayers.
Reasonable Property Taxes—Without a Broad-Based Sales or Income Tax
Reduce pressure on local property taxpayers by requiring the state to meet its responsibilities to schools and towns, spending responsibly at the state level, and opposing tax breaks that favor the ultra-wealthy and multinational corporations over New Hampshire families and communities.
Attainable housing that workers, families, and seniors can afford
Support locally driven efforts to expand housing that workers, young families, and seniors can afford—while respecting property rights, using existing infrastructure wisely, and keeping important land-use decisions in local hands.
Protect Our Lakes, Land, and Rural Character
Safeguard lake water, drinking water, shoreland, forests, and open spaces through practical, locally informed policies that respect property owners and protect the natural resources, rural character, and quality of life on which our communities and economy depend.
Support Local Businesses and Essential Infrastructure
Help local businesses succeed and create more year-round opportunities by supporting the roads, broadband, public safety, healthcare, childcare, and elder services that residents, employers, and workers depend upon.
