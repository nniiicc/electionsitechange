MY PLATFORM
Energy Affordability
Quality of life in Alaska begins with the availability and affordability of energy.
We need representatives who will identify and fight for practical solutions for families and businesses.
An all-of-the-above strategy that responsibly develops our resources is the best way to lower energy costs and keep more money in people’s pockets.
Strengthen Education
Every parent wants their child to be as proficient as possible when they graduate from school.
We must advocate for policies that strengthen academic achievement, support teachers, and ensure parents have a strong voice in their children's education.
We need to reconnect parents with schools by protecting parental rights and supporting real choice for families for the students' success.
Limited Government
Government should be accountable to the people it serves.
Public trust depends on transparency, responsibility, and clear limits on government authority.
I believe government works best when it focuses on its essential responsibilities, respects individual freedoms, and remains accountable to the people.
Pro-Life & Family
Every life has inherent value and dignity.
We should advocate for values that respect life and human dignity from beginning to end.
I am pro-life and will advocate for policies that support mothers, protect life, and strengthen families.
PFD
Alaskans deserve a government that follows its own laws.
Public trust depends on consistency and accountability in government.
I will advocate for policies that honor Alaska law and pay the PFD according to the formula in state statute.
Stable Budget
Our government should live within its means just as we do, individuals, families, and businesses.
Alaskans deserve responsible budgeting that protects essential services without creating unnecessary burdens.
I will advocate for balanced budgets, local tax caps, and State spending caps to ensure Alaskans get the responsible budgeting and stewardship of taxpayer dollars they deserve without sacrificing essential services.
Pro 2nd Amendment
The right to bear arms shall not be infringed
