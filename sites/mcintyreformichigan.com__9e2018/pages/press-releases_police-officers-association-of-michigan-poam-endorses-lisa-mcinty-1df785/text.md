“I am extremely honored to announce the endorsement of the Police Officers Association of Michigan .
Receiving the support of POAM is a powerful vote of confidence in our shared vision for Northville, Plymouth and western Livonia in HD22,” said Lisa McIntyre .
“Public safety isn't just about responding to crises—it's about proactive school safety initiatives, supporting officers navigating high-stress mental health calls, and guaranteeing dedicated trauma care for our first responders.
I am committed to being a strong, reliable partner for law enforcement in Lansing.” ### About Lisa McIntyre: Lisa McIntyre is the former President of the Northville Board of Education and a licensed mental health counselor.
She’s running for State Representative to bring compassionate leadership focused on strong schools and support for families.
As a mother and community advocate, Lisa is dedicated to achieving real results for kids and working families in Michigan.
About Police Officers Association of Michigan (POAM): The Police Officers Association of Michigan (POAM) is a full-service labor organization representing over 12,000 law enforcement professionals across more than 300 local police and county sheriff departments throughout the state.
Dedicated to protecting those who protect Michigan communities, POAM provides comprehensive legal, legislative, and collective bargaining representation to promote public safety and support the well-being of officers and their families.
For more information, visit poam.net .
