Cocktails & Conversations - Meet Lisa McIntyre
Join us for an evening of cocktails and conversation as we bring together neighbors, friends, and supporters from House District 22—including Northville, Plymouth, and western Livonia.
Meet Lisa McIntyre, who is running to serve as State Representative for Michigan's House District 22.
As Treasurer of the Northville Board of Education and a licensed mental health counselor, Lisa has dedicated her career to serving our community and is ready to bring that experience to Lansing.
This relaxed meet and greet is a chance to get to know Lisa, connect with fellow supporters, ask questions, and learn how she'll fight to make life more affordable, strengthen our public schools, protect our clean air and water, and ensure everyone has access to quality, affordable health care.
Whether you're just getting involved or have been with us from the beginning, we'd love to have you join us.
Together, we can strengthen our community, build momentum across House District 22, and win this November!
Please note: The event location will be shared with registered guests approximately 24 hours before the event
