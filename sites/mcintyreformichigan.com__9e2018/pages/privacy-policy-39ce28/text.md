Privacy Policy
Effective date: August 12, 2025
Campaign: Friends of Lisa McIntyre for Michigan
Contact: info@mcintyreformichigan.com
Lisa McIntyre for Michigan House District 22 (“the Campaign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, protect, and share information collected through our website, email communications, events, donations, volunteer activities, and text-message program.
Information We Collect
We may collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, mobile phone number, mailing address, ZIP code, donation information, volunteer interests, event RSVPs, survey responses, and other information you choose to share.
We may also collect limited technical information when you visit our website, such as IP address, device or browser information, pages visited, and website interactions, through cookies or similar technologies.
How We Use Information
We may use your information to:
- Communicate about the Campaign, events, volunteer opportunities, policy updates, and fundraising.
- Process donations and comply with campaign-finance reporting obligations.
- Respond to questions, requests, or support needs.
- Improve Campaign communications, outreach, and website functionality.
- Maintain records, prevent fraud, and comply with applicable law.
SMS/Text Messaging
If you opt in to receive text messages from the Campaign, you agree to receive recurring autodialed or automated political, informational, volunteer, event, and fundraising text messages from Friends of Lisa McIntyre for Michigan at the mobile number you provide.
Message frequency varies, including during active campaign periods, and may be up to 1 message per week or month.
Message and data rates may apply.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of making a donation, volunteering, receiving services, or otherwise supporting the Campaign.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message.
For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at info@mcintyreformichigan or 248-702-4406.
After opting out, you may receive one final confirmation message.
How We Share Information
We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate the Campaign, such as website hosts, payment processors, email platforms, texting platforms, data-security providers, and professional advisors.
We may also disclose information when required by law, including campaign-finance reporting requirements, or when necessary to protect the Campaign, supporters, or the public.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are not sold, rented, traded, or shared with third parties for their own marketing or promotional use.
Data Security and Retention
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information.
No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for Campaign operations, legal compliance, recordkeeping, and the purposes described in this policy.
Your Choices
You may unsubscribe from Campaign emails by using the unsubscribe link included in those emails.
You may opt out of text messages by replying STOP.
To update or request deletion of other information, contact us at info@mcintyreformichigan.com.
Some information may need to be retained to meet legal, regulatory, reporting, or recordkeeping requirements.
Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The effective date at the top will reflect the most recent version.
Continued use of our website or participation in Campaign communications after an update constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
