Planned Parenthood of Michigan endorses Lisa McIntyre for State Representative
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Wednesday, July 8, 2026
Contact: Sofia Camacho, sofia@mcintyreformichigan.com, (248) 702-4426
NORTHVILLE, MI — Lisa McIntyre today issued the following statement after receiving the endorsement of the Planned Parenthood Advocates of Michigan in the race for Michigan House District 22.
“Planned Parenthood Advocates of Michigan are proud to endorse Lisa,” said Paula Thornton Greear, PPAM CEO.
“Lisa understands what’s at stake for people across Michigan and has demonstrated a strong commitment to defending reproductive rights and access to care.
This is a critical moment, and Lisa is the right choice for it.”
“I am honored to be recognized by Planned Parenthood Advocates as a Sexual Reproductive and Health Champion for my campaign for Michigan House District 22,” said Lisa McIntyre.
“As a mental health counselor, I see every day how access to comprehensive, evidence-based sexual health care supports not just physical health, but emotional well-being, stability, and dignity.
I’ll continue working to ensure our communities have compassionate, informed care and the resources people need to make decisions about their own health.”
###
About Lisa McIntyre:
Lisa McIntyre is the former President of the Northville Board of Education and a licensed mental health counselor.
She’s running for State Representative to bring compassionate leadership focused on strong schools and support for families.
As a mother and community advocate, Lisa is dedicated to achieving real results for kids in Michigan.
