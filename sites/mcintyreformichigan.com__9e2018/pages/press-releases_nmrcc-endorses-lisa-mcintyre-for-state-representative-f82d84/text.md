“Lisa McIntyre has shown strength and support for the community in the past, and we’re proud to continue supporting her now,” said Eli Isaguirre, Political and Legislative Director, Northern Midwest Regional Council of Carpenters (NMRCC).
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Northern Midwest Regional Council of Carpenters (NMRCC) for my campaign for Michigan House District 22,” said Lisa McIntyre .
“Their dedication to skilled trades, fair wages, and building strong communities is essential to Michigan’s future.
I look forward to working alongside them to support workforce development, expand apprenticeship opportunities, and ensure every worker has the chance to succeed.” The dedicated and hard-working members of the Northern Midwest Regional Council of Carpenters (NMRCC) are fully committed to supporting candidates who consistently advocate for their interests and those of working families across Michigan.
By advocating for the values and needs of these communities, we ensure that their perspectives are represented in the political sphere.
### About Lisa McIntyre: Lisa McIntyre is the former President of the Northville Board of Education and a licensed mental health counselor.
She’s running for State Representative to bring compassionate leadership focused on strong schools and support for families.
As a mother and community advocate, Lisa is dedicated to achieving real results for kids in Michigan.
