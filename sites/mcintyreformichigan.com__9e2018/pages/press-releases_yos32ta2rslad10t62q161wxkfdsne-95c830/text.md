“I’m honored to earn the endorsement of SEIU Michigan,” said Lisa McIntyre .
“As a neighbor in House District 22, I’ve seen how healthcare workers, home care providers, and service employees keep our communities running—often without the pay, benefits, or respect they deserve.
This endorsement is a commitment that if elected in November, I’ll fight for living wages, safe staffing, and the right to organize.
When working people win, Northville, Plymouth, and Livonia win.” ### About Lisa McIntyre: Lisa McIntyre is the former President of the Northville Board of Education and a licensed mental health counselor.
She’s running for State Representative to bring compassionate leadership focused on strong schools and support for families.
As a mother and community advocate, Lisa is dedicated to achieving real results for kids in Michigan.
About SEIU: SEIU Healthcare Michigan represents tens of thousands of healthcare and home care workers across the state, including hospital, nursing-home, and Home Help caregivers.
In 2025, nearly 32,000 Michigan home care workers voted to join SEIU Healthcare Michigan in one of the largest union elections in the U.S. since 1999, launching contract campaigns focused on better pay, benefits, training, and safe, dignified care for clients and workers alike.
