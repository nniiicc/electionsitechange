Typography
Montserrat is a geometric sans-serif typeface with high readability used for all campaign headlines.
We use the semibold weight of Montserrat.
Montserrat was developed by Julieta Ulanovsky and can be downloaded for free here.
Our body copy font is Omnes, most often in regular weight.
Omnes was developed by Joshua Darden and is available on Adobe Fonts.
