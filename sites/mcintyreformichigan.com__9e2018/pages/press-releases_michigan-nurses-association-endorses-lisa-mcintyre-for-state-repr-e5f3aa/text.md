Michigan Nurses Association endorses Lisa McIntyre for State Representative
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Monday, August 3, 2026
Contact: Sofia Camacho, sofia@mcintyreformichigan.com, (248) 702-4426
NORTHVILLE, MI — Lisa McIntyre today issued the following statement after receiving the endorsement of the Michigan Nurses Association in the race for Michigan House District 22.
“I’m honored to receive the endorsement of the Michigan Nurses Association,” said Lisa McIntyre.
Nurses are on the front lines of our health care system every day, and their advocacy for patients, safe staffing, and healthy communities is essential.
I’m proud to stand with Michigan nurses and will continue fighting for policies that protect public health, strengthen our workforce, and make sure every family has access to the care they deserve.”
###
About Lisa McIntyre:
Lisa McIntyre is the former President of the Northville Board of Education and a licensed mental health counselor.
She’s running for State Representative to bring compassionate leadership focused on strong schools and support for families.
As a mother and community advocate, Lisa is dedicated to achieving real results for kids and working families in Michigan.
About Michigan Nurses Association:
As the largest union and professional association for registered nurses and healthcare professionals in Michigan, MNA is proud to support candidates who share our values and are committed to advocating for safe patient care, safe workplaces, and strong collective bargaining rights.
