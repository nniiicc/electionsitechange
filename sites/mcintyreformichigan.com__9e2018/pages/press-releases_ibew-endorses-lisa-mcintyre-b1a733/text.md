IBEW Local 58 Endorses Lisa McIntyre for State Representative
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Monday, August 10, 2026
Contact: Sofia Camacho, sofia@mcintyreformichigan.com, (248) 702-4426
NORTHVILLE, MI — Lisa McIntyre today issued the following statement after receiving the endorsement of the International Brothers of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 58 in the race for Michigan House District 22.
“I am honored to be recognized by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers for my campaign for Michigan House District 22,” said Lisa McIntyre.
“Their members do essential work that keeps our communities running, and I’m proud to stand with the working families they represent as we campaign for a stronger future in HD22 and across the state of Michigan.”
###
About Lisa McIntyre:
Lisa McIntyre is the former President of the Northville Board of Education and a licensed mental health counselor.
She’s running for State Representative to bring compassionate leadership focused on strong schools and support for families.
As a mother and community advocate, Lisa is dedicated to achieving real results for kids and working families in Michigan.
