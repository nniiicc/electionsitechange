Stand up for McIntyre for Michgan House
Comedy Night Fundraiser
Location: Genitti’s Hole in the Wall (Northville)
Date: Thursday, June 25
Time: 6:30pm
Politics is serious work—our paychecks, our kids’ schools, our health care, and our neighborhoods all depend on the choices we make in Lansing.
But that doesn’t mean the work to get those choices right can’t be fun.
I’m running a campaign that’s grounded in the real issues families face every day: wages, benefits, schools, mental health, and public safety.
But I also believe that building community, laughing together, and sharing a good time are part of the path forward.
When we’re together—in our neighborhoods, at fundraisers, and on the campaign trail—we’re reminded that our strength is in our connection to each other.
That’s why we are hosting a Comedy Night Fundraiser for McIntyre for Michigan.
It’s a chance to have a great night out, support my candidacy for Michigan House, and help build a future where our families come first.
