Sep 12, 2025
GEORGETOWN, TX – Today, Dr.
Charles Schwertner announced his initial endorsement list for re-election to Texas Senate District 5.
Schwertner has earned endorsements from President Donald J.
Trump, Governor Greg Abbott, Lieutenant Governor Dan Patrick, and 73 elected officials, grassroots activists, and community and business leaders who have united in support of his conservative leadership in the Texas Senate.
“I am honored to have the support of President Trump, Governor Abbott, Lieutenant Governor Patrick, and more than 70 elected officials and community leaders in my re-election campaign for the Texas Senate,” said Schwertner.
“These endorsements are not just a vote of confidence in my conservative record and leadership, but a shared commitment to protecting our values and keeping Texas strong so that it remains the greatest state in the country.
As your State Senator, I will continue to work hard and deliver conservative results that improve the lives of all families in this district.”
First elected to the Texas House of Representatives in 2011 and later to the Texas Senate in 2013, Schwertner has earned a reputation as one of the most effective and conservative members of the Texas Senate.
He has authored numerous key laws that protect the Second Amendment, strengthen border security and the enforcement of our immigration laws, defend private property rights, and cut taxes.
In President Donald J.
Trump’s endorsement statement, he said, "I am honored to give you, Charles Schwertner, my Complete and Total Endorsement in the upcoming Re-Election.
Together, we will MAKE AMERICA GREAT AGAIN!"
Governor Greg Abbott and Lieutenant Governor Dan Patrick both highlighted Schwertner’s proven conservative leadership in their endorsements.
“His conservative leadership on issues like border security will keep Texans safe!,” said Governor Greg Abbott.
“Senator Charles Schwertner is a rock solid conservative and bold leader in the Texas Senate,” said Lieutenant Governor Dan Patrick.
“I appointed him as Chair of the powerful Business and Commerce Committee.
He has done a remarkable job overseeing important business legislation to keep Texas the #1 job creator in the country year after year.
As Lt.
Governor and President of the Senate he has my full and complete endorsement."
Schwertner has also received endorsements from Land Commissioner Dawn Buckingham and every Republican State Representative in the district, including Representative Trey Wharton, Representative Angelia Orr, Representative Terry Wilson, Representative Paul Dyson, Representative Caroline Harris Davila, Representative Stan Gerdes, and Representative Janis Holt.

About Dr.
Charles Schwertner: Dr.
Schwertner’s late father was a decorated Air Force officer, and his mother is a retired mental health nurse.
He is a proud 6th-generation Texan from a family of farmers, ranchers, and landowners.
In addition to his role in the Texas Legislature, Dr.
Schwertner works as a practicing orthopedic surgeon, treating thousands of patients each year.
He and his wife, Belinda, reside in Georgetown and have three sons: Carson, Zachary, and Matthew.
