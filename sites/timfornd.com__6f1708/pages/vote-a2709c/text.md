VOTE BY MAIL Voting by mail is easy and secure in North Dakota.
Complete a simple Absentee Ballot Application and your official ballot will be mailed straight to you.
1 Request Your Absentee Ballot Application Download and print an Absentee Ballot Application by clicking here or request one in person from the Ward County Auditor/Treasurer's Office between 8 a.m. and 4:30 p.m.
Monday through Friday.
2 Complete & Sign the Application Fill out, sign and mail your application to: Ward County Auditor's Office PO Box 5005 Minot, ND 58702 Ballots may also be dropped in the Secure Dropbox in front of the Ward County Administration Building and 2nd floor of the Auditor/Treasurer's Office at 225 3rd St SE in Minot.
The dropbox will be available 24 hours a day from April 30th through June 9th at 7 p.m.
