Along with many of my neighbors in Franklin County, I have learned a lot about data centers this year.
Not one, but 2 hyperscale data centers have been proposed within HD 119.
These would take up hundreds of acres of farm land and have left the people of my district with many questions.
The county commissioners who are supposed to look out for the good of the citizens have been slow to listen and still have not provided a time for questions and answers.
As citizens ask about their safety, environmental impacts, financial impacts, and the way of life they expected when choosing to live in Franklin County the commissioners remain quiet.
My race is a state race, and currently the fight is a local one.
Unfortunately, we are not alone in this fight.
Data centers are trying to force their way in across the state.
This is complex issue which calls for more study.
Ideally, Missouri would declare a moratorium on data centers so that we all can learn more about the pros and cons.
However, with the heavy support from the governor and some lawmakers, a moratorium seems unlikely.
If elected, a priority would be to sponsor or support a bill at the state level which would provide some basic protection or Missourians who find themselves fighting to be heard by local officials.
Ideally there would be legislation to keep the citizens from paying increased utility bills to cover expansion of the grid required by the data centers, protect our water both from pollution and depletion, and protection from pollution including light and noise pollution.
