Smith campaigns in Ocean County, speaks at Turning Point Rally: “Vote for Jack” Excerpts by Rep.
Chris Smith (R-Manchester) Turning Point Rally for Jack Ciattarelli November 2, 2025 The great Dodger manager Tommy Lasorda once said “There are three kinds of people in this world: people who make it happen, people who watch what happens, and people who wonder what happened.” Like Turning Point’s great leader Charlie Kirk, Jack Ciattarelli makes things happen.
He’s a doer.
Jack’s an amazing leader, with a bold and achievable vision to revitalize New Jersey after 8 long years of chaos and misrule.
He is honest, dynamic and absolutely committed to each and every one of you.
Jack will: end the lawlessness of Murphy and Sherrell’s sanctuary state, make New Jersey affordable, fight to lower the unconscionable high tax burden on everyone living in our great state, support both private and public education without imposing the woke agenda on our kids, lower energy costs, end the misguided ocean wind experiment that not only will drive up energy costs to every consumer, kill whales and eviscerate our ocean environment but is extremely dangerous because it degrades and distorts marine and aviation radar, strongly support law enforcement Like President Trump, both Jack and his running mate Sherriff Jim Gannon will always have the backs of those women and men in uniform who put their lives on the line every day to protect our communities.
The fact that Congresswoman Sherrill voted to end qualified immunity is outrageous and puts every cop on the beat at risk.
The fact that Ms.
Sherrill refuses to release her records during the infamous 1994 Annapolis cheating scandal—and my House colleagues Jeff Van Drew, Tom Kean and I have publicly called on her to do so—begs the question: what is she hiding.
Ms.
Sherrill is AWOL in Washington and failed to vote on the most important national security legislation—the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA).
As Ronald Reagan succinctly put it—it’s time for a change.
Jack Ciattarelli for Governor!
It’s time.
You and I need to make it happen.
Get everyone you know to vote for Jack.
