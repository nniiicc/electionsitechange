ABOUT CLAY
Clay is a lifelong resident of Pickens County and currently lives in Pickens with his wife Krista.
A 2011 graduate of Liberty High School, he went on to obtain a degree in accounting from Clemson University.
Clay is employed by Sealevel Systems in Liberty as a sales manager.
His passion for our community is proven by his service in a wide range of areas.
At Liberty High School, Clay started a local news website with a few friends called “Liberty Speaks." The goal of this website was to improve visibility on local issues.
Clay has previously served as treasurer of the Liberty Area Chamber and was an original member of the revived Pickens County Recreation board.
Clay has also served on the US Selective Service System appeals board.
He recently retired as a member of the Pickens County Planning Commission and serves as the current president of the Liberty Lions Club.
Clay remains actively involved at his church Pickens First Baptist helping as an Awana teacher.
In his free time, Clay enjoys fishing, hunting, and farming.
