What exactly is a Fisher?
Fishers are medium-sized carnivorous mammals found in North America, particularly in forested areas like District 2B.
They are members of the weasel (not cat) family.
Known for their agility and strength, they primarily hunt small to medium-sized animals, including squirrels and rabbits - but not fish.
They are one of the few predators of porcupines.
