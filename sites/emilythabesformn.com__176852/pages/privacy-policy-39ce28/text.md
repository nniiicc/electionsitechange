Privacy Policy
Emily Thabes for House District 2B | Last updated: April 2026
Your privacy matters to us.
This policy explains what information we collect when you visit this website, how we use it, and how we protect it.
Who We Are
This website is operated by Emily Thabes for House District 2B, a Minnesota state legislative campaign committee.
You can reach us at emily@emilythabesformn.com, or by mail at PO Box 93, Shevlin, MN 56676.
Information We Collect
We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:
- Sign up for updates or contact us.
We collect your name, email address, and any message you send.
We use this to communicate with you about the campaign.
- Volunteer.
In addition to contact information, we may ask for your availability, skills, or location so we can connect you with opportunities to get involved.
- Make a donation.
Donation processing is handled by a third-party payment processor.
We collect the information required by Minnesota campaign finance law, which may include your name, address, employer, and occupation for contributions above certain thresholds.
This information is reported to the Minnesota Campaign Finance and Public Disclosure Board (CFPB) as required by law and becomes part of the public record.
How We Use Your Information
We use the information you provide to send campaign updates and event information, coordinate volunteer opportunities, process and report contributions as required by law, and respond to your messages.
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
We do not use your information for any commercial purpose.
Email Communications
If you sign up for email updates, you can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us directly.
We will honor all opt-out requests promptly.
Sharing Your Information
We may share your information with trusted service providers who help us operate this website and communicate with supporters — such as our email platform or payment processor — under strict confidentiality agreements.
We may also disclose information when required to do so by law, including mandatory campaign finance disclosures.We may share your contact information with the Minnesota DFL Party and affiliated political organizations for campaign-related purposes.
If you would prefer we not do so, contact us and we will honor that request.
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
We encourage you to be thoughtful about what personal information you share online.
Your Rights
You may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us at emily@emilythabesformn.com.
