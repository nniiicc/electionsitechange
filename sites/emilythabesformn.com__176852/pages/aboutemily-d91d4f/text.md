Hello/Boozhoo, my name is Emily
I look forward to meeting you.
I grew up in rural West Virginia and moved to Minnesota for a career in higher education and library leadership over two decades ago.
I moved to Bagley in 2020 and knew almost immediately that I was home - not just where I'd landed, but where I wanted to stay.
The land and the people felt more familiar to me than anywhere I'd lived in a long time.
I chose this place.
Showing up and committing to my neighbors is how I live that choice.
For the past five years I've served as Executive Director of the Beltrami County Historical Society, where I've secured state and federal grants, established award-winning programs, and built partnerships across tribal, county, regional, and state lines.
I am most proud of the work we've done preserving our collections to industry standards so that our children's children's children can access and learn from the history our ancestors treasured.
I think in generations, not election cycles.
