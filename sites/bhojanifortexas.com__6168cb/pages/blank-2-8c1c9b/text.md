Religious freedom is a fundamental right guaranteed to every American regardless of faith and practice.
As the first Muslim elected to the Texas Legislature, I believe true religious freedom has two pillars: free practice and zero state favoritism.
- Passed legislation ensuring state-mandated tests are not administered on religious holy days
- Founded the bicameral Religious Freedom Caucus to advocate for the right for Texans to practice their faith
- Hosted numerous events bringing together religious freedom leaders from 9 faith traditions with 50+ elected officials in attendance
I will advocate that the Texas Legislature:
- Establish marriage parity so a leader of any faith can legally officiate a wedding
- Protect every Texan's right to practice, or not practice, free from government coercion or favoritism
- Work to ensure that each faith and religion is recognized without being treated differently from the government
