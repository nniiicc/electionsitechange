Texas ranks a dismal 46th in the nation in per student funding, trailing by roughly $4,000 than the national average.
With inflation climbing and costs rising, public school budgets are being stretched to the breaking point.
As a father to two Texas-educated children, I understand intimately that investing in our children is an investment in our future.
- Guaranteed alternative testing dates for students with exceptions for religious holidays
- Championed critical financial relief with the creation of the “College for Y’all” initiative
- Created a non-voting student position on public school boards
I will advocate that the Texas Legislature…
- Increase per-student funding to keep pace with inflation
- Expand career and technical education pathways that connect students directly to Texas’s workforce needs
