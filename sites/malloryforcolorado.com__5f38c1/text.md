Mallory Martin grew up in Colorado as the daughter of a construction worker and a teacher, and she learned early that hard work deserves respect—and a fair shot.
As a long-time resident of House District 54, she's seen what rural families are up against: rising costs, limited access to healthcare, and a government that too often ignores the people it's supposed to serve.
With a background in healthcare and community advocacy, Mallory has spent her career fighting for real solutions that help everyday families—not the special interests.
She believes in common-sense policies that protect access to care, support local workers, and strengthen rural communities from the inside out.
Mallory knows rural Colorado deserves leaders who understand our way of life.
She's ready to be a strong, independent voice for HD 54 in the state legislature—one that listens to the people, fights for our values, and keeps Colorado's small towns thriving.
