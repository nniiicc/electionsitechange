NEW LEADERSHIP DELIVERING RESULTS FOR OKLAHOMA I'm a life long resident of Oklahoma.
I was born and raised in Tahlequah.
In 2012, I married my wife, Joie, and together we live in Fort Gibson with our Chihuahua, BB.
I was blessed with a "ready-made" family, as Joie had three children from a previous marriage.
Although all of our children are grown and out of our home, I couldn't love them more if they were my own.
We have five grandsons that we enjoy spending time with as much as possible! ​ Growing up in Tahlequah, I spent many hours helping my dad and step-dad on their small ranches raising cattle and horses.
I also helped my mom keep up with the house as she worked full-time while going to college to become a nurse, all while raising my siblings and I.
These years taught me the value of education and hard work.
I played many sports growing up, and was instilled with grit and perseverance.
After graduating from Hulbert High School, I attended Murray State College and I played baseball there.
After an injury, I came back to Tahlequah and attended Northeastern State University. ​ I started my current business CSI: Chris Sneed Insurance in 2011.
I am an independent agent who specializes in Medicare products, life insurance and annuities.
I enjoy helping my clients during their transition into retirement.
I feel as though I have been serving and helping others my entire life, and I am looking forward continuing to serve you as your State House Representative.
