As of June 2026, Eli is the Democratic and Working Families Party nominee for NY Assembly District 69.
There are no Republican or Independent candidates running for AD 69 this election cycle.
Eli’s name will appear on the ballot under both the Democratic Party and the Working Families Party, alongside a write-in option.
In order to become the Assemblymember-elect, a plurality of voters must vote for Eli on either party line.

How do I vote by mail?
Any registered voter in NYC can vote by mail.
You can request your mail-in ballot in person or online: Request online here by October 24 Request in-person at the New York County Board of Elections office by November 2: 200 Varick Street 10th Floor New York, NY 10014 ^ If you will be out of town in early November, make sure to request your mail-in ballot early and mail it in as soon as possible How do I request an accessible ballot?
If you are a voter with a disability, you can request an accessible ballot here by October 24 or in-person at the New York County Board of Elections office by November 2: 200 Varick Street 10th Floor New York, NY 10014 How do I return my mail-in ballot?
You have 4 options to return your mail-in ballot: Mail it using the pre-paid and pre-addressed return envelope Drop it off at the New York County Board of Elections office: 200 Varick Street 10th Floor New York, NY 10014 Drop it at an early voting site in Manhattan between October 24 - November 1 Drop it at an election day poll site in Manhattan by 9pm on November 3 Track your ballot status here What will be on my ballot?
General elections may include: State Assembly (like AD69) Congress (NY-12 and NY-13) State Committee (local Democratic party positions) Check out what will be on your ballot here .
How does early voting work?
Early voting in NYC is from October 24 - November 1.
