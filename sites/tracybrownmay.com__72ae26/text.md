WHY TRACY
Tracy Brown-May is someone who truly understands what it means to serve her community.
With a career rooted in helping others and bringing people together, she has spent decades working to improve the lives of families across Nevada.
Whether it’s advocating for better services, supporting education and healthcare, or standing up for those who often go unheard, Tracy leads with compassion, integrity, and a genuine commitment to doing what’s right.
She listens, she shows up, and she works hard to deliver real results.
Tracy isn’t just a leader—she’s a neighbor, a problem-solver, and a voice you can trust.
