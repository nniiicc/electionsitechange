We are proud to announce that Congresswoman Ayanna Pressley has endorsed Jimmy Gomez for Congress!
“Jimmy Gomez has been a champion for the people of California’s 34th District.
I’m proud to serve with him on the House Oversight and Reform Committee – where we’ve fought to hold this rogue White House accountable to the people – and to work alongside him to fight for Medicare for All, to protect our immigrant communities, to end police brutality, and to support our workers.
In this election – along with taking back the White House and the Senate – we have the opportunity to re-elect leaders like Jimmy, who will fight tirelessly on behalf of our communities.” -Rep.
