BIG News!
Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez endorsed Jimmy Gomez for U.S.
Representative!
Congresswoman Ocasio-Cortez said:
“Jimmy Gomez is a progressive champion for working people.
He understands our fights because he’s been there—he’s the son of immigrants, grew up uninsured, worked his way through school, spent years as a union organizer and rejects corporate PAC money.
Since day one, Jimmy has been an outspoken leader in the fight for Medicare for All and the Green New Deal, and I’ve stood with Jimmy and other activists as we protested overnight on the Capitol steps to successfully extend the eviction moratorium to protect renters.
Now, we’re working on legislation to lower the cost of housing and address our nation’s affordability and rental crises.
I wholeheartedly endorse Jimmy Gomez because we need him in the House as a progressive, effective fighter for working people.”
