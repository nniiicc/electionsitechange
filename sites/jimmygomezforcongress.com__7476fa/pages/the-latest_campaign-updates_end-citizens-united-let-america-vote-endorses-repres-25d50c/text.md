Jimmy Gomez for Re-election By: Jimmy Gomez Share End Citizens United // Let America Vote Endorses Representative Jimmy Gomez for Re-election End Citizens United // Let America Vote today endorsed Representative Jimmy Gomez for Congress in California’s 34th Congressional District.
As part of the endorsement, Gomez unveiled a comprehensive reform agenda aimed at getting Big Money out of politics, strengthening ethics rules, and protecting the freedom to vote.
“Representative Jimmy Gomez is a proven champion of democracy, fighting to fix our broken system while standing up for working families in California,” said Tiffany Muller, President of End Citizens United // Let America Vote .
“Representative Gomez believes that democracy can only be truly representative when all voices are heard in our government—not just the wealthy elite.
It’s why he is refusing to take a penny from corporate PACs and making democracy reform a cornerstone of his campaign.
We’re proud to endorse Representative Gomez and look forward to helping him win.” “At a time of deep mistrust in our democratic institutions, we have to increase transparency and accountability, and raise the standard for candidates and elected officials— that’s why I am pledging to not take any money from corporate PACs for my campaign,” said Representative Jimmy Gomez .
“I’m proud to be endorsed by End Citizens United // Let America Vote, who are leaders in protecting our freedom to vote and strengthening our democracy.
I look forward to working alongside them to root out corruption and strengthen the trust that voters expect and deserve from those in elected office.” Representative Gomez’s Democracy Reform Agenda Stop Big Corporations and Special Interests from Buying Political Influence: Gomez is refusing to accept money from corporate PACs and will work to pass legislation that bans corporate PACs.
Gomez will champion a constitutional amendment to overturn Citizens United .
Gomez will continue to fight to end dark money in our political system by passing the DISCLOSE Act , just like he did in the California legislature.
Strengthen Ethics Rules for Lawmakers: Gomez will fight to close the revolving door between Capitol Hill and K Street once and for all.
Gomez will fight to ban members of Congress from actively trading individual stocks to prevent insider trading and self-dealing.
Gomez will work to ban congressional offices from using taxpayer dollars for first class travel for lawmakers.
Protect the Freedom to Vote: Gomez will fight to pass the Freedom to Vote Act to protect access to the ballot, stop partisan gerrymandering, and ensure elections are safe, secure, and accessible.
Gomez will fight to pass the John R.
Lewis Voting Rights Advancement Act to restore the 1965 Voting Rights Act to its full strength and stop voter suppression in its tracks.
ECU // LAV is dedicated to combating the two biggest challenges facing our democracy: the corrosive impact of Big Money and attempts to block access to the ballot box.
Since its founding in 2015, the group has had over 1 million donors and raised $200 million, with an average donation of just $14.
The group has more than 4 million members nationwide.
