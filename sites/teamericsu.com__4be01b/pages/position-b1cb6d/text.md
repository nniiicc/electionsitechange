Healthcare: Supports expanding access to affordable healthcare and mental health services.
Education: Supports strong public schools, career and technical education, and parental involvement.
Open to practical reforms that improve student outcomes.

Abortion: Supports protecting reproductive rights while emphasizing healthcare access and support for families.
Gun Policy: Supports responsible gun ownership and common-sense safety measures to reduce gun violence.

Government Ethics: Supports stronger ethics laws, transparency, and accountability in Springfield.

Small Business: Supports reducing regulatory burdens and helping small businesses grow and hire locally.
