Issues That Matter In District 21
When I knocked doors across our community, families talked about the same concerns:
• The rising cost of living
• Property taxes
• Safer neighborhoods
• Strong schools
• Good jobs
Here’s where I stand and how I plan to help.
Property Taxes
Illinois homeowners are paying some of the highest property taxes in the country, forcing families and seniors out of the neighborhoods they love.
My Focus
Lower the burden on homeowners and bring transparency to local spending.
My Plan
• Increase the state's share of school funding so property taxes don't carry the entire load
• Cap runaway property tax assessments
• Audit local taxing bodies to eliminate waste
• Reward municipalities that consolidate services to save taxpayers money
A New Idea
Tax Transparency Dashboard
A simple online tool showing exactly where local tax dollars go.
Health Care
Too many families must choose between health care, groceries, and rent.
Affordable care and stronger preventive health programs.
• Protect Medicaid for children, seniors, and vulnerable residents
• Expand mental health services in schools and communities
• Lower prescription drug costs through bulk purchasing
• Expand telehealth for working families

Jobs
Too many people feel like they must leave Illinois to find good opportunities.
Support small businesses and create good-paying local jobs.
• Expand apprenticeship and trade programs
• Attract manufacturing, logistics, and clean energy jobs
• Support workers' rights to fair wages and benefits
• Simplify taxes and paperwork for small businesses

Education
Schools are too dependent on property taxes while teachers and families feel stretched.
Strong schools without overburdening homeowners.
• Fully fund public education
• Reduce property tax dependence for school funding
• Expand career and technical education programs
• Increase student mental health resources

Immigration

Integration, fairness, and economic opportunity.
• Protect families from discrimination
• Expand English language education
• Streamline professional licensing for immigrants
• Support safe and legal immigration pathways

Smart policing and strong community relationships.
• Support police training and mental health resources
• Invest in youth programs and mentorship
• Strengthen community policing
• Target illegal firearms and violent crime
