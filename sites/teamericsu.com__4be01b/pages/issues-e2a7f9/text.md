Of Happiness DONATE Donate Merch Volunteer Contact 🌐 Choose Your Language: Issues That Matter In District 21 When I knocked doors across our community, families talked about the same concerns: • The rising cost of living • Property taxes • Safer neighborhoods • Strong schools • Good jobs Here’s where I stand and how I plan to help.
Property Taxes Illinois homeowners are paying some of the highest property taxes in the country , forcing families and seniors out of the neighborhoods they love.
My Focus Lower the burden on homeowners and bring transparency to local spending.
My Plan • Increase the state's share of school funding so property taxes don't carry the entire load • Cap runaway property tax assessments • Audit local taxing bodies to eliminate waste • Reward municipalities that consolidate services to save taxpayers money A New Idea Tax Transparency Dashboard A simple online tool showing exactly where local tax dollars go.
Health Care Too many families must choose between health care, groceries, and rent.
My Focus Affordable care and stronger preventive health programs.
My Plan • Protect Medicaid for children, seniors, and vulnerable residents • Expand mental health services in schools and communities • Lower prescription drug costs through bulk purchasing • Expand telehealth for working families A New Idea Department of Happiness A pro people focused initiative to make wellness program more accessible.
Learn more here.
Jobs Too many people feel like they must leave Illinois to find good opportunities.
My Focus Support small businesses and create good-paying local jobs.
My Plan • Expand apprenticeship and trade programs • Attract manufacturing, logistics, and clean energy jobs • Support workers' rights to fair wages and benefits • Simplify taxes and paperwork for small businesses A New Idea Made in 21 Initiative Promote products and services created in District 21.
Education Schools are too dependent on property taxes while teachers and families feel stretched.
My Focus Strong schools without overburdening homeowners.
My Plan • Fully fund public education • Reduce property tax dependence for school funding • Expand career and technical education programs • Increase student mental health resources A New Idea Career Bus Program Mobile career labs introducing students to trades and local careers.
Immigration Immigrant families are a vital part of our community but often struggle with access to services and opportunity.
My Focus Integration, fairness, and economic opportunity.
My Plan • Protect families from discrimination • Expand English language education • Streamline professional licensing for immigrants • Support safe and legal immigration pathways A New Idea Fast-Track Licensing Task Force Help qualified immigrants quickly join the workforce.
Public Safety Residents want safer neighborhoods while maintaining trust between communities and law enforcement.
My Focus Smart policing and strong community relationships.
My Plan • Support police training and mental health resources • Invest in youth programs and mentorship • Strengthen community policing • Target illegal firearms and violent crime A New Idea Walk & Talk Wednesdays Monthly community walks with police and residents.
