Democratic State Representative Nathan Small is leading the way.
Nathan Small understands hard work.
He is a third-generation New Mexican from a family of teachers and ranchers.
His mother, the daughter of a rancher, was an educator at a New Mexico state prison and at several community colleges here.
Nathan grew up in El Cerro Mission in a single-wide trailer with a communal well.
Nathan is a lifelong outdoorsman with a passion for public service.
Elected to the Las Cruces City Council in 2007, Nathan led the effort to create the City of Las Cruces Sustainability Officer and to focus on economic development and care for existing neighborhoods.
Since 2016, Nathan has served Las Cruces in the State Legislature.
As Chair of the House Budget Committee, Nathan is focused on making our state budget work for families, and making sure it reflects the values of working families here.
Nathan doesn’t run from big fights.
He puts his head down and gets the job done.
Nathan Small’s Track Record for New Mexico’s Families
- Provided over $100 million to hire police and sheriff's officers
- Added $500 million for public schools
- Cut gross receipts tax
- Eliminated tax on Social Security
- Led bipartisan effort resulting in $54.7 billion in savings for the future
- Increased working families and child tax credits
- Provided millions to first-time homebuyers for down payment assistance
- Passed Healthy Soils Act
- Led bipartisan effort to save billions of gallons of freshwater annually
- Brought over 1,500 paid summer internships for students to Doña Ana County
PRIORITIES
Nathan’s priorities for the State Legislature are to:
- Reduce healthcare premiums and prescription drug prices; protect individuals with pre-existing conditions; and recruit new doctors, nurses, and dentists to Southern New Mexico.
- Fight price-gouging to protect consumers and continue growing and diversifying Southern New Mexico's economy.
- Hire more police officers and sheriff’s deputies; speed up trials; and expand drug treatment and mental health programs.
- Increase drought response measures that conserve water for farms and families while tapping new water sources through treatment and innovation.
- Safeguard reproductive rights for women, including keeping abortion safe and legal.
