Priorities
Across Howard County, residents are striving to improve their community and build a bright future for their families.
I'm running for State Delegate to improve the lives of the people in District 13, and I am committed to helping our community grow and thrive.
As your Delegate, my priorities will include:
Education
As a proud product of public schools who comes from a family of educators, Pam understands the importance of quality education for our future generations at every level of learning and development.
- Prioritize, recruit, and retain dedicated educators
- Champion universal Pre-K for our youngest learners
- Broaden access to higher education to equip learners with 21st century skills
Environment
Howard County prides itself on its incredible parks, open spaces, and environmental resources.
In the Maryland General Assembly, Pam will be a champion for safeguarding our natural resources, addressing climate change, and protecting our environment.
- Invest in renewable energy infrastructure to address climate change
- Safeguard the Chesapeake Bay through thoughtful resource allocation
- Protect our communities from flooding with smart stormwater management
Health Care
Pam will work to make healthcare more affordable for Maryland families by cutting the cost of prescription drugs, bolstering our public health agencies, and expanding access to essential mental healthcare services.
- Ensure access to quality healthcare, including reproductive healthcare, for all
- Lower the cost of prescription drugs for seniors and underinsured residents
- Continue pandemic recovery and strengthen our local health departments
- Expand access to affordable mental and behavioral health care resources
Safety & Stability
Pam has spent decades working to improve the lives of Howard County families through her work with various nonprofits.
As our State Delegate for District 13, Pam will continue this critical work to improve safety and community stability in Howard County.
- Ensure housing stability and accessibility for all
- Support small businesses recovering from the impacts of COVID-19
- Advocate for gun safety and secure storage requirements
