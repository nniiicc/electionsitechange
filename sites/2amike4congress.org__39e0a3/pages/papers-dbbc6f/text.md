Accountability Main paper An Independent External Audit of the United States Federal Government Executive summary Washington forces every public company, nonprofit, and state that touches federal dollars to submit to an independent outside audit — yet exempts itself, and hasn't earned a clean audit opinion in 28 years.
Make the government live by its own rule: an annual third-party audit that publishes the full balance sheet — unrecorded Social Security and Medicare obligations included — and a yearly, unfakeable measure of how far it stands from honest books.

Sound Money Main paper Ending the Hidden Tax and Restoring Honest Money Executive summary Inflation is a hidden tax no one voted for — the dollar has lost more than 96% of its value since 1913.
The rot began in 1792, when Congress first set the price of money by decree instead of letting the market discover it.
End the federal monopoly on money, restore honest banking, open the Fed's books, and repeal the legal-tender coercion that forces Americans to save in a currency designed to lose value.

Taxes Main paper Make Americans Wealthy Again: Stop Taxing Gains That Were Never Gains Executive summary The government debases the dollar, then taxes the inflation it manufactured as if it were profit — and since 1982 it has taxed your gains before returning your own principal.
Two takings, one principle: the United States may tax profit, but never debasement.
The Savings & Investment Fairness and Indexation Act indexes savings and investment — principal and earnings alike — to the currency's decline, counts the saver's own dollars first, and opens a simple after-tax account, capped at $10,000 a year, that every family can actually use.

End the Health Insurance Scam!
Health Care Main paper End the Health Insurance Scam: What They Sell You Isn’t Insurance Executive summary What Washington sells you as “health insurance” is not insurance at all — it is prepaid medical care, run through the most expensive billing department ever assembled, and chained to your job by a tax trick from 1943.
Insurance belongs in one box on the risk matrix: the rare, ruinous catastrophe.
Forcing the routine and the predictable through that same machine is what built a $1,055-a-year administrative tax on every American's existence.
Unchain coverage from the job, re-legalize real catastrophic insurance, restore the price tag, and fund the genuinely uninsurable in the daylight.
