Gabriella Romero knows how to fight to deliver results for our community.
She is currently the Assemblymember for New York State’s 109th Assembly District representing Albany, New Scotland, and parts of Guilderland.
As the daughter of two proud, unionized state workers, Gabriella grew up with a deep appreciation for public service and the transformative power of collective action.
Prior to being elected to the NYS Assembly, Gabriella served as a public defender working as a felony trial attorney, taking cases to verdict and handling mitigation files.
As a public defender, she learned about the deep cracks in our government systems that needed fixing.
