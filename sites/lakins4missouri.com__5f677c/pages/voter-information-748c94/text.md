Register to Vote
You can Register to Vote: Online, In Person, or By Mail
Voter Registration Qualifications
- 17 ½ years of age to register, 18 years of age to vote
- US Citizen
- Missouri Resident
Check Your Voter Registration
You should update your voter registration if:
- you changed your name
- you moved
- you changed your mailing address
Keeping your information current ensures you receive accurate voting location details and prevents problems on Election Day
What to Bring With You to Vote
When you go to the polls, you will be asked for a valid form of identification as required by current Missouri law.
Examples can include:
- Missouri non-expired state ID or driver’s license
- U.S. passport
- Military ID
