About Pennsylvania House District 20
As a result of redistricting, District 20 will unite all of Ross Township for the first time in 30 years!
The Boroughs of Avalon, Bellevue, and West View, as well as the Brighton Heights and Observatory Hill neighborhoods in the City of Pittsburgh remain in District 20 as well.
