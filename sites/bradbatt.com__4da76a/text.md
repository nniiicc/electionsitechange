A Tennessee that works for you
Tennessee still taxes your groceries.
Rent keeps climbing.
Healthcare is out of reach.
I'm running to change that.
What I'll fight for
An affordable life
Too many people are coming up short on groceries, rent, and the power bill.
I'll start by abolishing the grocery tax.
No one should pay a tax to feed their family.
Housing you can afford
Rent keeps climbing and a first home is out of reach.
I'll stop landlords from using software to fix rents and help more families here buy a home of their own.
Strong public schools
Every kid deserves a fully funded public school.
That means paying teachers enough that the good ones stay, instead of sending our tax dollars to private schools.
Your health, your choices
I'll protect IVF, birth control, and women's reproductive rights.
And I'll break up the Ballad Health monopoly so seeing a doctor doesn't wreck your budget.
And we'll take down every one of these cameras tracking your every move
No more 24/7, AI-powered government surveillance.
Meet Brad
Brad Batt is a dad and a small business owner who built his business and raised his family right here.
He's never taken a dime of corporate money, and he'll answer to his neighbors, not Nashville.
Most of all, he's tired of watching the Republicans who run this state look after everyone except the people who live here.
