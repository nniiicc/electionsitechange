Priorities
I’m dedicated to making Idaho a place where all families thrive, workers are respected, schools are strong, communities are safe, and our personal freedoms are honored.
Housing Affordability and Cost of Living
Idahoans should be able to live, work, and retire in the communities they choose.
I’m working to tackle rising housing costs, support seniors on fixed incomes, and promote responsible growth that keeps Idaho livable for all.
Strong Public Schools
Every child deserves a strong, well-funded public education without relying on constant bonds and levies.
I’m fighting for fair funding based on present realties, rural equity, and strong pathways for Idaho’s future workforce.
Affordable Healthcare and Safety
Healthcare should be accessible and affordable for every Idahoan.
I’m restoring common-sense protections, defending reproductive freedom, and working to ensure communities are safe and supported.
Freedom, Privacy, and Personal Autonomy
Freedom is foundational to Idaho’s shared identity.
I’m protecting personal healthcare decisions, ensuring data privacy and identity in the age of AI, and defending Idahoans from government overreach.
Justice and Access to Representation
A fair justice system is essential to safe, strong communities.
I’m working to fully fund public defense, support affordable civil legal services, reform prisons, and expand access to justice for all Idahoans.
A Sustainable Tax System
Idaho’s tax system should reflect our values: fairness, responsibility, and shared investment in essential services.
I support protecting working families and seniors while ensuring large corporations pay their fair share.
Protecting Land, Water, and Farmland
Idaho’s land and agricultural heritage define who we are.
I’m committed to conservation, tribal partnerships, farmland protection, water stewardship, and fair immigration policies that support our producers and workers.
Livable Wages & Workers’ Rights
A strong economy starts with strong workers.
I support livable wages, unions, workforce readiness, and policies that help Idaho families to thrive -- not merely survive.
