Annie Haws Standing for Healthy Families, Respected Workers, Strong Schools, & Safe Communities Annie Haws lifelong advocate , experienced attorney , and community-centered Idahoan working to protect the Idaho we love: one where families thrive, workers are respected, schools are strong, communities are safe, and our personal freedoms are honored.
An attorney and former clerk for federal judges , Annie has spent her career expanding access to justice, defending constitutional rights, and safeguarding Idaho’s land, water, and public institutions.
During law school, her work with the Nez Perce Tribe helped protect over 43,000 acres of tribal land from environmental degradation, and her in-court advocacy has strengthened Idaho’s ballot initiative process and access-to-justice pathways. ​ As a Representative for District 16, Annie is fighting for strong schools, affordable housing, livable wages, fair taxes, quality healthcare, responsible growth, and real protection for Idaho families.
She believes the government should open doors, not create barriers—and that Idaho thrives when we invest in people, protect our freedoms, and work together to solve real problems.
Annie lives in Boise’s Winstead Park neighborhood with her husband, Mo, and their dogs, Stanley and Jac.
They enjoy skiing, canoeing, cooking with family, and exploring Idaho’s public lands.
“I’m here to protect the Idaho we believe in — strong communities, fair opportunities, and the freedom for every Idahoan to live with dignity.” My Journey to Public Service I’ll be the first to admit that I never intended to run for office.
But, like so many Idahoans, I felt the increasing urgency for steady governance in this moment in our shared history.
When a seat in District 16 became available, I had to decide if being concerned from the sidelines was enough for me.
It wasn’t.
Now, I’m proud to show up and do the hard work advocating for policies that benefit all Idahoans.
I am seeking re-election in 2026 to continue the work.
Today, Idahoans are facing overlapping crises: affordability, healthcare, access to education, degradation of our public lands, pressure on our water.
Meanwhile, the legislature has been too focused on culture wars - passing unnecessary and often unlawful laws that diminish personal freedoms, such as civil rights, reproductive rights, and LGBTQ+ rights.
We continue to witness legislators who are out of touch with the needs of everyday individuals, families, and business owners who also call this beautiful state home.
It’s time to do something about it.
I am honored to serve District 16 alongside my colleagues, and bring pragmatic know-how and civility to the issues facing Idahoans today.
