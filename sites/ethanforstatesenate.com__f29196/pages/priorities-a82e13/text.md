We should be able to see a doctor, fill a prescription, and care for our loved ones with dignity and peace of mind.
But certain politicians, like State Senator Gary Byrne and Governor Mike Braun, have abandoned the fight for that freedom.
Their bill, SB2 , piles on red tape that blocks seniors, children, people with disabilities, and working families from Medicaid and Medicare.
It’s a deliberate choice that will further strangle rural hospitals, reduce access to lifesaving care, and force families to choose between groceries and medicine.
Harrison County Hospital and Ascension St.
Vincent in Salem have already been struggling and these new cuts threaten to push them over the edge.
That’s not leadership.
It’s cruelty by bureaucracy, and Hoosiers deserve better.
Ethan will fight for: Curtailing additional “work requirements” on Medicaid that cut people off from care and have been proven to harm health outcomes without increasing employment.
Restoring outreach programs that help seniors, rural residents, and working families enroll in coverage they need and qualify to receive.
Keeping rural hospitals open and fully staffed by restoring state funding for reimbursements and pushing back against federal cuts that threaten our hospitals and our health.
Guaranteeing continuous coverage for kids and postpartum mothers, without additional red tape.
Expanding investment in mobile clinics and telemedicine so no one is left behind.
Together, we can protect every Hoosier’s freedom to get the care we need: no exceptions.
Invest in Kids, Invest in Indiana Whether we work in fields, factories, or classrooms, every parent wants the freedom to know our kids are safe, supported, and learning what they need to thrive.
That’s what every family deserves.
But the people in power are breaking that promise.
Governor Braun and Senator Gary Byrne voted to cut early education and childcare while spending taxpayer money on luxury perks- a helipad at his personal mansion for the Governor, new $90,000 cars for the Lt.
Governor Beckwith and Secretary of State Morales, and huge pay raises for all of them.
Their SB1 slashed funding for Pre-K, denying 3,500 kids a headstart in life, while also stripping millions from schools across District 47.
Then they gutted the Child Care and Development Fund, hurting over 50,000 children and families who depend on affordable care and closing day cares all across our state.
That’s not fiscal responsibility- that’s neglect.
Ethan will fight for: Fully funding Pre-K and early learning programs.
Lowering childcare costs so no family spends more than 7 % of income.
Paying teachers what they deserve and ensuring mental health support in every school.
A “Grow Local, Teach Local” plan to train and keep teachers in rural Indiana.
Protecting food assistance for families and kids against Republican cuts.
Because when we invest in our children, we invest in a brighter future that everyone is willing to fight for.
Affordable Housing for the Heartland Every one of us deserves the freedom to have a safe, affordable place to call home- the foundation for stable families, strong schools, and thriving towns.
But across Indiana, that freedom is slipping away.
Rent and home prices have skyrocketed, wages haven’t kept up, and corporate investors are buying up single-family homes to profit off our struggle.
A quarter million Hoosiers are making less than $32,000 a year, and there are only 38 affordable units for every 100 of those families in desperate need.
Governor Braun and his allies have let housing costs spiral while giving tax breaks to the wealthy few.
Families in Southern Indiana are working two jobs and still can’t afford a place to live in the communities where they grew up.
That’s not the American Dream- that’s what happens when a political party with a monopoly on power stops caring about the things that matter most.
Ethan will fight for: Building and preserving affordable homes through transparent and accountable local partnerships and state investment, using LOCAL builders working directly with LOCAL residents in need of housing.
Restricting the ability of corporate bulk purchases of single-family homes to keep ownership local.
Passing an Implied Warranty of Habitability law , holding landlords and renters to equally enforceable standards of safe housing and rent due.
Fixing arbitrary property-tax hikes for middle-class homeowners and overhauling the assessment process.
Partnering with local governments to get through red tape and attract non-profit investment, revitalizing vacant lots and under-utilized spaces for more affordable housing.
Funding rental assistance and workforce housing in rural and small-town Indiana.
For too long, a good home has been treated like a luxury for the lucky, instead of the foundation of a community.
Together we’ll make sure every Hoosier has a place to belong and build a future.
Good Jobs, Strong Communities No matter where we live or what we look like, we all deserve the freedom to earn a good living, support our families, and plan for tomorrow.
But too many Hoosiers are working longer hours for less, while a wealthy few rig the rules to enrich themselves.
Multimillionaire Governor Braun immediately got a 65% pay raise, only to turn around and tell local governments to “do more with less.” Fitting, especially since Hoosiers haven’t seen a minimum-wage increase since 2009.
Meanwhile, nearly half of Indiana’s college graduates leave within five years because they can’t find good jobs here.
We deserve leaders who will rebuild an economy that works for all of us, not just elites and political insiders.
Ethan will fight for: Keeping local hospitals and schools strong — the largest employers in most counties.
Expanding apprenticeships, trade programs, and clean-energy jobs.
Protecting homeownership for families, not investment firms.
Supporting small businesses, where two-thirds of every dollar stays local.
Strengthening safety-net programs that help families recover when hardship hits.
Because when working people can build stability and security, our whole state thrives.
Protect Reproductive Freedom Every Hoosier deserves the freedom to make personal medical decisions without government interference.
For more than two decades, Indiana’s Republican leadership has built a system designed to shame, delay, and deny access to reproductive healthcare.
The result?
A mass exodus of OB-GYNs.
Maternity wards closing across rural counties.
Women forced to leave the state for lifesaving care, or worse, denied treatment until they are near death.
Ectopic pregnancies that resulted in hysterectomies instead of life-saving abortions.
These laws don’t make anyone safer.
They make people desperate.
They don’t reduce abortions- they just make them more dangerous.
And they fall hardest on low-income women, rural families, and survivors of sexual violence , who now face impossible barriers to care.
This isn’t about protecting life.
It’s about control- over women, over doctors, over families.
It’s about politicians telling women that their futures, their health, and their choices aren’t their own.
And after millenia of powerful men dictating to women how and when they will have children, Hoosiers are saying enough.
Women should be trusted to make their own medical decisions.
Doctors should be free to provide the best care without fear of prosecution.
And no one should have to flee their home state for basic healthcare.
Ethan will fight for: A Reproductive Privacy Act , guaranteeing that medical decisions stay between patients and providers: not politicians.
Data protections for digital health records, period trackers, and online searches, to keep personal information safe from surveillance.
Restoring full access to reproductive and maternal care in every county by funding women’s health clinics and OB-GYN training programs.
Comprehensive sex education and contraceptive access in schools and communities to reduce unwanted pregnancies and empower young people with knowledge and choice.
Freedom means having control over your own body and your own future.
No politician belongs in the exam room, and no one should trust a politician who wants that power.
Defend Equality and LGBTQ+ Rights Every Hoosier deserves to live with dignity, safety, and equality, no matter who they are or whom they love.
Across our state, a handful of politicians have tried to divide us with fear , attacking transgender kids, restricting books, and pushing laws that target families just trying to live their lives.
They hope that if we’re busy fighting each other, we won’t notice them handing more power to corporate donors and stripping freedoms from all of us.
But most Hoosiers know better.
We believe in the Golden Rule - treating others as we would want to be treated.
We believe in community, fairness, and the freedom to live openly and authentically.
That’s what equality means: the freedom to build a life, a home, and a family: no exceptions.
Ethan will fight for: Comprehensive statewide nondiscrimination protections for LGBTQ+ Hoosiers in housing, healthcare, employment, and public life.
Safe and affirming schools where every student — gay, straight, or trans — can learn without fear of bullying or censorship.
Access to inclusive healthcare , including gender-affirming care backed by every major medical organization.
Protection for LGBTQ+ families to marry, adopt, and raise children without discrimination.
Restoring trust and belonging through community education, mental health support, and local partnerships that make every Hoosier feel seen and valued.
Freedom means being able to live as yourself, love who you love, and build your family without fear.
