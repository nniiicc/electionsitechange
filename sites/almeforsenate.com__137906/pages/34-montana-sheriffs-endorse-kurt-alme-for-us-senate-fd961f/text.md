Senate BILLINGS, Mont. — Thirty-four sheriffs from across Montana today endorsed former U.S.
Attorney and Republican U.S.
Senate candidate Kurt Alme in Montana’s U.S.
Senate race.
“I was proud to work alongside the tremendous men and women of Montana law enforcement for years as a federal prosecutor and U.S.
Attorney, and I am honored to earn the support of 34 sheriffs across our state to serve as Montana’s next U.S.
Senator,” said Kurt Alme.
“We know what it takes to make our communities safer, and together, we will work each day to protect Montana families.
As your Senator, I will always support our law enforcement, because in Montana, we don’t defund the police — we back the blue.” After being nominated by President Trump and confirmed by the U.S.
Senate, Alme twice served as Montana’s United States Attorney.
Alme worked with sheriffs across Montana, was tough on violent crime, and cracked down on drug trafficking.
As Montana’s top federal prosecutor, Alme launched Project Safe Neighborhoods to target meth traffickers, armed robbers, and violent felons and led the formation of Montana’s Homeland Security Task Force to bring the fight directly to the cartels.
Under his leadership this past year, criminal prosecutions hit a 20-year high and made our communities safer.
Earlier in his career, Kurt served as an Assistant U.S.
Attorney in Montana, prosecuting white-collar crime, including fraud, recovering millions for taxpayers and other victims.
