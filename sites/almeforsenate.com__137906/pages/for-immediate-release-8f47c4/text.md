Senate candidate Kurt Alme in Montana’s U.S.
Senate race.
“I’m honored to be endorsed by the International Union of Painters and Allied Trades whose members embody our Montana values of hard work and service to our communities,” said Kurt Alme.
“Montana’s working families need leaders who will create good-paying jobs, keep taxes low so families can keep more of their paychecks, and invest in training our greatest resource – our people.
I have deep connections to working Montanans, and a record of spearheading two of the state’s largest tax cuts to create good-paying jobs and big investments in workforce development so Montanans are ready to go to work!” “At the International Union of Painters & Allied Trades, DC82, our federal engagement is focused on delivering results for working families, not party labels.
We have enjoyed a solid relationship with Senator Steve Daines, based on a shared commitment to workforce development, registered apprenticeships, and investment in the next generation of skilled trades professionals,” said Jeff Stark, Business Manager / Secretary Treasurer IUPAT, DC82.
“We are confident that Kurt Alme will continue this partnership in the United States Senate and champion policies that help create careers, not “temp-jobs”; strengthen Montana’s training infrastructure and address the growing demand for a skilled workforce across Montana.
We look forward to working together to advance these critical priorities.” IUPAT proudly represents and fights for 140,000 workers in the finishing trades across the United States and Canada.
They represent dozens of trades, primarily industrial painters, commercial and decorative painters, drywall finishers, glaziers and glass workers, sign and display workers, trade show workers, floor covering installers, and many more successful careers in the construction industry and public sector.
IUPAT is an organizing, fighting union that gives workers the power to have strength in the workplace.
They work every day to ensure all of their members can live with dignity, stability, and security.
As a former Department of Revenue Director and Budget Director, Alme worked to make life more affordable by investing in workforce development to create good-paying Montana careers, balancing budgets to limit inflation, and cutting taxes to put more money back into the pockets of Montanans.
Alme served as Director of the Department of Revenue under Governor Judy Martz where he spearheaded the largest income tax rate cut in state history and saved income taxpayers over half a billion dollars in just the first five years.
Alme also served as Montana’s Budget Director under Governor Gianforte where he balanced the budget, delivered one of Montana’s largest tax cuts, and helped make Montana debt-free.
