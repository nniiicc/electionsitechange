Just a Few Issues
Where Natalie stands — and why it matters for Idaho’s future.
Freedom & Rights
We will protect basic human kindness, religious freedom for all faiths, parental rights, and the moral foundations that give life meaning.
A free society depends on strong families, communities and the right to live by one’s faith without interference or persecution.
We are a nation of many faiths.
All people are children of God.
End of Story.
No excuses to discriminate or mistreat anyone.
Our constitution only works when we protect it.
Our constitution is intended to limit government and protect the people.
I will stand up for you and our constitution.
“Don’t tread on me” applies to ALL people.
Our separation of powers provides layers of protections for all of us.
No President shall give power to their pet federal agencies to dominate local communities.
If someone is sick, you help them.
We live in the most advanced civilization in the known history of our Planet, basic health care is a question of human decency, we can figure this out!
If someone is hungry, you feed them.
If we can send people to Mars, we can establish economic stability for all people.
I protect personal data as private property.
We will ban exploitative surveillance from corporations and the government, require informed consent, and restore digital autonomy.
Your private life belongs to you both online and off.
Economy & Land
I fight for the right of every family to own their land, their home, and their peace of mind.
We will curb corporate landlords, restore affordability, and revive small-town pathways to ownership and homesteading.
When families and communities plant roots in their own soil, communities flourish and the American Dream lives again.
I believe our economy should measure human thriving, not Wall Street profit margins or government overreach.
We will strengthen small businesses, cooperatives, and local supply chains that keep dollars circulating in our communities.
Strong families and communities, stable homes, and local prosperity that’s the true wealth of a nation.
America was built by people who came here and got to work.
A lean, clean, compassionate immigration process gets good people integrated and building — because messy systems don’t just waste money, they fuel corruption and cruelty.
I refuse to accept a system that traps Americans in lifetime servitude to interest and perpetual rents.
We will fight predatory lending, reform medical and student debt, and reward savings and asset ownership breaking free of perpetual monthly payments just to exist.
Freedom means owning your life, not renting it from the corporations.
We need to shift our food supply from primarily global to primarily local.
I stand with all the men and women who feed this country.
We will support regenerative practices, fair markets, and rural infrastructure that keep family farms and ranches alive.
When our producers thrive, America thrives from the soil up.
I honor Idaho’s heritage of open space, clear water, and shared wilderness.
We will balance conservation with responsible use, ensuring hunting, fishing, and hiking access for future generations.
These lands are our inheritance and our responsibility.
Healthy ecosystems are the foundation of healthy communities.
Drawing on years of direct climate research and ecosystem management work, Natalie will champion restoration of Idaho’s watersheds, forests, and grasslands — because when the land thrives, so do the people who depend on it.
“A kinder nation.
A stronger people.”
This decade decides who we become — a nation of renters or a nation of owners, of division or community, of bare ground or living land.
Together, we can restore what’s been lost — from the rain in our skies to the trust in our hearts.
Health & Food
I protect the soil, water, and families that feed America.
We will clean toxins from food and water, strengthen organic and regenerative farming, and safeguard rural health.
When we care for the land, it will care for us and our children will inherit abundance, not scarcity.
I believe in self-reliance in feeding and powering ourselves responsibly.
We will invest in regenerative energy, strengthen domestic production, and protect rural infrastructure.
A secure nation grows its own food, powers its own homes, and stands on its own two feet.
I stand beside those who’ve stood for us.
We will improve VA access, homes, streamline benefits, and expand mental health support for veterans and first responders.
A nation that honors its protectors honors itself.
“This is renewal.”
A movement of families, farmers, teachers, builders, and dreamers who still believe America can heal.
Let’s make this the generation that remembers who we are — caretakers of the land, defenders of freedom, and neighbors once again.
Government Reform
I demand integrity from those who claim to serve the people.
We will end the corporate stranglehold by overturning Citizens United, reforming campaign finance, and restoring transparency.
When policy serves principle not profit, democracy breathes again.
I believe the best decisions are made closest to home.
We will eliminate federal overreach, empower local leadership, and return authority to the states and communities that live with the results.
Freedom grows strongest when it starts at the local level.
I stand for a government that serves people not itself.
We will cut red tape, simplify bureaucracy, and design systems that respect your time and intelligence.
When the government becomes clear, simple, and honest, citizens can focus on living without fear.
Community & Values
I lead with kindness, respect, and moral courage, not outrage and division.
We will rebuild civic trust through ethics, education, and example.
When kindness becomes strength, politics becomes service again.
I stand beside those who’ve been knocked down by circumstance.
We will combine compassion with opportunity, Housing First, job training, rehabilitation, and mental health support.
Every person deserves a fair chance to rise and a community that helps them stand.
A garden for every school.
I believe in schools that teach students to do, not just memorize.
Bring back trades, arts, civics, financial literacy, media literacy, and real life skills that prepare students to thrive in the real world.
If you want to know what we should be teaching our children, imagine some strange event that removed everyone over 25, what would our children need to know to have the best future?
Technology
I defend your right to stay human in the digital age.
It’s time for a Digital Bill of Rights, regulate addictive design, and demand algorithmic transparency.
Technology should serve our values, not exploit them.
Your data is your property — full stop.
We will end mass surveillance by corporations and government agencies, establish comprehensive data privacy rights, require explicit consent for data collection, and hold violators accountable.
A free people cannot be a surveilled people.
“Together, we will bring life back to the land and decency back to public life — one family, one acre, one act of kindness at a time.”
