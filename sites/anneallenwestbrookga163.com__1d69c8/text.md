MY PRIORITIES WHILE SERVING THE 163RD:
We deserve laws that reflect our values and strengthen our communities:
- Expanding access to healthcare and investing in the health of all Georgians.
- Rooting out racial inequities in our laws, especially our criminal justice system.
- Fulfilling our constitutional obligation to educate every Georgia child.
- Restoring, protecting, and advancing voting rights.
- Passing a budget that expresses our values.
I am working to make this community a place where all of us can thrive.
