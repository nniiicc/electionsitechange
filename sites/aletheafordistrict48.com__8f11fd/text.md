Donate Volunteer ★ SHE WON THE DEMOCRATIC PRIMARY ★ Alethea Pugh will appear on the ballot for Florida State House District 48 ELECTION DAY — NOVEMBER 3RD Meet Alethea Alethea Pugh is a proud Democrat from Dundee, Florida, dedicated to service, leadership, and community empowerment.
She believes government should work for everyday people, seniors, working families, small businesses, and our youth.
With a comprehensive background spanning education, municipal government, healthcare payroll operations, insurance, utilities compliance, and juvenile justice, Alethea understands the real challenges facing District 48 families and brings proven experience to deliver solutions.
As a Professor of Computer Science, she prepares students for a rapidly changing economy.
As a U.S.
Army Reserves Veteran, she brings discipline, commitment, and deep respect for those who serve.
Her experience as Former Commissioner for the Town of Dundee gave her firsthand knowledge of local government, infrastructure needs, and what it takes to lead with integrity, transparency, and accountability.
