Bodily Autonomy
Whose Body Is It, Anyway?
Every human being, by right, is the sole owner of their own body.
It is their self, the only home of their consciousness.
A person who does not own their own body is a SLAVE.
Thus, every human being is the final arbiter of what should happen to their body.
No level of government has the right either to mandate or to forbid any form of medical care for that body.
Medical care is any drug, herb, or nutrient, surgery, or external manipulation, intended to alter or improve the function of the body.
Yes, that means reproductive freedom.
Women are not breeding livestock to provide babies that the legislature wants.
Yes, that means gender care if that is what a person needs.
Yes, to medical marijuana.
(recreational is a different argument) And yes, to death with dignity.
Yes, that means you can’t be required to take a vaccine by the government.
Your employer might,as a condition of employment, which is not a law.
We cannot be free to be wise if some people are not free to be fools.
However, public health measures such as quarantine and isolation, or closing schools or businesses, to slow the spread of a dangerous infectious disease, are not “medical care” as defined above.
They don’t alter anyone’s body.
Any level of government has the authority to employ these when necessary, and there are centuries of legal precedent for this.
If a person will not take treatment for a dangerous communicable disease (or no treatment exists), they may be isolated.
And if you won’t do the responsible thing and protect your child, maybe they should be learning remotely. [But seriously, what parent WANTS their child to catch dangerous diseases?
Why?]
From birth to the age of reason, and in case of mental disability, decisions must be made by parents or guardians, but they must be made in the best interest of the person.
Living wills made while a person was in command of their faculties must be honored.
