Skip to content Time For A Change Home June 28, 2024 politics , Uncategorized Without a Heart For fifty years this country had a legal decision that gave control over their bodies to women.
Abortion was legal up to fetal viability.
Later than that, only in medical emergencies.
There are a large number of possible such emergencies, far too many to specify each one in a law; that is why the decisions are best left to doctors and the affected family.
But then the Supreme Court threw out its own precedent, and immediately Idaho’s trigger ban on all abortions went into effect.
The consequences have been predictable but devastating.
Almost half the OB-GYNs in Idaho have left practice or left the state. (and some other doctors moved with them.
Doctors do tend to marry doctors.) Labor and delivery departments are closing, especially in rural areas.
Women may have to travel more than an hour just to get prenatal care, and are in serious danger if a complication arises.
So we the citizens of Idaho are trying to return to the Roe v Wade standard via initiative.
And opponents are going seriously nuts trying to defeat it.
Don’t believe everything you see in a text.
Read the whole proposed law at backtoidaho.com.
The anti-abortion contingent continues to lie over and over, despicably, about late term abortion.
No healthy fetus is “aborted” at 8 months.
That is called a Caesarean delivery.
Sometimes it’s the mother who needs the pregnancy to end early, due to preeclampsia or placental abruption or something else.
Sometimes it is a way to save the baby that would otherwise be stillborn due to placental malfunction or cord strangulation or any number of things.
Baby to the NICU, mom gets whatever problem it was treated, with luck and skill everybody goes home healthy eventually.
Almost a quarter of American babies are born that way.
If it needs to be done on an emergency basis, because the pregnancy is suddenly going sideways, that is NOT the time to be discussing it with the hospital legal department.
Docs need to be fast, to prevent baby from suffering brain damage from lack of blood supply.
But lying antiabortionists want to claim even worse, “abortion after birth”.
Trump claimed this repeatedly, during his campaign.
Impossible by definition.
They are trying to demonize and criminalize the worst tragedy that can befall a family: the birth of a child so badly deformed, lacking critical organs, that there is no chance of it surviving more than a few hours or days, in pain.
To allow that child to die wrapped in a parent’s arms, being told how much it is loved, instead of invading its tiny body with needles and machines to force a few more hours of agonized survival, is not “abortion”.
It is hospice care.
It is love and grief and mercy and heartbreak, and anyone telling those parents and doctors that they are criminals is a HEARTLESS MONSTER.
A late term abortion is the mark of such a tragedy, earlier.
It happens when a fetus is NOT viable, is dying, or even dead.
No one can carry a corpse “to term”.
THERE IS NO “TERM” FOR CORPSES.
It needs to be expelled or evacuated for the same reason a gangrenous toe has to be amputated.
Rational lawmakers would let doctors practice medicine, not try to do it themselves.
