Skip to content Time For A Change Home September 30, 2026 politics , public health , energy policy Green Energy is Energy Sovereignty If there is one thing the Iran war has taught us, it is that having lots of oil in your country does not shield you from the consequences of a global shortage.
We are a major oil- EXPORTING nation.
We have scads of crude.
But for one reason or another, we don’t have the refined products we need.
We export one grade of crude and import a different one.
Our refineries have been allowed to age and close instead of being upgraded, because that takes capital ($$$) and companies are averse to spending it.
But you know what?
Nobody can tariff the wind.
The sun doesn’t have to go through a strait on the other side of the world.
And we all live on a gigantic ball of superhot rock that won’t run out of heat for billions of years.
There are also locally available resources like hydropower, tidal energy, and more.
Energy generated in our own locale is the safest, cheapest way to power our future.
We should be promoting the renewable energy industries in every way possible.
While we in Idaho cannot do a lot about federal policies, we can: Restore a fair export credit to promote solar and wind installation.
Allow “balcony solar” as several other states already have, so that renters and people with less capital to spend can also generate some power.
Promote agrovoltaics, where solar panels and row crops or pasture share the same land, to the benefit of the crops.
Panels also pair very well with pollinator meadows and bee keeping.
Streamline permitting for smaller-scale wind projects.
We also have the local ability to promote energy efficient buildings and public transportation to save fuel.
Electric school busses not only cost less to run, but save the children riding on them from diesel fumes that cause respiratory problems.
Induction stoves in place of gas stoves cut the rate of asthma attacks by a lot.
Heat pumps are the most efficient way to heat and cool a building, and buildings can be far better insulated than is currently common.
Clean energy is a win-win situation.
Except for the fossil fuel barons currently gouging us for every penny.
They can go jump in a lake.
