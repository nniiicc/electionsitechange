The longer a single party holds power, the more unaccountable it considers itself.
Idaho has reached the point where its Republican supermajority legislature really does not want to hear from Idahoans.
They demand absolute party loyalty to their extreme platform, and censure any member who votes the way their constituents actually want them to vote.
The Bonneville county GOP censured two members who dared to step out of line, Rep.
Stephanie Mickelsen and Sen.
Kevin Cook.
The party platform is supposed to be a straitjacket.
Here are some of the highlights.
Oh, and throughout the document they present Christianity as the only acceptable religious stance and insist that the Constitution is based on the Bible [NOT EVEN CLOSE, people!] INTEGRITY IN AFFILIATION “I have read the Idaho Constitution and the Idaho Republican Party Platform.
I support the Idaho Republican Platform and accept it as the standard by which my performance as a candidate and as an officeholder should be evaluated.
I certify that I am not a candidate, officer, delegate or position holder in any party other than the Republican Party.” IRP Platform Preamble Believe in American Exceptionalism Human life begins at conception Article I: Responsibility in Government Social Security must be privatized Total abolition of inheritance taxes Repeal the 16 th Amendment of the U.S.
Constitution – the ability to tax income Repeal the 17 th Amendment of the U.S.
Constitution – requiring U.S.
Senators to be elected by the people, not appointed by state legislatures.
This takes away your right to vote for your U.S.
Senator and gives it to your opponent Abolish the Federal Reserve Bank Encourage citizens to systematically acquire precious metals as a replacement to paper currency Oppose term limits Give the Republican Party complete control of the redistricting process (exclude Democrats if they don’t hold at least two state constitutional offices over a 10-year period) Article II: Citizen Involvement in Government Republicans who do not declare their loyalty or allegiance to the IRP should not be a Republican Party candidate Encourage discussing the Bible in classrooms Create school vouchers (called Education Savings Accounts in the platform) Article V: Water Stop the use of water to augment fish conservation Continue funding the Idaho Constitutional Defense fund (uses taxpayer dollars to pay for defending unconstitutional laws – not sure why this is in the “Water” section) Article VI: Natural Resources and Environment Discourage regulations that attempt to halt the production of certain industrial byproducts (e.g. toxic waste); instead, encourage citizens to adopt buying habits that promote a clean Earth.
Article VIII: Idaho National Laboratories Support continued (and growing?) use of nuclear power.
Article IX: Private Property Rights Oppose any state regulation that would diminish a property owner’s right to develop his property (care to live next to a newly constructed pig farm?).
Article XIII: Health and Welfare Repeal the Affordable Healthcare Act in its entirety.
Support as supreme the authority of parents in all medical decisions for their children, including providers and treatments (except, apparently, if the child is transgendered).
Article XIV: American Family Limit marriage to that of a man and women – no same-sex marriage.
Nullify any federal congressional act that is contrary to “traditional marriage.” Abortion is murder from the moment of fertilization regardless of the circumstances of conception, including rape and incest.
Support criminalization of all murders by abortion Oppose all abortion (no exception to protect the life of the mother is mentioned).
Article XVII: National Defense Construct a border wall along the entire southern border of the United States (apparently with taxpayer dollars).
IRP Convention Resolutions (all of these were approved at the convention) Resolution #4 (submitted by Tammy Nichols) Prohibit Idaho law enforcement to work with federal directives – even in an emergency – that would infringe on the rights of the people to keep and bear arms.
Resolution #22 Privatize (and therefore eliminate) Public Television.
Resolution #24 (submitted by Jackie Davidson) Thwart absentee voting Tighten controls on voter identification Prohibit candidates or third-party entities from sending absentee ballot applications to voters.
Resolution #25 Severely limit who can vote in a Republican Primary (see the document for details).
Resolution #26 Require ALL elections in the State of Idaho to require partisan affiliation (except judicial elections).
Resolution #29 Prohibit the use of student identification cards for validating a voter’s identification.
Require proof of citizenship in order to register to vote or cast a ballot in any and all elections conducted in the State of Idaho.
So, if any one item of this is not what your constituents want, too bad.
Which is why when every single testimony is against a proposed law, THEY PASS IT ANYWAY.
And a committee chair feels perfectly justified in refusing to accept remote testimony from people all over the state who will be affected by a law, because their concerns don’t matter.
Only Republican ideology matters.
And our Attorney General can gaslight us that he is “protecting” Idaho children that he is actually bullying.
He supports pounding square pegs into round holes even though it breaks bits off of them.
Only boys and girls as he defines them are allowed to exist in Idaho.
And Chairman John Vander Woude can cancel a committee hearing planned to hear medical testimony on the bad effects of the abortion ban, because Republican feelings got hurt by a press conference where Democrats called them out on their inaction.
Their FEELINGS are more important than the women whose lives are at risk because they can’t find maternity care.
Republicans in Idaho have ceased to care who they hurt.
Unless the cruelty is the point.
It is way past time to change the guard.
It is time Idahoans had a real voice in their government.
