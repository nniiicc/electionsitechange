From whom?
Our leadership continues to be obsessed with sex.
Specifically, with people who do not fit neatly into the precise sex roles that they consider themselves empowered to define.
Anyone even talking or writing about different possible ways of living and being is DANGEROUS!
They must protect the children!
Such gaslighting.
But yes, they actually passed House Bill 421, and Governor Little signed it.
They are trying to deny the very existence of trans people, by law.
This law declares that there are only two possible sexes, that they are forever separate, that anyone thinking otherwise is sick and needs care to cure them of their belief.
It goes so far as to mandate that a doctor who attends an intersex birth MUST assign the child to one sex or the other, rather than document the medical facts in front of him.
This is the only circumstance in which the law allows that the determination might be changed later. [To the other sex, not to acknowledge the facts. ] All gender-affirming care for minors is forbidden.
Our AG is quite certain that he is “protecting” the very children he is bullying and trying to wish out of existence.
Their risk of suicide when denied care is not his problem, oh no.
Their bodies and behaviors are to conform to his wishes, or else.
“Conservatives” are bullies.
They are determined to pound all the human pegs into the holes they have designed for them, even when it breaks bits off of them!
We also can’t read about gay people, or even gay penguins apparently.
Or anything about sex.
At all.
(Better lock up those Bibles, parents, there is a whole lot of nasty going on in there!) Also signed into law is a bill that lets anyone challenge any book and the library MUST lock it up in an adults-only section.
And anyone who lets a child hear or read or see any such “dangerous to minors” material is a GROOMER!
Try to get a real definition out of someone who uses that word.
HA!
Or any trace of evidence, such as could be used in a courtroom.
Anything?
“Groomer” is the new “WITCH!” A completely fact-free bit of rabble-rousing.
Intended to arouse hateful and violent behavior toward the target, and thus, it is stochastic terrorism.
Accusing someone of evils they have not, in fact, committed, is DEFAMATION.
Might possibly be legally actionable.
Definitely violates the commandment against BEARING FALSE WITNESS.
Let’s check the arrest records.
Any drag queens or librarians arrested for child molesting?
How odd, no.
The danger to our children comes from the authority figures we have taught them to trust.
The priests and pastors, youth leaders and camp counselors, coaches and team doctors, and of course, family members and friends.
Those are the people who show up in the courtroom after molesting children or possessing and distributing child pornography.
You don’t protect children by denying to them that sex exists.
You protect them by teaching them, from the earliest possible age, what parts of themselves are off limits to others.
You empower them to say NO, and to raise hell if that NO is not honored.
And you back them up with social and legal power.
None of this shielding-the-good-old-boys-network stuff.
