Skip to content Time For A Change Home July 28, 2024 politics , Uncategorized Public Lands For the Public Our public lands are a huge part of what makes Idaho a great state to live or visit.
Idahoans like to hunt, fish, camp, hike, backpack, cross-country ski, visit hot springs, explore national parks and monuments and historic and archaeological sites.
We have a wealth of places where we can just get away.
But a long-standing plan from the wealthy wing of the Republican party is to sell off our lands, on any pretext they can think of, and extract any possible profit from the lands that remain.
See Project 2025’s section on the Department of the Interior, written by William Perry Pendley, former Trump administration official in the Bureau of Land Management, and by Kathleen Sgamma of the Western Energy Alliance, an oil and gas industry group; Dan Kish of the Institute for Energy Research; and Katie Tubb of the Heritage Foundation.
Donald Trump orchestrated the largest reduction of protected public lands in history.
Now Pendley is calling for a massive sell-off of OUR LAND.
The plan calls for putting BLM law-enforcement under the direct supervision of political appointees, weakening environmental protection, reducing national monuments, repealing the Antiquities Act, prioritizing oil, coal, and gas extraction and mining over all other land uses, even in wilderness areas currently protected.
Wildlife protections would be rescinded.
Our game animals and fish would not be managed responsibly any longer, the agencies involved would have their budgets slashed.
Timber sales would be increased and roads carved through our wild forests.
State level action: When public lands are sold, even piecemeal, the public often loses access to what remains as well.
Infamously, private owners of parcels that touch at the corner have prosecuted hunters and hikers that cross those corners, and blocked access to trails with locked gates.
This must stop.
The sales must stop, but I also propose mandatory access easements to public lands beyond private ones.
It must be illegal to deny the public access to public lands.
Private interests seek to wreck our wild lands for profit, and discourage any enjoyment of natural beauty and peace.
We cannot let this come to pass.
