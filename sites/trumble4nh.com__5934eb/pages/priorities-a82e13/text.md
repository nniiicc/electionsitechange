My Priorities
State funding for public schools
The state should live up to its Constitutional duty to fund the public schools, which would have three benefits:
Education: Guarantee every child the opportunity for a great education, including children who receive special education services.
Town services: Allow towns to fund other services like fire departments, roads, libraries and clean water.
My Priorities
Building a bright future for young people
Affordable housing: A fundamental right and essential to a healthy economy
Child care that meets the needs of working parents, is affordable and pays child care workers a living wage.
Clean water and air: Take steps to mitigate climate change, promote clean and renewable energy, protect our lakes and rivers, and support local agriculture.

Individual Rights
Reproductive freedom: The government should stay out of personal decisions about abortion and birth control.
LGBTQ+ rights: We should treat everyone equally and with respect in the eyes of the law.
Voting rights: Voting is our most fundamental right, upon which all our other rights stand, and should be protected.
Lower taxes: Provide local property tax relief
