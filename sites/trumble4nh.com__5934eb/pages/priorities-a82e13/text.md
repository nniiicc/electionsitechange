Lower taxes: Provide local property tax relief Town services: Allow towns to fund other services like fire departments, roads, libraries and clean water.
My Priorities Building a bright future for young people Affordable housing: A fundamental right and essential to a healthy economy Child care that meets the needs of working parents, is affordable and pays child care workers a living wage.
Clean water and air: Take steps to mitigate climate change, promote clean and renewable energy, protect our lakes and rivers, and support local agriculture.
Health care including mental health care for all My Priorities Individual Rights Reproductive freedom: The government should stay out of personal decisions about abortion and birth control.
LGBTQ+ rights: We should treat everyone equally and with respect in the eyes of the law.
Voting rights: Voting is our most fundamental right, upon which all our other rights stand, and should be protected.
