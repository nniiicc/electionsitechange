I am running for State Senate in District 8 because I believe everyone should be heard.
We need elected officials who will work for reasonable solutions and who will listen to the concerns of the people.
Senate District 8 is rural district of 20 towns from the Connecticut River to the Merrimack River watershed.
Our rural towns need a strong voice to speak for their interests, ranging from strong public schools, health care, affordable housing, protecting our lakes and rivers, and promoting our farms.
The town of Weare has been a good place to raise a family and run a small business.
I am running for office to help keep our region a great place for young people to come and raise a family (if they choose), and for people to stay and retire.
