MY VOTER PAGE Check your status, request an absentee ballot, register to vote, and find your polling location for upcoming elections by visiting the My Voter Page (MVP) on the Georgia Secretary of State’s website.
Everyone needs to check their voter registration now and often, especially young voters and new residents.
Check to make sure the green status checkbox is marked “active,” and that your address is correct.
Changes of address must be made 30 days prior to Election Day, so don’t wait!
