I truly believe I have what it takes to champion our freedoms in the Live Free or Die state!
That's why I'm running for State Representative as a Republican.
I didn't follow the typical education path; everything I've achieved has been through hard work and passion.
It has only made me work harder!
If it weren't for God guiding me through life's journey, I would be nothing.
In 2004, I launched my first small business in Manchester out of a deep love for landscaping.
I dedicated all my efforts to making it a successful business!
Since 2014, I have served as the President and CEO of Your Oil Tools in Hooksett, NH.
The company was founded after a life-changing truck fire that set my life on a new course.
It's hard to convey everything I've accomplished over the years.
For instance, I received the 2022 SBA District Director Award for assisting small business owners nationwide through SCORE and other organizations, helping them get their businesses online during the global shutdown!
I am also an alumnus of the Goldman Sachs 10,000 Small Businesses program through Babson College.
All of my experience led me on a path I would’ve never chosen for myself, but it brought an understanding you can’t get anywhere else.
I’m genuinely grateful to have the opportunity to run for State Representative for Ward 12 in Manchester.
If you trust in me and vote on November 5th, I will bring my passion and experience to Concord every day.
I promise to be accessible, hard-working and I will not let anyone convince me that we need to give up any of our freedoms!
