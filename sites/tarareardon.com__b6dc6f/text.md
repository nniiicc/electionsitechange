Tara Reardon for Senate New Hampshire District 15 Senate State Senate District 15 deserves a strong voice that comes from experience, years of relationship-building and proven success.
I want to be that Senator for you.
I pledge to bring all my experience, intellect and common sense to the job. ​ I am honored and humbled to have the endorsement of Congresswoman Annie Kuster, Governor John Lynch and Senator Sylvia Larsen.
These seasoned public servants know what it takes to do the job and have confidence in me to be the next State Senator for District 15. ​ The Capital Region faces unique challenges in the upcoming years: a new men’s prison, the widening of I-93, a new parking garage in our downtown and the demotion of an old one, and a forensic hospital in a residential neighborhood.
I have strong proven relationships at the state, county and local level to make residents' concerns heard, and I will listen and represent those voices.
DONATE Senate Priorities I am dedicated to protecting women’s health care rights, addressing our housing crisis with 30 years of expertise, supporting our public schools, and championing affordable clean energy for all.
Together, we can build a brighter, healthier future for New Hampshire.
