DEMOCRATS, GROUPS FIGHT BACK AFTER SUPREME COURT CLEARS WAY FOR TRUMP ATTACK ON MAIL VOTING
August 25, 2026
From Common Dreams:
Democratic leaders and a “pro-voter” coalition on Tuesday announced separate actions to combat President Donald Trump’s attack on voting by mail after the US Supreme Court’s right-wing majority delivered a “painful setback” for critics who argue the Republican’s executive order is unconstitutional.
The chairs of the Democratic National Committee (DNC) and Democratic Governors Association, US Senate and House minority leaders, and heads of the party’s campaign committee for each chamber of Congress on Tuesday announced the filing of a new motion for a preliminary injunction.
“Today, we are taking strong, joint legal action to stop Trump’s unlawful attempt to restrict mail-in voting through the Postal Service,” said DNC Chair Ken Martin, Kentucky Gov.
Andy Beshear, Sen.
Chuck Schumer (NY), Rep.
Hakeem Jeffries (NY), Sen.
Kirsten Gillibrand (NY), and Rep.
Suzan DelBene (Wash.) in a joint statement.
