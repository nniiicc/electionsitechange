DELBENE AND SILVA ADVANCE TO GENERAL ELECTION FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT 1
August 4, 2026
From KING5:
Incumbent U.S.
Rep.
Suzan DelBene and Mary Silva advanced to the general election in the race for Washington’s 1st Congressional District Tuesday.
DelBene advanced with 54% of the vote, with Silva advancing with 27% of the vote as of Tuesday night.
“Number one thing we have to do is to make sure we address high costs, that means the high costs of health care, housing, food, but also policies that we can do to move our country forward like the expanded child tax credit, that help our families help our kids get off to a great start,” [DelBene] said Tuesday.
That’s what we’ll be working on.”
