Nicole's Mission  Issues Making South Florida Affordable  You are paying too much for housing, insurance, healthcare, groceries, and utilities while corporations report record profits.
I will fight price-gouging, lower essential costs, protect consumers, and make billionaires and large corporations pay their fair share.
If you work hard, you should be able to afford a good life here.
Read More → Affordable Housing and Insurance  I will confront insurance companies that charge enormous premiums while abandoning homeowners, protect renters from extreme increases, build more affordable housing, and stop corporate investors from buying up our neighborhoods.
You should not be priced out of the community you call home.
Protecting Social Security and Medicare  You earned your Social Security and Medicare, and I will protect them.
I will oppose benefit cuts, privatization, and attempts to raise the retirement age.
I will fight to strengthen these programs so they remain available for today’s retirees and every generation that follows.
Read More → Ending Endless Wars  I will not support another unnecessary or unauthorized war.
I will put diplomacy first, oppose blank checks for governments that violate human rights, and demand transparency about where our money and weapons go.
Your tax dollars should improve life here at home, not enrich weapons manufacturers.
Read More → Defending Reproductive Freedom  I trust you to make your own healthcare decisions.
I will fight to restore abortion rights nationwide and protect access to contraception, fertility treatment, and emergency reproductive care.
Decisions about your body belong to you and your doctor, not politicians.
Affordable Healthcare  I believe healthcare is a right, not a privilege.
I support the fastest responsible path to universal healthcare.
Until we get there, I will fight to lower prescription prices, expand coverage, eliminate medical debt, and stop insurance companies from profiting by denying you care.
Read More → Fighting Corruption  I believe corruption is fueling our affordability crisis.
I refuse corporate PAC money because corporations should not be able to buy members of Congress.
I will fight to ban congressional stock trading, expose dark money, overturn Citizens United, close billionaire tax loopholes, and hold public officials accountable regardless of party.
Read More → Better Jobs and Stronger Wages  If you work full time, you should be able to support yourself and your family.
I will fight for wages that reflect the cost of living, paid family and medical leave, affordable childcare, stronger unions, and protections against wage theft.
I will also help small businesses compete against corporations and monopolies that have rigged the economy.
Making Billionaires Pay Their Fair Share  I will not accept a tax system where working people carry the burden while billionaires exploit loopholes.
I will fight to tax extreme wealth, make large corporations pay what they owe, and provide meaningful tax relief to working- and middle-class families.
We can invest that money in healthcare, housing, education, childcare, and infrastructure.
Keeping Our Communities Safe  You deserve to feel safe in your home, school, and community.
I support accountable policing, mental-health and addiction services, community violence prevention, universal background checks, safe firearm storage, and stronger protections for survivors of domestic violence.
