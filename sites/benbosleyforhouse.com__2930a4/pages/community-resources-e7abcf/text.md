Suggested prompt Add your situation below, or edit any part of the prompt before continuing.
I live in Burlington, Vermont.
Please help me find current local or Vermont resources for the situation I describe below.
Prioritize official government agencies, established nonprofit organizations, and Burlington/Chittenden County services when available.
Give me the best places to start, explain briefly what each resource provides, and include links to official websites.
If eligibility depends on my circumstances, explain that rather than assuming I qualify.
If you need more information to identify the right resource, ask me a few simple questions.
Here’s what I need help with: [Describe your situation or question here.] Open ChatGPT ↗ Copy prompt ChatGPT opens in a new tab.
If the prompt does not appear there, use “Copy prompt” and paste it into the message box.

Important note A helpful starting point—not an official service This page provides a suggested way to use ChatGPT to search for community resources.
ChatGPT can make mistakes, and programs, eligibility requirements, contact information, and availability can change.
Please verify important information directly with the organization or official source before acting on it.
For emergencies, call 911 .
For immediate crisis support, call or text 988 .
For your privacy, do not enter highly sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, passwords, financial account information, or medical-record numbers.
