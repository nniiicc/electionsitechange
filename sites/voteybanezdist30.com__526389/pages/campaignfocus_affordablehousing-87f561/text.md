Affordable Housing and Cost of Living
We know that the cost of living is expensive in Hawaii, and is therefore encouraging more locals to move to the mainland where the cost of living is cheaper.
Amanda wants to promote more affordable, and workforce housing, especially in urban core honolulu.
Amanda has attended numerous meetings covering this with Land stake holders, Kamehameha School, and Transient Orientated Development.
By advocating for more affordable housing, this will also address part of our homeless crisis.
