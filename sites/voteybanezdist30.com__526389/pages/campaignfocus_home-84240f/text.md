Amanda YBANEZ has 4 KEY ISSUES that she is, and continues to focus on and find solutions to when she becomes House Representative:
1.
Cost of Living / Affordable Housing:
To address the issue, local production of resources such as farming, agriculture, cattle must be mentioned and implemented.
In the meantime, we have to look at a variety of resources in regards to importing goods into Hawaiʻi.
Alongside the local production, affordable housing must be addressed.
The definition and the price point of affordable housing continues to scale up -- what would be considered affordable now may not be affordable next year.
It is completely dependent on the median-house price of a single family home.
There is an instituted formula set in place and because of that, it is obsolete and the people are once again paying the price.
This affordable housing scale must be reevaluated.
We also must increase the supply of affordable housing options.
This can be done around the Transit-Orientated Development, otherwise known as TOD.
In Kalihi, we can develop more workforce housing for citizens around the rail transit that is still currently under construction.
2.
Safety
I support all funds and programs that support our law enforcement, public safety and community safety.
3.
Homelessness and Mental Health
We need to look at — and I will support the funding and expansion of community-based rehabilitation programs and social services such as the Salvation Army ARC.
4.
Community Emergency Disaster Response
I support the expansion of community based emergency response programs such as CERENE (An emergency outreach program of Kapiolani Community College) and CERT.
Also, as legislator, I will support bills and legislation that will expand the use of community based emergency hubs — especially for all of our necessary needs such as food security.
I have been supporting the Hawaii FoodBank and will continue to do so for the purposes of supporting our communities’ needs especially during a disaster such as the Kona Lows and the Lahaina Fires.
