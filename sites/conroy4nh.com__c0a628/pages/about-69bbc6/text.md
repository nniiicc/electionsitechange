Helping New Hampshire thrive has been an important part of my personal and professional life.
My time serving on town commissions, local land trusts and the boards of several statewide nonprofits has been inspiring.
It taught me that people working together can have a real and positive impact.
And that’s why I’m running.
I want to make Weare, Goffstown and all of NH a place where everyone has an opportunity to succeed.
We can do better than what our current representatives are offering us.
We deserve better.
We are better.
Whether it’s working to lower property taxes, supporting public education, or protecting our environment, I believe I can better represent the people of Goffstown and Weare.
After all, that’s the real New Hampshire Advantage: everyone working together to make the state a better place for all of us.
Please join me!
