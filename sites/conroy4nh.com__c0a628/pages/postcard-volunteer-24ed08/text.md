Postcard Campaign
Please complete the following information to become a volunteer postcard writer for Chris.
After you fill out the form, we will deliver blank post cards, a sample message to write, and a list of addresses.
You can order for yourself or on behalf of a group!
A roll of 100 postcard stamps costs $61.
Be sure you buy postcard stamps, not standard stamps!
If you can write cards but not supply stamps, just click the "please send stamps" option on the form.
THANK YOU!
Each order will be logged as an "in kind" donation to the campaign.
Please come back when you're ready for more cards!
