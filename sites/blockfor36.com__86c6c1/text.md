DARRYL BLOCK
Darryl is an attorney, certified mediator, social worker, youth basketball coach, and longtime Seminole County resident whose life’s work has centered on helping people.
He began his career in social work, later became an attorney and mediator, and has also supported active-duty service members facing crisis.
Darryl is running for the Florida House because he believes every family deserves dignity, opportunity, and a government that listens.
He is focused on affordability, public education, responsible growth, and practical solutions that put people before politics.
