Privacy Policy
Friends of Sarah Mittelman
Last Updated: September 20, 2026
Friends of Sarah Mittelman respects your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains generally how information submitted through this website may be collected, used, and protected.
Information We May Collect
When you use this website, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, mailing information, volunteer interests, messages, and other information you choose to submit.
Information may be collected when you sign up for campaign updates, volunteer, request campaign materials, contact the campaign, make a contribution through a third-party contribution platform, or otherwise interact with the campaign.
How We May Use Information
Friends of Sarah Mittelman may use information you provide to communicate with you, respond to requests, coordinate volunteers, provide campaign information, administer campaign activities, maintain campaign records, and comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.
SMS and Mobile Information
If you provide your mobile number and opt in to receive text messages from Friends of Sarah Mittelman, we may use your mobile number to send campaign updates, election reminders, event information, volunteer opportunities, voter information, and other campaign-related communications.
We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data, mobile telephone number, or SMS consent information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Message frequency may vary.
Message and data rates may apply.
You may opt out at any time by replying STOP.
For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at info@sarah4wa.com or 360-210-3986.
Information Sharing
Friends of Sarah Mittelman may use service providers to help operate the website and campaign activities.
Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, legal obligations, or campaign reporting requirements.
SMS opt-in data, mobile telephone numbers, and SMS consent information will not be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Third-Party Services
This website may use third-party services to process forms, contributions, website hosting, or other campaign functions.
Those services may have their own privacy policies and practices.
Campaign contributions are processed through a third-party contribution platform.
Friends of Sarah Mittelman does not use this Privacy Policy to replace the privacy terms applicable to services operated by other companies.
Data Security
We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through this website.
However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Your Choices
You may choose not to provide optional information through this website.
If you have opted in to campaign text messages, you may opt out by replying STOP.
You may contact the campaign regarding your information or communication preferences at info@sarah4wa.com.
Changes to This Privacy Policy
Friends of Sarah Mittelman may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
