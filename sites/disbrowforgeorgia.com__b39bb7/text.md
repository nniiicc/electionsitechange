Featured Proposal The FAIR Act The FAIR Act (Fair AI & Innovation Reform Act) ensures workers are treated fairly as AI changes the workplace.
Companies must evaluate AI systems before replacing employees, and workers who are displaced receive severance, continued healthcare, and opportunities for retraining or reassignment.
If other positions are available, they must be offered first.
And if the AI system fails, workers get the first chance to return to their jobs.
