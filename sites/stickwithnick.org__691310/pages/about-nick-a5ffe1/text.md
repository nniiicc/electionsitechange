Growing up in a low income family, Nick developed a strong work ethic early in life and paid his own way through college.
He received a bachelor's degree in accounting from Western New England University and before graduating was offered a position with a large Fortune 500 company.
Along the way Nick met his beautiful wife, Jessica, but also discovered that a life in corporate America wasn't for him.
Sparked by a passion that has been with him since childhood to serve his community, Nick left accounting to pursue a career in law enforcement.
Through continued hard work and dedication, Nick was hired as a police officer and graduated from a full-time academy!
During his career he was the recipient of a Professional Policing Award for his efforts in keeping streets safe from drunk drivers.
First elected in 2011, Nick continues to proudly serve his community as the State Representative for the 3rd Hampden District.
As a principled conservative, Nick strongly believes in the values of economic liberty and personal freedom.
At the State House in Boston, he consistently stands up to tax and spend politicians and champions the voice of hardworking families, farmers, and small businesses.
He believes the American dream is still alive and has devoted himself to ensuring that a path towards that dream remains for future generations to pursue.
Nick and his wife Jess live in Southwick with their three children.
Together they are actively involved in youth sports and love to explore the outdoors and rich history that this great state has to offer.
