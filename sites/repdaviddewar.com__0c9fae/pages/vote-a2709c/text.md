There are a few ways to find your district, most involve many steps.
We made this easy for you in the spirit of Dave "the Doer" Dewar.
You need to be in the 35th district to vote for Dave.
VOTER CARD - The quickest way to check is with your voter card.
Look near the bottom to find your district number.
ONLINE CHECK - Don't have your card?
No problem.
Go to the Illinois Policy Find Your Repreresentative page, enter your information, and click submit.
Your result will display near the red arrow shown below.If incumbent Mary Gill is listed, you are in District 35.
Tip: If you're on a computer, use your mouse wheel to zoom in on the map.
On a mobile phone, you can pinch to zoom in and see how the district boundaries were drawn.
