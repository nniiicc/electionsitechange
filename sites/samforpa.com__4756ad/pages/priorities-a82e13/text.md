POLICY PRIORITIES
Strong Schools & Local Control
Keep education decisions close to parents and local communities, not distant state mandates.
Affordability for Working Families & Seniors
Address rising taxes, housing costs, and everyday expenses that are squeezing households.
Public Safety & Youth Mental Health
Support law enforcement and strengthen practical mental health resources for children and families.
Responsible Growth & Land Preservation
Balance development with protection of farmland, open space, and community character.
Parental Voice & Common-Sense Values
Ensure parents have a meaningful role in education and protect children from political agendas in the classroom.
