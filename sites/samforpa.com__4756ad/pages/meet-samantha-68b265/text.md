DONATE MEET SAMANTHA Samantha Horosky is a proud Hispanic-American educator and conservative leader running for Pennsylvania State Representative in District 26.
Raised in a household grounded in faith, military respect, and the American work ethic, she draws inspiration from her family’s legacy of strong women, including her seven-times great-grandmother Anna Nutt, the first colonial female industrialist who helped build Warwick Furnace and produced Franklin Stoves and Revolutionary War cannons.
A dedicated kindergarten teacher in the Owen J.
Roberts School District since 2008, Horosky brings 18+ years of classroom experience, having taught at French Creek and East Coventry Elementary.
She is married to Jack Horosky, mother of two, and has called Elverson home for over 20 years.
Her background as a Juvenile Crisis Counselor, combined with her service on PTA and community programs, equips her to advocate for local control, family affordability, strong schools, and common-sense solutions for Chester County families.
