He is a businessman, family man, farmer, and Sunday school teacher.
Baucom believes in an America First agenda for North Carolina so every family can thrive without the burden of an overreaching government.
He is a graduate of Piedmont High School and went on to earn his Associate’s Degree from NC State University.
Baucom is actively involved in the community and enjoys spending time with his family and friends.
He has been married to his wife Kim for 35 years, who is also a Union County native.
Together, they have three adult daughters: Kendra, Callie, and Carmen.
