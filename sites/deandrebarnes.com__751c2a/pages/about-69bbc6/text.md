KEY AREAS OF focus:
- We must modernize our classrooms.
This means expanding access to vocational training and STEM education to ensure Michigan students are the most competitive in the global economy.
- The future requires a reliable grid and high-speed internet for every corner of District 16.
I will advocate for smart investments in our roads, bridges, and digital infrastructure to support local businesses and agriculture.
- Focusing on the future means being good stewards of our land and water today.
I am committed to protecting Michigan’s Great Lakes and rural landscapes for generations to come.
- Supporting small businesses and attracting emerging industries to Michigan.
We need to create a tax and regulatory environment that encourages growth and keeps our talent right here at home.
