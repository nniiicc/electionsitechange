VOTE BY MAIL DEADLINES
Saturday, June 13: Last day to request your ballot online or by mail at requestballot.vote.nyc.
Monday, June 22: Last day to request your mail ballot in-person at your local Board of Elections office.
Tuesday, June 23: Last day to postmark your ballot.
(Ballots must be received by the Board of Elections by July 5.)
Voters who have requested to vote by mail can still vote in-person, but they will have to ask for an Affidavit Ballot at early voting or Election Day poll sites (instead of voting on a machine).
