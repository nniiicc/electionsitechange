I'm Joey Callahan, Republican candidate for Alabama House District 32 and a lifelong resident of East Central Alabama.
I was raised in Pell City and have called Lincoln home for 25 years.
My wife, Nicole, and I are proud to be raising our son, Jackson, a freshman at Auburn University.
My career has been built on service and leadership.
I spent 16 years as Assistant Fire Chief for the City of Lincoln, protecting families and keeping our community safe.
I then served nine years on the Lincoln City Council, where I worked to bring in new businesses, improve city services, and make life better for our citizens.
For more than 20 years, I've worked in construction.
Today I own and run GC Construction Group, a land development and general contracting business.
As a small business owner, I understand the challenges facing working families and small businesses in our local economy.
In the Legislature, I'll focus on practical policies that get results — stronger community services, better schools and job training, and smart growth for our district.
My priority is simple: making everyday life better for the families I serve.
I'll support efforts to bring in new industry and higher-paying jobs, and I'll stay accessible and accountable to the people of our district if elected to represent them in Montgomery.
