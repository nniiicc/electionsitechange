About David Golden
From Local Service to Beacon Hill: Leadership Rooted in Plymouth
Plymouth families are feeling squeezed from every direction: higher utility bills, new taxes, rising costs, and state policies that put Boston's agenda ahead of our community's needs.
David Golden is running for State Representative to restore balance, fiscal responsibility, and common sense to Beacon Hill.
David brings real-world experience from both public service and private industry.
He currently serves at a municipal services firm, traveling throughout the region to work directly with municipalities and client communities.
In this role, David provides expert advice, hands-on support, and sound property valuation services, helping towns implement fair and efficient property assessment systems.
Previously, David served as Vice President of the Regional Resource Group, overseeing human resources, client engagement, and business development.
His experience extends to housing and community development, where he served as a former Consultant and Program Director for the Plymouth Redevelopment Authority, helping administer affordable housing programs and connecting local residents with stable, affordable homes.
David's strong work ethic was evident even during his college years, when he balanced five jobs simultaneously, working at a local restaurant, in the campus center, as a resident assistant, as a shuttle driver, and in the Political Science Department's polling call center.
Education
- Bachelor of Arts – Merrimack College
- Master of Public Administration – Bridgewater State University
- Doctor of Education – Northeastern University, focusing on leadership, organizational effectiveness, and community development
A Record of Service
David currently serves as a member of the Plymouth Select Board, where he was elected by his peers to serve as Vice Chairman and later as Chairman.
As a former Select Board Chair, David worked to strengthen local government's responsiveness to residents, prioritize fiscal responsibility, and promote economic development that respects Plymouth's history and character.
David has also chaired the Charter Review Committee and the Memorials Advisory Committee.
His record of service includes roles on the Board of Health, the Manomet Steering Committee (as Vice Chair), and the Cultural Council.
He is also a Commonwealth of Massachusetts Notary Public and a Licensed Real Estate Broker.
Family & Values
As a husband and father, David understands the challenges that Massachusetts families face every day—from paying the mortgage and childcare costs to managing rising taxes and utility bills.
He believes in creating a Commonwealth where hard work is rewarded, where parents can afford to stay and raise their children, and where every decision on Beacon Hill considers the future we're leaving to those who come next.
