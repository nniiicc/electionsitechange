David Golden forState Representative
David Golden is running for State Representative to fight for affordability, local control, and government accountability on Beacon Hill.
Meet David
Father.
Husband.
Fighter.
David Golden brings real-world experience from both public service and private industry.
He currently serves on the Plymouth Select Board, where he previously served as Chairman after being elected by his peers.
David is ready to bring common-sense leadership to the 1st Plymouth District and Beacon Hill.
David's Agenda is You
Prioritizing Plymouth
Restoring balance, fiscal responsibility, and common sense to Beacon Hill.
Stop Tax Hikes
Oppose new taxes on meals, lodging, and medicine.
Defend Proposition 2½ and demand smarter spending.
Lower Energy Costs
Fight for affordable, reliable energy and dissolve wasteful programs like MassSave.
Accountability
Open the books, audit the Legislature, and hold government accountable to the people.
Transportation
Stop hidden mileage-based fees and driving restrictions that hurt working families.
Cut Red Tape
Reduce unfunded mandates and modernize procurement rules to give towns flexibility.
Local Control
Protect local zoning decisions and reform the MBTA Communities Law.
