Joe entered public service after experiencing firsthand the impact of government overreach during the COVID-19 pandemic, when his daughter’s school was shut down and families were threatened for making their own decisions.
That experience sparked a commitment to stand for parental rights, individual freedom, and accountable government.
In 2021, Joe co-founded Ottawa Impact alongside Sylvia Rhodea, helping mobilize citizens across West Michigan around the principles of freedom, family, and local governance.
Elected to the Ottawa County Board of Commissioners in 2022 and serving as Board Chair in 2023 and 2024, Joe led bold, results-driven reforms, including: Eliminating the county’s DEI department–America’s first local government to do so Strengthening parental rights and transparency Advancing fiscally responsible governance Defending local control and community decision-making Protecting children and preserving childhood innocence Joe has also been a strong advocate for energy reliability and local control—standing with residents to protect farmland and pushing to keep the J.H.
Campbell power plant open.
As he seeks to serve at the state level, Joe is committed to defending constitutional freedoms—including the Second Amendment—and ensuring law-abiding citizens can protect themselves, their families, and their communities from government overreach.
Today, Joe is running for the Michigan House because he believes strong leadership is needed to protect families, defend constitutional rights, and return government to its proper role—serving the people.
Joe Moss is a leader who doesn’t just talk about principles—he delivers results.
