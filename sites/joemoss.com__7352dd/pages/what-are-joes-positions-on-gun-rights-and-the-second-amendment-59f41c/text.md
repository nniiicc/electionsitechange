What are Joe’s positions on gun rights and the Second Amendment?
2026-06-02T13:44:10-04:00 May 30th, 2026 | I am a strong defender of the Second Amendment.
The right to self-defense is given by God, and belongs to all law-abiding citizens so they can protect themselves, their families, and others.
I fully support the Heller Supreme Court decision, which affirms the right to keep and bear arms is an individual right, not a collective right.
If we don’t exercise our rights, we will lose them.
I carry a firearm every day, and teach my kids about responsible gun ownership.
Key Policy Positions Constitutional Carry : Michigan must become a Constitutional Carry state.
Law-abiding citizens do not need a government license to exercise their Second Amendment rights.
Reciprocity : I support continued issuance of Concealed Pistol Licenses (CPLs) for anyone who wants one for out-of-state reciprocity until national reciprocity is passed.
Non Infringement : I oppose gun registration schemes, red-flag laws, so-called “assault weapons” bans, or any restrictions that punish responsible gun owners.
Proven Record : I am a lifetime member of Michigan Coalition for Responsible Gun Owners , Great Lakes Gun Rights , and Michigan Open Carry .
I have a long-standing, unwavering commitment to protecting the Second Amendment.
I will never vote for any anti-gun schemes or fall for attempts to erode our constitutional rights.
“The right to keep and bear arms shall not be infringed.” Additional Information I support the Heller decision (2008).
I support the Bruen decision (2022).
I agree the right to self-defense applies to carrying firearms on public property, and oppose restrictions on public property.
I support state preemption of local gun laws.
I support Michigan’s Stand Your Ground law.
I support Constitutional Carry.
I support repealing red flag laws.
I support repealing transfer requirements for firearms.
I support the right to own any type of firearm, including rifles like my Daniel Defense M4 I support strong protections for gun and ammunition manufacturers.
I support legislation to prevent DHHS from disarming foster parents.
