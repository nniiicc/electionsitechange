We Are Installing God Bless America Signs!
2026-07-01T17:46:53-04:00 July 1st, 2026 | We are installing brand new God Bless America signs in Muskegon, Ottawa, and Kent counties!

Two hundred fifty years ago, America chose freedom.
This year, we celebrate it in every city, town, and stretch of American road.

America remains a beacon of hope for the world because we’ve stood firm on what matters most: life, liberty, and the Truth found in the Bible.
I hope you have a Happy Independence Day!
