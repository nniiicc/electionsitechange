I am thrilled that Ottawa County elected so many local candidates who stand for The People!
I am excited to serve with my fellow Americans who love our country and the hardworking people of Ottawa County.

I am grateful for the support of the new board and look forward to continuing to serve The People with them in 2023.
The 2023 Board of Commissioners was elected based on their bold, public commitment to protect constitutional freedoms and American values.

The hard work begins now.
It is an honor to serve Ottawa County.
