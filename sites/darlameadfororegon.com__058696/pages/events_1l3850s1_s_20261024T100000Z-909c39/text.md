Home Meet Darla Priorities Endorsements Volunteer Events Contribute Events / Canvass with Darla Mead, Nicole Bassett, and Janelle Bynum Canvass with Darla Mead, Nicole Bassett, and Janelle Bynum Time Saturday, Oct 24, 2026 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM Location Canby, OR About this event Go door to door in Canby to talk to voters about Darla Meads campaign.
This canvass is exciting as it will also include volunteers for Janelle Bynum's congressional campaign and Nicole Bassetts Senate campaign.
Canvassing is a crucial part of the path to election for Darla and Nicole and re-election for Janelle.
Letting people know about the campaign is the most effective way to help win votes.
Training will be provided at the beginning of the event when we first meet.
We'll go to specific voters likely to be receptive to the campaigns.
Please come dressed in comfortable clothes and walking shoes.
Please also bring a fully charged phone.
We’ll see you then!
Once you sign up you will get details of park in Canby we are meeting at.
