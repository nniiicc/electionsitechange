Caring for Our Communities, Strengthening our Future " I'm a nurse, community advocate and foster parent who grew up right here in rural Clackamas County.
I'm running to represent HD51 because it's time to get back to the Oregon we love: healthy communities, a clean environment, and economic opportunity for all.
Expanding Access to Affordable Healthcare “As a registered nurse, I help my cancer patients navigate our healthcare system to get the care they need.
Too many of my patients are forced to make life-altering decisions based on what they can afford versus what they need to survive.
Every single Oregonian deserves access to affordable care.” Health insurance should not depend on your employment status, job quality, or employer.
In Salem, I'll use my front-line experience to pass real healthcare reform that lowers costs and guarantees access to affordable care.
It's past time for every Oregonian to have the care they need.
I'm ready to get it done.
Strengthening Rural Economic Opportunity "In 14 years serving Clackamas County families, I've seen firsthand what happens when communities like ours are left behind.
We need real solutions that offer families a path to stability: quality, career-focused education, affordable housing, and economic development." I grew up in rural Clackamas County and raised my own kids here, too.
I know that rural communities have tremendous potential—we just need a chance.
I'll fight to reduce the burden on small business, strengthen our schools, and grow our economies without losing the small-town character that makes our communities special.
Protecting Our Natural Resources and Environment "Our rich natural resources and beautiful wild spaces are what make Oregon special.
Right now, they're being threatened.
We need robust protections that preserve our waterways, forests, and public lands for future generations to use and enjoy." For too long, rural communities have been told we don't care about the environment.
Yet we know firsthand just how special this land is, and just how dangerous it can be when it's mismanaged.
I'll fight for solutions that mitigate natural disasters and invest in clean energy so we can pair climate protections with strong jobs.
