Enforcing Environmental Protections
Oregonians understand that our beautiful woodlands, waterways and wildlife need to be safeguarded for future generations.
However, our beautiful state is under attack from the current administration and greedy corporate interests.
Climate change is real.
Our communities were displaced during the 2020 Labor Day fires that destroyed our homes.
We need policies in place that will protect our communities from the threat of natural disasters and climate change in the future.
Protecting our forests.
The Trump administration plans to eliminate protections on our old-growth forests and intends to expand logging beyond the sustainability standards established by Oregonians.
Clean energy is a priority.
The current administration’s emphasis on oil-based fuels to favor its supporters is unacceptable.
This is also while hindering the development of clean energy sources and undermining progress in transitioning to electric transportation options.
The Trump Administration has a stranglehold on efforts to expand wind and solar energy sources.
Hydroelectric power, which relies on river volume, faces threats from climate change.
Innovative alternatives and solutions are needed to expand opportunities for the development of clean energy sources and transmission systems that facilitate the transfer of these sources from remote to populated areas.
To safeguard the progress we’ve made in reducing fossil fuel consumption and the reliance on gasoline-powered vehicles, it is critical that we mitigate setbacks driven by budget constraints.
We must preserve and expand incentives aimed at facilitating the transition to clean energy alternatives in both individual and business transportation sectors.
Protecting our communities from big tech.
Data centers are being built within rural neighborhoods where they deplete fresh water resources.
Existing regulations designed to protect our communities and environment are insufficient to effectively manage this issue.
