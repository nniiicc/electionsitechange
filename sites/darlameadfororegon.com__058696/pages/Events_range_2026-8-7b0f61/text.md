Beavercreek National Night Out
Borrowed from their website: https://www.beavercreek.org/events-listing/national-night-out
National Night Out in Beavercreek, Oregon, is an annual community-building event aimed at fostering stronger relationships between residents and law enforcement.
It's held on the first Tuesday of August, and this year, it will be on August 5th, 2025.
In Beavercreek, the event will take place at Korner Park from 5:30-8:00 PM.
The event will feature:
Community Engagement: Meet with neighbors, local law enforcement, and first responders like the Beavercreek Fire Department and Mountain Wave Search & Rescue.
Safety Information: Learn about community safety and preparedness from various organizations.
Food and Fun: Enjoy food options and family-friendly activities.
Interactive Exhibits: Explore safety booths and learn about community resources.
The Oregon Livestock Council will be in attendance so you can tour their new trailer, and Kissin’ Kates will be providing Tacos!
