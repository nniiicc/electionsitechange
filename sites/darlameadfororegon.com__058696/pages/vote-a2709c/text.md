Darla Mead, RN wins House District 51 Democratic Primary
Darla Mead, RN wins the House District 51 primary race for the Democratic party.
Darla is an oncology Nurse at OHSU.
She has key endorsements from the Clackamas County Democrats, the Oregon Nurses Association and Friends and Families for Single Payer Healthcare.
She is a native Oregonian who looks to bring Oregon back to an affordable place to live with healthcare for all and a growing economy.
