Caring for Others I have dedicated my life to caring for and advocating for those in need.
For the last 14 years, I've worked at Oregon Health and Science University (OHSU) caring for patients.
Currently, I serve as an Oncology Nurse Navigator for lung and head & neck cancer patients, guiding them through their cancer diagnosis, treatment and recovery.
I earned my Associates Degree from Clackamas Community College and worked as a Registered Nurse (RN) at OHSU while completing my Bachelor's Degree in Science with a focus in Nursing.
For a few years, I taught Clinical Nursing at Clackamas Community College.
Prior to returning to school to become a nurse, I worked in customer service and accounts receivable.
I have deep firsthand knowledge of the challenges families face navigating our healthcare and insurance systems.
I've seen too many patients who have to choose between necessary care and putting food on their table.
I'm running for office because I firmly believe we need more elected officials who understand these challenges and are ready to fight for accessible, affordable healthcare.
Outside of the healthcare space, I've been active in the foster care community.
A fter adopting a child through Clackamas County Foster Care, I volunteered for years on Long Term Placement Committees through DHS to help children in foster care find forever homes.
My commitment to others will guide my work as a state legislator.
Native Oregonian and Small Town Resident Born and raised in Clackamas county, I have a special love for our small rural communities.
I grew up in this area, started my career here, and settled down here.
My husband and I have lived and raised our family on our wooded acreage in Mulino for the last 26 years.
We experienced firsthand the wildfires of 2020 and saw how devastating natural disasters can be for rural Oregonians - and how our neighbors come together in times of crisis.
We value the closeness of living in small-town communities and treasure their unique charm.
There's something to be said for a friendly wave to your neighbors as you drive by and running into friends when out running errands.
Exploring Oregon's Natural Beauty Growing up, my vacations were all about camping in eastern Oregon and along the upper Clackamas River.
I spent my summers as a child visiting my grandparents on the Oregon coast and spending time on my aunt & uncle’s berry farm in Molalla.
Some of my most cherished childhood memories involve salmon fishing on the Willamette River in the ‘Hog lines’ with my dad.
Now that I have my own family, you’ll see us exploring the incredible hiking trails and scenic byways of our state.
From the Cascade range to the Gorge and out to the beaches, we’re always up for exploring, camping with friends, fishing from our kayaks, and soaking in the natural beauty.
We’re especially thrilled to spot native wildlife like deer, elk, hawks and bald eagles on our adventures.
As Oregonians, we are incredibly lucky to have so many untouched natural areas to enjoy.
I believe we live in the most beautiful state in the country and I'm dedicated to protecting our climate and environment from outside corporate influences.
