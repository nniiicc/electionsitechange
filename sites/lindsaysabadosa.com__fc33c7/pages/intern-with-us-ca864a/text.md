INTERN WITH US
Our office believes in paying interns who do not otherwise receive credit or compensation from an academic institution, etc. because we believe your time and effort is important and valuable.
Our office believes that anyone of any age can intern because people of all ages have wonderful things to contribute.
Our office believes that diversity is beautiful and welcomes all applicants.
Our goal is to make sure our office is a safe space for everyone.
We accept interns both at our Boston and Northampton offices.
Please specify which you prefer when applying.
We regularly accepted intern throughout the year and can be flexible with scheduling.
Some interns have been with us for a few weeks, others for a few months.
Please let us know when you are interested in interning.
Please include a brief message below indicating why you want to intern in our office, your policy interests, and what you feel you can contribute.
We will be in touch to schedule an interview & to request a writing sample and resume.
Thank you for your interest!
