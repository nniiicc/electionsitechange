Lateefah received contributions from 320 donors.
Lateefah does not accept corporate PAC money and is running a people-powered grassroots campaign.
“I’m humbled by the outpouring of love and support I received yesterday,” said Lateefah Simon .
“I knew from the beginning that it would take a village to run this race, but I never realized how big my village is.
This community is special, and I’m committed to making everyone so proud.
I look forward to getting out there to earn every vote so I can fight for these folks who are entrusting me with their voice.” ** \n ** Lateefah Simon is a 25-year veteran organizer and nationally recognized civil rights and social justice leader.
She currently serves on the BART Board of Directors and the Board of Trustees for the California State University.
