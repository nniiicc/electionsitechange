☰ Home Priorities Meet Connie Volunteer Contribute Strong Schools = Strong Communities Strong public schools are the foundation of thriving communities.
Tennessee ranks last in per-pupil public education funding, and our students deserve better.
I will fight for: Fully funded public schools Support for teachers Strong literacy and early learning Career & technical education Safe, well-resourced classrooms Our children deserve schools that prepare them for whatever comes next.
Quality Affordable Child Care = Working and Thriving Families The first three years of life build the foundation for all future learning — but quality child care is often out of reach for working families.
I will fight for: Expanded child care scholarships Investment in early childhood programs Capping child care costs at 7% of family income Support for early childhood educators Every family deserves access to child care that lets them work and thrive.
Health Care for All = Healthy Families Access to affordable, quality health care should be a right, not a privilege — regardless of income or zip code.
I will fight for: Expanded health insurance access and preventive care Stronger maternal and family health services Improved mental health services Lower prescription drug costs Everyone deserves a health care system that puts people first.
Economic Security = Dignity of Life Too many Tennesseans struggle to afford housing, food, transportation, and health care — and our government hasn't done enough to help.
I will fight for: Protections for seniors and fixed-income families Access to Medicaid for those who need it Resources to help families make informed decisions about housing and care Policies that protect the rights of people of all ages Every Tennessean deserves the dignity of economic security.
