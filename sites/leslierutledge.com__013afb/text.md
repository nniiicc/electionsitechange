MEET LESLIE
Leslie Carol Rutledge was sworn in on January 10, 2023 as Arkansas’s 21st Lieutenant Governor and the first woman to ever hold the State’s second-highest office.
Rutledge previously served two-terms as the 56th Attorney General of Arkansas from 2015-2023.
She was also the first woman and first Republican elected to the office of Attorney General.
