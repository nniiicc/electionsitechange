RUTLEDGE CAMPAIGN: NEARLY THREE-QUARTERS OF A MILLION DOLLARS CASH-ON-HAND
LITTLE ROCK – Today, Lieutenant Governor Leslie Rutledge announced that her re-election campaign enters the final quarter of 2025 with nearly three quarters of a million dollars cash-on-hand.
"I am deeply honored by the overwhelming support from Arkansans across our great state,” said Rutledge.
“Arkansans know that I will always stand up for our conservative values, fight to protect our freedoms, and keep pushing back against the radical Left’s agenda."
Rutledge, who served as Arkansas’s first female and first Republican Attorney General, was elected as the first female Lieutenant Governor in 2022.
Rutledge and Governor Sarah Sanders are the first independently-elected female governor and lieutenant governor to serve together in the history of the United States of America.
Rutledge has worked tirelessly to advance conservative leadership in every corner of Arkansas, ensuring that our state remains a place of freedom and opportunity for generations to come.
Rutledge continues to demonstrate the fundraising strength, grassroots support, and proven record of leadership necessary to secure victory in 2026.
The Arkansas Primary Election will be held on March 3, 2026.
