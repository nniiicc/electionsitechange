LIEUTENANT GOVERNOR RUTLEDGE NAMES CAMPAIGN MANAGER
LITTLE ROCK – Today, Lieutenant Governor Leslie Rutledge announced that Will Teeter will serve as Campaign Manager for her 2026 re-election campaign.
Lieutenant Governor Rutledge released the following statement:
"I am thrilled to have Will leading my re-election campaign.
He played a key role in my successful 2022 campaign, and I have every confidence in his leadership, vision, and work ethic.
Will’s love for Arkansas combined with his extensive experience traveling the state, working on conservative policy and winning campaigns makes him an asset to my team."
Newly-appointed Campaign Manager Teeter added:
"It’s an honor to continue serving Lieutenant Governor Rutledge and the people of Arkansas.
She has proven time and again to be a conservative fighter who delivers results, and I’m proud to help lead this campaign to ensure she can keep working for Arkansas families, farmers, and small business owners."
Teeter previously served as Deputy Campaign Manager for Leslie Rutledge’s successful 2022 campaign for Lieutenant Governor.
He brings extensive experience from his work with nonprofits and Republican campaigns across various levels.
A proud 8th generation Arkansan and native of Pottsville, Teeter is a graduate of the University of Central Arkansas and the Leadership Institute’s Campaign Leadership College.
He and his wife reside in Faulkner County.
To learn more, visit LeslieRutledge.com.
