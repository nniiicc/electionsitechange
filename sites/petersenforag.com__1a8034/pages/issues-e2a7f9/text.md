ISSUES
- Arizona needs an Attorney General who will enforce the law as written—not based on politics or personal opinion.
I will uphold our state’s laws with integrity and fight against any attempts to undermine them.
- The federal government continues to overreach into our state’s affairs.
I’ve fought against unconstitutional mandates, burdensome regulations, and attacks on our rights—and I’ll continue that fight as Attorney General.
- Public safety must always come first.
I will ensure our laws are enforced, hold criminals accountable, protect the rights of crime victims, and support law enforcement officers who put their lives on the line to keep Arizonans safe.
- When the current Attorney General refused to stand up for fairness in women’s sports, I took action.
I will continue fighting to protect opportunities for female athletes and ensure the law is upheld.
- From the Second Amendment to free speech, our rights are under attack.
I will use every legal tool available to defend the freedoms that make Arizona and America strong.
- Arizonans deserve an Attorney General who applies the law without bias.
I will bring fairness, accountability, and principled leadership to the office—ensuring justice is served, not politicized.
