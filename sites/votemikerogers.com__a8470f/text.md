MEET DELEGATE MIKE ROGERS A History of Service I brought my experience as a Colonel in the Army and as a community leader to shape District 32 into the best place to own a business, raise a family, and attend school.
The first 29 years of my professional life were in service to the United States.
For the last 8 years, I've served as your Delegate in District 32, fostering the philosophy of E2C2 — Education, Economy, Community, and Country.
This means strengthening our schools, growing a robust local economy, investing in the people and institutions that hold our neighborhoods together, and honoring the same commitment to country that guided my military service.
A good leader creates opportunities for everyone to succeed, and the people of our community deserve a leader who continues the same dedication and commitment I have demonstrated both in uniform and in office.
