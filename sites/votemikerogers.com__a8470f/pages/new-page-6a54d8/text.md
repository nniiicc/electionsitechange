About Delegate Mike Rogers
Colonel (Retired) Mike Rogers has spent the last 8 years serving District 32, applying the discipline, leadership, and commitment to duty he learned in the Army.
He served his country for 29 years and now he continues that same commitment serving his community as your Delegate.
Family
Mike is the proud father of Michelle and Jared; both college graduates raised in Laurel, Maryland.
Like their father, both are now serving their country in the uniformed service.
Mike is married to Tonya Rogers, also a retired Colonel, and together they are proud parents to three accomplished adult children Enfinity, Michelle and Jared.
Community
Mike has called Laurel home for more than two decades.
He previously served as a director with the Russett Homeowners Association, President of the Meade High School PTSA, and coach of the Severn Seminoles youth football team.
Today, he continues to advocate for his community as a state Delegate, attending community events and working closely with fellow Legislators to represent the interests of District 32.
country
Mike dedicated 29 years to the United States Army as a Medical Service Corps officer, retiring as a Colonel in 2015.
Throughout his career, he traveled across the world in support of America's interests abroad.
Today, he brings that same commitment and dedication to serving the constituents of District 32 as their trusted Delegate here at home, in Annapolis, and across the nation.
