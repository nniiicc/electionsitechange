Where to Drop Off Your Ballot Where to Drop Off Your Ballot Attention!
Your ballot only counts if it arrives by 7 pm on Tuesday, November 3rd.
Dropping it off early is the surest way to make sure your vote counts.
Find your closest drop box: Visit the Secretary of State’s “Where do I drop off my ballot or vote in person?” page and enter your address.
You’ll see the drop boxes and voting centers near you.
Before you drop it off: Sign the back of your envelope.
Unsigned ballots cannot be counted until you fix the signature.
Don’t wait until the last minute.
Drop boxes close at exactly 7 pm on Election Day.
Mailing it instead?
Send it back by October 26 to give it enough time to arrive.
Find more information here .
Most drop boxes are open 24 hours a day, so you can drop off your ballot whenever it works for you.
Questions?
Contact the Arapahoe County Elections Division at 303-795-4511 , or visit their website here.
