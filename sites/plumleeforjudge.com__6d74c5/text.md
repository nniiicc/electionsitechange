Citizens of Dallas County: I have some important news to share today.
This past weekend, a completely unexpected opportunity presented itself to me when a candidate who had been campaigning for appellate judicial office decided not to file.
After careful consultation with family, advisors and friends, I decided to pursue what has been a longtime dream of mine to serve as an appellate justice.
Consequently, in 2026, I will be campaigning for the 5th District Court of Appeals, Place 8.
I have ably served the 330th District Court for 15 years, handling thousands of cases and seeing firsthand the impact of family court proceedings on parents, children and families.
I am passionate about the work I do right now, but I have for a long time wanted to have a larger impact serving at the appellate level.
This is not a decision I made lightly.
I carefully considered how I can best serve our community with the skills and knowledge I have accumulated, and also how I can add value to the appellate court.
As it happens, the 5th District Court of Appeals needs family law expertise, and if successful in this campaign, I will be the only justice on this court who has focused primarily on family law.
As you may know, this appellate court handles civil, criminal and family appeals from six counties in North Texas, so it is important that this court have a justice who can provide the expertise and experience I can offer.
I will continue presiding over the 330th Family District Court through the end of 2026, and hope to assume the appellate bench in January, 2027.
As I follow this path, I ask for your moral support, your financial support for those who are able, and most importantly, your vote in the March 2026 Democratic Primary and November 2026 General Election.
The 5th District Court of Appeals is the largest appellate court in Texas.
The work done by this court has a direct impact on people, businesses and our community at large, as well as how the law is interpreted and shaped over the longer term.
I am ready to take on this important role, and I want you by my side as I campaign this year.
Warmest regards, Judge Andrea Plumlee Presiding Judge, 330th District Court Democratic Candidate for 5 th District Court of Appeals Place 8 YOUR VOTE MATTERS!
