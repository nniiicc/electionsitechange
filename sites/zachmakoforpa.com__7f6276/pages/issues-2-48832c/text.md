PROPERTY TAXES
Property taxes are out of control.
They are forcing senior citizens out of their homes, squeezing the checkbooks of middle-aged taxpayers, and making it nearly impossible for young people to buy a home.
Zach Mako continues to fight to eliminate and reduce property taxes so that families can purchase their own home and seniors can remain in theirs.
TAXES / SPENDING
Pennsylvanians deserve to know that their tax dollars are being used legally, effectively, and efficiently.
Zach Mako is a fiscal conservative who has focused on cutting waste, controlling spending, and preventing tax increases.
He has opposed massive tax and spending increases proposed by Governors Wolf and Shapiro.
EDUCATION
Zach Mako believes that every child deserves the opportunity to obtain a quality education.
He has advocated for better schools and to make sure that Lehigh Valley taxpayers are getting their fair share of Pennsylvania state education funds.
Zach also supports expanding education choice options such as charter schools, home schooling, vocational training, and education scholarships.
VETERANS
As a veteran, Zach understands what it means to serve our country.
Zach has consistently supported efforts to help Pennsylvania veterans.
From opening up employment opportunities to veterans and their families to support for veterans exposed to Agent Orange in the Vietnam War, Zach is a leader on veterans’ issues.
