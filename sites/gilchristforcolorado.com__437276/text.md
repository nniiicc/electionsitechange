Born and raised in Denver, Lindsay is deeply proud of her roots and considers it a privilege to represent HD8 and the community she holds so dear.
For over a decade, Lindsay has worked on policy issues both in and alongside the government.
Over the last two years, she brought that expertise and dedication into the Colorado State Legislature, continuing to be a champion for the causes she has long fought for.
In her first term in office, Lindsay passed 26 bills and was appointed Chair of the Health and Human Services Committee.
Now, Lindsay is running for reelection to continue serving House District 8 and is excited for the opportunity to continue pouring her passion for public service and her deep policy experience into improving the lives of all Coloradans.
Lindsay has always deeply believed in public service and that government should be a place where we come together to solve real problems.
That belief guides her every day in her efforts; she looks forward to continuing the work and earning the trust and support of her community.
