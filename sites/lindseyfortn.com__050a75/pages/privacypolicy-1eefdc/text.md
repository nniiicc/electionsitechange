Privacy Policy
Lindsey for TN respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following information when you interact with our campaign:
• Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
• Donation and contribution information
• Information you provide through forms, surveys, or event sign-ups
• Mobile phone numbers when you opt in to receive SMS/text messages
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
• Communicate campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities
• Process donations and maintain compliance with campaign finance laws
• Respond to inquiries and provide support
• Improve campaign outreach and engagement
Text Messaging & Mobile Data
If you opt in to receive text messages:
• Message frequency may vary
• Message and data rates may apply
• You can opt out at any time by replying STOP
• You can request help by replying HELP or contacting us directly
Important:
Mobile opt-in data and consent will not be shared with third parties for marketing purposes.
Information Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal information.
We may share information:
• With service providers who assist in campaign operations (e.g., email, texting, compliance tools)
• As required by law (e.g., campaign finance reporting)
The above excludes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, which information will not be shared with any third parties, provided that the foregoing does not apply to sharing (1) with vendors, consultants and other service providers who need access to such information to carry out work on our behalf (and who will not use such information for their own purposes); and (2) if we believe disclosure is required by any applicable law, rule, or regulation or to comply with law enforcement or legal process.
Data Security
We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no system is completely secure.
Your Choices
You may:
• Opt out of emails using the unsubscribe link
• Opt out of text messages by replying STOP
• Contact us to update or remove your information
