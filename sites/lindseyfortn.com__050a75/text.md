Lindsey Patrick-Wright has spent twenty years showing up for Tennessee families — not in headlines, but in the quiet, consistent work of public service.
As a librarian, she’s spent her career making sure every person, regardless of zip code or income, has access to the same resources and opportunities.
That’s what equity looks like in practice, and it’s the same lens she brings to the issues facing District 17 — affordable housing, public education, healthcare access, and infrastructure that too often gets decided without local input.
I’ve seen firsthand what it takes to represent a community well: you listen first, you understand the specific needs of your neighbors, and you fight for solutions that work for the people actually living here, not just the people with the loudest voice in the room.
Lindsey does exactly that.
District 17 needs a senator who treats equity as a practice, not a talking point, and who understands that the best policy starts with local knowledge.
I’m proud to endorse her for State Senate.
