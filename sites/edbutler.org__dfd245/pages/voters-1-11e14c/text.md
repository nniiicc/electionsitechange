HOW TO VOTE IN THE STATE OF TENNESSEE What are your voting options ?
You can vote in-person early, request a ballot, mail in your ballot, or vote in-person on voting day.
VOTING LOCATIONS Voters who vote in person during early voting or on Election Day must present a photo ID issued by the federal or Tennessee state governments.
These IDs may be current or expired.
