Hi, my name is Bart Schulz.
I am a proud fifth-generation farmer raising beef cattle on our family farm in Sidney.
My wife and I live in Cave City, where we are raising our four children.
In addition to farming, I serve as the General Manager of Spring River Paramedic Ambulance Service.
I have been a volunteer firefighter since 1987, and before taking office as your State Representative, I served on the Sharp County Quorum Court for more than 10 years.
During my time in the Arkansas House of Representatives, I have been honored to serve as Chair of the House Public Health–Health Services Subcommittee, Vice Chair of the House City, County and Local Affairs Committee, and a member of the House Public Health, Welfare and Labor Committee.
I also serve in several alternate and ex officio roles related to public safety, local government, energy, state insurance, corrections and legislative oversight.
The challenges facing our country, state and small communities are unlike anything I have seen.
As a dedicated public servant, I remain committed to ensuring that rural communities and conservative values are strongly represented in Little Rock.
As I complete my second term and look ahead to a third, I humbly ask for your continued prayers and support.
Thank you, and God bless.
