Education As an award-winning classroom teacher with 20+ years in public schools, I've seen the crisis up close.
California now ranks 44th in the nation — too many students graduate unable to read, write, or do basic math.
That's unacceptable.
I will fight for parental rights and school choice, defend parents' voice in their kids' education, and audit education spending so every dollar reaches the classroom.
Every student deserves the skills to succeed — in college, in a trade, or in the workforce.
Public Safety Public Safety Violent and property crime are rising, and Sacramento's soft-on-crime policies have made us less safe.
I oppose "Defunding the Police." I will fight for tougher penalties for repeat offenders, hold rogue DAs accountable, stop the refusal to prosecute, and keep dangerous felons behind bars.
It's time to put victims first.
Homelessness Protecting Kids and Parental Rights Public Safety Sacramento has spent billions on programs that only made homelessness worse.
We need real accountability.
I will call for a State Audit to track where the money went, expand mental health and addiction treatment to address the root causes, and make it easier to place people in care rather than leave them on the street.
Our neighborhoods must be cleaned up and kept safe.
Protecting Kids and Parental Rights Protecting Kids and Parental Rights Protecting Kids and Parental Rights Recent laws from Sacramento have put kids in danger and stripped parents of their right to protect them.
I'm a strong advocate for both kids and parents.
Parents must be informed if their child is at risk of self-harm.
Child removal by the state should be rare, and struggling families should get help early.
I will audit Child Protective Services, support families in crisis, and protect girls' private spaces and girls' sports.
How you can help: Sign the petition at ProtectKidsCA.com.
