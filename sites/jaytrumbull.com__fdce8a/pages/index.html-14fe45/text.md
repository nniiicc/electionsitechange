Jay Trumbull was elected to the Florida House of Representatives in 2014, where he served as Chair of the Appropriations Committee.
In 2022, he was elected to the Florida Senate.
During his many years of service to the people of Northwest Florida, he has championed numerous pieces of legislation to protect the elderly, fight inflation, and help to rebuild the area after Hurricane Michael and other natural disasters.
Jay's public service continues each day, as he works tirelessly with the elected officials and citizens of the region.
