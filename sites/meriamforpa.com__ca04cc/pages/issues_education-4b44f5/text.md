For over two decades, Meriam has chosen to live in Pennsylvania to raise her family.
There is no other place she would consider calling home.
Access to quality education is essential, and she will work tirelessly to ensure that our children have the support and resources they need to succeed.
Meriam’s approach centers on providing opportunities for teachers, parents, and students to collaborate on creating a robust and engaging learning experience.
She will fight to raise academic standards, encourage fair funding, and bolster our trade programs so our kids can continue to get the best education possible.
How Meriam Will Help Fair Funding for All Five Local Districts A state court ruled that Pennsylvania did not fund schools fairly.

No to Vouchers Most local families use public schools.

Public money belongs in public schools. ❌ Milou Mackenzie co-sponsored HB 1432 .

Supporting Teachers Good teachers need fair pay, smaller classes, and safe schools.
PSEA and AFT Pennsylvania have endorsed Meriam because she will stand up for public education. ❌ Milou Mackenzie backed plans that pull money from public schools.

Career and Technical Education College is not the only path to a good job.

Students gain real skills.
Local firms find good workers. ❌ Milou Mackenzie voted against a wide school funding plan.
