What Meriam Will Protect
No Drilling in State Parks or Forests
State parks and forests belong to all of us.
Keep them open for clean water, wildlife, and family trips—not oil and gas wells.
Milou Mackenzie was the main sponsor of HB 55.
Her bill would open state parks and forests to oil and gas drilling.
Protect Farmland and Green Space
Keep working farms in business, most of all in the Upper Perkiomen Valley.
State farm funds can help families hold their land.
Mackenzie’s drilling bill put oil and gas firms ahead of the public land we share.
Keep Water Clean
Saucon Creek and Green Lane Reservoir need care.
Fund stream banks, storm drains, and work that keeps dirty runoff out.
Milou Mackenzie voted no on HB 2241.
The bill would cut toxic waste from old batteries.
Build a Clean Energy Economy
Better windows and insulation cut power bills.
Grid work and clean power projects also create skilled jobs.
Our region should get both.
Milou Mackenzie voted no on SB 831.
The two-party carbon storage bill later became law.
Plan Growth Carefully
Growth should fit the town around it.
New projects must protect streams and farms, while paying for the roads and pipes they need.
