BOLD LEADERSHIP WE CAN COUNT ON
Donavan McKinney is a lifelong Detroiter, proud husband, and devoted father.
He grew up in northeast Detroit, and was raised by his mother and grandmother, where poverty wages led to many evictions and forced his family to move over 13 times.
A proud product of Detroit Public Schools and the first in the family to graduate from college (Go Blue!), Donavan worked as a leader of SEIU Healthcare Michigan and currently serves as State Representative where he is delivering tangible results for everyday people.
He credits his career in service to his mother, grandmother, and his surrounding community for coming together to invest in him despite their lack of resources.
Donavan wants to ensure Washington does the same for every Michigan family.
“I’m running FOR CONGRESS because our community deserves someone who has lived OUR struggles and will fight for us with the urgency that this moment demands.”
