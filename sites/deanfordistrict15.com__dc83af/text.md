Dean Tarulli for State Assembly
Elect a 15 year public servant, not a politician.
I’m Dean Tarulli, running to be the State Assembly representative for District 15 (Northeast Nassau County).
I’ve spent my career coordinating with government agencies and navigating state and federal programs.
The heavy reliance on tax payer money spent frivolous has to be put in check.
I firmly believe that local government should be doing a better job of utilizing what it has to reach more people in the most impactful way possible.
While currently working for the city of New York, I’ve also been the Director of Flood Mitigation for Westchester County and the Hurricane Relief Biologist for Superstorm Sandy across Long Island.
As someone with boots on the ground during tough times, I’m running to be your State level representative not as a career politician but as a concerned citizen who wants to protect my community from natural (and man-made) disasters, fix our aging infrastructure and see our tax dollars actually work in the neighborhoods we live in.
