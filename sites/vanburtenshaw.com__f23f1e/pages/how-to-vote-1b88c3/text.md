Request Your Absentee Ballot
COVID-19 has created a situation that no-one could have predicted.
ALL voting in the May 19, 2020 Primary Election will be done by absentee ballot, with no exceptions.
To request your absentee ballot please go to https://idahovotes.gov/vote-early-idaho/.
If you have questions call your County Clerk:

Clark County (208) 374-5304
Fremont County (208) 624-7332
Jefferson County (208) 745-7756
Please exercise your constitutional right to vote on May 19th!
