Meet Justin Pike
Justin has spent his career meeting people on hard days — as a soldier, then as a patrol officer and detective sitting at kitchen tables with families in crisis, standing beside victims who just needed someone to listen.
That work doesn't leave you.
It's why he sees this campaign the same way he's seen every call: show up, listen first, do the work.
"I don't meet voters in Whatcom County.
I meet the people who raised me, and the ones I'm raising my kids alongside.."
Whatcom County isn't just where Justin works — it's where his family has lived for generations, where his own kids are growing up.
Protecting this community means protecting the place that shaped him.

On the Issues
MAKING LIFE MORE AFFORDABLE
Justin will work to lower the cost of housing, groceries, utilities, and health care.
As a local business owner, he knows rising costs hurt families, workers, and small businesses.
BUILDING SAFER COMMUNITIES
Justin supports effective law enforcement, accountability for repeat offenders, and better access to behavioral health and addiction treatment.
HOLDING GOVERNMENT ACCOUNTABLE
Justin will push for transparent budgets, responsible spending, and measurable results.
