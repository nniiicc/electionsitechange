The Supreme Court only gave us 10 days to circulate and file nominating petitions to get on the ballot.
That did not deter our team as we collected more than 2,450 signatures to get on the ballot.
This is close to 5x (!!) the number of signatures required by law.
The outpouring of support from our community is overwhelming and incredibly humbling.
THANK YOU to everyone who knocked on doors, showed up at a petition signing event and helped me get on the ballot!
