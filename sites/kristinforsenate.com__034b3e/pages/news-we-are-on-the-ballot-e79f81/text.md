Thank you to over 1800 registered voters that signed a petition to place my name on the ballot to run for Senate in the 28th District.
I am humbled by the support for my candidacy and grateful that so many people share my belief that York County cannot afford to lose a strong voice for fiscal responsibility and reform in the Pennsylvania State Senate.
The journey continues, and with your help, I look forward to bringing my passion, drive, and knowledge to serving in the Pennsylvania Senate.
I will listen to you and bring your views and your values to Harrisburg.
One thing I know for certain regarding this campaign is that I can’t do it alone.
Please join our team.
By working together, we can change Harrisburg for the better.
