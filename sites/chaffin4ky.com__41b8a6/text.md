Rooted in Service
As a former Little Sister turned Big Sister and a Board member of Big Brothers Big Sisters, Alisha knows the power of giving back.
As Rotary’s Community Outreach Chair, she helps organize projects that uplift seniors, students, and neighbors.
Alisha is running for State House District 88 to ensure Kentuckians can live, work, and thrive in the communities they love.
Economic Relief for Working Kentuckians
Kentucky is strongest when working people can thrive.
I know that firsthand — my family was working-class, and when I was growing up, we sometimes relied on programs like SNAP and Social Security Survivor Benefits to get by.
Strong Public Schools
Every child deserves access to a quality public education, no matter where they live.
I’ll stand up for our public schools, teachers, and support staff and make sure classrooms have the tools they need to help every student succeed.
Healthcare Within Reach
Healthcare should be a right, not a privilege.
In an environment where insurance premiums are skyrocketing, I will support policies to bring down healthcare costs so families don’t have to choose between getting medical care and putting food on the table.
Affordable Housing
Every Kentuckian deserves a safe, steady place to call home.
Buying a house is one of the best ways to build security, but for a lot of young people, that dream feels out of reach.
I’ll work to make homeownership more affordable and protect renters until they get there, while supporting smart growth that keeps our neighborhoods strong and together.
Common-Sense Legislation
I believe Kentuckians deserve leadership that listens and leads with common sense.
Too often, politics gets in the way of progress.
In Frankfort, I’ll work across party lines to find solutions that put people before politics.
Let’s focus on what unites us rather than what divides us.
Pro-Union, Pro-Worker
I believe in the value of hard work and fair pay.
Unions built the middle class and continue to protect the rights and safety of working people.
My father was a union president, and I saw firsthand how strong unions bring dignity, stability, and opportunity to families and entire communities.
I’ll always stand with workers who want a fair wage, safe conditions, and a voice on the job.
