Rooted in Service: Alisha Chaffin Launches Campaign for Kentucky House District 88
Community Invited to Campaign Kickoff Celebration on November 4 at The Willow Lexington
LEXINGTON, KY — October 26, 2025 — The Campaign Fund for Alisha Dawn Chaffin for Kentucky House District 88 is proud to announce Rooted in Service: Alisha Chaffin’s Campaign Kickoff to be held on Tuesday, November 11, 2025, from 5:30 to 7:30 PM at The Willow Lexington, 825 National Avenue, Lexington, Kentucky.
This exciting event will mark the official launch of Chaffin’s campaign for the Kentucky House of Representatives.
Guests are invited to join for an evening of community, connection, and purpose as Chaffin and her team unveil their vision for a future rooted in service and dedicated to the people of Fayette and Scott Counties.
“This campaign is about more than politics—it’s about people,” said Alisha Chaffin, candidate for House District 88.
“Our communities deserve leadership that listens, serves, and acts with heart.
Together, we’re building something lasting—rooted in service, grounded in gratitude, and focused on the future of Kentucky families.”
The kickoff event will bring together neighbors, supporters, and community leaders who share Chaffin’s belief in collaboration and local progress.
Attendees will have the opportunity to meet the candidate, connect with others passionate about positive change, and learn how to get involved in the campaign.
Admission is free, and attendees are encouraged to RSVP to help the campaign plan for attendance.

About Alisha Chaffin
Alisha Chaffin is a Lexington-based community leader, small business owner, and advocate for service-driven leadership.
As a Democratic candidate for Kentucky House District 88, Chaffin’s campaign is grounded in gratitude, community, and action.
Her platform focuses on strengthening education, supporting working families, fostering sustainable growth, and ensuring government transparency—all rooted in service to the people she seeks to represent.
