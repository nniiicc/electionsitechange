I know that firsthand — my family was working-class , and when I was growing up, we sometimes relied on programs like SNAP and Social Security Survivor Benefits to get by.
Hard work is part of our identity , and I’ll support policies that help families build stability and opportunity by strengthening small businesses, expanding access to childcare, and easing the cost of living so people can get ahead, not just get by.
Economic opportunity shouldn’t depend on your ZIP code or who you know; it should be built on hard work, fairness, and community.
Strong Public Schools Every child deserves access to a quality public education, no matter where they live.
I’ll stand up for our public schools, teachers, and support staff and make sure classrooms have the tools they need to help every student succeed.
Teachers are the backbone of our education system.
They shape our children’s futures, and they deserve fair pay, respect, and the support to do what they do best, teach!
I know that strong schools build strong communities, and that investing in education means investing in Kentucky’s future.
Healthcare Within Reach Healthcare should be a right, not a privilege.
In an environment where insurance premiums are skyrocketing, I will support policies to bring down healthcare costs so families don’t have to choose between getting medical care and putting food on the table.
We must make prescriptions affordable , strengthen mental health care for children and adults, and protect access to quality care in rural communities so every Kentuckian deserves the chance to stay healthy and thrive.
Affordable Housing Every Kentuckian deserves a safe, steady place to call home .
Buying a house is one of the best ways to build security, but for a lot of young people, that dream feels out of reach.
I’ll work to make homeownership more affordable and protect renters until they get there, while supporting smart growth that keeps our neighborhoods strong and together.
Common-Sense Legislation I believe Kentuckians deserve leadership that listens and leads with common sense .
Too often, politics gets in the way of progress.
In Frankfort, I’ll work across party lines to find solutions that put people before politics.
Let’s focus on what unites us rather than what divides us.
I’ll always put the needs of our communities first and make decisions based on fairness, facts, and what’s right for Kentucky families.
Pro-Union, Pro-Worker I believe in the value of hard work and fair pay.
Unions built the middle class and continue to protect the rights and safety of working people.
My father was a union president, and I saw firsthand how strong unions bring dignity, stability, and opportunity to families and entire communities.
