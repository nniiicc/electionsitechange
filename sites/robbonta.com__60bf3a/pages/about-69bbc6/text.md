A Commitment to Justice Rob’s fight for justice is personal.
The son of farmworker organizers and an immigrant mom, he was inspired from a young age to fight back against corrupt insiders trying to exploit communities without access to wealth or influence.
His parents fled the Philippines to escape an authoritarian dictator and give Rob and his siblings the chance to grow up in a democracy, with freedom and the rule of law.
His career has been dedicated to fighting for the most vulnerable to have a fair shot.
He became a public attorney so hard-working families would have someone in their corner.
He served as a member of the Alameda City Council and spent nearly a decade as a State Assemblymember in the California legislature, where he introduced and passed legislation on everything from health care to civil rights to fair housing.
As California’s Attorney General, Rob has taken on the biggest and most important fights for justice, fairness, safety, and accountability.
Building a Future of Opportunity With California facing unprecedented challenges, Rob is running for re-election to keep fighting for your rights, protecting our democracy, and holding this lawless federal administration accountable whenever it crosses the line.
Rob will never stop working to protect Californians from bad actors, fight back against abuses of power, and build a future where justice and opportunity are available to each and every person in this great state.
Rob is a proud husband and father to three children — and he fights for your family as if it were his own.
