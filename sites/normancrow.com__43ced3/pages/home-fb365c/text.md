Skip to content About Experience Contact Donate Norman Crow About Experience Contact Donate Norman Crow Menu This Community’s Trusted, Conservative Leader Meet Norman Norman Crow is a native of the Tuscaloosa community and graduated in 1990 with a degree in accounting from the University of Alabama.
He began his career in the energy sector at Southern Natural Gas, Tenneco Energy, Duke Energy, Reliant Energy, and Innovative Energy Services.
He later owned and operated DT Freight, a local transportation business, and now serves as a licensed real estate agent with Hamner Real Estate.
Norman currently serves as the District Three representative on the Tuscaloosa City Council.
He is also a long-time businessman and former Tuscaloosa City Board of Education member.
With this experience, Crow brings a strong record of public service and private sector experience to the race.
Norman and his wife, Keeshan, have three children: Norman, Madeleine, and Meredith.
They are members of Trinity Presbyterian Church in Tuscaloosa.
