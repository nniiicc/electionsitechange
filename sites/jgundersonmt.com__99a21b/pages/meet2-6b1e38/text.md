Home Meet Events Get Involved On The Issues Gun Raffle Donate Home Meet Events Get Involved On The Issues Gun Raffle Donate A Bit About Me Jason Gunderson I grew up here in Montana in a small business family.
We didn’t have anything handed to us.
My parents worked long hours, kept their word, and lived within their means.
That’s just how I was raised.
You work hard.
You take responsibility.
You show up.
I chose a career in service because that felt natural to me.
For more than twenty two years, I’ve worked as a firefighter, EMT, and law enforcement officer.
I’ve been with people on some of the toughest days they’ll ever face.
When you see that side of life, it changes you.
It reminds you that policies and budgets aren’t abstract.
They affect real people in real moments.
Over the years, I’ve watched costs rise and trust in government fall.
I’ve seen working families stretched thin.
I don’t think government should make life harder than it already is.
It should stay focused, be accountable, and do its job well.
I’m running because I care about this place.
I want Montana to stay a place where hard work means something and where our kids can build a future without feeling pushed out.
I’m not interested in politics for its own sake.
I’m interested in steady leadership and doing the job the right way.
That’s the mindset I’d bring to Helena.
I believe our politics can be better.
When we put people above special interests, when we listen to the community and work for them, we can and will see change.
