Preserve Montana Ag Montana's agricultural heritage is the reason we are passionate about the outdoors, cautious about changes to our lifestyles, but most importantly, agriculture is the reason we are neighborly to those we don't know.
My opponent is a downtown Bozeman resident who is supported by the same people that try and change our way of life.
We need Montana Farmers and Ranchers.
Without them, we will lose the very root of what makes Montana special.
Protect Homeowners Owning a home is becoming its own form of taxation, whether its from costly property tax bills or simply the rising cost of living in Montana.
I will work tirelessly to bring significant, long-term changes to our property taxation, relieving some of the burdens of owning a home.
I will also work with my colleagues to find new ways of making Montana affordable through free market values that won't feed the beast that is government.
Keep Communities Safe As a member of the Livingston Police Department, I see firsthand the challenges that law enforcement officers and other public safety officers face due to government red tape and lack of funding.
Keeping our community safe will always be a top priority.
Demand Fiscal Accountability My opponent wanted to use taxpayer dollars to fund experimental weight loss medication rather than voting to fund crucial rural healthcare and affordability services.
He isn't cut out for Montana.
Our taxpayers dollars need to be used responsibly and effectively.
No more reckless spending.
Send me to Helena to be a voice for fiscal responsibility.
