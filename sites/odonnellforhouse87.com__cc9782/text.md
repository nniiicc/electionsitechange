Meet Tom
Tom O’Donnell grew up with his 13 siblings in an Iowa town of around a thousand people.
His father was a carpenter, and his mother was a homemaker and postal clerk.
Besides federal grants, Tom pumped gas, set hotel meeting rooms and had other jobs to pay his way through Iowa State University.
He started working for newspapers at 17 and spent more than 20 years as an Iowa reporter and editor, including almost 16 years with the Des Moines Register.
Tom next focused on science, first as a freelancer and then for 16 years at a nonprofit company.
Tom and his wife, Paula Mohr, live in her childhood home near Keosauqua.
He has served on the Bentonsport Improvement Association board and helped Van Buren County Conservation win a $1.6 million grant to rehabilitate the historic Bentonsport Pedestrian Bridge over the Des Moines River.
He volunteers at Van Buren County Community Schools.
Tom is a board member for the Rural Restoration Project, a nonprofit that seeks to build economic and social opportunities in the five counties of Iowa Senate District 44.
