On February 1, 2026, the Tom O’Donnell O’Donnell campaign launched with gatherings in Mt.
Pleasant, Fairfield and Bentonsport.
The Mt.
Pleasant event at St.
Michael’s Episcopal Church followed the weekly First Amendment rally at the Henry County Courthouse.
In Fairfield, supporters met at the public library.
The Bentonsport party was at the Stone House community building.
Tom delivered brief remarks and about 30 people at each party enjoyed cookies, snacks and beverages.
Our thanks to Eric Weston (Mt.
Pleasant and Bentonsport) and John McKerley (Fairfield) for their photography skills.
