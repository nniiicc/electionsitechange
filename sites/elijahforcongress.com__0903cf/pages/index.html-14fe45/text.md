Election Day
Tuesday, November 3, 2026 — polls open 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m.
Request your vote-by-mail ballot early; the deadline to have one mailed to you is 5:00 p.m. on Thursday, October 22.
01
Request your ballot from your county Supervisor of Elections, then return it by mail or to a secure drop-off location.
Ballots must be received by 7:00 p.m. on Election Day — postmarks don’t count, so mail early.
02
Skip the Election Day lines and vote in person at any early-voting site in your county.
Dates, hours, and locations are set by each county Supervisor of Elections — confirm before you go.
