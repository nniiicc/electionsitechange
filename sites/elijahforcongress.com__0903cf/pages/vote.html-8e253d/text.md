01 Vote by Mail Request your ballot from your county Supervisor of Elections, fill it out, and return it by mail or to a secure drop-off location.
Your ballot must be received by your Supervisor of Elections by 7:00 p.m. on Election Day — postmarks don’t count, so mail early.
Seminole Volusia 02 Vote Early Skip the Election Day lines and vote in person at any early-voting site in your county.
Early-voting dates, hours, and locations are set by each county Supervisor of Elections — confirm before you go.
