About Issues Endorsements News Vote Volunteer Contact Donate Key Objectives As a public figure, Ryan Elijah has been immersed in the community and has heard your concerns.
Once elected, he will bring them to those who can help get them addressed.
Affordability & Economy Congress must continue driving down costs for American families by keeping taxes low and achieving American energy independence.
Lower gas prices are critical to our economy because they reduce costs for families, businesses, and everything we buy.
It’s past time to put a stop to out of control government spending that acts to drive up everyday costs for American families and our national debt.
Ryan understands that burdensome federal regulations will slow job creation and economic growth.
By unleashing the world’s best economy through lower taxes, less regulation, and smart growth, we will make America more affordable and prosperous for millions of Americans.
Healthcare The United States must continue to lead the world in medical innovation.
The federal government can do more to lower healthcare costs.
Ryan supports solutions that deliver affordable, patient-centered healthcare options.
He also believes healthcare costs can be reduced by providing individuals with tax benefits, such as expanded Health Savings Accounts.
Additionally, Ryan supports greater transparency in the system, including requirements for clear pricing from hospitals, insurers, and other providers.
Consumer Protection Floridians lose hundreds of millions of dollars to fraud every year.
During his television career, Ryan spent years fighting for consumers—taking on complex federal cases and recovering thousands of dollars for victims.
He will support strong consumer-protection legislation and aggressively pursue those who prey on Floridians, especially our most vulnerable senior citizens.
Beach Protection Volusia County’s beaches drive a $4.3 billion economy and serve as our first line of defense against storms.
Hurricanes Ian and Nicole severely damaged our shoreline.
Ryan will prioritize working with federal agencies to secure long-term beach nourishment and storm-damage reduction projects.
Education Ryan will strongly support our schools.
His boys were educated in both public and private schools.
He believes both are critical to our children’s success and our community’s future.
That’s why he champions parental rights and school choice, empowering families to make the best decisions for their kids and have transparency on curriculum.
Federal funding is important for the district, with the most recent audit showing Seminole County’s budget is comprised of nearly 14% federal funding, while Volusia County is closer to 20%.
(National Center for Education Statistics, 2022/23 school year) We must protect local control and ensure every dollar serves our students effectively.
Faith, Family and Life Family is the foundation of our strong communities in Central Florida.
Ryan is active in his church and believes in protecting family values.
As your representative, he will champion policies that support parents, protect children, and keep families united and strong.
Ryan is pro-life and believes every life is a sacred gift from God.
The Military & Veterans Ryan comes from a military family, with both his father and grandfather having served.
We must ensure that the U.S. military remains the strongest and best-equipped fighting force in the world, backed by the most advanced technology.
We must also guarantee that our veterans receive the best possible care after their service to our nation.
Law Enforcement Ryan is a strong defender of law enforcement and deeply understands the daily challenges officers face.
He will strongly support legislation that protects law enforcement, including tougher penalties for assaulting or shooting officers.
He is a staunch supporter of the Second Amendment and has earned an “A” rating with the NRA.
Ryan has received multiple honors for his outstanding support of law enforcement.
Illegal Immigration America is a nation built by immigrants, however we must enforce the rule of law.
We must continue to secure our borders and oppose any measures that weaken enforcement or prioritize non-citizens over American safety.
Ryan will work with local law enforcement to stop the flood of illegal drugs that damage our Central Florida communities.
He strongly supports the Save America Act, which ensures only American citizens can vote in our elections — because citizenship must matter.
