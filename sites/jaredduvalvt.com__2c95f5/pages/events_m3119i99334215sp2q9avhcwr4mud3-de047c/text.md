The primary election is Tuesday August 11th.
For Montpelier voters, polls will be open at City Hall from 7am - 7pm.
Same day voter registration is available if you are not already registered to vote.
If you will not be able to vote on Election Day, you can vote early by going to the City Clerk’s office in City Hall during business hours.
