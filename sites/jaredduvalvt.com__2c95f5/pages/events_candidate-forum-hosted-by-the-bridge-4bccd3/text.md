The Bridge and ORCA media are hosting a forum with the three candidates running in the Democratic Primary for the two House seats representing Montpelier (Washington-4) in the state Legislature.
The forum will be held in City Hall and is open to the public.
For those who can’t attend in person, the forum will be recorded by ORCA media and streamed live on the websites of both The Bridge and ORCA Media.
