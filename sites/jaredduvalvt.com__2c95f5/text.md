Growing up in a working class family, my first commitment will always be to the economic security of Vermonters and Vermont families.
For policy-makers to meaningfully improve people’s lives, I know that good intentions are not enough.
My approach to policy making is grounded in evidence, open-mindedness, and collaboration.
I trust that with humility and hard work, together we can solve problems and help improve people’s lives.
Because that’s what politics is — or should be — about.
To me, politics matters because policy matters, and policy matters because people matter.
I love Montpelier and Vermont and offer deep experience in statewide policy research, analysis, and development – from economic to energy policy.
I hope to earn your support on or before the November 3rd general election and to serve alongside Representative Kate McCann in representing Montpelier (the Washington-4 District) in the Legislature .
