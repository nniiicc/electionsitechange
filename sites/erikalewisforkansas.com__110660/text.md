My name is Erika Lewis, and I am running to represent Kansas House District 98 because I believe our communities deserve practical leadership, honest conversations, and solutions that put people first.
Whether you're a lifelong Republican, Democrat, Independent, or someone who feels disconnected from politics altogether, you're welcome here.
Like many families across Kansas, I've experienced the challenges facing our communities firsthand.
I've seen families struggle with rising costs, schools stretched thin, small businesses working harder than ever, and neighbors feeling unheard by those elected to represent them.
I am running because I believe government works best when leaders listen before they speak, collaborate before they divide, and focus on solving problems rather than scoring political points.
As a mental health professional, community advocate, parent, and Kansan by choice, I've dedicated my career to helping people navigate challenges, build stronger relationships, and find practical paths forward.
Those same values will guide my work in Topeka.
This campaign is built on the belief that we have far more in common than we have differences.
Together, we can strengthen our schools, support working families, promote responsible economic growth, protect individual freedoms, and ensure government remains accountable to the people it serves.
I invite you to explore this website, learn more about my vision for District 98, and discover how you can become part of this campaign.
Whether through your vote, your ideas, your volunteer efforts, or your financial support, your involvement matters.
Let's build a stronger future for Kansas—together.
