Home Meet Erika Photos Issues Contribute Volunteer Yard Signs Contact The Issues That Matter I didn't enter this race to participate in political theater.
I entered this race to help solve problems.
Every day, I work with children, families, educators, and community members who are doing everything they can to build a better life.
They aren't asking for political talking points.
They're asking for practical solutions, responsible leadership, and elected officials who will listen.
These are the priorities I will fight for in Topeka.
Affordability & Economic Stability No family should have to choose between paying the electric bill, putting food on the table, or keeping a roof over their head.
The rising costs of groceries, housing, utilities, childcare, and everyday necessities continue to place enormous pressure on Kansas families.
We must come together to identify practical, realistic solutions that strengthen financial stability, support working families, and help ensure every Kansan can meet their basic needs with dignity.
Strong communities begin with stable households.
Property Taxes People should not be taxed out of the homes they worked their entire lives to afford.
I support responsible property tax relief that protects homeowners while maintaining strong schools, safe communities, and reliable infrastructure.
We need greater transparency, targeted relief for working families and seniors, and smarter government spending so taxpayers get value for every dollar they pay.
Education for Every Child A quality education should not depend on a family's zip code.
Every child in Kansas deserves access to excellent educational opportunities, whether they live in a rural community, a growing suburb, or an urban neighborhood.
Public education is one of the most important investments we make in our future, and we must continue working toward equitable access, resources, and opportunities for all students.
Our educators are experts.
They deserve our respect, our support, and a seat at the table when decisions impacting students are made.
When we invest in education, we invest in the future of Kansas.
Early Childhood Intervention & Family Support The earlier we identify challenges and provide support, the greater the opportunity to change a child's trajectory for the better.
Families should have access to the services and resources they need, including speech therapy, occupational therapy, developmental supports, services for neurodivergent children, and respite resources for caregivers.
Early intervention isn't just good for children—it's an investment in stronger families, healthier communities, and long-term success.
Strengthening Special Education Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, grow, and reach their full potential.
Kansas must continue working toward a special education system that is adequately funded, properly staffed, and equipped with the resources necessary to support students with diverse learning needs.
Students with disabilities and learning differences deserve the same access to high-quality education as every other child.
Families deserve strong advocates, educators deserve strong support, and students deserve every opportunity to succeed.
Expanding Mental Health Access Mental health is not a luxury.
It is a necessity.
Kansas faces significant challenges related to mental health access, provider shortages, and increasing needs among children, families, and adults.
We must prioritize mental health education, strengthen the workforce pipeline, improve access to care, and reduce barriers that prevent people from receiving support.
Mental health is deeply connected to education, workforce development, healthcare outcomes, foster care, adoption, and overall community well-being.
When we strengthen mental health systems, we strengthen Kansas families.
Child Welfare, Foster Care & Adoption Children deserve safety, stability, and the opportunity to thrive.
We must continue improving systems that support foster children, adoptive families, kinship caregivers, and biological families working toward reunification.
Stronger collaboration, earlier intervention, and better access to services can help improve outcomes for children while supporting the families who care for them.
The well-being of our children must remain a priority.
Collaboration Over Division The challenges facing Kansas will not be solved through partisan battles or political division.
Real progress happens when people come together, listen to one another, and work toward common goals.
I believe government works best when it elevates community voices, encourages collaboration, and focuses on solutions rather than conflict.
My commitment is simple: I will listen, learn, and work alongside the people of District 98.
No more division.
More collaboration.
More listening.
More solutions.
Because putting people before politics isn't just a slogan—it's how we move Kansas forward.
