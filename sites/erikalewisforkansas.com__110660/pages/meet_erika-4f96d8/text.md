Home Meet Erika Photos Issues Contribute Volunteer Yard Signs Contact Meet Erika About Erika Lewis I am a wife, a mother of six, a bonus mom to three more, a military family both veteran and active duty, a mental health professional, and a lifelong advocate for children, families, and stronger communities.
This year, I hope to add one more title: public servant.
I am not running for the Kansas House of Representatives because it's glamorous.
Quite the opposite.
I am running because, like so many Kansans, I am exhausted.
Exhausted by the political theatrics.
Exhausted by the constant division.
Exhausted by leaders who spend more time fighting each other than fighting for the people they were elected to serve.
The people of Kansas deserve better.
They deserve a representative who listens before speaking.
One who values people over party, solutions over slogans, and service over self-interest.
They deserve someone willing to sit at the table with people from every background, every belief system, and every political affiliation to do the hard work of moving our communities forward.
As a mental health professional, I have spent my career listening to the stories behind the struggles.
I have sat with families carrying impossible burdens, parents trying to make ends meet, children facing challenges they never should have to face, and individuals who feel unseen, unheard, and forgotten.
What I have learned is simple: people want to be heard.
They want to feel valued.
They want leaders who understand that policy decisions aren't just numbers on a spreadsheet—they affect real people, real families, and real lives.
Too many families are carrying more stress, anxiety, and uncertainty than ever before.
Too many are working harder while falling further behind.
Too many feel like their voices don't matter.
Enough is enough.
I am running to elevate the voices of Kansas—to ensure that every resident knows they have someone fighting for them, listening to them, and working on their behalf.
Putting People Before Politics isn't a campaign slogan.
It's my mission.
It's the belief that government should work for the people, not political agendas.
That leadership should be measured by service, not headlines.
And that when we choose compassion, accountability, and common sense over division, there is no challenge we cannot overcome together.
I am ready to get to work.
I hope you'll join me.
