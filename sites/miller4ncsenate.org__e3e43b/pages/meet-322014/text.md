Dedicated to liberty, rights, and ending government overreach in District 16
I am Jonathan D.
Miller, resident of Cary and a Wake County resident since 2007.
My family is from Pamlico County, North Carolina, going back several generations.
However, it was my great misfortune to be born outside of the great state of North Carolina.
I lived in Florida until I was 12, but I spent all my summers in Pamlico County.
I then lived in New Jersey and New York for a decade-and-a-half, finally making it back to my home North Carolina in my late twenties.
I’ve worked in carpentry and owned a few small retail businesses for most of the last 20 years, which enabled me to learn firsthand how overregulation hurts workers and small business owners every day.
And I’ve lived the effects of outdated laws on personal choices that lead to unfair consequences for non-violent people.
As a Libertarian, I stand for free speech, lower taxes, school choice for parents, and less government in our lives.
I believe in individual responsibility over politician control.
My goal is a freer North Carolina with true liberty and justice for all.
Let’s build it together!
