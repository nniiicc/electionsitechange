NC Senate district 16 includes wonderful communities across western Wake County, including Cary, Apex, Morrisville, Holly Springs, Fuquay-Varina, and surrounding areas.
I admire the hardworking families, innovative businesses in tech and retail, and strong independent spirit here.
People value opportunity, freedom, and hard work.
But high costs, regulations, and government interference hold us back.
With more liberty—less taxes, fewer rules, real choices—our entire district can thrive even more.
