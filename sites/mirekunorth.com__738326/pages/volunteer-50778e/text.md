Why Volunteer?
When you volunteer, you're not just supporting a campaign—you're investing in our community's future.
You'll meet neighbors who share your passion for progress, learn new skills, and make a real impact on the issues that matter most to you.
Together, we're building:
- Stronger schools that prepare every child for success
- Affordable housing so families can thrive in District 14
- Economic opportunity for small businesses and working families
- Healthcare access for all our neighbors
- A more just and equitable Maryland
How You Can Help
- Community Event Support: Help at community events and neighborhood gatherings
- Phone & Text Banking: Connect with voters from home through calls and text messages
- Host an Event or Party: Open your home for a meet-and-greet or community conversation
- Canvassing & Door Knocking: Meet neighbors face-to-face and have real conversations about what matters
- Display a Yard Sign: Show your support by placing a Bernice campaign sign in your yard or window
- Social Media & Digital Outreach: Create engaging content, graphics, and posts to amplify our message online
- Communications & Writing: Help with newsletters, emails, press releases, and campaign messaging
Have another unique skill or idea?
Every talent helps us build a stronger campaign!
