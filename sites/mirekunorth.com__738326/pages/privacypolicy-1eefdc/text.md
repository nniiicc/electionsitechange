Effective Date: June 2026

1.
Overview
Friends of Bernice Mireku-North ("Campaign," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy of supporters, volunteers, and constituents who engage with our campaign.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information — including mobile phone numbers — in connection with our SMS communications program and campaign activities.
2.
Information We Collect
We collect the following information when you complete our volunteer sign-up form or otherwise engage with the Campaign:
- Full name
- Email address
- Mobile phone number
- Volunteer interests and activity preferences
- SMS consent status and date of consent
We do not collect financial account information, medical information, or any other sensitive personal data through our volunteer sign-up form or SMS program.
3.
How We Collect Your Mobile Number
We collect mobile phone numbers through our online volunteer sign-up form hosted on the Campaign website.
At the point of collection, an unchecked SMS consent checkbox presents the following disclosures before any message is sent:
- The identity of the sender (Friends of Bernice Mireku-North)
- The types of messages to be sent (campaign updates, election reminders, volunteer coordination, and get-out-the-vote notifications)
- That message frequency varies
- That message and data rates may apply
- How to opt out (reply STOP)
- How to request help (reply HELP)
- Links to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service
No SMS message is sent until the supporter affirmatively checks the consent box and submits the form.
Consent is not a condition of participation in any Campaign activity.
4.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information solely for the following Campaign purposes:
- Sending SMS messages to opted-in supporters regarding campaign updates, election reminders, volunteer coordination, and get-out-the-vote notifications
- Coordinating volunteer activities and event participation
- Responding to inquiries submitted through our website
- Maintaining records of SMS opt-in consent for compliance purposes
We do not use your information for any commercial purpose and do not sell your information to any third party.
5.

Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of participation in any Campaign activity.
6.
No Third-Party Sharing
Friends of Bernice Mireku-North does not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent data with any third party for marketing or commercial purposes.
Text message opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
Your mobile number may be shared with our SMS platform provider solely to deliver messages on our behalf.
Such providers are contractually prohibited from using your information for any other purpose.
7.
Data Retention
SMS opt-in and opt-out consent records are retained for a minimum of four (4) years from the date of consent or the date of the last message, whichever is later.
Other personal information is retained for the duration of the campaign and for a reasonable period thereafter as required by applicable law.

You will receive one confirmation and no further messages will be sent.
Opting out does not affect your ability to volunteer or participate in Campaign activities.

1.
SMS Program Description
Friends of Bernice Mireku-North ("Campaign") operates a registered political SMS messaging program.
We send recurring SMS messages exclusively to supporters and volunteers who have provided express written consent through our online volunteer sign-up form.
Messages sent through this program include:
- Campaign updates and candidate news
- Election reminders and get-out-the-vote notifications
- Volunteer coordination and event information
- Civic engagement updates related to Maryland District 14
We do not send commercial marketing or promotional messages through this program.
2.
How to Opt In
You opt in to receive SMS messages from the Campaign by completing the online volunteer sign-up form on our website and affirmatively checking the SMS consent checkbox.
The checkbox is unchecked by default and clearly discloses:
- The sender identity (Friends of Bernice Mireku-North)
- The types of messages to be received
- That message frequency varies
- That message and data rates may apply
- How to opt out (reply STOP)
- How to request help (reply HELP)
- Links to our Privacy Policy and these Terms of Service
Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of volunteering, donating, or participating in any Campaign activity.
3.
Opt-Out — Reply STOP
You may opt out of receiving SMS messages at any time by replying STOP to any message.
Upon receipt of your STOP request:
- You will receive one final confirmation message
- No further SMS messages will be sent to your number
- Your opt-out will be recorded and retained for compliance purposes
To resubscribe, text START to the same number or contact us directly.
