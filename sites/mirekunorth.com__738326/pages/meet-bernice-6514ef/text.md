Contribute Build with Bernice Hello, My name is Bernice Mireku-North.
I am a Democrat and member of the House of Delegates, representing District 14.
Welcome Message from Bernice My Story Personal and Academic Background I'm a daughter of Ghanaian immigrants, wife, mom, sister, auntie, and cousin to family members from Ghana, across the U.S. and Mexico and Europe.
I am a product of Montgomery County Public Schools.
After graduating from Montgomery Blair High School's Communication Arts Program, I enrolled and graduated from the University of Maryland, College Park.
I earned my Juris Doctorate at Howard University School of Law.
I currently reside in the Fairland area of Silver Spring with my husband Terrill and daughter Adjoa.
Professional Background After two trial judicial clerkships, I served as Assistant State's Attorney for six years before going into private practice.
I am currently Managing Attorney of The North Law Group, LLC and have been active in various bar associations.
Since 2023, I serve as Delegate for District 14 to ensure we have laws that promote safer, stronger, and healthier communities for all.
In Annapolis, I serve on the Ways and Means Committee, Local Revenues Subcommittee, and Early Childhood Subcommittee.
I am also a member of the Legislative Black Caucus, Legislative Latino Caucus, Transit Caucus, and the Women Legislators of Maryland.
When I am not in session, I am serving individuals in my capacity as a lawyer in criminal defense, family law, wills and trusts and business litigation. ​ ​ Community Background I have served my community for over 15 years, with leadership roles that included being Chair of the Silver Spring Citizens Advisory Board, Co-Chair of the Reimagining Public Safety Task Force.
I am currently a member of the Eta Theta Sigma Alumnae Chapter of Sigma Gamma Rho Sorority, Inc., and a Board member of Silver Spring Village and WeAchieve.​ #BuildwithBernice Join the movement.
