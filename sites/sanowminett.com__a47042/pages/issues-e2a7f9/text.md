This means: putting rural communities first, balance — not extremes, and a transparent government.
01 Rural Communities Southwest Minnesota deserves strong communities, reliable service, and a voice at the table.
Goals Expand broadband access Increase rural infrastructure investment Support small town grant programs Protect local schools, healthcare, and emergency services Review and fix state systems that make rural development harder 02 Protect the Family Farm When farms disappear, communities disappear.
Goals Review subsidy structures to reduce harmful overproduction incentives Support biofuel innovation for energy independence and farm stability Push back against unchecked corporate consolidation Keep family farms economically viable 03 Government Transparency & Accountability We deserve honesty about where our tax dollars are being spent.
Goals Regular public stakeholder summits Transparent budgeting Review inefficient state contracting and bidding systems Performance reviews of state agencies and policies 04 Healthcare & Mental Health Rural healthcare is not optional infrastructure: it is necessary for the safety and growth of our communities.
Goals Increase rural healthcare funding Improve nursing home staffing standards Expand mental health access Protect reproductive healthcare access Prioritize mental healthcare and evidence-based approaches 05 Public Safety & Community Safety Public safety works best when communities trust the systems designed to protect them.
Goals Support first responders and emergency services Improve mental health and addiction intervention Encourage community trust and accountability Focus on violence prevention and practical solutions 06 Education Our educators and children deserve support to set them up for success.
Goals Teacher retention study and workforce investment Improved public school funding Local flexibility over statewide culture-war mandates Policies and initiatives that are student and educator led 07 Immigration We can support secure systems and still treat people with dignity.
Goals Support lawful, orderly systems Protect workers from exploitation Support local integration and community stability Oppose dehumanization 08 Energy & Environment We cannot protect the economy by ignoring the environment, and we cannot protect the environment by ignoring rural economies.
