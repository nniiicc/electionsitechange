Get in Touch About the Candidate Meet Caitlyn Caitlyn Sanow-Minett is a leader, advocate and lifelong southwest Minnesota resident running to represent District 21 in the Minnesota State Senate.
Raised in Tracy, she now lives in Lake Benton with her husband.
Caitlyn comes from a family rooted in agriculture, education and health care.
She is running because she believes politics should be community-driven.
Caitlyn's vision for District 21 is built on three core principles: putting rural communities first, choosing balance over extremes, and creating a more transparent government that works for everyone.
