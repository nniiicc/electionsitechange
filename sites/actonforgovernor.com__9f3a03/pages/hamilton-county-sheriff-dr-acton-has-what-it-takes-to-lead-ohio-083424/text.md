Hamilton County Sheriff: “Dr.
Acton Has What it Takes to Lead Ohio”
“Vivek Ramaswamy is dead set on using the power of the governor’s office to destroy local police and fire departments.”
COLUMBUS, OH– Last week, The Cincinnati Enquirer published an op-ed from Hamilton County Sheriff Charmaine McGuffey, who wrote about how a vote for Dr.
Amy Acton is a vote to “keep people safe.” Sheriff McGuffey described Vivek Ramaswamy’s “outright dangerous” tax plans that would slash “critical funding” for first responders.
The sheriff also highlighted Dr.
Acton’s “bold pledge” to fully fund all of Ohio’s local law enforcement, stand with labor, and strengthen community partnerships, demonstrating “the kind of courage” she wants Ohio’s governor to have.
READ: Why this longtime Ohio sheriff is endorsing Amy Acton for governor
- After serving the people of Hamilton County for 40 years, including the past six as sheriff, I have seen firsthand the impact that both strong and ineffective leadership in Columbus can have on public safety.
That’s why I’m endorsing Dr.
Amy Acton to become Ohio’s next governor.
- Keeping Ohioans safe has never been, and should never be, a partisan issue.
At its core, it’s about making sure our officers, deputies and all public safety officers have the resources they need to do their jobs well and return home safely every day.
It’s also about ensuring that families, whether they’re in Crosby or Canton, can trust that when they call, first responders will show up.
- As a doctor, Amy Acton understands just how vital that is.
- I know Dr.
Acton has what it takes to lead Ohio because I’ve already seen her in action.
Through her work as a lifelong public servant, most recently serving under Gov.
Mike DeWine, Dr.
Acton has never wavered in her commitment to keeping Ohioans safe.
- That work taught her a valuable lesson that career politicians seem to never learn: To keep people safe, you must invest in all aspects of public safety, like services for mental health and addiction recovery.
- Giving our communities the tools they need is just as important as having the most up-to-date equipment.
Dr.
Acton understands that, and she’s committed to supporting those programs.
She’s made a bold pledge to fully fund local law enforcement across Ohio, strengthen partnerships between local departments and their communities, and protect the collective bargaining rights of our hardworking officers across the state.
- It takes courage to unabashedly stand with labor, and that’s the kind of courage I want my next governor to have.
- Her opponent’s plans for Ohio aren’t just different from Dr.
Acton’s; they’re outright dangerous.
- Vivek Ramaswamy is dead set on using the power of the governor’s office to destroy local police and fire departments.
His plan to eliminate property taxes would cut the critical funding for these departments and would threaten the safety of Ohioans across the state.
- In Hamilton County, under Ramaswamy’s plan, there would be fewer officers on the road, longer waiting times for medical emergencies, and departments having to make impossible choices about cutting essential services.
He cannot claim to have the backing of law enforcement while running on this disastrous plan.
- Dr.
Acton often says that first responders helped her and her brother during their rough childhood in Youngstown.
Dr.
Acton experienced abuse, neglect and homelessness, and she’s said that it was first responders who didn’t look the other way that helped save her life.
- As a sheriff, my job is to keep people safe.
I know by voting for Dr.
Acton, I will be.
