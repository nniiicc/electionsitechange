Amy Acton on The 11th Hour: “I’ve worked with people on both sides of the aisle to try to improve the lives of Ohioans” COLUMBUS, OH– Yesterday, Dr.
Amy Acton appeared on MS NOW’s the 11th Hour with Ali Velshi, where she discussed the state of her campaign to become Ohio’s next governor.
Dr.
Acton reiterated that she is “laser-focused on the needs of everyday Ohioans” and called out self-funding billionaire Vivek Ramaswamy for having the most “out-of-touch” and “reckless plans possible for Ohio.” WATCH: Dr.
Amy Acton on MS NOW’s The 11th Hour with Ali Velshi “95% of what I hear about no one disagrees on.
It is the cost of everyday living, from child care to health care costs, trying to rent, let alone buy a house, property taxes going up, electric bills going up.” “People are longing for someone and for public servants who actually solve problems instead of making problems.” “[Vivek Ramaswamy] has some of the most… reckless plans possible for Ohio.” “I’m an Ohio girl through-and-through.
I grew up in Youngstown, Ohio, the stuff of Bruce Springsteen songs, and I experienced a lot of neglect and abuse.
I know what it’s like to feel this way.” “People delivering [babies] roadside in Ohio, the seventh largest state… [with] some of the largest health institutions.
And yet with these Medicaid changes coming, you know, we’re having rural hospitals collapse, urban.” “[Vivek Ramaswamy said] Medicaid and Medicare are mistakes, he wants to dismantle them.” “And recently he has said that Social Security needs work requirements.
