About Betsy Priorities Support Donate Contact Select Page About Betsy Meet Betsy Fogle Betsy Fogle is a lifelong Springfield resident and proud graduate of Springfield Public Schools.
She earned her degree in sociology from Missouri State University and a master’s degree from the University of Arkansas.
Betsy Fogle spent her career at Jordan Valley Community Health Center, helping low-income patients navigate barriers that went far beyond medical care.
She worked closely with families struggling to afford prescriptions, childcare, housing, and transportation.
Her work in the OBGYN department deepened her commitment to supporting mothers and babies and expanding access to care.
That experience made something very clear to Betsy: state government was not listening to the real needs of the community - so she ran for office.
In 2020, Betsy Fogle flipped a State House seat and went to represent Springfield in the State Capitol.
"I’ve worked to expand healthcare, invest in childcare and mental health services, and stand up for public schools, working families, and against corruption - and I’m running for State Senate to keep delivering results for Springfield." In the Legislature Betsy helped implement Medicaid expansion, granting healthcare access for more than 300,000 Missourians.
She fought to extend postpartum Medicaid coverage for new mothers.
She helped secure funding for a behavioral crisis center and a pediatric crisis center in Springfield, expanding much-needed access to mental health services.
She delivered millions of dollars in childcare investments to help working parents.
Betsy Fogle Stands up For public schools, union workers, family farmers, and first responders.
She supports organized labor and believes strong unions build strong communities.
She opposes special tax breaks for corporations if it means increasing taxes for everyone else.
Betsy is a bridge builder focused on results.
She believes in upholding the will of the voters, defending local control, and keeping government out of personal decisions.
Betsy believes in a Missouri where opportunity isn’t a limited resource, where small towns, big cities, and everyone in between can succeed.
Betsy Fogle is running for State Senate because Springfield deserves leadership that listens, delivers, and fights for you.
