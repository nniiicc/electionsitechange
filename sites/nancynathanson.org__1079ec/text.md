Legislative Work
Nancy works on a wide variety of issues, from health care, consumer protection, and transportation to getting the most out of taxpayer dollars.
Themes woven into her work include accountability, preparedness, and data-driven decision making.
Focus areas have included cybersecurity; climate change, energy conservation, and reducing reliance on fossil fuel; access to affordable prescription drugs and primary health care; protecting children, seniors, and consumers; and stable school funding.
Priorities
Some of these priorities are new as we move past the pandemic, including housing throughout the spectrum, from emergency and temporary to multi-family and middle housing, and mental and behavioral health supports.
Other priorities have been themes throughout my legislative work, writing and passing bills to fix problems, close loopholes, and move Oregon forward.
My priorities include protecting consumers and vulnerable Oregonians and steering Oregon toward the future - which is why I work on modernizing systems, investing in the future we want and not simply continuing the past, and advocating in specific areas like passenger rail service, clean air and clean water, and re-shaping health care and emergency response systems.
In the District
From helping a local child care business owner who pointed out costly state forms and mailings, and lactation consultants providing services to new moms, to pushing for safety improvements on Delta Highway, Nancy is always working for you.
“I spend time talking with and listening to the concerns of people here at home.
Some of those meetings have been the spark that spurred legislation that I passed.”
