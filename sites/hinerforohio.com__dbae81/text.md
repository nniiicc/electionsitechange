Meet Mark Mark and his wife, Veronica, have been married for more than 35 years and live in Knox County near Howard.
They are proud parents of two children and grandparents to three grandchildren and two grandpups.
Raised in Ashland, Ohio, Mark learned the value of hard work at an early age by helping in his family’s small business.
Whether assisting customers or repairing equipment, he developed a passion for solving problems and finding practical solutions—qualities that continue to guide his work in the Ohio House of Representatives.
While attending Ashland High School, Mark began an internship at WNCO Radio that led to a career in broadcasting.
Starting as a weekend disc jockey, he advanced into engineering and eventually became a business owner.
With a degree in Broadcast Engineering Technology and an MBA, Mark has built multiple successful businesses from the ground up, giving him firsthand experience with the challenges facing Ohio’s job creators, workers, and families.
As your State Representative, Mark has brought that business experience and common- sense approach to Columbus.
He understands how taxes, regulations, and government policies affect families, farmers, and small businesses, so he continues to fight for policies that strengthen Ohio’s economy while protecting taxpayers.
Still a strong supporter of the Second Amendment, he remains steadfast in protecting the constitutional rights of law-abiding citizens.
