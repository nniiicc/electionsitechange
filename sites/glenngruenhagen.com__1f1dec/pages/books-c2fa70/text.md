Health Care in Crisis: Is Government the Solution or the Problem?
Everyone agrees U.S. health care is in a crisis.
But who can fix it?
Will government provide the ultimate solution to provide affordable health care for everyone?
Or will government begin to ration medical care, and take control over life and death decisions?
What about ObamaCare?
Is it the answer, or the problem?
When you finish reading this book, you will no longer have questions, but you will want to do something about it.
