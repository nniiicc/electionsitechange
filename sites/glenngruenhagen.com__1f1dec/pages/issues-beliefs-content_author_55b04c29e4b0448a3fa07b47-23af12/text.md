Right to Life
Glenn is pro-life from conception to natural death and views the right to life as the most fundamental right we have.
MN Judicial Reform
Glenn believes we need to restore the proper role of the judiciary in Minnesota by electing judges that simply interpret the law instead of legislating from the bench.
MN Healthcare Reform
Glenn supports healthcare reform in Minnesota through the power of free-markets to both reduce cost and increase quality for the benefit of all.
2nd Amendment
Glenn is a strong supporter of the 2nd Amendment and people’s rights to defend themselves.
MN COVID-19 Policy
Glenn believes we need to reopen the MN economy and schools, while protecting the most vulnerable in our society by understanding ALL the risks to our actions.
Immigration
Glenn believes immigration is good, but that it must be done legally for the safety and benefit of both Americans and immigrants.
The Environment
Glenn believes in protecting the environment through practical and reasonable policies that wouldn’t destroy the economy.
The Economy
Glenn believes that reducing taxes and regulations unleashes the entrepreneurial spirit of Americans, leading to the prosperity of all.
MN Agriculture
Glenn supports Minnesota Farmers and is committed to keeping them competitive by opposing excessive taxes and regulations.
Federalism
America is a diverse country with many different values, cultures, and challenges.
We believe that most decisions made for a community, are best done by the community, through state and local governments.
Individual Liberty
We believe in the God-given, self-evident rights of life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness and that the path to success is through hard work, personal responsibility, and pursing education.
Limited Government
We believe the government does not exist to provide meaning or to manage and control my life.
Instead, it exists primarily to preserve my God-given freedom and liberty to chart my own adventure.
Healthcare
Glenn believes that quality and affordable healthcare is best accomplished through the free market principles of competition, deregulation, and transparency.
Racial Justice
Glenn is passionate about correcting the injustices caused by current and historic racism and injustice through education, school choice, and other important policies.
