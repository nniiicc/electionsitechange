The Militia means all citizens capable of taking up arms in defense of their unalienable rights.
As a strong supporter of gun ownership, personal protection, and the second amendment, Glenn believes that our right to own and bear arms shall not be infringed, period.
Glenn also supports Conceal and Carry Laws and has his permit to carry.
He is also an NRA member and supports passage of Stand your Ground laws.
