Core Policies.

Glenn believes in electing judges who interpret the constitution and laws as written and is opposed to judicial activism/legislation in all forms.
Glenn is pro-life from conception to natural death and views the right to life as the most fundamental right we have.
Glenn is a strong supporter of the 2nd Amendment and people’s rights to defend themselves.
Glenn believes in protecting religious freedom and that people should not be forced by law to compromise their beliefs.
Glenn believes immigration is good, but that it must be done legally for the safety and benefit of both Americans and immigrants.
Glenn supports policies and laws that protect both the integrity and access to this essential right.
Glenn believes in supporting the needy but through policies and incentive structures that lead to financial independence and dignity.
Glenn believes in energy independence with a focus on clean but also economically viable energy sources like nuclear.
Glenn believes in protecting the environment through practical and reasonable policies that wouldn’t destroy the economy.
Glenn believes that reducing taxes and regulations unleashes the entrepreneurial spirit of Americans, leading to the prosperity of all.
Glenn believes that quality and affordable healthcare is best accomplished through the free market principles of competition, deregulation, and transparency.
Glenn believes quality education is essential to our nation’s future and that people should have the freedom of school choice.
