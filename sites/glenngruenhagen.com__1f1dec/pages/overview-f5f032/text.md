Glenn is also a passionate educator on everything from our nation’s founding principles to some of the most pressing issues of the day.
This page gives an overview of the heart of conservative principles and solutions!
Founding Philosophy.
Glenn believes strongly in the principles, philosophy, and values that our country was founded upon.
All good policies flow from these underlying principles laid out by our founders in the Declaration of Independence and Constitution.
Core Policies.
Using the founding philosophy as a guide, Glenn has taken principled and pragmatic approaches to his governing policies.
Whether it’s healthcare or the economy, Glenn’s policy positions are rooted in the philosophy of limited government, liberty, and federalism.
Important Issues.
Within a world this dynamic, unique challenges and issues regularly arise.
Using his core policy positions, along with the latest scientific studies and a common sense pragmatism, Glenn has effective solutions to a number of the most pressing issues of our time.
