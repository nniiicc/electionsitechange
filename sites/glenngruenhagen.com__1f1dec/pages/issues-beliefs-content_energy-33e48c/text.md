Energy
Position Summary
All forms of alternative energy must prove to be economically viable before widespread implementation across this state.
We must build new nuclear power plants and utilize clean coal technology, along with expanded use of natural gas.
Our country has the resources to become energy independent and thanks to President Donald Trump we are now energy independent for the first time in 75 years.
This means the U.S. can stop relying on oil from the middle east which has inadvertently funded the terrorists who kill and maim our troops in endless wars.
We can start bringing our troops home to America.
