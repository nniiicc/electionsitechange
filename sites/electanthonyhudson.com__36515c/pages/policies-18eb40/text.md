Part Time Legislature with Part Time Pay Switching to a part-time legislature with part-time pay ensures that lawmakers stay connected to the real-world challenges of their communities.
This reform would save taxpayer money and reduce unnecessary bureaucracy, making Michigan’s government leaner and more efficient.
Lower Auto Insurance Transitioning from no-fault to at-fault auto insurance would lower premiums for Michigan drivers, offering more affordable options while holding reckless drivers accountable.
It’s a common-sense reform to save families money and improve fairness on the roads.
Eliminate Solar & Wind Farms Eliminate Solar and Wind Farms on state land to protect natural resources.
Large-scale solar and wind farms damage Michigan’s forests, lakes, farms, and wildlife.
Restricting these installations to rooftops safeguards our environment while still supporting renewable energy in a way that aligns with Michigan’s values.
Return Local Control Giving local communities the authority to decide on renewable energy projects ensures decisions are made with the interests of residents in mind.
It’s about empowering Michigan towns and cities to have a say in their future.
Eliminate Annual Auto Registration Eliminating annual auto registration fees would save Michiganians time and money by requiring payment only once per vehicle.
This common-sense change would make vehicle ownership more affordable and simplify the process for residents.
Eliminate Renewable Energy Mandates Ending mandates for renewable energy and electric vehicles puts control back in the hands of consumers.
Michigan residents should decide what energy solutions work best for their families and businesses, not the government.
Bring Back Natural Gas Restoring natural gas as a key energy source ensures affordable, reliable power for Michigan homes and businesses.
It supports energy independence and protects against the high costs of unreliable alternatives.
