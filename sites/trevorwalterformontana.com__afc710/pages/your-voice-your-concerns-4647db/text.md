Your Voice, Your Ideas
Share your concerns, solutions, and ideas to help shape the future
I believe good government starts with listening.
The people who know our communities best are the ones who live, work, and raise families here.
That’s why I’m asking you to share your thoughts directly with me.
What’s working well?
What needs to change?
What challenges are you facing?
Whether it’s about property taxes, education, infrastructure, public safety, healthcare, or anything else that matters to you, I want to hear it.
This page is your place to:
- Suggest new ideas or solutions for District 69 and Montana
- Share concerns about current policies or local issues
- Highlight needs in our community that aren’t being addressed
- Offer feedback on legislation or priorities
Every message is read, and while I can’t promise to act on every suggestion, I can promise to take your voice seriously.
Your input helps guide my priorities in Helena and ensures that the work I do reflects the needs and values of the people I represent.
Use the form to send your ideas—big or small.
Together, we can protect what makes Montana great and make smart changes where they’re needed most.
