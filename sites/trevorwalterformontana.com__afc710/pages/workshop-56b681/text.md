Tracking Montana Legislation: A Hands-On Voter Workshop
Fill Out the Form and I'll Contact You with Times for Our Voter Workshop
Understanding what happens in Helena shouldn’t require a law degree.
In this live, hands-on workshop, you’ll learn how to follow legislation step-by-step using official Montana resources.
Whether you want to track a specific bill, understand how amendments change language, see how your representative votes, or see where taxpayer dollars are being allocated, this session will give you the practical tools to stay informed and engaged.
You’ll walk away knowing exactly where to click, what to look for, and how to interpret what you’re seeing.
In This Workshop, You Will Learn How To:
- Track a bill from introduction to final vote
- Read and interpret amendments
- Understand fiscal notes and funding impacts
- Follow committee hearings and executive actions
- Monitor floor votes in both chambers
- Identify who sponsored and supported legislation
- Use official Montana legislative websites efficiently
Who This Course Is Designed For:
- Concerned citizens who want clarity, not confusion
- Business owners tracking regulations
- Parents monitoring education bills
- Taxpayers who want to understand spending
- Anyone who wants to be a more informed voter
