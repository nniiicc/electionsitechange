Some of the issues that are important to me If you’re ready for common-sense, Montana-first solutions — I’m ready to fight for you!
Kill Bad Bills Every session brings more bloated bills, more bureaucracy, and more spending — it’s time to stop the growth of government before it buries us in debt and red tape.
Eliminate Wasteful Spending Helena doesn’t have a revenue problem; it has a spending problem.
Millions are tied up in programs that have outlived their purpose or never worked in the first place.
It’s time to clean house, end the waste, and return that money to the taxpayers who earned it.
Create Real Transparency True transparency keeps government accountable — when taxpayers can see where every dollar goes, waste and runaway taxes don’t stand a chance.
Protect Family Values Family is the backbone of Montana, and it’s under attack.
We must defend parents’ rights, safeguard our kids, and ensure that family values—not government—guide our future.
Modernize Infrastructure From roads and bridges to our aging energy grid, Montana’s infrastructure needs real investment and smarter management.
Without it, we can’t grow our industries or move our goods.
Deregulate Industry Montana’s cattle, timber, farming, mining, drilling, and construction industries are being strangled by government red tape — it’s time to cut it and get Montana working again.
Some of you may remember, we used to have a time with a single income provided a great lifestyle here in Montana — lets get back to that.
