The Issues
What Sheila Will Focus On As Your Representative in the Statehouse To Make Your Life Better
Accessible, Affordable Healthcare
Everyone should have access to affordable healthcare.
You shouldn’t have to decide between food or medicine…housing or seeing a doctor.
Profit-driven insurance companies should not dictate which doctors you can see or what tests you can have.
As your state representative, Sheila will work to ensure that decisions about your health are made by your healthcare professionals and you.
Reinvesting Your Tax Dollars Into Public Education
According to the Ohio Education Association, nearly 90 percent of our students attend public schools.
Yet the legislature diverted over a billion dollars to private school vouchers last year.
Every dollar spent on vouchers is a dollar less for your public schools.
As your representative in Columbus, Sheila will fight to invest your tax dollars in public schools and support the professional educators who teach our children.
