Melanie Miller for GA House 124 Page 2 DONATE For decades, Melanie Miller has been a trusted voice in her community.
Her service on the City of Greensboro Housing Authority as a Board Commissioner and her years as a teacher in the Greene County School System inspired her to step up and do even more.
She knows firsthand the struggles families face and has dedicated her life to being there when her community needs a helping hand.
As a proud mother, community leader, and lifelong advocate for justice, she is committed to fighting for human rights, higher wages for working families, stronger public schools, expanded mental health resources, workforce housing in rural Georgia, Medicaid expansion, and common-sense gun laws to keep our children safe.
With over 23 years of experience as a paralegal, educator, and nonprofit business owner, Melanie brings the skills, compassion, and determination needed to deliver real solutions.
Born and raised right here in District 124, she understands the unique challenges we face—and the opportunities we can seize together.
Now, Melanie is ready to take the next step: to serve as a public official who will represent every voice in District 124.
Melanie Miller believes that representation is not just a privilege, it is our constitutional right.
She is running to make sure that right is protected and that every family, every worker, and every child in our district has a champion at the table.
