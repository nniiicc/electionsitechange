ABOUT ME
Aloha, I'm Mark Talaeai
Growing up in Whitmore Village near Wahiawā, I am a proud local with deep roots in our community.
A Leilehua High School alumnus, I continued my education at Honolulu Community College and built a life centered on family and service.
My blended family of 7 children and 12 grandchildren keeps me grounded every day.
Guided by strong faith, I bring honesty, hard work, and aloha to everything I do in public service.
Throughout my life, I have been an active voice for local communities.
This includes supporting small businesses, addressing everyday concerns like traffic, roads, and neighborhood issues, and standing up for the values that matter most to our families.
My Priorities
- Safe Communities
- Supporting Small Businesses
- Protecting Local Culture & Resources
- Supporting Local Farmers & Agriculture
- Addressing long-standing concerns with traffic, state roads, and highways
Above all, I remain committed to putting family, faith, and community first — delivering results with integrity and aloha for the people I serve.
I humbly ask for your vote.
