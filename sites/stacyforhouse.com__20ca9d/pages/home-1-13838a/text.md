Stacy is a banker, nonprofit president, daughter of a veteran, and proud resident of Washington’s 12th Legislative District.
She has spent years building community and solving problems.
Now she’s running to represent Washington’s 12th in the State House of Representatives.
AT A GLANCE
Professional Experience:
- 20 years of experience in financial services, including roles as a:
- Bank vice president
- Financial advisor
- Author and speaker specializing in the intersection of money and marriage.
Community Involvement and Service
- Lake Chelan Chamber of Commerce, Board Member
- Lake Chelan Rotary Club member
- Chelan Valley Hope, Board President
Chelan Business Women Connect, Founder
Stacy lives in the hills above Lake Chelan with her husband, dogs (Piper and Foster) and chickens (Pot Pie, Nugget, Kung Pao, Adobo, Teriyaki, and Russell).
She visits her grandchildren often.
FIGHTING FOR WHAT ACTUALLY MATTERS
Across the 12th District, families want good jobs, affordable healthcare, strong schools, safe communities, and a government that uses tax dollars responsibly.
Stacy’s priorities are grounded in real life and shaped by listening to the people who live and work here.
