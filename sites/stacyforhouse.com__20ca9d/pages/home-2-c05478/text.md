FIGHTING FOR WHAT ACTUALLY MATTERS
Across the 12th District, families want good jobs, affordable healthcare, strong schools, safe communities, and a government that uses tax dollars responsibly.
Stacy’s priorities are grounded in real life and shaped by listening to the people who live and work here.
Stacy's Priorities
Creating Good Jobs and a Strong Local Economy
Small businesses, agriculture, and family-owned enterprises are the backbone of the 12th District, and Stacy is committed to helping them succeed.
She supports smart, locally driven economic development that creates good jobs without sacrificing the outdoor heritage and quality of life that make this region special.
Stacy will champion workforce training, small business support, and partnerships that give local employers and workers the tools they need to grow and stay rooted here.
A Home for Every Family
Housing should be stable, affordable, and available in every community.
Stacy believes a safe place to call home is a foundation for working families, seniors, and first-time buyers.
She will fight to increase housing supply, crack down on predatory lending practices, and invest in rural and workforce housing so no one has to choose between paying rent and putting food on the table.
Strengthening Schools and Real Opportunity
Every child deserves a quality education and a clear path to opportunity, no matter their ZIP code.
Stacy supports fully funding public schools, backing teachers, and making sure classrooms have the resources students need to succeed.
She also believes in strong vocational and career-connected education programs that prepare students for good-paying jobs and allow young people to build a future right here in the district.
Infrastructure That Keeps Us Connected
Reliable roads, safe bridges, and fast broadband aren’t extras — they’re essential.
Stacy understands that infrastructure investments keep communities connected, support local businesses, and improve daily life.
She will fight for smart, long-term investments in transportation and broadband that serve rural and growing communities alike and help every corner of the district stay connected to the modern economy.
Budgeting Responsibly
Stacy believes in using taxpayer dollars wisely and responsibly.
She supports balanced budgets that invest in priorities like education, healthcare, and infrastructure while respecting the financial realities facing working families and seniors.
Her approach is practical and transparent: fund what works, cut waste, and make sure public dollars deliver real value for working families.
