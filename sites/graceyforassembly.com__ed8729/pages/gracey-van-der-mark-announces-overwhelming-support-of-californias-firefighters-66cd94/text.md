The coalition includes the local associations of Orange County Professional Firefighters, the Newport Beach Firefighters Association, the Huntington Beach Firefighters Association and the statewide California Professional Firefighters and CAL Fire Local 2881 – representing nearly every major firefighter in California.
"I'm incredibly excited to stand with the men and women who put their lives on the line every day to protect our families and communities," said Van Der Mark.
"Our firefighters do so much than respond to fires.
Whether they're responding to medical emergencies, confronting hazardous materials leaks like the one in Garden Grove earlier this year, or protecting our neighborhoods from devastating fires they are the ones we turn to.
"The devastating Palisades fire was a tragic reminder of what can happen when communities face catastrophic emergencies and lack the resources and preparation.
We must learn from that disaster and address shortcomings in resources.
Our firefighters deserve every resource necessary to protect lives and property, and our communities deserve to know we are prepared for the next emergency.
Our firefighters stand on the front lines for us every single day, and I will stand with them in Sacramento." Gracey Van Der Mark is running for California’s 72nd Assembly District.
For more information please visit www.GraceyForAssembly.com .
About Gracey Gracey Van Der Mark’s life embodies the American Dream.
As the daughter of immigrants raised in a struggling Los Angeles neighborhood, she overcame adversity and emerged as a passionate advocate for her community.
A survivor of domestic violence, Gracey rebuilt her life in Huntington Beach, where she and her husband, Pieter Van Der Mark, raised a blended family of seven children and now run a successful property management business.
Gracey is running to represent the 72nd Assembly District, currently held by Assemblywoman Diane Dixon, who is seeking a seat on the Orange County Board of Supervisors.
Along with Seal Beach, the 72 nd Assembly District is home to the cities of Aliso Viejo, Huntington Beach, Laguna Hills, Laguna Beach, Laguna Woods, Lake Forest, and Newport Beach.
