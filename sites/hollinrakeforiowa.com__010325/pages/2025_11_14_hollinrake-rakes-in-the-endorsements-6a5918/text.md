“In only a few short days this campaign has received an outpouring of support from Iowans.
I’m humbled by their support and encouraged by their passion to make sure Iowa elects strong conservative leadership,” said Hollinrake.
“It’s full steam ahead and I look forward to earning every vote I can.” Iowans are supporting John because they know he is the voice Ankeny needs in the Iowa Senate.
“I’m all in for John Hollinrake to represent Ankeny in the Iowa Senate.
John is a proven conservative who puts people first.
He’s a hard worker who knows Iowa, knows Ankeny, and knows how to get things done.
He’ll fight for Ankeny families day in and day out.” – U.S.
Senator Joni Ernst “John Hollinrake will be a champion for Ankeny in the Iowa Senate.
His family’s roots run deep in Ankeny and military service.

John knows it’s time to bring property tax relief to Iowa’s working families.” – Congressman Zach Nunn “John will work to invest in our schools because he knows every child deserves a world class education that prepares them for future success.
