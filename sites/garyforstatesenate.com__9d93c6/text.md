For Gary Byrne, preserving our Hoosier way of life is about defending the values that built our communities and run deep in each of us.
A proud husband, father and grandfather, Gary is a 7th-generation Hoosier farmer and local small business owner.
Fighting to Protect Our Southern Indiana Values:
- Cutting Taxes to Make Life More Affordable
- Protecting Hoosiers from Big Government Overreach
- Supporting Indiana’s Farmers
- Investing in our Local Roads and Infrastructure
- Putting Families First
As Our State Senator, Gary Byrne Has:
- Voted to cut property and income taxes and stood against members of his own party to oppose expanded tolling.
- Stood up to unelected government bureaucrats to protect Hoosier landowners and small business owners.
- Supported our farmers by lowering taxes on their property and equipment and cutting burdensome regulations.
- Secured millions of dollars to help improve our local roads and bridges.
- Put families first by prohibiting the government from infringing upon a parent’s right to direct the upbringing and care of their child.
About Gary
Gary and his wife Angie are from Morgan Township in Harrison County.
They have three sons and nine grandchildren with a tenth on the way.
Prior to serving as State Senator for District 47, Gary Byrne served two terms (8 years) on the North Harrison School Board and a term (4 years) on the Harrison County Council.
He farms cattle and hay, and is the owner of Byrne Satellite, a family-owned business that installs security and satellite systems in homes and businesses.
The farm has been in his family since 1806.
He is a member of the Farm Bureau and the National Rifle Association.
