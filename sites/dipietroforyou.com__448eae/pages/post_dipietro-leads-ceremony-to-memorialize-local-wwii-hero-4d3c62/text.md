All Posts Latest Political News Culture and Opinions Events Your Community Vaccines & Health Search DiPietro Leads Ceremony to Memorialize Local WWII Hero David Dipietro May 11, 2025 1 min read State Route 19 Dedicated to WWII Hero T Sgt.
Walter W.
Shearing Assemblyman David DiPietro (R,C-East Aurora), Sen.
George Borrello (R,C-57th Senate District) and Gainesville Town Supervisor David Granger honored the legacy of Technical Sergeant Walter W.
Shearing by dedicating a portion of State Route 19 in his name.
As a proud son of Wyoming County who gave his life during the Battle of the Bulge, T Sgt.
Shearing represents the very best of America.
“It was a privilege to help ensure T Sgt.
Shearing’s courage and sacrifice are never forgotten,” said DiPietro.
“This highway will stand as a permanent reminder of his service and the price of our freedom.
