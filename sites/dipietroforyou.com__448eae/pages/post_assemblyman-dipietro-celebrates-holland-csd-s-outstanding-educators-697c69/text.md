Assemblyman DiPietro Celebrates Holland CSD’s Outstanding Educators
Assemblyman David DiPietro (R,C-East Aurora) is touring his district to recognize teachers who demonstrate exceptional dedication, innovation and impact in building student growth and academic excellence.
At Holland Central School District, DiPietro proudly honored Timothy Buckenmeyer (Physical Education), Stephanie Fides (Special Education) and Andrew Ranic (Science) for their remarkable contributions to education.
These educators have inspired a love of learning and are driving academic success.
“It’s an honor to celebrate Timothy Buckenmeyer, Stephanie Fides and Andrew Ranic for their extraordinary work in shaping the minds and futures of our students,” said DiPietro.
“As someone who briefly taught, I know firsthand the profound impact educators have, and these individuals are true role models for our community.”
Assemblyman David DiPietro represents the 147th Assembly District, which includes parts of Erie and Wyoming counties.
