Assemblyman DiPietro Announces Passage of Bill to Dedicate Stephen C.
Ebling Memorial Bridge
Assemblyman David DiPietro (R,C-East Aurora) is proud to announce the passage of Assembly Bill A.6969B, which designates the bridge over the New York State Thruway along Eden-Evans Center Road in the Town of Evans as the “Maintenance Supervisor Stephen C.
Ebling Memorial Bridge.” This DiPietro-sponsored bill honors New York State Thruway Authority Maintenance Supervisor Stephen C.
Ebling, who tragically lost his life while working in the Silver Creek Section of the Buffalo Maintenance Division in November.
“Stephen Ebling was a selfless public servant,” said DiPietro.
“Naming this bridge in his honor ensures his legacy of service will be remembered.
My thoughts remain with his family, friends and colleagues.”
Stephen C.
Ebling was known for his dedication to public safety and his role as a supervisor for the Thruway Authority.
This memorial designation serves as a lasting tribute to his contributions to the Town of Eden and Erie County.
DiPietro extends his gratitude to his colleagues in the New York State Assembly for their support in passing this legislation.
Assemblyman David DiPietro represents the 147th Assembly District, which includes parts of Erie and Wyoming counties.
