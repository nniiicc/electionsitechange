APPLE VALLEY — Assemblyman Jay Obernolte will host the second annual Top Chamber Business Awards Ceremony, which will showcase local businesses from area chamber of commerce offices.
During the ceremony and banquet, Obernolte will help recognize three finalists for the business awards from each of the local chambers throughout the 33rd Assembly District.
He will also share a legislative update.
The finalists representing each chamber include:
Adelanto:
DeVoll Rubber, High Desert Alfa Romeo/FIAT, and the Holiday Inn Victorville.
Apple Valley:
Apple Valley Fire Protection District, Apple Valley Transfer and Storage, and Signify Designs.
Barstow:
Barstow Transfer and Storage, J&S Specialities, and PACE Services.
Big Bear:
Community Market, O Koo Run, and The Copper Q.
Hesperia:
ABC 123 Christian Academy, Saddlerock Reverse Mortgage, and Victorville Motors.
High Desert Hispanic Chamber of Commerce:
Bounce Realty, Cisneros Bro.
Plumbing, and Fuerza Marketing.
Lucerne Valley:
First Mountain Bank, Rock’s Place, and Security Storage and Towing.
Needles:
Big O Tire/Napa Auto Parts, Phillips Excavating, and River Valley Air Conditioning and Heating.
Victor Valley
Armstrong-Fairway Insurance, Coldwell Banker Homesource, and Kona Ice of the Victor Valley.
Tickets to the award banquet are $25 per person and can be purchased online at www.topchamberbusinessawards2017.ticketleap.com/top-chamber-business-awards-2017.
For more information, contact Shannon Dunkle at shannon.dunkle88@gmail.com.
The Second Annual ‘Top Chamber Business Awards’ is scheduled from 6 to 8 p.m. on Thursday at the Apple Valley Town Conference Center, located at 14955 Dale Evans Pkwy.
