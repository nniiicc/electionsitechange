Published: http://www.vvdailypress.com/news/20170614/obernolte-to-host-senior-scam-stopper
Assemblyman Jay Obernolte and the Contractors State License Board will host a free Senior Scam Stopper seminar from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m.
June 23 at Victorville City Hall, Conference Room D, 14343 Civic Drive, Victorville.
A panel of experts will provide information to seniors on how to protect themselves from becoming victims of fraud.
Attendees can get answers to their questions and obtain assistance if they have been scammed.
Also participating will be Victorville Mayor Gloria Garcia.
Mayor Garcia has been a victim of identity theft and will discuss her experience.
