Jesse West is the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Congress in Utah's 1st Congressional District in the 2026 election.
He is a Salt Lake City family law and trial attorney, a Brighton season pass holder, and Chair of the Salt Lake City Libertarian Party.
Jesse West is running for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Utah's 1st Congressional District.
The general election is November 3, 2026.
Jesse West is a Libertarian.
He won the Libertarian Party nomination for Utah's 1st Congressional District and appears on the 2026 general election ballot.
Jesse West's platform centers on three pillars: Peace, Truth, and Freedom.
Key positions include ending U.S. involvement in foreign wars, abolishing the federal death penalty, legalizing cannabis, protecting Utah's public lands and the Great Salt Lake, reducing taxes, and creating legal pathways for immigration.
Jesse West opposes forever wars and undeclared conflicts.
He supports bringing troops home from non-strategic deployments, requiring Congressional authorization for new military engagements, and redirecting military spending toward veteran care and domestic priorities.
His campaign slogan is 'A vote for me is a vote to Ski not War.'
Jesse West supports abolishing the death penalty at the federal level.
He argues the government should not have irreversible power over human life, citing over 190 death row exonerations since 1973.
Jesse West supports removing cannabis from the federal controlled substances list, expunging federal cannabis convictions, and letting states regulate cannabis like alcohol without federal interference.
Jesse West is the Libertarian option in a multi-way 2026 race that also includes Democrat Ben McAdams, Republican Riley Owen, and independent Elias Montgomery.
He runs no corporate PAC money and positions himself as a third option focused on peace, individual freedom, and Utah's environment.
