My name is Jesse West and I am a 30-year-old skier and attorney here in Salt Lake City Utah.
I am currently a Brighton season pass holder, family law practitioner, and the Chair of the Salt Lake City Libertarian Party.
I am not a career politician and I am not from Utah.
I started visiting Utah in 2018 to ski and visit friends, and I kept coming back because I loved it.
When I graduated from school, I chose Utah because I fell in love with the natural beauty, the snow, and the people.
Many people are aware of the lore regarding the "Out of the Blue" statue on 9th.
I gladly take up the mantle of the Liberty Whale to protect our environment and save the Great Salt Lake.
In running for congress, I hope to represent Utah in Washington D.C. on what matters most: ending needless wars, fighting for our environment, ending the federal death penalty, reducing taxes, and opposing oppressive immigration enforcement.
