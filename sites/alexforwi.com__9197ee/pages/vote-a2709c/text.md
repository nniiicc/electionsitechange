Wisconsin has four elections this year, in both the spring and the fall, and every one of them is important.
Voting is one of the simplest ways we can take care of our community and make sure our voices are heard , not just once every few years, but every time we are given the chance.​ ​ In 2026, we will have the Fall Partisan Primary on Tuesday, August 11, and the General Election on Tuesday, November 3.
I will be on the ballot in the fall, and it would truly be an honor to earn your support as I continue serving our community as your State Representative . ​ I believe showing up for every election matters.
It is how we look out for one another, set priorities, and pass on the values of civic responsibility to the next generation.
