Wisconsin Democrats renew call for paid medical and family leave
Current Wisconsin law requires employers of 50 workers or more to provide eight weeks of unpaid leave for the birth of a child and two weeks of unpaid leave to deal with a serious medical condition.
State Democrats announced a bill today that would guarantee Wisconsin workers up to 12 weeks of paid family or medical leave – and up to 14 weeks if they’re eligible for a combination of both.
