Information We May Collect
We may collect the following types of personal information:
- Name
- Email address

2.
How We Use Your Information
We may use your information for the following purposes:
- To communicate with you about campaign events, issues, or opportunities
- To process donations and comply with campaign finance laws
- To respond to your inquiries or requests
- To improve our website, emails, and engagement strategy

- To protect against fraud and ensure security
- To comply with legal obligations
3.
How We Share Your Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share it only in limited circumstances:
- With trusted service providers (e.g., donation processors, email/SMS platforms, website hosts)
- As required by law (e.g., FEC reporting requirements)
- With successor campaigns or political committees
- In compliance with court orders or government investigations
SMS Carrier Compliance: Your phone number will never be shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing purposes.
4.
SMS and Text Messaging Policy
If you provide your phone number, you consent to receive automated political calls and text messages from the candidate or committee.
We want to be clear about your rights and the terms:
- Message frequency may vary
- Message and data rates may apply
- Reply STOP to cancel
- Reply HELP for help
- Your opt-in status, opt-outs, and replies are logged for compliance
- Opt-out requests are honored immediately
- You may receive messages about events, voting reminders, or donation opportunities
- Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
5.
Use of Cookies and Tracking
Our websites may use cookies and similar technologies:
- To remember your preferences
- To track site usage and improve performance
- To serve relevant ads and measure results
- You can adjust cookie settings in your browser
- Some features may not function properly without essential cookies
6.
How Long We Keep Your Data
We retain your personal information:
- For as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes listed above
- For as long as required by campaign finance or other laws
- Once no longer needed, your data may be securely deleted or anonymized
7.
Your Rights and Choices
You have the following rights:
- Access: You may request a copy of the data we have about you
- Correction: You may request corrections to inaccurate data
- Deletion: You may request that we delete your personal information, subject to legal limits
- Opt-Out: You may unsubscribe from emails or reply STOP to text messages at any time
To exercise these rights, please use the contact information listed at the top of this policy.
8.
Data Security
