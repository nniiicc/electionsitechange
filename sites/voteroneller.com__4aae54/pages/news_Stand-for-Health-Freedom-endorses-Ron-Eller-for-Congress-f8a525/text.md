Stand for Health Freedom endorses Ron Eller for Congress
Physician’s Assistant on side of individual liberty, medical freedom, and informed consent
Raymond, MS – Stand for Health Freedom has endorsed conservative Republican Ron Eller for the Second Congressional District of Mississippi.
With the Republican primary just days away on March 10, Eller is again receiving the support of conservative activists nationwide against his primary opponent.
Eller, a Physician’s Assistant in Jackson, expressed his gratitude for the endorsement: “It is my great honor to receive Stand for Health Freedom's endorsement.
I have long respected this highly effective advocacy organization for its stalwart defender of individual liberty and health freedom.”
Like Stand for Health Freedom, Eller has consistently advocated individual freedom of choice and opposed vaccine and mask mandates and centralized government control over health care.
Eller stated, “Stand for Health Freedom supports candidates who make and support policy to protect and expand individual and family choice in matters of personal health.
I will defend and expand these rights for Mississippi residents.”
Leah Wilson, Executive Director of Stand for Health Freedom, says, “We are seeing an increasing number of medical professionals and physicians prioritize health freedom.
They see the value in protecting each individual in order to have a community of healthy and free individuals.
Eller is a shining example and will represent Mississippi with the backbone and principles that are needed to fix what’s broken in Washington.”
Stand for Health Freedom (SHF) endorses political candidates who prioritize individual liberty, medical freedom, and informed consent.
These endorsements highlight a commitment to reducing government overreach in personal healthcare decisions.
Key themes of endorsed candidates often include:
Medical Freedom: Protecting the right to make personal health decisions without government mandates.
Informed Consent: Ensuring transparency and personal choice in medical treatments.
Legislative Action: Opposing government overreach and defending constitutional rights.
