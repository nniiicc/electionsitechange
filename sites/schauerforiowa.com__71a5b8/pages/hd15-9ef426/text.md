Iowa House District 15
Now sure which district you live in?
House District 15 contains the entirety of Harrison County and a portion of Pottawattamie County.
Harrison County communities include the cities of Dunlap, Little Sioux, Logan, Magnolia, Missouri Valley, Modale, Mondamin, Persia, Pisgah, and Woodbine.
Pottawattamie County communities include the cities of Crescent, McClelland, and Underwood, rural Neola, and the outskirts of Council Bluffs.
