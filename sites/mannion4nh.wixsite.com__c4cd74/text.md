top of page Built on Home Accomplishments Roll Call Votes Contact Voting Record Dennis Mannion For State Representative About Dennis Conservative values As a longtime New Hampshire resident, Dennis Mannion has lived in Salem for over 20 years.
He brings more than 30 years of dedicated law enforcement experience to public service — including eight years serving as a police prosecutor.
Dennis was first elected to the New Hampshire House of Representatives in 2022 and was re-elected in 2024.
He previously served on the Criminal Justice and Public Safety Committee and was recently appointed Vice Chair of the Judiciary Committee.
Throughout his career, Dennis has witnessed firsthand the challenges and complexities of the criminal justice system.
He is deeply passionate about keeping our communities safe, supporting law enforcement, protecting victims’ rights, and ensuring a fair and just legal system.
As a legislator, he champions practical, common-sense policies that strengthen public safety, uphold constitutional rights, and defend the values that make New Hampshire strong.
Key Priorites Public Safety & Law Enforcement: Strengthening penalties for crimes against first responders, supporting our police, and delivering real justice.
Conservative Values: Defending free speech, Second Amendment rights, parental rights in education, election integrity, and lower taxes with no sales or income tax.
Criminal Justice Reform: Focused on accountability, victim protection, and effective policies that keep our families and neighborhoods secure. ​ Dennis is a retired law enforcement officer who remains committed to the “Live Free or Die” spirit of New Hampshire.
He continues to fight every day in Concord for the hardworking families of Salem and Rockingham District 25.
