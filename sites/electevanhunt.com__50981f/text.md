A LIFETIME OF SERVICE
Hunt is a retired U.S.
Air Force Lieutenant Colonel.
Feeling compelled to make a difference following September 11th, he enlisted and served with distinction as a B-1 Bomber Weapons Systems Officer and Electronic Warfare Officer.
He completed multiple combat deployments in support of Operation ENDURING FREEDOM and Operation NEW DAWN, flying over a dozen combat missions.
His military journey also included service as an instructor at the Air Force’s premier training exercise - Red Flag - where he helped shape future combat aviators.
Following active duty, Hunt continued his service in the Air National Guard, where he held key leadership roles including Master Air Attack Plans Chief, Squadron Commander, and Deputy Group Commander, leading both operations and maintenance units in high-stakes environments.
Hunt retired in 2022, having earned multiple Meritorious Service Medals and Air Medals.
Following his military career, Evan transitioned into the defense and aerospace industry, where he has held executive roles at leading companies — including publicly traded corporations like Raytheon, private equity-backed firms, and venture-backed startups.
With deep experience in national security, emerging technologies, and global strategy, Evan has become a respected voice in the defense innovation community.
As a business leader, Evan understands the power of innovation, the importance of smart economic policy, and the critical need to keep America competitive on the global stage.
He is focused on ensuring North Texas continues to be a hub for emerging technology, jobs, and security.

- Political leaders should not continue to run businesses while they are in office
- And the President should never serve for more than 2 terms
Let’s embrace and defend the goals and values of our country’s Founding Fathers.
Let’s deliver a nation to our children and grandchildren that has greater national strength, a more unified citizenry, and a deeper commitment to the democratic values that the United States has championed since its birth.

My parents raised me on the Golden Rule.
I believe in treating others the way you want to be treated.

We don’t need the federal government to tell us what type of medical treatment we can get, what we’re allowed to call ourselves, or what bathroom we can use.

No matter your immigration status, race, religion or orientation, you should be treated with dignity and respect.

This is a campaign that shows up for everybody.
We stand together against bullies and bigots, and remind others that love is more powerful than hate.

- In America today, too many families are struggling with rising medical costs, from prescriptions to insurance premiums to surprise ER bills.
No American should be a medical emergency or cancer diagnosis away from bankruptcy.
Our healthcare system is failing the people it’s supposed to serve.
Costs are skyrocketing, transparency is nonexistent, and too many Americans are forced to choose between their health and their financial stability.
This is a moral failure and failure of management.
I believe in a healthcare system that works for the people.
The ACA subsidies shouldn’t be expired until we have a better plan.
We need a public and private partnership that provides an affordable baseline, but also maintains some healthy competition and innovation.
We also should have a team of experts look at disruptive, transparent models we’ve seen with the likes of Mark Cuban’s Cost Plus Drug Company.
We need to:
- Lower Prescription Drug Prices
- Eliminate Wasteful Middlemen
- Bring Transparency and Accountability to the Entire Healthcare System
Beyond healthcare, our families and small businesses are also getting crushed by rising insurance premiums, from auto to homeowners.
I support reforms that lower costs and bring more transparency to the insurance industry.
That means cracking down on price gouging, increasing competition, and making sure insurance companies play by the rules , not raise rates without reason.
- My mother was a career educator, so I know that education isn’t political, it’s personal.
It’s about our kids, our teachers, and the future of our communities.
I believe in empowering parents, supporting teachers, and ensuring every student has the tools they need to succeed, whether they’re college-bound or headed into a skilled trade.
That means fully funding our public schools, promoting local control, and keeping politics out of the classroom.
I believe in honoring the hard work of our educators while being responsible stewards of taxpayer dollars.
Our community values quality education, and that starts with attracting and retaining great teachers.
That’s why I support reasonable, merit-informed increases in teacher pay, especially for high-performing educators and those in under-resourced or high-demand subject areas.
We must also work with local school boards and the state to ensure that schools have the flexibility to reward great teachers without overburdening taxpayers.
Let’s ensure teacher pay keeps pace with cost of living in fast-growing communities like ours.
Alternatively, I’m firmly against voucher initiatives which will inevitably harm public schools, especially in districts like Plano ISD, Frisco ISD, and Allen ISD, which already face financial challenges due to the state’s recapture system.
Voucher schemes, which are no more than welfare for the wealthy, threaten to drain funding from public classrooms and send taxpayer dollars to private institutions.
We need to strengthen public education and ensure that all students have access to quality schooling, not redirect funds to private options for the wealthy minority that will pay for private school anyway.
In terms of higher education, College costs are out of control, and it’s time for real solutions.
I support expanding access to community colleges and trade schools, increasing transparency in college pricing, and offering targeted financial aid that rewards hard work and responsibility.

Identify, arrest, prosecute, and remove violent offenders.

We can enforce the law.
We can secure the border.
We can deport undocumented criminals.

- We must get big money and special interests out of politics, and give our voice and vote real power again.
This requires bold systematic reforms.
I’m a charter member of the Take BAC Congress pledge, which calls for term limits, banning members of Congress from trading stocks, overturning or mitigating Citizen’s United, enforcing a cooling-off period before lobbying, and holding the Supreme Court to clear ethics standards.
When I get to Congress, I’m going to push for a nationwide ban against partisan, out-of-cycle gerrymandering that cracks our communities, silences voices and confuses voters.
Furthermore, I support uncapping the House.
TX-03 now has roughly one million people across seven counties, a mix of fast-growing suburbs and rural communities with very different needs.
And yet, we get one representative.
That’s not meaningful representation, that’s overload.
That’s why the Equal Voices Act (H.R. 4125) matters.
It would expand the House, shrink district sizes, and make representation more proportional to the population.
In plain terms, it would make representatives more accessible and more accountable.
