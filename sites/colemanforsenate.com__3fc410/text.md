My Priorities For Our Community
Helping The Family Budget
As a mom of three, I understand the financial pressure families are facing.
I’ve authored legislation to provide targeted tax relief where it’s needed most, including:
- Securing a $250 million tax cut for Minnesotans by lowering car tab fees
- Fighting to end taxes on tips and overtime for service and hourly workers
- Making life more tax-friendly for families with students and babies
Fighting Fraud And Protecting Tax Dollars
Minnesota has lost $9 billion dollars to fraud.
Enough is enough.
From authoring legislation for greater oversight, to supporting anti-fraud efforts, I am committed to protecting your hard-earned tax dollars.
Getting Our Students On The Right Track
With more than half our students not reading at grade level, I've been committed to improving student performance, empowering parents, recruiting top-talent teachers, and keeping our tax dollars in the classroom.
Keeping Our Streets Safe
As the daughter of a Ramsey County Sheriff’s Deputy, public safety is personal to me.
That’s why I authored legislation increasing penalties for fleeing a police officer, protected victims of stalking, fought to keep guns out of the wrong hands, and authored legislation for sticter penalties for straw gun purchases
