Helping The Family Budget
As a mom of three, I know how stretched family budgets are right now.
That’s why I’ve authored targeted tax relief to help Minnesotans keep more of their hard-earned money:
- Secured a $250 million tax cut by lowering car tab fees
- Fought to eliminate taxes on tips and overtime pay for service and hourly workers
- Advanced tax relief for families with children and newborns
