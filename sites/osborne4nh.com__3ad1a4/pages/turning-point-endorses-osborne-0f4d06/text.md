Skip to content Home News About Speaker’s Campaign Donate Osborne: “Winning The Argument Is Only Part Of The Job” AUBURN, N.H. – Turning Point Action today endorsed House Majority Leader Jason Osborne in his campaign for Speaker of the New Hampshire House.
One hundred fifty two sitting members and 2026 Republican candidates have committed their support to Osborne, and 56 have endorsed publicly.
“I’m proud to have the endorsement of Turning Point Action.
Charlie Kirk dedicated his life to spreading conservative ideas, reaching young Americans, and bringing our message to people the conservative movement had too often failed to reach,” said Osborne.
“Like Charlie, I believe winning the argument is only part of the job.
We have to build conservative majorities, win elections, and then use those majorities to actually deliver results.
“Turning Point understands that.
Their team is on the ground every day helping Republicans win in New Hampshire and across the country.
They are knocking doors, organizing voters, engaging young people, and putting in the long hours it takes to turn conservative principles into conservative victories.
“Without organizations like Turning Point joining us to do that work, we would not be talking about who will be the next Republican Speaker.
We would be talking about how to hold the status quo in the minority.
“I’m grateful for their endorsement, but I’m even more grateful for the work they are doing to elect Republicans up and down the ballot and build a conservative majority that can actually govern.
Elections are won by people willing to do the work, and Turning Point is here with us doing that work every single day.” The Full List of Public Endorsements Rep.
