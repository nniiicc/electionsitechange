Jason Osborne (R-Auburn) will become New Hampshire House Majority Leader when the new session begins, NH Journal confirmed.
The four-term representative had served as the Republican policy leader for the caucus during the prior biennium, a role colleagues credit with helping unify the House GOP.
The confirmation, the outlet reported, came as House Republicans set their leadership team for the term ahead.
The new role placed Osborne at the center of the House GOP’s strategy heading into the term, building on the policy portfolio he had already managed for the caucus.
According to NH Journal , his tenure had been marked by an unusual degree of unity among Republicans, which colleagues said translated into a long run of successful floor fights against Democratic priorities.
As NH Journal reported, sources credited Osborne with bridging the caucus’s internal divisions during a stretch that produced a string of legislative wins for Republicans.
Sources tell NHJournal Osborne helped bridge the divide between the caucus’ many factions, which led to a record-setting 77 sustained vetoes, squashing many Democrat priorities – including higher business taxes, an increased minimum wage, and a slew of gun-control bills.
NH Journal Osborne reflected on the caucus’s work over the prior two years and the cohesion that he said defined it.
