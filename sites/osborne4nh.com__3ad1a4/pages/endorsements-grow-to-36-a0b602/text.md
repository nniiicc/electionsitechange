Skip to content Home News About Speaker’s Campaign Donate Ten More Republicans Endorse Osborne for Speaker; More Than 120 Now Committed AUBURN, N.H. – House Majority Leader Jason Osborne today announced ten more public endorsements in his campaign for Speaker of the New Hampshire House, bringing the total number of public endorsers to 36.
In addition, more than 120 sitting members and 2026 Republican candidates have now committed their support to Osborne for Speaker.
“Between now and November, the focus is simple: grow our Republican majority, and then lead that majority to accomplish the goals that make New Hampshire an even better state to live, work, and raise a family,” said Osborne.
“More than 120 members and candidates have already committed to that mission, and I am grateful for every one of them.
This has grown bigger than any one race for Speaker, and we are just getting started.” The Ten Newest Endorsers Rep.
