AUBURN, N.H. – House Majority Leader Jason Osborne today announced five more public endorsements in his campaign for Speaker of the New Hampshire House.
More than 140 sitting members and 2026 Republican candidates have now committed their support to Osborne for Speaker.
“The next majority gets built in August, in districts most people in Concord could not find on a map, by people willing to give up a summer to unglamorous work.
December is just the arithmetic catching up,” said Osborne.
“There are Republicans out in ninety degree heat right now, four weeks from the primary, knocking doors for a job that pays a hundred dollars a year.
That figure is written into our Constitution and it has not moved since 1889, so nobody is out there for the money.
They knock because they watched what this majority got done and they want a hand in the next one.
“What I want out of the next two years is not complicated: Budgets that grow slower than the paychecks paying for them, a property tax bill that stops outrunning inflation, a permanent income tax ban that voters lock into the Constitution themselves, and parents deciding where their own children learn.
“I am already spending time out in the districts with candidates, helping build and expand the Republican majority which is delivering for the people of New Hampshire, because November is too late to start trying to win a majority and the Speaker’s gavel.”
