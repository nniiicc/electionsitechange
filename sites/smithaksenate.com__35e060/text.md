Explore Issues Proven Leadership The Vision for District C Alaska is rich in resources, but our most important resource is the people who are wondering whether their kids and grandkids will still be able to call this place home.
For years, we’ve watched the same problems circle in Juneau: no long‑term fiscal plan, unstable school funding, unpredictable ferry service, and rising costs that make it harder to live, fish, work, and retire here with confidence.
I’m running for State Senate to change that.
My focus is simple: a clear fiscal framework that protects the Permanent Fund and the PFD, stable support for our schools, dependable ferries and infrastructure for coastal communities, and practical energy and economic policies that make Alaska more affordable—not just this year, but for the next generation.
This campaign is about choosing a future where our kids don’t have to leave to find opportunity, and where Alaskans can finally count on their government to deliver.
