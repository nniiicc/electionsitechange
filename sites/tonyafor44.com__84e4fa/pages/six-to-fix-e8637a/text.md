01 | AFFORDABILITY The Problem: Washington families are feeling squeezed by the cumulative cost of taxes, fees, fuel, utilities, housing, and everyday necessities.
My Fix: First, reduce costs that hit household budgets directly, starting with the gas tax.
Before asking Washingtonians for more, audit where the money is going, measure results, and demand accountability for the dollars taxpayers already provide.
02 | HOUSING PEOPLE CAN AFFORD The Problem: The cost of land, permitting, regulations, utilities, fees, insurance, financing, labor, and materials all add up to the final cost of a home or monthly rent.
My Fix: Reduce unnecessary barriers, speed up permitting, encourage more starter homes and diverse housing choices, and protect the housing we already have.
After 30 years in housing, my approach is simple: you cannot make housing more affordable by making housing more expensive to build, own, and operate.
03 | PUBLIC SAFETY 04 | JOBS & SMALL BUSINESS The Problem: Communities are dealing with repeat crime, addiction, behavioral-health challenges, and a system that too often fails victims while cycling people through crisis.
My Fix: Be SMART, not simply soft or hard.
That means accountability for dangerous behavior, support for law enforcement, consequences for repeat offenders, and treatment and recovery when addiction or behavioral health is driving the cycle.
04 | JOBS & SMALL BUSINESS 04 | JOBS & SMALL BUSINESS The Problem: Taxes, regulations, rising operating costs, and Washington’s gross-receipts B&O tax can make it increasingly expensive to operate and grow a business.
My Fix: Reduce unnecessary barriers and costs, examine reforms to the B&O tax, and create an environment where businesses can invest, hire, and expand.
I want Washington to be a state where businesses come, stay, grow, and hire.
05 | BUILD PEOPLE, NOT PROGRAMS 06 | PROTECTING KIDS, RESPECTING PARENTS The Problem: Spending more money or creating another program doesn't automatically solve the problem.
Measure success by whether people are actually moving toward stability, recovery, employment, and independence.
My Fix: Audit programs, measure outcomes, and invest in approaches that demonstrate results.
Pair housing with treatment and recovery when needed, and make independence the goal.
Compassion should have a compass.
06 | PROTECTING KIDS, RESPECTING PARENTS 06 | PROTECTING KIDS, RESPECTING PARENTS The Problem: Parents should never feel excluded from their child's education.
Our schools should partner with families, protect students' opportunities, and keep the classroom focused on preparing children for a successful future.
I believe we need to get back to the fundamentals: strong academics, safe classrooms, transparency, accountability, and an education system that equips every child with the knowledge and skills to succeed.
My Fix: I will fight to restore and protect parental rights and ensure parents have access to important information about their children's education and well-being.
I want our education policy centered on a straightforward goal: create classrooms designed to prepare our children for a brighter future.
