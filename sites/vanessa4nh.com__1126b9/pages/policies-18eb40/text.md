Policies
Low taxes are always the result of less spending.
I have and will continue to work to keep our taxes low.
I will preserve the NH Advantage of no sales tax or income tax.
I will promote business partnerships and remove burdensome regulations.
I will always oppose any legislation that would seek to infringe on our Second Amendment rights!
I will always defend the rights of law abiding NH citizens to keep and bear arms.
I will continually fight for parents to allow their children to attend any school where they can thrive, whether it is public, private, home schooled or charter.
High-quality education, transparency, and local control are a priority.
I will protect free speech, privacy, and property ownership.
I will advocate for quality healthcare with more options for patience and price transparency.
I will oppose all sanctuary policies and support law enforcement and first responders.
