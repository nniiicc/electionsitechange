Introduction
My name is Vanessa Sheehan and I am honored to serve as your State Representative.
I am a lifelong Milford resident with deep family roots in town.
As I have seen Milford grow and change over the years, what remains the same is Milford’s unique charm and strong community.
I love our town and I love our Live Free or Die State.
As your State Representative, I have worked to ensure that we do not lose those qualities we value so much.
As a fiscal conservative, I have and will continue to work to keep our taxes down, live within our means and maintain a balanced budget.
Sincerely,
Vanessa Sheehan
