Get to Know Katie
Katie Robins is a single mother, an ICU nurse, and a life long Hoosier.
Caring for others isn’t just her job, it’s part of her everyday life.
She sees that same care reflected in Grant County, where neighbors know one another, show up when help is needed, and value kindness, community, and a slower pace of life.
Katie believes that we deserve that same level of care from our leadership in the Indiana House of Representatives.

Priorities & Vision for District 31
Katie’s values guide her work, and her priorities focus on delivering real results for the people of District 31.
Affordability & Opportunity
Katie believes hard work should lead to stability, not constant financial stress.
She supports policies that address the rising cost of living, including affordable housing solutions, responsible approaches to rent stabilization, and economic policies that help working families keep more of what they earn.
With utility costs continuing to rise across Indiana, Katie believes families deserve practical solutions that keep essential services affordable and protect households from unpredictable rate increases
Accessible Healthcare & Veterans Support
As an ICU nurse, Katie has seen how access to healthcare affects families across Indiana.
She believes healthcare should be affordable and accessible, especially in rural areas where services are often limited.
Katie supports strengthening local healthcare systems and ensuring veterans receive the funding and care they have earned through their service.
Education & Strong Communities
Strong public schools create strong communities.
Katie supports fully funded public education, ensuring students, teachers, and schools have the resources they need to succeed.
Investing in education means investing in the future workforce and long-term economic growth of Indiana.
Infrastructure, Transportation & Workers
Safe roads, reliable infrastructure, and good-paying jobs are essential to everyday life.
Katie supports investing in road improvements and expanding transportation access so residents can safely reach work, school, and healthcare.
She stands with working families and believes protecting workers’ rights, supporting fair wages, and creating pathways to stable, well-paying jobs helps build strong local economies.
Investing in infrastructure means investing in both public safety and long-term economic growth for our communities.
Encouraging Civic Engagement
Katie believes democracy works best when people feel heard and involved.
She is committed to encouraging civic participation and ensuring residents of all ages feel empowered to engage in the decisions shaping their communities.
