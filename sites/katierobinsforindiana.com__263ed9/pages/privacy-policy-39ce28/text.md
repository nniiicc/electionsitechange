Privacy Policy Last Updated: February 10, 2026 The Katie Robins for State Representative campaign respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through this website.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.
Information We Collect We may collect personal information when you: Sign up for email or text updates Volunteer or request campaign information Make a donation Fill out forms on our website Contact the campaign directly Information collected may include: Name Email address Phone number Mailing address Donation information (processed through a secure third-party provider) We may also collect limited non-personal information automatically, such as: Browser type Device information Pages visited on our website General location data (city or region) This information helps us improve the website and campaign communications.
How We Use Your Information We use your information to: Communicate campaign updates, events, and volunteer opportunities Respond to questions or requests Process donations Improve our website and outreach efforts Comply with applicable campaign finance and election laws We do not sell or rent your personal information.
Text Messages and Emails If you opt in to receive emails or text messages from the campaign, you may unsubscribe at any time by: Clicking the unsubscribe link in emails, or Replying STOP to text messages.
Message and data rates may apply for text messages.
Sharing of Information We may share information with trusted service providers that help operate the campaign, such as: Email or texting platforms Donation processing services Website hosting providers These providers are only permitted to use information as necessary to provide services to the campaign.
We may also disclose information if required by law or election regulations.
You can disable cookies through your browser settings if you prefer.
Data Security We take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
However, no online transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.
Children’s Privacy This website is not intended for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
