About the event
Join us on April 22nd from 4 PM to 7 PM at Matter Park, located at N River Rd & N Quarry Rd in Marion, IN.
Celebrate Earth Day with a variety of green exhibits, indulge in delicious offerings from food trucks, and explore eco-friendly vendors showcasing sustainable products.
The Katie Robins For Indiana will be there with information on registering to vote and handing out seed packets!
