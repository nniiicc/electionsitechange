Today I filed for re-election to continue to serve as your Representative of Oklahoma’s Third Congressional District.
Serving as the voice for rural Oklahoma has been the honor of a lifetime.
As the former Chairman of the House Agriculture Committee and the top Republican on the House Science, Space, and Technology Committee, I’ll ensure the conservative values of Oklahoma are not lost upon those in Washington, D.C.
Oklahoma’s prosperity has made tremendous bounds since many of our families first settled, but we still have work to do to ensure a better future for our children and their children’s children.
