Welcome to the campaign website of JJ Grow, Republican for State Representative, District 23.
We know your online privacy is important to you, and we want you to know how we may collect, use, disclose or store Personal Information on this website (“Website”).
“Personal Information” is your name, contact information and other data associated with your name or contact information.
This Privacy Policy applies to the campaign’s online collection of Personal Information via this Website.
Please note that this Policy does not govern the campaign’s offline collection of Personal Information or collection of Personal Information, online or offline, by the campaign’s affiliates, or any other entity, unless otherwise specifically stated.
This Policy does not apply to the campaign’s presence on a third-party social networking site or any mobile applications not offered for download on this Website – please see the privacy statements at those locations to learn how your information will be handled.
Personal Information
We collect the Personal Information you provide us when you request information, register with our Website, make postings to our Website using a registered account, make a contribution, volunteer Personal Information in a survey, communicate with us via email, or otherwise contact us.
The campaign uses such information to provide you service, improve its communication efforts and offerings, and advance its mission.
We may share your Personal Information with our service providers and affiliates, as well as other organizations that share our views.
We may make this information available to other third parties.
We will disclose Personal Information if required by law, and may share it in order to protect the legal rights, property or safety of us or third parties.
We also will disclose Personal Information to any new or successor entity, should the campaign be reorganized, acquired or merged with another entity, in whole or part.
Information Collected When You Donate
When you make a contribution to the campaign of JJ Grow, Republican for State Representative, District 23 – to comply with state law, we are required to use our best efforts to collect and report the name, mailing address, occupation, and the dollar amount of contributions of individuals who donate to our campaign.
These records are often made available to the public.
Any credit card information provided is only used to immediately process your contribution.
The campaign does not retain your credit card information once the contribution is processed online.
Generally, contributions are non-refundable.
Should you choose to contribute, you will receive an e-mail confirmation of your contribution.
Sharing of Information
We will not share, sell, rent, or disclose your personal information to any third parties, except as described in this Privacy Policy or when required by law.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data
We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
Cookies and Similar Technologies
Like many websites, this Website may use cookies, image tags, and similar technologies.
A cookie is a small text file containing a unique identification number that is stored on your computer.
Image tags (also called web beacons, web bugs, clear GIFs or single-pixel GIFs), which work in conjunction with cookies, are small image files that may be located on select pages of our Website or within web-based emails that we may send.
We may also collect your IP address and other information that your browser makes available as a function of visiting our Website.
We use these technologies to learn how our site is used, save your preferences, and improve the performance and offerings of our Website.
These technologies do not collect Personal Information, as they do not include your name or contact information.
We may share information collected via these technologies to improve our offerings, to advance our mission, or for other purposes.
Email, Mail and Telephone Communications
If you decide you no longer wish to receive periodic telephone contacts or emails from us and like-minded organizations, please let us know by following the instructions you can find on this Website or in the emails themselves.
For example, should you wish to opt-out from emails, you may follow the instructions in the email on how to unsubscribe from such mailings.
Children’s Privacy
We do not knowingly contact or collect Personal Information from children under 18, and our site is not intended to solicit information of any kind from children under 18.
When a user discloses Personal Information on the Website, he or she is representing his or her age as at least eighteen years.
If we are informed that we have unintentionally received Personal Information from a child under 18, we will delete that information.
Third-Party Links and Ads
This Website may include links to online offerings provided by third parties that the campaign does not own or control.
This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third-party offerings.
We encourage you to visit any privacy statement available at those locations to learn how your Personal Information may be collected and used.
This Website may contain advertisements provided by third parties.
Often, these third-party advertisers employ cookies and similar technologies.
This Privacy Policy does not apply to third party ads that may appear on our Website, even if we have relationships with those third parties and even if their advertisements appear on our Website.
Please visit the privacy statements of these third parties to learn about their practices.
Security
The campaign has taken steps designed to protect the loss, misuse, or alteration of your Personal Information.
The campaign has also taken precautions to ensure that communications initiated by our contribution form are kept secure.
Please note that Personal Information sent over an unsecured Internet connection could be obtained by third parties.
Updates to Privacy Policy
From time to time, we may use customer information for new, unanticipated uses not previously disclosed in our privacy policy.
If our information practices change materially at some time in the future, we will post an updated policy to our Website.
