Home About Platform ISSUES News Endorsements Volunteer Donate Menu Menu Platform JJ Grow is committed to keeping Florida free and advancing common sense, business-minded solutions to the issues most important to our community.
Making Florida More Affordable Families across Florida are feeling the pressure of rising costs from groceries to gasoline.
I’m committed to policies that ease the burden by cutting unnecessary regulations, opposing tax hikes and fee increases, and promoting a competitive market that keeps prices in check.
We must create an economic environment where working Floridians can thrive and keep more of their hard-earned money.
Improving the Property Insurance Market As a coastal district, skyrocketing property insurance rates are one of the biggest threats to homeownership.
I support reforms that reduce fraud and abuse in the system while holding insurers accountable to ensure fair and stable pricing for consumers.
We must continue to strengthen our legal system to prevent frivolous lawsuits and encourage insurers to re-enters the Florida market, increasing competition and lowering premiums.
Reducing Property Taxes to Keep Families in their Homes Homeowners deserve relief from rising property taxes.
I will fight to preserve and expand property tax exemptions for seniors, veterans, and working families.
We must also ensure local governments are using tax dollars efficiently and any increases in revenue should come with increased transparency and accountability.
Supporting Law Enforcement and Preventing Illegal Immigration Backing the blue isn’t just a slogan, it is a commitment that I pride myself on.
I strongly support our sheriff and law enforcement officers who work tirelessly to keep our communities safe.
I also believe that we must enforce our immigration laws and oppose sanctuary city policies that threaten our safety.
Our borders must be secure, and our communities protected from illegal activity.
Standing with our Farmers and Ranchers Agriculture is the backbone of Florida’s heritage and economy.
I’m working to protect our farmers from overregulation, ensure continued access to clean water, and invest in disease prevention programs like citrus greening research.
Supporting agriculture means supporting local jobs and the rural way of life that defines our region.
Keeping Florida Free Keeping Florida free means defending personal liberties, parental rights, and freedom from government overreach.
I will continue to stand against mandates that infringe on individual choice and fight form common-sense legislation that reflects the values of our community.
Florida is and will continue to be an example for the nation.
Improving Education for Parents and Students Every child in Florida deserves access to a high-quality education tailored to their needs.
I support expanding school choice options, empowering parents, and ensuring that our schools focus on academics, not radical agendas.
We must reward excellent teachers, promote vocational training, and prepare students for real world success.
