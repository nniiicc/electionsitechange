Congressman Joe Wilson is committed to a strong, stable economy built on responsible spending and predictable budgeting.
He supports policies that rein in Washington’s waste, protect taxpayers, and put the country on a path to a genuinely balanced budget.
By focusing on lower costs, fair regulations, and an environment where small businesses can grow, Joe works to ensure families and employers in South Carolina have the confidence to plan for the future.
As a steadfast advocate for a strong national defense, Joe Wilson believes America is safest when its military is well-resourced, well-trained, and ready to meet threats abroad before they reach our shores.
He champions investments in defense capability, supports our allies, and insists on clear, decisive policies that deter adversaries.
Peace through strength isn’t a slogan—it’s a guiding principle for securing American families.
Joe Wilson supports an all-of-the-above energy strategy that strengthens America’s grid, lowers costs, and promotes innovation for American energy independence..
He is a leading voice for expanding energy infrastructure—including small modular reactors, next-generation nuclear technology, and responsible domestic production.
By investing in modern, reliable energy systems, Joe is working to protect national security, create good jobs, and keep South Carolina at the forefront of America’s energy future.
As the proud father of four military sons, Joe Wilson understands the sacrifice made by service members and their families.
He has been a consistent champion for veterans—pushing for faster benefits, expanded healthcare access, improved transition services, and accountability within the VA.
His mission is simple: every veteran should receive the respect, support, and resources they earned through their service.
As the husband of a former educator, Joe stands for parent-driven education with quality educators and facilities, preparing future generations for challenges and opportunities.
Additionally, Joe sponsors the Right to Work legislation, which prevents employees from being forced to pay union dues.
South Carolina succeeds as a Right to Work state.
As a long term defender of Medicare, Congressman Wilson is committed to ensuring seniors do not suffer rising costs of healthcare, and that their coverage is protected.
Additionally, Congressman Wilson is a strong supporter of medical research and the medical community- including his second son who is a practicing orthopedic surgeon in South Carolina.
