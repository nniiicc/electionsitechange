Policy
We’re committed to protecting your privacy when you visit this site, donate to the campaign, or sign up for our communications.
This page explains what we collect and the choices you have.
Last updated · April 2026 · Paid for by Larson for Justice
Larson for Justice (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is the official campaign committee for Judge Dave Larson’s candidacy for Washington State Supreme Court, Position 5.
We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits our website, donates to our campaign, or engages with our communications.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.
By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.
We may update this Policy periodically — the date above reflects the most recent revision.
Your continued use of the site after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
When you fill out a form, sign up for updates, make a donation, volunteer, or otherwise engage with us directly, you may provide:
- Name, email address, postal address, phone number, and/or mobile number
- Employer name, occupation, and zip code (required for certain campaign finance disclosures)
- Payment information when making a contribution (processed securely by our donation platform)
- Any messages, endorsements, or other content you submit through our forms
When you visit our site, certain information is collected automatically through your browser and third-party services we use, including:
- IP address and general geographic location
- Browser type, operating system, and device information
- Pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring URLs
- Interaction data such as clicks and scroll behavior (used in aggregate, anonymous form)
This site renders fonts through Google Fonts and Adobe Fonts.
These services may receive your IP address and browser information as part of delivering font files to your browser.
If you opt into our mobile messaging program, we may collect your cell phone number, your carrier’s name, and the date, time, and content of messages you send to us.
See the Mobile Messaging & SMS section for full details.
We use the information we collect to:
- Send you campaign updates, newsletters, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities you’ve requested
- Process donations and comply with campaign finance reporting requirements
- Respond to your inquiries and support requests
- Improve the website and understand how visitors use it
- Send text messages if you’ve opted into our SMS program
We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.
SMS opt-in data and consent information will never be shared with any third party.
We may share information as required by law, including campaign finance disclosure obligations under Washington State and federal election law.
Donor information submitted through our contribution pages is subject to public disclosure requirements applicable to political campaigns.
The following terms govern our Mobile Messaging Program.
This language is required by our SMS service provider and must appear as written.
By signing up for our Mobile Messaging Program, you consent to receive the following types of text messages from Larson for Justice.
- Polling / Voting Text Messages: Such as election reminders and opinion polls.
- Public Service Announcement Text Messages: Including legislative updates, member updates, and voter education.
- Completing a sign-up form on our website and checking the text message consent box.
- Providing explicit consent in any other manner as indicated by Larson for Justice.
- Messages may be sent using an autodialer or similar technology.
- Message frequency may vary depending on your interaction with the Program.
- Standard message and data rates may apply.
- Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
To stop receiving messages, text STOP at any time.
You will receive a confirmation text confirming you have been unsubscribed.
After this, you will no longer receive messages from the Program unless you opt back in.
For help, text HELP or contact us at campaign@larsonforjustice.com.
You will receive instructions on how to use the service.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Online contributions to Larson for Justice are processed through eFundraising Connections, LLC (efundraisingconnections.com), a third-party campaign fundraising platform.
We do not store your full credit card or banking information on our servers — payment processing is handled entirely by eFundraising Connections, which maintains its own security standards and privacy policy.
By making a contribution through our donation page, you are also subject to eFundraising Connections’ User Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
You must be 18 years of age or older to make a contribution through the platform.
Processing fees may apply and are subject to change at eFundraising Connections’ discretion.
All transactions are final and non-refundable.
Washington State and federal campaign finance law requires us to collect and publicly disclose certain donor information — including name, mailing address, employer, and occupation — for contributions above applicable reporting thresholds.
By making a contribution, you acknowledge that this information may appear in public campaign finance filings.
If you provide your email during the donation process, we may send you campaign-related communications.
You may unsubscribe at any time via the link at the bottom of any email.
We send emails through Mailchimp.
SMS opt-in data is never shared with third parties.
We use cookies — small files stored in your browser — to improve your experience and understand how visitors use our site.
Cookies help us remember your preferences and analyze traffic patterns.
We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymized data about site usage, including pages visited and time on site.
This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.
The information is used solely to improve the campaign website and our outreach efforts.
You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings.
Most browsers allow you to block, delete, or receive warnings about cookies.
Disabling cookies may limit some functionality of the site.
To opt out of Google Analytics tracking across sites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
To opt out of interest-based advertising more broadly, visit the Digital Advertising Alliance at aboutads.info/choices.
We use trusted third-party services to operate this website and campaign, including:
- Mailchimp — email marketing and newsletter delivery
- Google Analytics — anonymized website traffic analysis
- Google Fonts / Adobe Fonts — web font delivery
- Formspree — form submission processing
- eFundraising Connections — online donation processing
Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.
We encourage you to review them if you have questions about how they handle data.
We do not authorize these services to use your information for their own marketing purposes.
Pages on this site may include embedded content such as videos, social media posts, or images hosted by third parties.
Embedded content behaves as if you had visited the originating website directly — those sites may set their own cookies, collect data about you, and track your interactions with the embedded content.
We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including SSL / TLS encryption for data transmitted through this site.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use caution when sharing sensitive information online.
You have the right to:
- Unsubscribe from email communications at any time via the link in any email we send
- Opt out of SMS messages by texting STOP to any message we send
- Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at campaign@larsonforjustice.com or (206) 207-2903.
Please note that certain information may be retained as required by campaign finance law regardless of a deletion request.
This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.
