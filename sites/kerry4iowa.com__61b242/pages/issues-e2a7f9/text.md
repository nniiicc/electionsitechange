DONATE Issues Economics Economic freedom allows each Iowan to define and pursue their own success.
Responsible budgets, while limiting overreaching regulations, make Iowa a better place to raise a family, start a business, and earn a living.
Education It is important to have a strong public school system, and vital that parents remain an essential partner in the education of their children.
Not all children learn the same way, and parents deserve the flexibility of seeking the education that best fits the needs of their child.
Law Enforcement & First Responders Iowa government should support law enforcement officers and first responders who put their lives on the line to protect us, our homes, and our quality of life.
Rural Communities As a lifetime rural Walcott resident, Kerry knows what our rural communities face every day.
Their concerns matter, and should be heard in our state government.
