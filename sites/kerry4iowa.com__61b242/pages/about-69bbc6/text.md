DONATE About Kerry Our Liberties We Prize, and Our Rights We Will Maintain State of Iowa Motto Kerry is a 5th generation farmer, a small business owner, and has lived in Walcott most of his 56 years.
He enjoys the opportunity to farm with his father and his son in Scott and Muscatine counties, while also maintaining investments in Davenport.
With professional and personal connections in Cedar, Scott, and Muscatine counties, Kerry is uniquely suited to serve the citizens of Senate District 41 in urban, rural, and agricultural issues in the State legislature. ​ Kerry has been a member of the Muscatine County Farm Bureau since 1994 and a member of the Muscatine Board from 2010 to 2022.
In addition to serving on various board committees, he has served as Secretary, Treasurer, Vice President, and completed his term as board President. ​ Kerry is an organizer and former co-host of the Quad City Conservative Breakfast Club, an informational discussion group.
Kerry is an advocate for agriculture, economic growth, and conservative policies.
He is a member of Risen Christ Lutheran Church in Davenport, and has one son and three grandchildren.
