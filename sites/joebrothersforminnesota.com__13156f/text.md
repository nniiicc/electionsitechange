Raised with small town Minnesota values.
Joe's grandfather and grandmother immigrated to southwestern Minnesota in the late 1880s.
His grandfather threshed up and down western Minnesota and eventually homesteaded first in North Dakota and then Montana.
Joe grew up in Montana in a four bedroom house in a family of nine kids.
After Joe graduated from college, he moved to Minnesota and immediately felt at home.
The values he was raised with were the same as the Minnesota values he experienced.
