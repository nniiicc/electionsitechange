Rally Celebrating Caregivers
This year the legislature appropriated $32 million in additional funding for caregivers whose wages have stagnated for years.
Caregivers celebrated with a rally in Anchorage and I was honored to be there.
Let's keep investing in core services that make Alaska a better place to live for people of all ages.
