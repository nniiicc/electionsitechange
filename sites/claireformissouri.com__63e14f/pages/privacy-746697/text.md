The Actual Privacy Policy Our web server tracks the IP addresses of the individual visitors to the site - all web servers do.
We also use Google Analytics to produce reports on web traffic.
Despite what you see on TV, we can't really tell who you are by your IP address.
Your ISP can, but we cannot.
The web server tracking is to help the server run faster and track anyone who launches an attack against the site.
Google Analytics produces reports to tell us things like how many unique visitors we had to the site and how many repeat visitors we saw (mostly this is my step-dad).
Otherwise, we don't really even look at the logs other than to see how many different people stopped by to say hello.
Obviously, if you fill out our contact form, we are keeping that info.
