Elizabeth Fitzgibbon is a mother, neighbor, and advocate running for Congress in Wisconsin’s 6th District because she believes government should serve the People, not protect itself. ​ Her path to this campaign was not traditional.
After a personal landmark family law case exposed serious failures in the court system, Elizabeth fought for the truth and pursued every avenue for accountability - taking her case all the way to the U.S.
Supreme Court. ​ That experience changed her.
It showed Elizabeth that not only is the system fundamentally broken - many in power want to keep it that way for their own benefit. ​ She realized that almost every struggle families face - from the high cost of living to healthcare frustrations - stems from a government that has drifted away from the Constitution to serve its own interests.
These aren’t just "policy glitches"; they are the predictable results of a foundation that has stopped answering to the People. ​ Elizabeth is running as an Independent because you can’t fix a foundation with a party-line patchwork.
While the two major parties offer temporary band-aids to quiet the crowd, Elizabeth is calling for a full Constitutional Audit to expose the gears of the machine and actually solve the problem.
She isn’t going to Washington to manage the decline; she’s going to restore the accountability our families deserve.
