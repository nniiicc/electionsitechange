Meet David Miller
As a lifelong educator, I’ve spent more than 35 years fighting for students and families.
I’ve taught in public schools, correctional facilities, and a drug-abuse prevention center—places where I saw firsthand the difference education can make.
I created hands-on vocational and drug education programs, served as Chair of a Special Education Department, and later taught English as a Second Language.
After a long career in the classroom, I made my home in Oak Ridge and continued serving the community as a member of the Anderson County School Board.
I’ve always believed in putting people first and finding practical solutions that work for everyone.
In the State Senate, I’ll keep fighting for:
- Strong Public Schools — making sure our tax dollars go to schools that need them, not vouchers that take away from public schools
- Tax-Free groceries — because feeding your family shouldn’t cost extra
- People over profits — so everyday Tennesseans come first
I’m ready to take East Tennessee’s common-sense values to the State House and work for a better future for all of us.
