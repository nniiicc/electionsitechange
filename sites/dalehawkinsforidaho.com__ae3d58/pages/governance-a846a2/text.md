Dale Hawkins for Idaho Home About Issues Governance Get Involved Donate Proper Governance Limited Government · Individual Liberty · Free Enterprise · Traditional Values The foundation of freedom I am a Republican dedicated to the preservation of our Constitutional Republic.
Governmental power must be limited, defined, and constrained by the state and federal Constitution while being kept in check by an informed electorate.
Our founders were wise to set up checks and balances to limit government, but it is up to every generation to preserve them.
The Constitution isn't a suggestion.
It's not a living document that changes with the political winds.
It's the supreme law of the land, and every elected official takes an oath to uphold it.
That oath means something to me.
It means that when I vote on legislation, I ask first whether it's constitutional, not whether it's popular or politically expedient.
Too often, politicians treat the Constitution as an obstacle to be worked around rather than a framework to be honored.
They expand government power, create new regulations, and pass laws invoking powers not granted to them, all in the name of solving problems government often created in the first place.
Constitutional Limits Government authority must remain strictly within the boundaries established by the Constitution.
When government exceeds these limits, individual liberty suffers.
Protection of Rights Our rights come from God, not from government.
The primary role of government is to protect these God-given rights, not to grant or restrict them based on political whims.
Rule of Law Laws must be applied equally to all citizens.
No one is above the law, and no one should be subject to arbitrary government action.
Due process and equal protection are non-negotiable.
Accountability to the People Elected officials serve at the pleasure of the people, not the other way around.
Government must be transparent, accountable, and responsive to those it serves.
Defending Idaho's sovereignty Idaho is not a subdivision of the federal government.
The 10th Amendment makes this clear: powers not delegated to the federal government are reserved to the states and the people.
Yet time and again, we see federal overreach that treats states as mere administrative units of a centralized government.
Idaho has the right and the duty to push back against unconstitutional federal mandates.
Whether it's land management, healthcare, education, or gun rights, Idaho should be free to chart its own course based on the values and priorities of its citizens.
I've worked to strengthen Idaho's sovereignty by opposing federal mandates, defending our natural resources, and ensuring that Idaho law reflects Idaho values.
I'll continue to stand firm against any attempt to turn Idaho into a puppet of Washington, D.C.
Accountability matters I believe elected officials should be transparent about their positions and accountable for their votes.
If you have questions about what I stand for or want to know how I voted on a particular issue, I want to hear from you.
