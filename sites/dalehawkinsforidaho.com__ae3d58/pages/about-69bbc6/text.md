Dale Hawkins for Idaho Home About Issues Governance Get Involved Donate About Dale Hawkins A common man standing up for Idaho's freedom and traditional values The journey to Idaho In 1998, my family made a life-changing decision.
We moved to Idaho seeking something that was becoming increasingly rare in America: the freedom to raise our children according to our values and beliefs.
We wanted the liberty to homeschool our six children, to live out our Christian faith without government interference, and to be part of a community that still believed in limited government and individual responsibility.
Idaho welcomed us with open arms.
Here, we found a state that understood what it meant to respect parental rights, to honor constitutional principles, and to let families make their own decisions.
That freedom is what drew us here, and it's what I'm fighting to protect for every Idaho family.
I'm not a career politician.
I'm not a lawyer looking to climb the political ladder.
I'm a husband, a father, and an everyday citizen who believes that government works best when it stays within its constitutional boundaries and lets people live their lives in freedom.
Faith, family, and freedom My Christian faith is the foundation of everything I do.
It shapes how I view government, how I approach policy, and how I interact with my fellow Idahoans.
I believe that our rights come from God, not from government, and that those rights must be protected at all costs.
As a father of six, I understand what's at stake when we talk about education policy, parental rights, and the future of our state.
Every decision I make in the legislature is guided by one simple question: Is this good for Idaho families?
Will this protect their freedom or diminish it?
I'm pro-life without apology.
I believe in the sanctity of human life from conception to natural death.
I believe in the Second Amendment.
I believe in property rights, self defense, and limited government.
And I believe that Idaho should remain a beacon of freedom in an increasingly regulated world.
Fighting for your freedom Since entering public service, I've worked tirelessly to defend the principles that make Idaho special.
I've fought for homeschooling rights, pushed back against federal overreach, defended the Second Amendment, pushed hard for stronger immigration enforcement, and stood firm on pro-life values even when it wasn't politically convenient.
I believe in accountability and transparency.
Elected officials should answer to the people, not to special interests or political insiders.
That's why I make myself available to constituents and explain my votes, and why I'm willing to stand alone when principle demands it.
Idaho is worth fighting for.
Our way of life, our values, and our freedoms are under constant attack from those who think government knows better than families.
I'm committed to standing in the gap, to being a voice for common-sense conservatism, and to ensuring that Idaho remains a place where freedom thrives.
From Fernwood, Idaho Proud to call Fernwood home and honored to serve the people of Idaho.
Together, we can preserve the freedom and values that make our state exceptional.
