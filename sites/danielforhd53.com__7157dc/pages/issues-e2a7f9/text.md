Housing is out of reach for first-time buyers.
Rent keeps rising.
Groceries, utilities, and insurance all cost more.
Meanwhile, taxes and fees continue to creep up.
We need to take this seriously.
I support cutting unnecessary red tape so we can build more attainable housing, not just luxury units.
I will fight for fiscal discipline in Denver, oppose unnecessary tax and fee increases, and defend TABOR so families can keep more of what they earn.
I will also work to push back on rising utility costs and hidden fees that hit working families and seniors the hardest.
Victim-Centered Justice What happened to Carissa should never happen to another family.
Our justice system must prioritize victims, not criminals.
Right now, too many families feel ignored while cases drag on and accountability falls short.
I support commonsense reforms like the Carissa Amendment: stronger penalties for hit-and-run cases involving serious injury, guaranteed victim input in bond and sentencing decisions, and a system that treats victims with the dignity they deserve.
Public safety and justice should not come at the expense of victims.
Parental Rights Parents, not government, should be in charge of their children’s upbringing and education.
Families deserve transparency and a real voice in what’s happening in their schools.
I support expanding school choice, strengthening parental involvement, and ensuring curriculum decisions respect families’ values.
This isn’t about politics—it’s about trust, accountability, and putting kids first.
Education Kids should go to school to learn the fundamentals: reading, writing, math, science, and real history, not to be indoctrinated with the latest social ideologies.
I want our children to receive a strong academic education in a safe environment, where they can develop critical thinking skills and discover who they are naturally, the way previous generations did.
Too often today, schools are pushing political and social agendas instead of focusing on the basics that actually prepare kids for life.
That needs to change.
I will fight to keep ideological indoctrination out of our classrooms and ensure our schools prioritize real education, academic excellence, and age-appropriate learning.
Parents and teachers in Fort Collins deserve schools that educate our children, not try to reshape them.
Mental Health & Medicaid Reform Colorado families deserve a system that actually works.
As a caregiver for my son, I’ve seen how Medicaid and mental health services can be difficult to navigate, slow to respond, and burdened by bureaucracy.
Families shouldn’t have to fight the system just to get basic care.
We need to streamline services, reduce administrative waste, and ensure resources are going directly to the people who need them.
That means focusing on outcomes, accountability, and real support—not more layers of government.
Improving these systems isn’t just good policy—it’s essential to lowering stress and financial pressure on families already doing their best to get by.
2nd Amendment Rights The Second Amendment is not a suggestion, a privilege, or a topic for endless debate.
The words are clear: the right of the people to keep and bear arms shall not be infringed.
That is absolute.
As a veteran who served in the Air Force and later as a contractor in Afghanistan, I understand what it means to depend on the ability to defend yourself and those around you.
That right belongs to every law-abiding citizen.
It is not granted by government and it is not up for negotiation.
I will oppose any effort to weaken, redefine, or chip away at this fundamental protection.
