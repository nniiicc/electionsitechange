About Catherine Born and raised in Adams County.
Catherine has deep family roots in this community.
She and her husband Dave raised their three children — Brianna, Brantly, and Sebastian — here, instilling the values of family, hard work, and service that define central Pennsylvania.
Catherine has spent years serving the public, both professionally and personally.
She owns and operates Orchard Views LLC, a small family farm in York Springs, supporting local agriculture and small-business growth.
She also brings extensive experience in state government, having served eight years in the Pennsylvania House of Representatives as District Director for the 193rd Legislative District — managing daily operations and staff, helping constituents navigate state services, securing grants for local organizations, and working directly with community leaders across Adams and Cumberland Counties.
Before entering government service, Catherine worked in healthcare for over a decade, assisting cancer patients and families through some of their most difficult moments.
That experience shaped her commitment to compassionate, practical leadership and strong constituent advocacy.
