Take Care of Kids
Education
Our children deserve a quality education that prepares them for life, whether they choose college, the trades, military service, or a career right here in Wyoming.
I support strong schools with local decision-making.
Take Care of Families
Jobs
Strong families need good jobs.
I support policies that encourage economic growth, strengthen Wyoming businesses, and create opportunities for people to build a career and raise a family in our state.
Housing
Too many working families, young professionals, and retirees are struggling to find affordable housing.
We need practical solutions that increase housing opportunities while preserving the character and quality of life that make Wyoming special.
Take Care of Communities
Essential Services
Safe communities depend on reliable services such as roads, public safety, healthcare, transportation, and essential services for the vulnerable: children, seniors, and those with physical disabilities.
We must be responsible stewards of taxpayer dollars while ensuring these essential services remain available for our neighbors who need us to stand up for them.
Local Control
The people closest to a problem are usually best equipped to solve it.
I believe decisions about our schools, communities, and local priorities should be made by local citizens and local leaders whenever possible.
Pro-Business
Small businesses are the backbone of Wyoming’s economy.
Government should create an environment where businesses can grow, hire workers, and invest in their communities without unnecessary regulations or uncertainty.
Take Care of Our Rights
Constitutional Rights
Our rights are enshrined in the US and Wyoming Constitutions.
I will defend our rights, like the Second Amendment, freedoms of speech, religion, assembly, and due process that make Wyoming and America the free and exceptional places to live, work, and raise a family.
