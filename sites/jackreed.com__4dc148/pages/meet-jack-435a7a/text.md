Growing up on Pontiac Avenue in Cranston, the son of a blue-collar family, Jack Reed knows firsthand how a good education, a good-paying job, access to high-quality health care, and an affordable place to call home helps hardworking Americans reach their full potential.
Those lessons drive Jack’s service to Rhode Island every day.
Jack Reed first took an oath to uphold and defend the Constitution at the United States Military Academy at West Point.
He qualified as a U.S.
Army Ranger and commanded paratroopers in the 82 nd Airborne during eight years of active-duty service.
Defining his leadership as a tested and trusted fighter for Rhode Islanders, Jack believes that our government exists to serve every American – not the powerful few.
Jack has dedicated his life to serving his fellow Americans, helping Rhode Islanders create a better life for their families, and forging a government that works for all of us.
In these troubling times, Jack Reed is the kind of fighter we need in Washington.
Jack uses the lessons he learned on Pontiac Avenue, at West Point, in the U.S.
Army, and as a proven and effective champion for Rhode Island to bring the fight to Donald Trump every day in the halls of Congress and to deliver real results for Rhode Islanders.
A Tested, Trusted Fighter for Rhode Island While Donald Trump abuses his power to serve his own political interests and skew our economy and tax system in favor of billionaires, Jack Reed is prioritizing regular Americans over the rich and powerful by fighting to lower the cost of living and clamp down on Trump’s lawlessness and corruption.
As Trump’s tariff chaos fuels inflation and drives up costs for working families, Jack is fighting to return the money Trump illegally grabbed from working Americans.
Using his hard-earned know-how and seats on the powerful Appropriations and Armed Services Committees, Jack Reed is leading the fight in the Senate against Donald Trump’s outrageous abuses of power and dangerous military policies that are weakening our national security.
Jack is widely recognized as a leading expert on national security in Congress.
He voted against the Iraq War, opposes Trump’s Iran War, and is using his voice and his vote in the Senate to stop Trump’s war and costly, unnecessary military operations around the world.
Jack is focused on the future.
He believes we have an opportunity to do better.
He is using his experience to fight for transformational investments in housing, education, health care, and our environment by making billionaires pay their fair share and reforming our tax system to help Rhode Island families.
Jack knows that we must confront the social, economic, and technological challenges from artificial intelligence and the global pressures that have been worsened by the Trump Administration.
That’s why he is using his expertise in defense, housing, education, and the economy to fight for wise investments in our future, building on the billions of dollars he has delivered for Rhode Island to lift up families and drive our state forward.
Congress has the tools to accomplish all of this and more.
When Democrats win back control of Congress this November, Jack is ready to get it done.
Jack Reed is a proven champion for Rhode Island, fighting for and winning resources for the Ocean State like over $300 million for affordable housing construction since 2020; new research investments to grow Rhode Island’s leadership in the Blue Economy; new Coast Guard ships; more jobs down in Quonset; improvements at T.F.
Green Airport to keep our state competitive; federal funding for our nonprofits; and investments to fix up our roads and bridges and expand public transit options across the state.
Jack gets the job done.
Fighting Trump Lawlessness & Dysfunction With Accountability & Results When Donald Trump and his Republican majorities forced their disastrous Billionaire Tax Giveaways through Congress, Jack Reed fought back.
He put up more than twenty amendments on the Senate floor, forcing Senate Republicans to show their true priorities to the American people and fighting for real change for working families.
Jack is ready to immediately reverse Trump’s $1 trillion cut to Medicaid and improve our health care system so that all Americans can get the care they need when they need it.
Jack is fighting to strengthen and expand the Affordable Care Act (ACA), protect and restore reproductive rights for all Americans, safeguard and boost Medicaid and Medicare from additional Republican cuts, and increase support for mental health programs like the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline that Jack helped create.
When Republicans in Congress tried to greenlight Trump’s rogue ICE and out-of-control DHS agencies to continue trampling on the rights of Americans, Jack Reed said no.
He voted against funding Trump’s quota-driven immigration raids and is fighting to rein in Trump’s ICE lawlessness.
Hammering Republicans for their out-of-touch priorities, Jack fought to redirect Trump’s unnecessary $62 billion ICE slush fund and use it instead to create millions of new affordable homes.
