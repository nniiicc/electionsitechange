Meet Jen
Jen Lunsford is a working mom, attorney, and your Assemblymember for New York's 135th District — Penfield, East Rochester, Perinton, Pittsford, and Mendon.
Since flipping the seat in 2020, Jen has fought to fully fund our schools, make childcare affordable, and deliver real investments in the east side communities she calls home.
