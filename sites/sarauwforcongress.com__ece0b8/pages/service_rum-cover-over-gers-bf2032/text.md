What is the rum cover-over?
It’s federal revenue returned to the Virgin Islands based on rum produced here and sold in the U.S.
It’s a major funding source pledged to support the Government Employees’ Retirement System (GERS).
Why does the 1999 rate matter?
The $13.25 rate was set over two decades ago.
Because it never adjusted for inflation, its real value has eroded — weakening the protection it was meant to provide.
What is Janelle's position?
Keep $13.25 as the guaranteed floor, but modernize the formula so it grows with inflation and market conditions, protecting retirees long-term.
