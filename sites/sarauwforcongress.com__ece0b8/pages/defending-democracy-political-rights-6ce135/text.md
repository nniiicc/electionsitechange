Still Waiting for American Rights.
For more than one hundred years, Virgin Islanders have been denied full democratic participation.
We are U.S. citizens — yet we cannot vote for President, and our representative in Congress cannot cast the deciding vote on the laws that govern our lives.
The century-old Insular Cases continue to justify unequal treatment under federal law.
That doctrine was wrong when it was written, and it is indefensible today.
Janelle's Federal Strategy A Coordinated Federal Civil-Rights Strategy Introduce legislation to expand the practical authority and procedural influence of the Delegate — recognizing existing committee voting rights while addressing limitations in final passage.
Advance congressional hearings examining the continued application of the Insular Cases.
Build a unified coalition across the U.S. territories.
Partner with the Congressional Black Caucus to elevate territorial disenfranchisement as a civil-rights issue.
Activate the Virgin Islands diaspora to advocate within their own congressional districts.
The Goal Equal voice.
Equal rights.
Equal standing under federal law.
