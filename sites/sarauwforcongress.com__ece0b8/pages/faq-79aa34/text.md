Sarauw?
Janelle is a public servant born and raised on St.
Thomas, and a proud product of the Virgin Islands public school system.
A former educator, she was elected to the 32nd Legislature in 2017 and served three consecutive terms as Senator, known for preparation, precision, and principled courage.
She is now running for Delegate to Congress.
What office is she running for?
Janelle is running to be the U.S.
Virgin Islands Delegate to Congress, the territory’s representative in Washington.
What makes her different?
Janelle asks the hard questions, challenges broken systems, and is not afraid to stand alone when the people’s interests are at stake.
Her campaign is people-powered and not funded by special interests.
When is the Democratic Primary Election?
The Democratic Primary is August 1, 2026.
How do I donate?
You can contribute any amount through the campaign’s ActBlue page.
A gift of just $# is more than a donation, it’s a statement that we’re ready for leadership that listens and delivers.
What is the deadline to register?
You must be registered by July 2, 2026 to vote in the primary.
Don’t assume your status is current, check it and encourage a friend or family member to do the same.
How do I register or check my status?
Visit vivote.gov to register or confirm your registration.
If you’re unsure about your summer plans, you can also apply for an absentee ballot there.
Where do I find Janelle on the ballot?
Janelle K.
Sarauw is #4 on the ballot.
Look for #4 for Delegate to Congress.
What is her position on the rum cover-over and GERS?
Keep the $13.25 rate as a guaranteed minimum, but modernize the formula so it grows with inflation and market conditions, protecting retirees for generations to come.
What will she do on healthcare?
Fight the federal Medicaid funding cap, strengthen local hospitals, expand access, and make sure no resident has to travel off-island for the care they deserve.
