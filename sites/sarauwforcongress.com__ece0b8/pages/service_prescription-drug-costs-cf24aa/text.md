Prescription costs continue to climb, putting essential care out of reach for too many in our community.
Janelle will work to lower the cost of prescription drugs and expand access, so families across the territory can afford the medicine they need to stay healthy.
Lower the cost of prescription medication Expand access for seniors and families Protect those managing chronic conditions Push for fair pricing in the territory Reduce out-of-pocket burdens on patients Keep essential medicine within reach Do you have any Questions?
Why are prescription costs a federal issue?
Drug pricing, access, and coverage are shaped heavily by federal policy — which is exactly where our Delegate to Congress can advocate for change.
Who does this help most?
Seniors, working families, and anyone managing a chronic condition who feels the strain of rising medication costs.
What will Janelle push for?
Lower costs, expanded access, and fairer pricing so no one is forced to ration or skip the medicine they depend on.
