Too often, our veterans face delays, red tape, and barriers to the benefits and care they’ve already earned.
Janelle will fight to ensure Virgin Islands veterans receive the benefits, healthcare, and respect they are owed, delivered fully and fairly, without the endless runaround.
Secure the benefits veterans have earned Improve access to veterans' healthcare Cut the red tape and delays Support families of those who served Advocate for VI veterans at the federal level Treat service with the respect it deserves Do you have any Questions?
What challenges do VI veterans face?
Many encounter long waits, complex paperwork, and limited local access to the benefits and care they’re entitled to.
How will Janelle help?
By advocating at the federal level for our veterans, pushing to streamline access, and fighting to deliver what they’ve already earned.
Why does federal representation matter here?
Veterans’ benefits and healthcare are federally administered, so a strong voice in Washington directly affects what our veterans receive.
