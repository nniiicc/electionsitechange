The Virgin Islands imports the overwhelming majority of its food.
That leaves us dangerously exposed, one hurricane, one supply-chain shock, one shipping disruption, and shelves go empty while prices climb.
Food security is not a luxury or an afterthought.
It is a matter of survival and self-reliance.
We must treat the strength of our local farmers and fishermen as the national-security issue it truly is.
Janelle's Federal Strategy Strengthening Local Food and Self-Reliance Expand Virgin Islands access to USDA and federal agriculture programs.
Secure federal investment in modern farming and fisheries infrastructure.
Support our farmers and fishermen with the tools, training, and resources they need.
Build a stronger, more self-sufficient local food system.
Strengthen food supply resilience so we are ready for every storm season.
The Goal A territory that can feed itself, in good times and in storms.
