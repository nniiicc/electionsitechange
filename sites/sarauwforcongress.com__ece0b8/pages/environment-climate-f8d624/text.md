We Protect Them or We Lose Them.
The environment of the Virgin Islands is not just scenery, it is our heritage, our economy, and our future.
Our coral reefs, our fisheries, and our marine economy sustain countless families.
But climate change, pollution, and rising seas threaten all of it.
Janelle has a proven record here, having banned reef-harming sunscreens and single-use plastics as a Senator.
She will keep fighting to protect what makes the Virgin Islands home.
Janelle's Federal Strategy Protecting Our Environment and Marine Economy Secure federal funding to protect our coral reefs and coastal waters.
Strengthen and grow our marine and blue economy.
Invest in real climate resilience for our communities and coastlines.
Reduce harmful pollution and single-use plastics in our waters.
Safeguard our natural resources for future generations.
The Goal A protected, thriving environment that sustains our people and economy.
