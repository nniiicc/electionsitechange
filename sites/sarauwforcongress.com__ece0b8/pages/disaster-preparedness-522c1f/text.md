We Should Never Be Caught Unprepared Again.
Living in the Virgin Islands means living with the threat of hurricanes, and we know the painful cost of being unprepared.
After the storms of recent years, recovery was too slow and accountability too weak, despite billions in federal funding.
Janelle will fight to strengthen our disaster preparedness and the resilience of our health and infrastructure systems, so our territory is ready before the storm and recovers faster after, never again sidelined in its own recovery.
Janelle's Federal Strategy Ready Before the Storm, Stronger After Strengthen disaster preparedness systems across every island.
Build resilient health infrastructure that stays standing through any storm.
Fight to speed up federal disaster-recovery funding when we need it most.
Demand accountability and transparency for every recovery dollar.
Protect critical infrastructure for faster, fairer recovery.
The Goal A territory that is ready before the storm and recovers stronger after.
