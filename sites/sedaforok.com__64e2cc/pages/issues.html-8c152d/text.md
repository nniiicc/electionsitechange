Fiscal Responsibility
Oklahoma’s budget has exploded over the years, and it’s time to rein in the wasteful spending that’s draining our resources.
I believe in fiscal discipline and efficiency.
I’ll fight to eliminate wasteful spending, cut unnecessary programs, and ensure your tax dollars are being used responsibly.
We need a budget that works for the people, not the special interests.
Cost of Living
Utility bills are out of control, grocery prices are skyrocketing, and insurance premiums are more than families can handle.
Oklahoma families should not be forced to choose between paying bills and feeding their children.
It’s time for real leadership to address the rising cost of living and provide relief for hardworking Oklahomans.
Property Tax & Senior Support
Many of Oklahoma's seniors are being pushed out of their homes because property taxes continue to climb.
The state must provide more protection for senior citizens, many of whom live on fixed incomes.
I will fight for a senior property tax freeze and work to ensure that seniors can remain in the homes they've built their lives in.
Insurance Reform
Oklahomans are seeing their insurance premiums soar with no end in sight.
Whether it’s health insurance, auto insurance, or homeowner’s insurance, premiums are rising while coverage is shrinking.
I’ll work for reforms that protect Oklahomans from price gouging and make insurance more affordable for families.
Energy & Consumer Protection
Energy prices continue to rise, taking a toll on every household.
Oklahomans deserve affordable energy solutions.
I will work to reduce energy costs and protect consumers from unfair pricing practices that benefit large corporations but harm everyday citizens.
Protecting the Family
Family is the cornerstone of our society.
As a father, I understand the importance of strong families and the need to protect the values that make our communities thrive.
I will work to strengthen family-friendly policies that allow families to thrive without overbearing government interference.
Whether it’s promoting a strong education system, supporting pro-life policies, or protecting parental rights, I will always fight to defend the family unit.
Defending Life
I am proudly pro-life and committed to protecting the unborn.
Every life is precious, and I will fight to ensure that the rights of the unborn are upheld in Oklahoma.
I will advocate for policies that protect life from conception to natural death, and I’ll stand up against any attempts to undermine those rights.
Education & Financial Literacy
Our children need the tools to succeed in the real world.
Financial literacy should be taught in schools so our kids can grow up understanding how to manage money, save, and make informed financial decisions.
I will advocate for integrating financial education into the curriculum and making sure our schools are safe and effective learning environments.
Second Amendment Rights
I believe in the right to bear arms, as guaranteed by the Second Amendment.
Oklahomans should be able to protect themselves, their families, and their property.
I will always stand up for your Second Amendment rights and work to ensure that our laws do not infringe on your ability to lawfully own and use firearms.
Limited Government
Government should serve the people, not control them.
I believe in a government that is smaller, more efficient, and focused on the issues that matter most to Oklahomans.
We need less regulation, less interference, and more freedom for individuals and businesses to succeed.
It’s time to stop burdening Oklahomans with bureaucratic red tape and allow them to live their lives freely.
Personal Responsibility & Accountability
Oklahomans should have the freedom to make their own choices, but with that freedom comes personal responsibility.
We must promote accountability in all areas of life, whether it’s in our schools, our communities, or our government.
I believe in empowering individuals to take responsibility for their decisions and helping our communities thrive through hard work, integrity, and perseverance.
Strong National Defense
While Oklahoma’s state legislature has its hands full addressing state-specific issues, we cannot overlook the importance of national security and the role Oklahoma’s economy and military bases play in our national economy.
A strong defense is essential for preserving our freedoms and ensuring that America remains safe from foreign threats.
I stand firmly with our military and will support efforts to strengthen our national defense.
