☰ Home Meet Roberto Issues Donate Support Facebook Find My Polling Location Meet Roberto Seda Roberto Seda is a husband, father, attorney, and longtime resident of District 91.
Having lived here for over a decade, he understands the challenges families face because he’s faced them too.
His commitment to integrity, accountability, and practical leadership drives his campaign to serve as a strong voice for the community at the State Capitol.
A Journey Rooted in Oklahoma Originally from Puerto Rico and raised in Lawton, Roberto chose to build his life in District 91 more than ten years ago.
He holds a Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Oklahoma State University, a Master’s degree from the University of Central Oklahoma, and a Juris Doctorate from the University of Oklahoma College of Law.
Over the years, Roberto has established a professional career grounded in service and trust.
From providing legal counsel to mentoring young professionals and supporting local nonprofits, he has continually sought ways to strengthen the community he calls home.
Faith and Family: The Foundation of Leadership Roberto’s approach to leadership flows directly from his faith and the values he shares with his family.
As a deacon at Central Church of Christ in Moore, he strives to live with humility, dedication, and honesty in every part of his life.
To Roberto, faith means putting love into action and standing with conviction for what is just.
He and his wife Becky, who owns Seda Real Estate Group, are raising three sons.
Their oldest, Alex, is studying Computer Science at Oklahoma State University.
Landon, their middle son, is pursuing Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at OSU as well.
Their youngest, Cole, is a lively eighth grader.
The family enjoys traveling and values the time they spend together, seeing it as a meaningful way to connect, recharge, and strengthen their bond amid busy lives.
The Path That Shaped Roberto’s Perspective Roberto L.
Seda didn’t follow the typical path to law.
Before becoming an attorney, he spent years managing manufacturing and operations at General Motors and Spirit AeroSystems.
That hands-on experience gave him a clear view of the challenges working families face — and shaped the practical, disciplined approach he brings to his legal work today.
As the founder of Seda Law Firm PLLC, Roberto focuses on probate and estate planning, guiding clients with clarity and respect.
His background in engineering, business, and law reflects a career built on real-world problem solving and service.
