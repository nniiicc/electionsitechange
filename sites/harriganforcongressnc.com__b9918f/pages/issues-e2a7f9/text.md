Pat on The Issues The Issues If you have questions that are not listed here, send them to us via email.
Email Us Economy The extreme base of the Democratic party and the policies of the Biden Administration wrecked an economy that was roaring in years past.
Their runaway spending, out-of-control inflation, crippling grocery prices, and high taxes are ruining the lives of hardworking Americans.
Now, NC-10 has a leader who knows what it’s like to sign the front of a paycheck; a business owner who employs over 50 people and knows how to stick to a budget.
Pat is fighting in Washington to curb inflation, cut wasteful spending, decrease taxes, and lower energy prices.
Energy America’s energy problem is an economic crisis and a matter of national security.
We must act now to become energy independent.
We can no longer afford to buy oil and other resources from our enemies.
We need an all-of-the-above approach to energy that aggressively puts America on the fast-track to independence.
That includes domestic oil exploration, pipeline construction, fracking, nuclear energy development, solar, wind, coal, and all other forms of energy that America has proven we can produce cleanly, safely, efficiently and effectively.
National Security President Biden’s Afghanistan disaster was criminally incompetent.
Here is the simple truth: When America fails to lead the world burns.
The weakness that America has displayed not only creates opportunities for international conflict, but it presents serious national security threats here at home.
As a Green Beret combat Veteran, Pat knows firsthand the dangers that we face inside and outside of our borders.
We must reinvest in our military, and give our soldiers the tools and support they need to do their jobs.
And we must reestablish America as the world’s leading superpower.
Immigration Under President Joe Biden, we didn’t have a border.
Without a border, we don’t have a country.
Open borders are a direct threat to our national security.
From gang violence to sex trafficking and drug smuggling to terrorism, the open border policy of the Biden Administration hurt all of us.
Now that President Trump has been reelected to the White House, we’re making the border secure and restoring safety across the country.
We owe it to our Border Patrol agents to provide them with every necessary resource.
Only then can they defend themselves, truly secure our frontlines, and ensure our national security.
Pat is working hand in hand with the Trump Administration to secure our border and enforce Federal law.
2nd Amendment Firearms are a valuable part of our identity as Americans.
From firearms, we derive our ability to hunt, defend ourselves and our families, and secure our freedoms.
As a Special Forces combat Veteran and owner of one of the fastest-growing manufacturers of firearms and firearm components in the country, Pat is keenly aware of the importance of the 2nd Amendment and what it means to us as Americans.
He will always fight to protect our right to keep and bear arms, believes in the fundamental right of Constitutional carry, and rejects any attempt to impose red flags on those exercising their Second Amendment rights.
Election Integrity When we vote as residents of North Carolina, we should have to show identification and confirm our identities in a meaningful way.
As the right to vote is one of the most sacred foundational responsibilities of our citizenry, we must endeavor that the result be secure and trusted—or we will have lost our country.
We must pass election laws that make it easier to vote, and harder to cheat, and that push power down to the local and state levels.
Cultural Integrity Pat sees conservative values as the bedrock of our nation.
To uphold these ideals, he fights to protect the unborn, stands firm with parental rights, defends women’s sports, and safeguards religious freedom.
Crime The first and primary mission of the US government is to keep our citizens safe and secure.
Joe Biden and the radical left abandoned that noble calling in favor of caving to the woke mob that supports them.
As a Green Beret, Pat is trained to keep our people safe, and that’s what we should be doing now.
He’s working alongside President Trump to crack down on violent crime, get drug traffickers and gangs off the streets, enforce immigration laws, and put fear back in the minds of criminals.
Pat is standing up to politicians who want to bring casinos and gambling to our neighborhoods, which will create more crime than jobs.
