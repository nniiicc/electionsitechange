Across Texas, families are feeling the pinch of skyrocketing home and auto insurance premiums.
From hailstorms to hurricanes, insurers are responding to rising risks with higher rates—but the burden is falling too heavily on everyday Texans.
As your next State Representative, I will fight to bring common-sense solutions that lower costs while protecting what matters most.
My Plan to Reduce Insurance Costs in Texas:
- Boost Market Competition: I will support legislation that attracts more insurers to Texas by cutting red tape and making it easier for reputable companies to offer affordable policies.
More competition means better rates for consumers.
- Invest in Risk Reduction: I’ll champion state-backed incentives for homeowners to make their properties more disaster-resistant—like upgrading roofs, reinforcing windows, and using fire-resistant materials—all of which can lead to lower premiums.
- Empower Consumers with Transparency: Texans deserve to know why their rates are going up.
I’ll push for greater transparency from insurance companies and support tools that help consumers compare prices, understand coverage options, and find better deals.
- Strengthen State Risk Pools: For families in high-risk areas, I will work to improve the Texas Windstorm Insurance Association (TWIA) and other risk pools, ensuring affordable, reliable coverage is available when the private market falls short.
- Crack Down on Fraud and Abuse: Fraudulent claims and lawsuit abuse drive up costs for everyone.
I’ll support smart reforms that curb these abuses without limiting the rights of legitimate policyholders.
- Support Smart Use of Technology I’ll encourage insurers to use better data and risk models so pricing is fairer and based on real factors—not blanket rate hikes.
Texans deserve fair, affordable insurance—not surprise hikes and shrinking coverage.
I’m running to make sure the system works for you, not against you.
Together, we can bring real relief to Texas homeowners and drivers.

Safe Communities Through Smart Policy

To achieve that, I will support:
- Strategic investment in local law enforcement: Ensuring our police and first responders are well-trained, well-equipped, and held to the highest standards of accountability.
- Community-based crime prevention: Supporting programs that build trust between law enforcement and the neighborhoods they serve.
- Addressing root causes of crime: Including mental health services, drug treatment programs, and youth outreach initiatives that steer people away from violence and toward opportunity.

Protecting Texas Students and Schools
School safety must be non-negotiable.

- Enhance campus security through funding for school resource officers (SROs), threat detection technologies, and infrastructure upgrades where needed.

- Encourage local partnerships between schools, law enforcement, and community organizations to identify risks early and act preventively.
