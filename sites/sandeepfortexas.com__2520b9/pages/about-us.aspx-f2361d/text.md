Sandeep Srivastava, a longtime Plano/Frisco resident, business owner, and community advocate, is officially announcing his candidacy for Texas House District 66 as a Democrat.
He will challenge the five-term Republican incumbent in the November 2026 election.
Srivastava is running to bring bold, fresh leadership to the Texas Legislature—leadership that reflects the diversity, values, and priorities of District 66.
A proud Texan and first-generation immigrant, he is committed to fighting for quality public education, accessible health care, reproductive rights, and real solutions to lower property taxes while investing in infrastructure and community safety.
“For too long, our district has lacked a representative who listens and acts in the best interest of our families and future,” said Srivastava.
“I’m running to give a voice to every resident of District 66 regardless of party who believes in progress, fairness, and opportunity for all.” With a strong background in business and civic engagement, Srivastava plans to build a broad coalition of supporters across party lines, focused on pragmatic solutions and real change.
As a parent, husband, and small business leader, he understands the challenges families in Collin County face every day and is ready to be their voice in Austin.
This campaign isn’t about him.
It’s about the community—the neighbors, the kids, and the future of District 66.
