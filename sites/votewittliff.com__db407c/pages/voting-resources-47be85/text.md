VOTING RESOURCES
Exercising your right to vote is the one of the most patriotic actions you can take.
Make a plan to vote today.
Remember:
- Early Voting runs October 19th-30th
- Election Day is Tuesday, November 3rd
- Bring your ID to vote
- Research the candidates and races on your ballot and bring your notes to the polls.
Harris County has over 90 races on the ballot and you cannot use your phone at the voting booth.
