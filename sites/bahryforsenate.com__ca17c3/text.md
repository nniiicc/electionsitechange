Platform for Rhode Island As an Independent candidate for U.S.
Senate, Michael Bahry is committed to a platform rooted in peace, term limits, religious liberty, the dignity of life, and economic justice for all Rhode Islanders.
Peace Through Faith, Mercy and Justice Rhode Island was founded on religious liberty.
Faith is something most humans have in some way or another.
Michael believes that our different beliefs should be talked about in honest respectful ways by everyone, including politicians.
He also believes that open discussion about different faith issues can and should contribute to how policy is formed and how budgets are formulated.
The goal should always be to make our state, our nation and our world more merciful and just.
Peace & Ending Endless Wars Michael believes America's participation in the endless wars are not making the world safer, they are making it more dangerous.
Looming global war is an existential threat to Rhode Island, our nation and the other eight billion people we share the planet with.
If elected, Michael will make ending war and international conflicts his focus.
He believes that there is nothing more important than protecting the innocent and doing our part in healing our broken world.
Peace in Politics - An Independent Man for Rhode Island True bipartisanship and flexibility: Without party loyalty, an independent can vote issue-by-issue based purely on what is right for Rhode Island, our nation and for the world.
An Independent can help dial down the mean-spirited dialogue and bring democrats and republicans together to effectively solve problems.
Peace and Term Limits Counters massive incumbency advantages: With reelection rates often above 90-95% despite Congress's abysmal approval ratings, term limits break the cycle of financial dominance, name recognition, and office perks that make it nearly impossible for challengers to win leading to more competitive, responsive elections.
Reduce the influence of special interest groups: Long-serving members of congress become beholden to lobbyists, PACs, and donors for reelection funds.
This is particularly true when it comes to military spending and the sale of bombs and other military equipment.
Will produce a more bipartisan congress: Members won't be concerned with re-election, and they can focus on what is good for the state, the nation and the world.
Brings fresh ideas and prevents stagnation: Career politicians often grow out of touch, prioritize seniority systems, and resist change.
Regular turnover injects real-world experience, new perspectives, and accountability, keeping Congress closer to the people rather than a self-perpetuating elite.
Incumbency Is an Unfair Advantage Political Capital: Career politicians fortify their power by responding to the requests of voters with their taxpayer financed staff.
This builds loyalty to incumbents because of gratitude rather than on the issues.
Fundraising dominance: Incumbents raise vastly more money from donors and PACs, funding superior campaigns while challengers struggle financially.
Name recognition: Voters already know incumbents from years in office, media coverage, and constituent services, giving them an immediate edge.
Office perks & deterrence: Free resources (staff, mailings, projects), experience, and the ability to scare off strong challengers make reelection much easier.
Economic and Environmental Peace Economic Boom from Redirected Resources: Ending wars frees trillions for infrastructure, innovation, and education, driving growth, reducing poverty, and boosting global trade in a stable world.
Unified Environmental Action: Without conflict and rivalries, nations collaborate on climate solutions, reforestation, and clean energy, restoring ecosystems and securing a healthier planet.
Long-Term Stability & Prosperity: Peace allows societies to focus on sustainable development, resilient economies, and healing environmental damage for lasting human progress.
I am Pro-Women, Pro-Baby, Pro-Life I believe we are all made in the image and likeness of God.
Every human life is precious and has immeasurable value.
I am Catholic and I am pro-life.
I support St.
Gabriel's Call from the Diocese of Providence and Rhode Island Right to Life .
They help moms with diapers, baby clothes, free ultrasounds, and ongoing support.
I believe we must approach this issue with empathy and humility, recognizing the pain many women carry after abortion.
Protecting moms and babies.
Love of Neighbor and Immigration Jesus said, "I was a stranger and you welcomed me." Every person has inherent dignity, and must be treated accordingly.
We're spending billions on mass deportations while our legal immigration system stays broken.
Rhode Islanders want a compassionate solution - not the removal of good hardworking people.
We must secure our border to stop human and drug trafficking.
It's time to end the political fighting and fix our immigration system - with fairness for citizens and mercy for those seeking a better life.
NATO, Russia and Ukraine NATO was created in 1949.
For over 75 years, the United States has carried the primary burden of defending wealthy Europe.
Russia repeatedly warned that NATO's eastward expansion to Ukraine crossed a red line.
The West ignored those warnings, and Russia invaded.
This does not excuse Putin's aggression.
Europe finds a way to make peace with Russia.
As for the United States, I believe it's time to withdraw from NATO.
What's in a Name?
Renaming the U.S.
Department of Defense to the Department of War was a serious mistake.
The name itself glorifies war over defense and peace.
War and military spending are not sustainable job programs.
Over the last 25 years, countless lives have been lost and we have spent trillions.
We must focus on diplomacy-not war.
Peace will bring security.
Women and Girls Sports Every child should be treated with dignity and honesty.
Some support biological males competing in women's and girls' sports out of genuine empathy.
But clearly, biological males should not compete in women's or girls' sports.
Title IX was created to give girls a safe, fair and equal chance to excel - we must honor that purpose and keep women's sports for women.
Gender Confusion I believe that all humans are "fearfully and wonderfully made." Many people support medical transitioning out of genuine desire to reduce suffering.
But no one can change biological sex through drugs or surgery.
We must protect our children from irreversible decisions they are too young to fully understand; after all it takes years for young people's brains to fully develop.
True healing and transformation come from within, not through permanent medical procedures.
Young people struggling with gender confusion deserve honesty, real counseling, compassion, and time to grow into the bodies God gave them.
