Let’s Keep Them That Way” Restoring Wisconsin means restoring the future of Wisconsin’s family farms.
Green and Dane Counties are dairy country under pressure from rising costs, volatile markets, and generational transition challenges.
Jon will be a loud voice for farmers in Madison.
NEW MARKETS : Open domestic and export markets, including sustainable aviation fuel (SAF), a rapidly growing opportunity for Wisconsin dairy and grain operations.
FARM SUCCESSION : Land trusts, apprenticeships, and less red tape so the next generation can afford to take over.
A farm that can’t be passed on disappears.
CHEMICAL TRANSITION : Support farmers who choose sustainable practices with research and market incentives, not unfunded government mandates.
TRADE & REGULATION : Stabilize trade policy to stop tariff whiplash; cut state red tape that adds cost without adding value for family farms.
HEALTHCARE OPTIONS : Farmers tell Jon they need options.
An association/group healthcare plan might lower costs by as much as 30%.
Jon favors a state law that makes this possible, plus support to maintain rural roads and find new markets.
NO FOREIGN OWNERSHIP OF WISCONSIN FARMLAND : Wisconsin must prohibit foreign countries buying up our farmland.
American farms are critical to our national security.
Restore Wisconsin “The next generation of Dane and Green County farmers has to have a future on the land their families built.
