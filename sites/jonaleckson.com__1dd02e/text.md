Watch on YouTube Meet Jon Who Is Jon Aleckson?
Jon is a husband, father, and grandfather who has called Wisconsin home his entire life.
He and his wife Mary live on 17 acres in the rural Town of Springdale near Mount Horeb, where they have been active members of St.
Francis Catholic Church for 37 years.
Jon also serves on the board of a Middleton free clinic, putting his faith and community into action every day.
A retired small business leader, Jon founded and led Web Courseworks , an educational software company, from 1992 to 2022.
He knows how to balance a budget, create jobs, and deliver results with fiscal responsibility, skills Wisconsin needs in Madison.
Jon formerly taught as an adjunct professor at UW–Platteville.
He is not a career politician: Jon pledges to serve only 2–4 years in the Assembly, solve real problems, and leave District 50 stronger for the next generation.
