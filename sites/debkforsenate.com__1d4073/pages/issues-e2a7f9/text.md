ISSUES
- Washington families are feeling the squeeze, from housing costs to groceries and gas.
I support practical solutions that ease the burden on working people: expanding affordable housing, protecting renters and first-time homebuyers, and making everyday necessities more affordable.
I’m not afraid to stand up to my own party when I believe in something.
I voted against an increase in the gas tax and unnecessary taxes on small businesses.
I proposed the first sales tax cut in Washington state in over 40 years because families deserve relief.
I’ll keep fighting to lower housing costs, hold corporations accountable for price hikes, and make sure every Washingtonian can afford to live and thrive in our state.
I lead the effort to expand the Property Tax Relief Program (SB6162), helping 50,000 additional households to qualify, providing needed assistance to seniors, people with disabilities, and disabled veterans to stay in their homes.
To combat rising energy bills, I voted to establish a low-income energy assistance program.
- Strong public schools are the backbone of our communities.
I’m committed to fully funding K–12 education, reversing declining test scores and chronic absenteeism, and ensuring every student is engaged, supported, and prepared for the future.
Washington can lead in early learning by expanding universal pre-K and childcare options, especially for working families.
I also believe in making college and career pathways more affordable with expanded access to apprenticeships, community college, and in-demand tech and trade programs that reflect our state’s economy.
Parents are essential partners in our schools.
I believe families should always have a voice in their children’s education, working hand in hand with teachers and school leaders to help every student thrive.
When parents, educators, and communities collaborate, students do better and schools grow stronger.
We must continue finding ways to help our schools get the funding they need to support students, like my legislation to lower the threshold needed for school districts to pass construction bonds.
Our crumbling schools must be rebuilt so that every student has a fair chance to succeed.
Investing in our education system should always include Pre-K programs.
That’s why I voted to establish the PreK Promise account, funding early education for 10,000 more children.
- I support full funding of our first responders, behavioral health services, and community-based violence prevention so that every Washingtonian feels safe at home and in their community.
I am proud to have voted to provide $100 million for new law enforcement officers and public safety programs across the state this past legislative session.
- Washington has led the way in expanding healthcare access, but too many still face high premiums, out-of-pocket costs, or gaps in coverage.
I support building on Washington's progress by capping prescription drug prices, protecting reproductive care, helping providers receive fair reimbursements, and ensuring rural communities and Tribal nations have strong healthcare infrastructure.
Last year, the federal government passed a massive cut to Medicaid that will have a devastating impact on medical systems across the state, and would also cut Planned Parenthood out of the Medicaid system entirely.
Our State government must do everything it can to lessen the impact of these awful cuts that will fall upon the lowest-income Washingtonians.
Every Washingtonian deserves affordable, quality care close to home.
That’s why I’m proud to have delivered $1,898,000 for local community health programs.
- As a lifelong Washingtonian, I know how much we value our environment.
With wildfires in all over Washington and rising sea levels on our coast, climate change is already here—and Washington must lead in finding solutions.
I support bold but balanced climate action: investing in clean energy jobs, and protecting forests, waters, and farmland.
We can grow our economy while reducing emissions and protecting the natural beauty that defines our state for future generations.
- Washington has long defended reproductive rights—and I’ll keep fighting to protect our rights.
I support maintaining strong legal protections for abortion, increasing access to reproductive and maternal care across rural and underserved areas, and ensuring birth control and preventive care remain affordable and accessible.
I was proud to sponsor and pass legislation to protect women’s medical care and reproductive rights in the emergency room (SB 5777).
In Washington, we trust women to make their own healthcare decisions—free from political interference.
