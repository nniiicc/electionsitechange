OUR APPROACH
- Taking Care of Our Environment
We all want to live in a place where the air is clean, the water is safe, and our kids can grow up healthy.
That starts with taking care of what we have.
Here’s what I believe we should do:
- Support local farmers who take care of the land the right way
- Hold companies accountable when they pollute our air and water
- Make sure businesses clean up the messes they create
- Invest in cleaner communities, more green spaces, cleaner air, and safer water
This isn’t just about the environment; it’s about our health, our families, and our future.
2.
Growing Our Local Economy
People here work hard.
They deserve real opportunities and fair pay.
Here’s how we make that happen:
- Support our local farmers and small businesses so money stays in our community
- Help farmers grow and get their products to local families
- Make sure workers are treated fairly and paid what they deserve
- Create more job opportunities through agriculture and local businesses
When we invest in our own people, we all move forward together.
3.
Improving Our Schools
Every child deserves a real chance to succeed, no matter where they come from.
Here’s what needs to change:
- Put more funding into our schools, teachers, and classrooms
- Support teachers so they can focus on teaching, not just getting by
- Teach real-life skills like agriculture, business, and job training
- Prepare students for good careers, not just tests
Because when our kids succeed, our entire community gets stronger.
