People deserve to know how their tax dollars are being spent and what decisions are being made in their communities.
Major growth proposals — like the rejected data center — should never proceed under a shroud of secrecy.
Also, an elected official should never sign a non-disclosure agreement.
NDAs are often used to keep the public in the dark until it's too late.
That is unacceptable!
Any potential project must be discussed openly, well in advance, so people can express their views and these views are taken into account in the decision making process.
View more Affordability EVERYTHING COSTS TOO MUCH.
Right now, many families in Meade County and Radcliff feel like they’re being squeezed.
Gas proces are through the roof.
Groceries cost more.
Utility bills cost more.
Just about everything costs more.
Government shouldn’t add to that pressure.
My goal is straightforward: help make Kentucky more affordable and protect the paychecks families work hard to earn.
View more Education SCHOOLS DESERVE OUR SUPPORT.
A strong community depends on strong schools.
Our district is fortunate to have excellent public schools, and we should work to maintain that quality while continuing to look for ways to improve.
That means backing our teachers, investing in classrooms, and making sure every child — no matter where they live — has the opportunity to learn and thrive.
We need to expand opportunities for all citizens to continue their education throughout their lives, to learn a new skill or trade.
View more Smart Growth NO INAPPROPRIATE DEVELOPMENT We need real opportunity - good jobs here at home, in enterprises we who live here agree belong here.
W e need healthy local businesses, and economic growth that fits in with our rural agricultural heritage.
We also need to continue building opportunities close to home.
Supporting local businesses and bringing the right kinds of jobs to our communities will help ensure economic growth benefits the people who already live here.
