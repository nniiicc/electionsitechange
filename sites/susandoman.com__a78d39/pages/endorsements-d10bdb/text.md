Skip navigation menu Home About Issues Volunteer Endorsements Contact Susan is Endorsed by Kentucky AFL-CIO Home About Issues Volunteer Endorsements Contact Susan is Endorsed by Kentucky AFL-CIO The Kentucky AFL-CIO Supports Susan For District 27 The Kentucky State AFL-CIO Committee on Political Education (COPE) is proud to endorse Susan Doman for Kentucky State Representative.
This endorsement reflects Susan Doman's strong commitment to Kentucky's working families and her support for the issues that matter most to working people.
The Kentucky State AFL-CIO represents more than 60 affiliated unions and over 131,000 union members across the Commonwealth.
The Kentucky State AFL-CIO believes Susan Doman will be a strong advocate in Frankfort for workers, families, and communities, and is pleased to support her candidacy for the Kentucky House of Representatives.
Endorsed by the Kentucky State AFL-CIO Committee on Political Education (COPE), June 18, 2026.
