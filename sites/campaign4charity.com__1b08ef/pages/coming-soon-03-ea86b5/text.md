Host a listening session or a fundraiser.
Our campaign is about building solutions together, and the best ideas come directly from the people who live and work in our communities.
That’s why we are inviting neighbors, friends, and community leaders to host a listening session or fundraiser in your favorite coffee shop, workplace, neighborhood association, faith space, or local gathering spot.
These sessions give residents the chance to share their concerns and hopes on issues like education, healthcare, transportation, sustainability, and economic opportunity.
Your voice and perspective are critical to shaping a platform that reflects the needs of Ohio House District 2. ​ By hosting a listening session, you can help bring together people you trust for an open, respectful conversation that ensures no voice goes unheard.
Our campaign team will provide support, materials, and guidance—you simply provide the space and the invitation.
Together, we can bridge divides, strengthen our neighborhoods, and ensure this campaign is built on the foundation of collective work and community-driven solutions.
