Melanie Stinnett Issues Leadership & Fiscal Responsibility Melanie believes in a government that lives within its means.
In the Missouri House, she supported the largest income tax cut in state history—$1 billion back into the pockets of hardworking Missourians.
She helped reduce unnecessary government spending by $1.5 billion, maintained over $1 billion in emergency reserves, and ensured that Missouri operates with zero state debt .
She knows that Missouri doesn’t have a revenue problem—it has a spending problem—and she’s committed to protecting taxpayers from a bloated government bureaucracy.
Healthcare & Disability Advocacy As a healthcare professional and small business owner, Melanie brings real-world experience to policymaking.
She sponsored HB 970 , reforming the Ticket to Work Health Assurance Program to give individuals with disabilities the opportunity to work, earn more, and build a life—without losing access to the healthcare services they need.
Melanie will continue to fight for market-based healthcare solutions, protect therapy access, and advocate for individuals with disabilities and underserved Missourians.
Education & Opportunity Melanie supports increased funding for public education and believes parents should have a stronger voice in their children’s learning.
She advocates for curriculum transparency, expanded school choice, and safe, supportive learning environments.
She’s also working to ensure schools have the counselors, social workers, and resource officers they need to support every student.
Public Safety & Law Enforcement Melanie proudly backs the blue and is the ONLY candidate endorsed by the Springfield Police Officers association.
She has supported increased funding for law enforcement, new training facilities, advanced public safety tools, and expanded resources for police and first responders.
She believes that safe communities start with well-equipped officers—and she’ll continue working to ensure Missouri’s law enforcement has what it needs to keep families safe.
Jobs, Workforce & Infrastructure Melanie helped secure major investments to reskill and upskill Missouri workers , expand job training programs, and fund career tech centers and apprenticeships.
She also supported funding to grow Missouri’s semiconductor and pharmaceutical industries and bring jobs back from overseas.
Additionally, she backed historic investments in infrastructure—expanding I-70 and I-44 , improving rural roads, and investing in ports, waterways, and high-speed broadband across Missouri.
Protecting Fairness in Women’s Sports Melanie believes in protecting fairness and opportunity in athletics.
She has supported legislation to ensure that female athletes compete on a level playing field in Missouri schools and colleges.
She will continue standing up for women and girls across the state.
