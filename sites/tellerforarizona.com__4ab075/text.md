Meet Ian
Ian Teller is a Diné leader from Tsaile, Arizona with a background in community advocacy and youth engagement.
He is a Democrat running for State Representative in Legislative District 6.
Ian plans to fight for strong schools, safe housing, an affordable Arizona and a sustainable future for Tribal and rural families.
