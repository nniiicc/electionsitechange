Here's Your Sign!
Here's your sign to get involved!
Join Team Truitt on Tuesday, September 1 from 6 to 8 pm at the Marion County Event and Sportsplex.
Swing by to grab your yard sign, and while you're there, let us know if you'd like to help out in other ways too.
Whether it's knocking on doors, making calls, or just spreading the word to neighbors, every bit helps.
We're building this together, one sign and one volunteer at a time.

Coffee with Carrie at Los Reyes Del Cafe
Grab a cup and pull up a chair!
Carrie is hitting the road for a series of Coffee with Carrie stops at coffee shops across the district.
It's a low key chance to meet her, share what's on your mind, and talk about what's happening in your community.
No agenda, no speeches, just neighbors talking with neighbors over coffee.
Everyone is welcome, so bring a friend and bring your questions.
