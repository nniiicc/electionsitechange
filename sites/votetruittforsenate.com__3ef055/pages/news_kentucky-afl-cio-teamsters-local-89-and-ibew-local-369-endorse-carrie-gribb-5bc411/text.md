Carrie Gribbins Truitt, candidate for Kentucky's 14th Senate District, announced today that she has earned the endorsement of the Kentucky AFL-CIO, Teamsters Local 89, and IBEW Local 369.
"Working people are the heartbeat of the 14th District, and they deserve a senator who listens to them and carries their voices to Frankfort," said Truitt.
"These endorsements mean a lot to me because they come from people who show up to work every day and simply want to provide for their families."
"Working people need leaders who listen to them and fight for them.
Carrie Gribbins Truitt understands that when workers succeed, Kentucky succeeds,” said Dustin Reinstedler, Kentucky AFL-CIO President.
“She is committed to supporting good jobs, strong communities, and policies that help working families thrive, and we're proud to stand with her in the 14th District."
"Our members move this state forward every single day, and they deserve a senator who has their back,” said Avral Thompson, President of Teamsters Local 89.
Carrie has shown that she understands what matters to working families and she's earned our support.”
"Carrie knows that the people who keep the lights on and the power running deserve a seat at the table in Frankfort,” said Eddie Devine, Business Manager, IBEW Local 369.
“She's committed to standing up for skilled trades workers and good jobs, and that's exactly the kind of leadership our members need in the state senate."
The Kentucky AFL-CIO represents more than 60 affiliated unions, over 131,000 members, and working families across the Commonwealth.
Founded in 1933, Teamsters Local 89 represents approximately 20,000 workers in Kentucky and Southern Indiana across dozens of industries such as trucking, shipping and logistics, warehousing, gaming, grocery and more.
A proud Marion County High School graduate, Truitt went on to earn degrees from the University of Kentucky and Campbellsville University before building a career devoted entirely to serving others through nonprofit work.
She chairs the Marion County Board of Education and is raising her three children with her husband Michael in the community she has always called home.
She is running for State Senate to fight for strong and safe schools, affordable housing and food security, and accessible healthcare for every family in the district.
Truitt will continue meeting with voters across the district in the coming weeks, including an upcoming candidate forum on July 11 hosted by the Loretto Motherhouse Peace Committee.
