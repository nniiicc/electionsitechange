Kentucky AFL-CIO, Teamsters Local 89, and IBEW Local 369 Endorse Carrie Gribbins Truitt for State Senate
CARRIE TRUITT EARNS ENDORSEMENT OF TEAMSTERS LOCAL 89
Carrie Gribbins Truitt Senate has earned the endorsement of Teamsters Local Union No. 89.
The men and women of Local 89 work hard every day to keep Kentucky moving, and this endorsement reflects a shared commitment to protecting working families and building an economy that lifts everyone up.
It is a responsibility I do not take lightly and will work each day to honor.
Teamsters Local 89 holds its endorsements to a high standard — backing only candidates who demonstrate integrity, character, and a proven record of supporting labor and working families.
In announcing their support, Teamsters Local 89 said: "You have proven over the years to be a hard-working individual with great integrity, character and intuition.
We know you will devote yourself to protecting the livelihoods and communities for our Teamster families and all working families in the state of Kentucky."
This endorsement reflects what this campaign is all about — fighting for the working families of central Kentucky, across party lines.
