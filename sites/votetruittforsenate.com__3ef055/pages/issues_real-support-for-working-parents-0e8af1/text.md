Meet Carrie Events Issues News Volunteer Contribute Real Support for Working Parents For too many families in our district, finding childcare isn't just expensive, it's nearly impossible.
Waitlists stretch for months, rural areas often have no licensed providers at all, and the cost of care can rival a mortgage payment.
That's not a personal budgeting problem, it's a childcare crisis, and it's holding back working families and our local economy alike.
Carrie believes no parent should have to choose between a paycheck and their child's care.
She will work to expand funding for childcare assistance programs so more families qualify for help, support incentives that make it easier for providers to open and stay open in rural areas, and invest in the workforce of early childhood educators who are too often underpaid for critical work.
This isn't just a family issue, it's an economic one.
When parents can't find reliable, affordable childcare, they can't fully participate in the workforce, and local businesses struggle to find and keep employees.
