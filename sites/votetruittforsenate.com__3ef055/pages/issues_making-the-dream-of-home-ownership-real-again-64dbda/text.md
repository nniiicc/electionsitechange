Making the Dream of Home Ownership Real Again
Families across our district are feeling overwhelmed when it comes to buying a home, especially first time buyers.
The average age of a first time homebuyer is now 40, and that number tells a heartbreaking story about how far out of reach homeownership has drifted for young families.
Here's the root of the problem.
The starter homes young families need simply aren't being built in enough numbers because the return on investment isn't there for builders.
Until that equation changes, families will keep getting squeezed out.
Carrie will work to change that equation.
That means increasing investment in the Affordable Housing Trust Fund administered by the Kentucky Housing Corporation, so there's real support behind building the homes our communities need.
It means supporting legislation that streamlines the permitting process, so builders aren't stuck in red tape instead of laying foundations.
And it means collaborating with local governments to make sure planning and zoning regulations actually support the construction of affordable housing, rather than standing in the way.
Every young family deserves a real shot at homeownership, not just a lifetime of renting while they wait for the numbers to work out in their favor.
Carrie will work alongside builders, local governments, and communities to make that shot possible again.
