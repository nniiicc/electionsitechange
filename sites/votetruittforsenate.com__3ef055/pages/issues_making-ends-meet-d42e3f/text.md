Making Ends Meet: Fighting Food Insecurity and the Rising Cost of Living
No family should have to choose between paying rent and putting food on the table, but that's the reality for too many households across our district right now.
As the cost of groceries, utilities, and everyday essentials keeps climbing, food insecurity isn't just a problem for the few, it's a growing strain on working families trying to stretch every paycheck.
Carrie believes affordability has to be part of the conversation whenever we talk about hunger.
It's not enough to treat the symptom without addressing the cost of living that's driving families to food banks and assistance programs in the first place.
That means supporting local food banks and pantries so they have the resources to meet rising demand, strengthening access to programs like SNAP and school meal programs so kids and families don't fall through the cracks, and working to bring down the everyday costs, from groceries to utilities, that are squeezing household budgets across the district.
Carrie will also work alongside local farmers and food producers to strengthen the connection between our own agricultural community and the families who need access to fresh, affordable food, keeping resources and dollars local instead of routing around our own communities.
No one should go hungry in a district as hardworking and resourceful as ours.
Carrie will fight to make sure help is there for families who need it, and work to bring down the costs that are making it harder to get by in the first place.
