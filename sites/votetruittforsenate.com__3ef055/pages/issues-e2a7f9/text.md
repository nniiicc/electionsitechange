Meet Carrie Events Issues News Volunteer Contribute Carrie on the Issues Too often, politics gets in the way of progress.
That's why this campaign is built around the issues that working families in central Kentucky actually face — the cost of living, good jobs, quality schools, and access to healthcare.
Carrie believes real solutions happen when we set aside party politics and work together toward common ground.
These aren't Republican issues or Democrat issues.
They're Kentucky issues.
Explore where Carrie stands and why these fights are personal.
Trusting Educators, Investing in Results Families want to know their children are being prepared for success, not just trained to pass a test.
I believe we place too much emphasis at the state level on standardized testing and not enough on science-based instruction and individualized learning.
Kentucky’s dramatic rise in math and literacy recovery rankings is proof that when we trust educators, invest in the right resources, and give students the time they need to succeed, results follow.
I will work alongside teachers and administrators to build standards that actually serve our children.
Read More Making the Dream of Home Ownership Real Again Families are feeling overwhelmed, especially first-time buyers.
The average age of a first-time homebuyer is now 40, and that number tells a heartbreaking story.
The starter homes young families need simply aren’t being built in sufficient numbers because the return on investment isn’t there for builders.
I will work to change that equation by increasing investment in the Affordable Housing Trust Fund administered by the Kentucky Housing Corporation, support legislation that streamlines the permitting process, and collaborate with local governments to ensure that planning and zoning regulations support the construction of affordable housing.
Read More Putting Patients Before Paperwork Our healthcare system has become driven by insurance companies rather than healthcare professionals.
I will work to strengthen insurance regulations, push for pricing transparency, and expand access to community health centers and rural clinics because primary care is always better than emergent care, for families and for the budget.
Read More On the Side of Labor Carrie knows the folks who haul our freight, wire our homes and businesses, and build the vehicles we drive deserve a voice in Frankfort that listens.
From wages that haven't kept up with the cost of living to attacks on collective bargaining and rising healthcare costs, working families are facing real pressure right now.
Carrie is committed to protecting the right to organize, supporting fair wages, and enforcing workplace safety, backed by the support of the Kentucky AFL-CIO, Teamsters Local 89, Teamsters Local 783, IBEW 369, and UAW.
Read More Making Ends Meet: Fighting Food Insecurity and the Rising Cost of Living No family should have to choose between rent and groceries, but rising costs are pushing more households across the district toward that choice.
Carrie will support local food banks and pantries, strengthen access to SNAP and school meal programs, and work to bring down the cost of everyday essentials so families aren't forced to rely on assistance in the first place.
She'll also partner with local farmers to keep fresh, affordable food close to home.
Read More Supporting Kentucky's Farming Families Family farms have shaped our communities for generations, and Carrie will fight to keep them viable with support for land succession, agricultural loans, and rural infrastructure like broadband and roads.
She'll work to strengthen local food systems and bring good jobs to Main Street, so rural communities aren't left behind in Frankfort's economic plans.
Read More Real Support for Working Parents Finding affordable childcare is nearly impossible for too many families in our district, with month-long waitlists and rural areas often lacking licensed providers altogether.
Carrie will work to expand childcare assistance funding, support providers in rural areas, and invest in the early childhood workforce, because no parent should have to choose between a paycheck and their child's care.
