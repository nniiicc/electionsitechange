Trusting Educators, Investing in Results
Families want to know their children are being prepared for success, not just trained to pass a test.
Carrie believes we've placed too much emphasis at the state level on standardized testing and not enough on trusting the educators who know our kids best.
Kentucky doesn't have to look far for proof that this approach works.
Our state ranks among the best in the nation for academic recovery since the pandemic, 8th out of 38 states in math growth and 5th out of 35 in reading, and districts right here in the 14th District, including Marion County where Carrie serves as board chair, are leading the way.
That progress didn't happen by accident.
It happened because teachers were trusted, districts were given the resources they needed, and students were given the time to succeed.
Carrie will fight for that same approach at the state level: trusting educators as the experts they are, making sure every district, not just the well funded ones, has the resources to help kids succeed, and building standards that serve our children instead of just measuring them.
Our schools are already showing us what works.
Carrie will make sure Frankfort follows their lead.
