Raised on a Marion County farm, I learned the values of hard work and community early in life.
A proud Marion County High School graduate, I went on to earn degrees from the University of Kentucky and Campbellsville University, before building a career devoted entirely to serving others through nonprofit work.
I chair the Marion County Board of Education and am raising my three children with my husband Michael in the community I have always called home.
Now, I am running for State Senate to fight for strong and safe schools, affordable housing and food security, and accessible healthcare for every family in the district.
Every dollar you give helps us reach more voters, share our stories, and fight for all our families!
