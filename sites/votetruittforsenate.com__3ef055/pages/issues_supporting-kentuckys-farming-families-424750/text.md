Supporting Kentucky's Farming Families
Agriculture isn't just an industry in our district, it's a way of life.
Family farms have shaped our communities for generations, and Carrie knows that keeping them viable is essential to keeping our small towns alive.
But farmers today are up against rising input costs, unpredictable markets, and a shrinking pipeline of young people entering the profession.
Carrie will work to support Kentucky's family farms with policies that make it easier to pass land down to the next generation, expand access to agricultural loans and grants, and invest in local infrastructure like roads, broadband, and water systems that rural communities depend on.
She'll also support efforts to grow local food systems and connect farmers directly with schools, hospitals, and grocers here at home, keeping more dollars circulating in our own communities instead of flowing out of the district.
Rural economic development isn't just about farming either.
It means bringing good jobs to Main Street, supporting small businesses trying to get off the ground, and making sure our rural communities aren't an afterthought in Frankfort's economic plans.
Carrie will fight for a Kentucky where you don't have to leave home to build a good life.
