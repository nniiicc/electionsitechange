On the Side of Labor
Carrie grew up watching hardworking people put in long hours to provide for their families, and she knows firsthand that the folks who haul our freight, wire our homes and businesses, build the vehicles we drive, and keep our communities running deserve a voice in Frankfort that actually listens to them.
Union members and working families across Kentucky are dealing with real challenges right now.
Wages haven't kept up with the cost of groceries, housing, and healthcare.
Too many workers are stretched between multiple jobs just to make ends meet.
Attacks on collective bargaining rights threaten the tools workers have used for generations to negotiate fair pay and safe conditions.
And rising healthcare costs hit union and non-union families alike, especially in a district where access to care is already stretched thin.
Carrie believes the answer starts with respecting the people who do the work.
That means protecting the right to organize and negotiate, supporting fair wages that keep pace with the cost of living, and making sure workplace safety protections are enforced, not just written on paper.
It also means working for policies that make healthcare more affordable so a medical bill doesn't undo a family's hard work.
That's why Carrie is proud to have earned the support of the Kentucky AFL-CIO, Teamsters Local 89, Teamsters Local 783, IBEW 369, and UAW.
These are organizations built by working people, for working people, and their support reflects Carrie's commitment to standing with labor, not just talking about it.
