Janie Lopez to Keep Protecting Texas Families AUSTIN, TX — The Texas Municipal Police Association (TMPA) has endorsed State Representative Janie Lopez for re-election, recognizing her strong commitment to supporting law enforcement officers and keeping Texas communities safe.
Throughout her time in the Texas House, Rep.
Lopez has stood with the men and women who serve on the front lines to protect their communities.
She has consistently advocated for policies that support law enforcement, strengthen public safety, and ensure officers have the resources and tools they need to do their jobs effectively.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Texas Municipal Police Association,” said Rep.
Janie Lopez.
“Our police officers put their lives on the line every day to protect our families, businesses, and communities.
I will always stand with those who stand on the front lines for us, and I look forward to continuing our work together to keep Texas safe.” TMPA represents thousands of municipal police officers across Texas and has been a leading voice for law enforcement and public safety issues at the Texas Capitol.
“Public safety is one of the most important responsibilities of the government, and I am proud to have the support of the men and women who protect our communities,” Rep.
Lopez added.
“As your State Representative, I will continue fighting for safer communities, strong law enforcement, and a Texas where families can live and work with confidence.” CAPITOL: P.O.
