JANIE LOPEZ TEXAS STATE REPRESENTATIVE FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE September 1, 2026 Contact: Parker Malone 512-463-0640 The National Federation of Independent Businesses Rallies Behind Rep.
Janie Lopez for Re-Election AUSTIN, TX — The National Federation of Independent Businesses (NFIB) has endorsed State Representative Janie Lopez for re-election, recognizing her strong record of standing up for Texas small businesses and supporting policies that encourage economic growth and opportunity.
As a member of the Texas House, Rep.
Lopez has worked to protect small businesses from unnecessary government burdens, promote a strong economy, and ensure that Texas remains the best place in the nation to start, grow, and operate a business.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of NFIB and the hardworking small business owners they represent across Texas,” said Rep.
Janie Lopez.
“As a small business owner myself, I understand firsthand the challenges that come with building a business, creating jobs, and keeping the doors open.
Small businesses are the backbone of our communities and our economy, and I will continue fighting for policies that give entrepreneurs the freedom to succeed without unnecessary government interference.” From family-owned businesses and local shops to independent contractors and entrepreneurs, small business owners face rising costs and increasing regulatory pressures.
Rep.
Lopez has made supporting a strong business climate and protecting Texans from unnecessary taxes and regulations a priority during her time in the Legislature.
“Texas has built its economic success by allowing people to work hard, take risks, and build something of their own,” said Rep.
Lopez.
“I am proud to stand with our small businesses and will continue working to keep Texas a place where entrepreneurs can turn an idea into a successful business and provide opportunities for the next generation.” CAPITOL: P.O.
