FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE NOVEMBER 12, 2025 FOR MORE INFORMATION: DISTRICT37.LOPEZ@HOUSE.TEXAS.GOV COMBINED LAW ENFORCEMENT AGENCIES OF TEXAS (CLEAT) ENDORSES STATE REP.
JANIE LOPEZ AUSTIN, Texas — The Combined Law Enforcement Agencies of Texas (CLEAT) has formally endorsed Janie Lopez for re-election to the Texas House of Representatives, citing her strong support for public safety and her consistent advocacy on behalf of Texas law enforcement officers.
CLEAT’s endorsement reflects confidence in Representative Lopez’s leadership and her record of working alongside law enforcement to address the real-world challenges officers face on the job.
During her time in the Legislature, Lopez has supported policies aimed at strengthening public safety, enhancing officer training, and reinforcing trust between law enforcement and the communities they serve.
“Representative Lopez has demonstrated a clear understanding of the needs of law enforcement professionals across Texas,” CLEAT leadership said.
“Her willingness to listen, collaborate, and take action has made her a reliable partner in advancing policies that protect both officers and the communities they serve.” Representative Lopez expressed gratitude for the endorsement and reaffirmed her commitment to supporting those who protect Texas communities.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of CLEAT,” Lopez said.
“Our law enforcement officers put their lives on the line every day to keep Texans safe.
They deserve leaders who will stand with them, ensure they have the resources they need, and treat them with the respect they have earned.” Throughout her tenure, Lopez has worked to advance legislation that promotes officer safety, expands access to training, improves mental health resources for first responders, and supports fair and effective criminal justice practices.
First elected in November 2022, Representative Janie Lopez represents House District 37.
She made history as the first Hispanic Republican woman elected from South Texas and the second statewide.
Lopez is a former teacher and school counselor and currently owns a small business providing mental health services to children, families, and veterans.
She resides in San Benito, Texas.
