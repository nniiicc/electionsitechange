JANIE LOPEZ TEXAS STATE REPRESENTATIVE FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE September 1, 2026 Contact: Parker Malone 512-463-0640 Texas REALTORS® Reaffirms Its Support for Rep.
Janie Lopez for Re-Election AUSTIN, TX — The Texas Real Estate Political Action Committee (TREPAC) has endorsed State Representative Janie Lopez for re-election, recognizing her commitment to protecting private property rights, supporting homeownership, and strengthening the Texas economy.
As Texas continues to experience rapid growth, Rep.
Lopez has worked to ensure that families and property owners are not left behind.
She has supported efforts to provide meaningful property tax relief, protect homeowners, and promote policies that make it easier for Texans to own and maintain a home.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of TREPAC and the Texas REALTORS® they represent,” said Rep.
Janie Lopez.
“For many Texans, owning a home is the foundation of financial security and the American Dream.
I will continue fighting to protect property owners, provide meaningful property tax relief, and make sure Texas remains a place where families can afford to put down roots.” Texas REALTORS® play a critical role in helping Texans buy, sell, and invest in property while contributing to the strength of local communities and the Texas economy.
Rep.
Lopez has made protecting private property rights and ensuring Texans can afford to live, work, and raise a family in their communities a priority.
