DONATE JANIE'S PRIORITIES Cut Taxes Create jobs, expand economic opportunities, and lower property taxes to provide much needed tax relief for our families and small businesses.
Keep Us Safe Reduce crime, invest in border security, support our local law enforcement and boost their pay to ensure that our families and communities are safe.
Improve Schools Boost Healthcare Increase funding, support pay raises for educators, improve classroom materials, and ensure access to higher education and trade schools for all students.
Expand access, lower premiums, provide pay raises for healthcare providers and cap prescription drug costs for those in need.
Support Seniors Enhance retirement benefits, increase support for senior services, and provide access to affordable housing to ensure seniors live with dignity and security.
Bolster Infrastructure Invest in modernizing roads, bridges, and public transportation systems, upgrade utilities, and water projects to build a more efficient and sustainable infrastructure for our communities.
Honor Veterans Protect Our Values Increase funding for veterans' services, ensure access to quality education and job training programs, and provide assistance to honor the sacrifices of our military veterans and their families.
Uphold the principles that make our community strong by defending individual freedoms, supporting family values, and promoting policies that protect and uphold our faith.
Fight Corruption Promote Tourism Increase penalties, enhance transparency in government, and enforce accountability to ensure that public trust is upheld and taxpayer resources are used effectively.
Modernize key tourist attractions and transportation systems.Foster a favorable environment for tourism growth by simplifying regulations and encouraging entrepreneurial ventures.
