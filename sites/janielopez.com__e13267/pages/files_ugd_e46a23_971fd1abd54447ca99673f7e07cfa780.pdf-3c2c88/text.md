TRUMP ENDORSES STATE REP.
JANIE LOPEZ FOR RE-ELECTION Washington, D.C. — President Donald J.
Trump has issued a formal endorsement of State Representative Janie Lopez, offering his complete and total support for her re-election and praising her record of conservative leadership and service to Texas.
President Trump commended Lopez for her character, work ethic, and commitment to the principles Texans value most, citing her professionalism and patriotism as defining qualities of her public service.
“In recognition of her professionalism, her patriotism, and her outstanding service to the great people of Texas, I am proud to give Janie Lopez my complete and total endorsement in her upcoming re-election,” President Trump said.
The endorsement reflects President Trump’s confidence in Lopez’s leadership and her ability to continue delivering results for Texans in the Legislature.
Texas Governor Greg Abbott, who has worked closely with Lopez during the legislative session, also announced his endorsement, pointing to her role in advancing some of the most conservative legislation passed this year.
Abbott noted Lopez’s success in delivering meaningful property tax relief, strengthening parental empowerment, and securing historic levels of education funding for her district.
Since taking office, Lopez has built a reputation for grit, faith-driven leadership, and a clear understanding of the challenges facing her district.
Her work in the Texas House has remained focused on supporting working families, rural communities, and the constituents she represents.
First elected in November 2022, Representative Janie Lopez represents House District 37.
She made history as the first Hispanic Republican woman elected from South Texas and the second statewide.
Lopez is a former teacher and school counselor and currently owns a small business providing mental health services to children, families, and veterans.
She resides in San Benito, Texas.
For more information about Representative Janie Lopez and her legislative priorities, visit www.janielopezfortexas.com.
