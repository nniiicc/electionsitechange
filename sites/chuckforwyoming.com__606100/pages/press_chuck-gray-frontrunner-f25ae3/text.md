Home About Issues Media Volunteer Contact Donate Back to Media Center For Immediate Release May 31, 2026 NEW POLL: CHUCK GRAY IS THE CLEAR FRONTRUNNER IN WYOMING GOP CONGRESSIONAL PRIMARY Contact: Clayton Henson | info@chuckforwyoming.com CHEYENNE, Wyo. – A new survey of likely Republican primary voters in Wyoming shows Secretary of State Chuck Gray firmly leading the GOP primary for Wyoming's at-large congressional seat – and his support surges dramatically once voters receive basic information about the candidates.
Conducted by the lead pollster for President Trump, Fabrizio, Lee & Associates, the poll finds Gray at 21% in the initial ballot test, ahead of Reid Rasner (14%), Steve Friess (10%), Jillian Balow (8%), Bo Biteman (7%), and Kevin Christensen (5%).
One-third of voters (33%) remain undecided.
After voters are informed about Gray, Rasner, and Friess, Gray's support more than doubles to 48% .
Rasner collapses to 3%, Friess falls to 6%, and the rest of the field remains in single digits.
Gray emerges as the undeniable frontrunner with a commanding lead and clear path to victory.
"Wyoming voters are ready for a proven conservative who fights for our values – and this poll shows they're supporting me to carry that fight for the people of Wyoming in the US Congress.
The more they learn, the more they support my campaign." — Chuck Gray "This race is about focusing on the truth — my track record of advancing the America First, conservative priorities of the people of Wyoming and my record of getting those priorities to the finish line.
I do not back down, even in the face of false attacks from the insiders and the radical Left.
That is what the people of Wyoming want, and that is why we are in a strong lead." — Chuck Gray The Fabrizio, Lee & Associates memo concludes: "Chuck Gray is currently best positioned to win this race.
He already holds the lead and the data shows that his lead will grow considerably once Gray's campaign informs voters about each of the candidates."
