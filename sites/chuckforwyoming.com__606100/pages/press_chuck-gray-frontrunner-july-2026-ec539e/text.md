House Seat CASPER, WY – July 6, 2026 – Wyoming Secretary of State Chuck Gray, a proven conservative leader and strong advocate for Wyoming values, has been named the clear frontrunner in the latest independent polling conducted by 1892 Polling for Protecting Wyoming Values PAC.
The poll shows Gray leading a competitive Republican primary field for Wyoming's at-large U.S.
House seat with strong support, positioning him as the top choice among likely Republican voters.
This momentum builds on Gray's proven track record of fighting for election integrity, energy independence, and fiscal responsibility.
"Wyoming voters are looking for a fighter who will stand up for our way of life in Washington.
This poll reflects the strong grassroots support we've built across the state—from energy communities to family ranches.
I'm honored by the confidence Wyoming Republicans are placing in me to carry our conservative values to Congress." — Chuck Gray As Secretary of State, Gray has been a national leader on election security, spearheading Wyoming's voter ID law and ensuring the integrity of the state's electoral process.
His campaign for Congress emphasizes key priorities including: ▸ Defending Wyoming's energy industry against federal overreach ▸ Lowering costs for working families ▸ Securing borders and restoring law and order ▸ Protecting Second Amendment rights and rural Wyoming values For more information about Chuck Gray's campaign for Congress and to get involved, visit ChuckForWyoming.com .
About Chuck Gray for Congress Chuck Gray is a lifelong Wyoming conservative, former State Representative, and current Secretary of State dedicated to putting Wyoming first.
He is running for Wyoming's lone U.S.
House seat to deliver results on energy, affordability, and election integrity.
