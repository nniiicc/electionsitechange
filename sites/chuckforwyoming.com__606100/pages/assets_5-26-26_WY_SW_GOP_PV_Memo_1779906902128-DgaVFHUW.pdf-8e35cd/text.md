CONFIDENTIAL MEMORANDUM TO: INTERESTED PARTIES FROM: TONY FABRIZIO, DAVID LEE & TRAVIS TUNIS RE: CHUCK GRAY IS BEST POSITIONED IN GOP PRIMARY FOR CONGRESS DATE: MAY 26, 2026 In a recently completed survey of Republican primary voters (RPV) in Wyoming (see methodology statement on last page), Fabrizio, Lee & Associates found that Chuck Gray leads in the Republican primary for Wyoming’s At-Large Congressional district.
Furthermore, when voters are given information about Gray and some of his opponents, Gray’s lead skyrockets and he is the clear frontrunner.
Chuck Gray Leads the GOP Primary for Congress, Driven by the Base of the Republican Party In the Republican primary for Congress, Chuck Gray leads with 21% of the vote.
He is up 7-points on Reid Rasner (14%), and even more on Steve Friess (10%), Jillian Balow (8%), Bo Biteman (7%), and Kevin Christensen (5%).
All other candidates are at 1% or less, while a third of RPV are still undecided.

METHODOLOGY STATEMENT: Fabrizio, Lee & Associates conducted a survey of 400 Republican voters in Wyoming who are likely to participate in the 2026 Republican primary, from May 1316, 2027.
The interviews were split 40% live-operator cell phone/15% live-operator landline/45% SMS to web.
Geography was matched to votes cast in previous GOP primaries in Wyoming.
Gender, age, race/ethnicity, and education were matched to demographic profiles of likely voters based on the voter file and past exit polls.
Respondents were randomly selected from lists of known previous GOP primary voters and newly registered Republican voters.
The margin of sampling error at the 95% confidence interval is ±4.9%.
