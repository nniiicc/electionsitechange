James Herriot Revisited
Many thanks to the Pennsylvania Farm Bureau, the Bedford County Farm Bureau, and the Blair County Farm Bureau for hosting their wonderful Legislative Farm Tour today at Legacy Livestock Veterinary Services in Bedford County.
When we think of our food supply chain, we tend to mostly think of farmers, but veterinarians play a very important role.
They’re the ones treating sick animals, making sure milk is withheld while an animal is medicated, or that meat is withheld during an outbreak.
The veterinarians are the first to diagnose a public health concern, file it with the FDA, and ensure that zoonotic diseases don’t transmit to other animals or to us.
Dr.
Jen Kauf and Dr.
Mary Wright presented extensive information about the scope and impact of practices like theirs.
I couldn’t help but recall passages of James Herriott’s “All Creatures Great and Small” series as they described driving to farms with their equipment in their truck to attend to large animals.
They even hosted a tour of their facility where we saw specimens in jars.
If they could expand their facility, they would add another veterinarian and technician.
Later, members of the Farm Bureau shared the summaries of bills they would like Congress to pass, like THE FARM BILL, which is still hanging out there.
I would pass it, mostly because it reduces costs to farmers for crop insurance, fuel blends, and interstate commerce.
I would also vote for G.T.
Thompson’s “Securing Agriculture’s Workforce Act,” SB225 “Eminent Domain Compensation,” and H.R. 9474 “Local Foods for Healthy Schools Act 2026”
I really appreciate everything I learned.
After that, I spoke with some farmers and made a date to visit a farm.
Together, we #ChooseDemocracy and good nutrition!
