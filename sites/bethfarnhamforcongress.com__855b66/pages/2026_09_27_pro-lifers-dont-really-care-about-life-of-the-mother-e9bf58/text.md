Ever since Roe v.

Since doctors wouldn’t risk their licensure and loss of freedom to 99 years in prison on the chance that legislators wouldn’t approve the abortion, they delayed care to those in need.
Maternal mortality rates leapt in Texas so much so, they decided to delay the reports.
And the mothers continued to die.
Josseli Barnica was one such mother.
Like 60% of those who seek abortion, she was actually already a mother, and this was a wanted pregnancy.
But the fetus had miscarried, opening her cervix, exposing her uterus to dangerous bacteria.
However, it still had a heartbeat and because of that, doctors would not perform the necessary operation to save her life.
No one could save the fetus, and three days later, no one could save her from the sepsis that followed.
Because a fetus had a heartbeat, a woman who wanted more children died and her daughter was left without a mother.
Now we learn that Texas will not release the reports on maternal mortality for 2022-2023 until after the midterms.
Why?
Because if American voters understood that conservatives don’t really care about mothers and children, then they would vote for Democrats – Democrats who actually support mothers and children with policies that strengthen social safety nets so that mothers and children receive the healthcare and nutrition they need not to simply exist, but to THRIVE.
You can read more about my position on Reproductive Freedom linked below.
And rest assured, I would not have voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act that our own US Representative, multimillionaire Joyce voted for – twice.
Already, over 11,000 SNAP recipients in our 13th Congressional District have lost their benefits since OBBBA SNAP changes went into effect.
Please vote for me, Beth Farnham, on November 3rd because I believe in women making the best choices for our own bodies, the freedom to create the families we choose, and the social safety nets that help mothers and children stay healthy.

I was further honored to have been officially endorsed by them.
Our Machinists fight for the rights of laborers across the country and I will be sure to carry that fight into the US House of Representatives.
In voting for the PRO Act, I’ll be sure that corporations will be required to give a seat at the table to unions formed within the prior 90 days of the meeting.
Corporations won’t be able to force their employees to watch “Right to Work” nonsense and no corporation will be able to deny union membership.
I’ll also advocate a national healthcare plan so workers won’t have to settle for jobs they hate to have their basic healthcare needs met.
President Biden once said, “The middle class built America and the unions built the middle class,” and he wasn’t wrong.
The good jobs have the wages and benefits they do because of the sacrifices of many union workers across the generations.
Working Families deserve the rights, wages, and working conditions that ensure work-life balance and dignity in retirement.
I can’t wait to represent them in Congress.
