No Term Limits Why don’t I believe in setting term limits for Members of Congress?
Because elections are term limits.
“But, Beth,” you say, “the same people keep getting voted in, even when they are not fit to serve.” I agree with that.
And the United States just went through a very weird time which I term, “Schrödinger’s Senator” when we didn’t know whether or not McConnell was alive or dead.
I say, “No term limits,” but I also say, “Members of Congress should be compelled by law to hold public, in-person town halls to which thousands of constituents attend and some ask questions live, on camera, to the legislator.
In other words, no more hiding from The People.
This way, we don’t judge someone for being older, but we do judge their competence and integrity.
And the voters will share anything of concern, determining the fitness of a legislator based on real observation.
No more Mitch McConnells.
No more Diane Feinsteins.
No more John Fettermans.
An informed voter is the best voter.
Please vote for me, Beth Farnham, on November 3rd so I can propose legislation like this to re-engage the voting public with the people who claim to represent us.

Legalizing Weed Someone tell Bill Clinton – I’ve never smoked marijuana, nor inhaled it.
But I have a FEDERAL policy position on it.
I approve of medical cannabis and would want it regulated like any Schedule III prescription.
I believe that recreational cannabis, like alcohol, should be regulated by both state and federal law.
Stateside – they can decide how cannabis is sold, distributed, imported, and possessed.
“Dry” zoning areas would be mooted because medical cannabis should not suffer such restrictions.
On the federal side – cannabis should have a minimum age of consumption of 21 years old, meet TTB standards of taxation and labeling, meet FDA standards of food safety and additives, meet FTC standards of marketing which does not target youth, and be supported by the ATF in targeting criminal enterprises that do not meet the above standards.
I would support the Secure and Fair Enforcement Banking Act of 2026 which protects financial institutions that serve state-legal cannabis businesses.
Please vote for me, Beth Farnham, on November 3rd, so I can enact legislation that defines, regulates, and protects medical and recreational marijuana use.
Together, we #ChooseDemocracy and freedom!
