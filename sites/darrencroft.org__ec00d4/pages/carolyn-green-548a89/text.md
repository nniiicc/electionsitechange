I have always known Darren to be honest, dependable, hard working, perceptive and diplomatic.
I was fortunate to work with him when he served as CIO, as a religious leader and in our community.
I have attended republican town halls where Darren shared his ideas for the future of our community and suggestions that would benefit all residents.
I have watched him organize and motivate people for different projects.
He doesn’t just ask others to work, but always works alongside them.
He uses wisdom in solving many kinds of issues, always with respect for others’ perspectives, taking time to understand their viewpoint.
At a time like this, we need principled people who can be reasonable in solving fiscal problems and that have the moral integrity to do the best job for us as a community and state.
I am very happy to endorse Darren Croft for the Utah House of Representatives.
