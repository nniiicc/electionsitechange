Key Primary Election Dates
| June 2 | Ballots begin arriving.
Check your mailbox. |
| Missing a ballot? | If you have not received your ballot by June 9, call county elections (385) 468-7400 |
| June 2 to June 16 | Safe to return your ballot by mail.
Drop your ballot in any mailbox or a county ballot drop box |
| June 17 to June 22 | Drop Box Only.
Do not mail your ballot after June 16; it risks arriving late and not being counted |
| June 23 Election Day | Final Deadline.
Drop boxes close at 8:00 PM.
In-person voting is open 7:00 AM – 8:00 PM. |

Smart Voting Tips
- Do your research and make your choice now, then fill out and return your ballot the day you receive it.
This prevents it from being lost or forgotten and ensures you have a say in choosing the winner!
- Note that since 2025, Utah mail-in ballots must be received by the county clerk by election day.
The postmark date is no longer considered.
- All in person voting requires identification as explained here.
- Who you vote for is always private, but whether you vote in each election is a public record.
Keep your civic participation record strong!
