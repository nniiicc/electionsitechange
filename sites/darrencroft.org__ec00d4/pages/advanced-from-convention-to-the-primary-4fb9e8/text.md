Darren Croft advanced out of the County Nominating Convention held April 18th at Cottonwood High School!
Three candidates were running and Delegates were equally split among the top two candidates.
A second vote was again almost equal as delegates chose to send the race to voters to decide the eventual nominee.
The primary election will be held June 23.
Ballots will start arriving after June 3.
In his convention speech, Darren outlined the messaging and outreach he will use to win the general election.
