Elizabeth Maier’s story starts the same place so many of ours do — with family. ​ Long before she became a candidate for State Representative, she was raised in a sixth-generation Cincinnati manufacturing business where the lessons came early and stuck for life: work hard, treat people well, and take responsibility.
It wasn’t about titles.
It was about doing the work and doing it right. ​ Today, Elizabeth brings that same heart to this campaign.
She’s a wife, a mom of four, a business leader, and a former school board member — someone who has spent years serving her community not because she had to, but because she genuinely cares. ​ Elizabeth is a proud Ohioan who believes families deserve safe communities, strong schools, lower costs, and leaders who not only listen but follow through.
A Personal Message from Elizabeth I’m Elizabeth Maier, and I’m running for Ohio State Representative because I believe we deserve more. ​ ​ More listening.
More respect.
More focus on getting things done in Columbus. ​ ​ Grounded in faith, family, and freedom, I’m running to bring conservative, commonsense leadership to Columbus.
I know what it takes to lead with integrity and deliver real results, and I’m ready to go to work for the people of District 27.
I believe in an Ohio where families come first, communities are safe, and every child has access to a strong, high-quality education focused on outcomes.
We need steady leadership that brings people together, focuses on what works, and follows through.
I believe in a representative government—one that reflects the people it serves.
That means listening carefully, understanding what matters in people’s daily lives, and carrying those priorities forward with clarity and conviction.
That’s the kind of leadership I’ve tried to practice in my own community.
And it’s the kind of leadership I’ll bring to Columbus.
