Meet Max
Max Deitchler (pronounced Dye-ch-ler) is a father, husband, lifelong Arkansan, attorney, and small business owner with a decade of experience fighting for fairer systems and stronger communities.
He served for over 11 years on the Washington County Election Commission, where he led efforts to expand early voting, establish a polling place on campus at the University of Arkansas, and make elections more accessible and secure.
We need to get more young people engaged in our democracy, now.
Max understands firsthand the importance of a high-quality education, the challenges working families face with childcare and housing, and the struggles of small business owners and entrepreneurs.
Max believes in strengthening public education, protecting Arkansas’s natural resources, and ensuring that every voice is heard at the ballot box.

On the Issues
Max will fight for working families, who have fallen further and further behind in our economy.
He’s committed to expanding access to Pre-K and affordable, quality childcare, will champion public education, and will work to address the housing crisis in Northwest Arkansas.
Max will fight to preserve and protect the Buffalo River and our public lands and waterways for future generations, while simultaneously expanding access and growing the outdoor recreation economy in Arkansas.
As a small business owner, Max supports additional state tools and resources to help Arkansans start and grow small businesses.
Max served on the Washington County Election Commission for more than 11 years and he is passionate about elections and voting.
