ABOUT NADINE
From her personal to professional background, get to know Representative Nakamura!
| Background Resident of Kapa'a, Hawai'i Married to Galen Nakamura, attorney-at-law (deceased) Mother of Casey (29), who is a clerk in the Federal District Court in Bostin; and Reis (24), second-year law student at the University of Hawai'i Richardson School of Law Member of All Saints' Episcopal Church, Kaua'i Member of Tenrikyo Honolulu Church, O'ahu | |
| Professional Experience 2016-Present: State House of Representatives, District 15 (was renamed from District 14 in 2022), and is currently the House Majority Leader.
Nadine has earned the distinction of being the first woman Speaker of the House since 2024.
Prior to being Speaker, she has served on numerous committees, including:
Chair, Housing Committee Member, Judiciary Committee Member, Transportation Committee Member, Economic Development Committee Member, Tourism Committee Member, Health & Human Services Member, Finance Commitee 2013-2016: Managing Director, County of Kaua'i (1,200 employees, 72,000 residents) 2010-2013: County Council Member & Vice Chair
Chair, Planning Committee Vice Chair, Finance & Economic Development 1992-2012: Principal, NKN Project Planning 1991-1992: Project Coordinator, Pacific Housing Assistance Corp. 1989-1991: Planner VI, Department of Land Utilization, City & County of Honolulu 1987-1989: Planner V, Department of Transportation, City & County of Honolulu 1985-1987: Development Coordinator, Neighborhood Reinvestment Corp 1984-1985: Planning, Staff Researcher, Hawaii Community Development Authority | Past Legislative Appointments: Act 279, DHHL Oversight Committee Hawaii Invasive Species Committee NCSL Health Task Force Hawai`i Interagency Council for Transit-Oriented Development Hawai`i Children’s Trust Fund Hawaii Broadband Assistance Advisory Council |
