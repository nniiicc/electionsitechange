Currently, 55% of the energy generated on Kaua`i is from solar, hydropower, and biomass.
90% of daytime energy demand is met on most sunny days on Kauaʻi. ​ I support measures that will help us reduce the use of fossil fuels and help mitigate the impacts of climate change on our community.
I strongly support innovative technologies, including energy storage solutions, that can help us advance our clean energy goals cost-effectively and equitably.
