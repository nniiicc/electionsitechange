| Dear Friends and Neighbors, Thank you for the honor of representing you at the Hawai‘i State Capitol.
I’m excited to run for re-election to continue working for a better Hawai'i.
In the next two years, I would like to continue to work to build more permanent supportive housing for our homeless, build more rental and for-sale housing for working families, find a dedicated source of revenues for the Department of Hawaiian Home Lands, encourage entrepreneurship and help create higher-paying jobs, use green fees to better steward our lands and waters, increase the number of public Pre-K classes, encourage regenerative, sustainable tourism, support locally-grown food, and help make health care more accessible.
By partnering with the Counties, private sector, nonprofit organizations, and communities, I believe we are making steady progress and would like to continue this critical work.
I humbly ask for your support in the upcoming primary election.
