Meet Simon
Simon Trautmann Cordova is a former Richfield City Councilmember and people’s attorney who has spent his life fighting for working families.
A lifelong resident of Senate District 51 of Minnesota, Simon has delivered real results — from expanding language access and recovering stolen wages to securing major infrastructure investments for Richfield.
