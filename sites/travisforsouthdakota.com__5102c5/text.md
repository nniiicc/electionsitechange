Home About Campaign Platform Volunteer / Contact Follow Donate Home About Campaign Platform Volunteer / Contact Hello, my name is Travis Ismay, and I am running for reelection to represent our community as your district 28B representative.
As a Christian man, my faith in the Lord guides my life, including my decision to enter the realm of public service.
I’m running to champion these things in Pierre: Eliminate Wasteful Spending & Bureacracy Protect our Property Rights Less Government, More Personal Responsibility Give Back Local Authority to the People Stricter Enforcement of Serious Crimes My journey into politics was not born out of ambition, but rather out of deep concern for the state of our local and state government.
Witnessing all the dysfunction firsthand, even at the local level, compelled me to step up and try to make a difference.
I am not a politician by trade; I am a rancher, welder and family man, driven by a commitment to uphold moral principles and fight against an ever-growing state government that seeks to increase regulations and encroach on our Constitutional rights.
I believe small local government, represented by principled citizens, is the most efficient government possible.
