Home About Campaign Platform Volunteer / Contact Follow Donate Home About Campaign Platform Volunteer / Contact Campaign Platform If elected to serve you from House Distict 28B, these are the items I will champion in Pierre.
Z Wasteful Spending & Bureacracy We need to lower taxes significantly in South Dakota by reducing government bureaucracy and waste.
I will route out the unnecessary and wasteful spending in Pierre, work with like-minded conservatives and eliminate it.
Z Protect our Property Rights Property rights in South Dakota are being attacked by not only government but private industry.
If elected I will champion bills to strengthen private and local control in our state and repeal bills that do not.
Z Right-Size Government Less government and more personal responsibility.
Z Law and Order We need to bring back strict enforcement of serious crimes, including mandatory sentencing, the death penalty for pedophilia and make jail punitive again.
If there is no deterrent, what is to stop people from breaking the law?
Z Reduce Unnecessary Regulations & Laws We don’t need new laws, we need to enforce the ones that are on the books and eliminate the ones that are unnecessary.
Z Restore Honor in Public Service Bring back the fear of the Lord to government: truth, honor, and integrity.
Z Validity in Legislation Work to build coalitions of legislators that want to do what’s right and research bills on validity, not popularity.
Z Reduce Tax liability across the board In large portions of my district, a complete elimination of property taxes would prevent county government from providing essential services to the public.
However, it is my goal to work to find as much tax relief as possible across the board.
I am committed to finding ways to reduce the size of government and reduce waste in government as much as possible, which would result in lower tax liability for citizens.
And for my critics, I had hoped in my first term that a complete elimination of property taxes would be possible, but as I have worked diligently to understand the tax code in South Dakota and how government is funded, I see that is likely not possible in my district.
Therefore, I remain committed to finding any way to reduce tax liability that preserves essential services and does not unduly burden my constituents.
