INVEST IN PUBLIC EDUCATION
Camille believes one of the most consequential issues she can address in Concord is funding public education.
As your State Rep she will fight to make sure we invest in the future of this state and ensure the State pays its fair share.
She also believes it is critical to limit the diversion of public funds to private education accounts.
SUPPORT FAMILIES THROUGH HOUSING + CHILDCARE
As a mom of two children, Camille knows what it is like to raise a family here.
She also knows our children cannot afford to stay in New Hampshire.
That’s why promoting the development of diverse housing options and small single-family homes will be front and center for her at the State House.
Combining that with the expansion of affordable child care will allow young people to get established in our communities.
PRIORITIZE TRANSPORTATION FOR ALL
Camille’s experience in public transportation means she knows firsthand how critical it is to invest in all modes of transportation.
Maintaining infrastructure including roadways, sidewalks, trails, and public transportation is critical for the future of the city and something she will work hard to promote in Concord.
PROMOTE EFFECTIVE GOVERNMENT
As someone who has an extensive background in government work, Camille knows what it takes to be effective and efficient.
She will support and facilitate programs to promote streamlined review processes and incentives for infill, adaptive reuse and development of a variety of housing options for our community.
PROTECTING OUR NATURAL RESOURCES + INVESTING IN GREEN INFRASTRUCTURE
Camille supports strong safeguards to keep New Hampshire’s water and air clean.
She backs efforts to reduce pollution, strengthen environmental standards, and invest in infrastructure that protects drinking water and air quality for families across the state.
