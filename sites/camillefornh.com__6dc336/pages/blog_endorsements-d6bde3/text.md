Over the past few weeks, we’ve been out knocking doors, meeting neighbors, and building real momentum.
A few highlights: We’ve continued to add new endorsements, and I’m grateful for each one.
Mayor Jim Donchess, Mayor of Nashua, NH Lori Wilshire, Nashua Alderman at-Large, President of the Board of Alderman Shoshanna Kelly, Nashua Alderman at-Large Tom Lopez, Nashua Alderman Ward 4 Senator Cindy Rosenwald, Deputy Democratic Leader, District 13 Melanie Levesque, Former State Senator, District 12 Bette Lasky, Former State Senator, District 13 Sue Newman, Representative, Hillsborough-04 Ray Newman, Representative, Hillsborough-04 On July 11, I spent the afternoon at the Nashua Democratic Summer BBQ connecting with dedicated volunteers, community leaders, and candidates.
The energy and commitment in the room were inspiring, and it was a great reminder of how many people are working hard to strengthen our community.
As we move closer to the September 8 primary, we’re keeping that momentum going.
I’m excited about the direction we’re heading and the people we’re meeting along the way.
Thanks for being part of this.
