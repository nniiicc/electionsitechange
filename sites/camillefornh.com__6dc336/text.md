ABOUT CAMILLE Camille Pattison is a government and community leader running for Ward 2 State Representative.
She brings extensive experience in land use and transportation planning, organizational management, economic development and transit and grant administration.

She hopes to lend her experience and love for Nashua to the State House, giving an authentic voice to the people.
