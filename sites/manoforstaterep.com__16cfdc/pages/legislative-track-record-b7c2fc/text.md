More than tripling the homestead exemption to $140,000, raising it to $200,000 for seniors and disabled Texans, providing a $125,000 exemption for business equipment and inventory, and further cuts to school property tax rates.
Since 2019, these reforms have helped drive school district tax rates down significantly—for example, reducing the M&O tax rate in SBISD and HISD from just over $1.00 to about $0.70.
This is real, long-term property tax relief that makes Texas more affordable.
But Mano knows more must be done to significantly reduce the property tax burden on Texas families and ensure homeowners can keep more of their hard-earned money.
Funding Schools And Protecting Children In the 89th Legislative Session, Mano voted to pass the largest investment increase in public education in Texas history—more than $8.5 billion through House Bill 2 to support teachers, strengthen our schools, and help students succeed.
HB 2 provides $4.2 billion for permanent teacher pay raises and invests more than $2.2 billion in student success, including funding for special education, early literacy and numeracy programs, career and technical education, and $430 million for school safety improvements.
Invested $1 billion in Education Savings Accounts to expand parental choice and give families more educational opportunities.
Mano’s work in the Texas legislature has helped increase state funding for public education and significantly reduce the burden of “Robin Hood” recapture on local taxpayers.
By increasing the state’s share of school funding to over 50% for the first time in decades, Mano successfully fought to reduce “recapture” by more than $350 million for HISD and SBISD.
These reforms reduced property taxes permanently and keep more local tax dollars in our community while increasing to investment in our teachers, school safety, instructional materials, and resources to support our students.
Fought to protect children and uphold Texas values.
Supported legislation to remove sexually explicit materials from school libraries, crack down on obscene and exploitative content, ban NDAs in child abuse cases, and strengthen online safety through the App Store Accountability Act.

Keeping Texas Businesses #1 To keep Texas the #1 state for business and job opportunities, Rep.
DeAyala cut taxes, streamlined regulations, and made it easier to start and grow a business here.
These efforts have built on Mano’s work from the past two legislative sessions to strengthen Texas’ business climate, including updating corporate laws, creating the Texas Business Court, and ensuring companies have the certainty they need to grow, invest, and create jobs across our state.
He co-authored The Texas Regulatory Consistency Act to bring regulatory uniformity to Texas, leading to a more stable and prosperous economic environment, prevented insurance providers from utilizing environmental, social, and corporate governance ratemaking (ESG), and co-authored the creation of a specialized business court system to address complex business cases.
This session, Mano worked to raise the business personal property tax exemption to $125,000 , sped up business filings , protected home-based entrepreneurs from local overreach , and required state agencies to modernize their websites to better serve Texans.
