About Me
State Senator & Assistant Majority Leader
In my first term, I helped lead the historic DFL trifecta as Assistant Majority Leader and Vice Chair of the Judiciary and Public Safety Committee.
I am proud to have ushered in landmark legislation like the PRO Act, which codified reproductive freedom into state law , and the North Star Promise, which created a tuition-free college program for Minnesota families.
I passed 69 laws in my first two years alone.
A Career in Public Service and Advocacy
Before you elected me to the Senate, I dedicated my career to public service
- As the Equity Manager for the City of Saint Paul, I worked to eliminate racial disparities in local government.
- As Chair of the Roseville Area Schools Foundation, I fought to ensure every student had the resources they needed to succeed.
- As a Political and Labor Organizer, I helped elect progressive candidates across Minnesota.
Fighter for justice
As a Black woman, the daughter of a Senegalese immigrant and a retired public school teacher, fighting injustice is not just a policy position for me—it is deeply personal.
It informs every priority I champion in the Senate.
I am committed to passing policies that ensure every Minnesotan has safe, stable housing, accessible healthcare, and a community where they can truly thrive.
I live in Saint Paul’s Como neighborhood with my husband, Garrison, our son, Leo, and our goldendoodle, Honey.
About Senate District 66
I am proud to have represented the communities of Saint Paul, Roseville, Falcon Heights and Lauderdale in the Minnesota Senate since first being elected in 2022.
