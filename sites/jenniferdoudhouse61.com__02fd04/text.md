Meet Jennifer I have been with the Constitution Party since 2006.
I have had several leadership opportunities including precinct Chair, Utah County Chair, County Vice Chair, County Treasurer, State 2nd Vice Chair, and State Treasurer.
I grew up in Washington State on a 5 acre farm.
We raised sheep, goats, pigs, and chickens.
I did 4 years in the Army after high school, in order to receive the VEAP program, which paid for 4 years at Brigham Young University (BYU).
The first year of being in the Army, I was sent to the Defense Language Institute in Monterey, California to learn the Polish language.
After my discharge from the US Army, I attended BYU and received a teaching degree in Computer Science with a Physics minor.
My husband and I met at BYU, married and raised 4 children.
We have lived in Provo since 2001.
I love the US Constitution.
It was created to control the government to keep it from infringing on the citizens’ rights.
Its purpose is to protect our rights to life, liberty and property.
I enjoy learning true history and about the founding fathers.
More about Jennifer (Experience) I have been involved with the Constitution Party for many years in the following leadership positions: State 2nd Vice-Chair, State Treasurer, County Treasurer, and County Chair.
I have been a member of the John Birch Society for at least 16 years, contacting Congress members at both the State and Federal level on important issues.
I have helped in many campaigns.
The first was Ron Paul, who ran for President, with the Republican Party.
I collected donations and held signs at many street corners.
I also supported Constitution Party candidates such as Jim Noorlander and Scott Bradley.
