“Our Missouri Constitution is our path to preserving liberty.
It was written to keep government in check and protect our God-given rights.
When we follow it, we keep power in the hands of the people, and we stop the government from growing beyond its proper role!” -Bryant Bryant Wolfin is a husband, father of four, and small business owner.
He is a graduate of Mineral Area College and a volunteer youth sports coach.
A lifelong Missourian, he is blessed to call Ste.
Genevieve home and serves as the local Republican club's treasurer.
He is also the county's Republican Central Committee Treasurer.
A former alderman for the city, he has always fought to defend individual liberty and shrink the role of government.
His priorities for the Missouri House include cutting taxes & wasteful spending, protecting the unborn, and supporting law enforcement.
Bryant's conservative platform can be summarized in 3 words: LIFE.
LIBERTY.
PROPERTY.
