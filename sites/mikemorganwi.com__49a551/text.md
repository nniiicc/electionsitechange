During his six years on the Wauwatosa Common Council, Mike became well informed on local transportation challenges through chairing the Transportation Affairs Committee.
As a council member, he championed practical reforms, including a wheel tax accountability amendment and budget initiatives to reduce reckless driving.
Mike has a long record of community service, founding the Junior Trojan Youth Baseball program in 2007 and volunteering with organizations including the Tosa West Booster Club and St.
Joseph's Catholic Church.
His professional career spans leadership roles in marketing, communications, public service, education, and manufacturing, giving him firsthand experience in both the public and private sectors.
Mike's commitment to fiscal responsibility, community engagement, and effective local government make him an excellent candidate to represent Brookfield, Elm Grove and Wauwatosa in the State Assembly.
