top of page PUBLIC SAFETY: THREE-POINT PLAN FOR WISCONSIN 1.
Hold Criminals Accountable and Support Law Enforcement Support consistent enforcement of state criminal laws and stronger penalties for repeat and violent offenders.
Strengthen coordination among local police, prosecutors and state law enforcement.
Wisconsin Department of Justice criminal justice grants can support effective local public safety initiatives with measurable results and accountability.
2.
Protect Children and Prevent Crime Strengthen Wisconsin’s Child Advocacy Center network, including the CARE Center serving Waukesha County, to improve child abuse investigations.
Support Wisconsin Department of Justice school safety grants and improve coordination between schools, police and emergency responders.
Expand evidence-based youth prevention and early-intervention programs that help at-risk youth avoid crime and keep neighborhoods safe.
3.
Give First Responders Proper Resources Help eligible providers access EMS Funding Assistance Program to support essential emergency medical services.
Support state and federal grants for fire equipment, protective gear, emergency vehicles, training and recruitment of firefighters and EMTs.
Review staffing, response times and equipment needs with local police, fire and EMS departments, while prioritizing cost-effective investments and regional cooperation.
My commitment: Protect children, enforce the law, support those who protect us, and ensure public safety funding delivers measurable results for Wisconsin taxpayers.
