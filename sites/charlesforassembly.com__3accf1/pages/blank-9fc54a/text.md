About Charlie
Charlie Potter has spent his career in service to his community and understands the unique needs of the 118th District.
He has built a record of leadership rooted in integrity, practical solutions, and a steadfast commitment to public service.
Throughout his professional life, Charlie has focused on issues that matter most to families in the region, including community development, economic resilience, and quality education.
His decision to run for the State Assembly is driven by a desire to expand his commitment to service on behalf of all residents in the district.
Why Charlie Is Running
Charlie is running for the New York State Assembly because he believes the people of the 118th District deserve strong representation that listens, acts, and delivers results.
He is committed to bringing common-sense solutions to Albany that reflect the values and priorities of local families, workers, and small businesses.
Charlie understands that leadership means being accountable, accessible, and focused on solving problems that impact people’s everyday lives.
