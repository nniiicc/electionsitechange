Charlie will work with state and local partners to close gaps in service, support infrastructure investment, and ensure that rural communities are not left behind in today’s digital economy.
This includes advocating for strategic funding and regulatory support to improve connectivity across the district.
02 Strengthen Infrastructure Across the District Roads, bridges, water systems, and public facilities are essential to safety and economic growth.
Charlie will fight for smart infrastructure investment that prioritizes long-term value and mobility, while also ensuring that taxpayer dollars are spent responsibly and with transparency.
03 Promote Economic Opportunity and Job Growth The foundation of thriving communities is a strong economy.
Charlie supports policies that encourage business development, support small business owners, expand workforce training programs, and attract new employers to the region.
His approach focuses on reducing unnecessary regulatory burdens while expanding access to opportunity for all residents.
04 Support Quality Education for Every Student Investing in education is investing in the future.
Charlie advocates for increased resources for schools, teachers, and students, while preserving local control so communities can best address their unique educational needs.
He believes in preparing children for success and ensuring that schools are equipped to deliver excellence in learning.
05 Responsible Government and Fiscal Integrity Charlie believes government should be transparent, accountable, and fiscally responsible.
As a local leader, he has championed balanced budgets and responsible spending, and he will continue to uphold these principles in the State Assembly by protecting taxpayers and ensuring effective use of public funds.
My Platform Charlie Potter believes in common-sense leadership that puts people first.
He is running to bring effective representation to Albany that reflects the values and priorities of working families, small business owners, and rural communities throughout the 118th District.
His platform is grounded in expanding opportunity, enhancing infrastructure, and ensuring government is responsible and accountable to the citizens it serves.
