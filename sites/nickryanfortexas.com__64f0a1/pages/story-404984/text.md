Menu Meet Nicholas Ryan Issues Volunteer Donate Meet Nicholas Ryan Who Nicholas Is At his core, Nicholas Ryan Holbrook is an ordinary father, builder, and Independent candidate who believes public life should begin with responsibility, honesty, and steady work.
Too many people feel unseen by systems that are supposed to serve them.
Leadership should stay close to real life, tell the truth, and remember who carries the cost when institutions fall short.
The Builder's Path This campaign did not begin with party machinery.
It grew out of a builder’s way of seeing the world: pay attention, keep learning, work through complexity, and stay accountable to what actually works.
That habit shaped his view of leadership.
Listen carefully.
Tell the truth about what is broken.
Fix what can be fixed.
Keep going until the result is better for the people living with it.
Family Pressure While Protecting Privacy Like many parents, he knows how quickly stability can feel fragile when pressure keeps rising around a household.
That experience made public problems feel personal.
It deepened his conviction that government should not make daily life harder for people already carrying real weight.
Witnessing Technology Transform Work Working close to technology gave him a clear view of how quickly change is moving and how unevenly its effects can land.
New tools can help people build, learn, and solve problems.
They can also leave workers, parents, and communities trying to catch up.
Public leadership has to meet that change with preparation, honesty, and a clear commitment to keep human beings at the center.
Why Public Service Became Necessary There came a point when concern was not enough.
When you can see the strain people are under, and you can see how disconnected public life has become, stepping forward starts to feel less like ambition and more like duty.
Faith shapes that duty through humility, truth, neighbor-love, and service.
Leadership should lift burdens where it can, tell the truth when it must, and serve people rather than dominate them.
How He Can Serve Well The work of public service needs practical problem-solving, independent judgment, technical thinking, and real-life responsibility.
Nicholas brings those habits to the table.
He is used to studying complex problems, thinking ahead, and staying focused on solutions that protect liberty, respect human dignity, and help people build more stable lives.
Connected To The People He Wants To Serve This campaign is rooted in care for family, respect for neighbors, and the belief that public life should serve real communities, not abstract systems.
The people of District 98 deserve leaders who understand how fragile stability can be and how important it is to protect opportunity, freedom, and trust.
Building A Future That Serves People This campaign is not asking for blind agreement.
It is asking for a fair hearing and the chance to earn trust through honest work.
