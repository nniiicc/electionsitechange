Priorities For A Stronger District 98
Politics gets loud, but the needs of families, workers, students, and small businesses are not complicated.
People want breathing room, safe communities, honest government, strong schools, protected rights, and a future they can prepare for instead of fear.
Nicholas Ryan Holbrook is running to focus on practical problems with humility, accountability, and a commitment to serve people before party machinery.
Affordability and Economic Breathing Room
Texas families should be able to cover the basics, absorb setbacks, and still have room to plan ahead.
When basic costs keep rising, stability can disappear one bill at a time.
Taxes, housing, utilities, insurance, groceries, health care, and everyday essentials all affect whether people can plan ahead or simply try to keep up.
Public leadership that respects how hard Texans work and helps them keep more breathing room in their lives is exactly what all Texans need right now.
Nicholas wants to deliver exactly that to District 98, and he knows the only way to do something right is to do it yourself.
Children, Education, and Family Stability
Children need protection, preparation, strong schools, and adults who take their future seriously.
All children need more than slogans from adults.
They need their parents respected, their teachers supported, their schools focused on learning, and communities that protect them while preparing them for real life.
Nicholas wants to level up educational institutions that will help students build practical knowledge, strong character, digital awareness, and wholesome pathways into adulthood.
Honest Government Accountability
Public power should belong to the people, not insiders, lobbyists, or political machines.
Public trust is earned when The People can see what the government is doing, how their tax money is being used, and whether the results match the promises.
It's no secret that Puppet Politicians are selling out Texas Families to lobbyist and special interest groups that steal opportunity from ordinary Texans.
Nicholas is prepared to bring servant leadership to the Texas government that tells the truth, cuts through excuses, measures outcomes, puts Texas Families first and treats public power as a responsibility.
Liberty and Constitutional Rights
Rights matter because they protect conscience, privacy, speech, family authority, and limits on government power.
Freedom is not just a word.
It depends on real limits on government power.
Free speech, religious liberty, privacy, due process, parental rights and constitutional protections help keep people from being pressured, silenced, or controlled by those in authority.
Nicholas wants to serve with humility, compassion, and a steady respect for every Texan’s conscience and inalienable rights.
Small Business, Work, and Opportunity
Workers and small businesses need room to build, grow, adapt, and create something lasting.
Workers, small business owners, builders, and entrepreneurs all understand the same basic truth: progress takes effort, risk, and room to move.
Public policy should make it easier For The People to work, hire, learn, adapt, and build something stable, not bury them under avoidable barriers.
Nicholas wants an economy where effort still matters, initiative is respected, and ordinary people have a fair chance to create something lasting.
Responsible Technology and Future Readiness
Technology should serve people, protect privacy, and prepare families for the changes already arriving.
Change in Technology is arriving fast.
New tools can help people learn, build, solve problems, and create opportunity, but they can also leave workers, institutions, and communities trying to catch up.
Corporations all over America are already "trimming the fat" while quickly integrating AI, automation, and blockchain into the systems we use everyday.
The People are at a disadvantage now that Corporations have complete control to mold this new era into the shape that best fits what their global investors' demand, instead of shaping things into what best serves their workers and their worker's communities that these business operates in.
Small businesses are barely getting by today; what happens when Corporations use AI to target what little market share Mom and Pop have left?
Nicholas understands today's cutting-edge technologies; he's prepared to fight for all Texans to ensure these systems are built to serve The People, not to control and rule them.
He's ready to roll up his sleeves and carefully build public systems that actually serve The People of Texas, instead of extracting value from them.
Independent People-First Representation
District 98 deserves representation that answers to neighbors before party machinery.
An Independent representative should answer to the district first.
Nicholas is running to offer leadership that listens honestly, works across disagreement when possible, and stays close to the people living with the results.
The goal is not party victory; it's victory for The People.
The goal is honest representation, practical service, and a government that remembers who it works for.
