DONATE SUBSCRIBE ABOUT KRISTA Mother, Wife, Business Owner Krista was born and raised in Minnesota.
She resides in Lake Shore, MN with her husband Kevin and their four children.
Krista and Kevin are the owners of Cowboy’s Restaurant in Lake Shore, MN.
Krista served on the Lake Shore City Council for eight years and as Mayor for two.
She has spent the last two years serving as MN House Representative for District 5a.
“Under total one-party Democrat control for past two years, an $18 billion surplus was squandered, $10 billion in new taxes and a state budget increase of 40%.
During my first term in the Minnesota House of Representatives, I am proud to say that I was a strong voice opposing the agenda of one-party Democrat control.
I stood firm in my Christian faith and used those morals and values to fight against the evil we are facing.
The love and protectiveness I feel for my four children, all our children, is what motivates me to fight to preserve and defend our way of life.
Here, we value life and liberty.
We stand strong in our faith.
We are proud of a hard day’s work.
We take care of our own.
And we are fed up with our over-reaching government getting in our way!
I am running for re-election for the Minnesota House of Representatives because we need a voice in St.
Paul that will champion our causes, built on the foundation of our values and morals.” ​ What are her priorities?
