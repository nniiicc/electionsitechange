Committed to building a better future for Greater Holland.
Nancy DeBoer has a proven record of success.
She has been a concerned citizen, City Councilwoman, and the first female Mayor of Holland!
Nancy understands the needs of the people she serves by encouraging collaboration, fostering communication, and persevering under pressure.
Legislative Accomplishments and Goals for 2026:
In her first term (2023-2024), DeBoer was a member of the House Appropriations Committee and passed bipartisan school safety legislation.
She was also an advocate for restoring state grants to local districts to support mental health services for students, as well as school resource officers and building security enhancements.
Rep.
DeBoer crossed party lines to ensure the passage of housing affordability legislation creating the state’s new Missing Middle housing program and legislation raising MSHDA’s home sales price limits which had not been updated since 2009.
A former high school teacher, DeBoer currently serves as Chair of the state House Education and Workforce Committee.
She is also a member of the Energy Committee, Health Policy Committee, and Rules Committee.
In 2025, DeBoer helped pass a bipartisan, balanced budget which addressed a number of major issues including increased local road funding and stabilization of the Medicaid program, which preserves specialty healthcare services and provides funding which prevents the forced closure or consolidation of smaller hospitals.
The budget provided record public school funding and more local control over how state funds can be spent to address local student needs.
DeBoer also secured a $650,000 state grant to aid Park Township’s acquisition and remodeling of the recently closed United States Coast Guard Station on Lake Macatawa into a base for water rescue operations on Lake Macatawa and Lake Michigan.
Rep.
DeBoer also secured $500,000 for Statewide Habitat for Humanity services.
In 2026, DeBoer plans to sponsor legislation to address several affordability issues around childcare and additional affordable housing legislation.
She is also working on education reforms to address literacy, declining attendance, and provide more flexibility for local districts to allow students to pursue non-college career paths like the trades and agriculture.
DeBoer also crafted a 6 bill health package that was passed unanimously across all party lines for the use of nasal spray epinephrine.
For her housing advocacy, Rep.
DeBoer was given the 2025 Habitat for Humanity Legislator of the Year award.
Click on “Issues” tab above to learn additional details about her legislative goals for Housing, Day Care legislation, Energy, and Education.
Mayoral accomplishments include:
- Decommissioning the Holland coal power plant, thereby initiating the potential for waterfront development
- Construction of a state-of-the-art natural gas plant, reducing the City of Holland’s carbon emissions by 50%, paying off the plant early and reducing the cost of electricity by 10%
- 8th Street City Block Development with Sperry Movie House, restaurants, and parking garage
- Initiating the process for fiber internet downtown and studying the potential for it to spread city-wide
- Renovating the Civic Center Place with no new taxes
