Pick-up: Heritage Park · 4425 W Cedar Hills Dr., Cedar Hills · Daily: 4:00 PM Biography My story begins in Utah with my mother, who grew up in Mount Pleasant, where she met my father at Wasatch Academy.
I was born in Fremont, California, raised in the suburbs of Chicago before moving to Tucson, Arizona, where I lived for 30 years.
At 18, I chose to make faith a central part of my life—a decision that continues to guide how I see and serve others.
I married young, raised three children, and, like many Utah families, balanced work, church, education, and family life.
My husband and I believed in hard work.
Of the five businesses we owned, our independent bookstore was my favorite.
It confirmed what I believe deeply: people are good, books make the world better, and stories have the power to heal.
Even while living elsewhere, Utah was always part of our story.
Like many Utah families, we returned often creating countless moments that kept us connected to the people, places, and values we love.
We purchased our first Utah property in 1998, added several more over the years, and in 2017 chose Utah as our forever home.
A Lifetime of Learning & Service For more than 40 years, I've built businesses, helped families, returned to school, and sought new perspectives through travel.
Every experience prepared me for public service.
I Built I've owned and helped build five businesses, managed long-term investment properties, and worked as a licensed real estate professional since 1994.
I Learned Returning to college in my 50's led to a BS degree in Family Studies and Human Development from the UofA and a Master's in Applied Positive Psychology from UPenn.
I learned that people thrive when they're listened to, treated as if they matter, and given opportunities to succeed.
I Explored Walking the Camino de Santiago, hiking the Grand Canyon Rim-to-Rim, and traveling abroad strengthened my resilience, curiosity, and appreciation for people from every background.
Now I Serve These experiences shaped how I lead—with curiosity, hope, and kindness.
They are why I'm running for the Utah Senate: to help bring out the best in Utah and in one another.
