We believe every member of our community should be able to access information about Aaron Gutierrez, his campaign for Colorado State Senate District 3, and opportunities to participate in the democratic process.
Our Commitment We are continually working to improve the accessibility and usability of AaronForPueblo.com.
Our goal is to follow recognized digital accessibility practices, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA, where reasonably possible.
Our accessibility efforts include working to provide: Clear and readable text with appropriate color contrast Alternative text for meaningful images Logical headings and page structure Keyboard-accessible navigation Descriptive links and buttons Accessible forms and interactive elements Captions or other accessible alternatives for multimedia content when available Responsive content that can be accessed across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices Accessibility Is an Ongoing Effort Digital accessibility is an ongoing process.
As our campaign website grows and new content is added, we will continue working to identify and address accessibility barriers.
Some portions of this website may include content, embedded services, social media, donation platforms, videos, documents, or other technology provided by third parties.
While we strive to select accessible services and content, we may not have complete control over the accessibility of third-party platforms.
Need Assistance?
If you experience difficulty accessing any part of AaronForPueblo.com, encounter an accessibility barrier, or need campaign information provided in an alternative format, please contact us.
When contacting us, it is helpful to include the page or feature you were attempting to access, the nature of the accessibility issue, and your preferred method of contact.
We will make reasonable efforts to provide the requested information in an accessible format and to address accessibility concerns as promptly as practical.
Feedback Is Welcome We welcome feedback about the accessibility of our website.
Accessibility helps ensure that more people in our community can participate, stay informed, and engage with the campaign.
