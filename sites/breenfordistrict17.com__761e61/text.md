Meet Dennis Your Voice at the Capitol.
Your Neighbor at home.
Dennis is a seasoned attorney, Navy son, husband, and father who believes public service is a responsibility, not a career.
After nearly four decades advocating for families and businesses, he’s running for NC House for District 17 to bring fresh leadership, independent thinking, and meaningful change to Raleigh.
Our district deserves a new voice and a renewed focus on the people.
A New Vision.
Real Solutions.
Protecting Healthcare Access Every family deserves affordable, comprehensive healthcare close to home.
I will fight to protect access to medical care, lower costs for working families and teachers, and uphold a woman’s right to make her own health decisions.
Healthcare is not a privilege.
It's a basic right.
Learn More Investing in Education Strong public schools build strong communities.
I oppose taking tax dollars away from our public schools in the form of private vouchers.
Our classrooms should be places of learning, not fear.
I will fight to keep guns out of schools, support our teachers, and give students the safe, well-funded education they deserve.
Learn More Supporting Smart, Balanced Growth Brunswick County is growing, and growth should benefit the people who already live here.
I support smart, locally guided planning that protects neighborhoods, preserves green space, and strengthens infrastructure.
Decisions about zoning belong in our community, not imposed from Raleigh or driven by special interests.

Protecting our natural resources isn’t partisan.
It’s about safeguarding public health and preserving what makes North Carolina home.
