The good news is that because Josiah didn’t back down, beginning in 2024 the House Ways and Means Committee is now requiring much greater transparency and detail in the earmark process.
The updated earmark form now requires specifics about each project, including the contact information of a person at the entity requesting, how the funds will benefit the public, when the project will be started and completed, and a detailed description of the project.
This information is all promised to be released to the public, for the first time ever.
