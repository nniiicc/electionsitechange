The truth. jemzpierson Nov 15, 2025 1 min read Updated: Jan 15 Too many people talking about prices being up or down but not including real data.
Yes, some things could be said to be down.
Eggs are down from their peak during the avian flu, but they aren't down to 2021 prices.
Beef continues to trend upwards as it has for years.
Electricity and natural gas are up by 50% since a few years ago.
Bottom line, prices are up in a big way.
