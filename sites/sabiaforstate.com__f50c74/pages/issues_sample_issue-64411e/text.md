Data Center Invasion
Big data centers are attempting to take over our rural way of life in the valley.
For example, consider just one borough in our district alone, Archbald, the town where my family resides.
Archbald became a focal point for developers looking to establish large-scale data facilities, with plans for several complexes significantly altering the local landscape.
Data Center complexes use up lots of land, electricity, and water, will destroy our natural settings, will bring noise and will no doubt increase water and electric bills.
Unfortunately, these destructive results impact our county as well.
Recognizing the problem immediately, realizing the urgency of the situation, and knowing a first strike defense was needed, I was agreeable to file suit.
As a plaintiff in a lawsuit against the Borough Council of the Borough of Archbald, I began fighting for you, my community to deter the data center invasion, for strong local rules.
