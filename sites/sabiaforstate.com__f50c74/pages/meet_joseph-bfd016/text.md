Meet Joseph J.
Sabia Jr.
Joe Sabia is a seasoned executive leader, entrepreneur, and retired U.S.
Navy veteran with over 30 years of distinguished experience in operations management, human resources, financial oversight, and organizational leadership.
A proud veteran, Joe served in the United States Navy from 1992 to 2013, where he specialized in Flight Operations, Operations Management, and Human Resources.
During his career he supervised teams of up to 71 personnel, managed multi-million-dollar operational budgets (including a $1.2 million annual flight operations budget supporting over 100,000 flight hours and a $5.7 million budget for new helicopter development and testing), and played a key role in planning, training, and safety programs across the U.S. and abroad.
He regularly briefed senior military leaders, Department of Defense agencies, contractors, media, and members of Congress on operational and acquisition matters.
Joe also led occupational, transportation, and aviation safety initiatives and was qualified by the Navy in safety training program design and aviation ground safety.
Following his military service Joe transitioned into civilian leadership roles.
He served as Director of Safety for The Topps Company in Scranton handling all safety matters in manufacturing for over 300 employees and handled Workers’ Compensation cases.
He then worked as an HR Specialist for the U.S.
Army Command (2017–2018), providing expert recruitment, placement, position classification, and human capital management support for specialized and hard-to-fill roles.
In 2018, Joe founded and became CFO/Owner of Cerberus Dynamic Solutions ( an Executive Security and Disaster Relief Co.), where he continues to lead the company’s strategic direction.
In this role, he oversees financial and tax strategies, budgeting, capital requests, performance monitoring, and operational best practices.
He also manages accounting, human resources, legal, tax, and treasury functions, while maintaining oversight of subsidiary companies and third-party partnerships.
Joe holds three advanced degrees from Marywood University: a Master of Business Administration (MBA) with an emphasis in Management (2018, Magna Cum Laude) and a Master of Health Service Administration (2016, Magna Cum Laude).
He earned his Bachelor of Science in Workforce Education and Development from Southern Illinois University at Carbondale (2012, Cum Laude).
Committed to professional excellence and community service, Joe maintains multiple active security licenses across Pennsylvania, Florida, and Texas (including Act 235, “D”, “G”, Commissioned Security Officer, Non-Commissioned Security Officer, Private Investigator, and Personal Protection Officer).
He is a longtime member of the Veterans of Foreign Wars and American Legion.
Joe Sabia brings a unique combination of military discipline, operational expertise, financial acumen and strategic leadership to every endeavor, with a proven track record of driving performance, managing complex organizations, and delivering results in both high-stakes military environments and dynamic private-sector businesses.
