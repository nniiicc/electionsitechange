Meet Ben Weber
Ben Weber is a proud Republican, husband, and small business attorney running to fight for Union and Marion Counties in the Ohio House of Representatives.
A devoted defender of liberty, faith, and the rule of law, Ben is running to push back against rising property tax bills, fight for our families, and advance a more affordable future that puts Ohio First.
ISSUES
- Delivering Real Tax Reform & Slashing Property Taxes Ohio has the eighth-highest property tax burden in the nation — and that’s unacceptable.
Property taxes have surged, leaving homeowners with bills they never saw coming and too often can’t afford.
Everywhere I go, I hear the same concern: families are being taxed out of their own homes.
That must change.
That’s why I’m running to slash property tax bills — not just talk about reform, but deliver real relief.
I will fight to cut property tax bills so families and small businesses can keep more of their hard-earned money and stay rooted in the communities they call home.
True tax reform isn’t just about dollars and cents — it’s about protecting families, strengthening communities, and ensuring Ohio remains an affordable place to live, work, and retire.
I’m committed to putting Ohio taxpayers first.
- Strengthening Families I’m a fighter for families — because everything I do starts with protecting and strengthening them.
I believe every child is a precious gift, which is why I am 100% pro-life.
I believe parents, not government bureaucrats, know what’s best for their children — especially when it comes to education and healthcare.
That’s why I stand for parents’ rights in the classroom, making sure families have a real voice in what their children are taught.
And it’s why I’ll always defend parents’ rights to make medical decisions for their families without government interference.
I’m also firmly against radical government overreach in our private lives.
Families deserve to make their own choices, guided by their values — not by mandates or political agendas.
Families are the backbone of Ohio — and I’ll never stop fighting to make sure they have the freedom, respect, and opportunity they deserve.
- Building an Affordable Future at Home Too many Ohio families are being stretched thin by rising costs — from groceries to gas to energy bills.
That’s why it’s time to push for a more affordable future that puts Ohio first.
We don’t need to look to the coasts or overseas for solutions.
The power to drive our future is right here beneath our feet and in our communities.
Ohio is rich in energy — from natural gas and manufacturing innovation.
If we unleash that potential, we can lower costs, create good-paying jobs, and catapult our economy into the next era.
An affordable Ohio means keeping our energy production local, our jobs here at home, and our prosperity in the hands of Ohio families.
It’s about building a future where opportunity doesn’t come from somewhere else — it starts right here.
