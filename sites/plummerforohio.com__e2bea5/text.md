Meet Phil Plummer About State Representative Phil Plummer possesses an extensive record of service to the people of Montgomery County.
Prior to being elected State Representative, Plummer served for 30 years in the Sheriff’s office of Montgomery County including 10 years as Sheriff.
As Sheriff, Plummer also had the distinct honor of serving as the SWAT Team Commander.
He brings rare experience to his role as state representative with executive training from The FBI National Academy, The FBI National Executive Institute, and the Secret Service Executive Leadership Academy.
Phil Plummer was born and raised in Montgomery County and is a proud graduate of Chaminade Julienne High School.
After high school, Phil further his education.
He earned an associate’s degree in criminal justice from the University of Toledo and a Bachelor of Science in Business Management from the University of Phoenix. ​ Phil Plummer resides in Dayton with his wife, Cindy.
They are proud parents of three grown children and three grandchildren.
Both Phil and Cindy are active in their local church and the Dayton community.” "Phil Plummer is a man of integrity and moral character.
As Montgomery County Sheriff, he proved himself to be a principled conservative leader who has consistently fought to defend individual rights and freedoms.
Phil tackles problems head-on and gets results for the citizens and communities he serves."
