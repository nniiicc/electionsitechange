I haven't made it out to nearly enough of these this season, but I'm always excited when I see it when I drive by.
This community center is really one of the jewels of Pembroke, in my opinion, and I was really, really grateful to have the ability to work with Congressman Bill Keating when I was on Pembroke Select Board to secure three million dollars in funding to get this project over the finish line and get this building built.
It is absolutely something that we needed in town and it was really a privilege and an honor to be able to work with our federal partners to find funding sources to make projects like this happen.
As your state legislator, I will work with federal partners and with all of the select boards in the various towns in the district to make sure that we can keep building new infrastructure, that we can keep improving our communities, that we can keep development at the right level in the right places in all of our towns so that all of these communities can thrive.
