Look, I'm the eldest daughter of two union members.
My mom was in the Mass Teachers Association and the Mass Nurses Association.
She served as the school nurse at Woodman Hanson High School for more than 20 years, and my dad was a member of the National Association of Letter Carriers.
Both of them were union reps in their respective organizations, and I grew up knowing that unions were the way that families earned good wages, built good lives, and had a fair shot.
Super proud to have the support of so many of the unions in our area, and excited to work together to make sure that we can build better conditions for working families throughout Massachusetts and in the 6th Plymouth.
