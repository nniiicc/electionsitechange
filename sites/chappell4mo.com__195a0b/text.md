MEET DARIN
"As your next state representative, I will continue my mission of protecting constitutional conservative principles."
Darin, a Springfield native, is a constitution and foundational documents advocate, and frequently a guest host of conservative radio shows.
He is a proven fiscal conservative.
Darin is a U.S.
Navy and Army Reserves veteran.
Darin's plan:
- Uphold constitutional rights
- Return power to the people
- Keep taxes low
- Defend the right to life
- Remove Critical Race Theory from schools
Darin is a proud husband, father and grandfather.
He has a Master's Degree in Public Administration and is a former adjunct faculty member at Missouri State University.
