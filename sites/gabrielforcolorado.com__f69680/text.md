Born and raised in Thornton, I live with my beautiful wife Raquel and our puppy, Konetl.
I’m a non-profit co-founder, engaged community member, and product of the community I aim to represent.
I’m running to represent the people and the community that I grew up with and right now we have a choice: We can choose to continue down the path to eroding trust between our elected officials and our community by letting billionaires and companies buy our politics- Or we can choose to send our community to the Capitol.
We can choose to ban investment firms from buying single family housing , we can give communities the ability to hold corporations accountable, whether they be polluters or landlords , get money out of politics , and we can create a legal pathway to criminally prosecute ICE agents abusing their authority .
We have the choice to choose that future, so let’s choose that future.
I’m asking you for your faith and for your vote by June 30th .
