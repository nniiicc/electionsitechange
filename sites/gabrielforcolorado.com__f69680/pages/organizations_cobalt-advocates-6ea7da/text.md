Cobalt Advocates
Cobalt Advocates is the 501(c)4 advocacy and political arm of Cobalt.
They take positions on abortion rights bills and issues at the municipal, state, and federal level, enforce accountability for officeholders on those issues, and endorse candidates.
They use grassroots organizational power to elect pro-abortion rights legislators, and financially support candidates and officeholders at the municipal, state, and federal level who share their values.
