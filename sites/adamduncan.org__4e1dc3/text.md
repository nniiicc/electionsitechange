About Adam
From the mat to the State House
Adam Duncan grew up here, wrestled here, and has spent his life coaching Oconee's young people.
He went to Columbia to do one thing: fight for the people of District 2 — not the special interests.
A graduate of West-Oak High School, Adam was part of the school's first state championship team and earned the Dave Schultz Excellence Award for the state and southeast region, recognizing wrestling success, scholastic achievement, and citizenship.
He went on to wrestle at the Division I level at the University of Tennessee at Chattanooga.
Adam has poured his time and talent into young people through JET Wrestling and West-Oak wrestling, helping bring home ten state championships for Oconee County.
More than the trophies, he's taught young men the value of hard work, discipline, and toughness.
In 2026, he was inducted into the West-Oak High School Athletic Hall of Fame.
He was raised with that same work ethic.
His father gave 43 years of service to Blue Ridge Electric; his mother was a small business owner.
With two brothers in law enforcement, Adam is a fierce supporter of the men and women in blue.
He supports local small business — not corporate lobbyists — and the conservative values Oconee families live by.
