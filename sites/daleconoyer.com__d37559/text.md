My family has remained in the northern farmlands of Saint Charles County through floods, tornadoes, economic crises, and every major change the county has seen.
Inspired by a family of union men and by what I saw in Jeff City, I decided to run because too many politicians have stopped listening to the people they represent.
Republicans in Missouri government told me, “Just because the Missouri people vote for something doesn't mean it's okay.” I got actively involved in politics in 2024 by knocking doors for Proposition A.
I believe elected officials must respect citizen ballot initiatives passed by Missouri voters.
I want representatives focused on schools, roads, hospitals, and everyday costs — not political games..
Top priorities What I want to fight for from day one.
My campaign centers on making government answer to the people, protecting Missouri's finances, and keeping harmful data center projects out of the district.
