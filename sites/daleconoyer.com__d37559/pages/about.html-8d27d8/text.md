About Me Deep roots in Saint Charles County.
The Conoyers have been in Saint Charles County longer than anyone can remember.
My family stayed in the northern farmlands of the county through floods, tornadoes, economic crises, and generations of change.
My father is a union man, and my grandfather was a union man too.
Inspired by that background, I got actively involved in politics in 2024.
One of the first things I did was knock doors and advocate for Proposition A, the citizen-led ballot initiative to give workers paid sick time and raise Missouri's minimum wage to $15 per hour.
What changed The moment that pushed me to run.
After Proposition A passed with the support of 1.6 million Missourians and a 400,000-vote margin of victory, I was stunned to learn that Republicans in power, including my opponent, planned to undermine it rather than honor the voters' decision.
Republicans in Missouri government told me, “Just because the Missouri people vote for something doesn't mean it's okay.” Hearing that directly from elected officials made my blood boil.
I decided later that year to run for office because I am not like them.
I believe the people have the final say in Missouri — not representatives, not senators, and not even the Governor. ★ Workers first I supported Proposition A because I believe working people deserve dignity, paid sick time, and fair wages. ◆ Respect the voters I will never vote against a citizen ballot initiative passed by the people of Missouri. ♥ Focus on real issues I want the state focused on schools, roads, hospitals, and a stronger economy instead of political distractions.
