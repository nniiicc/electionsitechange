Meet the Candidate
Sylvain Doré is a renowned brain doctor and former professor of Anesthesiology and Neuroscience at the University of Florida.
Originally from Neuville, Québec, he served for thirteen years at UF, rising to the university's Board of Trustees before retiring to focus on his passions for politics, real estate, and philanthropy.
As an active Democrat, Sylvain believes that with the right changes and leadership, Florida can become the safest and most affordable state in the nation.
His core values — democracy, freedom, and compassion for all people — have guided every chapter of his life.
He is running to serve the families of Alachua and Marion Counties — from Gainesville to Ocala.

The Story Behind the Logo
A Brain, a Bridge, and a Name that Means Gold
As a trained brain doctor, Sylvain incorporated the brain into his logo as a symbol of intellect and innovation.
With a nod to artificial intelligence, it represents the core of his life's work.
Both the left and right sides of the brain show a connection — symbolizing the importance of bridging different perspectives, whether on the political spectrum or the educational spectrum, linking STEM with the Arts.
The color gold?
A simple translation of his last name: Doré — which means "golden" in French.
