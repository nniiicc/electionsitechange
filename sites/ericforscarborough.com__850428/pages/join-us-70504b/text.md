We can’t wait to organize together with you!
Here are four examples of great ways to get involved!
1) Talk with your neighbors Knocking doors, or “canvassing”, is one of the best ways for a campaign to reach voters.
And its fun!
Not sure you know how to start a conversation?
Don’t worry, we’ll teach you the skills you need to do a great job.
Remember, if you’ve ever made a friend, you already know how to canvass!
2) Host a “Meet the Candidate Night” This can be a low key living room conversation with friends and neighbors, a meeting with your social club, faith, or volunteer group, or whatever else you have in mind!
3) Represent us at events Whether tabling at summerfest, attending town council with a button on, or any other way you like to show up in the community, there’s a way to represent the campaign at something you might have already been planning on attending!
4) Write or Record a “Community Endorsement” People trust people they know, it’s only natural.
And the internet gives us a powerful tool to spread the word.
Writing a short paragraph or recording a short video explaining why you support this campaign is an incredibly strong way to help us get the mesage out!
Don’t see something that feels right to you here but still want to help out?
Don’t worry, there is always lots to do!
Check out our volunteer sign up form for more ideas, or share yours with us!
