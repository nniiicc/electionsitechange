Make California More Affordable
Scott comes from a family of farm and produce workers and engineers, and he knows the Bay Area cannot keep its promise if teachers, workers, seniors, and young families are pushed out.
That’s why, on the Union City Council and as Vice Chair of the Housing Authority of Alameda County, he has fought to build more housing, protect renters, and secure funding for homelessness services.
In the State Senate, he’ll fight to lower the cost of housing, healthcare, childcare, groceries, and energy.
