Responsible Innovation That Works for California
California can lead in innovation while protecting workers, privacy, children, and democracy.
Scott understands that innovation from Silicon Valley is critical to our growth.
He understands the government must support innovative ideas for California to lead in the industries of the future, but with clear guardrails that provide accountability, protect workers, privacy, children, and democracy.
Scott’s record and perspective
- Brings legal training, financial, and operational experience, and a practical understanding of our innovation economy.
- Firmly opposed to ICE collaboration and clear about the need for values and accountability in the private sector as well as government.
What Scott will fight for in the State Senate
- Support innovation in AI, biotech, and clean energy with strong public guardrails.
- Protect consumer privacy, prevent deepfakes and fraud, and create stronger safeguards for children online.
- Adopt clear rules for AI in employment, housing, lending, healthcare, education, and public services.
- Invest in apprenticeships, retraining, lifelong learning, and workforce adaptation so workers and residents are at the center of, and benefit from, technological advances and a changing economy.
- Reject waste, failed programs, and corporate giveaways that do not deliver results, and make sure public dollars actually improve people’s lives.
Scott wants California to lead in the industries of the future, and believes that innovation should expand opportunity and fairness, not concentrate power.
Innovation should work for workers, families, and consumers, not just for executives and shareholders.
