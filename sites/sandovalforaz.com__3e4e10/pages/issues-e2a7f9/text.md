I support a living wage, eliminating sub-minimum wages for tipped workers, and strengthening union jobs.
Hard-working Arizonans deserve Paid Family Medical Leave, affordable housing, and heat protections.
I support job creation through workforce development and public‑private partnerships.
AFFORDABLE HEALTHCARE FOR ALL No Arizonan should struggle to access or afford medical care.
I support lowering prescription drug prices and expanding behavioral and mental health programs.
I will continue protecting Medicaid expansion and strengthening rural healthcare networks.
ENVIRONMENT We must protect Arizona’s natural resources and implement strong water policies.
We must hold polluters accountable, invest in renewable energy, and combat climate change for our future.
I support responsible water management and protecting public lands, wildlife, and sacred cultural sites.
PROTECT VOTER RIGHTS Every eligible voter should have a fair and equal opportunity to participate in democracy.
We must protect voting rights and ensure the integrity and accessibility of our elections.
QUALITY & AFFORDABLE EDUCATION Every child deserves a fully funded P–20 public education, including universal preschool and higher educator pay.
I support restoring community college funding and expanding early childhood programs.
IMPROVING VETERAN CARE We have made promises to our service members that need to be kept.
Veteran care is often specialized and requires dedicated approaches that can’t always be found in the civilian healthcare system.
We must increase service members’ access to mental healthcare and improve its timeliness and quality.
SAFE GUN POLICIES I support safe and responsible gun ownership.
Too many have fallen due to senseless gun violence and mass shootings.
We must address gun violence as a mental health issue and establish common-sense gun reform.
This includes universal background checks and eliminating loopholes that allow guns to fall into the hands of criminals and stricter prohibitions for domestic violence misdemeanants.
