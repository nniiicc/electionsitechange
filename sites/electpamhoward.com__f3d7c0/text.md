A resident of Calhoun County since 1996, Pam has spent years working with local organizations, businesses, and community leaders to strengthen the district and create opportunities for future generations.
Her commitment to service is reflected through her leadership roles with numerous civic and advocacy organizations, including Executive Board President of 2nd Chance, a domestic violence shelter, Alabama Arise, the Alabama Democratic Party, Great Things in Jacksonville and several other community-focused initiatives.
As a business owner, Pam understands the challenges facing working families, entrepreneurs, and local employers.
She believes strong communities are built through economic opportunity, quality education, accessible healthcare, and transparent government that listens to the people it serves.
Pam is married, the proud mother of three children, and grandmother of six.
Her family and community values guide her approach to leadership every day.
Pam believes elected officials should be accessible, accountable, and responsive to the people they represent.
She sees public service as more than voting on legislation—it is about building relationships, solving problems, and working together to move District 40 forward.
With a proven record of community involvement and a passion for helping others, Pam Howard is committed to being a strong voice for the people of District 40 and working today for a better tomorrow.

Carol Miller has been a political activist since she was a teen.
Her campaign experience includes assisting her father with multiple successful political campaigns in Virginia, as well as managing an equally successful city council campaign in North Carolina.
Family has always been Carol’s highest priority, and her dedication to building a more secure, principled society for her grandchildren continues to inspire her involvement in public life and political advocacy.
Carol's degree in history with a focus on Germany’s interwar years has significantly informed her understanding of today’s national and geopolitical challenges and strengthened her commitment to supporting candidates and policies that move the country and the state of Alabama forward rather than backward.
For more than forty years, Carol has been devoted to building local, sustainable food systems.
She has founded multiple farmers markets, served on the boards of many others, as well as creating and marketing her own line of locally sourced value-added products.
In addition to her political, civic, and entrepreneurial work, Carol is also a former professional singer and vocal instructor, believing that the arts are one of our most effective agents for enacting social change.

Alongside his professional career, he remains deeply committed to supporting working families and strengthening the communities he calls home.

Passionate about labor activism, Bradley is dedicated to building worker solidarity by helping workers organize unions, fighting for living wages, and advocating for safer working conditions.
