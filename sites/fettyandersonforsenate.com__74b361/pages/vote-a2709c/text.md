Key dates October 13, 2026 Voter registration deadline Last day to register to vote or update your registration for the general election.
October 21 – 31, 2026 Early in-person voting Vote early at your county courthouse, annex, or designated community voting location during business hours.
No excuse needed.
October 28, 2026 Absentee ballot application deadline Last day for your county clerk to receive your absentee ballot application.
November 3, 2026 Election Day Polls are open 6:30 AM – 7:30 PM.
If you're in line by 7:30 PM, you can vote.

GoVoteWV: official election hub The WV Secretary of State's central resource for all election information.
Voting absentee West Virginia requires a valid excuse to vote absentee by mail, such as being out of the county during the voting period, illness or injury, work hours, or a disability.
If you qualify, apply online or through your county clerk by October 28, 2026.
Every registered voter can vote early in person October 21–31 with no excuse.
If absentee rules don't fit your situation, early voting is the easiest option.
What to bring: voter ID West Virginia requires an approved, unexpired photo ID to vote in person (early or on Election Day).
Accepted IDs include: West Virginia driver's license or WV ID card U.S. passport or military ID Student photo ID from a West Virginia high school or college Voter registration card that includes your photo Photo ID rules do not apply to absentee-by-mail voters.
See the Secretary of State's ID guide for the full list and exceptions.
Election rules can change.
Confirm details with the WV Secretary of State or your county clerk before you vote.
