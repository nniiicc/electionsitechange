Voting absentee
West Virginia requires a valid excuse to vote absentee by mail, such as being out of the county during the voting period, illness or injury, work hours, or a disability.
If you qualify, apply online or through your county clerk by October 28, 2026.
Every registered voter can vote early in person October 21–31 with no excuse.
If absentee rules don't fit your situation, early voting is the easiest option.
What to bring: voter ID
West Virginia requires an approved, unexpired photo ID to vote in person (early or on Election Day).
Accepted IDs include:
- West Virginia driver's license or WV ID card
- U.S. passport or military ID
- Student photo ID from a West Virginia high school or college
- Voter registration card that includes your photo
Photo ID rules do not apply to absentee-by-mail voters.
See the Secretary of State's ID guide for the full list and exceptions.
