Campaign Ethics Rules (Financial)
The campaign does not accept funds from:
- Any organization that purports to lobby for or advocate for the interests of any foreign nation, foreign group, or entity located outside the United States.
- Any organization that purports to lobby for or advocate for the interests of industries including weapons, alcohol, THC products, nicotine products, surveillance ware, mercenaries, incarceration, detention, security, or defense-related products/services.
- Any organization that purports to lobby for or advocate for the interests of industries including energy, healthcare, medical technologies, pharmaceuticals, AI, transportation, or the communication industry.
Donors are required to identify themselves in accordance with state and federal law.
