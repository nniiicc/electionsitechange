This is the first state requirement that ensures clean drinking water in California’s public school system.
Supported a historical Clean Drinking Water package that allocated hundreds of millions of dollars to improve California’s water infrastructure and ensure all areas of the state have access to safe drinking water.
Supported funding for groundwater clean up in the San Gabriel Basin.
San Gabriel Basin Water Quality Authority Act (AB 2163) This bill extends the sunset from July 1, 2030, to July 1, 2050 for the San Gabriel Basin Water Quality Authority (Authority).
San Gabriel Basin Water Quality Authority: Annual Pumping Right Assessment (AB 279) This bill raises the maximum amount the San Gabriel Basin Water Quality Authority (Authority) can impose for an annual pumping assessment from $10 to $20.
San Gabriel Basin Water Quality Authority: Annual Pumping Right Assessment (AB 279) Increases the maximum allowable annual pumping rights assessment to $20/acre foot for the SGB Water Quality Authority to ensure cost effective, long term planning, for the continued remediation of the basin through its sunset date of 2050.
