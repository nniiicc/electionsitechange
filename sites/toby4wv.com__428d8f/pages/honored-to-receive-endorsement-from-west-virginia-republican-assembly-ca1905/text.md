Home About Issues Events Calendar Donations & Volunteer Opportunities News Contact Toby Contact Toby Honored to Receive Endorsement from West Virginia Republican Assembly by Toby Heaney | Apr 29, 2024 | Blog With just 15 days till election day, I am thrilled to share some fantastic news with you all: I have been endorsed by the West Virginia Republican Assembly for the upcoming May 14th, 2024 primary election.
This endorsement is a tremendous honor and a testament to my unwavering commitment to Conservative values and principles.
The West Virginia Republican Assembly, a chapter under the esteemed National Federation of Republican Assemblies, is a grassroots organization dedicated to advancing the Conservative movement.
Their endorsement recognizes my steadfast support for a range of Conservative ideals, including advocating for smaller government, fiscal responsibility, the right to life, the Second Amendment, school choice, religious freedom, and other fundamental values that have long defined our great nation.
Receiving this endorsement is not only a personal honor but also a validation of my dedication to representing the interests and values of the people of West Virginia.
If elected to the West Virginia Legislature, I pledge to continue fighting tirelessly to uphold our constitutional rights and preserve the principles that have made America a beacon of freedom and opportunity.
I extend my sincere gratitude to the West Virginia Republican Assembly for their endorsement and for their unwavering commitment to advancing Conservative causes.
Together, we will work to ensure that our state remains a bastion of Conservative values and principles.
I urge all fellow Republicans and supporters of Conservative ideals to join me in voting for candidates who will uphold our principles and champion the Conservative agenda in West Virginia.
Thank you for your continued support and trust.
