Home About Issues Events Calendar Donations & Volunteer Opportunities News Contact Toby Contact Toby Toby Heaney Earns Aq Rating from NRA: A Testament to Commitment to Second Amendment Rights by Toby Heaney | Apr 11, 2024 | Blog As a staunch defender of our constitutional rights, I am thrilled to announce that I have received an Aq rating from the National Rifle Association (NRA).
This rating is a testament to my unwavering commitment to protecting the Second Amendment rights of law-abiding citizens in West Virginia and across our great nation.
The NRA is the premier advocate for Second Amendment rights in America, and their ratings are highly respected indicators of a candidate’s stance on gun rights issues.
To receive an Aq rating means that I have demonstrated strong support for the Second Amendment and have answered their questionnaire in a manner consistent with their values and principles.
(Only candidates who have a voting history are eligible for a higher rating.) For me, the right to keep and bear arms is not just a constitutional right; it is a fundamental freedom that must be vigorously defended against any attempts to infringe upon it.
Throughout my campaign for the West Virginia House of Delegates, I have been vocal about my support for the Second Amendment and my commitment to protecting the rights of law-abiding gun owners.
Receiving an Aq rating from the NRA is an honor, but it also comes with a responsibility to continue fighting for our Second Amendment rights in the legislature.
If elected, I will work tirelessly to advance pro-gun policies, oppose any efforts to restrict our rights, and ensure that West Virginians can exercise their constitutional freedoms without fear of government overreach.
I want to thank the NRA for their endorsement and for their tireless advocacy on behalf of gun owners across America.
With their support and the support of West Virginians who cherish their Second Amendment rights, I am confident that we can defend our freedoms and uphold the values that make our state and our nation great.
In closing, let me reaffirm my unwavering commitment to protecting the Second Amendment and preserving our constitutional freedoms.
Together, we can ensure that the right to keep and bear arms remains secure for generations to come.
Thank you for your support.
