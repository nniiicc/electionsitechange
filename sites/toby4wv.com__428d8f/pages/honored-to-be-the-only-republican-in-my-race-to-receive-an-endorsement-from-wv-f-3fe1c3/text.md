Home About Issues Events Calendar Donations & Volunteer Opportunities News Contact Toby Contact Toby Honored to be the only Republican in my race to receive an Endorsement from WV for Life by Toby Heaney | Apr 14, 2024 | Blog Dear Fellow West Virginians, I am thrilled to share some exciting news with you all.
In the midst of our campaign for the West Virginia House of Delegates, I received a truly humbling endorsement from none other than WV for Life.
This endorsement signifies more than just political support; it represents a shared commitment to defending the sanctity of life and upholding our conservative values.
WV for Life, a leading pro-life advocacy organization in our state, carefully evaluates candidates based on their dedication to life issues, integrity, and unwavering commitment to protecting the most vulnerable among us.
To be endorsed by such a respected organization is an honor beyond words.
For me, standing up for life has always been non-negotiable.
From conception to natural death, every human life is precious and deserving of protection.
This endorsement from WV for Life reaffirms my steadfast commitment to championing policies that reflect these values and ensure that West Virginia remains a place where life is cherished and defended at every stage.
I want to express my deepest gratitude to WV for Life for their endorsement and for their tireless advocacy on behalf of the unborn.
With their support, I am more determined than ever to fight for the rights of the most vulnerable members of our society and to uphold the values that make West Virginia great.
As we continue our campaign journey, I am inspired by the outpouring of support from West Virginians who share our commitment to life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness.
Together, we can bring positive change to our state and build a future where every life is valued and protected.
Thank you for standing with me in this important endeavor.
Let’s keep moving forward, united in our mission to make West Virginia a beacon of hope for life and liberty.
