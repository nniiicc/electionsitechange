I am running for the House of Delegates in West Virginia, District 76, driven by a commitment to service and a vision for a brighter future for our community.
In order to make this vision a reality, I am reaching out to you for support in various ways.
1.
Volunteers for Phone Calls and Door Knocking: Our grassroots movement relies on the power of connection and community engagement.
If you have the time and passion to support our campaign, volunteering for phone calls and door-knocking can make a significant impact.
Whether you’re a seasoned campaigner or a first-time volunteer, your dedication to reaching out to fellow community members is crucial.
Please consider joining our team and being the voice that helps us connect with the people we aim to serve.
If you are concerned about the awkwardness of cold calling.
Don’t be.
We use a different system for making phone calls.
2.
Campaign Contributions: Running a successful campaign requires financial resources, and your contributions play a vital role in fueling our efforts.
I am grateful for any amount you can contribute, understanding that each dollar brings us closer to our goals.
It’s important to note that campaign contributions are limited to $2,800 per individual, ensuring transparency and accountability in the democratic process.
However, there is no amount too small.
As you may be aware, I cashed in my change jar to pay my filing fees.
“Change for change.” Your support, regardless of the amount, will directly contribute to the success of our campaign, allowing us to reach a wider audience and share our vision for a stronger District 76.
How to Contribute: Volunteer for Phone Calls and Door Knocking: If you’re interested in volunteering your time, please reach out to toby.heaney2@gmail.com or fill out the volunteer form on my website.
Financial Contributions: Checks must be made out to “Friends of Toby Heaney.” You can either contribute using the secure link on my website or mail your contributions to: Friends of Toby Heaney 2452 Jamison Rd Fairview, WV 26570 Your support, no matter the amount, is genuinely appreciated.
Conclusion: Building a stronger future for District 76 requires a collective effort, and I am confident that, together, we can make a meaningful impact.
Your support, whether through volunteering or campaign contributions, is instrumental in amplifying our message and connecting with the community.
Let’s work together to bring positive change and representation to West Virginia.
Thank you for your support.
