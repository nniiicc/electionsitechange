Issues
If elected, I will be tough on repeat drug dealers.
I support raising penalties for repeat drug dealers to include extending incarceration.
I would also consider reclassifying drug dealing as a violent crime.
I will be championing a bill that allows instate tuition, for any state university, to be granted to all veterans, regardless of status.
Currently, there is a federal bill but it requires the veteran to use their GI bill.
Many veterans either do not have access to it anymore because they used it all or signed it over to their dependents.
I have already submitted a bill for drafting in the legislature that helps fight against predatory claims for veterans.
I will continue to champion this bill in the upcoming legislative session until we can get the protection our veterans deserve.
Occupational Licensing
As an economist teaching at WVU, I’m focused on promoting fiscal responsibility and driving real economic growth in West Virginia.
That includes ending unnecessary licensing requirements that hold back small businesses and entrepreneurs.
I also believe we’ve spent too long neglecting the infrastructure that keeps our communities strong.
If we fail to address it, we risk driving businesses and opportunities out of our state — and that’s something we simply can’t afford to ignore.
If elected, I will adamantly oppose any bills that restrict our right to bear arms.
If elected, I will support measures that prioritize alternatives to abortion, such as access to comprehensive healthcare, education, and resources for expectant mothers.
