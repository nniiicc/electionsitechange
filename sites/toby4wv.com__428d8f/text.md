About Me
Toby Heaney, a conservative candidate for the West Virginia Senate, born and raised in the great state of West Virginia, brings a wealth of experience and expertise to the political arena.
Armed with a Master’s degree in Economics, Toby is well-versed in economic principles that drive prosperity and growth.
His commitment to fiscal responsibility and a free-market approach sets him apart as a candidate dedicated to ensuring financial stability and opportunity for the people of West Virginia.
Beyond his academic achievements, Toby’s dedication to service extends to his military background.
As a proud veteran, he has demonstrated leadership, discipline, and a profound sense of duty to his country.
Toby’s military experience has instilled in him a deep understanding of the importance of national security and the sacrifices made by those who serve.
In his campaign, Toby advocates for policies that prioritize economic vitality, small government, and a strong national defense.
Rooted in conservative values, he aims to represent the interests and values of the people of West Virginia, drawing on both his academic expertise and military service to shape policies that will enhance the well-being of the community and the state as a whole.
Why Choose Toby
Toby Heaney’s legislative agenda is a comprehensive roadmap toward a more prosperous West Virginia.
Fueled by the dual perspectives of a combat veteran and an economist, Toby recognizes the urgent need for solutions to address the economic challenges our state faces.
His agenda is rooted in conservative values, emphasizing small government and robust economic growth as the key drivers for lasting change.
