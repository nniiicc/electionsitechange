Ace Parsi:
Why I'm Running
I fled an authoritarian regime to find a home in America—a journey that taught me the true value of freedom and democracy.
I didn’t know any English, and my family experienced severe poverty.
But I succeeded through the power of public schools, government assistance, faith communities, and neighbors who believed that the American Dream is a collective effort.
Now, as a father and a community leader, I am running to protect those same ideals of liberty and justice for the next generation.
Motivated by a deep faith that bridges divides and a fierce determination to build a culture of decency for all, I am ready to fight for a future where we rise together.
In an age of technological overload, let’s reclaim what it means to be human.
In an age of political reality TV, let’s reclaim what it means to be kind.
In an age of oligarchies and control, let’s reclaim what it means to be empowered.
In an age when the government is stepping back from its commitments, let’s reclaim what it means to be a united people.
