Step 2.
We stop elections from being bought:
Elections should be decided by voters.
Not by who spends the most money.
I will push for strict spending limits.
The Problem: Limits on how much people can donate are easily bypassed.
The Solution: By capping how much a campaign can SPEND, we remove the incentive for these endless money chases.
The Impact: Levels the field.
Spending caps make it easier for everyday citizens to run for office.
Not just the wealthy or the well-connected.
When campaigns are capped at a reasonable budget, you don’t need a massive fundraising machine to compete.
It brings the focus back to community connection.
Anyone with good ideas can have a real path to victory.
