Home About Issues Donate Petition to Qualify Contact Select Page Petition to Qualify Help Us Get on the Ballot I am running as an independent candidate for Georgia State House District 73.
In order to appear on the ballot, Georgia law requires our campaign to collect thousands of petition signatures from registered voters in the district.
This is the most important step in our campaign.
Without these signatures, voters will not have the opportunity to choose an independent voice.
Contact Petition to Qualify Why This Matters This process is about more than just signatures.
It is about giving District 73 a real choice.
Too often, elections are decided before voters ever have a say.
By signing this petition, you are helping ensure that our community has the opportunity to hear a different perspective and consider a new path forward.
Who Can Sign?
To sign the petition, you must: Be a registered voter in Georgia Live in State House District 73 If you meet these requirements, your signature can help us take the next step.
How to Sign Georgia requires wet signatures, which means signatures must be collected in person.
To make this easy: Email: signatures@baynardfor73.com A member of our campaign team will coordinate with you and meet at a time and place that is convenient for you.
We are actively meeting with voters across the district every week.
How You Can Help Even if you’ve already signed, you can still make a big impact: Share with Others Encourage friends, family, and neighbors in District 73 to sign.
Volunteer Join our team and help collect signatures in your community.
Fill out the form at the bottom of this page or email us at volunteer@baynardfor73.com!
Our Goal We must collect thousands of valid signatures by the deadline to qualify for the ballot.
Every signature moves us one step closer to giving District 73 a real choice.
