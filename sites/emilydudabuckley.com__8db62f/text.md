SENATOR RICK SCOTT See All Endorsements → A Fifth-Generation Floridian Fighting for Central Florida Families Emily Duda Buckley was born and raised in Central Florida, part of a family that has farmed Florida land for five generations.
She is an adoptive mother, a licensed foster parent, and a longtime advocate for Florida's most vulnerable through her work as a Guardian Ad Litem and her leadership with the Florida Association of Foster and Adoptive Parents and the 4Roots Foundation.
Emily is running for the Florida House because families across District 38 are squeezed by the rising cost of living, and they deserve a representative who believes government should live within its means like the rest of us.
Her guiding conviction is simple: serve as the district's representative, not its decision maker.
