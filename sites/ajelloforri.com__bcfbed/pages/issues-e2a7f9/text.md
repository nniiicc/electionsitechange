What Edie is Fighting For
Bills Edie has sponsored or co-sponsored this session and the budget fights she is leading at the State House.
Budget · Healthcare
Stronger Medicaid reimbursement rates
Stem the loss of primary care providers and frontline healthcare workers by raising Medicaid reimbursement — a proactive defense of Rhode Island's healthcare workforce against federal cuts.
H 7760 · End-of-Life
The Lila Manfield Sapinsley Compassionate Care Act
Allow terminally ill Rhode Islanders the option of a peaceful death on their own terms.
Each year more colleagues sign on.
Edie has introduced this bill every session and is hopeful it will reach the Governor's desk soon.
H 8083 · Criminal Justice
Expand expungement for non-violent felonies
Allow Rhode Islanders to expunge multiple non-violent felony convictions after ten years — so a long-ago mistake doesn't keep someone from housing, employment, or a fresh start.
H 8119 · Civil Liberties
The Fourth Amendment Is Not For Sale Act
Require law enforcement to obtain a warrant before purchasing personal location data from a broker — closing a loophole that has let agencies buy what they would otherwise need a judge's order to get.
H 7129 · Housing
Ban algorithmic rent-setting software
Outlaw the corporate software that lets landlords coordinate rent increases across whole neighborhoods — software that has been a major driver of soaring rents in Providence and across Rhode Island.
H 7034 · Housing
End the criminal-record question on rental applications
Prohibit landlords from asking prospective tenants about prior incarceration — one of the largest barriers keeping people from re-entering the community after they have served their time.
