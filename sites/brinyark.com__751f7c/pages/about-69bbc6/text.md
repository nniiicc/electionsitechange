Home About Working For You District Map Get Involved Donate Facebook MEET BRYAN Bryan Brinyark was born and raised in Tuscaloosa County and graduated from Central High School in Tuscaloosa in 1986.
He then attended the University of Alabama, graduating with a bachelor’s degree in 1990 and his law degree in 1993.
Brinyark has lived in the Samantha/Windham Springs area for decades where he raised his family on a small farm.
Bryan Brinyark is a devoted family man, a Christian, and a proud member of the Republican Party.
He has been a stalwart of the legal profession in Tuscaloosa while also serving his larger community.
He is a partner attorney with Brinyark & Frederick, serving clients in West Alabama.
As a lawyer, he is passionate about advocating for his clients and upholding the law.
Bryan is running for re-election to the Alabama House of Representatives District 16 seat because he is passionate about serving his community and making a positive impact.
He believes in limited government, fiscal responsibility, and protecting individual freedoms.
With his experience as a lawyer and his dedication to his community, Bryan is ready to continue as a strong and effective voice for the people of District 16.
