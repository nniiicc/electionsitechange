Burlington is the community that raised me.
I grew up here and graduated from our public schools.
I have spent my career at the intersection of public policy and communications, working in climate policy, local economic development, and as a staffer for Vermont’s congressional delegation.
I moved back to Vermont a few years ago, driven by a sense of urgency about the rise of fascism and authoritarianism, and a belief that Vermont would get through okay, thanks to our strong community ties, mutual aid networks, and grassroots organizing.
I’ve spent the past few years contributing to that infrastructure, and I’m ready to bring that same urgency to the statehouse.
I am running because we need a fighter.
Instead of navigating a for-profit healthcare system in collapse, we must implement universal healthcare, starting with universal primary care.
Instead of accepting a housing market that treats a house as a speculative asset, not a home, we must urgently build permanently affordable housing, and ban private equity and large corporate ownership of our housing.
Instead of watching our region’s public transit authority go broke and cut services that our community depends on, we must invest in quality public transit and modern infrastructure so that people don’t need to have a car just to get around.
And when we’re facing a rapid erosion of trust in our government and public institutions, we must invest in our public goods and services by taxing the rich.
That’s how we’ll secure free healthcare, affordable housing, and shared infrastructure.
This campaign is 100% people powered so I’ll always be accountable to this community.

Jeffrey's Platform Universal Healthcare Our for-profit healthcare system is collapsing.
We need to finally implement Act 48 by securing sustainable, progressive funding to make sure healthcare is a right, not a privilege tied to your job or income.
A universal healthcare system will not only help address affordability concerns, it will improve the wellbeing of every Vermonter.
AFFORDABLE HOUSING Our housing crisis can’t be left up to developers to solve.
We need to pursue policies like taxing second homes, luxury properties, and short-term rentals and direct the revenue into social housing and permanently affordable homes .
Burlington’s diverse community fabric is our biggest strength and we can’t let more people get pushed out because their housing is treated as an investment asset.
RELIABLE PUBLIC TRANSIT We must invest in public transit as essential infrastructure that lowers the cost of living, connects communities, and reduces emissions.
For too long, Montpelier has hurt our region’s future by refusing to sustainably fund Green Mountain Transit.
