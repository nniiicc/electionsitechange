Meet Chris Dedicated public servant.
Accomplished law enforcement professional.
Proud father.
Chris Lancia has spent his life serving and protecting the people of his community—with integrity, courage, and heart.
Born and raised in Bridgeport, Connecticut , Chris now resides in Milford with his two children.
He is a proud veteran of the United States Coast Guard , where he developed the discipline and sense of duty that continue to guide his life today.
Following his military service, Chris became a Federal Law Enforcement Officer , using his training and experience to help ensure the safety of others.
He also brings leadership and innovation to the private sector as an OSP Manager at TNI , a top provider of fiber networks to major cable and communications companies.
Chris is a committed lifelong learner.
He holds a bachelor’s degree in Emergency Management from Post University and is a graduate of both the Federal Law Enforcement Training Center and the Maritime Law Enforcement Training Program .
Above all, Chris is a devoted father.
His son, Christopher (13) , is an accomplished athlete who plays football for Fairfield Youth Football and wrestles with the South Side team in Bridgeport.
His daughter, Angela (11) , is a rising star in competitive sports, already making her mark at the national level.
Chris cherishes time with his children and sees his role as a father as his greatest calling.
Chris is running for Congress because he believes Connecticut—and America—deserves better .
His decision to run was not made lightly, but came after witnessing policies that deeply troubled him: biological men in women’s sports, tampons in boys’ restrooms, and Americans being forced to choose between a vaccine or their livelihood .
He believes in standing up for common sense , parental rights , and individual freedoms .
He’s ready to fight for working families, law and order , and the values that make America strong .
Chris Lancia is running to give the people of Connecticut a voice—and to help President Donald Trump finish what he started: putting America First .
Why I’m Running for Congress I didn’t wake up one morning aspiring for a title or a career in Washington.
I’m running for Congress because I love this country—and I refuse to stand by while it’s weakened by the very people elected to protect it.
Too many career politicians have forgotten who they serve.
I haven’t—and I never will.
I believe in putting America First —not China, not Ukraine, not the United Nations.
Our policies, priorities, and promises must begin at home, with the American people at the center, every single time.
I’m running because I believe that border security is national security .
A nation without borders cannot remain sovereign.
We must end the chaos at our border that’s flooding our communities with crime, drugs, and fear.
The American people deserve safety, order, and leadership that stands with them—not against them.
I’m running to protect our children and preserve their future.
As a parent, I don’t need a degree to know that boys don’t belong in girls’ locker rooms , and that biological men should not compete in women’s sports .
I believe parents—not government bureaucrats—should raise our children and make decisions about their education and values.
In Congress, I’ll fight for lower taxes, a balanced budget , and an end to the reckless spending that’s driving up inflation and draining your wallets.
Like every American family, our government needs to learn how to live within its means.
Fiscal responsibility shouldn’t be optional—it should be expected.
I’m also committed to real reform in Washington .
That means enacting term limits, rooting out corruption, and ending the weaponization of our government.
Every citizen deserves equal treatment under the law— no more double standards, no more political targeting , and no more censorship.
I will always stand up for the Second Amendment , support our law enforcement, and restore accountability so that criminals fear breaking the law—not citizens fearing injustice .
This campaign is more than politics—it’s a mission.
A mission to restore faith, freedom, and fairness to a country that has given so many of us the opportunity to dream and thrive.
I’m not running to manage America’s decline.
I’m running to lead her comeback.
Because America is still worth fighting for —and together, we will take it back.
Thank you for your trust, your prayers, and your support.
God bless you, and God bless the United States of America.
This isn’t just a campaign.
