Families First Before I cast a vote, I'll ask one thing: is this good for families here at home?
What we pay for groceries, what healthcare costs, how we fund our schools.
On all of it, the people of Callaway County come first.
Not the party.
Not the lobbyists.
Community Always Around here, neighbors take care of neighbors.
That's how I was raised, and it's how I'll do the job in Jefferson City.
I'll stand up for our schools, our main streets, the folks who answer the call in an emergency, and the family farms that have always held this county together.
Callaway County roots Rooted Here.
Running for Us.
A lifelong Callaway County voice I've lived in Callaway County my whole life.
I'm running for the Missouri House to stand up for the people who keep this state running: working families and farmers, union members, our public schools, and the first responders all across Mid-Missouri.
To me that means keeping rural healthcare within reach, funding our schools the way they deserve, bringing down everyday costs, helping local businesses get by, and backing the people who keep us safe.
Working Families Family Farms Public Schools Rural Healthcare First Responders Our Home: Callaway County I know this district, because it's home House District 49 sits right in the heart of Callaway County.
It's the courthouse square in Fulton and the growing town of Holts Summit.
It's the farms down in the river bottoms and the little towns scattered in between.
Westminster and William Woods are here too, just across the river from the Capitol.
Most of all it's the farmers, teachers, tradespeople, and first responders who keep the place going.
People here still look after one another, and I'll carry that same way of doing things to Jefferson City.

Affordable, Accessible Healthcare Out here, getting care is about more than the bill.
It's the distance, and whether there's a doctor close by at all.
Nobody in Callaway County should put off care they need.
Strong Public Schools Our public schools hold these communities together.
They're where our kids grow up and where the whole town turns out on a Friday night.
Lowering Costs for Families The price of groceries, gas, and nearly everything else keeps climbing while paychecks stay flat.
Families need real help, not talking points.
Supporting Local Communities Small businesses and local workers keep our towns going.
I'll fight for them, and for the roads and reliable internet they need to compete.
Fair & Responsible Government Public service ought to mean serving the public, not the special interests.
You deserve someone who's honest with you and answers for their votes.
First Responders & Law Enforcement Our deputies, firefighters, EMTs, and corrections officers run toward trouble for the rest of us.
They've earned fair pay, real training, and the gear to get home safe.
