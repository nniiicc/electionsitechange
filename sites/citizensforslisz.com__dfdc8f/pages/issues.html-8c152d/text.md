Affordable and Accessible Healthcare Out here, getting healthcare isn't only a question of cost.
It's how far you have to drive and whether there's a doctor close by at all.
No family should put off care because the nearest clinic is an hour away or because they can't cover the visit.
Jessica will work to keep our rural clinics and hospitals open, bring costs down, and make sure a medical bill never breaks a family's budget.
Strong Public Schools In a small town, the school is a whole lot more than a building.
It's where our kids grow up, where neighbors run into each other, and where everybody shows up on a Friday night.
Jessica will fight to fund our schools the way they deserve, stand behind our teachers, keep the big decisions in local hands, and give every kid a fair shot no matter their zip code.
Lowering Costs for Working Families Families here are paying more for groceries, gas, and just about every necessity, and paychecks haven't kept up.
Jessica will push back on the policies driving those costs higher and fight for a tax system that doesn't dump the bill on working people.
Supporting Local Communities Small businesses and the people who work in them are the backbone of our local economy.
Jessica will invest in them and in the basics they need to compete: better roads, and the reliable internet that rural Missouri has done without for too long.
Our part of the state shouldn't keep getting left behind.
Fair and Responsible Government You deserve a representative who's straight with you and does right by this community.
Public service should be about the public, not the special interests writing the checks.
Jessica will be honest about her votes and answer to the people who sent her there.
First Responders and Law Enforcement Our deputies, firefighters, EMTs, and corrections officers do hard, dangerous work so the rest of us are safe.
Jessica will make sure they get fair pay, the right training, and the equipment to do the job, plus the support it takes to recruit and keep good people on the job.
Support These Priorities Families first.
Community always.
