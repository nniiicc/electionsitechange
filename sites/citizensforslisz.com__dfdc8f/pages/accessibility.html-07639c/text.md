Accessibility Statement
This campaign is for everyone in District 49, and that includes voters with disabilities.
We want this website to work for as many people as possible, and we're working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.
What we've done
We've worked to provide keyboard navigation, visible focus indicators, descriptive text alternatives for images, sufficient color contrast, clearly labeled forms, and a "skip to content" link.
The site is designed to work across phones, tablets, and computers.
Ongoing work
Accessibility is an ongoing effort, and some content may not yet fully meet our goals.
We welcome your feedback and will make reasonable efforts to fix issues that are reported to us.
