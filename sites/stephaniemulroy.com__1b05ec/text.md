Meet Stephanie
A proven community leader fighting for our families
Stephanie Mulroy is a small business owner, wife, and mother of four with deep roots in the Wachusett region.
For more than two decades, she has woven herself into the fabric of the Wachusett community while building a successful family dental practice.
As a former member and chair of the Board of Selectmen, Stephanie is ready to bring her common-sense leadership and dedication to fiscal responsibility to Beacon Hill.

The District
Representing the 1st Worcester District
The 1st Worcester District consists of the towns of Holden, Paxton, Princeton, Rutland, and Westminster, all in the county of Worcester.
These towns share strong community values and face common challenges that require experienced, dedicated representation.
From supporting quality schools and local businesses to ensuring smart growth that communities can sustain, Stephanie is committed to fighting for the issues that matter most to our neighbors.
