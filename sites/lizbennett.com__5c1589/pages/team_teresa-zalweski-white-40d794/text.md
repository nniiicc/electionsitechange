Teresa Zalweski-White
Teacher
I know that Liz will fight for our students.
When I was teaching in Cedar Rapids, Liz spoke to my students on multiple occasions, both locally and in the rotunda.
She always made time to make sure they felt heard and seen, and they were at ease talking to her about their concerns for their community.
She knows that her constituents don’t just include the Iowans at the polls, but also the young people needing lawmakers who will fight for them too.
Our students deserve to be safe, supported, and represented, and I know Liz will work tirelessly to make sure they are.
She’s a strong leader and active in the community, and I couldn’t be more excited to see her run for senate.
