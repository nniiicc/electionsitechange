Chellie Pingree is running to build an economy and health care system that works for everyday Mainers—not powerful corporations.
She’s fighting to lower prescription drug costs, protect reproductive freedom, strengthen Social Security and Medicare, tackle climate change with clean energy jobs, and ensure working families can earn a livable wage.
Grounded in her background as an organic farmer and small business owner, Chellie is fighting to protect Maine’s people, natural resources, and values while delivering practical, progressive solutions that improve lives.
Throughout her time in Congress, Chellie has stood up to the Trump Administration and its infringements on our basic rights and freedoms.
Whether pushing back on his backward tariff policies or defending our institutions from being defaced with Trump’s ego, Chellie is committed to standing up to Trump and putting Mainers first.
Meet Chellie Pingree Chellie Pingree has lived on the island of North Haven in Maine since her teenage years.
She has farmed organically, raised three children, and built small businesses.
She went on to serve as tax – assessor (a job no one else wanted), school board chair, and state senator from a largely Republican district.
Elected to Congress in 2008, she -. was the first woman to be elected to represent her district in Congress.
In Washington, she has focused on expanding opportunities for working families, supporting veterans, and improving access to health care as a member of the Appropriations Committee.
Throughout her career, she has emphasized practical problem-solving and accountability rooted in her small-community background.
