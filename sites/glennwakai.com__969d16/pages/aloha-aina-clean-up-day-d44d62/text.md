Wakai spearheads this annual event, drives a flat bed truck around the community, and pulls bulky items out of constituents’ homes.
The effort has resulted in about 50-tons of debris being properly disposed, and recyclable items turned into checks amounting to $8,500 for Moanalua High School athletics.
“There is nothing glamorous about Aloha Aina Day.
Glenn is not hiding in the shade directing traffic, he is out there driving the truck and hauling scrap metal out of people’s yards.
Then after he does the heavy lifting, he pays us.
His checks for recyclable items have helped to pay for an assortment of athletic expenses.” ~ Joel Kawachi , MHS Athletics Director CONNECT ONLINE © Glenn Wakai State House (D) All Rights Reserved.
