May 18, 2026 Dennis Malloy Amending the Constitution is a Big Deal For nearly 250 years NH has not had a state-wide income tax and I’m not about to vote for one.
Thursday May 16, the House defeated a bill that would amend the constitution restricting this and future legislatures from adopting a state wide income tax of any kind.
Income tax proposals have been consistently defeated since the founding of this state, but this time there were those who wanted to codify the income tax question in the constitution, a measure that would better be defined by law.
