I am running for my seventh term in the NH General Court.
I look forward to meeting you on the campaign trail as I knock on doors in Greenland and Rye.
I’m honored and proud to serve the residents of Greenland and Rye and encourage you to get in touch with me with your ideas for legislation and your concerns during the 2027-2028 term.
My priorities include affordable housing, clean drinking water, supporting public education, health care and ensuring that Greenland and Rye’s needs are heard and met in Concord.
My work on the Ways & Means Committee as deputy ranking member assures that New Hampshire’s state budget is balanced and that towns and taxpayers are protected from state expenses that are “downshifted” to local communities.
About I believe in state and local community service and continue to donate my time and energies to help make Greenland, Rye and the state of NH the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
As both a state and local elected official I will continue to serve to the best of my abilities.
