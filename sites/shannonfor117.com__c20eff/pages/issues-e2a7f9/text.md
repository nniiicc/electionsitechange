Issues
Public Safety
I have the utmost respect for those in our community who dedicate their lives to keeping our residents safe.
Their jobs are not easy, and we need to do our best to support them so they can support us.
I am proud to have been endorsed by the UPFFA in the past and commit to working together for the best interests of our district.
Education
Ensuring every child receives a quality education is vital to our community's success.
I will work tirelessly to support our schools, empower educators, and provide our children with the tools they need to excel in an ever-changing world.
Economic
Development
Working in my family's local small business, I know firsthand the challenges and opportunities facing businesses in our community.
I will continue to advocate for policies that support entrepreneurship, encourage business growth, and create more job opportunities right here in our district.
Environment
Protecting our environment is crucial for the health and well-being of our community.
I understand the importance of responsible stewardship of our natural resources.
As a member of the Environment Committee, I will continue to promote sensible environmental policies that preserve our beautiful surroundings for future generations to enjoy.
LGBTQ+ Rights
I am a member of Milford Pride, and have been an advocate for LGBTQ+ rights as long as I can remember.
It is important to me that we support those who have historically been treated unfairly in our society, and as a member of this community, I know the challenges we have to overcome on a daily basis in order to live our own lives.
I will continue to work to expand rights for our community, and forge alliances across the aisle to highlight the importance of this work.
