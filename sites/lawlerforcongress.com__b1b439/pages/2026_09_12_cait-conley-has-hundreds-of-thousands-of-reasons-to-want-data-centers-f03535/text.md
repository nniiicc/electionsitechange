Home About Issues Endorsements News Volunteer Signs Vote Shop Donate Donate News CAIT CONLEY HAS “HUNDREDS OF THOUSANDS OF REASONS” TO WANT DATA CENTERS IN THE HUDSON VALLEY September 12, 2026 A new 30-second spot from the Lawler for Congress campaign pairs Conley’s own words on data centers with the $328,283 her financial disclosure shows two artificial intelligence companies have paid her since January 2025, all documented on CaitConley.AI Pearl River, NY — September 12, 2026 — The Lawler for Congress campaign released a new 30-second ad, “Pay Day,” on Cait Conley’s financial relationship with the artificial intelligence industry and her support for building data centers in the Hudson Valley.
The spot begins airing today across broadcast, cable, streaming, and digital as part of the campaign’s $5 million fall advertising program.
Pay Day will have over seven figures behind it as it airs on broadcast, cable, digital, and other media.
Watch “Pay Day” here.
The ad opens with Conley’s own words: “We need data centers.” The narrator answers: “Cait Conley has hundreds of thousands of reasons why she wants to build data centers in the Hudson Valley.
Conley took a massive six-figure payday from the AI companies she steered contracts to when she worked for Biden.
No wonder Cait Conley wants them.” Conley closes the case herself: “We need data centers.
We’re not going to get rid of them.” Cait Conley’s full record is available at CaitConley.AI , which documents Conley’s ties to the artificial intelligence industry and the more than $300,000 she has been paid to consult for it.
The number behind the ad comes from Conley’s own filing.
Her personal financial disclosure, filed with the Clerk of the House on May 19, 2026 , reports $328,283 in salary and consulting fees since January 2025 from two artificial intelligence companies, Hidden Level and Primer.
Both companies advertise partnerships with Palantir , and both are federal defense contractors.
The Intercept and Politico first reported Conley’s work for the two firms in February .
She filed her May financial disclosure only after seeking an extension that would have kept it out of public view until August 13, weeks after the Democratic primary, and only after the Hudson Valley Digger reported on the delay .
She has declined to describe what she does for either company, telling voters she signed nondisclosure agreements .
Conley spent the Biden administration in the national security roles her employers sell into , first as a director for counterterrorism on the National Security Council staff and then as a senior adviser at the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency, part of the Department of Homeland Security, which she left in January 2025.
Primer’s website says its AI platforms “support DHS missions.” After Conley left her high-ranking job in the Biden White House and the Department of Homeland Security, she cashed in with a massive six-figure payday from the same AI/big-tech companies who raked in millions in government contracts during her time in the Administration, and whose lobbyists, lawyers, and executives she repeatedly squired around the White House, granting special access and insider perks.
“Cait Conley looked straight into a camera and said, ‘We need data centers, and we are not going to get rid of them.’ Her own financial disclosure shows she’s on the payroll of two AI companies that have paid her $328,283 since the start of last year,” said Lawler for Congress Campaign Manager Ciro Riccardi .
“She helped steer federal contracts to these companies while overseeing their work, and is now profiting with massive contracting gigs after leaving the Biden White House.” Cait Conley owes Hudson Valley voters direct answers to basic questions: What does she do for Hidden Level and Primer in exchange for $328,283?
Does she support Governor Hochul’s moratorium on new large data centers, yes or no?
If elected, will she recuse herself from legislation affecting the artificial intelligence companies that pay her?
Why did she try to keep her financial disclosure out of public view until after the primary?
“While Mike Lawler is fighting to protect our community.
Cait Conley is fighting to protect her fat AI company paychecks,” Riccardi said .
