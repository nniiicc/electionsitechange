Follow all instructions given by campaign staff, security personnel, and event organizers.

Failure to present valid photo ID upon request may result in denial of admission.
Be prepared for security checks upon entry.
Familiarize yourself with emergency exits and procedures in case of an emergency.

Respect privacy and avoid obstructing media personnel.

No tripods or camera stands unless you are a credentialed member of the press.
By admission to the event, you are acknowledging the rules listed above and agree to them.
Failure to abide by the rules may be grounds for removal.
