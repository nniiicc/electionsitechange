Pearl River, NY — October 2, 2026 — A new survey of New York’s 17th Congressional District shows Congressman Mike Lawler leading Democrat Cait Conley 50% to 47%.
The poll, conducted by McLaughlin & Associates on behalf of Lawler for Congress, surveyed 400 likely general election voters from September 28 to 30 and has a margin of error of plus or minus 4.9%.
Lawler holds 92 percent of Republicans, wins unaffiliated and third-party voters 52% to 40%, and takes 20% of registered Democrats, in a sample where registered Democrats outnumber Republicans 44% to 31%, a number he has held in every election.
Lawler is the only candidate in the race with a net-positive image, 50% favorable to 46% unfavorable.
Conley is underwater at 42% favorable to 48% unfavorable.
Among unaffiliated voters, she is at 36% favorable to 52% unfavorable.
At the top of the ticket, Republican Bruce Blakeman leads Governor Kathy Hochul 51% to 47% among NY-17 voters and carries unaffiliated voters 55% to 37%.
The results track a previous independent public survey of the district, a co/efficient poll of 1,292 likely voters released July 23 that had Lawler ahead 50% to 45%, winning unaffiliated voters 54% to 39% and taking 23% of Democrats.
“Mike Lawler is in pole position with a month to go.
Cait Conley is underwater with the district as a whole, worse with independents, and that will only get worse as more people learn about her shady pay-for-play, no-show job with AI defense contractors.
People in the Hudson Valley don’t want to be represented by a carpetbagger who doesn’t own a home here, doesn’t pay taxes here, and wants to raise their taxes by nearly $4,000,” said Lawler for Congress campaign manager Ciro Riccardi .
