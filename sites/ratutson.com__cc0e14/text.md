Who I Am I’m R.
A.
Tutson — Rhian, to most people.
Before I put a word on the page, I spent years working inside the systems I now write about: as a Florida Highway Patrol Trooper, and later as a school investigator handling misconduct, ADA, and Title IX cases within a major Florida school district.
I hold dual bachelor’s degrees in Criminal Forensic Studies and Criminal Justice, a master’s in Criminal Justice, and an anthropology minor — an academic background that shows up on every page of Scarred Systems .
I write fiction the way I worked cases: closely, carefully, and without looking away from the hard parts.

The Book Scarred Systems — Book One The Fourth Floor LeighAnne Rhodes is a profiler.
She reads people for a living, builds the case from the evidence, and never looks away — except from herself.
When she returns to the fourth floor of FDLE after a near-fatal childbirth and an emergency hysterectomy, she tells herself the hardest part is over.
But a new partner sees her too clearly, a case mirrors her own life, and someone inside the building has been quietly building a file on her.
The Fourth Floor is the story of a woman who finally becomes the investigator in her own case.
