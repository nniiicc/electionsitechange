Mail-in Ballots Voting by mail has never been easier or more secure in Pennsylvania.
Any qualified voter can request a mail-in ballot without a reason in order to vote in the coming election.
This option is perfect for those who work, are disabled, want a flexible option, or college students who will be away from home in November.
Request Your Mail-in Ballot Once you apply for an absentee ballot, your ballot will be sent to the address provided along with instructions on how to accurately fill it out.
Make sure to complete your ballot as soon as possible and put it in the mail or any of the conveniently located drop boxes in our area.
