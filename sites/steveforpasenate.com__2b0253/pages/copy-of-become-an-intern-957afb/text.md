Become an Organizing Fellow
The Santarsiero for State Senate Campaign is now accepting applications for our 2022 Fellowship program.
The goal of the program is to train individuals interested in a career in electoral politics and prepare them for future field organizing staff positions.
Successful applicants for this fellowship will split their time between both the campaign headquarters and the 10th Senatorial District.
Upon completion of the program, fellows will have gained an understanding of the electoral process and the experience necessary to pursue paid employment in campaign work.
This program is open to both students and non-students.
Requirements
- Full Time 4-5 days per week averaging about 40 hours
- Part Time 2-4 days per week averaging about 20 hours
- Interest in pursuing a career in professional campaign work
Benefits
- Field Organizer training
- Travel reimbursement
- College course credit where applicable
About Steve Santarsiero
Steve is a lawyer, a former teacher, and former State Representative, and a practicing attorney.

About State Senate District 10
The 10th District covers a large portion of Bucks County, a national bellwether county.
Steve is looking to be the first Democrat to be re-elected to the seat, after flipping the seat blue for the first time in 2018.
One Senate seat can be the difference between a simple majority and a veto-proof supermajority.
