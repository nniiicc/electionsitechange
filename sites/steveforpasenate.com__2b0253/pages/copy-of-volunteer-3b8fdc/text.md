Become an Intern The Santarsiero for State Senate Campaign is now accepting applications for our 2022 Internship program.
The goal of the program is to provide experience to those interested in learning about the process of running for office.
Upon completion of the program, interns will have gained a nuanced understanding of campaign work at the state level.
This program is open to both students and non-students.
Requirements Part-Time 2-# days per week averaging about 15-# hours Interest in pursuing a career in professional campaign work Benefits College course credit where applicable Travel reimbursement Flexible Scheduling Letter of recommendation upon request About Steve Santarsiero Steve is a lawyer, a former teacher, and former State Representative, and a practicing attorney.
He is also a leader on education, infrastructure investment, environmental protection, women’s health issues, and gun violence prevention.
About State Senate District 10 The 10th District covers a large portion of Bucks County, a national bellwether county.
Steve is looking to be the first Democrat to be re-elected to the seat, after flipping the seat blue for the first time in 2018.
One Senate seat can be the difference between a simple majority and a veto-proof supermajority.
