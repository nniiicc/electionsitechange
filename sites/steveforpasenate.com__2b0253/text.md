After witnessing the September 11th attacks from the window of his law office in Newark, New Jersey, he decided to seek a new career in teaching as a way to serve the community.
After a couple years of night classes at Holy Family University, Steve received his state certification and was hired as a social studies teacher at Bensalem High School.
At BHS, he worked hard to give the next generation a strong foundation in history and civics to help them succeed and participate fully as citizens of our country.
