The Fight for Our Residents
The 2025 legislative session brought a grave crisis to the doorstep of our state.
The majority party attempted to force the closure of critical Residential Habilitation Centers (RHCs).
They targeted facilities that provide 24/7 care to our most profoundly disabled adults.
This reckless plan ignored the harsh reality of our current infrastructure.
It left vulnerable residents facing the terrifying prospect of homelessness or death.
The state lacked any viable community care alternative.
This failure placed our most vulnerable neighbors in immediate danger.
The opposition viewed these human beings as mere spreadsheet line-items.
They prioritized irresponsible budgeting over the lives of Washington citizens.
I saw the danger clearly, and I heard the cries for help from families who feared for their loved ones.
The Root of the Crisis
I stood on the House floor to address this systemic breakdown.
I realized that this issue extended far beyond a single piece of legislation.
The core problem involves a fundamental failure in our state's ability to execute basic functions.
Closing these facilities without a safety net is an abdication of our moral duty.

A Victory for Accountability
I fought this closure with every tool at my disposal.
I worked tirelessly to stop the destruction of these essential facilities.
We successfully killed the Senate version of the closure bill.
My efforts forced the legislature to keep these facilities open.
We ensured that no further closures could occur without a concrete, safe plan in place, fighting to protect the Rainier and Yakima Valley schools.
This victory belonged to the families and the residents.
We proved that we can stand against reckless policy.
We proved that we can protect those who cannot protect themselves.
