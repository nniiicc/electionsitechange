Donate Now Log In All Posts Search Exciting News: I’ve Been Endorsed by the Center for Freethought Equality PAC Arienne Childrey Apr 5 1 min read I’m proud to announce that I have received the endorsement of the Center for Freethought Equality PAC.
This organization works to protect the constitutional principle of separation of church and state and supports candidates committed to evidence-based policies that treat all Ohioans fairly — regardless of religious belief or nonbelief.
I am honored by their support.
This endorsement reinforces my commitment to equality, science-based solutions, bodily autonomy, and LGBTQ+ rights.
In District 84, we need leaders who ensure our government serves every resident fairly — people of all faiths and none — without imposing any particular religious viewpoint or showing favoritism.
In Ohio, we need leadership that upholds our constitutional values so that all faiths and none can flourish without government interference or favoritism.
If you believe in a stronger future for our district with common-sense solutions that deliver real results for working families, I invite you to join our campaign.
