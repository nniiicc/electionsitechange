It’s time to stop turning a deaf ear to victims and stand up for justice.
Following the 2015 Mother Emanuel AME Church massacre, Representative Gilliard became the lead House sponsor of the Senator Clementa C.
Pinckney Hate Crimes Act to close South Carolina’s accountability gap.
Sentencing Enhancements: Clear state-level penalty enhancements for violent, bias-motivated crimes.
Faster Justice: Gives local law enforcement and state prosecutors a direct path to accountability.
Mandatory Tracking: Requires all 46 counties to report hate crimes and identify rising hotspots.

Second Chances: Supporting re-entry programs, fair hiring, and financial literacy for returning citizens.
