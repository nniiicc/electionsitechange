Justice & Safety
South Carolina is one of only two states without a state hate crime law.
It’s time to stop turning a deaf ear to victims and stand up for justice.
Following the 2015 Mother Emanuel AME Church massacre, Representative Gilliard became the lead House sponsor of the Senator Clementa C.
Pinckney Hate Crimes Act to close South Carolina’s accountability gap.
Sentencing Enhancements: Clear state-level penalty enhancements for violent, bias-motivated crimes.
Faster Justice: Gives local law enforcement and state prosecutors a direct path to accountability.
Mandatory Tracking: Requires all 46 counties to report hate crimes and identify rising hotspots.
Economic Dignity
Raising the minimum wage isn’t just moral—it’s smart economics.
Putting money in working people’s pockets drives our local economy and raises people out of poverty.
For over 15 years as President of USW Local 863, Representative Gilliard fought on plant floors and at bargaining tables for fair pay, safe conditions, and health benefits.
Raising the Floor: Championing state legislation to establish a $17/hr living wage in South Carolina.
Worker's Rights: Fighting to advanced organized labor in SC; Equal Pay For Women; Safety & Dignity in the Workplace.
Second Chances: Supporting re-entry programs, fair hiring, and financial literacy for returning citizens.
Housing Rights
Growth shouldn’t push out the people who built these communities.
We need development that lifts people up, not replaces them.
As Charleston and North Charleston grow, historic Black and working-class neighborhoods face extreme tax hikes, predatory development, and displacement pressure.
Tax & Housing Relief: Pushing for historic easement for urban & rural Gullah lands and senior homestead protections.
Affordable Housing Mandates: Requiring true affordable and workforce housing in new projects by providing State incentives to Cities & Counties.
Port Accountability: Addressing noise, air pollution, and traffic impacts & the creation of Economic Development program for impacted neighborhoods
