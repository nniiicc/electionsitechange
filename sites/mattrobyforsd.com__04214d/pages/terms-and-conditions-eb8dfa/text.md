Subscribers may receive text messages including, but not limited to: campaign updates and news, volunteer opportunities, event invitations and reminders, voting information (including dates, times, and polling locations for upcoming elections), get-out-the-vote reminders, fundraising appeals, and other communications related to the Friends of Matt Roby campaign.

Help.

Opt-Out.
To stop receiving text messages entirely, reply STOP to any message you receive from us.
You will receive a one-time confirmation that you have been unsubscribed, after which you will receive no further messages unless you opt in again.
