Committed to Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135.
Joe Poplawski is focused on the issues that directly impact Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135 families—strengthening infrastructure, supporting public safety, promoting economic growth, and ensuring responsible use of taxpayer dollars.
Here’s where he stands.
Homelessness Support Joe believes Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135 must do more to support its homeless community.
That's why when elected he will introduce the Stable Homes, Strong Communities Act .
He will advocate for funding to create a Homeless Launch Program—providing small, low-cost dwellings with a portion of rent set aside in “launch accounts” to help residents transition into stable housing.
The program will also include mental health and addiction recovery services to support long-term independence and self-sufficiency.
Affordable Housing Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135 families are being priced out, with rent for a 3-bedroom apartment jumping from $1,999 to $2,999 since COVID. ​ That's why when elected Joe will introduce the Stable Homes, Strong Communities Act .
Joe supports encouraging municipalities to reduce square footage requirements for homes and lots by one-third across all zoning districts, making housing more affordable without sacrificing quality of life.
He also backs smarter parking rules and growing local wages through revitalized manufacturing.
These changes will help close the cost-income gap and keep Pennsylvania House of Representatives District 135 livable for all.
Infrastructure Improvements Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135 deserves safe roads, strong bridges, and well-kept parks.
This is why when elected Joe will introduce the Smart Standards for Safe Infrastructure Act .
This will prioritize smart investments in infrastructure while supporting growth through revitalized manufacturing and more affordable housing.
These efforts will expand our tax base and help keep Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135 running smoothly for families, workers, and businesses alike.
Fiscal Responsibility Joe is committed to transparent, responsible budgeting that balances immediate needs with long-term goals.
By carefully managing Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135 finances, he will ensure taxpayer dollars are used effectively to support essential services, maintain infrastructure, and invest in sustainable growth.
His approach will help build a strong foundation for the district's future prosperity .
Economic Development Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135 was built on steel... and it’s time to bring that legacy back.
That's why when elected Joe will introduce the Carbon-Neutral Steel and Manufacturing Innovations Act .
Joe supports revitalizing local manufacturing with a focus on carbon-neutral steel production to create good-paying jobs and grow our economy.
By honoring our industrial roots while embracing innovation, we can build a stronger, more sustainable future for Pennsylvania's General Assembly Legislative District 135.
