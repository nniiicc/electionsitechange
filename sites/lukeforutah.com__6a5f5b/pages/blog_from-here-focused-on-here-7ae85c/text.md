Mar 30 Written By S Happy Spring and spring break to Wasatch schools!
I’ve really enjoyed the conversations I’ve had with many of you already and am looking forward to continuing those this week.
I’ll also be attending the Summit GOP County Convention Tuesday night at 5:30 pm at Ecker Middle School.
Come to my booth and say hi and grab a treat too.
In my last email, I mentioned my service on the County Council, which oversees many areas, including parks and recreation, the library, solid waste, several water districts, and the budget for many departments and elected offices.
I also wanted to share some of the other ways I’ve been involved in our community and with the Party over the years.
My eagerness to find ways to give back come from growing up in here in a place that gave me so much.
These opportunities have helped me grow, and I’m more grateful than ever to live in and be part of the Wasatch Back.

I’ve made it a priority to be involved, stay connected, and play a role in the Heber Valley.
I’ll continue to do that, and I believe that this connection is one of my strengths in this race.
