He feels lucky to be raising his family here with his wife, Savanah, and their three children, surrounded by family, friends, and the same community traditions and values he grew up with.
Luke serves on the Wasatch County Council and teaches business and marketing at Wasatch High School, where he also works with students in the Wasatch CAPS program.
His work with students and local industry partners allow him to follow his passion in helping young people succeed.
He is currently completing a master’s degree in education through Southern Utah University.
Luke has long been involved in the Republican Party, serving as a Wasatch County GOP State Delegate, State Central Committee member, President of the UVU College Republicans, and Vice Chair of the Utah Young Republicans.
He believes our party's platform and message are essential to our country's prosperity.
As your county councilman, Luke has been a steady conservative voice fighting to protect the valley’s heritage while planning wisely for our future.
