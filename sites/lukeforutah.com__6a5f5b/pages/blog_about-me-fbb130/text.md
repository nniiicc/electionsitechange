About Me
For those I have not yet met, I’m Luke Searle.
I grew up in the Heber Valley, am now a Wasatch High and Wasatch CAPS teacher (& Turning Point Advisor), Wasatch County Councilman, and a candidate for Utah House District 59.
I want to share my campaign launch video and explain why I’m running.
My focus is simple:
- Protecting families through cost of living and property tax relief & reform
- Keeping decisions local and close to the people here in the Wasatch Back
- Fight for Religious Freedom in our State
- Real-world learning opportunities and a focus on the basics in education
All the best,
Luke Searle
