Meet James Andrew
I’m running for re-election to the Pennsylvania State Senate because our communities deserve steady, practical leadership, and not more politics.
I’ve spent my life working hard, listening to people, and finding common ground to get things done.
From improving our schools and supporting small businesses to opening Sunday hunting and protecting our natural resources, I’ll keep delivering results that matter for the people who call Pennsylvania home.
