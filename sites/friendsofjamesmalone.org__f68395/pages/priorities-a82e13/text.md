Priorities
Lowering the Cost of Everyday Essentials
Families are feeling the squeeze from rising prices on everything from healthcare to groceries.
James is working to lower the cost of living by addressing healthcare costs, expanding access to affordable housing, and supporting policies that make life more affordable for working people.
Investing in Strong Public Schools
Every student deserves a safe, high-quality education.
James has championed fair funding for public schools and helped pass new protections to keep students safe, including legislation requiring schools to report incidents of digital harassment and exploitation.
He believes education is the foundation of opportunity and the best investment we can make in Pennsylvania’s future.
Building Strong Communities
When small businesses thrive, neighborhoods grow stronger, and first responders have the tools they need to keep us safe.
James is focused on strengthening communities by investing in reliable infrastructure, supporting local jobs, and ensuring the people who serve Pennsylvania families have the resources they need to do their work well.
Securing Local Control over Data Centers
James believes you should have a say in data center development in your communities.
That’s why he called on the Senate leadership to call the Senate back into session to pass common-sense regulations and guardrails that would give municipalities the power and tools to protect residents from pollution and rate hikes.
Stopping Corruption
Elected officials are there to serve their constituents, not their own special interests.
James introduced legislation to ban elected officials, public employees, and candidates for public office from accepting any cash gifts or gifts exceeding $50 in total from a single source per calendar year.
Protecting Our Land, Water, and Traditions
James believes Pennsylvania’s outdoors are part of who we are, from our forests and farmland to our hunting and fishing heritage.
He’s working to preserve those traditions through responsible conservation, support for Sunday hunting, and policies that protect livestock, natural resources, and rural communities for future generations.
Supporting Seniors
James is a trusted voice for seniors and retirees.
He’s committed to protecting pensions, lowering healthcare costs, and ensuring older Pennsylvanians can live with dignity, security, and peace of mind.
