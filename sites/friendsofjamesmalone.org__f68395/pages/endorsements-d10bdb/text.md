Endorsements
Former Ephrata Mayor Ralph Mowen (R):
“I served with Senator Malone on the Pennsylvania State Mayors Association.
He gets out among his constituents and listens to what they have to say.
He works extremely hard for the residents of his district and has brought millions of dollars to that district.
I strongly endorse James Malone for reelection to the Pennsylvania Senate.
He is the people’s candidate.”
Former Lititz Mayor Tim Snyder (R):
“I have known and worked with James Malone since he was the Mayor of East Petersburg.
I have found him to be a person who seeks out the facts on both sides of issues and a leader who always puts his constituents first.
I enthusiastically endorse Senator James Malone as the best candidate to represent Lancaster County.”
