Important legislative work is undermined when the public lacks faith in their elected officials to conduct their duties ethically.
Public faith has been rocked in their state government, and I will do everything within my power to help restore it.
Ethics enforcement is in need of a serious overhaul.
I support the Governor’s goal of Joint Commission On Public Ethics (JCOPE) reform; however, we must go further.
We must replace JCOPE and the Legislative Ethics Commission (LEC) with a single enforcement agency, and ensure this new iteration is built with true independence from those it is designed to oversee, and free from the influence of lawmakers.
