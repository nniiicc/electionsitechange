Bursting Onto the Scene
On March 1, 1954, I was born in Paola, Kansas, about 17 hours after the Castle Bravo nuclear blast lit up the South Pacific.
It was the largest explosion of a hydrogen bomb by the U.S. in history – 1,000 times greater than the bomb dropped on Hiroshima.
It was 2.5 times larger than expected, due to a wrong assumption and miscalculation.
My first five years were on our family farm.
It was a wonderland of learning and adventure.
It also taught me a little about nature’s mysteries and magic.
That set me on a quest for truth and prepared my life’s mission as a “solutionist”.
Elevating humanity to enjoy a solution-based world has been my mission since age ten.
My pioneering initiatives created headlines worldwide, even setting local news media records.
I was the first Vice-President of Special Projects for the DaVinci Institute.
Unifying Solutions
