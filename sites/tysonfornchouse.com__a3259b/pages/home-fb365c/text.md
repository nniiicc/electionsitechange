Home About Steve Events News On the Issues Endorsements Make Endorsement Photo Gallery Send us a Message Beginning January 1, 2027, local governments across most of North Carolina will no longer be able to require a minimum number of off-street parking spaces for new developments under Session Law 2026-39 (House Bill 162) .
Instead, builders can determine the appropriate amount of parking based on market demand.
The law exempts local governments located within North Carolina's coastal area as defined by N.C.G.S.
113A-103, except for certain historical properties.
For residential builders, especially for townhome developments, this law provides greater design flexibility by allowing projects to be designed around housing instead of mandatory parking requirements.
Communities across North Carolina have experienced growing demand for attainable housing while development costs have continued to rise.
Parking requirements often consume valuable land that could otherwise be used for additional homes, green space, or community amenities.
House Bill 162 removes one of those regulatory barriers and gives developers greater flexibility to meet local housing needs.

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE Representative Steve Tyson Ranks Among Top 5 in North Carolina House According to Free Enterprise Foundation Raleigh, N.C. — The North Carolina Free Enterprise Foundation (NCFEF) has released its latest rankings of all 120 members of the North Carolina House of Representatives, and Representative Steve Tyson earned one of the highest scores in the state, receiving a weighted score of 98.7 and ranking #4 overall.
The NCFEF evaluates legislators based on their voting records, sponsorships, and overall support for policies that encourage economic growth, job creation, and free enterprise across North Carolina.
“I’m honored to be recognized for my consistent commitment to advancing policies that strengthen our economy and support North Carolina’s businesses and workers,” said Rep.
Tyson.
“Free enterprise is the foundation of opportunity in our state, and I’ll continue to champion legislation that keeps North Carolina competitive and thriving.” The NCFEF’s rankings are widely regarded as a key indicator of legislators’ support for the state’s business climate and entrepreneurial growth.
About the North Carolina Free Enterprise Foundation: The NCFEF is a nonpartisan research organization that analyzes the intersection of politics and business in North Carolina.
Its legislative rankings highlight lawmakers’ records on policies impacting the state’s economic environment.
