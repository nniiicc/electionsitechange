A Common-Sense Leader for maryland's eastern shore
Josh Hastings is a common-sense, independent-minded, leader who supports better job opportunities, stronger schools, and a healthy, prosperous, Eastern Shore.
Josh Hastings is a husband, Dad, small business owner, and lifelong Wicomico County resident.
He grew up on a Mardela produce and poultry farm and built a career in supporting agriculture, natural resources, and connected communities.
On the County Council, Josh has worked to strengthen schools, improve parks, protect our environment, and support first responders.
Above all, he is focused on fairness, opportunity, and results that make a real difference for local families.
The Eastern Shore has been overlooked for too long.
Josh's priorities focus on delivering real opportunities and lasting improvements for our community.
