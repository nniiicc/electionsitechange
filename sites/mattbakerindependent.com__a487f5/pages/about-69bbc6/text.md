Home
Hampton Roads is home — not a campaign stop.
My dad retired from the Army out of Fort Monroe in 1970, and our family moved to Panama for his work with the Canal Zone Government.
That’s where I spent my formative years graduating from high school in 1982.
It took an extra year at Rice University, but I won an appointment to Annapolis and commissioned in the Marines in 1987.
My personal relationship with this area began with a submarine training cruise in 1984 aboard USS Baltimore.
I came back in 1998 for a three-year tour at the Hampton Roads NROTC (ODU, NSU and HU).
In 2012 my wife and I moved here for good.
We’ve raised three children here, all of whom will have graduated from high school in Virginia Beach.
I retired from the Marine Corps out of Fort Story and transitioned with a local firm in Chesapeake doing microgrid work and I am currently working in the energy sector as an engineer and business development executive.
During my first decade in the area, I voted in the 2nd District.
However, congressional district boundaries were redrawn for the 2022 elections, and my neighborhood was moved to VA-03.
I did not leave the district; the mapmakers simply shifted a line less than a mile to the east.
My community and the people I identify with are unchanged.
Fortunately, the law is clear, and I only need to be a resident of the state to run in the 2nd District.
And there is nowhere else I would rather represent.

How I'll Govern
My operating principles aren't talking points.
I will campaign the way I intend to govern: by engaging, not broadcasting.
Town halls will be conversations, not speeches.
I will ask questions as well as answer them, and I will listen — in both directions.
Be kind, courageous, and independent — all, at the same time.
Be independent in thought, in vote, in conscience.
Accept challenge; if it's easy, it's likely not worth doing.
Always be learning.
Be honorable in all things.
Laugh — most of all at yourself.
Care for yourself and others.
Do your best and help others do theirs.
Keep a bias for action — when things are unclear, lean toward doing.
These principles are how I live.
Not a platform.
Not a promise.
A track record.
