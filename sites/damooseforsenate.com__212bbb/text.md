Meet John Damoose
Senator John Damoose is a proven conservative leader shaped by the values of Northern Michigan: faith, family, hard work, personal responsibility, and service to community.
A longtime Harbor Springs resident, businessman, filmmaker, husband, and father of four, John has spent his career rekindling the roots of the American character - sacrifice, leadership, and industry.
Today, John brings that same sense of purpose to the Michigan Senate, where he represents Northern Michigan and the eastern Upper Peninsula with common sense, civility, and results-driven leadership.
He has been recognized for his work on behalf of Michigan manufacturing, the automotive industry, small businesses, public safety, human trafficking prevention, corrections officers, career and technical education, and policies that strengthen families and make life more affordable.
Above all, John is committed to defending Northern Michigan’s way of life and building a stronger year-round economy so future generations can live, work, and raise their families closer to home.
About Michigan's
37th District
State Sen.
John Damoose is the current State Senator for Michigan's 37th State District.
Elected in 2022, John Damoose represents Antrim, Charlevoix, Cheboygan, Emmet, Grand Traverse, Leelanau counties and parts of Mackinac and Chippewa counties.
