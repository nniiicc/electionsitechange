top of page Home About Me Voter Info Policies Farming Healthcare Housing Justice Constitution Foreign Affairs Cannabis Monopolies Education Data Privacy Campaign Reform Donate NO ONE IS ABOVE THE LAW America is supposed to be a nation of laws.
Those laws have to apply to everyone.
Presidents, billionaires, cabinet officials, federal agents, corporate executives, political donors, and government contractors should all answer to the same justice system as everyone else.
For too long, powerful people have operated under a different set of rules.
They commit abuses of power, enrich themselves through public office, violate constitutional rights, ignore court orders, hide behind executive authority, and assume the country will eventually decide that accountability would be too divisive.
That is how corruption becomes normalized.
That is how constitutional rights disappear.
And that is how democracy dies.
We will investigate the crimes, prosecute those responsible, defend the Constitution, and restore one system of justice for everyone.
Prosecute Donald Trump Donald Trump should not receive immunity from criminal accountability because he was president.
His previous federal prosecutions involving the attempt to overturn the 2020 election and the retention of national-defense information were stopped before they could be resolved at trial.
Those cases should be pursued again when he leaves office, along with investigations into criminal conduct committed during his current administration.
That includes crimes involving corruption, obstruction, abuse of government power, bribery, financial misconduct, violations of constitutional rights, and criminal conspiracies involving members of his administration or political network.
The presidency cannot become a four-year license to break the law.
Donald Trump should be investigated, prosecuted, and held accountable for his crimes just like any other American.
Prosecute the Attempt to Overturn an Election Trying to overturn the votes of millions of Americans is not normal politics.
It is an attack on democracy itself.
Trump was already federally indicted on charges involving conspiracy to defraud the United States, obstruction of the electoral certification, and conspiracy against the rights of Americans to vote and have their votes counted.
We will investigate and prosecute the entire effort to overturn the 2020 election, including attempts to pressure public officials, interfere with vote certification, submit fraudulent electoral documents, obstruct the peaceful transfer of power, intimidate election workers, and deprive Americans of their voting rights.
Accountability cannot stop with the person at the top.
Lawyers, political operatives, campaign officials, government employees, financiers, and outside organizations that knowingly participated in criminal conduct must be held accountable as well.
If we will not prosecute an organized attempt to overturn an American election, then our elections are protected by little more than tradition and good faith.
Prosecute the Classified Documents Case Donald Trump was also federally indicted for allegedly retaining national-defense information after leaving office and obstructing government efforts to recover it.
That prosecution should be restored.
Presidents do not own classified national-security information when they leave office.
They do not get a special exemption from laws that would be aggressively enforced against a soldier, intelligence officer, government contractor, or federal employee.
The same laws must apply to the former president.
National security cannot mean one law for ordinary Americans and another for Donald Trump.
Investigate Trump Administration Corruption Public office is not a business opportunity.
The presidency should never be used to enrich the president, his family, his business partners, political allies, or wealthy donors.
We will launch independent investigations into bribery, kickbacks, self-dealing, pay-to-play arrangements, government contracting fraud, misuse of federal resources, money laundering, obstruction of justice, illegal conflicts of interest, and the sale of government access or influence.
Donald Trump and members of his family have built enormous financial interests while he has exercised presidential power.
That demands scrutiny.
We will examine business transactions, government decisions, communications, financial records, cryptocurrency transactions, foreign investments, and relationships between people providing money to Trump-linked businesses and people seeking favors from the federal government.
The presidency belongs to the American people.
It is not a family business.
Investigate Trump's Cryptocurrency Empire A sitting president should not be able to personally profit from financial products while simultaneously controlling the agencies that regulate those products.
Trump-linked cryptocurrency ventures, meme coins, stablecoins, investments, and foreign financial relationships should receive a full independent investigation.
Investigators should examine who invested, how much they invested, what access they received, what government business they had, and whether presidential actions benefited people financially supporting Trump or his family.
Cryptocurrency cannot become a convenient way for corporations, billionaires, or foreign interests to anonymously purchase influence with the President of the United States.
Follow the money from the investor to the White House.
Investigate Foreign Money Foreign governments and foreign interests should never be able to purchase influence over an American president.
We will investigate foreign investments in Trump businesses and financial ventures, payments involving Trump-controlled companies, cryptocurrency purchases, licensing arrangements, gifts, real-estate transactions, and other financial relationships involving foreign interests.
Investigators will examine whether government policies, contracts, meetings, pardons, diplomatic decisions, regulatory actions, or other official benefits were connected to those financial relationships.
Bribery does not become legal because the payment travels through a corporation, cryptocurrency wallet, investment fund, or family member.
American foreign policy cannot be for sale to the highest bidder.
Investigate and Prosecute Corrupt Pardons The Constitution gives presidents broad authority to issue pardons.
It does not give presidents the authority to sell them.
We will investigate pardons and clemency decisions involving credible allegations of bribery, financial payments, political contributions, investments, business deals, gifts, or other benefits provided in exchange for presidential action.
Pardons involving wealthy donors, political allies, business associates, or people with financial relationships to the president deserve particular scrutiny.
The pardon power exists to provide mercy.
It does not exist to create a presidential marketplace for criminal immunity.
Release the Epstein Files The American people deserve the truth about Jeffrey Epstein, the people who participated in his trafficking operation, the people who enabled it, and the institutions that allowed it to continue.
We will release every Epstein-related federal document that can legally be released.
Victims and minors will be protected.
Politicians, billionaires, celebrities, donors, corporate executives, members of royal families, and presidents will not receive secrecy simply because disclosure would embarrass them.
Being named in a document does not establish guilt.
But government secrecy should never be used to shield powerful people from legitimate investigation.
Protect the victims.
Release the files.
Expose the network.
Investigate Donald Trump's Relationship With Jeffrey Epstein Donald Trump's relationship with Jeffrey Epstein should receive a complete and independent investigation.
That investigation should examine relevant communications, witness testimony, financial records, travel records, photographs, law-enforcement records, and any credible allegations contained in government files.
The purpose is not political theater.
It is to determine what Trump knew, what interactions occurred, whether criminal conduct took place, and whether anyone attempted to conceal or obstruct investigations into Epstein's trafficking network.
The same standard should apply to every powerful person connected to Epstein.
No president, former president, billionaire, or political donor gets a special exemption from a sex-trafficking investigation.
Prosecute the Epstein Network Jeffrey Epstein did not operate in isolation.
A trafficking operation requires people who recruit victims, facilitate access, move money, arrange transportation, provide locations, protect perpetrators, intimidate witnesses, and conceal criminal activity.
We will investigate and prosecute criminal conduct throughout that network.
That includes sex trafficking, sexual exploitation of minors, conspiracy, obstruction of justice, witness intimidation, destruction of evidence, financial crimes, facilitation of trafficking, and corruption by government officials who protected offenders or interfered with investigations.
Political affiliation will provide no protection.
Neither will wealth, fame, celebrity, or social status.
Everyone who participated in trafficking or helped protect traffickers should answer for it.
Prosecute Sex Trafficking Sex trafficking is a violent crime.
It destroys lives while traffickers and the people who enable them profit from exploitation.
We will dramatically strengthen federal investigations into child sex trafficking, interstate and international trafficking networks, online trafficking operations, financial networks supporting trafficking, and businesses knowingly facilitating exploitation.
We will follow bank records, shell companies, property holdings, payment systems, transportation networks, corporate structures, and other financial trails that can identify the people organizing and profiting from these crimes.
At the same time, we will expand safe housing, healthcare, trauma treatment, legal assistance, education, job training, and long-term services for survivors.
Prosecute traffickers and protect the people they exploited.
Investigate and Prosecute ICE Crimes A badge does not provide immunity from criminal law.
ICE officers, Border Patrol agents, detention officers, contractors, supervisors, and political officials who commit crimes should be prosecuted.
We will establish independent investigations into allegations involving unlawful detention, assault, excessive force, sexual abuse, falsification of records, obstruction of justice, destruction of evidence, corruption, medical neglect, abuse of detainees, and criminal violations of constitutional rights.
Federal immigration agencies cannot investigate themselves and expect the public to trust the results.
Independent investigators must have access to officers, facilities, video recordings, medical records, internal communications, personnel files, contracts, and use-of-force reports.
Law enforcement authority comes with greater responsibility, not greater immunity.
Independently Investigate ICE Killings When a government agent kills someone, the government owes the public a complete accounting of what happened.
Every fatal shooting and suspicious death involving federal immigration enforcement should receive an independent investigation.
Investigators must determine whether deadly force was legally justified, whether the person presented an imminent threat, whether officers followed the law, whether official reports were truthful, whether evidence was preserved, and whether supervisors or other officials attempted to interfere with the investigation.
If an agent unlawfully kills someone, prosecute the killing.
If officials falsify reports to cover it up, prosecute the cover-up.
A government badge cannot become a license to kill.
Investigate Deaths in Immigration Detention People detained by the federal government are under the government's care.
When they die, the government has an obligation to explain why.
Every death in immigration detention should receive independent review, including examination of medical treatment, staffing, use of force, mental-health care, suicide prevention, delays in treatment, facility conditions, and actions by detention contractors.
Deaths caused by criminal abuse, intentional denial of care, falsification of records, obstruction, assault, or other criminal conduct must result in prosecution.
Government custody creates government responsibility.
Nobody should enter civil immigration detention and disappear into a system where their death receives no meaningful investigation.
End Abuse in Immigration Detention Facilities Immigration detention does not erase human rights.
We will establish independent inspections of every federal immigration detention facility and private detention contractor.
Investigators will examine allegations involving physical abuse, sexual abuse, medical neglect, solitary confinement, unsafe conditions, inadequate food, sanitation failures, retaliation, treatment of children, deaths in custody, excessive force, and falsification of records.
Private detention corporations receiving billions in taxpayer money must face the same criminal accountability as government employees.
Contracts will not shield companies from prosecution.
If you imprison human beings in the name of the United States government, you are accountable for how they are treated.
Prosecute Unlawful Detention The government does not have the right to imprison people without legal authority.
We will investigate systemic unlawful detention by ICE and other federal agencies, including cases where officials continued holding people after courts ruled that detention was illegal or after officials knew there was no lawful basis to continue imprisonment.
Investigators will determine who created the policies, who implemented them, who received warnings that they violated the law, and who ordered officers to continue.
A bureaucratic mistake is one thing.
A government knowingly operating an unlawful detention system is another.
The power to take away someone's freedom is one of the greatest powers government possesses.
It must never be exercised outside the law.
Defend the First Amendment The government cannot punish Americans because it dislikes their political beliefs.
We will defend freedom of speech, freedom of the press, religious liberty, peaceful protest, assembly, and political organizing.
Federal agencies will not be used to punish journalists, protesters, students, activists, immigrants, or political opponents for lawful speech.
Immigration authority cannot become a weapon for silencing political dissent.
Neither can federal funding, government contracts, regulatory authority, or criminal investigations.
The First Amendment protects speech from government power, especially speech the government does not like.
Defend the Fourth Amendment The Fourth Amendment protects Americans from unreasonable searches and seizures.
We will defend it against warrantless surveillance, dragnet location tracking, unlawful searches, unconstitutional raids, unjustified seizures, and abusive law-enforcement practices.
The government should not be able to build databases tracking everyone's movements simply because technology makes it possible.
Immigration enforcement does not repeal the Fourth Amendment.
National security does not repeal the Fourth Amendment.
New technology does not repeal the Fourth Amendment.
The Constitution does not contain an exception for government convenience.
Defend the Fifth Amendment The federal government cannot deprive people of liberty without due process.
People detained by the government have the right to challenge that detention.
People facing deportation have legal rights.
People accused of crimes have constitutional protections.
No president should be able to order the federal government to arrest people, imprison them, remove them from the country, or strip away their rights without lawful process.
Due process is not a loophole criminals exploit.
It is protection against government tyranny.
Defend the Fourteenth Amendment The Fourteenth Amendment guarantees citizenship, due process, and equal protection under the law.
A president cannot erase those guarantees with an executive order.
We will defend birthright citizenship and oppose efforts to create different constitutional rights based on race, ethnicity, religion, national origin, or political identity.
Equal protection means government cannot decide that some Americans deserve constitutional rights while others do not.
Citizenship and equality are constitutional guarantees, not presidential favors.
Defend the Sixth and Eighth Amendments The right to counsel, the right to a fair trial, and protection against cruel and unusual punishment are fundamental to the American justice system.
We will defend those rights for everyone.
Government cannot build a system where people are imprisoned without meaningful access to lawyers, subjected to abusive conditions, or denied fundamental legal protections simply because they are politically unpopular.
Constitutional rights mean very little if they disappear the moment government accuses someone of wrongdoing.
Rights that apply only to popular people are not rights.
Protect Habeas Corpus The government cannot simply disappear human beings into detention.
People imprisoned by the government must retain the right to challenge the legality of their detention before an independent court.
We will defend habeas corpus against attempts by presidents or federal agencies to place people beyond the reach of judicial review.
No secret prisons.
No indefinite detention without legal review.
No moving detainees around to prevent courts from exercising jurisdiction.
The government must be able to explain to a judge why it has taken away someone's freedom.
Enforce Court Orders The President of the United States is not a king.
Federal agencies do not get to decide which judicial rulings they feel like obeying.
Government officials who conceal evidence, lie to courts, destroy records, obstruct judicial proceedings, hide detainees, or intentionally defy lawful court orders should be investigated and prosecuted.
An independent judiciary means nothing if the executive branch can simply ignore it.
Court orders are orders, not suggestions.
Investigate the Killing of People on Boats The United States should not become a country where the government kills people because it accuses them of criminal activity.
Military attacks on suspected drug-trafficking vessels must receive independent investigation.
We need to know who was killed, what intelligence identified them, whether they were civilians, whether they posed an imminent threat, whether interdiction or arrest was possible, what legal authority was used, who approved the strikes, and whether the attacks complied with American and international law.
Accusing someone of being a drug trafficker does not automatically create a legal right to execute them from the air.
Suspicion is not a trial, and accusation is not a death sentence.
Investigate Extrajudicial Killings The United States cannot claim the power to execute people anywhere in the world simply by labeling them criminals or terrorists.
Every credible allegation of an extrajudicial killing carried out by American forces should receive independent investigation.
Officials responsible for authorizing unlawful killings cannot hide behind classified legal opinions or vague claims of national security.
The government should disclose the legal authority for lethal operations and explain why capture was impossible when force was used outside a traditional battlefield.
Government officials do not get to secretly invent a legal theory that gives them the power to kill anyone they choose.
Prosecute War Crimes and Serious Violations of International Law International law applies to the United States too.
We cannot demand accountability when Russia, China, Iran, Israel, or another country violates international law and then declare ourselves exempt when American officials do the same thing.
Credible allegations involving intentionally targeting civilians, torture, unlawful killings, mistreatment of prisoners, or other serious violations of the laws of war should be independently investigated.
Responsibility does not stop with the soldier who carried out an order.
Commanders and civilian officials who ordered, authorized, or knowingly facilitated criminal conduct must be investigated as well.
There cannot be one international law for our enemies and another for ourselves.
Investigate Civilian Killings Civilian casualties cannot simply disappear into Pentagon statistics.
When American military action kills civilians, there must be transparent investigation into what happened.
Investigators should examine the target, intelligence, weapons used, precautions taken, proportionality assessments, civilian warnings, chain of command, and whether officials attempted to conceal mistakes or wrongdoing.
Not every civilian death is a war crime.
But every government that uses lethal force has an obligation to account for the people it kills.
Human life does not become meaningless because the bomb was American.
Follow the Chain of Command Accountability cannot stop with the lowest-ranking person involved.
When government crimes are systemic, investigators must determine who designed the policy, who authorized it, who ignored legal warnings, who ordered subordinates to continue, who falsified records, who destroyed evidence, and who participated in covering it up.
A frontline officer should not become the only person prosecuted while the officials who created an illegal policy retire to consulting jobs and television appearances.
Government power flows downward.
Accountability has to travel upward.
Prosecute Trump Administration Cronies Who Commit Crimes Cabinet officials, White House aides, political appointees, campaign officials, government contractors, donors, corporate executives, and political allies do not receive immunity because they are close to the president.
We will investigate corruption, bribery, fraud, kickbacks, obstruction, witness tampering, misuse of public money, illegal financial transactions, civil-rights violations, destruction of government records, money laundering, trafficking, and criminal conspiracies throughout the administration.
Political loyalty will not protect anyone from criminal accountability.
Working for the president does not put someone above the law.
Protect Whistleblowers Government corruption is often exposed because someone inside the system refuses to stay silent.
We will strengthen protections for federal employees, law-enforcement officers, military personnel, contractors, inspectors general, and others who lawfully expose crimes, corruption, fraud, constitutional violations, or abuse.
Government officials will not be allowed to destroy the careers of people who expose wrongdoing while protecting the people responsible for it.
Retaliation against lawful whistleblowers will carry serious consequences.
People who expose government crimes should be protected, not punished.
Restore an Independent Justice Department The Department of Justice belongs to the American people.
It does not belong to Donald Trump.
And it should not belong to the next Democratic president either.
Presidents should not be able to order criminal investigations of political enemies, shut down investigations of friends, protect donors, interfere with prosecutions, or turn federal law enforcement into a personal weapon.
We will restore the independence of federal prosecutors and strengthen safeguards against White House interference in individual criminal cases.
An independent Justice Department must be willing to prosecute political power regardless of which party holds it.
Justice cannot depend on who occupies the Oval Office.
One Constitution.
One System of Justice.
If an undocumented immigrant commits a crime, the law applies.
If an ICE officer commits a crime, the law applies.
If a billionaire commits a crime, the law applies.
If someone participates in sex trafficking, the law applies.
If a military commander commits a war crime, the law applies.
If a cabinet secretary commits a crime, the law applies.
And if the President of the United States commits a crime, the law applies.
That should not be radical.
It is the basic promise of a constitutional republic.
For too long, America has confused avoiding accountability with protecting democracy.
It does the opposite.
Every time powerful people escape consequences, the next administration learns that it can go further.
The Constitution does not enforce itself.
The rule of law does not defend itself.
Democracy survives only when people are willing to hold power accountable.
Protect the Constitution.
Prosecute Donald Trump.
Release the Epstein files.
Prosecute sex traffickers.
Investigate and prosecute ICE abuses.
Hold corrupt officials accountable.
Investigate unlawful killings and violations of international law.
And restore one system of justice for everyone.
