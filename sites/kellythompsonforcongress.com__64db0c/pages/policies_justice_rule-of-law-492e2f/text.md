NO ONE IS ABOVE THE LAW
The Constitution does not say the law applies to everyone except presidents.
It does not say billionaires get one justice system and working people get another.
It does not say federal agents can ignore constitutional rights because someone is an immigrant.
And it does not say powerful people get to hide behind classified documents, sealed records, political connections, or government office.
For too long, America has operated with two systems of justice.
One for ordinary people.
And another for the rich, powerful, and politically connected.
We are going to end that.
Protect the Constitution
The Constitution is not optional.
It does not disappear during a political crisis.
It does not disappear at the border.
And it does not disappear because a president finds it inconvenient.
We will aggressively defend the constitutional rights of every person in America, including:
- The First Amendment: freedom of speech, religion, the press, peaceful assembly, and protest.
- The Fourth Amendment: protection against unreasonable searches and seizures.
- The Fifth Amendment: due process and protection against abuse of federal government power.
- The Sixth Amendment: the right to counsel, a fair and public trial, and the ability to confront your accusers in criminal prosecutions.
- The Eighth Amendment: protection against excessive bail, excessive fines, and cruel and unusual punishment.
- The Fourteenth Amendment: citizenship, due process, and equal protection under the law.
- The right to habeas corpus: the government cannot simply disappear someone into detention without allowing the legality of that detention to be challenged before a court.
Constitutional rights are not privileges the government hands out when it feels like it.
They are limits on government power.
Defend the Fourth Amendment
Americans should not have to live in a country where government agents can stop people, search them, track them, enter homes, seize property, or build surveillance databases without meaningful constitutional limits.
We will defend the Fourth Amendment against warrantless mass surveillance, unconstitutional searches, dragnet location tracking, and abusive government searches and seizures.
Technology does not repeal the Constitution.
Neither does immigration enforcement.
Neither does national security.
The government should need a lawful reason to search you, seize you, or track you.
Defend Due Process
No president should have the power to decide that someone has no rights because of where they were born.
The Fifth and Fourteenth Amendments guarantee due process.
Government should not be able to arrest someone, imprison them, deport them, seize their property, or strip away fundamental rights without lawful procedures and meaningful access to the courts.
We will defend access to attorneys, hearings, judicial review, and the basic right to challenge government power.
Due process is not a loophole.
It is the Constitution.
Protect Birthright Citizenship and Equal Protection
The Fourteenth Amendment means what it says.
People born in the United States and subject to its jurisdiction are citizens of the United States.
We will defend birthright citizenship and oppose attempts by any president to rewrite the Constitution through executive order.
We will also vigorously enforce equal protection under the law.
Government power cannot be based on race, ethnicity, religion, national origin, political belief, or whether somebody has powerful friends.
No President Is Above the Law
Donald Trump should be subject to the same criminal laws as every other American.
We will support independent investigations into credible allegations of criminal conduct involving Trump, members of his administration, political associates, business interests, and anyone else who may have participated in illegal activity.
Where evidence establishes a prosecutable crime, prosecutors should bring charges.
Not because someone is a Republican.
Not because someone is a Democrat.
Because nobody is above the law.
Investigations must be independent, evidence-based, insulated from political interference, and conducted with full due-process protections.
The Justice Department should never become a president's personal law firm.
But neither should the presidency become immunity from accountability.
Investigate Corruption in the Trump Administration
Public office is not a license to steal.
We will establish independent investigations into credible evidence of:
- Bribery
- Kickbacks
- Procurement fraud
- Pay-to-play schemes
- Obstruction of justice
- Misuse of government resources
- Self-dealing
- Illegal conflicts of interest
- Destruction or concealment of evidence
- Corruption involving government contracts
- Selling government access or influence
- Conspiracies to violate federal law
That investigation will follow the evidence wherever it leads.
Cabinet secretary.
Billionaire.
Political donor.
White House aide.
Corporate executive.
Member of Congress.
Same law.
Same standard.
Same consequences.
Investigate and Prosecute Crimes Committed by ICE Personnel
A badge does not put anyone above the Constitution.
ICE officers, Border Patrol agents, contractors, supervisors, and administration officials who commit crimes should be investigated and, where the evidence supports it, prosecuted just like anyone else.
That includes credible allegations involving:
- Unlawful detention
- Assault
- Sexual abuse
- Excessive force
- Falsification of records
- Obstruction of justice
- Destruction of evidence
- Corruption
- Conspiracy to deprive people of constitutional rights
- Knowingly violating court orders
- Abuse of people in federal custody
We will create strong independent oversight with the power to investigate federal immigration enforcement agencies without interference from the agencies themselves.
We will protect whistleblowers who expose misconduct.
And we will create transparent reporting requirements for deaths, serious injuries, allegations of sexual assault, use of force, and other major incidents in federal immigration custody.
A uniform is not immunity.
Government Officials Must Obey Court Orders
America is a constitutional republic.
Presidents do not get to decide which court orders they feel like following.
Federal agencies do not get to ignore judges because they disagree with them.
We will defend an independent judiciary and hold government officials accountable when they knowingly obstruct courts, destroy evidence, conceal detainees, or illegally refuse to comply with lawful judicial orders.
The rule of law means the government follows the law too.

The American people deserve the truth about Jeffrey Epstein, his trafficking network, the people who enabled it, and any failures or corruption within government that allowed it to continue.
We will order the release of every lawfully releasable Epstein-related government record.
No redactions to protect:
- Politicians

Political influence is not a reason for secrecy.
Investigate the Epstein Network
Releasing documents is not enough.
Any credible evidence of criminal conduct revealed through those records should be investigated.
That includes potential:
- Sex trafficking
- Sexual exploitation of minors
- Conspiracy
- Obstruction
- Witness intimidation
- Financial crimes
- Evidence destruction

Follow the evidence.
Prosecute the crimes.
Take Sex Trafficking Seriously
Sex trafficking is not a political talking point.
It is a violent crime.
We will significantly strengthen federal efforts to investigate and prosecute traffickers, trafficking organizations, people who knowingly facilitate trafficking, and predators who sexually exploit children.
We will increase resources for specialized investigators and prosecutors focused on:
- Child sex trafficking
- Interstate and international trafficking networks
- Online trafficking operations
- Financial networks used to conceal trafficking proceeds
- Traffickers using hotels, transportation networks, and other businesses
- Organized exploitation and coercion
- Corrupt officials who protect traffickers
Federal investigators will follow the money as aggressively as they follow the traffickers.
Trafficking networks depend on financial systems.
Those financial trails can lead investigators to organizers, facilitators, and people profiting from exploitation.
Protect Victims, Not Just Prosecute Crimes
Justice also means helping survivors rebuild their lives.
We will expand funding for:
- Safe housing
- Trauma-informed healthcare
- Mental healthcare
- Legal assistance
- Witness protection
- Education
- Job training
- Long-term survivor services
Victims should never be treated as criminals for conduct they were coerced into performing.
Our enforcement priority will be the people who traffic, exploit, coerce, abuse, and profit, not the people they victimized.
Protect Whistleblowers
Some of the most important evidence of government corruption comes from people inside government willing to speak up.
We will strengthen protections for federal employees, contractors, law-enforcement officers, military personnel, and others who lawfully disclose evidence of corruption, criminal conduct, constitutional violations, fraud, or abuse.
Nobody should lose their career because they refused to participate in a cover-up.
Restore an Independent Justice Department
The Justice Department belongs to the American people.
Not the president.
Not a political party.
Not a billionaire donor.
We will strengthen safeguards protecting federal investigations and prosecutions from White House political interference.
Presidents should not be ordering prosecutors to investigate their enemies or protect their friends.
Prosecutors should follow:
The facts.
The evidence.
And the law.
One Constitution.
One System of Justice.
If an undocumented immigrant commits a crime, the law applies.
If an ICE officer commits a crime, the law applies.
If a billionaire commits a crime, the law applies.
If someone involved in Jeffrey Epstein's trafficking network commits a crime, the law applies.
If a president commits a crime, the law applies.
That principle should not be radical.
It is the foundation of a republic.
Protect the Constitution.
Release the Epstein files.
Investigate corruption.
Prosecute trafficking.
Hold abusive government officials accountable.
And restore one system of justice for everyone.
