HEALTHCARE IS A HUMAN RIGHT
No American should have to choose between seeing a doctor and paying the rent.
No parent should be afraid to take their child to the emergency room because they don't know what the bill will be.
And no insurance company should be able to make money by denying somebody the care their doctor says they need.
Healthcare is a human right.
It should not be a luxury, and it should not be a business model.
That is why we support Medicare for All: one national health insurance program covering every American.
Not a public option.
Not another complicated marketplace plan.
Medicare for All.
Cover Everyone
Under Medicare for All, every American will be covered automatically.
Coverage will include:
- Doctor and hospital care
- Emergency care
- Prescription drugs
- Mental healthcare
- Dental care
- Vision care
- Hearing care
- Reproductive and maternity care
- Preventive care
- Substance-use treatment
- Other medically necessary care
Coverage will no longer depend on your employer, your income, your age, or what state you live in.
If you need healthcare, you will have healthcare.
No More Premiums, Deductibles or Medical Bills
Working families already pay enormous amounts for healthcare.
We pay insurance premiums every month.
Then we pay deductibles before our insurance starts working.
Then come copays, coinsurance, prescription costs, and bills for services the insurance company decides it will not cover.
Under Medicare for All, those costs go away.
No premiums.
No deductibles.
No copays.
No surprise medical bills.
0Instead of sending thousands of dollars every year to private insurance companies, Americans will contribute to one national system based on their ability to pay.
For the overwhelming majority of working families, that means more money left in every paycheck.
Choose Your Doctor, Not Your Insurance Network
Your insurance company should not decide which doctor you can see.
Medicare for All eliminates restrictive insurance networks.
You will be able to choose your doctor, hospital, specialist, or healthcare provider without wondering whether they are "in-network."
No more calling an insurance company before making an appointment.
No more discovering after treatment that the anesthesiologist wasn't in your network.
No more changing doctors because your employer changed insurance companies.
Your doctor works for you, not your insurance company.
Take Healthcare Off the Backs of Employers
Businesses are paying thousands of dollars per employee every year for private health insurance, and those costs continue to rise.
That hurts workers, small businesses, and American competitiveness.
Medicare for All takes that burden off employers.
Instead of every business negotiating separately with insurance companies and administering complicated employee health plans, healthcare will be financed through one national system.
That means small businesses can compete for workers without having to compete with giant corporations on health benefits.
And workers will finally be free to change jobs, start businesses, organize a union, or work for themselves without losing their healthcare.
Stop Paying Insurance Companies to Tell Us No
The United States spends enormous amounts of money administering a fragmented healthcare system built around thousands of different insurance plans, billing rules, networks, deductibles, prior authorizations, and claims.
A single national program dramatically simplifies that system.
Doctors should spend their time treating patients, not fighting insurance companies.
Hospitals should spend their resources providing care, not employing armies of people to navigate different insurance plans.
And patients should not have to become insurance experts just to figure out what their healthcare will cost.
We will replace that bureaucracy with a simple guarantee: You get sick.
You go to the doctor.
You get treated.
Use Our Buying Power to Bring Costs Down
Right now, insurance companies, hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, and middlemen negotiate across a fragmented system.
Medicare for All puts more than 300 million Americans into one bargaining pool.
That gives the American people enormous power to negotiate fair prices for prescription drugs, medical equipment, hospital care, and other healthcare costs.
We will use that power on behalf of patients, not insurance-company shareholders.
Put Healthcare Dollars Into Healthcare
America already spends more on healthcare than any other wealthy nation.
The problem is not that we aren't spending enough.
The problem is where the money goes.
We spend hundreds of billions of dollars administering a complicated private insurance system while insurance companies and pharmaceutical corporations make enormous profits.
Even an analysis from the conservative Mercatus Center found that a Medicare for All proposal could reduce total national healthcare expenditures by roughly $2 trillion over ten years under its assumptions.
We can spend less by eliminating waste, negotiating better prices, and removing the insurance-company middlemen standing between patients and their doctors.
Healthcare Not Wealthcare
Medicare for All is not about creating another government program alongside the insurance industry.
It is about guaranteeing healthcare to every person in America.
- It means never losing your insurance because you lost your job.
- It means never staying in a bad job because your family needs the health plan.
- It means never putting off seeing a doctor because you haven't met your deductible.
- It means never opening a hospital bill and wondering whether it will bankrupt you.
We’re told America is the greatest nation in the world, yet we rank 30th out of 38 OECD countries in life expectancy, while 37 of those 38 countries guarantee universal healthcare.
If they can do it, why can’t we?
