Campaign Promises
Our campaign is focused on making life more affordable, putting Hoosiers ahead of corporate interests, and restoring accountability in Washington.

- Fight for Medicare for All.
- Fully fund education from child care to college by taxing Wall Street.
- End U.S. wars in the Middle East and bring our troops home.
- Put Hoosiers first, not corporations and foreign governments.
- Release the Epstein files and prosecute the criminals.
- Ban stock trading by Congress and the President.
- Make corporate polluters pay, not working families.
