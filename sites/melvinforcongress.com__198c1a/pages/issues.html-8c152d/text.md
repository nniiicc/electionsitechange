HOUSING REFORM & ANTI-HOUSING CORRUPTION
Agenda
Shift from renter instability to ownership, stability, and community control.
Action Plan
TOPA – Tenant Opportunity to Purchase Act (CORE POLICY)
Empower tenants to become homeowners and stakeholders.
- Require landlords to notify tenants of intent to sell
- Grant tenants the first right to make an offer before public listing
- Provide a structured time window for tenants to:
- Organize
- Secure financing
- Submit a competitive bid
- Mandate landlords to fairly consider tenant offers
- Provide tenants with:
- Legal guidance
- Financial resources
- Technical assistance
Goal: Turn renters into owners, stabilize neighborhoods, and prevent displacement
Additional Housing Actions
- Pass an Anti-Flip Tax to stop predatory speculation
- Expand Cease & Desist Zones to protect homeowners
- Reform SCRIE/DRIE & SCHE/DHE income caps to reflect real NYC costs
- Balance short-term rental policies to protect both homeowners and tenants
Outcome
More ownership → stronger communities → long-term affordability
