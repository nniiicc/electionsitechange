DONATE WHO IS MARK?
Mark Noland is a Montana-born businessman, community leader, and devoted family man who has spent decades serving the Flathead Valley.
A graduate of Whitefish High School, Mark and his wife have been married for over 45 years and have six children and twelve grandchildren.
For more than 40 years he owned and operated a business in the Flathead Valley while remaining active in his church and local organizations, including the Glacier Lions Club, Chamber of Commerce, and the Bigfork Emergency Resource Team.
Mark also served four terms in the Montana House and one term in the Senate, including multiple sessions as Chairman of the Business and Labor Committee, where he earned a reputation for steady, conservative leadership.
