Rick Beck State Representative District 65
Thank you for your interest in learning more about me.
My name is Rick Beck I am your Arkansas State Representative in House District 65 which includes Conway County and portions of Perry County.
My youth was spent in St.
Vincent, Arkansas and my home now is just west of Center Ridge.
Having lived and been involved in my community for the past 40 years I understand the challenges our families face in regards to jobs and education in the district.
I am dedicated to working with state government in order to put this district on the path to job growth.
I welcome your feedback, thoughts and ideas on how we can work together to bring industry and opportunity to this area.
