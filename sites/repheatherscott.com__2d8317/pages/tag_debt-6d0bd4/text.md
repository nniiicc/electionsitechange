As the tentative March 24th session deadline drew closer, there was an urgency to “get done and go home”.
This reckless pace of Pelosi-style “pass-it-to-find-out what’s-in-it” style of legislating is counter productive to what Idaho really needs and justification for a major overhaul of the committee system.
Idahoans deserve quality legislation.
Because of this, a group of liberty legislators took the initiative to actually have a few bills read out loud for the entire House.
