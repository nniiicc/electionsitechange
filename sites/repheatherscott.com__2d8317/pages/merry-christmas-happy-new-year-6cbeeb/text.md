Happy New Year!
The Christmas season is upon us!
My wish and prayer for you and your family is one of peace, contentment and true appreciation for our freedom.
We know we face a future of uncertainty and hardship but can be comforted in knowing American generations before us faced the same evil predicaments and overcame them by faith, persistence, and courage.
This world is so temporary, but while we are here, we must do our part to be salt and light and share the truth of the best gift of all.
“For a child will be born to us, a son will be given to us; And the government will rest on His shoulders; And His name will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Eternal Father, Prince of Peace.
There will be no end to the increase of His government or of peace, On the throne of David and over his kingdom, to establish it and to uphold it with justice and righteousness from then on and forevermore.
The zeal of the LORD of hosts will accomplish this.” (Isaiah 9:6-7) Let us embrace the New Year with a sense of purpose to share the truth of God’s love and commitment to save America and thus promote true freedom throughout the world!
Merry Christmas and Happy New Year!
In Liberty, Rep.
