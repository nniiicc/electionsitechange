We have been given such a gift with the birth of this nation and our declaration of independence from tyranny.

Today is no different.

May God have mercy on our country because of those willing to get on their knees and offer up prayers to God Almighty.
Only through His mercy will the Unites States return to the principles and beliefs that have made this country great.
