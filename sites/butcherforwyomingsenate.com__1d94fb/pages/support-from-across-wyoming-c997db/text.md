Support From Across Wyoming
I’m honored to have earned the confidence of people and organizations across many of the industries and institutions Wyoming families and communities rely on.
Their endorsement doesn’t mean we’ll agree on every issue, and it doesn’t determine how I’ll vote.
I’ll listen first to the people I represent, and I’ll also seek out the experience of people who know these issues firsthand.
Then I’ll ask questions, consider the consequences, and make the best decision I can for Senate District 21 and the people of Wyoming.
Representing the companies that mine and process Wyoming’s coal, trona, uranium, bentonite and other mineral resources.
Representing the livestock producers and ranching families who keep Wyoming’s agricultural heritage alive and productive.
Giving Wyoming physicians a voice in the decisions that affect their practices and the patients they care for.
Supporting candidates who understand the issues facing the companies that build Wyoming’s roads, infrastructure and public works.
