2023 Elections
As we approach the end of 2023, there is still one more election before we ramp up to the 2024 Elections.
- November Constitutional Amendment Election
- Voter Registration Deadline: October 10, 2023
- Vote by Mail Application Deadline: October 27, 2023
- Early Voting: October 23-November 3, 2023
- Election Day: November 7, 2023
2024 Elections
In 2024, there are three scheduled elections taking place.
Please note the below dates are subject to change.
Runoff elections may take place as necessary, but are not listed here.
- Primary Elections
- Voter Registration Deadline: February 5, 2024
- Vote by Mail Application Deadline: February 23, 2024
- Early Voting: February 20-March 1, 2024
- Election Day: March 5, 2024
- May Municipal Elections
- Voter Registration Deadline: April 4, 2024
- Vote by Mail Application Deadline: April 23, 2024
- Early Voting: April 22-May 4, 2024
- Election Day: May 4, 2024
- November General Election
- Voter Registration Deadline: October 7, 2024
- Vote by Mail Application Deadline: October 25, 2024
- Early Voting: October 21-November 1, 2024
- Election Day: November 5, 2024
