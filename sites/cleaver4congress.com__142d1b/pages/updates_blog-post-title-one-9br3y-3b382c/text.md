Happy Veterans Day!
Today, we recognize the bravery of the men and women who have selflessly served and fought for our country.
To our men and women in uniform: thank you for ensuring our freedoms.
Your immeasurable sacrifice is a debt we cannot fully repay.
Veterans are the human embodiments of patriotism and the American spirit.
Their dedication has protected our nation and upheld the freedoms we cherish.
We must do more than just offer them our words of encouragement: I’m committed and will always fight for our veterans and their families, working to ensure they receive the support and benefits they’ve rightfully earned.
To all of our nation’s veterans, thank you again for your service and sacrifice.
With gratitude,
Congressman Emanuel Cleaver, II
