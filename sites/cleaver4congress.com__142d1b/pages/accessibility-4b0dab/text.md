Cleaver for Congress is committed to ensuring that our website and digital services are accessible to all individuals, including people with disabilities.
We believe every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence, and we strive to make our online presence inclusive for all users.
Our Efforts We are working toward compliance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG 2.1), which outline best practices for designing and maintaining accessible websites.
While we do not currently offer an accessibility widget, we continue to review and improve our website to remove barriers and enhance usability.
Ongoing Commitment Despite our best efforts, some content may not yet fully meet accessibility standards.
This may be due to limitations in available technology or because an appropriate solution has not yet been identified.
We are dedicated to ongoing improvements and welcome feedback to help us serve every visitor better.
Here for You If you experience difficulty accessing any part of cleaverforcongress.com or need assistance with any aspect of our site, please contact us during normal business hours and we will be happy to assist.
