Primary Election 2026
- Absentee voting opens: Approximately six weeks before the primary on Tuesday, August 4, 2026.
- No-excuse, in-person absentee voting: Begins two weeks before the election.
General Election 2026
- Absentee voting opens: Approximately six weeks before the general election on Tuesday, November 3, 2026.
- No-excuse, in-person absentee voting: Starts two weeks before the election.
