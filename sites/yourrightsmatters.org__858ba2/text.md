About
Who Am I?
- I am proud to be an American.
- I honor the sacrifices of those who fought for freedom.
- I acknowledge past injustices and refuse to let them define my identity.
- I will learn from history and work to ensure equal opportunity for all.
- My pride is rooted in American ideals of liberty, opportunity, and rule of law.
- I reject inherited shame but accept responsibility to promote justice.
- I stand for unity, progress, and a future where everyone can thrive.
- I celebrate our national achievements while supporting honest reckoning and reform.
- I will donate my time and resources to causes that strengthen our communities.
- I will use my life experience to mentor, advocate, and support people in need.
Our Mission
Building a Better Tomorrow
Build trust between law enforcement and the state of Colorado by focusing on safety while ensuring residents are connected with the right social and support services.
Work to reduce disparities between wealthy, middle-class, and lower-income residents, ensuring opportunity and fairness for every family.
Encourage both new and long-standing businesses to grow, bringing more good-paying jobs and economic stability to the state of Colorado.
Champion safe spaces and opportunities for young people to grow, lead, and succeed.
Why
Why I Am Standing for Civil Rights
My faith has inspired me to step into leadership and service for my community.
I am community-driven, motivated by a deep sense of purpose and a passion to make a meaningful difference.
I believe in the power of faith, hope, and action to create positive change.
My commitment is rooted in love, compassion, transparency, and accountability—ensuring that systems meant to serve the people operate fairly and justly.
I want to bring awareness and a better understanding of the protections afforded by our constitutional rights.
Having personally experienced violations of these rights within our Colorado judicial system, I know firsthand how crucial it is to defend and uphold these fundamental freedoms.
It is essential that we address injustices to ensure fairness and justice for all.
My mission is to advocate for a legal system that truly respects and upholds the principles of justice, equality, and constitutional protections.
With a focus on inclusive, community-centered leadership, I strive to foster an environment where all residents can thrive.
