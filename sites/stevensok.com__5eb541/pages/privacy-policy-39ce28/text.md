Information We Collect We collect and process the following information when you interact with our website: - Personal Information:Name, email address, and phone number when you sign up for updates, volunteer, or complete forms. - Donation Information: When donating, you are redirected to Anedot, an independent, secure platform.

How We Use Your Information We use your information only for campaign purposes, including: - Sending email communications via Brevo (updates, news, and volunteer opportunities). - Processing donations and complying with Oklahoma and federal campaign finance regulations. - Improving and securing website performance. - Complying with state and federal reporting and legal obligations.
Information Sharing and Disclosure -With Campaign Vendors: Your contact information may be shared with trusted service providers such as Brevo (for emailing) and Anedot (for donations) strictly for campaign-related functions. - Legal Compliance: Information may be disclosed as required by campaign finance laws, regulatory authorities, or if compelled by legal process. - No Sales or Rentals:We do not sell, rent, or exchange your personal information with third parties for commercial purposes.
Data Security We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data.
However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.
We encourage you to use discretion when sharing sensitive information online.
Cookies and Tracking Our website does not use cookies, web beacons, or other tracking technologies.
We do not collect information about your browsing activity on our site, and we do not use analytics or advertising trackers of any kind.
Third-Party Services - Brevo: Used for managing and sending campaign emails.
Their privacy policy applies when you receive campaign communications through this service. - Anedot: Processes online donations.
Please review Anedot’s privacy and security policy before submitting payment information; our Campaign does not receive or store your credit card data.
Children’s Privacy Our website and campaign activities are intended for individuals aged 18 and older.
We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13.
If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us for removal.
Your Choices - Unsubscribe: You can opt out of email communications at any time by following the unsubscribe link in our emails. - Contact Us: To access, correct, or request deletion of your information, please use the contact details below.
Changes to this Policy We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy as needed.
Changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Effective Date.” Continued use of the website indicates acceptance of changes.
