Cole understands the strain rising property taxes place on homeowners, farmers, and small business owners in District 51.
He’s committed to cutting taxes and crushing waste so families aren’t taxed out of their homes or land.
His plan focuses on limiting government to its core duties and reducing reliance on property tax hikes through smarter, leaner budgeting.
He’s dedicated to building the brightest future for Oklahoma students through innovation and local leadership.
Cole supports empowering parents with real choices, expanding vocational and trade pathways, and aligning education with real-world careers.
His focus is on preparing every student to succeed in college or the workforce—without the weight of unnecessary debt.
Cole believes that individual freedoms are the cornerstone of a healthy republic.
He is committed to defending the rights of Oklahomans against unnecessary government intrusion—whether it’s protecting Second Amendment rights, safeguarding free speech, or ensuring due process under the law.
His position is rooted in the principle that personal liberty should not be surrendered in the name of convenience or overregulation.
Cole is a staunch advocate for Oklahoma’s sovereignty and the principle that state and local governance should take precedence over federal overreach.
He supports using all constitutional tools available to push back against unfunded mandates, federal dictates, and regulations that do not serve Oklahoma’s best interests.
With a focus on fostering economic growth, he promotes policies that reduce unnecessary regulations, encourage entrepreneurship, and make it easier for all businesses to thrive.
He believes in removing barriers that stifle innovation and in creating an environment where businesses can expand and hire more Oklahomans.
Cole supports stronger enforcement of existing immigration laws and common-sense reforms that protect jobs, public safety, and Oklahoma taxpayers.
He believes in securing the nation’s borders, preventing illegal employment practices, and ensuring that immigration policies prioritize those who follow legal processes.
He views excessive bureaucracy as one of the greatest threats to efficient governance.
He advocates for streamlining government processes, reducing redundant agencies, and holding state departments accountable for wasteful spending (DOGE).
In his vision, government should serve the people quickly and effectively—not trap them in endless red tape.
Cole adheres to the principles and original intent of the U.S.
Constitution.
He believes that government powers should remain strictly limited to those explicitly granted, and that personal rights enumerated in the Bill of Rights must be protected without dilution or reinterpretation by activist courts.
Cole is a strong supporter of Oklahoma’s energy industry.
He believes that energy independence strengthens both the economy and national security.
His stance includes fighting against federal policies that harm Oklahoma’s energy sector and promoting responsible development that benefits local communities.
He is Pro- Oil & Gas as his family business started in the Oklahoma oil patch and continues to hire more hardworking Oklahomans.
Cole is committed to ensuring fairness and transparency in how property owners are treated when government projects require land acquisition.
He believes compensation should reflect the true value and future potential of the property, protecting the long-term interests of local residents in District 51 while maintaining responsible infrastructure development.
