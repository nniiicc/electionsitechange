Cole is a lifelong Oklahoman, dedicated family man, and successful entrepreneur.
Raised in Middleberg and a proud University of Oklahoma baseball alum, he leads Stevens Trucking—growing it from a regional fleet into a nationwide industry leader recognized twice on the Inc. 5000 list.
Drawing on his experience, Cole champions common-sense solutions for Oklahoma’s future.
He played a key role in crafting and passing SB20, the “Oklahoma Secure Roads and Safe Trucking Act,” ensuring safer roadways for everyone.
Cole continues his service by working with state leaders to implement responsible technology use in schools, always prioritizing families and community safety.
Cole, his wife Tara, and their three children make their home in Middleberg, where faith, integrity, and service shape his commitment to District 51.
If elected, Cole promises to serve all constituents with honesty and energy, always guided by faith and a deep love for Oklahoma.
