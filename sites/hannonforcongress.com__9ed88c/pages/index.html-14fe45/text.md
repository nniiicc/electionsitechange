I’m running for NY-26 because the issues we face stopped being theoretical for me and became personal.
I grew up blue-collar, working in my dad’s construction business, and I learned early that nobody is coming to save you.
You work, you provide, you take care of your family.
Then I became a parent, and I watched powerful institutions try to push my child toward irreversible medical decisions without real transparency, without real safeguards, and with life-altering consequences.
I had to fight for my own kid the way any parent would and I realized if it can happen to my family, it can happen to yours.
So I’m stepping up - not because I’ve always wanted a title, but because I can’t stay on the sidelines.
I’m running to put working families first again: safer streets, lower costs you can feel, and common-sense protections that keep kids safe and let parents be heard.
I believe in problem solving, not self-serving political pandering or rhetoric.
Three priorities - clear plans, measurable outcomes, and honest leadership.
Safer communities
- Support local and federal agency cooperation through grants
- Enforce immigration and border protection policies with a priortization on serious public safety threats
- Strengthen worksite compliance and lawful hiring
- Aggressively disrupt fentanyl trafficking networks
- Keep the protected classes protected: Safeguard spaces and opportunities based on biological similiarities
- Support initiatives that strengthen father engagement and childhood involvement
Lower costs
- Provide relief to struggling working class families through tax rebates
- Lower prescription drug and health care costs - especially for seniors.
Support cures instead of profits & reform middle-men pharmacy benefit managers
- Prohibit state and local governments from banning or limiting specific energy sources
- Less red tape on federal and private land leases for energy or mineral extraction
Protecting children
- Protect kids online: stronger privacy safeguards, limits on targeting.
Prevent commercial AI from generating any content involving minors
- No child is born into the wrong body: End the maiming and sterilization of children by criminally targeting those who benefit from it
- Designate federally approved sex-ed instructional material that's focused solely around abstinence, STD prevention, and safe sex
- Reinstate capital punishment eligibility for convicted child rapists
A district where you can afford to live, feel safe on your street, and trust that kids are protected at school and online.
- Public safety that’s both tough and smart.
- A stronger economy built from the middle out.
- Respect for parents and protections for children.
- Constituent service that actually helps.
