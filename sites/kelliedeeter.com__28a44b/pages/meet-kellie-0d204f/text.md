Meet Kellie Deeter
Mother, wife, health care professional, small business owner, and trusted voice for Huron and Lorain Counties.
Kellie Deeter wears many hats…
She's called Huron County home for decades and has built her businesses here.
She's a dedicated healthcare professional as a certified nurse anesthetist and certified nurse practitioner.
She and her husband Keven have raised their children in Northern Ohio.
Kellie has spent her life raising her children and helping people throughout our community through her decades in healthcare.
She wants to use her business and healthcare experience to better our communities in Lorain and Huron Counties and find conservative solutions to our most pressing problems.
