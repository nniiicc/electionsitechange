About Bob
Bob Harvie was elected to the Board of Bucks County Commissioners in November 2019, and was sworn into office on January 6, 2020.
He was named chair of the board on January 5, 2022.
Harvie was re-elected to a second term in November 2023 and sworn into office on January 2, 2024.
