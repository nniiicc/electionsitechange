“Southwest Oklahoma deserves a senator who is accessible, grounded, and committed to representing the people— not political insiders.” Pastor Curtis Erwin is a lifelong Oklahoman, husband, father, and founding pastor of Crossroads Baptist Church in Elgin.
After working at the Goodyear plant in Lawton, he answered his call to ministry, growing Crossroads from just a handful of members to more than 600 through service and community outreach.
