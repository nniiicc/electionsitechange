CONTRIBUTE Log In Official Endorsements Rayneika is proud to have earned the support of organizations and community leaders who believe in the power of showing up, listening first, and working together to make life better for families in District 6. ​ For Rayneika, endorsements aren’t about politics — they’re about partnership.
They represent a growing coalition of people who share a commitment to stability, opportunity, and dignity for every neighborhood in our district.
As a working woman, a first‑generation college graduate, a union leader, and someone who has spent her career serving the public, she knows real progress happens when we bring people together and stay grounded in the everyday realities our communities face. ​ This campaign is built on conversations — honest ones.
It starts with listening to what people are going through, understanding what’s getting in their way, and then rolling up our sleeves to create solutions that actually help.
Every endorsement reflects that shared mission: to strengthen our neighborhoods, support working families, and build a Maryland where everyone can thrive. ​ Together, we are building something bigger than a campaign.
We are building a community that shows up for each other.
AFSCME Maryland Council 3 Rayneika’s connection to AFSCME Council 3 comes from years of working alongside Maryland employees and serving as a local chapter leader.
Their support reflects the work she has done within the union community and her deep belief in the values AFSCME stands for.
Being part of the green machine taught her to listen first, understand the needs of working people, and build real solutions that make a difference.
AFSCME has been one of her strongest sources of support, and the lessons she learned through this work continue to guide her every day.
Baltimore County Progressive Democrats Rayneika’s connection to the Baltimore County Progressive Democrats comes from shared values and years of community work.
Their support reflects a relationship built on service, listening, and a commitment to strengthening local neighborhoods.
She has long admired the organization’s focus on fairness, transparency, and community‑driven solutions.
Rayneika is grateful for their support and for the work they do to uplift voices across Baltimore County.
Maryland State Education Association Rayneika’s connection to the Maryland State Education Association is deeply personal.
As a student with a learning disability, she relied on the support, patience, and dedication of public school educators who helped her navigate challenges and find her voice.
Those experiences shaped who she is today and grounded her belief in the power of strong public schools.
Her commitment to protecting educators comes from that lived experience.
She understands how essential it is for teachers and school staff to be respected on the job, supported with real resources, and safe in their classrooms.
MSEA’s support reflects shared values and a shared belief in ensuring every student — especially those who need additional support — has the opportunity to succeed.
Moms Demand Action Gun violence has had a direct and lasting impact on Rayneika’s life.
She has seen firsthand how irresponsible gun laws can devastate families and leave permanent scars on the people they touch.
Those experiences shaped her understanding of what safety truly means for a community.
Her connection to Moms Demand Action reflects shared values around responsibility, prevention, and protecting families.
For Rayneika, common‑sense gun laws are not abstract policy ideas — they are a necessary part of keeping neighborhoods safe and preventing other families from experiencing the pain her own has endured.
She is grateful for the support of an organization that works every day to reduce gun violence and uplift the voices of survivors and impacted communities.
Mental Health Now!
Mental health has always been a deeply personal issue for Rayneika.
She believes no one should feel embarrassed or ashamed to talk about what they are going through.
Her experiences in community corrections and in her own life have shown her how failing to address mental health needs leaves individuals and entire neighborhoods vulnerable.
Being recognized by Mental Health Now reflects her commitment to treating mental health as a basic right, not an afterthought.
For Rayneika, supporting mental health access is about ensuring people can get help early, safely, and without stigma.
Maryland State and D.C.
AFL-CIO Rayneika is endorsed by the Maryland State and D.C.
AFL‑CIO, an organization that represents and supports unions across the region.
This endorsement is deeply personal to her.
As a long‑time union leader, she has spent years standing with Maryland’s public employees, advocating for fair treatment, and helping workers navigate real challenges on the job.
Having the support of an organization that brings together so many unions carries special meaning.
The labor movement shaped her leadership — teaching her to listen first, build trust, and create solutions that strengthen the lives of working people.
The endorsement from the Maryland State and D.C.
AFL‑CIO reflects those shared values and the work she has dedicated herself to within the union community.
