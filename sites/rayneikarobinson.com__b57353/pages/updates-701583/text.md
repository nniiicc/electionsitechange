MAY 3, 2026
Baltimore County Progressive Democrats Club Endorses Rayneika Robinson for State Delegate in District 6
Baltimore County, MD — The Baltimore County Progressive Democrats Club has officially endorsed Rayneika Robinson for State Delegate in District 6, recognizing her as a leader rooted in community, public service, and practical solutions that strengthen neighborhoods across East Baltimore County.
The club’s endorsement reflects Robinson’s long record of advocating for working families, improving public safety, and championing visible improvements that make daily life better for residents in Essex, Dundalk, Middle River, and surrounding communities.
“I am honored to receive the endorsement of the Baltimore County Progressive Democrats Club,” Robinson said.
“This support means a great deal because it comes from people who care deeply about our communities and who understand the importance of strong, accountable leadership.
They see the work I’ve done, and they believe in the work we can accomplish together.”
As President of AFSCME Local 3661 and a leader within Maryland’s public service workforce, Robinson has built a reputation for transparency, fairness, and community‑centered problem solving.
Her campaign focuses on practical, high‑impact issues such as safe travel routes for students and seniors, stronger public safety partnerships, stable housing, and responsive government services.
“District 6 deserves a delegate who shows up, listens, and delivers real results,” Robinson said.
“The club’s endorsement reinforces our shared commitment to building safer neighborhoods, supporting working families, and ensuring every resident feels seen and heard.”
The Baltimore County Progressive Democrats Club is known for supporting candidates who demonstrate integrity, community engagement, and a clear vision for progress.
Their endorsement adds growing momentum to Robinson’s campaign as she continues meeting with residents, volunteers, and local leaders across the district.
“This campaign is about building a stronger, more connected District 6,” Robinson added.
“I am grateful for this endorsement and excited to continue this work with the people who call our community home.”

Hitting the Ground Running

As a people-powered campaign, every contribution helps us reach neighbors, strengthen outreach, and build momentum.

This is only the beginning.
I am grateful for everyone who has already believed in this movement and excited to continue building something meaningful together.
