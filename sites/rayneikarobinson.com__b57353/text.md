Our Platform
We are dedicated to building a brighter Maryland through unity, leadership, and a commitment to serving every family in District 6.
Our core priorities turn community vision into real action.
Quality of Life and Public Safety
Rayneika believes public safety requires strong accountability and clear expectations.
She is committed to clean, safe neighborhoods, responsive municipal services, and holding systems and individuals accountable so residents can feel secure where they live.
At the same time, she supports second chances rooted in responsibility and opportunity, ensuring community safety is strengthened while people who do the work to rebuild can move forward.
Affordability and Economic Stability for Working Families
Rayneika believes affordability starts with fairness, including closing corporate tax loopholes so working families are not left carrying the burden.
She supports smart, values-driven state budgets that invest in people, strengthen communities, and promote long-term stability.
Rayneika is proud to have earned the support of organizations that share her commitment to putting people first.
Her work has always centered on listening to the community, showing up with compassion, and building coalitions that focus on practical, common‑sense solutions.
The endorsements she has received reflect collaboration, service, and dedication to strengthening neighborhoods across Maryland.
I am not a career politician.
I am a lifelong Marylander who knows what it means to struggle and to persevere.
I have experienced homelessness, watched addiction impact my family, relied on state-funded healthcare, and navigated life with a learning disability.
Those experiences shape how I lead, who I fight for, and why I believe people deserve dignity, support, and real opportunity.
Rayneika Robinson
