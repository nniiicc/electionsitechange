Information We Collect We collect the following information when you sign up: Full Name Email Address (required) Phone Number (optional; required only if you opt in to text message communications) Communication Preferences (email is always enabled; text message is optional) County of Residence Volunteer Interest Areas Optional Notes How We Use Your Information Your information is used to: Contact you about volunteer opportunities via email, and optionally via text message if you have opted in Coordinate campaign activities in your area Send updates about the campaign Organize volunteers by county and interest area Data Protection We take the security of your personal information seriously.
Your data is stored securely and is only accessible to authorized campaign staff.
We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties outside of the campaign.
Your Rights You have the right to: Request to see the information we have collected about you Request correction of any inaccurate information Request deletion of your information at any time Opt out of communications To exercise these rights, please contact us at juliefortier4il@gmail.com or comms.fortiercampaign@gmail.com Political Communication Consent By providing your email address, you consent to receive email communications from the Julie Fortier for Congress campaign.
Email communication is required for all volunteers.
Communications may include volunteer opportunities, event invitations, and campaign updates.
You may unsubscribe at any time.
Text Message Communications: If you optionally opt in to receive text messages, you may receive personal text messages from campaign staff and volunteers.
You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying to let us know you no longer wish to receive messages.
Federal Election Commission Compliance This volunteer signup system is operated in compliance with Federal Election Commission (FEC) regulations.
Volunteer time and activities are considered in-kind contributions and are subject to FEC guidelines.
Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
