Meet Julie Fortier
Veteran.
Scientist.

Julie Fortier knows what it means to serve.
A U.S.
Air Force veteran, cancer research scientist, mother, and longtime community volunteer, Julie has spent more than 25 years building her life and serving her community in Southern Illinois.
She's running for Congress because too many Washington decisions are making life harder for the people who live here.
Julie will bring a scientist's approach to government: listen, examine the evidence, solve problems, and measure results.
Whether it's healthcare, working families, or strengthening our local economy, she's ready to put Southern Illinois first.
