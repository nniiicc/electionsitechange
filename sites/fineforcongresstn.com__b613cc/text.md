Where I Stand Get Involved Meet the Candidate About Bruce Bruce Fine is running as an Independent candidate for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Tennessee's 2nd Congressional District.
He believes that the people of East Tennessee deserve a representative who answers to them — not to party bosses or special interests.
Frustrated by partisan gridlock and a Congress that prioritizes political games over real solutions, Bruce is stepping up to offer voters a genuine alternative.
He's committed to transparent governance, fiscal responsibility, and putting the needs of Knoxville and the surrounding communities first.
Bruce filed his candidacy with the Tennessee Secretary of State on February 27, 2026, and looks forward to working to earn your respect, trust, and your vote — because real representation shouldn't require a party label.

House of Representatives Tennessee District 2 so now I can go on to earn your respect, trust and your vote.
To bring honest common sense back to government.
I have been a lifelong registered republican and I simply can no longer agree with some things that are going on in the GOP and I am running as an Independent candidate.
I am not part of the entrenched political machine and have no ties to any special interests.
I am not a career politician and never will be.
I was not born into privilege or wealth.
I am among the working "class" and know real well what it means to work for a dollar and how to stretch that dollar as far as possible.
The basic struggle to survive paying the necessary everyday expenses has become more and more difficult for the vast majority of Americans.
Young couples starting out with rock solid jobs can no longer afford to buy a house or a new vehicle.
The difficulty to make ends meet and balance the checkbook has become increasingly worst.
The need for affordable housing has never been greater.
We live in the greatest and wealthiest country in the history of our planet.
Don't our children and grandchildren deserve the absolute best education possible whether they attend public school or are home schooled.
So that they can be the best prepared for jobs, research, innovation and creativity now and in the future to rank top in the world.
At the same time every citizen deserves to have the best possible healthcare.
I am extremely concerned about the cuts made to healthcare and may just cripple or destroy the small local clinics, like what we have in Washburn, and local Hospitals such as we have in New Tazewell.
The one thing that I find most troubling is the abandonment of fiscal responsibility as the federal government has wracked up a $38 Trillion debt that continues to increase while the few people who have the vast majority of the money and wealth in our country have been given one of the biggest cut in taxes ever.
For the rest of us taxes are taken out of our paychecks every week, no matter what.
The Platform Where I Stand Real solutions for real people — not talking points crafted by party strategists.
Economy & Jobs Supporting small businesses, bringing good-paying jobs to East Tennessee, and ensuring economic opportunity for every family.
Healthcare Making healthcare more affordable and accessible, protecting coverage for pre-existing conditions, and lowering prescription drug costs.
Veterans & Military Honoring our veterans with the benefits they've earned and ensuring our service members have the support they need.
Education Investing in public schools, supporting teachers, and expanding vocational training and community college opportunities.
Government Reform Term limits, campaign finance reform, and ending the revolving door between Congress and lobbyists.
Environment & Energy Protecting Tennessee's natural beauty while supporting an all-of-the-above energy strategy that keeps costs low.
