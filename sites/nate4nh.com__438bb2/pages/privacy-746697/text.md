Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 19, 2026
Overview
This privacy policy describes how Nate for NH ("we," "the campaign") collects, uses, and protects information submitted through nate4nh.com.
We are committed to protecting your privacy and will never sell your personal information.
Information We Collect
We collect information you voluntarily provide through our volunteer sign-up form, including:
- Name
- Email address
- ZIP code
- Phone number (optional)
- Whether you live in Concord Ward 6
- Whether you'd like to join our mailing list
We do not collect payment information directly.
Donations are processed through ActBlue, which has its own privacy policy.
How We Use Your Information
Information collected through the volunteer form is used solely to:
- Contact you about volunteer opportunities and campaign events
- Send campaign updates if you opt into our mailing list
- Coordinate canvassing and phone banking in your area
We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties outside the campaign except as required by law or to comply with New Hampshire campaign finance reporting requirements.
Analytics
This website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site.
Google Analytics collects anonymized data such as pages visited, time spent on site, and general geographic region (state/country level).
It does not collect personally identifiable information.
You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
Form Processing
Volunteer sign-up forms are processed by Netlify, our website hosting provider.
Netlify stores form submissions securely on our behalf.
Their privacy policy governs how they handle this data on our behalf.
Data Retention
We retain volunteer contact information for the duration of the campaign.
You may request removal from our contact list at any time by emailing us.
Your Rights
You have the right to:
- Request a copy of the information we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
- Opt out of campaign communications at any time
Contact
Questions about this privacy policy or your data can be directed to:
info@nate4nh.com
