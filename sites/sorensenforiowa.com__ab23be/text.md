Proud to Represent Southwest Iowa!
House District 23 includes all of Adair and Madison counties and portions of Dallas, Union, and Clarke counties.
Our district includes farming communities, small businesses, growing towns, and families whose livelihoods depend on a wide range of industries.
Each community has its own priorities, and I believe understanding those differences is an important part of representing the district. [Explore House District 23 →] More Than Politics...
Long before I entered the Legislature, I began painting the Freedom Rock® as a tribute to America's veterans.
That project has given me opportunities to travel, meet remarkable people, and hear stories of service and sacrifice.
It has also reinforced something I carry into public office: every community has a story, and every person deserves to be heard.
I'm grateful for the opportunity to serve the communities that helped shape my life. [Learn More About Bubba and the Freedom Rock® →] Connect With Bubba!
