JOHN DONOHUE
Constitutional Conservative
As a Republican candidate for the office of State Representative for Wisconsin’s 46th Assembly District, I greatly value:
- Individual liberty
- Economic opportunity
- Limited government
- Rule of law
- Personal responsibility
- Security at home and abroad
Achieving positive change in our state government requires that we strengthen the bonds of trust between all our citizens and our elected representatives, renewing respect for all points of view, while honoring those great values out of which all good public policy emanates.
As an Army veteran, having honorably served and defended our country for 27 years, I now stand ready to “serve and defend” Wisconsin to achieve the greatest effectiveness in our government and highest quality of life for all of our citizens.
Let’s get to work!
