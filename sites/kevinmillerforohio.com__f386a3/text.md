Kevin Miller
Our State Representative
Kevin Miller has served as a state trooper, labor relations management advocate, and post commander with the Ohio State Highway Patrol over the past twenty years.
He gained legislative experience as a liaison for the Patrol’s government affairs, and he’s putting that experience to work representing us at the Statehouse.
As a husband and father of two, he holds family values close to his heart and will be a leader we can look up to.
