“This milestone reflects the strong support we continue to receive from across District 22,” said Senator Wipf.
“I appreciate the trust and encouragement from voters and look forward to continuing to serve and earn their support in the months ahead.” Since his appointment by Governor Rhoden, Wipf has focused on supporting agriculture, expanding education opportunities, protecting local decision-making, and advancing policies that help South Dakota families get ahead.
Absentee voting begins April 17, 2026.
The Republican Primary Election will be held on June 2, 2026.
District 22 includes Beadle, Clark, and Spink counties.
