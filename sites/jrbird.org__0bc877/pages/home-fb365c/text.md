Why I’m Running
“I am a lifelong resident of rural Utah, father of four, business owner, and a former Mayor.
I enjoy farming, serving in the community, and living in a small town built on the values of faith, family, self-reliance, and hard work.
I'm running to represent these values at the state level and deliver common-sense solutions to the various issues we face as a district.
I will work to ensure that our area's unique challenges are at the forefront of policy decisions and lawmaking.
My priorities are economic stability, improving critical infrastructure, education, protecting our farmers and ranchers, fiscal responsibility, and bringing an unprecedented level of accountability to state governance.”
