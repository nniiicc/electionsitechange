Get Involved
Our campaign is powered by people like you.
Every action, every hour, and every contribution helps us build a stronger, fairer San Diego.
There are many ways you can join us in making a real difference in our community:
- Volunteer: Join our team of dedicated volunteers!
We need your help with phone banking, canvassing, organizing events, and more.
Together, we can reach every corner of our district and make sure every voice is heard.
- Host an Event: Bring the campaign to your neighborhood!
Organize a meet-and-greet, community gathering, or fundraising event in your home or local space.
We’ll provide support and guidance every step of the way.
- Attend Events: Come meet Juan and your neighbors at rallies, town halls, and community events.
Be part of the conversation, ask questions, and help shape the future of our district.
- Spread the Word: Amplify our message!
Share campaign updates with your friends, family, and neighbors.
Follow us on social media and encourage your community to get involved.
