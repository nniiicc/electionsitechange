Niki Tsongas.
The Westford resident is the fifth person to express serious interest in succeeding Tsongas when the longtime congresswoman steps away at the end of her term after a decade of service.
Trahan, a 43-year-old Democrat, filed an official statement of organization with the Federal Election Commission Sunday.
Since launching an online fundraiser on Sept.
13, she has raised more than $# toward her push for the 3rd Congressional District.
