Pressley Endorses Congresswoman Lori Trahan for Re-Election Oct.
29, 2019 Endorsed by Rep.
Ayanna Pressley (D-MA-07) “I am so grateful to have Lori as a friend and partner in Congress – she is a trailblazer in her own right, a committed and effective legislator, and a tireless advocate for the people of MA-03.
I am proud to have worked with Lori on the first bill I introduced in Congress – to provide back pay for federal contract workers impacted by the government shutdown – and I am deeply thankful for Lori’s continued leadership on behalf of workers, and on pressing issues like the cost of prescription drugs, access to affordable higher education, and equity in economic opportunity.
Lori’s work in Congress has helped to uplift communities across the MA-03.” — Rep.
