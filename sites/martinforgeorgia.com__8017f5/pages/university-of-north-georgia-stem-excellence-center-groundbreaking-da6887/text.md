University of North Georgia - STEM Excellence Center Groundbreaking
As Chairman of the House Higher Education Committee, I know few things matter more to me than ensuring Georgia's universities have the facilities to educate our workforce and prepare Georgians for the future of work.
That's why I was proud to join the University of North Georgia in Dahlonega on July 7 to break ground on its new STEM Excellence Center.
This is an investment of about $49 million that will prepare Georgia's next generation of scientists, engineers, and innovators when it opens in 2028.
Congratulations to UNG's students, faculty, and leadership — and to everyone who helped make this milestone possible.
"This is the kind of investment in Georgia's future I'll keep working to deliver." -- Rep.
Chuck Martin 🎓
