DONATE All Posts Search Senator Rob Kupec Earns Endorsement from both Minnesota Farmers Union and Farmers Bureau #ago 2 min read Endorsements from both groups reflects Senator Kupec’s record of fighting for Minnesota agriculture MOORHEAD, MN — Senator Rob Kupec is proud to announce endorsements from both the Minnesota Farmers Union and Minnesota Farmers Bureau, two of the state's largest farming advocacy groups.
The rare endorsement from both groups, which often differ ideologically, is a clear demonstration of the confidence in Kupec’s work to strengthen Minnesota’s agriculture economy and deliver results for farmers.
Kupec has been a strong advocate for Minnesota’s agriculture economy throughout his first term, and serves as the Vice Chair on the Senate Agriculture, Rural Development, and Broadband Committee.
Kupec was a key leader in securing research and investment to make Minnesota a national leader in Sustainable Aviation Fuel, a new and growing sector that will boost revenues for farmers and the state.
Kupec also authored legislation adding farm equipment to the list of products under Minnesota’s Digital Fair Repair Act, and was also instrumental in establishing the state’s first grain indemnity fund.
Recently, Kupec has called on the Walz administration to align with federal executive order allowing use of red-dyed diesel for on-highway use in Minnesota, to give farmers some needed relief in the face of skyrocketing diesel prices.
He is committed to help Minnesota farmers, their families, and rural communities thrive.
“MFU PAC is proud to endorse Senator Rob Kupec who has been a strong champion for Minnesota’s family farmers,” said Minnesota Farmers Union PAC Chair Gary Wertish.
“From working to ensure farmers have the right to repair their own equipment to passing new safeguards for farmers when grain buyers default to expanding the Beginning Farmer Tax Credit, Senator Kupec has worked tirelessly on behalf of farmers and rural communities.” “Senator Rob Kupec has been a supporter of Minnesota agriculture through his work on the state agriculture budget and biofuels policy,” said Minnesota Farm Bureau PAC Committee Chair Delvin Durheim.
“We commend his efforts on these issues and appreciate working together on shared priorities that support our farmers.” “I am honored to receive the endorsement of both of these organizations that work to protect and strengthen Minnesota’s agriculture economy,” said Kupec.
“Farming is the cornerstone of our state; supporting rural communities, creating jobs, and feeding the world.
I’m proud to advocate for our farmers by making it more affordable to farm, protecting their investments, and ensuring the next generation has the opportunity to succeed.
I look forward to working hard in the Senate to keep Minnesota agriculture strong.
