Tribal Communities An enrolled member of the White Earth Nation, Peggy would be the first Native American woman to serve in the U.S.
Senate.
She will continue the tradition of those who have held this Senate seat before her-Senator Paul Wellstone, Senator Al Franken, and Senator Tina Smith–leading on issues important to tribal communities and seeking a seat on the Senate Committee on Indian Affairs.
In her time as a legislator and as Lt.
Governor, Peggy has demonstrated that all Minnesotans benefit when we have strong relationships with Minnesota’s Tribal nations.
Under Peggy’s leadership, Minnesota has become the model for Tribal-State relations grounded in a foundational government-to-government relationship and consultation with Minnesota’s 11 Tribal nations on state actions that impact them.
At the federal level, the Trump Administration is ignoring treaty and trust obligations and threatening tribal gaming rights.
Peggy will fight to ensure the federal government honors its commitments and invests in tribal communities.
The federal government has never fully lived up to treaty and trust obligations , and the Trump Administration has been even worse.
Peggy will hold the Administration to higher standards for working with Tribal governments.
Everyone needs access to healthcare; Peggy will fight for permanent advance appropriations for the Indian Health Service required to meet the needs of Native people across Minnesota and our country.
The Trump Administration is proposing drastic cuts to Tribal Colleges and Universities for future budgets and the Bureau of Indian Education–another failure of the government to meet its trust and treaty obligations.
Peggy will fight for the funding needed for teachers, students, and staff at these vital higher education institutions .
Minnesota became the first state to tackle the epidemic of Missing and Murdered Indigenous Relatives by establishing an office to support families and law enforcement in solving and prosecuting these crimes.
Peggy will work to ensure a permanent federal office is established to end the trafficking of and violence against Native women, girls, and our two-spirit relatives .
Everyone deserves to be safe wherever they choose to live.
Peggy will continue the work of Senator Smith and push for the Justice for Native Survivors of Sexual Violence Act .
Veterans & Servicemembers Military service is one of our nation’s highest callings and our service members and veterans deserve the full support and respect of our country.
Minnesota is home to over 20,000 active duty service members, National Guard, and reserve members, and over a quarter of a million men and women who served this country in the military.
Minnesotans take pride in honoring and supporting veterans, servicemembers, and their families.
During Peggy’s time as Lt.
Governor Minnesota has made historic investments in programs to support veterans leading to the opening of three new veteran homes, functionally ending veteran homelessness in eight out of nine Minnesota regions, and a new military museum scheduled to open next year.
Peggy is proud to work with the brave men and women of Minnesota who show up for our country every day.
The Trump Administration is working to cut and privatize as much of the U.S.
Department of Veterans Affairs as they can.
Peggy believes that it is the responsibility of the United States government to deliver robust, accessible, and quality care for our veterans .
We can’t pass off this duty to some company focused on profit.
Peggy will take the work and success of functionally ending veteran homelessness in most of Minnesota to Washington .
With dedicated federal resources, we can take the Minnesota model nationwide and make sure our veterans have the secure housing they deserve.
Peggy will fight to invest in veterans’ mental healthcare .
Their unique experiences, and higher rates of PTSD, depression, and suicide deserve a comprehensive federal response from robust research to innovative treatments and access.
In Minnesota and across the U.S., grieving families are having to delay plans and jump through hoops to get death certificates signed.
Peggy supports the bipartisan plan to require that veteran death certificates be signed a maximum of two days after they pass so their families can move forward.
Peggy supports providing the healthcare and benefits our Guard and Reserve members have earned regardless of whether their orders were for active or reserve duty to put an end to the healthcare and benefits disparity based on a technical difference.
