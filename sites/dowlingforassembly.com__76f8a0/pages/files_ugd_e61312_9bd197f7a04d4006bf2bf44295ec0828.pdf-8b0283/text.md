Rachael Dowling is a County Board Supervisor in Winnebago County and a community advocate based in Menasha, Wisconsin.
She is a multi small business owner with nearly two decades of experience in operations, client service, project management, and communications.
Rachael also works professionally as an FAA Part 107 licensed drone pilot, providing aerial documentation and imagery used for inspection, safety, and marketing purposes.
Her background includes service on local and county committees, including Parks and Recreation and Information Technology, along with additional community leadership roles.
Rachael is committed to practical, transparent government and focused on solutions that improve daily life for residents.
She and her husband have been married for 18 years and homeschool their four boys.
