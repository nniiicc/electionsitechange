Events & Community Conversations
Some of the best conversations don't happen behind a podium...
they happen sitting across a table or pulling up a lawn chair.
Throughout the campaign, I'll be hosting Community Conversations across Wisconsin Assembly District 53.
Sometimes you'll find me at a local park with a camp chair.
Other times, I'll grab a table at a neighborhood restaurant or coffee shop.
I'll also be attending candidate forums, debates, community events, and other opportunities to meet with the people I'm hoping to represent.Community Conversations aren't rallies.
There's no speech, no presentation, and definitely no microphone.
They're simply an opportunity for neighbors to sit down and talk about what's happening in our community.
Come with a question.
Come with an idea.
Come because you disagree with me and want to tell me why.
Or just stop by and introduce yourself.You don't need to RSVP, and you don't need to stay for the entire event.
Five minutes or an hour... you're welcome either way.
What can we talk about?
Pretty much anything.
Housing, property taxes, data centers, utility costs, schools, state government, local businesses, government transparency... or something I haven't been talking about yet that you think I should be.
I'll spend more time listening than talking.
Don't See a Time That Works?
I'd still love to meet you.
If you'd like me to bring a Community Conversation to your neighborhood, local park, coffee shop, restaurant, or community gathering... reach out.
I'm intentionally holding these at different locations, days, and times because not everyone has the same schedule.
This campaign isn't supposed to happen around you.
It's supposed to happen with you.
