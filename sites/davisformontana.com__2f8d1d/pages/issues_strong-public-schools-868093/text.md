Ben will prioritize creating a state program to recruit, train, and retain teachers who teach in under-served areas.
As the director of a non-profit that works with children beginning when youth are 4-6 years old, Ben believes that our best investment in education is to support universal pre-kindergarten programs and early elementary reading literacy programs that instill a love of learning, build healthy social emotional skills, and foster essential learning skills that build a strong foundation for school success.
Ben will also advocate for greater investment in business and industry partnerships with high schools and trade schools to ensure that youth are graduating from high school with job-ready skills that strengthen industry and businesses.
