Ben believes the state has an important role in supporting affordable housing development.
He will advocate for increasing state funding for horizontal infrastructure development for water, sewer, and roads to help expand our housing supply.
Ben will also work closely with elected officials to support the implementation of zoning code reforms which prioritize early planning, local control, and flexible development.
Ben will also prioritize legislation that protects renters from predatory landlords who unfairly keep renters' security deposits at the end of a lease.
