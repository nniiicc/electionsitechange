Ben Davis believes in
Restoring Responsible Government
Ben believes in the importance of an independent, non-partisan judiciary and he will fight to protect our judicial branch from legislative and executive over-reach.
Ben is proud of Montana's strong transparency and open meetings laws and he will work to ensure that the legislature and Gianforte administration comply with all open meetings and records laws so that our government operates in a transparent and responsive fashion.
If elected, Ben will prioritize passing legislation that benefits all Montanans, and he will build relationships with Democrats and Republicans and work across the aisle to pass meaningful legislation that benefits the entire community.
