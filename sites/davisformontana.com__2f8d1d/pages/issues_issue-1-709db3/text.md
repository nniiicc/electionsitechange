Ben personally experiences the frustration of rising prices every week when he buys groceries for his family and pays their monthly insurance premiums.
Ben will partner closely with legislators on both sides of the aisle to address the drivers of rising costs and take on corporate interests that exploit inflation as an excuse to unfairly increase their prices.
He will work to reduce property taxes and fight to ensure that health insurance remains affordable and accessible for all Montanans.
We need an economy that works for everyone and Ben has built businesses and organizations that created dozens of living wage jobs.
Ben fundamentally believes that lowering our prices will create greater possibilities for all Montanans.
