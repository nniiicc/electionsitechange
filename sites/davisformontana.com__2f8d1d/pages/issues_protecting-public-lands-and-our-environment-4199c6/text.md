Protecting Our Public Lands and Environment
There is nothing more special about Montana than our extraordinary public lands.
We have to do everything we can to protect them from being sold off to the highest bidder or being polluted by unscrupulous extractive industries.
Ben is an advocate for investments in renewable energy resources and will champion state level innovations to bring job-creating renewable energy projects to our state while doing our part to address climate change.
