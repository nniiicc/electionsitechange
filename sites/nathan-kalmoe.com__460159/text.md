Our Senate District,
Wisconsin's 27th!
The 27th district occupies ancestral & contemporary Ho-Chunk land, a place known as Teejop.
American settlers failed to drive them out of Wisconsin despite decades of ethnic cleansing.
The land was not ours to take, and the harm to Ho-Chunk people was atrocious.
Today, we respect the inherent sovereignty of the Ho-Chunk Nation and Wisconsin's other First Nations, we fight for the rights of Native Wisconsinites, & we recognize our obligation to do all we can to repair irreparable harms.
