Mustello to Recognize Butler County Veteran of the Year This Thursday
November 5, 2025
WHAT: Rep.
Marci Mustello (R-Butler) will be hosting her annual Butler County Veteran of the Year Luncheon.
The event is co-hosted by the Butler County Veterans Committee.
The luncheon will include the presentation of the Butler County Veteran of the Year.
WHO: Mustello; U.S.
Congressman Mike Kelly; a representative from U.S.
Sen.
Dave McCormick’s office; local and state officials; and the Butler County Veterans Committee.
WHEN: Thursday, Nov. 6, from 11:30 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.
WHERE: American Legion Post 778, 150 Legion Memorial Lane, Butler.
Media contact: Rick Leiner, 717-260-6437, rleiner@pahousegop.com, and Amber Lepic, 724-283-5852, alepic@pahousegop.com.
