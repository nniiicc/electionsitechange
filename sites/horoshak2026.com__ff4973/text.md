Director of Saint Louis County 07 for the DFL, on the Board of Iron Range Labor Assembly, a member of the Liberal Gun Club and The Masonic Lodge of Eveleth.
He played hockey for Eveleth High School and still enjoys it when he has the opportunity.
Dan enjoys the land and lakes of our beautiful state, cooking a good meal, watching a Vikings game, going to watch live local music, golfing, and spending time with his friends and family Dan is a true champion of labor which has supported the Range way of life for over 100 years. ​ Dan believes in the Range over party, focusing on making basic needs more affordable, healthcare more accessible, and jobs more plentiful.
He will work on creating jobs that give workers options, alternative jobs for people with disabilities, and jobs that can help someone start their own business or buy a house.
Dan will advocate for expanded healthcare education on the Range, so people can get both quality healthcare and education without having to move.
He will support schools and advocate for needed funding and work on solutions for affordable daycare options so more people can work and actually improve their financial situations.
Together we can make the Range a better place to call home for generations to come.
Let's get to work!
