Meet Jo.
A lifetime of caring for others Jo Doll is a licensed physical therapist and has worked with patients across St.
Louis for over 25 years.
She meets Missourians every day who struggle to afford the rising cost of healthcare in our state.
After decades of providing quality care to patients across St.
Louis, Jo’s running for State Representative to expand healthcare, protect public education, and keep our kids safe.
Jo's Story An advocate for public education Elected to the Webster Groves School Board in 2017, Jo understands the kinds of opportunities a quality public education can provide for a family, and she wants to help build a future where every child in our state can receive those opportunities, no matter their zip code.
Learn More Ready to work for you Between working with her physical therapy patients, serving on the school board, and being an active community volunteer, Jo’s never forgotten about her most important job: mom.
She’s going to Jeff City to work for our families just as hard as she’s worked for her own.
