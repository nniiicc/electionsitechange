I'm an educator, 4th generation Montanan, mom of four, twenty-year military spouse, and experienced legislator.
I pride myself in serving others.
A quality education for our children with deep parental involvement is my passion.
I've served as a pre-school president, led with the "Raising Readers" Program, serve as a Girl Scout Service Unit Leader, and have served countless times as a PTA president.
I'm running for re-election to continue fighting for common-sense conservative solutions in Helena and foster a better community for our kids and families.
Born and raised in small town Montana I want to ensure those time-tested values are at the front and center of our legislative process.
I ran my first campaign on lower taxes, the establishment of a property tax Homestead Exemption, boosting teacher pay, and stronger schools.
I was proud to have co-sponsored the STARS act which delivered increased teacher pay , to have worked to pass the largest income tax cut in Montana history , and to have supported Governor Gianforte's Homestead Exemption which reduced property taxes on more than 90% of Great Falls homeowners by 20%.
Delivering Results for Great Falls & Montana As a freshman legislator I passed seven bills into law including legislation that bolstered teacher recruitement & retention, enhanced mental health service availability in schools, expanded speech pathology services at cost savings, and cut red tape/burdensome regulation.
There's still work to do.
As a memeber of the Education, Taxation, and Human services committees I'll continue to work tirelessly to represent you and Great Falls to the best of my god given ability.
