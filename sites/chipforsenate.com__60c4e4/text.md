Re-Elect Chip Curry For State Senate District 11 Meet Chip Sen.
Chip Curry, who resides in Belfast and has previously lived in Knox and Unity, is deeply dedicated to Waldo County.
His wife, Christi Goosman, has taught at Searsport District High School for over 30 years, and together with their daughter, who recently graduated from RSU 71, they are a family firmly established in Waldo County.
