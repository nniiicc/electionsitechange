About Ashley
Ashley Perez-Biliskov is a first-generation Cuban-American raised by her hardworking parents, alongside her two brothers.Raised in Miami, Ashley is a devoted wife to her husband, Jeffrey, and proud mother to their two daughters, Sophia and Leah.
A local graduate of Our Lady of Lourdes Academy, Ashley went on to earn her Bachelor of Science degree from Florida State University and her Master of Science degree from the University of South Florida.
With a fulfilling career as a Medical Speech-Language Pathologist, she has devoted herself to advocating for her patients and improving the lives of others.
