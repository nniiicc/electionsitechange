The 1st Amendment
The linchpin to all other liberties in this country, and the capstone to the active democracy necessary for our constitutional republic.
The first amendment defines our fundamental freedoms of speech, religion, press, assembly and petition.
Without it nothing else really matters.
It affords us the right to set our own values and pursuits, and enact the changes needed to live freely and without fear of government intrusion, ensuring that our government should always be of, by, and for the people.
Our first amendment has been under attack for quite some time, but this was never more evident than during and after the covid years and election of 2020.
Free speech, the right to worship, assemble, and even visit sick and dying loved ones was quickly curbed in the name of public safety.
It is important to note, that all regimes who seek to set up totalitarian systems always seek to remove these basic human rights first.
As leftist ideologies such as Diversity, Equity and Inclusion (DEI), Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Criteria, etc. continue their ultimate mission to remove this most essential amendment, now more than ever, we need to fight against these leftist ideologies at all cost!
In my mind there is no compromise on this issue, and no emotionally deceptive narrative, phrases, or movements, will change that.
