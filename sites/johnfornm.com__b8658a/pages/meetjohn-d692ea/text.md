A native New Mexican, John Block grew up watching his parents work long hours to provide for their family.
His mother was an elementary school teacher, while his father worked helping people with disabilities find guardians.
John learned the value of hard work early.
He bussed tables, volunteered at a community farm, worked at a hotel front desk, and worked his way through college before beginning a career in conservative advocacy, communications, and public policy.
John became active in Republican politics at a young age and joined the effort to elect Donald J.
Trump in 2015.
His professional experience later took him from the New Mexico Legislature to Washington, D.C., where he worked in government affairs and conservative advocacy, including for the pro-life legal organization Americans United for Life and one of President Trump’s largest independent allied organizations, The Committee to Defend the President.
In 2020, John founded the Piñon Post , which grew into New Mexico’s largest online conservative news outlet.
Through his reporting and activism, John exposed government wrongdoing, challenged the political establishment, and helped organize opposition to far-left legislation at the Roundhouse.
From Conservative Activist to State Representative In 2022, the people of House District 51 elected John to represent Alamogordo in the New Mexico House of Representatives.
Since taking office, John has developed a reputation as one of the most outspoken and dependable conservative voices in the Roundhouse.
He serves as Ranking Member of the House Government, Elections and Indian Affairs Committee and has maintained an extensive year-round legislative workload, including service on six interim committees.
John has introduced and fought for legislation defending the Second Amendment, voter ID, border security, law enforcement, veterans, parental rights, the sanctity of life, small businesses, and New Mexico taxpayers .
He has also used his knowledge of legislative rules and procedure to help stop harmful legislation that threatens the freedoms and values of the people he represents.
But John believes being conservative means more than simply voting “no.” He has worked across the aisle when it benefits New Mexicans, helping advance legislation supporting veterans and disabled New Mexicans, protecting law enforcement, improving access to health care, addressing New Mexico’s medical malpractice crisis, and securing millions of dollars for public safety, infrastructure, water, and other priorities in Alamogordo and Otero County.
His 2026 legislative record included successfully passing measures addressing medical malpractice, penalties for aggravated battery on peace officers, and statewide water infrastructure projects.
Fighting for Otero County Year-Round John approaches the Legislature as a year-round job.
He attends committee and interim meetings throughout the state, works directly with constituents, local governments, businesses, law enforcement, veterans, and community organizations, and sends regular legislative updates during session so the people of House District 51 know what their representative is doing in Santa Fe.
His work has earned recognition from conservative and small-business organizations, and Republican leadership selected him as a 2023 GOPAC Emerging Leader .
He was also named Freshman Legislator of the Year by Better Together New Mexico.
John’s philosophy remains simple: show up, do the work, fight for what is right, and never forget who sent you there.
Rooted in New Mexico John holds a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from Rutgers University and an MBA from Eastern New Mexico University in Portales.
He lives in Alamogordo and remains deeply committed to southern New Mexico and the people of House District 51.
In his free time, John enjoys hiking and exploring all our beautiful state has to offer — including mounting tall summits !
John is a Christian and is unashamed of his faith and his belief in the values that have shaped his life: integrity , faith, family, hard work, and service.
