IMMIGRATION
Comprehensive and Humane Reform
OUR PRIORITIES
Our current immigration posture and enforcement practices run counter to our essential American spirit.
Rather than welcoming the tired and the poor, we are vilifying and dehumanizing those who simply wish to join and contribute to the fabric of our nation.
A safe and secure nation is both reasonable and requisite.
Our path forward requires reckoning with this extreme moment- conscientious and comprehensive immigration reform, while preserving a steadfast commitment to national security.
COMPREHENSIVE REFORM
Codify legal frameworks for undocumented immigrants who already reside
and work in the U.S.
Reduce costs and massive waiting lists.
Eliminate family separation.
Simplify the pathway to citizenship.
HUMANE ENFORCEMENT
ICE must be regulated and accountable like other law enforcement agencies, respect legal due process, and treat immigrants and those seeking citizenship with dignity.
Eliminate mass detention centers.
Targeted legislation to counter unlawful executive orders such as ending birthright citizenship (EO14160) and penalizing sanctuary jurisdictions (EO14287).
Restore asylum protections for immigrants fleeing violence.
