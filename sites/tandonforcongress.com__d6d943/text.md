(Click for details.) 1) Cut Everyday Living Costs for Working Families Policy: Reduce everyday household expenses on essentials like groceries, utilities, transportation, insurance, and basic services.
Solution: Enact targeted middle-class relief, strengthen market competition, crack down on hidden junk fees, and support federal actions that lower core living costs without expanding bureaucracy.
2) Make Housing Affordable Again Policy: Increase housing affordability for renters, first-time buyers, and workforce families in high-cost regions like California.
Solution: Expand federal incentives for new housing supply, reward faster local permitting, support first-time homebuyer savings tools, and grow workforce housing near jobs and transit.
3) Lower the Cost of Education and Job Training Policy: Make college and career pathways more affordable and aligned with real job outcomes — and increase teacher pay in California to attract great talent.
Solution: Support tuition-pressure reforms, expand career and technical education, strengthen community-college transfer pipelines, and tie training programs to high-growth sectors.
4) Lower Healthcare and Prescription Drug Costs Policy: Reduce the medical cost burden on families and seniors.
Solution: Lower out-of-pocket costs, increase healthcare price transparency, reduce prescription drug costs, and expand access to preventive and primary care.
5) Stop Tax-and-Fee Overload on Families and Innovators Policy: Prevent excessive tax and fee burdens that weaken family budgets and discourage innovation.
Solution: Oppose layered tax increases and commuter-punishing fee schemes, simplify compliance, and promote tax fairness for workers, retirees, and small businesses.
6) Lower Energy, Utility, and Transportation Costs Policy: Deliver reliable, affordable energy and transportation to reduce monthly household costs.
Solution: Advance an all-of-the-above energy strategy, modernize the grid, improve fuel and power reliability, and invest in transportation efficiency to lower commute expenses.
7) Make Childcare and Elder-Care More Affordable Policy: Reduce care-related financial pressure on working families and caregivers.
Solution: Expand affordable childcare access, strengthen elder-care support systems, and provide flexible federal support that helps families stay in the workforce.
8) Protect Jobs, Raise Wages, and Back Small Business Policy: Improve affordability through higher wages, stronger local employment, and small-business growth.
Solution: Cut unnecessary regulatory barriers, support domestic investment, expand pro-growth small-business policies, and encourage employee ownership models that build wealth.
9) Keep Silicon Valley Competitive in the Next Economy Policy: Ensure U.S. leadership in AI, biotech, robotics, quantum, and advanced manufacturing.
Solution: Pass startup-friendly federal policy, strengthen IP protections, support R&D commercialization, and invest in innovation infrastructure that keeps talent and capital in America.
10) Restore Fiscal Discipline and Economic Stability Policy: Promote long-term affordability through responsible federal budgeting and pro-growth economics.
Solution: Reduce wasteful spending, prioritize high-impact investments, support growth-oriented fiscal reforms, and implement accountability measures that lower inflationary pressure over time.
11) Oppose California Billionaire Wealth Tax Policy: Oppose a California billionaire wealth tax that risks driving investment, founders, and job creators out of the state.
Solution: Focus on spending discipline instead of new wealth taxes—cut waste, streamline agencies, eliminate duplicative programs, and make state government lean and accountable.
Promote growth policies that keep capital, innovation, and high-paying jobs in California.
12) Oppose California Car Mileage Tax Policy: Oppose a statewide car mileage tax that would increase costs on commuters, working families, and small businesses.
Solution: Balance transportation funding by reducing government waste first, prioritizing road dollars for core infrastructure, and modernizing delivery.
Support a lean budget approach with a long-term tax reform goal: lower state income taxes toward 5% and property taxes toward 0.25% through phased implementation tied to spending reductions and economic growth.
America First Growth & AI Education Agenda My America First policy: lower taxes, less regulation, more innovation, and AI education for every student and worker.
That is how we protect jobs, raise wages, and keep America number one.
(Click for details.) 1) Cut Taxes for Working Families Policy: Put more money back in family budgets.
Solution: Expand middle-class tax relief, protect child tax support, and simplify the tax code so workers keep more of every paycheck.
2) Make Small Business Taxes Simple and Fair Policy: Help Main Street compete and grow.
Solution: Create a simplified federal small-business tax track, expand startup deductions, and reduce compliance costs that punish entrepreneurs.
3) Protect American Startups and Employee Ownership Policy: Keep high-growth companies and talent in the U.S.
Solution: Modernize tax treatment of stock options and employee equity so startups can reward builders without excessive tax penalties.
4) One-In, Two-Out Federal Regulation Rule Policy: End the culture of endless federal overregulation.
Solution: Require agencies to remove or update two outdated regulations for every major new one, with strict cost-benefit review and sunset deadlines.
5) Fast-Track Permits for U.S.
Innovation Projects Policy: Build America’s future faster.
Solution: Set firm timelines for federal approvals on data centers, semiconductor facilities, grid upgrades, labs, and advanced manufacturing sites.
6) AI in Every School: K–12 National Readiness Plan Policy: Every student should graduate AI-literate.
Solution: Launch a federal-state partnership to integrate AI fundamentals, critical thinking, ethics, and digital safety into K–12 standards and teacher training.
7) AI in Every College: Workforce-Ready Degrees Policy: College must prepare students for the real economy.
Solution: Tie federal higher-ed incentives to AI adoption across majors—business, healthcare, engineering, education, law, and public policy—not just computer science.
8) AI Upskilling for Workers and Veterans Policy: No American worker should be left behind by technology.
Solution: Fund rapid retraining grants, community college AI certificates, apprenticeship pipelines, and veteran-focused tech transition programs.
9) America First Innovation & IP Security Policy: American ideas must stay protected and profitable in America.
Solution: Strengthen IP theft enforcement, tighten safeguards on critical tech transfer, and prioritize U.S.-based R&D commercialization.
10) Buy American, Build American, Train American Policy: Federal dollars should grow U.S. jobs and U.S. capability.
Solution: Prioritize American vendors for strategic technology procurement, support domestic supply chains, and require workforce development plans tied to federal innovation grants.
Immigration Policy: Secure, Fair, Legal, and America-First My immigration plan: secure the border, enforce the law, protect American workers, welcome legal talent, and create a fair system that is humane, practical, and built to last.
(Click for details.) 1) Secure the Border and Enforce the Law Policy: America must have a secure border and consistent enforcement of immigration law.
Solution: Fully fund modern border security technology, targeted physical barriers where needed, anti-cartel operations, and rapid processing teams so unlawful entry is deterred and legal entry is prioritized.
2) End “Catch-and-Release” and Fix Asylum Processing Policy: Asylum should protect genuine persecution cases—not function as a backdoor for economic migration.
Solution: Set fast asylum adjudication timelines, expand immigration courts, require timely case resolution, and remove those with denied claims through lawful, humane, and efficient procedures.
3) Build a Real National Tracking System Policy: The U.S. must track visa overstays and immigration case status in real time.
Solution: Create an integrated federal entry-exit and overstay tracking platform linked to DHS, State, and DOJ systems, with regular reporting to Congress and clear enforcement triggers.
4) Prioritize Skills-Based Legal Immigration Policy: Legal immigration should align with U.S. workforce and innovation needs.
Solution: Expand and modernize merit-based pathways for high-skill talent in critical sectors, while protecting U.S. workers through wage safeguards and transparent labor-market tests.
5) Protect American Workers and Wages Policy: Immigration policy must never undercut wages or job access for U.S. citizens and legal residents.
Solution: Strengthen employer verification and anti-fraud enforcement, require market wages for guest-worker hiring, and impose strong penalties on labor exploitation and illegal hiring schemes.
6) Reform Guest Worker Programs (H-1B and Others) Policy: Temporary worker programs must be lawful, transparent, and pro-worker.
Solution: Require stricter employer accountability, stronger wage floors, anti-abuse audits, and complementary U.S. job-training pipelines so foreign-worker programs do not replace domestic talent development.
7) Pair Immigration with National Workforce Training Policy: Immigration reform and workforce policy must work together in the AI era.
Solution: Launch a national upskilling initiative in trades, healthcare, advanced manufacturing, and AI-adjacent jobs so Americans can compete and thrive as labor markets change.
8) Keep Families Together—With Clear, Practical Rules Policy: Family reunification matters, but the system must be orderly, clear, and sustainable.
Solution: Prioritize immediate/nuclear family categories, reduce backlogs through digital processing, and publish predictable legal timelines so families can plan legally—not through loopholes.
9) Make Deportation Humane, Efficient, and Cost-Conscious Policy: Deportation enforcement should be lawful, humane, and fiscally responsible.
Solution: Prioritize removals based on public-safety risk and final legal status, use faster legal processing, expand repatriation coordination, and reduce long detention costs through efficient case closure.
10) Establish a Bipartisan, Long-Term Immigration Compact Policy: Immigration should not swing wildly every time administrations change.
Solution: Pass durable bipartisan legislation with clear annual targets (border security, legal admissions, workforce alignment, overstay enforcement, and asylum integrity), plus mandatory congressional oversight reviews.
11) Protect the Safety and Security of Americans Policy: Public safety and national security must be core priorities of immigration enforcement.
Solution: Strengthen federal-state-local coordination to target violent offenders, drug/human trafficking networks, cartel activity, and repeat immigration violators; expand intelligence-sharing, improve detention standards for high-risk offenders, and accelerate lawful removal of individuals who threaten community safety.
12) Protect Election Integrity: Only U.S.
Citizens Should Vote Policy: U.S. elections must be decided only by eligible American citizens under federal and state law.
Solution: Support secure voter verification standards, stronger voter-roll maintenance, citizenship verification where legally required, and tougher penalties for election fraud—while protecting every eligible citizen’s lawful right to vote.
