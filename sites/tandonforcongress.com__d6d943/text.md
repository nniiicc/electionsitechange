That is how we protect jobs, raise wages, and keep America number one.

Policy: Put more money back in family budgets.
Solution: Expand middle-class tax relief, protect child tax support, and simplify the tax code so workers keep more of every paycheck.
Policy: Help Main Street compete and grow.
Solution: Create a simplified federal small-business tax track, expand startup deductions, and reduce compliance costs that punish entrepreneurs.
Policy: Keep high-growth companies and talent in the U.S.
Solution: Modernize tax treatment of stock options and employee equity so startups can reward builders without excessive tax penalties.
Policy: End the culture of endless federal overregulation.
Solution: Require agencies to remove or update two outdated regulations for every major new one, with strict cost-benefit review and sunset deadlines.
Policy: Build America’s future faster.
Solution: Set firm timelines for federal approvals on data centers, semiconductor facilities, grid upgrades, labs, and advanced manufacturing sites.
Policy: Every student should graduate AI-literate.
Solution: Launch a federal-state partnership to integrate AI fundamentals, critical thinking, ethics, and digital safety into K–12 standards and teacher training.
Policy: College must prepare students for the real economy.
Solution: Tie federal higher-ed incentives to AI adoption across majors—business, healthcare, engineering, education, law, and public policy—not just computer science.
Policy: No American worker should be left behind by technology.
Solution: Fund rapid retraining grants, community college AI certificates, apprenticeship pipelines, and veteran-focused tech transition programs.
Policy: American ideas must stay protected and profitable in America.
Solution: Strengthen IP theft enforcement, tighten safeguards on critical tech transfer, and prioritize U.S.-based R&D commercialization.
Policy: Federal dollars should grow U.S. jobs and U.S. capability.
Solution: Prioritize American vendors for strategic technology procurement, support domestic supply chains, and require workforce development plans tied to federal innovation grants.
My immigration plan: secure the border, enforce the law, protect American workers, welcome legal talent, and create a fair system that is humane, practical, and built to last.
(Click for details.)
Policy: America must have a secure border and consistent enforcement of immigration law.
Solution: Fully fund modern border security technology, targeted physical barriers where needed, anti-cartel operations, and rapid processing teams so unlawful entry is deterred and legal entry is prioritized.
Policy: Asylum should protect genuine persecution cases—not function as a backdoor for economic migration.
Solution: Set fast asylum adjudication timelines, expand immigration courts, require timely case resolution, and remove those with denied claims through lawful, humane, and efficient procedures.
Policy: The U.S. must track visa overstays and immigration case status in real time.
Solution: Create an integrated federal entry-exit and overstay tracking platform linked to DHS, State, and DOJ systems, with regular reporting to Congress and clear enforcement triggers.
Policy: Legal immigration should align with U.S. workforce and innovation needs.
Solution: Expand and modernize merit-based pathways for high-skill talent in critical sectors, while protecting U.S. workers through wage safeguards and transparent labor-market tests.
Policy: Immigration policy must never undercut wages or job access for U.S. citizens and legal residents.
Solution: Strengthen employer verification and anti-fraud enforcement, require market wages for guest-worker hiring, and impose strong penalties on labor exploitation and illegal hiring schemes.
Policy: Temporary worker programs must be lawful, transparent, and pro-worker.
Solution: Require stricter employer accountability, stronger wage floors, anti-abuse audits, and complementary U.S. job-training pipelines so foreign-worker programs do not replace domestic talent development.
Policy: Immigration reform and workforce policy must work together in the AI era.
Solution: Launch a national upskilling initiative in trades, healthcare, advanced manufacturing, and AI-adjacent jobs so Americans can compete and thrive as labor markets change.
Policy: Family reunification matters, but the system must be orderly, clear, and sustainable.
Solution: Prioritize immediate/nuclear family categories, reduce backlogs through digital processing, and publish predictable legal timelines so families can plan legally—not through loopholes.
Policy: Deportation enforcement should be lawful, humane, and fiscally responsible.
Solution: Prioritize removals based on public-safety risk and final legal status, use faster legal processing, expand repatriation coordination, and reduce long detention costs through efficient case closure.
Policy: Immigration should not swing wildly every time administrations change.
Solution: Pass durable bipartisan legislation with clear annual targets (border security, legal admissions, workforce alignment, overstay enforcement, and asylum integrity), plus mandatory congressional oversight reviews.
Policy: Public safety and national security must be core priorities of immigration enforcement.
Solution: Strengthen federal-state-local coordination to target violent offenders, drug/human trafficking networks, cartel activity, and repeat immigration violators; expand intelligence-sharing, improve detention standards for high-risk offenders, and accelerate lawful removal of individuals who threaten community safety.
Policy: U.S. elections must be decided only by eligible American citizens under federal and state law.
Solution: Support secure voter verification standards, stronger voter-roll maintenance, citizenship verification where legally required, and tougher penalties for election fraud—while protecting every eligible citizen’s lawful right to vote.
