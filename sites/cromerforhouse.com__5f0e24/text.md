South Carolina is often considered one of the “reddest” states in the country.
Our citizens are hard-working, God-fearing, and conservative.
Yet, it seems that for many of our elected officials these traits end on Election Day.
With taxes rising, our state budget ballooning, and an education system that is failing our students, it’s time for leaders who will show up, vote with integrity, and provide real solutions for South Carolina.
That is why I’m running to be your state representative.
As a businesswoman who has helped run a company of more than 100 employees, I know that throwing money at broken systems never works.
Rather than raising your taxes to, yet again, increase our state budget, I’ll work to bring down costs and make our government more efficient by slashing wasteful spending.
As a mother of two, I know that nothing is more important than our children.
In the legislature, I will fight to make sure the voices of parents are heard.
From providing real school choice for families to guaranteeing that Critical Race Theory is never taught in South Carolina schools, our children’s education will be a top priority.
As a lifelong conservative, I will make sure that our basic rights to life and the 2nd amendment are always protected.
Furthermore, I will hold our government accountable and push back against any overreach - including mandates that infringe on our medical freedom.
Finally, I will protect one of our most essential rights by fighting for true election integrity.
Anderson County deserves a representative who doesn’t see elected office as a career.
I promise to work for the people of District 6, to put their interests first, and to hold our government accountable.
As a businesswoman, a mother, and a conservative, I will listen to our citizens and bring common sense solutions to Columbia.
