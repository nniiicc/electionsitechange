Kris proudly calls Frederick home, having lived here since 1991.
He spent decades working to lift barriers and expand opportunity, helping create a more equitable and welcoming community for all Fredericktonians.
In 2023, with the support of friends and neighbors across Frederick, Kris went to Annapolis with one mission: to use his skills in consensus-building, data-driven solutions, and relentless energy to deliver real results for Frederick and Maryland.
LEARN MORE In Annapolis LGBTQ+ Caucus Kris is a founding member and the first chair of Maryland's first Legislative LGBTQ+ Caucus.
Transportation Advocate Kris began work on transportation issues day one.
He is appointed to the Washington Transportation Planning Board and elected the Transit Caucus legislative co-chair.
Secured Millions Kris's hard work and determination have brought home tens of millions of dollars in investment to our community, including nonprofits and local government initiatives.
