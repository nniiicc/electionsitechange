RALEIGH – Michael Whatley hit the 100 county milestone on Saturday.
When he announced for US Senate, Whatley made a commitment to hit all 100 counties and hit number 99 and 100 this past weekend.
Across the state, Whatley has met with North Carolinians who know they can’t afford to add six more years to Roy Cooper’s career as a Career Politician living off the taxpayer dime.
“Career Politicians like Roy Cooper get lazy, or old, and don’t have the interest or the strength to hit all 100 counties in North Carolina but every county is important and has needs that can only be appreciated in person in the county.
From Murphy to Manteo, I am proud to have had important conversations with North Carolinians across all 100 counties.
As your Senator, I will fight for every family to make more and keep more, keep our communities safe and preserve the American Dream for generations to come,” said Michael Whatley.
