RALEIGH – Today, Michael Whatley’s Campaign launched it’s first ad of the general election with a seven-figure ad buy focused on streaming services.
The ad titled “MILES” highlights Whatley’s first-time candidacy and backing of the Working Families Tax Cuts’ No Tax on Social Security provision which has put money back into the hands of seniors across North Carolina.
Juxtaposing it to Roy Cooper’s record of siding with radical far-left Democrats who want to raise taxes on North Carolinians by opposing the Working Families Tax Cuts that would tax money out of the pockets of seniors and families.
“Career politician Roy Cooper has spent the last 40 years taking in a taxpayer funded salary while raising costs for North Carolina families and seniors.
While opposing legislation that would put more money in the pockets of 1.9 million North Carolina seniors, Cooper’s family made more than a million dollars a year on a sweetheart solar deal.
Michael Whatley will always fight for our seniors and families across North Carolina to make more and keep more of their money,” said Whatley Campaign Spokesman DJ Griffin.
