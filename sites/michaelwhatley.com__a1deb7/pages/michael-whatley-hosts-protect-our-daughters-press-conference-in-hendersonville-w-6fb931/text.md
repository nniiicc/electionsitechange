He discussed Roy Cooper’s failure to protect our kids, including his veto of bills to keep men out of girls sports, to stop the transgender mutilation of kids, and of the Parents Bill of Rights.
Whatley was joined by Payton McNabb, a North Carolina women’s sports advocate and Independent Women’s sports ambassador who suffered a life-altering brain injury after being struck by a volleyball spiked by a biological male competing on an opposing women’s team.
The impact left her unconscious and resulted in lasting physical and neurological effects, including impaired vision, partial paralysis, and balance issues.
Since then, McNabb has become a leading national voice advocating to protect women’s sports, testifying before the North Carolina General Assembly and joining President Donald Trump as his guest at a joint address to Congress, where he recognized her advocacy.
