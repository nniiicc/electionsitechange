Whatley, sheriffs, and local leaders discussed how Governor Cooper always appointed soft-on-crime judges and emphasized Cooper’s disastrous decision to release more than 4,200 convicted criminals from prison, 50% of which went on to commit further crimes.
Whatley was joined by Alamance County Sheriff Terry Johnson,Randolph County Sheriff Greg Seabolt, Davidson County Sheriff Richie Simmons, NC Senator Amy Galey, and NC Representative Dennis Riddell.
