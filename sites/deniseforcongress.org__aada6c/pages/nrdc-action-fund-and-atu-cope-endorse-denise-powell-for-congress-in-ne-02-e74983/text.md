“I’m proud to have earned the endorsement of the NRDC Action Fund and ATU-COPE in this race to represent Nebraska’s Second District,” said Denise Powell.
“These are organizations that show up for working people — for the transit workers keeping our city moving, and for the land, air, and water future generations will inherit.
In Congress, I’ll do the same: fight to protect union jobs and worker protections, push for real climate action that creates good-paying jobs right here in Nebraska, and make sure every family in this district gets ahead.” “Denise Powell is committed to protecting the health and safety of Nebraska families.
In Congress, she will help stop the Trump administration’s relentless attacks on clean water, clean air, affordable clean energy, and our climate future,” said NRDC Action Fund Managing Director Jed Ober.
“We’re proud to support Denise Powell because she will put people over polluters.” The Natural Resources Defense Council Action Fund works to elect candidates committed to confronting the climate crisis, protecting our wildlife, and ensuring the rights of all people to clean air, water, and healthy communities.
The NRDC Action Fund supports environmental champions who will stand up to the polluter agenda on Capitol Hill and in states across the country.
“Denise Powell’s steadfast dedication to championing the needs of working people and their families has earned her the trust of the people in Nebraska’s 2nd Congressional District.
She has been a strong supporter of public transit and labor unions.
The ATU is proud to endorse Denise Powell and will be registering our members to vote, encouraging them to vote, engaging riders, and taking part in other actions to send Denise Powell to Congress,” said ATU International President John Costa.
ATU-COPE, the Amalgamated Transit Union’s political fundraising arm, is the largest transit worker Political Action Committee in the United States, and serves as the voice for transit workers, school bus employees, and over-the-road bus workers at all levels of government.
ATU-COPE helps elect pro-labor and pro-transit candidates for local, state, and federal office and holds them accountable once elected.
### About Denise Denise Powell is a mom, former executive, small business owner, public school champion and healthcare advocate who knows that a strong work ethic and a love of family and community is the Nebraska way of life.
She also knows the Good Life is getting harder to come by.
It’s why after Trump’s election she left a successful career in corporate America to focus on fighting for what makes Nebraska such a special place to live and raise a family.
Over the past decade, she has supported hundreds of Nebraskans running for local office, protected our public schools and the families they serve, and helped stop extremists from passing abortion bans in the state.
Every day, we see more of our friends and neighbors struggling to make ends meet while Donald Trump and MAGA extremists are focused on trade wars that are increasing our cost of living and passing massive tax breaks for billionaires like Elon Musk.
Strong families, strong businesses, strong communities – they ALL require work and investment, but everything we’ve fought for here at home is under threat.
From dismantling Medicaid and making it harder for seniors to get the benefits they’ve earned, to dismantling education, from the University of Nebraska to our public schools, to attacking our veterans and ripping away women’s rights — Denise is running to fight for the people of Nebraska.
We don’t need lip service from politicians, we need action.
