People are so tired of politicians making these empty promises.
They’re tired of working groups and studies, they just want some action.” [Watch the clip here.] Get the facts: While on the Omaha City Council, Harding voted for more than $1 million in dozens of taxes on Omaha residents and watched as property taxes consumed Omaha residents’ wallets.
Harding was the Director of Fall Foods, which failed to pay federal taxes on ten occasions, resulting in five liens against the company totaling more than $121,000 between 2015-2020.
Harding was a partner in Harding Investments, which failed to pay property taxes on 28 separate occasions and was put up for tax sale eight times between 2009 and 2024.
“Brinker can’t seriously expect voters to believe that if they send him to Congress he’ll work to lower taxes when his record on the City Council shows that he’s done the exact opposite.
He’s too busy to pay his own taxes on time, but he has no problem raising taxes on the rest of us – it’s the most offensive type of hypocrisy there is.
We can’t trust him to play by the rules when he clearly doesn’t think the rules apply to him.
Nebraskans are tired of this kind of nonsense and come November, they’ll be sending Denise to Washington to represent them,” said Meg Mandy, Powell campaign spokesperson.
About Denise Denise Powell is a former corporate executive turned small business owner and working mom who knows that a strong work ethic and a love of family and community is the Nebraska way of life.

Every day, we see more of our friends and neighbors struggling to make ends meet while Donald Trump and MAGA extremists are focused on trade wars that are increasing our cost of living and passing massive tax breaks for billionaires.
Strong families, strong businesses, strong communities – they ALL require work and investment, but everything we’ve fought for here at home is under threat.
From dismantling Medicaid and making it harder for seniors to get the benefits they’ve earned, to dismantling education, from the University of Nebraska to our public schools, to attacking our veterans and ripping away women’s rights — Denise is running to fight for the people of Nebraska.
We don’t need lip service from politicians, we need action.
