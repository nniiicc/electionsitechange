Founded in 1913, Nebraska Farmers Union is dedicated to protecting and enhancing the quality of life and economic well-being of family farmers and ranchers, and their rural communities.
“Too many in our community are working hard, playing by the rules, and still falling further behind,” said Denise Powell.
“While my opponent will be another rubber stamp afraid to stand up to party bosses in Washington, I am committed to supporting Nebraska farmers, overturning tariffs, and passing the farm bill.
I am proud to have the NEBFARMPAC and their parent organization, Nebraska Farmers Union, by my side and I will always be a fierce advocate for all of Nebraska’s farmers when I’m in Congress.” As Nebraska’s second largest family farm and ranch ag organization with over 4,000 family farm and ranch families as members, Nebraska Farmers Union is dedicated to the farm income issues which matter most to rural families.
With active members across the state, Nebraska Farmers Union is one of Nebraska’s oldest and strongest grassroots organizations.
“Nebraska Farmers Union (NeFU) and its PAC, NEBFARMPAC are nonpartisan as is National Farmers Union (NFU) and its PAC, NFU PAC.
Our state and national PACs have endorsed Denise Powell for Congress in CD2.
NeFU at the state level and NFU at the national level have harnessed the power of organization to build far more cooperatives at the state and national levels than any other organization, urban or rural.
We know first hand how important it is to work together for the common good.
At the state level, NefU worked with George Norris to create our unique nonpartisan unicameral system, and our nonpartisan 100% public power system that has served our state so well.
We believe Denise Powell is independent, an organizer, a problem solver, and she will work with her constituents and colleagues to build a better tomorrow for both urban and rural families.
Congress has failed to pass an updated and improved Farm Bill for the fourth year in a row.
They have failed our farmers, our nation, and our future.
It is time for a change.
We are honored to endorse Denise Powell for Congress in CD2,” said John Hansen, NEBFARMPAC Secretary and NFU PAC board member.
The Nebraska Farmers Union joins a broad and growing coalition of supporters backing Denise.
Denise’s campaign has over 60 local and national endorsements, including EMILY’s List, CHC BOLD PAC, Elect Democratic Women, New Dem Action Fund, former Democratic nominee Tony Vargas, and members of Congress from across the country.
About Denise Denise Powell is a mom, former executive, small business owner, public school champion and healthcare advocate who knows that a strong work ethic and a love of family and community is the Nebraska way of life.
She also knows the Good Life is getting harder to come by.
It’s why after Trump’s election she left a successful career in corporate America to focus on fighting for what makes Nebraska such a special place to live and raise a family.
Over the past decade, she has supported hundreds of Nebraskans running for local office, protected our public schools and the families they serve, and helped stop extremists from passing abortion bans in the state.
Every day, we see more of our friends and neighbors struggling to make ends meet while Donald Trump and MAGA extremists are focused on trade wars that are increasing our cost of living and passing massive tax breaks for billionaires like Elon Musk.
Strong families, strong businesses, strong communities – they ALL require work and investment, but everything we’ve fought for here at home is under threat.
From dismantling Medicaid and making it harder for seniors to get the benefits they’ve earned, to dismantling education, from the University of Nebraska to our public schools, to attacking our veterans and ripping away women’s rights — Denise is running to fight for the people of Nebraska.
We don’t need lip service from politicians, we need action.
