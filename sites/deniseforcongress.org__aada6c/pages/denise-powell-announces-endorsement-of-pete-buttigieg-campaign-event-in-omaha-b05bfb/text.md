Denise and Buttigieg to Campaign Together on July 14 in Omaha
OMAHA — Today, Pete Buttigieg, former U.S.
Secretary of Transportation and Mayor of South Bend, announced his endorsement of Denise Powell to represent Nebraska’s Second Congressional District in Congress.
Denise and Pete will appear together at a campaign event in Omaha on Tuesday, July 14.
“I am thrilled to have the endorsement of Secretary Pete Buttigieg in this race to flip NE-02,” said Denise.
“He is seeing across the country what I am seeing every day here in Nebraska: costs are soaring and families are struggling to make ends meet while Donald Trump and MAGA Republicans enrich themselves and their billionaire friends at the expense of everyday people.
In Congress, I’ll take on corrupt politicians and fight to lower costs for Nebraskans, starting with a tax break to middle class families.
I’m honored to have Pete on my team to flip this seat, take back the House, and get to work for Nebraskans.”
“Denise Powell is a fierce advocate driven by a commitment to defending Nebraska families,” said Pete.
“While Republicans in Washington seem to prioritize everything but the challenges people are facing in their everyday lives, Denise will be laser-focused on bringing down the cost of everything from groceries to housing and on building an economy that works for everyone from veterans to farmers.
Nebraskans deserve someone in Washington who will stand up for them, and that is why I am excited to endorse Denise for Congress.”
