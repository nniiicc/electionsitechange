Viv Ewing Endorse Denise Powell for Congress in NE-02 August 3, 2026 Omaha, NE – Today, Omaha Mayor John Ewing and his wife, Dr.
Viv Ewing, announced their endorsement of Denise Powell to represent Nebraska’s Second District in Congress.
“I’m honored to have Mayor and Dr.
Ewing’s endorsements in the race to serve Nebraska’s Second District in Congress,” said Denise Powell.
“John and Viv are strong community leaders here in Omaha – they understand firsthand the pressure that working Nebraskans are under because of the affordability crisis caused by failed leadership in Washington.
Omahans shouldn’t have to struggle to keep up while corrupt politicians line their own pockets on the taxpayers’ dime.
When I’m in Congress, I’ll stop at nothing to put the needs of everyday Nebraska families first.
I’m so grateful for the Ewings’ support in the ongoing fight to flip NE-02 and deliver the relief that Nebraska families deserve.” “As lifelong Nebraskans, Viv and I know what strong leadership looks like for our great state.
Denise is that kind of leader: selfless, bold, and committed to solving the problems that matter most to our neighbors,” said Mayor John Ewing.
“Denise has a track record of doing the hard work to actually get things done for Nebraskans: paid sick leave, public school funding, protecting reproductive rights, and raising the minimum wage.
Denise will fight in Congress to put our community first because she is a part of it.
Denise is dedicated to standing up for everyday Omahans, and that’s why Viv and I are proud to stand with her in this race.” John Ewing was born and raised in Omaha, NE, and became Omaha’s 52nd mayor on June 9, 2025.
Prior to taking office, John’s career in public service spanned 25 years with the Omaha Police Department and 18 years with the Douglas County Treasurer’s Office.
His wife, Dr.
Viv Ewing, is also an Omaha native and longtime community leader.
### About Denise Denise Powell is a mom, former executive, small business owner, public school champion and healthcare advocate who knows that a strong work ethic and a love of family and community is the Nebraska way of life.
She also knows the Good Life is getting harder to come by.
It’s why after Trump’s election she left a successful career in corporate America to focus on fighting for what makes Nebraska such a special place to live and raise a family.
Over the past decade, she has supported hundreds of Nebraskans running for local office, protected our public schools and the families they serve, and helped stop extremists from passing abortion bans in the state.
Every day, we see more of our friends and neighbors struggling to make ends meet while Donald Trump and MAGA extremists are focused on trade wars that are increasing our cost of living and passing massive tax breaks for billionaires like Elon Musk.
Strong families, strong businesses, strong communities – they ALL require work and investment, but everything we’ve fought for here at home is under threat.
From dismantling Medicaid and making it harder for seniors to get the benefits they’ve earned, to dismantling education, from the University of Nebraska to our public schools, to attacking our veterans and ripping away women’s rights — Denise is running to fight for the people of Nebraska.
We don’t need lip service from politicians, we need action.
