As we approach the final weeks of the campaign, voters have a clear choice in front of them: a working mom who has spent the last 17 years working across party lines to fight for hardworking Nebraskans or a career politician who works against them by opposing raising the minimum wage and building more affordable housing while celebrating Trump’s policies that have made everyone’s lives more expensive, including the illegal tariffs and the war in Iran.
In the debate, Powell laid out her plans to lower costs, make healthcare more affordable, and to put the interests of everyday Nebraskans – not powerful billionaires – back in focus.
“Denise showed tonight why she’s the clear front runner in this race and why voters will send her to Congress this November.
As Brinker failed to articulate a clear vision and failed to distance himself from Trump’s failed economy, Denise made clear that she’ll always fight for Nebraska’s working families.
With gas, groceries, housing, childcare, and medicine costs at an all time high, now is not the time for the same failed leadership Brinker is offering.
Denise will bring a fresh approach, new perspective, and can-do attitude with her to Congress.
Nebraskans can’t afford Brinker Harding and tonight they were reminded why,” said Meg Mandy, Powell campaign spokesperson .
Powell called out Harding on several issues important to Nebraskans, including: Harding’s support of Trump’s illegal tariffs, which have r aised costs for Nebraskans by more than $4,000 and cut Nebraska farmers and ranchers off from international export markets.
Harding’s support of Trump’s war of choice in Iran, despite a complete lack of direction and strategy.
Harding’s support of Trump’s One Big Beautiful Bill, which kicked more than 23,000 Nebraskans in the Second District off their health insurance, cut trillions from Medicaid, and tripled Affordable Care Act insurance premiums.
Harding’s opposition to raising the minimum wage – amid an affordability crisis – while on the City Council earlier this year.
Harding’s opposition to Omaha’s Affordable Housing Action Plan.
Harding’s opposition to Omaha’s Poverty Elimination Plan.
About Denise Denise Powell is a former corporate executive turned small business owner and working mom who knows that a strong work ethic and a love of family and community is the Nebraska way of life.
She also knows the Good Life is getting harder to come by.
It’s why after Trump’s election she left a successful career in corporate America to focus on fighting for what makes Nebraska such a special place to live and raise a family.
Over the past decade, she has supported hundreds of Nebraskans running for local office, protected our public schools and the families they serve, and helped stop extremists from passing abortion bans in the state.
Every day, we see more of our friends and neighbors struggling to make ends meet while Donald Trump and MAGA extremists are focused on trade wars that are increasing our cost of living and passing massive tax breaks for billionaires.
Strong families, strong businesses, strong communities – they ALL require work and investment, but everything we’ve fought for here at home is under threat.
From dismantling Medicaid and making it harder for seniors to get the benefits they’ve earned, to dismantling education, from the University of Nebraska to our public schools, to attacking our veterans and ripping away women’s rights — Denise is running to fight for the people of Nebraska.
We don’t need lip service from politicians, we need action.
