While Harding struggled to keep up with Powell and failed at defending his disastrous record and allegiance to Trump and MAGA Republicans, Denise laid out a clear plan to help working families get ahead by ending corruption in Washington and putting forth policies that will lower the costs of gas, groceries, and medicine.
Just yesterday, Powell announced a massive Q3 fundraising haul of nearly $2.4 million across her campaign committee and victory funds, giving her the cash she needs to get her message out to voters in the final three weeks of the election.
This follows the removal of all resources from Republican Super PAC Conservative Leadership Fund, making it clear that national GOP leaders have abandoned Brinker Harding.
Throughout the course of the debate, Powell laid out a clear choice for voters this November and proved, once again, that she is the only candidate committed to ending Trump’s war in Iran and the illegal tariffs that are costing American taxpayers over $1 billion per day , and the only candidate that everyday Nebraskans can count on to work for them – not billionaires or special interests.
“Throughout all three debates we saw the clear choice in front of us this election, between a fired up working mom who will always put Nebraska first and a career politician who will only deliver more of the same failed, corrupt leadership.
Denise is committed to lowering costs for working families and standing up to Trump’s corruption, and she’ll bring a breath of fresh air to Washington.
Denise won all three debates by showing voters who she is, what she stands for, and reaffirming who she’ll fight for – Nebraska families just like hers.
Brinker Harding, on the other hand, failed in all three debates at articulating any coherent plans to help Nebraskans and proved himself disqualifying as a candidate with his inability to defend his disastrous hyper-partisan record and allegiance to party bosses like Donald Trump.
In 19 days Nebraska voters will send a clear message that it’s time to send a working mom to Congress,” said Meg Mandy, Powell campaign spokesperson.
Powell again called out Harding on several issues important to Nebraskans, including: Harding’s support of Trump’s illegal tariffs, which have r aised costs for Nebraskans by more than $4,000 and cut Nebraska farmers and ranchers off from international export markets.
Harding’s support of Trump’s war of choice in Iran, despite a complete lack of direction and strategy.
Harding’s support of Trump’s One Big Beautiful Bill, which kicked more than 23,000 Nebraskans in the Second District off their health insurance, cut trillions from Medicaid, and tripled Affordable Care Act insurance premiums.
Harding’s opposition to raising the minimum wage – amid an affordability crisis – while on the City Council earlier this year.
Harding’s opposition to Omaha’s Affordable Housing Action Plan.
Harding’s opposition to Omaha’s Poverty Elimination Plan.
About Denise Denise Powell is a former corporate executive turned small business owner and working mom who knows that a strong work ethic and a love of family and community is the Nebraska way of life.

Over the past decade, she has supported hundreds of Nebraskans running for local office, protected our public schools and the families they serve, and helped stop extremists from passing abortion bans in the state.
Every day, we see more of our friends and neighbors struggling to make ends meet while Donald Trump and MAGA extremists are focused on trade wars that are increasing our cost of living and passing massive tax breaks for billionaires.
Strong families, strong businesses, strong communities – they ALL require work and investment, but everything we’ve fought for here at home is under threat.
From dismantling Medicaid and making it harder for seniors to get the benefits they’ve earned, to dismantling education, from the University of Nebraska to our public schools, to attacking our veterans and ripping away women’s rights — Denise is running to fight for the people of Nebraska.
We don’t need lip service from politicians, we need action.
