Her Story California Native Immigrant Patricia's father served for 23 years in the United States Air Force.
When her father retired from the military, he became a math teacher, worked weekends and nights as a juvenile counselor and completed his master's degree.
Patricia's mother was a nurse who somehow managed to raise six children all while holding down the homefront.
Both of her parents taught their children to never forget their rich mixed heritage, to love America and cherish the state of California.
Although a native born American, Patricia's roots run deep with Native American, Filipino and Mexican grandparents who have all played important roles into her appreciation and understanding of the native and immigrant history and traditions of our land.
It is her family — and the families of Assembly District 20 — who inspire her to fight for a better California every single day.
As a UCLA graduate, Patricia previously enjoyed a career in telecommunications including technical network as well as sales and administrative management positions.
She is currently a licensed California Certified General Real Estate Appraiser and Real Estate Broker.
After college she worked as an Emergency Medical Technician and volunteered with border health projects serving impoverished communities along the US/Mexico Border.
Additionally, she worked as a government relations assistant for a disproportionate share medical center, providing much needed healthcare to the medically indigent population of Los Angeles County.
As she advances in her professional career, her life here in the East Bay also finds her heavily involved in government and election related issues including serving for the last 12 years as an Election Worker and Vote Center Captain for both Alameda and Contra Costa Counties.
Patricia has served as a legislation witness in front of the State Legislature and spoken in front of the Alameda County Board of Supervisors and Elections Commission.
Patricia extensively networks with politically active Californians from San Diego to the northern rural counties.
She is an active participant in non-partisan efforts including the Election Integrity Team of Alameda County.
She organized efforts and fought against the COVID mandates and lockdowns.
She is currently a voting member of the Alameda County Republican Party Central Committee.
