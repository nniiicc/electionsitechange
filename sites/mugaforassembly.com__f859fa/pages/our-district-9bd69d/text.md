Assembly District 20 is in the "Heart of the East Bay" Assembly District 20 is often over-shadowed by more metropolitan communities to the north and south.
Some might say it is the "Fly Over Country" of the East Bay Region, not getting its due attention from either local or state officials.
Our community history is marked by a strong working class with generational ties among culturally diverse people.
Small businesses, public schools and universities as well as our beautiful regional parks play important roles in our balance of life.
We are people on the move, who depend on reliable transportation infrastructure without debilitating fees, taxes and regulations.

Cities & Communities Assembly District 20 includes a broad range of communities, each with its own character and challenges — but united by shared values and a common desire for effective, accountable representation.
San Leandro A proud working-class city on the edge of Oakland, San Leandro residents face rising crime, housing affordability pressures, and a need for leadership that puts community safety first.
West Pleasanton A family-friendly community with top-rated schools and a strong business presence.
Residents here expect safe streets, quality education, and a government that stays out of the way of economic growth.
West Dublin One of the fastest-growing areas in California, West Dublin is a young, diverse community grappling with infrastructure demands, school capacity, and the cost of housing.
Union City A diverse, working-class community at the crossroads of the East Bay, Union City residents face rising costs, public safety concerns, and infrastructure challenges that demand real leadership in Sacramento.
Hayward A diverse and dynamic city at the heart of the East Bay, Hayward residents face rising costs, public safety concerns, and infrastructure challenges that demand effective leadership in Sacramento.
Castro Valley A close-knit unincorporated community nestled in the hills above the East Bay flatlands, Castro Valley residents value strong schools, neighborhood safety, and a government that respects their way of life.
Ashland & Cherryland An unincorporated community in the heart of the East Bay, Ashland is a working-class neighborhood where residents deserve safe streets, quality schools, and a representative who actually shows up for them.
San Lorenzo A tight-knit unincorporated community with deep roots and a strong sense of local pride, San Lorenzo residents are looking for leadership that addresses rising costs, public safety, and crumbling infrastructure.

Why AD-20 Needs New Leadership Assembly District 20 is a district of enormous potential — but that potential is being squandered by Sacramento politicians who have lost touch with the people they represent.
The challenges below are not inevitable.
They are the direct result of policy choices made in Sacramento — and they can be reversed.
A Cost of Living Crisis The median home price in AD-20 has more than doubled in the past decade.
Rents have followed.
Young families who grew up here can no longer afford to stay.
The culprit is not the market — it is a Sacramento regulatory environment that makes building new housing prohibitively expensive and slow.
Rising Crime & Eroding Public Safety Retail theft, auto burglaries, and violent crime have all increased across the district.
Businesses are closing.
Residents feel less safe.
Sacramento's response has been to weaken penalties and reduce accountability — the opposite of what our communities need.
Failing Schools Despite record per-pupil spending, California's students rank near the bottom nationally in reading and math.
In AD-20, parents are increasingly frustrated by a system that prioritizes ideology over academic achievement and shuts them out of decisions about their own children's education.
Businesses Leaving California Major employers have relocated from the Tri-Valley to other states, taking jobs and tax revenue with them.
The regulatory burden, high taxes, and unpredictable legal environment have made California one of the worst states in the nation to run a business.
