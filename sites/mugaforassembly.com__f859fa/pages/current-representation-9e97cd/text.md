Who Holds the Seat The Incumbent - Current Representative Assembly District 20 is currently represented by Liz Ortega, a career politician and union insider, whose tenure has been marked by missed opportunities, broken promises, and a voting record that consistently puts Sacramento insiders ahead of the families and small businesses of our district.
While our communities struggle with rising costs, failing schools, and crumbling infrastructure, our representative has been busy advancing a progressive agenda that has made California less affordable, less safe, and less free.
Legislative Record A Voting Record Out of Step with California Here is how the Incumbent- Current Representative has voted on issues that directly affect the people of Assembly District 20 and ALL Californians.
YES AB 2624 - The Nick Shirley Act Privacy for Immigration Support Service Providers - Despite the misleading name given to this bill, this piece of legislation imposes civil sanctions on independent journalists who investigate stolen public funds in multi-million-dollar fraud schemes including those committed by foreign nationals.
This bill is a frontal assault on American journalism and our Free Speech.
YES AB 1421 - Vehicles: Road Usage Charge Technical Advisory Committee This California Milage Tax Proposal would require tracking every driver's milage and charging them six to nine cents per mile they drive.
Could result in a 2-car family paying an estimated $4,200 in additional taxes.
YES SB 164 - chaptered as Chapter 27, Statutes of 2026 (part of the 2026-27 state budget) This is the bill that sets Medi-Cal (California's Medicaid) countable assets/resource limit at $21,000 for one person, $31,000 for two people and $1,550 for each additional household member (up to 10), effective no sooner than July 1, 2027.
It applies primarily to non-MAGI eligibility groups — people whose Medi-Cal is based on being 65+, having a disability (physical, mental or developmental), living in a nursing home/long-term care or certain other non-income-based categories.
SUPPORTED Proposition 50 - Amendment to the California State Constitution Article XXI This legislatively referred constitutional amendment temporarily authorized use of new, Legislature-drawn congressional district maps (passed in response to Texas mid-decade redistricting) for elections through 2030, after which the independent Citizens Redistricting Commission resumes.
YES SB 1359 - Natural Gas Ratepayer "Protection Act" This bill is estimated to cost the average California homeowner tens of thousands of dollars to remove gas connections and install additional electric connections, on a grid that's notoriously unreliable.
If a ban on residential natural gas is imposed, experts warn it will cost the average homeowner $30K in retrofit costs to remove gas and convert to all-electric appliances.

Housing Affordability Crisis Home prices and rents have skyrocketed under the current representative's watch.
Families are being priced out of the communities they grew up in, yet Sacramento has offered nothing but more regulations and higher fees.
Highest Gas & Energy Prices in the Nation Californians pay the highest gas prices and some of the highest electricity rates in the entire country.
Democrat-backed energy policies have driven up costs for every family and business in AD-20, while our representative has done nothing to rein in the utility companies and radical green mandates that are making everyday life unaffordable.
Rising Crime & Public Safety Violent crime and property crime have increased across the district.
Instead of backing law enforcement and holding criminals accountable, our representative has repeatedly voted to weaken the tools police need to keep our neighborhoods safe.
Water & Infrastructure Neglect Our roads are crumbling, our water systems are aging, and our district's infrastructure needs have been ignored in favor of costly statewide ideological projects.
Failing Schools California's public schools rank among the worst in the nation for reading and math proficiency.
Our representative has blocked school choice and accountability measures that would give every child a path to success.
Small Business Exodus Businesses are leaving California at record rates.
Excessive regulations, high taxes, and anti-business policies championed by our current representative have cost the district thousands of jobs.
