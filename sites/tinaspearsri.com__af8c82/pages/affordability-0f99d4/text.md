AFFORDABILITY FOR SENIORS AND WORKING FAMILIES
Families deserve the opportunity to build a secure future without being overwhelmed by rising costs.
Proven Results
- Expanded the Social Security tax exemption for more Rhode Island seniors
- Created a refundable Child Tax Credit to help working families
- Increased access to affordable childcare and strengthened childcare providers
- Invested nearly $3 million to address food insecurity
- Increased support for Meals on Wheels, community senior programs, and nursing homes
Looking Ahead
I will continue fighting for tax relief, lower everyday costs, expanded childcare, and policies that help families stay and thrive in Rhode Island.
