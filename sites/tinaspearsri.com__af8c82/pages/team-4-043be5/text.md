Delivering for Older Rhode Islanders I’ve listened to seniors across our district who are concerned about access to primary care doctors, affording medications, staying in their own homes, and protecting their savings from scammers.
Their experiences have shaped my work at the State House and how I responded. ​ I met seniors struggling to find a doctor nearby or facing long waits for care.
I voted to increase funding for primary care, hospitals, and community health centers; pushed higher Medicaid reimbursement for EMS to keep ambulance service reliable in rural areas. ​ I talked with families wanting aging parents to stay safely at home, with limited options to help.
I voted to expand home- and community-based care, giving seniors more ways to age independently. ​ I saw retirees losing too much fixed income to state taxes and insurance gaps.
I voted to support exempting Social Security from RI income tax, expanding Medigap/Medicare access, and protecting Medicaid funding for seniors and people with disabilities. ​ I saw scammers targeting seniors through deed fraud and Bitcoin ATMs, while drug costs kept climbing.
I voted to create stronger deed fraud protections, crack down on Bitcoin ATM scams, increase AI safeguards in healthcare, and supported Pharmacy Benefit Manager reform to lower drug costs. ​ Looking Ahead I’ll keep listening and working toward a Rhode Island where every older adult can live with dignity, security, and independence.

Conserving the Ocean State.
As your Representative, I’ve worked to protect our coastline, forests, parks, and clean water through smart investments and commonsense policies, ensuring Rhode Island remains a place future generations are proud to call home. ​ Promises kept Delivered $53 million to protect forests, parks, farmland, clean water, and our coastline. ​ Protected public shoreline access and the Charlestown Breachway ​ Invested in healthy forests, wildlife habitat, and climate resilience. ​ Passed landmark protections against PFAs (“forever chemicals”) to protect drinking water and public health. ​ Reduced harmful toxins by banning dangerous rodenticides that threaten wildlife. ​ Advanced conservation efforts to preserve open space, forests, and farmland. ​ Led the effort to establish a Bottle Bill implementation study to develop solutions that reduce litter, increase recycling, and keep bottles and cans out of our waterways and neighborhoods. ​ Looking Ahead I will continue supporting practical environmental solutions that protect our communities, strengthen resilience, and preserve the character of Southern Rhode Island.
