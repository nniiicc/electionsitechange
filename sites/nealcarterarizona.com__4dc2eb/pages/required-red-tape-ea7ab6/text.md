Neal Carter for State Representative – District 15 (“Neal Carter” or “we” or “us” or “our”) has created this Privacy Policy in order to demonstrate our firm commitment to your privacy and security.
This Privacy Policy applies to all visitors of our website and users of our services.
To better protect your privacy, this Privacy Policy explains our online information practices and the choices you can make about the way your information is collected and used by Neal Carter for State Representative.
We encourage you to read the entire Privacy Policy so you fully understand how we collect, use, and store your personal information.
Information We Collect
We collect information about you in one way: 1) information you provide to us.
We do not buy, obtain, or share any information regarding our contacts through third parties.
Information You Provide
We receive information that you voluntarily enter on the websites and databases owned or administered by us, or otherwise provide to us.
For instance, when you subscribe to our newsletter and provide us with your email address or telephone number.
Whenever you provide information to us, we may use that information to communicate with you about Neal Carter’s upcoming events or news.
Cookies
When accessing this website, we use cookies to improve our service, including to help us verify your identity and remember your email address, settings, and preferences so that you do not have to enter the same information each time you visit our website.
You have choices with regard to the use of cookies and can block cookies by disabling them in your browser.
You do not need to have cookies enabled to access our website, but without cookies, certain features may not be available.
Information We Share
We do not sell, rent, license, or lease your information to third parties.
We do not sell, give out, or share any information, including your contact information, with third parties.
Information Security and Data Retention
We have technical, physical, and administrative safeguards in place to help protect against unauthorized access, use, and disclosure of the information that we collect or store, including your contact and payment information.
Our employees are trained on the importance of protecting privacy.
Under our practices and policies, access to sensitive information that could be used to identify you as an individual is authorized only for those who have a business need for such access, and sensitive information is retained only as long as necessary for business, accounting, tax, or legal purposes.
Although we work hard to protect the personal information that we collect and store, no program is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee that the safeguards we have in place will prevent every unauthorized attempt to access, use, or disclose your personal information.
If you become aware of a security issue, please contact us at neal@nealcarterarizona.com.
We will work with you to address any problems.
Your Choices & Controls
You have control over the information that you receive from us.
If at any time you want to opt out of any mailing list or text messages, you may opt out directly through the Unsubscribe link found in the footer of emails or by replying “stop” to a text message directly.
If you wish to change, update, or delete information that you have provided to us for any reason, please contact us at neal@nealcarterarizona.com to make those changes.
We use cookies to analyze website traffic and optimize your website experience.
By accepting our use of cookies, your data will be aggregated with all other user data.
