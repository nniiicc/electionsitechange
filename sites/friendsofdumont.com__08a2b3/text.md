Explore the Issues → 01 Affordability Helping families get ahead instead of falling behind.
Families are working hard, but affording basics like housing, groceries, childcare, healthcare, utilities, and property taxes keeps getting harder.
I want to lower those everyday costs so families have the breathing room to get ahead, plan for the future, and be ready when life throws something unexpected their way.
Because working more while earning less is not progress.
Learn more → 02 Healthcare No Georgian should choose between insulin and rent.
Getting sick shouldn't put your family's financial security at risk.
I'll work to make healthcare more affordable and accessible, lower prescription drug and out-of-pocket costs, and expand Medicaid instead of continuing with Georgia's limited Pathways program.
Families should be able to get the care they need without worrying about whether they can afford it.
Learn more → 03 Education Strong schools.
Supported teachers.
Stronger communities.
Strong public schools give children opportunities, support local families, and strengthen entire communities.
I'll fight to fully fund Georgia's public schools, modernize our outdated funding formula, support teachers with competitive pay, and expand early learning opportunities.
Every child deserves a strong start, and every family deserves to know their neighborhood school has the resources it needs to help their child succeed.
Learn more → → About Dumont LEADERSHIP THROUGH SERVICE.
Dumont Walker is a community leader who believes that the people closest to problems are closest to the solutions.
He's running for Georgia House District 46 because Georgia families deserve a representative who puts them first.
From expanding Medicaid to improving teacher pay to making housing attainable for working Georgians, Dumont's platform is built on the principle that every family deserves the opportunity to build resilience and to thrive.
