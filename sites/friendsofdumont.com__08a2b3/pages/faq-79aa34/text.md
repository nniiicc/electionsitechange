Here are answers to some of the most common questions about Dumont, his campaign, and his priorities.
About Dumont Walker Who is Dumont Walker? + Dumont Walker is a husband, father of two, nonprofit leader, and Democratic candidate for Georgia House District 46.
For nearly two decades, Dumont has worked in nonprofit leadership with a focus on strengthening communities and helping people thrive.
He is running to bring an approach rooted in listening, showing up, and staying focused on the issues affecting families every day.
Learn more about Dumont → What office is Dumont Walker running for? + Dumont Walker is running to represent Georgia House District 46 in the Georgia House of Representatives.
What political party is Dumont Walker? + Dumont Walker is a Democrat running for Georgia House District 46.
Dumont Walker on the Issues What are Dumont Walker's priorities? + Dumont's priorities include affordability, public education, healthcare, and protecting fundamental freedoms.
He also supports a statewide moratorium on new data center development while Georgia examines the costs and benefits of these projects and determines what protections should be in place.
Explore Dumont's priorities → Affordability What is Dumont Walker's position on affordability? + Dumont believes Georgia should focus on helping families manage the everyday costs putting pressure on household budgets.
His priorities include attainable housing, affordable childcare and early learning, lower healthcare costs, and policies that help families build greater financial security.
Learn more about Dumont's affordability priorities → What is Dumont Walker's position on housing? + Dumont supports practical efforts to increase access to attainable housing so more families can put down roots, build financial security, and remain in the communities they call home.
What is Dumont Walker's position on childcare? + Dumont supports expanding early learning opportunities for three- and four-year-olds, helping children get a strong start while easing some of the financial pressure childcare puts on working families.
Public Education What is Dumont Walker's position on public education? + Dumont supports fully funding public education, modernizing Georgia's school funding formula, improving literacy, supporting teachers with competitive pay, and expanding access to early learning.
Learn more about Dumont's education priorities → Does Dumont Walker support increasing teacher pay? + Yes.
Dumont supports competitive teacher pay as part of a broader effort to attract and retain great educators in Georgia's public schools.
Does Dumont Walker support early childhood education? + Yes.
Dumont supports expanding early learning opportunities for three- and four-year-olds to help children enter school ready to learn while easing some of the financial pressure childcare puts on working families.
Healthcare What is Dumont Walker's position on healthcare? + Dumont supports making healthcare more affordable and accessible for Georgia families.
His priorities include expanding Medicaid, lowering prescription drug and out-of-pocket costs, improving healthcare price transparency, and improving access to care.
Learn more about Dumont's healthcare priorities → Does Dumont Walker support Medicaid expansion? + Yes.
Dumont supports expanding Medicaid in Georgia so more Georgians can access affordable healthcare coverage.
What is Dumont Walker's position on Georgia Pathways? + Dumont supports Medicaid expansion rather than continuing to rely on Georgia's limited Pathways program.
Georgia Pathways currently provides Medicaid coverage to eligible Georgians ages 19 to 64 with household incomes up to 100% of the federal poverty level who meet other eligibility requirements, including at least 80 hours per month of qualifying activities.
Does Dumont Walker support lowering prescription drug costs? + Yes.
Dumont supports efforts to lower prescription drug and other out-of-pocket healthcare costs so families can get the medications and care they need without putting their financial security at risk.
Fundamental Freedoms What is Dumont Walker's position on reproductive freedom and abortion? + Dumont believes decisions about pregnancy, abortion, and reproductive healthcare are deeply personal and should be made by individuals in consultation with their families and doctors, not politicians.
He supports protecting reproductive freedom in Georgia.
Learn more about Dumont's position on fundamental freedoms → What is Dumont Walker's position on LGBTQ+ rights? + Dumont believes LGBTQ+ Georgians deserve dignity, equal treatment, and equal protection under the law.
He opposes discrimination based on sexual orientation or gender identity and efforts to take away the fundamental rights of LGBTQ+ Georgians.
What is Dumont Walker's position on voting rights? + Dumont believes every eligible Georgian should have a meaningful opportunity to vote.
He supports protecting access to the ballot while maintaining secure elections that voters can trust.
Data Centers What is Dumont Walker's position on data centers? + Dumont supports a statewide moratorium on new data center development while Georgia examines the impacts of these projects and establishes appropriate protections for residents, taxpayers, natural resources, and surrounding communities.
Learn more about Dumont's position on data centers → Does Dumont Walker want to ban data centers in Georgia? + No.
Dumont is calling for a moratorium on new data center development, not a permanent ban.
He believes Georgia should pause new development long enough to understand the costs and benefits, review current incentives, and establish appropriate protections before additional projects move forward.
Is Dumont Walker opposed to economic development? + No.
Dumont supports responsible economic growth and innovation.
He believes economic development should benefit Georgia communities and that major projects should move forward with appropriate transparency, oversight, and protections for residents.
Georgia House District 46 What is Georgia House District 46? + Georgia House District 46 is one of the districts represented in the Georgia House of Representatives.
Its elected representative serves residents of the district in the Georgia General Assembly.
