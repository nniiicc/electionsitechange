Families across our community are working hard.
But for too many, a good job and a steady paycheck don't stretch as far as they used to.
Housing, groceries, childcare, healthcare, utilities, and property taxes all compete for a bigger share of the family budget.
Even families doing everything right can find themselves with less left over to save, prepare for an emergency, or plan for the future.
Dumont believes Georgia should focus on the everyday costs putting pressure on families and make sure the state's growth creates opportunities for the people who live and work here.
Families shouldn't just be able to get by.
They should have the opportunity to thrive.
Make Housing More Attainable A safe, stable place to live is the foundation families build their lives around.
As housing costs rise, buying a first home or finding a home that fits your current situation in life has become harder.
Dumont supports practical efforts to increase access to attainable housing while respecting the communities where growth happens.
Families should have the opportunity to put down roots, build financial security, and remain in the communities they help make strong.
Make Childcare More Affordable For families with young children, childcare can be one of the biggest expenses in the household budget.
Dumont supports expanding access to early learning for three- and four-year-olds, helping more children get a strong start while easing some of the financial pressure childcare puts on working families.
Expanding early learning can create more opportunities for children while giving working parents more breathing room in the family budget.
Lower Healthcare Costs Healthcare can be one of the biggest sources of financial uncertainty for families.
Even with insurance, premiums, prescriptions, deductibles, and unexpected medical bills can quickly disrupt a household budget.
Dumont supports expanding Medicaid, lowering prescription drug and out-of-pocket costs, and making healthcare prices easier to understand.
Getting sick shouldn't put a family's financial security at risk.
Learn more about Dumont's healthcare priorities → Focus on the Everyday Cost of Living Affordability isn't about one bill.
It's what happens when every bill gets a little bigger.
A higher utility bill, rising property taxes, more expensive groceries, or an unexpected repair can quickly eliminate the breathing room a family thought it had.
And when wages don't keep pace with those rising costs, families are forced to stretch every dollar even further.
Dumont believes making Georgia more affordable means tackling both sides of the equation: lowering the everyday costs that strain family budgets and raising wages so working people can build greater financial stability.
A hard day's work should provide more than enough to simply get by — it should give families the opportunity to build a secure future.
Make Growth Work for the People Who Live Here Georgia's growth can create jobs, investment, and opportunity.
But that growth should also benefit the people and communities that make it possible.
Dumont believes decisions about economic development, tax incentives, infrastructure investments, and other major projects should consider who benefits, who pays, and what they mean for Georgia families over the long term.
A strong economy isn't just about attracting investment.
It should give the people who already live here greater opportunity to build secure, thriving lives.
