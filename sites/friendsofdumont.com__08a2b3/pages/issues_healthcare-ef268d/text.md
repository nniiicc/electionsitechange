Dumont Walker's priorities for affordable, accessible healthcare in Georgia.
Getting sick shouldn't mean choosing between seeing a doctor and paying the rent.
A prescription shouldn't force a family to cut back on groceries.
And getting the care someone needs shouldn't depend on whether they can afford an unexpected medical bill.
Too many Georgia families are paying more for healthcare while still worrying about whether they can afford to use it.
Dumont supports expanding access to affordable healthcare, lowering prescription drug and out-of-pocket costs, increasing price transparency, and expanding Medicaid so more Georgians can get the care they need.
Healthcare should help families stay healthy without putting their financial security at risk.
Expand Medicaid in Georgia Dumont supports expanding Medicaid so more Georgians have access to affordable healthcare coverage.
Georgia's current approach to healthcare coverage leaves too many people without access to the care they need.
Georgia Pathways to Coverage provides limited Medicaid coverage for some adults, but its eligibility and work requirements mean many Georgians still fall through the cracks.
Dumont supports fully expanding Medicaid so more Georgians can get the care they need.
That means being able to see a doctor, afford a prescription, manage an ongoing health condition, and get treatment early instead of waiting until a health problem becomes a crisis.
Lower Prescription Drug and Out-of-Pocket Costs Having health insurance doesn't mean much if families still can't afford to use it.
Premiums, deductibles, copays, prescription drugs, and other out-of-pocket costs can quickly put necessary care out of reach.
Dumont believes Georgia can do more to bring those costs down.
That means cutting unnecessary red tape that delays care, increasing transparency around prescription drug costs, and removing barriers that add costs for patients without improving their care.
Families shouldn't have to choose between seeing a doctor, filling a prescription, or putting another medical bill on a credit card.
Healthcare should be affordable when you need to use it, not just when you sign up for coverage.
Make Healthcare Costs Easier to Understand Families deserve to know what their healthcare is going to cost.
Too often, patients don't know the price of a medical service until a bill arrives later, making it difficult to plan, compare options, or understand what they're being charged.
Dumont supports greater healthcare price transparency so Georgians have clearer information about the cost of their care.
Healthcare is complicated enough.
Understanding what it costs shouldn't be.
Improve Access to Care Affordable coverage only matters if people can actually get the care they need.
Dumont believes Georgia must strengthen access to healthcare in every community, especially in rural areas where families may have to travel long distances just to see a doctor or reach a hospital.
That means investing in rural hospitals and healthcare providers, working to reopen or replace facilities in communities that have lost them, expanding access to doctors and mental healthcare professionals, and making preventive care and prescriptions easier to access.
No Georgian should have to go without care simply because of where they live.
When people need healthcare, they should be able to get it in their community.
Frequently Asked Questions What is Dumont Walker's position on healthcare? + Dumont Walker supports making healthcare more affordable and accessible for Georgia families.
His priorities include expanding Medicaid, lowering prescription drug and out-of-pocket costs, increasing healthcare price transparency, and improving access to care.
Does Dumont Walker support Medicaid expansion in Georgia? + Yes.
Dumont supports expanding Medicaid in Georgia so more Georgians can access affordable healthcare coverage.
What is Dumont Walker's position on Georgia Pathways? + Dumont opposes Georgia Pathways and believes the state should fully expand Medicaid.
Pathways puts unnecessary barriers between Georgians and the healthcare they need.
The program limits eligibility to certain adults with incomes up to 100% of the federal poverty level and requires 80 hours of qualifying activities each month.
Dumont believes Georgia should stop relying on a limited program that makes people clear additional hurdles to receive healthcare.
He supports Medicaid expansion to provide more Georgians with access to affordable coverage and create a stronger, more straightforward healthcare system for families across the state.
Does Dumont Walker support lowering prescription drug costs? + Yes.
Dumont supports efforts to lower prescription drug and other out-of-pocket healthcare costs so families can get the medications and care they need without putting their financial security at risk.
Does Dumont Walker support healthcare price transparency? + Yes.
Dumont supports greater healthcare price transparency so patients have clearer information about what their care will cost and can better understand their healthcare expenses.
