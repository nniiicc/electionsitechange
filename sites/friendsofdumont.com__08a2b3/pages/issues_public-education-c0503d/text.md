Dumont Walker's priorities for public education — because strong public schools create opportunities for children, support working families, and strengthen communities.
Education has always been important in Dumont's family.
His father was an elementary school principal, and his mother spent over 30 years working in higher education.
From an early age, Dumont understood how a quality education can transform a person's life.
Now, as a father of two young children, Dumont thinks about education from another perspective: making sure every child in Georgia has access to a strong public education and every teacher has the support and resources they need to help students succeed.
His priorities include improving literacy, fully funding public schools, modernizing Georgia's school funding formula, supporting and retaining great teachers, and expanding access to early learning.
Fully Fund Georgia's Public Schools Georgia's public schools should have the resources they need to serve every student.
Dumont supports fully funding public education and modernizing Georgia's school funding formula so it better reflects the needs of students and schools today.
Where a child lives shouldn't determine the quality of education they receive.
Georgia needs a funding system that gives schools the resources to meet students where they are and helps every child build a strong foundation for the future.
Support Georgia's Teachers Great schools depend on great teachers.
Dumont supports competitive teacher pay and investments that help Georgia schools attract and retain strong educators.
He also believes teachers need the resources and support to spend their time doing what they entered the profession to do: teach.
Supporting teachers is an investment in students, schools, and communities.
Improve Literacy Reading is the foundation for nearly everything that comes after it.
Strong literacy skills give children the foundation they need to succeed across every subject and throughout their education.
Dumont believes improving literacy should remain one of Georgia's core education priorities.
Schools and teachers need the resources to recognize when students are struggling and provide the individualized support they need early, so every student has a strong foundation for success.
Expand Early Learning A strong education starts before a child ever enters a kindergarten classroom.
The early years are critical for building the literacy, language, and learning skills children need to succeed throughout school.
Dumont supports expanding access to high-quality early learning programs for three- and four-year-olds so more Georgia children can enter kindergarten prepared to learn and thrive.
Expanding free Pre-K would also ease one of the biggest costs facing working families: childcare.
Investing in early education gives children a stronger academic foundation while giving families more breathing room in their budgets.
