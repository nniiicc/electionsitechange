Candidate for Georgia House District 46 — running to bring an approach rooted in service, listening, and showing up for the people of our community.
Dumont Walker has spent nearly two decades helping nonprofit organizations strengthen communities, build partnerships, and create opportunities for people whose voices are too often overlooked.
That work has reinforced something he learned long before his career began: leadership starts with service.
It means listening to people, bringing them together, and showing up when your community needs you.
Now, as a husband and father of two, Dumont is running for Georgia House District 46 to bring that same approach to the State Capitol.
A Life Shaped by Service Dumont was born in Virginia and raised by his mother after losing his father at an early age.
Although he never had the opportunity to know his father, his legacy as an elementary school principal remained an important part of Dumont's family story.
His mother went on to earn her Ph.D. and build a career in higher education, showing Dumont the value of education, perseverance, and public service.
Dumont carried those lessons with him to Xavier University in Cincinnati, where he became a standout rugby player.
The sport eventually took him around the world and taught him about discipline, resilience, and teamwork.
Later, he coached and mentored collegiate and professional athletes, helping young men and women grow as leaders on and off the field.
Those experiences helped shape the way Dumont approaches leadership today.
Nearly Two Decades Strengthening Communities For nearly two decades, Dumont has worked with nonprofit organizations to strengthen communities, build partnerships, and create opportunities for people whose voices are too often overlooked.
Throughout his career, Dumont has worked with people, organizations, and communities from a wide range of backgrounds, helping them navigate complex challenges and create stronger, more resilient communities.
Again and again, he has seen that lasting change doesn't start with someone arriving with all the answers.
It starts by listening to the people closest to the problem, bringing people together, and doing the work alongside them.
That's the kind of leadership Dumont believes government needs more of.
Why Dumont Is Running Today, Dumont and his wife are raising two young children, and the issues facing Georgia families have become even more personal.
Like parents across House District 46, Dumont thinks about the rising cost of everyday life, the schools his children attend, whether families can get the healthcare they need, and what kind of Georgia we're creating for the next generation.
He sees families working hard and still struggling to get ahead.
Teachers are being asked to do more.
Healthcare costs are putting financial pressure on families.
And decisions made under the Gold Dome are affecting people's rights, opportunities, and futures.
That's why he's running.
Dumont believes every family deserves the opportunity not just to get by, but to thrive.
His priorities include making everyday life more affordable, strengthening public education and supporting teachers, expanding access to affordable healthcare, and protecting fundamental freedoms.

If elected, Dumont will represent everyone in House District 46, not just the people who voted for him.
He'll bring the same approach that has guided him throughout his life and career: listen first, bring people together, do the work, and never lose sight of the people you're there to serve.
