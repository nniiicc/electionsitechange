Dumont Walker is running because state government should focus on the things that make a real difference in people's lives.
His priorities start with helping families thrive: making everyday life more affordable, strengthening public schools, expanding access to affordable healthcare, and protecting fundamental freedoms.
Affordability Helping families get ahead instead of falling behind.
Housing, groceries, childcare, healthcare, utilities, and property taxes continue to put pressure on family budgets.
Dumont wants to help families have more breathing room by addressing everyday costs, raising wages, expanding access to attainable housing, making childcare more affordable, and ensuring Georgia's economic growth creates real opportunity and stability for the people who live here.
Families should be able to pay their bills, prepare for the unexpected, and still have the opportunity to build a more secure future.

Supported teachers.
Stronger communities.
Strong public schools create opportunities for children, support working families, and strengthen communities.
Dumont supports fully funding Georgia's public schools, modernizing the state's school funding formula, improving literacy, providing competitive teacher pay, and expanding access to early learning for three- and four-year-olds.
Every child should have the opportunity to learn and succeed, and every teacher should have the support and resources they need to help make that possible.
Explore Dumont's priorities for public education → Healthcare Keeping families healthy and financially secure.
Getting sick shouldn't put a family's financial security at risk.
Dumont supports making healthcare more affordable and accessible for every Georgian.
That means fully expanding Medicaid instead of relying on Georgia's limited Pathways program, lowering prescription drug and out-of-pocket costs, increasing price transparency, and removing unnecessary barriers that make it harder or more expensive for people to get the care they need.
Families should be able to get the care they need without worrying about what it could do to their finances.
Explore Dumont's priorities for healthcare → Fundamental Freedoms Protecting the freedoms Georgians count on.
Dumont believes Georgians should have the freedom to make decisions about their own lives and futures.
That includes protecting reproductive freedom, treating LGBTQ+ Georgians with dignity and equality, protecting access to the ballot, and ensuring government respects the rights of the people it serves.
Georgia should be a place where people have the freedom and opportunity to build the lives and families that are right for them.
Explore Dumont's priorities for fundamental freedoms → Data Centers & Responsible Growth Growth should work for our communities.
Economic development can create opportunity, but communities should have a meaningful voice in decisions that affect their neighborhoods, natural resources, infrastructure, and household costs.
Dumont is calling for a statewide moratorium on new data center development while Georgia examines the full impact of these projects, including demands on energy and water, corporate incentives, infrastructure, and surrounding communities.
He believes Georgia should understand the costs, establish appropriate protections, and make sure future growth strengthens the communities that make it possible.
