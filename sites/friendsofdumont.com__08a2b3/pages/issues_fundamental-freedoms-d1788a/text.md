Freedom means being able to make decisions about your own life, your family, and your future.
It means being treated equally under the law and having a voice in the government that represents you.
Dumont believes government has a responsibility to respect and protect those freedoms.
His priorities include protecting reproductive freedom, standing against discrimination targeting LGBTQ+ Georgians, protecting access to the ballot, and ensuring every Georgian is treated with dignity and equality under the law.
Protect Reproductive Freedom Decisions about pregnancy, reproductive healthcare, and when or whether to start a family are deeply personal.
Dumont believes those decisions belong to individuals, their families, and their doctors, not politicians.
Dumont supports restoring and protecting reproductive freedom in Georgia.
That means working to roll back current restrictions on abortion and reproductive healthcare, opposing new restrictions, and protecting Georgians' ability to make decisions about their own bodies, health, and families.
The government should not stand between a patient and their doctor when they are making some of the most personal and consequential healthcare decisions of their lives.
Stand Up for LGBTQ+ Georgians Every Georgian deserves to live with dignity, safety, and equal protection under the law.
Dumont knows what it feels like to be judged before being known, and that experience has shaped his belief that no one should be singled out or denied equal treatment because of who they are.
He does not equate his experiences with those of LGBTQ+ Georgians, but they have reinforced a fundamental belief: our rights and freedoms are connected.
Dumont will stand against discrimination and efforts to roll back the rights of LGBTQ+ Georgians.
He supports protecting equal treatment under the law and the freedom of every Georgian to live openly, safely, and with dignity, regardless of sexual orientation or gender identity.
Protecting the rights of one community strengthens the freedoms we all depend on.
Protect the Right to Vote The right to vote is fundamental to our democracy.
Every eligible Georgian should be able to cast a ballot, have that ballot counted, and make their voice heard in choosing the people who represent them.
Dumont believes protecting that right is a basic responsibility of the government.
Georgia should have elections that are secure, accessible, and worthy of the public's trust, and election laws should make it possible for eligible voters to participate without unnecessary barriers.
Dumont supports protecting access to the ballot and pushing back against efforts that make voting unnecessarily difficult.
He believes Georgia should strengthen voting access, support the local election workers who administer our elections, and ensure voters have clear and reliable ways to participate.
The right to vote should never depend on where you live, how flexible your work schedule is, or how many unnecessary hurdles you can overcome.
Our democracy is stronger when every eligible voter has a meaningful opportunity to participate and every lawful vote is counted.
Equal Rights Mean Equal Rights Fundamental freedoms belong to everyone.
Dumont believes the people of House District 46 deserve equal treatment under the law, regardless of who they are, who they love, or whether they agree with him politically.
If elected, he will represent everyone in House District 46, not just the people who voted for him.
