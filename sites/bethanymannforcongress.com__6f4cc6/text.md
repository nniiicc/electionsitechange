MEET BETHANY A life of solving problems.
A heart for Missouri.
Bethany grew up in Foristell and now calls St.
Peters home.
As an environmental chemist and a working mom, she knows that the decisions made in Washington reach all the way to our kitchen tables.
Her work has taken her from helping farmers test their crops to helping communities test for lead and forever chemicals.
She’s running to bring that same commitment to Missouri’s 3rd District.
