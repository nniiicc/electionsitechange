A PURPOSE THAT’S PERSONAL.
A scientist, a working mom, and a neighbor ready to get to work.
Solving problems.
Bringing people together.
Bethany grew up in Foristell, Missouri, and now lives in St.
Peters.
She earned a Bachelor of Science in Chemistry from the University of Illinois Springfield and began her career as an intern with the Environmental Protection Agency.
Her work connects science to everyday life: helping farmers and producers test corn, soybeans, and forage, and helping communities test for environmental contaminants, including lead and forever chemicals.
As a technology specialist, she has worked with laboratories across agriculture, food and beverage, energy, and other industries.
She brings research, education, and practical experience to the challenges facing our communities.
A working mom.
A shared stake in the future.
Bethany is the daughter of an Air Force veteran and a working mother.
Her campaign is built around bringing Missourians together: supporting strong public schools, fair bargaining rights, reliable infrastructure, and healthier communities.
She is running as a Democrat for the U.S.
House of Representatives in Missouri’s 3rd Congressional District.
