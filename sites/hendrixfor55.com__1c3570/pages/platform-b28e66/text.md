Platform
Economic Growth
As your District 55 State Representative, I will remain committed to establishing a healthier economic ecosystem throughout our District.
I will place an emphasis on supporting local businesses, particularly minority and women-owned companies.
Public Safety
As your District 55 State Representative, I will sponsor legislation and support initiatives that center the safety and wellbeing of community members, and proactively address the underlying factors that contribute to crime and violent activities.
I believe that people have the right to feel safe in their neighborhood.
Education
As your District 55 State Representative, I will work to ensure that students and teachers receive tools and resources that support and strengthen the learning and teaching experience.
By investing in after school and enrichment programs that focus on technology and innovation we will equip our students with skills that are necessary for future occupations.
Equity
As your District 55 State Representative, I will always advocate on your behalf regarding important issues such as broadband access, and decreasing grocery tax.
Your voice will always be heard.
I will never stop fighting to eliminate barriers to opportunities, resources, and rights.
