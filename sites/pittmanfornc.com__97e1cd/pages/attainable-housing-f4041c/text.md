DONATE NOW Attainable Housing North Carolina has a housing problem.
As a young homeowner, I know firsthand how difficult it can be for families to find homes that fit their needs and finances.
We need to expand our housing options to make sure that hardworking families are not forgotten as our community grows.
Through expanding our supply of homes, revamping existing properties to become habitable, and creating financial pathways for future homeowners, we can solve the housing crisis in North Carolina.
While in office, I have introduced and supported solutions that make homeownership possible for people in Wilson and Nash counties, and I will continue to fight for current and future homeowners.
