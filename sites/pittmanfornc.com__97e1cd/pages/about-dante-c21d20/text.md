Wilson County is my home, and my local family ties go back generations.
After graduating from Wilson County Schools, I went on to college at UNC-Chapel Hill.
Public schools taught me the value of public service, and I know I wouldn't be where I am today without the support of dedicated educators. ​ After graduation, I returned home to serve the community that raised me and served on a variety of local non-profit and public service boards.
In 2024, I ran for office, and our community made history.
Together, we broke the Republican supermajority, restoring a critical check on extreme policies that were hurting working families.
That victory showed what’s possible when we organize and turn out. ​ As your representative, I strive to be a bridge builder and a problem solver.
I am dedicated to making life easier for the people of eastern North Carolina.
