Meet Jeff McNeely
Representative Jeff McNeely is a Republican State Representative from Statesville, NC.
He was selected by Iredell County GOP in July 2019 to fill out the term of Representative Rena Turner and was elected to the position by the public in 2020.
Jeff previously served as a County Commissioner in Iredell County.
He is a proven Conservative with over 4,000 recorded votes during his 24 years of experience serving Iredell County.
He has strong ties to the agricultural industry and is very involved in his local community, especially his church and other ministries.
