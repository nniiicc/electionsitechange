REBECCA’S POLICY PRIORITIES
- Agriculture Representative Reimer has always championed the families who feed South Dakota.
Whether it’s strengthening local markets, advancing value-added agriculture, or ensuring producers have the resources they need to grow, she is committed to protecting our rural way of life and supporting the next generation of farmers and ranchers.
- Public Safety Representative Reimer is committed to ensuring that every family feels safe in their homes, schools, and neighborhoods.
She supports providing law enforcement with the resources they need while backing policies that hold criminals accountable.
That includes working to get fentanyl, meth, and other dangerous drugs off our streets and out of our communities.
- Healthcare Representative Reimer is working to improve rural healthcare by supporting local providers, protecting community hospitals, and ensuring South Dakotans get the care they need, when and where they need it.
- Election Integrity Secure elections are the bedrock of democracy.
Representative Reimer is working to protect election integrity by supporting strong voter ID, transparent processes, and policies that ensure every legal vote is counted.
- Role of Government Government works best when it stays limited, efficient, and accountable to the people it serves.
Representative Reimer is committed to increasing transparency, reducing waste, and ensuring South Dakotans always know how their tax dollars are being used.
- Taxes Representative Reimer is committed to keeping taxes low, rejecting unnecessary spending, and standing firmly against any effort to create new taxes.
She believes South Dakotans, not the government, know best how to invest their own hard-earned dollars.
- Pro Life Representative Reimer is proudly pro-life and committed to ensuring that unborn children are defended and that mothers have the support they need.
She backs policies that value life at every stage while working to strengthen the resources, care, and community support available to South Dakota families.
- Second Amendment Representative Reimer is unapologetically pro-gun and will always defend the rights of law-abiding citizens to keep and bear arms.
She opposes any attempt—state or federal—to restrict gun ownership and firmly supports policies that protect self-defense, preserve our hunting traditions, and ensure South Dakotans can exercise their constitutional rights without interference.
- Outdoors & Conservation Representative Reimer is committed to protecting our hunting and fishing traditions by preserving access to public lands, supporting proper wildlife management, and ensuring our outdoor opportunities remain available for generations to come.
She believes in being good stewards of the land without unnecessary regulations, keeping South Dakota’s outdoor heritage strong and accessible for all.
- Economic Development Representative Reimer is committed to growing South Dakota’s economy by supporting small businesses, expanding workforce opportunities, and encouraging investment in rural areas.
She believes in cutting red tape, promoting entrepreneurship, and creating an environment where businesses can start, grow, and thrive.
