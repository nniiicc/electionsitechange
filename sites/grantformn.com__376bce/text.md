Economic Growth Senator Hauschild has championed the Mining, Forestry, and Tourism industries that make the Northland strong.
He advanced bipartisan permitting reform and a helium framework so that Northern Minnesota continues to foster economic growth.
Affordable Healthcare Reducing healthcare costs by holding insurance and drug companies accountable for skyrocketing prices will ensure no Northland families have to resort to fundraising to get the care that they need.
Strong & Safe Communities Senator Hauschild advanced funding to every community in Northern Minnesota to help fund local police departments.
He led the effort to finally address the rural EMS crisis in our region by passing bipartisan EMS funding legislation, and has expanded reimbursement rates and school funding for mental health services.
Social Security Tax Relief Senator Hauschild championed and passed the largest Social Security Tax Relief package in State history to provide relief for seniors on fixed incomes.
Fighting Fraud Senator Hauschild championed bipartisan legislation to establish an independent Office of Inspector General with real authority to investigate fraud and misuse of public funds, and he continues to lead efforts to strengthen oversight so taxpayer dollars are protected and abuse is held accountable.
