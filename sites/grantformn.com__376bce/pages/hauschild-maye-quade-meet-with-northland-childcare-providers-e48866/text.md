During his first term, Senator Hauschild and Senator Maye Quade championed several childcare bills.
This included expanding early learning scholarships, adding 5,200 voluntary pre-K seats across the state, establishing the Great Start Compensation Support Program to improve childcare workforce pay and benefits, and providing family child care facility grants to make upgrades necessary to meet regulations and expand capacity.
“Childcare is a top priority for me next session,” said Senator Hauschild.
“Affordable childcare is the next step toward building a Northland that can recruit business growth, inspire young people to raise their families here, and keep our communities strong.
Whether it’s a family that wants to participate in the workforce, a childcare worker who sacrifices so much to nurture our children, or our kids who will benefit from an investment in their most critical years of development, we must invest in childcare for the sake of our region.
I look forward to heading back to St.
Paul and getting to work.” “Senator Hauschild has been a fierce advocate for childcare in the Senate,” said childcare provider Courtney Greiner.
“When we went to him with our concerns, he not only listened but also offered ways we could work together to make a real impact toward addressing the childcare crisis.
