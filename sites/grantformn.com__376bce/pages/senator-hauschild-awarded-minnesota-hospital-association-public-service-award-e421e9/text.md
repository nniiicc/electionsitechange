The award was given to Hauschild at a stop on his ‘Defend Northland Healthcare’ Tour as he continues to visit hospitals and clinics across the district.
“Defending Northlanders’ access to affordable healthcare remains my top priority,” said Hauschild.
“I’m grateful to receive this award, but there is much more work to do.
As I travel the district and meet with healthcare professionals, the need to protect accessible healthcare has never been clearer with federal cuts coming.
I’ll continue fighting in St.
Paul to ensure Northern Minnesota continue to have access of healthcare in the communities we call home.” Hauschild was presented the award from Northland Hospital Leadership, including: -Aspirus Lakeview Hospital President, Greg Ruberg -Northshore Health CEO, Kimber Wraalstad -Ely Bloomenson Community Hospital CEO, Patti Banks -Cook Hospital and Care Center CEO, Teresa Debevec Members of the press are invited to join Hauschild as he continues traveling across the district to meet with healthcare leaders and residents to discuss the challenges facing Northland healthcare and the work needed to protect rural healthcare access.
