Mayors Across Northern Minnesota Back Grant Hauschild: “He Shows Up and Just Delivers”
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE
[Pictured in left photo: International Falls Mayor Drake Dill, Cook Mayor Dan Manick, Senator Grant Hauschild, and Ely Mayor Heidi Omerza] [Pictured in right photo: Two Harbors Mayor Lew Connor, Rice Lake Mayor Jayme Heim, Senator Grant Hauschild, Proctor Mayor Chad Ward, and Duluth Mayor Roger Reinert]
HERMANTOWN, MN. — State Senator Grant Hauschild hosted two ‘Mayors for Grant’ events today, beginning in Rice Lake and continuing on the Iron Range in Tower.
Mayors from across the largest and most rural district in the state, Senate District 3, joined Hauschild to discuss their work together and announce their support for his reelection.
“I served on City Council before becoming a State Senator, so I know how much local leaders are asked to do with limited resources,” Hauschild said.
“They know which road needs work, where an ambulance service is struggling, and what a big project could mean for local property taxes.
My job is to listen, work across party lines, and help them deliver.”
Those partnerships have helped secure funding for water and utility projects in International Falls, Cook, Rice Lake, Ely, and Grand Marais; Highway 61 improvements in Two Harbors; support for rural ambulance services; and investments in schools and community facilities across the district.
Hauschild also worked to help keep the Boundary Waters Care Center open in Ely, bring state bonding dollars to Cook for the first time in the city’s history, and save coast guard services on the Northshore after the federal government pulled out.
“We have different political views and different local priorities, but we all want our communities to succeed,” Hauschild said.
“That’s why I’ll keep working with mayors in every corner of this district and putting the Northland First.”
Mayors lending their support include: Chad Ward (Proctor), Drake Dill (International Falls), Tracy Benson (Grand Marais), Roger Reinert (Duluth), Jayme Heim (Rice Lake), Heidi Omerza (Ely), Dan Manick (Cook), Wayne Boucher (Hermantown), Lew Connor (as a private citizen – Two Harbors), Amy Phifer (Big Fork), Brennan Scott (Hoyt Lakes), and Dennis Wagner (Ranier).
