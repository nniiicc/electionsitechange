The group toured the facilities and discussed what needs to be done to protect healthcare as federal funding cuts loom.
Throughout his first term, Hauschild has fought to strengthen healthcare in the Northland, delivering critical EMS funding and securing gap funding for rural nursing homes and creating a rural hospital stability fund in 2026.
As Northland communities face severe challenges, including proposed Medicaid cuts under the federal “One Big Beautiful Bill Act,” Hauschild is launching the tour to hear directly from local healthcare providers and reaffirm his commitment to protecting access to affordable care heading into the 2027 legislative session.
As the tour continues, members of the press are invited to join Hauschild as he travels across the district to discuss the challenges facing Northland healthcare and the work needed to protect rural healthcare access.
