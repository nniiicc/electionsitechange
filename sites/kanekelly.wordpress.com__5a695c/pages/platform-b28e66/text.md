1.
Common Sense Public Education and School Funding
- Champion full, equitable funding for neighborhood public schools.
Support teacher pay raises and resources for diverse student populations.
- School choice options and transparency in school curriculum and spending.
2.
Common Sense Property Tax and Cost of Living
- Property tax reform and relief through increasing the homestead exemption or implementing further mechanisms to compress school district tax rates.
We have specific and realistic plans for how to lower the local burden.
3.
Common Sense Healthcare and Reproductive Rights
- Ethical protection of the unborn focused on protecting and restoring reproductive freedom so that families and medical doctors make the decisions based on medical needs.
Healthcare solutions, reform, and mental health resources while expanding access to affordable healthcare, including mental health services, and supporting maternal health initiatives.
4.
Common Sense Infrastructure, Transportation, and Development
- Investment in local infrastructure projects (roads, water, and flood management) that benefit the diverse cities in the district.
Policies that address traffic congestion and support local control/smarter development over state mandates.
5.
Common Sense Public Safety and Gun Policy
- Increase funding for law enforcement, border security and combating crime with gun safety that respect the Second Amendment while aiming to reduce gun violence.
6.
Common Sense Immigration
- Reform DACA by providing pathways to citizenship for DACA recipients.
These are friends and family.
Let’s give a pathway and opportunity to law-abiding DACA recipients.
- Reform immigration to focus on criminals of high crimes and stop unconstitution attacks on working families.
7.
Common Sense Equality for All
- Reform state-sponsored programs so that equality includes everyone regardless of class, ethnicity, race or sex.
