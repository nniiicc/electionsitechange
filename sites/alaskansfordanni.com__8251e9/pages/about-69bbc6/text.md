A candidate that truly is in it to create positive change.
My husband works in Alaska's oil industry, and thirteen years ago we made the best decision of our lives — we moved here.
We've been in the Huffman/O'Malley area ever since, and honestly, we wouldn't have it any other way.
Our two daughters are in high school, busy with their school activities and keeping us on our toes.
We raised them to love the outdoors and respect the land, and it stuck.
Some of our favorite memories are spending days on end on the Kenai, fishing — the kind of trips where you lose track of time and don't even mind.
My career has been defined by service — to customers, to families, to community.
I've led teams in the airline industry, both on the ground and in the air as a Flight Attendant.
I've worked as a Real Estate professional, helping families find their place.
As a professional river guide, I developed the leadership, problem-solving, and communication skills that have shaped who I am today.
I chose to step away from my career to raise my family, because I believe that investment matters.
I built and ran a small business, navigating the real challenges every entrepreneur in this community faces.
Today, I work within our school district, because the future of Alaska starts in our classrooms.
We feel incredibly lucky to live here.
Alaska has given our family so much, and we don't take a single day of it for granted.
This is our home.
And that's exactly why I'm running.
We need your help electing the candidate who listens.
