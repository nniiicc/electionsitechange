About Dr.
Eric Moore
Dr.
Eric Moore is dedicated to serving the residents of Georgia's District 44.
With a strong background in community service and political advocacy, he aims to bring positive change to our district and fight for our shared values.
Dr.
Eric Moore has over 20 years of public service improving Georgia communities, battling for full school funding, better police training, and boosting local economies.
Under his leadership, underfunded schools saw marked improvements in resources and test scores.
He's worked tirelessly to expand healthcare coverage to underprivileged communities and has concrete policy proposals for job creation and infrastructure upgrades.
Georgia needs leaders who are qualified AND passionate about necessary reforms.
