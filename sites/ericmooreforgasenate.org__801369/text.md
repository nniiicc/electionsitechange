Eric Moore Dr.
Eric Moore is dedicated to serving the residents of Georgia's District 44.
With a strong background in community service and political advocacy, he aims to bring positive change to our district and fight for our shared values.
Dr.
Eric Moore has over 20 years of public service improving Georgia communities, battling for full school funding, better police training, and boosting local economies.
Under his leadership, underfunded schools saw marked improvements in resources and test scores.
He's worked tirelessly to expand healthcare coverage to underprivileged communities and has concrete policy proposals for job creation and infrastructure upgrades.
Georgia needs leaders who are qualified AND passionate about necessary reforms.
Passionate about making a difference and creating positive change.

Community Empowerment Dr.
Moore believes in empowering every citizen to participate in our democracy and strive for positive change.
As he is a person who has experienced the everyday struggles of life and overcome them, he understands that people need someone to fight for them when career politicians are just trying to benefit themselves and their inner circle.
Healthcare Access A top priority will be improving access to affordable healthcare for all residents in our district.
Dr.
Moore believes that everyone deserves to have some sort of preventative and wellness insurance no matter their income.
Education Reform Focused on advancing educational opportunities and resources for all students and families.
Dr.
Moore will fight to FULLY subsidize public school funding to give teachers higher salaries and to give students better resources and attention while in school.
Economic Growth Committed to supporting local businesses and fostering economic development within the community.
Dr.
Moore wants all communities to become sufficient and thrive, while rejuvenating and unifying all communities via professional education and training then career placement.
No one should have to live paycheck to paycheck while deciding which bills need to be or can be paid at that time.
