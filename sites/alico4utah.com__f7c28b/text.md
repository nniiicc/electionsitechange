GET INVOLVED ABOUT TRAVIS I'm running because Utah deserves leadership that listens before it speaks.
Travis calls West Valley City home and believes Utah is at its best when families, small businesses, and communities come first.
He’s a project manager in the tech industry, leading teams, solving complex problems, and delivering real results; skills he believes government should use more often.
Growing up in the Boston area shaped his appreciation for the United States and its founding ideals, including personal liberty, civic responsibility, and self- government.
As an Eagle Scout and member of the Order of the Arrow, he learned the value of leadership, service, and putting others first.
Travis earned a degree in communications from Brigham Young University and an MBA from the Universityof Utah.
When he’s not working or meeting with neighbors, you’ll find him outdoors, hiking, camping, snowboarding, playing hockey, or enjoying beach volleyball with friends.
For Travis, public service isn’t about politics.
It’s about people.

WHY I'M RUNNING PRIORITIES REPRESENTATION Every voter I speak with, regardless of their party affiliation, shares the same frustration: Our political system too often protects the party’s interests instead of serving the people.
Elected officials should answer to the citizens who sent them to office and not party leadership.
I believe representatives must be accountable first and foremost to their constituents.
That means listening carefully, thinking independently, and voting for what’s best for District 26, rather than what advances a partisan agenda.
If elected, I will serve with integrity and independence .
I will represent you, and your best interests, in every matter before the Utah State Legislature.
SAVE THE GREAT SALT LAKE The Great Salt Lake has been shrinking for years and has recently reached dangerous levels.
As the lake recedes, toxic dust is exposed, putting thousands of Utahns at risk.
If we don’t act, that risk will only grow worse.
While we can’t control rainfall or snowpack, we can exercise responsible stewardship for our most precious resource: water .
Restoring the lake won’t be quick or easy.
It will require cooperation , accountability , and leadership willing to work across the aisle to protect Utah’s future.
