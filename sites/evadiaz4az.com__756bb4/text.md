Democrat Eva Diaz is a tireless fighter for families in our community and a a lifelong educator.
She prioritizes effective public education, quality affordable healthcare and champions women’s and minority rights.
Meet Eva
Eva is an Arizona native, who grew up in Phoenix and moved to Tolleson in 1992.
She is married and the mother of 2 children.
Her 27 years in the education arena include teaching in three area elementary school districts, and in administrative positions as Assistant Principal at the Isaac Elementary School District and as Principal at Avondale Elementary School District.
She has also been an instructor at South Mountain Community College and at Arizona State University.
Dr.
Diaz got her PhD from Arizona State in Curriculum and Instruction and Educational Leadership.
Eva has been part of the Red for Ed movement of the Arizona Education Association which began an aggressive fight for public education in the state in 2018.
She has also been active as a volunteer and in paid positions with Chicanos Por La Causa Community Center.
Eva Diaz and her mother Ruth Diaz are co-owners of Ni Hao Amigos Language Preschool in Avondale, where their vision is to prepare young children to be successful in kindergarten.
Over the years, Eva has also been part of the Children’s Ministry at her church.

Community Priorities
Education Champion
As a teacher, administrator, and mother of 2 children who attend AZ public schools, and as a member of the AEA who supported the Save Our Schools Petition drive/Red for Ed, I have long been fighting for improvements to our educational system.
Now, I want to take that fight to the Arizona Legislature so that every teacher in Arizona gets the support they need, and every child, the education they deserve.
Defender of Women’s Rights
Extremists in Arizona want to resurrect a 1901 Arizona anti-abortion law with no exceptions for rape, incest, or the health of the mother.
Around the country, restrictive laws like these are resulting in frightening stories of women being refused reproductive care from doctors who fear prosecution.
Anti-abortion extremists must realize that their loved ones are not immune from pregnancy complications.
My goal in the State Senate will be to make the changes needed so all women in Arizona get the healthcare they need.
Climate Activist
Everyone has a right to clean air, pure water, and affordable health care.
Arizona must address problems brought about by climate change, like drought and breathable air quality.
As your State Senator, I will work to ensure that all Arizonans have access to quality healthcare, clean water, and fresh air.
Together, we can preserve the way of life in Legislative District 22 and all of Arizona.
