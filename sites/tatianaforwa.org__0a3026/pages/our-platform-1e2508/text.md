OUR PLATFORM
Tatiana will bring the power of this Senate seat directly to the people of the 37th.
Our Platform is built by community leaders and in collaboration with our neighbors.
The policy memos and priorities below are a reflection of Tatiana’s commitment to collaboration across the 37th and Washington State.

- I will defend crucial state-funded programs like Running Start, Washington College Grant, and the Washington Education Legacy Trust account
- I will champion progressive revenue sources such as HB 2100, the Well Washington Fund to continue investing in the College Grant
- I will commit to championing bills such as SB 5906 (SAFE Act), to create the strongest protections to ICE-proof our schools, and community spaces
- I will ensure free K-12 meals are healthy, nutritious, and grown locally by supporting Statewide Food Security Strategy (HB 2238) and commit to further building on the 2008 Local Farms - Healthy Kids Act
- I will invest in CTE (Career and Technical Education) partnerships with local public schools, CTCs (Community Technical Colleges), and local unions to ensure students have access to high-quality, hands-on training that ensures access to a well-paying job
- I will work to pass legislation that bans exclusionary discipline (suspension, expulsion, arrests) for vague and subjective behaviors
- I will work to restore funding for the Outdoor Education for All Act (HB 2078) so our fifth and sixth graders can enjoy multi-day, outdoor learning
- I will fight to fully fund counselors, social workers, and community-based providers like Healthpoint so that students are easily able to access necessary care

- I will work with colleagues in the Legislature to pass a statewide moratorium on new hyper-scale AI data centers.
- I will pursue a Senate version of HB 2515, requiring data center developers to pay for their energy use, lower usage during peak hours, and comply with existing energy laws.
