During a recent interview on the CR Podcast, Daniel Horowitz highlighted Indiana State Representative Andrew Ireland as a leading conservative voice in state government.
“If we had an Indiana chamber full of Andrew Irelands… we would have a fortress for ordered liberty,” Horowitz said.
The conversation focused on issues impacting Indiana families, including farmland loss, data center incentives, immigration enforcement, and public safety.
Ireland emphasized the need for policies that prioritize working Hoosiers over special interests.
