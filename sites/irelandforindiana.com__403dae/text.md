Meet Andrew Ireland
A lifelong Hoosier fighting for home.
Andrew Ireland is an attorney, lifelong Hoosier, and strong conservative voice representing Indiana House District 90, including parts of Franklin and Perry Townships.
A former Deputy Indiana Attorney General, Andrew worked on some of Indiana’s most consequential cases and appeals—defending Indiana’s pro-life laws, protecting election integrity, and fighting the Biden administration’s open-border policies all the way to the U.S.
Supreme Court.
Andrew lives in Franklin Township with his wife, Maddie, and their daughter, Anna Grace.
They attend Indian Creek Christian Church.
