Upon endorsing Lou Correa, the Chairman of the Orange County Young Democrats Jesse Ben-Ron released the following statement: “Since 1998, we have advocated for Democratic Candidates around the state who we believe will help create a brighter future for our generation, and Lou Correa will do just that.
We know that he will fight for greater access to college and educational opportunities, equal-pay-for-equal work and fair wages, and green technology that will lead to new jobs and cleaner air.
We look forward to supporting him in the final weeks before the Election” Upon hearing news of the endorsement, former State Senator Lou Correa stated: “I am so proud to have received the support of the newest generation of Democratic voters in Orange County.
I’m looking forward to working with them to advocate for key policies such as limiting college loan debt, increasing access to healthcare for our young folks, and creating new career technical schools so that everyone has the opportunity to have successful careers.” In addition to today’s endorsement, Lou Correa has gained widespread support from a variety of labor, environmental, public safety,
