Home Meet Lou Support Lou Contact Press Package Donate Home Meet Lou Support Lou Contact Press Package Donate Andrew Scibetta October 21, 2016 CORREA GARNERS WIDESPREAD SUPPORT FROM YOUNG LATINO DEMOCRATS Andrew Scibetta October 21, 2016 The California Young Democrats Latino Caucus Backs Lou Correa for Congress Santa Ana, CA— Following weeks of sweeping endorsements from a variety of elected officials and influential organizations, including the International Union of Operating Engineers Local 12 and Congresswoman Lucille Roybal-Allard, today, the California Young Democrats (CYD) Latino Caucus announced their official support of former State Senator Lou Correa in the race to represent California’s 46th Congressional District.
The CYD Latino Caucus was established in 2006 by the California Young Democrats in an effort to engage a growing Latino population.
In a letter addressed to the campaign, California Young Democrats Latino Caucus Chair Sean Rivas wrote: “We are proud to support Lou Correa in his bid to be the next Congress Member in the 46th Congressional District.
Senator Correa understands the struggles our Latino youth face today, and we strongly support his race to the House of Representatives.” Upon hearing the news, former State Senator Lou Correa released the following statement: “I am proud to have the endorsement of the CYD Latino Caucus.
Now more than ever, it is critical that we engage young Latinos in the political process and encourage them to make their voices heard.
Millennials have the power to influence elections and government policies, and it is our job to ensure that they have the information and tools they need to voice their concerns and make a real difference.
In Congress, I will continue fight to improve our public education system, expand career technical and job training programs, and address the student loan debt crisis.” In addition to today’s endorsement, Lou Correa has garnered widespread support from a variety of labor, environmental, public safety, and business organizations, as well as elected officials and community leaders,
