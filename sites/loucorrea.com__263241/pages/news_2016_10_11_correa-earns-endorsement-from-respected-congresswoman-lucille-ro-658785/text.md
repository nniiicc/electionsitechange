Home Meet Lou Support Lou Contact Press Package Donate Home Meet Lou Support Lou Contact Press Package Donate Andrew Scibetta October 12, 2016 CORREA EARNS ENDORSEMENT FROM RESPECTED CONGRESSWOMAN LUCILLE ROYBAL-ALLARD Andrew Scibetta October 12, 2016 Congresswoman Roybal-Allard Joins Long List of National Leaders, State Legislators, and Local Officials Supporting Lou Correa for Congress Santa Ana, CA— On the heels of receiving key endorsements from Assemblywoman Cheryl Brown and Orange County Civil Rights Leader Sylvia Mendez, today, former State Senator Lou Correa secured backing from Congresswoman Lucille Roybal-Allard in his campaign to represent CD-46 in Congress.
In announcing her endorsement, Congresswoman Lucille Roybal-Allard stated: “I could not imagine a better candidate for CA-44.
Lou fights for Orange County with a dedication and passion that is unrivaled, and I have complete faith that he will continue to do so in Congress.
I am looking forward to working with him to secure good-paying jobs and quality educational opportunities for our families, safe neighborhoods, and comprehensive immigration reform.” Upon hearing news, former State Senator Lou Correa remarked: “I am honored to have the support of Congresswoman Roybal-Allard.
For over two decades, she has fought tirelessly for her community and advocated for just policies that lift up hardworking men and women in Congress.
As the first Mexican-American woman elected to Congress, she addressed the diverse needs of the Latino/a community by advocating for comprehensive immigration reform, expanding access to healthcare, securing good paying jobs and benefits for workers, and improving neighborhood safety for our families.
I consider her to be an inspiration.
I look forward to working alongside Congresswoman Roybal-Allard in Washington to develop policies that will address the needs of ALL communities.” In addition to today’s endorsement, Lou Correa has gained widespread support from a variety of labor, environmental, public safety, and business organizations, as well as elected officials and community leaders, including: Organizations: · California Democratic Party (CDP) · California Labor Federation · United Association Local Union 250: Steamfitters & Refrigeration · California School Employees Association (CSEA) · California State Association of Letter Carriers (CSALC) · The California Statewide Law Enforcement Association (CSLEA) · United Farm Workers of America (UFW) · California Police Chiefs' Association · Peace Officers Research Association of California (PORAC) · National Latino Peace Officers Association · California Small Business Association · Building Our Leadership Diversity (BOLD) PAC · International Union of Painters and Allied Trades District Council 36 · International Union of Operating Engineers Local 501 · Santa Ana School Police Officers' Association · Santa Ana Police Officers' Association · Anaheim Police Officer's Association Political Action Committee · Orange County Deputy District Attorneys · Orange County Employees Association (OCEA) · Garden Grove Firefighters Association · Garden Grove Police Officers Association · Californians for Humane Immigrant Rights Leadership Action Fund (CHIRLA Action Fund) · Orange County Business Council · Association of Orange County Deputy Sheriffs · Communication Workers of America · Laborers Local 652 · Orange County Young Democrats Elected Leaders: · House Democratic Leader & Congresswoman Nancy Pelosi · House Democratic Whip & Congressman Steny Hoyer · House Democratic Caucus Chair & Congressman Xavier Becerra · Congressional Progressive Caucus Co-Chair and Congressman Raúl M.
