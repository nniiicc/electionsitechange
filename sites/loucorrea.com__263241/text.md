Raised by working-class parents, Lou learned early the value of hard work, gratitude, and showing up for others.
Those lessons still guide him today.
Since 1998 , Lou has focused on one goal: helping Orange County families build better lives and reach the middle class.
He believes public service should be about people, not playing partisan games .
Known for getting things done, Lou has earned a reputation as a practical problem-solver who listens, collaborates, and delivers real results for his community.
His experience includes serving in the California State Assembly (69th District), the California State Senate (34th District), and as an Orange County Supervisor representing the 1st District.
Meet lou About California’s 46th District The 46th district includes Orange County cities of Anaheim, Santa Ana, Orange and Garden Grove.
IT is a middle-class, blue-collar community, that is home to many proud American Veterans, many from our Greatest Generation, as well as the largest number of Dreamers in the United States.
