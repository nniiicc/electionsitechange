The new rating from one of the nation’s leading nonpartisan election forecasters underscores the increasingly competitive landscape in Southwest Michigan and comes on the heels of McCann’s historic $1.8 million fundraising quarter, shattering records for a Democratic challenger in MI-04.
The shift comes as national Republican and MAGA-aligned groups continue pouring millions of dollars into the district to defend Huizenga.
“This rating change has confirmed what we have been seeing on the ground for months,” said Simone Archer Krauss, campaign manager for Sean McCann .
“Voters are frustrated with rising costs while Bill Huizenga looks out for himself and the special interests that have bankrolled his campaign.
Sean has shattered fundraising records and assembled a growing coalition of voters in Southwest Michigan ready for change.
The national GOP is pouring millions into a district they once considered safe because they see the same thing we do: Sean is on track to win this race and bring change to Southern Michigan.” The ratings change comes amid growing scrutiny of Huizenga’s support for Trump’s tariff policies , rising energy costs , and his ties to special interests .
With less than a month until Election Day, the Cook Political Report’s new rating places MI-04 among the nation’s most closely watched congressional battlegrounds.

### About Sean McCann: Sean McCann has spent his career standing up to the powerful and on behalf of people in Southwest Michigan.
From cracking down on slumlords to serving as a State Senator, Sean has fought to change broken systems.
In the State Legislature, he stood up to polluters to protect Lake Michigan, and he cut taxes for working families.
Sean knows Washington is broken and he’ll fight for the change we need – banning members of Congress from trading stocks, negotiating for lower drug prices, and stopping massive tax breaks for billionaires and big corporations, so we put hard-working people first.
Born and raised in Michigan, Sean attended Western Michigan University before serving as a Kalamazoo Commissioner, State Representative, and now State Senator for Southwest Michigan.
Along the way, he’s earned a reputation among Democrats and Republicans alike as a serious problem-solver who works across the aisle and stands up for families trying to make ends meet.
As a husband and father raising his family in Michigan, Sean understands what’s at stake for working families.
In Congress, he’ll bring the same work ethic, common sense, and determination he’s brought to public service throughout his career, and fight every day to make Washington work for people it’s supposed to serve.
About the race for Michigan’s 4th Congressional District: MI-04 is one of the country’s key 2026 battlegrounds.
The R+3 district has been named a top “Red to Blue” race by the DCCC and rated a toss-up by Sabato’s Crystal Ball.
Cook Political Report has consistently moved this race in Democrats’ direction, calling the district “ a bright spot for Democrats .” Poll after poll has shown Sean McCann leading Bill Huizenga, and McCann continues to post strong fundraising numbers.
According to Cook, Sean McCann’s State Senate seat (MI-19) “makes up about a third of the 4th District and includes 45% of the 4th’s primary electorate.” Governor Gretchen Whitmer and Secretary of State Jocelyn Benson each carried MI-04 with more than fifty percent of the vote in 2022.
MI-04 covers Southwest Michigan and includes the cities of Kalamazoo, Battle Creek, Holland, St.
Joseph, Benton Harbor and the outer suburbs of Grand Rapids including Ottawa County.
Sean has also received endorsements from Michigan Governor Gretchen Whitmer, Michigan Secretary of State Jocelyn Benson, Senator Elissa Slotkin, Congresswoman Hillary Scholten, former Congressman Mark Schauer, over 15 labor groups, and over 70 local elected officials and community leaders.
