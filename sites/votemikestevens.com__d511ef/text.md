Mike Stevens

Built here.

Working for you.
Mike Stevens has called Yankton home for more than five decades.
As an attorney, school board member, and state legislator, Mike has spent his career showing up for his neighbors.
He’s not chasing headlines, he’s doing the quiet work of building a community where families build their future.
Mike is married to Linda, a public school teacher of 42 years.
Together they have four children and seven grandchildren.
Mike is a partner at Blackburn & Stevens Prof.
LLC in Yankton.
Supporting Education
22 years on the Yankton School Board and Vice Chair of the House Education Committee.
Mike fights for stable funding and local control so schools can recruit great teachers and prepare students for the modern workforce.
“I spent 22 years on the school board because I believe in what happens inside those buildings.
The best thing Pierre can do is get out of the way and let great teacher’s teach.”
Keeping Communities Safe
As an attorney and Chair of the House Judiciary Committee, Mike backs law enforcement and a fair, firm justice system that protects families and keeps South Dakota one of the safest places to live.
“By supporting our police and maintaining a fair justice system, we keep South Dakota the best place to raise a family.”
Growing Jobs and Businesses
As an attorney and Chair of the House Judiciary Committee, Mike backs law enforcement and a fair, firm justice system that protects families and keeps South Dakota one of the safest places to live.
“South Dakota’s strength is its people.
When we keep taxes reasonable and invest in the basics, our businesses can do what they do best and that’s good for everyone.”
Strengthening Families
Mike views every bill through one lens: does this make South Dakota more affordable for the next generation?
Mike supports housing, healthcare access, and policies that empower parents — not grow government dependency.
“Every bill we pass should be viewed through a simple lens: does this make our state a better, more affordable place for the next generation to put down roots and raise families of their own?”
Local Decision Making
Mike consistently opposes one-size-fits-all mandates from Pierre.
Yankton knows what's best for Yankton and he champions the rights of local municipalities, school boards, and county commissions.
"Local communities should govern themselves.
I have always believed that the people of Yankton are better equipped to solve Yankton's problems than any distant mandate from Pierre."
