Chris Jeter, September 2026 T he Indiana Professional Fire Fighters PAC has endorsed my re-election in House District 88 for the November 3 general election.
The troopers of the Indiana State Police Alliance endorsed this campaign.
Now Indiana’s professional fire fighters, paramedics and EMTs have done the same.
I did not take either one for granted, and I am not going to pretend they are the same endorsement.
When you dial 911, the people who show up fall into two groups.
One comes because something was done to you.
The other comes because something happened to you.
A wreck on I-69.
A house fire in McCordsville.
A grandfather who stops breathing at the dinner table on a Sunday.
“Nobody calls 911 on their best day.” Rep.
Chris Jeter, House District 88 Nobody calls 911 on their best day.
Fire fighters and medics walk into the worst hour of a family’s life, do the work, and then go do it again for the next family.
They are not asking for much in return.
Mostly they are asking that the people making decisions at the Statehouse actually understand what the job costs.
I want to be straight about what this endorsement is built on, because their letter is straight about it.
They did not hand me a scorecard.
Here is what they wrote: “Through our discussions with you, we know that you are willing to listen thoughtfully and carefully consider issues that impact the delivery of Fire and Emergency Medical Services and the well-being of our members.
We know that you endeavor to represent your constituents in the same manner.” Indiana Professional Fire Fighters PAC, endorsement letter That is a fair description of where we are.
It is an endorsement built on access and on listening, and I would rather earn it that way than be handed it for a press release.
The work of turning those conversations into policy is ahead of us, not behind us, and I intend to do it.
What I can point to is that I did not start listening to Indiana’s fire service the week an endorsement was available.
That record is below.

Common questions about this endorsement What is the Indiana Professional Fire Fighters PAC?
It is the political action committee affiliated with the Professional Fire Fighters Union of Indiana.
It is funded by voluntary contributions from members, and it endorses and supports candidates based on their positions on fire and emergency medical services issues.
Did a fire department endorse Chris Jeter?
No.
Fire departments are units of local government and do not endorse candidates for office.
The endorsement came from the Indiana Professional Fire Fighters PAC, the political action committee affiliated with the fire fighters’ own statewide organization.
The two are separate, and the distinction matters.
What is the Professional Fire Fighters Union of Indiana?
PFFUI is the chartered Indiana state council of the International Association of Fire Fighters.
It represents more than 8,000 professional fire fighters, paramedics, EMTs and 911 telecommunicators across 97 local affiliates, and it marked its 90th anniversary in 2025.
What has Rep.
Jeter done on fire and emergency services issues?
In the 2025 session he co-authored House Resolution 11, celebrating the 90th anniversary of the Professional Fire Fighters Union of Indiana, and House Concurrent Resolution 10, honoring Chief Steven Orusa on his retirement from the Fishers Fire Department.
On broader public safety he authored House Bill 1258 in 2026, revising Indiana’s definition of a crime of violence, which passed the House 92 to 0 and is now Public Law 85-2026.
Which public safety organizations have endorsed Chris Jeter in 2026?
Two so far.
The Indiana State Police Alliance , which represents Indiana State Police troopers, and the Indiana Professional Fire Fighters PAC, affiliated with the Professional Fire Fighters Union of Indiana.
Who represents Indiana House District 88?
Rep.
Chris Jeter, a Republican from Fishers.
The district includes portions of Hancock, Hamilton, Madison and Marion counties.
He chairs the House Judiciary Committee, co-chairs the Bail and Release Review Commission, and is a practicing attorney and Navy JAG veteran of the Iraq War.
