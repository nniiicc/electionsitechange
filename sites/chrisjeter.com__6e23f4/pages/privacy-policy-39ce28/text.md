Home Bio Updates Connect Donate Select Page Privacy Policy Effective date: August 24, 2026 Who we are This website, chrisjeter.com, is operated by Jeter for Indiana, the candidate committee supporting Chris Jeter for Indiana House District 88.
References to "the campaign," "the Committee," "we," "us," and "our" mean Jeter for Indiana.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website and our communications, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.
By using this website or signing up for our communications, you agree to this Privacy Policy.
Information we collect We collect information that you choose to give us, including: Your name (first and last) Your mobile phone number Your email address Your mailing address (street, city, state, and ZIP) Information you submit through our forms, such as your interest in volunteering or in receiving a yard sign, and any message or comments you send us We may also automatically collect basic technical information when you visit the site, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, and general usage data through standard website analytics.
We use this information to operate and improve the site.
We do not knowingly collect more than you provide, and you can use most of the site without giving us any personal information.
Like most websites, chrisjeter.com uses cookies and similar technologies.
A cookie is a small text file a website stores on your device so it can recognize your browser on a later visit.
We use them in three ways: Essential.
Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, sets a short-lived cookie that tells real visitors apart from automated traffic.
The site cannot operate securely without it.
We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell the information cookies collect.
How we use your information We use the information you provide to support the campaign and to communicate with you, including to: Send you campaign updates, news, and event information Coordinate volunteer activities and respond to volunteer and yard sign requests Conduct get-out-the-vote (GOTV) outreach and voter contact Respond to your questions and messages Send you text messages and emails if you have opted in to those programs Maintain records required by campaign finance and election law Text messaging (SMS) program If you opt in, we send campaign text messages from Jeter for Indiana to the mobile number you provide.
Message frequency varies.
Message and data rates may apply.
Reply STOP to any message to opt out at any time.
Reply HELP for help.
Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any donation, volunteer signup, or other action.
Mobile opt-in and consent are not shared.
The mobile information you provide and your consent to receive text messages will not be shared with or sold to any third party for that third party's own marketing purposes.
We share your mobile number only with the service providers that help us operate our messaging program (described below), and only so they can send messages on our behalf.
Full text messaging program terms are in our Terms and Conditions .
We do not sell or rent your information We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing.
Note that under Indiana and federal campaign finance law, certain contribution information (such as a contributor's name, address, occupation, and employer for contributions above reporting thresholds) is reported to election authorities and becomes part of the public record.
That disclosure is a legal requirement and is separate from any marketing use.
Service providers we use We use trusted third-party service providers to operate the campaign's communications and website.
These providers process your information only to provide services to us, under terms that limit their use of your information.
They include, for example: An SMS / text messaging platform (currently RumbleUp) to send campaign text messages Email and customer relationship management (CRM) tools to send email and manage supporter records Website hosting and analytics providers to operate and measure the site As stated above, mobile opt-in information and SMS consent are not shared with any third party for that party's own marketing.
Data retention We keep your information for as long as needed to run the campaign and to communicate with you, and for as long as required to comply with campaign finance, tax, and other legal recordkeeping obligations.
When information is no longer needed for those purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details.
You may ask us to remove your information at any time using the contact details below, subject to records we are legally required to keep.
Your choices and opt-out Text messages: Reply STOP to any campaign text to stop receiving texts.
Reply HELP for help.
Email: Click the unsubscribe link in any campaign email, or contact us using the details below.
Access or removal: You may contact us to ask what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to remove it, subject to any records we must keep by law.
Children's privacy This website and our communications are intended for adults and are not directed to children under 13.
We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.
Data security We take reasonable measures to protect the information you provide.
No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.
Changes to this policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page.
Your continued use of the site or our communications after a change means you accept the updated policy.
