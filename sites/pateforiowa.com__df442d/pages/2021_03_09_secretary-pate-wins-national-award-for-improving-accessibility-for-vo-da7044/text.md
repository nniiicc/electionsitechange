For the second time in three years, Iowa Secretary of State Paul Pate earned a national award for his efforts to help Iowans with disabilities vote.
The U.S.
Election Assistance Commission (EAC) announced Thursday that the Iowa Secretary of State’s Office is the recipient of the 2020 Clearie Award for Improving Accessibility for Voters with Disabilities.
