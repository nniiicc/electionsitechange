Mariannette Miller-Meeks, R-Iowa, the winner of Iowa’s 2nd Congressional District.
The Committee on House Administration is considering a challenge from Democrat Rita Hart, who is contesting the state-certified results that place Miller-Meeks as the winner by just six votes out of nearly 400,000 cast in the race.
“Every step of the way, the administration of Iowa’s elections is either bipartisan or nonpartisan,” Pate wrote.
“Unfortunately, Rita Hart chose to bypass the nonpartisan Iowa process and take her challenge to the U.S.
