We consistently rank among the top 10 states for voter registration and participation.

H.R.
1, passed by the U.S.
House, and S.
1, currently under debate in the U.S.
Senate, are attempts by the federal government to take over elections.
Prior to ramming the bill through the U.S.
House, members who voted in favor it did not seek input from election officials.
If they had, they would have learned there is bipartisan opposition to it, for many reasons.
