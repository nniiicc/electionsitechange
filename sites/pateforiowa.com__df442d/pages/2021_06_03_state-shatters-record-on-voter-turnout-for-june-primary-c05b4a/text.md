Home Meet Paul Get Involved News Contact Donate Home News State shatters record on voter turnout for June primary Home News State shatters record on voter turnout for June primary State shatters record on voter turnout for June primary By wordpress@victoryenterprises.com | June 3, 2021 | News | No Comments DES MOINES, Iowa (KCRG) – Iowa Secretary of State Paul Pate announced Wednesday that Iowa broke its record for a primary election with how many voters participated in Tuesday’s primary.
More than 500,000 people cast ballots, early or in-person, for the election.
“The credit goes to Iowa voters, poll workers, and county auditors,” Secretary Pate said.
“Despite the COVID-19 pandemic and protests going on across the state, Iowans made their voices heard in record numbers.
I am so proud of everyone who participated and the people behind the scenes in all 99 counties that made it happen.
