Pate met with the Linn County Sheriff and area police chiefs to talk about resources and help for people in abusive relationships.
His office runs the Safe at Home program.
It provides protection for survivors of domestic violence, sexual abuse, and stalking, giving victims the ability to keep their information confidential, like their mailing address, driver’s license info, and other forms of identity.
The connection to Pate’s better-known duties as Iowa’s chief elections officer has to do with voter registration, which is a public record.
