Affordability Families should be able to pay their bills, stay in their homes, and build a future without falling further behind every month.
Chris will put working families ahead of billionaire special interests and fight to lower the costs squeezing household budgets.
Lower property taxes while protecting the funding our public schools need Fight to lower utility bills and prevent large corporations and data centers from passing their infrastructure costs on to families Make housing more affordable by increasing supply and reducing unnecessary permitting delays.
