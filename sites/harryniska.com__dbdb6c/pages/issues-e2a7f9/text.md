Principles and Priorities
I believe that government should empower you to live your best life, not dictate what you can do, say, and believe.
As your representative, I am fighting for:
- Accountability for fraud: Minnesotans are rightly furious about the multi-billion dollar fraud scandal that has embarrassed our state and stolen resources from taxpayers and those in need.
We need to make it easier to prosecute fraudsters, as well as hold accountable those in government who were supposed to prevent this fraud.
- Lower taxes and fiscal responsibility: You know how to spend your money better than the politicians and bureaucrats in St.
Paul.
I’ll fight to make sure you get to keep more of it, so you can invest in your families, businesses, and private charities.
- Public Safety: We should support and rebuild law enforcement, not defund it.
Minnesota has a law enforcement crisis, as more and more officers are feeling attacked and are leaving the profession.
We need creative solutions to recruit more good law enforcement officers, and leaders who celebrate them instead of tearing them down.
- Education: Parental involvement should be celebrated, not demonized.
I will fight for school choice, educational options including charter schools, and curriculum transparency.
- Free Speech and Religious Freedom: We need to get back to the American way of free speech and persuasion, not cancel culture and censorship.
- Election Integrity: To preserve our freedom, we must be able to hold our government accountable through free and fair elections.
I will work for reforms to our system that make it easy to vote, but impossible to cheat.
- Checks and Balances: As we have witnessed especially during the Walz administration, our freedoms cannot be subject to the whims of any one powerful person or branch of government.
- Protection of life: I am pro-life and will work to expand the legal protections for all human life, starting with the unborn.
- Second Amendment Rights: Each of us has the right to defend ourselves and others from violence.
