Gayle believes... ​In prudent, responsible financial spending In tackling critical housing and affordability issues​ In preserving safe communities In the value and necessity of encouraging neighborhood civic engagement That Your Voice Matters Meet Gayle Representing Chittenden-20 district (Colchester) in the Vermont House A strong advocate for equity and fairness, Gayle has been a leader in multiple arenas, spending decades providing support, resources and expertise to make a positive impact on individuals and the community.
A former mental health/substance abuse counselor, exotic pet consultant and small business owner and elected leader of her Resident Owned Community (ROC), Gayle is a dedicated leader with a strong work ethic who brings empathy, an open mind, and a commitment to hearing others and helping them be heard. ​ As a Mother and grandmother to two other Vermont residents, Gayle cares deeply about Vermont’s future, and believes that our system works best when people are engaged and active in the decisions that impact their lives and communities.
As your representative, Gayle will bring this experience and leadership to advocate for the residents of Colchester in the Vermont Statehouse.
Gayle looks forward to representing Colchester in the Vermont House of Representatives.
