Greatly shaped by the values of public service, Rhodesia spent much of her childhood working alongside her grandmother on volunteer projects to connect vulnerable families with food, housing, and other critical services.
Through this, Rhodesia learned early on that leadership is about serving others.
As a mom, Rhodesia knows how to get things done.
She has served our community as a non-profit leader, member of the Tracy City Council, and Congressional District Director, helping thousands of young people access mental health care and mentorship, building the first permanent homeless shelter in Tracy, and fighting to make our communities safer.
As our Assemblymember, Rhodesia is committed to always going the extra mile for our community, working to improve our quality of life, and making sure kids and families have more opportunities to succeed.
From a young age, Rhodesia’s grandmother Dorothea instilled in her a passion for community service.
