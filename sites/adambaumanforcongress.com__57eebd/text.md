Protecting Seniors
Social Security is currently estimated to remain fully in tact only until 2034 unless legislation is enacted by Congress.
Our Seniors and the generations to come deserve to receive the benefits that they have paid into throughout their working lives.
I fully support enacting changes that will ensure Social Security remains in place, however, raising the minimum age limit to receive these benefits is not one of them.
We need to enact common sense legislation that doesnt include making Seniors wait even longer to receive the benefits that they deserve.
