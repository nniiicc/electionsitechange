Fighting for our local schools As a retired elementary school teacher of 37 years, Gayle Manning has continued her commitment to helping children succeed.
She is a primary sponsor of HB 455, which modernizes Ohio’s education laws and reduces red-tape so that teachers have more time to focus on students.
Gayle is also a sponsor of HB 715, to increase transparency for private schools that receive state-funded scholarships.
Gayle Manning wants to make Ohio a national leader in vocational and technical training to ensure every student is career-ready when they graduate.
Throughout her time in the State Legislature, she has worked to advance local job training efforts by securing millions of dollars to support Lorain County Community College.
