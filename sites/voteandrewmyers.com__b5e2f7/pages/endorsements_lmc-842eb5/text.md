League of Minnesota Cities Press Release:
LMC has selected 16 members of the Minnesota House of Representatives and nine members of the Senate as Legislators of Distinction for 2024.
The honor recognizes legislators for specific actions that aided efforts of #MnCities during this year’s #MnLeg session.
Legislators of Distinction are annually approved by the League’s Board of Directors to recognize that in order to successfully serve commonly shared constituents, state and city officials must work together to meet the unique needs of rural, suburban, and urban residents all across Minnesota.
Today we’re sharing Minnesota House members we recognized.
