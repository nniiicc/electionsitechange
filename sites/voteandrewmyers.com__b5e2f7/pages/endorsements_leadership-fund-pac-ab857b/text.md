On behalf of the Minnesota Chamber of Commerce Leadership Fund PAC, I am pleased to inform you the PAC Board has voted to endorse your candidacy for the Minnesota House of Representatives.
Our endorsement signals to the business community that you are committed to building upon and improving our state’s economy, investing in our current workforce in addition to preparing the next generation, and creating an environment that encourages growth and innovation.
We are confident that you are a pro-business leader who shares the business community’s goals of economic vitality and a flourishing workforce in Minnesota.
You may publicize this endorsement in your campaign literature, media advertising, and public appearances.
