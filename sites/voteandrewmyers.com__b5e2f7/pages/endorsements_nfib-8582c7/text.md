PAUL, MN, June 18, 2024 —The state’s leading small business political action committee, NFIB MN PAC, today endorsed Representative Andrew Myers (Tonka Bay) for re-election in House District 45A.
“Representative Andrew Myers is a proven advocate for Main Street,” said John Reynolds, NFIB Minnesota State Director.
“In his first term, Andrew put his experience as a small business owner and community leader to work for the hardworking men and women in our state.
Now more than ever, we need strong allies like Andrew Myers in the Minnesota House of Representatives.” State legislative endorsements are determined by the NFIB MN PAC Board, which is comprised of small business owners in the state.
Incumbent legislative endorsements are based on the 2023-24 NFIB MN Voting Record, which reflects how a lawmaker voted on priority issues for small business owners.
Lawmakers must have a score of 70 percent or better to qualify for endorsement.
Myers holds a 100 percent pro-small business voting record.
NFIB represents more than 10,000 small businesses in Minnesota.
The NFIB MN PAC endorsement is an important signal to voters across the state that a candidate will stand with small business.
