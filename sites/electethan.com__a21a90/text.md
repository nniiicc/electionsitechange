SMALL BUSINESSMAN Through hard work, Ethan has built a successful real estate and auction business and he invests in our community.
He regularly volunteers his time at community auction events and serves local organizations.
His wife, Bridget, is a local doctor serving women from Miami and Cass Counties.
LOCAL FARMER Ethan raises beef cattle on his 7th-generation family farm and understands the importance of agriculture to our community and our state.
He has a passion for ensuring that rural Indiana remains strong.
We need more of our community in Indianapolis, not more of Indianapolis in our community.
COMMUNITY LEADER A lifelong Hoosier, Ethan has served our community at the state & local level in many ways, both in government and in local organizations, always remembering and fighting for our values.
As President of the Miami County Council, he balanced budgets and kept taxes low.
As State Representative, Ethan has fought for our way of life by authoring bills to boost funding for small and rural schools, bring more healthcare access to rural communities, and more.
He will continue to listen to our needs and fight for the people of House District 23.
