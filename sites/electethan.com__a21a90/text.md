Meet Ethan Manning
Ethan Manning is a small businessman, local farmer, and community leader.
Join the team
As a small businessman, farmer, and community leader, Ethan represents the best of our community.
As a farmer and Miami County native, Ethan will always fight for our small towns, small schools, local businesses, and our agricultural industry.
He stands for life at all stages, will promote fiscal responsibility, and will always defend our Second Amendment rights.
As State Representative, Ethan will take local concerns and values to Indianapolis, just as he has done in the past by responding to the needs of Miami and Cass Counties.
A seventh-generation farmer, Ethan will advocate for our local values and way of life.
He and his wife, Bridget, live in rural Cass County.
