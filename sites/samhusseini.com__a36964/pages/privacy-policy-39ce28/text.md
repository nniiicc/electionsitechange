Privacy Policy
Sam Husseini for Congress (“we,” “us,” or “our”) values your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect the personal information you provide through our website, www.samhusseini.com (the “Site”).
Information We Collect
We collect the following types of information when you interact with us online:

- Contact Information: When you sign up for updates, volunteer, donate, or complete forms, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, and mailing address.
- Donation Information: When making a contribution, we may collect payment information, employer, and occupation, as required by federal election law.
Automatically Collected Information
- Usage Data: We use cookies and analytics tools to collect technical data such as your IP address, browser type, pages viewed, and time spent on the Site.
- Device Information: We may collect info about the device you’re using to access the Site (e.g., mobile or desktop, OS type).
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Communicate with you about the campaign, events, volunteer opportunities, and other updates;

- Improve our website and digital outreach;
- Ensure compliance with legal and FEC requirements.
Sharing Your Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share your information with:
- Trusted service providers who help us operate our website and run the campaign;

We use cookies, pixels, and similar technologies to improve user experience and for campaign analytics.
You can control cookies through your browser settings.
