See his most recent writings here .
He's lived for over 20 years in Maryland's 4th district .
He's confronting officials, including at the State Department, on the Gaza genocide and other issues.
These included Antony Blinken , Chuck Schumer and Saudi Amb.
Turki .
He pushed governments to invoke the Genocide Convention against Israel -- which South Africa did in December 2023.
He did ground breaking work on Covid origins , and on the possible lab origins of the 2014 Ebola outbreak in West Africa .
He drafted Archbishop Desmond Tutu's last published article , which was on the threat of Israel's nuclear weapons, and appeared a year before the Archbishop died.
During his over 25 years at the Institute for Public Accuracy , Husseini drafted thousands of news releases on issues ranging from Medicare-for-All to the environment to reparations to civil liberties.
He is also a visual artist, see: BeThatEmpty.org He's worked as a computer programmer, helped manage a homeless shelter and been a substitute teacher in public schools.
He holds a double major in Applied Math (Computer Science specialty) and Logic and Computation (from Department of Philosophy) from Carnegie-Mellon University.
After graduating, he worked for a year on Wall Street at Moody's Investor Service, got a job offer from JP Morgan but decided he had a different calling.
Before working at the Institute for Public Accuracy, he worked at the American-Arab Anti-Discrimination Committee and the media watch group FAIR.
Outlets which have published his work include USA Today , The Nation , Salon, The Washington Post , The Chicago Tribune , CounterPunch, Common Dreams and AntiWar.com.
