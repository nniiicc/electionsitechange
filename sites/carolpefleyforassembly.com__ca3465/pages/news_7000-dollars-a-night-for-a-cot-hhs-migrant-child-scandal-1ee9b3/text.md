$7,000 a Night for a Cot: Fraud, Abuse, and a System That Failed Migrant Kids
Under the Biden administration, the number of unaccompanied minors entering the U.S. surged.
CBP reported more than 400,000 encounters with unaccompanied children from 2021–2024, and HHS placed over 300,000 children with "sponsors" during that period.
Meanwhile, a 2023 New York Times investigation found that HHS had lost contact with over 85,000 children after release to sponsors, an alarming breakdown in oversight that raised red flags about trafficking, labor exploitation, and fraud.
Becerra was Secretary of Health and Human Services from 2021-2024.
It was his job to oversee housing the children in shelters or emergency intake sites, providing food, medical care, education, and safety, vetting and approving sponsors as well as conducting follow up and safety checks.
As we now know, thousands of children were sent to non existent addresses and to unvetted people, never to be seen or heard from again.
Despite the scale of the failures, no senior officials have faced legal consequences.
