Taste Your Way Through Downtown
Saturday, September 19, 2026 | 12:00 PM – 5:00 PM
Experience the best of Los Gatos in one unforgettable afternoon.
Taste of Los Gatos transforms our historic downtown into a vibrant, pedestrian-only celebration of exceptional food, local wine, live music, shopping, and community.
Whether you're enjoying a day with family and friends, or planning the perfect date, we invite you to wander tree-lined streets filled with signature tastings, outdoor entertainment, family-friendly activities, and the welcoming spirit that makes Los Gatos one of the Bay Area's favorite destinations.
- Make the most of your afternoon with a Tasting Wristband, your passport to the festival’s signature culinary experience throughout downtown.
Upgrade to the Bites & Wine Tasting Wristband to add tastings from participating wineries.
Tasting Wristbands have sold out before festival day in past years.
Purchase early to secure advance pricing and ensure availability.
EXPLORE EVERY CORNER OF THE FESTIVAL Signature Culinary Tastings With your Tasting Wristband, enjoy signature bites from approximately 30 participating Los Gatos restaurants.
Enjoy one tasting per guest at each location.
From longtime local favorites to exciting new culinary destinations, each stop showcases the creativity and quality that define Los Gatos' dining scene.
Local Wine Experiences With your Bites & Wine Tasting Wristband, enjoy tastings from approximately 20 participating wineries, hosted inside downtown businesses for unique tasting experience that blends wine, shopping, and exploration.
Among the featured experiences, David Bruce Winery will offer a rare vertical barrel tasting of Pinot Noir, featuring three vintages tasted directly from the barrel.
Pedestrian Paradise Village Lane as well as North Santa Cruz Avenue from Main Street to Bachman Avenue, will be closed to vehicle traffic, creating a lively, strollable downtown festival.
Guests 21 and older may enjoy wine tasting included with the Bites & Wine Tasting Wristband or craft beer and hard cider purchased separately from the Chamber Tap Truck or Tap Wall while strolling within the closed festival streets.
Alcohol purchased directly from participating restaurants must be enjoyed on the restaurant's premises.

Santa Cruz County Board of Supervisors Meeting
Tuesday, August 11, 2026 · 9:00 AM North County Government Center, Board Chambers 701 Ocean Street, Room 525, Santa Cruz, CA 95060
Watch or join remotely: Zoom: https://santacruzcounty-us.zoomgov.com/j/1601236676 Phone: +1 669-254-5252 · Webinar ID: 160 123 6676 Meeting portal & full agenda packet: https://santacruzcountyca.primegov.com/public/portal
What's on the agenda
The Board of Supervisors meets Tuesday morning for its regular session.
A number of items on the calendar directly affect housing, land use, and county finances in AD-28's Santa Cruz County communities.
Housing fees and inclusionary housing requirements.
The Board will receive reports on the Affordable Housing Impact Fee Nexus Studies and the Inclusionary Housing Feasibility Study, and will direct staff on next steps for implementing Housing Element Programs H-3H and H-3J.
These studies set the framework for what the County can charge new development and what it can require of builders.
Short-term borrowing.
The Board will consider a resolution authorizing the issuance and sale of 2026-27 Tax and Revenue Anticipation Notes in an amount not to exceed $55 million.
Coastal land use and habitat.
A public hearing is scheduled on the Moran Lake Monarch Butterfly Habitat Management Plan, including a Riparian Exception, Biotic Approval, and a CEQA Notice of Exemption.
Supervisor pay.
Item 12 asks whether the Board should amend County Code Section 2.02.060 to end the practice of tying Supervisor salaries to Superior Court judges' salaries.
County code and ordinance changes.
The Board will consider approving in concept an ordinance amending Chapter 5.48 of the County Code to align with changes in state law, and a second ordinance updating automobile liability insurance requirements for the County.
How to be heard
- In person: Public comment is taken near the start of the meeting.
Speakers are limited to two minutes and may speak once.
- In writing: Comments emailed to BoardOfSupervisors@santacruzcountyca.gov must arrive by 5:00 p.m. the day before the meeting to be included with agenda materials.
Include the agenda item number.
- Online: Use the "Add Comment" tool on the Board's meeting portal.
- Consent items can only be pulled for discussion by a Board member, if you want an item pulled, contact your Supervisor's office before the meeting starts
