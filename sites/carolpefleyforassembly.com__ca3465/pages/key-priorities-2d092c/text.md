Store Donate Key Priorities My Key Priorities: California is at a crossroads.
Decades of one-party rule have led to skyrocketing living costs, burdensome regulations, rising crime, and a diminishing voice for parents in their children's education.
The Democratic supermajority in Sacramento has prioritized political agendas over the well-being of everyday Californians.In 2009, I launched my own real estate company in Silicon Valley.
Through dedication and perseverance, I’ve grown that business into a successful brokerage, with approximately 45 agents working under my license.
I am a mother to two wonderful children and, as a single parent, have experienced firsthand the challenges of raising a family in California’s increasingly unaffordable economy.
Lowering the Cost of Living: Advocate for tax relief and policies that make housing and essential goods more affordable for all Californians.
Reducing Overregulation: Streamline regulations that hinder small businesses and job creation, fostering a more robust economy.
Enhancing Public Safety: Support law enforcement and implement policies that effectively address crime, ensuring safer communities.
Protecting Parental Rights: Ensure that parents have a decisive role in their children's education and health decisions, respecting family values and choices. “ Together, we can build a California that offers opportunity, safety, and prosperity for all its residents.
Join me in bringing balanced leadership to Sacramento. ” — Carol Pefley, Candidate for California State Assembly District 28 Help Carol Pefley Win Donate Today Volunteer Newsletter Block This newsletter signup form needs a storage option.
