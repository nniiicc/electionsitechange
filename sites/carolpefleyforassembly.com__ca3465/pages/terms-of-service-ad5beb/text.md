Carol Pefley for Assembly 2026 sends recurring text messages about Carol Pefley's campaign for California State Assembly District 28, including campaign news, event and public-meeting alerts, volunteer opportunities, voting reminders and fundraising requests.
Frequency.
Message frequency varies, up to 8 messages per month, as we near Election Day (November 3, 2026).
Cost.
Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan.
Carriers aren't liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Opting out.
Reply STOP, END, CANCEL, UNSUBSCRIBE or QUIT at any time.
You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
Reply START to rejoin.
Help.
Reply HELP or email carol@carolpefleyforassembly.com Eligibility.
You must be 18 or older and the account holder of the mobile number, or have the account holder's permission.
Carriers.
Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Privacy.
Your mobile number and opt-in consent are handled under our Privacy Policy.
No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
Changes.
We may update these terms; the effective date above shows the latest version.
