Store Donate Host an Event The best campaigns are built in living rooms, not in Sacramento .
Carol is running to give our district a stronger voice and the most powerful way to build that voice is neighbor to neighbor.
A house party, a backyard BBQ, a coffee with a dozen friends : these are the conversations that win elections and shape what Carol carries with her to the Capitol.
You don't need a big house, a big budget, or a big guest list .
You just need a few people who care about the cost of living, local control, and whether anyone in Sacramento is actually listening.
Carol will do the rest.
We make it simple.
Tell us what you have in mind, and our team handles the logistics — invitations, RSVPs, sign-in materials, campaign literature, and any compliance details.
You provide the space and the guests.
Carol brings the conversation.
What an event can look like Coffee & Conversation — 8–15 neighbors, one hour, morning or afternoon Backyard BBQ or Happy Hour — casual, family-friendly, 25–50 guests Meet the Candidate Reception — evening event with light refreshments Small Business Roundtable — owners and operators talking shop and solutions Virtual Meet & Greet — 30 minutes on Zoom, perfect for busy schedules What we provide Digital invitations and RSVP tracking Yard signs, literature, and sign-in sheets A suggested run-of-show so you're never guessing A campaign staff contact from start to finish Carol — on time, prepared, and ready to listen What you provide A space (home, patio, office, community room, or Zoom link) A list of friends, neighbors, or colleagues to invite Refreshments, if you'd like- totally optional!
