Program Description.
Drew Peterson for District 19 operates an SMS messaging program to keep supporters informed about the campaign.
By opting in, you consent to receive recurring autodialed text messages from Drew Peterson for District 19.

Types of Messages.
Subscribers may receive text messages including, but not limited to: campaign updates and news, volunteer opportunities, event invitations and reminders, voting information (including dates, times, and polling locations for upcoming elections), get-out-the-vote reminders, fundraising appeals, and other communications related to the Drew Peterson for District 19 campaign.

Opted-in users may receive approximately 2–5 text messages per month.
Message frequency may increase as election dates approach.

Help.
