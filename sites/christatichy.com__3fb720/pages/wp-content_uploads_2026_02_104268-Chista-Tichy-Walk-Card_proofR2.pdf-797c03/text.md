Christa Tichy is a trailblazer in every aspect of her life.
She is the first female licensed master electrician in the State of Maryland.
She is a long-time Montgomery County resident who is seeking election to the Maryland House of Delegates, District 19 to advance her unique platform focusing on labor, education, housing and other important issues facing her community. @christatichyfordelegate /ChristaforMontgomeryCounty @christa_tichy BLUE COLLAR WORKER ★ BLUE RIBBON IDEAS 1.
Education/Investment into vocational education and a skilled workforce.
Demand for a skilled workforce is at an all-time high.
Whether administrative, healthcare, service, educational, or in the trades, we need to create opportunities to develop the skills necessary to fill the jobs.
I believe that begins in high schools.
2.
Increase affordable housing and equitable development.
Incentivize housing developers to build housing to meet the demands of people with moderate or lower incomes, for rental and for ownership.
Expand and always leverage the Moderately Priced Dwelling Unit program at levels more people can afford, while being mindful of existing communities.
3.
Equitable Transit-Oriented Development (TOD) TOD can solve several social issues if it is done through an equity lens.
We must leverage these projects to create permanently affordable housing, good jobs during and after construction, and build community wealth rather than displacement.
4.
Support small businesses and working families.
Entrepreneurship should be obtainable for anyone willing to work hard enough.
We need to incentivize and lift up small businesses through access to capital, targeted tax incentives, and streamlined regulation.
Large corporations get these perks, so why not our small businesses?
Above all, local and state taxes should never disproportionately affect small businesses.
5.
Protect seniors on fixed incomes.
Expand targeted property-tax relief, control cost drivers like housing and transportation, and prioritize efficient, cost-effective investments that help older residents age in place.
