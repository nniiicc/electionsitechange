DONATE SURVEY Home About Steve's Beliefs Endorsements Volunteer Contact Steve's Beliefs Here's A Brief List of Where Steve Stands on The Issues Pro-Family: Families are the fundamental building blocks of society, as recognized throughout history and philosophy.
John Adams famously stated, “The foundation of national morality must be laid in private families.” Parental Rights: Parents, not the state, know what’s best for their children.
This includes supporting School Choice, protecting Homeschool Rights, and ensuring transparency in public school activities & curriculum.
Preserving the jurisdiction of loving parents to make decisions for their children without undue interference from governmental entities has been and should always be a cornerstone of good governance.
Pro-Local Autonomy: Local governments should have the freedom to govern themselves without unnecessary interference from higher levels of government.
By empowering local authorities, communities can create policies that address their unique needs and challenges.
The principle of local autonomy fosters a greater sense of accountability, as decisions are made by those who are more closely connected to the community.
It also strengthens trust between citizens and their leaders, as residents can see the direct impact of governance on their daily lives.
In addition, local autonomy encourages innovation, allowing communities to develop solutions that work best for their specific circumstances while preserving the values and priorities of the people they serve.
Pro-Business: As owner of a regional trade magazine in Hampstead, Steve understands firsthand the burdens of Maryland’s licensing requirements and regulations on local businesses.
He will advocate for reducing regulations that stifle small business growth and work to create an environment that is attractive to outside resources, bringing more opportunities to the citizens.
Pro-2A: Steve is a staunch defender of our Second Amendment right to protect our families and our homes.
We must defend this right against any attempts at restriction and ensure that our constitutional freedoms remain intact.
Steve opposes any measures that restrict our constitutional right to bear arms.
Pro-Liberty: The COVID-19 response highlighted government overreach.
Mandates without proven effectiveness are unacceptable in a free society.
Steve will advocate for policies that respect individual liberty and personal responsibility.
The Maryland Piedmont Reliability Project (MPRP) is a great concern to our community.
Steve firmly opposes it.
Halting this project and preserving the rights and sovereignty of the citizens, as well as Carroll County's agricultural heritage, is an immediate priority.
Pro-Life: Human life is sacred from the moment of conception until natural death. © 2026 All rights reserved, Friends of Steve Patten.
