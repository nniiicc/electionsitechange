I'm Nisha Lodhavia , I’m running for the 15th District Senate seat to be your voice.
For me, it’s not about politics or pushing an ideology.
It’s about people.
It’s about focusing on common sense solutions that will lower costs, strengthen public schools, improve access to affordable health care, protect women’s health care, and grow an economy that works for all families.
For too long, partisan politics has distracted us from real progress.
My service in the community has convinced me of something simple and powerful: together, we can do better.
I am committed to working across differences to improve everyday life by creating opportunities for everyone.
So, let’s roll up our sleeves and show what we can achieve by uniting with purpose.
Your vote is your voice.
I would be honored to have your trust and your support.
