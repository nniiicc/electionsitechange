Media
Nisha Lodhavia is the independent, commonsense voice Western Kent County needs in the State Senate.
Communications to District 15 households should introduce Nisha as:
- A former educator
- A dedicated community advocate and leader
- Someone who has spent decades serving her community
- Not a career politician, she is a problem-solver
- Puts people over politics
Key Priorities
Lower the Cost of Living
Nisha will fight to lower the cost of living by addressing everyday expenses, including:
- Energy bills
- Grocery costs
- Rent
Her focus is on delivering real relief for working families.
Support Small Businesses
Nisha will be a strong partner to the small businesses that anchor local towns and Main Streets by:
- Helping them grow, hire, and thrive
- Including stakeholders in conversations before legislation is passed
- Decreasing regulatory burdens, streamlining processes, and encouraging intra- and interagency communication
Affordable Health Care
At the doors, Nisha consistently hears two concerns:
- Health care costs are too high.
- There aren't enough doctors.
As the former board president of a free clinic serving uninsured and underserved Delawareans, Nisha has seen these challenges firsthand.
She will work to:
- Make health care more affordable and accessible
- Bring more doctors and medical professionals to the community
Strong Public Schools
As a longtime educator, Nisha will stand up for strong public schools and quality education.
Protect Farms and Farmland
Nisha understands how important farms and farmland are to the community and will fight to:
- Make farming profitable
- Preserve farmland
- Decrease regulatory burdens
Support First Responders
Nisha will stand with the police, fire, and EMS professionals who keep the community safe by ensuring they have:
- Reliable funding
- Necessary equipment
- Proper training
- Adequate staffing
Leadership Message

- Listens first to get things done
