Information we collect Information you provide, such as name, email address, phone number, volunteer interests, and any message you submit through our forms.
Donation details shared with us by ActBlue, including contact information and contribution data required for campaign finance compliance.
Payment card details are handled by ActBlue and are not stored by the Campaign.
Automatic data from analytics tools, including IP address, device and browser type, pages visited, and approximate location.
How we use information Operate the site and respond to your requests.
Communicate about the campaign, events, and opportunities to volunteer or donate.
Comply with campaign finance, reporting, and legal obligations.
Improve site performance, measure outreach, and keep the site secure.
How we share information With service providers who help us operate the website, process forms, send emails, or provide analytics.
With ActBlue to process donations, and with vendors as required to fulfill contribution processing and compliance.
This policy does not apply to third-party sites you visit through links on this website, such as ActBlue or social platforms.
When required by law, regulation, or valid legal process.
We do not sell your personal information.
Your choices You can unsubscribe from campaign emails using the link in our messages or by contacting us.
You may request updates or corrections to your information.
You can choose not to provide information, but some services (like volunteering or donating) will not be available.
Data retention and security We retain information as long as needed for campaign operations and legal compliance.
We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system can be guaranteed secure.
Children's privacy This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
Updates We may update this policy from time to time.
