Care for DelawareHealthcare should be a source of security, not stress.
The Challenges We Face
- Skyrocketing Costs
- Severe Provider Shortage
- Unacceptable Wait Times
- The Rural Divide
My Commitment
Families should not have to choose between healthcare and paying their bills.
Yet, many Delawareans forgo much needed care because it’s just too expensive.
Healthcare is not a partisan issue; it’s a people issue.
Together with all sectors of healthcare, have an opportunity to create a healthcare system that focuses on accessibility, affordability and better outcomes for all Delawareans.
- I strongly support initiatives to actively recruit and retain high quality healthcare professionals including physicians, nurses, dentists, behavioral health providers and other specialists.
We should create stronger health career pathways that begin in high school, offer better loan repayment programs and scholarships, and increase clinical training opportunities.
The critical partnership with Thomas Jefferson’s Sydney Kimmel Medical School is a start in addressing our long-term shortages.
- Reducing wait times means expanding the healthcare workforce, improving access to primary and specialty care, and finding creative solutions, like telehealth and team-based care.
By using our existing healthcare professionals more effectively and investing in systems that connect patients with care sooner, we can ensure Delawareans receive timely treatment.
- Someone’s zipcode should not determine their access to healthcare.
Delaware must invest in rural healthcare by supporting community clinics, expanding mobile and telehealth services, improving recruitment and retention of providers in underserved areas, and strengthening partnerships that bring healthcare professionals closer to the people who need them.

Supporting Seniors and Veterans
Delaware has a large and growing senior population, which makes senior policy relevant to housing, healthcare, transportation, taxes, workforce, and community planning.
Our seniors deserve to age with dignity, access dependable healthcare, safe and affordable housing, and remain connected to their communities.
I am committed to making it easier for seniors to navigate programs and services available to them, while supporting caregivers and strengthening the community resources that help older Delawareans thrive.
After serving our country, every veteran should feel confident that their community will stand with them and ensure they have the opportunity to live with security, dignity, and purpose.
I am committed to ensuring our veterans have access to quality healthcare, mental health support, meaningful employment opportunities, and the benefits they have earned.
We should make it easier to navigate services, expand access to affordable housing and transportation, and strengthen connections to job training and employers.
Supporting veterans also means supporting their families, and ensuring that no one falls through the cracks when seeking the resources available to them.
Supporting Firefighters and the Law Enforcement Community
Our law enforcement officers and volunteer firefighters are essential in keeping our communities safe.
I am committed to making investments in their equipment, training, and staffing so they can continue to do their jobs safely and effectively.
I will also support programs to address recruitment, retention, and mental health and wellness.
Investing in these communities is investing in the strength of our communities.
