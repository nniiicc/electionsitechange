Skip to content Skip to main content Elect Ginny Ehrhart State Representative HD-36 Menu Home Meet Ginny Top Issues News Contact Facebook-f Instagram DONATE Meet Ginny I am honored to represent House District 36, which includes portions of western Cobb County, a community I have proudly called home for more than 45 years.
As the daughter of a U.S.
Army Lieutenant Colonel, I was raised on military bases across the country before my family eventually settled in Cobb County.
I attended Osborne High School and later graduated from Auburn University with a degree in International Trade.
Throughout my career, I have been blessed with a variety of opportunities that have shaped my perspective and strengthened my commitment to public service.
I taught high school economics and American government, homeschooled my children, and built a successful small business.
I also became a frequent speaker on regional and national television and radio, sharing my passion for Southern cuisine and hospitality.
As a television chef and home life consultant for FamilyNet Television, I authored two cookbooks, Sunday in the South and Seasons in the South, and hosted a popular weekly lifestyle radio show on Atlanta’s 920 AM.
My experiences as a hands-on small business owner, educator, entrepreneur, wife, and mother have deeply influenced my conservative principles and approach to governing.
I believe in supporting public safety, fostering a pro-business environment, and strengthening educational opportunities for our children.
I strive to bring a practical, common-sense, and solutions-oriented perspective to the Georgia General Assembly because I believe that is exactly what the people of our district deserve.
