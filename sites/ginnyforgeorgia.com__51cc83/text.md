TOP ISSUES Economic Relief I've already helped deliver real results by capping property tax increases, providing up to $500 in homeowner tax relief, and issuing direct tax rebates.
I voted to lower GA income taxes, suspended the gas tax, and held insurance companies accountable to reduce costs.
These conservative policies put money back in Georgians' pockets.
I'll keep fighting to ease these financial burdens.
Public Safety This session, I worked to strengthen law enforcement and protect the vulnerable by combating human trafficking through required training, cracking down on exploitation, and safeguarding seniors from financial abuse.
I also defended the right to worship without disruption.
I'll keep supporting strong laws, backing our law enforcement, and ensuring Georgia remains a safe place to live, work, and raise a family.
Education I'll continue making education a top priority by fully funding our schools and strengthening student outcomes.
This session, I supported expanding early literacy, improving math instruction, and growing career training opportunities.
I also backed policies that reduce classroom distractions.
I'll keep fighting to ensure every Georgia student is prepared for success.
