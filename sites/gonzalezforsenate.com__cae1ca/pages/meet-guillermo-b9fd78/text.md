As the son of immigrants, he learned early that in Mexico the government served the powerful, not the people.
In America, he saw that hard work and faith could change everything, and he pursued that same American Dream.
Those early years shaped his belief that the promise of the Valley and the opportunity to work hard, raise a family, and build a future must be protected for the next generation.
Back home in Bakersfield, Guillermo went to work early.
He spent his youth on construction sites, in local shops, and at the family restaurant, learning that every job has value and every worker deserves respect.
When the pandemic struck, he helped his father keep the restaurant open, doing whatever was needed to protect his family's business, while starting his service in the office of Congressman David Valadao.
Today, Guillermo and his family own and operate their restaurants— The Idle Spur in McFarland & Guillermo's Steakhouse in Delano.
Guillermo knows that government works best when it listens to the people it serves.
He has seen how overregulation and rising costs make it harder for families to stay in California, and how distant self-serving politicians ignore the realities of life in the Central Valley.
His approach to public policy is simple: trust and empower local communities to make decisions about their water, energy, and schools; cut the red tape that holds back small businesses; and make homeownership possible again so families can build a prosperous and stable life here in the Golden State.
For Guillermo, this campaign is not about titles or power.
It is about restoring the promise that brought his parents to California in the first place.
He believes the Valley's best days are ahead if leaders put people before politics, hold government accountable, and give every family the freedom to work, worship, and prosper.
That is the California Dream worth fighting for, and Guillermo is ready to make it real.
With firsthand experience of the American Dream through his family, Guillermo understands the will of the people he will represent and is committed to fighting to preserve those values in Sacramento and to securing a brighter future for the Central Valley for generations to come.
