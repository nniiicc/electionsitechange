Putting East Portland and North Clackamas First During Difficult Times
I’ve spent my career advocating for Oregon’s working families, strong schools, and quality public services because I know what it's like to rely on food assistance and worry about how to keep a roof over your head.
My dad is a pastor and my mom is a small business owner and they raised me to value hard work and taking care of our neighbors.
That’s why as an education and labor advocate, and now as your State Representative, I've fought to expand mental health resources, advocated to make college more affordable, and voted to lower taxes for hundreds of thousands of Oregonians.
I’m running to keep representing our community because I know how to listen to what communities need, fight to bring down costs for regular people, and work with local leaders to deliver results.
We will need creativity, collaboration, and smart solutions.
Together, we can get through these times and make Oregon a place where everyone feels safe, can put food on the table, and has the chance to thrive.
- Lamar
