I'm a lifelong Sandown resident , a graduate of Timberlane Regional High School, and a recent graduate of the University of New Hampshire — where I earned a degree in Political Science with minors in Justice Studies and Forensic Science.
Growing up in Sandown and attending Timberlane schools gave me a firsthand understanding of the issues that matter most to our community.
I'm running for State Representative because I believe Sandown deserves a voice in Concord that is accessible, hardworking, and willing to listen.
Public service is not a stepping stone for me — it's an opportunity to give back to the town that helped shape who I am.
As an active member of the Sandown Republican Committee, I've stayed engaged in grassroots outreach and community service that strengthened my understanding of the challenges facing New Hampshire families, students, taxpayers, and small businesses.
My promise is simple: to listen, lead with integrity, and work every day to be a strong voice for Sandown.
Where I Stand Fighting for Sandown's Priorities Education Reform I'm running on a five-point platform for Education Reform: expanding school choice with tax breaks for homeschooling families, updating our K-12 curriculum to prioritize practical life skills that prepare students for the future, and shifting funding away from administrative overhead so dollars go directly to student learning and classroom infrastructure.
Infrastructure Across Sandown and New Hampshire, roads and trees too close to powerlines knock out power for days on end.
My goal is to work with the DOT and pass bills ensuring potholes get fixed in a reasonable timeframe — never left for years.
Through legislation, we'll keep trees safely maintained and distanced from powerlines.
Voter Integrity I will ensure our laws keep our elections free, fair, and for citizens only.
Every legal vote must count — and every voter must be able to trust the outcome.
Lower Taxes I will never vote for new taxes and I support lowering property taxes to make New Hampshire Affordable Again.
Hardworking Sandown families deserve to keep more of what they earn.
My Roots A New Generation, A Long-Term Commitment Fresh Ideas, New Energy From the Classroom to Concord As a recent UNH graduate and proud Timberlane alum, I bring fresh ideas, new energy, and a genuine long-term stake in the future of our state.
The decisions made in Concord today will shape New Hampshire for my generation — and I'm ready to fight for it.
