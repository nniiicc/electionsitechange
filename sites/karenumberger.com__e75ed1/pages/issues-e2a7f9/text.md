CLEAN WATER It is essential that everyone in the state has confidence in their drinking water.
There has been a focus on eliminating PFAS and the state has made great progress.
A recent settlement with 3M and Dupont will bring additional dollars to the state to clean up drinking water.
There is also an ongoing testing program that is looking for contaminants in private wells.
I cannot stress enough the need for homeowners with private wells to test for contaminants.
CLEAN LAKES AND RIVERS Everyone living near a lake or river has a responsibility to keep chemicals from polluting nearby lakes and rivers.
Cyanobacteria blooms, e coli, runoff and pollution are mostly manmade problems.
We can fix this by being extremely careful about how we treat our environment.
LOCAL CONTROL OF LAND USE Our towns and cities are fully capable of managing the day-to-day issues facing them, whether it is planning, zoning, roads and bridges, financial matters and a myriad of other issues facing each town and city.
Concord needs to stay out of micromanaging!
Concord does need to lend financial support when appropriate and expertise when asked.
AFFORDABLE HOUSING There is no question the housing stock in New Hampshire is woefully inadequate.
Conway has several apartments either open for rent or will be soon.
How affordable they are is up to the individual renter.
We must continue to work with the MWV Housing Coalition to continue to promote affordable housing.
BALANCED BUDGET It is always difficult to balance the State budget.
There are often unmet needs throughout the State Government.
No agency or program receives all the money they request.
It is truly a balancing act to ensure the needs of every agency are met.
Health and Human Services and Education are most often in the news, but budget writers also must focus on Corrections, Dept of Information Technology, Human Rights Commission, Veteran’s Home and a myriad of other agencies, Commissions and Boards.
The House and Senate must prioritize state spending based on projected income and make sure the State lives within its means, just as every household must do.
SCHOOL FUNDING Public education is the backbone of our society and must be appropriately funded.
Public education includes traditional public schools, Charter Schools and the Virtual Learning Academy.
Each type of school has a formula for determining the amount of State dollars received.
For example, there are additional dollars for special education students, children qualifying for free and reduced lunch along with a variety of other items.
I will always support increased funding for public education.
In addition to public education students are also home schooled and the State offers Education Freedom Accounts.
Each child receives a set amount of dollars to be used for their education.
The base aid this school year is $4,265.
Additional dollars are provided if a student qualifies for free and reduced lunch, is a special education student as well as an English language learner.
Conway currently has 28 students enrolled in the EFA program.
NO NEW TAXES I will NEVER support a sales or income tax!
It is extremely important that we never lose the New Hampshire Advantage.
I have taken the CNHT Anti-Broadbased Taxpayer Pledge ABORTION I fully support the current state law which allows for abortions up to twenty-four weeks.
