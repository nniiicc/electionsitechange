ASSEMBLY DISTRICT 149
Assembly District 149 includes the Buffalo neighborhoods of Black Rock/Riverside, the Elmwood Village, Allentown, and the Upper and Lower West Side.
It also stretches down through Lackawanna and includes the entire town of Hamburg.
This district has strong working-class roots, where industrial and service union members have fought for dignified and secure lives for themselves and their families.
We know the fight for an even brighter future can only be won together.
Adam is running to unite working people in our district for a healthy, housed, and affordable Western New York.
