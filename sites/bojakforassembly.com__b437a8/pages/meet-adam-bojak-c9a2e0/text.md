In this November's general election, he's your Democratic nominee for NY State Assembly.
Adam grew up in a working-class household in East Aurora.
The son of a postal worker and a public school employee, his family experienced the same budget strain we all know today.
He also saw how union jobs can protect and provide for workers and their families alike.
After graduating from East Aurora High School, Adam earned his bachelor’s degree at Rochester Institute of Technology, and his law degree at SUNY Buffalo.
Today, Adam provides free legal services and advice to Western New Yorkers facing housing problems, including representing tenants facing legal disputes with their landlords.
He also currently serves families across the region as assigned counsel in Family Court.
Adam’s approach to his professional practice has been meaningfully shaped by his involvement in the Buffalo Democratic Socialists of America, where he has been an active member and leader since 2017.
Throughout his career and in his community, Adam has been a proven champion of everyday Western New Yorkers.
In his run to be your next State Assemblymember, he remains dedicated to building a better New York for you and your family, and will be steadfast in the fight for: Universal healthcare Universal childcare Tenant and small homeowner protections Workers’ rights A strong moratorium on AI data centers Taxing the rich Learn more about what Adam stands for by reading our platform.
