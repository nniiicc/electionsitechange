Meet Patrick Belmont
Patrick Belmont is a water scientist, energy expert, university educator, community advocate, and dad running to represent Utah House District 3, including Logan, River Heights, and Richmond.
Throughout his 20+ year professional career he has been known for asking the right questions to get to the heart of matters, listening and doing the hard work needed to understand all sides of complex issues, and bringing people together around common values.
As an independent, Patrick will bring serious, accountable leadership to the Utah Legislature.
