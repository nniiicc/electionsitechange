Strength Through Experience
Kevin Onstott
I’m Kevin Onstott, a proud life long resident of Iberia, Missouri, and I’m running to represent the 124th District in the Missouri House of Representatives.
As a 6th generation farmer I understand the importance of agriculture as the leading industry in Missouri, as well as the generational legacy it creates.
My 32 year career with MoDOT, The Missouri Veterans Commission, and The Office of Administration has allowed me the unique perspective of having worked with all 17 departments of state government as well as the Missouri House and Senate budget committees, and provided me an understanding that my opponents do not have.
As a small business owner I understand the challenges that come with daily operations in mid Missouri.
Missourians are a hard working group of people, and I recognize their ambition and will always stand along side of them to fight for an equal opportunity.
I have given back to my community for years as a medic, fire captain, and a state fire inspector.
Unlike other candidates I not only support our fire, EMS, and law enforcement, I have served along side them in Miller and Pulaski county for 26 years.
I've dedicated 20 years on the Iberia Fire Board, Miller County Ambulance Board, Iberia FFA Advisory Committee, Gascosage Advisory Committee, President of the Iberia Cemetery Committee, Coached Sports, and taught my sons to lead in the same way.
I have a proven history of giving back to our communities, and this campaign is another example of that.
Our founding fathers intended for business men to take their proven business experience and apply it to their government, not to make government their business experiment.
I know what it takes to fix issues in our communities and with your help I will advocate for and look for solutions to things that matter right here at home.
