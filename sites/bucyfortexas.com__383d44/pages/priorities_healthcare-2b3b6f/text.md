I’m fighting for a Texas where everyone has access to healthcare they need.
The Texas Legislature could make the decision overnight to expand Medicaid and provide immediate relief to 1 in 4 Texans who are uninsured.
This would save lives while bringing $110 billion of our tax dollars back into Texas and lowering health insurance costs.
Let’s bring our money home to Texas to focus on measures that:
- Reduce maternal mortality rates and increase cultural competency
- Prioritize mental healthcare through therapy for children who are neuro-atypical and increased availability for home and community-based care for adults with disabilities
- Focus on harm reduction policies and addressing growing addiction rates through intervention and treatment
