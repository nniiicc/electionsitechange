Our top priority is to support our constituents, especially on matters related to navigating the state government.
This includes but is not limited to licensing, regulation, interacting with the criminal justice system, and health and human services.
If you have a question or concern related to working with a state agency, please contact us and we will help you get answers in a timely manner.
In addition to constituent casework, we can also assist you with:
- Arranging a private Capitol tour or tour of the Capitol Dome (some restrictions apply)
- Providing Texas flags that have been flown over the Capitol
- Obtaining a resolution for occasions such as commendations or memorials
Email us at district136.bucy@house.texas.gov or call us at 512-463-0696

Artist of the Month
Each month at our Capitol Office we are proud to display and feature work from local artists.
Artists are responsible for load in, installation, and removal at the end of the display period.
Art must be able to be hung on the wall.
For artists who are underage, we respectfully request a parent or guardian apply on their behalf.
Community Advisory Board
The Community Advisory Boards (CABs) for Texas House District 136 consist of a diverse group of community leaders who help tailor Representative Bucy’s Legislative Agenda to ensure he meets constituent needs.
The CABs include the following policy areas: Education, Health Care, City & County Affairs, and Criminal Justice & Social Equity.
