I am honored to have been invited to participate in Political Advocacy Day, hosted by the National Association of Real Estate Brokers (N.A.R.E.B.), an organization committed to advancing civic awareness and engagement within minority communities.
This event is part of a broader initiative to foster meaningful dialogue around participation, representation, and the real impact of the political process.
The discussion will be moderated by Maxine Teele, who brings a thoughtful and structured approach to guiding conversations that matter.
I look forward to contributing to a dialogue that prioritizes clarity, accessibility, and real-world understanding.
Political Advocacy Day is grounded in a clear mission: to help individuals better understand how the political system works and where they fit within it.
Many people feel disconnected or uncertain about how to engage.
This forum is designed to bridge that gap by presenting civic participation in a way that is practical, relatable, and achievable.
This event is intentionally nonpartisan and is not structured as a debate.
Instead, it serves as a shared learning space focused on education, reflection, and community impact.
The discussion will explore how local and midterm elections shape everyday life and how individuals can take meaningful steps to become more involved in their communities.
Through clear examples and lived experiences, the panel will aim to simplify complex ideas and provide attendees with greater confidence in their ability to participate.
The goal is not only to inform, but to empower.
