As a former recipient of their Humanitarian Award, for my work in helping the Veteran homeless population, it is always an honor to attend.
As the CEO of Devil Dog USA I have collaborated with local and national organizations to help our city agencies and government officials.
From my service in the military to my work in both the public and private sectors, this experience has given me many abilities that are needed in the council seat.
One of those traits is my leadership, which is a crucial factor missing in our city council.
I believe having someone with this genuine characteristic will directly improve many the shortcomings that plague the 15th district.
Given the opportunity and my first-hand knowledge of the needs of our community, I have no doubt that with your support, the work we could accomplish would be incredible.
