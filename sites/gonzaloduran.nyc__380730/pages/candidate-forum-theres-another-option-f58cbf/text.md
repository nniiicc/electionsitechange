Their mission was clear: to strengthen conservative values, expand outreach, and build momentum for the movement in New York City.
At the heart of this effort was Curtis Sliwa, a true fighter for the people.
With his leadership, experience, and influence, he helped elevate candidates, amplify their voices, and push our cause forward.
His message of unity and cooperation resonated throughout the room, proving that with the right strategy and support, conservative candidates can and will break through in this city.
The discussions were insightful, strategic, and inspiring, equipping attendees with the tools to drive change.
The energy in the room was electric—a clear sign that conservatives in New York are more determined than ever to make an impact.
This was more than just an event; it was a defining moment for our movement.
The momentum is growing, and today was proof that the fight for a better, more balanced New York City is far from over.
Thank you to everyone who attended, contributed, and helped make this a success.
The work continues, and together, we will shape the future.
