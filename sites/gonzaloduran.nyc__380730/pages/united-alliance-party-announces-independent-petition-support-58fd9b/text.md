Just Us—United As the 2026 New York State election cycle approaches, the United Alliance Party is prepared to support candidates navigating the Independent Petition process beginning April 14.
With a focus on access, structure, and efficiency, the organization is committed to helping both first time and experienced candidates secure their place on the ballot.
The United Alliance Party operates as an Independent Petition organization dedicated to simplifying what is often a complex and technical process.
Candidates seeking legislative office must meet the requirements set forth by the New York State Board of Elections.
Beyond those requirements, the United Alliance Party provides guidance and administrative support to ensure petitions are properly prepared and submitted.
The organization assists candidates by supplying official petition forms and facilitating the submission process once completed.
This support is offered at no cost.
Candidates are only responsible for the direct expenses associated with printing and paperwork.
By removing unnecessary financial barriers, the United Alliance Party aims to create a more accessible path for individuals who are serious about public service.
In addition to petitioning support, the organization offers access to a broader network of campaign services through affiliated partners.
These optional resources are available to candidates who may require additional support as they build and expand their campaigns.
The mission of the United Alliance Party is rooted in one core principle: expanding access to the ballot while maintaining compliance with all legal and administrative requirements.
In doing so, the organization seeks to empower candidates who are ready to step forward and represent their communities.
Individuals interested in running for a legislative position in the 2026 election cycle are encouraged to make contact directly.
For more information or to begin the process, please email gonzaloduranyc@gmail.com or submit an inquiry through the official contact form .
