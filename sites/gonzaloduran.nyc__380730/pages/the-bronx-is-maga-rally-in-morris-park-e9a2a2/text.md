The event saw a strong turnout, with close to 200 people gathered at any given moment at this four hour event, all united by their commitment to Make America Great Again (MAGA) and their unwavering support for presidential candidate Donald J.
Trump.
The rally’s purpose was clear: to amplify the voices of Conservatives in the Bronx.
We want to be seen, heard, and represented.
Today’s gathering is a testament to the strength of our movement and the desire of Bronx Conservatives to help shape the future of our city, state, and nation.
