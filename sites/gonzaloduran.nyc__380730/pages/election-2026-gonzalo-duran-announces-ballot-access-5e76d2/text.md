At the same time, I am actively building an Independent line under the United Alliance Party , expanding the movement and creating additional pathways for voter engagement and representation.
With ballot access secured, my campaign now enters a new phase.
We are shifting from the mindset of simply getting on the ballot to a focused, disciplined approach centered on Election Day.
This transition represents not just progress, but purpose.
It is about building momentum, strengthening our message, and preparing to deliver meaningful results for the communities we serve.
My platform remains grounded in the principles I have consistently advocated through my public speeches, writings, and community work.
However, this next stage will bring greater clarity and definition.
We will refine our priorities, expand our policy positions, and continue to present a vision that is both practical and forward thinking.
Gonzalo Duran for New York Congressional District 15 Beyond my own campaign, I remain committed to supporting fellow candidates who share similar values and a commitment to public service.
Coalition building is essential, and together we can create a broader, more unified movement that speaks to the needs of everyday people.
For those asking how to get involved, there are several meaningful ways to contribute.
Donations, volunteer efforts, and resource support are all critical to building a strong campaign.
Many may not realize that providing resources can be documented as contributions, offering another way to participate in this effort.
For example, providing snacks for our volunteers, printing campaign flyers, and supporting outreach efforts all play an important role.
I am focused primarily on the Bronx in New York’s 15th Congressional District , but I am ready and willing to engage communities across New York and beyond as opportunities arise.
Election Day is November 3, 2026.
My campaign finance link will be available by the end of this week.
I encourage you to reach out through my contact form with any questions, ideas, or opportunities to collaborate.
