Ways to Get Involved
Tabling at a Community Event
Volunteers will support the campaign by tabling at local events or gatherings.
They will help set up materials, dispatch information about Patrick, and garner community responses!
Knocking On Doors
Our volunteers will be paired up and dispatched to a neighborhood in AD-26 to canvass homes, talk to registered voters, and gather feedback for the campaign.
Making Calls/Texts
Volunteers will call registered voters, let them know about Patrick, and remind them of upcoming election events!
