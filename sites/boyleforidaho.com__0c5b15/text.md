Donate Volunteer Follow on Facebook About Judy Upholding the Constitution Protecting the Sanctity of Life, Liberty & Property A strong voice for Rural Education Judy is an unapologetic conservative whose principles are based on the Bible & the Constitution which her votes reflect.
Her expertise is in natural resources, agriculture, and individual rights.
She has written & sponsored many of Idaho’s 2nd Amendment laws.
Judy believes the 2nd Amendment is the enabling language for all our Bill of Rights & the Constitution.
Judy has been a 4-H leader, helped start Idaho’s Ronald McDonald House, was a NICU support parent, March of Dimes volunteer, Congressman Helen Chenoweth’s director of natural resources, legislative director for the Idaho Farm Bureau, and served legislative district 9 since 2009.
Judy lives on her farm outside of Midvale where she has given unconditional love to numerous children besides her own son, daughter, and two grandsons.
