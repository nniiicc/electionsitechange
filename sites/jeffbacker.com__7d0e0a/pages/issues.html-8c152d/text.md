ON THE ISSUES
What I'm Fighting For:
Supporting Our First Responders
Strengthening the safety of communities by ensuring law enforcement and first responders have the necessary equipment and resources they need to serve and protect.
Eliminating Wasteful Spending
Promoting fiscal responsibility by reducing unnecessary government spending and lowering taxes to make living more affordable for citizens, emphasizing that government should also adhere to a conservative budget.
Ensuring Quality Education
Advocating for the return of control over educational policies and decisions to local communities and parents, rather than centralized state bureaucracy.
Supporting Agriculture
Supporting agricultural advancements and family farms through favorable policies, recognizing the essential role of agriculture in the economy and community sustainability.
Protecting Our Rights
Defending constitutional rights including freedom of religion, speech, the right to bear arms, and the sanctity of life at all stages.
Our Community

Your input is invaluable, and together, we can tackle any challenges our community faces.
