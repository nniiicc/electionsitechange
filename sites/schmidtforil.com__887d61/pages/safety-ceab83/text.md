Schmidt Issues Fighting for the 114th Growing the Economy Supporting Illinois Veterans Protecting Our Communities Volunteer Contact Contribute Facebook PROTECTING OUR COMMUNITIES There is no greater priority for Representative Schmidt than the safety, security, and prosperity of his constituents.
That’s why he’s a proud supporter of local law enforcement and a staunch advocate for crime prevention.
Recognizing the financial challenges many communities face in implementing public safety measures, he co-sponsored legislation ( HB1542 ) to establish a grant fund that helps local law enforcement agencies afford body cameras.
These tools are essential for accountability and transparency, and Schmidt is ensuring our first responders have access to the resources they need.
To protect vulnerable seniors, he also championed HB2100 , which enhances penalties for elder abuse.
This vital legislation sends a clear message that mistreating older adults will not be tolerated in Illinois and helps safeguard our parents and grandparents.
Furthermore, recognizing the widespread economic harm caused by the pandemic, Rep.
Schmidt introduced legislation ( HB3304 ) to extend the statute of limitations for prosecuting individuals who committed COVID-19-related fraud.
This ensures that justice is served for those who exploited government resources intended to help struggling families.
Representative Schmidt’s unwavering focus on community safety demonstrates his dedication to protecting every resident of the 114th District.
