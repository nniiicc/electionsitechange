GROWING THE ECONOMY
Representative Schmidt is laser-focused on boosting economic growth by creating jobs and attracting new businesses to the 114th District.
His leadership is paving the way for opportunity and revitalization in the Metro East region.
One of his key initiatives is the creation of the Metro East Development Authority (HB1216), which will be a driving force in bringing new businesses and industries to the area.
By proactively recruiting companies to invest in the region, Schmidt is positioning the district as a hub of economic activity and ensuring residents have access to good-paying jobs.
He also understands that affordable housing is the backbone of a thriving community.
That’s why he introduced HB1218, which offers a property tax abatement for properties where affordable housing has been built or is planned.
This measure not only makes housing more accessible but also encourages developers to invest, ensuring families in the district have secure and affordable places to live.
Additionally, Schmidt worked to extend the Rivers Edge Tax Credit (HB4106), an incentive that drives investment and development in the Metro East.
By fostering public-private partnerships and spurring projects in underserved areas, this initiative is helping breathe new life into communities that have long been overlooked.
Through bold, forward-thinking initiatives like these, Schmidt is delivering real, tangible results that strengthen the economy and improve quality of life for the people of the district.
