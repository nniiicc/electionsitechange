I will push to cut red tape for projects that include affordable units, convert vacant motels and offices into housing, and expand protections that keep renters and small landlords stable .
The reality is simple: families across our Desert-Mountain region are paying more than they can afford just to keep a roof overhead.
More than half of renters are “rent-burdened,” with many spending over 30% of their income on rent .

Local cities are planning thousands of new units, but they need state support to meet the challenge.
My approach combines new construction, adaptive reuse, and tenant protection , while ensuring growth is tied to water, roads, and public safety so our communities can keep pace.
