Our District
California’s 34th Assembly District is where the high desert meets possibility.
Stretching across Los Angeles, San Bernardino, and Kern Counties, AD 34 brings together communities linked by shared values, hard work, and a vision for a better future.
From the Antelope Valley to the Victor Valley, this district is defined by both its natural beauty and strategic importance.
Palmdale and Lancaster anchor the south, Victorville, Hesperia, and Apple Valley form a vibrant corridor along the I-15, and the northern reaches touch the eastern edges of Kern County.
Together, these communities are home to more than half a million people who represent the full diversity and strength of California.
Our district is a crossroads geographically, economically, and culturally.
Aerospace and defense industries coexist with small businesses and logistics hubs.
Families who’ve lived here for generations live side by side with new residents who chose this region for its promise and opportunity.
Our schools, workforce, and community organizations reflect a dynamic blend of cultures, making AD 34 a place where the California story continues to unfold.
Politically, AD 34 is a district that can’t be taken for granted.
With Republicans, Democrats, and No Party Preference voters all making up significant portions of the electorate, campaigns here aren’t won by talking past each other.
They’re won by showing up, listening, and delivering real results.
This is a district that rewards authenticity and vision.
The challenges we face are real: tackling the cost of housing, improving transportation and infrastructure, supporting local industries, and ensuring families have access to quality education and healthcare.
But the potential is even greater.
AD 34 has the talent, grit, and heart to lead California forward, and together, we can make that happen.
I’m running because this is home.
I’ve worked closely alongside families and small businesses here, and I know both the struggles we face and the strength we carry.
Our district deserves a representative who shows up, listens, and fights for us.
Not just when it’s convenient, but every single day.
If you share that belief, if you’re ready for leadership rooted in our community, then I’d be proud to earn your vote.
