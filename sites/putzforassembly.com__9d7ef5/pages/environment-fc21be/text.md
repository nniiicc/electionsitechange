Illegal waste piles rot in empty lots.
Industrial pollution creeps into communities.

Environmental justice is not an abstract issue here.
It’s personal.
Apple Valley residents live near tire-burning cement kilns.
Desert towns deal with toxic dumping.

We deserve clean air, clean water, and a future that is sustainable and just.
