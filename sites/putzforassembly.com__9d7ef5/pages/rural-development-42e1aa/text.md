Rural communities are the heart of this district, but they’ve been treated like an afterthought for too long.

Too often, economic development bypasses locals entirely, shipping jobs elsewhere.
I’ll work to change that.
Expanding broadband to every home and business is step one.
It’s a necessity, not a luxury.
I’ll push for investments in water infrastructure, road repairs, and clean energy development that benefits locals.
