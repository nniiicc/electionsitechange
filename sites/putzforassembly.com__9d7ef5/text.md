Meet Randy Learn About Randy Randall Putz has spent over 15 years serving Big Bear Lake and surrounding communities as a City Councilmember, Mayor, and regional leader.
He has championed local priorities as Chairman of the Mountain Area Regional Transit Authority and Big Bear Fire Authority, and represented District 11 cities on the Southern California Association of Governments, earning statewide recognition for his leadership.
A small business owner, education advocate, and co-founder of the Big Bear Valley Trails Foundation, Randall has deep roots in the community where he raised his family.
Known for bringing people together, Randall has built a record of representing all voices, regardless of party or ideology , and finding solutions that work for everyone.
In the State Assembly, he will bring practical leadership to expand educational opportunities, strengthen local economies, and protect the environment to ensure a future where our communities thrive.
