Issues
Reducing Property Taxes:
Colton has called to deliver a permanent property tax cut to Idahoans by lowering the maximum rate of annual tax increase set by the legislature and requiring efficiency with public funds from municipalities and taxing districts.
Currently property owners are paying painfully high sums just to keep a roof over their head and high taxes also harm tenants, increasing the rate of rent.
With the rising cost of rent young people are being priced out of home ownership.
Now is the time to identify and eliminate government waste.
Upholding His Oath:
As a veteran, Colton has taken an oath to uphold the Constitution.
As your next Legislator he will stand as a staunch defender of your Constitutional rights and liberties.
Government intrusion in our businesses and daily lives is unacceptable.
Colton is also fervently committed to protecting the 2nd amendment right to keep an bear arms.
If elected he will oppose any current or future restrictions on your rights.
Protecting Idaho Energy and Water:
Colton will work to preserve Idaho’s hydroelectric energy sector from those seeking to tear down the snake river dams.
Idaho is leading the nation in clean energy production, with 60% of our power coming from hydroelectric, sadly a number of extremists, including the Oregon state government, are engaged in a legal battle to tear down our dams to accommodate salmon spawning.
If the radicals are successful, such a ruling will cost jobs, drain water reserves, drive Idaho toward foreign fossil fuels dependence, and potentially double the electric bills for our homes.
Colton opposes these efforts and will fight to preserve Idaho’s energy and water resources!
