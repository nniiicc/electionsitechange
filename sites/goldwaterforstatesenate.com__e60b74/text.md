WHAT I'M FIGHTING FOR economic prosperity Things need to be not just affordable, but easily achievable!
I want to lower costs and put more money in your pocket.
It should not hurt to fill up your gas tank, pay the electric bill, or be a luxury to add hamburger to the grocery cart.
People are struggling to pay their rent or a mortgage, let alone afford Christmas gifts or a family vacation.
I don't want my family to struggle, and I don’t want yours to either.
I will work hard to make life more affordable and more prosperous for my district and all Michiganders.
DATA CENTER Freeze There are far too many concerns about data centers, their impact on our communities, and the way that big tech corporations and politicians cut deals and sell us out.
My opponent actually wrote the bill to give $90 million in taxpayer-funded breaks to data centers, and he voted to take away local control of energy zoning.
Our bills are already too high, and our lakes and waters are too important to let politicians tell us how it's going to be.That's why I support ordering a one-year moratorium on all data center projects in Michigan, and restoring local control over how our communities are run!
HEALTH INSURANCE Health insurance costs are skyrocketing , and many families are struggling to keep up.
Politicians have been promising to lower health care costs for years but only seem to make things worse.
By fighting for more pricing transparency and more health care options, we can help dramatically lower costs while ensuring people have access to quality health care . seniors Seniors have seen their Social Security, retirement, savings, and years of hard work crushed by inflation and rising costs .
It's time to change that, and I will take action!
Seniors have paid their dues, and what is happening to them will one day happen to us.
They shouldn't have to barely get by or pinch every penny.
Eliminating taxes for seniors will put more money in their pockets and boost the economy.
These are the golden years for a reason, and I will fight to make sure that they have them.
Public Safety I am committed to making public safety a top priority.
I proudly back the blue and support our dedicated law enforcement officers who put their lives on the line every day to protect our families and communities.
With violent crime and local terrorist attacks on the rise , we must ensure officers have the latest resources and training they need.
We can make our communities safer , reduce crime, and build trust between police and residents all across Michigan. strong & Safe schools I have six children in the Anchor Bay school system, from high school to kindergarten , and I understand the concerns parents have about their children's well-being.
That's why I want to make sure teachers have the resources they need to provide the best education possible, while also creating greater transparency so parents know what their children are being taught and can stay involved in their education.
With parents’ involvement, we can get back to teaching the basics, raise standards, and put our childrens' education first!
Skilled Trades Our schools and our government need to do a better job of promoting the incredible opportunities that skilled and vocational trades offer.
For every three skilled trades workers who leave the workforce, only one enters it.
As a state we need to do more to provide that education and hands-on training for the next generation.
Lake ST.
CLAIR In 2025, millions of gallons of raw sewage filled with toilet paper and solid waste were drained into our beautiful lake , causing foul odors and health warnings.
Steps have been taken over the years to fix this serious issue, but progress has been too slow.
I will seek funding to fast-track permanent solutions and make our lake safe for everyone.
Lake St.
Clair has untapped economic potential to attract tourism and business from around the world.
I will pursue the funding needed to improve our shoreline, boat access sites, marinas and beaches.
Together, we can make it happen.
PROTECTING WOMEN & GIRLS As a father of two amazing girls , the thought of them being forced to compete against biological males or share locker rooms, showers, or restrooms with them is deeply concerning.
I will support commonsense policies to protect opportunities for women and girls in sports and stop this from happening in our great state.
2ND AMENDMENT I strongly support the Second Amendment and believe that the right to bear arms shall not be infringed.
I have owned firearms for most of my life.
I’m an avid hunter and a responsible gun owner.
Our right to bear arms is absolutely necessary to preserve a free America.
RIGHT TO LIFE I am unapologetically pro-life , and will absolutely support the rights of the unborn.
I believe this is a right given by our Creator.
Unfortunately, Michigan's Constitution allows for abortion.
I believe we should encourage a culture of life, support those in need.
As your state Senator, I’ll work to provide the proper resources to mothers and fathers in difficult situations, saves the lives of the unborn, and work to encourage more Michiganders to be born and live out their lives to the fullest.
