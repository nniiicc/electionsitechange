top of page Re-Elect Luis Terrazas State Representative Home | About | Get Involved | Contact | Give Me Five DONATE ABOUT ME Hi, I'm Luis WHERE I COME FROM Luis Terrazas is a lifelong resident of Grant County.
He opened his first business in 1999 at 27 years of age and continues to be a leader in the local business community from Las Cruces to Silver City.
Luis is a political outsider who deeply values the region’s natural beauty, as well as its prosperous mining industry on which hard-working families have relied for decades.
He also believes in strengthening and building unity among members of his community by listening to their needs and following through on his promises.
Luis is active in the community and serves on the Foundation Board for Western New Mexico University.
He has also served on the advisory board for AmBank, Silver City-Grant County Chamber of Commerce, and Deming-Luna Chamber of Commerce.
Luis’s wife, Mandee works alongside him in the funeral industry, and together they have five children.
