Affordable Housing I want to reduce the cost of housing, especially for first time home buyers.
I believe this can be done by streamlining and simplifying the permitting and regulatory framework.
I also believe that some state programs, such as PIDs, have created severe problems for Utah tax payers and given money to real estate developers.
The goal is to reduce the cost of living, not line the pockets of a few.
The Proposed Data Center The proposed data center in Box Elder County is a problem both economically and environmentally.
KSL News has some excellent interviews that show some of these problems.
I oppose the data center for not only these economic and environmental reasons, but also because Utahns do not want it.
The wide-spread public opposition could not be more clear.
I am listening to you!
The Great Salt Lake The Great Salt Lake serves a critical role in many Utah industries and recreations.
For Utahns living in Northern Utah, the Great Salt Lake plays a major role in providing conditions that sustain life, including human life.
Finding ways to preserve and restore the Great Salt Lake while also providing water to agriculture and other needs is a high priority.
Elections The Constitution of the United States declares that elections are to be administered by the States.
I want to ensure that Utah maintains this right and does so in ways that benefits Utahns.
