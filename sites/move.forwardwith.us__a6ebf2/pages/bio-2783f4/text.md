James Rich for Utah House District 6 Home Donate Issues About James People and Places Events Videos Contact Career James Rich is the president of Edward A.E.
Rich & Associates.
Starting from an entry level position, James learned business software programming from his father.
In 2008 James bought the company and took over as president.
James enjoys learning to solve problems and listening to customers.
Listening to people and building solutions based on their needs is the core of his professional life.
Education James is a graduate of the University of Utah where he studied jazz composition.
Wanting to broaden his studies, he also focused on Math and Physics.
James attended Olympus High School in Salt Lake City where he served as Student Body Vice President.
Volunteer Work James has volunteered to serve people in many different ways, from coaching youth tennis to religious service.
Currently he happily serves as a morning crossing guard at Promontory School.
