Request a Vote-By-Mail Ballot
Florida now requires voters to re-register for Vote-by-Mail every election cycle, even if you’ve voted by mail before.
Your old request may no longer be active, so make sure your ballot still comes straight to your door.
Why we recommend it: your ballot arrives at home, you can fill it in at your
own pace with time to research every race, and you are not depending on one particular day
being free.
It is the surest way to make sure your vote is counted.
