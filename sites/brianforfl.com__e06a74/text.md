Meet Brian Nathan
Brian Nathan is a proud Navy veteran and dedicated union leader who is fighting to lower costs for working families and seniors in the state senate.
Brian has built his life on what he calls “steel-toed integrity” – showing up, doing the work, and having his neighbor’s back.
Whether on a job site, in the union hall, or in the state senate, he’s bringing the same values and work ethic that have guided him throughout his life to fight for the hardworking people of Hillsborough County.
In the state senate he’s keeping his promises to lower the cost of living for working families.
He’s doing it by holding insurance and utility companies accountable, voting against scams that would raise taxes and fees for renters, seniors, small businesses, and consumers, and ensuring working families have a voice in Tallahassee that is just as strong as the wealthy and well connected do.
