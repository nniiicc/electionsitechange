PROUDLY ENDORSED BY: I’m Paul Kendrick and I’m running to serve as your state representative for Illinois' 12th District.
As a father of two girls who loves this community, I am driven to build a Chicago that is safer and more affordable.
I’ve dedicated my life to serving communities—from the Obama White House to the Lincoln Park High School Local School Council to creating economic opportunity with Rust Belt Rising .
But threats to our freedoms eight years ago motivated me to begin mobilizing hundreds of neighbors as a local Indivisible leader.
I realized that I could bring that fight and fresh energy to Springfield.
My work making policy at the highest level and bringing people together in our community, neighbor to neighbor, makes me the best advocate to get things done for you.
We need state leaders to safeguard our communities from the federal government.
We also need to push Chicago to nail the basics on safety, schools, and bringing down costs so neighbors can stay in our community.
Lakeview, Lincoln Park, Old Town, Gold Coast, and Near North, let’s build a brighter future together.
