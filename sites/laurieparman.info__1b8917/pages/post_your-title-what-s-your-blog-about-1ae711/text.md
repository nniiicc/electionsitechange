Judi Zapp Sep 19, 2025 2 min read Updated: Sep 26, 2025 Thank You for Your Prior Support in the Illinois House Race Dear Friends, Supporters, and Voters, As I reflect on the recent Illinois House of Representatives race, I am overwhelmed with gratitude for the incredible support, endorsements, and trust you placed in me throughout this journey.
Your belief in our shared vision where families are empowered, and children are safe, schools are thriving, and Springfield works for the people not against them.
This election is I'll continue the fight and help restore trust, defend freedom, and build a thriving future for generations to come.
Your support has been the heartbeat of this campaign, and I want to take a moment to express my heartfelt thanks.
To every voter who cast a ballot, thank you for exercising your democratic right and making your voice heard.
Your participation in this process is what drives change and shapes the future of our communities.
Whether you knocked on doors, made phone calls, shared our message on social media, or simply offered words of encouragement, your efforts made a tremendous difference.
To the organizations and individuals who endorsed our campaign, I am deeply honored by your trust and confidence.
Your support amplified our message and helped us connect with so many across our district.
Together, we championed the issues that matter most: better schools , stronger communities, and opportunities for all .
I am committed to carrying that momentum forward.
While the outcome of the election may shape the path ahead, it does not diminish the impact of what we built together.
Your dedication, passion, and belief in a brighter future for Illinois inspire me every day.
This campaign was about more than just one race; it was about building a community united by hope, hard work, and a shared commitment to progress.
As I look ahead, I am excited to announce that I will be running again for the Illinois House of Representatives in 2026.
I humbly ask for your continued support as we build on the foundation we’ve created together.
I also look forward to building new relationships.
With your help, we can keep pushing for the change our communities deserve and work toward a future that uplifts every voice in our district.
As we move forward, I remain dedicated to serving our district and advocating for the values we hold dear.
Your support has laid a foundation for continued progress, and I am excited to keep working alongside you to make Illinois a better place for everyone.
From the bottom of my heart, thank you for standing with me.
Together, we will continue to make a difference.
With gratitude, Laurie Parman Note: I'll keep you posted on my campaign, community developments, and provide insights on policy issues that affect you and your family.
