You can decide to vote in person.
BUT…
You MUST bring your ballot to your polling place, surrender the ballot to the judges, sign the required affidavit, and then you can vote using election equipment.
Otherwise, it’s back home to get it.

⭐ Vote by Mail (VBM) (2/5/26 to 3/17/26)
Important Postmark Changes May Keep Your Mail in Ballot From Counting
- Previously your ballot was postmarked on the day you put it in the box.
- New regulations say your ballot will now be postmarked when it
is processed, adding possibly 1-2 days delay, and possibly more.
- Your ballot must be postmarked by Election Day to count
- Don’t risk a late postmark.
