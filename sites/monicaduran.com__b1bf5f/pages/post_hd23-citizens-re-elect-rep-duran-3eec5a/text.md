Duran Rep.
Monica Duran Nov 13, 2024 1 min read We Did It!
I am so proud and honored to announce that I have been re-elected into Colorado’s State House of Representatives for HD 23!
I am so appreciative and thankful for every volunteer, voter, and grassroots supporter who made tonight possible.
I am also very grateful for all of my staff -especially my campaign manager Emily Funk- who helped make this day possible!
While I am extremely grateful to be elected to represent my community for two more years, I know we have a lot more work ahead of us.
We need to protect our environment, fight against the rising cost of healthcare, enhance public safety, and invest in the wellbeing of our communities.
Together, we can tackle these challenges and create a future where everyone has the opportunity to thrive.
Thank you for your continued trust and support—I’m ready to get to work!
