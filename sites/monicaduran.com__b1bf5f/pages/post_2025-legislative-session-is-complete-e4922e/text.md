Rep.
Monica Duran Jun 22 1 min read On May 7th, 2025, the Colorado General Assembly Sine Die to end the 2025 legislative session.
I want to give a big thanks to all the non-partisan staff, my colleagues, and various stakeholders for all their hard work and service to our great state.
I was proud to have 21 bills signed into law this session.
I am very proud to have passed meaningful legislation that will help our workers get their earned wages, keep our communities safer, allow our home care workers to receive proper training and wages, and continue protection of our animals and wild horses.
On top of my work, House Democrats were committed to passing meaningful legislation that will continue to improve the lives of Coloradans.
