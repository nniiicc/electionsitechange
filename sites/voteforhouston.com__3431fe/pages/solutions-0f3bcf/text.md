Immigration
Imagine a fair immigration system where Native-born citizens get the first shot at jobs and opportunities, a government that works for them.
A legal immigration system that is fair for everyone, no back door deals or secret amnesty that allows people to skip the line ahead of those who do it right.
Strong vetting to prevent and remove criminals that threaten our families.
Keeping Americans safe means strong borders and thorough vetting; preserving this American experiment our forefathers enjoyed as we pass these opportunities to our children and their children's children.
Healthcare
Healthcare
It’s time for a solution that empowers individuals and families to make the health choices they want to, not what they're forced to barely afford.
We have built tremendous capabilities and America has always been the innovative and driving force of healthy living in the world.
We’ll do it again by building a system that works for everyone.
We must trust the American people to do the right thing and move away from this top-down model where choices are dictated by a limited few.
Start locally first, through the tremendous work of our local institutions, which are already on the front lines, by empowering them and giving them the support they need to provide tailored services based on localized needs.
Give people thousands of choices instead of focusing on limiting the cost of the handful of choices you have today.
Give people the ability to select the providers based on their needs and individual performance.
