I’m a proud South Bay native, born and raised in the best place to live in Southern California.

What matters, what doesn't, and what's worth fighting for.

I spent fifteen years working at venues and arenas across the country that bring communities together through sports, music, and live entertainment.
These days, I'm raising three daughters in the South Bay with my wife - a stay at home mom and the real homemaker.
We homeschool our kids, and I have the pleasure of serving on the boards of our homeschool co-op in Redondo Beach and of our church in Old Torrance.
