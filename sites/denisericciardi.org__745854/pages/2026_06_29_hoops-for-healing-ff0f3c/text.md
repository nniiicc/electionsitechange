I’m honored to help spread the word about the inaugural Hoops for Healing Basketball Dinner on August 29 at the Double Tree Hotel in Manchester.
This special event will feature NBA standout Duncan Robinson and raise funds for programs supporting mental health, heart health, and physical wellness across New Hampshire through the Robinson Family Foundation.
Events like this show the power of community coming together to support important causes and make a difference in people’s lives.
To learn more, purchase tickets, or become a sponsor, visit HoopsForHealing.com
