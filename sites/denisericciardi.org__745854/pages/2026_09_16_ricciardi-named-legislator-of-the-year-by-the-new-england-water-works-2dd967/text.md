Senator Denise Ricciardi of Bedford was recently named Legislator of the Year by the New England Water Works Association (NEWWA) for championing public policy that ensures safe drinking water for communities across the state.
This prestigious award recognizes members of the U.S.
Congress or State Legislators from New England who have substantially assisted in the promotion of legislation or the advancement of policy that protects public health and the environment while ensuring safe drinking water.
During the 2025-2026 Biennium, Senator Ricciardi sponsored SB 443 a bipartisan bill relative to wastewater engineering.
It was signed into law by Governor Kelly Ayotte on May 08, 2026.
At the award ceremony in Manchester Senator Ricciardi said, “Every Granite Stater deserves access to safe, clean drinking water and appropriate waste and storm water treatment to protect the health of our families and communities.
SB-443 was a bipartisan effort that not only protects our water supply and environment but also enhances affordable housing.
I want to thank the bipartisan cosponsors of SB 443 which has also strengthened standards and oversight for wastewater treatment systems.”
She continued, “I’m deeply honored to receive this recognition from the NEWWA and grateful for their continued commitment to protecting clean water for New Hampshire families.”
Congratulations to Senator Ricciardi on being recognized as Legislator of the Year by the New England Water Works Association for her work on bipartisan legislation protecting public health and the environment while ensuring safe drinking water.
