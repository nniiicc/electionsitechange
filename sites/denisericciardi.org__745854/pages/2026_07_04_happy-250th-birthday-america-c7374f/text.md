Happy 250th Birthday, America! 🇺🇸
Today we celebrate 250 years of the United States of America—a nation founded on the enduring ideals of liberty, self-government, and opportunity.
As we honor this historic milestone, we remember the courage and sacrifice of those who fought for our independence, the generations who built and defended our country, and the countless Americans who continue to strengthen our communities through service, hard work, and civic engagement.
Here in New Hampshire, we are especially proud of our role in our nation’s story.
Our commitment to individual freedom, local control, and community responsibility reflects the values that have guided America since 1776.
May we celebrate this anniversary with gratitude for our past, pride in our nation, and hope for the generations who will shape America’s next 250 years.
Happy Independence Day, and God bless the United States of America!
