Ricciardi’s Work For Towns Deserves Our Support DONATE Select Page Bipartisan Notes Jun 15, 2026 One of the things I value most about serving in the New Hampshire Senate is the opportunity to work across the aisle to get things done for the people we represent.
A recent review of the most bipartisan bills of the 2026 legislative session is a great reminder that meaningful progress happens when we focus on solutions instead of politics.
I’m proud to have voted in favor of every bill included in the report, while sponsoring SB 526 and co-sponsoring SB 469, SB 665, HB 707, HB 1602, and HB 1651.
From supporting families, to improving healthcare affordability, strengthening our communities, and making government work better, these bills share a common goal: making life better for New Hampshire families and communities.
Thank you to my colleagues on both sides of the aisle who continue to prove that respectful collaboration can produce real results.
When we focus on what unites us rather than what divides us, Granite Staters are the ones who benefit.
