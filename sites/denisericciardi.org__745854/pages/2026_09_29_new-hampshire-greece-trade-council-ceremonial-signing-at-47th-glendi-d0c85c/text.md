The print counterpart to this story in The National Herald ran in this week’s issue, which was distributed to Greek homes and businesses.
The story features the New Hampshire-Greece Trade Council in a story on page 7 titled, “New Hampshire-Greece Trade Council Ceremonial Signing at 47th Glendi.”
MANCHESTER, NH – New Hampshire and members of the state’s Greek-American community gathered on September 18 at the 47th annual Glendi Festival at St.
George Greek Orthodox Cathedral to celebrate the establishment of the New Hampshire-Greece Trade Council, a new initiative designed to strengthen economic and commercial ties between New Hampshire and Greece.
