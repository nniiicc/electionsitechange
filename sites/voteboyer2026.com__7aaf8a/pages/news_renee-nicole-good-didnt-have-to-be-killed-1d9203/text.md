Arthur Boyer, EdD for Florida State Representative, District 82 (D) Date: January 8, 2026 My heart is with the family of Renee Nicole Good who died during a recently reported immigration enforcement operation in Minnesota.
I offer my sincere condolences to her loved ones, and I am praying for every family shaken by what has been reported.
I also want to be clear about what this moment demands: when there are serious allegations of misconduct or excessive force by any enforcement personnel, there must be a full, transparent investigation—and accountability must follow the facts.
No agency, no badge, and no individual is above the Constitution.
Here in Southwest Florida, we are experiencing active immigration enforcement operations.
That reality makes this issue immediate for our neighbors, our schools, our workplaces, and our faith communities.
What happened in Minnesota is a reminder that tragedy can happen anywhere—at any time—when oversight is weak and due process is not protected.
Families should never have to live with the fear that a routine day could end in irreversible loss.
Let me be equally clear: every person—regardless of immigration status—deserves dignity, due process, and equal protection under the law.
Public safety depends on trust, transparency, and lawful conduct.
When enforcement actions become unpredictable or abusive, it undermines community trust, traumatizes children, and destabilizes families and local economies.
This is a time for calm strength—not panic.
We must unite.
We must protect one another.
And we must respond by all democratic and judicial means: through the courts, lawful oversight, peaceful civic engagement, and the ballot box.
District 82 deserves leadership that defends constitutional rights, supports families, and replaces fear with fairness.
Dr.
