Arthur Boyer, EdD is an educator, community leader, advocate and problem-solver with deep roots in District 82 and a solid track record of bringing people and institutions together across differences.
He has spent his life preparing for the work of leadership: in the classroom, in community service, in nonprofit advocacy, in organizational coaching, and in direct engagement with the people and challenges of District 82 for over two decades.
Now, he is stepping forward to serve on a broader stage with the same values that have guided his life and career.
Above all, as the father of 21-year-old, Arthur Boyer H., he believes the people of District 82 deserve a representative who will work as hard for them as they work for their families and their future.
Boyer brings a leadership style shaped by service, education, organization, and accountability.
He knows how to build teams, communicate across differences, and stay focused on mission.
He knows how to connect vision with implementation.
He knows how to move from problem identification to practical strategy.
District 82 is looking for leadership they can trust.
They are looking for someone who understands their concerns, respects their labor, and takes their future seriously.
Arthur is running to stand with families, students, workers, seniors, and communities that deserve honest advocacy and strong representation.
Boyer will bring experience, preparation, and a servant-leadership mindset to the state legislature to serve and protect the best interests of District 82.
