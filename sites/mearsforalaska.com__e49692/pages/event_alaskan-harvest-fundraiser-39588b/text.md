Alaskan Harvest Fundraiser
September 14, 5:30 pm - 7:00 pm
Local food, local leaders, and a campaign built the same way.
Please join guest speakers Yarrow Silvers and Carl Jacobs at the home of Diane Hirshberg and Craig Kasemodel for an evening of delicious Alaska-grown offerings and our final push to re-elect Donna Mears to the Alaska State House.
Monday, September 14, from 5:30 to 7:00 PM
4218 Chelsea Way
On the table: We’ll have smoked salmon and salmon dip, a farmer’s cheese spread made with milk from a local dairy and fresh herbs, plus a table loaded with offerings pulled from gardens and simmered on stoves.
All pescatarian and gluten-free.
If you cannot attend but would like to support Donna, you can make a donation at this link: https://secure.actblue.com/donate/mears26
We are grateful to all of our co-hosts for going above and beyond to help make this event a success!
Forrest Dunbar, Bill Wielechowski, Ted Eischeid, Andrew Gray, Yarrow Silvers, Carl Jacobs, Margo Bellamy, Dora Wilson, Nelta Edwards, Cady Lister, Rachel Morse, Pete Peterson, Tyler Watson, and Justin Weaver.
We would love to see you there for a great meal and to keep Donna working hard for South Muldoon!
