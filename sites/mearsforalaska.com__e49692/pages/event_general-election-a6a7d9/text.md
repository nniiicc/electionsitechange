Vote in the General Election:
October 21st: Early Voting Begins
October 26th: Deadline to Apply for Absentee Ballot

Renew your vote by mail application online here.
For Absentee Ballots, Remember to:
• Sign and Add a Voter Identifier
• Get a Witness Signature
• Add a Stamp OR Return to an Early Voting Location (like the Division of Elections at 2525 Gambell St.)
• Return your Ballot Early
To sign up for an absentee ballot and the latest information, visit the Division of Elections website elections.Alaska.gov
