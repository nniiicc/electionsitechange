Contribute About News Endorsements Get Involved Contribute About News Get Involved Contribute Meet Justice Gerald Titus A Career Dedicated to the Rule of Law Justice Gerald Titus was appointed in November 2025 to fill an unexpired term and was sworn into office on December 12, 2025.
Justice Titus was born in Morgantown and raised in Spencer.
He received a bachelor's degree in politics and American history (2000) and a law degree (2003) from Washington and Lee University, where he was elected President of the Executive Committee of the Student Body, which administers the University's student-run Honor System.
As a practicing attorney, he gained experience in both civil and criminal law, including serving as an assistant U.S.
Attorney in the Southern District of West Virginia in the Violent Crimes Division (2007-2009).
He joined the Charleston firm of Spilman, Thomas & Battle, PLLC in 2009, where he maintained a broad-based legal practice until his judicial appointment.
He was named a West Virginia Super Lawyers Rising Star (2015 to 2018) and was recognized by The Best Lawyers in America for commercial litigation (2019-2025).
He served on the West Virginia State Bar Board of Governors (2020 to 2024), including leading an initiative to raise awareness of the needs of foster children and caregivers in West Virginia; served as the president of the Judge John A.
Field, Jr.
Chapter, American Inns of Court (2011-2012); served as president of the Washington and Lee Alumni Association Chapter Board; and was a member of the Defense Trial Counsel of West Virginia (2018-2025).
At the time of his appointment to the Supreme Court, he served as vice president of the Mission West Virginia board of directors, during which he demonstrated a passion for supporting the needs of families and children in foster and kinship care in West Virginia.
He and his wife, Meredith have three children and live in Charleston.
