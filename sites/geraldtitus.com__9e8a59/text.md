Why I'm Running
Serving the people of West Virginia as a Supreme Court Justice is the highest honor of my legal career.
With more than 22 years of legal experience in both civil and criminal law—including when I served as a United States Attorney—I bring a broad, balanced perspective to the Court.
I want to continue my public service because I am committed to giving back to our State which has given me so much.
I firmly believe judges do not make law or policy.
I believe in a fair and independent court system to help make our communities safer, reduce crime and the drug abuse crisis and ensure the law is enforced.
I believe in a court system that upholds the rule of law, strengthens public trust, recognizes individual rights, supports economic growth, and helps makeWest Virginia a place where families can raise children, businesses can grow, and communities can build a stronger future.
I am running to continue serving with integrity, humility, and respect for the Constitution and the rule of law.

Priorities
Upholding the Rule of Law with Fairness, Restraint, and Integrity
Judicial Philosophy
I believe in the rule of law, judicial restraint, and upholding the Constitutions of the United States and West Virginia.
The role of a Justice is not to make law or policy, but to apply the law as written—fairly, independently, and without bias.
A strong and faithful adherence to the law is essential to maintaining safe communities, protecting individual rights, and ensuring that justice is applied evenly to all.
Judicial Approach
Every case deserves careful consideration, respect for all parties, and a steady judicial temperament.
As West Virginia’s court of last resort, the Supreme Court must reach the correct result under the law.
I bring humility, hard work, and integrity to that responsibility every day, understanding that consistent and principled decisions help reinforce public safety, uphold accountability, and strengthen confidence in our justice system.
