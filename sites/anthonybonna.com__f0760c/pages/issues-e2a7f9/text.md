Lower Taxes and Reduce Costs Florida families are facing rising costs from insurance premiums, housing, utilities, and everyday expenses.
Anthony Bonna will fight to lower taxes and fees, reduce unnecessary regulations, eliminate wasteful government spending, and support policies that make Florida more affordable for families, seniors, and small businesses.
Secure Our Border A secure border is essential to public safety and national sovereignty.
Anthony Bonna supports strong immigration enforcement, securing the border, ending incentives for illegal immigration, and ensuring that criminal illegal aliens are removed from our communities.
Invest in Infrastructure As Florida continues to grow, investments in transportation, water, sewer, drainage, and other critical infrastructure are essential.
Anthony Bonna supports responsible infrastructure planning that improves quality of life, reduces congestion, and prepares communities for the future.
Protect Parental Rights & Health Freedom Parents, not government bureaucrats, should make decisions regarding their children's upbringing, education, and healthcare.
Anthony Bonna supports parental rights, informed consent, medical freedom, and policies that respect individual liberties while protecting families.
Fix Florida's Insurance Crisis Property insurance costs continue to strain family budgets across Florida.
Anthony Bonna supports reforms that increase competition, combat fraud, improve accountability, and create a more stable insurance market so homeowners can obtain affordable and reliable coverage.
Support Public Safety Public safety is government's most important responsibility.
Anthony Bonna will continue supporting law enforcement officers & first responders by ensuring they have the resources, training, and legal protections needed to keep our communities safe while holding criminals accountable.
Protect Our Communities Every community is different, and residents should have a voice in shaping their future.
Anthony Bonna supports local decision-making, property rights, investments in infrastructure, and the preservation of parks, green space, and natural resources.
He will work to protect the quality of life that makes the Treasure Coast a special place to live, work, and raise a family.
Defend the 2nd Amendment The right to keep and bear arms is a fundamental constitutional freedom.
Anthony Bonna supports the Second Amendment and will oppose efforts to infringe upon the rights of law-abiding citizens to protect themselves, their families, and their property.
Support Small Business & Economic Growth Small businesses are the backbone of Florida's economy.
Anthony Bonna will support policies that encourage entrepreneurship, reduce regulatory burdens, attract investment, and create good-paying jobs while keeping Florida one of the best states in the nation to start and grow a business.
Combat Human Trafficking Human trafficking is a modern form of slavery that victimizes some of society's most vulnerable individuals.
Anthony Bonna has supported anti-trafficking initiatives and will continue working to strengthen prevention, enforcement, victim services, and public awareness efforts throughout Florida.
Strengthen Education & Expand School Choice Parents should have the freedom to choose the educational environment that best meets their child's needs.
Anthony Bonna supports school choice, educational excellence, workforce development, and a renewed focus on core academic subjects.
He also believes we must attract and retain high-quality teachers through competitive pay and strong support for classroom success.
Stand For Life & Religious Liberty Anthony Bonna believes every human life has inherent dignity and value.
He supports policies that protect innocent human life, assist mothers and families, safeguard religious liberty, and defend the freedom of Floridians to live out their faith without government interference.
