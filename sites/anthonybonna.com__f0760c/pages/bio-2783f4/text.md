Donate Meet Anthony Anthony Bonna's commitment to public service is rooted in gratitude.
Growing up on the Treasure Coast, Anthony benefited from the investment of countless people and organizations that helped shape his future.
He earned scholarships through Rotary, the Chamber of Commerce, and the Education Foundation.
He was a Little Brother in Big Brothers Big Sisters, earned the rank of Eagle Scout, and was active in his church and community.
Those experiences taught Anthony the importance of service, personal responsibility, and giving back.
After graduating from Georgetown University with a degree in Finance, Anthony chose to return home to build a business, raise a family, and serve the community that invested in him.
Today, many of the organizations that once helped Anthony are the same organizations he now serves.
He is Immediate Past Chairman of the St.
Lucie County Education Foundation, serves on the Big Brothers Big Sisters Board of Directors, has been a Rotarian since 2015, serves on his parish council, and volunteers with Scouting and numerous community organizations.
Anthony's commitment to leadership also led him to complete Leadership St.
Lucie Class 34, Leadership Martin County Class 27, and Leadership Florida Connect Class 14, experiences that deepened his understanding of the people and communities he serves throughout the Treasure Coast and Florida.
His public service began with his appointment to the St.
Lucie County Commission by Governor Rick Scott and continued with his election to the Port St.
Lucie City Council.
He has also served as Chairman of the Treasure Coast Regional Planning Council, on the City's Affordable Housing Advisory Committee, and in numerous leadership roles throughout the community.
Anthony has been a strong advocate in the fight against human trafficking.
Under his leadership, Port St.
Lucie was recognized as a Trailblazer of the Year by the Florida Alliance to End Human Trafficking, and he was recognized by then-Attorney General Ashley Moody for efforts to raise awareness, train employees, and protect vulnerable individuals from exploitation.
In 2024, Anthony had the honor of serving as one of Florida's Presidential Electors, casting one of Florida's 30 Electoral College votes for President.
Anthony and his wife, Tara, live in Port St.
Lucie with their three children, Aubrey, Anthony Jr., and Adele.
Guided by faith, family, and gratitude for the opportunities he was given, Anthony continues to dedicate his life to giving back to the community that invested in him and fighting to keep Florida affordable, safe, and free.
