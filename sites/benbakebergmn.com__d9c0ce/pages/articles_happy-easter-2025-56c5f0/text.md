Happy Easter from Rep.
Bakeberg
Thursday, April 17, 2025
This week, instead of a legislative update, I want to take a moment to reflect on the significance of Good Friday and Easter.
Good Friday reminds us of the depth of sacrifice and the power of grace.
Easter Sunday brings the promise of renewal, hope, and new life.
Together, they are a powerful reminder that even in times of struggle or uncertainty, light and redemption are never far away.
As we spend time with loved ones and reflect on these holy days, I pray you find peace, joy, and encouragement in the hope that Easter brings.
Wishing you and your family a blessed Good Friday and a wonderful Easter.
– Rep.
Ben Bakeberg
