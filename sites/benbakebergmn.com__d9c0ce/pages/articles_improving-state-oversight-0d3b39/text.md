Minnesota House Republicans Unveil Whistleblower Portal to Improve State Oversight
St.
Paul – [03.25.25] – Yesterday, the House Fraud Prevention and State Agency Oversight Committee announced the launch of MNFraud.com, a secure website created for whistleblowers to confidentially report suspected fraud within state government.
“MNFraud.com gives government employees the option to safely report instances of fraud, waste, and abuse,” said Representative Bakeberg (R-Jordan).
“This is an important step in the right direction as we work to reduce the more than half a billion dollars lost to fraud.”
The site is designed specifically to collect tips from individuals who have firsthand knowledge of waste, fraud, or abuse in state agencies or publicly funded programs.
Information submitted through the site will be reviewed by the staff of the House Fraud Prevention and State Agency Oversight Committee.
The information, including the identities of whistleblowers, is secure and protected, similar to a secure-payment processing website.
For more information, or to submit a report, visit MNFraud.com.
