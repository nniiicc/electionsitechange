An advocate for our families
Our families deserve a fierce advocate who fights tirelessly for our shared interests each and every day on Beacon Hill.
I will continue to work hard for you because I know how hard it is to save up to buy a first house, to send kids to college, and one day retire.
As a proud father, I know that our communities’ schools are places where our children should expand their worlds and grow into the leaders of tomorrow.
But I also know that our communities must be places where we can all retire with dignity and be supported in our golden years.

An advocate for Framingham & Ashland
Our communities thrive when our families thrive.
Working with the larger MetroWest Legislative Delegation, I have prioritized projects and funding for Ashland and Framingham.
This is where I have lived for over sixteen years.
This is where my children attend school and participate in community activities.
This is home.

- Expedited flooding mitigation along Route 9 and the construction of a crosswalk across a dangerous part of Route 9
- Successfully prioritized funding increases for the MBTA and Regional Transit Authorities (RTAs)
- Joined Framingham and Ashland Legislative Delegations in advocating for state funding for new schools
- Advocated for and secured local earmarks, including funding for a mobile health clinic, accessible playgrounds, family-friendly park improvements, pedestrian and bike path improvements, the Warren Barn restoration project, clean-up of the former Cadilac Paint site, Framingham High auditorium renovation, accessible vans for Framingham and Ashland Public Schools, a local mobile health clinic, and a future outdoor patio at the Ashland Senior Center
