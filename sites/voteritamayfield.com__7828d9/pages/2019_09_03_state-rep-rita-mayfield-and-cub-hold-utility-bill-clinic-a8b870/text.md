Rita Mayfield and CUB Hold Utility-Bill Clinic news by staff When: Thursday, October 10, 2019, 6:00 – 8:00pm Where: Waukegan Public Library, Meeting Room A, 128 North County Street, Waukegan What: State Rep.
Rita Mayfield and the Citizens Utility Board (CUB) hold a free utility-bill clinic.
Participants should bring gas, electric, and phone bills for an expert, one-on-one analysis with a CUB representative.
Subjects covered at the clinic include: how to spot unnecessary charges on electric, natural gas, and phone bills; which energy efficiency programs and rebates can help; what to watch for when shopping around in the energy market; and options when it comes to phone, TV, and Internet.
For more information and to register call CUB at 312-263-4282 ext.
111, or email events@citizensutilityboard.org with the subject line ‘Waukegan 10/10’.
