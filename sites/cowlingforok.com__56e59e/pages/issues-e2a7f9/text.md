Lower the Cost of Living Randy will work to lower the rising cost of living by encouraging the development of more affordable housing, supporting policies that strengthen local businesses and farmers, and expanding access to fair, low-interest financial alternatives for working families.
Demand Transparency on Data Centers Randy will demand full transparency before taxpayer incentives are awarded to large data center projects.
Oklahomans deserve to know how these projects affect our communities, water supply, electric grid, and local economy before deals are approved—and no more secret NDAs when public dollars are involved.
Support Our Public Schools Randy will always stand up for our public schools by ensuring they are fully funded, increasing classroom funding, restoring and protecting critical programs like summer feeding, and making sure teachers and support staff receive the pay and resources they need to help every student succeed.
Healthcare for Every Family Randy believes every child and every family deserves access to affordable healthcare.
He will work to lower healthcare costs, expand access to primary and mental health care, protect rural healthcare providers, and ensure no child goes without the care they need because of cost.
Affordable Childcare Randy will make childcare more affordable by expanding state childcare assistance, increasing funding for childcare subsidies, and supporting working families so parents can stay in the workforce without sacrificing quality care for their children.
