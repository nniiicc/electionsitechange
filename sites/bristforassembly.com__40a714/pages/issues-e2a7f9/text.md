Ben supports a statewide pause, or moratorium, on data center construction until a proper regulatory framework is put in place that prevents data centers from offloading their costs and risks onto everyday people, and gives communities a real say in where they go and if they're allowed.
The companies driving new electricity and water demand should pay for that infrastructure, not ratepayers.
Ben supports requiring transparency on water and energy use and mandating that the energy powering these projects is clean.
