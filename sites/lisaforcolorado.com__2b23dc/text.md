Dear Neighbors, It has been an honor to serve as your State Representative for House District 24 and I am excited to continue to serve!
I have spent my life serving others, through the military as a police woman , my time a social worker for elderly and disabled people, and over a decade of emergency management and disaster response work .
You deserve a leader with ground-level experience and strong problem-solving skills to listen and represent everyone in our great community.
You deserve an honest person to advocate for all our needs , like healthcare, and public safety.
My core values have always been: work hard, serve others, and speak up.
I am excited to continue my service for all our neighbors, no matter their political beliefs or background.
I am here to serve you!
Eternally grateful, Lisa Feret Campaign: Lisa@LisaforColorado.com ‬ Official: Lisa.Feret.House@coleg.gov P.S.

CHIP IN: $30 $120 $60 $250 $450 OTHER LISA’S TOP PRIORITIES Small Business Lisa has demonstrated success in supporting small businesses throughout Colorado.
She believes that removing some burdensome regulations for small businesses can help them thrive.
That is why she has removed excess regulation and paperwork for providers to allow them more time to provide services.
Lisa also believes in accountability for big businesses–large employers should help support their employees and avoid abusing our social systems in the process.
Healthcare Lisa’s experience spans from direct case management to state-level policy making through nonprofit, government and medicaid programs.
She has worked in nursing homes, hospitals, and community-based settings to help people remain in their homes and receive proper healthcare.
She has piloted and expanded programs to transition people from institutions to communities.
Lisa hopes to bring that ground level perspective and experience in order to create more effective and efficient programs for our community.
Emergency Management Lisa’s unique background as a Military Police Woman as well as in the Emergency Management and humanitarian space spans from responding to floods and fires in Colorado to deploying internationally to war-torn Ukraine and working in refugee camps.
Lisa’s experience rooted from her military service has allowed her to better understand policing policies and challenges.
She has advocated for smart public safety programs and climate-forward solutions that are informed by her experience.
Building A Resilient Community Lisa promotes policies that support Coloradans’ safety.
During her first term, Lisa: Strengthened co-responders benefits and protections Required First responders to be paid for the care they give during emergencies Local communities and nonprofits can access free emergency protective equipment (PPE) from the state Tackled food waste by increasing expanding liability protections for businesses and increasing who can receive donations Ensuring Access to Healthcare Lisa utilizes her experience as a social worker to build better healthcare policy.
During her first term, Lisa: Introduced a law requiring large companies (like Walmart and Amazon) to pay their fair share for their excessive Medicaid use Passed bipartisan law protecting disabled veterans from claim sharks trying to take their disability money Expanded access to dry needling services Simplified therapist licensing hours to allow them to start work sooner Streamlining Government Systems Lisa works to simplify government processes for Coloradans.
During her first term, Lisa: Cut red tape around applications and recertifications for safety net programs Standardized dementia care information across all nursing home and assisted care facilities Updated and simplified policies around child protection reporting and investigating Lisa is committed to working hard and promoting a healthy, safe, vibrant community where we ALL can thrive!
