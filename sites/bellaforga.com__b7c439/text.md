Bella Bautista was born and raised in Cartersville as the daughter of small business owners.
She grew up watching her family work long hours, make sacrifices, and rely on faith and perseverance to build a better life.
Bella did not grow up wealthy or politically connected.
She does not come from a political dynasty, and her path was not paved by an elite university or a famous last name.
Everything she has built came through hard work, faith, and the support of people who believed in her.
Like many people across District 14, Bella knows what it is like to watch grocery bills, utility costs, housing expenses, and everyday necessities become harder to afford.
She understands the pressure of trying to make every dollar stretch because she lives with those same pressures.
As a nonprofit leader and community organizer, Bella has spent years bringing people together and fighting for the issues her community cares about.
She has stood with neighbors demanding responsible growth, protection for local land, personal privacy, better mental health care, and greater accountability from government.
When residents felt ignored, Bella kept showing up.
Her decision to run is rooted in faith.
Bella believes she was called by God to serve her community, and she sees representing District 14 as the next step in answering that call.
Bella is not running to become a career politician.
She is running to serve the community that raised her and make sure everyday people have someone at the Capitol who understands their struggles, shares their concerns, and never forgets who she works for.

Register To Vote put georgia first Your Vote Should Be Earned District 14 deserves a representative who shows up, listens, and earns your vote.
The incumbent has held this seat since 2019 and ran unopposed in three straight general elections.
This seat should never be treated as guaranteed.
His campaign finance record shows a clear pattern.
From 2018 through 2024, nearly 78 percent of the money he raised came from nonindividual sources, including corporations, PACs, trade associations, and other political organizations.
About one dollar in ten came from outside Georgia.
That pattern continues today.
In the current PeachFile records, only $750 of $17,950 in itemized contributions came from individuals.
Available filings also list at least $2,300 from Georgia Power and its employee PAC since 2018, along with current support from the Texas based sports betting lobby.
Bella is the only candidate in this race who has not accepted contributions from corporations or industry PACs.
Her campaign is powered by people, and she is working to earn every vote because public office belongs to you.
