How to Vote in the Nov. 5 , 2024 General Election
Register to Vote
Register to vote online (before October 8).
After October 8, in order to vote in the General Election, you must register in person with the proper identification.
In-Person Registration is available here:
- October 8 – October 18, 2024 - at the Clerk’s Annex (Monday – Friday, 10AM to 5PM)
- October 19 – November 5, 2024 - at Early Voting locations (most locations open Monday – Saturday 10AM to 7PM)
- November 5, 2024 - at Election Day voting locations (7AM-7PM)
Please bring a photo ID and a current utility bill, bank statement, government check, paycheck, student identification card or other government document, including identification issued by an Indian nation, tribe or pueblo that shows your name and current address.
Absentee Voting by Mail
Absentee ballot requests will be received until October 22, 2024.
Absentee ballots will begin to be mailed out starting on October 8.
You can request an absentee ballot online by clicking here.
After you receive your ballot, return your ballot as quickly as possible, making sure that it’s filled out entirely.
You can track the status of your absentee ballot here.
You can also drop off your ballot at any of the Early or Election Day Voting sites (see below).
All absentee ballots may be returned to the County Clerk’s Office or any Election Day polling location no later than 7PM on Election Day, Tuesday, November 5, 2024.
Early Voting In-Person
Early voting (or In-Person Absentee) at the Clerk’s Annex (1500 Lomas Blvd NW, Suite A, 87104) is from October 8 – October 18, 2024 (Monday – Friday 10 AM. – 5 PM).
In observance of Indigenous Peoples’ Day, the Clerk’s Annex will be closed on Monday, October 14th.
