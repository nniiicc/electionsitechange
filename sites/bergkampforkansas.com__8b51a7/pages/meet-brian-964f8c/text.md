DONATE MEET BRIAN Experienced Leadership Delivering Results for District 93 Brian grew up on a farm near Cheney Lake.
He has two older sisters, both married and living in Nebraska; a younger brother who lives in Salina with his wife; and a youngest sister who is married and lives in Kansas City.
Brian's parents are recently retired and enjoying spending more time with their grandchildren.
In April 2026, Brian and Maria were married in Wichita, and they look forward to this next chapter together. ​ Brian graduated from Pretty Prairie High School and earned a degree in accounting from Friends University in Wichita.
He joined Koch Industries in 2008, where his roles spanned corporate taxation, logistics, fertilizer sales, and project management — including a five-month posting in Australia in 2015.
Those years taught him lasting lessons about business and about working with people across industries and backgrounds.
He left Koch in 2021 after being elected to the Kansas House of Representatives, where he currently represents the 93rd District in southwest Sedgwick County. ​ Outside of work, Brian values time with family and stays active in the Catholic Diocese of Wichita, volunteering with The Lord's Diner and other organizations. ​ Brian is continually struck by the generosity and hospitality of his fellow Kansans, and he's proud to call Kansas home.
