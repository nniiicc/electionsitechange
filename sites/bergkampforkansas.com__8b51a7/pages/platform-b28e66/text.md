LIST OF ISSUES
Kansas Values

Economy
For the people of Kansas to succeed, we need a broad business base and skilled workers.
A key to continued growth is letting employers know the state is easy to work with and will not be a barrier to entry.
Our job is to set the groundwork for entrepreneurs to build their businesses and thrive.
Government
Reign in spending while focusing on the core functions of infrastructure, safety and health.
While in Topeka, Brian worked to lower property taxes and cut back the budget.

Health Care
