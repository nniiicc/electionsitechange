Senator Jon Ossoff Endorses Jen Jordan for Georgia Supreme Court
Announcement follows endorsements by Vice President Kamala Harris, wide range of groups representing Georgians
ATLANTA — Today, U.S.
Senator Jon Ossoff endorsed Jen Jordan in the Georgia Supreme Court election being held on May 19, adding to the growing momentum behind her campaign.
The endorsement is part of a broad wave of support – from Vice President Kamala Harris to major labor organizations, civil liberties groups, and other organizations representing tens of thousands of Georgia voters.
In announcing his support for her campaign, Senator Ossoff said, “Jen Jordan has an impressive record protecting Georgians’ civil liberties under the law.
As a Georgia Supreme Court justice, she will stand as a sentinel defending the rule of law and civil and human rights for all Georgians.
Jen has my full support, and I encourage Georgians to vote early before Election Day on May 19.”
“I am grateful for Senator Ossoff’s support as my campaign continues to build a broad coalition focused on fairness, integrity, and equal justice under the law.
This campaign is about ensuring every Georgian can trust that our courts will protect their rights, and I’m honored to have his endorsement,” said Jen Jordan.
In addition to Senator Ossoff and Vice President Harris, Jen’s campaign has received endorsements from the following major organizations:
- EMILYs List
- Asian American Advocacy Fund
- Reproductive Freedom for All
- Georgia AFL-CIO
- SEIU Workers United
- Teamsters Local 528 & 728
- Fair Fight
- Georgia Conservation Voters
- Georgia Equality
