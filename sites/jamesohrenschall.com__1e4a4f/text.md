From the court room to the state legislature
James Ohrenschall
is fighting for all of us.
James Ohrenschall has deep roots in Nevada, shaped by his mother, former Assemblywoman Genie Ohrenschall, and her lifelong commitment to public service.
Inspired by her example, James became a juvenile public defender to ensure that children receive fair treatment in the legal system.
Today, James serves in the Nevada State Senate, where he is focused on protecting voting rights, lowering costs for Nevada families, and standing up to Donald Trump.

A Strong Progressive Record
Delivering Results For You
Stood Up to Donald Trump
James supported a bill to ban ICE agents from entering our schools and a resolution condemning President Trump’s pardon of the January 6 insurgents.
Fought to Lower Costs
He joined efforts to lower the cost of prescription drugs, voting to curb corporate home ownership, and eliminating junk fees for renters.
Showed Up for Our Schools
James has consistently delivered increases in per-student funding for our schools, securing the largest education budget in state history, and making permanent pay raises for our teachers.
