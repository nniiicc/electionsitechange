Washtenaw County Commissioner Jason Morgan running for state House
Jun 18, 2021
Updated: Jun 23, 2025
ANN ARBOR, MI - Washtenaw County Commissioner Jason Morgan, D-Ann Arbor, announced his bid for state House, according to a campaign release Thursday.
He seeks to replace Rep.
Yousef Rabhi, D-Ann Arbor, for the 53rd District seat, since Rabhi is term-limited.
