These endorsements recognize Rep.
Salazar’s record of supporting law enforcement, securing millions in federal funding for public safety, and ensuring officers and deputies have the resources needed to protect South Florida.
“Keeping our families safe starts with supporting the brave men and women who put their lives on the line every day,” said Congresswoman Salazar .
“I am honored to have the trust of these local and national law enforcement leaders.
I will always fight to give our officers the tools and support they need to keep our communities safe.” Why They are Endorsing Rep.
María Elvira Salazar Miami-Dade Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz said: “Congresswoman María Elvira Salazar has been a strong and reliable fighter for law enforcement in South Florida.
She understands that keeping our communities safe means making sure the men and women who protect us have the tools, resources, and support they need to do their jobs.
From securing federal funding to strengthen public safety operations and help law enforcement combat crime, to consistently standing with our officers and deputies, Congresswoman Salazar has proven she is committed to safer streets and stronger communities.
I’m proud to support her re-election.” South Florida PBA President Steadman Stahl said: “The South Florida PBA is committed to endorsing candidates who support the brave men and women of law enforcement and understand the unique challenges these officers face each and every day.
Congresswoman Salazar has made law and order a priority throughout her public service career and has remained dedicated to ensuring the safety and security of the residents she represents and the members we protect.
We are proud to support her re-election.” Florida State Fraternal Order of Police, District 6, Director Felix del Rosario said: “The Florida State Fraternal Order of Police, District 6, representing more than 4,500 law enforcement officers throughout Miami-Dade County, is proud to officially endorse your candidacy.
You have been a steadfast supporter of the men and women of law enforcement, and your commitment has extended beyond words through your efforts to secure critical federal resources for our police departments, including funding for marked police vehicles and other public safety initiatives.” Police Officers Defense Coalition President Bert Eyler said: “Representative Salazar’s leadership has delivered real resources for officers.
She has proposed and supported legislation that strengthens public safety and has exhibited unwavering support for those who put on the badge every day.
The officers and directors of the Police Officers Defense Coalition are proud to endorse Rep.
María Salazar for re-election and look forward to continuing to work with her in Congress.” Hispanic Police Officers Association President Carlos Arguelles said: “On behalf of the Hispanic Police Officers Association (HPOA), we are proud to endorse your candidacy for your re-election Campaign.
You are a champion of law enforcement, and we are proud to support your candidaсy.” These endorsements demonstrate strong local and national law enforcement support for Congresswoman Salazar and her proven record of fighting for safer streets, stronger communities, and the officers who protect them.
Rep.
Salazar Record on Public Safety Rep.
Salazar has secured $3.75 million to modernize technology for the Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office, $2.1 million for new City of Miami Police patrol vehicles, and $1.25 million for the Miami-Dade Sheriff’s Office Violence Reduction Initiative, which seeks to reduce gun-related deaths and strengthen public safety.
As the lead House sponsor of the bipartisan TAKE IT DOWN Act , Rep.
Salazar led the fight in Congress to protect victims from nonconsensual intimate images and AI-generated deepfakes.
Signed into law in May 2025, the legislation makes this abuse a federal crime and requires online platforms to remove the content.
Earlier this year, the law secured its first conviction, proving that Rep.
Salazar’s legislation is already holding online predators accountable and delivering justice for victims.
Rep.
Salazar regularly honors our local officers for acts of bravery and recognizes the service and sacrifice of South Florida’s law enforcement community in keeping our community safe.
