Miami-Dade Voters
On Election Day, Miami-Dade voters must vote at their assigned precinct.
Use the official Miami-Dade Elections Department resources below to confirm your voting location, review voter information, and get assistance with any questions.
Your assigned Election Day voting location is based on your registered address.
Voters should verify their precinct before heading to the polls.
You can find your voting location online, by checking your voter registration card, or by contacting Miami-Dade County for assistance.
