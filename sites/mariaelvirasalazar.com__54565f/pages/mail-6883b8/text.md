Requests are valid through the next regularly scheduled General Election and must be renewed periodically.
Voters without an active request on file may not automatically receive a ballot for upcoming elections.
The next elections in Miami-Dade County are the August 18 Primary Election and the November General Election .
Voters who plan to vote by mail should verify that they have an active Vote-by-Mail request on file and submit a request if needed.
Vote-by-Mail requests can be submitted online, by phone, or by email through the Miami-Dade Elections Department.
