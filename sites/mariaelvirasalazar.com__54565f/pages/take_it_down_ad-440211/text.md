María Elvira Salazar’s work to protect children and families from new threats created by artificial intelligence.﻿ ﻿ When artificial intelligence made it easier for predators to create and spread sexually explicit fake images without a victim’s consent, Salazar authored and introduced the TAKE IT DOWN Act in the House and led the effort to get it passed.﻿ ﻿ President Donald Trump signed the bipartisan legislation into law on May 19, 2025.﻿ ﻿ “One of my proudest moments as a Congresswoman was passing the TAKE IT DOWN Act,” Salazar says in the new ad.

Salazar’s work to pass the TAKE IT DOWN Act reflects a larger priority throughout her time in Congress: protecting families, supporting law enforcement, and giving public safety officials the resources they need to keep our communities safe.

Because of her strong record of standing with law enforcement, Rep.
