María Elvira Salazar is the daughter of Cuban exiles who fled communism in search of freedom and opportunity.
Before serving in Congress, she was an award-winning journalist who challenged dictators, exposed corruption, and reported from across Latin America.
Today, she fights for South Florida families by strengthening the economy, improving healthcare, supporting public safety, and defending the freedoms that make America exceptional.
