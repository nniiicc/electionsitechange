DONATE ON THE ISSUES I believe that together we can build a future full of hope, equity, and opportunity for each and every Coloradan.
Serving our community as the State Representative from House District 53 remains the privilege of a lifetime.
In these uncertain times, the challenges before us can feel overwhelming and insurmountable.
We must not lose faith in our ability to solve problems together.
As Margaret Mead reminds us: “Never doubt that a small group of thoughtful, committed citizens can change the world; indeed, it’s the only thing that ever has.” Affordable Housing Housing affordability remains one of the biggest challenges facing Fort Collins and communities across Colorado.
During my time in the State House, I have championed practical, bipartisan solutions that increase housing supply, expand homeownership opportunities, and protect hardworking families. ​ In 2026, I was proud to pass HB26-1001 , the HOME Act , which removes unnecessary barriers preventing schools, housing authorities, transit agencies, and nonprofits from developing affordable housing on land they already own.
This commonsense reform will help create more housing opportunities while making better use of existing land.
You can read the bill here . ​ I also sponsored HB26-1313 , which modernizes Colorado's affordable housing requirements under Proposition 123.
By establishing more realistic housing production targets, this legislation helps communities maintain access to critical housing funding while accounting for local economic conditions.
You can read the bill here . ​ In addition, I have been a leader in protecting mobile home park residents .
In 2026, I helped pass HB26-1224 , strengthening transparency and financial protections for mobile home owners.
This builds on my previous efforts to protect residents and help communities preserve affordable housing.
You can read the bill here . ​ These accomplishments build on two major reforms I helped pass in 2025: HB25-1272 , which reduces barriers to condominium and townhome construction, and HB25-1273 , which modernizes building codes to make smaller multifamily housing developments more feasible. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins ​ These reforms mean more opportunities for first-time homebuyers, stronger protections for mobile home park residents, and greater flexibility to build affordable housing in our community.
The goal is simple.
Keep Fort Collins a place where people who work here can also afford to live here. ​ I will continue fighting for affordable paths to homeownership, strong renter protections, and increased workforce housing, so that hardworking Coloradans can continue to live and thrive in the communities they call home.
Gun Violence Prevention Gun violence is not inevitable.
Our children should not live in fear.
The gun violence epidemic in the United States is a problem we can solve.
In my time in the General Assembly, we have taken historic action to reduce gun violence in our communities.
In previous sessions, I have passed legislation banning ghost guns in Colorado , as well as legislation regulating firearm dealers in our state.
Additionally, I am proud to have supported bills that: ​ Keep firearms out of the hands of those charged with domestic violence Establish the Office of Gun Violence Prevention Raise the age to purchase firearms in our state Mandate safe storage for firearms Require reporting lost or stolen firearms Allow for Extreme Risk Protection Orders (ERPO) Remove Special Immunity for the Gun Industry Create mandatory waiting periods Close the Charleston Loophole Allow for stricter local gun violence prevention laws In 2026, I helped pass HB26-1144, Prohibit 3D Printing Firearms & Components , which prohibits the unauthorized manufacturing of functional firearms and certain firearm components using 3D printing technology.
This legislation addresses emerging threats posed by privately manufactured firearms while preserving exceptions for licensed manufacturers and accredited gunsmithing programs.
You can read the bill here . ​ In 2025, I passed SB25-003, Semiautomatic Firearms & Rapid-Fire Devices , a bill that strengthened firearm safety regulations for semi-automatic firearms statewide.
You can read the bill here . ​ I was also a sponsor of HB25-1171, Possession of Weapon by Previous Offender Crimes , which improves public safety by clarifying when individuals with multiple prior felony convictions are prohibited from possessing weapons.
You can read the bill here . ​ These laws complement existing measures, such as Extreme Risk Protection Orders and safe storage requirements, that reduce the risk of violence while respecting responsible firearm ownership. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins These laws are intended to reduce gun violence, strengthen public safety, and help keep firearms out of the hands of individuals who are legally prohibited from possessing them. ​ Protecting our communities and respecting responsible firearm ownership are not mutually exclusive.
I will continue working toward policies that prevent violence and keep Colorado families safe.
Abortion & Reproductive Health Care Reproductive freedom is a fundamental right, and decisions about pregnancy, abortion, and reproductive health care belong between individuals and their health care providers, not politicians.
I've consistently supported legislation protecting reproductive rights and ensuring Coloradans can make personal medical decisions without unnecessary government interference. ​ In 2022, I stood alongside my Democratic colleagues through more than 24 hours of debate to pass the Reproductive Health Equity Act , protecting the right to abortion under Colorado law ahead of the Supreme Court's decision to overturn Roe v.
Wade.
In 2024, Colorado voters further strengthened these protections by enshrining abortion rights in our state constitution. ​ ​ In 2026, I supported and co-sponsored HB26-1335 , which expands access to medication abortion through student health centers at Colorado colleges and universities.
This legislation helps address barriers students may face when seeking reproductive health care.
You can read the bill here . ​ What This Means for Fort Collins These protections ensure that people in Fort Collins can continue to access reproductive health care safely and legally, without fear of government interference.
By safeguarding abortion rights in the Colorado Constitution, we provide long-term certainty for patients, providers, and families, and reaffirm Fort Collins as a community that values personal freedom, bodily autonomy, and access to essential health care.
Public Education Strong public schools are the foundation of thriving communities and a brighter future for Colorado's children.
As a former public school teacher in a Title I school and a parent of three children in the Poudre School District, I understand firsthand the challenges facing students, educators, and families.
Ensuring every child has access to a quality education remains one of my deepest commitments. ​ ​In 2026, I helped pass SB26-145 , bipartisan legislation that strengthens transparency and fairness in school funding decisions.
This law ensures that charter schools have an opportunity to present their facility needs when school districts consider asking voters to fund capital improvements, while preserving local school boards' authority over funding decisions.
You can read the bill here . ​ In 2025, I was a co-sponsor of HB25-1131, which eliminated enrollment caps at Colorado State University's veterinary medicine program, expanding opportunities for students pursuing careers in veterinary medicine.
You can read the bill here . ​ Throughout my legislative service, I've supported increased public school funding, universal preschool, and full-day kindergarten, helping reduce costs for families while giving students a stronger foundation for success.
I've also advocated for investments that direct resources into classrooms and support the educators who make quality public education possible.​ ​ ​ What This Means for Fort Collins These efforts support students and educators in the Poudre School District, expand educational opportunities at Colorado State University, and help families access quality education from early childhood through higher education. ​ Every child deserves the opportunity to succeed, regardless of their family's income or ZIP code.
I'll continue supporting strong public schools, dedicated educators, and educational opportunities that prepare the next generation for success.
Older Adults Older Coloradans deserve to live with dignity, independence, and financial security.
Whether that means remaining in their homes, accessing affordable health care, or staying connected to their communities, I believe we have a responsibility to ensure that seniors and retirees have the support they need. ​ I've worked to lower prescription drug costs and improve access to affordable health care.
In 2026, I helped pass SB26-167 , which will allow eligible prescription drug purchases to count toward patients' insurance deductibles and out-of-pocket maximums beginning in 2028.
You can read the bill here . ​ Also in 2026, I worked across party lines to introduce HB26-1204 , legislation designed to expand affordable housing opportunities for older Coloradans.
The proposal would have provided tax exemptions for qualifying senior cooperative housing developments, helping support alternative housing options for seniors with limited or fixed incomes.
Although the bill did not become law, expanding affordable housing for older adults remains an important priority. ​ ​ Beyond health care and housing, I support strengthening protections against financial exploitation and predatory lending, expanding access to home-based care, and improving transportation services that help older adults maintain their independence. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins These efforts address challenges facing older residents in Fort Collins, including rising housing costs, expensive medications, and barriers to independent living. ​ Every Coloradan deserves to live with dignity and security, no matter their age.
I'll continue supporting policies that help older adults remain healthy, financially secure, and connected to the communities they call home.
Transportation & Infrastructure A safe, reliable, and affordable transportation system is essential to Colorado's economy and quality of life.
As your State Representative, I have worked to improve transportation safety, expand public transit, and invest in infrastructure that connects our communities. ​ In 2026, I worked to help pass SB26-172.
Supporting the Front Range Passenger Rail District, this legislation strengthens the district's ability to plan and advance passenger rail service along the Front Range.
By modernizing the district's governance and providing greater flexibility for regional planning and voter-approved funding, this legislation helps lay the groundwork for connecting Fort Collins to communities along the I-25 corridor.
You can read the bill here . ​ I have also passed legislation to improve roadway safety, including measures addressing roundabout safety and transportation challenges in rural Colorado.
These reforms help protect drivers, cyclists, and pedestrians. ​ Additionally, I have supported expanding bike trails, e-bike programs, and public transportation options that give Coloradans more affordable and sustainable ways to travel.
I also championed legislation to modernize Colorado's electrical transmission infrastructure, supporting grid reliability and the state's growing energy needs. ​ ​ ​ What This Means for Fort Collins ​ These investments and reforms support safer roads, better regional connections, and more transportation choices for Northern Colorado residents. ​ My goal is to build a transportation system that connects our communities, supports economic opportunity, and makes it easier and more affordable for Coloradans to get where they need to go.
Health Care Affordable, accessible health care should be a reality for every Colorado family, not a privilege reserved for those who can afford it.
Throughout my time in the legislature, I've worked to lower health care costs, strengthen patient protections, and remove barriers that prevent Coloradans from getting the care they need. ​ In 2026, I helped pass SB26-167 , ensuring that eligible prescription medications purchased directly from pharmacies or manufacturers count toward a patient's insurance deductible and out-of-pocket maximum.
Beginning in 2028, this reform will help Coloradans better manage prescription drug expenses and avoid unnecessary out-of-pocket costs.
You can read the bill here . ​ ​ I've also championed legislation to limit excessive hospital facility fees, protecting patients from unexpected medical bills and increasing transparency in health care pricing.
These protections help ensure families aren't burdened with unnecessary charges simply for seeking medical care. ​ Additionally, I helped secure $1 million to expand culturally competent health care for LGBTQ+ Coloradans and underserved communities.
I've also passed legislation protecting health care workers from doxxing and harassment, allowing medical professionals to provide care without fear of intimidation.
Expanding access to affordable mental and behavioral health services remains an important priority, alongside continued efforts to reduce costs and strengthen Colorado's health care system. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins These reforms help Fort Collins families access affordable medications, avoid unexpected medical expenses, and receive quality care from protected and supported health care professionals. ​ No one in our state should have to choose between their health and their financial security.
I'll continue working toward a health care system that puts patients and families first.
Workforce & Economic Opportunity A strong economy starts with good-paying jobs, thriving local businesses, and opportunities for every Coloradan to succeed.
I've worked to strengthen workers' rights, support economic development, and ensure that working families have the resources and opportunities they need to build a secure future. ​ In 2026, I helped pass HB26-1014 , bipartisan legislation that helps Colorado attract and retain businesses creating new jobs.
By extending this successful economic development program through 2034, we're encouraging long-term investment, supporting business growth, and creating opportunities for Colorado's workforce.
You can read the bill here . ​ I've also been a strong advocate for workers' rights and collective bargaining, supporting legislation that expands organizing rights for county employees and strengthens workplace protections for public workers.
I've stood alongside striking workers on the picket line and believe every Coloradan deserves fair wages, safe working conditions, and a voice in their workplace. ​ Expanding access to vocational education, apprenticeships, and workforce training is equally important.
These programs help Coloradans develop valuable skills, pursue rewarding careers, and meet the evolving needs of our economy. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins A thriving Fort Collins depends on strong local businesses, skilled workers, and economic opportunities that allow families to build their futures here. ​ Economic growth should benefit everyone, not just those at the top.
I'll continue supporting policies that create good-paying jobs, protect workers, and help Northern Colorado businesses and families succeed.
Civil Rights & Equal Opportunity Colorado should be a place where everyone is treated with dignity, fairness, and respect.
Throughout my time in the legislature, I've supported policies that protect civil rights, combat discrimination, and ensure that every Coloradan has the opportunity to participate fully in their community.
In 2025, I was a co-sponsor of HB25-1239 , the Colorado Anti-Discrimination Act , which strengthens protections for individuals experiencing discrimination, especially Coloradans with disabilities.
You can read the bill here . ​ ​ I've also been a strong advocate for LGBTQ+ equality, supporting protections against discrimination, access to affirming health care, and policies that ensure LGBTQ+ Coloradans can live safely and openly. ​ As federal policies increasingly raise concerns about civil liberties and constitutional protections, I remain committed to protecting the rights of immigrants and refugees in our communities .
Everyone deserves fair treatment under the law, regardless of their background or immigration status.
I will continue supporting policies that safeguard constitutional rights, promote accessibility, and ensure equal opportunities in education, employment, and everyday life. ​ I also recognize the need to address disparities within our criminal justice system, including expanding diversion programs and improving access to mental health and substance use treatment. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins These civil rights protections help ensure Fort Collins remains a welcoming, safe community for all residents.
They provide meaningful legal remedies when discrimination occurs and protect individual dignity in education, housing, employment, and everyday life.
Elections & Democracy Colorado has built one of the nation's most accessible election systems, with mail-in paper ballots, opportunities for in-person voting, and safeguards designed to protect election integrity.
I've consistently supported legislation that protects voting rights, strengthens election security, and ensures Coloradans can participate in our democracy safely and confidently. ​ In 2026, I co-sponsored HB26-1084 , legislation addressing transparency requirements for statewide ballot measures.
I also supported HB26-1113 , which updates election administration procedures, strengthens voter registration systems, and expands access to voter information for students.
Both bills became law ​ Previously, I supported the Vote Without Fear Act , which restricts openly carrying firearms near polling locations, and the Election Official Protection Act , which protects election workers from doxxing and intimidation. ​ I also supported the 2022 Colorado Election Security Act , strengthening safeguards against unauthorized access to voting equipment and establishing additional qualifications for individuals responsible for administering elections. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins These protections help ensure that Fort Collins residents can participate in elections safely, election workers can perform their duties without intimidation, and voters can have confidence in the integrity of our election system.
Environment & Conservation Colorado's natural beauty is one of the things that makes our state such a special place to live.
From the foothills and trails surrounding Fort Collins to our rivers, forests, and open spaces, protecting our environment means preserving the places that make our communities unique. ​ Protecting Colorado's natural resources requires thoughtful stewardship and a commitment to conservation.
I've supported efforts to improve air and water quality, preserve wildlife habitats, and protect our public lands.
I also believe we can pursue environmental protection while supporting economic opportunity and accommodating Colorado's growing communities. ​ In 2025, I co-sponsored HB25-1318 , which invested $5 million in programs protecting Colorado's native wildlife, including threatened and endangered species.
These investments help preserve biodiversity, restore habitats, and support conservation efforts across the state.
You can read the bill here . ​ I've also supported investments in water conservation, clean energy, and sustainable transportation, recognizing that protecting our environment requires solutions that address both present challenges and long-term needs.
As Colorado continues to grow, we must balance development and outdoor recreation with the responsibility to preserve our natural resources. ​ What This Means for Fort Collins From Horsetooth Reservoir and the Poudre River to our extensive network of trails and open spaces, Fort Collins residents have a deep connection to the outdoors.
Protecting these resources means maintaining access to outdoor recreation, safeguarding wildlife habitats, and preserving the landscapes that contribute to our community's quality of life. ​ Future generations deserve to experience the same natural beauty and outdoor opportunities we enjoy today.
I'll continue supporting responsible conservation policies that protect Colorado's environment and keep our communities healthy and thriving.
