About Tim
Education has shaped my life from the very beginning.
My mom was my first-grade teacher, my dad was my middle school principal, and my grandfather testified before the Supreme Court in Brown v.
Board of Education.
We grew up modestly, but we believed deeply in the dignity of every child and the power of public education to strengthen communities and expand opportunity.
As a young person, I spent two years living in Africa—an experience that broadened my worldview and sparked a lifelong commitment to service.
Over my career as a chiropractic physician, I have worked on humanitarian missions, traveled widely, collaborated with NGOs, and represented my profession before the United Nations.
These experiences taught me a lasting truth: strong schools, strong institutions, and strong communities are the foundation of a healthy society.
For more than 45 years, I have also been a small-business owner who has signed both sides of a paycheck.
I know what it takes to meet a budget, support a team, and deliver results.
I believe leadership begins with listening, continues with transparency, and succeeds through trust and collaboration.
No one has all the answers—but when we work together, we find what works.
These values guide my campaign for the North Carolina House of Representatives.
My focus is on expanding opportunity for families and small businesses, strengthening programs that provide protection and stability, and addressing the affordability challenges squeezing the middle class.
I am committed to strong public schools, responsible investment in our communities, support for our public servants, and policies that help families get ahead—not fall behind.
I’m running because I believe New Hanover County deserves a representative who listens, brings people together, and delivers practical solutions for the future.
I welcome your ideas, your questions, and your partnership as we work to build a stronger North Carolina.
