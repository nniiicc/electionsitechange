Terri DeBoer — Official Photos Media Video State of the Race Immediately and early in the campaign, voters watching television across the Grand Rapids DMA need to see and hear the Terri DeBoer they already know and trust.
For more than 30 years, Terri was invited into West Michigan homes every day.
She became a familiar face and a trusted voice, delivering the weather and, most importantly, telling people the truth.
She built that trust one family, one community, and one day at a time.
Now, Terri is taking that same commitment to service to Washington.
She’ll put people before politics, fight to make healthcare more affordable, protect Social Security, work to bring good-paying jobs home, and stand up for greater accountability in Congress, including an end to insider trading by members of Congress.
West Michigan has known Terri for decades.
Now it’s time for voters to see that the woman they welcomed into their homes for 30 years is the same woman they can trust to stand up for them in Washington.
