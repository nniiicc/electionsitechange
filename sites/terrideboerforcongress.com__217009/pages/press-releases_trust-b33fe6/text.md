Terri DeBoer Launches 1st Television Ad of the Cycle
“Trust” highlights DeBoer’s Commitment of Putting People over Politics
Grand Rapids, MI - Today meteorologist and Republican candidate for Congress in MI-03, Terri DeBoer announced her first television ad of the 2026 general election.
“For thirty years, you trusted me to tell you the truth, and prepare you for the storms ahead.
We put people first,” said DeBoer.
“Michigan families are struggling.
I’m running for Congress to tell you the truth and deliver results; lowering costs, making healthcare affordable for all, bringing balance back to Washington - and I’ll work with either side to get results.”
“The people of West Michigan are ready for a change and I look forward to earning their support and delivering results on day 1."
The ad “Trust” can be found here: https://youtu.be/sVKmYN0W35w
Terri DeBoer is a trusted meteorologist, author, wife, mom, grandmother, and trailblazer who has dedicated over 30 years to serving West Michigan families.
Growing up in a military family, she witnessed the remarkable sacrifice, resilience, and service of those in uniform.
These powerful experiences instilled in her the core values of hard work, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to her community, principles that continue to drive her impact today.
Now, she’s stepping up to give the people of West Michigan the representation in Congress they deserve.
