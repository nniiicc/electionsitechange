“Everything is expensive, and costs continue to rise.
From fuel to groceries, I have watched as my representatives continue to ignore the issues impacting my neighbors.
We see it when we fill up our gas tanks, and we feel it when we buy the essentials.
My family felt it while navigating care for my late grandfather this past summer.
It is time for change.” — Letisha Hinds Letisha grew up in Northeast Arkansas, attending public schools and becoming the first in her family to attend college.
After earning her Bachelor of Science in Business Administration, she moved to Northwest Arkansas and built a life in the community she now calls home.
Today, she’s a wife, mom, and professional who has spent her career learning how to listen, negotiate, build relationships, and get things done.
Her family lives in this community.
They drive the roads here, shop at the same stores, and her daughter attends the public schools.
They experience the same rising costs and everyday challenges as families across District 16 and throughout Arkansas.
As a multigenerational caretaker, Letisha understands firsthand what it means to navigate a healthcare system that wasn’t built for everyday families while balancing work, caregiving, and the rising cost of simply getting by.
That experience isn’t abstract to her.
It’s personal.
Letisha is running because for too long, the same people have told us we don't deserve better and we can't do better.
She knows both are wrong.
She's running because she knows what it means to put others first, and because Arkansas and District 16 deserve better.
She's running to ensure we build a future that is bright for all Arkansas families.
She’s not a politician.
