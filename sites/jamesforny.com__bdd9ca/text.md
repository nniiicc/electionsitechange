Fighting for New York
Meet Letitia James
Letitia “Tish” James is proud to protect New Yorkers, taking on ultra-powerful interests and never backing down from a fight.
Since taking office in 2019, Attorney General James has focused on lowering costs for working families, holding bad actors accountable, and protecting New Yorkers’ rights and safety.
She has sued the Trump Administration more than 50 times to protect New Yorkers’ rights and funding for critical programs like healthcare, public education and good-paying jobs.
