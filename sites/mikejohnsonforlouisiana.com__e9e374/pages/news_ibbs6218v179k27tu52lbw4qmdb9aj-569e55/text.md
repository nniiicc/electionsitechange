Speaker Johnson Tops $50 Million Raised for House Republicans in 2025
July 15, 2025
The total includes $18.65 million raised by Speaker Johnson in the latest quarter.
"We accomplished more in the first six months of any Congress in modern history by delivering on the mandate voters entrusted to us last year, and we are just getting started.
From promises made and promises kept to the latest fundraising quarter, it is clear that House Republicans are on offense," said Speaker Johnson.
"With over $50 million raised for House Republicans so far this year, it is my great honor to lead this fight and ensure we are ready to defend and grow our majority in 2026."
This cycle, Speaker Johnson has now transferred more than $14 million to the National Republican Congressional Committee (NRCC), and more than $10.5 million to incumbent Republicans, the highest total ever transferred to incumbents by a Speaker through two quarters.
These transfers were part of the Speaker’s largest quarterly transfer ever last month.
Speaker Johnson’s latest fundraising efforts build on his record-setting first quarter to begin the 2026 cycle with more than $32 million raised.
