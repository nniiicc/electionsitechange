Speaker Johnson Raises $65 Million for House Republicans
October 15, 2025
That is the largest amount ever raised to this point by a Republican Speaker of the House in a midterm cycle.
"House Republicans enter the final quarter of 2025 energized, united, and with resources needed to stay on offense.
Meanwhile, House Democrats begin this quarter by capitulating to the far-left and shutting down the government for taxpayer benefits to illegal aliens.
The contrast between our parties and visions for America are on full display," said Speaker Johnson.
"With one year to go until the 2026 elections, we will continue to deliver for the American people and grow our majority."
Through the third quarter, Speaker Johnson has now transferred more than $18.3 million to the National Republican Congressional Committee (NRCC), and $12.5 million to incumbent Republicans, the highest total ever transferred by a Speaker through Q3.
Overall, Speaker Johnson has distributed $36 million to Members, campaigns, committees, and state parties as he leads the effort to hold and grow the House Republican majority.
Speaker Johnson’s latest fundraising efforts build on his record-setting efforts to begin the 2026 cycle.
