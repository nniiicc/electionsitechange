"House Republicans begin this critical election year by setting records, outpacing the Democrats, and solidifying a war chest to defend our incumbents and grow our majority," said Speaker Johnson.
"Over the past year, House Republicans have worked hand-in-hand with President Trump to deliver lower costs, working families tax cuts, a secure border, and peace through strength - and we have tremendous support nationwide to continue delivering on our America First agenda.
Our record haul over the past 12 months proves once again we are on offense and on pace to defy history in 2026."
In 2025, Speaker Johnson transferred $23.6 million to the National Republican Congressional Committee (NRCC).
The Speaker also transferred $13.4 million, or an average of $453,000 per campaign, in hard dollars directly to incumbents in the past year, which is the most ever transferred to Members from a Speaker in a single year.
Overall, Speaker Johnson has now distributed over $46.3 million to Members, campaigns, committees, and state parties this cycle as he leads the effort to hold and grow the House Republican majority.
This includes $11.6 million transferred in the fourth quarter, which nearly doubled initial estimates.
Speaker Johnson’s latest fundraising efforts build on his record-setting efforts to begin the 2026 cycle.
