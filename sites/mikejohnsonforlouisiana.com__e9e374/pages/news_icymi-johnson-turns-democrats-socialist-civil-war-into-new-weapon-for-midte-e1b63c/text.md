WASHINGTON, D.C. - Speaker Mike Johnson has unveiled a new website, ContrastForAmerica.com, highlighting the stark contrast between House Republicans and Democrats leading up to the 2026 Midterms.

“The 2026 midterms presents a clear contrast for America between common sense and crazy.
House Republicans have delivered working families tax cuts to put more money in your pocket, a secure border, safer streets, American energy dominance, an end to woke indoctrination for our kids, and renewed American strength abroad.
Meanwhile, Democrats have surrendered to the socialist and communist far-left that wants to abolish borders, defund police, abolish prisons, raise your taxes, and shred our Constitution, leaving America less safe, less prosperous, and less free,” said Speaker Johnson.
“Communism has led to the deaths of tens of millions of people, and in this midterm, we will make sure it is once again relegated to the ash heap of history.
By highlighting this decisive contrast for America and our common sense polices and America first candidates, House Republicans are ready to defy history and win the midterms.”
Speaker Johnson will continue to highlight the contrast throughout his August campaign swing that will include more than 30 Districts and cities across 18 states.
