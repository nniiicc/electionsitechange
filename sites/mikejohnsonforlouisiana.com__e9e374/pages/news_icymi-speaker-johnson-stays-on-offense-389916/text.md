Speaking in front of the former 3rd Police Precinct, still barricaded following the 2020 riots, Speaker Johnson discussed how the riots there were an enduring symbol of the Democrat presidential ticket’s disastrous records.
Speaker Johnson was joined by Rep.
Pete Stauber (MN-8), Rep.
Michelle Fischbach (MN-7), and former prosecutor Joe Teirab (MN-2 candidate).
"While Tim Walz let Minnesota burn, it was Kamala Harris who poured gasoline on the flames by helping to bail out those who held the matches," said Speaker Johnson.
"Kamala Harris helped raise money, not for the people whose businesses were destroyed or livelihoods ruined, but for the criminals who destroyed them." "The choice in this election could not be clearer," Speaker Johnson continued.
"Under President Donald Trump, our border was secure, our streets were safer, our economy was the strongest in history for every community.
Come November, with President Trump and with a new Republican Senate, we will begin our urgent work in earnest to make America great again." Today, Team Johnson released a new video and coverage below recapping the visit.
