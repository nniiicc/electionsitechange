Speaker Johnson Makes American Revival Tour Stop in Battleground Michigan
October 21, 2024
Contact: Greg Steele
FREELAND, MI – This weekend, Speaker Mike Johnson brought the American Revival Tour to Freeland, MI to discuss House Republicans' agenda in the next Congress to secure the border, fix the economy, restore peace through strength, and promote an American revival.
Speaker Johnson was joined on stage by Chairman Jim Jordan (OH-04), Mike Rogers, candidate for U.S.
Senate in Michigan, Tom Barrett, candidate for Michigan's 7th Congressional District, Paul Junge, candidate for Michigan's 8th Congressional District, Michigan businesswoman Tudor Dixon, and Pastor Lorenzo Sewell of 180 Church in Detroit.
"This election is about saving America and preserving the things that have made us the greatest nation in the world," Speaker Johnson said.
"By pursuing this conservative agenda, we can experience a true American revival."
In addition to the American Revival Tour stop in Freeland, Speaker Johnson campaigned alongside Tom Barrett in Lansing, MI, Paul Junge in Midland, MI, and incumbent John James (MI-10) in St.
Clair Shores, MI.
The Speaker was joined throughout his Michigan swing by Chairman Jim Jordan (OH-04), Chairman Jason Smith (MO-08), Rep.
John Moolenaar (MI-02), Rep.
Bill Huizenga (MI-04), Rep.
Tim Walberg (MI-05), and Rep.
Darin LaHood (IL-16).
