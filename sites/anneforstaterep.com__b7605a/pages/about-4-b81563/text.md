About Anne
My Story:
I come from a family of strong women, and learned what community leadership looks like from a young age.
My mother, a Danbury High graduate, became an architect and was the only woman in her class.
My grandmother was a small-business owner in Danbury.
They taught me the value of hard work and perseverance.
That the measure of a person is not in their wealth but in how well we care for one another.
I believe success is measured by the difference we make in people’s lives.
That’s why I spent my career fighting for fairness in the workplace.
As a working mom myself, I advocated for better policies like paid family and medical leave.
As an environmental lawyer, I advocated for sustainable development.
I served on the Sherman Conservation Commission and am currently a member of the Sherman Planning and Zoning Commission.
I’ve had enough of politicians who just talk about the problem or find someone to blame.
I have a different vision.
A vision of our communities where we work together to make everyone’s lives better.
And I am ready to get to work to turn that vision into a reality.
I hope you will join me!
