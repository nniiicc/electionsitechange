Family First
Dillan is a Husband and a father of two boys.
His Wife Alyssa and his sons Mason and Easton are the center of his life.
Dillan’s passion to serve starts with his family.
He stepped up to run for school board to ensure a quality education for his children.
Now Dillan is ready to fight for working families in Congress.
The American Dream
Dillan and his wife started their business DAME Fine Coffee out of a small trailer, now they have 8 stores across Illinois and Iowa.
Dillan knows the hard work and sacrifice required just to keep a business running in Illinois today.
In Congress, Dillan will be a voice for small businesses and will fight everyday to bring Manufacturing and real American Jobs back to Illinois.
Community impact
Dillan has served his community as a member of his local school board, chamber of commerce, eagle view community health board and he has previously served as State Legislative Director for SMART-TD Union.
He understands the challenges education, labor & healthcare systems are facing.
In Congress Dillan will push for funding to help Illinois Students, support Illinois Labor, and fight for price transparency in our broken healthcare system.
