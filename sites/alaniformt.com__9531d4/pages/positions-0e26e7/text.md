Alani Bankhead on the Issues: U.S.
Senate, Montana Alani Bankhead is a Democratic candidate for the United States Senate in Montana.
Her positions on the issues are below.
Enable JavaScript for the full interactive, searchable version.
Government Accountability Show up, and answer for the work.
For twenty-one years I was accountable for life-and-death decisions, and I never got to hide from the outcome.
Washington shouldn’t get to either.
Housing A Montana paycheck should buy a Montana home.
A Montana paycheck should be able to buy a Montana home.
Right now it can’t, and longtime owners get taxed just for trying to move.
Healthcare We already know universal healthcare works.
Millions of Americans already get their care through TRICARE, the military’s own government-run program, and we can pay to expand access by cutting the hundreds of billions we waste on paperwork and the drug middlemen, not anyone’s care.
And we keep rural hospitals open by protecting the Medicare and Medicaid payments they depend on.
Protecting the Vulnerable A record of putting child predators in prison.
I’ve spent more than two decades on the front lines of child protection, building the cases that put child sex abusers in jail.
I’ll bring that same fight to the Senate.
Relief for Our Seniors A lifetime of work shouldn’t end in poverty.
Montanans who spent a lifetime working shouldn’t be taxed out of the homes where they raised their families.
Reproductive Freedom Reproductive freedom: trust people, not the government.
Protecting a woman’s right to choose helps all Montanans.
In 2022 the Supreme Court took away a constitutional right for the first time in our history, and Montanans answered by voting to enshrine the right to make our own pregnancy decisions in our state constitution.
Public Lands Public lands stay public: period.
No selling of public lands ever.
No opening up public lands for exploitation ever.
I support corner crossing.
Period.
Education Schools worth the next generation.
Montana has the lowest starting teacher pay in the nation, about $36,682, dead last.
That’s how you lose the next generation of teachers, and the next generation of kids right behind them.
Agriculture Stand with the people who feed the country.
Montana agriculture feeds the nation, and family operations are getting squeezed out by rising costs and consolidation.
I’ll have their back.
Bringing Down Everyday Costs Lower the price of a normal life.
The price of groceries and gas is a national security issue.
What’s the point of protecting our borders if families can’t afford to live behind them?
Tribal Nations & Native Priorities Sovereignty, safety, and keeping our word.
Our nation’s history with Indigenous people is one I’m passionate about helping to heal.
On this campaign we don’t ask anything of the tribes; we support, and keep supporting, until our Native American neighbors decide we’re worthy of their trust: by listening, keeping promises, and matching our… Data Centers Montanans decide what gets built.
And no one gets to spy on them.
On public lands, AI and large-scale data centers are a flat no.
Everywhere else, my answer is no until we get honest studies, full disclosure, and real protections, and even then, only if a project truly earns it.
These are some of the richest companies on earth, and Montana is not their doormat.
Guns & Community Safety A gun owner who takes safety seriously.
I own guns, I use guns, and for twenty-one years I carried one for a living.
I respect what firearms mean in Montana, for hunting, for sport, and for protecting your own family.
That is not up for debate with me.
Climate Montana’s climate is already changing.
Let’s be ready for it.
The land is asking something different of us now.
After a severe fire, in more and more of Montana, the forest that comes back isn’t the forest that burned.
In 2021 every acre of this state was in drought at once and ranchers sold cows they had spent a lifetime breeding.
You don’t have to agree with me on why the climate is changing to agree on what we do about it: fund the work closest to the house first, make prevention a career, protect the whole ranch through drought as well as fire, restore the clean-energy credits through our co-ops with incentives and never mandates, stop letting companies waste the gas and stick you with the loss, and make foreign polluters pay at the border instead of Montana families at the pump.
Every dollar spent getting ready returns $3.75 in damage avoided.
Environment & Conservation Conservation isn’t partisan; it’s a responsibility.
I’m a third-generation protector of life, and I can’t stand a bully, and right now, Montana’s environment is being bullied.
My husband spent his career as a federal agent, including with the EPA, and refused to be part of rolling those protections back.
I bring that same conviction.
Immigration & Border Security Secure the border, fix a broken system, treat people like people.
I double-majored in Latin American Studies and Criminal Justice because I wanted to take down drug lords for a living.
My family was stationed at the U.S.
Embassy in Bogotá during the Escobar years, and I saw up close how America’s own drug demand, and our government’s meddling in the region,… Accessibility & Disability Rights Nothing about us without us.
I don’t come to this from a briefing binder; I come to it from showing up, even when it’s inconvenient, and listening directly to disabled Montanans and their families about what their lives actually require.
Courts & Judicial Reform Restore trust in the courts.
A Court that accepts undisclosed gifts and overturns long-settled rights has lost the confidence of many Americans.
I support real reform to restore that trust.
National Security & War Powers Congress must reclaim its war powers.
The founders made going to war hard on purpose; it was meant to take Congress, and the whole country, deciding together.
Over the decades we’ve drifted away from that, into a country that can stay at war for twenty years on a single vote most people never debated.
LGBTQ+ Montanans Live and let live, and that applies to everyone.
Every human being deserves basic dignity and respect.
This is about human rights, and I will never tolerate hate of any kind, projected at any group of people.
Military & Veterans Alani Bankhead’s military plan: fix how housing allowances are set, let the food allowance count the whole family, pay the full cost of military moves, premium-free health care and GI Bill parity for the Guard and Reserve, on-time pay for every uniformed service, child care help at every duty station, spouse careers that survive a move, protect the independent prosecutors who handle military sexual assault cases, and pass the Major Richard Star Act.
Israel & Palestine Not our wars: stop underwriting the suffering in Gaza.
I spent my career protecting children from the worst things adults do to them, so I am not going to look away from this.
What is being done to the children of Gaza is not an accident of war.
A United Nations commission has found it deliberate, and found that it meets the definition of genocide… Energy Affordable, reliable, Montana-made, with real choices.
Montana’s electric cooperatives were built on a simple idea: neighbors banding together to power the places the big utilities wouldn’t.
That same spirit should guide our energy future, and my first priority is keeping power affordable and reliable.
Rural families and farmers already stretch every… Special Education Keep the promise Washington made to our kids.
Every parent of a child with an IEP knows the drill: fight to get the services, then fight again to keep them.
Washington promised to pay 40% of the cost and pays less than 12%, so that fight lands on Montana classrooms and Montana families.
Jobs & Wages A day’s work should cover a day’s life.
Nobody working full-time in Montana should live in poverty, and right now too many do.
Work should pay enough to keep a roof overhead and food on the table.
For some reason, people are now calling ideas like this radical, but they used to be common-sense, nonpartisan beliefs this country always held to.
Iran Not our war: an illegal one Montana is paying for.
I am completely opposed to this war.
Trump’s own Director of National Intelligence, Tulsi Gabbard, testified that the intelligence community assessed Iran was not building a nuclear weapon.
The President overrode his own intelligence chief and took us to war anyway, with no imminent threat and no authorization from Congress… Foreign Aid Foreign aid is cheap security, and Montana grows it.
Foreign aid is one of the cheapest, most effective national-security tools we have.
It heads off wars, famines, epidemics, and mass migration before they ever reach us or cost us a single soldier… Foreign Policy America first, not America alone.
America First has to mean an America that leads, not one that walks away.
Our strength has never been just our military; it has been our allies and the respect we earned by doing right by others.
We protect ourselves by protecting them… Trade Fair trade, lower prices.
Start with your receipt: a blanket tariff is just a tax on your groceries, and it costs Montana farmers the export markets they spent decades building.
Our job in the world is not to stop other countries from using or trading their own resources.
Technology & National Security Lead on the tech that will decide the century.
The next century will be decided by who leads on artificial intelligence, quantum computing, and biotechnology, and I do not intend to hand that lead to China.
I spent my career keeping our most sensitive technology out of our adversaries’ hands… Corruption & Financial Crimes Follow the money.
If you want to catch the worst people on earth, you follow the money.
That is not a slogan for me, it is how I did my job.
Going after the financial-crimes angle was the single most effective way to take down traffickers and the people who abuse children… Rural Montana Out here, an emergency is a math problem.
How far, how fast, and is anyone coming.
In too much of Montana the honest answer is getting worse.
We are losing the ambulances, the air service, and the hospitals that decide whether a heart attack or a car wreck two hours from a city is survivable.
I am not going to let Washington treat those as luxuries to cut… Rule of Law No one is above the law.
Checks and balances, and equal justice, are not partisan.
No one, at any level, gets to put themselves above the law.
Elections Free, fair, and local.
Montana runs Montana's elections.
Protect the right to vote, restore the Voting Rights Act, and keep the president out of your voter file and the machines.
Dental, Vision & Hearing Your body doesn't come in separate plans.
Put dental, vision, and hearing into Medicare, and require real coverage in private plans too, not skimpy add-ons.
Mental Health We don't hide our struggles.
We treat them.
Treat mental health like the health care it is: real coverage, enforced parity, and providers who can actually reach rural Montana.
Insurance Denials Doctors decide, not algorithms.
Care your doctor ordered shouldn't be denied by an algorithm.
Open the black box, and hold insurers accountable when appeals overturn their denials.
Women's Health Women's health is not "DEI." It's how half this country survives and thrives.
Cutting women's health research and care doesn't save money; it costs lives, and it deserves to be funded like it.
Clean Water & Polluters Pay Polluters pay, not you.
Montana's water is not for sale or for dumping.
The people who profit from pollution clean up their own mess, not taxpayers.
Tar-Sands Pipeline For Montana oil.
Against being Canada's tar-sands doormat.
I'm for the Bakken and Montana energy.
I won't trade our rivers to be a transit corridor for Canada's dirtiest crude.
Medicaid Work Requirements Don't lose your healthcare to a paperwork trap.
So-called work requirements are red tape that kicks working people, caregivers, and the sick off coverage.
Montana already learned that lesson.
Wildfire & Forest Health Protect the home and the town first.
What saves a house is the house itself: a metal roof, ember-proof vents, and defensible space.
So help Montanans harden their homes, and fix the FEMA and insurance mess that leaves burned-out families waiting.
Do the science-based fuels work and the good fire that stop the high-intensity megafire, pay the volunteers and wildland firefighters who actually show up like the public servants they are, and give timber families real, year-round careers doing the work the woods need now.
Privacy & Data Rights Your data is yours.
It's not for sale.
Montana wrote privacy into our constitution.
Take that standard national: one clear privacy law, a warrant before the government tracks you, and an end to your every move being bought and sold.
Child Care Child care is the smartest investment Montana can make.
52% of Montana counties are child-care deserts.
Fund care where it doesn't exist, cap what families pay, and pay the people who do the work.
That's not a handout; it's a return.
Government That Works Fix the government, don't break it.
DOGE faked $110 billion in savings, fired the watchdogs, and copied 300 million Americans' records.
Investigate the damage, restore the inspectors general, and do real efficiency, not vandalism with a spreadsheet.
Civil Rights The law still protects you.
Someone has to enforce it.
Nobody repealed the Civil Rights Act.
They just fired the people whose job is to defend it.
Rebuild the offices that enforce your right not to be discriminated against.
Criminal Justice Reform Make the system work the way it should.
A justice system has two jobs: stop the people who actually hurt others, and be fair to everyone it touches.
Ours is failing at both.
Serious crimes go unsolved while we send people back to prison for breaking a rule of their parole, like missing a check-in when their car breaks down, not for hurting anyone.
Human Trafficking & Modern Slavery Follow the money, free the people.
This was my work for more than twenty years, and it taught me the thing most people get wrong about this crime: it is a business.
It runs on profit, it is closer to home than we let ourselves believe, and most of it is labor, not only what you picture.
Labor & the Right to Organize Montana already got this right.
Montana is not a right-to-work state.
It is the only state where, after a probation period, you can’t be fired without good cause, and its minimum wage rises with the cost of living.
Keep Washington from repealing the deal Montana already made: block the National Right-to-Work Act, pass the Protect America’s Workforce Act and the PRO Act, keep the NLRB working, and pay prevailing wage on federal work.
Taxes & Fair Share No billionaire should pay a lower tax rate than a working family.
Pass the Ultra-Millionaire Tax Act, a small annual tax on fortunes over $50 million that leaves 99.85% of American households alone, close the carried-interest break, reject another round of top-heavy tax cuts, and fund Social Security for good by removing the wage cap.
Local News & Media Ownership Montana’s news should answer to Montana.
Write the 39% national TV ownership limit into law with no FCC waivers, give local, independent and employee buyers the first shot when stations are sold, create a Local Ownership Certificate so owners who sell to local owners, employees or a community nonprofit can defer the capital-gains tax, clear the path for community-owned nonprofit papers, and keep government out of what reporters write.
Artificial Intelligence Write the rules while we lead.
And a human stays in the loop.
Binding safety rules for the most capable AI systems, tested before deployment with incidents reported.
A human in the loop for every life-or-death decision, and nuclear decisions categorically human.
Your face, your voice, and your name belong to you: enforce the takedown law, give victims the right to sue, pass the NO FAKES Act, and outlaw counterfeit candidates with parody explicitly protected.
Where health, safety, or personal freedom is at stake, AI is proven safe in the job before it takes the job; everywhere else, Washington stays out of Montana's businesses.
