The three ballot questions I-194 The Montana Plan Nonpartisan Bans corporations and other "artificial persons" from giving to candidates, parties, or ballot campaigns in Montana elections.
CI-132 Nonpartisan Judicial Elections Nonpartisan Constitutional amendment keeping all Montana judicial elections nonpartisan.
Judges keep running without party labels.
CI-133 Protecting the Initiative Process Nonpartisan Constitutional amendment protecting citizens' right to initiative and referendum.
Requires open, timely handling of ballot issues and bars rejecting petitions over minor technicalities.

One local measure you won't see Butte-Silver Bow only Not on the November ballot Local Initiative 00-02 · Data centers Brought by 406 People First, it would require a special election before Butte-Silver Bow could approve a new or expanded data center, and it would take a two-thirds majority of everyone registered to vote in the county, not just of those who turn out.
On September 23 the Montana Supreme Court ordered it onto the ballot, then vacated that order two days later because ballots had already gone out to voters.
It will not be on your November ballot.
Organizers have asked the court to reconsider, so watch for what comes next.
