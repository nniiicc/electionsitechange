On the Issues Meet Jon Maples Raised in Panama City, Florida, Jon learned the value of hard work, faith, and personal responsibility early in life while working alongside his father and brothers in the family irrigation business.
Jon was a high school standout in football and basketball.
He earned a Basketball scholarship to Palm Beach Atlantic University, where he served as captain of the basketball team and earned All-American honors.
Jon married his college sweetheart, Kristi, shortly after graduation.
Together, The Maples’s built their careers, raised their family, and put down deep roots in Palm Beach County.
Jon is a financial planner in Palm Beach Gardens and Kristi is a science teacher at The King’s Academy.
Jon and his family live in Jupiter’s Abacoa neighborhood and enjoy playing pickleball and board games together.
