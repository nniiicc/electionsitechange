The energy in the room was undeniable.
People from throughout the community came together not simply to celebrate the primary result, but because they believe deeply in what comes next .
Community leaders, business owners, families, longtime supporters, new friends, and some of the most recognizable names in the district showed up ready to work and ready to do their part to flip House District 87 red and bring strong, principled conservative representation back home to the district .
The evening was also a reminder that the Maples campaign has grown into something much larger than one candidate .
The primary victory was built by volunteers, supporters, donors, neighbors, and voters who took responsibility for helping move the campaign forward—and Thursday night’s turnout showed that the momentum has only continued to grow .
During his remarks, Jon Maples spoke boldly about the mission ahead and the responsibility supporters share between now and Election Day .
He emphasized that winning the primary was an important milestone, but not the finish line .
With November 3 approaching, the campaign is focused on building on its grassroots strength and carrying that energy through the general election .
At the center of that effort is the +10 Maples Movement.
The idea is simple: Every supporter is encouraged to identify 10 friends, family members, neighbors, or coworkers and personally help make sure those 10 people understand what is at stake, have a plan to vote, and remain accountable through Election Day .
Ten people may seem small on its own .
Multiplied across a district, however, it becomes a movement .
The +10 strategy helped energize supporters during the primary, and the campaign is now challenging voters to expand it heading into November .
That sense of personal responsibility could be felt throughout Thursday night’s celebration .
Supporters were not simply asking whether they could help—they were asking what they could do next .
From hosting neighborhood meet and greets to knocking doors, making phone calls, sign waving, donating, and bringing new voters into the +10 Maples Movement, the campaign is entering the general election with a growing network of people ready to get involved .
