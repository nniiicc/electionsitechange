Amy Kippenbrock was born in Martin County and grew up in Jasper, where she learned the small-town Indiana values of faith, family, and hard work.
A lifelong Hoosier, she and her husband Kevin have raised three sons—Josh, Wylee, and Kuper—and are proud grandparents to two amazing granddaughters.
From her teen years living near Fort Hood while her father served in the Army, Amy developed a deep appreciation for veterans and the families who support them.
That experience shaped her commitment to service—a value that’s guided her ever since.
As Dubois County Clerk, Amy modernized local government, implemented vote centers, and strengthened election integrity while ensuring every citizen was treated with grace and respect.
Married to a second-generation small business owner, Amy understands the challenges southern Indiana families and entrepreneurs face every day.
Known for her integrity, work ethic, and conservative values, Amy is ready to bring her hands-on leadership to the Statehouse—listening, collaborating, and passionately serving the people of southwest Indiana.
