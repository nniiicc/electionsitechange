Find Your Polling Place Step 3 Mark Your Calendar Early voting begins Sunday, October 19, 2026.

Step 4 Bring Your ID Bring a valid form of identification if you are a first-time voter, or be prepared to sign an affidavit.
Most voters simply sign their name on the voter list.
