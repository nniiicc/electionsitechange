I trained as an automotive technician at Norwich Technical High School, earned my ASE certifications, and went to work in the industry.
Today I own and operate my own small business — so I know firsthand what it means to make payroll, price a job, and watch Hartford add costs to both.
“I want to represent the 8th District in Hartford - not represent Hartford in the 8th.” — Will Gifford My Mission Why I'm Running Hartford has a habit: pass the law, take the credit, and send you the bill.
The cost shows up on your property taxes, your electric bill, and your rent — but nobody's name is attached to it.
I'm running to attach names to bills, starting with mine.
I grew up here.
I've watched friends and family leave Connecticut because there was nothing here for them.
I'm not willing to accept that — and I'm not willing to watch it from the sidelines.
This campaign is about making Hartford answer to the 8th District, not the other way around.
As a proud Republican, I will always stand up for you and will never go along with any policies that could negatively impact our towns.
