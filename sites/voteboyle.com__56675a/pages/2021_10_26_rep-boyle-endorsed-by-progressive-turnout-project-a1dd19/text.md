Progressive Turnout Project is the largest voter contact organization in the country, specifically dedicated to mobilizing the Democratic Party and defending democracy.
“I’m honored to receive the endorsement of the Progressive Turnout Project, an organization that is on the forefront of organizing across our country to engage in the most effective and basic way to win elections: talking directly to voters,” said Congressman Boyle.
“Whether at the doorstep, on the phone or through other means, the way that we as Democrats will succeed in 2022 and beyond is by proactively engaging with voters on priority issues.
This is how we won control of the House in 2018, and how we defeated Donald Trump and won control of the Senate in 2020.”
“I look forward to working with the PTP over the next year to activate as many volunteers to reach voters as possible in not only Philadelphia, but across Pennsylvania and beyond.”
