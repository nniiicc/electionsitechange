Meet Khristian Khristian Silvis knows what it means to serve.
From her time as a U.S.
Army combat medic to her role as District Director for U.S.
Representative Hillary Scholten, she has built a career on leadership, resilience, and putting people first.
In these roles, she has worked tirelessly to support veterans, strengthen communities, and ensure that every voice is heard.
Now, Khristian is bringing that same commitment to Michigan’s 84th House District, running to champion policies that support families, ensure quality education, and build stronger, safer neighborhoods.
