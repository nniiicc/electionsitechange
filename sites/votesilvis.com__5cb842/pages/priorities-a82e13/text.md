As a mother of two young children, Khristian knows how important it is that our future generations receive the best education by: Expanding career and technical education opportunities to prepare students for the jobs of the 21st century.
Ensuring all forms of higher education – whether they be college, community college, or skilled trades – are affordable and accessible.
Increasing the per-pupil funding for our K-12 public schools and keep it up with inflation.
Supporting our teachers with fair pay.
Protecting Governor Whitmer’s universal free breakfast and lunch program.
Investing in Our Veterans & First Responders Having served as a US Army Medic, Khristian knows what it means to put your life on the line in service of those around you.
In the State House, she’ll look after the veterans and first responders in our community by: Expanding access to veteran mental health resources.
Ensuring adequate resources to agencies that provide outreach and services to veterans and first responders.
Expanding existing college tuition assistance programs to include the children of disabled veterans.
Ensuring our first responders receive the resources and support they need to keep our communities safe.
Expanding Economic Opportunities for West Michigan Every year, West Michigan continues to make a case for itself as the ideal destination for families and businesses looking to settle down.
As State Representative, Khristian would work to keep the momentum going by: Funding new potential economic opportunities for the Grandville, Walker, and NW Grand Rapids area.
Upgrading our aging infrastructure to reflect the growing population and business needs.
Expanding access to public transportation systems which connect all our communities.
Promoting family-oriented policies like paid family leave and expanded daycare access to incentivize more families to start a life here in West Michigan.
Protecting Michigan's Environment for Future Generations Michigan’s natural beauty is part of who we are, from our lakes and rivers to our neighborhoods, parks, and farmland.
Khristian knows that protecting our environment means protecting our health, our economy, and our future by: Protecting our Great Lakes and local waterways from pollution.
Supporting clean energy solutions that create Michigan jobs.
Working to ensure every community has access to safe, reliable drinking water.
Encouraging responsible development that balances growth with environmental stewardship.
Access to Affordable & Quality Healthcare Access to affordable, high-quality healthcare is essential for every family.
Khristian is committed to protecting healthcare, lowering costs, and ensuring West Michigan families can get the care they need close to home.
As State Representative, she will prioritize a stronger, more affordable healthcare system by: Protecting Michiganders’ healthcare from policies, including impacts at the Federal level that could increase costs or threaten coverage.
Lowering prescription drug prices so families and seniors can afford the medications they need.
Supporting local healthcare providers by working with hospitals, healthcare facilities and professionals to strengthen care in our community.
Expanding access to care , including primary care, mental health services, and preventive healthcare.
