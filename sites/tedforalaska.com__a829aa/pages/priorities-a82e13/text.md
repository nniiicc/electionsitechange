Increase funding for education As a longtime teacher, Ted knows the importance of ensuring schools are properly funded.
Ted believes in the potential of our kids, and if elected will seek to provide sustainable, high-quality public education for all Alaskan students.
To attract and retain the best teachers, Ted supports unions and a defined benefit pension program for state employees.
Protect women’s right to choose In the wake of the Supreme Court’s decision to overturn Roe v.
Wade, Ted will fight for Alaskan women’s access to safe, affordable healthcare.
Ted believes that all people should have the freedom and privacy to make choices about their bodies and health.
The government should not be telling people how to live their lives!
Keep our community safe & secure Ted has heard your concerns, and if elected he will make it a goal to improve funding for public safety in Northeast Anchorage.
Ted will fight for increased funding to treat mental health and substance abuse to get people off of the streets and into affordable housing.
Ted is proud to have voted for HB78, the public employee defined benefits pension bill, so we can fully staff our first responders and all vacant public employee positions for the service we deserve.
Ensure a PFD that works for Alaskans Ted believes that the people of Alaska deserve a robust PFD.
Ted will work to ensure a fair PFD, as well as making sure Alaskans get their fair share from oil revenues.
Invest in new infrastructure & create jobs Ted wants to see greater investment in Northeast Anchorage, including improvements to aging roads, local energy supplies, and high-speed internet.
To deal with the impending natural gas crisis in South Central Alaska, Ted will seek to improve energy infrastructure, diversify energy sources, and fight for reliable and affordable energy for our community.
Ted also believes in investing in the Port for Alaska, and will look for ways to support small businesses and promote job growth in the district.
