A Life of Service A Life of Service Alex Hawkins is a veteran and community leader running for the Michigan House of Representatives to deliver practical, people-first solutions for District 55.
Alex's commitment to public service is rooted in a simple belief: leadership means showing up, listening, and doing the work.
Whether serving his country or his community, Alex brings a steady, accountable approach focused on results, not rhetoric.
Experience Trained to Lead Under Pressure Military Service Alex is a U.S.
Army veteran and an active National Guard Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) officer.
In that role, he's entrusted with safeguarding lives by neutralizing threats in high stress, high-risk environments.
Public Service Alex's service record extends beyond the military.
He worked in Congress, serving under then Congresswoman Elissa Slotkin and Senator Gary Peters, where he fought for constituents, advocated for veterans, working families, and accountability in government.
Community Involvement Alex has been actively engaged in veterans' advocacy, environmental conservation efforts, union advocacy, and mentorship programs for young leaders.
His time as a cadet and mentor in the Civil Air Patrol has given Alex the opportunity to help develop the next generation of leaders.
Core Values What Guides Alex's Vision for District 55 Service Before Self From the battlefield to the halls of Congress, Alex has always put service to others first.
Leadership means making sacrifices for the greater good.
Fighting for Working Families Alex believes in fair wages, safe working conditions, and opportunities for all.
He stands with unions and workers who build our communities.
Results, Not Politics Alex isn't running to play partisan games.
He's running to deliver real results for Michigan families who deserve better.
