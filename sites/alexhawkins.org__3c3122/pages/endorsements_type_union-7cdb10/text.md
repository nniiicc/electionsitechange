All Endorsements Elected Officials Labor Unions Organizations Community Leaders Michigan Building & Construction Trades Council Labor Unions The Michigan Building and Construction Trades Council is proud to officially endorse you for State House District 55.
You have demonstrated a strong commitment to working families, responsible economic development, and policies that strengthen our community while supporting good-paying union jobs.
Your leadership, integrity, and willingness to listen to residents make you the right choice to be the next State Representative for District 55.
As representatives of the skilled trades, we understand the importance of leaders who value infrastructure investment, workforce development, public safety, and fair labor standards.
You recognize the critical role union labor plays in building and maintaining strong communities, and you have shown commitment to ensuring that Michigan continues to grow in a way that benefits working people and families.
We believe that you will continue to bring thoughtful leadership, accountability, and a collaborative approach to this office.
You understand the challenges facing residents and are prepared to advocate for practical solutions that improve the quality of life throughout Michigan.
The Michigan Building and Construction Trades Council proudly stands with Alex Hawkins and encourages all residents to support his candidacy.
Michigan AFL-CIO Labor Unions On behalf of the Michigan AFL-CIO and our active and retired members, I am pleased to announce our endorsement of your candidacy for State Representative for the 2026 elections.
We believe that you have demonstrated an understanding of the issues facing working families.
We know that you will be a tireless advocate for working people, protect their right to join in union, and help them protect the pay and benefits that allow them to sustain their families.
We ask that, if elected, you continue to work with us to achieve these goals.
National Association of Social Workers - Michigan Chapter Labor Unions I am pleased to announce that you have received the endorsement of the political action committee of the National Association of Social Workers – Michigan Chapter (M-PACE: Political Action for Candidate Election) for your 2026 campaign for the Michigan House of Representatives, District 55.
NASW-Michigan’s membership of over 4,500 social workers join me in enthusiastic support for the next stage in your campaign.
Social workers are the largest providers of mental health services in the United States and can be found in virtually every setting across our state; including schools, hospitals, community mental health centers, jails, nursing facilities, and even in the legislature.
Michigan Nurses Association Labor Unions On behalf of the Michigan Nurses Association, I’m pleased to share that the MNA Board of Directors has voted to endorse you in your race for State Representative.
As the largest union and professional association for registered nurses and healthcare professionals in Michigan, MNA is proud to support candidates who share our values and are committed to advocating for safe patient care, safe workplaces, and strong collective bargaining rights.
We look forward to working with you to advance policies that support nurses, healthcare professionals, and the patients and communities we serve.
Michigan Professional Fire Fighters Union Labor Unions On behalf of the 7,500 active and retired members of the Michigan Professional Fire Fighters Union, we are proud to endorse your candidacy for State Representative in the 55th District in the upcoming general election.
Our members are confident, if elected, that you will continue to be a strong advocate for fire fighters—supporting safe staffing levels, protecting collective bargaining rights, and standing up for their interests in the Legislature.
We also appreciate your commitment to supporting working people across Michigan.
