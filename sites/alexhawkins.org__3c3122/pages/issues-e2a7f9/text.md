The Issues Why Alex Is Running Too many families feel like the system isn't working for them.
Alex is running to change that.
He's spent his life showing up when it matters - in uniform, in his community, and alongside people doing the hard work that keeps Michigan moving.
You deserve a state representative who listens, follows through, and puts people over politics.
Alex will bring steady, pragmatic leadership to Lansing, and fight for our community every single day.
What Alex Is Fighting For Strong Schools Put classrooms first, not politics or bureaucracy.
That means backing teachers, protecting public schools, and making sure every student has access to the support and opportunities they need to succeed.
Safe, Healthy Communities Public safety starts with prevention.
I will fight for mental health services, violence prevention, and resources that keep our neighborhoods, schools, and workplaces safe, without playing political games with public safety.
A Fair, Working Economy Working people are doing everything right and still falling behind.
I will stand up to powerful special interests, crack down on rigged systems, and fight to lower the everyday costs hitting families the hardest.
Government Accountability Too often, politicians make promises and walk away.
I will push for transparency, real accountability, and a government that answers to the people, not lobbyists or insiders.
