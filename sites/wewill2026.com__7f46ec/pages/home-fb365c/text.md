Platform
It’s time that DC started working for Mid-Michigan families, not billionaires in New York and California.

He will have four major priorities in DC:
1.
Lower the cost of living.
Everything is getting too expensive and Mid-Michigan families like us are feeling the crunch.
We need to:
- Protect social security by taxing the wealthy

- Guarantee healthcare for all
- Stabilize prices & markets
2.
Protect Pure Michigan.
Michigan’s water and farmland and our health are under attack from multi-national corporations, and too many politicians are afraid to stand up for Michiganders.
Will isn’t one of them.

- Pass a 12-month federal moratorium on new data centers

- Hold polluters accountable
3.
Say No to endless war.

That’s a lie.
We need to get our priorities straight: stop bombing kids overseas, start delivering for American families.

Restore the rule of law.
DC politicians are letting billionaires, bullies, and corrupt politicians get away with violating our constitutional rights and engaging in criminal behavior.

- Prosecute the Epstein class for human trafficking & tax evasion
