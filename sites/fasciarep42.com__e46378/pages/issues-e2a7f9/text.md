Casey's Law
June 24, 2026
Following passage by the General Assembly on June 10, Gov.
Daniel J.
McKee has signed “Casey’s Law,” legislation sponsored by Rep.
Arthur J.
Corvese and Senator David P.
Tikoian to add extra penalties for motor vehicle offenses when they involve road rage.
The bill is named in memory of Casey Bassignani, a 23-year-old Johnston woman who was killed in November in a collision that witnesses said occurred when another driver forced her car off Route 295 in Cranston.
The bill signing ceremony at the State House was attended by members of Bassignani’s family, including her parents David and Lynn Bassignani, who testified at the bill’s hearings.
“Road rage incidents are increasingly common, incredibly dangerous and entirely avoidable.
Road rage is a choice, and this bill is aimed at sending a message to drivers to choose differently,” said Rep.
Corvese (D-Dist. 55, North Providence).
“Enhanced penalties will act as a deterrent to encourage drivers to stop themselves before they do something impulsive and reckless on the road.
It’s my hope that Casey Bassignani’s legacy will be safer roads where cooler heads prevail.”
“We’re just happy Casey’s legacy will live on through this law,” added Lynn Bassignani.
In his remarks at the bill signing, Rep.
Corvese thanked his colleague, Representative Richard Fascia, for his support of the legislation.
Rep.
Fascia also attended the bill signing.

FROM THE HOUSE MINORITY OFFICE OF THE RI HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES
Kudos Representative Richard Fascia!!
Bills creating special license plates for disability access and to benefit RAMP ceremonially signed into law
STATE HOUSE — Legislation from Sen.
Lori Urso and Rep.
Earl A.
Read III creating special license plates for vehicles adapted for those with disabilities and from Sen.
Andrew R.
Dimitri and Rep.
Richard R.
Fascia creating special license plates to benefit the charity Real Access Motivates Progress (RAMP) were signed into law today at a ceremony at the Cranston Headquarters of the Rhode Island DMV.
The bill (2026-S 2185, 2026-H 7229) sponsored by Senator Dimitri and Representative Fascia empowers the Division of Motor Vehicles to make available special motor vehicle registration plates to benefit RAMP, a volunteer organization that works for break down barriers for those living with disabilities in Rhode Island.
“I am extremely proud to have sponsored legislation to create a RAMP charity license plate, which will generate critical awareness and support for accessibility in our communities,” said Senator Dimitri (D-Dist. 25, Johnston).
“Like so many other great causes, I am grateful that Rhode Islanders will now have the opportunity to display their support for this outstanding organization on their vehicles.”
Said Representative Fascia (R-Dist. 42, Johnston, Cranston), “I am very proud to have sponsored this important legislation in the Rhode Island House of Representatives.
This registration plate will not only help raise funds for RAMP but more importantly, it will highlight the innovative efforts of RAMP to break barriers and champion accessibility for the disabled.
As important contributors to our communities, this special plate will help to support a more equitable future for our disabled friends and neighbors.”
