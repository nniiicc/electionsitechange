The 12th Plymouth District
The 12th Plymouth District consists of Kingston; Plympton; Plymouth (Precincts 1, 2, 3, 5 and 13), Middleboro (Precinct 1 & 5A), Halifax (Precinct 1 & 2A) and Pembroke (Precinct 3).
This reflects official redistricting completed in 2023.
