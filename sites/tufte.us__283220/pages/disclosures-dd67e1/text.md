Judicial conduct Judicial candidates in North Dakota are governed by the North Dakota Code of Judicial Conduct, Canon 4.
Justice Tufte’s campaign is conducted in compliance with these rules, which prohibit, among other things, pledges or promises regarding how a candidate would rule on any issue that may come before the court.
The full Code of Judicial Conduct is available at ndcourts.gov .
