about Esther
Esther Fishbaugh, a distinguished Chief Executive Officer with an impressive 42-year career in campground management within the hospitality sector, brings a wealth of experience and knowledge to the political arena.
Having successfully managed contracts with the Federal Government, she has developed the patience and expertise necessary to navigate and collaborate with government agencies effectively.
Born and raised in a farming community north of Great Falls, Esther's deep-rooted connection to Montana is evident through her education at the College of Great Falls and Montana State University.
As a proud fourth-generation Montanan, her upbringing instilled in her a strong work ethic and a commitment to doing what is right, even in the face of adversity.
As a Representative, Esther's primary objective is to reform Montana's government by addressing corruption and restoring the core values that define the Montanan way of life.
With her background in business and her unwavering dedication to her community, she is poised to make a lasting impact on the state's political landscape.
