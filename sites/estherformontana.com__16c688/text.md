Esther Fishbaugh
As a proud Republican candidate for Legislative House District 62, I'm committed to fighting for the values that matter most to our community.
I'm dedicated to representing your voice in Helena.
Transparency in government is essential to holding our elected officials accountable.
That's why I'm calling for mandatory online posting of all budget proposals and past expenditures for every government entity and school.
You have the right to know how your tax dollars are being spent!
And speaking of taxes, it's time to put the brakes on out-of-control property tax increases.
I propose requiring taxing authorities to itemize all features that are included in a property's valuation to be included on each tax bill and I will work to cap valuation increases.
As a champion of life, I believe in protecting the unborn and providing mothers in difficult situations acceptable alternatives to abortion.
I'm proud to have earned the endorsement of the Montana Family Institute for my unwavering stance on this critical issue.
Let's work together to build a brighter future for Montana.
Vote for me in 2026 and let's make our voices heard!
