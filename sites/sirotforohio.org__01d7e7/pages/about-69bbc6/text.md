Mother, Scientist, Educator,
Animal Lover & Children’s Advocate
Living in rural Ohio is incredibly special.
I continue to be struck by the beauty and abundance of the farmland surrounding me, as well as the charm and vibrancy of the small towns nestled within.
How fortunate we are to live in community with neighbors who come together to support one another time and time again.
In times of celebration and uplifting, as well as in times of tragedy and need, there is a strength of camaraderie here—a sense that we’re in it together.
And that is what drives me to serve as your next State Senator.
My career as an entomologist (bug scientist), researcher and educator is rooted in thinking both critically and creatively to solve problems.
I have learned that transparency and maintaining a shared pool of information is foundational to the crucial conversations needed for finding pathways forward—collaboration is the most direct way to solving the challenges we face.
My experience has prepared me to represent everyone in our district with integrity and fairness.
