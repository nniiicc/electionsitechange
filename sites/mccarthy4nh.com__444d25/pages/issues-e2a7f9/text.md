Dian knows these challenges aren't abstract: she's lived them, worked through them, and spent decades helping her community navigate them.
In the State Senate, Dian will be laser-focused on the issues that matter most.
Lowering Property Taxes Making Healthcare Accessible and Affordable Lowering Costs for Granite Staters Strengthening Our Public Schools Supporting Small Businesses Investing in Families and Mental Health Lowering Property Taxes After years on the school board and budget committee, Dian knows how rising property taxes strain families and push seniors out of their homes.
She'll fight to restore the state's fair share of funding so your town—and your tax bill—gets the relief it deserves.
Making Healthcare Accessible and Affordable As a breast cancer survivor, Dian knows personally how critical access to quality, affordable healthcare can be.
She'll fight to lower costs, protect Medicaid, and push back against Republican policies that have made care harder to find and harder to afford for every Granite Stater.
Lowering Costs for Granite Staters From housing to healthcare to energy bills, the cost of living in New Hampshire is squeezing families.
Dian will fight to put more money back in your pocket.
Strengthening Our Public Schools With years of school board leadership, Dian knows that strong, well-funded neighborhood schools are the foundation of a thriving community, and she'll stand up against cuts and giveaways that leave our kids behind.
Supporting Small Businesses Dian has lived the challenges of running a small business in New Hampshire.
She'll fight for policies that protect Main Street from rising costs, reckless tariffs, and an economy rigged for the ultra-wealthy and out-of-state corporations.
Investing in Families and Mental Health After a decade on the front lines of mental health, substance misuse, and juvenile justice, Dian understands the gaps in our system.
She'll work to close them so every family in New Hampshire has the support they need.
