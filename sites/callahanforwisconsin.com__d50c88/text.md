WHY I'M
RUNNING
I am running for Re-Election to the Wisconsin State Assembly, I came to you five years ago with a vision that there should be no reason why your kids, grandkids, and great-grandchildren can't raise their families here at home in northern Wisconsin.
During this last session, we have made tremendous strides toward that vision.
I was proud to be a part of, co-authored, and voted for:
- The largest tax cut in state history, which was a Historic $4.4 billion cut for WI families and businesses, and voted for tax-free retirement income.
- Fought and held the University system accountable until one-third of DEI positions on campuses across the state were removed.
- Repealed the personal property tax so businesses can use that money to invest in their company, their employees, and their communities.
- I voted for and demanded that the DNR implement a wolf population goal after they removed it, so Wisconsin is prepared to implement a hunt when the wolf is removed from the endangered species list.
- Voted and co-authored legislation banning gender-altering surgeries for minors and legislation protecting women's sports by banning men from competing in those sports.
- Voting for our fair share of funding and supporting a massive, shared revenue increase.
A minimum increase of 20% in shared revenue for local governments statewide.
Many in the 35th District are seeing much higher returns than that.
- Invested Over $90 million in new money for childcare, helping families afford care, supporting worker recruitment/retention, and increasing childcare capacity.
- Signing on to two resolutions supporting Texas Gov.
Greg Abbott in his effort to stand up and protect the U.S. border with Mexico and requesting Gov.
Evers to send the National Guard to the southern border to aid in those efforts.
- And finally, voting for 17 pieces of legislation that would strengthen our elections and ensure Election integrity.
I will continue to demand fiscal responsibility and fight for our vision.
Cut taxes, stand up for our sporting heritage, fight for our fair share of funding, and fight for our conservative values.
I ask for your vote in August and November because together, and only together, can we continue to make the 35th Assembly a great place to live, work, and raise your families, as well as build a better Wisconsin!
Thank you!
I ask for your vote in August and in November!
