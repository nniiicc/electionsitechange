These priorities reflect the conservative values that have made our state strong and represent my commitment to building a future where families thrive, businesses prosper, and individual liberty is protected.
Together, let us forge a path towards a brighter future, where the values of our community are upheld and the voices of every citizen are heard and respected.
Your voice and your vote are essential to making this vision a reality.
Pro-Life Every human life has value and deserves protection under the law.
As your representative, I will champion legislation that defends the sanctity of life from conception to natural death, while supporting policies that strengthen Missouri families.
This includes ensuring that taxpayer funds are not used to support abortion providers and promoting other maternity, educational, and adoption services as positive alternatives.
Strong families are the foundation of strong communities, and government should support - not undermine - the traditional family structure that has served our society well for generations.
Your Vote Matters: Support candidates who will stand firm on life issues and vote for family-friendly policies that reflect Missouri values.
Religious Liberty and Free Speech The First Amendment guarantees our freedom of religion and speech - rights so important that our founding fathers listed them in the Bill of Rights.
Missourians should be free to practice their faith without government interference, whether in their personal lives, businesses, or community organizations.
Similarly, the right to express viewpoints - even unpopular ones - must be protected from censorship and political correctness.
These freedoms form the foundation of our democratic society, allowing open debate and the free exercise of conscience that make America strong.
Your Vote Matters: Support candidates who will defend your right to worship freely and speak your mind without government interference.
Second Amendment Rights The right to keep and bear arms is fundamental to American liberty and personal protection.
Missouri families should be able to protect liberty, themselves, families, hunt, and engage in sporting activities without unreasonable government restrictions.
Responsible gun ownership is a constitutional right that law-abiding citizens exercise safely every day.
Rather than punishing legal gun owners with more regulations, we should focus on enforcing existing laws against criminals who misuse firearms.
Our state constitution reinforces these federal protections, and Missouri should lead the way in defending Second Amendment rights.
Your Vote Matters: Choose representatives who understand that the Second Amendment protects law-abiding citizens and will resist efforts to restrict your gun rights.
Constitutional Principles and Limited Government The Constitution established a system of limited government with specific powers, reserving other rights to states and individuals.
When government exceeds these boundaries, it threatens the liberty that generations of Americans have fought to preserve.
This means supporting federalism, protecting states' rights, and ensuring that government power remains accountable to the people.
Constitutional principles should guide all legislation, and elected officials must remember they serve the people, not the other way around.
Your Vote Matters: Elect representatives who respect constitutional limits on government power and will fight federal overreach.
Economic Growth and Business Development Missouri's economy thrives when government gets out of the way of job creators and entrepreneurs.
Excessive regulations, high taxes, and burdensome licensing requirements prevent businesses from growing and creating good-paying jobs for Missouri families.
By reducing red tape, streamlining regulations, and maintaining competitive tax rates, we can attract new businesses and help existing ones expand.
Free market capitalism has proven to be the most effective system for reducing poverty and creating widespread prosperity.
Your Vote Matters: Support candidates who understand that a strong economy benefits everyone and will work to remove barriers to business growth.
Educational Excellence and Parental Choice Every child deserves access to quality education that prepares them for success in life.
Public schools serve many families well and our state constitution puts a high priority on funding our schools.
However, one size does not fit all when it comes to education.
Parents - not government bureaucrats - should decide what educational option works best for their children, whether that's traditional public school, private schools, homeschooling, or faith-based education.
For our public schools, local school boards should make most educational decisions, not distant state officials.
Our communities elect our school board members to make decisions and to be held accountable to those decisions.
The state of Missouri needs to get out of the way and let them do their jobs.
Your Vote Matters: Choose representatives who trust parents to make educational decisions and support local schools making their own decisions.
Agriculture and Rural Communities Missouri's agricultural sector contributes around $94 billion to our state economy and helps feed our nation, yet farmers face increasing government regulations that threaten their livelihoods.
Our farm families are the best stewards of the land because their success depends on it.
Reducing unnecessary environmental regulations, protecting property rights, and supporting energy independence through domestic production will help rural communities thrive while maintaining responsible stewardship of our natural resources.
Your Vote Matters: Back candidates who understand rural Missouri and will fight against excessive regulations on farmers and energy producers.
Election Inte grity Every eligible citizen should have confidence that their vote counts and that elections are conducted fairly and transparently.
This requires common-sense measures like voter ID requirements, accurate and secure voter rolls, and secure ballot handling procedures.
Your Vote Matters: Support candidates who will strengthen election security and ensure government remains accountable to the people.
