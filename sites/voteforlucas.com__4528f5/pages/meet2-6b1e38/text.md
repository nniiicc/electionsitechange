Meet Lucas
Rooted in Faith and Family
Born in 1981 and baptized at St.
Paul Lutheran Church in Jackson, Lucas was raised with strong Christian values in rural Crump, Missouri, alongside his five siblings.
This foundation of faith continues to guide his life today as he served as parish council president at Immaculate Conception in Jackson, where he and his family are active members.
Lucas and his wife Sarah both teach Sunday school and lead bible studies - she works with 5th and 6th graders while he teaches both adult classes and substitutes.
They also serve as chaperones for youth events, investing in the next generation.
Lucas is also a member of the local Knights of Columbus Council.
Lucas's commitment to Christian service extended beyond his community when he served as a summer missionary in Nicaragua in 2002 and 2004 with the American Baptists, managing construction projects and assisting visiting missionary teams.
This experience reinforced his dedication to serving others and putting faith into action.
Lucas and Sarah have been blessed with four children: Joseph, Lucas Jr, Samuel, and Mary.
As a former public-school teacher and breast cancer survivor, Sarah brings strength and perspective to their family that has shaped Lucas's understanding of the challenges facing Missouri families today.
Military Service and Sacrifice
At just 17 years old, Lucas answered the call to serve his country by joining the Missouri Army National Guard, dedicating 11 years of his life to military service.
His training as a Combat Engineer, Heavy Equipment Operator, and Topographic Engineer prepared him for multiple deployments that tested his character and commitment:
- Post-9/11 Olympic Security (2002): Deployed to provide armed security at the Winter Olympics
- Iraq - Operation Iraqi Freedom (2003-2004): Served 16 months as a Heavy Equipment Sergeant, earning an Army Commendation Medal for distinguished service
- Hurricane Katrina Relief (2005): Responded with the Missouri National Guard to help fellow Americans in their time of greatest need
As a Life Member of the Veterans of Foreign Wars (VFW) and member of the local American Legion, Lucas continues to stand with his fellow veterans.
Education and Professional Excellence
Lucas's educational journey began at Delta High School, where he played both basketball and baseball, learning the value of teamwork and perseverance.
He went on to earn his Bachelor's degree in Geography with a Mathematics Minor from the University of Missouri, followed by a Graduate Certificate in Systems Engineering from Colorado State University.
In 2012, he achieved Project Management Professional Certification, demonstrating his commitment to excellence in managing complex projects.
Proven Business Leadership
Today, Lucas and Sarah are proud owners of two Missouri businesses: the Sikeston Trade Fair, which they purchased from Lucas's retiring uncle in 2020, and Green's of Fruitland.
Operating these family businesses has given them firsthand understanding of the challenges facing Missouri's small business owners - from regulatory burdens to economic uncertainties that impact communities every day.
Prior to business ownership, Lucas spent over 15 years in the technology sector, advancing from technician to program director and specializing in Information Systems consultancy, data analysis, and project management.
His diverse experience includes working with:
- Military Ranges and International Weapons Safety
- Agriculture (Variable Spread Fertilizer Models) and Healthcare (HIPAA Compliance)
- State Government Operations and Emergency Response
- Facilities Management and much more
As part of corporate leadership teams, Lucas directed development in new technology and business lines that spun off of the companies as "tech startups" under Lucas's leadership.
This success was always by focusing on common sense solutions that deliver results.
A Problem Solver Ready to Serve
Throughout his military service and consulting career, colleagues have consistently recognized Lucas as a problem solver who efficiently guides people toward mutual goals.
Whether serving the nation overseas, helping communities recover from disaster, or growing businesses in the private sector, he has always focused on making situations better than he found them.
