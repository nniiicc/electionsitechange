My Beliefs
I am a West Virginian by choice!
I strongly believe that our Great State will flourish when we have good paying jobs for everyone!
Pro-life
Life begins at conception!
I have seen what abortion does, not only to the babies, but to the families.
The guilt and shame can leave huge scars for a woman, but what is seldom talked about is that men are also affected by this act.
As part of a family business, I can say: If only therapists and counselors could share those stories.
As the co-chair of the National Prayer Caucus and a member of the Faith-First Caucus, I am able to get input/support from Pastors all over the country on issues concerning the values of Christian Families
It is of the utmost importance to elect more God-fearing people into politics, because it’s in the numbers that we can bring back good laws to have balance in our lives.
Pro-family
We have children without food, no bed to sleep in, too many without parents, but blessed by their being cared for by their Grandparents, and Foster Parents.
It is difficult for me to see helping people from other countries when we have our own people living in abject poverty, many living in our streets and starving every day!
Pro-recovery
Having been part of the Celebrate Recovery program at Word of Life Church for over seven years has given me invaluable insights into the world of addiction.
It can happen to any one throughout any time in life, and it’s a carousel that no one wants to be on.
It takes a community to help the recovery process be successful.
Helping with the program had changed my heart and I want to enable others do the same.
Pro-second amendment
I believe that the Second Amendment is our most important and vital amendment for our Freedom and Democracy today!
Without our Rights under the Second Amendment – all the other amendments go along the wayside!
Without the ability to have guns, we would be enslaved by a runaway, socialist government.
All we have to do is look at the citizens of countries who have had their guns and their right to defend themselves taken away.
Yes, I am a member of the National Rifle Association (NRA), and a member of the West Virginia Citizens’ Defense League (WVCDL)!
I am also proudly endorsed by the Gun Owners of America (GOA).
