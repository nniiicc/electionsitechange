About Margitta Mazzocchi
A Life Built in West Virginia
I came to Logan County as a legal immigrant in 1999 and built my life here with my family.
Like so many in Southern West Virginia, I believe in hard work, faith, and community.
One of the most important days in my life was January 9, 2017—the day I became a citizen of the United States.
