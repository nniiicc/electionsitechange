Montana State AFL-CIO
- Do you support workers' freedom to join a union?
- Do you support majority sign up in union organizing drives?
- Do you oppose so-called right-to-work legislation in every form?
- Do you support the right of public employees to collectively bargain?
- Do you support funding infrastructure projects that generate good jobs?
- Do you support the responsible development of coal, rail transport of coal, industrial projects, and other traditional energy sources?
- Do you support the continued development of renewable energy and the creation of sustainable jobs that pay wages that will support a family, offer health insurance and provide defined benefit pensions?
- Do you support fully funding public education, public services, and higher education for all Montanans?
- Do you oppose efforts to weaken or repeal the Davis-Bacon Act that establishes prevailed wage rates?
- Do you support legislation that protects every eligible Montanan's right to vote?
- Do you oppose private school vouchers, tax credits, and private charter schools?
- Do you oppose any legislation that would encourage, expand, or mandate the contracting out or privatization of public services?
- Do you support legislation that promotes pay equity for women?
- Do you oppose tip credit for tipped employees?
- Do you oppose Montana’s current ban on Project Labor Agreement?
- Would you oppose bills that seek to weaken or eliminate Montana’s public employees’ retirement system?
