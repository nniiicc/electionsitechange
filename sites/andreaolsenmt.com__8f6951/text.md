A fifth-generation Montanan, Senator Andrea Olsen is working hard in the Montana Legislature for the people of Montana!
Andrea promotes, protects and honors the rights and dignity of all Montanans.
Andrea champions hardworking families as well as our most vulnerable.
Andrea advocates for accessible healthcare, affordable quality education, and a strong economy with good jobs.
Andrea believes that clean air, clean water, and access to public lands are Montana treasures vital to our quality of life.
During Andrea’s 10 years in the Montana Legislature, Olsen developed a track record of bringing people together to get things done, and do what’s right for Montana families.
A lifelong resident and homeowner in Missoula, Andrea will continue to work diligently for you.
