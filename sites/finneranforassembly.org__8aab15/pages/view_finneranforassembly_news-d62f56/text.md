For Immediate Release, June 5, 2026 Contact: Candidate Mary Finneran at finneranforassembly@gmail.com , 518-965-2935 Mary Finneran is beyond proud to announce she has picked up more endorsements, plus one designation.
She just received the Sierra Club endorsement, which is very special to her, as a lifetime Sierran and member of the executive and legislative committees.
In addition, following the May 31 three candidate "meet and greet", she received an endorsement from the Berne Democratic committee.
Jim Kaufman, chair of that committee, said "one candidate stood taller in my opinion because of the passion she demonstrated for the topics that were discussed.
She, more than the others, convinced me that she would fight to the finish on behalf of AD 102.
It is for that reason we decided to endorse Mary Finneran for Assembly in the upcoming primary." This brings the total endorsements to thirteen, having received the Eleanor's Legacy endorsement earlier, (a dual endorsement).
In addition to the endorsements, Finneran has also been designated a "Healthcare Champion" by the campaign for New York Health, an effort to make the NY Health Act law.
While not an endorsement, it recognizes Finneran's work in that regard over more than fifteen years.
Finneran is exeedingly grateful for the endorsements and recognition.
Julie Rockefeller (purposefully unaffiliated with any political party), said, “I have met her, I like her, and there is nothing in her platform with which I disagree.

For Immediate Release 2/24/26 Contact: Michael Gelfand, (518) 610-0326, gelgahan@verizon.net, finneranforassembly@gmail.com Candidate Mary Finneran wins the Working Families Party endorsement One day following the Finneran for Assembly campaign announcing with delight Mary Finneran's endorsement by Albany County Legislator William Reinhardt, Finneran was beyond elated on returning home from the first day of gathering ballot petition signatures to discover that she had been awarded the Working Families Party endorsement.
The candidate stated "The Working Family Party's determination to make affordability and quality in housing, childcare, healthcare and more a standard for all working people, along with their stalwart defense of immigrants and all people being oppressed, makes me beyond proud of this endorsement"; she added, "I am very inspired and energized to help the WFP achieve their goals." The WFP endorsement is Finneran's ninth.
Other endorsers include the upstate chapter of Third Act.
(Climate organizer Bill McKibben's organization) and the NY Progressive Action Network (NYPAN).
Along with Reinhardt, current and retired legislators Merton Simpson and Doug Bullock have also endorsed her, as has Elliot Adams, former mayor of Sharon Springs and past National President of Veterans for Peace.
Mary's home town Cairo Democratic Committee and former Cairo Town Supervisor, Ted Banta III were her earliest endorsers.
Very recently Reinhardt, when endorsing Finneran, said "We need more Progressive voices in the Assembly." The endorsement by the Working Families Party helps ensure this becomes a reality.
