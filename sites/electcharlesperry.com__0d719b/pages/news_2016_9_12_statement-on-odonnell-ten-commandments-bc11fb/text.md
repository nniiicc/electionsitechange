Home About Issues News Take Action Contact Donate Home About Issues News Take Action Contact Donate ELIZABETH CUSTY September 9, 2016 Statement on O'Donnell Ten Commandments ELIZABETH CUSTY September 9, 2016 For years the "Freedom From Religion Foundation" has fought to intimidate Christians into eliminating all public displays of faith from our society.
Last year they unsuccessfully targeted the Kountze Cheerleaders and the Childress Police Department.
Today, they are threatening O’Donnell High School with a lawsuit over their display of the Ten Commandments.
I am proud of the hundreds of students at O'Donnell that are standing up for their faith and starting a movement to save the Ten Commandments at their school.
Our office is working to ensure the school is in touch with the necessary experts to explain their rights and determine a plan of action.
