Donate Meet George I’m running to cast a bold, conservative vision for Arizona’s future and the North East Valley.
I’m proud to come from a line of courageous, freedom-loving Christians.
My family are Lebanese Christians, and I grew up hearing stories of my uncle who gave his life at 19 years old fighting for his family and Christian faith in the Lebanese Civil War.
My parents met in an underground bunker during that same war, got married, and legally came to the United States to raise their children in an environment where faith and family could be lived freely.
That’s the heritage of the Khalaf family and what I hope to pass on to my own children.
I’m proud to be a founder of The Resolute Group, First Day PR, and Data Orbital.
Our work has elected strong candidates, strengthened education freedom, and provided solid data on political races and key issues for Arizona and states across the country.
But over the last year, I’ve felt convicted that it’s time for me to put my name on the line to stand for the central truths and values that made our country and state the shining city that it has been for generations and to introduce common sense policies –even when they’re unpopular – that our state, our district, and Arizona families need to go into the future stronger and freer.
The policies I champion will be in service of that vision.
What defines me first and foremost is my faith and my family.
I’m a Christian and a believer in Jesus Christ.
I’m a proud husband to Briana and father to three sons.
And I’m an unabashed America-first, pro-family conservative.
I’m a proud graduate of Arizona State University, and Briana and I reside in the beautiful, one-of-a-kind City of Scottsdale.
Issues Grow a Pro-Family Economy My economic policy priority is empowering Arizonans and their families to work, start small businesses, save for the future, and keep more of their hard-earned money so they can devote more time to their children, grandchildren and loved ones.
I will be a vote for lower taxes, smaller and more efficient government, pro- small business policies that work for all Arizonans, and economic measures that encourage Arizonans to get married and form families.
Support Strong Families I will protect our kids from woke indoctrination and progressive ideology, and protect parents’ rightful role as the primary influence over their child’s upbringing – not bureaucrats or woke activists.
I will strive to make parenthood possible to achieve, not harder.
And I will champion Make America Healthy Again principles here in Arizona.
Make Education Work for Parents and Students My primary education goal will be to ensure Arizona’s K-12 system works for parents and their children.
That starts with focusing on core academics in our schools and strengthening Arizona’s position as a leading education freedom state where every parent is empowered to choose the education they desire for their child without burdensome regulatory roadblocks.
I also want to encourage growth and innovation in the education provider market so that more families can find the education settings and tools that fit their children’s needs.
Make our Communities Safe Safe communities start with a secure border.
While illegal crossings have dropped significantly under the leadership of President Trump, the job of securing our border is far from over and we cannot let down our guard.
I will support our brave men and women in law enforcement, vote for tough policies that keep criminals off our streets, and combat the epidemic of homelessness that is often driven by drug addiction and mental illness.
Defend Constitutional Freedoms I will always stand for life and defend the First and Second amendments, free speech, and religious freedom.
These are crucial to the foundation of our freedom as Americans and I hold them as core to my personal and political values.
Fight for Election Integrity None of this will be possible without election integrity.
Achieving free and fair elections with full transparency will be both a priority and an issue where I refuse to bend or back down.
This starts with cleaning up voter rolls so only eligible voters can vote and strengthening enforcement measures against ballot harvesting.
