She is focused on practical solutions that support small towns, farmers, and working families while making government more fair, efficient, and responsive to the people it serves.
Revitalize Small Towns Strengthen Agriculture and Support Farmers Combat Rising Property Taxes Fix Loopholes and Set Standards Affordable Prescriptions for All Revitalize Small Towns Sonja is committed to strengthening Minnesota’s small towns by investing in local infrastructure and drawing small business back.
Communities like Rapidan have seen firsthand how aging infrastructure can fail, and she believes rural areas should not be left behind.
Strengthen Agriculture and Support Farmers Sonja will advocate for Minnesota’s farmers by addressing rising input costs, including fertilizer, and working to reduce the impact of tariffs on agricultural producers.
She believes strong agricultural communities are essential to the state’s economy and should be supported with practical, forward-looking policies.
Combat Rising Property Taxes Sonja supports a more balanced property tax system that ensures small towns see greater reinvestment from the taxes they generate.
Her focus is on making sure rural communities are not disproportionately funding services in larger cities while struggling to maintain their own infrastructure.
Fix Loopholes and Set Standards Sonja believes government should work more efficiently and transparently by closing unnecessary loopholes and ensuring public resources are used responsibly.
She supports clear, consistent standards that prioritize essential services and improve accountability at every level.
Affordable Prescriptions for All Sonja supports efforts to lower the cost of prescription drugs for Minnesotans and make healthcare more affordable and accessible.
She believes that no one should have to choose between paying for medication and meeting other basic needs.
