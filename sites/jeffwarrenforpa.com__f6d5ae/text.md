My Dad
My dad taught me how.
When I was a kid, I decided to start a newspaper route.
At first, I thought it would be easy.
It wasn’t.
But my dad had my back.
He made me a deal: we woke up together every morning, no matter what, to deliver 70 newspapers to my neighbors – as long as I saved half of what I earned for my future.
It was a deal, and one that I needed his help to keep up, but one that paid off.
He showed up for me when I needed it, and I learned how to show up for others, even when it’s hard.
That lesson has stayed with me ever since.

About the District
The 137th House District is part of Northampton County, encompassing Bethlehem Township, Hanover Township, Lower Nazareth Township, Nazareth Borough, Palmer Township, Tatamy Borough, and Upper Nazareth Township.
Home to incredible businesses like Martin Guitar and located between Easton and Bethlehem, the district that Jeff and his neighbors call home has the potential to be a community that is fair, safe, growing, and thriving.
