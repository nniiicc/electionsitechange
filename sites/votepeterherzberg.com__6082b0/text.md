Peter Herzberg
DEMOCRAT FOR DISTRICT #25
I am a proud graduate of John Glenn High School and hold a Bachelor’s Degree in Finance from Wayne State University.
Over the past 13 years, I have been actively involved in local governance, attending Council meetings and study sessions, and serving as a Westland City Council member since 2016.
On April 30th, I was sworn in as State Representative, with my term set to conclude on December 31, 2024.
Growing up in Westland near Warren Road and Wayne, I have lived in the same neighborhood all my life.
This deep-rooted connection to my community has been a driving force in my public service journey.
