Privacy Policy
Effective Date: 4/8/25
Sprague for Ohio respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, forms, or other communication channels.
What Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
• Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
• Volunteer preferences and availability
• Donation information (processed through a secure third-party provider)
• Site usage data (via cookies or analytics tools)
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
• Communicate with you about campaign updates, events, and opportunities, either directly or through one of our partners.
• Organize volunteer and outreach efforts
• Process donations (via secure services)
• Improve website experience and performance
How We Protect Your Information
We take reasonable precautions to protect your personal data.
Access is limited to authorized staff and volunteers, and we use trusted third-party services for donation processing and data management.
We Don’t Sell Your Info
We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties.
We may share limited information with trusted vendors who help us operate the campaign (such as email platforms or database tools), but only as needed and with strict confidentiality.
Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties unless required by law.
Cookies & Analytics
Our site may use cookies or analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use our website and improve functionality.
You can disable cookies in your browser settings.
Opting Out
You may unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time by clicking the link at the bottom of our messages or by contacting us directly.
