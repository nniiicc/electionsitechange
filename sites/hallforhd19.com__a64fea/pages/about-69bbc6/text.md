Home About Kelly Issues Events Take Action Donate About Kelly A Lifetime of Service Kelly Hall is the Democratic nominee for Texas House District 19.
From Oklahoma to the Hill Country, he has spent his career organizing communities, helping neighbors in tough spots, and standing up for working families.
Now he is ready to bring that same heart and hustle to the Texas Capitol.
The Journey Kelly's Path to Public Service Every step has prepared Kelly to serve Texas District 19.
Midwest City High School Oklahoma Roots Kelly grew up in Oklahoma, where he was actively involved in FCCLA and SADD, developing his passion for community service and leadership.
Langston University Education Pursued higher education to build the foundation for a career dedicated to public service and helping others.
Correctional Officer Criminal Justice Career Served as a correctional officer, seeing up close how the criminal justice system actually works and why it needs fair, effective reform.
Nonprofit Leadership Community Service Built and led nonprofit work focused on housing, transportation, and job placement for neighbors facing hardship across Central Texas.
Texas House District 19 Democratic Nominee Kelly is the Democratic nominee for Texas House District 19, running to bring real representation to the Hill Country and the Northwest Austin suburbs.

Kelly in the community Personal Side Beyond Politics When Kelly is not on the campaign trail, he is out in the community.
Knocking doors across District 19, grabbing coffee with neighbors at the local diner, sitting in on town halls, and listening to people from every walk of life.
Those everyday conversations shape how he sees the work ahead.
Kelly believes every person has a story worth hearing, and that real change happens when we slow down long enough to actually understand one another.
