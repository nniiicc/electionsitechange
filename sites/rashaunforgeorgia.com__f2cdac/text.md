Better Is Before Us
As a first-generation college graduate, I have always said I owe it to my great-grandmother, who raised me, to do all I can to impact lives the way she positively impacted mine.
She instilled in me that “To Whom much is given, much is required”.
Let's get together and make a difference in our community.
I am honored to serve the Great State of Georgia and Senate District 38 as your State Senator.
My experience as a husband, father, teacher, principal, education advocate, government official, and community leader have prepared me to not only be ready for this role, but to also deliver results for the people of the 38th Senate District and the Great State of Georgia.
I am serving because it is incumbent on all of us to do our part in changing the trajectory of our state and country.
I am here to listen and work on behalf of my community focused on issues impacting our lives: education, cost of living, family and civil rights, and healthcare.
