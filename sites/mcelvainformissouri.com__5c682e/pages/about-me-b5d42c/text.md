Meet Roth
“Strong Communities come from resilient people coming together and working towards growing a better tomorrow.
I am running to show the state of Missouri that rural values still matter.”
Roth is a fifth generation farmer from Palmyra, Missouri.
He owns and operates McElvain Farms LLC. with his wife Kaitlynn and three children.
He is a member of St.
Joseph Catholic Church.
Roth is an alumni of Palmyra R-1 High School and Truman State University.
Currently Roth serves as the President of the Palmyra R-1 School Board of Education.
With his conservative Republican roots, Roth supports:
- Local Control of Government
- Pro Life with Family Values
- Quality education
- The 2nd Amendment
- Health Care Accessibility
- Cutting Wasteful Spending
- Fair Agriculture Policies
- Small Businesses and the Communities They Support
