Matt was proud to co-author and lead passage of the legislation implementing early voting.
The Early Voting location for Stamford residents is the Stamford Government Center, 888 Washington Blvd., 4th Floor .
Early Voting is available every day from October 19 through November 1 .
The hours are 10:00 AM - 6:00 PM each day except for two days with expanded hours (8:00 AM - 8:00 PM), Tuesday, Oct.
27, and Thursday, Oct.
29.
Of course, if you don't vote early, you can still vote on November 3 (Election Day).
Here's the 2026 Early Voting and Election Day Calendar: Voting by Absentee Ballot Eligibility ​ This election, for the first time — thanks to legislation Matt led passage of — * any eligible voter* can vote by absentee ballot . ​ How To Vote Absentee If you need to vote by absentee ballot, please click HERE to submit an online application .
Or, you can click HERE to download the absentee ballot application and submit by mail .
You can also contact your Town Clerk’s office to request an application.
Stamford: Email townclerk@stamfordct.gov or call (203) 977-5280 When filling out the application, make sure to note the following: Section I – Fill out your contact information Section II – Delivery address if it’s different than your registered address Section III – Date of the election: November 3 Section IV – Reason you are needing an absentee ballot Section VI – Sign and date the application Once complete return it via one of the three options below: ​ ​ ​ Place it in a drop box at the Government Center parking garage (888 Washington Boulevard) or the Harry Bennett Library next to the outdoor book drop (115 Vine Road).
Boxes will open on September 30.
Bring your application to the Town Clerk’s office Monday-Friday from 8:00 am to 3:30 pm and fill out a ballot and vote.
Starting now, mail your completed and signed application to: ​ Town Clerk – AB Application PO Box 891 Stamford, CT 06904-0891 ​ ​​ ​ Absentee ballots will be available starting October 7 and will be mailed out starting then if a completed application was returned. ​ As soon as it arrives, fill out your ballot and please vote for the endorsed Democrats on Row A .
Place your ballot in the inner envelope, sign the inner envelope, place the inner envelope in the outer envelope, and return it via the drop box or mail it back to your Town Clerk’s office.
Your absentee ballot MUST BE RECEIVED by your Town Clerk no later than 8:00 pm on Election Day, November 3 . ​ ​ You can check on the status of your absentee ballot here.
