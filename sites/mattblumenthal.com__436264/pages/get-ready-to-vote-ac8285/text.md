VOTING BY SECURE DROPBOX
Thanks to my colleagues' and my advocacy, you can submit your absentee ballot by dropbox.
If you have not already mailed your ballot, don't — instead, submit it by dropbox.
All votes must arrive at either the Town Clerk or in a dropbox by 8:00 PM on November 5th to be counted.
Stamford has four , and Darien has one.
Here are the locations of secure ballot dropboxes in Stamford:
STAMFORD
- Stamford Government Center Parking Garage (1st Floor) - 888 Washington Blvd
- Harry Bennett Branch of the Ferguson Library - 115 Vine Rd
