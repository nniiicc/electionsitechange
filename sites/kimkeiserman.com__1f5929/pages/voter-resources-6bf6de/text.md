Voter Resources
Below are some dates in reference to the 11/3/26 election.
Please go to nassauvotes.com for forms/information.
IMPORTANT DATES AHEAD OF ELECTION DAY:
• 10/24: Last day a voter registration application must be received by the Board of Elections to be eligible to vote in General Election.
• 10/24: Last day to register TO VOTE for the general election in person at
the Nassau County Board of Election, or at any Early Voting Locations.
• 10/24: First day of Early voting.
• 10/24: Last day for the Board of Elections to receive application for
absentee ballot for the general election.
• 11/1: Last day for Early Voting.
• 11/2: Last day to apply in person at the Board of Election for
Absentee/Early Vote By Mail Ballot.
• 11/3: Election Day!
Polls are open until 9pm.
