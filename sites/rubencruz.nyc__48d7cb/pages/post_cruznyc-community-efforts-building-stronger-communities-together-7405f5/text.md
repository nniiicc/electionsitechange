How CruzNYC Community Efforts Are Making a Difference
I want to share some concrete ways we’re turning words into action.
CruzNYC is more than a name.
It’s a movement focused on the heart of New York State Assembly District 38.
Here’s what we’re doing:
- Fighting for affordability: We’re pushing for policies that protect renters and homeowners from skyrocketing costs.
That means advocating for rent control measures and supporting programs that help families stay in their homes.
- Supporting small businesses: Small businesses are the lifeblood of our community.
We’re working to create economic development initiatives that provide grants, training, and resources to help local entrepreneurs grow and thrive.
- Protecting residents from predatory practices: From unfair lending to deceptive contracts, predatory practices hurt our neighbors.
We’re committed to strengthening consumer protections and ensuring that everyone has access to fair treatment.
- Building community spaces: Parks, community centers, and safe public areas are essential for connection and growth.
We’re investing in these spaces to foster a sense of belonging and pride.
- Encouraging civic engagement: Change happens when people get involved.
We’re hosting town halls, workshops, and volunteer opportunities to empower residents to have their voices heard.
