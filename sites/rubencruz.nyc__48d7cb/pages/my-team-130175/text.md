Team Majestic Seahorse
Why We Are Team Majestic Seahorse
Since October 30, 2020, Steve Cohen has owned the Mets, but being a Mets fan doesn't mean you have to be a "Cohen fan".
In the same way, being a supporter of a political movement shouldn't mean you are just a fan of the politician.
Most candidates name their teams after themselves, but I believe leadership is about the people, not the person in charge.
The Vision
- The Mascot: We chose the Majestic Seahorse because it represents resilience, perspective, and the unique coastal identity of our district.
- The Movement: We are riding the wave from across Queens to make this the best place to live—for our families, our seniors, and our small businesses.
- The Focus: We aren't here to build a cult of personality; we are here to build a "Full Circle" of governance that works for every neighbor.
In our district, we know that neighbors help neighbors.
Team Majestic is the engine that will drive that change, ensuring our "castle" remains secure and our community continues to prosper.
