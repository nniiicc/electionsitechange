A life built on service.
Lori Korzen is a wife, mother, and grandmother who has dedicated her life to service and community involvement in New Hampshire's North Country.
As a small business owner, she balances entrepreneurship with a deep commitment to making a positive impact on the lives of those around her — raised with the value that service to community is a fundamental responsibility.
Family First, Always
Lori's journey into public service took on new meaning when she became a grandmother.
The birth of her grandsons sparked a profound desire to create a better world for future generations — and that personal motivation drives every legislative effort she takes on today.
What Lori is fighting for.
Three priorities.
Real accountability.
A stronger future for every family in Coos 7.
Transparency & Accountability in DCYF
DCYF has gone too long with unchecked power and has lost its fundamental purpose of protecting children.
We need real oversight — and we need it now.
Standing With Victims
We need real accountability and oversight within our court system — especially the Family Court — so that victims are heard, protected, and never lost in the system.
Lower Taxes, Smarter Spending
Citizens are stretched thin by high property taxes.
NH ranks 7th highest in per-pupil spending while proficiency and enrollment decline.
We don't need more from taxpayers — we need accountability for every dollar.
Showing up.
Every day.
From volunteering at homeless shelters in Boston to supporting the New Hampshire Food Bank, Lori has spent her entire life answering the call to serve others.
Together with her husband, Lori fostered and adopted children in Massachusetts — demonstrating her commitment to child welfare through direct action.
That firsthand experience with the foster care and adoption system gives her unique insights into the challenges families face and the importance of protecting children's well-being.
It's not a campaign promise.
It's a lifetime of showing up.
