Dawn Loberg is a proud Central Minnesota resident, small business owner, and passionate advocate for the working families who drive our local economy.
With a strong background in marketing, sales, and strategic planning and through her work supporting manufacturers at Enterprise Minnesota, she understands how to grow jobs and strengthen our communities.
Dawn is running for the Minnesota House in District 13B to make our state more affordable, end wasteful spending, eliminate the unfair tax on Social Security benefits, and restore accountability in government.
For Dawn, it’s simple: Minnesotans deserve leaders who listen, take action, and protect the values that keep Central Minnesota strong.
