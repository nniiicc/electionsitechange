Fiscal Responsibility State government needs to stop wasting taxpayer dollars.
The recent fraud and runaway spending shows exactly what happens when accountability is ignored.
I will work to restore fiscal responsibility, cutting waste, tightening oversight, and ensuring every dollar spent delivers real value for Minnesotans.
Tax on Social Security Minnesotans who have worked their entire lives should not be punished in retirement.
The tax on Social Security is unfair and hurts seniors living on fixed incomes.
I will continue the fight to eliminate this tax once and for all, so retirees can keep more of what they’ve earned and enjoy the quality of life they deserve.
Affordability Everyday life is becoming too expensive for Minnesota families.
From groceries to energy costs, hardworking people deserve relief, not more taxes and regulations driving prices higher.
I’ll fight to make Minnesota more affordable by reducing the burden on families, protecting paychecks, and ensuring our communities remain a great place to live and raise a family.
