What I Stand For The Issues That Matter 🏫 Education Missouri's public schools are chronically underfunded.
I will fight against voucher programs that redirect taxpayer money away from public education, and advocate for fair teacher pay — especially for those just starting their careers. 💼 Fiscal Fairness Replacing the income tax with expanded sales taxes falls hardest on working and middle-class families.
I will stand for tax policy that protects the people state government should serve — not special interests. 🛣️ Infrastructure District 146 deserves real investment — in internet access, roads, bridges, and the infrastructure that supports daily life and economic opportunity for every family in Jackson and Cape Girardeau County. 🗳️ Democracy Missouri voters have passed referendums only to see them undone in Jefferson City.
I am committed to respecting the will of the people — not the demands of national political leaders — period.
Who I Am Rooted in This Community I grew up in Jackson, graduated from Jackson High School — as did my son Riley, the fourth generation of our family to do so.
My great-grandfather served as mayor of Jackson.
Public service runs deep in our family.
My mother, Marybelle Mueller, made history as the first woman elected judge in Missouri.
I carry that spirit of service into this campaign.
As a former government teacher, college educator at SEMO, and 15-year Instructional Designer, I have devoted my career to this region and its people.
I know what families in District 146 need — because I am one of them.
