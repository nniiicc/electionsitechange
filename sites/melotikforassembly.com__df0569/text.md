3rd Donate Request a Yard Sign State Representative Paul Melotik is seeking re-election to continue delivering results for Wisconsin’s 22nd Assembly District.
“It has been an honor to serve the hardworking people of the 22nd District,” said Melotik.
“Together, we’ve made meaningful progress on the issues that matter most to all Wisconsinites, such as lowering taxes, supporting law enforcement, strengthening our local economy, and protecting our communities.
I’m running for re-election to keep that momentum going.” As Chairman of the Workforce Development Committee, Representative Melotik has taken a leading role in advancing policies that strengthen Wisconsin’s workforce.
Having worked in small business prior to serving in the legislature, Melotik recognizes that strengthening job training pipelines and connecting employers with skilled workers is essential to keep Wisconsin competitive in a growing economy.
Above all, Melotik points to his commitment to staying directly engaged with constituents and addressing the issues that matter most to them.
“I am committed to representing the voices and values of the people I serve and maintaining an open, accessible office for constituents,” Melotik said.
“There’s still more work to be done to keep our communities safe, our economy strong, and our future bright.” Representative Melotik encourages residents of the 22nd District to stay engaged, share their concerns, and join the campaign effort as he seeks another term of service.
