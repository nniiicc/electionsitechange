2025 Session

During the 2025 session, I focused on finding the right balance between protecting Utahns and keeping government from standing in the way of innovation, entrepreneurship, and economic growth.

From modernizing vehicle registration and professional practice rules to improving motorcycle safety, beekeeping, veterinary services, and criminal statutes, these bills reflect a common-sense approach: regulate where regulation protects people, and get government out of the way where it doesn't.

My work in the Legislature builds on a career dedicated to protecting children and families, and that passion has only grown as I’ve worked on these issues.

What's Next
This year, I will continue building on the work I’ve done in artificial intelligence, including examining liability and accountability for agentic AI while further strengthening our laws to protect children from child sexual abuse material.
I will also remain focused on education and early literacy as we work together as a state to address the fundamentals of reading and ensure every child has the opportunity to succeed.
Supporting Utah families will remain a priority, with a particular emphasis on family-friendly policies and women’s health.
I look forward to another productive year of thoughtful, commonsense policymaking—working to make a meaningful difference today while helping ensure a bright future for Utahns.
