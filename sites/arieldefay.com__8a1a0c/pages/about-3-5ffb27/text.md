I'm a third generation resident.
My connection with our community began with my grandfather, who worked at Hill Airforce Base.
His legacy, along with those of my family, have shaped my bond with the region.
Davis County is not just where we live--it's a part of who we are.
Family I am blessed with three wonderful children and supportive husband of 20 years.
Together, we have built a business, raised our children and enjoyed recreating in Davis County.
I love running, reading, singing, skiing, hiking, and cheering on the Utah Jazz !
I have valued serving in many volunteer positions in my children's schools, our church, and boards within our community.
My commitment to bettering Davis County stems from a desire to serve our next generation. ​ Experience I am the current Representative for Utah House District 15 and serve as Vice Chair of the House Education Committee and on the Interim Higher Education Appropriations Committee.
I have also served on the House Economic Development and Workforce Services Committee, House Transportation Committee, and Business and Economic Development Appropriations Committee.
My legislative work has focused on practical policies that strengthen education, support families and small businesses, improve public safety and transportation, and responsibly address emerging issues in artificial intelligence.I began my career in state government as a senior legislative aide and have also managed a small family business, experiences that have shaped my commitment to effective, responsible government.
I hold a BA in Political Science from Utah State University and a Master of Public Administration from Ohio University.
