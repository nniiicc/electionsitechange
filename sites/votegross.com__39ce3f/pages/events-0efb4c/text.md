Last Day to Register to Vote Michigan Register to vote as soon as you can!
The last day to register online is October 19.
Starting October 20, you can only register to vote in person at your city or township clerk's office with proof of residency.

Statewide Early Voting Period Michigan This is a constitutionally-mandated early voting period.
All communities must offer in-person early voting at a designated early voting site starting October 24.
Contact your clerk by clicking here or visit mi.gov/vote to find out when and where you can vote early.
