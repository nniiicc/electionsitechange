The Nine Answers, On the Record ﻿ Elections Only American citizens should vote in American elections.
That is the law.
I support a version of the SAVE Act that requires a state or federally issued ID, with the same transparency standard Pennsylvania already has: full and public disclosure of who voted.
We should not make it hard to vote.
We should make it hard to cheat.
2020 Joe Biden was elected President.
I will not spend a term relitigating it.
After January 6th, I deployed to Washington with the National Guard to help secure the peaceful transfer of power to him.
I took that oath seriously then.
I take it seriously now.
Immigration and ICE Public safety here starts with local police.
I am proud to have the endorsement of the Fraternal Order of Police, Chester County Lodge.
Washington should do the work only Washington can do: the border, the cartels, human trafficking, and serious criminal offenders.
It should stay in that lane — and be held accountable when it does not.
Organized Labor Workers have the right to organize and to bargain.
I have spent twenty-five years in rooms where those contracts get negotiated, usually with the company on the edge of failing.
The minimum wage Pennsylvania’s has been $7.25 since 2009.
The Commonwealth should raise it.
No one in Chester or Berks is one wage vote away from affording a house here.
I would rather spend my time on the things that actually put homeownership within reach.
Reproductive policy My position is the current TRICARE rule: coverage when the life of the mother is at risk, and in cases of rape or incest.
I will not vote for a national ban.
That is the standard military families already live under.
People of good faith disagree here.
I am not going to pretend otherwise.
The Epstein files Release them.
Protect the victims.
Hold offenders accountable regardless of party, wealth, or title.
Congress should hold itself to that same standard, starting with the taxpayer-funded settlements used to bury misconduct in its own building.
Iran and war powers Congress declares war.
Congress controls the purse.
I will not rubber-stamp an unauthorized war or unauthorized spending — including from a president of my own party.
President Trump I will work with President Trump, as I would with any president, when it helps this district.
I will tell him no when it does not.
Specifically: spending that routes around Congress, military action without a vote, and healthcare, where my plan is mine.
I am a Chester County Republican.
I intend to vote this district.
