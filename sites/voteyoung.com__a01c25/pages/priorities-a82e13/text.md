Washington spends too much time arguing and not enough time solving problems.
Families and small businesses feel the consequences when real issues go unaddressed.
Throughout my life — in uniform, in business, and in this community — I’ve focused on doing the job the right way: understanding the problem, making hard decisions, and delivering results.
That same approach should guide how we govern.
Our country faces serious challenges.
Meeting them requires clear priorities, steady leadership, and policies that strengthen families, keep communities safe, and make it easier for people who work hard to build a good life.
