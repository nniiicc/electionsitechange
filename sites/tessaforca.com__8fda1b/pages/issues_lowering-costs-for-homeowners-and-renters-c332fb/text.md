Lowering Costs for Homeowners and Renters
The rising cost of housing is pushing families out of neighborhoods they’ve called home for decades and making it harder for young people and small businesses to plant roots in our communities.
If we want vibrant local economies and inclusive neighborhoods, we must prioritize housing affordability.
That means investing in a diverse range of housing options - from affordable rentals to pathways to homeownership - to meet the needs of working families, seniors, and young professionals.
In Congress, I’ll fight to make housing more accessible and affordable, because no one should be priced out of the place they call home.
