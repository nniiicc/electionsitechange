Bringing Good Jobs & Livable Wages
The 23rd District needs more than just another car wash - it needs good paying, skilled jobs.
With abundant space and access to two major interstates, the 23rd is uniquely positioned to attract new businesses and create high-quality jobs.
By increasing local employment opportunities, residents won't have to commute long distances to other parts of Southern California, keeping more money in our community and boosting the economy.
We can make that investment through federal legislation, improving our infrastructure and bringing in the kinds of jobs that will allow our residents to live, work, and stay here long-term.
