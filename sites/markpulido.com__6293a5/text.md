His history drives his passion for change and service.
His efforts during his life and career work to empower and uplift people, creating and providing opportunities for education, jobs, and success.
Mark's father and grandfathers were veterans of the U.S.
Navy and U.S.
Army, where they served with distinction and honor.
His parents are founding members of the community where he lives and raises his family and small business owners who broke down racist barriers and opened up the gates of success to countless other families.
Mark Pulido is a 54-year resident of Cerritos, where he lives and raised his family with his wife Gloria.
They have two children educated in local schools – schools Mark himself attended.
His activism and leadership has led to thousands of students connecting with their cultural roots and heritage, and planting the seeds for success in school, career, and life.
Mark's efforts to create opportunity and success for all manifest themselves in many ways: Student leader and activist Civil rights advocate and warrior Community leader, builder, and job creator As a student leader at UCLA, Mark fought to ensure that all students have access to the resources they need to succeed, despite economic, social, or ethnic barriers.
Mark led movements to fight discrimination, ensure access to jobs and education, and create inclusiveness.
As an elected official, Mark modernized schools, got funding for local education, increased the safety of our neighborhoods, built critical infrastructure and housing, and worked to create good, lasting jobs in our community.
Mark Pulido dedicates his life to the success of others, especially those who need it the most.
We need him fighting for us in the state Legislature.
