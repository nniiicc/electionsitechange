What Ryan stands for Ryan Neill is committed to serving the people of Sarasota, Florida, in District 74.
He believes in fostering a strong community, addressing key local issues, and working tirelessly to bring about positive change.
Discover the principles that guide his campaign and his dedication to public service.
Investment in Mass Transit Due to my father's involvement with the Sarasota County Aera Transit Authority, I believe strongly in having available mass transportation for those who are in need, especially the elderly and disabled that require additional help getting to places locally.
This helps connect our communities together.
Affordable Housing I am committed to keeping the country - country.
As a community, we need to make sure that we stop the home builders from encroaching on wetlands and the natural beauty of Sarasota.
Investment in Flood Control and Mitigation I support funding for flood control and mitigation.
As Sarasota's population has grown, the state needs to collect flood plain data for a more accurate and sustainable growth in our communities.
Reigning in the Rising cost of Homeowners Insurance As a homeowner in Sarasota, I understand how incredibly expensive the current homeowner's insurance has become.
I feel strongly that we need to reign in these costs and to have the insurer accountable for repairs that are required after storms have devasted our local community.
