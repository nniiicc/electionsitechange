Addressing the core challenges of District 74
As a candidate for Florida State House Representative District 74, I am committed to tackling the issues that matter most to our community.
Learn about the crucial concerns affecting our district and Ryan's vision for a brighter future.
Securing affordable insurance
The rising cost of homeowners insurance is a major barrier for families seeking to own a home in Florida, and it's contributing to an alarming upswing in foreclosures.
I will work to reform insurance policies, making them more accessible and affordable, to protect our residents and their investments.
Affordable housing for all
Home ownership and rent are increasingly out of reach for most working families in Florida.
This crisis impacts our community's stability and growth.
If elected, I will advocate for policies that increase access to affordable housing options, ensuring that every working family in District 74 has a place to call home.
Funding flood control and mitigation
Without proper flood plain data collection and dedicated funding for flood control and mitigation, our district will continue to face massive increases in insurance rates and significant risks to property.
I am committed to securing the necessary funding and implementing effective strategies to protect District 74 from the impacts of flooding.
Investing in mass transit and high-speed rail
As our state grows, we are rapidly running out of space for roadways.
Increased funding for a statewide mass transit system, whether through buses, light rail, or high-speed rail, is essential.
I support expanding our transportation infrastructure to improve connectivity, reduce congestion, and enhance the quality of life for all Floridians.
Our vision: Making Florida affordable again
If elected, my ultimate goal is to make Florida affordable for working families once again.
By addressing these critical issues head-on, we can build a future where every resident of District 74 can thrive.
