Why Ryan is the right choice for District 74 I'm not a politician.
I am a native Sarasotan, born and raised.
I am a graduate of Sarasota High School and USF.
I am acutely aware of the challenges facing the working families of Sarasota, from the rising cost of rent and housing to a livable wage, and affordable homeowners' insurance.
My deep roots and personal experience in this community make me the most locally knowledgeable candidate to represent you.
(1 / 1) 1 Addressing the critical issues My top priorities focus on the real challenges affecting Sarasota families.
I am committed to tackling affordable housing and rent by passing legislation that prevents or restricts Wall Street Investment Firms from buying up single-family homes.
If Wall Street wants to be landlords, they should buy apartment complexes.
I also plan to support legislation to offer rebates to first-time home buyers who reside in the house for ten years.
It is also time to look into legislation that reigns in the rising cost of rent.
Holding Insurance companies accountable My top priority is to pass legislation that reins in homeowners insurance rate hikes.
Hold insurance companies accountable to their policy holders.
Passing legislation that requires insurance companies quickly fix or pay policy holders claims and if they do not fix the issue in a timely manner to let the homeowner get a contractor to fix the issue at cost, paid by the insurance company at cost.
