MAKE A PLAN TO VOTE * * *
In New York, you can vote early in person, absentee ballot by mail, or on November 5 in person.
FIND AN EARLY VOTE IN PERSON LOCATION
Early voting in person begins October 26.
You can find locations and times to vote early by visiting the Monroe County early vote website.
VOTE ABSENTEE BALLOT BY MAIL
If you want to vote absentee by mail, you must request your ballot by mail or online by October 21.
You can also apply in person to vote absentee by mail until November 4.
You can find more information about voting by absentee ballot at the New York State’s absentee ballot information page.
VOTE DAY OF IN PERSON
You can vote in person on November 5.
Find your polling location using the New York State Polling Location Finder.
