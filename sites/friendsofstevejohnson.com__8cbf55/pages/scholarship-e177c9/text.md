Home Scholarship Legislation Keeping You In The Loop Endorsements Contribute Delegate Johnson's Legislative Scholarship Applicants must be a resident of District 34A.
Please note that various legislators provide scholarship opportunities as well.
To identify your legislators, please visit the following webpage: Members - Find My Representatives (maryland.gov) Every year, Delegate Johnson offers his legislative scholarship to students who reside in District 34A.
Please reach out to steve.johnson@house.maryland.gov to be placed on our email list for info on the application, as we update it each cycle.
Funds are limited and we encourage you to apply as soon as possible.
Students must attend/plan to attend a college, university in Maryland (or apply for a Unique Major Status for an out-of-state institution).
If you have received a scholarship from Delegate Johnson, you must reapply to be eligible for another scholarship.
We look forward to reviewing your application!
Please reach out to steve.johnson@house.maryland.gov if you have any questions about the application process.
