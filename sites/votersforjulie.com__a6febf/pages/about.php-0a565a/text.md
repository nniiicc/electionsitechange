A Proud 'Regionite' Born, raised, and educated in Northwest Indiana, Julie's life experiences, problem solving skills and servant attitude make her the best choice as your state representative.
The second oldest of 7 children, Julie attended Lew Wallace High School.
She earned a Bachelor of Arts degree with honors from Indiana University Northwest, and paid for her schooling by working as a cake decorator at Glen Park Bakery.
Growing up in Gary and living in Lake County gave Julie a solid appreciation for the rich diversity in our community, something to be celebrated.
Employed at Ad-com Advertising for 9 years, she quickly earned the position of art director.
Branching out to start VIA Marketing in 1987, an award-winning local marketing communications company, Julie grew the company to 15 employees.
In 2007 the recession hit hard and just like most small companies, she learned the pain of letting a few good employees go.
The team at VIA made it through the pandemic.
Strength of character and innovation are built during hard times.
VIA is an IDOA Women Business Enterprise (WBE certified since 1998) and INDOT Disadvantaged Business Enterprise (DBE).
After 38 years, Julie sold VIA Marketing.
Her wisdom, compassion, and resilience are still utilized as Chair Emerius.
