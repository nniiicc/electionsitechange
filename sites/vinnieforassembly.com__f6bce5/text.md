Meet Vinnie Miresse
I am running for re-election to the Wisconsin State Assembly because I truly believe that good politics can improve people’s lives.
In a time of increasing skepticism about the role of government, my goal remains clear: to inspire optimism, create meaningful change, and deliver real results for the people of Central Wisconsin.
Thank you for your continued support as we work together to serve our community.

Key Issues
Affordability
An Economy that works for every Wisconsinite with people-centered, smart growth—driven by innovation, opportunity, respect, and sustainability—is the foundation of a balanced Wisconsin economy.
Affordable Access to Quality Healthcare
Healthcare is a human right.
Nobody should have to choose between seeing the doctor and putting food on the table.
Let’s expand Medicaid.
Ecological Stewardship
Reinstate Knowles-Nelson Stewardship funding and protect public land.
Education
We need to grow our own talent by investing in public schools, technical colleges, and universities, and creating strong partnerships between childcare providers, local businesses, manufacturers, and entrepreneurs.
Protecting Reproductive Health Freedoms
Government has no place making decisions over women’s bodies.
I will fight to repeal Wisconsin’s 175-year-old Criminal Abortion Statute, which makes this important, personal healthcare decision a felony.
Supporting Family Farms
Every dollar we spend with local farmers pays dividends to our future.
Let’s build thriving rural communities and create a strong rural/urban partnership through regenerative agriculture.
