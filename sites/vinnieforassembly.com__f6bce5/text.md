Get to Know Vinnie Key Issues Affordability An Economy that works for every Wisconsinite with people-centered, smart growth—driven by innovation, opportunity, respect, and sustainability—is the foundation of a balanced Wisconsin economy.
Affordable Access to Quality Healthcare Healthcare is a human right.
Nobody should have to choose between seeing the doctor and putting food on the table.
Let’s expand Medicaid.
Ecological Stewardship Reinstate Knowles-Nelson Stewardship funding and protect public land.
Education We need to grow our own talent by investing in public schools, technical colleges, and universities, and creating strong partnerships between childcare providers, local businesses, manufacturers, and entrepreneurs.
Protecting Reproductive Health Freedoms Government has no place making decisions over women’s bodies.
I will fight to repeal Wisconsin’s 175-year-old Criminal Abortion Statute, which makes this important, personal healthcare decision a felony.
Supporting Family Farms Every dollar we spend with local farmers pays dividends to our future.
Let’s build thriving rural communities and create a strong rural/urban partnership through regenerative agriculture.
