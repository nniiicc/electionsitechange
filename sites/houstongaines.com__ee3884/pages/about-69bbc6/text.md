Donate About Issues Volunteer News Endorsements Donate Meet Houston Houston was born and raised in Athens, where he still lives.
He’s served in the State House since 2019 representing Barrow, Clarke, Jackson, and Oconee counties.
Professionally, Houston has spent his career helping grow small businesses.
Today, he works with Carter Engineering, where he's focused on expanding this successful small business in Oconee County.
In the State House, Houston has been a champion for conservative policies and helped oversee efforts to maintain the Republican Majority.
He was named James Magazine’s Top Lawmaker last year.
He’s been awarded by the American Conservative Union Foundation (ACUF) for conservative achievement and has a 100% voting record with the Faith & Freedom Coalition and an A ranking with the NRA.
In 2022, he was named one of the University of Georgia’s 40 Under 40.
While at UGA, Houston served as student body president.
Houston has authored more than 20 bills that have passed the legislature - including a bill to prohibit local governments from defunding the police.
He’s led the fight to crack down on illegal immigration at the state level and put a stop to sanctuary policies.
He helped craft legislation to rein in rogue district attorneys, like Fani Willis and Deborah Gonzalez, and he carried legislation to stop cashless bail in Georgia.
Houston and his wife, Gabrielle, attend Athens Church.
He is an 8th generation northeast Georgian.
