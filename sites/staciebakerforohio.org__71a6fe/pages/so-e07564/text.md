Iimayl u dir volunteers.bakerforohiosenate@gmail.com Paid for by Citizens for Stacie Baker Ku Saabsan Musharraxa Hogaamiye Adeegga Ka Sameeyay Stacie Baker wuxuu in ka badan 15 sano u hibeeyay adeegga dadweynaha, isagoo la shaqeynaya dowladaha hoose, hoggaamiyeyaasha bulshada iyo qoysaska si loo xoojiyo bulshooyinka bartamaha Ohio.
Intii uu shaqeynayay, wuxuu ku kasbaday sumcad hoggaamin miyir leh, mas'uuliyad maaliyadeed iyo isu keenista dadka si loo xalliyo caqabadaha adag.
Xubin golaha magaalada Reynoldsburg oo At-Large ah muddo saddex xilli ah iyo Council President Pro Tempore hadda, Stacie wuxuu gacan ka geystay hoggaaminta mid ka mid ah bulshooyinka ugu koritaanka badan bartamaha Ohio, isagoo ah guddoomiyaha Finance Committee, halkaas oo uu kormeeray miisaaniyad mas'uul ah iyo maamul hufan oo lacagta cashuur-bixiyaha ah.
Shaqadiisu waxay diiradda saartay ilaalinta khayraadka dadweynaha, xoojinta xaafadaha iyo hubinta in dowladdu ka jawaab-celiso dadka ay u adeegto.
Isagoo Reynoldsburg uga wakiil ah guddiga West Licking County Joint Fire District, Stacie wuxuu ka shaqeeyay xoojinta iskaashiga gobolka ee wanaajiya badbaadada dadweynaha iyo diyaargarowga xaaladaha degdegga ah.
Wuxuu si joogto ah u taageeray maalgashi kaabayaal oo caqli leh, korriin dhaqaale iyo siyaasado abuura fursado qoysaska shaqeysta, isagoo bulshooyinka ka caawinaya qorsheynta mustaqbalka.
Qalin-jabiyay The Ohio State University wuxuuna ka qaatay Master of Public Administration jaamacadda Ohio University, Stacie wuxuu isku daraa diyaargarow tacliimeed iyo sanado adeeg dadweyne oo gacanta lagu qabtay.
Isagoo nin guursaday, aabbe laba carruur ah iyo adeege dadweyne noloshiisa oo dhan, wuxuu aaminsan yahay in Ohio ay xaq u leedahay hoggaamiyeyaal marka hore dhegeysta, ku hoggaamiya daacadnimo, dadkana ka hormariya siyaasadda.
