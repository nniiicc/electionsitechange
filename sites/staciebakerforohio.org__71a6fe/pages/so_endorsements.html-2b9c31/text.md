Ururrada shaqaalaha, hoggaamiyeyaasha bulshada, iyo saraakiisha la doortay ee garab taagan Stacie Baker ee Degmada 3.
TAAGEERADA RASMIGA AH EE 2026
Xisbiga • 2026
Ohio Democratic Party
Codka heer-gobolka ah ee dimuqraadiyiinta Ohio.
Taageerada xisbiga gobolku waxay culayska buuxa ee ururka Dimuqraadiga ee Ohio saaraysaa ololihiisa uu ku doonayo inuu ku guuleysto Degmada Senate-ka 3 isla markaana Ohio horay u wado.
Franklin County Democratic Party
Codka rasmiga ah ee dimuqraadiyiinta Franklin County, oo ah county-ga ay ku dhex taallo Degmada Senate-ka 3.
Taageerada xisbiga county-gu waxay muujinaysaa taageero dimuqraadi oo deegaanka ah oo qoto dheer oo loo hayo taariikhdiisa adeegga dadweynaha iyo qorshihiisa uu Ohio horay ugu wado.
Hilliard Democrats
Xisbiga Dimuqraadiga rasmiga ah ee Hilliard, Ohio.
Taageeradoodu waxay taageerada Stacie u fidinaysaa galbeedka Franklin County, iyagoo dhisaya isbahaysiga magaalooyinka badan ee looga baahan yahay in lagu guuleysto Degmada Senate-ka 3.
Pickaway County Democratic Party
Ururka Xisbiga Dimuqraadiga ee Pickaway County, oo xaruntiisu ku taallo Circleville.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay dimuqraadiyiinta aagga ku qabaan taariikhdiisa adeegga dadweynaha iyo ololihiisa uu ugu tartamayo Senate-ka Ohio.
Madison County Democratic Party
Ururka Xisbiga Dimuqraadiga ee Madison County, oo fadhigiisu yahay London, Ohio.
Taageeradoodu waxay ballaarinaysaa isbahaysiga dimuqraadiga ah ee county-yada ee garab taagan ololihiisa.
Ururrada Shaqaalaha • 2026
Ohio AFL-CIO
Isbahaysi shaqaale oo heer gobol ah oo matala 700,000+ shaqaale oo Ohio ah oo ka kala socda qayb kasta oo warshadeed.
Waa aqoonsiga ugu sarreeya ee ururrada shaqaalaha ee siyaasadda Ohio, wuxuuna muujinayaa in ururrada shaqaalaha ee gobolka oo dhan ay ku kalsoon yihiin in Stacie uu garab istaago qoysaska shaqaalaha ah.
Central Ohio Labor Council
Isbahaysi heer aag ah oo ka kooban ururrada shaqaalaha ee deegaanka, matalana tobanaan kun oo shaqaale ah oo ku kala baahsan aagga magaalada Columbus.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay ururrada shaqaaluhu ku qabaan ballanqaadka Stacie ee mushahar lagu noolaan karo iyo ilaalinta shaqaalaha.
Ohio Education Association
Ururka shaqaalaha waxbarashada ee ugu weyn Ohio, oo matala in ka badan 120,000 macallin, macallimiin jaamacadeed, iyo xirfadlayaal taageerada waxbarashada.
Waxay Stacie u taageeraan ballanqaadkiisa ah in si buuxda loo maalgeliyo dugsiyada dadweynaha, la ixtiraamo macallimiinta, ardaydana laga hor mariyo voucher-yada dugsiyada gaarka loo leeyahay.
Ohio Federation of Teachers
Laanta Ohio ee American Federation of Teachers, oo matala macallimiinta iyo shaqaalaha dugsiyada ee ku jira ururrada deegaanka ee gobolka oo dhan.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay macallimiinta Ohio ku qabaan ballanqaadkiisa ah dugsiyo dadweyne oo xoog leh, mushahar cadaalad ah oo macallimiinta, iyo mustaqbalka arday kasta.
AFSCME Ohio Council 8
Golaha heer gobol ee Ohio ee American Federation of State, County and Municipal Employees, oo matala tobanaan kun oo shaqaale adeegga dadweynaha ah oo socodsiiya bulshooyinkeenna.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay ururrada shaqaaluhu ku qabaan ballanqaadkiisa ah adeegyo dadweyne oo xoog leh, mushahar cadaalad ah, iyo xaqa gorgortanka wadajirka ah.
OCSEA / AFSCME Local 11
Ururka ugu weyn ee shaqaalaha dowladda ee Ohio, oo matala shaqaalaha gobolka ee ka hawlgala wakaalad kasta.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay ururrada shaqaaluhu ku qabaan ballanqaadka Stacie ee gorgortanka wadajirka ah, mushaharka cadaalada ah, iyo shaqaalaha socodsiiya dowladda Ohio.
AFSCME Retiree Chapter 1184
Laanta hawlgabyada ee AFSCME Ohio Council 8, oo matala shaqaalihii adeegga dadweynaha ee xirfaddooda oo dhan ku adeegayay bulshooyinka Ohio.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay xubnaha hawlgabka ah ee ururku ku qabaan ballanqaadkiisa ah ilaalinta gunnooyinka hawlgabka, ammaanka hawlgabnimada, iyo hay'adaha dadweynaha ee ay dhiseen.
OAPSE / AFSCME Local 4
Ohio Association of Public School Employees
In ka badan 34,000 oo darawal bas oo dugsi ah, shaqaale nadaafad, xoghaye, kaaliye, iyo shaqaale cunto ah oo socodsiiya dugsiyada Ohio.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay shaqaalaha taageerada dugsiyadu ku qabaan ballanqaadkiisa ah mushahar cadaalad ah, dugsiyo badbaado leh, iyo in la ixtiraamo shaqaale kasta oo dhismaha ku shaqeeya.
Columbus/Central Ohio Building & Construction Trades Council
Waxay matalaan xirfadlayaasha dhismaha: korontalayaal, nijaarro, tuubbo-yaqaanno, birtumayaal, iyo kuwo kale.
Waxay taageeraan ballanqaadka Stacie ee ku aaddan mushaharka rasmiga ah, barnaamijyada shaqo-barashada, iyo maalgashiga kaabayaasha dhaqaalaha ee abuura shaqooyin ururka shaqaalaha ka tirsan oo mushahar wanaagsan leh.
Sheet Metal Workers Local Union #24
Shaqaalaha birta saxanka ah ee deegaanka Columbus ee ka tirsan SMART, oo ah International Association of Sheet Metal, Air, Rail and Transportation Workers.
Taageeradoodu waxay garab taagan tahay ballanqaadkiisa ku aaddan mushaharka rasmiga ah, barnaamijyada shaqo-barashada ee diiwaangashan, iyo xirfadaha dhisaya bartamaha Ohio.
Brotherhood of Locomotive Engineers and Trainmen
Ohio State Legislative Board
Shaqaalaha tareenka ee Ohio: injineerada iyo shaqaalaha tareenka wada ee socodsiiya shabakadda tareenka ee Ohio.
Waxay taageeraan Stacie sababtoo ah wuxuu garab taagan yahay dib-u-habaynta badbaadada tareenka iyo ilaalinta shaqaalaha.
Teamsters Ohio D.R.I.V.E.
Guddiga ficilka siyaasadeed ee Ohio Teamsters
Guddiga ficilka siyaasadeed ee Ohio Teamsters, oo lagu maalgeliyo deeqo ikhtiyaari ah oo xubnaha ka yimaada.
Taageeradoodu waxay Ohio Teamsters ku daraysaa isbahaysiga ururrada shaqaalaha ee garab taagan ololaha Stacie.
FOP Dewey R.
Stokes Capital City Lodge #9
Fraternal Order of Police
Lodge-ka Fraternal Order of Police ee bartamaha Ohio, oo matala saraakiisha u adeega Columbus iyo Franklin County.
Waxay taageeraan Stacie sababtoo ah wuxuu garab taagan yahay ragga iyo dumarka ka shaqeeya sharci-fulinta, wuxuuna diiradda saarayaa xaafado badbaado leh oo adeeg fiican la siiyo.
Hoggaanka Sharci-dejinta • 2026
Ohio Legislative Black Caucus
Ururka xubnaha madowga ah ee Ohio General Assembly, oo la aasaasay sannadkii 1967.
Waxay taageeraan Stacie sababtoo ah taariikhdiisa ah ballaarinta fursadaha dhaqaale iyo hubinta in bulshooyinka Degmada 3 ay cod dhab ah ku leeyihiin Statehouse-ka.
Askartii Hore • 2026
Buckeye Veterans Coalition PAC
Guddi ficil siyaasadeed oo garab taagan musharraxiinta wax u qabta askartii hore ee Ohio iyo qoysaska ciidamada.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay askartii hore ee Ohio ku qabaan taariikhdiisa adeegga dadweynaha iyo ballanqaadkiisa ku aaddan ragga iyo dumarka soo adeegay.
Sinnaanta • 2026
Ohio Pride PAC
Guddigii ugu horreeyay ee ficilka siyaasadeed ee LGBTQ+ ee heer gobol ka jira Ohio, oo taageera musharraxiinta u dooda sinnaanta kuna qiimeeya xildhibaan kasta oo Ohio ah diiwaankiisa sinnaanta.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsooni ku aaddan ballanqaadkiisa xuquuqda iyo sharafta qof kasta oo reer Ohio ah.
Hoggaamiyeyaasha Diinta • 2026
Baptist Ministerial Alliance of Greater Columbus
Waxay isku keentaa hoggaamiyayaasha kaniisadaha Baptist-ka ee aagga Columbus, iyagoo matalaya jamaacado u adeega tobanaan kun oo qoys oo bartamaha Ohio ah.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay bulshada diintu ku qabto dabeecadda Stacie, qiyamkiisa, iyo ballanqaadkiisa caddaaladda dhaqaalaha.
Ururrada Deegaanka • 2026
Ohio Environmental Council Action Committee
Ururka ugu hormuudka ah ee Ohio ee u dooda deegaanka, oo u shaqeeya hawo nadiif ah, biyo nadiif ah, iyo tallaabo cimilo oo macno leh.
Taageeradoodu waxay xaqiijinaysaa in Stacie uu u doodi doono deegaanka Ohio iyo caafimaadka dadweynaha gudaha Senate-ka Gobolka.
Save Ohio Parks
Isbahaysi gebi ahaanba mutadawiciin ah oo ka kooban dad reer Ohio ah oo ka shaqeeya in fracking laga fogeeyo beeraha dabiiciga ah ee gobolka Ohio, kaymaha, iyo aagagga duurjoogta.
Taageeradoodu waxay muujinaysaa kalsoonida ay u doodayaasha dhulalka dadweynaha ee Ohio ku qabaan ballanqaadkiisa ah ilaalinta dhulalka dadweynaha ee uu qof kasta oo reer Ohio ah wadaago.
