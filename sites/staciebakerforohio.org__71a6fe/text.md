About the Candidate
Stacie Baker has dedicated more than 15 years to public service, working alongside local governments, community leaders, and families to strengthen communities across Central Ohio.
Throughout his career, he has earned a reputation for thoughtful leadership, fiscal responsibility, and bringing people together to solve complex challenges.
A three-term At-Large Reynoldsburg City Councilmember and current Council President Pro Tempore, Stacie has helped lead one of Central Ohio's fastest-growing communities while serving as Chair of the Finance Committee, where he has overseen responsible budgeting and transparent stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
His work has focused on protecting public resources, strengthening neighborhoods, and ensuring government remains accountable to the people it serves.
As Reynoldsburg's representative on the West Licking County Joint Fire District Board, Stacie has worked to strengthen regional partnerships that improve public safety and emergency preparedness.
He has consistently supported smart infrastructure investments, economic growth, and policies that create opportunity for working families while helping communities plan for the future.
A graduate of The Ohio State University and holder of a Master of Public Administration from Ohio University, Stacie combines academic preparation with years of hands-on public service.
As a husband, father of two, and lifelong public servant, he believes Ohio deserves leaders who listen first, lead with integrity, and put people ahead of politics.
