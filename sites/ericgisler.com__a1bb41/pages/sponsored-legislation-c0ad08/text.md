Amends the current Official Code of Georgia Annotated in regards to the Department of Public Health and its treatment of federal immigration detainees.
This bill calls for a third party monitoring of detention facilities to ensure they are compliant with state and federal regulations.
The third party will provide an audit of their findings, and this bill would allow state and local governments to cease any cooperation with federal agencies if violations are found.
This bill would allow families on the state health plan who experience a stillbirth to qualify for parental leave.
Gives families the time they need to recover from the physical, mental, and psychological stress caused by this hardship.
Taking this protective mental health measure will allow the family to return to the workforce in a more sound and productive state of mind.
Link: https://www.legis.ga.gov/legislation/73476
Comment: This issue was brought to me by a constituent who had experienced a stillbirth and was initially denied the ability to take leave.
They were not given their leave until they got an attorney involved, so this bill would have clarified that stillbirth is a qualified reason for parental leave.
