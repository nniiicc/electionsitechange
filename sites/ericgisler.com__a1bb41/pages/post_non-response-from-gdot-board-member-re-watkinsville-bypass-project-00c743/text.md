The District 10 GDOT Board Member is Jamie Boswell .
On October 2, I mailed a letter to Mr.
Boswell, asking for his assistance in ensuring that all relevant data and studies related to the Watkinsville Bypass Project are released to the public before any decision is made or the next Public Information Open House is held.
I followed up with an email the following week.
To date, no response has been received, and no new materials have been added to the project website .
Therefore, I am making my letter public.

The public needs to know.
