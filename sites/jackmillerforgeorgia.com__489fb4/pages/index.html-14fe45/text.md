Meet Jack
A local financial planner with a record of serving his community.
Jack Miller grew up in Milton, met his wife Taylor at Milton High School, and came back after the University of Georgia to build his life and business here.
He serves as president of the Milton First Responders Foundation, supporting the officers and firefighters who answer when we call.
In 2025, the City of Milton honored his work with the Silver Horseshoe of Service Award.
