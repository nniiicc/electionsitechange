Wyndmere senior center building campaign reaches 85% of goal after $10,000 donation
Terry Goerger's contribution helps advance plans for a new facility that will serve local seniors and provide space for community gatherings.
"I've been blessed to have had a good farming career and a good seed business career.
I want to give back to those communities that supported me.
I think small towns need a place to meet.
Seniors utilize their current facility a lot, and my understanding is this could be a meeting place for other organizations in the community.
It's a plus for a community to have a center like that."
