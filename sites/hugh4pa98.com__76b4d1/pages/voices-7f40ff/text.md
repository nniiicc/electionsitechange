Why the ACA matters
Before the Affordable Care Act became law in 2010, I was stuck.
I was 23 years old when my two-year-old child was diagnosed with a pre-existing condition, and suddenly my job wasn’t just a paycheck — it was the only way to keep my family insured.
The ACA changed everything.
It ensured my child couldn’t be denied coverage, and it let me keep my children on my insurance until they turned 26.
For my family, that law wasn’t politics.
It was protection.
