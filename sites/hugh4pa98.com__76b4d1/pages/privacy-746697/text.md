Information We Collect Automatically
If you visit our website to browse, read, or download information, your web browser automatically sends us (and we may retain) information such as the:
- Internet domain through which you access the Internet (e.g., yourServiceProvider.com if you use a commercial Internet service provider, or yourSchool.edu if you use an Internet account from your school);
- Internet Protocol address of the computer you are using;
- type of browser software and operating system you are using;
- date and time you access our site; and

We will use this information as aggregate data to help us maintain this site, e.g., to determine the number of visitors to different sections of our site, to ensure the site is working properly, and to help us make our site more accessible and useful.
We will not use this information to identify individuals, except for site security or law enforcement purposes.
Except as described under “Advertising and Retargeting” below, we will not obtain personally identifying information about you when you visit our site, unless you choose to provide such information.

Website Analytics
We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how visitors use this site — for example, which pages are viewed and how many people visit.
Cloudflare Web Analytics is privacy-focused: it does not use cookies, does not perform cross-site or cross-device tracking, and does not collect or store personal information or build a profile of you.
We use the aggregated measurement data only to improve this site.
For more information, see Cloudflare’s privacy documentation.
Advertising and Retargeting
This site carries advertising tags that support the campaign’s paid advertising.
Unlike the analytics described above, these do involve third-party tracking: they may set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser, record that you visited this site, and allow the campaign to show you advertising on other websites, apps, and connected-TV services you use later.
Specifically, the site carries a retargeting tag operated by Simpli.fi, and a Google Tag Manager container operated by the campaign’s advertising vendor.
Google Tag Manager is a container that allows the vendor to load additional measurement and advertising tags — which may include Google Analytics and similar services, and which may change over time without a corresponding change to this page.
Those tags are operated by the vendor and the relevant third parties rather than by the campaign, and any information they collect is handled under their own privacy policies.
These tags are active on every page of this site and apply to all visitors.
How to opt out.
You can limit or prevent this tracking by using your browser’s settings to block or delete third-party cookies, by enabling a “Do Not Track” or tracking-prevention feature, or through the industry opt-out tools operated by the Digital Advertising Alliance (optout.aboutads.info) and the Network Advertising Initiative (optout.networkadvertising.org).
Opting out does not affect your ability to use this site.
