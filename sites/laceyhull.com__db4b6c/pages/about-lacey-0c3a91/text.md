About Lacey Hull
Lacey Hull is a native Houstonian who was born and raised in House District 138, attended Spring Branch ISD schools and earned a bachelor's in political science and history from the University of Houston.
First elected in 2020, Lacey and her family live in Spring Branch and attend Bayou City Fellowship Church.
Lacey currently serves as Chair of the House Committee on Human Services which covers issues about which she is passionate, including child welfare and services for Texans with disabilities.
Lacey was also appointed to serve on the State Affairs committee and State Affairs subcommittee on Telecommunications and Broadband.
She serves as the House of Representatives’ ex officio member to the Permanent Judicial Commission for Children, Youth, and Families.
In 2024, Lacey was appointed to serve on the powerful Sunset Advisory Commission, which regularly audits state agencies and programs.
She was also appointed to the Joint Committee to study the Effects of Media on Minors.
Lacey was re-elected to serve as Vice-chair of the bipartisan Criminal Justice Reform Caucus and has previously served as Chair of the Harris County delegation and Vice-chair of the House Republican Caucus.
Lacey is a also proud advocate for the economic prosperity of the Houston area through her membership of the Texas Legislative Ports Caucus and the Aerospace Caucus.
Lacey has championed legislation to Make Texas Healthy Again by promoting nutrition services, education, and consumer transparency; protect victims of crime, particularly crimes against women and children; safeguard children online; and provide protections for the rights of families in the public education and child welfare systems.
Prior to being elected to the Texas House, Lacey worked with a variety of nonprofits to advocate for policies that protect the liberties of Texas families.
As a State Representative, Lacey is committed to preserving individual liberty, unleashing economic opportunity, ensuring public safety, and providing quality education for all students.
As our state representative, Lacey has a proven record of fighting for our values:
✓ TOUGH ON VIOLENT CRIME
Lacey Hull voted to stop no-cash bail for violent criminals and voted to end parole for rapists and murderers.
✓ LOWERING THE COST OF HEALTH CARE
Lacey Hull supports policies that lower the cost of insurance and expand access to healthcare, helping all Texas families.
✓ PAY RAISES FOR PUBLIC SCHOOLS
Lacey Hull voted for pay raises for public school librarians, counselors, nurses, and teachers who take on harder teaching jobs.
✓ AI REGULATIONS
Lacey Hull passed AI regulations, including banning behavioral manipulation, social scoring, and deepfakes.
✓ REIN IN HOME APPRAISAL PROCESS
Lacey Hull will fight to lower property taxes by reining in the out-of-control home appraisal process which will bring down property taxes once and for all.
