When life brings you to Idaho and gives you potatoes… MAKEPEACE!
Introducing our complete skin care bath line made entirely from 100% organic Idaho potatoes.
The nostalgic romance of hand-made soap is the inspiration for our signature formula consisting of only the finest ingredients.
Our soap can be used for all skin types.
Nourishing organic potato softens and smooths skin.
Hygiene and physical dignity is a global human right.
Soap is a basic life necessity, but for millions of displaced people around the world, this is unattainable.
For every product sold MAKEPEACE will donate one bar of soap to a child or adult in a refugee camp.
