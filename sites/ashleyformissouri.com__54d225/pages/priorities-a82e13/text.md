Reproductive Freedom Preserving Access to Life-Saving Care Bodily autonomy is under attack once again in Missouri with another Amendment 3 on the ballot—but this time it's an abortion ban.
Missouri spoke loud and clear in 2024 by enshrining abortion access in our consititution via Amendment 3, but as usual, Republicans in Jefferson City think they know better than their voters, and are asking us to vote on it again.
This time, however, they're misleading voters by claiming the measure is about healthcare for trans kids.
Make no mistake: if you want to protect reproductive freedom in Missouri, you'll vote NO on Amendment 3 .
As your State Representative and the Democratic Floor Leader, I remain committed to working across the aisle to improve Missouri's social safety nets for pregnant women and families with newborns.
I will never stop fighting for your freedom to control your most personal healthcare decisions—because it’s none of my business what you do in the privacy of an exam room.
Veterans Issues Proudly Serving our Military Members In 2021, Governor Parson appointed me to the Missouri Military Preparedness and Enhancement Commission (MMPEC) , where I work with colleagues and stakeholders from across the state to make Missouri the best possible place for military families and veterans to live.
From developing employment resources for military spouses to allocating more of our state budget to installations that need it, we are always looking for ways to give back to the folks who have sacrificed so much for our freedoms.
I've carried bipartisan legislation to eliminate property tax burdens for 100% service-disabled military veterans, and I will continue to focus on the issues most important to our military community.
Economic Opportunity FOR ALL Protecting Workers' Rights As a small business owner, I am committed to promoting responsible economic development and attracting good paying jobs to the Northland.
I believe our economy should work for everyone.
