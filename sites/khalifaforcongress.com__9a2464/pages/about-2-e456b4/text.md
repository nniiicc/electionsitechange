Fighting for Its Future.
Zyon Khalifa, a native of Aiken County, is an Air Force reservist and former track star at the University of South Carolina.
At age 25, he is the youngest nominee for a congressional seat in South Carolina history, having prevailed in a June 2026 runoff over the Democrats’ 2024 nominee.
He faces incumbent Rep.
Joe Wilson, a Republican who has held the Aiken-Lexington-Columbia- area seat since the year Zyon was born.
Having spent part of his teenage years in foster care, Zyon learned that life wasn’t going to wait for him to be ready to live it.
The starting gun fired early on Zyon’s adulthood, and he’s been running — literally and figuratively — ever since.
At Silver Bluff High School, he was the state’s top-ranked high school track athlete.
He also played football, was a ranked wrestler, golfed and fenced.
The Aiken Standard in 2019 named him male athlete of the year.
Zyon joined the Air Force reserves in 2020 as a specialist in transportation logistics while running track at the University of South Carolina, adding rugby to his sports.
He also worked part time as a mechanic before earning his BA in business administration in 2023.
In May he earned a Juris Doctor from Southern University Law Center in Baton Rouge, Louisiana, where he worked with the local public defender’s office.
Shaped by strong community, meaningful opportunity and responsible institutions, Zyon believes a person’s starting position in life should never determine how far they go.
That philosophy has informed his work with the ACLU of Colorado, the Alliance for Children’s Rights and community-based law offices in South Carolina and Louisiana.
Leadership That Listens Zyon is running to ensure South Carolina’s District 2 finally has a representative who listens to working families, rural communities, young voters, and small-town residents who too often feel ignored.
His campaign is rooted in the belief that government should serve the people who live in the community — and that real change begins when leadership understands the lives of the people it represents.
Building the Future — Together Zyon Khalifa isn’t running to be a career politician.
He’s running to bring real representation, real opportunity, and a real future to the place he calls home.
Because South Carolina’s District 2 deserves leadership from someone who grew up here, believes in its people, and is ready to fight for its future.
My Approach My approach is simple: listen first, act with purpose, and focus on results that create real opportunity for our communities.
I believe safer neighborhoods, stronger schools, and growing local economies all start with one thing — making sure families and young people have real pathways to success.
Leadership isn’t about talking louder in Washington; it’s about delivering solutions that work here at home in South Carolina’s District 2.
