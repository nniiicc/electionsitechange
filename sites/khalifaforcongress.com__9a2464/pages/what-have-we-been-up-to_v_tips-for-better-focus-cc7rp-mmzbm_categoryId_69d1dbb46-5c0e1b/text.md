Lexington County Democratic Convention
When I stood at the Lexington County Democratic Convention, one question drove everything I said, why not us?
Why not the people who’ve been overlooked and told to wait their turn?
And yes, I’m young.
But that’s not a weakness, it’s our strength.
My generation isn’t guessing about the challenges we face, we’re living them.
We bring energy, urgency, and a real connection to the future we’re fighting for.
My age isn’t a barrier, it’s a bridge.
A bridge to new voters, new ideas, and new possibilities.
So I’ll ask it again—why not us?
And more importantly—why not now?
