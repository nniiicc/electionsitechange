Community Conversations: Building the Future Together
Join congressional candidate Zyon Khalifa for Community Conversations, an engaging and solutions-focused event bringing together voices from across our community.
This event will feature local leaders and candidates, including Malcolm Green (SC House District 86) and Darnell Holston (SC House District 84), as we discuss the issues that matter most to Aiken County and beyond.
This is more than just a speaking event, it’s an opportunity to be heard.
Attendees will have the chance to share their concerns, ideas, and vision for the future directly with candidates who are committed to putting people first.
At a time when many feel overlooked, Community Conversations is about restoring connection, accountability, and real dialogue between leaders and the communities they serve.
Your voice matters.
Your perspective matters.
This is your seat at the table.
