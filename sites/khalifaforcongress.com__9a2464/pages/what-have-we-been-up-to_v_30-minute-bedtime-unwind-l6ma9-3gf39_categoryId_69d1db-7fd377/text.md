The Type Of Candidate I Am
I’m running to be the candidate who truly understands the struggles of all people, not just a select few.
Because too often, leadership speaks for communities without ever listening to them.
I’ve seen what it looks like when people feel overlooked.
I’ve seen families working hard just to stay afloat, young people questioning if opportunity still exists for them, and communities being told to wait while their needs go unmet.
That’s not leadership, that’s disconnect.
This campaign is different.
It’s about listening first, showing up, and making sure every voice is heard, no matter your background, your zip code, or your story.
Because when we actually understand each other, we can build solutions that work for everyone.
That’s the kind of leadership I’m fighting for.
Why not us?
