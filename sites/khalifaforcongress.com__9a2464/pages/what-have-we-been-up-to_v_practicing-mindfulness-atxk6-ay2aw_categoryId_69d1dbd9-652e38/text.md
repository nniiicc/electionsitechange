No Kings Rally
At the No Kings Rally, Zyon Khalifa delivered a powerful message centered on unity, people power, and the belief that leadership should always answer to the people—not rule over them.
Speaking to a diverse crowd, he emphasized that this movement goes beyond party lines.
It’s about standing together, rejecting division, and recognizing that real power comes from the collective voice of the community.
This speech is a call to action, for people of all backgrounds to step forward, get involved, and reclaim their role in shaping the future.
Because this is bigger than politics.
This is about us.
