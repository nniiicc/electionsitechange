Watch the ad here .
In the ad, Rutinel narrates in Spanish his story of growing up on Medicaid and being raised by a hardworking single mom and his time as an economist in the U.S.
Army Corps of Engineers.
He then emphasizes his frustration with political corruption and walks voters through his work to lower insulin prices, crack down on grocery price gouging, and building more affordable housing in the Colorado State House.
The ad will be backed by a buy of at least $50,000 on Spanish-language broadcast TV and digital platforms.
