“When it comes to abortion, the government needs to respect Americans’ freedom to make our own decisions about our own families without interference from politicians or anyone else.
Politicians have no place in a private medical decision,” said Rutinel.
Reproductive Freedom for All joins Planned Parenthood in backing Manny Rutinel as he continues to make access to health care a central focus of his campaign.
The endorsement comes as Rutinel takes on Rep.
Gabe Evans, who blatantly lied to Coloradans about cutting their Medicaid.

Between Rutinel and Evans, the contrast could not be clearer who will stand up for Coloradans’ health care.
