The easiest and most secure way to vote is to return your ballot as soon as you can to a drop box.
Ballots must be delivered to clerks by 7PM on Tuesday, November 3 and a postmark does not count, so if you’re sending your ballot in the mail rather than a drop box, we recommend mailing it by Monday, October 26.
Make sure to sign the back of your ballot envelope before dropping it off or putting it in the mail.

Is it too Late?
Register or update your address online at GoVoteColorado.gov by Monday, October 26 to have a ballot mailed to you.
After October 26 you can register and vote in person at any voter service and polling center in your county, through 7PM on Tuesday, November 3.
Be sure to bring an ID .
Adams County In Person Voting Weld County In Person Voting Larimer County In Person Voting Can I Vote In Person?
Yes, you can also vote in-person at a voter service and polling center location.
In-person voting is available from 10/19-11/3, please see your clerk's website for weekend dates and times.
Adams County In Person Voting Weld County In Person Voting Larimer County In Person Voting Who's Eligible to Vote for Manny?
All registered voters living within Colorado’s Congressional District 8 are eligible to vote for Manny in the general election.
Unsure if you live in Manny’s district?
Look up your registration at GoVoteColorado.gov .
If you do not live in Congressional District 8, you are not eligible to vote for Manny.
How Do I Know My Ballot Was Counted?
Sign up for BallotTrax to get a text or email when your ballot is mailed to you, when the county receives it, and when it's counted.
If there's a problem with your signature, the county will contact you.
Other Questions?
If you have another question about voting for Manny, you can contact us .
If you have a question about voting processes or procedures not answered here, you can also reach out directly to your county’s election office, or the Colorado Democratic Party’s voting website.
