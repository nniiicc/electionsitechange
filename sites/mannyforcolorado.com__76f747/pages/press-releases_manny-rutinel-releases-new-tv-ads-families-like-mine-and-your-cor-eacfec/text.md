“Your Corner” is jointly funded by the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee (DCCC).
In “ Families Like Mine ,” Rutinel highlights how he’ll fight to lower costs for families and take on corruption in Washington.
WATCH “FAMILIES LIKE MINE” Transcript Families like mine don't have millions of dollars to give to politicians.
Single moms, kids on Medicaid, folks working two or three jobs, and we're getting ripped off by corporations buying off Congress.
I'm Manny Rutinel.
I don't take their money.
And in Congress, I'll fight to ban corporate donations to politicians, ban stock trading by Congress and finally pass term limits.
I approve this message to get Congress working for us.
In “ Your Corner ,” Rutinel calls out Gabe Evans’ record voting 100% of the time with Trump – including to cut Medicaid, double health insurance premiums, protect cost-hiking tariffs, and greenlight a reckless war in Iran.
WATCH “YOUR CORNER” Transcript The first fighter I ever knew never threw a punch.
My mom worked two jobs and we survived on Medicaid during tough times.
She always fought for us.
Gabe Evans only fights for Trump.
He's in Trump's corner.
He votes 100% with Trump.
He voted to cut Medicaid, double health insurance premiums, [and] for the war and tariffs raising our gas and grocery bills.
I'm Manny Rutinel.
My mom taught me to fight for what matters.
