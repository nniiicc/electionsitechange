Meet April Endorsements Campaign News In the News Press Releases Get Involved Get Updates Volunteer Press Releases New Poll: McClain Delaney Maintains Significant Lead Jun 10, 2026 New Poll: McClain Delaney Maintains Significant Lead With two weeks until Election Day and early voting starting tomorrow, a new poll shows Congresswoman April McClain Delaney maintaining her commanding 15% lead over David Trone.
The poll, conducted by Hart Research Associates between June 2 and 4, 2026, found Congresswoman McClain Delaney leading with 52% of the vote to David Trone’s 37%.
While Congresswoman McClain Delaney’s advantage has remained consistent throughout the primary, this is the first poll showing her support surpassing 50% as she consolidates undecided voters who are rejecting Trone’s unwarranted and relentlessly negative campaign.
“Voters across Maryland’s Sixth District are overwhelmingly backing Congresswoman April McClain Delaney because of her strong record of delivering for her constituents and standing up to Donald Trump,” said McClain Delaney campaign manager Nick London.
Congresswoman McClain Delaney has also secured the endorsements of Governor Moore, Attorney General Brown, Lt.
Governor Miller, Senators Van Hollen and Alsobrooks, Congressman Jamie Raskin, and the entire Maryland Democratic congressional delegation, as well as local leaders and unions across the district.
Her broad and growing coalition is a reflection of her tireless efforts to focus on the issues that matter most to families in Maryland’s Sixth District: Holding the Trump Administration Accountable: Leading the fight against dangerous executive overreach and opposing illegal military actions.
Stopping the ICE Warehouse near Hagerstown: Standing with local communities to successfully block a proposed detention facility in the district.
Lowering Costs: Advancing her bold affordability agenda to address the rising costs of housing, healthcare, and everyday essentials, while demanding that billionaires pay their fair share.
