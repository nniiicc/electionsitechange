“As the proud Representative of Maryland’s amazing 6 th District, I have been showing up everywhere and fighting for the safety, health and well-being of everyone in the District – including our 35,000 government workers, our farmers, our first responders, our military and national parks personnel, our hospital workers and healthcare communities and especially our hard working families trying to make ends meet.
We must work to lower costs for working families, to protect access to healthcare, to preserve SNAP and hunger assistance programs and to reinforce support for our government and those who serve it.
As a mother of four daughters, I’ll never stop fighting for a woman’s right to choose and ensuring women have a seat at the table.
My work is grounded by my values and a firm belief in the singular importance of our democracy, my view that we bow to no king, and by the strength, optimism and virtue of our people and our communities.
“Sadly, the intent of this Administration is to ‘Make America Break’ by undermining public trust in our government, in our democratic institutions and in one another – these are not normal times.
These lawless, cruel hearted budget cuts and policies are meant to dismantle government and instill fear, and will ultimately result in hurting everyone irrespective of zip code or circumstance.
The current shutdown of our federal government, and the continued refusal of our GOP colleagues to engage in bipartisan discussions to prevent millions of Americans from having their healthcare costs skyrocket, has strengthen my resolve to push back against these attacks and to fight for my district.
Accordingly, I am filing for re-election.
“I am very fortunate to be a part of Team Maryland, including our collaborative Democratic Congressional Delegation and the leadership of Governor Wes Moore.
In Maryland we strive to “leave no one behind” and to further a brighter, more prosperous and inclusive future for everyone.
I thank the Governor, his Administration and the entire Democratic Congressional Delegation for their support and look forward to making the progress needed by our state at this critical moment.” With the news of her announcement, the Governor and Maryland Delegation shared the following endorsements: “Congresswoman McClain Delaney is the fighter Maryland’s 6th District needs as our state faces unprecedented challenges,” said Governor Wes Moore.
“She is an essential member of Team Maryland that delivers for our state—working for flood assistance in Western Maryland and standing up for our furloughed federal workers.
