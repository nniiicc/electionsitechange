CONGRESSWOMAN APRIL MCCLAIN DELANEY ENDORSED BY LT.
GOVERNOR ARUNA MILLER AND ATTORNEY GENERAL ANTHONY BROWN
Gaithersburg – November 13, 2025 – Just days after announcing her re-election campaign and being endorsed by the entire Federal delegation and Governor Wes Moore, Congresswoman April McClain Delaney received endorsements from Lt.
Governor Aruna Miller and Attorney General Anthony Brown.
“Congresswoman April McClain Delaney has been a fierce advocate for her district and all of Maryland, standing up for our federal workers, fighting for flood recovery funds, and pushing back against the tariffs that are rising costs for Maryland families,” said Lt.
Governor Aruna Miller.
“She has proven herself to be a leader, and as one of only three women in the Maryland delegation, a welcome advocate for women and families.
We need her in Congress, and I’m proud to endorse her reelection.”
“Congresswoman McClain Delaney is the fighter Maryland’s 6th District needs as our state faces unprecedented challenges,” said Governor Wes Moore.
“She is an essential member of Team Maryland that delivers for our state—working for flood assistance in Western Maryland and standing up for our furloughed federal workers.
Together, we will continue to use bipartisan solutions to build a Maryland that leaves no one behind.” – Governor Wes Moore
“Every day, I work closely with our state leaders to make life better for the families of Maryland’s 6th District,” said Congresswoman McClain Delaney.
“Their partnership and support mean so much to me as we continue delivering results that matter—good jobs, safe communities, and a government that works for everyone,” said Congresswoman April McClain Delaney.
Since taking office, Congresswoman McClain Delaney has been a proven advocate for Maryland families—delivering results and working hand-in-hand with state, county, and local partners to strengthen our communities.
From standing up for federal workers targeted by the Trump administration to securing funding for critical infrastructure and fighting for flood recovery projects, she has shown an unwavering commitment to the people she serves.
In addition to Lt.
Governor Aruna Miller and Attorney General Anthony Brown, Congresswoman April McClain Delaney has been endorsed by Governor Wes Moore and the entire federal delegation including Senator Chris Van Hollen, Senator Angela Alsobrooks, Congressman Jamie Raskin, Congressman Steny Hoyer, Congresswoman Sarah Elfreth, Congressman Kweisi Mfume, Congressman Glenn Ivey, and Congressman Johnny Olszewski.
