April McClain Delaney Releases First Television Ad
Frederick, MD — April McClain Delaney’s campaign has released its first ad, focusing on her decades of experience in public service.
The ad will run district wide and is the first in an advertising blitz set to include television, mail, and digital advertising.
The ad speaks to April’s focus on “common sense, common ground” solutions and the need to build coalitions to get things done for the families of the 6th District.
Watch the ad here.
The ad follows the announcement of a slate of endorsements from leaders across the District.
Transcript:
Having raised four daughters – I know how to work problems out.
I’m April McClain Delaney, running for Congress to rein in MAGA extremists and find common ground.
I served in the Biden Administration as a tech policy leader.
And helped build a non-profit dedicated to protecting kids on-line.
Let’s safeguard kids’ privacy, address the mental health crisis — and for my 4 daughters and yours, regain the right to choose.
I’m April McClain Delaney and I approve this message – it’s time for some common sense.
