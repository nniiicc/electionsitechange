Get to Know
April McClain Delaney
April brings more than 30 years of experience in Communications Law, Regulatory Affairs, and Public Advocacy to the race for Maryland’s 6th Congressional District.
Most recently, April served as the Deputy Assistant Secretary for Communications in the Biden Administration’s Department of Commerce.
Prior to her presidential appointment, April served as the Washington, D.C.
Director and Board Member of Common Sense Media, a leading nonprofit dedicated to understanding how the ever-changing media and technology landscape impacts children’s health and wellbeing.
