Why I am running
I'm Genevieve Duchesneau.
My wife and I chose Proctor as our home in 2022, drawn to Vermont's local-first spirit and a community we wanted to be part of for the long haul.
I spent more than a decade in technology building tools to make people's lives easier.
I'm running for the Vermont House to put that same problem-solving to work for Pittsford and Proctor, and to be a representative who shows up, listens, and delivers all year round.
