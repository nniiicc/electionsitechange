See campaign updates → Why I’m running I’ve spent my career in public service, education, and civil-rights oversight.
I’ve seen government at its best—and I’ve seen what happens when institutions stop listening, oversight disappears, and ordinary people are expected to absorb the consequences.
Families across NJ-03 are dealing with housing costs, healthcare bills, student debt, utility costs, threats to civil rights, and a government that too often seems more responsive to powerful interests than working people.
I’m running because government should help people live with dignity—and because Congress needs people willing to ask hard questions, follow the evidence, protect rights, and actually do the work.
