I’m running as an Independent to represent NJ-03 with steady judgment and real oversight—without automatic party loyalty or donor-driven incentives.
Independence: policy positions are guided by constituents and constitutional responsibility.
Clarity: clear rules about what I will and won’t accept.
Transparency: timely disclosure, consistent with federal law.
Accountability: decisions explained in plain language, with an emphasis on oversight.
What I will not accept To reduce conflicts of interest and keep incentives aligned with voters, I will not accept: Corporate PAC contributions Industry trade association PAC contributions These boundaries are intended to reduce conflicts of interest and avoid situations where voters reasonably wonder whether a vote was influenced by the political spending of a corporation or regulated industry.
Organizational support I may consider As permitted by federal law, I may consider lawful support from organizations grounded in worker or member representation, civic participation, civil liberties, democratic reform, or evidence-based public policy.
Organizational support does not automatically mean a contribution to the campaign.
Examples of categories I may consider include: Labor organizations Membership-based organizations Issue-based advocacy organizations Democracy reform organizations Civil liberties and constitutional rights organizations Science and evidence-based policy organizations An endorsement or other form of support does not buy influence, create control, or mean agreement on every issue.
My responsibility remains to the people of NJ-03 and the Constitution.
Disclosure and accountability Campaign contributions are disclosed in accordance with federal law.
Federal campaign-finance filings are public, and I will keep the campaign website updated with clear information about the campaign’s funding policies and public disclosures.
What you can expect Compliance with federal campaign-finance reporting requirements Clear disclosure of the campaign’s funding boundaries Plain-language explanations of organizational support and contributions Clear pathways to public campaign-finance filings If you see a filing or public statement and have a question, I’d rather address it directly than let assumptions fill the gap.

What PAC contributions or organizational support will you accept?
I do not accept contributions from corporate PACs or industry trade association PACs.
As permitted by federal law, I may consider lawful support from labor, membership-based, or issue-focused organizations consistent with the campaign’s funding principles.
No contribution or endorsement buys influence or determines my positions.
How can I verify what you’re saying?
Federal campaign-finance filings are public.
I will also make it easy to find relevant disclosures and plain-language explanations on the campaign website.
If you have a question about a filing or the campaign’s funding policy, I welcome it.
Does support from an organization mean you agree with them on everything?
No.
Support is not control, and an endorsement is not the same thing as a campaign contribution.
I will work with people and organizations in good faith when it benefits NJ-03 and remains consistent with the Constitution and the law.
When I disagree, I’ll say so.
