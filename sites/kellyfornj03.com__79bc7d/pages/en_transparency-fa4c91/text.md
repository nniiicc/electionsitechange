Funding principles
My campaign is built to earn support, not purchase it.
I’m running as an Independent to represent NJ-03 with steady judgment and real oversight—without automatic party loyalty or donor-driven incentives.
- Independence: policy positions are guided by constituents and constitutional responsibility.
- Clarity: clear rules about what I will and won’t accept.
- Transparency: timely disclosure, consistent with federal law.
- Accountability: decisions explained in plain language, with an emphasis on oversight.
What I will not accept
To reduce conflicts of interest and keep incentives aligned with voters, I will not accept:
- Corporate PAC contributions
- Industry trade association PAC contributions
These boundaries are intended to reduce conflicts of interest and avoid situations where voters reasonably wonder whether a vote was influenced by the political spending of a corporation or regulated industry.
Organizational support I may consider
As permitted by federal law, I may consider lawful support from organizations grounded in worker or member representation, civic participation, civil liberties, democratic reform, or evidence-based public policy.
Organizational support does not automatically mean a contribution to the campaign.
Examples of categories I may consider include:
- Labor organizations
- Membership-based organizations
- Issue-based advocacy organizations
- Democracy reform organizations
- Civil liberties and constitutional rights organizations
- Science and evidence-based policy organizations
An endorsement or other form of support does not buy influence, create control, or mean agreement on every issue.
My responsibility remains to the people of NJ-03 and the Constitution.
Disclosure and accountability
Campaign contributions are disclosed in accordance with federal law.
Federal campaign-finance filings are public, and I will keep the campaign website updated with clear information about the campaign’s funding policies and public disclosures.
What you can expect
- Compliance with federal campaign-finance reporting requirements
- Clear disclosure of the campaign’s funding boundaries
- Plain-language explanations of organizational support and contributions
- Clear pathways to public campaign-finance filings
If you see a filing or public statement and have a question, I’d rather address it directly than let assumptions fill the gap.
