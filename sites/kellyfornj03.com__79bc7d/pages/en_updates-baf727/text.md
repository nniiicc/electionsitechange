Accountability and the NJ-03 Nomination Petition Process September 25, 2026 I want NJ-03 voters to hear directly from me about public records I obtained from the New Jersey Division of Elections concerning Steven Welzer’s 2026 nomination petition.
In this statement, I discuss what the records show, New Jersey’s petition-circulator requirements, the State’s forms and instructions, and the questions I believe deserve answers.
I also discuss why the State should review its procedures so future candidates and circulators receive clear guidance.
No court or election official has determined Steven Welzer to be disqualified.
I believe that context matters.
My goal is to put the documented facts before voters, distinguish what is known from what remains unresolved, and make appropriately redacted source records available for independent review.

I spent nearly 13 years working in DHS's Office for Civil Rights and Civil Liberties (CRCL).
I support restoring CRCL and strengthening civil-rights and civil-liberties oversight so these offices have the resources they need to do their work effectively.
I also support stronger statutory protections for independent oversight functions so they are much harder for any administration to simply dismantle.
Government works better when there are institutions empowered to ask hard questions, protect rights, investigate concerns, and hold agencies accountable.
