The League originally invited candidates on July 20 and requested responses by July 23.
The video below also explains the election-law significance of September 19.
Federal law generally requires a validly requested absentee ballot for an absent uniformed-services or overseas voter to be transmitted no later than 45 days before a federal election when the request was received at least 45 days before the election.
For New Jersey’s 2026 General Election, September 19 is the commencement of mailing of general-election mail-in ballots.
It is not the deadline for military and overseas voters to vote.
New Jersey lists October 30 as the deadline to apply to receive a General Election mail-in ballot by electronic means for qualified overseas civilian and military voters.
The October 5 forum therefore takes place after mail ballots begin going out, while military and overseas voting options remain available under New Jersey law.
