Privacy
We respect your privacy.
This website collects only the information you choose to submit, such as through volunteer and email signup forms.
What we collect
- Information you provide (such as name, email, phone number, ZIP code, or messages you send us)
- For student or minor volunteer administration, information you choose to provide about a parent or guardian, emergency contact, volunteer availability, or recommendation request
- Basic technical information (such as browser type or pages visited) to help us maintain and improve the site
Donations
Donations are processed by a third-party provider (Donorbox).
We do not store your full payment card information on this website.
Donation-related information is handled in accordance with Donorbox’s policies and those of its payment processors.
Volunteer and email signups
Volunteer and email signup forms are handled through Action Network.
Information submitted through those forms is used only for campaign-related communication and organizing.
How we use your information
- To communicate with you about the campaign
- To respond to questions or volunteer inquiries
- To administer student and minor-volunteer participation, safety procedures, and volunteer recommendation requests
- To understand how people use the site and improve it
Sharing
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share information with trusted service providers that help operate the campaign, only as necessary to provide those services.
Your choices
- You can unsubscribe from campaign emails at any time using the link provided in each email.
- You may request updates or deletion of your information by contacting us.
Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at info@KellyForNJ03.com.
This policy may be updated as campaign tools evolve.
The current version will always be posted on this page.
