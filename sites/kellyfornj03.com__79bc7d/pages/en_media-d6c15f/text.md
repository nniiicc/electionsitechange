Mailing address: PO Box 68, Hightstown, NJ 08520 Candidate bio Ryan Michael Kelly is an Independent candidate for Congress in New Jersey’s 3rd District.
An elected municipal official with a background in public service, education, and civil rights, he has worked in federal civil rights oversight and taught at both the secondary and higher education levels.
Kelly is running on a platform centered on affordability, healthcare reform that reduces waste and middlemen, student loan relief, and strong ethics and accountability in government.
