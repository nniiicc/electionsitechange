Accessibility
We want this website to be usable for everyone, including people with disabilities.
We’re working to keep the site clear, readable, and compatible with assistive technologies.
Accessibility goals
- Readable typography and clear page structure
- Keyboard-friendly navigation where possible
- Reasonable color contrast and legible text
- Descriptive labels and link text
- Compatibility with screen readers and browser accessibility tools
Known limitations
Some parts of this site use third-party services (for example, donation and signup forms).
Those services may have their own accessibility standards and limitations.
If something isn’t working for you, please tell us—we’ll do our best to help and to improve what we control.
Need help or want to report an issue?
If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please email info@KellyForNJ03.com with:
- The page URL
- What you were trying to do
- What went wrong (and any error message)
- Your device, browser, and any assistive technology you’re using (optional)
We review accessibility feedback and will prioritize fixes that improve access for the most people.
