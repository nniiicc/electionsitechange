Read about the Rowan volunteer fair → For campaign volunteers Requesting a letter of recommendation Volunteers who have made a meaningful contribution may request a recommendation for college, scholarships, internships, employment, or other opportunities.
Please provide your volunteer dates and duties, the purpose of the letter, the recipient or program, the deadline, and submission instructions.
After submitting the request form, send your résumé and any supporting materials to info@KellyForNJ03.com and note that you submitted a recommendation request.
A request does not guarantee a letter.
Please provide as much advance notice as possible so the campaign can determine whether an appropriate recommendation can be provided.
