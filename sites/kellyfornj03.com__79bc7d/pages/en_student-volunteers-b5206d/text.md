Student volunteer signup
If you are under 18
The campaign may request parent or guardian consent before certain volunteer activities.
Minor volunteers should not canvass door-to-door alone and may be asked to participate with a parent, guardian, or approved adult volunteer.
- Confirm whether you are under 18
- Provide parent or guardian contact information when requested
- Follow the campaign’s minor-volunteer safety guidelines
Complete parent/guardian consent onlineDownload the paper consent form
The paper PDF is available for families who cannot or prefer not to complete an electronic consent form.
For campaign volunteers
Requesting a letter of recommendation
Volunteers who have made a meaningful contribution may request a recommendation for college, scholarships, internships, employment, or other opportunities.
Please provide your volunteer dates and duties, the purpose of the letter, the recipient or program, the deadline, and submission instructions.
After submitting the request form, send your résumé and any supporting materials to info@KellyForNJ03.com and note that you submitted a recommendation request.
A request does not guarantee a letter.
Please provide as much advance notice as possible so the campaign can determine whether an appropriate recommendation can be provided.
