Patrick’s Parade Irish Festival.
Ryan Michael Kelly at the Burlington County St.
Patrick’s Parade Irish Festival on September 19, 2026.
Ryan Michael Kelly spent part of the day volunteering in his personal capacity alongside other volunteers serving drinks at the Burlington County St.
Patrick’s Parade Irish Festival.
After his volunteer shift, Kelly walked around the festival, enjoyed the music and community activities, and gave away copies of his children’s book to anyone who wanted one.
He also spoke with people who chose to share concerns and questions about politics and issues affecting their families and communities.
The day was an opportunity to volunteer, meet neighbors, listen, and enjoy a community tradition with the people who made it possible.
Kelly volunteered in his personal capacity alongside other festival volunteers.
