26th Senate District Map
The 26th District includes parts of Algonquin, Barrington, Deer Park, Hoffman Estates, Kildeer, Lake in the Hills, Lake Zurich, Libertyville, Long Grove, Mundelein, North Barrington, Palatine, Port Barrington, Rolling Meadows, Schaumburg, South Barrington, Tower Lakes, Vernon Hills, Volo, and Wauconda.
