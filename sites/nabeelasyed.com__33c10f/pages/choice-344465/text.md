Choice
As a State Representative, Nabeela has been a steadfast champion for women’s healthcare and the fundamental right to choose.
She has consistently stood up to extremist efforts to roll back reproductive freedoms and is committed to keeping Illinois a national leader in protecting access to care.
Representative Syed has led the charge by:
- Fighting for legislation that ensures Illinois remains a safe haven for reproductive care in the Midwest and beyond
- Advocating for legal protections for healthcare providers and their out-of-state patients seeking care in Illinois
- Opposing extreme political attacks on reproductive healthcare and the right to choose
Legislative wins:
- Helped pass landmark legislation prohibiting discrimination based on reproductive health decisions
- Expanded the Illinois Human Rights Act to strengthen protections for women and families
- Co-sponsored legislation expanding protections for college students’ reproductive health and access to care
Nabeela will always stand up for the right to choose.
She’ll fight to keep personal medical decisions out of the hands of politicians and where they belong: between a woman and her doctor.
