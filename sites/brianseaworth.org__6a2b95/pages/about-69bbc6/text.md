Brian Seaworth…
…is one of two State Representatives for Pembroke, having served seven terms (2011-2012 and 2015-2026).
…is a member of Division III of the House Finance Committee.
The Finance Committee is responsible for introducing the biannual State of New Hampshire Budget plus any other appropriations of State Funds.
In Division III, we focus on the Department of Health and Human Services, which represents more than half of the State’s operating budget.
…sits on the House Committee on Labor, Industrial, and Rehabilitative Services.
…represents the House as a member of the Workers’ Compensation Advisory Council.
…is an alternate member of the Health and Human Services Oversight Committee.
…is the Chairman of Pembroke Planning Board.
…is a member of the Pembroke Budget Committee.
He has also served previously on the Capital Improvement Program Committee and on the Roads Committee.
…was previously the Vice Chairman of the House Committee on Labor, Industrial, and Rehabilitative Services for two terms.
He has also served two terms as Clerk of that committee.
…was Vice-Chairman and a charter Member of the Pembroke Republican Town Committee.
…has been consulting for the utility industry since 2000.
In 2004, he formed his own company, Seaworth Consulting, LLC specializing in the design and maintenance of remote control systems for electric distribution networks.
…is the proud father of Evelyn and Alex.
…married wife, Eve in 1999.
…has chickens and a dog.
…shoots Pistol League at Sunset Mountain Fish and Game in Canterbury.
…is a Range Safety Officer and sits on the Board of Directors at Sunset Mountain.
…is a member of Pro-Gun New Hampshire, and a lifetime NRA member.
…was born in Fort Campbell, Kentucky
…graduated from High School in Central Pennsylvania, and went on to college at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute in Troy, New York.
He graduated with a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering.
…designed rockets and other spacecraft systems while working for McDonnell Douglas Space Systems Company.
…worked for Gensym Corporation, where he was a Software Development Manager.
