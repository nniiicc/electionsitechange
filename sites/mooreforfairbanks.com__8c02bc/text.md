Home CONTRIBUTE Contact Strong Leadership A Better Future For Alaska ABOUT AIMEE I am a born-and-raised Alaskan, and my commitment to this community runs deep— in fact, I still live in the very same downtown Fairbanks house where I grew up.
This district isn't just a place on a map to me; it is my home, my history, and my future.
My career has been defined by hard work and public service.
I know what it takes to run a small business, and I understand the challenges local employers face. l've also dedicated years to supporting our schools, having worked for two school districts-one in California and right here at home in Fairbanks.
Today, I'm proud to serve our community every day as a school bus driver and a member of the Teamsters, building on a lifelong career where I have previously belonged to two other unions.
I've never shied away from hard work, and I believe Fairbanks deserves a representative who works just as hard as you do.
I am running for the State House to be a voice exclusively for us.
I won't be answerable to special interests or party hosses—my only boss will be the people of District.
