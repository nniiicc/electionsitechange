My entire life has revolved around faith, family, work and stewardship.
Raised by entrepreneurs, I was taught to trust God, work hard, commit to family, and seek purpose.
My grandfather started Fellowship Home on our family farm, a senior living community, as a ministry to serve the seniors of our community, and I have continued that tradition as a third generation owner and operator of Fellowship Senior Living with 6 communities and 300+ employees.
South Georgia is blessed with tremendous people and resources, but we also face challenges—from jobs and healthcare to attacks on our values.
Often times, we feel voiceless - like Atlanta always takes priority over us.
When I ran for office, I promised to give South Georgia a strong voice at the State Capitol.
And I believe I have delivered on that promise, helping bring new jobs and investments here.

I have fought the Radical Left as they try to impose their way of life on us and force their radical ideologies on our kids.
