I was raised in Eastern Oregon and attended Oregon State University.
I have had a diverse career, from working at Ford Motor Credit to various roles in the insurance industry.
I’ve also been a devoted member of my community, serving as a Development Officer for the Oregon FFA Foundation and a bus driver for the Oregon City School District.
My extensive involvement in community organizations, such as the St.
Patrick Knights of Columbus, Canby HS Boosters, Clackamas County Farm Bureau, and my current role on the Clackamas County Fair board, reflects my deep commitment to Oregon and Clackamas County.
My family is very important to me.
My wife and I have been married for 41 years and we have been blessed with three grown children and 5 grandchildren.
My commitment to my family and community demonstrates my dedication to continue to create a brighter future for all residents of House District 51.
