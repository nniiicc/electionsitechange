NEW AD President Trump: Brindisi “Total Disaster,””Lied”
Brindisi Betrayed Upstate New York, President says
NY22 – Claudia Tenney, Republican candidate for New York’s 22nd District, released a new television ad this morning featuring President Donald Trump in his own words.
The President called Brindisi a “total disaster” who is an “automatic puppet vote” for Nancy Pelosi– voting with her 90% of the time.
In remarks delivered on September 27th, President Trump said Brindisi “betrayed the people of Upstate New York by voting for the impeachment” and voted “for the Democrats’ anti-police bill, gut your police departments.”
The 30-second ad will run on television district-wide. [WATCH THE AD HERE]
The President’s remarks contrast with Anthony Brindisi’s false claims that he can work with President Trump, who made it clear he firmly supports Claudia Tenney for Congress in New York’s 22nd District.
Claudia Tenney said:
“It is a privilege to have President Trump’s support.
The President supports my campaign, not Anthony Brindisi because he knows Brindisi lied to the people of Upstate New York on impeachment, votes with Nancy Pelosi nearly 90% of time, and pushed to defund our local police.
Brindisi opposes President Trump at every turn.”
