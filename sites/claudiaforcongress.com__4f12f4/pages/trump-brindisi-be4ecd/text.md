President Trump Says Brindisi “Ineffective,””Not For You”
“No one listens to”“total disaster” Brindisi, President says at Tenney Rally
NY22 – President Donald Trump denounced Anthony Brindisi as “ineffective” who “no one listens to” on a Sunday tele-rally for Claudia Tenney.
The President called Brindisi a “total disaster”and “automatic puppet vote” for Nancy Pelosi who is“not for you, New York.”
President Trump said Brindisi “betrayed the people of upstate New York by voting for the impeachment” and votes in lockstep with Nancy Pelosi and Chuck Schumer.
The President’s remarks contrast with Anthony Brindisi’s false claims that he can work with President Trump, who made it clear he firmly supports Claudia Tenney for Congress in New York’s 22nd District.
According to the New York Times, Brindisi votes against President Trump nearly 90% of the time.
President Trump says Brindisi opposes the President “at every single turn.”
