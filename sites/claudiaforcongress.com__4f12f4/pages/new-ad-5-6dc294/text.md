New Ad: Claudia to Fight For Middle Class, Trump Agenda
Tenney Outlines Priorities to Voters on Police, Spectrum
NY22—Today, Claudia Tenney, Republican candidate for Congress for the 22nd District, launched a new ad highlighting her support for middle class families, President Trump’s agenda, and how to fix Spectrum.
[Watch the Ad “First” HERE on Youtube]
In the ad,Claudia Tenney pledges to “rebuild our economy by supporting President Trump’s America First agenda… fight to keep our communities safe by supporting the police… solve the problem with Spectrum by stopping Como’s insider deal that rips us off.”
The 30-second ad, “First,” features Claudia Tenney outlining her priorities for New York’s 22nd District and her support for President Trump’s America First agenda and how to fix Spectrum.
The digital ad will play across platforms district-wide.
Tenney notes that her plan is to rebuild our economy with President Trump, not impeach him like Anthony Brindisi did.
Tenney will never vote to defund the police like Anthony Brindisi did.
And Claudia will tackle Spectrum by ending Cuomo’s crooked deal that rips off Upstate New York customers.
