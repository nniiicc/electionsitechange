MOMENTUM: Claudia Tenney Raises Nearly $1 Million in Q3
$55 average donation from over 17,000 donations & across NY-22
Fueled by overwhelming grassroots support, Tenney received an average donation of $55.
Supported by over 10,000 first-time donors, the campaign received over 17,000 donations.
Donations poured in from every county in the 22nd Congressional District showing that Tenney’s grassroots supporters and team are the best in the state.
Claudia Tenney said: “The generous support of the voters across Central New York and the Southern Tier is humbling.
New York’s 22nd District deserves a representative who will fight for them and stand with President Trump – not betray them by impeaching the President, defunding our local police, and voting with Nancy Pelosi and the radical left like Anthony does.
We promise to use these funds to effectively and efficiently take that message to the voters and win November.”
