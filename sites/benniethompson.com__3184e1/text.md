Roots Hometown Leader Congress Homeland Security Defending Democracy Still Fighting Chapter 1 Roots 1948 Born in Bolton, Mississippi Bennie Gordon Thompson is born in Bolton, the small Hinds County town he still calls home.
1948 1968 1968 Tougaloo College graduate Earns a B.A. in political science from Tougaloo College, at the heart of the civil rights movement.
Chapter 2 Hometown Leader 1969 Elected Bolton alderman Wins his first public office at 21 and starts a lifetime of service to his neighbors.
1969 1972 1972 Educator Earns an M.S. in educational administration from Jackson State University.
He also taught school in Madison, Mississippi.
1973 Mayor of Bolton Leads his hometown as mayor from 1973 to 1980.
1973 1975 1975 Civil Rights Plaintiff for equal education Becomes one of the original plaintiffs in the Ayers case, challenging discrimination in Mississippi's public universities.
1980 Hinds County Board of Supervisors Serves the people of Hinds County as supervisor for thirteen years, from 1980 to 1993.
1980 Chapter 3 Congress 1993 1993 Elected to Congress Wins the April 1993 special election runoff with 55% and begins representing Mississippi's 2nd District.
Chapter 4 Homeland Security 2005 Ranking Member, Homeland Security Becomes the top Democrat on the newly permanent House Committee on Homeland Security.
2005 2007 2007 First Chairman of Homeland Security The first Democrat and the first African American to chair the Committee on Homeland Security.
2007 Law Wrote the 9/11 Commission law Introduces H.R.
1 to put the 9/11 Commission's recommendations into action.
It becomes law in 2007.
2007 2019 2019 Chairman again Returns to the gavel and leads the Committee on Homeland Security through 2023.
2020 Permanent Chair, Democratic National Convention Chosen to serve as Permanent Chair of the 2020 Democratic National Convention.
2020 Chapter 5 Defending Democracy 2021 2021 Chairman, January 6th Committee Leads the House Select Committee investigating the attack on the U.S.
Capitol, from 2021 to 2023.
Chapter 6 Still Fighting 2023 Ranking Member, Homeland Security Holds the top Democratic seat on Homeland Security as Mississippi's only Democrat in Congress and dean of the delegation.
2023 2026 2026 Wins the Democratic nomination Wins the March 2026 primary for Mississippi's 2nd District.
