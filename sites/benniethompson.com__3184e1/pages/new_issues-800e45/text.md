Civil rights Justice for every citizen Congressman Thompson has diligently fought to reverse discrimination in the higher education system of Mississippi as an original plaintiff in the Ayers Desegregation Case.
He has historically used the court system as an avenue for change on the local, state, and federal levels.
His lawsuits include efforts to have duly elected officials assume office and state redistricting efforts, and he played an instrumental role in the Black farmers case, paving the way for more than 20,000 farmers to file under the consent decree.
Congressman Thompson believes that our courts should provide rulings of non-biased justice, that hiring and promotional practices should flow evenly across gender lines, that small business owners should have the opportunity to expand and grow, and that everyday citizens should be able to live without the threat of encroachment on their civil rights.
