All of the endorsed candidates share concern for the New Hampshire environment, support action on climate solutions that benefits all residents, and a vision to lower pollution thus protecting public health and wildlife.
The endorsed candidates agree that critical issues – like just and reasonable electric rates, clean air and safe drinking water – must be addressed with solutions by the next legislature and governor.
