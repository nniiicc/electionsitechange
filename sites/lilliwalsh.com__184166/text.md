I grew up in Hudson and spent more than 23 years in Nashua, building a career and a life centered around hard work, responsibility, and community.
While working in Procurement for Wang Laboratories and later Hewlett-Packard, I attended New Hampshire College evening classes, now Southern New Hampshire University.
I earned my Bachelor of Science in Business Administration (B.S.B.A.), with a minor in Economics , while continuing to work full-time.
Over the course of my procurement career, I negotiated multi-million-dollar contracts for goods and services—experience that taught me the importance of careful planning, strong negotiation, accountability, and making every dollar count.
I later joined my husband in his electronics sales representative business.
Working alongside him gave me a whole new perspective on business.
I developed my marketing skills and experienced firsthand the challenges, risks, and rewards of running a small business.
Those experiences gave me a deep appreciation for the people who work hard every day to build and sustain their businesses.
In 2010, my husband and I moved to Hampstead and made our home at the lake house he had purchased in 1979.
What began as a family connection to this beautiful community became our permanent home—and Hampstead quickly became where my heart is.
I have always believed that experience matters.
My years in business, procurement, negotiation, marketing, and small business have shaped the way I approach public service today: listen carefully, do your homework, spend taxpayer dollars responsibly, and never lose sight of the people you are there to represent.
Hampstead is home, and I am honored to serve the community I love.

I will continue to bring our community’s interests to the State House and stand up for the values and priorities that matter to the people who call Hampstead home.
Keeping taxes low is a priority.
Our property taxes are among the greatest financial pressures facing Hampstead families, with education costs representing a significant portion of the burden.
I will continue to advocate for responsible state spending, fiscal discipline, and policies that respect the hard-earned money of New Hampshire taxpayers.
Protecting local control is equally important.
Decisions about development, growth, and the character of our communities should, whenever possible, remain in the hands of the people who live there and the local officials they elect.
I will continue to oppose efforts that unnecessarily override municipal authority and will work to preserve Hampstead’s ability to make decisions that are right for our town.
Supporting parental choice in education is another priority.
Parents know their children best, and families should have meaningful choices when it comes to their children’s education.
I will continue to support policies that respect parents’ rights and provide families with options that best meet their children’s individual needs.
I will also continue to support small businesses, defend our Constitution and individual rights, and work to keep government accountable to the people it serves.
After two terms, I know that representing Hampstead is about more than a title.
It is a responsibility I take seriously.
If I am privileged to serve a third term, I will continue to put Hampstead first.

Living on the water has deepened my appreciation for the natural resources that make Hampstead such a special place.
I care deeply about protecting the health of our lakes, waterways, wildlife, and open spaces so they can be enjoyed by our families and preserved for generations to come.
The quality and quantity of our drinking water is especially important to me , and I believe protecting our water resources must remain a priority.
My love of animals goes back to childhood.
I have always been an advocate for animals—both the pets who share our homes and the wildlife that belongs in the natural world around us.
I believe being a good steward of our community also means caring for the animals and natural habitats that are part of it.
And then there are the things that simply make me happy!
Gardening and golf have always been favorite pastimes , although both took a bit of a back seat when I entered the world of politics.
I still enjoy getting out in the garden, spending time on the lake, appreciating the outdoors, and, whenever I can, getting out for a round of golf.
