Meet Olaleye. *
Olaleye Onikuyide is an attorney and community advocate running for the 19th District State House seat in Connecticut.
Raised in West Hartford, he has deep local roots and brings fresh energy to public service.
Learn more about Olaleye and how he’s ready to serve the Avon and West Hartford community.
