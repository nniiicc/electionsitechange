Affordability and a fair economy
A full-time job ought to be enough to build a stable life.
Cost of living+
A person working 40 hours a week should be able to afford housing, groceries, utilities, childcare, and healthcare.
The focus is on the costs families actually face every day.
Living wage+
Anyone working full time should earn enough for food, housing, healthcare, transportation, and a little breathing room.
Taxpayers should not subsidize major employers that pay poverty wages.
Housing affordability+
Teachers, nurses, first responders, young families, and retirees should be able to afford a home in their own communities.
The campaign supports more starter homes, assessment protections, and long-term homeownership.
Taxes+
Lower taxes for people in District 31 and oppose schemes that shift more of the burden onto homeowners and consumers while wealthy corporations receive exemptions.
Consumer protection+
Stand up for honest prices, fair contracts, safe products, and control over personal information — including protections against hidden fees, price manipulation, predatory lending, and data misuse.
People over profits+
Georgia’s economy should reward hard work, not only wealth and political connections.
Lower costs, better jobs, consumer protections, and accountable corporate citizenship belong together.
