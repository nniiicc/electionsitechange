List of Services Healthcare As the only healthcare provider currently serving in the Idaho Legislature, I bring a perspective to healthcare policy that is grounded in what happens beyond the walls of the Capitol.
I continue to care for patients, work with families, and see firsthand the challenges Idahoans face when trying to access affordable, timely healthcare.
That experience has shaped my work in the Legislature.
I have focused on increasing access to care, strengthening Idaho’s healthcare workforce, reducing unnecessary barriers for providers, and protecting healthcare services in rural and underserved communities.
I have worked on legislation addressing rural nursing shortages, expanding opportunities within the healthcare workforce, improving long-term care, and modernizing healthcare delivery so patients can receive more of the care they need closer to home.
I also understand that access means little if we do not have the healthcare professionals available to provide that care.
Idaho must continue finding responsible ways to recruit, train, and retain nurses, physicians, advanced practice providers, emergency medical professionals, and other essential members of our healthcare workforce.
Healthcare policy should ultimately be about the patient—not politics.
I will continue bringing my experience from the exam room to the Statehouse and working to make sure Idaho families have access to high-quality, affordable healthcare close to home.
Healthcare Public Safety Keeping our neighborhoods safe is one of my highest priorities.
During my time in the Legislature, I have consistently supported our law enforcement officers, firefighters, EMS providers, and first responders, as well as the infrastructure and resources they need to serve our communities effectively.
Public safety is about more than responding to emergencies.
It means making sure our roads and infrastructure are safe, our emergency services can keep pace with growth, and the men and women answering the call have the tools, training, and support they need to do their jobs.
As District 15 and the greater Boise area continue to grow, I will continue working to ensure public safety remains a priority and that Idaho families can feel safe in their homes, neighborhoods, schools, and communities.
Public Safety Affordable Housing Housing affordability is one of the biggest challenges facing families in District 15 and across Idaho.
As our communities grow, we need to make sure working families, young professionals, seniors, and the next generation of Idahoans can still afford to live in the communities they call home.
In the Legislature, I have supported efforts to increase housing opportunities, reduce unnecessary barriers to development, and encourage the construction of more attainable housing.
Increasing supply is an important part of addressing affordability, but we also have to make sure growth is thoughtful and that roads, schools, utilities, and other infrastructure can keep pace.
I believe the state should focus on removing unnecessary obstacles and giving local communities the tools they need to respond to housing demand while still respecting local decision-making and protecting the character of our neighborhoods.
There is no single solution to Idaho’s housing challenges, but we can continue working to create more opportunities for homeownership, expand housing choices, and make it possible for Idaho families to build a future here.
Affordable Housing Responsible Growth, Infrastructure & Tax Relief Idaho’s growth brings tremendous opportunity, but it also requires thoughtful planning.
As District 15 and the greater Boise area continue to grow, we must make sure our roads, schools, infrastructure, emergency services, and public resources keep pace without losing the quality of life that makes our community such a great place to call home.
I have supported investments in the infrastructure our communities need while also keeping a close eye on the impact government spending has on Idaho families.
During each year I have served in the Legislature, I have supported lowering taxes and returning more money to Idaho taxpayers, because families should be able to keep more of what they earn.
Responsible growth means planning ahead, prioritizing essential services, eliminating unnecessary government burdens, and making sure taxpayer dollars are spent wisely.
We can prepare for Idaho’s future without abandoning the conservative fiscal principles that have helped keep our state strong.
My goal is simple: manage growth responsibly, invest in the infrastructure Idaho families depend on, and continue working to keep taxes low so Idaho remains an affordable place to live, work, raise a family, and retire.
