Brandyburg’s campaign focuses on the issues that truly matter, not just sound bites that divide.
These initiatives aim to create positive change and a new direction for the citizens of Texas.
As your Representative for Texas House District 85, I pledge to work diligently for all Texans, across every community and background, addressing the concerns that matter most to you: Public Education: Supporting quality education for every child, ensuring our schools have the resources they need to help students succeed.
Immigration: Addressing immigration concerns with practical, fair solutions that respect our laws and the dignity of all individuals.
High Cost of Living: Tackling the increasing expenses that impact Texas families, from groceries to housing to healthcare, so you can better provide for your loved ones.
Rising Energy Costs: Working to lower the cost of energy by supporting reliable, affordable resources and protecting consumers from unnecessary price hikes.
Education: Ensuring access to quality education and resources for all students.
