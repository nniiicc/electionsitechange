RED ROCK, AZ – Today, JoAnna Mendoza released the following statement after the case against Karla Toledo, a Tucson DACA recipient arrested by federal immigration officers, was dismissed.
“Yesterday’s dismissal confirms what Southern Arizona has said from the start: this never should have happened.
No one should be forced from their home, separated from their family, or denied due process without a lawful basis,” said Mendoza.
“As a Marine veteran, I believe in law and order — and that means the law must apply to everyone, including those in government.
We can enforce the law while protecting due process and treating every person with dignity.
Karla’s case must be a wake-up call.
I call on elected officials and candidates of every party to work together to uphold the rule of law and hold those responsible for failures accountable.”
