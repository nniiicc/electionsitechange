RED ROCK, AZ – Today, JoAnna Mendoza – retired Marine Corps Drill Instructor, working mom, and Democrat running for Congress in Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District – released the following statement in response to the State of the Union:
“Arizonans aren’t fooled by campaign slogans or buzz words.
We know Juan Ciscomani is failing us.
Costs are too high, our health care is on the chopping block, and thousands of good-paying jobs are at risk because of Ciscomani’s agenda.
“We need leaders who are willing to put people over party.
Time and again, Ciscomani caters to DC party bosses and rubberstamps reckless policies that hurt our communities.
Just this month, he voted to protect tariffs that cost families an extra $1,000 last year while devastating Arizona’s ranchers and farmers and hurting small businesses.
After warning about the ‘massive’ consequences of not extending the Affordable Care Act subsidies, Ciscomani voted against them.
And despite sounding the alarm about cuts to Medicaid, Ciscomani signed off on the largest cuts to AHCCCS in history.
“Now more than ever, we need tough, accountable leadership – not lip service.
Arizona must come first.”
