Right now, families are paying too much – for groceries, gas, healthcare, housing, and utilities.
Instead of getting ahead, families are falling further behind.
In Congress, JoAnna will work to lower costs by cracking down on price gouging costing families an extra $2,000 a year by strengthening consumer protections and stopping unfair price disparities in rural communities.
A nursing mom should be able to afford a can of formula.
She will work to modernize the Child Tax Credit to deliver relief to working families and oppose reckless tariffs that drive up costs.
JoAnna is committed to delivering real-practical solutions because our families deserve a stable healthy future.
Social Security is a lifeline for older Arizonans who have worked their entire lives, paid into the system, and deserve to retire with dignity.
And as someone with an aging parent, she knows that the social security benefit isn’t enough to cover basic necessities.
JoAnna will oppose any efforts to cut seniors’ hard-earned benefits.
She strongly supports strengthening Social Security and exploring ways to increase benefits, especially for seniors living on fixed incomes.
