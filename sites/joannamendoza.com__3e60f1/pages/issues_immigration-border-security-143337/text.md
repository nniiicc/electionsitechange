As a single mother, JoAnna worries about her child, and wants every child to grow up in a safe, crime-free community.
People who break the law must be held accountable, including and especially those that commit violent crimes or smuggle illicit drugs like fentanyl and dangerous weapons into our neighborhoods.
As a retired Marine, JoAnna understands the call to serve and has deep respect for men and women in law enforcement who do their difficult jobs with honor.
It’s why she supports funding for law enforcement and ensuring officers have the critical resources to keep every Arizonan safe, including training and body cameras.
JoAnna does not support slashing police budgets, and she also believes that public safety is stronger when we invest in hiring more crisis response professionals, improve transparency, and build trust between law enforcement and the communities they serve.
In Congress, JoAnna will work with both parties to strengthen border security at ports of entry, deporting violent criminals, holding cartels accountable, and ensuring that immigration enforcement is professional, properly trained, and consistent with the law.
JoAnna believes in a fair immigration system that reflects our values as Americans.
She supports creating legal pathways to citizenship for people who work hard, follow the law, and contribute to their communities.
She supports legislation to strengthen oversight of detention facilities and restore independent audits.
JoAnna also supports Senator Kelly and Senator Gallego’s bicameral Stop Excessive Force in Immigration Act.
