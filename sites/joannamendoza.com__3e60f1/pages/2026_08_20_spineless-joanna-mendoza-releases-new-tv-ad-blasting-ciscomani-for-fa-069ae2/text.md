Six figure ad buy is running on broadcast, cable, streaming, and digital platforms
RED ROCK, AZ – This week, the JoAnna Mendoza for Congress campaign released a new TV ad slamming Juan Ciscomani’s spineless votes that are devastating Southern Arizona.
“Spine” will air across broadcast, cable, and streaming in Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District as part of a six figure ad buy.
The ad highlights Ciscomani’s cowardly votes to approve tariffs that drive up the cost of living, gut our veterans’ health care, and rips health care away from thousands of Arizona families.
“Every time people go to the grocery store or the gas pump, or pay their electric or utility bill, they are reminded of how Ciscomani has been absolutely spineless in standing up to Trump,” Mendoza recently told POLITICO.
The latest ad comes as momentum is on Mendoza’s side, as she continuously outraises Ciscomani every quarter this year, and as four polls show her ahead of Ciscomani in a head-to-head matchup.
Mendoza will continue to run laps around Ciscomani until Southern Arizonans fire him once and for all in November.
