Jo's Positions
JoAnna knows firsthand how critical access to healthcare is.
Growing up, her family relied on Medicaid, and that access allowed her to grow up healthy and strong enough to go on to serve her country in military uniform for 20 years.
That experience shaped her belief that every child and family deserves that same opportunity to live a healthy life.
Healthcare today costs too much.
Everyone, regardless of their insurance coverage, feels the pinch when millions of people lose access to healthcare.
Rural healthcare facilities across Southern Arizona are especially at risk because of the cuts in the One Big Beautiful Bill.
That’s why JoAnna will work to reverse disastrous cuts to healthcare, including to AHCCCS and enhanced Affordable Care Act (ACA) subsidies.
In Congress, JoAnna will fight to protect healthcare for people with pre-existing conditions, expand the number of prescription drug prices Medicare can negotiate, and invest in training more doctors and nurses.
She opposes privatizing Medicare, which would lead to higher prices and worse care for patients, and supports expanding coverage to include vision, hearing, and dental.
