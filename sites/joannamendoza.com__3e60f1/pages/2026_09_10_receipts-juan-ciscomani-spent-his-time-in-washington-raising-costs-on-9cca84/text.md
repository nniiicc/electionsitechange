Meet Jo On the Issues Endorsements News Get Involved Donate Contact Store Contribute Meet Jo On the Issues Endorsements News Get Involved Donate Contact Store Contribute Press Releases RECEIPTS: Juan Ciscomani Spent His Time in Washington Raising Costs on Arizonans September 10, 2026 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE September 10, 2026 Contact : press @joannamendoza.com Arizonans are paying $3,600 more per year thanks to Juan Ciscomani’s votes in Congress RED ROCK, AZ – Ahead of tonight’s Clean Elections Debate, the JoAnna Mendoza campaign for Congress is highlighting Rep.
Juan Ciscomani’s record of making life more expensive for Arizonans.
In fact, a new report found that Arizona families had to spend an additional $3,600 on essential goods and services thanks to rising inflation, tariffs, the Iran War, and healthcare cuts – all things Rep.
Ciscomani voted for.
“Juan Ciscomani will attempt to lie and distract voters in tonight’s debate because he knows his record in Washington is indefensible.
Gas, groceries, healthcare, and energy are more expensive because of Ciscomani, plain and simple.
Tonight, Arizona voters will see a clear contrast between Juan Ciscomani who has been spineless in standing up to Donald Trump and JoAnna Mendoza who will bring commonsense solutions to lower costs for all Arizonans,” said James Gravitt, Campaign Manager.
Here’s the receipts: HEALTHCARE After promising that he “ cannot and will not ” cut Medicaid, Ciscomani voted for the largest ever cuts to Medicaid history, which is projected to cost Southern Arizona hospitals $110 million every single year and kick more than 200,000 Arizonans off their health care .
After warning of “ massive ” consequences and “ real harm ” for Arizonans if the Affordable Care Act (ACA) tax credits expired, Ciscomani refused to join bipartisan efforts to protect healthcare and force the House to vote on the bill, leading to more than 107,000 Arizonans losing their ACA insurance coverage this year .
FOOD ASSISTANCE: Ciscomani voted for the One Big Beautiful Bill that led 500,000 Arizonans – half of all SNAP recipients in the state – to lose critical nutrition assistance.
This includes nearly 200,000 children and 7% of all Pima County residents .
TARIFFS: Ciscomani voted nine times to protect these disastrous tariffs that have raised costs on Arizonans – leading to families to pay more than $2,500 in tariff costs , devastated Arizona’s agriculture industry, and hurt small businesses .
UTILITY BILLS: Ciscomani voted to roll back energy production in Arizona, which according to Ciscomani created “ life changing jobs ” in the district – a decision that’s already led to over 1,000 layoffs in Casa Grande and raised energy prices for all Arizonans.
IRAN WAR: Rep.
Ciscomani has long defended and voted five times for Trump’s war in Iran that has raised gas prices to over $4.50 a gallon in Arizona and cost the average American more than $1,200 since the war began .
While Juan Ciscomani has spent his time in Washington slashing healthcare and raising costs for constituents, JoAnna Mendoza will go to Congress with commonsense solutions to make life better for all Arizonans.
In Congress , she will fight to: Reverse the Trump tariffs driving up our grocery bills.
End the Iran war driving up our gas costs.
Expand healthcare access for all Americans.
Crack down on corporations that overcharge us and engage in price-fixing.
Lower prescription drug prices by expanding the number of drugs Medicare can negotiate the prices of.
Ban members of Congress from trading stocks and getting rich on the job.
Expand access to job training and apprenticeships in Southern Arizona.
Invest in infrastructure to support the jobs of the future, such as energy efficiency and sustainable manufacturing.
