“Daughter” tells Mendoza’s story: she grew up working alongside her parents in the cotton fields of Eloy, then went on to join the military and serve her country for over two decades.
Now, she’s running for Congress to continue her oath of service and deliver a better future for Arizonans.
Today’s ad unveiling comes as Mendoza doubled Ciscomani’s fundraising haul last quarter, $2.4 million to $1.1 million – a reflection of her campaign’s growing momentum and an impressive feat for a first-time congressional candidate.
“Daughter” joins several other recently released ads, including “ Heat ,” “ Never Expires ,” and “ What Matters Most ”.
