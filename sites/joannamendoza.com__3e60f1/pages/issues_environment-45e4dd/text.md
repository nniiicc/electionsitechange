Jo's Positions
Having grown up in rural Arizona, JoAnna knows that rising energy costs and water shortages put real pressure on families, farmers, and rural communities.
She believes Arizona can lead the way in building a more affordable, reliable, and self-sufficient future.
In Congress, JoAnna will support investments in American-made energy, including clean energy, which will save money for ratepayers.
While Ciscomani has sided with utility companies and voted to roll back clean energy investments, JoAnna will stand up and protect local solar projects that bring energy online faster, lower costs, and reduce harmful pollution in our air. .
As new industries like data centers expand, JoAnna will fight to make sure that these industries bring their own energy and don’t drive up costs for Arizonans.
Her priority will be to keep utility costs from rising, protect workers and consumers, and safeguard our limited water supply.
JoAnna understands that water security is critical to Arizona’s future.
She will fight to strengthen water infrastructure, secure Arizona’s fair share of Colorado River resources, protect groundwater, and clean up contaminants like PFAS to ensure safe drinking water.
As the daughter of farmworkers, JoAnna grew up working Southern Arizona’s cotton fields.
She has a deep respect for the state’s agricultural economy, which is essential to providing for and feeding the nation.
JoAnna will push for a five year Farm Bill that provides certainty to farmers and ranchers, invests in rural communities, and ensures a healthy food supply for our families.
Stability and consistency for farmers, also means stopping reckless tariffs that jack up prices on equipment and fertilizer and make it difficult for farmers and other small business owners to plan ahead.
JoAnna will also fight to restore funding for programs that help farmers, including enhanced ACA subsidies.
