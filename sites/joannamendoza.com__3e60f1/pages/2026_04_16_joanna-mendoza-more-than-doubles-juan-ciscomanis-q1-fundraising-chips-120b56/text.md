RED ROCK, AZ – Democrat JoAnna Mendoza, retired Marine Corps Drill Instructor and working mom challenging Juan Ciscomani in Arizona’s 6th Congressional District, today announced that her campaign raised more than double the amount of Ciscomani in the first quarter of 2026.
“I’m so humbled by the support our campaign has received as we move forward into the rest of 2026,” Mendoza said.
“Our grassroots movement is growing by the day, and as Juan Ciscomani continues to hide from his constituents and lie to our faces, communities across southern Arizona are noticing, and they’re fed-up.
Failure is not an option!”
The groundbreaking quarter comes as Mendoza is among the top-raising House candidates in the nation in the first quarter of 2026, with more than 80,000 individual supporters and an average contribution of $32since launching her campaign in February of last year.
Mendoza also recently earned her spot on the Democratic Congressional Campaign Committee’s (DCCC) Red to Blue Program, which recognizes top tier campaigns that can flip Republican held seats.
