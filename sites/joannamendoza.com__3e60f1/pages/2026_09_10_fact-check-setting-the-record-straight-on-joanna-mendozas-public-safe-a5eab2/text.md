THE FACTS:
Mendoza has repeatedly advocated for more police funding and more resources for law enforcement.
- In 2020, Mendoza stated, “I do not support defunding the police… I support ensuring they have the resources.”
- In an op-ed in the Arizona Daily Star, Mendoza stated, “The answer is not to defund law enforcement – it’s to fund law enforcement with intention.
That includes ICE.”
Mendoza has made public safety, including border security, a core part of her vision for the district, stating:
- “I want to ensure our communities are safe.
We need to make sure our border is secure, our police have the resources they need to serve our communities, and that we’re working together across all levels of government to give our families a fair shot.” (Motherly, May 2026)
- “One hundred percent, the border must be secure.
I served 20 years in the military, and national security is a top priority.
This is a border district.
We share 80 miles of border with Mexico.” (Copper Courier, April 2026)
