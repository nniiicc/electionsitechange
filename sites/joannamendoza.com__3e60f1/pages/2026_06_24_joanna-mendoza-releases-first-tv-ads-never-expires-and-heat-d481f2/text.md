“Never Expires” features eight military veterans, including Mendoza, reflecting on their oath of service to the United States.
The ad ends by thanking “all who swore the oath for 250 years,” referring to America’s 250th birthday this July 4th.
The spot echoes Mendoza’s campaign themes of patriotism, service, and love for country.
Mendoza’s ads come after a new survey this week shows Mendoza beating Juan Ciscomani in a head-to-head matchup, the latest in a string of polls showing Mendoza’s dominance in AZ-06.
Mendoza also more than doubled Ciscomani’s fundraising haul last quarter, $2.4 million to $1.1 million – a reflection of her campaign’s growing momentum.
