Meet Jo On the Issues Endorsements News Get Involved Donate Contact Store Contribute Meet Jo On the Issues Endorsements News Get Involved Donate Contact Store Contribute Jo's Positions Bring Good-Paying Jobs to Southern Arizona & Protecting Workers Growing up in rural poverty and the proud daughter of farmworkers, JoAnna saw how hard her parents worked and (still) struggled to make ends meet.
She believes working one job should allow you to provide for your family through a livable wage, benefits, and the ability to retire with dignity.
JoAnna also understands that a 4-year college degree isn’t the only path to success.
She supports workforce training, apprenticeships, and technical education that prepare Arizonans for good-paying jobs in the trades and growing industries.
In Congress, JoAnna will work to support small businesses, bring good-paying jobs back to our cities and towns, and ensure that companies who put roots down in Arizona hire and retain workers from our communities.
JoAnna also strongly supports the right to unionize and believes workers should be treated with dignity and have a fair shot at the American Dream.
