WATCH HERE Mendoza on why she’s running for Congress: “I’m from the district, born and raised.
I grew up in rural poverty.
My family used safety net programs… I want to make things better for folks in the district, and they’re suffering right now because of costs, unaffordable health care, and now what we’re seeing with the war in Iran .” Mendoza on public safety and border security: “As a Marine who did a couple of tours to Iraq and Afghanistan, national security and keeping our communities [safe] is the top priority… we really need to make sure that we have a secure border … ICE agents need to be held accountable for the things that they’re doing.
If they are deviating from the rule of law and the way that policy and procedure dictates that things should be done, so accountability, transparency, and of course, we need to make sure that they’re actually trained.” Mendoza on lowering costs for Southern Arizonans: “Once we take the House back, we need to give Americans immediate relief – putting money back in their pockets .
One way we can do that is actually with tariffs… These tariffs are killing us.
That cost is being imposed on consumers… We can also start with clawing back some of the tax cuts that are given to billionaires.” Mendoza on importance of bipartisanship: “I firmly believe that when government is not focused on the issues that are impacting people, this is what we get.
We get extreme left, extreme right, and the issues that people really care about don’t go addressed.
So for me, I believe that the work, the real work, is in the middle .
Having those tough discussions with people across the aisle, making compromises on how you can get things done.” Watch the full interview here.
