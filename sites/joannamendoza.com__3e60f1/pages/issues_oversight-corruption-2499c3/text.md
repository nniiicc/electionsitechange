Too many politicians in D.C. use their positions to benefit themselves instead of the people they serve.
JoAnna has zero tolerance for corruption.
JoAnna supports banning members of Congress, their families, and other top government officials from trading individual stocks while in office.
She will work to strengthen transparency laws, and ensure that the public can clearly see how decisions are made and how taxpayer dollars are spent.
Restoring trust in government means making Congress accessible to the people it serves.
In her first 100 days, JoAnna will bring Congress back to the community by reinstating town halls and launching “Congress in Your Community” events across AZ-06 – including rural and underserved areas – so that every Arizonan has a direct line to their representative.
JoAnna will also work to modernize how Congress connects with constituents by adopting a mobile-first constituent engagement platform to provide real-time updates on legislation, explain how decisions impact families, and give constituents a direct way to raise concerns, report issues, and share their experiences.
