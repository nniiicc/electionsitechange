Ciscomani previously admitted that his vote to gut energy tax credits “jeopardize[d]” Southern Arizona jobs; hundreds are now losing their jobs

“Juan Ciscomani betrayed Arizonans when he voted to gut our health care, jack up food costs, and jeopardize hundreds if not thousands of good-paying jobs,” said Mendoza.
“Ciscomani promised to stand up for us, but he consistently puts D.C. party bosses ahead of Southern Arizonans.
Our communities deserve a representative who fights for us, not for a political party or its leaders.”
