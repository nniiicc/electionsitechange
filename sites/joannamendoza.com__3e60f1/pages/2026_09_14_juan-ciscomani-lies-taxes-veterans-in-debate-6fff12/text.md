The verdict: True .” “In December 2024, Ciscomani said in a press release that, in light of the national debt surpassing $36 trillion, he was thrilled to join the DOGE Caucus to reduce waste and streamline government bureaucracy.
“When we talk about staff getting cut, no doctors were let go,” Ciscomani said.
“That was not the case.
That did not happen, OK?
That’s a fact.” Policy changes tied to DOGE, however, did lead to a net loss of physicians and medical staff, according to the VA.
The Center on Budget and Policy Priorities reports that the Trump Administration cut nearly 28,000 VA employees in 2025.
VA staffing cuts included 2,700 nurses, more than 1,000 medical officers, and more than 1,000 psychologists and social workers, as well as over 1,800 people tasked with evaluating veterans’ claims for benefits.
