“I grew up in a farmworker family in Pinal County, and I worked those fields.
I understand that water policy is not an abstract debate between lawyers in D.C.
The plan shows Arizona could bear the majority of the Lower Basin cuts, as much as 40 percent, in a state already facing drought.
Water cuts will determine whether farms survive, whether rural jobs remain, and whether food becomes more expensive.
And most importantly, it will determine whether our kids and future generations can raise a family here.” “Juan Ciscomani has rolled over as this administration froze the $4 billion Congress appropriated for Colorado River drought relief , and he voted for a bill that would have slashed every state’s clean and drinking water funding by more than 90 percent, ” Mendoza continued .
“Southern Arizona deserves a representative who will stand up to this administration.
I’m ready to work across party and state lines for a fair, Basin-wide agreement with measurable contributions from every state, so that a temporary emergency never becomes Arizona’s permanent water future.” ### Meet Jo On the Issues Endorsements News Get Involved Donate Contact Store Terms & Conditions Privacy Policy Mailing Address: Mendoza for Congress P.O.
