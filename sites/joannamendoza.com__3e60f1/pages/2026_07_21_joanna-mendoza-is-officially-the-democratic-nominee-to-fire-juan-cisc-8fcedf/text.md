Mendoza wins primary election, charges toward general with $3.1 million warchest
RED ROCK, AZ – Today, JoAnna Mendoza, retired Marine Corps Drill Instructor, single mom, and native Arizonan running to represent Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District, released the following statement after winning the 2026 primary election, officially locking in her place as the Democrat who is going to face off against Congressman Juan Ciscomani in November.
“I’m so grateful for Southern Arizona’s support for our campaign as we move onto the general election in November,” said Mendoza.
“This victory comes with the somber reality of our warriors overseas fighting a war that doesn’t have a clear military strategy and objective.
Spineless Members of Congress like Juan Ciscomani continue to vote to drive up the costs of groceries, energy, and gas, put healthcare out of reach, and make our communities less safe.
Now more than ever, our families deserve servant leadership in Congress, and I’m honored to be the nominee our district is counting on to address the issues that matter most.
We can, we must, and we WILL win.
Onward to victory!”
The primary victory comes as Mendoza again doubled Ciscomani’s fundraising in the second quarter of 2026, raising over $2 million compared to Ciscomani’s $1.1 million.
She is heading into the general election with a warchest of over $3.1 million and the support of over 100,000 individuals, a major feat for a first time congressional candidate.
The campaign also released its first television ads of the summer, “Heat” and “Never Expires”.
