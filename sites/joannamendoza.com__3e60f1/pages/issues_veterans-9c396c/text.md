As a U.S.
Navy and Marine Corps veteran, and later as a veteran caseworker for Congressman Tom O’Halleran, JoAnna spent countless hours serving our country.
She is committed to caring for our nation’s veterans and military families.
Like thousands of others in the district, JoAnna gets her healthcare from the Tucson VA Medical Center.
She will always fight to protect veterans’ healthcare and benefits, and will be a vocal opponent of cuts to the VA.
In Congress, JoAnna will fight to restore funding to the VA that were the result of the reckless DOGE cuts..
She will work to expand VA specialty care in rural communities, increase access through telehealth and mobile services, and fix transportation barriers by improving eligibility and reimbursement rates.
She will support legislation that strengthens mental health services, expands housing support, and invests in job training and transition programs.
JoAnna will also work to modernize VA electronic health records to improve coordination of care and strengthen protections against predatory “claim sharks” who exploit veterans seeking benefits.
Above all, she will fight to fully fund and protect the VA to ensure no veteran is left behind.
