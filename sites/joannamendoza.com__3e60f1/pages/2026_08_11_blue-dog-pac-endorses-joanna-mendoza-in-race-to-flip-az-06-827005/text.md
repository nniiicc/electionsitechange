RED ROCK, AZ – Today, the Blue Dog PAC announced they are endorsing JoAnna Mendoza, the retired Marine Corps Drill Instructor, single mom, and native Arizonan who is running to flip Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District.
The Blue Dog PAC works to support the House Blue Dogs, a coalition of House Democrats who stand for fiscal stability, national security, and embrace bipartisanship to find common sense solutions to the challenges facing our country.
“I’m answering the call to serve again because families across our district are struggling due to the rising cost of groceries, housing, gas, and health care, and the career politicians in Washington couldn’t care less,” Mendoza said.
“Southern Arizona has gone too long without a representative who won’t buckle under party pressure.
I’m proud to earn the Blue Dogs’ endorsement and committed to serving everyone in Arizona’s Sixth District, regardless of political ideology.”
Her campaign continues to build support from local, state, and national organizations and leaders, along with over 100,000 grassroots donors who believe she’s the challenger who will defeat Ciscomani in November.
