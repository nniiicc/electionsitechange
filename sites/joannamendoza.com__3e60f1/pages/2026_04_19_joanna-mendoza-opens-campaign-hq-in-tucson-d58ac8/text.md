TUCSON, AZ – Today, JoAnna Mendoza, retired Marine, working mom, and Democratic Candidate for Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District, joined supporters and members of the community to officially open her first campaign office in Pima County.
“If you all weren’t patriots, you would not be here with us today,” Mendoza said to the group of supporters.
“You are giving it everything.
People are showing up in any way they can.
This race is not about me, it’s about all of us.
We need everyone in this fight, and it’s going to take all of us.
We can, we must, and we will win in November.”
Mendoza’s campaign continues to build support from local, state, and national organizations as the challenger who will defeat Juan Ciscomani in November.
