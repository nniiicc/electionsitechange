TUCSON, AZ – In case you missed it, JoAnna Mendoza — retired Marine Corps Drill Instructor, working mom, and Democrat running for Congress in Arizona’s Sixth Congressional District, joined Governor Katie Hobbs to hear directly from local residents, healthcare workers, and families who are feeling the squeeze from rising costs.
“We’re hearing a lot about the rising cost of utilities, rent, groceries, and now gas with the war in Iran.
Here in Southern Arizona, people are still having to pay a lot, way more, for gas.” said JoAnna Mendoza.
“People are upset that Juan Ciscomani is unaccessible and unable to be held accountable for supporting the polices that are hurting us.
I have traveled all over the district, and we’re hearing these same themes over and over again.”
The roundtable was held as a new survey reveals that nearly 2 in 3 Arizona voters say gas prices will influence their vote on Election Day.
And as grocery and healthcare premiums soar, Ciscomani continues to cave to his DC bosses and not address the concerns Southern Arizonans sent him to Congress to fix.
