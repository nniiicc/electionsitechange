NOV 2025 SENATOR NELSON’S NEWSLETTER Property Tax Update It’s on Everyone’s Mind Property taxes are an ongoing topic of discussion.
Most say they are too high and unpredictable, and I agree!
The Comprehensive Property Tax Taskforce, convened by the legislature after the 2025 session, continues to meet, and many ideas are being discussed.
There is still much to develop and consider on this complex subject.
However, you can be certain of three things from me.
First, I will not support new taxes or the shifting of taxes.
Instead, Pierre needs to cut spending.
Second, Introducing a tax to “reduce” property taxes is not an option.
Over time, the tax reduction will rise again, along with the new tax, resulting in taxpayers paying double.
Third, I cannot support any ideas that give Pierre complete control over our counties and/or schools' funding.
Pierre is not doing an exemplary job with money now; how would they be any different with additional funds from local entities?
The task force's next and final meeting is on October 22, 2025, in Pierre.
My fellow District 18 legislator, Rep.
Julie Auch, serves on this committee.
Our state needs to shift from expensive spending to smart investing.
Our priority should be getting youth back on track early, before they end up in adult prisons, which cost YOU about $35,000 a year per inmate.
Two areas I’m particularly interested in are diversion and mentoring programs.
Both show tremendous success.
I’m working towards a collaborative effort in Pierre this winter to pass legislation that will help reduce pressure on our schools and provide meaningful support for struggling youth.
Interim Committee Success The Community Support Providers (CSPs) Interim Committee held its final meeting.

Locally, we have two CSPs: Ability Building Services (ABS) in Yankton and Southeast South Dakota Activity Center (Sesdac) in Vermillion.
