Congress last week voted to restore Florida’s permitting authority over wetlands.
But it’s unclear whether the process can be jump-started anytime soon.
U.S.
Rep.
Aaron Bean introduced an amendment last week on a larger bill, the Creating Confidence in Clean Water Permitting Act (HR 7023), that would codify Florida’s authority to issue permits.
Bean, a Fernandina Republican, said it’s critical to restore Florida’s 404 Program, which transferred permitting authority from the U.S.
Army Corps of Engineers to the Department of Environmental Protection (DEP).
