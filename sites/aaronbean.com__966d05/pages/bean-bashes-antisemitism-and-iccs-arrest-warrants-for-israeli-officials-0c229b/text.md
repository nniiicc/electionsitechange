Many House Republicans have stood firm on the side of Israel since the attacks of October 7th where 1,200 innocent civilians were murdered, bashing the Biden Administration’s actions hampering the Jewish State’s ability to destroy Hamas.
Representative Aaron Bean (R-FL) sat down with The Floridian to discuss the displays of antisemitism on college campuses in recent weeks and bashed the calls from the International Criminal Court (ICC) for the arrests of Israeli officials, including Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.
While Rep.
Bean was not familiar with the Doral ceasefire resolution controversy, he did respond to the intended message of such pro-Hamas sentiment being expressed in America by students and agitators by describing his recent visit to Israel and the sites of violence on October 7th.
“I went to Israel to see and study the history of how we got here and to see the ground zero where Hamas was pure evil.
Attacked, raped, brutally, savagely- you do not hear the word ‘savage,’ but they were savages in their brutality against civilization,” said Rep.
Bean, further describing how he met with the families of victims and hostages and learned of the horrors they experienced.
