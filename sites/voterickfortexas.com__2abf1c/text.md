top of page RICK MARTINEZ FOR TEXAS HOUSE • DISTRICT 125 DONATE About Our Priorities Endorsements Community Impact Get Involved Contact YOUR VOICE FOR TEXAS Candidate for the Texas House of Representatives District 125 RICARDO "RICK" MARTINEZ DONATE About Rick Martinez Born and raised on a United States Army military base in Germany, Rick Martinez learned early the values of discipline, service, and love for his country.
With deep Texas roots passed down from his father, Rick’s life journey has always led him back to the Lone Star State—whether during his high school years in Corpus Christi or later as a young man attending college near his sister in Lubbock.
For more than 10 years now, Rick has made San Antonio his home.
Bringing with him a strong entrepreneurial spirit, he established and expanded his company while building a life in the city he deeply loves.
Rick is an active community leader and the founder of his own charity.
He is also involved with several local organizations, including the Loving Heart Foundation, the San Antonio Food Bank, and The Ferrari Kid Foundation.
Whether he’s feeding the homeless or supporting homeless veterans, Rick is constantly seeking new ways to serve and uplift those around him.
His dedication to making a difference is the driving force behind his decision to run for Texas House Representative, District 125.
Rick wants to help not only at the local level, but across the entire state of Texas.
Rick has demonstrated commitment and leadership through both his successful business ventures and the strong relationships he has built with community members, leaders, and representatives.
He is ready to fight for every individual—regardless of status, background, or affiliation.
His mission is to restore safety, strengthen prosperity, and stand tall for all citizens of the great state of Texas.
Let Rick Martinez be your voice for Texas.
