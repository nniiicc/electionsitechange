Learn More $3M for Statewide Funding for United Food Banks following SNAP Cuts The SNAP cuts from H.R.1 (the 2025 Federal Budget) have been devastating to Arizona.
Over 400,000 Arizonans have lost access to life-saving food assistance since July 2025, including nearly 200,000 children.
United Food Bank partners with over 125 local food pantries and serves families all across Arizona.
They have provided a lifeline for families struggling to eat.
This year, Rep.
Austin helped negotiate $3M in key state funding for United Food Bank, to ensure that all Arizonans have access to the food assistance they need.
Learn More Major housing reforms to build more multi-family homes Housing prices have skyrocketed, and Rep.
Austin believes that the solution is to increase the supply of available housing, particularly affordable housing.
In 2024, Representative Lorena Austin sponsored two bills that have already had a positive impact on housing costs by allowing the construction of accessory dwelling units (casitas) and requiring that middle housing be integrated in residential areas near downtown cores.
Learn More Relocation funding for senior mobile home evictions Over the last year, many Mesa residents of mobile homes have been forced to relocate due to land sales and other factors.
Particularly for older residents with limited options and fixed incomes, these relocations can be prohibitively costly, leaving residents with few if any options for housing.
Rep.
Austin was proud to advocate for increases to state relocation assistance payments and expansion of the geographic area (statewide) that applicants may move to.
