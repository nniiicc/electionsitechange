ABOUT TAYLOR About Taylor Taylor Rehmet is a U.S.
Air Force veteran, aircraft mechanic, union president, working-class Texan, and the State Senator for District 9.
Born and raised in Garland, Taylor grew up in a working-class family.
His dad was an aircraft mechanic, and his mom worked in a local salon.
He learned the value of hard work early, working as a plumber’s assistant and on a ranch before joining the Air Force.
After his service, Taylor returned home and put those skills to work as a machinist building F-35s.
Taylor’s commitment to fairness and dignity on the job led him to union organizing.
His coworkers elected him president of his local chapter of the International Association of Machinists and Aerospace Workers, and he later became president of the union’s Texas State Council.
He has spent his career bringing people together to fight for better pay, safer workplaces, and a fair deal for working families.
In January, Taylor won a historic special election because voters were ready for a leader focused on lowering costs for the middle class, not favors for special interests or divisive culture wars.
He got to work immediately, holding the district’s first town hall in years, closing over 1,000 constituent cases in his first 100 days, and talking with hundreds of community members across Senate District 9.
Now, Taylor is working across the aisle to lower property taxes and everyday costs, make insurance more affordable, protect families from rising utility bills, repeal the private-school voucher scam, and fully fund public schools.
He isn’t a career politician.
He is a working-class leader who listens, shows up, and delivers real results.

CHECK YOUR REGISTRATION STATUS MAKE YOUR PLAN TO VOTE Lowering Costs for Working Texans Texans are working harder than ever, but it’s getting harder to get ahead.
Taylor will fight to lower everyday costs by cracking down on corporate price gouging, cutting taxes for working families instead of billionaires, and making smart investments in public schools, healthcare, and infrastructure so families aren’t stuck paying more out of pocket.
That means preventing utility companies from making ratepayers foot the bill for political spending and lobbying, exempting grocery stores from inventory taxes so those costs aren’t passed on at the checkout line, and regulating “dynamic pricing” practices that can raise prices in real time when demand increases.
Taylor believes Texas needs to focus on practical solutions that put money back in people’s pockets.
If you work full-time, you should be able to afford your home, your groceries, and your future, and he’ll focus on solutions that put money back in people’s pockets, not politics that drive costs up.
Lowering Property Taxes Property taxes, rising home prices, and rapidly increasing insurance costs are crushing working families while billion-dollar corporations get carve-outs and breaks.
Taylor will fight for a fairer tax system that shifts the burden off homeowners and renters and makes sure multi-billion-dollar corporations pay their fair share.
Taylor supports capping appraisal spikes, expanding tax exemptions for veterans and seniors, holding insurance companies accountable for excessive rate increases, and increasing the state’s share of public school funding so local districts don’t have to rely as heavily on property taxes to keep classrooms running.
Securing the Border Taylor believes in securing our border, enforcing our immigration laws, and keeping our communities safe, while treating people with dignity and respect and protecting the natural beauty of Big Bend National and State Parks.
Our federal, state, and local law enforcement agencies should have the resources, training, and support they need to hold cartels, human traffickers, and dangerous criminals accountable to the fullest extent of the law.
Ensuring the public safety of Senate District 9 is a top priority, and as your Senator, Taylor will continue working across the aisle to protect our way of life in Senate District 9 and across Texas.
Protecting Our Public Schools Taylor is a proud graduate of public schools and believes every student deserves the opportunity to build a future, whether that means college, the military, or a skilled trade.
He’ll fight to reinvest in public education, expand high school vocational programs, support our educators, and rebuild the pipeline between schools and good-paying jobs.
He supports fully funding public schools and repealing the expensive private school voucher scams that divert your tax dollars to private schools who do not have the same accountability, standards or requirements as public schools.
Taylor believes the state must pay its fair share of public school funding so local homeowners aren’t forced to shoulder the burden through higher property taxes.
He’ll also fight to protect local control of our schools and push back against state takeovers that strip communities of their ability to make decisions for themselves and their neighbors.
Making Healthcare More Affordable Texans are expected to see a 35% jump in their health insurance premiums this year alone.
No Texan should have to choose between their health and paying their bills because a powerful insurance company, hospital system, pharmaceutical company, or other corporation is putting profits ahead of patients.
Taylor will fight to curb insurance premium hikes, lower prescription drug prices, and rein in exorbitant hospital costs that raise costs across the board.
Responsibly Managing Data Center Growth The rapid growth of AI data centers in Texas needs closer oversight.
These projects can bring investment and opportunity, but they’re also putting pressure on our energy grid, water supply, and local infrastructure.
Taylor believes Texas needs common-sense rules to ensure data centers are built responsibly, with meaningful input from the communities they impact, and without shifting the costs onto everyday Texans.
Taylor supports a moratorium on new AI data centers until the Legislature establishes clear, responsible guidelines for their growth.
He’ll fight to protect local control, so communities have the final say over whether these facilities are built in their backyards, while making sure Texas can continue to welcome innovation.
Supporting Texas Veterans Taylor is a veteran who knows firsthand what it means to serve and what it feels like to be forgotten.
He’s seen far too many veterans come home and feel frustrated trying to navigate the systems to get the support they’ve earned.
That’s not how we should treat the men and women who sacrificed for our country.
Taylor will fight to fully fund mental health care, job training, housing, and support services for veterans and their families.
That means helping unhoused veterans get back on their feet, creating real pathways to good jobs and public service, and making sure every veteran is treated with the dignity and respect they’ve earned.
He also supports legislation to provide employers with display posters containing information on how veterans can access their benefits and grants, and expanding veteran home loan programs to better serve those who have served us.
Protecting Workers and Creating Good Jobs As a union president and machinist, Taylor believes the backbone of Texas isn’t the boardroom, it’s the workshop floor.
He knows the best way to raise wages, protect jobs, and ensure dignity on the job is through strong unions and worker power.
Taylor will lead efforts to strengthen workers’ rights, expand collective bargaining, and bring good-paying, union jobs back to Texas communities.
He supports reclassifying gig workers to guarantee benefits and will fight for apprenticeship pipelines and workforce training opportunities that create quality career paths for the next generation.
Making Housing More Affordable Taylor believes the people who live and work in our communities should be able to afford to call them home.
Too many working families are being priced out while corporate landlords and investors buy up homes and drive up costs.
Taylor will fight to make housing more affordable, strengthen protections for renters, and invest in public and nonprofit housing that working families can actually afford.
He believes Texans deserve a fair shot at owning a home, renters deserve to be treated with dignity, and our neighborhoods should be places to build a life, not just another way for billionaires to make a profit.
Improving Transportation for Texans Taylor believes Texans should be able to get to work, school, and home without spending hours in traffic or breaking the bank to get there.
As Tarrant County and North Texas continue to grow, our transportation system needs to keep pace with options that are affordable, reliable, accessible, and built to last.
Taylor supports expanding and modernizing public transit, including TEXRail, improving high-traffic corridors like 121, 820, and 183, and fixing Texas’ broken toll road pricing model so families aren't paying outrageous fees just to get around their own communities.
Strong infrastructure is the foundation of a strong economy, and Taylor will fight for the investments our district needs to keep people moving and our communities and economy thriving.
Keeping Texans Safe and Supporting Law Enforcement Every Texan deserves to feel safe at home and in their community.
Taylor believes public safety starts with supporting law enforcement with the staffing, training, and resources they need to do their jobs.
He’ll work to crack down on violent crime, human trafficking, organized crime, and the criminals bringing fentanyl into our communities.
He also supports stronger mental health and addiction treatment, resources for survivors, and programs that help prevent crime before it happens and treat addiction like the disease it is.
Taylor knows we can support law enforcement, hold criminals accountable, and maintain high professional standards at the same time.
Public safety shouldn’t be partisan, it should be about what works to keep our families and neighborhoods safe.
