FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Monday, July 13, 2026 Contact: press@jeffwadlin.com, 479.370.5710 BENTONVILLE, AR - Jeff Wad lin, the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Senate in Arkansas, today released a new introductory campaign video presenting his background, political philosophy, and plan to challenge the two-party establishment in November.
The two-minute video opens with the message: “This race is not left versus right.
It’s Arkansas versus D.C.” Wadlin argues that the Democratic and Republican parties have become more interested in partisan conflict and culture wars than in addressing the economic pressures facing Arkansas families.
“Life has become unaffordable, the American Dream is getting harder to reach, and too many Arkansans feel that Washington has stopped fighting for their families and their paychecks,” Wadlin said.
“The two parties will spend this campaign telling voters whom to fear and whom to vote against.
I’m running to give Arkansans someone and something to vote for.” In the video, Wadlin describes himself as a pragmatic Libertarian committed to personal freedom, personal responsibility, limited government, peaceful foreign policy, fiscal discipline, and genuinely competitive free markets.
Wadlin also discusses his career in large companies, his experience starting four businesses, and his decision to make Arkansas his home.
“I chose to live in Arkansas, for my career and to raise my family,” Wadlin says in the video.
“Arkansas has been good to me, and now it is time to return the favor.” Wadlin said his path to victory depends on building a broader coalition than either major party can assemble alone.
“We need independents, Libertarians, disengaged voters, pragmatic Democrats, and conservatives who no longer feel represented by Washington,” Wadlin said.
“This campaign is not about moving Arkansas from one political tribe to another.
It is about putting Arkansas ahead of both.
The video is available to view here.
As the campaign progresses toward the general election on November 3, Wadlin is visiting every county in the state to engage voters across Arkansas.
He invites everyone who is tired of the status quo to join this movement focused on principled leadership and the preservation of the American Dream.
For more information regarding Jeff’s platform or to find out more about the campaign, please visit the official campaign website at jeffwadlin.com.
### About Jeff Wadlin Jeff Wadlin is the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Senate in Arkansas.
Based in Bentonville, Wadlin has spent his career in business, working inside large organizations and building ventures of his own.
That experience shaped his belief that America works best when people who work hard can afford a stable life, start businesses, raise families, and move up without being crushed by high costs, red tape, political dysfunction, or a government that forgets who it serves.
His campaign is focused on lower costs, higher wages, personal liberty, fiscal responsibility, and restoring the American promise that hard work should still lead to a better life.
