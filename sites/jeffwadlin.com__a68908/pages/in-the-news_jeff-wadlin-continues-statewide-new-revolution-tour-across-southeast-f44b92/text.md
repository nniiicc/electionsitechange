ARKANSAS - Libertarian U.S.
Senate candidate Jeff Wadlin is continuing his ambitious "New Revolution Tour," a statewide campaign effort to visit all 75 Arkansas counties and 185 towns with populations over 1,000.
Over the past three weeks, Wadlin traveled across northern, western, and central Arkansas, engaging directly with voters at local parks, county courthouses, festivals, and community gatherings.
Tour Recap: Weeks 1-3 Highlights
- Week 1 (Aug 1-9): Kickoff in Pulaski and Franklin Counties, featured stops at the National Biker Roundup in Little Rock, local town squares in North Arkansas (Mountain Home, Harrison, Jasper, Salem), and regional events including the Tontitown Grape Festival and Gravette Days.
- Week 2 (Aug 11-16): Focused on Washington, Benton, Crawford, Scott, Montgomery, Garland, Hot Spring, Saline, Perry, and Yell Counties with community park meet-and-greets and local county courthouse stops in Van Buren, Hot Springs, and Malvern.
- Week 3 (Aug 18-22): Stops included Recreation Center in Bentonville, Basin Spring Park in Eureka Springs, River Valley City Elders in Fort Smith, and courthouse lawn stops across Johnson, Pope, Logan, Polk, Sevier, Little River, Miller, and Hempstead Counties.

About Jeff Wadlin
Jeff Wadlin is the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Senate in Arkansas.
Based in Bentonville, Wadlin has spent his career in business, working inside large organizations and building ventures of his own.
That experience shaped his belief that America works best when people who work hard can afford a stable life, start businesses, raise families, and move up without being crushed by high costs, red tape, political dysfunction, or a government that forgets who it serves.
His campaign is focused on lower costs, higher wages, personal liberty, fiscal responsibility, and restoring the American promise that hard work should still lead to a better life.
