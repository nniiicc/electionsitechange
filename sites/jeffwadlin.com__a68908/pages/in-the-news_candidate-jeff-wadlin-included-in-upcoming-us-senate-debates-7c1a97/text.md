SENATE DEBATES Aug 20 Written By Annette Magnus-Marquart Wadlin’s Campaign for the U.S.
Senate Continues to Gain Momentum FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE: Thursday, August 20, 2026 Contact: press@jeffwadlin.com, 479.370.5710 ARKANSAS - Jeff Wadlin, the Libertarian candidate for the U.S.
Senate in Arkansas, has officially been invited to participate in two major U.S.
Senate debates alongside the other candidates in the race.
Wadlin has confirmed his participation in the Arkansas TV statewide debate series, which takes place at their studio on the University of Central Arkansas campus in Conway during the week of October 12 - 16.
He will also participate in the second televised debate hosted by ASU-TV in Jonesboro on October 22, 2026.
Wadlin’s inclusion in both debates highlights the mounting momentum his campaign has built across the state and underscores that his platform is resonating with voters across the state.
"Being included in these debates shows that Arkansans are hungry for real representation and a candidate who listens to their concerns," said Jeff Wadlin.
"Our campaign has built tremendous momentum, and our inclusion on the debate stage proves that this movement is being taken seriously.
I look forward to sharing our vision directly with voters and standing alongside the other candidates to debate the vital issues facing our state and our county." The inclusion of Wadlin in these high-profile debates reflects a growing recognition of the campaign’s viability, strong grassroots support, and expanding voter base across Arkansas.
To view the campaign's statewide tour schedule, platform details, or learn more about the campaign, visit jeffwadlin.com .
### About Jeff Wadlin Jeff Wadlin is the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Senate in Arkansas.
Based in Bentonville, Wadlin has spent his career in business, working inside large organizations and building ventures of his own.
That experience shaped his belief that America works best when people who work hard can afford a stable life, start businesses, raise families, and move up without being crushed by high costs, red tape, political dysfunction, or a government that forgets who it serves.
His campaign is focused on lower costs, higher wages, personal liberty, fiscal responsibility, and restoring the American promise that hard work should still lead to a better life.
