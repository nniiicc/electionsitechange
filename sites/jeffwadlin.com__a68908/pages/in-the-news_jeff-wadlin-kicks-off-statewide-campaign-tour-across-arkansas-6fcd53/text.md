Senate in Arkansas, today officially kicked off his statewide campaign tour.
Over the next few weeks, Wadlin will visit all 75 counties in Arkansas and every town with 1,000 or more residents.
He will present his background, political platform, and plan to challenge the two-party establishment this November.
Wadlin will host and attend events across the state to engage all voters.
The tour launches with a clear message: “This race is not left versus right.
It’s Arkansas versus D.C.” Wadlin argues that the Democratic and Republican parties have become more interested in partisan conflict and culture wars than in addressing the economic pressures facing Arkansas families.
“Life has become unaffordable, the American Dream is getting harder to reach, and too many Arkansans feel that Washington has stopped fighting for their families and their paychecks,” Wadlin said.
“The two parties will spend this campaign telling voters whom to fear and whom to vote against.
I’m hitting the road to give Arkansans someone and something to vote for.” Throughout the tour, Wadlin will engage voters directly to discuss his background as a pragmatic Libertarian committed to personal freedom, personal responsibility, limited government, peaceful foreign policy, fiscal discipline, and genuinely competitive free markets.
He will also share insights from his career in large companies, his experience starting four businesses, and his decision to make Arkansas his home.
“I chose to live in Arkansas, for my career and to raise my family,” Wadlin said.
“Arkansas has been good to me, and now it is time to return the favor.” Wadlin emphasized that winning in November requires building a broad coalition that spans beyond traditional party lines.
“We need independents, Libertarians, disengaged voters, pragmatic Democrats, and conservatives who no longer feel represented by Washington,” Wadlin said.
“This campaign is not about moving Arkansas from one political tribe to another.
It is about putting Arkansas ahead of both.” As the tour moves across the state toward the general election on November 3, Wadlin invites everyone tired of the status quo to attend a local tour stop and join the movement for principled leadership.
To view the tour schedule, platform details, or learn more about the campaign, visit jeffwadlin.com .
### About Jeff Wadlin Jeff Wadlin is the Libertarian candidate for U.S.
Senate in Arkansas.
Based in Bentonville, Wadlin has spent his career in business, working inside large organizations and building ventures of his own.
That experience shaped his belief that America works best when people who work hard can afford a stable life, start businesses, raise families, and move up without being crushed by high costs, red tape, political dysfunction, or a government that forgets who it serves.
His campaign is focused on lower costs, higher wages, personal liberty, fiscal responsibility, and restoring the American promise that hard work should still lead to a better life.
