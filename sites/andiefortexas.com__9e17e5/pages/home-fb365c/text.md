What I Stand For
- Pathways to Citizenship Millions of people are already living, working, raising families, and building lives in communities across the country.
So, what does a responsible and humane path forward actually look like?
- Transparency & Accountability in Grimes County A massive new development could reshape Grimes County for decades.
The question isn’t only what it could bring—it’s what residents deserve to know before those changes happen.
- Environmental Conservation Texas is growing, and the decisions we make now will shape what we leave behind.
Can we protect our land, water, and communities while continuing to create jobs and opportunity?
- Healthcare for All Too many working Texans earn too much to qualify for Medicaid but still cannot afford private insurance.
I support expanding Medicaid to improve access to care and strengthen the hospitals and providers our communities depend on.
- Strong Public Schools Texas has committed $1 billion to a new education savings account program.
But in communities where public schools are often the only practical option, where should our education dollars be going?
