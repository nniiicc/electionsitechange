The project has been presented as a major investment that could bring thousands of jobs to the area.
That kind of economic development deserves serious consideration, but so does what a project of this size could mean for the people who already live here.
A facility this large could place new demands on local roads, water, utilities, emergency services, and other infrastructure.
It also raises reasonable questions about nearby land, the environment, public incentives, and how quickly surrounding communities may have to adapt to new development.
Those questions do not mean we have to oppose every new project or turn away good jobs.
They mean we should understand what we are agreeing to before making decisions that could reshape a community for decades.
My concern with the SpaceX project has been the need for greater transparency and meaningful public involvement.
Grimes County residents deserve clear information about the scale of the development, what resources it will require, what protections are being put in place, and what commitments have been made on their behalf.
Growth can be good for our communities, but it should be responsible growth.
The people who live in Grimes County should have a voice in how their community changes and confidence that their needs are being considered alongside the economic opportunities the project may bring.
