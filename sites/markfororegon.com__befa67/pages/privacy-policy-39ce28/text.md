Information we collect We collect information you provide directly to us, including: Name, email, ZIP code, and (optional) phone number when you sign up.
Volunteer interests, availability, and skills when you submit the volunteer form.
RSVP information when you register for an event.
The content of any message you send us.
Donation information processed through our payment processor, including amount and billing details required by Oregon Secretary of State reporting rules.
We also collect limited technical information automatically: Standard server logs (IP address, user agent, page requested, timestamp).
Anonymous analytics to understand which pages and posts are useful.
We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
3.
How we use information To respond to your messages and questions.
To coordinate volunteer activity and event logistics.
To send campaign updates, event reminders, and (with your separate opt-in) fundraising appeals.
To comply with Oregon and federal campaign-finance reporting rules.
To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud or abuse.
4.
SMS / Text messaging program Friends of Mark Norman operates an SMS program under the name Friends of Mark Norman SMS Program .
The program is opt-in only.
What we collect The mobile phone number you provide on a sign-up, contact, or volunteer form.
A timestamped record of your consent (which checkbox you ticked, on which page, at what time, from what IP address).
How we use phone numbers Informational messages — campaign updates, event reminders, volunteer-shift coordination, replies to inquiries.
Promotional messages (separate opt-in) — fundraising appeals, donation drives, and event invitations tied to the campaign.
Consent covers both calls and text messages at the number you provide.
Messaging may include requests for donation.
Msg frequency may vary.
Msg & data rates may apply.
You can reply STOP to opt-out, or HELP for help.
Data retention We retain phone numbers and timestamped consent records for the duration of the 2026 election cycle plus 5 years, to satisfy campaign-finance recordkeeping rules and to honor opt-out requests.
Numbers that opt out are added to a permanent suppression list so we never message them again.
Data deletion requests You can request that we delete your phone number, email, or other personal information at any time by emailing mark@markfororegon.com or calling +1 503-905-9275 .
We honor opt-out requests within 10 business days, and full deletion requests within 30 days, subject to any legal recordkeeping obligations.
Text Messaging Opt-In Data Text Messaging Opt-In Data: We will not share or sell your text messaging opt-in data, consent, or related personal information with any third parties, unless required by law.
SMS opt-in data is excluded from any aggregated, partner, or vendor data sharing described elsewhere in this policy.
5.
How we share information We share information only in the following circumstances: With service providers who help us run the campaign (email platform, payment processor, voter file vendor) under written agreements that limit their use to providing services to us.
With government agencies when required by Oregon or federal law.
To protect the rights, property, or safety of the campaign, staff, or the public.
We do not sell personal information.
SMS opt-in data is never shared, even with the service-provider categories above, except to the SMS aggregator that physically delivers your message.
