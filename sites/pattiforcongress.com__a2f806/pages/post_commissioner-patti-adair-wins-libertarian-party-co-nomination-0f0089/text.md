Commissioner Patti Adair Wins Libertarian Party Co-Nomination
PORTLAND, OR- Today, Deschutes County Commissioner and candidate for Oregon’s 5th Congressional District, Patti Adair, announced she won the Libertarian Party nomination for Oregon’s 5th Congressional District in addition to the Republican nomination.
Commissioner Adair stated:
“I am honored and humbled to win the Libertarian Party of Oregon’s co-nomination.
When I announced my candidacy in November, I promised to build a broad coalition that truly reflects our district.
Unlike Janelle Bynum, who consistently refuses to vote for commonsense bipartisan bills, I will work with members of all parties to deliver real results for Oregonians.
Libertarians will always have a seat at my table when I am in Congress and I look forward to working with them to bring back common sense to the 5th congressional district.”
