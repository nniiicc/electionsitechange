Fight for our Future I’m Timothy Grady, and I’m running for governor because Ohio needs bold leadership to break the cycle of corruption, decline, and business-as-usual politics.
I believe in a Rustbelt Renaissance, in an Ohio that builds, that leads, that creates real opportunity in every corner of the state.
I’m not part of the old system.
I’m an independent, a reformer, and I’m building a new movement to fight for the future; fight for a robust economy, honest government, and a state that finally works for all of us.
Our Vision We have the boldest, most comprehensive plan for making Ohio affordable, innovative, and prosperous.
It begins with investing in Ohio’s cities, it’s people, and the things that connect us.
But it’s so much more than that.
Learn more about the Ohio Vision.
Principles Three overarching principles will guide this campaign, our policies, and our approach to governing: Integrity, Freedom, and Improvement.
So… IFI.
Integrity is our commitment to character and holding ourselves and others to a high standard of behavior, to honesty, and public service.
Freedom is our commitment to individual liberty, choice, and opportunity that this country was founded upon.
Improvement is our commitment to building a better future and a stronger country for ourselves and for future generations.
Integrity Integrity is the Foundation of Trust.
Without integrity, there is no trust .
And without trust, nothing works, not our economy, not our democracy, not our society.
Corruption is destroying us.
When people get ahead not by merit and hard work, but through bribery and connections, it drains our energy, our determination, and our sense of civic duty.
It creates a system where dishonesty thrives, where government serves the powerful instead of the people, and where the fabric of society unravels.
We must root out corruption.
Honesty, integrity, and public service must be restored, not just in government, but in our culture.
Trust is what holds us together, and we must rebuild it.
As a culture of corruption has spread, we have collectively lost faith in the institutions that made us great and strong, in our schools, in our justice system, in our financial institutions, in our local governments, in the government as a whole.
Restoring that trust is essential to our future success and must be a priority.
We will hold ourselves to the highest standards.
It doesn’t matter how corrupt our opponents are.
It doesn’t matter what others do.
We do what’s right because we are in this for our families, our neighbors, our communities, and our country, not for personal gain.
Honesty is non-negotiable.
We will not lie for votes, for money, or for power.
Politicians fear the truth because they believe people prefer comforting lies.
But the truth is what people deserve, even harsh truths.
The truth builds trust.
The truth allows us to face the world as it is and shape it into what it should be.
Public service is about duty, not ambition.
It means putting the public before party, the country before self.
It means leading with character, making decisions based on principle, and always working toward the greater good.
Freedom Freedom is Choice.
Freedom means protecting our individual rights; freedom of speech, religion, assembly, expression, and privacy.
It means the right to pursue happiness, make our own decisions, and learn from our own mistakes.
Freedom keeps power in check.
No one person, institution, or central authority can control everything.
When decisions are decentralized, ground-up, and made at the lowest practical level, we thrive.
Freedom means free exchange and free markets.
People, not bureaucracies, are the best judges of their own needs and desires.
When people can act on their own judgment, work, trade, and collaborate voluntarily, they create innovation and prosperity.
Markets are not just economic systems; they are the natural result of a free society.
Opportunity is essential to freedom.
If your future is determined by the circumstances of your birth, you are not truly free.
We must build a society where everyone has the means to advance, achieve, and create a better life.
Freedom from want ensures that economic choices are made from strength, not survival.
Expanding freedom means expanding choice.
More choices, better choices, and empowering people to make their own.
So in this campaign, in policy, and in life, we ask: Does this expand freedom?
Does this create opportunity?
Does this give people more choices?
Because a free society is built on the ability to choose.
Improvement and Innovation Improvement is Building a Better Future.
It is the spirit of National Improvement , the vision of leaders like Henry Clay and John Quincy Adams.
It is the belief that we prosper when we invest in ourselves; our people, our communities, and our country.
We invest in what connects us.
Roads, railways, airports, telecommunications; the infrastructure and networks that allows us to work together and achieve more than any one person could alone.
We invest in where we live.
Strong cities and thriving villages.
Affordable housing.
Clean air and water.
Stewardship of our land and natural resources that belong to all and future generations.
We build.
Great works that transform millions of lives, and small improvements that make everyday life better.
We invest in discovery.
Progress comes from innovation, through science, engineering, trial and error.
We learn.
Research institutions, moonshots, universities, community colleges, and schools; laying the foundation for anyone to climb higher and achieve more.
The sort of Improvement that Clay and Adams and further back to our Founders believed in was not just National Improvement but the Improvement of Ourselves.
That’s the idea behind this country, that people will be free to pursue their dreams and rise above.
That’s the power of democracy and liberty: the freedom to explore who we are and strive for more.
Self-Improvement.
It’s the heart of this movement.
It’s about people investing in themselves and each other.
Gaining new skills, imagining new possibilities, being better neighbors, friends, and family.
Prospering by our own hands, our own talents.
And as we improve ourselves, we improve our communities.
A strong nation isn’t just built by policy or government, it’s built by the choices we make every day.
By how we support one another.
Because no matter who is running the show, we can control and are responsible for our own lives.
Yeah, our government may be corrupt and incompetent, but we can still be better and do better and that will matter a whole lot more and it will eventually trickle through to every institution.
We create the fabric of our nation by the lives we lead, not who we vote for.
This is a campaign for the governorship of Ohio but it is also a campaign to mobilize thousands of people to work to improve themselves and their communities and that will be 1000x more impactful, in the end, regardless of the outcome.
