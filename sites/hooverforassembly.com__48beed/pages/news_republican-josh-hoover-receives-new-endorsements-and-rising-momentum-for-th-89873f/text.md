Republican Josh Hoover Receives New Endorsements and Rising Momentum For The General Election
SACRAMENTO COUNTY, CA - - Today, Josh Hoover candidate for Assembly announced an impressive list of new endorsements that he has received from around the district.
Among the list of endorsements is Former Sacramento County Sheriff John McGinness, Former Assemblymember Roger Niello, and notably Citrus Heights Water Board Member Ray Riehle who was Josh Hoover's opponent in the Primary Election on June 7.
"I am proud to have received the endorsements of the following community and political leaders," said Josh Hoover.
"The amount of support I have received in the Primary encourages me to continue to push on to victory in November."
Former Congressman Doug Ose
Former Sacramento County Sheriff John McGinness
Former Assemblymember Roger Niello
Sacramento County Supervisor Sue Frost
Former Sacramento County Supervisor Roberta MacGlashan
Former Sacramento County Supervisor Sandy Smoley
Citrus Heights City Councilmember Jeannie Bruins
Citrus Heights Water Board Member Ray Riehle
Placer County Supervisor Bonnie Gore
“Josh Hoover is backed by local law enforcement because he understands what it takes to keep our communities safe,” Sheriff John McGinness said.
“We need leaders in Sacramento who will prioritize public safety and restore accountability, that’s why I’m proud to endorse Josh Hoover for State Assembly.”
“I am happy to endorse Josh Hoover for Assembly District 7,” said former Assemblymember and current Senate District 6 candidate Roger Niello.
“He will be a strong voice and advocate for our community in the State Legislature.
We can trust him to fight for common-sense policies that will improve the lives of Californians.”
To view a full list of endorsements, go to www.HooverForAssembly.com.
