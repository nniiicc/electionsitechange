Josh Hoover Releases New TV Spot In 7th Assembly District Election
FOLSOM, CA –Today Josh Hoover announced his campaign launched a new tv ad for his campaign for the 7th Assembly District.
The ad features a local resident in the district named Samantha and highlights Assemblyman Ken Cooley's vote record that has hurt local families trying to make ends meet.
"Assemblyman Ken Cooley’s voting record at the Capitol has made inflation worse and that's why I'm running for Assembly," said Josh Hoover.
"There are thousands of people like Samantha in our district who feel betrayed by Sacramento politicians.
We need a representative in the Legislature who will stand up for the hardworking families and residents in our community.
Their voice needs to be heard.”
For more information about the election for the newly drawn 7th Assembly District please visit www.HooverforAssembly.com.
