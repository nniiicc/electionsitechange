"Folsom residents are struggling in this post-pandemic economy.
Small businesses have shut down and the cost of living is still on the rise," said Folsom Vice Mayor Rosario Rodriguez.
"I have endorsed Josh Hoover because I believe he will be a voice in the Assembly to stop out-of-control spending in Sacramento, and will best represent Folsom values." "I am honored to have received these endorsements," said Josh Hoover.
"Sacramento is out of touch with our communities.
As an Assemblymember, I intend to advocate for fiscal responsibility and to work to make California affordable for the taxpayers who have had to struggle for too long." The newly drawn 7th Assembly District includes the Sacramento County communities of Carmichael, Citrus Heights, Fair Oaks, Folsom, Foothill Farms, Gold River, North Highlands, Orangevale, Rancho Cordova, and Rosemont.
For more information please visit www.HooverForAssembly.com .
