“I am grateful for the support of the Folsom Chamber of Commerce in my Assembly race.
California continues to be one of the least friendly states to operate a small business and that has to change.
I will continue to fight for better policies at the State Capitol that make it easier for small business owners in our community to create jobs, serve our residents, and pursue the American Dream,” said Assemblyman Hoover.
Assemblyman Josh Hoover has distinguished himself as a proactive advocate for the residents of the 7th Assembly District, addressing key issues that impact their daily lives since being elected in 2022.
His efforts have led to policies that reduce financial pressures on families and individuals, helping to decrease the cost of living.
Hoover’s commitment to creating safer neighborhoods and bolstering public confidence has garnered him the support of taxpayer protection groups and the state’s leading law enforcement organizations.
For more information please visit www.HooverForAssembly.com.
Hoover was elected to the State Assembly in 2022 to represent the voters of California’s 7th Assembly District in one of the most competitive elections in the state.
Encompassing the northeastern part of Sacramento County, the district includes the cities of Folsom, Citrus Heights, and Rancho Cordova.
In the March 2024 Statewide Primary Election, Assemblyman Hoover led his nearest competitor by 23 percentage points.
