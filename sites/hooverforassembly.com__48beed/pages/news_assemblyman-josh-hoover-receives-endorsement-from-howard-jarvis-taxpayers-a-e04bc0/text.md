Assemblyman Josh Hoover Receives Endorsement from Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC
Folsom, CA — Assemblyman Josh Hoover has announced the endorsement of the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC (HJTAPAC), California’s leading organization dedicated to defending taxpayers.
This significant endorsement solidifies Josh Hoover as the taxpayers' choice for Assembly, affirming his commitment to fiscal responsibility and the protection of taxpayer rights.
Josh Hoover expressed his thanks, saying, “I am truly honored to get the endorsement from the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC.
They work hard to defend Proposition 13 and protect Californians from over-taxation.
I am fully committed to making sure taxpayers continue to have a voice in the State Capitol.”
With the government constantly pushing for more taxes, HJTA’s role as a guardian of Proposition 13 is vital.
Their efforts have saved California taxpayers about $528 billion.
The average family of four in California has saved over $60,000 because of HJTA’s actions.
HJTA’s endorsement highlights Hoover’s dedication to fighting unnecessary taxes and protecting taxpayer rights.
As Assemblyman, Hoover has shown a strong record of promoting fiscal responsibility.
He successfully pushed for an audit of California’s homelessness spending, exposing a lack of transparency in how over $24 billion of taxpayer money was used while the homelessness issue worsened.
This audit is an important step in making state spending more transparent and accountable, showing Hoover’s commitment to better management of taxpayer funds.
