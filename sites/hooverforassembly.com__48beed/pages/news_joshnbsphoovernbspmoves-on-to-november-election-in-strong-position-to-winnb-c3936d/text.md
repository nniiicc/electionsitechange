Josh Hoover Moves on to November Election in Strong Position to Win Assembly District 7
Democrat Ken Cooley’s big bucks and political games don’t help him in Statewide Primary Election
Folsom, CA - - The last report from the Sacramento County elections office has set the stage for a competitive run-off election between Folsom Republican Josh Hoover and Democrat Assemblyman Ken Cooley.
Josh Hoover bested three other Republicans with a commanding 34% of the vote.
On the other hand, Cooley finished slightly over 50%, a disappointing number considering the resources he expended in the primary.
Current Election Results
33.98% Josh Hoover (R)
8.42% Ray Riehle (R)
5.55% Jeffrey Perrine (R)
1.11% Quintin Toshi Levesque (R)
50.91% Ken Cooley (D)
Ken Cooley and the Democrat Party invested hundreds of thousands of dollars promoting expelled Republican Jeffrey Perrine, Ken Cooley’s preferred run-off candidate, who only managed to garner slightly more than 6,000 votes districtwide.
A clearly embarrassing number for Ken Cooley to attach himself to the Perrine campaign.
