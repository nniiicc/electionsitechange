Josh Hoover Announces Three More City Leader Endorsements
Folsom, Calif. -- Today, Josh Hoover announced that he has received endorsements from three local city leaders in his campaign for Assembly District 7: Councilmember Sarah Aquino from Folsom, Vice Mayor Tim Schaefer and Councilmember Bret Daniels from Citrus Heights.
"Josh has been a tremendous advocate for families and students on the school board and is always willing to work collaboratively with the City Council to serve the residents of Folsom," said Folsom City Councilmember Sarah Aquino.
" He will be a strong proponent for local control in the State Assembly and has a track record of protecting taxpayers."
This endorsement demonstrates the support that Hoover has gathered around the district going into this election cycle.
Recently he has announced the endorsements of the California Republican Party as well as Assemblyman Kevin Kiley and Board of Equalization Member Ted Gaines.
You can view more of his endorsements at HooverForAssembly.com.
Josh currently serves as a School Board Member for the Folsom Cordova School Unified School District.
He earned a Bachelor’s Degree in political science and public policy from UCLA and a Master’s Degree in public administration from USC.
He lives in Folsom with his wife Nicole and their three children.
The newly drawn 7th Assembly District includes the communities of Carmichael, Citrus Heights, Fair Oaks, Folsom, Foothill Farms, Gold River, North Highlands, Orangevale, Rancho Cordova, and Rosemont.
