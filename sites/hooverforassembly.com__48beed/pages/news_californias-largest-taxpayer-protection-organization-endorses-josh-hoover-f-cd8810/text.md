California’s Largest Taxpayer Protection Organization Endorses Josh Hoover for State Assembly – Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC Endorses
Folsom, Calif. – Today, Josh Hoover announced receiving the endorsement of Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC in his campaign for the 7th Assembly District.
The Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC is the political organization that represents the largest taxpayer protection organization in the State of California.
The endorsement is noteworthy as the Howard Jarvis Taxpayers Association PAC is one of the most coveted endorsements in the State of California.
Hoover has all the momentum in the campaign for the 7th Assembly District, recently Hoover announced the endorsement of the California Republican Party as well as a strong list of community leaders from around the district.
Howard Jarvis Taxpayer Association PAC was founded by Howard Jarvis to back candidates for office who support Proposition 13.
Howard knew that once Proposition 13 was in place it would be attacked by those who support unlimited taxation and spending, and he wanted to make sure taxpayers were represented by strong advocates in the Legislature.
