The three law enforcement associations add their name to a coalition of law enforcement that supports Josh Hoover for California’s new 7th Assembly District.
“Law enforcement will have a trusted friend in the State Legislature when Josh Hoover is elected to the Assembly,” said Scott Somersett, President of the Folsom Police Officers’ Association.
"He is ready and willing to work alongside law enforcement to meet the challenges facing our officers and fight for policies that make our communities safer.
That’s why he has our support." Josh has built an impressive coalition of law enforcement leaders in his campaign.
The newly announced endorsements join the Sacramento County Deputy Sheriffs' Association, Sheriff Scott Jones and former Sheriff John McGinness in endorsing Josh Hoover.For more information please visit www.HooverForAssembly.com .
The newly drawn and open 7th Assembly District is shaping up to be one of the most competitive elections in the entire state.
The district is entirely nested within eastern Sacramento County.
The election will be held in conjunction with the November Statewide General Election on November 8th, 2022.
