There is no excuse for driving under the influence .” “After speaking with Shawn, I am asking him to suspend his campaign immediately, and when elected, I will formally ask for his resignation ,” Minogue said. “ Shawn is a father and grandfather, and he needs to put his family first.
He needs to do the work to ensure this never happens again.
If he needs help with that, I will help him .” Minogue said the moment also calls for leadership and accountability from those who seek public office. “ As governor, I will put the Commonwealth first ,” said Minogue. “ That starts with accountability, something Massachusetts has not seen from Maura Healey. ” Join Team Minogue Sign Up Form - Vertical First Name Email Zip Code Phone By providing your cell phone or mobile phone number and opting in, you are consenting to receive calls and texts, including autodialed and automated calls and texts, to that number with donation messages and notifications from Mike Minogue for Governor.
