3 Ways To Vote
Vote by Mail
Want to vote from home?
Any registered Massachusetts voter can request a Vote by Mail ballot.
Step 1 – Request Your Ballot
Your Vote by Mail application must be received by your local election office by Tuesday, October 27 at 5 PM.
Don’t wait until the deadline.
Request your ballot early so you have plenty of time to receive and return it.
Step 2 – Complete and Return Your Ballot
You can return your completed ballot:
- By mail
- At an official ballot drop box
- At your local election office
- At an early voting location during early voting hours
If you’re returning your ballot close to Election Day, consider using a drop box or returning it in person.
Vote Early
No need to wait until Election Day.
Massachusetts offers two weeks of in-person early voting.
Every city and town will have at least one early voting location, including voting hours on both weekends.
