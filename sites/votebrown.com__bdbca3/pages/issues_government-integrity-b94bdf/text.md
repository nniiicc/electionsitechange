Government Integrity
Government should work for the people — not the powerful.
Expanding Voter Eligibility
ACTION:
Enact same day voter registration and end voter purging.
No eligible Georgian should lose their right to vote because they missed a deadline or skipped an election.
Georgia should end voter registration purges and allow same-day registration so every citizen can make their voice heard.
Democracy works best when everyone participates.
Making Government Truly Representative
ACTION:
Establish a full-time legislature and fair pay for lawmakers.
Georgia’s legislature handles billions in state business, yet meets only 40 days a year and pays salaries too low for ordinary citizens to serve.
That leaves governing to the wealthy or well-connected.
By creating a full-time legislature with fair pay, we can build a government that reflects Georgia’s people — teachers, nurses, small-business owners, and working families alike.
