FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE – 1/22/26 Tacoma Deputy Mayor Joe Bushnell Announces Campaign for Washington State House of Representatives, 29th Legislative District, Position 2 TACOMA, Wash. - Joe Bushnell, current Tacoma Deputy Mayor and City Councilmember since 2021, officially announced his candidacy today for 29th Legislative District, State Representative, Position 2.
Translating a legacy of service into legislative action, Bushnell is committed to delivering stability, competence, and compassion when Washington state needs it most.
"We are living through a moment that requires steady hands and serious leadership," said Bushnell.
"To assume that the status quo will continue to work for our community is no longer an option.
From the cost of groceries to the reliability of our healthcare, families are facing new realities.
We need to be honest about these challenges and act with urgency to support our neighbors." Bushnell’s campaign centers on insulating families in Washington state from economic volatility and ensuring local communities—from Tacoma, to Parkland and Spanaway—have the resources they need to thrive.
"Regardless of the shifting political landscape at the national level, and a combative Trump administration, we have a responsibility to stand up for our residents right here at home," Bushnell continued.
"We cannot wait for the 'perfect time' to address affordability or healthcare access.
We must govern for the moment we are in, building resilience so that families in Washington state do not have to absorb economic shocks alone." Bushnell outlined a platform focused on an economy that rewards hard work and ensures prosperity is shared.
He advocates for tax structures that relieve the burden on working families and seniors, rather than relying on regressive taxes that punish those on fixed incomes.
"An economy that works for everyone does not punish success, but it does ensure that working people aren’t squeezed for every dime they have, while corporations and the ultrawealthy continue to post record breaking profits," said Bushnell.
"We must push for wages that keep pace with the cost of living and implement tax relief that helps families stay afloat, including extending the Working Families Tax Credit." On healthcare, Bushnell emphasized the need to protect coverage for Washington state’s most vulnerable populations, including seniors and low-income families.
"It is imperative that Washington state defends access to healthcare,” said Bushnell.
“Nobody should face the daunting choice between seeing a doctor and paying their bills.
I am committed to protecting our state healthcare systems, and pushing for universal healthcare, to ensure our neighbors remain covered, regardless of changes to federal policy." Since joining the Tacoma City Council in 2021, Bushnell has been a driving force behind improved public safety and community engagement.
His tenure has seen a reduction in emergency response times, the hiring of additional officers, and targeted crime reduction in key areas such as South Hosmer Street.
"These improvements did not happen in a vacuum," Bushnell noted.
"It took concerted effort, engaging with local community leaders, and listening to residents.
We need to take that same holistic approach at the state level—building neighborhoods where everyone has the opportunity to thrive in a safe, clean environment." The son of a Cambodian refugee and a U.S.
Marine Corps combat veteran, Bushnell views public office as a continuation of service.
While our country is in a moment of crisis, Bushnell has the lived experience needed to stand up for Washingtons working families and protect our communities from the Trump administration and executive overreach.
"I believe in leading by example.
We must face our challenges head-on, with honor, courage, and a deep commitment to the community we serve," Bushnell concluded.
"As our families navigate uncertain times, Washington state must be strong enough to have their backs." For more information, contact Joe at (253) 792-1857, Joe@JoeBushnell.com, or visit www.JoeBushnell.com to learn more.
