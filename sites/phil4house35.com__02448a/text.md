The Plan
We believe Indiana works best when the government focuses on practical solutions that improve the lives of Hoosiers.
- Strengthen Public Education: Keep schools focused on educating students—not constantly fighting for funding.
Protect rural schools so every community has access to quality education.
- Protect Fair Representation: End partisan gerrymandering and ensure Hoosiers choose their elected officials, not the other way around.
- Stand Up for Rural Communities: Protect farmland, water resources, and local communities from unchecked data center development that offers limited long-term jobs while receiving significant tax incentives.
- Defend Rural Healthcare: Keep rural hospitals open, expand access to affordable health insurance, and ensure every Hoosier can receive quality care close to home.
- Legalize and Regulate Marijuana: Keep tax revenue in Indiana instead of sending billions of dollars to neighboring states.
Legalize adult-use marijuana, decriminalize possession, and prioritize treatment and rehabilitation over incarceration for nonviolent offenses.
Our approach is simple: Protect Indiana's communities, invest in people, and build a stronger future for every Hoosier.
