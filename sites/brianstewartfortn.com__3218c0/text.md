About Brian
A respected small business owner of One Stop Realty and Auction since 2019, Brian Stewart was raised in Goodlettsville, Tennessee, and has dedicated his career to serving the families and businesses of his community.
An influential leader in Sumner County, Stewart is interesting continuing the tremendous progress of the current Republican led legislature and will focus on helping to continue to balance the state’s budget, cut taxes, improve education, stop illegal immigration from threatening our communities, protect our seniors from abuse, and be a watchdog for military families.
Known for his hands on leadership, strong work ethic, and unwavering support for neighbors in need, Stewart is guided by a genuine desire to serve, a deep love for the community that raised him, and an unshakable belief in integrity and accountability.
He currently serves as President of the Sumner County Realtors Association, is a member of the Hendersonville Morning Rotary (Paul Harris Fellow), Board member of Grace Place, Tennessee Realtors Government Affairs Committee, Habitat for Humanity and involved with philanthropic associations in the community.
Stewart strongly supports the 2nd Amendment and is a member of the National Rifle Association, Tennessee Right to Life, and was awarded Sumner County Realtor of the Year in 2021.
Brian is married to his wife Kelly, a fellow small business owner in Sumner County for over 12 years.
