Jim Guzofski Jr.
Governance Over Gamesmanship
A Steady Hand For Our District
I currently serve as your State Representative for Rockingham District 1, representing the towns of Northwood and Nottingham, where I sit on the Election Law committee.
My focus has been bringing clarity, accountability, and practical solutions to the legislative process — especially in the areas that directly impact our local communities.
I have introduced legislation to clarify the authority and responsibilities surrounding police commissions and municipal governance, and sponsored a bill to strengthen access to suitable public facilities for use as polling locations — a bill that passed committee unanimously and the House on a voice vote, and is currently under consideration in the Senate.
Beyond Concord, I have been elected three times as a Selectman in Northwood and currently serve as chair of the Selectboard.
I approach budgeting the same way many families do — with discipline, priorities, and the understanding that every dollar matters.

Defending Constitutional Rights
The foundation of our country is built on clearly defined rights — not negotiable privileges.
I will defend ALL Constitutional Rights, but especially Freedom of Speech, Freedom of Religion, and the Right to Bear Arms.
I will not support any legislation that weakens, redefines, or infringes upon our rights.
Local Control & Live Free
"Live Free or Die" is more than a motto — it reflects how government is supposed to function.
Government exists to protect freedom, not manage it.
I oppose top-down mandates and one-size-fits-all policies.
Individuals and local communities are best positioned to make decisions that affect their lives.
Standing With First Responders
Through my role as Chaplain with Northwood Fire and Rescue, I have seen firsthand the demands placed on those who serve.
Our first responders and veterans carry burdens most people never see.
They deserve real support — services, resources, and community backing that reflects the seriousness of their role.
Fiscal Discipline.
No New Taxes.
Government must operate with the same discipline families use every day: prioritizing spending, eliminating waste, and making tough decisions when necessary.
I will not support a state income tax or a sales tax — period.
New Hampshire's tax advantage is what keeps us free, and I will defend it.
