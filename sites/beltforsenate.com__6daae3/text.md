about Christopher belt
State Senator Christopher Belt grew up in Centreville and is a lifelong resident of Illinois’ Metro East area.
He was elected to represent the Illinois’ 57th Senate District in 2018, which encompasses communities in St.
Clair and Madison counties, including Belleville, Collinsville, O’Fallon, Granite City, Freeburg, Mascoutah and Scott Air Force Base.
