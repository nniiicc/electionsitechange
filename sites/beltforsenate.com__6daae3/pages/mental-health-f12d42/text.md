We need to make sure our young people have the resources they need to succeed, and that starts with good mental health.
As much as our kids have suffered, so have our teachers.

I’ve also partnered with local hospitals, case workers, and clinical professionals to offer mental health resources to our community during the pandemic, and my office will continue to be a bridge between our community and the mental health resources we need.
