I believe everyone should have access to affordable, high quality healthcare.
Here in the 57th District, we have several safety net hospitals that care for patients when no one else will.
That’s why I secured a $2 million grant for Touchette Regional Hospital in Centreville to continue providing services to thousands of residents.
I also stand strong in my support for women’s reproductive rights and will fight to make sure Choice is protected in Illinois for all women, but especially for those without means to travel to get care.
