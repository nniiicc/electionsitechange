I will stand firm against outside interests that threaten them and ensure our waters work for Alaskans.

Fiscal Responsibility I support a responsible budget that prioritizes essential services, reins in unnecessary spending, and ensures Alaska is getting a fair return from its natural resources.

Rising rents, limited inventory, and changing demographics are making it harder for families to stay in our communities.
