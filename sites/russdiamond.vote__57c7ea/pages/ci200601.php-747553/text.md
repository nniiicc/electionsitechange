Q: You were notified on May 21st; why didn't you self-quarantine for a full 14 days?
A: Self-quarantining is intended to isolate a person with "contact" for 14 days, the presumed incubation period of the virus.

Since my last opportunity for "contact" was on May 14th when the House adjourned at 4:51 pm, my self-quarantine ended at 4:51 pm on May 28th.
