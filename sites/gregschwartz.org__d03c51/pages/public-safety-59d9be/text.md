Public Safety On public safety, I am dedicated to supporting our emergency responders.
I have sponsored legislation that makes it easier for first responders who contract a contagious disease in the line of duty to receive benefits as compensation.
I have also signed onto Home Rule Petitions for the towns of Newton and Brookline regarding residency requirements for first responders and the appointment of special police officers respectively.
Furthermore, I have co-sponsored several other public safety bills that keep ICE out of our communities, protect the public from PFAS, and close a loophole in the state’s ability to prosecute perpetrators of child sexual assault.
Safety is a top priority of mine.
