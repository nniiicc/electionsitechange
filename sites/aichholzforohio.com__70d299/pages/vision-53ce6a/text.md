top of page Home Meet Nancy Nancy's Vision Get Involved Menu Close DONATE NANCY'S VISION Bringing proven, practical leadership to residents and families across Ohio's 27th District Safe Streets Every family deserves to feel safe in their own community.
Police officers and first responders are the backbone of a healthy community and it will be one of my top priorities in Columbus to ensure they have not only the resources they need, but the support from our elected leaders to do their jobs.
This includes: Protecting and investing in the Ohio Police and Fire Pension Fund.
Supporting funding increases for hiring new officers, upgrading critical equipment, and expanding training opportunities.
Providing our men and women in blue with the access to, and funding for, mental health resources.
Strong Communities Strong communities begin with strong schools.
I've served on my local school board, where I led the development of the school district's strategic plan.
The result of that plan was an historic investment in operating and capital expenditures to lead our community into the future.
Beyond providing fiscal responsibility and strategic thinking, I also helped the district keep kids in our schools during the COVID crisis.
While school districts across the country kept children out of school to their detriment, our district returned to in-person education quickly.
Our kids, and their parents, deserved schools that looked out for their best interests, and that's exactly what I did during my time as a school board member.
When elected, I'll fight for increased funding for education and policies that put our children's futures first.
Steady Leadership I'm a proven business leader, entrepreneur, and public servant with decades of experience building organizations, creating opportunities, and bringing people together to solve complex challenges.
Throughout my career, I've led marketing strategy for a global, multibillion-dollar company and founded two successful businesses of my own.
I've helped strengthen an organization dedicated to supporting women-owned businesses, giving more entrepreneurs the tools and resources they need to succeed.
You deserve elected leaders who have spent more time building businesses and communities than building political careers.
I'm a problem-solver who won't let politics get in the way of doing what's right for all of our communities, not just some.
