Why It Matters
Illinois Is Bigger Than One City
Chicago is one of the greatest cities in the world and an essential driver of our state’s economy.
But Illinois is far more than one city or one county.
The people of Southern Illinois, Western Illinois, Central Illinois, the collar counties, and communities across the state deserve just as much attention and advocacy.
Too many politicians forget that Illinois has 102 counties and that most Illinoisans live outside Chicago.
Don Tracy is running to make sure every part of the state has a voice in the U.S.
Senate.
My Commitment to Illinois
A Senator Who Knows the Whole State
Don has deep ties to all of Illinois.
He was born in Urbana in Eastern Illinois, raised in Mt.
Sterling in Western Illinois, and raised his family in Springfield in Central Illinois.
He has led statewide organizations and represented families, farmers, and small businesses across Illinois.
That experience has shown him how diverse this state really is.
While many Illinois families face the same broad concerns — like rising costs, safer communities, and better opportunities — those challenges do not look the same in every region.
Don understands that suburban communities have different needs than rural towns.
Industrial areas facing lost manufacturing jobs have different concerns than farming communities dependent on agriculture.
Effective leadership means understanding those differences and representing them all.
