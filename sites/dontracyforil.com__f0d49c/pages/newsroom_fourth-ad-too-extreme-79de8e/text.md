PRESS RELEASE
For Immediate Release
September 11, 2026
SPRINGFIELD — Republican U.S.
Senate candidate Don Tracy has released his fourth advertisement of the general election, “Too Extreme,” taking aim at the policies championed by Juliana Stratton that have helped drive jobs and reliable power out of Illinois while families and businesses absorb higher costs.
The new digital ad strengthens Tracy’s message that Illinois cannot afford another round of extreme ideology in Washington.
“Illinois families are not asking for a socialist revolution.
They are asking why private sector employers and their jobs keep leaving Illinois, why electric bills cost more, and why the Chicago Democrats in charge of Illinois keep doubling down on taxes and government spending,” Tracy said.
“You do not grow a state by incentivizing private employers and Illinoisans to move to other States and shutting down the power that allows communities to grow and prosper.”
The ad points to job creators that have left or are considering leaving Illinois, including Caterpillar’s headquarters move, Morton Salt’s departure from Chicago, the Chicago Bears’ plans to move to Hammond, Indiana, and recently announced closures of Illinois power plants as utility bills climb.
“First they make it harder to create a job here.
Then they make it more expensive to keep the lights on.
Then they act surprised when communities start to hollow out.
That is not compassion.
That is not leadership.
That is a failed government policy, and Illinois has already paid enough for it.
“We can have a stronger Illinois.
However, we will not get there by electing the same extreme blueprint that has already cost us jobs, population growth, power plants, and too many of the great businesses that helped built this state.
Stratton is just too extreme for Illinois.”
