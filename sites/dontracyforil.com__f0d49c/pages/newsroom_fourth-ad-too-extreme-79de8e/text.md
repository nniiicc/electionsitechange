Senate candidate Don Tracy has released his fourth advertisement of the general election, “Too Extreme, ” taking aim at the policies championed by Juliana Stratton that have helped drive jobs and reliable power out of Illinois while families and businesses absorb higher costs.
The new digital ad strengthens Tracy’s message that Illinois cannot afford another round of extreme ideology in Washington.
“Illinois families are not asking for a socialist revolution.
They are asking why private sector employers and their jobs keep leaving Illinois, why electric bills cost more, and why the Chicago Democrats in charge of Illinois keep doubling down on taxes and government spending,” Tracy said .
“You do not grow a state by incentivizing private employers and Illinoisans to move to other States and shutting down the power that allows communities to grow and prosper.” The ad points to job creators that have left or are considering leaving Illinois, including Caterpillar’s headquarters move, Morton Salt’s departure from Chicago, the Chicago Bears’ plans to move to Hammond, Indiana, and recently announced closures of Illinois power plants as utility bills climb.
“First they make it harder to create a job here.
Then they make it more expensive to keep the lights on.
Then they act surprised when communities start to hollow out.
That is not compassion.
That is not leadership.
That is a failed government policy, and Illinois has already paid enough for it.
“We can have a stronger Illinois.
However, we will not get there by electing the same extreme blueprint that has already cost us jobs, population growth, power plants, and too many of the great businesses that helped built this state.
Stratton is just too extreme for Illinois.” “Too Extreme” can be viewed here.

No power.

When our communities die next, we’ll know just who to blame: Juliana Stratton… just too extreme for Illinois.
### About Don Tracy: Don is Senior Counsel at Brown, Hay & Stephens, the oldest law firm in Illinois, where Abraham Lincoln famously practiced law for four years.
Public service is important to Don, with a lifetime spent in community service, most often in volunteer positions.
He has served as Chairman of the Illinois Republican Party, Chairman of the Illinois Gaming Board, Secretary of the Illinois Bar Foundation, President of the Sangamon County Bar Association, Chairman of the Illinois Corporate Acts Advisory Committee, and President of the Abraham Lincoln Association, President of the Oak Ridge Cemetery Board, among other community leadership positions.
Born in Urbana, raised in Mt.
Sterling in Western Illinois, and having raised his own family in Springfield in Central Illinois, Don has deep ties to “downstate Illinois.” As the oldest of 12 children, family has always been important to Don.
BACK TO NEWSROOM Don Tracy is committed to strengthening Illinois by growing the economy, supporting small businesses, and making life more affordable for working families.
With decades of leadership in business and public service, he believes in accountable government, safe communities, and practical, commonsense solutions that create opportunity for future generations.
