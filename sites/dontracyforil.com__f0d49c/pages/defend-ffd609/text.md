Senate Illinois Families Need a Fighter for the American Dream Don stepped up to run because he saw what career politicians with extreme agendas are doing to the country — and how everyday Illinois families are paying the price.
From open borders to taxpayer-funded benefits for non-citizens, from attacks on law enforcement to policies that make life more expensive, too many leaders have lost touch with the priorities of working families.
In Illinois, families are dealing with higher utility bills, rising housing costs, expensive groceries and gas, and health care costs that continue to climb.
Don believes Illinois needs a senator who will champion common sense, defend our freedoms, and fight for the people who work hard, raise families, run businesses, and keep our communities strong.

Contact the Campaign Questions voters ask the most.
Why is Don Tracy running for the U.S.
Senate?
Don is running because he believes Illinois needs strong, common sense leadership that will defend the American Dream, lower the cost of living, and stand up for working families instead of extreme political agendas.
What are Don Tracy's top priorities?
His top priorities include lowering the cost of living, supporting safer communities, securing the border, protecting constitutional freedoms, growing the economy, and representing all of Illinois in Washington.
What does Don Tracy mean by “defending the American Dream”?
He means making sure hard work still leads to opportunity.
That includes lower costs, stronger communities, better economic opportunity, and leadership that helps families build a secure future.
How will Don Tracy help working families?
By fighting to lower taxes, energy costs, inflation, and health care costs; reducing unnecessary regulations; supporting job creators; and advancing policies that help families keep more of what they earn.
Why does representation matter so much in this race?
Because Illinois is a diverse state, and too many communities outside Chicago feel overlooked.
Don believes every county and every family deserves a voice in Washington.
How is Don Tracy different from his opponents?
Don presents himself as a common sense conservative with real-world experience in business, family life, and statewide leadership — not a career politician driven by ideological extremes.
