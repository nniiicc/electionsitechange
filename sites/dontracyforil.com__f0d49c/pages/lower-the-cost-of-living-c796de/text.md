Lowering the Cost of Living for Working Families For too many Illinois families, the cost of living has made the American Dream feel out of reach.
Higher taxes, rising utility bills, expensive groceries, unaffordable housing, and growing health care costs are squeezing household budgets and forcing people to make impossible choices.
Don Tracy is running to fight for common sense solutions that make life more affordable, strengthen the economy, and help working families get ahead again.

Contact the Campaign Questions voters ask the most.
Why is the cost of living such a major issue in Illinois?
Because working families are being hit from every direction — high taxes, rising utility bills, expensive housing, growing health care costs, and higher prices for groceries and gas.
Too many people feel like they are working harder but falling further behind.
How would Don Tracy help lower costs?
By advancing common sense policies that reduce taxes, cut wasteful spending, lower energy costs, support affordable housing, increase health care price transparency, and reduce unnecessary regulations on businesses.
Why is energy policy so important to affordability?
Because energy costs affect everything — gas prices, utility bills, manufacturing costs, transportation, and the price of everyday goods.
Lower, more reliable energy costs help lower pressure across the economy.
How would this help small businesses and workers?
Lower taxes and fewer regulatory burdens make it easier for businesses to grow, hire, invest, and create better job opportunities in communities across Illinois.
Why does Don Tracy connect affordability to the American Dream?
Because when families cannot afford basic necessities, it becomes harder to build a stable future, buy a home, raise children, or save for retirement.
Don believes public policy should help restore opportunity, not put it further out of reach.
