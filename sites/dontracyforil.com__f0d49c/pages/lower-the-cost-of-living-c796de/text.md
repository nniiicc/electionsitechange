Lowering the Cost of Living for Working Families
For too many Illinois families, the cost of living has made the American Dream feel out of reach.
Higher taxes, rising utility bills, expensive groceries, unaffordable housing, and growing health care costs are squeezing household budgets and forcing people to make impossible choices.
Don Tracy is running to fight for common sense solutions that make life more affordable, strengthen the economy, and help working families get ahead again.

WHAT DON TRACY IS FIGHTING FOR.
- Lower the Cost of Living for Working Families – Put affordability at the center of public policy so families can keep up, get ahead, and plan for the future.
- Lower Taxes and Reduce Inflation – Cut wasteful spending, improve efficiency, and help families keep more of what they earn.
- Lower Energy and Utility Costs – Support a reasonable energy policy that uses available energy sources, protects reliability, and reduces household costs.
- Make Housing More Affordable – Support policies that expand opportunity and remove barriers that make it harder to build affordable homes.
- Lower Health Care and Drug Costs – Promote price transparency, more competition, and reforms that help reduce insurance and prescription costs.
- Support Small Businesses and Job Creators – Cut excessive regulations and lower the cost of doing business so employers can grow and hire.
- Stand Up for Manufacturers and Farmers – Fight for a fair playing field for the industries that power communities across Illinois.
- Restore the American Dream – Build an economy where hard work leads to opportunity, stability, and a better future.

Why It Matters
Life Is Too Expensive in Illinois
Working families across Illinois are being stretched to the limit.
Utility prices are at record highs.
Housing costs are making it harder for seniors to stay in their homes and harder for young families to get started.
Gas and groceries remain too expensive, and health care costs continue to rise.
When everyday essentials become unaffordable, families are forced to make painful choices.
That’s not the future Illinoisans should have to accept.
Don believes Washington should focus on lowering costs, expanding opportunity, and making it possible for families to live with greater stability and confidence.

Lower Taxes and Inflation
Illinois families already carry too much of the burden.

Affordable, Reliable Energy
Energy policy affects nearly everything people pay for — from gas to utilities to the cost of goods.
Don supports an all-of-the-above, common sense energy approach that lowers costs, protects energy independence, and supports a responsible clean energy future.
Lower Health Care Costs
Families need relief from rising insurance premiums and prescription prices.
Don supports greater price transparency, more competition, and reforms that bring down health care costs for everyday Illinoisans.
More Affordable Housing
Seniors and young families alike are feeling the pressure of rising housing costs.

Stronger Small Businesses
