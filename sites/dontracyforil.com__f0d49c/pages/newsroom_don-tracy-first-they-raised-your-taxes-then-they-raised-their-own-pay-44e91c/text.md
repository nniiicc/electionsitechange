Don Tracy: First They Raised Your Taxes.
Then They Raised Their Own Pay.
SPRINGFIELD — According to a new report , when the Pritzker-Stratton administration approved a record $55.9 billion budget with more than $800 million in new taxes and tax increases, they also increased legislator pay to a record level.
Since 2019, Illinois lawmakers have increased their base salaries by 44.9%, nearly 34% faster than the average Illinois worker’s paycheck has grown during Juliana Stratton’s time in office.
“They raised your taxes, and then they raised their own pay, “said US Senate Candidate Don Tracy. “ While your family is paying more for groceries, gas, utilities and just about everything else, Juliana Stratton’s state government team made sure the paychecks of her political allies keep growing.
“For nearly eight years, Stratton has stood shoulder to shoulder with Gov.
Pritzker and the politicians passing really big spending budgets, raising taxes and growing government.
Now we learn they’ve also been rewarding themselves while Illinois families struggle just to keep up.
“Government exists to serve the people, not the other way around.
Abraham Lincoln practiced law at the same firm where I practice today.
He understood that simple truth.
Somewhere along the way, too many of our elected officials forgot that.
“There is a clear choice in this election: Juliana Stratton wants to take the same political culture that raised taxes, rewarded insiders and made Illinois less affordable to Washington.
I’ll fight to bring common sense back to government and make life more affordable for the people who make the state work.
It’s not complicated.” ### About Don Tracy: Don is Senior Counsel at Brown, Hay & Stephens, the oldest law firm in Illinois, where Abraham Lincoln famously practiced law for four years.
Public service is important to Don, with a lifetime spent in community service, most often in volunteer positions.
He has served as Chairman of the Illinois Republican Party, Chairman of the Illinois Gaming Board, Secretary of the Illinois Bar Foundation, President of the Sangamon County Bar Association, Chairman of the Illinois Corporate Acts Advisory Committee, and President of the Abraham Lincoln Association, President of the Oak Ridge Cemetery Board, among other community leadership positions.
Born in Urbana, raised in Mt.
Sterling in Western Illinois, and having raised his own family in Springfield in Central Illinois, Don has deep ties to “downstate Illinois.” As the oldest of 12 children, family has always been important to Don.
BACK TO NEWSROOM Don Tracy is committed to strengthening Illinois by growing the economy, supporting small businesses, and making life more affordable for working families.
With decades of leadership in business and public service, he believes in accountable government, safe communities, and practical, commonsense solutions that create opportunity for future generations.
