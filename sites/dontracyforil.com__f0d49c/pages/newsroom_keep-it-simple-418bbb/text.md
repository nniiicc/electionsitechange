Senate candidate Don Tracy released his second campaign this week, “Keep It Simple: Make Illinois Business Friendly,” highlighting the real-world consequences of Illinois’ high-tax, excess regulatory approach to business.
The ad cites Caterpillar, Morton Salt and Johnsonville Brats as examples of iconic Illinois businesses that have recently moved jobs or operations out of Illinois, while noting that even the Chicago Bears have threatened to leave the state.
Contrasting Tracy’s approach with the extreme tax and spend agenda championed by Lt.
Governor Juliana Stratton, the ad reinforces Tracy’s broader campaign argument that Illinois families are paying the price for an agenda that has and will produce higher costs of living and fewer economic opportunities.
It offers a new path forward in Tracy’s common sense approach and a return to practical, time-tested principles.
“Cut taxes.
Encourage investment.
Let businesses grow and create jobs.
Give our kids a reason to build their futures here,” Tracy says.
“It’s not complicated.
Let’s make Illinois business friendly.” “Keep It Simple: Make Illinois Business Friendly” can be viewed here, and is airing statewide on streaming and digital platforms.
Transcript I’m Don Tracy.
I like checkers and cheeseburgers.
I keep it simple… When things get complicated job creators jump ship… Like Caterpillar, Morton Salt, Johnsonville Brats… Even our Chicago Bears might leave.
And Juliana Stratton’s extreme regulations and tax increases chase them away.
Illinois, it’s not complicated: cut taxes, promote job growth, make Illinois business friendly.
I’m Don Tracy.
I’m running for US Senate.
Let’s keep it simple.
### About Don Tracy: Don is Senior Counsel at Brown, Hay & Stephens, the oldest law firm in Illinois, where Abraham Lincoln famously practiced law as a young lawyer.
Public service is important to Don, with a lifetime spent in community service, most often in volunteer positions.
He has served as Chairman of the Illinois Republican Party, Chairman of the Illinois Gaming Board, Secretary of the Illinois Bar Foundation, President of the Sangamon County Bar Association, Chairman of the Illinois Corporate Acts Advisory Committee, President of the Abraham Lincoln Association, and President of the Oak Ridge Cemetery Board, among other community leadership positions.
Born in Urbana in Eastern Illinois, raised in Mt.
Sterling in Western Illinois, and having raised his own family in Springfield in Central Illinois, Don has deep ties to Illinois.
As the oldest of 12 children, family has always been important to Don.
BACK TO NEWSROOM Don Tracy is committed to strengthening Illinois by growing the economy, supporting small businesses, and making life more affordable for working families.
With decades of leadership in business and public service, he believes in accountable government, safe communities, and practical, commonsense solutions that create opportunity for future generations.
