Email text support Privacy Policy Program summary Program name: Caylin Young for Delegate Campaign Text Alerts Support: info@caylinyoung.com or (443) 333-9809 Text HELP for help Text STOP to opt out Program name and description The program name is Caylin Young for Delegate Campaign Text Alerts .
The program sends campaign-related SMS messages to supporters who choose to opt in, including volunteer follow-up, campaign updates, event reminders, voter reminders, and other information related to the Caylin Young for Delegate campaign.
Message frequency Message frequency varies based on campaign activity, volunteer needs, events, and election reminders.
Subscribers may receive recurring messages.
Message and data rates Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier plan.
The campaign does not charge a separate fee to participate in the text messaging program.
Support contact information For support, contact the campaign at info@caylinyoung.com or call (443) 333-9809 .
Opt-out and help instructions To get help with the program, reply HELP .
To stop receiving campaign text messages, reply STOP at any time.
After you opt out, you should no longer receive further campaign text messages except for a final confirmation message where applicable.
Consent and eligibility By opting in, you confirm that you are the authorized user of the mobile number provided and that you agree to receive campaign text messages from Caylin Young for Delegate.
Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of donating, volunteering, or voting.
Carrier disclaimer Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Privacy Information collected in connection with the text messaging program is also governed by the campaign's Privacy Policy , including the statement that mobile information and SMS opt-in data will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
