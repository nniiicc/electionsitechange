Delegate Caylin Young represents District 45 in the Maryland House of Delegates.
His campaign focuses on the needs residents raise most often: safer neighborhoods, stronger schools, housing stability, jobs, youth opportunity, and responsive constituent service.
What the House of Delegates does for District 45 The House of Delegates is one chamber of the Maryland General Assembly.
Delegates introduce and vote on state legislation, review the state budget, work in committees, and help residents navigate state-level issues.
For District 45 residents, that work matters when state decisions affect public safety funding, school policy, housing law, public health, transportation, and local services.
A strong delegate should be reachable, visible in the district, and serious about translating local needs into policy.
