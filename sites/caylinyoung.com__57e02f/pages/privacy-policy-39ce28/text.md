This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through caylinyoung.com, how it may be used by the campaign, and what visitors can do if they want their information updated or removed.

Email a privacy request Texting terms Last updated June 20, 2026 Applies to Volunteer and campaign contact submissions Email, phone, and donation links on the website Basic technical information collected through hosting systems What data this website collects If you fill out a campaign form on this website, send an email, call, or otherwise contact the campaign through links on the site, the campaign may receive information such as your name, email address, phone number, ZIP code, and any message or volunteer interest you choose to provide.
The site and its hosting systems may also collect limited technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and general website activity needed to operate, secure, and improve the website.
How the campaign uses collected information To respond to your questions or requests To follow up about volunteering, events, voting, or campaign activity To organize supporter outreach and campaign operations To keep internal records related to campaign communications No sale or marketing sharing of your information Personal information submitted through this website is not sold and is not shared with third parties, affiliates, or outside organizations for their own marketing or promotional purposes.
Limited service-provider use The website uses third-party service providers only to operate the site and deliver campaign communications, including website hosting, form delivery, and donation processing.
These providers may process information only to provide those services to the campaign and not for their own marketing use.
SMS privacy and non-sharing Mobile information, mobile numbers, and SMS consent or opt-in data will not be shared, sold, rented, or transferred to third parties, affiliates, or lead generators for marketing or promotional purposes.
How SMS consent is collected Website visitors may provide a mobile number and separately choose whether to check the campaign's SMS consent box.
That consent choice is optional, not preselected, and intended to be separate from the general act of contacting or volunteering with the campaign.
Who may access information inside the campaign The campaign does not publish your personal contact information on the site.
Information may be accessed only by campaign staff, volunteers, consultants, or service providers who need it for campaign operations, compliance, communications, or technical support.
Information may also be disclosed if required by law.
External links This site links to outside websites, including social media platforms, official government resources, and donation pages.
Once you leave this site, those services may apply their own privacy policies and terms.
Data retention Campaign contact information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for campaign operations, follow-up, recordkeeping, or legal compliance, unless the campaign determines it should be removed sooner.
Your choices You may choose not to submit a website form.
You may choose not to opt in to text messaging and still use the website or contact the campaign.
You may request that the campaign update or delete information you provided.
You may stop using external services linked from this site at any time.
Privacy requests and contact To ask about information you submitted through this website, or to request an update or deletion, contact the campaign at info@caylinyoung.com or call (443) 333-9809 .
Official legislative office matters should continue to go to caylin.young@house.maryland.gov .
