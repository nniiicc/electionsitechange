The People's Candidate
Robert Williams was born and raised in a small farm community where he was taught to break bread and pray over a kitchen table.
As a military veteran, he is not afraid to serve and protect the principles of our country, and as a proud father and husband, he understands the balance and needs of working class families.
A southern raised Democrat, he understands that more must be done to bring the voice back to the people, not the big businesses and special interest groups.
He is committed to passing policies that increase opportunity for all people, not just for those at the top.
