Rep.
Frank Iler named interim co-chair of Joint Legislative Transportation Oversight Committee
Speaker Tim Moore recently announced interim committee assignments, and appointed Brunswick County’s Rep.
Frank Iler (R-Brunswick) as co-chair of the Joint Legislative Transportation Oversight Committee.
“Transportation has been one of my top priorities throughout my time in the State House, and I am grateful that Speaker Moore has again appointed me to a leadership role on a key transportation committee,” said Rep.
Iler.
“Transportation is critical to our quality of life, public safety, and economic growth, and I look forward to continuing my work in this area.”
