Under conservative leadership, we’ve cut taxes and worked to make North Carolina the most business-friendly and family-friendly state in the U.S.
We’ve also implemented Voter ID to make our elections more secure.
While we’ve accomplished a great deal, we must remain vigilant.
We still have work to do, and that’s why I respectfully ask for your support and your vote in the 2024 election.
