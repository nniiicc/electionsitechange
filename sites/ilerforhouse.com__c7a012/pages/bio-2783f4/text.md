Skip to content Common Sense - Competent - Conservative Home About Frank Iler Accomplishments Issues News Legislative Updates Donate Contact About Frank Iler Frank Iler came to North Carolina at the age of fourteen.
He is an Eagle Scout, as are his son and three of his grandsons.
Frank graduated from Greensboro Senior High School (now Grimsley) and went on to earn a degree in business from Campbell University.
He was actively involved in the North Carolina Jaycees in the 1970s and served on the Brunswick Community College Board of Trustees for 18 years, from 2007 to 2025, and was awarded recognition as a Trustee Emeritus.
His professional experience includes working as a cost accountant and budget supervisor in the textile industry, owning a fast food franchise and several sales and marketing positions.
Frank has served as Chairman of the Brunswick County Republican Party, Chairman of the 7th Congressional District Republican Party and as a precinct chairman.
Frank currently lives near Calabash.
He formerly lived in Oak Island for 19 years and was a member of Southport Baptist Church, where he has served as a Sunday School teacher and a Deacon.
Frank chairs, co-chairs or sits on a number of powerful legislative committees, including: Chair, N.C.
House Transportation Committee Chair, N.C.
House Transportation Appropriations Committee Vice Chair, N.C.
House Appropriations Committee Chair, N.C.
House Select Committee on N.C.’s Transportation Future Member, Joint Legislative Transportation Oversight Committee Member, N.C.
