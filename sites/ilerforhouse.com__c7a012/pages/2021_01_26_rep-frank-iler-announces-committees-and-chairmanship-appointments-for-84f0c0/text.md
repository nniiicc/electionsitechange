Frank Iler announces committees and chairmanship appointments for upcoming legislative session By Frank Iler | January 26, 2021 N.C.
House Speaker Tim Moore recently appointed Representative Frank Iler of Brunswick County to the following House standing committees and chairmanships for the 2021-22 session of the NC General Assembly: Chair – Transportation Committee Chair – Appropriations/Transportation Committee Vice Chair – Appropriations Committee Vice Chair – Environment Committee Member Commerce Education K – 12 Election Law and Campaign Finance Reform Marine Resources and Aquaculture “I am excited to serve on these committees that are so important to the people of our coastal area and across the state.
Transportation, in particular, deals not only with highways, but also with our ports, ferries, airports, rail, and all facets of passenger and freight transportation.
Election law, Education, and Environment are critical at this time.
Marine Resources is a newly revived committee and should be impactful for our coast.
I thank the Speaker for honoring my committee assignment requests.” Posted in Press Releases & Statements © # Frank Iler for N.C.
