Earl L.
Harris, Jr.
Indiana State Representative
House District 2
Represents Indiana House District 2.
He is the Assistant Democratic Floor Leader.
Serves on the following legislative committees: Ways and Means, Roads and Transportation, and Government and Regulatory Reform.
Founder and owner of Motivation Media, a television and video production company.
Vice-Chair of the Indiana Black Legislative Caucus and Vice-Chair of the Board of Latino Legislative Leaders.
Son of former Indiana State Rep.
Donna Harris and the late Indiana State Representative Earl Harris Sr.
