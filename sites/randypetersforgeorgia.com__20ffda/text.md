If you ever have any questions don't hesitate to call me on my cell at 770-241-6286 Randy Peters About Randy Meet Randy Peters Randy Peters is a businessman, job creator, and devoted family man who believes deeply in the promise of the Georgia Dream.
Adopted at age 13 and raised by a single mother in Georgia, Randy learned early the values of hard work, faith, and perseverance.
He attended Georgia College & State University — the very institution that anchors House District 149 — where he served as president of his fraternity and graduated with a degree in business.
Randy later founded a clinical laboratory headquartered in Milledgeville that grew to employ more than 100 people across the Southeast, creating good-paying jobs and strengthening Middle Georgia's economy.
Randy and his wife are the proud parents of three daughters, who attend John Milledge Academy.
Their family is actively involved in Northridge Christian Church in Milledgeville, and Randy has served on several civic and community boards throughout the region.
After building his business and contributing to the local economy for years, Randy is transitioning away from day-to-day operations and dedicating his time and energy to public service.
