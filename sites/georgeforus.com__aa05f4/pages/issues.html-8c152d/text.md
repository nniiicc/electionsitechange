It promotes an opportunity mindset , ownership culture , and reduces dependency .
Today we face dysfunctional and toxic politics.
Smart and innovative energy at the state level are our path forward.
In Montana we passed the largest tax cuts in state history, cut red tape/regulations, paid down all state debts, de-regulated the housing industry, and created a Homestead Exemption that lowered property taxes for 94% of Great Falls residents.
As someone who has operated and grown a business, employed Montanans, and managed budgets, I know that the first step to economic success is understanding the difference between investments and irresponsible spending.
As your representative I will always listen to you.
I will work tirelessly to keep your taxes low and spend taxpayer money efficiently.
I will identify and cut through red tape and waste , support trade-school and apprenticeship expansion, and follow an "all of the above" energy policy that unleashes Montana's natural resource economy . ​ PROPERTY TAX REFORM "I appreciate Representative Nikolakakos' partnership in securing tax relief for hard-working Montanans." - Governor Greg Gianforte ​Meaningful property tax reform is the most important issue facing the next legislature.
We must focus on rebuilding our shrinking industrial tax base but in the meanwhile can move on improving the Homestead Exemption we passed for primary residences and boost property tax reducing “circuit breakers” for seniors and others.
I am proud to have passed millions in property tax relief legislation and to have worked to establish a Homestead exemption that lowered property taxes for 94% of Great Falls residents.
There is still more work to do.
MILITARY AND VETERANS ISSUES Montana is at the top of the national list when it comes to the number who have answered the call to serve.
A twenty year Air Force and Montana Air National Guard veteran with deep experience in both Great Falls military missions I will be an unwavering advocate for our service members and vets.
The incoming Ground Based Strategic Deterrent upgrade project offers our area a fantastic opportunity.
No one will work harder to protect and expand our local military missions and keep our promises to our veterans.
Central Montana is underserved in it's need for a veterans home and the need for mental health care is at crisis levels.
With an ever expanding mission set including firefighting, pandemic response, law enforcement assistance, and overseas efforts etc. we are asking more of our Montana service members than ever.
At a time of recruiting and retention struggles we should bolster state education and other benefits for our National Guard.
We should also join other states in the favorable tax treatment of military retirees.
Montana is a state with deep military tradition and roots.
We need more veterans who have walked the walk serving in our government to promote our causes.
PUBLIC SAFETY Safe communities are critical to a strong economy and high quality of life.
As many localities around the country are learning, failure to robustly support law enforcement and enforce quality of life standards quickly leads to increased crime and a downward spiral.
As your representative I will work closely with law enforcement leaders across the state to make sure they have all the resources, tools, and training they need to fully execute their selfless mission. ​ OUR LIBERTIES, 2A, & PUBLIC LANDS​ Our individual liberties (including the second amendment) are more than issues, they are a way of life for Montanans and a central part of our cultural heritage and tradition.
I was proud to sponsor and pass legislation protecting our 2A.
Montana hunters, sportsmen, and anglers are the backbone of the American model for conservation and create a billion dollar economy for our state in the process.
I will always promote access to public lands.
I believe our way of life is unique and god given and that elected officials have an obligation to preserve it for the next generation.
