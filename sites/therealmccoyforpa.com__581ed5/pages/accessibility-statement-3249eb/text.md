If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our site or have any accessibility-related feedback, please contact us. ​ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT This statement reflects our ongoing efforts to make TheRealMcCoyforPA.com accessible to all individuals.
We understand the importance of ensuring that our website is usable and accessible to everyone.
What web accessibility means to us Web accessibility means providing equal access to people with diverse abilities.
At TheRealMcCoyforPA.com, we strive to ensure that all visitors, regardless of their abilities, can navigate and interact with our website effectively.
Our commitment to accessibility We have taken steps to align TheRealMcCoyforPA.com with WCAG [2.0 / 2.1 / 2.2] guidelines.
Our efforts include implementing features to enhance accessibility, such as: Utilizing accessible design elements and user interface components Providing alternative text for images to support screen readers Ensuring keyboard navigation and focus indicators for improved navigation Optimizing color contrast for better readability Offering text transcripts for multimedia content Minimizing the use of content that may cause seizures or physical reactions Making forms and interactive elements accessible Providing a clear and consistent website structure Our commitment extends to evaluating and addressing the accessibility of third-party content and services that may affect the overall user experience on TheRealMcCoyforPA.com.
As part of our commitment, we declare partial compliance with the standard due to potential accessibility limitations associated with third-party content.
