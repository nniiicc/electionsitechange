Proven Leadership.
Allen Daggett is a native of Kennebunkport who understands the value of hard work.
He started lobstering at the age of 12 and has spent his life building a strong foundation in our local economy.
Today, Allen owns and operates three successful local seafood businesses: Cape Porpoise Lobster Company, Cape Pier Chowder House, and Cape Porpoise Bait Company .
He knows firsthand what it takes to make payroll, deal with state regulations, and grow a business in Maine.
Public service has always been a priority.
Allen served as a Town Selectman for 20 years , bringing common sense and fiscal responsibility to local government.
Now, he's ready to take that same no-nonsense approach to Augusta.
