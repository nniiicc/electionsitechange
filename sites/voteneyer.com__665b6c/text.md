Jerry Neyer was born and raised in Isabella County, where he developed deep roots in the community.
He spent over 30 years working his family's 150-cow dairy farm alongside his dad and brother.
Additionally, Jerry dedicated more than 20 years to serving on the Isabella County Farm Bureau, the Michigan Dairy Marketing Committee, and the Isabella County Planning Commission.
In 2022, Jerry was elected as a State Representative.
He wasn't interested in an easy job—his experience as a farmer made him no stranger to hard work.
What motivated Jerry was his commitment to doing the right thing for the people of his community.
The 92nd State House district includes all of Isabella County and the townships of Seville, Pine River, Bethany, Emerson, and Arcada as well as the cities of Alma, St.
Louis, and Ithaca in Gratiot County.
