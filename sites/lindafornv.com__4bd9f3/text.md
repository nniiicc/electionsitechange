About Linda’s Wins: Championing solutions through state policy AB220 authorizes Nevada Department of Health and Human Services to issue temporary identification cards to Nevadans experiencing homelessness to access critical resources and services.
AB417 strengthens enforcement against illegal street racing and stunt driving by requiring vehicle inspections, DMV reporting, and registration cancellation for unsafe or noncompliant vehicles until issues are corrected.
AB361 improves transit safety and increases protections for public transit operators by requiring large counties and Regional Transportation Commissions to install protective infrastructure, safety technology, and public signage.
