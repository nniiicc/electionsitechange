About Issues Media Volunteer Donate Media gallery Assemblymember Linda Hunt proudly represents Nevada’s 17th District, bringing decades of experience advocating for working families as a longtime Culinary Workers Union Local 226 leader and food server at the El Cortez Hotel and Casino.
Elected in 2024, she serves on the Education, Government Affairs, and Health and Human Services committees.
Hunt focuses on practical solutions rooted in community.
Whether she’s championing issues of public safety to access to services, Linda leads with fairness, integrity, and a deep commitment to the people she serves.
