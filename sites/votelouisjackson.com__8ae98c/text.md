Louis Jackson is running to make Oklahoma more affordable, keep our communities safe, improve our schools, and make sure Lawton families have a strong voice at the State Capitol.
MAKE OKLAHOMA MORE AFFORDABLE Oklahoma families are feeling the pressure of rising costs.
Louis will fight to lower property taxes, reduce homeowners insurance costs, keep utility bills down, restrain government spending, and make it easier for working families to get ahead.
KEEP OUR COMMUNITIES SAFE Strong communities start with safe neighborhoods.
Louis will stand with law enforcement, hold criminals accountable, protect victims, and support tough, common-sense policies that keep our children, families, and communities safe.
BUILD BETTER SCHOOLS Every Oklahoma child deserves a great education.
Louis will support teachers and local schools, focus on improving student outcomes, expand choices for parents, and demand accountability for results.
LOWER HEALTHCARE COSTS Healthcare and insurance cost too much for too many Oklahoma families.
Louis will support policies that lower costs, make health insurance more affordable, and expand access to quality healthcare — especially in communities like Lawton.
PROTECT OKLAHOMANS FROM AI & BIG TECH New technology brings new opportunities — but it also requires common sense and accountability.
Louis will support safeguards that protect Oklahomans from the dangers of AI, preserve local control over where massive data centers are built, and make sure Big Tech pays its own way for the infrastructure and utilities its growth requires.
KEEP ENERGY AFFORDABLE & RELIABLE Louis has firsthand experience working on energy policy at the Oklahoma Corporation Commission.
He will fight for affordable, reliable energy, protect Oklahoma’s energy independence, and oppose policies that unnecessarily increase utility costs for families and small businesses.
DEFEND OUR OKLAHOMA VALUES Louis is pro-life, pro-Second Amendment, and believes faith, family, freedom, and personal responsibility matter.
At the Capitol, he’ll defend our constitutional freedoms and stand up for the values that make Oklahoma strong.
