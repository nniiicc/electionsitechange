Modoc County Superintendent Mike Martin Endorses Heather Hadwick for State Assembly
Modoc – Modoc County Superintendent Mike Martin endorses Republican Heather Hadwick for CA State Assembly District 1.
Superintendent Martin joins a growing list of elected and former officials from Northern California endorsing Hadwick’s campaign for Assembly.
Superintendent Mike Martin began his education career as a math teacher in 1992.
He moved to Modoc County in 1994, serving as a teacher, Assistant Principal, Principal, and Deputy Superintendent.
As Superintendent, Mike Martin works with dedicated educators, students, and families to bring a quality education to our Modoc County schools.
Heather Hadwick released the following statement regarding the endorsement.
“Superintendent Mike Martin is a champion of student education in Northern California.
I am thankful to have earned his endorsement for my campaign to the State Assembly.
Right now, student learning is one of California schools’ biggest challenges statewide.
I look forward to working alongside Superintendent Martin to ensure we bring all students a high-quality education that prepares them for life.”
To learn more about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com.
