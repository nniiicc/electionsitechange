Siskiyou County Supervisor Michael Kobseff Endorses Heather Hadwick for State Assembly Oct 5, 2023 1 min read Modoc County – Today, California Assembly District 1 candidate Heather Hadwick announces the endorsement of Siskiyou County Supervisor Michael Kobseff.
Supervisor Michael Kobseff has been a resident of Siskiyou County for 54 years.
For 30 years, Michael and his wife, Lynn, owned and operated a local restaurant in Mount Shasta.
Supervisor Kobseff is a high-profile community leader and elected official in Siskiyou County, first being elected to serve in 2006.
“Supervisor Michael Kobseff is a small business owner and community leader with deep ties to Northern California,” said Heather Hadwick.
“Supervisor Kobseff understands the challenges of operating a small business and raising a family in California.
I am grateful he has endorsed our campaign for State Assembly and shares our vision for a less Sacramento-regulated, more affordable Northern California.” Heather Hadwick’s campaign for State Assembly is focused on lowering unaffordable prices for California families, keeping our neighborhoods safe from rising crime, and protecting our communities from devastating wildfires.
With the backing of Supervisor Michael Kobseff, Hadwick’s momentum continues to grow, garnering support from community leaders who recognize her mission to bring a stronger voice for Northern California to Sacramento.
To learn more about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com .
