Assemblymember Megan Dahle Endorses District 1 Candidate Heather Hadwick
Updated: Jan 22, 2024
Modoc County, CA – California Assemblymember Megan Dahle endorses Republican candidate Heather Hadwick for Assembly District 1.
Megan Dahle has represented Northern California in the State Assembly since 2019.
She joins CA State Senator and '22 Republican Gubernatorial nominee Brian Dahle in endorsing Heather's campaign.
“Heather’s extensive background in public safety will enhance our rural communities and protect our way of life in the North State.
She knows the soft-on-crime policies from the state, directly impact the local level and have real public safety implications.
Heather isn't afraid to work hard.
She will get the job done, and I’m honored to give her my endorsement,” said Megan Dahle.
Heather Hadwick released the following statement regarding the endorsement.
“Megan is a mother of three, a small business owner, and a farmer.
She is committed to North State families, fighting to lower the cost of living, protecting our streets, and investing in wildfire prevention.
I am thankful she has endorsed our campaign and shares our vision of bringing a strong Northern California voice to Sacramento.”
Heather Hadwick’s campaign for State Assembly is focused on lowering unaffordable prices for California families, keeping our neighborhoods safe from rising crime, and protecting our communities from devastating wildfires.
With the backing of Brian and Megan Dahle, Hadwick’s momentum continues to grow, garnering support from community leaders districtwide.
To learn more about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com.
