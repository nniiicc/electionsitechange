Heather Hadwick Earns Endorsement from California Professional Firefighters Nov 8, 2023 1 min read Updated: Jan 22, 2024 Modoc – Heather Hadwick, candidate for Assembly District 1, proudly announces an endorsement from the California Professional Firefighters (CPF), the largest statewide organization dedicated to serving the needs of full-time firefighters with 35,000 active members.
Heather Hadwick, a former Deputy OES Director, has worked alongside police and fire when managing natural disasters affecting the North State.
Heather’s first-hand experience has given her a deep understanding of the unique challenges facing first responders.
The support from the CPF and its 35,000 members behind Heather’s campaign stands as a testament to her commitment to securing full funding for public safety, ensuring that firefighters have the necessary resources to keep us safe, and investing in proactive wildfire prevention.
Heather Hadwick expressed her gratitude for the endorsement, saying, “I am thankful to have earned an endorsement from the California Professional Firefighters.
Their brave members are the true heroes, on call for emergencies and deployed to combat California’s most dangerous wildfires.
In the State Assembly, I will work tirelessly to ensure that our firefighters, police officers, and all law enforcement members have the resources they need to protect our communities and keep us safe.” The California Professional Firefighters add their name to a growing list of public safety leaders and organizations supporting Heather Hadwick’s campaign.
As she continues to earn endorsements and gain momentum, Heather is establishing herself as the candidate strongest to stand up for and protect North State values in Sacramento.
For more information about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com .
