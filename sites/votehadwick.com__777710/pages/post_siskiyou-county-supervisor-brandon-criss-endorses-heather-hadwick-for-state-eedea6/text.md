Siskiyou County Supervisor Brandon Criss Endorses Heather Hadwick for State Assembly
Updated: Jan 22, 2024
Modoc County, CA – Today, California State Assembly candidate Heather Hadwick announces an endorsement from Siskiyou County Supervisor Brandon Criss.
Criss is a fourth-generation Siskiyou County farmer who assumed office as a Modoc Supervisor in 2013.
Supervisor Criss has also served as a volunteer firefighter and Emergency Medical Responder.
“I am thankful to have earned an endorsement from Supervisor Criss,” said Heather.
“Supervisor Brandon Criss is a farmer and local community leader who knows what it takes to raise a family and run a business in Northern California.
I am grateful that he has backed our campaign and supported our vision to protect the way of life for Northern Californians.”
Heather Hadwick’s campaign for the State Assembly is focused on lowering the price of living, keeping our neighborhoods safe from rising crime, and protecting our communities from devastating wildfires.
With the endorsement of Supervisor Brandon Criss, Hadwick’s momentum continues to grow, garnering support from community leaders who recognize her mission to bring a bigger voice for rural California to Sacramento.
For more information about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com.
