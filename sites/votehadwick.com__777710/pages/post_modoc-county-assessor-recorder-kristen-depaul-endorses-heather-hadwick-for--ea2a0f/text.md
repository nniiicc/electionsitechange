Modoc County Assessor-Recorder Kristen DePaul Endorses Heather Hadwick for State Assembly
Modoc – Today, Modoc County Assessor-Recorder Kristen DePaul endorsed Republican CA State Assembly District 1 candidate Heather Hadwick.
Heather Hadwick released the following statement regarding the endorsement.
“I am grateful Modoc County Assessor-Recorder Kristen DePaul endorsed our State Assembly campaign.
Elected officials, community leaders, and business owners continue to support our campaign because they know I will bring a North State voice to Sacramento.
California families are struggling with the increasing prices of gas, groceries, and everyday items.
I am running to the State Assembly to lower the cost of living, make prices affordable, and protect the safety of our neighborhoods.”
Heather Hadwick lives in Alturas, CA, with her husband Bryon, where they run their family farm.
As a small business owner and farmer, Heather understands how government regulations, tax hikes, and radical policies increase the burden on middle-class families.
Heather’s campaign continues to garner support districtwide, with a vision of bringing a stronger North State voice to Sacramento and protecting the California Dream for hard-working families.
To learn more about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com.
