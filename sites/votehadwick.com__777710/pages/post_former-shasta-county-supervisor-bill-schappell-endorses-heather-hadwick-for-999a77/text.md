Former Shasta County Supervisor Bill Schappell Endorses Heather Hadwick for Assembly
Modoc – Today, Assembly District 1 candidate Heather Hadwick proudly reports the endorsement of Former Shasta County Supervisor Bill Schappell.
Bill Schappell is a former Lake Shasta marina owner, former Supervisor, and 45-year Shasta County resident.
Bill Schappell, a well-respected leader in Shasta County, has given his full support to Heather Hadwick, recognizing her commitment to serving the residents of the North State.
Heather responded to the endorsement, "I am thankful to receive an endorsement from Supervisor Bill Schappell.
Bill Schappell is a Shasta County leader who took on the community's biggest challenges to pave a brighter future for North State families.
I plan to serve the North State with the same commitment and dedication towards helping middle-class families get ahead."
Former Supervisor Schappell joins a growing list of elected officials and community leaders backing Heather's bid for State Assembly.
Heather's campaign continues to gain momentum as local leaders across Assembly District 1 join her campaign to represent the North State in Sacramento.
For more information about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com.
