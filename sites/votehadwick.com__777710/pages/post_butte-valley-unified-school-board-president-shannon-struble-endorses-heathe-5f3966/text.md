Butte Valley Unified School Board President Shannon Struble Endorses Heather Hadwick
Updated: Jan 22, 2024
Modoc County, CA – Today, Heather Hadwick, Republican candidate for California State Assembly District 1, announces an endorsement from Shannon Struble, President of the Butte Valley Unified School Board.
Struble’s endorsement underscores the growing support of Hadwick’s campaign and her commitment to ensuring students of the North State receive an education that prepares them for life.
“I am thankful to receive an endorsement from Butte Valley School Board President Shannon Struble,” said Heather.
“I commend Shannon’s dedication to improving our local schools and share his mission for ensuring a high-quality education for North State students.
I look forward to working with him to address the challenges facing California schools and building towards a brighter future for all students.”
Assembly District 1 candidate Heather Hadwick is a business owner, farmer, and former teacher running to bring a strong voice to Sacramento for the North State.
As a former teacher and charter school board member, Heather understands the challenges facing California schools and students’ education.
Heather is determined to bring California’s educational resources to rural communities across the state.
For more information about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com.
