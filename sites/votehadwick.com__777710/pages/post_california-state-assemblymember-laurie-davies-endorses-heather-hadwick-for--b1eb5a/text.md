California State Assemblymember Laurie Davies Endorses Heather Hadwick for AD01 Jan 19, 2024 1 min read Modoc - Today, Heather Hadwick announces an endorsement from California State Assemblymember Laurie Davies.
Laurie Davies, a small business owner and former Mayor of Laguna Niguel, was first elected to the State Assembly in 2020 and was re-elected in 2022.
Following the endorsement, Laurie Davies stated, “Heather Hadwick is the embodiment of boots on the ground leadership, bringing a wealth of expertise in emergency management and education to the forefront.
Her commitment to the well-being of our communities is unwavering.
Heather has a deep understanding of her district and a genuine passion for rural California.
I am proud to endorse Heather Hadwick and look forward to working with her.” “I am a business owner, emergency manager, teacher, and mother,” said Heather.
“I know what it takes to run a farm and raise a family in California.
I have the real-world experience we need to fight for working people and our rural communities.
I am thankful Laurie Davies has endorsed my candidacy and supports my mission of being a strong voice for the North State in Sacramento.” Heather’s campaign gains momentum as Laurie Davies adds her name to a growing list of supporters, which include State Senator Brian Dahle, Assemblywoman Megan Dahle, the California Professional Firefighters, and many others.
For more information about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com .
