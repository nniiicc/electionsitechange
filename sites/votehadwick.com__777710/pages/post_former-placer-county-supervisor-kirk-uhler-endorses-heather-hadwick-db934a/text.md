Former Placer County Supervisor Kirk Uhler Endorses Heather Hadwick Nov 2, 2023 1 min read Modoc County, CA – Today, Republican candidate Heather Hadwick announces an endorsement from former Placer County Supervisor Kirk Uhler.
Kirk Uhler became the second youngest supervisor in California history when he was elected at 25 years old in 1992.
Uhler served on the Placer County Board of Supervisors for 18 years until his retirement on January 4th, 2021.
Kirk Uhler is a local leader who has been a resident of Placer County for over 50 years.
“Supervisor Uhler has been incredibly important to a thriving Placer County,” said Heather Hadwick.
“As a supervisor, he prioritized fiscal responsibility, economic development, and constantly supported small businesses.
I am thankful Supervisor Uhler has backed our campaign and my commitment to creating a thriving economy, promoting small businesses, and making costs more affordable for the North State.” Former Placer County Supervisor Kirk Uhler’s endorsement is a testament to Heather’s vision of fighting for middle-class families in the North State.
Heather Hadwick is a farmer and small business owner who has seen how inflation, government regulations, and rising costs make it exceedingly difficult to run a business.
As a candidate for State Assembly, Heather is determined to cut costs for Californians, making everyday expenses more affordable.
For more information about Heather Hadwick and her campaign, please visit www.VoteHadwick.com .
