Education

Business Career
Worked in the family steel business, Soule Steel in San Francisco and Los Angeles.
In response to the Great 1906 San Francisco Earthquake, the company was founded in 1911 by my grandfather, Edward Lee Soule, Sr. a pioneer in the reinforcing steel business.
Over the years, I held positions in:
- Commercial Property Management of the 10 acres in SF that included office and warehouse space.
- Worked in accounting and finance.
- Hands on experience in the Architectural Products Division - fabrication and installation of Aluminum Curtainwall projects.
Next time you are in Campell, check out our work on the Pruneyard Towers!
Civic Engagement
Served on the San Jose Civil Service Commission from 2007 to 2011.
Republican Volunteer
