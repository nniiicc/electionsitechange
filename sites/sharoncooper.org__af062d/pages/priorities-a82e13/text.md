As a leader and faithful representative of Cobb County throughout her time in office, Sharon Cooper has demonstrated a true appreciation for the people in her district and a true passion for making a difference in the lives of Georgians.
She understands the responsibility of our state government and has worked tirelessly to build relationships and get results.
Known for being thorough in her research, Sharon approaches her position as an educator.
She is dedicated to learning everything she can to make the best possible decisions, and continues to be an asset to our district and to other leaders throughout our state legislature.
Conservative Values: Securing the Foundation of Georgia’s Success
Sharon understands that our shared conservative values are what aligns the hearts and goals of citizens across this district and our state.
The liberal agenda is pushing too much, too fast, without any regard for the taxpayer’s money or long-term impact on society.
Sharon believes that making people dependent on our government is not the answer and that smaller government is set up to help people help themselves and then get out of the way.
This is the foundation of our conservative agenda and this is where Sharon continues to stand up and make a positive impact on people’s lives.
In order to secure Georgia’s long-term success, Sharon will continue to be the conservative voice we can trust.
Healthcare: Fighting for Quality Patient Care
Sharon is passionate about taking care of people – it is what she does.
With a Masters in Nursing, Sharon worked her whole adult life in the healthcare field as a nurse and training others in the profession.
As Chairman of the Health & Human Services committee, Sharon’s approach to healthcare policy mirrors her approach as a healthcare provider.
In every circumstance, she evaluates what a policy will do for providing and fostering quality patient care.
She will continue her fight to represent and speak up in a way that protects the doctor-patient relationship and promotes proper care.
Education: Supporting Lifelong Learning Initiatives
Sharon believes that education is the bedrock of our society.
In her early career, Sharon leveraged her Masters in Education to teach disadvantaged learners.
This provides her with a unique perspective as she pursues resources that support our K-12 students, teachers and a local school system that we can continue to be proud of.
Cobb County is blessed with a great school system, and Sharon supports school choice giving parents a voice to determine the best educational solution for the unique needs of their child.
Sharon also recognizes that education throughout life is what it takes to keep up with the way the world is moving and changing.
She will continue to champion technical education and junior college programs that provide more options for our young adults and expand horizons for our future generations.
Economy: Creating Job Opportunities for Quality of Life
When our citizens are employed and successfully prepared for employment, that creates hope, self-worth and changes the trajectory of people’s lives.
We must continue to champion the things that make Georgia the #1 place to do business:
- Promoting less taxation
- Reducing regulations that create unnecessary burdens for employers
- Supporting agriculture as the #1 industry in our state and protecting local farmers
- Enhancing technical college programs that help employees meet the requirements of emerging changes intheir jobs
