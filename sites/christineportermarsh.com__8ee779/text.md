ABOUT
CHRISTINE
I am proud of my 32 years in the classroom.
As an English teacher, I viewed my classroom as a place to provide skills and knowledge to my students that would equip them to make the world a better place.
In 2016, I was incredibly honored to be named Arizona’s Teacher of the Year.
Through that experience, I had the opportunity to travel across the state and see first-hand the issues that public schools are facing on a daily basis.
Now, I carry those lessons with me as I run for re-election to the State Senate.
In the Legislature, I've gotten straight to work on solving the problems which impact everyday Arizonans.
-Christine
