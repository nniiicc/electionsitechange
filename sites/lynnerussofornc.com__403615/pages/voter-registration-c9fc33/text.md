Take a moment to check your registration status.
It only takes a minute, and it ensures everything is correct before it’s time to vote.
Check Your Status Not Registered or Need to Update Your Information?
If you’re not registered—or if you’ve moved, changed your name, or haven’t voted in a while— you can register or update your information.
The sooner you take care of it, the better.
Deadlines can sneak up quickly, and getting this done now gives you peace of mind.
Update Your Registration Help Others Do the Same After you’ve checked your registration, please take a moment to check in with your family and friends.
A quick text or conversation can make all the difference.
When more of us participate, our communities are stronger and better represented.
