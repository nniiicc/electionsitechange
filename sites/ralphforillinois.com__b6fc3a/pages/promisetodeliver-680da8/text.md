RALPH BLACK’S Promise to Deliver
BRING COMMON SENSE BACK.
RALPH will fight to turn Illinois around.
- Immigration I am a strong believer in legal immigration.
The people who followed the law to come here deserve fairness and respect, not policies that reward those who don’t.
I will fight against reckless immigration policies that strain our schools, neighborhoods, and taxpayers.
- Property Tax Relief Illinois has some of the highest property taxes in the nation, forcing families and seniors to leave.
I will fight to lower property taxes, eliminate property taxes for seniors over a certain age, and stop Springfield politicians from raising taxes year after year.
Every mandate Springfield passes is another increase in our local property taxes.
- Bring Good Education Back Parents should have a voice in their children’s education.
I oppose one-size-fits-all curriculum mandates from Springfield and believe in more local control and parental involvement.
While Fred Crespo supported piling on new mandates, I will fight to bring quality education back to our schools.
- Bring Safety Back We are fortunate to have good local police, but they need our support.
I will stand with law enforcement to keep families safe and restore accountability.
- Bring Accountability Back Illinois has been defined by corruption and broken promises for too long.
I support:
- Term limits for state legislators.
- Fair maps to end gerrymandering.
- Voter ID to protect election integrity.
- Ending the culture of corruption left behind by Mike Madigan and his allies.
