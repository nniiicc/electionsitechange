Running to represent Chittenden County's 10th district for a third term
Legislative accomplishments and current efforts
For my first two terms, I served on the Government Operations and Military Affairs Committee that oversees a broad array of issues, from elections law, municipal charter changes, ethics for state and local governments, open meeting law, regulation of substances, state government structure, operations of the judiciary system, oversight of law enforcement policies, and others.
In serving in this capacity, I helped develop legislation that has expanded access to professions for folks in Vermont and make progress addressing our workforce challenges; worked with administration officials, organizations and municipalities to find better ways to get state money out the door to the places that put it to good use serving Vermonters; drafted resolutions to recognize the contributions of the Rotary organization throughout the state; brought a public health lens to policy-making; and elevated the concerns and needs of our city and our district regarding changes to land use law and education.
I think it is important to expand economic opportunities for Vermonters, to make it easier for Vermonters to enjoy healthy lives, and to increase safety and security.
In my second term, I especially focused on accountability, affordability, and clarity.
