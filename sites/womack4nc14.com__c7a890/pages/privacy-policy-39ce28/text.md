Privacy Policy
LaKesha Womack for Congress
Effective Date: December 5, 2025
LaKesha Womack for Congress (“the Campaign,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy.
This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information you share with us through our website, digital forms, emails, events, and other campaign-related platforms.
By using our website or providing your information, you agree to the terms in this Privacy Policy.
Information We Collect
We may collect the following types of information:
Information You Provide Directly
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Mailing address
- Donation information (processed securely through our payment vendor)
- Volunteer sign-up details
- Event registrations
- Messages or concerns you send through our contact forms
Automatically Collected Information
When you visit our website, we may automatically collect:
- IP address
- Browser type
- Device information
- Pages visited
- Cookies or tracking technologies for analytics and site functionality
We do not collect sensitive personal information unless you voluntarily provide it.
How We Use Your Information
We use your information to:
- Communicate campaign updates, news, and opportunities
- Respond to your messages and questions
- Process donations and send required contribution receipts
- Register you for events or volunteer activities
- Improve website performance and user experience
- Comply with FEC reporting requirements
- Build supporter, volunteer, and voter outreach lists
Your information helps us stay connected with you and grow a campaign rooted in community engagement.
Sharing of Information
We do not sell your personal information.
We may share limited information with:
- Trusted vendors (email platforms, payment processors, event tools)
- Volunteer management and outreach partners
- Digital or communications firms working with the Campaign
- Federal Election Commission (as required for donation reporting)
All partners are required to protect your information and use it only for campaign-related services.
Donation Information
All contributions are processed through secure third-party vendors.
The Campaign does not store full credit card numbers or bank details.
Required donor information (name, address, occupation, and employer) may be reported publicly to the Federal Election Commission, as required by federal law.
Cookies & Tracking
The website may use cookies and similar technologies to:
- Improve functionality
- Analyze traffic
- Personalize content
- Measure digital advertising
You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some features may not function correctly.
Email & Text Communications
By providing your email or phone number, you consent to receive campaign-related communications.
You can opt out at any time:
- Email: Click “unsubscribe” at the bottom of any message
- Text messages: Reply STOP to stop receiving texts
We never charge for SMS messages, but your carrier’s rates may apply.
Protecting Your Information
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data from loss, misuse, or unauthorized access.
Despite these efforts, no system is 100% secure; please share information at your discretion.
External Links
Our website may include links to third-party websites or platforms.
We are not responsible for their privacy practices or content.
Children’s Privacy
This website is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically.
The “Effective Date” will reflect the most recent version.
