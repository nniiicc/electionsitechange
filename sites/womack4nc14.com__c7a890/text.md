Meet LaKesha Womack
A New Vision for North Carolina’s 14th Congressional District
Listening to You, Learning from You, and Leading for You in Congress
LaKesha Womack is a strategic leader, community advocate, and lifelong champion for economic justice.
She is ready to bring bold, people-centered leadership to North Carolina’s 14th Congressional District.
With more than two decades of experience in financial services, small business development, and community empowerment, LaKesha has committed her life to expanding access, strengthening families, and creating real opportunities for the people who need them most.
As a consultant, minister, and Chief Strategy Officer of an emerging CDFI, she has worked directly with entrepreneurs, working families, and community organizations.
She listens to their stories, learns their challenges, and fights to help them thrive.
Her passion for service is rooted in faith, shaped by lived experience, and guided by a deep belief that our government should reflect the needs and potential of the people it serves.
LaKesha is running for Congress because North Carolina’s 14th District deserves a representative who shows up, pays attention, and leads with integrity.
She brings a proven record of turning vision into action, championing equitable policy, and supporting communities through both crisis and opportunity.
From protecting voting rights and affordable health care to expanding economic mobility and helping working families, LaKesha’s leadership will center the people of this District at every step.
This campaign is grounded in a simple but powerful promise: Listening to you.
Learning from you.
Leading for you in Congress.
Most candidates focus their campaign on their agenda.
But I believe authentic leadership requires listening.
I don’t want to simply present a list of priorities.
I want to work with you to build an agenda that reflects our shared values and the future we want for North Carolina’s 14th District.
I know what’s important to me: affordable health care for every family, real pathways to economic mobility, and access to equitable, high-quality education in every community.
But I also want to hear from you.
What issues matter most in your daily life?
What challenges are you facing?
What opportunities do you want to see created for your family, your business, and your neighborhood?
Your voice will shape this campaign.
Your concerns will guide my priorities.
Together, we can build a vision that truly represents the people, not the powerful, and deliver the kind of leadership that listens first, learns continually, and leads with integrity in Congress.
