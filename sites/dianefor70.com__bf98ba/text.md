Diane Boulai for GA House 70 A Vision for District 70: From My Life to Yours Diane Boulaì for Georgia House of Representatives District 70 A Thriving Community Built on Different Backgrounds and Common Goals DONATE About Diane Diane Boulaì (pronounced Bowl eye or Boo lay) brings a grounded, service‑driven, and community‑centered perspective to public life — shaped by a childhood rooted in both Caribbean heritage and American military values.
Born and raised in the U.S.
Virgin Islands, Diane also has deep family ties to Anguilla, St.
Lucia, and several other Caribbean islands.
She later spent part of her childhood on Fort Campbell, Kentucky, where her father served in the 101st Airborne Division.
Growing up in a military family taught her discipline, service, faith, and the importance of community — values that continue to guide her life.
Service runs deep in Diane’s family.
Many of her relatives have proudly served in the U.S. military, and others continue to work in essential roles as nurses, teachers, and police officers.
This legacy shaped her belief that strong communities are built when people step up for one another — a belief that guides her work today.
Diane has called Georgia home since 2000 and has lived in Coweta County’s District 70 since 2015.
As a mother and grandmother, she understands the hopes families carry for their children and the challenges they face in building a stable, healthy future.
Her life has not been without hardship.
Diane has faced significant health challenges and lives with a disability.
These experiences have strengthened her compassion, deepened her resilience, and shaped her commitment to public service.
She believes civic duty gives her purpose and is determined to use her voice to advocate for those who often feel unheard.
