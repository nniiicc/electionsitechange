A Bit About Me I am a husband, a father, and a concerned, engaged citizen.
My family has lived in Farmington for nearly 20 years.
My kids have grown up here.
We love Davis County and the amazing opportunities it provides for all of us.
My wife is an incredible healthcare provider in our community and my kids are active and engaged in their schools.
We are your typical busy family with too much on their plates trying to make it through the day.
Because I live and raise my family here, I care deeply about the future of our district.
Like many families, I’ve seen how rising costs, strained schools, and a broken healthcare system make everyday life harder than it should be.
I believe everyone who works hard deserves a fair shot, public schools should be strong and well-supported, healthcare should care for people instead of bankrupting them, work deserves dignity, and democracy works best when government is honest and accountable.
More than any label, I’m guided by a simple idea: we do better when we take care of each other .
