Meet Senator Keith Grover
From humble beginnings in Provo to serving in the Utah Senate, Keith Grover’s life has been defined by hard work, faith, and family.
Losing his mother as an infant, Keith learned early the value of perseverance and compassion—lessons that carried him through college, a career in education, and decades of community service.
Today, Keith and his family have built a life grounded in service, gratitude, and faith.
Whether in the classroom or the Senate, Keith believes in empowering people, not government, to make the greatest difference.

My Core Values
Utah’s greatest strength begins at home.
Strong families, faith, and freedom are the foundation of everything good in our state.
I believe in limited government, individual responsibility, and the idea that people—not programs—build strong communities.
Fiscal Responsibility
Keeping government small and living within our means to protect Utah’s future and strengthen our economy.
Personal Liberty
Protecting families, faith, and free enterprise from government overreach—because freedom fuels opportunity.
Accountability
Every taxpayer dollar belongs to the people—not the politicians.
Utahns deserve transparency and integrity in government.
Freedom, family, and faith—these are the principles that have made Utah the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
