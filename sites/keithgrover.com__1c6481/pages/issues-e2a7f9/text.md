Conservative Leadership Grounded in Utah Values
Keith believes Utah’s greatest strength begins at home, with strong families, strong faith, and the freedom to build a good life without government getting in the way.
His work in the Utah Senate is guided by a simple principle: government should protect freedom, live within its means, and remain accountable to the people it serves.
Keith believes strong communities are built by parents, neighbors, small business owners, teachers, and congregations.
Government’s role is to protect that space, keep taxes low, remain accountable, and keep decisions close to Utah families.
