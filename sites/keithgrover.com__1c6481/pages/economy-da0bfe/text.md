Conservative Policies That Deliver Results Keith believes Utah families and small businesses know how to spend their money better than government does.
That is why he supports lower taxes, responsible budgeting, and policies that encourage job creation, economic growth, and opportunity across Utah.
A strong economy does not happen by accident.
It requires disciplined leadership, limited government, and a commitment to protecting taxpayers while keeping Utah competitive.
KEITH'S PRIORITIES Building an Economy That Works for Utah Families Lower Taxes Continue reducing Utah's income tax burden so hardworking families can keep more of what they earn.
Increase Housing Opportunity Support policies that increase housing supply, reduce unnecessary barriers, and help more Utah families achieve homeownership.
Grow Utah's Economy Reduce unnecessary regulation so businesses can invest, expand, hire employees, and create better-paying jobs.
Responsible Government Keep state spending under control while focusing taxpayer dollars on Utah's most important priorities.
KEEPING MORE MONEY IN YOUR POCKET 4.45% Utah's State Income Tax Rate Utah reduced the state income tax rate to 4.45% , continuing a series of tax cuts that help families and businesses keep more of their own money.
THE UTAH WAY Opportunity Grows When Government Gets Out of the Way Utah's economic success is built on conservative principles: balanced budgets, low taxes, responsible regulation, and a strong workforce.
Keith will continue working to protect those principles so Utah remains a place where families can prosper, entrepreneurs can build, and businesses can grow.
Government should live within its means so Utah families can thrive.
Keith Grover Let's Keep Utah Working Together, we can keep taxes low, grow our economy, and protect the conservative principles that make Utah the best place to live, work, and raise a family.
