Leading on Education
Keith brings firsthand classroom experience to education policy at the Capitol.
Education Isn't Just An Issue.
It's Keith's Life's Work.
Before serving in the Utah Senate, Keith Grover dedicated his career to education.
He served as an administrator in the Alpine School District and earned a Doctorate in Education from the University of Utah.
That experience gives him a unique perspective on the challenges facing students, parents, and teachers.
Rather than imposing one-size-fits-all solutions from the Capitol, Keith believes education policy should empower classrooms, strengthen families, and prepare students for successful futures.
Keith's Priorities
Fund Classrooms
Support putting more education dollars directly into classrooms instead of administrative overhead.
Reduce Distractions
Support phone-free learning environments and clear technology standards that keep students focused.
Empower Parents
Protect parental rights and ensure transparency in what is being taught in Utah classrooms.
Prepare Students
Expand career and technical education so students graduate ready for college, apprenticeships, or the workforce.

A Lifetime in Education
Keith spent decades working with students, teachers, parents, and administrators before ever serving in elected office.
His education policy is rooted in real classroom experience, not political talking points.
