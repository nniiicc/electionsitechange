Public Safety Is a Core Responsibility of Government Every Utah family deserves to feel safe at home, at school, and in their community.

Keith believes Utah should support officers, protect victims, improve correctional capacity, and address substance abuse in ways that make communities safer.
KEITH'S PRIORITIES Protecting Families and Supporting Those Who Keep Us Safe Back Law Enforcement Ensure Utah's police officers, first responders, and public safety agencies have the resources and support they need to protect our communities.
Hold Criminals Accountable Support strong sentencing policies, better crime clearance rates, and a justice system focused on protecting victims and the public.
