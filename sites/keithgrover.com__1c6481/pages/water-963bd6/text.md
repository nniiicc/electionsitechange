Keith is working to protect it.
Working Where Utah's Water Decisions Are Made Keith Grover serves on Utah's State Water Development Commission, helping guide policies that will shape Utah's water future for generations.
From protecting the Great Salt Lake to strengthening water infrastructure and preserving Utah's water rights, Keith believes careful planning today will protect families, agriculture, and economic growth tomorrow.
KEITH'S PRIORITIES Protecting the Water Utah Depends On Protect Our Water Supply Invest in reliable water infrastructure that supports Utah families, communities, and continued growth.
Protect the Great Salt Lake Support practical solutions that improve the health of one of Utah's most important natural resources.
Protect Agriculture Ensure Utah's farmers and ranchers have the water resources they need to keep producing food and supporting rural communities.
Keep Decisions in Utah Defend Utah's authority to manage its own water and push back against unnecessary federal interference.
INVESTING IN UTAH'S FUTURE $66 Million+ Invested During the 2026 Legislative Session Utah invested more than $66 million in water infrastructure, Great Salt Lake restoration, dam safety, and projects that strengthen our state's long-term water security.
Utah's future depends on responsible stewardship of one of our most precious resources.
