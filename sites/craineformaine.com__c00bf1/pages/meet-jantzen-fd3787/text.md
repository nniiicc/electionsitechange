MEET JANTZEN
Husband.
Father.
Airman.
Candidate for Maine State Representative, District 7
I am a town councillor in Houlton, Maine, and a finance analyst with the United States Air Force through the Maine Air National Guard*.
Above all, I am a husband and a father, and that shapes how I approach leadership, service, and the decisions I make for our community.
I earned my Bachelor of Science in Business Management from Pensacola Christian College and am currently completing my MBA.
My background is in leadership, financial management, and operations, giving me a practical understanding of how to manage budgets wisely, operate efficiently, and be a responsible steward of what has been entrusted to me.
Serving on the Houlton Town Council has given me the opportunity to work directly on issues that affect our community every day.
I have focused on transparency, fiscal responsibility, and making sure local government works the way it should, clearly, honestly, and in the best interest of the people.
My service in the Maine Air National Guard continues to strengthen my commitment to disciplined leadership and public service.
It has reinforced the importance of responsibility, structure, and staying focused on the mission at hand.
My wife Anna and I are raising our five children, Mercy, Emily, Elijah, Micah, and Ezra, here in northern Maine.
Our family is actively involved in our church and community, and we care deeply about the kind of future we are building, not just for our own children, but for families across our district.
My priorities are simple and grounded: faith, family, and a commitment to strong communities built on personal responsibility, freedom, and truth.
