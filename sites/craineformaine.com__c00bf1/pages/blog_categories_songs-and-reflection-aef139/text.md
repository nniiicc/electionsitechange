Songs and Reflection
In the middle of all the noise, it’s important to pause—and remember what matters most.
Each week, I share a time of worship and reflection at the piano: a song to encourage your heart, and a short reminder of God’s truth for today.
Whether through familiar hymns, new worship songs, or an occasional musical moment, this space is about stepping back, refocusing, and finding strength beyond the headlines.
