DONATE MY VALUES I’m not running to play politics — I’m running to represent the values that built this country and continue to define our community here in Northern Maine.
Faith, family, and hard work aren’t just slogans; they’re the foundation of life for families, farmers, and small businesses across our district.
These are the principles that will guide every decision I make in Augusta, and they are the reason I’m asking for your support.
I BELIEVE IN...
Courage With Grace I believe politics should be principled, not poisoned.
We can disagree without demeaning.
I will always stand on truth—but I will never use it as a weapon.
We need more courage, yes—but we also need more grace.
That’s the kind of leadership I strive to live out daily.
Faith, Freedom, and the Constitution I believe our rights come from God—not government.
I stand for religious liberty, free speech, and the right to live out one’s faith without fear of censorship or punishment.
The Constitution is more than a document—it’s the foundation of a free society, and I believe in honoring it without compromise.
Dignity of Hard Work I’ve spent my life working real jobs with real people—often paycheck to paycheck.
I believe every working American deserves dignity, opportunity, and the freedom to provide for their family without government interference or elite condescension.
Hard work should be respected, not regulated to death.
Limited Government & Fiscal Responsibility Government should serve the people—not control them.
I believe in balanced budgets, common-sense spending, and an end to bloated bureaucracy.
Fiscal discipline isn’t just good policy—it’s moral responsibility to future generations.
Personal Responsibility and Self-Government I believe freedom only works when paired with personal responsibility.
A strong nation depends on individuals who take ownership of their choices, care for their families, contribute to their communities, and live with integrity—even when no one’s watching.
Government can never replace the moral compass of self-governed people.
Responsibility isn’t just a virtue—it’s the backbone of a free society.
Parental Rights and the Protection of Children Parents—not institutions—should have the primary voice in raising, educating, and protecting their children.
I believe in school choice, transparent curricula, and the right of families to raise their children according to their faith and values.
Children deserve truth, protection, and a childhood—not political agendas.
Secure Borders and the Rule of Law A sovereign nation must control its borders.
I believe in compassionate, lawful immigration—but also in enforcing existing laws and protecting American communities.
Justice and mercy both matter—but they require order first.
Energy Independence & Stewardship Maine families are paying too much to heat their homes and fuel their vehicles.
We need to lower costs by using America’s abundant energy resources—oil, natural gas, nuclear, and hydropower—while supporting responsible forestry and practical local solutions.
Mainers should never be dependent on foreign powers to keep the lights on.
Honest Elections and Voter Confidence Every Mainer deserves confidence that our elections are free, fair, and secure.
That means protecting the ballot, verifying voter rolls, enforcing the law, and ensuring transparency at every step.
In Augusta, I will work to strengthen election safeguards so that every legal vote counts, and the people of Maine can trust the results.
Law, Order, and Respect for Authority I believe in standing with law enforcement and restoring respect for those who protect our communities.
Accountability matters—but so does gratitude.
A safe society depends on both justice and order.
