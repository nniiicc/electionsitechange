Questions About Tiffany Byrne and LD 25
Straight answers about the candidate, the district, and the 2026 election.
Who is Tiffany Byrne?
Tiffany Byrne is a Democratic candidate for the Arizona House of Representatives in Legislative District 25 in 2026.
She is a 30-year Arizona resident and a career educator with more than 20 years as a high school chemistry teacher.
What office is Tiffany Byrne running for?
She is running for State Representative in the Arizona House of Representatives, representing Legislative District 25, on the 2026 ballot.
What party does Tiffany belong to
Tiffany is a Democratic candidate focused on practical, common-sense solutions that strengthen working families and create opportunities for people to thrive.
She believes in bringing our community together, finding common ground, and moving forward rather than deepening the divisions that pull us apart.
Does Tiffany get contributions from any special interest groups?
No, Tiffany is proud to be a Clean Elections candidate.
That means she had to get two hundred $5 donations from neighbors just like you, submit them to the Clean Elections Commission, and then they provided her with financing to run a campaign.
Clean Elections candidates cannot accept financial contributions from any special interests, corporations, or PACs.
Tiffany was purposeful in becoming a Clean Elections candidate, because she believes in fighting for the community, not corporations.
What areas does Arizona Legislative District 25 cover?
Legislative District 25 covers Arizona's West Valley from Bell Road and the Loop 303 south and west through Buckeye, Surprise, and Waddell in western Maricopa County, extending to Yuma and northern Yuma County.
What are Tiffany Byrne's main issues?
Education, economy and opportunity, water and environment, and accountable government.
What is Tiffany Byrne's background in education?
More than 20 years teaching high school chemistry, followed by a role successfully overseeing District wide K-12 science instruction.
She was recognized as Teacher of the Year in her district, and later served as an Assistant Principal.
When is the 2026 Arizona general election?
Election Day is November 3, 2026.
The voter registration deadline is October 5, 2026, and the last day to mail back a ballot is October 27, 2026.
Does Tiffany support local Public Safety officers?
Yes, Tiffany believes that all community members should feel safe in their neighborhoods, work places, and schools.
She respects the efforts of our neighbors who work as local police officers and school resource officers.
