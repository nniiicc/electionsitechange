Here is where she stands.
Education Economy & Opportunity Water & Environment Accountable Government Issue 01 Education Every Child Deserves a Great Neighborhood School Tiffany Byrne supports moving Arizona off the bottom of national education funding rankings, strengthening success in neighborhood public and public charter schools, and expanding workforce training and career pathways from pre-K through postsecondary.
Arizona's neighborhood public schools, and public charter schools, are the backbone of our communities.
But for too long our neighborhood schools have been underfunded, understaffed, and undervalued.
With three decades in Arizona classrooms, Tiffany Byrne knows exactly what students and educators need to succeed.
She will fight to move Arizona off the bottom of the nation in education funding and ensure every student has access to a high-quality education, from pre-K through postsecondary education.
She will also expand workforce training and career pathways so students can step confidently into good paying jobs.
Tiffany Byrne does not view parental choice and strong public schools as an either-or issue.
She supports strong public schools and the original intent of Arizona's ESA program, which was ensuring that students with special needs and unique learning challenges have access to educational opportunities that best meet their individual needs.
She supports parental choice in education, but believes the newly implemented universal ESA vouchers should include common-sense accountability and fiscal guardrails to ensure taxpayer dollars are used responsibly and educational outcomes remain the priority.
Strong schools build strong communities.
When we invest in education, we invest in Arizona’s economy, stronger neighborhoods, and real opportunity for every family.
Issue 02 Economy & Opportunity Building an Economy That Works for Everyone Tiffany Byrne supports workforce development and job training, a level playing field for small businesses, wages that keep pace with Arizona's cost of living, and expanded access across the housing continuum from rentals to ownership.
Legislative District 25 is home to hardworking families, small business owners, and a growing workforce.
Tiffany believes that economic opportunity shouldn't depend on who you know or where you were born, it should depend on your willingness to work hard and contribute to your community.
She supports policies that invest in workforce development and job training, level the playing field for small businesses, ensure fair wages that keep pace with Arizona’s rising cost of living, and attract responsible employers to our region.
She is also committed to expanding access to the housing continuum from rentals to ownership, so that young people can live, work, and thrive in the communities where they were raised if they choose to do so.
Economic growth that benefits everyone, not just those at the top, is both good policy and good values.
Tiffany will bring that vision to the Arizona Legislature.
Issue 03 Water & Environment Protecting Arizona's Most Precious Resources Tiffany Byrne supports science-based water management, investment in water infrastructure, sustainable development standards, and transparent oversight of water allocation to ensure we have water for years to come.
Water is Arizona's most critical issue, and in a district that stretches from the West Valley to Yuma, it couldn't be more relevant.
Tiffany supports science-based water management policies that protect our rivers, groundwater, and natural habitats for generations to come.
She believes in smart, responsible land use that balances growth with sustainability.
Arizona's rapid expansion cannot come at the cost of the natural systems that sustain us, and any policy that ignores the long-term health of our water supply is a policy that fails our children.
Tiffany will advocate for investment in water infrastructure, sustainable development standards, and transparent oversight of water allocation across Arizona.
Issue 04 Accountable Government Government You Can Trust Tiffany Byrne supports protecting voting rights, transparent and ethical conduct in government, and bipartisan cooperation where it serves the public interest.
Democracy only works when voters can trust that their voices matter and their votes are counted.
Tiffany is committed to protecting voting rights, ensuring transparent and ethical conduct at every level of government, and holding elected officials accountable to the people they serve.
She believes that good governance starts with civility, honesty, and a genuine commitment to finding common ground.
Arizona's challenges are too serious for partisan games, families in LD 25 deserve representatives who work across the aisle when it's in the public's best interest.
