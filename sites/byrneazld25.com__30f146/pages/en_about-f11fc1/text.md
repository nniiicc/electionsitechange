Tiffany Byrne Candidate, Arizona LD 25 Her Story Three Decades of Roots in Arizona Tiffany Byrne is a Democratic candidate focused on practical solutions for the Arizona House of Representatives in Legislative District 25 in the 2026 election.
A 30-year Arizona resident, she spent more than 20 years as a high school chemistry teacher before serving as a K-12 science education leader, and was named Teacher of the Year in her district.
Her campaign focuses on public education funding and success measures, economic opportunity, water and land-use policy, and government accountability.
Legislative District 25 covers Arizona's West Valley from Bell Road and the Loop 303 south and west through Buckeye, Surprise, and Waddell in western Maricopa County, extending to Yuma and northern Yuma County.
Tiffany Byrne has called Arizona home for more than 30 years, building her life in the community where she has lived, worked, and raised her children.
Throughout her career, she has invested in the people and neighborhoods that make the state great.
Over her three decades in Arizona, Tiffany worked in education, spending more than 20 years as a high school chemistry teacher before becoming an education leader, overseeing Kindergarten through 12th Grade Science.
Tiffany was recognized as Teacher of the Year in the district she served in, and later served as a state level leader.
She has dedicated her career to strengthening support for families, students, and educators.
Through this work, she saw firsthand how strong foundations for all create stability, opportunity, and growth for everyone.
These experiences shaped her belief in practical, common-sense leadership that puts people before politics.
She has seen what works and what does not.
She knows that the decisions made at the state legislature directly affect families, and she is ready to fight for them.
