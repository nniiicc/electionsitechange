A Proven Leader for Arizona's Future
Tiffany Byrne is running to be the Arizona State House Representative for Legislative District 25.
She has called Arizona home for more than 30 years, building her life in the same community where she has lived, worked, and raised her children.
After 20+ years as a classroom teacher and education leader, she knows what it takes to build strong foundations for families, students, and communities — and she's ready to bring that leadership to the Arizona State Legislature.
