A 1987 graduate of Nevada High School and a 1991 graduate of Westminster College in Fulton, Missouri, Mike returned home and built a career helping families and businesses invest in their communities through real estate.
Mike married Julia in 1998.
Together, they raised five children and built their life in Nevada.
Those experiences taught Mike that state government works best when it supports local communities instead of standing in their way.
If elected, Mike will work to strengthen public education by listening to parents, teachers, and local school leaders.
Mike will support efforts to expand housing opportunities by improving infrastructure and encouraging responsible growth throughout the district.
He will also fight to preserve access to quality healthcare so families can receive care close to home.
Mike believes the people of Missouri's 28th District deserve a representative who lives, works, and raised a family in rural Missouri.
Mike will take the values, concerns, and common sense of rural Missouri to Jefferson City and fight to make sure the district's voice is heard.
For nearly 30 years, Mike built and operated McCaffree Real Estate.
That experience taught him the challenges Missouri families, homeowners, and small businesses face every day.
Years of working with families, businesses, and local communities gave Mike a firsthand understanding of both the strengths and challenges of rural Missouri.
Mike saw small towns fight for jobs and housing, schools stretch limited resources, and rural hospitals struggle to keep healthcare close to home.
