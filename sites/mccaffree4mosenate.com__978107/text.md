About Mike Born and raised in Nevada, Missouri, Mike embodies the values of community and integrity.
With roots firmly planted in his hometown, Mike has dedicated 17 years to serving on the Nevada R-5 Board of Education and countless hours serving his community.
He’s ready to support rural Missourians by creating quality jobs, supporting public education, advocating for affordable housing and improving the district’s infrastructure, and ensuring hospitals and clinics stay accessible and available in small communities. .
