Joshua Thompson State Representative · 13th District Home Meet Joshua Platform Events Get Involved Donate The Platform 10 Priorities to Fix Washington State The Central Washington we call home is under attack by the bureaucrats of Olympia.
These are the priorities Joshua will fight for to protect the families, businesses, and way of life that make the 13th District home.
Priority 01 Protect and Encourage Families Never in Washington State's history have so many bills been anti-family — and that's the center of the problem.
How can any couple raise a family when housing is unaffordable, successful companies are leaving over taxes and regulation, children are under attack in schools, parents' rights are being dissolved, and laws encourage crime and unsafe streets?
Joshua will put families first in every vote he casts in Olympia.
Priority 02 Business-Friendly Policy We need to get back to a business-friendly Washington immediately.
Right now the state is hostile to companies large and small — the large ones are leaving, and the small ones are going out of business.
Joshua will work to cut the taxes and red tape that drive employers and jobs out of Central Washington.
Priority 03 Power & Water for Central Washington Remarkably, with one of the greatest rivers in the country running through our backyard, we face a shortage of both clean and accessible water.
We need to finish the Columbia Basin Project, protect our dams, and develop new energy policies that let Central Washington flourish.
