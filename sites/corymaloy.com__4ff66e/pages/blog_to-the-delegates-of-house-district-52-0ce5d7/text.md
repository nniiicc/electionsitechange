By Representative Cory Maloy Share Link copied Reviewing a bill during the 2026 Utah Legislative Session.
You were elected as a Republican delegate on March 17.
Congratulations!
That is not a small thing.
You stepped up, your neighbors trusted you, and now you carry their voice into one of the most important moments in our party’s process.
That is exactly how a republic is supposed to work, from the ground up, neighborhood by neighborhood.
I do not take that lightly.
And I hope you don’t either.
As your Representative for Utah House District 52, I am honored to ask for your support at the Utah County Republican Convention on April 18.
Serving this district is one of the privileges of my life.
Lehi, American Fork, Saratoga Springs, these are my neighbors.
My family lives here.
I shop here, worship here, and I think about this community every time I walk into the Utah State Capitol.
I want to keep doing that work, and I want to do it with your support behind me.
Before the convention gets underway, come find me at my booth so we can chat.
I will be there in the morning before the opening session, and I would love to meet you, answer your questions, and hear what is on your mind.
You represent real people with real concerns, and that kind of direct conversation is something I genuinely look forward to.
After the convention opens and the opening program wraps up, House District 52 will hold its caucus breakout in Room D153 at approximately 10:45 to 11:00 a.m.
That is where our district’s vote takes place, and I hope to see you there.
Here is where I stand on the issues that matter most to our district: Lower taxes and balanced budgets.
Government should live within its means.
Parental rights and educational choice.
Parents, not bureaucrats, should be making decisions about their children’s education.
Protecting women’s sports and biological truth.
This is common sense, and I will defend it.
Election integrity.
Utahns deserve confidence that every legal vote counts and every election is secure.
I will keep fighting for it.
Pro-life protections for the unborn.
Life matters.
Full stop.
Strong law enforcement and safe communities.
Our neighborhoods deserve to feel secure.
Constitutional freedoms, including the Second Amendment.
These are not negotiable.
Legal immigration and the rule of law.
Borders and laws exist for a reason.
I’m opposed illegal alien presence, and fully support legal immigration and the path to naturalization.
Responsible growth that protects our quality of life.
District 52 is growing fast.
I believe we can grow smarter.
Sound water policy for future generations.
In Utah, water is everything.
We have to get it right.
These are not talking points I picked up this election cycle.
This is the record I have been building for several years in the legislature, and it is the foundation I will continue to build on.
As Chair of the House Business, Labor, and Commerce Committee, I have worked to strengthen Utah’s economy, reduce unnecessary regulation, and protect the prosperity that makes our district such a great place to raise a family.
That work continues.
See you at convention.
Stop by my booth before we get started.
I look forward to the conversation.
