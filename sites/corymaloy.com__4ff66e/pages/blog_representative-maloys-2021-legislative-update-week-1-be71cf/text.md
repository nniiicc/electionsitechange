The 2021 Legislative Session is underway, and it will be unlike any other.
We have a lot of work to do to ensure our state recovers from the COVID-19 pandemic.
Over the next few weeks, I will keep you updated on what I am working on and the highlights of the session.
If you ever have questions, please reach out.
Committee Assignments
For this session I have been assigned to:
- House Business and Labor Committee
- Chair, House Government Operations Committee

Working to Protect and Keep Families Connected
One of the things I am working on this session is making sure we can stay connected with loved ones while keeping our emotional, mental, and physical health in mind.
In a recent article about this work, I said, “Certainly we are in a pandemic, and we all need to do our part to keep each other safe, but there are people going months and months without contact.
It is a major detriment to their well-being.”
Bill Highlight: H.B. 143
The work we do in the Legislature isn’t always exciting, but it is important.
Each week I will highlight a bill I am working to pass into law.
This week’s bill is H.B. 143, which:
- Prohibits the Driver License Division from suspending a license based solely on a person’s failure to pay certain fines.
- Prohibits a court from ordering a driver license suspension or revocation under certain circumstances.
Policy Priorities
We have a lot of work to do over the forty-five-day session.
A few of our priorities are:
- Broaden economic success. 2020 brought a host of economic challenges, but Utah was well prepared.
Spending taxpayer funds to maximize every dollar, prepare for the next crisis, and broaden success to every corner of the state will be critical to Utah’s continuing rebound.
- Build a connected future.
Continued investment in transportation, recreation, and technology infrastructure lays the foundation for Utah’s long-term prosperity and quality of life.
- Extend opportunity to all.
Improving access to healthcare, housing, and education opens the door of opportunity to everyone.
Utah reaches its full potential only when all Utahns can make the most of their talents.
- Balance emergency power.
After nearly a year of a global pandemic, it is clear we must reassess how we respond to emergencies, allowing swift action but never at the expense of the will of the people.
I have served as your representative since 2016, and it has been the honor of my lifetime.
Thank you.
— Representative Cory Maloy, Utah House District 6
