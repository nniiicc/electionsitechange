The progressive left’s agenda on sex and identity has no place in our public institutions, and least of all in our schools.
Biological truth means people are born male or female, and Utah’s schools, sports, and government should make their decisions from that fact.
Biology supplies that reality.
I hold to biological truth, and I vote to keep public policy anchored to it.
It is one of my core principles, and it does not change.
The progressive left’s ideology and agenda on sex and identity has no place in our public institutions, and least of all in our schools.
I will keep voting to keep it out.
Where it shows up in my votes Girls’ sports come first.
I voted for H.B.
11 , which reserves girls’ school sports teams for girls.
When the bill faced a veto, I voted to override it.
Public facilities come next.
I voted for H.B.
257 , which defines male and female in Utah law and ties restroom and locker room use in public schools and government buildings to a person’s sex.
The bill came before the Business and Labor Committee, which I chaired.
I voted for it there and on the House floor.
College housing followed.
H.B.
269 came before the same committee.
It requires public college and university dorm rooms designated for men or women to house students of that sex, and it keeps gender-neutral housing available.
I voted for it in committee and on the House floor.
Schools and government programs round it out.
I voted for H.B.
261 , which ended diversity, equity, and inclusion offices and statements in public colleges, schools, and government agencies.
It replaced them with programs open to every student.
I also voted for H.B.
183 , which replaces “gender” with “sex” in state law.
The House passed it.
The Senate did not advance it.
The same principle governs how I vote on gender identity policy in schools and government, and on DEI programs that sort people by identity.
Sex is male or female.
People stand before the law as individuals.
My principle guides policy.
It does not measure anyone’s worth.
People on every side of this issue live in House District 52, and I answer to all of them.
I invite you to ask me about any vote above.
I stand on every one of them.
