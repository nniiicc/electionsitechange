The past few days have been quite strenuous on our American institutions, including our political process.
Following the guidance of our President and Governor, the Utah County Republican Party has enacted social distancing policies to protect our community.
As a result of precautionary measures, several changes will take place with this years Caucus and Convention, as followed: Caucus is postponed.
All Utah County delegates will continue to serve in their current capacity.
Delegate vacancies will be filled by the current alternate delegates, as they were elected in 2018.
If alternate delegates are unable or unwilling to serve, The Precinct Chair will appoint a replacement.
If a Precinct Chair position is vacant, the elected Vice Chair will be given the opportunity to fill that role.
If they decline or are unable, the Legislative District Chair will appoint a new Precinct Chair.
All delegate vacancies are to be reported to the Utah County Secretary by March 19, 2020.
All vacancies are to be filled and reported to the Utah County Secretary by March 25, 2020.
The completed delegate list will then be available to all candidates.
The Utah County Convention will then follow with this structure: Candidates will have the opportunity to submit a campaign speech video to the County Party, not to exceed 5 minutes, by April 7, 2020.
Candidate videos will be posted no later than April 11, 2020.
Voting will be conducted by mobile app.
Delegates will have the opportunity to vote starting April 16, 2020 (8am) and ending April 18, 2020 (5pm).
Election results will be posted in the evening of April 18, 2020.
The Utah County Republican Party is exploring opportunities to provide delegates with digital campaign activities, like town halls and debates, although nothing is currently scheduled.
The Party will communicate any future opportunities to all candidates.
Delegate data requests should be communicated to Karen Ellingson, by emailing [email protected] .
