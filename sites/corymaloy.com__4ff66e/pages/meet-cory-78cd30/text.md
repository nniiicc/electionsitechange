It is my street.
Linda and I chose Lehi.
We planted here, built our lives here, and stayed.
Our three adult children have families of their own now, including grandchildren and a great-grandson we are proud of.
We may not have little ones underfoot anymore, but that does not make us less invested in this community.
If anything, our stake here is about leaving District 52 better than we found it.

Those values go with me into every vote I cast.
I believe in the sanctity of life, the strength of the family, and the kind of community where neighbors look out for each other.
That is not a campaign line.
It is how Linda and I live.

Chair, House Business, Labor, and Commerce Committee
The Record
I chair the House Business, Labor, and Commerce Committee and previously chaired Government Operations, where I remain a member.
No bill I have run has ever failed a floor vote in either chamber.
Those bills include landmark regulatory reform through Utah’s Regulatory Sandbox Program, which opened doors for entrepreneurs, business, and innovation across the state.
They include SB 115, the firearm preemption bill I wrote.
I ran it twice in the House and it did not get a floor vote in the Senate either time, so on the third try I had a senator run it there and I carried it on the House floor.
That is the version that became law.
They include HB 146, which closed the gap that left neighborhood pools and playgrounds open to registered child sex offenders.
They include HB 295, which created the physician workforce pipeline grant program to expand medical residency capacity.
They include SB 24, which I floor-sponsored to expand the scope of practice for physician assistants.
They include HB 209, confirming that only U.S. citizens may vote in Utah elections.
And they include HB 269, the ambulance provider payment framework that protects patients from balance billing.
I do not grandstand.
I show up, learn the policy, build the coalition, and get results.
