The Pool in Your Neighborhood
Lehi Police Chief Darren Paul came to me with a problem.
Parents were calling about registered child sex offenders at neighborhood pools and playgrounds, and his officers wanted to act.
The law would not let them.
It kept offenders out of public parks and public pools but said nothing about the pool your HOA owns or the playground at your apartment complex.
Too gray to enforce.
HOA neighborhoods and apartment communities have multiplied across this district in recent years, and those are exactly the facilities the old law missed.
The pools and playgrounds our kids use every summer.
We worked it out together and I ran the bill.
HB 146 bars registered child sex offenders from those spaces and makes violating it a class A misdemeanor.
Police now have the teeth to enforce what parents were asking them to enforce all along.
That is the difference between just writing bills and being the one police call when the law falls short.
