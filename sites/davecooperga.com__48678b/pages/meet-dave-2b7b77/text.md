A citizen-soldier.
Retired public servant at all levels of government. a small city two urban counties state park ranger and highway worker consortium of 13 states United States Environmental Protection Agency Founder of a international humanitarian nonprofit to help keep our soldiers safer by sending over 1,400 boxes of student-donated school supplies and other aid to: Afghanistan Horn of Africa Honduras Life-long activist. professional and personal contributions to the environmental field. birthing care movement created hospital birthing rooms and increased family participation.
Dave wasn’t born with a silver spoon in his mouth.
His father was a carpenter.
His mother was a librarian.
He understands the blue-collar life and the minimum wage worker life.
He’s been an industrial spray painter, a metal/wood worker and a milling machine operator; a fast-food worker, a pizza delivery driver and a gas station attendant.
Dave covered news and features for a weekly newspaper, which opened his eyes to the positive impact of public service.
Dave is the grandfather of two young boys, a pilot, and a solid tennis player.
As your state Senator, my three top priorities must be: 1) protect Georgia’s election system so that everyone’s voice is heard and their vote counted, 2) prevent corruption and prosecute those who violate the public trust— rather than re-write laws to protect them, and 3) assure that government agencies do their jobs.
I will author legislation that elevates Georgia’s low rankings in health, education, and wages, and block economic opportunism that relies on subsidizing corporations with our hard-earned tax dollars.
The Georgia legislature needs members who understand the working people, and who already know how to govern.
I understand the vast difference between for-profit companies and public service.
I took an oath to defend the Constitution, and I will actively oppose those that undermine it.
