A dozen times Dan Sullivan has voted to jack up our prices.
Sullivan’s fuel price hikes affect everyone – from our smallest fishing boat in the ocean, to families heating their homes through winter, to remote communities in Alaska like Shungnak who are now going to be paying $26.82 a gallon of gas throughout the entire winter until their spring barge comes.
“Alaskans have had enough.
We need a senator who is willing to solve the problem, not one still voting for a war that keeps driving prices up while enriching himself – it’s time for change.” Dan Sullivan has voted in support of the price-hiking war in Iran more than ten times , even as it sends fuel prices to record highs – spiking prices nearly 50% – and causes real economic pain for Alaska families.
Meanwhile, Mary has introduced popular plans to lower energy costs and put more money back in Alaskans’ pockets .
Alaskans will send Mary Peltola to the Senate this November to put Alaska first.
