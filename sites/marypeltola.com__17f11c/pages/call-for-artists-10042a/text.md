This call is open to artists living and working in Alaska.
What types of art can be submitted?
All visual arts media are welcome, including: Painting Photography Collage Sculpture (3D art) Mixed Media Video Do I need to submit new work?
Yes.
Only new work proposals specifically created for this campaign will be accepted.
Existing work cannot be submitted.
How many grant applications can I send?
Only one grant application per artist is allowed.
Is there a submission fee for grant applications or artwork submissions?
No, there is no fee to apply to the grant application nor artwork submission.
Is this a paid opportunity?
Yes.
Selected artists will receive funding for materials to complete their project proposals based on their submitted grant application.
Please be sure to fill out your projected funding needs for materials in this proposal.
Payment details will be shared upon selection.
You are welcome to submit your art to this Call for Artists after the grant application deadline, if you do not find funding for your project necessary, and your art too will be open for exhibit selection and cash prizes.
What kind of work are you looking for?
We are seeking work that reflects Alaska’s communities, cultures, land, and future.
Projects that uplift local Alaskan’s perspectives, stories, and traditions are especially encouraged.
What happens if I am selected?
Selected artists will be notified late September that their work will be featured in a public exhibit highlighting Alaska’s creative voices.
From the STANDARD exhibit only, the selected artists will compete for CASH prizes.
1st place: $3,000 2nd place: $1,500 3rd place: $500 When will submissions open and close?
Grant applications and artist submissions both open on Tuesday, July 14th, 2026.
Grant applications are and are due by Friday, August 7th , 2026 .
We do suggest submitting earlier rather than later for these!
All artwork submissions are due Friday, September 25th, 2026.
When will artists be notified if they received a grant?
Notifications will be sent on Monday, August 10th , 2026.
When are projects due?
Final projects must be completed by September 25th, 2026.
When will artists be notified if their artwork made it into the exhibit?
Notifications will be sent October 2nd.
Do I have to live in a specific part of Alaska?
No, artists from all regions of Alaska are welcome to apply.
Can collaborative teams apply?
Yes, collaborations and collectives are welcome.
How will the artwork be used?
Selected work will be featured in campaign exhibits, materials, events, and outreach.
Full details will be provided to selected artists.
Who owns the work?
By submitting your artwork, you are granting Fish Family Freedom Fund (and each of its joint fundraising participants, including Alaskans for Mary), a license to publish, reproduce, display, distribute, modify, use, and sell the work and transferring ownership of the physical piece itself to Fish Family Freedom Fund.
Selected pieces will be sold at an auction fundraiser for Fish Family Freedom Fund.
Submitted artwork may not be returned to you.
Who do I contact with questions?
For questions, please email [email protected] .
