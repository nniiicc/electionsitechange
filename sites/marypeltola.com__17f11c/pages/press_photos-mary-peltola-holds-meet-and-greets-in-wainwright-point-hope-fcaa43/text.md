Senator.
“It was great to be welcomed into both Wainwright and Point Hope and speak directly with Alaskans about how we can take on DC corruption and lower costs for working families,” said Mary Peltola.
“The skyrocketing cost of fuel, groceries, and housing is making it harder and harder for families in Wainwright, Point Hope, and across our state to get by.
As an independent voice for Alaska, I’ll fight to stand up for Alaskans’ subsistence rights, put local communities in the driver’s seat on important projects, and ensure the next generation of Alaskans has the same kind of abundance we grew up with.” At the meet and greets and in meetings with local community leaders and officials, Mary heard from Alaskans suffering the fallout of DC price hikes who are finding it harder and harder to afford gas and housing and make a life in Alaska.
Across every conversation, the message from Alaskans is clear: the rigged system in DC is harming our Alaska way of life, and they want a senator who will actually bring down costs and put Alaska first.
Yesterday’s visits follow Mary’s announcements of bold plans to lower costs , save our fish , boost oil and gas production , take care of our veterans , and fix the rigged system in DC that is harming our state, and comes as over 15,000 Alaskans have signed up to help elect Mary to the Senate.
