“It’s great to be in Kotzebue and talk with Alaskans about how we can lower prices and restore abundance,” said Mary Peltola.
“The skyrocketing cost of fuel, groceries, and housing is making it harder and harder for families in Kotzebue and across our state to get by.

Across every conversation, the message from Alaskans is clear: the rigged system in DC is harming our Alaska way of life, and they want a senator who will actually bring down costs and put Alaska first.
Today’s visit follows Mary’s announcements of bold plans to lower costs , save our fish , boost oil and gas production , take care of our veterans , and fix the rigged system in DC that is harming our state, and comes as over 15,000 Alaskans have signed up to help elect Mary to the Senate.
