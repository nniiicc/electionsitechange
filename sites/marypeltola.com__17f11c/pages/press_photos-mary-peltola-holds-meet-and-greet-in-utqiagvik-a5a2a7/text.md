Senator.
“From the Arctic to the Aleutians, I’m making my way around our state to reach every community I can, and make sure no Alaskan goes unheard in the U.S.
Senate,” said Mary Peltola.
“The skyrocketing cost of fuel, groceries, and housing is making it harder and harder for families in Utqiaġvik and across our state to get by.
As an independent voice for Alaska, I’ll fight to boost oil and gas production, stand up for Alaskans’ subsistence rights, and fight to ensure the next generation of Alaskans has the same kind of abundance we grew up with.” At the meet and greet and in meetings with local community leaders and officials, Mary heard from Alaskans suffering the fallout of DC price hikes who are finding it harder and harder to afford gas and housing and make a life in Alaska.
Across every conversation, the message from Alaskans is clear: the rigged system in DC is harming our Alaska way of life, and they want a senator who will actually bring down costs and put Alaska first.
Yesterday’s visit follows Mary’s announcements of bold plans to lower costs , save our fish , boost oil and gas production , take care of our veterans , and fix the rigged system in DC that is harming our state, and comes as over 15,000 Alaskans have signed up to help elect Mary to the Senate.
