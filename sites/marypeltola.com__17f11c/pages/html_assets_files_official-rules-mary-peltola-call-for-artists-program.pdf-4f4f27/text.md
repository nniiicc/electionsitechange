7.​ How to Enter.
To enter, take the following steps during the Promotion Period: o​ First, if desired, complete and submit an optional Grant Application between June 26, 2026, and August 7, 2026.
Grant applications can be submitted by visiting https://marypeltola.com/call-for-artists/.
If you are selected to receive a grant, you will be notified by August 10, 2026.
Grants will cover the cost of materials used to create an artwork for submission.
You are eligible to submit an artwork regardless of whether you receive a grant. o​ Next, create an artwork interpreting the theme “Protecting Our Way of Life.” Artists are encouraged to explore what makes Alaska special, the traditions and livelihoods they value, and the people, places, and experiences they believe are worth protecting. o​ Finally, during the Promotion Period: ●​ Visit https://marypeltola.com/call-for-artists/ (the “Site”), complete the online submission form for the Promotion, and follow the directions to submit your completed artwork.
