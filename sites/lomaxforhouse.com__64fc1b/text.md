ABOUT GET TO KNOW James Lomax Fresh Conservative Leadership James Lomax, elected to the Alabama House in 2022, represents South Huntsville, Hampton Cove, and Monte Sano, emphasizing conservative values, economic growth, and strong families.
He serves on the Rules, Financial Services, Ethics, and Economic Development & Tourism committees, advancing job creation, fiscal responsibility, and accountable government.
He has supported efforts to remove foreign election influence, grow the economy responsibly, and improve schools with practical solutions.
A real estate business leader, he brings private-sector experience to Montgomery, focusing on protecting taxpayers, supporting law enforcement, strengthening education, and defending Alabama’s core values; he lives in Huntsville with his wife Elizabeth and two daughters.
