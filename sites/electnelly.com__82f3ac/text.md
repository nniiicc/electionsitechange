Meet Nelly
Dr.
Nelly Burdette is a psychologist and first-generation immigrant bringing a front-line perspective to public service.
Not a career politician, she has spent the last two decades listening and helping people navigating healthcare, housing, and economic challenges.
She’s running to strengthen communities and support small businesses.
"We are excited that Nelly Burdette has decided to run for State Senator in our district.
While this is her first foray into the political world; Nelly’s life story, professional successes and history of community involvement are testament to the significant skills and experience she would bring to the office of RI State Senator.
She offers the people of District 17 a candidate with a much needed fresh voice and fresh perspective.”
