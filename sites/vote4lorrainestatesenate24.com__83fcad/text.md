“As a State Senator I understand that agriculture is the backbone of Columbia County, and I deeply respect the leaders and families who have protected this land and sustained our local economy for generations.
I’m stepping forward to continue that legacy—and will fight for the policies that keep our farms strong, our land protected, and our rural communities thriving for the next generation.” -Lorraine Lorraine Thomas is a Gulf War Veteran, Registered Nurse, and 20-year resident of Columbia County who has dedicated her life to service — to her country, her faith, and her community.
Born in Birmingham, England, Lorraine brings both global perspective and deep local commitment to her work.
For the past two decades, she has served Columbia County and CSRA families on the front lines of healthcare as a registered nurse, advocating for patients, protecting the vulnerable, and navigating complex systems to ensure people receive the care they deserve.
Lorraine earned her Bachelor of Science in Nursing from the Medical College of Georgia (2005).
During her studies, she was inducted into the Sigma Theta Tau International Honor Society of Nursing (2005).
She received the George Stony Scholarship and was among the first recipients of the Medical College of Georgia Nursing Scholar Award (2006) for academic excellence.
She was awarded Distinguished Honor Graduate upon completing Licensed Practical Nursing school during her military service (1992).
Meet Lorraine A woman of strong Christian faith, Lorraine believes leadership is a calling rooted in integrity, accountability, and compassion.
As a domestic violence survivor, she understands firsthand the courage it takes to overcome adversity.
Her life experience has strengthened her resolve to stand up for all families, including those who feel unheard, marginalized, or forgotten.
Lorraine is committed to ensuring state resources are distributed responsibly and fairly across District 24 — strengthening public and school safety and supporting small businesses.
She will fight for teacher retention, education, fair and expedient healthcare access, and improved mental health services for veterans and working families.
She believes no citizen of Columbia County should fall behind because of outdated or biased policies.
Public funds must be distributed responsibly and equitably, ensuring that opportunity is not limited by zip code, income, or background.
