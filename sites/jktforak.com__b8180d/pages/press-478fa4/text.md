PLUMBERS & STEAMFITTERS UA LOCAL 367 ENDORSES KREISS-TOMKINS FOR GOVERNOR
Today, Democratic candidate for Alaska governor Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins (JKT) and lieutenant governor running mate Zac Johnson announced the sole endorsement of Plumbers and Steamfitters UA Local 367 (UA 367).
UA Local 367 represents skilled tradespeople working throughout Alaska in plumbing, pipefitting, welding, HVACR service, utilities, facility maintenance, and related industries.
UA 367 members build and maintain the infrastructure that Alaskans depend on every day—from schools, hospitals, military installations, and public facilities to utilities, energy projects, and major commercial and industrial construction.
The union advocates for safe working conditions, family-supporting wages and benefits, and opportunities for Alaskans to build careers in the skilled trades.

41 ALASKA FISHERIES LEADERS ENDORSE KREISS-TOMKINS FOR GOVERNOR
Today, Democratic candidate for Alaska governor Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins (JKT) announced the endorsement of 40 Alaskan fisheries leaders.
The endorsers, who come from communities across the state, support JKT’s vision to conserve Alaska’s fisheries resources, prioritize science-based management, and restore balance to the Board of Fish.

50 ALASKA NATIVE LEADERS ENDORSE KREISS-TOMKINS FOR GOVERNOR
Today, Democratic candidate for Alaska governor Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins (JKT) announced a new slate of 51 endorsements from Alaska Native leaders from across the state.
The endorsers — representing communities from Naknek to Klawock — are backing JKT’s vision to support Alaska’s schools, protect fisheries, and lower everyday costs like healthcare, electricity, fuel, and housing.
“I’m honored to have the support of so many leaders in Alaska Native communities, who have stewarded our lands and waters since time immemorial,” Kreiss-Tomkins said.
“We need a governor who will defend subsistence, support Native culture and language, and stand up for rural Alaska.”
JKT RELEASES 20-MINUTE SHORT FILM DOCUMENTING YUKON RIVER TRIP
Democratic candidate for Alaska governor Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins (JKT), who in July completed a four-day, 11-village, 450-mile campaign swing down the Yukon River by boat, today released a 20-minute documentary shot during the trip that captures the stories and way of life of the Middle and Lower Yukon.
“From Day 1, we wanted this campaign to be for all of Alaska — including rural Alaska,” said Kreiss-Tomkins.
“This film capturing the stories and concerns from the Yukon villages is a small way for us to follow through on that commitment to rural Alaska.”
