Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins (also known as JKT), born and raised in Sitka, represented Southeast Alaska for a decade in the legislature, bringing new energy to Juneau and working across the aisle to protect public schools and lower the cost of energy and housing.
JKT is running for governor to bring a new generation of leadership to the governor’s office.
Let’s support our schools, lower the cost of energy, build new homes, and balance the budget — so that the next generation will also call Alaska home.
Fully fund our schools and reverse the decline of public education in Alaska.
Pass a constitutional amendment to “forward fund” education and put an end to the awful annual budget guessing game.
School districts and families shouldn’t have to wonder how many teachers are going to be laid off (only to be rehired a month later) on an annual basis.
Ensure enough funding for Alaska schools to offer sports, electives (shop, music, art, etc.), and extracurriculars that enrich students’ lives.
Get cell phones out of classrooms.
Pay teachers fairly.
Alaska should attract and retain the best educators in the country.
Create a defined benefit retirement system that ensures we don’t lose our best teachers to the Lower 48.
Support Our Schools Support development of all energy sources — renewables, hydro, natural gas — to lower utility bills for Alaskans.
Require the four Railbelt utilities to cooperate with each other in order to provide cheaper electricity for Alaskans.
Partner with industry and support the development of an Alaskan natural gas pipeline.
Support the Alaska Energy Authority’s Renewable Energy Fund.
Lower Energy Costs Protect Medicaid expansion.
Partner with tribal health organizations to secure maximum federal funding for Alaskans’ healthcare.
Reduce healthcare costs by promoting telehealth.
Support appropriate scopes of practice for healthcare practitioners such as nurse practitioners, physician assistants, optometrists, and dental health aides.
Expand access to in-home care for seniors and people with disabilities so that Alaskans can live with dignity in their own communities.
Support our patients, healthcare workers, and caregivers by working to strengthen safety standards in healthcare facilities.
Bring down medical costs by outlawing excessive drug price markups and requiring transparency about the prices hospitals and drug companies charge behind closed doors.
Support Healthcare Access Protect Our Fisheries Rein in bottom trawl bycatch and conserve Alaska’s fisheries resources.
Prevent permits from leaving Alaska for the Lower 48.
Alaska’s commercial fishing permits should be fished by Alaskans.
Fully empower the Division of Investments (Commercial Fishing Revolving Loan Fund) and CFAB to finance the entry of new and young fishermen into the fleet.
Fully fund ADF&G.
Prioritize restoring the crashed Chinook runs in the Yukon, Kuskokwim, and Southcentral Alaska rivers.
Support balance on the Board of Fish.
The BOF should not be dominated by any one user group.
Support the independence of CFEC.
Nominate Alaskans to the North Pacific Council who support the interests of Alaska-based fishermen and Alaska coastal communities.
Prioritize capital investment in port and harbor infrastructure.
Regulate Data Centers and AI Only candidate to support a moratorium on all new data centers (similar to New York State ) until the legislature can pass comprehensive regulation on data center development.
Require local approval before data centers can be built, with binding community benefit agreements.
Make data centers pay the full cost of their electricity, without any direct or indirect subsidies from existing ratepayers.
Prevent financial risk from being shifted to the public.
Champion legislation to hold AI companies liable in Alaska courts when their products cause harm.
Require human decision makers whenever a state agency uses AI in decisions affecting Alaskans.
Require political advertisers to disclose AI-generated content in political advertising.
Establish laws prohibiting deepfakes when used to mislead the public.
Defend Alaskans from AI-enabled crime, including Internet scammers and deepfakes that target children and seniors.
Support and enforce laws to protect children and families against harmful AI content.
Build More Homes Bring down the cost of housing by cutting red tape so we can build affordable homes where Alaskans need them most.
Unlock state land parcels within and adjacent to Alaska cities and towns for housing development to create more housing supply.
Eliminate regulatory barriers to low-cost manufactured and modular housing (trailers, tiny homes, etc.) to provide safe and affordable homes across the state.
Deploy state corporations (AHFC and also AIDEA) as financing partners for new housing development.
Stop leaving federal housing dollars on the table.
Funding from HUD, USDA Rural Development, and tribal housing programs can supercharge homebuilding.
Create mechanisms to tax “dark homes” — second homes and vacation homes owned by nonresidents — as a means to create a housing market more friendly for Alaskans who live here.
Create a governor’s housing task force (modeled after Atlanta’s) to identify developable land and create an expedited permitting system to build more housing, faster.
End the Domestic Violence and Sexual Assault Crisis Invest in shelters, safe housing, and the local and tribal organizations that help victims reach safety and rebuild their lives.
Expand sexual assault response teams and forensic medical care across Alaska, so survivors get care and evidence gets collected close to home.
Arrest and prosecute perpetrators, and increase statewide coordination between law enforcement, prosecutors, and tribal and state courts to stop cases from falling through the cracks.
Provide dedicated law enforcement support to locate missing and murdered Indigenous people (MMIP) and deliver justice for victims and families.
Better Government; NO Corruption Get corporate money out of politics: remove political spending power from the list of powers the State of Alaska grants its corporations.
Reinstitute campaign contribution limits for state elections.
Appoint only qualified Alaskans to state boards and commissions — not political allies with no qualifications for the positions.
No foreign junkets.
Building on laws I helped pass as a legislator , state officials should not accept lavish vacations from foreign governments or deep-pocketed individuals when there is little to no benefit to the State of Alaska.
Protect Alaskans’ Personal Freedoms Defend Alaska women’s right to choose.
Ensure access to contraception.
Protect Alaskans' privacy by issuing a statewide ban on Flock cameras and similar AI-driven mass-surveillance technology.
Protect Alaskans’ — including Alaska Natives and LGBTQ Alaskans — right to live their lives free from discrimination and prejudice.
Protect Alaskan voters’ personal information from federal intrusion.
Defend SubsistEnce Protect the rural preference and put an end to litigation that undermines Title VIII of ANILCA.
Adequately fund the Division of Subsistence at ADF&G.
Fix Our Infrastructure Prevent tax dollars from going to glitzy megaprojects that don’t serve our critical needs and rarely materialize.
Build and maintain roads, airports and airstrips, bridges, power plants, and ports.
Infrastructure supports small businesses and lowers costs.
Rebuild the ferry system.
Support adequate funding.
Build a new generation of mainline vessels, financed by GO bonds.
Get politics out of the Alaska Marine Highway System by creating an independent, non-political governing board, similar to the Alaska Railroad and the University of Alaska.
Support our first responders by funding and equipping our police, fire, and emergency medical personnel.
Adequately fund the Village Public Safety Officer program to provide law enforcement in rural Alaska.
Keep Alaskans Safe Balance the Budget Make the Permanent Fund permanent: ensure that the entirety of the Permanent Fund is constitutionally protected from overspending so that the fund will grow and benefit future generations of Alaskans.
Ensure that Alaskans get a fair share from our state’s oil wealth.
All Alaska oil and gas producers should contribute to the Alaska budget by paying corporate income tax.
Hilcorp should pay its fair share just the way ConocoPhillips does.
