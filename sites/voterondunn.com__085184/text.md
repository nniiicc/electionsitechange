Register & Find Your Polling Location Click Here for Information Ron Dunn NH State Representative My name is Ron Dunn.
I am currently your New Hampshire State Representative for Londonderry, NH District 16 Rockingham County.
I am the son of Vietnam era Marine.
I am a Lifetime NRA member and strongly support the second amendment.
I will never vote for an income or sales tax.
I support Parents' Rights and school choice.
I will listen to all your concerns and issues.
I will hold open houses so you, the voter, have the ability to meet me and tell me your issues.
I serve on the Resource, Recreation and Development Committee at the State House and am the Vice Chairman.
We deal with PFAS issues, lakes, rivers, dams, State Parks and wastewater.
Currently, I serve on five Town Committees: Londonderry Town Council Chairman - Elected 2023 Londonderry Utility Committee - Liaison Londonderry Conservation Commision - Liaison Londonderry, NH Concerned Citizens for Clean Water - Citizen's Group State of NH HB 737 PFAS Study Committee - Appointed as a Londonderry Town Council Representative For the past 35 years I have been a pharmaceutical retail store manager as well as a Certified Pharmacy Technician.
I earned a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration from the University of New Hampshire.
I have lived in New Hampshire for the first 21 years of my life.
Then, I moved to Boston for work.
I found a career path and met the love of my life, my wife Cara.
After many years of city life, my wife and I moved back to New Hampshire in 2013 for a better quality of life that includes a strong sense of community.
My wife and I have 2 dogs: Moose, a Great Dane who is a rescue and Tank, a Bulldog, with an over the top personality.
(See Tank’s web page on this Web Site.) After reviewing the issues facing us today, I am committed to upholding your conservative values.
I trust and ask you to donate to my campaign, volunteer to support me at sign waves, and at other get-out-the vote events or put up a lawn sign.
Your help is always appreciated.
