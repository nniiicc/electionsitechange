January 28, 2026 Press@conniechansf.com SAN FRANCISCO LABOR COUNCIL ENDORSES CONNIE CHAN FOR CONGRESS San Francisco - Connie Chan has been endorsed by the San Francisco Labor Council (SFLC) for her campaign to succeed Speaker Emerita Nancy Pelosi in California Congressional District 11.
SFLC is the local body of the AFL-CIO.
Over 150 unions are affiliated with the SFLC, representing more than 100,000 union members and their families.
“The Labor movement is the answer to rebuilding the middle class because it empowers everyday people to organize and advocate for a better future collectively.
And in solidarity, I am always proud to stand with workers to fight for better wages, fair contracts, improved benefits and safe working conditions,” said Connie Chan.
“I’m honored to have the support and endorsement of the working people of San Francisco and I’m ready to fight to bring down the cost of living so every family can thrive.” “Workers know we can count on Connie Chan.
She doesn’t just drop in at election time - she’s there with us day in and day out doing the hard work that doesn’t always get headlines but does get results,” said Mike Casey, President, San Francisco Labor Council.
“Speaker Emerita Nancy Pelosi was our champion in Congress and we need Connie Chan to carry on the fight and be our voice in Washington, DC.” On Monday, the Chan campaign announced the support of the two largest unions representing California teachers, the California Teachers Association and the California Federation of Teachers joining UNITED HERE Local 2.
You can see a full list of endorsements at Conniechansf.com.
With this vote, SFLC made clear there is one candidate for Congress working people can count on: Connie Chan. -30-
