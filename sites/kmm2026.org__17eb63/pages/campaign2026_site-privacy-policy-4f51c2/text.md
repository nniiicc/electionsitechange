Information We Collect
This website does not collect or store personal information except the information that is provided by visitors in the Donations, Business Cards, and Contact forms.
We do not share this information.
Credit Card Information
Credit card information is processed by Authorize.net.
We do not store card data except the last four digits of the card number.
Donation Records
As required by Texas law, we record electronic contribution information for campaign reporting.
This information is the contributor's full name and address; the contribution date and amount; and, if the donation is over $110 in the reporting period (Jan-Jun and Jul-Dec), the donor's occupation and employer.
Cookies
We use only essential cookies.
Since no one logs into this site, there is no personal information collected except as provided by visitors in the donation, volunteer, and contact forms and that information is not shared.
