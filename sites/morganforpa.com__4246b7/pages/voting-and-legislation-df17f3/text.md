Through support of automatic voter registration, mail-in-ballots and free public transportation on election days. ​ Redistricting Additionally, Morgan supports fair redistricting in Pennsylvania by the formation of an independent citizens redistricting commission (IRC) and passing legislation that will ensure transparency and public input. ​ Bipartisanship and Legislation Morgan believes that in order for legislation to pass, members of all parties need to work together.
Morgan supports procedural rule changes that will force bills that have clear bipartisan support to be brought to a vote in a committee and in the House of Representatives chamber. ​ ​ bottom of page
