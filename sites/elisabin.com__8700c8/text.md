Eli Sabin
for New Haven
Born and raised in New Haven, Eli will bring new leadership, energy, and ideas to the fight for our future.
Why I'm running
In this urgent moment, I believe we need new leadership to fully fund our schools, bring down the cost of living, and fight back against the Trump administration.
With Connecticut getting harder to afford and the federal government out of control, people are losing hope.
I love our city and our state.
I know that with new representation that listens, organizes, and leads, we can make New Haven and Connecticut more affordable, safer, and more fair.
And by ensuring our government actually works to improve people's lives, we can restore faith in our future.
