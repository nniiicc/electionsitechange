Absentee Ballots:
DO NOT MAIL YOUR ABSENTEE BALLOT AT THIS TIME.
It will not arrive in time to be counted.
Absentee ballots MUST be received by the City Clerk by 8pm on Tuesday, August 11.
You can drop your absentee ballot off in the Ballot Collection Boxes outside City Hall on Orange Street or Church Street.
If you are unable to return your absentee ballot, you can just go to the polls and vote Early or on Election Day.
They will simply disregard your absentee ballot application.
