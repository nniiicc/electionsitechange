Request Absentee Ballot Meet Ryan Otis A Life of Service in Maine "I believe that electing new leaders with a new approach would create the change we need to put Maine on a more affordable path." Ryan has led a life of service, from joining his father at community pancake breakfasts to serving as Potentate of the Nobles of Anah Shrine to leading the Belfast Lions and acting as treasurer of Belfast Lodge #24.
Now, Ryan is a member of the RSU 71 School Board and is the owner of Rollie's Bar & Grill, and Rollie's Remote Catering.
Ryan and his wife, Brooke, are the proud parents of Thatcher, Jedsen and Archer.
