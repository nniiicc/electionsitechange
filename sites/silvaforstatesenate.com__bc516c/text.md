Hawaii Needs More.
More cooperation between the community and government, more responsibility for our 'Āina and ourselves, and more accountability for the things that need to be done.
We need a new look at how things are being handled and protected for our people and way of life.
These are the things that make this community so special.
We need to focus on what is happening to our families as they grow and age.
In these modern times, it is easy to lose sight of what has shaped Hawaii.
Growing up here, I have a sense of responsibility and hope to protect that Aloha for future generations.
"Pono ʻo Hawaiʻi i ka nui aʻe…"
