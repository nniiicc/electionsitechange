Bringing Common Sense Back To Government
Welcome to the Official Website for Bill Bain, Candidate for Michigan State Senate, 27th District.
The People's Senator
Bill Bain is not a career politician.
He is currently serving his second term as Flushing Township Trustee, is a Genesee County Metro Alliance Trustee, a former U.A.W Local 651 Executive Board Officer, a former small business owner, and a published author.
He's a community member and a neighbor who believes it's time for Lansing to listen to the people again.
With a focus on supporting workers, rebuilding our infrastructure, and ensuring government is accountable, Bill is committed to serving every resident of the 27th District.
He believes that by working together, we can create a better future for our families and for Michigan.
In The Community
Bill Bain represents all our citizens, workers, families, the small business community, our farmers, our veterans, and those self employed.
He understands the every struggles people face, and as State Senator Bill Bain will represent our community as a whole.
Supporting Road Workers
On September 3rd, Bill stood with road workers at the State Capitol to voice concerns over funding cuts.
He supports proper road funding to preserve good-paying jobs and fix our roads.
Standing with Nurses
On September 4th, Bill joined striking nurses at Henry Ford Genesys.
He stands with them on the critical issue of nurse-to-patient ratios to ensure patient safety and care.
