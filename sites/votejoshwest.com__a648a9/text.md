“I am a combat veteran, man of faith and family, businessman and constitutional conservative.
I’m 100% pro-life and endorsed by the NRA.
I work hard, have a heart for our community and do my best to represent your values with courage and honor.”
In 2016, Josh West, a decorated combat veteran, took on a new mission when he was elected state representative.
His goals were simple.
Fight for rural values, healthcare and education.
And be true to veterans and his constituents, ensuring our voice is heard and our Delaware County values represented in the state House.
Josh and his wife, Elizabeth, have been married for 12 years.
The couple has four children, Ethan (16), Walker (16), Katie (10) and Aubrey (9).
They are active members of Elk River Baptist Church.
Josh was born and raised in Grove.
He graduated from Grove High School in 1995 and attended Missouri Valley College on an athletic scholarship before deciding his mission was to join the U.S.
Army.
Nine years later, following medical retirement from the military, he returned to Grove where he is raising a family.
Josh works in agriculture and real estate, and he and his wife are small business owners.
Josh served combat tours in Afghanistan and Iraq.
On a combat patrol in 2003, Josh came under enemy fire while protecting civilians.
He was shot several times in the legs and abdomen, but continued to return fire and shield his wounded battalion commander with his body, eventually dragging him to safety.
For his heroism, Josh received the Purple Heart and the Bronze Star for Valor.
