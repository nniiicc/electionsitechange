Kathleen Cates:
Your Advocate in the Round House
Delivering for District 44, Defending Freedoms for All
Kathleen delivers for our district, from ensuring our farmers have the water they need, to supporting our first responders with equipment and services.
She defends all our freedoms, including our right to read what we want, a woman’s right to make her own health care decisions, and the right of people with disabilities to have access and equal treatment.
welcome!
I am proud to represent New Mexico District 44 as your advocate in the Roundhouse.
District 44 comprises the village of Corrales as well as parts of the cities of Rio Rancho and NW Albuquerque.
I love meeting my constituents, and you may see me in your neighborhood someday.
But until then, I am frequently at Village Council and City Council meetings, as well as other public forums.See the Events Calendar to learn where I’ll be next.
In addition,we can now also meet at my legislative office at the CNM Workforce Training Center.
So please come by and say hello.
Hope to meet you soon!
Kathleen Cates
Representative, NM House District 44
