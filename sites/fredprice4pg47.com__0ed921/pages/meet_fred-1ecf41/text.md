Meet FRED Issues Volunteer Contribute Meet Fred Price Jr.
I’m Fred Price Jr., and I’m proud to call District 47A in Prince George’s County home.
My life has been built on service.
I had the honor of serving in the United States Marine Corps, where I learned the values of discipline, leadership, and putting mission and people before self.
That experience shaped who I am and continues to guide how I approach challenges today.
After my military service, I remained committed to serving my community.
I had the privilege of serving as the local chapter President of the National Association for the Advancement of Colored People (NAACP) , where I worked to advocate for fairness, opportunity, and equal treatment for all.
That role deepened my understanding of the issues facing our communities and strengthened my commitment to stand up for what’s right.
I’ve also been actively involved in volunteering throughout Cheverly , working alongside neighbors to support local families, uplift our youth, and improve the quality of life in our town.
I believe real leadership isn’t about titles—it’s about showing up and doing the work.
Like many of you, I’ve seen the challenges our community faces—rising costs, public safety concerns, and the need for better opportunities for our children.
That’s why I’m running for delegate.
I believe District 47A deserves leadership that is present, accountable, and focused on delivering real results.
As your delegate, I will continue to serve with integrity, fight for working families, and put our community first—just as I have my entire life.
This campaign is about service, leadership, and building a stronger future for all of us.
