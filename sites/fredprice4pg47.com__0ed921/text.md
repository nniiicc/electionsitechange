Fred Price Jr. for Delegate – District 47A
I’m Fred Price Jr., and I’m running to represent District 47A in Prince George’s County because I believe our community deserves leadership that puts people first and delivers real results.
I understand the challenges families in our district face every day—from rising costs and public safety concerns to the need for stronger schools and real economic opportunities.
Like many of you, I want to live in a community where our neighborhoods are safe, our children can succeed, and our local businesses can grow and thrive.
My commitment is simple: I will listen, I will show up, and I will fight for you.
I believe government should be transparent, accountable, and focused on serving the people—not special interests.
I will work to strengthen public safety, support small businesses, improve education, and ensure that development in our community is responsible and benefits residents.
I’m not running for a title—I’m running to serve.
District 47A deserves a delegate who will stand up, speak out, and get things done.
Together, we can build a stronger, safer, and more prosperous future for our community.
