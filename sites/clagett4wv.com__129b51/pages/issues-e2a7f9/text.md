Chris Clagett for West Virginia Delegate District 91
Chris Clagett has a long history as dedicated public servant for his country and state serving with distinction as a US Navy Officer.
Chris has a vision for West Virginia District 91.
With a focus on encouraging more small business growth in our community, improving the quality of education in our public schools, and prioritizing medical freedom and safety, Chris brings a wealth of past leadership experience to the table.
He is committed to finding effective solutions, navigating governmental complexities, and delivering real results for the people of District 91.

End the Rain Tax:
Most of the rain that falls in our district is not managed by the City of Martinsburg rainwater runoff management system.
In fact, God is the person managing all of our rainwater runoff that eventually makes its way into the Potomac River.
Why should District 91 residents be taxed for funding the management of the city of Martinsburg’s runoff management system which does not handle or have anything to do with our own district rainwater runoff?
This is an unfair taxation of District 91 citizens for the benefit of people living elsewhere, and must be repealed.
