I am a wife and a proud mother of three wonderful children, and I have been blessed to call McCalla, Alabama my home for many years.
My roots here run deep, and so does my love for this community.
I believe in the strength of neighbors coming together and the promise that real progress brings.
I am running for State Representative for District 15 because I care about the people who live here, families just like mine, who want safer neighborhoods, brighter opportunities, and leaders who listen with compassion and lead with integrity.
My journey has never been about chasing titles.
It has always been about serving others.
From helping families through financial challenges to supporting programs that uplift our youth to speaking out for change in our justice system, I have always stood with the people.
Today, I am pursuing my law degree at Miles Law School because I believe that lasting change starts with truly understanding the system and then working tirelessly to make it better for everyone.
I have faced challenges, just like so many families in our community.
Those experiences have made me resilient.
I understand the struggles of everyday people because I have lived them, which makes me relatable.
And with the skills, dedication, and vision I carry forward, I am ready to serve District 15 with compassion, integrity, and strength.
For Families, For Neighbors, For You!
