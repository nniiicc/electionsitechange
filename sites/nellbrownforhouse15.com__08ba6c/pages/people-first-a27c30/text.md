Here's how:
Strengthening Public Education
As a mother of three and a proud graduate of public schools, I know that education is the foundation of every opportunity.
Our children deserve safe classrooms, dedicated teachers, and modern resources that prepare them for success.
What they do not deserve are outdated materials and constant budget cuts that hold them back.
Why it matters to us all
When we invest in public education, we invest in the future of our communities.
Strong schools reduce crime, open doors to better jobs, and give every child the chance to thrive.
They also strengthen our local economy by raising property values and attracting quality businesses and jobs to District 15.
Expanding Access to Healthcare
No family should ever have to choose between putting food on the table and getting the care they need.
I have seen neighbors and friends struggle because they could not afford basic health services.
I believe healthcare is a human right, not a privilege, and every person deserves access to the care that keeps them and their loved ones healthy.
Why it matters to all of us
When we expand access to Medicaid and improve mental health and preventative care, we reduce unnecessary trips to the emergency room, lower overall healthcare costs, and ensure that our families and workforce stay strong and healthy.
Demanding Transparency and Accountability in Montgomery
Too often, politicians make promises and then disappear after Election Day.
That is not leadership.
I believe you deserve to know how your tax dollars are being spent and to have a representative who truly listens and acts on your concerns.
Government works best when it is open and honest.
I will show up, tell the truth, and make decisions with your input because our community deserves leaders who are present, engaged, and committed to serving the people.
