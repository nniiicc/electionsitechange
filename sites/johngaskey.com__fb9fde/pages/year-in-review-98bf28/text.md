2025 Year in Review
Legislative & District Narrative: Representative John Gaskey
A Year of Service, Advocacy, and Community Connection
The 2025 legislative session served as a foundational year for State Representative John Gaskey.
Transitioning from 23 years of distinguished service in the U.S.
Coast Guard, Rep.
Gaskey established himself as an advocate who refuses to settle for "business as usual." His first year was defined by a mission-first approach to constituent service, fierce defense of constitutional rights, and a deep commitment to the people and traditions of Carver, Wareham, and Middleborough.
I.
Crisis Response and Direct Constituent Advocacy
Representative Gaskey's "mission-first" mindset was tested in his first thirty days.
During a brutal January cold snap, he personally intervened to secure emergency shelter for a pregnant woman living in her car, bypassing bureaucratic delays to get her the streets before the life-threatening storm hit.
This standard of direct advocacy continued throughout the year:
Restoring Livelihoods
He successfully advocated for two hard-working individuals whose driver's licenses were suspended and more than 100 constituents in navigating the state's unemployment system, ensuring families received the benefits they were owed.

II. 2025 District Highlights: Celebrating the Community
Beyond the State House, Rep.

Honoring First Responders & Law Enforcement

Veterans Advocacy
