Meet John Gaskey
Veteran.
Leader.
Representative for the 2nd Plymouth District.
Coast Guard Veteran
John is a veteran of the US Coast Guard and during his 23 years was stationed throughout the country.
His service instilled in him the values of duty, honor, and commitment to community that guide his work as a public servant.
Experience & Leadership
With decades of leadership experience both in the military and civilian sectors, John brings a unique perspective to the challenges facing our district.
He understands the importance of fiscal responsibility, transparent governance, and putting the needs of constituents first.
Fighting for Our District
As your State Representative for the 2nd Plymouth District, John is committed to addressing the issues that matter most to the residents of Carver, Middleborough, and Wareham.
From supporting small businesses to advocating for veterans' services, John works tirelessly to represent our community's interests on Beacon Hill.
Family & Community
John and his family are proud to call the 2nd Plymouth District home.
He understands the challenges facing local families because he experiences them firsthand.
His deep roots in the community inform his approach to public service and his commitment to making our district an even better place to live, work, and raise a family.
