About John
John is a veteran of the US Coast Guard and during his 23 years was stationed throughout the country.
He has seen good and bad representation but he has never seen it worse than here in Massachusetts.
Growing up in El Paso, TX, John saw his fair share of issues with immigration and the current disaster at the border has angered him and broken his heart for the home he loved.
Now he has to witness it happening 2000 miles away in his current home state.
John Gaskey will work to get this crisis under control and ensure law enforcement works with federal authorities to enforce the laws of the land and end the sanctuary status of Massachusetts and return our shelters back to its veterans and citizens.
