“I am proud to endorse Alleria Stanley for Maryland House of Delegates for District 4.
Frederick families are being priced out of their own neighborhoods, and Alleria has built a housing platform that meets that reality head on.
She backs reforming the transfer tax to lower closing costs for first time buyers, and social housing models that use public land to build mixed income communities..
She backs zoning reform for missing middle housing, statewide Just Cause eviction protection and the elimination of predatory rental fees.
Her work on tenant protection mirrors work I am pursuing in The City of Frederick.
Alleria's healthcare agenda draws directly from her twenty years as a military medical leader.
She wants to treat healthcare delivery as community infrastructure, expanding regional clinic networks, funding mobile health units, and protecting Maryland's community health centers.
She has committed to defending the for school based mental health programs and to funding youth psychiatric care.
She wants real teeth behind Maryland's Prescription Drug Affordability Board and a transparent Medicaid model that cuts out predatory pharmacy benefit managers.
Alleria understands that healthcare access and financial stability are the same fight.
District 4 needs a delegate who is willing to fight for the real issues that impact our communities.
Alleria will be that delegate.”
