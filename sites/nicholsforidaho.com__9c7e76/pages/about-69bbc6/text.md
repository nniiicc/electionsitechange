About ME
Born and raised in Boise, Idaho, I know the value of hard work, perseverance, and Idaho values.
I hold a science degree from BYU-Idaho and run a small property management business, but serving others is at the heart of what I do, through my business, in my community, with local organizations, and in my church.
I’ve coached youth sports, served in Scouts, volunteered in our schools, and served in several church callings.
My experience in business, banking, and serving on several boards has taught me financial responsibility, and I believe public funds deserve the highest standards of stewardship.
As a Central Committeeman in Canyon County and your District 10 State Senator, I fight for liberty, limited government, and Idaho families.
I chair the Senate Agriculture committee and serve on the Commerce and Human Resources and the Education committees.
I also co-chair the Idaho Freedom Caucus, using my voice to defend freedom and the values that make Idaho strong.
