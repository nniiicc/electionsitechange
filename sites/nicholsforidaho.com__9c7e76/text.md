Greetings, Idaho!
I’m Tammy Nichols, your State Senator for District 10.
I’m not just a politician—I’m a peopletician, because I work for YOU!

I fight to:
- Restore truth and liberty
- Ensure your voice is heard
- Keep government within its proper boundaries
- Protect Idaho’s values and way of life
I represent District 10, but my work is for all Idahoans, because legislative actions affect the entire state.
I listen, I act, and I take responsibility, even when it means standing alone.
