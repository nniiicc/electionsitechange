We are committed to protecting the privacy of everyone who visits our website, donates to our campaign, or engages with our communications.
This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.
By using this site, you agree to the practices described here.
We may update this Policy periodically — the date above reflects the most recent revision.
Your continued use of the site after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
Information We Collect Information You Provide Voluntarily When you fill out a form, sign up for updates, make a donation, volunteer, or otherwise engage with us directly, you may provide: Name, email address, postal address, phone number, and/or mobile number Employer name, occupation, and ZIP code (required for certain campaign finance disclosures) Payment information when making a contribution (processed securely by our donation platform — see Donations & Financial Information below) Any messages, endorsements, or other content you submit through our forms Information Collected Automatically When you visit our site, certain information is collected automatically through your browser and third-party services we use, including: IP address and general geographic location Browser type, operating system, and device information Pages visited, time spent on the site, and referring URLs Interaction data such as clicks and scroll behavior (used in aggregate, anonymous form) This site loads typography from Google Fonts , and Google may receive your IP address and browser information as part of delivering font files to your browser.
This site is delivered through Cloudflare , a content delivery network.
Cloudflare processes the request data necessary to route traffic, defend against malicious activity, and serve cached content efficiently.
Cloudflare’s data practices are governed by its own privacy policy.
Information from Form Submissions Forms on this site (endorsement, volunteer, and contact forms) are processed through Formspree (formspree.io).
When you submit a form, the contents of the form along with your IP address and the time of submission are transmitted to Formspree, which forwards them to us by email and/or stores them on our behalf.
Formspree’s privacy practices are governed by their own privacy policy.
Information from SMS Interactions If you opt into our mobile messaging program, we may collect your cell phone number, your carrier’s name, and the date, time, and content of messages you send to us.
See Mobile Messaging & SMS below for full details.
How We Use Your Information We use the information we collect to: Send you campaign updates, newsletters, event invitations, and volunteer opportunities you’ve requested Process donations and comply with campaign finance reporting requirements Respond to your inquiries and support requests Improve the website and understand how visitors use it Send text messages if you’ve opted into our SMS program We do not sell or rent your personal information.
We may share information with service providers, campaign vendors, compliance professionals, donation processors, email/SMS platforms, analytics providers, and other entities where necessary to operate the campaign, comply with law, process contributions, communicate with supporters, or otherwise support campaign activity described in this Policy.
SMS opt-in data and consent information will not be sold or shared for third-party marketing purposes.
We generally do not share supporter contact information (such as email lists) with outside political organizations, party committees, or allied campaigns.
In limited circumstances where such sharing serves the campaign’s purposes (for example, joint events, coalition activity, or required legal disclosure), any sharing will be consistent with this Policy.
You may contact us at hello@aprilforwashington.com with questions about specific data-sharing practices.
We may share information as required by law, including campaign finance disclosure obligations under Washington State election law.
Donor information submitted through our contribution pages is subject to public disclosure requirements applicable to political campaigns.
Mobile Messaging & SMS The following terms govern our Mobile Messaging Program.

Public Service Announcement Text Messages: Including legislative updates, member updates, and voter education.

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Donations & Financial Information Online contributions to April for Washington are processed through eFundraising Connections (efundraisingconnections.com), a third-party payment processing platform serving political campaigns.
We do not store your full credit card or banking information on our servers — payment processing is handled entirely by eFundraising Connections, which maintains its own security standards and privacy policy.
By making a contribution through our donation page, you are also subject to eFundraising Connections’ terms of service and privacy policy.
You must be 18 years of age or older to make a contribution.
Processing fees may apply and are subject to change at eFundraising Connections’ discretion.
All transactions are final and non-refundable except as required by law.
Washington State campaign finance law requires us to collect and publicly disclose certain donor information — including name, mailing address, employer, and occupation — for contributions above applicable reporting thresholds.
By making a contribution, you acknowledge that this information may appear in public campaign finance filings with the Washington State Public Disclosure Commission (PDC).
If you provide your email during the donation process, we may send you campaign-related communications.
You may unsubscribe at any time via the link at the bottom of any email.
We send emails through Mailchimp.
SMS opt-in data is never shared with third parties.
We use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymized data about site usage, including pages visited and time on site.
This data is aggregated and does not identify individual users.
The information is used solely to improve the campaign website and our outreach efforts.
To opt out of interest-based advertising more broadly, visit the Digital Advertising Alliance at aboutads.info/choices .
Email Communications If you sign up to receive email updates, we will send you newsletters, event invitations, fundraising appeals, and volunteer opportunities.
We send these emails through Mailchimp , which receives and stores your email address and engagement data (such as opens and clicks) in order to deliver and report on the messages.
You may unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email.
Unsubscribe requests are processed immediately.
We do not share or rent email subscriber lists with outside political organizations or with any third party for commercial or marketing purposes.
Email data is processed through Mailchimp as described in How We Use Your Information above.
Third-Party Services We use trusted third-party services to operate this website and campaign, including: Mailchimp — email marketing and newsletter delivery Google Analytics (GA4) — anonymized website traffic analysis Google Fonts — web font delivery Formspree — form submission processing (endorsement, volunteer, and contact forms) eFundraising Connections — online donation processing Cloudflare — content delivery network and traffic security Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy.
We encourage you to review them if you have questions about how they handle data.
We do not authorize these services to use your information for their own marketing purposes.
Embedded Content Pages on this site may include embedded content such as videos, social media posts, or images hosted by third parties — for example, news article previews that link to coverage on outside publications, or images served from the Washington State House Republicans’ official media library.
Embedded content behaves as if you had visited the originating website directly — those sites may set their own cookies, collect data about you, and track your interactions with the embedded content.
Security We use industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including SSL/TLS encryption for data transmitted through this site.
However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.
While we work hard to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security and encourage you to use caution when sharing sensitive information online.
Your Rights & Opt-Out You have the right to: Unsubscribe from email communications at any time via the link in any email we send Opt out of SMS messages by texting STOP to any message we send Request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@aprilforwashington.com or by mail at the address below.
Limitations on deletion requests.
Deletion requests do not apply to records the campaign is legally required to maintain or disclose, including campaign finance records (per Washington State PDC retention requirements — typically 3 to 5 years), contribution records, refund records, compliance records, and records needed to document SMS consent, email opt-in, unsubscribe, or opt-out status under TCPA and CAN-SPAM.
Deletion requests may also be limited where data is needed to protect the campaign’s legal interests or to respond to law enforcement requests.
We will honor deletion requests for any data not subject to these retention obligations.
California Residents If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to request deletion, and the right to opt out of any sale or sharing of your personal information.
We do not sell your personal information.
To exercise your California rights, contact us at hello@aprilforwashington.com .
Children This website is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.
If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at hello@aprilforwashington.com and we will promptly remove it.
Changes to This Privacy Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
Material changes will be communicated via the website and, where appropriate, via email to subscribers.
