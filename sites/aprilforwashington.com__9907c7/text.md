The Tri‑Cities economy runs on reliable, affordable power and the federal cleanup mission at Hanford — thousands of high‑skill, family‑wage jobs that ripple through every neighborhood, school, and small business in the district.
In Olympia, April is a consistent voice for protecting that economy: standing up for the Hanford workforce and the federal commitment behind it, defending hydropower and nuclear, and opposing state energy and tax policy that drives up costs for working families and puts Tri‑Cities jobs at risk.
