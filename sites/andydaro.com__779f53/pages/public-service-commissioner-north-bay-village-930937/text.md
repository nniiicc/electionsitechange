Commissioner of North Bay Village -
Elected as Harbor Island Commissioner in 2022; Key initiatives:
- As a Commissioner of North Bay Village, Andy Daro has helped drive over $2 billion in new development by supporting zoning modernization, infrastructure improvements, and strategic partnerships—positioning the city as one of Miami-Dade’s fastest-growing waterfront communities.
- The Island Walk Initiative enhances walkability along North Bay Village’s waterfront by improving pedestrian paths, streetscapes, and connectivity—creating a safer, more vibrant community.
- Led numerous public art initiatives through the Community Enhancement Board, helping beautify North Bay Village and shape a new identity and soul for the community.
Meet Andy Daro
North Bay Village Commissioner
Andy Daro is a dedicated public servant, business leader, and advocate for the future of North Bay Village.
As Commissioner, he brings a unique combination of private-sector experience, strategic vision, and a deep commitment to the community he calls home.
Known for his results-driven leadership and collaborative approach, Andy is focused on positioning North Bay Village as one of South Florida’s most vibrant and forward-thinking waterfront communities.
About Andy
Andy Daro is the founder of The Daro Team at Compass, with over a decade of experience in luxury real estate across Miami and New York.
Throughout his career, he has led complex negotiations, managed high-value transactions, and built a reputation for integrity, execution, and results.
This professional background translates directly into his role as Commissioner—bringing a business-minded, solutions-oriented approach to city leadership, with a focus on long-term planning, responsible growth, and community impact.
A proud resident of North Bay Village, Andy is deeply invested in the success of the community.
Outside of his professional work, he is a passionate traveler, musician, and lifelong soccer enthusiast—reflecting his belief in a well-rounded, connected, and culturally vibrant life.
Commissioner Priorities
Andy’s leadership is centered on delivering measurable results while building a stronger, more connected North Bay Village.
His approach is grounded in transparency, collaboration, and forward-thinking planning.
The Island Walk: A Signature Vision
One of Andy’s key initiatives is The Island Walk—a transformative waterfront project designed to redefine how residents and visitors experience North Bay Village.
The vision includes:
- Public Waterfront Access – Expanding access to Biscayne Bay with scenic viewpoints and open spaces
- Health & Wellness – Dedicated areas for walking, jogging, and outdoor activity
- Economic Activation – Driving foot traffic to support local businesses and enhance tourism
Key Initiatives & Accomplishments
Infrastructure & Resiliency
Advancing critical infrastructure improvements, including flood mitigation and roadway upgrades—highlighted by the long-overdue resurfacing of Harbor Island.
Economic Growth
Helping guide and approve nearly $2 billion in new development, positioning North Bay Village as a premier destination for thoughtful, high-quality investment.
Walkability & Connectivity
Leading comprehensive sidewalk and crosswalk improvements across the three-island community, creating safer, more accessible streets.
Quality of Life
Enhancing public spaces through park improvements, public art installations, and community-focused programming that enriches daily life for residents.
A Vision for North Bay Village
Andy’s vision is clear: a resilient, walkable, and thriving waterfront community that balances growth with quality of life.
By combining real-world experience with a deep commitment to public service, he continues to work toward a North Bay Village that is innovative, inclusive, and built for the future.
