A Wave of New Development
A new generation of projects is redefining the skyline and elevating the standard of living in North Bay Village.
Notable developments include:
- Shoma Bay Residences
- Continuum Club & Residences Miami
- Pagani Residences – the Village’s first branded luxury project
- 7918 West Drive & 7913 West Drive Marina View
- Waterfront developments by MG Development & Prosper Group
In addition, Related Group is expected to introduce a landmark project—widely rumored to be a Ritz-Carlton Residences—further reinforcing the Village’s position in the luxury market.
Transformational Growth & Master Planning
Beyond individual developments, large-scale planning is shaping the future of the entire community.
Sunbeam Properties, the largest landowner in North Bay Village, is preparing a transformative mixed-use project spanning over 12 acres.
Early concepts suggest a dynamic urban plan featuring residential towers, retail, and public spaces—potentially including buildings rising up to 60+ stories.
This signals a shift from fragmented growth to a cohesive, long-term vision for a true waterfront destination.
Public Investment & Community Infrastructure
The City of North Bay Village is investing alongside private development to support long-term livability and resilience.
- A new City Hall will serve as a modern civic anchor
- Plans for a new Community Center are underway
- A partnership with the Argentine Football Association (AFA) will introduce a world-class training facility and youth academy
These initiatives reflect a commitment to building not just density—but a complete, connected community.
