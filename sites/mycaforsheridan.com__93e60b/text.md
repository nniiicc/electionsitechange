What matters most to Sheridan?
Why I’m showing up I’m Myca Sturtevant, a small business owner, mother, and Sheridan neighbor who believes the best government is the smallest one, and the one closest to home.
For over a decade, my husband Nate and I have put down roots here, nurturing our businesses, our church, and our community.
As the owner of Whirly Girl Flowers, I’ve learned to listen first, work carefully with what’s available, and tend to the details that make something memorable.
That’s exactly how I’ll serve Sheridan.
I’m running because good values without action aren’t enough.
As an independent Republican, I’m accountable to my neighbors in Sheridan, no one else.
