Richard Gessling: For Opponent: Unknown Learn about Farmers and Ranchers Richard Gessling: Committed Leader for Carbon County I grew up in a family where volunteering and neighborliness weren't just talked about—they were lived out through my parents' example.
From an early age, I learned that many people face real challenges and sometimes just need a helping hand.
As a health care professional and practicing dentist for over 28 years, I've seen those struggles up close—and I know that many of them still persist today.
I moved to Red Lodge in 1988, when the sky was red and live embers were falling from the Yellowstone fires.
I opened my dental practice in May of 1989 and continued practicing through 2017.
I took great joy in caring for people, and my staff shared that commitment.
Those were the early days of dental insurance, both private and Medicaid.
I joined the Red Lodge Lions Club after being invited and was drawn to their vision for Lion's Park.
Over the years, I helped maintain the park, replaced old basketball hoops, participated in highway cleanups and Halloween events, and served burgers to support the newly established Red Lodge Area Community Foundation Fun Run.
I have served on the Chamber of Commerce Board, the Carbon County Historical Board, the Red Lodge Rendezvous Board, and the Red Lodge City Council from 1993 to 2003.
I was Mayor from 2005 to 2006 and later served on the Beartooth Billings Clinic Foundation Board and the First Interstate Bank Advisory Board.
I was also District Governor of the Montana Lions from 2015 to 2016.
I have been a member of the Rocky Fork Free Trappers Muzzleloading Club since 1991.
I've enjoyed canoeing the Missouri, Yellowstone, and Marias Rivers, often camping in mountain man dress and traditional shelters.
I love gardening and have planted fruit trees everywhere I've lived.
Hunting with my muzzleloader has been another passion of mine, allowing me to provide food for my family.
I married my wife, Jean, in 1999.
My daughter, Melissa, graduated from Red Lodge High School in 1993 and is now a dentist in Atlanta, Georgia.
Jean's son, Nathan, attended elementary school and spent summers in Red Lodge.
He is now a commercial photographer in Seattle, where he lives with his wife, Virginia, and our granddaughter, Flora.
Every once in a while, I wish I had lived as a trapper in Jim Bridger's brigade during that era.
My favorite book is The Journal of a Trapper by Osborne Russell.
