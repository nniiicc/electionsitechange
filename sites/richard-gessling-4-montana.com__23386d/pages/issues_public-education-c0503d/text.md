Montana's Constitutional Promise "It is the goal of the people to establish a system of education which will develop the full educational potential of each person.
Equality of educational opportunity is guaranteed to each person of the state." — Montana Constitution, Article X, Section 1 I believe we are falling short of this goal.
It's time we made a real commitment to fulfilling this constitutional promise to every Montana student.

Early Childhood Education Currently only 40% of Montana kindergartners are ready for kindergarten.
State-supported early childhood education would change that — and reduce expensive special-education placements down the road.
Supporting Our Teachers Montana teachers earn one of the lowest salaries in the U.S.
Many leave the state by their third year.
I'll support raising salaries so we retain experienced teachers in every classroom.
Simplifying School Funding The current funding scheme is convoluted, forcing districts to chase grants with strings attached.
I'll support giving districts the funding they need upfront so schools can focus on kids.
Protecting Rural Schools Rural schools are the heart of our communities.
I'll fight efforts to consolidate or close them, and push for the state support superintendents and teachers need to thrive.
Why Teacher Pay Matters The "Stars" program raised pay for first-time teachers this year — but every other teacher was left out .
Many don't reach Montana's livable income until mid-career, and many leave the state by their third year.
This turnover hurts our students and our communities.
Our children deserve experienced, stable teachers in their classrooms — and that means paying them what they're worth.
