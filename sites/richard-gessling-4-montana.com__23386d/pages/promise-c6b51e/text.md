Protect what makes Montana, Montana.
These are the commitments I'm making to you and to Carbon County.
As your representative in Helena, I will fight for these values and principles every single day.
Strengthen Rural Public Schools Fair funding, cost-of-living adjustments, and real solutions to recruit and keep teachers, including affordable housing.
Stop Shifting Burdens to Local Taxpayers End the practice of shifting costs onto small businesses and agricultural families who are already stretched thin.
Keep Public Lands Public Fight to keep public lands in public hands and protect our access to the land and water that define our way of life.
Support Strong Families Restore childcare support and invest in early childhood education so young families can afford to stay and thrive here.
Real Solutions to Affordability Oversight of rising utility rates, meaningful property tax reform, and ensuring seniors can stay in their homes.
I oppose a sales tax that would hit working families the hardest.
Protect Rural Healthcare Protecting rural healthcare isn't optional—it's essential to our community's wellbeing.
Accountable Government Make government accountable to everyday Montanans—not just decisions made in Helena.
Regular Town Halls and Listening Sessions Hold regular town halls and listening sessions because you deserve to be heard, not just during election season, but every day I serve.
A Government That Listens I pledge to hold regular town halls and listening sessions—because you deserve to be heard, not just during election season, but every day I serve.
Get Involved Together, we can build a stronger, more prosperous Carbon County where rural values are respected, families can thrive, and our way of life is protected.
