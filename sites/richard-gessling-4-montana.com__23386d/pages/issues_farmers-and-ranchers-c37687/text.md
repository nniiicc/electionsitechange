Farmers Face Unique Cost Challenges Our farmers provide all our food — yet their cut of food prices is the lowest in the chain.
We should acknowledge the unique pressures they carry.
Fuel & Fertilizer Costs Global conflict has drastically raised the cost of fuel and the fertilizers farmers depend on.
These essential inputs are squeezing margins thinner every season.
Equipment & Parts Tariffs have driven up the cost of major equipment and repair parts.
Manufacturers' stranglehold on parts and manuals — combined with new digital tech — makes repairs harder than ever.
Healthcare Costs When ACA subsidies weren't extended, premiums for many farming families ballooned 2–4x, with higher deductibles and copays.
Farmers are considered high risk by insurers.
Market Challenges Federal decisions like inviting Venezuelan beef into the market help meat packers — not Montana producers.
Our beef ranchers are paying the price.
A Crisis We Must Address Because of the stress of these policies and costs, last year our farmers had record high bankruptcies and a record high suicide rate .
The lack of mental health services in rural areas was a significant factor.
Our farmers are getting the wrong end of the stick.
I hope you'll thank our farmers — and I'll be diligent in Helena to support Montana farmers with the resources and policies they need to thrive.
What I'll Fight For Real, common-sense policies that put Montana farmers and ranchers first.
The Right to Repair Act I support legislation allowing farmers and independent mechanics to purchase parts and access manuals — reducing costs and crippling downtime during planting and harvest.
State-of-Origin Labeling Labeling food with its state of origin helps consumers support local farmers and gives Montana producers a fair shot in the marketplace.
Restore Healthcare Subsidies We must restore and expand healthcare subsidies so farming families can afford to stay healthy without losing the farm.
