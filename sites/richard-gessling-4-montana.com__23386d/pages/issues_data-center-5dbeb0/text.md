Richard Gessling is against building new data centers in Montana.
Richard Gessling Against Opponent For Understanding Data Centers Data centers are not a new development.
They have been used by technology-dependent companies for over 25 years.
When you search online, your inquiry is routed to a data center.
Your cloud storage is not in the sky, it is in a data center.
These centers have gotten bigger over time.
Artificial Intelligence is dependent on them to be able to know all knowledge that has been discovered or developed by man.
There are a multitude of problematic issues associated with A.I.
The first that comes to mind is that they cannot yet determine fact from fiction or truth from lies.
There is a fear factor that Artificial Intelligence will take over humanity and destroy our civilization.
Science fiction you might say, but looking from the Dick Tracy watch to our current smart watches, makes us wonder what tomorrow might bring.
The Problems with Large Data Centers The larger data centers have presented us with a multitude of problems.
Here are the core concerns.
Water Use Large data centers consume enormous amounts of water for cooling.
In Montana, we have already adjudicated more water rights than we have surface water.
The needs of farmers and citizens using municipal water systems should be prioritized above data center cooling.
Power Strain The power needed to operate these huge facilities is straining our electrical grid, probably causing blackouts during high usage.
Households are then hit with rate increases that affect affordability for 95% of families.
Privacy Threat These companies want to accumulate everything they know about you and sell it to other companies or the government.
With large data centers, the surveillance of everyday life will only get worse.

Limitations Artificial Intelligence cannot yet determine fact from fiction or truth from lies.

The impact of the water discharged from data centers to the environment is suspect and unknown.
The power needed to operate these huge data centers is straining our power grids, probably causing blackouts during high usage from all sources.
In the west, quite a bit of power has come from coal, bringing environmental concerns, and about less water in the reservoirs to power turbines.
Renewables, solar and wind, are making great gains, but current Federal Government policies have slowed progress.
The companies providing the electrical power need to make more power but cause rate increases to households.
High rate increases affect affordability issues for 95% of families in the United States.
Why Are Companies Building Them?
Why are companies building such large data centers with the associated water and power issues?
These companies want to accumulate everything they know about you and locate that information so they can sell it to other companies or the government.
This is their profit incentive.
These companies can't be trusted to keep their word because they dictate the signing of non disclosure agreements with the governing agencies involved.
It has always amazed me that companies want us to review their twenty-page privacy policies when we already have no privacy.
Your phone has GPS, your TV is connected, everything is connected to the web, and on and on.
With these large data centers it will only get worse.
As we are aware history repeats itself.
Spying on each other is nothing new, and each time it surfaces in a new and unknown way we should take notice.
When an "ICE" employee tells a person we have a data base about everyone, they mean it.
"I am against building new data centers in Montana.
I want to see boundaries legislated that will protect all citizens.
I will fight for the rights of all citizens to secure their right to privacy guaranteed by the Fourth Amendment of the Constitution of the United States." — Richard Gessling, candidate for District 55 in the Montana House My opponent Brad Barker is in favor of building data centers in Montana.
We need to be vigilant in reminding our governments about our right to privacy.
Protect Montana's Future When you vote for me, you're voting for a representative who will prioritize our water, our energy grid, and our constitutional right to privacy over corporate data center development.
