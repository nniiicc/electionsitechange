Houston, TX—Today, Congresswoman Lizzie Fletcher (TX-07) released the following statement following the Democratic primary election:
“I am honored and proud to be the Democratic nominee to represent Texas’ diverse and dynamic Seventh Congressional District in 2022.
I am so grateful for the support of a broad coalition of people across our community who have made it possible to serve our community and our country at this historic time, and I am committed to bringing the values of our district to Washington, D.C.”
