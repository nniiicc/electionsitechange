Houston, TX–Today, Congresswoman Lizzie Fletcher (TX-07) launched her first digital ad of the 2026 general election cycle, “Costs.” The 30- and 15-second cuts will run on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and streaming platforms through Election Day, November 3, as part of a six-figure investment to help elect Democrats up and down the ballot.

In “Costs,” Fletcher speaks to voters’ concerns about the chaos Donald Trump is causing and how much it costs.
Fletcher reminds voters that they have the power to change things by voting for Democrats.
