Tim Hayes
A Fighter for the 143rd District
Bucks County Born and Raised
Tim has spent his career fighting for the people of Bucks County and the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, as a soldier, councilman, and environmental policy expert.
Now he’s running to take on Harrisburg and deliver real relief for working families.
Endorsed byGovernor Josh Shapiro
The Issues
Affordability
Energy, groceries, and housing are out of reach for working families.
Tim will work to expand energy capacity, lower food costs with local farmers, and keep corporations from buying up homes.
Conservation
An outdoorsman and environmental policy expert, Tim will protect the district’s open space, invest in state parks and forests, support the Clean Streams Fund, and eliminate PFAS.
Public Safety
Tim has secured funding for Dublin’s police and volunteer fire departments.
He will push for sustainable, long-term funding for first responders across the 143rd.
Protecting Access to Healthcare
Having lost his own coverage, Tim will work to keep healthcare out of private equity hands, expand Medicaid, protect pre-existing conditions, and cap the cost of insulin.
Education
A product of public schools through graduate school, Tim supports fully funding public education and keeping public dollars in public schools.
Raising the Minimum Wage and Creating Jobs
Tim will be a champion for a $15 minimum wage and, as author of the VETSS Act, will keep working to invest in Pennsylvania’s workforce.
In Depth
Energy prices are spiking, grocery bills are rising, and accessible housing is out of reach for working folks.
Families in the 143rd District are feeling the pain of failed economic policies of career politicians.
Making life affordable for the people of Pennsylvania isn’t a campaign talking point, it’s something Tim’s seen and experienced firsthand.
There isn’t a single person that’s not feeling the effects of the affordability crisis.
To address energy costs, as state representative, Tim will introduce and support legislation that will expand energy capacity on our grid with a more diversified energy portfolio, bolster low-income energy assistance programs, and improve energy efficiency for the industrial and commercial sectors.
Addressing food costs, Tim will work with farmers to address issues with existing agricultural supply chains that are driving up costs at the grocery store.
And for housing, he’ll expand access to home repair and weatherization programs, crack down on large corporations buying up every home and inflating prices, and work with townships and boroughs across the district to create a regional development plan, developing where it makes sense while preserving our rural open space too.
Tim is an outdoorsman and an environmental policy expert who loves hiking at Nockamixon or Peace Valley.
Protecting our district’s open space is his top priority.
He’s also focused on diversifying our energy portfolio to reduce carbon emissions while lowering costs for families, putting guardrails on data centers to protect our communities and energy grid, and updating that grid to improve our state’s energy efficiency and further lower utility costs.
Beyond energy issues, he will work to protect our air, water and land.
He’ll introduce legislation to make critical investments into our state parks and forests, increase support for the Clean Streams Fund, eliminate PFAS, and bolster investments in the Agricultural Innovation Grant Program.
Tim attended public schools for his entire education from pre-k through high school, public tech school, college, and graduate school.
He completely supports fully funding our public schools so that every child, whether they want to enter a trade or earn a PhD, can get a great education suited to their needs.
Tim will increase state support for recruiting and retaining educators by cutting red tape for certification and will fight to increase wages.
He’ll fight to increase investments in career and technical education to support participation in the trades.
And he’ll stand up to any effort to siphon off public dollars for charter or private schools.
Tim knows a lot of folks feel excluded from politics.
Our system is imbalanced in favor of the powerful instead of everyday people.
Tim has never let that stop him from fighting for our community, so when he gets to Harrisburg he’ll advocate for a slate of policies to help level the playing field and restore public trust in government.
That includes enacting contribution limits for state elections, calling for an amendment to overturn the Citizens' United decision, term limits for legislators, a ban on unreported gifts to lawmakers, and withholding pay from legislators if the state budget is not passed in a timely manner.
He has also taken a No Corporate PAC money pledge.
Tim understands gun safety from his time in the National Guard, and he respects the right of all law-abiding citizens to own firearms.
He supports common-sense gun safety measures, including background checks and red flag laws, to keep guns out of the hands of anyone who poses a threat to themselves or others.
Tim’s lived through the tough experience of losing his health coverage and believes no one else should experience that.
He’ll fight to keep our healthcare system out of the hands of for-profit private equity companies, expand coverage for programs like Medicaid, protect coverage for people with disabilities or pre-existing conditions, and cap the cost of insulin and other essential drugs.
In the 143rd District and across Pennsylvania, police, fire, and EMS companies are struggling to get by, due to a lack of funding and resources.
Volunteer fire companies in particular may have to revert to becoming paid companies, which will cost millions of dollars.
Tim has worked in Dublin to get its police and volunteer fire departments more funding.
For the police department, he secured new handguns, holiday pay for part-time officers, and he even personally donates his council check back to the police officers so that they can buy new clothing.
For the fire department, he used the savings from cutting wasteful spending to create a new budget line item that directly invests into the firefighters.
And for Point Pleasant EMS, Dublin has donated over $10,000 for new equipment.
As state representative, Tim will continue his staunch support of first responders and create more opportunities to invest in the police, fire and EMS departments in the 143rd.
He’ll push to develop a plan for sustainable, long-term funding for volunteer companies.
He’ll fight to secure additional funding for police departments to buy new gear and retain officers.
And he’ll help EMS companies in the district retain their call boxes so that they don’t lose municipal funding.
The minimum wage in our state is the same as it was when Tim’s mom worked minimum wage jobs to pay their family’s bills.
He’ll be a champion for raising the minimum wage in our state to $15 an hour.
He’ll also push the state government to invest in its workers and make Pennsylvania the economic powerhouse of the country.
He’s already authored House Bill 894, the Veterans Entering the Sustainability Sector (VETSS) Program Act.
This bill would establish a new program to train veterans in jobs in clean energy, land conservation, water quality, and energy efficiency.
As our next State Representative, Tim will continue fighting for workers in Pennsylvania.
He’ll protect collective bargaining rights, oppose union-busting, and support prevailing wage and project labor agreement standards, and he’ll work to expand access to apprenticeship programs and trade schools.
Tim truly believes that the government should never interfere with a patient’s reproductive health choices.
At a time when individual rights are under attack nationwide, he will protect access to reproductive healthcare and defend the right to privacy here in Pennsylvania.
Tim will stand up for our democracy against any attempts to disenfranchise citizens or make it harder for them to cast a ballot.
He supports expanded mail-in ballot drop boxes, early voting, same-day voter registration, and pre-registration for teens so that they’ll be ready to cast a ballot when they turn 18.
He supports open primaries, giving registered independents more say in their representation.
Meet Tim Hayes
- Lifelong public servant
- Elected as a councilman in 2019 and has served Dublin Borough for six years
- Served in the Pennsylvania Army National Guard for six years
Tim has been dedicated to serving the people of Pennsylvania his entire life.
The son of a union sheet metal worker and Wawa cashier, the values of hard work, community, and looking out for the little guy were instilled in him at a young age.
Before Tim was even a legal adult, he knew that he was ready to step up and serve his community and Commonwealth.
When he was 17 years old, he enlisted in the Pennsylvania Army National Guard, serving as a Field Artillery Automated Tactical Data Systems Specialist.
He served with folks from all walks of life, united by a shared mission of service.
His unit was activated for state active duty several times, where he worked alongside the Philadelphia Police Department to help with crowd control during the protests in 2020.
- Enlisted at 17
- Elected at 21
- Dublin Council President
- Environmental policy expert
Tim took that mindset of service he learned in the military and ran for Dublin Borough Council.
He was elected in 2019 at the age of 21, becoming the youngest elected official in Bucks County at the time.
Back then, Dublin was a Republican stronghold without a single elected Democrat.
He helped turn the tide and flipped Dublin blue in 2023, where he was elected by his peers as Council President.
Dublin Borough has grown to be one of the primary economic hubs in Bucks County.
By the end of this decade, it’s projected to have millions of dollars in private sector investments, welcoming dozens of new businesses and nearly 200 new housing units.
As Council President, Tim has helped to eliminate outdated regulations, reduce wasteful spending, make critical investments into Dublin’s police and volunteer fire departments, improve sustainability, and upgrade infrastructure.
And he did all of this WITHOUT raising taxes.
Tim also works for an environmental advocacy group as their State Policy Manager.
He’s spent countless days in Harrisburg in the halls of the Capitol Building advocating for pro-environment, pro-labor legislation.
He’s helped to secure millions of dollars for land conservation, water decontamination, clean energy manufacturing and deployment, home weatherization and energy efficiency, and sustainable agriculture.
With what little free time he has, Tim enjoys spending time outdoors hiking and volunteering at a local farm that donates its crops to folks in need.
He enjoys going on adventures with his fiancé Talyn and exploring the beauty of the natural world.
Tim is a veteran of the Pennsylvania Army National Guard.
