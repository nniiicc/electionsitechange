ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT This statement was last updated on March 27, 2026.
At Desch for House, we strive to make our campaign website, www.deschforhouse.com , accessible to everyone, including individuals with disabilities.
Understanding Web Accessibility Web accessibility means that all visitors, including those with disabilities, can access and enjoy our site with the same ease as others.
We aim to achieve this through our website's design and the use of assistive technologies.
Accessibility Enhancements on This Site We have made improvements to this site in line with WCAG 2.1 guidelines, achieving an AA compliance level.
Our content is designed to work seamlessly with assistive technologies like screen readers.
To enhance accessibility, we have: Utilized accessibility tools to identify and address potential issues Specified the primary language of the site Organized content in a logical order Created clear headings for all pages Provided alternative text for images Ensured sufficient color contrast for readability Minimized motion effects on the website Made all media content accessible Feedback and Assistance If you encounter any accessibility challenges on our website or need further assistance, please reach out to Jenny@DeschForHouse.com .
