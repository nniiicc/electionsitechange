My father, a Holocaust survivor, dedicated his life to serving as a naval officer.
I earned my engineering degree from Stanford through an ROTC scholarship and later pursued my M.S. in Technology and Policy at MIT.
I served as a Captain in the USAFR, with three years of active duty and thirteen years in the Inactive Reserve.
My professional journey includes being the COO of AND1 Basketball, where I played a key role in transforming the company from a startup into a prominent apparel and footwear brand.
Additionally, I was the founding COO of B-Lab, a nonprofit advocating for B Corporations and ethical business practices.
My career also includes impactful roles in education, having worked as a chemistry teacher in North Philadelphia as part of the Teach for America Corps.
I served as President and COO/CFO for Springboard Collaborative, a nonprofit dedicated to enhancing early childhood literacy in underserved communities across the country.
I am a proud mother, living with my family in Chester County for 30 years.
Notably, I made history as the first woman elected to Pennsylvania’s 6th Congressional District and the first Democrat since the Civil War.
In Congress, I am honored to serve on the Armed Services Committee and the Select Committee on Intelligence.
