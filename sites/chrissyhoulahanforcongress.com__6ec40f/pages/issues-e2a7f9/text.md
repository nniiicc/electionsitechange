What I believe and why it matters
It’s the honor of my lifetime to serve as your representative in Washington, D.C.
Since 2019, I have prioritized the transparency of my actions, and accountability to my community, Commonwealth, and country.
My voting record is publicly available, and I’m very proud to be a three-time Democracy Award winner from the Congressional Management Foundation.
Progress has been made but there is still work to be done.
Too many of us struggle to pay for basic needs, too many of us worry about public health and safety, and too many of us wonder if our next generation will be able to fulfill their own version of the American Dream.
Please know that I hear you, I see you, and I won’t stop working for you.
From issues of affordability to issues of national security and foreign policy, I’m proud of my record and my service to our country.
