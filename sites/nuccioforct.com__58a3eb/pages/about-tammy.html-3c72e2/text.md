State Representative Tammy Nuccio is a tireless advocate for Connecticut taxpayers and the proud representative of the 53rd General Assembly District, serving the communities of Tolland, Vernon, and Willington.
She was elected to her first term in November 2020.
As Ranking Member of the General Assembly & Appropriations Committee, Rep.
Nuccio has earned a reputation as Connecticut's leading budget watchdog.
Drawing on more than three decades of experience in finance and her professional background as a financial analyst currently working for Cigna, she is known for carefully scrutinizing state spending, demanding accountability for taxpayers, and fighting to ensure their dollars are spent responsibly.
The Appropriations Committee oversees the state budgeting process.
Other matters under the committee’s jurisdiction include - state employees’ salaries, benefits and retirement, teachers’ retirement and veterans’ pensions and collective bargaining agreements and arbitration awards for all state employees and Medicaid funding and federal waivers.
Rep.
Nuccio also serves on the Insurance and Real Estate Committee, which legislate all matters relating to insurance and real estate law, and the Executive and Legislative Nominations Committee, which reviews all non-judicial executive and legislative nominations.
Never one to shy away from difficult conversations, Rep.
Nuccio has become a respected and outspoken voice at the State Capitol.
Whether challenging wasteful spending, advocating for families and seniors, improving access to mental health services, pushing back against bills that would jeopardize public safety, or standing up for the needs of her communities, she brings a direct, principled approach to public service and isn't afraid to say what others are thinking.
She has been appointed to several Committees including the CT 2Gen Initiative, the Domestic Violence Advisory Committee, the Governor's Workforce Council, the Poverty Task Force, the Transforming Children’s Behavioral Health committee and the Select Committee on Special Education.
In 2022, her first term, Rep.
Nuccio was selected as a lead Republican to work on the House Priority bill, HB5001, which established stronger mental health provisions for children including the opening of three Urgent Care Crisis centers.
She continues to prioritize mental health initiatives including expanding the Connecticut State Police CRISIS program to every Troop in Connecticut.
Rep.
Nuccio has been a staunch advocate for property tax relief continuing to push for more adequate funding of education at the state level and programs to help total and permanently disabled Veterans.
She has pushed for tax relief for senior citizens and working-class families while prioritizing programs that help people become independent and move off state programs.
A former member and chairwoman of the Tolland Town Council, Rep.
Nuccio remains deeply involved in her community and has dedicated countless hours to local organizations and volunteer service.
Her leadership, integrity, and unwavering commitment to the people she serves have made her a trusted voice both in Hartford and throughout northeastern Connecticut.
She is a current board member of the Manchester Community College Foundation and a former board member of the Eastern Highland Health District.
Nuccio holds an Associate’s degree from Manchester Community College, a Bachelor’s and Master’s Degree in Business Administration from Bay Path University.
She is a more than 40-year resident of Tolland where she resides with her husband and where they raised their three daughters.
