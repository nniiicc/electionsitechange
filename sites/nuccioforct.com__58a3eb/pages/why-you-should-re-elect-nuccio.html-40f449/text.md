Proven Results
As your State Representative, Tammy secured $27 million for the 53rd District through state bonding, education grants and program funding.
She has secured bonding to support fire station rebuilds, Tolland mutual aid upgrades, road improvements, law enforcement body cams, and repairs and upgrades for the Vernon Housing Authority, sewers, dams and Fox Hill Tower.
Tammy increased Vernon's educations funding by nearly $3 million and protected Tolland and Willington from $1.5 million in state cuts.
She passed legislation saving Tolland $6.5 million in taxes after the Birch Grove School construction audit.
Focused on the needs of all residents, she helped secure $700k in grants for school-based mental health services and launched the CRISIS program at Troop C for social worker services.
Your Life, Your Money
Promises Kept
As ranking member of the Appropriations Committee, Tammy served as the financial watchdog for the state, helping to craft a bipartisan budget that ensured the first income tax cut since 1991.
Tammy played an integral role in passing substantial income tax relief for seniors and property tax relief for disabled veterans.
Focused on residents' security, she co-sponsored legislation imposing tougher penalties for street takeovers and helped restore the tools law enforcement needs to do their job effectively.
Your Town, Your Community
Prioritizing Reform
For Connecticut to be a more affordable state to live in we must achieve continued tax relief, and Tammy is committed to reducing your tax burden with a more efficient, effective government.
Tammy's top priorities in the next two years include establishing Equitable Education Financing for all towns, allowing for strong local schools without overburdening property taxpayers, legislation to improve public safety by addressing crimes committed by repeat offenders, and expanding the CRISIS program throughout the state.
