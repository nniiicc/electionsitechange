Join the Campaign View the Platform Her Story Regina's work has always started with people.
Before running for State Representative, Regina was already doing the kind of work that makes neighborhoods stronger: listening to families, guiding students, standing with seniors, and helping residents navigate everyday challenges with dignity.
As a longtime Detroit educator, she saw how state decisions show up in classrooms, homes, and neighborhood corridors.
She understands that school funding, workforce training, housing stability, insurance costs, public safety, and senior services are not separate issues for families trying to build a better life.
They are connected.
That is why Regina is running a campaign focused on practical delivery.
District 4 deserves a representative who knows the blocks, respects the people, and will fight for resources without waiting for permission to care.
