About: Meet Tim Moffitt With old names like Corn, Laughter, Hill, McCarson, and Willis on his family tree, Tim Moffitt’s roots run more than 250 years deep in the mountains of Western North Carolina.
Tim was raised in Henderson County and is a graduate of West Henderson High School.
Although a college career wasn’t in the cards back then, Tim inherited an entrepreneurial spirit from his father Bobby, who, after serving in the U.S.
Army, ran a local pest control company for more than 40 years.
His dad also taught him the value of faith, hard work, and common sense — virtues that are too often in short supply among politicians these days.
Tim is now a successful, self-made businessman who manages the day-to-day operations of several businesses, including a small real estate firm, a mountaintop vacation and events venue, and Moffitt International, an executive search and management consulting firm.
For more than three decades, he has assembled executive management teams and overseen a wide-array of high-profile projects for governments, Fortune 500 companies, and charitable organizations locally and all over the world.
Tim has been endorsed by: • National Rifle Association • GrassrootsNC • The North Carolina Chamber of Commerce • The National Federation of Independent Business • Associated Builders and Contractors of the Carolinas • North Carolina Troopers Association • NC Association of Realtors • Republican National Hispanic Assembly of North Carolina Tim and his wife Dina have five adult sons, two of whom are Air Force veterans.
