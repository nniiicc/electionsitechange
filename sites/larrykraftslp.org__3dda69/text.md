A Climate Champion

As a State Representative and before that a City Council member, I work hard to listen, understand people’s priorities, and create good policy.
I love representing St.
Louis Park and our values and leadership at the state level.
Among our state’s highest priorities must be climate change.
It has to be a lens through which we view all legislation – and the stakes are high; we have to get climate policy right.
But climate change is also an incredible opportunity – to create good jobs, reduce pollution and improve public health, and address social inequalities.
And I look forward to working on these and many other issues to improve the lives of all people in our community.
As your Representative, I’ll continue to use tools I’ve learned in high-tech leadership positions, as well as my nonprofit and City Council work.
I bring to state government a focus on listening, a commitment to responsiveness and transparency, and the ability and curiosity to dig in and understand complex issues.
I hope to continue representing our shared values in House District 46A, and I look forward to earning your support.
