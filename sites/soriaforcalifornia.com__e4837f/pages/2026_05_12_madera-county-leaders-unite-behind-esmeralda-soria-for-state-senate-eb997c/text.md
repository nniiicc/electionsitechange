Endorsers include: Madera County Supervisor Leticia Gonzalez Madera County Supervisor Bobby Macaulay Madera Mayor Pro Tem Jose Rodriguez Madera City Councilmember Anita Evans Madera City Councilmember Artemio Villegas Madera City Councilmember Elsa Mejia Madera City Councilmember Steven Montes Madera Unified School District Trustee Lucy Salazar Madera Unified School District Trustee Nadeem Ahmad Madera Unified School District Trustee Joetta Fleak State Center Community College Trustee Destiny Rodriguez Madera County Fair Director Isabel Barreras Madera County Farm Bureau Director Jay Mahil Madera County Democratic Party Chair Martha Garcia These endorsements reflect the growing, unified support behind Soria’s mission to bring a proven Central Valley voice to the State Senate.
“The Valley will win when our leaders are united and fighting for the same thing, a better future for our families and our communities.
I’m deeply honored to have the support of so many dedicated Madera County leaders, and I will carry that trust with me to Sacramento,” said Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria.
Soria’s campaign has secured endorsements from over 100 elected officials across California, including Merced City Councilmember Mike Harris and Merced Mayor Matthew Serrato, reinforcing her sweeping support across the Central Valley.
