Here’s what we’ve accomplished together: Health Care Secured $300 million for the Distressed Hospital Loan Program, providing emergency funding for hospitals on the brink of closure.
Led the fight to reopen Madera Community Hospital, securing $57 million to restore healthcare access for thousands of residents.
Public Safety Delivered $4 million to build Merced’s new Fire Station & Emergency Operations Center, strengthening disaster response and emergency readiness.
Secured $3 million for Kerman’s new Public Safety Center, ensuring law enforcement has the tools needed to keep our neighborhoods safe.
Flood Recovery & Disaster Relief Fought for $20 million in flood recovery funding to help Planada and rural farmworker communities rebuild after devastating winter storms.
Investing in Economic Growth & Education Secured $100 million for the Promise Housing Project, a partnership between UC Merced and Merced College to expand affordable student housing.
Empowered local economic development by authorizing an Enhanced Infrastructure & Financing District for the City of Merced.
Stronger Communities $8 million invested to build Mendota’s new Community Center, creating a hub for residents to access critical services and programs. $3.5 million for a public park in Franklin-Beachwood, ensuring families have more green spaces to enjoy. $3 million to improve streets and infrastructure in Tranquillity, keeping our agricultural communities strong and accessible.
Supporting Veterans & Protecting Workers Expanded the College Fee Waiver for Veteran Dependents program, ensuring more military families can access higher education.
Established a fund for maintaining veterans’ memorials in Capitol Park to honor their legacy.
Authored worker protection laws to ensure safer workplaces and prevent exploitation.
Agriculture & Rural Communities Protected bee populations essential for Valley agriculture through AB 1752, ensuring pollinators remain safeguarded for farming.
Increased support for farmers impacted by climate change through the Climate Smart Agriculture programs.
Advocated for funding to support water infrastructure and ensure sustainable access for Valley farmers and families.
Standing Up for the Central Valley Fought against special interests and career politicians who push policies that drive up costs for Central Valley residents and small businesses.
Worked across party lines to ensure our communities get their fair share of state resources—instead of being overlooked by Sacramento.
