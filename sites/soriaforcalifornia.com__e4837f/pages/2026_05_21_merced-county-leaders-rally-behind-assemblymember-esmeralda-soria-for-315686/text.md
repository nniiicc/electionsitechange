Merced County Leaders Rally Behind Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria For State Senate
Mayors, councilmembers, education leaders, and county officials across Merced County unite in support of Soria’s Senate District 14 campaign.
MERCED, CA – Today, Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria’s campaign for State Senate announced a sweeping round of endorsements from local leaders across Merced County, including mayors, city councilmembers, school board trustees, education leaders, and county officials who serve communities throughout the region.
The growing coalition of support reflects Soria’s deep ties across the Central Valley and her record of working closely with local governments, educators, and community leaders to deliver results for families.

“This kind of support means a great deal because it comes from the people who are closest to our communities and the challenges they face every day,” said Assemblymember Esmeralda Soria.
“I’ve worked side by side with local leaders across Merced County to bring resources home, strengthen our schools, and expand opportunities for families.
I’m grateful for their partnership and proud to have their support in this campaign.”
Soria’s campaign has secured endorsements from over 100 elected officials across California, as well as major labor unions and community organizations, establishing her as the clear choice for working families across the Central Valley.
