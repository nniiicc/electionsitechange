The endorsement celebrates Esmeralda Soria as a homegrown leader who has consistently delivered concrete results for working women, families, and communities across the Central Valley.
The Historic Fresno County Women’s Democratic Club (FCDWC) is proud to endorse Assemblymember Soria for California State Senate District 14.
The endorsement celebrates Esmeralda Soria as a homegrown leader who has consistently delivered concrete results for working women, families, and communities across the Central Valley.
“Assemblywoman Soria has proven time and again that she fights for her community with real action,” said Carole Laval, President of the Fresno County Democratic Women’s Club.
“From protecting farmworkers to securing funding for our hospitals, she shows up for Fresno women and families.
That’s the kind of leader we need representing us in the State Senate.” The FCDWC is a longstanding organization dedicated to advancing women’s equality and Democratic values in Fresno and across the Central Valley.
Founded in 1932, the club has been a powerful voice for working women and families, advocating for quality healthcare, economic opportunity, and equal representation at every level of government.
Soria’s record in the Assembly demonstrates her unwavering commitment to Democratic values of equality, opportunity, and community.
She has consistently centered the needs of working women, immigrant families, and communities of color in her legislative priorities.
“I am grateful for the endorsement of the Fresno Women’s Democratic Club,” said Assemblymember Soria.
“This organization has been a powerful voice for women’s equality and Democratic values in Fresno for decades.
I believe that women deserve equal pay, safe working conditions, quality healthcare, and the opportunity to build strong futures for our families.
I will bring that same commitment to every vote I cast in Sacramento.” The Fresno Women’s Democratic Club joins the sweeping coalition of support Assemblymember Soria has received from over 100 elected officials across California and labor unions, including SEIU, United Nurses Associations of California/Union of Health Care Professionals, and Latinas Lead, reinforcing her sweeping support for the Senate District 14 campaign.
