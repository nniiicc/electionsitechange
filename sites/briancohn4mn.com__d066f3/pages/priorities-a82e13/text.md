PRIORITIES
My priorities are simple: keep Minnesotans safe, enhance prosperity, and expand opportunity for every family and community in our state.
Government should work for people — protecting our rights, lowering costs, and helping everyone build a better future.
Safety
Everyone deserves to feel safe at home, at school, and in their community.
I support research-backed public safety solutions that reduce violence, strengthen communities, and respect responsible gun ownership.
Safety also means protecting our democracy by defending our constitution, safeguarding the freedom to vote, and supporting secure, convenient access to vote by mail and early voting.
It also means protecting civil rights, preventing government overreach, and ensuring that all law enforcement agencies — including federal immigration authorities — operate with transparency, accountability, and respect for the communities they serve.
Prosperity
Minnesotans should be able to build a good life without being crushed by rising costs.
I will fight to lower costs for working families by supporting affordable child care and housing, lowering health care and prescription drug costs, protecting paid family and medical leave, and standing up for strong union jobs and living wages.
I also support expanding access to affordable health care and protecting reproductive freedom, because families deserve both economic security and the freedom to make their own private medical decisions.
Seniors also deserve affordable access to housing, health care, transportation, and end-of-life care.
Opportunity
Every Minnesotan deserves a fair shot to succeed.
That starts with fully funding our public schools and expanding access to trade schools, internships, and other pathways that help young people succeed.
Opportunity also means improving access to mental health and addiction care, protecting our environment, investing in clean energy, and defending equal rights so every person has the freedom and chance to prosper.
