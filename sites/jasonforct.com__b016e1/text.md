GET TO KNOW JASON
State Representative Jason Buchsbaum is seeking reelection to continue representing Connecticut's 69th House District, which includes Bridgewater, a portion of New Milford, Roxbury, and most of Southbury.
Jason currently serves on the General Assembly's Public Safety and Security, Energy and Technology, and Commerce Committees.
Throughout his time in public service, Jason has taken a results-driven, data-based approach to decision making, while also advocating for transparency, civil discourse, and collaboration in government.
Jason's priorities include lowering Connecticut's exorbinant energy costs, supporting public safety and emergency services, opposing unfunded mandates and excessive legislative overreach, and protecting the right of our towns to govern themselves.
His focus in the year ahead will remain supporting common sense policies to make Connecticut more affordable and reduce the cost of living.
Before being elected as State Representative, Jason was elected to five terms on the Southbury Board of Selectmen, where he served as Chairman Pro Tempore and played a central role in the Town's effort to form an independent police department.
Jason is passionate about public service and is looking forward to continuing to serve the residents of the 69th District.
“I have the professional experience, the record of government service, and the drive and ability to get things done.
Our residents deserve strong, common sense leadership in Hartford.
It would be my great honor to continue representing the citizens of the 69th District to help build a more affordable and prosperous Connecticut." - Jason
