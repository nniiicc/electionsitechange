Dan with his family Serving his community At home in the 4th District Where Dan Stands Dan's Priorities for the 4th District Growth That Pays Its Own Way Require major developments to fund roads, drainage, water, and emergency-service capacity before residents move in, not years after taxpayers foot the bill.
Strengthen Public Safety Provide our police, firefighters, and first responders with the resources they need to keep our families safe.
Health Care People Can Afford Recruit more doctors and mental-health providers to Sussex County and demand price transparency for patients.
Preserve Farmland, Water & Rural Character Protect Delaware's farms, wells, waterways, and open space with conservation that works with property owners.
Protect Homeowners From Abandoned Obligations Require performance bonds and public obligation checklists so developers can't leave residents stuck with unfinished roads and drainage.
Strong Schools & Parental Rights Prioritize reading, math, and career prep, and protect parents' right to know what their children are being taught.
Veterans Deserve Care Close to Home Expand specialty and mental-health care access in Sussex County so veterans aren't forced to travel to Wilmington.
Rights, Responsibility & Representation Defend the Second Amendment, parental rights, and the dignity of human life, with a representative who is accountable and available.
Lower Costs & Reliable, Affordable Energy Oppose new taxes and fees, require large electricity users like data centers to pay their fair share of the grid, and keep energy policy focused on reliability, not mandates that raise bills.
