Voters should ask themselves, what are a candidate’s priorities?
Will those strengthen our communities, strengthen our residents’ quality of life, and strengthen our democracy?
Erica’s work as an educator, a member of her city council and a leader in her teacher’s association says YES to each of those questions!
- 30-year resident of Wheaton.
- Married and Mother of two sons that were raised through the Wheaton Public schools along with a multitude of community organizations (it takes a village – or city).
- Wheaton City Councilwoman At-Large since 2019.
- 33 years high school civics and psychology teacher – retired.
- Bachelor’s degree from Illinois State University in Social Science Education.
- Master’s degree from Northern Illinois University in Political Science through the James Madison Memorial Foundation Fellowship Award.
- Elected leader in the Illinois Education Association.
- Active volunteer in my church and other local community organizations.
- Member League of Women Voters, Wheaton – current and active.
- Milton Democratic Precinct Committeeman – past.
