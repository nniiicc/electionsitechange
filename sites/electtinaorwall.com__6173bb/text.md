A lifelong social worker, proud mom of two, and your State Senator.
Tina Orwall is committed to improving and protecting South King County communities.
She is focused on:
- Fighting back against federal overreach and ensuring Washingtonians are protected from harmful policies.
- Working to make housing more affordable and accessible for all families.
- Prioritizing mental health resources, public safety, and creating healthier environments for every resident.
