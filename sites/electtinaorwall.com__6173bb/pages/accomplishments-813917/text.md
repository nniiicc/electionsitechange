Accomplishments
Tina Orwall has built her career on standing up for Washington’s families, communities, and values.
Whether it’s resisting harmful federal overreach, tackling housing affordability, expanding access to healthcare and education, or protecting our environment, Tina is a proven leader focused on delivering real solutions to the challenges facing our communities.
-
Protecting Washington Against Federal Overreach
Since the Trump-Vance administration took office this year, Tina has wasted no time in taking action to defend Washington’s core values.
She is standing up against illegal deportations, attacks on voting rights, and harmful shifts in education policy.
Tina is committed to defending Washingtonians from overreach and preserving the state’s values of inclusion, safety, and equity.
- Spoke out against Trump-era federal funding freezes that threatened health programs and infrastructure in Washington.
- Took action to oppose illegal deportations and protect due process for immigrants.
- Defended voting rights from Republican-led federal legislation seeking to restrict access to the ballot.
- Championed Washington’s diversity through resolutions honoring Transgender Day of Visibility, Ramadan, the African Diaspora, and AAPI heritage in public schools.
-
Expanding Access to Affordable Housing
Washington’s housing crisis is deepening, and Tina is leading efforts to reverse it.
In South King County, lack of affordable housing and stagnant development have caused growing homelessness.
That’s why Tina has led efforts to protect homeowners, build affordable housing, and fight predatory real estate practices.
- Passed the Foreclosure Fairness Act to give families legal support and mediation before losing their homes.
- Supported the Housing Trust Fund to build affordable housing across counties.
- Backed the Health Home Program, creating 1,000+ units for homeless families, veterans, and people with disabilities.
- Sponsored legislation to track housing demand and hold counties accountable for meeting it.
- Proposed limits on excessive corporate homebuying and increased transparency in housing ownership.
-
Improving Safety & Mental Health
As both a lawmaker and former social worker, Tina knows we must face difficult issues like violence, addiction, and mental health head-on to keep our communities safe.
She’s not afraid to tackle complex, under-discussed challenges to protect families.
- Led the charge on rape kit reform, clearing the backlog and establishing trauma-informed response standards.
- Strengthened the 988 suicide prevention system, training response teams and implementing crisis intervention programs.
- Introduced laws to criminalize AI-generated deepfakes that exploit minors.
- Supported gun safety by expanding background checks and limiting firearms near children.
- Pushed forward behavioral health screenings and violence intervention programs in schools and communities.
-
Strengthening Our Education System
Tina believes every child deserves a quality education in a safe, supportive environment because she knows it is the foundation for their future.
Having worked closely with schools, families, and nonprofits to ensure students feel prepared for life after graduation, Tina has won more funding for schools, promoted equity, and improved mental health support for students.
- Advocated for increased public school funding through the School Levy.
- Partnered with schools and nonprofits to improve language access for families of all backgrounds.
- Passed legislation for behavioral health training in schools to support struggling students.
- Introduced bills for free school meals and required financial literacy education in high schools.
- Advanced policies to prevent isolation and ensure gender inclusivity in classrooms.
-
Protecting Our Environment
Tina is committed to preserving Washington’s natural beauty and ensuring future generations inherit a healthy environment.
In the 33rd District, especially near SeaTac Airport, she has worked closely with researchers, local leaders, and the community to tackle pollution and environmental health.
- Passed a bill to reduce noise and chemical pollution near SeaTac by funding home improvements like triple-pane windows.
- Supported bans on plastic cutlery and bags to reduce waterway pollution.
- Worked with UW researchers to monitor air quality in schools, particularly near high-risk areas.
- Backed clean energy bills requiring solar panels on new school construction.
- Advocated for stronger solid waste systems and environmental equity in port communities.
Tina Orwall is deeply committed to fighting for a more affordable, safe, equitable, and sustainable Washington—and her legislative record proves it.
