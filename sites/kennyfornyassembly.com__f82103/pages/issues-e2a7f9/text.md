Just the People of the 113th District.
Power Belongs to the People — Right Here at Home In the 113th District, we believe something simple: no one is above the people.
Not billionaires, not political insiders, not anyone.
Our communities from Saratoga Springs to the towns and villages across Washington County deserve a government that listens, respects, and delivers.
This campaign is about bringing power back where it belongs: in the hands of the people who live and work here.
Built by and for Working Families Whether you’re commuting to work, raising a family, running a small business, or trying to make ends meet, you deserve leadership that has your back.
We’re fighting for good-paying jobs, strong unions, and an economy that works beyond just a few zip codes.
The 113th District is full of hardworking people — and it’s time Albany started working just as hard for them.
Protecting What Makes Our Communities Strong Our district is defined by its character, vibrant small businesses, local farms, strong schools, and neighborhoods where people look out for one another.
We’ll fight to keep it that way by supporting local economies, investing in public education, and making sure growth benefits everyone, not just developers or outside interests.
Democracy You Can Trust People here deserve a government that is transparent, accountable, and accessible.
That means making it easier to vote, ensuring fair representation, and reducing the influence of big money in politics.
Democracy isn’t just a concept — it’s something we live every day, and it should work for everyone in the 113th District.
Standing Up to Concentrated Power Too often, decisions are made by and for the powerful, leaving communities like ours behind.
This campaign is about changing that.
We’ll stand up to corporate interests, fight for fair taxation, and ensure that no one can use their power or position to silence the voices of everyday people.
A Future That Works for All of Us From affordable housing to healthcare access to protecting our environment, we’re focused on practical solutions that improve everyday life.
We can build a future where young people stay, families grow, and seniors can retire with dignity — all right here in the 113th District.
